## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed failures: the plain-text part is empty (0 chars), and the Unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400 when probed. Eleven images are served over `http://`, and the `<head>` has a malformed charset declaration and an empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe returned 400.** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1MmZpSFY0ajRYThIDMVN5GJaVpAYgAg&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&...` (link text "Unsubscribe") failed the probe. This may be a probe artifact, because Attentive's `dvm` tokens can be session- or user-agent-bound. It is unconfirmed either way and should be verified in a real client.
- **429 responses are not defects.** The "Ready to check out? Complete your order" link and the image link (`de642685-...jpg`) returned 429 from `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. This is rate limiting from the probe, and the QA tool says the links may be valid.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 43 links were probed, and the 31 skipped links are unverified.
- **Tracked links are opaque.** All observed links, including the "web version" link, are wrapped in Attentive redirects (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`), so destinations can't be checked from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is empty.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). Clients that need or prefer a text part get nothing, and spam filters may score it down.
- **11 images use `http://`** on `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/...`. These are the logo, `a6ec5198...jpg`, and the Text, App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons. Clients may block or warn on mixed content. The same host serves the https `de642685...jpg` image, so https is available on that host.
- **Missing alt text on three images:**
  - `de642685-77b3-46fa-950b-5bd9ee6800e1.jpg`: this one is also a linked image, so the link has no accessible name.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`
  - The tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` (low impact).
- **Charset declaration is malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and no `charset=` attribute, so it declares nothing. Rendering depends on the HTTP/MIME header. The preheader contains `͏` and soft-hyphen characters, which makes this more relevant.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This hurts web-version and screen-reader context.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400** (see section 2). If it is dead for real recipients, it breaches CAN-SPAM's requirement for a functioning opt-out.
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a gap in the check and not a confirmed failure.
- **Not verifiable from the data:** the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address in the footer. The HTML is truncated and the headers weren't provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The wrapped Attentive redirects hide the final URLs, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified from the source. The two 429 responses also blocked following the CTA to its destination. Whether the cart link restores the abandoned cart is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the unsubscribe URL** in a real client. If it fails there too, fix it, and add a `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header.
2. **Add a plain-text alternative** (multipart/alternative) with the cart CTA and unsubscribe URL.
3. **Switch the 11 `http://` image URLs to `https://`** on `image.emails.skechers.com`.
4. **Add `alt` text** to `de642685...jpg`, `a6ec5198...jpg` and the open pixel (`alt=""`).
5. **Fix the charset meta** to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`, and populate `<title>`.
6. **Capture the raw headers** (Authentication-Results, List-Unsubscribe) in the QA harness. Re-probe the 429 links with backoff and follow them to the landing page to check UTMs and cart restoration.
