# Feel Good With BOBS Styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- I open this one and — of course — that same wall of raw computer code is sitting right there in the preview where actual words should be. Thirteen emails in a row now, by my count. Somebody at Skechers has to know this is happening. At this point I'm starting to wonder if anyone over there is even checking their own inbox.
- Inside the email the layout is clean enough — big BOGO offer at the top, some shoe photos, a sale section below. But here's my problem: BOBS is Skechers' women's line. The model in the hero shot is a woman with a dog. The shoes look like women's slip-ons and mary janes. I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for a cushioned walking shoe. This email was not sent for me. I'd probably mark it read and move on — I wouldn't forward it to anyone either, unless I was passing it along to my wife or daughter.
- The BOGO 50% off is a real offer, and that counts for something. But a good deal on shoes that aren't made for me is still not a reason to click.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off call-out is front and center at the very top — I see the offer before I even process anything else, which is how it should be.
- "SHOP NOW" buttons are big, visible, and tappable — easy to find on a phone screen without squinting.
- The email body itself renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, nothing jumbled. If the preview weren't a disaster, the email would feel pretty polished.
- The up-to-40%-off section lower down stacks another concrete discount — two price hooks in one send is solid.
- Star ratings / "LOVE!!!" social proof section is a nice trust cue, even if it's small.

## What's weak

- This is a women's brand email sent to a man. BOBS by Skechers is charity-linked women's footwear. The hero model is a woman, the styles look feminine. I'm not the target customer for this creative.
- That preview text is raw JSON schema code — again. This is the thirteenth straight Skechers send with the same broken preview. At some point this isn't a bug, it's a policy failure.
- No comfort language anywhere — no cushioning, no arch support, no "all-day wear." Skechers is supposed to know I care about that. Nothing in here speaks to why these feel good on your feet, only that they look good.
- No deadline on the BOGO offer. "Buy one, get one 50% off" is fine but without an end date there's zero urgency — I'll tell myself I'll come back later and I won't.
- No Skechers Plus mention. I'm a member; they know that. A member-price callout or point-earning reminder would've pulled me in more.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Send this to women, not men.** BOBS is a women's sub-brand. If I'm in the men's segment, this creative should never have hit my inbox. Fix the segmentation before touching a single word of copy.
- 2. **Fix the preview text.** This is the most damaging problem in the entire Skechers email program right now. Raw JSON in the preview window destroys credibility before the email is even opened. It needs to be real, human-written copy.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline to the BOGO offer.** "Ends Sunday" or "48 hours only" turns a shrug into a reason to act now.
- 4. **Add one comfort detail to the hero.** Even one line — "Memory foam insole" or "arch-fit cushioning" — signals that these shoes earn the "Feel Good" promise with something real, not just vibes.
- 5. **Surface the Skechers Plus member benefit.** A "members earn double points on BOBS" callout or similar would make me feel like a known customer, not a stranger.

## Technical Audit — "Feel Good With BOBS Styles" (2026-06-30)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email sends on Salesforce Marketing Cloud infrastructure and is structurally sound, but has a broken tracking pixel (missing URL scheme), a broad HTTP mixed-content problem across all hosted images, and two missing alt attributes. No unresolved merge tokens or CAN-SPAM violations were detected in the visible source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Broken: Return Path pixel missing protocol scheme**
```
pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499
```
The `src` value has no `http://` or `https://` prefix. Browsers and email clients will treat this as a relative path and the pixel will silently fail to fire. Inbox placement/reputation monitoring via Return Path is non-functional for this send.

**Third-party DMP pixels present**
Three `beacon.krxd.net` (Salesforce Krux DMP) pixels and one `ink1000.com` pixel fire on open. These are HTTPS for Krux but HTTP for ink1000 (see §3). No action required on Krux; ink1000 needs upgrading.

**26 click-redirect links not probed**
All outbound links route through `click.emails.skechers.com`. Destination URL health and UTM coverage cannot be confirmed without following redirects (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — 10 affected assets**

All assets hosted on `image.emails.skechers.com` are served over plain HTTP. Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook 2016+, Apple Mail) load images through a proxy that rewrites to HTTPS; older clients and some privacy proxies will block these outright, showing broken image placeholders.

Affected assets include the Skechers logo, app store badges, social icons, and service icons:
```

http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/9bb508d0-...png  (Google Play)
... (6 more)
```
The ink1000 tracking pixel also fires over HTTP:
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif
```

**Missing alt text — 2 images**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — alt="" is acceptable for purely decorative/tracking pixels but the attribute is absent entirely, which is a markup error
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` (hosted on `image.emails.skechers.com`) — content image with no alt; screen readers will either skip it or announce the filename

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`**
Set globally on `*`. This prevents iOS from resizing small text to readable sizes. Should be `100%` unless small text is intentional.

### 5. Compliance

**Sender authentication** — Sending domain is `emails.skechers.com` (subdomain of `skechers.com`). Authentication headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment) are not available in the HTML source and were not included in the QA data. No issue confirmable from available evidence.

This is a legal/privacy question, not a code defect, and is flagged for awareness only.
