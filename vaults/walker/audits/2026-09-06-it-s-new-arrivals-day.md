- Six shoes in the grid and I'd maybe consider one — too much neon, trail tread, and a pink suede "fashion" sneaker, not enough plain cushioned walking shoes for a guy like me.

Decoded `jwtP` payload shows destination `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=...`.

- Tracking pixels present: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...` (open tracking) — both load as `<img>` with no alt text (see §3).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
