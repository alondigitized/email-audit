# Win up to $1,000 + FREE Shoes tomorrow from 7am
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival again, and this is the third Thanksgiving-day send I've gotten from them. The subject promised "Win up to $1,000 + FREE Shoes tomorrow from 7am." I opened it looking for that contest. What I got was the same "Happy Thanksgiving / Black Friday Doorbusters" email I've been getting since the 13th. The $1,000 and the free shoes aren't in the body anywhere I can see. The nearest thing is a small "Play Word Stride" box near the bottom, and that's a daily game, not a 7am giveaway.
- The layout is the usual big red and gold blocks with the prices in large type. That part I can read on my phone. But almost all of it is women's tall boots, clogs and booties. The Skechers tile at $39.98 and the Nike 30% off are the only things that look like they're for me. There's no comfort, cushioning, or arch support talk, and nothing slip-on.
- Would I click? Maybe the Skechers tile, if I weren't tired of this sender. I wouldn't forward it. The subject set a hook and the body didn't deliver, so it reads as bait and switch to me.

## What's working

- The "Skechers starting at $39.98" tile is easy to find. Skechers is a brand I trust for comfort.
- The prices are big and readable without zooming.
- The "Extra 25% off, code GIVETHANKS" banner is clear about the offer and how to use it.
- The "MENS" link in the bottom nav bar is large enough to tap.
- The $10 Shoe Perks reward and the Afterpay box tell me what the membership gets me.

## What's weak

- The "Win up to $1,000 + FREE Shoes tomorrow from 7am" subject doesn't match anything in the email. I can't see a contest or a 7am event.
- Most of the email is women's and kids' boots, clogs and booties. Only two tiles are even close to my kind of shopping.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support, or hands-free slip-ons, which is what I buy on.
- The "HEYDUDE" tile is a bare $34.98 with no shoe I can recognize and no word on comfort.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and hard to read, and the "Extra 25% Off" exclusions are buried in it.
- It's the same Black Friday creative I got on the 24th, 25th and this morning.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the body. If there's a contest, put it at the top with the date and time. If there isn't, don't mention it.
- 2. Add a men's comfort row near the top with Skechers slip-ins, HEYDUDE and Hoka, and a line like "cushioned, arch-supported, slip-on."
- 3. Cut the women's boots from the first screen for male subscribers.
- 4. Pick one clear call to action. Right now there are about ten "Shop ..." links.
- 5. Make the fine print bigger and move the main exclusions up.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Skechers from $39.98 + extra 25% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's comfort shoes: Black Friday doorbusters`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code GIVETHANKS takes 25% off. Slip-ons, walkers and Nike 30% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters open tomorrow at 7am. Shop men's comfort first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Of the visible source and QA data, two tracking-pixel URLs carry problems: one has an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder and the other a stray zero-width character. Link destinations, the unsubscribe mechanism and authentication could not be verified, because the HTML is truncated and 31 links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved token in pixel URL:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` contains a literal `[UNIQUE]`. If this is not substituted at send time, the open or click pixel won't resolve to a per-recipient ID.
- **Hidden character in URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261126_boots_thanksgiving_PM​` ends with a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_PM`. This can corrupt the campaign value or break the request.
- **Malformed `t=` parameter:** `t=utm_campaign=SCA_…` nests a `key=value` inside a parameter value without URL-encoding. Whether the tracker parses it correctly is unconfirmed.
- **Probe coverage:** 31 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped (`emltrk.com`). The final destinations and their HTTP status are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on two images.** Both are the `emltrk.com` tracking pixels above. This is low impact if they are 1×1 pixels, but they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Both are ineffective as written.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and self-closing tags. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Positives seen in source:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - A viewport meta tag is present.
  - Media queries cover 660px and 450px.
  - Apple and Samsung autolink fixes are included.
- Body content was truncated, so I can't check alt text on content images, table roles or contrast.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the send pipeline for why `[UNIQUE]` was not substituted in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel. Confirm whether it is a template bug or a capture artifact.
2. Strip the U+200B from the campaign string `SCA_20261126_boots_thanksgiving_PM` and URL-encode the nested `t=utm_campaign=…`.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Re-run the audit with full HTML and raw headers. That would let you verify `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
