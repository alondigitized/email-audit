# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. That's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom," a women's shoe I never put in a cart, and it takes up the top third of the email. I saw this same cart nag on 10/9 and four times yesterday ("Your cart expires soon!", "Did you forget something?"). "We noticed you noticing us…" is still a little creepy. I'd rather not be told I'm being watched.
- The bottom half is better. There's a men's slip-on, the Relaxed Fit Altman, and the Contour Foam Cozy Fit, and those are my kind of shoe. But they're small, and they sit under a hero I don't want. The 10% off sitewide for members is real money, but it's tucked in a dark banner near the bottom, ends today, and the fine print is tiny.
- Would I click? Probably not from this one. I'd skim it, see the pink shoe, and mark it read. Would I forward it? No. If a friend got a nag like this, I'd tell them to ignore it.

## What's working

- The men's slip-ins (Altman, Contour Foam Cozy Fit) are in the grid. That's the comfort I shop for.
- "Complete your order" is a clear button with a clear job.
- The 10% off banner is big, readable white-on-navy text. The "valid thru 10/11" deadline is honest and visible.
- The Men / Women / Kids / Sale list is big and easy to tap on a phone.
- The SKECHERS logo and navy look match the brand, so I trust that it's real.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's floral sneaker I never carted. That's the fifth or sixth time I've seen it, and it makes the "cart" claim feel wrong.
- "We noticed you noticing us…" feels like surveillance.
- Half the grid is kids' shoes (boys' and girls' Garage sneakers), which means nothing to me.
- The grid captions are tiny and hard to read without zooming, and there's no price on any shoe.
- The 10% off is buried below the product grid, and the fine print is microscopic.
- There's no "Hi, Walker" and no points balance here, though the 10/10 "Next-level tech in every step" email had both. This one treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show what is actually in my cart, or drop the cart framing. Don't show a shoe I never picked. If there's no real cart item, lead with the men's slip-ins.
- 2. Put the 10% member offer at the top, next to the button.
- 3. Show prices and a "Slip-ins" or "Arch Fit" tag under each shoe, and make the captions larger.
- 4. Drop the kids' shoes and match the grid to what I browse.
- 5. Limit cart reminders to one per day. Six in two days is too many.
- 6. Add "Hi, Walker" and my points balance.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Abandoned-cart email sent through Attentive (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com`) with a failing unsubscribe probe (HTTP 400), no plain-text part (0 chars), and a mostly `http://` image set. Two CTA links returned 429, so their destinations are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe returned 400.** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1...&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m50&externalId=2U3eG&attn_sid=6a12841d-...`.
  - This is a tokenized Attentive link. A 400 may come from the automated probe (bot user-agent, or a method or token that isn't valid outside the recipient session), so it may not be broken for real users.
  - It can't be confirmed from the data given. Treat it as a high-priority manual check, because a dead unsubscribe is a compliance risk.
- **Two CTAs are unverified (429).** "Complete you Order" and "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting..." both go to `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` wrappers. Neither the redirect nor the cart destination was validated.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 26 skipped, so most footer, social and secondary product links are unchecked.
- **Click tracking is fully wrapped.** The "web version" link and the logo link are both `attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirects. Their final destinations can't be seen in the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.** Nearly all images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`, including:
  - the logo (`m/1/dde00662-...png`)

  - the product-recommendation image (`m/5/737031d9-...jpg`)
  - the App Store, Google Play, Afterpay, Find a Store and social icons (`m/11/...`)
  
  Some clients will block or warn on these. The one `https://` image is `m/21/021bd784-...gif`.
- **Missing alt text:**
