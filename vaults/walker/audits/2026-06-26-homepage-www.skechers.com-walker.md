# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- I opened the Skechers app and got hit with a young woman striking a pose surrounded by paint-splattered sneakers — some kind of artist collab called Britto. That's not why I'm here. I'm here because my feet need cushion and arch support, and this homepage right now looks like it's trying to sell me on a museum gift shop. The BOGO 50% banner and the Arch Fit nav link are the two things keeping me from tapping away.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — that blue bar is the first thing my eye hits. Concrete, no asterisk visible, and it says "Applied at Cart" which means I don't have to hunt for a promo code.
- **Member Month loyalty strip** — a $250 gift card giveaway with a real deadline (6/28) gives me a reason to log in or engage rather than bounce.
- **Arch Fit and Slip-ins in the nav** — exactly the two categories I'd tap first. They're right there at eye level, large enough to tap easily on my phone.
- **Clean layout** — nothing is crashing into anything else. Text is readable, spacing is decent. Doesn't fight me the way some mobile sites do.

## What's weak

- **The Britto hero is not my world** — I don't know Romero Britto from a shoe rack in DSW. A young woman doing an art-fashion pose doesn't make me think "my feet will feel better in these." It makes me think I accidentally opened the wrong app.
- **No CTA button on the hero** — "Available for Men and Women" is not a button. I'm staring at a big image with no obvious next tap except hunting the nav.
- **No comfort / support language anywhere above the fold** — I see "Arch Fit" in the nav, but zero words about cushioning, support, or walking comfort in the hero or banners. That's the whole reason I shop Skechers.
- **No free shipping mention** — DSW reminds me every visit. I don't see it here, and that matters when I'm comparing tabs.
- **Nothing tells me what the BOGO applies to** — is it everything? Just Britto? I'd have to tap "details" to find out, which is a small friction but real.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Britto hero for an Arch Fit or Slip-ins lifestyle shot** — show someone my age on a walk or doing light yardwork. Keep the BOGO banner but tie the hero image to comfort product, not artist collab.
- **Add a CTA button to the hero** — "Shop Men's Comfort" or "Shop Slip-ins" with a visible button under that Britto copy. Even a ghost button would be better than nothing.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the BOGO banner or Member strip** — one extra line, "Free shipping on $65+" makes the value stack feel complete and keeps me from opening a competing tab.
- **Promote Arch Fit or Slip-ins as a featured category strip below the hero** — a horizontal scroll rail of three or four products with prices and ratings would give me something to engage with immediately without having to navigate deeper.
