# Get Extra 10% Off Clearance!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Here's another DSW clearance email, and it's about the fifth this week. I opened it because "Extra 10%" at least has a number in it. This time the grid is mostly sneakers, which is a step up from the wall of pointy heels I got on the 26th and 27th. But look closer and they're retro fashion sneakers: a Puma, an Adidas Samba-type, a red platform Adidas, a silver metallic. The bottom row goes back to strappy heels, sandals and a leopard bootie. I don't see a cushioned walking shoe or a slip-on in the bunch.
- The offer is clear enough: extra 10% online with code GOLDEN, plus 30% off in stores. But I have to type a code, and the dates (Sept 28-30) are down in the fine print, not up top. Nothing here says "men," and the "Shop by Size" block runs 5 to 11, which is women's sizing. I'd skim it and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The red hero banner with "EXTRA 10% OFF CLEARANCE ONLINE" is big and easy to read on my phone.
- Sneakers finally show up in the grid (Puma, New Balance, Adidas). That's better than last Friday's heels.
- The "Shop Wide Width" button is useful. Fit matters to me, and it's a big, easy tap target.
- The "Shopping Made Easy" strip (free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup) is simple and clear.
- The New Balance and Adidas logos give me a comparison-shopping feel, since I cross-shop those brands.

## What's weak

- Nothing is labeled for men. The grid mixes in a women's Mary Jane flat, heels, sandals and a bootie, and there's no comfort or arch-support talk anywhere.
- I have to remember and type "GOLDEN." No one-tap apply.
- The urgency and the deadline are buried in tiny gray fine print. The subject and hero say nothing about when it ends.
- "Shop by Size" stops at 11, and the sizes look women's. There's no 12 or 13 for a fellow like me.
- The fine print takes up a third of the email, in small type I'd have to pinch-zoom to read.
- Another clearance email so soon after "EXTRA 10% off clearance 🎉" earlier today. It feels like the same promo on repeat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the grid by audience and tag the shoes, e.g. "Men's comfort sneakers & slip-ons on clearance." Lead with Brooks, Skechers, Hoka and New Balance walkers, and drop the heels from a broad send.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Wednesday") and auto-apply the code through the button link.
- 3. Add men's sizing, 12 to 14, and a "Men's" shop-by-size row.
- 4. Cut the frequency, or make each send different so it doesn't feel like the same email.
- 5. Show one price ("Now $59, was $110") on a real walking shoe so I can do the price-vs-quality math.

# Technical Audit: DSW, "Get Extra 10% Off Clearance!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one probed link returning HTTP 400 (the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA), a plain-text part that is 84% URLs, and several source-level accessibility gaps. The HTML source is truncated and no headers were supplied, so compliance and UTM checks are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** The CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/lIY-Ps2XBRj4QgDYj1DSkg~~/AAQRxRA~/Kg4Na2Cp…` (a ~700-character redirect token). The probe got a 400.
  - A 400 on a click-tracking redirect can mean a malformed or expired token, or that the ESP rejects automated probes. The QA data can't tell these apart.
  - If real, it is the highest-value link in the email, since it drives SMS acquisition. Confirm with a real-client click test.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 55 links were probed and 43 were skipped, so link health is unverified for about 78% of links.
- **Tracking pixels:** Two open-tracking images are present:
  - `ablink.dsw.com/q/63vbflXEH4hUbmzksmYPew~~/…`
  - `mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20260928_P251277_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_1`
  - Two independent open pixels (ablink and mi.dsw.com) means two tracking systems. That is redundant, and each adds a third-party request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Both flagged images are tracking pixels: `vm0ix2bS24oPqYR9…` and `o.gif`. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) concern for low-vision users.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't select the correct pronunciation.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`, so neither is functional.
  - The charset declaration is ignored, which is a risk if the ESP doesn't send `Content-Type: …; charset=utf-8`.
- **Weak `<title>`:** The title is just "DSW", which is non-descriptive for web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Web fonts:** Web fonts load from `braze-images.com` (woff/ttf) with no visible fallback stack in the truncated source. Most clients strip `@font-face`, so verify the fallback fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Unverified:** The QA data includes no failure for the unsubscribe link, but I can't confirm the following:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.

- **SMS opt-in:** The CTA "Opt-In to Texts" is broken (see section 2). A working link matters for TCPA-consent flow.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- **Campaign identifier:** The pixel carries `mi_ecmp=20260928_P251277_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_1`, so campaign attribution exists on the mi.dsw.com side. I can't confirm it matches the ablink campaign or any analytics UTMs.

2. **Probe more links.** Raise the probe limit or run a full crawl, since 43 of 55 links are unchecked.
