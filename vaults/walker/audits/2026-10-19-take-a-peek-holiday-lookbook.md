# Take a peek 👀 Holiday Lookbook 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, but at least it isn't another BOGO rerun. After nine BOGO emails in five days, a holiday lookbook with a slipper up top is a relief. The UGG slipper at $99.96 is the first thing I see, and a slip-on slipper is something I'd actually wear. Then the email turns into a gift catalog: pink panels, young folks hugging presents, Nike chunky sneakers, Birkenstock, Timberland, a beanie, sunglasses.
- I don't see much of me in it. Nothing about cushioning, arch support or all-day walking. The models are 20-somethings, and it reads as "shop for someone else." I'd tap the UGG button, glance at the price, and probably leave. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread-adjacent and move on.

## What's working

- The UGG slipper hero is right at the top, with a big price and a big black "Shop Now" button. I knew what the email was about in two seconds.
- $99.96 is a clear price, and the text is large enough to read on my phone without pinching.
- The brand tiles (UGG, Nike, Birkenstock, Timberland) each link to one name. That's easy to tap and easy to skip.
- The size grid with "Shop Wide Width" at the bottom is a real help for a guy with wide feet.
- The email looks the way DSW looks on the website, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- No discount is shown. $99.96 means nothing without a "was" price, and the fine print about "COMP. VALUE" pricing doesn't build my confidence.
- It's all gifting and fashion, with nothing on comfort, cushioning or arch support. Birkenstock is the closest it gets, and it isn't framed that way.
- The models are young and the styling is trendy, so it feels aimed at my grandkids' generation.
- The three buttons in the middle ("Shop the Lookbook" twice, "Shop All Women") are vague. "Shop All Women" is irrelevant to me.
- There's no deadline and no member perk near the top. The VIP banner is buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a real, comfort-framed offer, such as "Cushioned slip-ons for gift-giving, from $X," and show the original price next to the sale price.
- 2. Add a men's comfort tile (slip-on walking shoes, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance) to the middle of the email. Right now men's comfort is missing.
- 3. Replace the repeated generic buttons with specific ones like "Shop men's slippers" or "Shop wide-width shoes."
- 4. Move the VIP and free-shipping perks near the top, and add a gift-by-date deadline for urgency.
- 5. Segment by past purchase so a comfort buyer sees a comfort hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
No hard failures were found (0 issues, 2 warnings, 75% pass rate). The weak spots are two images with no alt text, malformed `<meta>` tags in the head, and an auth status that couldn't be checked. The HTML is truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links go through the Braze click-tracker `ablink.dsw.com/q/...`. The final destinations and any UTM parameters aren't visible in the source.
- QA probed 12 of 59 links (47 skipped) and reported no broken links. The 47 skipped links are unverified, mostly footer, utility and social links.
- One `<img>` has a `src` on the click-tracking path: `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/L9gtEYPpGIwR5fPvpzKN-w~~/AAQRxRA~/Z7vpGe-68RiWt5DkTU0tJ5o3dbEwNW3GkIkAudP5aMvZVm-asC...`. If this is a content image, it depends on a redirect hop to load, which can slow or break rendering. If it's a pixel, the path is unusual. Either way it should be checked.
- A second tracking pixel, `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261019_P261261_V1_W-_Offer_Trend`, carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Two `<img>` elements have no `alt` attribute: the `ablink.dsw.com/q/...` image and `o.gif`. The pixel should have `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions. The other image needs descriptive alt text if it carries content.
- `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither does anything. The charset should be declared with `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `<meta charset>`.
- The viewport tag sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- I couldn't check the body for table roles, `lang`, or font fallback stacks because the HTML is truncated. The custom fonts (`Futura_PT_*`, `HelveticaNow_*`) load from `braze-images.com`, and their fallbacks aren't visible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appear in the visible source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `Authentication-Results` wasn't found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the AgentMail relay, so this is an unverified item rather than a confirmed failure.
- The sender `dsw@e.dsw.com` and tracking domain `ablink.dsw.com` are both on `dsw.com`, which suggests aligned branding.
- QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set. The truncated source doesn't show it, and no `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were supplied. I couldn't confirm the unsubscribe mechanism or the physical postal address.
- The plain-text part is 19,984 of 23,070 characters URLs (about 87%). That is a spam-filter risk because of the long tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
I couldn't assess this. The wrapped links hide the destination URLs and UTMs. The 12 probed links returned no flagged errors, but QA didn't report their final URLs or parameters. The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261019_P261261_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` in the pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to `o.gif`. Add meaningful alt text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/...` image, and confirm why an image points at a click-tracking URL.
2. Fix the head: use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
3. Reduce the URL share of the plain-text part with shorter tracking links, or by trimming repeated links. This should bring it well under 70%.
4. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers from raw headers captured outside the relay.
5. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the wrapped links to their final URLs so UTM parameters and landing pages can be audited, and probe the 47 skipped links.
