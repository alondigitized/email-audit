# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Well, now this is more like it. That's an actual men's shoe — a Slip-in, the Garza-Gervin, brown leather-looking casual sneaker, not that pink floral D'Lites nonsense they've been shoving at me all week. It's still one I looked at and didn't buy, but at least this time it's believable — I probably did click around on Slip-ins looking for something hands-free and cushioned.
- The 20% off code and the row of other slip-ins and arch-fit shoes below it actually caught my eye — the Max Cushioning Arch Fit and the Arch Fit Arcade look like real options for me. That said, it's still a "you left something in your cart" retread, and I'm getting a little tired of hearing about "great taste" from a company that emails me every day.
- I'd click through on the Garza-Gervin or maybe the Max Cushioning pair to compare against what I've seen at Hoka or New Balance. Wouldn't forward it to anybody, but I wouldn't delete it on sight either.

## What's working

- Actual men's slip-in shoe front and center — finally relevant to what I shop for, not a random floral sneaker.
- "Here's some more great options" row gives me real alternatives — the Arch Fit and Max Cushioning styles are exactly my kind of shoe.
- 20% off with a clear code (UPGRADE) and a defined date range in the fine print — feels like a real, time-bound deal, not vague.
- "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" button is a plain, obvious CTA — no guessing what happens when I tap it.

## What's weak

- "Still thinking about it? You've got great taste!" is a little cutesy for me — I don't need flattery, just tell me the shoe and the price.
- No price shown anywhere near the shoe — I have to click through just to see what it costs before or after the 20% off.
- Big gap of white space between the headline and the shoe photo — feels like padding before the phone screen even gets to the point.
- The 20% off banner only applies to "Cozy Fit & Glide Step" styles, but it's not obvious if the Garza-Gervin up top even qualifies.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price and the discounted price right under the shoe photo — I shouldn't have to tap through to do math.
- 2. Clarify whether the featured shoe qualifies for the 20% code — right now the banner and the hero shoe feel disconnected.
- 3. Cut the "great taste" line — swap in something that tells me why to come back, like fit or comfort detail.
- 4. Move the "more great options" row higher, right after the hero shoe, so I don't have to scroll as far to compare.
