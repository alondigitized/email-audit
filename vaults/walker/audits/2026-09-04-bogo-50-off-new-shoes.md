There's a "Shop by Size" grid that goes up to 11, a "Shop Wide Width" and "Wide Calf" button — every signal on this page is built for a woman shopping her own closet, not me.

- The "Shopping Made Easy" strip at the bottom (free shipping, in-store pickup, Afterpay) is clean and I can read it fine on my phone.

## What's weak

- Every single product photo is a women's shoe — clogs, mary janes, wedge booties, leopard heels, ballet flats. Not one men's walking shoe or sneaker anywhere in the whole scroll.
- "Shop by Size" tops out at 11 and includes "Wide Calf" — that's a women's boot sizing chart, another reminder this wasn't built with me in mind.
- I've now gotten this same BOGO 50% off pitch close to five times this week from DSW, just re-skinned each time — "Booties," "Fall Lookbook," "New Shoes." It's starting to feel like the same email wearing a new hat.
- The "Can You Keep a Secret??" VIP teaser is just vague noise — no actual benefit stated, just "get the app for deets soon," which tells me nothing.

- 2. Cut the send frequency — five BOGO emails in a week with the same deal is fatigue, not urgency. Space it out or vary the actual offer.
- 3. Replace the vague VIP teaser with a real, quantified benefit now, not a "deets soon" placeholder.
