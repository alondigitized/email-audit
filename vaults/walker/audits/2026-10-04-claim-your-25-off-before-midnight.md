# Claim your 25% off before midnight...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count of these since "Happy VIP Days" on 10/01. It's the same giant white "25% OFF*" on purple I saw in "Last day! VIPs get 25% off!" this morning, and in "[Last Chance] Join VIP" earlier today. This one says "before midnight," so it's the third "it's ending" email in a day. The deadline is real, but I've heard it so often it doesn't move me.
- The grid under the banner is the real problem for me. It's nine women's boots, booties and a sandal. The size picker only runs 5 to 11 in half sizes, which is women's sizing. The only thing that looks like my kind of shoe is the Dr. Scholl's platform sneaker, and that's a women's shoe too. "Shop Men" is a small underlined link in the banner, and it's easy to miss.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read. If I wanted a walking shoe, I'd go straight to Hoka or New Balance.

## What's working

- The giant "25%" is impossible to miss. Even on my phone I know the offer in a second.
- The code VIPFUN and "regular-price styles only" are right in the banner, so I don't have to dig for the main rule.
- The prices show the old price crossed out and the new price next to it, so I can do the math fast.
- The "Shop by Size" and "Shop Wide Width" buttons are big, black, and easy to tap. Wide width matters to my feet.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and "In-store pickup" are clearly laid out, and I like knowing I can try shoes on in the store.

## What's weak

- Everything shown is women's boots. There's nothing for a 62-year-old man who wants cushioned, slip-on, arch-supported shoes.
- The fine print says Skechers, Clarks and other brands are excluded. Those are the brands I'd shop here for. I don't like finding that out at the bottom, after the asterisk.
- "It only happens twice a year…" doesn't ring true. I've had a version of this offer all week.
- The hero button says "Become a VIP," not "shop." Then there are four more links and a "Shop Now" button below the grid. That's too many choices.
- The legal text at the bottom is tiny. I'd have to pinch and zoom to read it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Show men's comfort and walking shoes to men: cushioned sneakers, slip-ons, and wide widths. Leave the boot grid for the women on the list.
- 2. Put the exclusions up front. Say "25% off, excludes Skechers & Clarks" in or right under the banner. That's more honest, and it spares people a wasted click.
- 3. Send one deadline email, not three. Pick the final-day send and skip the rest.
- 4. Add a comfort row, like "Cushioned & arch-supported picks," with fit details or ratings.
- 5. Make the hero button a single clear "Shop Men's" or "Shop Women's" instead of "Become a VIP." Show the join message only to people who aren't members.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 25% off, VIPs only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's comfort sneakers: 25% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code VIPFUN works through midnight. Walking shoes, slip-ons & wide widths included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Regular-price styles only. Check which brands qualify before you shop.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: DSW VIP Days, "Claim your 25% off before midnight..."

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken CTA, the SMS opt-in link, which returned HTTP 400 on its Braze/SendGrid tracking redirect. It also has a few minor source-level accessibility gaps. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and header checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA:** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400** on `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/rDEY-K9kaZZpDcrf27yWVA~~/AAQRxRA~/ODwZnpSgVXiG…`. The response status text was empty.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking redirect usually means the token is malformed or truncated, or the redirect rejected the probe. The probe may have been blocked as a bot rather than the link being dead.
  - Re-test with a browser user agent before treating it as definitely broken for subscribers.
  - If it is broken, this is a revenue-relevant CTA (SMS acquisition) and the highest-priority fix.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 72 links were probed and 60 were skipped (ranked by click likelihood). The other 60 are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Tracking domains:** all links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` (first-party branded click tracking) and Movable Ink (`mi.dsw.com`). No mixed or third-party tracking domains were observed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on two images.** Both are tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""`:
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/5P-b_eeLVtMjU0LtLp-juw~~/AAQRxRA~/XXI4k_1Iy…` (open pixel)
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=…` (Movable Ink pixel)
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither takes effect. Charset then relies on the HTTP `Content-Type` header, which was not provided.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Fonts:** custom `@font-face` fonts (Futura PT, Helvetica Now) are loaded from `braze-images.com`. Clients that block webfonts will fall back, but fallback stacks were not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `%%` were observed, but the source was truncated, so the body and footer are unverified. The Movable Ink pixel carries a numeric subscriber identifier (`mi_u=225090073`) in a URL query string. That is normal for Movable Ink, but it is an identifier embedded in a loadable URL.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part:** [WARN] 27,144 of 31,560 characters (about 86%) are URLs. That is a spam-filter risk. It is likely driven by long `ablink` tracking URLs, and the plain-text version should be trimmed or simplified.
- **Unsubscribe, physical address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe` headers:** not verifiable. The footer was truncated and no headers were supplied. The QA run always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it.
- **Sender:** `dsw@e.dsw.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** not verifiable. Destination URLs are hidden behind `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed from the source.
- **Campaign identifier:** the Movable Ink parameter `mi_ecmp=20261004_P261342_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_D` encodes today's send date. That matches the "before midnight" timing, so no stale-content issue is visible.
- **Landing pages:** the one probed CTA failed (400), so continuity can't be confirmed for it. The other probed links did not fail.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link.** Re-probe with a browser user agent. If it still returns 400, regenerate the tracked link in the ESP.
2. **Trim the plain-text part** by shortening or removing tracking URLs to get under the URL-heavy threshold.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
5. **Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"`** (and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` for the `IE=Edge` tag) to the meta tags.
6. **Add `alt=""`** to both tracking pixels.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML with raw headers.** That would let unsubscribe, `List-Unsubscribe`, authentication results, UTMs, and merge tokens be checked.
