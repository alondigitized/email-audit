# Last Call for Workday Comfort Savings!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Same shoe, same 25% off, same "Crocs at Work" pitch I already saw three days ago on the 12th — just with "last call" slapped on top and a deadline tonight. It's a plain light-blue clog on a plain light-blue background, no cartoon charms, no Yu-Gi-Oh nonsense, nothing aimed at my granddaughter — so credit where it's due, this is the one style of Crocs email that's actually in my lane. But I've already seen this pitch, and seeing it again three days later just makes it feel like a nag rather than a real deal.
- The bigger problem is I scrolled the whole thing and never found a button to press. There's a headline, a sentence of copy, a picture of the shoe, and then a bunch of app-store badges and social icons — no "Shop Now," nothing that says where I'd even click to get the 25% off.
- I wouldn't click this one, mostly because there's nothing to click. I'd maybe remember "oh right, Crocs has a work-shoe deal" and go type crocs.com into my browser myself later — but that's me doing the work the email should've done. Not forwarding it to anyone.

## What's working

- The clog itself is a plain, work-appropriate style — no gimmicks, actually something I or my son-in-law would wear on shift.
- 25% off is a real, specific number, not some vague "save big" line.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — nothing overlapping, nothing broken, easy to read without squinting.
- "Last call" and a hard deadline (tonight, 11:59pm) gives me an actual reason to act today instead of putting it off.

## What's weak

- I can't find a single button or link to actually shop the sale — no "Shop Now," no "Get 25% Off," nothing. That's the whole point of the email and it's missing.
- This is a rerun of the September 12th "Crocs at Work: 25% Off Select Styles!" email — same product shot, same discount, same message, just three days later with urgency bolted on. Feels like they're recycling the same send rather than actually escalating.
- "Exclusions Apply" sitting right there with no explanation of what's excluded — makes me wonder if my size or the style I'd actually want isn't even included.
- Huge stretch of blank space between the product photo and the app-store badges — the email feels unfinished, like content is missing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an actual "Shop the Sale" button under the product photo — right now there's a 25%-off headline and a picture and nowhere to click, which kills the whole purpose of a "last call" email.
- 2. Don't just resend the same creative from three days ago — swap in a different work-clog colorway or add a second product (a slide, a bootie) so it doesn't read as a repeat.
- 3. Spell out what "Exclusions Apply" actually means, even briefly ("excludes clearance styles") so I don't feel like I'm being set up for a bait-and-switch at checkout.
- 4. Trim the dead space before the app/social footer so the email doesn't feel like it trails off.
