# Clearance savings await >>>
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- "Brands 101," new UGGs "for him, for her, for them," Birkenstock "first-day faves," a Jansport backpack ad — this is a back-to-school email, and I don't have a kid heading back to campus, so about two-thirds of this scroll just isn't for me. It's a long email too, all that college-lifestyle imagery before I even hit an offer.
- That said, once I got down to the "Up to 60% off Clearance" block, it's clean — real headline, a size grid I can actually tap (I go 10.5, close enough between the 10 and 11 shown), and the always-30%-off-in-stores line is useful since I like trying shoes on before I buy. Compare that to the DSW clearance email from earlier today with the same "up to 60%" headline — this one's basically the same offer wrapped in a heavier back-to-school theme.
- I'd scroll past the UGG/Birkenstock/backpack stuff without a second look, but I'd probably still tap into the clearance section since I keep an eye on that. Not forwarding this one — nothing here is for a friend of mine, we're all past backpack season.

## What's working

- The "Up to 60% Off Clearance" headline near the bottom is clear and the biggest offer number I've seen from DSW this week.
- Tappable size buttons (5 through 11) let me jump straight to my size without hunting.
- "Clearance in stores is always 30% off" is honest, useful info for someone like me who prefers to try shoes on.
- Free shipping for VIPs and the loyalty perks bar (5% back, birthday gift, free shipping) is called out near the bottom, so I know my membership still matters.

## What's weak

- The whole top half — "Brands 101," UGG "for him/her/them," Birkenstock "first-day faves," Jansport backpacks — is straight back-to-school/campus content aimed at parents and students, not a 62-year-old comfort shopper.
- The models in the UGG and Birkenstock shots are all clearly young — college-age or younger — nothing there signals "this brand knows who I am."
- The real offer (60% off clearance) is buried after a very long scroll of lifestyle imagery; on my phone that's a lot of thumb-swiping before I get to something I'd act on.
- No mention of comfort, arch support, cushioning, or slip-on styles anywhere — not even in the clearance section — despite that being the whole reason I open a shoe email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the clearance offer, not the back-to-school theme — put "Up to 60% Off Clearance" and the size grid right after the header, then let campus content follow for people who want it. That's the single biggest fix for someone like me.
- 2. Cut the length in half for mobile — three big lifestyle blocks (UGG, Birkenstock, backpacks) before the offer is too much scrolling on a phone.
- 3. Add a men's comfort/walking callout — a "cushioned & comfortable" or "all-day support" tile would actually catch my eye instead of another dorm-room shoe pairing.
- 4. Segment this — if DSW knows I'm not buying backpacks or kids' sneakers, skip the campus theme for me entirely and just send the clearance deal.
