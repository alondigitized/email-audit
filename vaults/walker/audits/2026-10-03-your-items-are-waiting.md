# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- The pink flower sneaker is back. It's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom," the women's shoe that has followed me since 9/17, and it's the whole hero again. I'm a 62-year-old man and I never put that in a cart. This is the same cart nag as "Your cart expires soon!" and "Did you forget something?" earlier this week, and the third one today under a different subject line. "We noticed you noticing us..." is a little creepy to me. It's not charming.
- The useful stuff is further down. There's a row of six shoes, and one of them is a Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman in a tan slip-on. That's my kind of shoe. There's also the Skechers Plus Members BOGO 50% off banner, but it's buried under the cart nag and the grid. The grid also mixes in boys' and girls' shoes, so it doesn't look like they know who I am.
- Would I click? Probably not on the big button, because the pink shoe isn't mine. I might scroll to the Altman slip-on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the plain BOGO email.

## What's working

- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman is in the grid. It's hands-free and comfortable, and it's what I'd actually wear.
- The BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair banner is clear, with a "Thru 10/7" deadline. It matches the "Treat yourself" send from earlier today.
- The SKECHERS logo and navy headline are familiar, so I trust who it's from.
- The Women / Men / Kids / Sale list near the bottom is big and easy to tap with my thumb.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's pink floral sneaker I never touched. It's the fifth or sixth time I've seen it since 9/17.
- The "Complete your order" button points at a cart I don't recognize, and it has the same copy as the earlier nags.
- The "more great options" grid has a boy's shoe and a girl's shoe next to the Men's Altman. Only one of the six is for me.
- The best offer, the BOGO, is below the fold and below all that. I scrolled a long way to reach it.
- The text under each shoe is tiny. I'd have to zoom in to read the names.
- The fine print is very small, and the offer excludes sale styles and "website premieres."
- The Skechers Plus banner doesn't greet me by name, and it doesn't show my member points like the "Hi, Walker" emails did.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't put a shoe in the hero that the customer never added. Show something from the real browsing or cart history, or lead with the member BOGO.
- 2. Move the BOGO banner above the product grid, and say "You're a Plus member" up front.
- 3. Filter the grid by gender and category. For me that would be Men's Slip-ins and walking shoes, not kids' shoes.
- 4. Enlarge the shoe names and prices.
- 5. Cap the cart nags at one a day. I've had four or five in three days.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a 75% QA pass rate with two hard failures. The tracked Unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400, and the message has no plain-text part (0 chars). There are also 13 images on `http://` and several accessibility gaps.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400 (needs verification).** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r…`. The probe is automated, and Attentive's endpoint may reject non-browser requests or expect a POST or one-click flow. I can't tell from this data whether real recipients hit the same 400, so test it manually. If it does fail for them, it's a CAN-SPAM problem (see §5).
- **The two 429 responses are not defects.** "Complete you Order" and "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting..." both returned 429 from the `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirects. This is probe rate-limiting, and QA marks the links as possibly valid.
- **Redirect wrapping:** All probed links go through opaque Attentive click-tracking redirects (`/e/c/<token>`). The final destinations weren't captured, so I can't confirm where they land.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 37 links were probed. The 25 skipped footer, social and utility links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images use `http://`.** All come from `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`. They are the logo, "Complete you Order", "Here's some more great options…", a6ec5198, "Text", App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook and Youtube. Clients can block or warn on mixed content. The same host already serves `https://` for `m/21/021bd784-…gif`, so HTTPS is available.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif`
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` (likely a content image)
  - the open-tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`. For the pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Typo in alt text and link text:** "Complete you Order" is missing the "r" in "your".
- **Charset meta is malformed:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it doesn't declare the encoding.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe:** A link with the text "Unsubscribe" exists, but it returned 400 in QA (see §2). If real recipients get the same error, that breaks the working-opt-out requirement.
- **Plain-text fallback is missing (0 chars).** A multipart/alternative message without a text part can raise spam-filter scores. This may partly reflect how the message was captured, so check the raw MIME.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, a `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header, and the physical postal address in the footer. The headers weren't supplied and the HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is an Attentive redirect, and QA didn't record final URLs or query strings. The visible `attn_*` parameters belong to the unsubscribe link only. Resolve the redirect chains for the "Complete you Order" and cart links to confirm that UTM parameters survive and that the cart restores correctly.

1. Manually test the unsubscribe URL in a browser, and also by POST for one-click behavior. Fix it if it fails.
