- Shoe Carnival again. I've lost count since the 13th. This one is "Ends today: cozy sale," but the cozy sale is women's and kids' boots, flats and slippers. - The Shoe Carnival logo and the red and gold look match what I've seen all week, so I trust it's the real store.
- The bottom nav has a MENS button, so at least men's shoes aren't hidden.

## What's weak

- Every hero is women's or kids': tall boots, cozy styles, flats. I see no men's product anywhere.
- The coupon is the same $10 / $25 code from earlier in the week, so "Ends today" feels manufactured.
- I see no comfort or brand signals like Skechers, Hoka, Brooks or New Balance, and no cushioning or arch-support language.
- The "Select styles" fine print on every price, plus the long disclaimer at the bottom, makes me doubt how good the deals are.
- It's long, and the Word Stride, SMS and Afterpay tiles near the bottom are clutter.
- The coupon requires $74.98 or $149.98 minimums, so it only helps if I'm already spending a lot.
