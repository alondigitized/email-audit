- Men's and women's versions sit side by side. I can see the men's black Arahi without hunting for it.
- The black "Shop Arahi 9" and "Shop Gaviota 6" buttons are wide and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The look matches the HOKA site and the earlier emails, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- There's no price anywhere. I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- The email never tells me which shoe fits which walker. "Max foam cushioning" is on the Gaviota only, but nothing connects that to my daily walks.
- The exploded-shoe drawing near the bottom is cute, but it's tiny and takes up space without telling me anything. The gait and run-after-run copy talks to runners, not walkers.
- The footer is crowded: "Unlock Free Shipping," "FLY for 30 Guarantee," and the legal text in small blue and white. The free shipping offer is buried there.
- The lavender shoes lead the hero, and the men's black Arahi only shows up small, down in the thumbnails.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's destination URLs and UTM tagging decode cleanly and consistently. The main confirmed defects are the missing plain-text part (0 chars), 13 images with no alt text (most of them inside links), and viewport and meta markup problems. The 406/403 link failures are probably an artifact of the probe, not a real outage (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures are unverified.** All 12 probed `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/...` redirect links returned 406, and the unsubscribe link returned 403. Every one is a Cordial click-tracking redirect. A uniform 406/403 across every link, including the unsubscribe link, points to WAF or bot filtering of the probe's user agent. It does not look like 13 independently broken links. I decoded the `jwtP` payloads, and each `linkUrl` is a well-formed `https://www.hoka.com/...` URL, or `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...` for the unsubscribe link. The 26% pass rate is therefore mostly a probe artifact. I did not confirm real-client behavior.
- **Indistinguishable links.** The links for `US_20261018_Comparison_a.jpg` and `_b.jpg` both resolve to `/en/us/stability-shoes/`. `_c.jpg` and `_d.jpg` both resolve to `/en/us/arahi/`. All decoded links share one identical `utm_campaign` and no `utm_content` or `utm_term`, so clicks on duplicate-destination links can't be told apart in analytics.
- **Hashed email in URLs.** Every decoded destination also carries `hmail=9153f33f...` and `cordial_id=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9`. `hmail` looks like a 64-character hex hash, probably of the recipient's email, and it is passed to hoka.com and into the `options.hoka.com` unsubscribe URL. This is a privacy and referrer-leak consideration, not a functional bug.
- **Two open-tracking pixels from different hosts.** One is `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and the other is `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`. Both lack alt text. This is a duplicated open signal, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 13 images.** The 13 images are `US_20261018_Comparison_a` through `_g`, the four product images (`US_Arahi9_TopLeft_1176570-PGM.jpg`, `US_Arahi9_TopRight_1176531-FLCK.jpg`, `US_BottomLeft_Gaviota6_1171933-SLWL.jpg`, `US_Gaviota6_BottomRight_1171932-SDSTB.jpg`) and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. The QA output reports the two tracking pixels without alt as well. The QA text shows image filenames as the link text for 8 of the 12 probed material links. A screen reader or an images-off client therefore gets no usable link label. The 1200×2218 and 1200×1758 images (`Comparison_c`, `_e`, `_g`) are tall enough that they probably carry copy. If so, that copy is unavailable without images.
- **Zoom disabled.** The head has two `viewport` metas, both with `maximum-scale=1`. The first has no `user-scalable`, and the second sets `user-scalable=yes` but still caps scale at 1. The duplication is redundant and conflicting, and `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing. The charset has to come from the HTTP or MIME `Content-Type` header.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid CSS declarations.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values, so clients drop them.
- **Focus outlines removed.** The global `* {outline:none; ...}` rule removes focus outlines in clients that support keyboard navigation.
- **Web fonts.** `TheFutureHOKA` is loaded from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...`, with no confirmed fallback stack in the visible (truncated) source. I couldn't confirm the fallback stack because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The source was truncated, so this is limited to what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This is a multipart/alternative gap that hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **Unsubscribe link is present.** It appears as "Unsubscribe" and routes through the tracked link to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...`. The 403 is from the same probe filtering as the other links, so working status is unconfirmed. A manual or browser-UA check is needed.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC unverified.** QA reports no `Authentication-Results` header, because the message came through the AgentMail relay. This is a tooling limitation, not a finding against the sender.
- **Not evaluable.** The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't included in the data. The physical postal address wasn't visible in the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM structure is consistent:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261018_Comparison_ArahivsGaviota_STO_A90Day_270StabilityTag_720StabilityPurchasers_270WalkingTag_270ShoeFinderTag_0315ComparisonOpeners` on every decoded link.
- **The campaign value encodes the audience segments.** It is long and mixes the campaign name with segment tags, which makes it hard to read in reports. This is a hygiene point, not a break.
- **Destinations match the subject.** They go to `/stability-shoes/`, `/arahi/`, `/gaviota/`, and four product pages with `dwvar_<style>_color=` parameters (1176570/PGM, 1176531/FLCK, 1171933/SLWL, 1171932/SDSTB). The women's and men's product paths are consistent with the images. I did not load the landing pages, so I can't confirm the product pages are live or the colors are in stock.
- **No `utm_content`** (see section 2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text MIME part. This is the highest-confidence fix.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 13 images. Use empty `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` on the tracking pixels. Make the alt for image-only links describe the destination, for example "Compare Arahi 9 vs Gaviota 6 – stability shoes".
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, delete the duplicate viewport meta, change the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, fill in `<title>`, and clean up the empty-value CSS rules.
4. Add a `utm_content` per link, for example `hero_a` and `hero_b`, so identical destinations can be told apart.
5. Re-run link probing with a browser-like user agent, or validate the decoded `linkUrl` targets directly. That will separate real failures from bot filtering. Manually confirm the unsubscribe flow and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers.
6. Review whether `hmail` should be passed in plain URL parameters to the site and to the unsubscribe URL.
7. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw message headers in the receiving mailbox, since the relay hid them from this check.
