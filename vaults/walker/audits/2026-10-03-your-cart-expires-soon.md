# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- The pink flower sneaker is back. It's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom" again, the women's shoe that has followed me since 9/17. I'm a 62-year-old man and I never put it in a cart. That's the fifth or sixth cart nag in a few days, after "Did you forget something?" and "Your Items Are Waiting!" on the 2nd. The "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" line is cute, but I didn't like these, so it falls flat.
- The bottom half of the email does better. There's a row of Men's Slip-ins, including the Relaxed Fit Altman and the Contour Fit leather sneaker, which is the stuff I actually shop for. Then there's a 20% off banner for members. I'd glance at that and probably leave it there. I wouldn't click the hero. I might tap a slip-in if I were already on my phone, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone.

## What's working

- The 15% guests / 20% members banner is big and easy to read, and it makes my member status worth something.
- Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit shoes show up in the best-sellers grid. That's my kind of shoe.
- "Complete Your Order" is a clear dark button with a verb and a noun, and it's easy to tap.
- The type is large and the layout is one simple column, which is good for reading on a phone.
- The Skechers logo and the navy and white look match what I see on their site, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's pink floral sneaker I've never shown any interest in. That makes me feel like a stranger, not a known customer.
- The date doesn't add up. The banner says "Thru 10/1," but the fine print says valid 9/23–9/28 and only on boots and outdoor styles. That feels like bait-and-switch, and today is 10/3.
- The best-sellers grid has two Boy's Garage Sneakers, and neither the grid nor the best-sellers button says anything about cushioning, arch support or hands-free. I can't tell comfort from the product names alone.
- Below the offers there's a long stack of Women, Men, Kids and Clothing links, then texting, app download, Afterpay and Klarna. It's a lot to scroll past, and none of it helps me decide on a shoe.
- Nothing says "cart expires" apart from the subject line. There's no timer, no date and no mention of what's in my cart.

## Recommendations

- 1. Only put the item that's really in my cart in the hero, or lead with a Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit shoe I've actually browsed. Never show a women's shoe to a man who hasn't touched one.
- 2. Make the offer and its dates match. If the deal ended 10/1, don't send it on 10/3. Show one honest deadline and say which styles qualify.
- 3. Add comfort details under the shoes: "Arch Fit," "Air-Cooled Memory Foam," "Hands-free." A price and a star rating would help too.
- 4. Cut the bottom of the email down to about two links. Space the cart reminders out so they stop piling up.
- 5. Add a real cart line, like "Your D'Lites are saved until 10/5," with the actual deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Slip-ins are still in your cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off for Members ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Arch Fit comfort, hands-free. Your size is still in stock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members take 20% off through Sunday. Finish checkout.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400 (FAIL).** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1MmZpSFY0ajRYThIDMVN5GKmS4QUgAg&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&...&attn_sid=f0247190-...`. The probe may have caused this: the link is tokenized and per-recipient, and the prober may have used a method or headers the endpoint rejects. Confirm with a real browser GET before treating it as broken.
- **Two 429s, not confirmed broken.** "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." both go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. The server says "link may be valid", so this is likely rate limiting on the Attentive redirector.
- **Opaque redirects.** The CTA, header and "web version" links all go through Attentive's `/e/c/` redirector. The final destinations can't be read from the HTML, so UTM and landing-page checks aren't possible here.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 links were probed and 25 were skipped, so footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.** 12 images load from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/...`. They are the logo, "Complete Your Order" hero, App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, "Text", and `a6ec5198-...jpg`. Some clients may block or rewrite them, and mixed-content warnings are possible.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` has no alt.
  - The open-tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` has no alt. It's a low-severity case, but `alt=""` is preferred.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is a minor issue.
- **Malformed charset meta.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing. Encoding then depends on the MIME `Content-Type` header. The preheader's non-ASCII filler characters (`͏`, soft hyphens) could garble if a client guesses the encoding.
- **Good practice.** The preheader is hidden correctly, `lang="en"` is set, the responsive `@media (max-width:620px)` rules are present, and there are `mso-` table resets for Outlook.
