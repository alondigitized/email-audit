# Time’s almost up!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again — another Crocs email. I've lost count of how many of these have hit my inbox since late May, and I almost didn't open this one. "Time's almost up!" is enough of a nudge to get me in, and to Crocs' credit, the first thing I see is a big, bold "Extra 50% Off" with "ends tonight" right underneath it. That's a clear message, and for a guy who keeps one eye on prices, 50% off sale styles is real money.
- But here's what killed me: I scrolled the whole email top to bottom and there is not a single button to click. No "Shop Now." No "See Sale Styles." Nothing. The hero image of the three clogs just sits there looking nice, and then it's straight to app store badges and Instagram icons. I'm 62 and shopping on my phone — I need a big green button, not a puzzle. I ended up not knowing where to tap.
- I'd read it, I'd feel the pull of the deal, and then I'd set my phone down confused. Mark as read, move on. I'm not forwarding this to anyone either — there's nothing to forward them to.

## What's working

- **The offer is big and immediate.** "Extra 50% Off" in a large, bold font is the first thing my eye lands on — no hunting required.
- **The urgency is credible.** "Ends tonight. Available in-store and online." tells me exactly what I need to know. No vague "while supplies last" nonsense as the only timer.
- **The clogs look good.** Three Baya styles in neutral colors (bone, olive, light gray) — these are practical, recognizable shoes. No charm towers, no neon fashion looks.
- **Clean render, readable text.** Nothing broken, no overlapping copy, text is big enough that I'm not squinting.
- **"Exclusions Apply" is right there up top.** I appreciate when they don't bury that in the footer fine print.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button anywhere in the email.** This is the single biggest miss. I have a deal, I'm interested, and there is nowhere to tap. That's broken, full stop.
- **The preview text is garbage.** My inbox showed me a string of nothing — invisible characters. A brand this big should know how to write a preheader. That blank preview cost them the open-consideration moment.
- **Subject gives me zero info about the offer.** "Time's almost up!" could be my car insurance renewal or a pizza deal. At least say what's ending.
- **Still no comfort signals.** Not one word about cushioning, arch support, or fit. For someone like me who picks a shoe based on what it does for my feet, this is all surface.
- **Another Crocs email.** I audited one on June 4th and one on May 31st — before that it was a wall of them all through late May. The cadence has worn my patience down to nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a CTA button.** This is not optional. One big green button, "Shop 50% Off Styles," directly below the offer copy. Without it the email has no exit ramp.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject to include the offer.** Give me the deal in the subject line so I know what I'm opening.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off ends tonight — shop sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off Crocs sale: ends at midnight`
- 3. **Write a real preheader.** The invisible-character trick to suppress the preheader is a wasted slot.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Final hours — extra 50% off sale styles, in-store and online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends tonight: your extra 50% off is about to expire.`
- 4. **Name the product family, not just "sale styles."** Even one line like "Including Baya Clogs and Classic Slides" gives me something concrete to shop toward.
- 5. **Consider pulling back the email cadence.** I've gotten a Crocs promo nearly every other day for three weeks. At this point I'm tuning them out before I even check the offer.

**Missing alt on tracking pixel**

```html
<img style="visibility:hidden;display:none" height="1" width="1"
  src="https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?...">
```

`alt=""` is absent. Screen readers and strict validators will flag this. Add `alt=""` (empty string, not omitted) to mark it as decorative.

**Malformed charset meta tag**

```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```

Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Without it, some legacy parsers (Outlook 2016 via Word renderer) may not honour the charset declaration. Correct form:

```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```

**Custom font fallback** — `TT Crocs` is loaded via `@font-face` from `media.crocs.com`. Gmail strips `<style>` blocks in some contexts; ensure all text has an acceptable web-safe fallback stack declared inline or in the CSS rule.

### 5. Compliance

```

```

Expected check: `dig TXT crocs-email.com` for SPF; DKIM `d=crocs-email.com` in raw headers.
