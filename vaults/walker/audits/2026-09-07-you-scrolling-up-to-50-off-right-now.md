# You scrolling? Up to 50% off right now!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a plain one — two stacked discount offers and a light blue clog, no cartoon charms, no college logos, no TikTok bracelet shots. That's the good news. The bad news is there's nothing here for me at all — it's the same classic clog Crocs sends me every few days, just with the numbers rearranged. "Buy 2, Take 30% Off" then "Up to 50% Off" — I can't even tell if those stack or which one actually applies to what I'd buy.
- The subject line "You scrolling?" is a strange thing to ask a 62-year-old — I'm not scrolling, I'm reading email at the kitchen table. That's a young person's line, not mine.
- I'd give this one a glance and move on. Not forwarding it, not clicking it — just marking it read and going back to what I was doing.

## What's working

- No cartoon charms or licensed logos this time — just a plain clog, which is at least honest merchandise.
- Two numbers are visible right away — 30% and 50% off — so there's a real offer, not vague "sale" language.
- Layout is clean and uncluttered, easy enough to read without zooming.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers stacked on top of each other with no clarity on which applies where — that's confusing, not enticing.
- "Exclusions Apply" twice with zero specifics on what's excluded — feels like a catch waiting to happen.
- The subject "You scrolling?" doesn't sound like it's talking to me at all.
- It's a clog again — still nothing here that reads as a men's walking or comfort-support shoe. This is the fourth or fifth clog pitch this week; I noted the same thing on 2026-09-04's clearance clog email.
- No price shown, no CTA button visible in the image — I don't even see anything to tap.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE offer and lead with it clearly — two vague percentage-off banners back to back just create doubt, not urgency.
- 2. Add a visible "Shop Now" button under the hero image — right now there's nothing obvious to tap.
- 3. Show an actual dollar price next to the percentage off — I think in dollars, not percentages.
- 4. Send me something that isn't a clog for once — a real walking shoe with arch support would actually get a click.
