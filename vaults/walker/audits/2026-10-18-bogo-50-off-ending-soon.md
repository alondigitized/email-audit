# BOGO 50% OFF! ENDING SOON!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. That's the eighth BOGO email in about five days, and I got "BOGO 50% off ends *TODAY*" earlier today. This one has the same green banner I've been looking at all week. The countdown clock reads 12:43:50, so at least the deadline is believable. But I'm tired of being told it's ending soon. If it ends every day, it isn't ending.
- The all-caps subject with the exclamation points reads like a shout, not a note from a store I know. The body is mostly Christmas gift-guide stuff: UGG boots, a Timberland boot, a kid in Nikes, and a toy-box-style shoe pile. Nothing here speaks to a guy who wants a slip-on with cushion and arch support. There's a "Shop by size" block with Wide Width, which is something I do care about. It's buried near the bottom, though.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I did tap in, it would be the Shop Men link up top, and only because the 50% off second pair is real money.

## What's working

- The green banner with "BOGO 50% OFF*" in big white type is easy to read on my phone. I knew the offer in a second.
- The countdown timer feels credible and tells me exactly how long I have.
- The Shop Women / Men / Kids links sit right under the offer, so I can get to Men without hunting.
- "Shop Wide Width" and the size buttons are useful to a guy with wider feet.
- The Timberland boot is a big, clear hero image with a plain "Shop Timberland" link.

## What's weak

- The subject line is all caps with an exclamation point. It looks like spam, and it's the same urgency I've gotten all week.
- The email is built around gift-giving: UGG, a kid in Nikes, a woman hugging a gift box. None of that looks like a 62-year-old man.
- I don't see any comfort or walking shoes, and no cushioning, arch support or slip-on messaging. Skechers, Hoka, Brooks and New Balance aren't in the picture.
- The links under the images ("SHOP UGG," "SHOP NIKE") are small, thin text. They're hard to tap with a thumb.
- The BOGO fine print is a wall of tiny type at the bottom, and it's hard to read without zooming. The asterisk on the offer makes me want to read it, and I can't.
- The shoe pile in the middle is cluttered. No single shoe stands out, and I can't tell what to shop for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send fewer of these. Eight BOGO emails in five days is too many. One clear email with the countdown would do more than eight near-identical ones.
- 2. Lead the men's section with comfort. Show a walking shoe or a slip-on with the price after the BOGO discount, like "Two pairs for about $X each."
- 3. Make the "Shop Men" link a real button the size of "Shop Now," not a small underlined link.
- 4. Pull the Wide Width link up higher and give it a button. It's the most useful part of this email for me.
- 5. Put a short, plain-language version of the BOGO terms right under the banner. Mention the exclusions in a sentence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off ends tonight at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort shoes: buy one, get one half off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walking shoes, slip-ons and wide widths. Second pair half off through tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken CTA: the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" tracked link returns HTTP 400. It also has accessibility gaps (missing alt text, zoom-blocking viewport) and an unverifiable authentication posture. The HTML source was truncated and only 12 of 65 links were probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL, HTTP 400):** the link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZVHmY7W1Xf7Ww2XgQQTlvw~~/AAQRxRA~/KfOCtjoA…XdeYQ~~`.
  - The wrapper is about 700 characters long and the response carried no status text.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking redirector usually means a malformed or expired tracking token. It can also be a bot-rejection of the probe's user agent.
  - Re-test with a real browser UA before treating it as a send-side defect. If it reproduces, the SMS opt-in, a high-value acquisition CTA, is dead for every recipient.
- **Link coverage:** 53 of 65 links were skipped, so the 12 probed links are the only ones with status evidence.
- **Two tracking domains:** the email uses `ablink.dsw.com` for click wrapping and `mi.dsw.com` for the open pixel (`/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif`). This is not a defect, but it means two systems have to be healthy for attribution to work.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, linked image:** the image with `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/rs9noejBMjdBcdQ9pXu8EQ~~/AAQRxRA~/Oe8--C70…` has no `alt`. If it sits inside an `<a>`, the link has no accessible name.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `o.gif` has no `alt`. It should be `alt=""` (with `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip it.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on clients that honor it.
- **Meta tags without `http-equiv`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so as written they do nothing. The charset normally comes from the MIME header, so the impact is low, and this could be an artifact of the capture.
- **Dark mode and fonts:** `color-scheme`, `supported-color-schemes`, `prefers-color-scheme` rules and `@font-face` web fonts (braze-images.com) are present. No structural problems were visible in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. Only the first part of the source was available, so tokens later in the body are unchecked.
- **Recipient ID in the pixel URL:** `mi_u=225090073` is a recipient identifier carried in a URL. It is expected for this kind of pixel, but note it if the URLs are logged or shared.
- **Possible empty value in the campaign name (unconfirmed):** `mi_ecmp=20261018_P261260_V1_W-_Offer_Occas…` contains `W-_`, which could be an empty segment value. Confirm with the sender; this is not proven.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Authentication unknown (WARN):** no `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status cannot be confirmed from this capture. This is a limitation of the AgentMail relay, not evidence of a sender failure.
- **Sender and tracking domain:** the From domain is `e.dsw.com`, and tracking runs on `ablink.dsw.com` and `mi.dsw.com`, all under `dsw.com`.
- **Unsubscribe not verifiable:** the QA tool states an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none is reported as failing. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address were not in the data, so they are not assessed.
- **Plain-text part (WARN):** 21,417 of 26,033 characters (about 82%) are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and raises spam-filter risk. The cause is the long `ablink` wrappers repeated in the text version.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters not verifiable:** every link is wrapped in an `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, so the final destinations and any UTM parameters aren't visible in the source.
- **Landing page alignment not verifiable:** no landing-page data was captured for the BOGO 50% offer.
- **Campaign identifier:** the only visible one is `mi_ecmp=20261018_P261260_V1…`, which carries the date and version.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or replace the SMS opt-in link.** Re-test `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZVHmY7W1…` with a browser UA. If it still returns 400, regenerate the tracked link or point it directly at the opt-in page.
2. **Add `alt` text.** Give the linked `ablink.dsw.com/q/rs9noejB…` image a descriptive `alt`, and set `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Shorten the plain-text part.** Use shorter link wrappers or fewer links so URLs are well under 70% of the text part.
5. **Fix the meta tags.** Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags.
6. **Improve QA coverage.** Capture the full HTML and the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`). Probe the remaining 53 links and resolve the redirect chains to check UTMs and landing pages.
