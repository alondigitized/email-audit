# Go Bold with These All-New Shades
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- Well, Crocs again, and this one is a change from the Buy 2/3/4 ladders I got on 10/18 and 10/19. The offer is simple: "$30 Off $100" in big white type on blue, and it's marked Crocs Club member exclusive. I read it in two seconds. Then the hero is a teal clog with little pearl charms, "new hues that go deep." That's a color story, and I don't buy shoes for color.
- I'd say this is a better offer than the NBA clog wall on 10/20. It's still a clog email, with nothing about arch support, cushioning or walking. The fine print says the offer ends tonight, 11:59pm MT, but the body never says "ends tonight." I'd only know if I scrolled to the bottom. I'd glance at it, maybe tap "Shop and Save" if I needed a pair, and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "$30 Off $100" headline is big and easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- The Crocs Club member label tells me they know I'm on the list.
- The black "Shop Classic Clog" button is large and has a clear verb and noun.
- The layout is clean, with one hero and one product. After the NBA wall, that's a relief.
- The four category buttons (Clogs, Jibbitz Charms, Sale, Sandals) are big pill shapes that are easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The deadline is buried in the fine print. "Ends tonight" would have been a real reason to act, and I only found it at the bottom.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the offer, which makes me wonder what's left out.
- The hero is all about a teal color and charms. Nothing says comfort, cushion or support, which is what I shop for.
- $30 off $100 means spending $100, and one clog is about $50 to $60. That pushes me toward buying two pairs, and I'd rather buy the one pair that fits.
- The models are missing and so is any review or social proof, so nothing in it says "this is for a 62-year-old guy."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the headline area: "Ends tonight." It's the one real urgency cue and right now it's hidden.
- 2. Tie the hero to comfort. Add a line like "All-day cushioning" or "Easy slip-on," and show a man's shoe or a walking use.
- 3. Show a star rating or a review quote under the Classic Clog.
- 4. Show one or two popular price points so I can work out the $100 math.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save $30 on $100 today only. New Deep Dive Classic Clog is in.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The source is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, link and UTM checks can't be confirmed. QA reports 86% pass, 0 issues and 1 warning. The one warning is an `<img>` without alt text on a tracking pixel.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **19 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.crocs-email.com`), so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10203&bmt=0`. It is 1x1, has `alt=""`, and sits in a `height:1px;overflow:hidden` div. No issue.
- **Third-party Adobe Audience Manager (Demdex) pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…`
  - It has no alt attribute, which is the QA warning.
  - The `d_dpuid` parameter is a per-recipient identifier. It was present in the captured copy, so it may be a literal value and not a merge token.
  - Fix: add `alt=""`, and `width="1" height="1"` if they are absent.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags (confirmed in source):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it declares nothing.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it is also inert.
  - Charset then depends on the HTTP/MIME header, which is usually fine for ESP sends but still invalid markup.
- **Missing alt:** the Demdex pixel (see above).
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>`. This is low impact, since email clients rarely show it.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` for "TT Crocs" (400/700/900) loads from `media.crocs.com`. It has `font-display: swap`. The fallback stack can't be verified because the source is truncated.
- **Clean on this list:** the Gmail hack (`u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`), `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and the `mobileHide`/`mobileView` utilities (incl. `mso-hide`) are all in place.
- **Not verifiable:** body content, alt text on product images, and Outlook/MSO conditional comments are all past the truncation point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated.
