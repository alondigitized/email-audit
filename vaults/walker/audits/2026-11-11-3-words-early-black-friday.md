# 3 words: Early. Black. Friday.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. The subject says "Early. Black. Friday." but the hero is really a Veterans Day offer. That's $15 off $49, $20 off $99 and $60 off $199, in stores and online with code VETERANS. I'm not a veteran, so the top of the email, with its ticking countdown clock, felt like it was meant for somebody else. Past that, the layout is a long scroll of women's heels, boots and flats. That's the "Weekly Holiday Recap" from yesterday and the day before, with one change: it now has an offer I can't use.
- The one thing I'd actually look for is the men's section. There's a "Shop Men" link in the hero and a nav bar, but I don't see a single men's shoe in the body. I got TINSEL 20% off for a week, then UGG 30% three times, then the mystery box this morning. Now it's a veterans promo. DSW is trying everything, and nothing here is cushioned, arch-supported or a slip-on for a guy my age.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, not even one who's a veteran, because the real news is buried in tiny type.

## What's working

- The $15 / $20 / $60 tiers are big, bold and easy to read on my phone.
- The red countdown banner at the top makes it clear the deal is short-lived.
- The "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons near the bottom show some thought about fit.
- The "Shop Under $50" block with a pair of sneakers is the closest thing to my price-vs-quality way of shopping.
- The sale blocks are cleanly separated, with black buttons that are easy to tap.

## What's weak

- "Veterans Day Only" and "VETERANS" on a promo code makes me wonder if the deal is for everybody. It's confusing for non-veterans, and the eligibility is buried in fine print.
- The body is almost all women's heels, flats and boots. There are no men's walking shoes, no comfort messaging and nothing about arch support or slip-ons.
- The recap blocks have no prices, no brand names and no reasons to buy. "You'll Fa La La Love These" doesn't tell me anything.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. I'd have to zoom to read the exclusions, and that's the part I'd most want to read.
- This is at least the eighth DSW email since the 5th, and the creative keeps repeating with different offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero clear about who the offer is for. Put the broad $15 / $20 / $60 savings up front for everyone, and give the military 10% extra its own clearly marked strip.
- 2. Add a men's comfort block with a real product, such as a walking shoe or slip-on, with a price and a line about cushioning or arch support.
- 3. Put prices and brand names on the product tiles.
- 4. Make the fine print bigger and move a one-line version of the exclusions up near the buttons.
- 5. Pace the sends. Don't repeat the same recap three days in a row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built, SendGrid-tracked XHTML template with a probe-confirmed failing CTA link (the SMS opt-in). The remaining issues are a mis-declared charset, a zoom-blocking viewport, and missing `lang` and alt attributes. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (400):** the probe for "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned HTTP 400. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/lmSW-9syq3YDQGHd0qyBSA~~/AAQRxRA~/33haFV0A2gDIHXUzpSyptIm...`. This is a revenue-relevant CTA (SMS acquisition with an incentive).
  - A 400 from a click-tracking wrapper can come from a malformed or expired redirect payload, or from the tracker rejecting automated requests. The QA data can't tell which, so re-test it from a real client.
- **Two tracking pixels:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/L6Ruiz8k...` (the `/q/` path is the open-tracking pixel)
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261111_P250961_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_C`
  - The second pixel carries a `mi_u` numeric ID in its query string, which is likely a per-recipient identifier.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 85 links were probed, so 73 are unverified. Only the one failure above is confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Charset declaration is ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. The same problem affects `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`, which has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Neither tag does what it intends. Rendering is likely fine if the MIME header sets UTF-8, but the declaration is invalid.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the pronunciation language.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):** both are the tracking pixels listed above. Add `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. This is low severity.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `DSW`. This is minor; it is not the subject line.
- **Fonts:** the custom web fonts load as `.woff` and `.ttf` from `braze-images.com`. Client support is inconsistent, but the fallback stack was not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered Liquid or `{{ }}` tokens appeared in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The `mi_u=225090073` value in the pixel URL does show that per-recipient data is being passed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping it. It is a gap in the QA data, not a confirmed sender failure.
- **Plain-text part:** 31,331 of 37,061 characters (about 85%) are URLs. This is a spam-filter risk. Long tracked URLs in the text alternative inflate this ratio.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it. I can't confirm `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers or the postal address from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Nothing could be assessed. Links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects with opaque payloads, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs are not visible. The SMS opt-in target couldn't be resolved because of the 400.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/lmSW-9syq...`) from a real client. If it still fails, regenerate it in Braze or SendGrid.
2. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
6. Consider dropping the duplicate open-tracking pixel.
7. Shorten the URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of tracked links in it.
8. Re-run the QA tool against the full HTML and raw headers to check the unsubscribe headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the UTM parameters on the 73 unprobed links.
