# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-30**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I got as far as picking out a shoe and then the trip fell apart. On my phone I never got logged in, the "Add to Cart" tap didn't add anything, and the cart page came up completely blank. The catalog itself is decent. The Men's Shoes page and the "comfort shoes" search both show real products with readable prices. But a shopper who wants a comfortable shoe and can't buy it leaves and goes to Hoka, Brooks or DSW.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10** (yesterday: 3/10, so no net change)

- **Improved:** search for "comfort shoes" gave 1,651 results, and the product page showed a clear price with the Klarna and Afterpay breakdown.
- **Regressed or still broken:** the cart badge still read **0** after "Add to Cart." The cart screenshot was a blank white page. Login never visibly happened.

I'm not scoring higher, because the one thing that earns money, checkout, didn't work.

## 3. What's Working

- **The comfort filters up top are good.** "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit" and "Max Cushioning" in the top row are exactly what I shop for. I wish they were bigger and not cut off at the edge.
- **The Men menu is easy to follow.** Big text, wide rows, arrows on the right. It has Shoes, Comfort Technologies and Shop by Activity. I could use it without squinting.
- **The product page shows its price clearly.** $100.00 is large and bold, with the monthly-payment option underneath.
- **The size buttons are large and easy to tap.** Sizes 7 through 14 are laid out in a grid.
- **Search returned relevant items.** Slip-ins Contour Fit and Relaxed Fit are comfort-labeled shoes. That's a step up from a generic result.

## 4. What's Weak

- **The cart didn't work.** The cart count stayed at 0 after Add to Cart. Then the cart page was blank white, with no message, no "your cart is empty" and no error. I'd assume the site was broken and leave.
- **The Add to Cart tap didn't do anything visible.** The size screen showed a red "Please select a size" warning right under the buttons, and no size was highlighted. I think the button needs a size picked first. If so, the page should make that obvious and scroll or nudge me to it, not leave a red line I could miss.
- **Login didn't visibly happen.** The "Log In" step looks identical to the homepage. The banner still says "Login or Join to Reveal," and the search results say "Login to Unlock Mystery Discount." The site never recognized me. This is the biggest problem for the "personalized" test.
- **Nothing felt personal.** There was no "Welcome back," no suggestions based on my past shoes, and no comfort-first content. The homepage hero is two models in Day of the Dead hoodies and sneakers, which has nothing to do with cushioning or support.
- **The first search results were women's shoes.** The top two were "Women's" styles. A man who started in Men > Shoes shouldn't be shown women's wedges.
- **The "Mystery Deal" gimmick is confusing.** It shows up in the top banner, again in the black banner, and again on every product. "Mystery discount" doesn't tell me what I'm getting, and it pushes me to sign up before I can see a price.
- **Mixed signals on price.** The product page says "Excluded from promotions" while the site shouts "Up To 50% OFF" at the top. That feels like a bait-and-switch.
- **Nothing about arch support or cushioning on the product page.** I saw a price and a skull print, with no comfort rating, no "good for walking" and no comparison with Hoka or Brooks. The "Check in-store availability" box does ask me to pick a size first, which is a small hurdle.
- **The top comfort row is cut off.** "Max Cus…" runs off the screen, and I'd have to swipe sideways to find it.
- **The first-screen filters crowd the list.** On Men's Shoes, the Filter, Sort by, Size and Width buttons take up a lot of room and the width option is clipped.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart first.** A blank page is a bug. Show a clear empty-cart or error message, and make sure an item added from a product page shows up and the badge updates.
2. **Make Add to Cart size-aware.** Highlight the size row, scroll to it, and show the error where I'm looking. Consider pre-selecting the most common size or the one I bought before.
3. **Confirm login actually works and show it.** Show my name or "Hi, Walker" in the header and use it. Stop showing "Login to Unlock" once I'm logged in.
4. **Lead with comfort for people like me.** Put Slip-ins, Arch Fit and Wide Fit in the hero, not only in a cut-off strip. Add a "Walker's picks for walking" row.
5. **Respect the department I'm in.** If I went through Men, search should rank men's shoes first or offer a Men/Women toggle.
6. **Be straightforward about discounts.** Say what the mystery deal is, or show a plain number. Don't mix "Excluded from promotions" and "Up To 50% OFF" on the same screen.
7. **Add comfort facts and value math to product pages.** Cushioning level, arch support, weight, and a short "vs. Hoka / Brooks" or price-per-wear note.
8. **Keep text and tap targets bigger.** Most text is readable, but the small blue "Size Chart," "details" and "Shop | Details" links are tiny for a 62-year-old thumb.

## 6. Bottom Line

I'd keep shopping at Skechers if I could actually buy the shoe. On this trip I couldn't. Login didn't take, nothing went into my cart, and the cart page showed nothing at all. Until that works, the comfort features are just window dressing. I'd go back to the shoe store down the street.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

- The audit data is incomplete. LCP and CLS are `?` for all 8 steps. The sampled HTML for Log In and Men category reports the URL `https://www.skechers.com/`, the same as the homepage. So either the steps didn't navigate, or they were SPA/overlay transitions that never changed the document or its URL.
- The View Cart step hit an HTTP 429 on `/cart/`. The 4 axe violations for that step (no `<title>`, no `lang`, no `<main>`, no `<h1>`) are most likely from the block or error page, not the real cart.
- Anti-bot rate limiting (Kasada, `x-kpsdk-v`) returned repeated 429s during the journey. This is probably triggered by the automated browser, so treat it as a test-environment artifact until confirmed with a real device.
- The homepage has the most substantive accessibility defects: 2 critical, 1 serious, and several landmark-structure failures.

## Accessibility

18 axe violations total, across 3 steps. After de-duplication there are 13 distinct rules.

**Homepage (10)**
- **Critical, `aria-required-parent`:** ARIA roles that need a specific parent (e.g. `listitem`, `tab`, `option`) are not inside one.
- **Critical, `button-name`:** one or more buttons have no accessible name, probably icon-only controls.
- **Serious, `link-name`:** links have no discernible text, probably image or icon links.
- **Landmark structure:**
  - `landmark-no-duplicate-main`
  - `landmark-main-is-top-level`
  - `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`
  - `landmark-unique`
  - `region`

  Together these point to more than one `<main>`, with at least one nested inside another landmark, and an `<aside>` nested in a landmark. Content such as third-party injected widgets sits outside any landmark.
- **Minor, `aria-allowed-role`:** a role is set on an element that doesn't permit it.
- **Moderate, `page-has-heading-one`:** no `<h1>`.

**View Cart (4)**
- `document-title` and `html-has-lang` are serious. `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one` are moderate.
- These are consistent with the 429 response body (see Console & Network Errors). They need re-verification against a successfully rendered cart.

**Search "comfort shoes" (4)**
- The same pattern as the homepage: `aria-allowed-role`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, `region`.
- This suggests the issues come from shared header or footer templates.

**Not evaluated:** the product detail, Add to Cart, Men category, and Men > Shoes steps have no axe results. I can't tell whether they were clean or never scanned.

## Mobile Optimization

- `--vh` is set by JavaScript (`6.64px`, so a 664 px viewport). That is a workaround for mobile browser chrome and depends on script execution.
- The viewport meta tag and touch-target sizes can't be verified from the truncated HTML or the axe output.
- The unnamed buttons and links are also unlabeled for screen-reader users on mobile (VoiceOver, TalkBack).
- The third-party tag load will hit CPU-constrained mobile devices harder than the desktop-class test environment.

## Console & Network Errors

**Network: 7 errors, all HTTP 429**
- Six hit the Kasada fingerprint endpoint (`/…/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.940`), repeated across steps.
- One hit `https://www.skechers.com/cart/`. A blocked cart navigation means the cart journey was not actually tested.
- The 429s match the console `Failed to load resource … 429` entries. Likely cause: bot-mitigation rate limiting of the automated session. Real-user impact is unconfirmed.

**Console: 27 reported, 10 shown**
- **429 resource failures** (3 shown): the Kasada calls above.
- **"Unsupported Summarizer API languages…"** (2 shown): the page or an injected script calls Chrome's built-in Summarizer API without a supported language setting. Likely a third-party or extension script.
- **"Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed"** (5 shown): scripts register `unload` handlers. Chrome is phasing these out, and they also block bfcache.
- 17 console errors were not provided, so this section is incomplete.

## Recommendations

1. **Re-run the audit with the bot challenge handled.** Use an allowlisted IP or header, or slower pacing. Confirm `/cart/` returns 200, then re-scan the cart for a, b, and c below. This is the highest priority, because the cart audit is currently invalid.
   - a. title
   - b. lang
   - c. main
2. **Fix the instrumentation.** Register `PerformanceObserver` for `largest-contentful-paint` and `layout-shift` before navigation (or with `buffered: true`). Use real navigations per step so each URL and TTFB is distinct.
3. **Homepage ARIA and names**
   - Give every icon-only `<button>` and `<a>` an `aria-label` or visible text.
   - Fix the parent/child role relationships behind `aria-required-parent`.
   - Remove or correct invalid `role` attributes.
4. **Landmarks**
   - Use exactly one top-level `<main>`.
   - Move `<aside>` elements out of nested landmarks.
   - Give repeated `nav` and `aside` landmarks unique `aria-label`s.
   - Make sure injected widgets sit inside a landmark.
5. **Add one `<h1>`** to the homepage and the search results page.
6. **Third-party tags**
   - Remove duplicate loads (`ytc.js`, `creativecdn`).
   - Move tags into a tag manager with consent and priority control.
   - Self-host or preload the web fonts.
7. **Replace `unload` handlers** with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`. Identify which third-party script registers them.
8. **Find the source of the Summarizer API call** and pass a supported `outputLanguage`, or remove it.
9. **Extend axe coverage** to the Men category, Men > Shoes, product detail, and Add to Cart steps, and capture truncated HTML including `<head>` for SEO checks.
