# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- It's the cart nag again, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. This is the same routine as the "Did you forget something?" send earlier today and the "Your Items Are Waiting!" sends on 10/21, 10/23 and 10/28. At least this one shows me a shoe I might wear. The hero is a men's-looking GO WALK Workout Walker in olive, a lot better than the pale pink women's shoe they showed me last time.
- I don't see a "Hi, Walker" or my Member points anywhere at the top. The 10/23 and 10/24 emails had those, and I liked them. Here the 20% OFF for Skechers Plus members is stuck down below the shoes, and it says "valid thru 11/2." I'd have clicked sooner if the discount were up top. The "Complete your order" button is clear, but it doesn't say what's in my cart or what it costs.
- Would I click? Maybe, because of the Workout Walker and the 20% off. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it read and wait for the next one, which will probably show up tomorrow.

## What's working

- The hero shoe is a men's slip-ins walker, so it's the first cart email that looks like it was meant for me.
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is big, dark blue and easy to tap on my phone.
- The 20% OFF banner is plain and easy to read. The end date, 11/2, is right there.
- The Men's slip-ins John Deere boots and the Contour Foam slip-in are hands-free comfort picks I care about.
- The Women, Men, Kids and Sale links are big and stacked, so I can tap them without zooming.

## What's weak

- There's no "Hi, Walker" and no Member status or points. It treats me like a stranger, and the 10/23 and 10/24 sends were better at this.
- The 20% OFF is buried below the shoe grid. The cart reminder and the discount feel like two different emails.
- The product captions are tiny, with no prices, no sizes and no mention of cushioning or arch support. I can't tell what the cart shoe costs.
- Most of the "more great options" are women's and kids' styles, with a toddler sneaker in the mix. Only two of the six are for me.
- It's another cart nag. "Let's make it official" feels a bit cute and pushy for a shoe I never picked.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "Hi, Walker, you have 1000 points" and the 20% member offer at the top, above the hero shoe.
- 2. Show the cart item with its price, size and a short line like "cushioned, slip-in, machine washable". That would help me decide.
- 3. Use men's, comfort-focused picks in the grid (walking, slip-ins, arch fit) with prices, and drop the kids' shoe for me.
- 4. Space out the cart reminders. One a day with a different pitch is too many.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, your GO WALK is waiting. 20% off thru 11/2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your slip-ins are saved, plus 20% off for members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% through 11/2. Your cushioned walker is still in your cart.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free returns, easy sizing, and your Plus discount applied at checkout.`
