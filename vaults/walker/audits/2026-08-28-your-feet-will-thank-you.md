# Your feet will thank you
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Well now, this one's actually different — thirteenth HOKA email and it's not a racing shoe or a trail cliff-jumper for once. It's a slip-on recovery shoe, laceless, sock-like, "mega-cushy foam" — that's a shoe I could actually see myself wearing after I've been on my feet at the store all day. The "Give your foot a hug" headline is a little cutesy for my taste, but I get what they're going for.
- That said, it's still not quite my lane — this is pitched as an "active recovery" shoe, something you slip on between workouts, not an everyday walking shoe. I'm not doing workout sets I need to recover from. It's closer to what I want than eleven straight racing-shoe emails, but it's still talking past me a bit, like I'm a guy hitting the gym rather than a guy who just wants his feet to stop aching by 3pm.
- Would I click? Maybe, just to look at the slip-on — no laces is genuinely appealing at my age. Wouldn't forward it to anybody. Might scroll past the members-only sage colorway and the shoe finder quiz at the bottom without much interest.

## What's working

- Slip-on, no-lace design is exactly the kind of hands-free comfort I look for.
- "Mega-cushy foam" and "sock-like upper to hug your foot" speaks directly to comfort, which is my whole buying criteria.
- Clean product grid showing men's and women's colorways side by side, easy to scan on my phone.
- Shoe Finder Quiz at the bottom is a nice touch if I'm unsure on fit — beats guessing my size.

## What's weak

- It's framed as a "recovery" shoe for between workouts, not an everyday comfort shoe — that's not really my use case.
- The neon "Find Your Best Fit" runner photo at the bottom is back to that racing look I've complained about before — feels bolted onto an otherwise good email.
- Members-only sage colorway push (with a "Join for Free" button) feels like a distraction from the actual product I might want.
- No price shown anywhere, no mention of arch support or stability — the two things that actually decide it for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Reframe this as an everyday comfort/lounge shoe for guys like me, not strictly "active recovery" — say something like "on your feet all day? slip these on when you get home."
- 2. Show the price up front. I don't chase a shoe blind — I want to know if it's $70 or $150 before I click.
- 3. Drop a line about arch support or stability foam if it's actually got it — that's the deciding factor over "cushy."
- 4. Cut the runner-leaping quiz photo at the bottom or swap it for someone just walking — keep the whole email in the "comfort" lane instead of sliding back into performance imagery.
