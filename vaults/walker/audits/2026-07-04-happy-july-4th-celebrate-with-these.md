- **Shoe Carnival · Happy July 4th! Celebrate with THESE 👀 · 2026-07-04**

## What's working

- The **Brooks Price Break starting at $89.98** is a concrete price point from a brand I actually trust for support and cushion — that specific dollar figure stops my thumb cold
- **"STORES CLOSE AT 6PM"** banner across the top is genuinely useful on a holiday — practical, not promotional fluff
- The **Skechers Slip-Ins section** got my attention for a beat — Slip-Ins are the hands-free style I hunt for
- **Member $10 off and 250X Points** both visible — they at least know I'm in the loyalty program
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout held together on my phone

## What's weak

- **"Shop Women's Slip-Ins"** right under the Skechers Slip-Ins block — you teased the product I want, then pointed me to the wrong aisle entirely
- The **MEGA SALE banner** screams 50% and 60% off before quietly adding "Women's & Kids'" in smaller text below — by the time I read that, I'd already gotten my hopes up
- **Broken preview text — again** — this is the second time I've seen a raw tracking URL in the preheader; June 24 had the same problem and nothing's been done about it
- **"Celebrate with THESE 👀"** is a cute mystery hook, but if "THESE" turns out to be women's sandals, I didn't need the suspense
- Fourth consecutive email from this brand, following July 1, 2, and 3 — that's a lot of noise for one holiday weekend; the loyalty well is starting to feel squeezed

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader.** It's been broken since at least June 24. One plain line — "50-60% off + Brooks from $89.98, today only" — earns more trust in the inbox than a URL slug that looks like a phishing attempt
- 2. **Surface a men's Slip-Ins or men's comfort walking section** — if the inventory exists, give Walker a direct lane instead of burying him under a Women's CTA
- 3. **The $10 off threshold is oddly specific in the hero** — "$74.88+" reads clunky on a phone; round it to "save $10 on $75+" so it processes in one glance
- 4. **Rename the hero CTA** from the generic direction to the specific sale — "Shop Men's Deals" or "See Brooks Prices" beats "FIND YOUR SHOE" for a guy who knows what he's looking for
- 5. **Rest after the weekend** — a single-offer "last chance" send Monday will land cleaner than email #5 in a five-day run

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**CRITICAL — Zero-width space corrupts two tracking URLs**

The QA checker raised an `'ascii' codec can't encode character '\u200b'` error on both of these URLs (character at position 91, immediately after the UTM string):

```
https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?rd&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260704_nike_brooks_skechers_sandals_AM​
https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?rl&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260704_nike_brooks_skechers_sandals_AM​
```

U+200B is invisible but breaks URL parsing in clients that enforce ASCII encoding. Affected links will fail silently for a subset of recipients — likely the primary CTA and/or unsubscribe redirect links given the `?rd` / `?rl` parameters. Root cause is almost certainly a copy-paste artifact in the ESP template editor.
