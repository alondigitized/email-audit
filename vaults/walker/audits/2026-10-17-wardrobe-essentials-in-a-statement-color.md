- The black "Shop now" button is big and easy to tap with my thumb.

- "Free shipping on orders $75+" at the top is a good, honest perk.

## What's weak

- The hero is two young models in tracksuits. That's a clothing ad, not a shoe ad, and it doesn't look like me.
- The best offer, 25% off, is buried at the bottom under shopping bags. Its "Shop now" is only a small text link, not a button.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The New Balance email is a standard ESP-templated build (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `link.email.newbalance.com` tracking domain) with dark-mode support. The QA data shows 3 images missing alt text and an unverifiable authentication state, and the HTML provided was truncated, so I could not check footer, unsubscribe, or per-link content.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- No broken links are reported. The QA probed 12 material links and skipped 24 (footer, utility, and social links were deprioritized), so those 24 are unverified.
- The plain-text part is flagged as >70% URLs (11,496 of 13,328 chars). This indicates long tracked/wrapped redirect URLs in the text alternative. It is not a breakage, but it hurts readability for text-only clients and may be a spam-filter signal.
- The tracking pixel `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12449990_6062111_23470432.gif` has no alt text (see below).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Three images are missing alt text:
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png` (`https://link.email.newbalance.com/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingIcons_06.png`)
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` (`.../myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`)
  - `egR1f966kc_818512772_12449990_6062111_23470432.gif` (the `/mo/` open-tracking pixel)
- The tracking pixel should carry `alt=""` and be treated as decorative. The two shipping assets are content images: if they carry meaning, they need descriptive alt text. If they are decorative, they need `alt=""`.
- The shipping asset paths contain `myNB_Welcome`. This suggests they were reused from a welcome-email template. It is worth confirming they are intended in a promotional send.
- The template has leftover boilerplate. The `<title>` is "New Balance Template", and the CSS includes `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}`, an insecure `http://` placeholder URL that would be a mixed-content or broken request if the class were applied.
- `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv` attribute, so it does nothing. The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional while the `<html>` tag uses an XHTML namespace, a mismatch that is harmless but sloppy.
- Dark mode is declared with `color-scheme` meta tags, `:root`, and `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` overrides. The override `a {color:#cf0a2c !important;}` forces all links to red in dark mode, which may conflict with `.la2` classes that set white links on dark backgrounds. I could not verify this from the truncated source.
- The `@font-face` declarations have no `src`, so they do nothing. Fonts fall back to Arial or sans-serif in practice.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source. The truncated HTML shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{ }}`. The QA data reports none.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `Authentication-Results` header not found. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The QA notes this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so it is a test-harness limitation and not evidence of a sending problem. The `From` address is `newbalance@email.newbalance.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is the usual setup for alignment.
- The unsubscribe link was reportedly included in the probe set, and no failure is reported. I cannot confirm the physical postal address, the `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, or the unsubscribe URL from the supplied data.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not assessable. The truncated source includes no `href` values, and the QA output lists no landing-page URLs or UTM parameters. The tracking domain `link.email.newbalance.com` redirects, so UTM presence would need to be checked on the resolved destination.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add `alt=""` to the `/mo/…gif` pixel. Add descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if decorative, to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`.
2. Confirm that the `myNB_Welcome` shipping assets belong in this send.
3. Remove the `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder, set a real `<title>`, fix `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`, and add `src` to the `@font-face` rules or remove them.
4. Check that the dark-mode `a {color:#cf0a2c !important;}` rule doesn't override link colors on dark-background modules.
5. Shorten the plain-text part, or reduce tracked URLs in it, to bring URL density below 70%.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, the postal address, and UTM parameters on the 24 unprobed links.
