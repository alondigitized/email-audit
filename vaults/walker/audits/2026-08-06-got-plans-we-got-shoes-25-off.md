## Executive summary

- Another day, another DSW email, and this one's got a real problem before you even get to the shoes — there's a massive blank gap right under the header, like the email forgot to load half of itself. My eye did a double-take wondering if something broke. Once you scroll past all that dead space, it's the same script I've been getting all week: a "So New, So You" grid of women's heels and strappy sandals, then a big "40% off Big-Deal Brands" pitch with logos for Nike, New Balance, Crocs, Adidas, and Birkenstock — brands I actually wear — but not one photo of an actual men's shoe.
- The size chart goes 5 to 11, which is women's sizing, so even that "shop by size" module isn't built for me. And the school-year framing ("START THE SCHOOL YEAR RIGHT") tells me I'm not who this was written for — I'm 62, no kids buying backpacks and sneakers this month.
- I wouldn't click, and I definitely wouldn't forward it. This one goes straight to "mark as read" — worse than most of DSW's recent sends because of that broken-looking blank space up top.

## What's working

- The brand logo row (Nike, New Balance, Crocs, Adidas, Birkenstock) at least names brands I actually cross-shop, even if there's no product shot to back it up.
- "UP TO 40% OFF" in bold black type is easy to read without squinting.
- The DSW VIP perks module at the bottom (5% back, free shipping, birthday gift) is a clean, simple pitch if I were paying attention that far down.

## What's weak

- That big blank white gap between the header and "CLAIM 25% OFF" looks flat-out broken on my phone — like a chunk of the email didn't render.
- Every shoe in the "So New, So You" grid is a women's heel, wedge, or strappy sandal — same miss as the last few sends.
- Size chart only goes up to 11 with half-sizes like 5.5, 6.5 — that's women's shoe sizing, not mine.
- "School Year" framing has nothing to do with a guy my age shopping for comfort shoes.
- The subject line's arrow (">>>") and the "Got plans?" hook feel like a gimmick rather than a real offer — took real digging to find out what the offer actually was.

- 1. Fix that blank-space render bug first — that's the kind of thing that makes me think the email is broken and just close it, before I even see the offer.
- 2. Put at least one men's comfort shoe (New Balance walker, Skechers slip-on, Hoka) in the hero grid instead of an all-heels lineup.
- 3. Add a men's size range (7–14) to the size-chart module, or split it by gender.
- 4. Drop the "school year" hook for a segment like me — swap in something age-neutral tied to comfort or an actual dollar/percent callout.
- 5. Lead the subject with the real number instead of a vague "Got plans?" tease.
