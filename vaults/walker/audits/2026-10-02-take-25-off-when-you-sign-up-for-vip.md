# Take 25% off when you sign up for VIP!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this is the fourth VIP Days email I've gotten in two days. The "25% OFF*" on purple is the same banner I saw on 10/01 in "Happy VIP Days" and "Ooooh you get 25% off...". This time the subject says "when you sign up for VIP," which makes it sound like a membership pitch. The deal is really just a code, VIPFUN, on regular-priced styles.
- The booties are the main event, and booties aren't what I'm shopping for. The "comes in wide width" badges did catch my eye, since I notice that kind of thing. The rest is a long scroll of women's heels, a ThredUp recycling pitch, and a size grid. I'd like more of the men's side, which is only a "Shop Men" text link up top.
- I'd skim it and not click. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for something that has to do with walking shoes.

## What's working

- The giant "25% OFF*" on purple is readable on my phone without zooming.
- The code VIPFUN is printed right in the banner, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The "Shop Women / Men / Kids" links under the banner at least let me jump to my section.
- The "Comes in wide width" badges and the "Shop by size" grid with Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons are useful to someone who cares about fit.
- The "Free shipping for VIPs" and in-store pickup icons are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero is women's booties. The model, the heels and the "On (& Off) Duty" copy aren't aimed at a 62-year-old man.
- It's the fourth email in two days with the same 25% banner, and it feels like a repeat.
- The subject says "sign up for VIP." The body then has a "Become a VIP" button and a "Join for free" link, so it isn't clear whether I need to be a member for the 25%.
- The asterisk leads to dense fine print. It excludes brands like Skechers, Kelly & Katie, Naot and others, and it says regular-price only. I can't tell whether it applies to anything I'd buy.
- The "Shop Men" link is tiny text, and nothing in the email shows cushioning, arch support or slip-ons.
- It's very long, with a ThredUp section and barcode blocks that aren't relevant to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and show men's comfort and walking shoes in the hero. For me that means slip-ons and cushioned sneakers, with a clear "Shop men's comfort" button.
- 2. Say plainly what the 25% covers and whether I have to be a member. If Skechers and other comfort brands are excluded, I'd rather know up front.
- 3. Don't send the same banner four times in two days. Combine these into one email.
- 4. Shorten the email and move the fine print into a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off regular-price shoes, code VIPFUN`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wide widths, comfort picks, 25% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's walking and slip-on styles included. Free to join, ends Oct 4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code VIPFUN online or show the barcode in store. Exclusions apply.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed link failure: the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" tracked link returned HTTP 400. It also has a plain-text part made almost entirely of tracking URLs, and a few markup and accessibility defects. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, body links and headers were not available to check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". It is a roughly 1 KB `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/UhbtCQUatBHHudErTnds9A~~/AAQRxRA~/1v0mf8qoFK0r…` redirect.
  - The 400 comes from the click-tracking host, before any DSW landing page loads.
  - The probe may have been rejected because it isn't a real recipient. The data can't distinguish that from a malformed or expired token.
  - Retest with a real recipient click. If it still fails, the SMS opt-in CTA is dead.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 links were probed and 58 were skipped, so link health is unverified for most of the email.
- **Tracking pixels:** Two pixels were detected: `ablink.dsw.com/q/MzFdGbnUfqajDmzATLH2Yw~~/…` and `mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261002_P261386_V3_W-_Offer_Value`. Both are functioning pixels with no QA failure.
  - The `mi.dsw.com` pixel carries a user ID (`mi_u`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Both images flagged by QA (the `ablink.dsw.com/q/` image and `o.gif`) have no alt attribute. They look like tracking pixels. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` declares no language, so screen readers must guess pronunciation.
- **Invalid meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither does anything. A Content-Type header can still supply the charset.
- **Non-descriptive title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is generic. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `${}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so this covers only the part I could see.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found in the available data. QA always probes the unsubscribe link, and none failed. The footer and the postal address were cut off in the truncated source. No SPF, DKIM or DMARC header values were provided, so authentication is unverified.

**Deliverability (WARN):** The plain-text part is about 84% URLs (21,974 of 26,228 characters). That ratio is a spam-filter signal, and it comes from the long tracked URLs. Shorten the plain-text links or add real text content.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but little could be checked. Click links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`, so the destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be seen without following the redirects. Campaign attribution appears only in the pixel's `mi_ecmp=20261002_P261386_V3_W-_Offer_Value`. The landing pages of the 58 skipped links were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/UhbtCQUa…`) with a real recipient click. If it still returns 400, fix the link in the ESP, because it is the only confirmed broken CTA.
2. Shorten the plain-text version by reducing or simplifying the tracked links, so URLs make up well under 70% of it.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
6. Rerun the audit with the full HTML and headers so the footer, unsubscribe headers, authentication and UTM checks can be completed.
