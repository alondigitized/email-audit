# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Well, I land on Skechers and the whole screen is pink flowers and a mom with her little girl. Nice picture, I suppose — but I came here looking for Slip-ins, not a Mother's Day gift. Skechers is clearly pushing a seasonal campaign hard right now, and there's a real deal up top (BOGO 50% off), but none of it is pointed at a guy like me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — that blue bar is the first thing I see and it gets my attention. A deal that good makes me want to poke around even when the hero isn't for me.
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary strip** — a second offer stacked right below is good reinforcement. I know there's a sale happening.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — big, black, full-width. I know exactly where to tap. No squinting.
- **Clean mobile layout** — logos, icons, and text are all sized properly. Nothing feels cramped or broken on a phone screen.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is Mother's Day, women's, pink florals** — I feel like I wandered into the wrong department. There's no signal anywhere that this store sells men's comfort shoes.
- **No Slip-ins or Men's anywhere above the fold** — my reason for being here is completely invisible.
- **No loyalty/Skechers Plus callout** — I've been a member long enough to expect to see my rewards. Nothing.
- **"SHOP NOW" goes to Mother's Day, not Men's** — the one CTA above the fold takes me somewhere I don't want to be.
- **No sizing, comfort-tech, or Slip-ins messaging** — the whole comfort/hands-free angle that brought me here in the first place is absent.

## Recommendations

- **Add a secondary hero tile or category strip for Men's above the "SHOP NOW" button** — even a small "Men's Comfort | Shop Slip-ins" banner keeps male shoppers from bouncing during a women's campaign.
- **Surface the Skechers Plus rewards balance or a member-only badge in the nav bar** — I should see "Hi Walker, you have X points" or a loyalty icon the moment I land.
- **Swap the generic "SHOP NOW" to a split CTA** — "Shop Her Gifts | Shop Men's" so returning male shoppers aren't funneled into Mother's Day inventory.
- **Put the BOGO banner to work for Men's** — a one-line note like "BOGO includes Men's Slip-ins" would make that deal feel like it's actually meant for me.
