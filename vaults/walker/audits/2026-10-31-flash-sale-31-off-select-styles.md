# Flash Sale: 31% off select styles!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again. I got the Halloween "Scary-good deals" email at 31% off yesterday, and today it's the same 31% with "Flash Sale" in front. The text is big and I could read it on my phone without squinting, and there's one button. But a "flash" sale that repeats yesterday's number doesn't feel like a flash to me. It feels like a sale that never ends, because this is the fifth Crocs offer I've seen in about ten days.
- The hot pink Baya clog is the only picture. I'm a 62-year-old man who wants arch support and cushioning, and that's not what this shows me. It says nothing about comfort, fit, or who it's for. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on. If I wanted a Crocs clog I'd go to the site on my own.

## What's working

- "31% Off" in big black type is easy to read with no zoom. It's the first thing I see.
- There's one black "Treat Yourself" button. I know where to tap.
- The green Crocs logo and the free-shipping bar at the top tell me right away who sent it.
- The expiry date at the bottom, Sunday, November 1st at 11:59pm MT, gives me a real deadline.

## What's weak

- "Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply" don't tell me whether the shoes I'd actually wear are included. I have no idea what's on sale.
- The only shoe is a neon pink clog. It isn't aimed at a man my age, and it says nothing about comfort or support.
- "Treat Yourself" doesn't say what I'd be shopping for. It sounds like a gift for someone else.
- The fine print is tiny and sits below the footer links. It says the discount can't be combined and is for select styles only. I'd want to see that before the button, not after.
- A big blank gap sits between the button and the shoe, so the email feels thin.
- Nothing here says I'm a Crocs Club member, even though the 10/22 email did.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the actual shoes that are 31% off. Put two or three men's styles with the sale price, such as a walking clog or a lined slip-on, in place of one pink clog.
- 2. Name the end date at the top: "Ends Sunday night." That's more believable than just calling it a flash sale.
- 3. Change the button to something that says what it does, like "Shop Men's Clogs."
- 4. Drop the empty space and move the key fine print, "select styles, can't combine," up near the button.
- 5. Space the offers out. Five different sales in ten days teaches me to wait for the next one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 15 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they're click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.crocs-email.com`). Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10208&bmt=0` is a first-party domain with `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`. No issue.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. It carries a per-recipient identifier (`d_dpuid`) in the URL. This is not a defect, but it is a third-party data-sharing call that the privacy policy should cover.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so clients ignore it. The same applies to `<meta content="IE=edge" />`, which lacks `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Non-ASCII characters are at risk. The source contains invisible-character preheader padding such as `͏ ‌ ­` and `™`-type glyphs, so the charset should be declared properly (`<meta charset="utf-8">` or `http-equiv`) and the MIME `Content-Type` header should be confirmed.
- **Missing alt text (QA warning confirmed):** the `demdex.net/event` `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is non-descriptive. This is minor and only matters in web-view rendering.
- **Remaining body:** the source is truncated, so alt text on content images, button markup, table roles and the font fallback stacks for `TT Crocs` weights 400/700/900 are unverified. The webfonts are hosted at `media.crocs.com/raw/upload/marketing/…`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear. The body past the truncation point is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance

The From domain (`crocs-email.com`) matches the tracking domain (`click.crocs-email.com`), which is consistent with an aligned ESP setup. Verify against raw headers (`Received-SPF`, `DKIM-Signature`, `d=`).
