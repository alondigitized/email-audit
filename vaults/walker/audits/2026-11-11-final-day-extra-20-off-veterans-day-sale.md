# Final Day: Extra 20% Off Veterans Day Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival again. Yesterday's Veterans Day email promised a sleigh and then showed a Veterans Day banner, and this is the same banner with "FINAL DAY" added. The red and gold "EXTRA 20% OFF" is huge and I can read it on my phone without my glasses. That's the one thing this email does well.
- Below the banner it's all women's tall boots and "Women's Boots BOGO Free." I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants a cushioned slip-on, and there isn't one pair of men's shoes in the whole email. The extra 10% off for veterans and military is a nice touch, but it's the only thing here aimed at someone like me. The men's link is just a small word in the footer bar.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim it and mark it read, and I wouldn't forward it. I've had this brand in my inbox about every day this month, and the "final day" line feels like a pattern now rather than real urgency.

## What's working

- The "EXTRA 20% OFF" with the code VETSDAY is big and easy to read. I know the deal in two seconds.
- The extra 10% for veterans and active military is a real perk, and it says how to get it (Shoe Perks login online, military ID in store).
- "FINAL DAY" up top gives a reason to act today.
- The Shoe Perks reward and Afterpay tiles near the bottom are easy to read and make it clear I'm a known member.
- The text is large and the layout is one simple column, which is good on a phone.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is a women's boot. Nothing is comfort, slip-on, walking, or men's, so it feels sent to the wrong person.
- The "Shop Boots" and "Shop BOGO Free" links are tiny, plain text under big photos. They're hard to tap, and nothing in the hero looks like a button.
- There are asterisks everywhere and a wall of tiny fine print listing excluded brands. The exclusions include Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance, and the other comfort brands I actually buy. That doesn't feel honest.
- The inbox preview shows a raw tracking link instead of real copy. That looks sloppy.
- It's the same Veterans Day banner as yesterday, so it's the fifth or sixth near-identical coupon blast in two weeks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and category. Send me a men's comfort and walking hero, such as cushioned slip-ons, with the 20% code on a real, tappable button. That one change would lift this email more than anything else.
- 2. Make the CTAs buttons with specific copy, like "Shop Men's Slip-Ons," in large type.
- 3. Move the veterans 10% higher or pair it with a short "Veterans: here's how to claim it" line. Cut the fine print down to one plain sentence about what's excluded.
- 4. Fix the preheader so it shows real copy and not a link.
- 5. Don't send a "final day" email after a near-identical one the day before. Pick one send, or change the creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: extra 20% off + 10% for vets`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: 20% off comfort for your feet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code VETSDAY online. Veterans take an extra 10% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends tonight at 11:59 PM ET. Men's and women's styles included.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally workable, with responsive media queries and client-specific resets. The QA data flags three images with missing alt text, one of which is a content image. It also shows an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder in a tracking pixel URL and a malformed campaign parameter. The HTML source was truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 23 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on tracking domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Stray character in the pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261111_veterans_day_bogof_AM​` ends with a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. Depending on how the ESP parses it, this can corrupt the campaign value in reporting.
- **Malformed query string:** The `t=utm_campaign=...` value nests a key/value pair inside a parameter. It is not a standard UTM query string, so analytics tools may not parse it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content image missing alt text:** `https://image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/5f1aa1c8-44eb-4583-a9c5-e05e02309d6b.jpg` has no `alt`. With images blocked, or for screen readers, that content is lost.
- **Tracking pixels missing alt:** `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` and `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` also lack `alt`. They are decorative, so they should have `alt=""` to be skipped explicitly.
- **Links with no visual cue:** The global rule `a, a:link, a:visited … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` removes the default link styling. Links then depend entirely on inline styles or button treatment.
- **Ineffective meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Doctype and namespace mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. This is not harmful in practice, but it is inconsistent.
- **Other checks not possible:** Table layout, `role="presentation"` and the preheader were in the truncated portion, so I could not check them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved placeholder:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still contains the literal `[UNIQUE]` token. If that is meant to be a per-recipient ID, it was not substituted. The open pixel would then record a non-unique or invalid ID.
- **No other tokens seen:** No other unresolved tokens appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The QA notes this is expected with the AgentMail relay, so it is a limitation of the test setup and not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Not assessable:** The unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the truncated HTML or the supplied header data.
- **Sender domain:** The From address is on `email.shoecarnival.com`, so it uses a branded subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverified destinations:** The only UTM evidence is on the tracking pixel. Because the 23 link redirects were not probed, the final landing URLs and their UTM carry-through are unverified.
- **Campaign name mismatch:** The campaign string is `SCA_20261111_veterans_day_bogof_AM`. The `bogof` (buy-one-get-one-free) tag does not match the subject "Extra 20% Off Veterans Day Sale". This may mean the campaign ID was reused, which would mis-attribute reporting. I can't confirm it from the data given.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to `5f1aa1c8-44eb-4583-a9c5-e05e02309d6b.jpg`, and set `alt=""` on both `emltrk.com` pixels.
2. Fix the `[UNIQUE]` substitution in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel and confirm that per-recipient IDs populate at send time.
3. Strip the trailing U+200B from the `utm_campaign` value, and use a standard `utm_campaign=` query parameter instead of nesting it inside `t=`.
4. Check that the campaign name matches the actual promotion (Veterans Day 20% off vs. BOGOF).
5. Rerun the QA with the full, untruncated HTML and the raw headers. This should cover:
   - the unsubscribe and address footer;
   - `List-Unsubscribe` headers;
   - SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment;
   - destination URLs and UTM carry-through, resolved through the redirects rather than skipped.
6. Add `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the IE meta tag, or remove it. Keep link underlines or equivalent affordances for body text links.
