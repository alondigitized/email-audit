# ⏳ Limited-Time Offer: 30% Off + Free Shipping
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one finally says something plain: 30% off and free shipping, in big black letters on a yellow background. After a week of fuzzy slippers and a camo clog, I could read the point of it in about two seconds. That's a lot better than the Burlebo email on 9/29, where I had to squint at the logo.
- The trouble is it's a sale with no real shoe story. I get one black Baya clog and the word SALE spun around it like a pinwheel. It doesn't say which styles are included, and "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the button. The Baya clog was already 30% off on 9/26 and 9/27, so this feels like the same sale coming back. I'd probably click, because 30% off a clog is real money. I wouldn't forward it. It's a sale notice, not something a friend needs to see.

## What's working

- The headline "Take 30% Off AND Get Free Shipping!" is huge and easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- There's one clear black button, "Shop the Sale," with plenty of room around it for my thumb.
- The shoe in the picture is a plain black Baya clog, which is the kind of simple, cushioned shoe I'd actually wear.
- The Crocs logo is at the top, so I know who it's from right away.
- The end date is in the fine print: Wednesday, October 7th at 11:59pm MT. A real date makes me trust it more than "Hurry, this offer won't last."

## What's weak

- "Hurry, this offer won't last" is vague, and the real deadline is buried in tiny print at the very bottom.
- "Exclusions Apply" and "select styles only" make me wonder if the shoes I care about are even on sale.
- The button goes to "the Sale" in general. It doesn't go to a specific shoe, and nothing tells me about fit, arch support or comfort.
- The SALE pinwheel is busy and makes the black clog harder to pick out.
- The bottom half is app downloads, social icons and a refer-a-friend offer. None of that helps me buy a shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Oct 7," instead of "Hurry, this offer won't last." That's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Link the picture directly to the Baya clog, and name the shoe and its sale price in the copy.
- 3. Add one line about comfort, something like "lightweight, cushioned, slips on," since that's what I shop for.
- 4. Say which styles are excluded, or at least show 3 or 4 shoes that are included.
- 5. Drop the pinwheel SALE graphic and the extra footer items.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Crocs + free shipping, ends Oct 7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Baya Clogs 30% off, free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-on comfort for less. Sale ends Wednesday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our lightest, most cushioned clogs are on sale. Shipping's on us.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crocs, "⏳ Limited-Time Offer: 30% Off + Free Shipping"

The HTML was truncated and the QA run skipped probing 20 tracking links, so the body, footer and headers were not available for review. Findings below are limited to what the visible source and the QA data confirm.

## 1. Technical Summary
The `<head>`, preheader and pixels are mostly sound, with no unresolved tokens visible. Two confirmed defects are in the markup: seven footer and app images are served over plain `http://`, and the charset and `X-UA-Compatible` meta tags are malformed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped 20 links as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?6RL47WIUI47UBDIEBINGNYUHGY.10205&d=10205&bmt=0` is HTTPS, 1×1, `alt=""`, and sits inside a hidden div. No issue.
- **Second tracking pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…` has no `alt` attribute (QA flagged it). It is hidden with `display:none`, so the impact is negligible, but `alt=""` is the correct value. It also fires a second open-tracking call to a third-party domain (Adobe Audience Manager), alongside the first-party pixel. Because `display:none` images are often not fetched, open counts from this pixel may be understated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images (confirmed, 7 instances).** All come from `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`:
  - App Store badge (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Google Play badge (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)

  Clients and webmail that enforce mixed-content or HTTPS-only image loading can block these, which leaves the footer badges and social icons broken. The same host is already used over HTTPS elsewhere in Crocs' sends, so switching the scheme should be trivial.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Without the attribute, neither tag does anything, so the charset declaration is not in the document body. This is low risk if the Content-Type header carries `charset=utf-8`, but the header was not available to confirm.
- **Alt text:** the seven `http://` images do have alt text ("Download on the App Store", "Instagram", etc.). The only image missing it is the hidden demdex pixel.
- **Positives:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - A viewport meta tag and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` are present.
  - The Apple data-detector override is present.
  - A mobile breakpoint is defined at 639px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder of the body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
Not verifiable from the supplied data. The unsubscribe link, physical postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results all fall outside the truncated HTML and the QA output. Nothing is flagged, but nothing is confirmed either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 20 click-tracked links resolve through `click.crocs-email.com`, and the QA run did not follow them, so the final destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "30% Off + Free Shipping" offer are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change all seven `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image sources to `https://`. This is the only confirmed rendering defect.
2. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags, or confirm that the MIME header declares UTF-8.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex `event` pixel.
4. Rerun QA with the full, untruncated HTML and a redirect-following probe on the tracking links. That would cover unsubscribe, footer address, UTM presence and final landing-page status. Also pull the raw headers to check List-Unsubscribe and authentication.
5. Fix the QA tooling's scoring. It reported "100% pass rate, 0 issue(s), 0 warning(s)" while listing 8 `[WARN]` entries under INFO, so the `http://` image findings are not reaching the summary count.
