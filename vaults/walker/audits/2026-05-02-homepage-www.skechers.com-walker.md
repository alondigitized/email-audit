# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Full review
Well now, that's not a bad start. Right at the top there's a BOGO 50% OFF deal that catches my eye — that's the kind of thing that makes me want to stick around and see what's on offer. I'm on a fixed budget and I like to feel like I'm getting my money's worth, so that banner did its job. There's also a Spring Apparel Sale with 25% off, which I noticed but honestly I'm not really here for the clothes.

The two folks in the hero shot — Brooke Burke and some fella named Tony — they look about right. Not teenagers. The guy's got on a pair of clean white and blue sneakers and he doesn't look like he's trying too hard. That reads to me. But I had to scroll down to get to the part I actually care about: the Hands Free Slip-ins headline. That's my thing. That's why I'm here. I wish that wasn't buried — lead with it!

The navigation is clean enough. Big tap targets on the top icons, which I appreciate because I'm not always precise with my fingers on a small screen. The MENU button is there in plain sight. No tiny print jumping out at me, which is a relief.

What's missing for me is anything that says "hey, Skechers Plus member, here's something for you." I'm loyal. I've earned points. A little nod to that — a member deal, early access, anything — would've made me feel like they knew I was coming. Instead it feels a little generic, like it's talking to everyone and no one at once.

**6/10**
