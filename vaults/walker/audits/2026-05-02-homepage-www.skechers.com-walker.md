# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Right away I can see this is a shoe brand that knows who it's talking to — they've got Slip-ins front and center and a solid BOGO deal staring me in the face before I even scroll. The homepage feels comfortable and clean on my phone, not cluttered. They're pushing spring apparel and their celebrity folks (looks like Brooke Burke and Tony Hawk) to give it a little glamour, but the real hook for me is that Slip-ins headline at the bottom.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **"HANDS FREE Slip-ins" section** — that's exactly why I come to Skechers; seeing it this early in the scroll tells me I'm in the right place
- **BOGO 50% OFF banner at the very top** — I notice a deal before I notice anything else, and "applied at cart" means no coupon code fumbling, which I appreciate
- **Tony Hawk lookalike guy in the hero** — looks like a man my age, relaxed, sitting down, wearing something I'd actually wear; that resonates
- **Clean mobile layout** — big text, nothing crammed together, easy to read on my phone without pinching and zooming
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary banner** — stacked promotions tell me there's real value here right now, worth my time

## What's weak

- **No visible hero headline copy above the celebrities** — the hero photo is nice but it just shows two people sitting; I don't know what shoe they're wearing or why I should care until I scroll further
- **"Shop the Spring Apparel Sale"** — I'm here for shoes, not clothes; apparel feels off-target for me personally
- **Brooke Burke celebrity name label** — I can barely read it over the image, too small for my eyes on a phone screen
- **No Skechers Plus / loyalty callout above the fold** — I'm a member and I'd love to see my status or member pricing acknowledged right up top

## Recommendations

- **Put the Slip-ins product name or a key comfort feature (memory foam, arch fit) directly on the hero image** — let me know what shoe the guy is wearing without waiting to scroll down
- **Add a "Welcome back, Walker" or loyalty member badge** in the top nav next to the cart icon — recognizing me as a Plus member would immediately feel personal
- **Move the Slip-ins section up** so it's the first thing I hit after the hero, not something I discover mid-scroll
- **Swap the apparel sale banner** for a footwear-first deal or a "New in Men's Comfort" callout — more relevant to why I'm visiting
