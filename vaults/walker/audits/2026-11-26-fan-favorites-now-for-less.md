# Fan favorites, now for less

## Executive summary

- This one finally reads like a sale email and not another cart nag. After a dozen "Your cart is waiting" sends since 11/20, a plain "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" red banner is a relief. I knew what the deal was in about two seconds.
- The hero is a family opening gifts by a Christmas tree, with "Loved by Thousands" across it. It's cheerful, but it's not me. I don't see a man my age anywhere. The four shoe cards below are where it gets useful. Each one has a real customer quote and separate Women's and Men's buttons. The Arch Fit card is the one I'd tap, since that's the comfort I'm shopping for.
- I'd click, probably the Men's Arch Fit button or the Slip-ins one. I wouldn't forward it. It's a decent holiday sale email, and the "enough points for a $5 reward" line at the top barely moves me.

## What's working

- The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner at the top is clear, and I can read it without zooming.
- The customer quotes ("Went for a four-mile walk the day I received them") are the kind of proof I trust. They talk about walking and comfort.
- Separate Men's and Women's buttons on every card mean I don't have to hunt for my section.
- The Slip-ins, Cozy Fit and Arch Fit cards line up with what I look for: easy on, soft, supportive.
- The extra 15% for Plus members with code BF2026 is a real second reason to act. The code is shown right in the banner.

## What's weak

- The hero is a young family with kids and gifts. Nothing in it speaks to a 62-year-old man buying for his own feet.
- The same "Shop Now" red button shows up twice, and then there's a second row of buttons in every card. That's a lot of tapping choices.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. The "30-50%" comes with exclusions like "select" boots and "OG Anunoby" items, so the big number feels a bit like bait.
- The shoes are small thumbnails. The Arch Fit shoe looks nice, but I can't see the cushioning or the sole.
- The 1000 points / $5 reward line is small and easy to miss. That's fine by me, but it's not much of a member perk.
- This is still the same Skechers inbox I've been getting daily. Even a good email lands in a crowded week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a man in his 60s in a slip-in on the hero, walking, instead of the family scene. Then lead with comfort: "Walk all day. Now for less."
- 2. Cut the duplicate buttons. One Men's button per card is plenty for me.
- 3. Show the real discount on the product cards (e.g. "$XX → $XX") so I can do the price-versus-quality math myself.
- 4. Make the exclusions one plain line near the banner, not buried at the bottom.
- 5. Slow the cadence. One strong sale email a day beats a cart nag plus a sale.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting, `"Hi ,"`) and several tracking-pixel defects. These include a scheme-less pixel URL that cannot load, two HTTP-sourced images and missing alt text. The HTML supplied was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and CTA URLs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken pixel URL.** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the Return Path/Validity seed-monitoring pixel never fires. It also has no `alt`.
- **Opaque click tracking.** 41 links on click-redirect domains (`click.emails.skechers.com`) were skipped for HTTP probing, so their destinations are unverified.
- **Third-party beacons.** Four `beacon.krxd.net` and `ink1000.com` pixels are present. They are hidden with `display:none` or `width="0"`, and several have no `alt`.
- **Mixed content.** The `ink1000.com` pixel (`http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif`) uses HTTP, so it may be blocked or rewritten by image proxies.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **HTTP images.** `http://www.ink1000.com/.../o.gif` and `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/.../dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` can be blocked by clients that enforce HTTPS. The PNG is a content-bearing asset, not a pixel.
- **Missing alt.** `dde00662-….png` and `o.gif` have no `alt` (QA-confirmed). The `krxd.net` and `returnpath.net` pixels also lack `alt` in the source.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset is therefore not reliably declared.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` prevents iOS text resizing.
- **Layout tables.** The visible layout tables (e.g. `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Duplicate selectors.** The CSS repeats the same rule blocks across the 375px and 640px media queries. This is not a defect, but it adds weight.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot.** The greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` because the first-name field was blank. The audit inbox's subscriber record likely has no first name, but the template has no fallback (e.g. "Hi there,"). Any subscriber without a name will see this.
- **Hashed email sent to a third party.** `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=…_sha25&partner_uid=9153f33fe5cdf59baf088bb835d3b39ef90f188bd8540a4faba92899227ced00` carries a SHA-256 value, presumably of the recipient's email address (I can't confirm what it hashes). That is a data-sharing point to confirm against the privacy policy. It is not a merge-token error.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication headers: unverifiable.** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA flags this as expected under the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is not a confirmed defect.
- **Footer not inspected.** The supplied HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` headers or the physical postal address.
- **Sender address.** `From: no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address, which is not itself a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters unverifiable.** Click URLs are encoded SFMC `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects, and the probe skipped them. The only campaign identifier visible is `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11262026`, in the pixel URLs.
- **Offer alignment unchecked.** The preheader claims "Save 30-50% sitewide, plus an extra 15% off everything for members", and the landing page was not verified against it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge (e.g. `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstName),"there",@firstName)=%%`) so the greeting never renders as `"Hi ,"`.
2. Add `https://` to the Return Path pixel (`https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`) so it loads. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Serve `ink1000.com/.../o.gif` and `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-….png` over HTTPS. Give the PNG descriptive `alt` text.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` rule, and add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Re-run the audit against the full HTML. Check the unsubscribe link, the `List-Unsubscribe` headers, the postal address and the resolved click URLs' UTM parameters. Pull the raw headers directly from the receiving mailbox to confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
