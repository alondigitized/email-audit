## What's weak

- "Built to fly" hero — that's runner talk, not something that speaks to a comfort-first guy like me.
- That neon yellow band with the black racing shoe reads more like a track ad than a men's casual pitch.
- The whole back half is apparel — leggings, running tops, outfit flat-lays — nothing to do with what I came for, which is shoes.

- No mention of arch support, cushioning specifics, or comfort features anywhere in the copy — I have to guess from the pictures alone.

- 2. Cut the apparel section entirely from a "best sellers" shoe email, or split it into its own send — mixing tights and trail tops in with shoe best-sellers just muddies the message.
- 3. Put an actual number in front of me — percent off, price, something concrete — "going fast" doesn't tell me why I should act today.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email renders and sends correctly, but the unsubscribe link returns a 403 and the plain-text MIME part is empty, both of which are compliance-relevant defects; accessibility is weakened by systemic missing alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed material links passed (12/12); no broken CTAs found.
- The unsubscribe link itself failed: `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...` returns HTTP 403. Decoding the embedded `jwtP` payload shows the true destination is `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260927_BestSellers_STO_A90Day_270BestSellersTag_180Purchasers_BestSellersPLPBrowsers_AIRFMHigh&hmail=...&cordial_id=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9` — the redirect wrapper (Cordial ESP click-tracker) is the failure point, not the landing URL itself.
- Tracking pixels present: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` (open tracking) and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...` (secondary open pixel) — both load as `<img>` tags, standard ESP instrumentation, no issues found.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 14 images missing `alt` attributes, including all hero/product imagery (`US_20260927_BestSellersTrendingColors_*`, `US_Clifton11_*`, `US_Bondi9_*`, `US_BondiSR_*`, `US_Clifton11GTX_*`) and the footer logo (`LogoFooterPrimary.png`). This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades screen-reader and images-off rendering (Outlook default) to a blank email.
- Both tracking pixels also lack `alt` (expected/acceptable for 1x1 trackers, but not for the content images above).
- Responsive CSS includes malformed mobile rules: `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` — missing numeric values make these declarations invalid CSS; they will be dropped by parsers, likely an unintended residual from a template variable that failed to interpolate.
