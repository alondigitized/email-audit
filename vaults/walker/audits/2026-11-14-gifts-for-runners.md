# Gifts for runners
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least this one leads with shoes and not shopping bags. Still, it's a runner email, and I'm a walker. The top of my phone is a man and woman posed next to a stack of red wrapped presents. The headline says "The Intelligent Choice for runners" with two black buttons under it. It looks nice, but it never tells me why I should buy anything today.
- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline anywhere. The only offer is a thin "Free shipping on all orders" line at the very top. After the 25% and "up to 25% off" emails earlier this week, this one feels like a catalog. I'd glance at it and move on. I wouldn't forward it to a friend.

## What's working

- The big hero photo is clean and easy to read, and the red presents say gift season without much fuss.
- Two plain black buttons, "Shop now" and "Visit a store," are easy to see and big enough for my thumb.
- Real shoe photos make up most of the lower half: the 1080v15, the Ellipse, the Rebel v5, and a close-up of cushioned soles. That's more shoe than I got in the last two baseball emails.
- The footer has "Running," "Walking" and "Lifestyle" links, so I can find walking shoes if I look.

## What's weak

- Every product is for runners. Nothing says walking, slip-on, arch support or all-day comfort.
- I don't see a price anywhere, not even under the "Running bestsellers." I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- There's no deadline and no sale. The free shipping line at the top is small and easy to miss.
- The grid of tiles has a lot of apparel. Sports bras and jackets crowd out the shoes, and the models are young and fit. I don't see anyone like me.
- The product names under the bestsellers are tiny, and the labels like "The Ellipse" mean nothing to me without a description.
- Walking sits only in the footer, and "Pick out the perfect gift" is a weak closer with a small "Visit a store" link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one specific offer in the hero, like "Free shipping on gifts, order by Dec 15," with a single button.
- 2. Add a walking and comfort row, such as "Cushioned walkers for dad," with prices and a mention of arch support.
- 3. Show prices and a one-line comfort note under every bestseller.
- 4. Trim the apparel tiles and show more shoes, with a wider range of ages in the photos.
- 5. Tag the email by interest so a walker like me sees walking shoes first.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gift-ready shoes, free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfortable gifts for dad, from $89`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned picks for walkers and runners. Free shipping on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery. See our top-rated comfort shoes.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible head markup is mostly sound: it declares dark-mode support, has a viewport meta and a charset. It also has template leftovers (a placeholder `<title>`, an `XXXXX` placeholder image URL) and 3 images without alt text. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and links could not be checked. Authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Placeholder asset in CSS:** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` is an unresolved template placeholder. It is also plain `http://`, which clients may block as mixed content. It will 404 wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** QA reports 12944 of 14535 characters (about 89%) are URLs. That is the likely result of long ESP tracking URLs on `link.email.newbalance.com`. It is a spam-filter risk on the text/plain alternative.
- **Probe coverage was partial:** 12 links were probed and 29 skipped. The prompt did not include per-link results (status codes or redirect chains), so I can't confirm any broken links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `egR1f966kc_818512772_12654745_257456_23906257.gif`. The `/mo/` path suggests this is the open-tracking pixel, so it should carry `alt=""`.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - `<title>New Balance Template</title>` was never replaced with the subject or a real title.
  - The image paths `custloads/.../myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/` suggest assets reused from the myNB Welcome email.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `.bg1` is defined twice, first as `#ffffff` and then as `#58546d`. The later rule wins, so any element using `.bg1` renders `#58546d` in light mode. This may be unintended.
- **No-op font rules:** every `@font-face` block in the visible CSS has no `src`, so none of them load a font. The fallback stacks (`Arial,sans-serif`) are the only fonts that apply.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the `<html>` tag carries an XHTML `xmlns`. This is harmless but inconsistent.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags and a `prefers-color-scheme` block are present. The dark-mode rule `a {color:#cf0a2c !important;}` forces all links to red. I can't confirm contrast from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated HTML. The body was not visible, so this is incomplete coverage.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** QA found no `Authentication-Results` header. It attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This is a limit of the test setup and not evidence of a sender misconfiguration. The sender is `newbalance@email.newbalance.com`.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was provided. The footer was truncated, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link, the postal address or a `List-Unsubscribe` header.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data provided. No destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page probe results were included. The only domain visible is the ESP tracking domain `link.email.newbalance.com`, which redirects to unseen destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove or replace `url('http://image.XXXXX.png')` in `.bgwhite`, or delete the rule if it is unused.
2. Add alt text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Set a real `<title>` and fix the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Remove the duplicate `.bg1` definition and confirm which colour is intended.
5. Add `src` to the `@font-face` declarations or remove them.
6. Shorten the text/plain URLs, for example by using fewer tracked links or a plain-text-specific link format.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, the raw headers (including `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and the link-probe output. That is needed to cover the unsubscribe link, UTMs and landing-page continuity.
