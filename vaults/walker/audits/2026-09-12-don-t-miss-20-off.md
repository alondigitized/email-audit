# Don’t miss! 20% off
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW newsletter, same story every time this week — "20% OFF" in big bold letters up top, and I'll admit that catches my eye for half a second. But then it's booties, mules, heeled ankle boots on a woman with red boots literally balanced on her head, then knee-high burgundy suede boots on a model sitting cross-legged. "Shop Burgundy & Red Styles" — none of that is men's, none of that is a walking shoe I'd wear to Costco or on my morning route.
- I scroll all the way down past the size chart and the "wide width / wide calf" options, and there's not a single sneaker, slip-on, or anything with a cushioned sole in sight. This is the fifth or sixth DSW email in a row that's 100% women's fall boots with a coupon code slapped on top. At this point I don't even open these expecting to find something for me — I'm just seeing if they've finally figured out I'm not who this email is for.
- Would I click? No. Forward to a friend? Only if she's shopping for fall boots — definitely not for me. This one gets archived without a second look.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" banner up top is legible and impossible to miss.
- "Shop Men" isn't even offered as a nav option this time, but at least the top nav has "MEN" listed so I know the site itself carries men's shoes even if this email doesn't bother showing me any.
- Size chart and wide-width/wide-calf callouts near the bottom are clear and easy to read on my phone.
- Free shipping for VIPs and BOPIS (in-store pickup) icons near the footer are useful info, when I actually get there.

## What's weak

- Every single hero image is women's boots — the "well red" boot-on-head shot, the trending booties/mules collage, the knee-high burgundy suede boots. Nothing for me at all.
- The CTAs are all "Shop Burgundy & Red Styles" and "Shop All Women" — not one button takes me anywhere near men's comfort shoes.
- This is the same "20% off VIP" offer I've now seen four or five times this week from DSW with barely any variation in the pitch.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or comfort brands anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men vs. women sends — if DSW has my browsing or purchase history, this shouldn't be going out to me as a wall of fall boots. Send me a men's comfort/walking shoe version of this same 20% VIP promo.
- 2. Swap at least one hero slot for men's casual/comfort styles — Skechers, Hoka, or New Balance slip-ons — even in a shared-template email, so men aren't just an afterthought nav link.
- 3. Lead with the comfort story, not just the color trend — "bold fall color" means nothing to me; "all-day cushioning" would.
- 4. Vary the offer creative — this exact "20% OFF VIP" framing is getting stale after this many repeats this week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall walking shoes, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, arch support — all in your size`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals from Skechers, Hoka & more`
