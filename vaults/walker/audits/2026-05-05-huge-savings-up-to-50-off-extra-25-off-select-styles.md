## What's working

- **The headline is clear.** "Up to 50% Off + Extra 25% Off Select Sale Items" — I understood the deal in two seconds. That's good. No tricks, no weird phrasing.
- **The layout is clean and uncluttered.** Nothing is broken. Text is readable. I didn't have to zoom in to see what they're selling.
- **The deadline is present.** May 11th is there — it's in tiny fine print, but it's there. A sale with an end date is more trustworthy than one that "never ends."

## What's weak

- **There is no CTA button.** None. This is the biggest miss. I'm on my phone, I'm interested in 50% off, and there's nowhere to tap. The whole email just stops after the shoe photo. That's like a TV commercial with no phone number.
- **The products shown don't speak to me.** Baby blue, hot pink, and cream clogs arranged like a fashion spread. I'm a 62-year-old man. None of those colorways say "this is for you, Walker." Where's a simple black or navy men's clog? Where's a men's slide or anything that reads masculine?

- **The footer is mostly app badges and social icons.** I don't need to download the Crocs app. I need a link to shop.
