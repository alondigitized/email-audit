# Final call for 20% OFF
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the tenth or so send on the same HAYRIDE 20% code since the 8th. This morning I got "20% off ends tonight!", then "Remember that 20% off? It ends *tonight*", and now "Final call." The fourth "last chance" in one day makes it hard to believe any of them. I'd have trusted the deadline more if they'd sent it once.
- The big white "20% OFF*" on green is easy to read on my phone without zooming. But the products under it are all women's boots and flats, including a fur-fringe boot. I scrolled past and saw nothing for me. The "SHOP MEN" link is there, but it's small underlined text under the button.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd mark it as read. I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted shoes, I'd already have used the code in the last three days.

## What's working

- The giant "20% OFF*" is readable at a glance, with no squinting.
- The code HAYRIDE is shown in plain text, and there's a barcode at the bottom for the store.
- "CLAIM 20% OFF" is a big black button that's easy to tap.
- The "Shop by Size" block with wide width and wide calf options is useful. Wide is what I need in a comfort shoe.
- The in-store pickup, price matching, and shoe repair icons feel like a real store I could walk into.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is women's. Nothing is cushioned, slip-on, or walking-oriented, and the fur-fringe boots are the opposite of my taste.
- "Final call" is the fourth deadline message today, so it feels like crying wolf.
- The "SHOP MEN" link is a small underlined line, easy to miss with my thumb.
- The tiny legal text at the bottom is hard to read, and the exclusions are long. Skechers is listed as a qualifying brand, but I'd have to dig to learn that.
- The adidas banner at the top competes with the 20% offer, so I don't know which deal I'm supposed to follow.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send one deadline email, not four. Pick the last one and stop.
- 2. Show men's comfort shoes, such as slip-ons, walking shoes, and cushioned sneakers from Skechers, Hoka, and Brooks, for male subscribers. Show the wide-width option with them.
- 3. Make "SHOP MEN" a real button, not a text link.
- 4. Put the exclusions and qualifying brands in plain sight above the fold, in larger type.
- 5. Remove the adidas banner so there is one offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly sound (Braze/SendGrid-style tracked links, dark-mode CSS, web-font fallbacks). It has one failing CTA link (HTTP 400) on the SMS opt-in, an extremely URL-heavy plain-text part, and several accessibility and markup defects in the `<head>` and images. The HTML source was truncated, so the body and footer could not be fully audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The link "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **HTTP 400**.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/qeiDCa_6aEjI9iMXRWOocw~~/AAQRxRA~/C0-wseLK4-…`
  - The 400 comes from the click-tracking redirect (`ablink.dsw.com`), not the final destination. The tracked token may be malformed or expired, or the endpoint may reject the QA probe. Either way, it needs verification with a real client, because it is the SMS acquisition CTA.
- **Plain-text part:** 26,618 of 31,100 characters (~86%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long `ablink.dsw.com` wrapped URLs cause this, and it is a spam-filter risk for the text/plain alternative.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 69 links were probed and 57 were skipped. Footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (2):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/qQj3VITYitzuRIiuKmDd3w~~/AAQRxRA~/8CBd3wt7FloRRB_UDunKGy9i_rFsJjfFUkSz5cUnj39LmfFi08…`. If this is a content image, screen readers get no description. If it is a tracker, it needs `alt=""`.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=…`. This is a tracking pixel and should have `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`/`xml:lang` attribute, so screen readers cannot pick the right pronunciation.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. The charset declaration relies on the HTTP/MIME header instead.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>DSW</title>` is the same for every send. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=225090073` value in the Movable Ink pixel is a rendered recipient ID. Personalization in the truncated portion is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
No issues found in the available data. The sender is `dsw@e.dsw.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice. The QA probe always includes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address in the footer, were not in the supplied data, so they are unverified rather than confirmed missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters cannot be seen from the source. The final destination and UTM parameters need a redirect-chain check.
- The campaign is identifiable via `mi_ecmp=20261011_P261379_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` (truncated), which suggests an offer-based Movable Ink campaign.
- The one probed failure (the 400 above) means landing alignment for the SMS opt-in CTA is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **High:** Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts" link in a browser and mail client. If it is truly broken, fix or re-issue the tracked link before further sends.
2. **Medium:** Shorten or reduce the URL load in the plain-text part. Use fewer wrapped links, or a more compact tracking-link format, to get under 70% URL content.
3. **Medium:** Add `alt` text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to `o.gif`.
4. **Medium:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. **Low:** Fix the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Make `<title>` campaign-specific.
6. **Verification gap:** Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers to check UTMs, the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
