# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 4/10 (prev 4/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**
## Full review
---

## 1. Executive Summary

Walked through Skechers on my phone today. The homepage grabbed my attention right away — Hands Free Slip-ins front and center, BOGO deal in the banner, a guy my age on screen. Getting to the Men's Slip-ins section was actually pretty painless. Found a nice-looking shoe, price was fair, deal was clearly shown. Then everything fell apart. The cart page loaded completely blank — nothing on screen. Couldn't see what was in my cart. And when I searched "slip in," the first results shown were Women's shoes. I'm a Plus member who logged in, and the site still showed me women's shoes. Same score as yesterday. Nothing got better, the cart problem is still breaking the purchase.

## 2. Business Impact Score: 4/10

*(No change from yesterday's 4/10 — no regressions, but no improvements either. Cart failure is still killing the purchase.)*

---

## 3. What's Working

- **BOGO deal is impossible to miss.** Top blue banner, on every page. I saw "Buy 1, Get 1 50% OFF" on the product page too. That's smart — it nudges me toward buying two pairs.
- **Slip-ins are prominently featured on the homepage.** The "HANDS FREE Slip-ins" block with big bold text was right below the hero image. I didn't have to hunt for my category.
- **Men's menu is clean and senior-friendly.** Large text, full-width tap targets, "Hands Free Slip-ins" listed as a direct shortcut below the main categories. I didn't need reading glasses to navigate that menu.
- **Product listing page (272 results) is straightforward.** Fit filters (Wide, Extra Wide, Medium) right up top — that matters to me. Grid layout with clear product photos.
- **Product detail page price and deal are readable.** $105.00 in large text, the BOGO reminder right below. I knew what I was paying before I scrolled.
- **ADD TO CART button is excellent.** Full-width, large, bold blue. Easy to tap even with older fingers.
- **Size picker grid is well-sized.** Big boxes, easy to tap, covered 7.0 through 14.0. No tiny tap targets.

---

## 4. What's Weak

- **Cart page is completely blank.** I tapped the cart and got a white screen. Nothing. No products, no totals, no buttons. I can't check out. This is the single biggest problem on the entire site and it's a showstopper.
- **No login confirmation visible anywhere.** I went through a login step but the screenshots show no "Welcome back, Walker" or name in the header. The account icon looks the same as a guest. I don't know if I'm actually logged in. For a loyalty member this feels disrespectful.
- **Search "slip in" surfaces Women's shoes first.** I searched as (presumably) a logged-in male customer and the first two products I see are labeled "Women's." There are 1,025 results — I shouldn't have to scroll past women's items to find men's slip-ins.
- **Product has zero reviews.** "No Reviews" with an empty star row does not give me confidence. At 62, I rely heavily on other people's experience before dropping $105. A product page with no social proof makes me hesitant.
- **No personalization visible anywhere.** As a Skechers Plus member, I expected to see my points balance, a member-specific deal, or even just my name. I got the same generic experience a brand-new visitor would get.
- **"Please select a size" error is shown on the Add to Cart screenshot.** The system flagged this before I even tried to add — meaning the script tried to add without a size selected. That's a test/automation artifact, but it's worth noting the error message itself is small and easy to miss for real shoppers.
- **Popup step showed no actual popup.** Step 2 just shows a different promo banner — the "Get Mom a Gift" text swapped in. If there was a popup to dismiss, it either didn't appear or self-dismissed. Either way, Mother's Day messaging is not relevant to me.

---

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page — this is P0.** A completely blank cart screen means zero revenue conversion. Every single add-to-cart action goes nowhere. This needs to be the first thing engineering looks at Monday morning.

2. **Gender-filter search results for logged-in users.** I'm a male customer who just browsed Men's > Shoes. When I type "slip in," show me men's results first. The data to do this is already there.

3. **Show logged-in state clearly.** Put my first name or "Hi, Walker" in the header. Show my Plus points balance. Remind me of my member benefits. Make me feel like a loyal customer, not a stranger.

4. **Push reviews onto the product page.** A $105 shoe with zero reviews is a trust problem. Pull in review content from other colorways or similar models, or at minimum surface a "Be the first to review" prompt that builds community. Consider pulling in verified reviews from GO WALK family shoes.

5. **Add a "Men's" or gender context pill to search results.** A single toggle at the top of the 1,025-result search page — "Men | Women | Kids" — would immediately fix the gender-mismatch problem without requiring algorithmic personalization.

---

## 6. Bottom Line

The site gets me from the homepage to the right product pretty well. The navigation is logical, the text is big enough, and the BOGO deal is everywhere I need it to be. But I cannot complete a purchase because the cart is blank. That's a total failure. And I don't feel recognized as a Plus member at any point in this journey. Until those two things are fixed — working cart, personalized experience — this site is leaving loyal customers like me stranded.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

Platform: Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware), evident from `/on/demandware.static/Sites-USSkechers-Site/` paths. The session was partially blocked by Kasada bot-protection, which inflates error counts and makes the View Cart page data unreliable. 16 axe violations across 3 steps; the View Cart page shows signs of rendering as a bare HTML fragment (no `<title>`, no `lang`). Third-party script surface is unusually large.

## Accessibility

**Homepage — 9 violations**

| Severity | Rule | Root cause |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | `aria-required-parent` | ARIA role used without its required ancestor container (e.g. `role="option"` outside `role="listbox"`) |
| Serious | `link-name` | Icon-only links with no `aria-label` or visually-hidden text |
| Moderate | `landmark-main-is-top-level` + `landmark-no-duplicate-main` | Two `<main>` elements, one nested inside another landmark — likely a carousel or modal overlay injected by Monetate/personalization layer |
| Moderate | `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` | `<aside>` nested inside a sectioning element rather than at the document root |
| Moderate | `heading-order` | H-level skipped (e.g. H1 → H3), typical of component-level heading choices that aren't coordinated globally |
| Moderate | `landmark-unique` + `region` | Multiple `<nav>` or `<section>` landmarks without distinguishing labels; content outside any landmark |
| Minor | `aria-allowed-role` | `role` applied to an element that doesn't support it (e.g. `role="button"` on a `<div>` that is already interactive) |

**View Cart — 4 violations (structural)**

| Severity | Rule | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Serious | `document-title` | `<title>` is empty or absent — screen readers announce nothing on page load |
| Serious | `html-has-lang` | No `lang` attribute — screen readers can't select the correct speech profile |
| Moderate | `landmark-one-main` | No `<main>` element — keyboard users can't skip to content |
| Moderate | `page-has-heading-one` | No `<h1>` — document outline is undefined |

These four together indicate the cart is either rendering inside an `<iframe>` without its own HTML shell, or the Kasada 429 caused a fallback/error page to load that bypasses the standard template. Needs verification in a non-bot-flagged session.

**Search "slip in" — 3 violations**

Repeated `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, and `region` — same pattern as homepage, likely from the same shared sidebar/filter component.

## SEO

- **View Cart `<title>` missing** — Googlebot will index the page with no title signal. Even if the cart is `noindex`, the missing title is a crawl signal problem.
- **View Cart `html[lang]` missing** — affects hreflang resolution for international variants.
- **Placeholder GA4 property `G-1234567890`** is firing in production (visible in Homepage and Log In HTML source). This is a dummy/test measurement ID; if GTM is sending hits to it those events are silently dropped and attribution data is lost.
- **Heading hierarchy gaps** on homepage — affects document outline that search engines use to weight topical relevance.

## Mobile Optimization

- `--vh: 6.640000000000001px` CSS custom property is set via JS — this is the standard mobile Safari `100vh` fix (window.innerHeight / 100). The floating-point noise (`6.640000000000001` vs `6.64`) is harmless but indicates the calculation is running on every resize without rounding.
- `vice-managed-src` attribute on several `<script>` tags is TrustArc's consent-gated deferred loading pattern — correct for GDPR/CCPA compliance, but these scripts still occupy DOM nodes and GTM still fires its own copies unconditionally alongside them.
- No responsive image (`srcset`/`<picture>`) evidence in the truncated source — cannot confirm without full product image markup.

## Console & Network Errors

**429 Rate Limiting — 6 occurrences**

All six 429 errors target:
```
https://www.skechers.com/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/
  2d206a39-8ed7-437e-a3be-862e0f06eea3/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.386
```

The UUID path pattern and `x-kpsdk-v` query parameter identify this as **Kasada's client-side fingerprinting SDK** (`kpsdk` = Kasada SDK). The `fp` endpoint is the fingerprint submission call. The audit browser session was flagged as automated and Kasada throttled/blocked it — hence also the 429 on `https://www.skechers.com/cart/`. These errors will not appear for real users but they do mean:

1. The View Cart data in this audit (missing title, lang, etc.) may reflect a Kasada challenge/error page, not the real cart template.
2. Any automated monitoring (synthetic tests, uptime checks) using headless Chrome will hit this same wall unless Kasada exemptions are configured.

**CORS — 1 occurrence**

```
Access to CSS stylesheet at 'https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css
  ?base=hotspots&theme=skechers&version=a633fea7cb'
has been blocked by CORS policy: No 'Access-Control-Allow-Origin' header
```

Stylitics's CDN is not returning `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://www.skechers.com` for this stylesheet. The Stylitics outfit widget will render without its theme styles. This is a Stylitics-side configuration issue (their CDN needs to whitelist skechers.com's origin on that asset path).

## Recommendations

**P0 — Fix or verify**
1. **Confirm View Cart HTML shell** — run the cart audit from a session that passes Kasada (logged-in, non-headless). If `<title>` and `html[lang]` are genuinely missing from the live cart template, fix them immediately — these are blocking accessibility and SEO failures.
2. **Remove `G-1234567890` from GTM/production config** — a placeholder GA4 measurement ID firing in production silently discards analytics events.

**P1 — High impact**
3. **Fix Kasada synthetic-test exemption** — configure an exemption token or trusted-IP bypass in Kasada for your own monitoring/synthetic-test user agents so that automated quality checks get real page responses, not challenge pages.
4. **Fix Stylitics CORS** — contact Stylitics support to add `https://www.skechers.com` to the allowed origins for `web-assets.stylitics.com`. Until then the outfit styling feature is broken for all users.
5. **Deduplicate Yahoo YTC and Creativecdn tags** — two identical script injections per page are wasted network and parse cycles. Audit GTM and the TrustArc container for duplicate tag entries.
6. **Fix `aria-required-parent` (critical)** — locate the component using the orphaned ARIA role (likely a search autocomplete or carousel) and wrap it in the correct parent role container.
7. **Add `aria-label` to icon-only links** — the serious `link-name` violation means screen-reader users encounter unlabeled navigation controls.

**P2 — Moderate**
8. **Collect LCP and CLS** — the audit has no LCP or CLS data for any step. Instrument with real-user monitoring (Quantum Metric is already installed) or add CrUX/PageSpeed API calls to the audit pipeline to get actual CWV values before drawing performance conclusions.
9. **Audit landmark structure** — the duplicate `<main>` and nested `<main>`/`<aside>` pattern is consistent across steps, suggesting a shared layout component is emitting incorrect landmark nesting. Fix at the template level; it will resolve violations across all pages simultaneously.
10. **View Cart TTFB** — 125 ms vs. 24–29 ms site-wide warrants investigation independent of Kasada. Confirm whether cart endpoints bypass CDN caching or have an upstream latency issue.
