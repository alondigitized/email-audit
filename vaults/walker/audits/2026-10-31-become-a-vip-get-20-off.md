# Become a VIP & get 20% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the eleventh send or so in five days, and the same VIP/FABULOUS 20% pitch I got yesterday in "Become a VIP today & get 20% off!" and "Want 20% off? Become a VIP (it's free!)." Same subject as the one from the 29th, too. The giant "20% OFF*" is hard to miss, but I've seen it so many times it just blurs together.
- The big hero is a pink sneaker and a ballet flat with "DAY" in coral. It's a leftover Halloween pun ("It doesn't have to be Halloween for you to dress like a ballerina"). It's women's shoes, and I'm a 62-year-old man looking for cushioned slip-ons. The "SHOP MEN" link is a small underlined line way up top, and nothing below the fold is for me. I wouldn't click. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The 20% off is the first thing I see, in huge type, with the code FABULOUS right under it.
- "BECOME A VIP" is a big black button, and I know what it does.
- The size picker, including wide width, is handy. I wish it were up top.
- The "Price Matching" and "Expert Shoe Repair" icons make it feel like a store I can trust.
- The text is large enough to read on my phone without zooming.

## What's weak

- Nearly the whole email is women's shoes: ballet flats, boots, heels. Men's gets one small link.
- The hero's "DAY" looks cut off, and I can't tell what the word is supposed to say. The image tells me nothing about the offer.
- The asterisk and fine print hide the catch. The deal is regular-price only, and the brand exclusions include Skechers, which is one of my main brands.
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support, or slip-ons.
- It's the same VIP pitch for the third day running, so the urgency feels fake.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same VIP pitch daily. Cap it at one or two a week.
- 2. Use my purchase history. Show men's walking and slip-on shoes in the hero instead of ballet flats.
- 3. Put the Skechers exclusion up near the offer, not buried at the bottom.
- 4. Fix the hero. Show the whole word and a real product.
- 5. Add a men's row (comfort, wide width, slip-on) alongside Boots, Active and Heels.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience flag: the SMS opt-in CTA's tracked link returned HTTP 400. Link coverage is thin (12 of 70 links probed), and the accessibility markup has gaps. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned 400.**
  - Evidence: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZeqyB1-I1r-m9X9h7su26w~~/AAQRxRA~/6lNf4hjg89P-…` (wrapped Braze click-tracking URL).
  - Caveat: the URL printed in the QA output ends abruptly (`…tuwNb`), and the CTA's visible text is empty in the report. The 400 may come from the probe truncating or malforming the URL, or from the tracker rejecting a non-browser request. It needs a manual click-through before it's treated as a live break.
  - If it is live, this is the main conversion path for the SMS opt-in.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 23,066 of 27,323 characters (84%) are tracking URLs, so the text alternative is effectively unreadable. It is also a mild spam-filter signal.
- **Low probe coverage.** 58 of 70 links were skipped (footer, utility, social), so 12 were probed and their status can't be confirmed from this data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Three images have no `alt` attribute:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/5vcxb-6jGIPJcoPwvW0iXQ~~/…` is a Braze open pixel.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261031_P261624_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` is a tracking pixel.
  - `https://braze-images.com/…/6ada210634628d00a82400d4/original.png` is unidentified, and may be a content image.
  - The two pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. The `original.png` image needs real alt text if it carries content.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on mobile.
- **Charset and compatibility metas are missing `http-equiv`.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. Charset still comes from the Content-Type header, so the impact is low.
- **Web fonts have no visible fallbacks.** The `@font-face` fonts (Futura_PT_*, HelveticaNow_*) are served as WOFF/TTF from braze-images.com. Most clients ignore them. I couldn't see the inline `font-family` stacks in the truncated source, so I can't confirm fallbacks exist.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML and QA data show no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA notes say it's expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `e.dsw.com` is unverified. This is a gap in the test setup, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe unverified.** The QA data reports no unsubscribe link result, and the footer is cut off in the provided source. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not supplied, so one-click compliance can't be assessed.
- **Physical address unverified.** The postal address is not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be audited.** All destinations sit behind opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the probe results don't expose final landing URLs or UTM values. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261031_P261624_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` on the pixel.
- **Offer values differ.** The subject promises "20% off" for VIP, while the CTA says "Get 15% Off" for text opt-in. These may be separate offers, but the landing page for the broken link should be checked for consistency.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Click the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZeqyB1-…` SMS opt-in link in a real browser. If it still fails, fix or regenerate it in Braze before further sends.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels and meaningful alt text to `original.png` if it is a content image.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Shorten the plain-text part or use fewer tracked URLs there.
5. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to the charset meta, or drop it.
6. Re-run the audit with full headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and the untruncated HTML. Also expand link probing to cover the unsubscribe and primary CTAs, and capture the final redirect URLs to verify UTMs.
