- The Bondi 9 and Bondi SR grid is big and easy to see. That's the cushioned, all-day walking shoe I want.
- "Go all day" and "Made for the long haul" speak to comfort and walking, not racing.
- The headlines are large and black on white. I can read them on my phone without squinting.
- The shoe quiz at the bottom helps me pick a fit without guessing.
- The "Shop Now" button is wide and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The hero shows young runners with the ID.me logo over their faces. It's not my age, my shoe or my kind of walking.
- The only dollar figure, 20% off, sits halfway down the email.
- "See if You Qualify" and "Verify with ID.me" send me through a sign-up step before I can see any savings. If I don't qualify, I get nothing.
- The Bondi grid has no price, no cushioning or arch-support detail, and no reviews. I'm left guessing what each shoe costs.
- The fine print is tiny and gray on black, and I can't read it. It says the offer ends 11/16, but that date isn't in the headline.
- The bottom links ("Locate a Store", "Unlock Free Shipping", "FLY for 30 Guarantee") are small, and they're the only trust cues.

- 2. Send non-ID.me people to a general offer, or at least tell us why this email reached us. I'm 62 and I'm not the target here.
- 3. Add a price and one comfort line under each shoe, like "max cushion, wide sizes", and a star rating.
- 4. Make the "ends 11/16" deadline big and visible.
- 5. Make the footer links and fine print larger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed tracking links (11 returning 406, the unsubscribe returning 403) failed QA, and the plain-text part is empty. Because every probe failed, including the unsubscribe, the failures look like the tracker rejecting the QA probe rather than the links being dead. The source also has accessibility defects (missing alt text, zoom lock, empty `<title>`), template-variable leakage in the CSS, and inconsistent UTM construction.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12/12 probed links failed.** All are Cordial redirects on `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…:1/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`.
  - 406: logo ("HOKA"), `US_20261111_IDme_a/c/d/f.jpg`, both Bondi 9 tiles, both Bondi SR tiles, `US_20261109_TripleBlackStyles_d.jpg`, and `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`.
  - 403: Unsubscribe.
- **Likely a probe artifact, not confirmed breakage.** A uniform 406/403 across unrelated destinations points to bot or header filtering (406 is "Not Acceptable") on the tracking domain. The QA data cannot show whether end users hit errors. The final destination pages were never validated, since the probe stopped at the redirector.
- **Duplicate and conflicting params on one link.** The `IDme_f` link decodes (jwtP `linkUrl`) to `/en/us/discount-programs/?utm_source=retention&utm_medium=email&utm_content=militaryveteransidme&content=militaryveteransidme&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=…`. `utm_source` appears twice with different values (`retention`, then `US_Broadcast`), and `utm_medium` and `content` are also duplicated.
- **Stale module reuse.** The `US_20261109_TripleBlackStyles_d.jpg` link carries a different `mtlID` (`6aece78e…`) from the rest (`6aeb7bf2…`), and its filename is dated 20261109. Its destination is `discount-programs/` rather than anything Triple Black-related.
- **Two open-tracking pixels from different hosts.** One is `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and the other is `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. This is likely ESP-level plus MTA-level injection. It's harmless but redundant.
- **Asset naming mismatch.** `CA_BondiSR_BottomLeft_1110521-BBLC.jpg` has a `CA_` prefix but links to `/en/us/…`, so it may be a Canadian asset in a US send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text missing on 13 images.** Eleven are content images: the IDme hero/modules, the Bondi 9 and Bondi SR tiles, the TripleBlack banner, `US_Q3_ShoeFinder_Blue.jpg`, and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. Most are the only content inside their `<a>`, so screen readers get the filename or URL as the link name (the QA shows link text `[image: …jpg]`). The other two are the tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""`.
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters.** There is no `text/plain` alternative.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing.
- **Zoom disabled.** Both viewport tags set `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). The two tags also conflict on `user-scalable`, since the second sets `user-scalable=yes`.
- **Broken CSS from unpopulated template variables.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` are invalid declarations.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the markup, such as fallback font stacks or `role="presentation"` on layout tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `{{ }}`-style tokens appear in the visible source.
- The empty `px` values above show variables rendering as blank in the template.
- Per-recipient `hmail=` and `cordial_id=` are populated in the link URLs.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unsubscribe link present.** It resolves via the tracker to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`. The probe got 403, so one-click function is unverified. Re-test with browser-like headers.
- **Authentication unverifiable.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, and QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay. This is a capture limitation, not a confirmed SPF/DKIM failure. The From domain `emails.hoka.com` matches the tracking subdomains `e.` and `mg.`.
- **Not assessable from the data:** `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address in the footer (HTML truncated).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM structure is non-standard.**
  - `utm_source=US_Broadcast` is a send-type label, not a source.
  - The content tag is the bare `content=militaryveteransidme`, and `utm_content` is present only on the `IDme_f` link.
  - `utm_campaign` is a ~150-character string (`H_US_BR_20261111_IDme_IDmePromo_STO_A90Day_…_WishlistAdd`) that encodes audience segments, which makes reporting brittle.
- **The `IDme_f` source conflict (section 2) will misattribute that link's sessions** to `retention` or `US_Broadcast`, depending on the analytics tool's parsing.
- **Product links use `dwvar_<pid>_color` and `dwvar_<pid>_width`** to preselect variants. That is good practice, but the destinations were not verified because probes ended at the redirector.
- 14 links were skipped by QA, so continuity for those is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe with a browser-like `User-Agent` and `Accept` headers and follow redirects to the final URLs. Treat the 406/403 as unconfirmed until then, and verify the unsubscribe link specifically.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all linked images, `alt=""` to the tracking pixels, and a descriptive `<title>`.
3. Generate a `text/plain` part.
4. Fix the charset to `<meta charset="utf-8">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the duplicate viewport tag.
5. Remove or populate the empty `px` CSS rules in the template build.
6. Deduplicate the UTM params and standardize on `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content`. Shorten `utm_campaign`.
7. Confirm the `TripleBlackStyles` and `CA_` assets are intentional in this send.
8. Capture the raw headers (Authentication-Results, List-Unsubscribe) directly from the received message to complete the compliance check.
