## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no automated failures (88% pass, 0 issues, 1 warning). Technical debt is visible in the source: leftover template placeholders, invalid `@font-face` and meta markup, three images without alt text, and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so body links, footer and headers could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text bloat:** QA flags `12670/14694` characters (about 86%) of the plain-text part as URLs. This is consistent with long redirect-wrapped links on `link.email.newbalance.com`. It can hurt spam scoring and makes the text version hard to read.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 42 links were probed and 30 were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). Those 30 are unverified, not confirmed healthy.
- **Non-HTTPS placeholder:** `.bgwhite { background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png'); }` is an unresolved placeholder URL over plain HTTP. It will 404 and could trigger mixed-content warnings wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.
- **Unverified tracking pixel:** the open pixel `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12361731_4857309_23308196.gif` has no alt attribute (see §3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking GIF `.../mo/egR1f966kc_..._23308196.gif`. This one should use `alt=""`.
- **Invalid `@font-face` rules:** the declarations (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, etc.) have no `src` descriptor, and some give a font-family list (`font-family: proxima-nova,sans-serif`). They are ignored, so clients fall back to Arial or Times New Roman. One rule contains a duplicate `font-family` inside `font-style: italic;font-family: ...`.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an `xmlns` attribute and XHTML-style self-closing tags.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `.bg1` is defined twice, first as `#ffffff` and then as `#58546d`. The later rule wins, which is probably unintended.
- **Dark-mode contrast:** the `prefers-color-scheme: dark` rule sets `a {color:#cf0a2c !important}` on a `#151415` background. That is about 3.3:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimum for normal-size link text.
- **Template leftovers:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is unchanged. The `myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/` path in the shipping assets shows they were reused from a welcome-email template.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or subject, but the truncated body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found. QA always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure. No header data (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) was supplied, so those could not be assessed. The footer and physical address were outside the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. No UTM parameters or landing-page results were provided. Links are wrapped in ESP click tracking on `link.email.newbalance.com`, so destination URLs and UTM presence can't be confirmed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder or remove the `.bgwhite` background-image rule.
2. Add `alt` text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Generate a cleaner plain-text part with shorter URLs, or fewer links, to bring the URL ratio down.
4. Fix `@font-face` blocks by adding `src` or removing them, and correct the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove the duplicate `.bg1` rule and set a real `<title>`, such as the subject line.
6. Lighten the dark-mode link color to reach at least 4.5:1 against `#151415`.
7. Rerun QA on the full HTML plus raw headers, and probe the 30 skipped links, to cover compliance, tokens and UTM checks.
