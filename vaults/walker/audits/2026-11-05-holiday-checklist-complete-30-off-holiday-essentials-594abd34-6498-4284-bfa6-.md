# Holiday checklist complete ✅ 30% off holiday essentials
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- Skechers has finally sent me something that isn't a cart reminder. After five "Your cart is waiting" emails in a week, when I haven't put a thing in a cart, this one at least has a real offer. "Hi, Walker" and "1000 points, enough for a $5 reward" at the top is the first time in days they've treated me like a customer.
- The layout is a mess of two discounts, though. The big headline says Members take 20% off sitewide. Then halfway down there's a separate banner for 30% off boots, outdoor and slippers. The subject line says 30%, but the biggest number in the email is 20%. I had to stop and work out which one applied to me. The fine print then says the 20% is "select styles," not sitewide. I don't like that.
- Would I click? Probably the Slip-Ins tile, since that's the hands-free shoe I care about. I wouldn't forward it. It's an okay send, but it's mostly holiday gift wrap and not much about comfort.

## What's working

- "Hi, Walker" with the Member tag and 1000 points is a nice touch. It looks like they know me.
- The 20% and 30% numbers are huge and easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- Slip-Ins is the first tile in the grid. That's the hands-free comfort I shop for.
- The four green buttons (Slip-Ins, Cozy Fit, Best Sellers, New Arrivals) are big and easy to tap.
- The boot and slipper photos look warm and seasonal. It feels like November, not leftover Halloween.

## What's weak

- The subject says 30% off, but the hero says 20% off sitewide. Two offers with two different deadlines and conditions confuse me.
- "Sitewide" is followed by an asterisk and "select styles." That's not honest, and it makes me trust the offer less.
- The checklist is Slip-Ins, Cozy Fit, Arcade and Hotshots. I don't know what Arcade and Hotshots are, and the email never says.
- The hero is gift boxes and a checklist. It doesn't tell me anything about cushioning, arch support or walking comfort.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and I couldn't read it without zooming. The dates and exclusions are all in there.
- The shoes shown look like women's boots and slippers. The only men's shoe is the brown boot in the bottom corner, and men's is one more link in a list further down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and lead with it. If it's 30% on boots, put that in the hero and make the subject match.
- 2. Say which styles are included right under the number. "Sitewide" shouldn't need a footnote.
- 3. Show a men's slip-in on a man my age, with one line on cushioning and arch support. Add a few reviews or star ratings.
- 4. Move Men up in the nav and make the fine print larger.
- 5. Skip the cart reminders for a week after a send like this. I've had five.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: "Holiday checklist complete ✅ 30% off holiday essentials"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect, an empty greeting slot ("Hi ,"). The markup also has a protocol-less tracking pixel URL, malformed `<meta>` tags and two non-HTTPS images. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links were not available to check.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Protocol-less pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Email clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Return Path/Validity seed tracking never fires. It also has no `alt`.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11052026` (QA WARN). It is 1×1, so clients that block mixed content will drop it, and that loses the measurement.
- **Unprobed links:** QA skipped 33 tracking links (`click.emails.skechers.com` redirects) because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect health are unverified.
- **Third-party beacons:** four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels, plus the open pixel at `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`, sit inside `display:none` containers. The two `1x1_usermatch.gif` calls pass `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00`, a 64-hex value consistent with a SHA-256 hash of the recipient's email address. Third-party matching on hashed PII is a privacy and consent surface worth flagging.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration is valid. The charset is not actually declared in the head.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):**
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`) is hosted on the brand's own image domain and is likely a content image.
  - `o.gif` is a tracking pixel.
  - The `krxd` and Return Path pixels in the source also have no `alt`, but they are hidden.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `dde00662-….png` loads over `http://`, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. The image host does support HTTPS.
- **Zoom restrictions:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` in `*` both restrict user text scaling and pinch zoom.
- **Table semantics:** the visible layout tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Language:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," with the first-name field empty. The send went out with no fallback value for the missing name. SFMC's `%%FirstName%%` needs a default via `IIF(Empty(…), "there", …)` or similar.
- No other unresolved tokens were identified in the available source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unverifiable. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is not evidence of a sender-side failure.
- **Unverified:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the truncated source or the QA data. I can't confirm or deny compliance on these.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address. This is not a violation, but it gives recipients no reply path.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters are unverifiable.** The campaign identifier `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11052026` appears in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixel URLs, but no `utm_*` values were visible. All CTAs are wrapped in click-redirect URLs (33 skipped by QA).
- **Landing page alignment** could not be assessed, because no destination URLs were resolved.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting fallback** so an empty first name falls back to a default or drops the greeting entirely. This is the only confirmed rendering failure.
2. **Add the scheme to the Return Path pixel**: `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. **Serve `ink1000.com/o.gif` and `dde00662-….png` over HTTPS**, and add `alt` to the content image (`alt=""` if it is decorative).
4. **Correct the meta tags** to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. **Add `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"`** to the layout tables, and remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` unless there is a specific reason for them.
6. **Review the SHA-256 email hash passed to `beacon.krxd.net`** against the privacy policy and consent basis.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers** so the footer, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, link destinations and UTM parameters can be checked.
