# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Same shoe. I said this on 9/17 and I'm saying it again on 9/28 — that pink floral D'Lites "Spring Bloom" is still sitting in some cart that isn't mine, front and center under "READY TO CHECK OUT?" It's the fourth or fifth time this exact reminder has landed in my inbox this month, and my answer hasn't changed: no, I didn't forget anything, because I never put a women's floral sneaker in a cart in the first place.
- Buried down past the shoe grid there's actually something I'd look at — 20% off boots and outdoor styles, valid through today. That's real, timely, and in my lane heading into fall. Problem is it's the sixth item on the page, after two full rows of shoes I have zero interest in.
- I'm not clicking the hero CTA — it's not my order to complete. I might scroll to the boots banner if I'm already in the email, but honestly this reads like a templated blast, not something Skechers sent because they know me. Marking as read, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The 20% off boots & outdoor styles banner is a real, dated offer (thru 9/28) and it's actually relevant to what I'd shop for this time of year.
- One men's item — the Slip-ins Relaxed Fit: Altman "Murdock" — made it into the grid, so I'm not totally invisible in this send.
- Layout's clean, no broken images or overlapping text, and the CTAs are easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- "Shop now, pay later" and "Find a Store" icons at the bottom are useful if I were actually in buying mode.

## What's weak

- The hero product is, again, a pink floral women's D'Lites — I've flagged this exact shoe chasing me since 9/17, and it's still not mine.
- No arch support, cushioning, or hands-free slip-on messaging anywhere near the top — the stuff that actually gets me to click.
- This is a cart-abandonment reminder for a cart I don't have; it doesn't feel personalized, it feels like a mis-fire.
- The one offer I'd actually care about (boots, 20% off) is stranded near the bottom under a wall of "SHOP ADDITIONAL STYLES" links.
- No loyalty/member pricing shown even though I've got a Skechers account.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending me cart reminders for a cart that isn't mine — if the system can't confirm the shopper's gender/category history matches the abandoned item, don't send the "forgot something" template at all.
- 2. Move the 20% off boots & outdoor offer to the top of the email — that's the one thing in here with a shot at my wallet.
- 3. Swap the hero image for something in men's comfort/outdoor if this is going to a male subscriber — Relaxed Fit or an arch-fit slip-on, not a floral D'Lites.
- 4. Add a line of comfort-focused copy near the top: cushioned, arch support, slip-on — that's what actually gets me past the subject line.
