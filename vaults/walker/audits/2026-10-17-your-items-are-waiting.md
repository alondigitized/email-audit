# Your Items Are Waiting!

## Executive summary

- This is the same cart nag again, and the pink flower sneaker is back. I never put the D'Lites "Spring Bloom" in a cart, and it's a women's shoe. "We noticed you noticing us..." is a little creepy. I haven't been noticing anything. This is the fourth one like it this week, after "Your cart expires soon!" and "Did you forget something?"
- The bottom half is a bit better. There are some slip-ins in the "more great options" grid, and I like the 20% off for members line. But the offer sits well below the fold, under a shoe that isn't mine. The grid is mostly women's and boys' shoes, with one pair of men's waterproof boots.
- I wouldn't click. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it either.

## What's working

- The big navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is easy to see and easy to tap with a thumb.
- "Members take 20% off select full-priced styles" is a real offer. It's clearly stated, with a date (valid thru 10/21).
- The Slip-ins: Arch Fit Arcade shoes in the grid are the hands-free, arch-support kind of thing I look for.
- The Men / Women / Kids / Sale list is large and simple to tap on a phone.
- The sender is Skechers, which I know, and the logo is clear at the top.

## What's weak

- The hero is the pink flower women's sneaker I never carted. It's wrong for me, and this is the same shoe from the last few cart emails.
- "We noticed you noticing us..." feels like being watched, and it doesn't match anything I did.
- The 20% off is buried halfway down. The top of the email never mentions a deal.
- The product grid is mostly women's and kids' shoes. Only the Tanzier boots are for a man like me, and they're a John Deere work boot, not a casual walker.
- The fine print under the footer is tiny. I'd need to zoom to read the exclusions on the 20% off.
- Texting, app, Afterpay and Klarna blocks pile on at the bottom. It's too much for a cart reminder.

## Recommendations

- 1. Only send a cart email if there's something actually in the cart. Show a men's item, or the real item, or send nothing.
- 2. Move the 20% member offer up into the hero, right under the headline, so the discount is the first thing I see.
- 3. Pick the grid shoes from what I browse: men's slip-ins, walking shoes, arch-support styles. Leave out the kids' shoes.
- 4. Cut the cart email frequency. Four near-identical sends in a week looks desperate.
- 5. Make the fine print bigger, or link it clearly.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Abandoned-cart email sent through Attentive (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com`). It has a failing unsubscribe link probe, no plain-text part, a malformed charset declaration, and many `http://` image sources. The QA pass rate is 62%.

  - The token is tied to a session and recipient, so the 400 may come from the automated probe and not from real clicks. It still needs a manual check, because a dead unsubscribe link is a compliance risk.
- **Two probes got 429 and are unverified.** These are the "Complete you Order" CTA and the "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting..." link, both on `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`.
  - The 429s come from the Attentive redirector and don't prove the links are broken. The primary cart CTA's destination was not confirmed.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 links were probed and 26 were skipped, mostly footer and social links.
- **All links are redirect-wrapped** through `/e/c/<opaque token>`. The final destinations can't be inspected from the source.
- **Tracking pixel is present** at `https://skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...`. It is served over HTTPS and is not an issue by itself.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed-content images.** 13 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`.
  - They include the logo (`dde00662-...png`), the hero CTA (`dfcc8fd7-...jpg`), the product-recommendation images (`737031d9-...jpg`, `a6ec5198-...jpg`), and the app, social and Afterpay icons in `/m/11/`.
  - Clients that block or upgrade non-HTTPS resources may show broken images. The same host already serves `https://` for `021bd784-...gif`, so switching the rest is likely a one-line fix.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif`
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`
  - The open-tracking pixel. A decorative pixel should have `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Alt-text typo.** The hero CTA image's alt text (and the link text) reads "Complete you Order". It should be "your".
