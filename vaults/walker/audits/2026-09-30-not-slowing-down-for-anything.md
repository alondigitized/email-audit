# Not slowing down for anything
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Well, "Full speed" and a pack of dirt bikes kicking up dust. That's a motorcycle ad, not a shoe ad, and it took me a second to find the shoe. I'm 62. I'm not chasing anybody across a dirt lot. After a week of HOKA sending me trail runners, race packs and a movie trailer, this one feels like more of the same. Yesterday's "Learning to Fly" and last Sunday's trail faves already had me wondering if they remember who I am.
- To be fair, the shoes are easy to see once I scroll. It's four Speedgoat 2 colors in a neat grid, plus a couple of close-ups of the sole and the mesh. But Speedgoat is a trail shoe, and the copy talks about "grip without compromise" and "fast-paced living." I don't see cushioning or arch support mentioned anywhere. There's no price, no sale and no walking. I did like the "Wides go hard" email from 9/28 and the walking one on 9/17. This isn't either of those.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either. My buddy with the bad knees would ask me why I sent him a motocross ad.

## What's working

- The four Speedgoat 2 photos on a clean white background are easy to see and easy to tell apart.
- The "Shop Speedgoat 2" buttons are big, black and rounded. They're easy to tap on my phone.
- The small text blocks like "Vibram® Megagrip outsole" and "Rugged mesh upper" at least tell me something about how the shoe is built.
- The footer lists "Unlock Free Shipping" and "FLY for 30 Guarantee." Those are the kind of things I'd want to know before I order.
- The layout is a simple single column, so I don't have to pinch and zoom.

## What's weak

- The hero is dirt bikes and the headline is "Full speed." That's not a comfort message, and it's not aimed at a man my age.
- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline. I have nothing to compare against Brooks or New Balance.
- The words cushioning, arch support, walking and wide sizes don't appear anywhere.
- The footer perks are small, gray-on-black lines that are easy to miss. The fine print at the very bottom is tiny and hard to read.
- The "Y2K NOT?" section talks about a "high-energy" retro runner. I don't run, and I don't care about Y2K.
- Every button says "Shop Speedgoat 2," so there's really only one thing to click, and it's a trail shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the fit and comfort story. Tell me how the Speedgoat cushions and what the wide sizes are, or put a walking shoe up top for people like me who open these emails.
- 2. Put a real offer or price in the hero. Free shipping and the 30-day guarantee should be up high in big type, not buried in the footer.
- 3. Send different heroes to different customers. I've opened walking and wide-fit emails before, and the send should reflect that.
- 4. Show me a real person wearing the shoe on a sidewalk or a park path instead of motorcycles.
- 5. Give me two different buttons, like "Shop Men's Walking" and "Shop Wide Sizes."
- 6. Make the footer perks bigger and easier to read.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has three confirmed technical failures: no plain-text alternative, two probed links (including Unsubscribe) that returned 403, and 15 images without `alt` text. The 403s are likely bot-blocking of the Cordial click-tracker, but the QA run couldn't confirm the destinations resolve.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Return-start link returns 403.**
  - Link text is "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/", and the URL is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6aa877483f043ed4f70ebb26:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/675c0735?jwtH=…`.
  - The JWT payload (`jwtP`) decodes to `linkUrl=https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260930_ProductSpotlight_Speedgoat2_…`, so the destination is well-formed.
  - The 403 comes from the tracking redirector (`e.emails.hoka.com`), not from a malformed destination. This is probably bot or scanner blocking. Treat it as unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Unsubscribe link returns 403.**
  - Its URL is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/…:1/d607b02e?jwtH=…`, and it decodes to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast…`.
  - The 403 is the same redirector behavior. Someone should confirm manually that it resolves for a real client, because a broken unsubscribe is a compliance risk (see section 5).
- **Two open-tracking pixels.** One is on `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/1664:…/eyJ0eXAi…` (Cordial) and the other on `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/eJyMzUFu…` (a second sender domain). This duplicates open tracking and inflates open counts.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 28 links were probed, so the other 16 links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 15 images.**
  - All 13 hero and product images come from `images.usw2.cordial.com/1664/…`, including `US_20260930_ProductSpotlightSpeedgoat2_a.jpg` through `_h.jpg`, and `US_Speedgoat2_TopLeft_1162710-CYBY.jpg`.
  - The footer logo `LogoFooterPrimary.png` and both tracking pixels are also missing alt text.
  - The stylesheet forces `img {display:block !important}`. With images blocked, the email is unreadable if the content is image-only, as it appears to be.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The `<title></title>` tag has no content.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute, so it isn't a valid charset declaration.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags.**
  - The first has `maximum-scale=1` and the second has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`.
  - `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch zoom in some clients, which hurts accessibility.
- **Invalid CSS.** Examples are `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`. These declarations have no values and are silently dropped, so they are harmless but sloppy.
- **Web fonts.** The `@font-face` rules load "TheFutureHOKA" from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Most clients ignore web fonts, so make sure the font stack has a fallback (not visible in the truncated source).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `hmail` and `cordial_id` values in the decoded link payloads look correctly populated. The source is truncated, so tokens further down could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and it is the one FAIL in the deliverability category.
- **Unsubscribe is present but unverified.** The footer link exists and points to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`, but the probe returned 403 (see section 2). The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided, so one-click unsubscribe can't be assessed.
- **SPF, DKIM and DMARC are not assessable.** No headers were supplied. The sending and link domains (`emails.hoka.com`, `e.emails.hoka.com`, `mg.emails.hoka.com`) share the same parent domain, so branding and alignment look consistent.
- **Physical address not confirmed.** The visible truncated source doesn't show one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present and consistent.** Decoded links carry `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260930_ProductSpotlight_Speedgoat2_A30Day_HighestEngaged_365LifestyleTag_365LifestylePurchasers_365TrailPurchasers_0710Speedgoat2Openers`. The campaign name is long and encodes audience segments, which is fine for reporting.
- **Locale asset mismatch.** `CA_Speedgoat2_BottomRight_1162710-NZB.jpg` (a "CA" asset) sits in a US broadcast next to `US_…` assets. This is likely a wrong-locale image. Confirm which product and locale it links to.
- **No landing-page verification.** The probes stopped at the redirector 403s, so landing-page alignment couldn't be confirmed for the failed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part** to the Cordial template (currently 0 chars).
2. **Manually verify the Unsubscribe link**, and check that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present.
3. **Ask the ESP whether the redirector blocks non-browser user agents.** If it does, treat the two 403s as false positives. If not, fix the click-tracking domain.
4. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to all 13 content images and the footer logo. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
5. **Remove the duplicate tracking pixel** (`mg.emails.hoka.com` versus `e.emails.hoka.com`), or confirm both are intentional.
6. **Fix the head:** set `<title>`, use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, and keep a single viewport tag without `maximum-scale=1`.
7. **Check the `CA_Speedgoat2_BottomRight` image** for the correct locale and destination URL.
8. **Remove the empty-value CSS rules** (`padding-top:px`, `border-top:px solid`).
