# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- So this is a "you left something in your cart" email, and the first thing I notice is... I don't remember putting Care Bears kids' sneakers or hot pink ladies' shoes in any cart. Now, I get it, this is probably tracking somebody's browsing, but if it's mine, whoever's running this list has me mixed up with a grandkid's Christmas list. "You Liked These, And You Weren't Wrong" — cute line, but I didn't like a single thing on this page.
- The only shoe in the whole email that's even in my universe is buried in the second row of thumbnails: "Men's Slip-ins: Delson 3.0 - Roth Shoes." One shoe, no photo bigger than a postage stamp, no mention of arch support or cushioning, nothing. Everything else — Care Bears prints, pink suede, a boy's shoe — is a swing and a miss.
- This is the fourth or fifth Skechers email this week (following the "2 for $100" one yesterday), and unlike that one, this one doesn't even have a discount to pull me in. No percentage off, no dollar amount, just "Complete Your Order." I'm not completing an order for shoes I never looked at. This gets a glance and a mark-as-read — no click, no forward, no thumbs moving today.

## What's working

- The "Complete Your Order" button is big, blue, and easy to find — no confusion about what to tap if I actually wanted to.
- Layout is clean, no overlapping text or broken images that I can see.
- It's clearly Skechers — logo up top, consistent branding all the way down.
- The "Shop All Best Sellers" black banner is a decent visual break in an otherwise long scroll.

## What's weak

- Every product shown — Care Bears sneakers, pink suede shoes, a boy's shoe — is nowhere near a 62-year-old man's closet. This cart clearly isn't mine.
- Zero discount or offer anywhere in the email — after a week of "2 for $100" and "25% off," this one shows up empty-handed.
- The one men's shoe in here is a tiny thumbnail with no callout, no price, nothing to make me stop scrolling.
- No mention of comfort, arch support, or slip-ins in the hero copy — just "you liked these," which, again, I didn't.
- It's a long scroll of grey navigation bars (Shop Women, Men, Kids...) before you even get to the footer — feels like padding.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cart-matching. If I didn't put it in my cart, don't show it to me — that's the single biggest problem here and it undermines the whole "you liked these" premise.
- 2. If there's a men's item in my history, lead with it big, not buried third-row-small next to a boy's shoe.
- 3. Give me a reason to come back — a discount, free shipping, something. An empty cart reminder with no incentive is easy to ignore.
- 4. Cut the giant nav-link list (Women/Men/Kids/Clothing/New Arrivals/Sale) down — it's dead weight before the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Skechers cart expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping if you finish up today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The slip-ins you looked at are almost gone.`
