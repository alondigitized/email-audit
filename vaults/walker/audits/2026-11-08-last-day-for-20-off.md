# LAST DAY for 20% OFF!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the sixth 20% pitch I've gotten since the 5th, so "LAST DAY" doesn't hit me the way it would have a week ago. The big "20% OFF*" on pink is easy to read on my phone, and the TINSEL code is the same one from the 5th, 6th and 7th. After the "ends tomorrow" email yesterday, a "last day" email is at least honest.
- Then I scrolled and found a free butter squishy, a pair of black heels, a fuzzy bootie and a snakeskin sneaker. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for cushioned walking shoes, and there isn't a single one in the new-arrivals grid. The "Shop Men" link is a small underlined line, easy to miss. The "Shop wide width" button is the one nod to my kind of foot.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I happened to need shoes this weekend. I'd probably just skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, because it's all women's product and I'd have nothing to say to a friend about it.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" headline is huge and readable without squinting.
- The black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is hard to miss.
- The deadline is honest this time. It matches the dates in the fine print, Nov 5–8.
- "Shop Men" is at least listed next to Women and Kids in the top hero.
- "Shop wide width" and the size row (5 to 11) are practical, though the sizes only go up to 11.
- The extra 10% for veterans and active military is a nice touch for plenty of folks my age.

## What's weak

- Every product in the "So new, so you" grid is a women's style, mostly heels and fashion boots. Nothing there is for me.
- The size row stops at 11, and men's sizes don't look like they're covered at all.
- The butter squishy promo is a kids' novelty item. It needs a $75 purchase and a VIP signup, so it feels like clutter.
- The legal text at the bottom runs very long and is tiny. The brand exclusions list includes Skechers, so a Skechers fan like me might not get the discount on what I want.
- I've seen the same 20% offer all week, so the urgency feels manufactured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show men's comfort and walking shoes in the product grid, with a sample of cushioning and arch-support brands. Send a men's version to men on the list.
- 2. Make "Shop Men" a real button, not a small link.
- 3. Say in the hero which brands are excluded, so the discount doesn't turn out to be a letdown at checkout.
- 4. Cut the squishy block and the Afterpay and SMS clutter.
- 5. Space the 20% emails out. Six in a week is too many.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off men's walking shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight: 20% off ends, code TINSEL`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Comfort sneakers and slip-ons for men are included. Code TINSEL, ends midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 20% code expires tonight. Shop men's, wide widths and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Braze/SendGrid-style email with dark-mode CSS and web fonts. Automated QA found one broken tracked link (the SMS opt-in CTA), one image without alt text that matters, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The HTML source was truncated, so some categories can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (HTTP 400):** the CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns 400 on `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/3rsd9DT8Ci8JKc78L_g1sQ~~/AAQRxRA~/rHGbTTWv8TnE…`.
  - This is the only FAIL, and it sits on a conversion CTA.
  - A prober's 400 can be bot or user-agent filtering on an SMS-opt-in redirect, so test it in a real mobile client before treating it as a confirmed outage.
  - The QA output shows an empty label after "Broken link (400):", so the error body wasn't captured.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 76 links were probed (64 skipped). Footer, social and utility links are unverified.
- **Tracking image:** `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/IZjnURiVVb5wtwj1Cfg8Jg~~/…` is a tracked image link, so its alt text is functionally the link label (see section 3).
- **Two tracking systems:** `ablink.dsw.com` handles click tracking and `mi.dsw.com/p/up/…/o.gif` is the open pixel. That is normal, but it makes the final destinations opaque to QA.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, tracked image:** the `ablink.dsw.com/q/IZjnURiV…` image has no alt. If it is content or a link, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** the `mi.dsw.com/…/o.gif` pixel has no alt. This is low severity. A 1×1 pixel should have `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. The charset relies on the MIME header instead.
- **Web fonts:** the `.woff`/`.ttf` fonts come from `braze-images.com` and will be ignored by Gmail and Outlook. This is standard fallback behavior and not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA data. The pixel URL carries `mi_u=225090073`, a per-recipient ID, which is expected for tracking.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the AgentMail relay stripping the header, not a confirmed sender fault.
- **Unsubscribe:** the QA run always probes the unsubscribe link and reports no failure for it. The footer and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers aren't visible in the truncated data, so I can't confirm one-click support or the physical address.
- **Plain-text part:** 26,747 of 33,000 characters (about 81%) are URLs, over the 70% warning threshold. Long tracked-link tokens inflate the text part and can raise spam-filter scores.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destinations are hidden behind `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the source doesn't show whether UTM parameters survive the redirect.
- The one visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261108_P261640_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` on the pixel, truncated in the data. It suggests a dated offer campaign, but no landing-page alignment can be checked from this data.
- The broken opt-in link means the "15% off" texts offer has no verifiable landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Open the "Opt-In to Texts" link in a real mobile client. If it 400s there too, fix the tracking redirect or SMS-opt-in landing page.
2. Add meaningful `alt` to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image, and `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` to the `o.gif` pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Shorten the plain-text part (fewer links or shorter tracking tokens) to get under 70% URL content.
6. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` against the raw message, and confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
7. Widen link probing beyond 12 of 76 links, and record the redirect chain to check that UTM parameters survive to the landing page.
