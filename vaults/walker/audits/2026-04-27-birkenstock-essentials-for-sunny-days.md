### 1. Technical Summary

A standard broadcast email built on an ESP platform (Selligent/Campaign Commander based on `click.email.famousfootwear.com` redirect pattern). No broken links or missing images detected, but authentication headers were not captured and the image layer has pervasive missing alt text across all content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**34 tracking links skipped** — all outbound links pass through `click.email.famousfootwear.com/?qs=…` click-redirect. This is expected ESP behavior; no broken destination URLs were confirmed but none could be verified due to redirect skipping.

**Open pixel** present and accounted for:
```
https://click.email.famousfootwear.com/open.aspx?ZIJ2EEBWLPWEJLZNQFAMWP27ZE.70250&d=70250&bmt=0
```
Standard 1×1 pixel; no issues.

**Plain-text version is URL-heavy:** 9,055 of 11,318 characters (80%) are URLs. This is a spam-filter risk — some filters flag messages where the plain-text part is primarily URL content rather than readable copy. Likely caused by the ESP auto-generating the plain-text from HTML without stripping tracking links.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**23 images are missing alt text.** This is the most significant technical finding. Every content image — hero, product shots, dividers, footer logos — has `alt=""`. Examples:

- Logo image: `3b6a7dc1-…ee.png` — `alt=""`
- Hero/banner: `c8d6af31-…71.jpg` — `alt=""`
- Product images: `9a20b849-…dc.jpg`, `d6147ef4-…bb.jpg`, etc. — all `alt=""`

Divider/spacer images (`4ccff4ad-…38.jpg`, 2px height) using empty alt is correct for decorative images, but product and CTA images require descriptive alt text for screen readers and images-off rendering.

**`<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` is malformed** — missing the `http-equiv` attribute. Should be:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Most clients tolerate this but it is technically invalid.

**`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta is deprecated syntax; `user-scalable=no` is the correct value. Minor; broadly supported either way.

**`lineapacing:20px`** is a typo in an inline style on the rewards section `<td>`:
```html
style="...lineapacing:20px;..."
```
This property is not recognized and is silently ignored.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 34 outbound links pass through the ESP click-redirect (`click.email.famousfootwear.com`). UTM parameter decoration cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain. The QA tool skipped HTTP probing on all tracking links.

**Risk:** If the ESP is not configured to append UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) to destination URLs, all click traffic will appear as direct in site analytics rather than email.

Recommend spot-checking one resolved destination URL manually to confirm UTM decoration (e.g., `utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=birkenstock-essentials`).
