## Executive summary

- Well, at least this one leads with something I can use — 15% off sitewide, today only, no fine print buried three scrolls down. That's the kind of straightforward deal that gets my attention, unlike some of these brands that make you hunt for the actual number. But then it's the same Shoe Carnival shuffle I've seen all week: women's clogs, a gal in white jeans kicking up her heel, kids' character sale — and my section, "For Him," is one photo of dress loafers and oxfords stacked up like a shoe rack. Not a sneaker or a walking shoe in sight.
- Fall Sale hero is 100% women's fashion — the $29.98 women's/kids' pricing, a model in a sleeveless top, nothing for me there either. So I'd use the 15% code if I already needed something, but this email isn't the reason I'd go looking. I read it, I see there's no arch-support or comfort-brand talk anywhere, and I move on. ## What's working

- 15% off sitewide, code clearly stated (FALL15), "Today Only" urgency — that's a real, specific offer I can act on without digging.
- The coupon block up top is dead simple — big type, one code, one button. I didn't have to squint.
- There IS a "For Him" section this time, which beats some of the recent all-Nike/Jordan sends — small win.
- Fall leaves and seasonal styling at least signals current inventory, not some stale summer promo held over.

## What's weak

- My "For Him" section is dress shoes — loafers and oxfords. I'm not shopping for wingtips, I want something cushioned I can slip on and walk the dog in.
- Hero "Fall Sale" banner is entirely women's — model, pricing, boots — before I ever get to anything for me.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease — nothing that speaks to how I actually pick a shoe.
- No comfort-brand names at all (no Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance callout) — just generic "Men's" styling shots.
- Sitewide 15% is nice but generic — doesn't tell me it applies to the comfort brands I actually buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the "For Him" photo from dress shoes to a casual/comfort lineup — slip-ons, walking shoes, something with visible cushioning — since that's what actually gets a guy my age to click.
- 2. Move a men's product block above the fold, not after two rounds of women's content — right now I'd have given up scrolling before I got there.
- 3. Name-drop a comfort brand (Skechers, New Balance) if it's included in the 15% — that's the trust signal that gets me past "just another sitewide sale" email.
- 4. Add one line about fit/comfort features (arch support, cushioned insole) near the men's section — nobody's saying it anywhere.
