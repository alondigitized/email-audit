## What's working

- **BOGO ½ off is a genuine offer** — big, bold, at the top of the email, no hunting required.
- **Free shipping for Rewards members** landed clearly right below the hero; as a loyalty member I clocked that immediately.
- **July 4th "Stars, Stripes and Savings" gives the timing a purpose** — this doesn't feel like a random mid-week blast, it feels like a holiday send.
- **2X rewards points callout near the bottom** — I noticed it, and for someone watching a points balance that's a nudge.
- **Layout held together cleanly** — no broken images, no overlapping text, sections read top-to-bottom without confusion.

## What's weak

- **Same broken preheader as the last three sends** — raw tracking URL in my inbox again, same issue I've been silently noting since June 22. This is just negligence at this point.
- **"Vacay mode" is not how I talk or shop** — I'm 62, not 32. That phrase signals this email wasn't built for me.
- **"60% off women's sandals" is the featured deal** — I'm a man. That callout is the most prominent offer in the lower half of the email, and it has nothing to do with me.
- **Not a single word about comfort, cushioning, or arch support** — that's literally my entire buying criteria and Famous Footwear knows I'm in their Rewards program. Zero personalization on product type.
- **"Browse Now" CTAs are vague** — after a good hero offer, pointing me at a generic clearance browse instead of a specific men's comfort sandal feels like a missed layup.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a men's sandal comfort callout** — if I'm a Rewards member and I've been buying men's footwear, surface a men's clearance module with equal or better prominence than the women's 60% off block. Even "Men's clearance sandals from $19.99" would've changed my calculus.
- 2. **Fix the preheader** — this is the most actionable thing on the list and they keep not doing it. One line of real copy — "BOGO ½ off clearance, plus free shipping for Rewards members" — would have added a full click-likelihood point before I even opened the email.
- 3. **Swap "Browse Now" for something specific** — "Shop Men's Clearance" or "Find My Size in Clearance" gives my thumb a reason to move.
- 4. **Drop "Vacay mode"** — call it what it is: summer clearance. Beats trying to sound like a Gen Z brand when the audience isn't.
- 5. **Add one comfort signal** — "cushioned footbed," "arch support," anything. Famous Footwear carries Skechers, Crocs, New Balance — name-drop a comfort feature and I'm actually considering a click.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] 32 images missing alt text** — every product image and most UI images have no `alt` attribute (confirmed by QA). Screen readers will skip all product content. Also degrades rendering in image-blocking clients.

**[MEDIUM] Logo image has `alt=""`** — the logo link (`3b6a7dc1-6955-49f8-868a-bf62a15122ee.png`) renders as an anonymous clickable void for screen readers. Should be `alt="Famous Footwear"`.

**[MEDIUM] Malformed charset meta tag** — missing required `http-equiv` attribute:
```html
<!-- actual -->
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />

<!-- correct -->
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Without `http-equiv`, this tag is inert. Encoding fallback depends on the ESP's `Content-Type` header.

**[LOW] Inline style typo** — `lineapacing:20px` in the nav cell is not a valid CSS property (likely intended as `line-height` or `letter-spacing`). Silently ignored by all clients.

**[LOW] `user-scalable=0`** — non-standard integer value in the viewport meta tag. The spec requires `user-scalable=no`. Widely tolerated but technically incorrect, and blocking zoom is an accessibility concern on its own.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unsubscribe / physical address** — not visible in the truncated HTML provided. CAN-SPAM requires both a functioning unsubscribe mechanism and a valid postal address in every commercial email. These must be confirmed in the full source; if absent, this is a legal violation.

**Authentication headers** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) not provided in the QA data. Cannot verify from HTML source alone — requires header inspection from a received copy.
