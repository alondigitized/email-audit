# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the pink flower sneaker again. I never put the D'Lites "Spring Bloom" in a cart, and it's a women's shoe. "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG" is just wrong for me. I liked nothing here. The subject said my cart expires soon, and I don't have a cart. This is the same cart nag I've been getting all week, from "Did you forget something?" yesterday back to "Your Items Are Waiting!" on the 15th.
- Under the hero is a grid of six shoes. Four are women's or boys', and one is a men's John Deere waterproof Slip-in. The 20% off members banner sits way down the page. I would have liked it much higher, because it's the only thing that gives me a reason to click. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd swipe it away.

## What's working

- The "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner is big and easy to read, and it gives a clear date: "Valid thru 10/21."
- The men's John Deere waterproof slip-in is a hands-free style I'd look at. The shoe pictures are large and clean.
- The "Shop Additional Styles" list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) has big tap targets that are easy on a phone.
- The text is large and the layout is simple. The dark blue "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is easy to see.

## What's weak

- I never started a cart, so "YOU LIKED THESE" and "Your cart expires soon!" feel made up. That costs the brand my trust.
- The hero is a floral women's sneaker. It's the same one from the 15th and 16th, and nothing in it is for a 62-year-old man.
- Only one of the six grid shoes is a men's shoe. The boys' garage sneakers are no use to me.
- The 20% off offer is buried below the shoes, and the fine print at the bottom says "select full-priced styles" and lists exclusions. I can't tell if my shoe would qualify.
- There is nothing about cushioning, arch support, or comfort. Nothing helps me compare against Hoka or Brooks.
- This is the fourth cart-style nag in a few days. It looks like the same email with the same shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send a cart email when there's no cart. If you do send one, show the actual item, or switch to a men's slip-in and comfort message based on what I've browsed.
- 2. Move the 20% members offer to the top, directly under the logo.
- 3. Fill the grid with men's walking and slip-in shoes. Add short labels like "arch support" and "cushioned."
- 4. Cut the cart emails to one per week at most. Alternate them with real offers like the 2 for $100.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: 20% off slip-ins through 10/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's slip-ins, easy on your feet. 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free comfort with arch support. Members save through 10/21.`
