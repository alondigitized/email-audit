# Get 30% off with app-only savings
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Finally, an adidas email with an actual offer. It's 30% off, and I could find that without squinting. But the hero is a blurry close-up of striped fabric with "APP EXCLUSIVE SALE" across it. I can't tell if it's a shirt, a blanket, or a curtain. There isn't a shoe anywhere. That's the same complaint I've had about the last six or seven of these.
- The offer is also less than it looks. It only works in the app, and I'd have to download the app to get it. Then comes the fine print, and it's a wall of exclusions: Samba, Gazelle, Campus, Superstar, Stan Smith, select Ultraboost. Those are the shoes people actually know adidas for. It's also the fourth or fifth promo I've gotten from them since the 15th. I wouldn't click. I'd mark it read, and I'd skip forwarding it. My buddy would just ask which shoes are included, and the email doesn't say.

## What's working

- "LIVE NOW: 30% OFF" is big, plain and easy to read on my phone.
- The code TREAT is spelled out in the body and also in the top line, so I wouldn't have to hunt for it.
- The black "Download the app" button is large and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The dates are listed in the fine print (October 20–26), so I can see the sale really ends.
- The layout is simple. It's one column with a lot of white space, and it doesn't feel cluttered.

## What's weak

- The hero image is blurry fabric with no product. I have no idea what's on sale.
- The offer is app-only. I'd have to install something just to save on shoes, and I don't shop that way.
- The exclusions cover the best-known shoes, Gazelle, Samba, Stan Smith, Superstar and some Ultraboost. So "30% off" feels like bait-and-switch.
- The fine print is a dense block of tiny text. Anyone over 60 will give up halfway down.
- The black "Explore the latest best sellers / Shop now" bar sends me to a different place than the main button. That's two competing calls to action.
- Nothing here speaks to comfort, cushioning or arch support. It could be for any shopper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the fabric hero with real shoes. Show two or three of the styles that are actually included in the sale, ideally comfortable walking or lifestyle pairs.
- 2. Say what's excluded in one plain line near the button, so it isn't buried in the fine print.
- 3. Drop the competing "Shop now" bar, or make it a web link for people who don't want the app.
- 4. Offer the code on the website too, or explain in a sentence why the app is worth it.
- 5. Make the fine print bigger and shorter.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off in the app through Oct 26 — code TREAT`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off walking and everyday shoes, app only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code TREAT at checkout. Ends October 26. Some styles excluded.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Full-price and sale styles, 30% off in the adidas app this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, responsive email with a `lang="en"` attribute and a viewport meta tag. QA found one failure (no plain-text part) and several warnings: insecure `http://` image sources, missing alt text, and 13 click-tracked links that could not be probed. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking links not verified.** QA skipped the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure image sources.** Three `http://` image references were flagged:
  - `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` ("arrow"). It is flagged twice, so it appears twice in the markup.
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1698a6168-2bf09356-.../__WbPgEFzzPaX3GmRwyUH26wb50QWRRASIIEZkf9xPY=452`. This looks like a tracking or open-pixel image.
- Clients that block mixed content may drop these images. Dropping the tracking image would also undercount opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on two images:
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/a2ead092-a09f-4c51-8c52-ee32eb0e955b?...`
  - The `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...=452` image. If this is a tracking pixel, `alt=""` is acceptable, but the attribute is absent.
- **Charset declaration likely ineffective.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute. Parsers that rely on the meta tag may ignore it. The MIME header usually covers this, but I couldn't see it.
- **Duplicated CSS.** There are two near-identical `@font-face` blocks for AdihausDIN and AdineuePRO, plus repeated `@media` rules. This adds weight and has no functional benefit.
- **Fonts never load.** Every `@font-face` `src` is only `local('Arial')`, `local('Helvetica')` and similar. The custom families fall back to system fonts, so the declarations do nothing.
- **Plain-text part is empty (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars, so there is no multipart/alternative fallback for text-only clients, some accessibility tools, or spam filters.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and QA output show no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. Since the HTML is truncated, I can't rule them out later in the document.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA says this is expected through the AgentMail relay, so it is a limitation of the test setup and not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address unverified.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided. I can't confirm either way.
- The missing plain-text part (section 3) also counts against deliverability.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 13 tracking links go through redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com`, `image.link.adidas.com`), and QA didn't resolve them. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "app-only savings" subject can't be confirmed from the data given.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text alternative** (multipart/alternative). This is the only QA FAIL.
2. **Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`.** That covers `image.link.adidas.com/.../b0c011a2-...jpg` and the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` tracking image.
3. **Add alt text.** Give the `dv.adidas.com/o/a2ead092-...` image a descriptive `alt`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
4. **Fix the charset meta.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
5. **Deduplicate the CSS.** Merge the repeated `@font-face` and `@media` blocks. Either supply real font URLs or remove the declarations.
6. **Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers** to check the footer unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the postal address, and the resolved destinations and UTMs of the 13 tracking links.
