# Animal prints for fall. RAWR!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, there's the same BOGO 50% off deal again — that's five, six times this week now, I've lost count. This time it's dressed up as "Spotted: Animal Print," with leopard flats, cow-print sneakers, and a whole spread of women's boots and heels. Not one thing here is for me. I scrolled the whole thing looking for a men's walking shoe or anything with real arch support and came up empty — just cats-and-cow puns and a "Shop By Size" grid that tops out at 11, so even the sizing chart is telling me this isn't my email.
- The layout's fine, I'll give them that — clean green buttons, readable text, no glitches I could see. But when a shoe warehouse email is basically all leopard-print ballet flats and slingback heels, it's not competing for my dollar. I'm marking this read and moving on, not forwarding it to anybody I know.

## What's working

- The "BOGO 50% off" banner is clear and easy to spot right at the top, no hunting for it.
- Buttons are big, green, and say plainly what they do — "Shop Animal Print," "Shop All Women" — no guessing.
- No broken images or overlapping text that I could see; the page rendered clean.
- The size chart and wide-width/wide-calf options are a nice touch for people who need them, just not me.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — sneakers, flats, boots, heels — is women's animal print. Nothing for a guy like me at all.
- "Animal Print" and "cat's meow" puns don't land with me; I want a shoe description, not a joke.
- This is the same BOGO deal I've seen practically every day this week — DSW, I get it, I'm not going to buy it any faster from repetition.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere, which is the only thing that'd get my attention in a shoe email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men out of the "animal print" campaign entirely — send me a men's comfort/walking shoe email instead, or at minimum include a men's row.
- 2. Retire the BOGO banner reuse for one send this week — even swapping the visual gets stale when the offer text is identical day after day.
- 3. If DSW must email me from this campaign, drop in a "Men's Comfort Picks" tile so I've got a reason to scroll past the leopard print.
- 4. Lead with something concrete like cushioning or arch support callouts for shoes that qualify, not "wild prints are trending."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort shoes: BOGO 50% off, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your walking shoes just got 50% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, arch support — all BOGO 50% off this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort picks for him, half off the second pair`
