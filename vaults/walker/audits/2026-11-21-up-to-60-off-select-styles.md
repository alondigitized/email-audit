- The "$30 classic clogs" block is a clear price I can understand.

## What's weak

- The "ADD TO CART" banner repeated three times looks pushy and out of place.
- "Exclusions apply" and "select styles" make me wonder which shoes actually get the discount.
- The TikTok "FYP" and "Shop the Hype" section is aimed at someone a third of my age.
- Nothing mentions arch support, cushioning, or walking comfort. There are no reviews and no men's styles.
- Three buttons say "Shop All Doorbusters" or "Shop Black Friday Deals" and probably go to the same place. The small fine print is hard to read.

- 2. Cut the "ADD TO CART" banner and drop the TikTok block. Put a men's comfort row in its place: fleece-lined clogs and cushioned slip-ons, with a line about arch support and star ratings.
- 3. Use specific button text such as "Shop Men's Clogs $30" instead of repeating "Shop All Doorbusters."
- 4. Fix the deadline conflict in the fine print and state one clear end date up top.
- 5. Send fewer of these. After a week of near-identical emails, the deals stop feeling special.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible part of the HTML is structurally sound: it has a `lang` attribute, a viewport meta tag, a preheader, a 1x1 open pixel and mobile media queries. Two `<meta>` tags are malformed, one tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute, and the truncated source and relay-stripped headers leave compliance and link behavior unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** The QA run skipped 19 tracking links because they use click-redirect domains, so none of them were checked for status or final destination. The run did not find broken links, and the destinations are simply unverified.
- **Open pixel:** The pixel at `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10210&bmt=0` has `width="1" height="1" alt=""`. This is correct.
- **Demdex beacon:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is a second third-party tracking pixel. It is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event call.
  - QA flagged it as an image with missing alt text.
  - It adds a third-party request to every open, and the `d_dpuid` value is a per-recipient identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** Both are missing `http-equiv`.
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` is not a valid charset declaration. It should be `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no effect. It should be `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
- **Missing alt text:** The demdex image has no `alt` attribute (QA finding). It should be `alt=""`.
- **Not issues:** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tag and the preheader hiding (`display:none !important`) are all fine.
- **Truncation:** The source is cut off, so alt text on the content images and the table markup (`role="presentation"`) could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication headers:** QA reports that `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the AgentMail relay, which does not preserve the header, not anything in the email itself. This is a gap in the check, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer elements:** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the truncated source or the QA data, so they could not be verified.
- **Sender details:** The From address is `email@crocs-email.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain. This is consistent with the SFMC click and open domains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 19 click-tracked links were not resolved, so the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters cannot be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the two meta tags by adding `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon `<img>`.
3. Resolve the 19 skipped tracking links by following the redirects and logging the final URL, status code and UTM parameters.
4. Capture the raw headers from the original delivery, before the relay. The `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe` and `DKIM-Signature` headers are needed for a real compliance check.
5. Run the unsubscribe and postal-address checks against the full, untruncated HTML.
