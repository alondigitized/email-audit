But look closer and it's still not really aimed at me: that grid of nine sneakers is heavy on the trendy chunky-dad-shoe look in cream, pink, and pastel green, and the "wide calf" button at the bottom is a boot term, not a sneaker term — tells me this template got recycled from a women's send.

- Sneakers instead of boots or heels — first email all week that's even plausible for me to wear.
- The $59.99 hero number and the Brooks $75 callout are both clear, specific prices, no guessing.
- Clean grid layout, nothing overlapping or broken, easy enough to scan on my phone.
- "SHOP NOW" is a plain, obvious button — I know exactly what happens if I tap it.

## What's weak

- The shoe styles skew fashion-sneaker (chunky retro, pastel colors, a pink pair) — not the plain cushioned walking shoe I'm hunting for.
- "Shop Wide Calf" makes no sense next to sneakers — feels like a leftover from a women's boot template, not built for me.
