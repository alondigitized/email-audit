# New Inflation Relief: $14.98 Women's & Kids'  Sandals
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- The Inflation Relief banner is back. I spotted it the second I scrolled past the Crocs hero — that same yellow-and-red block, same as it's been in what feels like every Shoe Carnival email I've opened for the past month. But here's the bigger problem: this email isn't for me. The subject said Women's & Kids' Sandals and they weren't lying — pink flip-flops, wedge slides, kids' Nikes. Every last product. I'm a 62-year-old man shopping for arch support and something I can slip on without bending over. Not one thing in this email is aimed at my foot.
- Then there's the preview text. My inbox showed "Shoe Carnival https://click.email.shoecarnival.com/?qs=..." — a raw tracking URL bleeding in where real copy should be. I genuinely thought my phone was glitching. That's not a good first impression before I've even tapped to open.
- I would not click. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read, maybe grumble about getting the women's email again, and move on.

- The **$14.98 price** is big, bold, and unmissable — I knew the offer before I had to read a word.
- The **Crocs Miami Jelly hero** is well-photographed — clean staging, good color, summer feeling.
- The **coupon block** at the bottom ($10 off $74.98+, $15 off $99.98+) looks like real money with real barcodes — usable in-store.
- The **Shoe Perks $10 reward with a hard 6/30 date** is a solid call-to-action with a credible deadline.
- The **email body renders clean** — no broken images, no garbled text, nothing fighting for space.

## What's weak

- **Wrong gender, wrong customer** — Women's & Kids' from the subject line to the last product image. I'm not in that aisle.
- **Preview text is a raw tracking URL.** That's a technical fumble that makes the brand look sloppy before I've even opened it.
- **The Inflation Relief banner has lost all stopping power.** I've now seen it in the June 5th send, June 6th, June 7th, June 9th — by the time it showed up here, my eye just slid right past it.
- **No men's product anywhere.** Not a slipper, not a walking sandal, not a casual slip-on. I'm invisible to this send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the list — send this to women.** That's the entire fix. A 62-year-old male subscriber should never receive a Women's & Kids' Sandal Blowout.
- 2. **Fix the preheader.** A raw URL in the preview is a deploy error — someone needs a QA check before this hits inboxes.
- 3. **Retire or redesign the Inflation Relief banner.** It has shown up in every send I've reviewed since early June. Test a new visual; this one is wallpaper now.
- 4. **Build a men's counterpart.** Same $14.98 anchor price, same coupon mechanic — but put a men's comfort sandal or walking shoe in the hero. That's the email that would get me to click.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Shoe Carnival: "New Inflation Relief: $14.98 Women's & Kids' Sandals"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard table-based HTML build with multi-breakpoint responsive CSS; structure and meta tags are sound. One confirmed broken tracking link (caused by an embedded Unicode zero-width space) is the sole critical defect; eight images are missing alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**CRITICAL — Broken click-tracking URL (zero-width space in URI)**

The QA probe failed with:
```
'ascii' codec can't encode character '\u200b' in position 76
```
Affected URL:
```
https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?rd&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260611_crocs_sandals_AM​
```
A Unicode zero-width space (`U+200B`) is appended after `_AM` — invisible in most text editors but fatal to URL parsers. Any click routed through this redirect will fail (HTTP error or dropped session). The affected campaign tag is `SCA_20260611_crocs_sandals_AM`, so attribution for those clicks is also lost.

**INFO — 29 tracking links skipped**

Click-redirect domains were not probed (expected behavior for `emltrk.com` et al.). No issue, but the broken URL above falls within this domain and the zero-width space is what caused it to surface as a hard error rather than a skip.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 8 images confirmed by QA**

| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| 4 product shots | `i8.amplience.net/s/scvl/…?fmt=auto&$webPdpProduct$` |
| Logo (×2) | `image.email.shoecarnival.com/…/951dfca3-078c-47e4-a9c6-b5630a845bf2.png` |
| Open pixel | `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=…` |
| Open pixel | `0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` |

Product and logo images missing alt text fail WCAG 2.1 AA (1.1.1) and degrade screen-reader experience. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string), not omit the attribute — some clients render a broken-image icon when `alt` is absent.

**Rendering — No issues found**

DOCTYPE (`HTML 4.01 Transitional`), viewport meta, `color-scheme` meta, MSO/ExternalClass fixes, and Apple/Samsung autolink overrides are all present and correctly structured. Three responsive breakpoints (320 px, 450 px, 660 px) with a separate 600 px text-size block are redundant but not harmful.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`[UNIQUE]` token in open pixel** — `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]`

This is almost certainly intentional ESP substitution (replaced at send time per-recipient). However, if QA is running against a pre-send seed or test address where substitution did not fire, it would indicate the open pixel is tracking all opens against a single literal string rather than unique recipients. Confirm the token was resolved in live sends.

No other unresolved merge tokens are visible in the provided HTML.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM / Unsubscribe / Authentication)

Sending domain `shoecarnival@email.shoecarnival.com` uses a dedicated ESP subdomain — standard practice. No raw authentication headers (DKIM, SPF, DMARC) were provided for review; cannot confirm pass/fail.

Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link are required by CAN-SPAM but not visible in the truncated HTML. This is likely present in the footer (outside the provided excerpt) — verify the footer contains both before treating as clear.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameter `utm_campaign=SCA_20260611_crocs_sandals_AM` is present in tracking URLs and will carry through to the landing page on successful redirects. However, the broken link identified in §2 means any click routed through the `ieezzy5m` redirect with the zero-width space will not reach the landing page at all — those sessions will be lost entirely, not just untagged.

No UTM `source` or `medium` values are visible in the provided URLs; confirm the redirect destination appends a full UTM set (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_content`) rather than campaign alone.
