# Love Silver? Or Gold?

## Executive summary

- Slides — a two-strap sandal with a fancy buckle, silver or gold. That's it. No price anywhere in this whole email, not one number. "Pick your shine" — I'm 62, not picking out jewelry, I'm picking out something to walk the dog in without my knees hollering at me. There's zero mention of cushioning, arch support, or all-day comfort, which is the only reason I open a shoe email in the first place.
- It's a step up from that crocodile mascot nonsense a few days back, at least there's an actual product front and center, but it's aimed at somebody younger and more into the "trending on TikTok" side of things than me. The "Most Wanted" banner and the "silver girls, gold girls" line tell me right off this isn't written with a guy my age in mind.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm sure not forwarding this to a buddy — this is straight to read-and-delete.

## What's working

- The hero shot is clean and the sandal itself is easy to see, no clutter.
- "Recommended for You" row at least shows me some Classic and Baya Clogs, which are more my speed than the metallic buckle thing.
- Afterpay call-out is clear if I were the type to finance a $50 sandal, which I'm not, but I get what it's offering.
- Big tappable buttons at the bottom (Shop Clogs, Shop Sandals) are easy to hit on a phone screen.

## What's weak

- No price, no percent off, no dollar figure anywhere — I have no idea if this is a $40 sandal or a $70 one.
- "Love Silver? Or Gold?" and "silver girls, gold girls" language is clearly aimed at a younger, female shopper, not me.
- Not one word about comfort, cushioning, or arch support — the whole reason I'd even consider a Crocs email.
- The two-strap buckle sandal looks more like a going-out shoe than something I'd wear mowing the lawn.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual price or discount on the hero image — even "$54.99" would beat silence. That's the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Swap the "silver girls, gold girls" copy for something that includes guys — or just drop the gendered framing entirely and talk about the shoe.
- 3. Add one line about the footbed or comfort — that's what actually gets me to tap.
- 4. Lead the "Recommended for You" section with the Classic Clog instead of burying it after the metallic buckle pitch — that's the shoe that fits my life.
