# See what's new
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- Well, HOKA sent me a shoe email again, and "Shift into neutral" in big white letters over tan sneakers is easy to read. The beige look is calm and fits my closet. But the offer is a free sock 3-pack buried in the thin yellow bar at the very top, and I nearly missed it. After a week of Grit, Black, and Road icons, this is the same "new colors" email in a different outfit. It looks like the Oct 2 "New colors: Bondi 7" send, which is the one I liked best.
- The Bondi 7 and Bondi 9 in the grid are the cushioned walkers I care about. The Mach Remastered is a running shoe, and I'm not running. Then comes a "Road Running Guide" with two joggers, which is not me, and a big free-shipping block that only applies to a text-message signup. I'd open it. I'd probably tap one Bondi, but I wouldn't forward it. The first-order shipping offer and the sock deal aren't things I'd pass along.

## What's working

- "Shift into neutral" is huge and readable on my phone without zooming.
- The tan and cream shoes are the kind I'd wear. They are not neon or trail-mud.
- Bondi 7 and Bondi 9 show up in the grid. That's the cushioned shoe I know.
- Each shoe has a name and a "Women / Men" label under it, so I know which are mine.
- The sock offer sits right at the top, and the email matches what I'd see on the HOKA site.

## What's weak

- The sock deal is in a thin yellow bar with tiny text, and the real conditions are in fine print at the bottom.
- Two "Shop Now" buttons go to the same general place. I can't tell which shoe each one goes to.
- The grid has no prices, no arch-support or walking details, and no reviews, so I can't do my usual price-versus-quality math.
- The road-running guide is for joggers, and the two runners in the photo aren't anyone like me.
- "Free Expedited Shipping" has asterisks and only applies if I sign up for texts. That feels like a catch.
- The footer text is tiny and hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear offer in the hero, in big type: the sock deal or a real price. Right now my eye goes to the shoe and misses the deal.
- 2. Link each shoe to its own page and show a price and a comfort note, like "cushioned all-day walking."
- 3. Swap the running guide for a "best HOKA for walking and standing" guide aimed at people like me.
- 4. Drop the text-signup shipping block, or put it near the bottom with plain wording.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 2+ items and get free socks. Cushioned walkers in calm colors.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup and click-tracking have three confirmed problems: all 16 images lack `alt` text, there is no plain-text part (0 chars), and the head has invalid or conflicting meta tags. All 11 probed tracked links, including the unsubscribe link, returned 4xx. The uniform pattern points to ESP bot-blocking rather than dead destinations, but that is unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probed links all fail.** 10 links returned 406 and the Unsubscribe link returned 403. All are Cordial redirects of the form `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`. A 100% failure rate on links that include the logo, product tiles and the return link looks like the redirector rejecting a non-browser User-Agent. It does not look like 11 separate broken destinations. Re-probe with a browser UA before treating these as outages.
- **Destinations decoded from `jwtP.linkUrl` are well-formed `hoka.com` URLs:**
  - Hero banner (`US_20261006_SneakerWeekPromo.jpg`) goes to `/en/us/best-sellers/`.
  - Logo goes to `/`.
  - `NewArrivals_a`, `_b` and `_c` all go to `/en/us/lifestyle/`.
  - Product tiles go to `/en/us/womens-lifestyle/bondi-7/1110519.html`, `/en/us/all-gender-footwear/mach-remastered/1176250.html` and `1176251.html`, and `/en/us/all-gender-footwear/ora-primo/1141570.html`.
  - The footer link goes to `/en/us/return-start/`.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 29 material links were probed. The `US_Bondi9_BottomRight_1162013-WNX` tile and the remaining links were skipped, so their status is unknown.
- **Two open-tracking pixels from two hosts.** One is `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/1664:…` (Cordial) and the other is `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…` (a Mailgun-style host). Both lack `alt`. Open counts may be duplicated, and the two sending-infrastructure hosts add to the authentication surface.
- **A per-recipient `hmail=9153f33f…ed00` token (64 hex chars, apparently a hash) is appended to every destination URL.** This is a persistent identifier in query strings. It will appear in site analytics and referrer logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 16 images.** Examples: `US_20261006_SneakerWeekPromo.jpg`, `US_20261011_NewArrivals_a.jpg` through `_d.jpg`, all six product tiles, `US_20260727_RoadRunningGuide.jpg`, `US_Q3_SMSCTA_DoubleAsterisk.jpg`, `LogoFooterPrimary.png`, and both tracking pixels. Because the images sit inside `<a>` tags, those links have no accessible name, and the QA output falls back to filenames as link text. With images off, the email shows blank blocks. Any text baked into the image is also unavailable to screen readers.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing.
- **Duplicate viewport metas with conflicting values.** One has `maximum-scale=1`, the other `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Invalid CSS.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values. The global `* {border:none;outline:none;}` removes focus outlines.
- **Web fonts load from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…/TheFutureHOKA-*.woff2`.** The visible HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm a fallback stack. Most email clients ignore `@font-face`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML shows no unrendered tokens. The only per-recipient values are the `hmail` and `cordial_id` parameters, which are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative.** The QA check reports a text version of 0 chars. A missing text part is a deliverability and spam-scoring risk, and it also affects accessibility.
- **Unsubscribe link present but unverified.** It is wrapped in a Cordial redirect that resolves to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`. The probe got a 403, which is probably bot-blocking like the other 4xx results. Manually confirm that it works.
- **Not evaluable from the supplied data:** the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and the physical postal address (the HTML is truncated before the footer). I'm not flagging these as failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present on every destination checked:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261011_NewArrivals_TaupePack_GWP_A180Day_365NewArrivalsTag_270GWPTag_365LifestyleTag_AIRFMHigh`.
- **No `utm_content` or `utm_term`.** Every link, from the hero banner to the footer return link, carries identical UTMs. Click-level attribution by placement or product is impossible in site analytics.
- **`utm_source` holds the send type (`US_Broadcast`) instead of the platform.** This is a naming-convention issue and may break channel groupings.
- **Possible hero mismatch.** The banner asset is named `SneakerWeekPromo` and is dated 20261006, but it links to the generic `/en/us/best-sellers/`. Verify that this is intended.
- **Locale-prefixed asset on a US send.** `CA_MachRemastered_BottomLeft_1176251-ASY.jpg` links to `/en/us/…`, so the destination is US.
- **Fixed size parameters in product URLs.** `dwvar_1110519_size=7`, `dwvar_1176250_size=9` and `size=M8/W9` are the same for every recipient. This is not an error, but the sizes are not personalized.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a meaningful `alt` to all 14 content images. Use `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. The current text version is empty.
3. Re-run the link probe with a browser User-Agent, or have Cordial allowlist the QA client. Manually click the unsubscribe link, and confirm that the `List-Unsubscribe` headers and the footer postal address are present.
4. Fix the head. Add `<meta charset="utf-8">`, set a real `<title>`, remove the duplicate viewport tag, and drop `maximum-scale=1`. Delete or fill the empty `px` CSS rules, and don't strip focus outlines globally.
5. Add `utm_content` per placement (hero, tile slot, footer) to the Cordial link templates.
6. Check why two open pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com` and `mg.emails.hoka.com`) are injected. Remove one if it is redundant.
7. Confirm that putting a hashed identifier (`hmail`) in landing-page URLs fits HOKA's privacy policy.
