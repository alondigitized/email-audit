# Final hours to double up on comfort
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- Finally, an email that doesn't claim I left something in a cart. "Hi, Walker," my Member status and the 1000-point $5 reward are right at the top. That's the same personal touch I liked in the 10/19 "Real reviews, real comfort" send. The giant "2 FOR $100" is easy to read on my phone, and I understood it right away.
- The "final hours" in the subject doesn't match the email. The fine print says the offer is valid through 10/21, and this is the 10/22 send. If it really is the last day, say so loudly. As it stands, "Valid thru 10/21" sits in small type under the hero. It's also the same 2-for-$100 offer I've seen since 10/18, so "final hours" feels like a pressure tactic.
- I'd probably open it, glance at the men's slip-on, and maybe click. The navy slip-on is the one shoe here that fits me: hands-free, cushioned sole. But nothing tells me about arch support or comfort, and I don't see a price. I wouldn't forward it. I'd likely mark it read and move on unless I needed shoes this week.

## What's working

- "Hi, Walker" and the Member line with 1000 points and a $5 reward make me feel like a known customer.
- "2 FOR $100" is huge and clear, and I understood it in a second.
- The navy slip-on in the Men's block is my kind of shoe: a slip-in, no-laces, thick-soled style.
- The Women's, Men's and Kids' buttons are big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- Free returns within 45 days (90 for Members) is a real confidence-builder when I'm buying shoes I can't try on.

## What's weak

- "Final hours" is a stretch. The deadline is a small "Valid thru 10/21" line, and this arrived on 10/22. That hurts my trust.
- The Men's slip-on is the middle shoe, with the women's pink-ish sneaker above it. I had to scroll past one shoe that isn't mine to reach mine.
- No comfort talk at all. I don't see anything about cushioning, arch support or "Slip-ins," and no price per pair.
- Two different deals stack up. There's 2 for $100, then 20% off for Members "almost everything else," and I'm not sure which applies to me.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. I couldn't read it without zooming, and "Exclusions apply" makes me wary.
- Kids' is a tab that's no use to me, and the shoe above it is pink again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the deadline honest and prominent: put "Ends tonight" or "Ends 10/21 at midnight" in the hero, or stop saying "final hours" once the deadline has passed.
- 2. Lead with Men's for male subscribers, and show the slip-on first with a line like "Slip in, no hands, all-day cushion."
- 3. Put a price and a comfort cue under each shoe, such as "2 pairs, $50 each" and "Arch-supported."
- 4. Use one clear offer per section. Tell Members plainly whether 20% off stacks with 2-for-$100.
- 5. Make the fine print larger, or link to a short plain-English version.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 2 pairs for $100`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, 2 slip-ons for $100 ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free comfort, two pairs for $100. Members also get 20% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free returns for 90 days as a Member. Last day to double up.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The send is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud email (`click.emails.skechers.com`) with a broken personalization slot, no plain-text part, and several markup and tracking defects. Compliance footer, CTA URLs and UTM parameters sit in the truncated part of the source, so they could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no `http(s)://` or `//`. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request never reaches Return Path and that seed and inbox-placement measurement is lost. The QA check did not flag this.
- **Insecure image sources (QA WARN):**
  - `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_10212026_B`
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - Clients may block or rewrite these. The second is the sender's own image host, which is a content asset, so a blocked load leaves a visible gap. The first is a tracking pixel.
- **Open pixel in a hidden container:** `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?...` and the `beacon.krxd.net` pixels sit inside `<div style="display:none !important">` within a `display:none` `<td>`. Some clients skip images in hidden containers, which can undercount opens.
- **Third-party beacons:** four `beacon.krxd.net` calls (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) plus `ink1000.com`.
  - The `partner=…_sfmc_100007688_sha25` and `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00` parameters look like a SHA-256 hash of the recipient's email. That is my inference from the naming; the source doesn't say.
  - These pixels may have privacy-disclosure and consent implications.
  - They are blocked by image proxies and privacy features.
- **Unverified links (QA):** 32 tracking links skipped, so redirect targets and 404s are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** `o.gif` and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` have no `alt` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang`.
- **Layout tables:** the visible tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **No plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. Clients that need it, such as text-only clients, some accessibility tools and spam filters, get nothing.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting (QA FAIL):** the greeting renders as `Hi ,` because the first-name field is empty and has no fallback value. This shows a broken-looking greeting to any subscriber without a first name.
- No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were visible in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Authentication (QA WARN):** no `Authentication-Results` header was found. The check notes it is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown rather than failing.
- **Unverified items:**
  - The unsubscribe link, postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or QA data.
  - The sender is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. This is not a violation by itself, but confirm the opt-out mechanism works.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Unverified:** CTA hrefs and UTM parameters are not in the visible source, and the 32 tracking links were not probed.
- **Campaign ID:** the only visible identifier is `campaignid=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_10212026_B`, which sits in a third-party pixel.
- **Offer match:** the preheader "2 for $100 ends today" can't be checked against a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field, for example an AMPscript `IIF(Empty(@firstName), "there", @firstName)`, or drop the name from the greeting when it is empty.
2. Generate a multipart text/plain alternative. SFMC can auto-generate one.
3. Fix `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` to use `https://`.
4. Serve `image.emails.skechers.com` and `ink1000.com` assets over HTTPS, and add `alt` text to the content image (`alt=""` for pixels).
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` / `"X-UA-Compatible"` to the meta tags, add `lang="en"` and add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Move the open pixel out of the `display:none` container, for example to the bottom of the body.
7. Review the `krxd` hashed-identifier pixels against the privacy policy and consent.
8. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and the raw headers to check the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, UTM parameters and CTA landing pages.
