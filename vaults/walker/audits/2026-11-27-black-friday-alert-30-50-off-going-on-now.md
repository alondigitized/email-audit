- Finally, a sale email that doesn't pretend I left something in a cart. Since 11/20 I've had a dozen "Your cart expires soon" nags, so a plain red-on-black "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" is a relief. I got the deal in about two seconds. It does look like every other Black Friday blast, though.

- "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" is huge, red and readable on my phone without zooming.
- "Hi, Walker" with the 1000-point balance and "REDEEM" shows they know I'm a member.
- The Plus members banner says an extra 15% off with code BF2026. That's a real extra for members, with the code shown.
- The "Shop Men" button and the boots/slippers/outdoor tile with a man around my age are easy to find.
- The Doorbuster section says "Starting at $49.99" in plain type. It's a clear price I can do the math on.

## What's weak

- Nothing says slip-in, cushioned or arch-supported. For comfort shoppers it's all price and no product.
- Four "SHOP" buttons and two "SHOP NOW" links compete in the top section, so I don't know which one is the main CTA.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense, and I can't read it on a phone. "Valid thru 11/29" up top and "11/23–11/29" below make the dates feel a little muddled.
- "Sale Styles" and "Doorbusters" overlap with the sitewide deal, and I can't tell which discount applies to what.
- The model photos lean young and trendy, especially the apparel tile, and the shoes are hard to see.

- 2. Cut the four top buttons and the repeated "Shop Now" links down to one main button and a clear men's path.
- 3. Say how the 15% stacks. Something like "Sale price $X, minus 15% with BF2026, you pay $Y" does the price-vs-quality math for me.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders as a table-based SFMC template, but the personalization slot is empty ("Hi ,"). It also has a scheme-less tracking pixel, two insecure `http://` images, and no verifiable authentication or unsubscribe data in the provided material.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no `http(s)://` scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the seed or placement tracking never fires. It also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11272026_AM` is HTTP, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Third-party beacons:** Five 0×0 or 1×1 images load from `beacon.krxd.net` and `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`. The `krxd.net` `usermatch` pixels pass `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00`, which looks like a SHA-256 hash, plus a subscriber ID. They are third-party data-matching calls, and none carries `alt=""`.
- **Unprobed links:** The QA run skipped 41 click-redirect links, so destination health and 404s were not checked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000.com)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`)
- **HTTP image source:** The `dde00662…png` image is served over `http://`. If it's content rather than a pixel, it may be blocked and leave a hole in the layout.
- **Meta tags (source):** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so the charset and compatibility declarations are ineffective.
- **Zoom restriction:** The viewport is `maximum-scale=1`, which restricts pinch-zoom where honored.
- **Layout tables:** The visible layout tables (e.g. `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Text sizing:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` blocks user text-size scaling in WebKit clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL (QA):** The greeting renders with an empty name field ("Hi ,"). The data field resolved to null with no fallback. This is a broken-experience bug, and it likely means the subscriber record has no first name and the template has no default.
- No raw unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the excerpt.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unverified**, not failing. The From domain is `emails.skechers.com`, and its alignment can't be assessed.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** The HTML is truncated before the footer, so the unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers could not be checked.
- **Privacy:** The hashed-email `krxd.net` pixels should be covered by the sender's privacy disclosure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking ID:** The campaign ID `PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11272026_AM` is consistent across the pixels.
- **UTM parameters:** All 41 links go through SFMC click redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are **not verifiable** from the provided data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the greeting merge field (e.g. `Hi there,`). Alternatively, suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel by adding `https://`, and add `alt=""`.
3. Move the `ink1000.com` pixel and `image.emails.skechers.com` image to HTTPS, and add `alt=""` to the pixels and descriptive alt to the content image.
4. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers, and resolve the redirect chains. This would confirm the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM values.
