# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-11-11**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I came to Skechers on my phone looking for a slip-on with cushioning and arch support. The menus got me to the right shelf quickly, and "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit," "Hands Free Slip-ins" and "Comfort Technologies" are all there. But I couldn't finish anything. When I tapped Add to Cart it asked me to pick a size and nothing was added. The cart page was a blank white screen. When I searched "comfort shoes," the first results were women's shoes. The login step looked identical to the homepage, so I couldn't tell whether I was signed in.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10.** Yesterday was also 3/10, so my score hasn't moved.

- **Regressions:** I only have today's screenshots, so I can't point to anything that got worse.
- **Improvements:** I can't point to any either.
- **What holds the score down:** the blank cart is a dealbreaker. Someone who gets that far and sees nothing leaves.

## 3. What's Working

- **Comfort vocabulary up front.** The homepage chips (Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit, Max Cushioning) and the Men's menu (Comfort Technologies, Shop by Activity) match what I shop for.
- **Men's Shoes sub-categories.** "Hands Free Slip-ins" and "Walking Shoes" are the first two tiles on the Men's Shoes page. That is exactly my need.
- **Menu drawer.** It has big text, plenty of spacing, a clear Back button and a clear close X. It is easy to tap.
- **Size picker.** The buttons are large, two to a row, and easy to hit with a thumb. Sold-out sizes are struck through. The red "Please select a size" message is clear. The Add to Cart button is big and blue.
- **Product page.** Price, star rating and the Klarna/Afterpay payment options are readable.

## 4. What's Weak

- **The cart is a blank white page (step 8).** There is no header, no "your cart is empty," no items and no error message. I can't tell whether it's broken or just empty. Either way it makes me distrust the site.
- **Add to Cart did nothing visible (step 7).** I tapped it without a size and got an error. That is fair, but the page showed me the size grid and not the shoe. The cart badge stayed at 0, so I had no confirmation of anything.
- **Login looks the same as the homepage (steps 2-3).** I see no "Hi, Walker," no account menu and no change. The experience doesn't feel personalized.
- **Search is not relevant to me (step 9).** "comfort shoes" returned 1,653 results. The first two were a Women's wedge and a Women's slipper. Nothing was filtered to men or to the Men's page I had just come from.
- **Homepage hero is Christmas clothing, not shoes (step 1).** It shows a couple in sweatshirts with the shoes barely visible. It says nothing about comfort or arch support.
- **First products on Men's Shoes are loud graffiti-pattern sneakers (step 5).** That isn't what a comfort-first 62-year-old is looking for. Nothing at the top is sorted by comfort.
- **Confusing discounts.** Two banners compete: "30% off Members" and "25% off Work & Safety." The product page says "Members get 30% off! Applied at Cart." I can't see my real price until checkout, and I don't like doing math in my head.
- **Small text and cut-off controls.** Prices, star counts and the "Members Get 30% Off" lines are small. The Filter and Size pills get cut off on the right ("Wi…"), and the top chip row cuts off at "Max Cus…".
- **No comparison help.** There is nothing about how Skechers compares to Hoka or Brooks. The only price math is the Klarna "$14/month" line, and I care about price versus quality.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page.** Show a header, the cart contents or an empty-cart message, and a "Continue shopping" button. Add an error state and log blank renders. This is the top priority.
2. **Confirm Add to Cart.** Show a toast or mini-cart with a "View Cart" button, and update the cart badge right away. Put the product image above the size grid.
3. **Make login visible.** Show "Hi, [name]" or a member badge in the header after sign-in. Use the order history to put slip-ins and arch-fit shoes first.
4. **Carry the department into search.** If I browse Men, search should default to Men, or at least offer a Men / Women toggle at the top.
5. **Lead with comfort on the homepage.** Replace the apparel hero with a Slip-ins or Arch Fit hero, such as "Walk all day, no bending down."
6. **Show the real price.** Display "$56 with Member 30% off" next to the list price, and collapse the two competing banners into one.
7. **Use larger type and wrap the controls.** Raise the price and promo text to at least 16px. Wrap the filter pills and chip row, or add a scroll hint, so nothing is cut off.
8. **Add comfort cues on product tiles.** Show an Arch Fit or Max Cushioning badge, cushioning/support ratings, and "Slip-ins" badges.

## 6. Bottom Line

Skechers has the right words for me, but the site stops me before I can buy. A blank cart page and a login that looks like nothing happened cost trust. If I can't see my cart, I'll go to Hoka or Brooks, or walk into DSW. Fix the cart first, then make search and the homepage speak to comfort-first men.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

- **Cart step is unreliable.** `GET /cart/` returned **429**. The View Cart axe results (no `<title>`, no `lang`, no `<main>`, no `<h1>`) match a bare error or rate-limit page, not the real cart. This is an inference from the violation set. The response body wasn't included.
- **Core Web Vitals are missing.** LCP and CLS are `?` for all 8 steps, so only TTFB can be assessed.
- **Step data looks suspect.** The Log In and Men category steps both record URL `https://www.skechers.com/`, and their HTML samples are identical to the homepage (same `--vh` value, same head). Those steps may not have navigated, or the URL capture is stale.
- **Axe found 18 violations.** There are 2 critical, 2 serious on the homepage, 2 serious on View Cart, 11 moderate and 2 minor. 10 are on the Homepage, 4 on View Cart and 4 on Search.
- **Console noise.** 21 errors were reported, but only 10 were listed (3 Summarizer API, 7 `unload` policy violations). The other 11 can't be assessed.

## Accessibility

**Homepage**
- **Critical:** `button-name`. At least one button has no accessible name (probably an icon-only control).
- **Critical:** `aria-required-parent`. An ARIA role is missing its required parent, e.g. `listitem` or `tab` outside `list` or `tablist`.
- **Serious:** `link-name`. At least one link has no discernible text.
- **Moderate:** `landmark-main-is-top-level` and `landmark-no-duplicate-main`. Together these mean there are multiple `<main>` landmarks, at least one nested. This is likely template composition or injected content.
- **Moderate:** `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, `region` and `heading-order`.
- **Minor:** `aria-allowed-role`.

**View Cart**
- **Serious:** `document-title` and `html-has-lang`. Both are missing.
- **Moderate:** `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one`.
- This is probably the 429 error page (see Summary).

**Search "comfort shoes"**
- `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, `region` (all moderate) and `aria-allowed-role` (minor).
- These repeat the homepage's header and footer landmark issues. They are likely site-wide template problems.

**Not reported:** No violations were listed for Log In, Men category, Men > Shoes, PDP or Add to Cart. Either axe didn't run on those steps or they were clean. The data doesn't say which, and the first two share the homepage DOM anyway.

## SEO

- The truncated HTML doesn't show `<title>`, meta description, canonical, or structured data, so these can't be assessed.
- **View Cart:** The missing `<title>` and `lang` are likely the 429 response. Confirm the real cart is `noindex`.

- **LCP and CLS:** Not captured, so no Web Vitals verdict is possible.
- **Third-party load:** The homepage `<head>` already contains about 25 third-party tags:
  - GTM, 3 gtag destinations, Quantum Metric
  - Monetate (2), Pulse Insights, Tune (go2sdk), Bing
  - TikTok, Pinterest, Yahoo, Impact, RTB House, Bloomreach
  - `webfontloader` from cdnjs
- **Category page tags:** The category page adds Taboola, Snapchat, Reddit, Stape, Facebook, Nextdoor and Snap pixels.
- **Risks:** Main-thread contention and request count are the likely issues. This can't be quantified without LCP, TBT or a waterfall.
- **Font loading:** Fonts load via a JS loader (`wf-mulish-*` classes) from a third-party CDN. That adds an extra connection and a FOUT/CLS risk.
- **Monetate:** It loads `custom.js` and `entry.js` asynchronously. Its A/B tests can cause layout shift, but CLS is unmeasured.

## Mobile Optimization

- **Viewport height:** `--vh` is set inline by JS (`6.64px`, about 664 px viewport). Modern CSS `dvh`/`svh` units remove the JS dependency and the pre-JS layout jump.
- **Viewport meta and tap targets:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, and no axe target-size violations were reported.
- **Unverified steps:** Log In and Men category appear not to have been exercised as distinct mobile views (see Summary).

## Console & Network Errors

- **Network:** 429 on `https://www.skechers.com/cart/`. This is the only network error. Likely causes are bot or WAF rate limiting of the automated session, or origin throttling. The `Retry-After` header and response body were not captured.
- **Console, `Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed in this document.` (7 listed):** Something is registering `unload` handlers, which Chrome is disabling by default. The source script is not identified in the log, and third-party tags are the usual cause.
- **Console, `Unsupported Summarizer API languages…` (3 listed):** This comes from Chrome's built-in Summarizer API being called with unsupported language options. The caller isn't identified, so it may be a page script, a third-party tag or the browser or automation environment. Treat it as unattributed.
- **Count mismatch:** 21 errors were reported but only 10 were listed.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the audit harness (high).**
   - Capture LCP and CLS with `PerformanceObserver({buffered:true})`.
   - Record the real `location.href` after each step.
   - Retry `/cart/` with backoff on 429 and re-run axe on the real cart.
2. **Investigate the 429 on `/cart/` (high).** Check whether the WAF or bot rules rate-limit real mobile users. A throttled cart is a conversion risk, not just a test artifact.
3. **Fix the critical axe issues (high).** Add accessible names to the unlabeled buttons and links, and correct ARIA parent/child roles. Run axe with `--tags` and node selectors to pin the exact elements, since the report only gave rule-level data.
4. **Fix landmark structure (medium).** Use one top-level `<main>`, unique labels for repeated landmarks, move `<aside>` to top level, and wrap stray content in landmarks.
5. **Audit third-party tags (medium).** Defer or consent-gate non-essential pixels, and measure their TBT and LCP impact. Self-host or `font-display`-optimize fonts instead of loading `webfontloader` from cdnjs.
6. **Replace `unload` handlers (low).** Find the offending scripts via DevTools stack traces and move them to `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`.
7. **Replace the JS `--vh` hack (low).** Use `dvh`/`svh` units.
