# Week of Deals: Save on Gazelle, Soft Lux, and more
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- At last, an adidas email with a number in it: up to 50% off. Even so, I'm 62 and I walk for comfort, and the hero is a black track jacket on red wrapping paper. There's no shoe in it. The subject names Gazelle and Soft Lux, but I couldn't tell you what a Soft Lux is from this picture. Compared with the Metallica tee and the Messi jersey earlier this week, this one is at least about money saved.
- The copy is easy to read, and the three big black buttons are easy to tap with a thumb. But "up to 50%" on "select" styles makes me think the good stuff is the 20% stuff. I'd glance at it, maybe tap Shop Men out of curiosity, and move on. I wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- "UP TO 50% OFF" is large and plain, and I found it without squinting.
- The body text is a readable size and names real products (Gazelle, Spezial, Soft Lux).
- The three buttons are big, black and well spaced, so they're easy to tap on a phone.
- The layout is clean, with one column and nothing broken or overlapping.
- It's a straight deals email. Compared with the Messi and Metallica sends, this one tells me why it's in my inbox.

## What's weak

- The hero is a jacket on wrapping paper, and there's no shoe in it. I came for comfort footwear.
- "Up to 50% off" on "select" styles is vague. I can't tell what's actually marked down.
- Nothing shows cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease or walking. Gazelle is a fashion sneaker, and I can't tell what Soft Lux is.
- The "Week of Deals" lettering is thin, widely spaced serif type over a busy red image. It looks fancy, but it's harder to read than it should be.
- It's yet another adidas email, and there have been about ten since the 15th. The "Special deals all week long" line at the very top is small gray underlined text that I almost took for a link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real shoe in the hero, and show the price (for example "Was $100, now $60") instead of a jacket.
- 2. Show three or four specific products with sale prices under the headline, so I can see what the deal is.
- 3. Add a "Shop men's walking and comfort" button in place of the generic Men/Women/Kids stack. Mark the deadline clearly, since "while supplies last" has no date.
- 4. Let a known customer see something chosen for them, such as a past category, instead of the same blast everyone gets.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has a good responsive structure, but the automated QA found no plain-text part (0 chars), and the rest of the data is limited. Authentication status is unknown, 21 tracking links went unprobed, and several images use `http://`. The HTML source is truncated, so nothing below the `<style>` blocks was inspected.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All 21 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains, so no destination, status, or redirect chain was verified.
- The tracked-link pattern is `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/<id>/...`, which is plain `http://`. Click wrappers over HTTP expose the redirect token in transit and may trigger mixed-content or security warnings in some clients.
- The sender domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` shares a parent with the click domain `click.comms.adidas.com`, which is good for alignment.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Plain-text fallback:** QA marks it FAIL, with a text version of 0 chars. If this is not an AgentMail capture artifact, it is a multipart/alternative gap.
- **HTTP images:** The "arrow" image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is flagged six times. Some clients block non-HTTPS images.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/509b4523-5380-42dd-a08b-8d2a7050ca4b?...` has no alt. It is not known whether it is decorative or content.
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a18fef4949-.../WjfOUB07oJ8bs0jfFaenUu-HUznPC0XvJpj7LbTMmv8=452` has no alt and loads over HTTP. It looks like an open-tracking pixel, which would make the missing alt low severity.
- **Charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it is not a valid charset declaration. This only matters if the transfer headers don't specify charset.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0" />` omits `width=device-width`. The email relies on the `@media` breakpoints at 640px and 639px.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is present, and the `@font-face` rules fall back to Arial/Helvetica via `local()`.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source or the subject line ("Week of Deals: Save on Gazelle, Soft Lux, and more"). I saw no unrendered tokens, but the truncated body couldn't be checked fully.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Authentication-Results:** QA found no header, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay. It is not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the supplied data. They can't be confirmed or flagged.
- The lack of a plain-text part (section 3) also affects deliverability and some accessibility clients.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
No UTM or landing-page data could be assessed. Every link is wrapped in the `click.comms.adidas.com` redirect, and the probe skipped them. Whether campaign parameters survive to the destination, and whether the destinations match the Gazelle and Soft Lux offers, is unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part. The QA FAIL is the only hard failure.
2. Serve the click-tracking domain and images over HTTPS, including `image.link.adidas.com` for the arrow image and `click.comms.adidas.com` for links and the pixel.
3. Add `alt` to `dv.adidas.com/o/509b4523-...`, using `alt=""` if it is decorative. Also add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Change the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`.
5. Add `width=device-width` to the viewport meta.
6. Re-run the audit with the full untruncated HTML and raw headers. That would let you verify the unsubscribe link, the `List-Unsubscribe` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and resolved click-through destinations with their UTMs.
