## What's working

- **"We Noticed You Noticing Us…"** — That headline is friendly and a little self-aware. Doesn't feel like a robot sent it. I can appreciate that.
- **The hero shoe is the right shoe.** Showing me the Glide-Step Vortex Slip-in I actually had in my cart is exactly what this email should be doing. I didn't forget — I was still thinking about it.
- **"Complete Your Order" button is prominent.** Big, blue, right under the product. Easy to tap on my phone. No hunting required.
- **20% OFF is a real enticement.** That's the kind of savings that tips me from "thinking about it" to "buying it." Wish it was higher on the page.
- **App download, Curbside Pickup, BNPL options** — Practical utility links. I like knowing I can pick up in-store if I want.

## What's weak

- **Women's shoes in my recommendations.** I'm a 62-year-old man. I can see Women's Bay-Gen styles right in my "we think you'll love" grid. That's not me. If Skechers has my purchase history, they should be able to filter this. Seeing women's shoes makes me feel like they don't actually know who I am.
- **"Mother's Day Sale" in the fine print.** Why is that down there? I'm Walker, not somebody's husband shopping for his wife. Nobody pointed me at this promo, so it just reads as noise.
- **The navigation bar is long and generic.** WOMEN, MEN, KIDS, CLOTHING, NEW ARRIVALS, SALE — this is the full site menu crammed into an email. I don't need a site map. I need one path: "buy the shoe in my cart."
- **20% off is buried.** It comes after the product recommendations, after a "Shop All Best Sellers" button. If that discount is real, lead with it.
- **"Let's Get Texting" felt like an interruption.** I'm already in the email. Don't try to move me to another channel mid-pitch.

- 1. **Move the 20% off banner above the product grid** — or at minimum, reference it in the hero: *"Still thinking? It's 20% off right now."*
- 2. **Men's-only recommendations.** I don't need to see women's styles. Filter by my profile and show me three men's comfort picks — preferably Slip-ins.
- 3. **Cut the full site navigation.** Replace the WOMEN/MEN/KIDS menu strip with two links max: **Men's Slip-ins** and **Men's Best Sellers**.

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Your Items Are Waiting!" (Cart Abandonment)

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is served via Attentive ESP (`skechers.attentivemail.com`). The source is structurally sound for a table-based email, but all click-tracking and image URLs are delivered over plain HTTP rather than HTTPS, and the preheader padding technique includes soft hyphens that can trigger spam filters.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**HTTP on all tracked links — confirmed.**
Both click-through URLs use `http://` instead of `https://`:
```
http://skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=u001.LNc6Vor...
```
Affects: "web version" link, logo link, and (based on truncation pattern) all product CTAs. HTTP redirect chains are flagged by Gmail and Outlook Safe Links as insecure; some corporate proxies block them outright.

**Image assets also served over HTTP:**
```
http://image.emai…   (src truncated but prefix is http://)
```
Mixed-content warnings will suppress images in HTTPS-rendered webmail (Gmail, Outlook.com).

**UTM parameters not verifiable.** Destination URLs are fully encoded inside Attentive's `upn=` parameter. Cannot confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, or `utm_content` values are being injected at redirect time without inspecting the decoded destination.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some email clients (Outlook desktop, Samsung Mail) display the `<title>` value as a tab label or screen-reader document title. Leaving it blank is a minor but unnecessary gap.

**Global link decoration suppression:**
```css
#MessageViewBody a { color: inherit; text-decoration: none }
```
Strips underline from all links inside Gmail's `#MessageViewBody` wrapper. No issue if CTAs are button-style, but any inline text links will have zero visual affordance.

**Preheader padding method uses dual invisible-character sets:**
```
͏ ͏ ͏  (U+034F Combining Grapheme Joiner — 150+ instances)
­ ­ ­  (U+00AD Soft Hyphen — 200+ instances)
```
CGJ + SHY mixed padding is common but Barracuda and SpamAssassin rules (particularly `INVISIBLE_TEXT`) can score against excessive hidden Unicode. The preheader itself (`You left something in your cart…`) is well-formed and under 150 chars before the padding begins — only the padding technique is the concern.

**Image alt text:** Cannot confirm from truncated source whether product images carry `alt` attributes. Logo `<img>` is not visible in excerpt — flag for manual verification.

**From display name** (`SKECHERS`) matches sender domain (`msgs.skechers.com`) — no deceptive sender identity issue.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All links route through `skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click` before redirecting to destination. UTM injection would occur at redirect resolution, not in the raw HTML. **Cannot confirm UTM coverage without decoding a live redirect.** Recommend spot-checking one decoded `upn=` URL to verify:

- `utm_medium=email`
- `utm_campaign` populated with campaign ID
- `utm_content` distinguishing CTA placement (logo vs. product vs. button)

If UTMs are absent, all cart-abandonment sessions will land in GA4/analytics as direct traffic, not email.
