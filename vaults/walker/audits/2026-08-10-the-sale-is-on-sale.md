# The sale is on sale! 😮

- Alright, now we're talking — this is the first Crocs email in a while that actually leads with a number I can do math on. "Extra 25% Off" on top of styles "already up to 50% off," that's a real deal, not just a mood board. I did the mental math without even trying, which tells you it's clear enough.
- That said, there's still not a shred of anything here for me. One pink clog on a hot pink background, no men's styles shown, no sizing, no mention of the clog I'd actually wear — the beige one pictured is fine, but it's floating there with zero context, no price shown next to it, nothing. And Jibbitz charms up top? I've never bought a charm in my life and I'm not about to start.
- I wouldn't click, but I also wouldn't delete it right off — the offer's clear enough that if I were already shopping Crocs I'd remember "oh right, 25% off," and go straight to the site rather than clicking through the email. Not forwarding this to anybody.

## What's working

- The "Extra 25% Off" headline is big, bold, and impossible to miss scrolling past it.
- They actually told me the deal stacks on styles "already up to 50% off" — that's real value math, not vague.
- There's an honest deadline in the fine print (August 17th, 11:59pm MT) even if it's buried down at the bottom.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no cartoon spider, no kid legs dangling, at least this one isn't obviously not-for-me on its face.

## What's weak

- Not one product I'd actually wear — single clog shown, no men's colorway, no lineup to browse.
- No CTA button anywhere that I can see — where do I even click to shop the sale? I don't see "Shop Now" or anything like it.
- Zero mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support — the stuff that actually gets me to buy a shoe.
- The image looks like something's cut off at the very top — text is clipped above the Crocs logo, which reads like a render glitch.
- "Exclusions Apply" with no link or detail feels like the fine print trap dressed up as plain honesty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real "Shop the Sale" button under the offer copy — right now there's nothing obvious to tap.
- 2. Show at least one men's style, ideally something like the Classic Clog or a slide, not just the single pink-background shoe.
- 3. Work in one line about comfort or cushioning — even "all-day comfort, now 25% off" would connect with a guy like me.
- 4. Fix whatever's cut off at the top of the render — that clipped text banner looks broken on a phone screen.
- 5. Move the exclusions/expiration date up near the offer instead of burying it in the footer fine print.
