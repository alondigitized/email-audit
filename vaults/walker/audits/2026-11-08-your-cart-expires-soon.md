# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- Same cart nag again, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. "You liked these" is the same black sneaker with the blue skull I've now seen on 11/05, 10/31 and earlier today. I never looked at it. It's a women's-style novelty shoe, and I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants something cushioned and easy to slip on. Telling me I "weren't wrong" about it is a little presumptuous.
- The bottom half is better. The 20% off for Skechers Plus members is easy to read, and a few of the slip-ins (the waterproof Orson boot, the Murette slipper) are close to what I'd wear. But that's buried under a hero I don't care about. I'd skip it. I wouldn't forward it either.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF banner for Plus members is big and clear, with a "Valid thru 11/10" deadline. I can read it on my phone.
- The men's slip-ins in the grid (Orson waterproof boot, Murette slipper) are in my lane: hands-free and comfortable.
- The big "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" button is easy to tap.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids and so on) is large type and simple to navigate.

## What's weak

- The "cart" is the same skull sneaker as the 11/05 emails, and I've never touched it. The "You liked these" claim feels off.
- "Your cart expires soon!" promises urgency, but nothing in the email says when it expires or what's in the cart.
- The hero shoe isn't for me, and half the grid is women's styles.
- The 20% off is buried below the shoes, so I have to scroll to find the only real offer.
- This is the sixth cart-style send in about two weeks. It's tiring, and it makes me trust the brand less.
- The fine print is small, though the exclusions at least appear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart reminders to people who have no cart. If someone really did leave something, show the real item and say what's in it.
- 2. Move the 20% member offer to the top, under the logo.
- 3. Lead with men's slip-ins and arch-support styles when the reader is a 62-year-old man.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the subject, like "ends 11/10".
- 5. Cap how often these go out. Three a week is too many.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Cart-abandonment email routed through Attentive (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com`) with a confirmed missing plain-text part, 11 images served over `http://`, and an unsubscribe link that returned 400 when probed. SPF/DKIM status couldn't be verified, and the HTML was truncated, so footer and address checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400 (FAIL):** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_lid=m60…` (link text "Unsubscribe"). `attn_macro` decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, so this is the real unsubscribe endpoint. The 400 may be caused by the probe, since the token is recipient-bound and may be rejected for a non-browser request. It needs a manual click-through to confirm.
- **Two 429 responses (not confirmed broken):** "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." Both are `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirects. The rate limit prevented validation of their destinations.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 44 links were probed. The other 32, mostly footer and social links, are unverified.
- **Opaque tracking:** every link is wrapped in a base64-style Attentive redirect, so final destinations can't be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is empty (FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. There is no `text/plain` alternative, which hurts deliverability and screen-reader or text-only clients.
- **11 images use `http://`:** all are on `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`. They include the logo `dde00662-…png`, the "Complete Your Order" hero `dfcc8fd7-…jpg`, and the App Store, Google Play, Afterpay, Curbside Pickup, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons. These risk mixed-content blocking or rewriting in some clients. Serve them over HTTPS.
- **Missing alt text:** `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` (a content image) has none. The `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` tracking pixel also has no alt, which is low severity.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this is a minor issue for web-version rendering and accessibility.
- **Charset declaration is ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so some clients may ignore it.
- **Mobile CSS:** the `@media (max-width:620px)` rules target `.row-18`, `.row-19` and `.row-21`. The truncated HTML doesn't let me check them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (e.g. `{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The product block ("You liked these…") sits beyond the truncation point, so it couldn't be inspected.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unsubscribe:** present and labelled, but it returned 400 in the probe (see §2). It is a hosted unsubscribe page, which is acceptable if it works.
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `msgs.skechers.com` are unverified. The cause is the AgentMail relay, so this is a data gap rather than a confirmed failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** not supplied in the QA data, so I can't assess RFC 8058 one-click support.
- **Physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.
- **Sender:** `hello@msgs.skechers.com` is a clear, consistent sender.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. Click-tracking wrappers hide the destination URLs and any UTM parameters, and the two key CTAs returned 429. The "web version" link is also an opaque Attentive redirect. Verify the "Complete Your Order" destination by following the redirect with a real browser session. Check it lands on the cart with the items restored.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` part.** It is currently 0 chars, and this is the highest-confidence deliverability fix.
2. **Switch all 11 `http://image.emails.skechers.com` image sources to `https://`.**
3. **Manually verify the unsubscribe URL** (`skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…`) in a browser to rule out a real 400. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present.
