# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- Same shoe, same story. That pink floral D'Lites is back in my cart — that's the fifth time this week according to my own inbox, and it still isn't mine, because I never put it there. "We noticed you noticing us" is cute copywriting for a shoe I never clicked on, and it just makes me trust the whole operation less, not more.
- Buried below the fold there's actually a decent offer — 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step with a code — which is the kind of thing that would get me to click if it led the email instead of hiding under a wall of "more options" I didn't ask for. As it stands, I'm marking this read and moving on. Not forwarding it, not clicking it — I already trashed the last four of these.

## What's working

- The 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step banner near the bottom is a real, specific offer with a code I can actually use.
- "Shop All Best Sellers" is a clean, unambiguous button if I did want to browse.
- Payment and pickup options (Afterpay, Klarna, curbside) are listed clearly enough, even if I don't need them.

## What's weak

- That floral women's sneaker again, front and center — I don't wear it, didn't cart it, and seeing it every single day this week feels less like marketing and more like a glitch.
- The recommended styles below it are a grab bag — kids' Garage Sneakers, women's BOBS, a basketball shoe — nothing says "comfort, cushioned, slip-on for a 62-year-old guy."
- The real offer (20% off) is buried two-thirds down the page, after a fake cart and a scattershot product wall.
- Headline "YOUR CART IS STILL WAITING" is just false — there's no cart of mine waiting on anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake abandoned-cart framing entirely for me — I've gotten this same shoe five times now and it's actively pushing me away. Lead instead with the 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step deal, since that's the one thing in here I'd actually act on.
- 2. Swap the "more options" row for men's comfort styles only — Arch Fit, Slip-ins, Glide Step — not kids sneakers and basketball shoes.
- 3. Move the 20% off banner to the top of the email, above the fold.
- 4. Fix whatever's re-adding that pink floral sneaker to my cart reminder — it's not a style preference miss, it's a broken personalization signal.
