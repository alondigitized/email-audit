# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- Another cart nag, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. This is the same "Did you forget something?" routine I got on 10/23 and the "Your Items Are Waiting!" sends on 10/21, 10/23 and 10/28. "Ready to check out?" in giant gold letters is a bit much for a guy who was only browsing.
- The hero shoe is a black GO WALK Workout Walker with a loud green swirl sole. It's a walking shoe, which is my category, but it's flashy and I didn't ask for it. Below it are six shoes, and most of them are women's or a boy's sneaker. Only two are men's, and the John Deere boot and the Contour Foam leather slip-in are the only ones I'd look at twice. There's a 20% OFF for Skechers Plus members banner, but it's buried under the shoes. There's no "Hi, Walker" and no points balance, which I liked in the 10/22 and 10/24 emails.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it, maybe tap the men's slip-in, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF banner is a real number, in big white type on dark blue, and it's easy to read.
- The Skechers logo is at the top and the layout is simple, so I know who sent it.
- The two men's Slip-ins, the John Deere boot and the Contour Foam sneaker, are my kind of hands-free shoe.
- The WOMEN / MEN / KIDS / SALE list is big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The offer dates are spelled out (valid thru 11/2).

## What's weak

- "Ready to check out?" when I have nothing in a cart feels pushy and a little creepy.
- The hero shoe is a flashy green-and-black thing, not the plain comfort shoe I wear.
- Four of the six grid shoes are women's or kids'. It doesn't feel like it's for a 62-year-old man.
- The 20% banner sits below the fold, under all the shoes. It should be the first thing I see.
- The product captions are tiny, and there's no price, no arch-support note and no reviews.
- The fine print says Skechers Plus members only, but nothing at the top tells me whether I'm one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Only send a cart email when there's actually a cart. If there isn't one, send a browse email built around the 20% offer.
- 2. Put the 20% banner right under the logo, with a clear "Hi, Walker" and my member status.
- 3. Make the hero a men's Slip-in with a plain line like "Hands-free, arch-supported, 20% off."
- 4. Fill the grid with men's walking shoes and add the price and a star rating.
- 5. Make the captions bigger, since I read on my phone.
