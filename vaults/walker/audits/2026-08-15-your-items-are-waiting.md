# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sneaker, front and center, big as life — the exact one Skechers has been hammering me with since at least the 11th. "We Noticed You Noticing Us..." Well I noticed alright — I noticed I never put that shoe in any cart, and I noticed this is the third or fourth day running with the same picture. It's not even close to my size or style; I don't wear women's floral D'Lites, so whatever tracking they're using is broken.
- Buried under all that cart nonsense is actually a real sale banner — up to 40% off summer styles — which is the only thing in this email that could get my attention, but it's stuck two-thirds of the way down after I've already tuned out. The "more great options" grid has some men's shoes mixed in — the Arch Fit Arcade slip-ins and the Delson 3.0 Roth — but they're tiny thumbnails competing with more shots of that pink shoe.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on. Not clicking, not forwarding — my buddy doesn't need to see Skechers still can't figure out I'm a 62-year-old guy who wears a size 11 in slip-ins, not a floral sneaker in whatever size that model's cart supposedly had.

## What's working

- The 40% off summer styles banner is a legit, specific offer with a clear "Shop the Sale" link.
- "Complete Your Order" button is big, blue, and easy to tap on my phone.
- The Arch Fit Arcade slip-ins and Delson 3.0 Roth thumbnails are at least in my category — hands-free and men's casual.
- Payment options (Afterpay, Klarna) and curbside pickup are visible if I wanted them, though I don't need financing for a pair of shoes.

## What's weak

- That pink floral D'Lites is back for what feels like the fifth straight day — I never put it in a cart, and this "we noticed you noticing us" guilt-trip framing is getting old fast.
- The recommendation grid is mostly women's shoes and one basketball shoe — none of that speaks to a guy shopping for cushioned, arch-supported walking shoes.
- The real offer — 40% off — is buried below a fake cart-abandonment pitch and a grid of irrelevant shoes instead of leading the email.
- No sizing, fit, or arch-support detail anywhere, which is the first thing I actually care about before I'd click through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake cart-abandonment angle entirely for a shoe I never touched — lead with the 40% off sale since that's the only honest, working offer here.
- 2. Fix the recommendation engine — if I'm getting served the same floral sneaker for the fifth day, whatever "you noticed" logic is running needs to actually track browsing, not just replay one SKU.
- 3. Put men's comfort styles (the Arch Fit Arcade, the Delson) up top where the floral shoe currently sits — that's what a repeat men's shopper would actually respond to.
- 4. Add one line of fit info — cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease — next to the hero product so I know it's worth a tap before I click.
