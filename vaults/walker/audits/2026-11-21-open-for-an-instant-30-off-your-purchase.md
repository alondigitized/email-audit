# Open for an INSTANT $30 OFF your purchase
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival again. I've lost count. This is the same $10 / $20 / $30 off coupon I've had since the 13th, and this time the subject line says "INSTANT $30 OFF." Look at the coupon and the $30 only applies at $149.98 or more. Nothing instant about it. The Black Friday banner and the big "GIFT MORE, SAVE MORE" scroll are easy to read on my phone, which I appreciate.
- Then I scroll and it's boots, boots, boots. Women's boots, tall boots, a Christina Royale designer feature, a gift shop. The only men's item is one $49.98 boot block, and it shows dressy Chelsea-style leather boots. No cushioning talk, no arch support, no slip-on, no Skechers or Hoka in sight. That's not how I shop.
- Would I click? Probably not. Forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the Skechers email, like the 25% off one on the 19th.

## What's working

- The coupon scroll is big and the $10/$20/$30 tiers are easy to read without my glasses.
- "Friday, Saturday & Sunday" makes the weekend window clear.
- The "Get In Store Coupon" button is big and easy to tap.
- The barcodes at the bottom with the tiers are handy, so I could just show my phone at the register.
- The "Up to 70% OFF" Black Friday banner fits the season.

## What's weak

- "Instant $30 off" in the subject is misleading. The $30 needs a $149.98 purchase, and that's buried in small print on the coupon.
- It's the same coupon I've seen for a week. I count at least four or five variations since the 13th.
- Nothing is about comfort. No cushioning, arch support, walking, or slip-on shoes, and no brand names I trust.
- Men's gets one block of dress boots. Most of the email is women's boots and a designer feature.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and I can't read it without zooming.
- The preview text came through as a long tracking link instead of real copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with what I buy. Put a men's comfort row (Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance) right under the coupon, with "slip-on" and "all-day cushion" in the copy.
- 2. Make the subject honest. Say the real thresholds, like "$10 off $60, up to $30 off $150."
- 3. Stop sending the same coupon every day. Rotate the creative and give the offer a real end date.
- 4. Make the minimum-spend numbers bigger, not footnote-sized.
- 5. Cut the designer and women's blocks for people who've shopped men's.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort shoes for him: coupon inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Show the barcode in store Fri–Sun. Men's walking and slip-on styles included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save up to $30 on cushioned comfort brands this weekend.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based Listrak-style (emltrk.com) send. The two flagged images are tracking pixels, one of which has a malformed URL and one an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder. The source was truncated, so the body links, footer and headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed).** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261121_christina_royale_coop_AM​` ends with a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. This can corrupt the campaign value or break the request in some clients and proxies.
- **Malformed query structure.** The UTM is nested inside the `t=` value (`t=utm_campaign=...`) rather than passed as its own parameter. Downstream parsers may not read it as `utm_campaign`.
- **Duplicate open pixels.** Two separate emltrk.com pixels (`ieezzy5m`, `0di86k1v`) are present. This may double-count opens.
- **Unverified links.** 27 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status are unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pixel alt text.** The QA check flags both tracking pixels for missing alt text. This is low impact, but they should carry `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them.
- **Ineffective meta tags (confirmed in source).**
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. The charset attribute alone is generally honored, but the `content` value is malformed.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an XHTML `xmlns` and self-closing `/>` tags.
- **Inconsistent breakpoints.** Media queries use 600px, 660px and 450px, and the `.responsive-text` rules use 600/601. Layout and text sizing can switch at different widths.
- **Link styling override.** A global `a { color:inherit !important; text-decoration:none !important }` removes link affordance for any text links. Check that this doesn't make inline links indistinguishable.
- **Positive.** `lang="en"` and a viewport meta tag are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved placeholder (likely).** The pixel `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` contains a literal `[UNIQUE]` in the delivered message. If it is meant to be substituted per recipient, the open event can't be attributed to a recipient. If it is a static convention of the ESP, confirm with the vendor.
- No other merge tokens could be assessed in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results not found.** The QA data attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a monitoring-side gap rather than a sender defect. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status for `email.shoecarnival.com` is unverified.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` header:** not determinable. The HTML was truncated and no header data was provided. This is not flagged as a failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM scope.** The only UTM visible is in the open pixel: `utm_campaign=SCA_20261121_christina_royale_coop_AM`. Whether the 27 click links carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` can't be confirmed.
- **Internal naming exposed.** The campaign string contains a personal name and "coop" in a recipient-visible URL. This is a hygiene note, not a functional bug.
- **Landing pages.** No destination URLs were probed, so landing page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the U+200B from the `ieezzy5m` pixel URL, and pass the UTM as a proper query parameter.
2. Confirm that `d=[UNIQUE]` is resolved at send time. If it isn't, fix the ESP substitution.
3. Remove the redundant second open pixel, or confirm that both are intentional.
4. Add `alt=""` to both pixels.
5. Fix the meta tags (`http-equiv="Content-Type"`, `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`) and use a single consistent mobile breakpoint.
6. Extend the QA run to the full HTML and headers. That would let it check the unsubscribe link, the `List-Unsubscribe` header, the footer address and the UTM coverage on click links, and resolve one redirect per tracking domain to confirm the destination.
7. Capture raw headers upstream of the relay to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
