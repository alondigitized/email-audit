# Shop clearance up to 60% off
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW clearance email I've seen three times this week, just with the number back up to "60% off" instead of yesterday's flat 60. Size grid up top is 5 through 11, all women's sizing, and everything below it — booties, heels, strappy sandals, loafers with little bows — is women's product too. Not one men's shoe on the page, not one sneaker that isn't a women's colorway. I scroll all the way down past six different "shop" sections and there's nothing here for me.
- I'd mark this as read without clicking. Wouldn't forward it — nothing to forward, it's just not for me.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF CLEARANCE" headline is at least a real, specific number I can see without hunting for it.
- Layout is clean, no overlapping text or broken images, easy enough to scroll on my phone.
- "Buy Now, Pay Later with Afterpay" and free shipping icons near the bottom are legible, though irrelevant to me today.

## What's weak

- Size grid is 5–11, that's women's sizing — I wear a 10.5 or 11 men's, so this told me in the first three inches of the email that it's not for me.
- Every single product shot — booties, heels, sandals, loafers — is a women's shoe. No men's casual, no walking shoes, no arch support anywhere.
- "Your Mid Week Mood Boost... Shop Women" section makes it explicit this send is women-only, yet it landed in my inbox anyway.
- I've now gotten this same "up to 60% off clearance" pitch from DSW basically every day this week — 9/14, 9/15 twice, and now 9/16 again. Nothing new to see.
- VIP tote offer and points messaging at the bottom is the same loyalty pitch I've already seen and shrugged off before.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender before sending — if I've never clicked a women's shoe in my browsing history, don't put me on this list. That's the single biggest fix; nothing else matters if the product is wrong.
- 2. Swap in a men's comfort/clearance module — Skechers, Hoka-style, walking shoes with arch support — if DSW wants my click at all.
- 3. Cut the send frequency. Four nearly identical "up to 60% off" emails inside three days trains me to stop opening, not to buy.
- 4. Lead with something other than the size grid if it's staying women-only — at least let me self-select out faster instead of scrolling.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's clearance: up to 60% off comfort shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your savings — up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns in men's walking & casual styles`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort picks now up to 60% off`
