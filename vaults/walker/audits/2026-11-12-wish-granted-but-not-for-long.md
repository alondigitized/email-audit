# Wish granted, but not for long ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight deal and I like that. After a week of the skull sneaker cart nags (11/05, 11/08, and the three on 11/11), a plain "30% off sitewide for members" is a relief. "Hi, Walker" and the member bar are at the top, so I feel known. The 11:11 "wish" gimmick is cute, but it's just decoration on top of a sale.
- Then I got to the fine print. "Valid online 11/11/26 only," and the email's own date is 11/12, so I'm not sure whether I'm looking at a sale that's already over. That's a trust problem. It's also the third 30% or 20% member promo in three days, so the urgency doesn't feel real. I'd click to look, because 30% off a comfort shoe is real money. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF is huge and I can't miss it. One offer, one red SHOP NOW button.
- "Hi, Walker" with the member tag and the 1000 points ($5 reward) shows they know who I am.
- Slip-ins and Cozy Fit are right at the top, and those are the hands-free, cushioned shoes I want.
- The Men's size buttons are big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The text is large enough to read without zooming.

## What's weak

- "Valid online 11/11/26 only" next to a 11/12 send date makes me think it's expired. That's an honesty problem.
- It says "Sitewide," then the fine print excludes premieres, sale styles, select accessories and more. That's a bait-and-switch feel.
- The four tiles show no prices, no model names, and nothing about arch support or cushioning. The red mesh sneaker doesn't look like me.
- There are too many size grids, including Women's and Kids'. The Little Kids and Big Kids rows are irrelevant to me and make it a very long scroll.
- The "Wish Granted" and "11:11" theme says nothing about shoes or comfort.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the date so the email and the deadline agree, and say "ends tonight" in plain words. Send it before the deal ends.
- 2. Put one real hero shoe, such as a men's slip-in walker, with a price and "now $X with member 30%". Add a line about cushioning or arch support.
- 3. Shorten the size section. Show it as one men's row, or skip it, and drop the kids' grids for a male 62-year-old.
- 4. Move the exclusions up near the offer instead of burying them at the bottom.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Don't send a third member promo this week.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Wish granted, but not for long ✨"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering and personalization failure (an empty greeting) and several markup defects in the visible source. The most serious is a scheme-less tracking pixel URL. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body link checks are limited. The QA run also didn't capture authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `https://` or `//`. Clients resolve it as a relative path against the message or host, so the request fails or 404s. The Return Path/Validity deliverability pixel will not record opens. The QA run did not flag this.
- **Insecure `http://` pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_11112026_PM`. Proxies and strict clients may block it. The QA warning is confirmed.
- **Hashed identifiers sent to a third party:** Two `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` pixels pass `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00` (a SHA-256-length value, `partner=…_sha25`) and `partner_uid=121173391` (`…_subid`). A hashed identifier is being sent to an ad-tech domain on every open.
- **Hidden open pixels:** The `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx` pixel and the krxd pixels sit inside `display:none !important` containers. Some clients skip loading images in hidden elements, so open counts can be undercounted.
- **Unprobed links:** The QA run skipped 55 tracking links (click-redirect domains). Destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (ink1000) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` have no `alt` attribute. Both are served over `http://` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-…png`). The png may be a content image, which would break if blocked, but I can't confirm its role from the truncated source.
- **Malformed `<meta>` tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declaration is valid. The charset is not actually declared in the HTML.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`. Screen readers can't pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.
- **Layout tables:** The visible tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Mobile/desktop toggle:** `.mobile-content {display:block}` and `.desktop-content {display:none}` rely on `@media (min-width:768px)` to switch. Clients that strip or ignore media queries will show only the mobile variant. I can't confirm these classes are used in the truncated body.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers®</title>` is generic and not tied to the campaign.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`, which indicates the first-name field resolved to empty with no fallback value. This is a broken-experience defect. The SFMC personalization string needs a default, such as `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstName),"there",@firstName)=%%`.
- No other unresolved tokens are visible in the supplied source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header wasn't found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a limitation of the AgentMail relay path and not a confirmed failure. The sender `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` and click domain `click.emails.skechers.com` share the `emails.skechers.com` subdomain, which suggests alignment.
- **Unsubscribe, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** I can't evaluate these. The HTML is truncated before the footer, and the headers weren't supplied.
- **Hashed-ID sharing:** The krxd usermatch pixels (section 2) may warrant a privacy-policy and consent check. I can't confirm a violation from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_11112026_PM` appears in both the krxd impression pixel (`campaignid=`) and the ink1000 pixel (`mi_ecmp=`), so the ID is consistent across vendors.
- **UTM parameters and landing pages:** No body links or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source, and the 55 tracking links weren't probed. UTM presence, landing-page match and the 30% offer on the destination can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
2. Move `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to HTTPS.
3. Add a fallback to the first-name merge (`Hi there,` or drop the name). Add a send-time QA gate that blocks any `Hi ,` render.
4. Add `alt` text to `dde00662-….png`, or `alt=""` if it is decorative. Add `alt=""` to the 1×1 pixels.
5. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `outline:none`. Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
7. Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers to check the footer unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, the physical address, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, UTM parameters, and a sample of the 55 skipped click links via redirect-following.
8. Have privacy or legal review the krxd hashed-identifier pixels against the stated privacy policy.
