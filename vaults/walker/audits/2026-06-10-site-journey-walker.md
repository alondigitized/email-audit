## 1. Executive Summary

Same score, different problems. Yesterday I was frustrated. Today I'm frustrated *and* blocked — the cart page is a blank white screen. That's a regression, full stop. A few things held steady: the Men's navigation menu is still solid with big tap targets, and the Hands-Free Slip-ins category is easy to find. ## 3. What's Working

- **BOGO 50% OFF** is front and center in the top banner — I see it immediately without scrolling. That's money in my pocket and I notice it.
- **Men's navigation menu** (Step 4) is excellent. Large text, generous tap targets, clean rows. "Hands Free Slip-ins" appears as a featured shortcut at the bottom — exactly what I want without digging.
- **Hands Free Slip-ins landing page** (Step 5) has a useful fit filter row — Wide Fit, Extra Wide Fit, Medium Fit — in prominent blue buttons. As a guy who needs a comfortable fit, I can filter right away.
- **Product page price and deal** (Step 6) — $120 is clear, and "Members Get Buy 1, Get 1 50% OFF in Cart" in red catches my eye. Good.
- **Size grid** (Step 7) is well-spaced. Each size box is large enough to tap without squinting. The "Please select a size" warning in red with a triangle is clear enough that I know what I need to do before tapping Add to Cart.
- **ADD TO CART button** is a full-width blue bar pinned to the bottom of the screen. Hard to miss, easy to tap.
- **Search results** (Step 9) returned 1,481 results for "comfort shoes" — that's a real, relevant catalog response. First visible results are Arch Fit Slip-ins, which is appropriate.

## 4. What's Weak

**Critical:**
- **Cart page is a blank white screen** (Step 8). Nothing loaded. No items, no total, no checkout button — just white. I have no idea if the shoe is in there. This is a hard stop on the purchase path. This is a regression from yesterday.

**Significant:**

- **Cart count stayed at 0 throughout** — even in the Add to Cart step (Step 7), the cart badge shows 0. That's disorienting. Did my tap do anything?

**Moderate:**

Nothing on this hero says "comfort," "arch support," or "walks without back pain." The word "Summer" is just starting to appear at the bottom. Fashion-first framing.

- **Only 15 reviews on the Kynley** (Step 6). I cross-shop. Hoka and Brooks products I look at have hundreds of reviews. 15 doesn't give me confidence. I want to know what other guys my age think of this shoe after 6 months.

I do not see "Max Cushioning" explained, no mention of memory foam, no mention of how this compares to the Arch Fit line. That's actually fine by me — I hate popups — but it means the site didn't try to capture my email either, which seems like a missed loyalty moment.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page immediately.** A blank cart is a complete checkout blocker. If this is a render/hydration failure it needs a hotfix today. This is the single highest-priority issue.

2. **Make login persist and surface a welcome state.** If I'm a member, show me. Show my name in the nav. Show my points balance. Make the BOGO deal feel like it's mine, not a generic teaser.

3. **Surface comfort language on the product page above the fold.** "Max Cushioning," "memory foam insole," "arch support" — these should be the first three bullet points I see after the price, not buried below the fold. That's what I'm buying.

4. **Add a "sort by most reviews" or "top rated for walking" option to the Slip-ins listing page.** 298 results is too many to browse on a phone. Give me a confidence signal upfront.

5. **Rethink the homepage hero for the Boomer male segment.** At minimum, the Slip-ins / Arch Fit category tiles in the horizontal nav are doing the right job — but the hero image directly below should rotate to include a 50s–60s male walking comfortably, not just a young fashion shot.

6. **Fix the Men's/Women's ordering in search results** when the session context is men's navigation.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

Site runs on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFCC/Demandware), evidenced by `/on/demandware.static/Sites-USSkechers-Site/` paths. Bot fingerprinting is handled by Kasada (`x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.470`). The most severe finding is the `/cart/` endpoint returning HTTP 429, which is likely causing View Cart to render a degraded/error page — explaining that page's complete absence of `<title>`, `lang`, and `<main>`. 20+ async third-party scripts load on every page. Two third-party script tags are duplicated.

## Accessibility

**15 violations across 3 pages.**

**Homepage (8 violations):**
| Severity | Rule | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| critical | `aria-required-parent` | ARIA role elements lack required ancestor roles — screen readers will misparse content |
| moderate | `heading-order` | Heading hierarchy is non-sequential |
| moderate | `landmark-no-duplicate-main` | Multiple `<main>` elements present |
| moderate | `landmark-main-is-top-level` | `<main>` is nested inside another landmark |
| moderate | `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` | `<aside>` is nested inside another landmark |
| moderate | `landmark-unique` | Duplicate landmark roles present |
| moderate | `region` | Content exists outside any landmark |
| minor | `aria-allowed-role` | Invalid ARIA role on an element |

The duplicate/nested landmark pattern on the homepage is consistent with third-party content injection (likely Monetate A/B variants or Taboola widgets) being inserted outside the base template's landmark structure.

**View Cart (4 violations — most severe):**
| Severity | Rule | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| serious | `document-title` | Missing `<title>` element |
| serious | `html-has-lang` | `<html>` has no `lang` attribute |
| moderate | `landmark-one-main` | No `<main>` landmark on page |
| moderate | `page-has-heading-one` | No `<h1>` present |

All four View Cart violations are consistent with a 429-triggered fallback/error page rendering instead of the real cart template. The real cart page almost certainly has these elements.

**Search (3 violations):**
Same landmark/region pattern as Homepage — likely the same injected widget issue.

## SEO

- View Cart's missing `<title>` is a P1 defect, though cart pages should carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`. If that directive is also absent from the fallback page, a bot crawling a cart 429-response could index an empty-title page.
- The homepage `<html lang="en">` is correctly set, confirming the View Cart `lang` omission is specific to the error/fallback render path.
- No structural SEO issues observed on Homepage, category, or PDP from the available HTML samples.

## Mobile Optimization

- CSS custom property `--vh: 6.640000000000001px` is set inline on `<html>` — a standard JS workaround for mobile browsers' dynamic viewport height (`100dvh` is not universally supported on the tested device). The floating-point imprecision (`6.640000000000001` vs `6.64`) is harmless but indicates the JS is not rounding before assignment.
- The `wf-*` classes (`wf-mulish-n3-active` through `wf-mulish-n8-active`) on `<html>` confirm WebFontLoader is loading Mulish in 6 weight variants synchronously enough to add classes before first paint — this is typically a FOUT risk on slow connections but the low TTFB suggests fonts are CDN-cached.
- No viewport `<meta>` tag visible in the truncated HTML; assumed present but not confirmed.

## Console & Network Errors

| Type | Count | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| HTTP 429 | 5× | Kasada fingerprint endpoint: `/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/.../fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.470` |
| HTTP 429 | 1× | `/cart/` — the cart page itself is rate-limited |
| CORS block | 1× | `web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css` blocked; no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header |
| `net::ERR_FAILED` | 1× | Downstream failure, likely the CORS-blocked Stylitics request |

**Kasada 429s:** The Kasada bot protection script is rate-limiting its own fingerprinting calls. This is atypical — it may indicate the script is firing more frequently than the Kasada service expects (e.g., SPA navigation re-initializing the SDK on each route), or the Kasada tenant config has a low request budget. On a real user device this would degrade the bot-protection signal, potentially allowing actual bots through or causing legitimate sessions to be challenged.

**Cart 429:** This is the most impactful finding. The `/cart/` URL returning 429 means the Kasada challenge is being applied to the cart page load itself, blocking the request before SFCC can render the cart template. This would present as a broken or empty cart to affected mobile users — a direct revenue impact.

**Stylitics CORS:** The outfit recommendation widget's stylesheet is blocked. The widget will render without styles (or fail silently). The fix requires Stylitics to add `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://www.skechers.com` to their CDN response headers, or for Skechers to proxy the asset through their own domain.
