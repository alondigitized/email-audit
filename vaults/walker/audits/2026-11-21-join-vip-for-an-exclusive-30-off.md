# Join VIP for an exclusive 30% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count. This is the same pink "Black Friday Sale" banner and the same giant black "30% OFF*" I've now seen about six times in two days, from "Join VIP, get access to 30% off!" on the 20th through this morning's "Save 30% as a VIP (Sign up for free!)." Only the subject line changes. The big number is easy to read on my phone without my glasses, and I'll give them that.
- There's a lot of footwear below the banner, but it doesn't speak to me. I see lug-sole boots, fashion sneakers, a red mary-jane and a fuzzy wedge. I don't see cushioned walking shoes, slip-ons or arch support. The only shoes that look like mine are the Adidas and New Balance sneakers, and the prices are "up to 25% off," not the 30%. The "almost everything" line and the long fine print tell me the 30% comes with strings, and I like that less each time.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. I'm not a Black Friday shopper hunting for a boot, and I'm tired of being asked to become a VIP.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF*" is huge and I can read it on my phone without squinting.
- The black "Become a VIP" button stands out clearly against the pink.
- The Shop Women / Men / Kids links are right under the banner, so I can get to men's quickly.
- The "Shop by Size" block has big, readable numbers and a Wide Width button. Wide width matters to my feet.
- Free shipping for VIPs and in-store pickup are listed plainly.

## What's weak

- It's the sixth or so near-identical VIP pitch in two days, and I'm numb to it.
- The product grid is mostly women's and fashion styles. I see no comfort, walking or slip-on shoes.
- The asterisk and the "(almost) everything" wording make me suspect exclusions. The fine print is tiny and buried at the bottom, including "Brands which qualify include Skechers, Kelly & Katie…". I shouldn't have to scroll to learn that.
- The product tags are inconsistent. "Up to 25% off," "Extra 30% off," "Up to 50% off" and "Up to 30% off" all appear, so I can't tell what I'd actually pay.
- The top ticker banner is cut off mid-word ("BIGGE"), and the "Early Black Friday: Up to 30% Off Nike" line above it competes with the main banner.
- The UGG/Birkenstock 500-point offer needs a purchase and a VIP signup. It's a lot of hoops for a points balance, and points don't decide my purchase anyway.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same pitch under five subject lines. One VIP email a week would do, and the rest of the sends should show something different.
- 2. Put one clear message in the hero. Either "Join VIP" or "30% off Black Friday" is fine, but both together muddy it.
- 3. Add a men's comfort row to the grid with slip-ons, walking shoes and cushioned sneakers from Skechers, Hoka and Brooks. I'd click on a shoe I could walk in.
- 4. Show the actual sale price on each shoe instead of a mix of "up to" percentages.
- 5. Move the exclusions near the offer. A short line like "Excludes Nike, UGG, Hoka" up top would earn some trust.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned sneakers and slip-ons from Skechers, Brooks and New Balance. Code BFDEALS.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run found one confirmed failure: the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA tracking link returned HTTP 400. The remaining warnings are mostly low-severity or caused by the test setup. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible and to the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA link (HTTP 400).** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" and the URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/SuZQ6ixdYoio4_d7WBMmbw~~/AAQRxRA~/rLnZLgcg…`. It is a tracked redirect on `ablink.dsw.com`, and the QA data does not show where it resolves.
  - The 400 may be real (a malformed or expired redirect token), or it may come from the probe, since some trackers reject automated or non-browser requests. The data can't tell these apart, so the link needs a manual check.
  - If the 400 is real, the SMS opt-in CTA is dead for every recipient.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 82 links were probed and 70 were skipped, so the other links' status is unknown.
- **Tracking-wrapper hosts.** All links are wrapped on `ablink.dsw.com`, and the open pixel is on `mi.dsw.com`. Both are first-party subdomains, so there is no cross-domain tracking-host issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images, both likely tracking pixels:**
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261121_P251273_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_D` is an open-tracking GIF.
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/qDapUzxbMc1z…` is a `/q/` path, probably an open pixel too.
  - Pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. Impact is low.
- **Meta tags without attributes (from the visible source):** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Without `http-equiv`, neither tag does anything. Encoding then depends on the MIME `Content-Type` header alone.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which screen readers use to pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` does not describe the message.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The pixel's `mi_u=225090073` is a resolved user identifier. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found.** The QA note says this is expected through the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, not failing. It is a test-environment limitation and not a confirmed defect on the sender's side.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (31,594 of 37,213 chars). That is a spam-scoring risk. It comes from long tracking URLs such as the `ablink` links above, which can run to roughly 500 characters each.
- **Unsubscribe link.** The QA notes say it is always included in probing, and no failure was reported. The footer was truncated from the source, so the physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters cannot be verified. Every link goes through the `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, so the final landing URLs, and any `utm_*` values on them, were not captured. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261121_P251273_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_D` on the pixel. The QA data has no landing-page alignment checks beyond the one 400.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Check the SMS opt-in link first.** Open the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/SuZQ6ixd…` URL in a real browser and with a browser-like User-Agent. If it still returns 400, fix the redirect or re-issue the link in the ESP before the next send.
2. **Probe the 70 skipped links** with redirect-following GETs, and record each final landing URL and its `utm_*` values.
3. **Fix the meta tags.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`, `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, and a more descriptive `<title>`.
5. **Slim the plain-text part** by trimming or shortening tracking URLs.
6. **Capture the raw headers** (`Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) from a direct inbox seed, since the relay hides them.
