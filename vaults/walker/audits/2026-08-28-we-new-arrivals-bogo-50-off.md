# We ❤️ new arrivals + BOGO 50% OFF
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW playbook again — BOGO 50% off up top, then a wall of clogs, mules, mary janes, leopard heels, and strappy sandals. I've now seen this exact deal five days running (24th through the 27th), and today it's dressed up as "new arrivals," but it's the same women's shoe wall I've been scrolling past all week. Not one men's shoe until you get way down to a small "Athletic & Sneakers" tile with what looks like an adidas Samba — and even that's paired with a women's shoe right next to it.
- There's a "Free IMBOX Protection Spray with any in-store purchase" banner that's mildly interesting if I'm already going in, but it's buried under a screen and a half of women's footwear I'll never buy. This one's a scroll-and-delete. Wouldn't forward it, wouldn't click — I've made my peace with DSW just not stocking anything in my size or style by now.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly at the top, no fine print hunting required.
- The size grid (5 through 11, plus wide width/wide calf) is clear and easy to read.
- Free shipping for VIPs and buy-now-pay-later with Afterpay are visible if I were closer to pulling the trigger.
- Layout is clean, no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- "Hey, what's new?" followed by six rows of women's heels, clogs, and flats — nothing new to me, and nothing in my department.
- No men's casual, walking, or comfort shoe shown until one tiny sneaker tile buried two-thirds down the page.
- No cushioning, arch support, or comfort-brand language anywhere — this reads like a fashion email, not a comfort-shopper email.
- The IMBOX spray offer is a decent hook but it's stuck below a mountain of irrelevant product, so I'd never scroll far enough to see it on a normal day.
- Same BOGO 50% off deal I've now gotten daily since the 24th — the "new arrivals" hook doesn't disguise it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me a men's-only version — lead with a men's comfort sneaker or slip-on, not six rows of women's clogs and heels. That's the single biggest fix.
- 2. Move the IMBOX free-spray offer up near the top — it's actually a decent incentive and it's getting buried.
- 3. Vary the deal. Five straight days of BOGO 50% off trains me to stop opening these — earn back my attention with something different, even if it's just framed differently.
- 4. Add a comfort callout — arch support, cushioned insole, walking-friendly — somewhere in the copy so I know there's a reason to look past the discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New men's comfort styles + BOGO 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Slip-on, cushioned, on sale: BOGO 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's walking shoes just landed — buy one, get 50% off a second pair`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort-first arrivals for fall, all BOGO 50% off`
