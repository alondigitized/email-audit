# Up to 30% off UGG 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the eighth or ninth boots email in about a week, and I almost didn't open it. The "UP TO 30% OFF* UGG" hero is big and pink-red, and I can read it on my phone without my glasses. But it's UGG, and I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants a cushioned slip-on. A fuzzy boot with a fleece cuff doesn't fit that.
- Below the hero it's five women's boots, "5 Boots to Fa La La Love," then a VIP points offer and a size grid. Every model is a woman and every boot is a women's style. Nothing here is for a man, and nothing mentions cushioning or arch support. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, and I wouldn't click unless I was shopping for my wife.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF*" is huge and easy to read on a phone without my glasses.
- The "Shop UGG" button is a clear black bar, so I know where to tap.
- The size grid (5 to 11) has big numbers, plus "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons. That's a nice touch for fit.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup and the Afterpay line are easy to find near the bottom.
- The "Online only. Details below." note is honest about the exclusions.

## What's weak

- Everything pictured is women's. There's no men's boot or slip-on anywhere, and the nav bar's "Women" comes first.
- Five numbered boots (Party, Chocolate, Cold-Weather, Riding, Cozy) is a long scroll. None of them have prices or ratings.
- The size grid is women's sizing, 5 to 11. My size isn't even on it.
- The Black Friday banner, the Nike banner and the UGG offer all compete at the top. I can't tell which deal is the real one.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny gray text I can't read on a phone.
- There's no mention of comfort, cushioning or arch support. The copy is all about parties and dancing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send men's customers a men's version. Put a slip-on or a comfort boot in the hero, with a "Men's" button next to "Shop UGG."
- 2. Pick one offer for the top of the email. Right now the Nike banner, the Black Friday banner and the UGG 30% all show up together.
- 3. Cut the five-boot story to two or three items, and show a price on each.
- 4. Add comfort language like "cushioned," "arch support" and "all-day walking," and show some customer ratings.
- 5. Space out the sends. Eight boots emails in a week trains me to skim.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's and women's UGG styles, plus wide widths in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly sound, but the QA run recorded one failed link (the SMS opt-in CTA returned HTTP 400) and two missing-alt warnings. Authentication, UTM alignment and several compliance items could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] SMS opt-in CTA returns 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off", and the QA report shows an empty label before the URL. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/sk7EHalfwz0j.../AAQRxRA~/...`, a roughly 600-character Braze/SendGrid-style tracked redirect.
  - The 400 could be a real defect, such as an expired or malformed tracking token.
  - It could also be a probe artifact, since some SMS-opt-in endpoints reject non-browser requests.
  - The data doesn't separate these, so a manual click-through from a real client is needed.
- **Probe coverage was limited.** Only 12 of 60 links were probed and 48 were skipped (footer and social links were deprioritized), so the rest are unverified.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 19,916 of 24,270 characters (82%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking URLs probably cause this. A text part that is mostly links is a spam-filter signal.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images.**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/RI9wGBdi9J8okgkmAr0ldQ~~/AAQRxRA~/uMYZxYKp...` has no alt attribute.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?...` has no alt attribute. This is a tracking pixel, so add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to keep it out of the accessibility tree.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom and conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Two meta tags are malformed.**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` is missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` is missing `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Without `http-equiv`, neither tag does anything, so charset declaration falls back to the headers.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` doesn't describe the email, which matters for the web-view version.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `mi_u=225090073` parameter in the pixel URL is a resolved recipient ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication could not be verified.** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `dsw@e.dsw.com` is unknown. This is a gap in the test environment, not confirmed non-compliance.
- **Unsubscribe link:** QA says one is always probed, and no unsubscribe failure was reported.
- **Not verifiable from the data:**
  - the physical postal address, because the HTML is truncated before the footer;
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, because no headers were supplied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is wrapped in an `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters are hidden. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261116_P261711_V1_W-_Offer_New_U` in the pixel URL, which is truncated. Landing-page alignment with the UGG offer can't be confirmed without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Click the SMS opt-in CTA from a real mail client. If it still returns 400, regenerate the link in the ESP.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and a descriptive alt to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/...` image.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the two meta tags with `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
5. Shorten the plain-text part by using a shorter tracking domain or fewer tracked links in the text version.
6. Re-run QA with full headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and the untruncated HTML. Resolve the redirect chains to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
