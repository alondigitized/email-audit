**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**

- Alright, this one's a nice change of pace — no pink floral D'Lites nonsense, no fake cart-abandon guilt trip. It opens with "Hi, Walker," my points balance, and a straight 20% off for Plus members before I even scroll. That's the honest kind of email — a real number, a real discount, no games.
- Then it dives into a "September Trend Report" with three shoe styles, and this is where it loses me a little — a burgundy sneaker, a suede chukka boot, and a loafer, styled with "Rich Shades," "Elevated Materials," "New Silhouettes." Fine-looking shoes, actually, especially that chukka boot, but nothing here calls out comfort, cushioning, or arch support — the stuff that actually gets me to click. - I'd open this one — the subject and the 20% off banner earn that. Would I click? Maybe on that suede boot, just to see the price and whether it's got their memory foam sole. Wouldn't forward it to a friend, but I wouldn't roll my eyes at it either, which for Skechers this week is a win.

## What's working

- "Hi, Walker" and my 1000-point balance right up top — feels like they know who I am.
- The 20% off Plus member banner is front and center, no digging for it.
- No pink floral D'Lites in sight — first email this week that didn't recycle that same tired shoe.
- The suede chukka boot actually looks like something I'd wear, decent styling shot.

## What's weak

- Not one word about comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — that's the whole reason I buy Skechers, and it's missing.
- "Rich Shades," "Elevated Materials," "New Silhouettes" is trend-copy language, feels aimed at somebody twenty years younger than me.

- 1. Put a comfort callout on at least one hero product — "memory foam insole," "arch support built in" — somewhere in that trend report. That's the hook that gets me past the shoe photos.

- 3. Swap in a customer photo or two closer to my age group, or at least someone who looks like they're walking, not posing on a porch.
- 4. Lead with the 40% off banner or fold it into the top block — burying a bigger discount two-thirds down the email is backwards.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via standard SFMC-style responsive markup with multiple tracking pixels; one confirmed personalization bug (empty greeting) and several non-HTTPS image assets are the primary technical issues.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 37 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect domains not validated for liveness) — no confirmed breakage, but unverified.
- Multiple tracking mechanisms fire on load: SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?...`), two Kroxid (`beacon.krxd.net`) user-match pixels, one Kroxid ad-impression pixel, a Return Path pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net`), and an ink1000 pixel (`www.ink1000.com/p/up/...`) — six total tracking calls before body content loads.
- The ink1000 pixel is served over `http://` (see Rendering section) — this can silently fail to log opens under mixed-content/security policies in some mail clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on two images: `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) and `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` (a content image, not just a tracking pixel — this one affects screen-reader users).
- **Non-HTTPS (`http://`) image sources** — 9 total, which may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies:
  - `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-...png` (Skechers logo)
  - `image.emails.skechers.com/.../49468f73-...png`
  - App Store / Google Play badges
  - Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store icons
  - Instagram, Facebook, YouTube social icons
  - `www.ink1000.com` tracking pixel
- CSS includes standard Outlook/Gmail/Apple Mail resets (`.ExternalClass`, `.mobile-pi`, `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, `x-apple-data-detectors`) — no issues found in the reset block itself.
- Media queries cover 375px, 480px, 640px, and 680px breakpoints plus a 768px desktop/mobile content-swap — no gaps observed in the provided source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot**: the greeting renders as "Hi ," — the first-name merge field resolved to empty/null and no fallback (e.g., "Hi there,") was applied. This is a confirmed broken personalization token and the most severe finding in this audit.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Sender: `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` — consistent with a dedicated ESP subdomain (SFMC), which typically carries its own SPF/DKIM; however, no authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC results) were provided in this audit payload, so pass/fail cannot be confirmed — **not evaluated, insufficient data**.
- Truncated HTML does not include the footer/unsubscribe block, so unsubscribe link presence/compliance and physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM requirement) **cannot be confirmed from the provided source** — flag for follow-up if the full source is available.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identifier `MKG_US_NONPUR_U_TREND_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_09062026` is consistently applied across the Kroxid ad-impression pixel and the ink1000 pixel — internally consistent campaign tagging.
- No destination/CTA link URLs were included in the truncated HTML (all click-throughs route through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect infrastructure, whose targets weren't probed), so **UTM parameter presence and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed** from the provided source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting merge-token fallback so it never renders "Hi ," — add a default value (e.g., "Hi there,") when the first-name field is null/empty.
2. Migrate all 9 `http://` image assets (logo, app badges, payment/store icons, social icons, tracking pixel) to `https://` to prevent blocking/flagging by mail clients.
3. Add `alt` text to `49468f73-...png` (content image) for accessibility; decorative tracking pixels (`o.gif`) can use `alt=""` explicitly rather than omitting the attribute.
4. If not already covered elsewhere, obtain and audit the email footer (unsubscribe link + physical address) and raw authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) separately, since they weren't present in the truncated source.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 37 skipped tracking/redirect links manually to confirm they resolve correctly, since automated probing skipped them.
