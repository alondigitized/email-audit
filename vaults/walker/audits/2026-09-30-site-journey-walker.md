# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I couldn't get a pair of shoes into my cart today, and that's the headline. On the product page I never picked a size, and the screen showed "Please select a size." The cart icon still said 0, and the cart page came up as a blank white screen. Up to that point the site was easy to get around, and search for "comfort shoes" gave me plenty of results. But I wouldn't know whether the cart works or whether I'd be charged correctly, and I wouldn't buy on a site like that. I'd go look at Hoka or Brooks instead.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10.** That's the same as yesterday, so there's no improvement. Browsing and search are solid, but the path to a purchase is broken from where I stand. A blank cart page is the kind of problem that costs real sales.

## 3. What's Working

- **The menu is clear.** The MEN drawer has big, well-spaced rows (Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections, Collaborations, Clothing & Accessories), plus a Back button and a large X. I could tap these with my thumb.
- **The shortcuts speak to what I want.** The chips under the header (Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit, Max Cushioning) are exactly my shopping list. The Men's Shoes page also has photo tiles for "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes."
- **The product page is clean.** It shows a big "Skechers Slip-ins: Aero Burst" title, a 4.5-star rating with 172 reviews, and a large price of $165.00. The Hands Free Slip-ins badge is easy to spot.
- **The size buttons are easy to tap.** They're roomy and sit in a clear grid (7.0 to 13.0). The blue Add to Cart button is wide and easy to hit.
- **Search returned results.** "comfort shoes" found 1,572 items with prices, colour swatches and sale info. "Slip-ins: Contour Foam – Cozy Fit Luxe Comfort" and "Relaxed Fit: Glide-Step Comfort" are sensible matches.
- **Prices and discounts are visible.** Shoe cards show price ranges such as "$59.99 - $88.00" with "15% OFF or Log In for 20% OFF!" in red.

## 4. What's Weak

- **The cart page was blank.** Step 8 was nothing but white. That could be a failure or a slow load, but either way I can't tell what's in my cart or what I'd pay.
- **Add to Cart didn't go through.** The page said "Please select a size," and the cart badge stayed at 0. The error also appeared before I had touched anything, which is confusing. Nothing tells me a size is needed until I've already tried.
- **I never saw anything that looked logged in.** The Log In step just shows the homepage, and nothing in the header changed. The search page still says "Log In for 20% OFF," so I wasn't signed in. Nothing felt personalised to me.
- **The "Dismiss Popups" step looks identical to the homepage.** There was nothing to dismiss, which is fine. But these three screens (homepage, popups, login) are pixel-for-pixel the same, so I can't tell the steps did anything.
- **The homepage doesn't speak to me.** The first screen is a neon-green "Outrun the Night" running shoe ad. I'm a comfortable walker, not a runner, and I have to scroll past it.
- **The shortcut row is cut off.** "Max Cus…" runs off the edge, and it isn't obvious the row scrolls sideways.
- **The promos contradict each other.** The top banner says "Members Save 20% OFF," but my $165 shoe says "Excluded from promotions." I trust a brand less when the banner and the page disagree.
- **The text is small.** The grey promo line and the small "details" links would need zooming for me, and the "Size Chart" and "Don't See Your Size?" links are small too.
- **The first search results were women's shoes.** Both were labeled "Women's" even though I'd been browsing Men's.
- **There's nothing to compare.** No comparison with other comfort brands, no price-versus-quality breakdown, and no arch support or cushioning explanation on the pages I saw.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page first.** It should never be blank. Show the items, or a clear error with a retry button.
2. **Fix the size prompt.** Don't show the red error until someone taps Add to Cart without choosing a size. After a size is chosen, show "Added to cart" clearly and update the badge.
3. **Make the logged-in state obvious.** Show a greeting or name in the header, and use it to put men's slip-ins and wide-fit options first.
4. **Lead with comfort, not running.** Put a Hands Free / Arch Fit / Max Cushioning hero on the homepage for people like me.
5. **Reconcile the promo messages.** If a shoe is excluded, say so in the top banner or explain why.
6. **Make text larger.** Use bigger text for promo strips and links, and add an arrow or fade to show the chip row scrolls.
7. **Respect the gender I browsed.** Search results should lean toward Men's when I've been in Men's.
8. **Add a "why this is comfortable" section.** Show the cushioning and arch support in plain words, with an honest comparison to Hoka, Brooks and New Balance.

## 6. Bottom Line

I like the shoes and the shortcuts, and the menu is easy on the thumb. But I couldn't finish a purchase, and a blank cart is a dealbreaker. The score stays at 3/10 until I can put a pair of shoes in the cart and see it there.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

The journey completed, but automated traffic was rate-limited by bot protection. Five of the six network errors are 429s on the Kasada endpoint (`/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.797`), and the sixth is a 429 on `/cart/`. The View Cart axe results look like they come from that 429 response page rather than the real cart.

The Homepage has 9 axe violations, including 2 critical ones. Search has 4, and View Cart has 4 (probably an artifact of the 429). LCP and CLS were not captured for any step, so Core Web Vitals can't be assessed.

Sampling limits:
- The HTML samples are post-JS DOM snapshots, not raw server HTML.
- The Log In and Men category snapshots both report the URL `https://www.skechers.com/`, so those steps were probably client-side transitions that didn't change the URL.
- Only 10 of the 24 console errors were provided.

## Accessibility

**Homepage (9 violations):**
- **[critical] `button-name`:** one or more buttons have no accessible name.
- **[critical] `aria-required-parent`:** an element has an ARIA role that needs a specific parent role, and that parent is missing.
- **[serious] `link-name`:** one or more links have no discernible text.
- **Landmark structure:** `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region` all fired. Together these point to nested or duplicated `<main>` landmarks, `<aside>` elements inside other landmarks, and content outside any landmark. This is likely caused by third-party widgets or a template that injects a second `<main>`.
- **[minor] `aria-allowed-role`:** a role is set on an element that doesn't allow it.

**Search (4 violations):**
- `aria-allowed-role`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region`. These repeat the Homepage template issues, so they are likely one shared-layout defect.

**View Cart (4 violations):**
- **[serious] `document-title` and `html-has-lang`:** the page has no `<title>` and no `lang` attribute.
- **[moderate] `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one`:** the page has no main landmark and no `<h1>`.
- These are consistent with a bare 429 or interstitial response, since the Homepage has `lang="en"`. Re-test with a session that isn't rate-limited before treating them as real cart defects.

## SEO

- The Homepage has `<html lang="en">`, so language declaration is fine there.
- View Cart has no `<title>` and no `<h1>` (see above). The cause is probably the error response. Cart pages are normally `noindex`, so the impact is low.
- The snapshots don't include the `<head>` metadata (canonical, meta description, structured data, viewport). It could not be assessed.
- The Log In and Men category steps reported the Homepage URL, so their URL structure and titles could not be verified.

## Performance

- **TTFB:** all steps are under 800 ms.
  - Homepage 403 ms, Log In 412 ms, Men category 412 ms, View Cart 300 ms.
  - Search is the slowest at 511 ms.
  - Men > Shoes (26 ms), Product detail (29 ms) and Add to Cart (29 ms) look like cache hits, prefetched data or client-side navigation rather than full document loads.
- **LCP and CLS:** not captured for any step (`?`). This is a measurement gap. Check that the PerformanceObserver is registered before navigation and that its entries are being read.
- **Third-party load:** the Homepage head injects a large number of async third-party scripts:
  - Ad and tracking pixels: TikTok, Pinterest, Yahoo, Facebook, Snapchat, Reddit, Taboola, Nextdoor, Bing, Impact, RTB House and Google Ads/gtag.
  - Analytics and testing: Quantum Metric, Monetate, Bloomreach, Pulse Insights, go2sdk and GTM.
  - The number of third-party requests likely hurts main-thread time and LCP.
- **GTM preview parameters:** the GTM tag is loaded with `gtm_preview=env-1&gtm_auth=…`, which is a non-default GTM environment on a production page. Confirm this is intentional. It can serve unpublished container versions.
- **Font loading:** fonts load through `webfontloader.js` from cdnjs, which means a third-party dependency and a JS-driven font load (`wf-*-active` classes). This risks FOIT/FOUT and layout shift, so verify it against CLS once that metric is captured.

## Mobile Optimization

- `--vh` is set as an inline style on `<html>` (`6.64px`), so viewport height is computed in JS. That is a common workaround for mobile browser chrome and is fine.
- The viewport meta tag and touch-target sizes are not in the samples, so they could not be verified.
- The `button-name` and `link-name` failures affect screen-reader users on mobile.

## Console & Network Errors

- **429 on the Kasada fingerprint endpoint (5 requests):** `/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.797`. The bot-mitigation script is being rate-limited or challenged, and it repeats across steps.
- **429 on `/cart/`:** the cart document was throttled, which affects the View Cart audit's validity.
- **Permissions policy violation (`unload` not allowed):** 5 of the 10 visible errors. Some script registers an `unload` handler, which is deprecated, blocked by policy in this document and harmful to bfcache eligibility. Identify the originating script, likely a third-party tag.
- **Summarizer API unsupported languages (2 of the 10 visible errors):** probably a browser-side or extension-side call that specified unsupported languages. Trace the initiator, since it may be a third-party script.
- Only 10 of the 24 console errors were provided, so the counts above are partial.

## Recommendations

1. **Re-run the audit with a non-throttled session.** Use a lower request rate or an allow-listed test client for Kasada. Then re-check View Cart, because its 4 violations may not be real.
2. **Fix the Homepage critical issues.** Add accessible names to icon-only buttons and links, and correct the ARIA parent/child roles.
3. **Fix the landmark structure in the shared template.** Keep a single top-level `<main>`, keep `<aside>` at the top level, give repeated landmarks unique labels, and move stray content into landmarks. This one change should clear most of the Homepage and Search violations.
4. **Capture LCP and CLS.** Make sure the observers attach before navigation and are read at the end of each step.
5. **Audit the third-party tags.** Defer or consolidate the pixels, confirm the GTM `env-1` preview parameters are intentional, and consider self-hosting fonts instead of using the webfontloader CDN.
6. **Remove or replace the `unload` listeners** with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`, so bfcache works and the policy errors go away.
7. **Get the full console log** (all 24 errors) and confirm the source of the Summarizer API errors.
