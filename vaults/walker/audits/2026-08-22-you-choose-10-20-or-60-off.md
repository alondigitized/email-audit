# You choose: $10, $20, or $60 OFF
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Same $10/$20/$60 tiered deal DSW's been running all week now — at least the coupon math stays simple, I'll give them that consistency. But once again it's straight into "Sandal Season" with a woman in a red dress dangling strappy sandals, then a grid of four women's sandals, then a heeled sandal with a purple sleeve and "Pedi. Set. Go!" — there's a "Shop All Men" nowhere to be found until the size chart way down at the bottom, and even that's mixed in with women's sizing. Nothing here says "men's comfort shoe" to me at any point above the fold or below it.
- This is the same email I've now seen four times this week with a different hero graphic pasted on top. I wouldn't click, I wouldn't forward it, and honestly I'm getting close to just not opening the next one — the subject line's the only thing still doing any work here.

## What's working

- The $10/$20/$60 off tiers are clear and I don't need a calculator — that part's consistent and I appreciate it.
- There's a "Shop Men" link in the top nav bar, so at least it's one tap away if I bother to look for it.
- The "Shop By Size" grid near the bottom is genuinely useful layout — I like seeing sizes as tap targets.
- Wide Width and Wide Calf call-outs are a nice touch for guys like me who need extra room.

## What's weak

- Every visual — hero, product grid, secondary banner — is women's sandals. Not one men's shoe shown anywhere in the body.
- "Sandal Season," "Pedi. Set. Go!" — none of that speaks to a guy looking for a slip-on walking shoe.
- The "Shoeprise Savings Hunt" in-store scavenger hunt banner is confusing clutter — red arrows, calendars, "up to $50 off" that's vague and unrelated to the main offer.
- This is the fourth near-identical DSW email this week with the same tiered discount — the cadence is wearing thin.
- No comfort, cushioning, or arch support language anywhere — nothing that tells me this brand knows what I actually shop for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men on-brand-file into a men's-led version of this template — swap the hero model and product grid for men's comfort/casual shoes. This is the single biggest miss, and it's been the same miss all week.
- 2. Cut the "Shoeprise Savings Hunt" banner or move it to a P.S. line — it competes with the main offer instead of supporting it.
- 3. Slow the send cadence — four emails in five days with the same discount tiers trains me to stop opening.
- 4. Add a men's comfort/cushioning callout row (arch support, slip-on, walking shoe) so I know DSW carries what I actually buy.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort — up to $60 off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-on comfort styles starting at $39.99`
