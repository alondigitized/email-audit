# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Well, first thing I see when I open this page is a mom and her little girl — pink flowers everywhere, script font, "The Mother's Day Shop." That's a nice campaign and all, but I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for a pair of Slip-ins for my feet, not a Mother's Day gift. Skechers clearly knows how to run a sale — BOGO 50% off is hard to miss — but nothing on this screen is talking to me specifically.
- **7/10**
- + returning-shopper hook: "Recently Viewed" text is just barely visible at the bottom edge of the screen
- + concrete offer: BOGO 50% OFF in the blue top banner, plus 25% OFF Apparel in the secondary strip — two distinct offers above the fold
- + primary CTA: "SHOP NOW" button is large, black, unmissable
- + visual hierarchy: banner → secondary strip → hero image → CTA — eye flows naturally
- + no render bugs: clean layout, no broken images or overlapping text
- + current campaign/season: Mother's Day is right on time for May
- + offer feels honest: "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart" is straightforward, no asterisk games visible
- − hero doesn't speak to my persona: "The Mother's Day Shop" with a mother/daughter image targets gift-buyers, not a 62-year-old male comfort shopper
- − demographic mismatch: pink flowers, feminine styling, no male presence at all above the fold
- − loyalty/membership not visible: I'm a Skechers Plus member — my rewards balance or member pricing should be greeting me, not hiding

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — blue, high contrast, instant attention. I see it before I see anything else and it makes me want to poke around.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — full-width, bold, no confusion about what to tap. I appreciate that — I don't need fancy.
- **25% OFF Apparel strip** — stacked right under the nav, reinforces that there are deals to be had.
- **Clean, uncluttered mobile layout** — one hero, one button. The page isn't yelling at me with ten things at once, which I like on my phone.
- **Current seasonal relevance** — Mother's Day is this week, so the timing is spot on. I might be shopping for my wife.

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% feminine** — mother and daughter, pink flowers, pink shoes, pink script font. As a guy looking for Men's Slip-ins, I feel like I landed on the wrong page.
- **No Men's entry point above the fold** — I have to tap the hamburger menu to even find "Men's." That's an extra step I shouldn't need on a brand I'm loyal to.
- **My Skechers Plus membership is invisible** — I'm a member and I see zero acknowledgment: no points balance, no member pricing badge, nothing. That's leaving loyalty on the table.
- **"SHOP NOW" goes to Mother's Day** — that CTA sends me into a gifting section, not to my shoes. I'd bounce right back out.
- **No Slip-ins or comfort-tech callout** — the product line that brought me to Skechers in the first place isn't mentioned anywhere I can see.

## Recommendations

- **Add a gender-split below the hero** — a simple "Shop Men's | Shop Women's" two-tile row right under the SHOP NOW button would let me route myself in one tap instead of hunting through the hamburger menu.
- **Surface the Skechers Plus balance or a member-only offer in the top banner** — swap one line in that blue bar to "Plus Members: Extra 10% on top" so I feel recognized the moment I land.
- **Swap or supplement the hero for returning male shoppers** — if Skechers knows I browse Men's, show me a men's comfort lifestyle image. Even a rotating hero would do it.
- **Add "Slip-ins" as a visible quick-link** — a scrollable chip row ("Slip-ins | Walk · Run | Wide Width | New Arrivals") right below the hero gives me a one-tap path to what I actually came for.
