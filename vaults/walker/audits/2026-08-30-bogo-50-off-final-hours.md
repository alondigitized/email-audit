# BOGO 50% OFF: FINAL HOURS!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Same BOGO 50% off deal, ninth day I've seen it now — the 24th through the 29th, and now the 30th, dressed up this time as "FINAL HOURS!" Whether that's actually true or just another version of the "last day" line from yesterday, I've got no way of knowing, and after a week and a half of the same headline I'm not inclined to trust it. The "shop by size" grid down near the bottom is the one thing that actually works for me — I wear a 10, wide, and having size buttons right there beats hunting through a page — but it's buried under a wall of women's heels, mules, and leopard print that has nothing to do with what I'm shopping for.
- The product grid up top is six pairs and every single one is women's — clogs, mary janes, strappy heels, leopard pumps. Not one men's shoe until you scroll well past the fold. I'd close this one without clicking, and I definitely wouldn't forward it — nothing here is new information after nine straight days of the identical offer.

## What's working

- "Shop by size" with actual number buttons (5 through 11, wide width, wide calf) is genuinely useful once you get to it — that's the kind of specific detail I want.
- The BOGO 50% headline is at least big and legible, no squinting required.
- "Free Imbox spray with any in-store purchase" is a new wrinkle I hadn't seen in the last several sends — mildly interesting even if I don't know what it is.

## What's weak

- Ninth day of the identical BOGO 50% off deal — "FINAL HOURS!" has zero credibility left at this point.
- Every featured product is a women's shoe — heels, mules, leopard pumps — nothing here says "you, a 62-year-old guy who wants a cushioned slip-on."
- "MEN" is just a tiny nav link at the very top; there's no actual men's product shown anywhere near the top of the email.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — the thing I actually care about — just a generic shoe deal.
- DSW VIP rewards banner is way down at the bottom after a wall of women's shoes and shipping icons — I'd never scroll that far.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the product grid by browsing/purchase history — if I've ever looked at men's shoes on their site, don't lead with six women's styles. Put a men's comfort shoe up top.
- 2. Retire "FINAL HOURS" and "last day" language until it's actually true two or three sends from now — I've been burned by fake urgency from this brand all week.
- 3. Move the "shop by size" module up near the top — it's the most useful thing in the email and it's currently below the fold.
- 4. Add one line of comfort-specific copy — "cushioned, arch support, slip-on and go" — somewhere near the hero for shoppers like me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles: BOGO 50% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort — BOGO 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned slip-ons and walking shoes, half off the second pair`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop by size — men's 8 to 14 now BOGO 50%`
