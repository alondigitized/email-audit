# Gear up for school…WITH BOGO 50% OFF!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off, that's a real number I can do math on in my head — no gimmicks, no "for members only" fine print I gotta dig for. That got my attention right off. Trouble is, the second I scroll past the headline, it's a wall of wedge booties, platform mules, leopard ankle boots, and pointy-toe pumps. Every single shoe in "So New, So You" is a women's shoe. Not one.
- The size grid is a nice touch — 5 through 11, wide width, wide calf — but that's a women's size run, not mine. I'm not the guy this was built for, and I know it inside of two seconds of looking at it. This isn't the clearance grab-bag they sent me the last few days, at least — it's a clean, single offer — but it's aimed at somebody else's closet. I'm not clicking, and I'm sure not forwarding this to my buddies. Straight to archive.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is a real, specific offer I can understand without a calculator — better than the usual "$10 off $50" runaround these DSW emails pull.
- Layout's clean this time — one offer, one hero, not the clearance shoe-dump I've been getting all week.
- "Free Shipping for VIPs," "In-Store Pickup," "Buy Now Pay Later" — practical stuff, I noticed those icons even if I skimmed past.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, nothing overlapping, text is legible.

## What's weak

- Every shoe pictured is a women's style — wedges, mules, kitten heels, leopard booties. Nothing for me, and no "Shop Men" callout anywhere near the hero.
- "Gear up for school" and "BOGO" as a combo tells me this is aimed at moms and college kids, not a 62-year-old guy who just wants a cushioned walking shoe.
- Preview text — "Ladies and gentlemen, start your semester" — I don't have a semester to start. That line alone told me to skip it.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to open my wallet.
- Top banner mentions "Nike styles up to 30% off" but I never see a men's Nike shoe anywhere in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this by past purchase or browse category — if DSW knows I've looked at men's walking shoes, put men's product in the hero grid, not an all-women's lineup.
- 2. Swap "Gear up for school" for something that doesn't assume I've got a kid or I'm the kid. A comfort-brand BOGO angle would pull me in a lot faster.
- 3. Add a men's product row or at minimum a "Shop Men" tile next to the women's grid — right now the visual hierarchy tells me to move on.
- 4. Work "cushioned," "arch support," or a comfort-brand name (Skechers, Hoka, Brooks) into the subject or hero copy if you want my kind of shopper to stop scrolling.
