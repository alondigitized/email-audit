# Stock up! BOGO 50% off shoes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. After a week of CIDER $15-off countdowns, this one is calmer, and I'm glad of it. The big green banner says "BOGO 50% OFF*" and I understood it in about two seconds. Buy one pair, get 50% off a second pair. That's real math I can do, and I like math.
- The products let me down. Six shoes: fuzzy lace-up boots, a wedge sneaker, a flat with a buckle strap, a leopard-print ankle bootie, a clog, a shearling boot. Every one looks like it was picked for a woman, and not one is a slip-on walking shoe or a cushioned men's casual. There's a "SHOP MEN" link under the banner, but the email never shows me anything for men. The "Shoebox Shuffle" game, with a woman laughing in a denim jacket, is not for me. It reads like a coupon-clipping game and I wouldn't play it.
- I'd open it, scroll, and tap "SHOP MEN" once. I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted two pairs of walking shoes I'd cross-shop Skechers and Hoka first, so I'd only click if the men's page showed Skechers or Clarks at a good price.

## What's working

- The "BOGO 50% OFF" headline is huge and clear, and the white "SHOP NOW" button is easy to tap.
- The banner shows the dates. The fine print says Oct 15-18 and "no code needed," which is a relief after the CIDER code emails.
- "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN / SHOP KIDS" sit right under the banner, so I can go straight to my section.
- The "Shop by Size" block with wide width is a good idea. Wide feet are a real thing at my age.
- The fine print names Skechers, Clarks and Crown Vintage as qualifying brands. Skechers is one I'd shop.

## What's weak

- All six products are women's styles: furry boots, a wedge sneaker, a Mary Jane flat. Nothing for me.
- There's no men's comfort product, no slip-on, no mention of cushioning or arch support.
- The "SHOP BY SIZE" numbers go 5 to 11. A men's size 11 is a medium and I'd need 12 or 13, so it doesn't fit me.
- The "BOGO 1st pair only!" labels in small red type confuse me. Is it 50% off or isn't it?
- The Shoebox Shuffle section is a lot of purple and a game I don't want. It pushes the offer down the page.
- The legal text at the bottom is tiny and long, and the asterisk on "50% OFF*" makes me suspicious.
- This is yet another DSW email in a week full of them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put men's products in the grid. Show a men's slip-on, a walking shoe, a casual loafer, with the brand name and the price after the discount.
- 2. Make the BOGO math concrete. Show two pairs and the savings, for example "Two $80 pairs = $120."
- 3. Put "SHOP MEN" inside the banner as a button, not as a small link underneath.
- 4. Cut the game, or move it below the sizes. Fix the size list so it includes 12 and 13.
- 5. Use one consistent label on the products and say plainly which pair gets the discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1 pair, get 50% off the 2nd — through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Two pairs of comfy walking shoes, one low price`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers, Clarks & more. No code needed, ends Oct 18.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's slip-ons, sneakers and loafers: second pair half off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The only hard failure is the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA, which returned HTTP 400 on its `ablink.dsw.com` tracked link. The other findings are lower severity: a bloated plain-text part, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, and some missing alt text. Authentication and unsubscribe compliance couldn't be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400)** on the CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off":
  `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/Ee7eWTi8TmHkqimE9UNv6g~~/AAQRxRA~/vrYOSOmV…`
  - The status message is blank after "(400):", so the failure reason isn't captured.
  - A 400 on a tracked redirect can mean a malformed or expired token. It can also mean the redirect host rejects automated probes. Confirm with a manual click before treating it as a real outage.
  - If it is real, the SMS-acquisition CTA is dead.
- **Probe coverage is limited.** Only 12 of 70 links were probed and 58 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The other links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is bloated.** 25,278 of 30,122 characters (84%) are URLs, because the long `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/…` tracking tokens are inlined. This is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261015_P261418_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…` is a second tracker on a different domain from the `ablink.dsw.com` click tracking. It works as designed, but it adds a second tracking domain to the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Two images lack alt text:**
  - The `ablink.dsw.com/q/Yfl1gheQ…/sioCWCFRCvZqTKIf8opykXhoRw0…` image. The `/q/` path suggests an open pixel, but this isn't confirmed. If it is a content image, it needs descriptive alt text.
  - `o.gif` (the tracking pixel) should carry `alt=""`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Meta tags missing `http-equiv`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />`
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`
  - As written, neither tag does anything. The charset then relies on the MIME `Content-Type` header, which I couldn't see.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Screen readers fall back to a default language.
- The HTML was truncated, so table roles, image `width`/`height` and the rest of the dark-mode CSS were not checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]` or Liquid tags. The HTML was truncated, so this applies only to the portion provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA notes this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so it's a harness limitation rather than a confirmed sender defect. Verify against the original headers or a seed-list test.
- The unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source. The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for it. Treat these as unverified, not as failures.
- The From domain (`e.dsw.com`) and click-tracking domain (`ablink.dsw.com`) are both subdomains of dsw.com, so they look aligned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every link is wrapped in an opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, and the QA data doesn't resolve the final destinations. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "BOGO 50% off shoes" can't be confirmed.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261015_P261418_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…` in the pixel URL, and it is truncated.
- The 400 on the SMS opt-in link breaks continuity for that CTA entirely.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click-test the "Opt-In to Texts" link from a real client. If it fails, regenerate the tracked link or fix the ESP template.
2. Resolve the full redirect chains for the main CTAs to confirm the destination URLs and UTM parameters, and that the BOGO landing page matches the subject line.
3. Change the viewport to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1` (drop `maximum-scale=1`).
4. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`, and meaningful alt text to the `/q/` image if it is visible content.
6. Shorten or restructure the plain-text part, for example by using shorter link tokens or fewer links, so URLs are well under 70% of it.
7. Re-run with full headers and the full HTML (not truncated) to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, the postal address and merge tokens. Also widen link probing beyond 12 links.
