# Shoe of the month giveaway
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least it's not soccer cleats with dragon faces or a K-pop star this time — that's about as far as my patience stretches for adidas lately. This one's a sweepstakes for a shoe called "Hyperboost Euphoria," which looks like a basketball performance shoe with a mesh upper in orange and black — not something I'd ever lace up for a walk to the mailbox, let alone all day on my feet at the mall.
- There's no CTA to actually enter the giveaway that I can see — no button, no link, just "don't miss your chance to make them yours." Make them mine how? I'd have to go hunting. And even if I did enter and somehow won, I wouldn't want the shoe.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on. Not forwarding it, not clicking it. Following up "Y-3 dragon cleats" and "thanks Leo" with a sweepstakes for a shoe I'd never wear is not turning this around for me.

## What's working

- The layout is clean and uncluttered — no visual bugs, nothing overlapping, easy enough to read on my phone.
- "SEPTEMBER SHOE OF THE MONTH GIVEAWAY" is at least a clear, specific headline — no guessing what this email is about.
- It's a giveaway, not another straight sales pitch, so it's a small change of pace from the last several sends.

## What's weak

- The shoe itself — Hyperboost Euphoria — is clearly a basketball or performance sneaker, nothing to do with comfort, cushioning, or casual walking shoes I'd actually wear.
- I don't see an actual "Enter Now" button anywhere — just a generic "Shop Now" banner up top pointing to best sellers, which isn't even related to the giveaway.
- "adiClub members" in the preview line — I don't know if I'm a member or not, and nothing in the email tells me my status or points.
- No mention of sizing, comfort, arch support, anything I'd actually weigh before caring about a shoe.
- Feels like a low-effort filler send between their skateboarding and soccer emails — not tailored to me at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual "Enter to Win" button front and center — right now there's no clear way to participate, which kills the whole point of a giveaway email.
- 2. Feature a giveaway prize that fits a broader audience — a comfort/lifestyle shoe would at least give guys like me a reason to care, instead of a basketball shoe.
- 3. Tell me if I'm already an adiClub member and what my status/points are — that's the one thing this loyalty program email should lead with.
- 4. Add real deadline info — "enter by [date]" — there's zero urgency or entry window here.
