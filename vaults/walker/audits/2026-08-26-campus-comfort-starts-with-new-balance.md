# Campus Comfort starts with New Balance
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Subject line said New Balance, and I do wear New Balance, so I gave it a shot. Big mistake — this is another one of those "family athletics" catalog dumps with a young woman in a ponytail and ball cap sitting courtside in white sneakers. That's not campus comfort for me, that's back-to-school tennis team.
- There's also a busted black box right up top that just says "IN STORE ONLY" over what looks like a broken or blank image — nothing loaded there, just a smudgy dark rectangle. Made me think my email app choked before I even got to the good stuff.
- Nothing here is my size, my style, or my life stage. I'd scroll past, not click, and definitely not forward this to anybody. Straight to "seen it" pile.

## What's working

- The $20 off $99.98 / $10 off $74.98 coupon banner near the bottom is at least a real, usable number with a code (SAVEMORE).
- Shoe Carnival's name up top is recognizable — I know the store even if I don't love this particular blast.
- The barcodes at the bottom mean I could still use this in-store without hunting for a code, which is how I actually redeem stuff.

## What's weak

- Hero model is a young woman on a tennis court — nothing about that says "62-year-old guy who wants arch support," so I felt invisible from the first scroll.
- That black broken-image box at the very top with "IN STORE ONLY" looks like a rendering glitch, not intentional design — bad first impression.
- Nike, Jordan, Adidas, Converse "Family Sale" and "Athletics Sale for the Family" blocks are all kids/performance-athletic focused, nothing casual-comfort for me.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or slip-on ease anywhere — just percentages and team sports branding.
- "Campus Comfort" in the subject never shows up anywhere in the actual email body that I can see — feels like bait.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with an actual New Balance comfort shoe on a man my age — walking shoe, not tennis gear on a college kid. That's the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Fix or remove that broken black "IN STORE ONLY" box at the top — it reads as a glitch, not a banner.
- 3. Move the $20/$10 coupon block up near the top instead of burying it after three unrelated sale sections.
- 4. Cut or de-emphasize the Nike/Jordan/Adidas "Family Sale" blocks if the subject is going to promise New Balance — pick a lane.
- 5. Add a line about cushioning or arch support in the New Balance section — give me a reason tied to comfort, not just "All-Star Faves."
