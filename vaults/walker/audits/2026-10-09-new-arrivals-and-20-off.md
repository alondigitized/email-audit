# NEW ARRIVALS (*AND* 20% OFF)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and it's the same HAYRIDE 20% code I've been getting all week. The subject yelled at me, and the preview said "Sorry for shouting, we were excited." That's cute, and I'll give them a smile. But it's the same 20% as this morning's "20% off dressy fall styles" and yesterday's "FYI: we've got 20% off right now." The big white "20% OFF*" on green is easy to read on my phone, even without my readers.
- Then I scrolled and the "new arrivals" were all women's boots, heels and flats. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for a cushioned slip-on. "Shop Men" is a small underlined link buried under the banner. Nothing here says comfort, arch support or walking. The Adidas sneaker tile is the closest thing, and it's tiny.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, unless my wife wants boots.

## What's working

- The green "20% OFF*" banner is huge and readable on my phone, and the HAYRIDE code is right there.
- The "Sorry for shouting" preview is a friendly touch and made the brand sound human.
- The size grid near the bottom (5 to 11, wide width) is simple, and I like seeing "Shop Wide Width."
- "Free shipping for VIPs," the in-store pickup and the Afterpay icons are clear and easy to scan.
- The Adidas "up to 30% off" orange block is bold and easy to understand.

## What's weak

- Every "new arrival" is a women's style. Not one men's shoe is shown in the hero grid.
- "Shop Men" is a tiny underlined link under the banner. It's hard to tap and easy to miss.
- It's the same 20% HAYRIDE code as all week. The "new arrivals" angle feels like a repaint of the same promo.
- Product captions like "Extra 20% off reg. price!" are tiny. I'd have to squint or zoom.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text, and the asterisk on "20% OFF*" makes me wonder what's excluded.
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-on comfort anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Lead the men's list with men's comfort and walking styles, and put a big "Shop Men" button next to "Shop Women." Right now it reads as a women's email.
- 2. Show prices, not just "Extra 20% off." Say "Was $89, now $71" so I can do my price-vs-quality math.
- 3. Make the offer different from the last five sends. Adidas up to 30% off could lead, or the email could add a clear end date.
- 4. Add a comfort row with cushioned and slip-on picks, plus the Skechers, Hoka and Brooks names I cross-shop.
- 5. Make the product captions and tap targets bigger for phone readers.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's slip-ons and cushioned walkers just landed. Code HAYRIDE saves 20%.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Braze-sent, table-based XHTML template with dark-mode support. QA shows one broken CTA link (400) on the SMS opt-in, two images missing alt text, and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so I couldn't check anything past the `<style>` blocks.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Broken link (400):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned HTTP 400. Its URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/lqH8ZYFM88Vm6lfYO96wUQ~~/AAQRxRA~/dR2-lxoDsCvuMSIINsorL8jrf3lwVM1DCTSo79SOuxwrncgxIWjwERvRswdaOULqAlI2…`. This is a Braze/SendGrid-style wrapped click-tracking redirect, and the path is very long (about 700 characters).
  - A 400 on a tracked redirect usually means a malformed or truncated encoded payload, or a rejection of the probe's request, such as bot filtering or a missing user-agent or cookie. The QA data can't tell these apart.
  - The log shows the link text but an empty anchor label (`link text: ""` before the URL), so the link may be an image link with no text. That would explain the alt-text finding.
  - If the link is truly broken, subscribers can't take up the 15% SMS offer.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 76 links were probed and 64 were skipped, so the rest are unverified. The unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, and no failure was reported for it.
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261009_P251285_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_2` is a third-party open pixel with a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`). It is a normal tracking element, not a defect.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text, tracked image:** The image at `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/C8OdRJPApUvnJ-BKlVViWA~~/AAQRxRA~/SpxMeqZXC0_pWzizqCas1TUWpFV6D2iUY0ZrvMI6hUYhw0LYDh-34Nf61h02x` has no `alt`. This is probably the Braze open-tracking pixel, since it sits on the `ablink` `/q/` path. If so, it should carry `alt=""`, and it should be `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Missing alt text, `o.gif`:** The `mi.dsw.com` pixel has no `alt`. Add `alt=""`.
- **Unverified:** Neither image's role (decorative or content) is confirmed from the truncated source. If either is a content image, a missing `alt` is a real accessibility failure.
- **Head markup:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they have no effect. The charset is not actually declared via meta (Content-Type headers usually cover this).
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Positive:** The template declares `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes`, has `prefers-color-scheme` dark-mode rules, and uses an Outlook-aware `mso-` reset.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` uses `Src:` with a capital S. CSS property names are case-insensitive, so this is harmless.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA data. The source is truncated, so the body wasn't checked. `mi_u=225090073` is a resolved per-recipient ID.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unsubscribe:** The probe set reportedly always includes an unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported for it. I couldn't see the link or the physical postal address in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.
- **Authentication headers:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` were not provided, so I can't assess them. The `From` is `dsw@e.dsw.com`, a sending subdomain that matches the `ablink.dsw.com` tracking domain. That alignment is good practice.
- **Plain-text part:** 27,804 of 32,629 characters (85%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The long tracked URLs inflate the text part, and a URL-heavy text part can hurt spam scoring slightly. This is a deliverability warning, not a compliance failure.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Links go through `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so the final destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be seen in the HTML. I can't confirm UTM consistency or landing-page alignment.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261009_P251285_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_2` on the pixel. Its `WM-_Offer` segment has a stray hyphen/underscore pairing, which looks like an empty template variable. That's a minor naming-hygiene issue in the campaign ID.
- The subject promises "20% OFF", but the one failing CTA offers "15% Off" for SMS opt-in. These are separate offers (new arrivals vs. SMS signup), so I'm not flagging a mismatch.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix or re-generate the failing "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link (HTTP 400). Check whether the redirect payload is intact and whether it works with a real browser user-agent. Also confirm the final destination resolves.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels (`ablink.dsw.com/q/…` and `mi.dsw.com/…/o.gif`). Give the SMS-opt-in image a descriptive `alt` if it is an image link.
3. Give the SMS-opt-in link an accessible label (link text or image `alt`), since the QA log shows an empty anchor label.
4. Shrink the plain-text part, for example by using shorter link-tracking paths or fewer links, to bring URL density under 70%.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta to allow zoom, and add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to the charset meta.
6. Check the `mi_ecmp` campaign ID template for the empty variable (`WM-_Offer`).
7. Re-run QA with the full HTML and the raw headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`). Probe the 64 skipped links too, or at least a sample.
