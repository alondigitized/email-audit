# Fresh Members-only colors
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, HOKA's back to shoes, and that's a relief after last week's dirt bikes and the movie trailer. "Rare pairs" in big blue letters over a black trail shoe is easy to read. My eye also went straight to the yellow bar at the very top: "Buy 2+ items, get a free sock 3-pack." That's the one concrete deal in the email, and it's squeezed into a thin strip.
- Then it lost me. The first shoe is a women's Challenger 8 GTX, and most of the other shoes are trail or GORE-TEX styles. The one men's shoe, a plain white Solimar 2, is tiny and has no price. "Members-only" shows up over and over, but nothing says what a member gets. I'm a member, so it didn't treat me like a known customer. It never told me what I'd save or what's different about these colors.
- Would I click? Maybe "Shop Members-Only" if I had a minute. I wouldn't forward it. I'd read it and move on, much like the Oct 6 Arahi 9 and Oct 4 "Black goes with everything" sends.

## What's working

- "Rare pairs" is big and readable on my phone, and the shoe sits right under it.
- The sock offer is at the very top, so I saw it before anything else.
- The buttons are wide, rounded and easy to tap with my thumb.
- A men's Solimar 2 is in there. It's a light everyday shoe, closer to what I'd wear.
- "Free Expedited Shipping" and "FLY for 30 Guarantee" at the bottom are trust cues I noticed.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is a women's Challenger 8 GTX, and the second row is mostly women's too. I'm a 62-year-old man and I had to hunt for anything in my size.
- "Members-only" is never explained. I don't know if it means a better price, early access or just a different color.
- There are no prices, no percentages and nothing about cushioning or arch support. Nothing helps me compare it to a Brooks.
- The sock deal is in tiny type at the top, and the fine print is tiny too. The free shipping needs a text signup, and the footer says "Free expedited shipping on first full-price order." That's buried, and it feels like a catch.
- "Join for Free" appears twice, and I'm already a member.
- The trail and GORE-TEX shoes look aggressive. I walk the mall and the neighborhood, not mountains.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's walking or slip-on shoe, and say what the member perk is. For example: "Members get early access plus free socks."
- 2. Add a price or a savings figure under each shoe, plus one comfort line like "Max cushion" or "Easy on/off."
- 3. Hide "Join for Free" from people who already belong, and use a "Welcome back" line instead.
- 4. Move the sock offer up into the hero, and give it a deadline in plain, larger type.
- 5. Cut the trail and GORE-TEX section, or label it "Wet weather walking" so it fits my day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members get free socks with 2 pairs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New men's colors, members first`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soft, cushioned colors you won't find anywhere else. Free sock 3-pack with 2+ items.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member early look: Solimar 2 and more, in men's sizes.`

# Technical Audit: HOKA® "Fresh Members-only colors"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text part, and the QA probe got 403s on the returns link and on the unsubscribe link. It also has 19 images flagged for missing alt text and some invalid CSS. I could only see the truncated HTML, so some checks (headers, footer address, merge tokens) can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 403 (QA FAIL).** The link is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…:1/a7f20326?jwtH=…`. Its JWT payload decodes to a destination of `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_LOYALTY_BR_20261007_…`.
- **Returns link returned 403 (QA FAIL).** The link text is `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`. The wrapper is the same Cordial `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/…/28d74dc0` pattern, and it decodes to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…`.
- **The 403s are probably bot blocking.** Both failures come from the Cordial click-tracking redirector, not the destination pages. A scripted probe with no JWT-validated browser context is the likely cause. Neither is confirmed broken for real recipients, so verify with a manual click-through.
- **Link expiry is a possible risk.** The decoded `jwtP` payload contains `"ce":86400`. If that is a click-expiry in seconds (my inference, not confirmed), tracked links would stop working after about 24 hours. The unsubscribe link would be affected too.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 31 links were probed and 19 were skipped, so link health for the remaining links is unknown.
- **Two open-tracking pixels.** `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…` are both present, which suggests two tracking systems or a duplicate injection. This is not a defect on its own, but the second pixel is on a separate `mg.` host.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged 19 `<img>` elements, including every hero and product image on `images.usw2.cordial.com/1664/…` (e.g. `US_202607_MembershipNewColors_a.jpg`, `US_Challenger8GTX_TopLeft_a.jpg`, `LogoFooterPrimary.png`).
  - The two tracking pixels are included in that count. Empty alt is correct for those, so they are not real issues.
  - The truncated HTML can't show whether the other 17 have an empty `alt=""` or no attribute at all.
  - If the images carry the offer text, image-blocked and screen-reader users get no content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Ineffective charset meta.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags with conflicting values.** One has `maximum-scale=1`, and the other has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` can block pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Invalid CSS.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have units with no values and are dropped by parsers. These look like unfilled template variables.
- **Global reset removes focus outlines.** The `* {… outline:none; …}` reset removes keyboard focus indicators in clients that support them.
- **Web fonts.** `@font-face` loads `TheFutureHOKA` from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Gmail and Outlook ignore this. The font-family fallback stack is cut off in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible portion. The truncated source means I can't rule them out lower down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 characters. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and spam filters can score it negatively.
- **Unsubscribe is present** in the footer and points to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`, but the probe returned 403 (see §2).
- **Can't verify, no data provided:** `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and whether the footer has a physical postal address. The sender (`info@emails.hoka.com`) and tracking hosts (`e.`/`mg.emails.hoka.com`) share the `emails.hoka.com` subdomain, which is consistent with aligned authentication.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present and well-formed on both decoded links:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=H_US_LOYALTY_BR_20261007_Membership_NewColors_GWP_STO_AllMembers_HighestEngaged_AIUnsubLow`. Both links also carry `hmail=<hash>` and `cordial_id=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9`.
- **Weak `utm_source`.** `US_Broadcast` describes the send type, not the platform or list. This is a naming-convention issue only.
- **UTMs on the unsubscribe URL** are unnecessary but harmless.
- **No `utm_content`** appears on the two decoded links, so click attribution can't be split by link position.
- **Landing alignment can't be checked.** The QA data decoded only the returns link and the unsubscribe link. I can't confirm that the CTAs go to Members-only color pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real plain-text part. It should include the offer copy, the CTA URLs and the unsubscribe link.
2. Manually click the unsubscribe and returns links from a real inbox to confirm the 403s are probe artifacts. Confirm the link-expiry window (`ce:86400`) with Cordial and make sure unsubscribe links don't expire.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and decorative images.
4. Fill in `<title>`, fix the `http-equiv` charset meta, and drop the duplicate viewport tag. Remove the maximum-scale restriction.
5. Remove or fix the empty-value `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border` rules.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` headers, DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment and the footer postal address from the raw message. They weren't in the data provided.
7. Add `utm_content` per link, and consider consolidating to one open-tracking pixel.
