# BOGO 50% off ends *TODAY*
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the seventh BOGO email in about five days, and the "Ends tomorrow" one came just yesterday. The green banner is the same one I've been seeing all week. The only new thing is the countdown clock, which says about 17 hours left. I understood the offer in two seconds: buy a pair, get the second 50% off. I just wish they'd stop sending it.
- The email is clear and the buttons are big enough for my thumbs. But none of the shoes below the banner are for me. There's a fuzzy bootie, a ballet flat, a leopard ankle boot, a stiletto, a pink clog and a metallic Mary Jane. That's six women's styles. I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for a comfortable slip-on, and I don't see a single men's shoe.
- Would I click? Probably not. If I wanted the sale I'd have gone to the store already, since I've had a week of notice. I wouldn't forward it either. I'd mark it read and move on. If DSW has a good cushioned walking shoe in the BOGO, they haven't shown me one.

## What's working

- The banner is huge and simple: "BOGO 50% OFF*" with "Buy any pair of shoes, get 50% off a second pair" underneath. I got it right away.
- The countdown timer at the top ("Ends in 16:53:52") gives a believable reason to act today.
- The "Shop Men" link is easy to find under the banner, and the nav bar has "Men" too. Text is large and the buttons are easy to tap.
- The size row, with "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons, is helpful. Wide widths matter for comfort shoppers.
- The fine print is spelled out. It says the offer runs through Oct 18, no code is needed online, and there's a barcode for in-store use.

## What's weak

- Every shoe shown is women's, and one is a stiletto. Nothing in the email says "this is for you" to a guy my age.
- Nothing is about comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-on ease. Brand names appear only in small print.
- Some tags say "BOGO 1st pair only!" while others say "BOGO 50% off!". That's confusing, and it makes me wonder if the deal has strings attached.
- This is the same BOGO message I've had all week. The "*TODAY*" subject is a repeat of "Ends tomorrow."
- The preview text is a long tracking link, not real copy. It looks sloppy in my inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send one BOGO reminder, not seven. If you have to send it again, show something new, like men's walking and slip-on comfort shoes.
- 2. Put a men's row in the "So new, so you" section, with cushioned slip-ons and walking shoes from brands like Skechers, New Balance and Hoka. Mention comfort in the product tags.
- 3. Make the tags consistent, or explain what "1st pair only" means.
- 4. Fix the preheader so it shows real copy.
- 5. Show a pair price example, like "Two $80 pairs = $120," so I can do the math.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Braze/SendGrid-sent email renders from a standard XHTML table layout with dark-mode support, but one tracked SMS opt-in CTA returned HTTP 400 and the document has several source-level accessibility defects. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by QA):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400** at `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/sCbPUpLAj-_jTUufUX39AA~~/AAQRxRA~/OYrQuD-xIFl…`. This is a revenue-bearing CTA (15% incentive). A 400 from the click-tracking redirect suggests a malformed or expired tracking token, or a link the redirect rejects when probed without a user context. Check this in a real client before treating it as a send-time defect.
- **Empty link label in the QA record:** The failure shows a blank label before "(link text: …)". The anchor may wrap an image, or the text may be split across nodes.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 68 links were probed and 56 were skipped, so the other links are unverified. The QA summary shows a 62% pass rate with only 1 FAIL and 2 WARN, so check how skipped links are counted.
- **Plain-text part:** 25,097 of 29,805 characters (about 84%) are URLs, because the long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking URLs are expanded inline. This is a spam-filter risk and makes the plain-text part hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=…` is a tracking pixel.
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/dKhf2ZC2JzyNtwA1vtH0xw~~/AAQRxRA~/2zMsldbW…` looks like an open-tracking pixel.
  - Both should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so the charset declaration is not valid.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` does not describe the message.
- **Web fonts:** Futura_PT and HelveticaNow are loaded from `braze-images.com` in `@font-face` rules. The visible CSS doesn't show whether web-safe fallbacks are declared, so I can't confirm the fallback stack.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered `{{ }}`, `${}` or `[%…%]` tokens appear in the truncated HTML or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found (expected via the AgentMail relay), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a limitation of the test harness, not evidence of a sender failure. Verify from raw headers or a seed-list test.
- **Footer not inspected:** The unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked because the HTML is truncated. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed and it raised no failure, which suggests it resolved, but this is not confirmed.
- **Sender identity:** `dsw@e.dsw.com` is a subdomain of `dsw.com`, which is consistent with the tracking hosts (`ablink.dsw.com`, `mi.dsw.com`). No mismatch found.
