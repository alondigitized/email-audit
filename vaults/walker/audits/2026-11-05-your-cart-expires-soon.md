# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- Another cart nag, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. This is the same routine as "Your cart is one click away!" and the 10/31 "Did you forget something?" send. The "cart" is a black sneaker with a blue skull painted on it. That's a Day of the Dead novelty shoe, and I have never looked at anything like it. "You liked these" is just wrong for me.
- The 25% off for Skechers Plus members is the one useful thing here. It's buried under the shoes, though, and it's not the headline. The offer is "2 days only," but the fine print says it ran 11/3 to 11/4. Today is 11/5, so it's already over. The subject line says the cart "expires soon," and the offer expired before I opened it. That feels dishonest.
- I wouldn't click. The six "more great options" are at least my kind of shoe: slip-ins, Arch Fit, waterproof boots, Contour Foam. I might glance at the Orson boots. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The six suggested shoes are mostly slip-ins and Arch Fit, which is what I shop for.
- The men's waterproof Relaxed Fit boots and the Contour Foam sneaker speak to a 62-year-old guy who wants cushion.
- The 25% off banner is big and readable on my phone.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is large text with big tap targets, and it's simple to navigate.
- The Afterpay/Klarna, store finder, and curbside pickup blocks are tidy. They're nice to have, though I wouldn't buy because of them.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's-looking, skull-painted novelty sneaker. I never viewed it, and it has nothing to do with comfort or me.
- The 25% promo ended 11/4 per the fine print, but the email says "2 days only." It was stale when it arrived.
- The subject says the cart expires, but nothing in the body says what is in my cart, when it expires, or why.
- The member discount is below the fold and tiny ("Valid thru 11/4"). The hero gets all the space.
- Four of the six picks are women's, so it didn't treat me like a known male customer.
- The email has no prices, no star ratings, and no reviews. I can't do any price-vs-quality math from it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send a cart-abandon email to someone with no cart. If you do send it, show the real item, its price, and the real deadline.
- 2. Put the member offer at the top and make sure the dates are still valid when the email lands.
- 3. Match the picks to the browsing history. For me that means men's slip-ins and Arch Fit, with a comfort line like "cushioned all-day walking."
- 4. Add star ratings and the price under each shoe.
- 5. Replace the generic "Complete your order" button with specific copy, such as "Get 25% off my Orson boots."
