# Sale alert
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Bondi 9 email in two days. Yesterday's "On sale now: Bondi 9" had the navy knit. Today it's a cream pair, and the big brown "The cult fave, on sale" is easy to read on my phone. The Bondi is the cushioned walker I actually buy, so I'm interested.
- The email doesn't say how much is off, though. The only number is in the tiny fine print at the bottom: up to 20% off, "for a limited time, while supplies last." The Shop Now button is a thin outline on black and doesn't stand out. The second half is a red trail shoe splashing in a puddle, selling free shipping if I sign up for texts. That's not for me, and I don't want more messages from HOKA.
- I'd click Shop Now to see the price. I wouldn't forward it. It's a decent reminder but a weak sale email.

## What's working

- The Bondi 9 is front and center, and the huge headline needs no zooming.
- The cream shoe photo is clean and fills the top of the screen.
- It matches yesterday's Bondi email, so I trust it more. What I saw is what I'd find on the site.
- The black block with white text is easy to read.
- The fine print states the sale start date (11/19) and says the discount applies automatically at checkout.

## What's weak

- No percentage appears in the body. I had to read the tiny footnote to find "up to 20%." That feels buried.
- The Shop Now button is a thin outline with small text. It's a small tap target and easy to miss.
- The free shipping section is aimed at new text subscribers on their first full-price order. That excludes me, and it's a bait-style hook.
- The red trail-running photo has nothing to do with the Bondi. It's the trail theme from the 18th again.
- The footer links (Locate a Store, Unlock Free Shipping, FLY for 30 Guarantee) are in a different, smaller serif font. They look unfinished.
- The legal text at the bottom is tiny and hard to read.
- That's two Bondi emails in two days, and the sale has been running since the 19th.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Up to 20% off Bondi 9." Don't leave it in the footnote.
- 2. Make the button a solid, high-contrast fill with specific copy like "Shop Bondi 9 Sale."
- 3. Replace the trail photo with a second Bondi colorway or a walking shot. Show a few colors with prices.
- 4. Cut the text signup pitch for people who already get emails, or move it to the bottom.
- 5. Fix the footer fonts and make the fine print larger.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bondi 9 is up to 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our softest walker, now on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New colors, only at HOKA.com. Discount applied at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned comfort for less. While supplies last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Image-only layout with no plain-text part, and 3 of the 5 probed tracked links failed (400/403), including the unsubscribe link. Alt text is missing on 5 content images and 2 tracking pixels, and SPF/DKIM could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[400] SMS CTA image link.** The `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6af39deed80f5cca4e07937a:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/534063b7?jwtH=…` redirect failed. The decoded `jwtP` `linkUrl` points to `https://hoka.attn.tv/p/ie7/landing-page?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&…`. The failure could be the Cordial redirect or the Attentive destination. This probe can't tell which.
- **[403] `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`.** Tracked redirect `…/6a7fe59f?…`, destination `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…`.
- **[403] Unsubscribe.** Tracked redirect `…/5c8bf8fd?…`, destination `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast…`.
- **Likely probe artifact.** A 403 from a bot-protected destination often comes from the automated probe, not from real users. The unsubscribe 403 in particular needs a manual click-test before it's treated as a real failure.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 links were probed and 7 were skipped, so the hero and product CTAs are unverified.
- **Duplicate open trackers.** Two tracking pixels come from different hosts: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. The `mg.` pixel may be injected by the relay and not by HOKA's ESP, but the data doesn't confirm that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 content images.** All five are on `images.usw2.cordial.com/1664/`:
  - `H_US_Hero_Bondi9Markdown_3.jpg`
  - `H_US_Subcopy_Hero_Bondi9Markdown_1.jpg`
  - `H_US_CTA_Hero_Bondi9Markdown_2.jpg`
  - `US_Q3_SMSCTA_DoubleAsterisk.jpg`
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`

  The hero, subcopy and CTA images appear to carry the entire message, so with images blocked or in a screen reader the email is effectively blank. The SMS CTA image is also a link, so the link has no accessible name.
- **Missing alt text on both tracking pixels.** The `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…` pixels have no `alt`. This is low impact.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it isn't a valid charset declaration.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags.** The first has `maximum-scale=1` with no `user-scalable`. The second has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`. Clients may apply either, and `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom.
- **Malformed CSS in the `<style>` block.**
  - `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` has no values, so the declarations are dropped.
  - `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` is also invalid.
  - These look like unresolved template variables. The effect is limited because the rules are dropped.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or subject, and the tracking parameters (`hmail`, `cordial_id`) resolved. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative.** The QA check reports a 0-character text version. This hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility.
- **Authentication unverified.** The `Authentication-Results` header is absent (relay limitation), so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a gap in the test setup and not evidence of a failure.
- **Unsubscribe link present but probe-failed.** The link exists in the source and the destination is `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`, but the probe returned 403. See section 2.
- **Not verifiable from the data.** The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address aren't in the provided material (HTML truncated, headers not supplied).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present and consistent.** `utm_source=US_Broadcast` and `utm_medium=email` are on the `return-start` and `attn.tv` links. The unsubscribe link also carries them.
- **Campaign name leaks segmentation.** `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261120_Bondi9_Markdown_FollowUp_STO_A120_HighestEngaged_270BondiTag_550SaleTag_LYAnniPurchasers_1DayOptIn_ExcludeYesterdayOpenNonClick` encodes internal audience logic (`HighestEngaged`, `LYAnniPurchasers`, `ExcludeYesterdayOpenNonClick`) in a recipient-visible URL. The value is also very long.
- **No link-level differentiation.** No `utm_content` or `utm_term` was observed on the probed links, so analytics can't separate the hero, CTA and footer clicks.
- **Third-party hop.** The SMS CTA goes to `hoka.attn.tv`, off the primary domain, and returned 400. Because the probe didn't reach the destination, landing-page alignment for that link can't be assessed.
- **Main CTAs unchecked.** The hero and product CTAs were among the 7 skipped, so their landing-page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click-test the unsubscribe, `return-start` and SMS CTA links from a real client. Treat the 403s as probe artifacts only if they resolve. Fix the 400 on the `attn.tv` link if it reproduces.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all five content images, including the sale message on the hero, subcopy and CTA images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Add a multipart plain-text part that includes the offer, the main links and the unsubscribe link.
4. Fill in `<title>`. Fix the charset to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`. Remove the duplicate viewport tag and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Fix the empty CSS values in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border`.
6. Shorten `utm_campaign` and move segment details to internal IDs. Add `utm_content` per link.
7. Send a test through a path that preserves `Authentication-Results`, and confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the postal address.
8. Raise probe coverage to the hero and product CTAs.
