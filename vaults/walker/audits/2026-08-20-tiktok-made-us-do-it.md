# Technical Email Audit — Crocs "TikTok made us do it 😏"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard SFMC-style responsive HTML email with open-tracking pixel, Adobe Analytics beacon, and igodigital product recommendation blocks. Primary issues are mixed-content (HTTP) asset loading and PII exposure in recommendation image URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open tracking pixel present and correctly sized (1×1, hidden container): `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?BTEBRRJCGRMU7BXBHA6XWJ6GR4.10204&d=10204&bmt=0`
- Secondary tracking beacon (Adobe Demdex) also present: `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33fe5cdf59baf088bb835d3b39ef90f188`
- **PII exposure in recommendation URLs**: the four igodigital product-recommendation image requests embed the recipient's raw email address in plaintext in the URL path, e.g. `https://10745993.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/685c6ddd06fd7c003f1cd785/i/1/walker@agentmail.to?category=Recommended+for+You`. This exposes the subscriber's email to any intermediary (proxies, CDNs, browser extensions, referrer logs) that sees the image request — should be a hashed/tokenized identifier instead of a raw address.
- 29 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect domains, not itself a defect — could not verify final destinations).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 5 images missing `alt` text: the Demdex tracking pixel (acceptable, decorative) and the 4 igodigital recommendation images (not acceptable — these are content images with no accessible fallback; their `alt` currently resolves to the raw email address string, which is both an accessibility gap and reinforces the PII leak above).
- Outlook/Gmail compatibility resets (`#MessageViewBody`, `u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`, `x-apple-data-detectors`) and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are correctly implemented.
- Mobile breakpoint at 639px with standard stacking/hide utility classes — structurally sound, not independently visually verified here.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`) found in the visible/truncated source.
- The recipient email `walker@agentmail.to` appears correctly resolved (not a broken token) but is misused as a URL path segment/alt-text value in the recommendation images — see Section 2/3.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Truncated HTML does not include the footer, so unsubscribe link and physical mailing address cannot be confirmed present or absent — flag for manual check of the untruncated source rather than assuming a violation.
- No SPF/DKIM/DMARC header data was supplied for this audit — cannot assess authentication compliance from HTML alone.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot assess: the truncated HTML does not expose the resolved destination URLs (29 links were tracking-redirects skipped by the probe), so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from available data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the raw email address in the igodigital recommendation image URLs with a hashed/tokenized subscriber ID to eliminate PII exposure via image requests and alt text.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 recommendation product images (e.g. product name) instead of leaving them keyed to the recipient's email.
3. Convert the 6 HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources (App Store, Google Play, Store Locator, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube icons) to HTTPS — non-HTTPS assets are blocked or flagged by most modern mail clients, risking broken footer branding/social icons.
4. Manually verify the unsubscribe link and physical address are present in the full (non-truncated) footer, and confirm final tracking-link destinations carry correct UTM parameters aligned to landing pages.
