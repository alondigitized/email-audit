## Executive summary

- Alright, this one at least leads with a number I can do math on — 2 for $50, that's clear, no guessing games like the sandal email or the TikTok clog nonsense from earlier this week. But I scrolled this whole thing top to bottom looking for a "Shop Now" button and there isn't one. Not one clickable-looking button anywhere in this email, just black text on white and a picture of two crocs floating in a blue rectangle.
- There's also some garbled overlapping text right up at the top, above "THIS WEEKEND ONLY!" — looks like fine print bleeding through the header, which doesn't inspire confidence that anything else is going to render right. And then it's crocs — the rubber clog kind, not the shoe I actually wear. I don't do the foam clog thing, that's more my daughter's speed.
- Bottom line: I'd glance at the headline, register "2 for $50," and probably swipe past. Nothing here pulls me in to tap, and even if I wanted to, I'm not sure where I'd tap. Not forwarding this to anybody.

## What's working

- The offer is stated plainly — "2 For $50" — no vague "up to" language, I like that.
- "Available online & in-store" is useful, I don't always want to order online.
- End date is specific enough (Sunday, August 23rd) buried in the fine print at the bottom, even if it's small.
- Product shot is clean, at least I can tell what's being sold.

## What's weak

- No visible button or CTA anywhere in this email — nothing that looks tappable. How am I supposed to buy this?
- Overlapping/garbled text at the very top of the email, above the headline — looks broken before I even get to the offer.
- It's the classic rubber clog — not my style, no cushioned walking shoe in sight, nothing that says "comfort for a guy on his feet all day."
- A whole second section pushing "40% Off Accessories" with no picture of what those accessories even are — Jibbitz charms, straps, who knows.
- Way too much empty white space between sections, I had to scroll forever to get to the app store badges and social icons at the bottom for nothing.

- 2. Fix whatever's causing that text overlap at the top before this goes out again.
- 3. Show a shoe that fits my life — if Crocs has any slip-on with actual arch support or a walking-friendly sole, feature that instead of the standard clog.
- 4. Cut the dead space — this could be half as long and hit harder.
- 5. Drop the accessories upsell or at least show a picture — a blind 40% off with no visual is just filler.
