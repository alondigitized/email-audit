## Executive summary

A waterproof shoe does sound good for a wet fall, though.
- "Weather any storm" is a clear message, and the six shoes in a grid are easy to scan. But nothing tells me about cushioning, arch support, or whether any of these slip on. ## What's working

- The hero shows an actual shoe, big and clear, so I know what this is about right away.
- "Weather any storm" and the line about wet weather speak to a real fall problem I have.
- The six-shoe grid has plain names and plenty of white space, easy to read on my phone.
- The GORE-TEX line explains in plain words that it keeps rain out and still breathes.
- The "Walking" link in the footer at least tells me they know people like me exist.

## What's weak

- Almost everything shown is trail or running. Nothing says walking, cushioning, or arch support, which is what I shop for.
- No prices anywhere. I can't do my price-versus-comfort math, and the 25% off is easy to miss in the gray strip.
- The deal ends 10/29, but the fine print is tiny and sits at the bottom. That's the "buried" kind of deadline I don't trust.
- The jacket, "Warm layers" and the shopping-bag picture with the sale line pull me off the shoes. The bag image is the same one from 10/20 and 10/21.
- The grid captions are small and gray, and I'd have to pinch to read them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deal and the deadline up top, something like "25% off waterproof shoes through tomorrow", right under the hero. Put prices under each shoe.
- 2. Add a walking-specific waterproof shoe, with a one-line comfort note such as "cushioned, easy on and off."
- 3. Cut the jacket and bag sections, or move them below one strong shoe button. Make the type bigger.
- 4. Label the buttons "Shop waterproof shoes" instead of "Shop now."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML and QA data show no hard failures (0 issues, 2 warnings, 75% pass rate). The source has template leftovers, including a placeholder background URL and `@font-face` rules with no `src`. Three images lack alt text, and authentication status couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, links, and footer weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 26. No broken or redirecting links were reported among the probed ones, but the 26 skipped links are unverified.
- The plain text part is 11,684 of 13,473 characters URLs (about 87%). This is a QA warning (">70% URLs") that suggests very long tracked URLs in the text alternative, and it can slightly hurt spam scoring.
- Click wrapping goes through `link.email.newbalance.com`, a branded tracking domain. That is fine, but the unwrapped destinations couldn't be checked from the data provided.
- The `.bgwhite` rule in the `<style>` block points to `http://image.XXXXX.png`. This is a placeholder URL left in the template. It is non-HTTPS and will 404 or trigger a mixed-content request wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** (confirmed by QA):
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png` (`https://link.email.newbalance.com/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingIcons_06.png`)
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` (same path)
  - `egR1f966kc_818512772_12532685_428417_23648129.gif` (`/mo/` path, an open-tracking pixel). An empty `alt=""` is acceptable for the pixel, but the two content images need descriptive alt.
- **Font rules have no sources:** Every `@font-face` (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, `proxima-nova-condensed`, `ITC Garamond Book Condensed`, and others) has no `src`, so they declare nothing loadable. Rendering falls back to `Arial`/`Times New Roman`, as the stacks anticipate.
- **Duplicate class:** `.bg1` is defined twice with different values (`#ffffff`, then `#58546d`). The later one wins, so the intended white background is overridden. The dark-mode override also sets `.bg1{background:#151415 !important}`.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Document type mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute and self-closing tags. This is harmless in practice but inconsistent.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html>` has none, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Generic title:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is a template default, not specific to this send.
- **Dark-mode link color:** `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` forces `a {color:#cf0a2c !important;}` on every link. I couldn't check contrast against the dark backgrounds, so this is worth a manual test.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No UTM parameters or landing-page alignment could be assessed. Destinations are hidden behind `link.email.newbalance.com` redirects, and the visible HTML was truncated. This is not confirmed as a defect.

1. Remove the `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder from `.bgwhite`, or replace it with an HTTPS asset.
