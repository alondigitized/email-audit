# Most-wanted gifts
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- It's another HOKA shoe email, and the shoes lead, which I like. "Holiday hits" is huge, white on a deep teal photo, and I can read it on my phone without squinting. But the hero shoe is a pink and magenta women's Clifton. That's not me, and it made me think this was a gift email for somebody else.
- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline anywhere in it. It's just "Don't guess" and a Shop Now button. I've seen this pattern a lot lately, like the "Triple-black styles" email yesterday and "Fall's top colors" on the 7th. Pretty shoes, no reason to act today. I'd skim it, maybe tap into the Gaviota 6 Men, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Holiday hits" headline is big, high contrast and easy to read on a phone.
- The shoe grid is clean. Each shoe has a name and a Men or Women label, so I can find mine fast.
- "Gaviota 6 Men" and "Clifton 11 Men" are cushioned, stable walking shoes, which is what I look for.
- "Members-only colors" nods to loyalty without making me do points math.
- The "Gifts for Him" button near the bottom is easy to find and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- There's no offer at all. No price, no sale, no free-shipping callout up top. "Unlock Free Shipping" is buried in the footer links.
- The hero shoe is a pink women's model, so the first impression isn't me.
- The two Shop Now buttons don't say where they go. I can't tell if I land on a gift guide or the homepage.
- Nothing about fit, such as wide sizes, arch support or cushioning. I want to know why a shoe is good for my feet.
- The runners in the lifestyle photo are young and moving fast. There's no one who looks like a 62-year-old walking to the store.
- The footer text is tiny and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real reason to buy in the hero: a price, a gift-card promise or a ship-by date. Right now there's nothing pulling me to act.
- 2. Lead with a men's walking shoe in the hero, or let the reader pick "For Him" or "For Her" at the top.
- 3. Add one line under each shoe, like "Max cushion, all-day walking" or "Wide available," plus a price.
- 4. Make the buttons specific, like "Shop Gifts for Him."
- 5. Move the free shipping and 30-day guarantee up where I can see them without scrolling.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Every probed Cordial tracked link failed (11× HTTP 406, 1× 403), the plain-text part is empty, and 18 images have no alt text. The uniform 406/403 pattern on every `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/...` link looks like the probe being bot-filtered rather than real breakage (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links failed.** These are 11× 406 (hero, product tiles, "HOKA" logo link, `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`) and 1× 403 (Unsubscribe).
  - Every one is a `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6ad6...:1/<hash>?jwtH=...&jwtP=...&jwtS=...` Cordial wrapper.
  - A 100% failure rate across unrelated destinations points to the probe being rejected (406 is typically a User-Agent/Accept rejection), not 12 dead pages.
  - **Unconfirmed:** a real browser or mail client may resolve these fine. Re-probe with a browser-like UA and `Accept` headers before treating them as defects.
- **Decoded destinations look valid.** The `jwtP` payload carries `linkUrl`, which decodes to these targets:
  - `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/best-gifts/` (hero images a, b, c)
  - `.../womens-everyday-running-gear/clifton-11/1176573.html?dwvar_1176573_color=RWY`
  - `.../mens-everyday-running-gear/clifton-11/1176572.html?dwvar_1176572_color=FLCK`
  - `.../womens-everyday-running-shoes/gaviota-6/1171933.html?dwvar_1171933_color=FYGL`
  - `.../new-arrivals/?srule=popular-descending`
  - `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...`
  - None of these destinations were probed directly, so their live status is unverified.
- **JWT link expiry.** `jwtP` contains `"ce":86400`, which looks like a 24-hour link-expiry claim. If it means that, clicks on a 2026-11-10 send could fail the next day. This is inferred from the payload and unconfirmed.
- **Dual open-tracking pixels.** There is one from Cordial (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/1664:...`) and one from Mailgun (`mg.emails.hoka.com/o/eJyM...`). Both lack alt text (see section 3).
- **Coverage gap.** 19 of 31 links were skipped by the probe, including footer and social links, so they are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **18 images have no alt text.** They are:
  - hero images `US_20261110_GiftGuideDrip2_a` through `_h`
  - the product tiles: Clifton 11 (RWY, FLCK, RRYB_MO, BVRT_MO) and Gaviota 6 (FYGL, MLFD)
  - `US_20261013_MembershipWeek_new_a.jpg`
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
  - both tracking pixels
- Because the images carry no alt text, the image-only links fall back to the filename (e.g., `[image: US_Clifton11_TopLeft_1176573-RWY.jpg]`). That means no usable link text for screen readers or images-off clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Charset declaration is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute.
- **Conflicting viewport metas.** There are two. The first sets `maximum-scale=1`, which restricts pinch-zoom; the second sets `user-scalable=yes`.
- **Invalid CSS.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have no values, so they are dropped.
- **Focus outlines removed.** The global `* {outline:none}` removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Web fonts.** `TheFutureHOKA` loads from `hoka.com` via `@font-face`. Most clients ignore this, so check the fallback stack in the full source.
- **Stale or mismatched asset names.**
  - `US_20261013_MembershipWeek_new_a.jpg` is an October asset reused in a Nov 10 send.
  - `CA_Gaviota6_BottomRight_1171932-MLFD.jpg` is a CA-prefixed asset linking to a `/en/us/` URL.
