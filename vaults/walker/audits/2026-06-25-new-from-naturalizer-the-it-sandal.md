## Technical Audit — Famous Footwear: "NEW from Naturalizer: The It Sandal"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard table-based HTML build with no critical rendering blockers, but carries a pervasive alt text omission across 35+ images and a plain-text body composition that raises deliverability risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 click-redirect links not probed** — all hrefs route through `click.email.famousfootwear.com` (e.g., `https://click.email.famousfootwear.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOj...`). The QA crawler correctly skipped these; destination URLs and HTTP status codes are unverified. Broken destination links would be invisible in this report.

**Open pixel present** — `https://click.email.famousfootwear.com/open.aspx?FWHHZXGV3FJURPS4B5NDFT4GQA.70256&d=70256&bmt=0` is a 1×1 tracking pixel with empty `alt=""`. Functional, but will be blocked by Apple MPP and some corporate proxies — open rate data will be understated.

No broken or malformed href values detected in the visible source fragment.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**35 images missing alt text** (QA confirmed 35 distinct warnings). This includes the logo (`3b6a7dc1-…ee.png`), divider bars (`4ccff4ad-…38.jpg` appears 4 times), and every product image. Screen readers will skip all content images silently. With images disabled (common in B2B inboxes), the email will render as a blank white column — no fallback text communicates any offer or product.

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag (`maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`) prevents pinch-zoom on mobile — an accessibility violation under WCAG 1.4.4 and discouraged by iOS/Android guidelines.

`<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` is missing the `http-equiv` attribute — it should be `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`. Some legacy clients (Outlook 2007–2013) may misinterpret encoding without it.

HTML doctype is `HTML 4.01 Transitional` — acceptable for email but dated; not a functional issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[FNAME]`) visible in the source fragment. Loyalty/rewards copy ("Want Reward Cash and other perks?") is static — no personalization tokens observed, which may be intentional for a broadcast send.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Plain-text alternative — HIGH RISK**: QA reports plain-text body is 90.1% URLs (9,088 of 10,082 chars). A plain-text part composed almost entirely of raw redirect URLs (e.g., `https://click.email.famousfootwear.com/?qs=ABB7…`) with no human-readable copy is a strong spam filter signal. CAN-SPAM requires a "clear and conspicuous" means to identify the message as an ad; a URL-only plain-text part may also fail Gmail and Outlook bulk filtering heuristics.

**Unsubscribe / CAN-SPAM**: Cannot confirm presence of physical mailing address or unsubscribe link from the truncated source — these are required by CAN-SPAM §5. The sending domain (`email.famousfootwear.com`) is consistent with a legitimate ESP subdomain, suggesting footer compliance is likely present but was cut from the source excerpt.

Authentication headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) not provided for review — cannot assess.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All destination links are wrapped behind `click.email.famousfootwear.com` redirects with opaque query strings (e.g., `?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOj...`). Because destination URLs were not probed, it cannot be confirmed whether:
- UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended at redirect resolution
- Landing pages match the featured product (Naturalizer "Brandy" sandal per preheader)

This is an unverified gap — recommend spot-checking 2–3 redirect chains manually to confirm UTM pass-through and landing page alignment.
