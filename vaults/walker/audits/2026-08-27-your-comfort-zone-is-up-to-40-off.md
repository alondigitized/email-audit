- Alright, "Comfort Zone" — that's actually a phrase that speaks to me, and for once the top of the email backs it up with a real, familiar deal: Brooks Ghost 17 and Revel 8, up to $40 off. - Brooks Ghost 17 & Revel 8 up to $40 off is a real product I know and want, front and center with no digging required.

## What's weak

- The hero image for "Comfort Zone" is a woman's hands and legs, not a guy — doesn't match who's supposedly shopping for comfort here.
- The Skechers "New From Skechers" shot is also a young woman on stairs — two demographic misses in one email.
- Nothing about arch support, cushioning, or actual comfort-fit detail — just price call-outs, no reason given for why these are "comfort zone" shoes.

- 1. Swap the hero photo to a man actually lacing up or wearing the shoe — if you're going to call it "my comfort zone," show me in it.

- 4. Trim the number of separate offers (SAVE20, Brooks, Skechers, Family Sale, in-store $20/$10) — four different deals in one email is a lot to sort through on a phone.
