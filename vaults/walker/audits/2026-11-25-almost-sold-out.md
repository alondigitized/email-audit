# ALMOST SOLD OUT
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "Here we go again." This is the fourth or fifth HOKA email I've gotten this week with the same Black Friday sale. The "Going, going..." headline is huge blue type on black, and I can read it without squinting. But it doesn't tell me what's going, and "ALMOST SOLD OUT" is a big claim when the body shows no stock numbers and no sizes.
- The shoes shown are the Arahi 8 and Kawana 3, in women's and men's pairs. The Arahi is a stability running shoe and the Kawana is a lighter everyday trainer. Neither is the cushioned Bondi walker I actually buy. The "up to 40% off" line is buried near the bottom, so I had to scroll a long way to find the one thing I care about.
- I probably wouldn't click. I'd skim it, see no Bondi, and move on. I wouldn't forward it either. It's a "mark as read" for me.

## What's working

- The "Going, going..." headline is big and high contrast, so I can read it on my phone without zooming.
- The "Shop Sale" buttons are clear and easy to tap.
- The four shoes are on clean backgrounds with the name and men's/women's label under each, so I know what I'm looking at.
- The close-up of the blue and yellow shoe shows off the thick cushioned sole, which is what I like about HOKA.
- The fine print says the offer ends 11/26/26, so there's a real deadline.

## What's weak

- The top of the email shows no price, no percentage and no deadline. I have to scroll to the bottom to learn it's "up to 40% off."
- The Bondi isn't here. I'd have clicked on that.
- "These won't last long" and "ALMOST SOLD OUT" read as pressure. Nothing shows low stock or which sizes are left.
- Nobody here looks like me. The shoes are fine, but the email gives me no walking or comfort angle, and the arch support story is missing.
- The footer links ("Locate a Store," "Unlock Free Shipping," "FLY for 30 Guarantee") are small and hard to tap. The free shipping and 30-day guarantee are what would help me decide, and they're hidden down there.

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Up to 40% off" with the end date, right under the headline.
- 2. Show a Bondi, since I've been getting Bondi emails all month, and say it's cushioned for walking.
- 3. Back up the scarcity claim with real info, like "Limited sizes left in Arahi 8."
- 4. Move free shipping and the 30-day guarantee up near the buttons.
- 5. Make the footer links bigger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on Cordial (`e.emails.hoka.com` click tracking, `images.usw2.cordial.com` image CDN). The automated checks found two real problems: no plain-text part (0 chars) and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The unsubscribe link also returned 403 when probed, but that may be bot-blocking rather than a real failure. The HTML itself also has several coding defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe returned 403** on the Cordial wrapper `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6af4964e…/8757b89f?jwtH=…`. The JWT payload decodes to a `linkUrl` of `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261125_PM_AlmostSoldOut_…&hmail=…&cordial_id=…`.
  - A 403 from a signed tracking redirect is often bot or user-agent filtering, so this is unconfirmed. Only a real-client test can confirm whether unsubscribe works.
  - The QA output shows an empty URL label before "(link text: Unsubscribe)", which suggests the probe lost the final destination.
- **UTM parameters on the unsubscribe URL.** The `utm_*` parameters are unnecessary on a utility link. The `utm_campaign` value also exposes internal segment logic: `HighEmailEngagers_720StabilityPurchasers_365StabilityTag_30SaleTag_AOVSub120_SearchArahiKawana_NonSMSPromoRecipients`.
- **Two open-tracking pixels.** There is one on `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and one on `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. This is redundant and sends two separate hosts open data. Neither has `alt`.
- **Other links.** 12 links were probed and 3 skipped. No other failures were reported, but the footer, utility and social links were deprioritized and not verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No plain-text alternative.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". This is a deliverability and accessibility defect.
- **16 content images have no `alt`.** The hero (`H_US_Hero_StylesThatWillSellOut_1.jpg`), subcopy, headlines and CTAs (`H_US_CTA_BFEA_1.jpg`, `H_US_CTA_ShopSale.jpg`, `H_US_CTA_BFEA_2.jpg`) are image-only and have no `alt`. The product tiles (`US_Arahi8_*`, `US_Kawana3_*`) and `LogoFooterPrimary.png` are also missing it.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the offer copy and CTA labels are lost entirely.
  - The two tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Charset declaration is malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it declares nothing.
- **Conflicting duplicate viewport metas.** One has `maximum-scale=1`, the other `user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` also restricts pinch-zoom.
- **Invalid CSS from empty template variables.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have no values.
- **`* {…outline:none;}`** removes focus indicators for all elements.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the truncated source. The rest of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is present** in the body (text "Unsubscribe", target `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`) but returned 403, as above.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided** in the data, so one-click unsubscribe support can't be confirmed.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.** The QA check found no `Authentication-Results` header, because the message came through the AgentMail relay. The sender is `info@emails.hoka.com`.
- **Physical postal address.** It isn't visible in the truncated source, so it can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only parameters I could verify come from the unsubscribe link: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261125_PM_AlmostSoldOut_A7Day_…`. They follow a consistent naming pattern.
- I could not check whether the CTA and product links (Arahi 8, Kawana 3, Clifton 10 sale) carry UTMs, or whether they land on matching pages. The source is truncated, and the QA output gives no destination detail for the probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify unsubscribe with a real client.** If it truly fails, fix it. If it is bot-blocking, allowlist the QA user agent. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
2. **Add a multipart plain-text part** with the offer copy, CTA URLs and unsubscribe link.
3. **Add meaningful `alt` text to all 16 images.** Use the actual headline, subcopy and CTA text for the image-only banners, and `alt=""` for the tracking pixels.
4. **Fix the head and CSS.**
   - Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`.
   - Fill in `<title>`.
   - Merge the viewport metas and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Remove or populate the empty `px` rules.
   - Don't set `outline:none` globally.
5. **Strip UTMs from the unsubscribe URL**, and keep internal audience segment names out of any recipient-visible URL.
6. **Drop one of the two open pixels**, unless both are needed.
7. **Run the SPF/DKIM/DMARC check against the original message headers**, not the relayed copy.
