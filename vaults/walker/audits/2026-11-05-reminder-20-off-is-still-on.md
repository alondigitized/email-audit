# Reminder: 20% OFF is still on!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and I'm tired of it. This is the same 20% pitch I've gotten all week, with the same TINSEL code as the "20% off starts now!" email earlier today. The subject says "Reminder," and that's honest. It's the same email as before with a nudge on top. The "20% OFF*" up top is big and easy to read on my phone, and the black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is hard to miss.
- The "SHOP MEN" link is a small underlined line, and below it the grid has no men's shoes. Six shoes: a loafer, two women's boots, a sandal, a sneaker and a slipper. Nothing with cushioning or arch support, and no slip-ons I'd wear. The whole thing is also a long scroll with a wall of fine print.
- I'd probably skim it, and I wouldn't forward it. A man-sized "Shop Men" button and one real comfort shoe would get my thumb moving. I'd click only if I needed shoes this weekend, since the deal ends the 8th.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" is huge and readable without squinting, and the offer is obvious.
- The black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is large and easy to tap.
- The "SHOP WOMEN / MEN / KIDS" links under the hero let me skip straight to my section.
- The "SHOP BY SIZE" block, with wide width options, is useful for my feet.
- The barcode for in-store use is handy, and the deadline (Nov 5–8) is spelled out in the fine print.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the product grid is for a man. It's all women's styles and a slipper, with no walking or comfort shoes.
- The "SHOP MEN" link is small, underlined text, and a thumb could easily miss it.
- It's the same TINSEL 20% I've seen all week. I'm getting a "reminder" for an offer I've already seen.
- The asterisk exclusions are long, and the brand list rules out Skechers, Clarks and TOMS, the very comfort brands I look for.
- "Extra 20% off reg. price" under the shoes says the same thing as the hero, and the fine print for the offer is tiny.
- The hero says "Happy Gifting," but I'm not shopping for a gift.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Send men a hero with a comfort shoe and a clear "Shop Men's Comfort" button.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Sunday") instead of burying it in the fine print.
- 3. Add a "Cushioned & Easy On" row with slip-ons and arch-support styles.
- 4. Cut the repeat sends or vary the offer. A reminder should add something new.
- 5. Shorten the legal block and put the key exclusions near the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off ends Sunday: code TINSEL`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort shoes for him: 20% off through Nov 8`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ons, walkers and wide widths. Use code TINSEL by Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last 4 days to save 20% on regular-price styles in stores and online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent DSW promo. One probed link, the SMS opt-in CTA, returned HTTP 400 (62% QA pass rate). The source has several markup problems: ineffective `<meta>` declarations, no `lang` attribute, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The HTML was truncated, so the body and footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed, FAIL):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400** at `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/GH6c_AuEr0n9tT7WFLzATw~~/AAQRxRA~/jOf0Pw…`. The QA output shows an empty label before the link text, so the anchor may have no accessible name or a malformed href. The probe can't show whether the 400 comes from the tracker, the redirect target, or bot-blocking of the SMS-signup destination. Verify with a real browser or mobile user agent before treating it as a live outage.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 73 links were probed and 61 were skipped, so the rest are unverified. The unsubscribe link is always probed, and the QA output doesn't list a failure for it.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261105_P261640_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` is a second-party open pixel. It adds a separate tracking domain alongside `ablink.dsw.com`. This is not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, confirmed):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/gQzbQMZHF8PkR4A11NLHag~~/AAQRxRA~/6g3Lj5qM…` is a tracked content image. Its absence from alt leaves it unreadable with images blocked or with a screen reader.
  - `o.gif` is a tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Ineffective meta tags (source):** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration works. Charset then depends on the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Plain-text part (WARN):** 25,796 of 31,054 characters (about 83%) are URLs. Long Braze-wrapped links make the text version nearly unreadable and can raise spam-filter scoring.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appeared in the truncated portion. The body wasn't fully available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication (WARN):** no `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The QA notes this is expected through the AgentMail relay. Treat it as a gap in QA coverage, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer unverified:** the physical postal address and unsubscribe markup were beyond the truncated source. The QA shows no unsubscribe failure, but this audit can't confirm either element.
- **Sender:** `dsw@e.dsw.com` is a branded subdomain, consistent with a properly delegated sending domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Every link is wrapped by the `ablink.dsw.com` click tracker, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs can't be seen in the HTML. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261105_P261640_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` on the pixel, and it is truncated. Landing-page alignment is unverified, and the one probed CTA that failed can't be compared to the offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the "Opt-In to Texts" link in a real browser and on mobile. If it fails, fix the destination or link generation in Braze, and make sure the anchor has visible text.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the tracked content image and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
3. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags.
4. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
5. Shorten the text part, for example with a shorter link wrapper or fewer links in the text version.
6. Capture the full raw message headers to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Re-run QA on the full HTML to verify the footer address, the unsubscribe link and the UTM parameters after redirect.
