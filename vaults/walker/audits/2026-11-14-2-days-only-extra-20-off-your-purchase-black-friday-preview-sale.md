# 2 Days Only! Extra 20% off your purchase + Black Friday Preview Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival again. I've lost count of how many of these I've gotten. The top of this one is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. A big red "20% OFF" with "2 DAYS ONLY" and the code HOLIDAY20 leaves no doubt about the deal. But it's the same 20% off I got on Nov 11 with the Veterans Day banner, and the "$25 off" on Nov 12. They keep giving me a new name for the same coupon.
- After the hero, it becomes a long catalog of clogs, Birkenstocks, kids' shoes and a game. The only things for me are one Skechers Slip-ins tile and a tiny "Men's" thumbnail. The young woman in the hero and the slip-ins photo isn't who this is talking to. I'd probably tap once for the Slip-ins at $59.98, since I like not bending over to put on shoes. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd only open it again when I need shoes.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" hero is huge and red, and I can read it at arm's length. The code HOLIDAY20 is right under it.
- "2 DAYS ONLY" gives a real deadline, and the fine print confirms it ends Sunday.
- The Skechers Slip-ins tile, "Starting at $59.98", is the one thing aimed at a guy like me. Hands-free is what I want.
- The Shoe PERKS banner at the top and the "2-3X points" on Birkenstock are there, but they don't get in my way.
- The Women's / Men's / Kids' split makes it easy to jump to my section.

## What's weak

- The scratch-off "Saturday" banner at the very top pushes the actual offer down and doesn't say what I'm scratching.
- Seven or eight different deals are stacked in one email. I can't tell which one matters, and after the 20% off I stopped reading closely.
- The Men's shoe thumbnail is small, and "Shop Men's" is tiny, thin link text. That's a hard tap on a phone.
- There's no comfort, cushioning or arch-support language anywhere. It's all price, no fit.
- The asterisks and the wall of fine print at the bottom are too small to read without zooming. "Even on sale prices!" has exclusions I can't make out.
- The Birkenstock "FINAL DAY" and the Wordle-style "Word Stride" game feel like filler. This is the 8th-plus email in two weeks, so it feels like a flood.

- 1. Open with one offer, the 20% off with the code, and a big button: "Shop Men's Comfort Shoes". Cut it down to a hero, one Slip-ins block and one men's block.
- 2. Add something for my kind of shopping: cushioned, arch-supported and walking styles, with a star rating or review count.
- 3. Make the links large buttons. "Get In Store Coupon" and "Shop Men's" should be tap-friendly.
- 4. Send fewer emails, and don't repeat the same 20% off under a new name every other day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based, responsive template with valid `lang="en"` and a viewport meta tag. The QA data shows three real problems: an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder in a tracking-pixel URL, a campaign tag naming a different brand, and missing alt text on the hero image. The source was truncated, so the body, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved token in a pixel URL:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still contains the literal `[UNIQUE]`. If the ESP was meant to substitute a per-recipient value, it didn't, so this open pixel may not attribute opens correctly.
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261114_skechers_birkenstock_AM​` ends with a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. This can corrupt the parameter value or break the request. The query string is also malformed: `t=utm_campaign=...` nests a key=value pair inside `t`.
- **Campaign tag mismatch:** The tag contains `skechers_birkenstock`, but the sender is Shoe Carnival and the subject is a 20% off / Black Friday Preview offer. This looks like a reused template or campaign ID, so reporting may be misattributed. I can't confirm the cause from the available data.
- **Unverified links:** 31 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status and redirect chains are unverified. That is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Hero image has no alt text:** `https://image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/7b6ef0a3-667b-46a4-b16f-69cbf867cd77.jpg` has none. If it carries the offer, it is invisible with images blocked and to screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels have no alt:** The two `emltrk.com` pixels should use `alt=""`, which is low severity.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`. `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and self-closing `/>` tags.
- **Link styling removed:** The global rule `a, a:link, ... { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` strips link styling. Any text link not styled as a button is visually indistinguishable from body text.
- **Small mobile text:** `.responsive-text` is set to 12px at max-width 600px, which is below the usual 14px minimum.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `d=[UNIQUE]` is the only unresolved token found (see section 2).
- No other tokens were found in the visible source, but the truncated body can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is missing. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is not a confirmed sender-side failure.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** The unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and postal address are not in the truncated source or QA output. I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only UTM evidence is the pixel's `utm_campaign=SCA_20261114_skechers_birkenstock_AM`, which mismatches the brand and offer (see section 2).
- Click links are wrapped in redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM propagation and landing-page alignment with the "Extra 20% off" / "Black Friday Preview Sale" offer are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the ESP substitute a real value for `d=[UNIQUE]`, or remove the pixel if it is stale.
2. Strip the U+200B from the `ieezzy5m` pixel URL and fix the `t=utm_campaign=...` query structure.
3. Check why the campaign ID contains `skechers_birkenstock` and correct the tag for the Shoe Carnival send.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the hero image `7b6ef0a3-...jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
5. Fix the meta tags (`http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`), and align the doctype with the markup.
6. Keep underline or other link styling for inline text links, and raise mobile `.responsive-text` to at least 14px.
7. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Use a follow-up probe that resolves the 31 redirect links to confirm landing pages and UTMs, and check the unsubscribe link, headers and address.
8. Get raw headers from the original delivery (not the AgentMail relay) to validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
