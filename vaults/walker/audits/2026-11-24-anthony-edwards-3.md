# Anthony Edwards 3 🧊🏀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email, and this one isn't for me. It's a basketball shoe for a young NBA player, and I'm 62 and walking the mall. The photography is moody and dark, and the shoe floats in a glow. It looks like a magazine ad, but there's no price and no deal. After a week of "up to 60% off" Black Friday emails, a shoe launch with nothing to save feels odd.
- The one line that got my attention was "Hyperboost bringing a new level of comfort and energy." Comfort is my word. But it's buried in small grey type, and it's talking about explosive moves on a court. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The headline is big and easy to read without squinting.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons are black with white text, and they're big enough for my thumb.
- The layout is one clean column with lots of white space. Nothing overlaps in the main body.
- The mention of Hyperboost cushioning at least nods at comfort.
- It looks like the adidas I know, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline. The Black Friday emails I've been getting had all three.
- It's a basketball shoe for hoopers. Nothing here speaks to a man who wants slip-on, cushioned, arch-supported walking shoes.
- The body copy is small and grey on white, which is hard to read on my phone.
- The "ANTHONY EDWARDS 3" lettering is hidden behind the player's body in the second photo. The "NY" and "ED" are hard to make out.
- The two "SHOP NOW" buttons and the banner button all look the same. They don't say what I'd be shopping for. The top banner says "best sellers," which is a different message from the shoe below it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or a member offer in the hero. Even "$140, free shipping" gives me something to weigh against Hoka and Brooks.
- 2. Cut the second half. It repeats the first half and doesn't add anything.
- 3. Make the buttons say what they do, like "Shop Anthony Edwards 3."
- 4. Put a line about cushioning and fit up high, in bigger type, for people who aren't playing basketball.
- 5. Move the top banner to the bottom, or match it to the shoe.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hyperboost cushioning, now in stock. Sizes 7 to 15.`
