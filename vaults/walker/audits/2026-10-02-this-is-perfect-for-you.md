# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- Fourth abandoned-cart-style nudge in two days, and this one is a little different. The pink flower sneaker is gone, and in its place is a black Glide-Step Altus with a pink and peach sole. It's a Slip-ins shoe, which is my kind of idea. But the pink sole looks like a women's shoe to me, so I still feel like the email is guessing at who I am. "Still thinking about it?" after "Your Items Are Waiting!" and "Your cart expires soon!" this week makes me feel chased.
- The Buy One, Get One 50% Off banner for Skechers Plus members is the best thing here, and it's the only part that says anything about me as a member. It sits way down the page, below six more shoes. Three of those six are kids' shoes, and two are women's. Only one is a men's shoe, and it's a brown slip-on I'd actually look at.
- I'd open the app and look at that brown Relaxed Fit slip-on. I wouldn't click this email's main button, and I wouldn't forward it to anybody.

## What's working

- The "Take Another Look" button is big, dark navy and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit: Altman - Murdock Sneaker is exactly my type: hands-free, casual, and a color that goes with everything.
- The Plus Members banner is clear: "Buy One, Get One 50% Off or 20% off a single pair," with a deadline of 10/7.
- The shop-by-category list (Women, Men, Kids...) is big text and easy to read on a phone.
- The Skechers logo and clean white layout match what I've seen from this brand all week, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- "Still thinking about it?" is the third nag in two days. I'm tired of being followed.
- The hero shoe has a pink sole and peach trim, and it reads women's to me. Nothing says it's a men's shoe.
- Of the six "options we think you'll love," only one is a men's shoe. Three are kids' shoes, and I have no idea why they think I want a Garage Sneaker.
- The member deal is buried below the product grid, and the fine print under it is tiny and hard to read without zooming.
- There's no mention of cushioning, arch support or price on any shoe, so I can't compare it to my Hokas or Brooks.
- The shoe captions under the grid are very small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the member BOGO banner to the top, right under the headline. It's the one thing that makes me act.
- 2. Pick the hero shoe from the men's side, and fill the grid with men's slip-ons, not kids' shoes.
- 3. Add the price and one comfort detail under each shoe, like "Arch Fit, cushioned insole, $79."
- 4. Cut the cart reminders to one per week. After a few, it feels like pestering.
- 5. Make the fine print and shoe names bigger.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, members get BOGO 50% off through 10/7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Slip-ins are still here, plus 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members: buy one, get one 50% off men's slip-ins thru 10/7.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Arch Fit comfort you can step into, with member pricing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Most of the 12 probed links resolve, but the footer SMS-signup and Unsubscribe links return HTTP 400. The email has no plain-text part (0 chars), serves about a dozen images over `http://`, and has several images without alt text. QA pass rate is 67%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (400), "LET'S GET TEXTING":** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/pUK/joinsms/?utm_content=footer&utm_term=text-signup&...&attn_lid=12&externalId=2U3eG&attn_sid=b755205e-...`
- **Broken link (400), "Unsubscribe":** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1...&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_lid=m46&externalId=2U3eG&attn_sid=b755205e-...`
- **Possible probe artifact:** both failing URLs are on the Attentive `attn.tv` domain, which may reject automated or non-browser requests. The 400 is confirmed by QA, but whether real recipients get the same error is not.
- **Wrapped links:** the visible `<a href>` values are `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>` redirect wrappers. The final destinations can't be checked from the source.
- **Skipped links:** 24 of 36 links were not probed (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). Their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images:** about 14 images load from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`. These include the logo, "Take Another Look", the app-store badges, Afterpay, Find a Store and the social icons. The QA warning says clients may block them, which would leave blank slots.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `150510_BKMT_HERO_LG` (`https://images.skechers.com/image;width=auto%2Cformat=auto/150510_BKMT_HERO_LG`) is the hero image.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` is a content image.
  - The Attentive open pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...`) also has no alt. This one is low severity because it is a tracking pixel.
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title></title>`.
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** the source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so some clients may ignore it.
- **Plain-text part:** the text version is 0 chars. This is also a deliverability issue (see Section 5).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `externalId=2U3eG` value is populated in the URLs. The source was truncated, so the lower part of the email was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link:** present, but it returned 400 in the QA probe. If real recipients also get an error, that is a CAN-SPAM risk, because the opt-out must work.
- **Plain-text alternative:** missing (0 chars). Multipart emails without a text part can score worse with spam filters.
- **Not verifiable:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC, the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address were not in the provided data. I'm not flagging them as issues.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs:** the SMS link carries a full set: `utm_medium=email`, `utm_source=trig`, `utm_campaign=TRG_US_EN_ATTBROWSE_1_09282026_ELSE`, `utm_content=footer`, `utm_term=text-signup`.
- **Campaign date:** `09282026` in `utm_campaign` is presumably the trigger's creation date, not the send date. I'm not flagging it.
- **Hero link:** the hero image is hosted on `images.skechers.com` and is linked through an Attentive wrapper. I couldn't verify that it lands on the product shown.
- **Product links:** the QA data lists no failures among the probed links. Landing-page alignment for the product links can't be assessed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or verify the unsubscribe endpoint (`skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=...`) with a real browser or GET request. If it fails for recipients, treat it as a compliance blocker.
2. Check the `joinsms` link the same way, and repair it or drop it from the footer if it's broken.
3. Add a plain-text part with the main CTAs and the unsubscribe URL.
4. Switch all `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` image URLs to `https://`.
5. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero (`150510_BKMT_HERO_LG`) and `a6ec5198-...jpg`. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel.
6. Populate `<title>` and change the charset meta to `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
7. Re-run the probe with the 24 skipped links included, and confirm the SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` headers.
