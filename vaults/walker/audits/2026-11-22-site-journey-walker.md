# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 4/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-11-22**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I walked skechers.com on my phone and the browsing half works well. The menus have big text, and "Hands Free Slip-ins" and "Arch Fit" are easy to find. The buying half failed. I never got a size selected, my cart badge stayed at 0, and the cart page showed up as a blank white screen. I also never saw a login screen, so I can't say whether I was logged in or whether anything was personalized for me. Search for "comfort shoes" did return comfort shoes, but women's and men's styles were mixed together.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**4/10** (yesterday: 3/10)

This is a small improvement, though I only have yesterday's number and not its details. Product discovery is solid and the cart is still broken. A shopper who can't get to checkout is worth nothing to the store, however nice the menus are.

## 3. What's Working

- **The Men menu is easy for a 62-year-old.** It's a full-width drawer with large bold labels (Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies). Each row is tall and has a clear arrow, plus visible Back and X buttons.
- **The comfort features are on the homepage.** The top row shows "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit" and "Max Cus…" (cut off). That is what I shop for, and it's one tap away.
- **The Men's Shoes page has the right shortcuts.** "Hands Free Slip-ins" and "Walking Shoes" sit above the results, with big photos and captions. Filter, Sort, Size and Width buttons are easy to tap.
- **The product page text is readable.** The price is $85.00 and the name is bold. It shows 4.5 stars with 9 reviews, and Klarna and Afterpay payment options.
- **The size grid is easy to tap.** The size buttons are big and well spaced. A sold-out size (6.5) is crossed out.
- **The Add to Cart button is large.** It's a wide navy button that's hard to miss.
- **Search shows sale prices clearly.** A loafer is marked $70.00 down to $49.99, and the slip-in shows "Members Get 30% OFF!"

## 4. What's Weak

- **The cart page is blank white.** This is the worst problem. If I'd tapped "View Cart" myself, I'd have thought the site was broken and gone to Brooks or Hoka.
- **Add to Cart didn't add anything.** No size was chosen, so I got a red "Please select a size" message and the cart badge stayed at 0. That message is correct, but it only appeared after I tapped. The size grid should have been the obvious next step. Nothing confirmed that anything had been added.
- **I saw no login screen.** The Log In step looks identical to the homepage, with no form and no greeting. I couldn't tell whether I was logged in, and nothing was personalized.
- **The popups step shows nothing.** The screen only shows the promo bar scrolling a little. I can't tell whether a popup was dismissed or whether none ever appeared.
- **The promo messages contradict each other.** The banner says "Members Take 30% OFF Sitewide," but the shoe I picked says "Excluded from promotions." I wouldn't trust the email-to-site match.
- **The product doesn't suit me.** The journey landed on a colorful art-print sneaker, "Britto: S-1992," with a lace-up style and no mention of cushioning or arch support. The breadcrumb reads "Technologies > Collections > Website Premieres," which doesn't tell me anything.
- **Search results mix men's and women's shoes.** The first result is a Women's Cypress loafer. A men's shopper has to skip past it. The filter row is also cut off at the right edge, so Width isn't fully visible.
- **There's no comparison or value content.** I saw no comparison with Hoka, Brooks or New Balance, no price-versus-quality information, and no comfort ratings in any step I could see.
- **The top of the page is crowded.** There are two banners stacked above the logo. The second one scrolls sideways and cuts off words ("owns Starting at $49.99"), which is annoying to read.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page first.** A blank page needs an empty-cart message with a "Continue shopping" button, at minimum. Find out why it rendered blank.
2. **Show the size error before the tap, or scroll to the size grid.** A short line like "Select a size to continue" above the button would help. After adding, confirm it with a mini-cart or toast and update the badge.
3. **Make the login step show something.** Show a greeting like "Hi, Walker" and a points balance after login, and use them to suggest slip-ins in my usual size and width.
4. **Fix the promo conflict.** If the 30% off excludes some shoes, say so in the banner or label it on the product card. Don't show both messages to the same shopper.
5. **Put comfort first on product pages.** Show a "Slip-ins," "Arch Fit" or "Max Cushioning" badge near the title, and add a short comfort note plus "Wide available."
6. **Filter men's results when I'm in a men's session.** Or at least group the results by gender.
7. **Make the filter row wrap or scroll with a visible edge.**
8. **Add a simple comparison.** Something like "How Skechers compares for walking comfort" would speak to someone who cross-shops.

## 6. Bottom Line

I'd still browse Skechers, because the slip-in and arch-fit shortcuts are exactly what I look for. But a blank cart and no feedback after Add to Cart would send me to another brand's site. Until the cart works, the rest doesn't matter.

**Versus yesterday (3/10):** I can't list specific regressions or improvements because I don't have yesterday's screenshots or notes. Today's browsing steps all rendered properly, which supports a small bump. The blank cart keeps the score low.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

- **Coverage is limited.** The audit covers 8 steps, 18 axe violations (2 critical, 2 serious on Homepage, 2 serious on View Cart), 21 console errors (10 shown) and 1 network error.
- **No Web Vitals were captured.** LCP and CLS are `?` for every step, so Performance findings are limited to TTFB and the script inventory.
- **Several steps appear to reuse one URL.** The HTML samples for Log In and Men category both report `https://www.skechers.com/`, the same URL as Homepage. Men > Shoes, PDP and Add to Cart all show an identical TTFB of 22ms. This points to SPA or overlay transitions, or a stale navigation entry being read, rather than real document loads. Treat per-step metrics with caution.
- **View Cart appears to have been served an error response.** `/cart/` returned 429, and that page has no `<title>`, no `lang`, no `<main>` and no `<h1>`. This is consistent with a rate-limit or bot-mitigation body rather than the real cart template. That is an inference, since the cart HTML was not included.
- **The homepage loads about 30 third-party scripts**, listed under Performance.

## Accessibility

**Homepage (10 violations)**
- **Critical, `aria-required-parent`:** an element with a role that needs a parent (e.g. `listitem`, `tab` or `menuitem`) is not inside the required container.
- **Critical, `button-name`:** one or more buttons have no accessible name.
- **Serious, `link-name`:** one or more links have no accessible name.
- **Moderate, landmark structure:** `landmark-no-duplicate-main` and `landmark-main-is-top-level` together suggest a `<main>` nested inside another landmark, plus a second `<main>`. `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region` also fail.
- **Moderate, `page-has-heading-one`:** there is no `<h1>`.
- **Minor, `aria-allowed-role`:** a role is set on an element that doesn't allow it.

**Search (4 violations)**
- Moderate: `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region`.
- Minor: `aria-allowed-role`.
- The same landmark template defects appear on Homepage, which points to a shared layout component.

**View Cart (4 violations)**
- **Serious, `document-title`:** no `<title>`.
- **Serious, `html-has-lang`:** no `lang` attribute.
- **Moderate, `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one`:** no `<main>` and no `<h1>`.
- Homepage does set `<html lang="en">`, so the missing `lang` and `<title>` here is consistent with the 429 error page. Re-test with a successful cart load.

## SEO

- **No `<h1>` on Homepage** (and on View Cart).
- **Cart `<title>` is missing.** This matters little if the page is `noindex`, but the 429 response should not be cacheable or indexable.
- **Homepage `lang="en"` is present**, which is correct.
- **Canonical, meta description, structured data and hreflang could not be assessed**, because the HTML samples are truncated before `<head>` content.
- **URL did not change between steps.** The Log In and Men category samples both show `/`. If category navigation does not update the URL or history, those views aren't addressable or crawlable. Verify that real category URLs resolve on direct load.

## Performance

- **TTFB**
  - Homepage is 462ms and Log In and Men are 442ms, which is within the 800ms "good" threshold.
  - The 22–24ms values on later steps are implausibly low for a network fetch. They suggest cache hits, bfcache restores or a stale entry.
  - The 102ms for View Cart is likely the 429 response.
- **LCP and CLS are not measured.** Register the `PerformanceObserver` with `buffered: true` before navigation, or use `web-vitals` attribution, so these populate.
- **Third-party load is heavy.** Scripts seen at load include:
  - **Tag managers and analytics:** GTM and gtag (several destinations), Quantum Metric, Bloomreach (`brsrvr`) and Pulse Insights.
  - **Ad and retargeting pixels:** TikTok, Pinterest, Bing, Yahoo (`ytc.js`), RTB House (`creativecdn`), Impact (`utt.impactcdn`), Tune (`go2sdk`), Snapchat, Reddit, Facebook, Nextdoor, StackAdapt (`stapecdn`) and Taboola. Several of these were added after the homepage by the Men category step.
  - **Personalization:** Monetate.
  - Pulse Insights is the only one in the sample with SRI. The `vice-managed-src` attributes indicate consent-gated loading, which is appropriate.
- **GTM snippet carries `gtm_preview=env-1` and `gtm_auth`.** This binds the page to a specific GTM environment. Confirm that production is meant to load it and not a staging or preview container.
- **Fonts are loaded via `webfontloader` from cdnjs.** This adds a third-party dependency and a JS-driven font swap (the `wf-*` classes). `font-display` with self-hosted fonts or preloads would remove it.
- **`unload` handlers likely make pages ineligible for bfcache.** See Console & Network Errors.

## Mobile Optimization

- **Viewport meta, tap-target sizes and responsive images could not be verified**, because the source samples are truncated. axe's `target-size` rule is not part of the reported results.
- **`--vh: 6.64px` is set inline on `<html>`**, which implies a ~664px viewport and a JS-computed `vh` workaround. CSS `dvh`/`svh` units would remove the script dependency.
- The unnamed buttons and links on Homepage are probably icon controls in the mobile header or nav. That is not confirmed without selectors.

## Console & Network Errors

- **Summarizer API (4 of 10 shown):** "Unsupported Summarizer API languages were specified". Some script calls Chrome's built-in Summarizer API with unsupported language codes. Identify the caller (likely a third-party or in-house AI feature) and pass supported `outputLanguage` and `expectedInputLanguages`, or feature-detect.
- **Permissions policy violation, `unload` (6 of 10 shown):** a script registers an `unload` listener while `unload` is disallowed by Permissions-Policy. Chrome is deprecating `unload`. Third-party tags are the usual source. Replace with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`.
- **Only 10 of the 21 errors were listed**, and they cannot be attributed to specific steps.
- **`429 https://www.skechers.com/cart/`:** rate limiting or bot mitigation on a core checkout route. This is likely triggered by the automated session, but real users behind shared IPs could hit it too. Check the `Retry-After` header and whether the bot rules treat headless Chromium differently.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the audit instrumentation.** Capture LCP and CLS with buffered observers. Record the actual URL and `document.title` per step. Add a retry or backoff when a response is 429. Re-run View Cart.
2. **Resolve the critical axe issues** on Homepage:
   - **`aria-required-parent`:** correct the parent and child role structure.
   - **`button-name` and `link-name`:** add `aria-label` or visible text to the icon controls.
3. **Fix the landmark structure.** Use one top-level `<main>`, make `<aside>` elements top-level or label them, and give repeated landmarks unique `aria-label`s. This applies to the shared layout (Homepage and Search).
4. **Add an `<h1>`** to Homepage and the cart template.
5. **Make sure the cart error and 429 response** includes `<title>`, `lang` and `<main>`, and sends `Cache-Control: no-store`.
6. **Audit the third-party tag inventory.**
   - Defer or remove redundant pixels.
   - Move the rest behind consent and `requestIdleCallback`.
   - Verify the GTM environment parameters.
   - Replace `unload` listeners to restore bfcache eligibility.
7. **Fix or guard the Summarizer API call** to stop the repeated console errors.
8. **Self-host fonts** instead of loading `webfontloader` from cdnjs.
9. **Re-run with full HTML** (head, viewport meta, canonical, structured data) to assess mobile and SEO items that couldn't be verified here.
