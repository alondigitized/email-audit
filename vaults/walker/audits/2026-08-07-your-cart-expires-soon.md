# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Same three shoes as the last "you forgot something" email — the Care Bears kids' sneaker, the hot pink women's high-top, and now that white/red/blue one up top which at least looks like something a guy might wear. But two out of three items still aren't mine, so this cart tracking is either broken or scraping browsing history instead of an actual cart. The "You Liked These / And You Weren't Wrong" headline doesn't help — cute line, wrong audience for two-thirds of what's under it.
- Below the fold there's an actual sale — 25% off kids' back-to-school styles — but that's buried under a wall of "shop by category" links and app-download nagging that I'm never going to tap on my phone. I'm not clicking this one. It's the same recycled email I've seen twice this week with a coupon slapped on the bottom.

## What's working

- The top shoe (Arch Fit D'Lites Star Line) is at least a style I could see myself wearing — white with the red/blue trim looks like a proper walking shoe.
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is big, blue, and easy to find on a phone screen.
- The 25% off kids sale banner has a real number and a clear deadline (thru 8/13).
- Payment options (Afterpay, Klarna) and store finder are visible if I ever do want to buy — not that I need them.

## What's weak

- Two of three "cart" items are Care Bears kids' sneakers and a hot pink women's shoe — I never touched either one, same as the last cart email.
- This is basically a repeat of the "Your cart expires soon!" send from 8/4 and the "Did you forget something?" one — same broken cart, new subject line.
- The good sale (25% off kids) doesn't apply to me at all — no kids in this house — so the one concrete offer in the whole email is irrelevant.
- No mention of men's comfort styles, arch support, or slip-on options anywhere despite the top shoe teasing it — the email doesn't follow through on what caught my eye.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cart-tracking bug before sending another one of these — showing me kids' and women's shoes I never clicked erodes trust fast, and this is the third time in a row.
- 2. If the cart really is empty or garbage data, don't send the email — a wrong "you forgot something" is worse than no email.
- 3. Lead with a men's/comfort offer if you've got one, not a kids' back-to-school discount that doesn't apply to half your list.
- 4. Trim the category link wall (Women/Men/Kids/Clothing/New Arrivals/Sale) — on a phone that's a lot of scrolling past stuff I'll never tap.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Arch support, slip-on comfort — check out before it's gone.`

# Technical Audit — SKECHERS "Your cart expires soon!" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on the standard Attentive ESP template (BEE/Attentive click-tracking via `skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click`) and renders via responsive `@media (max-width:620px)` rules with MSO conditional fallbacks. Automated QA found a broken unsubscribe link (400), a broken product-image link (401), and a missing plain-text MIME part — the unsubscribe failure is the most severe finding as it's a CAN-SPAM compliance risk.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (401)** — product image link "Image of Men's OG Anunoby Limited Edition: SKX Nexus NYC Blue (Pre-Order) Basket" returns HTTP 401 (unauthorized) through the Attentive click-tracking redirect.
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400)** — the **Unsubscribe** link itself returns HTTP 400 (bad request). This is a functional failure, not just a compliance gap (see §5).
- **[WARN] Rate-limited (429)**, inconclusive — "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." links returned 429 during probing. These may be valid; the ESP redirect service is throttling automated checks rather than confirming brokenness.
- All tracked links route through `skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=...` (Attentive's link-wrapping/redirect service), consistent with standard ESP click-tracking. A separate open-tracking pixel is present at `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...`.
- Coverage note: 12 material links were probed and 27 were deprioritized (footer/utility/social), per QA methodology — footer/social links were not individually verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 image assets load over `http://`** instead of `https://` (host: `image.emails.skechers.com`), including the header logo, "Complete Your Order" hero image, app store badges, payment/curbside icons, and social icons. Mixed-content images are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing TLS, degrading render fidelity.
- **2 images missing `alt` text**: `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` and the open-tracking pixel at `/wf/open?upn=...`. Missing alt text on the tracking pixel is low-impact (it's 1x1/invisible), but the product image lacking alt text is a real accessibility/screen-reader gap.
- Responsive CSS is present and scoped correctly (`.mobile_hide`, `.desktop_hide`, `.stack .column` at `max-width:620px`), with row/column-specific mobile padding overrides — no structural issues found in the truncated markup.
- MSO/Outlook handling (`x-apple-data-detectors` override, `#MessageViewBody` reset) is present and standard.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, product-recommendation tokens) are visible in the truncated HTML provided. Subject line and preheader use static copy with no personalization tokens exposed. Cannot fully assess dynamic/personalized blocks (e.g., cart contents) without the untruncated source — the visible portion cuts off mid-image-block.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken (400)** — this is a functional CAN-SPAM violation risk: the mechanism must actually work, and QA confirms it currently does not resolve.
- Physical mailer address/footer content is not visible in the truncated HTML — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM postal-address requirement is met from the provided source.
- Sending domain is `msgs.skechers.com` (a dedicated subdomain), which is good practice for ESP/brand domain separation, but **SPF/DKIM/DMARC header values were not provided** in this payload — authentication cannot be verified from HTML source alone and requires raw message headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All CTA/product links are wrapped in Attentive's opaque redirect format (`/ls/click?upn=...`), not raw destination URLs, so **no UTM parameters are visible or verifiable** in the provided source — the actual `skechers.com` landing URL and its query string are only revealed after redirect resolution.
- Because destination URLs are obfuscated behind the ESP redirector, landing-page alignment (e.g., does "Complete Your Order" land on the correct cart URL) cannot be confirmed from this HTML alone; this would require following the 302 redirect chain, which is outside the current QA data (the 401/400/429 results are against the redirector, not the final page).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe link (400 error)** immediately — highest priority, direct CAN-SPAM functional risk.
2. Investigate the 401 on the Anunoby product image link — confirm whether it's an expired/misconfigured Attentive campaign link.
3. Re-probe the two 429-rate-limited links ("Complete Your Order", "You liked these...") outside of automated bulk-checking to confirm they resolve correctly.
4. Serve all image assets over `https://` — 11 assets currently load via `http://image.emails.skechers.com`, risking client-side blocking.
5. Add `alt` text to the product image (`a6ec5198-...jpg`) for accessibility.
6. Add a plain-text MIME alternative — current plain-text part is 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and accessibility for text-only clients.
