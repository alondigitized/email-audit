# There's still time! Join VIP to get 30% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've now seen this same pink Black Friday banner and the giant "30% OFF* (ALMOST) EVERYTHING" so many times since the 20th that I could recite it. "There's still time!" is a new subject line on the same pitch I got in "Last day to join VIP…" earlier today. Calling it "still time" after a "last day" email doesn't help the credibility.
- I scrolled down looking for something for me. It's mostly women's boots, loafers and heels, with a "Shop Women" button at the top and a lot of Yule-themed gifting. Some of it was men's-looking: a green Reebok-style sneaker, and the slippers and cozy boots near the bottom. The shoe pictures are small and the captions under them are tiny. I'd have to pinch-zoom on my phone to read "Early 8/F Extra 30% off."
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either, because there's no walking-shoe or comfort-brand story anywhere in it.

## What's working

- The big black "30% OFF*" on pink is easy to read from arm's length, and I know right away what the deal is.
- The FREE TOTE with a $75 purchase is a concrete extra, and it's in stores only, which I'd see as a reason to stop by.
- The "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons are a nice touch. Shop by size is useful for a guy who cares about fit.
- The buttons are big and black, easy to tap with my thumb.
- The email looks like the DSW site, so I trust it's the real thing.

## What's weak

- It's the sixth or seventh VIP pitch in three days. This is fatigue, not excitement.
- Nothing is aimed at a 62-year-old man. It's mostly women's heels and boots, and I couldn't find a men's section.
- Nothing here mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-on comfort. I see no Hoka, Brooks, New Balance or Skechers, and no comfort comparison.
- The product captions are tiny, and "Early 8/F Extra 30% off" doesn't make sense to me.
- The asterisk on "30% OFF*" and the "(almost) everything" wording tell me there's fine print. The legal text at the bottom is a wall of microscopic type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same VIP pitch every few hours. One email a day, with a different hook each time, would help most.
- 2. Send me a men's comfort email for guys like me. Lead with walking shoes and slip-ons, with an arch-support callout and the brands I cross-shop.
- 3. Make the captions bigger and say what the discount actually is, like "Now $64.99 (was $90)."
- 4. Put the actual shoe names and prices under each pair.
- 5. Say plainly what "30% off" excludes, right under the headline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort shoes, 30% off this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your VIP 30% ends Sunday at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walking shoes and slip-ons from the brands you know, plus a free tote in store.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Join VIP free and take 30% off men's cushioned sneakers and slip-ons.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source shows well-formed, table-based Braze markup with dark-mode support. QA confirms one broken CTA link, the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" button. It also found two missing-alt images and a high URL-to-text ratio. The HTML was truncated and 82 of 94 links were not probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by QA):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns HTTP 400.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/iuL-FYX0y6BvQBKPSDKw9w~~/AAQRxRA~/eEnEeuQVphsZb19M…`
  - This is a Braze tracked redirect, and the QA output shows an empty resolved target.
  - A 400 on SMS opt-in links can be a bot-probe artifact, for example a missing user-agent or `sms:` deep-link handling. It still needs a manual click test.
- **Probe coverage gap:** only 12 material links were probed and 82 were skipped. All of those were deprioritized footer, utility and social links, so they are unverified rather than known good.
- **Plain-text weight:** 37,171 of 43,751 characters (85%) in the plain-text part are URLs, because of long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking strings. This exceeds the 70% threshold and is a mild spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, image 1:** `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/VMEDj_ZccWneqCq_I8ySRA~~/AAQRxRA~/TUwmRwhD3-…`. When images are blocked, nothing describes it.
- **Missing alt text, image 2:** the tracking pixel `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif`. It should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` where applicable).
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Meta tags missing `http-equiv`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they are ignored by parsers. The charset then depends on the MIME `Content-Type` header, which is usually fine but redundant here.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `${}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The tracking pixel carries a rendered recipient ID (`mi_u=225090073`) and a campaign ID (`mi_ecmp=20261122_P260598_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_D`). The truncated source cannot show the body, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication headers:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender-side defect.
- **Unsubscribe:** the QA probe always includes the unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported for it. The truncated source cannot confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers or the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Every link is wrapped in a Braze `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, so the final destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be verified from the source. Beyond the 400 above, no landing-page mismatch was found among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" on mobile and desktop. If it really returns a 400, fix the Braze link template or the SMS opt-in destination before the next send.
2. Add `alt` text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image, and set `alt=""` on the `o.gif` pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge" />`.
5. Shorten the plain-text part by adding real readable copy, or use shorter link-tracking domains and paths.
6. Re-run the audit with full HTML, raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`) and the footer links probed, so the compliance and UTM sections can be settled.
