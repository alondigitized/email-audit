# Go WILD: $15 off 🦁🐍🐆
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've had a pile of these since the 8th, so my first thought was "what's the code this time?" It's CIDER now, not HAYRIDE. The top is a green countdown banner and an orange tier offer: $15 off $49, $20 off $99, $60 off $199. That part is easy to read. Then the email turns into a fashion spread of animal-print boots, leopard handbags and a woman in a red coat. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for a cushioned slip-on, so none of it is aimed at me.
- The men's link is there, but it's one small text link in the top banner. The "Shop Wide Width" and sneakers bits are the only things that looked close to my feet. The UGG banner is at least warm and cushy, but it's tiny and sits near the bottom. I'd skim it, maybe tap "Shop Men" out of habit, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. Hard to say whether I'd click at all.

## What's working

- The tiered offer ($15/$20/$60 off) is big, bold and readable on my phone without zooming.
- The countdown timer gives a real deadline, which beats the vague "ends soon" in the earlier sends.
- The "Shop by Size" block with Wide Width is useful. I do care about fit.
- The "Claim $15 Off" button is a clear black rectangle and easy to tap.
- The code (CIDER) is stated plainly, and there's a barcode for in-store use.

## What's weak

- The whole hero is women's animal-print boots. Nothing there speaks to a man my age.
- Men's shopping is one small underlined link in the orange banner. Nothing is built for me.
- No comfort, cushioning or arch-support talk anywhere. For a comfort-first buyer, that's the whole reason I'd shop.
- This is about the dozenth DSW send this week with a different code. It feels like noise, not a relationship.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense, and the offer has exclusions. "$60 off $199" with an asterisk makes me doubt the deal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and age. Send men like me a hero with cushioned walking shoes or slip-ons, not leopard boots.
- 2. Put "Shop Men" as a real button, not a text link.
- 3. Add a comfort row: "Cushioned. Slip-on. Arch support." with sneaker and loafer picks.
- 4. Cut the cadence. One strong send beats five reminders about the same code.
- 5. Move the UGG block higher, or drop it. The warm-and-fuzzy angle suits me better than the animal print does.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent message (`ablink.dsw.com` click tracking, `braze-images.com` assets). The QA run found one failing CTA link (HTTP 400) and a few minor markup gaps. Only part of the HTML was available, and no headers were provided, so several compliance and continuity checks can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned HTTP 400.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/3e1VGXCwWThCpWz6mtfyOA~~/AAQRxRA~/rqGOpD4v…Ig1q7HWy3ztAIa2EL6DtWxgfj57w~`
  - It is a Braze-wrapped redirect. The 400 may come from the tracker rejecting the probe request, or from a destination that needs a mobile or SMS context. Either way it should be verified manually, because this is the SMS-acquisition CTA.
  - The QA result lists the link text but an empty label before it (`Broken link (400):  (link text: …)`). That may mean the anchor has no accessible name apart from the image or text inside it. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 59 skipped, so the other links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261012_P261435_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) and campaign ID (`mi_ecmp`). It works as designed, but it adds a second tracking domain. It also has no alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - the `ablink.dsw.com/q/sSvEtCGF5UBK7QI0JAWUlQ~~/…` image (an open or tracking pixel wrapped by Braze)
  - `mi.dsw.com/…/o.gif`
  - `braze-images.com/…/6abbd79fddb208008a198912/original.png?179069532`
  - The two pixels should have `alt=""`. The `original.png` image may be content and needs a real description if it is.
- **Meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. Both are minor, since the charset is also set by the Content-Type header.
- **Language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is generic and doesn't reflect the subject line. This is minor.
- **Fonts:** Futura PT and Helvetica Now load through `@font-face` from `braze-images.com`. Most clients (Gmail, Outlook) ignore web fonts, so fallbacks matter. The font-family fallback stacks were not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens were visible in the available HTML. The `mi_u` value in the pixel URL is populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part (WARN):** 21,994 of 26,343 characters (about 83%) are URLs. This is a known spam-filter signal. The cause is long Braze-wrapped links, for example the ~600-character `ablink` URL above.
- **Unsubscribe link:** The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no unsubscribe failure was reported. The footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm its presence or the postal address.
- **Authentication headers:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` were not supplied. I can't assess them. The From domain is `e.dsw.com` and the tracking domain is `ablink.dsw.com`, which is a sensible alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are wrapped in Braze `ablink` redirects, so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters aren't visible in the source or QA output. The only readable campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261012_P261435_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` on the pixel. Landing-page alignment can't be checked because the redirect targets weren't resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually follow the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link on desktop and mobile. If it is really broken, fix the Braze link target. If the 400 comes only from the probe, document it so QA stops flagging it.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to `original.png` if it carries content.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Fix the meta tags to `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
4. Shorten the plain-text version, for example by using shorter link-tracking aliases or fewer repeated links, to bring the URL ratio down.
5. Re-run the audit with full HTML, the raw headers, and resolved redirect destinations. That would cover the unsubscribe and footer checks, authentication, and UTM and landing-page continuity.
