# UGG SEASON IS HERE!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this is the fifth email I've had in about two days. The 25% VIP Days banner and the "Extra 10% off clearance" code GOLDEN are both gone, and now it's UGG slippers and boots. The yellow frame and the big UGG logo are easy to see on my phone. But there's no price anywhere up top. I scrolled the whole thing looking for a deal and only found "15% off" for texting sign-ups, down in the small icons.
- The photos are mostly young women in plaid skirts and leg warmers. "Warm and fuzzies" sounds like a slipper, and a cushioned slip-on is right up my alley. But there's not one man in it, and the buttons say "SHOP UGG" and "SHOP ALL WOMEN." Wide Width and Wide Calf are there, which is nice. The size row only runs 5 to 11, so it's a women's chart, and I wear bigger than that.
- Would I click? Probably not. I might forward it to my wife, but I'd mark it read. If they want me to look at a pair of slippers, they should show me a men's slipper with a price on it.

## What's working

- The big UGG logo and the "warm and fuzzies" line told me right away what this was. I didn't have to guess.
- The "SHOP UGG" and "SHOP ALL WOMEN" buttons are big, black and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The sizes and the Wide Width button are right on the email. I like seeing fit information up front.
- The "Shopping Made Easy" row (free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup, app) is clear and the icons are readable.
- The new look is a nice change from the same purple banner all week, and it matches the fall season.

## What's weak

- I couldn't find an offer in the hero. After the "25% off" banners earlier this week, a no-price email feels like a step backward.
- It's all women's models and women's styles. There's no men's slipper and no men's button, so it doesn't feel meant for me.
- The 15% off for texting is buried near the bottom, and the fine print is tiny.
- It's the fifth DSW email in two days, and the subject line is shouting. That's tiring.
- The size row only goes 5 to 11. I'd have to go looking to find out if they carry a men's 11 or 12.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer in the hero, like "$X off UGG" or a VIP bonus points number, and put the same thing in the subject line. Right now the preheader hints at Bonus Points but the body never explains them.
- 2. Add a men's row. A men's UGG or Hoka-type slipper with a price would speak to guys like me, and "SHOP MEN'S" should be a button.
- 3. Show prices or "from $" under the products, and a star rating if they have one.
- 4. Cut the frequency, or at least vary the message. Five sends in two days trains people like me to ignore the sender.
- 5. Show men's sizes (7–14) or make the size selector change with the category.

# Technical Audit: DSW, "UGG SEASON IS HERE!"

## 1. Technical Summary
One confirmed defect: the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" tracked link returned HTTP 400 (QA pass rate 75%). The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and header checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400**. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/YISt776vPnAp2frhAA-UmA~~/AAQRxRA~/Y4BBYJK3ZW5W5pCJ...`.
  - It is a Braze-wrapped redirect with a roughly 700-character opaque token.
  - A 400 from the redirector may mean the token is malformed or expired. It could also mean the endpoint rejects the QA prober, or that the target is an SMS opt-in flow that rejects non-mobile clients. QA data alone cannot tell these apart.
  - Either way, the link failed for the checker. It is also the email's SMS-acquisition CTA, so a real failure would cost conversions.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 links were probed and 39 were skipped, as footer, social and utility links were deprioritized. Other broken links may exist.
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261002_P261387_V1_W-_Trend_Trend` is a Movable Ink pixel. It loads from a separate third-party domain from the Braze link domain (`ablink.dsw.com`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/C5Vd9KU0ZH4ndm81IBwZTA~~/...`
  - `o.gif` (the Movable Ink pixel). It should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech.
  - The first is a tracked image and may be content. If so, it is a real accessibility gap.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset then relies on the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **Dark mode:** The CSS has a `prefers-color-scheme` block and `color-scheme` meta tags. The source was cut off mid-block, so I could not assess it fully.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The tail of the HTML was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it. I could not see the footer, so I cannot confirm the link is present or that a physical address is included.
- **Plain-text part (WARN):** 17,707 of 20,597 characters (about 86%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The long Braze-wrapped tracking URLs cause this, and it is a spam-filter risk.
- **Authentication headers:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` were not provided, so none can be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every link is wrapped in a Braze redirect (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/...`), so the final destination and any UTM parameters cannot be seen in the source or QA data.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261002_P261387_V1_W-_Trend_Trend`, which is a Movable Ink parameter, not a UTM.
- Landing-page alignment could not be assessed because the redirect targets were not resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or replace the SMS opt-in link.** Re-test it from a mobile user agent. If it returns 400 there too, regenerate the Braze link or point it to a direct opt-in URL. This is the highest priority.
2. **Add `alt` text.** Use a descriptive value for the `ablink.dsw.com/q/...` image. Use `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`** to the two meta tags.
5. **Shorten the plain-text version.** Use a custom short-link domain or fewer wrapped links so URLs fall below 70% of the text.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML.** Include the raw headers and resolve the redirect chains, so unsubscribe, authentication and UTM checks can be completed.
