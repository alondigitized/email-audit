# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- The pink flower sneaker is back. It's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom," a women's shoe, and it fills the top of the screen again. I'm a 62-year-old man and I never put it in a cart. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" is simply wrong for me. This is the same subject as the 10/3 send, and it comes right after "Your Items Are Waiting!" on 10/4. By now it reads like a nag, not a reminder.
- There is one bright spot. Down in the navy banner, Skechers Plus members get Buy One, Get One 50% off, or 20% off a single pair, through 10/7. That's a real offer, and I'd have liked it at the top. The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman in the grid is the only shoe that looks like mine. I might tap it, but I wouldn't tap "Complete Your Order," because that button belongs to the flower shoe.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the Altman, and not forward it. A friend who wants hands-free comfort shoes would get nothing out of it.

## What's working

- The navy BOGO 50% off banner for Skechers Plus members is clear, and "Thru 10/7" gives it a real deadline.
- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman is in the grid. Slip-on is exactly what I wear.
- "Complete Your Order" is a plain verb-and-noun button in a big dark box, easy to tap on a phone.
- The Skechers logo and the layout match what I expect from them, so I trust who sent it.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, Sale) is big and easy to tap, and I can read it without zooming.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's floral sneaker I never added to a cart. It makes the "you liked these" line feel wrong, and it's the same shoe I've been seeing since 9/17.
- The BOGO offer is buried below the shoe grid. The top of the email gives me no reason to care.
- Four of the six grid shoes are kids' or women's styles. That tells me they don't know who I am.
- There's nothing about arch support or cushioning, and no reviews or price math. I compare comfort brands, so I'd want those.
- The fine print excludes sale styles, apparel and more. That takes some shine off the 50% off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop showing a cart item that someone else's browsing created. Use what Walker actually looked at, like the men's slip-ins, or skip the cart nag.
- 2. Move the member BOGO banner above the hero. Put the offer first and the shoe second.
- 3. Add one comfort line under each shoe, such as "Arch Fit, Air-Cooled Memory Foam," plus a star rating.
- 4. Lead the grid with men's slip-ins for known men's shoppers.
- 5. Pull back on cadence. Don't send a third cart reminder in three days.
