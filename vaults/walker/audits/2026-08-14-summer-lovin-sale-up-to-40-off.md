# Summer Lovin' Sale, Up to 40% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one's a nice change of pace — no more of that pink floral sneaker getting shoved at me for the fourth day running. "Summer Lovin' Sale, Up to 40% Off" is a real offer with a real deadline (thru 8/19), and there's an actual men's shoe pictured, not just gals' sandals. That said, the hero photos are a couple in their twenties lounging poolside in Santorini or wherever — that's not who I am, and nothing here mentions cushioning, arch support, or slip-on comfort, which is the whole reason I even open Skechers emails.
- The good news is the layout's clean and I can find "SHOP MEN'S" without hunting. The bad news is it's just another generic site-wide percent-off blast — could be selling flip-flops to my grandkids for all the personalization in it. I'd probably tap "SHOP MEN'S" out of habit more than genuine excitement, but I wouldn't forward this to anybody. It's fine, not memorable.

## What's working

- Clear offer up top — "Up to 40% Off Summer Styles" with a real end date (8/19) instead of vague urgency.
- Big blue "SHOP NOW" button right under the headline, easy to hit with a thumb.
- Dedicated "SHOP MEN'S" section with its own product shot and button — I don't have to dig through women's stuff first.
- No repeat of that pink floral shoe nonsense that's been clogging my inbox all week — feels like a fresh creative for once.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup options listed, which tells me they're at least thinking about how real people shop.

## What's weak

- Hero models are a young couple by a pool — doesn't look like anyone my age, no comfort/arch-support angle at all in the copy.
- The men's shoe shown looks like a boat-style slip-on with a chunky sole, but there's zero mention of cushioning, arch support, or all-day comfort — the stuff that actually sells me.
- No loyalty/points callout even though I've got 1000 points sitting in my account per their last email — feels like a missed "member gets extra X%" moment.
- It's a generic site-wide sale email, not targeted to me specifically — nothing here says "based on what you looked at" or "for guys who want comfort."

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero lifestyle photo for someone closer to my age, and add a line under the offer like "All-day comfort, up to 40% off" — connect the discount to the comfort pitch, not just fashion.
- 2. Add a loyalty tie-in: "Skechers Plus members get an extra 10% — you've got 1000 points to use."
- 3. Put a comfort/arch-support callout badge on the men's shoe image itself (e.g., "Arch Fit® cushioning") so I know before I click.
- 4. Trim "SHOP ADDITIONAL STYLES" down — six stacked category rows plus three product blocks is a lot of scrolling for a phone.
