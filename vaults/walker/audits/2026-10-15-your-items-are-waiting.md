# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same cart nag again, and the pink flower sneaker is back. I never put the D'Lites "Spring Bloom" in a cart. It's a women's shoe, and it sits at the top under "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US...". That line is a little creepy, and it's the same headline as the "Your Items Are Waiting!" send on 10/14. That makes at least five cart-reminder emails in three days, along with "Did you forget something?" and "Your cart expires soon!". After that, a cart reminder feels like nagging, not service.
- The better stuff is further down. The "Members take 20% off select full-priced styles" banner is clear and has a deadline. There's a John Deere waterproof Slip-in boot, a slip-on, hands-free style I'd actually look at. But it's buried in the third row, on my phone screen, after a lot of scrolling past kids' sneakers.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. My buddy would ask why I'm getting a flower shoe email.

## What's working

- The 20% off members banner is big, plain and easy to read. It says "Valid thru 10/21," so I know the deadline.
- "Complete Your Order" is a clear navy button, easy to tap with a thumb.
- The John Deere waterproof Slip-in is a men's, hands-free, comfort-type shoe. That's the first thing in here that looks like me.
- The Women / Men / Kids / Clothing / New Arrivals / Sale list is big, simple and easy to read on a phone.
- The Skechers logo and navy headline look like the Skechers I know, so I trust it's real.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's floral sneaker I never touched. It's the same one as 10/14, and it doesn't fit me at all.
- "We noticed you noticing us..." feels like being watched, not helped.
- The 20% off offer is below the fold, well under the hero. The email leads with the nag, not the deal.
- Only one of the six "more great options" is a men's shoe. Two are boys' sneakers, and the rest are women's. Nothing says arch support, cushioning or walking comfort.
- The fine print is tiny. It says "select full-priced styles" and lists exclusions, so I can't tell whether the 20% applies to what I'd want.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending a hero product the customer never carted. Lead with the men's slip-on I browsed, or the John Deere Slip-in, and move the 20% member banner up under the hero.
- 2. Cap cart reminders at one or two, spaced out. Five in three days wears people out.
- 3. Drop the "noticing us" language for something friendly, like "Still thinking about these?"
- 4. Add comfort cues under the products: "Arch Fit," "hands-free," and "waterproof."
- 5. Put the real deal in the headline: "20% off through 10/21." Skip the stacked-exclusions look.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still thinking it over? 20% off thru 10/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Slip-ins are waiting, Walker`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on select styles until 10/21. Easy on, no bending.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart is saved. Arch Fit comfort, now 20% off for members.`
