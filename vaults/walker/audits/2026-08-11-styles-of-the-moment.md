# Styles of the moment
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Well, HOKA's at it again — that's five, six times in a couple weeks now, and it's still the Bondi 9 in some loud green front and center. "Best sellers for a reason" is a fine enough hook, and at least the Bondi's cushioning talk is aimed at something I actually care about. But then it swings right into "Cleared for takeoff" with a men's trail shoe (Stinson 8) and a whole UTMB Mont-Blanc ultra-running section, and I'm back to being an afterthought in my own inbox.
- The good news: Clifton PRO shows up again in the "Men" row, so there is something here for a guy who just walks the dog and needs arch support that doesn't quit. The bad news: I have to hunt for it in a four-tile grid mostly stacked with women's colors and mountain-race stuff.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the Clifton PRO tile if I'm bored, but I'm not forwarding this to anybody and I'm not clicking "Shop Best Sellers" as the main CTA — too generic, too much noise around it.

## What's working

- Big clear "Best sellers for a reason" headline — I know what I'm looking at right away.
- Clifton PRO in the men's row is a shoe I actually recognize from past HOKA emails and it's the one built for daily walking, which is my whole deal.
- Cushioning callout copy ("HOKA signature cushioning... confidence to fly further") speaks directly to comfort, which is the thing I shop on.
- Free Expedited Shipping banner at the bottom is a real, concrete offer — I noticed that immediately.

## What's weak

- Bondi 9 hero is in "Tart Apple" — that neon green — same color-forward gimmick as the last two HOKA sends I got. I'm numb to it at this point.
- Stinson 8 and the whole UTMB Mont-Blanc trail-running section are dead weight for me — I'm not running an ultra through the Alps, I'm walking to the mailbox.
- Two of the four "Cleared for takeoff" tiles are women's shoes (Arahi 8, Bondi 9) — fine for the catalog, but it dilutes my scan when I'm trying to find "my" shoe fast.
- The shipping offer is buried under a text-signup ask, not a straight discount — feels like a bait to get my phone number more than a real deal.
- No price shown anywhere, no member/loyalty pricing — just "shop" and hope.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with the Clifton PRO, not the neon Bondi — that's the shoe I keep circling back to, and HOKA already knows it from six weeks of showing it to me.
- 2. Cut the UTMB trail-race block entirely for a segment that's never clicked run-specific content — swap it for a second walking/casual shoe or a fit-guide callout.
- 3. Put actual numbers on the shipping offer — "Free Expedited Shipping" with no dollar threshold or timeframe reads soft; give me a deadline.
- 4. Reorder the grid so men's styles lead — I shouldn't have to scroll past two women's shoes to find mine.
- 5. Vary the color story — three sends running with the same lime/neon Bondi is starting to feel like there's nothing else in the line.
