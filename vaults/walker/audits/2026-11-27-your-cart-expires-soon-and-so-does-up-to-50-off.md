# Your cart expires soon! (and so does up to 50% off)
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is my 13th or so nag since 11/20, and I don't have a cart. "Your cart expires soon" is the same line in a new coat, and I'm tired of it. Once I opened it, though, it was one of the better ones. The red-on-black 30-50% OFF SITEWIDE banner tells me the deal in a second, and the big slip-in walking shoe underneath is the kind of thing I actually wear.
- The "YOU LIKED THESE" headline got an eye-roll, because I never liked those. The grey Glide-Step with the cushy sole did catch my eye, and the "Complete Your Order" button is easy to find. The text is big enough for my phone and the buttons are wide.
- Would I click? Probably yes, for the Slip-ins. Would I forward it? No. It's a fine sale email dressed up as a cart reminder, and the cart part makes me trust it a little less.

## What's working

- The black and red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner at the top is readable without squinting, and I got the deal in about two seconds.
- The hero is a Slip-ins GO WALK Glide-Step with a thick cushioned sole. That is hands-free comfort, which is my whole thing.
- The "Complete Your Order" button is navy, wide and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The red Skechers Plus box offering an extra 15% off stands out, and it's honest about being a member perk.
- The men's Slip-ins row, with the Relaxed Fit Murette loafer and the Waterproof boots, is closer to what I'd shop for.

## What's weak

- I have no cart. "Your cart expires soon" and "You liked these" are claims that aren't true for me, and that's the same complaint I made about the 11/27 "Did you forget something?" send.
- Half of the "more great options" grid is women's shoes. The Arch Fit Arcade sneakers aren't for me, and nothing there tells me which pairs are the cushioned walkers.
- There are no prices, no ratings and no widths. I'm told "30-50% off" but can't do the price-versus-comfort math I do against Hoka and Brooks.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and long. The 30% / 40% / 50% tiers depend on the category, so the headline "up to 50%" feels like a bait setup. Boots and slippers are 40%, and the 50% is on select apparel.
- The footer is crowded with Afterpay, Klarna, the app, a text-signup and a store finder. It feels like a lot of clutter after the shoes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the cart items, or stop calling it a cart. If nothing was left in a cart, send a plain sale email with the real deal as the headline. That alone would fix most of my irritation.
- 2. Show the actual price and the sale price under each shoe, plus a one-line comfort cue such as "Arch Fit" or "Slip-ins, no bending."
- 3. Split the grid by Men's and Women's, or lead with the Men's Slip-ins row if you know the reader is male.
- 4. Put the "50% off" tier next to what it actually covers.
- 5. Trim the footer to one row, and make the unsubscribe link bigger.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Slip-ins you looked at are 30-50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cyber Week ends 12/1: comfy Slip-ins on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 15% off for Skechers Plus members. No tying, no bending.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive Attentive/Bee-style build with `lang`, a viewport meta and a preheader. Its main problems are a failing unsubscribe probe, no plain-text part, 12 images served over `http://`, and an empty `<title>` with a malformed charset meta. The HTML source is truncated, so footer markup and headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe returned HTTP 400** (QA FAIL):
  - URL: `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…&attn_sid=b22943ea-…`.
  - The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, so this is the intended unsubscribe endpoint.
  - The 400 may come from the probe (a bot request with no session, or a one-time token). The QA data can't tell us which, so verify manually. If it is real, the unsubscribe flow is broken.
- **Two CTAs returned 429 and are inconclusive, not broken:**
  - "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." both point to `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`.
  - The 429s reflect rate limiting by the redirector, so the destination wasn't resolved.
- **Probe coverage was partial:** 12 links were probed and 32 skipped, so most links are unverified.
- **Tracked links are opaque:** every `attentivemail.com/e/c/…` link is an encoded redirect. The final destination can't be confirmed from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images use `http://`** (QA WARN), all on `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`:
  - Affected images: Skechers logo, "Complete Your Order", `a6ec5198-…jpg`, "Text", App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook, Youtube.
  - Mixed content can be blocked or rewritten by some clients and proxies.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` has no alt. It sits in the same module as the "Complete Your Order" image, so it is likely a content image.
  - The Attentive open pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`) also has no alt. This is low impact because it is a tracking pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Malformed charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so the browser ignores it. The preheader contains non-ASCII padding characters, so a valid declaration matters.
- **No issues found with:**
  - the `lang="en"` attribute;
  - the viewport meta;
  - the 620px mobile media query;
  - the `mso-` table resets.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FirstName]`. The cart contents behind "Complete Your Order" can't be verified from the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part is missing** (QA FAIL, 0 chars). This is a deliverability and accessibility gap, and spam filters often penalize it.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown:** no `Authentication-Results` header was found. The report attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a test-harness limitation, not a sender fault. Check it on a direct-delivery copy.
- **Unsubscribe link exists** (text "Unsubscribe") but failed the probe, as described in section 2.
- **Not verifiable:**
  - the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, because headers weren't provided;
  - the physical postal address, because the footer is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.** The CTAs are wrapped in Attentive redirects, and the probes returned 429 or were skipped.
- **Several domains are in use:**
  - sender `msgs.skechers.com`;
  - tracking `skechers.e.attentivemail.com`;
  - images `image.emails.skechers.com`;
  - unsubscribe `skechers.attn.tv`.

  This is typical of the platform and isn't flagged as an error. It does mean image and click domains need to stay on the allow-list.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually load the unsubscribe URL in a real browser. If it still returns 400, fix the Attentive unsubscribe configuration. This is a CAN-SPAM risk.
2. Add a plain-text alternative part. The current one is 0 chars.
3. Serve all images over HTTPS. Switch `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to `https://`.
4. Add `alt` text to `a6ec5198-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to the open pixel.
5. Fix the head by populating `<title>` and changing the meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
6. Re-run the QA with throttled probing to resolve the two 429 CTAs and extend coverage past 12 of 44 links.
7. Capture the raw headers to confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and `List-Unsubscribe` one-click support.
