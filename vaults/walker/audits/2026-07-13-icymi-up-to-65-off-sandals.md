# ICYMI: Up to 65% off sandals 🩴👡
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Twelve. By my count, this is the twelfth time DSW has hit my inbox this week — and I say that as a man who actually likes DSW, or at least tolerated them until Monday. This one's the "ICYMI" version of the same sandals campaign they blasted me with earlier today, which is their way of saying "we know you didn't open the last one." They're right, I didn't — but not because I missed it. I skipped it because it was women's sandals, same as this.
- I open the email and I'm staring at a young woman with big curly hair holding platform sandals, surrounded by strappy heels and flip-flops. The CTA button literally says "SHOP SANDALS FOR WOMEN." Not even a token nod to my side of the store. The offer is real — up to 65% off, starting at $39.99, clean layout, no broken images — but it's aimed squarely at a 30-something woman who wants summer vibes. I'm a 62-year-old man who wants arch support.
- I'd mark this as read and move on. Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward. If anything I'm starting to wonder if I accidentally signed up for a women's shoe newsletter.

## What's working

- **The offer is concrete and credible.** "Up to 65% off, starting at $39.99" — that's a real number I can evaluate, not a vague "save big" promise.
- **Clean layout, no render bugs.** Text is readable, images loaded, nothing overlaps. DSW's email production is solid.
- **The ICYMI framing at least acknowledges the cadence** — they know they're re-serving and they're owning it, which is more honest than pretending it's a fresh send.
- **Price floor gives me an anchor.** $39.99 tells me this isn't a clearance-for-a-reason situation — that's a real sale on real product.

## What's weak

- **Every visual signal says "not for you, Walker."** Young female model, platform sandals, strappy heels, flip-flops — nothing that looks like a walking shoe, a slip-on, or anything I'd put on my foot.
- **"SHOP SANDALS FOR WOMEN" is the primary CTA.** DSW knows my gender — I've bought men's shoes there. There's zero reason that button shouldn't say something that includes me.
- **The size grid runs 7–10.5** — that's women's sizing in this context. No men's size chart, no men's category link anywhere I can see.
- **ICYMI as an opener is tired jargon for my generation.** I had to think about what it means, and I'm not interested in decoding acronyms to find a deal.
- **Twelfth email in a week.** At this point the cadence itself is the problem — no offer is urgent when the brand cries wolf twelve times in seven days.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment by gender before sending.** This is the single biggest lift available to DSW right now. I have a purchase history. They know I'm a man. There is no excuse for a "Women's sandals" hero and a "Shop Sandals for Women" CTA landing in my inbox.
- 2. **Drop the ICYMI framing — and fix the cadence.** Sending a "you missed it" re-send three hours after the original send isn't urgency, it's noise. Space sends out, or at minimum suppress for recent openers.
- 3. **If you must re-send this campaign, A/B a men's version.** Same 65% off hook, different hero — a men's slide, a walking sandal, something with a footbed. One swap, massive relevance gain.
- 4. **Add a loyalty callout.** DSW Rewards exists. I have points. Show me my balance or tell me these sandals earn bonus rewards — that would at least give me a reason to look even if I'm not the target.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's sale: up to 65% off sandals & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `65% off summer shoes — your size is in stock`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walking sandals from $39.99 — arch support included`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's slides, walkers, slip-ons — all on sale now`
