- **Charset declaration ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Clients that rely on it may mis-detect encoding.

1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part. The QA check requires more than a trivial length.
