## Walker Miles — Skechers.com Mobile Journey Review
**Date:** July 7, 2026 | **Yesterday's score:** 4/10

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Another frustrating trip. The navigation bones are solid — Skechers clearly knows guys like me want Slip-ins and wide fit up front — but the experience falls apart in exactly the moments that matter: logging in doesn't appear to have done anything, the cart page came up completely blank (a white screen, nothing there), and a search for "comfort shoes" served me Women's results first. I came in ready to buy a $130 shoe and walked away empty-handed. That's not a minor UX quibble, that's a lost sale.

---

## 2. Business Impact Score: 4/10

No change from yesterday. The cart failure is a critical regression or an unresolved carry-over. Search personalization is still broken for gender. Login state is invisible. The good navigation work in the Men's drawer is wasted if the bottom of the funnel is broken.

---

## 3. What's Working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner is persistent and clear.** I saw it on every screen. That's a deal I notice.
- **Men's nav drawer is well-structured.** "Comfort Technologies," "Hands Free Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," and "Extra Wide Fit" are all one tap away after opening the menu. That's exactly what I'm looking for — someone at Skechers organized this for people like me.
- **Product page text is readable.** "Skechers Slip-ins: Arch Fit D'Lites 330" — the name, price ($130), and the Slip-ins badge are all clear without zooming.
- **Size grid on Add to Cart is excellent.** Large tap targets, full size run visible (7.0–14.0), "Please select a size" error is clear and prominent in red. Clean, no confusion.
- **"Ship it" and "Check in-store availability" options are visible** on the add-to-cart screen. I like having the store option — I might want to try them on first.
- **Breadcrumb (Men > Shoes)** is present on the category page. Helps me know where I am.

---

## 4. What's Weak

**CRITICAL — Cart page is a blank white screen.** Step 8 rendered nothing. No items, no total, no checkout button. If I'd actually tapped through to buy, I would have thought the site was broken and left. This is the same failure point as yesterday — it hasn't been fixed.

**Login produced no visible change.** Steps 1, 2, and 3 all show the identical homepage screenshot. I can't tell if I'm logged in. There's no "Welcome back, Walker" or name in the header. The account icon looks the same. If my loyalty points and saved address aren't loaded, what was the point of logging in?

**Search "comfort shoes" returned Women's products first.** 1,425 results — fine, plenty of inventory — but the first two cards I see are labeled "Women's." I'm a 62-year-old man who just navigated through the Men's section. Even if I'm not logged in as a recognized user, the search has no memory of my session context. That's a jarring disconnect.

**Hero image skews young.** The homepage hero shows two people who look about 25, wearing streetwear, doing something athletic outside a Skechers building. I don't see myself there. Nothing on the homepage first screen signals "this brand is for someone my age who needs comfort."

**Product has only 7 reviews and 3 stars.** That's not confidence-inspiring at $130. And the red "Excluded from promotions" warning right below the price stings — I just saw a BOGO 50% OFF banner at the top of every page, and now I'm being told this shoe doesn't qualify. That feels like a bait-and-switch.

**No cushioning or arch support details above the fold on the PDP.** I see the name, price, and a photo. I can't see foam specs, arch support language, or what makes this shoe worth $130 without scrolling. That information is why I'm here.

**No popup to dismiss.** Step 2 was supposed to dismiss a popup — nothing appeared. Either the popup never fired or it was already gone. Minor, but worth noting — if an email promo triggered a popup for a logged-in user, I missed it entirely.

---

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page immediately.** A blank cart is a total conversion killer. Whatever the rendering issue is, this should be P0.
2. **Show login confirmation in the header.** Even just "Hi, Walker" or a filled-in account icon tells me the login worked and my data is loaded.
3. **Filter search results by session gender context.** If I came from Men's navigation, default search results to Men's. Show a "Showing Men's results" label I can override.
4. **Add comfort callouts above the fold on the PDP.** One line — "Memory foam insole · Arch Fit support · Hands-free entry" — before the price would do it. That's what justifies $130 to me.
5. **Promote alternatives or BOGO-eligible swaps on excluded products.** If this shoe is excluded, show me one that isn't. Don't just dead-end me with a warning.
6. **Swap the hero image on a rotation** that includes someone 55+ walking comfortably. I need to see myself in that shoe.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

Site runs on **Salesforce Commerce Cloud** (Demandware). Key infrastructure: PerimeterX/HUMAN Security bot detection (KPSDK), TrustArc consent management (`truste_*`, `vice-managed-src`), Monetate A/B, Bloomreach search, Quantum Metric session recording, Stylitics outfit widget. The journey reveals three actionable failure classes: a broken cart page render, active bot-protection rate limiting hitting real users, and a CORS-blocked third-party asset.

---

## Accessibility

**16 violations across 3 steps.**

**View Cart — 4 serious/moderate violations (highest severity):**
- `document-title` [serious]: `<title>` element is empty or absent. Screen readers announce the tab as untitled; search engines may index a blank title. Homepage HTML confirms `lang="en"` is set there, so this is a cart-specific render failure, not a global template issue.
- `html-has-lang` [serious]: `lang` attribute missing on `<html>`. Contradicts Homepage source which has `lang="en"` — indicates the cart page is served from a different template path or a JS-driven shell that strips the attribute.
- `landmark-one-main` [moderate]: No `<main>` landmark. Cart content is inaccessible by landmark navigation.
- `page-has-heading-one` [moderate]: No `<h1>`. Heading outline is broken for AT users.

**Homepage — 8 violations:**
- `aria-required-parent` [critical]: ARIA role used on element outside its required parent container. Likely a carousel or list widget where `role="option"` or `role="tab"` exists outside `role="listbox"`/`role="tablist"`.
- `landmark-no-duplicate-main` + `landmark-main-is-top-level` [moderate]: Multiple `<main>` elements or `<main>` nested inside another landmark. Common in SPA shells that inject a second `<main>` via JS.
- `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` [moderate]: `<aside>` nested inside `<main>` or another sectioning element. Repeated on Search page.
- `heading-order` [moderate]: Heading levels skip (e.g., `<h1>` → `<h3>`).
- `landmark-unique` + `region` [moderate]: Duplicate landmark roles without distinguishing labels; content outside any landmark region. Repeated on Search page.
- `aria-allowed-role` [minor]: `role` attribute value invalid for its host element (e.g., `role="listitem"` on a `<div>` that is not inside `role="list"`). Repeated on Search page.

## Mobile Optimization

- **`--vh` custom property** set via JS (`--vh: 6.640000000000001px`): standard mobile fix, but the floating-point precision (`6.640000000000001` vs `6.64`) suggests direct `window.innerHeight / 100` without rounding — cosmetically harmless but indicates the shim is unpolished.
- **`device_type=desktop`** in PulseInsights survey URL parameters despite this being a mobile audit context — survey tool is misclassifying device type, which would serve wrong survey variants and pollute analytics segmentation.
- No viewport meta tag visible in provided source excerpts (may be present but truncated). Should verify `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">` is present.
- TrustArc consent banner CSS injects inline `<style>` blocks in `<head>` — minor render-blocking risk on slow connections.

## Console & Network Errors

**429 — PerimeterX/HUMAN Security fingerprint endpoint (6 hits):**
```
/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/2d206a39-8ed7-437e-a3be-862e0f06eea3/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.522
```
KPSDK is the HUMAN Security bot detection script. Repeated 429s mean the bot protection layer is rate-limiting its own fingerprint collection calls — likely triggered by automated test traffic (Playwright/headless Chrome), but if reproducible in real mobile sessions, this indicates a misconfigured rate limit on the sensor endpoint. The sensor failing silently degrades bot detection coverage.

**429 — Cart endpoint (1 hit):**
```
429 https://www.skechers.com/cart/
```
The cart page itself returned 429 during the audit. This is a direct revenue impact: authenticated users attempting to view cart would receive an error. Likely the same bot-protection misconfiguration as above.

**403 — PulseInsights survey (3 hits):**
```
403 https://survey.pulseinsights.com/serve?udid=...&device_type=desktop&identifier=PI-89866762
```
API key `PI-89866762` is returning 403 — either the key is domain/origin restricted and the test environment doesn't match, or the key is inactive. Survey widget silently fails; non-blocking but wastes a network request on every page.

**CORS block — Stylitics CSS (1 hit):**
```
Access to CSS stylesheet at 'https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css?base=grid&theme=skechers...' blocked by CORS policy: No 'Access-Control-Allow-Origin' header
```
Stylitics's CDN is not returning `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://www.skechers.com` for this stylesheet. The outfit/shop-the-look widget will render unstyled or broken. This is a Stylitics-side CDN configuration issue — Skechers needs to file a support ticket with Stylitics to add the origin header.

## Recommendations

**P0 — Revenue/blocking:**
1. **Investigate 429 on `/cart/`** — verify whether real mobile sessions reproduce this or if it's confined to bot-detected test traffic. If real, escalate to HUMAN Security to adjust sensor rate limits.
2. **Fix View Cart `<title>` and `lang` attribute** — these are template-level defects. The cart shell is rendering without the standard `<html lang>` and `<head><title>` present on all other pages. Check if the cart uses a separate base template or if a JS framework is stripping these during hydration.

**P1 — Functional breakage:**
3. **Stylitics CORS** — contact Stylitics support to add `https://www.skechers.com` to the `style.css` CORS allowlist for the `skechers` theme endpoint.
4. **Fix Homepage `aria-required-parent` critical violation** — locate the widget using orphaned ARIA roles (likely a carousel component) and wrap children in the correct parent role container.

**P2 — Data integrity:**
5. **Remove duplicate script tags** — `tags.creativecdn.com` and `s.yimg.com/wi/ytc.js` load twice; audit GTM container for duplicate tag triggers. Double-firing inflates conversion pixel counts.
6. **Fix PulseInsights 403** — disable the tag or update the API key. Currently fires a failing network request on every page load.
7. **Fix `device_type=desktop` misclassification** in PulseInsights parameters on mobile sessions.

**P3 — Accessibility compliance:**
8. **View Cart landmark structure** — add `<main>` and `<h1>` to cart page template.
9. **Homepage landmark deduplication** — audit for double `<main>` injection (JS hydration artifact) and nest `<aside>` elements outside `<main>`.
10. **TTFB regression on Log In / Men category (446ms)** — investigate whether these routes bypass CDN due to personalization headers or session cookies; consider stale-while-revalidate or edge-side personalization to restore CDN hit rates.
