## Technical Audit — Shoe Carnival: "Get Some Sun & Save an Extra 10% OFF"

**From:** shoecarnival@email.shoecarnival.com

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Three tracking URLs contain an embedded Unicode zero-width space (`U+200B`) that causes ASCII encoding failures, likely breaking pixel fire and redirect behavior for all affected links. All other issues are advisory-level.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**CRITICAL — Zero-width space in tracking URLs (3 failures)**

All three QA-flagged failures share the same root cause: a `U+200B` zero-width space appended at character position 85–86 of the tracking URL, immediately after the `utm_campaign` value.

Affected URL pattern:
```
https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?rd&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260518_nike_adidas_new_balance_AM​
                                                                                                            ^
                                                                                              U+200B here (invisible)
```

This affects:
- `?rd` — redirect/click link
- `?rl` — likely a redirect variant
- `?p` — open/pixel beacon

**Impact:** Any HTTP client that rejects non-ASCII characters (standard behavior per RFC 3986) will fail to resolve these URLs. Click tracking and open tracking will undercount, and downstream redirects may 404.

**Root cause:** Almost certainly a copy-paste artifact from a rich-text editor or Subject line (the Subject also contains a `U+200B` after the final `​`). The zero-width space leaked into the template URL field.

**26 additional tracking links skipped** — these were not probed, so pass/fail status is unknown. If the zero-width space was introduced at the template level rather than per-URL, some or all of these may share the same defect.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing `alt` text — 3 images confirmed**

| Image | `src` |
|---|---|
| Content image | `fcf7c998-c6f2-4f06-82af-785f6595003d.jpg` |
| Open-pixel beacon | `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=...​` |
| Tracking pixel | `0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` |

The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string, not absent) to suppress screen-reader announcement. The content image needs a descriptive `alt` value.

**Other rendering notes (from HTML source):**
- Three separate `<style>` blocks declare overlapping media query breakpoints (600px, 660px, 450px). No functional breakage, but redundancy increases parse overhead and maintenance risk.
- `<meta content="IE=edge" />` is missing the `http-equiv` attribute (`http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`), making it a no-op.
- `color-scheme: light` is hardcoded — dark-mode rendering is suppressed entirely rather than tested.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM)

The `utm_campaign` value embedded in tracking URLs is:

```

```

This value is structurally consistent (date-stamped, brand-tagged). However, due to the `U+200B` contamination noted in §2, UTM parameters passed through the redirect chain may arrive at the landing page malformed or truncated depending on how the tracking platform handles the encoding error. Landing page attribution cannot be considered reliable for this send until the URLs are corrected.
