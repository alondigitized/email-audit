# Sneaker + Ballet flat = ✨The Sneakerina✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the fifth or sixth send in about three days, and the SOBRIGHT ladder is still here from "$15 off...but only till midnight ⏳" and "$15 off ends tomorrow!". The "Sneakerina" hero is a pretty one, but it's all women's ballet-flat sneakers, a pink skirt and a fuzzy blue coat. None of it is for a 62-year-old man looking for cushioning.
- The $15 / $20 / $60 numbers are big and easy to read, and that's the only part that spoke to me. It sits way down the page, below a hero that's trying to sell me a "ballet flat." There's no arch support, no slip-on or hands-free talk, and no cushioning story. I'd mark this read. I wouldn't forward it to anyone, unless my wife wanted a Sneakerina.

## What's working

- The "$15 OFF $49 / $20 OFF $99 / $60 OFF $199" ladder is huge and easy to read on my phone.
- The code SOBRIGHT is shown right under the numbers, and the black "CLAIM $15 OFF" button is clear.
- The nav bar (Women, Men, Kids, Clearance) is simple, and "SHOP MEN" is at least there under the offer.
- The "Shop by size" grid has big numbers and a "Shop Wide Width" button. That's useful for my feet.
- The DSW logo up top matches the store I know, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The hero is all women's styling, with ballet flats, a skirt and a faux-fur coat. Nothing in it says "me."
- The "Sneakerina" pitch is about looks ("delicate look of a ballet flat"). I care about comfort, and cushioning and arch support are missing.
- The offer is buried below three coral buttons and a "So many styles" row of women's shoes, boots and heels. I had to scroll a long way to find the $15.
- The preheader says "Ends tonight: $15 off!", but the subject doesn't mention it. After the earlier "ends tomorrow" and "till midnight" sends, the urgency feels worn out.
- The fine print is a long block of tiny text. The "valid October 26-28" deadline is only in the legal copy, not in the offer.
- The three hero buttons are a bit crowded, and "Shop Athletic & Sneakers" is the only one close to what I'd click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer and a clear deadline ("$15 off $49, ends tonight"), then the product. After this many sends, the repeated ladder is what people see.
- 2. Segment by gender. Send men a comfort-first hero, like cushioned walking shoes or slip-ons, and keep the Sneakerina for women.
- 3. Add one comfort line to each product tile (cushioned, arch support, wide widths).
- 4. Add a men's row to "So many styles to love," instead of only women's shoes, boots and heels.
- 5. Cut the cadence. One strong send with a countdown beats six near-identical ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $15 off $49 at DSW`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cushy sneakers + $15 off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SOBRIGHT. Spend $49, save $15 online or in store.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort walking shoes in wide widths, plus buy more, save more.`

## Technical Audit: "Sneaker + Ballet flat = ✨The Sneakerina✨" (DSW)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent HTML email using ablink.dsw.com click wrapping. One high-priority CTA, the SMS opt-in link, returned HTTP 400 in the probe. The HTML also has several head-level defects, and the supplied source and headers are too limited to verify authentication or the unsubscribe mechanism.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] SMS opt-in CTA returns 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/unqwh0GiSSriZvL30NAiQg~~/AAQRxRA~/IQHH13hoJxcwGkjqokwrc0X…`. The QA data shows an empty anchor target (`Broken link (400):  (link text…)`), so the destination may be missing or malformed in the source. A 400 can also come from the probe being rejected, since some SMS opt-in endpoints refuse non-browser clients. Click this link in a real client to confirm before treating it as a hard break.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 23,086 of 27,389 characters (84%) are URLs, which comes from long ablink tracking strings. This can hurt spam scoring and makes the text version barely readable.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 58 skipped (footer and social links were deprioritized), so the unprobed links are unverified.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images.**
  - The first is a linked image at `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/5GrprChfjKOoEmlHZw5SxA~~/…`. If it is a content image, it needs descriptive alt text.
  - The second is the tracking pixel `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif`. Give it `alt=""` with explicit 1×1 dimensions.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Charset declaration is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare the charset. The emoji in the subject and body could be affected on clients that ignore the HTTP and MIME charset.
- **IE compatibility tag is inert.** `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lacks `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. This is low impact.
- **No language attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` gives no context.
- **Dark mode block is truncated.** `color-scheme` and the `prefers-color-scheme` rules are present, but the source was cut off, so I can't verify the rest of the dark-mode handling.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=225090073` parameter in the pixel URL is a rendered subscriber identifier, which is expected. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA notes this is expected because mail came through the AgentMail relay, so it is a verification gap, not a confirmed failure. The From domain `e.dsw.com` should be checked against the original message headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` header can't be confirmed.** The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and it reported no failure, but the footer and the physical postal address are outside the truncated source. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Campaign identifier present.** The only visible campaign ID is `mi_ecmp=20261028_P261611_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` on the pixel. Resolve the redirects to confirm that UTM values and landing pages match the "Sneakerina" content.
- **Unresolved link.** The opt-in link's destination is unknown because it returned 400.

1. **Fix or verify the SMS opt-in link first.** Open `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/unqwh0GiSS…` in a browser and check that the underlying href isn't empty. This is the only confirmed broken CTA, and it carries the 15% offer.
