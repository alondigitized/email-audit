- The men's Altman Murdock slip-in down in the grid is the one thing that stopped my thumb — comfort language in the name, slip-on, my category. Everything above it is noise. I'd scroll past the top half, maybe glance at the 40% off banner, and mark it read. Not forwarding this one.

- Same pink floral D'Lites Spring Bloom as the last five sends, still leading the email under fake cart urgency.

- Text-to-83828, app download, curbside pickup, Afterpay/Klarna — all clutter before I've even seen a shoe I'd wear.

- 2. Swap the hero image — put the Altman Murdock or another men's cushioned slip-on up top where the pink floral sneaker is now.
