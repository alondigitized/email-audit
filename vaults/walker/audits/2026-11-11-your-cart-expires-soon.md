# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- It's the skull sneaker again, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. This is the same black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" I got on 11/05, 11/08 and in the "Your cart is one click away!" email yesterday. "You liked these and you weren't wrong" is just wrong. I never looked at that shoe, and a black sneaker with a painted skull isn't what a 62-year-old with sore arches goes looking for.
- The one useful thing is the grid of six shoes below it. The Slip-ins John Deere waterproof boot, the Easy Going boot and the Murette slipper are all hands-free and cushioned, which is how I shop. They're buried under a cart nag, though. The 20% off banner also says "Valid thru 11/10," and today is 11/11. So the one real offer looks expired.
- I wouldn't click the big button. I might scroll to the slip-ins, but I'd mark this read and move on. I wouldn't forward it. A friend would ask why I'm getting cart reminders for a skull shoe.

## What's working

- The six-shoe grid has real slip-ins and Relaxed Fit styles, and they match what I wear.
- The "Complete Your Order" button is big, dark blue and easy to tap on my phone.
- The Skechers logo and look are consistent with the site, so I trust who it's from.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is large, readable text with easy tap targets.
- Naming the shoe under the hero shows exactly what they think I left behind.

## What's weak

- The cart claim is false. My cart is empty, and the "cart expires soon" subject makes it feel like a trick.
- The skull sneaker is the wrong shoe for me, and I've now seen it four or five times in a week.
- The 20% off banner says it's valid through 11/10, and it's already 11/11. That's stale, and it undercuts the whole email.
- No price shows on any shoe, so I can't do my price-vs-quality math.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and I'd need to zoom to read it.
- The hero takes a whole screen of scrolling before I see anything comfort-related.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart emails to people with no cart. Send them only to people who actually added something. This is the single biggest fix.
- 2. Lead with slip-ins and arch support for a man my age, based on what I've browsed, and push the skull shoe down or drop it.
- 3. Make sure the offer dates are current before sending. An expired banner costs trust.
- 4. Add prices and a star rating under each shoe in the grid.
- 5. Put the member 20% banner above the grid, not below it.
- 6. Make the fine print larger.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Slip-ins our members love: 20% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, comfort picks for your feet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free, cushioned and arch-supported. Members save 20% through tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best-selling slip-ins, with prices and real reviews.`
