# Celebrate your 26.2
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one's a marathon email and I'm a walker. The top of my phone has a gray "Gear up for your next great race" strip, then the logo and menu, then a red "Free shipping on all orders" line. Under that is a big photo of three happy people in maroon marathon jackets. It looks nice, but it's finisher apparel for a race I didn't run.
- I've been getting the same "25% off $125+" wall from New Balance for two weeks, so I'll give them credit for changing it up. Still, this isn't aimed at a 62-year-old looking for cushioned slip-ons. I didn't see one price, one discount, or a single word about cushion or arch support.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? Only to my nephew who ran New York. I'd leave it unread and move on.

## What's working

- The "Free shipping on all orders" line is the only real offer, and it's easy to spot in red.
- The hero photo has a man about my size and build in it, plus a man in a jacket. It's not all young women like the 10/30 send.
- The headline "Celebrate your 26.2" is big and easy to read without zooming.
- The two black "Shop now" and "Shop all running" buttons are large and easy to tap on my phone.
- The "Running / Walking / Lifestyle" links at the bottom show me they do have a walking section.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is about walking, comfort, cushioning, or arch support. It's all running and race gear.
- The hero is apparel. The shoes are tiny at the bottom of the photo, and I can't tell what model they are.
- I don't see any price, discount, or deadline. "Shop now" doesn't tell me what I'd be shopping for.
- The second photo of the young runner in a beanie and tights is a long way from my life.
- The "Shop men" and "Shop women" links are small underlined text. They're harder to tap than the black buttons.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send a version to the walkers and comfort buyers. Put a walking shoe in the hero, with a plain line about cushioning and all-day support.
- 2. Name the shoe and the price. "Fresh Foam walkers, $89.99" beats "Shop now."
- 3. Make the "Free shipping" offer bigger and give it an end date.
- 4. Make the "Shop men" links real buttons.
- 5. Move the "Walking" link up from the footer and give it a button.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: New Balance, "Celebrate your 26.2"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email sends from a branded sender domain and no broken links were reported. The HTML source has leftover template artifacts (a placeholder background URL, a template title, malformed meta and font rules), two images with no alt attribute, and a plain-text part that is almost all URLs. SPF/DKIM could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Placeholder asset URL:** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` is an unresolved placeholder. It is also plain `http://`, so it would be a mixed-content/insecure request if the class were applied.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** QA flags 9148 of 10460 characters (about 87%) as URLs. This is likely long tracking URLs, and it can look spammy to filters.
- **Probe coverage:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 16, and it reported no failures among the probed ones. The skipped footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12605053_273780_23801530.gif` looks like an open-tracking pixel (`/mo/` path). It has no alt attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` (`https://link.email.newbalance.com/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`) is a content image, so it needs descriptive alt text.
  - `egR1f966kc_…gif` is the tracking pixel and should have `alt=""`.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **Ineffective `@font-face` rules:** Every `@font-face` block in the visible source declares `font-family`, `font-weight` and `font-style` but no `src`. These declarations load no fonts, so clients fall back to Arial/Times New Roman. One block also repeats `font-family` (`proxima-nova,sans-serif; font-weight: 700; font-style: italic;font-family: …`). `ITC Garamond Book Condensed` is unquoted in its declaration.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `.bg1` is defined twice, first `#ffffff` and then `#58546d`. The later rule wins, which is probably unintended.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`.
- **Template title:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` was never replaced with real content.
- **Dark mode:** The email declares `color-scheme: light dark` and has a `prefers-color-scheme` block, and it uses a dark-mode banner asset. I can't check contrast because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens. The `XXXXX` in the background URL is a placeholder asset path (section 2), not a merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** QA found no `Authentication-Results` header. The message arrived through the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `email.newbalance.com` is unknown. This is a tooling gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA includes an unsubscribe link in every probe set and reported no failure. The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't confirm its placement, the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, or the physical postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Reused asset path:** The banner lives under `/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/`, which suggests assets are reused from a welcome-series template. The shipping-banner content is not visible, so I can't say whether the offer is consistent with the campaign.
- **UTM parameters:** I found no UTM data in the visible source or QA output, so I can't assess it. The links are routed through the ESP click-tracking domain `link.email.newbalance.com`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove or replace the `url('http://image.XXXXX.png')` background in `.bgwhite`. If it must stay, use HTTPS and a real asset.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Fix the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules or remove them, and de-duplicate `.bg1`.
4. Replace the `<title>` with a meaningful value, and align the doctype with the markup (HTML5 or XHTML).
5. Shorten the plain-text version by trimming link tracking in the text part, or add more descriptive plain copy.
6. In the next run, capture the full headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `DKIM-Signature`) and the full HTML, so the compliance and UTM checks can be completed.
