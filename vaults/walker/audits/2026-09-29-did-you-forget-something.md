# Did you forget something?

## Executive summary

- Same pink floral D'Lites "Spring Bloom" again. I've now seen it on 9/17, 9/27 and several times on 9/28, and I saw it earlier today too. I'm not shopping for that shoe. It's a women's sneaker with embroidered flowers, and I haven't left it in any cart. "Did you forget something?" doesn't ring true when I've seen the same thing a dozen times.
- There's a bright spot. Farther down, the men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman and the Contour Foam Cozy Fit Sneaker are the kind of thing I'd actually wear. They're small, though, and sit below a hero that has nothing to do with me. The 15% off for guests and 20% off for members is a real offer. ## What's working

- The 15% guests / 20% members split is easy to read, and I like that members get more.
- The men's Slip-ins Altman and Contour Foam Cozy Fit are in the grid. That's my kind of shoe: slip-on, cushioned.
- The orange "Complete Your Order" button stands out.
- The Skechers logo and clean layout are familiar, so I know who it's from.
- The "Shop Additional Styles" list (Women, Men, Kids and so on) is big and easy to tap on my phone.

## What's weak

- The hero is that pink floral women's shoe again. Nothing about it is me.
- The "Complete Your Order" button appears twice, and the black "Shop All Best Sellers" bar adds a third call to action. I don't know which one to press.
- Half the grid is kids' Garage sneakers, which means nothing to me.
- The grid captions are tiny and hard to read without zooming.
- The fine print says the offer is for boots and outdoor styles, but the banner doesn't say that. That feels like bait-and-switch.
- The offer window ended 9/28, and this email arrived 9/29. The offer looks expired.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the shoe I actually looked at, or a men's slip-in, as the hero. Drop the recycled pink floral if it's been shown a dozen times.
- 2. Cap the reminders. Two abandoned-cart emails is enough, not seven in two weeks.
- 3. Use one call to action, and say what the offer covers ("20% off boots" instead of a bare percentage).
- 4. Make the men's Slip-ins bigger, with arch support and cushioning called out.
- 5. Remove the kids' shoes unless the customer has shopped kids.
