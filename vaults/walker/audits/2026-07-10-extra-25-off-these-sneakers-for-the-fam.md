- **Clean layout, no broken images.** On my phone the sections render fine and nothing overlaps or disappears.

## What's weak

- **"For the fam" isn't me.** I'm a 62-year-old man shopping for himself. Family framing tells me this email wasn't built with my account in mind.
- **Women's sneakers lead the grid.** The first product row I see is women's shoes. Men's comes second. That ordering alone tells me I'm not the priority audience here.
- **Jordan and Nike performance dominate Men's.** High-top court shoes and flashy sport silhouettes — not what I'm looking for. Nothing about cushion, support, or walkability in sight.
- **Preview text is a raw tracking URL.** In my inbox, the preview slot reads a long ugly link instead of copy. That's the first thing I see next to the subject line, and it reads like a broken email.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** Seven emails in six days. Even when the offer is good, I'm starting to scan-and-archive before I read.

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately.** A tracking URL where the preview copy should be is the single biggest fix — it undermines the subject line before I even open.
- 2. **Lead the Men's sneaker grid, not Women's.** If my account history says I'm a 62-year-old male, the product order should reflect that. Show me men's comfort options first.
- 3. **Surface the Brooks Ghost 17 higher.** That's the one product in this email I'd actually consider. Put it in the hero secondary or at least above the fold in the Men's section with a comfort callout.
- 4. **Drop the family framing for this segment.** "For the fam" works on a young parent. For my demographic, lead with savings and comfort, not family gifting.

## Technical Audit — Famous Footwear "Extra 25% off sneakers" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard HTML 4.01 Transitional table-based layout with click-tracking via `click.email.famousfootwear.com`. Primary technical concerns are a wholesale alt-text omission across all product images and a plain-text body that is URL-dominated, which raises spam-filter risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**All 32 CTAs route through `click.email.famousfootwear.com`** — standard ESP redirect, not a bug, but unverifiable by automated probe (all 32 skipped).

**Pixel tracker present:**
```
https://click.email.famousfootwear.com/open.aspx?V5W2WD3ZAZWUFO6XV6CIAUUOSI.70249&d=70249&bmt=0
```
1×1 transparent pixel with `alt=""` — correct implementation.

**No issues found** with link structure itself; destination URLs could not be validated due to redirect wrapping.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — alt text missing on 25 images**, including the logo (`3b6a7dc1…png`) and all product shots. Several images appear duplicated in the warning list (e.g., `4ccff4ad…jpg` appears 4×), indicating reuse of the same divider/spacer image without `alt=""` set explicitly.

The logo anchor has `alt=""` — acceptable for decorative images, but the logo should carry `alt="Famous Footwear"` for screen readers.

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile — an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

`<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` is missing the `http-equiv` attribute — should be `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`. Without it, the charset declaration is ignored by some parsers.

Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional — legacy but standard for email; no issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens visible in the truncated source (no `{{`, `%%`, `[%`, or similar patterns). No issues found in the provided HTML.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe:** Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link not visible in the truncated HTML provided — cannot confirm presence. These are required; must verify in the full source footer.

**Plain-text ratio flag:**
> `[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs: 9141/11792 chars are URLs`

77.5% of plain-text content is URL strings. Spam filters (SpamAssassin, Gmail) penalize high URL density in the text alternative. The plain-text part appears to be auto-generated from the HTML without stripping redirect URLs — it should be a human-readable summary only.

**Authentication headers:** Not assessable from HTML source alone — DMARC/DKIM/SPF must be verified at the envelope level. Sending domain `email.famousfootwear.com` should be aligned with DMARC policy on `famousfootwear.com`.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTAs wrap through `click.email.famousfootwear.com` with opaque query strings (e.g., `?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5MzN9…`). Whether UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to the final destination URL is unverifiable without resolving the redirects. If the ESP does not inject UTMs at redirect time, GA4/analytics attribution will be lost for all 32 clicks.
