# 20% OFF! VIPS ONLY!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Big blue "20 OFF" — can't miss it, I'll give them that. But then I scroll down to "SO NEW, SO YOU" and it's the same story as every DSW email this week: clogs, suede ankle booties, a wedge bootie with buckles, two pairs of mary janes. Six products, zero of them anything I'd put on my feet walking the dog or running errands. And now there's a second offer stacked on top — "30% off adidas" banner up at the very top — so I don't even know which deal I'm supposed to care about.
- The "shop by size" grid running 5 to 11 in half sizes is the tell — that's women's sizing, not mine. Between that and the product photos, this whole email is built for someone else and just happens to have my email address on it.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read. Same verdict as the "Want 20% off?? Become a VIP" send from earlier today — DSW keeps sending me the women's catalog with a bigger discount slapped on top.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" number is huge and impossible to miss scrolling past on my phone.
- VIP Exclusive badge at least signals this is member-only, not just another blast.
- "Shop Men" link exists in the nav row, so the category isn't entirely absent — just not featured.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup, and app download icons are clear and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- Every single featured product — clogs, booties, mary janes — is a women's shoe. No men's casual or walking shoe in sight.
- Two competing offers (30% off adidas banner up top, 20% VIP offer as the hero) muddy which deal I'm even supposed to act on.
- Shop-by-size grid tops out with half-sizes (5–11), which is a women's size run, not mine.
- Fine print at the bottom is a wall of exclusions — comp pricing, Afterpay, SMS opt-in — before I even get to what's actually 20% off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the "So New, So You" grid to men's arrivals when sending to a male subscriber — that's the single biggest fix, same as I said on the last two DSW emails this week.
- 2. Pick one offer and lead with it — don't stack the adidas 30% banner on top of the VIP 20% hero, it splits my attention before I even get past the fold.
- 3. Add a men's size row (7–14) if you're going to show sizes at all — right now it just confirms this wasn't built for me.
- 4. Cut the exclusions text down to the one line that matters (dates + what's excluded), move the legal wall to a "details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your VIP 20% off, men's styles inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off for VIPs — new arrivals, your size`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Comfort picks up to 20% off, VIP pricing applied`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New men's arrivals just dropped — 20% off for VIPs`
