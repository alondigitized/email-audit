# Walker Miles — Skechers.com Mobile Journey Review
**Date:** April 19, 2026 | **Yesterday's Score:** 3/10

---

## 1. Executive Summary

I'm a 62-year-old guy who loves my Skechers Slip-ins. I'm a Plus member. I came to this site ready to buy. And I still couldn't complete the purchase. The cart page was a **completely blank white screen** — nothing on it. Not a single word. That's not a bad experience, that's a broken website. Everything else is noise until that's fixed.

Beyond the cart failure: from the moment I landed on the homepage to the moment I searched for "slip in," this site treated me like I don't exist. Pink Pack Collection. "Get Mom a Gift She'll Love." Women's and Girls' tabs front and center. I'm a loyal Skechers customer who logged in, and the site never once acknowledged I'm a man who buys comfort shoes.

---

## 2. Business Impact Score: **3/10**

No improvement from yesterday. The cart is broken. A broken cart means zero revenue from any customer who reaches it. Everything else — the clean Men's menu, the good BOGO messaging, the readable product page — is irrelevant if I can't check out.

---

## 3. What's Working

**Men's navigation menu (Step 4)** — This was genuinely the best screen in the journey. Large text, generous touch targets, clean list layout. I could read every item without squinting. "Hands Free Slip-ins" was right there near the bottom, plain as day. For a guy my age on a phone, that menu works.

**Slip-ins category page (Step 5)** — The copy explaining Heel Pillow technology was a nice touch. 267 results, fit filters (Wide, Extra Wide, Medium) visible and tappable. Breadcrumb navigation was clear. I knew where I was.

**Product page (Step 6)** — The shoe name, price ($135), star rating (4 stars, 11 reviews), and the BOGO deal were all visible above the fold. The "MEN'S" label was there. The Skechers Hands-Free Slip-Ins badge gave me confidence I had the right product. Text was readable without zooming.

**ADD TO CART button (Step 7)** — Large, full-width, blue. Hard to miss. The size grid boxes were a decent size for my thumbs. The red "Please select a size" warning was visible and clear.

**BOGO 50% OFF** — Consistently shown throughout the journey in the top banner and on the product page. I never had to hunt for the deal.

---

## 4. What's Weak

**Cart page is blank (Step 8) — Critical failure.** The cart loaded as a completely white screen. Nothing. No items, no buttons, no error message. If I had added a shoe to my cart, I would have no idea what happened to it. This is a broken purchase flow. This may be the same bug as yesterday or a new one — either way it's unacceptable.

**Homepage is 100% women-targeted — every single visit (Steps 1, 2, 3).** The hero image is a mother-daughter Pink Pack promotion. The promo bar says "Get Mom a Gift She'll Love!" The visible category tabs are Women's and Girls'. Men's is nowhere in the above-the-fold view. I had to go hunt for it. I've been a Plus member for years — the site shouldn't be showing me a Mother's Day promotion every time I arrive.

**Login produced zero personalization (Step 3).** The screenshot after logging in looks identical to the pre-login homepage. No "Welcome back, Walker." No "Men's picks for you." No Skechers Plus acknowledgment. The logged-in state is invisible to me.

**Search returned Women's shoes first (Step 9).** I searched "slip in" as a logged-in male customer. The first two visible results — Arch Fit Arcade and Glide-Step Altus — are both labeled Women's. With 1,011 results, there are obviously men's slip-ins in there, but the algorithm pushed women's styles to the top for a male account. That's a personalization failure that costs conversions.

**"Get Mom a Gift She'll Love!" shown across the entire journey.** It appeared in Steps 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 9. Every single page. This promotional bar never adapted to the fact that I'm a logged-in male customer. It's mildly annoying the first time. By the sixth page it's insulting.

---

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart immediately.** A blank cart page is a revenue-zero state. Every customer who reaches it is lost. This is the only Priority 1 issue.

2. **Suppress gender-mismatched promotions for logged-in users.** If my account is male, "Get Mom a Gift She'll Love" should not appear. Show me men's deals, Plus member benefits, or nothing. This is a basic personalization rule.

3. **Make Men's visible on the homepage without scrolling.** The hero and category tabs should include Men's above the fold. Right now I land on a women's shoe site.

4. **Surface Slip-ins prominently on the Men's homepage.** It's Skechers' signature comfort tech for my demographic. It shouldn't require three taps to find — it should be on the Men's landing banner.

5. **Fix search ranking for logged-in users by gender.** A male account searching "slip in" should see men's results ranked first, not women's. This is table-stakes personalization.

6. **Add a logged-in greeting or Plus status acknowledgment.** Even a small "Hi, Walker — Plus Member" in the header would make me feel like a valued customer instead of a stranger.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

Skechers.com runs on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware). TTFB is fast site-wide except the cart (137ms). The primary issues are: (1) Kasada bot-protection SDK rate-limiting its own fingerprinting calls, breaking the cart; (2) Stylitics CORS misconfiguration silently killing a product feature; (3) 32 axe violations concentrated on the product page; and (4) extreme third-party script bloat on every page load.

## Accessibility

**32 violations across 4 pages. Critical issues block keyboard/AT users on the product page.**

| Critical | `aria-required-children` | View Product | Parent role missing required child roles — entire widget tree broken for AT |

The View Product page has the highest concentration of critical violations (5 of the 4 critical total), which is the conversion-critical step. The `button-name` violation on that page likely affects add-to-cart or size-selection buttons.

## SEO

- **Duplicate `<script>` tag:** `tags.creativecdn.com/dhGobFICKSkk6J9nvQPw.js` is injected twice on the Men's Category page. While not a direct ranking signal, duplicate script execution wastes parse time and risks double-firing ad/tracking events.
- **Placeholder GTM tracking ID:** The HTML includes `id=G-1234567890` wired into a `googletagmanager.com/gtag/destination` call. This appears to be a test or default ID that was never replaced. If live, it sends GA4 events to a non-owned property.
- **Protocol-relative URL:** `//se.monetate.net/js/2/.../entry.js` uses `//` instead of `https://`. On HTTPS pages this resolves correctly, but it is a stale pattern; if served from HTTP context it degrades to unencrypted load.
- **Landmark/heading violations** (see Accessibility) also degrade structured content parsing by crawlers.

## Mobile Optimization

- **Viewport-height JS polyfill** (`--vh`) is present and active — correct approach for mobile Safari, but adds a synchronous layout read.
- **WebFontLoader loading 5 Mulish weights** (`n3`–`n7`) from Cloudflare CDN. Unless all 5 weights are used in UI, unused variants should be dropped. Each represents a separate request + render-blocking font swap period.
- **No `preconnect` hints** visible for the highest-priority third-party origins (Monetate, Quantum Metric, Bloomreach). Only `dns-prefetch` is used for Monetate — upgrading to `preconnect` reduces handshake latency.
- **Cart TTFB spike (137ms)** on a page that requires authentication state suggests server-side session resolution is not cached at the edge. For mobile users on high-latency connections this compounds.

## Console & Network Errors

### Kasada Bot Protection — 429 Rate Limiting (Critical)

```
429 https://www.skechers.com/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/
    2d206a39-8ed7-437e-a3be-862e0f06eea3/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.308
```

This UUID-keyed path is the **Kasada KProtect fingerprinting endpoint** (`/fp`). It fires 5 times and returns 429 on all attempts. The cart endpoint (`/cart/`) also returns 429.

**Root cause:** Kasada's client-side SDK is retrying fingerprint submission faster than the server-side Kasada integration allows. This indicates either: (a) the Kasada rate-limit configuration is too aggressive for real user sessions, or (b) the SDK version (`j-1.2.308`) has a retry loop bug. When the `/fp` endpoint 429s, Kasada cannot issue a valid `kpsdk` token, which downstream blocks the `/cart/` request with another 429 — **this means real users are being blocked from completing checkout**.

### Stylitics CORS Failure (Serious)

```
Access to CSS stylesheet at 'https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css
  ?base=hotspots&theme=skechers&version=63ae28fe52'
from origin 'https://www.skechers.com' has been blocked by CORS policy:
No 'Access-Control-Allow-Origin' header is present
```

This fires twice (once as a CSS load, once as an XHR). The Stylitics widget (outfit inspiration / hotspot overlays on product images) is completely non-functional due to a missing CORS header on `web-assets.stylitics.com`. This is a **Stylitics-side configuration issue** — `skechers.com` must be added to the allowed origins on Stylitics's CDN.

## Recommendations

**P0 — User-blocking:**
1. **Kasada 429 loop → cart blocked:** Engage Kasada support with the SDK version (`j-1.2.308`) and the rate-limit threshold. Audit server-side Kasada configuration for session retry budget. Confirm real users (non-bot traffic) are not being issued 429s on `/cart/`.

**P1 — Feature broken:**
2. **Stylitics CORS:** File a ticket with Stylitics to add `https://www.skechers.com` to their CDN's `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` allowlist for `web-assets.stylitics.com`.

**P1 — Accessibility / legal risk:**
3. **Product page critical violations:** `button-name`, `aria-required-children`, `aria-valid-attr-value`, and `aria-dialog-name` on the View Product page are WCAG 2.1 Level A failures. The add-to-cart flow is the highest-risk location for ADA compliance exposure. Prioritize these 4 rules on that template.
4. **`link-name` on Homepage:** All anchor elements must have non-empty text content or `aria-label`. Likely icon-only links missing labels.

**P2 — Data integrity:**
5. **Remove or replace `G-1234567890`:** Audit all GTM/gtag containers for placeholder tracking IDs and remove before they send production event data to a non-owned GA4 property.
6. **Deduplicate `creativecdn` script injection** on Men's Category — this double-fires ad tracking events.

**P3 — Performance:**
7. **Audit Mulish font weights:** Drop any of the 5 loaded weights (`n3`–`n7`) not actively used in CSS. Each is a separate font fetch.
8. **Upgrade `dns-prefetch` → `preconnect`** for Monetate, Quantum Metric, and Bloomreach origins to reduce handshake time on mobile.
9. **Capture LCP and CLS** on the next audit run — current metrics are incomplete and cannot confirm Core Web Vitals pass/fail.
