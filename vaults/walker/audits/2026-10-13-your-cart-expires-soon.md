# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. That's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom," a women's shoe I never put in a cart. It fills the top third of my screen under "YOU LIKED THESE." I've now seen this cart nag on 10/11 ("Your Items Are Waiting!"), twice on 10/12 ("Your cart expires soon!" and "Did you forget something?"), and again earlier today. Same subject as yesterday, same shoe. I'm not clicking "Complete Your Order" for a shoe that isn't mine.
- The bottom half is better. There are slip-ons with the Relaxed Fit and Arch Fit names I look for, and a 25% off banner for Skechers Plus members. But the 25% banner sits far below the fold, and the fine print says it ends 10/13. That's today, and the subject says "expires soon," so I can't tell which deadline they mean. I'd scroll, maybe tap the Altman slip-on, and then mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The Men's Slip-ins row (Altman Relaxed Fit, Contour Foam, John Deere boots) is what I actually shop for.
- "25% OFF for Skechers Plus members" is a clear number, and it says the discount applies automatically at checkout.
- The navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" and black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" buttons are big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The big SKECHERS logo and plain layout match what I see on their site.
- The Men category link is easy to find in the list, and the Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup blocks are easy to spot.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's floral sneaker I never added to a cart. The "You liked these" line is wrong for me, and it hurts trust.
- Two Boy's Garage Sneakers are in my best-sellers row. They're irrelevant to me.
- The 25% offer is buried under the product grid, and the deadline (10/13) clashes with "expires soon."
- The product names are tiny and gray, and I had to squint to read them. There are no prices, no sale prices and no star ratings.
- The fine print at the bottom is small and dense. Lots of exclusions are tucked away there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the item I actually carted, or skip the cart nag if the data is wrong. Never lead with a women's shoe to a man who browses men's slip-ons.
- 2. Move the 25% Plus banner to the top and put the real deadline in the headline ("Ends tonight").
- 3. Change the best-sellers row to men's comfort items with prices, and drop the kids' shoes.
- 4. Make the product names bigger and add the sale price after the discount.
- 5. Stop sending the same cart nag four times in three days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off ends tonight for Plus members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, your slip-ons are still here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Relaxed Fit and Arch Fit slip-ons, 25% off through midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Plus discount applies automatically at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, 600px layout with a mobile media query and a hidden preheader. Automated QA found two hard failures: the Unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400, and the plain-text part is 0 characters. It also found 12 non-HTTPS image sources and two images with missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link failed the probe (400).** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m49&externalId=2U3eG&attn_sid=1078e2f7-…`.
  - The `attn_macro` value base64-decodes to `unsubscribeLink`.
  - The 400 may come from a single-use or session-bound token, or from the prober's request. Either way, the check failed, so it needs a manual test with a real recipient token.
- **Two links returned 429 and are unverified.** These are "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." Both are `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirects. The 429 is rate limiting, so the links may be valid. They are the primary cart-recovery CTAs, though, and need a re-probe.
- **Click tracking is opaque.** Every observed link goes through Attentive's `/e/c/<token>` redirector, including the "web version" link. The final destinations can't be seen in the HTML.
- **Coverage is partial.** QA probed 12 links and skipped 25, so most footer, utility and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/...`.** Mail clients may block or warn on mixed content. The affected images:
  - Logo `dde00662-…png`
  - "Complete Your Order" hero `dfcc8fd7-….jpg`
  - Product image `a6ec5198-….jpg`
  - Footer icons: Text, App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
- **Missing alt text on two images.**
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` is a content image, so this affects accessibility.
  - The open-tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` has no alt. This is minor, but it should have `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Malformed charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute.
