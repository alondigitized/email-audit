# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Well, I can see Skechers knows it's almost Memorial Day — the whole page is draped in red, white, and blue, and they've got the BOGO deal front and center before I even scroll. That part I like. But then I look at those two models and they look like my grandkids, not somebody who's going to relate to the kind of shoe I'm actually after. It's a solid promotional setup, it just isn't speaking to me personally.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF strip at the very top.** I notice that immediately. Doesn't ask me to hunt for a deal code or scroll for it — it's the first thing I read.
- **"Members Only – Earn a $5 Reward on $75+" banner.** Short sentence, clear condition ($75+), date attached (until 5/25). That's the kind of loyalty message I actually read instead of ignore.
- **Category nav row — Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs.** Arch Fit and Slip-ins are exactly what I came for. One tap from right here.
- **Memorial Day timing.** The Red, White & Blue collection framing is seasonally spot-on. Feels like the brand is paying attention to the calendar.
- **Clean layout.** Large text on the hero, nothing is cramped, easy to read on my phone without pinching.

## What's weak

- **Both models are young — early 30s at best.** The man's wearing a denim jacket and jeans like he's going to a backyard barbecue in 2003. I get the vibe they're going for, but it doesn't look like my feet, my pace, or my life.
- **No primary "Shop Men's" button.** The nav tabs are fine, but there's no obvious action button below that hero block. My eye wants something to tap that says "Shop Men's" or "Shop Comfort."
- **Patriotic collection hero doesn't mention comfort, cushioning, or arch support.** That's what's going to get me to try a pair, not the flag theme alone.
- **Cart shows zero items.** Not really a weak point on its own, but it makes the account icon and cart feel like they're for logged-in regulars, and there's nothing nudging me to sign in or sign up beyond that one membership banner.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero sub-headline.** Instead of just "The Red, White & Blue Collection," run something like "Memorial Day Comfort — BOGO 50% on Arch Fit & Slip-ins." Tie the seasonal hook directly to the features I actually shop.
- **Add one 60+ lifestyle shot alongside the young models.** Doesn't have to replace them — just put a silver-haired guy in there. A 62-year-old looking comfortable in his yard says more to me than a 28-year-old in a denim jacket.
- **Drop a "Shop Men's" button below the hero copy.** Big, blue, tappable. Right now there's no button — just nav tabs. A prominent button converts better on mobile.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold.** I can't see it above the fold. Knowing "free shipping on $75+" (if that's the threshold) would push me toward building a cart rather than browsing and bouncing.
