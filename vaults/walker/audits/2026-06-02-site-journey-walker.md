Same problems, new wrinkle. No change from yesterday. The blank cart is a conversion-killing bug. A missed sale is a missed sale. The search personalization failure means the site doesn't know who I am even after I supposedly logged in.

## 3. What's Working

**The Men's menu is genuinely good.** When I tapped MENU and drilled into Men's, I saw a clean full-screen overlay with large, well-spaced rows: Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections — and right below the divider, "Hands Free Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Extra Wide Fit." That's exactly what I care about, right in front of me without hunting. Touch targets are generous. Text is large and readable without squinting.

**Hands Free Slip-ins as a featured category** is smart positioning. The category page loaded cleanly with a "291 Results" count and filter buttons for Wide Fit and Extra Wide Fit right at the top — important for a guy whose feet aren't the same shape they were at 35.

**The size picker is excellent.** Large square tiles, good spacing, easy to tap without misfire. The full-width sticky "ADD TO CART" button at the bottom is exactly how mobile shopping should work.

**BOGO 50% OFF is prominent.** That blue banner stayed pinned at the top across every page. That's a real value signal and it registered every time.

**Product detail layout is clean.** Price ($88.00), promo callout, product image with swipe arrows — readable and uncluttered.

---

## 4. What's Weak

**Cart page is completely blank.** Step 8 shows a white screen — nothing. Not a spinner, not an error message, not a product. If I was actually trying to buy these shoes today, I would have assumed the site was broken and closed the tab. This is the most critical failure in the entire journey.

**Login didn't appear to complete.** Steps 1, 2, and 3 all show the same homepage with "Login | details" in the loyalty bar — the account icon in the header shows no logged-in state. If I'm not logged in, there's no personalization, no saved preferences, no reason for me to trust the site remembers me.

**Search returned women's shoes for a man.** Step 9 shows "comfort shoes" returned 1,455 results, and the first two products shown are both labeled "Women's." Not a neutral default — the wrong gender, leading with the wrong product for me. If I'm logged in as a male customer, this should never happen.

**Homepage hero is wrong for me.** "GAME ON!" with bold red, green, and blue athletic shoes targets a completely different customer. I'm not here to play golf or look sporty — I want cushioning, arch support, and something I can slip on. The hero doesn't speak to me at all, and "Slip-ins" doesn't appear in the main visual real estate.

**No comfort specs on the product detail page.** The Contour Foam Cozy Fit Kiroh shows a price and a promo, but no above-the-fold callout of cushioning specs, arch support details, or what "Cozy Fit" actually means for my feet. "No Reviews" makes that worse — there's nothing to build confidence.

**Popup dismiss step showed no popup.** The promotional banner just rotated from the loyalty message to an apparel sale — no overlay or modal appeared. That's actually fine by me (I hate popups), but if a discount popup was supposed to fire and didn't, that's a missed acquisition moment.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page immediately.** A blank cart is a zero-revenue page. This is P0.
2. **Verify the login flow end-to-end on mobile.** The account icon should change state when logged in; the "Login" link in the loyalty bar should disappear. Right now it looks like the session isn't persisting.
3. **Filter search results by the logged-in user's gender by default.** A 62-year-old male searching "comfort shoes" should see men's results first — always.
4. **Put a comfort/Slip-ins message in the homepage hero.** Even a secondary banner below "GAME ON!" aimed at walking/comfort would capture people like me who aren't here for the athletic campaign.
5. **Surface comfort tech specs on the product page above the fold.** One line — "Memory foam insole | Arch support | Hands-free entry" — closes the gap between browsing and buying.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

skechers.com runs on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFCC/Demandware). The site carries a heavy third-party script payload (~20+ async trackers), active bot-detection middleware (Kasada), and a consent management layer (TrustArc with `vice-managed-src` deferred loading). The View Cart page has a critical document-shell regression — missing `<title>` and `lang` — consistent with a SPA-style page transition that bypasses full HTML initialization. The Kasada fingerprint endpoint is self-rate-limiting across the session, and the cart endpoint itself returned HTTP 429.

---

## Accessibility

**16 violations detected across 3 pages.**

**Homepage (9 violations):**
- `aria-required-parent` [critical] — ARIA role hierarchy broken; child roles exist without required parent containers. Likely caused by dynamic component injection (Monetate or similar) rearranging the DOM after render.
- `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` [moderate] — Multiple `<main>` elements or nested landmark structure. Probable cause: CMS template + injected personalization widgets each rendering independent landmark trees.
- `heading-order` [moderate] — Non-sequential heading levels (e.g., `h1` → `h3`), likely from independently authored content modules.
- `empty-heading` [minor] — At least one `<h*>` tag with no text content; may be a loader placeholder that wasn't cleaned up.
- `aria-allowed-role` [minor] — Element has an ARIA `role` incompatible with its tag.
- `region`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` [moderate] — Content outside landmark regions; `<aside>` nested inside `<main>` or similar.

**View Cart (4 violations — serious):**
- `document-title` [serious] — `<title>` is empty or absent. This page is not a separate URL route; it appears to be rendered as a client-side overlay or injected shell where the document title is never updated.
- `html-has-lang` [serious] — `lang` attribute missing from `<html>`. Combined with the missing title, this strongly suggests the cart view is rendered into a document shell that bypasses normal page initialization (e.g., a Demandware-generated partial or overlay that doesn't inherit the full template).
- `landmark-one-main`, `page-has-heading-one` [moderate] — No `<main>` or `<h1>` in the rendered cart DOM.

**Search (3 violations):**
- Same `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, `region` pattern as homepage — inherited structural issues from the shared template.

## Mobile Optimization

- **`--vh` custom property** (`style="--vh: 6.640000000000001px"`) is set inline on `<html>` via JavaScript. This is a deliberate workaround for the iOS Safari `100vh` bug. The value `6.64px` = `window.innerHeight / 100`, computed at runtime. The approach is valid but the floating point precision artifact (`6.640000000000001`) is a minor implementation sloppiness — should use `toFixed(2)` or similar.
- The Kasada fingerprint endpoint (`/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.443`) firing 5+ times per session is Kasada's client-side browser fingerprinting for bot detection. On mobile, where headless detection is less reliable, this polling cadence could cause significant rate-limit self-interference (see Console Errors). The 429s confirm the client is exceeding its own allowed call rate — likely a misconfigured SDK polling interval.
- No evidence of `<meta name="viewport">` issues, responsive images (`srcset`), or touch target sizing problems from available HTML — these require full render inspection.

## Console & Network Errors

**Kasada self-rate-limiting (5× HTTP 429):**
```
/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/2d206a39-8ed7-437e-a3be-862e0f06eea3/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.443
```
The Kasada SDK is hitting its own fingerprint collection endpoint faster than the server allows. This is a Kasada configuration issue — the polling interval or retry logic in `j-1.2.443` is misconfigured. Each 429 means bot-detection data collection failed for that call, potentially degrading protection accuracy.

**Cart endpoint rate-limited (1× HTTP 429):**
```
GET https://www.skechers.com/cart/ → 429
```
Real users hitting the cart during high-traffic periods would receive a 429, blocking checkout access. This is the most user-impacting error in the dataset.

**CORS — `tm.skechers.com` (own subdomain):**
```
Access to fetch at 'https://tm.skechers.com/' blocked by CORS policy: No 'Access-Control-Allow-Origin' header
```
A first-party subdomain (`tm.*`) is missing `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://www.skechers.com`. Whatever service lives on `tm.skechers.com` (likely a tracking/telemetry endpoint) is silently failing on every page load. Fix: add the CORS header on the `tm.` origin server, or proxy through `www.skechers.com`.

**CORS — Stylitics CSS:**
```
https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css → blocked by CORS policy
```
Stylitics (outfit/completes widget) CSS is failing to load. This will break the visual rendering of any Stylitics-powered "Complete the Look" or outfit modules. Stylitics must add `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` or `https://www.skechers.com` to their CDN response — Skechers needs to file a bug with Stylitics.

**DoubleClick 400:**
```
GET https://ad.doubleclick.net/ccm/s/collect?... → 400 Bad Request
```
Malformed analytics collection call to DoubleClick. The `auid` and `gtm` parameters may be mis-assembled by the GTM tag. No user-facing impact but ad attribution data is being dropped.

**Pulse Insights 403 (survey widget):**
```
GET https://survey.pulseinsights.com/serve?udid=... → 403 Forbidden
```
Fires twice (two pageviews). The survey widget is blocked — likely a domain allowlist issue on Pulse Insights' side, or a configuration mismatch between the `identifier=PI-89866762` and the registered domain. Survey functionality is silently broken.

## Recommendations

**P0 — Immediate:**
1. **Cart 429**: Investigate rate-limit configuration on `/cart/` — if this fires under normal user load, it's a direct conversion blocker. Check Akamai/CDN WAF rules for the cart endpoint.
2. **View Cart document shell**: The cart view is missing `<title>` and `html[lang]`. Fix the SFCC template or client-side SPA router to set these on cart render. This is an accessibility legal risk (WCAG 2.1 SC 3.1.1) and an SEO defect.

**P1 — High priority:**
3. **Kasada polling rate**: Reduce the fingerprint endpoint call frequency in the Kasada SDK config. 5 rate-limit errors per session means the SDK is broken as configured. Contact Kasada support with the `j-1.2.443` version.
4. **`tm.skechers.com` CORS**: Add `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://www.skechers.com` to the `tm.` subdomain. Likely a missing header on an internal telemetry service.
5. **Duplicate script tags**: Remove the duplicate `tags.creativecdn.com` include from the Men category template, and audit GTM for the `s.yimg.com` double-fire. Each duplicate wastes bandwidth and can cause double-counting in analytics.

**P2 — Medium priority:**
6. **Stylitics CORS**: File a support ticket with Stylitics — their CSS CDN needs CORS headers. Widget is silently broken.
7. **ARIA landmark structure**: The duplicate `<main>` and nested landmark violations on homepage are almost certainly caused by Monetate DOM injection. Add landmark validation to the personalization template constraints.
8. **`aria-required-parent` (critical)**: Audit SFCC component templates for orphaned ARIA role usage; run axe in the staging environment against the un-personalized page to isolate whether the violation is in the base template or injected content.
9. **WebFontLoader**: Replace `cdnjs.cloudflare.com/webfontloader/1.6.28` with native `@font-face` + `font-display: swap` — the library is abandoned and adds a synchronous JS-driven font loading step.
10. **`--vh` floating point**: `(window.innerHeight / 100).toFixed(4)` eliminates the precision noise in the inline style attribute.
