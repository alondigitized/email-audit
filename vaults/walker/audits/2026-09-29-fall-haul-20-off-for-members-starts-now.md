# Fall Haul 20% off for members starts now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- Now this is a better one. After a week of that pink floral D'Lites "Spring Bloom" shoe following me around in the cart reminders (9/17, 9/27, and again today), I got a big 20% OFF for members. I'm a member, so it caught my eye. The rust and gold fall look is easy on the eyes, and the numbers are big enough that I don't need my readers.
- The "Shop Men's" button is right there, and I see a tan slip-in and a brown lace-up boot with a cushioned sole. That's closer to what I actually wear. But the email only half-talks to me. Nothing about cushioning, arch support, or slipping in without bending over. The 20% is nice, but I'd still be hunting for the right shoe myself. I'd click, mostly for the men's section, and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" headline is huge and unmissable. I know the deal in two seconds.
- It says "Hi, Walker" and "Member" up top, so it knows me. The "1000 points, enough for a $5 reward, REDEEM" line is a nice touch.
- "Valid through 10/1" gives me a real deadline without shouting.
- Four plain buttons (Women's, Men's, Kids', Wide) are easy to tap with a thumb. Wide sizes are a good thing to offer a guy my age.
- The Best Sellers slip-on and the New Arrivals sneaker with the thick sole are the kind of shoes I'd look at.

## What's weak

- Guests get 15% and members get 20%. The 5-point gap isn't much of a reason to sign in, and the double offer clutters the hero.
- There's nothing about comfort, cushioning, arch support, or hands-free slip-ins. The Best Sellers shoe is embroidered suede that looks more like a women's style. The "Slip-ins" label on the sole is too small to read.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and I'd need to zoom. It lists a pile of exclusions, and "sitewide*" doesn't quite mean sitewide.
- The preview text in my inbox was junk, with a URL and my points balance instead of a sentence. It didn't sell me anything.
- After all the "Did you forget something?" and "Your Items Are Waiting!" emails, this feels like the seventh Skechers email this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's comfort hero, such as a Slip-ins shoe with "Step in. No bending. 20% off for members." Use the boot on the right as the main image, not the women's ankle boot.
- 2. Add one line of comfort proof under the hero, like "Arch-supported, cushioned, machine washable," with a rating or review count.
- 3. Fix the preheader so it's real copy and not the URL and points text.
- 4. Drop the guest 15% from the hero and move it to the footer, so the member offer stands alone.
- 5. Make the fine print larger, or link to a plain "See exclusions" page.
- 6. Skip sending this to me right after a cart-reminder email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect, an empty greeting slot ("Hi ,"). It also has a malformed tracking pixel URL, 11 images served over `http://`, and several header and accessibility problems in the head. The HTML was truncated and no headers were supplied, so compliance and UTM checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken tracking pixel (scheme missing):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `https://` or `//`. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request never reaches Return Path and seed and deliverability tracking is lost.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_09292026` is plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling. QA also flags it for missing alt.
- **Third-party beacons with a likely hashed identifier:** three `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` and `1x1_ad_impression.gif` calls are embedded. One passes `partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00`, a 64-hex value that looks like a SHA-256 of the recipient identifier. This is a privacy and disclosure item, not a rendering bug. Confirm it matches the privacy policy.
- **Unverified links:** 37 tracking links (click-redirect domain) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status are unconfirmed.
- The primary open pixel `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?...` is well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images:** QA flags these as non-HTTPS on `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`:
  - the logo `dde00662-…png`
  - `49468f73-…png`
  - the App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay and Find A Store badges
  - the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons
  
  That is 11 images in total. Clients that block mixed content may drop them.
- **Missing alt text:** `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` (a content image) and the tracking pixel `o.gif`.
- **Malformed head meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so the charset and compatibility declarations are ignored. Encoding then depends on the MIME header.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure.
- **Focus outline removed:** the global rule `a { outline: none; }` removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Missing document attributes:** `<html xmlns="…">` has no `lang` attribute. The layout tables visible in the truncated source (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Preheader conflict:** the hidden preheader reads "Guests save 15% off their purchase" while the subject says "20% off for members". Inboxes will show the preheader beside the subject.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** QA detected a greeting with an empty name field ("Hi ,"). The first-name field is rendering empty, either through a missing fallback or a blank profile attribute in the send.
- No unresolved raw tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** the HTML was truncated before the footer and no raw headers were provided. The unsubscribe link, physical address, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results could not be checked.
- The From address `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is on a dedicated sending subdomain. That is not a defect in itself, but a no-reply sender gives recipients no reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing page alignment could not be evaluated, because the 37 tracked links were skipped and the source was truncated.
- The campaign ID `PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_FULL_EN_09292026` appears consistently in the ink1000 and Krux beacons.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
2. Add a fallback to the greeting personalization, for example "Hi there," when the first name is empty, and suppress the greeting when the name is blank.
3. Switch the ink1000 pixel and all 11 `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to `https://`.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `49468f73-…png`, and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
5. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and stop suppressing `outline` on links, or replace it with a visible `:focus` style.
7. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
8. Align the preheader with the subject's offer (20% for members).
9. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML plus raw headers to confirm the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Also run the UTM check against the 37 skipped links.
