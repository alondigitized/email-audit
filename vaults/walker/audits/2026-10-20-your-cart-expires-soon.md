# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. The subject says my cart expires soon, and the headline says "YOU LIKED THESE." I didn't like these. The D'Lites Spring Bloom is a women's shoe with embroidered flowers. I've now seen the same shoe on 10/18 ("Your cart expires soon!") and 10/19 ("Your Items Are Waiting!"), and "Did you forget something?" went out earlier today. That's four cart nags in three days.
- The one thing that does help me is the 20% off for members, which is real money. But it sits in the bottom half under a women's shoe, a row of kids' garage sneakers and a "Complete Your Order" button for an order I never started. The sneakers I'd actually look at are the Slip-ins Arch Fit Arcade and the Waterproof John Deere boots. They're small, and the labels are tiny on my phone.
- I wouldn't click the hero. I might scroll down and tap the 20% off banner, but I'd probably mark the email read and move on. I wouldn't forward it. Skechers makes slip-ins that suit me, and this email doesn't show them.

## What's working

- The "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner is big and clear. I can read the number without zooming.
- The Slip-ins Arch Fit Arcade shoe is in the grid. It's hands-free with arch support, which is what I look for.
- The navigation list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, Sale) is one tap per row and easy to use with big thumbs.
- The Skechers logo and layout look the same as their other emails, so I trust that it's really them.
- The fine print says the offer runs through 10/21 and excludes sale items. It's honest about the terms.

## What's weak

- I never started a cart, so "Your cart expires soon" and "Complete your order" feel false. That costs them trust.
- The hero is a women's floral sneaker, and it takes up the whole first screen. The grid below is mostly women's and kids' shoes.
- Only one of the six shoes in the grid is something I'd wear. The small product text is hard to read on a phone.
- It's the same pink sneaker as 10/18 and 10/19. That repeat is annoying.
- The member deal is buried below the products. Nothing says "Hi, Walker," unlike the 10/19 "Real reviews, real comfort" email, which made me feel known.

## Recommendations

- 1. Only send a cart email to someone who actually has a cart. If I don't, send the 20% member offer with men's slip-ins in the hero instead.
- 2. Put the 20% off banner at the top, above the hero, the way the 2 for $100 banner worked on 10/18.
- 3. Show men's Slip-ins Arch Fit as the hero, with a line like "hands-free, cushioned, arch-supported."
- 4. Make the product names and prices bigger, and tell me the price after 20% off.
- 5. Don't send more than one cart-style email in a day.
