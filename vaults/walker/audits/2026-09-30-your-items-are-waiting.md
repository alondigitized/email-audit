**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Here it is again: the pink floral D'Lites "Spring Bloom" sneaker, which has been following me since 9/17. I've seen it in the 9/27 and 9/28 cart reminders, again yesterday, and now in a second "cart" email today. It's a women's shoe with embroidered flowers. I'm a 62-year-old man and I'm not buying it. Skechers earlier today sent me "Step into cozy season," a nice one with my name on it, the Member badge and my 20% discount. This one undoes that goodwill. I'd call it a stranger's email, not one for a known member.
- The "We noticed you noticing us…" headline is a bit creepy for a guy my age. The best-sellers grid is mostly women's and kids' shoes: two women's slip-ins, two boys' Garage sneakers and a girl's Glam Racer. Only one thing is really for me, the men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman in Murdock. It's the smallest thing on the page and buried in the middle. That's exactly the hands-free, roomy-fit shoe I'd look at, and it should have been the hero.
- Would I click? Probably not on the cart shoe. I might tap the Altman slip-on if I were curious, and the 20% member number is decent. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The big navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is easy to see and easy to tap on a phone.
- The 15% for guests / 20% for members banner is large and readable. Being a member, I'd take the 20%.
- The headline is big and the text is large, so I can read it without squinting.
- The men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman is in the grid. That's the kind of shoe I actually wear.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) is simple to navigate with a thumb.

## What's weak

- The pink floral women's shoe is the hero again. It's about the sixth time I've seen it, and it's not for me.
- The "options we think you'll love" are mostly women's and kids' shoes. Only one of six is a men's shoe.
- Nothing says "Hi, Walker" or acknowledges I'm a member with points. The 20% member offer sits low in the email and doesn't tie to my cart.
- The "Thru 10/1: See below for details" line is small. The footer says "20% OFF BOOTS & OUTDOOR STYLES - ONLINE ONLY" and "Offer valid 9/23/26 - 9/28/26". That doesn't match the 15%/20% banner or the 10/1 date, so I can't tell what I'm getting.
- There's no cushioning or arch-support talk, and no price or reviews. I can't tell what the shoe costs.
- The "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US…" line feels like being watched.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a shoe that matches what I browse: a men's slip-in or Arch Fit. Drop the women's floral shoe if I've ignored it this many times.
- 2. Put my member discount up top, with my name and the 20% off, and show the price.
- 3. Make the fine print match the banner. One offer, one clear end date.
- 4. Limit the grid to shoes in my category, with comfort details like "Arch Fit" and "Relaxed Fit" and star ratings.
- 5. Cap the cart reminders. This is the 4th or 5th in a week.
