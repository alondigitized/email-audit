## What's working

- Two comfort tech names — Cozy Fit and Glide Step — right up front, and both look like the cushioned, chunky-sole shoes I actually wear.
- Clear 20% off with a code (UPGRADE) instead of some vague "save big" nonsense.
- Dedicated "Men's Cozy Fit" and "Men's Glide Step" buttons mean I don't have to wade through women's and kids' styles to find my size category.
- Skechers Plus member reward called out separately — I'm in that program, and it's nice to be treated like I'm actually a member instead of a stranger.
- Deadline is today (8/31), which after a week of fake "your cart expires" nonsense actually feels believable since it lines up with a real end-of-month sale.

## What's weak

- "Exclusions apply... see below for details" right under the CTA is the kind of fine-print hedge that makes me wonder what's NOT included before I even click.
- No price on anything — I've got to click through and do math myself to know if 20% off is actually a good deal or not.
- Wide-width option is buried down under Cozy Fit/Glide Step instead of up top — I know guys my age who'd want that called out sooner.
- Text-to-83828 and app download clutter at the bottom I'll never use — feels like every retailer email these days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual price or "starting at $XX" on the hero shoes — I shouldn't have to click through just to see if 20% off gets me under $70.
- 2. Trim "exclusions apply, see below for details" out of the CTA area — bury the legal stuff at the very bottom where it already lives, not right under the button.
- 3. Move "Wide" sizing up next to Men's/Women's instead of as a fourth-place afterthought — that's a real differentiator for guys like me.
- 4. Lead the subject with the specific shoe, not just "top sellers" — I want to know it's Cozy Fit/Glide Step before I even open it.
