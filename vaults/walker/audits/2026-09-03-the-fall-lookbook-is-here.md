# The Fall Lookbook is HERE 🍂
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Alright, BOGO 50% off up top is real — I said that yesterday too. But then I scroll and it's the whole "Fall Lookbook" — tall boots, animal print, retro sneakers, "autumnal browns," "reimagined loafers" — every single one of those women's fashion boots and heels. There's a "SHOP BY SIZE" grid at the bottom with a "SHOP WIDE WIDTH" button, which is the first thing all week that's acknowledged a guy with actual foot problems exists, but even that's generic — no men's category anywhere near it.
- This is basically the same DSW email machine I've been getting all week, just dressed up as a "lookbook" instead of a booties push. I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding — my wife might actually like the tall boots section, so maybe I forward it to her, but that's not this brand doing anything for me.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is a legit, understandable offer — no "spend $X get $Y" math homework.
- "SHOP WIDE WIDTH" button at the bottom is the one nod to comfort/fit concerns that matters to me.
- Layout's clean, no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.
- "SHOP MEN" link exists right up top in the nav bar, so at least it's one tap away.

## What's weak

- Every single lookbook section — boots, animal print, retro sneakers, loafers — is women's product. Not one men's shoe pictured anywhere in the whole scroll.
- "The Fall Lookbook" as a subject and hero promises trend content, and I've got zero use for trend content — I want cushioning and arch support, not "chocolate & cognac & taupe."
- Same offer, same brand, same week — this is basically a reskin of Sunday's booties email with a trendier bow on it, like the "For the Love of Loafers" one from 2026-09-01.
- No mention of DSW VIP points or loyalty perks tied to this specific offer, even though there's a generic club blurb at the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me a men's-specific version — comfort walkers, slip-ons, wide widths — instead of assuming I want to see a five-section women's trend guide. That's the single biggest miss.
- 2. Move "SHOP WIDE WIDTH" and comfort-brand call-outs (Skechers, Hoka, New Balance) up near the top, not buried after five fashion blocks.
- 3. Drop "lookbook" language for guys — swap for something concrete like fit, cushioning, or price.
- 4. Tie the BOGO offer to a men's comfort shoe example in the hero image, not a red trench coat and snakeskin boots.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's Comfort Shoes: BOGO 50% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy One, Get One Half Off — Walking Shoes Included`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, wide-width, and easy on your feet — 50% off the second pair`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your comfort brands are in the mix. Grab two, save big.`
