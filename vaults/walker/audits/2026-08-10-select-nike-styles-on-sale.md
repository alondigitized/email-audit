# Select Nike styles on SALE 🎉
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Well, "Select Nike styles on SALE" got my thumb to open it — Nike's a name I know, up to 40% off is a real number — but then it's the same bait-and-switch DSW's been running all week. One line about Nike up top, and everything below the fold is stiletto heels, strappy sandals in gold and burgundy, "gotta-have heels that won't break your budget." I don't know a single guy who's forwarding that to anybody. This is a hard scroll-and-close for me — the subject got me in the door, the email showed me the exit.
- The "shop by size" and "shop wide width / wide calf" boxes near the bottom are actually useful, and that's the one thing that made me pause. But there's not a men's shoe, not a sneaker, not a walking shoe anywhere in this whole thing until you hit the tiny "Women's Shoes / Athletic & Sneakers / Sandals" row way down at the bottom — and even that "Athletic & Sneakers" tile looks like a kid's shoe, not a Nike walker.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? No — unless it's to my wife, who might actually want the heels. Marking this one read and moving on.

## What's working

- "UP TO 40% OFF" with the Nike swoosh right under it caught my eye at the very top — that's a real, specific number.
- The size grid (5 through 11, plus wide width/wide calf) near the bottom is genuinely handy — I wear a wide, and that's the kind of detail that makes me trust a retailer knows their customer.
- "FREE SHIPPING FOR VIPS," "BUY NOW PAY LATER WITH Afterpay" footer icons are clean and easy to read at a glance.

## What's weak

- The subject promised Nike, but the whole visual body is heels — dressy sandals, bow-tied pumps, strappy metallics. Not one sneaker shown.
- "HEEL THYSELF" and "MORE WAYS TO TREAT YO' SELF" — that's not copy aimed at a 62-year-old guy looking for a cushioned walking shoe, that's aimed at someone thirty-plus years younger than me.
- Zero men's product anywhere until a small icon row near the very bottom, and even that's easy to miss on a phone scroll.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — nothing that speaks to how I actually pick a shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. If the subject says Nike, show me Nike — specifically men's Nike walking or athletic styles — in the hero, not three screens of heels. That mismatch is what kills this for me.
- 2. Move "Athletic & Sneakers" and "Men's Shoes" tiles up near the top, not buried after two heel showcases.
- 3. Swap "HEEL THYSELF" for something that actually describes what's on sale — a plain "Nike: up to 40% off" banner would serve me better than a pun I don't relate to.
- 4. Segment this — send guys like me a version built around men's athletic/comfort styles instead of one blast to everyone.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cushioned Nike kicks, up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus wide widths in stock — shop men's now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort styles from $29.99, while sizes last`
