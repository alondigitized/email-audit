# Final drop: the MLS Archive collection
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Another one from adidas that's got nothing to do with what's on my feet. This is a whole email about soccer jerseys — eight MLS clubs, "90s-inspired fits," a bunch of young folks in a locker room set draped in team gear. No shoes to speak of, no price, no offer, nothing about comfort or fit. It reads like a fan-club mailer, not something aimed at a 62-year-old who wants a cushioned walking shoe.
- I'll give them this — the jersey lineup shot at the bottom is clean and easy to read, and there's no clutter. But I'm not a soccer jersey guy and never will be. This is a straight delete, same as the Real Madrid jersey email a few days back (2026-08-12). I won't forward it, I won't click it, and honestly I'm starting to wonder why adidas keeps sending me sports apparel when the "best sellers" banner up top is the only thing in the whole email that might actually have a shoe in it.

## What's working

- The jersey flat-lay at the bottom is sharp and well organized — I can actually see all eight designs clearly.
- No layout glitches, nothing overlapping or broken.
- That top black banner — "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" — is at least a generic doorway to shoes, even if it's buried under jersey content.

## What's weak

- Not a single shoe featured anywhere in this email — it's jerseys, full stop.
- No price, no percentage off, no offer of any kind I can see.
- The hero photo is a bunch of young people posing in a locker room — doesn't look like anyone I'd relate to, and definitely doesn't scream "casual comfort shoe for a 62-year-old."
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support, or slip-on comfort — the stuff that actually gets me to click.
- "Final drop" urgency for a product I have zero interest in doesn't move me one bit.

## Recommendations

- 1. If adidas wants my click, lead with an actual shoe and an actual number — a walking or comfort shoe with a specific % off, front and center, like the Freehiker send did on 2026-08-11.
- 2. Move that "best sellers" banner's link destination to a men's comfort/walking shoe category, not a jersey collection page.
- 3. Drop the jersey/team-gear sends from my list entirely, or let me opt into a "footwear only" track — I've told this brand with my inbox behavior three sends running that I don't want jerseys.
- 4. If they insist on cross-promoting apparel, at least pair it with a shoe from the same line so there's something for me to look at.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your next everyday walking shoe is here`
