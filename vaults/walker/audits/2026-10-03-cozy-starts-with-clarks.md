- The barcodes and "Add to mobile wallet" buttons are handy if I'm already in the store.
- The Clarks name and "Comfort Starts Here" line speak to what I care about, and Clarks is a brand I trust for comfort.
- The Men's tab in the footer nav at least tells me there's something for me.

## What's weak

- The subject promises Clarks, but the Clarks block sits near the bottom, after about four screens of coupons and boots.
- Everything above the Clarks block is "Select Women's," including BOGO Boots and the clogs. I'm a 62-year-old man and I scroll right past it.
- The Clarks boot is a women's-style lace-up with a side zipper, and there's no price, no "men's" and no sizes. I can't tell whether Clarks even has anything for me here.
- The only "Shop Clarks" link is a tiny underlined line under the image. That's a poor tap target on a phone.
- The fine print is tiny and dense, with long exclusion lists. The Clarks and Hoka names I'd look for in the exclusions are unreadable.
- Sixth near-identical send in a week. FALL15 has been hammered since September 16th.

- 1. Put the Clarks hero at the top and make it match the subject. Show a men's slip-on or walking shoe with a price, a cushioned-sole callout and a real button.
- 2. Split the send by gender. A guy like me shouldn't have to dig through women's boots to find comfort shoes.
- 3. Add a comfort line such as "Easy on, all-day cushion" so I know what I'm getting.
- 4. Cut the cadence. One strong send beats five recycled ones.
- 5. Make the CTAs real buttons, not small underlined text.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run reports 100% pass, but the HTML source has two defective tracking pixels: one with a malformed query string and one with an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder. The source was truncated and 23 tracked links were never probed, so links, footer, and headers are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved placeholder in a pixel:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still has the literal `[UNIQUE]` token. The per-recipient identifier was never substituted, so opens or clicks from this pixel can't be attributed to a recipient.
- **Malformed pixel query string:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261003_clarks_bogof_AM`
  - `t=utm_campaign=...` puts a second `=` inside a single parameter value. A parser will read `t` as `utm_campaign=SCA_...` instead of reading `utm_campaign` as its own parameter.
  - The reported URL ends with a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. If it is in the real `src`, it may break campaign matching.
- **Unverified destinations:** 23 tracking links were skipped because they are on click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes, and redirect chains were not checked.
- **QA summary is inconsistent:** it says "0 issue(s), 0 warning(s)" while listing three `[WARN]` entries. The checker is not counting its own warnings.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** both `<img>` elements above (`ieezzy5m`, `0di86k1v`) have no `alt` attribute. They are tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Link underline removed globally:** `a, a:link, a:visited ... { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` strips underline and color from every link. Anything not styled as a button then can't be told apart from body text (WCAG 1.4.1).
- **Small mobile text:** `@media (max-width: 600px) { .responsive-text { font-size: 12px !important; } }` makes mobile text smaller than the 14px desktop size.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv`. The `IE=edge` tag does nothing, and the first relies on the `charset` attribute alone.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. This is benign in most clients.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, and `color-scheme` are all set.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `d=[UNIQUE]` in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel is a visible unrendered placeholder (see section 2).
- No other tokens were found in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
Not verifiable. The HTML was truncated before the footer, and no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM, or DMARC header data was supplied. No violations are asserted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only campaign tag visible is `utm_campaign=SCA_20261003_clarks_bogof_AM`, and it sits inside the pixel URL. It follows a clear date/brand/offer/send naming pattern.
- `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and the landing-page URLs could not be checked. Every link goes through an `emltrk.com` redirect, and none were resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ESP template so `d=[UNIQUE]` is substituted at send time, and confirm the pixel renders with a real value in a test send.
2. Fix the `t=utm_campaign=...` query format on the `ieezzy5m` pixel. Strip any U+200B character from the URL.
3. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"`, and `style="display:block"` to both tracking pixels.
4. Restore underlines on inline text links, or style them explicitly, instead of the global `text-decoration:none !important`.
5. Raise the mobile `.responsive-text` size to 14px or more.
6. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags, or remove them.
7. Re-run QA with the full HTML. Resolve the redirects to verify landing URLs and UTMs. Capture the headers and check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
8. Fix the QA summary so warnings are counted.
