# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- Oh, there she is again — that same pink floral D'Lites sneaker, front and center under "READY TO CHECK OUT?" That's not my cart. I've gotten this exact shoe thrown at me six times this week now, and I'll say it again: I don't shop for floral women's sneakers, and no amount of "did you forget something?" is going to change that. It reads like a robot that hasn't updated its notes since spring.
- Buried below the fold, though, there's actually a decent men's lineup — the Slip-ins Arch Fit, the Relaxed Fit Altman, even a 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step banner with a real code. That's the stuff I'd click on. Problem is I have to scroll past a hero image that has nothing to do with me to get there.
- Bottom line: I'm not clicking "Complete Your Order" on a shoe that was never in my cart, and I'm not forwarding this to anybody. If they'd led with the Cozy Fit deal instead of the floral D'Lites, I'd have opened my wallet, not just my inbox.

## What's working

- The 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step banner with a visible code (UPGRADE) is a real, usable offer.
- Men's Slip-ins Arch Fit Arcade and Relaxed Fit Altman thumbnails show up in the grid — comfort, slip-on, arch support, exactly my category.
- Shop Now, Pay Later options (Afterpay/Klarna) and Curbside Pickup are practical touches I'd notice, even if I don't use them.
- No render bugs — everything loaded clean, text is legible, buttons are clear.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is a pink floral women's D'Lites — same one that's chased me all week — and it's presented as MY abandoned cart, which it isn't.
- "Did you forget something?" implies I left this in a cart, which is just false and a little insulting at this point.
- The good stuff for me (men's comfort shoes, the 20% off deal) is stacked way down the page, past a lot of scrolling.
- This is basically the fourth or fifth near-identical "come back and check out" email this week — the cadence is wearing thin.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop leading with a product I've never looked at — swap the hero to whatever I actually browsed, or default to the best-seller men's grid if there's no real browsing signal.
- 2. Move the 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step deal up top, above the fold — that's the one thing in this email I'd act on.
- 3. Drop the fake urgency framing ("did you forget something") when there's no real cart data behind it — it just erodes trust after six repeats.
