- Nothing here is speaking to me specifically. It's a general-merchandise coupon dump — take 20% off everything, then another 20% off in-store, then $20/$10 off tiered pricing, then a mobile wallet reminder. - The 20% off code (LABORDAY) is dead simple and visible without digging.
- "Today Only" gives it real urgency, unlike some of the vaguer sends this week.
- Skechers does show up in the athletic-brands row, even if it's logo #5 of 5.
- The tiered $20/$10 off coupon is at least a concrete, familiar discount structure.

## What's weak

- It's another all-brands logo wall — Nike, adidas, New Balance, Converse, Skechers, same cast I've seen in every recent send, no product for me singled out.
- Sandal Blowout right after yesterday's sandal email — feels like they're not tracking what they already pitched me this week.
- "Buy Any Shoe & Get 50% Off a Pink Sticker Shoe" makes no sense to a first-time reader — no product shown, no explanation.
- Way too many stacked offers (20% off, another 20% in-store, sandals 70%, pink sticker 50%, $20/$10 tiered) — my eye doesn't know where to land first.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, arch support, or men's casual — the stuff that actually gets me to click.

- 4. Move Skechers or a comfort brand into the first or second logo slot, not last.
- 5. Add one line of fit/comfort copy — "slip-on, memory foam, all-day support" — something that actually matches how I shop.
