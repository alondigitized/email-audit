# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we go again — another "you forgot something" email, and once more it's mostly stuff I never touched. That first shoe, the Arch Fit D'Lites in white with the red and blue trim, that's actually a decent-looking chunky sneaker I could see myself in. But then it's straight into Care Bears kids' shoes — a UNO covered in cartoon bears and a hot pink pair called "Brighten The Day" — and I'm sitting here thinking, did my cart get mixed up with my granddaughter's wish list? This is the same complaint I had yesterday with the Care Bears cart-abandonment email, so either their tracking is broken or they're just cramming in whatever's trending regardless of who's looking.
- The rest of it is a scattershot grid of six more shoes — some women's slip-ins, a kids' garage sneaker, only one thing (the Delson 3.0 Roth) that's actually in my lane. Then it pivots to a 25% off Back-to-School kids sale, which, fine, that's honest about who it's for, but it's not me.
- Would I click? Maybe on that first pair, if I even remember what I was looking at. Forward to a friend? No reason to. Mostly I scroll past this one and go check my actual cart on the website if I really wanted something.

## What's working

- The hero "Ready to check out?" banner with the black CTA button is clear and easy to read on my phone, no squinting needed.
- That first shoe, the Arch Fit D'Lites 330, actually looks like something I'd wear — good arch support branding built right into the name.
- The "Complete Your Order" button repeats partway down, so I don't have to scroll back up if I do want to act.
- Payment and pickup options (Afterpay, Klarna, curbside) are laid out clean at the bottom if I ever need them.

## What's weak

- Two of the three "recommended" shoes up top are kids' Care Bears sneakers — nothing to do with anything a 62-year-old guy would have in a cart.
- The six-shoe grid is a grab bag — women's slip-ons, a boy's sneaker — with just one item, the men's Delson, that's actually for me.
- The whole bottom half turns into a Back-to-School kids sale, which tells me this "abandoned cart" email is really just a general catalog blast wearing a disguise.
- No sizing, fit, or comfort details on any of these — for slip-ins and arch support, that's exactly the info I'd want before clicking back.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the personalization — if I looked at men's comfort shoes, show me men's comfort shoes in that cart reminder, not Care Bears kids' sneakers. This is the same miss as yesterday's cart email, two days running now.
- 2. Drop the kids' Back-to-School sale entirely from a cart-recovery email, or at least push it below a men's cross-sell section instead of dominating half the layout.
- 3. Add one line of comfort-specific copy under the actual cart item — "Arch Fit cushioning, true to size" — since that's the deciding factor for me, not the picture alone.
- 4. If you're going to show alternatives, filter them to my gender/category instead of a random six-shoe mixed grid.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your slip-ins are still in your cart`
