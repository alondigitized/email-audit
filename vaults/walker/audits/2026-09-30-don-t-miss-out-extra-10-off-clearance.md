# Don't miss out: Extra 10% off clearance
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- It's DSW again. By my count this is the seventh clearance email in a week. The subject tells me nothing new. The top banner has a real number, "Extra 10% off clearance online" with the code GOLDEN, so I didn't toss it right away.
- Then I scrolled into the grid. Twelve shoes are under "THESE *JUST* DROPPED," and about nine of them are women's sandals, heels and slides. I count one Reebok sneaker and a couple of casual pieces. There's a brown suede Clarks-style shoe that might suit me, but it's lost in the pile. Nothing is labeled "men's," "cushioned" or "slip-on." I don't see any prices either, so I'd have to tap through to find out what "clearance" means in dollars.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it, see the heels again, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted a deal I'd go to the Brooks or New Balance pages myself.

## What's working

- The coral banner with "EXTRA 10% OFF CLEARANCE ONLINE*" and the code GOLDEN is big and easy to read on my phone.
- "Always 30% off in stores" is a second real number, and it tells me what I get if I'd rather walk into a store.
- The "Shop by size" block with wide width and wide calf buttons is a good touch. Those are big black buttons I can actually tap.
- The "Shopping made easy" icons (free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup, app) are simple and clear.
- The brand name and look match what I'd see on dsw.com, so I trust who it's from.

## What's weak

- The product grid is nearly all women's sandals and heels. That's about nine of twelve shoes, and none are aimed at a 62-year-old man.
- There are no prices, no percent-off tags and no brand names on the shoes. "Clearance" with no dollar figure doesn't tell me anything.
- I see no comfort language anywhere: no cushioning, arch support, walking or slip-on. It's a wall of shoes with no reason to pick one.
- The "Shop by size" list is women's-style half sizes from 5 to 11, and nothing tells me it covers men's sizes. I'd have to tap to find out.
- The *JUST* in "THESE *JUST* DROPPED" with asterisks looks sloppy. Also, the sandal-heavy grid in late September feels off-season.
- The fine print takes up a third of the email, and the offer is still starred and buried in it: limit 1 per customer, not valid in Canada, online only.
- This is the same message as "PSA: Extra 10% off clearance" from 9/29 and "Get Extra 10% Off Clearance!" from 9/28.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the grid by who's reading. Send me a men's version with walking shoes, sneakers and slip-ons, each with a price and a "was/now" tag. That one change would matter most.
- 2. Put a "Men's comfort clearance" button next to the general one. The tap targets are fine, but the category is missing.
- 3. Show brand names and prices under each shoe, such as "Brooks $75," so I can do the price-versus-quality math without tapping.
- 4. Give the week's offers a rest. Seven emails saying the same thing teaches me to ignore you.
- 5. Move the code and its deadline into one line near the top, so I don't have to go looking for them in the fine print.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: DSW, "Don't miss out: Extra 10% off clearance"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken link, the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned HTTP 400 on the probe. It also has some head and accessibility markup issues. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer, headers and most links could not be reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] SMS opt-in CTA returns 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ceA4mR4JULGMWUG7aTWwwg~~/AAQRxRA~/-0w66Ole…` and the tracked redirect returns 400.
  - A 400 on a signed tracking wrapper can mean the redirect payload is malformed or expired. It can also mean the destination rejects the probe's request method.
  - Re-test with a real GET and a browser user agent before treating it as a live customer-facing break.
  - This is the only link the QA data confirms as failing. 12 links were probed and 45 were skipped.
- **Plain-text bloat.** 20,965 of 24,860 characters (about 84%) of the plain-text part are URLs. The cause is the long `ablink.dsw.com` tracked links, so the text part is mostly link strings. This is a deliverability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images.**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/AFQcc72dXD7aHJpWccfdUA~~/…` has no alt.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?…` is a tracking pixel with no alt. It should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed head meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. Some clients could misdetect the charset.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` is the same for every campaign. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appeared. The `o.gif` pixel carries `mi_u=225090073`, a per-recipient ID, which is expected for tracking. The truncated body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Could not verify.** The footer and unsubscribe markup fell outside the truncated HTML, and no `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were supplied.
- **Authentication not checked.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC results were not provided. The sender is `dsw@e.dsw.com` and the tracking domain is `ablink.dsw.com`, so both are on DSW-controlled subdomains.
- **Physical address not confirmed** in the visible source.
- The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no unsubscribe failure was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** Every link is wrapped in the `ablink.dsw.com` redirect with an opaque payload, so final destinations and UTM parameters could not be seen. 45 links were also skipped.
- The campaign is identifiable only by the pixel's `mi_ecmp=20260930_P251277_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_1`.
- The clearance offer in the subject could not be matched to a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link with a real GET and a browser user agent. If it still returns 400, regenerate the tracked link or fix the destination, since it is a lead-capture CTA.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and a descriptive alt to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the head meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and use a campaign-specific `<title>`.
6. Shorten or replace the plain-text part, with fewer raw tracked URLs and more readable text, to improve the text-to-link ratio.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers to check unsubscribe, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM parameters.
