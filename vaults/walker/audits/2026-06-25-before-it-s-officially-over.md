# before it’s officially over…
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- I've been getting Crocs emails for weeks — "Extra 25% Off" showed up in my inbox back on June 13th, and here it is again, word for word. The subject line teases "before it's officially over" but when I scroll to the fine print, the offer expired three days ago — June 22nd. I'm reading this on June 25th. That's not urgency, that's a mailing that went out late and nobody caught it.
- The whole email is a clog-and-Jibbitz show. Bright red, bright pink, very feminine styling. No men's comfort walkers, no cushioning angle, no arch support pitch. There isn't even a real "Shop Now" button that I can find — just product images, an app download block, and a wall of fine print disclaimers. For someone like me who cross-shops Hoka and New Balance and opens Crocs emails hoping to find a comfy slip-on deal, this is a dead end.
- Mark as read and move on. Wouldn't click. Wouldn't forward. Mildly annoyed.

## What's working

- **The headline is legible.** "Extra 25% Off" in large type reads clearly on a phone screen without zooming — I'll give them that.
- **The stacked deal (25% off on top of up to 50% off) is a real offer** — or would be if it hadn't already expired.
- **No garish collab licensing clutter.** After Harry Potter, Toy Story, and Friends collabs back-to-back, at least this one is just... shoes.

## What's weak

- **The offer is dead.** Fine print says it expired June 22nd. Sending an urgency email three days after the deadline is a trust-killer.
- **No CTA button anywhere I can see.** The email goes hero copy → product images → Jibbitz promo → app store badges. Where do I tap to shop?
- **The product imagery is aimed at someone else entirely.** Bright pink and red clogs on a pink background — I'm a 62-year-old guy shopping for comfort walking shoes. This reads as women's marketing.
- **Preheader is broken junk.** From the inbox it would show garbage characters. The subject does zero work telling me what's on sale, and the preheader doesn't save it.
- **Same offer as June 13th.** "Extra 25% off" for the second (third?) time in two weeks. At this point I'm tuning it out automatically.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill this send or resend with a live offer.** An expired discount isn't a "before it's over" moment — it's embarrassing. If you can't extend the offer, don't mail.
- 2. **Add a visible Shop Now button** above the product images so I know where to tap. Right now the email is a dead-end poster.
- 3. **Show me men's styles — something beige, navy, or black with a cushioned footbed.** Not hot pink clogs.
- 4. **Fix the preheader.** Write one plain sentence that supports the urgency angle ("Sale ends tonight — up to 50% off, no code needed").
- 5. **Retire "Extra 25% Off" for at least two weeks.** I've seen this offer so many times it registers as background noise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, your sale ends tonight — extra 25% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off + extra 25% — men's styles included`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends tonight at midnight — no code needed, already in cart`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned clogs, sandals, and slides — up to half off right now`
