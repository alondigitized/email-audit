### 1. Technical Summary

Four tracking/redirect URLs are broken due to a zero-width space (`U+200B`) embedded at position 76–77, likely introduced during template authoring. All other structural concerns are accessibility-level warnings with no other hard failures.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**CRITICAL — Zero-width space in tracking URLs (4 failures)**

All four broken links share the same root cause: a `U+200B` (ZERO WIDTH SPACE) character appended to the `t=` parameter value in the emltrk redirect URL. This causes the ASCII codec to reject the URL, breaking click tracking and likely preventing the redirected destination from loading correctly.

Affected URL pattern:
```
https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?rd&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260712_nike_athletics_AM​
                                                                                                    ^
                                                                              U+200B here (invisible)
```

Affected redirect types: `?rd` (redirect), `?rl` (redirect logged), `?p` (pixel/open), `?f` (forward). The `?p` and `?f` failures mean open tracking is also broken for some recipients.

**Evidence:** QA log positions 76–77 across all four failures correspond to the character immediately after `_AM` in the campaign token value.

**Action:** Strip the zero-width space from the campaign token field in the template/ESP merge tag. Check copy-paste source (Google Docs, Notion, Slack) — these tools frequently inject U+200B.

**26 tracking links skipped** — not failures, but coverage is incomplete. Confirm destination URLs resolve correctly in a pre-send spot check.
