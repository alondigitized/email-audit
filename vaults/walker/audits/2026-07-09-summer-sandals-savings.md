# Summer = Sandals & Savings
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Nine emails in nine days, and today I open to find sandals everywhere — except any I'd actually wear. The top of the email has a clean $10 off code and a bright summer hero that catches the eye. I'll give Shoe Carnival that. But the moment I scroll down even a little, it's jellies for women, women's blowout sandals, a BOGO teaser marked "Coming Soon" — also women's — and then a whole kids' section. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for something cushioned I can slip on for a walk. I'm not sure I exist in this email.
- The $10 off $74.98+ offer is right where it should be, and "Sun's Out Sandals On!" is at least on-brand for the season. But after that, the creative team built this one for a mom buying jellies for herself and sandals for her kids. That's fine — she's probably a customer too — but I'm left scanning for a men's section that never comes.
- Mark as read. Might revisit if I'm running low on sandal options, but I'm not clicking anything here today. Wouldn't forward it to a friend either — the guys I know are looking for the same kind of comfort-fit men's summer shoes I am, and this email doesn't deliver that.

## What's working

- **$10 off is front and center.** The coupon code banner is the first thing I see — no digging required. That's how you do it.
- **"Sun's Out Sandals On!" is punchy and seasonal.** Didn't need much brainpower to understand what's on sale and why. July sandals message lands right.
- **$14.98 sandal blowout price is visible.** A real number I can evaluate immediately — not just "big savings."
- **No clutter at the top.** The offer and hero are cleanly laid out. Text is readable without zooming on my phone, which I can't always say about Shoe Carnival.

## What's weak

- **No men's section visible anywhere.** Jellies, women's sandals, BOGO for women, kids — the word "men's" does not appear in anything I can see. Nine consecutive emails and they haven't figured out I'm a 62-year-old man.
- **"Coming Soon" BOGO is a tease, not an offer.** You're telling me about a deal I can't act on today. That's frustrating, not exciting.
- **The preview text is a raw tracking URL.** Before I even opened this, my inbox showed me "Shoe Carnival https://click.email.shoecarnival.com/?qs=..." — that looks like spam and tells me nothing.
- **Jelly sandal trend section is irrelevant to me.** Jellies are a women's category. Showing me three pairs of translucent women's sandals as the email's trend content assumes I'm not the target, which I'm not, apparently.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show me men's sandals.** One hero block — Skechers Foamies, HOKA Ora Recovery Slides, Crocs — anything cushioned in men's sizing. That's the single biggest lift for a subscriber like me. If the email had a men's section below the women's, I'd have scrolled to it.
- 2. **Fix the preheader immediately.** A tracking URL in the preview slot destroys inbox credibility before the email is even opened. Write one real sentence about the offer.
- 3. **Put a deadline on the $10 off.** "Ends Sunday" or "3 days only" gives me a reason to act now rather than flag it for later and forget.
- 4. **Make the BOGO real or cut it.** "Coming Soon" in a promotional email is a dead spot. Either launch the offer or save the real estate for something I can buy today.

## Technical Audit — Shoe Carnival "Summer = Sandals & Savings"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Table-based HTML 4.01 Transitional email with four responsive breakpoints and two emltrk.com open-tracking pixels. No blocking issues; three accessibility gaps and one potential merge-token risk warrant attention before future sends.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 click-redirect links** routed through Shoe Carnival's ESP click-tracker — QA probes were skipped for these, so destination health is unverified. Recommend periodic spot-checks on final landing URLs, especially for time-sensitive offers.

**Open tracking pixels:**
- `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260709_bogof_kids_AM​` — UTM parameter appended to an open-pixel URL. This is unusual; UTM parameters on tracking pixels don't fire GA/analytics (pixels aren't clicked). Likely harmless, but suggests a copy-paste error in the template.
- `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` — contains an unreplaced merge token `[UNIQUE]`. **If this token is not substituted at send time, every open event resolves to the same subscriber ID**, corrupting open-rate uniqueness in the ESP. Verify ESP is replacing this token before delivery.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — content image:**
```
src: https://image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/465aca37-a584-4d52-a5a1-61002d176bb3.png
```
This is a named content image (not a tracker). Missing `alt=""` means screen readers skip it silently and images-off rendering shows a broken placeholder with no fallback label.

**Missing alt text — tracking pixels:**
Both emltrk.com pixels lack `alt=""`. Tracking pixels should have `alt=""` (empty string, not absent), which explicitly marks them decorative and suppresses screen reader announcement.

**Dark mode:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">` and `supported-color-schemes: light` — no dark mode variant declared. On Apple Mail dark mode, unstyled white backgrounds may invert unexpectedly. Not a bug, but a known rendering gap.

**Four separate `<style>` blocks** in `<head>` with overlapping breakpoints (320px, 450px, 600px, 660px). Some declarations are duplicated across blocks (e.g., `.pad20`, `.photo`). No rendering breakage observed, but consolidating would reduce parse overhead and template maintenance risk.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Active risk:** `[UNIQUE]` in the second tracking pixel URL (see §2). No other visible merge tokens in the truncated source, but the full template should be scanned for any `[...]` or `{{...}}` patterns before send.

No subscriber-level personalization (first name, loyalty tier, etc.) detected in the visible source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Sending domain:** `shoecarnival@email.shoecarnival.com` — dedicated ESP subdomain. SPF/DKIM alignment depends on ESP configuration for this subdomain; not verifiable from HTML alone.

**Truncated source** prevents confirming physical mailing address and unsubscribe link presence — both required under CAN-SPAM. QA summary shows 0 compliance failures, suggesting these elements exist in the non-truncated footer.

No issues found in the visible HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Campaign ID mismatch:** The open-pixel UTM value is `utm_campaign=SCA_20260709_bogof_kids_AM` (BOGO, kids). Subject line reads "Summer = Sandals & Savings" with no kids or BOGO framing. If the 26 click links carry a different `utm_campaign` value than what lands on the destination page, attribution will split across campaign IDs. Recommend verifying click-link UTMs match the campaign slug and that the landing page `utm_campaign` echoes `SCA_20260709_bogof_kids_AM` (or a consistent alias).
