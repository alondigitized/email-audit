# You forgot your 15% OFF Welcome Offer! 🛍️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-01**
## Executive summary

- I'll be straight with you — I'm a Skechers guy, not a Crocs guy. But I signed up for their list at some point and they shot me this welcome offer. The 15% off caught my attention right away, that's fair. But the minute I opened it, I felt like I was looking at an email meant for someone twenty years younger than me. Big hero image of two young folks sitting around, clogs in pastel colors, and a bunch of lifestyle stuff that doesn't speak to me at all. I almost closed it before I even got to the offer redemption instructions. And finding those was a chore too — small text, a lot to scroll through. My thumb got tired.
- The discount is real and useful, but the presentation and targeting miss me badly. I'd probably let it sit in my inbox unopened if this came through on a normal day.

## What's working

- **The 15% headline is bold and front-and-center.** That gets my attention. I'm price-conscious and "15% Off" in big letters right at the top — I see that before anything else.
- **The best-selling classic section with the clog** is straightforward. I can see it's a recognizable product, simple shot, clear stars/rating treatment. No fuss.
- **Afterpay callout** — I don't personally use buy-now-pay-later, but I appreciate that they spell out "Buy Now, Pay Later" in plain English underneath the logo. That's the kind of thing I can actually understand without needing to look it up.
- **App download section with QR code** — that's handy if I ever did want to use their app. Easy enough to understand what it does.

## What's weak

- **The hero image doesn't speak to me.** Two young people sitting in what looks like a dark performance venue. That's not my world. I want to see someone my age wearing comfortable footwear doing something I'd actually do — walking the dog, running errands, relaxing on the patio.
- **Text is tiny in the body sections.** The "Redeem Your Offer Now" block and the Membership Perks column — I had to really squint. On my phone I'd need to zoom in. That's a friction point for me every time.
- **Pastel color grid of clogs** — the product carousel near the top is mostly pinks, blues, tans. Nothing that screams "men's." I don't see a shoe in there that I'd look twice at.
- **Too much stuff to scroll through.** Membership perks, best-selling classic, Charming Additions section, Afterpay, app download — I'm tired before I get to the offer. For me, get in, show me the deal, let me decide.
- **"Charming Additions" section** — I see some kind of charms/Jibbitz accessories. That is absolutely not for me. Might as well be invisible.
- **The preview text was garbage** — when this arrived in my inbox I saw "Crocs" and then a bunch of nonsense spaces. That almost made me skip the whole thing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a men's product shot** — even one image of a men's Classic Clog or sandal in a neutral/earthy colorway would signal that this email is for me too.
- 2. **Bigger fonts throughout** — specifically in the redemption instructions and the perks section. If I have to pinch-zoom, I'm done.
- 3. **Trim the scroll depth** — cut it to three modules max: the offer, one product, and the CTA. Save the Jibbitz and app download for a separate send.
- 4. **Fix that preview text** — it's supposed to be my second hook after the subject line. Right now it's invisible noise.
- 5. **One clear CTA button** — "Shop Now & Save 15%" — and put it higher up, right after the offer explanation, not buried mid-scroll.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Crocs Welcome 15% Off Reminder

### 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Demandware/demdex open pixel, `click.crocs-email.com` redirect domain). Eight image assets are served over plain HTTP and one heading-area merge token appears to have rendered empty, producing a broken sentence fragment.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text version is URL-saturated.**
QA measured 12,917 of 16,330 characters (79%) as URLs. Spam filters penalize plain-text parts that are nearly all URLs with no prose; this is a deliverability risk for ISPs that weight the text/plain part.

**35 click-redirect links skipped.**
All CTAs route through `click.crocs-email.com/?qs=…`. QA could not probe destinations. UTM parameter presence on final landing URLs is unverified (see §6).

**No issues** with the redirect domain itself (standard SFMC click-tracking pattern).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (6 assets).**
The following images are served over `http://` and will be blocked or proxied by Gmail, Apple Mail, and most corporate mail clients that enforce HTTPS:

| Asset | Filename fragment |
|---|---|
| App Store badge | `7462caab-b5fb-4f36-ad57-598effe0e45e.png` |
| Google Play badge | `f744a0ca-a299-4bd9-a0dc-e259d2d5f780.png` |
| Unidentified image | `3bf2b026-9650-4bab-a555-717c3e4cbe36.png` |
| Instagram icon | `072197b5-fcef-4784-809b-3453b8b7abea.png` |
| TikTok icon | `ddc4ffa6-81a7-4dda-b456-d3314351e7d0.png` |
| YouTube icon | `1ba1d5d1-3ccc-4ae1-9d8a-a7e54e1b9bb4.png` |
| Facebook icon | `92f76fa0-21d5-4955-9faa-763e761e496a.png` |

All are on the same CDN (`image.crocs-email.com`) — the HTTPS path for that host works (other images in the email use it). This is a build/template misconfiguration.

**Missing alt text (2 images).**
- Open/tracking pixel: `demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?…` — has no `alt` attribute. Should be `alt=""` (empty string) so screen readers ignore it.
- Unidentified decorative image: `3bf2b026-…png` — no alt text.

**`<meta charset>` tag malformed.**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Correct form:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
Without `http-equiv`, some older Outlook/Lotus clients may ignore the charset declaration.

**`@import` web font.**
```css
@import url("https://use.typekit.net/vqb8ega.css");
```
Gmail and Outlook strip `@import` at render time. The font (proxima-nova) will never load in the two highest-volume clients. This is a known limitation rather than a bug, and the inline `font-family:'proxima-nova', sans-serif` fallback handles degradation correctly — flagging for awareness only.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Empty merge token in headline.**
The heading renders:
```html
Take <span style="white-space:nowrap"></span>
```
The `<span>` is structurally consistent with a first-name personalization slot (e.g., `Take, {{first_name}},`) that resolved to an empty string. This produces the sentence fragment "Take 15% Off" rather than the likely intended "Take, [Name], 15% Off" (or similar). Confirm against the SFMC content block — if the token is `%%=v(@firstName)=%%` or equivalent, a null-guard is needed:

```
%%[ IF @firstName != "" THEN ]%% , %%[firstName]%%, %%[ ENDIF ]%%
```

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM physical address:** Not present in the truncated HTML. Confirmed absence would be a legal violation; verify the full template footer contains a valid postal address.

**Unsubscribe link:** Not visible in the provided HTML truncation. Standard for triggered welcome emails; must be confirmed present in the full source.

**Authentication headers:** Not assessable from HTML source alone. Recommend verifying SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass in MTA logs for `crocs-email.com`.

**d_dpuid in open pixel** is a hashed value — acceptable. No PII in plaintext in the tracking pixel.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 35 click links pass through `click.crocs-email.com` redirects. Because QA skipped probing these (correct behavior for redirect chains), UTM parameters on final destination URLs are unverified. Minimum expected params for a triggered welcome campaign:

```
utm_source=email&utm_medium=trigger&utm_campaign=welcome-15peroff-reminder
```

The `campaignID=us_x_trigger_welcome-15peroff_reminder` value in the open pixel matches the expected campaign slug — a good sign the send-time data is consistent, but landing page UTMs still need manual spot-check.
