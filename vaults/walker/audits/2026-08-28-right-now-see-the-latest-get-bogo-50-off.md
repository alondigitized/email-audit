# Right now, see the latest + get BOGO 50% off
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW playbook I've been getting all week — BOGO 50% off up top, then a wall of women's booties, clogs, heels, and leopard-print flats. I run through the same math I did on the 24th, 25th, 26th, and 27th: real deal, zero relevance to me. "Hey, what's new? Oh, just every single one of these styles" is a cute line but it's aimed at somebody browsing heels, not a guy who wants a slip-on walking shoe.
- There's an "athletic & sneakers" tile buried halfway down and a size grid that stops at 11, which at least tells me they're not totally ignoring men — but I have to scroll past a booze cruise of ankle boots and strappy sandals to find it. Back-to-school banner, a free shoe-spray promo, VIP rewards blurb — it's a kitchen sink of a newsletter, not a targeted send.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is the same email I've marked as read five times this week, just reshuffled.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly at the top, no fine-print hunting required.
- There's an "athletic & sneakers" category tile, so men's-adjacent stuff exists somewhere in here.
- Size grid (5–11) is at least visible without a login wall.
- Layout is clean, no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- Every hero product shot is a women's shoe — booties, clogs, kitten heels, leopard flats. Nothing says "this is for you, Walker."
- "Shop Women," "Shop New Arrivals," "Shop All Women" are the only real CTAs above the fold — I'd have to dig for a men's link.
- This is essentially the same BOGO email I got the 27th, reshuffled with different product photos. The cadence is wearing thin.
- Back-to-school banner and shoe-spray promo feel like filler tacked on to a generic blast, not anything built around my habits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out — if DSW has any purchase history on file showing I buy men's comfort shoes, send me a men's-only version of this BOGO email instead of the full women's catalog.
- 2. Move "Athletic & Sneakers" and any men's comfort/walking category up top, not buried two screens down.
- 3. Cut the reshuffle-and-resend cadence — I've seen this exact offer four times this week under slightly different subject lines.
- 4. Lead with a specific men's comfort product (something cushioned, slip-on) rather than a generic "what's new" grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort shoes, BOGO 50% off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your style: BOGO 50% off men's`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-on comfort, half off your second pair.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned. Casual. BOGO 50% off men's styles.`
