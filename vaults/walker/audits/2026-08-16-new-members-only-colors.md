# New Members-only colors
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Well, that's HOKA again — eighth email in about two weeks now — but at least this one's a different angle than the usual colorway blast. It's a "members-only" pitch: sign up free, get exclusive colors. The lead shoe is some trail runner in tan and navy that looks rugged enough, but the actual product grid underneath has a Kaha 3 Low GTX boot and a Transport 2 that at least look like something a guy my age could wear walking the dog.
- Problem is, I'm not a member and don't especially want to be. This isn't a "shop now" email so much as a "join our club so we can email you more" email, and after eight sends in two weeks I'm not exactly itching to sign up for a ninth channel of contact. The trail-running UTMB section and the "sign up for texts" pitch at the bottom just pile on more asks I didn't come for.
- I'd skim it, notice the Kaha boot for half a second, and mark it read. Not forwarding this one — no real offer here, just membership bait.

## What's working

- The Kaha 3 Low GTX boot actually looks like my kind of shoe — sturdy, not neon, not a trail-running costume.
- Clean layout, one shoe per row with a plain "Shop Now" link — easy enough to scan on my phone without squinting.
- No loud color explosions this time, which is a nice break from the last several HOKA sends.

## What's weak

- The whole email is a "join to unlock" pitch, not an actual offer — there's no discount, no price, nothing concrete for me to act on.
- Two of the three "members only" shoes are trail/running-focused (Speedgoat 7, Transport 2) — not my lane at all.
- Then it pivots to a UTMB trail-running festival in France and a "sign up for texts" pitch — none of that has anything to do with me.
- Eighth email from HOKA in two weeks and this is the one where they finally ask me to sign up for even more contact from them. That's a lot to ask.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an actual number — if "members-only" means a real discount, say the percentage in the subject and hero. Vague "exclusive colors" isn't a reason to click for someone my age.
- 2. Cut the Speedgoat 7 and Transport 2 from a "comfort dad" send, or at least lead with the Kaha boot since that's the one with real crossover appeal.
- 3. Drop the UTMB trail-festival block and the text-signup pitch entirely from this email — two different asks in one send dilutes both.
- 4. Ease up on frequency — eight emails in two weeks is starting to make me tune out the subject line before I even open it.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email is 60% pass rate with 3 broken (403) tracked links and a missing plain-text fallback; images throughout lack alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Three Cordial-wrapped tracking links return 403 when probed:
  - "Join for Free" → wraps `hoka.com/en/us/login/` (link ID `6a7e2f19a01ae743560e3cfe`)
  - "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/" → wraps `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` (link ID `...560e3d14`)
  - "Unsubscribe" → wraps `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe` (link ID `...560e3d17`)
- All three use the same redirector host `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...` with JWT-encoded `jwtH`/`jwtP`/`jwtS` params. A 403 on this host-level pattern (not just one link) suggests the redirector itself is rejecting the request — could be bot/UA filtering on the QA prober rather than a genuinely broken link for end users, but it should be verified with a real click test since the Unsubscribe link failing has compliance implications (see §5).
- 12 of 28 total links were probed (ranked by click likelihood); 16 footer/social/utility links were deprioritized and not verified.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images missing `alt` text, including all primary content images (`US_20260816_MembershipHeader_black.jpg`, `US_20260816_NewHero.jpg`, `US_20260816_MembersOnly_IMG2/3/4.jpg`, `US_20260816_MembersOnly_CTA2.jpg`, `US_20260811_BestSellers_IMG3.jpg`, `US_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`, `LogoFooterPrimary.png`) plus two 1x1-style tracking pixel images with base64/JWT-like `src` values (open/tracking pixels from `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`) — these two are tracking pixels and don't need alt text, but the 8 content images do, and their absence fails WCAG 1.1.1 for screen reader users.
- Broken/malformed CSS declarations present in the mobile media query: `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` — both have empty numeric values (`px` with no digit), which are invalid CSS and will be dropped by parsers, likely an unintended value substitution failure in the template.
- Standard email-client resets and MSO-safe table CSS are present and correctly structured.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) are visible in the truncated source. No issues found in the visible portion.
