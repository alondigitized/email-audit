# Psst...20% off ends tomorrow 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the fifth send this week with the same 20% off and code JINGLE. The subject says "ends tomorrow," and the fine print says the offer is valid October 22-25. So the deadline is real. That's the first thing in a week that gives me a reason to act. The green "20% OFF*" banner is huge and easy to read on my phone, and I'll give them that.
- Then the email loses me, like the others did. The big grid is almost all women's shoes: a mary jane, heels, a pointed flat, a tall boot, a mule. There's one gray chunky sneaker and one maroon lace-up that look like something I'd wear. I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for cushioned, easy-on shoes, and nothing here says "comfort" or "slip-on." The "BRAND NEW…AND SO YOU" headline isn't talking to me. I'd skim it and not click. I wouldn't forward it, since I've seen this one all week.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" banner is huge, and the code JINGLE is in plain sight. I get the offer in two seconds.
- "Ends tomorrow" matches the Oct 22-25 dates in the fine print, so the urgency is honest.
- The SHOP MEN link is right under the banner, so I can skip the women's grid if I look for it.
- The size row, with the wide width button, is easy to tap and useful for my feet.
- The barcode is handy if I'm standing in a store.

## What's weak

- Of the 15 shoes in the grid, about 13 are women's styles. That's the same problem as the last four emails.
- There's no comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-on messaging anywhere. The one chunky gray sneaker has no label or price.
- There are no prices, no reviews and no brand names under any shoe. I can't tell whether a shoe is worth it.
- The fine print says "regular-price styles only," and the exclusions list is long. I can't tell whether the sneaker brands I like qualify. Clearance and "Final Sale" are out.
- It's the fifth near-identical 20%/JINGLE email this week. The cadence is tiring.
- The fine print is tiny and I'd have to pinch to read it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's comfort row under the banner: cushioned walking shoes and slip-ons, with brand names and prices.
- 2. Put prices and a "now $X with code" line under each shoe.
- 3. Split the product grid by gender or let me choose, so I'm not scrolling past heels.
- 4. Cut the cadence. One send with a real countdown beats five reminders.
- 5. Add a short "which brands qualify" line near the banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off comfort walkers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `JINGLE ends tomorrow: 20% off men's slip-ons`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned sneakers and easy slip-ons, 20% off through Saturday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Regular-price styles only. Use code JINGLE before it ends.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent HTML template with one confirmed broken CTA, the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link, which returns HTTP 400. It also has a few markup and accessibility gaps. Authentication status could not be checked. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (confirmed):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA returned **400**. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/yBiEbXQoc6vtHPkfQw-0CA~~/AAQRxRA~/NrB3Xy-bp…`, a Braze click-tracking wrapper. The QA output shows an empty link-text field before the URL. The 400 could come from the probe, for example a missing user agent or a one-time token. It could also be a malformed or expired wrapped destination. Treat it as a real defect until a browser or mobile test shows otherwise.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 73 links were probed and 61 were skipped, so the other links are unverified.
- **Link weight:** every link is wrapped in a long `ablink.dsw.com` token, which is why the plain-text part is more than 70% URLs. That is 28,481 of 33,010 characters.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261024_P251273_V3_WM-_Offer_MI_B` is a second vendor's open pixel next to Braze's. It carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, tracked image:** `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/p0E7oRk-DRgi2NBDDNUEqQ~~/AAQRxRA~/uP-9Duyd…`. If this is content and not a pixel, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Missing alt text, `o.gif`:** this is a tracking pixel, so it should have `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Charset meta ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it isn't a valid charset declaration. It is also missing on `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`, which needs `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Charset sniffing then falls back to the HTTP headers.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom blocked:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta disables pinch-zoom. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Dark mode:** the `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` metas and the `prefers-color-scheme` rules are present. The truncated source gave no evidence of problems.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and the subject line (`Psst...20% off ends tomorrow 👀`) have no unrendered Liquid or `{{ }}` tokens. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found because the message came through the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are therefore **unverified**. This is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender fault.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the QA report says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it flagged no failure. The unsubscribe URL, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source or QA data, so they could not be confirmed.
- **Plain-text ratio:** the URL-heavy plain-text part is a mild spam-filter risk (see section 2).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverified:** every link goes through the `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, and the final destinations were not resolved in the QA data. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment therefore can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign ID:** the only visible campaign identifier is the `mi_ecmp` value on the pixel, `20261024_P251273_V3_WM-_Offer_MI_B`.
- **SMS opt-in CTA:** this is the one link with a confirmed failed landing (400), so continuity is broken for it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Open the "Opt-In to Texts" link in a real browser and on mobile. If it still fails, fix or regenerate the Braze link and the SMS opt-in endpoint.
2. Add `alt` text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image. Set `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
3. Change the metas to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Resolve the redirects for the top CTAs and check that UTMs reach the landing pages. Raise the probe coverage above 12 of 73 links.
6. Check SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` against the original message headers, not the relay copy.
7. Consider a shorter plain-text version with fewer tracked URLs.
