- BOGO 50% off is a clear, real number right at the top — no bait-and-switch headline this time.
- "Shop Men" is one tap away in that first button row, so I'm not totally locked out.

## What's weak

- "School Supply Stock-Up" and a kid model — I've got no kids in school and no reason to care about backpacks.
- Three of four "chapters" (fashion kicks, boots, clogs) are styled for a much younger, mostly female shopper.
- No mention of comfort, arch support, or cushioning anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to buy.
- Wall of text at the bottom (shipping, Afterpay, size chart) that I'd never scroll to.
