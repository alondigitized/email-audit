# New arrivals
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one's an eight-shoe grid dump with no offer to speak of. "New arrivals, just landed" — okay, but which one's for me? I see a lace-up shoe getting tied up top, decent enough, but then it's straight into eight little product tiles: Ellipse, 1080v15 Suede, SuperComp Elite v6, SuperComp Rebel, 9060, 204L, BB950, 327 — half of those are running racing flats and retro dad-shoe styles, not one word about cushioning or arch support to help me tell them apart.
- Then it pivots into "Performance running essentials" with two twentysomethings in bike shorts and running gear, which tells me straight off this send isn't for me — I'm not doing tempo runs, I'm walking the dog and standing at the grill. Back to school, kids' new arrivals, women's new arrivals — this is a everybody-gets-the-same-blast email, not something built for a guy my age looking for a comfortable walking shoe.
- No discount, no price, nothing that tells me why today's the day to buy. I'd scroll past this one and mark it read without clicking anything. Not forwarding it — nothing here is worth passing along.

## What's working

- The hero shot of someone actually lacing up a real shoe is a decent opener — feels authentic, not staged.
- Clean grid layout, easy enough to scan on my phone without pinching to zoom.
- "Shop now" and "Visit a store" buttons are big, clear, and easy to tap.
- Free shipping banner up top ($75+) is at least a real, visible perk.

## What's weak

- No offer, no percent off, no dollar figure — just "new arrivals," which doesn't move me to buy anything.
- Eight shoes thrown at me with zero context on comfort, cushioning, or fit — I have no idea which of these is built for all-day wearing versus which is a racing shoe.
- The "Performance running essentials" section with two young models in workout gear has nothing to do with a guy my age who wants a comfortable walking shoe.
- It's a shotgun blast — men's, women's, kids', back-to-school all crammed in — doesn't feel like it's talking to me specifically.
- Compare to their August 20th "Bestsellers restocked" email — that one had one hero shoe front and center. This one buries everything in a grid with no lead product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero shoe — ideally something like the 9060 or a cushioned walking style — and lead with it big, the way the bestsellers email did, instead of an eight-tile grid.
- 2. Put an actual offer in there. "New arrivals" alone isn't a reason to click today.
- 3. Cut the "Performance running essentials" block entirely for a segment like me, or swap in comfort/lifestyle copy instead of a runner's outfit shot.
- 4. Add one line of fit info under each shoe — "cushioned," "wide-fit friendly," "arch support" — something that helps me pick without clicking through.
- 5. Segment this — send me the men's comfort styles, not the whole family catalog in one scroll.
