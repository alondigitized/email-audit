# Your cart expires soon!

## Executive summary

- Well, it's that pink floral D'Lites again — same one, same "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button, same story I've been telling for four days now. I've never clicked on that shoe, never browsed it, never so much as glanced at it, and Skechers is still convinced it's the thing standing between me and a purchase. It's the "AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG" line that gets me — no I wasn't wrong, because I never picked it in the first place.
- Buried down below the fold there's actually something I'd look at — a row of men's slip-ins, Arch Fit Arcade, Relaxan Fit Altman, real cushioned comfort shoes — but by the time I scroll past a giant floral sneaker and a full-width "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button I've already half-decided this email isn't for me. The 20% off banner for Plus members is honest and I like that it's spelled out with fine print right there, no games. But it's stuck two-thirds of the way down after a wall of stuff I didn't ask for.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm sure not forwarding this to anybody. Marking it read and moving on — same as the last three of these.

## What's working

- The men's slip-in row (Altman, Arch Fit Arcade) is exactly my lane — cushioned, slip-on, no laces to fuss with.
- The 20% off Plus member banner is clear and doesn't bury the terms — dates and conditions right there.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup options are useful if I ever do pull the trigger.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — nothing overlapping, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- Leading with a women's floral D'Lites I've never touched, for the fourth time this week — it's not a nudge anymore, it's noise.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" implies I have a cart going, which I don't — feels like a guilt trip, not a real reminder.
- My relevant products (men's slip-ins) are stuck below the fold, competing with a shoe that has nothing to do with me.
- No "Hi, Walker," no points balance up top — this one skipped the personal touch that the good sends from Skechers have had this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop showing me the pink floral D'Lites — swap the hero for whatever I've actually looked at, or default to a men's comfort bestseller if there's no browse history.
- 2. Move the 20% off Plus banner to the top, above the fold — that's the one thing that'd actually get me to open my wallet.
- 3. Drop "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" language when there's no real cart behind it — it reads as a lie, and I've caught it four times now.
