- The 25% off strip with "$125+" is real money off, though it's easy to miss.

## What's weak

- The hero is a young woman in leggings with a shoe the size of my thumb. Nothing in it says it's for a 62-year-old man.
- The six shoes are each labeled only "327." No prices, no sizes, no colors named, and no word on comfort or fit.

- 3. Swap the leggings and bras for men's walking or slip-on picks. Keep the "Shop kids" part short.
- 4. Spread out the sends. Tell me what's different about this one instead of repeating the same sale.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several leftover-template defects in its CSS and markup: a placeholder background URL, `@font-face` rules with no `src`, and a malformed meta tag. It also has three images with no `alt` attribute and a plain-text part that is about 87% URLs. The HTML supplied is truncated and the QA run found no hard failures, so the sections below cover only what that data confirms.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Placeholder URL in CSS:** `.bgwhite {… background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` points to a non-existent host over plain HTTP. If `.bgwhite` is applied, clients will request a dead URL, and some will flag mixed content. Remove the rule or replace it with a real HTTPS asset.
- **Coverage gap:** The QA probed only 12 of 37 links, and the other 25 were skipped as footer, utility or social links. No broken links were reported among the probed ones. The skipped 25 are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,895 of 13,715 characters (about 87%) are URLs. That is a spam-filter risk and a poor experience for text-only readers. It is most likely long tracking-redirect URLs pushing out the text content.
- **Tracking pixel:** `…/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12442434_1106121_23455095.gif` is the open-tracking pixel. It is on the same `link.email.newbalance.com` subdomain as the other assets, which is correct. See section 3 for its missing `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes** (QA-confirmed):
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `egR1f966kc_…gif` (the tracking pixel). Use `alt=""` since it is decorative.
  - The two shipping images look informational. With images blocked, that content disappears.
- **Dead font declarations:** Every `@font-face` block (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and others) has no `src` descriptor. They cannot load a font, so text renders in the fallbacks (`Arial`, `Times New Roman`). Several also use an invalid comma-separated family value, such as `font-family: linotype-didot,serif;`. `ITC Garamond Book Condensed` is unquoted. In email clients that support web fonts, the font stack is not delivering what it appears to.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Conflicting doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an `xmlns` attribute on `<html>` (XHTML-style). It is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Duplicate selector:** `.bg1` is defined twice (`#ffffff`, then `#58546d`). The later rule wins, so the white background is silently overridden.
- **Dark-mode link contrast:** The dark-mode block sets `body {background:#151415 !important;} a {color:#cf0a2c !important;}`. By my calculation, `#cf0a2c` on `#151415` is about 3.2:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 threshold for normal text.
- **Leftover title:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is a generic template title. This is minor, but it shows the template wasn't cleaned up.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the supplied HTML. The only placeholder is the `XXXXX` image URL noted in section 2. The truncated body can't confirm anything beyond that.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found. The QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. This is a limit of the capture path, not a confirmed defect in the email.
- **Unsubscribe:** The QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in link probing, and no failure was reported. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address aren't in the supplied data, so I can't confirm them.
- **Sender alignment:** The From domain is `email.newbalance.com` and the assets are on `link.email.newbalance.com`. Both sit under the same organizational domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. The truncated source exposes no CTA hrefs, UTM parameters or landing pages, so I can't assess this section. No probed link failures were reported.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Delete or fix the `url('http://image.XXXXX.png')` rule in `.bgwhite`.
2. Add `alt` text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking GIF.
3. Either add real `src` descriptors to the `@font-face` rules (with a fallback strategy) or remove them. Fix the invalid family syntax.
4. Fix the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Remove the duplicate `.bg1` rule and set a real `<title>`.
5. Change the dark-mode link color to something that reaches at least 4.5:1 against `#151415`.
6. Trim the plain-text part so it has more readable copy and fewer long tracking URLs.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers. That would let the QA check authentication results, `List-Unsubscribe`, UTM parameters and the 25 unprobed links.
