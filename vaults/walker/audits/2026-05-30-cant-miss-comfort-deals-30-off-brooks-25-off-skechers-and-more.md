# Cant-Miss Comfort Deals: $30 off Brooks, 25% off Skechers and More
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll give them this much — "Brooks" and "Skechers" in the same subject line is the fastest way to get me to stop scrolling. Those are exactly the two brands I've been going back and forth on for my next pair, and putting $30 off the Ghost 17 front and center in the body is smart. The CANT-MISS COMFORT DEALS hero section landed right where I needed it to.
- That said, this is somewhere around the eleventh email from Shoe Carnival in ten days. Yesterday they told me it was "FINAL DAY." Today here we are again with another "LIMITED TIME" countdown timer. At some point "limited" stops meaning anything. And if I'd checked my inbox preview before opening, all I'd have seen was a broken tracking URL sitting right after the sender name — not exactly a confidence builder.
- Still — the actual content inside the email is more on-target for me than most of what I've been getting from this brand lately. The Brooks Ghost 17 and Skechers Slip-Ins are real shoes I'd consider buying. I'd click the Brooks section. I would not forward this to a friend because the brand-fatigue feels palpable by now.

## What's working

- **Subject line named both of my brands** — "Brooks" and "Skechers" in the same line is precisely targeted at how I actually shop; I slowed down on this one.
- **$30 off Brooks Ghost 17 is a real number** — not a percentage game, a concrete dollar amount on a shoe I've been watching.
- **Skechers Slip-Ins call-out is perfect for me** — hands-free, no laces, exactly the comfort-first slot I'm shopping. Good placement in the grid.
- **Shoe Perks $10 reward visible** — I don't let points drive the decision, but seeing the reward block reminds me the loyalty program is working; it feels like the brand knows me a little.
- **Clean render, no broken images** — everything loaded, nothing overlapped, easy to read on a small screen.

## What's weak

- **Preview text is a raw tracking URL** — in my inbox this rendered as "Shoe Carnival https://click.email.shoecarnival.com/?qs=ABB7…" which looks like a tech malfunction; it made me hesitate before opening.
- **"FINAL DAY" yesterday, new promo today** — the "LIMITED TIME" countdown banner has zero credibility when the last email said it was over. I notice things like that.
- **Hey Dude section is not for me** — younger brand, nothing about comfort or support; it's just noise at my age.
- **Afterpay block feels off-demographic** — I'm not splitting a $100 shoe into four payments. This space could have highlighted arch support, width options, or return policy instead.
- **Model doesn't match my peer group** — the Brooks section features a younger man, probably 30s. Doesn't make me feel like this was built with me in mind.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — the broken URL in the inbox preview is the single biggest drag on open rate. Replace with something like "Brooks Ghost 17 drops $30. Skechers Slip-Ins 25% off. Today only."
- 2. **Retire the countdown timer until there's a real deadline** — yesterday was "FINAL DAY." The timer now reads as theater. A specific date ("Ends Sunday at midnight") would recover credibility fast.
- 3. **Swap Hey Dude slot for a comfort-focused brand** — Hoka, New Balance, or Crocs would speak directly to a buyer who opened this email for the comfort angle.
- 4. **Replace Afterpay block with fit/comfort proof points** — wide widths, arch support callout, easy return policy — the stuff that actually closes the deal for someone like me.
- 5. **Add one CTA with a brand name in the button copy** — "Shop Brooks Ghost 17" beats "SHOP NOW" every time; I know exactly where I'm going.
