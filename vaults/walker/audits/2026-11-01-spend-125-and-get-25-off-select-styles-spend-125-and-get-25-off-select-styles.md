- The offer itself is plain: 25% off select styles when I spend $125 or more. That's the same promo as 10/29, now with a spend threshold. The word "select" is doing a lot of work, and the fine print says "exclusions apply." The down-page products are better. ## What's working

- The headline is big, easy to read, and states the deal: 25% off when I spend $125.
- There's one black "Shop now" button. I can see it and it's wide enough to tap.
- The 468 Slip On is hands-free, which is exactly what I look for.
- The Fresh Foam X 880v15 is a cushioned everyday shoe I'd actually consider.
- The "Walking" link in the footer is a nice nod to how I shop.
- The price tiers (Under $50, $50-$100, Over $100) help me shop by budget.

## What's weak

- The hero is shopping bags again. It shows no shoe, no person, and nothing about comfort.
- The $125 minimum is a hurdle for a one-pair buyer. A pair of 880s might not get me there without adding socks.
- The "select styles" wording and the paragraph of fine print make me wary. I can't tell if the shoes I want are included.
- The price tiers sit in gray boxes with no products attached. They look like buttons but don't say what's behind them.
- The product names have no prices, no sale prices, and no width or arch-support details. I can't do my price-versus-comfort math.
- The footer text is tiny, and I'd have to squint at it on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the bag photo with the 880v15 or the 468 Slip On on a man's foot, with the offer in the top line.
- 2. Show sale prices under each product, and say whether it's included in the 25%.
- 3. Lower the threshold, or show how a single pair plus socks reaches $125.
- 4. Add a "Walking & comfort" row with cushioning, arch support and width options.
- 5. Make the fine print shorter and the type larger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's QA run had 0 failures and 2 warnings (75% pass rate). The source has several template-hygiene defects: a placeholder background URL, a placeholder `<title>`, conflicting CSS rules, and a subject line with its text repeated. The HTML was truncated, so compliance and UTM checks could only be partly done.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 27 were skipped. No broken links were reported among the probed set. The skipped links, mostly footer and social, are unverified.
- **Placeholder asset URL:** `.bgwhite { background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png'); }` is a leftover template placeholder.
  - It is non-HTTPS and will 404 wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.
  - Mixed-content blocking can also trigger.
- **Unlabeled tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12530681_498630_23708900.gif` is an open-tracking pixel with no alt text. It is harmless to rendering, but empty `alt=""` is the correct form.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, from QA):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking GIF above
  - The two shipping images are content images, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,634 of 13,392 characters (87%) are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold. The plain-text alternative is mostly raw tracking links and is hard to read.
- **Placeholder title:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is shipped as-is. Some clients and screen readers expose it.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Conflicting CSS:**
  - `.bg1` is defined twice, first as `#ffffff` and later as `#58546d`. The later rule wins, so the light-mode background may not be white.
  - The dark-mode block also sets `.bg1 { background:#151415 !important }`.
- **Fonts:** every `@font-face` rule (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and others) has no `src`. They cannot load from this stylesheet, so rendering depends on a webfont link elsewhere (not visible in the truncated source) or falls back to Arial and Times New Roman.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is paired with an `xmlns` attribute and self-closing tags, which is inconsistent. It is mostly tolerated by clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Duplicated subject text:** the subject reads "Spend $125 and get 25% off select styles, spend $125 and get 25% off select styles". It looks like a subject and preheader, or a dynamic-content variable, rendered twice. The cause is not provable from the data given.
- **Merge tokens:** none were visible in the truncated source, and no unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were reported by QA.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, because the AgentMail relay strips or doesn't expose the header. This is a measurement gap, not a confirmed failure. The sender is `newbalance@email.newbalance.com`, and the click-tracking domain is `link.email.newbalance.com`, a subdomain of the same sending domain.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was provided. The footer and `List-Unsubscribe` headers are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, because all links go through the `link.email.newbalance.com` redirector and the destination URLs are not exposed in the data provided. UTM presence cannot be confirmed.
- **Landing page alignment:** the probed destinations are not in the data, so I can't confirm that the page matches the "spend $125, get 25% off select styles" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove or replace the `http://image.XXXXX.png` background in `.bgwhite`. If a background image is needed, use an HTTPS URL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking GIF.
3. Fix the subject-line duplication at the template or ESP variable level.
4. Set a real `<title>`, correct the `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` meta tag, and remove the duplicate `.bg1` rule.
5. Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules, or drop them and rely on the linked webfont.
6. Trim the plain-text version's URLs, for example by dropping tracking parameters or collapsing repeated footer links.
7. Capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and the full HTML in the next audit run. Then SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer address and UTM tagging can be verified.
