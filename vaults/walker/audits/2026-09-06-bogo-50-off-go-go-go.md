# BOGO 50% OFF! GO, GO, GO >>>
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Same BOGO 50% off deal, now for the sixth or seventh time this week — "Long-Weekend Stock-Up" is just this week's costume for it, "COMING IN HOT" they say, but I've seen this exact promo dressed up as trend reports, fan faves, and new arrivals since Tuesday. I scroll through the whole thing — loafers, mary janes, mules, wedge booties, heeled sandals, platform clogs — and count maybe two pairs total that a 62-year-old guy could put on his feet without getting funny looks. No men's category shown anywhere in the visual spread, even though "SHOP MEN" sits right there in the top nav.
- This one goes straight to read-and-delete. Nothing here speaks to arch support, cushioning, or slip-on comfort — it's a women's fashion catalog with a men's link buried at the top I'd have to go hunting for. Wouldn't forward it, wouldn't click past the nav bar.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off headline is clear and impossible to miss right up top.
- "SHOP MEN" is at least present in the top nav, so I'm not completely locked out.
- "Under $50" and "Under COMP. VALUE" price call-outs give me an honest sense of value before I even click.
- Size grid and wide-width/wide-calf options near the bottom are a nice touch for anyone unsure of fit.
- Loyalty perks (free shipping for VIPs, birthday gift, 15% off text signup) are laid out clean at the bottom.

## What's weak

- Every single product shot — loafers, mary janes, mules, sandals, booties — is women's. Not one men's shoe in the whole visual scroll.
- I've seen this identical BOGO 50% off deal wrapped in a new headline nearly every day this week; the novelty's worn off completely.
- No cushioning, arch support, or comfort-tech language anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to click.
- Six or seven "SHOP X" black buttons stacked down the page is a lot of scrolling with nothing for me until the very end.
- Subject line "GO, GO, GO >>>" with all the arrows feels like a used-car lot ad, not something from a shoe store I trust.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this — if DSW knows I've clicked men's before, swap the hero grid to men's comfort styles instead of making me scroll past seven women's collections.
- 2. Cut the promo cadence. Sending the same BOGO deal daily under a new name is training me to ignore all of them, mine included.
- 3. Add one comfort-forward line for guys like me — arch support, all-day cushioning — even a single callout box would beat zero.
- 4. Drop the "GO GO GO" urgency gimmick — a real deadline date reads more credible than exclamation-arrow spam.
- 5. Move "SHOP MEN" up near the hero button, not just buried in the nav strip.
