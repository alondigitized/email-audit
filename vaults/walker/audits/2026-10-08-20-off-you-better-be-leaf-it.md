# 20% OFF?! You better be-leaf it 🍁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this is a calmer one after the BOGO barrage. The giant white "20% OFF*" on green is impossible to miss, even on my phone without my readers. I didn't have to squint, and I appreciate that. The code HAYRIDE is right there too.
- Then I scroll and the fine print catches me. "Regular-price styles only." After a week of BOGO 50% off, which ended yesterday, a 20% off that excludes sale items feels like a step down. The product tiles say "Extra 20% off reg. price!", which is the same offer repeated in tiny red text. Everything shown is suede boots and Mary Janes. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for cushioned, slip-on, arch-supported shoes, and nothing here says comfort to me.
- Would I click? Maybe "Shop Men," since it's right under the banner. Would I forward it? No. It's a mark-as-read unless I happen to need shoes this week.

## What's working

- The huge "20% OFF*" is readable at a glance. This is the best use of large type I've seen from them.
- The "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is black on green and easy to tap. The code HAYRIDE is spelled out, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- Shop Women, Men, Kids and Everything links sit right under the offer, so I can jump to Men quickly.
- The "Shop by Size" grid has big numbers and "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons. Wide width matters to a man my age.
- The barcode for in-store use is handy.

## What's weak

- Not one men's shoe appears in the product grid. It's all women's boots and flats. The email treats me like a stranger.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning or arch support. No brand names I'd shop for, like Skechers, Hoka, Brooks or New Balance, show up in the images.
- The 20% is regular-price only, with the details buried in the dark fine print. After the BOGO run, this feels thin.
- The "DSW On Location: Montana" block is style inspo for trendsetters, not for me.
- The product tiles' red "Extra 20% off" text is small and light. The sizes list stops at 11, and I can't tell it covers men's sizes.
- It's the seventh DSW email in a few days. I'm tired of them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Show men's comfort and walking shoes to men, with brand names and cushioning callouts, instead of suede boots.
- 2. State the exclusions up top: "20% off regular-price styles." Don't make me find it in the fine print.
- 3. Cut the repeated "Extra 20%" captions. Put a price and a star rating under each shoe instead.
- 4. Space out the sends. After six BOGO emails, a new offer needs a day of quiet first.
- 5. Replace the Montana block with a "Comfort Picks" row for slip-ons and walkers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall comfort sale: 20% off walking & slip-on shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code HAYRIDE through Oct. 11 — regular-price styles, in stores and online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's SMS opt-in CTA failed its link probe with HTTP 400, which is the only confirmed functional defect. The HTML also has malformed `<meta>` declarations, no `lang` attribute, and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The source is truncated, so the footer and headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned **400**.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/2gJxVHzI671vJy_cXLOokw~~/AAQRxRA~/JRmQyHl_9GDuYGizohg-…~~`
  - It is a wrapped redirect with a very long encoded payload (~700 chars).
  - A 400 on a tracked redirect can mean an expired or invalid token, or the click-tracker rejecting the probe's user-agent. A real client may behave differently, so verify manually with a real client or seed inbox before treating it as a customer-facing failure.
- **Plain-text/URL ratio (WARN):** 25,445 of 29,711 characters (~86%) are URLs. The long `ablink.dsw.com` wrappers inflate this and can hurt spam scoring and text-part readability.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 69 links were probed and 57 were skipped. Link health for the rest is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (WARN), two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/QxPRZI4JTwQcX2cjyU2ehA~~/AAQRxRA~/QJIFNJaR…` (the `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel).
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…` (a Movable Ink pixel).
  - Both look like tracking pixels. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Meta tags without `http-equiv`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute. Neither declaration takes effect. Charset then depends on the HTTP/MIME `Content-Type` header.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Web fonts:** the `@font-face` rules load `.woff` and `.ttf` files from `braze-images.com`. Verify the fallback font stacks, since most clients ignore web fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated body limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- No compliance failures were reported by QA, and the unsubscribe link is always included in probing.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:** the footer (physical address, unsubscribe URL), `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. The source is truncated and no headers were supplied.
- The From address uses the dedicated subdomain `e.dsw.com`, and links use the aligned `ablink.dsw.com` branded tracking domain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every inspected link is wrapped in the `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, so final destinations and UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML.
- The Movable Ink pixel carries a campaign identifier (`mi_ecmp=20261008_P261379_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…`), so campaign attribution exists on that channel.
- The probe didn't report landing-page URLs or redirect chains, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or re-validate the SMS opt-in link.** Re-test it from a real client. If it still returns 400, regenerate the tracked link or point it at a direct, working opt-in URL. It is a primary conversion CTA.
2. Add `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels.
3. Correct the meta tags: use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Shorten tracked URLs, or make sure the plain-text part has real content, to bring the URL ratio down.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers to check the unsubscribe footer, `List-Unsubscribe`, authentication results, and UTM parameters on final destinations.
