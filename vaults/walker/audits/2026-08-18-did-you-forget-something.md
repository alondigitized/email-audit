# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Same one again — that pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sneaker, still sitting under a "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner like I've got something in a cart. I don't. I never picked it up, never sized it, and it's not even close to what I'd wear. That's at least six times now since the 11th, and it's getting to be background noise I scroll past without reading.
- Below the fake-cart shoe there's actually a decent men's grid — a couple of boy's sneakers, a basketball shoe, and two Slip-ins that catch my eye more than anything else in this email — plus a real "up to 40% off summer styles" banner. That's the stuff I'd look at if it led the email instead of being buried under a made-up checkout reminder.
- I'm not clicking "Complete Your Order" for a shoe I never ordered. I might scroll far enough to notice the Men's Slip-ins, but honestly at this point I'm reaching for delete before I even get there. Not forwarding this to anybody.

## What's working

- The 40% off summer styles banner is a real, specific offer with a clear "Shop the Sale" link.
- There's a genuine Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit shoe in the grid — that's actually in my lane, comfort and slip-on.
- Big, legible fonts and buttons — easy to read on a phone without zooming.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup options are visible for anyone who cares about payment flexibility.

## What's weak

- The whole email is built around a fake "cart" that doesn't exist — I never selected this shoe, so the urgency is manufactured and it shows.
- Lead product is a pink floral women's sneaker, which has nothing to do with a 62-year-old guy's inbox — it's been repeated so many times this week it's just noise now.
- The best-fit product for me (Men's Slip-ins) is buried third row down instead of leading the email.
- Grid mixes boy's sneakers, a basketball shoe, and women's styles with zero segmentation — feels like a generic blast, not something aimed at me specifically.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake "cart abandonment" framing entirely for recipients who never actually added anything — lead instead with the 40% off sale and a men's comfort shoe up top.
- 2. Swap the hero product to the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit or Arch Fit style — that's the one that matches what I actually buy.
- 3. Cut the boy's/women's mixed grid down to a "Men's Picks" row when the recipient's browsing history skews that way.
- 4. Stop repeating this exact D'Lites - Spring Bloom shoe across six emails in a week — rotate creative even if the offer stays the same.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% Off Summer Styles — Men's Slip-ins Included`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Comfort Picks Are on Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ins, Arch Fit, and more men's styles — up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No cart, no problem — here's what's on sale for you`
