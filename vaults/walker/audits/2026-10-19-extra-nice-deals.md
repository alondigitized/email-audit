# ✨ Extra-nice deals ✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this one is a change from the BOGO barrage I've been getting all week. After nine BOGO emails in five days, I'll give them credit for trying something different. But "Extra-nice deals" with a woman in a red coat laughing and snakeskin boots is not talking to me. It's a women's holiday gift email. The only men's thing I can find is the "MEN" link in the nav bar.
- The UGG slipper at the top is the one thing that nearly caught me, because a slipper is comfort. "Now only $99.96" is a clear price. But it's tucked in a small banner above the real hero, and the shoe is shown on a woman's legs. Past that it's a long scroll of boots, flats, and slippers I'd buy for my wife, not me.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it, maybe tap the UGG, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. If my wife wanted gift ideas, maybe.

## What's working

- The UGG slipper at the top has a plain price, "$99.96", and a black "SHOP NOW" button I can find easily.
- Prices sit right on the photos ($39.99, $49.99, $59.99, $69.99), so I don't have to guess what things cost.
- The gift-price tiles (under $25, $50, $75) are simple and easy to tap.
- The "Shop by size" row with wide width and wide calf buttons is useful. Wide width matters to me.
- Changing the creative from the green BOGO banner is a relief after a week of the same email.

## What's weak

- The whole body is women's fashion. There's nothing for a 62-year-old man, and I see no men's shoes anywhere in the body.
- "Our kind of nice list" and "extra-nice prices" are cute, but they don't say what the deal is. There's no percent off and no clear offer.
- The shoe photos are tangled arms, legs, and clothing. I can't tell what the shoes look like, and none of the copy says cushioning, arch support, or slip-on.
- The size numbers (5 to 11) look like women's sizes. I'd need 10 to 13, so the size row feels like it isn't for me.
- Text in the product tiles and the fine print is tiny on a phone, and I'd have to pinch to read it.
- The pink "Shop holiday gifts" and "Shop all women" buttons are pale and low-contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Men who have browsed or bought men's comfort shoes should get a men's hero, such as a walking or slip-on shoe with cushion and arch support.
- 2. State the deal in the hero. "Nice list" is clever, but I need to know it's X% off or a price drop.
- 3. Show one shoe clearly per tile, on a plain background, with a short comfort callout.
- 4. Make the type bigger and the buttons darker and bolder, like the black ones lower down.
- 5. Add a men's size row (8 to 14).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts for him, from $39.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Slippers & cozy styles from $39.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `UGG slippers $99.96, plus men's and women's gift picks under $50`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned comfort for him and her, ready by the holidays`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a standard table-based Braze/SendGrid-style template, but the QA run found one failing CTA link (HTTP 400, "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off"). The HTML also has malformed `<meta>` tags and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. The QA pass rate was 62%, and only 12 of 58 links were probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] CTA returns 400:** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" links to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/59Yg5n8Z42C3Swq8MLAPNw~~/AAQRxRA~/wg31c3Tn…` (a very long, ~700-character tracking token). This is the SMS-signup CTA and is the only broken-experience finding.
  - The probe reported empty text after "Broken link (400):". The failure may come from the tracker rejecting a non-browser probe, or from an expired or malformed token. The data can't distinguish the two.
  - Treat it as a real issue until it is re-tested from a browser or a mobile user agent.
- **Coverage gap:** 46 of 58 links were skipped by design (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). Their status is unverified.
- **Redirect-wrapped links:** All links go through `ablink.dsw.com` click tracking. The final destinations and any UTM parameters can't be checked from the data given.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, both flagged by QA):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/wJ0X5Sow03JaPbY13lC4mw~~/AAQRxRA~/NC4FaBDpe8LK…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?…` is a tracking pixel, so it should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech.
  - The first image's role is unclear from the truncated data. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything. The charset declaration is ineffective. Clients that don't default to UTF-8 could garble the emoji in the subject or body.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Plain-text part:** 20,058 of 23,230 characters (86%) are URLs. The long tracking links bloat the text alternative to the point of being hard to read, and this can contribute to spam scoring.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` rules load from `braze-images.com` (woff/ttf) and are ignored by most clients. The truncated source doesn't show the fallback font stacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `${ }`, `%%…%%`) are visible in the truncated source. The tracking pixel carries a user ID (`mi_u=225090073`), which indicates the per-recipient ID was rendered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not exposing the header, so it is a verification gap and not a confirmed sender failure.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the physical postal address and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers can't be confirmed.
- **Sender:** `dsw@e.dsw.com` is a branded subdomain consistent with the tracking domains (`ablink.dsw.com`, `mi.dsw.com`).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable:** Links are wrapped by `ablink.dsw.com`, and the QA data gives no resolved destinations.
- **Campaign ID:** The pixel URL carries `mi_ecmp=20261019_P261530_V3_W-_Value_BAU_C`, so DSW tags campaigns with a custom `mi_` parameter rather than UTMs. Whether that parameter persists on the landing pages is unconfirmed.
- **SMS CTA:** The failing "Opt-In to Texts" link means the SMS landing page can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/59Yg5n8Z…`) in a real browser and on mobile. If it still returns 400, fix or regenerate the tracked link and re-send the template.
2. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
4. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`. Add meaningful alt text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image if it is visible content, or `alt=""` if it is a pixel.
5. Shorten the plain-text part by using a link-light text version, or fewer and shorter tracked URLs.
6. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from a direct-delivery copy, since the relay hides them, and confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` headers.
7. Next run, probe more of the 46 skipped links, or at least a sample of the footer links, and log each link's final URL to confirm tracking-parameter continuity.
