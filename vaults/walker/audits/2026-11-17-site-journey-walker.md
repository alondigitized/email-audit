# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-11-17**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I'm Walker, 62, shopping on my phone, and I couldn't buy a pair of shoes today. I never got a pair into the cart, and the cart page came up as a blank white screen. Shopping for comfort shoes was only half-easy, because the menu and the "Hands Free Slip-ins" and "Arch Fit" labels point me the right way. After that the site fell apart.

Four things stood out:
- **Add to Cart:** I tapped it with no size picked and got a red "Please select a size." The cart badge stayed at 0.
- **Cart:** The cart screenshot is completely blank white, with no items, no message and no way out.
- **Login:** The "Log In" step looks the same as the homepage. I saw no sign I was logged in, no name and no personalization.
- **Promo banner:** The banner shouts "BOGO 50% OFF" while the shoe I picked says "Excluded from promotions."

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10.** That's the same as yesterday's 3/10. The comfort-shopping signals are decent. But the funnel still ends in a dead end: nothing got into the cart, and the cart page rendered blank.

## 3. What's Working

- **The category chips are what I want.** The homepage strip shows "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit" and "Max Cus…" across the top. Those are my words.
- **The Men's Shoes page leads with the right tiles.** I see "Hands Free Slip-Ins," "Walking Shoes" and "Boots." The Filter, Sort by, Size and Width buttons are visible, and the "804 results" count tells me how big the pile is.
- **The menu is easy to use.** The drawer has big, readable bold text and a full-width row for each choice. "Comfort Technologies" is its own entry. The Back and X buttons are obvious.
- **The product page is readable.** The price ($155.00) is large. I can see the star rating, "100 Reviews," and Klarna and Afterpay payment options.
- **The size buttons are big.** They're easy to tap with a thumb. Sold-out sizes (12.5, 14.0) are crossed out with a bell for notifications, so I know what's available.
- **Search returned a lot.** "comfort shoes" gave 1,655 results, with colour dots and an "Also in Wide" tag.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Cart is blank (Step 8).** I can't tell whether my shoes are in there, whether the page broke, or what to do next. A man my age walks out when a page goes blank.
- **Add to Cart didn't add anything (Step 7).** The size error is correct behavior, but it's easy to miss. It sits above the button, in small red text. The page doesn't scroll to it or highlight the size grid. The badge still shows 0.
- **The promo story doesn't match (Steps 1 and 6).** "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart" runs across every page. Then the shoe I picked says "Excluded from promotions" in small orange text. My trust in the brand drops when the banner and the product page disagree.
- **The shoe I landed on isn't a comfort shoe pitch.** The product page I reached (Aero Burst, $155) shows price and payment plans first. I didn't see anything about cushioning, arch support, or how it compares with Hoka, Brooks or New Balance. I did not see a price-vs-quality case anywhere.
- **The homepage doesn't speak to me.** The hero is a holiday ad with a young couple, confetti and ornaments, labelled "AI-generated models." It's pretty, but no shoe and no comfort message is in sight. The "AI-generated models" label bothers me a bit.
- **Search results don't start with my shoe.** The first result is "Cypress - Chic Surge," a Women's black loafer. The second is a Men's Slip-ins style. The results aren't ordered for a man looking for comfort.
- **Small text.** The notices under the banner ("Limited Time Only! 25% OFF Work & Safety") and the "Men's" label on product pages are small. I'd have to pinch to zoom on some of it. The Filter, Size and Width buttons are also fairly small.
- **No personalization.** Steps 2 and 3 are identical to the homepage. I saw no login screen, no greeting and no "welcome back." The only way to know whether I'm logged in is to look at the little person icon, which looks the same either way.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the blank cart page first.** Show an empty-cart message, or the items, and a clear "Continue shopping" button. Make sure it loads quickly and doesn't depend on something that fails.
2. **Make the size error obvious.** Scroll to the size grid, outline it in red, and put the error next to the button. Better still, let "Add to Cart" show "Select size" until I pick one.
3. **Match the banner to the product.** Don't show "Applied at Cart" sitewide if many shoes are excluded. Tag each shoe "BOGO eligible" or "Not eligible" on the listing page, not after I've opened it.
4. **Lead the product page with comfort.** Put short lines near the price: "Arch support," "Cushioned," "Slip-in" or "Machine washable," plus a line on how it compares with Hoka or Brooks. A simple "Comfort rating" would help me more than the Afterpay line.
5. **Put a comfort shoe on the homepage hero.** Show a man's slip-in with "Hands-Free. Arch-Supported. All Day." Use real people.
6. **Make search smarter.** Default "comfort shoes" to the shopper's gender when known, and show the Men's and Wide results first.
7. **Make the logged-in state visible.** Show "Hi, Walker" or my initial in the header, and show past purchases or my usual size and width.
8. **Bump the text size.** Larger body text and bigger filter buttons would make this easier for a 62-year-old.

## 6. Bottom Line

The shelves are stocked with what I want. But I couldn't check out, the cart came up blank, the sale banner and the shoe page contradicted each other, and nothing told me the site knew who I was. A 62-year-old will go to Hoka's or Brooks's site long before debugging Skechers's cart. Score: **3/10**, same as yesterday.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

- **Scope:** 8 journey steps on a mobile viewport (the `--vh: 6.64px` value implies a ~664px-tall viewport). Axe reported 18 violations on 3 pages, the console logged 21 errors, and there was 1 network error.
- **Measurement gaps:**
  - LCP and CLS are `?` for every step, so no Core Web Vitals could be assessed. This is a test-harness gap, not a site finding.
  - The HTML samples for "Log In" and "Men category" show the URL `https://www.skechers.com/`, the same as the homepage. Either those steps did not navigate or the URL was captured before navigation. Their results may not reflect the intended pages.
  - Only 10 of the 21 console errors were provided.
- **Cart step:** `/cart/` returned **HTTP 429**. The View Cart axe violations (no `<title>`, no `lang`, no `<main>`, no `<h1>`) are what an error or rate-limit page typically looks like. The cart page was probably not exercised. Treat those four violations as unconfirmed for the real cart.
- **Most serious issues:** the homepage has two critical axe violations, and the page loads a heavy third-party tag stack.

## Accessibility

18 violations in total: Homepage 10, View Cart 4, Search 4.

**Homepage:**
- **critical `aria-required-parent`:** an ARIA role is used without its required parent (for example `listitem` or `tab` outside a `list` or `tablist`).
- **critical `button-name`:** one or more buttons have no accessible name, such as icon-only buttons with no `aria-label`.
- **serious `link-name`:** links have no discernible text, such as icon or image links without alt or label.
- **Landmark structure:**
  - `landmark-no-duplicate-main`: more than one `<main>`.
  - `landmark-main-is-top-level` and `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`: `<main>` and `<aside>` are nested inside other landmarks.
  - `landmark-unique`: landmarks lack distinguishing labels.
  - `region`: content sits outside any landmark.
- **moderate `page-has-heading-one`:** no `<h1>` on the page.
- **minor `aria-allowed-role`:** a role is set on an element that doesn't permit it.

**View Cart (likely the 429 response page):**
- serious `document-title`
- serious `html-has-lang`
- moderate `landmark-one-main`
- moderate `page-has-heading-one`

**Search "comfort shoes":**
- minor `aria-allowed-role`
- moderate `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`
- moderate `landmark-unique`
- moderate `region`

**Patterns across pages:**
- `aria-allowed-role`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region` appear on both Homepage and Search. They likely come from shared header, footer or template markup.
- The duplicate and nested `<main>` landmarks on the homepage suggest component-level `<main>` or `role="main"` usage.
- No violations were reported for Log In, Men, Men > Shoes, Product detail or Add to Cart. The data can't distinguish "scanned clean" from "not scanned".

## Console & Network Errors

**Console (21 total, 10 shown):**
- **`Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed in this document.`** (6 of 10 shown). A script is registering an `unload` listener where the permissions policy disallows it. This is typically from third-party tags, and it also hurts bfcache eligibility. Replace with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`.
- **`Unsupported Summarizer API languages were specified…`** (4 of 10 shown). Something is calling Chrome's built-in Summarizer API with unsupported language options. The source is unattributed: it could be a third-party script, an extension, or browser-injected code. Find the calling script from a stack trace.
- The error counts repeat across pages, which points to site-wide scripts and not page-specific code.

**Network:**
- **`429 https://www.skechers.com/cart/`:** rate limiting or bot mitigation, likely triggered by the automated audit. It could also be a real capacity or WAF issue on the cart path. Check whether a normal user session gets the same response. Either way, the cart step is not a valid test of the cart page.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the test harness first.**
   - Capture LCP and CLS, for example via `PerformanceObserver` with `buffered: true`.
   - Verify that Log In and Men actually navigate.
   - Allowlist or throttle the audit client to avoid the `/cart/` 429, then re-run the cart step.
2. **Fix the critical axe issues on the homepage.**
   - Add accessible names to icon-only buttons and links (`aria-label` or visually hidden text).
   - Correct the ARIA parent/child role structure.
3. **Fix landmark structure site-wide.**
   - Use a single top-level `<main>`.
   - Move `<aside>` elements out of nested landmarks.
   - Add `aria-label` to repeated `<nav>` and `<aside>` landmarks.
   - Wrap remaining content in landmarks.
4. **Add an `<h1>` to the homepage and verify one on every template.**
5. **Cart error handling.**
   - Serve a proper error document with `lang`, `<title>` and `<main>`.
   - Return a retryable 429 with `Retry-After`.
   - Confirm `/cart/` is `noindex`.
6. **Trim third-party tags.**
   - Audit the ~20 vendors for necessity.
   - Remove duplicate loads (Yahoo `ytc.js`, RTB House).
   - Defer or consent-gate the rest.
   - Confirm the GTM `env-1` binding.
7. **Replace `unload` handlers** with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`, and identify the vendor responsible.
8. **Trace the Summarizer API errors** to their source script and remove or fix the call, or confirm they come from the browser or an extension.
9. **Self-host fonts** instead of loading `webfontloader` from cdnjs.
