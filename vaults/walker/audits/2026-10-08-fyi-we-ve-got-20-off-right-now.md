# FYI: we've got 20% off right now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and it's the same HAYRIDE 20% code I got this morning in "20% OFF?! You better be-leaf it 🍁." This one just says it quieter. The green banner with the big white "20% OFF*" is easy to read on my phone. The little line at the very top, "Psst... select adidas are up to 30% off," is easy to miss, and it's the only thing here that's even close to my kind of shoe.
- Then I scroll, and it's women's heels, loafers and flats in the first grid. Under $75 and under $100 it gets better for me. I see a couple of sneakers and a suede loafer that look like something I'd wear. But I never see the word "cushioned," "arch support" or "slip-on," and I see no men's shoes called out as men's. The price tiers are a nice idea, but the whole email is a fashion lineup, and I'm shopping for comfort.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already going to DSW. Would I forward it? No. It's the second 20% send in one day, after a week of BOGO noise, and I'm tired of it. I'd probably read it once and move on.

## What's working

- The giant "20% OFF*" on green is unmissable, and I didn't need my readers.
- The code HAYRIDE is printed right in the banner, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The "Shoes under $50 / $75 / $100" sections fit how I shop. I do the price-versus-quality math in my head.
- "Shop Men" is right there under the banner, and the size row with "Shop Wide Width" is a good touch for my feet.
- The black "Shop Now" buttons are big and easy to tap with my thumb.

## What's weak

- The first grid is mostly women's heels, flats and loafers, and the whole email leans women's. I'm a 62-year-old man, so it didn't feel like it was for me.
- There's no comfort language anywhere: no cushioning, arch support or hands-free slip-on. That's the only thing I shop for.
- The adidas "up to 30% off" is buried in the thin line at the top. It's the best offer for me and they hid it.
- It's the second 20% HAYRIDE email today, right after the BOGO emails of the 5th through the 7th. I'm getting fatigued.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text, and the "regular-price styles only" exclusions are easy to miss under that asterisk.
- The "Fall Lookbook" block is another fashion pitch, not a comfort one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a men's comfort row up front, with a "Walk-all-day comfort" or "Slip-on sneakers under $75" block that actually names cushioning. Otherwise it all reads as a generic fashion send.
- 2. Move the adidas 30% off out of the thin top line and into its own block. Fewer, clearer offers beat three overlapping ones.
- 3. Make the first product grid match the shopper. Segment men's openers away from the heels and flats.
- 4. Don't send a second 20% message the same day. Space these out or fold them together.
- 5. Put the key exclusions ("regular-price only") next to the button, not only at the bottom.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent DSW message (`ablink.dsw.com` tracking, `braze-images.com` fonts). The QA run found one failing link, the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned a 400. It also found a plain-text/URL imbalance, missing alt attributes on two images, and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The HTML was truncated, so anything past the dark-mode CSS is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **HTTP 400**. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/DH88K0hGlsaJJa8C4IOaqg~~/AAQRxRA~/TdtOMIYK…`, a ~700-character Braze-wrapped link. A 400 on a tracking redirect usually means the token is malformed or truncated, or the endpoint rejected the probe. I can't tell which from the data, so verify it manually from a real client. If it also fails there, the SMS-acquisition CTA is dead.
- **Plain-text bloat (WARN):** 22,860 of 27,267 plain-text characters (about 84%) are URLs. That is well over the 70% threshold. The cause is long `ablink.dsw.com` wrapped URLs on every link, and it raises spam-filter risk for the text part.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 64 links were probed and 52 were skipped. Footer and social links were deprioritized, so their health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, image 1:** the `ablink.dsw.com/q/PgXbHX9nOMzEUHMiOtKdWg~~/AAQRxRA~/ptoqm7fz7anekulybd5H3j1SkLobAlscJoVOiM9RaDHXCfumZ_…` image has no `alt`. This is a Braze-wrapped `/q/` URL, so it is likely a tracked image or a pixel.
- **Missing alt text, image 2:** the tracking pixel `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261008_P251275_V1_W-_Offer_MI_20` has no `alt`. Pixels should use `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so browsers ignore them. The `Content-Type` header carries the charset, so impact is low. If the sanitizer didn't strip the attribute, this is a template bug.
- **No other issues found** in the visible HTML. The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, with Outlook resets (`mso-table-lspace`, `mso-line-height-rule`), dark-mode support and a `<title>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated portion is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found in the data provided. The QA run always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The data does not include SPF, DKIM, DMARC or `List-Unsubscribe` header results, or the footer postal address. Those items are unverified, not confirmed clean.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- The pixel's campaign ID `20261008_P251275_V1_W-_Offer_MI_20` identifies the send and is consistent with today's date.
- The one failed link is the SMS opt-in, so its landing page can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Manually test the SMS opt-in link.** If it fails outside the probe, regenerate it in Braze and confirm the destination resolves.
2. **Add `alt=""` to the two flagged images** and set explicit `width="1" height="1"` on the `o.gif` pixel.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Fix or remove the two meta tags** that lack `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
5. **Reduce the plain-text URL ratio** by trimming the number of tracked links in the text part. Alternatively, use short link domains or a hand-written text version.
6. **Run a full-link crawl** that follows the `ablink` redirects, to confirm UTM presence and landing-page status on the 52 unprobed links.
7. **Capture the raw headers** (Authentication-Results, List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post) so the compliance section can be confirmed rather than assumed.
