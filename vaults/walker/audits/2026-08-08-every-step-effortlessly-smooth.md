# Every step, effortlessly smooth
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Now THIS is more like it — a guy in his 30s, sure, but he's sitting on a curb in regular clothes wearing a shoe that actually looks like something with cushioning and structure, not another hot pink kids' sneaker landing in my inbox for the fifth time this week. The "Glide-Step" line with that thick two-tone sole looks like exactly the kind of arch-support, all-day-comfort shoe I'd actually go try on. "Responsive cushioning, effortless style, all-day comfort" — that's speaking my language for once.
- That said, my eye's getting pulled six different directions — New Arrivals, Best Sellers, a women's/men's split, then a slip-in with five stars and "I still love them," then a Back-to-School kids sale bolted on the bottom. It's like three different emails got stapled together. I'd scroll past most of it, but I would stop on that Glide-Step Best Sellers shoe and probably click through just to check the price and if they've got my size.
- Compared to the barrage of "your cart expires" nonsense with the Care Bears sneaker I've been getting all week, this one actually reads like a real product email. I'd open it, I'd click the Best Sellers or Glide-Step button, and I might even mention it to my buddy who's been complaining about his knees on our walks. Wouldn't forward it outright, but it's the first one this week that earned a real look.

## What's working

- Hero model and shoe finally look like something a guy my age would wear — no pink, no cartoon characters.
- "40% Off Select Styles" up top is a clear, specific number, not vague "big savings" talk.
- The Glide-Step Best Sellers shoe with the visible chunky midsole is exactly the cushioned, arch-support look I go looking for.
- Men's/Women's split section is a nice touch — makes it easy to jump straight to my category without wading through everything.
- "Third pair of these and I still love them!" felt like an actual person talking, not marketing copy.

## What's weak

- Too many offers crammed into one email — 40% off, New Arrivals, Best Sellers, Slip-Ins, then a 25% Back-to-School kids sale I have zero use for.
- No mention of arch support or cushioning tech specifics beyond "responsive cushioning" — I'd want to know if this is their arch-fit insole or something new.
- Nothing here says "member" or references any points/rewards, and I know I've got a Skechers account.
- The kids' Back-to-School block at the bottom is dead weight for me — same complaint I had on the Aug 5 "everyone's invited" email that also ignored I don't have kids at home.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one clear offer and one hero shoe — lead with Glide-Step Best Sellers front and center, drop or shrink everything else below a "shop more" link.
- 2. Add one line of real product detail — arch support, memory foam, wide-width availability — since that's the actual reason I'd click.
- 3. Drop the kids' Back-to-School block entirely from sends to customers without kids' purchase history — it's just clutter for me.
- 4. Work in a members-only line since I know I've got an account — even a small "Members get an extra 10%" would beat being treated like a stranger.
