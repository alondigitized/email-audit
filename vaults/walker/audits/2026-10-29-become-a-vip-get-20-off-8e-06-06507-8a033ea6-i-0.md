# Become a VIP & get 20% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the eighth or so send in about four days, and I'm tired of it. After "Final. Hours. to. SAAAAAAAVE." and the SOBRIGHT ladder, now it's a different code, FABULOUS, for 20% off. The giant "20" is easy to read on my phone, I'll give them that. But "Become a VIP" is a button asking me to sign up, and I don't know what it costs me.
- Then I scroll and find five women's shoes and one slip-on. The slip-on is black, low and clean, and it's the only thing here that looks like me. The rest are heels, ballet flats and a mule. Nothing says cushion, arch support or walking. I'd skim it and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The big "20% OFF*" is the first thing I see, so I don't have to hunt for the offer.
- The code FABULOUS is printed right in the hero, so I don't need to guess how to use it.
- The black slip-on sneaker in the grid is my kind of shoe.
- The "Shop by Size" block with wide width is helpful. My feet aren't narrow.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and "In-store pickup" are laid out plainly with icons.

## What's weak

- The grid is almost all women's dressy shoes, and nothing is men's, comfort or walking. For a man my age it's irrelevant.
- The "Shop Women / Men / Kids / Everything" links under the button are faint white-on-pink and hard to read. I'd miss them or mistype.
- The asterisk and the fine print at the bottom are tiny. I'd have to zoom to see that it's regular-price only and that Skechers is excluded.
- Skechers is on the exclusion list, and Skechers is one of my brands, so that stings.
- The headline is "Become a VIP," not shoes. It feels like a sign-up pitch, not a sale.
- This is another discount email on top of the SOBRIGHT and JINGLE sends. I'd stop trusting any one of these deadlines.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send men's and comfort-first versions to people like me, with slip-ons and walking shoes up top. Put arch support, cushioning and "hands-free" in the product names.
- 2. Say what VIP costs, "Free to join," right next to the button. Right now it's a mystery.
- 3. Put the exclusions in plain, readable text near the offer, not at the bottom.
- 4. Make the Shop Women/Men/Kids links real buttons with dark text.
- 5. Cut the cadence. One strong offer a week beats eight near-identical ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free to join: 20% off your next pair`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's comfort picks, 20% off with VIP`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code FABULOUS through Nov 1. Regular-price styles only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ons, walkers and wide widths, all 20% off for VIPs.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Braze-style, table-based XHTML template with dark-mode support. QA confirms one failing tracked CTA link (HTTP 400) and two images without alt text. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Failing CTA (confirmed by QA):** the link "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400**. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/_RwNGxgd2lUX4PS6OWkI_Q~~/AAQRxRA~/3GiSmUzx…`. The probe reported empty text for the response, so the cause is unconfirmed. It could be an expired or malformed tracking token, or the click-tracker rejecting the automated probe. Verify manually before treating it as a campaign bug. If it fails for real users, it is the SMS opt-in CTA.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 69 links were probed and 57 were skipped as lower priority. Footer and social links are unverified.
- **Link wrapping:** all links go through the `ablink.dsw.com` click-tracking redirector. Final destinations can't be seen in the HTML, so they can't be audited statically.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA):**
  - A linked image at `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/bFBbVBAI2m6MZBitziXNsA~~/…`. This is a content image inside a link, so screen readers get no link name.
  - The open-tracking pixel `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif`. It should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute** (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`). Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Malformed meta tags in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it declares nothing.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv`.
  - The charset is therefore not actually declared at the meta level. This is low risk when the HTTP/MIME header sets it.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Custom web fonts:** `Futura_PT_*` and `HelveticaNow_*` load from `braze-images.com` via `@font-face`. Most clients ignore web fonts, so fallback stacks matter, but they aren't visible in the truncated source.
- The rest of the head looks standard: `color-scheme` meta, dark-mode media queries, Outlook `mso-` resets and the gutter hack.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered Liquid or merge tokens such as `{{ }}` or `{% %}`. The visible portion also shows no personalization to check. The truncation means the body wasn't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found (QA):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be confirmed. This likely comes from the AgentMail relay stripping or not adding the header, so it is an audit-environment limit and not a confirmed sender failure. Check the raw headers for `dsw.com` / `e.dsw.com` alignment.
- **Plain-text part:** 25,179 of 29,535 characters (85%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate the text/plain part and can raise spam-filter scores. It should be kept to key links.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. I couldn't check the footer postal address or `List-Unsubscribe` / one-click headers because the source is truncated and headers weren't supplied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Because every link is wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, UTM parameters and final landing URLs aren't visible. The beacon URL has a campaign identifier (`mi_ecmp=20261029_P261592_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…`), so campaign tracking exists through the `mi.dsw.com` pixel. The 400 on the SMS opt-in link means that CTA has no confirmed landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click-test the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link in a real client. If it still 400s, regenerate it in Braze and confirm the SMS opt-in landing page resolves.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the linked image, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the `o.gif` pixel.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Fix the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Trim the text/plain part to reduce the URL ratio.
7. Re-run the audit with full raw headers and the untruncated HTML. That would let you verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address, UTM parameters and merge tokens.
