## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's in blue, it's bold, and it's the very first line I read. That's the hook that keeps me from tapping back to Google.
- **Tony Romo in casual Skechers** — I know that face from Sunday afternoons on the couch. Seeing him in a relaxed sitting pose wearing these shoes says "everyday comfort," which is my language.
- **"HANDS FREE Slip-ins" at the bottom of the viewport** — just those four words tell me I'm in the right place. That product line is why I have the app on my phone.
- **Clean mobile navigation** — the nav bar is minimal: logo, search, account, cart, MENU. Nothing cluttered, nothing confusing. Easy to find what I need with my thumb.
- **Spring Apparel Sale strip** — secondary banner is readable and the 25% number is concrete. I appreciate that they didn't bury the number.

## What's weak

- **No Men's Shoes CTA above the fold** — the only "Shop" link on screen goes to the apparel sale. I came for shoes and I'm being funneled somewhere I didn't ask to go.
- **Brooke Burke leads the hero panel** — I understand the dual-celebrity approach, but as a male shopper I'm seeing a woman's shot first. Tony Romo on the right is the relevant image for me, but he's secondary.
- **My Skechers Plus membership is completely invisible** — I'm a loyal member. No points balance, no welcome-back message, no member-pricing badge. They don't know I'm here.
- **BOGO scope is ambiguous** — "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart" doesn't say shoes or apparel. I have to tap "details" in small text to find out if this even covers what I want to buy.
- **No free-shipping information** — I have no idea if there's a threshold to hit. That matters when I'm deciding whether to add something to the cart.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Men's Slip-ins" button** directly on or immediately below the hero — a real tappable button, not a text hyperlink, in the same blue as the BOGO banner.
- **Clarify BOGO scope in the banner** — change it to "BOGO 50% OFF Shoes — Applied at Cart" so there's no doubt and no reason to click away to read fine print.
- **Flip the hero panel order on mobile for male visitors** — put Tony Romo on the left side so he's the first thing a 62-year-old man sees when the image loads.
- **Add a one-line Skechers Plus strip** below the nav — something like "Plus members: your BOGO is ready" turns a loyal customer into a motivated one instantly.
