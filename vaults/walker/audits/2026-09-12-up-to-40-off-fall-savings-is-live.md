# Up to 40% off fall savings is live 🍂

## Executive summary

- Well, now we're talking — this is the first one all week that isn't soccer cleats, a K-pop star, or a Messi tribute. Actual percentage off, actual sale, actual "Shop Men" button right there. My eye went straight to "UP TO 40% OFF STARTS NOW" and I appreciated that.
- That said, it's still a pink silk banner and generic "best sellers" — nothing here tells me there's a walking shoe or a slip-on in that 40% pile, or whether it even touches Adidas' comfort lines like the Puremotion or Ultraboost. I'd click "Shop Men" out of curiosity more than conviction.
- I'd open it, I'd probably click through once to see what's actually on sale, but I wouldn't forward it — there's nothing here specific enough to tell a buddy about yet.

## What's working

- A real number — 40% off — right up top, no digging required.
- Clean black-and-white "Shop Men / Shop Women / Shop Kids" buttons are easy to tap and impossible to miss.
- "No code needed" — I hate hunting for promo codes, so that's a plus.
- Compared to the recent soccer-cleat and K-pop nonsense, this at least reads like a normal, adult sale email.

## What's weak

- No shoe on the page at all — just a pink fabric graphic with stacked type. I have no idea what's actually discounted.
- "Refresh your daily rotation" and "best sellers" is vague marketing-speak, not a specific product or comfort feature.
- "Exclusions apply" with zero detail makes me suspicious the shoes I'd actually want are the ones excluded.
- Nothing here signals menswear/comfort-fit particularly — it's the same generic layout they'd send anybody.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual shoe photo in that hero — ideally a cushioned slip-on or walking shoe, not stacked-letter typography on silk. Show me the product.
- 2. Add one concrete comfort callout under the headline — "cushioned comfort styles included" or similar — since that's what gets me to click, not the percentage alone.
- 3. Tell me what's excluded, or at least reassure me the good stuff isn't. One line of honesty beats vague fine print.
- 4. Consider a men's-only teaser image higher up, since "Shop Men" is buried third visually after the fabric banner.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall sale: 40% off men's shoes, no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned everyday shoes, now up to 40% off.`
