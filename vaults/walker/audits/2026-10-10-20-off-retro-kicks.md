## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/SendGrid-tracked send with dark-mode and Outlook handling in the CSS. The QA run found one hard failure: the SMS opt-in CTA returned HTTP 400. It also flagged two images without alt text and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The audit is limited by the truncated HTML (headers and footer not visible) and by probing only 12 of 66 links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] SMS opt-in CTA returns 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/5L_Ii_d2zSSv4Y9AoaX3FA~~/AAQRxRA~/0TMNzBQb…` (a very long encoded redirect). The 400 comes from the `ablink.dsw.com` redirector, so the encoded payload may be malformed or expired, or the redirector may reject the probe's request. This needs manual confirmation with a real client. If the 400 is real, this high-intent CTA is dead for every recipient.
- **The probe's URL label is empty.** The failure line shows a blank where the destination should be, which suggests the link has no resolvable target. Check whether the underlying `href` is a `sms:` or deep-link wrapper that the tracker can't resolve.
- **Link coverage is partial.** Only 12 links were probed and 54 were skipped, so the other 54 are unverified.
- **Click tracking is behind opaque redirects.** The `ablink.dsw.com` links hide the final destinations, so landing URLs can't be checked from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/57_eRefI3DCrTQiddeMuRg~~/AAQRxRA~/zAPaFdT7…` is a `/q/` path, most likely the open-tracking pixel.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=…` is a 1×1 `o.gif` tracking pixel.
  
  If both are pixels, the fix is `alt=""` so screen readers skip them, not descriptive text. Without the `alt` attribute, some clients announce the file name or URL.
- **Malformed meta tags in `<head>`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it does nothing. `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it is also inert. The charset is likely still set by the MIME header, so this is low risk.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`, so screen readers can't pick a language. The `<title>` is the generic "DSW".
- **Web fonts depend on external hosts:** Futura PT and Helvetica Now load from `braze-images.com` (`.woff` and `.ttf`). Most clients ignore `@font-face`, so this is only a fallback-stack concern. No fallback font stack is visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No issues found in the evidence provided.** The QA report says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and none of the probed failures is an unsubscribe link.
- **Not verifiable:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address are not in the supplied data. The sender `dsw@e.dsw.com` is on a branded subdomain.
- **Plain-text part:** 21,981 of 26,493 characters (about 83%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate the text part, which can hurt spam scoring and readability for text-only clients.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID is present in the pixel URL.** `mi_ecmp=20261010_P261407_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` is a Movable Ink campaign ID. It follows a dated naming pattern, matches today's date, and indicates an offer/trend campaign.
- **UTM parameters can't be confirmed.** Redirects were not followed, so the final URLs and any `utm_*` parameters are unknown.
- **Landing page alignment can't be confirmed.** Whether the "20% off retro kicks" landing page matches the subject offer was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Manually click the SMS opt-in CTA** in a real inbox and on mobile. If it fails, regenerate the Braze link or SMS keyword/deep link. This is the only confirmed blocker.
2. **Add `alt=""`** to both tracking pixels, or `width="1" height="1"` with `alt=""` and `style="display:none"`.
3. **Fix the meta tags** with `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Use a more descriptive `<title>`, such as the subject line.
4. **Shorten the plain-text version.** Strip tracking from the text part, or use short links.
5. **Rerun QA with broader link coverage**, including the 54 skipped links. Capture the full headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and follow redirect chains to check UTMs and the landing page.
