- "Sneaker gooooals" tells me absolutely nothing about what's inside. I opened on brand recognition alone; the subject line did zero work.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Malformed charset meta tag | `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` is missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. The attribute is silently ignored; charset encoding is undefined in clients that rely on the meta tag. |

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM / Unsubscribe / Authentication)

The HTML is truncated before the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical address cannot be confirmed from the source provided. Authentication headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) are not visible in HTML; these must be verified at the MTA/header level, not from the HTML source.

Sending domain `email.famousfootwear.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain — standard and consistent with DKIM alignment expectations, assuming the ESP is configured correctly.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM)

All 31 CTAs resolve through `click.email.famousfootwear.com` redirect chains. Because QA skipped probing these, UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs is unconfirmed. The tracking URLs themselves contain no visible UTM parameters in the pre-redirect query string — UTM attribution depends entirely on what the redirect appends.

**Risk:** If the redirect layer fails to append UTM params (e.g., on a misconfigured link), all click attribution is lost silently.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Fix the charset meta tag** — add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to restore the declaration: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`.
2. **Remove `user-scalable=0`** or replace with `user-scalable=yes` to restore WCAG 1.4.4 compliance.
3. **Add descriptive alt text** to the logo image (`"Famous Footwear"`) and all product/content images. Divider/spacer GIFs may retain `alt=""`.
4. **Fix the `lineapacing` typo** — identify the intended property (`line-height` is most likely) and correct it.
5. **Populate the preheader div** with campaign-appropriate preview text before send.
6. **Audit plain-text version** — at 85% URL density (5,802 of 6,812 chars), this is well above the 70% threshold that triggers spam filters. Strip redundant tracking URL repetition or regenerate the plain-text alternative.
7. **Spot-check 3–5 redirect chains** manually to confirm UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are being appended on final destination URLs.
