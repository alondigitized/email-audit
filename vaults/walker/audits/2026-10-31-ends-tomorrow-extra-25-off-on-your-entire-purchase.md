- The "25% OFF" with code TREAT25 is huge and readable. I know the offer in two seconds.
- "Ends Tomorrow" and "Ends at midnight" give me a deadline I believe.
- Shoe Carnival is a store I know, and the layout looks the same as the other emails I've seen. That consistency builds trust.
- The "Add 25% Off" mobile wallet block and the barcode make it easy to redeem in the store.
- The Shoe Perks $10 reward block near the bottom is a nice touch for a member.

## What's weak

- The top half is kids' boots and clogs and women's Madden Girl boots. I don't shop for either.
- The men's and comfort stuff (Nike, New Balance) is buried below two screens of other people's shoes.
- The fine print excludes Brooks, Crocs, Skechers and other brands I'd want, so "ENTIRE purchase" isn't really entire.
- The "Get Store Coupon" and "Shop … Sale" links are small and low-contrast. Those are tiny tap targets for my thumb.

- 2. Name the excluded brands near the offer, not in 6-point type at the bottom.
- 3. Turn the links into big, high-contrast buttons like "Shop Men's Walking Shoes".
- 4. Fix the preheader so it's real text, not a link.
- 5. Vary the creative from week to week instead of resending the same coupon layout.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has solid client-compat scaffolding: viewport meta, Outlook/Gmail/Samsung fixes, and responsive breakpoints at 660px and 450px. It has two concrete defects: an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder in a tracking pixel URL, and a malformed UTM parameter in another. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and CTA destinations could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved merge token in the pixel URL:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` contains the literal `[UNIQUE]`. The ESP did not substitute it. If this is a per-recipient open or click identifier, opens may not be attributed per recipient, or all recipients may collapse into one ID.
- **Malformed UTM value in the pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261031_Corrected_2_kids_boots_nike_halloween_AM `
  - `t=utm_campaign=…` nests a key/value pair inside a `t` parameter value, so analytics tools won't parse it as `utm_campaign`.
  - The URL ends in a trailing space, which can break strict parsers or get percent-encoded as `%20`.
- **Unverified tracking links:** 26 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Redirect resolution, landing-page status, and chain length are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `048318e9-6719-4331-9fa0-e8129f30fd7d.jpg` (`https://image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/048318e9-6719-4331-9fa0-e8129f30fd7d.jpg`) has no `alt` attribute. With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, this content is lost or read out as a filename.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` and `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` also lack `alt`. This is low severity, but add `alt=""` so they are skipped by assistive tech.
- **Ineffective compat meta:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing. `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` also lacks `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. The `charset` attribute still works.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the `<html>` tag carries `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. This is harmless in practice but inconsistent.
- **Link affordance removed:** The global rule `a, a:link, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` strips default link styling everywhere. Unless each link is styled inline, links may not be distinguishable from body text, which is a WCAG 1.4.1 concern.
- **Small text on mobile:** `.responsive-text` is forced to `font-size: 12px !important` at ≤600px.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ESP personalization so `[UNIQUE]` resolves in `0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=…`. Confirm in a seed send that it renders as a real value.
2. Correct the `ieezzy5m` pixel URL. Pass `utm_campaign=…` as a proper query parameter instead of `t=utm_campaign=…`, and strip the trailing whitespace.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `048318e9-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Change the compat metas to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`. Align the doctype with the XHTML namespace, or drop the namespace.
5. Make sure links have a visible non-color affordance, such as inline underline or color on content links. Consider raising the 12px mobile text size.
6. Re-run the audit against the full HTML and raw headers to verify the following. Without them, the compliance and UTM checks remain open.
   - Unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` headers
   - Physical address
   - SPF/DKIM/DMARC
   - UTM continuity on CTA destinations
7. Confirm the campaign naming (`Corrected_2_kids_boots_nike_halloween_AM`) matches this send's content.
