# BOGO 50% off + so fresh for fall
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off — that's a real deal I can do the math on standing in line, and it's right up top where I can see it without hunting. But then it's "Fall's Top 4" — tall boots, animal print heels, a red sock with... something, women's retro sneakers — and not one single item in that whole four-square grid is a men's shoe. There's a "Shop Men" tab up in the nav, sure, but the entire body of this email is built around a woman's fall wardrobe.
- I already flagged this same BOGO 50% offer yesterday in the "Gear up for school" email — different headline dressing, same deal, same problem. This one at least drops the school gimmick and just states the offer plain, which I appreciate. But between the size chart topping out where it does and every model in this email being female, it's clear who they built this for, and it isn't me.
- I'd glance at the subject line, maybe open it for the BOGO math, then close it out without clicking anything. Not forwarding this one — nothing here says "comfort shoe for a 62-year-old guy."

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly with a real dollar-math headline — "Great deals. Easy math." — no fine print hunting required up top.
- "Shop Men" is right there in the nav bar, so at least there's a door in if I want to walk through it myself.
- The size-by-number grid and wide-width/wide-calf buttons are clean and readable, decent font size for my eyes.
- Loyalty callout at the bottom (5% back, free shipping, birthday gift) is legible and not buried too deep.

## What's weak

- Every single "Fall's Top 4" product — boots, brown loafers, animal print flats, retro sneakers — is shown on a woman, styled for women. Nothing cushioned, nothing arch-supported, nothing that reads "walking shoe for a guy."
- "Shop All Women" is a featured button in that hero grid; there's no equivalent men's callout anywhere in the main body.
- The "Summer" lookbook banner sitting in a fall email is confusing — is it summer or fall stock, and either way it's more women's inspo, not my category.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — just trend chasing (animal print, tall boots) which isn't how I shop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Build a men's version of this same BOGO email with actual men's comfort shoes in the hero grid — Skechers, Hoka, New Balance walkers — since the offer itself is genuinely good and I'd act on it if I saw my category.
- 2. Cut the "Fall's Top 4" down to trend content that at least includes one men's row, or segment sends by browsing history so guys like me get product they'd wear.
- 3. Drop the stray "Summer" lookbook banner from a fall email — it reads like a leftover template, hurts trust.
- 4. Add one line of comfort language somewhere — cushioned, slip-resistant, arch support — since that's the actual decision driver for me, not "icon status."
