# THIS IS IT: 25% OFF 📣
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one's a total miss for me and it doesn't even try to hide it. "COMING IN HOT" with a "weekly mood booster" — that's not language a 62-year-old guy uses or wants to read — and every single shoe pictured below it is a woman's sandal, kitten heel, or sparkly slingback. Adidas sneaker aside (and even that's styled women's), there isn't a men's walking shoe or slip-on comfort shoe anywhere in this email.
- The 25% off banner at the very top is at least easy to spot, I'll give them that — same trick DSW's used all week. But then it just falls apart into "MADE FOR SUMMER DAYS" sandals and "EVERYONE LOVES THESE" heels. Nothing here speaks to cushioning, arch support, or hands-free comfort — nothing I'd wear walking the dog or standing at a job site.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm sure not forwarding this to a buddy. Straight to "mark as read" and move on — this is the same story as DSW's back-to-school blasts, just with a different theme slapped on the same women's product feed.

## What's working

- The 25% off claim banner up top is legible and easy to find on a phone screen.
- Layout is clean, no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.
- "SHOP BY SIZE" grid and "SHOP WIDE WIDTH" / "SHOP WIDE CALF" buttons are a nice touch for a comfort-focused shopper, if only they applied to men's product.
- In-store pickup / shoe donation / IMBOX protection strip near the bottom is a decent trust cue for people who visit physical DSW stores.

## What's weak

- Every product shown — sandals, kitten heels, slingbacks, sparkly heels — is women's. There's not one men's walking or casual shoe in the whole scroll.
- "COMING IN HOT" and "weekly mood booster" is not how I talk or think about buying shoes — feels aimed at someone a third my age.
- "SHOP WOMEN" is a top CTA button; there's no "SHOP MEN" button anywhere I can see.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — just style and "fresh" language.
- The 25% off offer has zero context — no end date, no code visible on this screen, so it doesn't feel urgent or real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a men's comfort section — even a small one — with walking shoes, slip-ons, something with visible cushioning. Right now I'm invisible in this email.
- 2. Put an actual deadline on the 25% off — "ends Sunday" beats an open-ended banner that could be from any week.
- 3. Drop "weekly mood booster" — replace hero copy with something concrete like new arrivals or fall comfort styles.
- 4. Add a "SHOP MEN" button next to "SHOP WOMEN" at the top so I don't have to hunt.
- 5. Segment sends by past purchase/browse category — if I've never clicked a heel, stop sending me heels.
