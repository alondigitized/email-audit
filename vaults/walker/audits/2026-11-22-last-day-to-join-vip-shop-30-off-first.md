# Last day to join VIP & shop 30% off first…
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the sixth or seventh VIP pitch in three days. I've now seen the pink banner and the giant black "30% OFF*" so many times that I could recite it. The "Last day" subject is the only new thing, and that's a deadline I've stopped believing. Yesterday's "Want 30% off first? Become a VIP!" and "Save 30% as a VIP (Sign up for free!)" made the same promise.
- The number itself is easy to read on my phone without my glasses, and I'll give them that. But the email's one button says "BECOME A VIP," and that's a sign-up chore, not a shoe. Nothing up top shows me a cushioned slip-on or a walking shoe. I'd scroll, glance at the sneakers, and move on. I wouldn't click. I wouldn't forward it either, because I'd have nothing new to tell a friend.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF*" is readable without zooming. It's the first thing my eye lands on.
- "Black Friday starts…NOW!" fits the season.
- The "Shop by Size" block with Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons is useful. Big, tappable black buttons suit my thumbs.
- The Free Shipping, In-Store Pickup and Afterpay icons tell me how the shopping works.
- The nav includes MEN, and "Shop Men" is one of the links under the hero.

## What's weak

- Same creative as the last six sends. I feel like a stranger on a mailing list, not a known customer.
- The only button, "BECOME A VIP," asks me to sign up before I see any shoes.
- The "SO NEW, SO YOU" grid is mostly women's boots and trendy sneakers. Nothing says cushioned, arch-supported or slip-on, and the men's and comfort angle is missing.
- The asterisk on "30% OFF*" leads to a wall of tiny gray fine print at the bottom, with exclusions like Skechers, Kelly & Katie and Hoka-type brands. I know my comfort brands are probably carved out, which makes the offer feel less honest.
- The free tote with $75 purchase is in-store only and doesn't interest me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the hero's CTA on a real shoe category, such as "Shop Men's Comfort Walkers." Keep VIP as a small secondary link.
- 2. Show a men's comfort row with prices and brand names: slip-ons, cushioned walkers, arch support.
- 3. Say plainly which brands are excluded from the 30%, up near the offer.
- 4. Slow the cadence, or give me something new each send. Seven near-identical emails in three days is too many.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's walking shoes, 30% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: 30% off, VIP is free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned slip-ons and walkers included. Code BFDEALS.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free to join. Your 30% off ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Two QA failures were confirmed: a tracked SMS opt-in link returned HTTP 400, and the raw merge token `%%gender%%` appears in the output. The HTML source is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA, HTTP 400.** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/qtOKB4HirNfBhO6KqeENTw~~/AAQRxRA~/111IaTFh…`. It is a long click-tracking redirect, and the probe got a 400 from the tracker itself.
  - Possible causes are a mangled or truncated redirect token, or the tracker rejecting the probe's request. The data doesn't say which, so retest from a real client before treating it as a send-blocker.
  - The QA output shows an empty href label before the link text, which suggests the anchor may be malformed.
- **Skipped links.** 12 links were probed and 66 skipped, so most of the click surface is unverified.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261122_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` carries a user ID (`mi_u`) in the query string. This is normal for an open pixel, but note it for privacy review.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Charset not declared.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it isn't a valid charset declaration. `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` is missing `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` and does nothing.
- **Zoom blocked.** The viewport tag `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing alt attributes (2 images):**
  - The image linked from `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/DkD6kP-86FFlV3yTO4HapA~~/AAQRxRA~/JqFIhlTCVZL…`. Since it is linked, it needs descriptive alt text.
  - The `o.gif` tracking pixel. This one only needs `alt=""` and is low severity.
- **Plain-text part.** 27,662 of 33,446 characters (about 83%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate the text part and can add to spam scoring.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Raw token in output: `%%gender%%`.** This looks like an unresolved Braze/ESP variable. If it sits in a URL, a class or an attribute, it may also break links or targeting. The truncated source doesn't show where it appears, so locate it in the full HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** This is a limitation of the AgentMail relay, per the QA note, not evidence that SPF or DKIM is failing. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified and need checking on a direct-delivery copy.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address.** The QA data reports no unsubscribe failure and says an unsubscribe link is always probed. The truncated source doesn't show the footer, the postal address or the `List-Unsubscribe` header, so none of these could be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable.** The links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and no final destination URLs were resolved in the provided data.
- **Landing-page alignment not checked.** Because the SMS opt-in CTA failed, its destination couldn't be checked either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or regenerate the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/qtOKB4Hi…`) and retest it from a real client. This is the highest priority because it is the primary conversion CTA for the 15% offer.
2. Find `%%gender%%` in the full HTML and fix the Liquid/merge syntax, or add a default fallback.
3. Fix both meta tags by adding `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked image, and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
5. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers on a direct-delivery copy.
6. Rerun QA against the full HTML, with a wider link-probe limit, to check the footer, UTMs and the remaining 66 links.
