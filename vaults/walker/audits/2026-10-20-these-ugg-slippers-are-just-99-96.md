# These Ugg slippers are just $99.96 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This one at least has a real product and a real price up top: UGG Classic Slipper, $99.96. After the BOGO barrage last week, and the "Gift-worthy shoes at holiday-budget prices" and "Holiday Lookbook" sends yesterday, this is the third holiday-gift email in two days. The slipper at the top is a slip-on, so it's in my lane, and I can see the price and the black "Shop Now" button right away.
- Then the email turns into a fashion magazine for someone half my age. Hot pink Nike runners, a woman in a blue feathered top, glittery silver Adidas. "Their next fave pair" is a gift pitch, so it isn't for me. Nothing here mentions cushioning, arch support or walking, and there's no men's section. I'd click the UGG once, and I wouldn't scroll much further. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The UGG hero shows the price big and clear, "Now only $99.96", with a black Shop Now button I could tap.
- The subject line matched the hero, so what I saw was what I was promised. That builds trust.
- The slipper is a slip-on, so it speaks to my hands-free comfort habit.
- The "Shop by size" block with Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons is practical. I'd use Wide Width.
- The footer has free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and the DSW VIP rewards line, so I know where I stand as a member.

## What's weak

- Everything below the UGG is women's and younger, with bright sneakers and models in costume-y outfits. Nothing looks like me.
- I see no cushioning, arch support or comfort talk anywhere. It's all style.
- The nine middle screens have no price or clear offer, just "Gift them the newest sneakers." That's a lot of scrolling for nothing.
- The text is small. The footer and size numbers would need a pinch-zoom on my phone.
- The preview text, "+ The newest, most-giftable sneakers are here", starts with a stray plus sign and then trails off into blank filler.
- The holiday-gift theme has been the same for three emails in two days, and it sits on top of a week of BOGO.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's comfort pick. Put the UGG next to a men's slip-on, like the Skechers Slip-ins or Hoka, with a comfort callout.
- 2. Cut the middle fashion section down to a few products with prices and a "Shop men's comfort" button.
- 3. Add a "Shop Men" button next to "Shop All Women" and "Athletic & Sneakers." I only see women's.
- 4. Space out the holiday sends so I don't get three in two days.
- 5. Put a member price or a "VIP gets free shipping" line near the top.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built XHTML template with tracked links through `ablink.dsw.com`. One probed link, the SMS opt-in CTA, returned HTTP 400. There are also a few markup and accessibility defects, and SPF/DKIM could not be verified. The QA pass rate was 62%, with 12 of 54 links probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by probe):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400** at `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/GuYBZJEF-zVEZZLRfP5bag~~/AAQRxRA~/78eVLPNM9-4E8K…`.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking redirect can mean a malformed or truncated tracking token. It can also mean the redirector rejects non-browser probe requests.
  - The probe data can't tell these apart, so verify the link manually in a real client.
  - The link is a high-value conversion path (15% SMS incentive), so a real failure would hurt.
- **URL-heavy plain text:** 18,657 of 21,672 characters (86%) of the text part are URLs, above the 70% threshold. The long `ablink` tokens bloat the text/plain alternative and can hurt spam scoring.
- **Coverage gap:** 42 links were skipped, so their status is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (4 images):**
  - `o.gif` (`mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif`) is a tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.
  - Two `original.png` images are on `braze-images.com`: `…/6ace30dace0824008bee092a/original.png` and `…/6ace30d999b7c4008dcb208a/original.png`.
  - One image has `src` `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/gES73ElODsDo3mcICLDAJw~~/…`.
  - If the Braze images or the `ablink` image carry content, they need descriptive alt text. Otherwise use `alt=""`.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so the charset declaration has no effect.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv` either.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Web fonts:** Futura and Helvetica Now load from `braze-images.com` as WOFF/TTF. Most clients ignore them, so this is a fallback-stack check rather than a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `{first_name}`) appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, because the message came through the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` can't be confirmed. This is a limit of the test setup, not necessarily a sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and address not confirmed:** The QA probe always includes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The footer address and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't visible in the supplied data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable from the data:** Links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters aren't visible. Landing pages for the Ugg slipper offer ($99.96) weren't tested.
- **Campaign ID:** The Movable Ink pixel carries `mi_ecmp=20261020_P261531_V3_W-_Offer_New_B`, which identifies the campaign. Nothing checked whether it matches the click-side tracking.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/GuYBZJEF-zVEZZLRfP5bag~~…`) in a real browser. If it fails, regenerate the Braze/SendGrid tracking link.
2. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`. Add descriptive alt text to the two `original.png` images and the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image if they carry content.
