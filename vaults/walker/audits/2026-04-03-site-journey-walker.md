# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-04-03**
## Full review
---

## 1. Executive Summary

Walked the full Skechers mobile journey today as a 62-year-old guy who just wants to slip on comfortable shoes without bending over. The homepage got me excited — big Hands Free Slip-ins hero right up front, BOGO deal in my face. Navigation into Men's was clean and organized. The product page looked solid. Then it all fell apart: the cart loaded a completely blank white screen, and the search showed me Women's shoes first even though I'm logged in. Two fundamental failures that would have sent me to the competitor. **Regression from yesterday — down to 4/10.**

---

## 2. Business Impact Score: 4/10

**Down 1 point from yesterday's 5/10.** The blank cart page is a hard stop on conversion. Any purchase I was ready to make — gone. The search personalization failure compounds it.

---

## 3. What's Working

**Slip-ins prominently featured on homepage.** First thing I see below the nav is a big "SKECHERS HANDS FREE Slip-ins" callout with lifestyle photos. That's exactly what I came for — didn't have to dig.

**BOGO deal is persistent and clear.** Blue banner at the top on every page. I noticed it, I understood it. That's how you motivate a price-conscious guy like me.

**Men's nav drawer is clean.** Tapping MENU > MEN gives me a simple, well-spaced list. "Hands Free Slip-ins" is right there as a quick link — I didn't have to scroll or guess. "Comfort Technologies" is its own category, which I appreciate.

**Product page lays out the essentials well.** Name, price ($135), star rating, BOGO reminder, and that big Slip-ins badge all visible without scrolling. Reassuring for a skeptical buyer.

**ADD TO CART button is excellent.** Big, blue, sticky at the bottom of the page. Easy tap even with older fingers. No hunting for it.

**Width filtering on the shoe listing page.** Wide Fit and Extra Wide Fit buttons right at the top of results. Comfort shoppers live and die by fit — this matters.

---

## 4. What's Weak

**Cart page is completely blank.** Step 8 loaded a white screen. Nothing. No items, no total, no buttons. This is a conversion-ending bug. I would have thought my item was lost and given up or called the 1-800 number. This is the single worst thing that can happen in an e-commerce flow.

**Search shows Women's products first.** I searched "slip in" and the first two results I see are both labeled "Women's." I'm a logged-in male customer. The site knows who I am. Why is it showing me women's shoes? 1,014 results with no male filtering by default is a mess.

**No confirmation that I'm actually logged in.** The login step screenshot looks identical to the homepage — no "Welcome back, Walker" message, no name in the nav, no personalized greeting. I'd have no idea the login worked. For an older user less tech-confident, this is disorienting.

**Skechers Plus member not recognized.** I'm a loyalty member and the site is still showing me "Join Now for free" in the scrolling banner. That's the opposite of feeling valued.

**257 results with no helpful default sort.** The Slip-ins listing page has 257 shoes and no indication they're sorted in any useful way for me. Best sellers or "most comfortable" first would help a lot.

**"Medium" width button cut off.** The width filter row clips the "Medium" option off-screen to the right. If I don't know to scroll that row, I might not realize medium widths are available.

---

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page immediately.** A blank cart is a P0 bug. Every day it's broken is lost revenue. Needs same-day resolution.

2. **Filter search results by the logged-in user's gender by default.** I'm a man. Show me men's shoes first. This is table-stakes personalization.

3. **Show login confirmation.** After I log in, display my name in the nav header ("Hi, Walker") or show my Skechers Plus points balance. Confirm the login worked.

4. **Stop showing "Join Skechers Plus" to existing members.** That scrolling banner should show my points balance or member benefits, not an invitation to join a club I'm already in.

5. **Default sort on Slip-ins listing to Best Sellers or Top Rated.** 257 results is overwhelming. Give me the most popular ones first so I don't have to work.

6. **Fix the width filter row.** Make sure "Medium" is visible — either wrap the buttons or make the row scrollable with a visible indicator.

---

## 6. Bottom Line

Skechers knows their customer. The homepage, the nav, the product page — they're all pointing at the right things for a guy like me. But a **blank cart page and a search engine that doesn't know I'm a man** are unforgivable at this stage of a purchase. I came ready to spend $135 on a pair of Slip-ins. Instead I'm staring at a white screen wondering if my order went through. That's a lost sale — and possibly a lost customer. Fix the cart. Fix the search. The bones are there; the execution is breaking down at the finish line.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

Audit covers 8 steps of the mobile purchase funnel on skechers.com (Salesforce Commerce Cloud / Demandware). TTFB is fast site-wide except View Cart. The primary infrastructure issue is self-inflicted 429 rate limiting from the Kasada bot protection SDK, which also hits the cart endpoint directly. 32 axe violations span critical ARIA structure failures and missing accessible names. Third-party script sprawl is significant.

---

## Accessibility

**Critical (fix immediately):**
- `aria-required-parent` (Homepage): ARIA roles missing required parent containers — likely carousel or list items rendered outside their required wrapper.
- `aria-required-children` + `aria-valid-attr-value` (View Product): Malformed ARIA on a component (likely size selector or swatch widget); invalid attribute values will cause assistive tech to misinterpret or ignore the control entirely.
- `button-name` (View Product): One or more `<button>` elements have no discernible text. Common in icon-only CTAs without `aria-label`.

**Serious:**
- `aria-dialog-name` (View Product): Modal/dialog opened during product interaction has no accessible name — screen reader users get no context on what the dialog is.
- `aria-input-field-name` (View Product): ARIA input fields (likely custom quantity or size inputs) missing labels.
- `link-name` (Homepage): Anchor tags with no text content and no `aria-label` — likely image links without alt text on the `<img>` child.
- `link-in-text-block` (Men's Shoes): Links within body text distinguished only by color — fails WCAG 1.4.1 (Non-text Contrast).
- `color-contrast` (View Product): Foreground/background contrast below WCAG AA 4.5:1 ratio.

**Moderate:**
- `landmark-no-duplicate-main` + `landmark-main-is-top-level` (Homepage): Multiple `<main>` elements detected; the main landmark is nested rather than top-level. Indicates CMS template compositing `<main>` inside another `<main>`.
- `heading-order` (Homepage): Heading levels skip (e.g., h1 → h3), likely from independently authored content blocks.
- `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`: `<aside>` elements nested inside other sectioning content.
- `region`: Page content outside any landmark region.

## Mobile Optimization

- `--vh` CSS custom property is set inline via JS on `<html>` (`style="--vh: 6.64px"`). This is the standard workaround for mobile browser dynamic toolbar affecting `100vh`. Correct pattern, but dependent on JS execution before layout.
- `wf-mulish-*-active` classes on `<html>` indicate WebFont Loader is managing Mulish font loading. Using WebFont Loader (deprecated library, last release 2017) introduces a render-blocking synchronization point. Prefer `font-display: swap` with direct `<link rel="preload">` instead.
- No viewport `<meta>` tag visible in truncated source — expected to be present further in `<head>` but not confirmed.

## Console & Network Errors

**429 — Kasada fingerprint endpoint (5 occurrences):**
```
https://www.skechers.com/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/
  2d206a39-8ed7-437e-a3be-862e0f06eea3/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.308
```
This is the Kasada bot protection SDK's own fingerprinting call returning 429 (Too Many Requests) against itself. The SDK is exceeding the rate limit configured on Kasada's server-side rule. This indicates a misconfiguration in the Kasada deployment — either the per-session call frequency is too high, or the rate limit threshold is too aggressive. Kasada 429s also degrade the SDK's ability to classify traffic, which can cause legitimate users to be misidentified.

**429 — Cart page:**
```
https://www.skechers.com/cart/
```
The cart endpoint itself returned 429. This directly explains the 145ms TTFB on View Cart (the server is throttling the page request). If this fires for real users under load, it will cause cart load failures — a direct conversion impact.

**CORS block — Stylitics stylesheet:**
```
Access to CSS at 'https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css?base=hotspots&version=320dd7a42c'
blocked by CORS policy: No 'Access-Control-Allow-Origin' header present
```
The Stylitics CDN is not returning an `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header for `https://www.skechers.com`. This is a Stylitics-side configuration issue (their CDN must allowlist the skechers.com origin). The Stylitics hotspot/outfit widget will render without its stylesheet — layout will be broken or invisible.

**`net::ERR_FAILED`:** One resource failed to load entirely (no URL captured in provided data). Likely a blocked or mis-configured third-party endpoint.

## Recommendations

1. **[High / Revenue] Investigate and fix 429 on `/cart/`** — Identify whether this is a WAF/CDN rate limit rule misconfiguring real user sessions as bots (possibly caused by the Kasada SDK failures upstream). Coordinate with CDN/WAF and Kasada support.

2. **[High] Fix Kasada SDK rate limit misconfiguration** — Work with Kasada to audit the per-session fingerprint call frequency. The SDK should not be hitting its own rate limit under normal browsing; this suggests either the SDK version (`j-1.2.308`) has a polling bug or the server-side rule is too restrictive.

3. **[High / A11y] Fix critical ARIA violations on View Product** — `aria-required-children`, `aria-valid-attr-value`, `button-name`, `aria-dialog-name`: these four together make the product page and its modal(s) unusable for screen reader users. Audit the size selector, swatch, and overlay components specifically.

4. **[High / A11y] Fix `button-name` and `link-name`** — Add `aria-label` to all icon-only buttons and image-only links. This is a WCAG 2.1 Level A failure.

5. **[Medium] Resolve Stylitics CORS error** — Contact Stylitics to add `https://www.skechers.com` to their CDN CORS allowlist for `web-assets.stylitics.com`.

6. **[Medium] Remove duplicate script tags** — Deduplicate `creativecdn.com` and `ytc.js` loads. Audit GTM container for duplicate tag firing rules.

7. **[Medium] Fix duplicate `<main>` landmark** — Template compositing is injecting a second `<main>` element. Enforce single `<main>` at the template level.

8. **[Low / Performance] Replace WebFont Loader** — Migrate from the deprecated `webfontloader.js` to native `<link rel="preload">` + `font-display: swap` for Mulish. Removes an external library dependency and eliminates the FOUT synchronization overhead.

9. **[Low] Capture LCP and CLS in RUM** — Quantum Metric is already instrumented; ensure Web Vitals are being forwarded. Without LCP/CLS data, Google Search ranking signal impact from CWV cannot be assessed.
