# New arrivals you don't want to miss
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Jack Harlow sitting on a park bench in wide-leg trousers — that's who this is for, and it isn't me. This is a lifestyle drop email, sneaker-collector stuff, not a pitch about comfort or support. I can see eight shoe tiles down the page — 471, 950, 530, 740, 9060R, 204L — but not one word about cushioning, arch support, or how these feel on your feet for a few hours of walking. It's all "just landed," "Olmsted," aesthetics.
- The good news buried at the top: "Get up to 30% off select styles" with a "Shop now" link right in that gray banner. That's a real offer and I'd have missed it if I weren't looking for it — it's tiny text above the giant hero photo.
- I'm not clicking this one. Nothing here tells me these shoes are comfortable for standing around all day, there's no mention of a 471 or 530 being their walking-friendly line, and a guy in an olive beret on a park bench isn't selling me anything. Straight to read-and-delete, not forwarding this to anybody.

## What's working

- The 30% off banner up top is a real, specific number, even if it's easy to miss.
- Product grid is clean and easy to scan — eight shoes, clear names underneath each.
- "Shop now" and "Visit a store" buttons are both legible with decent size.
- Photos are sharp, no broken images or layout glitches that I can see.

## What's weak

- The whole email is styled around a young hip-hop artist's lifestyle drop — nothing here is aimed at a 62-year-old who wants comfort and support.
- Not a single word about cushioning, fit, or arch support anywhere on the page — my top priority is completely absent.
- "Complete the look" section with graphic tees and matching sets for a couple who look about 25 — further confirms I'm not who they're writing to.
- The offer banner is a throwaway line of small text competing with a massive fashion photo — my eye goes to the guy on the bench, not the deal.
- No loyalty callout, no mention of membership pricing, even though I've gotten enough of these to expect it by now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the list — send guys my age the walking/comfort shoes (471, 530, 740) with copy about support and all-day comfort, and save the Jack Harlow lifestyle drop for a younger segment. That's the single biggest fix.
- 2. Move the 30% off offer into a real headline with its own button, not a thin gray strip at the very top.
- 3. Add one line of practical detail per shoe — cushioned sole, breathable knit, wide-fit available — something a comfort shopper can actually use to decide.
- 4. Cut or relocate the "Complete the look" apparel section for older subscribers who came for shoes, not outfits.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The 471 and 530 just got a lot cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `All-day cushioning, now up to 30% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the walking shoes that don't quit on you`
