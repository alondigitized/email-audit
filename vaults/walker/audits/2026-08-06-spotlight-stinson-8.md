## Executive summary

- Another HOKA in my inbox, and this one's a trail shoe with a gravel-bike-inspired outsole and "stability tech" that reads like it's for someone running mountain ultras, not walking my dog around the block. I've now seen this brand pivot from the Clifton PRO, to the Clifton 11, to Speedgoats, to a back-to-school guide, and now a trail runner called the Stinson 8 — that's six sends in under a month and I still haven't gotten one that says "comfortable everyday shoe for a 62-year-old who just wants his feet to stop hurting."
- The shoe itself looks rugged and well-built, I'll give them the photography — that action shot of someone bounding down a dirt trail is sharp. But there's zero mention of cushioning-for-standing-all-day, arch support, or anything hands-free. This is a performance trail shoe pitch, full stop, and the model on this one and the "Trail Running Guide" section is a woman who looks like she just finished an ultramarathon, sweat and dirt included. Not exactly speaking to me.
- I'd scroll past this one. Not annoyed, just not the audience — I'll mark it read and move on. Wouldn't forward it, wouldn't click.

## What's working

- The hero action shot is genuinely well-shot — trail runner mid-stride, off-grid, gives a real sense of what the shoe's built for.
- Product shots are clean and show the shoe from multiple angles (side, sole, heel) so I can actually see the tread and structure.
- "Shop Stinson 8" and "Explore More" buttons are big, black, high-contrast — easy to tap on a phone even with my eyes.
- The feature call-outs (breathable mesh, gravel-bike outsole, H-Frame stability) are short and scannable, not a wall of text.

## What's weak

- This is a trail running shoe pitched with cycling-inspired outsole tech — nothing here is about all-day comfort, arch support, or slip-on ease, which is what actually gets my money.
- The trail guide model is sweaty, dirty, mid-ultra — that's a serious-athlete image, not a guy like me who wants a comfortable walking shoe.
- Sixth HOKA email in under a month covering a different shoe or theme almost every time — Clifton PRO, Clifton 11, Speedgoat, back-to-school, buyer's guide, now Stinson 8. It's starting to feel like a firehose rather than a curated conversation.
- No price, no offer, no mention of loyalty points — just a straight product spotlight with nothing that makes me want to act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of trail-running spotlights entirely — if HOKA has any purchase or click history on me, none of it points to off-road performance shoes. Send me their walking/comfort line instead (something like the Bondi or Clifton positioned for everyday wear, not trail).
- 2. Add a price or member perk. Even performance-shoe browsers want to know what it costs before clicking through.
- 3. Swap the "who is this for" framing — lead with comfort and stability benefits in plain language before the gravel-bike/H-Frame jargon, since not everyone parses trail-running tech specs.
