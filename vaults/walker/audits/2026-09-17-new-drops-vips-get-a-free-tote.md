# New drops & VIPs: get a free tote
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- Another "up to 60% off clearance" banner right at the top — same number I've seen literally every single day this week, so that hook's dead before it even started. Then it swan-dives straight into strappy heels, a floral sundress skirt, sandals, "Fall Stars" occasion shoes — every single item is women's, and the size grid up top is 5 through 11 half sizes, same as every other DSW send in my inbox lately. There's not one men's shoe, not one flat, cushioned, or arch-support anything anywhere in this email.
- The "Free Tote" VIP offer at the bottom is at least a new beat for this brand — but it's a purse-sized tote with a $99 purchase in a category I'm not shopping, so it does nothing for me either.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this to anybody. Straight to archived-and-ignored, same as the last five from this brand.

## What's working

- The "Free Tote" VIP banner near the bottom is genuinely different creative than the clearance loop I've been getting all week.
- Layout's clean, no rendering glitches, images all loaded fine.
- "Buy Now, Pay Later with Afterpay" icon row at least signals value-conscious shopping, which I respect even if it's not why I'd buy.

## What's weak

- Every product shown — heels, sandals, a lace-trim skirt, a floral dress shoe — is women's occasion wear. Nothing for me.
- Size grid at the top (5–11, half sizes) confirms this is a women's-sizing send, not mine.
- "Up to 60% off Clearance" headline is the exact same number and phrasing I've now seen four times this week from this brand — I could recite it.
- No cushioning, arch support, slip-on, or men's casual anything — none of what actually gets me to buy a shoe.
- "JUST IN" and "Fall Stars" sections are all styled around going-out heels, which isn't remotely my shopping occasion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men's customers out of the women's dress-shoe blast entirely — send me a men's comfort/casual assortment or don't send at all. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Retire the "up to 60% off clearance" headline for a while — I've seen it daily, it's lost all urgency for me specifically.
- 3. If the Free Tote VIP offer is meant to be the real hook this time, lead with it instead of burying it after four product blocks of shoes I won't buy.
- 4. Show a men's size grid and men's clearance styles if this is genuinely a clearance-wide push, not just a women's-only visual.
