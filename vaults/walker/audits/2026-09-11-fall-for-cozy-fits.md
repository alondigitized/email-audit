# Fall for cozy fits
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Now this one's actually worth a look. My name's up top, my points balance is right there — 1,000 points, enough for a $5 reward — and there's a real 20% off code for members. That's different from the last few weeks of that same pink floral D'Lites sneaker chasing me around. This is the first Skechers email in a while that felt like they know I'm a member, not just a name in a spreadsheet.
- The Slip-Ins in the "Men's" section caught my eye — those are the hands-free ones I actually care about, no bending down to deal with laces. Big cushioned sole, matches what I look for whether it's Skechers or Hoka or New Balance.
- I'd click through to the Men's Cozy Fit page. Wouldn't forward it to a buddy — it's just a seasonal sale email, nothing that special — but I'd probably use that $5 reward redemption link before it expires, and take a look at that grey Slip-In.

## What's working

- My name and points balance are front and center — feels like they know who I am, not a stranger blast.
- Clear 20% off code (EXTRA20) with an actual expiration date (9/14) instead of vague "limited time" nonsense.
- Four clean category buttons (Women's, Men's, Kids', Wide) — easy to tap and get right to what I want.
- The men's shoe shown is a Slip-In — that's exactly the hands-free comfort shoe I'm shopping for.
- Fall leaves and cozy/quilted language actually fits the season, not some stale summer promo.

## What's weak

- No price on any of these shoes — I have to click through just to see what "cozy fit" is going to cost me.
- No mention of arch support or cushioning tech (like their Arch Fit line) in the copy — just "quilted materials," which sounds warm, not supportive.
- The redemption link for my $5 reward is buried up top in tiny text — I almost skipped right past it scrolling for the sale.
- "Wide Cozy Fit" button is nice to see, but it's the same size as everything else — for guys like me who need wide, that could be pushed higher.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "starting at $XX" under the Men's Slip-In shoe — I'm not clicking blind to find out what it costs.
- 2. Call out arch support/cushioning by name (Arch Fit, Contour Foam, Memory Foam) instead of just "quilted" and "luxury" — that's the language that actually sells me.
- 3. Make my $5 reward redemption a bigger, separate button, not a small inline link next to the points count.
- 4. Lead with Men's or Wide before Women's in the shoe stack, or at least don't bury Wide as an afterthought category button.
