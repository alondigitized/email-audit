# Psst…get select adidas on SAAALE!

## Executive summary

- Subject said adidas on sale, so I clicked in half-expecting another leopard-print bootie parade like the one they sent this morning — and yep, "Creature Comforts" is the hero again, same red-jacket-and-animal-print moccasin shoot, just with a fresh headline slapped on it. The "Up to 40% off" banner at the very top with the adidas logo underneath is honest at least, but then the whole rest of the page is loafers, slides, and a leopard-print crossbody bag — not one actual adidas shoe pictured anywhere I can see.
- There's zero here for a guy my age looking for a cushioned walking shoe. No sneakers with arch support, no men's section featured, nothing that says "here's what fits." I scrolled the whole thing hunting for adidas product and all I found was the little three-stripes logo up top as a teaser.
- I'm marking this read and moving on. Not clicking, not forwarding — this is the third or fourth DSW send this week aimed at somebody who isn't me.

- The "Up to 40% off" headline is big, bold, and easy to read without zooming.

- "Shop by Size" grid near the bottom is genuinely useful — clear tap targets, easy to pick my size.
- Free shipping / in-store pickup / VIP perks section at the bottom is legible and simply laid out.

## What's weak

- Subject promised adidas, but the entire visual body is boots, loafers, sandals, and a handbag — no adidas product shown.
- "Creature Comforts" animal-print theme is the same creative beat as their last couple sends — nothing new here.
- No men's shoes anywhere in the hero or product shots — just women's footwear and accessories.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, or arch support — the stuff that'd actually get me to click.
- CTA buttons say "Shop Animal Print" and "Shop All Women" — neither applies to me.
