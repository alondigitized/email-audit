# BadBo by adidas x Bad Bunny
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- It's another adidas email, and I stopped counting them a while back. This one opens with a black Black Friday banner and a countdown clock, which got my attention. Then it turns into a Bad Bunny sneaker story. The shoes are two chunky skate-style high-tops in grey and tan. I'm 62 and I walk for cushion and arch support, so they aren't for me.
- I like seeing a real shoe up top, but the copy is about "storm clouds" and "Benito's everyday style." It says nothing about comfort, fit or price. The "up to 60% off" is only for members, and nothing in the email shows me what that buys. The Toca Boca cartoon section at the bottom made me stop reading.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and go back to the Hoka and Brooks emails.

## What's working

- The black countdown banner is easy to read, even on my phone. "Up to 60% Off for Members" is clear at a glance.
- The hero shoe photo is big and clean, and I can see what the shoe looks like.
- The buttons are big black blocks with white text, so they're easy to tap.
- It offers "Find Store Now." I like trying shoes on in person.
- The copy has a plain tone, with short paragraphs and no jargon.

## What's weak

- The shoes are built for style, not comfort. There's nothing about cushioning, arch support or walking.
- No price is shown anywhere. "Up to 60% off" sounds nice, but I can't tell which shoes it applies to. Badbo looks like a new release, which usually isn't discounted.
- The Bad Bunny tie-in is aimed at someone decades younger than me.
- The Toca Boca cartoon section is for kids or gamers. It has nothing to do with shoes.
- Two buttons, "Shop Now" and "Find Store Now," sit stacked together. The sale banner and the new-release story pull in different directions.
- The fine print about limited stores and "first-come, first-served" makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one message. Either run the Black Friday member sale with real shoes and real prices, or run the Bad Bunny launch. Doing both muddles it.
- 2. Put a walking or comfort shoe in the hero, with a price and the discount worked out.
- 3. Cut the Toca Boca block. It doesn't belong in a shoe email.
- 4. Say plainly which shoes the 60% off applies to, and how a member gets it.
- 5. Replace the poetic copy with something concrete about fit, cushion and everyday wear.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: up to 60% off starts in 4 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your member deal on adidas shoes, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See which comfortable everyday shoes are marked down for adiClub members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access to Black Friday prices, plus a look at the new BadBo.`

- **The click-tracking host serves over plain HTTP.** One tracking asset loads from `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1dcbce45a-03fddf36-dca5-45c2-ac70-3db553b0d2d9-000000/QFTMWQOIRxIMigk94cVQjfiiq…`. It is flagged as an image with an `=452` suffix and no alt text, which is consistent with an open-tracking pixel. Over `http://` it can be blocked or rewritten by clients that enforce HTTPS.
