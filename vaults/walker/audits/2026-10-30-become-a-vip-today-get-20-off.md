# Become a VIP today & get 20% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the tenth or so send in about four days, and the third VIP/FABULOUS 20% pitch since yesterday ("Become a VIP & get 20% off!", "Join VIP -> Get access to 20% off", "Want 20% off? Become a VIP (it's free!)"). The subject was reworded once more, and the offer is the same.
- The hero doesn't help. "Wish-List Wonders" is a pink and green holiday collage of Nike, UGG, adidas and Birkenstock, plus a tumbler, sunglasses and a pom-pom hat. The 20% box is way down the page, below all of that. The subject promised me the deal, but the email opens with gift ideas. The only thing for me is a Birkenstock clog, and I'm not sure it's even a men's shoe. I didn't see a cushioned walking shoe or an arch-support message anywhere.
- Would I click? Probably not. It's the same 20% for the fourth time, and I'd have to scroll past the gifts to find it. I'd leave it unread or delete it, and I wouldn't forward it. If I did tap anything, it would be "Shop Men."

## What's working

- The "VIP Exclusive 20% OFF*" block is big and easy to read, with the code FABULOUS right under it.
- "Shop Women / Men / Kids" links under the offer. I can pick Men without hunting.
- The UGG "Classic Slipper, now only $99.96" panel has a real price, and a slipper is a comfort shoe I might actually wear.
- The big black "Shop Wide Width" button helps. Fit matters to me, and the shoe-size buttons are easy to tap.
- The footer lists free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and app download. It's easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The 20% offer is buried under a giant gift collage, so the subject and the first screen don't match.
- The models and styles lean young and female. The woman in the blue fringe top and the fuzzy boots aren't aimed at a 62-year-old man.
- It's the same code and the same 20% I've been sent several times this week, so it doesn't feel special.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, and the asterisk on "20% OFF*" has exclusions. The "regular-price styles only" line is in small type, so it feels a bit buried.
- There's nothing on cushioning, arch support or slip-on comfort, and no brand comparison. I see Skechers only in the fine print, as an excluded brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% VIP offer at the top, above the gifts. The subject made a promise and the hero should keep it.
- 2. Segment by who the customer is. A man my age should get a comfort-and-walking hero, with something like Skechers, Hoka or New Balance slip-ons, not a Birkenstock clog and a pom-pom hat.
- 3. Space out the sends. Four VIP pitches in two days is too many. Send one and move on.
- 4. Make the exclusions clearer next to the offer, and use larger text for phone readers.
- 5. Make "Become a VIP" more specific, for example "Join free, take 20% off."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join free and save 20% on cushioned walking shoes. Code FABULOUS.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/SendGrid-style send with click and open tracking through `ablink.dsw.com` and `mi.dsw.com`. One probed CTA ("Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off") returns HTTP 400. Auth status couldn't be verified, and the HTML source was truncated, so footer and body checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by probe):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returns **HTTP 400**. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/bQWm7ifJqL1IsfGzlIrnjw~~/AAQRxRA~/IdCr0cock-W-HX…` (very long opaque redirect token).
  - A 400 from a tracked redirect can mean a malformed or expired token. It can also mean the destination rejects non-browser probes, such as an SMS deep link or bot filtering. Verify manually in a real browser and a real mail client before treating it as a live break.
- **Probe coverage is partial:** 12 of 64 links were probed and 52 were skipped. Those are mostly footer, utility and social links, so only the high-click-likelihood links were checked.
- **Pass rate:** The overall pass rate is 62%, with 1 failure and 2 warnings. Part of that figure probably reflects the skipped links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` sets `maximum-scale=1`. That restricts pinch-zoom in clients that honor the tag, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/egJiiwf0JqXYqshzEqqx0A~~/AAQRxRA~/FrDLO1jY…` (tracked image, no alt).
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?...` (tracking pixel, no alt).
  - The pixel should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. If the first image is visible content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Meta tags (low confidence):** Two tags in the source appear without their `http-equiv` attribute: `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`. This may be a capture or sanitization artifact. If it's in the sent HTML, the charset declaration is ineffective.
- **Plain-text part:** 21,074 of 25,145 characters (~84%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The long tracking tokens inflate the text part, which can hurt spam scoring and plain-text readability.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `${}`, `%%`) appear in the visible source. The pixel carries `mi_u=225090073` and `mi_ecmp=20261030_P261623_V1_W-_Offer_New_N`, both resolved. The source was truncated, so the body and footer weren't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the AgentMail relay, not a confirmed failure. Check the raw headers at the original recipient, or check the DNS records for `e.dsw.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link:** The probe always includes it, and no failure was reported for it. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided, so one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. The physical postal address is in the truncated footer and also unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters can't be verified. All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the final destination URLs weren't captured. Apart from the failed opt-in link, no landing-page mismatches were detected among the probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the opt-in CTA** (HTTP 400). It's the one confirmed failure on a conversion path.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Add `alt=""`** to the `o.gif` pixel and give the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image appropriate alt text.
4. **Confirm the `http-equiv` attributes** on the charset and X-UA-Compatible meta tags in the sent HTML.
5. **Shorten tracking URLs** or add a richer plain-text body to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
6. **Capture raw headers** (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and the resolved redirect chains, including UTMs, so the auth, compliance and continuity checks can be completed.
