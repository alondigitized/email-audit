## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based Marketing Cloud-style template with responsive media queries, but it has no plain-text part. It also loads some images over `http://` and leaves some images without alt text. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe markup and body links could not be checked.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 13 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (QA WARN). Their destinations are unverified.
- The tracking pixel and click redirects use plain HTTP: `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a152269a46-4f1d8064-3f81-4942-a04c-7b2a47c660c2-000000/3jWWdAcR1lTKXQihY1xMR5wmAxR1xtprrYbezqAaFjM=452`. Click-tracking requests sent over HTTP leak the recipient token in cleartext and may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set. This is harmless for email clients, but it strips referrer data if the email is opened in a browser view.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **No HTTPS on images:** the `arrow` image (`http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`) appears twice with an `http://` source. The tracking image above also uses `http://`. Non-HTTPS sources can be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/bafa57ed-ea85-4c8e-b8e8-24b5d918289c?...`, an `<img>` with no `alt`.
  - The `http://click.comms.adidas.com/...` image, which is probably the open pixel. It should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Stub `@font-face` rules:** `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are declared with `src: local('Arial'), local('Helvetica')`, so no web font loads. The same declarations appear in two `<style>` blocks. This is redundant but harmless; the fallback is effectively Arial.
- **Possible `<meta>` problem:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv` attribute, so it does not declare a charset. This is rendering-relevant only if the MIME `Content-Type` header lacks a charset, which was not available.
- **Mixed doctype and markup:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with self-closing `<meta ... />` tags. Browsers tolerate this.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]` were observed, but the source is truncated.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is missing (0 chars).** A multipart/alternative text part is expected. Its absence hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header** was found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown rather than failing. This is a measurement gap, not confirmed sender misconfiguration.
- The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers could not be verified because the HTML is truncated and headers were not provided.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not assessable. All 13 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't show the final destination URLs or any UTM parameters. Click redirects are on `click.comms.adidas.com`, and the sender is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, so the domains are aligned.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part with content and links. This fixes the one hard QA failure.
2. Serve all images and tracking pixels over HTTPS, including `image.link.adidas.com` (the `arrow` image) and `click.comms.adidas.com`.
3. Add `alt` text to the `dv.adidas.com/o/bafa57ed-...` image, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Fix the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers. That would allow checking the unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe` headers, the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and the destination URLs and UTM parameters.
