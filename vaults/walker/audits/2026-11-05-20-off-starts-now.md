## Executive summary

- DSW again. I'll say it plainly: this is the same 20% pitch I've been getting all week, now with a new code (TINSEL) and a gift theme. The "20% OFF*" up top is huge and I can read it without squinting, and the black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is easy to find. But that asterisk is carrying a lot, and the shoes below it are all women's. I had to scroll past a loafer, a heeled sandal and a slingback to get anywhere.
- The offer window is short, November 5-8, and that's at least a real deadline. It's buried in the fine print at the bottom, though. Up top it only says "starts now."

## What's working

- The giant "20% OFF*" on pink is readable on my phone with no zooming.
- The "CLAIM 20% OFF" and "SHOP NOW" buttons are big, black and easy to tap.
- The code TINSEL is shown in plain text, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The Women / Men / Kids split under the hero lets me skip straight to Men.
- Shop by Size, with Wide Width, is a nice touch. Wide matters for my feet.
- The extra 10% for veterans and military is a decent perk. It doesn't apply to me, though.

## What's weak

- Every shoe in the grid is women's, plus a boot. Nothing for a 62-year-old guy who wants slip-on comfort.
- The Skechers exclusion is buried in the dark fine print at the very bottom. Skechers, Hoka-type comfort brands and others are carved out, and that's what I shop for. It feels like bait.
- The footer text is tiny, grey on black, and hard to read. The deadline is hidden there too.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or walking, and I see no reviews.
- "Happy gifting" is a gift theme, but I'm shopping for my own feet. It's also the 12th-ish send in a week with the same 20%.
- The logo looks like it has the "Wonderland" banner overlapping the "Warehouse" text.

- 2. Show men's comfort shoes in the grid: slip-ons, walkers, cushioned sneakers. Segment by who I am.
- 3. Add one line on comfort or fit, such as "Cushioned walkers, wide widths available."
- 4. Cut the frequency. One strong offer a week beats eleven near-identical ones.
- 5. Fix the logo overlap and make the footer text bigger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/SendGrid-sent XHTML table layout with dark-mode support. QA found one broken link (the SMS opt-in CTA returned 400) and a few accessibility and header-level gaps. The HTML provided is truncated, so body-level checks are limited to what's visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL, HTTP 400):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returns 400. The href is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/hQzYkcYtX07U5TiodLohuQ~~/AAQRxRA~/GCq5MH0f...` (about 600 characters, with an empty link-text field in the probe output).
  - A 400 on a click-tracking redirect can mean a malformed or expired token, or it can be the probe being rejected. Verify manually before treating it as a confirmed outage.
  - If it's real, the SMS acquisition CTA is dead for every recipient.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 72 links were probed and 60 were skipped (footer, social and utility links). The other links are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261105_P261640_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` puts a user identifier (`mi_u`) in a query string. That's expected for open tracking, but it isn't a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - A linked image at `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/k0noHiEYX9ll91zpDmNFtQ~~/...` has no alt. If it's a clickable content image, screen readers announce only the URL.
  - `o.gif` is the tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""` explicitly, so this is low severity.
- **Charset and compat meta tags are malformed:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect. Fix: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="...">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Web fonts:** Futura and Helvetica Now are loaded via `@font-face` from braze-images.com. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, so confirm the fallback font stacks are defined (not visible in the truncated source).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the AgentMail relay. This is a gap in the QA data, not a confirmed failure, so check the raw headers of a direct-delivered copy.
- **Plain-text part:** 25,796 of 31,052 characters (83%) are URLs, from long click-tracking links. This is a spam-filter risk, and the text version is hard to read.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed. The QA run always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links are wrapped in opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so final destinations and UTM parameters can't be checked from the HTML or the QA data.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261105_P261640_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` on the pixel (truncated).
- The one probed CTA that failed (the SMS opt-in) breaks continuity for that path.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **High:** Manually click the SMS opt-in link, or re-probe it with a browser user-agent. If it still returns 400, regenerate the Braze/SendGrid link and re-send or patch the live version.
2. **Medium:** Fix the meta tags by adding `http-equiv` to the charset and X-UA-Compatible tags.
3. **Medium:** Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
4. **Medium:** Add descriptive alt text to the linked `ablink.dsw.com/q/...` image, and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
5. **Low:** Check the raw headers for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `e.dsw.com`.
6. **Low:** Shorten the plain-text part, for example by using a shorter tracking-link format or fewer links, to reduce the URL ratio.
7. **Process:** Raise the probe limit, or probe all CTAs, so the 60 skipped links don't hide further breakage.
