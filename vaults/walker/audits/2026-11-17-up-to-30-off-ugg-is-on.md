# Up to 30% off UGG is on!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and UGG again. This is at least the eleventh email in about a week, and the second UGG send in two days. Yesterday's "Up to 30% off UGG 🎁" was nearly the same. The big coral "30% OFF*" over the UGG logo is easy to read on my phone, but I've seen it so many times I scrolled right past it.
- The whole email is fuzzy slippers and boots, and the Birkenstock row is clogs. I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants a cushioned slip-on I can step into. Nothing here shows a man, a walking shoe, or arch support. The 500 bonus points are a nice touch, but points don't decide my purchase, so I'd skip this one. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 30% OFF*" is huge and coral, and I can read it on my phone without my glasses.
- The black "SHOP UGG" button is big and obvious, so it's easy to tap.
- The green VIP box with "500 BONUS POINTS" is clear. It also says UGG and Birkenstock, so I know which shoes count.
- The "Shop by size" grid with half sizes is handy, and "Shop Wide Width" is a thoughtful touch for a guy my age.
- The "Free shipping for VIPs" and "In-store pickup" icons are easy to understand.

## What's weak

- It's UGG and Birkenstock, and the top section says "Merry UGG Season!" The shoes are women's. The buttons say "Shop Women's UGG" and "Shop Women's Birkenstock," so it clearly isn't aimed at me.
- The "DSW Designer Shoe Warehouse" header logo has the red "Wonderland" tag overlapping the "Warehouse" text. It looks sloppy.
- I don't see any comfort, cushioning, or arch-support language, and no walking or slip-on picks for men.
- The 30% has an asterisk, and the fine print at the bottom is tiny and long. The offer can't be combined with other offers, and the points are capped at one use per day. I don't trust it as much because of that.
- I've gotten near-identical UGG and boots emails all week, so the "Black Friday Deals" pitch doesn't feel urgent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and category. Send me a men's comfort and slip-on version (Skechers Slip-ins, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance) and keep UGG for the shoppers who buy it. This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Cut the cadence. Two near-identical UGG sends in two days trains me to ignore DSW.
- 3. Add a men's row with a comfort cue, such as "cushioned, slip-on, easy on the feet."
- 4. Fix the header so the "Wonderland" tag doesn't cover the logo text.
- 5. Put the real deadline and the key terms next to the offer, not only in the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's slip-on comfort, up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walking shoes your feet will thank you for`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, easy-on styles from Hoka, Skechers and more. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping for VIPs, plus 500 bonus points on select styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a standard table-based XHTML template with dark-mode support. The one confirmed defect is the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned HTTP 400 on probe. The source also has a few head-level and accessibility gaps. SPF/DKIM could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (400), SMS opt-in CTA.** Link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off", on `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/FQqBOv2n11iYi_lyDMcFgQ~~/AAQRxRA~/evwYl4rsHNh6…`.
  - This is the only hard failure in the probe (12 material links probed, 50 skipped).
  - A 400 on a tracked redirect can mean the redirect target is malformed or expired. It can also mean the destination rejects non-mobile or bot user agents, so a manual check on a phone is needed before treating it as a confirmed outage.
  - If it is real, it breaks the email's incentive CTA.
- **Tracked wrapper hides destinations.** All links go through `ablink.dsw.com` (click-tracking redirects). The final landing URLs could not be checked from the source.
- **Skipped links.** 50 links were not probed, so link health is only partially verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, tracked image.** `src="https://ablink.dsw.com/q/BrRBj-z_rw22dfSEdpyqQA~~/AAQRxRA~/Z1MmpX8makAh…"` has no `alt`. This is the `ablink.dsw.com/q/` image URL, so it is probably a content image rather than a pixel.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel.** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif` has no `alt`. It should have `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so clients ignore it.
- **Ineffective compatibility meta.** `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No language attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` does not describe the message.
- **Plain-text part.** 24,287 of 28,706 characters (about 85%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The tracked URLs bloat the text part, which can hurt spam scoring and readability.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The truncation means the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA note says this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a gap in the test setup rather than evidence of a sender failure.
- **Unsubscribe and footer not confirmed.** The QA run says an unsubscribe link was probed and it was not reported as failing. The footer was cut off in the source, so the physical address, the `List-Unsubscribe` header and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) could not be verified.
- **Sender domain.** The From domain `e.dsw.com` is a subdomain, which is normal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Could not be assessed. Every link is wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`, and the destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible in the source or QA data. The tracking pixel's `mi_ecmp=20261117_P261718_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_U` suggests a campaign ID is in use. No landing-page alignment findings can be made.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the SMS opt-in link.** Open it on a mobile device, and if it still fails, repair the redirect.
2. **Add `alt`.** Use `alt=""` on `o.gif`, and descriptive alt text on the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image.
3. **Correct the head meta tags.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and make the `<title>` descriptive.
6. **Shorten the plain-text part** by using fewer or shorter tracked URLs.
7. **Verify authentication separately.** Check the raw headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`) from a direct-delivery inbox rather than the relay.
8. **Resolve the final landing URLs** for the top CTAs to check UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
