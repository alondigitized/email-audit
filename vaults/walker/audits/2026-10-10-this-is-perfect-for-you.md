# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- For once the pink flower sneaker is gone. This time the hero is a brown suede Slip-ins shoe, the Contour Foam Cozy Fit. It's a men's slip-on with a thick cushioned sole, and that's the kind of shoe I wear. After a week of cart nags (the 8th, 9th and twice today), this is a relief.
- But "Still thinking about it?" is the same cart-nag tone again, and the subject line "This is perfect for you!" promises more than the email delivers. Below the hero, half the "options we think you'll love" are kids' garage sneakers, a girl's glitter shoe and a can of waterproofer spray. I'm 62, and I have no use for the Boy's Garage Sneaker or the Girl's Glam Racer. That's sloppy.
- I'd probably tap "Take another look" because the hero shoe is right for me. I wouldn't forward it. The 10% off for members is small next to the 30% I got on the 8th, so it's a mild nudge at best.

## What's working

- The hero shoe is a men's Slip-ins Contour Foam in a calm brown. It's hands-free and cushioned, and it looks like something I'd wear to the store.
- The "Take another look" button is big, dark blue and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman and the Contour Foam leather shoe in the grid are both my kind of shoe.
- The "Members stack an extra 10% off sitewide" banner is clear. It has a deadline of 10/11, so I know how long I have.
- The text is large and the layout is one simple column, which works on my phone. The Women/Men/Kids/Clothing/Sale list is easy to hit.

## What's weak

- Three of the six "great options" are for kids, and one of those is pink and glittery. That says they don't know me at all.
- A can of waterproofer spray is not a shoe I'd love. It looks like filler.
- "Still thinking about it?" is another cart reminder, the fifth nag in a few days. I can't tell what I supposedly left behind, because the hero isn't labeled as a cart item.
- Only 10% off is thin. The email doesn't tell me about arch support or cushioning, or what the shoe costs.
- The product captions in the grid are tiny. I'd have to zoom in to read them, and there are no prices or reviews.
- The fine print on the 10% is crammed in at the bottom in small type. It says I have to be logged in to Skechers Plus and that exclusions apply, which makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fill the grid only with men's comfort shoes, such as walkers, slip-ons and arch-support styles. Drop the kids' shoes and the spray for anyone who has shopped men's.
- 2. Put a price and a one-line comfort benefit under each shoe, like "Arch Fit, machine washable, $79."
- 3. Say clearly what this email is. Either it's a reminder about the shoe I looked at, or it's a recommendation, but not both. Combine the member 10% into the hero button, like "Take another look, 10% off for members."
- 4. Space the cart reminders out. Five in a week is too many.
