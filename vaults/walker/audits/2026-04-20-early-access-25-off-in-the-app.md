# Early access: 25% off in the app 📲
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-04-20**
## Executive summary

- **Email:** "Early access: 25% off in the app 📲" | DSW | April 20, 2026
- I opened this thing hoping for a deal on some comfortable men's shoes — maybe some walking sneakers or casual loafers — and instead I got a full fashion spread for women. Strappy heels, dress sandals, Mother's Day gifts. Every single module is aimed at a woman. I'm a 62-year-old guy who shops DSW occasionally for wide-width comfort shoes, and this email treated me like I don't exist. The 25% off hook got me in the door, but the entire email was the wrong room. I closed it in about ten seconds.
- Strong discount offer, zero targeting. The deal is real but it's buried inside content that has nothing to do with me.

## What's working

- **The 25% off hook is clear and prominent.** The top banner — "FIRST DIBS! IN-APP EXCLUSIVE 25% OFF*" — reads immediately on my phone screen. No squinting required. That number registers.
- **The $39.99 price point is visible and specific.** I like a concrete number. Vague language like "up to X% off" wastes my time; a real dollar figure doesn't.
- **Text is readable.** Font sizes are decent enough that I'm not reaching for my glasses on the hero section.

## What's weak

- **Completely wrong audience.** "Hello, Gorgeous (Sandals!)" — that's not for me. Neither is the wedding season spread, the dressy heel photography, the Mother's Day gift module, or the "Shop Wide Calf" CTA. I scrolled the entire email and there is not a single men's shoe, not a single comfort or walking technology callout.
- **App download required for the deal.** You want me to stop, download an app, create an account, find a promo code — all just to redeem 25% off? That's friction stacked on friction. I'm not a tech holdout, but I'm also not doing that for shoes I'm not even sure you carry in my size.
- **Preview text leaked formatting junk.** That string of blank characters after "Dress sandals starting at $39.99" tells me something went sideways in the technical setup, even if I can't quite put my finger on it. It just looked messy in my inbox.
- **Zero personalization evident.** This reads like a mass women's blast that accidentally landed in my inbox. DSW has my purchase history — I've bought men's New Balance and Rockport there. None of that intelligence shows up here.
- **Mother's Day module is irrelevant to me as a buyer.** Maybe I'd shop for my wife, but there's no men's gifting angle, no "get her something she'll love" framing — just more women's product pushed at a female buyer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment by gender before sending.** I should be getting men's shoes front and center, not sandal spreads. This is basic.
- 2. **Simplify the app redemption path.** If the deal requires the app, either make that benefit feel worth it ("app-only pricing, always better deals") or offer an alternative code for non-app users. Don't lock the offer behind a download.
- 3. **Lead with comfort technology for guys like me.** Something like "Wide-width walking shoes, 25% off" would've made me scroll further.
- 4. **Fix the preview text padding.** Clean it up — it looked broken on my iPhone.
- 5. **Use my history.** I've bought from DSW. Show me what I might actually want.

| 3 | All `@font-face` blocks | `Src:` uses a capital `S` — invalid per the CSS spec. Browsers tolerate it, but it will silently fail in strict parsers and some email clients. Should be lowercase `src:`. |
