## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has no confirmed hard defects, but it ships with no plain-text part and no alt text on any of its 13 images, and the QA tool couldn't verify any tracked link. Every 406/403 link failure comes from the Cordial click-tracker rejecting the probe, so none of them is confirmed as a real broken link.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links failed**: 11 returned 406 and the unsubscribe link returned 403. They all use the same wrapper, `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6adfbec87dea1b8dba088629:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`.
  - The wrapper is a signed JWT redirect, and a uniform 406/403 across every wrapped link looks like bot or User-Agent filtering. It doesn't look like a dead destination.
  - The `jwtP` payload decodes to valid `linkUrl` targets, for example `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/best-sellers/black/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…` and `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=…`.
  - **Verdict:** QA coverage is inconclusive. The failures are not evidence of broken links.
- **Wrapped links:** None of the probed links is a direct `hoka.com` href. Every link, including unsubscribe, depends on `e.emails.hoka.com` staying up and on the JWT staying valid. The payload has `"ce":86400`, which looks like a 24-hour expiry. If that is an expiry, clicks after 24 hours may fail on the signed links (including unsubscribe); I could not verify this from the data.
- **Coverage gap:** 14 links were skipped, so footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are two open pixels, `https://e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/1664:…` and `https://mg.emails.hoka.com/o/eJyM…`. Both lack `alt`, and the second is on a different subdomain (`mg.`) from the click tracker.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 13 images, including every hero and product image such as `US_BondiSR_TopLeft_1110521-BBLC.jpg`, `US_20261109_TripleBlackStyles_a.jpg`…`_e.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - Each of these images is wrapped in an `<a>`, so the link text reads as `[image: <filename>]`. A screen reader or an image-blocked client will announce the filename as the link name.
- **No plain-text part:** The text version is 0 chars, so image-blocked and text-only clients get nothing. The email also reads as an HTML-only message to spam filters.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **Charset meta:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does not declare a charset.
- **Conflicting viewport tags:** The first has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on some clients. The second has `user-scalable=yes`. These contradict each other.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values. They are harmless but are template leftovers.
- **Web fonts:** The `@font-face` sources point to `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Most clients ignore them, so this only matters if the fallback stack is poor. The truncated source doesn't show the fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FirstName]` appear in the visible source. The recipient identifiers (`hmail=`, `cordial_id=`) are populated in every decoded URL. The HTML is truncated, so I could not check the body copy.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe:** A link is present (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9…`). It returned 403 to the probe, which is probably the same tracker filtering. The destination is not confirmed to work.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found because the AgentMail relay strips it. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown, not failing.
- **Not verifiable:** The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (one-click) and the physical postal address in the footer. The HTML is truncated and no headers were supplied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTMs are consistent across every decoded link:
- `utm_source=US_Broadcast`
- `utm_medium=email`
- `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261109_TripleBlackShoes_STO_A60Day_270BlackShoesTag_270BestSellersTag_AIRFMHigh_1105Openers_WishListAdd`

Destinations are on `hoka.com`. The product links carry `dwvar_<SKU>_color=BBLC`, which matches the black-colorway theme:
- Bondi SR `1110521`
- Clifton 11 `1176572`
- Bondi 9 `1162012`
- Kaha 3 GTX `1162530`

Four hero and banner images (`_a`, `_b`, `_c`, `_d`) all point to the same `/best-sellers/black/` URL. That is a design choice, not a defect. The landing pages weren't fetched here, so page content and stock are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracked links with a browser-like User-Agent or from a whitelisted IP, or decode `jwtP` and check the `linkUrl` targets directly. Don't treat the 406/403 results as real failures until then.
2. Add a plain-text alternative part.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to all 13 images. Use descriptive alt on the product and hero links, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. Fill in `<title>`, change the charset tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, and remove the duplicate `maximum-scale=1` viewport tag.
5. Check the `List-Unsubscribe` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw message. The AgentMail relay strips them.
6. Confirm the Cordial link and unsubscribe JWT expiry (`ce:86400`) doesn't break clicks after 24 hours.
