# Up to 40% OFF: Select Nike you *need*
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- Nike up top got me to open it, but that's the last thing in this email meant for me. Two seconds of scrolling and it's heeled boots, red high-tops in what look like women's sizing, fringy sandals, rhinestone slingbacks, and a big green banner shouting "heels from $29.99." Not one shoe on this page is a men's walking or comfort shoe.
- This is the same DSW pattern I've been getting all week now — Nike or some other men's-recognizable name gets me to tap open, then it's wall-to-wall women's footwear the second the page loads. At this point I know the drill before I even finish scrolling.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on. Nothing here gets forwarded, nothing gets clicked. If DSW wants my business they need to show me a men's section, not bait me with a swoosh and switch me to stilettos.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF SELECT STYLES FROM NIKE" hero with the Nike swoosh is bold, readable, and got my thumb moving in the first place.
- The layout is clean and scannable — clear section breaks, price tags under each shoe, no rendering glitches.
- "Shop By Size" and "Shop Wide Width" near the bottom are a nice touch for guys like me who need specific fits, if only there'd been men's product to size.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — boots, sandals, heels, flats — is women's. Not one men's sneaker or walking shoe anywhere on the page.
- "Your Mid Week Mood Boost" and "so-you shoes" is marketing-speak that doesn't talk to a 62-year-old guy looking for something comfortable to wear.
- "SHOP WOMEN" is the first and most prominent button — no "Shop Men" button exists anywhere I can see.
- The green "Heels from $29.99" banner near the bottom is the loudest specific price in the whole email, and it's for a shoe I'll never buy.
- The Nike headline promised something relevant, but there isn't a single Nike product pictured — total bait-and-switch, same complaint I had on the 40% off Nike send from two days ago.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men from women — if DSW knows I've never clicked a heel or a sandal in months of emails, stop sending me the women's template. That's the single biggest fix.
- 2. Show actual Nike men's product under that headline instead of using the brand name to open the email then pivoting entirely to women's boots and flats.
- 3. Add a "Shop Men" button with equal visual weight to "Shop Women" — right now there isn't one anywhere in the layout.
- 4. Lead with comfort/cushioning language for a men's segment — arch support, all-day comfort, slip-on ease — not "mood boost" copy.
- 5. If there genuinely is no men's Nike sale this week, don't put Nike in the subject line at all — send me the general clearance email instead and skip the bait.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers, Hoka, New Balance and more — all under $60`
