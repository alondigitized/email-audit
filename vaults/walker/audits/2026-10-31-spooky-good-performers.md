- The "Members Only: $30 off $200+" banner is a real, specific offer.
- "Shop Best Sellers" is a big black button that's easy to tap with my thumb.
- The line about gummy bears at an aid station made me smile. It's friendly, not pushy.

## What's weak

- The "Spooky-good" subject has nothing to do with the body, so I felt a little tricked.
- The $30 offer is in a skinny strip up top, and I have to spend $200+ to get it. The fine print at the bottom is tiny and I can't read it without pinching.
- The "Fit to rip" trail shoe and the splashing runner in red shoes are for athletes. There's no walker, no slip-on, and no mention of arch support or comfort.
- "Free Expedited Shipping * *" is only for new text signups on a first full-price order. That's a bait-ish feeling for someone who's already a customer.
- The "Shop Wide Sizes" and "Get Free Shipping" buttons are small, and the footer links ("Locate a Store", "FLY for 30 Guarantee") are plain, tiny text.

- 1. Make the subject say what's inside, like the best-selling cushioned shoes, instead of a Halloween pun that never shows up.
- 2. Move the $30 offer into the hero as a real button or badge, with the deadline ("ends tonight") written big. The fine print says 10/31, but I only found that by squinting.
- 3. Add a line under the best sellers about cushioning and comfort, such as "most cushioned" or "all-day comfort", and a price under each shoe so I can do my price-vs-quality math.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Cordial-sent HTML broadcast. All 12 probed tracking links failed in the automated probe, the plain-text part is empty, and 17 images have no alt text. The probe failures are probably the tracker rejecting the QA client rather than real dead links (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Every probed link failed.** 11 links returned 406 and the Unsubscribe link returned 403. All are on `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...:1/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`.
  - Affected links: the hero and bestseller banners, Clifton 11, Bondi 9, Bondi SR, the HOKA logo, and `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`.
  - Failures on one wrapper domain, all with 4xx bot-style codes (406/403), point to the tracker blocking the probe's user agent. That is more likely than 12 separate broken destinations.
  - This is unconfirmed. Destination health was not verified, because the JWT-wrapped redirect was never followed to a 200.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 18 of 30 links were skipped by design, so footer, social, and utility links are untested.
- **Link text is unusable for image links.** The probe records link text as `[image: US_Clifton11_TopLeft_1176573-WWH.jpg]` and similar. This is a symptom of the missing alt text in section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **17 images have no alt text.** Examples:
  - `US_20261029_WidStyles_a.jpg`
  - `US_20261031_Bestsellers_update.jpg` through `_d.jpg`
  - Six product tiles (Clifton 11, Bondi 9, Bondi SR, Clifton 11 GTX)
  - `US_Q3_SMSCTA_DoubleAsterisk.jpg`
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
  - Both tracking pixels: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`
  - Because the product tiles are images wrapped in links, the links have no accessible name when images are off or a screen reader is used.
  - The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Charset declaration is invalid.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute, so it doesn't declare a charset.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Duplicate viewport metas.** The first has `maximum-scale=1` and the second has `user-scalable=yes`. The first also restricts pinch-zoom.
- **Malformed CSS in the mobile block.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have no values, so the declarations are dropped.
- **Focus outlines removed.** The global reset `* {… outline:none; …}` removes keyboard focus indicators. This matters little in email clients but is still an accessibility regression.
- **Remote webfonts.** The `@font-face` rules load `TheFutureHOKA` from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...`. Most clients ignore these rules, so confirm the font stack has fallbacks. The truncated HTML doesn't show it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `hmail=` and `cordial_id=` parameters are populated (see section 6). The HTML was truncated, so the body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing.** The QA check reports a text version of 0 chars. This is a deliverability and accessibility gap, and spam filters weigh it.
- **Authentication is unverified.** `Authentication-Results` was not found. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, because AgentMail relay stripped or didn't expose the header. This is a QA-environment limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Unsubscribe link is present but unverified.** It appears in the footer and resolves via the tracker to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`, decoded from the `linkUrl` claim in the JWT payload. The probe got a 403, probably the same bot-blocking as above.
- **Not verifiable from the provided data.** The `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address are not in the data. The HTML was truncated and the headers weren't provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are consistent.** Decoded from the JWT payload, every destination carries `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261031_BestSellers_PromoBanner_A180Day_270Purchasers_…_WishlistAdd`. This is one campaign value across all links, which keeps attribution clean.
- **No `utm_content` or `utm_term`.** Every link shares the same campaign string, so analytics can't tell which tile or banner drove a click.
- **Destinations match the content.** Product links go to specific PDPs with colour pre-selected, for example `/en/us/womens-everyday-running-gear/clifton-11/1176573.html?dwvar_1176573_color=WWH`. Bondi 9 additionally sets `dwvar_1162012_width=Regular`.
- **A hashed email identifier is appended.** Every destination URL carries `hmail=9153f33f…` and `cordial_id=69e56048…`. `hmail` is a hash of the recipient's email address. The `cordial_id` value also appears as `cid` on the unsubscribe URL. The Cordial tracker redirect itself uses signed JWTs (`jwtH`/`jwtP`/`jwtS`).
- **The wrapped links can't be shared.** Each wrapped link carries a per-send token and an `iat` claim (`1793448400`, 2026-10-31). Forwarded emails therefore resolve with the original recipient's identifiers.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` text to all 17 images.** Use `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels. Start with the linked product tiles and the banner images.
2. **Add a plain-text MIME part.** The current text part is 0 chars.
3. **Re-test links with a browser-like user agent or a real inbox click-through.** Confirm that the 406/403 responses are tracker bot-blocking. Prioritise the unsubscribe link.
4. **Fix the head.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, add a `<title>`, merge the two viewport tags, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. **Remove or complete the empty-value CSS rules** (`.nav_padding`, `.nav_border`). Reconsider the global `outline:none`.
6. **Add `utm_content` per link or slot** (for example `hero`, `clifton11_tl`) so per-tile performance can be measured.
7. **Capture raw headers.** Check `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` from a direct delivery, not the AgentMail relay. Check the full HTML footer for the postal address.
