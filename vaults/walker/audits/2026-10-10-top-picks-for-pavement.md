# Top picks for pavement
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- Well, HOKA's got a shoe ad again, and the lime green is hard to miss. "Road icons" is huge and I can read it without squinting. But it's a running email. My feet go to the mailbox, not a marathon. "Efficiency" and "Rocket X 3" and "race day" aren't me. I'm 62 and I walk.
- The top bar caught me first: "Buy 2+ items, get a free sock 3-pack*." That's a real offer, but it's a thin strip at the top with an asterisk. The fine print at the bottom says it ends October 11 and has exclusions. Today is the 10th, so that's a real deadline, and the email never says so up top.
- Two products speak to me: Clifton 11 and Bondi 9 under "For smooth comfort." Bondi is the cushioned shoe I looked at in the October 2 email. ## What's working

- "For smooth comfort" is a heading I understand right away. Clifton and Bondi are the shoes I'd actually look at.
- The headlines are big and the product names are easy to read on my phone.
- The black "Shop Now" bars are wide, so they're easy to tap with a thumb.
- The sock offer is a real dollar-value freebie, and the fine print gives a clear end date.
- It's a shoe email from a brand I know, which matches the Oct 2 Bondi email I saw.

## What's weak

- The offer is squeezed into a small top banner. The deadline is buried at the very bottom.
- Half the email is about speed and marathons. "You + 26.2" and "Rocket X 3" aren't for a walker.
- There are no prices anywhere. I can't do my price-vs-quality math against Brooks or New Balance.
- Four shoes get one "Shop Now" button, and it's unclear where it goes. Only the Clifton is a woman's shoe.
- Nothing says arch support, slip-on or all-day walking. The models are young runners.
- The footer links are small, and the legal text is tiny and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the sock offer and the Oct 11 deadline in the hero, in big type. Then it feels like a real reason to act today.
- 2. Lead with cushioned walking shoes, with a men's Bondi and Clifton and an easy-on option. Cut the race-day section or make it a short footer item.
- 3. Add prices and one line on cushion or support under each shoe. Link each shoe to its own page.
- 4. Give each shoe its own button, like "Shop Bondi 9," instead of one "Shop Now."
- 5. Show a few men's walking shoes in a regular setting, with older models.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure is standard table-based Cordial markup, but there are two confirmed defects: no plain-text part (0 chars) and no alt text on any image (15 of 15, including every linked image). All 12 probed links returned 403/406, which is probably the ESP rejecting the scanner rather than genuinely dead links (see section 2). The HTML source was truncated, so footer, header and merge-token checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 links returned 406 and the Unsubscribe link returned 403.** Every probed URL is a Cordial click-tracking redirect (`https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6ab6c76202f812c8090dee79:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`).
  - This is almost certainly bot filtering on the tracking domain, not broken destinations. The same status on every link, including the unsubscribe and the hero, makes a content problem unlikely.
  - I can't confirm the links work for real recipients. Treat this as unverified, not as confirmed breakage.
  - A 403/406 on the unsubscribe link needs a manual or real-client check, because a failing opt-out would be a compliance issue.
- **The destination URLs are readable in the `jwtP` payload.** These are the ones I decoded:
  - `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/best-sellers/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=…`
  - `…/womens-everyday-running-gear/clifton-11/1176573.html?dwvar_1176573_color=SLWL&dwvar_1176573_size=7&dwvar_1176573_width=Regular&utm_…`
  - `…/mens-everyday-running-shoes/bondi-9/1162011.html?dwvar_1162011_color=FLCK&…`
  - `…/womens-road/mach-7/1171938.html?…`
  - `…/race-shoes/rocket-x-3/1168724.html?dwvar_1168724_color=FLCT&…`
  - `…/return-start/?utm_…`
- **Redundant click-tracking hops.** Each link goes through two layers: `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/…` (Cordial) and a JWT-wrapped redirect. That's normal for the ESP, but it is the only reason the destinations can't be checked directly.
- **Open-tracking pixels are present.** There are two: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. The second is on a different subdomain, `mg.`, which looks like a Mailgun-style path. Two pixels on two infrastructures is unusual, though not a defect on its own.
- **The footer "return-start" link text is a bare URL.** `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` is shown as the visible text, but the href is a tracking wrapper. Some clients flag mismatched visible and actual URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on all images (15 of 15).** The QA data lists these, including every linked image:
  - The promo banner, `US_20261006_SneakerWeekPromo.jpg`.
  - The hero and banner images, `US_20261010_Road_BestSellers_a/b/c/d/e/f/g.jpg`.
  - The product images (Clifton 11, Bondi 9, Mach 7, Rocket X3).
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - Both tracking pixels.
  - Because the images are the link content, the 10 linked images (plus the "HOKA" logo link) expose only filenames or nothing to screen readers. With images blocked, all email content disappears, since the copy appears to be baked into the images.
  - The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** The head has no title text.
- **Conflicting viewport metas.** There are two viewport tags. One has `maximum-scale=1` and no `user-scalable`, and the other has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`. Capping the scale at 1 blocks pinch-zoom in some clients, which is an accessibility problem.
- **The charset meta is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it isn't a valid declaration. This is usually harmless, since the MIME header sets the charset.
- **Invalid CSS.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values from a template variable that didn't resolve. Clients ignore them, but they show the template wasn't fully populated.
- **Web fonts are hosted on `hoka.com`.** `TheFutureHOKA` is loaded from `/on/demandware.static/…` over HTTPS. Most email clients ignore `@font-face`, so a fallback stack is required. I couldn't see one in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%…%%` or similar tokens appear. The truncated HTML means I couldn't check the body or footer. The empty `px` CSS values in section 3 are the only sign of an unresolved template variable.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is present** and resolves (via the tracker) to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`. The probe returned 403, so this is unconfirmed (see section 2).
- **Plain-text part is missing.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:**
  - The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
  - The physical postal address, which is likely in the truncated footer.
  - The sender address `info@emails.hoka.com` matches the tracking domains `e.emails.hoka.com` and `mg.emails.hoka.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are consistent on every decoded link:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261010_BestSellers_Road_GWP_STO_A120Day_720Purchasers_365RoadTag_270BestSellersTag_270RaceTag`.
- **`utm_source=US_Broadcast` names the send type, not a source.** Analytics rules that expect a value like `email` or `cordial` would group this unusually.
- **`utm_campaign` embeds audience segmentation** (`720Purchasers`, `365RoadTag`, `270RaceTag`). It is visible in the URL and fragments reporting, because each segment variant becomes its own campaign value.
- **Every link also carries `hmail=<64-hex hash>&cordial_id=…`.** The 64-character hash appears to be a hashed email identifier passed to the site as a query parameter. Confirm that it is intended and covered by your privacy policy.
- **UTMs on the unsubscribe URL and the "return-start" utility link** add noise to campaign attribution for non-commercial clicks.
- **Landing alignment can't be fully checked** because the redirects block the probe. The decoded paths are consistent with the subject ("Top picks for pavement"): a `best-sellers/?prefn1=shoeSurface&prefv1=road` filter plus road-shoe product pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text multipart alternative. This is the clearest confirmed defect.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 13 content and logo images. Use `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels.
3. Verify the 403/406 results with a real-client click-through, or allowlist the scanner on the Cordial tracking domain. Check the unsubscribe flow in particular.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, de-duplicate the viewport meta, and fill in `<title>`.
5. Clean up the unresolved `px` CSS values from the template.
6. Normalize `utm_source`, and move audience segmentation out of `utm_campaign` into `utm_content` or `utm_term`. Strip UTMs from the unsubscribe and utility links.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers to check `List-Unsubscribe`, authentication results, the physical address and any residual merge tokens.
