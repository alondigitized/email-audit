# Your favorites won't wait
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- Another HOKA Black Friday email. This is the seventh or so this week, after "Look who made the sale" and "Not like other sales" yesterday. The big white "It's go time" on dark blue is easy to read on my phone without glasses. The Bondi 9 below it is the shoe I'd actually buy. But the subject, "Your favorites won't wait," promised something about me, and nothing in the email is about *my* favorites. It's the same member-pricing blast I've already seen.
- The offer math is also muddy. The yellow banner says up to 30% off. The hero says $40 off $200 and up. The Bondi box says on sale. The fine print says up to 20% off Bondi and up to 30% off other styles. I had to squint at that tiny type to find out what I'd really save on the shoe I want. I'd click the Bondi button because I like that shoe, but I'd check the price on the site first. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Black Friday / It's go time" headline is huge, high contrast and readable without zooming.
- The Bondi 9 as a single hero matches what I buy: "super-cushioned" is the word I care about.
- The Shop Now buttons are wide, rounded and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The $40 / $50 / $60 spend tiers are clear, simple price math.
- The "FLY for 30 Guarantee" and free shipping links in the footer reassure me about trying a pair.

## What's weak

- The Bondi text sits in a gray, semi-see-through box over a blurry shoe. It's low contrast and hard to read.
- The offers conflict: up to 30% off, up to 20% off Bondi, and member-only spend tiers. I can't tell what I'd actually pay.
- The member requirement and the 11/27–12/1 dates are buried in tiny fine print, so the urgency isn't visible up top.
- The shoe is a tight close-up of the heel in pink and white. There's no price, no men's color, and no sense of the arch support or walking comfort I'm shopping for.
- The cadence is exhausting, with the same Black Friday message again and again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the real Bondi 9 sale price, with a plain "was / now," and a men's color. This would do the most for me.
- 2. Put one offer at the top instead of three. State the member requirement and the end date (12/1) in the headline area, not the fine print.
- 3. Put the Bondi text on a solid background so it's readable.
- 4. Change the button copy from "Shop Now" to "Shop the Bondi 9," and add a line about cushioning and all-day walking.
- 5. Make the "favorites" claim true by showing shoes I've viewed or bought, or cut the word.
