# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- It's the cart nag again, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. This is the same routine as "Did you forget something?" earlier today and "Don't leave these styles behind!" yesterday. The "cart" here is a black sneaker with a blue skull painted on it, the Day of the Dead UNO. I'd never put that in a cart. It's a long way from the cushioned, slip-on comfort I shop for.
- The 20% OFF for Skechers Plus members banner is the best thing in the email, but it sits way down the page below the product grid. The fine print says online only and excludes select styles. Six "more options" show up, and three of them are women's. Only the Contour Foam slip-in and the Murette slipper really look like me.
- I'd skim it and not click. I wouldn't forward it either. Mark it read.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF banner with "Valid thru 11/2" is clear and has a real deadline.
- The Men's Slip-ins Murette slipper and the Contour Foam sneaker are my kind of shoe.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" is a big, obvious button with large text I can read on my phone.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) is easy to tap.
- The Skechers logo and look are familiar, so I trust the sender.

## What's weak

- The "cart" item is a skull-painted sneaker I never looked at. It makes the whole cart claim feel fake.
- Three of the six "options" are women's shoes, so it doesn't read as aimed at a 62-year-old man.
- The best offer, 20% off, is buried below the fold and doesn't show up until after the product grid.
- The fine print excludes sale styles and select items, and it's online only. That takes some of the shine off the 20%.
- It repeats the cart-nag pattern from 10/28, 10/29 and 10/30. I'm tired of it.
- There's nothing about arch support, cushioning or hands-free comfort, and no prices on any of the shoes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Only send a cart email when there really is a cart, and show the actual item. A shoe I never looked at breaks the trust.
- 2. Put the 20% Plus offer at the top and tie the CTA to it.
- 3. Fill the grid with men's slip-ins and comfort features, with prices and sale prices.
- 4. Cap the cart reminders at one per week and let the Halloween offer do the work.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% online on comfort slip-ins. Ends Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders structurally (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, mobile media queries), but it has a failing unsubscribe probe, no plain-text part, and 12 images served over `http://`. Authentication status and the final click destinations couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe returned HTTP 400 (QA FAIL).**
  - URL: `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…&externalId=2U3eG`.
  - The cause is unconfirmed. It could be a single-use token or a probe-client rejection rather than a dead link. It needs a manual check, because a broken unsubscribe is a compliance problem (see section 5).
- **Two 429s are inconclusive, not failures.**
  - Both are on `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirects: "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong."
  - The probe was rate-limited, so I can't say whether the links resolve.
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 32 skipped. Footer and social links are deprioritized, so their status is unknown.
- **Every link I can see is wrapped in an Attentive click-tracking redirect.** That includes the "web version" link in row 1. The destinations aren't visible in the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.** 12 images load from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`.
  - Affected: the logo (`dde00662…png`), the "Complete Your Order" hero (`dfcc8fd7…jpg`), `a6ec5198…jpg`, and the footer assets. The footer assets are the App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and "Text" images.
  - Some clients may block or rewrite them.
- **Missing alt text.** `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` has none. The Attentive open pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`) also has none. For the pixel, `alt=""` is the fix, and it is low severity.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Ineffective charset meta.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it doesn't declare the charset.
- **No `role="presentation"` on layout tables.** None of the `nl-container`, `row` or `row-content` tables in the visible source has it, so screen readers may announce them as data tables. The source is truncated, so I can only confirm this for the portion shown.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or subject. The `attn_macro` value in the unsubscribe URL decodes to the literal string `unsubscribeLink`, which is Attentive's normal macro naming.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link failing (400).** CAN-SPAM requires a working opt-out, so this is the highest-priority item if the failure is real.
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". If this isn't a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay, it's a deliverability and accessibility gap.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC unknown.** `Authentication-Results` wasn't found, which is expected via the relay. Nothing can be concluded either way.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address in the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The destinations sit behind Attentive redirects, and the primary CTA "Complete Your Order" returned 429, so I couldn't confirm that it lands on a restored cart. UTM parameters and the final landing page weren't observed. Three domains are involved: the sender `msgs.skechers.com`, the tracking host `skechers.e.attentivemail.com`, and the image host `image.emails.skechers.com`. I'm noting that only as context, not as an issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually open the unsubscribe URL in a real browser. If it's a real failure, fix the Attentive unsubscribe token or endpoint.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part, or confirm that the relay is stripping it.
3. Switch all 12 `image.emails.skechers.com` image sources to `https://`.
4. Add alt text to `a6ec5198…jpg`, `alt=""` to the open pixel, and a non-empty `<title>`.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables, and `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to the charset meta.
6. Re-probe the 429 links with throttling or retries. Resolve the full redirect chain to check UTM parameters and cart-restore behavior on "Complete Your Order".
7. Pull the raw headers to check SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, and the footer address.
