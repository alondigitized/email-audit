# Technical Audit: "Did you forget something? (Psst... Up to 50% off is live)"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an Attentive-sent abandoned-cart email (From: `hello@msgs.skechers.com`). Its tokenized unsubscribe link failed the probe with HTTP 400. The message has no plain-text part, and many images are served over `http://` or lack alt text. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and later rows could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link failed (400):** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1MmZpSFY0ajRYThIDMVN5GJmSqgYgAg&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&...`
  - It is the only hard failure in the QA run.
  - The token may be single-use or tied to the recipient, so the probe itself could have caused the 400. A manual click-through is needed to confirm.
- **Primary CTA is inconclusive:** "Ready to check out? Complete your order" and the linked image `de642685-...jpg` both returned 429 from `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. This is rate limiting, not a confirmed break, and the destination was never resolved.
- **Probe coverage was partial:** 12 links were probed and 31 skipped, so most links are unverified.
- **Redirect wrappers hide destinations:** the "web version" link and the CTAs all use opaque `attentivemail.com/e/c/<blob>` URLs. Their final destinations can't be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources:** 11 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115.../m/...`.
  - They include `a6ec5198-...jpg`, the app badges ("Available on the App Store", "Android app on Google Play"), the "Curbside Pickup", "Afterpay" and "Find a Store" icons, and the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons.
  - The same host serves `https://` for other images in this email, such as `de642685-...jpg`, so HTTPS is probably available.
  - Clients that block non-HTTPS content can drop these images.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `de642685-77b3-46fa-950b-5bd9ee6800e1.jpg` is wrapped in a link, so the link has no accessible name.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` also has no alt text.
  - The open-tracking pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...`) has none either. This one is minor.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this hurts accessibility and the web-view title.
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing. The encoding then depends on the MIME header alone.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`:** the tables in the visible source, such as `.row-1` and `.nl-container`, have no role attribute, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens appear in the preheader or the visible rows. Cart-item content, if any, sits in the truncated part and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe is present but failing:** the link text is "Unsubscribe" and the target returned 400 (see §2). If it really is broken, that is a CAN-SPAM exposure.
- **No plain-text alternative:** the QA run reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). This hurts deliverability and accessibility. It may partly come from how the AgentMail relay stores the message.
- **Authentication unverified:** no `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an observability gap in the relay, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Not verifiable from the data:** the physical postal address and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. The footer is truncated and no headers were provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be confirmed:** all CTAs go through Attentive redirectors, so UTM or attribution parameters can't be seen at the source. 429 responses stopped the QA run from following them.
- **Landing-page alignment is unconfirmed:** the subject promises "Up to 50% off", and the preheader says "Your cart is waiting. So is the Cyber Week sale." The 429 responses mean the cart and sale destinations were never fetched.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Click the unsubscribe link manually from a live inbox to confirm it works. If it fails, fix the Attentive unsubscribe macro (`attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r`) before the next send.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. Attentive templates support this.
3. Switch all 11 `http://image.emails.skechers.com` image URLs to `https://`.
4. Add `alt` text to `de642685-...jpg` and `a6ec5198-...jpg`. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel.
5. Fill in `<title>`. Change the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`. Add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
6. Re-probe the CTAs with throttling, or follow the redirects with a browser session. This will resolve the 429s and let you check UTM parameters and the landing page.
7. Capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) from the original message rather than the AgentMail relay. That would settle SPF, DKIM and DMARC and the one-click unsubscribe check.
