# BOGO 50% off starts…NOW!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- Finally, something different from DSW. After a week of purple "25% OFF*" VIP banners, this one is a loud orange "BOGO 50% OFF*" and I noticed the change right away. Buy a pair, get half off a second pair is a deal I can follow, and I like that "no code needed" is in the fine print. It's a long weekend, so the timing makes sense.
- The trouble starts below the hero. The shoes shown are a wedge sneaker, a platform clog, a Mary Jane flat, a shearling boot, and two ankle boots. That's a women's lineup, and nothing in it suits a 62-year-old guy who wants cushioning and arch support. The "Shop Men" link is there, but it's small and crowded in with the others. The footnote text is tiny and I'd have to squint at it on my phone.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on "Shop Men," because BOGO is real money when I'm buying walking shoes anyway. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and maybe walk into the store, since the barcode is at the bottom.

## What's working

- The giant "BOGO 50% OFF*" in orange is easy to read at arm's length, and it's a clear change from the purple 25% banner I've seen all week.
- The "Buy any pair of shoes, get 50% off a second qualifying pair" line is plain English. I know the deal in one glance.
- "LONG WEEKEND, BIG DEAL" feels timely, and it doesn't shout "last chance" like the earlier VIP emails did.
- The in-store barcode and "no code needed" make it easy to use. I shop the stores as much as the phone.
- The wide width and wide calf buttons under "Shop by Size" are a nice touch for older feet.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is women's and trendy: wedges, clogs, flats. Nothing is built for comfort or walking, so I don't feel like the target.
- The "Shop Men" link is a small text link in a row of four, and it's easy to miss when my thumb is trying to tap it.
- The deadline is hidden. Oct 5–7 shows up only in the tiny print and not in the hero.
- The footnote says Skechers is on the list of brands that qualify, but the email never names a comfort brand or mentions arch support or cushioning anywhere.
- It's long. The holiday services block, shop by size, app download, SMS signup, VIP pitch and Afterpay all pile up after the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give men their own hero or product row. Show slip-ons and walking shoes from Skechers, Hoka, Brooks and New Balance, and make the "Shop Men" button big.
- 2. Put the dates in the hero ("Oct 5–7, no code needed") so I know I have three days.
- 3. Put cushioning and slip-on cues on the products, such as "Memory foam," "Arch support," and "Slip-ins."
- 4. Cut the bottom half down to one short strip: shipping, pickup, and the VIP pitch.
- 5. Add a "pair two comfort shoes, save more" line so I can do the price-versus-quality math.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off shoes, Oct 5–7. No code.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy one pair, get half off the second`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walking shoes and slip-ons included. 3 days only, no code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pair up Skechers, Hoka or New Balance and save. Ends Oct 7.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

It also has a very URL-heavy plain-text part and two images without alt attributes. The HTML source is truncated and no headers were supplied, so several categories can't be fully assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link (`https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/sTWjAJptI6zLU0hDakk9bA~~/AAQRxRA~/hRZUUcnO_…`) returned 400 on probe. This is the only hard failure. A 400 from a Braze tracking redirect can sometimes come from the probe rather than the link, for example an unsupported client or a token problem. Verify manually from a real client before treating it as a send-time defect.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 67 links were probed and 55 were skipped, so the rest of the email's link health is unverified.
- **Very long tracked URLs:** The failing link is about 900 characters of opaque base64 path. This is typical of Braze link wrapping and is the cause of the plain-text warning below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/3-ZOeYwgP61p--VQhuAejg~~/AAQRxRA~/n0oCMqoAD9…`, a tracked image from the `/q/` path.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif…`, a tracking pixel.
