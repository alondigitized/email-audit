# Feast on this: 30% OFF + a FREEBIE 🤤
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same pink banner and giant "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th, and I've lost count of how many that is. The subject promises a "freebie" and the preview mentions boot doorbusters. The freebie turns out to be a duffel bag that only comes with a $99 purchase. That's a bonus for spending, not a freebie. The doorbusters are squeezed into a thin black bar at the very top.
- I'm 62 and I buy comfort: cushioned, slip-on, arch support. Nothing here says that. The "So New, So You" row shows a fuzzy winter boot, a retro sneaker and two loafers with gold buckles. They're fine shoes, but nothing in the row tells me they're comfortable. Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. It gets marked read and deleted.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF is huge and I understood it in one second.
- The "SHOP MEN" link is there, so I can skip the women's stuff.
- The size grid with "Shop Wide Width" is useful. Wide fit matters at my age.
- Free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and the Afterpay line were easy to spot.
- The DSW name and logo are familiar, so I trust it's the real store.

## What's weak

- The subject said "freebie" and the preview said boot doorbusters. I had to scroll to find a bag with a $99 minimum, and the doorbusters are in a bar at the top.
- The email is the same creative I've seen all week. It's the seventh or eighth pink "30% OFF*" in a row.
- The top banner text is cut off. "THE SEASON'S BIGGEST DEALS ARE ON!" runs off the edge, and the logo's "Wonderland" tag overlaps the text.
- The product row has no prices or comfort cues. The loafers and fashion sneaker don't look like they were picked for someone my age.
- The asterisk on the 30% leads to a wall of small grey fine print at the bottom. I can't read that on my phone without zooming.
- The "Shop Women / Men / Kids / Everything" links are small and crowded together for my thumbs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the hero. If the boots are the draw, lead with the boots and show them above the fold. Don't promise a freebie that needs a $99 purchase.
- 2. Pick one clear offer, and don't send the same creative every day. Change the creative, or send fewer emails.
- 3. Add a comfort row for people like me: slip-ons, cushioned walkers and wide widths, with prices.
- 4. Make the tap targets bigger and the fine print simpler.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Boots from $19.96 + 30% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free bag with $99: Black Friday at DSW`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code BFDEALS takes 30% off almost everything, in stores and online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned slip-ons and wide widths are on sale. Boots start at $19.96.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: an unrendered `%%gender%%` merge token and a tracked CTA link ("Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off") that returned HTTP 400. Everything else is a warning, or can't be assessed because the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **CTA link returned 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/gmg7GJkOxn_wP0oPqgMGtg~~/AAQRxRA~/aQeBUV...` (long tracked redirect).

  - It could also be the prober being rejected, so re-test with a real client before declaring it broken. If it is real, this is the text-signup incentive CTA, so it is a high-value break.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 of 77 links were probed and 65 were skipped. The other links' status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, wrapped image.** An image under `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/X8KQn12XFVgZPXy-bQoBlQ~~/...` has no alt. If it is a linked content image, it will be blank to screen readers and with images blocked.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel.** `o.gif` has no alt. It is a 1x1 pixel, so the fix is `alt=""`, which is low severity.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. Charset is likely still resolved from the Content-Type header.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 27,109 of 33,495 characters (about 81%) are URLs. The long tracked links bloat the text part, which is a deliverability and readability risk.
- **Dark mode and fonts.** `color-scheme` meta tags and `prefers-color-scheme` rules are present. Custom `@font-face` is declared via braze-images. No defects are visible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

  - The `%%…%%` syntax doesn't match the Braze Liquid (`{{ }}`) style used elsewhere in the asset URLs. A template or ESP mismatch is possible.

## 7. Recommendations
