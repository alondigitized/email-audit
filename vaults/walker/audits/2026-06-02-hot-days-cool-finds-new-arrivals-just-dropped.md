# Hot Days, Cool Finds - New Arrivals Just Dropped
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Third Skechers email in two days, and I almost didn't open it. The preview in my inbox looked like someone scrambled a plate of alphabet soup — raw JSON code where the preview text should be. I only opened it because I know the brand and I'm still looking for a summer walking shoe.
- Once I'm in, the BOGO 50% banner grabs me right away — that part they got right. But then the wheels fall off. The big hero is a woman in a white sundress on a beach. The Court & Classics section looks pink and feminine. I see Mary Janes called out as a category. I'm a 62-year-old guy shopping for something cushioned I can slip on for a walk around the neighborhood — I am not the person this email was dressed for.
- The Cozy Fit section, buried as the third block down, is the one thing that actually speaks to me. Slip-on, comfort-focused, men's-friendly silhouette. But by the time I scroll to it I've already lost the thread. I'd file this one under "read and moved on." Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% sits in a bold banner right at the top** — I see the offer before I see anything else, no hunting required.
- **"Made for Comfort" in the hero subhead** — those three words are the only line in the whole email that speaks to why I'm actually shopping Skechers.
- **Cozy Fit® section** — slip-on silhouette, brand-specific comfort line, exactly the category that moves me. Wish it led the email.
- **Personal greeting ("Hi, Walker")** — small thing, but after the recent Member Month email that used my name and points balance, I notice when it's there.
- **No broken images, clean rendering in the body** — whatever render problems exist are in the preview, not the email itself.

## What's weak

- **The preview text is a technical disaster** — raw JSON schema data is showing in my inbox where marketing copy should be. First impression is that something went wrong.
- **Hero model and hero category are squarely women's** — white sundress, beach, Mary Janes as a called-out category. I felt like I opened someone else's email.
- **Court & Classics shoes look and feel feminine** — the pink colorways and the styling say "not for me" before I can even register the offer.
- **Men's content is three scroll-lengths down** — there's a MEN text link buried in the footer, but zero men's product in the visual storytelling.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — this is the third email in two days. The "new arrivals just dropped" urgency doesn't land when I already got a full inbox hit yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — this is the most damaging single problem. A broken preview is the first thing a subscriber sees; it signals technical carelessness and tanks opens.
- 2. **Lead with Cozy Fit or another men's comfort section, not women's summer staples** — if Walker is on the list, Walker should see a Walker shoe above the fold, not a sundress.
- 3. **Move the BOGO offer into the subject line** — "BOGO 50%" is the one concrete hook; the subject line throws it away.
- 4. **Give the Cozy Fit section specific product detail** — a callout like "wide widths available" or "removable insole" would stop a comfort shopper mid-scroll.
- 5. **Add at least one men's model in the hero** — a guy on a boardwalk, at a park, at a weekend market. You don't have to kill the women's hero, just acknowledge I exist.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% Off: New Comfort Styles Just In`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Summer Arrivals for Men — BOGO 50% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy Fit slip-ons, court classics, and summer staples — all at BOGO pricing.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your next walking shoe just dropped. BOGO 50% off sitewide.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Hot Days, Cool Finds" (06-02-2026)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken experience (empty personalization greeting) plus a systemic HTTP image-serving issue affecting all assets hosted on `image.emails.skechers.com`. All other issues are warnings with moderate deliverability/render risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No issues confirmed** on click links — 27 click-redirect URLs behind `click.emails.skechers.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe and cannot be verified for destination integrity or redirect chain depth.

**Tracking pixels present (informational):**
- Open pixel: `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?KRMOZ3NP7HSETCWB65YBNTDFT4...` — standard SFMC open tracking
- 3× Salesforce Krux DMP beacons: `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` and `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif` — audience sync + campaign impression logging
- Validity/Return Path seed pixel: `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499` — no explicit protocol on this `src`, inherits document context; should be explicit `https://`
- Movable Ink pixel: `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` — **plain HTTP**, see §3

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL — Systemic] All `image.emails.skechers.com` assets served over HTTP:**

Every image hosted on Skechers' SFMC image library uses `http://` rather than `https://`. Confirmed instances:

```

http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/9bb508d0-...png  (Google Play badge)
```

Apple Mail 16+, Gmail (web), and ProtonMail will proxy or block mixed-content HTTP image sources. This is a configuration issue in the SFMC content library — the CDN domain almost certainly supports HTTPS; the `http://` scheme is being written into templates explicitly.

**[WARN] Third-party tracking pixel over HTTP:**
`http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` — same blocking risk as above, plus no `alt` attribute on the `<img>`.

**[WARN] Missing alt text:**
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — non-decorative image (no alt provided)
- `o.gif` (ink1000 pixel) — tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly rather than omitting the attribute

**CSS conflict — desktop/mobile content toggling:**

Two conflicting show/hide strategies coexist:

```css
/* Strategy 1 — default block, hide on desktop */
.mobile-content  { display: block; }
.desktop-content { display: none; }
@media (min-width: 768px) { .mobile-content { display: none !important; } .desktop-content { display: block !important; } }

/* Strategy 2 — .show class used in multiple @media blocks at 640px and 480px */
.show { display: block !important; width: 100% !important; ... }
```

These overlap in the 640–768px range and may produce double-rendered or invisible content blocks in tablet-width clients (Samsung Mail, some webmail clients that partially parse media queries).

**[FAIL] Empty first-name merge token — broken greeting:**

QA confirmed: the greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` with no name value. The subscriber record has a null or empty `FirstName` field and the template has no fallback token defined (e.g., no `%%=IIF(EMPTY(FirstName),"there",FirstName)=%%` guard in SFMC AMPscript, or equivalent).

This is the only confirmed broken-experience issue in the send.
