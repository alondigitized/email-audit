"We noticed you noticing us" — no, you didn't, because I never noticed that shoe in the first place. I scrolled straight past it and, credit where due, the "more options" row underneath actually had two things worth a second look — the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman and the Women's Slip-ins Arch Fit Arcade, which oddly enough is more my speed even though it says women's, because Arch Fit means something to my feet.
- The 20% off banner near the bottom is the only real news in this whole email, and it's buried under six other shoe photos I don't care about. If that banner had been the headline instead of an afterthought, I'd have paid attention faster.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on. Not forwarding it, not clicking. Same phantom cart, same routine.

- The 20% off "Cozy Fit & Glide Step" banner with a real code (UPGRADE) is the one legible, honest offer in the email.
- Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman showed up in the grid — plain, brown, sensible, the kind of shoe I'd actually wear.
- Layout's clean enough, nothing overlapping or broken that I could see.
- Afterpay/Klarna and "Find a Store" at the bottom are handy if I did want to buy — good to know the option's there.

## What's weak

- That pink floral D'Lites again. Sixth, seventh, I've stopped counting — I never put it in a cart, so "your cart is still waiting" is just wrong.
- The one hero product shown full-size is a shoe I have zero interest in, while the men's-relevant stuff is crammed into tiny thumbnails further down.
- The real offer — 20% off — is stuck two-thirds of the way down after a wall of shoes I have to scroll past first.
- No loyalty mention at all, and I've bought from them before — feels like they're talking to a stranger, not a repeat customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero image — lead with the Slip-ins or Arch Fit shoe, not a floral D'Lites nobody in my house asked for. That's the single biggest fix.
- 2. Move the 20% off banner up top, right under the headline, not buried after six shoe photos.
- 3. Drop the "cart is still waiting" framing entirely when there's no actual cart — it just makes the email feel dishonest.
- 4. Work in a line about loyalty points or member pricing if I've got an account with them.

Mixed content on an HTTPS-rendered client (or Outlook/webmail proxy) may block these images.
