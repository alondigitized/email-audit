## What's weak

- Five or six offers compete for attention, so nothing stands out. The hero says 15% off, then "Step Up & Save," then a roundup.
- Everything is "select styles" or "some exclusions apply." The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and I can't read it without pinching the screen.
- The BOGO tile is marked "Select Women's," and nothing in the pictures looks like me. I see no men's comfort or walking shoes.
- Brooks at $89.98 is the only comfort-style shoe I recognize, and it's a running shoe, not what I need.
- The preheader is a long tracking link instead of real copy. That looks sloppy.
- The "Shop Now" and "Get Coupon" links are small text, not real buttons, so they're hard to tap with my thumbs.

- 2. Cut the roundup to three tiles. Drop the repeated BOGO boots and clogs that I've now seen three times this week.
- 3. Make the CTAs full-width buttons with larger text.
- 4. Add a men's comfort row with cushioning and arch-support callouts, plus a "Free returns" or "Fit guarantee" line.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based template with extensive client-compatibility CSS. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links unverified:** 28 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and HTTP status are unconfirmed.
- **Possible unresolved token in a pixel URL:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still contains a literal `[UNIQUE]`. If the ESP does not substitute this at send time, every recipient shares the same value and open tracking can't be de-duplicated.
- **Odd character in the open-pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261002_friday_deals_AM​` ends with an invisible zero-width character (U+200B). That can corrupt the `t=` value on the tracker side. It may also be an artifact of the QA extraction, so check the raw source.
- **Nested query format:** the pixel's `t=utm_campaign=...` value is a key=value pair inside a parameter. This is non-standard but probably vendor-specific.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flags both `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` and `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` images. These are tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. The `charset` attribute still works in HTML5, but the content-only form is invalid.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and self-closing `/>` tags. It's harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Link affordance removed globally:** `a, a:link, a:visited... { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` strips underline and color from all links. Unless the template adds styling elsewhere (not visible in the truncated source), text links may be indistinguishable from body text. That is a WCAG 1.4.1 risk.
- **Small mobile text:** `.responsive-text` is forced to `font-size: 12px !important` at `max-width: 600px`, which is small for mobile readability.
- **Light mode only:** `color-scheme: light` and `supported-color-schemes: light` are declared with no dark-mode handling. This is a minor point.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"` and the viewport meta tag are both set.
