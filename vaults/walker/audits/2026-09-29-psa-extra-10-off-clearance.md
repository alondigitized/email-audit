# PSA: Extra 10% off clearance
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. Well, "PSA" is a new way to say the same clearance thing I've been getting all week, so I didn't jump out of my chair. The top banner does have a real number, "Extra 10% off clearance online" with the code GOLDEN, and I saw that right away. That's what got me in.
- Then the hero is a Birkenstock clog with pink socks and rolled-up jeans, followed by a grid of clogs and mules. It's all "smooth transition" and "fresh clogs and mules." That's a fall fashion pitch, not a comfort pitch. It's also aimed at women. The chunky studded heeled clogs and the "Shop All Women" button gave that away. The clearance code is buried in a banner while the pictures push a totally different message. The small "Sneakers" tile in the "Also trending" row is the only thing near my kind of shoe.
- I'd skim it and probably not click. I'd forward it to nobody. I'd mark it read and wait for the sneaker or Brooks email they occasionally slip in.

## What's working

- The "Extra 10% off clearance online" banner is right at the top, with the code GOLDEN. I got the deal in two seconds.
- The Birkenstock hero at least says "comfort" to me. A cork-footbed clog is a shoe I'd look at.
- The "Shop by Size" block with half sizes, plus "Shop Wide Width," is helpful. I wear wide, so that's a real plus.
- The "Free shipping / In-store pickup" icons are clear, and the text is large enough to read on my phone.

## What's weak

- The middle of the email is women's clogs, mules, and a heeled studded clog. Nothing here is for me.
- Nothing is for men. There's a "Men" tab up top, but the body has no men's button. "Shop All Women" is the only catch-all.
- The 10% code is a small line in a coral banner, and the big orange hero has nothing to do with it. Then I had to read fine print to see it's online only and excludes a lot.
- "Smooth Transition" in spaced-out letters is hard to read and says nothing. What's the offer?
- The Shoebox Shuffle contest takes a big purple block for a gift card game. I don't want to play a game to buy shoes.
- There's no price, no arch support or cushioning talk, and no brand names I'd compare, except Birkenstock.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the send by who I am. If I've bought sneakers or walking shoes, lead with men's comfort: Brooks, Skechers, New Balance, with the 10% code on the same image.
- 2. Put the code and the deal in the hero, not a thin banner above it.
- 3. Add a "Shop Men's" button next to "Shop All Women." Right now it's one-sided.
- 4. Show real prices ("Comfort sneakers from $59") so I can do my price-versus-quality math.
- 5. Move the Shoebox Shuffle down, or drop it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken tracked link (the SMS opt-in CTA, HTTP 400) and two images without alt text. The visible source also has a few head-level accessibility and markup defects. The QA run gave a 75% pass rate, and only 12 of 65 links were probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns HTTP 400.** The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/cquF3AKPp4gO03ubBYbrHQ~~/AAQRxRA~/0xh8ZZcB…` (an ESP click-tracking redirect).
  - The failure is on the tracking hop, not a confirmed dead landing page. A 400 from the redirector can come from a malformed or truncated token, or from the probe being rejected.
  - Either way, the CTA needs a manual click test from a real inbox. If it also fails there, the SMS opt-in path is dead for every recipient.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 53 of 65 links were skipped as footer, utility or social links. Their status is unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 21,290 of 25,661 characters (83%) are URLs. This is a deliverability warning caused by long tracking URLs in the text/plain alternative. It is not a broken link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (2).**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/ZAU4rmJVe5Ea_qXcMXMkZA~~/AAQRxRA~/zVOK0J_4…` has no alt attribute. It is likely the open-tracking image.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?…` is a tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` to both so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile clients, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Two meta tags are ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing.
- **No language attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`. This affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` gives no per-message context.
- The visible dark-mode CSS is fine (`color-scheme` meta and `prefers-color-scheme` block). The HTML was truncated, so the body markup could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues confirmed.
- QA states an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and no unsubscribe failure was reported.
- The truncated HTML and QA output do not include the footer postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, or SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. These could not be verified.
- The sender is `dsw@e.dsw.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, and the tracking domain `ablink.dsw.com` is also on-brand.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed.
- Links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so the destination UTM parameters are not visible without following the redirects.
- The tracking pixel URL carries `mi_ecmp=20260929_P261381_V3_W-_Offer_New_B`, which shows campaign attribution exists.
- The QA data did not report final landing URLs, so UTM and landing-page alignment could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Click-test the "Opt-In to Texts" CTA from a real send. If it also fails there, regenerate or fix the tracked link in the ESP template. If it works, treat the 400 as probe rejection.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking images.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Add `lang="en"` (and `xml:lang`) to `<html>`.
6. Shorten or simplify the text/plain part, for example with a short URL per CTA, to bring the URL ratio down.
7. Re-run QA with a higher probe limit, or include the footer and unsubscribe links, and capture the full headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and resolved landing URLs.
