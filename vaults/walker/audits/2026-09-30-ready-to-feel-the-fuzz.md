## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based ESP (SFMC-style) build with a working responsive stylesheet, but it has a malformed `<meta>` charset declaration, seven `http://` image sources, and missing alt text on the recommendation images. The HTML was truncated and no headers were supplied, so the footer, unsubscribe and continuity checks below are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 29 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.crocs-email.com`). Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- The open pixel `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?REIYEAXPK25ENE6HEBHVPP5GGA.10203&d=10203&bmt=0` is present and well-formed.
- The Adobe Audience Manager pixel `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` has no `alt` and is hidden with `visibility:hidden;display:none`. Some clients won't fire an image that is `display:none`, so open and audience-sync data from it will be undercounted.
- The recommendation image URLs (`10745993.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/…/i/1..4/walker@agentmail.to?category=Recommended for You`) have two problems:
  - The `category` value contains unencoded spaces. Most clients tolerate this, but it should be `%20`.
  - The recipient's plaintext email address is in the URL path, which leaks it to a third party and into proxy and log data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything. The charset is effectively undeclared. This matters here because the hidden preheader padding uses non-ASCII characters (`͏`, `‌`, `­`) and could render as mojibake in a client that guesses the encoding.
- **Missing alt text:** four `igodigital` recommendation images (`/i/1` to `/i/4`) have no `alt`. They are likely product images, so screen readers and image-blocked views will get nothing. The `demdex` tracking pixel also lacks `alt`; it should have `alt=""`, as the open pixel does.
- **Non-HTTPS images (7):** all are served from `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`: App Store, Google Play, Find Your Store, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube. Clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings. Alt text is present on these.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` doesn't describe the email. This is minor.
- **Otherwise fine:** the viewport and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` metas, the Gmail and Apple data-detector overrides, the Outlook `-ms-text-size-adjust` rule, and the `@font-face` fallbacks are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source, and the recipient address resolves in the recs URLs. The rest of the source was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
Not verifiable. The unsubscribe link, physical address and footer were past the truncation point, and no SPF/DKIM/DMARC or `List-Unsubscribe` header data was provided. I found no confirmed violation. From `email@crocs-email.com`, the sending domain `crocs-email.com` matches the click and image hosts, which is consistent with aligned branded tracking.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 29 tracked links were skipped, so their final URLs and any UTM parameters are unknown. The subject "Ready to Feel the Fuzz?" and preheader "The Classic Cozzzy Slipper…" can't be matched to a landing page from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
2. Switch all seven `image.crocs-email.com` sources to `https://`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four recommendation images. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex` pixel.
4. URL-encode the `category` parameter. Consider a hashed or opaque subscriber ID in the `igodigital` URLs instead of the plaintext email.
5. Re-run QA with the full HTML and headers. Then check the tracked-link destinations (with a redirect-following probe), UTM presence, the unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
