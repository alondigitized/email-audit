## What's weak

- The "We noticed you noticing us..." line is creepy. It's the same cart nag I've seen for days.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based, responsive template, but the unsubscribe link probed as HTTP 400, there is no plain-text part, and about 12 images load over `http://`. The QA pass rate was 62%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_lid=m60`. The decoded macro is `unsubscribeLink`. The 400 may come from the probe lacking a valid session or user agent, so verify it manually. If it is real, it is a CAN-SPAM risk.
- **Two CTAs returned 429 (rate limited), so they are unverified, not broken.** These are "Complete you Order" and "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting...". Both are `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` redirect links.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** Only 12 of 43 links were probed and 31 were skipped, so most footer and product links are unverified.
- **Every link is wrapped in an Attentive redirect.** Final destinations are opaque to the QA check, so the landing target for the cart CTAs could not be confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.** At least 13 images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...`:
  - Logo: `dde00662-…png`
  - Hero: `dfcc8fd7-…jpg`
  - Recommendations: `737031d9-…jpg`
  - Others: `a6ec5198-…jpg`, `49468f73-…png`, and the App Store, Google Play, Curbside, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons
  
  Some clients block or warn on mixed content. The same host also serves some images over `https://` (`021bd784-…gif`), so the host supports TLS and these URLs can simply be switched.
- **Missing alt text** on `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif`, `a6ec5198-…jpg`, and the Attentive open pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`). The pixel is low priority.
- **Weak or incorrect alt text.** One image has alt="Text" (`49468f73-…png`), which is non-descriptive. The hero has alt="Complete you Order", a typo for "your".
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is a minor issue.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />`, which has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it does nothing. Some clients may guess the encoding. The preheader's `͏` and `­` padding characters make this more relevant.
- **`<html lang="en">` and viewport meta are present.** The mobile breakpoint is `@media (max-width:620px)`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible portion of the source. The HTML was truncated, so the lower sections were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link:** it is present, but the probe returned 400 (see §2).
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports the text version as 0 characters. This is a deliverability and accessibility gap, and some spam filters penalize HTML-only mail. It may also be an AgentMail extraction artifact, so check the raw MIME.
- **Authentication:** no `Authentication-Results` header was found. This is a relay limitation, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It is not evidence of failure.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** the physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All tracked links go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`, so UTM parameters could not be inspected.
- The cart-recovery CTAs ("Complete you Order", "Your cart is still waiting...") returned 429, so their landing pages could not be confirmed to resolve to the cart.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Check the unsubscribe URL manually** (`skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…`) in a browser or with a real user agent. If it returns 400, fix it first.
2. **Add a plain-text MIME part** with the unsubscribe link, or confirm the QA extraction is at fault.
3. **Change all `http://image.emails.skechers.com` image URLs to `https://`.**
4. **Add descriptive alt text** to `021bd784-…gif` and `a6ec5198-…jpg`. Replace alt="Text" with descriptive text, fix "Complete you Order" to "your", and add `alt=""` to the open pixel.
5. **Fix the charset declaration** to `<meta charset="utf-8">` or add `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, and populate `<title>`.
6. **Re-probe the 429 CTAs** with backoff, and follow the redirects to check the landing page and UTMs.
7. **Check the raw headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, since the AgentMail relay hides them.
