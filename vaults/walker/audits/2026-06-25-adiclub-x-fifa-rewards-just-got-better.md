# adiClub x FIFA Rewards just got better 🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-16**
## Executive summary

- I'm an adiClub member, so the name in the subject pulled me in — I'll give them that. But the second I opened this thing, I knew exactly what it was: another FIFA email. I've been keeping a mental tally. Father's Day soccer jerseys on June 9th. Cleats on the 11th. A running shoe on the 13th. Then finally, on the 19th, they sent me something I could actually use — thirty dollars off a hundred-dollar order. That was the one that earned a click. And now we're right back to FIFA loyalty programs and Messi memorabilia.
- There's one section in here — "Unlock Double Points" — that almost reaches me. I'm an adiClub member, double points sounds useful, the SHOP NOW button is big and obvious. But then I read that it's only on "FIFA Rewards eligible products." Which means jerseys and cleats I'll never own. The raffle prizes — signed Messi jersey, game-used match ball — are for someone else entirely. Someone who watches soccer, follows World Cup brackets, knows what a Tango cleat is.
- I'd mark this read and move on. Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward it to anyone I know.

## What's working

- The adiClub branding in the newsletter header at least signals "this is a loyalty email" — I opened it because I'm a member
- SHOP NOW and ENTER NOW buttons are big, clear, and easy to tap — no squinting required on a phone
- The three-panel layout reads logically top-to-bottom; visual hierarchy is clean and uncluttered
- Email renders without issues — no broken images, no overlapping text, no obvious junk where the copy should be
- adidas consistently uses their own recognizable design language — I always know who sent it before I even look at the sender name

## What's weak

- This is approximately the fifth FIFA/World Cup email in two weeks from adidas — I'm starting to tune out the sender name entirely, which is not where they want me
- Not a single walking shoe, cushioned sneaker, or comfort product anywhere in the scroll — this is all jerseys, cleats, and a soccer ball
- The double points offer is tantalizing until you notice it's FIFA-gated — might as well be invisible to me
- "For limited time" with no date attached is lazy; it reads like a placeholder, not real urgency
- The preheader that leaked into my inbox preview was unsubscribe/manage-preferences junk — wasted the one chance they had to hook me before the open

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pull comfort-shoe buyers off the FIFA blast entirely.** My purchase history is walking shoes. Send me double points on Cloudfoam or Ultraboost, not FIFA gear I'll never buy.
- 2. **Give the double points offer a real end date.** "For limited time, through June 30" earns a click. "For limited time" earns a scroll-past.
- 3. **Fix the preheader.** The inbox preview showed manage-preferences text instead of an actual offer — that's a full slot wasted.
- 4. **Rotate the raffle prizes toward something that fits the subscriber.** If I'm a loyalty member with a walking-shoe history, enter me to win a $200 adidas gift card, not a signed cleat from an athlete I don't follow.
- 5. **Ease up on the cadence.** Five FIFA emails in fifteen days is conditioning me to assume nothing from adidas is for me.

## Technical Audit — adidas adiClub x FIFA Rewards

**Sender:** adidas@us-news.comms.adidas.com | **Date:** 2026-06-24

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One click URL contains a malformed relative path that will 404 on click, and the email has no plain-text MIME alternative, both of which are confirmed FAIL-level issues. Several HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources and missing alt attributes add secondary risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken redirect URL**
QA detected an unresolvable path in one link's intermediate redirect:
```
unknown url type: '/g/e3a5f3d4-4f1f-462b-86cb-3014a28ecf4a?txn=d4357400-7055-11f1-8d17-aea38baf8e0c'
```
The outer click-tracking wrapper (`dv.adidas.com/o/...`) contains an encoded destination that resolves to a bare `/g/` path rather than a fully-qualified URL. This suggests the ESP template did not interpolate the destination URL correctly before encoding. Clicks on this link will land on a 404 or redirect error.

**[WARN] 21 tracking links not probed**
All CTA links route through `dv.adidas.com` click-redirect with a base64-encoded `cp_tp` payload. The broken link above is the only one that was partially decoded — the other 20 cannot be verified without following the full redirect chain.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Malformed charset meta tag**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Missing the required `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute. Without it, some older email clients (notably Lotus Notes, older Outlook) will not apply the charset declaration, risking character encoding corruption on non-ASCII content.

**[WARN] Duplicate `@font-face` blocks**
`AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are declared twice across two separate `<style>` blocks with identical rules. This inflates HTML payload size with no functional benefit.

**[WARN] Custom fonts will never render**
All `@font-face` declarations reference only `local()` sources (Arial, Helvetica, Calibri). There are no `url()` web font sources. Custom adidas typography is unavailable in any email client; the fallback chain (Arial/Helvetica) always fires.

**[WARN] HTTP image sources — may be blocked**
Two distinct images use `http://` rather than `https://`:
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
```
(Arrow icon, referenced twice in HTML)
```
http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019eced5897a-617d82f2-838a-4e48-84a7-28edc7ec6ad2-000000/...
```
(Tracking pixel — likely open-tracking beacon)

Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook.com proxy/block non-HTTPS image loads. The arrow icon will appear broken; the open-tracking pixel will fail to fire, suppressing open-rate data for affected clients.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on two images**
- `dv.adidas.com/o/e3a5f3d4-...` — appears to be a content image with no `alt` attribute
- `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` — tracking pixel; should have `alt=""` (empty, not absent)

Screen readers will read the raw URL string on the first; the second violates WCAG 2.1 technique H67 (decorative images must have empty alt).

**[INFO] Overlapping media query breakpoints**
Breakpoints at `max-width: 640px` and `max-width: 639px` are declared in separate blocks targeting the same viewport range. The 639px block overrides the 640px block for most mobile viewports. Consolidating these would reduce CSS weight and eliminate the off-by-one ambiguity.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `[%`) visible in the truncated HTML source. Cannot confirm body personalization fields (first name, adiClub tier, etc.) without the full source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] No plain-text MIME alternative**
```
Text version is 0 chars
```
CAN-SPAM does not explicitly mandate a text part, but its absence is a primary spam-filter signal. SpamAssassin, Postfix milters, and most enterprise gateways penalize HTML-only messages. It also breaks accessibility for clients that display text-only by default (some corporate Outlook configurations, screen-reader-only setups).

**[INFO] ROBOTS noindex present** — `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW" />` is set, which is correct for email HTML pages served at open-tracking URLs.

**[INFO] Referrer suppressed** — `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` strips the HTTP Referer header on link clicks. This is intentional (privacy), but it means any server-side referrer logging on the destination domain will receive no referrer. All attribution must come from UTM/ESP params in the URL itself.

Authentication headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) cannot be audited from HTML source alone; sender domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` should be verified as an authorized ESP subdomain in adidas's SPF record.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTAs route through `dv.adidas.com/o/` with an opaque base64-encoded `cp_tp` parameter. Standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are not visible in the outer click URL. Whether they are embedded inside `cp_tp` (decoded at the ESP redirect layer and appended to the final destination) cannot be confirmed without following the redirect chain.

The broken link (`/g/e3a5f3d4-...`) means at minimum one CTA has zero chance of landing page attribution — the user hits an error before reaching any adidas URL.
