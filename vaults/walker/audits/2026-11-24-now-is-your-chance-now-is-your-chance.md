- "Free shipping on all orders" is a clear perk near the top.

- The email is all running shoes, and I'm a walker. Nothing mentions walking, arch support, or slip-on comfort.
- Not one shoe shows a price or a sale price. "On sale now" tells me nothing.
- The Black Friday "up to 40% off" is buried near the bottom, and "select styles" is vague.
- The shopping bags are back, the third time this month. The fine print is small and hard to read without zooming.

- 3. Drop the shopping bags and use one more shoe.
- 4. Make each shoe in the grid link to its own page, and add star ratings.
- 5. Fix the doubled subject line.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from an ESP template (`link.email.newbalance.com`) that still carries unfinished template artifacts: a placeholder `<title>`, a placeholder background URL, and malformed head tags. The QA run found no broken links, but the plain-text part is mostly URLs and three images lack `alt`. The HTML source was truncated, so several checks below could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 11,462 of 13,243 characters (about 87%) are URLs. This is a spam-filter risk and gives a poor plain-text experience. It is probably caused by long tracking-wrapped links.
- **Link probing was partial.** 12 links were probed and 23 were skipped as footer, utility or social links. The unsubscribe link is always probed. QA raised no failures, so nothing is confirmed broken. The 23 skipped links are unverified.
- **Placeholder asset URL in CSS.** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` is an unresolved placeholder. It is also plain `http://`, so it would trigger mixed-content blocking or a failed request if the class is used.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png` (`.../myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingIcons_06.png`)
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` (`.../myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`)
  - `egR1f966kc_818512772_12731134_101567_24043024.gif` (`link.email.newbalance.com/mo/...`) is almost certainly the open-tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""`.
- **Placeholder title.** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` was never replaced. Some clients and web-view renderers show it.
- **Malformed meta tag.** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Inert `@font-face` rules.** The source shows rules for `proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot` and others with no `src` descriptor, so no web font loads. Rendering falls back to `Arial`/`Times New Roman`. The `.ff` and `.fst*` classes name these fonts, so the declared typography is never delivered.
- **Duplicate selector.** `.bg1` is defined twice: `#ffffff`, then `#58546d`. The later rule wins, and this is likely unintended.
- **Dark mode.** The email declares `color-scheme: light dark` and a `prefers-color-scheme` block. A dark-mode image asset (`ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`) is present, but it has no `alt`.
- **Doctype and namespace mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with an `xmlns` attribute. This is low severity and harmless in most clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appeared in the visible source or in the QA output. The `XXXXX` in the CSS is an asset placeholder, not a merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication headers unverifiable.** `Authentication-Results` was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a measurement gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address not visible.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer. QA always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure, but I can't confirm the postal address or the `List-Unsubscribe` header from this data.
- The From address (`newbalance@email.newbalance.com`) is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with the sending domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable.** The visible links go through the ESP redirect domain `link.email.newbalance.com`. The final destination URLs and their UTM parameters are not in the truncated source or the QA output.
- **Reused template assets.** Image paths contain `myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`, so this send reuses a welcome-flow template. The subject, "Now is your chance, now is your chance," appears to have no template-level tie to those shipping assets. Whether the landing page matches the content could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
2. Replace `<title>New Balance Template</title>` with the campaign title.
3. Remove or replace `url('http://image.XXXXX.png')`, and use HTTPS if a background image is intended.
4. Fix the head tags:
   - Change the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
   - Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules, or delete them.
   - Remove the duplicate `.bg1`.
5. Shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part, for example by using fewer tracked links or a text-only variant.
6. Re-run the audit with the untruncated HTML and raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`). Then verify the unsubscribe link, the postal address, the UTM parameters on the final destination URLs, and the 23 unprobed links.
