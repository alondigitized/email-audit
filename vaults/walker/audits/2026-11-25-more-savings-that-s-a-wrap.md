# More savings? That's a wrap. 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again, and it's the eleventh one in about two weeks. The big "60% OFF" is easy to read on my phone, and the code COZY20 is simple enough. But it's the same "up to 60% off plus an extra 20%" I've been getting since 11/21, with the percentage changed. The subject line promised a "wrap" and I got a pile of ribbons and a TikTok pitch.
- The sandals on the red bows are at least a shoe I'd consider, since a slide is hands-free. The rest of the email isn't aimed at me. A Minnie Mouse clog and "From Your FYP: TikTok viral styles" are for somebody 40 years younger. I don't have a FYP. I'd skim it, maybe tap the main button, and then go back to comparing Hoka and Brooks.
- I wouldn't forward this to anyone. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "60% OFF" is huge and I can read it without squinting.
- The code COZY20 is stated plainly, with "at checkout" next to it, so I know what to do.
- The two-strap sandals on the red ribbons are a real hands-free shoe and look easy to slip on.
- The "Shop Black Friday" button is big and black, with an easy tap target.
- The fine print gives a firm end date, Sunday, November 29th.

## What's weak

- It's the same sale as the last week of emails, so "More savings?" feels like noise. The extra 20% is down from the 25% on 11/21.
- "Exclusions Apply" and "(ALMOST) everything" make me doubt the offer. They don't say what's excluded.
- The Minnie Mouse banner and the TikTok section aren't written for a 62-year-old.
- There's nothing about cushioning, arch support, or walking comfort, and no prices, so I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- The "wrap" gift line in the subject doesn't match what's inside. It's a sale email with no gift angle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one comfort product, such as the slide or a cushioned clog, with a real price and the sale price next to it. Add one line on arch support or cushioning.
- 2. Say what the exclusions are, or link to them.
- 3. Cut the back-to-back repeats. Send fewer emails with a different message each time.
- 4. Replace the Minnie and TikTok blocks with a "Comfort picks" row of men's styles.
- 5. Make the button copy specific, like "Shop Slides, 60% Off".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your COZY20 code ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort slides, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 20% off with code COZY20 through Sunday, 11/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned slides and clogs, now up to 60% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is clean and uses standard ESP patterns: a Salesforce Marketing Cloud open pixel, a hidden preheader, and responsive CSS. Two meta tags are malformed. The source is truncated, so most body-level checks (links, footer, alt text, tokens) could not be confirmed. The QA run reported 0 issues and 1 warning.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 15 tracking links because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Open pixel.** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10212&bmt=0` is a 1x1 `<img>` with `alt=""` and explicit width and height. No issue.
- **Third-party pixel without alt.** QA flagged `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`, an Adobe Audience Manager open-event sync, as missing alt text. It is a tracking pixel, so the impact is minor, but an `alt=""` attribute is expected. The pixel also loads from a second tracking domain, which image-blocking clients may suppress.
- **QA output may be truncated.** The `d_dpuid` value in the QA output looks cut off at 39 characters. This is likely an output limit, not a defect in the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags (confirmed).**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so the charset declaration has no effect.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it has no effect either.
  - Charset is normally set by the MIME `Content-Type` header, so the practical risk is low. The tags should still be fixed.
- **`<title>`** is the generic "Crocs". This is minor and only matters in the web view.
- **Present and correct:**
  - `lang="en"`.
  - Viewport meta.
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.
  - `format-detection`.
  - Gmail and Outlook web view fixes (`#MessageViewBody`, `u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`).
  - A `max-width: 639px` mobile breakpoint.
  - Webfont `@font-face` rules with `font-display: swap`.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:**
  - Image alt text on content images.
  - Table `role="presentation"`.
  - Font fallback stacks.
  - Whether the `#new-gmail-hack` element exists.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]`. The body is truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a limitation of the test setup and not evidence of a sender misconfiguration.
- **Domain alignment looks consistent.** The From address `email@crocs-email.com` matches the tracking host `click.crocs-email.com`.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:**
  - The unsubscribe link and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
  - The physical postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Because the 15 click links are redirect-wrapped and were not probed, the final URLs are unknown. That leaves UTM parameters, landing page status, and whether the offer messaging ("extra 20% off Black Friday sales") matches the landing page unchecked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
3. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Also resolve the click-tracking redirects, for example with a follow-redirect probe or by capturing the SFMC click-through destinations. That would cover the footer, unsubscribe link, UTM parameters and landing page alignment.
4. Capture the raw message headers (`Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`, `List-Unsubscribe`) from a direct-delivery test, not the AgentMail relay, to confirm authentication and one-click unsubscribe.
