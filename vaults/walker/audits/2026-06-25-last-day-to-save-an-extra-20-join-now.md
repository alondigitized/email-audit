# LAST DAY to save an extra 20% 🌟 Join NOW
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- I almost didn't open this one. The preview text in my inbox was a wall of gobbledygook — a raw tracking URL, same mess as the June 8 email where I nearly sent it straight to spam. Famous Footwear had just earned back some goodwill with that Brooks 603 email on June 13 and the clean 20%-off send on June 14, so seeing another garbled preview was a step backward. Good thing I know the sender well enough to give it a second look.
- Once I got inside, the email cleaned up. The "Rewards Member Exclusive — Save an extra 20% off" banner lands right at the top, which is what I want. No hunting. But then I scroll down to "We picked these just for you" and I'm looking at Converse, Vans, Reef, Dr. Martens — none of those are on my shopping list. These feel like shoes for someone's grandson, not for a guy my age with a bad knee and a preference for cushioning. The one bright spot: Brooks at $140 marked down to $109.99. That I noticed.
- Here's where it goes sideways, though. The subject says "Join NOW" — like I'm a stranger who wandered in off the street. I've been getting Rewards Member Exclusive offers from Famous Footwear for months. Don't tell me to join a club I'm already in. I'd mark this as read and maybe circle back to that Brooks deal later, but I wouldn't click anything on impulse.

## What's working

- **20% off Rewards Member Exclusive is front and center** — I didn't have to scroll or squint to find the offer; it's the first thing my eye landed on.
- **Brooks markdown is genuinely useful** — $140 down to $109.99 with a clear "SHOP NOW" is something I'd actually act on; Brooks is in my rotation.
- **Email renders clean** — no broken images, no overlapping text, product photos are crisp. After that garbled preview, the inside surprised me in a good way.
- **"These markdowns won't last long"** — paired with "LAST DAY" up top, the urgency stacks in a credible way, not hysterical. That works on me.

## What's weak

- **Preview text is a raw tracking URL** — this is a repeat of the June 8 problem; seeing a link dump in my inbox preview made me hover over the delete button. This needs to be fixed before anything else.
- **"Join NOW" in the subject when I'm already a member** — that's tone-deaf; it made me feel like Famous Footwear doesn't know who I am after months of emails.
- **"Picked just for you" product grid is off-target** — Converse, Vans, Dr. Martens, Reef are not comfort-walking shoes; that curation doesn't feel like it knows me at all.
- **20% off fatigue is real** — this is at least the fourth or fifth version of this same offer since early June; the discount is starting to feel less like an event and more like wallpaper.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — this is the single highest-leverage fix; a tracking URL leaking into preview text kills open rates and erodes trust.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject for existing members** — drop "Join NOW" and speak to the loyal customer, not a prospect.
- 3. **Swap the product grid for comfort/walking brands** — if the system knows I've looked at Brooks and NB walking shoes, serve me those; Converse and Vans belong in someone else's "picked for you."
- 4. **Add one comfort-specific hook in the body** — even a line like "extra cushioning, arch support, and walking shoes on sale" would signal you know what I care about.
- 5. **Vary the offer structure** — six 20% off emails in two weeks trains me to wait for the discount; consider a free-shipping offer or a loyalty points bonus to break the pattern.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rewards Members: 20% off Brooks & more — last day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your member deal ends tonight — Brooks, NB, and more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 20% off comfort shoes — no code needed, just sign in`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Brooks, New Balance, and walking shoes are all in — shop before midnight`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[BUG] Charset meta tag is non-functional.**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
The `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute is missing. Without it, the charset declaration is ignored by parsers. Correct form:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Risk: non-ASCII characters in dynamic content (accented names, special promo symbols) may render as mojibake in older clients.

**[BUG] `max-height:auto` is invalid CSS.**
```css
img { max-width:100% !important; height:auto !important; max-height:auto !important; }
```
`auto` is not a valid value for `max-height` (valid values: `none`, length, percentage). Browsers silently ignore invalid declarations; the rule is dead weight but causes no active breakage. Should be `max-height:none` or removed.

**[WARN] `user-scalable=0` — deprecated numeric value.**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="..., user-scalable=0" />
```
The HTML spec requires `yes`/`no`. `0` is tolerated by most engines but is non-standard and may be rejected by stricter parsers.
