## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`link.email.newbalance.com` tracking domain) with dark-mode support. The automated QA found no hard failures (0 issues, 2 warnings). However, 7 images lack alt text, the template has leftover placeholder code, and the plain-text part is almost entirely URLs. The HTML supplied is truncated, so body, footer and link-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 34 skipped, and no probe failures were reported. I can't confirm any broken links, and the skipped footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 15,604 of 17,156 characters are URLs (91%). The text alternative is dominated by long tracking-wrapped links, which is a spam-filter and readability risk. Plain-text link labels should be short or the URLs trimmed.
- **Placeholder URL in the stylesheet.** `.bgwhite {… background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png'); }` is a template stub. It is insecure (`http://`) and unresolvable. It only matters if `.bgwhite` is applied, but it should be removed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Seven images have no alt text** (QA data):
  - `md_1758292.gif`, `md_1758303.gif`, `md_1758294.jpg` and `md_1758305.jpg` under `/custloads/818512772/`
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` under `/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`
  - the tracking pixel `/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12625063_756268_23845156.gif`
- **The pixel should carry `alt=""`.** This is the lowest-impact case of the seven.
- **The four `md_*` images are probably content images.** Without alt text, the offer content is invisible with images blocked and to screen readers.
- **The shipping assets come from another template.** They live under `myNB_Welcome`, which suggests reuse from a welcome-email template.
- **Invalid viewport/compat meta.** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Mismatched doctype and namespace.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with an `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` attribute and self-closed tags.
- **Default title.** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is an unedited template title. It is shown by some clients and may be read aloud by assistive tech.
- **Stray font rule.** `@font-face { … font-style: italic; font-family: proxima-nova,sans-serif; }` repeats `font-family` inside one rule. None of the `@font-face` blocks have a `src`, so they are inert declarations.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The body was not fully available, so this is not a full verification.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication status unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not found because the message came through the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are not verified. This is a limit of the capture path, not evidence of a sender problem. Check the headers on a direct-delivery copy.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address unconfirmed.** The truncated HTML doesn't show them. QA reports that an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was given. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided either.
- **From address aligns with the sending domain.** `newbalance@email.newbalance.com` matches the `link.email.newbalance.com` tracking subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No visible UTM parameters.** The visible links are redirector-wrapped (`link.email.newbalance.com/...`). Whether the final destination carries UTM parameters can't be determined without redirect-chain data for the 12 probed links.
- **Landing-page alignment not assessed.** No probe destinations were provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four `md_*` content images and the shipping assets. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel `/mo/egR1f966kc_…gif`.
2. Remove or replace the `url('http://image.XXXXX.png')` placeholder in `.bgwhite`.
3. Change `<title>` to the campaign subject, "Essential gifts for everyone on your list".
4. Fix `<meta content="IE=edge" />` to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`, or drop it.
5. Use a consistent doctype, either XHTML 1.0 or HTML5, so it matches the `xmlns` attribute.
6. Shorten or clean the plain-text part, using fewer and shorter link labels so it isn't 91% URLs.
7. Audit the reuse of `myNB_Welcome` assets and confirm they are intended for a holiday promotion.
8. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and redirect-chain results. That would let the unsubscribe, authentication and UTM checks be completed.
