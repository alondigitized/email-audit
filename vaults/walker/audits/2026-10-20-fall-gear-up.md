# Fall gear-up
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a jacket email, and I came to HOKA for shoes. "Fit for fall" is big, neon yellow-green on black, and easy to read. But the first thing I see is a young man in a fleece zip-up, not a shoe. After a week of the walking, stability and Arahi 9 versus Gaviota 6 emails, this one feels like a detour.
- The shoe finally shows up about two-thirds of the way down. "Walk on" has a man striding past a storefront in airy cushioned shoes, and "Shop Walking" is the one button that speaks to me. "Find your best fit" with the Shoe Finder Quiz is also useful, because fit is how I decide. Both sit below a lot of apparel scrolling.
- I'd skim it and probably tap "Shop Walking" if I had a minute. I wouldn't forward it, since there's no offer, no price and no shoe name. It's a nice-looking catalog page, but not a reason to buy today.

## What's working

- "Fit for fall" is huge and high-contrast, and I can read it on my phone without squinting.
- "Shop Walking" is a plain button on a walking message, and it's the only thing aimed at my kind of day.
- "Find your best fit" and the Shoe Finder Quiz fit how I shop: I want help finding what fits my foot.
- The footer has "Unlock Free Shipping" and "FLY for 30 Guarantee," which tell me I can try shoes without much risk.
- The blue, black and neon look matches the last few HOKA emails, so I trust it's the same brand.

## What's weak

- The hero and most of the email sell apparel. I'm shopping for cushioning and arch support, not a base layer.
- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline. Nothing tells me why I should click today.
- "Shop Fall Apparel" shows up twice, and the copy buttons are small and thin. The second one is a full-width black bar, which is easier to tap.
- The "walk stars" shoes in the photo aren't named, so I can't tell which model I'd be clicking on.
- The models are young and athletic. The only one who looks like a man who strolls is a tattooed guy mid-stride, and he's the walking one.
- The footer links ("Locate a Store," "Unlock Free Shipping") are plain white text. The fine print at the bottom is tiny blue on blue, and I can't read it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the walking shoe, not the jacket. Put the "Walk on" block at the top with a named model, like a Bondi or Clifton, with a price and a line on cushioning.
- 2. Name the shoes and say what they do ("plush cushion, all-day support"), and link each one to its own page.
- 3. Make the quiz the main call to action. Make that button bigger, and say what the quiz does: "Find my fit in 2 minutes."
- 4. Add one real offer or deadline, such as free shipping with no minimum, or the 30-day guarantee up top.
- 5. Cut the apparel to one block, or send it to a separate list.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our most cushioned walking shoes, plus a 2-minute fit quiz.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping and a 30-day guarantee on every pair.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracking and UTM structure is consistent, but it has no plain-text part and no alt text on any image. It also has several template and markup defects in the head. The 9 probe failures (406/403) all come from the Cordial click-tracking redirector, so they are probably bot filtering rather than dead links. I could not confirm that from the data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **9 of 9 probed links failed.** Eight returned 406 and the Unsubscribe link returned 403. All are on `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6ac6772942e7cad066052c46:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`.
- **Likely a probe artifact, not broken links.** Every failure is a signed-JWT redirect on the ESP domain. A 406 or 403 on all of them, including the unsubscribe link, points to the redirector rejecting the probe's headers or user agent.
- **Destinations were never verified.** I decoded the `linkUrl` field in each `jwtP` payload, and they resolve to:
  - `https://www.hoka.com/`
  - `/en/us/apparel-new-arrivals/` (five tiles)
  - `/en/us/walking-shoes/`
  - `/en/us/hoka-shoe-finder/`
  - `/en/us/return-start/`
  - `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`
  
  The QA run never followed the redirect, so landing-page status is unknown. The "12 probed, 11 skipped" line is also contradictory.
- **Per-tile click attribution is weak.** The five apparel tiles (`US_20261020_Apparel_a` to `_e`) go to the same `/apparel-new-arrivals/` URL with identical UTMs and no `utm_content`. The ESP's per-link hash may still separate them, but web analytics cannot.
- **The click URL carries what looks like a SHA-256 hash of the recipient's email.** It appears as `hmail=9153f33f…` in every destination URL. That is a design choice rather than a defect, but it passes through to the landing-page query string and analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on all 10 flagged images.** The six apparel tiles, `US_Q3_ShoeFinder_Blue.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png` are all linked or content images. The two tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`) also lack the attribute. Because the images sit inside `<a>` tags, those links have no accessible name, and the QA output shows the filename as the link text (e.g. `[image: US_20261020_Apparel_a.jpg]`). With images blocked, the email renders as empty space.
- **The plain-text part is 0 characters.** Text-only clients and some spam filters get nothing.
- **The `<title>` is empty:** `<title></title>`.
- **The charset declaration is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so clients ignore it.
- **Two conflicting viewport tags are present.** One has `maximum-scale=1` and the other adds `user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` can block pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Unfilled template values in the CSS.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` are invalid and ignored.
- **Focus outlines are removed globally** by `* {…outline:none;…}`, which hurts keyboard navigation in clients that support it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` were found in the truncated source. The only personalization is the hashed `hmail` and the `cordial_id` parameters, which resolved correctly. The CSS blanks noted above are the only sign of unfilled variables. The source was truncated, so the body past the CSS was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The Unsubscribe link is present**, in the footer, and goes to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`. The probe got a 403, so I can't confirm it works. This is probably the same bot filtering as above, but it should be tested manually.
- **SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.** There is no `Authentication-Results` header because the message came through the AgentMail relay. This is a limit of the capture, not an email defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided**, so one-click unsubscribe can't be assessed.
- **The physical postal address is not visible** in the truncated source, so it can't be confirmed.
- **The missing plain-text part** is also a deliverability weakness.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are consistent on every link:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261020_Apparel_STO_A30Day_HighestEngaged_365ApparelTag_270StyliticsTag_ApparelPLPBrowser_AIUnsubLow`.
- **There is no `utm_content` or `utm_term`** to tell links apart (see section 2).
- **The unsubscribe link carries the same marketing UTMs.** That's harmless but adds noise to campaign analytics.
- **Landing-page alignment couldn't be checked** because redirects were never followed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the links properly.** Follow the redirects with a browser-like user agent, or decode the `jwtP` `linkUrl` and test the final `hoka.com` URLs. Then confirm the unsubscribe flow manually.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to every linked image and the logo. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. **Add a plain-text MIME part** with the links and the unsubscribe URL.
4. **Fix the head.**
   - Add a real `<title>`.
   - Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>`.
   - Merge the two viewport tags and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Remove or fill the empty `px` CSS rules.
5. **Add per-tile `utm_content`** values, such as `apparel_a` through `apparel_e`.
6. **Verify the headers on a direct capture.** Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `emails.hoka.com`, and the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. Check the footer address in the full source.
