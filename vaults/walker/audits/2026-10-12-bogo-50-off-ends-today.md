# BOGO 50% off ends *TODAY*
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count, and "BOGO 50% off ends *TODAY*" is the fifth different deadline I've seen from them in a week. The orange hero is big and clear. BOGO 50% off is a real deal, and it's a better one than the "$15 off" and "CIDER" code games from earlier today. But the fine print says the promo was valid October 5-7, and today is the 12th. The "long weekend" line and the "ends today" subject don't match that. That mismatch makes me trust them less.
- I'd skim it and move on. Below the hero it's boots, mary-janes and platform clogs, all women's styles. The one thing for me is the small "SHOP MEN" link under the hero, which is easy to miss. I wouldn't forward it. If I needed a pair of walkers, I'd click that link and look for New Balance or Skechers. But I wouldn't rush because of "today."

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is huge on the page and easy to read, even on my phone.
- "Buy any pair, get 50% off a second pair" is plain English, with a clear green SHOP NOW button.
- The Women/Men/Kids/Everything links under the hero let me go straight to men's.
- The shop-by-size grid and the Wide Width button are useful. I do care about a roomy fit.
- The barcode for in-store redemption is handy if I'm at a DSW anyway.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is women's: booties, a mary-jane, a clog. Nothing for a 62-year-old man.
- No comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-on messaging anywhere. Nothing from Skechers, Hoka, Brooks or New Balance is featured. The gift row has a few sneakers but no details.
- The fine print says the offer is valid Oct 5-7. That conflicts with "ends today," so it doesn't feel honest.
- The "$15, $20, or $60 off!" tags under each product are red and tiny, and they muddy the BOGO message with a second offer.
- The "Santa's workshop" gift section is out of season in mid-October. Along with the fall-themed sends this week, it feels like a grab-bag.
- The legal text is a wall of small type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the dates so the email is honest: the fine print should match the real deadline, and "ends today" should only go out when it does.
- 2. Personalize the product grid by browse history. Show men's walking and slip-on comfort shoes to men like me, with the brand names visible.
- 3. Move "SHOP MEN" into a real button, not a small underlined link.
- 4. Pick one offer per email. Drop the "$15, $20, or $60 off" tags or the BOGO headline.
- 5. Cut the cadence. One clear reminder is enough; I've had about a dozen sends since the 8th.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken CTA, the SMS opt-in link, which returns HTTP 400. It also has minor accessibility gaps (missing `lang`, zoom disabled, two images without alt) and a link-heavy plain-text part. The HTML source is truncated, so the body, footer and headers couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/xZESJW8N0fVmasWXiESxKg~~/AAQRxRA~/qBTBiw5Sz62S8XOCZcjsjOOINogZgdVT9ScS-…`.
  - The QA report shows a blank label after "Broken link (400):", so the probe got no useful error body.
  - This is the only failure among the 12 links probed. A 400 on a tracking redirect can also mean the click-tracker rejected the probe (for example a bot user-agent or missing params). Re-test the link from a real mobile client before treating it as a customer-facing outage.
  - If it is real, a subscriber who taps it hits a dead end on the 15%-off SMS offer.
- **Coverage gap:** 55 of 67 links were skipped by the probe (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized), so their health is unverified.
- **Plain-text size:** 24,675 of 29,298 characters (about 84%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/…` tracking URLs inflate the text part, which can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/-3NFd4ZzXVWH1ZrIc_oGjw~~/AAQRxRA~/VLe1m7PyuMo…` has no alt text. If this is a content image, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=…` is a tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so this is low severity.
- **`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.** Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** blocks pinch-zoom on iOS and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Two meta tags are malformed:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` is missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` is missing `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - As written, neither tag does anything. The charset is normally covered by the MIME header, so the impact is low.
- **Web fonts:** the `@font-face` rules load from `braze-images.com`, and the fallbacks weren't visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The only per-user value seen is `mi_u=225090073` in the pixel URL, which is a resolved ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- No QA failure was reported for the unsubscribe link, and the probe always includes one.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:** the footer postal address, the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. The footer was truncated and the headers weren't supplied.
- The From domain `e.dsw.com` and the tracking domain `ablink.dsw.com` share the organizational domain `dsw.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the probe results don't show the final landing URLs or any UTM parameters. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261007_P261361_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` on the pixel. The "*TODAY*" subject can't be matched to any landing-page offer from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/xZESJW8N…`) from a real client. If it still returns 400, fix or re-issue the Braze/SendGrid link wrapping for that CTA before further sends.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `http-equiv` to the charset and X-UA-Compatible meta tags.
5. Add descriptive alt text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image, and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
6. Shorten the plain-text part by replacing the long tracked URLs with fewer or shorter links. This should bring the URL share under 70%.
