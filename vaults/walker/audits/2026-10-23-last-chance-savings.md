## What's weak

- "One more run" and the splashing-through-puddles photo are for runners. I'm a walker looking for comfort and arch support.
- There are no people in the email at all, so nothing speaks to a 62-year-old.
- "Up to 30%" is vague. I can't tell if the Clifton 10 itself gets 30% or only some styles. I have to read the fine print to find out.

- 2. Speak to walkers and comfort. Add a line like "Cushioned all-day comfort for walking" and show a real person in the photo.
- 3. Give the free-shipping offer to everyone, or drop it. A text-signup catch weakens the sale message.
- 4. Link the button to the Clifton 10 sale, not a general sale page. Add a star rating next to the shoe.
- 5. Fold this into one email with the weekend's other sends instead of sending a seventh email this week.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a Cordial-tracked, table-based layout with several real markup defects. These are a missing plain-text part, an ineffective charset declaration, missing alt text on linked images, and invalid CSS. All 7 link-probe failures (400, 403 and 406) come from Cordial redirect URLs. They look like bot-blocking of the probe rather than dead links, but that isn't confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures:** 7 of the 12 material links were probed and all 7 failed. The failures are 406 on `/1/b8033551`, `/1/2c8e3836`, `/1/911804e4`, `/1/24cc5552` and `/1/28ef1e66`, 403 on the unsubscribe link `/1/a0a57673`, and 400 on the SMS CTA `/1/2cdf1e4b`.
- **Likely cause:** Every failing URL is a `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…` redirect carrying `jwtH/jwtP/jwtS` parameters. Uniform 406/403 across unrelated destinations points to ESP-side bot filtering of the probe's user agent, not broken destinations. I can't confirm the real destination status from this data.
- **Destinations:** Decoding the `jwtP` payloads shows the targets are valid.
  - `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/sale/` (three image links)
  - `https://www.hoka.com/` (the logo link)
  - `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/stores.html` (Locate a Store)
  - `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` (return start)
  - `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…` (unsubscribe)
  - `https://hoka.attn.tv/p/ie7/landing-page` (SMS CTA)
- **SMS CTA:** The 400 on `/1/2cdf1e4b` is the odd one out. It redirects to a third-party Attentive domain, `hoka.attn.tv`, so it may be a real failure. It needs a manual check.
- **Redundant links:** Several image slots link to the same `/en/us/sale/` URL with different tracking IDs (`b8033551`, `911804e4`). This is expected for tracking and not a defect.
- **Coverage gap:** 7 links were skipped by the prioritizer, so the remaining links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text missing:**
  - `US_20261023_LastChanceClifton10_b.jpg` (1200x72)
  - `US_20261023_LastChanceClifton10_c.jpg` (1200x1856)
  - `US_Q3_SMSCTA_DoubleAsterisk.jpg` (1200x1276)
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
  - All of these are wrapped in links, so the links have no accessible name. The `c` and SMS images are large, and with images off they would render as blank link targets.
  - Two tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…`, `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`) also lack alt. This is minor, but `alt=""` is the convention.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Charset declaration ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so clients ignore it.
- **Conflicting viewport tags:** There are two `<meta name="viewport">` tags. The first sets `maximum-scale=1` and the second sets `user-scalable=yes`. The `maximum-scale=1` setting restricts pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Invalid CSS:** Several rules have empty values: `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`. They are harmless but dead.
- **Focus outline removed:** The global `* {…outline:none…}` removes focus outlines, which affects keyboard navigation in clients that support it.
- **Image-heavy:** The hero content is delivered as full-width JPGs. Combined with the missing alt text, the email degrades badly with images blocked. I can't tell from the truncated source whether live text exists below the images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%` or `[[ ]]` tokens appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part:** The QA check shows the text version is **0 chars** (FAIL). The email is HTML-only (no `multipart/alternative` text part), which hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **Unsubscribe link:** A visible link labelled "Unsubscribe" points to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe` via a Cordial redirect. The probe got 403, which is likely bot-blocking, but I can't confirm it works. Verify manually.
- **Auth headers:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The probable cause is the AgentMail relay, not the sender. This is a tooling gap, not a confirmed sender failure.
- **Not verifiable:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058) and the physical postal address weren't in the provided data. The HTML is truncated and no raw headers were supplied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs present and consistent** on the decoded destinations: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261023_Clifton10_Markdown_PromoBanner_A30Day_90Purchasers_720MensPurchasers_MensRoadPLPBrowsers_SalePLPBrowsers_365SaleTag_ExcludeAIAOVHigh`.
- **Overloaded `utm_campaign`:** It packs audience segmentation (purchaser and browser segments, AOV exclusion) into the campaign name. This is an analytics-hygiene concern because the string is exposed in URLs, but it is not a functional bug.
- **Identifiers in landing URLs:** The destinations carry `hmail=<64-hex string>` (likely a SHA-256 of the recipient email) and `cordial_id=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9`. A hashed email in a query string can leak into server logs and referrers.
- **Not evaluated:** Landing page content alignment wasn't checked, because probes were blocked and no post-click data exists.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part (currently 0 chars).
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four linked images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Manually click-test the unsubscribe link and the `hoka.attn.tv` SMS CTA. Re-probe the Cordial links with a browser-like user agent to separate bot-blocking from real failures.
4. Fix the head: add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to the charset meta, merge the duplicate viewport tags and drop `maximum-scale=1`, and fill in `<title>`.
5. Remove the empty-valued CSS rules and restore focus outlines.
6. Check the raw headers for SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`. Check the full HTML for the postal address.
7. Consider moving segmentation out of `utm_campaign`, and reviewing whether `hmail` needs to be passed in landing URLs.
