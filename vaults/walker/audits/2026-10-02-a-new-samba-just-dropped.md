- "SAMBA" in huge letters makes it obvious in one second what this is about.

## What's weak

- There's no price, no discount and no shipping offer. I have nothing to weigh against Hoka or New Balance.
- There's no word on comfort, cushioning, arch support or walking. A spotted fashion sneaker doesn't speak to my feet.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner crowds the top. It sends me somewhere else before I've even seen the Samba.

- 3. Drop the top banner, or move it to the bottom, so the Samba is the first thing I see.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure is a conventional table layout with responsive media queries. The automated QA flagged one hard failure: no plain-text alternative (0 chars). It also flagged mixed-content (`http://`) images and missing alt text. The HTML supplied is truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 17 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect wrappers. Destination URLs, status codes, and redirect chains are unverified.
- The tracking links use `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...`, not HTTPS. The QA also found an image on this host, `rThiK9gf6QAG96c6lyhcuN6kZOq6LwlRvZlHevdm2s0=452`, loaded over plain HTTP. That is likely the open-tracking pixel. Over HTTP, its request can be blocked, rewritten, or stripped by privacy proxies, and it exposes the recipient's request in cleartext.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is present. It has no effect in most email clients, but it does show that referrer data is not available for attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback:** [FAIL] The text version is 0 chars. This is a deliverability and accessibility problem, since text-only clients and some spam filters expect a text part.
- **Mixed content:** The "arrow" image (`b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`) is served over `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/...`. It appears 4 times, and clients may block it. The tracking pixel above is also HTTP.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `dv.adidas.com/o/b32fbb66-8bc9-4346-829c-08828d7598aa?...` has no `alt`.
  - The tracking pixel has no `alt`. A pixel should use `alt=""` explicitly.
  - If the `b32fbb66…` image is the primary content image, screen readers and image-blocked views will lose the message.
- **Charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it is not a valid charset declaration. Charset then relies on the MIME header alone. This is low risk if the header is correct.
- **Doctype:** `HTML 4.01 Transitional` with self-closed XHTML-style tags is inconsistent but harmless in practice.
- **Fonts:** The `@font-face` rules for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` use only `local('Arial')` and similar sources, so they always resolve to system fonts. The `<style>` blocks are also duplicated, which adds weight with no benefit.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated HTML means the rest was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** The footer and unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion of the HTML. No headers were supplied (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results), and QA reported no failures here.
- **Indirect evidence:** The From address is `adidas@us-news.comms.adidas.com`, and the tracking hosts (`click.comms.adidas.com`, `image.link.adidas.com`) share the `adidas.com` organizational domain. That is consistent with DMARC alignment, but alignment is not confirmed.
- **Plain-text part:** The 0-char text part also means there is no text-side unsubscribe or physical-address fallback.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing pages could not be verified. The destination URLs are encoded in opaque redirect tokens (`/CI0/010201a0face46e1-.../rThiK9...`), and the probe skipped all 17 links.
- Nothing in the visible source shows a broken or mismatched destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a multipart/alternative text part.** It fixes the one QA FAIL and covers text-only clients.
2. **Serve all images and tracking over HTTPS.** Switch `image.link.adidas.com` (the 4 "arrow" images) and `click.comms.adidas.com` (the pixel and links) to `https://`.
3. **Add alt text.** Use descriptive `alt` on `dv.adidas.com/o/b32fbb66-…`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and decorative arrows.
4. **Fix the charset declaration.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers.** That would let us confirm the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` headers, the physical address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Resolving the redirect chains would also let us check UTM parameters.
