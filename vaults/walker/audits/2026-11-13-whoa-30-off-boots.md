# WHOA 👀 30% OFF BOOTS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this is the fourth boots-and-UGG-type email in two days. The "30% OFF BOOTS*" banner at the top is big and green, so I can read it on my phone without my glasses. That part works. But then I scroll into a giant pink "WARM WISHES" section that is all slippers, and I'm not sure what the email is actually about: boots or slippers? It's trying to be both.
- The slippers are the part that interests me. A moccasin slipper and an UGG-style slip-on, "starting at $34.99," is the hands-free comfort I like. The problem is that the sale is on boots and the pictures are slippers, so the discount doesn't apply to what I'm looking at. After "Holiday savings alert" yesterday and the "30% OFF BOOTS!? DECK YEAH!" email, I feel like I'm getting the same sale on repeat. I'd glance at it, maybe tap the slippers, and not forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF BOOTS*" banner is huge and high-contrast, and the code BOOTFRIDAY sits right under it. I can read that without zooming.
- "Shop Boots" is a clear white button on green, easy to find and easy to tap.
- The slippers at $34.99 are a real, concrete price, and a moccasin slipper is right up my alley for comfort.
- "Shop by Size" with wide width and wide calf buttons is a nice touch. Fit matters more to me than the logo.
- The sender is DSW and the look matches the website, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The 30% off applies to boots, but the whole middle of the email is slippers. Which offer am I supposed to care about?
- The "Shop Women," "Shop Men," "Shop Kids" links under the banner are small. The only men's link is a tiny line of text.
- Nothing is for a 62-year-old man. Every product shown is women's or UGG-style, and the "And while you're here" row has a high heel in it.
- The "Warm Wishes" and "gift these to yourself" copy is cutesy and long. I had to scroll a long way past the pink art to reach any buttons.
- There's no cushioning, arch support or comfort information anywhere. The price is shown, but nothing says why the shoe is worth it.
- The 30% sale has a lot of exclusions in the fine print, which is very small and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer. Either lead with boots and show boots, or lead with slippers and drop the boots banner. Mixing them is what loses me.
- 2. Add a men's row with comfort language, such as "Men's slip-on slippers and boots with cushioned insoles," and make "Shop Men" a real button.
- 3. Make the slipper price a button: "Shop slippers from $34.99."
- 4. Cut the pink art section by half so the buttons show up sooner.
- 5. Add a line that says whether the 30% off and the slippers can be used together.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off boots, code BOOTFRIDAY, through 11/15`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Warm slippers from $34.99 + 30% off boots`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned men's boots and slip-on slippers, all in one place.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code BOOTFRIDAY at checkout. Wide widths in stock.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: DSW, "WHOA 👀 30% OFF BOOTS"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken CTA, the SMS opt-in link, which returns HTTP 400. It also has two images without alt text and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs. QA pass rate was 62%, with 12 of 72 links probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA returns 400 through the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/0l5YJ09Tpe2RyydeMo44ww~~/...` click-tracking redirect.
  - The failure is at the tracking-redirect layer. The ~800-character encoded path is a candidate cause (truncation or corruption), but this is unconfirmed.
  - The probe also reported an empty URL label before the link text. That suggests the `href` may be malformed or the anchor is wrapping something unusual.
  - Because this is the incentive CTA (15% off), any recipient who clicks it likely hits an error page.
- **Probe coverage:** 60 of 72 links were skipped (footer, utility, social). Their status is unknown, though an unsubscribe link is always probed and no failure was reported for it.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261113_P261697_V1_W-_Offer_Value` carries the recipient ID (`mi_u`) and campaign ID (`mi_ecmp`). This is normal and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked image with no alt:** `src: https://ablink.dsw.com/q/aL1E8nP8oetK5FP8KFapgQ~~/AAQRxRA~/BlRAuw5S...`. If this image is inside an anchor, the link has no accessible name. It is also a blank box when images are blocked.
- **Tracking pixel with no alt:** `o.gif` should have `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Ineffective meta declarations in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Without `http-equiv`, neither tag does anything. Charset then relies on the MIME header alone.
- **Plain-text part:** 26,025 of 30,642 characters (85%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. That is mostly long click-tracking URLs. It is poor for text-only clients and may be a spam-filter signal.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML is truncated, so body-level checks (table structure, `lang`, `role="presentation"`) could not be done.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens appear in the visible source or in the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be verified. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a limitation of the capture, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unverifiable from the provided data:** the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address. The truncated HTML does not show the footer.
- The sender domain `e.dsw.com` uses `ablink.dsw.com` tracking, so the sending and link domains are aligned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects with opaque encoded payloads. UTM parameters and final landing URLs cannot be seen from the HTML, and only 12 links were probed.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261113_P261697_V1_W-_Offer_Value`, on the pixel.
- The SMS opt-in destination fails (400), so landing-page alignment for that CTA cannot be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or replace the SMS opt-in link now.** Re-generate the tracked URL and test it in a live send. Check whether the 400 comes from the ESP link wrapper, an expired or oversized token, or the destination rejecting the redirect.
2. **Add alt text** to the linked image (`ablink.dsw.com/q/aL1E8nP8...`), describing its destination or action. Set `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
3. **Correct the head meta tags** by adding `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
4. **Improve the plain-text part.** Shorten or remove tracking on plain-text links, or add readable text around the URLs.
5. **Re-run QA with full headers** (Authentication-Results, List-Unsubscribe) and the untruncated HTML. Probe the remaining 60 links, or at least the footer and unsubscribe links, to close the compliance and continuity gaps.
