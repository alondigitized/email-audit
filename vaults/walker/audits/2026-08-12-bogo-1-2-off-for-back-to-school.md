# ✨ BOGO 1/2 off ✨ for back-to-school
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- Famous Footwear again — I checked, this makes it my twelfth email from them this summer, and I'm about done scrolling past these. The BOGO half-off banner up top is bold enough that I can't miss it, and normally that'd get a look, but then it says "We picked these just for you" and shows me eight straight pairs of kids' skate shoes — Adidas, Converse, Vans, DC. I don't have kids buying school shoes anymore, so whoever's algorithm wrote "just for you" got it flat wrong.
- The Birkenstock, Nike, and Jordan blocks further down at least have some adult appeal, but they're generic brand banners with no product shown — no idea what styles, no sizes, nothing I can actually picture on my feet. And the fine print buried at the bottom telling me BOGO excludes Birkenstock, Crocs, and half a dozen other brands means the one comfort brand I'd actually shop is carved out of the deal that got me to open the email in the first place.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on. Nothing here is worth a click, let alone forwarding to anyone.

## What's working

- The BOGO 1/2 off banner is big, colorful, and impossible to miss at the top.
- "Free Shipping" and rewards link are visible right up top without digging.
- The $10 Reward for Store Pickup is a real, specific offer if I were already planning a trip in.
- No render bugs — everything displayed clean, images loaded fine.

## What's weak

- "We picked these just for you" is eight pairs of kids' skate sneakers — nothing further from a 62-year-old man's closet.
- No men's comfort shoes anywhere in the whole email — no cushioning, no arch support, nothing hands-free.
- Birkenstock, Nike, and Jordan blocks show zero actual product photos, just logos and vague "up to X% off" banners.
- Fine print at the bottom excludes Birkenstock (and other comfort brands) from the BOGO deal — feels like bait-and-switch after leading with that banner.
- This is at least my twelfth Famous Footwear email this summer; the back-to-school kid stuff has worn out its welcome for someone shopping for himself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of "just for you" kids' back-to-school picks entirely — if there's no purchase history showing kids' sizes, don't show a men's subscriber a wall of skate shoes for grade-schoolers.
- 2. Lead the "picked for you" grid with actual men's comfort shoes — Skechers, Hoka, or New Balance slip-ons — if BOGO applies to those, show them.
- 3. Put real product photos under the Birkenstock/Nike/Jordan banners instead of logo-only tiles; I want to see the shoe before I click through.
- 4. Move the BOGO exclusions up near the banner, not buried in the footer — if Birkenstock's excluded, say so where I'll see it before I get excited.
