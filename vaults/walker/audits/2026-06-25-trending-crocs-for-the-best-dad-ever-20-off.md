# Trending Crocs for the BEST DAD EVER + $20 Off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival lands in my inbox again, and I'll admit the subject pulled me in — Crocs are on my short list, and $20 off is real money. But I'm sitting here on June 25th, four days after Father's Day, looking at a "DEALS for DAD LIMITED TIME!" hero banner, and I can't shake the feeling I'm getting yesterday's newspaper. The holiday window closed Sunday. This feels like clearance dressed up in its Father's Day suit.
- That said, I give them credit once I got inside. "DAD LOVES COMFORT" is exactly how I talk about shoes, and seeing Crocs and DUDE side by side at 30% off, with code FORDAD right there in the open, is a clean move. The barcode at the bottom for in-store scanning — that caught me off guard in the best way. I actually shop in the store more often than I'll admit.
- I'm not clicking today, but not because the offer is bad. The timing is what kills it. If this email had landed Monday or Tuesday — even better, a few days before Father's Day like the June 14 "New Deals for Dad" send — I'd have been shopping. As it stands, I'm filing it away and maybe walking into Shoe Carnival this weekend if my wife and I are already out.

## What's working

- **"DAD LOVES COMFORT" copy** is exactly the language I use — it put me squarely in the target audience and I felt it.
- **Code FORDAD is front and center**, no hunting through fine print, no asterisk chase.
- **The man in the Trending Crocs photo** looks my age, walking outside in a pair of Crocs-style sandals — that's a demographic match I actually register.
- **In-store barcode at the bottom** — I can take this to the register without printing anything. That is genuinely useful for someone like me.
- **Shoe Perks $10 reward reminder** — good to know my points balance is waiting; treats me like a member, not a stranger.

## What's weak

- **Father's Day is over.** Four days past the holiday and the hero still reads "BEST DAD EVER" — the creative team needed to rotate this template by Monday morning.
- **Preview text is a raw tracking URL.** The very first thing I see after the subject line in my inbox is a wall of "https://click.email.shoecarnival.com/?qs=ABB7In..." — that makes me pause, and not in a good way.
- **Too many sections stacked.** Trending Crocs → New → Goal Worthy Styles → Dad Loves Comfort → Last Chance → Price Break → Shoe Perks — I'm scrolling and scrolling and my eye doesn't know where to settle.
- **$20 off requires $99.50+ spend.** A single pair of Crocs doesn't always clear $100, so I'd need to add something to get there — which is fine, but it's a mental hurdle I wasn't expecting from the subject line.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the Father's Day creative immediately.** Reframe as post-holiday comfort clearance — lead with "Summer Comfort Deals" or "Crocs Clearance Just Dropped" to match where the calendar actually is.
- 2. **Fix the preheader — this is the single biggest miss.** Even "30% off Crocs + use code FORDAD" as preheader text would dramatically improve what I see at the inbox level before I decide to open.
- 3. **Cut the email to three sections max.** Hero offer → Crocs 30% off → Shoe Perks reminder. Everything else is noise that buries the main call.
- 4. **Add a lower entry offer** — "$10 off $59+" alongside the $20 off threshold would make the discount feel reachable for someone buying one pair.
- 5. **Drop one comfort detail in the Crocs product block** — "massaging cushion footbed" or "hands-free slip-on" — something that gives my feet a reason to show up at the CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Crocs 30% Off — Comfort Deals, Limited Time`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$20 Off: Crocs, DUDE & More On Sale Now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `30% off Crocs + DUDE — code FORDAD, use in-store or online`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your $10 Shoe Perks reward is waiting — plus Crocs at 30% off`

### 1. Technical Summary

Four critical link failures caused by a zero-width space (U+200B) injected into tracking URLs, rendering open tracking, pixel firing, read/forward beacons, and at least one click redirect non-functional. Two product images and the tracking pixel also lack alt text, creating accessibility gaps.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**CRITICAL — Zero-width space in tracking URLs**

All four failures share the same root cause: a U+200B character appended after `_AM` in the `t=` query parameter, making the URL non-ASCII and breaking the link resolver's codec.

Affected URLs (evidence — `​` = U+200B at position 75–76):
```

https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?p&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260617_crocs_heydude_AM​
```
