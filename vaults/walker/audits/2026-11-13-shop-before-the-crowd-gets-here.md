## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is well-built, with a responsive media query, Apple and Gmail client resets, and `lang="en"`. The HTML is truncated, though, so links, footer and compliance elements could not be checked. The only confirmed defects are a malformed meta tag and a missing `alt` on a third-party pixel.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Skipped probes:** 12 tracking links were not HTTP-probed, because the QA tool skips click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and final status codes are unverified.
- **Third-party beacon:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event pixel. It sits alongside the first-party open pixel at `click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10212&bmt=0`.
  - A second pixel adds a third-party request and a second open signal. Where blocked, it can also break open counts.
  - The `d_dpuid` value (in the QA excerpt) is truncated, so I can't confirm it is a resolved ID rather than an unresolved merge token.
- The first-party open pixel is correctly formed (`width="1" height="1" alt=""`) and wrapped in a hidden container.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags (confirmed):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Both are inert as written. Most clients use the MIME `Content-Type` header, so impact is low, but the charset declaration is ineffective.
- **Missing alt (confirmed by QA):** the demdex `<img>` has no `alt` attribute at all. Add `alt=""` as on the first-party pixel.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is not descriptive. Impact is minor and only applies in web-view or browser rendering.
- Not verified because the source is truncated: image alt text on content images, table `role="presentation"`, and a font fallback stack for `TT Crocs`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The `d_dpuid` value is the one item worth checking in a full copy.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** the QA warning says the header was not found because the email came through the AgentMail relay. That makes it a gap in the test harness, not a defect in the email. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Alignment:** the visible `From: email@crocs-email.com` matches the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`, which is consistent for alignment.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
- **Third-party tracking:** the Adobe demdex beacon is an additional data-collection point that the privacy notice should cover. I can't confirm that from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The truncated source exposes no destination URLs, and all 12 tracking links were skipped. UTM presence, landing-page alignment with the "2 pairs for $50 / Black Friday early access" offer, and redirect integrity were not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel, and give it `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:block"` if it lacks them.
3. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, with no truncation, to check the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and alt text coverage.
4. Resolve the tracking-link redirects, for example with a follow-redirects probe or by capturing the `qs=` destinations. Confirm each lands on a live Crocs page with consistent UTMs and that the landing page supports the offer.
5. For the harness: capture the original headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `DKIM-Signature`) before relay, or query the sending domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC records directly.
