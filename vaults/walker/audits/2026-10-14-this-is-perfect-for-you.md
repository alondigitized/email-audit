## What's working

- The hero is a men's waterproof Slip-in. It's hands-free and practical, and it's a good match for me.
- The headline is big and the blue-on-white text is easy to read on my phone.
- "Take Another Look" is a wide, dark button that's easy to tap.
- The 20% off members banner is big and clear, with a "valid thru 10/21" date.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is a simple stack of big tap targets. That's the kind of navigation I like.

## What's weak

- Five of the six "options we think you'll love" are women's or boys' shoes. That tells me they don't know who I am.
- The product captions under the grid are tiny. I'd have to zoom to read them.
- The 20% off offer sits below the whole grid, and the hero never mentions it.
- The subject line "This is perfect for you!" doesn't match the "still thinking about it" body. It's vague and a bit pushy.
- Nothing about arch support, cushioning or price. There are no reviews and no price on the hero shoe.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense block of tiny type.
- "(Almost) everything else" is a little cute for an exclusion list I can't see until I scroll to the legal text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fill the grid with men's slip-ins and walking shoes, based on what I browsed. That one change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Move the 20% off members banner up under the hero button, or put it in the hero text.
- 3. Put a price and a star rating under the hero shoe. Mention comfort features like Arch Fit and Relaxed Fit.
- 4. Enlarge the product captions.
- 5. Hold this send until the cart reminders have cooled off. Four or five emails in one day is too many.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Two automated QA failures: the unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400 when probed, and the email has no plain-text part (0 chars). Smaller issues are 13 `http://` image sources, missing alt text on 3 images, and an empty `<title>`. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, headers and most link URLs could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link failed (400):** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1MmZpSFY0ajRYThIDMVN5GNXj9QUgAg&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m42&externalId=2U3eG&attn_sid=…`. The `attn_macro` value base64-decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. This is a tokenized Attentive endpoint, so the 400 may come from the QA probe (missing session or user-agent) rather than from real recipients. It is still a hard failure in the automated check and should be verified manually.
- **Click tracking:** All inspected links (web version, logo) are wrapped in `https://skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`. The final destinations and any UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 25 were skipped, so link health is only partly verified.

- **Insecure image sources:** 13 images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`.
