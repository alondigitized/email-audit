# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "oh, another cart nag." This time it isn't the skull sneaker I've been pestered about since 11/05, which is a relief. It's a black Slip-ins Contour Foam shoe with rose-gold accents. That looks like a women's shoe to me, and I never put it in a cart. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" is cute, but it doesn't ring true when I haven't shown any interest.
- The Black Friday "Buy One, Get One 50% Off" banner is the best thing in here, and it's what would make me look twice. But it sits halfway down the page, under the cart nag and six shoes. Then I read the fine print. It says the offer ran 11/12 to 11/17, and today is the 20th. So I'm told my cart "expires soon" and handed a deal that already ended. That costs trust with a guy who cares whether the email matches what he finds on the site.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the men's Respected boots or the slip-ons below the hero. Would I forward it? No. I'd leave it unread, same as the last few.

## What's working

- The hero shows one shoe with one big "Complete Your Order" button in navy. It's easy to see and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The Black Friday banner is big and readable: "Buy One, Get One 50% Off, or 20% Off a Single Pair." That's real math I can work with.
- The "more great options" grid has men's slip-on and boot styles. The Slip-ins Murette slipper and the Respected 2.0 boots are right in my lane.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is plain text in a big font. I can read it without pinching the screen.

## What's weak

- The fine print says the offer ran "11/12/26 – 11/17/26." Today is 11/20, so the promotion looks expired. That's a bait-and-switch feeling.
- The hero shoe looks like a women's style. I have never shown interest in it, so "You liked these" feels like a guess.
- The best part of the email, the BOGO deal, is buried below the cart shoe and six more products.
- Nothing about me as a member shows up. I have 1,000 points and no "Hi, Walker" greeting appears. I get no member pricing and no mention of my points.
- The product captions are tiny. The footwear names are hard to read without zooming, and the long fine print at the bottom is worse. There are no reviews, no widths, and no mention of arch support or cushioning.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the dates. Don't send a Black Friday promo with last week's dates. Either extend the offer or show a live countdown.
- 2. Move the BOGO banner above the hero, or put it right beside the cart item. Lead with the price and let the shoe support it.
- 3. Show a men's or unisex hands-free item in the cart slot, or only send the cart reminder when I actually added something. Right now it feels like a guess.
- 4. Add "Hi, Walker" and my points balance near the top, like the 11/19 "Holiday Ready" send did.
- 5. Add one comfort line under the hero, like "Hands-free. Arch Fit. Cushioned." and a rating or review count.
- 6. Bump the caption text size for mobile.

Its `attn_macro` value base64-decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, which confirms it is the unsubscribe link. - The "web version" link is also an `attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirect. No tracking problem was found beyond the above.

They include the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the hero "Complete Your Order" image (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), the app-store badges, Afterpay, and the social icons. - **Plain-text part is empty.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars", so there is no `text/plain` alternative. This affects deliverability scoring and accessibility. It may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so confirm it on the raw MIME.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- The two main CTAs were rate-limited (429), so their destinations were never resolved. There is no evidence for or against cart deep-linking.
- The sender domain is `msgs.skechers.com`, images are on `emails.skechers.com`, and links are on `attentivemail.com`. Alignment between these is not verifiable from the data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually open the unsubscribe URL (`skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…`) with a normal browser user agent. If it still fails, fix it first, since it is the compliance-critical item.
