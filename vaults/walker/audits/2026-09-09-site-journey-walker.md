# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 2/10 (prev 2/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-09**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I walked through skechers.com on my phone the way I'd normally shop — bounce past the promo banner, try to log into my account, browse Men's, look at a slip-in, and try to buy it. The browsing part is fine — clean layout, big product photos, sensible category names like "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes" that speak directly to what I want. But two things broke the trip cold: tapping "Log In" did nothing at all, and after fumbling the size selection on the product page, the View Cart screen came back **completely blank — no header, no items, no "your cart is empty" message, nothing.** For a 62-year-old on a phone, a blank white screen isn't "empty," it looks like the site crashed. I closed the app at that point.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**2/10** — unchanged from yesterday. Browsing and search still work reasonably, but the two most important moments in the funnel — sign-in and cart — are both non-functional from where I sit. A shopper who can't log in and can't see their cart isn't converting, no matter how nice the product photos are.

## 3. What's Working

- Homepage hero and top nav banner (comfort features: Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit) are front and center — good, that's exactly the language I look for.
- The Men's hamburger menu is well organized: Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections — I can find "Shoes" without hunting.
- Men's Shoes landing page leads with "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes" tiles right at the top — that's my whole persona in two taps.
- Product page for the Aero Burst clearly shows star rating (166 reviews), price, and a "Hands Free Slip-ins" badge — reassuring, relevant.
- Search for "comfort shoes" returned genuinely relevant results (slip-ins, Max Cushioning) with 1,516 matches — the engine understands the query.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Log In does nothing.** The screenshot after tapping the account icon is pixel-identical to the prior screen — no modal, no redirect, no error. I have no idea if I'm signed in or not.
- **View Cart is a blank white page.** After going through Add to Cart, the cart screen shows literally nothing — not even a header or "empty cart" message. This reads as broken, not empty.
- **Add to Cart silently failed.** I never got a size selected (12.5 and 14.0 were grayed out/out of stock, which is fine), but the "Please select a size" warning stayed up and the cart badge stayed at 0 — the flow doesn't guide me back to fix it.
- $165 for a shoe with "Excluded from promotions" sitting right under the price is a letdown right when I'm deciding — the 20%-off banner up top got my hopes up for nothing.
- No arch support / cushioning comparison content anywhere in the journey — I never saw anything explaining Arch Fit vs. a competitor, which is the actual decision I'm trying to make.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix Login immediately** — a tap that produces zero visible response is the kind of thing that makes an older shopper assume the site is broken and give up, not retry.
2. **Fix the blank cart page** — at minimum render "Your cart is empty" with a way back to shopping. A blank white screen is a dead end.
3. Make the size-selection error more visible (scroll-to, red border on the size grid, or a toast) so tapping Add to Cart without a size doesn't just leave me confused.
4. Surface "Excluded from promotions" as a smaller, less alarming note, or better — apply eligible promos automatically so I don't feel baited.
5. Add a simple comfort-technology comparison blurb (Arch Fit vs. standard insole, cushioning type) on the PDP — this is the #1 thing driving my purchase decision and it's absent.

## 6. Bottom Line

Browsing feels genuinely better-organized than a lot of shoe sites, and the search engine understands what I'm looking for. But none of that matters when the two moments that turn browsing into buying — sign in, see my cart — are broken. Until Login responds to a tap and View Cart actually renders something, this journey ends the same place it did yesterday: no purchase, no account, no visibility into what I have selected.

## 7. Evidence

**Step 1 — Homepage:** A clean hero image of a couple walking, promo banner up top ("Extra 20% off"), and a horizontally-scrolling comfort-feature bar (Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit, Max Cushioning) right under the header. Easy to read, nothing crowded. Felt relevant to me immediately — no personalization yet, but the categories match what I shop for.

**Step 2 — Dismiss Popups:** No modal popup appeared to dismiss; instead a second promo strip ("25% off Apparel & Bags — Applied at Cart") appeared under the header. Not a popup in the traditional sense, but harmless. Text is small but legible without zooming.

**Step 3 — Log In:** Screen is identical to Step 2 — tapping the account/login icon produced no visible change whatsoever. No modal, no navigation, no error. This is a hard failure — I can't tell if I'm supposed to wait, if the tap didn't register, or if something crashed. No way to judge personalization since I never got past this screen.

**Step 4 — Men category:** A slide-out drawer opened from the hamburger menu, showing "MEN" with clear sections: Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections, Collaborations, Clothing & Accessories. Large tap targets, readable text, good use of chevrons to indicate drill-down. This part felt genuinely well-designed for a phone.

**Step 5 — Men > Shoes:** Landing page opens with three big tiles — "Hands Free Slip-Ins," "Walking Shoes," "Athletic Sneakers" — exactly the categories I care about, front and center before I even scroll to the 741-result grid. A "Free pickup at [location]" toggle is a nice touch. Filter/Sort controls are small but tappable.

**Step 6 — Product detail:** Skechers Slip-ins: Aero Burst, $165.00, 166 reviews at ~4.5 stars, large product photo, "Hands Free Slip-ins" badge. Clean layout, but the "Excluded from promotions" note right under the price undercuts the 20%-off banner I saw twice already — mild bait-and-switch feeling.

**Step 7 — Add to Cart:** Size grid (7.0–14.0), two sizes correctly grayed out as unavailable, red "Please select a size" warning visible, QTY dropdown, and an Add to Cart button with a focus outline (suggesting it was tapped without a size chosen). The cart badge in the header still reads "0" — so the tap didn't do anything except surface the warning. Confusing dead end for a shopper who taps the big blue button expecting it to work.

**Step 8 — View Cart:** Completely blank white screen — no header, no nav, no "empty cart" message, nothing rendered at all. This is the single worst moment in the journey. As a 62-year-old, my instinct here is that the site or my phone browser has crashed, not that my cart is simply empty.

**Step 9 — Search "comfort shoes":** 1,516 results, clean two-column grid, first result "Skechers Slip-ins: GO WALK Joy – Cozy Fit Cozy Easy" ($85) is spot-on for me. Second result is a Women's shoe (Max Cushioning Elite 3) — mildly off-target since gender wasn't filtered, but overall the results felt relevant and readable, with color swatches and price ranges shown clearly.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary
Audit of an 8-step mobile journey (Homepage → Log In → Men category → Men > Shoes → PDP → Add to Cart → View Cart → Search). TTFB is fast to good on most steps but elevated on the first three (Homepage 471ms, Log In 398ms, Men category 398ms) before dropping sharply (56–133ms) on later steps. LCP/CLS were not captured in this run. 17 axe violations were found, concentrated on Homepage, View Cart, and Search, including 2 critical and 3 serious issues. Network logs show repeated 429s against a bot-detection fingerprinting endpoint and against `/cart/` itself, plus a CORS failure blocking a third-party stylesheet.

## Accessibility
**Homepage** (critical/serious):
- `button-name` [critical] — one or more buttons have no discernible text (icon-only buttons missing `aria-label`).
- `aria-required-parent` [critical] — ARIA role used without its required parent role context.
- `link-name` [serious] — links with no accessible text.
- `heading-order`, `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-unique`, `region` [moderate] — heading hierarchy and duplicate/non-top-level landmark structure issues.
- `aria-allowed-role` [minor].

**View Cart** (serious/moderate):
- `document-title` [serious] — page has no `<title>`.
- `html-has-lang` [serious] — `<html>` missing `lang` attribute.
- `landmark-one-main` [moderate] — no `<main>` landmark.
- `page-has-heading-one` [moderate] — no `<h1>`.

This cluster suggests the cart view is rendered as a partial/fragment (e.g., AJAX-swapped content) rather than a fully-formed document — worth confirming whether `/cart/` is ever a direct-navigation target.

**Search "comfort shoes"**: `aria-allowed-role` [minor], `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, `region` [moderate] — duplicate/misplaced landmark roles, likely from a search results widget injecting an `<aside>` outside the main landmark tree.

## SEO
- View Cart's missing `<title>` and `lang` attribute are technical SEO defects independent of accessibility impact — if this URL is ever crawlable/indexable, it ships with no title and no declared language.
- Missing `<h1>` on View Cart weakens on-page structure/semantic signal for that URL.
- No canonical, meta description, or structured-data issues were captured in this run — data not available to assess.

## Performance
- TTFB: Homepage 471ms, Log In 398ms, Men category 398ms — then Men > Shoes 65ms, PDP 56ms, Add to Cart 56ms, View Cart 133ms, Search 56ms. The pattern (slow first three, fast rest) is consistent with cold cache/CDN warm-up on initial navigation rather than a systemic backend latency issue, but should be confirmed with repeat-visit data.
- LCP and CLS were not captured for any step — this is a data gap, not a "no issues found." Re-run with full Web Vitals instrumentation before drawing conclusions on paint/layout stability.
- Homepage HTML shows 20+ third-party scripts loaded via tag manager (GTM, Monetate ×2, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, Reddit, Bing, Taboola, Quantum Metric, Impact, CreativeCDN, Stape, Pulse Insights, go2sdk, WebFont Loader, plus Google Ads/Analytics destinations). Most are `async`, but this volume is a plausible contributor to the elevated homepage TTFB and to main-thread contention affecting LCP once measured.

## Mobile Optimization
- Homepage/Log In/Men category HTML sets an inline `--vh` custom property on `<html>` (`style="--vh: 6.64px"`), indicating JS-based viewport-height calculation — a common mobile-Safari 100vh workaround. This introduces a layout-affecting script dependency that runs after initial paint, a candidate contributor to CLS (unmeasured here).
- No other mobile-specific markup defects (viewport meta, responsive image srcset, tap-target sizing) are visible in the provided HTML samples; full assessment blocked by missing CLS data.

## Console & Network Errors
- **429 (4×)** on `.../fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.779` — a Kasada bot-detection fingerprinting endpoint. Repeated rate-limiting on this call during normal navigation could degrade or fail the bot challenge for legitimate mobile users.
- **429 (1×)** on `https://www.skechers.com/cart/` — a first-party page URL returning rate-limit status, not a third-party asset. This is the more notable finding: if genuine users hit rate limits loading their own cart, it's a functional defect, not just noise.
- **CORS failure**: `web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css` blocked — no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header, causing `net::ERR_FAILED`. This stylesheet backs a Stylitics outfit/recommendation widget; its styles are not applying wherever that widget is embedded.
- **Summarizer API warnings (4×)**: Chrome's experimental on-device Summarizer API is being invoked with an unsupported language and aborting. Source (site script vs. extension) is not identified in this data — needs confirmation before treating as a site defect.

## Recommendations
1. Investigate the 429 on `/cart/` and the repeated 429s on the Kasada fingerprint endpoint — confirm bot-protection thresholds aren't throttling legitimate mobile sessions during checkout.
2. Add `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` to the Stylitics stylesheet response (or self-host/proxy the asset) to fix the broken widget styling.
3. Ensure `/cart/` renders as a complete document with `<title>`, `<html lang>`, a single `<h1>`, and a top-level `<main>` landmark.
4. Fix critical axe issues on Homepage: add `aria-label`/text to icon-only buttons (`button-name`) and correct ARIA role nesting (`aria-required-parent`).
5. Add accessible text to icon-only links flagged by `link-name`.
6. Resolve duplicate/non-top-level landmark roles on Homepage and Search results (`landmark-unique`, `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`).
7. Audit and consolidate the third-party tag footprint on the homepage (20+ scripts) to reduce main-thread contention and improve TTFB.
8. Re-run the audit with LCP/CLS capture enabled — current data cannot support paint-timing or layout-stability conclusions.
9. Identify the source triggering Summarizer API calls with an unsupported language and gate/remove the call.
