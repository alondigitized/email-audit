# Ready for Black Friday?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Well, here come the shopping bags again. The Nov 13 send opened with Christmas ornaments and one earlier this month with shopping bags. This one opens with three paper bags and a big "Black Friday Preview" headline. At least the offer is clear: up to 40% off, with free shipping on everything.
- The shoes come late. I have to scroll past the bags and a "Shop by gender" block before I see any sneakers. Then it's a grid of six, and most of them are fashion or trail looks, not walking shoes. I'd skim it, maybe tap "Walking" at the bottom, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- "Black Friday Preview" and "Up to 40% off select styles" are big and easy to read on my phone.
- Free shipping on all orders is a good thing to see up top.
- The "Shop now" button is black and wide, and I could hit it with my thumb.
- The 990v6 and 574 are shoes I know, and they look sturdy. That's the cushioned, supportive kind of New Balance I'd actually wear.
- There's a "Walking" link in the footer, so I can find my category without searching.

## What's weak

- The top third of the screen is paper bags. Shoes are the point, and I had to scroll to find any.
- "Up to 40% off select styles" doesn't say which styles. I can't tell if the 990v6 is on sale or if only some odd colorway is.
- None of the six shoes shows a price or a sale tag. It's just the name under the picture.
- Walking is buried in small text at the very bottom. The Hierro v9 trail shoe, the 1906R and the pink 740 aren't for me.
- Nothing says I'm a customer. No loyalty mention and no "men's walking" section.
- "Shop by gender" is a lot of vertical space for three words.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a men's walking or cushioned shoe in the hero with a real sale price, such as "990v6, now $XXX," in place of the bags.
- 2. Show was/now pricing on each product tile.
- 3. Add a "Walking" and "All-day comfort" row near the top, and cut the gender block or shrink it.
- 4. Say what the 40% actually covers, for example "40% off select walking and lifestyle styles."
- 5. Show the member price or points if I'm in the loyalty program.

# Technical Audit: New Balance, "Ready for Black Friday?"

Only the truncated HTML head/CSS and the automated QA output were available. The body markup, footer and link `href`s were not visible, so those areas are marked as unverified rather than clean.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's QA run showed no hard failures (0 issues, 2 warnings, 75% pass rate). Remaining problems are missing alt text on 3 images, template leftovers in the CSS, and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage:** 12 of 36 links were probed and 24 were skipped (footer, utility and social). The QA output reports no broken links among the 12 probed. The 24 skipped links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,512 of 14,244 characters (about 88%) are URLs. The plain-text alternative is mostly wrapped tracking links, which is a spam-scoring risk and unreadable for text-only clients.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12690873_2276449_23983314.gif` has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and explicit dimensions.
- **Placeholder asset URL:** `.bgwhite { background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png'); }` is an unreplaced placeholder. It is also non-HTTPS. If `.bgwhite` is applied anywhere, it triggers a failed request and a possible mixed-content warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking GIF above
- **`@font-face` without `src`:** every declaration (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and so on) has no `src` descriptor. No web font can load, so all text falls back to Arial or Times New Roman. This is likely unintended, given the `.ff` and `.fst*` classes that reference them.
- **Conflicting `.bg1`:** it is defined twice, as `#ffffff` and later as `#58546d`. The later rule wins, so any element using `.bg1` renders purple-grey.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Template leftover:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is shipped as the document title.
- **Dark mode:** dark-mode support is declared (`color-scheme`, `prefers-color-scheme`). The `a {color:#cf0a2c !important}` override applies to every link, including links inside buttons that use `.cta1*` with `color:#ffffff`. Verify that CTA text stays legible. The truncated source does not confirm a failure.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens appeared, though the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** the header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. This reflects the AgentMail relay and is not evidence of a failure. Re-check against raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the QA output says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure. The footer markup and the postal address were not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links appear to be wrapped through `link.email.newbalance.com` redirects (Marketing Cloud-style `custloads` paths). Final destination URLs and any UTM parameters were not visible, so I can't assess campaign tagging or landing-page alignment.
- Image assets come from `/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`, a welcome-series folder. It is reused in a Black Friday promo, which suggests shared template assets. Confirm they are intended for this send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or replace the `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder in `.bgwhite`. If it is kept, it must be HTTPS.
3. Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules, or delete them and rely on the Arial fallback stack.
4. Remove the duplicate `.bg1` rule, and fix the `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">` tag.
5. Replace `<title>New Balance Template</title>` with the campaign subject.
6. Shorten the plain-text part. Drop the tracking wrappers on secondary links and add readable text.
7. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC on a direct-delivery copy, and re-run link probing on the 24 skipped footer and utility links.
8. Re-run this audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check unsubscribe, address, merge tokens and UTM parameters.
