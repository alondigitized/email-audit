- Non-HTTPS images may be blocked or rewritten by clients. A blocked tracking pixel would undercount opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Missing alt text (QA WARN):
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/c99f49af-1b10-4ae3-bfe3-4a37923b9ad1?cp_tp=…`
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…/XQ_PNwBrPtG-dpJExWcm7u0KO…=452`. This one is likely a 1×1 pixel, so it should have `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech.
- `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it does not declare a charset. The subject contains "é" (Pokémon). Rendering depends on the Content-Type header, which isn't visible in the provided data.
- The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional. `lang="en"` is present, which is good.
- The CSS has redundancy. The `@font-face` blocks for AdihausDIN and AdineuePRO are declared twice, in the second and third `<style>` blocks. Both use only `local('Arial')` and `local('Helvetica')` sources, so they load no actual web font. The footer media queries (`.net-footer-logo-v2` and others) are also duplicated. This adds weight, which matters in Gmail's ~102KB clipping threshold. The HTML was truncated, so total size is unknown.
- The global rule `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` forces centering on every `<p>` and could cause unintended alignment in nested content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source was truncated, so this applies only to the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. This harms deliverability and accessibility, and spam filters often penalize HTML-only messages.
- The truncated source and QA data show no `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, no unsubscribe link, no physical address, and no SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. These cannot be confirmed or flagged from the data provided.
- The From domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but its authentication is not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be validated. All 13 links go through opaque `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects that the QA probe skipped, and the truncated source doesn't reveal the destination URLs.
- Recommend a follow-up probe that resolves the redirect chain and checks for `utm_*` retention and the landing page on `adidas.com`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a multipart plain-text alternative** (QA FAIL). This is the only hard failure.
2. **Switch all image sources to HTTPS**, including `image.link.adidas.com` (arrow) and the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…` tracking pixel. Confirm that the ESP serves both over TLS.
3. **Add alt text**: a descriptive alt for `dv.adidas.com/o/c99f49af-…`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
4. **Fix the charset declaration** with `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`. Confirm the MIME Content-Type header also specifies UTF-8.
5. **Deduplicate the CSS** (`@font-face` and the footer media queries) to reduce size and clipping risk.
6. **Re-run QA with untruncated source and full headers** to check unsubscribe, `List-Unsubscribe`, physical address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the redirect and UTM chain.
7. **Fix the QA summary.** It reports "1 issue(s), 0 warning(s)" while listing six WARN lines, so the counting is inconsistent.
