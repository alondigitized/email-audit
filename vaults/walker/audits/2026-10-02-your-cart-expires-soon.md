# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. This is the D'Lites "Spring Bloom" that has followed me since 9/17, and it was in the "Did you forget something?" email yesterday. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" is cute copy, but I'm a 62-year-old man and I never liked these. I've now seen this shoe in at least eight emails.
- The one thing that helps is the 15% for guests / 20% for members banner. But it's buried below the shoe and the grid, and it says "Thru 10/1." Today is 10/2. The fine print says the offer was valid 9/23 to 9/28 and covers boots and outdoor styles only. I can't tell which date is real, and that makes me trust the whole email less. There's also no "Hi, Walker," Member badge or points balance, which the 10/1 member emails had.
- Would I click? No. I might glance at the men's Altman slip-in in the grid, but I'd just go to the site myself. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The headline is big and bold, and the navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is easy to see and tap on my phone.
- The men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman is in the grid. That's the closest thing to my shoe, hands-free and roomy.
- The 15% / 20% banner is large and easy to read, and it shows that members get more.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) is in big text with wide tap bars. That's good for my eyes and thumbs.
- Nothing is broken. The images all loaded and nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's floral sneaker I've ignored for two weeks. It's the same shoe in every cart email.
- Most of the grid is women's and kids' shoes. Only one of the six is for a man my age.
- The dates don't agree. The banner says "Thru 10/1," the fine print says 9/23 to 9/28, and the fine print only mentions boots and outdoor styles. It feels like fine-print bait-and-switch, and the offer is already expired.
- The text under the shoes and the fine print are tiny. I'd have to zoom in.
- There's no member recognition: no name, no badge, no points. This reads like a stranger's cart reminder.
- The footer is crowded with texting, app, Afterpay and Klarna, which dilutes the message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop showing the same abandoned shoe after the first or second reminder. Use my browsing and purchase history (men's slip-ins, Arch Fit, Relaxed Fit) for the hero instead.
- 2. Put the offer right under the hero, with one deadline that matches the fine print. Never send an expired date.
- 3. Add "Hi, Walker," the Member badge and my points, like the 10/1 "Last chance: Members save 20%" email did.
- 4. Fill the grid with comfort picks for men: slip-ins, arch support, walking. Add star ratings so I can compare against Hoka and Brooks.
- 5. Make the fine print and the product captions larger, and move the app and BNPL blocks out of the main flow.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, your slip-ins are still in your cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart + 20% member savings, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Relaxed Fit comfort, saved for you. Members get 20% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size is still in stock. Check out before the offer ends.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. A single 400 from an automated probe may reflect a bot or missing-session rejection rather than a dead link, but it is the one link that must work, so it needs manual verification.

- **Probe coverage was partial.** Only 12 of 37 links were probed and 25 were skipped, so the rest are unverified.
- All links go through the Attentive click-tracking redirect (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<opaque>`). This is expected. The final destinations can't be seen from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images use `http://` sources** on `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`. This covers the logo, the "Complete Your Order" hero, the app-store badges, the Afterpay, Curbside Pickup and Find a Store icons, and the social icons.
