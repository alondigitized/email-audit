# Memorial Day Deals Inside
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we are — Memorial Day Monday, and Shoe Carnival is leading off the holiday with the exact same red-and-yellow "Inflation Relief" banner I've now seen every single day for going on two weeks. The only real twist today is the slap of "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" over the top and a military-only 20% off block near the bottom. Otherwise it's the same $20-off-$99.98 stacked offer, the same BOGO Free women's & kids sandals, the same Jellies, the same Crocs sandals at $39.98 I've already scrolled past three times this week.
- For a 62-year-old guy looking for cushioned, hands-free walking shoes, this email tells me nothing. The hero is women's sandals. The second hero is women's & kids sandals. The third is jellies — I had to look up what those even are. Crocs at $39.98 is the only thing remotely in my lane, and even those are styled for somebody half my age. The military 20% off is a nice gesture for Memorial Day but I'm not a vet, so it's just real estate.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward to a friend? Maybe the BOGO to my daughter for the grandkids. Otherwise it's a delete — and at this cadence I'm getting close to hitting unsubscribe.

## What's working

- The Memorial Day banner at top finally acknowledges the actual holiday — that's a beat they hit on the right day.
- BOGO Free on women's & kids sandals is a clear, easy-to-understand offer my wife or daughter would notice.
- Military 20% off is the right kind of message for the day — feels respectful, not opportunistic.
- The $10 SHOE PERKS reward block at the bottom is a real loyalty signal — that's the kind of thing that gets me to log in.
- Prices are huge and readable on a phone — no squinting required.

## What's weak

- Not a single men's shoe in the entire email on Memorial Day weekend — the biggest casual-walking-shoe selling weekend of the spring.
- Same red-and-yellow "Inflation Relief" banner I've stared at for two straight weeks — at this point I tune it out.
- "Jellies Sale" — I'm 62, I had to look that up. Not for me, not for anyone in my house.
- The $20 off $99.98 minimum feels like a hurdle, not a deal — most casual shoes I'd buy land at $70-90.
- Eleven-plus emails in two weeks with the same creative — this is approaching unsubscribe territory.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a men's comfort shoe in the hero on Memorial Day weekend.** Skechers Slip-Ins, Hoka Bondi, Brooks Ghost — any one of those with a Memorial Day price would beat sandals and jellies for half the list.
- 2. **Lead with the military 20% off, not bury it.** That's the only block that actually feels like Memorial Day. Make it the hero, then stack the family sandal deals below.
- 3. **Rotate the banner creative.** Two straight weeks of "Inflation Relief" red-and-yellow has gone from urgent to wallpaper. Even just swapping to a Memorial Day red-white-blue treatment would feel fresh.
- 4. **Segment by gender.** I've audited a dozen of these now and not once have I seen a men's-led hero. There's no way the list isn't tagged.
- 5. **Drop the $99.98 threshold to $79.98** or make the $20-off automatic on any pair — the current floor feels like it's designed to push a second item I don't need.
