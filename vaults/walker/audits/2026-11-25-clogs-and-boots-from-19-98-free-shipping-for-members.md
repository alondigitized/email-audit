**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

The body doesn't match it. I see no free-shipping message anywhere, only a small "Join SHOE PERKS" strip at the very top. That mismatch bothers me. I trust a brand more when the email matches what the subject said.

Almost all of it is women's and kids' clogs, cozy slippers and boots, though. The only things for a 62-year-old man are the Nike 30% off, the Jordan 40% off, the Skechers from $39.98 and a small "MENS" link at the bottom. I'd skim it, maybe tap Skechers, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's weak

- The subject says free shipping for members, but I can't find it in the email. The offer I was promised isn't there.
- Nearly every hero is women's or kids' clogs, cozy slippers and boots. Nothing is aimed at a man who wants cushioning, arch support or slip-ons.
- The Skechers tile is a small strip near the bottom, with no shoe shown and no mention of Slip-ins or comfort.
- It's the same Black Friday creative as the 22nd, 23rd and 24th emails, now in a different order. I'm tired of it.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Shoe Carnival, "Clogs and boots from $19.98 + free shipping for members"

### 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound for email, with responsive media queries, a viewport meta and Outlook/Gmail/Samsung fixes. I found two real defects, both in the tracking pixels: an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` macro and a stray zero-width character in a URL. Source truncation and the relay stripping headers left the compliance and click-link checks unverifiable.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved placeholder in a tracking pixel.** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still contains the literal `[UNIQUE]` token. The ESP did not substitute it at send time. Open attribution or dedup for this pixel is probably broken or collapsed to a single value.
- **Malformed pixel URL.** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261125_extra_doorbusters_nike_jordan_boots_AM​` has two problems:
  - The URL ends in a zero-width character (U+200B, visible in the QA output after `_AM`). This can corrupt the campaign value on the tracker side.
  - `utm_campaign=...` is nested inside the `t=` parameter instead of being its own query parameter, so standard analytics parsing won't read it as UTM.
- **Campaign slug mismatch.** The slug says `nike_jordan_boots`, but the subject is about clogs and boots from $19.98. This may mean the template was reused with stale campaign metadata, which would skew reporting.
- **34 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them because they are click-redirect domains, so click-through resolution and destination status are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two content images:**
  - `https://image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/8596c416-45c9-4d51-afc5-a39814038aba.jpg`
  - `https://image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/56f4d386-deaa-4ef4-83a9-77da7e03c3a0.jpg`
  
  With images blocked or a screen reader, these render as nothing or as the filename.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** Both `emltrk.com` pixels lack `alt`. Low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct fix for decorative or tracking images.
- **Invalid meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so the IE=edge directive has no effect. `charset="utf-8"` still works. This is cosmetic.
- **Doctype and namespace mismatch.** The page declares an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with an XHTML `xmlns`. This is common in email, and I saw no rendering failure from it.
- **Not a defect.** The source includes the Apple/Samsung autolink resets, a `color-scheme: light` declaration and a `lang="en"` attribute.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `d=[UNIQUE]` in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel is an unrendered token (see section 2).
- No other unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|`) appear in the visible source, but the HTML was truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** QA expected this from the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are **unknown**, not failed. Verify against raw headers or a deliverability seed test.
- **Unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address:** Cannot be determined. The HTML was truncated before the footer, and no header data was supplied. I'm not flagging them as missing.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No destination URLs or UTM values were available for the 34 skipped click links, so landing-page alignment is unverifiable.
- The only visible campaign parameter is the malformed `utm_campaign` inside the pixel `t=` value, with the `nike_jordan_boots` mismatch (see section 2).

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ESP macro so `d=[UNIQUE]` resolves at send time, and confirm that pixel records opens correctly.
2. Remove the trailing U+200B from the campaign string. Pass `utm_campaign` as a top-level query parameter instead of nesting it in `t=`.
3. Correct the campaign slug (`nike_jordan_boots`) to match this send.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images, and add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
5. Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers, then verify the following:
   - the unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` headers
   - the physical address
   - SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment
   - final destinations and UTM carry-through on the click links, by following redirects for a sample of the 34 tracked links
