"WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." — no, you didn't, because I never noticed it in the first place. I don't know whose cart that is, but it isn't mine.

- The 20% off member banner with a clear code (EXTRA20) is a real, usable offer once you get to it.
- A couple of the "more options" thumbnails are actually in my lane — the men's slip-in Arch Fit and the men's basketball shoe.
- Layout's clean, nothing broken or overlapping, easy enough to read on my phone.
- The bottom has the practical stuff I'd actually use — find a store, curbside pickup, pay-later options.

## What's weak

- Same pink floral women's D'Lites cart nag as before — I never touched that shoe, and slapping a new headline on it doesn't change that.
- Headline and subhead are both about "your cart" — a cart that isn't mine, which just tells me their targeting's off.
- The real offer, the 20% off, is stuck way down the page instead of up top where I'd see it.
- Kids' Garage sneakers in my "more options" row — I'm 62, not shopping for a kid.
- This is at least the fourth near-identical cart-recovery email this week with the same shoe, just reworded.

- 2. Lead with the 20% off Plus member deal at the top, not buried two-thirds down the page — that's the one thing here I'd actually act on.
- 3. If the recommendation engine's going to guess for me, keep it to men's comfort/slip-on shoes, not kids' light-up sneakers.
- 4. Ease off the cart-abandon cadence — same shoe, fourth spin this week, I've stopped reading past the headline.
