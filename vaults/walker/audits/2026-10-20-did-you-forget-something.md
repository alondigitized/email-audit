## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has a broken Unsubscribe link (HTTP 400) and no plain-text part. It also loads 11 images over `http://` and has several images with no alt text. Authentication status couldn't be verified.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Unsubscribe link returns 400.** The QA probe got a 400 from `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1MmZpSFY0ajRYThIDMVN5GN6-9wUgAg&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&...&attn_sid=4f0dd852-...`. The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, so it is the real unsubscribe endpoint. The token may be single-use, expired, or rejected for a non-browser client. Re-test it from a real client before treating it as definitively broken.
- **429 on two Attentive tracking redirects.** These are the "Ready to check out? Complete your order" CTA and the `de642685-...jpg` image link, both on `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. A 429 is rate limiting, so the links are probably valid. Their destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Click coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 25 skipped, so footer, social and utility links were not checked.
- **Click tracking is wrapped.** All links go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`, so the final destination URLs aren't visible in the source.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Insecure image sources.** 11 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`. These are the logo (`dde00662-...png`), `a6ec5198-...jpg`, the app-store badges, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, and the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons. Some clients block or rewrite them, and they can trigger mixed-content warnings. The same host works over `https://` for `de642685-...jpg`.
- **Missing alt text on content images.** These images have none:
  - `246215_BKOR_HERO_LG` (the hero image, `images.skechers.com/image;width=auto%2Cformat=auto/246215_BKOR_HERO_LG`)
  - `de642685-...jpg`
  - `a6ec5198-...jpg`
  
  The `de642685-...jpg` image is also a link, so its link text falls back to the filename. If the product images carry the message, image-blocking clients will show nothing.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt.** `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` is a tracking pixel, so this is low severity. Add `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **Malformed charset meta.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it does nothing. The charset relies on the Content-Type header.
- **Mobile.** `viewport` is set and the layout stacks at 620px. Nothing wrong was found.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `[[`) appeared in the truncated source. The cart contents themselves weren't visible, so they can't be checked.

**5. Compliance**
- **Unsubscribe is non-functional in the probe.** See section 2. If it fails for real users, that is a CAN-SPAM problem, because opt-out must work.
- **Missing plain-text alternative.** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't reported by QA.** I can't confirm they are present or absent.
- **Physical address.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so it is not confirmed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
Not assessable. Tracking redirects hide the destination URLs, and the two probed CTAs returned 429. Neither UTM parameters nor landing-page alignment can be confirmed.
