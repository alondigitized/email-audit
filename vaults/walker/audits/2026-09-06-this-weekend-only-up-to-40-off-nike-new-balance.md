# THIS WEEKEND ONLY: Up to 40% off Nike & New Balance​
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we go again — another "up to 40% off athletic brands" blast, and once more I'm digging through a logo lineup to find anything that's actually mine to wear. Nike, adidas, Jordan get top billing, New Balance and Converse squeeze in the middle, and Skechers is dead last with an "and more!" tacked on — that's actually worse placement than yesterday's email where Skechers at least landed third. Then it just keeps going: a Crocs banner, a "buy any shoe get 50% off a pink sticker shoe" thing I don't understand, some cut-off banner ad, and two stacked coupon codes. It's a lot of shouting and not much that speaks to a guy who wants a cushioned slip-on and nothing more.
- My thumb's not moving on this one. If I did click anything it'd be "Find Your Store" out of habit, not because the 40% off grabbed me — the subject line put New Balance in the headline but buried it in the graphic behind three basketball logos. Not forwarding this to anybody, and I'm marking it read and moving on.

## What's working

- The "40% OFF" banner is big, red, and impossible to miss scrolling past on my phone.
- Loyalty sign-up strip up top ("Shoe PERKS") is at least visible if I were a new customer.
- Labor Day framing is timely — I know why I'm getting this email today.
- The $20/$10 mobile wallet coupon is a genuinely simple, no-nonsense discount if I were already headed to a store.

## What's weak

- Skechers, the one brand I actually track, is shoved to dead last in the logo row after Nike, adidas, Jordan, New Balance, and Converse.
- New Balance is in the subject line but you'd never know it from the hero image — it's a small logo, not featured product.
- That blue banner near the bottom is literally cut off mid-word ("LI...") — looks broken on my screen.
- Too many separate offers crammed in one email — athletic 40%, in-store 20%, Crocs $39.98, buy-one-get-a-sticker-shoe, then two more coupon codes. I don't know which one I'm supposed to act on.
- Not one actual shoe I'd wear is pictured — no walking shoe, no comfort model, just logos and a pink sticker gimmick.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero image with an actual New Balance or Skechers comfort shoe, not a five-brand logo wall — if New Balance is in the subject, put a New Balance shoe front and center.
- 2. Cut it down to one primary offer per email. Right now there are five competing asks and none of them win.
- 3. Fix that cropped banner before it goes out again — it reads as broken to anyone scrolling past it.
- 4. Give the comfort-brand shopper his own CTA — something like "Shop Comfort & Support" that skips past the basketball sneaker section entirely.
