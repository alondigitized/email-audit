# Your Skechers Plus Monthly Summary is Here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a different kind of Skechers email — they're sending me my monthly loyalty summary, which means somebody over there is at least thinking about treating me like a known customer. I open it and the big blue banner hits me right away: 1,000 points, a $5 reward sitting there with my name on it. That gets my attention. A monthly account statement from a shoe brand I actually buy from — yeah, I'm reading this one.
- But here's the thing. The moment I look at what's below that, the email sort of loses the thread. There's a "Buy Again" section, which is genuinely smart for a guy like me who finds a shoe that works and then wants to get another pair when these wear out. And then "We picked these just for you" with some sneakers I can actually look at. Clean enough. I'd probably tap VIEW MY ACCOUNT to see about that $5 reward.
- What I would NOT do is forward this to a friend. This is a personal account email, not a deal worth passing along. Mark as read after I check the reward — that's my play here.

## What's working

- **1,000 points and the $5 reward are front and center** — the hierarchy is perfect, I don't have to hunt for what I earned.
- **"Buy Again Now" is the right idea for me** — I'm exactly the guy who finds a comfortable shoe and reorders it. This feature should be featured more prominently, not buried below the summary.
- **"We picked these just for you" shows actual shoes** — product images, not stock art, not generic banners. I can at least look and see if anything catches my eye.
- **Layout is clean and readable** — no tiny text, no cluttered columns, big enough to scan on my phone without squinting.
- **"Find a Store" link and free shipping/returns reminder** — both matter to me. I like being able to walk in and try something on, and free returns take the risk out of ordering online.

## What's weak

- **That preview is broken JSON. Again. Fourteenth time by my count.** The previous email on this same day — "Stars, Stripes, and Savings" — had the same garbage in the preview slot. I open this one knowing before I even tap in that Skechers has never once fixed this. At some point I start wondering if anyone is minding the store.
- **The "JOIN NOW" button at the bottom makes no sense** — I'm in Skechers Plus. That's why I'm getting this email. Showing me a join button looks like they forgot who they're talking to.
- **No comfort language anywhere** — not a word about cushioning, arch support, or walking. That's the only reason I'm in this brand's orbit. The shoe recommendations could be anything.
- **Women, Kids, Clothing in the nav** — I scroll past that stuff without a second thought. It eats space that could've been used to show me more men's styles.
- **"Shop Now, Pay Later" with Afterpay and Klarna** — I pay for my shoes when I buy them. This is for a different customer, not me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text** — this is number one, two, and three. Fourteen consecutive sends with broken JSON in the preview. If one change lifts this program it's this: write a real preheader. "You've got a $5 reward — here's what's new for you" would outperform silent garbage every time.
- 2. **Move "Buy Again" above the fold, or make it the hero** — this feature is the most personally relevant thing in this email. For a guy who found a comfortable shoe, a one-tap reorder is compelling. Lead with it.
- 3. **Add comfort copy to the product picks** — "Arch Fit cushioning" or "memory foam insole" next to the shoe images. That's why I'm a Skechers customer; remind me.
- 4. **Remove the "JOIN NOW" button** — I'm already a member. Showing this to a Skechers Plus subscriber looks like a segmentation error.
- 5. **Filter the nav to MEN and SALE** — I don't need Women, Kids, or Clothing links. Tighten the nav to what's relevant for me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your $5 reward is ready — plus 1,000 points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Monthly round-up: your points, your picks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `You've earned a $5 reward. Here's what's new in men's comfort styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `1,000 points and counting — tap to see your personalized picks.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers Plus Monthly Summary
**From:** no-reply@emails.skechers.com | **Campaign:** TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_06302026

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a systemic HTTP/HTTPS mixed-content problem across the majority of its image assets, a staging CDN domain (`image.devemails.skechers.com`) leaking into a production send, and a broken deliverability monitoring pixel caused by a missing URL protocol.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Broken Return Path pixel — missing protocol**
```
src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499"
```
No `https://` prefix. Email clients treat this as a relative path — the request 404s. Validity/Return Path inbox monitoring for this send is non-functional.

**ink1000.com pixel loaded over HTTP**
```
src="http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=..."
```
Flagged by QA. HTTP pixel will be blocked by Outlook, Apple Mail (MPP), and most modern clients. Also missing `alt=""`.

**24 click-redirect links unverifiable**
All CTAs route through `click.emails.skechers.com` — QA correctly skipped HTTP probing. UTM parameter coverage and destination alignment cannot be confirmed from this audit (see Section 6).

**Campaign ID date mismatch**
`TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_06302026` embeds date `06302026` (June 30). Send date is July 2. Minor, but campaign attribution in analytics will be off by 2 days if this ID is used as a date key.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Staging CDN domain in production (high severity)**
```
src="https://image.devemails.skechers.com/lib/fe33157075640674761d74/m/1/summary-tier-blue-angle.png"
```
This image — which appears to be the loyalty tier badge/graphic — is served from `image.devemails.skechers.com`, not `image.emails.skechers.com`. This domain may be rate-limited, unavailable to production traffic, or firewalled. If it 404s, the tier visual breaks for all recipients.

**HTTP image assets — 12 confirmed instances**
All footer images are served over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/`:
- Skechers logo (`dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`)
- App Store badge, Google Play badge
- "Curbside Pickup", "AfterPay", "Find A Store"
- Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube icons
- `49468f73` (unidentified content image)

Apple Mail with Mail Privacy Protection, Outlook 2019+, and Gmail on Android will suppress or break HTTP images. Footer navigation and social links will render as broken image icons for a significant share of recipients.

**`@import` for Google Fonts inside `<body>` `<style>` block**
```css
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Mulish:wght@400;700;900&display=swap');
```
This is injected via a `<style>` tag inside a `<td>`, not in `<head>`. Gmail strips all `@import` rules regardless of placement. Mulish will fail to load in Gmail (web and app), falling back to the system font stack.

**Missing `alt` text — 4 confirmed**
| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| `o.gif` | `http://www.ink1000.com/...` |
| `summary-tier-blue-angle.png` | `https://image.devemails.skechers.com/...` |
| `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` | `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` |

Images-off rendering (common in Outlook corporate) will show empty boxes for these. The tier badge missing alt is especially problematic — it's presumably the main personalized visual.

**Malformed charset meta tag**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. While most clients tolerate this, it's non-compliant with the HTML spec and may cause charset detection fallback behavior in older Outlook/Lotus Notes environments.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

The preheader `"See how many points you earned last month"` appears to be static text with no merge token for the actual point value. If the intent is to show personalized point counts, the token is absent from the truncated source visible here — flagged for verification against the full template.

The `mi_u=8107766165` parameter in the ink1000.com pixel appears to be a static subscriber ID rather than a dynamic merge token, which would mean all recipients share one tracking identity for this pixel — verify in the ESP template.

Krux/Salesforce user-match pixels use `partner_uid=121173391` which also appears static — confirm this is expected behavior for this pixel type.
