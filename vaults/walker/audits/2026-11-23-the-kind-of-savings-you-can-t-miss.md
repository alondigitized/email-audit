# The Kind of Savings You Can't Miss
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again. This is the ninth one in about two weeks, and I'm worn out on it. The "60% off" hero is big and I can read it on my phone without squinting. But it's the same "up to 60% off select styles" I got on 11/21 ("UP TO 60% OFF SELECT STYLES") and again on 11/22 ("Your Cart's Getting a Big Deal"). The "Exclusions Apply" line sits right under the button, which doesn't help my trust.
- The middle of the email is the part I'd actually look at. "Classic clogs starting at $30" is a number I can do math on. The TikTok "From Your FYP" block is aimed at somebody 40 years younger than me. The kids' clogs under $25 are for a grandkid at best.
- Would I click? Probably not. I might glance at the $30 clog and move on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "$30 classic clogs" block gives me a real price I can compare against my Hoka and Brooks budget.
- The big "60% OFF" on a plain background is easy to read on a phone.
- The buttons are large, black, and easy to tap with my thumb.
- "Offer ends Sunday, November 29th" gives a real deadline, even though it's in the fine print.
- The footer is simple. Unsubscribe and Update Preferences are easy to find.

## What's weak

- "Select styles" and "Exclusions Apply" make me wonder whether the shoe I want is even included.
- The hero shoe is a chunky black platform sandal. That isn't for me, and nothing in it says cushioning or arch support.
- The TikTok/FYP copy is clearly written for younger people, and I'm not part of that crowd.
- Three of the four buttons say "Shop All Doorbusters" or something close, so I can't tell where each one takes me.
- There's nothing for men, and nothing about slip-on comfort or walking. The slippers are the closest thing, and they're shown on a woman.
- It's the same deal I've seen all week, so there's no new reason to open it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send fewer of these. Nine in two weeks is the biggest problem, and a better design won't fix it.
- 2. Put a men's comfort slip-on in the hero, with the price and "arch support" or "all-day cushion" next to it.
- 3. Name which styles are included, or link straight to them, instead of "select styles."
- 4. Give each button its own label, like "Shop $30 Classic Clogs."
- 5. Move the Sunday, Nov. 29 deadline up into the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Classic Clogs from $30 through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort clogs, $30 and up. Ends 11/29`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Retro prices on our most comfortable clog. Men's sizes in stock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 60% off, plus the $30 clog. Ends Sunday night.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is structurally sound, with `lang`, a viewport meta tag, a hidden preheader and mobile media queries. Most compliance, link and continuity checks can't be confirmed, because the source was truncated and 19 links were skipped. The automated QA run reported 0 issues and 1 warning, and the pass rate was 86%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped 19 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Their destinations, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=...&d=10210&bmt=0` is a 1×1 image with `alt=""`. It sits on the sender's own click domain, which is correct.
- **Audience pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...` is an Adobe Audience Manager event pixel. It is a third-party tracker that carries a per-recipient ID (`d_dpuid`) in the query string. Third-party domains are more likely to be blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and some clients, so open-based data from this pixel will be undercounted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA warning):** The `demdex.net/event` image has no `alt` attribute. If it is a tracking pixel, it should carry `alt=""` so screen readers don't announce the URL.
- **Meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so as written they declare nothing. The viewport and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` tags are correct, since they use `name`. This may be an artifact of how the source was captured, so check it against the raw message.
- **Fonts:** `TT Crocs` loads from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. Most clients ignore web fonts, and the inline fallback stack falls outside the truncated source, so it can't be verified.
- **Checks that pass:** `lang="en"`, the viewport tag, the Gmail and Apple data-detector overrides, and the `.mobileHide` / `.mobileView` utilities are all present. The preheader (`Up to 60% off. Hurry, this one won’t last.`) is followed by zero-width padding characters, which is standard.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the visible source. The truncation means the full body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** QA reports "Authentication-Results header not found", and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The warning says this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so it reflects the test setup and not necessarily a sender defect. Check authentication against the original message headers.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't in the truncated source or the QA data, so they can't be assessed.
- **Alignment:** The From address (`email@crocs-email.com`) and the click and open-tracking host (`click.crocs-email.com`) share the same registrable domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not assessable:** The 19 link destinations weren't probed and aren't visible in the truncated HTML. The UTM parameters, landing-page alignment with the "Up to 60% off" offer, and any redirect or 404 behavior are all unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA with the full HTML and with the redirect-resolving probe enabled for `click.crocs-email.com`, then validate the final URLs, UTMs and the "up to 60%" landing page.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex.net/event` pixel.
3. Confirm that `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` are in the raw source. If they aren't, add them.
4. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, plus the `List-Unsubscribe` headers, from the original message headers or a direct-delivery seed.
5. Confirm the footer has a working unsubscribe link and a physical address.
