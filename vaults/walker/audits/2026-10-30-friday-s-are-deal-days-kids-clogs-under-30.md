- The subject was about kids' clogs, but the kids' tile is near the bottom, so the subject and the email don't match.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based layout with media queries and Outlook, Apple and Samsung client fixes. The checks found three alt-text warnings, one malformed tracking-pixel URL and one unresolved placeholder token. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer, unsubscribe link and CTA hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **29 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so the QA tool skipped the HTTP probe. Destination resolution, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Malformed pixel URL.** The pixel `src` is `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261030_friday_deals_AM - 20261028_120211​`.
  - It has literal spaces and a trailing zero-width space (U+200B) at the end of the value.
  - It also has a nested `=` inside the `t=` value, and the parameter isn't URL-encoded. Some clients or proxies may truncate or reject this URL, which would drop open tracking.
- **Unresolved placeholder.** The second pixel is `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]`. The literal `[UNIQUE]` was not replaced. This is probably a cache-buster or recipient-ID token that the send platform failed to substitute, so it is a merge failure (see section 4).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images** (QA WARN):
  - `34e7d81c-f435-45e9-b0a4-751255703190.jpg`, from `image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/`. This is a content image, so it needs descriptive alt text. With images blocked, it renders as nothing.
  - The two `emltrk.com` tracking pixels. These should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity.
- **Malformed meta tags.**
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
  - The UTF-8 `charset` attribute is present, so the impact is minimal.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The document declares HTML 4.01 Transitional but also carries `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and self-closing `<meta ... />` tags. This is harmless in most clients but not valid.
- **Global link styling.** `a, a:link, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` removes underlines and link colour from every link. Any inline text link then looks like plain text. Whether this matters depends on body content I couldn't see.
- **Rendering fixes are present.** Viewport meta, `color-scheme: light`, `mso-table-lspace/rspace`, `-ms-text-size-adjust` and Gmail/Samsung fixes are all there. No issues found beyond the above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `d=[UNIQUE]` in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel is an unresolved token.
- No other tokens, such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`, appear in the visible source. The rest is unverifiable because of truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** The report says this is expected via the AgentMail relay. It is a test-harness limitation, not a defect in the email. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are **unverified**, not failing.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical postal address and List-Unsubscribe headers** are not visible in the truncated source or the QA data. They can't be confirmed or flagged.
- **Sender domain.** `email.shoecarnival.com` matches the image host `image.email.shoecarnival.com`, so there is no obvious from-domain and asset-domain mismatch.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only UTM data visible is in the pixel URL: `utm_campaign=SCA_20261030_friday_deals_AM - 20261028_120211`. The campaign name has spaces and a timestamp suffix, and `utm_source` and `utm_medium` are not present.
- Because the 29 tracked links were not resolved, UTM propagation and landing-page alignment with "Kids' Clogs Under $30" could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[UNIQUE]` placeholder in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel. Confirm the send platform substitutes it, and check the other sends in this campaign.
2. URL-encode the `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` pixel parameter. Remove the spaces and the U+200B character from the campaign string, and use a consistent campaign name.
3. Add descriptive alt text to `34e7d81c-f435-45e9-b0a4-751255703190.jpg`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to both tracking pixels.
4. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags, or remove them.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and with raw headers (capture from the original send, or seed a test inbox that preserves SPF/DKIM/DMARC results). That would let the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` headers, CTA hrefs and UTM parameters be checked.
6. Resolve the 29 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe (HEAD or GET) to confirm landing URLs, status codes and UTM carry-through.
