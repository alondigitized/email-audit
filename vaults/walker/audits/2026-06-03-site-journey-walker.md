# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 4/10 (prev 4/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Full review
---

## 1. Executive Summary

I came to Skechers.com looking for a comfortable hands-free slip-on — the kind of shoe I can put on without bending down, that won't destroy my feet after a few hours of walking. What I found was a site that looked like it was built for a 25-year-old running a half marathon. The homepage hit me with "AERO BURST" in flashing colors and a guy sprinting in a field. The menu navigation was actually decent — Hands Free Slip-ins right there, easy to tap. The product page was readable. But the cart loaded completely blank, and when I searched "comfort shoes," every result it showed me was labeled Women's. I never felt like this site knew who I was or what I needed. Couldn't even tell it recognized me as a loyalty member — I don't think I was ever truly logged in.

---

## 2. Business Impact Score: 4/10

**No change from yesterday.** A blank cart is a critical failure that directly kills conversions. The search results showing women's shoes to a man who typed "comfort shoes" is an ongoing insult to basic relevance. These two problems alone would make me close the tab and check DSW. The navigation menu is genuinely good — that's the bright spot — but you can't buy a shoe through a menu.

---

## 3. What's Working

- **Men's navigation menu (Step 4)** is the best screen in the whole journey. Large touch targets, clean list, "Hands Free Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," and "Extra Wide Fit" all visible without scrolling. A 62-year-old with thick fingers can tap those confidently.
- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** is persistent across every page. It's in my face from start to finish — I notice it, I understand it, I like it. That deal framing works on me.
- **Product name and price on the PDP (Step 6)** are large and readable. "$88.00" and "Buy 1, Get 1 50% OFF" — I don't need to squint.
- **Size selector tiles (Step 7)** are reasonably sized. I can tap a size without accidentally hitting the wrong one. The "Please select a size" error is clear and direct.
- **294 results for Hands Free Slip-ins (Step 5)** — that's a deep catalog and the page title says exactly what it is.

---

## 4. What's Weak

- **Cart is completely blank (Step 8).** I added the shoe, went to the cart, and got a white screen. Nothing. If this happened to me for real, I'm done. I don't call customer service. I go to Hoka.com.
- **I was never actually logged in.** Step 3 was supposed to show a login, but the screenshot shows the same homepage with the "Login" prompt still visible in the promo banner. Every single subsequent page continues prompting me to log in. The loyalty messaging — "Member Month! Earn 2X Points thru 6/13" — is always there but always aimed at a stranger. Never at me by name.
- **Homepage hero is completely irrelevant to me (Steps 1–2).** AERO BURST with athletic 20-somethings sprinting. I'm 62. I want to walk to the pharmacy without my arches screaming. This homepage has zero for me above the fold.
- **Search for "comfort shoes" returns Women's results first (Step 9).** I typed "comfort shoes" on my phone. The site knows I've been browsing men's shoes the whole session. It shows me two women's products as the lead results out of 1,467. That's not a minor miss — that's the site not paying attention to me at all.
- **Zero reviews on the product (Step 6).** "No Reviews" under the product name is a trust killer for me. I don't buy without reading what other people say. At $88 I need to hear from someone with bad knees or wide feet that this shoe held up.
- **No hands-free or arch support copy visible in the product description viewport.** The most important thing I care about — can I get into this shoe without sitting down? — isn't visible on the product page without significant scrolling. I might never get there.
- **Horizontal category scroll bar (Steps 1–3)** cuts off mid-word. I can see "Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs, G" — the "G" is cut. I don't know what's there. Older users don't instinctively swipe those bars.

---

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart.** This is P0. A blank cart page is a broken purchase funnel. Nothing else matters until this works.
2. **Use session context in search.** I browsed Men's the entire visit. "Comfort shoes" search should default to Men's, not a gender-mixed grab bag. A simple filter pre-applied based on my navigation history would have fixed this.
3. **Confirm login state visually.** Show my name or "Hi, Walker" somewhere in the header when I'm logged in. The promo bar should swap from "Login" to my points balance the moment I authenticate. Right now I can't tell if the login worked.
4. **Front-load comfort language on the PDP.** Within the first scroll on the product page, I need to see: Hands-Free, Contour Foam cushioning, arch support details. Move that above the fold, not buried below size selection.
5. **Get reviews on new products.** Even 5–10 reviews changes the conversion math for a buyer like me. Seed reviews or add a "Be the first to review" prompt with an incentive.
6. **Replace the hero for returning comfort-segment visitors.** If the site knows I've clicked Slip-ins and Arch Fit before, show me a hero that speaks to that — not a performance athlete campaign.

---

## 6. Bottom Line

The bones are here. The navigation menu is good, the deal is prominent, and they have the right products for me. But a blank cart is a conversion killer, the search is ignoring who I am, and the homepage looks like it doesn't know I exist. Until the cart is fixed and the site stops showing me women's shoes when I search, I'm not buying. **4/10 — no change from yesterday.**

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

Site runs on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware). TTFB is CDN-fast on most pages but View Cart shows a 4-5x spike that directly correlates with a 429 on `/cart/`. The Kasada bot-protection SDK is firing repeated rate-limited requests throughout the session (6 of 9 console errors). View Cart renders without a `<title>` or `lang` attribute — structural HTML failures, not styling. LCP and CLS are unsampled across all steps; incomplete vitals data limits performance conclusions.

---

## Accessibility

**17 violations. Two pages have critical structural failures.**

**Homepage (10 violations)**
- `aria-required-parent` [critical] — ARIA role used on element outside its required container. Screen reader tree is broken in that subtree.
- `button-name` [critical] — One or more `<button>` elements have no accessible text (no label, aria-label, or visible text). Keyboard/AT users cannot identify these controls.
- `link-name` [serious] — One or more `<a>` elements with no discernible text (likely icon-only links with no aria-label).
- `heading-order` [moderate] — Heading levels skip (e.g., h1 → h3), breaking document outline.
- `landmark-main-is-top-level` / `landmark-no-duplicate-main` / `landmark-unique` [moderate] — Multiple `<main>` landmarks or nested landmarks; indicates partial-page injection (Monetate/GTM snippets likely inserting fragment HTML into the DOM without landmark context).
- `region` [moderate] — Content outside any landmark region; AT users navigating by landmark will miss it.

**View Cart (4 violations — highest severity per page)**
- `document-title` [serious] — `<title>` element is empty or absent. This is a cart page with no document title, which also fails basic SEO.
- `html-has-lang` [serious] — `<html>` element has no `lang` attribute on this page, despite `lang="en"` being present on all other sampled pages. Strong signal of a separate template or a server-side rendering path that omits the attribute.
- `landmark-one-main` / `page-has-heading-one` [moderate] — No `<main>` landmark and no `<h1>`. Cart is likely rendered as a modal overlay or injected fragment without full page scaffolding.

**Search (3 violations)**
- Duplicate/nested complementary landmarks and content outside regions — same class of issue as Homepage, likely shared template.

---

## SEO

- **View Cart missing `<title>`** — crawlers and social previewers will index a blank title for `/cart/`. If Googlebot ever crawls a shared or cached cart URL, it has no title signal.
- **View Cart missing `lang="en"`** — affects language-targeted indexing and hreflang resolution.
- **GTM tag `G-1234567890`** appears in the `<head>` script block across all sampled pages. This is a well-known placeholder/test GA4 Measurement ID. Either it is intentional (a dummy tag in GTM) or a misconfigured property ID — either way it fires real network requests to Google's servers with no valid destination.
- **Duplicate third-party scripts** on Men category: `tags.creativecdn.com/dhGobFICKSkk6J9nvQPw.js` is injected twice; `s.yimg.com/wi/ytc.js` is injected twice on Homepage and Log In. Duplicate pixel fires corrupt attribution data.

View Cart's 140ms TTFB is 4-5x the baseline. The concurrent 429 on `/cart/` indicates Kasada is blocking or throttling the request — the server returns a rate-limit response before serving the page, forcing a retry cycle that adds ~100ms.

**LCP and CLS: not sampled.** Without these, no statement can be made about Core Web Vitals pass/fail for CrUX or Search Console ranking signals.

**Third-party script volume (counted from HTML source)**

20+ distinct third-party script origins loaded on every page: GTM (4 separate tag IDs), TikTok Pixel (3 files), Monetate (2 files), Yahoo/Verizon (2 duplicate calls), Pinterest, Reddit, QuantumMetric, Impact, Creative CDN, Bloomreach, Bing, Snapchat, Stape CDN, Facebook, Nextdoor, Taboola, PulseInsights, Tune/Go2SDK, WebFontLoader, Kasada. All are `async` but each is a separate TCP connection + parse/execute cost on low-end mobile hardware.

**WebFontLoader** (`cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/webfont/1.6.28`) loaded async — Mulish font classes (`wf-mulish-n*-active`) are applied by JS after load, creating potential FOUT. Version 1.6.28 is the last release (2017); the library is unmaintained.

**Floating-point precision bug** in mobile viewport calculation: `--vh: 6.640000000000001px` — minor but indicates raw JS division result written directly to the CSS custom property without `toFixed()`.

---

## Mobile Optimization

- `--vh` custom property pattern is correct in intent (workaround for mobile browser chrome height), but the floating-point leak (`6.640000000000001px`) should be `parseFloat(...toFixed(2))` before assignment.
- No `preconnect` hints observed in the sampled `<head>` — only `dns-prefetch` for two Monetate origins. With 20+ third-party origins, missing `preconnect` on critical render-path resources (fonts, above-fold images) costs meaningful RTT on mobile.
- No LCP or CLS data means we cannot confirm image lazy-loading correctness or layout stability on mobile viewports.

## Console & Network Errors

**Kasada bot-protection rate limiting (6× 429)**
```
https://www.skechers.com/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/2d206a39-.../fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.443
```
The Kasada SDK (`x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.443`) is firing fingerprint requests that are being rate-limited by Kasada's own infrastructure. This means the bot-protection challenge loop is not resolving cleanly — the client is retrying without backing off. During real user sessions this creates redundant network requests; during automated audit tooling it triggers hard blocking. The 429 on `/cart/` is the downstream consequence: Kasada blocked the cart page request itself.

**Cart page 429**
```
429 https://www.skechers.com/cart/
```
The cart page returned 429 to the test client. This directly explains the 140ms TTFB spike. A real user in a flagged session (VPN, unusual UA, rapid navigation) would see the same failure. Cart is a conversion-critical page — rate-limiting it without a graceful fallback is a revenue risk.

**Stylitics CORS block**
```
Access to CSS at 'https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css?...' blocked by CORS policy: No 'Access-Control-Allow-Origin' header
```
Stylitics CDN is not returning a CORS header for this origin. The outfit/complete-the-look widget CSS will not load, breaking the widget silently. This is a Stylitics CDN configuration issue that Skechers needs to raise with their Stylitics account team — the `style.css` endpoint needs `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://www.skechers.com` (or wildcard).

**DoubleClick 400**
```
400 https://ad.doubleclick.net/ccm/s/collect?auid=977051431...
```
Malformed or expired `auid` parameter in the CM360 pixel. Attribution data for this impression is lost. Likely a stale tag version in GTM.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix View Cart HTML shell** — add `<title>` and `lang="en"` to the cart page template. These are P0 — they are structural document requirements, not enhancements.

2. **Investigate Kasada rate-limit loop** — coordinate with Kasada support on why `fp` endpoint returns 429 repeatedly in the same session without the client receiving a valid challenge token. Implement exponential backoff in the SDK config if available, and ensure `/cart/` is excluded from hard-blocking rules for authenticated users.

3. **Remove or fix `G-1234567890` GA4 tag** — audit GTM container for placeholder/test tags firing in production.

4. **Deduplicate script injections** — `creativecdn` and `ytc.js` firing twice inflates pixel event counts and wastes bandwidth. Audit GTM triggers for firing conditions that cause double-fire on page load.

5. **Resolve Stylitics CORS** — file a ticket with Stylitics to add `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` to `web-assets.stylitics.com`. Until resolved, the complete-the-look widget is broken for all users.

6. **Fix `button-name` and `link-name` (critical/serious a11y)** — add `aria-label` or visible text to unlabeled interactive elements. These block screen reader users from operating the page.

7. **Sample LCP and CLS** — re-run audit with a tool that captures Web Vitals (Lighthouse, WebPageTest, or field data from CrUX). Without LCP/CLS, Core Web Vitals status is unknown.

8. **Fix `--vh` floating point** — `style.setProperty('--vh', parseFloat((window.innerHeight * 0.01).toFixed(2)) + 'px')`.

9. **Add `preconnect` for critical origins** — at minimum, the font CDN and above-fold image CDN should have `<link rel="preconnect">` in `<head>`.
