# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Same cart nag again, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. "Ready to check out?" is the fourth or fifth version of this since the 10/19 send. The 10/23 "Your Items Are Waiting!" and the earlier "Did you forget something?" were the same idea. The hero shoe is a women's sneaker with a painted skull on it. Nobody I know would call that something I left behind.
- The 20% off for Plus members is the only part that talks to me. It sits about two-thirds of the way down, under a pile of shoes that mostly aren't mine. The 10/22 "Your weekend just got better!" put that same offer right at the top with "Hi, Walker" and my points. This one buries it. One man's boot, one kid's sneaker and four women's styles isn't a cart. It's a catalog.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the next one with a real offer.

## What's working

- The 20% off box is clear: "20% OFF" in big white type on dark blue, with the $5 reward on $75+.
- I know the Skechers name, and the black logo at the top looks the same as always.
- The Men's Slip-ins John Deere boot and the Contour Foam sneaker are at least things I might wear.
- The text is big and the buttons are wide, so I can tap them on my phone.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) is simple and easy to read.

## What's weak

- "Ready to check out?" and "Complete your order" are false. I never started an order, so it feels like they're watching me.
- The hero is a women's skull-painted sneaker. It's seasonal, but it has nothing to do with me.
- The grid is four women's shoes, a kid's Garage sneaker and two men's styles. Only two of the six are for a man my age.
- The 20% off is buried below the products and below a second "Complete your order" button.
- There's no "Hi, Walker" and no mention of my points, even though the 10/22 emails had both.
- The fine print is a long block of tiny type. The offer says "Valid thru 10/26" and also "Plus members only," and I have to read down to find the conditions.
- Nothing here mentions arch support, cushioning or comparing prices, which is how I decide on a shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the member offer and my name. Put "Hi, Walker, 20% off for Plus members through 10/26" and my points at the top, and drop the cart story unless there's really a cart.
- 2. Use a men's slip-in comfort shoe as the hero, such as the Contour Foam or a GO WALK Slip-in, and say what it does: arch support, cushioning, hands-free.
- 3. Cut the grid to three men's styles with a price or a "now $X with 20% off" line.
- 4. Send the cart reminder only to people who actually have something in a cart. Everyone else should get a different template.
- 5. Add one short line of social proof, such as a rating or "Podiatrist-friendly arch support."
- 6. Move the fine print into a short "Details" line and put the full terms behind a link.
