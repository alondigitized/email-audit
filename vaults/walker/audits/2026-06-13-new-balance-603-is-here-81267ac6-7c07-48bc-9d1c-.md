# New Balance 603 is here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- New Balance is in my regular rotation — I've had NB walking shoes that outlasted half my wardrobe, and "New Balance 603" in the subject line made me slow down instead of swipe. Famous Footwear knows I pay attention to comfort brands, and naming the specific model is exactly the right call. No riddles, no emojis, no hype — just the shoe and the drop. That part they nailed.
- Before I even opened it, though, my inbox served me a wall of random letters and a web address where the preview text should be. I've seen enough phishing emails to know what that looks like, and this looked exactly like that. Famous Footwear has enough goodwill with me to survive the suspicion, but barely. The June 8th "Final 72 hrs" email had the same preview disaster, and they apparently still haven't fixed it.
- Inside, the Rewards Member 20% off banner is right at the top, the SHOP NOW button is unmissable, and I can actually see multiple 603 colorways without hunting. I'd click. But I'm also annoyed — Famous already dropped a 20% off email in my inbox earlier today, and now here comes a second. At some point the offer stops feeling special and starts feeling like noise. I'd click, but I'd think twice about whether I need to hurry.

- Subject line does one job perfectly — brand, model number, and "is here." I know what I'm opening before I tap it.
- Rewards Member Exclusive 20% off is right at the top; no scrolling required to find the offer.
- Multiple 603 colorways in the grid give me real browsing options without forcing a click just to see the selection.
- SHOP NOW button is clean and impossible to miss — no guessing which tap takes me to the shoe.
- Body layout is uncluttered and familiar — Famous Footwear emails follow a consistent pattern I've learned to navigate.

## What's weak

- Preview text in my inbox was a raw tracking URL — looked exactly like a phishing attempt. This is the same bug from the June 8th send and it still isn't fixed.
- The hero model looks like a guy in his mid-30s wearing a backward cap and trendy streetwear. Famous Footwear knows my loyalty history; the shoe is in my lane but the creative isn't.
- Two 20% off emails before noon is cadence fatigue. The offer loses urgency when it showed up once already today.
- A "30% off" section lower in the email competes with the member exclusive up top — now I'm doing math instead of clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text** — a raw tracking URL there is unacceptable, especially after it burned the June 8th send too. This kills opens before the subject even gets credit.
- 2. **Add comfort copy to the hero** — "all-day cushioning" or "the 603's supportive build" closes the gap between "shoe I like" and "shoe I need right now."
- 3. **One offer, one story** — 20% off Rewards Member Exclusive is the headline. The 30% off section below it muddies the message and makes me wonder if I'm getting the best deal.
- 4. **Pull back the cadence** — sending two 20% off emails on the same day trains me to wait and see what shows up next, not to act.
- 5. **Age the creative** — swap or add a model who reads closer to 60, or drop the fashion model entirely and lead with a comfort-first shoe shot.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — 2 HTTP image sources** (will be blocked in clients enforcing HTTPS):
```
http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5MDZ9AAcAAAAAAqeV4v7yzaQkZd...

```
These appear to be Salesforce Marketing Cloud LiveContent (dynamic/personalized) image blocks. Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail all block mixed HTTP content, silently dropping the image.

**26 images missing `alt` text** — includes the logo (`3b6a7dc1-...png`), divider bars (`4ccff4ad-...jpg` appears 4×), product images, and social icons. Images rendered with `alt=""` provide no fallback for screen readers or image-off rendering.

**Malformed charset meta tag:**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute. Correct form:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```

**CSS typo — invalid property `lineapacing`:**
```html
style="...lineapacing:20px;..."
```
Found on a content `<td>` in the header region. Property is silently ignored; intended value (`line-height` or `letter-spacing`) is not applied.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) violation; also fails Apple App Store and Google Play accessibility guidelines for embedded webviews.
