# 20% off dressy fall styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and it's the same HAYRIDE 20% code I've been getting all week. After "Inside: 20% off & must-have tall boots" earlier today, I'm tired of it. The green "20% OFF*" banner is big and easy to read on my phone, and I like that. Then the whole body is high heels, a evening clutch and a wedge sandal. Nothing in it is for me.
- The "Shop Men" link is up top under the banner, but the hero and everything after it is women's dressy shoes. I see no slip-ons, no cushioned walkers, and no mention of arch support. The only men's shoe is a small brown sneaker in the "More styles" row, labeled "Women's Shoes." I'd mark this read and move on. I wouldn't forward it to anyone except maybe my wife, and she gets her own DSW emails.

## What's working

- The big white "20% OFF*" on green is readable without my readers.
- The code HAYRIDE is right there, plus a "Claim 20% Off" button, so I know what to do.
- "Shop Women / Men / Kids" links sit right under the banner, so I can skip to men's.
- The "Shop by Size" block with wide width and wide calf buttons is useful. I need wide widths.
- The buttons are large black bars that are easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The hero is stilettos and handbags. A 62-year-old guy has no reason to read past it.
- The "*" and the fine print are at the bottom, buried under a long block of legal text. It says "regular-price styles only" and lists brand exclusions, and I have to scroll a long way to see which brands are left out.
- It's the fifth or sixth 20%-or-BOGO note in a week, and nothing about it feels new.
- The Shoebox Shuffle game is a gimmick. I won't play a game to win a gift card, and it pushes the shoes down the page.
- There is nothing on comfort, cushioning or arch support anywhere in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Anyone who has browsed or bought men's shoes should see men's comfort, not heels.
- 2. Move the exclusions up. Put "Excludes Skechers, Hoka, Brooks and other brands" in one line under the banner.
- 3. Cut the frequency. Send one 20% reminder, not a new version of the same offer every day.
- 4. Put comfort picks in the "More styles" row, such as slip-on walkers and wide-width cushioned sneakers.
- 5. Move the Shoebox Shuffle section below the Shop by Size block.

Only 12 of 66 links were probed, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **HTTP 400**.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/1rtSWSAkqSROYd1qbiDV4A~~/AAQRxRA~/lt6d502x…`
  - It is a long, single-use-style tracking token on the `/f/a/` path.
  - A 400 from the redirector suggests a malformed or expired token. It could also be the probe being rejected rather than a real user failure.
  - Treat it as a defect until a manual click test shows otherwise.
  - The QA output shows an empty label before the link-text field, so the link's anchor text or title may be blank in that check. I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Unprobed links:** 54 of 66 links were skipped (footer, utility and social). Their health is unverified.
- **Redirect dependency:** Every link goes through `ablink.dsw.com`, so a redirector outage would break all CTAs at once.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/GOoUeS8jTOcHndKhAa9ewQ~~/AAQRxRA~/6V13QnjAC5P2b3q8dUjJGlcX-S-ixcuOP1N73Ovi9JOjoDhJpq…`
