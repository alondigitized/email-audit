# Shop the best gifts before everyone else
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's another adidas email, and this one is about as plain as they come. There's a big photo of three young folks on a bench in matching sweats. A headline says "GET AHEAD ON GIFTING" and three black buttons sit below it. I'm 62, and nothing here speaks to me. I do see Samba-style sneakers in the photo, with the tan gum sole at the bottom left. But they're on a young woman's feet, and the cushioning isn't mentioned.
- It's also the third gifting email in a few days. On the 5th it was "Gifts they'll wear beyond the holidays," and earlier today it was "Step up your gifting game with Samba." This one says "before everyone else," but I've heard that three times now. Nothing is on sale, there's no percentage and no free shipping, and no actual product is named. "Cozy matching sets" is for somebody else's grandkids.
- Would I click? Probably not. Maybe "Shop Men" if I were gift shopping, but I'd land on a general page and have to dig. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The adidas logo is up top and the sender is a name I know, so I trust it's real.
- The text is big and easy to read on my phone, with no squinting.
- The three buttons are large, black and well spaced. My thumb won't miss them.
- The layout is simple and clean, with nothing broken or overlapping.
- Splitting it into Men, Women and Kids at least lets me skip straight to my section.

## What's weak

- There's no offer at all. No dollar amount, no percent, no free shipping, no deadline.
- The shoes are a tiny part of the photo. The picture is mostly sweatpants and hoodies on young people.
- "Cozy matching sets, essential accessories, and iconic sneakers" is vague. I can't tell what I'd be buying.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning or arch support, which is what I shop for.
- The black banner at the top, "Explore the latest adidas best sellers," is a second "Shop Now" that competes with the three below. I don't know which one to tap.
- It's the third gifting message this week, so it feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real product in the hero, with its name and price, and show the whole shoe. A cushioned men's walking or lifestyle shoe would beat a group photo of sweats.
- 2. Give me a reason to shop early. Say something like "free shipping on gifts" or "extended returns through January." Right now "before everyone else" doesn't tell me what I get.
- 3. Cut the extra top banner, or make it match the main button, so there's one clear path.
- 4. Make the Men button more specific, like "Shop men's sneakers," so I know where it goes.
- 5. Space out the gifting sends and make each one different.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gift shoes with free returns through January`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Samba and Gazelle gifts, shipped free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order early, and returns are free until January 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's sneakers, cozy sets and gifts under $75.`
