# Hey friend, start your week with THESE 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- "Hey friend" from a store I've never met in person. I opened it on my phone and got the same orange "BUY MORE, SAVE MORE" coupon block I've seen all week, including 10/10 and 10/11. It's $10 off $74.98 or $25 off $149.98, code SAVEMORE. This is at least the seventh Shoe Carnival email in a row, so I'm tired of it.
- Below that is a young woman in black boots lying on the floor, and "Start your week on the right foot!" That's a cute line, but the picture is for somebody 40 years younger than me. The rest is a grid of women's boots, kids' clogs, girls' sneakers and a few work shoes. I did spot a Skechers slip-resistant work shoe and a pair of Dockers, but nothing says cushioned, arch-supported or slip-on.
- I wouldn't forward this to a friend. I might tap through to see the Skechers work shoe, then back out. Mostly I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The coupon block is big and easy to read, even on my phone without zooming. The $10 and $25 numbers jump out.
- The "SHOE PERKS" bar at the top and the $10 reward box at the bottom remind me I'm a member.
- The barcodes and "Add coupon to your mobile wallet" buttons are handy. I can show the cashier my phone in the store.
- "Sneakers Under $75" is a plain, honest section header. It tells me what I'll find.
- Nothing looked broken. The images all loaded and the text didn't overlap.

## What's weak

- "Hey friend" feels fake. A real friend would know I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants comfort shoes.
- Most of the grid is women's and kids' shoes. That's women's boots, Crocs kids' clogs, girls' Steve Madden clogs and girls' Skechers light-ups. I'm not shopping for any of that.
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support, slip-ons or walking. I can't tell which shoes are comfortable.
- The tap targets are small text links like "Shop Boots & Clogs" and "Shop for Less." I'd want big buttons.
- The fine print is tiny and long, and it lists a lot of excluded brands. I'd worry the $25 deal only works if I spend $150.
- This is the same coupon block again. It's the fourth or fifth day running, and the offer isn't new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's comfort hero, such as a Skechers, Hoka or Brooks slip-on on a man my age. Keep the coupon underneath it, not above it.
- 2. Add a short line to each product: "Slip-on," "Memory foam" or "Arch support." Put size and width availability on the card.
- 3. Use big, full-width buttons like "Shop Men's Slip-Ons" instead of small underlined links.
- 4. Send me fewer emails, or at least change the creative from day to day. Use the order history to send me men's comfort shoes instead of "Hey friend."
- 5. State the end date in large type next to the coupon, not in the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's slip-on comfort picks + $10 off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Easy-on, all-day cushioned shoes — $25 off $150`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers, Dockers and more men's walking shoes. Show your coupon in store or online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code SAVEMORE through 10/31 — comfort brands included.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a responsive, table-based layout with reasonable client-compatibility CSS. The automated QA run found 0 failures and 1 warning (88% pass). Two tracking-pixel images have problems: one URL contains an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder, and one carries a stray zero-width character. The HTML source was truncated and no raw headers were supplied, so the checks below cover only what was provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 60 tracking links were skipped, so none were checked for status codes or destinations. They are all on click-redirect domains, which suggests every body link is wrapped.
- **Stray zero-width character:** the first pixel URL is `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261012_start_your_week_off_AM​`. It ends with an invisible zero-width space after `AM`. Depending on how the tracker parses `t=`, the campaign value may be recorded as `..._AM%E2%80%8B`, which would split reporting.
- **Malformed `t=` value:** the value is `utm_campaign=...`, so the query string has a nested `=` inside `t=`. This may be intentional for the ESP, but it isn't standard UTM syntax.
- **Unresolved placeholder:** the second pixel URL is `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]`. The literal `[UNIQUE]` was not replaced at send time. If it is rendered this way, the per-recipient open or click attribution on that pixel is lost.
- **Plain-text part:** the plain-text alternative is 11,810 of 14,481 characters URLs (about 82%), above the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk and a poor text-only experience, caused by long tracking URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** both `emltrk.com` pixel `<img>` tags have no `alt` attribute. For 1×1 pixels, `alt=""` is the correct fix, since a missing attribute can make screen readers announce the file name or URL.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing. Both are invalid as written.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the `<html>` tag uses `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and self-closing `/>` syntax. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Link styling:** the global rule `a, a:link, a:visited ... { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important }` removes underline and color from all links. Any text link without separate styling (for example, a wrapper `<span>` or button styling) has no visual affordance. This is an accessibility risk under WCAG 1.4.1.
- **Small text:** `.responsive-text` is set to 12px at ≤600px and 14px above, so it is smaller on mobile than on desktop. 12px is below the usual 14px mobile minimum.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` is locked to `light`. Clients that force-invert, such as Gmail on iOS, may ignore this.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, the viewport meta is present, and Apple and Samsung auto-link fixes are included.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed issue:** the unresolved `[UNIQUE]` token in the `0di86k1v` pixel URL (see section 2).
- **Greeting:** the visible greeting, "Hey friend", is generic. This is expected for a fallback, but I can't tell from the truncated source whether a first-name token with a fallback exists elsewhere.
- **Other tokens:** none were visible in the supplied HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Cannot be confirmed:** the footer, physical address, unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results were not in the truncated HTML or the QA output. No violations are confirmed, but none are verified either.
- **Sender:** the From address is `shoecarnival@email.shoecarnival.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, which is good practice.
- **Tracking-domain alignment:** `emltrk.com` is a third-party tracking domain. Its alignment with the sending domain was not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs seen:** the only UTM data in the supplied evidence is `utm_campaign=SCA_20261012_start_your_week_off_AM`, and it appears inside the pixel URL, not on a destination link. The campaign name is well structured, with a date and daypart.
- **Not checked:** `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_content` were not visible. With all 60 links skipped, the final landing URLs, UTM persistence through the redirect, and landing-page alignment could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[UNIQUE]` token in the `0di86k1v` pixel. Confirm the ESP merge syntax resolves it, and add a pre-send check that fails on literal `[...]` or `{{...}}` in any URL.
2. Strip the zero-width space from the `utm_campaign` value in the template or campaign-name field. Normalize campaign names to ASCII.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Restore a visible link treatment, such as an underline on text links, or confirm every link is a button or has other affordance.
6. Raise mobile `.responsive-text` to at least 14px.
7. Shorten the plain-text part by trimming tracking-URL length or using shorter redirect slugs, to bring it below 70% URLs.
8. Re-run the audit with full HTML and raw headers so the footer, unsubscribe headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC can be verified. Also resolve a sample of the tracked links to check final URLs and UTMs.
