# Join VIP, get access to 30% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. After a week of UGG and Nike, this one finally changes the subject. The giant black "30% OFF*" on the pink banner is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. "Black Friday starts…NOW!" at least feels like news. It isn't another boot send.
- Then I scrolled and it got muddy. The headline says 30% off "(almost) everything." The button under it says "BECOME A VIP." So I'm not getting the deal yet, I'm being asked to sign up first. The shoe grid is mostly women's boots and a loafer, with a couple of sneakers that might work for a guy. There's nothing about cushioning or arch support, and no slip-on picks aimed at me.
- Would I click? Maybe "Shop Men" if I had a few minutes, since a flat 30% is real money. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and leave it for the next one.

## What's working

- The huge "30% OFF*" is readable on a phone with no zooming.
- "Black Friday starts…NOW!" gives me a real reason to look today.
- The code "BFDEALS" is stated plainly, so I know how to redeem it.
- "Shop Men" is a visible link in the hero, and there's a men's tab in the nav.
- Shop by size, wide width and wide calf buttons are big and easy to tap.
- Free shipping for VIPs and in-store pickup are listed clearly.
- The barcode at the bottom is handy if I go into a store.

## What's weak

- The "BECOME A VIP" button contradicts the "30% off everything" headline. It feels like a membership hoop.
- The "So new, so you" grid is mostly women's boots and a loafer, so it doesn't feel aimed at a 62-year-old man.
- There's no comfort messaging at all: no cushioning, arch support or slip-on.
- The grid's "Up to 50% off," "Up to 25% off" and "30% early access" labels are tiny and confusing next to the hero's flat 30%.
- The offer fine print is a dense wall of small type. It says brands like Skechers are excluded, and that's a brand I shop.
- The scrolling banner at the top is cut off ("THE SEASON'S BIGGEST…"), and the logo tagline overlaps itself a bit.
- This is another DSW send in a very crowded week, including three UGG sends and a Nike one just in the past few days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA match the headline. Use "SHOP 30% OFF NOW" and put VIP signup in a smaller line underneath.
- 2. Add a men's comfort row with slip-on and walking shoes from Skechers, Hoka and Brooks if they're not excluded, with real prices.
- 3. Put the exclusions right under the hero in plain words instead of burying them.
- 4. Pick one discount number for the grid and use it consistently.
- 5. Cut the send frequency. One strong Black Friday email beats five similar ones.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email fails 2 of 4 automated checks. The SMS opt-in CTA ("Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off") returns HTTP 400, and a raw `%%gender%%` merge token was left unrendered. The HTML source is truncated, so findings on footer, compliance and landing pages are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The link "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns **400**.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/SLtNmttsgHMhYhFpHqhVKw~~/AAQRxRA~/FME9ItP0…`
  - Its tracking token is unusually long, and the QA check reports empty link text next to the URL. A 400 on a click-tracking redirect usually means a malformed or truncated encoded payload, or the redirect rejecting the probe request. The probe cannot tell these apart, so verify with a manual click-through.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 76 links were probed and 64 were skipped, so the other links are unverified. This is a coverage limit, not a defect.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261120_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` is an open-tracking pixel. Its `mi_u=225090073` parameter carries a per-recipient ID.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `o.gif` from `mi.dsw.com` is a tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/Dkv0Itz-9t5QIvjL1ypvPg~~/AAQRxRA~/rIz1OC_RLv-dBL91…` has no alt text. It is routed through the tracking domain, so it may also be a pixel. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration is valid. The charset declaration is the one that matters, because some clients may fall back to a different encoding.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Raw token (FAIL):** `%%gender%%` appears unrendered in the output. It is not Braze Liquid (`{{ }}`) syntax, so it may be a leftover from another platform's template. The truncated source doesn't show where it sits. If it is inside a URL or visible copy, it will break the link or show to the recipient. If it is in a comment or hidden block, impact is low. Confirm its location in the full HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified (WARN):** No `Authentication-Results` header was found. The QA notes this is expected for the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a measurement gap, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Plain-text bloat (WARN):** 26,360 of 31,178 characters (about 85%) of the plain-text part are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and can raise spam-filter scores. The cause is long encoded `ablink.dsw.com` tracking URLs.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** Neither is visible in the truncated source. The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure for it, but I can't confirm presence of the unsubscribe link, the `List-Unsubscribe` header or the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** Links use `ablink.dsw.com` click-tracking wrappers. The final destinations and any UTM parameters are not visible in the source or QA output. The only campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261120_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` on the pixel, which is truncated.
- **SMS opt-in destination:** This link returned 400, so it can't be checked for landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or replace the SMS opt-in link.** Re-test the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/SLtNmttsgHMh…` URL manually. If it fails for real users, regenerate the tracking link, since this CTA drives the "15% Off" offer.
2. **Find and resolve `%%gender%%`.** Locate it in the template and remove it or map it to the correct merge syntax. Add a pre-send check that fails on `%%…%%` patterns.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Correct the meta tags** to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
5. **Set `alt=""`** on tracking pixels, and give any content image a meaningful alt.
6. **Shorten or simplify the plain-text part**, for example with shorter link wrappers or fewer tracked links, to bring URL share below 70%.
7. **Verify authentication and compliance outside AgentMail.** Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` on the original message. Confirm the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` header and postal address in the full HTML.
