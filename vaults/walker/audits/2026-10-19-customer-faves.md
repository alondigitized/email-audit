## What's working

- "Fan favorites" in huge type is easy to read on my phone.
- The Shana quote has five stars and a real job title. It reads like a person, not an ad.
- The Skyward Laceless is shown in men's and women's, and that slip-on style fits how I like to shop.
- The black "Shop Faves" and "Shop Bondi SR" buttons are wide and easy to tap.
- The layout is clean, with plenty of white space and a simple two-by-two grid of shoes.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the grid tells me about cushioning, arch support or walking. It's just shoe names with no detail or price.
- "Free Expedited Shipping*" is the only offer. It only applies if I sign up for texts and place my first full-price order, so it isn't really for me.
- The shipping banner shows a runner splashing through water in red shoes. That looks like a younger trail runner, not a 62-year-old walking the dog.
- Bondi SR is a work shoe, and the quote comes from a hospital worker. It's cushioned, but it isn't framed for walking, and the email never says so.
- The small fine print at the very bottom is hard to read, and the "Get Free Shipping" button is small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the comfort proof into the hero. Under "Fan favorites," add one line that quotes a walker about cushioning or arch support, and put a price on every shoe.
- 2. Add a short "Why they love it" line under each shoe in the grid, such as "slip-on," "all-day cushion" or "good for walking." Right now I only get a name.
- 3. Make the offer clear and put it above the fold. If free shipping needs a text signup, say so in one plain sentence, not behind an asterisk.
- 4. Show a man closer to my age in the shipping banner, walking, not splashing through water.
- 5. Space out the sends. This is the sixth in nine days, and a "best of" email would land better after a quiet week.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links all route through a Cordial redirect on `e.emails.hoka.com` with consistent UTMs, but the QA run found no plain-text part and no alt text on 11 content images. Every probed link returned 403 or 406. That is most likely the ESP rejecting the probe's user agent, not a user-facing outage, but it is unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 of 11 probed redirect links failed.** Ten returned 406 (the logo, five product tiles, the `best-sellers/` banners a, b, c, d and e, and `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`). The Unsubscribe link returned 403. All are on `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…/<hash>?jwtH…&jwtP…&jwtS…`.
  - A 406 or 403 on a signed JWT redirect usually means bot or user-agent filtering, not a dead link. The QA data can't tell the two apart.
  - The JWT payload decodes to valid destinations, for example `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/best-sellers/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…` and `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`.
  - Verify manually with a real browser UA. The unsubscribe 403 is the one to confirm first.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 14 links were skipped, so the footer, social and utility links are unverified.
- **No `utm_content` or per-tile differentiator.** Banners a, b, c and d all resolve to the same `/en/us/best-sellers/` URL with identical UTMs, so click reports can't separate them.
- **The destination URL carries `hmail=<64-hex>&cordial_id=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9`.** Both are per-recipient identifiers passed to the landing page. I can't tell from the source whether the site uses them.
- **Tracking pixels are on two domains.** They are `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`, and neither has alt text. This is a minor point.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 content images have no alt attribute**, including the hero GIF `US_20261019_CustomerFavorites_a.gif` and the images used as links (`US_BondiSR_TopLeft_1110521-VMM.jpg`, `US_CliftonPRO_TopRight_1176510-WBLC.jpg`, `US_SkywardLaceless_*`). The footer `LogoFooterPrimary.png` and the two tracking pixels are also missing alt.
  - Each linked image is therefore a link with no accessible name.
  - With images blocked, the offer, products and CTAs would be invisible. The QA tool reports their link text as `[image: filename]`.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta … content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` caps zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. A second viewport meta is also present, with `user-scalable=yes` and `maximum-scale=1`. The duplicate tags are redundant and contradict each other.
- **The charset meta is malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block.**
  - `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}`
  - `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`
  - These look like unrendered template values. They are ignored by clients but indicate a template variable that never resolved.
- **`* {outline:none}` is set globally.** This removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Web fonts load from hoka.com** (`TheFutureHOKA*.woff2`). Gmail and Outlook ignore them, so the fallback stack should be checked. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm the stack.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` tokens are visible in the supplied source. The only unrendered values are the empty `px` CSS values noted above. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Plain-text part is missing.** The QA tool reports "Text version is 0 chars". A multipart/alternative with no text part hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- **Unsubscribe link exists** but returned 403 on probe (see section 2). I can't confirm it works from a real client.
- **The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't supplied**, so one-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.** `Authentication-Results` wasn't present because the message came via an AgentMail relay. This is a limitation of the capture, not a defect in the email.
- **The physical postal address wasn't visible** in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.
- The From domain `emails.hoka.com` matches the tracking subdomains `e.` and `mg.emails.hoka.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTMs are consistent on every decoded link:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261019_CustomerFavorites_STO_A60Day_180Purchasers_270BestSellersTag_60OptIn_AIPurchasePropensityHigh`.
- **`utm_source` holds a program name** (`US_Broadcast`), not a platform. `utm_campaign` embeds segmentation logic (`180Purchasers`, `AIPurchasePropensityHigh`) in a URL the recipient can see. This is a hygiene note, not a defect.
- **Product deep links carry variant parameters**, for example `dwvar_1110521_color=VMM`. The final Bondi SR link also sets `dwvar_1110521_size=7&dwvar_1110521_width=Regular`.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.** Every redirect failed the probe, so I couldn't check for 404s, out-of-stock SKUs or selector pre-selection. Check these by hand: Bondi SR `1110521-VMM`, Clifton PRO `1176510-WBLC`, Skyward Laceless `1168877-MBLW` and `1168876-CYCS`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the 12 links with a browser-like UA, or whitelist the QA probe. Confirm the unsubscribe flow end to end first.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 11 content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and any decorative images. Link-wrapped images need meaningful alt.
3. Generate a plain-text part, so the message goes out as multipart/alternative.
4. Set a real `<title>`, fix the charset meta (`<meta charset="utf-8">` or `http-equiv`), and remove the duplicate viewport meta. Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Fix the template variables behind the empty `px` CSS values in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border`.
6. Remove or scope `* {outline:none}` so keyboard focus stays visible.
7. Add `utm_content` per tile or banner so the duplicate best-sellers links can be told apart.
8. Capture raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) in the QA pipeline. The AgentMail relay currently hides SPF, DKIM and one-click unsubscribe status.
