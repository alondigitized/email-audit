# BOGO 1/2 off is here!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Famous Footwear again — I feel like I should just set up a folding chair in this inbox at this point. Same BOGO ½ off banner I've seen with different color trim, same "2X points" kicker, same everything. The one thing that did catch my eye scrolling down was "Up to 30% off Nike" — that's a real, specific number and Nike's a brand I actually trust for walking shoes, so my thumb paused there for a second.
- Past that, it's a wall of stuff that isn't for me — backpacks, lunch boxes, a HydroJug hydration flask, kids' sneakers, a "back-to-school lookbook." I don't have kids in school, I don't need a Nike dad-cap water jug, and none of this tells me a single thing about arch support or all-day cushioning, which is the only reason I'd actually buy a shoe. I'd skim past the top half entirely, maybe tap on the Nike section out of habit, then mark it read. Not forwarding this to anybody — there's nothing here worth sharing.

## What's working

- The purple BOGO block up top is bold and easy to read even without my glasses.
- "Up to 30% off Nike" with the swoosh is a clean, specific offer that actually made me stop scrolling.
- "Prices as marked" under the Nike CTAs is a small honesty touch — no bait-and-switch feeling there.
- The Famous Rewards $10 reward block is at least member-relevant if I've signed up.

## What's weak

- This is back-to-school themed top to bottom — sandals, backpacks, kids' shoes, lunch boxes. I'm 62, that ship sailed decades ago.
- No comfort or fit language anywhere — nothing about cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease, none of what actually gets me to buy a shoe.
- The hero product shot is a kid's sneaker on a school locker background, not a men's casual or walking shoe.
- HydroJug tumblers and coolers in the middle of a shoe email is just noise to me — I don't know why that's here.
- Same BOGO + points formula I've seen from them a half dozen times this month already, just re-skinned.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Nike 30% off block instead of burying it two-thirds down — that's the only section with real crossover appeal for a guy like me, put men's comfort styles front and center there.
- 2. Cut back-to-school entirely for segments without school-age kids — swap in men's walking/comfort shoes and drop the backpacks and lunch boxes.
- 3. Add one line of actual product substance — cushioning, arch support, slip-on — instead of just "Cool for school."
- 4. Space out the send cadence. Six-plus BOGO emails this month is wearing me down to the point I stop opening at all.
