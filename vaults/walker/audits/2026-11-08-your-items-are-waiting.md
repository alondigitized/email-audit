# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth cart nag in about two weeks, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. "We noticed you noticing us" is a little creepy, and it's the same skull sneaker as the 11/05 sends. I've never looked at that shoe. Down at the bottom there's a real offer, but the top of the email doesn't lead with it.
- The "more great options" grid did catch my eye, because it has Slip-ins and Arch Fit boots and slippers. That's my kind of shoe. Still, half of it is women's styles, and I'm a 62-year-old man. I wouldn't click the cart button, but I might tap a men's slipper. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The grid has men's Slip-ins, a waterproof boot and a Murette slipper. That's the hands-free, cushioned stuff I look for.
- "Arch Fit" and "Relaxed Fit" are in the product names, so I can see what the shoe does without tapping.
- The 20% off Skechers Plus banner is big, dark and easy to read on my phone. It shows a clear deadline of 11/10.
- The Women / Men / Kids / SALE list is big, simple and easy to tap with my thumb.
- Afterpay and Klarna, curbside pickup and a store finder are all there if I want them.

## What's weak

- "Your cart is still waiting" is false for me, and I've said so for two weeks now. It erodes my trust.
- The skull sneaker is a poor hero for me. Nothing about it says comfort, and it's the same one as before.
- The 20% off is below the fold. The cart button leads, and the discount that would actually move me is buried.
- The product names are in tiny print, and there are no prices or star ratings anywhere.
- The grid is mixed, with three women's items out of six. The email doesn't act like it knows me.
- The member discount is the same 20% as the 11/05 "Holiday checklist" email. Nothing here is new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer. Put the 20% member price at the top, and show the shoes after it.
- 2. Stop sending cart reminders to people with no cart. Send a "back in stock" or "popular with walkers" email for what I actually browsed.
- 3. Show men's Slip-ins and Arch Fit first, with prices, a "was/now" price and star ratings.
- 4. Make the product names bigger and add "Shop men's Slip-ins" as a button.
- 5. Space out the cart emails so I get fewer of them.
