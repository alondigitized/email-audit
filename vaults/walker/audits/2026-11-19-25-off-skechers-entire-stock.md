# 25% OFF SKECHERS ENTIRE STOCK
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Now this one got my attention. Shoe Carnival again, but this time it's Skechers, and Skechers is a shoe I actually wear. The big red "25% OFF" under the Skechers logo is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. For once I know right away what's on offer.
- The products fit me better than most of their sends this week. Hands Free Slip-ins and Max Cushioning are the two things I look for. I did notice the models, though. The Slip-ins guy is a younger fellow, probably in his 30s, and there's no one my age. The email also goes on far too long. There's a Black Friday banner, a $10/$25 coupon, barcodes, Word Stride, SMS, Afterpay and a Perks reward before I get to the footer. This is the same HOLIDAY coupon I've seen since the 13th.
- Would I click? Yes, probably on Slip-ins or Max Cushioning. Would I forward it? Maybe to my brother-in-law, who also wears Skechers. The rest I'd scroll past.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF" under the Skechers logo is huge and clear, and I can read it without zooming.
- The Slip-ins tile is exactly what I shop for: hands-free and easy to put on.
- The Max Cushioning tile speaks to comfort, which is what I buy on.
- The tiles are big, with clear "Shop Slip-ins" and "Shop Max Cushioning" links I can tap.
- It's a recognizable brand pairing. Skechers is a name I trust, and the Shoe Carnival header is clean.

## What's weak

- No model looks like me. The Slip-ins guy is young, and the other tiles show a woman, a girl in a Santa-style scene, and kids.
- The Glide-Step, Work and Kids tiles dilute the message for a man shopping for himself. I'd have liked a men's casual or walking tile.
- The email is very long. The Black Friday 60% banner, the coupon and the barcodes stack up below the Skechers deal, and it feels like a different sale.
- The asterisk on "25% OFF" and the tiny fine print make me wonder about exclusions. I can't read that print on my phone.
- It's the fifth or sixth send this week. The $10/$25 HOLIDAY coupon is the same one from the 13th and 16th.
- The inbox preview text is just a tracking link, which looks sloppy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down. Lead with the Skechers 25%, show four men's and unisex comfort tiles, and drop the Word Stride, SMS and Afterpay boxes.
- 2. Add a men's walking or casual tile with an older model, and show a price or a star rating on each.
- 3. Put the sale end date up near the hero, and state the exclusions in plain larger text.
- 4. Fix the preheader so it isn't a raw link.
- 5. Drop the repeated coupon or give it its own send, because I've seen it all week.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "25% OFF SKECHERS ENTIRE STOCK" (Shoe Carnival)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has sound responsive scaffolding (viewport meta, `lang="en"`, media queries at 660/450/320px). Two content images lack alt text, one tracking pixel contains an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder, and the link layer could not be verified because the HTML was truncated and all 30 tracking links were skipped. The QA pass rate was 86% with 0 issues and 1 warning.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on all 30 links because they are click-redirect domains. Final landing URLs, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Unresolved placeholder in a pixel:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` contains the literal string `[UNIQUE]`. This looks like a merge or cache-buster token the ESP did not substitute. If it is not replaced at send time, the open pixel is not unique per recipient or message.
- **Malformed pixel query string:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261119_skechers_AM​`
  - The UTM is passed inside a `t=` value as `t=utm_campaign=...`, with a nested `=` that is not URL-encoded.
  - The string ends with a zero-width character (U+200B) after `_AM`, visible as `AM​`. That character can corrupt the campaign value in downstream analytics.
- **Two tracking pixels:** One pixel per ESP or tracker is normal, but confirm both are intentional, since duplicates inflate open counts.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `…/m/1/23a03225-7c93-49fa-988c-8821675cac1b.jpg`
  - `…/m/1/fe4e293c-bccf-488a-bbf7-db06bf5745de.jpg`
  
  With images blocked or a screen reader in use, these render as nothing or as a filename.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The `ieezzy5m` and `0di86k1v` pixels have no alt. For decorative pixels this should be `alt=""` rather than absent, and `width="1" height="1"` should be set.
- **Link affordance removed globally:** The CSS rule `a, a:link, a:visited, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` removes underline and color from every anchor. Any inline text link is therefore indistinguishable from body text unless it is styled some other way, such as a button or image. The truncated source does not show whether it is.
- **Invalid or no-op meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` is missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Declaring `charset` alone is acceptable, but the `content` attribute is redundant.
- **Doctype and namespace mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Small mobile text:** `.responsive-text` is forced to `font-size: 12px !important` at ≤600px, which is below the common 14px minimum for mobile body text.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are locked to `light`. This is intentional but means no dark-mode adaptation.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Unresolved token confirmed in one URL: `?d=[UNIQUE]` (see section 2).
- No other merge-token leakage (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `*|…|*`) is visible in the truncated source. The remainder could not be inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** QA reports no `Authentication-Results` header, expected because the message came through the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unverifiable** from this data and is not a confirmed defect. The sender domain `email.shoecarnival.com` and the image and tracker subdomains are consistent with an ESP-aligned setup.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so presence is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** Not provided in the QA data, so unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only campaign identifier visible is `utm_campaign=SCA_20261119_skechers_AM`, and it appears only inside the tracking pixel.
- UTM parameters on the 30 CTA and product links (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_content`), their landing pages and the match with the "25% OFF SKECHERS ENTIRE STOCK" offer could not be verified because the probes were skipped. The data does not confirm consistency.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the two content images (`23a03225…jpg`, `fe4e293c…jpg`). If they are purely decorative, use `alt=""`.
2. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` to both tracking pixels (`ieezzy5m.emltrk.com`, `0di86k1v.emltrk.com`).
3. Fix the `?d=[UNIQUE]` placeholder in the `0di86k1v` pixel. Confirm in a live send that it resolves to a per-recipient value.
4. Correct the `ieezzy5m` pixel URL. Remove the trailing U+200B and URL-encode or restructure the nested `t=utm_campaign=…` parameter.
5. Resolve the 30 redirect links manually or with a redirect-following probe. Verify the final landing URLs, UTM parameters and HTTP status.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML plus raw headers. Confirm unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
7. Restore a visible link style (underline or color) for inline text links. Raise the mobile `.responsive-text` size from 12px to at least 14px.
8. Clean up the meta tags: remove or fix `<meta content="IE=edge">`, and align the doctype with the `xmlns` declaration.
