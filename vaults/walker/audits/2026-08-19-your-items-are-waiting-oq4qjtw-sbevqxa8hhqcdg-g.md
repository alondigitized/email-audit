# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again — that same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sneaker, front and center under "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." with a big "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button underneath it. I noticed nothing. I never clicked that shoe, never sized it, never came within a mile of a cart. That's the fourth or fifth time this week I've gotten some version of this exact "your cart's waiting" email with the exact same women's floral shoe, and it's getting to be background noise at this point.
- The one bright spot is scrolling further down — there's a men's slip-on section (Altman - Murdock and an Arch Fit) mixed into the "more options" grid, and the 40% off summer styles banner near the bottom is a real, legible offer. But it's buried under a fake cart nudge and a wall of unrelated kids' and boys' sneakers that have nothing to do with me.
- I'm not clicking the "Complete Your Order" button since there's nothing to complete, and I'm not forwarding this to anybody. If I scroll long enough I might tap into the 40% off sale, but honestly at this point I'm more likely to just mark it read and move on — same as I did with the last three of these.

## What's working

- The 40% off summer styles banner near the bottom is a clean, specific offer with a clear "SHOP THE SALE" link.
- Men's slip-ins (Altman - Murdock, Arch Fit Arcade) show up in the "more options" row — at least somebody's algorithm knows I wear men's slip-ons.
- Layout's clean and text is big enough to read without zooming on my phone.
- Skechers name and logo are right up top, so I know who it's from before I even open it.

## What's weak

- Same phantom cart, same pink floral shoe I've never touched — this is now a running joke in my inbox, not a nudge.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is flatly wrong — there is no order to complete, which makes me trust the sender less, not more.
- Half the "more options" grid is boys' sneakers and basketball shoes — I'm 62, not shopping for my grandkid or pickup games.
- The real offer (40% off) is buried below a fake urgency section and a product grid that doesn't match me at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the fake-cart framing entirely — I've gotten this same "your cart is waiting" pitch with the identical shoe five times this week; it's actively burning trust. Lead with the 40% off sale instead, since that's the one real thing in here.
- 2. Swap the hero product for something in my actual browsing history — men's slip-ins or walking shoes — not a floral women's D'Lites I've never looked at.
- 3. Cut the boys'/kids' sneakers from my "more options" row; if there's no real personalization data, just show me best-selling men's comfort shoes instead.
- 4. Move the 40% off banner up near the top so it's the first thing I see, not something I have to scroll past three irrelevant sections to find.
