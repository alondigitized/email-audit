# FINAL DAY: Birkenstock Up to 25% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Now this one actually put a man in the picture — guy in a golf cart with his Arizonas kicked up on the dash, "GOES FURTHER" splashed next to him. That's the first Shoe Carnival email in weeks that bothered to show me someone who looks like me instead of just the wife's New Balances and the kids' sneakers. Birkenstock is a real comfort brand too, not some fashion label they're trying to talk me into.
- That said, the "TRUE COMFORT" hero shot is a young woman in skinny jeans carrying a grocery bag, and 25% off Birkenstocks doesn't automatically mean arch support — some of those cork footbeds are flat as a pancake, and I'd want to know if the specific style is the contoured kind before I click anywhere near "Shop Men's."
- I'd open this one on the strength of the subject and I'd probably tap through to the men's section just to look, but I'm not forwarding it to anybody and I'm not buying today. Twenty-some days of daily Shoe Carnival mail has worn a groove in me — this is a decent one, but it's still just Tuesday's email with a red banner slapped on.

## What's working

- A man actually shows up in this email — golf cart, Arizonas up, "GOES FURTHER" — first time in a while I've felt like they remembered I exist.
- The offer's right up top and easy to read: "LIMITED TIME! BIRKENSTOCK SALE, UP TO 25% OFF" in a big red block, no hunting for it.
- Birkenstock is a name I know and trust for comfort, so the brand itself isn't a hard sell.
- "Shop Men's" is its own button, separate from the women's and kids' sections, so I'm not stuck scrolling past stuff I don't need.

## What's weak

- "Up to 25%" and "select styles" — I've been burned by that before, half the time the shoe I actually want isn't in the sale.
- No mention of arch support or which Birkenstock styles qualify — some are flat, some aren't, and that's the whole reason I'd buy this brand.
- The Shoe Perks "2X-3X points" bubble and the "$10 Reward" banner at the bottom are cluttering up what should be a simple "here's the shoe, here's the price" pitch.
- Still no actual price shown anywhere — just a percentage. At 62 I want to see the dollar number, not do math in my head.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the men's section higher up — right under the hero banner, not buried after the women's shot — and put actual before/after prices on the Arizona style shown.
- 2. Say plainly whether the sale styles include the contoured/arch-support Birkenstocks, since that's the whole reason a guy my age reaches for this brand.
- 3. Drop or shrink the Shoe Perks points bubble — it's competing with the sale banner for my eye.
- 4. Fix the preview text — right now it's just a tracking link dumping into my inbox, not real copy.
