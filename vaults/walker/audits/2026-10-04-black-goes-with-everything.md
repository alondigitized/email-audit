- The big black Speedgoat is the first thing I see. There's no hunting for the shoe this time.
- "GORE-TEX waterproof protection" tells me something useful for wet weather. Walking in the rain with wet socks is no fun.
- The four-shoe grid is easy to read. It's labeled Men and Women, with large photos and plain names.
- The Clifton 11 GTX is a shoe I know. It's the cushioned one in the bunch.
- The buttons are big, white-on-black or black-on-white, and easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- "Dark mode activated" is tech talk. It tells me nothing about the shoe.
- No prices anywhere. I can't do my price-vs-quality math, and that's how I decide.
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support, or easy on and off. Two of the four shoes are hiking boots with laces.

- The small gray fine print at the bottom is hard to read without zooming.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from Cordial-hosted, image-heavy markup with a structurally sound table layout, but it ships with no plain-text part, at least 13 content images without alt text, and a few head and CSS defects. Two tracked links returned 403 to the QA prober, including the unsubscribe link. The prober is probably being bot-blocked, so I treat that as unverified rather than confirmed broken.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 403.** The URL is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:.../32c79d6f?jwtH=...`. Its decoded JWT payload `linkUrl` points to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&...`.
  - The 403 comes from the tracking redirector `e.emails.hoka.com`, not from the destination. The other 10 probed links apparently passed through the same domain.
  - This is consistent with WAF or bot filtering of the QA user agent. I can't confirm a real recipient failure from the data.
  - Manually verify with a real browser UA, since a broken unsubscribe is a compliance risk.
- **`return-start` link returned 403.** The link text is the raw URL "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/", and the decoded destination is `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&...`. It has the same likely cause as above.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 27 links were probed, and 15 were skipped (footer and social links). Their status is unknown.
- **Tracking pixels.** There are two open-tracking images: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`. A second tracking domain (`mg.emails.hoka.com`) is a different host from the click domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 15 images.**
  - 13 are content images on `images.usw2.cordial.com/1664/`: `US_20261004_GoreTex_a.jpg` through `_g.jpg`, the four product tiles (`US_Speedgoat7HikeGTX_TopLeft_1176671-BKLB.jpg`, etc.), `US_20260701_NewFESBanner.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - The large 1200px-wide slices (e.g. 1200x1400, 1200x1592) likely carry the message as image text. With images blocked, the email would be blank or unreadable to screen reader users.
  - The two tracking pixels also lack alt. They should have `alt=""`, and this is low severity.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid charset meta.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing.
- **Duplicate viewport metas.** The second one adds `user-scalable=yes`, but both set `maximum-scale=1`, which restricts pinch-zoom.
- **Invalid CSS with empty values.** These are `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`. Clients ignore them, but they point to a template bug.
- **Focus outlines removed.** The global `* {outline:none;}` and `img {display:block !important}` rules remove keyboard focus indication.
- **Link colour.** The `#000001` and `#fffffe` link colours are the usual Outlook/dark-mode workaround, so I don't flag them.
- **Web fonts.** They load from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...`. I can't see the fallback stack in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML was truncated, so tokens further down aren't covered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL, confirmed).** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and is a spam-filter negative signal.
- **Unsubscribe link is present** in the footer (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`), but see the 403 caveat in section 2.
- **Not verifiable.** The data includes no `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, no SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and no visible physical postal address in the truncated source.
- **Sender alignment.** The From domain is `emails.hoka.com`, matching the click and pixel domains, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present** on the decoded destinations: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261004_GoreTex_FES_STO_A90Day_720GTXPurchasers_365FESTag_270BlackShoesTag_AIAOVHigh`.
- **No `utm_content` or `utm_term`.** Link-level attribution (hero vs. product tile vs. footer) is impossible from UTMs alone.
- **Segmentation leaks into the URL.** The `utm_campaign` string exposes audience logic (`720GTXPurchasers`, `365FESTag`, `270BlackShoesTag`, `AIAOVHigh`) to anyone who reads the destination URL.
- **Identifiers in destination URLs.** `hmail=9153f33f...` (a hashed email) and `cordial_id=...` are appended to the destination URL and persist in the recipient's browser history and in site analytics.
- **Landing page for the probed link.** `/return-start/` is a returns page. It is a utility link and not aligned with the campaign's product theme, but it appears to be the footer-style link. I couldn't verify landing pages for the product tiles.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify the unsubscribe and `return-start` links manually with a real browser UA, and allowlist the QA prober in the WAF on `e.emails.hoka.com`.
2. Add a multipart plain-text alternative. It is currently 0 chars.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to all 13 content images (especially the 1200px slices that carry the message). Add `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels.
4. Fix the head. Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, populate `<title>`, remove the duplicate viewport meta, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove or fix the empty-value CSS (`padding-top:px`, `border-top:px solid`), and restore focus outlines on links.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, and the postal address in the full HTML.
7. Add `utm_content` per link. Move audience-segment tags out of the visible `utm_campaign`, for example into a Cordial-side field.
