# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom, "READY TO CHECK OUT?" front and center like it's some big reveal. That is not, has never been, and will never be my cart. That's at least the seventh time this exact shoe has shown up in my inbox this week, and I'm not sure who Skechers thinks is reading these, but it isn't a 62-year-old guy who wears an 11 wide. Scroll past that mess, though, and there's actually something I'd look at — a men's slip-in, a 20% off code on "Cozy Fit & Glide Step," and a decent lineup of best sellers with real men's styles mixed in.
- The good stuff is buried under a cart-recovery gimmick that doesn't even apply to me. I'm not clicking the top of this email, but I might scroll down to that 20% off banner if I'm already killing time on the couch. Not forwarding this to anybody — there's nothing here worth sharing, and I'd feel silly forwarding a pink floral sneaker ad to my buddy John.
- Bottom line: skim it, maybe tap the 20% off, then archive. This one earns a shrug, not a delete-on-sight, but Skechers is burning goodwill running the same fake-cart trick on me daily.

## What's working

- The 20% off "Cozy Fit & Glide Step" banner with a real code (UPGRADE) is the kind of concrete offer that gets my attention.
- The best-sellers grid actually has men's slip-ins and a basketball shoe I could see myself considering, so there's real product variety once you get past the header.
- "Shop Now, Pay Later" and curbside pickup icons are practical touches — I don't use Afterpay, but I know people who do.
- Clear category navigation at the bottom (Men, Women, Kids, Sale) — easy enough to find my aisle if I bother scrolling that far.

## What's weak

- That pink floral D'Lites "cart" is not mine and hasn't been all week — I said this the last six times, and it's exhausting seeing it dressed up as "READY TO CHECK OUT?" again.
- The whole top third of the email — the big gold banner, the shoe, the orange "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button — is wasted space built around a false premise.
- No men's-focused hero image anywhere near the top, which is what actually would've stopped my thumb.
- The real offer, 20% off, is buried more than halfway down — most guys my age won't scroll that far before giving up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending me "abandoned cart" emails for a shoe I never put in a cart — lead with the 20% off deal instead, since that's a real, honest offer that applies to everyone, not a manufactured urgency gimmick.
- 2. Put a men's product up top — something like the Relaxed Fit Murdock slip-in — instead of the floral D'Lites, so the hero image actually matches who's opening the email.
- 3. Move the 20% off banner to the very top, right under the logo, so it's visible without scrolling.
- 4. Cut the fake "complete your order" framing entirely for subscribers who never had an active cart — segment your lists.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code UPGRADE at checkout, online only through 8/31`
