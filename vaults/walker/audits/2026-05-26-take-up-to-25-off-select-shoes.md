# Take up to 25% off select shoes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Alright, here we go again. New Balance, Memorial Day weekend, 25% off select shoes and 30% off select clothing. Same sale they've been pushing on me for weeks — I scored the May 16 "Don't forget" send an 8 because at least it was direct. This one's basically the same offer but with a code (MEMORIAL DAY26) and now they've added insoles to the bottom. The hero image is a stack of empty New Balance shoe boxes next to a shopping bag — *again*. That's the third or fourth time I've seen packaging instead of shoes. I'd rather see the shoe.
- The good news: I scroll down and there's an insole feature at the bottom — 25% off all insoles. Now THAT got my attention. I'm 62, my arches don't fix themselves, and an insole at 25% off is something I'd actually click. Problem is they buried it under a wall of empty cardboard.
- Would I click? Probably the insole "Shop now" button, yeah. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read? Mostly skim and move on if the insole wasn't there.

## What's working

- Insole section at the bottom — that's the most relevant thing in here for a guy my age, and the cutaway photo of the insole interior is actually informative.
- "Shop the sale" with three big tappable tiles (Men/Women/Kids) is easy on my thumb and easy on my eyes.
- The offer is concrete — 25% off shoes, 30% off clothing, promo code visible, end date stated.
- Free shipping over $99 banner up top is the kind of thing I look for before I commit.

## What's weak

- The hero is *empty shoe boxes and a paper bag*. I want to buy a shoe, show me a shoe.
- Same 25% off Memorial Day sale they've been recycling for two weeks straight — the inbox fatigue is real.
- "More to explore" shows socks, kids' shoes, and a kids' bungee-lace shoe. None of that is for me. Where are the men's walkers?
- Promo code "MEMORIALDAY26" is rendered small and gray under the headline — for a 62-year-old reading on a phone, that needs to be bigger and bolder.
- The insole hero is buried below three other sections. That's the one thing I'd actually buy and I almost missed it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the insoles or with a men's walking shoe on foot.** The cardboard hero is the third repeat — kill it. A real product is more persuasive than packaging.
- 2. **Make the promo code huge.** MEMORIALDAY26 should be a button-sized chip, not gray fine print.
- 3. **Segment the "More to explore" grid by who's opening.** A 62-year-old man does not need kids' bungee-lace shoes shown to him.
- 4. **Mention arch support or cushioning by name** in the insole copy — "25% off all insoles" is fine, "25% off arch-support insoles" makes me click.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use MEMORIALDAY26 — free shipping over $99. Ends 5/25.`
