# The sneakers on your list are selling out fast!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. It's the same pink banner and giant "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th, and now there's a countdown clock and "79,662 people are already shopping." The subject says sneakers are selling out. The email never shows me which sneakers or what's running low. I scrolled looking for something I'd wear and found one New Balance running shoe at $69.99 near the bottom. The rest is young folks in Converse and fashion-forward kicks.
- The "ONE-STOP SHOP" block and the gift-guide buttons are aimed at someone buying for the people on their list. I'm 62 and I want a cushioned walking shoe, not a gift. The shop-by-size row with a wide width button is the one thing that made me nod. Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF headline is huge and the code BFDEALS is right there. I know the deal in one glance.
- "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" under the size grid are things I'd actually use. Someone is thinking about real feet.
- The New Balance shoe at $69.99 is a real product with a real price, and New Balance is on my cross-shop list.
- The size buttons are big and easy to tap with my thumb.

## What's weak

- The subject promised "selling out fast" sneakers, but nothing shows which ones or how many are left. It's just pressure with nothing behind it.
- The hero shows Converse and trendy styles, and the models are young. Nothing here says cushioned, arch-supported or slip-on to me.
- It's the ninth or tenth near-identical send since the 20th. The countdown and "79,662 people" feel like more shouting, not new information.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny gray text. The asterisk on "30% OFF*" says "(almost) everything," so I don't trust it.
- Two freebies are teased, but they sit way down the page: a sherpa weekender and a tote. The tote needs $75 and the sherpa needs $99. I'm not buying a duffel bag for a pair of shoes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the actual sneakers that are selling out, with a "low stock" tag. If the subject makes a promise, the hero should keep it.
- 2. Add a comfort lane near the top. Walking and slip-on shoes, cushioned brands like Hoka, Brooks and New Balance, men's styles in larger type.
- 3. Pull the gift-giving language and the youth styling back for this sender. Show some people my age.
- 4. Cut the repeat cadence. Fewer sends with a real change in each one would feel less like noise.
- 5. Move the fine print into a short, readable "Details" block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walking shoes, 30% off — sizes going fast`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wide widths + cushioned sneakers, 30% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Balance, Brooks and Skechers in your size. Code BFDEALS.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort picks from $69.99. Free tote with $75 purchase.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's one probed failure is a 400 on the SMS opt-in CTA's tracked link. It also has a malformed `<meta>` charset tag, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and three images without alt text. Only 12 of 77 links were probed, and the HTML source was truncated, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL, HTTP 400):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZL05eXEng9MJWs7Q…` (Braze click-tracking wrapper). The probe reported an empty status label after "400".
  - A 400 from a Braze wrapper may be a probe artifact, such as an over-long token or rejection of the automated client. It could also mean the token is malformed or expired. The QA data can't tell which, so re-test in a real client before treating it as confirmed breakage.
  - It is the only failure among the 12 probed links, and it sits on a conversion CTA.
- **Wrapped image link:** The image `iupZ-dV76rka30q7q8r-…` is served through `ablink.dsw.com/q/…`, so it goes through the tracking redirector. It was not reported as failing.
- **Coverage gap:** 65 of 77 links were skipped by the click-likelihood ranking, so the other links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/ps7K70JjhGSlx7LBte2MPg~~/…` (wrapped image).
  - `https://braze-images.com/.../6afc8a14936f600094a1fa75/original.jpg` (content image).
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…` (Movable Ink tracking pixel). An empty `alt=""` is correct for this one, so it is low severity.
- **Malformed meta tags in `<head>`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. Charset then depends on the HTTP/MIME `Content-Type` header.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Custom fonts:** The `@font-face` rules point to `braze-images.com` `.woff`/`.ttf` files. Support for these is limited in many email clients. The fallback font stack is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags and a `prefers-color-scheme` block are present. No defects are visible in the portion shown.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid/merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA output. The Movable Ink pixel carries a user ID (`mi_u=225090073`) and a campaign ID (`mi_ecmp=20261127_P261762_V1_W-_Offer_Value`), which look correctly populated. The truncated source limits this check.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool says this is expected through the AgentMail relay, so it is a tooling limitation and not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Plain-text part:** 24,752 of 31,811 characters (about 78%) are URLs, above the 70% threshold. The long Braze-wrapped links inflate the text part, which is a spam-filter risk.
- **Unsubscribe / physical address:** The footer is not in the truncated source. QA always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure on it. `List-Unsubscribe` headers and the postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Opaque destinations:** Every probed link goes through `ablink.dsw.com`, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be checked from the HTML or QA data.
- **Campaign ID:** The only visible campaign identifier is the Movable Ink `mi_ecmp` value above.
- **SMS opt-in CTA:** Its 400 means the "15% off" path may not reach the opt-in page, so continuity is unverified for that CTA.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZL05eXEng9MJWs7Q…`) in a real browser and mail client. If it fails there too, fix the Braze link template or regenerate the token.
2. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the wrapped image and `original.jpg`. Set `alt=""` explicitly on the `o.gif` pixel.
5. Shorten the plain-text part, or strip tracking parameters from its links, to get below 70% URLs.
6. Run a full-coverage link check on the 65 skipped links and confirm UTMs survive the Braze redirects.
7. Capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) from a direct-delivery test to verify authentication and one-click unsubscribe.
