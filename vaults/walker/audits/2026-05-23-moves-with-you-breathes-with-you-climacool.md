# Moves with you, breathes with you: Climacool
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I'm losing count of how many adidas emails I've opened this month — it's somewhere north of eight or nine by now — and when I saw "Climacool" in the subject, I'll admit I perked up slightly. Breathable, lightweight, moves with you? That sounds like it could be a walking shoe I'd actually put on my feet. But what I got when I opened it was a young person doing yoga-adjacent gymnastics, a FIFA World Cup ticket sweepstakes in the banner, and a section inviting me to track my nutrition with MyFitnessPal. I'm 62. I track my sodium, not my macros.
- The Climacool section has some real copy — "light, soft, and breathable from every angle" — and that's the closest this email gets to my world. But there's no price, no CTA button tied to Climacool itself, and no mention of what kind of shoe this even is. Is it a runner? A trainer? A casual walker? I genuinely don't know. And the World Cup sweepstakes banner at the top has nothing to do with footwear at all — it's pulling the eye in the wrong direction before I even get to the product.
- I'd mark this as read and move on. Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward it. If you want to reach me, show me a cushioned walking shoe with a price tag on it.

- **The model is not me.** Young, flexible, doing a pose I couldn't do in 1990. adidas keeps showing me the same demographic and it keeps reminding me this stuff probably isn't designed with my feet in mind.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — adidas Climacool Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One click-tracking redirect is returning a malformed relative URL, producing a broken destination for at least one CTA. The email also has no plain-text alternative, which is both a deliverability risk and a CAN-SPAM compliance gap.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken redirect — click-tracking layer returning invalid URL**
The QA probe of the open-tracking/redirect endpoint:
```
https://dv.adidas.com/o/3a1e0220-2c7b-41a0-9a03-fc6a07c9d775?cp_tp=v3.eJzj...
```
resolves to an "unknown url type" with path:
```
/g/3a1e0220-2c7b-41a0-9a03-fc6a07c9d775?txn=1a19a980-565e-11f1-bd3a-6e9b95041fa3
```
The redirect layer is emitting a relative path (`/g/...`) instead of an absolute destination URL. The `/g/` vs `/o/` path change suggests a routing or rewrite fault in the ESP's click-tracking infrastructure. Any recipient clicking the affected CTA lands on an error.

**[WARN] 24 tracking links unverified** — all click-redirect domains were skipped by the probe. Destination health of the remaining CTAs is unknown.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] Missing `http-equiv` on charset meta**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Should be:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Without `http-equiv`, some legacy clients (Outlook 2007–2013) may misinterpret character encoding.

**[WARN] HTTP image sources — may be blocked**
The arrow image `b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is loaded four times over plain HTTP:
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
```
The open/click tracking pixel is also HTTP:
```
http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019e5303cae9-...
```
Clients with mixed-content blocking (Gmail web, Apple Mail with enhanced tracking protection) will suppress these. HTTPS equivalents should be used.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on multiple images**
- `b89f1065-48c3-4e59-9035-701fd3a63f68.png` — no `alt`
- Open-pixel image `3a1e0220-2c7b-41a0-9a03-fc6a07c9d775` — no `alt` (acceptable for tracking pixels if `alt=""` is explicit, but it must be present)
- Tracking pixel at `click.comms.adidas.com` — no `alt`

Screen readers will announce the filename for images without `alt`, degrading accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1).

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` declarations**
`AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are each declared twice across two separate `<style>` blocks with identical `src: local(...)` fallback chains. No web font URL is ever fetched — the custom fonts will never render, making the declarations effectively dead weight. This is harmless but adds ~2 KB to the HTML payload for no gain.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved `{{token}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`, or similar merge placeholders are visible in the source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text alternative missing**
QA confirmed 0 characters in the text part. CAN-SPAM §7 does not mandate a text part, but its absence is a significant spam-filter signal (SpamAssassin `MIME_HTML_ONLY` rule adds score). Most ESPs recommend a minimum 20% text-to-HTML ratio. This also means screen-reader-only clients receive nothing.

**[INFO] `no-referrer` meta in head**
```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />
```
This suppresses the `Referer` header on outbound clicks. In email context this is usually intentional (privacy), but it will cause GA4/Adobe Analytics to report all clicks as `(direct)` if the ESP's click-redirect domain is not explicitly listed as a referral source exclusion. See section 6 for impact.

**[INFO] Physical address and unsubscribe link** — not visible in the truncated source provided. Could not verify CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) (physical address) or §5(a)(3) (opt-out mechanism). Confirm both are present in the full source footer.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**[WARN] `no-referrer` meta breaks referral attribution**
If adidas.com or any landing page is not whitelisted as a GA4 referral exclusion for `click.comms.adidas.com`, the referrer suppression caused by the `no-referrer` meta will collapse ESP-tracked clicks into the `(direct)` bucket after the redirect chain resolves. UTM parameters in the destination URLs will still be captured correctly only if they survive the broken redirect described in §2.

**[WARN] Broken redirect in §2 means UTM params on that CTA are undeliverable** — regardless of whether they're correctly appended to the destination URL, recipients can't reach the page.
