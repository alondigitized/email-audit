# Your cart expires soon!

## Executive summary

- There it is again — that pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sneaker, same exact shoe Skechers has been shoving at me all week, now wearing a new hat: "You liked these and you weren't wrong." I did not like that shoe. I've never clicked it, never put it in a cart, never even paused on it scrolling past. This is the fourth or fifth time this week I'm seeing this same women's floral sneaker headline my inbox, and it's starting to feel less like marketing and more like a broken record stuck on repeat.
- Below the fold there's actually some decent stuff — a men's slip-on Delson 3.0 and a couple of Arch Fit options I'd genuinely look at — but they're buried three rows down under a shoe I have zero interest in, with a "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button sitting right under a product I never ordered. That's just confusing, not persuasive.
- This one's getting marked as read and moved past. Not clicking, not forwarding — I'd only forward it to my wife as a "look, they're still doing this" joke, not because it's worth her time.

## What's working

- The 40% off summer styles banner is clear and the deadline (8/14-8/19) is spelled out plainly in the fine print, no games there.
- Men's Delson 3.0 slip-on and the Arch Fit Delson-style options are shown in the grid — that's actually in my wheelhouse, comfort and slip-on both checked.
- Big blue Skechers logo up top, easy to recognize who this is from at a glance, no squinting needed.
- Bottom nav with "Find a Store" and "Curbside Pickup" — appreciate that, I like to touch a shoe before buying.

## What's weak

- The hero product is a floral women's sneaker I've never looked at once — "you liked these" is just plain wrong for me.
- Fourth+ time this week seeing this exact shoe as the headline image, per the brand history I've been keeping. It's the same cart-nagging email reshuffled with new copy.
- "Complete Your Order" button implies I abandoned a cart, but I never had one — that's a trust problem, not just an annoyance.
- The stuff I'd actually want (men's slip-ons, arch support options) is stuck in a small grid way below the fold instead of leading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop leading with the floral D'Lites shoe for men on the list — if I've never clicked it in five sends, the algorithm should've learned that by now. Lead with the men's Delson 3.0 or Arch Fit Arcade instead.
- 2. Don't call it "cart" language ("complete your order," "you liked these") unless there's an actual cart or browse history behind it — false urgency erodes trust fast.
- 3. Move the "up to 40% off summer styles" banner higher, right under the header — that's the real offer, not a shoe I never touched.
- 4. Vary the creative day to day. Five near-identical sends this week with the same hero image reads as spam, not personalization.
