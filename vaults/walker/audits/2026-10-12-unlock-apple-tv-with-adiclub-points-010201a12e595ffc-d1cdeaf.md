# Unlock Apple TV with adiClub points
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's another adidas email, and this one barely mentions a shoe. The first thing I saw was a black box with Apple TV on it, a pickleball show, a Gary Oldman show and a soccer ball. That's a TV ad, not a shoe ad. I'm 62 and I came for cushioning, not to stream MLS matches.
- Past that, it's a pile of giveaways: a pair of black Samba OGs, signed footballs from rookies I've never heard of, and a college running pack in Louisville red. The Samba is a flat lace-up, so no help to my arch. I don't see anything here that's hands-free or slip-on. This is the 8th adidas email I've logged in a week, after "Chaos has a dress code" yesterday and the text-message-points one before it.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either, because my buddies would just ask what the points actually cost.

## What's working

- The Apple TV hero is big and clear, and the "Redeem Now" button is easy to spot. My thumb couldn't miss it.
- The text is dark on a light background and large enough to read without zooming.
- The black Samba on the orange square is a clean, handsome picture.
- It's marked as my adiClub newsletter at the top and in the header, so I know why I got it.
- Each section has its own button or link, so it's easy to see where to tap.

## What's weak

- It never says how many points the Apple TV subscription costs. The only fine print is "Terms apply", which makes me trust it less.
- Nothing here is about comfort, cushioning or arch support. It's streaming, football and college merch.
- The rookie giveaway is about young football players I don't know, and the Louisville-colored pack isn't for me either.
- Three giveaways in a row, with plain "Enter Now" links, feel like a lottery rather than a sale. There's no price and no shoe I could actually buy.
- The black banner at the top says "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" but shows no shoe. The "Shop Now" button is small and the banner is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the point cost in the hero, something like "2 months of Apple TV for 3,000 points". Without a number, I can't do the price-versus-value math I like to do.
- 2. Lead with one walking or comfort shoe and show how many points it costs, so members like me have a reason to open this.
- 3. Cut the three giveaways to one, and make the "Enter Now" links real buttons.
- 4. Add a line on how many points I have, and when the Apple TV offer ends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2 months of Apple TV, paid for with your points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your adiClub points just got more useful`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See what your points buy this month, plus a shot at free Sambas.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Redeem for Apple TV or enter to win. Offer ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has a serious deliverability gap: there is no plain-text alternative (QA: 0 chars). It also has some image defects, including missing alt text and an HTTP image source. The truncated HTML shows no other confirmed code-level defects.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 16 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified. They are on `click.comms.adidas.com` (`/CI0/…`).
- An image is served over plain HTTP from the click-tracking domain: `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a12e595ffc-d1cdeaf6-6c91-44cc-bd72-fab9d060a930-000000/QxiJIoMcV3ClhwDh6cKNKFfpO…`. This looks like an open-tracking pixel or a tracked image. Because it uses `http://`, it can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and open tracking would then under-count.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Two images are missing `alt` attributes (QA WARN):
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/c787502a-8f76-4603-8940-030eac9cea05?cp_tp=…`
  - The `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…` image. If this is a tracking pixel, an empty `alt=""` is acceptable, but the attribute should still be present.
- If `dv.adidas.com/o/c787…` is a content image, such as a hero or product image, screen readers and image-blocked clients get no information from it.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm other rendering details such as `role="presentation"` on layout tables or Outlook/MSO conditionals. The `@font-face` rules fall back to local Arial/Helvetica, and `mso-font-alt: Arial` is set, so font fallback is handled.
- The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW">` tags are harmless for email.
- The `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` tag has no `http-equiv`, so it doesn't declare the charset. This is minor. The charset is normally set by the Content-Type header, so I'm not treating it as a defect.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Plain-text part:** the QA check `[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing or too short: Text version is 0 chars` is confirmed. Multipart emails without a text/plain part score worse with some spam filters and are unusable in text-only clients and some accessibility tools.
- Headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) weren't provided. The footer, which holds the unsubscribe link and physical address, was cut off. I can't confirm either, so I'm not flagging them. The sender domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, a dedicated subdomain.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All links go through the `click.comms.adidas.com` redirector, so the final destination and any UTM parameters couldn't be checked from the source or probed by QA.
- The image URL on `dv.adidas.com` carries `cp_tp=…`, a signed or encoded image-transform parameter. It is not a campaign parameter.
- Continuity can't be assessed without resolving the redirects.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a text/plain MIME part with the offer copy, key links and unsubscribe link. This fixes the only QA FAIL.
2. Serve the `click.comms.adidas.com` image over HTTPS. Use `https://` for all image URLs.
3. Add `alt` text to the `dv.adidas.com/o/c787502a-…` image. If the image is decorative, use `alt=""`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking image too.
4. Resolve the 16 redirect links in a follow-up run, using a redirect-following probe or a real-browser check. That would confirm the destinations, the UTM parameters and that nothing returns a 4xx/5xx.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers, to cover the footer, unsubscribe mechanism, authentication results and any unresolved tokens.
