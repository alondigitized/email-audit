# Need help styling your Crocs?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I open a Crocs email thinking there might be a sale — maybe a new clog I haven't seen yet that actually supports my arch the way I need it to. Instead, I get a styling lookbook. Photos of young women in colorful outfits wearing Crocs like runway pieces. "Classic Clogs." "Sandals & Slides." "Styles With Extra Height." None of that is me. I'm not trying to coordinate my footwear with a floral jumpsuit. I want to know if the shoe is comfortable and whether it'll hold up walking around the neighborhood.
- The whole email reads like it was written for a 24-year-old TikTok shopper who already loves Crocs and wants outfit inspo. I'm a 62-year-old guy who bought Crocs because they're slip-on and my feet don't hurt after two hours in them. That's the whole story. Nothing here speaks to that.
- The one silver lining is the Afterpay block at the bottom — buy now, pay later is something I can appreciate — but even that feels tacked on, not targeted. I'd mark this as read without clicking.

## What's weak

- **Every model is a young woman in a fashion outfit** — I felt zero connection. Not one gray-haired guy walking the dog or standing in a garden. Completely wrong demographic mirror.
- **No comfort language anywhere** — Cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease — none of it. These are the only words that would make me keep reading.
- **No offer, no deal, no reason to act now** — The whole email is editorial content. I didn't get a discount, a member price, nothing. Why send this to me today?

- **"Need help styling your Crocs?" is the wrong question for me** — I don't need styling help. I need to know which clog won't make my knees ache after a long walk.

- 2. **Show me a male model my age** — One photo of a guy in his 60s walking outside in Crocs would do more than six photos of young women in trendy outfits.
- 3. **Add a concrete offer** — Even a modest "members get free shipping this week" or "20% off Classic Clogs" gives me a reason to click today rather than close the app.
- 4. **Fix the preheader** — The inbox shows gobbledygook padding instead of actual copy. Write one real sentence: something like "Comfort that fits any occasion — shop the full lineup."
