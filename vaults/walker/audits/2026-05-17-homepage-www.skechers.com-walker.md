# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Skechers knows what it's selling, I'll give them that — "Hands Free Slip-ins" is front and center, and that's exactly why I come to this site. Two big promo banners before I even see the shoes, which tells me they're running a real sale right now. The models in the hero are twenty-somethings by a pool, which isn't my mirror, but the product they're wearing is exactly mine.

## What's working

- **"BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart"** — that top blue bar is the first thing my eye hits. No guessing, no coupon code to hunt for, just applied at cart. That's how you run a sale for someone like me.
- **"Hands Free Slip-ins / GLIDE-STEP"** — they put their comfort flagship front and center. I don't have to go hunting for it. That's my shoe and they know it.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — I've been on this site before. That tab telling me I can jump back to what I was looking at last time is genuinely useful. I'm not a young guy with infinite patience for browsing.
- **Spring Apparel Sale banner** — a second offer stacked on top of the BOGO. Two deals visible before I scroll. Gets my attention.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — big, full-width, high-contrast white on a light background. Easy to tap on a phone. I'm not squinting.

## What's weak

- **Young models in the hero** — the man looks like he's 30, the woman maybe 25. I'm 62. I'm the one who actually *needs* a slip-in shoe. Would have been more convincing with someone closer to my age wearing these on a walk or at the grocery store.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook above the fold** — I have points with Skechers. There's no indication of that, no "You have X points" or even a generic "Members get more." That's a missed nudge.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — after a hero specifically about Slip-ins, the CTA should say "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Men's Slip-ins." "SHOP NOW" sends me… where, exactly?
- **No free-shipping callout** — one of the first things I check before I add something to a cart. It's not visible here.
- **No trust signals** — no "Free returns," no star ratings on the featured product, nothing that reassures me if the shoe doesn't fit when the box arrives.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Men's Slip-ins"** — or at minimum split the CTA into "Shop Men" / "Shop Women" below the hero. I want to get to my product in one tap, not land on a unisex landing page.
- **Add a free-shipping line to the promo strip** — even if it's just "Free shipping on orders $75+" right next to the BOGO banner. That closes a mental gap before I scroll.
- **Cast a model in their late 50s or 60s** — the Slip-in is literally a shoe for people whose backs hurt bending over. Show me someone who looks like they'd actually benefit from that.
- **Surface the loyalty balance or a points teaser above the fold** — even "Earn points on every order" does the job. If I'm a returning customer, remind me there's a reason to buy here instead of DSW.
