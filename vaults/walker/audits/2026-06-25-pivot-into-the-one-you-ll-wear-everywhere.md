- **Zero offer.** No price, no percentage off, no free shipping — nothing for me to act on. The June 13th "Extra 25% off" email at least gave me a reason to tap. This one gives me a mood board.

**Malformed `<meta>` tags (MEDIUM):**

Both charset and IE-compat tags are missing their `http-equiv` attribute:
```html
<!-- as sent — broken -->
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
<meta content="IE=edge" />

<!-- correct -->
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />
```
Without `http-equiv`, these are no-ops. The charset declaration in particular will cause Outlook Desktop to use its default encoding detection instead.

**Tracking pixel missing `alt` attribute:**

`<img ... src="https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?...">` — no `alt=""`. Screen readers will announce the raw URL. Low severity since the element is `display:none`, but flagged by QA.

**Custom font fallback** — `TT Crocs` will not render in Outlook (any version) or older Android clients. Acceptable if the ESP template has a system-font fallback in the element-level `font-family` declarations (not visible in truncated source; confirm in full HTML).
