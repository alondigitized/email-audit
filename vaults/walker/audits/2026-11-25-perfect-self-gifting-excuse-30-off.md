- It's the same 30% pitch I've seen at least six times this week, so I can't tell what's new.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-templated XHTML 1.0 Transitional message with dark-mode support and click and open tracking. I confirmed only minor markup and accessibility issues in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so authentication, unsubscribe and merge-token checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click-tracking wrappers:** Links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com/q/...~~/AAQRxRA~/...` redirects. The final destinations and any UTM parameters can't be seen in the source.
- **Link probing coverage:** The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped 65. Probe results were not included, so I can't confirm the status of the skipped links. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't shown.
- **Plain-text URL ratio:** 24,225 of 29,805 plain-text characters (about 81%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This comes from the long tracking URLs. A text part this URL-heavy can add a small spam-filter risk.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261125_P261757_V1_W-_Offer_New_3` is an open-tracking pixel. It carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) in the query string. It's a standard practice, though it's blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and image blocking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked image without alt:** The image `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/EqCyMdGD-rhrhcYqHB7iXQ~~/AAQRxRA~/kCnIvKpJJJgxCCDWbihqPAyqHfKdXUAT5bHXeWZBQFBljG9Hjp4Ptl4TbWxP_` has no alt attribute. Its `src` is a tracking redirect rather than an image file, and it looks like a second tracking pixel or an image served through the click-tracker. Screen readers may read out the URL.
- **Pixel without alt:** `o.gif` has no alt text. This is minor and `alt=""` is the usual fix.
- **Viewport zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Meta tags missing `http-equiv`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration does anything. The MIME `Content-Type` header normally supplies the charset, so the impact is low.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Dark mode:** The `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` metas and `prefers-color-scheme` rules are present and correctly formed. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (such as `{{ }}` or `%%`) appear in the visible source. The truncation means I couldn't check the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Sender domain:** `dsw@e.dsw.com` is on a dedicated sending subdomain, consistent with good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. UTM parameters and landing-page URLs can't be assessed because of the redirect wrapping and the missing probe results. The campaign identifier `20261125_P261757_V1_W-_Offer_New_3` appears in the pixel URL only. The subject promises "30% off", and I couldn't verify the landing page's offer against it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to both flagged images. Use `alt=""` for `o.gif`, and investigate the `ablink.dsw.com` image, which may be a misconfigured tracking element.
