- That opening dark, empty-looking banner with "IN STORE ONLY" wasted my first scroll — no offer, no product, just texture and text.
- Not one shoe in here is aimed at a guy my age looking for slip-on comfort — it's a family sale, court sneakers, and kids' Puma high-tops.

- This is the fourth-plus email from Shoe Carnival this week and it's blending together with the last few — nothing here breaks the pattern.
