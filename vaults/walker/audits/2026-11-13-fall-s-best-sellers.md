# Fall's best sellers
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is another HOKA shoe email, and the shoes lead, which I like. "Road running MVPs" is huge, neon yellow on charcoal, and I can read it on my phone without squinting. The hero shoe is a pale cream Arahi, a stability shoe with a good wide sole. It's closer to my kind of walking shoe than the winter boots on 11/12 or the pink Clifton on 11/10.
- The middle lets it down. Four small shoe tiles sit in pale pastels, and half are women's. Then the email swings to a black GORE-TEX hiking boot and a trail runner splashing through water. I'm not hiking, and I'm not running in puddles. It's the same free-shipping message again, now with a $250 minimum and fine print. No price shows anywhere, so I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- Would I click? Maybe the Arahi 9 Men, out of curiosity. I'd not forward it. I'd read it and move on.

## What's working

- "Road running MVPs" is big, bold, and easy to read on a phone with no zooming.
- The hero shoe fills the top of the email, so I know right away it's a shoe email.
- Arahi and Clifton are named, with "smooth ride and stable support." That's the cushioned, supportive feel I shop for.
- The men's and women's versions are labeled and split out, so I can find mine.
- The black "Shop Best Sellers" buttons are wide and easy to tap with my thumb.

## What's weak

- No prices or deals anywhere. A "best sellers" email with no dollar amounts gives me nothing to compare against Brooks or New Balance.
- The GORE-TEX hiker and the water-splash trail shoe are outdoor gear, not what I buy. They pull the email away from comfort and walking.
- The pastel shoes on pale gray tiles are hard to see. The women's shoes take half the grid, and the tiles don't show the cushioning or arch support I care about.
- Free expedited shipping appears three times. It has a $250 minimum and a text-signup catch with two asterisks. That's a lot of fine print for a shipping offer.
- The tiny legal text at the bottom is too small for me to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price on every shoe tile, with a line like "Cushioned. Stable. All-day comfort." under each one. That would do the most.
- 2. Cut the GORE-TEX and trail blocks, or send them to a segment that buys trail shoes. Use the space for a men's walking or Bondi block.
- 3. Show the real shipping threshold in one clear line, not three. Put the fine print where I can read it.
- 4. Use darker backgrounds or higher-contrast photos so the pale shoes stand out.
- 5. Make the buttons say what they lead to, like "Shop Men's Arahi" or "Shop Men's Clifton."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is image-heavy. The HTML is functional, but it has no plain-text part and no alt text on any content image. All 12 probed tracking links returned 403 or 406. Those codes look like bot filtering by the Cordial redirector, not dead destinations, so they need a re-test with a realistic client.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 links returned 406 and the Unsubscribe link returned 403.** All go through `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6aeb9f6a54658610940264f2:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/<id>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`.
  - Decoding the `jwtP` payload shows valid destinations. Examples: `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/best-sellers/?utm_…`, `…/womens-everyday-running-gear/arahi-9/1176570.html?dwvar_1176570_color=VWL`, and `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`.
  - 403/406 on every redirect, including the unsubscribe, points to the ESP rejecting the probe's user-agent or headers. I can't confirm real end-user breakage from this data. Re-probe with a browser UA before treating these as live defects.
- 16 links were skipped by the prober, so coverage is incomplete.
- Several image links point to the same destination (`/best-sellers/`, images `_a`, `_b`, `_c`, `_d`, `_e`) with separate tracking IDs. That is normal for per-link click attribution, not a defect.
- Two tracking pixels are present: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. The second is on a different subdomain, which suggests a second sending or tracking system alongside Cordial. This is informational, not a fault.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 14 content images.** Examples: `US_HOKAHeader_Black.jpg`, `US_20261113_BestSellers_a.jpg` through `_f.jpg`, `US_Arahi9_TopLeft_1176570-VWL.jpg`, `US_Clifton11_BottomLeft_1176573-ARMR.jpg`, `LogoFooterPrimary.png`, `US_Q3_SMSCTA_DoubleAsterisk.jpg`.
  - The QA output shows the missing `alt` text also leaves most links with no accessible name. The link text appears as `[image: …jpg]`.
  - With images blocked, the email renders blank. The `1200x1640` and `1200x1276` images likely carry the offer copy and legal text, so that content becomes unreadable to screen readers and unavailable when images are off.
  - The two tracking pixels also lack `alt`. That is harmless but easy to fix with `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it does nothing.
- **Conflicting viewport tags.** The first has `maximum-scale=1` with no `user-scalable`. The second has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`. Both set `maximum-scale=1`, which restricts pinch-zoom on some clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}`
  - `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`
  These come from empty template variables and are ignored by clients, but they show an unfilled template.
- **Focus outlines removed.** The reset `* {…outline:none…}` removes keyboard focus indicators.
- The link color `#000001` and footer link color `#fffffe` are deliberate hacks to avoid client color overrides. They are not defects.
- The HTML was truncated, so I could not check the body structure (`role="presentation"` on layout tables, hidden preheader text).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `hmail=` parameter is a populated hash, not a token. The body is truncated, so the check covers the available source only.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing:** "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). A multipart/alternative with a text part is expected for deliverability and for text-only clients.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown because the message came via the AgentMail relay. This is a gap in the test setup, not a confirmed failure. Check the raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Unsubscribe link exists** (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`). Its wrapper returned 403 to the probe. Verify it resolves in a real browser, since a failing unsubscribe is a CAN-SPAM risk.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** not provided in the data, so I can't confirm them.
- **Physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.
- The sender is `info@emails.hoka.com`, aligned with the tracking domain `e.emails.hoka.com`. No mismatch found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTMs are present and consistent on all decoded destinations: `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261113_BestSellers_FES_STO_A7Day_HighestEngaged_60BestSellersTag_365RoadTag_270GTXTag`. Neither `utm_content` nor `utm_term` is set.
  - Without `utm_content`, GA can't tell clicks on the multiple image links to `/best-sellers/` apart. The ESP's own link IDs partly cover this.
  - The campaign value is long and packs audience segments into one field, which makes reporting harder.
- Product links go to specific PDPs with a color variant (`dwvar_1176570_color=VWL`, `dwvar_1176531_color=SCCG`, `dwvar_1176573_color=ARMR`, `dwvar_1176572_color=ATPS`), which matches the "best sellers" framing.
- The destination URLs include `hmail=<hash>` and `cordial_id=…`. This puts a hashed email identifier into the landing page URL, which may be logged by analytics or third parties. Confirm it is intended and stripped client-side.
- The unsubscribe URL also carries the full UTM set. That is unnecessary on a preference-center link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 14 content images (the header, the product images, and the `_a`–`_f` banners), and use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels. This also gives the image links accessible names.
2. Add a plain-text alternative part. It is currently 0 chars.
3. Re-probe the 12 failed links with a browser-like User-Agent. Confirm the unsubscribe link resolves, and check whether the Cordial redirector blocks non-browser clients.
4. Inspect the raw headers for `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and confirm the physical address in the footer.
5. Clean up the template: fill in `<title>`, fix the charset to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, remove the duplicate viewport tag and `maximum-scale=1`, and remove the empty-value CSS (`padding-top:px`, `border-top:px solid`).
6. Restore a visible focus style instead of the global `outline:none`.
7. Add `utm_content` per link, and consider shortening `utm_campaign`.
8. Drop the UTMs from the unsubscribe URL, and confirm the `hmail` hash in destination URLs is intended.
