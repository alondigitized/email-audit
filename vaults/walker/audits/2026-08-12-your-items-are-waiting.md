# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom shoe, front and center, "your cart is still waiting." That's the fourth time this week I've gotten this exact abandoned-cart line (8/8, 8/9, and now 8/12), and it's still not my cart. I don't own floral anything, and I sure didn't put a women's embroidered sneaker in my basket.
- The "here's some more options" grid at least tries to recover — there's a men's Delson slip-on and that blue Anunoby basketball shoe that might catch a second look — but then it's buried under a Boy's Garage Sneaker and two more women's BOBS styles. And the big blue banner at the bottom is a kids' back-to-school sale, 25% off. None of this is for me. I'm not clicking, not forwarding — straight to archived, and I'm getting numb to these "your items are waiting" emails at this point.

## What's working

- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is bold, high-contrast navy, easy to spot even for my eyes.
- Men's Delson 3.0 - Roth Shoes and the blue Anunoby basketball shoe in the "more options" grid are at least things a guy could look at.
- Layout is clean and uncluttered — big fonts, single column, nothing overlapping.
- Afterpay/Klarna and "Find a Store" up front is a nice touch if I actually wanted to buy something here.

## What's weak

- Hero shoe is, again, a pink floral women's sneaker I never touched — this is the same wrong-cart problem as the last three sends.
- The "more options" row is mostly women's and boys' styles; my one or two matches get lost in the shuffle.
- The 25% off banner at the bottom is a kids' back-to-school sale — zero relevance to me.
- This is the third near-identical "abandoned cart" email this week with the same shoe up top. It's starting to feel like a template blast, not a real cart reminder.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cart-matching logic — if I never added that floral shoe, don't lead with it a fourth time. That's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. If there's no real cart data for me, swap to a men's comfort pick as the hero instead of guessing wrong every time.
- 3. Move the men's/comfort items to the top of the "more options" grid instead of burying them.
- 4. Cut the kids' back-to-school banner for recipients with no kids' purchase history, or replace it with a men's promo.
- 5. Don't repeat this exact abandoned-cart template three sends in a row — space it out or vary the framing.
