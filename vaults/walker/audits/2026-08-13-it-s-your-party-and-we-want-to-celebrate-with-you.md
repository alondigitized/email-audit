# Technical Audit: SKECHERS PLUS "It's your party" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a birthday-capture campaign built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (emails.skechers.com) with standard Outlook/Gmail-safe CSS resets; it fails one broken-experience check (empty personalization token) and serves 6 images plus 1 tracking pixel over unencrypted HTTP.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 4 tracking pixels fire in the hidden preheader block: SFMC open tracker (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?XMSAEQSKW47ULE2SIAWLYMDJBY.100225`), two Krux/Salesforce DMP user-match beacons (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif`), and a Krux ad-impression beacon (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif?campaignid=TRG_US_EN_BDAYCAPTURE_2_07312026`).
- A fifth pixel from a third-party ESP (`ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=TRG_US_EN_BDAYCAPTURE_2_07312026`) loads over **plain HTTP**, is missing `alt=""`, and sits outside the `emails.skechers.com` tracking domain — worth confirming this vendor is still an intended integration.
- A malformed pixel src is present: `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?...` is missing its `http(s)://` scheme entirely, so it will not resolve as an absolute URL in most clients.
- 30 click-tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for SFMC-wrapped redirect links — not itself a defect, but means destination URLs are unverified in this pass).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 2 images missing `alt` text: `o.gif` (ink1000 pixel) and `49468f73-...png`. Decorative tracking pixels are fine without alt, but `49468f73` should be checked — if it's content-bearing, screen readers get nothing.
- 6 images served over `http://` instead of `https://` on `image.emails.skechers.com`, which many mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) will block or flag as mixed content: the main Skechers logo (`dde00662-...png`), `49468f73-...png`, App Store/Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, and all 4 social icons (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube). This is a broad, template-wide issue, not an isolated asset.
- Head includes proper mobile/desktop toggle classes (`.mobile-content`/`.desktop-content`) and responsive breakpoints at 375px/480px/640px/680px/768px — standard multi-client scaffolding, no structural issues found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL]** Empty greeting slot: the greeting renders as "Hi ," with no first-name merge value substituted. This is a customer-facing broken-experience bug, not just a QA warning — recipients without a stored first name will see visibly broken personalization.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- The provided HTML is truncated before the footer, so the unsubscribe link, physical mailing address, and CAN-SPAM footer block are **not visible in this source** — cannot confirm compliance either way from the given evidence.
- No email authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) were provided in the source for review; sending domain `emails.skechers.com` is a dedicated subdomain, consistent with typical DKIM/SPF alignment setups, but this cannot be verified from HTML alone.
- Recommend re-running this audit against the full (untruncated) HTML to check the footer/unsubscribe block specifically.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identifier `TRG_US_EN_BDAYCAPTURE_2_07312026` is consistently applied across the Krux beacon and the ink1000 pixel (`mi_ecmp=`), indicating consistent campaign tagging at the tracking-pixel level.
- No visible click-through CTA URLs with UTM parameters were present in the untruncated portion of the source (all 30 outbound links were SFMC-wrapped redirect links skipped by the prober) — landing-page UTM alignment cannot be verified from this evidence and needs the resolved redirect targets.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the empty first-name merge field — add a fallback (e.g., "Hi there,") for contacts with no stored first name to prevent the broken "Hi ," rendering.
2. Migrate all 6 `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` asset URLs to `https://` — same host, scheme only.
3. Move the ink1000.com pixel to HTTPS or remove it if the vendor integration is deprecated; add `alt=""` to it.
4. Add `alt=""` (or descriptive alt text if content-bearing) to `49468f73-...png`.
5. Fix the malformed `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` src by adding the missing `http://` or `https://` scheme.
6. Re-audit with the full/untruncated HTML to verify the CAN-SPAM footer, unsubscribe link, and resolved click-tracking destination URLs for UTM/landing-page alignment.
