# BOGO 50% OFF ENDS TOMORROW
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off, right up top, plain and simple — same honest number I've been seeing from this brand all week. But then it's slides with polka dots, a purple Nike sneaker in what looks like a women's colorway, and a soirée dress shoe with a lace skirt — this whole email is styled for someone about a third my age going to summer parties. "Keep it on the low, no one has to know how little you paid" — that's not a line that means anything to a 62-year-old buying shoes because his feet hurt.
- I didn't see a single walking shoe, slip-on, or anything with visible cushioning or arch support in the whole scroll. The "Shop by Size" grid only runs up to 11, no wide options shown until you dig past it. This is the same story as every other DSW send this week — real offer, wrong closet.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Mark as read and move on — though I'll note the shoe donation section for $10 back is a decent idea, if buried way too low to matter.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly at the top — no bait-and-switch headline this time.
- "Shop Men" is at least a visible nav link at the very top, so it's not totally absent from the email.
- The National Shoe Donation Day banner with $10 VIP Bonus Reward is a genuinely useful, honest offer if it applied to me.
- Layout is clean and scannable, no broken images or overlapping text that I could see.

## What's weak

- Every featured product — sandals, sneakers, dress shoes — reads teen or young-adult women's styling: platform slides, pink Nike lowtops, lace soirée dress with heels.
- "Keep it on the low" copy about hiding what you paid for vacation/concert outfits has nothing to do with how I shop.
- Zero mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, or hands-free/slip-on — the entire reason I'd open a shoe email.
- Size grid tops out at 11 with wide width buried as a small button below, easy to miss scrolling fast on a phone.
- Nothing here signals "we know you're a repeat customer" — feels like a mass blast, not personalized to me at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap in a men's comfort/walking shoe as the hero product with a plain "cushioned, all-day comfort" callout — that's the single biggest miss.
- 2. Cut the "keep it on the low" copy entirely — it reads gimmicky and isn't aimed at my life stage.
- 3. Move the National Shoe Donation Day $10 reward higher — that's a real incentive for someone like me who already has old shoes lying around.
- 4. Show men's sizing and wide-width options in the same visual block as the hero offer, not scrolled past three sections down.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles: BOGO 50% off ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: BOGO 50% off walking shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on styles for your everyday miles`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: donate old shoes, get $10 back`
