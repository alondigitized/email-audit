# Surprise: $25 off your purchase!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- It's Shoe Carnival again, and I'm tired of it. This is the seventh or eighth send in about two weeks. Yesterday was the Veterans Day "FINAL DAY" banner, and the day before was the sleigh email. The subject says "Surprise: $25 off," but there's no surprise. It's the same $10 off $74.98 or $25 off $149.98 holiday coupon I've seen before.
- The Birkenstock sale at $44.98 caught my eye. A cushioned, arch-supported sandal is closer to what I buy than anything else in here. The numbers are big enough to read on my phone without glasses. After that, the email drifts into a retro Christmas photo of a young guy in Converse and a Puma wedge sneaker. That's not my foot, and it's not my style.
- Would I click? Maybe the Birkenstock link, if I'm in the mood. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably read it and move on.

## What's working

- The Birkenstock "starting at $44.98" block is big, bold and easy to read. It's the one comfort-footwear item in the email.
- The coupon block at the top is clear. $10 off $74.98 or $25 off $149.98, with the code HOLIDAY.
- The 2-3X Shoe Perks points callout is a nice touch for a member.
- The barcodes near the bottom are handy. I can show one in the store without hunting for a code.
- The Family Athletics sale lists New Balance, Jordan, Adidas and Puma, and the $59.98 adult price is easy to read.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a "surprise," but the coupon is the same one they've been sending all month.
- The hero photo is a young man in a tie and Converse for "Have a Very Retro Christmas." That's not for me, and it has nothing to do with comfort or arch support.
- There's no slip-on, walking or cushioned men's section. No Hoka, Brooks or Skechers-style comfort shoes show up, and that's what I shop for.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and I couldn't read it without zooming. It's also where the exclusions are buried.
- The email is very long, with a lot of scrolling past the coupon, Word Stride, SMS signup and Afterpay boxes. The "Some exclusions apply" notes make me trust the offers less.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear offer at the top and drop the repeat coupons. If the surprise is real, make it a different offer, not the same holiday code.
- 2. Add a men's comfort and walking section. Show cushioned slip-ons and name the brands, with a price I can compare.
- 3. Vary the models. Show someone my age wearing the shoe, and make the text bigger.
- 4. Cut the length. Move the barcodes up and remove the fine-print wall, or link to it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based, responsive template with Hotmail/Outlook/Gmail/Samsung fixes. It has two confirmed defects: a malformed, partly unresolved tracking-pixel URL, and missing alt attributes. The HTML source was truncated and the 30 tracking links were not probed, so link, footer and compliance checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved placeholder:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still contains a literal `[UNIQUE]` token. If the ESP doesn't substitute it at send time, the open pixel won't attribute to a recipient.
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261112_birkenstock_puma_AM​` ends with a zero-width character (invisible in the QA output).
  - `t=utm_campaign=…` nests a key=value pair inside a single parameter, so the UTM may not parse as `utm_campaign`.
  - The stray invisible character may be appended to the campaign value.
- **Unverified links:** 30 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their redirect chains and final destinations are unconfirmed (QA WARN).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (2 content images):** `23a03225-7c93-49fa-988c-8821675cac1b.jpg` and `fe4e293c-bccf-488a-bbf7-db06bf5745de.jpg`, both on `image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/`.
  - Screen readers will announce the filename or nothing.
  - These images may carry content, such as an offer or CTA.
- **Missing alt (2 tracking pixels):** the `emltrk.com` pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
  - The `charset="utf-8"` attribute still works in most clients.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the `<html>` tag carries `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. This is harmless in practice but inconsistent.
- **Links indistinguishable from text:** the global rule `a, a:link, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` removes link color and underline everywhere. Text links are then identifiable only by context, which is a WCAG 1.4.1 risk unless buttons or underlines are styled inline.
- **Small mobile text:** `.responsive-text` is set to `font-size:12px !important` at ≤600px, which is small for body copy.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` are forced to `light`, so dark-mode behavior is client-dependent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `[UNIQUE]` in the pixel URL (see §2) is the only token-like artifact found.
- No visible `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `*|…|*` tokens in the visible portion. The rest of the body was truncated and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA check attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is not evidence of failure.
- **Unsubscribe link, List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers, and physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source and not covered by the QA output. They could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only UTM seen is `utm_campaign=SCA_20261112_birkenstock_puma_AM`, and it sits in a pixel URL, not a click link (see §2).
- Landing-page UTMs and destinations were not verified because all 30 tracking links were skipped.
- The campaign name references Birkenstock/Puma while the subject is a generic "$25 off your purchase". Check that the landing pages match the offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[UNIQUE]` token and the `t=utm_campaign=…` pixel URL in the ESP template. Strip the zero-width character from the campaign string.
2. Add `alt` text to the two content images, and `alt=""` to both `emltrk.com` pixels.
3. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Restore a visible link style, or ensure inline-styled buttons or underlines exist, to meet WCAG 1.4.1. Raise the 12px mobile text.
5. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML and the raw headers. Check:
   - the unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers;
   - the postal address;
   - `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) from the original message;
   - the final URLs and UTMs of the 30 tracked links, using a redirect-following probe or a Tracking domain allowlist.
