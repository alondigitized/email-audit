## What's working

- The Skechers Hands-Free Slip-Ins block is clean, priced clearly ($59.98), and says "Men's & Women's" so I know it's not just for my wife.
- Brooks Ghost 17 / Revel 8 "up to $40 off" — I've now tracked this same offer across the week, so it reads as a real, standing deal, not a one-day gimmick.
- Each product block is its own clear tile — Nike, Jordan, Skechers, Kids' Skechers, Brooks — easy to scan and skip the ones that aren't for me.
- "3 Day Sale, This Weekend Only" up top gives a real deadline, not vague "limited time" mush.
- Shoe Perks $10 reward and the $20/$10 coupon codes at the bottom are visible for anyone who's a member.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is a young woman in a crop top and heels-adjacent sneaker fashion shot — nothing about that says "62-year-old guy needs arch support."
- I have to scroll past Nike, Nike Court, and Jordan blocks — none of which are my brand or price range — before I hit anything relevant.
- The "LIMIT..." banner right before Shoe Perks looks cut off / cropped, like part of the graphic got chopped.
- No mention of arch support, cushioning, or all-day comfort anywhere near the Skechers block — just price and "hands-free," which is good but incomplete for someone comparing fit, not just style.
- Fine print block at the very bottom is a wall of gray text I'd never read on my phone — feels like it's there to cover the company, not inform me.

- 1. Move the Skechers Hands-Free Slip-Ins block up near the top, right under the hero — don't make comfort shoppers scroll past three sneaker-fashion blocks to find their category.
- 2. Add one line under the Skechers price tag about cushioning or arch support — "hands-free" alone doesn't tell me if these are all-day-on-my-feet shoes.
- 3. Fix or re-crop that broken "LIMIT..." banner before Shoe Perks — it looks like a rendering error, not a design choice.
