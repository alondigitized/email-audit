- This is the same cart nag again, and it's the pink flower sneaker. The D'Lites "Spring Bloom" is a women's shoe I never put in a cart. It sits in the middle of the screen under "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." That line is cute, but it also feels a bit watchful. I saw this same shoe in the 10/12 "Did you forget something?" and "Your cart expires soon!" emails, and in the 10/11 "Your Items Are Waiting!" email. This subject line is a repeat of that last one.
- The "Complete Your Order" button is easy to see and big enough for my thumb. Below it, the grid has a few shoes I'd actually look at. The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman and the Contour Foam sneaker are close to what I wear. They're packed in with two boys' sneakers and a women's shoe, though.
- The 25% off for Skechers Plus members is the part I'd care about, and it's buried halfway down. It ends 10/13, which is today. I'd skim it, maybe tap the slip-ins, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. A friend who asked me for shoe advice would get a link to the shoe, not this email.

## What's working

- The "Complete Your Order" button is dark blue, clearly labeled, and big enough to tap on my phone.
- The 25% off banner is bold and says "2 DAYS ONLY." Both the offer and the deadline are clear.
- The Altman slip-on and Contour Foam sneaker in the grid are my kind of shoe. Slip-ins are exactly what I want.
- The Women / Men / Kids / Sale list is large type, easy to read and easy to tap.
- It's the real Skechers sender and the usual look, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The hero is a pink floral women's sneaker I never looked at. It's the fourth time I've seen it, and it makes the "we noticed you" line feel off.
- The 25% off is the best reason to click, and it's way down below the shoe grid. I only found it after scrolling.
- The grid is a jumble of two boys' garage sneakers, a women's shoe and three men's shoes. Nothing says "picked for you."
- The 25% is for Skechers Plus members only, and "must be logged in" is in tiny print at the bottom. It's too small for me to read without zooming.
- The product captions are tiny and long, like "Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit: Altman - Murdock Sneaker." I can't read them on a phone without pinching.
- There are no prices, no star ratings and nothing on arch support or cushioning.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 25% off banner to the top, right under the headline. Say who it's for in the banner itself, so I don't have to dig for it.
- 2. Show what was actually in my cart, or stop saying "your cart." If the shoe isn't mine, the whole email falls apart.
- 3. Build the grid from what I've browsed: men's slip-ins and Arch Fit. Leave out the boys' and women's shoes.
- 4. Add a price, a star rating and a short "Arch Fit, cushioned" line under each shoe, in bigger type.
- 5. Don't send this same cart nag again and again over three days. Cut it to one or two.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Your Items Are Waiting!" (SKECHERS `hello@msgs.skechers.com`)

## 1. Technical Summary
The abandoned-cart email has two QA failures: the unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400, and the plain-text part is 0 characters. It also loads about 14 images over insecure `http://`. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and header checks are limited (see sections 5 and 6).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe failed (400).** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_lid=m50…` returned 400.
  - The macro decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, so it is a real Attentive unsubscribe URL.
  - The 400 may come from the probe lacking a valid session or user agent, or from a token that is single-use or expired. The probe cannot tell these apart.
  - Treat it as unverified until it is tested from a real inbox.
- **Two CTAs were not validated.** "Complete you Order" and "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting..." (both `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`) returned 429.
  - Their final destinations and cart-restore behavior are unconfirmed.
  - Only 12 of 38 links were probed.
- **Every link is wrapped in Attentive redirects.** The links go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<opaque token>`, so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters cannot be read from the source.
- **The `web version` link is an opaque tracked redirect.** It uses the same `e/c/` pattern, so its target was not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.** At least 14 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`.
  - Affected images: logo `dde00662-…png`, hero "Complete you Order" `dfcc8fd7-…jpg`, "Here's some more great options" `737031d9-…jpg`, `49468f73-…png`, App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and `a6ec5198-…jpg`.
  - These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients and webmail.
  - The same host already serves one GIF over HTTPS: `https://image.emails.skechers.com/…/m/21/021bd784-…gif`. HTTPS is available.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif` (HTTPS). Meaningful images with no alt are unusable with images off or with a screen reader.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` (HTTP).
  - The `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` pixel. This is minor for a tracking pixel; `alt=""` is enough.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and the web-version view use it.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute. The `͏` and soft-hyphen preheader padding could mis-render if the client does not assume UTF-8 from the MIME header.
- **Alt text typo.** The alt text "Complete you Order" is missing the "r" in "your". It is read aloud with images off.
- The viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, the 600px layout with mobile stacking, and the hidden preheader are present. No issues found with them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. Because the source is truncated, cart item names and prices could not be checked for correct population.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (0 chars).** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts spam scoring and accessibility, and some filters penalize HTML-only mail.
- **Unsubscribe link is unverified.** It is present, labeled "Unsubscribe", and one click away, but it returned 400 in probing (section 2). A broken opt-out would breach CAN-SPAM if it fails for real recipients, so verify it by hand.
- **Not verifiable from the supplied data:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
  - The physical postal address in the footer (the source was cut off before it).
  - Do not read these as passing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing pages are unverifiable.** Redirect tokens hide the destinations, and the two primary cart CTAs returned 429.
- **Cart restoration is unconfirmed.** The email promises "You left something in your cart", but no probe confirmed that the CTA lands on a cart-restoring URL.
- **Click host differs from the sender domain.** Links use the shared `skechers.e.attentivemail.com` and `skechers.attn.tv` hosts while the sender is `msgs.skechers.com`. This is low severity.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the unsubscribe URL** by hand from a real delivered message, and confirm it returns 200 and processes the opt-out. Check the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058).
2. **Add a plain-text part** with the CTA URLs, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
3. **Switch all `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` image sources to `https://`.** Configure the template or ESP asset setting so it stops emitting HTTP.
4. **Add meaningful alt text** to `021bd784-…gif` and `a6ec5198-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to the open pixel. Fix "Complete you Order" to "Complete your Order".
5. **Fill in `<title>`** and change the charset tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` (or `<meta charset="utf-8">`).
6. **Re-probe the two 429 CTAs** with throttling or a retry, to confirm they resolve to a cart-restoring landing page and carry UTM and campaign parameters.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers** to cover the footer address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and merge-token population.
