# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, they've done it again — "You Liked These" and there's the Care Bears kids' sneaker and a hot pink women's high-top staring back at me like I'm supposed to remember browsing those. I did not. There's one shoe up top, the white/red/blue Arch Fit D'Lites with the chunky sole, that I might've actually clicked on at some point, but it's buried under two picks that have nothing to do with a 62-year-old guy. Then the "Complete Your Order" grid is six more shoes, four of them women's or kids', like whoever built this cart tracker just grabbed everything in my browsing history without sorting by gender.
- This is the same cart from Monday's "Your Items Are Waiting!" email, just reshuffled. I said it then and I'll say it again now — if you're going to remind me what's in my cart, at least remind me of things I'd actually buy. The 25% off banner is for kids' back-to-school styles only, which does nothing for me either.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on. No click, no forward — there's nothing in here worth passing along to a buddy, and honestly this is turning into background noise from Skechers at this point.

## What's working

- The top shoe — the Arch Fit D'Lites 330 Star Line — has a real chunky sole and looks like something I'd actually consider, same as the pair that caught my eye in Friday's back-in-stock email.
- "Complete Your Order" button is big, blue, easy to spot and tap.
- Layout is clean and readable at a glance, no overlapping text or broken images.
- Payment options (Afterpay, Klarna) and store locator are visible if I did want to buy, which I appreciate for a big-ticket item.

## What's weak

- Two of three "you liked these" shoes are the Care Bears kids' sneaker and a hot pink women's high-top — I've never looked at either, and it's the same mismatch from Monday's and Tuesday's emails.
- The "Complete Your Order" grid is mostly women's styles again — four out of six pairs aren't for me.
- The 25% off headline banner is kids-only, so as a childless 62-year-old it's just dead space I have to scroll past.
- Subject line "Your cart expires soon!" implies urgency but there's no countdown or deadline anywhere in the body — feels like an empty threat.
- Third email in under a week with this exact same cart contents recycled — it's starting to feel like a broken feed, not a real reminder.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "liked these" logic so it only shows items matching my actual browsing gender/category — showing me Care Bears kids' shoes after three straight emails is a targeting bug, not a feature.
- 2. Put an actual expiration time or date on the cart if the subject says "expires soon" — right now it's a bluff.
- 3. Swap the "Complete Your Order" grid to lead with men's styles if I've got any in my history, instead of burying the one relevant shoe at the top and filling the grid with women's picks.
- 4. Drop or de-prioritize the kids' back-to-school banner for customers without kids' items in their purchase history.
- 5. Slow the cadence — this is the third near-identical cart email in a week; space it out or vary the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still thinking about the Arch Fit D'Lites?`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart closes out tonight at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The chunky-sole slip-in you checked out is almost gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Complete your order before these sell out.`
