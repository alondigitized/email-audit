# You Searched, We Delivered
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- That broken preview text in my inbox was the first thing I noticed — a wall of code gibberish where the hook copy should be. Made me think the email was corrupted before I even opened it. I only clicked because I recognize Skechers and figured whatever they sent had to be better than whatever that mess in the preview was.
- Inside, the BOGO banner at the top is the saving grace — big, clear, buy one get one 50% off. I got that in two seconds. After that, though, the email feels like a category catalog dropped on my lap. Hands Free Slip-Ins, Go Walk, Arch Fit — those three sections are exactly my language, and I appreciated seeing them. But sandwiched between a female model up front and a 25% Off Apparel section at the bottom, it starts to feel like I wandered into the wrong aisle.
- The subject says "You Searched, We Delivered" — so I'm expecting something personalized. Maybe the Max Cushioning Glide-Step I had in my cart a week ago, or the Go Walk I was eyeing. What I got was a best-sellers grid aimed at everyone. I'll mark it read, maybe come back to the BOGO. I won't forward it.

## What's working

- **BOGO banner leads the email** — buy one, get one 50% off is front and center, readable in under two seconds, exactly how a deal should be presented
- **Hands Free Slip-Ins section hits my language** — "Hands Free" is the phrase that stopped me cold in their May 12 send, and it still works here
- **Go Walk and Arch Fit sections are present** — both categories I cross-shop; having them in the same scroll means I don't have to dig
- **"New & For You" men's section** — says "Men's Active and Casual," shows actual men's shoes; feels like someone remembered I exist
- **No visible layout breakage inside the email** — renders clean, images load, text isn't overlapping

## What's weak

- **Preview text is completely broken** — what showed in my inbox was raw JSON schema code, not a human word; that's a bad first impression and nearly cost them the open
- **Hero section features a female model** — "Most Searched: Most Loved" with a woman in athletic gear is not me; the May 10 Mother's Day email made the same mistake and I called it out then
- **Subject promises personalization it doesn't deliver** — "You Searched, We Delivered" raised my expectation of seeing my actual searches or cart items; I got a generic catalog instead
- **25% Off Apparel is dead weight for me** — I don't buy clothing from Skechers; that whole section is real estate I'm scrolling past
- **No "Hi Walker" or loyalty points visible** — the May 11 birthday email and the May 8 cart reminder both greeted me by name; this one treated me like a stranger

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — that JSON leak is a technical error; it should be the single highest-priority fix before the next send. Replace it with real copy that complements the subject.
- 2. **Make the hero male, or at least neutral** — put a men's slip-on or walking shoe as the hero image; I'm not the customer in that first photo and my eyes tell me so instantly.
- 3. **Back up the subject with actual personalization** — if the subject says "You Searched," show me what I searched. Pull the cart item, the browsed product, or at least the category I've opened before. The May 8 cart recovery email did this well.
- 4. **Cut the apparel section or push it to the very bottom** — lead with shoes, end with shoes; apparel as a third-from-bottom block makes it easy to skip but still wastes scroll depth.
- 5. **Add a deadline to the BOGO** — "Ends Sunday" or "3 days left" would have pushed me to click right then instead of bookmarking it mentally and forgetting.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, your searched shoes are waiting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `BOGO 50% — the styles you've been eyeing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands Free Slip-Ins, Go Walk, Arch Fit — all on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy one, get one 50% off. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Skechers "You Searched, We Delivered" (2026-05-15)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One broken-experience failure: the greeting merge token rendered empty, producing a malformed salutation. Additionally, all hosted images are served over HTTP rather than HTTPS, creating a widespread image-blocking risk in modern email clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**27 click links** all route through `click.emails.skechers.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect). UTM parameters on destination URLs cannot be verified without resolving the redirects; this should be spot-checked against the landing page source.

**Open pixel** — served over HTTP:
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NONPURCLICK_U_MULTI_NONPURCH_EN_05152026
```
Protocol mismatch will cause suppression in clients that enforce mixed-content policies (Gmail, Outlook 365).

**Krxd third-party beacons** (3 pixels) fire on open for audience matching and ad impression attribution:
```
beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25
beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid
beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif?confid=so3yexmpm&campaignid=MKG_US_NONPURCLICK_U_MULTI_NONPURCH_EN_05152026
```
These pass a hashed subscriber ID (`partner_uid`) and a raw subscriber ID (`121173391`) to a third-party domain. Confirm this is disclosed in the privacy policy and compliant with applicable regulations.

**Return Path inbox monitoring pixel** present:
```
pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499
```
Protocol not explicit in the source fragment; verify it resolves over HTTPS.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL — Broken experience]** The greeting contains an unresolved name token, producing:

```
Hi ,
```

The subscriber name field was empty at send time or the merge tag fallback was not configured. This affects deliverability perception and trust. A safe fallback (e.g., `Hi there,` or `Hi Skechers Fan,`) must be set in the ESP template before the name token.

---

### 5. Compliance

HTML is truncated; the following must be verified against the full source:

- **CAN-SPAM physical address**: confirm a postal address is present in the footer.
- **Unsubscribe link**: confirm a one-click or clearly labeled unsubscribe mechanism is present and functional.
- **Third-party data sharing (Krxd)**: the `sha25` pixel passes a hashed email (`partner_uid=9153f33fe5cdf59baf088bb835d3b39ef90f188bd8540a4faba92899227ced00`) to Krxd. Ensure this is covered by the subscriber's consent and privacy policy disclosure.

No issues confirmed in the visible source, but the above must be spot-checked in the full render.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `MKG_US_NONPURCLICK_U_MULTI_NONPURCH_EN_05152026` appears consistently in both the Krxd impression pixel and the ink1000 open pixel — internal attribution appears consistent.

UTM parameter pass-through on click links cannot be confirmed because all 27 destination URLs are behind the `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect domain. Spot-check at least the primary CTA to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` are appended to landing page URLs.

Preheader text ("Most-Wanted Styles are BOGO 50% Off") is functionally independent from the subject line ("You Searched, We Delivered") — not a technical issue, but worth confirming the offer referenced in the preheader is present on the landing page.
