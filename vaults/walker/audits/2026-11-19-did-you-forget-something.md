# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- The skull sneaker again. It's the black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" I've been nagged about on 11/05, 11/08, three times on 11/11, and in "Your Items Are Waiting!" earlier today. I have never put it in a cart. Seeing it blown up in the middle of this one made me roll my eyes. "Ready to check out?" is a polite nudge, but it's the fifth or sixth time I've heard it.
- The bottom half is better. The red 30% OFF early access banner is the first thing here that would make me spend money, and the Slip-ins boots and the Contour Foam sneaker are the kind of shoes I'd look at. But the banner sits below the skull and a long run of product tiles, so I had to scroll a long way to reach it. I'd skim it, maybe tap the boots, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The red "30% OFF sitewide for members" banner is clear and easy to read, and it's the one real reason to act.
- The Slip-ins boots and the Contour Foam Cozy Fit sneaker are hands-free and cushioned, which is what I shop for.
- The product names are plain and tell me what I'm getting, like "Waterproof Relaxed Fit" and "Leather".
- The big Men / Women / Kids list is easy to tap with a thumb, and the text is large enough that I don't have to zoom.
- Listing the dates, 11/18 to 11/22, makes the deadline feel believable.

## What's weak

- The skull sneaker is a hero I've never shown interest in, and I've seen it too many times. It makes the whole send feel automated.
- The sale is buried under the hero, two buttons and six products. A 30% sale should lead the email.
- Two "Complete Your Order" buttons that go to the same place feel pushy.
- The product tiles are small, with tiny caption text and no prices, ratings or sizes. I can't tell whether a boot is worth it.
- The fine print says "Skechers Plus members" and "must be logged in," so I can't tell if I qualify. That makes the 30% feel less honest.
- Women's boots are mixed in with the men's, and nothing compares comfort or price against Hoka or Brooks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 30% off banner first, and if I'm a member, say so. Retire the skull sneaker after this many sends and show what I actually browsed, or men's Slip-ins.
- 2. Add prices, with the sale price beside the original, and a star rating under each product.
- 3. Use one button, such as "Shop Men's Slip-ins," not two identical ones.
- 4. Cut the women's styles from my send and add a line about arch support or cushioning.
- 5. Say plainly in the body whether I'm a Plus member and how to qualify.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your members' 30% is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's Slip-ins and waterproof boots, 30% off for members through 11/22.`
