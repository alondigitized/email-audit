## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based layout, but QA scored it at 62% pass. The main problems are a failing Unsubscribe link probe (HTTP 400), an empty plain-text part, and 13 `http://` image sources. The HTML source was truncated, so footer content and headers could not be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe returned 400 (FAIL).** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1MmZpSFY0ajRYThID…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r…`. The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. A 400 may mean the one-time token was consumed or the endpoint rejects non-browser requests. Either way, treat it as a possible dead unsubscribe path until verified manually.
- **Rate-limited probes (429, inconclusive).** These were the "Complete you Order" CTA and the "We noticed you noticing us… Your cart is still waiting…" link. Both are `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirects. This is not a confirmed break, but the two primary cart CTAs were not validated.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 43 links were probed; 31 were skipped.
- **Opaque click tracking.** All observed links, including the "web version" link, go through Attentive's `e/c/` redirector, so destinations cannot be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters (FAIL).** There is no text/plain alternative. This hurts text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks, and it is a spam-filter signal.
- **13 images use `http://`, from `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`.** They include the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the cart image (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), the app-store badges, Afterpay, Find a Store and the social icons. Some clients may block or warn on mixed content. The same host already serves `https://` for `m/21/021bd784-…gif`, so HTTPS is available.
