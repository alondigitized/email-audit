# EXTRA 10% off clearance 🎉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Well, "extra 10% off clearance" — that's DSW's fourth or fifth different way of saying the exact same thing this week. I opened it because the subject had a real number in it for once, but the second I hit that 12-shoe clearance grid I'm doing math in my head: loafer, sneaker, sneaker, sneaker, sneaker, sneaker, sneaker, heel, heel, leopard bootie, heel, silver sneaker. That's actually not bad — six or seven of those are honest-to-goodness sneakers, including a New Balance-looking one I'd tap on if I were browsing in person.
- Problem is the offer itself is confusing before I even get to the shoes — "10% off online, 30% off in stores," with a code I have to remember. My brain doesn't do mental math on stacked clearance percentages, I just want the dollar number on the shoe. And "always 30% off in stores" sitting right next to "extra 10% off online" makes we wonder why I'd even bother clicking through on my phone instead of just driving to the store.
- Bottom line: I'd scroll through it, maybe tap on that New Balance sneaker tile out of curiosity, but I'm not clicking "Shop All Clearance" cold. Not forwarding this one — nothing here says "Walker, here's your shoe."

## What's working

- The sneaker count is actually decent this time — five or six real athletic/walking shoes in that grid, better ratio than the last few DSW sends I've gotten.
- "EXTRA 10% OFF" is a real number up top, not vague "clearance" language — I'll give them that.
- Big bold "SHOP ALL CLEARANCE" button is easy to find and tap with my thumb.
- Shop-by-size grid near the bottom is genuinely useful — I know my size, I don't have to guess if they carry it.

## What's weak

- Stacking "10% off online + 30% off in stores" plus a promo code is too much mental math for a clearance email — just tell me the price.
- Half the grid is still women's heels, slingbacks, and a leopard bootie — none of that is for me, and it's mixed right in with the sneakers instead of being separated.
- No product names, sizes, or prices shown on any of the tiles — I can't tell if that gray sneaker is even in an 11 until I click through.
- "No, you're not dreaming... THESE ARE ALLLLL on clearance" is trying too hard — feels like a teenager wrote it, not my speed.
- Nothing here calls out men's or walking-specific shoes — it's just "clearance," so I'm doing the filtering work myself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the grid or add a "SHOP MEN'S SNEAKERS" button right under the hero — let guys like me jump straight past the heels.
- 2. Drop the stacked-percentage math and show one clear price or "extra 10% off, already-marked-down prices" in plain language.
- 3. Put actual prices or "starting at $X" under at least the sneaker tiles — I want to see the number before I click.
- 4. Cut the "no, you're not dreaming" copy — a plain "clearance just got deeper" fits a 62-year-old shopper better.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 10% off clearance sneakers — starting at $29`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Men's sneakers just got an extra 10% off`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance sneakers, now even cheaper with code GOLDEN`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size, your sneaker, extra 10% off today`
