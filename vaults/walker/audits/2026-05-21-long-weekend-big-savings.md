# Long Weekend, Big Savings
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we are again — Skechers hitting my inbox with another preview full of curly brackets and URL gibberish. I've flagged this broken preview text in four or five of their recent sends, and they still haven't fixed it. By the time I decided to open this one, it wasn't because the subject hooked me or the preview teased something — it was pure brand familiarity and the fact that "Long Weekend" told me a Memorial Day sale was probably inside.
- Once I got in, the picture cleared up fast. BOGO 50% off, clean hero, that red slip-on front and center — okay, now we're talking. I know that shoe style. It's the kind of thing I'd try on at DSW on a Saturday. The Skechers Plus $5 reward for $75+ is a nice nudge for someone already on the fence about spending a little more.
- That said, nowhere in this email does it mention comfort, cushioning, arch support, or anything about how that shoe actually feels on my foot. I'm not buying footwear for how it photographs — I'm buying it because my knees thank me after a two-mile walk. I'd open the Men's section and start clicking around, but the email itself didn't do the heavy lifting of convincing me. Mark as read and maybe come back later. Not a forward.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off is immediately visible** — no hunting, no squinting. That's the kind of offer that stops the scroll.
- **"OR 20% off a single pair"** gives me a fallback if I only want one shoe, which I appreciate. Flexibility matters.
- **The red slip-on in the hero** looks like a Hands Free or slip-in style — that's in my wheelhouse. Just putting it front and center is enough to get my attention.
- **"SHOP MEN'S" is its own button**, not buried in fine print. I don't have to dig through Women's and Kids' to find what I want.
- **Skechers Plus reward is called out** — $5 for a $75 purchase reminds me I'm a member and makes the math feel slightly better.

## What's weak

- **That preview text is broken again** — fourth or fifth time I've seen JSON schema code leaking into my inbox from Skechers. At this point it's embarrassing for a brand this size. I almost swiped it away on reflex.
- **No comfort language anywhere in the email** — no cushioning, no arch support, no mention of why this shoe is good for a guy who's on his feet all day. For me that's the whole decision.
- **No sale end date visible** — "Long Weekend" tells me it's Memorial Day, but I don't know if I have until Sunday night or Tuesday midnight. That vagueness makes me less likely to act today.
- **The apparel section feels like filler** — 25% off clothing is not why I opened this. That block competes with the shoe offer without adding anything for me.
- **Models skew young and athletic** — nothing in the imagery reflects someone my age or body type. That's a miss when Skechers' comfort line has a strong 55+ customer base.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text.** This is the single biggest lift available. It's been broken for weeks across multiple sends. Real copy there — even just "BOGO 50% off ends Monday — Men's styles included" — would meaningfully improve open rates.
- 2. **Add one comfort proof point to the hero or near the featured shoe.** Something like "Memory foam insole" or "Hands Free Slip-ins" next to that red shoe image. That's what closes the deal for me, not the photo.
- 3. **Put an explicit deadline in the email body.** "Offer ends Monday, May 26" removes the excuse to come back later — and maybe forget.
- 4. **Pare down or move the apparel block.** If it has to be there, push it below the fold. The shoe offer should own the entire visible area on my phone screen.
- 5. **Age up the imagery at least once** — a lifestyle shot with someone who looks like he's walking a trail at 62 rather than running a 5K at 28 would make me feel like this brand is actually talking to me.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's Hands Free styles and more — sale ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `50% off your second pair + $5 reward for Plus members`

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken personalization token will render a blank greeting for all recipients. Additionally, all image assets are served over HTTP, creating a widespread mixed-content vulnerability that will cause images to be blocked in security-conscious email clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination links confirmed** — 27 click-redirect links through `click.emails.skechers.com` were not probed (expected for redirect domains).

**Third-party pixel exposure:** Three distinct tracking domains are loaded in the preheader tracking block:
- `beacon.krxd.net` (Salesforce Audience Studio / Krux) — two usermatch pixels + one ad impression pixel
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net` — deliverability/inbox monitoring pixel
- `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/...` — additional open tracking pixel (HTTP — see §3)

The `beacon.krxd.net` ad impression pixel encodes the campaign ID in plaintext: `PRO_US_NONPURCLICK_U_SITEBUNDLE_NONPURCH_EN_05212026`, which leaks send-date, targeting tier, and locale in the URL.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] Mixed-content / HTTP image sources — affects 13+ assets**

Every image served from `image.emails.skechers.com` uses `http://` instead of `https://`. Affected assets include the Skechers logo, app store badges, footer icons (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), and feature images:

```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png  (logo)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-...png
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-...png  (App Store)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/9bb508d0-...png  (Google Play)
...and 9 additional footer/icon images
```

The open-tracking pixel at `http://www.ink1000.com/...` is also HTTP-only. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy or block non-HTTPS image loads; recipients will see broken image placeholders across the entire email.

**[WARN] Missing alt text — 2 images**

- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — no `alt` attribute
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — no `alt` attribute; unknown content, but if it is a content image this is an accessibility gap

`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` is applied globally via `* {}`. This suppresses the user's font-size accessibility preference on iOS Safari — use `100%` instead of `none`.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] Empty greeting slot**

QA confirms: the greeting block renders as **"Hi ,"** — the first-name merge token resolved to an empty string. This is a data/template pipeline failure (missing fallback value). The fix is a conditional default, e.g.:

```
Hi %%=IF(EMPTY([FirstName]) "there" [FirstName])=%%,
```

(or equivalent in SFMC AMPscript/Handlebars depending on the ESP template engine in use.)

No other unresolved merge tokens were detected in the truncated source.
