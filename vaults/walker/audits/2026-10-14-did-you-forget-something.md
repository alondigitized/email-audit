# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the same cart nag again, and it's the third "come back and finish your order" send I've gotten since the 13th. The big gold "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner is a little much for something I never started. The hero shoe is the black D'Lites "Spring Bloom" with the flower art on the side. It isn't the pink one from the other emails, but it's still a women's-leaning style I never put in a cart.
- The product grid under it doesn't make sense for me. I count four women's shoes, two boy's sneakers with toy cars on them, and one men's waterproof slip-in boot. I'm a 62-year-old guy, and the boy's sneakers say "you're a stranger" to me. The 20% off Skechers Plus banner is the only thing that sounds like a reason to act, and it sits below all the shoes.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd tap the men's slip-in boot if I were bored, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone. It gets marked as read.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF (ALMOST) EVERYTHING ELSE" banner is large and easy to read on my phone, and the dollar-and-percent math is simple.
- The Men's Slip-ins John Deere Waterproof boot is the one item that speaks to hands-free comfort, and the "Slip-ins" wording caught my eye.
- The black SKECHERS logo and the layout match what I'd expect from this brand, so I trust that it's real.
- The nav list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is big, plain text that's easy to tap with a thumb.
- The "Valid thru 10/21" date gives me a real deadline.

## What's weak

- I never put a flower sneaker in a cart. Calling it "Ready to check out?" feels off and a bit pushy.
- Boy's sneakers and women's shoes fill most of the grid. Only one shoe out of seven is for a man.
- The discount is buried below six products. I scrolled a long way before finding the one thing that mattered.
- The product captions are tiny. I'd have to zoom in to read the shoe names.
- There are two "Complete Your Order" buttons, and one is a black bar that blends into the banner. It's also the third cart nag in two days, after "Your Items Are Waiting!" and "Your cart expires soon!".
- The fine print about exclusions is dense and in small type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% member offer at the top, above the hero, and tie it to the cart: "Your cart + 20% off, ends 10/21."
- 2. Show only items I actually looked at, or fill the rest with men's slip-ins and walking shoes instead of kids' styles.
- 3. Space out the cart reminders. One email a day is plenty, and three in a row is too much.
- 4. Add price, cushioning and arch-support details next to the products so I can compare them with Hoka or Brooks.
- 5. Make the product captions larger and use one clear orange button.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, 20% off your cart through 10/21`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on your saved styles. Offer ends 10/21.`

The `attn_macro` value base64-decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. That is the Attentive macro name, not a resolved URL.
