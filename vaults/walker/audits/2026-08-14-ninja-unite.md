# Ninja unite!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, my grandkids would probably lose their minds over this, but it's not for me. This is a LEGO Ninjago clog with little minifigure Jibbitz clipped all over it — some kid jumping around in a green light-swirl like he's in a laser tag ad. I get that Crocs has to sell to families too, but this landed in my inbox same as every other Crocs email, and there's nothing here telling me it's a kids' line versus something I'd wear.
- The clog itself — black, chunky, with the ninja charms — actually isn't a bad-looking shoe shape if you strip off all the toys, but that's not the point they're making. No mention of sizing, no arch support talk, nothing about comfort for adult feet, just "the power of spin is at your feet." I'm not spinning anywhere, I'm walking the dog and standing at the hardware store counter.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only if I had a grandson into Ninjago, and even then I'd probably just tell him about it rather than forward a marketing email. Straight to read-and-delete.

## What's working

- The clog photography is sharp and the neon-green motion graphics are eye-catching, I'll give them that.
- "Comfort Goes Undefeated" headline at least gestures toward comfort, which is the one thing that could've hooked me if they'd followed through.
- Four clear "Shop" buttons at the bottom, including a "Shop Sale" option, so navigation itself isn't confusing.
- Clean sans-serif text, nothing squinty or too small to read.

## What's weak

- This is a kids' novelty collab through and through — minifigures, a guy leaping around like a ninja — nothing here says "grown man looking for a comfortable everyday shoe."
- No price, no percent off, no dollar figure anywhere in the whole email — just like the "Shades That Pop" and mascot emails I've seen from them lately, all mood and no math.
- Zero mention of cushioning, arch support, or actual fit — the "Comfort Goes Undefeated" line is just a tagline, there's no substance behind it.
- No adult model wearing the shoe walking around — just charms glued onto a clog on a table, so I can't picture it on an actual foot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this one — send the Ninjago charms push to the parents/kids list and keep my inbox for the actual comfort-forward Crocs lines like the clog from 2026-08-12's "Styles for Resting Like a Pro" send, which at least had an outsole diagram.
- 2. Put a real offer in the subject and hero — a percent off or bundle price, same fix I asked for on the "Sale is on sale" email that actually worked.
- 3. If they insist on sending me collab emails, show it on an adult foot, standing, with a one-line comfort spec (cushioning, arch support) instead of just charms and light trails.
- 4. Drop the "Ninja Unite!" cutesy CTA button copy for something concrete like "Shop Ninjago Clogs."
