- "Prepare for takeoff" is big, black and readable on my phone without squinting.
- The red shoe in the hero is clean and easy to see, and the lime box makes the button stand out.
- The four-shoe grid labels each pair with a name and Men or Women, so I can find the men's shoes fast.
- The Bondi 7 is in there, and it's the closest thing to my kind of shoe.
- The footer has clear links for "Locate a Store" and a 30-day guarantee, which makes me trust it more.

## What's weak

- The headline and body copy don't say what's new or why I should care. "Feel more like flying" means nothing to a guy who walks the dog.
- There's no price, no discount and no deadline anywhere up top. The free shipping is buried at the bottom with a asterisk and small print.
- The Grit Pack and the puddle-splash shoe are trail and running again. My feet go to the mailbox, not a mountain.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-on ease, and no reviews either.
- The "Free Expedited Shipping*" offer needs a text signup and is for first-time orders only, so it's no use to me as a longtime subscriber. The footer text is tiny and hard to read.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links, including unsubscribe, return 406/403 to the automated probe, so their end-to-end behavior is unconfirmed. The message has no plain-text part and no alt text on any content image. SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` status could not be verified from the data given.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 of 12 probed links returned 406 and the unsubscribe link returned 403.** All go through the Cordial click-tracker (`https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:.../<id>?jwtH=...&jwtP=...&jwtS=...`).
- **This is most likely bot-blocking, not a confirmed user-facing failure.** Every failing URL shares the same host, path pattern and JWT structure, and a uniform 406/403 across unrelated destinations points to the tracker rejecting the probe's client signature. I can't confirm real-client behavior from this data.
- **The JWT payloads decode to valid destinations.** For example, the header link resolves to `https://www.hoka.com/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&...`. Destination reachability was therefore never tested.
- **14 links were skipped.** Footer, utility and social links were not probed.
- **Every tracked URL carries `hmail=9153f33f...` (a 64-hex hash) and `cordial_id=69e56048...`.** These identify the recipient, so forwarded emails or shared links carry that identifier. This is an observation, not a defect.
- **Two tracking pixels are present:** `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`. They are not a problem, but they mean two sending/tracking domains are in play.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **All 12 content images have no `alt` attribute.** This includes the header `US_HOKAHeader_Black.jpg`, the hero images `US_20261013_NewArrivals_a`–`e.png`, the four product tiles (Arahi 9, Clifton 11 GTX, Challenger 8, Bondi 7), the SMS CTA banner `US_Q3_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg` and the footer `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
- **Missing alt on linked images is the worst case.** The QA tool reports link text as `[image: US_Arahi9_TopLeft_1176570-PGM.png]`, so the links have no accessible name. With images blocked, the email is a blank column of broken-image boxes.
- **The two tracking pixels should have `alt=""`.** Missing alt there is harmless.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **The charset meta is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute.
- **There are two conflicting viewport metas.** One has `maximum-scale=1` and the other adds `user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` can block pinch-zoom.
- **Template variables were left unpopulated in the CSS.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` are invalid declarations. They are ignored by clients and look like unfilled placeholders.
- **`* {outline:none; border:none; ...}` removes default focus outlines.** This hurts keyboard navigation in clients that support focus.
- **Custom `@font-face` is loaded from `hoka.com`.** Clients that don't support web fonts will fall back, and no fallback stack is visible in the truncated source.
- **The HTML is truncated, so body-level checks aren't possible.** I could not check table roles, `lang` on nested content or the dark-mode CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source or QA output.**
- **The empty CSS values in section 3 are the only leftover-template artifacts found.**
- **Personalization is carried in the URLs** (`hmail`, `cordial_id`) and not in visible content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing:** "Text version is 0 chars." A text alternative is standard practice, and its absence can hurt deliverability and hurts accessibility.
- **Authentication could not be verified.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, and the QA note says it was "Expected via AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM status unknown." This is a tooling gap, not evidence of failure. Sender `info@emails.hoka.com` and the tracking hosts `e.` and `mg.emails.hoka.com` share the `emails.hoka.com` subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending.
- **The unsubscribe link exists but returned 403 to the probe.** It resolves to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e56048...`, so a working in-body unsubscribe is plausible but unverified. It is a 403 and not a 404 or 5xx, which again suggests bot-blocking.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided**, so one-click unsubscribe compliance can't be assessed.
- **A physical postal address was not visible in the truncated source.** I can't confirm or deny one in the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are consistent on every decoded link:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261017_NewArrivals_STO_A120Day_365NewArrivalsTag_270NewColorsTag_NewArrivalsPLPBrowsers_AIRFMHigh`.
- **Destinations match the creative's intent:**
  - The hero images link to `/en/us/new-arrivals/` and `/en/us/new-colors/`.
  - The product tiles link to PDPs with variant parameters, such as `.../arahi-9/1176570.html?dwvar_1176570_color=PGM&dwvar_1176570_width=Regular&dwvar_1176570_size=7`.
  - The footer returns link goes to `/en/us/return-start/`.
- **Destination availability and PDP stock were not verified**, because the probes never reached the final URLs.
- **Minor observation:** the image filenames use `20261013` while the campaign tag uses `20261017`. This is probably a creative-prep date versus send date, and it is not an error.
- **`utm_campaign` is a long (~180-character) concatenated string.** It works, but it is fragile for analytics parsing.
- **There is no `utm_content` per link.** The hero and tile clicks can't be told apart in analytics beyond the destination URL.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the tracked links with a real-browser user agent or the Cordial test-send tooling.** The 11 × 406 and 1 × 403 results are probably bot-blocking, so confirm that before escalating. The destination URLs in the JWT payloads can also be probed directly.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to all 12 content images.** Use `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels. This matters most for the linked hero and product tiles.
3. **Add a text/plain MIME part** to this campaign's Cordial template.
4. **Fix the template placeholders** in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border`, and populate or remove those declarations.
5. **Add a meaningful `<title>` and a valid charset declaration** (`<meta charset="utf-8">`). Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. **Capture the full raw headers** (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) in the QA pipeline so section 5 can be assessed. This is a gap in our tooling, not in the sender.
7. **Consider adding `utm_content` per module** if link-level attribution matters to the sender.
