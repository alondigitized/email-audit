# Friend, start your week on the right foot!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this is a lot of email for one shoe. "Friend" in the subject tells me nobody knows who I am. The big yellow "Start Your Week on the Right Foot" banner with a woman in tall boots isn't aimed at me. The headline is easy to read, but the picture and the first product rows are women's boots, women's slippers and kids' Nikes.
- I did find a few things I'd shop for further down. The "Hit Your Step Goal" section has Men's Skechers slip-ins and a Max Cushion running shoe, and that's the kind of hands-free comfort I look for. But I had to scroll past three screens to reach them. The orange "Buy More, Save More" coupon block at the bottom is the same one I've been seeing all week, so I know it by heart. I'd skim it, maybe tap the Skechers, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Hit Your Step Goal" row has Men's Skechers slip-ins and Max Cushion shoes. That's my kind of shoe.
- The $10 off $74.98 or $25 off $149.98 coupon is big, plain and easy to read without my glasses.
- Product names sit right under each photo and are in readable type. I like that the prices and "On Sale" tags are called out.
- The Shoe Perks $10 reward and the "Add to mobile wallet" coupon are handy if I'm standing in the store.
- The email is fall-appropriate and matches the boots-and-comfort season. It has no broken images that I can see.

## What's weak

- "Friend" in the subject feels like a form letter. It doesn't treat me like a known customer.
- The hero is a woman in boots, and most of the first two rows are women's or kids' shoes. Only a couple of men's pairs show up, and the Adidas Evo Blaze isn't really a comfort shoe.
- The preview text is just the brand name and a long link. That's wasted space in my inbox.
- The email is very long, with four different offers at the bottom: coupon, wallet, SMS and Afterpay. The headline offer gets buried, and I can't tell which one to pick.
- The coupon block is the same one I've seen all week, so it feels stale. I also had to scroll past it and the barcodes to find the fine print and the 10/31 end date.
- The "Shop Newness" and "Shop Trending" links are small text, hard for my thumb on a phone.
- There's nothing on arch support, cushioning or walking. The bottom Perks and Afterpay blocks don't help me decide on a shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the $10/$25 coupon right under the hero, with one big button, and cut the SMS, Afterpay and wallet blocks from this send.
- 2. Lead with a men's comfort shoe, such as the Skechers slip-ins, in the hero instead of women's boots. Show cushioning and arch support in the copy.
- 3. Use my real first name, or drop the greeting. "Friend" feels worse than nothing.
- 4. Write a real preheader that states the offer and the deadline, instead of leaking the brand name and link.
- 5. Make the "Shop Athletics" links full-width buttons so they're easy to tap on a phone.
- 6. Put the end date (10/31) next to the coupon, not in the fine print.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard for a responsive ESP template, with a few confirmed defects. The tracking-pixel URLs are malformed, and two `<meta>` tags are missing `http-equiv`. The HTML source is truncated and SPF/DKIM could not be checked, so the body, footer and headers are only partly verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved placeholder in a pixel URL:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still has the literal `[UNIQUE]` in the `d=` parameter. The per-recipient token was not substituted, so opens for this pixel can't be attributed to a recipient.
- **Malformed UTM in a pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261019_start_your_week_AM` nests a second `=` inside the value of `t=`. It also ends with a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. That character corrupts the campaign value if the URL is parsed or logged literally.
- **54 tracking links were not probed.** All of them go through the `emltrk.com` click-redirect domain, so destination status and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** QA reports 10,549 of 13,067 characters (about 81%) are URLs. The long wrapped tracking links dominate the text part and can raise spam-filter scores.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on both images flagged by QA:** both are `emltrk.com` open-tracking pixels. Impact is low if they are 1×1 and hidden, but add `alt=""` to avoid screen-reader noise.
- **Two `<meta>` tags without `http-equiv`:** `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />`. The `IE=edge` tag is inert without `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, and the Content-Type tag only works through its `charset` attribute.
- **Mixed doctype and syntax:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the root has `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the tags are self-closed XHTML-style. It is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Link styling removed globally:** `a, a:link, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important }` strips underline and colour from every link. Links then depend entirely on surrounding styles to be identifiable, which is an accessibility concern for text links. I couldn't confirm which links are text-only in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Likely fallback value in the subject:** "Friend, start your week on the right foot!" looks like a default for a missing first name. This is inferred, since the recipient profile isn't visible.
- **Unresolved token in the body:** `[UNIQUE]` in the pixel URL (see section 2) is a confirmed unrendered merge placeholder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown rather than failing. It can't be treated as a sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` header and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source or QA data, so unverified. No issues can be claimed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only UTM seen is `utm_campaign=SCA_20261019_start_your_week_AM`, and it is malformed (see section 2). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or other parameters appear in the visible source.
- Because all 54 links are wrapped in `emltrk.com` redirects and were not probed, UTM propagation and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[UNIQUE]` substitution in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel URL.
2. Remove the zero-width space and the nested `=` from the `t=utm_campaign=…` pixel URL. URL-encode the value, or use `&utm_campaign=…`.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Correct the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Shorten the plain-text part, for example with a shorter redirect domain or fewer wrapped links, to bring URL density down from about 81%.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. Probe a sample of redirects, and capture the raw headers to verify `List-Unsubscribe` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC outside the AgentMail relay.
