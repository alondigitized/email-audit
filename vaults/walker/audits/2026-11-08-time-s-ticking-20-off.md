# Time’s ticking… 20% OFF!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the seventh 20% TINSEL pitch since the 5th, so "Time's ticking" doesn't scare me. I got "LAST DAY for 20% OFF!" and "ENDS TONIGHT" earlier today, and the code hasn't changed. The "20% OFF*" on pink is huge and easy to read on my phone, I'll give them that. But I scrolled past a free butter squishy and a lot of heels before I saw anything for a man.
- The shoes in "So New, So You" are a loafer, a slingback heel, three boots and sneakers, and a slipper-ish mule. Almost none of it is for me. The "Shop Men" link is a small underlined line, easy to miss. Nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-ons.
- Would I click? Maybe "Shop Men" if I needed shoes. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" headline is big, with the TINSEL code right under it. I can read it without zooming.
- The "Claim 20% Off" button is black on pink and easy to tap.
- The Shop Women / Men / Kids links let me skip straight to my section.
- The Shop by Size block with Wide Width is useful for a guy with wide feet.
- The extra 10% for veterans and active military is a nice touch for those who qualify.

## What's weak

- I've had this same 20% offer all week. "Time's ticking" is the third fake-feeling deadline in two days.
- The hero and the product grid lean women's. I count one loafer that might suit me.
- The "Shop Men" link is tiny and underlined, which is a hard tap target on a phone.
- The butter squishy ("Selling fast! Butter get yours ASAP") is a gimmick for a $75 purchase. It's in-store only and has nothing to do with comfort.
- The product tiles say "Extra 20% off reg. price!" with no prices and no comfort details. The fine print is a wall of tiny text.
- There's no mention of comfort, cushioning or arch support, and no comparison to Hoka, Brooks or New Balance.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same offer daily. Send fewer emails and tie each one to a real new reason.
- 2. Use my purchase history. Show men's comfort and slip-on styles in the grid, not heels.
- 3. Make "Shop Men" a real button, the same size as "Claim 20% Off".
- 4. Put price and comfort details under each shoe, such as cushioned insole, arch support, slip-on and price.
- 5. Move the squishy promo below the fold, or cut it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Comfort picks for men, 20% off with TINSEL`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Slip-ons and cushioned walkers, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code TINSEL works on Skechers, Hoka-style cushion and more through Wednesday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's walking shoes with arch support, now 20% off`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one failing tracked link, the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA, which returned HTTP 400. Several accessibility and markup problems are visible in the truncated source. SPF, DKIM and DMARC could not be verified, and most of the HTML and 62 of 74 links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA (400):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/AjFot_P96o3w-…~~`. The 400 could come from an expired or malformed tracking token, or from the click-tracker rejecting the QA probe. The QA data doesn't say which. Re-test it with a real client or browser before treating it as a user-facing failure. Note that the QA report shows a blank failure message after "(400):".
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 links were probed and 62 were skipped (footer, social and utility links). The other links are unverified.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 26,747 of 33,003 characters (about 81%) in the plain-text part are URLs, mostly long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking redirects. That ratio can hurt spam scoring and makes the text part unusable for readers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv`. Both declarations are ineffective. Charset is normally set by the MIME header, so the impact is low, but the tags are invalid.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing alt text, linked image:** The image whose `src` is `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/jRO4ATV50oPYW4xHDbbAmQ~~/…` has no `alt` attribute. If it is a linked image, it is unlabeled for screen readers and for images-off clients.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…` has no `alt`. It should be `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is non-descriptive. This is minor.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags and a `prefers-color-scheme` block are present. No issues are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The tail of the HTML was truncated, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** This is a limitation of the AgentMail relay, not a confirmed defect in the email. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` are unverified. Check the raw headers at the receiving mailbox.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none was reported failing. The footer is beyond the truncation point, so I could not confirm the physical postal address or a `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every link is wrapped by `ablink.dsw.com`, with the destination encoded in the token. UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be seen from the source, so they are unverified.
- The pixel URL carries a campaign ID, `mi_ecmp=20261108_P261640_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` (truncated). That is useful for attribution, but nothing could be checked against the landing pages.
- The one broken link is the SMS opt-in CTA, so that conversion path is not confirmed to work.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in tracked URL outside the QA prober. If it still returns 400, regenerate the link in the ESP.
2. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags, or remove them.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
5. Shorten the plain-text URLs, or generate a text part with shorter or unwrapped links, to cut the 81% URL ratio.
6. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the raw message headers, and confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` headers and the footer address.
7. Run the QA probe on the 62 skipped links, or at least a sample, and follow the redirects to confirm UTMs and landing pages.
