# Up to 60% Off? Yes Please.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again, and this time the deal finally changed. After a week of "2 for $50," I get "Up to 60% Off" and "Classic Clogs starting at $30." The "60% OFF" is big and white on dark maroon, so I can read it on my phone without squinting. But "Up to" and "Exclusions Apply" sit right under the button, so I don't know what's actually 60% off.
- The $30 clog and the $25 cozy lined clog are real numbers I can do math on. Still, this is clogs and holiday ribbon. I see no arch support, no walking shoe and no men's anything. The Black Friday timing is credible, but I've had this brand in my inbox for two weeks straight.
- Would I click? Maybe the cozy section, since a lined slip-on for $25 is hard to argue with. I wouldn't forward it. I'd read it and move on.

## What's working

- The "60% OFF" headline is huge and easy to read on a phone.
- "Classic clogs starting at $30" and "cozy styles start at just $25" are real prices I can compare against Hoka and Brooks.
- The lined green clog is the one thing here that looks like what I'd actually wear around the house.
- The buttons are big, black and well spaced, so my thumb won't miss them.
- The email dropped "2 for $50" and gave me a new offer, which is a welcome change from the last week of sends.
- Free shipping is in the top banner.

## What's weak

- "Up to 60%" with "Exclusions Apply" tucked under the button doesn't tell me which shoes get 60%.
- Two nearly identical black buttons in each section, "Shop Classic Clog Deals" and "Shop All Doorbusters," feel repetitive.
- Nothing speaks to comfort, cushioning or arch support. It's all gift ribbons and colour.
- The fine print at the bottom is small and hard to read. It's also where I find out the offer ends November 29 and can't be combined with other offers.
- The bright red clogs and pink background look young and trendy, not like me.
- This is another near-daily send, after 11/15's "Black Friday Deals Start Now!" and its "Early Access Ends Tonight!" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name what is 60% off, or put a real example in the hero ("Classic Clog $30, was $50") so "up to" isn't a mystery.
- 2. Move the deadline, "Ends Nov 29," out of the fine print and up near the hero.
- 3. Add a comfort angle for people like me: cushioning, arch support, all-day wear, men's styles and slip-ons for walking.
- 4. Collapse the doubled buttons into one clear button per section.
- 5. Slow down the send cadence. Two emails a day makes me tune out.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a well-structured responsive email with an SFMC open pixel, `lang="en"`, and standard client resets. The HTML was truncated before the body content, so links, footer, unsubscribe and UTM parameters could not be verified. QA found 0 failures and 2 warnings: unverifiable authentication headers and one alt-less tracking pixel.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **19 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as "Tracking/click-redirect domains", most likely `click.crocs-email.com`. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **The Adobe Audience Manager pixel has no `alt`.** The QA warning names `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`. Because it has no `alt`, clients with images blocked may show a broken-image placeholder or the raw URL.
- **The Adobe pixel also embeds a per-recipient identifier.** The `d_dpuid` value is a long per-recipient token. Pixels from third-party domains such as `demdex.net` are often blocked, so open tracking through this one is unreliable.
- **The SFMC open pixel is fine.** `click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?...` is 1×1, has `alt=""`, and sits in a `height:1px;overflow:hidden` wrapper.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The charset and X-UA-Compatible declarations are inert.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />`, both without `http-equiv`. Without that attribute, neither declaration does anything.
  - This is low risk, because the MIME `Content-Type` header normally sets the charset.
  - It may also be an artifact of how the HTML was captured.
- **The `<title>` is generic.** It is only "Crocs", which is a minor point for "view in browser" and assistive-technology contexts.
- **Preheader handling is correct.** It uses `display:none !important` with the zero-width/soft-hyphen padding block.
- **Client-compatibility code is present.** It includes `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, the Gmail `u + .email-body` hack, and a 639px media query.
- **Web fonts are declared** (`TT Crocs`, `font-display: swap`). The fallback font stacks are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.
- **Content image `alt` text could not be assessed.** The body was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The body is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`[WARN]` Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a test-harness limitation and not evidence of a failure.
  - Authentication should be verified from raw headers at the final receiving mailbox.
- **Alignment looks sound.** The From domain `crocs-email.com` matches the click and open tracking domain.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` headers could not be verified.** They normally sit in the footer or headers, and neither was available here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 19 tracking links were unprobed and the HTML was truncated before any hrefs. The final URLs, UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to the `demdex.net` pixel, and consider whether the Adobe `d_dpuid` identifier is needed in the URL.
2. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
3. Resolve the 19 tracking redirects with a follow-redirect probe to confirm final destinations, status codes and UTM parameters.
4. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML to check the unsubscribe link, postal address, image `alt` text and merge tokens.
5. Capture the raw message headers from the final inbox (Gmail "Show original") to confirm SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`.
