# The ✨new✨ DSW VIP is here
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Well, I've been getting DSW's "VIP" pitch about six times this week already, and now they've decided to explain the loyalty program to me again — this time with a chart. Points math, a tote bag offer, three perk icons. Not one shoe in the whole email that I can actually buy today. I run through this whole thing and there's nothing for me to put in a cart.
- I'm not anti-loyalty-program, but I already know how DSW rewards work, I've seen this pitch four separate ways this week alone. This one's at least honest about being a program explainer rather than dressing up as a "sale," but it's also the least useful one yet if what I actually want is a pair of shoes. Not clicking, not forwarding — this gets archived same as the rest.

## What's working

- The points math table (CLUB/GOLD/ELITE) is at least clear and honest — no fine print games, just straight numbers.
- "FREE SHIPPING FOR VIPS," in-store pickup, and Afterpay icons are legible and simple, good for reading quick on my phone.
- No fake urgency stacked on this one — no "FINAL HOURS" screaming at me like every other DSW send this week.

## What's weak

- Zero shoes shown until you get to the free tote graphic near the bottom — for a shoe company email, that's backwards.
- Every model pictured is a young woman; nothing here says "we know a 62-year-old guy opens these."
- It's pure program mechanics, not a product push — after "Become a VIP for free (& save)" two days ago, this is basically the same pitch a third time with a new layout.
- "FREE TOTE with your $99 purchase" only works in-store or with a promo code online, and it's a handbag — not remotely relevant to what I'm shopping for.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, men's styles — nothing that tells me this brand carries what I actually wear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put actual men's comfort shoes above the fold — a hero product shot beats another purple gradient with program copy.
- 2. Cut the loyalty-tier explainer down to one line; I don't need the full math lecture again, I've had it three times this week.
- 3. Swap the tote-bag exclusive for something in my category, like a free-shipping code on comfort walking shoes.
- 4. Show at least one male model or a men's shoe grid so I know this brand is talking to me, not just women shoppers.
- 5. Space this out — four to five VIP/loyalty emails in one week is fatigue, not persuasion.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your DSW points are worth more now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New VIP perks: free shipping, faster rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See what $1 gets you in points this year`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping, birthday reward, and more — no cost to join`
