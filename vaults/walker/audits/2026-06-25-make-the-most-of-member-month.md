# Make the Most of Member Month
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- I opened this one because I recognize Skechers and "Member Month" caught my attention — I'm a member, so this should be for me. But the first thing I see when I scroll is that big 2X POINTS hero, and right underneath it says "THRU 6/15." I'm reading this on June 25th. That offer is ten days dead. That's not a small detail buried in fine print — it's the entire hero of the email. Skechers sent me to their front door and the lights are already off.
- And that preview text. I've now seen that raw JSON garbage — the `{"@context": "http://schema.org/"` wall — three times in the last month. June 2nd, June 3rd, and now again. A real subscriber would start to think the brand doesn't proofread anything it sends. When something keeps breaking the same exact way, that's not a typo — that's a process problem nobody's bothering to fix.
- The BOGO 50% off below the fold is a perfectly decent offer, and the "Pick Up Where You Left Off" section actually showed me men's slip-ons I recognize. But I'd have already closed this once I realized the headline deal expired before the email hit my inbox. Marked as read, not clicked.

## What's working

- **Personalization in the header** — "Hi, Walker" with my 1,000 points and $5 reward still works. Shows they know I'm a member, not a cold prospect.
- **BOGO 50% off is legible** — the offer block is clean, big type, easy to read without squinting on a phone.
- **"Pick Up Where You Left Off" shows men's shoes** — slip-on casual styles in that section are directly in my lane. White, navy, brown — comfort-friendly silhouettes.
- **No layout breakage in the body** — images loaded, text didn't overlap, buttons are visible and tappable.

## What's weak

- **The hero offer is expired** — 2X Points "THRU 6/15" delivered on June 25th. That's not a minor oversight; that's the number-one visual in the email.
- **Broken JSON preview text, again** — third time in four weeks. At this point I'm expecting it, and that's not a good thing for Skechers.
- **Hero image is a woman in a summer dress** — I'm a 62-year-old guy. I get that it's a lifestyle shot, but it signals this wasn't built for me.
- **BOGO has no visible deadline** — the one live offer in this email doesn't tell me when it expires. If you want urgency, tell me I have three days.
- **"LOG IN / JOIN NOW" buttons on an already-logged-in member** — I can see my name and points at the top; those buttons feel like template copy left over from a prospect version.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the expired hero immediately** — if the 2X Points window closed June 15th, that block has no business being in a June 25th send. Either pull it or replace it with the current live offer as the hero.
- 2. **Fix the structured-data preview text bleed** — this is the third send with the same broken preheader. One line of QA in a preview client would catch this before it goes out to everyone.
- 3. **Put a deadline on the BOGO** — "BOGO 50% Off — Ends Sunday" is the difference between archive and click for someone like me. Anchor it in time.
- 4. **Lead with men's comfort product** — show me a slip-on with arch support above the fold instead of a woman in a maxi dress. Even a single hero shoe with "cushioned, hands-free" copy would close more sales from my demographic.
- 5. **Replace generic LOG IN / JOIN NOW** with a member-specific CTA — "Shop with your $5 Reward" uses the benefit I can actually see and gives me a reason to click today.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your $5 Reward + BOGO 50% Off Ends Soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Member Month: Buy One, Get One 50% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off ends [date] — your $5 reward is ready to use.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab BOGO 50% off before Member Month ends — tap to shop men's styles.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Make the Most of Member Month"

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken experience (empty personalization token) and a systemic HTTP image-serving issue that will cause image blocking in major clients. The Return Path pixel also has a malformed URL (missing protocol scheme).

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Return Path pixel — missing protocol scheme (bug)**
```
src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499"
```
No `http://` or `https://` prefix. Most clients will treat this as a relative URL, resolving to a path on the current origin (none, in email context) — the deliverability monitoring pixel will silently fail to fire.

**Krux user-matching pixels — third-party PII leakage**
```
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=9153f33fe5cdf59baf088bb835d3b39ef90f188bd8540a4faba92899227ced00
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid&partner_uid=121173391
```
Both pixels transmit the subscriber's SHA-256 email hash and raw subscriber ID to Krux (a Salesforce DMP). Not a rendering bug, but a technical data-sharing artifact worth flagging for legal review under CCPA/GDPR depending on recipient geography.

**26 tracked click links** — all wrapped through `click.emails.skechers.com`; not directly probeable but consistent with standard SFMC click-tracking. No broken link evidence from QA.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — will be blocked in Gmail, Outlook.com, and iOS Mail**

12 images are served over plain HTTP from `image.emails.skechers.com`. Gmail and Outlook.com proxy all images and block non-HTTPS sources. Affected assets include the logo, a decorative image, app store badges, footer icons (App Store, Google Play, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store):

```

http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-...png  (App Store badge)
... (8 more)
```

The ink1000.com open-tracking pixel is also HTTP:
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_L7D_U_SITE_ENGAGEDCLICK_EN_06042026
```

**Missing alt text on 2 images**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 open pixel) — low severity for a tracking pixel, but technically non-conformant
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unknown content image; if it carries meaningful content, the absence is an accessibility gap

**CSS media query duplication** — breakpoints at `max-width: 375px`, `max-width: 640px`, `max-width: 680px`, and `max-width: 480px` are defined across two `<style>` blocks with partially overlapping rules. This is noise but unlikely to cause rendering failures in practice.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**CRITICAL — Empty name field in greeting**

QA confirms the subscriber's first name merge token resolved to an empty string:
```
Greeting rendered as: "Hi ,"
```
This indicates the `%%FirstName%%` (or equivalent SFMC AMPscript field) was either null in the contact record or the fallback default value was not configured. This is a broken experience for every recipient whose first name field is blank.

No other unresolved tokens were flagged in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — The HTML is truncated before the footer; cannot confirm presence of physical mailing address or one-click unsubscribe. Not flagging as a failure — needs full-source verification.

**Authentication headers** — Not available from HTML source alone. DKIM/SPF/DMARC pass/fail status requires raw email headers; sending domain is `emails.skechers.com`. Recommend verifying against MX Toolbox or a seed test.

**Campaign ID date discrepancy** — Pixel contains `campaignid=PRO_US_L7D_U_SITE_ENGAGEDCLICK_EN_06042026`. The `06042026` suffix resolves to June 4, 2026; today is June 24, 2026. This suggests the creative or campaign ID was reused from an earlier send, or the segment is a re-engagement cohort ("last 7 days, site-engaged, clicked") built on June 4 data. Not a compliance issue, but worth confirming the send date and audience definition match.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 26 click links route through `click.emails.skechers.com`, which should append UTM parameters on redirect — but this cannot be verified without resolving the redirects. The campaign naming convention (`PRO_US_L7D_U_SITE_ENGAGEDCLICK_EN_06042026`) is consistent and structured, suggesting UTM wiring exists at the ESP level.

No direct evidence of stripped or missing UTM params from the available source.
