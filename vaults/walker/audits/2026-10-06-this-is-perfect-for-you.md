# This is perfect for you!

## Executive summary

- The first thing I saw was a pink-and-teal kids' shoe, the "Skechers Garage – Glam Racer," filling the top of the screen. I'm a 62-year-old man, so it's the wrong shoe again. The "Still thinking about it? You've got great taste!" line is a cart nag. I never put that shoe in a cart, and it's about the sixth nag this week. The "pink flower sneaker" from 10/4 and 10/5 has just been swapped for a pink kids' sneaker.
- The row of "more great options" has one thing I'd care about. It's the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman, the tan slip-on in the middle of the second row, and it's hands-free, which is what I want. It's a tiny thumbnail with a small caption, and nothing links it to the "perfect for you" subject. Of the six shoes, only one is for a man my age. Two are boys' shoes.
- The BOGO 50% banner is clear and honest, and it runs through 10/7. It sits far below the fold, though. I'd skim it, maybe tap the Altman, and mark the rest read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Buy One, Get One 50% Off or 20% Off a Single Pair" banner is clear, with a real deadline of 10/7.
- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman shows up in the grid, and that's the hands-free comfort I shop for.
- The SKECHERS logo and navy blue match what I know, so I trust it.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) is large and easy to tap on a phone.
- The fine print is complete and spells out the dates and exclusions.

## What's weak

- The hero is a girls' glittery sneaker. It has nothing to do with a 62-year-old man.
- "This is perfect for you!" is a stretch when the main shoe is for a child.
- The email has no "Hi, Walker," no Member badge and no points balance. The 10/4 and 10/5 emails had all three, so this one treats me like a stranger.
- The grid captions are tiny. I'd have to pinch to read them, and none show a price or any arch-support or cushioning detail.
- "Take Another Look" is vague, and it points at a shoe I never looked at.
- The sale banner is buried below the grid, and the whole email is another cart reminder. I got "Your Items Are Waiting!" earlier today and "Did you forget something?" and "Your cart expires soon!" on 10/5.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the member offer, and put a men's slip-in in the hero if my browsing shows I look at men's shoes. Don't show me a kids' shoe I never viewed.
- 2. Say what the shoe is for. "Arch Fit" and "Relaxed Fit" are in the product names, but a short line about cushioning and slip-in ease would help me.
- 3. Add my name, the Member badge and my points balance back in. They helped the 10/5 "fall footwear" email score 8.
- 4. Make the grid captions larger, and add prices and a "Shop men's slip-ins" button.
- 5. Space out the cart reminders. Send one a week, not several a day.
