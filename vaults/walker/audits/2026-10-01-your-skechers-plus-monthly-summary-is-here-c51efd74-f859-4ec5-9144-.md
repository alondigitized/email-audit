## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with an HTTPS open pixel. It has several hygiene problems: a scheme-less tracking pixel that can't resolve, cleartext `http://` asset and tracker URLs, and missing accessibility metadata. The source was truncated, so footer, links and headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. It resolves as a relative path, so the request goes to the wrong host or fails, and the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking beacon never fires. The QA run did not flag this.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_10012026_B`. The subscriber identifier `mi_u` travels unencrypted, and the pixel can be blocked or fail under mixed-content rules.
- **Third-party identity beacons:** three `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` and `1x1_ad_impression.gif` pixels pass `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00` (a 64-hex value, apparently a SHA-256 hash) and `partner_uid=121173391`. They sit inside a `display:none` div. They send identifiers to an ad-tech partner and add 3 extra third-party requests.
- **Unprobed links:** 37 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe ("Tracking/click-redirect domains"). Click-through destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **QA summary mismatch:** the summary reports "100% pass rate, 0 issue(s), 0 warning(s)" while listing 14 `[WARN]` lines. The tooling is under-reporting.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?…` uses HTTPS. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** about 11 images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`, including the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the App Store and Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store and the social icons. Clients that enforce HTTPS can block them. The host should serve HTTPS.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000)
  - `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`
  - The `krxd` and `returnpath` pixel `<img>` tags also have no `alt` attribute in the source.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resize disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect.
- **Missing lang:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute.
- **No table roles:** the layout tables, such as `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Style-stripping clients:** `.desktop-content { display: none; }` is unconditional and only overridden at `min-width: 768px`. A client that strips `<style>` blocks, or is wider than 640px but narrower than 768px, shows the mobile variant only. I couldn't confirm this against the truncated body.
