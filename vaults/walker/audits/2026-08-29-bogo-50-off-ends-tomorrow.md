# BOGO 50% off ends TOMORROW
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Same BOGO 50% off deal I've been seeing from DSW basically every day this week, and now they're topping it with a "30% off select Nike styles" line above the logo that's already fighting for attention before I even get to their own headline. Grid of eighteen shoes below is almost entirely women's — booties, mules, leopard heels, strappy sandals — and I count maybe two things in that whole wall that could pass for men's, and even those are buried. There's a wide-width and wide-calf button down near the size chart, which is the first thing all week that's actually spoken to a guy my age with wide feet, but I have to scroll past a mountain of women's shoes to find it.
- I'm not clicking this one and I'm not forwarding it. It's the same math I did on the 24th through the 28th — real discount, zero relevance to what I actually shop for.

## What's working

- "GREAT DEALS. EASY MATH." headline is blunt and I appreciate that — no games, tells me straight what BOGO 50% means.
- Wide Width / Wide Calf buttons are a genuine nod to comfort-shopper concerns like mine, first time I've noticed that in this run of emails.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup, and app icons at the bottom are laid out clean and easy to scan.
- Barcode and offer terms right in the email means I could actually use this in-store without hunting for a code.

## What's weak

- Grid of new arrivals is nearly all women's footwear — heels, mules, leopard print — nothing that reads as "for Walker."
- The "Up to 30% off select Nike styles" teaser at the very top is a second, competing offer before I even get to the BOGO headline — confusing which deal is the real one.
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support, or slip-on comfort anywhere — my whole reason for opening a shoe email.
- This is the same BOGO 50% deal I've now seen five or six days straight; nothing new to make me stop and look twice.
- Size chart only goes up to 11 with no wide sizing shown inline until I scroll further down — a small thing but it's the one part of the email actually built for guys like me and it's not up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the send by gender interest — if DSW has any purchase history on me, a men's comfort/walking shoe grid up top would beat generic new arrivals every time.
- 2. Drop the competing Nike teaser line at the very top — pick one offer to lead with, not two.
- 3. Move the Wide Width/Wide Calf buttons up near the hero, right under the BOGO headline — that's the one thing in this email actually built for me.
- 4. Add one line of comfort language — "cushioned, arch support, all-day wear" — since that's the actual reason I'd click, not the shoe style.
- 5. Vary the creative — I've now gotten near-identical BOGO layouts all week; even a different color scheme or new hero shot would help.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, arch-supported, and buy-one-get-half-off`
