# Plus an extra 25% off?!

## Executive summary

- Crocs again, and the "extra 25% off" is a new wrinkle after the "Up to 60% Off? Yes Please." send on 11/16. The big "60% OFF" on yellow is easy to read on my phone, and I get the gist in two seconds. But it's "select sale styles" with "Exclusions Apply" tucked underneath. I've seen this same "up to" song all week, and I still can't tell what a shoe costs.
- The pictures are two young women in a TikTok-style setting, and then a Grinch clog and "From Your FYP." I'm 62. I don't have an FYP. Nothing here says cushioning, arch support or all-day walking comfort. That's what I care about, and it's what Hoka and Brooks talk about.

## What's working

- The "60% OFF" and "PLUS AN EXTRA 25% OFF" are big and bold, so I can read them without zooming.
- The "Shop Black Friday Deals" button is a big black pill. It's easy to tap with my thumb.
- The "Free shipping on qualified orders" bar at the top is a plain, honest perk.
- The fur-lined clog shot near the bottom looks warm and comfy. That's the closest thing to my needs.
- The offer dates are in the fine print, so I can see when it ends.

## What's weak

- "Up to" and "select sale styles" mean I can't tell which shoes get the full discount.
- The models and the TikTok copy are aimed at someone 40 years younger than me. "From Your FYP" meant nothing to me.
- The Grinch banner is a novelty. It isn't a shoe I'd wear to walk the dog.
- Nothing mentions comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-on ease. There are no men's styles anywhere.
- The fine print is a mess. One block says the offer ends November 29 and the next says November 22. I can't tell which one applies to me.
- It's the sixth Crocs email in a short stretch. The "2 for $50" run through 11/15 and now this feel like more of the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one real product and a real price. For example: "Classic Clog, was $50, now $30, with the extra 25% off, about $22." Show the math so I don't have to guess.
- 2. Show a man in my age range and talk about comfort: "Cushioned, slip-on, easy on your feet."
- 3. Make the deadline clear and consistent, and put it in the hero. Don't bury two different dates in the fine print.
- 4. Add a men's comfort or walking row, and drop the TikTok block for older shoppers.
- 5. Space out the sends. Fewer emails with real news would work better than six near-identical ones.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is structurally sound: it has a `lang` attribute, a viewport meta tag, Outlook and Gmail resets, and a preheader. Two real defects show up: malformed `<meta>` declarations and an alt-less third-party tracking pixel. SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the footer compliance elements could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA run skipped 15 tracking links (click-redirect domain, HTTP probe skipped). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0` is a 1x1 image with `alt=""` and `width`/`height` attributes. It is correctly formed and sits in a `height:1px;overflow:hidden` wrapper.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) call, and QA flagged it for missing alt. It passes an identifier (`d_dpuid`) and an open-state flag to a third party. That is a privacy and consent surface, and it is a second open-tracking beacon alongside the first-party one.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Ineffective meta tags (confirmed in source):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it has no effect either.
  - Impact is low, since the transport charset normally governs, but the HTML declares no charset.
- **Missing alt:** The demdex image has no `alt` attribute (QA warning). It is a tracking pixel, so the fix is `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, or `display:none`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is the same for every send. This is minor and only matters in web-view or archive contexts.
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` loads from `media.crocs.com` (woff2 and woff, `font-display: swap`). Clients that don't support web fonts will use the fallback stack, which was in the truncated portion and could not be checked.
- **Not verifiable:** The source is truncated, so image alt coverage, bulletproof buttons, and table semantics for the body content are unconfirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncation means the rest of the body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results missing:** QA could not find the header, and the note says it is expected via the AgentMail relay. This is a limitation of the capture path, not evidence of an SPF/DKIM failure. DMARC alignment of `crocs-email.com` remains unverified.
- **Footer elements:** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or QA output, so they cannot be assessed.
- **Sender domain:** The From domain (`crocs-email.com`) matches the tracking domain (`click.crocs-email.com`), which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Link destinations and UTM parameters are not in the visible source, and the 15 tracking links were not resolved. Click wrapping (`click.crocs-email.com`) may hide UTM parameters until redirect resolution.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the meta tags: `<meta charset="utf-8">`, `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel, and review whether Adobe Audience Manager open-tracking needs consent gating for GDPR/CCPA audiences.
3. Resolve the 15 click-tracking redirects with a follow-redirects probe to confirm final destinations, status codes and UTM presence.
4. Capture full raw headers (not the relay-stripped copy) to verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`.
5. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address), alt coverage and merge tokens.
