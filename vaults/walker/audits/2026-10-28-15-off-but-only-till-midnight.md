# $15 off...but only till midnight ⏳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the fourth or fifth send in two days with the same SOBRIGHT "buy more, save more" ladder. It's the one from "$15 off ends tomorrow!" and "Buy more = SAVE more" yesterday, and the one from "Have you used your $15 off yet?" the day before. The big pink banner is easy to read on my phone. The numbers are $15 off $49, $20 off $99 and $60 off $199. I'll give them that. But I've seen it so many times I could recite it.
- The "only till midnight" deadline is the only new thing. The fine print says the offer runs October 26-28, so it really does end tonight. Still, this is the third "last chance" they've sent me this week, and it's wearing thin. Below the banner it's all fashion: mary janes, loafers, a stiletto, a black slip-on and a fancy sneaker. I see nothing about cushioning or arch support. The one black slip-on is the only thing close to what I wear.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted a cushioned walking shoe I'd go to Hoka or Brooks directly. I might look at "Shop Men" if I needed something and had the code handy.

## What's working

- The pink banner with the $15 / $20 / $60 ladder is big and I can read it without zooming.
- The code SOBRIGHT is shown clearly, and there's a barcode for in-store use.
- The fine print lists the real dates, October 26-28, so the "midnight" claim holds up.
- "Shop Men" is a visible link, and there's a "Shop Wide Width" button. Wide width matters to me.
- The size grid is large and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- I've had this same offer for two days. The cadence is tiring and "only till midnight" feels like manufactured urgency.
- The product picks are women's shoes and dressy styles. Only one slip-on is anywhere near my taste, and none are comfort-first.
- Every product caption says "$15, $20, or $60 off!" in tiny red text. There are no prices or brand names, so I can't tell what I'd actually pay.
- Nothing says cushioned, arch support or walking. "So new, so you" doesn't speak to me.
- The fine print is long and dense. It lists brand exclusions, including Skechers, but the banner doesn't say so.
- The "DSW Designer Shoe Warehouse" logo text overlaps and gets cut off at the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show men's and comfort products. Use slip-ons and walking shoes with the brand, the price and a star rating.
- 2. Put the exclusions near the banner in one short line instead of burying them in the dark fine print.
- 3. Change the cadence. Send me one clear reminder, not a new subject line for the same offer every day.
- 4. Make the button say "Shop men's comfort" instead of "Claim $15 off".
- 5. Fix the logo overlap at the top.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SOBRIGHT works in stores and online through tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ons, walkers and wide widths. Save up to $60.`
