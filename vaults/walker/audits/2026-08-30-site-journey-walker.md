# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 2/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-30**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I got a fair way into buying a pair of the Slip-ins today — found the hands-free shoe I wanted, saw the price, even got to the size chart. But the wheels came off at checkout: my cart came up as a **blank white screen**, and I never actually got logged in even though I tapped Login. For a shoe I'm ready to buy today, that's the kind of thing that sends me right over to the Hoka app instead.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10**

## 3. What's Working

- The Men > Shoes flyout menu is clean, with big tappable rows (Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies) — easy for my thumbs.
- The Men's Shoes landing page leads with exactly what I'm shopping for: "Hands Free Slip-Ins," "Walking Shoes," "Athletic Sneakers" tiles right up top. Somebody there understands guys like me.
- The Slip-ins Contour Foam product page is well laid out — big photo, clear $88 price, "HANDS FREE Slip-ins" badge, star rating with review count.
- Search for "comfort shoes" pulled back genuinely relevant results (Max Cushioning Endeavour, GO Walk Arch Fit) with sale pricing and a "Also in Wide" badge — that wide-width flag matters a lot to me and my feet.
- Size selector on the product page has good-sized buttons, easy to tap without a magnifying glass.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Cart page is a blank white screen.** Nothing. No error, no "your cart is empty," no items. I have no idea if my shoe made it in or not.
- **Login never happened.** I tapped "Log In," and the page looked exactly the same afterward — still says "Login" in the corner, no "Hi Walker," nothing. If I'm a logged-in member I should see it.
- Because I never picked a size before hitting Add to Cart, the site correctly told me "Please select a size" — but then it seems the cart badge stayed at 0 all the way through search. So it's unclear if Add to Cart even works once a size IS picked, since I never got confirmation either way.
- Nothing on this journey ever felt personalized to me — same "20% Off Top Sellers" banner and same homepage art (running couple, not exactly comfort-first Boomer energy) the whole way through, whether logged in or not.
- No comparison-shopping content anywhere — nothing telling me why Skechers Slip-ins beat a Hoka or a Brooks on comfort or price. I do that homework myself, off-site.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page — it's showing a blank screen.** This is a hard stop on any purchase and should be priority one.
2. **Fix or clearly diagnose the login flow.** If tapping Login doesn't visibly log a member in, you're losing the loyalty-tier value entirely, and I can't tell if my $5 reward on $75+ even applies to me.
3. Add a visible cart confirmation ("Added to cart!" toast or badge update) right when Add to Cart succeeds, so I'm not left guessing.
4. Once logged in, personalize the homepage banner for return/logged-in shoppers — even just "Welcome back, Walker" would build trust that the email and site match up.
5. A short "why this vs. Hoka/Brooks/New Balance" comparison module on product pages would help me decide faster instead of leaving to comparison shop elsewhere.

## 6. Bottom Line

The product-discovery half of this site actually gets me — hands-free, comfort tech, wide widths, right up front. But a shopping site that can't show me my own cart or confirm I'm logged in isn't a shopping site I can finish a purchase on. I'd have closed the tab at step 8.

**Compared to yesterday (2/10):** Marginal improvement — product/search relevance held up and felt slightly better organized — but the blank cart page is a new, serious regression that caps this well below where good navigation alone would put it. Net: still broken at the finish line.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary
Mobile journey across 8 steps (Homepage → Search) was audited for code-level issues. The site runs on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (demandware.static asset paths) with a heavy third-party tag stack (18+ external script origins in `<head>`) and Kasada bot-mitigation (`x-kpsdk`) fingerprinting. Key findings: repeated 429s from the bot-mitigation endpoint (including one on the cart page itself), a broken third-party stylesheet blocked by CORS, and 18 axe violations concentrated on Homepage, Cart, and Search — several critical/serious. LCP and CLS could not be captured for any step (instrumentation gap, see Performance).

## Accessibility
18 violations across 3 of 8 steps (Men category, Men>Shoes, PDP, Add to Cart steps clean).

- **Critical** — Homepage: `button-name` (icon-only buttons with no accessible label), `aria-required-parent` (ARIA role used without required parent container, e.g. an `li`/menuitem role outside its owning `ul`/menu).
- **Serious** — Homepage: `link-name` (links with no discernible text, likely icon/image links missing alt/aria-label). Cart: `document-title` (empty/missing `<title>`), `html-has-lang` (missing `lang` attribute on the cart view's document).
- **Moderate** — Homepage: `heading-order`, duplicate/non-top-level `landmark-main`/`landmark-complementary`, `landmark-unique`, `region` (content outside landmarks). Cart: `landmark-one-main`, `page-has-heading-one` (no `<h1>`). Search: `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, `region`.
- **Minor** — `aria-allowed-role` on Homepage and Search (same invalid role reused across templates).

The recurring `landmark-*`/`region` pattern plus duplicate `main` on Homepage indicates a shared layout component rendering multiple `<main>`/nested landmark wrappers (likely from a modal, quick-view, or promo overlay injected into the DOM rather than the primary content area). The Cart page's missing `<title>`/`lang`/`<h1>` suggests it may be rendered as a fragment or through a different template than the rest of the site.

## SEO
- Cart page missing `<title>` and `lang` attribute — both are baseline on-page SEO/indexing signals; if this pattern extends to other transactional pages, it will affect how those URLs are indexed/labeled in search results (cart itself is typically noindex, but the missing `<title>`/`lang` implies a template gap worth checking on other pages using the same base template).
- No `<h1>` on Cart (`page-has-heading-one`) reduces topical signal on that template.
- Heavy async tag footprint (TikTok, Pinterest, Bing, Yahoo, Impact, CreativeCDN, Bloomreach, Quantum Metric, 2x GTM/gtag destinations, Monetate, PulseInsights, Go2SDK, WebFontLoader) adds significant head-of-document script volume; while `async`, this increases total page weight crawlers must process and can delay when crawlers see below-the-fold content on JS-rendering fallback.

## Performance
- TTFB is healthy on server-rendered entry points: Homepage 446ms, Log In 409ms, Men category 409ms.
- TTFB drops to 26–31ms for Men>Shoes, PDP, and Add to Cart, and 28ms for Search — consistent with client-side/SPA-style transitions after initial load rather than fresh server-rendered navigations. View Cart is an outlier at 117ms, suggesting a server round-trip (cart state fetch) on that step.
- **LCP and CLS were not captured for any of the 8 steps.** This is a measurement/instrumentation gap, not a confirmed absence of issues — Web Vitals collection should be re-run with a paint-timing observer attached before/after each navigation (SPA-style route changes often don't fire standard `LCP`/`CLS` PerformanceObserver entries the same way full page loads do, which likely explains the blanks here).
- Third-party script count (18+ distinct origins) on Homepage/Log In/Men category is high and is a common driver of poor INP/TBT on mobile even when individually marked `async`.

## Mobile Optimization
- The document sets `style="--vh: ...px"` via JS on `<html>`, a common workaround for mobile viewport-unit inconsistencies (100vh vs. visible viewport under browser chrome). This is a reasonable pattern but signals dependency on JS execution for correct viewport-based layout — if that script is delayed by the third-party script queue, layout relying on `--vh` will be incorrect until it runs.
- Cannot confirm the `<meta name="viewport">` tag or responsive image (`srcset`/`sizes`) usage from the truncated HTML samples provided; recommend explicit verification.
- No device-specific errors were observed beyond the shared console/network issues below.

## Console & Network Errors
- **429 (rate limited) — Kasada bot-mitigation endpoint** (`/…/fp?x-kpsdk-v=…`): fired 5 times across the session, including once against `/cart/` directly. Repeated 429s against a bot-detection fingerprinting endpoint on a real navigation path indicates the mitigation layer is throttling this session — on mobile networks with retries/re-fetches this could degrade or block legitimate checkout flows.
- **CORS failure**: `web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css` blocked by CORS policy, followed by `net::ERR_FAILED` — the Stylitics "complete the look" stylesheet is misconfigured (missing `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`) and fails to load entirely, meaning that integration's styling is broken in production for cross-origin fetches.
- **Summarizer API warnings** (x4): "Unsupported Summarizer API languages were specified" — this is Chrome's built-in on-device AI Summarizer API (`window.ai`/`Summarizer`) being invoked with an unsupported language and aborting. This points to a feature-detection or third-party script probing this experimental browser API without a supported-language check, producing console noise on every load (not user-facing breakage, but indicates unvetted script behavior in production).

## Recommendations
1. Fix icon-only buttons/links on Homepage to include accessible names (`aria-label`/visually-hidden text) — resolves `button-name` and `link-name`, the two most severe a11y findings.
2. Audit the layout component producing duplicate/non-top-level `main`/landmark roles on Homepage; likely a modal or injected widget breaking out of its container.
3. Investigate the Cart template for missing `<title>`, `lang`, and `<h1>` — check whether it shares a base template with other pages or renders as a partial/fragment.
4. Re-run Web Vitals collection with proper LCP/CLS observers spanning SPA route transitions, not just full page loads, to close the current measurement gap.
5. Investigate why the Kasada `/fp` fingerprinting endpoint is returning 429 mid-session, including on `/cart/` — confirm this isn't throttling real mobile users during checkout.
6. Fix or remove the Stylitics CORS misconfiguration so the stylesheet loads (or fails gracefully without a broken cross-origin request).
7. Remove or gate the Summarizer API probing script behind a supported-language/feature check to eliminate the recurring console errors.
8. Consider auditing/consolidating the 18+ third-party script origins loaded on Homepage/Log In/Men category for their impact on mobile INP/TBT, independent of the LCP/CLS gap above.
