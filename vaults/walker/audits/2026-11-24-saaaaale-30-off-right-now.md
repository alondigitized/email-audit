# 📣 SAAAAALE! 30% off right. now.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same pink banner and giant black "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th. Yesterday's "BLACK FRIDAY'S 30% OFF STARTS… NOW!" and the two "ICYMI" and "30% OFF!" sends told me this already. I don't need a sixth copy to know it's on. The subject's "SAAAAALE!" stretched out like that reads as shouting, and the megaphone emoji doesn't help.
- The email itself is easy to read. The big number is hard to miss, and the code BFDEALS is right there under it. But the hero shoe photo is a pile of UGG-style boots, sneakers and Nikes, and nothing in it says comfort or arch support. The "SHOP MEN" link is a small text link, and I'd have to hunt for it on my phone. I did like the "Shop wide width" button. That's the first thing in the email that spoke to my feet.
- Would I click? Probably not today, since I've already seen the deal. I'd tap "Shop Men" only if I were already shopping for shoes. I wouldn't forward it. My buddies are getting the same emails.

## What's working

- The giant "30%" is impossible to miss, and the code BFDEALS sits right under it.
- "Claim 30% Off" is a big black button that's easy to tap with my thumb.
- The "Shop by size" grid with Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons is useful. Wide width is something I actually need.
- The "Free shipping for VIPs / In-store pickup" row is clear and simple.
- The brand voice matches the earlier sends, so I trust it's the real DSW.

## What's weak

- It's the sixth or seventh near-identical Black Friday send since the 20th. I'm tired of it.
- The hero photo is mostly women's boots and fashion sneakers. Nothing says walking, cushioned or slip-on.
- The only men's entry point is a small "SHOP MEN" text link, and it sits next to Women, Kids and Everything.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny gray text. The exclusions are buried down there, and "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" is a lot of weasel words. The 30% excludes Skechers, Clarks and others I'd compare.
- The featured product is a kids' adidas sneaker at $41.2. That's not for me.
- The top banner says "Up to 30% off select Nike styles", which is a slightly different offer from the 30% hero, so I'm not sure what the deal really is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same creative daily. Send one email with something new: a men's comfort or wide-fit pick, with a real price.
- 2. Put a men's comfort hero up top, like a slip-on or a cushioned walker with its price, not a pile of fashion boots.
- 3. Make "Shop Men" a real button, and add "Comfort & Walking" as its own button.
- 4. Say clearly which brands are excluded, near the offer rather than in the fine print.
- 5. Cut the stretched-out "SAAAAALE" and the emoji.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off ends Monday: men's walking & comfort shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wide widths, 30% off. Code BFDEALS`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned walkers and slip-ons for men. Wide sizes in stock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code BFDEALS online or in store through Nov 29.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: DSW "SAAAAALE! 30% off right. now."

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on Braze with tracked links on `ablink.dsw.com`. One CTA ("Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off") returned HTTP 400 in probing, and three images lack alt text. SPF/DKIM/DMARC could not be verified. The HTML source is truncated and only 12 of 70 links were probed, so some findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The link "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400**.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/38Uof3R_7JF6SgQYPiVVhA~~/AAQRxRA~/ieU8_ujURNt…` (a very long tracked path, roughly 700 characters).
  - A 400 from a Braze-wrapped link can mean a malformed or truncated token, or the probe being rejected. The probe can't tell which, so this needs a manual click-through. If the 400 is real, the SMS opt-in CTA is dead.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 70 links were probed, and 58 were skipped (footer, utility, social). Their status is unknown.
- **Wrapped links:** Every observed link goes through the `ablink.dsw.com` redirector. Final destinations can't be checked from the HTML alone.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/ZcDn5-KmNuf5W6jkBZL6DQ~~/AAQRxRA~/2KC-huHC…` is a tracked image. If it sits inside an `<a>`, the link has no accessible name.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=…` is a tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - `https://braze-images.com/appboy/communication/assets/image_assets/images/6af5f0c6a188c30092ab6b84/original.jpg` has no alt text.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset then depends on the HTTP/MIME `Content-Type` header.
- **Plain-text/URL ratio:** 22,337 of 28,011 characters (about 80%) in the plain-text part are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk. It comes from long tracking URLs.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML or the QA data. The `mi_u=225090073` pixel parameter is a populated per-recipient ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication not verifiable (WARN):** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, and the QA notes it is expected via the AgentMail relay. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status for `dsw@e.dsw.com` is unknown. This is a limit of the test setup, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unverified in the data provided:**
  - The QA reports no unsubscribe-link failure, and one is always probed.
  - The physical postal address and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or QA output, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** Not verifiable. All links are opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be seen.
- **Campaign ID:** The pixel's campaign ID (`mi_ecmp=20261124_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…`) is cut off, so the full campaign and offer naming can't be checked.
- **Landing alignment:** Not assessable because no redirect chains were resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority:** Manually test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/38Uof3R_…`). If the 400 is real, regenerate the Braze link or fix the destination.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked tracked image, and `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel. Add alt text to `original.jpg`.
3. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Reduce the URL share of the plain-text part, for example with shorter or fewer tracked links.
5. Check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from raw headers, and confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and the postal address in the footer.
6. Re-run QA with the full HTML and follow the redirects, so the destination URLs and UTM parameters can be checked.
