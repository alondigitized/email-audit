# 20% OFF NOVEMBER TRENDS >>>
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. The big "20% OFF*" at the top is easy to read on my phone, and the black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is hard to miss. But I scrolled the whole thing and didn't see a single man. It's a women's gift guide: cold-weather boots, a pink fuzzy boot, cozy slippers, and a pom-pom beanie. The only men's thing is the "MEN" link in the top nav.
- This is also the same 20% pitch I've had all week. On 11/5 it was "20% off starts now!" with the TINSEL code, and before that came the VIP/FABULOUS run. This one swaps in "November Trends" and a gift theme. I'm numb to it by now.
- Would I click? Probably only the "Shop by Size" Wide Width button, since I need room in the toe box. Forward it? No. I'd keep it for the day I'm actually out buying shoes.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" headline is huge and readable without squinting.
- The code TINSEL sits right under the headline, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The "Shop by Size" block with **Wide Width** and **Wide Calf** buttons is useful for a comfort buyer like me.
- The layout is a clean single column with large tap-friendly buttons. It's easy on a phone.
- The extra 10% for veterans and active military is a nice touch, and it's stated plainly.

## What's weak

- Every model and product is women's. Nothing in the trends section speaks to a 62-year-old man.
- There is nothing on cushioning, arch support, or slip-on comfort. The slippers are the closest thing, and they're shown as a fashion item.
- The offer is wrapped in asterisks. "Regular-price styles only" and the fine print about exclusions make me doubt the 20% will cover the shoes I'd actually buy.
- Five product tiles with five separate "SHOP" links, and I'm not told which brands are in them. I can't compare Hoka, Brooks, or New Balance in any useful way.
- The deadline is buried in the fine print. The top of the email gives me no reason to act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and category. For me, lead with men's walking and slip-on comfort brands (Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance), with 20% off shown on those shoes.
- 2. Put the end date next to the hero offer, for example "Ends Saturday", instead of leaving it in the footer.
- 3. Add a few comfort cues to the tiles: cushioning, arch support, brand names, and reviews or star ratings.
- 4. Give the Wide Width button more prominence. That's the one place I felt the email understood me.
- 5. Cut the screaming caps and the ">>>" from the subject line.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cushioned walkers for fall: 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wide widths in stock. Use code TINSEL in stores & online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hoka, Brooks & New Balance included. Ends Saturday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, but QA found one failing tracked link (the SMS opt-in CTA), a very URL-heavy plain-text part, and no verifiable SPF/DKIM result. The truncated HTML and the redirect-wrapped links also limit what can be confirmed about footer compliance and UTMs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (HTTP 400):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned 400. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/W0Nwp8QHpseinDtl6f-_jQ~~/AAQRxRA~/msEZh7Xuo...` (about 700 characters of opaque token). The cause is unconfirmed. It could be a malformed or expired tracking token, or the prober being rejected by the click-tracker. Either way, a subscriber may hit an error on a primary conversion CTA.
- **Long redirect URL:** The same link is very long, which raises the risk of truncation or wrapping in plain-text clients.
- **Limited probe coverage:** Only 12 of 71 links were probed, so the other 59 are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261105_P261660_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` puts a subscriber identifier (`mi_u`) in a URL. This is normal for open tracking, but it breaks if images are blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 2 images:**
  - the `ablink.dsw.com/q/3GELr4eCOPuiYiIdGOnrNQ~~/...` image
  - `o.gif` (the tracking pixel). Add `alt=""` to the pixel.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The source shows `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so the browser ignores them.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` does not describe this message.
- **Plain-text part:** 21,874 of 27,340 characters (about 80%) are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter and readability risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved Liquid/Braze tokens appear in the visible source, and `mi_u` is populated. Only a truncated portion of the HTML was available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be confirmed. This is a gap in the test harness (AgentMail relay), not proof of failure.
- **Footer not visible:** The unsubscribe link was included in probing and not flagged. The physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers could not be verified because the source is truncated.
- **Sender domain:** From is `dsw@e.dsw.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable:** Every link is wrapped in an `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, and the destinations were not resolved in the QA data. UTM presence and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed.
- **Campaign ID in pixel:** `mi_ecmp=20261105_P261660_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` is the only visible campaign identifier. It uses a different parameter scheme from standard UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts" link manually with a browser User-Agent. If it still fails, regenerate the link in Braze and check the SMS landing page.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and a descriptive alt to the `ablink.../q/3GELr4...` image.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Change the meta tags to `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Shorten the plain-text part by using fewer or shorter links, or by reducing link wrapping in the text version.
5. Capture the full raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and the untruncated HTML. That would let the audit confirm authentication and the footer address.
6. Follow the redirects on key CTAs to record the final landing URLs and check UTM parameters.
