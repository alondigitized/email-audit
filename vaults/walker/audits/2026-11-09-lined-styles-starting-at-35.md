## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a ExactTarget/SFMC-style template with a responsive `<style>` block and a hidden preheader. It has seven `http://` image sources and a malformed `<meta>` pair. The HTML was truncated and the Authentication-Results header was unavailable, so the footer, unsubscribe block and link bodies could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** QA skipped 20 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains (for example `click.crocs-email.com`). Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Missing alt on a tracking pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-beacon image with no `alt` attribute. It is a 1x1 pixel and the impact is minor.
- **Insecure image URLs:** seven images load over `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`: App Store, Google Play, Find Your Store, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. See section 3.
- **Open pixel:** the SFMC open pixel at `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10209&bmt=0` is correctly HTTPS and has `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** the seven `http://` assets above can be blocked or proxy-rewritten by some clients. When that happens, the app-download, store-locator and social icons are lost. The alt text is present (e.g. "Download on the App Store"), so the fallback is readable. The same host serves HTTPS elsewhere in the template, so this looks like a template/CDN setting.
- **Malformed meta tags:** two `<meta>` tags in `<head>` have no `http-equiv` attribute:
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />`
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />`

  Without `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, neither declaration is valid. Charset then depends on the MIME header, which risks encoding problems with special characters.
- **Layout and text sizing:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, the viewport meta and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` are present. Mobile rules at `max-width: 639px` and the Gmail hack (`u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`) are in place.
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` is loaded through `@font-face` from `media.crocs.com`, with a `font-display` fallback. I could not see the fallback font stack in the inline styles because the source was truncated.
- **Preheader:** `<div class="preheader">` with `display:none !important` and padding filler is correctly implemented.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the supplied portion. The body was truncated, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** QA reported "Authentication-Results header not found". The note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `email@crocs-email.com` are unverified. This is a limitation of the test setup, not a confirmed defect.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the supplied data, so they cannot be confirmed or flagged.
- **Sender domain:** the From address on the dedicated `crocs-email.com` domain matches the tracking and image hosts, which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The tracking links go through SFMC redirects, and the final landing URLs and UTM parameters were not captured. Continuity cannot be evaluated. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the seven `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image URLs to `https://`. This fixes the only QA warning set with a direct rendering impact.
2. Fix the head meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon `<img>`.
4. Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers. That would verify the footer, unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe` headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Resolving the 20 redirect links to their final URLs would let the UTM and landing-page checks run.
