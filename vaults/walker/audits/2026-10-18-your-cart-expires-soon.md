- The text is large. The "WOMEN / MEN / KIDS / CLOTHING / NEW ARRIVALS / SALE" list is simple, and I can find "MEN" without squinting.
- The Skechers logo is up top and the layout is clean. I know who sent it.
- The fine print states the dates and the exclusions, so nothing is hidden.

## What's weak

- I never put anything in a cart, so the "cart expires" subject feels false.
- The women's pink flower hero shoe has nothing to do with me, and "YOU LIKED THESE" is just wrong.
- Four of the six grid items are kid's or women's shoes. The spray and the work sneaker aren't what I shop for either.
- The grid is untitled, so I can't tell whether these are things I looked at or just best sellers.
- The only men's items are a text link at the bottom. There's no slip-in, cushioning or arch support message anywhere.
- The 20% off excludes sale items and requires a Skechers Plus login. It's below the fold and easy to miss.
- This is the fifth near-identical nag in a week. It feels like spam.

- 2. Move the 20% members offer to the top, and show men's slip-ins (Hands Free Slip-ins, Arch Fit) in the hero for men like me.
