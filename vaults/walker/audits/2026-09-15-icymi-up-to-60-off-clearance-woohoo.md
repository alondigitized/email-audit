# ICYMI: Up to 60% off clearance (Woohoo!)
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Alright, "up to 60% off" got my attention for about three seconds — that's a real number, not another "become a VIP" lecture. But then I hit the size grid and it's 5 through 11 in half sizes, which is women's sizing, and the product wall underneath is wedges, strappy sandals, slingback heels, ballet flats. I count maybe two pairs in that whole 12-shoe grid I'd even glance at — a pair of New Balance sneakers and some black low-tops.
- This is the same DSW carousel treatment I've griped about all week (the VIP chart on 9/15, the "FINAL HOURS" pitch on 9/14) — big clearance headline up top, then a wall of women's shoes, then a totally unrelated Hispanic Heritage Month donation pitch, then a "Fall in Love with DSW Paseo Colorado" store-services block. None of it's built for a guy looking for slip-on comfort shoes.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is for me. I'd mark it read and move on, same as the last five.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF CLEARANCE" headline is big, bold, and has an actual number on it — better than another loyalty-points lecture.
- "Clearance in stores is always 30% off" gives me a fallback if I don't want to deal with shipping.
- The New Balance sneakers and the black low-top pair in the grid are at least shoes I'd consider — my eye caught those two immediately.
- "FREE SHIPPING FOR VIPs" and "IN-STORE PICKUP" icons near the bottom are practical, no-nonsense options I actually use.

## What's weak

- Shop-by-size grid runs 5 to 11 in half sizes — that's women's sizing, not mine. Tells me right away this send wasn't built with a guy like me in mind.
- Ten of twelve shoes in the "JUST DROPPED" grid are wedges, strappy sandals, slingbacks, and pumps — zero relevance to comfort/walking shoes for men.
- The Hispanic Heritage Month donation block and the "Fall in Love with DSW Paseo Colorado" store-services block are both dropped in the middle with no connection to the clearance offer — feels like three different emails stapled together.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or comfort fit anywhere — DSW clearly doesn't know that's what gets me to click.
- "*" asterisk on the 60% off headline with the real terms buried at the very bottom in tiny print — feels like a bit of a bait-and-switch until I scroll all the way down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split by gender/category right after the hero — a men's clearance rail with actual men's sizes (8-13) would've at least gotten me scrolling instead of bailing after the size grid.
- 2. Swap at least half the product grid to include men's comfort/walking styles — right now it's almost entirely women's dress shoes.
- 3. Cut the Hispanic Heritage Month and Paseo Colorado blocks out of this send entirely, or make them separate emails — they dilute the one thing (the discount) that actually pulled me in.
- 4. Move the offer terms/asterisk explanation closer to the headline, not buried in six paragraphs of fine print at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off men's clearance — comfort styles included`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off clearance: your size, your comfort`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on cushioned, easy-on styles — shop before sizes sell out`
