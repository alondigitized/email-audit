## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML carries leftover template debris (placeholder background URL, `@font-face` rules with no `src`, generic `<title>`). It also has two images without alt text and a plain-text part that is 86% URLs. The HTML supplied was truncated, and no headers or probe results were included, so compliance and continuity checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 9,936 of 11,493 characters (about 86%) are URLs. That points to long tracked redirect links, and this ratio can hurt spam scoring.
- **Placeholder asset in CSS.** `.bgwhite { background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png'); }` is an unreplaced placeholder. It uses insecure `http://` and will 404 wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.
- **Template asset path reused.** The banner is served from `/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`. That is a Welcome-series folder, so the asset may be shared or stale.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 19 skipped. No probe failures were reported, so no broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed), two images:**
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` is content-bearing and needs descriptive alt text.
  - `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12403380_361967_23368716.gif` is probably the open-tracking pixel. It should still carry an explicit `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Generic title.** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is the unedited template name, not the campaign name.
- **Dead font declarations.** Every `@font-face` block (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and others) has no `src`, so none can load. Rendering falls back to `Arial` or `Times New Roman`. The unquoted multi-word family `ITC Garamond Book Condensed` is also invalid-prone.
- **Invalid meta tag.** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Conflicting duplicate class.** `.bg1` is defined as `#ffffff` and again as `#58546d`. The later rule silently wins.
- **Doctype mismatch.** An HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an `xmlns` XHTML attribute on `<html>`.
- **Dark-mode link contrast.** `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { body{background:#151415 !important} a{color:#cf0a2c !important} }` gives about 3.3:1 contrast by my calculation. That fails WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal-size link text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found in the data provided. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result was not reported. Raw headers were not supplied, so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC could not be verified. The sender domain `email.newbalance.com` and tracking domain `link.email.newbalance.com` share the same parent domain, which is fine for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Destination URLs and UTM parameters fall outside the truncated source and the QA output, so nothing is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove or replace the `.bgwhite` placeholder `http://image.XXXXX.png`. If the class is used, point it at an `https://` asset.
2. Add descriptive alt text to `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and set `alt=""` with 1×1 dimensions on the tracking GIF.
3. Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules, or delete them and rely on the Arial fallback stack.
4. Set a campaign-specific `<title>`.
5. Fix `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`, remove the duplicate `.bg1`, and align the doctype with the markup.
6. Use a lighter link colour in dark mode to reach at least 4.5:1 on `#151415`.
7. Shorten tracked URLs, or trim the plain-text part, to bring the URL share well below 70%.
8. Re-run the audit with full HTML, raw headers and complete probe results to verify unsubscribe, authentication and UTM handling.
