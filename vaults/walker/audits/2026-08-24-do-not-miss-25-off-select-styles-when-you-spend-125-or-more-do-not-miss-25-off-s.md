**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Stack of shoe boxes again — that's twice now, they used this exact "boxes piled up" shot back on August 19th for the 30% off code email. I get it, boxes mean "sale," but I've seen this photo before and it's about as exciting as a parking garage. "Don't miss out," 25% off $125 or more, clean black button — fine, functional, I know what I'm getting. Below that it splits nicely into price tiers and a "more to explore" grid that's actually got some real variety in it: walking shoes, running shoes, even a 574 that catches my eye.
- Where it loses me is the subject line — it says the same thing twice, "do not miss" then "do not miss" again, like somebody's keyboard stuttered. That's the second time this month I've seen a doubled-up subject from these guys (the 25% off one on the 22nd had the identical glitch). Makes me wonder if anybody's proofreading before it goes out.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I actually needed shoes right now — the 574 or the 9060 in that pink tone would get a second look. But I'm not forwarding this to anybody. It's a fine, workmanlike sale email, nothing that makes me want to share it.

## What's working

- Clean price-tier breakdown (Under $50, $50-$100, Over $100) makes it easy to jump straight to my range without scrolling through junk.
- The "More to explore" grid has real product variety — running, walking, lifestyle, even socks — so there's something for a guy like me who just wants comfortable shoes, not just runners.
- One clear offer, one clear button, no hunting for the deal.
- Fine print on the offer terms is right there under the CTA, not buried at the bottom in six-point type.

## What's weak

- That doubled subject line — "do not miss... do not miss" — reads like a system glitch, and it's the second time this month I've caught the exact same stutter from New Balance.
- The hero image is just stacked shoe boxes again, same well they've gone to before — no actual shoe on a foot, no lifestyle context, nothing that says "this is for you."
- Nothing here specifically calls out comfort, cushioning, or arch support — for a guy who cares about that, it's just generic "select styles."
- The "more to explore" grid mixes in a running shoe like the Fresh Foam Arishi in a loud pink/orange that's clearly not aimed at me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject line duplication — that's an easy, obvious win and it's happened before, so somebody should catch it in QA before send.
- 2. Swap the hero shot for an actual shoe on a foot — the 574 or 9060 walking down a sidewalk beats a pile of boxes every time.
- 3. Lead the "more to explore" grid with comfort/walking styles up top instead of mixing in loud performance colorways first.
