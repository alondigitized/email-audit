## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting, `Hi ,`) and several markup-level defects. These are a scheme-less tracking pixel, two `http://` images without alt text, and ineffective meta declarations. Link, UTM and compliance checks could not be completed because of the truncated source and skipped probes.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken tracking pixel (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. It resolves as a relative path against the email or viewer origin, so the Return Path/Validity pixel will 404 or never fire.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11162026` is plain HTTP, so Gmail's image proxy and other clients may block or rewrite it.
- **Unverified links:** 34 links on click-redirect domains (`click.emails.skechers.com`) were skipped for the HTTP probe. Destination validity is unknown.
- **Hidden pixels:** Five tracking images sit inside `display:none` containers. The open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?…`) and the three `beacon.krxd.net` beacons are among them. Clients that strip or ignore `display:none` content may not fire them, so open counts may be undercounted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`, served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`.
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000 pixel). This one is a tracking pixel, so a missing alt is low impact.
- **Mixed content:** Both images above load over `http://`. The Skechers-hosted `.png` is a content image, so it may be blocked or show a broken-image icon in some clients.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset is then left to the clients' sniffing.
- **Zoom restriction:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Focus outline removed:** The global `a { outline: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.
- **Document semantics:** No `lang` attribute is visible on `<html>`. The layout tables shown (`<table cellpadding="0" …>`, `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) carry no `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,` with the name field empty. There is no fallback or default value in the template logic (for example, an AMPscript `IIF(Empty(@firstName), "there", @firstName)` pattern). Recipients without a first name get a visibly broken line.
- No unresolved raw tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were seen in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. This is a relay artifact (AgentMail), so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is not evidence of failure, and a direct-to-inbox test is needed to confirm.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** Not verifiable, because the footer is cut off in the truncated source. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided.
- **Third-party data sharing:** The `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` beacons pass what look like a SHA-256 hashed identifier (`partner_uid=9153f33f…`) and a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=121173391`) to a third-party DMP. This is worth checking against the privacy policy and consent language.
- The sender is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. A no-reply sender is not itself a CAN-SPAM violation, but it gives recipients no direct reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** Not verifiable. All 34 links are wrapped in click-tracking redirects and were not followed, so final URLs and UTM parameters are unknown.
- **Campaign ID:** `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11162026` appears in the ink1000 and Krux pixels. It could be compared against the landing-page UTMs once the links are resolved.
- **Offer alignment:** The preheader says "get a second for 50% off". The landing page could not be checked for this offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the empty-name fallback.** Add a default such as "Hi there," or drop the name when the field is empty. This is the only confirmed broken-experience item.
2. **Add the scheme to the Return Path pixel:** `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?…`. As written it never fires.
3. **Move all `http://` images to HTTPS.** That covers `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-….png` and the ink1000 `o.gif`.
4. **Add `alt` text to the content image**, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
5. **Correct the meta tags:** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** and the global `outline: none`. Add `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on the layout tables.
7. **Verify the unknowns:**
   - Resolve the 34 redirect links through a tracking-aware probe and check their UTM and landing-page alignment.
   - Review the full footer for unsubscribe and address.
   - Send a copy through a path that preserves headers, to check SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`.
