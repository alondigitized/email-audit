# 100+ NEW styles for back-to-school 🤯
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Famous Footwear again — and this one's not even trying to talk to me. The whole email is back-to-school kids' shoes: a kid in overalls holding a basketball, six rows of tiny sneakers labeled "Kids," a "3X Points on kids' character fun" banner. My grandkids are grown, I'm not buying backpacks this month, and there's nothing here about comfort, cushioning, or arch support for a guy my age.
- The BOGO ½ off banner up top is the same purple-and-pink block I've seen from this brand a half dozen times this summer — I mentioned it after the 8/3 and 8/4 sends too. At least there's a "SHOP MEN" button buried in the category row, so I know they haven't forgotten I exist, but it's one link among four, not the point of the email.
- I'm not clicking this one. Won't forward it either — nobody in my house needs back-to-school shoes. Straight to "read and ignored."

## What's working

- BOGO ½ off + 2X points banner is easy to spot right away, same clean layout they always use.
- "SHOP MEN" is right there in the category row if I did want to poke around — they didn't wall me out entirely.
- Everything renders clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, nothing glitchy.
- The "$30 / $50 / $75 and under" budget banners are a nice, honest touch — clear price ceilings, no games.

## What's weak

- This is a kids' shoe catalog wearing a Famous Footwear wrapper — every hero image is a kid or a kids' shoe grid.
- Nothing about comfort, slip-on ease, or arch support anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to open my wallet.
- The "SHOP MEN" button is one of four equal-weight buttons, not a real invitation — feels like an afterthought.
- Same BOGO banner, same purple trim I've now seen close to a dozen times this summer between all their sends.
- The preview text in my inbox was just a raw tracking link, not real words — looked broken before I even opened it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this list — send back-to-school to households with kids, and send guys like me a comfort-focused version with the same BOGO deal on men's walking shoes up top.
- 2. Fix that preview text — a dangling URL in the inbox looks like spam, not a $10 store campaign.
- 3. If I am getting this email, at least put "SHOP MEN" or a men's comfort shoe in the hero slot instead of six rows of kids' sneakers before I see anything for me.
- 4. Rotate the creative — I've seen this exact BOGO/points formula enough times now that it's starting to blend into wallpaper.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, easy on the feet — new styles for every budget`
