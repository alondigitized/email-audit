# BOGO 50% off ends SOON
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off, right up top in plain letters — I'll give DSW credit, that part's honest and I've said it before with this brand's emails this week. But "School Supply Stock-Up" and then "Now & Later" with a woman in ankle socks, platform sneakers and heeled booties striking a yoga pose is exactly the same not-for-me routine I've been getting from this sender all week. I scrolled the whole thing looking for a men's shoe and found exactly zero — one boot in the "New & Trending" section that's still styled for a younger crowd, plus a "Shop By Size" chart that runs women's sizing top to bottom.
- Nothing here says comfort, cushion, or arch support, and nothing says "men's." I'm not the school-supply shopper and I'm not the back-to-school parent buying kid shoes and bags either — near as I can tell there's not even a kids' item pictured despite the header. This is a scroll-and-delete for me, not a forward.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly near the top with no bait-and-switch headline — same honesty I noted on 8/13 and 8/12 sends.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup, and a rewards teaser are visible near the bottom for anyone already a member.
- "Shop Men" is at least a top-nav link, so it's one tap away if I bother to hunt for it.

## What's weak

- Every model and every shoe shown — booties, platform sneakers, heeled loafers, the Frye boot — is women's. Nothing cushioned, hands-free, or arch-supported in sight.
- "School Supply Stock-Up" and "Back to School" themes don't apply to me — I'm not shopping for a kid's backpack or sneakers.
- The size chart running 5 through 11 is a women's chart, which just underlines that this whole email wasn't built with a guy like me in mind.
- Same template, same offer, same demographic miss as the last six DSW emails in my inbox this week — feels like I'm getting blasted, not marketed to.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men on your list into a men's-only creative — even swapping one hero model and one product block for men's comfort shoes would beat the current one-size-fits-nobody approach.
- 2. Put a men's comfort shoe (walking shoe, slip-on, something with visible cushioning) in the hero spot for subscribers tagged male, instead of burying "Shop Men" in the nav.
- 3. Drop the "School Supply Stock-Up" framing for recipients without kids in the household, or segment it out entirely for me.
- 4. Show a men's size run (7–13) if you're going to bother with a size chart in a segmented send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off — men's comfort shoes included`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off your 2nd pair. Grab the cushioned ones.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ons, walkers, and wide widths — buy one, get 50% off the second.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort picks for fall, half off your second pair.`
