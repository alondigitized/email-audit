## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Cordial-sent HOKA broadcast with consistent UTM tagging. The QA run flags 12 of 12 probed links as broken (406 and 403), but the uniform failure points to the probe being blocked, not to dead links. The confirmed defects are a missing plain-text part, missing alt text on 16 images, and several head and CSS errors in the HTML source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe results.** 11 links returned 406, and the Unsubscribe link returned 403. They all go through `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`. The failures include the logo link, hero images, product tiles and the return-start link.
- **Likely cause.** A 100% failure rate across unrelated destinations is more consistent with ESP or CDN bot filtering of the QA client than with broken links. The decoded `linkUrl` values in `jwtP` are well-formed `https://www.hoka.com/...` URLs, for example `/en/us/hiking/?prefn1=shoeSurface&prefv1=trail running|Hiking|Hiking`. I can't confirm the links work. The result is inconclusive, not a confirmed breakage.
- **Coverage.** Only 12 links were probed and 17 were skipped, so destination status is unverified for those.
- **Possible asset/destination mismatch (low confidence).** The image `US_Speedgoat7HikeGTX_TopLeft_1176671-SZL_b.jpg` links to `/mens-trail-hiking-shoes/speedgoat-7-hike-gtx/1176638.html?dwvar_1176638_color=BCKPB`. The filename references product 1176671 (the women's SZL colorway), while the link goes to 1176638. It is probably just a naming slip, but the product tile should be checked.
- **Inconsistent product URL paths.** The decoded product URLs use different category paths: `/women-hiking/`, `/mens-trail/`, `/womens-trail/`, `/men-hiking/` and `/mens-trail-hiking-shoes/`. They may all resolve, but I couldn't verify that.
- **Tracking pixels.** Two open pixels from different hosts: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. This is not a defect, but it is duplicate open tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 16 images.** The 14 content images are HikeTrail_a/b/c/d/e/f, the four Speedgoat/Stinson/Challenger/Kaha product tiles plus the other Speedgoat variants, `US_Q3_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`, and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. The other two are the tracking pixels. Because most of these images sit inside `<a>` tags, the links have no accessible name, and the QA tool falls back to `[image: filename]`.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags with conflicting values.** One is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1` and the other adds `user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` can block pinch-zoom in some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid CSS from unpopulated template values.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have no numeric value or color. They are ignored, but they show template variables that were never filled in.
- **Focus outlines removed.** The global `* { … outline:none; … }` rule removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Web fonts.** `TheFutureHOKA` is loaded from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Most clients will ignore it, so check that the fallback font stack is defined.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't evaluate the body markup beyond this.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA output. The `px` and empty-value CSS in section 3 is the only sign of an unpopulated template variable. The `hmail=` (a 64-character hex value) and `cordial_id=` parameters are populated in every link.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing.** QA reports `Text version is 0 chars`. This is a confirmed deliverability weakness, and some filters penalize HTML-only messages.
- **Authentication unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC can't be assessed. The cause is the AgentMail relay, not a failure by the sender.
- **Unsubscribe link present but unverified.** It is `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_…`, and the probe got a 403, likely from the same bot filtering. Verify that it works manually.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided.** The `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address were not in the data (the HTML is truncated).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Consistent UTMs on every decoded link.** They are `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261028_BestSellers_Hike_Trail_STO_A60Day_270TrailTag_270HikeTag_720HikePurchasers_60BestSellersTag_AIAOVHigh_WishlistAdd`.
- **No link-level differentiation.** There is no `utm_content` or `utm_term` on any link, so analytics can't tell the hero, product tiles, logo and return link apart.
- **Segment names exposed in URLs.** The campaign string encodes audience logic such as `HikePurchasers`, `AIAOVHigh` and `WishlistAdd`. The URL is visible to the recipient and in landing-page analytics.
- **Landing-page alignment unverified.** The probes didn't reach any destinations, so I can't confirm that the pages match the email.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe with browser-like `User-Agent` and `Accept` headers, or an allowlisted client, to resolve the 406/403 results before treating any link as broken. Test the unsubscribe link manually.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. A 0-character text part is the clearest confirmed deliverability gap.
3. Add `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels. At minimum, give every image-only link an accessible name.
4. Fix the head and CSS: add a populated `<title>`, use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>`, remove the duplicate viewport tag and `maximum-scale=1`, and fill in or delete the `px` values in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border`.
5. Add `utm_content` per placement, and keep audience-segment names out of `utm_campaign`.
6. Check the `…_b.jpg` Speedgoat tile (asset 1176671-SZL vs. link 1176638 BCKPB).
7. Capture the full raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and the footer address in the next run so section 5 can be assessed.
