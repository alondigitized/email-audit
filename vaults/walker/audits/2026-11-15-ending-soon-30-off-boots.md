# Ending soon ⏰ 30% off boots >>>
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the seventh or eighth boots email since Wednesday, and I'm skimming now. The big white "30% OFF BOOTS*" on green is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. But I've seen that banner so many times I could draw it from memory. "Ending soon" is the only new thing, and after a week of "early" and "now" and "whoa," I don't believe the urgency.
- Then I scroll to "So New, So You" and it's a Sorel-style snow boot, a fuzzy fur-trim bootie, a mule, a platform sneaker, a leopard-print ankle boot and a glittery sneaker. None of that is me. There's no men's anything, no slip-on walking shoe and no cushioned comfort shoe. The "Shop Men" link is a tiny underlined line at the bottom of the hero. I wouldn't click, I wouldn't forward it, and I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF BOOTS*" banner is huge, high-contrast and readable without glasses.
- The code BOOTFRIDAY is printed right under the headline, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The black "Shop Boots" button is big enough for my thumb.
- The "Shop by Size" block has big numbers, plus "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons. Wide width is something I care about.
- The DSW logo and green look match every earlier DSW email, so I trust it's the real sender.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is women's. There's nothing for a 62-year-old man looking for a slip-on or a cushioned walker.
- "Shop Men" is a small underlined text link, easy to miss on a phone.
- The "Ending soon" urgency isn't credible. It's the same 30% boots deal as the last week of emails, and the fine print says it runs through Nov 12-15.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text. It also says brands like Skechers are excluded from the 30% off, which matters to me.
- The 500 bonus points need an UGG or Birkenstock purchase. Neither is a hands-free comfort shoe in the way I mean it.
- The "Holiday Name Drop: Up to 30% off Nike" bar at the very top competes with the main offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and past browsing. Show me men's comfort and walking styles, not women's fashion boots.
- 2. Be honest about the deadline. Say "Ends tonight at midnight" if it does, and stop sending near-identical boots emails every day.
- 3. Put "Shop Men's" and "Shop Comfort" in real buttons, not small text links.
- 4. Say plainly in the body which brands are excluded.
- 5. Add one comfort-led row, such as slip-ons, cushioned soles and arch support.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Braze-style template, but the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA returned HTTP 400 in QA, and several head-level meta tags are malformed. Only 12 of 74 links were probed, and the HTML source was truncated, so footer, header and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by QA, FAIL):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/B8dGsh9Fp7Sg-l4oO8JoEQ~~/AAQRxRA~/yn7SatsdSdeeIlPmimsgHkFzE81xFhhH0...` and returned **400**. The link text is empty in the QA record, so the anchor may contain only an image or no text node.
  - The cause isn't determined. It could be an expired or malformed redirect token, or the tracker rejecting the QA probe. A manual click test should confirm which.
  - This is the only FAIL, and it is on a conversion-incentive link.
- **Very long tracking URLs:** The plain-text part is 26,199 of 31,648 characters URLs (83%, above the 70% threshold). The redirect tokens run to several hundred characters each, which makes the text part mostly unreadable and a possible spam-filter signal.
- **Coverage gap:** 62 links were skipped by the click-likelihood ranking and not verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?...` has no `alt`. It appears to be the open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` stops screen readers announcing the filename.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem for low-vision users.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so clients ignore both. The charset declaration is therefore not applied in the HTML. The Content-Type header normally covers this.
- **Fonts:** `.ttf` web fonts are declared for HelveticaNow. I can't see the font-family fallback stacks in the truncated source, so I can't confirm whether they degrade safely.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tracking pixel URL carries `mi_u=225090073` (a recipient identifier), which is working as intended for tracking. The truncated body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. This is a gap in the relay and QA harness (AgentMail), not evidence that the sender failed authentication.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none was flagged as failing. The footer, the physical postal address and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the provided data, so they are **unverified**.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All probed links go through the `ablink.dsw.com` click-tracking redirect, so destination UTM parameters can't be read from the source. The final landing URLs weren't captured.
- The pixel campaign tag `mi_ecmp=20261115_P261649_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` shows a dated campaign ID, but it is cut off.
- Landing-page alignment with the "30% off boots" offer is **unverified** because only 12 links were probed and the destinations weren't resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or replace the opt-in CTA.** Click-test the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/B8dGsh9Fp7Sg...` link manually, regenerate the tracked link if it is expired, and make sure the anchor has text content.
2. **Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to the charset meta** (and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the IE meta), or drop the IE one.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
4. **Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel.**
5. **Shorten the plain-text version.** Use shorter link tokens, or reduce the number of tracked URLs in it.
6. **Improve QA coverage.** Capture the raw headers (Authentication-Results, List-Unsubscribe), follow redirects to record final URLs and UTMs, and probe all links or a larger sample.
