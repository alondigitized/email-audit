## Executive summary

- Well, this one's different — no pink floral nonsense in sight, finally. Instead I'm looking at a Skechers Slip-ins John Deere Waterproof boot, which, credit where it's due, is an actual men's shoe with real tread and a rugged look to it. "Still thinking about it?" — no, I wasn't, but I get the browse-abandonment angle and at least the shoe they picked to remind me about is one I could plausibly have looked at.
- The problem is the name of the thing: "Relaxed Fit: Respected - Tanzier." I have no idea what that means and it doesn't tell me anything about comfort, arch support, or how it fits. - I'd probably tap on the boot photo itself since it's genuinely eye-catching, but I'm not clicking through that "here's six more options" grid — too much, too small. Wouldn't forward this one, but I wouldn't roll my eyes at it either like I did with the floral shoe emails.

## What's working

- The hero shoe is a real men's boot with visible tread and waterproof branding — that's the kind of thing I actually buy.
- "Take Another Look" is a clear, honest button — no bait-and-switch language.
- The 20% off member banner near the bottom is legible and ties to a real date (thru 9/7).
- No sign of that pink floral shoe that's been haunting my inbox all week — I'll take the win.

## What's weak

- "Relaxed Fit: Respected - Tanzier" tells me nothing — no mention of cushioning, arch support, or slip-in ease, which is exactly what I care about.
- The six-thumbnail "more options" grid is too small to read comfortably on my phone — I'd need to zoom to tell the sneakers apart.
- The 20% off deal is buried way down near the bottom, after a giant static of shoe options — it should've been up top with the hero.
- This whole email is built around one boot I apparently looked at once — no real personalization beyond that.

- 1. Move the 20% off member banner right under the hero image — that's the actual reason I'd click, not "still thinking about it?"
- 2. Rewrite the product name in plain English: "Waterproof Slip-in Boot — All-Day Comfort" beats "Relaxed Fit: Respected - Tanzier."
- 3. Cut the six-thumbnail grid down to two or three larger images with readable labels.
- 4. Add one line about arch support or cushioning tech under the hero shoe — give me a reason beyond "you looked at this once."
