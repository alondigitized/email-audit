# THESE are our comfort MVPs🏆
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- "Comfort MVPs" got my attention as a headline — that's exactly the language I care about — but then the hero photo undercuts it: a young woman perched on a ledge in red joggers, wearing New Balance ML408s. That's a women's shoe, women's styling, nowhere near my closet. Skechers does show up further down, which is a nice change of pace from the Nike/adidas wall I've been climbing all week (like the 9/8 "Best Deals of the Season" send), but it's competing for real estate with Nike kids' sneakers, a word game, and a mobile wallet coupon.
- The $15/$30-off coupon code (STEPUP) is the most useful thing in this whole email — clear dollar amounts, clear thresholds, easy code. That's the kind of math I can do in my head at checkout. But I have to scroll past a lot of noise to get there, and nothing in the email tells me these shoes come in men's arch-support styles. I'd skim it for the coupon code and skip everything else — not worth forwarding to anyone.

## What's working

- "Comfort MVPs" as a headline speaks directly to what I shop for.
- The Skechers block calls out "Arch Fit" by name and "Earn 2X Pts" — that's a real signal for me as a member.
- The $15/$30 off coupon is dead simple — clear thresholds, clear code, no fine print buried up front.
- Mobile wallet "Add $30 / Add $15" buttons are a nice touch for someone who doesn't want to dig up a printed coupon.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe and model are women's — a woman in red joggers on a rooftop ledge, New Balance ML408 women's — nothing there is for me.
- Nike block is "Kids' Air Max Invigor" — also not my category, and it's prime real estate right under the hero.
- No mention of arch support, cushioning, or slip-on ease anywhere except the Skechers "Arch Fit" name-drop — the rest is just percentage-off noise.
- Word game and mobile wallet blocks feel like filler that pushes the one relevant offer (Skechers) further down the scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with a men's comfort shoe — swap the New Balance women's ML408 for something like a men's Fresh Foam or a Skechers Slip-in with an older male model, since "Comfort MVPs" as copy is already good, just point the visual at the right customer.
- 2. Move the Skechers Arch Fit block up top where the hero is — it's the only block actually selling comfort/arch support, and it's buried third.
- 3. Cut or shrink the Kids' Nike block — irrelevant to me and just adds scroll before I hit anything useful.
- 4. Add one line under the Skechers block about slip-on ease or all-day cushioning — give me the actual comfort pitch, not just a percentage.
