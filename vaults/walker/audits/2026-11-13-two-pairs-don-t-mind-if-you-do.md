## What's weak

- "Shop Early Access" is spelled "Acccess" in the headline. It looks sloppy and makes me trust the brand a little less.

# Technical Audit: "Two Pairs? Don't Mind If You Do. 😉" (Crocs)

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible head and preheader markup are mostly sound, with a few malformed `<meta>` tags and one unlabeled tracking pixel. Authentication, unsubscribe, UTM, and body-level checks can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains. The one visible example is the open pixel `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0`, which looks like Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Destination resolution and redirect health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel has no `alt` attribute.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. QA flagged it. Setting `alt=""` is the correct fix for a decorative pixel.
- **Second open-tracking call.** The email fires both the SFMC open pixel and the demdex pixel. This is not a defect, but it means two third-party requests per open.
- The `d_dpuid` value is a 40-character hex string, which looks like a hashed subscriber ID. I couldn't confirm what it is derived from.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed `<meta>` tags (confirmed in source):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it does not declare the charset.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
  - Without a valid charset declaration, the email depends on the `Content-Type` MIME header for encoding. That matters here because the preheader padding uses non-ASCII characters (`͏ ‌ ­`).
- **Positive findings:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - A viewport meta tag is present.
  - The `.mobileHide` / `.mobileView` utilities and the Gmail/Apple data-detector resets are in place.
  - The preheader uses `display:none !important`.
  - The web font `@font-face` rules use `font-display: swap`.
- **Unverified:** the `font-family` fallback stacks (truncated), `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and alt text on content images. Only the demdex pixel was flagged by QA.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible portion. The body was not available to check.
