# Find your comfort
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Now this one's actually built for a whole family of shoppers, and buried right in the middle is my aisle — "Hands Free Slip-Ins" with a guy about my age walking down the sidewalk in a bomber jacket. That's the first time all week Skechers has bothered to show me a man wearing a shoe I might buy. Points balance sitting up top, $5 reward on $75+, 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step near the bottom — there's real structure here, not just a cart-chaser.
- But it's a catalog dump, not a targeted email. I've got to scroll past Casuals (a woman on a stoop), Athletics (just a shoe on gravel, fine), then finally Comfort Tech with my Hands Free Slip-Ins and Arch Fit — good — then right back to Color with two more women's shots. It's shop-by-everything, which means it's really shop-by-nothing in particular for me.
- I'd open it again for the $5 reward and 20% off code, and I'd actually tap on the Hands Free Slip-Ins section since that's my exact ask — hands-free, comfort tech. Wouldn't forward it to anyone, but I wouldn't delete it in three seconds either. Middle of the road, leaning positive because they finally used a photo of a man my style.

## What's working

- "Hands Free Slip-Ins" section with a man in a jacket, comfortable shoes, walking — that's literally my category, first time this week.
- Arch Fit called out by name — that's a real feature I look for, not marketing fluff.
- $5 reward on $75+ and 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step are concrete, easy-to-understand offers.
- Points balance and "Hi, Walker" up top — feels like they know I'm a member, not a cold blast.
- Clean section dividers (Shop by Category, Comfort Tech, Color) make it easy to skip to what I want on my phone.

## What's weak

- Three of five lifestyle photos are women — Casuals, Classic Neutrals, and the crate photo — for an email that's clearly not just targeted at me.
- No hero shot up top — I have to scroll a long way past categories that don't apply before I hit anything relevant.
- The 20% off code "UPGRADE" doesn't say what it applies to beyond "Cozy Fit & Glide Step" — never heard of Glide Step, no price shown.
- It's one long scroll of everything in the store — feels like a general newsletter, not something built around what I've clicked before.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the men's Hands Free Slip-Ins photo as the hero, right under the $5 reward banner — that's the section that actually applies to me, don't make me scroll six screens to find it.
- 2. Put an actual price or dollar savings next to the 20% off code instead of just "Cozy Fit & Glide Step" — tell me what shoe and what it costs after the discount.
- 3. Cut the email in half by segmenting — if I've browsed men's comfort shoes before, don't show me three women's lifestyle shots in the same send.
- 4. Add a line under Arch Fit or Hands Free explaining the actual comfort tech (memory foam insole, no-tie slip-on) — a sentence, not just a label.
