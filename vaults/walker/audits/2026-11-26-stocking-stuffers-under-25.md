# Stocking stuffers under $25!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. It's the same pink banner and giant "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th, now with a "stocking stuffers under $25!" subject on top. I opened it hoping for cheap gift ideas, and the email doesn't deliver them. The stocking-stuffer line is a thin strip at the very top. Below it the email is the usual 30% hero, then a long scroll of women's boots, heels and a floral red pump.
- This is at least the eighth near-identical send in a week. Nothing here is for a 62-year-old guy who wants a cushioned, slip-on walking shoe. The men's link is a tiny "Shop Men" under the hero. The only shoe that looked like mine was a New Balance Fresh Foam near the bottom, at $69.99. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The 30% off banner with the code BFDEALS is easy to read and I know what the deal is.
- The "Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids" links give me a way to skip ahead.
- The New Balance running shoe at $69.99 is the one product that spoke to me. It's a brand I cross-shop, and it shows a real price.
- The "Shop by Size" section with a Wide Width button is useful for my feet.
- The DSW logo and layout match what I see on their site, so I trust the sender.

## What's weak

- The subject says stocking stuffers under $25, but I can't find a single item under $25 in the email.
- It's almost all women's boots, heels and a floral pump. Nothing is hands-free, cushioned or arch-supported.
- The offer is "30% OFF* (almost) everything" with an asterisk and a wall of fine print at the bottom. That doesn't feel like an honest deal.
- The email is very long, so on my phone I'd be scrolling forever. The product captions and the fine print are tiny.
- The "Dress the Halls" and "Party-Ready Styles" copy is cute but irrelevant to me. It's the same creative as the last week of sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject deliver. Put real under-$25 items (socks, insoles, shoe care, slippers) right under the header, and keep the 30% banner second.
- 2. Split sends by gender. Men should see a men's comfort block, with slip-ons, walking shoes and wide widths, above the women's boots.
- 3. Cut the frequency or vary the creative. Eight sends of the same pink banner trains me to delete.
- 4. Shorten the email and enlarge the product captions and the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts under $25 — socks, slippers & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfy gifts for him, all under $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned socks, insoles & slippers from $9.96. Plus 30% off with BFDEALS.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide widths in stock. Slip-ons and walkers for him, 30% off today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with click-wrapped links on `ablink.dsw.com`. The QA run found one failing link, the SMS opt-in CTA (HTTP 400), plus two deliverability warnings and two missing-alt warnings. The QA pass rate is 62%. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **HTTP 400** on `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/WaVazQ7z4EpS8D4HGu8_3A~~/AAQRxRA~/MLSFB6ixc2q3_…` (a roughly 900-character tracking URL).
  - This is the SMS-acquisition CTA, and it fails at the redirect hop rather than at the destination.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking domain can also be a probe artifact, such as a bot user-agent or a missing header. Re-test in a real client before treating it as a confirmed outage.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 71 links were probed and 59 were skipped, so the other links are unverified. The unsubscribe link is always probed and no failure was reported for it.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261126_P261758_V1_W-_Offer_Occas…` is a second tracking domain (`mi.dsw.com`) alongside `ablink.dsw.com`. This is a note, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/AJIX2Cl7tWZZA4uTYa8zvg~~/AAQRxRA~/2f4EHxZT…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/…/o.gif`
  - The second is a tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` to it so screen readers skip it. Check whether the first is content or decorative and give it matching alt text.
- **Ineffective meta tags (confirmed in `<head>`):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it declares nothing.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has the same problem.
  - The charset should come from the `Content-Type` header, but the meta tags are still malformed.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs make up 22,971 of 28,694 characters (80%, above the 70% threshold). This hurts the plain-text alternative's readability and is a spam-filter signal. It is likely caused by long Braze-wrapped URLs, so consider shorter link wrapping or a trimmed text part.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source or the QA data. The truncated body cannot be fully ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a measurement gap rather than a confirmed sender failure. Verify against the raw headers of an original delivery.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the unsubscribe link was probed with no failure reported. The footer and postal address fall outside the truncated source, so they are unverified rather than flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues confirmed. Every link is wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`, so the final destination URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible in the source or the QA data.
- The one place continuity is confirmed broken is the SMS opt-in CTA (section 2), which never reaches a landing page.
- Resolving the 12 probed redirects to their final URLs would let UTM and landing-page alignment be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/WaVazQ7z…`) in a real client and in Braze. If it still returns 400, fix the link or the campaign's SMS-keyword setup before the next send.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` tracking pixel and meaningful alt text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image.
3. Fix the `<meta>` tags to `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
4. Shrink the plain-text part by using shorter wrapped links or fewer links, to get under the 70% URL ratio.
5. Capture raw headers from a direct delivery to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com`.
6. Follow redirects for all material links to record the final URLs and UTM parameters for continuity checks.
