## What's working

- The hero shoe is a real slip-in with visible cushioned sole — that's my whole reason for shopping this brand, hands-free and comfortable.
- One clear CTA button ("TAKE ANOTHER LOOK") right under the product, no hunting for it.
- The 20% off member code (EXTRA20) is a real, visible offer with a deadline (9/14) printed right on the banner.
- No pink floral repeat — first email this week that didn't feel like the same recycled cart chase.
- Shop Now Pay Later and Find a Store options at the bottom are handy since I like checking store pickup before I commit.

## What's weak

- The recommendation grid mixes women's and kids-adjacent styles in with what should be men's slip-in options — a couple of those "See Ya There" sneakers look like women's cuts.
- No name, no points balance up top — feels like a stranger email even though I've got a Skechers Plus account with 1,000 points sitting there.

- The fine print about the 20% off (member-only, sale styles only, online-only) is buried at the very bottom in tiny gray text instead of near the code.

- 2. Fix the recommendation grid so it's actually men's slip-in styles only, not a mixed bag.
