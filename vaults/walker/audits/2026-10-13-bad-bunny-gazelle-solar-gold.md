## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed QA failure, a missing plain-text alternative (0 chars). It also loads several images over `http://`, has two images without alt text, and has a `<head>` with an ineffective charset declaration and an incomplete viewport meta. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM details could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **19 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity is unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **The tracking or open pixel loads over plain HTTP.** The image `SMWLpMefW7o2h4ECjx60tjt6BbxHLjIpDrxJKBmcHUo=452` has `src="http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1365d4814-…"`. Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or rewritten by clients, which would undercount opens.
- **The same image has no alt text.**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **HTTP images:** the `arrow` image (`b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`) is referenced four times from `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/…`. These may be blocked as non-HTTPS content.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `dv.adidas.com/o/92045bc6-0515-42fd-9ccb-f0bf1e6d5696?...` has no alt attribute. If it is a content image, it is invisible with images off or to screen readers.
  - The tracking pixel above also has no alt.
- **Charset meta:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it is not a valid charset declaration. This is low risk if the MIME header declares UTF-8.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0" />` has no `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may not scale as intended, although the media queries and `.container{width:100%!important}` partly compensate.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is present and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is set.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
