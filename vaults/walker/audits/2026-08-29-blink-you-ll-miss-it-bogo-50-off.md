# Blink & you'll miss it: Bogo 50% off
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off, again — that's six or seven days running now I've been getting this exact same offer from DSW, and today they've dressed it up with "blink and you'll miss it" urgency that doesn't hold water when I saw the identical deal on the 24th through the 28th. The grid underneath is fifteen items and I count maybe two — the athletic-look sneaker with the orange sole and one pair of what might be slip-on loafers — that a guy my age would even glance at; the rest is booties, leopard heels, strappy sandals, and wedges. It's a women's catalog wearing a subject line that pretends it's a flash sale.
- The free IMBOX spray add-on is a new wrinkle I haven't seen from them before, but I have no idea what it protects or why I'd drive to a store for it — feels like clutter bolted onto a stale promo. I'm not clicking, not forwarding, and honestly I'm getting tired enough of the daily repeat that I might just stop opening these.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly near the top with real terms ("buy any pair, get 50% off a second qualifying pair") — I can do that math myself.
- Shop by Size grid is right there below the fold, which is the one part of a shoe email I actually use.
- Shop Wide Width and Shop Wide Calf call-outs are a nice touch for a guy who's had trouble finding shoes that fit comfortably.
- Free shipping for VIPs and 15% off for opting into texts are visible perks, not buried.

## What's weak

- I've now gotten this same BOGO 50% off deal every single day for over a week — today's "blink and you'll miss it" urgency is not credible when it was also true yesterday, the day before, and the day before that.
- Fifteen products in the grid and it's almost entirely women's heels, mules, and boots — nothing about men's comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere on the page.
- The "FREE IMBOX Spray" banner is confusing — no explanation of what it actually does, just a black box with a can graphic.
- No loyalty/VIP tier detail beyond "5% back in Rewards" buried near the bottom — doesn't feel personalized to me as a repeat opener.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rotate in a men's-only module — even three or four comfort sneakers or slip-ons up top — because right now this reads as a women's email that happened to land in my inbox.
- 2. Stop reusing "blink and you'll miss it" copy on a deal that's run unchanged for a week; if it's not actually expiring, don't fake urgency — I'll just tune it out faster next time.
- 3. Explain what IMBOX spray is in one line before asking me to drive to a store for it.
- 4. Segment sends by browsing history — I've shown zero interest in heels or leopard print in five straight emails; give me the men's comfort aisle instead.
