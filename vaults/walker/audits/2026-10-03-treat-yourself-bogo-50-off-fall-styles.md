## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering bug, the empty greeting `Hi ,`, plus several markup and tracking-pixel defects. The pixel defects include a scheme-less `returnpath` URL and two `http://` images. The HTML was truncated, so headers, footer and the 33 click links were not available for verification.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less tracking pixel:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no `https://` or `//`. Clients resolve it as a relative path against the message or webmail origin, so the request fails or 404s. The automated QA did not flag this.
- **Unprobed links:** 33 click links on tracking domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their status is unverified.
- **Pixels in a hidden container:** the Krux beacons (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif`, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) and the SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?…`) sit inside `<div style="display:none !important">`. Some clients don't fetch images in `display:none` containers, which can undercount opens and impressions.
- **`http://` tracker:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=…` is plain HTTP and may be blocked as mixed content.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000.com)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`)
  - The returnpath `pixel.gif` also has no `alt`, which QA did not flag.
- **`http://` content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/…/dde00662-….png` is non-HTTPS and may be blocked or show a mixed-content warning. It is a content image on the brand's own domain, not a tracker, so it is the higher-impact one.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither takes effect.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Layout tables:** the visible tables have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Focus outline removed:** the global `a { outline: none }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders `Hi ,` because the name field resolved to empty. No fallback default (for example `Hi there,`) was applied, which is a broken personalization experience.
- The hidden `partner_uid` and subscriber ID values in the Krux URLs were populated. The failure is limited to the first-name field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address are cut off in the truncated HTML. Headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) were not supplied.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address on a dedicated sending subdomain. I can't confirm that authentication aligns with this domain.
- **Third-party data sharing:** the Krux match beacons pass `partner_uid=9153f33f…ced00`, which looks like a SHA-256 hash, and a subscriber ID, `partner_uid=121173391`, to a third party. Check that the privacy policy covers this and that these beacons honor opt-out and consent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** all 33 click links were skipped by the probe and are not in the truncated source, so UTM parameters and landing pages could not be checked.
- **Campaign identifier:** the only one visible is `campaignid=MKG_US_NA_U_NEW_ENGAGEDL60D_EN_10032026` in the Krux impression beacon.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name merge field, or suppress the greeting when it is empty. This is the only confirmed [FAIL].
2. Fix the returnpath pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`, and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Change `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` and `http://www.ink1000.com/…` to HTTPS, and add real `alt` text to `dde00662-…png`.
4. Change the meta tags to `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and restore a visible `:focus` style on links.
6. Move the open pixel out of the `display:none` container, for example into a 1×1 visible element at the bottom of the body.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and headers to verify unsubscribe, `List-Unsubscribe` and authentication, and UTM and landing-page continuity for the 33 click links.
