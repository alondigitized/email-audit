## Technical Audit — Shoe Carnival "BOGO Sandals" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Two click-tracking URLs contain a zero-width space character (U+200B) that breaks ASCII URL encoding, rendering those links unresolvable for recipients. Eight images lack `alt` text, with one tracking pixel also carrying an apparent unresolved merge token.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[CRITICAL] Zero-width space in two tracked URLs**

Both failing URLs contain U+200B at position 70, immediately after the `utm_campaign` value:

```
https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?rd&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260615_sandals_AM​
https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?rl&i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260615_sandals_AM​
```

The trailing `​` is the invisible U+200B character. When the click-redirect service attempts to forward the request, the non-ASCII byte in the URL will cause a 400/encode error. Recipients clicking those links receive a broken destination. Likely introduced via copy-paste from a rich-text editor or CMS field that silently injects invisible Unicode.

**[INFO] 28 tracking links skipped by probe** — all on `emltrk.com` redirect domains (SFMC). Expected behavior; no actionable issue.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] 8 images missing `alt` text**

| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| 4 product shots | `i8.amplience.net/s/scvl/...` |
| 2 email images | `image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/...` |
| 1 open-tracking pixel | `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=...` |
| 1 tracking pixel | `0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` |

Product images displayed with images-off (common in Outlook, corporate mail) will show as blank boxes with no context. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly to suppress broken-image icons.

**[MINOR] Malformed IE compatibility meta tag**

```html
<meta content="IE=edge" />
```

Missing `http-equiv` attribute — should be:

```html
<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />
```

Without `http-equiv`, this tag is invalid and has no effect in legacy IE/Outlook rendering engines.
