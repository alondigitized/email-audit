# SIGN UP FOR TEXT MESSAGES, EARN 500 POINTS
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's another adidas email, and this one is about as plain as it gets. It's a big photo of four young folks and a dog, then a headline telling me to sign up for texts and I'll get 500 adiClub points. There's no shoe anywhere in it. I'm 62 and I want cushioned, easy-on shoes. This didn't speak to me at all.
- The email also says "Hi ," with nothing after the comma. That's right at the top of the text, and it made me trust the sender a little less. Then there's the fine print: points land "within 5 days," and it's only for new text sign-ups. I'm not handing over my phone number for 500 points when I don't know what 500 points buys. I've gotten seven of these this week already. I'd mark it read and move on, and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The headline is big, black and easy to read on my phone without squinting.
- The offer is plain: text sign-up, 500 points. I didn't have to dig for it.
- The button is big and black, and it says "SIGN UP HERE" in clear letters.
- The page is short and uncluttered. I could take it all in with one thumb scroll.

## What's weak

- There's "Hi ," with a blank where my name should be. It looks sloppy.
- There's no shoe, no cushioning and nothing about arch support. The photo is young people in sweats, so it feels like it was made for my grandkids.
- It doesn't say what 500 points are worth. "Discount vouchers, rewards, and more" is vague, and I won't give out my number for that.
- The fine print is buried. I only learn at the bottom that points take up to 5 days. The "only for new text sign-ups" line is inside the paragraph, so it's easy to miss.
- I get a lot of adidas emails, and this one adds more clutter. Asking for my phone number on top of that feels pushy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the points value up top. Something like "500 points = $5 off" tells me what I'm getting. Without that, I can't tell if it's worth my time.
- 2. Fix the greeting. Use a fallback like "Hi there," when there's no name.
- 3. Use a real product photo, ideally a cushioned walking shoe, so it feels like it's for me.
- 4. Say in one line what the texts will be about and how often they'll come. I'd like to know I can reply STOP.
- 5. Move "new sign-ups only" up into the main copy so nobody feels tricked.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Points land in your account within 5 days. Join in under a minute.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure and ESP click-wrapping look sound. Most of the 22 QA failures are false positives from the doubled-domain check. The confirmed issues are a missing plain-text part (0 chars), an empty greeting slot, missing alt text on all linked images, and an `http://` tracking pixel.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Doubled-domain checks (19 FAIL): likely false positives.** Every flagged URL has the form `https://click.comms.adidas.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.adidas.com%2Fus...`. This is the standard ESP click-redirect wrapper, with the destination URL-encoded inside the path. The domain appears twice because of the wrapper, not a merge error. The destinations decode cleanly (`https://www.adidas.com/us`, `/us/go/signup/phonenumber_text`, `/us/account-login`, `/us/help`, privacy policy). The check should skip or decode known tracking-wrapper paths (`/CL0/`) before testing.
- **Redundant links:** `HeroImage`, `HeroCTA1` ("SIGN UP HERE") and `HeroCTA1Icon` all point to the same `/us/go/signup/phonenumber_text?token=...` destination. That is three adjacent tab stops for one action, and click attribution splits across three `cm_mmc` placements.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1274cfeaa-.../sgv-xr65M5NzGTCg3B0X_AsWWBzPhLOVPW0Xvzx81t4=452` is served over plain HTTP. Some clients block it, which undercounts opens, and it can trigger mixed-content handling.
- **Skipped probes:** 16 tracking links were not HTTP-probed, so destination status codes are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** `l2clptetxrt7ax9lgabs.png` (header logo link), `hic1xz4qube7jvarpmoe.png` and `yinhvkknpbvmeav6ykqx.png` (hero, mobile/desktop variants), `upsysvjqqehperxqygf9.png` (CTA icon), and the tracking pixel. Three of these are the only content inside `<a>` tags, so those links have no accessible name and screen readers will announce the raw URL. With images blocked, the logo link and hero show nothing.
- **Invalid CSS in the second `<style>` block:** `font-family: &#x27;AdihausDIN-Regular&#x27;` and the matching `src: url(&#x27;...&#x27;)` use HTML entities inside `<style>`. Entities aren't decoded there, so those `@font-face` rules are invalid. The first `<style>` block declares the same fonts correctly, so the impact is low. It does suggest an escaping bug in the template pipeline.
- **Hidden preheader padding:** the `display:none` preheader ("Get adidas updates right to your phone") is followed by repeated `‌​‍‎‏` characters. This is a standard preheader-spacer pattern and is fine.
- **Mobile/desktop toggling:** the `lattewb-*` classes use `display:none !important` inside `@media (max-width:599px)`. It is fine, but it depends on clients that support media queries.
- No other rendering problems are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting slot (QA FAIL):** the greeting renders as `Hi ,` with the name field empty. The truncated source doesn't show this line, so I'm relying on the QA data. It needs a fallback (`Hi there,`) or the greeting should be dropped when the name is missing.
- **Per-recipient values:** the signup URL carries a per-recipient `token=` (double-encoded, e.g. `%252F`), plus `cm_mmca4=0b2a02b2-...` and `campaign_id=c37bc5b5-...`. These resolved correctly with no unresolved `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing:** the text version is 0 chars. This is a deliverability and accessibility gap, since many spam filters penalize HTML-only mail.
- **Footer links present:** Privacy Statement, Support, and My Account are all there.
- **Not verifiable:** the truncated source and QA data don't show the unsubscribe link, the physical postal address, `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, or SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. I can't confirm compliance either way, so I'm not flagging it. Check the footer and raw headers.
- **Sender alignment:** the From domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` shares the organizational domain `adidas.com` with the click domain `click.comms.adidas.com`. This is consistent with a properly branded sending setup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Landing alignment:** the CTA goes to `/us/go/signup/phonenumber_text`, which matches the subject ("SIGN UP FOR TEXT MESSAGES"). Header and footer links go to `/us/` and `/us/account-login`.
- **Tracking scheme:** the email uses the `cm_mmc` / `cm_mmc1-2` / `cm_mmca3-4` convention plus AppsFlyer parameters (`af_adset`, `af_ad`, `af_channel`, `pid=email`, `is_retargeting=true`) instead of `utm_*`. This is internally consistent, with unique `af_ad` placements (`HeaderAdidasLogo0`, `HeroImage`, `HeroCTA1`, `HeroCTA1Icon`, `FooterAccount0`, etc.). Analytics must map these `cm_mmc` params, since `utm_*` is absent.
- **Tracking on utility links:** `is_retargeting=true` and AppsFlyer parameters are appended to utility links like Privacy and Support. This is harmless, but it pollutes those URLs.
- **Token handling:** the signup URL's `token=` is single-use or personal in nature, and nothing in the source shows its expiry. Confirm it still works for recipients who click days later.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text MIME part. This is the highest-impact fix.
2. Add a fallback for the empty first-name greeting (`Hi there,`), or conditionally omit it.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the logo (`adidas`), hero (`Sign up for text messages, earn 500 points`) and CTA icon (`alt=""` if it's decorative). Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Serve the open pixel over `https://`.
5. Merge `HeroImage`, `HeroCTA1` and `HeroCTA1Icon` into one link, or mark the redundant ones `tabindex="-1"` and `aria-hidden`.
6. Fix the HTML-entity escaping in the second `<style>` block, or remove the duplicate `@font-face` rules.
7. Update the QA tool to decode click-wrapper URLs (`/CL0/https:%2F%2F...`) before running the doubled-domain check. This would remove about 19 false failures and make the 19% pass rate meaningful.
8. Verify the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw message, since the truncated source didn't include them.
