# BadBo by adidas x Bad Bunny
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email. Since the 14th I've gotten the Black Friday countdown banners and the "up to 60% off" ones, and this one has none of that. It's a sneaker drop with a Bad Bunny name on it. I'm 62. I had to read the paragraph to work out who "Benito" is. These are hi-top suede fashion sneakers, not a walking shoe.
- The pictures are nice and the type is big and readable, so I didn't have to squint. But there's no price, no discount and no mention of cushioning or arch support. The only thing near an offer is the fine print saying purchase "is not guaranteed" and is first-come, first-served at a handful of stores. That tells me this isn't meant for me.
- Would I click? No. Forward it? No. I'd swipe it away and go back to comparing Hoka and New Balance.

## What's working

- The headline is big, black and plain, and I could read it without zooming.
- The two shoe photos are clean on a quiet grey background, so nothing fights for my attention.
- The two buttons, "Shop Now" and "Find Store Now," are large black blocks that are easy to tap with a thumb.
- Offering a local store is useful to someone like me who likes to try shoes on.
- The layout is a simple single column and I didn't have to hunt for anything.

## What's weak

- There's no price anywhere. I can't tell whether these are $90 or $190.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-on ease. That's what decides my purchase.
- It's a streetwear collab aimed at a much younger crowd. The "Benito" copy assumes I know the artist.
- The fine print says "first-come, first-served" and "purchase is not guaranteed," and that undercuts the "Available now" line at the top.
- The black "best sellers" banner at the top points somewhere different from the main story. It also looks a little cut off at the edge.
- The two stacked buttons do almost the same job, and it's unclear which one I should press.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send this to the people it fits, and send me something different. For an older comfort buyer, lead with a men's walking or casual shoe, with cushioning and arch support called out in the copy.
- 2. Put a price and the shoe's name on the hero. Something like "BadBo 1.0 Grey, $___."
- 3. Move the store-availability fine print up into the body, in plain words, instead of burying it at the bottom.
- 4. Cut to one main button and make the second one a text link.
- 5. Keep the preheader out of the tiny underlined text at the top, and have it give new information instead of repeating the headline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BadBo 1.0 in Grey, now in select adidas stores`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Two new BadBo colors, online and in store`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grey and Simple Brown suede, in stores this week while supplies last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Find the nearest store carrying BadBo before it sells out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text alternative (QA FAIL, 0 chars) and loads several images over insecure `http://`. Authentication could not be verified because of the relay. Merge tokens show no defects in the visible HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **19 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so the QA skipped the HTTP probe. Final destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking image.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a205f34576-48025ef7-11bd-46c7-8d45-ee58d36c8f0b-000000/ct13v2UoAqg5S6j8wPb9thp01BvchHyPbCSAIX2ZhR4=452` is served over `http://` and has no alt attribute. It looks like the open-tracking pixel. Because it is plain HTTP, open tracking can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and the request leaks the recipient's identifier in clear text.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** is present. It has no effect in most email clients, but it can strip referrer data where the email renders in a webview.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images.** The "arrow" image is loaded 4 times over HTTP: `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`. Clients that enforce HTTPS may block it, leaving a broken icon. The same asset should have an HTTPS URL.
- **Missing alt text.** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/5e58caf6-b9e4-46a5-b71b-0eecff8af634?...` has no alt attribute. If the image is decorative, it needs `alt=""`. If it carries content, it needs descriptive alt text. The tracking pixel above also lacks alt.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute, so it is not a valid charset declaration. Clients that ignore the MIME header charset may mis-render non-ASCII characters.
- **Viewport.** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0" />` omits `width=device-width`. Mobile clients that honor the viewport may not apply the `@media` breakpoints correctly.
- **Plain-text fallback missing** (see Compliance).
- `lang="en"` is set on `<html>`, which is correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated HTML shows no unrendered tokens, and the QA reports none. The rest of the body was truncated and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text part (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and it is a spam-filter signal.
- **Authentication-Results header not found (QA WARN).** The QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping it. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `us-news.comms.adidas.com` are unverified. This is a limitation of the test setup, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` headers.** None were available in the truncated HTML or the QA data. They cannot be confirmed or flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. All 19 links are wrapped in click-tracking redirects and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. The CTA destinations need to be resolved through the redirects to check them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Switch the `http://image.link.adidas.com/...b0c011a2...jpg` and `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` URLs to HTTPS.
3. Add `alt=""` to decorative images, including the tracking pixel. Add descriptive alt text to the `dv.adidas.com/o/5e58caf6...` image if it carries content.
4. Fix the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. Use `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` in the viewport meta.
6. Capture raw headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) by receiving the email directly instead of through the relay.
7. Extend the QA to follow the redirects on the tracking links, so UTM parameters and landing pages can be checked.
