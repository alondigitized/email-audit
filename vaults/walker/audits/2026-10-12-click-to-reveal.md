# Click to reveal…
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this time they're playing a guessing game with me. "Click to reveal" is a scratch-off gimmick, and I'm 62. I don't want to tap a mystery button to find out what a sale is. The orange hero is loud and easy to read, but the second "SURPRISE!" is covered by the model's head and shoulder. It reads "S RPRISE!" That looks sloppy.
- After the "20% off" HAYRIDE barrage since the 8th, including last night's "Final call for 20% OFF," this is a new angle. The offer is actually in the fine print at the bottom: $15 off $49, $20 off $99, or $60 off $199, with code CIDER. That's a decent deal, but they buried it under a button. Nothing here is about cushioned, slip-on comfort shoes, and the model is a young woman in a baseball cap and a skirt.
- Would I click? Maybe once, out of curiosity, and only because I want to know if it beats the 20%. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably leave it for later and forget.

## What's working

- The big "CLICK TO REVEAL" button is green on orange. I can see it and tap it with my thumb.
- The tiered discount at the bottom is clear once I find it: $15, $20 or $60 off, depending on how much I spend.
- The nav bar (Women, Men, Kids, Brands, Clearance) is large and simple, and Men is right there.
- The "Shopping made easy" icons (free shipping, in-store pickup, app) are easy to read. The barcode for in-store use is handy.
- The DSW logo is big and familiar, so I know who it's from.

## What's weak

- The offer is hidden on purpose. I shouldn't have to click to learn what the deal is, and it feels like bait.
- The model's head covers the second "SURPRISE!", so it reads "S RPRISE!". That looks like a render bug.
- No shoes are shown at all. It's a young woman in a skirt, with nothing for a man who wants comfort and arch support.
- The real offer is buried in small print at the bottom. The text is tiny and I'd have to zoom to read it.
- It's another send in a week that's already been a pile of DSW emails, and now they've switched codes from HAYRIDE to CIDER. That's confusing. Is the 20% over?

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "$15, $20 or $60 off your order," with the code CIDER shown right there. Make the button say "Shop Men's Comfort Shoes."
- 2. Fix the headline overlap so "SURPRISE!" isn't covered by the model.
- 3. Show real product, such as slip-on walking shoes with cushioning. Segment so a man my age sees men's comfort brands.
- 4. Say plainly whether this replaces the 20% HAYRIDE offer or stacks with it.
- 5. Make the offer details larger, and keep them out of the dark footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$15, $20 or $60 off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Code CIDER: up to $60 off shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $49, $99 or $199 and save. Online and in store.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort sneakers and slip-ons included. Ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked SMS opt-in CTA returned HTTP 400 in the QA probe, and the head has a malformed charset/compat meta declaration. Only 12 of 35 links were probed and the HTML source was truncated, so the checks below are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (QA FAIL, 400):** the link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/L8pDgrQzM0HobjMGFo_Y-A~~/AAQRxRA~/oDQvufpi…` (about 700 characters of encoded token).
  - It is a click-tracking redirect on the `ablink.dsw.com` branded domain, and it returned 400 instead of a 3xx redirect.
  - Possible causes are a malformed or expired tracking token, or the redirect rejecting the probe's request. The data doesn't separate these, so re-test the link from a real mail client.
  - If the 400 is real, the highest-value conversion link (the 15% SMS incentive) fails for every recipient.
- **Very long tracking URLs** inflate the plain-text part (see section 3).
- **Coverage gap:** 23 links were skipped, mostly footer, utility and social links. Their health is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Meta tags are malformed:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare a charset. Clients that rely on it may mis-decode characters.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it has no effect.
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 2 images):**
  - The `ablink.dsw.com/q/_HwGHEiWiT9l_uX2SRFDww~~/…` image is the open-tracking pixel.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261012_P261391_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…` is a second tracking pixel.
  - Both are decorative, so they should have `alt=""`. A missing attribute can make screen readers read out the filename.
- **Plain-text part (QA WARN):** 14,023 of 17,511 characters (about 80%) are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk, and it makes the text version hard to read.
- No other rendering issues are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject ("Click to reveal…") and the visible head contain no unresolved merge tags or Liquid/Braze syntax. The body was truncated, so tokens further down are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues confirmed.
- The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none is reported as failing.
- The footer physical address and the headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results) were not supplied. These items are unverified, not passed.
- The From domain `e.dsw.com` and the tracking domain `ablink.dsw.com` are both under `dsw.com`, which is consistent for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided.
- All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so the final destinations and any UTM parameters are not visible.
- The campaign ID `20261012_P261391_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…` appears in the `mi.dsw.com` pixel, which suggests a campaign naming convention.
- The 400 on the opt-in CTA means its landing experience couldn't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the "Opt-In to Texts" tracked link.** Open it from a real inbox. If it still returns 400, regenerate the link in the ESP, or use a direct `sms:` or landing-page URL.
2. **Correct the meta tags:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to both tracking pixels.
5. **Shorten the plain-text part:** add meaningful text content, and avoid listing every tracked URL in full.
6. **Re-run QA with the full HTML and headers** so the 23 skipped links, UTM parameters, `List-Unsubscribe` and authentication results can be checked.
