# BOGO 50% off is going...going…
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Same BOGO 50% off from DSW, eighth day I've counted — 24th through the 29th, and now the 30th too, this time with "is going...going…" trying to auction-house me into urgency. Doesn't work when I've seen the identical deal every single morning for over a week. Every product shot in this thing — loafers, mules, strappy heels, leopard flats, wedge clogs — is women's. Not one men's shoe, not one comfort/walking style, nothing for me anywhere in this scroll.
- The one thing that stopped my thumb was "New Balance" showing up under Everyone Loves These, but it's a women's colorway styled next to floral slingbacks and bow heels, so even that near-miss doesn't land for me. This is a mark-as-read, and honestly at this cadence I'm close to just unsubscribing — there's nothing here for a guy my age looking for a cushioned walking shoe, and the sameness of the offer isn't earning a second look anymore.

## What's working

- Clean logo and nav bar up top, easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- The BOGO 50% off headline is bold and unmissable, whatever else is wrong with it.
- "Details below" and the clearance/size grid at the bottom is at least honest about being findable, not buried in tiny print.
- Free shipping / in-store pickup / buy now pay later icons near the bottom are clear tap targets.

## What's weak

- Wall-to-wall women's footwear — loafers, mules, heels, sandals, clogs — with zero men's product anywhere in the send.
- Same BOGO 50% off deal I've now seen eight days straight; the "is going...going…" framing is fake urgency dressed up to look new.
- No mention of comfort, arch support, cushioning, or anything a walking-shoe shopper like me cares about.
- The "New Balance" line is the only brand name I recognize from my rotation, and it's a women's shoe, not a men's trainer.
- Preview text is just IMBOX spray filler junk, no real teaser copy at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men's shoppers into their own version of this email — swap in men's walking/casual styles (New Balance, Skechers, comfort brands) instead of running one all-women's creative to the whole list.
- 2. Retire the daily BOGO repeat or at least vary the hero art so it doesn't read as the same email eight days running.
- 3. Drop the fake "going...going" urgency language unless there's an actual end date attached — I've caught this bluff too many times now.
- 4. Lead with a men's comfort category (arch support, slip-on, walking) if DSW wants my click at all.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles: BOGO 50% off ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort — BOGO 50% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Balance & Skechers walking shoes, buy one get one 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned, slip-on, easy — all BOGO 50% off through Sunday`
