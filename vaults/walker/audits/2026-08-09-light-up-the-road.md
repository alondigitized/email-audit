# Light up the road
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Third HOKA in about a week and a half, and now it's the Bondi 9 and Clifton PRO getting a paint job. Neon green, orange-yellow fade, pink shimmer — that's a lot of color for a guy who just wants a shoe that doesn't hurt his feet by mile two. I get that they're trying to freshen up the same models I've already seen (Clifton PRO again, still), but "new colorways" isn't really an offer, it's a merchandising refresh, and there's zero mention of price, cushioning, or arch support anywhere in this email.
- There's men's and women's Bondi 9 and Clifton PRO laid out side by side, which is at least organized clean and easy to scan on my phone. But nothing here tells me why I'd want THIS shoe over the ones already sitting in my closet or the ones HOKA already showed me twice this month. No sale, no story about comfort, just colors.
- I wouldn't click. Wouldn't forward it either — nobody I know picks shoes by "eye-catching colorway." Straight to read-and-archive.

## What's working

- Clean grid layout — four shoe tiles labeled by model and gender, easy to scan without pinching to zoom.
- Hero shot of the Bondi 9 in that orange-to-yellow fade is genuinely eye-catching, I'll give them that.
- Two clear "Shop New Colors" buttons, so I'm not hunting for what to tap.
- No render bugs — images loaded fine, text is legible, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- Not one word about cushioning, arch support, or fit — the stuff that actually gets me to buy a HOKA.
- No price, no discount, no loyalty mention. This reads as a catalog update, not something worth opening my wallet for.
- Colors like neon green and shimmer pink skew younger and flashier than what a 62-year-old wants walking the dog.
- Clifton PRO again — that's at least the fourth time this shoe has shown up in my inbox since July.
- "Glimmers Pack" at the bottom feels like a completely different, younger audience got tacked onto the same email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with fit and function, not color — one line like "Same plush Bondi 9 comfort, now in colors that pop" would at least connect the visual to something I care about.
- 2. Add an actual offer. A colorway refresh with no price incentive gives me no reason to act now.
- 3. Cut or separate the Glimmers Pack section — it clashes with the rest of the email and doesn't fit a comfort-first shopper.
- 4. Vary the hero model — I've now seen the Clifton PRO in four different sends; give it a rest and push Bondi 9 or something newer.
- 5. Offer at least one muted/neutral colorway option for guys like me who don't want to look like a highlighter on my walk.

# HOKA "Light up the road" Email — Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Two links resolve to HTTP 403 (returns link + unsubscribe link), the plain-text MIME part is empty, and all content images lack `alt` attributes — otherwise the template's tracking/redirect infrastructure (Cordial ESP) is functioning normally.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Returns link 403**: link text "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/" resolves through the Cordial redirect (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...`) to a decoded destination of `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&...` — the redirect endpoint itself returns 403, not the destination, so this is a tracking-domain/link-wrapping failure rather than a bad landing URL.
- **Unsubscribe link 403**: same redirect domain, decoded destination is `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&...` — also 403. This is the more serious of the two (see Compliance below).
- All other probed links (12 material links) passed; 14 non-material (footer/social) links were deprioritized and not probed.
- Click-tracking wrapper is Cordial (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/...`) with JWT-encoded params (`jwtH`/`jwtP`/`jwtS`) carrying the destination URL, UTM string, and a hashed email (`hmail`) — structurally normal for this ESP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- All 12 content/footer images are missing `alt` text, including the hero (`US_20260809_NewHero.jpg`), subcopy, product shots, and footer logo (`LogoFooterPrimary.png`). With images blocked (default in most mail clients), the email renders with no fallback text.
- Two tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`) also lack `alt`, but this is expected/inconsequential for 1x1 open-tracking pixels.
- CSS includes malformed declarations in the `@media (max-width:480px)` block: `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` — missing numeric values make these rules invalid and they will be dropped by parsers, likely an unintended side effect of a templating token not being substituted.
