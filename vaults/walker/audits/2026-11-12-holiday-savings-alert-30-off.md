# Holiday savings alert: 30% off 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the third boots email I've gotten today or yesterday. This morning it was "30% OFF BOOTS!? DECK YEAH!" and then "Click for 30% off thousands of NEW boot styles." Now it's "Holiday savings alert," and the offer hasn't changed. The "30%" is huge and I can read it without my glasses, and the code BOOTFRIDAY is right there. But I've seen all of this already.
- The pictures are the problem for me. Of six shoes, I count a loafer, a mary jane, a fuzzy lace-up bootie, a women's snow boot, a snakeskin sneaker and a pink high heel. None of them are for a 62-year-old man. There's a "SHOP MEN" link under the banner, but it's small and sits between other links. Nothing here mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-ons, which is what I shop for.
- I probably wouldn't click. I'd skim it and mark it read, and I wouldn't forward it to anyone. If I needed boots I'd tap "Shop Men," but I'd also check Brooks and Skechers first.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF BOOTS*" on green is easy to read on my phone with no zooming.
- The code BOOTFRIDAY is printed right under the offer, so there's no hunting for it.
- "SHOP BOOTS" is a big white button that's easy to tap.
- The "Shop by Size" grid with "Wide Width" is useful. I have wide feet and appreciate that.
- Free in-store pickup and free shipping are shown in simple icons.

## What's weak

- The "So New, So You" photos are almost all women's styles, and a pink stiletto isn't for me.
- "SHOP MEN" is a tiny underlined link buried in a row of four. It should be a button.
- It repeats the boots deal I've already seen twice. Three near-identical emails in about a day feels like nagging.
- The asterisk sends me to a wall of tiny fine print at the bottom, including brand exclusions. I can't tell whether the boots I'd want are included.
- Nothing speaks to comfort, cushioning or arch support. There are no reviews and no prices.
- The "gifts for kids" and Sonic block is off-topic for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show men's boots in the hero grid. Use comfort brands, with a line like "Cushioned, all-day walking boots."
- 2. Make "Shop Men" a real button next to "Shop Boots," not a small link.
- 3. Space out the sends. Combine the boots offers into one email instead of three.
- 4. Add one line of fine print above the fold saying which brands are included.
- 5. Show a sale price on at least one boot so I can do the price-vs-quality math.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure is mostly standard. The main confirmed problem is a failing SMS opt-in CTA (HTTP 400). Secondary issues are an ineffective charset meta tag, a zoom-disabling viewport, and bloated tracking URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and compliance elements could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by QA probe):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **HTTP 400**. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ffZyjSllE18hPkP0jDRpEA~~/AAQRxRA~/-PQPfnXkVn4N0R_...`. This link is a conversion path for SMS acquisition.
  - A 400 on a wrapped tracker can come from a malformed redirect target. It can also come from the click-tracker rejecting a non-browser probe. Re-test it in a real client before treating it as definitely broken.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 71 links were probed (59 skipped by priority ranking), so the 62% pass rate reflects partial coverage. Footer and social links are unverified.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 26,248 of 30,627 plain-text characters (about 86%) are URLs. The long `ablink.dsw.com/.../~~` tracking redirects make the text part spam-prone and nearly unreadable.
- **Open pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261112_P261649_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` works as a tracking pixel. The `mi_u=225090073` parameter looks like a per-recipient identifier embedded in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 2 images):**
  - A linked image at `ablink.dsw.com/q/2yeHHk4n2jOEmOYLZefXSg~~/...`. If this is content or a CTA, it is an accessibility failure.
  - `o.gif`, the tracking pixel. This is low severity, but it should have `alt=""`.
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it does nothing. The same applies to `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`, which has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. This risks encoding problems in some clients, such as the 🎁 in the subject or special characters in the body, if the MIME header doesn't declare UTF-8.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- Dark-mode handling is declared via `color-scheme`, `supported-color-schemes` and `prefers-color-scheme` classes. No defect was found in the visible portion. `.dm-bg-white` maps to `#ffffff`, which is intentional.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA note says this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender defect. Verify against the original headers.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the QA run reports no unsubscribe-link failure, but the footer is outside the truncated source. I can't confirm the presence of the postal address or `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't verify. All links are wrapped in opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and destination URLs aren't visible. Campaign identity appears only in the pixel's `mi_ecmp=20261112_P261649_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...`. No landing-page mismatches were evidenced.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the SMS opt-in link first.** Re-test `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ffZyjSllE18hPkP0jDRpEA~~` in a real browser and mobile client. If it fails there too, repair the redirect target or the tracker config.
2. Add `alt` text to the linked ablink image, and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
3. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Shorten the plain-text version. Use fewer links or shorter tracking domains/paths to improve text-to-URL ratio.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and original headers. That would confirm the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and UTM parameters on the final landing URLs.
