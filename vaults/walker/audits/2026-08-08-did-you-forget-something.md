# Did you forget something?

## Executive summary

- Well, at least the top shoe's got some substance to it — that white, red and blue Arch Fit D'Lites has a real chunky sole and looks like something I'd actually consider. But then it's right back to the same routine: a Care Bears kids' sneaker and a hot pink slip-on staring back at me like this is somehow "my" abandoned cart. I did not put a pink pair of anything in a cart, I promise you that.
- The "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner up top is at least clear about what it wants from me, and I'll give them credit for that. But scroll down and it's the exact same grab-bag of six mixed items I've been seeing all week, capped off with a 25% off kids' back-to-school sale that has nothing to do with me. - Not clicking, not forwarding. I'd mark it read and move on — though that top shoe is the one I'd actually go look up on my own later if I remembered the name.

## What's working

- The hero shoe — Arch Fit D'Lites 330 Star Line — has real cushioning and structure, matches the comfort-first look I'm after.
- "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner is big, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is bold and unambiguous — I know exactly what they want me to do.
- Men's Delson 3.0 - Roth Shoes in the grid is another one I'd actually consider.

## What's weak

- Care Bears kids' sneaker and hot pink women's shoes right up top again — same mismatched cart items I've flagged in the last three emails from this brand.
- The 25% off kids' back-to-school deal at the bottom is completely irrelevant to me — I'm not shopping for kids.
- No sizing, arch-support callouts, or fit info on any product — just pictures and names.
- This is basically the same "you forgot something" cart template as 8/6 and 8/7, just re-skinned. Feels automated, not personal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Filter the "abandoned cart" grid by what I actually looked at, or at minimum by gender/category signals from my browsing — stop showing me kids' and women's items.
- 2. Drop the kids' back-to-school 25% off block entirely for a customer who's never shown interest in kids' shoes — swap in a men's comfort offer instead.
- 3. Lead with the Arch Fit D'Lites and Delson 3.0 since those are the two items that'd actually get me to click — put them first, not buried in a six-pack grid.
- 4. Add one line of fit/comfort detail under the hero shoe — arch support, cushioning, slip-on or lace — since that's what decides it for me.
