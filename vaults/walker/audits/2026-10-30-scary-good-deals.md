- "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the button, and that makes me wary.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code-level structure is mostly sound: it has `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and a mobile media query. Three things couldn't be verified: authentication headers, the tracking-link destinations, and the footer, because the source was truncated. The confirmed defects are two malformed `<meta>` tags and one alt-less tracking image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified link destinations (QA WARN).** 16 links were skipped from HTTP probing because they are click-redirect domains. The visible host is `click.crocs-email.com`, an ESP redirect wrapper. Final destinations, redirect chains and 404s can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Open pixel.** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10206&bmt=0` is 1x1 with `alt=""` inside a `height:1px;overflow:hidden` div. No issue.
- **Third-party pixel.** A request to `demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` fires an Adobe Audience Manager "email opened" event. It carries a per-recipient `d_dpuid`, so it is a second tracker alongside the ESP open pixel. It is not a defect, but it adds a third-party request and a privacy-disclosure dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image missing alt (QA WARN).** The `demdex.net/event` image has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers may announce the URL. Fix: `alt=""`.
- **Ineffective meta tags.** Both are in `<head>`:
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.

  Without the attribute, neither tag does anything. Charset then depends on the MIME `Content-Type` header, which wasn't provided, so non-ASCII characters (the 😱 in the subject, the preheader padding characters) rely on that header being correct.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, `<title>Crocs</title>`, viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, Gmail hack (`u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`), and Apple data-detector overrides.
- **Preheader.** The `BOO! 31% off…` text is hidden with `display:none !important`, followed by zero-width/soft-hyphen padding. This is standard.
- **Not assessable:** font fallback stacks, table roles and alt text on content images, because the source was truncated. The `TT Crocs` `@font-face` rules load from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The rest of the body was truncated, so this applies to the visible portion only.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results missing (QA WARN).** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not adding the header, so it is likely an artifact of the test setup rather than a sender fault. Verify it from the raw message headers at the original receiving MTA.
- **From:** `Crocs <email@crocs-email.com>`. The sender domain matches the tracking domain (`click.crocs-email.com`), which is good for alignment.
- **Unverifiable from the provided data:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. None appear in the truncated source or the QA output. These are not confirmed violations.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. Every link is wrapped in the `click.crocs-email.com` redirect, and the probe was skipped. UTM parameters are not visible, and they may be appended at redirect time. Landing-page alignment with the "31% off select styles" offer is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex.net/event` image.
2. Correct the head meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Or drop them, and confirm the MIME header declares UTF-8.
3. Inspect the raw headers for `Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`, `Received-SPF` and `List-Unsubscribe`. If the relay is stripping them, capture them upstream of AgentMail.
4. Resolve the 16 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe, or a real click-through in the browser. That would confirm the final URLs, status codes, UTM parameters and whether the 31% off offer lands on the matching page.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, not the truncated copy, to cover the footer, unsubscribe and address, and the content-image alt text.
