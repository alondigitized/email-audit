# Want 20% off? Youuuu got it.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and it's the same "20% off with code JINGLE" I got earlier today in "Open for 20% off!" The giant "20% OFF*" on green is still easy to read on my phone. The difference is that the UGG slipper run is gone. Now it's court sneakers, which is not what I shop for. I want slip-on, cushioned, arch-supported shoes. This is all flat-soled fashion sneakers with laces, aimed at someone half my age.
- The email is clean, and there's a "Shop wide width" button, which is a nice touch. But I get a DSW email every day, and this one is a repeat of this morning's offer. I'd skim it, maybe tap the Men link out of habit, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" headline in huge type on green is readable without zooming.
- The code JINGLE is right there in the hero, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- There's a "Shop Men" link. At least they don't assume I'm shopping for women.
- "Shop wide width" and "Shop wide calf" buttons are big and black. That's real help for a guy with wider feet.
- The size grid is large and easy to tap.
- The "Valid October 22-25" date gives me a real deadline.

## What's weak

- Every shoe pictured is a lace-up court sneaker, mostly women's styles. There's no slip-on, no cushioning, and no arch support.
- It's the same 20% code as this morning's send. It feels like they're just repeating themselves.
- The exclusions list is long. It leaves out Skechers, Clarks, and others I actually wear, and it's buried in tiny print at the bottom. The asterisk says "regular-price styles only."
- The "$64.99" bubble doesn't say whether that's the sale price or the starting price, and there's no brand name on the page.
- The "Meet your match" copy and the hand holding a shoe are cute, but they don't tell me anything about comfort.
- The top "Shop Now" button is pale pink on green. It's not a strong tap target.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's comfort block. Show slip-on walking shoes from Skechers, Hoka, and Brooks with a price. Right now the pictures only suit a younger shopper.
- 2. Don't send the same 20% code twice in one day. Pick one send, or give me a different offer.
- 3. Move the exclusions up near the hero, or say which brands qualify. Don't hide them at the bottom.
- 4. Make "Shop Now" a dark, high-contrast button.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/SendGrid-style send with branded click-tracking (`ablink.dsw.com`). One probed CTA, the SMS opt-in, returned HTTP 400. The HTML source was truncated, so compliance, token and UTM checks are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by probe):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned **400**. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZYxrXwPXPIz4sqRTPCVsgA~~/AAQRxRA~/N9UG3bsr…` (~900 chars). The QA output shows the link text but no resolved destination.
  - A 400 could mean the tracked redirect is malformed or expired. It could also mean the SMS opt-in target rejects non-mobile or non-browser probes. The data doesn't say which, so verify manually on a mobile client.
- **Opaque tracking:** All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/…~~` redirects. Final destinations can't be read from the source. Only 12 of 60 links were probed (48 skipped, deprioritized as footer or social links), so coverage is partial.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 2 images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/chgVRQkMrn6qYzNUpB27lw~~/AAQRxRA~/xlfYfvZV…`. Whether it's a content image or a tracking pixel is unclear from the data.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261022_P261582_V1_W-_Offer_Value`. This is a 1×1 tracking pixel (Movable Ink). Low severity, but it should carry `alt=""` and ideally `role="presentation"`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Meta tags missing `http-equiv`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they have no effect. Charset is still likely declared by the Content-Type header. This may also be an artifact of the capture or sanitizer.
- **Dark mode:** Custom `dm-*` classes and `color-scheme` meta tags are present. No defects are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `${ }`, `%%`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated source can't rule them out further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found. QA notes it is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a gap in the test harness, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe:** The QA run always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address were not in the data provided, so they are unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 19,992 of 23,974 characters (~83%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk driven by the long tracked URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated because the tracked links hide destinations.
- Campaign ID `20261022_P261582_V1_W-_Offer_Value` appears only in the Movable Ink pixel URL.
- Landing page alignment can't be confirmed, and the broken opt-in link means one destination is known to fail.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ZYxrXwPX…`) on a real mobile client. If the 400 is real, fix or regenerate the tracked link, or point to a direct `sms:` or landing URL.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `alt=""` to the Movable Ink `o.gif` pixel. Add meaningful alt text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image if it's visible content.
4. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags, if they're missing in the real source and not just in the capture.
5. Shorten the plain-text version, for example by using fewer or shorter tracked links, or dropping tracking on non-essential links.
6. Re-run with full HTML, raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and resolved redirect chains to close the unverified gaps in sections 4–6.
