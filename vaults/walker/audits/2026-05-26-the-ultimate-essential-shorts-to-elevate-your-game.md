# Technical Audit — adidas "The ultimate essential shorts to elevate your game"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders on standard ESP infrastructure (Salesforce/MessageGears-style tracking domains) but ships with a malformed redirect URL, a missing plain-text part, and several non-HTTPS image references that will harm deliverability and accessibility.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken redirect — unknown URL scheme.** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/794b4f7d-a584-4a99-8cee-46fa44cc9181?cp_tp=v3.eJz…` resolves to `unknown url type: '/g/794b4f7d-a584-4a99-8cee-46fa44cc9181?txn=d1464700-5922-11f1-b5b9-4e61e507eb6e'`. The decoded `cp_tp` payload is producing a relative path (`/g/...`) without a host, which the redirector cannot follow. Clicks on this CTA will fail.
- 34 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the probe (`click.comms.adidas.com`, `dv.adidas.com`) — coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes** on at least 4 images, including a hero/product asset and the primary CTA image:
  - `b89f1065-48c3-4e59-9035-701fd3a63f68.png`
  - `fe76eca3-dd45-4f32-ad8a-31444137bc7b.jpg`
  - `55e6a97f-b40b-4697-bf65-9871436adb35.jpg`
  - `794b4f7d-a584-4a99-8cee-46fa44cc9181` (the CTA-linked image)
- **Non-HTTPS image sources** will be blocked or downgraded by Gmail/Outlook proxy fetchers:
  - `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` ("arrow", referenced 6×)

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text alternative is 0 chars.** Missing/empty `text/plain` MIME part. This degrades inbox placement (Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender guidance) and breaks accessibility for text-only clients. Confirmed by QA.
- `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW">` and `referrer="no-referrer"` present — appropriate.
- Unsubscribe/list-management headers were not in the truncated source provided; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers from this audit. Verify out-of-band.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot evaluate. The primary CTA redirect (§2) does not resolve to a landing URL, so UTM parameters and destination alignment with `adidas.com` are unverifiable from this sample.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `cp_tp` template generating the malformed `/g/…` relative path inside the v3 payload — confirm the encoded redirect resolves to an absolute `https://www.adidas.com/...` URL before send.
2. Switch all `http://image.link.adidas.com` and `http://click.comms.adidas.com` references to `https://` in the template; both hosts already serve TLS.
3. Add `alt` attributes to the 4 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable only for the tracking pixel).
4. Generate a real plain-text MIME part from the HTML body — empty text alternatives are a known deliverability liability for bulk senders.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers are present on the actual envelope (not in the HTML body).
6. Re-probe the 34 skipped tracking links via a follower that allows the `click.comms.adidas.com` / `dv.adidas.com` 302 chain to confirm end-to-end click integrity.
