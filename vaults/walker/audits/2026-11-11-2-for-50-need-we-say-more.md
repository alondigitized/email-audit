# 2 for $50. Need We Say More? 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again. That's my sixth one in about two weeks. Now it's "2 for $50," which is the same two-pair idea as the 11/6 "Buy 2, 30% off" and the 11/10 "2 Pairs for $50" I got yesterday. I can read the big white headline on the green block without squinting, and the offer is easy to understand. But I feel like I'm getting the same email on repeat with a new hat on.
- The hero clog is a purple Classic Clog with snowflakes around it. It's pretty, but it's not for me. The "Recommended for You" row is Classic Clog, Baya Clog, Kids' Classic Clog and Toddler Classic Clog. Two of those are for kids. At 62 I'm not buying a toddler clog, though a grandkid might. Nothing here talks about arch support, cushioning or walking, and nothing is slip-on in a way that says "easy on the feet" to me.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted a pair of clogs I know where to find them, and I've seen this deal three times this week.

## What's working

- "2 FOR $50" is big, white on dark green, and I can read it on my phone with no zoom.
- There's one clear button, "Shop Early Access," and it's easy to tap.
- The "Offer ends November 14th" date at the bottom is a real deadline, so I know how long I have.
- The look matches the Crocs emails I've been getting, so I trust that it's really from them.
- Free shipping is up top in the black bar, where I can see it right away.

## What's weak

- I've seen this exact deal twice in the last week (11/6 and 11/10). The "Early Access" banner doesn't make it feel new, especially when it's printed three times.
- The deadline is in tiny gray print at the very bottom. The top of the email says nothing about when it ends.
- Half the "Recommended for You" row is kid and toddler clogs. That tells me they don't know who I am.
- Four near-identical black clogs don't give me a reason to pick one. There are no prices, no comfort details and no reviews.
- The Afterpay block takes up a lot of space. "Buy now, pay later" isn't how I shop, and the small print about late fees puts me off.
- "Exclusions apply" and "discount applied in cart" make me wonder which styles count. Nothing shows me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the frequency, or make each send clearly different. Right now the same 2-for-$50 offer shows up almost daily.
- 2. Put the end date next to the headline, like "2 for $50 — ends Nov 14."
- 3. Make the recommendations fit the reader. For me that means men's slip-on comfort styles with a line like "cushioned, easy on, easy off," and no kids' items.
- 4. Put a price on each product and say which styles qualify for the deal.
- 5. Shrink the Afterpay block and move it below the products.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crocs, "2 for $50. Need We Say More? 👀"

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is generally well built, with responsive CSS, Gmail and Apple client hacks, a preheader and a hidden open pixel. It has a malformed `<meta>` head, several third-party tracking images without `alt`, and recipient email addresses embedded in image URLs. The HTML is truncated and the authentication headers were unavailable, so compliance and UTM checks are partly unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 23 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.crocs-email.com`). Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10213&bmt=0` is a 1×1 image with `alt=""` inside a hidden `<div style="height:1px;overflow:hidden">`. It is correctly formed, with `&amp;` escaped.
- **Third-party pixels:** The email loads two additional trackers beyond the ESP pixel:
  - `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` (Adobe Audience Manager)
  - `https://10745993.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/…/i/1` through `/i/4` (Marketing Cloud Personalization)
- Each extra third-party host adds a point of failure and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration is valid. The charset only works if the HTTP `Content-Type` header supplies it. The fix is `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 5 images):**
  - the `demdex.net/event` pixel
  - `igodigital.com/rr/v2/685c6ddd06fd7c003f1cd785/i/1` through `/i/4`
  - If the `igodigital` images are visible recommendation images, screen readers get no description. If they are tracking pixels, they need `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. Either way the attribute is missing.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` doesn't distinguish this campaign. This is minor.
- **Good practices:** `lang="en"` is set, the viewport meta is present, the `max-width:639px` breakpoint is defined, and `font-display: swap` is used on the `TT Crocs` web fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.
- The recipient's email address (`walker@agentmail.to`) is written into the path of the `igodigital.com` image URLs. These are resolved correctly. However, they expose the address in plaintext in third-party request logs, referrers and any proxy caching of image URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** The QA warning "Authentication-Results header not found" comes from the AgentMail relay. It is a limitation of the test setup, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `email@crocs-email.com` are unverified. This is not a confirmed defect.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The footer, the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source or the supplied headers. They cannot be verified either way.
- **Sending domain:** The `From` domain `crocs-email.com` matches the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination URLs and UTM parameters sit behind the skipped redirect links and the truncated source, so continuity cannot be assessed. No mismatches are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the meta tags by adding `http-equiv="Content-Type"` (or use `<meta charset>`) and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` for the IE edge setting.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex.net` and `igodigital.com` pixels. If any `igodigital` image is a visible product tile, give it descriptive alt text.
3. Remove the recipient email address from the `igodigital` image URLs by using an opaque ID or hash.
4. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the 23 redirect links with a redirect-following probe, and confirm the unsubscribe link, the postal address and UTM parameters.
5. Capture the raw message headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) from a direct inbox delivery. The AgentMail relay strips or omits them.
6. Make the `<title>` campaign-specific. This is optional.
