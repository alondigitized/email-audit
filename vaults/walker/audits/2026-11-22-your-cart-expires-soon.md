# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Same cart nag again. I've seen this black women's Contour Foam Slip-in with the rose-gold eyelets in the 11/20 sends ("Your cart expires soon!" and "Your Items Are Waiting!") and the 11/21 one. This isn't a new email, it's the same one in a new coat. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" is a cute line, but it's a women's shoe in a cart I never filled on purpose.
- The row of six "more great options" is where I'd actually look. Three of them are men's: the John Deere waterproof slip-ins, the Murette slipper and the Orson boot. That's my kind of shoe. The 30% off for members banner is down in the middle, after all the scrolling. The 11/21 "Comfort for every budget" email put it right under my name and points, and that worked better. I'd skim this one and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero shoe is big and clear, with a plain "Complete Your Order" button.
- Several men's Slip-ins are in the grid: the John Deere waterproof, the Murette slipper and the Orson boot. The hands-free comfort angle is there.
- The red 30% OFF banner stands out, and it's clear it's for members and ends 11/22.
- The text is large enough to read on my phone. The category list (Women, Men, Kids) has big tap targets.
- It looks like the Skechers site, and the 30% matches what I've been seeing all week, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's shoe. I'm a 62-year-old man, and it's the fourth or fifth time I've seen it.
- The best offer is buried under six products and a black "Shop All Best Sellers" bar. The top says nothing about price.
- Nothing is addressed to me, with no "Hi, Walker" and no points balance, even though I'm a member.
- The shoe captions give no price, no arch support or cushioning detail, and no reviews. I can't do any price-vs-quality math.
- The "expires soon" subject isn't backed by a visible deadline in the body. The only date is in the fine print: valid through 11/22.
- The footer is crowded: the app, curbside pickup, Afterpay/Klarna, texting and social links. It's a lot to scroll through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% off member banner to the top, under a "Hi, Walker, you have 1000 points" line. Put the end date in big type.
- 2. Stop sending the same cart reminder over and over. One a day is plenty. Rotate in a men's item from my browsing history.
- 3. Add a price, a star rating and a short fit note like "arch fit, machine washable" under each shoe.
- 4. Trim the footer to a few links.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, 30% off ends tonight on your Slip-ins`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Slip-ins are in the cart, 30% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 30% sitewide through 11/22. Auto-applied at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hands-free comfort, still in your cart. Plus men's picks from $X.`
