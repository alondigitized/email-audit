- First thing I saw when I opened this was the BOGO at the top banner — buy one, get one 50% off, or 50% off a single pair. That grabbed me. Skechers knows I'm not going to buy a new pair every week, so a discount that works either way feels like they're actually thinking about how I shop, not just pushing me into a bundle.

I've got bad knees, I don't want to be bending down to tie laces, and Skechers has been doing this slip-in technology better than anyone. Seeing it called out with its own section — with multiple colorways lined up — that felt like the email was written for me.

- **BOGO at the very top** — no hunting for the offer, it's the first thing I see, big and plain.
- **"Cloud-like cushioning" and "Lightweight. Breathable."** — that's the copy that speaks directly to why I buy Skechers over a generic sneaker.
- **Hands Free Slip-Ins get their own dedicated section** — showing multiple colorways side by side made it feel like a real product showcase, not just a throwaway mention.
- **Men's section with a male model** — I spotted the SKX block with a man in it; feels like at least part of this email remembered I exist.
- **Clean layout, large buttons** — reading this on my phone wasn't a chore. Buttons are tappable, text isn't microscopic.

## What's weak

- **"Thank You, Teachers!" section is dead weight for me** — that green block is jarring and irrelevant; it broke my momentum right before the footer.
- **No deadline on the BOGO** — I'd feel more compelled if it said "this weekend only" or "ends Sunday." Without that, I'll table it and forget.
- **No specific product recommendation** — I've bought from Skechers before; showing me "you bought the Go Walk 6, here's the Glide-Step Slip-In next" would've converted me faster than a wall of styles.
- **Preview text is broken garbage** — I saw schema JSON markup in my inbox preview instead of real copy. That made Skechers look sloppy before I even opened it.

- 2. **Add a deadline to the BOGO offer** — Even "Offer ends Sunday" is enough to get me off the fence. A hanging discount with no expiry is easy to ignore.
- 3. **Lead with Hands Free Slip-Ins in the hero, not generic Glide-Step** — The slip-in innovation is the strongest hook for comfort shoppers. Put that product first, then BOGO as the reason to act now.
- 4. **Drop or minimize the Teacher Appreciation block for non-teacher segments** — Segment it out, or at minimum shrink it so it doesn't hijack the lower half of the email.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — "Your Smoothest Stride Yet" (Skechers, 2026-05-14)

**1. Technical Summary**

One confirmed broken experience (empty personalization token in greeting) and a systemic HTTP image-serving issue affecting 10+ assets. No structural HTML errors detected.

---

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**

- 27 click-redirect links through `click.emails.skechers.com` — not probed, but all pass through a single redirect domain. A misconfiguration or downtime there would silently break every CTA simultaneously.
- Three separate tracking pixel domains in use (`click.emails.skechers.com`, `beacon.krxd.net`, `ink1000.com`) — increases surface area for deliverability flags and image blocking.
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif` loaded over implicit HTTP (no scheme specified) — browser behavior is undefined; should be explicitly `https://`.

---

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

**HTTP images (10 assets affected):** `image.emails.skechers.com` is serving all footer/body images over `http://`, not `https://`. Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook 365, Apple Mail) proxy or block non-HTTPS images. Affected assets include the logo, App Store badge, Google Play badge, social icons (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, and Find A Store badges.

Evidence: `src: http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...` (14 instances flagged in QA).

**Missing alt text:** Two images lack `alt` attributes — `o.gif` (tracking pixel, acceptable) and `49468f73...png` (appears to be a content image — not acceptable). Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no fallback.

**Duplicate media query blocks:** The CSS defines `@media only screen and (max-width: 375px)` and `@media only screen and (max-width: 640px)` with identical rule sets, then a third `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block in a separate `<style>` tag. This is redundant dead weight and could cause specificity conflicts in certain clients.

---

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

**[FAIL — Broken Experience]** The greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` — the subscriber first-name merge token resolved to empty string. This indicates either: (a) a missing fallback value in the merge tag (e.g., no `|default:"there"` or equivalent SFMC fallback), or (b) a segment pull that included records with null `First_Name`. Either way, this shipped to live list.

Recommended fix: `%%=v(@firstName)=%% ` → replace with `%%[IF EMPTY(@firstName) THEN SET @firstName = "Valued Customer" ENDIF]%%` or platform-equivalent null guard.

---

**5. Compliance**

- Preheader (`"Glide-Step Cushion That Keeps Up With You - Now BOGO 50% Off"`) is present and correctly hidden via `display:none !important`.
- Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated HTML — assumed present based on standard Skechers template; should be verified in full source.
- CAN-SPAM `List-Unsubscribe` header not visible in provided data — verify it is set at the ESP level.

---

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**

The campaign ID `MKG_US_NONPURCLICK_U_GLIDE_NONPURCH_EN_05142026` appears in three tracking pixel URLs, confirming campaign tagging exists at the pixel level. However, without seeing the actual click-through URLs (all 27 are behind the redirect domain), it is not possible to confirm UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to landing page URLs. This should be spot-checked against a decoded redirect on a sample CTA.
