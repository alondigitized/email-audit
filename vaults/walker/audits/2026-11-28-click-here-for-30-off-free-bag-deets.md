# Click *here* for 30% off (+ free bag deets)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same pink banner and giant "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th, and I've stopped counting how many that is. This one is the Saturday version, with a countdown clock and "79,662 people are already shopping" on top. The subject says "Click *here*," which sounds like a chain letter from my brother-in-law, not a shoe store.
- The offer is easy to find and easy to read, and the big numbers are good for my eyes. Past that it's a wall of the same thing I got yesterday. The shoes I actually care about sit way down the page: one boot, a sneaker and two loafers. There's nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-ons. The email has 30% off, a bag and a tote, but nothing about comfort.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I needed shoes this weekend. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF*" is readable on my phone without squinting.
- The code BFDEALS is spelled out right under the button, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The "Shop Wide Width" button is useful. Wide feet are a real thing at my age.
- The shop-by-size row lets me skip the browsing.
- The top bar says boots from $19.96, which is a concrete price and a decent hook.

## What's weak

- It's the same pink banner and 30% off I've gotten nearly every day since the 20th. The sameness numbs me to it.
- The "FREE SHERPA WEEKENDER" and the free tote both need a $99 or $75 purchase. The word "free" is doing a lot of work, and the fine print is a wall of tiny text.
- The top banner's "THE SEASON'S BIGGEST DEALS ARE ON!" scrolls and cuts off mid-word, and the DSW logo overlaps the "Designer Shoe Warehouse" text. It looks sloppy.
- Only four shoes are shown, and none are comfort or walking shoes. I see no Skechers, Hoka, Brooks or New Balance, and the men's casual shoe I'd want isn't here.
- The size buttons are small. The "Shop Women/Men/Kids" links are tiny text, easy to miss with a thumb.
- The "79,662 people are shopping" line and the countdown clock feel like pressure tactics.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a comfort and walking shoe row for men, with brand names I know (Skechers, Brooks, New Balance, Hoka), and stop making the bag the star.
- 2. Send one email a day, not several. Vary the creative when you do send.
- 3. Put the fine print behind a "Details" link in plain words, and put the real conditions ("$99 minimum") next to the word "free."
- 4. Make the category links real buttons. They're too small for a thumb.
- 5. Fix the logo and banner overlap.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off almost everything, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort shoes from the brands you wear, 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code BFDEALS. Boots from $19.96, free tote with $75 purchase.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide widths, walking shoes and slip-ons, all in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: a raw `%%gender%%` merge token and a tracked "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA that returned HTTP 400 when probed. There are also minor markup and accessibility gaps. The QA pass rate was 50%, with 12 of 78 links probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA (400).** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/yamlMcM36gYZ3Uwwj5cJsQ~~/AAQRxRA~/B1EBPEcaqq…` and it is roughly 1,000 characters long.
  - The QA log shows an empty value where the final or resolved URL would go.
  - A 400 on a click-tracking redirect may be bot or probe rejection rather than a dead link. Verify with a real-client click before treating it as a hard failure.
  - If it is a real failure, the SMS opt-in, which is the email's incentive CTA, is dead.
- **Long redirect URLs.** The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (28,871 of 35,899 chars), which comes from the long `ablink.dsw.com` tokens. This can hurt deliverability scoring, and some clients or filters truncate very long URLs.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 links were probed and 66 were skipped, so the other links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/mAiKGumHEWBKwutuB9Pefw~~/AAQRxRA~/d_TjvhUnGz2…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?...`, which is a tracking pixel.
  - Add `alt=""` to the pixel so screen readers skip it. Check whether the first image is content or a pixel. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Invalid meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. As written, neither tag does anything. The charset declaration is the more important one.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- Dark-mode support is declared (`color-scheme: light dark` plus `prefers-color-scheme` rules). The truncated source doesn't let me evaluate it further.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%gender%%` is present in the rendered output.** This is a merge field that wasn't resolved. The QA data doesn't show where it sits, so confirm whether it is in a URL, alt text or body copy.
  - If it is in a URL, the link may be broken or mis-segmented.
  - If it is visible copy, recipients will see the literal token.
  - The fix is a default or fallback value in the template, for example Liquid `| default:`, or suppressing the block when the attribute is null.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** The QA tool says this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. This is a tooling limitation, not a confirmed defect on DSW's side.
- The unsubscribe link is always probed and no failure was reported for it. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm the footer's physical postal address or the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. Verify those on the full message.
- The sender `dsw@e.dsw.com` and the tracking domains `ablink.dsw.com` and `mi.dsw.com` are aligned on `dsw.com`, which is positive for DMARC alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All sampled links go through `ablink.dsw.com` redirect tokens, so the destination and any UTM parameters can't be seen in the HTML. They can't be confirmed from the supplied data.
- The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261127_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` on the pixel.
- The failed opt-in link gives no landing-page data to check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts" link with a real browser or client. If it still fails, fix the Braze link or campaign setup, because it is the key CTA.
2. Fix the `%%gender%%` merge. Find where it renders and add a fallback or conditional.
3. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` tracking pixel, and add real alt text to the other flagged image if it carries content.
6. Check the full message's raw headers for SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and confirm the physical address in the footer.
