# (!) Last call for BOGO 50% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- DSW's been in my inbox every single day this week, and I've clicked most of them — that's just where we are. But this one? I opened it already knowing what was inside before the image even loaded. "Last call for BOGO 50% off" is the fourth or fifth variation of the same deal I've seen in the last week alone. The June 22nd "BOGO 50% off…starting NOW!" scored a 9 in my head because it felt like an event. This one feels like the cleanup announcement.
- The hero is big, black, and punchy — you can't miss that BOGO 50% OFF in white letters. That part still works. The SHOP BY SIZE section actually caught my attention in a useful way, because the Wide Width button down there? That's real information for someone my age with feet that've logged a few decades. But then the "SO NEW, SO YOU" grid rolls in and I'm looking at strappy heels and platform sandals and nothing I'd put on my own feet. No men's walking shoes, no cushioned slip-ons, no comfort angle whatsoever.
- Mark as read. I might swing back if I've got some time to dig into the men's section myself, but DSW isn't making it easy for me here. I wouldn't forward this — nothing in it that a friend my age and situation would find useful.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF hero is unmissable** — bold white text on black, huge type, my eyes went straight there even on a small phone screen.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is clear and visible** — no guessing what the button does, it's right under the offer.
- **Shop by Size section is genuinely useful** — seeing "Wide Width" and "Wide Calf" buttons tells me DSW knows that not everyone's foot is a size 9 medium.
- **DSW VIP block is present** — I'm a loyalty-program guy, so seeing the rewards callout at a glance reminds me I've got skin in the game here.
- **Email renders cleanly** — no broken images, no scrambled layout, text didn't overlap anything.

## What's weak

- **"SO NEW, SO YOU" is not for me** — heels, strappy sandals, feminine silhouettes across the whole product grid; I scrolled it looking for a men's walking shoe and came up empty.
- **No comfort or fit language in the hero** — cushioning, arch support, slip-on, breathable — none of my words showed up anywhere above the fold.
- **"Last call" fatigue is real** — this is the same BOGO deal I've seen dressed up four or five different ways since June 22nd; the urgency has started to sound like the boy who cried wolf.
- **Preview text doubles down on the subject instead of adding anything** — "Hurry before it's too late" tells me nothing the subject didn't already say, and it leaks a wall of invisible filler characters behind it that's just wasted space.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the "SO NEW, SO YOU" grid to include at least two men's comfort or walking shoes.** If DSW knows I'm a men's shopper (they should), serving me four women's heel styles is a miss. One slip-on or cushioned walker in that grid changes everything.
- 2. **Add a men's-specific secondary CTA below the hero.** Something like "Shop Men's Styles" alongside the generic SHOP NOW. I don't want to hunt.
- 3. **Give the hero one line of comfort language** — "cushioned soles, BOGO pricing" or "walkers, sneakers, slip-ons — BOGO 50% off" is more actionable than the deal alone.
- 4. **Retire the identical BOGO deal or reframe it.** At this cadence, "last call" needs a hard end date and time to feel real — "Ends tonight at midnight" beats "before it's too late" every time.
- 5. **Fix the preview text to add information, not echo urgency.**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: BOGO 50% off men's comfort shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `BOGO ends tonight — men's walkers & slip-ons`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your size is still in stock — grab a second pair for half off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide widths included. Sale ends at midnight.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |

| `text-size-adjust: none` applied globally | `*{-ms-text-size-adjust: 100%; -webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%; text-size-adjust: none}` — the prefixed properties set `100%` (correct) but the unprefixed standard property is set to `none`, which disables iOS auto-scaling and can harm readability for small text on mobile |
| Futura PT fonts: WOFF only | No `.woff2` variant and no `.ttf` fallback. Clients that support web fonts but not WOFF will silently fall back to system fonts |
| HelveticaNow fonts: TTF only | No `.woff` or `.woff2` variant. Inconsistent format strategy versus Futura PT — one family prioritizes older format, the other newer |
| `Src:` capitalized in all 10 `@font-face` blocks | e.g. `Src: url("https://braze-images.com/.../original.woff")` — CSS property names are case-insensitive so this parses correctly in browsers, but strict XML/XHTML parsers and some lint tools will flag it; worth normalizing to lowercase `src:` |
