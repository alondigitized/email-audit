## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders structurally (table layout, `lang="en"`, preheader, responsive media query), but it has two confirmed QA failures: the unsubscribe probe returned 400, and there is no plain-text part. It also loads 11 images over `http://` and has a malformed charset declaration.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…`. The `attn_macro` value is the base64 of `unsubscribeLink`. The 400 may come from the probe (a tokenized, per-recipient URL fetched without the expected context) rather than a dead link. It is unconfirmed either way, and since this is the compliance-critical link it needs a manual click test.
- **Click-tracking URLs are opaque.** Every material link goes through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`.
- **Two probes returned 429.** These were the "Ready to check out? Complete your order" CTA and the linked image `de642685-…jpg`. Attentive's rate limiting is the likely cause, but the destinations are unverified.
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 31 skipped, so footer, social and utility links are largely unchecked.
- **The sending, link and unsubscribe domains differ.** From is `msgs.skechers.com`, links use `attentivemail.com`, the unsubscribe uses `attn.tv`, and images use `emails.skechers.com`. This is typical for an ESP, but it is worth confirming that SPF/DKIM/DMARC align on the sending domain and that link-tracking domains are branded consistently.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed charset meta.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and no `charset` attribute. As written, the declaration is ineffective.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **11 images use `http://`** (`image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`). They are the logo, the `a6ec5198-…jpg` image, "Text", App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. Clients may block or rewrite them (mixed content). The same host serves `de642685-…jpg` over `https://`, so HTTPS is available.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `de642685-77b3-46fa-950b-5bd9ee6800e1.jpg` is wrapped in a link, so its link text is empty to screen readers.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` has no alt text.
  - The `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` open pixel has no alt text. This is low impact.
- **Preheader is present and hidden correctly.** No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The truncation hides the cart/product block, so personalization there is not verifiable.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and it leaves no text-only way to unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, and the QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay. It is a gap in the audit, not a confirmed sender failure.
- **Unsubscribe link.** A link with text "Unsubscribe" is present, but the probe returned 400 (see section 2).
- **Not verifiable from the data provided.** The `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address (in the truncated footer) could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Destination URLs and any UTM parameters are hidden inside Attentive redirect tokens. The primary CTA ("Complete your order") returned 429, so its landing page was never resolved. Whether it deep-links to the cart or the homepage is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click-test the unsubscribe URL from a real inbox. If it truly returns 400, fix it first, because it is a CAN-SPAM exposure.
2. Add a plain-text MIME alternative that includes the unsubscribe URL.
3. Switch all 11 `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` image sources to `https://`.
4. Fix the charset declaration to `<meta charset="utf-8">` or `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, and fill in `<title>`.
5. Add descriptive alt text to `de642685-…jpg` (the linked image) and `a6ec5198-…jpg`. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel.
6. Re-probe the 429'd CTA with backoff or a different client, to confirm the cart destination and capture UTMs.
7. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC results (the relay stripped `Authentication-Results`).
