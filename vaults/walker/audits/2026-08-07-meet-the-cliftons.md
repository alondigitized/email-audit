# Meet the Cliftons
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Here's HOKA again, and this time they're doing the two-for-one — Clifton PRO and Clifton 11 stacked in one email. My eye landed on the Clifton 11 section because "Refined for daily run and walk" and "Cushioned, effortless smooth feel" is exactly what gets me to slow down and read. The Clifton PRO half, with its "PROGLIDE+ tech" and "next-level training" talk, might as well be written in a different language for a guy who walks the dog and does errands.
- I already met the Clifton 11 on July 16 and got asked to "get to know" it again on July 22. Now it's back a third time, just wearing a new outfit with its performance sibling. That's a lot of Clifton for one summer.
- No price anywhere in this email — not a sale, not a member discount, nothing. So even with the walking-shoe copy catching my eye, there's no real reason to click today versus next week. I'd skim it, maybe glance at the Clifton 11 photos, and archive it without clicking. Not forwarding this one — nothing here is worth telling a buddy about.

## What's working

- "Refined for daily run and walk" and "Cushioned, effortless smooth feel" under Clifton 11 is the one line in the whole email actually written for someone like me.
- "CMEVA foam for soft landings" — that's a real cushioning detail, the kind of thing I actually weigh when picking a shoe.
- Clean layout, one shoe per section, easy to follow on my phone without a lot of zooming.
- Both men's and women's colorways shown side by side — no confusion about what I'm looking at.

## What's weak

- Half this email — the whole Clifton PRO section — is a performance running pitch I have zero use for. "Fast, responsive," "next-level training" — I'm not training for anything.
- The button under the Clifton 11 section still says "Shop Clifton PRO" — that's a mix-up, and if I tapped it expecting Clifton 11 I'd land somewhere I didn't mean to.
- Not one price, discount, or member perk anywhere. No reason to act today instead of whenever.
- I've now had Clifton 11 in my inbox three times in three weeks (July 16, July 22, and now) — feels like the same shoe keeps getting recycled with new copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix that "Shop Clifton PRO" button sitting under the Clifton 11 section — that's a straightforward copy error costing clicks.
- 2. Split these into two separate emails instead of bundling — send the Clifton 11 (walk/comfort) pitch to people like me, and save the Clifton PRO performance pitch for the training crowd. Bundling them just buries the half I care about.
- 3. Put an actual number in there — price, percent off, or at least "now available" with a date — something concrete to act on.
- 4. Lead the subject and hero with the walking/comfort angle since that's the half of this email doing any work for a customer like me.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Two new Cliftons — one built for miles on your feet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soft landings, effortless feel — meet the new Clifton 11`
