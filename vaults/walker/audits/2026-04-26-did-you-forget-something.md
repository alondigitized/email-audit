### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html

```
Some email clients (Outlook desktop, Samsung Mail) display the `<title>` value as the preview or tab label. An empty title is a minor rendering gap.

**Preheader soft-hyphen padding**
The preheader uses both Unicode filler characters (`͏`, U+034F) and `­` (soft hyphen, `&shy;`):
```html

```
Soft hyphens render as visible `-` characters in certain older Outlook and Lotus Notes versions. The U+034F characters are safer for this technique.
