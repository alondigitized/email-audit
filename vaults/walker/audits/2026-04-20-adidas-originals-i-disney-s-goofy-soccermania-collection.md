- **Wrong brand entirely.** I'm a Skechers guy. I don't know why adidas has my email. I'm not their customer in any meaningful way.
- **This is for kids.** The copy literally says "Designed for young fans" and "Perfect for kids." I'm 62. My kid days are a few decades behind me, and so are my kids' kid days.
- **Disney's Goofy.** I'm not sure what to even say about this. Goofy is a cartoon dog from the 1940s. I get the nostalgia angle, but this doesn't feel like nostalgic Boomer content — it feels like a grandkid's birthday present category.
- **No price, no deal, no reason to act.** There's a "SHOP NOW" button up top but no number attached to it. What does it cost? Is there a sale? Nothing to grab me.
- **No men's comfort angle.** No slip-ins, no walking shoes, no "guys like you will love this." Absolutely nothing aimed at a 62-year-old man who cares about his feet.
- **Preheader text is garbled.** Whatever was supposed to show at the top of the email before the navigation bar looks like broken or placeholder copy. That's a bad first impression even before I get into the meat of it.
