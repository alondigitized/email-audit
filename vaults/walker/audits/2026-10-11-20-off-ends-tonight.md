# 20% off ends tonight!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the eighth or so send on the same HAYRIDE 20% code since "20% OFF?! You better be-leaf it" on the 8th. Yesterday's "20% off ends tomorrow 🚨" already told me this was ending. Now it's "ends tonight," so the deadline has been running for two days. The giant white "20" on the green banner is easy to read on my phone. I'll give them that.
- Then I scroll and find six women's shoes: a shearling bootie, a Mary Jane, a heeled ankle boot, a pump. There's nothing for a 62-year-old man with sore arches. "Shop Men" is one small underlined link in a row of four. That's a miss, because I'd wanted a slip-on walking shoe.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. The shoe aisle has been trained to ignore this sender, and I'd mark it read. If I really needed shoes tonight I'd think about it, but a "tonight" deadline I've seen for two days doesn't move me.

## What's working

- The huge "20% OFF" banner is readable without my glasses.
- The code HAYRIDE is printed right under the banner, and there's a barcode at the bottom for the store.
- The "Claim 20% Off" button is a clear black block on green.
- The in-store services row (pickup, shoe repair, price matching) is something I'd use.
- The "Shop by Size" block with wide width is useful for a guy with wide feet.

## What's weak

- All six products are women's fashion shoes (booties, a Mary Jane, a pump). Nothing for a man, and nothing about comfort, cushion or arch support.
- "Shop Men" is a small underlined link in a row of four. I'd miss it.
- The deadline feels fake. It's the same code since the 8th, and "ends tonight" follows "ends tomorrow."
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the brand exclusions are buried. Skechers is on the qualifying list, but I only know that from reading it.
- The black "adidas up to 30% off" bar at the top competes with the 20% offer, and the size grid takes up a lot of space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and category. A man who has clicked walking or slip-on shoes should see men's comfort brands (Skechers, Brooks, New Balance, Hoka) in the product grid, not booties.
- 2. Retire the fake urgency. If the offer ends tonight, say it once, with a time like "ends 11:59 PM ET."
- 3. Put "Shop Men" in a real button the same size as the others, and add a "Comfort & Walking" button.
- 4. Put one line of the brand exclusions next to the offer instead of in the fine print.
- 5. Add a "Skechers, Hoka & more qualify" line to the banner. That's a price-vs-quality comparison I'd actually read.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is mostly well-formed, with a Braze/SendGrid-style tracked-link setup and dark-mode support. It has one failing CTA link (HTTP 400, the SMS opt-in), a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and a few markup omissions. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer, and headers could not be fully audited.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **[FAIL] Broken link (HTTP 400)** on the CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off":
  `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/sv_A6xllk_ispDk3F8LXBA~~/AAQRxRA~/rJL9IWLG…` (~900-char tracking token).
  - This is a prominent conversion CTA, so recipients who click may land on an error page.
  - A 400 on a redirector can also come from the probe itself, for example a missing user-agent or an SMS deep-link target. Re-test with a mobile user-agent and in a real client before treating it as confirmed.
  - The failure appears in the QA data with an empty status text, so the destination was never resolved.
- **Very long tracked URLs.** The plain-text part is 26,618 of 31,108 characters URLs (86%, above the 70% threshold), which can raise spam-filter scores.
  - All links are wrapped in the `ablink.dsw.com` redirector, so the final destinations and UTM parameters can't be seen in the source.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 69 links were probed. The other 57 are unverified.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing `alt` on two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/hJq3k7xPpUbepl6FgwcKLw~~/AAQRxRA~/sVeNs2hJ5-…` is a tracked image with no alt text, so it is invisible to screen readers and blocked-image views. It needs descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if it is decorative.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=…` is an open-tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither takes effect. Charset then relies on the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `DSW`. This is minor.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags and `prefers-color-scheme` classes are present, with no issues seen.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The tracking pixel carries per-recipient IDs (`mi_u=225090073`), which is expected. The truncated body wasn't checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unverified.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, and no raw headers were supplied. SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and the physical postal address couldn't be checked.
- The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no unsubscribe failure.
- The sender `dsw@e.dsw.com` is on a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.
- The broken SMS opt-in link is a TCPA/consent-flow concern. A failed opt-in means no consent is captured, but it creates no compliance exposure by itself.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not verifiable. Redirect wrapping hides destination URLs and UTMs. The campaign ID in the pixel (`mi_ecmp=20261011_P261379_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…`) is truncated. Landing-page alignment with the "20% off ends tonight!" subject was not checked, since the redirects weren't resolved.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **High:** Fix or re-verify the "Opt-In to Texts" link (HTTP 400). Check the Braze link template and the SMS keyword or landing URL, and test on iOS and Android.
2. **Medium:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Medium:** Add alt text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image, and `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions to `o.gif`.
4. **Low:** Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and make the `<title>` descriptive.
5. **Low:** Shorten the plain-text version, for example with a link-light plain-text template or short links, to bring the URL ratio down.
6. **Follow-up:** Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers to cover the footer and unsubscribe, authentication results, and UTM and landing-page checks.
