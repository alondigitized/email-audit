# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll tell you — I opened up Skechers expecting to find something in my size and instead I walked into what feels like a florist's shop. The whole page is pink flowers, a mom and her little girl, and something called "The Mother's Day Shop." Nothing wrong with that if I'm buying a gift, but I came here for a new pair of Slip-ins for myself. The BOGO deal at the top is nice, though — that part caught my eye.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — blue bar at the very top, impossible to miss, and it applies to everything including my shoes. That's real money and I noticed it right away.
- **Clean page layout** — everything is where I expect it: logo top-left, search and account top-right, hamburger menu. No clutter, loads fast, no janky shifting. My phone handled it fine.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — simple, large, high contrast on white. I know what to tap. No guessing.
- **Seasonal relevance** — I may need to pick something up for my wife, so the Mother's Day angle isn't completely lost on me even if I came for myself.

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% not for me** — "The Mother's Day Shop" with a mom and daughter in pink sneakers surrounded by flowers. I'm a 62-year-old man shopping for Men's Slip-ins. I felt like I'd walked into the wrong store.
- **No Men's category shortcut visible** — The hamburger menu hides everything. I have to tap an extra step just to find my section. On mobile at my age, that friction adds up.
- **No Skechers Plus loyalty callout** — I'm a member. Show me my points, a member discount, something. Zero sign of it above the fold.
- **Apparel promo feels off-target** — "25% OFF Apparel" is the secondary banner. I don't come to Skechers for shirts. That's real estate I'd rather see used for a comfort-tech shoe deal.
- **No trust signals** — No star ratings, no "free returns," no "X people bought this" — nothing that tells me I'm in good hands if I pull the trigger.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the sub-banner copy** for something I'd act on: "Men's Slip-ins — BOGO 50% OFF. Shop Men's." One tap, I'm in my aisle.
- **Add a returning-member strip** below the header: "Hi Walker — you have X points. Redeem today." Skechers Plus loyalty is a real differentiator; put it to work.
- **Surface a Men's category tile below the hero** — even a single "Men's Comfort" or "Slip-ins" thumbnail before the scroll would stop guys like me from bouncing.
- **Pin a trust micro-copy line** near the CTA: "Free returns · Skechers Plus rewards apply." Two seconds of reading, a lot more confidence tapping.
