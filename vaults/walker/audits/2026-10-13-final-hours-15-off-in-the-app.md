# FINAL HOURS: $15 OFF IN THE APP!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same CIDER $15/$20/$60 deal I've now had since the 8th, with "Extended" and "Get $15 OFF in the app!" from this morning already in my inbox. A black countdown bar reading "ENDS IN 06:33:50" sits over a big green block, and it's easy to read. But I've been told "last chance" about a dozen times this week, so I don't believe the clock anymore.
- The deal is app-only, and I shop on my phone browser. The email wants me to download an app to save $15. That's a hassle for a 62-year-old who just wants a good walking shoe. Then the products below are a leopard-print ankle boot, a platform clog, a Mary Jane flat and a fuzzy lace-up bootie. Not one is for a man, and not one is a cushioned slip-on. Nothing here speaks to me.
- I wouldn't click. I'd probably mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it either.

## What's working

- The offer is big and clear: $15 off $49, $20 off $99, $60 off $199. The numbers are large enough to read without zooming.
- The countdown banner at the top catches the eye first, and the green block is easy to scan.
- The code CIDER is stated plainly, and the fine print at the bottom is thorough.
- The "Shop Men" link is there, along with a size grid (5 to 11) and "Shop Wide Width," which is the one thing here I'd actually use.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is women's: boots, a clog and a flat. Nothing for me, and nothing hands-free or cushioned.
- The deal only works in the app, and the only real button is "Download the App." That's a hurdle for someone who browses in a phone browser.
- This is the same deal I've seen all week, so the "FINAL HOURS" urgency feels made up. I've watched "ends tonight" turn into "Extended" before.
- The "Shop Men," "Shop Women" and "Shop Kids" links are small, thin underlined text, which is hard to tap with my thumb.
- The size grid is just bare numbers with no men's or women's labels. It's unclear what I'm supposed to do with it, and the sizes stop at 11, so no 12 or 13.
- The "Up to 30% off adidas" strip at the very top is a second offer competing with the main one. It's easy to miss and confusing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me something that looks like me. Put men's comfort and walking shoes in the grid, such as slip-ons and cushioned sneakers from Skechers, Hoka and Brooks, which DSW already carries.
- 2. Let me use the deal in a browser, or at least give me a plain "Shop Men's Comfort" button next to the app download.
- 3. Stop calling every send the final one. Pick one real deadline and send fewer emails.
- 4. Make the "Shop Men" link a full-width button instead of tiny underlined text.
- 5. Label the size grid "Men's" and "Women's," and extend it past 11.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email's source and tracking are mostly sound, but the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returns a 400 and a few images lack alt text. The HTML source was truncated, so I could not check the footer, unsubscribe markup, or most body links. Findings below rest only on the QA data and the visible head of the source.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (confirmed by QA):** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns HTTP 400. The URL is an ablink.dsw.com wrapper: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/2xMr6QtF9kiEKZLsD603Yg~~/AAQRxRA~/el4CPi8k...`. The QA output shows an empty href label before the URL. The wrapped token may be malformed, expired, or rejected by the tracking redirector.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 74 links were probed. The other 62 are unverified, so more broken links are possible.
- **Click-tracking wrapper:** The ablink.dsw.com domain is a branded click-tracking domain, which is good practice. Ablink is the Braze-style link-wrapping subdomain. I can't confirm from the QA data whether it uses a valid certificate or redirects correctly.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/gFwS0EkECdfzD35UTQOU8w~~/AAQRxRA~/TiFDqX5jsdUWeMqTSitNor84nZTXaFWwG31BG2jqq4AkR1sTCC...` is an image served through the tracking wrapper. It is likely a tracking pixel or an unlabeled image. If it is the pixel, it needs `alt=""` and a 1x1 size. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261013_P261396_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` is a tracking pixel (o.gif). It needs `alt=""` for screen readers.
- **Doctype:** XHTML 1.0 Transitional is the standard for email.
- **Meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so the charset declaration and IE compatibility hint do nothing. This is low risk, since the Content-Type header usually sets the charset.
  - The viewport tag is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags are present, plus a `prefers-color-scheme` block. Note that `.dm-bg-white` forces `#ffffff` in dark mode, which is likely intentional.
- **Fonts:** Web fonts load from braze-images.com in `@font-face` with `.woff` and `.ttf` sources. I saw no fallback stack in the visible source. Check that the inline `font-family` values have fallbacks.
- **Syntax in the head:** `Src:` is capitalized in the `@font-face` rules, and `Margin: 0` is also capitalized. CSS properties are case-insensitive, so this is not a bug.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. I saw no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `mi_u=225090073` in the pixel URL is a rendered user ID, not a leak. Only the truncated source was available, so I could not check the body.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe:** The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it. I could not see the footer markup, so I can't confirm the link or the physical postal address.
- **Headers:** No raw headers were supplied, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` can't be assessed.
- **Sender:** The From address is `dsw@e.dsw.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, which is aligned and good practice.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Campaign tracking:** The `mi_ecmp=20261013_P261396_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` parameter encodes the send date (2026-10-13), which matches today. That makes the "FINAL HOURS" subject plausible for the date.
- **UTM parameters:** The links are wrapped, and the destinations are hidden behind the redirects, so I can't check UTM parameters or landing-page alignment.
- **Broken CTA:** The failing 400 link is the SMS opt-in offer. Its text promises 15% off, so a broken link here breaks that offer's path.
- **App-install CTA:** The subject promises "$15 OFF IN THE APP!", but I could not see or test the app-download or deep-link destination in the truncated source or probed set.

7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or regenerate the SMS opt-in link.** Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" URL, since it returns 400. Check that the Braze/ablink token is intact and not truncated or double-encoded, and that the destination still exists. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. **Add `alt=""` to both pixels** (`o.gif` on mi.dsw.com and the ablink `/q/` image), or descriptive alt text if the ablink image is visible content.
3. **Raise probe coverage.** Only 12 of 74 links were checked. Probe the app-download CTA specifically, since the subject depends on it.
4. **Drop `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag so users can pinch-zoom.
5. **Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`** to the two meta tags that lack it. This is low priority.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full source and raw headers** to verify the unsubscribe link, the postal address, `List-Unsubscribe`, authentication results, and UTM alignment.
7. **Plain-text part:** The QA warning says 26,290 of 30,946 characters (85%) are URLs, which can raise spam-filter scores. Shorten or reduce the tracking URLs in the text version, or add more readable copy.
