# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- There's that pink floral D'Lites again — that's four times this week now that Skechers has parked the same sneaker in my inbox telling me my "cart is still waiting." It wasn't waiting on Monday, it isn't waiting today, and I'm starting to think their system just picked a shoe at random and decided I wanted it. I never touched it, and it's a women's floral sneaker, so somebody's data is way off.
- The one thing that saved this from being a total waste of a click was scrolling down — there's a real "Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit: Altman - Murdock" sneaker in the "more options" grid, and that 20% off for Plus members banner is legit useful since I am a member. But I have to wade through cart-reminder nonsense and a wall of boy's and women's shoes to get there.
- I'm not clicking the "Complete Your Order" button — that's not my order. I might scroll straight past the top and go looking for the Men's category link near the bottom, but honestly at this point I'd probably just delete it. Not forwarding this to anybody.

## What's working

- The 20% off member discount banner is clear and I know I'm getting it since I'm logged into Plus.
- One genuine men's shoe — the Altman Murdock slip-in — shows up in the recommendation grid, plain grey, looks comfortable.
- "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" and the "MEN" category link near the bottom give me a real path to what I actually want.
- Big, readable text and buttons — no squinting needed on my phone.

## What's weak

- That pink floral D'Lites cart reminder is not my cart — this is the fourth near-identical send this week claiming I abandoned a shoe I never looked at.
- The whole top half of the email is dead weight to me: hero shoe, "complete your order" button, and headline copy are all about an item I have zero interest in.
- Two "Boy's Garage Sneaker" entries in the recommendations — I don't have kids that age, that's not relevant to me at all.
- No arch support, cushioning, or comfort-tech language anywhere — for a guy who buys on foot feel, that's a miss.
- Fine print buried at the bottom says the 20% off doesn't even apply to Skechers Slip-ins or certain styles, so the deal isn't as clean as the banner makes it look.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake cart-reminder gimmick entirely for me — I've gotten this same trick four times this week and it's actively eroding trust in the brand. Lead with something I've actually shown interest in, like Arch Fit or slip-ins for men.
- 2. Swap the hero image and headline for the Men's Slip-ins Altman Murdock or an Arch Fit style — that's the one item in this whole email that fits me.
- 3. Cut the Boy's Garage Sneakers from my recommendation grid — irrelevant, wastes space I could use for another men's option.
- 4. Move the 20% Plus member discount banner up near the top, above the fold — that's the one offer here I'd actually act on.
- 5. Add a line of comfort-tech copy — "memory foam cushioning," "arch support built in" — since that's what actually gets me to click, not a phantom cart.
