# ADIZERO Evo SL is the perfect gift for every runner
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- It's another adidas email, and I've lost count since the 15th. This one finally has a shoe in it, a pair of blue Evo SLs on gray tile, and I'll give them that. But the black "GIFT CARDS" banner sits on top of the shoe and fights it for my attention. I couldn't tell whether I was being sold a shoe or a gift card.
- I'm 62 and I walk for comfort. "Gift the feeling of fast" isn't me. The body copy mentions Lightstrike Pro cushioning, which is the closest thing to comfort in the whole email, but it's buried in a small paragraph. There's no price, no percent off and no deadline.
- I wouldn't click. I might look at the shoe for a second. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The shoe photo is big and clear. I can see what's being sold, which wasn't true of the striped-fabric email on 10/23.
- The "Shop Men" and "Shop Women" buttons are large black blocks, easy to tap with my thumb.
- The paragraph text is a decent size and I could read it without zooming.
- The Evo SL name is in big type, so I know exactly which shoe it is.
- Mentioning cushioning and traction is at least a nod to what I care about.

## What's weak

- The gift card banner and the shoe headline are two different pitches stacked together. I couldn't tell which one the email was selling.
- There's no price, no offer and no deadline. The 10/23 email at least had 30% off.
- "Fast" is the whole pitch. It's a racing-style shoe, and I walk for comfort.
- The tiny underlined line at the top, "Evo SL: Gift the feeling of fast," looks like leftover text.
- The footer links are small and close together, which is hard on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one message. Either lead with the shoe and a price, or run a gift card email. Don't do both.
- 2. Put a real number in the hero, such as the price or a deal, so I have something to weigh.
- 3. Say what the cushioning does for everyday walking and standing, not just for racing.
- 4. Make the buttons say what they lead to, for example "Shop Evo SL men's" instead of just "Shop Men."
- 5. Give the shoe a deadline for holiday shipping.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Evo SL: cushioned for every step, now $X`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday gift: lightweight cushioning he'll wear daily`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lightstrike Pro cushioning and Continental traction. Order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Light, cushioned and grippy. See men's sizes and free returns.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email fails the plain-text fallback check, with a 0-character text part. It also loads four instances of an arrow image and a click-domain image over plain `http://`. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-destination checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 18 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Final destinations, redirect chain health and UTM preservation are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking image:** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1951c0967-e7faded9-b8de-4f60-be6f-31999c584faf-000000/BiVhNFMVMjxf4u7MqRQlqVBoh…` loads over HTTP. It looks like an open-tracking pixel. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may fail to record opens.
- **Mixed content:** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` (alt "arrow") appears 4 times. Clients may block it or show a mixed-content warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/8c2b0241-9577-4a6b-8b52-52894ebf977b?cp_tp=…` has no `alt` attribute. If it is content, screen readers will read the filename or URL. If it is decorative, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixel alt:** the pixel (`…BiVhNFMVMjxf4u7MqRQlqVBoh9LMQOP1VHkJNxlHDkc=452`) has no `alt`. It should be `alt=""` so assistive tech skips it.
- **Charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it is not a valid charset declaration. Clients that don't honor the MIME header charset could mis-render non-ASCII characters.
- **No plain-text part:** the text version is 0 characters (QA FAIL). This hurts text-only clients, some accessibility tooling and spam scoring.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with `lang="en"` set. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens or fallback placeholders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, not failed. The sender domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` could not be checked from the data provided.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the truncated source does not show an unsubscribe link, a physical address or `List-Unsubscribe` headers. I can't confirm or refute them.
- **Plain-text part:** its absence is a deliverability weakness, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 18 tracking links were skipped, so UTM parameters, redirect targets and landing-page alignment with the "ADIZERO Evo SL" subject are unverified. No evidence of a problem was found.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text alternative:** a `text/plain` part with the product, CTA URLs and unsubscribe link (fixes the one QA FAIL).
2. **Serve all images over HTTPS:** switch the `image.link.adidas.com` arrow and the `click.comms.adidas.com` pixel to `https://`.
3. **Fix alt attributes:** add `alt=""` to the pixel and a descriptive `alt` to `dv.adidas.com/o/8c2b0241-…` (or `alt=""` if decorative).
4. **Correct the charset meta:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`.
5. **Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers:** this would let us check the footer, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, and the final URLs and UTMs behind the 18 redirects.
