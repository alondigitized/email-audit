# LAST DAY 😱 BOGO 50% OFF 😩
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Same BOGO 50% off deal I've now seen every day this week — Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, and now the "LAST DAY" version. At least this one's honest about it being a deadline instead of dressing it up as a trend report or a "secret." The all-black clogs, booties, and boots in the "So New, So You" row are actually shoes I'd look twice at — chunky heel boot in the bottom middle isn't bad — but there's not one cushioned walking shoe or slip-on sneaker anywhere in this email, so it's not really talking to me.
- The layout's clean enough and I can read the big BOGO 50% text without squinting, that's a plus. But between the "keep a secret" VIP app teaser, the shop-by-size grid, and a wall of fine print at the bottom, this is a lot of scrolling for a deal I've already seen five times. I'd mark it read and move on — not forwarding this to anybody.

## What's working

- The big black BOGO 50% OFF text is dead simple to read on a phone, no squinting required.
- "LAST DAY" in the subject at least gives me a real deadline instead of another costume for the same sale.
- The boot and bootie photos in "So New, So You" are decent quality and not blurry or broken.
- Shop-by-size grid is a nice touch if I already knew what I wanted — quick tap targets.

## What's weak

- Not a single comfort shoe, walking sneaker, or slip-on in sight — it's all dress boots and booties, none of which fit how I actually shop.
- No men's section featured at all in the product grid, just women's boots and clogs, even though "SHOP MEN" is up top.
- This is the same BOGO promo I've seen at least six times this week, just with a "LAST DAY" label slapped on — feels like crying wolf on urgency.
- The "Can You Keep a Secret??" VIP app banner is just noise in the middle of a sale email, doesn't add anything for me.
- Way too much scroll before I hit anything useful — by the time I get to the fine print I've forgotten what the offer even was.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put men's comfort shoes in the hero row, not a random assortment of women's boots — if this is going to my inbox, show me something I'd actually buy.
- 2. Cut the VIP app teaser entirely from a last-day urgency email — it dilutes the one thing you're trying to get me to do (shop before it ends).
- 3. Tone down the deadline fatigue — if this is genuinely the last day, say what time it ends, not just "last day."
- 4. Lead with a specific product like a Skechers or Hoka-style walking shoe with the BOGO badge on it, not generic "So New, So You" boots.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight — BOGO 50% off, no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab a second pair before midnight — comfort styles included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide widths, wide calf, and BOGO 50% — today only.`
