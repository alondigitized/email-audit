# Get BOGO 50% off popular styles 🤩
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off again — that's now most of last week and this week too, I honestly can't keep track anymore. The one thing that made me actually stop and look this time was the little green "Brooks Starting At $75" banner most of the way down, next to a hiking shoe that looks like it's got some real cushion to it. That's the first thing in any of these DSW emails that's spoken to me directly — everything above it is heels, sandals, mules, and strappy stuff that's clearly aimed at my wife, not me.
- Sneakers do show up — there's some New Balance and what looks like Adidas mixed into "Shop Sneakers" and "Shop Active Sneakers" — but they're buried four or five scrolls down, sandwiched between rows of women's dress shoes. I'd scroll past the top two-thirds of this without slowing down.
- I wouldn't click from the top of this email. If I click at all, it's on that Brooks banner near the bottom, and only because I already know Brooks makes a good arch-support shoe. Not forwarding this to anybody — it's not offensive, it's just not built for a guy like me.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off math is stated plainly up top — buy one, get a second qualifying pair half off, no gimmicks.
- "Prices reflected at checkout, details below" is honest — no hunting for a code.
- The Brooks $75 banner is the one spot that actually named a brand and price I care about.
- New Balance and Adidas sneakers do appear in "Shop Sneakers" and "Shop Active Sneakers," so there's something for me if I keep scrolling.
- "Shop By Size" and "Shop Wide Width" near the bottom is a nice touch — wide width matters to me.

## What's weak

- The whole top half — "Summer's Best," sandals, dress shoes, heels — is women's product, and there's a lot of it before anything for me shows up.
- No men's shoes at all until deep in the email; I have to scroll past six or seven rows of women's heels and sandals to find sneakers.
- Nothing here talks about comfort, cushioning, or arch support — just "cart-topping styles" and category buttons.
- The DSW VIP perks box ("3% back, birthday gift") is generic loyalty filler, not tied to anything I'd actually buy.
- Same BOGO offer I've now seen daily for over a week — the deal's gone stale even if the deal itself is fine.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put men's comfort sneakers (New Balance, Adidas, Brooks) in the first screen, not buried past the fold — I shouldn't have to scroll through women's sandals to find my category.
- 2. Move that Brooks $75 banner to the top of the email — it's the only line that actually named a brand and price I trust.
- 3. Add a line of copy that mentions comfort or arch support next to the sneaker rows, not just "Shop Sneakers."
- 4. Vary the offer language or add something new — I've read "BOGO 50% off" from DSW almost every day this week and it's starting to blur together.
- 5. Consider a men's-only or "for him" module the way the women's styles get "Summer's Best" — right now it reads like an afterthought.
