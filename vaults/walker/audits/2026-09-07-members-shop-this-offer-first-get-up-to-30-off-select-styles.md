# Members shop this offer first, get up to 30% off select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Now this one I recognize — same shoe box pile as August 19th, but at least this time there's a code I can actually read (LABORDAY), a real deadline ("offer ends today" up top), and two buttons that make sense: "Shop now" and "Visit a store." That's how I actually shop — I like knowing there's a Foot Locker or a mall store I can walk into and try these on before I buy.
- The price-bracket buttons — Under $50, $50-$100, Over $100 — that's smart, that's exactly the kind of value math I do in my head anyway. But then it drops straight into "More to explore" and starts throwing kids' socks, a lime-green racing flat, and a women's Ellipse shoe at me. Only the MX608v5 and maybe the 577v1 look like something I'd actually put on my feet.
- I'd click "Shop now" out of habit — the deadline's real and the discount's real — but I wouldn't forward this to anybody. It's a generic blast, not something that says New Balance knows I'm a comfort-and-arch-support guy.

## What's working

- "Offer ends today" banner at the very top — clear, credible urgency, no guessing.
- LABORDAY code and 30% off spelled out plainly, not buried in fine print.
- "Visit a store" button — I like having the option to try shoes on in person.
- Price-tier shopping buttons (Under $50 / $50-$100 / Over $100) match how I actually decide.

## What's weak

- Same shoe-box photo I've now seen on August 19th and again here — recycled art, feels lazy.
- "More to explore" tiles are a grab bag — kids' socks, a neon racing flat, a women's shoe — none of it says comfort or arch support to me.
- Nothing about cushioning or support anywhere in the copy, just "up to 30% off select styles."
- Fine print says exclusions apply but doesn't tell me which shoes are excluded until I click through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the "More to explore" grid for men's comfort/walking styles (Fresh Foam, 990 series) instead of mixing in kids' and racing gear — right now it feels like they didn't segment the send at all.
- 2. Add one line of copy under "Don't miss out" naming cushioning or arch support specifically — give guys like me a reason beyond just price.
- 3. Use a fresh hero photo instead of recycling the same shoe-box stack from three weeks ago.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members-only: up to 30% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, arch-support styles included — deadline's tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop online or find a store near you`
