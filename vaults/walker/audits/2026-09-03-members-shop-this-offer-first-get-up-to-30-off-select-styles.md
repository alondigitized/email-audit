# Members shop this offer first, get up to 30% off select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's actually clean — a big stack of shoe boxes up top, clear "Up to 30% off select styles," a code I can see right there (LABORDAY), and two buttons — "Shop now" and "Visit a store." That store button is the first time in six New Balance emails I've seen anything that acknowledges I might actually walk into a Famous Footwear or an actual New Balance store instead of clicking through my phone. That matters to me.
- Where it loses me is the same place every one of these does — the "More to explore" grid is six shoes and not one of them says "comfort," "arch support," or "walking" anywhere in the name. I've got the 1080v15 which I know is a decent cushioned trainer, but then it's running shoes, a skate shoe (NB Numeric 480), and a kids'-looking Rave Run in pink and teal. Nobody sorted this for a 62-year-old walker.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the "Shop now" button gets me, mostly because the offer's clear and I already have a code in hand. Would I forward it? No, this isn't forward-worthy, it's just an ordinary sale email that finally got the layout right.

## What's working

- The hero is a real photo of stacked shoe boxes, not another jargon-y grid — simple, doesn't need explaining.
- "Up to 30% off select styles" plus the LABORDAY code sitting right there on the page — I don't have to hunt for the offer or click through to find it.
- Two CTAs — "Shop now" and "Visit a store" — the second one respects that some of us still like walking into a store and trying a shoe on before buying.
- Price-tier shopping ("Under $50," "$50-$100," "Over $100") is a smart way to let me sort by budget without digging through filters on my phone.
- Free shipping banner up top ($75+) is visible without scrolling.

## What's weak

- The "More to explore" grid is a running shoe, a skate shoe, a bungee-lace throwback, and a pink kids-looking trainer — none of it screams "cushioned walking shoe for a retiree."
- No callout anywhere for arch support, cushioning, or hands-free/slip-on styles, which is the whole reason I'd open a shoe email in the first place.
- Fine print block repeats twice (once mid-email, once in the footer) — feels like padding I have to scroll past.
- This is at least the seventh New Balance send I've gotten this month and it's yet another generic all-styles sale — nothing here is new to me after "For every stride" and the members' offer from the 31st.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the "More to explore" grid to comfort/walking styles only (1080, Fresh Foam, anything with real cushioning) instead of mixing in running and skate shoes — that's the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Add one line under the hero offer calling out "cushioned comfort styles included" so I know the 30% actually applies to what I'd buy.
- 3. Cut the repeated fine print block — one mention in the footer is plenty.
- 4. Segment me out of the general blast — I've gotten too many of these look-alike sale emails this month; a "styles picked for you" subject would stand out more.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members-only: 30% off cushioned comfort styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day discount is ready — up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop cushioned, arch-support styles before the code expires Sept 7`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use LABORDAY in-store or online — sale ends Sunday`
