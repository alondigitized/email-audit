# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, would you look at that — it's the pink floral D'Lites again, this time under "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." like they caught me window shopping. I have never once clicked on that shoe, and this makes at least four sends this week parading it in front of me. Whoever's running this campaign needs to actually check whose cart it is.
- Here's the thing though — scroll past the cart nonsense and there's actually something useful this time: a real "HERE'S SOME MORE GREAT OPTIONS" grid with men's slip-ins, an Arch Fit Arcade sneaker, a John Deere waterproof boot. That's more my speed than florals. And the 20% off for Plus members banner near the bottom is a real offer with a real deadline (thru 9/7).
- But I'm not clicking today. The whole email opens with a shoe that isn't mine, and by the time it gets to stuff I'd actually wear, I've already half-checked out. Marking as read, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The men's slip-in grid (Altman, Delson 3.0, Tanzier boots, Arch Fit Arcade) is actually in my lane — slip-on, cushioned-looking, no laces to fuss with.
- The 20% off Plus members banner has a real deadline and clear "discount applied at cart" language — no hunting for a code.
- "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" is a plain, honest CTA — I know exactly what I'll get.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup up front — good for anyone price-conscious, though I pay in full myself.

## What's weak

- Leading with the pink floral D'Lites again — that's not my cart, and I've said so every time this week.
- "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US" is a cutesy line that just reminds me I'm being tracked, not persuaded.
- The men's options I actually like are buried three scrolls down, after the shoe I don't want and a big blue button for it.
- Too many "SHOP ADDITIONAL STYLES" category links (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing...) — feels like a sitemap dumped into an email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the product-match bug first — if the system keeps showing me a women's floral sneaker I've never touched, nothing else in this email matters. Lead with what I've actually browsed.
- 2. Move the men's slip-in grid up top, right under the headline, since that's the only part that's actually relevant to me.
- 3. Put the 20% off Plus banner higher — that's a real incentive, don't bury it after two irrelevant CTAs.
- 4. Trim the footer link farm — a phone screen doesn't need six category rows plus app/curbside/pay-later/store-finder all stacked.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's Slip-Ins You Actually Looked At`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% through 9/7 — no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort picks based on what you browsed`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The template resolves and renders correctly, but ships with a broken unsubscribe link and no plain-text MIME part, both of which are compliance-relevant deliverability defects.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links route through Attentive's click-tracking redirector (`skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=...`), consistent throughout.
- "Complete you Order" CTA and the primary cart-reminder link both returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) during probing — inconclusive, not confirmed broken, but worth a manual re-check given they're the primary conversion links.
- 12 of 38 links were probed (26 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); footer/social links were deprioritized and not individually verified.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Two images missing `alt` text: `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif` and `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`, plus the open-tracking pixel (`wf/open?upn=...`) also lacks `alt`.
- 12 images served over `http://` instead of `https://` (logo, product/CTA images, app-store badges, payment/social icons), e.g. `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/.../dde00662-...png`. Mixed content may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading.
- Responsive/MSO scaffolding (media queries, `mso-hide`, Outlook conditionals) is present and structurally sound.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{`, `%%`, `[[`) or literal placeholder tokens present in the visible HTML source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Broken unsubscribe link**: QA probe returned HTTP 400 for the "Unsubscribe" link (`.../ls/click?upn=u001.LNc6VorXOZu5avyAvDTfGQbua6...`). A non-functional unsubscribe mechanism is a CAN-SPAM violation risk and should be treated as a blocking issue, not cosmetic.
- **[FAIL] Missing/empty plain-text part**: text version is 0 chars. CAN-SPAM doesn't mandate a text/plain part, but its absence increases spam-filter risk and fails common ESP/deliverability best practice.
- Sender domain uses `msgs.skechers.com` (`hello@msgs.skechers.com`) — consistent with a dedicated sending subdomain, suggesting SPF/DKIM alignment is set up at the domain level, but authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) are not present in the supplied source/QA data and cannot be verified from this material.
- Postal/physical address footer is not visible in the truncated HTML provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM physical-address requirement from available evidence.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot assess: all outbound links are wrapped in Attentive's opaque `ls/click?upn=...` redirector rather than exposing direct destination URLs with UTM parameters. Actual landing-page URLs/UTM values are not visible in the truncated source and were not resolved by the QA probe.

7. Recommendations
- Fix or replace the unsubscribe link immediately — it currently returns 400 and is the highest-severity finding.
- Investigate the two 429s on the primary CTA links; re-probe outside of rate-limiting to confirm they resolve.
- Generate a non-empty plain-text MIME alternative.
- Serve all images over `https://` (12 identified) to avoid mixed-content blocking.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 2 content images; tracking pixel `alt=""` is acceptable as-is.
- Confirm the physical mailing address and unsubscribe mechanism are present outside the truncated portion of the source, since only a partial HTML payload was reviewed.
