# *Deep breathe* 20% OFF ENDS TONIGHT
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've now had a 20% TINSEL email nearly every day since the 5th. After "LAST DAY for 20% OFF!" this morning, "ENDS TONIGHT" lands as the third "last chance" in 48 hours. The "20% OFF*" on pink is still big and easy to read on my phone. But I've stopped believing the deadline.
- I scrolled the whole thing and didn't find a single men's shoe. It's loafers, mary janes, heels, boots and an UGG slipper boot. The nav has MEN, but the body ignores me. The only thing close to my needs is the "Shop Wide Width" button near the bottom. Nothing says cushioned, arch-supported or slip-on.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read. I'd only go to DSW if I was already after a sale on comfort shoes.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" headline is huge and readable without zooming, and the black "Claim 20% Off" button is hard to miss.
- The TINSEL code is the same one from earlier in the week, so I didn't have to learn anything new.
- The size row (5 to 11) with "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" is useful. Fit matters to me.
- The footer has free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and Afterpay. Those are honest, practical perks.

## What's weak

- There are no men's products at all. It's women's flats, heels and boots, so it clearly isn't aimed at a 62-year-old guy.
- The "Ends tonight" urgency is hollow. It's the sixth 20% pitch in four days, so I don't believe it.
- The email is too long and has too many offers. There's 20% off, extra 10% for veterans, free squishy, UGG 30% off and a VIP pitch. I couldn't tell which one was the point.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny gray text. I couldn't read it on a phone, and the "*" exclusions make me suspicious.
- The "Free Butter Squishy" toy is irrelevant to me. It's a gimmick next to a shoe sale.
- Nothing mentions comfort, cushioning or arch support, so I can't compare this to Hoka or Brooks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Send men a men's comfort block (walking shoes, slip-ons, wide widths) instead of the women's gift guide.
- 2. Cut the number of sends. If it really ends tonight, send one email, not three "last chance" ones.
- 3. Lead with one offer and drop the squishy and UGG blocks. Keep the 20% and the code.
- 4. Add comfort cues like "cushioned" and "arch support", plus ratings, under the products.
- 5. Make the fine print larger and shorter.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off ends tonight: men's walking shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Code TINSEL expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned walkers and slip-ons, wide widths included. Use code TINSEL.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours for 20% off regular-price styles, in stores and online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built send with click tracking through `ablink.dsw.com`. Automated QA found one confirmed broken CTA link (the SMS opt-in, HTTP 400), a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs, and no visible SPF/DKIM evidence. The HTML was truncated, so the checks below cover only the head, CSS and the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by QA):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned **HTTP 400**. The link is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/yW-iDTOZAq0vjC-TtGiCTA~~/AAQRxRA~/8HLLXZSO…` (about 600 characters of encoded payload). The 400 comes from the tracking-redirect layer, not the destination.
  - A 400 on a signed redirect usually means a malformed or truncated token. It can also mean the redirector rejects non-browser probes.
  - Re-test with a browser user agent before treating it as a production defect. If it is real, the sign-up CTA is dead.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 34,329 of 41,581 characters (about 83%) of the plain-text part are URLs. Long `ablink` tracking URLs in the text part hurt text-only clients and can add spam-filter weight.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 90 links were probed and 78 were skipped. Link health for the rest is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing. The charset declaration is ineffective, and the encoding depends on the MIME header.
- **Missing alt attributes (QA):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/z3Is8QEw4segTEmBnS8eBg~~/AAQRxRA~/aGeuWNP2…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261108_P260598_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_2`

  Both look like open/tracking pixels (the `/q/` path and `o.gif`). Low severity, but they should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Redundant CSS:** The universal selector declares `-ms-text-size-adjust` twice, first `100%` and then `none`. This is harmless but sloppy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible HTML. The footprint is limited because the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** `Authentication-Results` was not found (QA WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `dsw@e.dsw.com` could not be confirmed. This is a gap in the test harness (the AgentMail relay), not proof of failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The QA run reports no unsubscribe failure and always probes an unsubscribe link. The footer, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the supplied data, so they cannot be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs cannot be seen in the source. Continuity cannot be validated without following the redirects.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261108_P260598_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_2` on the `mi.dsw.com` pixel.
- No other continuity issues can be confirmed from the data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link in a real browser. If it still returns 400, regenerate it in Braze (the SMS opt-in/deep-link configuration).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
5. Shorten the plain-text part, or use shorter link-tracking domains and paths so URLs stop dominating it.
6. Verify authentication from raw headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`) outside the AgentMail relay.
7. Expand link probing to the 78 skipped links, and follow the redirects to check UTMs and landing pages.
