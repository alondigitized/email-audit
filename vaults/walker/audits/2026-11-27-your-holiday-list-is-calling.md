# Your holiday list is calling
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's New Balance again. That's the fourth gift-box-and-holiday send I've gotten this week. This one has a curly-haired boy hauling a stack of red presents, and he looks about ten. I'm a 62-year-old man shopping for my own feet, so a child in the hero doesn't speak to me. "No time like the present" is a nice line, but it's the same up-to-40%-off I saw on the 24th ("Gifting in motion") and on the Black Friday previews yesterday.
- The product grid is where it falls apart for me. I see a black slip-resistant work shoe, a running shoe, a kid's 2010, a kid's Hook & Loop, a three-pack of socks and a 530 Bungee that looks like a youth or women's colorway. Nothing says cushioning, arch support or walking comfort. The 574 NEW-B Hook & Loop and the 530 Bungee are close to hands-free, but nothing says so.
- I'd mark this as read. I wouldn't forward it. If I clicked at all, it would be "Walking" down in the footer, and that's a long scroll for a link that small.

## What's working

- The offer is plain: "Take up to 40% off select styles" sits right under the headline, with a clear "Shop now" button.
- Free shipping on all orders is in red at the top. I like that, since shipping charges spoil a sale for me.
- The "Shop by price" strip (Under $50, $50-$100, Over $100) matches how I do price-versus-quality math.
- The type is big and the buttons are chunky and dark. That's easy on my eyes and my thumb.
- The date range is stated: November 19 through December 28, 2026. That's honest, not a fake countdown.

## What's weak

- The hero is a kid carrying presents. Nothing in it is for a 62-year-old man, and it repeats the gift-box look from the 22nd and the 24th.
- The product grid is mostly kids' shoes, socks and a work shoe. I see no men's walking shoe and no cushioning or arch-support callout.
- The products have no prices, no sale prices and no star ratings. With "up to 40% off" I can't tell what's actually marked down.
- "Walking" is buried in the footer, and the top nav doesn't have it. I see Men, Women, Kids and Holiday.
- This is another near-duplicate of this week's sends. The Black Friday label on the grid is a little odd when the hero is about the holidays and gifts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a men's walking or slip-on shoe in the hero, with a real price, like "Fresh Foam walkers, now $79." The hero should show a shoe, not presents.
- 2. Add the sale price and a star rating under every product, and keep the grid to shoes the subject line implies.
- 3. Add a "Walking" or "Comfort" row to the main nav and the price strip, and one line like "cushioned, all-day comfort."
- 4. Cut the cadence. One strong send beats four near-identical ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walking shoes, up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cushioned comfort for less, thru Dec 28`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh Foam and 574 styles for men, plus free shipping on every order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, with no QA failures, but it contains leftover template artifacts (placeholder title, a placeholder background URL, a repeated class definition, `@font-face` rules with no `src`) and three images missing alt text. Authentication headers couldn't be verified, and the source was truncated before the body, footer and unsubscribe markup.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 25 were skipped by the click-likelihood ranking, so the 25 skipped links are unverified.
- Tracking is wrapped through `link.email.newbalance.com`, including the open pixel `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12745326_92079_24067509.gif`.
- The plain-text part is 13,207 of 14,943 characters URLs (88%), so the text alternative is mostly long tracking links. Spam filters can penalize that ratio.
- The `.bgwhite` class in the `<style>` block points to a placeholder, non-HTTPS URL: `background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png')`. If the class is applied anywhere, it will request a dead host over HTTP, which can trigger mixed-content or blocked-image behavior. I couldn't confirm usage in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Three images lack alt text:
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking pixel `egR1f966kc_..._24067509.gif`
- The two shipping images are content-bearing, so they need descriptive alt text. The pixel should have `alt=""`.
- Both shipping asset paths are under `/myNB_Welcome/`, so the module was reused from a welcome-series template.
- `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is an unreplaced template title.
- `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional while `<html>` carries an `xmlns` attribute and the tags are self-closed XHTML-style. This is a mismatch, though it is tolerated by clients.
- Every `@font-face` rule (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and others) has no `src` descriptor, so no web fonts load. Rendering falls back to Arial or Times New Roman everywhere, and `ITC Garamond Book Condensed` is declared but never defined.
- `.bg1` is defined twice. `{background-color:#ffffff}` is overridden by the later `{background:#58546d}`, so in light mode `.bg1` resolves to `#58546d`. That is probably unintended, since the dark-mode rule sets `.bg1{background:#151415 !important}`.
- The dark-mode media query forces `a {color:#cf0a2c !important}` globally. That red may have low contrast on the `#151415` dark background, and it overrides any link color set for buttons. I couldn't measure the contrast from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `email.newbalance.com` could not be verified. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a tooling gap rather than a confirmed failure.
- The unsubscribe link is always probed and no failure was reported. The footer and physical address fall after the truncation point, so I can't confirm them. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header values were not supplied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. Click destinations and UTM parameters were not visible in the truncated source, and the link probes reported no landing-page failures. The links are redirect-wrapped, so UTM presence on the final destination is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or replace the `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder in `.bgwhite`.
3. Fix the `<title>` and the `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">` tag.
4. Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules, or remove them and rely on the Arial fallback.
5. Resolve the duplicate `.bg1` rule so the light-mode background is intentional.
6. Trim the plain-text part to cut its URL ratio, for example by dropping the tracking wrappers on repeated links.
7. Verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` headers on the raw message, since the relay hid them from QA.
8. Check the shipping-module template for other welcome-series leftovers.
