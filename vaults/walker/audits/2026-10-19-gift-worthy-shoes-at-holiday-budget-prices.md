# Gift-worthy shoes at holiday-budget prices
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This one is the holiday gift pitch, and it's a break from the BOGO emails I got all last week, including "Final hours" on the 18th. The UGG slipper at the top is the first thing I notice. It's a slip-on, so it gets my attention. After that the email turns into a pile of women's shoes, and I lose interest.
- The pink "Our Kind of Nice List" block is a woman in snakeskin boots, then high-tops, flats, slippers and sneakers with prices floating over them. None of it is aimed at a 62-year-old man. The "Men" tab is in the nav bar, but nothing in the body points me there. I'd skim this and move on. It's not annoying, but it isn't for me. I wouldn't forward it. I'd click the UGG slipper only if I were shopping for a gift for someone.

## What's working

- The UGG slipper at "NOW ONLY $99.96" is a clear price and a clear "Shop Now" button. A slip-on is something I'd look at.
- It's a change from the BOGO repeats. After nine near-identical sends, this one feels fresh.
- The gift budget buttons ("Under $25 / $50 / $75 / Over $100") are easy to tap and match how I'd shop for gifts.
- The "Shop by Size" block with "Wide Width" and "Wide Calf" buttons is a useful touch. Wide fit matters to me.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and "Join for free" are clear loyalty cues, though they don't decide anything for me.

## What's weak

- Almost everything is women's. The hero, the model, the boots and the four product tiles are all aimed at women. There's no men's casual or walking shoe anywhere.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or comfort. That's what I shop for.
- The product tiles are cluttered, and the prices ($59.99, $49.99, $39.99, $69.99) are small and float over the photos. I can't tell what the shoes are or what brand they are. That's hard to read on a phone.
- The only CTAs under the tiles are "Shop Holiday Gifts" and "Shop All Women". The second one tells me right away it's not for me.
- The fine print is tiny, and the "COMP. VALUE" pricing language makes me doubt the "extra-nice prices."

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me a men's or comfort-led version. Lead with the slipper and add a men's row with cushioned slip-ons, walking shoes and brands like Skechers, Hoka and Brooks.
- 2. Label each product with a brand and name, not just a price.
- 3. Add a "Shop Men's Comfort" button next to "Shop All Women."
- 4. Make the prices bigger and put them below the photos so they're easy to read on a phone.
- 5. Give me one gift deal with a clear end date, like "Free shipping on gifts through 12/10."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email sends through a branded click-tracking domain (`ablink.dsw.com`), and the one failed probe is the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned HTTP 400. The HTML has a few invalid or accessibility-hostile `<head>` declarations. The truncated source and the QA data don't let me verify authentication, the unsubscribe header or UTM parameters.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA wraps `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/AlD802AzA1Oq_CQMqoWZEA~~/AAQRxRA~/sjhybhtK…` (about 600 characters of opaque payload). This is the only hard failure, and 62% of probes passed.
  - **Not confirmed as a real user failure:** a 400 on a tracking redirect can come from the probe (HEAD request, missing UA or cookies) rather than a dead link. The QA data doesn't say which, so re-test it with a browser-like GET.
  - **If it is real:** a broken CTA on a 15%-off offer hurts conversion and SMS list growth.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 20,018 of 23,187 characters (86%) are URLs. The long wrapped tracking links bloat the text alternative, which can hurt spam scoring and readability in text-only clients.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 58 material links were probed, so the other 46 are untested.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images.** Both are likely tracking pixels, since the second `src` is an `/q/…` open-tracker path:
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/FeugzhlvOSDqiznlHcla7A~~/AAQRxRA~/cxJwpC-…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?…`
  - Add `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them. This is low severity.
- **Invalid meta tags.** Both are missing `http-equiv`, so the charset and IE compatibility declarations are not valid:
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />`
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`
  - Clients that rely on the Content-Type header won't be affected. Strict parsers may ignore the charset declaration.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) on mobile.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` is the same on every send and gives no per-email context.
- **Web fonts.** Futura and Helvetica Now load from `braze-images.com` via `@font-face`. Support in email clients is limited, so I can't tell from the source whether the stack falls back safely (the HTML is truncated).
