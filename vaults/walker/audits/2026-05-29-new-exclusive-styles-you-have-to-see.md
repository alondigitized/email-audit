# New Exclusive Styles You Have to See
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Alright, here we go again. I'm opening this one because it's Skechers and I've been in the market for a new walking pair — not because "New Exclusive Styles You Have to See" told me anything useful. And then there it is in the preview: that same JSON garbage. Curly brackets, schema.org URLs, logo links. Fifth or sixth time I've seen this on a Skechers email in the last two weeks. At this point it's not a typo — it's neglect.
- Inside the email, the BOGO 50% banner is right at the top, which is fine. I know this deal — they've been running it since at least the 21st. But then the whole email opens on a woman in a casual jacket, then there's a sandals block, then a sneakers block, and a big "SHOP WOMEN'S EXCLUSIVES" button in orange before I ever see anything for me. I had to scroll to find the Men's section, and when I get there it's one blue athletic shoe that looks like it belongs on a track, not a boardwalk.
- And then — just like May 27th — there's the soccer sweepstakes again. Same "Ready for Kick Off" banner, same jersey prize package. Two days later and they're recycling it. I almost wonder if I opened the wrong email. I'd mark this as read and move on, not click, definitely not forward.

## What's working

- **BOGO banner leads the email** — it's the first thing I see, no scrolling required. Clear offer, clear math.
- **"Signature comfort" copy in the hero** — at least someone wrote the word "comfort." That's Skechers talking my language, even if briefly.
- **"Hi, Walker" greeting in the nav** — small thing, but I notice when a brand knows my name. Beats "valued customer."
- **Men's section does exist** — there IS a men's CTA with a visible shoe. I'd click it if I could get to it faster.
- **No broken images or overlapping text** — email rendered clean, no visual wreckage.

## What's weak

- **Broken preview text — again.** Same JSON schema.org junk I've been flagging since the 17th. Five-plus sends and nobody's fixed it. That's not a glitch, that's a process problem.
- **Women's content leads the email.** I'm a 62-year-old man. The hero model is a woman, the first two product tiles are women's styles, the first big CTA button is "SHOP WOMEN'S EXCLUSIVES." I have to scroll past all of that to find me.
- **Soccer sweepstakes is back — two days later.** I reviewed this exact block on May 27th. Running it twice in 48 hours makes me feel like I'm being spammed with filler, not engaged as a customer.
- **No product detail.** No cushioning rating, no arch support callout, no width options. "Explore the latest styles" doesn't help me decide if my feet will thank me by Tuesday.
- **Subject line is pure hype.** "You Have to See" is the oldest trick in the clickbait playbook. Tell me what I'm seeing — men's comfort walking shoes, new colorways, whatever.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text — this week.** Whatever JSON-LD block is leaking into the preheader, kill it and replace it with a single line of actual copy. This is free money left on the table every single send.
- 2. **Lead with Men's or go gender-neutral for segmented lists.** If you know I'm Walker Miles, 62, you know I'm not shopping women's sandals. Put the men's shoe up front or at least in the hero alongside women's. The current order tells me nobody checked who's on this list.
- 3. **Put a comfort claim in the hero** — not just "signature comfort" in small body copy. Make "cushioned insole," "arch support," or "all-day comfort" the headline, not a footnote.
- 4. **Retire the soccer sweepstakes or don't rerun it 48 hours later.** It felt like a mismatch on May 27th; it feels like spam on May 29th.
- 5. **Add one deadline to the BOGO.** "Through Sunday" or "Ends Monday" would give me a reason to act now rather than saving the email and forgetting about it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's Exclusives Now In — Plus BOGO 50% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Skechers Men's Styles: BOGO 50% Off Today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Comfort-first walkers, slip-ons, and casuals — shop men's new arrivals now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New men's styles just added — grab the BOGO deal before it's gone.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination links confirmed** — the 27 click-redirect links all route through `click.emails.skechers.com` and were intentionally skipped by the HTTP probe (expected behavior for click-tracking redirectors).

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (12 assets)** — all images served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com` rather than `https://`. Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook all enforce HTTPS for remote content; these images will be blocked or proxied, producing broken image placeholders.

Affected assets include:
- Skechers logo (`dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`)
- App Store badge (`0ec56c9f-…`)
- Google Play badge (`9bb508d0-…`)
- Curbside Pickup icon (`00100b23-…`)
- AfterPay icon (`07cb60b5-…`)
- Find A Store icon (`fc08601a-…`)
- Social icons: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube

**Missing `alt` text** on two assets:
- `o.gif` (ink1000.com open-tracker) — low severity, tracking pixel
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unknown content; if decorative, `alt=""` should be explicit

**`maximum-scale=1`** in `<meta name="viewport">` suppresses pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure.

**Duplicate `@media` breakpoints**: identical rule blocks appear for both `max-width: 375px` and `max-width: 640px` with identical declarations. Not a rendering bug, but it inflates payload unnecessarily.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**FAIL — Empty first-name token.** QA confirms the greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` — the name merge field resolved to empty. This affects all recipients whose first name was null or blank in the send list. The token itself is not visible in the truncated HTML but the QA probe confirmed the empty slot.

No other merge token failures detected in the visible markup.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Cannot fully verify** from truncated source — the footer (physical mailing address, unsubscribe link) is cut off. No unsubscribe link is visible in the provided HTML segment; presence must be confirmed in the full source.

**Krux DMP beacons** (`beacon.krxd.net`) carry a `partner_uid` that links the recipient's SFMC subscriber ID (`121173391`) to a cross-site ad profile. Under CCPA this constitutes a "sale/share" of personal data unless the subscriber has opted out via a GPC signal or explicit opt-out. Worth confirming the privacy disclosure and opt-out mechanism covers this.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** (global `*` selector) — disabling text scaling is an accessibility concern and may conflict with WCAG 1.4.4 compliance requirements.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_05292026_B` appears consistently across the SFMC open pixel and the Krux impression beacon — internal attribution is coherent.

**UTM parameter coverage on destination links cannot be verified** because all 27 click-through links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domain). Manual spot-check of 2–3 destination URLs is recommended to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are appended and consistent with the campaign ID pattern above.
