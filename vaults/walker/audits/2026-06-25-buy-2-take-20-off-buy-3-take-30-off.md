# Buy 2, Take 20% off! Buy 3, Take 30% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- Crocs is back, and this time they actually led with math I can understand. "Buy 2, save 20%, buy 3, save 30%" — no riddle, no buried offer, just a straightforward stacking deal right there in the subject line. After weeks of "extra 25% off" emails that kept resetting their own "last chance" deadlines, this at least feels like a real pivot. The Independence Day hook makes sense — it's the week before the holiday, I might actually buy a pair to wear to a cookout.
- That said, once I'm inside the email, the momentum fades fast. The big red SALE hero looks sharp, but below it I'm hit with "25% Off Select Work Styles" — I'm 62, I'm not buying work shoes — and then "40% Off Accessories," which is Jibbitz charms I'll never put on my feet. Two out of three sections in this email are not for me. And the whole thing has no deadline anywhere I can read clearly; if it's an Independence Day sale, tell me when it ends.
- I'd click the main buy-2-save-20% offer but only if I knew the Classic Clogs or a comfort model I already like are included in "Select Styles." That asterisk is doing a lot of work. Mark as read and maybe come back later — I'm not rushing.

## What's working

- **The math is right in the subject line.** 20% for 2, 30% for 3 — I don't have to decode a mystery offer. That's a first for Crocs in my inbox lately.
- **Independence Day framing is timely.** It's late June, this lands right before a holiday, and "celebrate in comfort" is something I actually think about when I'm picking footwear for a summer gathering.
- **The hero image is bold and clean.** Big red Classic Clog, the word SALE spelled out — the eye goes right to it. No clutter, no overlapping text.
- **The core offer stacks logically.** If I already want one pair, the nudge to grab a second for extra savings is the right kind of upsell for someone like me who buys for comfort year-round.
- **"Exclusions Apply" is stated upfront.** I appreciate that they're not hiding the fine print in the footer — it's right there under the headline.

## What's weak

- **The preheader is invisible garbage.** Whatever I'd see next to the subject in my inbox is blank or junk characters. That's a wasted shot at my attention.
- **"25% Off Select Work Styles" is irrelevant to me.** I'm not buying work shoes at 62. It eats space and muddies what the email is really about.
- **No deadline anywhere readable.** This is supposedly an Independence Day sale, but I have to squint at the tiny fine print to figure out when it ends. A 62-year-old on his phone is not doing that.
- **"Select Styles" appears three times and means nothing without clicking.** I have no idea if the shoes I'd actually want are included. That friction keeps me from committing.
- **After the June 22nd "last chance" that wasn't,** my trust in Crocs' urgency language is shot. They cried wolf. This email gives me no reason to believe this offer is actually limited.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a real deadline on the offer — in the hero, not the fine print.** "Sale ends July 4th" in the sub-headline would give me a reason to act now instead of closing the tab.
- 2. **Kill the Work Styles section or move it to a footer footnote.** Lead with comfort and casual, which is why I'm on their list in the first place. Dedicate that real estate to the Classics or slides.
- 3. **Show a specific shoe in the hero, not just a concept shot.** A Classic Clog with a brief callout — cushioned footbed, easy slip-on — would pull me toward the click without me having to guess what's included.
- 4. **Fix the preheader.** Right now it's dead space. Even "Sale ends July 4th. Classics included." would double the open rate.
- 5. **Drop the Accessories section.** I don't buy Jibbitz. If they must keep it, put it after a clear separator so it doesn't dilute the main offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stock Up Before July 4th — 2 Pairs: 20% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort deal: Buy more, save more this 4th of July`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends July 4th. Classic Clogs, Slides, and more included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The more pairs you grab, the more you save — through July 4th.`

If `media.crocs.com` is unreachable or the client blocks remote fonts (Outlook desktop, older Android), text will render in the client's default serif — verify fallback is `Arial, sans-serif` or similar.
