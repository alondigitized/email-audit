# TODAY ONLY! 25% OFF FOR VIPS
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, would you look at that — DSW again. Big "25% OFF" this time, promo code ERASER, and a valid date stamped right in the fine print (September 14) so at least I know this one's actually today and not another recycled "TONIGHT" banner. But the second I scroll past the number, it's the same six shoes I've been seeing all week: ankle booties, a wedge bootie thing with buckles, three pairs of loafers/flats, and one floral flat. Not a single men's shoe, not a single sneaker, nothing with actual cushioning or arch support in sight.
- I don't wear any of this. I'm not a "SO NEW, SO YOU" guy when everything pictured is for someone else entirely. The "Shop by Size" grid tops out at 11, women's sizing — doesn't even fit my foot. Between this and the six nearly-identical emails I got this week, DSW has made it pretty clear I'm not who they're actually emailing, they just haven't bothered to route me out of the general blast.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, and honestly close to unsubscribing at this point. Mark as read and move along.

## What's working

- The 25% off number is huge and impossible to miss — that part of the layout does its job.
- There's an actual promo code (ERASER) spelled out on the banner, not buried, so if I did want to shop I wouldn't have to hunt for it.
- "VIP Exclusive" tag and "Shop by Size" grid are clean, legible, decent tap targets on a phone.
- Shipping/pickup/app perks strip at the bottom is at least useful info if I were a shopper here.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown is a women's shoe — booties, loafers, a wedge sneaker-bootie, floral flats. Nothing for me.
- Zero mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support — the stuff I actually care about isn't even a category here.
- This is the same "20% off" (now "25% off") banner and "SO NEW, SO YOU" layout I've gotten close to seven times this week — just numbers and code swapped.
- "TODAY ONLY" in the subject but the fine print says the offer's valid through the day — fine, but after this many recycled sends the urgency doesn't land anymore.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender/category — if DSW has any browse or purchase history on me, swap the hero grid to men's casual/walking shoes. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Cut the send frequency. Seven near-identical emails in a week trains me to stop opening, not to buy.
- 3. Add a men's comfort callout — a small "Men's Comfort & Casual" band with cushioned/slip-on styles would actually get my eye.
- 4. Vary the creative between sends instead of just swapping the discount percentage and code.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles, 25% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your shoe: 25% off VIP deal`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals in men's casual and walking shoes — code ERASER`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned. Slip-on. 25% off for VIPs today only.`
