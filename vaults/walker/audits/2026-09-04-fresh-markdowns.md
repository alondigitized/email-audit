- I'd mark this as read and move on. Nothing to click, nothing to forward.

- The offer is at least concrete — $20 off $150 for members, dates spelled out (9/3-9/7) in the fine print, so it's not vague fluff.
- Big, clean type — I could read "Spend $150, get $20 off" from across the room, no squinting needed.
- Two clear buttons ("Shop Sale" and "Shop Now") so I know where I'd tap if I cared to.

## What's weak

- Still no actual shoes shown — "Fresh markdowns" and I can't see one single markdown item. That's the whole point of the email and it's missing.
- Same runner-crosswalk-action-shot demographic as every other HOKA send — young, sprinting, urban. Nothing that looks like me or what I'd wear to Costco.
- "Members Only" ticker banner is cut off mid-word on the right edge — looks a little sloppy on my phone screen.
- The $20-off-$150 offer requires spending real money to get a modest kickback — not the straightforward percentage-off deal that'd catch my eye.
- No sizing, no comfort/cushioning callout, no price-vs-quality info anywhere — nothing for a guy trying to comparison-shop.

- 1. Show me the actual marked-down shoes. If it's a markdown email, put product photos front and center — not a moody crosswalk photo.
- 2. Feature at least one comfort/walking style explicitly, with price crossed out and the new price shown — that's the "concrete offer" that gets me to tap.
- 3. Swap the runner action shot for lifestyle photography that includes more than one age group — right now it's all 20-somethings sprinting.
- 4. Fix the "Members Only" ticker that's getting clipped on mobile.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses Cordial ESP tracking redirects with JWT-encoded destination URLs; both a probed CTA link and the unsubscribe link return HTTP 403, and the plain-text MIME part is empty, which are the two material defects.

- **[FAIL] 403 on primary CTA**: link text "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/" resolves through `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6a8dbebfbc52777679083c94:ot:.../c6d5ead7?jwtH=...&jwtP=...&jwtS=...` and returns 403. Decoded `jwtP` payload targets `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260904_...`. The Cordial redirect itself is failing, not the final landing page.
- **[FAIL] 403 on Unsubscribe link** (see Compliance below) — same `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/...` redirect pattern, also 403.
- 12 material links were probed, 7 deprioritized (footer/social/utility) per QA methodology; no other broken links reported among the probed set.
- All tracked links route through Cordial's `c2` click-tracking redirector with per-link JWTs (`jwtH`/`jwtP`/`jwtS`) — standard ESP behavior, not itself a defect, but it means the 403s are an ESP-side redirect failure rather than a bad merchant URL.
