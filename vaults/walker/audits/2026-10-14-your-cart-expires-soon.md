## What's weak

- The hero is a women's floral sneaker. It's not my shoe, and I never carted it.
- Nothing says when the cart expires, so the subject's urgency isn't backed up in the email.
- Two boys' sneakers and three women's styles fill the grid, and only one shoe is for a man my age.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The cart-abandonment email has two QA failures: the probed Unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400, and the plain-text part is empty (0 chars). It also has a `<meta>` charset tag that is malformed, an empty `<title>`, and about a dozen images served over `http://`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400 (FAIL).** The link is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkz…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…&attn_sid=9ccbee9e-…`.
  - The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`.
  - A 400 could mean an expired or single-use token, or the probe being rejected. I can't tell which from the data, so verify it manually with a real recipient click.
- **429 responses on the "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." links.** Both go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`. The QA tool flags these as possibly valid, and I'm not treating them as broken.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 links were probed and 25 were skipped, so the remaining links are unverified.
- **Click tracking.** Every link I can see is wrapped in an opaque Attentive redirect (`/e/c/<blob>`). That is normal for the platform, but destination URLs can't be checked from the source.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` is the open pixel and has no alt attribute. This is a minor accessibility flag on a pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is empty (FAIL).** A 0-char text part hurts deliverability and breaks text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks.
- **Insecure image sources.** At least 13 images load from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`. They include the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the hero "Complete Your Order" image (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), the app-store badges, Afterpay, Find a Store and the social icons. Mixed-content blocking or security warnings can stop them rendering in some clients and webmail. Serve them over HTTPS.
- **Missing alt text.** `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` has no alt attribute. It is also served over `http://`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Malformed charset declaration.** The tag is `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it declares nothing. Clients will fall back to the MIME header, which usually works. The non-ASCII preheader padding (`͏`, `­`) is a risk if the MIME charset is wrong.
- **Alt text that isn't descriptive.** One image has alt "Text" (`49468f73-…png`). It is probably a text-as-image block.
