# Technical Audit: "Your Skechers Plus Monthly Summary is Here"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an SFMC (Salesforce Marketing Cloud)-sent transactional/marketing hybrid with standard responsive table markup; the main defects are widespread `http://` (non-HTTPS) image sources and several images missing `alt` text, plus one image referencing a non-production (`devemails`) host.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 37 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected for `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect domains — not independently verifiable, but not flagged as broken).
- Multiple tracking pixels present: SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?...`), Krux/Salesforce DMP beacons (`beacon.krxd.net`), a Return Path pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif`, missing `https://` scheme prefix in the `src` as shown), and an Ink1000 pixel (`www.ink1000.com`) served over plain `http://`.
- No issues found with tracking parameter structure itself (`mi_ecmp`, `bmt`, `d`, `confid`/`advertiserid`/`campaignid` all populated, not empty/placeholder).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes** on 3 images: `o.gif` (tracking pixel — low impact), `summary-tier-blue-angle.png`, and `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`. The latter two are content-bearing images and should have descriptive `alt` text for accessibility/screen readers and for when images are blocked.
- **Mixed content / non-HTTPS sources** on 10 images, including the primary "Skechers" logo (`image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-...png`) and the full footer app-badge/social icon set (App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube). Many mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) will block or warn on `http://` image loads, causing broken-image icons in the footer nav.
