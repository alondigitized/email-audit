# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-11-26**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I couldn't buy anything today. On the product page I tapped "Add to Cart" without a size picked, and the site showed a red "Please select a size" error. The cart page then came up completely blank white, with no cart, no empty-cart message and no header. The login step also looked like nothing happened. The Black Friday banners are loud and clear, and the Men's Shoes page has "Hands Free Slip-ins" right up top, which is what I came for. But I never got through a purchase, and a blank cart page makes me wonder whether the site is broken.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10.** That's the same as yesterday's 3/10. The browsing screens are decent. The cart is where it falls apart.

## 3. What's Working

- **Slip-ins up front.** On Men's Shoes, "Hands Free Slip-ins" and "Walking Shoes" are the first two tiles. That's what I'm shopping for.
- **Comfort filters on the homepage.** "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit" and "Max Cus…" run across the top. That's the kind of thing I look for.
- **Easy menu.** The Men menu has big rows with plain words (Shoes, Comfort Technologies, Collections) and arrows on the right. It's easy to tap and read.
- **Product page basics.** The price ($115.00), the 4.5-star rating with 27 reviews, the Klarna and Afterpay installment options and the Slip-ins logo are all visible without scrolling much.
- **Size buttons.** They're big, roomy squares that are easy to hit with a thumb. Sold-out sizes (12.5 and 14.0) are crossed out with a little bell for notifications.
- **Clear sale message.** 30-50% off and code BF2026 are hard to miss.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Add to Cart failed, and the page didn't help.** I didn't pick a size, and all I got was a small red line above the quantity box. The button's glow is the only cue that something went wrong, and I'd miss it. A better page would scroll to the sizes, or just highlight them.
- **The cart page is blank white.** That's the worst thing I saw. There was no items list, no "your cart is empty" and no header. A customer with money in hand would leave.
- **Login did nothing visible.** The "Log In" step looks exactly like the homepage. I didn't see a login form, a "Hi, Walker" greeting or any sign I'd been recognized. The site didn't feel personalized to me at all.
- **The popup step showed nothing.** It looks identical to the homepage, so I can't say whether a popup was dismissed or never appeared.
- **Search missed.** "Comfort shoes" gave 1,660 results. The first two were a women's loafer and a women's wedge, and I'm a man looking for cushioned shoes. I'd expect men's comfort shoes first, or a men's/women's choice up top.
- **The sale price is confusing.** On the product page it says "$115.00" and then "30% OFF! Applied at Cart." I don't know what I'd actually pay until I'm in the cart, and my cart was blank.
- **Small print.** "Discount automatically applied at cart. Exclusions apply*" is tiny and hard to read. The "Shop | Details" links in the code banner are small too.
- **Cart count.** The bag icon still showed 0 after the add attempt, which matches the failed add.
- **No comparison help.** Nothing on any screen helped me weigh Skechers against Hoka, Brooks or New Balance on price or cushioning. I saw no arch-support explainer and no price-per-comfort help.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the blank cart page first.** Show the header and either the items or a clear "Your cart is empty" with a button back to shopping. Find out why it renders blank, since it may be a load failure.
2. **Make the size error obvious.** Scroll the shopper to the size grid, outline it in red, and make the message bigger. Better still, pre-select a size or show "Select a size to add."
3. **Show the real sale price on the product page and in search results**, not "applied at cart." Seeing $80 beats seeing $115 plus a footnote.
4. **Make the login visible.** Show a sign-in screen, then a greeting and member pricing once I'm signed in.
5. **Fix search relevance.** For a men's shopper, "comfort shoes" should lead with men's Slip-ins, Arch Fit and Max Cushioning. Consider a Men/Women toggle at the top.
6. **Enlarge the fine print.** The promo terms and links should be readable without zooming.
7. **Add a plain-language comfort guide.** Cushioning level, arch support, and wide widths on the product page would help me decide.

## 6. Bottom Line

I like Skechers' slip-ins, and the browsing part of the site is easy enough. But I got an error when I tried to buy and a blank white cart after that. If I were really shopping, I'd go over to Hoka or Brooks and finish my purchase there. Fix the cart and the add-to-cart message, and this score would climb quickly.

**Versus yesterday (3/10):** No score change. I can't see yesterday's screens, so I can't name specific regressions or improvements. What I can say is that today's blank cart is a serious problem on its own.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

- Measurable evidence covers 18 axe violations (2 critical, 2 serious on the Homepage plus 2 on View Cart), 21 console errors (10 shown), and one network error: `429 https://www.skechers.com/cart/`.
- LCP and CLS were not captured for any step (`?`), so only TTFB can be assessed. The Web Vitals instrumentation needs fixing before the next run.
- The View Cart results look like an error page, not a cart (details under Accessibility and Console & Network Errors). The cart step was probably never audited.
- The `Log In` and `Men category` steps both report URL `https://www.skechers.com/`, and the HTML samples for Homepage, Log In and Men category share an identical `<html>` tag. These steps may be client-side state changes, or the journey may not have navigated. I'm inferring this from the data, not confirming it.

## Accessibility

**Homepage (10 violations):**
- **[critical] `button-name`:** one or more buttons have no accessible name.
- **[critical] `aria-required-parent`:** an ARIA role is missing its required parent, for example a `role=option` or `role=tab` outside its container.
- **[serious] `link-name`:** links have no discernible text, probably icon-only links.
- **Landmark structure:**
  - `landmark-no-duplicate-main`: more than one `<main>`.
  - `landmark-main-is-top-level`: `<main>` is nested inside another landmark.
  - `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`: an `<aside>` is nested.
  - `landmark-unique`: landmarks share the same role and name.
  - `region`: content sits outside any landmark.
- **[moderate] `page-has-heading-one`:** no `<h1>`.
- **[minor] `aria-allowed-role`:** a `role` attribute is invalid for its element.

**View Cart (4 violations):**

- **[moderate] `page-has-heading-one`:** no `<h1>`.

- **LCP/CLS:** not measured, so no assessment is possible.
- **Third-party script load is heavy.** The `<head>` injects about 20–30 async tags on the Homepage, and more on the Men category:
  - **Tag manager and analytics:** GTM, GA/gtag (`G-XZTV9LQ9DQ`, `DC-9630990`, `AW-1066305778`), Quantum Metric, Bing UET.
  - **Ad and conversion pixels:** TikTok, Pinterest, Yahoo, Snapchat, Reddit, Facebook, Nextdoor, Taboola, RTB House.
  - **Other vendors:** Impact, Bloomreach, Monetate, Pulse Insights, Tune (`go2sdk`), Stape.
  - Aggregate main-thread cost is likely significant.
  - Several use `vice-managed-src`, so a consent manager defers them, which is good.
  - The Men category page loads additional ad vendors (Taboola, Snapchat, Reddit, Facebook, Nextdoor) that the Homepage doesn't.
- **Webfont loading** uses `webfontloader.js` from `cdnjs.cloudflare.com`, a third-party host. Font loading is gated on JS, so text rendering waits on that script. Self-hosting with `font-display: swap` and `<link rel=preload>` would remove that dependency.

## Mobile Optimization

- The viewport appears to be about 664px tall (`--vh: 6.64px` × 100), set by a JS `--vh` shim. This is a legacy workaround. `dvh`/`svh` units are now widely supported and avoid the JS dependency and layout shift.
- Viewport meta, tap-target sizes and responsive images are not visible in the truncated samples, so they could not be assessed.
- The unlabeled buttons and links (`button-name`, `link-name`) are probably the icon controls used for touch navigation, so they affect screen-reader use on mobile too.

## Console & Network Errors

**Console (21 total, 10 shown):**
- **`Unsupported Summarizer API languages…`:** 4 occurrences. This is Chrome's built-in Summarizer API being called with unsupported languages. The caller isn't identified. It could be a page or third-party script, or a browser feature or extension. It needs attribution via the stack trace.
- **`Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed in this document`:** 6 occurrences. Some script registers an `unload` handler, which is blocked by permissions policy. This is typical of third-party tags. `unload` handlers also prevent bfcache eligibility. Replace with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`.
- The other 11 messages were not provided.

**Network (1):**
- **`429 https://www.skechers.com/cart/`:** rate-limited or bot-mitigated. This was likely triggered by the automated journey (rapid add-to-cart then cart navigation). It matches the bare-page axe results for View Cart, so the cart step likely failed to render. It is not necessarily a user-facing defect, but if real users can hit it, it is a cart-availability risk.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the measurement first:** capture LCP and CLS (e.g. `PerformanceObserver` with `buffered: true`). Confirm each step actually navigates. Log In and Men category share the Homepage URL, and Men > Shoes through Search show TTFB of 22–30ms.
2. **Re-run the View Cart step.** Handle the 429 with a backoff, a longer delay, or a trusted bot allowlist. Then re-run axe on the real cart.
3. **Critical a11y fixes:**
   - Give the unlabeled buttons an `aria-label` or visible text.
   - Fix the `aria-required-parent` structure, for example by wrapping `role=option`/`role=tab` children in their `listbox`/`tablist`.
   - Add accessible names to icon-only links.
4. **Landmarks:** use exactly one top-level `<main>`, un-nest `<aside>`, and give repeated landmarks unique `aria-label`s. Fixing this in the shared header/footer template should clear violations on the Homepage and Search at once.
5. **Add an `<h1>`** to the Homepage and cart templates.
6. **Third-party audit:** consolidate ad and analytics pixels through GTM or server-side tagging, and load them after interaction or idle. Identify the source of the `unload` handlers and the Summarizer API calls.
7. **Fonts:** self-host fonts with `font-display: swap` and preload critical weights, instead of loading `webfontloader.js` from cdnjs.
8. **Replace the `--vh` JS shim** with `dvh`/`svh`.
9. **Give `Log In` and `Men category` distinct URLs** if they are navigable pages, for crawlability and measurement.
