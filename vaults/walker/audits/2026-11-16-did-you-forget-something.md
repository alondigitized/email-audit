# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- The skull sneaker again. It's the same black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" I've been nagged about on 11/05, 11/08, three times on 11/11, and more times since. This is the third or fourth cart nag in two days, and I have never put a thing in a cart. "Ready to check out?" means nothing to me. There's no cart, no price and no size.
- The bottom half is better. The slip-in boots and the Murette slipper are the kind of shoe I'd look at. The Black Friday banner is clear: buy one, get one 50% off, or 20% off a single pair. But it sits well below a skull sneaker I'd never wear, and "Complete your order" shows up twice for an order that doesn't exist.
- I wouldn't click the hero. I might scroll to the slip-ins and look at the Murette. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wonder why they keep guessing wrong.

## What's working

- The Black Friday banner is the one clear offer: BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair, in big white type on dark blue.
- The slip-in boots and slippers in the grid are my kind of shoe, and the "Relaxed Fit" and "Slip-ins" names tell me they're easy to wear.
- The type is large and the layout is one column. It's easy to read on my phone.
- The "Shop All Best Sellers" bar is a wide black button that's easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The skull sneaker is the hero again. It's loud, young and nothing like what I buy. It's also the same shoe as every previous cart email.
- Two "Complete Your Order" buttons for a cart I never made feels like they're guessing.
- There's no price, size or comfort detail on the hero shoe. Nothing about cushioning or arch support.
- The offer is buried under the product grid, and the dates sit in fine print ("Offer valid 11/12/26 - 11/17/26"). The email never says the deal ends tomorrow.
- It's a long scroll. The text, app, curbside and Klarna blocks are clutter after the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart reminders to someone with no cart. Send a browse-based email with the slip-ins I've actually looked at.
- 2. Move the Black Friday offer to the top, and say "ends tomorrow, 11/17" in the headline.
- 3. Lead with a comfort hero, such as a Slip-ins Relaxed Fit shoe with a note on arch support and cushioning, instead of the skull sneaker.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the grid.
- 5. Cut the cadence. I got the same message earlier today.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Slip-ins for 50% off a second pair, ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, your comfort picks are on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one, get one 50% off. Men's Relaxed Fit slip-ins, online only through 11/17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Easy-on, cushioned and arch-supported. Pick your pair before the sale ends.`
