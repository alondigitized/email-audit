# Want 30% off first? Become a VIP!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I lost count of how many today. This is the fourth VIP pitch in two days, after "Become a VIP to shop 30% off first!", "Save 30% as a VIP (It's free!)" and "Join VIP, get access to 30% off!". The big black "30% OFF* (ALMOST) EVERYTHING" on the pink banner is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. But I've now seen this banner so many times I could recite it.
- Below the banner it turns into a fashion magazine. There's a woman in a beige jacket, "Slay bells are ringing," "Should you get these? Yeah, man." That's not talking to a 62-year-old guy looking for cushioned, slip-on shoes. The shoes are a New Balance-looking sneaker, a green Adidas-style one, a burgundy high-top and a wedge sneaker. Nothing is labeled as comfort or arch support, and I couldn't see a single price.
- Would I click? Maybe "Shop Men" up top, if I happened to be shoe hunting anyway. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the next one tomorrow.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF*" headline is huge, and I can read it on my phone without zooming.
- The "Black Friday starts…NOW!" line fits the calendar, so the timing feels real.
- "Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids" sits right under the banner, so I can jump straight to men's.
- The "Shop by Size" block with wide width and wide calf buttons is useful. Wide width is something I actually need.
- The free shipping, in-store pickup and Afterpay icons near the bottom are clear and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The asterisk and the "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" wording make me suspicious. The fine print is a wall of tiny gray text at the bottom, and I can't read it.
- The main button says "Become a VIP," so the 30% isn't for me until I sign up. The email does say "30% off first." I'd rather just see the shoes.
- The middle of the email is a fashion collage with a female model and cute headlines. Nothing is aimed at a 62-year-old guy, and no men's comfort shoes appear except maybe the sneakers.
- None of the shoes show a price, a brand name or a comfort feature. There are no cushioning, arch support or slip-on callouts. "Running Shoes / Boots / Slippers" is the only category row, and it feels like an afterthought.
- The UGG and Birkenstock 500-bonus-points offer is another UGG push after four UGG emails this week. I'm tired of it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one men's comfort hero near the top, such as a slip-on or walking shoe with a real price and a "Shop Men's Comfort" button. Cut the female model and the cute captions for people on the men's list.
- 2. Make the offer plain. Say "30% off with code BFDEALS" and drop the "Become a VIP" gate, or say what VIP gets me in one clear line.
- 3. Send one VIP email a day, not four. I'd also like to see a cadence change before the next send.
- 4. Label the shoes with brand, price and a comfort tag like "cushioned" or "wide width." Add star ratings.
- 5. Move the "Shop by Size" block higher, and add men's sizes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off men's comfort shoes, code BFDEALS`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wide-width walkers, 30% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned slip-ons and walking shoes from New Balance, Skechers and Hoka. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code BFDEALS at checkout. VIP members get first dibs on Black Friday prices.`
