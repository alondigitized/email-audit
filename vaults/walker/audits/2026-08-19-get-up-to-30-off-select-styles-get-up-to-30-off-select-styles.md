## Executive summary

- Shoe boxes stacked up, "Don't forget," 30% off with a code — that's it, that's the whole email. No models, no lifestyle shot, no "for the runner in your life" nonsense, just product and a price. I'll give them credit, this is the cleanest layout they've sent me all week. But it's also generic as can be — could be for anybody, any shoe. I did notice they repeated the shoe-box grid photo (9060 Lace) twice in the "more to explore" section, which is a sloppy touch for a big brand.
- There's a decent mix of styles down in the grid — some chunky dad-shoe looking ones (9060, 574) that actually look like something I'd wear, next to a running shoe I have no interest in. No mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, none of that — just names and pictures. I'd glance at this one for a few seconds longer than most, because at least it's not another running ad or a bunch of teenagers, but I wouldn't click unless I already had a specific shoe in mind. Not forwarding it to anybody.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout — no clutter, no wall of text, easy to scan on my phone.
- "Up to 30% off" with a code (BTS26) right up top, no hunting for the offer.
- Two clear buttons — "Shop now" and "Visit a store" — good for a guy like me who still likes going into a store sometimes.
- Product grid includes a couple of styles (9060, 574 Core Lace) that actually look like shoes I'd wear, not just running sneakers.
- No models this time, so no demographic mismatch to complain about for once.

## What's weak

- Nothing here says "comfort" or "arch support" or anything that speaks to why I'd actually buy a shoe — it's just a discount code slapped on a stack of boxes.
- The 9060 Lace shows up twice in the "more to explore" row — looks like a mistake, not intentional.
- "Shop the sale" by price bracket (Under $50, $50-$100, Over $100) is a filler section that doesn't tell me anything about the shoes themselves.
- Subject line literally repeats itself twice — "Get up to 30% off select styles, get up to 30% off select styles" — that's a glitch, not copy, and it makes me trust the email less before I even open it.
- No loyalty tie-in at all — I've bought from them before through this same inbox, and there's nothing here acknowledging that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix that doubled subject line first — that's an obvious bug and it's the first thing anybody sees.
- 2. Swap the duplicate 9060 Lace image in the grid for a different style so it doesn't look like an error.
- 3. Add one line of copy anchored to comfort/fit — even just "cushioned comfort, all-day wear" under the hero would do more for me than "Don't forget."
- 4. Lead the product grid with the comfort-casual styles (9060, 574) instead of burying them, since that's who's more likely to actually buy from a generic sitewide email.
- 5. If I'm a known past customer, say so — a small "welcome back" or loyalty points nod would go a long way toward feeling like more than a mass blast.
