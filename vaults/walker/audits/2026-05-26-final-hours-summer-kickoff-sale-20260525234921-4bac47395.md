# Final hours: Summer Kickoff Sale
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Alright, HOKA, here we go again — "Final hours" on the Summer Kickoff Sale. I'll be honest, after the run I've been on with these folks — eight, nine emails in two weeks now — my eyes glaze over before the screen even finishes loading. The blue banner is bright, the words are big enough I can read 'em without my readers, and "Snag your favorites before they're gone for good" at least has some urgency to it. But there's no actual offer in front of me. No percent off, no dollar amount, nothing. Just "Shop Sale."
- Then I scroll and see the Clifton 10 — women's and men's — and that's the part that always gets me half-interested, because that's a shoe I know by name. But again, no price, no "was $X now $Y." Just two product shots and a button. The bigger panel underneath is "Free Expedited Shipping" — which, fine, that's something, but I have to sign up for HOKA texts to get it. No thanks, I already get enough of their email.
- Would I click? Probably not — I'd flip to the May 23rd "Up to 40% off" email still sitting in my inbox, because at least that one told me a number. Forward to a friend? Nope. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- Big readable text on the hero — I didn't have to pinch-zoom to read "Summer Kickoff Sale."
- Clifton 10 men's shot is right there and labeled clearly — that's the shoe I'd actually buy.
- "Final hours" gives it some legitimate urgency, assuming the sale really does end tonight.
- The men's Clifton being shown alongside women's tells me they know guys read this too.

## What's weak

- No actual discount visible anywhere — last week's email said "40% off," this one says nothing. Did the price get worse or better?
- "Free Expedited Shipping" requires signing up for texts. Hard pass for a 62-year-old who doesn't want another channel buzzing.
- The action shot under the shipping banner is a blurry runner — that's not me. I'm walking the dog, not training for a 10K.
- This is the ninth-ish send in two weeks. Cadence is wearing thin and the creative is recycled.
- The "Shop Sale" button goes to a generic sale page, not to the men's Clifton I'm staring at.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the discount IN the hero.** "Final Hours: Up to 40% Off — Ends Tonight" beats "Final Hours: Summer Kickoff Sale" every time. Don't make me hunt for the number.
- 2. **Show the men's Clifton 10 price.** Was/now pricing under the product image. That alone would push me to click.
- 3. **Drop the "sign up for texts" gate** on free shipping for a last-hours email. Just give it to me. Friction at the finish line is the worst spot for it.
- 4. **Replace the blurry runner shot** with a guy in his 60s on a casual walk. The active-runner imagery keeps telling me HOKA isn't sure I'm their customer.
- 5. **Slow the cadence.** This is the tenth promo in two weeks — pick the one with the real offer and let it breathe.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's and women's styles in stock — ships free over $60.`
