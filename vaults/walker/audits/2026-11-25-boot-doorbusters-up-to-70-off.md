# ✨Boot Doorbusters✨Up to 70% OFF✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. Same pink banner and giant "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th. The subject promised boot doorbusters up to 70% off. The email buries that in a thin bar at the very top, and the big hero is the 30% code. The preview text mentions a "freebie" for Thanksgiving Eve. That turns out to be a free tote, and it's way down the page, in stores only, with a $75 purchase. That's not free.
- Everything below the hero is women's boots, slippers, heels and flats. I count maybe two men's-looking shoes, and those are loafers and sneakers way at the bottom. There's no cushioning or arch-support talk and no slip-on comfort angle. "Shop Men" is a small link in the hero. I wouldn't click. I'd skim it and delete it, and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The black "30% OFF*" is huge and I can read it without zooming.
- The "Shop Men" link is right under the hero, so I can skip the women's stuff.
- The "Shop by Size" block with a "Wide Width" button is useful for my feet.
- The free shipping, in-store pickup and text-signup icons are simple and easy to tap.
- Black buttons on white are clear and large enough for a phone.

## What's weak

- The boot doorbusters from the subject are only in a skinny top bar. The hero sells something different from what got me to open it.
- This is the seventh or eighth near-identical send since the 20th, including "ICYMI: 30% off for Black Friday starts today" and "Boot Doorbusters from $19.96." It feels like noise.
- The products are almost all women's. Slippers, heels and a leopard boot aren't for me.
- "Freebie" in the preview oversold a tote that needs a $75 in-store purchase.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text. The "up to 70%" and "(almost) everything" wording makes me doubt the offer.
- The "Weekly Holiday Recap" in script type is hard to read, and the shoe labels are tiny.
- No comfort info, no arch support, no reviews and no prices on most shoes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer in the subject. If it's boots, make boots the hero with a real price, not a code. If I've been getting this sale for days, tell me what's new today.
- 2. Segment by gender. Give me men's walking shoes, slip-ons and comfort brands up top, with "cushioned" or "arch support" in the copy.
- 3. Cut the send frequency. One or two emails a week would work better than the daily repeat.
- 4. Show real prices and a star rating under each shoe, and make the labels bigger.
- 5. Be straight about the tote. Say "in stores only, $75 minimum" in the preheader.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/SendGrid-style send with click tracking through `ablink.dsw.com` and a Movable Ink-style open pixel on `mi.dsw.com`. The QA run found one failed link, the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned HTTP 400. It also found two missing-alt images and a few source-level markup problems. Only 12 of 79 links were probed, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Failed link, the only hard QA failure:** the CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **HTTP 400** at `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/8-oP7ZM0oU0syobj89hMzw~~/AAQRxRA~/Ouf99T2B…`.
  - It's a long, opaque tracking URL of roughly 1,000 characters, so the destination can't be read from the source.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking redirector can mean a malformed or expired token. It can also mean the probe was blocked, for example by a bot or header check. The QA data can't tell these apart, so verify it with a real client before treating it as a live defect.
  - If it is real, it hits the highest-value acquisition CTA in the email, the 15% SMS-signup offer.
- **Coverage gap:** 67 of 79 links were skipped. The 62% pass rate is based on a small sample, so the other links are unverified.
- **Link wrapping:** every link, and at least one image (`https://ablink.dsw.com/q/ZHkhqXt_…`), goes through the `ablink.dsw.com` redirector. This is normal, but each link depends on that domain's uptime.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/ZHkhqXt_Plm28n7sE8UHmA~~/AAQRxRA~/j6rxXA_v5j1N…`. This is a tracked content image, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get no description. It should have real alt text.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261125_P250961_V2_WM-_Offer_MI_C`. This looks like a 1×1 tracking or personalization pixel. Add `alt=""` so it's treated as decorative. This one is low severity.
- **Malformed meta tags in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it declares nothing.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Neither does anything as written. Encoding then depends on the HTTP/MIME headers, which is a risk for the emoji in the subject and body.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. That conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` carries no content-specific information. This is minor.
- **Dark mode:** the dark-mode CSS (`prefers-color-scheme`, `.dm-bg-*`, `.dm-font-*`) is present. The source was truncated, so I can't confirm full coverage.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source or the QA data.
- The open pixel carries `mi_u=225090073`, a numeric recipient identifier, in a query string. It's expected for this kind of tracking, but it ties each open to a person. Confirm it matches DSW's privacy disclosures.
- The truncated source means I can't rule out tokens later in the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** the QA data has `[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found`. The email arrived through the AgentMail relay, which likely strips or rewrites the header. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, not failing. No conclusion can be drawn.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result for it appears in the QA findings, and the footer isn't in the truncated source. The unsubscribe link, postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are therefore **unverified**.
- **Plain-text part:** `[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs: 30600/36698 chars are URLs`. About 83% of the plain-text alternative is raw tracking URLs. That can raise spam-filter scores and makes the text version hard to read.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing pages can't be assessed. Every link is wrapped in an opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirect token, and the QA run didn't record the final destinations after redirect.
- The only probed result was the 400 on the SMS opt-in link, so no landing-page alignment data exists. This isn't a defect, just a limit of the data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-test the SMS opt-in link** (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/8-oP7ZM0oU0syobj89hMzw~~/…`) in a real client and with a browser-like user agent. If it still returns 400, fix or regenerate the tracking link, since this CTA drives the 15%-off SMS signup.
2. **Fix the head meta tags:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag to restore pinch-zoom.
4. **Add alt text:** meaningful alt on the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image, and `alt=""` on the `o.gif` pixel.
5. **Shrink the plain-text part:** cut tracking URLs there, or shorten link tokens.
6. **Verify authentication and unsubscribe handling** by checking SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` on the original message, outside the AgentMail relay.
7. **Probe more links:** probe more than 12 of the 79 links, and record final redirect URLs so UTM and landing-page checks are possible.
