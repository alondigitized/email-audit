# LAST. DAY. $10 OFF. HURRY!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Same $10/$20/$60 tiered coupon DSW's had me on for a solid week now — I could write the fine print myself at this point. Sizes 5 through 11, heels, sandals, one pair of leopard-print sneakers buried in a "So New, So You" grid — none of it's for me. There's a shoe-size grid halfway down, which is a nice touch in theory, but it stops at 11 and doesn't say a word about width or a men's section, so it's not doing me any favors either.
- The "Shoeprise Savings Hunt" banner made me stop and squint for a second because it's visually loud, but then I realized it's an in-store scavenger hunt for a "red arrow," which isn't something I'm driving to the mall for. Nothing here moves me to click, and I definitely wouldn't forward it — I'd just mark it read and move on like I have the last five times.

## What's working

- The $10/$20/$60 tiers are laid out big and readable — no squinting, no math, same as every DSW email this week.
- "Free Shipping for VIPs" and the loyalty perks strip at the bottom at least acknowledges I might be a member.
- The size grid is a decent idea for skipping straight to what fits, even though it's built for women's sizing here.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown is a women's heel or sandal — nothing cushioned, hands-free, or arch-supported in sight.
- No men's section link up top-level with the offer; MEN is just one tab among five in the nav bar.
- The "Shoeprise Savings Hunt" in-store scavenger hunt is a lot of visual real estate for something totally irrelevant to how I shop online.
- Same tiered coupon I've now seen four-plus days running — the cadence is wearing thin.
- Subject says "LAST DAY" but nothing in the body backs up a hard deadline with an actual date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero product grid for men's comfort styles — Skechers, Hoka, walking shoes — instead of six women's heels and sandals; that's the single biggest miss.
- 2. Cut or shrink the in-store scavenger hunt block — it eats prime scroll space for something I can't act on from my phone.
- 3. Put an actual expiration date/time next to "LAST DAY" so the urgency feels real instead of just loud.
- 4. Add a men's size grid alongside (or instead of) the women's one shown.
