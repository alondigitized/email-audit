## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The Crocs "Shop NBA" email is structurally sound: it has a responsive media-query set, a `lang` attribute, a viewport meta tag and a preheader. QA found 0 failures and 1 warning (86% pass rate). The issues are mixed-content `http://` image sources, one image with no alt text, and authentication headers that couldn't be verified.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 26 tracking links were skipped because the HTTP probe doesn't follow click-redirect domains. That means destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified, not confirmed good.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?2ADA2N6NMWZUVDYZUVQALI25N4.10203&d=10203&bmt=0`. It is HTTPS, 1x1, and has `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative image.
- A second hidden beacon, `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`, is an Adobe Audience Manager pixel. It is `display:none` and `visibility:hidden`, so some clients may not fire it. Opens will be undercounted wherever Apple Mail Privacy Protection or image blocking applies.
- The demdex pixel carries a `d_dpuid` value that looks like a per-recipient identifier in the URL. This is not a rendering bug, but it is a privacy and compliance consideration (see section 5).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** the `demdex.net/event` `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. It is a hidden tracking pixel, so screen-reader impact is minimal. Adding `alt=""` would remove the warning and make the markup valid.
- **Mixed content (QA WARN, 7 images):** these images load over plain `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - `9c36c0ef-…png` ("Download on the App Store")
  - `13246762-…png` ("Get It On Google Play")
  - `c4df71ec-…png` ("Find Your Store")
  - `e18949ff-…png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-…png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-…png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-…png` (YouTube)

  Clients that proxy or enforce HTTPS may block them. In that case the footer icons and app-store badges would drop out. They all have alt text, so the failure degrades gracefully. The fix is to switch to `https://`, since the same host already serves HTTPS elsewhere in the email.
- **`<meta>` tags malformed:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. Charset is not declared by a working tag. Most clients take the encoding from the MIME headers, but a correct `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` or `<meta charset="utf-8">` is safer.
- **Custom font:** the TT Crocs `@font-face` URLs are HTTPS with `font-display: swap`. Most clients ignore web fonts, so the fallback stack is what renders. I couldn't see the inline `font-family` values in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the fallbacks.
- **Hidden preheader padding:** the `͏ ‌ ­` filler characters are standard preheader-padding technique and are fine.
- The HTML was truncated, so body-level checks (table `role="presentation"`, button markup, contrast) couldn't be done.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `d_dpuid` and open-pixel IDs are rendered values.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Authentication (QA WARN):** no `Authentication-Results` header was present because the message came through the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, not failing. The From domain `crocs-email.com` should be checked directly against its DNS records or a header from a direct delivery.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** I couldn't verify these. The HTML is truncated before the footer and no headers were supplied. The 26 skipped tracking links probably include the unsubscribe link, but that isn't confirmed.
- The demdex beacon sends a recipient-level identifier to a third-party ad-tech domain. That may require disclosure in the privacy policy under GDPR/CCPA. This is a flag to check, not a violation.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not assessable. All 26 links go through `click.crocs-email.com` and were not resolved. I couldn't confirm UTM parameters, destination URLs or whether the NBA landing page matches the email. The visible content has no direct crocs.com URLs.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Change the 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image URLs to `https://`. This is the one confirmed rendering defect.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel `<img>`.
3. Replace the two `<meta>` tags with `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Re-run the audit with a resolver that follows the `click.crocs-email.com` redirects. This would let you verify destinations, UTM parameters and the NBA landing page.
5. Capture the raw headers on a direct send to confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address.
