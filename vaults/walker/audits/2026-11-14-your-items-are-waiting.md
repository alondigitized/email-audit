- The navy "Complete Your Order" button is big and easy to tap on a phone.
- The Skechers logo and look match what I'd see scrolling, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The cart-reminder framing is false. I had no cart, and it's the same skull sneaker I've been sent all week.
- The BOGO deal is buried below two screens of shoes. The hero should have been the offer.
