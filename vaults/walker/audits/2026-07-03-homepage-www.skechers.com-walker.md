# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- All right, I open this up and the first thing I see is a big red-white-and-blue splash pushing a BOGO deal — timely with the 4th of July coming, I'll give them that. Skechers is clearly leaning hard on its loyalty club right now, stacking two separate member-exclusive offers on one screen. As a brand it reads "deals first, shoes second" — I don't see a single shoe above the fold, which is a little odd when I'm here to shop for footwear.

## What's working

- **The stacked loyalty messaging** — the top bar calls out BOGO already applied at cart, then a second bar offers an additional 15% off with a code. As a Skechers Plus member I'm feeling rewarded before I've even scrolled once.
- **"Automatically Applied at Cart"** — I don't have to fish around for a promo code box or wonder if my deal worked. That's a relief at my age; I've lost discounts before fumbling with code fields.
- **"Arch Fit" in the horizontal nav** — it's right there alongside Slip-ins and Sandals. One tap and I'm in the category built for feet like mine.
- **The 4th of July theming** — the stars-and-stripes design matches the calendar. I notice when a brand is current, and this tells me someone's paying attention.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — I was probably on this site earlier this week. The fact I can jump back to what I was looking at without hunting around is a small thing that goes a long way.

## What's weak

- **Not a single shoe is visible above the fold** — I came here to look at shoes. The entire first screen is promotional graphic. I have to wonder, what exactly am I buying with this deal?
- **No primary CTA button** — "Shop" in the secondary banner is a text link, easy to miss. The BOGO hero has no button. As a guy who browses on his phone, I need a big obvious tap target.
- **No men's hero imagery or copy** — the page defaults to "Womens" in the tab strip below the hero. If I didn't notice the "Mens" tab, I might think this sale isn't for me.
- **No comfort-focused language in the hero** — "cushioned," "wide width," "arch support," "slip-resistant" — none of that appears anywhere above the fold. This reads like a coupon page, not a comfort footwear store.
- **"Exclusions Apply*" with no detail on screen** — when I see that asterisk I wonder if the things I actually want are excluded. The fine print isn't surfaced.

## Recommendations

- **Add one lifestyle product shot** — swap half the BOGO hero for a photo of a comfortable-looking men's walking shoe; the deal copy can share the screen with an actual product.
- **Make the "Shop Men's Deals" a real button** — put a clearly tappable CTA inside the hero pointing men directly to the sale, rather than a small "Shop" link buried in a secondary bar.
- **Lead with a comfort signal** — even one line like "Arch Fit & Slip-ins included" inside the hero tells me the deals cover what I actually care about, so I don't second-guess the exclusions.
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold** — a simple "Free shipping on orders $50+" in the top banner would remove the last friction point before I decide to load up my cart.
