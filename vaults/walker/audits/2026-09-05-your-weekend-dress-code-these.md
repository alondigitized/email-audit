# Your Weekend Dress Code: These
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least this one's a shoe I'd actually put on my feet — a metallic-buckle slide sandal, not a clog covered in cartoon charms. But "your weekend dress code" is a strange way to talk to me. I don't have a dress code for mowing the lawn or grabbing coffee, I just want something I can slip on and walk in without my arches screaming. The mauve/lilac color and the gold buckle hardware read pretty feminine to me too, so even though the style itself (a slide, buckled, some structure) isn't a bad idea for a guy like me, I don't feel like they built this one with me in mind.
- The $19.99 starting price is the one thing that'd stop my thumb mid-scroll — that's a real number, not some vague "up to" nonsense. But there's no arch support callout, no mention of a men's version, and "exclusions apply" right under a splashy price always makes me figure the shoe I'd actually want isn't included.
- I'd give this one a glance, maybe a half-second longer than the Pokémon and college-logo ones, but I'm not clicking and I'm sure not forwarding it. Straight to read-and-archive.

## What's working

- One real, concrete price — $19.99 — front and center, no games.
- The buckle sandal actually looks like a normal, wearable shoe, not a novelty item.
- Big, clean CTA buttons (Shop Clogs, Shop Sandals, Shop Sale) are easy to tap on a phone.
- No render bugs — everything lined up, logo and images loaded fine.

## What's weak

- The sandal color and hardware skew feminine — nothing here signals "this is for men."
- "Your Weekend Dress Code" is a cute line for a copywriter, not a shopper my age looking for comfort.
- Zero mention of arch support, cushioning, or all-day comfort — the stuff that actually gets me to click.
- "Exclusions Apply" sitting right under the big price number makes we wonder if the deal's even real.
- Four identical-looking pill buttons with no visual hierarchy — my eye doesn't know which one matters most.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a men's colorway of the sandal (or a men's model) next to the price — right now this reads as a women's send that happened to land in my inbox.
- 2. Cut the "dress code" wordplay and lead with the comfort features — cushioned footbed, arch support, slip-on ease.
- 3. Move "Exclusions Apply" or clarify it — tell me right there if the $19.99 covers this exact sandal or just some styles.
- 4. Make one button visually bigger/bolder as the primary CTA instead of four equal pills.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's Sandals Now Starting at $19.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Slip-On Comfort, Weekend Price`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buckle sandals, cushioned soles, starting under $20`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sandals for the guy who just wants comfort — from $19.99`
