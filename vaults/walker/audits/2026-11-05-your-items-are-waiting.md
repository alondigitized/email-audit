# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- It's the same cart nag again, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. This is the fifth one in about a week. "Your Cart Is Still Waiting" sits over a black sneaker with a blue skull painted on it, and I've never looked at that shoe. Calling it "my cart" doesn't make it so. It was a little creepy last time and it's still creepy ("We noticed you noticing us...").
- The bottom half is better for me. The slip-ins row has men's relaxed-fit slippers and a waterproof slip-in boot. That's my kind of shoe, hands-free and easy. The 25% off for Skechers Plus members is easy to read, but it's buried below the shoe grid, and it ends 11/4, which is yesterday. So the one real offer in here looks expired.
- Would I click? Maybe on the brown Murette slipper, if I were already shopping. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The men's slip-in slippers and the waterproof Tanzier boot match how I shop: hands-free, comfortable, easy to get on.
- "25% OFF" in big white type on the navy bar is clear and easy to read on a phone.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" is a plain button with a verb and a noun.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) is big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- It looks like Skechers. The logo and layout match the site, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The skull sneaker is nothing I looked at, so the "cart" claim feels false. It's the same skull shoe as the 11/5 "Your cart expires soon!" email.
- The 25% offer says "Valid thru 11/4" and "2 days only," and the date has already passed. That reads stale and a little dishonest.
- The offer is for Skechers Plus members only, and the fine print says I have to be logged in. I don't know if that applies to me.
- The product captions are tiny. I'd have to zoom in to read them, and there are no prices.
- The hero is huge and empty. I have to scroll a long way past it to reach anything useful.
- The shoe grid is mostly women's styles. Only about half of it fits me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show what was actually in the cart, or drop the cart language when there isn't one. If it was browsing, say "Still looking at slip-ins?" and show that shoe.
- 2. Put the member offer right under the hero and make sure the date is still good when the email lands.
- 3. Lead with men's slip-ins and add prices, plus a simple line like "Arch Fit, cushioned, no bending."
- 4. Add star ratings under each shoe, and make the captions bigger.
- 5. Space these reminders out. I can't tell them apart anymore.
