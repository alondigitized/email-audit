# Luxe styles in new shades
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- Another HOKA email, and this one has shoes up top. "Level up" is huge white type over a close-up of a brown leather shoe with a yellow Vibram-looking sole. It reads fine on my phone. But it looks like a hiking boot, not the cushioned walking shoe I buy from HOKA. The Elite Terrain Tecton X 2 and Tor Summit LTR below it are rugged and brown. They're not slip-on comfort.
- The Clifton Utility in grey is the closest thing to my kind of shoe. It's buried in the middle, though, and the copy never says whether it's cushioned or easy to get on. The bottom half drifts into trail running and a free shipping push, and a free shipping banner shows up twice. I'd skim it and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it. A buddy who wants a "luxe" boot-style sneaker might like it.

## What's working

- "Level up" is huge, high contrast, and readable on my phone without squinting.
- Shoes lead the email. There's no ID.me stamp or lifestyle fluff up top like the Nov 11 send.
- The Clifton Utility block is bold and has a full-width "Shop Clifton Utility" button I could tap easily.
- The "All Gender" labels and plain product names make it clear what I'd be looking at.
- The free shipping bar at the top gives me a clear number to check against my cart: $250.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe looks like a trail boot. I couldn't tell if it has the cushioning or arch support I care about.
- No prices, no sizing or fit info, and nothing on cushioning, so I can't do my usual price-versus-quality math.
- The "Free Expedited Shipping **" headline has two asterisks, and the fine print is tiny. It needs $250+ and expires Nov 16, and there's a separate text-signup version. That's confusing, and I trust it less.
- The trail running photo shows three young runners, which isn't me. The red shoe splashing in water isn't me either.
- The footer links ("Locate a Store", "Unlock Free Shipping") are small, and the tiny legal text is unreadable without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear product in the hero with a price and a comfort cue, like "cushioned all-day walking," and not just a mood shot.
- 2. Give the Clifton Utility a real block with price, key features and a slip-on or easy-fit note. Move it above the Elite Terrain pair.
- 3. Pick one shipping offer and state it plainly with the deadline, instead of repeating it twice with asterisks.
- 4. Show the Bondi or an all-day walking shoe next to the "luxe" styles, so a walker has a place to click.
- 5. Make the footer links and fine print bigger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Cordial template with valid UTM-tagged destinations. However, the automated QA run has a 26% pass rate: it has no plain-text part, none of the 10 flagged images has alt text, and the template has malformed CSS and conflicting viewport tags. The 400/403/406 link failures look like prober rejection at the Cordial redirector, not confirmed dead links (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 of 12 probed links returned an error.** Ten returned 406, one returned 400 (`.../b6e6769c`, the `hoka.attn.tv` SMS landing page), and the unsubscribe link returned 403.
- **Likely cause, not confirmed:** every failing URL is a `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:.../` Cordial click-tracker. A uniform 406/403 across a single redirect host usually means the redirector rejects non-browser clients. The decoded `linkUrl` values in the JWTs point to normal `www.hoka.com` pages, so I can't confirm any are actually dead. Re-probe with a browser-like User-Agent before treating these as defects.
- **Image links have no readable text.** Link text for hero and product tiles is only the image filename (e.g. `[image: US_TectonX2TP_TopLeft_1176645-AVSP.jpg]`). With no alt text, these links have no accessible name.
- **Stale asset name:** `US_20260913_HOKAGritPack_h.jpg` (a September asset) is used in a 2026-11-14 send, and its link goes to `/en/us/trail-running-guide/`. This is a reuse signal only.
- **Coverage gap:** 13 links were skipped, so footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text missing on 10 content images.** Examples: `H_US_Banner_FES_1.jpg`, `US_20261114_TerrainPackNewColors_a.jpg` through `_d.jpg`, `US_TectonX2TP_TopLeft_1176645-AVSP.jpg`, `US_TorSummitLTRTP_TopRight_1182950-AQLV.jpg`, `US_Q3_SMSCTA_DoubleAsterisk.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. QA also flagged the two tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`), but those are harmless; give them `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>`.
- **Malformed `<meta>`:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` attribute, so it declares no charset.
- **Conflicting viewport tags:** two are present. The first has `maximum-scale=1` and the second has `user-scalable=yes`. The `maximum-scale=1` value blocks pinch-zoom in some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Broken CSS from empty template variables:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`. These declarations are invalid and ignored.
- **Web fonts:** `TheFutureHOKA` is loaded from `hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...`. This is unreliable in most email clients, and fallbacks are needed. I can't see the font stack in the truncated source.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so I can't check the rest of the body for table structure, `role="presentation"` or dark-mode handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) appear in the visible source or the decoded URLs. Part of the source is truncated, so this only covers what was visible.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part missing:** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This affects deliverability and accessibility.
- **Authentication unverified:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found (AgentMail relay). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `emails.hoka.com` are unknown.
- **Unsubscribe link is present** (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...`) but returned 403 on the probe. This is probably bot-blocking, but a human click-test is warranted because it is a compliance-critical link.
- **Not verifiable:** the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address in the footer (not in the visible source).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM tagging is consistent and complete.** The decoded destinations carry `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261114_EliteTerrain_FES_STO_A15Day_HighestEmailEngaged_365NewArrivalsTag_270LifestyleTag_365LifestylePurchasers`. The `/en/us/new-colors/` hero and the product pages (`/elite-terrain-system-tecton-x-2/1176645.html?dwvar_1176645_color=AVSP`) match the "new shades" subject.
- **Campaign name leaks segmentation logic.** Audience tags (`HighestEmailEngaged`, `365LifestylePurchasers`) sit in the URL, which is visible to recipients. This is minor.
- **Identifiers in every URL:** `hmail=9153f33f...` and `cordial_id=69e56048...` are appended to every destination.
- **Landing-page behavior is unverified.** Every probed link was blocked, so I couldn't confirm landing page content or promo carry-through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe with a browser-like User-Agent (or an allowlisted IP) to separate real 4xx errors from redirector bot-blocking. Manually click the unsubscribe link.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Add a plain-text MIME part (currently 0 chars).
4. Fix the template head: add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to the charset meta, fill in `<title>`, and keep a single viewport tag without `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove or default the empty `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border` values.
6. Capture the full raw headers to verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and `List-Unsubscribe`.
7. Review the truncated body for the postal address, and confirm font fallbacks.
