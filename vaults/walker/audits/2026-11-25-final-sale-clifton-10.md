## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is image-based, with the hero, subcopy and CTA all delivered as JPGs. It has no plain-text part, missing alt text on its content images, and two link probes that returned 403. The 403s come from a tracking-redirect domain and may be bot filtering rather than real breakage. Authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Both probed links returned 403.** These are the `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` link and the Unsubscribe link. Both go through the Cordial tracker `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…/ff0cd2d3?jwtH=…` and `…/cc6c76bf?jwtH=…`.
  - Decoding the JWT payloads shows the destinations are `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=…` and `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&…`. Both are well-formed.
  - Both links return an identical 403 from the same tracker host. That points to bot/WAF filtering of the probe, not necessarily broken links for real recipients. **Unconfirmed**, so re-test with a browser-like client.
  - If the 403 is real, the Unsubscribe breakage is a compliance problem (see section 5).
- **Probe coverage was limited.** 12 material links were found and 7 were skipped. The primary product CTA links (Clifton 10, Bondi 9) are not confirmed in the data I have, and the HTML source is truncated.
- **Tracked-link JWTs have an unverified lifetime.** The payload carries `"ce":86400`. If that is a 24-hour expiry, older links may fail after the send window. I can't confirm what the field means from this data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 content images.**
  - `H_US_Hero_LastChanceClifton10_2.jpg`
  - `H_US_Subcopy_Hero_LastChanceClifton10_2.jpg`
  - `H_US_CTA_Hero_LastChanceClifton10_2.jpg`
  - `H_US_Secondary_Bondi9Markdown_1.jpg`
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
  - The hero headline, subcopy and CTA are all images (1200×1400, 1200×188, 1200×224). With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the offer and CTA are invisible.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. Low impact, but an empty `alt=""` is cleaner.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **Duplicate, conflicting viewport metas.** The first has `maximum-scale=1` and the second has `user-scalable=yes`. Clients resolve this inconsistently, and `maximum-scale=1` restricts zoom.
- **Invalid CSS in the media query.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values from an unrendered template variable.
- **Global `* {outline:none; border:none}`** removes focus indicators.
- **Web fonts:** `TheFutureHOKA` is loaded from `hoka.com`, so fallback behavior in most clients depends on the font stack. I can't see the stack in the truncated source.

No issues found. No unrendered tokens were visible in the truncated source, apart from the empty CSS values noted above. The campaign name suggests a purchaser segment (`720ClittonPurchasers`), but I can't confirm any in-body personalization.

## 5. Compliance

- **Authentication is unverified.** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA tool says this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so it is a harness limitation, not a finding against HOKA. SPF, DKIM and DMARC remain unconfirmed.
- **Unsubscribe link is present** and resolves to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`. Whether it actually works is unconfirmed because of the 403.
- **Not verifiable from the data:** the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address in the footer (the source is truncated).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTMs are present and consistent** on the links I decoded: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261125_AM_STO_LastChanceClifton10_Launch_A60Day_720ClittonPurchasers_365SaleTag`.
- **The campaign string has two problems.**
  - It is long and appears to contain a typo (`ClittonPurchasers`).
  - It exposes internal segment and targeting naming (`A60Day`, `720…Purchasers`) in a customer-visible URL.
- **The unsubscribe URL also carries UTMs,** which is unnecessary.
- **Landing-page alignment is not confirmed.** The one link I could inspect is a returns page, not the Clifton 10 product page, and the product CTAs weren't probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracked links and the unsubscribe link with a browser-like user agent. If the 403s persist, fix the tracker or WAF rule. The unsubscribe link must work.
2. Add a plain-text alternative. Cordial supports auto-generation.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the hero, subcopy, CTA and secondary images (offer, product name, CTA label). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels. Consider live-text or bulletproof HTML buttons for the CTA.
4. Fill in `<title>`, remove the duplicate viewport meta, and delete or fix the empty `padding-top:px` and `border-top:px` rules.
5. Remove or scope the `outline:none` reset.
6. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers from the raw message headers, not the relay.
7. Verify the Clifton 10 and Bondi 9 CTA destinations, which the probe skipped, and check that they load.
8. Shorten and sanitize `utm_campaign`, and drop UTMs from the unsubscribe link.
