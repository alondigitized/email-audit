# Member Month is Here! Don't Miss Out
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- My name is right there at the top — "Hi, Walker" — and I can see my points balance sitting underneath it. After a string of Skechers emails this past week that all started blurring together, that greeting earns a few extra seconds. The 2X Points through June 30 is the freshest thing in this send; everything else below it — the Spring into Summer hero, the BOGO 50% off or 20% off 1 pair — I've been looking at since at least May 26. At this point that banner is wallpaper.
- The layout moves clean enough on a phone: loyalty hook up top, sale offer below, then product sections. But the product sections lead with women's shoes and "SHOP WOMEN'S" as the first big button. I have to scroll past that to find the men's dark casual sneakers. And nothing in this email tells me whether those shoes have cushioning, arch support, or a slip-on option — the stuff I'd actually click for.
- The 2X Points deadline (end of June) is real and would pull me back to it before the month's out. But I'm not clicking today, and I'm definitely not forwarding it.

## What's working

- **Personalized name + points balance at the top** — "Hi, Walker" with my balance showing makes me feel like a known customer, not a cold prospect.
- **2X Points is genuinely new** — After a week of recycled BOGO copy, Member Appreciation Month with double points through 6/30 is an actual new reason to buy this month.
- **Clean render, no broken images** — Everything loaded, nothing overlapped, easy to scan even on a small screen.
- **Men's section does appear** — Dark low-profile casual shoes in the Men's block look like they could be everyday walkers; at least my category is in here.
- **Offer structure is readable** — 50% off / 20% off laid out in large type, no guessing required.

## What's weak

- **Women's shoes lead the whole email** — Hero image is a woman in a flowing summer dress; first product CTA is "SHOP WOMEN'S." I'm a 62-year-old guy; this tells me the template wasn't built with me in mind.
- **Inbox preview is broken JSON** — Before I even opened this, my inbox showed raw schema markup code instead of real copy. That almost cost them the open entirely.
- **Same BOGO, same language, again** — This deal has been in every Skechers email since May 26. At this point it feels like a permanent fixture, not urgency.
- **Zero comfort-feature copy** — Arch support, memory foam, cushioning, slip-on, wide widths — not one word. Shoes are presented as fashion items, not functional footwear.
- **CTA overload** — LOG IN, JOIN NOW, SHOP TODAY, SHOP WOMEN'S, SHOP MEN'S, SHOP KIDS', LET'S GET TEXTING, DOWNLOAD THE APP. Too many asks at once; I lose the thread.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader — this is the single most urgent fix.** The inbox is showing raw JSON-LD schema markup. One line of human copy here — anything — would be better than what's running now.
- 2. **Lead the product section with men's comfort shoes**, not a woman in a sundress. If the system knows my gender and age, use that data above the fold.
- 3. **Name a comfort feature in the men's section** — even "memory foam insole" or "hands-free slip-on available" is enough to make me tap through.
- 4. **Put a hard deadline on the BOGO** — "Valid through June 7" converts better than "SHOP TODAY" running for two-plus weeks straight.
- 5. **Collapse to two CTAs** — primary: SHOP MEN'S; secondary: EARN 2X POINTS. Everything else can move to the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, 2X Points All Month — Plus BOGO 50% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Member Month: Double Points Thru June 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn double points on every online purchase through June 30 — no code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your reward: 2X points this month + buy one, get one 50% off.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Member Month is Here! Don't Miss Out"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken-experience bug (empty personalization token in greeting) plus a systemic HTTP image-serving issue that risks blocked assets across all major email clients. No structural rendering failures detected.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination links confirmed.** 26 click-redirect links through `click.emails.skechers.com` were not probed (expected for tracking domains).

**Third-party pixel exposure:** Three distinct tracking pixels fire on open:
- `beacon.krxd.net` (2× user-match + 1× ad impression) — Salesforce/Krux DMP sync, exposes `partner_uid=121173391` (appears to be a raw subscriber ID)
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net` — inbox deliverability monitoring (standard)
- `ink1000.com/o.gif` — secondary open tracker, served over **HTTP** (see §3)

The Krux `partner_uid` value in the ad impression pixel (`campaignid=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_LOYL_EN_06012026`) is plaintext and subscriber-linkable — low severity but worth noting for privacy hygiene.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**FAIL — HTTP image sources:** 13 images are served over `http://` instead of `https://`. Affected domains:
- `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/...` (logo, app store badges, social icons, footer icons)
- `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` (open tracker)

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy/block mixed-content HTTP images. These assets will render as broken images or be silently suppressed in a significant share of the subscriber base.

**Missing alt text:** Two images have no `alt` attribute:
- `o.gif` (open tracker — acceptable, but flagged)
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — unknown content image; alt text required for screen readers and image-blocked rendering

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is set globally on `*`. This suppresses iOS auto-size entirely and can make small text unreadable on older iPhones without a compensating `font-size` floor.

**Duplicate media query blocks:** The responsive styles for `max-width: 375px` and `max-width: 640px` are fully duplicated — identical rule sets defined twice. No rendering impact, but increases payload and maintenance risk.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**FAIL — Empty greeting:** The automated QA confirms an unrendered merge token: the greeting resolves to `"Hi ,"` with a blank name field. The token (likely `%%First_Name%%` or equivalent SFMC AMPscript) was not populated, indicating either:
- No fallback default defined (e.g., `DEFAULT("Member")`)
- The contact record has a null/empty `First_Name` value and no conditional fallback block

This is a **broken experience** — visible to any subscriber with a missing first name in the send list.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe:** Unable to verify from the truncated HTML whether a physical mailing address and functional unsubscribe link are present in the footer. The HTML is cut before the footer. Assumed present based on Skechers' established send history, but should be confirmed against the full source.

**Authentication headers:** Not available in the provided data (headers were not included). DKIM/SPF/DMARC status for `emails.skechers.com` cannot be assessed from HTML source alone.

**Sender domain:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` with opens/clicks through `click.emails.skechers.com` and images through `image.emails.skechers.com` — subdomain structure is consistent and expected for an ESP-managed send.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Campaign ID is consistent** across pixels: `PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_LOYL_EN_06012026` appears in both the Krux ad impression pixel and the ink1000 open tracker — campaign naming is coherent.

**UTM parameters on destination links** cannot be verified without probing the 26 click-redirect URLs. If the ESP wraps links without appending UTMs before the redirect, GA4/site analytics will lose email attribution. This should be confirmed by spot-checking one unwrapped destination URL from `click.emails.skechers.com`.

---

| Priority | Issue | Fix |
