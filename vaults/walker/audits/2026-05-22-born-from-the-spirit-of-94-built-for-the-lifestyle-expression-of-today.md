# Born from the spirit of '94, built for the lifestyle expression of today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Here we are again — another adidas email, and I'm counting eight or nine from them in the last week or so. This one reaches back to the 1994 World Cup, and I'll be honest, that summer does mean something to me — I was 30, working, watching games at my buddy's place. For a half second, the "From '94 to '26" headline got a reaction out of me. Then I realized this is a pitch for officially licensed soccer collection pieces, not anything that goes on my feet. I don't follow soccer. I don't collect jerseys.
- The sweepstakes at the top — win World Cup tickets — is an interesting grab. But I squinted at the fine print and saw it's for a match in Los Angeles on July 2. I'm not going to Los Angeles. And that's before I even get to the restrictions text at the bottom.
- I'd mark this read without clicking. Wouldn't forward it either. My friends who follow soccer might appreciate it, but none of us are in the market for a licensed apparel collection.

## What's working

- The sweepstakes banner at the very top is high-contrast and attention-grabbing — "WIN FIFA WORLD CUP 26™ TICKETS" in yellow and black gets my eye before anything else
- "From '94 to '26" is genuinely clever — connects two World Cup moments for people who actually remember that summer
- The email renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, nothing that looks busted on my phone
- At least they're acknowledging I'm a member with the sweepstakes entry — that's more than some of their recent sends have done

## What's weak

- This is an apparel email, not a shoe email — wrong product for me, full stop
- "Lifestyle expression" in the subject line is marketing-speak I tune out immediately; I don't talk that way and neither does anyone I know
- The CTA is plain text — "Shop online or at your local adidas store" — no button, no urgency, I'm not clicking plain text at the bottom of a scroll
- This is email eight-ish in roughly a week from adidas; I'm starting to delete before reading just from the sender name
- Whatever leaked into the top of the email before the logo — that text at the very top doesn't belong there and looks like an unintended artifact

## Recommendations

- 1. **Send me a shoe email.** Any email about Cloudfoam, Lite Racer, or men's casual walkers would get a real look. This is the right brand, wrong product, every single time.
- 2. Turn the CTA into an actual button — "Shop the US94 Collection" in a high-contrast button is all it takes, but make it tappable on a phone.
- 3. Throttle the send frequency — I've gotten more adidas emails this week than any other brand combined. It's training me to ignore the sender name.
- 4. The sweepstakes restriction copy at the bottom (Los Angeles, July 2 only) should come up near the banner. Don't bury the geography limitation in fine print after you've already got my attention.
- 5. If you're going for Boomer nostalgia, go all the way — we were adults in '94, not kids. Lead with "You watched it live" or something that actually speaks to that age bracket.
