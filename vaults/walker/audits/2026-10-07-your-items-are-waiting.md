- The navy headline and big dark buttons are easy to read on my phone, and the tap targets are large.
- The Women / Men / Kids list is simple to navigate, and I like having Men one tap away.

## What's weak

- The hero is a pink floral women's sneaker I never added, so "Your cart is still waiting" feels wrong for me.
- This is the same pink sneaker from the 10/5 and 10/6 sends, with only the headline changed.
- Only one of the six grid shoes is a men's shoe. Two are boys' sneakers, and the "love" claim doesn't land.

- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and I'd have to squint to read it.
