- Four real shoes with model numbers at the bottom, same comfort-brand grid style I liked on 9/11.
- "Free gift with $200+ purchase" and "Member Days perks" are at least concrete, dated offers (Sept 17-23).
- No young-runner-at-dawn marathon imagery — this is at least a walking/casual product, not performance gear.

## What's weak

- Hero image is a jacket on a young woman — not a shoe, not aimed at me, wastes the first screen.
- Have to scroll through jacket, tote bag, and a moody flatlay before hitting actual sneakers.
- The four shoes at the bottom have no price, no "shop this" link, no fit/comfort callouts — just a name under a photo.
- "Member Days," "Member Product," "Member gift" — I'm not sure if I'm even a member, and nothing here tells me my status or points.
- Everything requires "log in" first — that's friction before I even see if there's a deal for me.

- 2. Put prices and a "Shop" link directly under each of the four shoes — right now they're just labeled, not sold.
- 3. Tell me if I'm a member or not, right up top — don't make me guess before I click "Log in."
- 4. Cut the tote-bag detour entirely for a guy like me; a free tote isn't why I open a shoe email.
