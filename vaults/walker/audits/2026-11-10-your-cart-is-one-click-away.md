# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- It's the skull sneaker again. I still haven't put a thing in a cart, and this is another "cart" nag. The black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" with the blue skull is the same shoe I've now been shown on 11/05, 11/08 and in yesterday's and today's "Your cart expires soon!" sends. I never looked at it. It looks like a women's-style fashion sneaker, so I don't know why Skechers keeps pinning it to me.
- The better part is lower down. The 20% off for Skechers Plus members is a real offer, and a few of the slip-ins in the grid are the kind of shoe I'd wear. But I have to scroll past a giant hero for a cart I don't have to get there. Compare that with "Comfort is the gift that keeps on giving" on 11/09, which actually spoke to me. This one is a reminder to a man with an empty cart.
- I wouldn't click the big button. I might scroll to the 20% off, and I'd probably mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The 20% off for Plus members is a clear number, and I like that it's a real discount.
- The "Valid thru 11/10" deadline is honest, and the fine print spells out what's excluded.
- The slip-in styles in the grid, like the Orson waterproof boots, are close to what I shop for.
- The navy "Complete Your Order" button is big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The Men / Women / Kids list is large, plain text I can read without zooming.

## What's weak

- The cart claim is false. I haven't added anything, and "Your cart is ready when you are" reads like they're guessing.
- The skull sneaker takes up the whole top of the screen. It isn't my style and I never looked at it.
- The "more great options" grid is mostly women's shoes. Three of the six are women's styles, and none of the product text says cushioning or arch support.
- The 20% off is buried below the fold. It should be at the top, and it ends the same day, 11/10, which makes it feel last-minute.
- The product captions are tiny and hard to read on my phone.
- There's no price on any shoe, so I can't do my own value math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart emails to people with no cart. If there's no cart, send a men's comfort email instead.
- 2. Put the 20% Plus offer at the top, above the shoe.
- 3. Show men's slip-ins and walking shoes, with the price and a short note like "arch support" or "cushioned."
- 4. Use larger caption text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off slip-ins for Plus members, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, comfort shoes you can slip right on`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% on regular-priced styles through tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned, arch-supported men's slip-ins, no bending over.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Cart-abandonment email sent through Attentive (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com`). QA found a failing unsubscribe link (HTTP 400), no plain-text part, and 12 images loaded over `http://`. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and headers couldn't be fully inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m60&externalId=2U3eG&…`. The `attn_macro` value base64-decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. A 400 may be a bot-probe artifact from a missing user-agent, or a token that is single-use or bound to the recipient. Verify manually in a real client.
- **429 responses on the primary CTA and a secondary link.** These were "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." (both `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`). The 429 is rate limiting by the Attentive redirector, not a confirmed broken link, so this is unverified rather than a defect.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 44 links were probed and 32 were skipped as footer or social links, so those are unvalidated.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt.** `https://skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` is flagged as missing alt text. This is minor, but add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

## 5. Compliance
- **Unsubscribe link fails (400).** This is the main compliance risk, because CAN-SPAM requires a working opt-out mechanism. Confirm it resolves for a real recipient.
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". A missing `text/plain` part is a deliverability and spam-scoring negative. It may also be an artifact of the AgentMail relay capture.
- **Authentication headers unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is undetermined. That is a limitation of the relay, not a finding against the sender. The From domain is `msgs.skechers.com`.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source.** I couldn't check the physical postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, or the footer text.

- The 429s prevented resolution of the "Complete Your Order" cart link. Its destination, cart-restore behavior and UTM carry-through are unconfirmed.
