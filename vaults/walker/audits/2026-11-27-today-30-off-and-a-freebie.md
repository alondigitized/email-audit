# 🖤 TODAY! 30% OFF *AND* A FREEBIE!! 🖤
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count of how many of these I've gotten since the 20th. Same pink banner, same giant "30% OFF*", and now a countdown clock and "79,662 people are already shopping." This one has a freebie sherpa weekender bag, a free tote, and a $25 bonus card. That's a lot of noise for a man who just wants a comfortable shoe.
- The giant 30 is easy to read, and I do like that. But I had to scroll past the whole top half to find any shoes, and the shoes shown are a brown sneaker, a New Balance, an Adidas Samba knockoff and a loafer. None of them is labeled cushioned, arch-supported or slip-on. The "Shop by size" row with "Wide Width" is the first thing that sounds like it's meant for me. A duffel bag isn't going to make my feet feel any better.
- Would I click? Maybe once, to see if the wide-width shoes are any good. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and go on with my day.

## What's working

- The big "30% OFF*" is readable on my phone without zooming.
- The "CLAIM 30% FOLLOW" button is clear and black on pink, and the code BFDEALS is right there.
- "Shop Wide Width" and the size buttons are big, so I can tap them easily.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup and Afterpay are in one tidy row.
- The countdown timer gives me a real deadline, and Black Friday is the right season for it.

## What's weak

- The subject says "30% off and a freebie," but the freebie needs a $99 purchase, and the tote needs $75 in stores only. That's not a freebie, that's a minimum spend.
- The fine print takes up half the email and is tiny, so I'd need to zoom in to read it.
- Nothing speaks to comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-on. It's all "new arrivals" and fashion.
- The top banner scrolls "THE SEASON'S BIGGEST DEALS ARE ON!" and cuts off mid-word.
- It's at least the tenth DSW email in the pile, with the same creative every time.
- The shoe prices aren't shown, only "up to 25% off" labels, so I can't do my price-versus-quality math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real comfort shoe, with price and sale price, right under the 30% hero, for example a cushioned walking shoe from a name I know.
- 2. Make the freebie conditions clear in one line near the button, not in the fine print.
- 3. Send fewer emails. The ones from the 25th and 26th were nearly identical.
- 4. Add a "Comfort & Walking" shop link next to Men and Women.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: a raw `%%gender%%` merge token and a click-tracked "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link that returned HTTP 400. The visible HTML source also has accessibility and markup gaps. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probable broken CTA (400).** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link (`https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/mt9YoeI4CAzQO0LTngXSLA~~/AAQRxRA~/dEBzciAiW8…`) returned 400. The QA output shows the link text only inside the parenthetical, with an empty label before it.
  - Caveat: this is a Braze/SendGrid-style `ablink` redirect, and a probe can get a 400 for reasons that don't affect real recipients, such as a bot or missing-header rejection. Re-test with a real client before treating it as a live break.
  - If real, the opt-in CTA and its 15% SMS incentive are non-functional.
- Only 12 of 80 links were probed (68 skipped by priority ranking), so link health for the rest is unknown.
- All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so final destinations can't be seen from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, per QA):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/Ab4lHL12f41-zrzMRwgANg~~/AAQRxRA~/MxXrpVqGxZNYHsgcrw1QUzYmKJ…` is a click-tracked image with no alt. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif` is the open-tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""` explicitly. This is low severity.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Ineffective meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Plain-text part.** 29,185 of 36,207 characters (about 81%) are URLs, which is over the 70% warning threshold. The tracking-wrapped links bloat the text alternative, and that can hurt spam scoring and readability.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL: raw token `%%gender%%` rendered unresolved.** The token was not substituted, so the personalization or segmentation field failed or was missing for this recipient.
  - Depending on where it sits, it could show in visible copy, a URL or an attribute.
  - The excerpt doesn't show the location. Search the full source for `%%gender%%`.
  - No fallback or default value was applied.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication headers: unverifiable.** `Authentication-Results` was not found because the message came through the AgentMail relay. This is a limitation of the capture path, not evidence of a sender failure. SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `e.dsw.com` need a direct header check.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address.** The QA data says an unsubscribe link was always probed, and no failure was reported for it. The truncated HTML doesn't let me confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers or the postal address.
- No other issues confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters can be evaluated because every link is wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects and the final URLs were not captured in the QA data.
- The SMS opt-in CTA's destination is unreachable (see section 2), so landing alignment for that offer can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the SMS opt-in link with a real client. If it still returns 400, fix the Braze link or its destination, since it carries the 15% incentive.
2. Find where `%%gender%%` sits in the template and add a default fallback. Check the sender's Braze/ESP personalization logic for why it was not resolved.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to the click-tracked image and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Fix the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
6. Shorten the plain-text part by dropping tracking URLs from non-essential text.
7. Verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers against the raw message, since the relay hid them. Capture the full HTML and resolved redirect chains so UTM and landing-page checks can run.
