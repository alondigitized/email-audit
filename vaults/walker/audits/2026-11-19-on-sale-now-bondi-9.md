# On sale now: Bondi 9
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a HOKA email with my shoe on top. The Bondi 9 is the cushioned walker I actually buy, and the navy knit with the cream sole fills the top of the screen. "This is big" is easy to read on my phone. I'll admit I didn't expect to see the Bondi after the trail and recovery emails earlier this week, like the 11/18 trail gift guide.
- The sale itself is buried. The only hint is "on sale now," and the real number ("up to 20% off") is in tiny print at the very bottom. I had to hunt for it. Then the middle goes off on young runners and a text-signup pitch for free shipping that only works on a *full-price* order. That undercuts the sale I just read about.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because it's the Bondi and there's a "Shop Sale" button. I'd check the price before getting excited. I wouldn't forward it. It's the ninth HOKA email since the 9th, and I'm tired of the pace.

## What's working

- The Bondi 9 is the hero, and I can see the cushioned sole up close.
- "This is big" is huge type and readable without zooming.
- "Shop Sale" is a clear white pill button on black, easy to tap with my thumb.
- The line about "new shades only at HOKA.com" gives me a reason to look.
- Black and white with a blue border looks clean and matches HOKA's site.

## What's weak

- The discount isn't in the body. I only found "up to 20% off" in the tiny footnote after scrolling past everything.
- The "Free Expedited Shipping**" headline is misleading. It needs a text signup and applies only to a first full-price order, so it contradicts the sale.
- The blurry sprinting runners aren't me. I walk on pavement and at the mall.
- The footer links ("Locate a Store," "Unlock Free Shipping," "FLY for 30 Guarantee") are in a thin serif font that doesn't match the rest. They look unfinished and are small to tap.
- The footnote text is tiny, with gray-on-blue address lines I can't read.

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Bondi 9, up to 20% off." Say it before the button, not in the footnote.
- 2. Drop the text-signup shipping block, or move it below the sale and say plainly what it is. It fights the sale it follows.
- 3. Replace the runners with someone walking in Bondis, ideally an older man, and add one line about cushioning or arch support.
- 4. Fix the footer links so they use the same font and bigger tap areas.
- 5. Make the legal text larger and higher contrast.

- **403 on Unsubscribe.** The wrapper `…/1c800103?jwtH=…` resolves to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_…`. This is the same bot-block pattern, but it is a compliance-critical link and should be verified manually.
