# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the same cart nag I got on 10/17, with the same pink flower sneaker, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. "We noticed you noticing us" is a little creepy. The D'Lites "Spring Bloom" is a women's shoe with embroidered flowers, and nothing about it is for me. The subject line is word for word the "Your Items Are Waiting!" email from 10/17.
- The six shoes under "more great options we think you'll love" are mostly women's and boys' styles. There's one men's waterproof John Deere slip-in, which is the only shoe that looks like it was picked for a 62-year-old guy. The 20% off for members is the one real thing here, and it's far down the page. It's also fine print, because I'd have to be logged in to a Skechers Plus account to get it.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read. If anything I'd take a look at that John Deere boot on my own, but I'd have to hunt for it, and nothing here points me to it.

## What's working

- The 20% off banner has big, easy-to-read numbers and a clear "Shop Now." It's valid through 10/21, so the deadline is real.
- The text is large. The headline, the buttons and the category list (Women, Men, Kids) are all easy to read and tap on my phone.
- It's a plain, single-column layout, and the SKECHERS logo at the top tells me who it's from.
- The John Deere slip-in boot is a real fit for me: waterproof, relaxed fit, slip-in.
- The small print is spelled out at the bottom, so nothing is hidden. I'd just rather it were near the offer.

## What's weak

- The pink women's flower sneaker is front and center. I never added it to a cart, so the "your cart is still waiting" line is just wrong.
- The recommendations are mostly women's and boys' shoes, including two boy's Garage Sneakers. Only one of the six is for a man.
- The 20% off is buried below the product grid and the black "Shop All Best Sellers" bar. The hero should have led with it.
- "Complete Your Order" is a pushy button when there's no order to complete.
- The "We noticed you noticing us" line feels like being watched. This is at least the third cart nag in four days.
- The offer requires a Skechers Plus login, which the email doesn't make clear up top, and it excludes sale items.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send a cart email unless there's actually a cart. If the send is a general promo, lead with the member 20% off at the top and say so honestly.
- 2. Match the shoes to the person. Show men's slip-ins and walking shoes for someone who has browsed them, with the John Deere boot or an Arch Fit slip-in as the hero.
- 3. Move the 20% off banner above the product grid, and put "Members, log in to save" next to it.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Four nags in a week makes me trust the brand less.
- 5. Drop the creepy copy. Something like "Comfort picks for your feet" is fine.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 20% off comfort styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free Arch Fit, cushioned and easy on the feet. Plus members save 20% through 10/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Waterproof, relaxed-fit slip-ins for men. Log in to take 20% off.`
