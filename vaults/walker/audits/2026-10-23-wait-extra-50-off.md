# Wait...Extra 50% Off?! 🏃‍➡️🏃‍➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again. This is the third offer change in a week: the Buy 2/3/4 ladders on 10/18 and 10/19, then "$30 Off $100" on 10/22, and now "Extra 50% Off!" It's easy to read. Big black type on pink, one button, done in two seconds. But it's the same white clog on a "SALE" backdrop, and "Select Styles" tells me nothing.
- Here's what bugs me. "Extra" 50% off on top of what? "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the button. The real terms are in tiny print at the bottom: it's in-cart, select styles only, and it can't be combined with any other offer. That cancels the $30 off I got yesterday. I don't trust a headline that needs that much footnote.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of curiosity, to see whether any cushioned slip-on is in the sale. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The headline "Extra 50% Off!" is huge and clear, and I read it without squinting.
- The black "Shop the Sale" button is big enough for my thumb.
- The deadline is stated at the bottom: Sunday, October 25th at 11:59pm MT. The "before the weekend ends" line gives me a rough idea of the timing too.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or crowded text.
- The Crocs name is familiar, and it looks like the other Crocs emails I get.

## What's weak

- "Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply" make me doubt the 50%. I can't tell what's actually included.
- The picture is a plain white clog. It shows nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-on ease, which is what I shop for.
- There's no price, no sale example and no product I can tap, only a generic sale button.
- The fine print at the bottom is small, and it's where the "no stacking" rule is buried.
- I got a different offer yesterday and a different one the day before. It feels like they're throwing deals at me to see what sticks.
- The cart-discount wording is vague, and the "Refer a Friend" and app blocks pull my eye away from the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Say what the 50% applies to, and show two or three actual products with sale prices. A comfort clog with "was $50, now $25" would sell me.
- 2. Move the key terms (in-cart discount, no stacking, end date) up near the button, in text I can read.
- 3. Use a specific button label like "Shop Men's Clogs on Sale".
- 4. Show a comfort product, such as a walking or slip-on style, and mention the cushioning.
- 5. Don't change the offer every day. Pick one and hold it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is generally well-built: `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, mobile media queries, an Outlook/Gmail client-fix block and a hidden preheader. The confirmed issues are 7 non-HTTPS image sources, a third-party pixel with no `alt`, and an authentication check that couldn't be run. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe block and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 20 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains (likely `click.crocs-email.com`). Destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel without `alt`:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…ef90f188`. This is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Possible identifier in the URL:** `d_dpuid` is a 40-character hex string, which looks like a hashed identifier. I can't confirm what it encodes from this data, but it deserves a privacy and consent review.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is correctly sized (1×1, `alt=""`). No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **HTTP images:** 7 images load over `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`:
  - App Store (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Google Play (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)

  These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and they sit in the footer. The `alt` text is present, so broken images still have labels. Switch the host to `https://`.
- **Ineffective meta declarations:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration is applied. The Content-Type header likely covers the charset, but fix the tags.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is the same on every send. This is a minor accessibility point.
- **Subject emoji:** `🏃‍➡️` is a ZWJ sequence from recent Unicode versions. Older clients may show it as two separate glyphs.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** the check is `[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found`. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely an artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than a sender defect, so inspect the raw headers at the receiving mailbox.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** these could not be verified because the source is truncated. I can't confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, the unsubscribe link or the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
