# Meet the trail faves
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Well, "Meet the trail faves" — right off the bat this is HOKA telling me it's a trail running email, and I don't run trails. I don't run, period, at my age — my knees filed for retirement a decade before I did. That hero shot of a fella's legs mid-stride kicking up dirt with a bright blue trail shoe is nice photography, sure, but it's the same "young person running" playbook this brand's been running for months — no Sasquatch mascot even to make it fun this time.
- The lineup underneath — Challenger 8, Mafate 5, Stinson 8, Tecton X4 — is all trail and speed shoes, carbon plates, "off-road speed machine." Not one word about walking, not one mention of comfort for a guy who wants to get the mail and mow the lawn without his feet barking at him. This is a runner's email dressed up and mailed to my inbox regardless.
- I'd scroll past this one and mark it read. Nothing here pulls me in, and I sure wouldn't forward it — unless it's to my son-in-law who actually does trail races. For me, no click, no thought about it again.

## What's working

- The layout's clean — shoes lined up in neat pairs with names and gender labels, easy enough to read even on my phone.
- "Free Expedited Shipping" section at the bottom stands out with its own black block, so I did notice the offer even if it's not for me.
- No overlapping text or broken images that I can see — everything rendered fine.
- The HOKA logo up top and at the bottom is unmistakable, so I know exactly who this is from.

## What's weak

- Every single shoe is a trail runner — Challenger, Mafate, Stinson, Tecton X4 — nothing for a walker like me, so the whole email's a miss on relevance.
- Hero copy "Cushion for the trail" and "trail flying" assumes I'm out there logging miles on dirt, which I'm not.
- No price, no percent off, no sale mentioned anywhere — just "Shop Now" buttons with nothing pulling me toward buying today.
- The "Free Expedited Shipping" offer requires signing up for texts, which is a hoop I'm not jumping through for shipping I might not even use.
- Demographic-wise it's all young, fit runners in action shots — nobody who looks like they're my age or shopping for all-day comfort.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if HOKA knows I've only ever looked at walking shoes, don't send me a trail-running blast at all. That's the single biggest fix.
- 2. If they must send it to everyone, add one section for comfort/walking shoes like the Bondi or Clifton so there's something for guys like me in the mix.
- 3. Put an actual offer front and center — a percent off or a price, not just "Shop Now" with no reason to act now.
- 4. Drop the text-signup requirement for free shipping, or at least offer an email-only alternative — I'm not big on giving out my phone number for a shipping discount.
- 5. Show a person who looks like they could be my neighbor, not just twenty-somethings sprinting on trails.

# HOKA Email Technical Audit — "Meet the trail faves"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses standard ESP (Cordial) redirect/tracking links and a JWT-signed unsubscribe URL, but automated QA flagged two broken links (both returning 403) and a missing plain-text MIME part — both are functional deliverability/compliance defects, not design issues.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (403)** — "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/" CTA resolves through the Cordial redirect wrapper (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...`) to a decoded target of `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_...`, but the redirect itself returns HTTP 403. This is a broken customer-facing link, not a bad destination URL — the ESP tracking domain is rejecting the request.
- **[FAIL] Broken link (403)** — Unsubscribe link (see Compliance below) resolves through the same `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/...` redirect pattern and also 403s.
- Both failing links share the same redirect infrastructure (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...ot:...`) and JWT parameter structure (`jwtH`/`jwtP`/`jwtS`), suggesting a systemic issue with this click-tracking domain/token set for this send, rather than two isolated link errors.
- 12 of 27 total links were probed (ranked by click likelihood); 15 lower-priority footer/social links were skipped per QA methodology — no data on their status.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **14 images missing `alt` text**, including all primary product/lifestyle images (`US_20260926_CushionfortheTrail_a–f.jpg`, shoe images for Challenger8, Mafate5, Stinson8) and the footer logo (`LogoFooterPrimary.png`). Screen readers and blocked-image clients will render no fallback content for the entire visual narrative of the email.
- Two tracking pixel `<img>` elements (open-tracking beacon `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`) also lack `alt=""`, which is a minor best-practice miss (should be empty alt, not absent, to be explicitly ignored by assistive tech) but not user-impacting.
- No structural rendering failures detected in the truncated `<head>`/CSS (media queries, MSO conditional resets, font-face declarations, and mobile class overrides all present and well-formed).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No issues found. No merge tags (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, or similar) are present in the truncated source; personalization is handled server-side via the `hmail` and `mtlID` hashed parameters embedded in tracking URLs rather than visible template tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken (403)** — the required functional opt-out mechanism (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...`) is unreachable through the wrapping redirect. This is a CAN-SPAM compliance risk: the law requires a working, honored opt-out mechanism, and a 403 on the unsubscribe path means it currently cannot be honored via this link.
- No List-Unsubscribe header data was included in the provided source/QA output — cannot confirm presence of `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe). Flagging as unverifiable rather than fabricating a pass/fail.
- Sender address `info@emails.hoka.com` and footer sender identity were not fully visible in the truncated source — physical mailing address element could not be confirmed present or absent from available data.
- No authentication header data (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) was supplied in this audit payload — cannot assess.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Confirmed UTM parameters on the return/CTA link: `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260926_Trail_Cushion_STO_A60Day_720TrailPurchasers_270TrailTag_60BestSellersTag_0923BestSellersTrailOpeners` — internally consistent naming convention (channel_date_theme_audience-segment tags).
- Same UTM campaign string is consistently applied to the unsubscribe link as well (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=...`) — attaching marketing UTMs to an unsubscribe link is unusual but not harmful; flagging for awareness only, not as a defect.
- Cannot verify landing page alignment (page content/availability at `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`) since the link itself 403s before reaching the destination — this is actually the wrong URL to be the primary CTA target for a "Meet the trail faves" product email (a return-start page rather than a PDP/PLP), but confirming intent is a content/strategy question outside this technical audit's scope; only the 403 itself is in scope here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority — fix the 403s**: Investigate the Cordial click-tracking domain (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...`) for this send; both failing links share the same redirect host/JWT structure, pointing to a token-signing or redirect-service config issue rather than two unrelated bad links.
2. **Priority — unsubscribe must work**: Since CAN-SPAM requires a functioning opt-out, resolve the unsubscribe 403 before/immediately after send, or trigger a corrective follow-up if already sent.
3. Add `alt` attributes to all 14 flagged content images (concise, descriptive) and `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels.
4. Generate and attach a proper plain-text MIME part — current text version is 0 chars, which hurts deliverability scoring with several mailbox providers and breaks accessibility for text-only clients.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status separately, as this data wasn't available in the current audit payload.
