### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text (4 images):**

| Asset | URL pattern |

| Content image | `…/951dfca3-078c-47e4-a9c6-b5630a845bf2.png` |

Tracking pixels conventionally use `alt=""` (empty string); content images require descriptive alt for image-off and screen-reader rendering.

**Dark mode lock:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">` + `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light">` explicitly blocks dark mode adaptation. Apple Mail and Outlook 2021+ will still attempt forced inversion, which can produce broken contrast on text-over-image sections.

**Multiple `<style>` blocks:** Four separate `@media` blocks appear in `<head>`. Gmail clips messages exceeding 102KB and may discard non-first style blocks, breaking all responsive breakpoints in clipped renders.

**Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` is missing `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Inconsequential in practice but invalid markup.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The open-tracking pixel URL embeds `utm_campaign=SCA_20260618_new_balance_AM`. This campaign tag is dated **June 18** — if this email is a copy-forward or resend, the campaign identifier is stale and will misattribute any analytics events that flow from this send into the June 18 campaign bucket.

All 26 click-redirect URLs were blocked from QA probing, so UTM parameter presence on landing pages is unverified. Manual spot-check of at minimum the primary CTA URL is needed.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Fix `[UNIQUE]` immediately before any resend.** Identify the correct ESP merge syntax (e.g., `%%emailaddr%%`, `{{unique_id}}`) and repopulate the `d=` parameter. Open attribution for this send is currently broken.
2. **Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels** and descriptive alt text to the two UUID-named content images.
3. **Consolidate `<style>` blocks** into a single block to eliminate Gmail clipping risk for responsive breakpoints.
4. **Update `utm_campaign`** in the open-pixel URL to match the current send date/campaign ID (`SCA_20260618` is stale).
5. **Spot-check 2–3 CTA click URLs** manually to confirm UTM parameters survive the redirect chain to landing pages.
6. **Test dark mode** in Apple Mail and Outlook with the `color-scheme: light` lock in place; add targeted dark-mode overrides (`@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`) or remove the lock if inversion artifacts appear.
