# Get $15 OFF in the app!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I opened this one because of the "last call (for real)" line. After a dozen "last chances" since the 8th, I wanted to see if they were joking. They weren't. It's the same CIDER code and the same $15/$20/$60 ladder I got on the 12th in "Your $15 Off Offer Is Ending Soon" and "Go WILD: $15 off". Now the deal only works in the app.
- The big green block is easy to read, even on my phone without squinting. The shoes underneath are all women's: a fuzzy bootie, a wedge sneaker, a Mary Jane flat, a snake-print sandal. Not one slip-on walking shoe for a man. I wouldn't forward this to anybody. I'd probably skip it, or tap "Shop Men" only if I needed a pair anyway. I'm not downloading an app for $15.

## What's working

- The green "In-App Exclusive" block is huge. I can read $15 off $49, $20 off $99 and $60 off $199 at a glance.
- The countdown clock (17:03:53) tells me the deadline without any guessing.
- The code CIDER is printed right there, so I'm not hunting for it.
- "Shop by Size" has big, readable numbers, and "Shop Wide Width" is a real plus for my feet.
- The black nav bar with Women, Men, Kids, Brands and Clearance is simple, and the DSW logo up top is a trusted look.

## What's weak

- All six featured shoes are women's styles. Nothing here says comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-on to me.
- The deal only works in the app. That's a hurdle for a guy who shops on his phone browser and likes things simple.
- "Last call (for real)" has lost its meaning. I've heard "final call" and "ends tonight" from DSW all week, and the offer kept coming back.
- The "Shop Women / Men / Kids / Everything" links under the hero are small and crowded, and they're hard to tap with my thumbs.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny gray text on black. The asterisk on $60 off $199 and the long brand-exclusion list make me doubt how good the deal is.
- The "Up to 30% off select adidas" banner at the top is a second offer, and it competes with the main one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me the men's version. Lead with a slip-on or a cushioned walking shoe (Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance) and say "easy to slip on, all-day cushion." Show real men's styles instead of women's boots.
- 2. Let me use the offer on the website, or at least put a plain "Shop Men's Comfort" button next to "Download the App."
- 3. Cut the number of "last chance" emails. If a deadline keeps moving, I stop believing it.
- 4. Make the shop links real buttons with big tap targets, and move the fine print behind a short "See details" link.

# Technical Audit: DSW, "Get $15 OFF in the app!"

## 1. Technical Summary
One confirmed broken CTA, the SMS opt-in tracked link, which returns HTTP 400. The plain-text part is about 85% URLs, and the head has several ineffective or accessibility-hostile meta tags. The HTML source was truncated and only 12 of 74 links were probed, so findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/UlmNxsUSWlucfOU1N3IhNQ~~/AAQRxRA~/e9Y2kxEr…hw~~`
  - The link is a very long tracked redirect of roughly 700 characters. A 400 from the tracking domain means the click resolves to an error rather than the opt-in page.
  - I couldn't tell whether this is a bot-specific rejection or a real failure for subscribers. Replay the link from a real client to confirm.
- **Plain-text ratio:** 26,290 of 30,950 characters (about 85%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The cause is long link-wrapped URLs, which raises spam-filter risk for the text/plain part.
- **Probe coverage:** 62 of 74 links were skipped as footer, utility or social links. Their health is unverified.
- **Extra tracker:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261013_P261396_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…` is a second-party tracking pixel carrying a numeric user ID. It sits alongside the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/geS9xvWgcJRo3b_UdF86ug~~/AAQRxRA~/6YNKJI7e…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/…/o.gif`
  - Both look like tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so neither takes effect. The charset is not declared in the head.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Custom fonts:** the `@font-face` fonts load from `braze-images.com`. Web fonts are not supported in Gmail, so the fallback stack matters. I couldn't confirm it because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible portion of the source. The remainder was truncated and is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues confirmed. The QA probe always includes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. Raw headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and the physical-address footer were not supplied or fell in the truncated section, so they can't be verified here. Sender (`e.dsw.com`), click domain (`ablink.dsw.com`) and tracker (`mi.dsw.com`) are all subdomains of `dsw.com`, which is consistent for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All probed links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be seen in the source. UTM presence and consistency are unverified.
- **Offer mismatch:** the subject promises "$15 OFF in the app", while the broken CTA reads "Get 15% Off" via text opt-in. These are two different offers and mechanisms. The visible source doesn't show where the $15-in-app CTA lands, so whether it's an app deep link, an App Store link or a web fallback is unconfirmed.
- The SMS CTA's 400 error means that landing path is broken.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or replace the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/UlmNxsUS…`). Test it in a real browser and on mobile, then re-probe.
2. Add `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels. Consider consolidating `mi.dsw.com/…/o.gif` and the Braze `q/` pixel into one.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Fix the head meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Ship a hand-authored text/plain part, or shorten the tracked URLs, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
7. Re-run the audit with full HTML and headers to cover `List-Unsubscribe`, authentication results, UTM parameters and the 62 skipped links.
