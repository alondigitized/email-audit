# Buy one, get one 50% off shoes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and it's the third BOGO email in two days. The "Stock up!" one and "BOGO 50% off shoes is on!" both landed yesterday, so this one reads like a rerun. The green banner says "BOGO 50% OFF*" and I got it in two seconds. Buy one pair, get half off a second. After last week's CIDER $15 countdowns, I'm glad there's no timer bar.
- Then it turns into a boot parade for women. Riding boots, leopard-print boots, dress booties, and every model is a young woman. The one thing near my world is a single "Cozy Comfort" UGG boot. I saw nothing on cushioning, arch support or slip-on ease. The "Shop Men" link is only a small underlined line under the banner.
- I'd skim it and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it to a friend. The BOGO is real, but I'd have to dig for the men's section.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% banner is big, green, and easy to read on my phone.
- The nav bar (Women, Men, Kids, Brands, Clearance) is simple and gives me an easy way in.
- "Shop by Size" with big tappable numbers, plus "Shop Wide Width," is useful for my feet.
- "Boots starting at $49.99" gives me a price I can judge.
- The DSW logo and the green match what I see on their site, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- Every model and nearly every product is women's. I'm a 62-year-old man, and nothing here was for me.
- The "Shop Men" link is small underlined text under the banner, hard to tap with my thumb.
- It's the third BOGO send in two days, so it feels like clutter.
- The copy is cutesy ("ME-OWWWWW," "Sporty *AND* Stylish") and says nothing about comfort or fit.
- The fine print at the bottom is long and tiny. The asterisk on the BOGO is a little worrying, since I can't tell which shoes are excluded.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Send men like me a men's walking and comfort version, with a men's hero and a "Shop Men" button as big as "Shop Now."
- 2. Fold this into one BOGO send per week instead of three in two days.
- 3. Show comfort claims (cushioned, arch support, slip-on) and ratings on the products.
- 4. Put the key BOGO exclusions in one plain line under the banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off men's comfort shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Two pairs, half off the second: walking shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned walkers and slip-ons included. Ends Nov 15.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy one pair, get 50% off the second. Men's styles inside.`

  - A 400 on an `ablink` URL usually means a malformed or truncated token, or a redirect that rejects the probe's request. It does not necessarily mean the link is dead.
  - I can't tell which from the data. The URL printed in the report is cut off, and the "link text" field appears to be blank in the failure line.
  - Re-test it with a browser-like user agent and the full, untruncated URL before treating it as a send-blocking defect. If it also fails there, it's a broken high-value CTA.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 68 links were probed and 56 were skipped, so the rest are unverified. The report says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set, but no result for it was given.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 2 images:**
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=…` is a Movable Ink tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. This is low severity.
  - The `ablink.dsw.com/q/Cu1soNuFWcg9wo-ZCq4ggA~~/…` image, which is likely the Braze open pixel or a wrapped image, also has no alt. If it is a content image, it needs descriptive alt text. If it is the open pixel, `alt=""` is enough.
- **Charset and compatibility meta tags are malformed:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration is valid. Rendering clients that rely on the in-document charset instead of the HTTP `Content-Type` could mis-decode non-ASCII characters. Fix with `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
- **Custom web fonts:** these are `@font-face` rules from `braze-images.com` (woff/ttf). Clients that don't support them fall back, and I can't confirm the font stack from the truncated source.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so I can't assess table structure, body-level accessibility attributes (`role="presentation"`, `lang`) or dark-mode coverage beyond the head styles.
