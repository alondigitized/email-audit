# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, I came here to look at some Slip-ins for myself and the first thing I see is a mom and a little girl in a garden full of pink flowers. Nice enough photo, but that ain't me. Right now Skechers is running a Mother's Day push hard — the whole screen is basically a gift shop for someone else. I can see there's a BOGO deal up top, so the price is right, but I've got to dig past the flowers to find anything for a guy like me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — that blue strip at the top is the first thing I see and it stops me from leaving immediately. A deal is a deal, even if the page isn't aimed at me.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — big, full-width, black on white. Easy to tap on a phone. No hunting.
- **Visual clarity** — the layout is simple. Logo, search, cart, menu. Nothing confusing for someone like me who just wants to get in and find shoes.
- **25% Off Apparel secondary strip** — two offers above the hero means I know there's something going on price-wise today.
- **"Recently Viewed" label at the bottom edge** — just barely visible, but that's actually useful for a returning shopper like me who browsed last week and wants to pick up where I left off.

## What's weak

- **"The Mother's Day Shop" hero** — I'm a 62-year-old man. A mother-daughter lifestyle shot in a flower garden with pink shoes is not speaking to me. I'm not even sure if this page has men's shoes right now.
- **No Men's navigation shortcut** — I can see a hamburger menu but there's no quick-tap Men's or Slip-ins category visible. I have to go dig.
- **No Skechers Plus / loyalty callout** — I'm a member and I get nothing from this screen acknowledging that. No points balance, no member price badge, nothing.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — shop what, exactly? A Mother's Day gift? My own shoes? It doesn't tell me where it goes.
- **Zero trust signals visible** — no star ratings, no "Free shipping on orders over $X," no returns mention. Nothing that would give me confidence before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Men's" or "Shop Slip-ins" strip directly under the Mother's Day hero** — even a simple secondary banner so guys like me know we haven't stumbled onto the wrong site.
- **Swap the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA for something specific** — "Shop Mother's Day Gifts" or at least tell me where it goes. Or put two buttons: one for gifts, one for Men's.
- **Show my Skechers Plus points balance or a member-badge** in the top nav bar — one line of text would do it. Right now logging in means nothing visible to me.
- **Put the free-shipping threshold in the top banner alongside the BOGO** — something like "BOGO 50% OFF + Free Shipping on $50+" would give me two reasons to stay instead of one.
