## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code is generally sound: it declares `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, a hidden preheader, and responsive `@media (max-width: 639px)` rules. The confirmed issues are 7 `http://` images, a third-party pixel with no `alt`, and two malformed `<meta>` tags. Authentication status, the unsubscribe mechanism and UTM parameters can't be confirmed from the truncated source and QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** 25 tracking links on the Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) click domain `click.crocs-email.com` were skipped by the QA probe ("Tracking/click-redirect domains"). Destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unknown.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) open/ID-sync beacon. It sits alongside the SFMC open pixel (`click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10212&bmt=0`). The two work independently, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection or image blocking will suppress both, so open data from either is unreliable.
- **Mixed-protocol assets.** The 7 images below are served over plain `http://` (see section 3). This is a link-hygiene issue as well as a rendering one.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources (7 images).** All are on `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - `9c36c0ef-…png` ("Download on the App Store")
  - `13246762-…png` ("Get It On Google Play")
  - `c4df71ec-…png` ("Find Your Store")
  - `e18949ff-…png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-…png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-…png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-…png` (YouTube)

  Some clients and proxies block or rewrite non-HTTPS images, and Gmail proxying may show mixed-content warnings. In the footer, these images carry the app-download, store-locator and social links.
- **Missing alt text.** The demdex tracking `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. It is a pixel, so the impact is low, but it should carry `alt=""`. The SFMC open pixel already has `alt=""`.
- **Ineffective meta declarations.** Two tags in the head are written as `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />`. Neither has `http-equiv`, so neither works as a declaration. The charset one is the more significant. Without `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `<meta charset>`, the email relies on the HTTP/MIME charset. That matters because the preheader padding uses the Unicode characters ͏ (U+034F), ‌ (U+200C) and soft hyphens (U+00AD).
- **Dark-mode support not seen.** No `color-scheme` meta or `prefers-color-scheme` rules appear in the visible head. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Web fonts.** `TT Crocs` is loaded via `@font-face` from `media.crocs.com`, and `font-display: swap` is set. Most clients that block web fonts will fall back, and whether the fallback stack is defined can't be seen in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The rest of the body is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** The QA flagged `Authentication-Results header not found: Expected via AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM status unknown`. This is a limitation of the capture path, not a confirmed failure. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `crocs-email.com` should be checked from the raw message.
- **Unsubscribe and address not confirmed.** The visible source doesn't show the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address or the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. These are not flagged as missing, since the content is truncated, but they need checking against the full message.
- **From address.** `email@crocs-email.com` is a dedicated sending domain, and the click, image and tracking hosts all share it, so the domain alignment is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 25 tracked links are SFMC redirects that were skipped, and no destination URLs or `utm_*` parameters are visible in the truncated source. Nothing is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all 7 footer images over `https://` (`image.crocs-email.com`). It's a one-line fix per asset, or a CDN-level HTTPS setting.
2. Fix the head meta tags. Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` (or `<meta charset="utf-8">`) and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel `<img>`.
4. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers so the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` headers, footer address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results and UTM parameters on resolved link destinations can be checked.
5. Optionally, follow the redirects on a sample of the 25 `click.crocs-email.com` links to confirm they resolve and keep campaign parameters.
