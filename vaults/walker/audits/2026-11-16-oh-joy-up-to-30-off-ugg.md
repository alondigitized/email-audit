# Oh joy! Up to 30% off UGG 🎁
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count of the boots and UGG emails this week, and the one from this morning, "Up to 30% off UGG 🎁", was nearly the same. The big coral "30% OFF*" over the UGG logo is easy to read on my phone without glasses. But it's UGG boots again, and women's boots at that. Everything below the hero is women's: the party boot, the riding boot, a gal in a red outfit lacing up a winter boot.
- The only thing for me is the "Shop by Size" block near the bottom, with Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons. The wide-width button is useful, but it's buried under five boot pictures. There's no men's slip-on, cushioned walker or arch-support shoe anywhere. The header strip says "Up to 30% off Nike" and that's a different deal from the headline. I would not click, and I would not forward it. I'd swipe it away.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF*" hero is huge and readable without glasses.
- Navigation is simple: Women, Men, Kids, Brands, Clearance in plain text.
- The Wide Width button under "Shop by Size" is the one thing aimed at me.
- DSW is a store I know and trust, and the "In stores & online" line is handy.
- The free shipping, in-store pickup and app icons near the bottom are clear.

## What's weak

- This is the ninth or tenth near-identical boots and UGG send this week.
- It's all women's boots, so there's nothing for a 62-year-old man who wants comfort.
- The Nike line at the top competes with the UGG hero, and I can't tell which deal this is.
- The fine print is tiny, and the asterisk on "30%" makes me wary.
- The 500 bonus points offer needs a VIP sign-up and only applies to UGG and Birkenstock.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or comfort.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me a men's comfort email. Slip-ons, walking shoes and arch support from Skechers, Hoka and Brooks would all fit. Segment by gender and past purchases, and stop sending men the women's boot story.
- 2. Cut the frequency, or let me choose how often you send.
- 3. Put the Wide Width button higher and offer men's sizes.
- 4. Pick one deal per email, either Nike or UGG.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound (XHTML doctype, viewport, dark-mode CSS, Braze/Movable Ink tracking). The one hard failure is a 400 on the SMS opt-in CTA. The source was truncated and 48 of 60 links were not probed, so several categories can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] SMS opt-in CTA returns HTTP 400.**
  - Link text: "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off".
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/M5f34h8GhxwteJojOpkkXQ~~/AAQRxRA~/0K7_LwztxT69R_...`
  - The anchor also reports an empty title/label, so the probe saw no accessible text for the link.
  - The cause isn't confirmed. A 400 from a Braze/SendGrid-style `ablink` redirector can mean an expired or malformed tracking token, or a probe that the redirector rejects (no cookies, non-browser UA). Re-test from a real client before treating it as a live outage.
  - This is the one CTA with a measurable incentive (15% off), so a real failure would directly cost conversions.
- All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`, so final destinations can't be read from the source.
- Only 12 of 60 links were probed. Footer, utility and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Ineffective meta declarations.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute. Both are inert, so the charset relies entirely on the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` prevents pinch-zoom on iOS and Android. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/B4DupzrEkVk8XS9GuM9m9Q~~/AAQRxRA~/Six4P-...` (a `/q/` path, most likely the Braze open-tracking pixel).
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=...` (Movable Ink pixel).
  - Both are tracking pixels, so severity is low. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Generic `<title>`.** It reads `DSW` and doesn't reflect the email content. This is minor.
- The dark-mode classes (`.dm-bg-*`, `.dm-font-*`) and `color-scheme` meta are present. No issues were found in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens appear in the visible HTML or subject. The tracking pixel carries `mi_u=225090073`, which looks like a resolved user ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it's a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address not confirmed.** QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure for it. The truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so the postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not confirmed.
- **Link-heavy plain text.** The plain-text part is 19,936 of 24,295 characters URLs (82%). That exceeds the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk. The cause is long tracking-redirect URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not assessable. The tracking redirects hide destination URLs and UTM parameters, and only 12 links were resolved.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261116_P261711_V1_W-_Offer_New_U`. It is cut off in the QA output.
- The landing page for the UGG offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-test the SMS opt-in link** (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/M5f34h8G...`) from a real browser and device. If it still returns 400, fix the redirect or SMS opt-in endpoint. Ensure it has a visible, accessible label.
2. **Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`** to the two meta tags so they take effect.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to the Braze and Movable Ink pixels.
5. **Reduce plain-text URL density** by shortening the tracking links or trimming the text part.
6. **Re-run QA with the full, untruncated source and raw headers.** This will confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, footer address, and UTM or landing-page alignment on the skipped links.
