# Want up to 30% off UGG? You got it!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. It's the third UGG email in two days, and the third in a row that reads as a repeat. Yesterday I got "Up to 30% off UGG?! (Spoiler: YES!)", and earlier today "UGG. Up to 30% off. It's a thing." At least it's a break from the week of TINSEL 20% emails. The big coral "30% OFF*" is easy to read on my phone, and so is the "Early Black Friday Sale" banner.
- UGG slippers and boots aren't what I shop for. I want cushioned, arch-supported slip-ons for walking. The rest of the email is mostly women's loafers, heels and sandals. The "Shop Men" link is just a word in the nav bar. For a 62-year-old guy, that tells me I'm on the wrong list, or at least the wrong segment.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and keep going.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF*" headline is huge and readable without squinting. One clear number.
- The black "SHOP UGG" button is easy to tap and sits right under the hero.
- The extra 10% for military and veterans is a nice touch, and it's clearly marked.
- The shop-by-size row, including Wide Width, is something I'd actually use.
- The footer explains Afterpay, free shipping for VIPs and in-store pickup in plain icons.

## What's weak

- Nearly everything is women's. The grid, the "Hey, what's new?" block and the "Shop Women" button all skip me.
- The hero is a fluffy UGG boot. I'm not shopping for that, and nothing here says cushioning or arch support.
- It's the same UGG 30% pitch I got yesterday and this morning. I'm tired of it.
- The asterisks make me wonder what's excluded, and the fine print is tiny and dense at the bottom.
- The product grid has no prices, so I can't do my price-versus-quality math. Many of the shoes don't even have names.
- The adidas 25% block shows a logo, not a shoe. It's the only brand I might care about, and I can't see what's on sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and category. Send me a men's comfort and walking email, with a hero like a cushioned slip-on, a price and a button saying "Shop men's slip-ons".
- 2. Cut the repeat sends. Three UGG emails in 48 hours is too many.
- 3. Show real products and prices in the grid. Add "was/now" pricing.
- 4. Show an adidas shoe in the adidas block, ideally a walking or casual one.
- 5. Move the Men link and the comfort or wide-width options higher up.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort picks for your feet, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned slip-ons and sneakers, plus 30% off UGG through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide widths in stock. Early Black Friday prices on men's comfort`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken tracked link (HTTP 400 on the SMS opt-in CTA) and some accessibility gaps in the head markup. The QA data shows no other hard failures. Authentication and most of the body could not be verified from the supplied data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** the CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/lafoexw5w8GLi0Rgq1Ozmg~~/AAQRxRA~/km3HjIlH…` and returns 400.
  - The destination is the SMS-signup offer, a conversion path.
  - The probe shows no link text or redirect target, so the cause isn't visible. A 400 on an `ablink` redirect can come from a malformed or truncated tracking token, or from the click tracker rejecting a non-browser probe. Re-test with a browser UA before treating it as confirmed in the wild.
- **Click-tracking domain:** all links go through `ablink.dsw.com`, so the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible in the source.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 73 links were probed, and the other 61 were skipped as low priority (footer, utility, social). Those links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261110_P261678_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_U` is present. Nothing is wrong with it beyond the alt text noted below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 2 images:**
  - The `ablink.dsw.com/q/47zGK04cpKe8iqRljOqV9A~~/…` image has no `alt`. If it is content, it is invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
  - `o.gif` has no `alt`. It is a tracking pixel and should carry `alt=""`.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute:** the opening tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Ineffective meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Both are inert. The charset is likely still set by the MIME header.
- **`<title>`:** it is the generic `DSW` and does not describe the message. This is minor.
- **Web fonts:** the fonts load from `braze-images.com` with no fallback visible in the truncated source. Clients that block web fonts will fall back to defaults.
- **Dark mode:** the dark-mode `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block is present and well-formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The pixel's `mi_u=225090073` is a resolved user ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note says this is expected because of the AgentMail relay. This is a limit of the test setup, not a confirmed sender defect. Verify with a direct-to-inbox header capture.
- **Unsubscribe:** the QA run always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The link and the physical address fall in the truncated section, so I could not confirm them. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not supplied either.
- **Sender:** the From address is `dsw@e.dsw.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain. This is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** these can't be verified. Every destination is wrapped in an opaque `ablink.dsw.com` token. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261110_P261678_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_U` in the pixel.
- **Landing alignment:** the UGG offer in the subject can't be matched to a landing page. The only probed failure is the SMS opt-in link.
- **Plain-text version:** 26,569 of 31,637 characters (about 84%) are URLs, which is a **[WARN]** for text-heavy spam scoring. The cause is long click-tracking URLs repeated in the text part.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or re-test the SMS opt-in link.** Confirm whether `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/lafoexw5…` returns 400 for real browsers. If it does, regenerate the tracking link in the ESP, since this is a revenue-relevant CTA.
2. Add `alt` text to the `ablink…/q/47zGK04…` image, and set `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`, or remove them.
5. Shorten the plain-text part by reducing repeated tracked URLs, or add more descriptive text around them.
6. Capture the raw message headers to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers.
7. Expand link probing to cover the 61 skipped links. At least check the unsubscribe and preference-center links with a real-browser UA.
