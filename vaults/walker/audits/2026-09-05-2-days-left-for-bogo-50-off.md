# 2 days left for BOGO 50% off!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Same BOGO 50% off deal, fifth time this week now — I'm not even surprised anymore, just tired of it. Subject line says "2 days left," which is at least a real deadline, but then I scroll into "Brand New... And So You" and it's the exact same wall of shoes I've been getting all week: clogs, mary janes, leopard print heels, gold strappy sandals, an ankle boot in animal print. Fourteen products and I count maybe two — a pair of brown sneakers and some sandals in the back row — that even remotely resemble something a 62-year-old guy would put on his feet.
- The "Shop By Size" grid at the bottom is a nice touch, and "Shop Wide Width / Wide Calf" tells me they know their older, bigger-footed customers exist — that's the one part of this email that felt like it was built for someone like me. But it's buried under a dozen women's items first, so by the time I get there I've already tuned out.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on. Not clicking, not forwarding — DSW's had four cracks at me this week already and none of them showed me a walking shoe or a Skechers-style comfort sneaker.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off headline is big, bold, and impossible to miss at the top.
- "2 days left" gives me a real deadline instead of vague urgency.
- Shop By Size grid and Wide Width/Wide Calf buttons acknowledge practical fit needs.
- Clean white layout, no overlapping text or broken images that I can see.

## What's weak

- Product grid is almost entirely women's styles — heels, wedges, mary janes, animal print boots — nothing that reads as men's comfort footwear.
- I've seen this identical BOGO offer and near-identical "new arrivals" grid four times already this week under different subject lines.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or slip-on comfort anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to buy shoes.
- DSW VIP banner at the bottom is an afterthought when loyalty perks could've pulled me in higher up.
- "SHOP NOW" button and category links are tiny text-only links, not something I can tap easily on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the product grid with men's comfort shoes — sneakers, walking shoes, slip-ons — for segments like me instead of burying two men's items in row four.
- 2. Cut the repetition — I've gotten this same offer wrapped in five different themes this week; space these out or make each send meaningfully different.
- 3. Add a line about comfort features (cushioned insole, arch support) since that's what actually moves a guy my age to click.
- 4. Make the CTA buttons bigger and more tappable — right now "SHOP NOW" is a small underlined link, easy to miss on a phone.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort shoes: BOGO 50% off ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days left: BOGO 50% off cushioned sneakers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-on comfort, arch support, half off your second pair`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before the weekend — BOGO ends Monday`
