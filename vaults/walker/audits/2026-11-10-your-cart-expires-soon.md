## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Cart-abandonment email with a failing unsubscribe probe (HTTP 400), no plain-text part (0 chars), and 13 images served over `http://`. Most links are wrapped in Attentive click-tracking redirects, so the final destinations and UTM parameters could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…&attn_sid=4584e2cd-…`. The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, so this is the real unsubscribe endpoint.
  - A 400 from a tokenized endpoint may come from the probe itself (bot or HEAD request, or a token consumed by an earlier request). It may also mean the link is broken for real users.
  - Treat it as unverified-broken. Re-test with a browser-like GET, since the QA run can't tell the two cases apart.
- **Two CTAs returned 429.** These are "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." (both `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`). Rate limiting by the redirector, not evidence of a broken link.
- **Coverage gap.** 12 links were probed and 32 skipped, so most footer and secondary links are unchecked.
- **Redirect-wrapped links.** The "web version" link and the CTAs all go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<opaque token>`. Destination URLs are not visible in the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images use `http://`** on `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`. Affected: logo (`dde00662-…png`), hero "Complete Your Order" (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), `a6ec5198-…jpg`, "Text", App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook, Youtube.
  - Clients that enforce HTTPS or image proxying may block these. Gmail typically proxies them, but some clients won't load them.
  - The logo and hero are among the affected images, so a block degrades the main content.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` has no alt attribute.
  - The open-tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` has none either. That is low impact, but an empty `alt=""` is the correct form for a decorative pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Malformed charset meta.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it declares nothing. Parsers fall back to header or default encoding.
- Positive: `lang="en"` is set, the viewport meta is present, and the mobile breakpoint is `@media (max-width:620px)` on 600px-wide tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible (truncated) source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The rest of the HTML was cut off, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This is a multipart/alternative deliverability gap and a minor accessibility gap. Confirm it isn't an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping the text part.
- **Unsubscribe endpoint returns 400.** See section 2. If it fails for real recipients, it is a CAN-SPAM exposure.
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** This is a relay limitation, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is not a confirmed failure.
- **Not verifiable from the data.** `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address in the footer are both unchecked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues, but this could not be verified. The "Complete Your Order" CTA returned 429 before its redirect resolved, so the cart landing URL, UTM parameters, and cart restoration are untested. Attentive normally appends its own tracking, and the source shows no direct `utm_*` parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the unsubscribe URL with a real GET and browser headers. If it still returns 400, escalate it as a compliance defect.
2. Switch all 13 `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` image URLs to `https://`.
3. Add alt text to `a6ec5198-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to the `wf/open` pixel.
4. Populate `<title>` and fix the charset tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
5. Add a plain-text MIME part. First rule out relay stripping.
6. Re-run the probe with throttling or retry-after handling to resolve the 429 CTAs. Then confirm the final cart URL and UTMs.
7. Capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) from a direct-delivery copy.
