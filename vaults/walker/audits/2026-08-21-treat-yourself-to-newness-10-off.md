# Treat yourself to newness + $10 off!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- Same $10/$20/$60 tiered deal DSW's been running all week — fine, I don't have to do math, I'll give them that. But then it's heels, strappy sandals, ankle-strap block heels, one section literally called "That's Not All, Folks" with more heels and sandals, and a size chart that starts at a 5 and tops out at 11 with "wide calf" as an option. That's not my shoe rack, that's not even close.
- This is the same story as every DSW email I've gotten this week — adidas or "new arrivals," and every time it's women's dress and sandal inventory front and center. I scroll past all of it, and by the time I hit the sweepstakes banner about winning $50 in-store I've already mentally checked out. Not clicking, not forwarding, just marking it read and moving on.
- The one thing that'd get me to open a DSW email is a men's comfort walking shoe or a Skechers/Hoka-style sneaker up top with that same $10 off tiered deal attached to it — and they haven't done that once in six sends now.

## What's working

- The $10/$20/$60 tiered offer at the very top is clear and consistent with what I've seen from DSW this week — no mental math required.
- "Buy More, Save More" headline is straightforward, no gimmicks.
- The size grid and wide-width/wide-calf options show they think about fit, which I respect as a concept — just aimed at the wrong department for me.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — heels, strappy sandals, ankle-strap flats — is women's. Not one sneaker, walking shoe, or slip-on for a guy like me.
- "Shop by Size" runs 5 to 11, women's sizing, reinforcing this wasn't built with me in mind at all.
- The "Shoeprise Savings Hunt" in-store sweepstakes needs me to physically hunt for a red arrow this weekend — too much effort for a maybe-$50 prize on shoes I'm not even shopping for here.
- Six product tiles, zero variety in category — no athletic, no comfort, no men's at all, which makes this feel like a copy-paste from a women's-only send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this list — send men a version led with athletic/comfort sneakers, not heels and sandals. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Swap the hero grid to show at least one men's walking or slip-on shoe, since I've now seen six DSW emails this week with zero for me.
- 3. Drop the "hunt for a red arrow in-store" mechanic for something I can act on from my phone — a straightforward extra % off feels more my speed.
- 4. Keep leaning on the tiered $10/$20/$60 offer — it's the one thing DSW's gotten right and consistent about lately.
