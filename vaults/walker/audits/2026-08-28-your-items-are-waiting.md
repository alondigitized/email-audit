## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on the Attentive ESP (Iterable-style redirect links via `skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click`), uses no personalization/merge tokens visible in the truncated source, and fails 3 automated QA checks: two broken tracking-redirect links and a missing plain-text MIME part.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (401)** on the product image CTA "Image of Men's OG Anunoby Limited Edition: SKX Nexus NYC Blue (Pre-Order) Basket" — the Attentive `ls/click` redirect returns HTTP 401 (unauthorized/expired tracking token), which will send clicking users to an error page instead of the PDP.
- **Broken link (400)** on the **Unsubscribe** link — the `ls/click` redirect returns HTTP 400. This is the highest-priority fix: a non-functional unsubscribe link is both a compliance risk (see §5) and a deliverability risk (recipients who can't unsubscribe are more likely to hit spam-report buttons).
- Two additional links ("Complete you Order" CTA and the cart-reminder banner link) returned 429 (rate-limited) during probing — QA correctly flags these as inconclusive, not confirmed broken, since 429 indicates the prober was throttled rather than the destination being invalid. Recommend a manual re-check outside the automated probe.
- All links route through Attentive's tracking redirector (`skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=...`), which is expected for an ESP-managed campaign and not itself an issue.
- QA notes 12 of 38 total links were probed (prioritized by click likelihood); 26 lower-priority footer/social links were not checked and should be spot-verified separately.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **9 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`**, including the primary Skechers logo, the "Complete your Order" CTA image, a secondary product recommendation image, and all footer/app-store/social icons. Mixed-content images are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) on an otherwise HTTPS-rendered page, risking broken/missing images for recipients.
- **2 images missing `alt` text**: a tracking pixel-adjacent GIF (`021bd784-...gif`) and a product image (`a6ec5198-...jpg`, which is also one of the `http://` offenders above). Missing alt text degrades the experience for screen-reader users and for any client that blocks images by default.
- The 1x1 open-tracking pixel (`wf/open?upn=...`) is also missing `alt` text — low impact since it's not user-visible content, but flagged for completeness.
- Responsive CSS (`@media (max-width:620px)`) is present with mobile-specific padding/font-size overrides for rows 16/17/19, and `.desktop_hide`/`.mobile_hide` classes are correctly implemented for Outlook (`mso-hide:all`) and other clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[%%FIRSTNAME%%]`) are visible in the truncated HTML provided. No unrendered/broken token syntax was found in the available source. - A "web version" link is present and appears functional (not flagged by QA).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
All outbound links use Attentive's obfuscated `upn=` redirect token rather than exposing final destination URLs or UTM parameters directly in the HTML, so UTM/landing-page alignment can't be verified from the source alone — it depends on Attentive's server-side redirect mapping. Given the 401/400 failures on two of these redirects, recommend verifying the full redirect chain (not just the `ls/click` hop) resolves to the correct, live PDP/unsubscribe pages with intended UTM tagging.
