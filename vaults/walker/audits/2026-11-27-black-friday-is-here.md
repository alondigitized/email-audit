# Black Friday is here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction: another HOKA Black Friday email, and I've lost count. This is the fifth or sixth one this week, counting "Don't leave savings on the table" yesterday and "ALMOST SOLD OUT" the day before. "Black Friday is on" is huge white type on navy, and I can read it without my glasses. The offer is clear, but it's the same story again.
- The offer is different this time, though. Yesterday it was "up to 40% off sale styles." Now it's $40 off $200, $50 off $250 and $60 off $300, plus a free beanie at $250. That means I have to spend a lot to save a little, and I don't buy three pairs of shoes at once. The photo is a runner's leg in motion, which isn't me. I walk, and I want a cushioned Bondi-type shoe, not a "rotation."
- Would I click? Maybe, but only if I'd already planned to buy. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The headline is enormous, white on dark blue, so I can read it on my phone without zooming.
- The spend tiers are stacked in a simple list: $40, $50, $60. I understood them at a glance.
- The "Shop Now" button is a wide, easy tap target, which my thumbs appreciate.
- Mentioning the free HOKA Membership gives a loyalty angle without making me chase points.
- The yellow beanie banner at the top is a nice little bonus and easy to spot.

## What's weak

- The runner photo is a young, athletic runner, and nothing in it says walking or all-day comfort.
- The savings depend on spending $200 or more. For one pair of Bondis, I'm not getting much.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny blue-on-blue text, and I couldn't read it without zooming. Members only, exclusions apply, and the dates are all buried there.
- "New arrivals" is a generic card with no named shoe, price or size. It also pushes new stuff when I want a deal.
- It repeats the Black Friday message I've gotten all week. Nothing in it feels like it was meant for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a specific cushioned walking shoe, like the Bondi, with a real price and the member discount on it. Make the CTA say "Shop Bondi walking shoes."
- 2. Put the deadline (ends 12/1) up near the offer instead of in the fine print.
- 3. Add a lower tier, or a simple "% off one pair," so a single-pair shopper can win.
- 4. Make the footer text larger and a higher-contrast colour.
- 5. Segment by browsing history, so walkers see a walking hero and runners see a running one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$50 off Bondi & Clifton, members only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cushioned walkers: $40 off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free beanie at $250. Member savings end Dec 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign in free to unlock $40–$60 off cushioned walkers.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email passes 56% of the automated QA checks. The confirmed problems are a 0-character plain-text part, missing `alt` on 9 images, and two probe 403s, one of them on the unsubscribe link. Both 403s are probably bot-blocking by the click tracker rather than real breakage, but the probe could not confirm that.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe 403 on the unsubscribe link:** the link text is "Unsubscribe". The Cordial tracker URL is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6af6…:1/3031d9a4?jwtH=…`. Its JWT payload decodes to `linkUrl: https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`.
- **Probe 403 on the returns link:** the link text is "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/". The tracker URL is `…/c2/…/7a3f9e1e?jwtH=…`. Its payload decodes to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…`.
- **Likely cause of the 403s:** both are tracker-wrapped redirects that returned 403 to an automated client. This is probably bot or JWT rejection rather than a dead destination, so a real-client check is needed.
- **JWT expiry:** the payload contains `ce: 86400`, which looks like a 24-hour expiry (inferred from the decoded fields). If it is one, tracked links including unsubscribe could fail after a day.
- **Coverage gap:** only 3 of 12 material links were probed. The other 9 are unverified.
- **Two tracking-pixel hosts:** `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. The second host (`mg.`) is a different sending domain from the first. This is a minor observation, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 9 images.** Seven are content images. The two tracking pixels are harmless but should still carry `alt=""`.
  - `H_US_Banner_GWPBeanie_1.jpg`
  - `H_US_Hero_BF_2.jpg`
  - `H_US_Subcopy_Hero_BF_2.jpg`
  - `H_US_CTA_Hero_Bondi9Markdown_2.jpg`
  - `H_US_Secondary_NewArrivals_KTT_2.jpg`
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
  - the two tracking pixels
- **Text baked into images:** the banner, hero, subcopy and CTA are all images without `alt`. With images blocked, the offer, the CTA and the "Black Friday" message are all lost.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Duplicate viewport meta:** there are two tags, one with `maximum-scale=1` and one with `user-scalable=yes`. They conflict, and `maximum-scale=1` hampers pinch zoom.
- **Invalid CSS in the visible source:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values from unresolved template variables.
- **Web fonts loaded from `www.hoka.com`:** support is limited in many clients. The fallback stack is not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`. The source is truncated, so the body was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part missing:** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This hurts deliverability and accessibility. It may partly reflect how the AgentMail relay captured the message, but it should be confirmed against the original MIME.
- **Authentication unverified:** no `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe link:** it exists (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`) but returned 403 to the probe.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** not available in the data, so one-click unsubscribe compliance can't be assessed.
- **Physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM structure is consistent on the one decoded link:**
  - `utm_source=US_Broadcast`
  - `utm_medium=email`
  - `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261127_AM1_BlackFriday_Launch_A365_AllMembers_AnniPurchasers`
  - `hmail=<hash>`
  - `cordial_id=…`
- **Not verifiable here:** the landing pages of the other links and the offer alignment, because only the returns and unsubscribe links were decoded.
- **Returns link in a Black Friday email:** the link points to `/return-start/` and probes as 403. Whether the page itself loads was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the two 403 links with a browser-like client or with follow-redirects. Confirm that the unsubscribe flow completes.
2. Check the tracked-link JWT lifetime (`ce: 86400`) and confirm that unsubscribe links don't expire.
3. Add a multipart `text/plain` part.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images and `alt=""` to the pixels.
5. Fix the `<head>`:
   - add a title
   - use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>`
   - remove the duplicate viewport tag
   - drop `maximum-scale=1`
6. Fix the template variables that render empty in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border`.
7. Capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) from the original message rather than the relay copy.
8. Raise probe coverage above 3 of 12 links.
