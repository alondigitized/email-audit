# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-12**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I got as far as the "Add to Cart" button, and then the trip fell apart. The homepage and the Men's menu are clean and easy to read on my phone. The "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," and "Arch Fit" tabs along the top are what I came for. But the one shoe I opened was a $225 racing shoe with no reviews. The cart count stayed at 0, and the cart page came up as a completely blank white screen. I never saw a login. I'd have closed the browser and gone to Hoka, Brooks, or the DSW sale rack.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10**, the same as yesterday.

- **Better than yesterday:** I can't confirm any improvement. I don't have yesterday's screenshots, only the score.
- **Same as yesterday:** the cart still doesn't leave me with a purchase.
- **Regression:** I can't claim one. The blank cart page is the worst thing I saw, but I can't tell whether yesterday was any better.

## 3. What's Working

- **Comfort tabs on the homepage.** "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit," and "Max Cushion" are right under the logo. That's my shopping list, and nobody else puts it first.
- **Men's menu.** It's a big, plain list: Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, and so on. The rows are tall and easy to tap with a thumb, and there's a clear Back and X.
- **Men's Shoes page.** "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes" are the first two pictures I see. Free pickup is offered.
- **Size buttons and "Add to Cart" button.** Both are big and readable. The button is a strong blue and it's easy to hit.
- **Skechers Plus banner.** The "25% off" line is easy to read.
- **Search results.** The "comfort shoes" search returned 1,602 results with prices and a "Members Get 25% OFF" line.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Cart broke.**
  - Step 7 shows "Please select a size" in red and the cart badge still at 0.
  - Step 8 is a totally blank white page, with no header, no "your cart is empty" message, and nothing to tap.
  - If I had picked a size, I can't say it would have worked. A blank page makes me doubt the whole site.
- **Login did nothing I could see.** Steps 2 and 3 look identical to the homepage. I never saw a sign-in screen, and I never saw my name or any sign I was recognized. The product page still says "Sign in for free shipping & returns!"
- **The first product wasn't for me.**
  - The Aero Elite is a $225 racing shoe, "Excluded from promotions," with "No Reviews."
  - That's the opposite of a cushioned, arch-supported walking shoe at a fair price.
  - The product page shows no comfort details, such as arch support, cushioning, or a comparison, in what I saw.
- **Sizes.** 9.0, 10.5, 11.0, 11.5, and 12.5 are crossed out. The little alarm-clock icons are tiny and I wouldn't know they mean "notify me."
- **Search showed me the wrong shoes.** The first two results are women's: a pink sneaker and a black wedge loafer. I'm a man looking for comfort. The search didn't know that.
- **Small text.** The breadcrumbs, "773 results," "Women's," and the size-chart link are small. I'd have to zoom.
- **The deal is confusing.** The banner says "25% off Apparel & Bags – Applied at Cart," and results say "Members Get 25% OFF! Applied at Cart." I can't tell whether the shoes qualify, and the product I opened said they don't.
- **Nothing helps me compare brands or work out price against quality.**

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page first.**
   - Show the header and either the items or a plain "Your cart is empty" message, never a blank page.
   - Add a "Continue shopping" button.
2. **Show a clear error and scroll to the size selector** when a size is missing. Better, scroll to it automatically.
3. **Make login visible and confirm it worked.** Greet me by name, and show my size or my last-viewed shoes.
4. **Lead men's pages with comfort shoes.**
   - Put the Slip-ins and Arch Fit lines first.
   - Move racing models like the Aero Elite lower down.
5. **Make search aware of the shopper.** If I'm browsing Men's, show men's results first when I search "comfort shoes."
6. **Add plain-language comfort info on product pages.** For example, "Arch support: high. Cushion: plush. Best for: all-day walking."
7. **Make the text larger and add a "notify me" label** next to the crossed-out sizes.
8. **Say plainly whether a shoe qualifies for the 25% off** before I reach the cart.

## 6. Bottom Line

The front of the store looks like it was built for a shopper like me. The back half, with the cart and personalization, isn't working. A comfort shopper loses patience quickly. A blank cart page and a $225 racing shoe don't earn a second visit when Hoka and the DSW sale aisle are a tap away. The cart is the hole in the bucket, so that's the fix to make.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

The journey was largely blocked by bot protection, so most findings are limited or unreliable.
- **Kasada throttling:** Kasada (`x-kpsdk`) returned HTTP 429 on its `/fp` fingerprint endpoint five times and on `https://www.skechers.com/cart/` once.
- **Stuck on the homepage:** The HTML samples for Homepage, Log In and Men category all report URL `https://www.skechers.com/`. Those steps most likely never navigated, or the captured URL is stale.
- **Missing vitals:** LCP and CLS were not captured for any step, so only TTFB is available.
- **Cart audit is an error page:** The View Cart axe results (no `<title>`, no `lang`, no `<main>`, no `<h1>`) match a bare 429 response body, not the real cart template. Treat them as artifacts of the block, not site defects.

## Accessibility

18 axe violations across three steps. The Homepage ones are the most credible.

**Homepage (10):**
- **critical `button-name`:** one or more buttons have no accessible name.
- **critical `aria-required-parent`:** ARIA roles are used without their required parent roles.
- **serious `link-name`:** one or more links have no discernible text.
- **moderate landmark structure:**
  - `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-main-is-top-level` and `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` indicate multiple or nested `<main>` elements, plus a nested `<aside>`.
  - `landmark-unique` and `region` mean landmarks aren't distinguishable and content sits outside landmarks.
- **moderate `heading-order`:** heading levels are skipped.
- **minor `aria-allowed-role`:** a role is set on an element that doesn't allow it.

**Search results (4):** `aria-allowed-role`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region`. These repeat the homepage template issues, so they are likely shared header and footer components.

**View Cart (4):** `document-title`, `html-has-lang`, `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one`. These are probably from the 429 page (see Summary), so re-test on a successful cart load.

Product detail, Men > Shoes and Add to Cart have no reported violations. Given the navigation problem, I wouldn't read that as a pass.

## SEO

- **Homepage `lang`:** `<html lang="en">` is present, which is good.
- **Cart:** The cart response lacked `<title>` and `<h1>`. This matters only if a real crawler gets 429 on `/cart/`; otherwise it isn't an SEO concern, since carts are normally `noindex`.
- **Other signals:** Canonical, meta description, structured data and robots directives are not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be assessed.

## Performance

- **TTFB:** All steps are under 420ms. The highest are the Homepage (416ms), Log In (404ms) and Men category (404ms). The rest are 57–124ms, which suggests CDN or cache hits.
- **LCP and CLS:** Not measured, so no verdict is possible.
- **Third-party scripts:** Head-level async tags are numerous and load on every page.
  - **Analytics and tag managers:** GTM, gtag (several IDs), Quantum Metric, Bloomreach and Monetate.
  - **Ad and social pixels:** TikTok, Bing, Snap, Reddit, Taboola, Nextdoor, Pinterest, Facebook, Yahoo, Impact, Criteo/RTB House and Tune.
  - **Other:** Pulse Insights and Stape.
  - These add many extra connections and main-thread work, and are the likeliest source of LCP and TBT regressions.
- **Font loading:** Fonts load through WebFont Loader from cdnjs, a third-party origin, which adds a request chain. Whether that causes layout shift is unknown without CLS data.
- **Viewport height:** `--vh: 6.64px` is set inline on `<html>`, indicating a viewport height of about 664px. It is computed by script, which is a minor layout-after-JS dependency.

## Mobile Optimization

- **Viewport height variable:** A JS-computed `--vh` is used instead of `dvh`/`svh` units, which are now widely supported.
- **Click handler:** A global click listener calls `preventDefault()` on `.js-ignore-click` until `app:JsLoaded` fires. This guards against early taps, but those taps are silently dropped on slow connections.
- **Other:** Touch target size, image sizing and responsive images can't be assessed from the data provided.

## Console & Network Errors

**Network (6):**
- 5× 429 on the Kasada fingerprint endpoint `/…/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.864`.
- 1× 429 on `/cart/`.
- This is rate limiting or bot challenge by the WAF, not an application bug. It does mean the cart was unreachable to this client, and an overly aggressive threshold could also affect real users on shared IPs.

**Console (24, 10 shown):**
- **429 resource failures:** Three are shown, corresponding to the network errors above.
- **`Unsupported Summarizer API languages…`:** This comes from the browser's built-in Summarizer API, called with unsupported language options. Its source is unconfirmed. It may be a browser extension or an on-page script, and it is not clearly first-party.
- **`Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed`:** A script registers an `unload` handler, which is deprecated and blocks back/forward cache (bfcache) eligibility. Third-party tags are the likely source.

## Recommendations

1. **Re-run the audit with fixes to the test run:**
   - Verify each step actually navigates, since three steps report the homepage URL.
   - Add backoff or pacing.
   - Use an allowlisted IP or a Kasada-approved test header to avoid 429s, and re-capture LCP/CLS.
2. **Fix the Homepage critical axe issues:** Add accessible names to icon-only buttons and links, and correct ARIA parent/child role structure.
3. **Fix the landmark structure:** Ensure a single top-level `<main>`, a top-level `<aside>`, and unique labels (`aria-label`) on repeated `nav`/`aside` landmarks. This will clear most of the repeated Homepage and Search violations.
4. **Fix heading order and `role` misuse:** Correct the skipped heading levels and remove disallowed `role` attributes.
5. **Remove or replace `unload` listeners:** Use `pagehide` or `visibilitychange` to restore bfcache eligibility, and identify which tag registers them.
6. **Audit third-party tags:** Defer or consolidate the ad and analytics pixels, and self-host or preload the fonts instead of loading WebFont Loader from cdnjs.
7. **Review Kasada rate limits** on `/fp` and `/cart/` for false positives against legitimate mobile traffic.
8. **Use `dvh`/`svh` units** instead of the JS-computed `--vh`.
