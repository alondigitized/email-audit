# FYI, $10 off disappears soon
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Same $10/$20/$60 coupon I've now seen from DSW four days running — at this point I could recite it in my sleep, and honestly the "disappears soon" subject line finally gives it a little urgency the last few didn't bother with. But once I open it, it's the same story every time: a full grid of heels, strappy sandals, a leopard-print pump, ankle booties, gold sandals — twelve shoes and I count maybe one pair (the white sneaker, second row) that isn't women's dress footwear. There's a "Shop by Size" grid down at 5 to 11, which tells me exactly who this email is for, and it isn't me.
- The "Shoeprise Savings Hunt" banner in the middle is a total non-sequitur — an in-store scavenger hunt for up to $50 off, happening today and tomorrow, with zero connection to anything else on the page. Feels like two different emails got stapled together.
- I'd mark this read and move on. Nothing here pulls a 62-year-old guy looking for a slip-on walking shoe into the store, and I wouldn't forward it to anybody — unless my wife wants the sandals.

## What's working

- The coupon tiers up top are big, green, and easy to read without squinting — no math required, same as every send this week.
- "Claim $10 Off" button is clear and sits right under the offer, so at least the primary CTA isn't buried.
- The in-store scavenger hunt has a real deadline (today and tomorrow, 11-6) which is more urgency than most of their sends bother with.

## What's weak

- Twelve product photos, and it's almost entirely women's heels, sandals, and booties — I scanned the whole grid looking for anything resembling my closet and came up empty.
- The "Shop by Size" bar tops out at 11 with half-sizes like 6.5, 7.5 — that's a women's sizing chart, not men's, so it just confirms this wasn't built with me in mind.
- The Shoeprise Savings Hunt banner has nothing to do with the coupon or the shoes above it — feels bolted on, not designed.
- Not one men's shoe in the whole "Brand New" wall except that one white sneaker — DSW carries Skechers and New Balance for men, they just didn't show me any.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap at least a third of that product grid for men's comfort styles — Skechers slip-ons, a New Balance walking shoe — since DSW stocks them; right now the email tells me who it's really for, and it's not me.
- 2. Cut the "Shoeprise Savings Hunt" banner or move it to its own send — it interrupts the coupon message and doesn't earn its space here.
- 3. Add a men's size row (7-13) next to the women's one, or split "Shop by Size" into tabs, so guys aren't scrolling past a chart that isn't theirs.
- 4. Lead the hero with a lifestyle shot that includes a man, not just women's heels — right now the demographic signal is 100% one-sided.
