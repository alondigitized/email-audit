# Fresh batch of trail releases
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Well, that's HOKA number eleven if I'm counting right, and this one's back to the same "Tecton X" trail racer they showed me on the 20th — a fella leaping off a cliff again, just a different jump this time. There's a "Stinson 8" and a "Speedgoat 7" mixed in, which is at least something closer to what a guy my age would wear on a Saturday walk, but the whole email is built around ultra-marathoners, a Strava challenge, and a race in France called UTMB. None of that is me. I'm not linking up local paths for an ultra, I'm looking for something with good arch support I can slip on for the mall or a walk around the block.
- I wouldn't click this one. It's not offensive or broken, it's just not talking to me — this is HOKA's serious-runner catalog dressed up as a newsletter, four shoes shown with no price, no sale, no mention of comfort or fit. I'd scroll past it same as I have the last several from this brand.

## What's working

- The hero shot of the Tecton X 4 is clean and the shoe itself renders sharp, no glitches.
- Four different models shown in a grid (Stinson 8 men's/women's, Tecton X 4 women's, Speedgoat 7 men's) at least gives some variety instead of one shoe repeated.
- The Stinson 8 looks like a beefier, more cushioned trail shoe — that's closer to what I'd actually wear than the racing flats they usually push.
- Layout is simple enough to read on my phone without squinting, big bold headline up top.

## What's weak

- Every bit of copy is aimed at "trail runner," "ultra," "peak," "race" — nothing about comfort, cushioning, arch support, or all-day wear for a regular guy.
- No price anywhere on any of the four shoes, and no offer or discount at all — just "Shop Trail."
- The Strava challenge and the UTMB Mont-Blanc section eat up more than half the email and mean nothing to me — I'm not racing in Chamonix in a week.
- Same cliff-jumping, extreme-athlete imagery I called out on the 20th — this brand keeps hitting the same note.
- No mention of wide/X-Wide sizing this time, which was the one thing that actually landed with me back on the 17th.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the Strava challenge and UTMB blocks entirely for a segment like me — swap that real estate for the Stinson 8 with a price, a comfort callout, and wide-width availability.
- 2. Put a price or a "starting at $X" under each shoe in the grid — right now there's nothing to weigh.
- 3. Lead the hero with the Stinson 8 (the one that actually looks cushioned and walkable) instead of the racing-flat Tecton X.
- 4. Bring back the "wide and X-wide" messaging from the 8/17 send — that's the one thing that's made me pay attention in eleven emails.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Stinson 8 is here — wide sizes available`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, supportive, ready for the trail — or just the neighborhood`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email fails on two links returning HTTP 403 (including unsubscribe) and ships with no plain-text MIME part; images are otherwise well-formed but universally missing alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken (403)**: Return-start CTA — tracked URL `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...` resolving to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260823_...`. This is a return-portal link, not campaign-related, but currently unreachable.
- **Broken (403)**: **Unsubscribe link** — tracked URL resolving to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=...`. Critical — see Compliance section.
- 12 of 27 links were probed (click-likelihood ranked); 15 footer/social links were skipped by design, not confirmed broken.
- All probed CTA links carry consistent Cordial redirect wrapping (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/...` → JWT-encoded target) with full UTM params attached.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images (all product, hero, and footer images plus 2 tracking-pixel-style assets) have no `alt` attribute — no issues beyond this were surfaced by QA (no broken image src, no layout-breaking CSS detected in the truncated source).
- No issues found with responsive/media-query structure in the visible `<style>` block (mobile width overrides, `.hide`/`.show` classes present as expected for Cordial templates).

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge-token/personalization placeholders (e.g. unresolved `{{...}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`) present in the visible source.
- `hmail` (hashed email) and `cordial_id` parameters are present in tracked links, indicating per-recipient personalization is applied at the link layer — no issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link returns 403** — this is a CAN-SPAM violation risk if recipients cannot opt out via the one-click link. Confirmed via QA broken-link check above.
- No footer/physical-mailing-address content was present in the truncated HTML to confirm CAN-SPAM address requirement — cannot verify from available source.
- No authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) were supplied in the provided data — cannot assess from HTML/QA alone.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM params are consistent across probed links: `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260823_NewArrivalsTrail_ABChampion_A30Day_720TrailPurchasers_365NewArrivalsTag_AIAOVHigh`.
- Return-start landing page (`hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`) is unreachable (403) — breaks continuity for any recipient clicking that link.
- Cannot verify other landing pages' alignment (e.g. PDP pages for Stinson 8, Tecton X4, Speedgoat 7) since only 12/27 links were probed and full destination list wasn't in QA output.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the 403 on the unsubscribe redirect immediately — this blocks compliant opt-out and is the highest-priority issue.
- Fix or remove the return-start link (403) before send, or confirm it's a known post-send portal issue unrelated to this campaign.
- Add a plain-text MIME alternative — currently 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and breaks accessibility for text-only clients.
