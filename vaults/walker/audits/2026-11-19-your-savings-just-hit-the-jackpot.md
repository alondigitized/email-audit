- Two buttons, "Shop Black Friday Deals" and "Shop All Doorbusters," sound like the same thing. I don't know which one to pick.

- I've seen near-identical sends all week. This one has less in it than the others did.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound: it has `lang`, a viewport meta, a preheader, a mobile media query and an ESP open pixel. The source is truncated, so links, footer and unsubscribe could not be inspected. Two QA warnings apply: no authentication headers were captured, and one image has no alt attribute.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 13 tracking links on Crocs' click-redirect domain were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA WARN). Their destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed, not shown to be broken.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) open-event pixel. It is a second tracking beacon alongside the ESP's `click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0`. It carries a per-recipient `d_dpuid`. That is expected behavior, but it adds an extra third-party request.
- No broken or malformed `href`s are visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (QA WARN):** the `demdex.net/event` image has no `alt` attribute. It is a tracking pixel, so the fix is `alt=""`. Screen readers may otherwise announce the filename or URL. The ESP open pixel at `click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx` already has `alt=""`.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration is valid or effective. The charset is then governed only by the MIME `Content-Type` header, which is usually fine for ESP sends.
- **Positives:** `<html lang="en">`, `<title>Crocs</title>`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the Outlook text-size resets and a hidden preheader.
- Web fonts (`TT Crocs`) load from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. The fallback stack is not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication (QA WARN):** no `Authentication-Results` header was found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown and is not evidence of a sender misconfiguration. Verify against the raw message headers (`Received-SPF`, `DKIM-Signature`, `ARC-*`).
- **Unverifiable:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or the QA data. They are not assessed.
- The From domain `crocs-email.com` matches the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 13 content links were skipped by the probe and the HTML is truncated, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified. The visible pixel parameters (`d=10211`, `d_dpid=196107`) are tracking identifiers, not campaign UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML and the raw `.eml` headers. Then confirm the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, the postal address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
2. Resolve the 13 click-redirect links, following redirects with a browser-like request, to confirm final URLs return 200 and carry the expected UTM parameters.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex.net/event` pixel.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Treat the Authentication-Results warning as a gap in the test harness and have the pipeline capture headers directly.
