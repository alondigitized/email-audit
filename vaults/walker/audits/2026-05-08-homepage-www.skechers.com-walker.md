# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- I opened Skechers and the first thing I see is Mother's Day — flowers, a mom and her daughter, pink everything. Nice campaign I guess, but I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for a pair of Slip-ins, so this page isn't exactly talking to me. The deals up top are real, though — BOGO 50% off is the kind of thing that gets my attention even when the hero image doesn't.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner at the very top** — it's blue, bold, and impossible to miss. Price-conscious shopper like me notices that immediately.
- **"25% OFF Apparel" strip** — a second clear offer stacked right below. Two discounts before I even see the hero. That's the right order of priority.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — big, centered, high contrast. I can tap it without squinting. Good for someone browsing on a phone.
- **"Recently Viewed" peek at the bottom** — reminds me there's a personalized section waiting. That's useful for a returning member who already knows what he wants.
- **Page is clean** — no popups, no forced modals, nothing trying to trick me. Just loads and shows me what it's got.

## What's weak

- **Hero image is the Mother's Day shop** — a mom and young daughter in pink shoes surrounded by flowers. I'm a 62-year-old guy. This scene has nothing to do with me.
- **No Men's entry point above the fold** — I have to hunt for it. The only way in is through the hamburger "MENU" — one extra tap to find Men's. Direct nav link to Men's or Slip-ins would help.
- **Zero comfort-tech messaging** — no mention of Arch Fit, Slip-ins, or anything that says "built for people whose feet need a break." That's my whole reason for shopping here.
- **No Skechers Plus / loyalty visibility** — I'm a Plus member. Show me my rewards or member-only pricing. Nothing above the fold acknowledges I've been here before.
- **No trust signals** — no "free returns," no review count, no "X million sold." I don't see anything that reassures me before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Men's / Slip-ins shortcut directly in the nav strip**, below the banners — even a single "Men's | Women's | Sale" link row would let me get to my category without opening the full hamburger menu.
- **Swap the hero for a gender-neutral or Men's-adjacent campaign** — or at minimum add a second hero card for Men's that I can swipe to. Mother's Day gifts for Dad is a natural angle that runs the same week.
- **Surface my Skechers Plus rewards balance or a member-exclusive badge** in the top banner — "Plus Members: BOGO applies to your account" would stop me cold and feel personal.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line** somewhere in the top banner strip — even "Free shipping on orders $50+" keeps me from bouncing to check the fine print later.
