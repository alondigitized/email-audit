# Members, it's your time to shine
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-04-23**
## Executive summary

- Hold on — this isn't even Skechers. This is an adidas email. I'm a Skechers Plus member. I don't remember signing up for any adidas club. Right off the bat, I'm already confused and a little suspicious. The subject line told me it was "my time to shine" like I was a valued member, but the whole email feels like it was written for a 25-year-old who follows soccer and wears Yeezys. There's a FIFA World Cup giveaway, a Calvin Klein collab, dark moody photos, and very small text that I'd have to squint to read on my phone. Nothing here says "comfortable shoes for a 62-year-old guy who just wants to slip something on and walk the dog." I'm out in about 10 seconds.
- Complete persona mismatch. Wrong brand for me, wrong product focus, wrong tone, wrong age target. The only reason this isn't a 1 is that the "members" framing at least signals they want me to feel included — but it falls flat immediately.

## What's working

- The "adiclub" membership hook in the subject line does create a mild sense of belonging — "your time to shine" sounds personal enough that I opened it.
- The "ADICLUB OFFERS FOR YOU" section near the bottom at least gestures toward personalization — if the offers actually matched what I buy, this structure could work.
- The newsletter format is organized. I can see distinct sections without everything jumbling together.

## What's weak

- **Wrong brand entirely for me.** I'm a Skechers guy. I don't own adidas shoes. Even if this email were perfect, it's selling to the wrong customer.
- **FIFA World Cup Quarterfinal Giveaway** — I don't follow soccer. This does nothing for me.
- **Yeezy-inspired bottle + pro ball** — "Yeezy" means nothing to me. A pro ball is a sports item, not something a comfort-walker cares about.
- **Calvin Klein collab** — Random. I didn't come to a shoe brand for fashion crossovers.
- **Dark, high-contrast photography** — Hard to read on a phone. The images look like action sports. No men's casual comfort shoes in sight.
- **Small text throughout** — I'd need to pinch-zoom just to read the body copy. That's a deal-breaker on my phone.
- **"Unlock discounts on vouchers"** — Convoluted. Just tell me the discount. Don't make me unlock anything.
- **Points module** — Buried and unclear. How many points do I have? What can I do with them? Not answered.

## Recommendations

- 1. If adidas wants my attention, lead with casual men's footwear — walking shoes, slip-ons, comfort soles — not soccer giveaways.
- 2. Make the text bigger. This renders tiny on mobile and I'm not going to zoom in to read a promotional email.
- 3. Kill the Yeezy and Calvin Klein noise for a segment like me. That's for a 28-year-old streetwear guy, not a retired guy buying comfortable shoes.
- 4. Replace the giveaway module with a clear dollar-off or early-access offer tied to something I've actually bought or browsed.
- 5. If there are adiclub points to use, tell me my balance right at the top. That's the one hook that could keep me reading.

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard failures: a broken tracking link using an unsupported URL scheme, and a completely absent plain-text fallback. Four HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources also risk silent blocking in modern mail clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken click-tracking URL — unsupported scheme**

The QA probe returned `unknown url type: '/g/5023a20f...'` on the following redirect:

```
https://dv.adidas.com/o/5023a20f-e90a-4519-b846-f97d43689628?cp_tp=v3.eJzj...
```

The redirect target resolves to a relative path beginning with `/g/` rather than an absolute `https://` URL. This means the link will 404 or produce an error for any recipient who clicks it. The destination asset/product page needs to be verified and the redirect target corrected to an absolute URL.

**[WARN] 29 tracking links unverified**

All click-tracking links route through `click.comms.adidas.com`. The QA prober skipped HTTP probes on these, so destination URL health is unconfirmed. A targeted spot-check of the primary CTA links is recommended before send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] 6 images with HTTP sources (non-HTTPS)**

The arrow icon (`b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`) appears 4× and a tracking pixel appears once, all served over `http://`:

```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019dbaaa248a-.../w8Uy5KRwGBEM8FnARaOAC6WNN...
```

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy or block mixed-content images. The arrow icon will silently disappear for a significant portion of recipients. All image `src` values must use `https://`.

**[WARN] 2 images missing `alt` text**

- The hero/content image served from `dv.adidas.com` (UUID `5023a20f-...`) has no `alt` attribute.
- The tracking pixel from `click.comms.adidas.com` has no `alt` attribute.

Screen readers will either skip these or announce the raw URL. Hero images should carry meaningful `alt` text; tracking pixels should use `alt=""` (empty, explicit).

**`<meta charset>` malformed**

```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```

The correct form is `<meta charset="UTF-8">`. The current tag omits the `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute, making it non-functional as a charset declaration. While most clients default to UTF-8, this is technically incorrect and should be fixed.

**Duplicate `@font-face` blocks**

The custom font declarations (`AdihausDIN`, `AdineuePRO`) are repeated verbatim in two separate `<style>` blocks. This is dead weight — remove the duplicate block to reduce HTML payload.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is empty (0 chars)**

The multipart/alternative text part contains zero characters. CAN-SPAM does not mandate a text part, but its complete absence degrades deliverability scoring (SpamAssassin and similar rule sets penalize HTML-only messages) and breaks rendering in plain-text-only clients. A minimal text version with the key offer, primary CTA URL, and unsubscribe link must be present.

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM authentication status unverifiable**

The `Authentication-Results` header was not present in the captured headers (expected via the AgentMail relay). SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this audit. Verify that `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is covered by adidas's published SPF record and that DKIM is signing with a valid selector before send.

Unsubscribe link and physical mailing address were not flagged by QA — assumed present in the footer (consistent with the footer CSS classes visible in source). No issues found on those CAN-SPAM elements.

The broken redirect (`/g/5023a20f...`) means at least one destination URL is non-functional — continuity is severed for that link entirely.
