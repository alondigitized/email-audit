# The New Café Slipper Is Here!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Now this one I could actually get behind. A fuzzy slip-on slipper with a textured grippy sole and a "goes anywhere" pitch — that's exactly my Sunday-morning-coffee footwear. No mascots, no LEGO nonsense, no crocodile in a cubicle. Just a product, a diagram, and a plain-English pitch about comfort. That earns my attention, at least at a glance.
- Where it loses me is the same place a lot of these do — there's no price anywhere on this thing. I can see the slipper, I can see there's a "360° of boucle sherpa," but I have no idea if it's $25 or $60, and there's zero mention of sizing or whether they run true to size, which matters plenty when you're talking about a house slipper you'll wear daily. I'd click through mostly out of curiosity about the price, not because the email closed the deal.
- I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's a decent product email, not exciting enough to share — but I'd probably scroll it before archiving, which is better than most of what Crocs has sent me lately.

## What's working

- The pink cloud-swirl banner with "comfy inside and out" actually catches the eye without being obnoxious.
- The four colorway shot (black, tan, pink, white/grey) tells me at a glance what my options are.
- The callout diagram on the pink slipper — "360° of boucle sherpa" and "textured sole for added grip" — is the kind of concrete product detail I actually want, not fluff.
- "Cozy lining, slip-on comfort & perfect for the season ahead" speaks directly to what I look for: hands-free, cushioned, easy on-off.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no wall of text, easy to read on my phone without squinting.

## What's weak

- No price anywhere in the email. I want to know what I'm getting into before I tap a button.
- No sizing info or fit guidance — for a slipper, that's the first thing I'd want to know.
- The CTA buttons ("Shop Clogs," "Shop Jibbitz Charms," "Shop Sale," "Shop Sandals") don't even include a direct link to the Café Slipper itself — odd, since that's the whole point of the email.
- Nothing loyalty-related visible — I'm not sure if I'm getting any member perk here, or if this is just a general blast.
- The subject line teases "The New Café Slipper" but the preview text in my inbox was a string of garbled symbols — that's a miss before I even open it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price on the slipper, front and center — that's the single biggest thing missing for a comfort shopper like me weighing this against Skechers or Hoka options.
- 2. Fix that CTA row — add a "Shop Café Slippers" button that actually links to the featured product instead of making me hunt through clogs and sandals.
- 3. Add a line about sizing/fit ("true to size," "order half size up," etc.) since that's a real purchase-decision factor for slippers.
- 4. Fix the preview text — right now it's just junk symbols, not real copy, and that's a wasted opportunity to hook me before I even open the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the Café Slipper: Cozy Slip-On Comfort`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Boucle Slippers Just Landed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plush lining, grippy sole, slip-on ease — starting at $[price]`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The coziest way to end your day just got restocked`
- 5. If there's a loyalty discount or member-early-access angle, surface it — give me a reason this email is for me specifically, not just anyone on the list.
