# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- The skull sneaker is back. It's the same black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" I've had cart nags about on 11/05, 11/08, three times on 11/11, and again on 11/13 and 11/14. I have never put anything in a cart. "You liked these and you weren't wrong" is a stranger telling me what I think. Now the subject says my cart "expires soon," and there's no cart. Nothing in this email is actually mine.
- The bottom half is better. Slip-ins, arch fit, a Murette slipper and Respected boots are the kind of thing I'd look at. The Black Friday BOGO 50% banner is a real offer. But it sits below the fold, under a shoe I'd never wear, and the fine print says it excludes sale styles. I'd skim it, shrug and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Buy one, get one 50% off or 20% off a single pair" banner is a clear offer. The 11/12–11/17 dates are in the fine print, which at least gives me a deadline.
- The six "more great options" are mostly slip-ins and arch fit. Men's Respected boots and the Murette slipper are exactly my kind of shoe.
- The men's and women's category list is big and easy to tap with a thumb. The text is large enough to read.
- The app, curbside pickup and find-a-store tiles are useful, and I wouldn't mind having them.

## What's weak

- The hero is a skull-painted sneaker. It's not my style, I never touched it, and I've now seen it about eight times.
- "Your cart expires soon!" implies urgency about a cart I never made. That's a trust hit, since this is exactly the mismatch that makes me trust the brand less.
- "Complete your order" is the only big button, and there's no order to complete.
- The BOGO banner sits below the fold. The hero says nothing about the sale, so the email and the offer feel like two different emails.
- The fine print excludes sale styles, so the discount might not cover the shoes I'd actually buy. My points balance isn't mentioned either.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart-abandon emails to people who never carted. If there's no cart, send the Black Friday BOGO as the hero.
- 2. Lead with slip-ins and arch fit for men, in a bigger grid, with prices and star ratings so I can do the price-versus-comfort math.
- 3. Put the offer above the fold and tie the CTA to it, e.g. "Shop BOGO slip-ins".
- 4. Cut the cadence. Three cart nags a day teaches me to ignore the sender.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's arch fit, 2nd pair half off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free comfort, buy one get one 50% off through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-in boots and slippers, with cushioned arch support. Online only.`
