- I won't click and I definitely won't forward it — there's nothing here for a guy my age looking for a comfortable walking shoe. The size chart at the bottom (5 through 11, wide width, wide calf) confirms it's built entirely around women's footwear this round.

## What's weak

- Zero men's product anywhere — this is a women's animal-print fashion email start to finish.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, or slip-on ease — none of what actually gets me to buy a shoe.
- "SPOTTED: ANIMAL PRINT" is the same trend-report costume the last three or four emails wore, just a different pattern.

- 3. Add a men's comfort callout — arch support, slip-on, cushioned sole — somewhere in the body so I know there's a reason to open past the subject line.
