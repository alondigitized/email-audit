# When art meets adidas Originals, the Argentina FIFA World Cup 26™ Away Jersey
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-04-19**
## Executive summary

- Wrong store, wrong aisle, wrong customer. This is an adidas email selling an Argentina soccer jersey dressed up as an art exhibit. I'm a Skechers guy who shops for comfortable shoes I can slip on without bending over. I have zero idea why this landed in my inbox. The whole thing felt like I accidentally walked into a museum gift shop — dark moody photos, talk about "paintings across the nation" and "Argentinian expression." I scrolled through the whole thing waiting for something that applied to me. Never found it.
- Dead on arrival for me. Wrong brand, wrong product category, wrong emotional pitch.

## What's working

- **The 25% off hook is clear.** Even buried below all the art talk, "25% OFF ISN'T OVER YET" read cleanly. The discount and the code (EXCLUSIVE) are right there. If I were an adidas fan, I'd know what to do.
- **The top banner "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" with a SHOP NOW button** is simple enough. That part I understood on the first pass.

## What's weak

- **I'm not an adidas customer.** I shop Skechers. This email has no business being in my inbox.
- **Soccer jersey.** I don't follow soccer, don't own soccer gear, never searched for it. Why would I want an Argentina World Cup away jersey?
- **Art-forward copy fell flat.** "Inspired by drawings, paintings and every artistic expression" — I genuinely don't know what that has to do with a shirt. It felt like they were trying to make a jersey sound like a Picasso.
- **App exclusive sale is a wall.** They want me to download the adidas app to use the 25% off code. I'm not doing that. I'm not going to download a separate app for one discount on a product I didn't come looking for.
- **Text is small.** The body copy and especially the legal fine print at the bottom are tiny. On a phone, I'd need to pinch-zoom just to read the conditions.
- **Dark imagery is hard to read on a phone screen.** The Argentina jersey hero photo is dramatic but the text layered on top — "Argentina" repeated in stylized lettering — blends together.
- **No women's, no lifestyle, no footwear.** Not even an attempt to cross-sell something that might apply to a broader audience.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Don't send this to me.** Seriously — if I'm not an adidas purchaser or jersey searcher, this email is spam by another name. Tighten the list.
- 2. **Lead with the offer, not the art concept.** Move the 25% off above the fold. The artistic backstory can live lower for the people who care.
- 3. **Kill the app gate.** Or at least offer a web alternative. Forcing a 62-year-old to download an app to claim a discount is how you lose the sale.
- 4. **Increase font size** on body copy. Minimum 16px for a mobile email. The current size requires zooming.
- 5. **Add a "Shop Best Sellers" escape hatch** earlier — if the jersey doesn't click, give me somewhere else to go that might.

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has one confirmed broken CTA link and a missing plain-text fallback — both are deliverability/experience-breaking issues. Eight image assets are served over HTTP and will be silently blocked by most modern email clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken redirect — malformed inner URL**
The QA probe on the primary hero/CTA link detected `unknown url type: '/g/f329ccdd-906b-4b31-86da-2af6cc29265a'`. The outer `dv.adidas.com` click-redirect wrapper contains a relative path as its destination instead of an absolute URL. Recipients clicking this link will land on a broken or empty page.

- Outer tracking URL: `https://dv.adidas.com/o/f329ccdd-906b-4b31-86da-2af6cc29265a?cp_tp=v3.eJzj...`
- Inner destination resolves to: `/g/f329ccdd-...` (relative — invalid)

**[WARN] 36 tracking links not verified**
All click links route through `click.comms.adidas.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect domain). Destination URLs and UTM parameter integrity could not be confirmed without following redirects.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] 8× HTTP image asset — will be blocked**
The arrow icon (`b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`) is referenced 8 times via plain HTTP:
```
src="http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg"
```
Gmail, Outlook 365, and Apple Mail all block mixed-content images. All instances need `https://`.

**[WARN] Tracking pixel served over HTTP**
```
src="http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019da422d417-6ab62f85-f9ba-4494-9af1-cf26c6f36fba-000000/Yp9urS-..."
```
Open-tracking pixel will fail silently in clients that enforce HTTPS, producing inaccurate open-rate data.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on 2 images**
- Hero image: `f329ccdd-906b-4b31-86da-2af6cc29265a` — `alt=""` or absent
- Tracking pixel: `Yp9urS-tHhnQCICsUVoT8z_wk...` — `alt=""` acceptable for tracking pixels but flag the hero

**[WARN] Malformed charset meta tag**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Correct form:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Without `http-equiv`, some legacy clients (Outlook 2007–2016) may misread encoding.

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` declarations**
`AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are declared twice across two separate `<style>` blocks with identical `src` values. No functional breakage, but adds ~800 bytes of unnecessary payload.

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 characters**
QA reports `Text version is 0 chars`. A missing multipart/alternative plain-text part:
- Triggers spam filters (SpamAssassin, Proofpoint) that penalize HTML-only messages
- Breaks rendering in text-only clients and some corporate mail gateways
- May constitute a CAN-SPAM violation if the unsubscribe mechanism is only in the HTML part

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM authentication status unverified**
`Authentication-Results` header was not present via the AgentMail relay. Sender domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com` (SFMC subdomain). Cannot confirm DKIM signature or SPF alignment from available data.
