- The photo is mostly women's boots and heels. The one guy looks like a 25-year-old in yellow pants, and nothing there looks like me.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/SendGrid-style send with click-wrapped links through `ablink.dsw.com`. Automated QA found one hard failure: the SMS opt-in CTA returned HTTP 400. Authentication, footer compliance and landing-page UTMs couldn't be verified from the truncated source or the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] The SMS opt-in CTA is broken (HTTP 400).** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off", and the URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/4HJSEtoMSuBfh8HO8ch_Sw~~/AAQRxRA~/MPpjdOdQn7lL…`. This is the only confirmed broken link, and it sits on a conversion CTA.
  - The 400 comes from the tracking redirect itself, not from the destination.
  - Probe artifacts such as an unrecognized user agent or a missing recipient-specific token could cause it. A manual click from a real client is needed to rule that out.
  - The QA output also shows an empty value before the link-text field, so the link's `title` or `alt` may be blank.
- **Link tracking is long.** The opt-in URL alone is roughly 700 characters. This feeds the deliverability warning below.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 68 links were probed, and 56 were skipped as footer, utility or social links. The remaining links have no QA verdict, so "no other broken links" can't be asserted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Two images have no `alt` attribute:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/-Yxo_liDv7ZGoFlWSO5lhQ~~/AAQRxRA~/Xsl-4Po8…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - Both look like open-tracking pixels (`/q/` path and `o.gif`), so the screen-reader impact is low. Adding `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` would clear the warning.
- **Two head `<meta>` tags are malformed.** Both are in the visible source:
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Neither tag does anything as written. The charset is normally covered by the MIME `Content-Type` header, so the impact is minor.
- **No other rendering issues are evidenced in the visible portion.** The viewport meta, dark-mode `@media (prefers-color-scheme)` rules, MSO resets and web-font fallbacks are present. The HTML is truncated, so body markup (alt text on content images, table roles, preheader) wasn't reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `${}`, `%%`) are visible in the supplied source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a capture limitation, not evidence of a sender misconfiguration. The `From` domain is `e.dsw.com`, a subdomain separate from the click domain `ablink.dsw.com`, so alignment should be checked from raw headers.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (22,162 of 27,689 characters). This is a spam-filter heuristic risk, driven by the long wrapped tracking URLs.
- **Unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, the footer unsubscribe link and the physical address are not visible in the truncated source or QA output. The probe note says an unsubscribe link is always included, but no result is reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be evaluated.** All probed links are opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the final destination URLs weren't captured, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `mi.dsw.com/…/o.gif?mi_ecmp=20261125_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` shows a campaign ID in the query string, so campaign attribution looks wired up.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or re-test the SMS opt-in CTA.** Open the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/4HJSEtoMSuBfh8HO8ch_Sw~~` link manually. If it still returns 400, regenerate it in the ESP. It is the highest-value broken element.
2. Add `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels.
3. Correct the two head metas to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Shorten the plain-text part. Use a descriptive text body with fewer, shorter links, or reduce tracking-URL length.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers. That would let it verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, `List-Unsubscribe`, footer compliance, and resolved landing-page URLs with UTMs.
