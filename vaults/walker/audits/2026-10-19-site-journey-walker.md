# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-19**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I'm Walker, 62, on my phone, looking for something cushioned and slip-on. The front of the site speaks my language. The top bar has chips for **Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit and Max Cushioning**, the Men's page has a "Hands Free Slip-Ins" tile, and a search for "comfort shoes" gave me sensible comfort shoes. But I couldn't finish the one thing I came to do. The Add to Cart step stopped on a "Please select a size" error. The cart page then came up as a **completely blank white screen**. The sizes I'd probably wear (10 to 11) were also crossed out. I never got to a cart or a price, so the money-saving offers never paid off for me. I would go back to the Hoka or Brooks site, or the DSW aisle.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10**, the same as yesterday.

The browsing and search half of the site is solid, and that's why this isn't lower. It stays at 3 because the checkout half is broken. A blank cart means no sale. This shopper will not come back to see whether it fixes itself.

## 3. What's Working

- **Comfort shortcuts at the top of the homepage.** "Slip-ins · Wide Fit · Arch Fit · Max Cus…" tells me right away that they sell what I want. This is the best thing on the site for me.
- **The Men's menu is simple.** The big bold list (Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections…) has a clear Back button and an X. The rows are tall and easy to tap, and I never got lost.
- **Men's Shoes starts with my kind of shoe.** The category tiles lead with "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes."
- **Product page basics are clear.**
  - The price is large ($88.00) and there's a 4.5-star rating with 68 reviews.
  - Payment-plan options are spelled out.
  - The Slip-ins hands-free badge is prominent.
- **Search worked.** "comfort shoes" gave 1,619 results, and the first ones were Slip-ins and Arch Comfort styles. Those are exactly what I wanted.
- **The Add to Cart button is big.** It's wide, dark blue and impossible to miss.

## 4. What's Weak

- **The cart page is blank white.** There's no header, no message and no spinner. For someone my age that looks like the site crashed, and I'd stop trusting it.
- **Add to Cart failed.** I tapped it and got "Please select a size." That is fair on its own. But the size error sits above the button and the page doesn't scroll or highlight it, so it's easy to miss.
- **Most sizes are crossed out.** Only 8.0, 8.5 and 9.0 look available. Many of the rest have a small bell icon, and I couldn't tell what it meant. Nothing explains it or suggests another shoe that comes in my size.
- **Confusing discounts.** One banner says "20% OFF Sitewide – Applied at Cart." The one below it says "2 Pairs for $100" and the product page says "20% OFF for Members!" Which one do I get? I can't do the price math until I reach a cart that works.
- **The promo banner changed between steps.** At step 2 it read "25% off Apparel & Bags" and at step 3 it was back to "2 Pairs for $100." Seeing that on a phone makes me wonder what's true.
- **Small text.** The promo bars, the "Details" links and the filter and sort chips are small. The product page is fine, but the stacked banner text on top is hard to read without zooming.
- **The homepage is a lifestyle photo with no copy.** There's a young crowd in an alley, and no shoe, headline, price or "comfort" message above the fold. I don't see myself in it.
- **No brand comparison or value math.** There's nothing like "cushion vs. Hoka" or "cost per year of wear." I have to work that out myself.
- **The login step looks identical to the homepage.** I saw no sign-in form, no "Hi, Walker," and the cart badge stayed at 0. The product page still said "Sign in for free shipping & returns!" so nothing felt personalized.
- **The filter row is cut off.** "Width" and "Size" are on screen, but the row scrolls sideways and the filters I care about (width, arch support) are partly hidden.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the blank cart first.** It should always show a header and either the items or a clear "Your cart is empty" message. Find out why it rendered nothing after a failed add.
2. **Make the size error obvious.** Scroll to the size grid, outline it in red and keep the "Add to Cart" button visible. Better yet, preselect the first available size, or show "Select a size" on the button itself.
3. **Explain sold-out sizes.** Say what the bell icon means ("Notify me"), and say "Out of stock in your size? Try these similar Slip-ins" with real links.
4. **Pick one deal message.** Show a single clear offer near the price, for example "You pay $70.40 after 20% off," instead of three overlapping banners.
5. **Put comfort on the homepage.** Use a hero with a shoe, a plain headline such as "Slip in. Walk all day." and one large button to the Slip-ins and Arch Fit pages.
6. **Increase text and tap size.** The promo text should be at least 16px, and the filter chips and links should be 44px tall or more.
7. **Add a comfort filter and short guide.** Filter by "Arch support / Cushioning / Hands-free." A small "How we compare" block with honest price-per-wear math would help me decide.
8. **Make login visible.** After signing in, show the first name in the header and a banner for points or free shipping.

## 6. Bottom Line

Skechers has the right shoes and the right words on the front, but a shopper like me can't buy them. A failed add-to-cart followed by a blank cart is a dead end. When the cart doesn't load, a loyalty email or a discount doesn't matter, and the shoe I can actually order wins. Right now that shoe is probably a competitor's.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

- **Evidence is limited.** LCP and CLS were not captured on any of the 8 steps. The HTML samples are truncated, so `<title>`, meta tags, structured data and the viewport meta could not be checked.
- **Bot mitigation interfered with the run.** Kasada (`x-kpsdk`) fingerprint requests returned 429 five times, and `/cart/` itself returned 429. The "View Cart" findings therefore describe an error response, not the real cart.
- **The journey may not have navigated.** The samples for "Log In" and "Men category" report URL `https://www.skechers.com/` and have the same `<html>` and head markup as the homepage. Their results may be duplicates of the homepage.
- **Homepage a11y is the real problem.** There are 2 critical violations and 1 serious one, plus structural landmark errors, which suggests nested or duplicated `<main>` elements.
- **Third-party load is heavy.** The head shows about 30 async third-party tags, and GTM is loading a non-live environment.

## Accessibility

17 axe violations across 3 steps.

**Homepage (9)**
- **`aria-required-parent` (critical):** an element with a child-only role (e.g. `listitem`, `tab`, `menuitem`) is not inside its required parent. This is likely a menu or carousel built with ARIA.
- **`button-name` (critical):** one or more buttons have no accessible text, probably icon-only buttons (menu, search, cart, carousel controls) missing `aria-label`.
- **`link-name` (serious):** links have no discernible text, probably image or icon links.
- **`landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` (moderate):** together these point to more than one `<main>` or `role="main"`, with landmarks nested inside other landmarks.
- **`landmark-unique` (moderate):** landmarks of the same type share the same label.
- **`region` (moderate):** content sits outside any landmark. Likely sources are injected third-party widgets (TrustArc `truste_*` elements, Pulse Insights, Monetate).
- **`aria-allowed-role` (minor):** a `role` value is invalid for its element.

**Search "comfort shoes" (4)**
- `aria-allowed-role` (minor), `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` (moderate), `landmark-unique` (moderate), `region` (moderate). These are the same template-level issues as the homepage and probably come from the shared header and footer.

**View Cart (4)**
- `document-title` (serious), `html-has-lang` (serious), `landmark-one-main` (moderate), `page-has-heading-one` (moderate). The 429 response is the likely cause. A bare error body has no title, lang, `main` or `h1`. This is not a confirmed defect in the cart template.
- If the 429 page is what a blocked user sees, it still needs those four basics.

**Not reported**
- Log In, Men, Men > Shoes, Product detail and Add to Cart have no violations listed. Given the URL issue above, don't treat this as clean until each step is confirmed to be a distinct page state.

## SEO

- **Homepage:** `<html lang="en">` is present.
- **Cart:** the 429 response has no `lang` and no `<title>`. If crawlers get this response, they get no indexable signals. The cart should be `noindex` anyway.
- **Rate limiting:** a 429 on a top-level document URL can cause crawlers to back off. Confirm that known search bots are allowlisted in the bot-mitigation layer.
- **Not assessable from the supplied data:** the title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph tags, structured data and heading hierarchy on the other pages. The `page-has-heading-one` failure applies only to the cart's 429 response.

## Mobile Optimization

- The `--vh: 6.64px` custom property implies a viewport height of about 664 px, so the run used a mobile-sized viewport.
- The viewport meta tag and touch-target sizes cannot be verified from the truncated HTML. Axe's `target-size` rule was not reported.
- Icon-only buttons and links without names (`button-name`, `link-name`) matter most on mobile, where icons replace text labels.
- A JS-driven `--vh` workaround is in use. It suggests the CSS may not use `dvh`/`svh` units.

## Console & Network Errors

**Console**
- 24 errors were reported, but only 10 were listed, so the other 14 could not be reviewed. The listed ones fall into three types:
  - **`429` on resource load (3×):** these match the Kasada fingerprint requests below.
  - **`Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed` (4×):** a script is registering an `unload` handler (probably a third-party tag) on a page whose Permissions-Policy blocks it. `unload` handlers also disable bfcache in most browsers.
  - **`Unsupported Summarizer API languages were specified` (3×):** the page or a script calls Chrome's built-in Summarizer API without a supported `outputLanguage`. The request is aborted. This is most likely from a third-party or injected script.

**Network**
- **Kasada bot-detection fingerprint, `/…/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.904` (5×):** it returned 429 every time. The anti-bot challenge is being rate-limited or rejecting the automated client. This is the likely cause of the cart 429 and may be the cause of the unchanged page states.
- **`/cart/` (1×):** it returned 429, so the cart could not be audited.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the journey harness first.** Confirm that each step navigates (record the URL after each click), retry or back off on 429, and rerun the cart step. Otherwise, steps 2 through 8 are unreliable.
2. **Fix LCP/CLS capture.** Register `PerformanceObserver` with `buffered: true` for `largest-contentful-paint` and `layout-shift` before navigation, and read the values after `visibilitychange` or page hide.
3. **Homepage a11y:**
   - Add accessible names to icon-only buttons and links (`aria-label` or visually hidden text).
   - Fix the `aria-required-parent` violation.
   - Reduce the page to a single top-level `<main>`.
   - Give same-type landmarks unique `aria-label`s.
   - Remove invalid `role` values.
4. **Search page:** fix the shared header and footer landmark and role issues, since they also appear on the homepage.
5. **Error and cart responses:** make sure any 429 or error document includes `<title>`, `<html lang>`, a `<main>` and an `<h1>`. Keep the cart `noindex`.
6. **Bot mitigation:** review the Kasada rate limits for legitimate traffic and search crawlers. Allowlist the monitoring client or give it a sanctioned bypass.
7. **Third-party hygiene:**
   - Verify that the GTM `env-1` preview parameters are intentional in production.
   - Remove or replace the `unload` listener with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`.
   - Find the source of the Summarizer API call and either supply a supported `outputLanguage` or remove it.
   - Defer non-critical marketing tags until after interaction or idle.
   - Add SRI where tags are pinned, and self-host or preload the web fonts.
8. **Re-run with full HTML** so the title, meta, canonical, structured data and viewport meta can be audited.
