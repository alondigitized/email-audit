**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- I like this one better than the cart nags I've been getting all week. "Hi, Walker," my Member status and 1000 points are at the top, and then a plain "20% OFF" banner with a $5 reward. It's the same 20% sitewide offer as the 10/22 "Your weekend just got better!" send, so the offer isn't new. The email is just dressed up in fall colors this time.
- The hero is a grid of leather, suede and sherpa swatches. It's pretty, but I had to squint to read "Fall's Top Textures" because the words run over the swatches. Then four shoes in a row: a navy leather sneaker, a brown suede loafer, a sherpa-lined boot, and a corduroy slip-in. The suede loafer and the slip-in are the two that look like me. I'd tap through, but I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- "Hi, Walker" with Member status and 1000 points up top treats me like a known customer. The $5 reward line is a nice touch.
- The 20% OFF banner is big and has a clear number. I don't have to hunt for it.
- The suede loafer is a slip-on style I'd actually wear, and so is the corduroy Slip-ins shoe. They're hands-free, which is what I care about.
- One shoe per section with a plain SHOP button under each is easy to follow on my phone.
- "Crafted for comfort" in the hero copy fits what I'm shopping for.

## What's weak

- The hero text sits on top of the swatches, so "FALL'S" and "TEXTURES" are cut up and hard to read.
- The SHOP buttons are small outlined boxes with thin text. They're not great for my thumb or my eyes.
- The offer terms are tiny gray print at the bottom, and I can't read them without zooming. The "see below for details" note on the banner sends me down there.
- No prices, no cushioning or arch-support talk, and no reviews. It's all looks and no comfort details.
- The preview text is a long web link instead of real copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the comfort story up front. Tell me which of these have cushioned insoles or arch support, and which are Slip-ins. That's what makes me click.
- 2. Make the SHOP buttons bigger and filled, with specific labels like "Shop suede slip-ons."
- 3. Fix the hero so the headline sits on a clean background.
- 4. Show a price or a "from $X after 20% off" under each shoe.
- 5. Fix the preheader so it's real copy and not a link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-personalization defect (empty greeting, `Hi ,`) and several markup and pixel problems: a scheme-less tracking pixel URL, two `http://` images without alt text, and malformed `<meta>` tags. SPF/DKIM could not be verified and the unsubscribe footer was not in the truncated source, so those items are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1" />` has no scheme. Clients will resolve it as a relative path, so it will 404 and never register. It also has no `alt`.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_10242026` is plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. It is also missing `alt` (QA WARN ×2).
- **Third-party identifier sharing:** the three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels pass `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00` (a 64-hex SHA-256 value) and `partner_uid=121173391` (a subscriber ID) to a third-party domain. Whatever the hash is derived from, it is shared with an external party. This is a privacy and disclosure point, not a functional break.
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped 34 tracking links (click-redirect domains), so destination status codes and redirect chains are unknown.
- The first-party open pixel `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` blocks user text scaling on iOS.
- **Broken meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so both are non-functional. The charset declaration is ineffective, though the Content-Type header likely covers it.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** `o.gif` and `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The PNG is on a non-HTTPS host and may be a content image, which would render as a blank or broken block if blocked.
- **Document structure:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute. The outer layout tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`. `<title>` is `Skechers®`, not the subject line.
- **Pixel containers:** the `display:none` open-pixel wrapper and the `width="1" height="1"` table are harmless, but both depend on clients not stripping them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The first-name field is empty and has no fallback. This is likely a missing AMPscript default or a blank `FirstName` on the subscriber record. No unrendered `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were reported by QA.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN):** the header is absent because the message was captured via the AgentMail relay. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, and this is a capture limitation, not a confirmed sender failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the truncated source doesn't include the footer, so the unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers could not be checked. No issue is asserted.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`. This is not itself a violation, since the unsubscribe mechanism is what CAN-SPAM requires.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** all 34 links are behind click-redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- **Campaign ID consistent:** `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_10242026` appears in both the `krxd.net` and `ink1000.com` pixels, so campaign tagging is consistent across vendors.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a greeting fallback**, for example `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstName),"Hi there","Hi "+@firstName)=%%`, or drop the name from the greeting when the field is blank.
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel** to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`, and add `alt=""`.
3. **Serve all images over HTTPS**: `image.emails.skechers.com` and `www.ink1000.com` (or remove the ink1000 pixel if it doesn't support TLS). Add `alt=""` to decorative and tracking images, and descriptive alt text to `dde00662-….png` if it carries content.
4. **Fix the `<meta>` tags** with `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`. Use `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%` if needed.
6. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
7. **Review the `krxd.net` identifier sharing** against the privacy policy.
8. **Re-run QA with full source and raw headers** to cover unsubscribe, `List-Unsubscribe`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and a resolved check of the 34 tracked links with UTM parameters.
