# Ends Tonight! 30% off select boots, slippers, & outdoor
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- Two different deals are fighting for my attention here, and that's my first complaint. The big green hero says "Members take 20% off sitewide." Halfway down, a blue banner says 30% off boots, outdoor and slippers. Subject line and hero don't match, and I trust a brand less when the email doesn't match what I was promised. I got this same 30% offer earlier today in "Ends Tonight! 30% off select boots, slippers, & outdoor," and the 7/10 I gave that one was mostly for having a real offer.
- The "Slip-Ins" and "Cozy Fit" checklist in the hero is the best thing here. Slip-ins are the exact hands-free thing I look for. Putting the gift-wrap art in front of it makes it feel like a card, not a shoe sale. I'd probably open it and scroll, but I'd click only if the slip-in tile took me straight to men's.
- Would I forward it? No. Would I click? Maybe, once. It's the fourth Skechers email today, and the "Last Chance" tag feels stale when I've seen "Ends Tonight" twice.

## What's working

- "Hi, Walker" and the member bar ("1000 points, enough for a $5 reward") show they know who I am.
- The "Shop Slip-Ins" and "Shop Cozy Fit" tiles speak to what I actually shop for.
- The men's size buttons are big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The big "30% OFF" and "20% OFF" numbers are readable without zooming.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" tells me I don't need a code.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers, 20% and 30%, with no clear winner, and I can't tell if they stack.
- The 20% is for Plus members only, and the fine print says Skechers Plus. I'm marked "Member," so I don't know if I qualify.
- The four product photos show a women's-looking shearling bootie and a few boots. Only one or two look like me, and there are no prices.
- The pile of gift boxes is decoration, not product, and it pushes the shoes below the fold.
- The legal text at the bottom is tiny, and it's where the real conditions are buried.
- Slip-ins, my favorite item, get just one small tile, and arch support and cushioning aren't mentioned anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer, and make the subject and the hero say the same thing. If 20% is for members, say "Members: 20% off" in the subject.
- 2. Show men's slip-ins in the hero with a price, and mention cushioning or arch support.
- 3. Put my size row and a men's link right under the hero, before the second banner.
- 4. Say once, in plain words, whether the 20% and 30% deals can be combined.
- 5. Cut the extra sends today and make the deadline honest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: 20% off slip-ins, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, your 1,000 points + 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free slip-ins and cushioned boots, 30% off through tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-personalization defect (empty greeting, `"Hi ,"`) and several tracking-pixel and head-markup defects. The pixel defects are a scheme-less `returnpath` pixel and two `http://` images without alt text. Auth headers, unsubscribe and UTM parameters could not be verified because the HTML was truncated and the 46 click links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking pixel never fires. It also has no `alt` attribute.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SITESELECT_NONPURCH_EN_11102026` loads over plain HTTP. It can be blocked by clients or proxies that upgrade or block mixed content. It carries a subscriber identifier (`mi_u`) in cleartext.
- **Third-party pixels:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) sit in the hidden block. They have no `alt` attribute at all, and `width="0" height="0"`. The `usermatch` pixel's `partner_uid` is a 64-hex value, apparently a SHA-256 hash, and the other `usermatch` pixel's is the numeric subscriber ID `121173391`. Both identifiers are sent to a third party.
- **Skipped probes:** 46 tracking links on `click.emails.skechers.com` were skipped, so their destinations are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declaration is valid. The charset is not actually declared in the head.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` also disables text resizing in WebKit.
- **Missing alt:** QA flagged `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`) and `o.gif`. The `.png` is loaded over `http://` and may be blocked. It looks like a content image, so missing alt there is a real accessibility gap.
- **Table and language markup:** The layout tables visible in the excerpt (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table width="1" height="1" …>`) have no `role="presentation"`. `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Not assessable:** I can't check the rest of the body, since the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The QA check found `"Hi ,"`, so the first-name field rendered empty. This is a real broken-experience defect. The failing element is in the truncated portion, so I can't quote the source. Either the merge field has no fallback, or the test subscriber has no `FirstName` value. The email needs a default value, or the greeting should be dropped when the name is empty.
- No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the excerpt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** The QA run couldn't find this header, which it attributes to the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `emails.skechers.com` is therefore unknown, not failing.
- **Not verifiable:** Unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and physical postal address are all outside the truncated HTML. I can't confirm or deny them.
- **Privacy:** The hashed-ID and subscriber-ID beacons to `krxd.net`, plus the `ink1000.com` ID, are third-party data sharing. Whether that is disclosed is a privacy-policy question that this audit can't resolve.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID consistent:** The same campaign ID `PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SITESELECT_NONPURCH_EN_11102026` appears in both the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels, so pixel-level attribution is aligned.
- **UTMs unverified:** Click links go through `click.emails.skechers.com` (SFMC), and none were probed. UTM params and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the empty-greeting fallback so it renders a default value or omits the greeting.
2. Add `https://` to the `pixel.app.returnpath.net` pixel, or remove it if the seed-tracking program is retired. Add `alt=""`.
3. Switch `http://www.ink1000.com/...o.gif` and `http://image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-…png` to HTTPS. Add `alt=""` to the pixel and meaningful alt text to the PNG.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `lang` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
6. Re-run the audit against the full HTML plus raw headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`) and follow a sample of redirect chains to check UTMs and landing pages.
