# Remember that 20% off? It ends *tonight*
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the ninth or so send on the same HAYRIDE 20% code since "20% OFF?! You better be-leaf it" on the 8th. This morning's "20% off ends tonight!" already said it ends tonight, and now this one says "Remember that 20% off? It ends *tonight*." That's the same deadline twice in one day, and it makes me trust the deadline less. The green 20% OFF* banner at the top is big and I can read it on my phone without zooming. I'll give them that.
- Below the banner it's a long scroll of women's heels, flats, and booties. The men's shoes are mostly sneakers, and the only men's options are a tiny "Shop Men" link and a "Shop Everything" button. I didn't see any slip-on or cushioned walking shoes. Nothing says arch support or comfort.
- I'd skim it and not click. I wouldn't forward it. If I needed shoes tonight I'd go to Hoka or Brooks, where the comfort is the point. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The big green "20% OFF*" banner with the HAYRIDE code is easy to read on my phone, and it's the first thing I see.
- The "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is clear, and the code is shown right there in the email.
- The "Shop by size" block with half sizes and a "Shop Wide Width" button is useful. I have wide feet, so that gets a nod.
- The free shipping, in-store pickup, and Afterpay icons at the bottom are simple and tell me how to buy.

## What's weak

- Almost all of it is women's heels, flats, and booties. I'm a 62-year-old man and I scrolled past most of it.
- Men's shoes get one small "SHOP MEN" text link, and the sneaker section is a handful of fashion-style trainers. There are no slip-ons or walking shoes, and there's no mention of cushioning or arch support.
- It repeats the same HAYRIDE 20% code I've seen all week, and "ends tonight" has now been said at least twice today.
- The fine print block at the bottom is long and tiny, and the "regular-price styles only" exclusion is easy to miss. Skechers and a lot of other brands are excluded, so the offer feels less honest.
- It's a very long scroll, with seven or eight different sections on a phone. The headline voice ("THEY'RE JUST SOOOO YOU," "gotta want") sounds like it's written for somebody much younger than me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the send. Same code, ninth time. Send one reminder, not two on the same day.
- 2. Put men's comfort shoes up top. Show slip-ons, walking shoes, and cushioned styles for men with a "Shop Men's Comfort" button, so I'm not hunting for the men's link.
- 3. Tell me the deadline in clear terms, like "Ends 11:59 PM tonight", and say which brands are excluded right in the banner, not in the fine print.
- 4. Shorten the email to two or three sections, with larger tap buttons.
- 5. Segment by what I've bought or browsed. Don't send me the heels.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walking shoes, slip-ons and wide widths. Code HAYRIDE ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built XHTML template with Movable Ink personalization. It has one confirmed failing link, the SMS opt-in CTA, and a few accessibility and head-markup defects visible in the source. The HTML is truncated and only 12 of 80 links were probed, so body, footer, header and UTM checks are partly unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (HTTP 400):** the CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns 400.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/ASmt0fypkKU7ezt8f-uOgw~~/AAQRxRA~/v-xfpVYf40an…` (about 700 chars of encoded redirect token).
  - A 400 from a click-tracking redirect usually means a malformed or expired token, or a probe the tracker rejects. A bot-rejection false positive can't be ruled out from this data, so check it with a real send-to-device click.
  - If it is genuinely broken, it is a high-priority fix. This is the SMS acquisition CTA, so every click dead-ends.
- **Link text/label missing from QA output:** the failure line shows an empty label before the link text. Check that the anchor isn't wrapping an image without alt text.
- **Coverage gap:** 68 of 80 links were skipped (footer, social and utility links). Their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/zZ5DCcNHEA8o0mLYSApbiA~~/…`: a `/q/` path on the tracking domain, likely the open-tracking pixel.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261011_P260598_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_2`: a Movable Ink pixel.
  - Both are tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. This is low severity.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute:** the source shows `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom on some clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv`.
  - Both tags are no-ops. The charset declaration is ineffective, though the Content-Type header usually covers it.
- **Generic `<title>`:** it is just "DSW". This is minor.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs are 32,048 of 36,942 characters (about 87%). The long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking tokens dominate, which is a spam-filter risk. Add a plain-text-specific short-link or reduced-link version.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered Liquid or merge tokens. The Movable Ink pixel renders with a populated `mi_u=225090073` and `mi_ecmp` value. The rest of the body was truncated, so tokens there can't be confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found in the evidence provided.
- The QA tool always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure on it.
- The sender `dsw@e.dsw.com` and tracking host `ablink.dsw.com` are both on the `dsw.com` domain, so link and sender domains line up.
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` headers were not supplied, and the physical address in the footer fell in the truncated HTML. These are unverified rather than passing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data. All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the destination URLs and UTM parameters are hidden behind the encoded tokens. The probe results do not report the final landing URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or re-test the SMS opt-in link** (400) from a real client. If it fails, regenerate the Braze link or replace it with a direct opt-in URL.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels (`ablink.dsw.com/q/…` and `mi.dsw.com/…/o.gif`).
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Correct the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Reduce the plain-text URL density with fewer or shorter links, plus real text content.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. That would cover the footer, the unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe` headers, authentication results, and the destination-URL UTM resolution.
