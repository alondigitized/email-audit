# Restocked faves ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "Okay, the Bondi is back." The maroon hero with huge pink "Restocked faves" is easy to read on my phone, and the Bondi SR and Bondi 7 are the cushioned walkers I actually buy. But the email never says which shoes or sizes were restocked, so "These colors and sizes flew off the shelves" tells me nothing. Four shoes in a grid, with no prices, is a guessing game.
- The real offer, up to 40% off sale styles, is buried at the bottom in a black block after a big blue shoe photo. I had to scroll a long way to find it. That's the same Black Friday head start I got from HOKA yesterday ("Black Friday head start," "Leaders of the pack"). It's the third near-identical send in about three days, and I'm tired of it.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the Bondi SR, since it's my kind of shoe. I'd forward it to nobody. I'd probably read it and move on.

## What's working

- The "Restocked faves" headline is huge and readable without zooming.
- The Bondi SR (men's, black) and Bondi 7 are in the grid. That's the cushioned walker I buy.
- The men's and women's labels under each shoe are clear, so I know which are mine.
- The "Shop Now" button is a big, easy tap target.
- The "Unlock Free Shipping" and "FLY for 30 Guarantee" links near the bottom build some trust.

## What's weak

- There's no price, no sale mark, and no sizes on any of the four shoes. "Restocked" doesn't tell me what's actually available.
- The 40% off offer is at the very bottom, under a big decorative photo. It should be near the top.
- Half the grid is Clifton 11, a lighter running shoe. I walk, I don't run.
- The footer text and the legal print are tiny and low contrast on blue. I can't read them without zooming.
- It repeats the Black Friday message I got yesterday, so it feels like spam from the same sender.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "Up to 40% off sale styles" in the hero or right under it, and show a sale price on each shoe in the grid.
- 2. Say which sizes are back, for example "Bondi SR back in men's 8–13."
- 3. Link each shoe to its own product page. Add star ratings and a "cushion level" tag so walkers can compare.
- 4. Lead with the Bondi for walkers, and use a "walking and all-day comfort" section instead of Cliftons.
- 5. Make the footer text larger and higher contrast.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bondi SR is back in your size`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Back in stock + up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bondi and Clifton restocked, plus sale styles up to 40% off through 11/26.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned walkers are back. Sizes go fast, so shop today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built entirely from images and has no plain-text part. Its only two probed links, return-start and Unsubscribe, returned 403 to the QA probe. Auth status and the remaining 11 links could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probed links returned 403.**
  - These are the `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` link and the **Unsubscribe** link, both wrapped in Cordial click-tracker URLs (`https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6af4859b…/2eeed8a5` and `…/a3a81a86`).
  - Decoding the `jwtP` payload shows the destinations are `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261124_PM_STO_RestockedFaves_…` and `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&…`.
  - A 403 on a signed JWT tracker most likely means the ESP is blocking the automated probe. The data doesn't show whether real users are affected. Re-test with a browser-like client, because a failing unsubscribe link would be a compliance problem.
- **Probe coverage is low.** 11 of 12 material links were skipped, including the hero, product and CTA links. Their health is unknown.
- **Hashed email in landing URLs.** The decoded destinations carry `hmail=9153f33f…` alongside `cordial_id`. This is not a defect, but it puts an identifier in landing-page URLs and downstream analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 15 images**, per the QA data.
  - 13 are content images: `H_US_Hero_RestockedFaves_2.jpg`, `H_US_Subcopy_Hero_RestockedFaves_1.jpg`, `H_US_CTA_Hero_RestockedFaves_1.jpg`, `H_US_Headline_Merch_RestockedFaves_2.jpg`, the four product tiles (Clifton 11, Bondi 7), the `H_US_*_BFEA_*` images and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - The headline, subcopy and CTA are separate images, so with images blocked or in a screen reader the email has no readable message or call to action.
  - The other two are tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…`, `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`). They should carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Charset meta is malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it doesn't declare the encoding.
- **Duplicate, conflicting viewport metas.** One sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom, and the other sets `user-scalable=yes`. Behavior varies by client.
- **Invalid CSS.** The source has `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`. Both have empty values and are ignored.
- **Web fonts.** The `@font-face` fonts load from `hoka.com`, and most mail clients ignore them. Fallback stacks weren't visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The truncated HTML can't confirm this for the full body.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part missing (0 chars).** This hurts deliverability and fails the multipart best practice.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC unverified.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, because the AgentMail relay strips it. This is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender fault.
- **Unsubscribe link exists** (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`) but its 403 probe result needs a manual check. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address were not provided, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Decoded URLs carry consistent UTMs: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261124_PM_STO_RestockedFaves_A3Day_HighestEngaged_30BrowseCart_LYAnniOpeners_90DayNonEmailOpeners_1125Openers_WishlistAdd`.
- The campaign value is very long and encodes segmentation (the audience definition). It is not a defect, but it is exposed in URLs.
- Landing alignment for the product and CTA links is unverified because those links were not probed.
- `utm_content` is absent in the decoded links, so per-module click attribution isn't possible from UTMs alone.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the Unsubscribe and return-start trackers with a browser-like User-Agent and confirm they resolve. Probe the hero, CTA and product links too.
2. Add a plain-text multipart alternative that includes the unsubscribe URL.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to all 13 content images, since the headline, subcopy and CTA are images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
4. Fix the head: use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, set a real `<title>`, keep a single viewport meta and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove the empty-value CSS declarations (`padding-top:px`, `border-top:px solid`).
6. Check the raw headers for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `emails.hoka.com` and for `List-Unsubscribe` with one-click support.
7. Add `utm_content` per module.
