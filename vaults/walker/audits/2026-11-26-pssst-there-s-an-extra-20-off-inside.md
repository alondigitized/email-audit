# Pssst...there's an EXTRA 20% off inside.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again. That makes the thirteenth in about two weeks, by my count. I can read the "60% OFF" fine on my phone, and COZY20 is easy to follow. But it's the same "up to 60% off plus an extra 20%" I got yesterday ("EXTRA savings with a 🎀 on top!", "More savings? That's a wrap.") and on 11/23. The "Pssst" subject promises a secret, and what's inside is the same Black Friday sale everyone gets. I don't feel like an insider.
- Below the hero it turns into a kids' clog banner, Minnie Mouse, TikTok viral styles and Afterpay. None of that is for a 62-year-old looking for cushioned, arch-supported, slip-on walking shoes. The black platform clog in the hero looks like a fashion piece, not something for my feet.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. My grandkids might like the under-$25 clogs, but a $30 classic clog is the only thing here I'd even consider.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" and "USE CODE COZY20" block is big and clear. I can read it without zooming.
- The "Shop Black Friday" button is a big black pill that's easy to tap with my thumb.
- The "Offer ends Sunday, November 29th" fine print gives me a real deadline.
- The "Classic clogs starting at $30" banner has a price I can work with. I could do the price-versus-comfort math on that.
- It looks like the Crocs I know, and the Black Friday theme fits the date.

## What's weak

- "Pssst" suggests a secret, but it's the same offer as the last ten or so emails. That feels like bait.
- "Up to 60% off" plus "extra 20% off (almost) everything" is confusing, and "Exclusions Apply" makes me doubt the deal.
- The hero shoe is a platform clog. There's no cushioning, arch support or walking talk anywhere.
- Minnie Mouse, kids' clogs under $25 and TikTok "FYP" copy are aimed at someone much younger than me.
- The Afterpay block means nothing to me. I pay cash.
- The legal text at the bottom is tiny, and I can't read it without zooming.
- Three buttons say "Shop All Doorbusters" or "Shop the Hype". Those don't tell me what I'd be shopping for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same offer every day. Send fewer emails, and make each one about one thing.
- 2. Put a men's comfort product in the hero, like a walking clog or slip-on with a cushioning or arch-support callout. Let me tap straight to men's.
- 3. Make the code and the deadline one line: "Take 20% off with COZY20 through Sunday."
- 4. Drop the TikTok and Minnie content for people like me. Segment it, and give older male subscribers a men's comfort block instead.
- 5. Use specific button text such as "Shop men's clogs from $30."
