# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- Same shoe, same fake cart, third or fourth costume this week — now it's "You Liked These, And You Weren't Wrong" over a "Complete Your Order" button, for a pink floral D'Lites sneaker I never touched, never sized, never put anywhere near a cart. That's a women's shoe, and I'm a 62-year-old guy who buys arch-support slip-ins. Whatever tracking they've got on me is broken.
- Buried below that nonsense there's actually a decent grid — some men's slip-ins, a real 40% off sale banner running through 8/19. That's the email I'd want. Instead I have to scroll past a phantom cart pitch to find it.
- I'm not clicking the top button — there's nothing to "complete." I might scroll down to the slip-in styles out of habit, but honestly this is deleted or ignored, not forwarded to anybody.

## What's working

- The 40% off summer sale banner with the 8/14-8/19 date range is a real, specific offer — the best thing in this email.
- Men's Skechers Slip-ins: Arch Fit Arcade and Delson 3.0 show up in the grid — that's actually my category, cushioned and hands-free.
- Shop by category links (Men, Women, Kids, etc.) are big and legible on a phone.
- Find a store / curbside pickup up front — useful for a guy who'd rather try shoes on.

## What's weak

- "You Liked These and You Weren't Wrong" over a women's floral D'Lites sneaker I never looked at — that's now four straight days of this exact fake-cart routine, just reworded.
- "Complete Your Order" implies I abandoned a cart. I didn't. That's the kind of manipulative nudge that makes me trust a brand less, not more.
- The hero product doesn't match my demographic or past behavior at all — no man my age is shopping pink floral D'Lites.
- The actual relevant products (men's slip-ins) are crammed into a small grid below the fold instead of leading the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the fake-cart framing entirely — I've gotten "Did you forget something?", "Your Items Are Waiting!", and now "Your cart expires soon!" all pushing the same shoe I never touched. Lead with the real offer instead: the 40% off summer sale.
- 2. Swap the hero product for something in my actual browsing history — men's slip-ins or arch-support walking shoes — not a women's floral sneaker.
- 3. Move the 40% off banner to the top of the email, above the fold, since it's the one honest, time-bound offer here.
- 4. Stop the "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" CTA language for browse-abandon or recommendation sends — reserve it for actual cart abandonment.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's slip-ins are on sale this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Arch support, zero laces, real savings — see what's new`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `40% off summer styles, online only through 8/19`
