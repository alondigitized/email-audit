# New collab: HOKA x BEAMS Japan
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a different animal from the usual HOKA blast — no neon, no "send it," no cliff-jumping. This is some kind of fashion-house tie-up with a Japanese clothing outfit called BEAMS, dressing up the Bondi 7 in camo-brown and off-white with a zen-garden theme. Photography's sharp, moody, almost artsy — I'll give them that, it doesn't look like every other HOKA email cluttering my inbox lately.
- But here's the thing — I have no idea what BEAMS is, I'm not shopping for "limited collab" sneakers, and nothing here tells me a lick about cushioning or arch support beyond a throwaway "ultra cushioned for daily moves" bullet buried two-thirds down. This reads like it's aimed at some sneakerhead 30 years younger than me who cares about hype drops and camo colorways, not a guy who just wants his feet to stop aching by 3pm.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is the seventh-ish HOKA email in the last couple weeks and honestly the least relevant yet — at least the trail and Bondi color ones were shoes I might actually consider buying.

## What's working

- The photography is genuinely striking — the floating shoe over the rock garden is a nice visual, even if it's not selling me on comfort.
- "Shop the Collab" and "Shop HOKA x BEAMS" buttons are clear, black, easy to find and tap.
- The Bondi 7 is a shoe I recognize from prior HOKA sends, so there's at least brand/model continuity.
- Only two colorways shown side by side up top, so it's not overwhelming with fifty options.

## What's weak

- No price, no discount, nothing — this is a straight product announcement, not an offer, so there's zero reason for me to act now.
- "BEAMS Japan," "Karesansui," "limited collab" — none of that language means anything to me or my buying decision.
- The bullet points about comfort ("ultra cushioned," "open mesh construction") are an afterthought, crammed at the bottom after four photo blocks I had to scroll through.
- Camo and cream colorways on a "limited" release scream resale-market hype shoe, not something a regular guy grabs off the shelf at DSW.
- No sizing info, no price, no "available now vs. preorder" — I'd have to click just to find out if I can even buy it in my size.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with price and availability — if this is a real product I can buy, tell me the cost and whether it's in stock in my size before I scroll through five photo blocks.
- 2. Move the comfort bullets ("ultra cushioned," "all-day comfort") up near the hero, not buried at the bottom — that's the one thing that might actually get my attention.
- 3. Cut the "Karesansui zen garden" art-school copy in half — one line is plenty, the rest is wasted words for someone deciding whether to buy shoes.
- 4. If this really is a limited hype drop, say so plainly ("Limited release — while supplies last") instead of me having to infer it from vague language.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Bondi 7 colorway — same comfort, new look`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Limited-edition Bondi 7: camo & cream, while they last`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same ultra-cushioned Bondi 7 you know, new colors inspired by Japan`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited run — get it before it's gone`
