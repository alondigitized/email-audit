# Heels from $29.99 👠
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW playbook again — third time this week I've gotten one of these. Subject says "Heels from $29.99," the Nike headline up top is 40% off, and then the whole scrolling middle is nothing but women's heels, booties, strappy sandals, and slingbacks. Not one men's shoe until I hit the "SHOP BY SIZE" grid near the bottom, and even that's just numbers with no men's/women's split shown. This is the same complaint I had about the "Go go go" and "Select Nike styles" emails from yesterday — Nike gets you in the door, then it's a bait-and-switch into the women's department.
- The "Shoe-It-Forward" donation thing with the $10 VIP reward is actually kind of a nice idea, and the "shoe donation" tie-in to Soles4Souls feels like a real program, not just marketing fluff. But it's buried under a wall of heels I have no use for.
- I'm not clicking. I skim past this in about four seconds and move on. Not forwarding it to anybody — my buddies aren't shopping for stilettos.

## What's working

- The 40% off Nike callout at the top is a real, recognizable brand and a real number, so it got me to open.
- The shoe donation / $10 VIP Bonus Reward program feels legit and is the one thing in here I'd read twice.
- Free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup, and Afterpay icons at the bottom are useful info if I were buying.
- Layout is clean, no broken images or overlapping text — easy enough to scroll through even if none of it applies to me.

## What's weak

- Every single product photo — nine-plus shoes — is a women's heel, bootie, or sandal. Zero men's product shown.
- "Heels from $29.99" as the subject line guarantees I already know this isn't for me before I even open it.
- "That's Not All, Folks" category tiles are Heels, Athletic & Sneakers, Sandals, Women's Shoes — no Men's category anywhere in sight.
- The Nike 40% off banner up top has nothing to do with the heels flooding the rest of the email — feels like two different emails stapled together.
- Size chart at the bottom is just numbers, no indication if any of it is men's sizing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender — if DSW knows I've bought men's shoes before, don't send me an all-heels email. Swap in men's comfort/walking styles for male subscribers.
- 2. Match the subject to the actual content or match the content to the subject — if the hero says Nike 40% off, lead the whole email with Nike men's and women's styles, not heels.
- 3. Add a "Men's" tile to the "That's Not All, Folks" grid so guys have a one-tap way out of the women's flood.
- 4. Move the shoe donation / VIP Bonus Reward higher — that's actually a good hook and it's buried two-thirds down.
