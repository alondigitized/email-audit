# Black Friday preview is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Well, here come the gift boxes. After the shopping bags on the 19th and the Christmas ornaments on the 13th and 18th, New Balance has handed me a young woman hugging a stack of red presents. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for a walking shoe, and the biggest thing on my phone screen is a gift pile. The headline is clear enough, "Up to 40% off select styles." But the "Black Friday preview" in the subject never shows up as a Black Friday look in the body.
- The shoe grid at the bottom is the part I wanted, and I had to scroll past a lot of gift wrap to reach it. Even there, the picks are an AC Runner, a Bungee Lace and a couple of chunky fashion sneakers. The 1080v15 is a decent cushioned shoe, but nothing is labeled for comfort, arch support or slip-on ease. Nothing shows a price or a discount either. "Up to 40% off" tells me nothing about which shoe, or how much off.
- Would I click? Maybe the "Walking" link in the footer, and only because I'd go looking for it. I wouldn't forward this to anyone. I'd mark it read and head to the site myself.

## What's working

- The headline "Up to 40% off select styles" is big and plain, and I can read it without zooming.
- The two black buttons, "Shop now" and "Visit a store," are large and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The "Free shipping on all orders" banner is a nice bonus, and it's stated without conditions.
- The "Shop by price" tiles (Under $50, $50–$100, Over $100) suit how I think about price against quality.
- The footer has a "Walking" link, so someone remembered that people like me exist.

## What's weak

- The hero is a young blonde woman with gift boxes. It's holiday gift wrap, not shoes, and it doesn't look like me or anyone I'm buying for. It also fills most of the first screen on my phone.
- The shoe grid shows no prices, no sale tags and no "was/now," so I can't tell what 40% off means for any of them.
- The styles are fashion sneakers with bungee laces and chunky soles. Nothing says cushioned, arch-supported or easy slip-on.
- The fine print under the hero is tiny and gray. It says exclusions apply and the offer can't be combined, and that lowers my trust in the "40%."
- This is the fourth or fifth hero in two weeks built on gifts, bags or ornaments. It feels like the same email wearing a new coat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the gift photo with shoes on a man's feet, or a hero that says "Comfort Black Friday: cushioned walkers up to 40% off." Put the product above the fold.
- 2. Put prices on every shoe in the grid, with the sale price next to the original, and label each one ("Max cushion," "Slip-on," "Wide widths").
- 3. Add a men's walking row (the 877, the Fresh Foam walkers) so a walker has somewhere to land.
- 4. Make the footer "Walking" link a real button in the body.
- 5. Bring the key exclusions up near the offer instead of burying them in small gray type.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh Foam and walking styles, plus free shipping on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access to our Black Friday prices. Sale ends Dec 28.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Black Friday preview is here" (New Balance)

## 1. Technical Summary
The send has a 75% QA pass rate (0 issues, 2 warnings). The HTML source shows leftover template placeholders and missing alt attributes on three images. SPF/DKIM could not be verified because the relay strips authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 of 36 links were probed and 24 were skipped by the click-likelihood ranking. No broken links were reported among the 12 probed, but footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 16,658 of 18,386 characters (about 91%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This is typical of long tracking-wrapped links on `link.email.newbalance.com`. It is a deliverability and readability risk for the text alternative.
- **Placeholder asset URL in CSS.** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png')}` is an unreplaced placeholder over insecure `http://`. It will 404 or trigger mixed-content handling wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, confirmed by QA):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12711312_434268_24017686.gif`. This is likely the open-tracking pixel, so an explicit `alt=""` is appropriate. The other two need descriptive alt text or `alt=""` if decorative.
- **Template title not replaced.** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` shows in some clients' tab and preview chrome.
- **Invalid or no-op meta tag.** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Conflicting `.bg1` rules.** `.bg1` is defined as `#ffffff` and later as `#58546d`. The later rule wins, which may be unintended in light mode. A dark-mode override sets `.bg1{background:#151415 !important}`.
- **`@font-face` blocks have no `src`.** `proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot` and the others declare no source, so web fonts never load and clients fall back to Arial or Times New Roman. One block also has a duplicated `font-family` declaration.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 doctype is combined with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Dark-mode handling is present** (`color-scheme` meta, `:root` and `prefers-color-scheme`). The global `a {color:#cf0a2c !important}` override may affect links on dark backgrounds, but that cannot be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source. The `XXXXX` placeholder in section 2 is the only template leftover observed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results not found.** This is an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, not failed. Verify it in a direct-to-mailbox test.
- **Unsubscribe link:** the QA probe notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the truncated source and QA output don't show its URL or result. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided, so one-click unsubscribe compliance cannot be confirmed.
- **Physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so it is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found in the available data. The click-tracking wrapper (`link.email.newbalance.com`) hides the destination URLs, and no UTM parameters or landing-page probe results were provided. Final-destination and UTM verification needs a redirect-chain trace of the primary CTAs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking GIF.
2. Replace or remove the `http://image.XXXXX.png` background, and use HTTPS if a background image is needed.
3. Set a real `<title>` (for example, the subject line) and fix or remove the `IE=edge` meta tag.
4. Remove the duplicate `.bg1` rule, or confirm that `#58546d` is intended.
5. Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules, or drop them and rely on the Arial fallback stack.
6. Shorten tracking URLs or add a plain-text-friendly version to cut the URL ratio in the text part.
7. Re-run the audit with a direct-delivery header capture to verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`.
8. Probe the 24 skipped links, at least the footer and unsubscribe links, and trace the primary CTA redirect chains to check UTM propagation.
