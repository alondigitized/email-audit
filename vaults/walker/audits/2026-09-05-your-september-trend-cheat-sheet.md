# Your September trend cheat sheet
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again — same BOGO 50% off banner, and now it's dressed up as a "September Trend Report." I scroll down expecting maybe one style I'd actually put on my feet, and instead I get tall boots in five colors, animal print skirts, retro sneakers on a model doing a squat pose, "reimagined loafers" that look like a boot stitched to a sock, and a woman lounging in a striped poncho boot. Every last item is women's. Not one sneaker, walking shoe, or anything with visible cushioning for a guy like me.
- The UGG banner near the bottom at least says "warm & fuzzies" which sounds comfortable, but it's still framed as a women's cold-weather push, not the hands-free, arch-support stuff I actually shop for. I'd scroll past this one in about four seconds and mark it read. Not forwarding it — nobody I know would want a "reimagined loafer."

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is a real, understandable offer stated plainly up top — no gimmick math, I know exactly what I'm getting.
- The layout is clean and easy to scroll on my phone — big buttons, readable text, no squinting required.
- "Shop by Size" grid near the bottom is a nice touch if I were actually buying — no zooming needed on the size buttons.
- Free shipping for VIPs and DSW VIP rewards blurb are visible, so if I were a member I'd at least know my perks are in play.

## What's weak

- This is DSW's fourth-plus near-identical BOGO email this week (I flagged the same deal on 9/2, 9/3 twice, and 9/4 twice) — it's just wearing a "trend report" costume this time.
- Not a single men's shoe anywhere in this email — tall boots, animal print, retro sneakers on a woman, loafers, UGGs — all women's fashion.
- Zero mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support — the stuff that actually gets me to click.
- "SHOP MEN" is buried as a tiny nav link at the very top and never referenced again in the body.
- Nothing here reflects that I'm even a repeat visitor — feels like a mass blast, not something built for a known customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender/purchase history — if DSW has any signal I browse men's, send me men's comfort shoes, not a "September Trend Report" of women's boots and loafers.
- 2. Lead with a men's comfort category (walking shoes, slip-ons, cushioned sneakers) alongside the BOGO offer instead of assuming everyone wants animal print and tall boots.
- 3. Retire the BOGO banner for a few days — I've now seen it daily since 8/31 and it's starting to feel like white noise instead of urgency.
- 4. If UGG is the one seasonal item that could cross gender lines, feature it higher and note if there's a men's UGG option — right now it reads purely as a women's cold-weather push.
