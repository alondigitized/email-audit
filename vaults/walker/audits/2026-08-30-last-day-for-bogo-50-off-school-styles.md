# Last day for BOGO 50% off school styles!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW BOGO 50% off deal, seventh day running by my count — I saw it on the 24th, 25th, 26th, 27th, 28th, and 29th, and now it's back again with "last day" slapped on it. I'll give them credit for finally putting a real deadline on it instead of "blink and you'll miss it" nonsense, but the product wall underneath is once again wedge sneakers, clogs, mary janes, leopard booties, and strappy heels — not one men's casual or walking shoe in sight. The "free IMBOX spray" add-on means nothing to me; I don't know what that product is and it's competing with the BOGO for my attention.
- This is a mark-as-read, maybe an unsubscribe-someday. If the deal really is ending today that's the one thing that might make me tap through just to see if there's a men's comfort shoe worth grabbing, but nothing in this email tells me that's even a possibility. Not forwarding this to anybody.

## What's working

- "Last day" in the subject actually gives this urgency some teeth after six days of the same offer — that's new.
- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly with the math spelled out ("buy any pair, get 50% off a second qualifying pair") — no guessing games.
- Shop by Size grid is genuinely useful and easy to tap on a phone.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup, and BOPIS icons are clear and readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — wedge sneaker, clogs, mary janes, heels, leopard booties — is women's. Zero men's casual, walking, or comfort shoes visible, so this reads like it wasn't sent to me at all.
- "FREE IMBOX SPRAY" means nothing to me and clutters the top of the email with a product I don't recognize instead of showing me a shoe.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or comfort — nothing here speaks to how I actually shop.
- I've now gotten this near-identical BOGO 50% offer daily for a week; even with "last day" attached, my brain has tuned it out.
- Dark, cramped hero block with the offer stacked so tight it feels more shouted at me than explained.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men's customers into a men's-only version of this email — swap the six product images for men's walking/casual shoes so it actually looks like it's for me.
- 2. Cut the IMBOX spray block or move it way down — it's just noise competing with the one thing (BOGO deadline) that should be driving the click.
- 3. If this really is the last day, say so in the hero copy too, not just the subject line — "TODAY ONLY" above BOGO 50% would reinforce urgency where my eye actually lands.
- 4. Add one line about comfort/fit somewhere — even "cushioned styles included" would make this feel less like a random woman's-shoe blast.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends today — BOGO 50% off, all sizes & widths`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wide widths and wide calf included — shop before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned, slip-on styles now BOGO 50% off`
