## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. The D'Lites "Spring Bloom" fills the top half of my screen, and it's a women's shoe I never put in a cart. I'm 62 and I wear men's walking shoes. This is the same cart nag I've been getting since the 6th ("Your cart expires soon!", "Did you forget something?"), so I'm tired of it. The subject line, "Your Items Are Waiting!", is one I've already seen on the 7th.
- The "more great options" grid has one shoe I'd look at, the men's slip-in Relaxed Fit Altman in the Murdock style. It's one small picture in a grid with women's sneakers and boy's light-up shoes. The 10% off stack for members is a decent perk, but it sits near the bottom, below the part I'd already have scrolled past.
- I wouldn't click. I might tap the Altman if I was already shopping, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone. I'd mark it read and wait for the next one, which will probably have the same flower shoe in it.

## What's working

- The Altman men's slip-in is in the grid, and slip-ins are the hands-free comfort I want.
- The big navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is easy to see and easy to tap.
- The text is large, and the stacked MEN / WOMEN / KIDS list is simple to use on my phone.
- The SKECHERS logo up top is a name I know, so I trust it.
- The "Members stack an extra 10% off" banner is clear, and the Oct. 11 end date is easy to find.

## What's weak

- The only product I'm shown at the top is a women's floral sneaker I never put in a cart. Telling me "your cart is still waiting" with it feels wrong.
- "We noticed you noticing us…" is a bit creepy for a shoe ad.
- Of the six suggested shoes, only one is a men's style. Two are boy's light-up sneakers and three are women's. The email doesn't seem to know who I am.
- The 10% off offer sits below the big best-sellers bar and is easy to miss.
- There's nothing about arch support, cushioning or reviews, and I can't tell if my size is in stock.
- The fine print is tiny, and it says the discount only applies when I'm logged into Skechers Plus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a shoe that's actually in the cart, or leave out a product I never picked. If the cart has a women's shoe in it, don't show it to me as mine.
- 2. Fill the "more options" grid from what I've browsed: men's slip-ins first, and drop the kids' shoes.
- 3. Move the 10% member offer up next to the main button, and put the end date in the headline.
- 4. Give me a reason to buy, like a comfort or arch-support line and a star rating under each shoe.
- 5. Stop sending near-identical cart reminders every day. Space them out and change what's in them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400 (FAIL).** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m50…`.
  - The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, so this is the real unsubscribe endpoint.
  - A 400 on a token-bearing URL may be a probe artifact, such as a missing session or a single-use token. It is still the highest-priority item because I can't confirm it works.

- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 26 were skipped, so footer and social link health is unknown.
- **Tracking pixel** is present: `https://skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`. Its `alt` is missing, which is harmless for a pixel.
