## Technical Audit — Adidas Manchester United 90/92 Jersey

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed failures: a broken click-through link and a missing plain-text body. Several images are served over HTTP and lack alt text, creating both rendering and accessibility risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken product link**
QA reports `unknown url type: '/g/a8f49645-6334-4066-9454-bead930954eb?txn=...'` for the redirect target of:
```
https://dv.adidas.com/o/a8f49645-6334-4066-9454-bead930954eb?cp_tp=v3.eJz...
```
The resolved destination uses a `/g/` path that the click-tracking system cannot parse. This link is non-functional — clicks reach a dead end.

**[WARN] 17 tracking links unverified**
All click-redirect URLs route through `click.comms.adidas.com`. QA skipped HTTP probing for these. The broken `/g/` link above is one confirmed casualty; others cannot be ruled out without resolved-URL validation.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Two images missing `alt` text**
- Hero image: `dv.adidas.com/o/a8f49645-6334-4066-9454-bead930954eb?cp_tp=v3.eJz...`
- Tracking pixel: `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019de71594c0-fecc02b3.../GN8HDARm8allMfvs...`

Missing `alt` on the hero image breaks content for image-blocked clients. The pixel needs `alt=""` (empty, not absent) for screen-reader hygiene.

**[WARN] Three image `src` values use HTTP, not HTTPS**
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg  (appears twice)
http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019de71594c0-fecc02b3.../GN8HDARm8allMfvs...
```
Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy images over HTTPS and will re-fetch these; some clients will block them outright. The arrow icon (`b0c011a2`) appears duplicated in the source — may indicate a copy-paste error in the template.

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` declarations**
`AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are each declared twice across two separate `<style>` blocks in `<head>`. This is dead weight (~800 bytes); no rendering defect, but indicates template assembly error.

**[INFO] `<meta charset>` missing `http-equiv`**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Should be:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Without `http-equiv`, Outlook (Word rendering engine) may not honor the charset declaration.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved `{{tokens}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`, or similar placeholders visible in the HTML source.

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing**
QA reports `Text version is 0 chars`. CAN-SPAM and most ESP policies require a non-empty plain-text part. A zero-length text part also increases spam score on content filters (SpamAssassin `MIME_HTML_ONLY` rule fires).

**[INFO] `referrer: no-referrer` suppresses analytics**
```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />
```
This is intentional for privacy but means any landing page that relies on `document.referrer` to attribute the email channel will receive a blank referrer. Confirm UTM params on links are the sole attribution mechanism (see §6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The broken `/g/` link (§2) is the primary continuity failure — it is undeliverable regardless of UTM state.

The `cp_tp`, `cp_cid`, `mi_cid`, and `mi_mid` query parameters on the `dv.adidas.com` redirect URLs appear to be adidas's internal tracking tokens, not standard UTM params. If the landing page depends on UTM (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) for attribution in GA4 or similar, those are not present in the visible redirect URLs. Whether the click-tracker appends UTMs post-redirect could not be confirmed from the truncated source.
