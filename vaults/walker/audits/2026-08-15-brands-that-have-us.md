# BRANDS THAT HAVE US 😍
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- "Brands that have us" — that's a woman in a green coat sprawled on a car hood, then a young thing on a park bench in platform sneakers, then somebody doing a leg-kick pose in an adidas jogging fit. Not one of these is me, not close. Frye & Co., Converse, Steve Madden, Reebok, adidas — I know some of those names, but nothing about how it's shown here says "62-year-old guy who needs arch support." I scrolled a long way — Frye, Converse, Steve Madden, Reebok, adidas — before I hit anything that looked like an actual deal.
- The one honest thing in this email is buried at the bottom of all that: BOGO 50% off, and a "Nike under $60" price alert. Same story as every DSW email I've gotten this week — real number, wrong wrapping. I've said it five times now about this brand and I'm getting tired of saying it.
- Not clicking. Not forwarding. This gets marked read and forgotten, same as the last six.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly near the bottom, no games — I'll always give credit for that.
- The "Price Alert: Select Nike under $60" block is a real, specific number, which is more than most of this email offers.
- Shop-by-size grid (5 through 11) is clean and easy to tap on a phone.
- Loyalty perks section at the bottom — free shipping, birthday gift, 15% off texts — is at least legible, even if it's an afterthought.

## What's weak

- Five straight product blocks — Frye, Converse, Steve Madden, Reebok, adidas — and every model is a young woman. Nothing men's, nothing that looks like it'd fit a foot like mine.
- The actual offer (BOGO 50%) doesn't show up until after a full screen's worth of scrolling past fashion shots. That's backwards.
- "THE OBSESSION IS REAL" as a headline tells me nothing about a shoe, a price, or comfort — just noise.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease, anything a comfort shopper like me actually reads for.
- Subject "BRANDS THAT HAVE US" is vague marketing-speak; doesn't hint there's a real deal in there at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the BOGO 50% off and the Nike price alert — that's the only content in here that gets me to act. Put it above the fold, not after five scrolls of lifestyle shots.
- 2. Swap at least one or two of those hero product spots for men's comfort/casual styles — Skechers, Hoka, New Balance walkers — instead of an all-women lineup top to bottom.
- 3. Drop or shrink "THE OBSESSION IS REAL" — replace with something that names the deal.
- 4. Add one line about comfort features (cushioned, arch support, slip-on) somewhere near the men's shoes, if you're going to bother showing any.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off — plus Nike under $60`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's comfort picks, half off the 2nd pair`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy any pair, get 50% off a second. Nike deals start at $60.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort, half off pair two.`
