# Get in the flow
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Now this is a different animal — a whole grid of shoes laid out for me, no marathoner leaping off a cliff in sight. Problem is, it's still a running shoe blast. Clifton PRO, Bondi 9, Skyward X 2, Mach 7 — these are runner's shoes, most in the neon pink/green colorways that scream "I'm training for something," not "I need something to wear to the hardware store that won't kill my knees." The men's Bondi 9 in black with a lime swoosh is at least a normal-looking shoe I could picture on my feet, but nobody's telling me a thing about cushioning or arch support — it's all "new color energy" and "get in the zone."
- The Members Only section with the Kawana 3 colorway might've gotten me if I knew what a Kawana 3 even was, and the "kit" section at the bottom — cap, tank top, arm sleeves, running shorts — that's for somebody doing intervals at 6am, not me doing a lap around the block.
- I'd scroll past this one. Not a click, not a forward — maybe a glance at the Bondi 9 men's shoe before I close the app.

## What's working

- The Bondi 9 men's colorway (black/lime) looks like an actual shoe I'd wear, not a race-day special.
- Clean grid layout — six shoes, clearly labeled by name and gender, easy to scan on my phone without pinching to zoom.
- "Shop the Pack" button is big, black, easy to tap.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- This is a running-focused send yet again — like the July emails, it's runners and racers, and I'm not one.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or all-day comfort anywhere in the copy — just "new color energy" and vague flow talk.
- The apparel kit (tank, shorts, arm sleeves) at the bottom is 100% not for a 62-year-old who wants a comfortable walking shoe.
- No price, no discount, no member perk spelled out beyond "join for free" — nothing pulls me toward actually buying.
- "Feel it?" as a header doesn't tell me anything about what I'm supposed to feel or why I should care.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an everyday-comfort shoe up top instead of burying the one wearable option (Bondi 9 black/lime) in a six-pack grid — put a recovery or walking shoe in the hero spot, not a racing silhouette.
- 2. Add actual product language — "plush foam," "extra cushioning," "arch support built in" — somewhere in the copy, even just under one shoe.
- 3. Drop the apparel kit section entirely for a customer like me, or swap it for something like a comfortable travel sock or slide.
- 4. Put an actual price or percentage off somewhere — I don't shop on "energy," I shop on value.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fresh colors for your everyday shoe`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned comfort in six new colorways`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No racing required — just great color`
