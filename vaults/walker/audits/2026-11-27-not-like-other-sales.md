# Not like other sales
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Another HOKA Black Friday email, and I've stopped counting. After "Black Friday is here" this morning and "Don't leave savings on the table" yesterday, "Not like other sales" doesn't tell me anything new. But this one has the best thing I've seen all week. The Bondi 9 is 20% off, and that's the cushioned walker I actually buy.
- "Turn it up" is huge white type and I can read it fine. The tiers ($40 off $200, $50 off $250, $60 off $300) are clear enough, but the shoe is a flashy blue running shoe and that's not me. The Bondi block is below it, and I had to scroll to find it. If it had been up top, I'd have tapped it.
- I'd probably click, but only the Bondi button. I wouldn't forward it. My friends get the same HOKA emails.

## What's working

- The Bondi 9 at 20% off is the one thing in here for me, and it's called "super-cushioned." That's the word I shop on.
- Text is big and high-contrast. I can read the headlines without my glasses.
- The buttons are wide, full-width pills, easy to tap with my thumb.
- The spend tiers ($40/$50/$60 off) are simple math. I can see what I'd save.
- The dates are in the fine print: 11/27 to 12/1.

## What's weak

- The hero is a running shoe, and I walk. The Bondi is buried in the second block.
- "Members save more. Sign in or join for free" is annoying. I have to sign in before I even see my discount.
- The fine print is tiny and light gray on blue. I can't read it, and the footer links (Privacy, Unsubscribe) are almost invisible.
- Both buttons just say "Shop Now." I don't know where they go.
- The Bondi picture is a close-up of the heel, in beige. I can't tell what the shoe looks like or what shades are on sale.
- The "Not like other sales" subject doesn't match what I see. It's the same Black Friday sale as the last five emails.
- The beanie banner at the top, "Spend $250+ to get a free beanie," is a gimmick.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the Bondi 9 at the top. Show the whole shoe, the colors, and the price with 20% off, like "$150 → $120."
- 2. Make the buttons specific: "Shop Bondi 9" and "Shop Men's Walking."
- 3. Skip the sign-in gate. Show the sale price to everybody, and mention the member discount separately.
- 4. Make the fine print bigger, with dark text on a light background.
- 5. Send fewer emails. Daily is too much.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bondi 9 is 20% off, new colors`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The cushioned walker you like, on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $40 to $60 off when you spend $200 or more. Ends 12/1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Super-cushioned comfort for every step. Sale ends Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links, Cordial click-tracking and UTM tagging are in place. However, there is no plain-text part, 8 images have no alt text, and the QA probe got 403 responses on two links, including Unsubscribe. The QA pass rate was 56%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures (HTTP 403)**
  - Footer "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/" → `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6af61cb6…:1/b77fa152?jwtH=…`
  - "Unsubscribe" → `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6af61cb6…:1/3c2385a5?jwtH=…`
- **Probable cause:** I decoded the `jwtP` payloads. They redirect to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_…` and `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`, so the targets look correct. The 403 most likely comes from Cordial or the CDN rejecting the automated probe, not from broken destinations. It is **unverified** and needs a manual click-through.
- **Probe coverage:** only 5 of 12 material links were probed (7 skipped), so the other links are untested.
- **Tracking pixels:** two open-tracking pixels are present, `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. They have no alt attribute (see §3). Two tracking domains (`e.` and `mg.`) are in use, which is not a defect but is worth confirming as intentional.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback missing:** the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and affects clients and users that need plain text.
- **Missing alt text on 6 content images:**
  - `H_US_Banner_GWPBeanie_3.jpg`
  - `H_US_Hero_NewArrivals_KTT_1.jpg`
  - `H_US_Subcopy_Hero_BF_1.jpg`
  - `H_US_CTA_Hero_NewArrivals_KTT_2.jpg`
  - `H_US_Secondary_Bondi9Markdown_3.jpg`
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
- **Consequence:** the email looks fully image-based (1200px-wide Cordial images, including the CTA graphic), so it is unreadable with images blocked or under a screen reader.
- **Tracking pixels:** these two should carry `alt=""`.
- **Viewport meta:** the source has two conflicting `<meta name="viewport">` tags. One sets `maximum-scale=1` and the other sets `user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Charset meta:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it is not a valid charset declaration.
- **Title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **CSS reset:** `* {… outline:none}` removes keyboard focus indicators.
- **Unrendered template values in CSS:**
  - `.nav_padding {padding-top:px; padding-bottom:px}`
  - `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`
  - These are invalid declarations and are ignored, but they suggest empty template variables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible (truncated) source. No unrendered `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens were seen. The only trace of empty variables is the CSS noted above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe:** a link is present (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe` via the Cordial redirect), but the probe returned 403. Click-test it manually, since a non-functional unsubscribe is a CAN-SPAM risk.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found because the AgentMail relay strips or omits it. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. This is a limit of the test setup, not a confirmed defect.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:** the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address, since the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM structure:** it is consistent and correct: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261127_AM2_MemberTieredPromo_A120Day_720Purchasers_LYNovOpeners`.
- **Extra parameters:** `hmail=<hash>` and `cordial_id=<id>` are appended, and the unsubscribe link also gets UTMs and `cid`.
- **Landing-page check:** the return-start landing page could not be checked because of the 403. The campaign name references a member-tiered promo, but I couldn't confirm that any promo link lands on matching content from the probed set.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text MIME part:** the current text version is 0 chars.
2. **Add alt text to all content images:** use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. **Manually click-test the Unsubscribe and return-start links** from a real client, and allowlist the QA probe if Cordial is blocking it.
4. **Fix the head and CSS:**
   - Remove the duplicate viewport tag and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`.
   - Add a `<title>`.
   - Remove `outline:none` from the global reset.
   - Clean up the empty `px` CSS values in the template.
5. **Verify authentication and headers** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`) on the original send path, not the AgentMail relay.
