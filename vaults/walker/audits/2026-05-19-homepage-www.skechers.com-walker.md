# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Well, they're leading with the Slip-ins again — and honestly, that's the right call for me. That "Hands Free" headline is exactly what I came here for. This feels like a brand that knows its bread and butter: comfortable, easy-on footwear pushed through a clean phone layout with a sale banner you can't miss. The models are young enough to be my kids, but the product is mine.

## What's working

- **"BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart" banner** — blue, top of the page, no hunting for the deal. That's the kind of thing that keeps me from opening a second tab for DSW.
- **"Hands Free Slip-ins" hero copy** — that phrase is why I came. Whoever wrote that headline understands my knees.
- **Members strip with a deadline** — "Until 5/25" on a $5 reward gives me an actual reason to log in and buy today rather than put the phone down.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — I always end up back on something I looked at before. That tab in the bottom nav means I don't have to remember where I was.
- **"SHOP NOW" button size** — wide, high contrast, easy tap on a phone screen. No squinting, no pinching.

## What's weak

- **Young models, zero age representation** — the guy sitting on the ledge might be 28. I need to see someone with gray at the temples wearing these, someone walking a dog or standing in a Costco line, not poolside in their 20s.
- **"Women" tab is pre-selected** — when I land on this page, the nav defaults to Women. I have to tap "Men" before any of this becomes relevant to me. Small friction, but it stings.
- **No free-shipping call-out above the fold** — I'm not going to pull out a calculator to figure out if the BOGO gets me there. Put the shipping threshold right up top.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — it could go anywhere. "Shop Men's Slip-ins" would make me move faster.
- **No trust signals at a glance** — no star ratings, no "Free Returns" badge, nothing that tells me I'm safe tapping my way toward the cart.

## Recommendations

- **Swap one model for a 55–65 lifestyle shot** — a guy on a morning walk or standing in a line, not lounging poolside at 25. That image alone closes the demographic gap.
- **Lock the tab default to the shopper's last category** — returning visitors on a phone shouldn't have to retap "Men" every visit; use the Recently Viewed data you're already collecting.
- **Change the CTA copy to "Shop Men's Slip-ins"** — one small word change ties the button to my gender and my product in one tap; it also helps if I landed from a men's email.
- **Add a free-shipping line to the blue banner** — "BOGO 50% OFF | Free shipping on $75+" fits in the same bar and removes the mental math that makes me hesitate.
