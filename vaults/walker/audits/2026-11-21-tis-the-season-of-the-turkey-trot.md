# 'Tis the season of the turkey trot
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one at least opens with shoes and a fellow in motion, no shopping bags or Christmas ornaments this time. But he's running, and I'm a walker. "From training to trotting" is cute for a turkey trot, but I'm not training for anything. I'm trying to get through a morning on cushioned soles.
- The shoe grid is clean and easy to read: six sneakers, names underneath, big enough for my thumbs. The trouble is there's no price, no deal and no word about cushioning or arch support. All I get is "Free shipping on all orders" in tiny red text up top. After the Nov 18 "25% off when you spend $125+" send, this feels like a brand email with the offer taken out. I'd glance and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero is a single clear image with a big serif headline and two plain black buttons, "Shop now" and "Visit a store."
- The six-shoe grid is tidy, with big photos and names I can read without zooming.
- It opens with footwear, which is a relief after last week's shopping bags and Christmas ornaments.
- "Free shipping on all orders" is a simple, honest line with no fine print visible.
- The "Walking" link in the footer shows they know people like me exist.

## What's weak

- Everything is about running. The runner, the running apparel and the "Running" footer link all assume I'm training for something.
- There's no price, percentage or deal anywhere, so I have no reason to act today.
- The free-shipping line is tiny red text on a pale strip, and my eyes would skip right over it.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support, width or comfort. The 880 and 1080 are comfy shoes, but the email never says so.
- Half the length is apparel and a "More to explore" grid of socks and more sneakers, and I'd be scrolling a long way on my phone.
- There's no walking shoe in the grid. The 1540 is down in "More to explore," and it isn't labeled as one for walking or standing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a walking or all-day-comfort shoe in the hero slot and say why: cushioning, arch support, easy on and off. Keep the turkey-trot angle for the runners and send me a "walk it off after dinner" version.
- 2. Make the free shipping the headline offer. Put it in a bold banner with an end date, not tiny red type.
- 3. Add a price under each shoe so I can do my price-versus-quality math.
- 4. Cut the apparel block and the second shoe grid. One grid and one button is enough.
- 5. Add a short line under each shoe: "Cushioned daily trainer," "Wide widths available."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has no confirmed broken links, but it has several template-hygiene problems: a leftover placeholder asset URL, `@font-face` rules with no `src`, and a malformed meta tag. Three images lack alt text, and the plain-text part is 90% URLs. SPF/DKIM could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 14,213 of 15,772 characters (about 90%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This points to long click-tracking URLs in the text alternative. It is a spam-filter risk and makes the text version hard to read.
- **Low probe coverage.** Only 12 of 43 links were probed and 31 were skipped, so link health is mostly unverified. The 12 probed links produced no failures.
- **Placeholder asset URL in the CSS.** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png')}` is unresolved template content. It is also plain `http://`, which can trigger mixed-content warnings in some clients. The class would 404 if it were ever applied.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on three images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png` (`https://link.email.newbalance.com/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingIcons_06.png`)
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` (same path)
  - `egR1f966kc_818512772_12698901_76347_23999408.gif` (`/mo/` path, likely a tracking or open pixel)
- **`@font-face` rules have no `src`.** For example, `@font-face { font-family: proxima-nova,sans-serif; font-weight: 700; ... }` and the `linotype-didot` rules declare no font source. Custom fonts will never load, and every client falls back to Arial or Times New Roman. One rule also has a duplicated `font-family` declaration inside it (the 700/italic block).
- **Malformed meta tag.** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv` attribute, so it does nothing.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The page uses an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with an XHTML `xmlns` on `<html>`. This is not a functional break, but it is inconsistent.
- **Duplicate `.bg1` definitions.** One sets `background-color: #ffffff` and the other sets `background: #58546d`. The later rule wins, so the base background is `#58546d` in light mode unless a dark-mode override applies.
- **Dark mode.** The email declares `color-scheme: light dark` and uses `prefers-color-scheme`. The shipping banner is named `_DarkMode_`, so a dark asset is present. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm how the light and dark images are swapped.
- **Reused template assets.** The images come from a `myNB_Welcome` folder and the `<title>` is "New Balance Template". The email appears to be built from a welcome template, and the shipping assets may not match this campaign.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens. The only unresolved placeholder is the `XXXXX` image URL noted above, which is an asset path and not a merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found. The QA tool says this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and address not confirmed.** The QA data says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but it doesn't show the URL or the result. The truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link, the physical postal address or `List-Unsubscribe` headers, so these can't be verified.
- **Sender.** The `From` address is `newbalance@email.newbalance.com`, a branded subdomain consistent with the link domain `link.email.newbalance.com`. No mismatch was found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this is limited by the data. Links go through the `link.email.newbalance.com` click-tracking redirect, and the truncated HTML doesn't show the final destination URLs or UTM parameters. The 12 probed links produced no failures, but the final landing pages weren't reported.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three flagged images. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and for the tracking GIF.
2. Remove the `.bgwhite` rule or replace `http://image.XXXXX.png` with a real HTTPS asset.
3. Add `src` to the `@font-face` rules, or delete them and use a web-safe stack. Right now they have no effect.
4. Fix `<meta content="IE=edge">` to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`, or remove it. Remove the duplicate `.bg1` definition.
5. Shorten the plain-text version by using fewer links or shorter tracking URLs.
6. Check that the `myNB_Welcome` shipping assets are intended for this campaign, and rename the `<title>` from "New Balance Template".
7. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the raw message headers outside the relay. Raise the probe limit so the 31 skipped links, including the unsubscribe link, are checked, and log the final destination URLs and UTM parameters.
