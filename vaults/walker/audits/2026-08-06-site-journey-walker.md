# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 2/10 (prev 2/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I made it from the homepage all the way to a search results page, but two of the nine steps in this "journey" flat-out failed: tapping into "Log In" never showed me a login screen — it dropped me into the Men's hamburger menu instead — and "View Cart" showed me nothing. A blank white screen. Not an empty cart message, not a "your cart is empty" prompt — nothing. I stared at my phone for a second wondering if it froze. The cart icon stayed at "0" through the whole trip, which tells me my Add to Cart tap likely failed too (I got hit with a red "Please select a size" warning before I'd even picked one). For a 62-year-old trying to buy a pair of Arch Fit slip-ins, this is the kind of thing that makes me put the phone down and drive to the DSW instead.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**2/10** — unchanged from yesterday. Browsing and product pages are genuinely pretty good on mobile — big text, clean layout, the product page is solid. But the two most important conversion steps in this whole journey — logging in and viewing my cart — are broken. A shopper who can't log in and can't see their cart isn't converting, full stop.

## 3. What's Working

- **Product detail page (step 6)** is clean and readable without zooming: product name, star rating, price in bold, and a clear "Hands Free Slip-ins" badge front and center.
- **Category navigation (step 4)** — the mobile menu has large, clearly labeled rows (Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections) with generous tap targets and a visible Back/X to escape.
- **Hands Free Slip-ins landing page (step 5)** surfaces fit filters (Wide/Extra Wide/Medium) right up top with big blue buttons — genuinely useful for a guy with wide feet.
- **Size grid on the PDP (step 7)** is big, tappable squares — no tiny dropdown to fight with.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Login is broken.** Tapping "Log In" never surfaced a sign-in form — I landed back on the Men's navigation menu. I never got a personalized experience of any kind in this whole session.
- **Cart page is blank.** Step 8 is a totally white screen. No header, no "empty cart" message, nothing. This is a hard stop for anyone trying to check what's in their bag before checkout.
- **Add to Cart likely failed.** The screenshot for that step shows a red "Please select a size" warning already active, and the cart badge stayed at "0" for the rest of the journey — meaning the shoe was never actually added.
- **Search results aren't relevant to me.** Searching "comfort shoes" as a logged-in-attempt male shopper returned Women's shoes at the top (Contour Foam - Cozy Fit, Relaxed Fit: Glide-Step). No gender/persona relevance at all.
- **Popup dismissal did nothing meaningful.** Steps 1 and 2 look nearly identical — the only difference is the promo banner text swapped from "40% off" to "Skechers x Care Bears." No popup appeared or was dismissed that I could see.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page rendering** — a blank white screen on "View Cart" is a checkout-killer. This should be priority one.
2. **Fix the login flow** so tapping "Log In" actually opens a sign-in form instead of silently routing to the nav menu.
3. **Require size selection before the Add to Cart tap fires**, or make the "select a size" error non-blocking/clearer, so a real add-to-cart action can complete.
4. **Bias search results toward the shopper's browsing context** — if I've been in Men's, don't show me Women's clogs first for "comfort shoes."
5. **Make the popup/promo banner behavior visible** — if there's no popup to dismiss, don't have a "Dismiss Popups" step; if there is one, make sure it actually renders so it can be tested.

## 6. Bottom Line

The bones are here — the product page and category browsing are genuinely comfortable for someone like me on a phone. But I can't log in, I can't see my cart, and I'm not sure I actually added anything to it. That's not a rough edge, that's the checkout pipeline not working. Same 2/10 as yesterday — no regression, but no improvement either, and the cart blank-screen is the one thing I'd flag as urgent since it wasn't obviously called out before.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary
Mobile journey across 8 steps (Homepage → Log In → Men category → Men > Shoes → PDP → Add to Cart → View Cart → Search) surfaced 16 axe violations, 9 console errors, and 7 network-level failures, concentrated on the **View Cart** step (missing document `<title>`/`lang`, no landmarks) and repeated **429 rate-limiting** on a bot-detection/fingerprinting endpoint. Site is built on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware) with a heavy third-party tag stack (GTM, Monetate, TikTok, Quantum Metric, Bazaarvoice, Stylitics, etc.).

## Accessibility
16 violations total:
- **Critical (1):** `aria-required-parent` on Homepage — ARIA role used without its required parent role, breaking assistive-tech tree structure.
- **Serious (2):** `document-title` and `html-has-lang` both on **View Cart** — the cart page is missing a non-empty `<title>` and an `lang` attribute on `<html>`, both of which screen readers depend on to announce page context and select correct pronunciation/voice.
- **Moderate (11):** Landmark/region issues repeated across Homepage, View Cart, and Search — `landmark-one-main`/`landmark-main-is-top-level`/`landmark-no-duplicate-main`/`landmark-unique`/`landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `heading-order`, `region` (content not contained in a landmark), and `page-has-heading-one` (View Cart has no `<h1>`).
- **Minor (2):** `aria-allowed-role` on Homepage and Search — role attribute value not valid for the element it's on.

Pattern: landmark/region and heading structure issues recur across templates, suggesting a shared layout component rather than isolated one-off markup bugs.

## SEO
- **View Cart** page has no `<title>` element and no `<html lang>` — both directly harm indexability/snippet quality and locale signaling for that URL, consistent with the accessibility findings above.
- Homepage/Log In/Men category HTML heads are dominated by ~20+ synchronous/async third-party tracking scripts (GTM, TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, Taboola, Monetate, Quantum Metric, Bing, Impact, CreativeCDN, etc.) ahead of primary content signals — this doesn't block crawling but adds significant head-parsing weight before render-relevant content.
- No canonical/meta description/structured data was present in the truncated samples provided; insufficient sample depth to confirm presence or absence — recommend a full `<head>` capture per template to verify.

This is a gap in the audit run, not a finding about the site; recommend re-running with LCP/CLS instrumentation enabled before drawing performance conclusions.

## Mobile Optimization

## Console & Network Errors
- **429 (rate limited) x5** against `https://www.skechers.com/149e9513-.../2d206a39-.../fp?x-kpsdk-v=...` — this is a Kasada (`kpsdk`) bot-detection fingerprinting endpoint, hit repeatedly and throttled during the session. Also one 429 directly on `/cart/`, indicating the rate limiter is impacting core page delivery, not just the anti-bot beacon.
- **403 (forbidden)** on `BazaarvoiceProductData-GetProduct?pid=test` — the literal `pid=test` query param strongly suggests a hardcoded/placeholder product ID left in a review-widget call rather than the real SKU, which Bazaarvoice's API then rejects.
- **CORS block** on `https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css` — missing `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header on the Stylitics stylesheet response, so the browser refuses to apply/read it cross-origin from `www.skechers.com`.
- **`net::ERR_FAILED`** — a generic network failure, likely the direct consequence of the CORS-blocked or rate-limited request above rather than an independent failure.

## Recommendations
1. Fix the `pid=test` hardcoded parameter in the Bazaarvoice product-reviews call — it's returning 403 on every page that fires it.
2. Add proper CORS headers (or self-host/proxy) for the Stylitics stylesheet to stop the blocked cross-origin load.
3. Investigate the Kasada fingerprinting endpoint's rate limits — repeated 429s there and on `/cart/` point to a client retry loop or an overly aggressive server-side threshold that's affecting real page delivery, not just bot detection.
4. Add a non-empty `<title>` and `<html lang="en">` to the View Cart template, plus a single `<h1>` and a top-level `<main>` landmark — these are template-level fixes given the same gaps appear at Homepage and Search too.
5. Consolidate/dedupe landmark roles (`main`, `complementary`, `aside`) sitewide and audit heading order — recurring across 3 of 8 steps sampled, indicating a shared layout issue.
6. Investigate the 351ms TTFB on Log In and Men category (vs. ~22ms baseline) — check for uncached personalization/session logic on those routes.
7. Re-run the audit with LCP/CLS capture enabled — those fields were empty for every step in this run, leaving rendering performance unassessed.
