# Up to 60% off: Black Friday first access ends today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email, and this one is the shortest and cleanest of the lot. The black banner with the countdown clock (17 hours, 43 minutes) and "UP TO 60% OFF" in big white letters got me first. I didn't have to squint, which I appreciate at 62. "Last chance" is also the first honest closing line I've seen from them since the 14th, when "first access" started.
- The trouble is the picture. Three people walk down a city street carrying shopping bags, and nobody shows me a shoe I'd wear for cushion or arch support. There is no price and no product. "Iconic sneakers, jerseys, fleece" tells me nothing about comfort. The buttons are big and easy to tap, but they're just Men, Women and Kids.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of curiosity, because 60% off a walking shoe would be worth a look. Would I forward it? No. I'd leave it unread and wait to see whether Hoka or Brooks has a real sale.

## What's working

- The countdown clock is large and clear, and "ends today" feels like a real deadline.
- "UP TO 60% OFF" is huge and readable on my phone without zooming.
- The three black buttons are big, with plenty of space between them, so I won't hit the wrong one.
- It's short. One idea, one scroll, done.
- The headline "Last chance for first access" says plainly where I stand.

## What's weak

- I see no shoe I'd wear, and nothing about comfort, cushioning or arch support.
- "Up to 60%" doesn't say which items get that discount. I assume it's the odd leftover pair, not the popular styles.
- The models are young and fashion-forward. Nobody looks like me.
- The "Shop Men" button goes to a whole category, not a walking or comfort shoe.
- This is at least the fifth deal email in a week. The countdown has lost some force since the 15th.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show one real men's walking or cushioned shoe with a was/now price, like "$130 → $65". That would change the email more than anything else.
- 2. Cut the unneeded Women and Kids buttons for me. Show a "Shop Men's Walking & Comfort" button instead.
- 3. Say the member price needs a free adiClub sign-in. Right now I can't tell if I'm in.
- 4. Put in a short line on fit or cushioning, plus a star rating.
- 5. Send fewer of these. One strong send beats five lookalikes.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Comfort sneakers, fleece and more. Member prices end at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text part (0 chars, QA FAIL), and the HTML loads several images over `http://`, including an `http://` open-tracking pixel. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM checks can't be confirmed from the truncated source and relayed headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains such as `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...`. Their destinations are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Insecure tracking pixel.** The image `AQkscsq3lW8qahB6Os6-nuuyx-2R0K-wIeNkV_V8fck=452` is served from `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1eefc202b-...`. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop it, which undercounts opens.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** is set. Where clients honor it, the referrer is stripped on click-through, which can hurt attribution if the landing pages rely on it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **HTTP images.** The "arrow" image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` appears 6 times over `http://`. Clients may block it, and the host should support HTTPS.
- **Missing alt text.** Two images have none:
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/2c553555-361f-474b-a8df-ee3e0b4e13b9?cp_tp=...`
  - the tracking pixel above (a pixel should carry `alt=""`).
- **Charset declaration likely ineffective.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Some clients may ignore it and mis-render special characters.
- **Plain-text fallback missing.** The text version is 0 chars (QA FAIL). This hurts accessibility and spam scoring, and it leaves nothing for clients that render text only.
- **No further rendering issues** are visible in the truncated source. The `@font-face` rules use `local()` fallbacks with `mso-font-alt: Arial`, which is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** The `Authentication-Results` header is not present (WARN). That is expected through the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It isn't evidence of a sender-side failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address not verifiable.** The `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated source or QA output. Treat them as unconfirmed rather than failing.
- **Plain-text part missing** (see section 3), a deliverability and best-practice gap.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified. All 22 links are wrapped in redirectors and were not resolved, so the final URLs and their UTM params are unknown. Nothing in the evidence shows a mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part (currently 0 chars).
2. Serve `image.link.adidas.com` assets and the `click.comms.adidas.com` pixel over HTTPS.
3. Add `alt` text to the `dv.adidas.com/o/2c553555-...` image and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Change the charset tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. Reconsider `referrer: no-referrer` if landing-page analytics depend on referrer data.
6. For the next QA pass, capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`) and the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm UTM params and destinations.
