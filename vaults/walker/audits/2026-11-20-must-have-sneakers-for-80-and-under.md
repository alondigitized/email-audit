# Must-have sneakers for $80 and under
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email. I've lost count since the 15th. This one is calmer than the Black Friday countdown banners, and "$80 and under" is a price I can work with. But the picture is a maroon suede sneaker and a leopard-print one, and that's not me. I wear a cushioned slip-on. The copy says "non-stop comfort," but nothing shows me cushioning or arch support.
- Then the email turns into a Mercedes F1 bag giveaway with a leather jacket and a cap. That has nothing to do with buying shoes. I'd skim it and probably not click. If a friend told me adidas had decent sneakers at $80, I'd look. I wouldn't forward this one.

## What's working

- "SNEAKERS $80 AND UNDER" is big and plain. I didn't have to squint.
- The price ceiling is a real number, and I do the price-versus-quality math on shoes.
- The body copy names actual models (VL Court, Runfalcon, Barreda, Daily 1.0), so I can search for them.
- Separate "Shop Men" and "Shop Women" buttons are large and easy to tap on a phone.
- It's clean and uncluttered, with no overlapping text or broken pictures.

## What's weak

- The hero shoes are a fashion pair, maroon suede and leopard print. Neither looks like a comfort or walking shoe for a man my age.
- The copy says "non-stop comfort," but there's no cushioning, arch support, or fit detail anywhere.
- The Mercedes F1 giveaway takes up half the email and has no connection to $80 sneakers. It dilutes the offer.
- "$80 and under" doesn't say what's actually discounted. I can't tell whether these are sale prices or just the regular price on cheaper shoes.
- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" bar at the top competes with the headline, and its "Shop Now" button is small.
- There are no prices, reviews, or product links under the models it names.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the leopard and suede shoes with a men's walking or casual shoe, and show prices and star ratings under each of the four named models.
- 2. Move the F1 giveaway below the footer, or cut it. Keep the email to one idea.
- 3. Add one line on comfort, like "cushioned midsole, all-day wear," and make it specific.
- 4. Say whether $80 is a sale price. Show something like "was $100, now $75."
- 5. Link each named shoe to its own page instead of only "Shop Men" and "Shop Women."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `VL Court, Runfalcon, Barreda and Daily 1.0 — gift-ready prices.`

# Technical Audit: adidas, "Must-have sneakers for $80 and under"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive HTML build with a viewport meta tag and media queries. QA found one hard failure (no plain-text part) and several mixed-content (`http://`) image sources. The HTML supplied was truncated and the headers were incomplete, so the findings below are limited to what the QA data and visible source show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP check because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Click tracking is served over plain HTTP.** A tracked image uses `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1f7202438-ce3f9a1b-12e7-4081-af10-09705be1ad76-000000/CRT2nQoPi4puVyyeW_ScfewAF...`. This is a click-domain URL used as an image source, and it is flagged as non-HTTPS.
- **Insecure image host.** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` ("arrow") is referenced 6 times over HTTP. Clients that block mixed content may drop these images, and the HTTPS variant of the host should be used.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/83685c39-6339-4cb2-b682-4988c210f047?cp_tp=...`
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...CRT2nQoPi4puVyyeW_ScfewAFD_etiSqf_jKC_m-2-4=452`
  
  With images off, or in a screen reader, these are unlabeled. If either is the primary hero or CTA image, its content is lost.
- **Non-HTTPS images may be blocked.** The 6 `arrow` images and the click-domain image are affected (see section 2).
- **Fallback fonts only.** The `@font-face` rules for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` use only `local('Arial')`, `local('Helvetica')` and similar. This is a safe fallback, not a defect, but it means the brand fonts never load.
- **Doctype and charset.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, and the `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` tag has no `http-equiv` attribute, so it declares nothing. Charset should still come from the `Content-Type` header, but the tag is ineffective.
- **Positive:** `<html lang="en">` is set, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is present, and the mobile breakpoints are defined.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The HTML was truncated, so this applies only to the portion provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] No plain-text alternative.** The text version is 0 characters. A multipart/alternative message without a text part is a deliverability and spam-scoring risk, and it hurts text-only clients and accessibility.
- **Authentication unverified.** QA did not find an `Authentication-Results` header, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `us-news.comms.adidas.com` could not be confirmed. This is a gap in the relay's data, not a confirmed sender failure.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address not confirmed.** The truncated source and QA output don't show `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, an unsubscribe link, or a physical address. I can't call these missing or present from the data provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 22 tracked links were skipped, and the final destination URLs and any UTM parameters are hidden behind the click-redirect wrapper. The landing pages could not be checked against the "$80 and under" claim.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain part** with the offer, key links and the unsubscribe link (fixes the [FAIL]).
2. **Serve all images and the click-tracking domain over HTTPS**, specifically `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com`.
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to `dv.adidas.com/o/83685c39-6339-4cb2-b682-4988c210f047` and the click-domain image. Use `alt=""` if either is purely decorative.
4. **Give the `arrow` images a consistent alt value** (`alt=""` if decorative), and make sure `width` and `height` attributes are set.
5. **Fix the charset declaration** to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
6. **Capture the full headers and untruncated HTML in QA.** That would let the next run verify `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and tokens, and resolve redirects to check UTMs and landing-page continuity.
