# Join VIP to use this 20% off before it's too late
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and I've stopped counting. This is the same VIP / FABULOUS 20% pitch I've gotten all week, from "Become a VIP & get 20% off!" on the 29th through "Become a VIP & get 20% off!" and "20% off giftable holiday picks >>>" earlier today. The subject has been reworded one more time. The giant "20% OFF*" is easy to read on my phone, I'll give them that.
- Then I scroll down and it's a wall of women's shoes: pumps, slingbacks, a pink stiletto, a leopard heel. Of the 15 shoes, maybe two are anything I'd wear, a brown loafer and a black slip-on. Nothing says cushion, arch support or walking. I do see the "Shop Men" link, but it's tucked under the banner and the shoes below ignore it. The fine print also says Skechers is excluded from the code, and Skechers is half of what I buy.
- Would I click? Probably not. Forward it? No. I'd mark it read and move on. If I did tap, it would be "Shop Men," not the big black "Become a VIP" button.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" is huge and readable without zooming, and the code FABULOUS sits right under it.
- The black "Become a VIP" button is big and easy to tap with a thumb.
- "Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids" gives me a way in, even though it's small.
- The shoe-size row, with "Shop Wide Width" buttons, is useful. I do care about width and fit.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and "Join for free" lower the barrier.

## What's weak

- The product grid is almost all women's dress shoes. It feels aimed at someone else entirely.
- The subject says "before it's too late," but the fine print shows the offer runs from October 29 to November 1. It's the same deal I've been offered all week, so the urgency feels fake.
- The offer excludes Skechers, Clarks and other comfort brands, and that's buried far down the page. The headline doesn't mention it.
- The "Brand New…and So You" heading and the "Wonderland" banner in the logo don't say anything about comfort, cushioning or arch support.
- The fine print is tiny and runs long. I can't read it on my phone without pinching.
- The size numbers are plain text and don't look tappable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the grid follow what I've browsed. Show men's walking and slip-on comfort shoes, such as Hoka, Brooks, New Balance and Clarks, to someone like me.
- 2. Put the brand exclusions next to the offer, not at the bottom.
- 3. Stop sending the same code every day. Either rotate in a different reason to open, like gifts or a comfort edit, or send less often.
- 4. Make the "Shop Men" link a real button and put it above the women's shoes.
- 5. Make the size numbers look tappable and enlarge the fine print.

## Technical Email Audit: DSW, "Join VIP to use this 20% off before it's too late"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-link failure, the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned HTTP 400. It also has a few markup and accessibility defects in the head and images. The HTML was truncated and 62 of 74 links were not probed, so coverage is partial.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **SMS opt-in CTA returns 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off".
  - The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/RwP8ZXII5mKAFruRwAoarw~~/AAQRxRA~/A6qxIRU6E5Jj…`, a long `ablink.dsw.com` click-tracking redirect.
  - A 400 can mean the redirect token is malformed or expired. It can also mean the tracker rejects the QA probe, for example on its user-agent or lack of a referrer. This is the only hard failure, and it sits on a conversion CTA, so verify it with a real client before treating it as confirmed breakage.
  - The anchor's visible `href` text is empty in the QA output ("Broken link (400): "), which suggests the link label or destination may be malformed. Check this in the full source.
- **Probe coverage is limited.** Only 12 of 74 links were probed. The 62 skipped links are mostly footer, utility and social links, so their status is unknown.
- **The plain-text part is mostly tracking URLs.** 29,055 of 33,641 characters (about 86%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom in some clients, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
- **Two head `<meta>` tags are malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect. Charset detection then falls back to the HTTP header.
- **Linked image has no alt text.** The `src` is `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/N0vFiYwZ8mTu0uH52BLvkQ~~/…` and the image sits inside a tracked link. With images blocked, the link is unlabeled and screen readers announce a bare URL.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt attribute.** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/…/o.gif` should have `alt=""`. This is low severity, since it is decorative.
- **Web fonts are 10 `@font-face` rules from `braze-images.com`.** Two of them are `.ttf` files, which have limited email-client support. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source, so confirm one exists.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` appear in the visible source. The truncated HTML means this is not conclusive for the full body.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication could not be verified.** `Authentication-Results` is absent because of the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. This is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe link:** the QA tool always probes it and reported no failure for it.
- **Not verifiable from the data given:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address in the footer.
- **Sender domain:** `dsw@e.dsw.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be audited.** All destinations are wrapped in opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the final landing URLs were not captured.
- **Campaign ID appears only in the pixel.** It is `mi_ecmp=20261031_P251273_V3_WM-_Offer_MI_B`, in the `mi.dsw.com` open-tracking URL. Without resolved redirect chains, the campaign ID cannot be tied to the click-through URLs.
- **The broken SMS CTA has no landing page.** Since the redirect returned 400, there is nothing to check for offer alignment.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or re-issue the SMS opt-in tracking link.** Re-test it with a browser-like user-agent to rule out bot-blocking. If it fails for real clients, it is a high-priority fix because it blocks SMS list growth.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Correct the head meta tags.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the linked image (`rL0BQ9fdhl…`) and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
5. **Reduce URL density in the plain-text part.** Use shorter or fewer tracked URLs, or add more descriptive copy.
6. **Raise probe coverage** and capture resolved redirect chains so UTM and landing-page alignment can be audited.
7. **Run the audit against a relay that preserves `Authentication-Results`**, and capture `List-Unsubscribe` headers.
