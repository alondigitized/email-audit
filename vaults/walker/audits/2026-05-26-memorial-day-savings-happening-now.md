## Executive summary

- Alright, this is at least the tenth Crocs email in my inbox this month, and what do I see when I crack it open? A bright red classic clog. No studs, no florals, no platform wedges, no women's sandals with bows on them. Just a clog — the actual shoe I might wear in my own yard. That's a win compared to the parade of TikTok creative they've been pushing at me.
- Memorial Day, up to 50% off select styles, plus a Buy 2 Take 30% Off deal on Jibbitz charms and packs. Fine. The offer is real and the headline is big enough to read without me hunting for my reading glasses. But there's still no men's product shown, no sizing info, no mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, or anything that tells me this brand sees me.
- Would I click? Maybe — that red clog is at least in the right neighborhood. I'd probably tap "Up to 50% Off" out of curiosity, see that men's classics aren't actually 50% off, and bounce. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero is finally a plain classic clog — no studs, no platforms, no Jibbitz overload. That's the silhouette I'd actually consider.
- "Up to 50% Off!" is big, bold, and readable at arm's length without zooming.
- Two distinct offers stacked clearly — site sale on top, Jibbitz Buy 2 Take 30% below. Easy to follow on a phone.
- Layout is clean and uncluttered. White background, big type, no cartoon nonsense.
- Memorial Day tie-in is at least seasonally appropriate — feels current, not stale.

## What's weak

- No CTA button. Anywhere. I see the headline and the shoe, but there's no "Shop Sale" or "Shop Men's" button to tap. That's a layout miss.
- No men's product, no gender callout, no styling cues that say this sale includes anything for a 62-year-old guy in a size 10 wide.
- "Exclusions Apply" sitting right under the hero — translation: the shoe I want probably isn't 50% off.
- Zero comfort language. Not one word about cushioning, arch support, all-day wear — the reasons I'd buy a clog in the first place.
- App store badges before any clear product link. I don't want your app — I want to see the sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a visible primary CTA button** — "Shop the Sale" right under the hero. As it stands there's nothing to tap, which is a baffling miss on a promo email.
- 2. **Show a men's product, or at least a men's section link.** A red clog is a start, but tell me the men's classics are part of the sale and link me there.
- 3. **Lead with one comfort line** — "LiteRide cushioning. All-day wear." One sentence is all I need.
- 4. **Move "Exclusions Apply" out of the hero area.** Putting it right under "Up to 50% Off" kills the offer's credibility before I've even processed it.
- 5. **Push the Jibbitz block down or kill it.** I'm here for shoes; the charms-and-packs deal is bait for the kids and grandkids, not me.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 32 tracking/click-redirect links skipped by automated HTTP probe — not verified reachable end-to-end. Recommend periodic live-probe of a sampled subset.
- Open-tracking pixel served from `demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?...` — third-party Adobe Audience Manager domain. With Apple MPP and Gmail image proxying, open rates from this pixel will be inflated/unreliable; acceptable but worth noting.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **7 social/utility images served over `http://`** (`image.crocs-email.com/...`) — Gmail and Outlook will block or warn on mixed content:
  - App Store badge (`9c36c0ef-...png`)
  - Google Play badge (`13246762-...png`)
  - Find Your Store CTA (`c4df71ec-...png`)
  - Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube icons
- **Tracking pixel missing `alt=""`** — should be explicit empty alt to mark as decorative for screen readers (currently `<img>` with no alt attribute at all).
- Preheader uses both a `.preheader` div AND a long zero-width-character snow block; redundant but harmless. Good practice already in place.
- `lang="en"` set on `<html>` — good.
- Font-face declarations include `font-display: swap` and woff2/woff fallback — correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
