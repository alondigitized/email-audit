## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a responsive table-based template with a mobile media-query set and a client-fix CSS block. The QA run flagged missing alt text on four images and one unverifiable authentication check. Its two tracking pixels show malformed or unresolved URL parameters. The HTML source is truncated, so the body, footer and headers could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 33 tracking links were skipped by the probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261127_black_friday_AM​`
  - The `t` parameter value itself contains `utm_campaign=...`, which is a nested key=value in one query slot.
  - The QA output shows an invisible trailing character after `_AM`. It looks like a zero-width space (U+200B), but I can't confirm that from the output alone.
  - If a zero-width space is present, it can corrupt campaign attribution.
- **Unresolved placeholder:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` contains a literal `[UNIQUE]`. It may be an ESP macro that was not substituted, or a deliberate cache-buster. If it is the former, open-tracking for this pixel will not resolve per recipient.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** four images flagged as missing alt:
  - Content images, both on `image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/`:
    - `8596c416-45c9-4d51-afc5-a39814038aba.jpg`
    - `56f4d386-deaa-4ef4-83a9-77da7e03c3a0.jpg`
  - Tracking pixels `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` and `0di86k1v.emltrk.com`. These are lower priority, but should carry `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so the IE=edge directive and the content-type declaration do nothing. `charset="utf-8"` still works.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and self-closing `/>` tags. This mix is not strictly valid but is common in email and unlikely to cause rendering failures.
- **Link styling:** the global rule `a, a:link, ... { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` strips underlines and link colour from all anchors. Links are then distinguishable only by surrounding styling, which is a potential WCAG 1.4.1 concern. This depends on how link text is styled in the body, which was not visible.
- **Mobile layout:** viewport meta is present, `<html lang="en">` is set, and `color-scheme` is declared light-only. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge tokens are visible in the truncated source.
- The only token-like string is `[UNIQUE]` in the pixel URL (see section 2).
- No other issues found.

## 5. Compliance

- **Unsubscribe, physical address and List-Unsubscribe:** not assessable. The footer and headers were not in the truncated source or QA data.
- **Sender domain:** `email.shoecarnival.com` is a branded subdomain. Link and image hosts are also branded (`image.email.shoecarnival.com`), which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The only UTM evidence is `utm_campaign=SCA_20261127_black_friday_AM`, and it appears inside a pixel URL rather than on a landing link.
- Because the 33 click links were not probed, `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and landing-page alignment are unverified.
- The subject promises "BOOTS UNDER $20 in-store + Online Doorbusters", but destination pages were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `ieezzy5m` pixel URL: separate `t` and the UTM parameters properly, and strip the trailing invisible character.
2. Confirm whether `[UNIQUE]` in the `0di86k1v` pixel resolves at send time. If it is a literal, fix the ESP substitution.
3. Add `alt` text to the two content images (`8596c416…jpg`, `56f4d386…jpg`) and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers to verify unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe, physical address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and UTM consistency on a sample of the 33 click links.
6. For the pipeline: make the AgentMail relay preserve original authentication headers, or note them separately. The `Authentication-Results` warning will otherwise recur on every audit.
