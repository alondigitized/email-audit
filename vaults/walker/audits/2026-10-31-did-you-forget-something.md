# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same cart nag I've gotten all week, and I still haven't put a single thing in a cart. "Ready to check out?" over a gold Halloween-ish banner is just wrong. There's no cart here, only a taupe sneaker and a grid of "best sellers." It's the same routine as "Did you forget something?" on 10/29 and "Your cart is one click away!" the same day. After "Don't leave these styles behind!" yesterday, I'm tired of it.
- The one useful thing is buried halfway down: 20% off for Skechers Plus members, valid through 11/2. I'd have liked to see that first. The men's Slip-ins, the Contour Foam sneaker, the waterproof Orson boot and the Murette slipper are right up my alley. But half the grid is women's shoes, and nothing tells me price, width or cushioning.
- Would I click? Maybe, because of the boot and the 20%. Forward to a friend? No. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The Slip-ins styles (Contour Foam, Orson waterproof boot, Murette slipper) are hands-free and comfort-first, which is what I shop for.
- "20% OFF for Skechers Plus members" is a clear number with a clear end date (valid thru 11/2).
- The SKECHERS logo at the top is familiar, so I trust it's real.
- The black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar and the orange "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button are big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- Product names like "Arch Fit" and "Relaxed Fit" tell me something about comfort.

## What's weak

- "Ready to check out?" and "Complete your order" imply I left something in a cart, and I didn't. It feels pushy and a little dishonest.
- The 20% offer sits below six shoes, so I'd never see it without a long scroll.
- Three of the six shoes are women's, and the big hero sneaker doesn't say men's or women's.
- There are no prices, no sale prices, no star ratings and no sizes. I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- The fine print is tiny. "Must be logged in" and "select styles excluded" make the 20% feel conditional.
- There's a big empty gap under the banner before the hero shoe, and the "web version" link is tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake cart-abandonment framing unless there's a real cart. Lead with the 20% Plus member offer and a men's Slip-ins hero.
- 2. Make the grid men's comfort only, with price, sale price and star ratings under each shoe.
- 3. Move the 20% banner above the product grid and bump up the text size.
- 4. Cut the duplicate "Complete your order" and "Shop all" buttons down to one.
- 5. Make the fine print readable, or put "Plus members only, log in to save" right on the banner.
- 6. Tighten the cadence. Cart nags every day or two make me tune out.
