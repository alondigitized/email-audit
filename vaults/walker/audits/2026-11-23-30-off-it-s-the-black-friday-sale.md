# 30% OFF! It's the 🖤 Black Friday Sale 🖤
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same pink banner and giant "30% OFF*" I've been seeing since the 20th, and it's the fourth or fifth one I've deleted this week. At least this one skips the "join VIP" nagging from the 22nd. It just says the sale is on and gives me a code, BFDEALS. That's the same message as this morning's "BLACK FRIDAY'S 30% OFF STARTS… NOW!", so I'm getting two of these in one day.
- The whole email is women's booties, with a pretty girl in a blue fur coat and a laughing woman in a red jacket. I'm a 62-year-old man who wants a cushioned slip-on, and I didn't see a single men's shoe. "SHOP MEN" is a small text link under the banner, and that's all I get. The "Shop by size" row and the "wide width" button are handy, but the wide-width button is the only comfort cue anywhere.
- I'd skim it and move on. I'd click only if I were already planning a trip to the store, and I wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF*" is the first thing I see, and the black "CLAIM 30% OFF" button under it is easy to find.
- The code BFDEALS is right there, so I don't have to go hunting for it.
- The "Shop by size" grid has big numbers, so I can read it on my phone without zooming.
- "Shop Wide Width" is a useful option for feet like mine.
- The footer row (free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup, Afterpay) tells me what I need to know about getting the shoes.

## What's weak

- Everything below the banner is women's booties and heels. The only thing for me is a small "SHOP MEN" text link.
- The asterisk on "30% OFF*" and "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" makes me think the fine print is hiding something. The exclusions are down at the bottom in tiny type I can't read.
- Nothing in this email mentions cushioning, arch support, slip-ons or walking. I get no comfort cue at all.
- It's the same creative and same offer I've seen all week, and the scrolling "THE SEASON'S BIGGEST DEALS ARE ON!" strip at the top is cluttered.
- The "Free Tote" with a $75 purchase is a gimmick, and it's in-store only. I don't care about it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me a men's version. Put a comfort shoe on the hero, like a slip-on walker or a cushioned sneaker, and make "SHOP MEN" a real button.
- 2. Send one Black Friday email a day. Right now I get several for the same sale.
- 3. Say what the asterisk means up front, for example "30% off regular-price shoes, in stores and online."
- 4. Add a comfort row with brand names, such as "Cushioned walkers from Skechers, Brooks and New Balance."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is mostly sound technically, with Braze click-wrapped links, dark-mode support and web fonts. QA found one failing material link (the SMS opt-in CTA, HTTP 400), two images with missing alt text, and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs. The HTML source was truncated and 60 of 72 links were not probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA, HTTP 400.** Link text: "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/SJejTPEgCPEZjT_qRlonUg~~/AAQRxRA~/o5AlAAAQ…`.
  - The QA report shows an empty destination after the status code, so the redirect target is unknown.
  - The wrapped URL also appears cut off in the report (it ends `…lDB6`). Check whether the 400 comes from truncation in the probe or from a real Braze link failure or expiry.
  - A 400 from a tracked link can also be bot-rejection. Re-test in a real client before treating it as broken for subscribers.
  - If it is real, the SMS-acquisition CTA is dead for every recipient.
- **Plain text is mostly URLs.** 23,909 of 29,370 chars (about 81%) are URLs, because of long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking strings. That is above the 70% threshold and can raise spam-filter scores.
- **Click coverage.** Only 12 of 72 links were probed, so the other 60 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (linked image).** `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/rUIcnWK5JCSEhwBi86iUgg~~/AAQRxRA~/KjvVHxQzhN-ex-rSkJh0HAyZ_YW56YpM1I9gFZnPE-s9xb8Jh1…`
  - This is a Braze-wrapped image. If it is a clickable image, screen readers will announce the raw URL, and with images blocked there is no fallback text.
- **Missing alt (tracking pixel).** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=…`
  - This is a tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so it is treated as decorative.
- **Malformed meta tags.** The head contains `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`. Both lack `http-equiv`, so they have no effect. Declare the charset with `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
- **Font declarations.** `Src:` is capitalized in the `@font-face` rules. CSS is case-insensitive here, so this is harmless.
- No other rendering defects are visible in the truncated source.

No issues found. No unresolved tokens, such as `{{ }}` or `%%`, appear in the visible HTML or the QA data. The truncated portion was not reviewed.
