# Back-to-school best sellers 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Famous Footwear's back in my inbox again — I've lost count, but between the "100+ NEW styles," the BOGO ones, and now this "best sellers" email, that's got to be my seventh or eighth from them this summer alone. This one's a mixed bag. Credit where it's due: there's finally a real Men's row with the Asics trail runner, the Nike Court Vision, an Adidas Duramo, and some Stag skate shoe — that's more than I got in the "Snap-worthy" or kids-heavy sends. But it's still sandwiched between a Women's row and a Kids' row, and none of the men's picks scream comfort or arch support to me — a trail shoe and a skate shoe aren't exactly what I'm shopping for.
- What actually caught my eye wasn't the "best sellers" framing at all — it was the purple BOGO banner up top (buy one, get one half off, plus 2X points) and the Birkenstock/Nike/Jordan strip further down. Those are real, concrete deals. The subject line itself, though, told me nothing — "best sellers" isn't an offer, it's just a claim, and the preview line in my inbox was a garbled tracking link, not real preview copy.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already shopping — the BOGO offer is decent and the Men's CTA is right there. Would I forward it to a buddy? No. Nothing here is specifically for a guy my age looking for slip-on comfort, and I've seen this brand hit my inbox so often lately it's starting to blur together.

## What's working

- The BOGO half-off + 2X points banner is impossible to miss — first thing my eye lands on.
- There's an actual Men's section this time, not just an afterthought — four shoes, clear photos, clean labels.
- "SHOP MEN" is a real, tappable button, not buried in a paragraph.
- The Birkenstock/Nike/Jordan strip near the bottom has legitimate percentage-off numbers, not vague "save big" talk.
- Layout is clean top to bottom — no overlapping text, no broken images, easy to scroll on my phone.

## What's weak

- None of the men's shoes shown (trail runner, court sneaker, running shoe, skate shoe) read as "comfort, cushioned, slip-on" — feels like a generic men's assortment, not curated for someone like me.
- The subject line "best sellers" tells me nothing about what's actually on sale — no dollar figure, no percentage, no urgency.
- The preview text that showed up next to the subject in my inbox was just a tracking link — junk, not real copy.
- Still sandwiched between Women's and Kids' sections before I even get to anything for me, and this is at least my seventh Famous Footwear email this summer, so the cadence is wearing thin.
- The fine print ("Exclusions apply," "Prices as marked") on the Birkenstock/Nike/Jordan deals means I'd have to click through just to know if my size or style even qualifies.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject line with the actual offer — "BOGO 1/2 off + 2X points" beats "best sellers" every time; specificity is what gets me to open.
- 2. Fix the preview text — right now it's a raw tracking URL leaking into my inbox instead of real copy.
- 3. If you're going to send me a men's row, put comfort-brand names in it — Skechers, Hoka, New Balance walking shoes, not just a trail runner and a skate shoe.
- 4. Put the brand-name deals (Birkenstock, Nike, Jordan) higher up — those percentages are more compelling than "best sellers" ever will be.
- 5. Cut back the send frequency — this is close to eight emails from Famous Footwear this summer, and repetition is dulling the impact of a genuinely decent offer like today's BOGO.
