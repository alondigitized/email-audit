# Member Deals Ahead! Don't Miss Out
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- Another Skechers Plus email, another morning where the first thing I see in my inbox is that same wall of raw code gibberish where the preview text should be. I've lost count — this is somewhere around the ninth or tenth email in a row with that broken JSON showing up before I've touched a single button. At this point I've stopped being surprised and started being insulted. You'd think whoever runs the list over at Skechers HQ would've caught this weeks ago. The June 18th "It's your month" send had the exact same problem, and so did every single one before it this month.
- Once I actually tap in, the email is decent enough. The BOGO deal is front and center for members, the layout doesn't break on my phone, and I can see the men's shoe section without having to hunt around. The $250 gift card sweepstakes at the top is a nice hook — I'd actually enter that, because two-fifty in Skechers store credit would last me a good while.
- But this email is broadcasting to everyone, not talking to me. The hero is a woman in a polka-dot sundress. Nothing on the screen says "comfort," "cushioning," or "arch support." If I didn't already know what Skechers makes, I'd think I was looking at a back-to-school sale flyer. I'll mark it read, maybe click over to the men's section, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off offer is immediately readable — big font, clear structure, and a "SHOP TODAY" button that isn't hiding anywhere.
- Skechers Plus member framing is right up front; being called out as a member rather than just a random email address felt like they know I belong to the club.
- "Member Appreciated Month" plus the gift card sweepstakes is a genuine loyalty play — $250 is real money and worth a few seconds of my time.
- The men's shoes section is visible without scrolling on my phone and shows actual walking-style shoes that look like something I'd wear.
- Email body renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, nothing to confuse me once I'm inside.

## What's weak

- That JSON preview text is back. Again. Tenth time in a row, roughly. A real subscriber has stopped giving them the benefit of the doubt.
- The hero model is a woman in a sundress — that's the first human image I see, and it immediately signals this email isn't built for me.
- "Don't Miss Out" in the subject line is the laziest urgency trick in the book; there's no end date, no countdown, nothing that makes it feel genuinely time-sensitive.
- The gift card sweepstakes and the BOGO deal compete for attention at the top — two different asks in the first scroll means I have to figure out which one matters, and I'm not doing homework for a marketing email.
- afterpay and Klarna logos at the bottom are noise for someone my age — I pay what I owe when I owe it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill that JSON preview immediately.** This is the single biggest thing dragging this program down. Every open starts in the hole because of it. Whatever schema markup is leaking into the preheader needs to be stripped or quarantined before it touches the envelope.
- 2. **Lead the hero with a man walking in comfortable shoes, not a woman at a patio party.** Walker Miles does not see himself in that image. One well-shot photo of a guy in his early 60s wearing cushioned slip-ons on a walking path would do more for my click rate than any coupon.
- 3. **Pick one top offer, not two.** Sweepstakes entry OR BOGO — not both stacked on top of each other. If the gift card is the hook, make it the hook. If the BOGO drives revenue, lead with that.
- 4. **Add a deadline to the urgency.** "Don't Miss Out" means nothing. "Member Month ends June 30" means I might actually move today.
- 5. **Mention comfort.** One line — "cushioned picks for all-day wear" or similar — would make this feel like it was written for someone with my priorities, not just a generic member blast.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Member Month Deal: BOGO 50% Off — Ends June 30`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Skechers Plus BOGO is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one pair, get a second 50% off. Shop men's comfort styles now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Member Appreciated Month: BOGO deal + enter to win a $250 gift card.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — SKECHERS PLUS "Member Deals Ahead" (06/21/2026)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard SFMC-deployed promotional send with functioning structure and responsive CSS. The primary technical concerns are pervasive HTTP image sources (13 assets), a third-party pixel loaded over plain HTTP, and two missing alt attributes on non-decorative images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links use click-redirect domains** (`click.emails.skechers.com`) — expected for SFMC, not a defect, but confirms all click data is ESP-owned and no direct destination links are exposed for validation.

**Open pixel loaded over HTTP:**
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_LOYL_EN_06212026
```
This third-party open-tracking pixel (Marin Software / ink1000.com) uses plain HTTP. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy images over HTTPS — this request will either be blocked or silently upgraded, potentially corrupting open-tracking attribution for this vendor.

**Returnpath pixel missing protocol:**
```html
<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" ...>
```
No `https://` prefix. This is a malformed URL — the browser/client will attempt to resolve it as a relative path, meaning the Validity (Returnpath) inbox monitoring pixel fires zero data for this send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**13 image assets served over HTTP** from `image.emails.skechers.com`. Since this is Skechers' own ESP subdomain, an HTTPS certificate should be trivially available. Clients that enforce mixed-content blocking (Gmail Android, some Outlook builds) will suppress these images entirely, breaking the visual layout.

Affected assets include the Skechers logo, App Store/Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube icons.

**Two images missing `alt` text on non-decorative assets:**
- `49468f73...png` — `src` path (`/m/11/`) suggests a content image, not a spacer; alt="" is ambiguous at best
- `o.gif` (ink1000 pixel) — alt="" is acceptable for a tracking pixel but flagged by the QA tool

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is set globally via `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }`. This disables iOS font scaling for users with accessibility font-size preferences set. Standard practice is `100%` not `none`.

**Duplicate responsive breakpoints:** The same ruleset is defined twice — once for `max-width: 375px` and again in the `max-width: 640px` block, plus a third `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block in a second `<style>` tag. This is functional but increases parse overhead and maintenance risk.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `%%firstName%%`, `{{subscriber.FirstName}}`) are visible in the truncated source. Preheader text is static:
```
Members can enter to win a $250 gift card and BOGO 50% off or 20% off 1 pair
```
No personalization token failures detectable from available source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM:** Physical mailing address and unsubscribe mechanism are expected in the footer — not visible in the truncated source provided. QA summary reports 0 issues, suggesting these are present in the untruncated portion.

**Authentication headers:** Not inspectable from HTML source alone. SFMC sends for `emails.skechers.com` typically carry SPF/DKIM alignment — no evidence of misconfiguration in the source, but DMARC alignment should be verified against actual received headers.

**Krux/Salesforce DMP pixels** are present:
```
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=9153f33fe5cdf59baf088bb835d3b39ef90f188bd8540a4faba92899227ced00
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid&partner_uid=121173391
```
The `sha25` variant passes a hashed subscriber identifier; the `subid` variant passes a plaintext subscriber ID (`121173391`). Passing a raw subscriber ID to a third-party DMP pixel without explicit consent language may warrant legal review depending on jurisdiction (CCPA/GDPR applicability for US loyalty members).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The campaign ID `PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_LOYL_EN_06212026` appears consistently across the Krux impression pixel and the ink1000 open pixel, indicating campaign tagging is internally consistent.

All 26 click links route through `click.emails.skechers.com` — UTM parameters on destination URLs are not verifiable from the source alone. If UTM params are appended at redirect time by SFMC, this is not an issue; if they rely on landing page defaults, attribution gaps are likely.
