## What's weak

- The hero is a women's pink floral sneaker I've seen over and over. It has nothing to do with me.
- The "more great options" grid is mostly kids' and women's shoes. Only one of six is for a man my age.
- The fine print says the 20% off is for boots and outdoor styles only, online only. That doesn't match the shoes shown, and it feels like a bait-and-switch.

- Cadence is the worst part. This is the same cart reminder again, on top of yesterday's four.
