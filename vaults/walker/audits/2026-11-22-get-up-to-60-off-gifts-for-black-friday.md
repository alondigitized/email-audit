# Get up to 60% off gifts for Black Friday
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email. I've lost count since the 14th. This one is the plainest of the bunch, and I mean that kindly. "UP TO 60% OFF" in big white letters across the photo is easy to read without squinting. Then there's a young couple against a brick wall, and none of that is aimed at a 62-year-old guy looking for comfortable shoes.
- The body is simple, and I like that. One headline, two sentences, and three big black buttons. The buttons are easy to tap with a thumb. But "Shop Men, Women, Kids" just sends me to a department-store hallway. And "up to 60%" doesn't tell me whether the shoes I'd wear are 60% off or 10% off.
- Would I click? Maybe the Galaxy 7 running shoe, out of curiosity about the price. Would I forward it? No. I'll mark it read and move on. It's the fourth or fifth near-identical Black Friday email this week. Compared to the 11-18 countdown send, which had a real deadline, this one has no urgency at all.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" text is big and high contrast. I can read it on my phone without zooming.
- The three Shop Men / Women / Kids buttons are large and stacked. They're easy to tap.
- The layout is clean and short, with no clutter to scroll through.
- Showing the Galaxy 7 running shoe and the Campus 00s with their names gives me something to look at.
- It's the same adidas look I've seen all week, so I trust it's the real thing.

## What's weak

- The hero is two young people in streetwear. Nothing in it speaks to comfort, cushioning or walking.
- There are no prices on any product. I can't tell the discount from the regular price.
- "Up to 60% off" with no deadline. There's no clock or end date like the 11-18 send had.
- The top banner, "Explore the latest adidas best sellers," has a tiny Shop Now button and repeats what's below it.
- The product shoes are small. The Galaxy 7 is a plain black runner, but nothing says it's cushioned or has arch support.
- Nothing shows that I'm a member or a past customer. It reads like it went to everybody.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put sale prices on the top sellers, like "Galaxy 7, $49.99, was $80." That would do more than anything else.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Monday midnight."
- 3. Add a "Shop Men's Walking & Comfort" button instead of a generic Men button.
- 4. Feature a comfort shoe with a line about cushioning or fit, and use an older model in the hero.
- 5. Drop the redundant top banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Galaxy 7 and more, up to 60% off through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's cushioned shoes up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walking and running shoes from $39. Sale ends Monday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort picks for men, marked down for Black Friday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based template with responsive media queries. Automated QA found two real problems: no plain-text alternative (0 chars) and several `http://` image sources, including the open-tracking pixel. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **25 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination, redirect chain and final status are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel loads over plain HTTP:**
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a2010540d6-9aa22a3d-32e3-4563-ad70-0d4899c79593-000000/PawJUQWm9vfeKt3-X1VpHh_Om…`
  - It also has no `alt`, which QA flagged as `PawJUQWm9vfeKt3-X1VpHh_Om0enqOygX9r6g2TUXgY=452`.
  - Mail clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS images may skip it and under-count opens.
- **The click-tracking domain differs from the sending domain.** Links use `click.comms.adidas.com` and `image.link.adidas.com`, while the From domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`. All are subdomains of `adidas.com`, so alignment is acceptable, but I can't confirm the tracking domain's SSL or CNAME setup from this data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images:** the `arrow` image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is used 6 times over HTTP. Some clients may block it, which would leave broken-image placeholders.
- **Missing alt text:** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/1348660e-c671-45e3-a842-099bf521e7bf?cp_tp=…` has no `alt`. If it is content or a CTA, its meaning is lost with images off or in screen readers. It is also the only image besides the tracking pixel flagged for this.
- **Malformed charset meta:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing. Encoding then depends on the MIME header alone.
- **Legacy doctype:** `HTML 4.01 Transitional` is paired with self-closing `<meta … />` tags. It is tolerated by clients but inconsistent.
- **Custom fonts don't load:** the `@font-face` rules for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` use only `src: local('Arial'), local('Helvetica')…`. Nothing is downloaded, so these fall back to Arial or Helvetica. This is harmless but redundant. The `@font-face` blocks are also duplicated across two `<style>` blocks, which adds weight.
- **Good practice found:** `<html lang="en">`, a viewport meta, and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text part:** QA reports `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. A missing text/plain alternative in multipart mail is a deliverability and accessibility weakness. It can occur because the capture pipeline stored only the HTML part, so confirm against the raw MIME.
- **Authentication unverified:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The relay (AgentMail) likely strips it, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown rather than failing.
- **Not checkable:** the physical postal address, the unsubscribe link and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers are not visible in the truncated source or the QA output. I am not flagging them as missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are click-redirect URLs, and QA did not follow them, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "up to 60% off gifts" cannot be confirmed. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `ROBOTS: NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW` are present, which is normal for web-view copies.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all images, including the tracking pixel and the `arrow` asset, over HTTPS. Switch `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com` references to `https://`.
2. Add `alt` text to `dv.adidas.com/o/1348660e-…`. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and for the tracking pixel.
3. Include a text/plain part with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL.
4. Fix the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. Follow at least the primary CTA redirects in the QA harness and record final URLs and UTM parameters, so continuity can be audited.
6. Capture the full raw headers, or the Gmail "Show original" view, to check `Authentication-Results` and `List-Unsubscribe`, and the full HTML to check the footer address and unsubscribe link.
7. Optional cleanup: merge the duplicate `@font-face` blocks and drop the local-only font rules.
