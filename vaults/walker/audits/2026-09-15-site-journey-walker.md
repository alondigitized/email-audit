# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 2/10 (prev 2/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I went to buy a pair of comfortable slip-on walking shoes on my phone — same as always. The homepage looked fine, browsing to Men's Shoes was easy, and the product page for the Slip-ins Contour Foam had what I look for (hands-free, cushioned, $88, 4 reviews). But the moment it mattered — actually getting the shoe into a cart I could check out from — the site fell apart. I never got past "please select a size," and when I asked to view my cart, I got a **blank white screen**. No items, no error, nothing. I also couldn't tell if logging in did anything at all — the screen after "Log In" looked identical to the homepage, cart still at zero, no name, no "Welcome back."

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**2/10** — unchanged from yesterday. Browsing and product discovery are fine, but the cart is the one job that has to work on a shopping site, and it's flat-out broken (blank page). A blank cart page after "adding to cart" will make a lot of people assume the site crashed and just bail — I nearly did.

## 3. What's Working

- Homepage is clean, big logo, clear 20% off banner, hamburger menu is a familiar pattern.
- Men > Shoes menu drill-down (Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections) is logically organized and the back arrow is easy to find.
- Men's Shoes landing page leads with "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes" tiles right at the top — that's exactly my kind of shoe, front and center.
- Product detail page has what I need to decide: price, member discount code, star rating, review count, and a callout badge for "Hands Free Slip-ins."
- Size grid on the PDP is large, tappable, and clearly greys out sold-out sizes.
- Search for "comfort shoes" returned relevant, on-topic results (slip-ins, max cushioning) with prices and discounts visible without a tap.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Cart is broken.** After tapping into cart, I got a totally blank white page — no header, no items, no "your cart is empty" message, nothing. This is a full failure of the single most important step in the entire journey.
- **Add to Cart never completed.** I hit the size grid and got "⚠ Please select a size" — the flow stalled there in the captured journey and the cart badge stayed at 0 the entire time, including after the cart step and the search step. I never actually got a shoe into my bag.
- **Login is a no-op, or at least invisible.** The screenshot after "Log In" is pixel-for-pixel the same as the homepage — same generic person icon, same cart count, no name or account indicator anywhere. If I'm logged in, the site gave me zero signal of it.
- **No personalization anywhere.** No "Welcome back, Walker," no recently viewed, no loyalty points balance, nothing tailored — even though this persona is receptive to loyalty messaging as long as the shoe still wins.
- Top promo banner ("20% OFF Sitewide") and secondary banner ("Fall Preview Sale") stack up and eat a big chunk of the screen before I see anything useful — on a phone that's a lot of scrolling past marketing before real content.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page rendering bug immediately** — a blank white screen after adding to cart is a checkout-killing, revenue-killing bug. This should be priority one, full stop.
2. **Verify the add-to-cart flow completes and updates the cart badge** — right now there's no visible confirmation a shoe was actually added (badge stuck at 0 throughout).
3. **Make login state visible** — swap the person icon for a name/initials, or show a toast/banner confirming sign-in, so people like me trust that logging in actually did something.
4. **Add real personalization for logged-in users** — order history, saved size, loyalty point balance. This persona explicitly won't be swayed by points alone, but the absence of any personalization at all is a missed trust signal ("Trusts a brand more when the email matches what he saw scrolling").
5. **Trim the stacked promo banners on mobile** to get to real content faster.

## 6. Bottom Line

Browsing feels like a real shoe store built for phones. But you can't buy a shoe here right now — the cart is blank and nothing ever visibly entered it. Until that's fixed, everything else is decoration. **No change from yesterday's 2/10** — same critical failure, cart still broken.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

The mobile journey traverses 8 steps on skechers.com (SFCC/Demandware storefront). The build loads a heavy third-party tag stack (30+ async scripts: GTM, Monetate, TikTok, Snapchat, Taboola, Quantum Metric, Bazaarvoice, etc.) on every page. Two significant defects surfaced: (1) the bot-detection/fingerprinting endpoint (Kasada, `x-kpsdk-v`) and the `/cart/` page itself returned HTTP 429 during the session, and (2) the View Cart step rendered without a `<title>` or `lang` attribute, indicating either a broken/partial page state or a non-standard render path for that step.

## Accessibility

18 axe-core violations across 3 of 8 steps (Homepage, View Cart, Search).

- **Critical**: `button-name` and `aria-required-parent` on Homepage — buttons without accessible names and ARIA roles used without required parent roles will not be announced correctly by screen readers.
- **Serious**: `link-name` (Homepage) — links with no discernible text. `document-title` and `html-has-lang` (View Cart) — both fail WCAG 2.1 A and will affect assistive tech language handling and browser/tab identification.
- **Moderate**: Duplicate/non-top-level `main` and `complementary` landmarks (`landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`) on Homepage and Search — points to nested or duplicated layout regions, likely from the third-party widget injection pattern (Monetate/Stylitics slots inserting competing landmark roles). `heading-order` and `region` violations on Homepage indicate content outside landmark regions and non-sequential heading levels. View Cart also fails `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one`, consistent with the missing-title/lang issue — this page appears to not have loaded its normal document shell.
- **Minor**: `aria-allowed-role` on Homepage and Search (misused role attributes).

Men category, Men > Shoes, Product Detail, Add to Cart, and Log In steps: no violations reported.

## SEO

- View Cart is missing `<title>` and `<html lang>` — both are basic on-page SEO/indexability requirements; if this reflects the actual served markup (not just a timing artifact), any crawler hitting this state loses page identification and language targeting.
- Homepage heading order and landmark/region structure issues (see Accessibility) degrade the semantic outline search engines use to understand page hierarchy.
- No issues found in canonical/meta tag presence, crawlability, or hreflang based on the provided HTML samples (Homepage/Log In/Men category all show correct `<html lang="en">` and structured `<head>`).

## Performance

TTFB was captured for all 8 steps; LCP and CLS were not captured (instrumentation gap, not a site finding — flag for re-run with a full Web Vitals collector, e.g., PerformanceObserver for `largest-contentful-paint` and `layout-shift` entries).

- TTFB is elevated on the first three steps — Homepage (433ms), Log In (435ms), Men category (435ms) — consistent with server-rendered SFCC responses plus personalization (Monetate) round-trips.
- TTFB drops sharply on Men > Shoes (26ms), Product Detail (31ms), Add to Cart (31ms), and Search (29ms), suggesting these are served from cache/CDN or client-side navigation rather than fresh SSR.
- View Cart TTFB (124ms) is mid-range but should be read alongside the 429 on `/cart/` and the missing-title/lang finding — the cart response may have been served in a degraded/error state rather than a normal cacheable response.
- LCP/CLS: cannot assess — no data returned by the audit tooling for any step. Recommend re-instrumenting before drawing conclusions on visual load performance.

## Mobile Optimization

- No mobile-specific viewport, tap-target, or media-query issues surfaced in the provided data.
- The volume of synchronous/async third-party tags (30+ scripts on Homepage alone) is a mobile-relevant concern: each contributes to main-thread contention on lower-power devices, though no blocking/long-task metrics were captured to quantify impact here.
- CLS data is unavailable, which is the primary metric that would confirm/deny mobile layout-shift issues from late-loading ads/personalization slots (Monetate, Stylitics, Pulse Insights). Recommend re-running with CLS capture given the number of injected widgets.

## Console & Network Errors

- **429 (Too Many Requests)** on the Kasada bot-detection fingerprint endpoint (`/…/fp?x-kpsdk-v=…`), recurring 4 times, plus a **429 on `/cart/` itself**. This indicates the client was rate-limited by the bot-mitigation layer during the automated session — likely a side effect of the audit tooling's request pattern being flagged, but the fact that the actual cart page returned 429 rather than the fingerprint call alone is notable and would block real users in the same rate-limit bucket.
- **CORS failure**: `web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css` blocked by CORS policy (missing `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`) — this third-party outfit-recommendation widget's stylesheet fails to load cross-origin, likely a misconfiguration on the Stylitics-served asset rather than skechers.com.
- **Summarizer API errors** (×4): repeated "Unsupported Summarizer API languages" — this is the Chrome built-in `Summarizer` API being invoked with an unsupported language parameter, aborting each call. Points to a first- or third-party script probing/using the experimental on-device AI API without checking supported locales first.
- `net::ERR_FAILED` corresponds to the blocked Stylitics stylesheet request above.

## Recommendations

1. Investigate why `/cart/` and the Kasada fingerprint endpoint returned 429 — confirm whether this is bot-mitigation misfiring on legitimate mobile traffic or an artifact of the audit's request cadence; if real-user-reproducible, this is a conversion blocker.
2. Fix the View Cart step's missing `<title>` and `<html lang>` — verify whether this is a genuine broken render path (e.g., an error/interstitial state) or a false capture, since it also drives the `landmark-one-main`/`page-has-heading-one` failures on the same step.
3. Address Homepage `button-name` and `link-name` violations — audit icon-only buttons/links for missing `aria-label`/accessible text.
4. Resolve duplicate/nested `main` and `complementary` landmarks, likely introduced by third-party widget injection (Monetate/Stylitics/Pulse Insights) — scope injected DOM so it doesn't compete with the page's native landmark structure.
5. Fix the Stylitics stylesheet CORS misconfiguration (coordinate with Stylitics or self-host/proxy the asset).
6. Remove or gate the Summarizer API call so it only fires with a supported language, or remove it if unused in production.
7. Re-run the audit with LCP/CLS collection enabled — current data can't validate Core Web Vitals or confirm/deny layout-shift risk from the heavy third-party tag load.
