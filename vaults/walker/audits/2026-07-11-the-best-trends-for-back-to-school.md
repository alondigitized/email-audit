## What's weak

- **Preview text is a raw tracking URL** — this looked like spam or a broken send; almost didn't open it
- **"BEST trends" is teen-speak** — I want cushioning and arch support, not whatever is trending on TikTok
- **Every model in the hero is under 25** — I'm 62; not a single face in this email looks remotely like my demographic
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — this is the eighth Famous Footwear email I've opened in a week; the deals are blurring together
- **No urgency or deadline** — the 25% off floats without a clock, so there's no reason to click today over tomorrow

- 2. **Reframe the offer around comfort fit, not trends** — lead with Wide Fit, cushioned walkers, memory foam insoles; that's the hook for guys like me
- 3. **Add at least one male model over 50** — a single hero image of someone who looks like he has a bad hip would do more work than five teens in Jordans

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Famous Footwear "Best trends for back-to-school"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for an ESP-generated send (Salesforce Marketing Cloud / Cheetah pattern), but has a pervasive alt-text gap across all 33 product images, a CSS typo, a misplaced `<style>` block, and a plain-text body that is 85% raw URLs — a meaningful spam-filter risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **33 click-tracked links** all route through `click.email.famousfootwear.com` — QA probing was skipped for all, so broken-destination detection has no coverage. UTM passthrough cannot be confirmed (see §6).
- **Open pixel** present: `https://click.email.famousfootwear.com/open.aspx?V3MUXG66YOUUPEJUZGUUS4RKPY.70251&d=70251&bmt=0` — standard; no issue.
- No raw destination URLs are exposed in the HTML, so direct link-rot checks are not possible without following redirects.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **33 images missing alt text** | Every content image (`*.jpg`, `*.png`) has `alt=""`. QA confirms 33 instances. The logo (`3b6a7dc1-…ee.png`) also has `alt=""` — should be `alt="Famous Footwear"`. |
| **`user-scalable=0` in viewport** | `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` — blocks pinch-zoom on mobile; WCAG 1.4.4 failure. |
| **CSS typo: `lineapacing`** | `<td … style="…lineapacing:20px;…">` — invalid property, silently ignored. Intended value appears to be `line-height` or `letter-spacing`. |
| **`<style>` block in `<body>`** | `<style> div.preheader { display: none !important; } </style>` is injected inside `<body>` rather than `<head>`. Some older clients ignore body-level `<style>` blocks, which could expose the preheader as visible text. |
| **Missing `http-equiv` on charset meta** | `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` is missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Without it, some clients may fall back to ASCII/Latin-1 rendering. |

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No merge tokens or dynamic personalization variables are present in the visible HTML. This appears to be a fully static batch send. No un-resolved tokens or broken syntax detected.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **Unsubscribe link**: Not visible in the truncated source. Presence assumed in footer (not provided); cannot confirm CAN-SPAM compliance from supplied HTML.
- **Physical mailing address**: Not visible in truncated source; must be confirmed in footer per CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5).
- **Authentication headers**: Not available from HTML alone — DKIM/SPF/DMARC must be verified at the envelope level. Sending domain `email.famousfootwear.com` is a standard ESP subdomain; alignment should be confirmed.
- **Plain-text body**: QA reports 17,024 of 20,006 characters (85%) are URLs. This is a strong spam-filter signal and may cause deliverability failures with aggressive ISP filters.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All 33 destination links are wrapped in `click.email.famousfootwear.com` redirects. UTM parameters cannot be inspected from the HTML source; they would need to be verified by following redirects or inspecting the ESP link configuration.
- No untracked `famousfootwear.com` direct links were found — all clicks pass through the tracking layer, which is correct.
- Cannot confirm whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` are consistently applied across all 33 links.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**High priority**
1. **Add descriptive alt text to all 33 images** — at minimum, the logo (`alt="Famous Footwear"`) and all CTA/product images. Screen reader users and image-blocked clients currently get no content.
2. **Remove `user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta — or at minimum `maximum-scale=1`. This is a WCAG failure and causes friction on mobile.
3. **Audit plain-text version** — reduce the URL-to-text ratio below 50%. Add actual human-readable copy mirroring the HTML content. Current 85% URL ratio will flag spam filters.

**Medium priority**

4. **Fix `lineapacing` typo** — replace with the intended property (`line-height: 20px` or `letter-spacing: ...`) on the affected `<td>`.
5. **Move preheader `<style>` block to `<head>`** — prevents accidental preheader text rendering in clients that ignore body-level styles.
6. **Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to charset meta** — `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`.

**Low priority / verify**

7. **Confirm footer compliance** — verify physical address and unsubscribe link are present in the unsupplied footer HTML.
8. **Spot-check UTM coverage** — follow 3–5 sampled click URLs through the redirect to confirm `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=email`, and a campaign-specific `utm_campaign` are being appended to all landing pages.
