# BOGO 50% off ends tonight—gooooo!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- "Head for the Heels" in big orange letters, a woman's leg in the air showing off a gold stiletto, another woman's manicured hand holding up a strappy heel — that's the headline visual, and none of it is speaking to a 62-year-old guy looking for something cushioned to walk the dog in. Same BOGO 50% off banner I've seen from DSW all week, same "School Supply Stock-Up" tag that makes no sense in mid-August for me, and now they've bolted on "Homecoming" styling too, which tells me this thing is just getting recycled and relabeled.
- To be fair, way down at the bottom there's a "Shop by Size" grid and a couple of icons for sneakers mixed into the "keeping it casual" row — that's the first flicker of anything I'd actually click all week. But I'd have to scroll past a whole homecoming shoe party to get there.
- I'm marking this one read and moving on, same as the last six. Not forwarding it, not clicking. If DSW wants my email open rate to mean something, they need to remember I'm not shopping for prom.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off number is stated plainly at the top, no games, same honest offer as their other sends this week.
- "Shop by Size" section near the bottom at least gives a functional path if I were hunting for my size.
- Sneaker icon shows up in the "when you're keeping it casual" row — small, but it's the only shoe in this whole email built for a man like me.
- Free shipping and in-store pickup icons near the bottom are useful, no-nonsense info.

## What's weak

- Hero visual is two women's legs and stiletto heels — nothing here says "this is for you, Walker."
- "Head for the Heels" and "Hold Court at Homecoming" are themes that have zero overlap with comfort-first casual shoes for a retired guy.
- I've now gotten this same BOGO pitch with barely-changed dressing five or six times this week — the cadence is wearing thin.
- No men's casual, cushioned, or slip-on product shown anywhere above the fold — I'd have to dig for a category that's actually mine.
- "Give Shoes, Get $10" donation pitch is a nice idea but buried mid-email with no clear tie to my next purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's category block — sneakers, slip-ons, walking shoes — right under the BOGO banner instead of burying it two-thirds down. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Drop the "Homecoming" and "Head for the Heels" theming for segments like mine; swap in comfort/casual imagery for recipients who've only ever browsed men's or comfort-brand categories.
- 3. Space these BOGO sends out — six near-identical emails in a week trains me to stop opening them at all.
- 4. Put "Shop by Size" and the men's casual row higher up, since that's the only actionable content for someone in my bucket.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's slip-ons and walkers: BOGO 50% off tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: BOGO 50% off comfort styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, arch-support styles from $39.99 — offer ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort pick — BOGO 50% off before midnight`
