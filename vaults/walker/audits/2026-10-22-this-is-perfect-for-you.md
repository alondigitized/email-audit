# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- "This is perfect for you!" is a big claim, and I'm not buying it. The email opens with "Still thinking about it?" and "You've got great taste!" Then the hero is a kid's Skechers Garage sneaker with a toy car in the sole. I'm 62, and I haven't looked at any kid's shoe. It's the same cart-nag template I've been getting all week ("Did you forget something?" on 10/22 and 10/20, "Your Items Are Waiting!" on 10/21), just with a different shoe.
- The bottom half is better. There are two men's slip-ons in the "more great options" grid. The Contour Fit and the Relaxed Fit Altman are the kind of hands-free shoe I actually shop for. The 20% off for Members banner is clear, and it's the first offer I could read at a glance. It sits too far down the page, though.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the Contour Fit slip-on, but not on the hero. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and move on. The "perfect for you" subject also doesn't match the toy-car shoe, and I trust a brand less when the email doesn't match what I've been looking at.

## What's working

- The 20% off Members banner with the $5 reward on $75+ is easy to read. White type on a dark bar stands out.
- Two men's slip-ons are in the grid (Contour Fit, Relaxed Fit Altman). Slip-on and Cozy Fit are exactly my words.
- The "Shop the Sale" link and the valid-through date (10/26) give a believable deadline.
- Category buttons (Men, Women, Kids) are large, full-width and easy to tap on a phone.
- The big navy headline is readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- The hero is a boy's Garage sneaker. I'm a 62-year-old man, and that's a miss.
- "Still thinking about it?" assumes I looked at something. This is the fourth near-identical nag this week.
- The subject says "perfect for you," but the hero and half the grid are kids' and women's shoes (the Garage sneaker, the Glam Racer girl's shoe, the women's Arch Fit).
- The 20% off is buried below six tiny product photos. The product captions are small, and none show a price.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. The member-only, online-only and excludes-sale-items terms feel like they're hiding something.
- The email has no cushioning or arch-support talk and no reviews. The women's Arch Fit shoe is the only fit hint, and it's a women's style.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer and a men's comfort slip-on: "Members take 20% off" above the fold, with a hero shoe that matches my category. Don't send a kids' shoe to a 62-year-old man.
- 2. Stop sending cart-abandonment copy to people who never started a cart. If there's no cart, send a "new in comfort" email instead.
- 3. Put the price and one fit detail (cushioning, arch support, machine washable) under each product, and add a star rating.
- 4. Use bigger caption text and cut the fine print to one readable line.
- 5. Reduce the grid to the three shoes that fit the reader, instead of mixing men's, women's and kids' styles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off Members slip-ons, thru 10/26`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, comfort slip-ons are 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy Fit and Relaxed Fit slip-ons, plus a $5 reward on $75+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members save 20% online through Monday. No laces needed.`
