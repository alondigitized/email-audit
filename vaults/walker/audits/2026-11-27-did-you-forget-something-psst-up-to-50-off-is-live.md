# Did you forget something? (Psst... Up to 50% off is live)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same cart nag I've been getting since 11/20, and I've lost count of how many that makes. "Did you forget something?" is the same line they used on 11/25, and I'm tired of it. I don't have anything in a cart. The big "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner and the two "Complete Your Order" buttons are talking to somebody else, and it feels a bit like being watched.
- The black "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner at the top is what saved it. It's like the 11/26 "Fan favorites, now for less" send that I liked. I knew the deal in two seconds, and the text is big enough to read on my phone. The GO WALK Glide-Step slip-in is also right up my alley.
- Would I click? Maybe, because it's Black Friday and I do want a slip-in. I'd go straight to the shoe and ignore the checkout talk. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and probably go check Hoka and Brooks too.

## What's working

- The black "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner is the first thing I see, and the percentage is big and plain.
- The hero shoe is a Slip-ins GO WALK Glide-Step. That's hands-free and cushioned, which is what I shop for.
- Two of the three men's styles are slip-ons, the Murette slipper and the Relaxed Fit boots. I can see something I might wear.
- The red "Skechers Plus members stack an extra 15% off everything" box tells me my membership counts for something.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is big, stacked, and easy to tap with my thumb.

## What's weak

- The "Ready to check out?" hero and "Complete Your Order" buttons imply a cart that I don't have. That feels pushy.
- There's a big blank stretch between the banner and the hero shoe. I had to scroll through white space.
- The hero shoe has no price, no "was/now," and no mention of arch support or cushioning. I'm left to guess how much the 30-50% saves me.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart | see details below" sends me to a wall of tiny fine print. The fine print says the sale ends 12/01, but the deadline isn't in the email body up top. I only learned that by squinting.
- The grid mixes women's sneakers in with the men's boots and slippers, so I have to sort through it. And three different "shop" buttons compete with each other.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake cart language when there's no cart. If you do know what I looked at, show that exact shoe with the sale price. Otherwise just say it's a sale.
- 2. Put the deadline ("Ends Monday 12/1") right next to the 30-50% banner in large type.
- 3. Add a price and one comfort line under the hero shoe, such as "Slip-in, Arch Fit, now $XX."
- 4. Trim the blank space and cut it to one main button.
- 5. Show only men's comfort and slip-on styles to people who've been browsing them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Slip-ins up to 50% off through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your GO WALK slip-ins are on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free comfort, 30-50% off sitewide. Plus extra 15% for members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends 12/1. Men's slip-ins and arch-support walkers inside.`
