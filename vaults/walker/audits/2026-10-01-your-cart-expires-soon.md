## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed failures: the unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400 in the probe, and there is no plain-text alternative (0 chars). Smaller problems are insecure `http://` image sources on the `image.emails.skechers.com` host, missing alt text on two images, and an empty `<title>` and incomplete charset meta tag in the head.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link failed (400).** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkz…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m49…`. The probe got a 400. The token is per-recipient, so the probe may have caused the 400 (for example a bot-flagged request, or a token that only works from the original context). Verify it manually with a real send before treating it as a confirmed break.
- **Two "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these…" links returned 429.** These are `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` links. QA marked both as possibly valid, so they are not defects. They do mean the primary cart CTA was not verified.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 25 skipped, so the remaining links are unverified.
- **Every link goes through an Attentive redirect** (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`), including the "web version" link. This is expected for click tracking, but the final destinations can't be checked from this data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No plain-text part.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This is a multipart/alternative gap that affects deliverability and text-only clients.
- **`http://` image sources.** About 12 images load from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`. These include the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the hero "Complete Your Order" (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), the app-store badges, Afterpay, Find a Store and the social icons. Clients that block mixed content, or proxy images and fail the fetch, may not show them. The CTA image is among them.
- **Missing alt text on two images.** One is the product image `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`. The other is the Attentive open pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`), where an empty `alt=""` would be the right fix.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is minor, but screen readers and the web-view rendering use it.
- **Malformed charset declaration.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it has no effect. Encoding then depends on the MIME header, and this email has a non-ASCII preheader padding string.
- **No issues found** with `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, the mobile breakpoint at 620px, the Outlook `mso-` table properties, or the hidden preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncation means the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The unsubscribe link failed its probe** (see section 2). A working unsubscribe mechanism is a CAN-SPAM requirement, so this is the highest-priority item to verify.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and the physical postal address in the footer. No finding is made on these.
- The sender is `hello@msgs.skechers.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain. That is consistent with proper authentication, but it is not confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Landing page alignment can't be assessed. All CTAs are Attentive-wrapped redirects whose destinations and any UTM parameters are hidden in the opaque tokens. The probes got 429s instead of resolving the redirects, so there is no evidence on whether the cart CTA deep-links to the abandoned cart or to a generic page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the unsubscribe link manually.** Fix it if it fails for real recipients, and confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present.
2. **Add a text/plain alternative** with the cart CTA and unsubscribe URL.

4. **Add alt text** to `a6ec5198-…jpg` and `alt=""` to the open pixel.
5. **Fix the head.** Set `<meta charset="utf-8">` (or add `http-equiv="Content-Type"`) and give `<title>` a value.
6. **Re-run link QA with throttling or retries** to resolve the 429 CTAs. Confirm that the "Complete Your Order" redirect lands on the cart and carries UTM or attribution parameters.
