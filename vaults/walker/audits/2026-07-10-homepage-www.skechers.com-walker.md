# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is flashing a sale banner at me the second I land, which I appreciate — I'm not the type to dig for a deal, so putting it front and center is smart. But then they hit me with a hero image of two kids who look like they're shooting a streetwear lookbook in front of their headquarters, and I'm suddenly not sure this store is for me. Brand feels big and confident, and right now they're pushing a BOGO.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — that blue strip at the very top is the first thing I see on my phone, and "Applied at Cart" makes it feel legit rather than tricky
- **Quick-nav strip** — Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs: exactly the four things I'd actually browse, all in one tap
- **"Arch Fit" is named in the nav** — I know that line, I've bought it before; just seeing the name gives me a reason to tap
- **25% off Apparel secondary banner** — stacked offers read as a real sale event, not a one-trick promo

## What's weak

- **Hero image is wrong demographic entirely** — a young man in a beanie and a young woman in a crop top posing like they're in a sneaker commercial; I feel like I wandered into the wrong store
- **No hero copy** — all I see is the Skechers logo over the photo; no headline, no comfort message, nothing that tells me why I should care about this image
- **No loyalty hook** — I've bought Skechers before; there's no "sign in to see your rewards" or member-price badge anywhere on this screen
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — for a phone browser, I always want to know if I'm close to free shipping before I start adding to cart
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no review counts, no return-policy mention above the fold

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a lifestyle shot that matches the buyer** — show a man 55–65 walking a trail, a dog park, a boardwalk — somewhere Arch Fit or Slip-ins make sense; keep the BOGO strip exactly where it is
- **Add a headline to the hero** — something like "All-Day Comfort, Half the Price" gives the BOGO context and speaks to what I'm actually buying for
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the top bar** — rotate the BOGO strip to also show "Free Shipping on $50+" so I know what I'm working with before I tap anything
- **Add a sign-in / rewards nudge below the nav strip** — one line, "Sign in to see your Skechers Elite points" would pull returning shoppers like me deeper without any extra screen real estate
