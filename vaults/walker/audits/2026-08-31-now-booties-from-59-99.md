# NOW! BOOTIES FROM $59.99!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Well, that took me all of two seconds. Every single boot on this page is a women's bootie — snakeskin, cowboy-style, pointy stiletto heel, the whole lineup. I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for something slip-on with a cushioned sole for walking the dog, and DSW just spent an entire email showing me heels. Doesn't matter how loud the "$59.99" price tag is in that orange starburst — it's not for me.
- The "dress shoes / casual shoes / sneakers" strip near the bottom at least nods toward men's categories, but it's an afterthought after five screens of women's booties. I'm scrolling straight past this one — not clicking, not forwarding, just marking it read and moving on.

## What's working

- The $59.99 price is called out clearly, twice, in a bright orange circle — easy to spot without squinting.
- "Shop All Boots" and "Shop All Women" buttons at least tell me where I stand before I waste a tap.
- The size grid near the bottom is a nice touch if this were actually my category — clean, tappable boxes.
- DSW VIP rewards blurb at the bottom (5% back, free shipping) is a fair loyalty reminder, though buried too deep.

## What's weak

- Every product shot is a women's bootie — stiletto heels, snakeskin, cowboy cut. Nothing cushioned, nothing slip-on, nothing that speaks to a guy like me.
- No arch support, comfort, or walking-shoe language anywhere near the top — just "so much new, so much wow," which tells me nothing about fit or comfort.
- The men's category strip (dress shoes, casual shoes, sneakers) is crammed in as one small row near the bottom, after I'd already have stopped scrolling.
- This is basically the same BOGO-fatigue problem in a new outfit — DSW's been hammering me with women's-skewed or repetitive promos for over a week now, and this one's no different, just a new gimmick.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the send — if DSW knows I browse men's comfort categories, this whole email should lead with men's casual or walking styles, not booties.
- 2. Move the men's category strip (dress/casual/sneakers) up top, or send me a completely separate men's-focused email instead of a footnote.
- 3. Add actual comfort language — cushioned insole, arch support, easy on-off — somewhere visible above the fold if they want a shot at guys like me.
- 4. Drop the "so much new, so much wow" fluff for something concrete about fit or comfort.
