# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. I've seen the D'Lites "Spring Bloom" since 9/17, and it was in this morning's "Your cart expires soon!" too. It's a women's shoe with embroidered flowers. I'm a 62-year-old man and I never put it in a cart, so a "Ready to check out?" headline feels like the store has me confused with someone else.
- Two things are better this time. The grid underneath finally has a men's slip-on, the Relaxed Fit Altman - Murdock, which is the kind of shoe I'd actually wear. The 15% guest / 20% member banner is clear too. But the Altman is a tiny thumbnail in the sixth slot, below a giant women's shoe and next to boys' and girls' sneakers. There's also no "Hi, Walker" and no member badge, even though they know I'm a member.
- I wouldn't click the hero. I might scroll down for the Altman, but I'd probably mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. My son's kids might like the Garage sneakers, but that's a stretch.

## What's working

- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman is in the grid. That's my kind of shoe: hands-free and easy to wear.
- The 15% guest / 20% member banner is big and easy to read. It makes the member perk clear.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals, Sale) has large tap targets. It's easy to use on a phone.
- The Skechers logo at the top and the clean layout look like the brand I know.
- The Afterpay and Klarna options are visible if I want to spread out a purchase.

## What's weak

- The hero is that pink floral women's sneaker again, after about eight sends of it. It's the main reason this email doesn't feel written for me.
- "Ready to check out?" and "Complete your order" assume I have a cart. I don't have this shoe in a cart, so the email feels like a mistake.
- The "Did you forget something?" subject led me to expect a reminder about my own items. Instead I got a mix of women's, boys' and girls' shoes.
- Two orange and black "Complete your order" and "Shop all best sellers" buttons don't tell me what I'm getting. I can't tell whether they lead to a cart or a catalog.
- There's no "Hi, Walker" and no member badge, even though the offer is a member discount. The fine print says the promo ended 9/28, and the banner says "Thru 10/1". Those two dates don't match, so I don't trust it.
- The product captions are tiny, and I'd have to zoom in to read them.
- The email shows nothing about cushioning, arch support or comfort. For me that's the whole reason to buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop leading with a shoe I never carted. If it's a cart reminder, show what I actually left behind. If it isn't, don't use cart language. Lead with the men's slip-ons I've been browsing.
- 2. Put the Altman Murdock in the hero spot, with a short line like "Slip-ins: step in, no bending."
- 3. Put "Hi, Walker" and a Member badge at the top, and show the 20% member price on the shoe itself.
- 4. Fix the date mismatch. The banner and the fine print should agree.
- 5. Make the product captions bigger and add a line about arch support or cushioning.
- 6. Cap the cart reminders. I'm seeing about one a day, and that's enough to make me tune them out.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders structurally (lang set, preheader present, responsive CSS), but the QA run found two failures: the unsubscribe link returned a 400, and there is no plain-text alternative (0 chars). It also has widespread insecure `http://` image sources and missing alt text on key images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Unsubscribe link returned 400.**
  - URL: `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m49…`
  - The link text is "Unsubscribe". The `attn_macro` value base64-decodes to `unsubscribeLink`.
  - The 400 may be an artifact of probing a tokenized, single-recipient URL without the expected session or POST semantics. It still needs manual verification, because a dead unsubscribe link is a compliance problem (see section 5).
- **Two links returned 429 and are not confirmed broken:**
  - "Ready to check out? Complete your order" (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/rjcvyGNG…`). This is the primary CTA, so it should be re-probed.
  - The product image link (`…/e/c/AsjQHSNZ…`).
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 37 links were probed and 25 were skipped, so link health is only partially verified.
- All links are wrapped in Attentive redirects (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`). The final destinations can't be seen in the source, so tracking parameters can't be audited (see section 6).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear. The source is truncated, so cart-item rendering can't be verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link failed the probe (400)**, as described in section 2. CAN-SPAM requires a functioning opt-out mechanism, so this needs manual confirmation.
- **No plain-text part.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and it is a common spam-filter signal.
- **Not evaluable:** the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address in the footer. None were provided or visible in the truncated source. The sender domain `msgs.skechers.com` differs from the image host `emails.skechers.com`, so alignment should be checked in the full headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is an opaque Attentive redirect, so the destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be seen. The one tracked link whose final destination could be checked, the CTA, was rate-limited (429).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually test the unsubscribe URL (`attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…`) in a browser and via the `List-Unsubscribe` one-click POST. Fix it if it is truly failing.
2. Add a multipart plain-text alternative.
3. Serve all 11 images over `https://`. The host already supports it, since `de642685….jpg` loads over HTTPS.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `de642685….jpg` and `a6ec5198….jpg`, and `alt=""` to the open pixel.
5. Fill in `<title>` and correct the charset meta tag.
6. Re-probe the 429 links with throttling, and probe the 25 skipped links, to confirm destinations and UTMs.
7. Capture the full headers (authentication results, `List-Unsubscribe`) so the compliance section can be completed.
