# 20% off ends TONIGHT
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW email, sixth or seventh time this week, just a new subject line — "ends TONIGHT" instead of "1 Day Left" or "Don't miss." I've seen this exact "20% OFF" banner, "COMING IN HOT," and "THEY'RE JUST SOOOO YOU" layout so many times now I could describe it with my eyes closed. And once again, scrolling through loafers, Mary Janes, block-heel sandals, ballet flats, and a fringe-collar bootie — not a single men's shoe anywhere in this thing until "SHOP MEN" as a tiny link way up top.
- I'll give credit where it's due, the "SHOP SNEAKERS" section has some Samba-style sneakers that could pass for unisex, but they're still shown in what looks like a women's product line. Nothing here says cushioned, arch support, slip-on comfort — nothing that speaks to a 62-year-old guy looking for a shoe he can actually walk in all day.
- This is a mark-as-read, no question. I'm not clicking, and I sure wouldn't forward it to a buddy unless it was to joke about how DSW clearly doesn't know who's on this list.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" banner at the very top is bold and easy to spot at a glance.
- "VIP Exclusive" tag is a nice touch if I actually cared about the program.
- The promo code (YAYVIP) is stated plainly instead of buried somewhere.
- "SHOP MEN" exists as a link option even though it's not represented anywhere in the product photos.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — loafers, Mary Janes, heeled sandals, booties, flats — is women's footwear, same as every other DSW email I've gotten this week.
- No comfort, cushioning, or arch-support language anywhere — my whole reason for shopping shoes isn't touched on.
- "COMING IN HOT," "THEY'RE JUST SOOOO YOU" — this breezy tone doesn't read like it's talking to me at all.
- It's the same template, same banner, same section headers I've now seen five or six times this week — nothing new to make this one worth opening.
- "Ends TONIGHT" urgency feels manufactured since they've said basically this same thing three times already this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the send — if DSW has my purchase or browse history, show me men's comfort shoes, not another all-women's grid. That's the single biggest lift possible here.
- 2. Lead the hero section with an actual men's comfort shoe if this is going to a mixed list, not bury "SHOP MEN" as a small link under the banner.
- 3. Cut the repeat cadence — I've gotten near-identical "20% off" emails daily this week. Space it out or vary the offer.
- 4. Add real product substance for guys like me: cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease — even one callout line would help.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles — 20% off tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Slip-on & cushioned: 20% off ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Arch support, all-day comfort — men's picks inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort — shop before midnight`
