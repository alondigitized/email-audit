# Save an extra 30% on full price and sale styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- My first thought was that this is another adidas email and I've stopped counting them. This one is a plain dark banner reading "The Holiday Sale", with no shoe in it, just a blurry close-up of fabric stripes. The offer is clear enough: 30% off with code COZY. But it sits below that big picture, and on my phone I'd have to scroll to find it.
- Then I read the fine print. Gazelle, Samba and Campus are all excluded, and those are the shoes adidas pushed at me on 11/9 ("Gift iconic style with Samba") and 10/31 ("Save on Gazelle, Soft Lux"). Some Ultraboost and Stan Smith are excluded too. They advertise the shoes and then leave them out of the sale, which doesn't sit right with me. The deadline, November 11, is only in that tiny print.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd tap Shop Men only if I were already thinking about adidas. I wouldn't forward it. It's a mark-as-read for me, though it's tidier than some of the earlier ones.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF HOLIDAY SAVINGS" headline is big, plain and easy to read without zooming.
- It gives me a real number and a real code (COZY), which beats the vague apparel emails from 10/31.
- The Shop Men, Shop Women and Shop Kids buttons are large black blocks. They're easy to tap with my thumb.
- Layout is simple, one column and not cluttered. It's the kind of navigation I like.

## What's weak

- There's no shoe anywhere. I shop for cushioning and arch support, and this shows me nothing I could walk in.
- The picture is a dark, blurry fabric close-up with "The Holiday Sale" on it. It tells me nothing about what's for sale.
- The exclusions are buried in tiny print. They cut out Gazelle, Samba and Campus, the very shoes adidas has been showing me. That feels like bait-and-switch.
- The deadline (Nov 11) isn't in the main copy. Nothing in the body tells me to hurry.
- The black "best sellers" banner at the top is shifted over from the picture below it. The edges don't line up, so it looks a bit sloppy.
- Nothing speaks to a 62-year-old walker, with no comfort, no fit and no men's casual.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real shoe in the hero, ideally a men's walking or lifestyle pair, with the price after the discount. Show me "$X with code COZY" and I can do the math.
- 2. Move the 30% and the end date into the hero, and say plainly what is excluded. A short line like "Excludes Samba, Gazelle, Campus" up top builds trust.
- 3. Replace the Men/Women/Kids buttons with something like "Shop men's shoes" and "Shop comfort walkers", so the click lands on something I care about.
- 4. Match the email to the site. I trust a brand more when the email looks like what I saw scrolling their site.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based HTML 4.01 template, but it has no plain-text part (0 chars) and loads several images over plain `http://`. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, UTM and token checks can't be fully confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

It lacks alt text and is likely the open-tracking pixel. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop it, which undercounts opens and may trigger security warnings.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images:** the "arrow" image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is flagged 6 times, so it is reused 6 times. Clients may block or rewrite it.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/f6816636-0d03-4680-a456-48752d73dcf8?cp_tp=...`
  - The tracking image `VUF6lvj_...=452`. A tracking pixel should use `alt=""` explicitly.
  - Any content image without alt shows nothing when images are blocked and gives screen readers no usable text.
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it is not a valid charset declaration. Charset then relies on the MIME header alone.
- **Incomplete viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0" />` has no `width=device-width`. Responsive behavior then depends on the `@media (max-width:639px)` rules and client defaults.
- `lang="en"` is present, and table-based layout with `mso-font-alt` fallbacks is in place. No other issues were confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Authentication-Results not found** (QA WARN). The report attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a limit of the test setup rather than proof that adidas lacks SPF/DKIM. The sender domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is a branded subdomain, which suggests aligned authentication. Run a header check on a direct-delivery copy to confirm.
- **Not verifiable:** the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical postal address are not in the supplied excerpt or QA data. I'm not flagging them as missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set. That is acceptable for the email, but confirm that attribution on adidas.com relies on the redirect-appended parameters rather than the referrer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **High:** add a `text/plain` alternative part with the offer, key links and unsubscribe link.
2. **Medium:** switch `image.link.adidas.com` (the "arrow" image) and `click.comms.adidas.com` (the tracking pixel) to `https://`.
