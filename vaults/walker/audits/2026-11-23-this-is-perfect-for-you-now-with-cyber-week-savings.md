# This is perfect for you! Now with Cyber Week savings
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Another cart nag, and it's the eighth since 11/20. The "perfect for you" subject made me think this was something new. I opened it and found the same women's black Contour Foam Slip-in with the rose-gold eyelets I've now seen in almost every one of these. "Still thinking about it?" doesn't help. I never put that shoe in a cart.
- The Cyber Week banner at the top is big and easy to read, and it's the best part. But "30-50% off" is a range, and I can't tell what I'd actually save. The fine print says the 30% is for regular-priced shoes and the 40% is for select boots and slippers. The 50% is for select apparel and accessories, so shoes don't get it. The shoe grid below finally has some men's slip-ins and boots, which is closer to what I wear. But they're small and the captions are tiny.
- I wouldn't click the hero. I might scroll to the grid if I were already shopping, but I'd mark this read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The black banner with red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" and a SHOP NOW button is easy to spot and read.
- The Skechers Plus "EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING" block is clear, and it's the one place member pricing shows.
- The grid has a few men's slip-ins and waterproof boots, like the John Deere Relaxed Fit, which matches what I'm after.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is stacked in big text with wide tap areas. That's easy on my thumbs.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" tells me I don't have to hunt for a code.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is the women's black sneaker again. This is the same creative as the 11/22 "Did you forget something?" and "Your cart is one click away!" sends. It feels like a stranger's cart, not mine.
- "30-50%" is a vague range. The fine print says shoes get 30% and 40% only on select boots and slippers, so the headline overpromises.
- The product captions are tiny. On my phone I'd have to zoom to read the names.
- Three of the six picks are women's styles. That tells me they don't know who I am.
- The fine print is a wall of small type, and the Plus code (BF2026) is buried in it instead of near the offer.
- "Take Another Look" is a weak CTA. It doesn't say what I'm looking at.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart reminders for a product the person never added. If it's a browse-based send, lead with the men's slip-in the subscriber actually looked at.
- 2. Replace the "30-50%" range with a specific number and tie it to shoes, such as "30% off all shoes, 40% off boots."
- 3. Make the product grid larger, with 2 columns instead of 3 and bigger captions with prices. I want to see the price math.
- 4. Put the BF2026 code next to the Plus block.
- 5. Rename the main button to "See the Contour Foam Slip-in."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Slip-ins are still 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free comfort, plus an extra 15% off for Plus members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boots 40% off, shoes 30% off. Ends 12/1.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from a standard Attentive/Stripo-style table layout with a mobile media query. Its main technical defects are a failing unsubscribe link probe, a missing plain-text part, and 12 images served over `http://`. The HTML source was truncated, so some checks below are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe failed (QA FAIL):** `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=...&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_lid=m57...` returned HTTP 400 for the "Unsubscribe" link. The token is a per-recipient Attentive URL, so the 400 may come from the probe. A non-browser client or a replayed or expired token could trigger it. Treat it as unconfirmed until it is retested from a clean session. It is still the highest-priority item because it affects compliance.
- **All links are wrapped redirects:** the "web version" link goes to `https://skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`, an opaque click-tracking redirect. Final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Mixed link domains:** the sender domain is `msgs.skechers.com`, while links use `skechers.e.attentivemail.com` and `skechers.attn.tv`, and images use `image.emails.skechers.com`. This is typical for Attentive, but the tracking domain isn't aligned with the sender domain.
- **Probe coverage was partial:** 12 links were probed and 31 skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). The other 31 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images use `http://`** (`image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`). The affected images are the logo, "Take Another Look", "Here's some more great options…", an unnamed `a6ec5198-…jpg`, "Text", the App Store and Google Play badges, "Curbside Pickup", "Afterpay", "Find a Store", and the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons. Clients such as Gmail proxy these, but others may block or warn on mixed content.
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - `150413_BKRG_HERO_LG` (`images.skechers.com/image;width=auto,format=auto/150413_BKRG_HERO_LG`) is a product or hero image, which is a real accessibility gap.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` also has no alt text.
  - The `attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` tracking pixel has none. That one is low severity, but the pixel should carry `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`.
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title></title>`.

Some clients may fall back to a default encoding. The preheader's zero-width padding characters (`͏`, soft hyphens) are the characters most at risk.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, a mobile `@media (max-width:620px)` stacking rule, and the `mso-*` table resets are all present.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link:** it is present, but the probe returned HTTP 400 (see section 2). If this reproduces for real recipients, it violates the CAN-SPAM requirement for a functioning opt-out.
- **No plain-text alternative (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. A missing text/plain part raises spam-filter scores and hurts accessibility and deliverability.
- **Authentication unverified (QA WARN):** no `Authentication-Results` header was found. The email passed through the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a monitoring gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Not verifiable:** the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided. The physical postal address would be in the truncated footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the unsubscribe URL from a clean client. If the 400 persists, escalate to the ESP, since it is a compliance risk.
2. Generate and attach a text/plain part with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL.
3. Serve all 12 images over `https://`, or enable HTTPS on `image.emails.skechers.com`.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to `150413_BKRG_HERO_LG` and `a6ec5198-…jpg`. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel.
5. Fix the head: set `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` (or `<meta charset="utf-8">`) and populate `<title>`.
6. Capture the full raw message headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) so SPF, DKIM and DMARC and one-click unsubscribe can be verified.
