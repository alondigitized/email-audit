# Spezial is the must-have holiday gift
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email, and I've lost count since the 15th. The black banner with the countdown clock got my eye first, and "Up to 60% Off for Members" is a real offer. Then the picture is a pair of pink suede sneakers held up by a hand with a big silver ring. That's not a shoe for a 62-year-old man with a cushion-and-arch-support list.
- "Unmatched comfort" gets one line of copy and no proof. There's no price, no cushioning detail and no arch support. The buttons are big, which my thumb appreciates. But the 60% off is "up to", and nothing here tells me what the Spezial would cost me. I'd skim it and not click. I wouldn't forward it, because nothing in it is useful to a friend.

## What's working

- The black banner says "Up to 60% Off for Members" in large white type, so I could read it without squinting.
- The countdown clock (2 days, 3 hours) gives the sale a believable deadline.
- The three "Shop Men / Women / Kids" buttons are large and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The banner matches the "Black Friday First Access" email from the 14th, so it feels like the same brand.
- The copy is short and the layout is clean, with one picture and no clutter.

## What's weak

- The pink suede Spezial and the model's styling look aimed at a young fashion buyer. They don't look like something I'd wear.
- "Unmatched comfort" is a claim with nothing behind it. I see no cushioning, arch support or fit details.
- There's no price, so I can't tell whether "up to 60%" applies to this shoe at all.
- All three buttons go to a department, not to the Spezial. I'd have to hunt for the shoe the email just showed me.
- The first line at the top is small gray underlined text, which is hard to read on a phone.
- It's the fourth or fifth adidas send this week, and it still doesn't tell me why I should care.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price on the Spezial, with the member price next to it, such as "$100, now $60 for members".
- 2. Link the main button straight to the Spezial page, and name it "Shop Spezial Men's".
- 3. Say what the comfort is: the cushioning, the sole and the weight, in two plain lines.
- 4. Show a men's shoe in a neutral color, worn by someone closer to my age.
- 5. Make the preheader text larger and readable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members save up to 60% on Spezial`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday first access ends in 2 days`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handball Spezial, now with member pricing and all-day comfort.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access is on. Pick up a comfy gift before the crowds.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text alternative (QA FAIL, 0 chars) and loads several assets over `http://`. Its 22 tracked links and its authentication headers couldn't be checked, because the links are redirect-wrapped and the headers weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure tracking asset.** The image `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1e7ad6573-.../7eWWHiAtLavJ1oNnnqXzY2zh7S9-0eAHi8gEcwXjuPU=452` is served over `http://`. It is likely the open pixel, and it can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, which would undercount opens.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** the `arrow` image (`http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`) is loaded over `http://` six times. Clients that block non-HTTPS sources will drop it.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/20b7d69a-d4d4-44a3-b434-0422396ff641?...` has no alt attribute.
  - The `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...=452` image also has no alt attribute. If it is a tracking pixel, it should carry `alt=""`.
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** the source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Clients that ignore the MIME header's charset may mis-decode characters.
- **Legacy doctype:** the document uses the HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE but contains XHTML-style self-closing tags such as `<meta ... />`. This is low severity and renders fine in practice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source or QA output. The HTML was truncated, so the rest is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility. It may also be a capture artifact, so check the raw MIME for a `text/plain` part.
- **Authentication unverified (QA WARN):** no `Authentication-Results` header was found. The QA harness expected it via the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** the truncated source and QA data show neither the footer unsubscribe link nor `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. I can't confirm their presence or absence.
- The sender uses a dedicated subdomain, `us-news.comms.adidas.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 22 links are redirect-wrapped (`click.comms.adidas.com`) and were not resolved. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Spezial" holiday-gift subject can't be assessed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part. Confirm whether the 0-char result comes from the capture or from the send.
2. Move `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com` assets to HTTPS, including the six `arrow` image references and the pixel.

4. Change the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. Re-run QA with raw headers captured to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`.
6. Follow the tracking redirects (for example with a GET that follows redirects) to check final URLs, UTMs and landing-page status.
