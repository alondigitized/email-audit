- Okay, I can read this one without my glasses. "Up to 40% off sale styles" is huge white type on black, with a big blue-and-white running shoe under it. The message is clear: it's a sale. By now it feels like a stranger shouting at me, not a company that knows me.

- The white "Shop Sale" button is clear, and it sits right under the offer.
- The shoe photo fills the top of the screen, so I see it right away.
- The footer lists "Unlock Free Shipping" and "FLY for 30 Guarantee," which tell me I can try the shoes and return them.

## What's weak

- It's the same sale as the last four or five sends. Nothing is new or specific.
- The hero shoe looks like a running shoe. It doesn't say cushion, walking or arch support.
- The beanie offer wants $250, which is a lot for me. The models are young, and it has nothing to do with comfort.
- "Shop Sale" goes to a whole sale page, and I can't tell which shoes are marked down or by how much.
- The fine print is tiny, and the dark blue contact text on the bottom is nearly invisible. The footer links are plain serif text that's easy to miss.
- The offer ends today (11/26), but the email never says so up top. The sale is dated 11/23 to 11/26 only in the fine print.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two probe failures (HTTP 403), including the unsubscribe link. It has no plain-text part, six images with no alt text, and several template-level HTML defects. SPF/DKIM could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Unsubscribe link returned 403.** The link is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…/dbca109f?jwtH=…`. The JWT payload decodes to `linkUrl=https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=…&hmail=…&cordial_id=…`. The 403 came from the Cordial tracking host, before any redirect to `options.hoka.com`. A 403 can be bot filtering of the probe rather than a real failure. This audit can't tell which, so the unsubscribe path needs a manual click-through.
- **[FAIL] `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` link returned 403.** It is also on the Cordial tracker (`…/9aebf2a0?jwtH=…`), and the decoded destination is `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_…`. The same caveat applies: the probe may have been blocked rather than the link being broken.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 5 of 12 material links were probed, so 7 are unverified.
- **UTM and tracking parameters on the unsubscribe URL.** The decoded URL carries `utm_*`, `hmail=9153f33fe5cd…` (a 64-hex value, apparently a hashed email) and `cordial_id`. Campaign UTMs on an unsubscribe link are unnecessary, and a hashed email in a query string can leak through logs and referrers.
- **Two open-tracking pixels on different hosts.** One is `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and the other is `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. Both are served from the email's own tracking domains, and the second looks like a different sending or tracking system. This is worth confirming as intentional.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Six images have no alt text.** Four are content images and two are tracking pixels:
  - `H_US_Subcopy_Hero_BFEA_2.jpg` (1200x188)
  - `H_US_CTA_BFEA_1.jpg` (1200x160)
  - `H_US_Secondary_GWP_Beanie_1_V2.jpg` (1200x1616)
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
  - the two pixels, which need `alt=""`
- **Image-only content likely.** The image names (`Subcopy_Hero`, `CTA`) and sizes suggest text and the CTA are baked into images. If images are blocked, those elements would be blank with no alt fallback. The HTML shown is truncated, so this is inferred.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing.
- **Conflicting viewport tags.** There are two `<meta name="viewport">` elements. One sets `maximum-scale=1` and the other sets `user-scalable=yes`. The `maximum-scale=1` value restricts pinch-zoom.
- **Broken CSS from unfilled template variables.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have no values, so the declarations are invalid and dropped.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only decodable destination, the return-start link, has complete UTMs:
  - `utm_source=US_Broadcast`
  - `utm_medium=email`
  - `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261126_LastChance_GWP_A30Day_NovAnniPurchasers_LYNovSMSEngagers_1127FinalHoursOpeners_90DayNonEmailOpeners_Exclude2DayPurchasers`
- The `utm_campaign` value exposes audience-segmentation logic (purchaser, SMS-engager and non-opener segments) to recipients and in site analytics.
- The 403 means the landing page was not confirmed reachable. Destinations for the hero, CTA and GWP-beanie links were not among the decoded or probed links, so continuity for those can't be verified.
- `utm_content` and `utm_term` are absent on the decoded link, which limits per-link attribution.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click the unsubscribe and return-start tracking links from a normal client and via the probe's user agent. If they still 403, fix the Cordial link or allowlist the probe.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part with the offer, link URLs and unsubscribe URL.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to the hero, subcopy, CTA and beanie images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels. Move key copy and the CTA into live text or bulletproof buttons.
4. Fix the head and CSS:
   - Populate `<title>`.
   - Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`.
   - Remove the duplicate viewport tag and `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Fix or remove the valueless `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border` rules.
5. Remove UTMs and the `hmail` parameter from the unsubscribe URL. Consider shortening or generalizing `utm_campaign` so segment names aren't exposed.
6. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `emails.hoka.com` (check the raw headers, not the relay), and that a postal address is in the footer.
7. Rerun with the probe limit raised so all 12 material links are verified.
