# These Scary-Good Deals Disappear at MIDNIGHT👻
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival again, and this is the third or fourth week in a row of the same orange-and-black coupon blast. The top banner is easy to read: a jack-o'-lantern, "Take an extra 25% off your entire purchase," and the code TREAT25. That's a real offer and I'd notice it. Then the next big block is a little girl in a witch costume selling kids' boots and clogs, and that has nothing to do with me.
- The Nike 30% off block and the Family Athletics block (New Balance, Puma, Jordan) are the first things that look like my kind of shopping. There's nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-ons, and no men's walking shoes anywhere. It also feels like the same email I got on the 30th, with a Halloween costume on it. Would I click? Maybe the Nike or New Balance block, if I had a minute. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The 25% off, code TREAT25 and "Shop Now" at the top are big and readable on my phone without glasses.
- "Disappear at midnight" gives a real deadline. The fine print says 10/31 at 11:59 pm, so it holds up.
- The New Balance logo in the Family Athletics block is a brand I wear, and "30% off" is easy to read.
- The Add Coupon to Your Mobile Wallet block and the barcode mean I can use the deal in the store without typing a code.
- The Shoe Perks strip at the top and the $10 reward block near the bottom remind me I'm a member without crowding the offers.

## What's weak

- The second-biggest block is kids' boots and clogs starting at $29.98. I have no use for that.
- The Madden Girl block is women's boots at $54.98. This is the second women's-fashion email from this brand in about ten days. I saw the same thing on October 21st.
- Nike 30% off is a "SALE" banner with a photo of running shoes. It doesn't say men's, walking or comfort, so I have to guess.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and it lists a long set of exclusions: Brooks, Crocs, Birkenstock, Skechers-type names and more. I can't tell whether the shoes I want are even covered by the 25% off.
- The Word Stride, Afterpay and SMS blocks near the bottom are clutter. The "Mens" tab in the nav is small and tucked at the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a men's comfort or walking block high up, for example New Balance and Nike walkers with "Cushioned all-day comfort." Right now the men's side is nearly invisible.
- 2. Say plainly which brands are excluded right under the 25% code, not in the fine print at the bottom.
- 3. Cut the kids' block and the Madden Girl block for subscribers who have only ever clicked men's or athletic shoes.
- 4. Move the "Mens" nav up to the top, and make the "Shop Nike Sale" and "Shop Family Athletics Sale" links bigger tap targets.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based ESP template (Salesforce/emltrk tracking) with responsive CSS, but it ships an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` token in a tracking pixel and a malformed campaign-tracking pixel URL. SPF/DKIM/DMARC could not be verified, and the source was truncated before the body, footer and unsubscribe markup.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA run skipped 26 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains. It did not probe destinations, so redirect chains, 4xx/5xx responses and landing-page parity are unconfirmed.
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261031_kids_boots_nike_halloween_AM​`
  - The `t=` value contains a nested `utm_campaign=` key/value, which is a malformed query string.
  - The URL ends with a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. The campaign value may be recorded as `..._AM%E2%80%8B` and split from the clean `..._AM` value in analytics.
- **Unresolved placeholder:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` (see section 4).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes:** QA flagged three images.
  - `048318e9-6719-4331-9fa0-e8129f30fd7d.jpg` (`image.email.shoecarnival.com/lib/fe3311737164047d701077/m/1/`) is a content image with no `alt`. Screen readers will read the filename, and nothing displays when images are blocked.
  - The two `emltrk.com` tracking pixels have no `alt`. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly. This is low severity.
- **Link styling:** The global rule `a, a:link, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` removes underline and colour from every link. Any inline text links are then indistinguishable from body text, which fails WCAG 1.4.1 (use of colour). This applies only if text links exist in the body, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- **`tel` links:** `a[href^="tel"] { pointer-events:none }` disables tap-to-call on all clients, not only desktop.
- **Meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` also has no `http-equiv`. It works only through the `charset` attribute.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with an `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` attribute, which is inconsistent. This is low risk.
- **Breakpoints:** Media queries use three different thresholds (600px, 660px and 450px). `.responsive-text` sets 12px on mobile, which is below common 14px minimum guidance.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta, and Apple/Samsung autolink fixes.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` contains a literal `[UNIQUE]` in the received message.
  - If the ESP was supposed to substitute a per-recipient or per-send ID, it did not.
  - Open tracking for this pixel is then non-unique or broken.
  - Confirm whether this is a send-time rendering bug or a placeholder the tracker tolerates.
- **Other tokens:** No other merge tokens could be assessed in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, which the QA run attributes to the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `email.shoecarnival.com` are therefore unverified. This is a limitation of the test harness and not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:**
  - The physical postal address.
  - The visible unsubscribe link.
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers.

  Re-check these against the full HTML and raw headers.
- **From domain:** `shoecarnival@email.shoecarnival.com` is on a dedicated subdomain, and the image host `image.email.shoecarnival.com` uses the same subdomain. This is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM evidence:** The only UTM evidence is `utm_campaign=SCA_20261031_kids_boots_nike_halloween_AM`, and it appears inside a pixel URL, not on a click link.
- **Campaign naming:** The slug (kids boots, Nike) does not obviously match the subject, which is a sitewide "Scary-Good Deals Disappear at MIDNIGHT". This may be a segmented send, but it is worth confirming that click-through UTMs use a consistent `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`.
- **Landing pages:** Landing-page alignment could not be checked because all 26 click URLs were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[UNIQUE]` placeholder in `0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]`, or confirm the tracker resolves it. Check the ESP's send-time rendering.
2. Strip the trailing U+200B from the `utm_campaign` value and fix the `t=utm_campaign=...` nesting in the `ieezzy5m` pixel URL.
3. Add `alt` text to `048318e9-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Re-run the QA against the full HTML and raw headers. Specifically verify:
   - `Authentication-Results`
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
   - the footer address and unsubscribe link
5. Resolve a sample of the 26 tracking links, for example with a redirect-following probe. Confirm the final URLs return 200, carry consistent UTMs, and land on the promoted content.
6. Restore link underline or colour for in-body text links (replace the global `!important` inherit rule).
7. Add `http-equiv` to the two meta tags (`Content-Type` and `X-UA-Compatible`), or remove them.
