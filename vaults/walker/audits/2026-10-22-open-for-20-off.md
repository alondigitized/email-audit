# Open for 20% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this time they finally dropped the UGG slipper hammering. The giant "20% OFF*" on green is hard to miss, even on my phone, and the big type is easy on my eyes. I got the 20% number and the code JINGLE in about two seconds. That's more than I can say for the BOGO barrage last week or the four UGG slipper emails since the 19th.
- The asterisk bothers me, though. Then the product grid is all women's: Mary Janes, a loafer, a slingback, a leopard bootie. Nothing there for a 62-year-old man who wants cushion and arch support. The only thing for me is the "Get him what he *really* wants" banner with the slippers, and that's a gift pitch, not a shoe for my own feet. I might tap SHOP MEN, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone.

## What's working

- The giant "20% OFF*" is clear and readable without zooming. I know the deal right away.
- The code JINGLE is stated plainly, with "regular-price styles only" right next to it, so the main limit isn't hidden.
- The "Shop by size" block and the Wide Width / Wide Calf buttons are big black tap targets. I like that fit gets attention.
- The barcode for in-store use is handy if I'm at the DSW off the highway.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and the VIP rewards bar show loyalty benefits, though I don't let points decide for me.

## What's weak

- The "So New, So You" grid is all women's styles, so it doesn't feel like it was sent to me.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-ons. There are no Skechers, Hoka, Brooks or New Balance names to compare.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and I'd have to scroll a long way to read it. Skechers, Clarks and other brands are excluded, which are exactly the comfort brands I buy. That is buried at the bottom.
- The "Extra 20% off reg. price!" labels on only two shoes confuse me, because I can't tell whether the 20% applies to everything.
- The sale runs only Oct 22-25, and the email is long. The "Holly-jolly" holiday framing in October feels early.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a men's comfort product row at the top, such as slip-ons and walking shoes with cushioning, instead of a women's-only fashion grid.
- 2. Move the key exclusions (Skechers and others) up next to the code, not into the dark fine print.
- 3. Show real urgency, like "Ends Sunday, Oct 25", right under the offer.
- 4. Make the men's gift banner a shoe I'd buy for myself, and call out slip-on and wide-width options.
- 5. Drop the "Extra 20%" captions or explain them.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Regular-price styles only. Ends Oct 25. Use code JINGLE.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one probe-confirmed broken CTA (HTTP 400 on the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" tracked link). SPF/DKIM/DMARC can't be verified because no Authentication-Results header was captured. Only 12 of 68 links were probed, and the HTML source was truncated, so some checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns 400.
  - The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/aSBWUlwNDCe_p5gcCHJhjg~~/AAQRxRA~/HfJBMgoHH27A1XoSNFL-X0uufLXPRNqyeJOs0PhbBFiV62ydZ…` (token roughly 700 chars).
  - The QA output shows an empty status description after "(400):".
  - A 400 from a click-tracking wrapper can mean the token is malformed or truncated. It can also mean the redirector rejects the probe's request, so confirm with a real-client request before treating it as a live defect.
  - If it is real, the SMS opt-in offer is unreachable for every recipient.
- **Tracking pixels:** The email carries two open-tracking pixels.
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/pucS_x6FJyrsqJlBghiU8w~~/AAQRxRA~/OQ6AV-AGC8f0r--…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261022_P261522_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…`
  - The second URL carries a numeric subscriber ID (`mi_u`) as a query parameter.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 56 were skipped, so the other links are untested. The unsubscribe link is always included in the probed set, and no failure was reported for it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Both tracking pixels flagged above have no `alt` attribute (QA check). Impact is low since they are 1×1 pixels, but `alt=""` is the correct form.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom in some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so the charset declaration is inert.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it is also inert.
- **Plain-text part:** The QA check warns that 24,712 of 28,960 characters (about 85%) are URLs. That is mostly long tracking URLs, and it is a spam-filter and readability risk for the plain-text alternative.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` is declared and a `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block exists. No issues found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The only recipient identifier seen is `mi_u=225090073` in the pixel URL.

- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool notes this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so it is a visibility gap rather than a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** The unsubscribe link was probed with no failure reported. The footer was not in the truncated source, so the physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers can't be confirmed either way.

The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261022_P261522_V3_WM-_Offer_Stan…`, which looks truncated. ## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link from a standard browser client. If it still returns 400, regenerate it in the ESP, because the tracking token may be truncated or malformed.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. Fix the meta tags: use `http-equiv="Content-Type"` for the charset and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` for IE=Edge. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
4. Capture raw headers (Authentication-Results, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) in the pipeline to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe.
5. Follow redirects on the top CTAs in the QA run to record final URLs and UTM parameters. Consider raising the probe limit above 12.
6. Add more descriptive text to the plain-text part so that URLs are less than 70% of it.
