# Make Your First Pair Your Coziest
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- That broken preview text again. I've lost count now — this is at least the fifth Skechers email in June where the preview in my inbox is a wall of raw code instead of something that might actually make me want to open it. By now I've been trained by Skechers to just ignore it and tap anyway, because I know the shoes. But that's not how you want your customers thinking about you before they open.
- Once I'm inside, the email earns some goodwill back. The BOGO 50% off is right up top, unambiguous. And then I scroll and see "Hands Free Skechers Slip-Ins" — that's my language. Easy on, no bending down, cushioned. That section alone is why I'd keep reading. But then I hit Mary Janes. Not for me. Then a whole clothing block that feels grafted on from a different send. The email starts strong and loses the thread halfway down.
- I'd click the Slip-Ins section. I would not forward this — most of my friends are men too, and the further you scroll the more this looks like it wasn't quite aimed at us.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off leads and is impossible to miss** — biggest text above the fold, no fine print obscuring the offer before you read it.
- **"Hands Free Skechers Slip-Ins" section speaks directly to me** — that exact language (hands-free, slip-on) is why I shop Skechers over Brooks or New Balance right now.
- **Lifestyle hero uses an older couple** — the man and woman walking look like my generation, not a 28-year-old runner, which immediately made the email feel like it was at least partly for me.
- **Visual hierarchy holds in the top half** — offer → lifestyle image → benefit copy → product section flows cleanly; my eyes don't have to hunt.
- **"COZY all AROUND" is clear and honest benefit copy** — I know what kind of shoe this is before I read another word.

## What's weak

- **Broken JSON preview text — again, for the fifth or sixth time in June** — this is a chronic issue Skechers has not fixed, and at this point it's eroding the open instinct even for subscribers who already trust the brand.
- **Mary Janes section has no business being in a male subscriber's inbox** — it's not a small mention, it's a full hero section; signals that the list isn't segmented by gender.
- **No member pricing or loyalty hook visible** — after "Member Month" and the points-heavy June 1st email, I came in expecting some kind of Plus member angle. Nothing here.
- **All CTAs say "Shop Now"** — the Slip-Ins section deserved "Shop Slip-Ins Men's" or similar; generic buttons kill the momentum the section copy just built.
- **No deadline on the BOGO** — I can't tell if this expires tonight or next month, which makes it easy to put my phone down and forget about it entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text.** This is one engineering ticket. Six sends in June with raw JSON in the inbox preview is not acceptable, and it's the first thing suppressing opens from subscribers who would otherwise engage.
- 2. **Segment by gender or at minimum, lead with Men's.** Move the Men's Slip-Ins and Men's comfort sections to the top for male subscribers; push or remove Mary Janes entirely.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline to the BOGO.** "BOGO 50% Off — Ends Sunday" is the difference between clicking now versus intending to click and never coming back.
- 4. **Make the CTA copy match the section.** "Shop Slip-Ins" right below the Slip-Ins block, not a third "Shop Now."
- 5. **Show a loyalty line for Plus members.** Even one sentence — "Plus Members: stack your points on top of BOGO" — gives me a reason to act that the generic email doesn't.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Hands-Free Comfort: Buy One, Get One Half Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Easy-on Slip-Ins and cushioned Cozy Fit® styles — both BOGO 50% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skechers Slip-Ins, Cozy Fit® shoes, and more — grab your pair before BOGO 50% off ends.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**27 click-redirect links** all route through `click.emails.skechers.com` — expected for SFMC, not auditable without resolving the redirects. UTM continuity cannot be confirmed (see §6).

**Two malformed `<meta>` tags** (missing `http-equiv` attribute):
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />           <!-- missing http-equiv="Content-Type" -->
```

These are benign in modern clients but technically invalid XHTML per the declared DOCTYPE.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (10 confirmed):** Every asset served from `image.emails.skechers.com` uses `http://`. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy/rewrite image URLs and may silently upgrade or block these. Affected assets include the logo, footer icons (App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store), and all social icons (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube).

```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png  ← logo
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-...png ← (no alt)
... [8 additional assets]
```

**Missing alt text (2 confirmed):**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel) — decorative, alt="" acceptable, but currently absent
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` — non-decorative asset with no alt; content unknown from truncated source but a non-empty alt is required

**Global `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** on `*` selector prevents OS-level font scaling across all content — accessibility violation for low-vision users on iOS.

**`maximum-scale=1`** in viewport meta blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile — violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize text).

**Duplicate responsive breakpoint blocks:** `@media (max-width: 375px)` and `@media (max-width: 640px)` contain identical CSS rules across two separate `<style>` blocks. Redundant, adds ~1KB, no functional break.

**`.show` class defined twice with different breakpoints** (`640px` in first block, `480px` in second `<style>` block) — the 480px rule wins due to cascade order, likely unintentional.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL — BROKEN EXPERIENCE]** Empty first-name merge token: QA confirms the greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` — the subscriber name field was blank or the merge tag (`%%First_Name%%` or equivalent SFMC AMPscript variable) resolved to empty with no fallback default defined.

**Required fix:** Add a fallback value in the AMPscript or personalization string, e.g.:
```
%%[ IF EMPTY(First_Name) THEN SET @fn = "there" ELSE SET @fn = First_Name ENDIF ]%%
Hi %%=v(@fn)=%%,
```
This affects all subscribers without a stored first name — a segment-wide rendering defect, not edge-case.
