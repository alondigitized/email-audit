# Thankful for 30% off and a Freebie ❤️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. It's the same pink banner and giant "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th. This one is at least the ninth in the pile. The subject says "Thankful for 30% off and a Freebie," and the freebie turns out to be a Sherpa Weekender bag. It only comes with a $99 purchase and a code, and there's a pile of fine print under it. I wanted cushioned walking shoes, and I got a bag.
- The shoe grid is mostly sneakers, a women's-looking boot and a loafer, with discounts from 25% to 50%. The Nike "up to 30% off" line at the very top is the only hint that anything here is for me. The men's link sits in a row of tiny text links under the big black button.
- Would I click? Probably not. I might tap "Shop Men" out of habit, but this isn't worth forwarding. I'll mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The giant "30%" is impossible to miss, even on my phone. I can read it without zooming.
- The code BFDEALS is shown right in the hero, so I know what to type at checkout.
- The "Shop by Size" block with a Wide Width button is a good idea for my feet.
- The free shipping, in-store pickup and Afterpay icons are clear and easy to read.
- The Skechers brand list in the fine print at least tells me my usual brands exist here.

## What's weak

- It's the same pink "Black Friday Came Early!" creative I've seen all week. "Came early" is stale by now, since Black Friday is tomorrow.
- The "freebie" has a $99 minimum, an online-only code and "while supplies last." That isn't free the way the subject made it sound.
- Nothing speaks to comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-ons. The shoes are sneakers and fashion pieces, one is a women's boot, and there's no Hoka, Brooks or Skechers in sight.
- The "Shop Women / Men / Kids / Everything" links are small and cramped. I'd miss them with my thumb.
- The fine print takes up half the email. The 30% has exclusions, including Skechers, Clarks and Hoka-type comfort brands, and that feels like a bait-and-switch.
- The top banner is cut off ("THE SEASON'S BIGGEST DEALS ARE ON! - THE SEASON'S BIGGES").

## Recommendations

- 1. Put men's comfort and walking shoes in the hero. Show a cushioned slip-on, a price and a "Shop Men's Comfort" button.
- 2. Say up front which brands are excluded from the 30% so I don't have to dig for it.
- 3. Retire the week-old pink banner and rotate the creative.
- 4. Make the men's and women's links big, separate buttons.
- 5. Put a deadline on it, like "Ends Sunday."
- 6. Segment me. I've bought men's comfort shoes, and this email never acknowledges that.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run scored 50% (2 failures, 2 warnings). The two failures are a broken SMS opt-in CTA link (HTTP 400) and an unrendered `%%gender%%` merge token. Only 12 of 76 links were probed, and the HTML was truncated, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (400):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/oZdpIE2XpMD927Secv_ARA~~/AAQRxRA~/prWyROuR…` and returns HTTP 400. The tracked redirect either fails or rejects the probe. This is a promotional CTA, so confirm whether it fails for real users or only for automated clients.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261126_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` carries a recipient identifier (`mi_u`) in the query string. This is normal for open tracking, but the image is blocked by default in many clients.
- **Probe coverage:** 64 links were skipped (footer, utility and social). Their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 2 images:**
  - The linked image `x1CStWlbe_b8JFOf-oJTvGckrYgiKWFYNQ4aPk7cRvIClmEy25…` (src on `ablink.dsw.com/q/…`) has no alt. A linked image without alt gives screen readers no link name.
  - `o.gif` is a tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset declaration is not honored.
- **Plain-text part:** 27,209 of 33,425 characters (about 81%) are URLs. This is a deliverability warning. The tracked links are very long and make the text alternative largely unreadable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Raw token `%%gender%%`:** the token is unrendered in the delivered message. Its location is not visible in the truncated source. If it sits in a link query string or a conditional block, it is a personalization or segmentation failure. This one needs fixing.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** the header was not found. The QA notes it is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown and not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the unsubscribe link was probed and no failure was reported. The footer and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers are not in the truncated data, so they cannot be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues found that I can confirm. Links go through `ablink.dsw.com` redirect wrappers, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible in the supplied data. Only the 400 CTA above is a confirmed continuity break.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or replace the SMS opt-in tracked link and re-test it from a real client. A 400 on a CTA means lost conversions if it also fails for users.
2. Add a fallback or default for `%%gender%%` and fix the template so the token renders. Add a pre-send check for unrendered `%%…%%` tokens.
3. Add `alt` text to the linked image, and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags.
6. Shorten the tracked URLs or trim the plain-text part to improve the text-to-link ratio.
7. Re-run the QA with raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and a wider link-probe budget to cover the 64 skipped links.
