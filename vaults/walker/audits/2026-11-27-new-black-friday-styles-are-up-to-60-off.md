# New Black Friday styles are up to 60% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email. It's the same "up to 60% off" Black Friday message I got on the 22nd and the 26th, with a fresh "new styles added" label on it. The big white "UP TO 60% OFF" is easy to read without squinting, and I'll give them that. The countdown clock says about a day and a half left, which at least feels like a real deadline.
- None of it is about my feet. The hero is three young people in tracksuits on a stoop. The second photo is a pair of black Sambas on a young woman in ruffle socks, and I'm 62 and want a cushioned walking shoe. Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-on, and no shoe has a price. The "new sale styles" copy doesn't name one product.
- I wouldn't click. If I did, it would be "Shop Men" just to see whether there's a walking shoe in there. I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" in big white letters is readable at a glance on my phone.
- The countdown (1 day, 13 hours) gives me a believable deadline.
- Copy is short and plain: "Check off your gift list" tells me what the email wants.
- The black buttons are large with bold white text, easy to tap with my thumb.
- The adidas logo is at the top, so I know who it's from.

## What's weak

- Nothing here speaks to comfort, cushioning, arch support or all-day walking. It's lifestyle fashion.
- The models are all young, and the Sambas photo is a fashion shot, not a shoe I'd wear to the mall.
- "Up to 60%" is vague. I can't tell which items get 60% and which get 10%, and no actual shoe or price is shown.
- The three stacked buttons (Men, Women, Kids) send me to category pages. They don't show a product.
- The ticker banner under the countdown is cut off at the edges ("LACK FRIDAY"), which looks sloppy.
- The "Small gifts" section pushes socks, hats and bags. That's not why I'd open a shoe brand's email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real comfort or walking shoe in the hero, with a named price like "Now $59," and a button that goes straight to it.
- 2. Segment by who I am. Show men 55+ a walking or cushioned section with reviews and a few comfort details.
- 3. Replace "up to 60%" with a specific deal I can check, such as "Top-rated walking shoes, 40% off."
- 4. Make the three buttons into one clear "Shop Men's Sale" button for male subscribers.
- 5. Fix the clipped ticker text so it doesn't look broken.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New men's styles added. Comfort picks and prices inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `1 day left. See the top-rated walking shoes on sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's main confirmed defect is that it has no plain-text alternative (0 chars). Smaller rendering problems are 8 `http://` image sources, an ineffective charset meta tag and missing alt text. The HTML source was truncated and the 28 tracking links weren't probed, so the footer, unsubscribe link and destination URLs could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **28 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **The click-tracking open pixel is insecure.** It is served over `http://`: `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a21e35ec78-3628e5e6-73af-494c-aad5-0b1985f2afaa-000000/w3nRnZ2U5gtkcb4PaOEcDBVVAeGwqkAwlKdKEGE3orM=452`. Clients that block or upgrade non-HTTPS content may fail to load it, which would undercount opens.
- **No other link defects are confirmed.**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Eight `http://` images.** The "arrow" image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` repeats eight times. Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Missing alt text.** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/5f38c9f6-1fcb-47a1-8789-37bec7fe4e11?cp_tp=...` has no alt. The tracking pixel `...w3nRnZ2U5gtkcb4PaOEcDBVVAeGwqkAwlKdKEGE3orM=452` also has none. An empty `alt=""` is the right fix for the pixel.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute. Parsers may ignore it, so any reliance on it for encoding is unsafe.
- **No other issues found.** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta and the media-query breakpoints are present. The `@font-face` rules fall back to local Arial/Helvetica, so they cause no rendering failure.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The source is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text part.** The QA check failed with "Text version is 0 chars". This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and it is a spam-filter signal.
- **Authentication unverified.** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it may be an artifact of the capture rather than a sender fault.
- **Unsubscribe and address not confirmed.** The truncated HTML and the QA data don't show the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address or `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers. This is a gap in the data, not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 28 links are wrapped in `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects and weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment couldn't be checked. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a multipart/alternative `text/plain` part.** This is the only hard QA failure.
2. **Move all images to HTTPS.** That covers `image.link.adidas.com` and the `click.comms.adidas.com` open pixel.
3. **Fix the alt text.** Add alt text to the `dv.adidas.com` image and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. **Fix the charset meta.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. **Re-run the audit with full headers and untruncated HTML.** Resolve the redirect chain for a sample of links, then check `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe` and the footer.
