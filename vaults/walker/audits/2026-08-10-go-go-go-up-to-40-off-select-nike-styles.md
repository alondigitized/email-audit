# Go go go—up to 40% off select Nike styles!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW playbook I've been seeing all week — "Up to 40% off select styles from Nike" got me to open it, that's a real brand name and a real number. But two seconds in, that Nike headline is a bait-and-switch just like the one from yesterday. The whole rest of the email — "Heel Thyself," strappy sandals, floral pointed-toe heels — is women's dress shoes. Not a sneaker in sight until I hit the tiny "Athletic & Sneakers" thumbnail way down near the bottom.
- There's nothing here for a guy my age looking for a cushioned walking shoe. I'd scroll past this whole thing without clicking a single button, and I'm not forwarding it to anybody — my wife maybe, not me.

## What's working

- The "Up to 40% off select styles from Nike" headline up top is a name I recognize with a real discount number.
- Clean layout, big readable text, no glitches or overlapping images that I can see.
- Prices are shown clearly ($29.99) right on a couple of the product shots.
- The "Shop by Size" and "Wide Width / Wide Calf" section at the bottom is at least useful info, even if it's aimed at women's shoes.

## What's weak

- Nike gets one line at the top, then the entire email is women's heels and sandals — that's not what the subject promised me.
- Every model shown is a woman in strappy heels — nothing matches a 62-year-old guy's shopping list at all.
- "Heel Thyself" and "More Ways to Treat Yo' Self" — that cutesy voice isn't for me.
- The men's/athletic option is buried as one small thumbnail at the very bottom, practically an afterthought.
- Zero mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, or slip-on ease — the stuff that actually gets me to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. If the subject says Nike, open with Nike product — sneakers, not heels. Match the hero image to the headline claim.
- 2. Segment this — send guys like me a version led with men's athletic/walking shoes, not a women's dress-shoe blast with a Nike teaser slapped on top.
- 3. Swap the "Heel Thyself" hero for a comfort-forward hero if targeting a male, older-skewing list: cushioning, arch support, slip-on ease.
- 4. Move "Athletic & Sneakers" and "Wide Width" higher — those are the categories I'd actually stop scrolling for.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nike men's sneakers: up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Nike pick, up to 40% off — comfort styles included`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned Nike styles from $29.99, while sizes last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save on Nike walking shoes — men's sizes in stock`
