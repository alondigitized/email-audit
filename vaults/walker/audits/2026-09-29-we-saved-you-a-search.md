## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard SFMC (ExactTarget) build with click-wrapped links and several third-party tracking pixels. Confirmed issues are a staging image host in a production send, missing `alt` attributes, and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Staging asset host in production:** Two images are served from `https://image.staging.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/110/` (`cc57a950-573b-40d7-91f6-f59c435298a5.png`, `3fe183ac-1f44-41a9-86a4-4929a2610275.png`). A staging CDN may be unauthenticated-but-unmonitored, may be purged, or may be inconsistent with the production `image.link.adidas.com` host. If it is purged, the images break.
- **Unprobed links:** 21 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains). Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Three `beacon.krxd.net` 1x1 GIFs load on open (`1x1_ad_impression.gif`, and two `1x1_usermatch.gif` calls with `partner=LEzkELqx_sfmc_10973357_subid` and `..._sha256`). They have no `alt` attribute. They create additional third-party requests, and if Krux is deprecated or blocked they will 404 silently.
- **Open pixel:** `click.link.adidas.com/open.aspx?...` uses `alt=""`, which is correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (11 images flagged):**
  - Content images on `mi.adidas.com/p/rp/`: `5533ab4cb0cee2d5.png`, `3cf90e756855ed4a.png`, `dbbade4e9e7fece9.png`, `31be50d4a9f42cf7.png`, `7debb02b2b7cf231.png`, `5b6161154b7d3b56.png`, plus the two staging images above. These are likely product or dynamic-content images, so images-off clients get no fallback text.
  - Tracking pixels `mi.adidas.com/p/up/b7ca433f5d84cc1a/o.gif`, which appears 4 times. These should carry `alt=""`.
- **Preheader:** `<div class="preheader" style="font-size:1px;display:none !important"></div>` is empty. The preheader text ("New products, your style") sits in a separate hidden div. Some clients may pick up the empty first div or the nearby whitespace.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with `<meta charset>` in `http-equiv`-less form (`<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`, no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute). This form is not a valid charset declaration, so some parsers may ignore it.
- **`<title>`:** `adidas ` (bare brand with trailing space).
- Positives: `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, and media queries at 640, 639, 480, 414 and 320px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens were found in the visible source. The `mi_u=ILDCWJD70W1BE32X` and `partner_uid` values in the pixel URLs are populated, so they appear to have rendered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** The footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` headers are not in the truncated source or the QA data. SPF, DKIM and DMARC results for `us-news.adidas.com` are also not provided. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Privacy note:** The `1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=..._sha256&partner_uid=9153f33f...` URL appears to pass a hashed identifier to a third party (krxd.net) on open. This is not a CAN-SPAM violation, but it is a privacy and consent item for GDPR and CCPA review.
- **Plain-text part:** `[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs: 4769/5677 chars are URLs`. This is a spam-filter risk and gives a poor text-only experience.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. All visible links go through `click.link.adidas.com` wrappers, and final destination URLs and UTM parameters were not probed (21 links skipped). Continuity cannot be assessed from the available data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the `image.staging.link.adidas.com` URLs with the production image host, and audit the template for other staging references.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six `mi.adidas.com/p/rp/*.png` content images and the two staging images. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` and `krxd.net` pixels.
3. Rebuild the plain-text part with readable copy and fewer raw tracking URLs, to bring the URL ratio well below 70%.
4. Fix the charset declaration to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` or `<meta charset="UTF-8">`.
5. Put the preheader text inside the `.preheader` div, or remove the empty div.
6. Review the Krux `usermatch` pixels, including the hashed-ID pass-through, for consent and privacy compliance, and confirm the vendor is still active.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and headers to verify the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`, the physical address, authentication results, and the destination and UTM parameters of the 21 skipped links.
