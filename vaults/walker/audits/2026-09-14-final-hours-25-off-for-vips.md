# FINAL HOURS: 25% OFF FOR VIPS!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW carousel I've been riding all week, just with a "FINAL HOURS" stamp slapped on this time. My gut reaction: this brand thinks I don't notice I've gotten this exact "25% OFF FOR VIPS" pitch — with the code ERASER — at least twice already, once yesterday as "TODAY ONLY." Nothing here moves me.
- The shoes in the grid are, once again, every one of them women's — ankle booties, ballet flats, a fringy wedge sneaker. I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for a slip-on with actual arch support, and DSW keeps showing me stuff for someone else's closet. There's also a big dead blank space between the "VIP Exclusive" badge and the "SO NEW, SO YOU" headline — looks like something didn't load.
- I'm marking this one as read and moving on. Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward — there's nothing here for me, and the urgency doesn't land after seeing the same "final hours" story on repeat.

## What's working

- The "Up to 60% off clearance, anyone" line up top is at least honest that it's for everybody, not just VIPs.
- Shop-by-size grid is easy to scan and the tap targets are big enough for my thumbs.
- Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons are a nice touch — my wife needs those, actually.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown is a women's shoe again — no men's category represented at all.
- There's a big empty gap in the middle of the email where nothing rendered — looks broken.
- "FINAL HOURS" urgency doesn't feel real after the same 25% VIP offer showed up as "TODAY ONLY" and "1 Day Left" earlier this week.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, or arch support — just fashion booties and flats.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men out of the "SO NEW, SO YOU" grid — show me men's walking shoes or slides, not repeat the same women's booties I've seen five times.
- 2. Fix or remove that blank section between the badge and the headline — it reads as a broken email on my phone.
- 3. Stop recycling "final hours" language across multiple sends in the same week — pick one real deadline and stick to it, or I'll tune it out entirely.
- 4. Lead with the clearance callout since that's actually new copy, instead of burying it under the repeated VIP 25% pitch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles now 25% off for VIPs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance hits 60% off — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ons, walkers, and wide widths included`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code ERASER before midnight`
