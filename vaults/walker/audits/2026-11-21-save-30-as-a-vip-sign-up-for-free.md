# Save 30% as a VIP (Sign up for free!)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the fifth VIP pitch in two days, after "Want 30% off first? Become a VIP!" this morning and "Become a VIP to shop 30% off first!" yesterday. Same pink banner, same giant black "30% OFF*," new subject line. I'm not sure anyone at DSW is counting.
- The top of the email reads fine on my phone without my glasses. But the "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" line and the asterisk make me suspicious, and the body leads with women's snow boots, a woman in a red bow and ladies' heels. I did spot a "Shop Men" link, but no men's comfort shoe anywhere. Nothing here mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-ons.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, because my buddies don't care about Sorel boots or heels. If DSW showed me a men's walking shoe from Skechers, Hoka or Brooks with a price, I'd look.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF*" on the pink banner is easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- The "BFDEALS" code is clear and tells me exactly what to do at checkout.
- The "Shop by Size" grid with wide width and wide calf options is handy. I'd use the wide width button.
- The "Lucky Basel boot at $74.96" strip at the top gives one real price.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and "Opt-in to texts, get 15% off" are good, simple perks.

## What's weak

- The email is almost all women's boots and heels. I see no men's shoes and no one my age except a woman in a hat.
- "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" with an asterisk is a hedge. The fine print says brands like Skechers, Clarks and Crocs are excluded, and those are the brands I actually buy.
- The scrolling banner at the very top is cut off ("THE SEASON'S BIGGEST DEALS ARE ON! - THE SEASON'S BIGGE"). It looks sloppy.
- The only button for the 30% off is "Become a VIP," so I have to sign up before I see any savings.
- It's the fifth near-identical VIP email in two days. That feels like nagging, not a relationship.
- There's no price, no star rating and no comfort detail on any shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send fewer emails, and tell me up front which brands are excluded. If I'm a men's comfort shopper, lead with men's walking and slip-on shoes from brands that are included.
- 2. Put a price on every shoe shown, and add star ratings.
- 3. Fix the cut-off marquee at the top.
- 4. Make the main button say "Shop Men's 30% Off Styles" instead of only "Become a VIP."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is Braze-sent, with click and open tracking on `ablink.dsw.com`. One CTA link failed the QA probe with HTTP 400, and the plain-text part is dominated by tracking URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be checked. QA pass rate was 62%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA (HTTP 400):** the link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/cXecyR2Cbe5dFWpcYU6wSA~~/AAQRxRA~/qVRjx1XR…`. It is a very long (~600 char) tracking-wrapped URL. A 400 could come from a malformed or expired token. It could also come from the probe being rejected, since SMS opt-in flows often reject non-browser clients. The QA data can't tell these apart. Verify manually in a real browser or on a device. If it is a real 400, this is the highest-impact defect, because it breaks the SMS-capture CTA.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 22,336 of 26,851 characters (~83%) are URLs, from long Braze redirect tokens. This is a known spam-filter signal for the text/plain alternative.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 71 links were probed (59 skipped, mostly footer and social). Their health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, both look like tracking pixels):**
  - `src="https://ablink.dsw.com/q/paWBadWlnQAJdWddiCRonw~~/…"`
  - `src="https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…"`

  Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. If the `ablink` `/q/` image is meant to be visible content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. The charset declaration is effectively missing. This is low risk if the Content-Type header carries the charset.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens were visible in the available source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unverified. This is likely an artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than a sender defect, so don't treat it as a confirmed failure. Check the raw headers at the receiving mailbox.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so they couldn't be checked. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reported no failure for it. No confirmed issue.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers:** not evaluated in the supplied data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs can't be seen without following the redirects. The QA probe didn't report destination mismatches for the 11 links that passed. The tracking pixel URL carries `mi_ecmp=20261121_P261750_V1_W-_Offer_Occas…`, so campaign IDs are present.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts" CTA in a browser and on mobile. If it really returns 400, regenerate the link or fix the SMS-capture endpoint.
2. Add `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Shorten or reduce the URL load in the text/plain part, for example by using short-link domains or link-aliasing in Braze.
6. Re-run QA with the full HTML source to cover the footer and unsubscribe, and capture raw headers to confirm SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`.
7. Raise probe coverage beyond 12 of 71 links, or at least spot-check the remaining product and category links.
