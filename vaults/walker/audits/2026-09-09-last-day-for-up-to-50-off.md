# Last day for up to 50% off!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one's clean at least — no cartoon charms, no college logos, nothing aimed at a teenager. Just a plain "up to 50% off" and a "buy 2, take 30% off" clog deal with a hot pink product shot. There's not a single CTA button anywhere I can see, which is odd for something pushing this hard on urgency, and there's no man or shoe styled for someone my age — it's all the classic clog and flip-flop lineup. I'm not clicking this one, and I sure wouldn't forward it. It's the same discount-stacking format Crocs has sent me a few times this week (I remember the one from 2026-09-07 with the light blue clog — this is basically that email again with a different headline).

## What's working

- The "FINAL HOURS!" and "Up to 50% Off" headline is big, bold, and easy to read without squinting.
- No irrelevant charms-and-logos clutter — it's a straightforward discount pitch, which I can at least respect.
- The clearance stacking offer ("Buy 2, Take 30% Off") is spelled out in plain English, not buried in fine print.
- Text size throughout is large enough that I don't need to zoom on my phone.

## What's weak

- There's no shoe here I'd actually wear — clogs and a thong sandal, nothing that reads as a men's casual or walking shoe.
- I don't see any button to click — no "Shop Now," nothing. If I wanted the deal I wouldn't know where to tap.
- Zero mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support — the stuff that actually gets me to buy a shoe.
- This is the fourth or fifth near-identical "up to X% off" email from Crocs this week — it's starting to blur together and I'm tuning it out.
- "Exclusions Apply" shows up twice with nothing telling me what's excluded — feels like there's a catch they're not upfront about.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real, visible CTA button under each offer block — "Shop the Sale" or similar — so there's something obvious to tap.
- 2. Feature at least one product that fits a guy my age — the Realtree camo boat shoe from the 2026-09-02 email is a much better fit than another clog and flip-flop lineup.
- 3. Cut the redundant second offer block or clearly differentiate it — two stacked discounts back to back is confusing, not motivating.
- 4. Say something about comfort or cushioning in the copy — that's the actual hook for me, not just the percentage off.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: comfortable clogs, up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight — 50% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned comfort, marked way down — through tonight only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack savings before the clock runs out.`
