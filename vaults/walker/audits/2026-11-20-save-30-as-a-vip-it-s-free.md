# Save 30% as a VIP (It’s free!)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this is the same VIP pitch as this morning's "Join VIP, get access to 30% off!" with a new subject line. At least it isn't another UGG blast. The big "30% OFF* (ALMOST) EVERYTHING" on the pink banner is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. "Black Friday starts…NOW!" feels right for the date.
- Then I scrolled, and it turned into a women's gift-guide catalog. The wish-list section is a woman in a blue jacket and sneakers, with UGG, adidas and Birkenstock shown as gift-wrapped items. Nothing for a 62-year-old guy who wants a slip-on with arch support. The "SHOP MEN" link is tiny and sits under the offer, and the "SHOP ALL WOMEN" button is much bigger. The bonus points offer is for UGG and Birkenstock, and I'm not chasing points for a boot.
- Would I click? Maybe "Shop Men" if I'm already looking for shoes. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF*" headline is huge, black on pink, and I can read it without zooming.
- "Black Friday starts…NOW!" and the "BFDEALS" code give a clear, current reason to act.
- "Become a VIP" is a plain black button, and "It's free!" in the subject takes the cost worry away.
- The "Shop by size" block has big tap targets, and the "Wide width" button is a nice touch for my feet.
- The free shipping, in-store pickup and text-signup row is simple and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The asterisk and the long fine print make "(almost) everything" feel like it hides exclusions. I can't tell what's left out without reading tiny type.
- The gift section is women's-heavy. The only men's cue is a small "Shop Men" text link.
- There's no cushioning, arch support, slip-on or walking content. Brooks, Hoka and Skechers aren't featured at all.
- The 500 bonus points are tied to UGG and Birkenstock, so the member perk doesn't help me.
- This is the same VIP message as this morning's send, and I've had about a dozen DSW emails this week. I'm tired of the cadence.
- The top banner text is cut off at the edge ("BIGGE…"), which looks sloppy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the men's and comfort entry points real buttons. Add a "Men's Comfort & Walking" block with slip-ons and cushioned sneakers.
- 2. Say what's excluded in one line next to the 30% ("Excludes UGG, Nike…") instead of burying it.
- 3. Cut the send frequency, or send me a version based on what I browse. I don't need the same VIP offer twice in one day.
- 4. Fix the cut-off top banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `VIP is free: 30% off, code BFDEALS`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off comfort shoes for him, today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ons, walkers & wide widths: Black Friday starts now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Join free, use code BFDEALS in store or online`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The probed links mostly resolve, but the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA returned HTTP 400, and the 62% pass rate comes mainly from that failure and the warnings below. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and address markup could not be checked directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA (HTTP 400):** link text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off", URL `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/tC6kQXp7ajDkFTgvzq4oHg~~/AAQRxRA~/hcGgIGCH…`.
  - Its destination is the SMS opt-in, which is a conversion path, and the wrapped link returns a client error.
  - The probe didn't show whether the cause is an expired or malformed Braze redirect token, or the opt-in endpoint rejecting a non-browser request. Re-test it with a real browser User-Agent before treating it as a confirmed outage.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 70 links were probed and 58 were skipped. The remaining links are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Tracking pixels:** two are present.
  - A Braze open pixel at `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/ZrkZOgTna5_Or0x4Xrl7yg~~/AAQRxRA~/Uchj2glP3x…`.
  - A `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261120_P261746_V1_W-_Offer_New_D` pixel, which looks like a second vendor (Movable Ink-style).
  - Both are expected, but the second pixel carries a user ID (`mi_u`) in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** both flagged images are the tracking pixels above (`o.gif` and the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image). They should have `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. This is low severity, since neither is content imagery.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing. Charset is still covered by the transport `Content-Type` header, but the tags are dead code.
- **Plain-text part:** 22,696 of 27,717 characters (82%) are URLs. This is a deliverability WARN, caused by long Braze-wrapped tracking links.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` value in the pixel URL is resolved. The source was truncated, so tokens further down the body can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header wasn't found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a limitation of the AgentMail relay capture, not evidence of a sender failure.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it. The footer markup, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't in the truncated source or QA output, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** can't be verified, because every link is wrapped in an `ablink.dsw.com` redirect with an opaque token. UTMs, if any, are only visible after redirect resolution, and the QA output doesn't include final landing URLs.
- **Campaign ID:** the `mi_ecmp` value (`20261120_P261746_V1_W-_Offer_New_D`) shows a consistent campaign ID, but it can't be matched to a landing page from the data provided.
- **Broken opt-in link:** the 400 means the SMS opt-in destination is unreachable from the email, so the continuity check fails for that CTA.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Retest the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link with a browser User-Agent. If it still returns 400, regenerate the Braze link or fix the opt-in endpoint. This is the only confirmed conversion-affecting defect.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Fix or remove the two meta tags missing `http-equiv`.
5. Capture the full HTML (footer included) and the raw headers, then check for the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for the primary CTAs and log the final URLs so UTM and landing-page alignment can be checked.
7. Probe more than 12 links, or at least all primary CTAs, to reduce the 58 unchecked links.
