# Shopped Black Friday Yet?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again, and the deal finally moved. After the "2 for $50" run through 11/15, then "Up to 60% Off? Yes Please." on 11/16 and the Space Jam send earlier today, this one stacks "Starting at $30" on classic clogs and "Up to 60% Off" on top of each other. It reads like two emails stitched together. I can read the big black type on my phone, so no complaints on size.
- The hero picture is a tiny red clog floating in a huge orange field of starbursts. I scrolled past a lot of empty space to find a shoe. The kids' clogs "under $25" block is a nice picture, but I'm 62 and not shopping for kids. Nothing here is about cushioning, arch support or walking. It's loud, it's cheap, and it's aimed at somebody else.
- Would I click? Maybe the "Shop the Deal" button, out of curiosity about a $30 clog. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- "Starting at $30" in big black type on pink is easy to read on my phone.
- The buttons are big, black and rounded, so they're easy to tap with my thumb.
- "Free shipping on qualified orders" sits in the top banner where I'd look for it.
- The "Black Friday deals are here" block with "60% OFF" in white on black is a clear second offer.
- The footer states the end dates, Nov 29 and Dec 7, so I know the sale isn't forever.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is tiny and lost in a sea of orange starbursts. I can't see what I'm buying.
- "Up to 60% off select styles" and "Exclusions Apply," printed twice, feel like fine print I can't trust. Which styles?
- The kids' clogs "under $25" section takes a big share of the email and has nothing to do with me.
- There's nothing about comfort, arch support or all-day walking. For me that is the whole reason to buy a shoe.
- Two different offers and two different deadlines in the fine print made me wonder which one is real. The email didn't tell me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a real product, big and clear, in the hero. Put a men's clog or a slip-on in it with a comfort line like "Cushioned all day."
- 2. Pick one offer. Either $30 clogs or 60% off, not both stacked.
- 3. Say which styles are included in the 60% off, or show three of them with prices.
- 4. Show men's styles near the top, or send me a version that does. Kids' stuff belongs in a separate send.
- 5. Put one clear end date in the body, not buried in the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Classic clogs from $30 — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort clogs, up to 60% off this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lightweight, cushioned, easy on and off. Doorbuster prices through Nov 29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our softest clogs for all-day wear, now from $30. Free shipping on qualified orders.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped 16 tracking links (click-redirect domain), so no destination, status or redirect-chain data exists. The only visible tracking URL is the open pixel `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10213&bmt=0`, which looks like a Salesforce Marketing Cloud pattern. It was not probed.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) call. It fires on open and passes a persistent ID (`d_dpuid`) in the query string. This is a privacy and consent-surface consideration, and a likely source of blocked-image or Apple MPP noise.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (QA-confirmed):** the demdex `<img>` has no `alt`. Add `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"` and a hiding style if not already present.
- **Ineffective meta tags (source-confirmed):**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it declares nothing.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it is also inert.
  - Charset is normally fixed by the MIME `Content-Type` header, so the practical risk is low, but the tags are invalid.
- **Fonts:** `TT Crocs` is loaded from `media.crocs.com` (woff2/woff, `font-display: swap`). A fallback stack could not be checked in the truncated body. Most clients ignore `@font-face` anyway.
- **Hidden elements:** the preheader and spacer-character div are hidden with `display:none`. No problem found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this is only partly verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Not verifiable:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the truncated source or QA data. The From domain `crocs-email.com` should be checked for alignment with the DKIM `d=` domain on a raw-message review.
