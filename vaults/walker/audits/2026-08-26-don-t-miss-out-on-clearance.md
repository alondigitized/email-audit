# Don't miss out on clearance!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW clearance email, different day — "up to 60% off," a size grid up top, and then a mile-long scroll through wedge booties, platform clogs, strappy heels, and glitter mules. I get maybe three items in this entire thing I'd wear: the New Balance sneaker and the boat-shoe-looking loafer in the "Everyone Loves These Names" block. Everything else — heeled sandals, sparkly slingbacks, tortoiseshell platforms — is aimed at a woman, not a 62-year-old guy looking for something cushioned to slip on.
- The size grid (5 through 11) is the one thing that's genuinely useful to me on this phone screen, and it's right up top where I can tap it fast. But this is the fourth or fifth near-identical clearance email from DSW this week — I flagged the same "up to 60% off" headline on the 24th and 25th — and scrolling through seven or eight product sections of women's shoes to find nothing in my size or style gets old fast.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. I'll glance at the size grid out of habit, see nothing changed, and archive it.

## What's working

- The "SHOP BY SIZE" grid is right at the top and big enough to tap without squinting.
- "Up to 60% off clearance" is a real number, not vague coupon math.
- New Balance sneaker in the "Everyone Loves These Names" row is something I'd actually consider.
- Clean white background, decent contrast — text is readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- Nearly the entire product lineup — heeled mules, glitter sandals, platform clogs, strappy sandals — is women's product; nothing here says "men's casual" or "comfort."
- "MADE FOR SUMMER DAYS" sandal block is nice-looking but not remotely aimed at me.
- This is the same "up to 60% off" clearance beat DSW's run daily this week — nothing new to notice.
- No men's category link anywhere near the top — I'd have to hunt through the nav bar myself.
- Huge scroll for a phone: eight or nine product sections before I even hit "SHOPPING MADE EASY."

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a men's clearance shortcut right next to the size grid — even one row of men's sneakers/slip-ons up top would keep me scrolling instead of bailing after ten seconds.
- 2. Segment this send — women get the heels and clogs, I get comfort/casual men's styles. DSW clearly has my purchase history; use it.
- 3. Cut the redundant "just dropped," "summer days," "everyone loves these names" blocks into one shorter email — five sections deep and I still haven't seen a men's shoe.
- 4. Vary the subject/creative day to day — three straight "up to 60% off" sends in a row is training me to skip it without opening.
