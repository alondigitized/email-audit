**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Another cart nag, and the third "waiting" email I've had from Skechers in two days. At least it's the black Contour Foam Slip-ins from this morning's "Your cart expires soon!" and not that skull sneaker I've been pestered about since 11/05. The shoe is big and easy to see. Then the email buries the 30% off. The sale banner sits halfway down, below a grid of six shoes.
- The "More great options" grid is better for me than I expected. It has men's Slip-ins boots and a Relaxed Fit slipper, which is my kind of footwear. But the text under each shoe is tiny. On my phone I'd have to pinch-zoom to read "Waterproof Relaxed Fit: Respected 2.0 – Orson Boots." I'd probably click, but only because I already know the 30% is coming. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero shoe is large and clean against white, so I know right away what's in my cart.
- "Complete Your Order" is a wide navy button. It's easy to tap and says what it does.
- The men's Slip-ins picks (waterproof boot, Murette slipper) are hands-free styles I'd actually wear.
- The red 30% off Black Friday banner is hard to miss once I scroll to it. It lists the 11/22 deadline.
- The Women / Men / Kids / Sale list is big, plain text that's easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The 30% member discount is about halfway down the email. It should be at the top, next to the cart.
- The product captions under the grid are far too small for my eyes.
- "Hi, Walker" and my points balance are missing, even though the 11/19 "Holiday Ready" email had both. This feels like a mass blast.
- The headline "We noticed you noticing us…" is a bit creepy. I don't want to be watched.
- The cart shoe isn't one I'd pick, and the email shows no price, sizes, or reviews. The Contour Foam shoe is a women's style with rose-gold touches.
- The fine print about the offer is tiny and sits at the very bottom.

- 3. Make the product captions at least 14px and show a price on each one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is Attentive-wrapped cart-abandonment HTML. It has two confirmed defects: the unsubscribe link probe returned HTTP 400 and the plain-text part is empty. It also has a malformed charset declaration and a large amount of non-HTTPS image sourcing. The HTML source was truncated, so I could not check everything.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe failed (400).**
  - URL: `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m60`.
  - The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. This is a one-click token endpoint, and a bare probe may be rejected without browser context or a POST. The 400 is therefore not proof the link is dead for real recipients, but it is unverified and should be checked manually.
- **Primary CTAs unverified (429).** Both cart CTAs were rate-limited by `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`: "Complete you Order" and "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting...". The destinations are unconfirmed. 31 of 43 links were skipped, so coverage is partial.
- **Every link is an opaque Attentive redirect** (`/e/c/<token>`), including the "web version" link. Final destinations cannot be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Charset declaration is malformed.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. That makes the declaration inert. There is also no `<meta charset="utf-8">`. The preheader contains `͏` and soft-hyphen characters, so any client that doesn't assume UTF-8 may mis-render the preheader padding.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **Mixed-content images.** 12 or more images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`. These include the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the hero (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), the app badges, the Afterpay icon and the social icons. The same host serves `https://` for `m/21/021bd784-…gif`, so HTTPS is available. Some clients and webviews may block or warn on these.
- **Missing alt text on three images.**
  - `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif`.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`.
  - The Attentive open pixel, `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`. It should have `alt=""` and a hidden role.
- **Alt text typo on the CTA image.** The CTA image uses alt "Complete you Order", which a screen reader will read aloud.
- The head includes a viewport meta, `lang="en"`, and 620px stacking media queries. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The product and cart blocks fall in the truncated portion, so I could not verify them.

## 5. Compliance
- **Empty plain-text part.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". The email is HTML-only (no `text/plain` part), which hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- **Unsubscribe link functionality is unconfirmed.** See section 2. A visible unsubscribe link exists (`attn_macro=…unsubscribeLink`).
- **Authentication status is unknown.** The `Authentication-Results` header was absent in the captured message. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `msgs.skechers.com` cannot be confirmed. This is a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay, not a confirmed failure.
- **Other compliance items unchecked.** The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address were not in the provided data or the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing alignment cannot be assessed.** Destinations are hidden behind Attentive redirects, and the two cart CTAs returned 429. No UTM parameters are visible in the source.
- **Tracking is Attentive-native.** It is click-wrapped, with an open pixel and `attn_*` parameters on the unsubscribe link. That is consistent but not auditable from this capture.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually verify the unsubscribe URL (`attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…`) in a real client, and confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present.
2. Add a `text/plain` alternative part.
3. Fix the charset: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
4. Change all `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` image sources to `https://`.
5. Add alt text to the GIF and the `a6ec5198…jpg`, add `alt=""` to the open pixel, fix "Complete you Order" to "Complete your Order", and fill in the `<title>`.
6. Re-probe the two cart CTAs with backoff to confirm their destinations and UTM and cart-restore parameters.
7. Capture full headers (`Authentication-Results`, `Received-SPF`, `DKIM-Signature`) from a direct-delivery copy to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
