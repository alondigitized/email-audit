# Sleek stability
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a HOKA shoe email, and it's more my kind of shoe email than the trail ones were. "Stability stars" is huge, white on purple, and I can read it on my phone without squinting. The shoe is right there, with a line about "lightweight cushioning" and H-Frame stability. I walk the mall and the sidewalk, so that line speaks to me more than "Born to romp" did on 10/29.
- The middle is just four shoes in a grid: Arahi 9 and Gaviota 6, each in Women and Men. The Gaviota is the "plush" one, and plush is what I'm after. But there's no price, no sale and no reason to click today. Then the big photo is two young runners on cracked pavement. I don't run. It's the same story as "One more run" on 10/23.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on the Gaviota 6 Men, just to see the price. Would I forward it? No. It's a nice catalog page, but nothing in it would make me tell a friend.

## What's working

- "Stability stars" is big and easy to read, and the shoe on top is the first thing I see.
- "Lightweight cushioning" and stability are close to what I want, which is cushion and support under my feet.
- The Arahi and Gaviota are labeled Men and Women, so I can find mine fast.
- "Plush + stable" is plain talk. I know right away the Gaviota is the softer ride.
- The "Shop Stability" button is black, easy to see and easy to tap with a thumb.

## What's weak

- There's no price or offer anywhere. I cross-shop Brooks, New Balance and the DSW sale aisle, so with no number I have nothing to compare.
- The running photo is two young, fit runners, and that's not me. The "Road Running Guide" below it is also a running pitch, not a walking one.
- The shoe names have no details. I can't tell if there's a wide size, how much arch support it has, or if it's slip-on.
- The footer text is tiny: "Unlock Free Shipping" and "FLY for 30 Guarantee." That's the part that might actually tempt me, and it's buried in small type.
- This is the sixth HOKA email I've audited in about two weeks, and it asks for nothing new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price, or a free-shipping or 30-day-guarantee line, right under the hero. That one change would lift this email the most.
- 2. Say "walking" or "all-day comfort" next to the stability copy, not only the runner talk.
- 3. Add one line per shoe, such as "Wide sizes available," "Cushion: plush" or "$150," so I can compare without tapping.
- 4. Swap the runner photo for someone my age walking, or drop it.
- 5. Make the "30 Guarantee" and free-shipping lines bigger and move them up.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: HOKA "Sleek stability"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is Cordial-sent, table-based, with consistent UTM tagging and a wrapped unsubscribe link. It has no plain-text part, almost no image alt text, and its `<head>` has a few markup defects. The automated link probe flagged all 9 probed links, but the uniform 406/403 pattern points to click-domain bot filtering, not confirmed dead destinations.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 9 probed click-tracked links failed**, including the unsubscribe link. They are all on `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6adfabd16e8efff867076e39:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/<hash>`. Eight returned 406 (HOKA logo, Arahi 9 image, Gaviota 6 image, Shoe Finder image, "Locate a Store", "hoka.com", "Unlock Free Shipping", "FLY for 30 Guarantee") and the unsubscribe link returned 403.
- **Probable cause:** a WAF or bot filter on the Cordial click domain rejecting the probe's user-agent. A real 406 on every distinct link is unlikely. **Unverified:** this is an inference, not a confirmed destination failure.
- **Decoded `jwtP` destinations are well-formed**, e.g. `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/mens-everyday-running-gear/arahi-9/1176531.html?dwvar_1176531_color=FLCK&utm_...`. The probe could not confirm they resolve.
- **Audience data in tracked URLs.** The decoded `utm_campaign` is `H_US_BR_20261105_Stability_STO_A30Day_365StabilityPurchasers_365StabilityTag_30NewSubs_1018Openers_AIPurchasePropensityMedium_WishListAdd`. It carries segment definitions, including purchase-propensity and wishlist flags, into every destination URL and the client's analytics.
- **Hashed email in the query string.** Each URL includes `hmail=9153f33f…ced00`, a 64-hex value consistent with an unsalted SHA-256 of the recipient's address. It is passed to the destination and any third-party scripts there.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 1 skipped (QA WARN). Coverage is incomplete.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback missing.** QA FAIL: "Text version is 0 chars." This is a deliverability and accessibility issue.
- **Alt text missing on 14 images.** These include the hero images `US_20261105_Stability_a/b/c/d/e.jpg`, the product tiles (`US_Arahi9_*`, `US_Gaviota6_*`), `US_Q3_ShoeFinder_Black.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - Several of these are the only content of CTA links. The QA link text falls back to `[image: US_Gaviota6_BottomLeft_1171933-SLWL.jpg]`, so with images blocked or in a screen reader the links have no meaningful name.
  - The two tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…`, `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`) are fine without alt text.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Charset declaration ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare a charset.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags** with conflicting values. One has `maximum-scale=1`, which restricts pinch-zoom on some clients, and the other has `user-scalable=yes`.
- **Invalid CSS values.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` are missing their numeric values and will be dropped. This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Global reset risk.** `* {… border:none; outline:none; …}` removes focus outlines, which hurts keyboard navigation in clients that support it.
- **Web fonts.** `@font-face` pulls `TheFutureHOKA` from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...`, which is a Salesforce Commerce Cloud path. A site deploy can change or remove it. I haven't checked that the URLs resolve.
- The HTML source was truncated, so layout and body markup were not audited.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data. The body was truncated, so this applies only to the visible portion.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link present.** It is labeled "Unsubscribe" and decodes to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_...`. The probe got a 403 on the click-tracking wrapper, so a working one-click path is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication unknown.** QA WARN: `Authentication-Results` not found, "SPF/DKIM status unknown" because the message arrived via the AgentMail relay. The sender is `info@emails.hoka.com`, and I can't confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** were not provided, so RFC 8058 one-click support can't be assessed.
- **Physical postal address** was not visible in the truncated source or QA data, so I can't confirm it.
- The missing plain-text part (see section 3) also affects spam scoring.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM tagging is consistent** across all decoded links: `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261105_Stability_…`. No issues found.
- **Product links go to specific PDPs with color preselected.** For example, Arahi 9 goes to `dwvar_1176531_color=FLCK` and Gaviota 6 to `dwvar_1171933_color=SLWL`. The landing pages match the image filenames.
- **`utm_source=US_Broadcast`** is a campaign-type label, not a source name. This is a convention issue only and doesn't break tracking.
- The landing pages themselves were not verified because the probe was blocked.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart plain-text alternative.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially those that are the sole content of a link. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Whitelist the audit probe or re-test the links with a browser-like user-agent. Confirm the unsubscribe and CTA destinations resolve before treating the 406/403 results as real failures.
4. Remove audience-segment names and the `hmail` hash from outbound URLs. Use an opaque campaign ID and resolve segments server-side.
5. Fix the `<head>`:
   - Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`.
   - Add a real `<title>`.
   - Keep a single viewport tag without `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Remove or fix the `px`-only CSS rules.
6. Confirm the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results and `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers on the raw message, since the relay hid them.
7. Self-host the web fonts, or fall back to system fonts, so a site deploy can't break them.
