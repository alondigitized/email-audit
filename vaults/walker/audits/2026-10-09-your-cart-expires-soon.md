The 8th's "30% off the shoes you've been eyeing" at least said "Hi, Walker" and gave me a real discount.
- The one shoe that fits me is down in the grid: the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit: Altman in Murdock. It's a hands-free slip-on, which is what I wear. ## What's working

- The Skechers logo sits at the top, so I know who it's from right away.
- The "Complete Your Order" button is big, dark navy and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The Altman slip-on is in there. It's the right shoe for me, even if it's buried.
- The 10% off bar for members is readable, and it says "Valid thru 10/11," so I know the deadline.

## What's weak

- The hero is a women's floral sneaker I never touched. I've seen it in every cart nag this week, and "You liked these" is simply wrong.
- Only one men's shoe appears in the six-shoe grid, and the rest are women's or boys' styles.
- None of the shoes has a price. I can't do any price-versus-quality math without clicking.
- There's no "Hi, Walker" and no points balance. Yesterday's email had both, and this one treats me like a stranger.
- It's the same email again. Yesterday's 30% off became 10% off today, so waiting a day cost me.
- The list of categories, the apps, Afterpay and Klarna, and the texting pitch all pile up. They bury the one shoe I might want.

- 2. Cap the cart reminders. One or two, then stop. Don't send three nags a day with different subjects.
- 3. Show prices, and show the member price on each shoe.
- 4. Put the Altman in the hero, with the words "Hands-free" and "Relaxed fit" next to it.
- 5. Don't undercut yesterday's offer. If you gave me 30% off, don't follow it with 10%.
