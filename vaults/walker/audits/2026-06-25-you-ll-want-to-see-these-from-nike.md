## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival's in my inbox again — no surprise there. I've been opening these almost every day for the better part of a month. - **Preview text is a raw tracking URL.** What I saw in my inbox before opening was "Shoe Carnival" followed by a long link. That's not copy — it reads like something broke, and it does nothing to pull me in.
- **Basketball focus doesn't land for a 62-year-old.** "Bring the Heat," "Shop Basketball," Nike court sneakers, Jordan drops — this is a send built for someone thirty years younger than me.
- **The $10 reward is buried.** The one element that might actually bring me into a store is sitting near the footer, below three other content sections. That's backwards.
- **Not one word about comfort.** Cushioning, arch support, fit, ease of entry — none of it. For a shopper like me, that's the whole conversation, and this email skips it entirely.

- 2. **Fix the preview text immediately.** A tracking URL in the preheader is a production error. One line of real copy would be a complete upgrade.
- 3. **Segment comfort walkers out of the basketball send.** I don't need to see court sneakers. If there's a New Balance comfort walker or a cushioned Skechers on the site, that's my email — not this one.
- 4. **Add one comfort/walking block even within the Nike frame.** There are Nike comfort walkers out there. Slot one panel in for the guys like me who aren't in the Jordan market.

## Technical Audit — Shoe Carnival: "You'll want to see these from Nike"

**From:** `shoecarnival@email.shoecarnival.com`
**Campaign:** `SCA_20260608_nike_AM`

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Five of seven broken-link failures share a single root cause: a Unicode zero-width space (U+200B) is embedded inside the click-tracker URL parameter string, making the URLs non-ASCII and causing codec failures at the redirect layer. A second tracker domain (`0di86k1v.emltrk.com`) is unreachable entirely.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Critical — U+200B in tracker URLs (5 links)**

Every failure on `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` includes a zero-width space at character position 66–68 of the URL, immediately after the `utm_campaign` value:

```
…t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260608_nike_AM​   ← U+200B here (invisible)
```

Affected redirect types: `?rd`, `?rl`, `?p`, `?f`, and the open-tracking pixel (`?i=`). This character is almost certainly introduced by a copy-paste from a rich-text source (Word, Google Docs, Notion) when building the campaign template. At minimum, click tracking is broken for all links in this batch; at worst, the open pixel also fails silently.

**Fix:** Strip all non-ASCII characters from the URL token field before injection. Run `url.encode('ascii')` or equivalent on the `t=` parameter value at template render time.

**Critical — Second tracker domain unreachable (2 links)**

Both `?rd` and `?rl` requests to `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` timed out:

```
https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?rd&d=[UNIQUE]
https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?rl&d=[UNIQUE]
```

Note that `[UNIQUE]` appears to be an unfilled merge token in the QA snapshot (see Section 4). Whether the timeout is infrastructure failure or a token-substitution failure preventing resolution is unclear without live send data.

**Info — 24 tracking links skipped**

QA skipped HTTP probes on 24 redirect URLs by policy. These were not validated.

Product images without alt text fail screen readers and degrade experience in image-blocked clients. Tracking pixels conventionally use `alt=""` (empty string) to suppress display — verify these are explicitly set to `""` rather than omitted entirely, as omission triggers some accessibility scanners.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unfilled token — `[UNIQUE]`**

The QA snapshot shows `d=[UNIQUE]` in the `0di86k1v` tracker URL rather than a resolved subscriber ID. This is either:
- A test/seed address that received the literal token instead of a substituted value, or

If the latter, every recipient received `[UNIQUE]` verbatim, making per-recipient deduplication on that pixel impossible.
