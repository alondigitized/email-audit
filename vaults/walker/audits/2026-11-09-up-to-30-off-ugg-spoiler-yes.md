# Up to 30% off UGG?! (Spoiler: YES!)
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this time it's a different offer, which I'll admit is a relief after a week of the TINSEL 20% emails. "Early Black Friday Sale, up to 30% off UGG" is big and easy to read on my phone. But UGG boots aren't really my shoe. I want a cushioned walking shoe, not a fuzzy boot.
- The rest of the email lost me fast. The whole middle is dress shoes for a party: women's heels, men's loafers, kids' Mary Janes. It's a long scroll of models posing around a gift card. Nothing here says arch support, walking, or slip-on comfort. I'd skim it and probably not click. I might forward the UGG part to my wife, but not the rest.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 30% OFF*" headline is huge and I can read it without zooming.
- The UGG logo and the boot photo make the one offer clear right away.
- It's a fresh offer after the repeated 20% TINSEL pitches, so it doesn't feel like the seventh copy of the same email.
- The "Extra 10% off for military & veterans" banner is clearly set apart and easy to read.
- The size grid with "Shop wide width" is a nice touch for a guy with wider feet. The buttons are big enough to tap.

## What's weak

- The middle of the email is all party and dress shoes. I'm not shopping for that, and it feels like a different email stapled on.
- There's no comfort, cushioning, walking or slip-on content anywhere, not even a New Balance or Brooks tile.
- The sale is "online only" with fine print at the bottom, and the asterisk on the percentage makes me wonder what the catch is.
- It's very long, and the legal text at the bottom is tiny. I'd have to zoom to read it.
- The subject says UGG, but most of the email isn't about UGG. The "Prepare for compliments" copy is a bit cutesy for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the dress-shoe block with a comfort block: cushioned walking shoes and slip-ons for men, with a price like "Brooks, New Balance, Skechers, up to 30% off".
- 2. Keep UGG at the top, but add a men's UGG slipper or a Tasman option so it fits guys like me.
- 3. Pick one main call-to-action, and put the wide-width and size shortcuts higher up.
- 4. Say what the asterisk means right under the headline, like "Select styles, ends Nov. 11".
- 5. Don't send a different "last chance" email every day. Space the sends out.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only through Nov. 11. Plus extra 10% off for military & veterans.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a Braze-built XHTML table email with dark-mode support. The confirmed issues are a zoom-blocking viewport tag, two malformed `<meta>` tags, two images with no `alt`, and a link-heavy plain-text part. The source is truncated and the QA run had no authentication headers, so body-level checks (tokens, footer, unsubscribe) are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 20117 of 24489 characters (about 82%) are URLs. This comes from long `ablink.dsw.com/q/...~~/AAQRxRA~/...` tracking redirects. A text part dominated by raw URLs can hurt spam scoring and is hard to read in text-only clients.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 of 60 links were probed and 48 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). QA reports no broken links among the 12 probed, but the other 48 are unverified.
- **Click tracking is opaque.** Links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects. The final destinations are not visible in the source or QA data, so I can't confirm where they land.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) in clients that honor it.
- **Two `<meta>` tags are malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so both are ignored. The charset is then undeclared in the document. Most clients fall back to the MIME header, so the practical risk is low.
- **Missing `alt` on the tracked image.** QA flags `IcFAbioaYKh_D4BhO1oI4R73ZS59d__BL74HsbRionspeY1v_j` (src `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/DXZCxWiFgzzLYUz9B8hFMw~~/AAQRxRA~/...`). The `src` is a click-tracking URL, which suggests either a tracked image or a mis-wrapped image URL. If it is a content image, it has no accessible name and no fallback when images are blocked.
- **Missing `alt` on the open pixel.** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif` has no `alt`. A tracking pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- **Web fonts are limited to a few clients.** Futura PT and Helvetica Now load from `braze-images.com` (`.woff` and `.ttf`). Gmail and Outlook ignore `@font-face`, so those clients depend on the fallback stack. I can't see the stack in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `${...}`) appear in the subject, sender or visible source. The remainder of the body was truncated and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication is unverified.** QA found no `Authentication-Results` header, noting it was "Expected via AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM status unknown". This is a limit of the capture path, not evidence of a sender failure. The From domain `e.dsw.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign, but SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unconfirmed.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address are unverified.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the result isn't in the findings. I can't confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers or the postal address, because the footer is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs can't be verified.** Every link goes through `ablink.dsw.com`, and the final URLs aren't in the data, so UTM parameters and landing alignment with the UGG offer can't be confirmed.
- **The open pixel shows campaign IDs.** `mi.dsw.com/.../o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261109_P261677_V1_W-_Offer_New_U...` carries a campaign ID, so the platform has campaign-level attribution. The `mi_ecmp` value is truncated.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
2. Fix the two meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel. Check whether the tracked image `IcFAbioaYKh_...` is a content image, and give it a descriptive `alt` if so.
4. Shorten the plain-text part. Drop the raw tracking URLs from it, or use a text-specific template with readable links.
5. Re-run QA with full headers, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` can be checked. Also resolve the redirect chains for the CTA links to confirm the final URLs and their UTMs.
