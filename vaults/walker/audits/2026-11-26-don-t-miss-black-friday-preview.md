# Don't miss: Black Friday preview
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's New Balance again. Today's the third "Black Friday preview" I've gotten, so my reaction was "I've seen this one." The hero is a family on a couch with red gift boxes. That's the same gift-box idea as the 22nd, and a few friendly young faces don't do much for a 62-year-old man looking for a walking shoe. The shoes only show up after a long scroll.
- The offer itself is clear: up to 40% off select styles, with free shipping across the top. The shoe grid at the bottom is decent. It has the 880v15 and 1080v15, which are the cushioned ones I'd actually look at. But nothing tells me which are cushioned, which have arch support, or what they cost. I'd probably skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. I might click if I were already shopping Black Friday.

## What's working

- "Up to 40% off select styles" is big and easy to read on my phone.
- The free shipping banner at the top is a nice extra.
- The 880v15 and 1080v15 are the comfort-running shoes I'd cross-shop against Hoka and Brooks.
- Two plain black "Shop now" buttons are easy to tap.
- The Men / Women / Kids tiles are large and simple to navigate.

## What's weak

- The gift-box hero is the same idea as the 22nd, and none of the people look like me.
- No shoe appears until well below the fold.
- The shoe tiles have a name and nothing else: no price, no sale price, no "cushioned" or "arch support." I can't do my price-versus-comfort math.
- The fine print sits right under the buttons in tiny type, with "Exclusions apply." That makes me wonder which shoes are really 40% off.
- The sale has run from November 19 to December 28, so there's no real urgency. "Don't miss" is empty when the sale runs five weeks.
- The shoe tiles sit on a dark background that clashes with the white sections above them.

- 2. Put prices and a short comfort tag on every tile ("Max cushion," "Wide widths").
- 3. Have a real reason to send this one. I've had this preview three times and nothing new has been added.
- 4. Add a men's walking or comfort row, since that's the shopper reading this.
- 5. Move the fine print further down and name the excluded styles.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders with a template-reuse footprint: placeholder values are left in the source, and three images are missing `alt`. The QA run found no broken links, but SPF/DKIM could not be verified and the plain-text part is almost entirely URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and header findings below are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Placeholder asset URL in CSS:** `.bgwhite {… background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png'); }`. It is a non-HTTPS placeholder that cannot resolve. Any element using `.bgwhite` will trigger a failed request, and Gmail or Outlook may flag it as mixed content.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 13207 of 14933 characters (about 88%) are URLs. The tracked redirect URLs on `link.email.newbalance.com` are long, which can raise spam-filter scores.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 25 skipped. No probe failures were reported for the probed set, so the 25 skipped links (footer, utility, social) are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12741736_95921_24056768.gif` is flagged for missing `alt`. This is minor for a pixel, but `alt=""` is the correct form.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking GIF above

  The first two are content images, so they need meaningful alt text.
- **`@font-face` rules have no `src`:** Every block, for example `@font-face { font-family: proxima-nova,sans-serif; font-weight: 400; … }`, lacks a `src` descriptor and is invalid. Web fonts never load, and `.ff` and `.fst*` fall back to Arial or Times New Roman. One block also has a duplicated `font-family` declaration.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Template `<title>`:** `New Balance Template` is left over from the template.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an `xmlns` attribute and XHTML-style self-closing tags.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `.bg1` is defined twice, first `#ffffff` and then `#58546d`. The later rule wins unless the dark-mode override applies.
- **Dark-mode link contrast:** The `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block forces `a {color:#cf0a2c !important;}` on `#151415`. By my calculation that is about 3.3:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 for body-size text. The same rule may also override white link text inside CTA buttons, but the truncated source doesn't show that.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear. The body past the truncation point was not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC are unknown. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is not necessarily a sender fault. Verify against the raw headers.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not provided, so one-click support for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules is unconfirmed.
- **Physical address:** It is not visible in the truncated source, so it is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Link wrapping:** Links use the ESP wrapper `link.email.newbalance.com`, so the final landing URL and any UTM parameters are not visible and can't be assessed from the source.
- **Reused template assets:** Image paths contain `/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/…`, so the shipping modules are lifted from a "myNB Welcome" template. This can be a content-sync risk if those assets carry stale shipping terms. It is also consistent with the placeholder CSS and title above.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove or replace `url('http://image.XXXXX.png')` and any `.bgwhite` usage.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking GIF.
3. Fix or remove the `@font-face` blocks by adding `src` URLs, or drop them and rely on the Arial fallback stack. Set a real `<title>`, and correct or remove the `IE=edge` meta tag.
4. Raise dark-mode link contrast above 4.5:1 and scope the `a` override so CTA text isn't affected.
5. Shorten the plain-text version, for example with a shorter tracking-domain path or fewer inline URLs, to bring the URL ratio down.
6. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` headers from the original message, not the relay copy.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (address, unsubscribe), the unprobed links, and UTM parameters.
