## What's working

- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner up top at least gestures toward general shopping, even if it's vague.
- No render bugs — images loaded clean, text is legible, nothing overlapping.
- The 40% off summer sale copy near the bottom is a real, usable offer if I'd scrolled that far.

## What's weak

- The whole hero section — headline, three-photo collage, paragraph of copy — is 100% jersey, 0% shoes. I don't play soccer, don't have a team, this isn't me.
- Models are all young guys in their 20s on a stone plaza — nothing about this scene reflects a 62-year-old comfort shopper.
- The one thing I'd actually care about, the 40% off sale, is stuck below a giant unrelated campaign with no shoe product shown at all — just a vague blue fabric banner.
- No CTA button on the sale section — the jersey pitch gets a "Shop online or at your local adidas store" line, but the sale just... stops, no button, nothing to tap.

- 1. Flip the order — lead with the 40% off summer sale and an actual shoe photo, push the jersey content down or drop it for people who've never engaged with soccer content (that's most of my history with this brand).
- 2. Add a real CTA button under the sale section — right now there's copy and no way to act on it.
- 3. Show a specific shoe in the sale banner instead of an abstract fabric close-up — I need to see what's actually discounted.
- 4. Stop sending jersey emails to my address altogether if I've got zero click history on soccer gear — this is the fourth or fifth one this month with nothing for me.
