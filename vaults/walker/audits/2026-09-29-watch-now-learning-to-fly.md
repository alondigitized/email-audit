# Technical Audit: HOKA® "Watch now: "Learning to Fly""

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text part, and none of its images has alt text, including the linked SMS CTA image. The QA probe also flagged three links as broken, but the 403s look like bot-blocking of the probe rather than confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **SMS CTA link returned 400.**
  - Wrapped URL: `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6aa876a5…/5db1fb2a?jwtH=…`
  - The JWT payload decodes to `https://hoka.attn.tv/p/ie7/landing-page?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260929_LearningToFlyFilm_…`.
  - A 400 (not 403) from the Cordial wrapper suggests the request was rejected as malformed, or the token was rejected. This is the strongest candidate for a real defect.
  - It could still be probe-specific, so re-test with a real client.
- **`return-start` link (403) and Unsubscribe link (403).**
  - Both go through the same Cordial wrapper and decode to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_…` and `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…&utm_…`.
  - A 403 on both, from the same wrapper domain, points to bot or WAF filtering of the QA probe. I can't confirm they fail for real subscribers.
  - Verify the unsubscribe link manually, since it is the one that matters.
- **Tracking wrapper is opaque.** Each link uses a `jwtH/jwtP/jwtS` signed redirect. The payload includes `"ce":86400`, which looks like a 24-hour expiry. If that is a link TTL, clicks after 24 hours may fail. This is worth confirming with Cordial, since the QA probe result doesn't prove it.
- **Tracking pixels.** Two open-tracking images are present: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. Two pixels from different subdomains (Cordial and Mailgun-style `mg.`) suggests a redundant or mixed sending path. This is not a defect on its own.
- **Coverage gap.** 12 material links were found, but only 4 were probed and 8 were skipped.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 images.** These are `US_20260929_LearningToFlyFilm_a/b/c.jpg`, `US_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`, `LogoFooterPrimary.png`, and the two tracking pixels.
  - The SMS CTA image is inside a link, so the link has no accessible name. The QA output shows its link text as `[image: US_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg]`.
  - If the images are blocked, the email is blank apart from the footer.
  - The pixels are less important, but an empty `alt=""` is standard for them.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Charset declaration is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute. Some clients may ignore it, which matters here because the subject contains curly quotes.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags with conflicting values.** One has `maximum-scale=1` and the other has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`.
  - `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom in some clients.
- **Invalid CSS from empty template variables.**
  - `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` are invalid declarations.
  - Browsers ignore them, so the impact is low, but they suggest unresolved template variables.
- **Global reset.** `* {…border:none;outline:none…}` removes focus outlines in clients that support keyboard focus.
