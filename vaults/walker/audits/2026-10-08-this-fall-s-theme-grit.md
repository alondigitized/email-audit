- The "All-season styles" headline is big and readable on my phone without zooming.
- The free sock 3-pack with 2+ items is a real offer I can understand.
- The GORE-TEX section matters to me. Dry feet in fall are worth something.
- The men's and women's shoes sit side by side, so I can find mine fast.
- The buttons are big, so they're easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The sock offer sits in a thin yellow strip at the top, and the hero image and headline overpower it.
- Every shoe is a trail-running model. Nothing here says easy walking, comfort or slip-on.
- No prices anywhere. I can't do my price-versus-quality math.

- 4. Make the buttons say what's behind them, like "Shop GORE-TEX shoes" instead of "Shop the Pack." I'd also send them to the GORE-TEX page, not a general pack page.
- 5. Make the fine print bigger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Cordial-style table layout and has a valid `lang="en"` and a responsive `<style>` block. It has several confirmed defects: no plain-text part, missing alt text on every content image, and a malformed charset declaration. The probe failed all 12 tracked links, but that is most likely scanner blocking and needs a manual check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links failed, but this is probably a scanner artifact.**
  - 11 links returned 406 and the unsubscribe link returned 403, all on `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…`.
  - Every probed link failed, including the `HOKA` logo link to the homepage. A uniform failure across unrelated destinations points to the click-tracker rejecting the probe's user agent or headers, not 12 independent dead links.
  - The wrapped destinations decode cleanly from the `jwtP` payload, for example `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/best-sellers/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…` and `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/new-colors/?…`.
  - Treat this as unverified, not confirmed broken. Re-test with a browser-like user agent or a real client.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 17 links were skipped (social and footer), so those are untested.
- **No `utm_content` is visible in the decoded URLs.**
  - Four hero/banner images (`GritPack_a`, `b`, `c`, `d`) all resolve to the same `/en/us/new-colors/?utm_…` destination.
  - They can't be told apart in analytics beyond the tracker's per-link `/c2/…/<hash>` IDs.
- **Empty image links.** The image links have no alt text, so the anchor text is empty (the QA tool renders it as `[image: …]`). See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 14 content images.** All of these are `images.usw2.cordial.com/1664/…`:
  - `US_20261006_SneakerWeekPromo.jpg`
  - `US_20261008_GritPack_a` through `_f`
  - `US_Stinson8_TopRight_1176670-ARVR.jpg` and `US_Stinson8_TopRight_1176637-RYS.jpg`
  - `US_Speedgoat7GTX_TopLeft_1176677-DMVB.jpg` and `US_Speedgoat7GTX_TopRight_1176674-BKRV.jpg`
  - `US_Challenger8GTX_BottomLeft_1171959-SZL.jpg` and `US_Challenger8GTX_BottomRight_1171958-SPCD.jpg`
  - `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
  - Most of these are the linked images and the full-width hero blocks. With images blocked, the email is blank and the links have no accessible name.
  - Image-only email is a risk. The `.hide` and `.show` rules and the heights (for example `1200x1878`) suggest text is baked into images.
- **Two tracking pixels lack alt text.** They are at `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. Add `alt=""`.
- **The charset meta is malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare an encoding. Use `<meta charset="utf-8">`, or rely on the Content-Type header.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This matters for web-view and screen-reader contexts.
- **Duplicate and conflicting viewport metas.**
  - One has `maximum-scale=1`.
  - The other has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`.
  - `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid CSS declarations in the media query.**
  - `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}`
  - `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`
  - These are empty-value placeholders, ignored by parsers but harmless noise.
- **The `*` reset** (`border:none; padding:0; outline:none`) removes focus outlines. This is a minor accessibility issue for web-view contexts.
- **Remote web fonts.** The `@font-face` rules load from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Most clients ignore them. Whether a fallback stack is set can't be checked because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or subject. The source was truncated, so the body copy wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link:**
  - It is present, with the text `Unsubscribe`, and decodes to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`.
  - The probe returned 403. This is likely the same scanner block as above, but verify manually that it resolves, because a non-working unsubscribe is a CAN-SPAM violation.
- **Plain-text part is missing.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". Multipart/alternative with a text part is expected for deliverability, and its absence is a spam-score factor.
- **Not verifiable from the data provided:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. The From domain is `emails.hoka.com`; check alignment with the envelope sender (`mg.emails.hoka.com` appears in a pixel).
  - The physical postal address, which is likely in the truncated footer.
- **Hashed email in link URLs.** Each destination carries `hmail=<64-hex>` (apparently a SHA-256 of the recipient's address) plus `cordial_id=…`. This passes a persistent recipient identifier to the web property and to any third-party scripts on it. Confirm that this is covered by the privacy policy.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present and consistent across links:**
  - `utm_source=US_Broadcast`
  - `utm_medium=email`
  - `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261008_NewColors_HOKAGritPack_GWP_STO_A90Day_365NewColorsTag_365GWPTag_365TrailPurchasers_AIRFMMedium_AIAOVHigh`
- **Non-standard source value.** `US_Broadcast` is a send type, not a source, so it will fragment source reporting in GA. Use something like `utm_source=cordial`, with the broadcast type in the campaign.
- **Internal segmentation is exposed in the URL.** `utm_campaign` embeds audience and model names (`365TrailPurchasers`, `AIRFMMedium`, `AIAOVHigh`). Any recipient can read how they were segmented.
- **Missing `utm_content` and `utm_term`** (see section 2).
- **Destinations are well-formed** for product links (`/en/us/womens-trail/stinson-8/1176670.html?dwvar_1176670_color=ARVR`, `/en/us/mens-trail/speedgoat-7-gtx/1176674.html?dwvar_…_size=9&dwvar_…_width=Regular`). I could not verify the landing pages' live state, because the probe didn't reach them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-run link QA with a realistic user agent**, or whitelist the QA scanner in Cordial. Confirm manually that the `Unsubscribe` link and one product link resolve.
2. **Add a plain-text part** (currently 0 chars) that includes the unsubscribe URL.
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text to all 14 content images.** Use `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels. Make sure the hero images carry the key offer text in alt or live text.
4. **Fix the head:**
   - Replace the charset meta with `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
   - Fill in `<title>`.
   - Remove the duplicate viewport tag and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Delete the empty-value `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border` rules.
5. **Clean up UTMs:**
   - Replace `US_Broadcast` as the source value.
   - Add `utm_content` per link.
   - Strip audience-segment names from `utm_campaign`.
6. **Review the privacy exposure** of `hmail=` in the destination URLs.
7. **Verify authentication separately**, by pulling the raw message headers for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment and the `List-Unsubscribe` header. This audit had no access to them.
