- The "Fan faves" heading is big and readable on my phone.
- The footer lists free shipping and the "FLY for 30 Guarantee." Those are trust cues for a guy who worries about fit.

## What's weak

- The 40% off is buried at the bottom. The hero shows a running photo with no offer at all.
- The hero is a young runner in the rain. Nothing in it looks like a 62-year-old walking.
- None of the four shoes shows a price or a sale tag, so I can't do my price-vs-quality math. I can't tell which ones are even on sale.
- The "Locate a Store," "Unlock Free Shipping," and "FLY for 30" links in the footer are small, gray-ish text on black and hard to read. The legal fine print is tiny blue-on-blue.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: HOKA "Leaders of the pack"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Cordial-sent broadcast with a consistent UTM scheme and JWT-signed click tracking. Its main technical gaps are 15 images with no alt attribute and a missing plain-text part. The two 403s on tracked links need manual verification, because they may come from the probe rather than the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probed links returned 403.** Both are on the click-tracking wrapper `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6af3be6d.../`:
  - the `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` link (`/983a2a85`)
  - the `Unsubscribe` link (`/31ac505b`)
- **The 403s are probably probe artifacts, not confirmed breakage.** Both fail the same way, on the same wrapper domain and with the same JWT structure (`jwtH`/`jwtP`/`jwtS`). That points to bot filtering or JWT/UA validation at the tracker, not a bad destination. The decoded `linkUrl` values are well-formed:
  - `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_...`
  - `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...`
- **Manual check needed:** click the unsubscribe link from a real client before treating it as healthy.
- **Raw URL as link text.** `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` is shown as the visible text for a link that goes through a tracking wrapper. The text and the real destination don't match, which some filters read as a phishing signal.
- **Coverage is low.** Only 1 of 12 material links is reported as probed, with 11 skipped (the QA message is ambiguous about the exact count). The remaining product and CTA links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **15 images have no `alt` attribute.** 13 are content images, including the hero, headline, subcopy and CTA images (`H_US_CTA_Hero_BestSellers_1.jpg`, `H_US_Headline_Merch_BestSellers_1.jpg`) and the product tiles (`US_Clifton11GTX_TopLeft_1176575-SZD.jpg`).
  - The headline, subcopy and CTA are image-only, so with images blocked or a screen reader the email has no readable message or call to action.
  - The other two are tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`). They should carry `alt=""`.
- **Charset declaration is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it declares nothing.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags with conflicting values.** One has `maximum-scale=1` and the other `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom in some clients, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Invalid CSS declarations in the `<style>` block:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`. Clients ignore these, but they are dead code.
- **Global reset `* {border:none;outline:none;padding:0}`.** `outline:none` removes focus indicators wherever interactive elements are exposed.
- **Web fonts load from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...`** (TheFutureHOKA woff2/woff). Support is partial across clients. The available HTML doesn't show whether a system-font fallback stack is set.
- **Stale asset names may be reused or mis-slotted.** `US_20260711_NewColors_i.jpg` (July) and `H_US_20261103_GTXOrchUpdate_b.jpg` (Nov 3) sit in a Nov 23 "Bestsellers" send. This is a hint only, because the HTML provided is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the excerpt or QA output. The HTML was truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". Without a `text/plain` alternative, spam-filter scoring suffers and plain-text clients get nothing. This may come from how the message was captured through the AgentMail relay, so check the raw MIME source to confirm.
- **Authentication status is unknown.** `Authentication-Results` is absent because of the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC can't be assessed. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed failure. From/Return-Path alignment for `emails.hoka.com` and `mg.emails.hoka.com` needs a check on a direct copy of the message.
- **List-Unsubscribe headers could not be assessed.** They are not in the provided data. Check for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click), which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Unsubscribe link is present** in the footer (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...`). Its working status is unverified, because of the 403 above.
- **Physical address.** The footer was truncated, so no conclusion can be drawn.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters are consistent and present** (decoded from `jwtP`):
  - `utm_source=US_Broadcast`
  - `utm_medium=email`
  - `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261123_PM_STO_Bestsellers_A7Day_90Purchasers_EmailHighEngagers_270BestsellersTag`
- **The campaign string carries segmentation metadata** (`90Purchasers`, `EmailHighEngagers`, `A7Day`). It will appear in analytics and URLs, which exposes audience-segment logic.
- **Unsubscribe URL also carries UTMs**, plus `hmail=<hash>` and `cordial_id`. This is unnecessary on an opt-out link, though harmless.
- **No landing-page alignment issue was verified.** The only link probed for destination was `return-start`, a utility page that doesn't match the Bestsellers theme. It is a footer link, so this is expected. Product and CTA destinations were not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 13 content images. The headline, subcopy and CTA images carry the whole message. Set `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels.
2. Generate a `text/plain` MIME alternative. First confirm in the raw source whether it is truly absent or only dropped by the relay.
3. Manually test the unsubscribe link in a real mail client. If it is fine, add the probe's user-agent or IP to the tracker allowlist so QA stops reporting false 403s.
4. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and one-click headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, from a direct (non-relay) copy of the message.
5. Fix the head markup:
   - change the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`
   - add a real `<title>`
   - merge the two viewport tags and drop `maximum-scale=1`
   - remove the empty `px` CSS declarations
6. Replace the raw-URL link text on the returns link with descriptive text such as "Start a return".
7. Probe the remaining product and CTA links. Coverage is currently about 8% of material links.
