# Become a VIP to shop 30% off first!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the third one today: "Join VIP, get access to 30% off!" and "Save 30% as a VIP (It's free!)" came first, and this is the same pitch with a new subject line. Nobody at DSW seems to be counting. The "30% OFF*" on the pink banner is still big enough to read on my phone without my glasses, and I'll give them that. But I've now seen this banner three times in one day.
- The offer itself isn't bad: 30% off almost everything with code BFDEALS, and a "BECOME A VIP" button I can find easily. But the asterisk is doing a lot of work. I'd have to scroll to the bottom to learn that Skechers, Clarks, TOMS and others are excluded. Skechers is one of the brands I actually shop. A "(Almost) Everything" headline that leaves out my brands isn't honest. The product grid is mostly boots, a loafer and trendy sneakers. I see nothing slip-on, nothing cushioned, and nothing built for a guy my age.
- Would I click? Maybe, but only to check the exclusions. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the sale to show up in the store.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF*" is readable on a phone without zooming.
- The "BFDEALS" code is shown in plain text, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The black "BECOME A VIP" button stands out against the pink.
- "Shop by size" with wide width and wide calf buttons is useful to me, since fit matters a lot for my feet.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and in-store pickup are laid out simply.

## What's weak

- The same VIP pitch has now landed three times today. It feels like spam.
- The fine print excludes Skechers and other brands I shop, and I only find that at the bottom.
- The product grid is fashion-forward: white-soled sneakers, boots, a buckle loafer. There's no comfort, arch support or slip-on message anywhere.
- I have to join VIP before I can use the discount. "Free" still means handing over my information.
- The "Black Friday: Up to 50% off" and "Extra 30% off" tags under the products are tiny and hard to read, and they muddy the main 30% message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the key exclusions up next to the offer, and name them in plain words. Don't make me scroll to the legal text.
- 2. Segment by customer. Show me men's comfort and walking shoes, not trend boots.
- 3. Cut the volume. Send one clear VIP email, not three variations in a day.
- 4. Make the product tags bigger so I can read them on a phone.
- 5. Add a simple price comparison or reviews. I'd like to see why a shoe is worth buying.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email fails 2 of 4 automated checks. A tracked "Opt-In to Texts" CTA returns HTTP 400, and an unresolved `%%gender%%` merge token reached the rendered HTML. Several lower-severity rendering and plain-text issues are also present.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400**. Its URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/xRn7PjUEhcI9H4F8JYb-6g~~/AAQRxRA~/HrsFwWuh…` (about 700 characters of encoded redirect payload).
  - The cause isn't confirmed. A 400 can mean a malformed or expired tracking token, or the SMS-opt-in destination rejecting the probe's request. Verify it manually in a browser.
  - If it's real, it breaks a primary conversion path.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 75 links were probed and 63 were skipped. Footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Plain-text bloat (WARN):** 26,380 of 31,205 characters (about 85%) of the plain-text part are URLs. The long wrapped `ablink.dsw.com` redirect URLs are the cause, and this raises spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/po2BBmebXi2o1bNfTxwHkQ~~/AAQRxRA~/yatpmWOrX6ePPZw…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?…`
  - `o.gif` is almost certainly a tracking pixel, so this is low impact. It should still carry `alt=""`.
  - The `ablink` image needs checking. If it is a visible, linked image, it has no accessible name. If it is also a pixel, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Ineffective meta declarations:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`. The charset declaration is therefore not honored. The `Content-Type` header normally covers this, but the markup is malformed.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Custom fonts:** Ten `@font-face` rules load fonts from `braze-images.com` (Futura_PT_*, HelveticaNow_*). The fallback font stacks fall in the truncated portion of the source, so I can't confirm them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL:** The raw token `%%gender%%` was found unresolved. The truncated source doesn't show where it appears (body copy, URL parameter or hidden field).
  - If it's inside a link, recipients get a literal `%%gender%%` in the destination URL.
  - The syntax doesn't match the Braze Liquid used elsewhere in the template, which points to a leftover from a different platform or template.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a limitation of the AgentMail relay path and not a confirmed defect in the email.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** The QA run always includes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The footer and physical address fall in the truncated source and can't be checked. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided.
- **No confirmed compliance defects.**

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** All links go through the `ablink.dsw.com` redirect wrapper, and the final destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't exposed in the data provided. The only probe result in the data is the 400 on the opt-in CTA, so landing-page alignment can't be confirmed for it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the opt-in CTA.** Manually test the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/xRn7…` link. If it fails for real users, regenerate the link or fix the SMS opt-in destination.
2. **Resolve `%%gender%%`.** Find where it renders, replace it with the correct Liquid tag, and add a fallback value or a pre-send token lint.
3. **Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels.** Give the `ablink` `/q/` image descriptive alt text if it is a visible image.
4. **Correct the meta tags.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
5. **Trim the plain-text part.** Use shorter link wrapping or a leaner text version so URLs aren't about 85% of it.
6. **Check authentication directly.** Review the raw message headers for SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` headers.
7. **Re-run QA with fuller coverage.** Include the footer links and capture the final redirect destinations and UTMs.
