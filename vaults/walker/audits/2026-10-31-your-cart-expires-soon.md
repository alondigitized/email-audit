# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Fourth cart nag in about a week, and I still haven't put a thing in a cart. "Your cart expires soon" is the same line as "Your cart is one click away!" and "Did you forget something?" from earlier today. This time the "cart" is a black sneaker with a blue skull painted on it. That's a Day of the Dead novelty shoe, and I'd never touch it. "You liked these" is wrong, and "and you weren't wrong" is just cute.
- The one useful thing is further down: 20% off for Skechers Plus members, which is a real offer. It's buried below six shoes and a black banner, though. The row of six shoes does have a couple of men's slip-ins I'd look at, the Contour Foam and the Murette slipper. But the lead image, the CTA and the subject all promise a cart that doesn't exist.
- I wouldn't click the big "Complete Your Order" button. I might scroll to the men's slip-ins if I were bored. I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The 20% off for Skechers Plus members is a specific, honest offer with a date (thru 11/2).
- Men's Slip-ins show up in the grid, and the Contour Foam and Murette Scofield Slipper are the kind of shoe I wear.
- Product names say "Arch Fit," "Slip-ins" and "Waterproof," which are the comfort words I look for.
- The nav list (Women / Men / Kids...) is big, plain and easy to tap on a phone.
- The Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup icons are handy trust cues.

## What's weak

- The "cart" shoe is a skull-painted novelty sneaker. It doesn't match anything I browsed, so the "You liked these" claim feels fake.
- "Cart expires soon" implies urgency, but nothing in the email shows an expiry or a date. It's pressure with nothing behind it.
- Three of the six "more great options" are women's shoes. Half the grid isn't for me.
- The best offer, 20% off, sits three screens down. The shoe names are tiny gray text, and the fine print is smaller still.
- This is the same cart nag as 10/29, 10/30 and earlier today, so it's starting to feel like spam.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the member 20% off at the top, since it's the strongest reason to click. Put it above the hero.
- 2. Show an actual cart item or a recently viewed shoe, or drop the cart framing if there isn't one.
- 3. Cap the cart reminders at one a week and stop sending three versions in a day.
- 4. Show men's styles to men, and make the grid follow the shopper's gender and browsing.
- 5. Make the shoe names bigger and add price, so I can do the price-versus-comfort math.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your slip-ins are still here, plus 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% online through 11/2. Arch Fit and Slip-ins included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hands-free comfort, still in your size. Member price ends 11/2.`
