# Reveal this can’t-miss deal 👀
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This one is a mystery-box email: a pink gift box, "Click to Reveal," and no offer anywhere. I've had UGG 30% off three times in two days and TINSEL 20% before that, so I can't tell if this is a bigger deal or the same one wearing a bow. The subject says "can't-miss deal," but I can't find a deal in the email.
- I'm a 62-year-old who wants a cushioned slip-on, not a puzzle. There's no shoe in it, no price, no brand and no percent. I'd probably tap the reveal once out of curiosity. After that I'd be annoyed if it landed on another 20%. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "MAJOR SAVINGS!" headline is big and easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- The DSW logo and the black nav bar (Women, Men, Clearance and so on) make it obviously DSW. I trust that.
- "Click to Reveal" is underlined, so it's clear that's the thing to tap.
- The "Shopping Made Easy" row with free shipping for VIPs and in-store pickup is simple and useful for someone who likes to try shoes on.
- The VIP "Join for free" strip is clear, though I'm hardly the audience for it.

## What's weak

- There is no offer at all. Not a percent, not a dollar amount, not a product. "Can't-miss deal" promises something the email doesn't show.
- The reveal link is small text under a big empty pink box. The box looks like it should be the button, and it doesn't look tappable.
- There's no men's, walking or comfort content anywhere. No Skechers, Hoka, Brooks or New Balance. It's a gift box and nothing about feet.
- After the TINSEL and UGG blitz, another "today only, don't wait" email feels like crying wolf.
- The fine print is a long dark block at the bottom, and none of it applies to anything I can see. It makes the email feel like it's hiding something.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero, like "20% off men's walking shoes today," and drop the mystery. Older shoppers don't like gimmicks.
- 2. Make the gift box itself the tap target and add a real button, such as "Shop Today's Deal."
- 3. Show a few comfort shoes from the brands I cross-shop, with prices.
- 4. Hold back sends when the offer hasn't changed. I've had five emails in two days.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken link, the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA, which returned HTTP 400 when probed. It also has a few minor accessibility and markup issues. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe block and most body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL, HTTP 400):** the link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/p9ZwTZ_bNfC-yulr3WfLyg~~/AAQRxRA~/T0He5Ez3x82...`. This is a Braze click-tracking redirect.
  - The probe reported an empty status description after the 400.
  - A 400 on a tracked redirect usually means the wrapper is malformed or expired, or the destination rejected the request.
  - The probe may also have been rejected as a bot. I can't confirm the cause from the data, so verify manually in a real client.
  - If the link is genuinely broken, it is the email's main SMS-acquisition CTA, so the impact is high.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 34 links were probed and 22 were skipped. Links outside the probed set are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/_Mjq2yZLwzQtu8ID8hvQxQ~~/AAQRxRA~/m-HKowoMS826...` is probably the Braze open-tracking pixel.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?...` is a tracking pixel.
  - Neither is content-bearing, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing. The charset is not declared via this tag. This is a minor issue.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` does not describe the message.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tags (`{{ }}`, `${}`, `%%`) appear in the visible HTML or the QA data. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be verified. The QA notes this is expected via the AgentMail relay, so it is a limitation of the check rather than a confirmed sender fault.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 13,710 of 16,337 characters (about 84%) are URLs, above the 70% threshold. The cause is long Braze `ablink` tracking wrappers. This can raise spam-filter scores and makes the text version hard to read.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, and the QA reported no unsubscribe failure. I can't confirm either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All visible links are wrapped in opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be checked from the source.
- The Movable Ink pixel carries `mi_ecmp=20261111_P261648_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...`, which is a campaign identifier. The date prefix matches today's send.
- Landing-page alignment is unverified because the redirects were not resolved, apart from the failing 400 link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **High:** Manually resolve the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/p9ZwTZ_...`) in a real client. If it is broken, fix the Braze link or the destination before further sends.
2. **Medium:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Medium:** Reduce the URL share of the plain-text part, for example by shortening tracking wrappers or adding descriptive text.
4. **Low:** Add `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels, fix the `http-equiv` attributes on the meta tags, and use a more specific `<title>`.
5. **Process:** Re-run the QA on the full HTML and include raw headers, so the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` header, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM parameters can be verified.
