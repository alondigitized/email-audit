- I looked at this and the first word that came to mind was "golf." Now, I'm a guy who walks for exercise and watches my budget — golf is at least in the neighborhood of something a 62-year-old Boomer male might relate to. - Golf is at least a Boomer-adjacent category — closer than soccer or Care Bears.

## What's weak

- "Reimagined for the Course" is a retro fashion sneaker, not a walking or comfort shoe — the copy never mentions fit, cushioning, or anything that tells me how it feels.
- The Back to School Backpacks section has zero relevance to me whatsoever — feels like they ran out of content and threw it in.

- 3. **Add a price or offer.** No dollar amount, no sale, no reason to act. Even "Under $100" in the hero would anchor the decision.
- 4. **Fix the preview text.** The top of the email is leaking nav links into the inbox. That's wasted real estate.

## Technical Audit — Adidas "Escape into an endless summer"

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken click path will silently dead-end subscribers on at least one CTA; the plain-text MIME part is entirely absent, creating both a deliverability risk and a CAN-SPAM compliance gap. All other issues are medium or low severity.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken click redirect**
The click-redirect at `dv.adidas.com` resolves to a relative path `/g/151efcca-e11b-4ad5-94b5-e8aeea2a7db0` instead of an absolute destination URL. The QA checker surfaces this as `unknown url type: '/g/...'`. The full redirect chain:

```
https://dv.adidas.com/o/151efcca-e11b-4ad5-94b5-e8aeea2a7db0?cp_tp=v3.eJzj...&txn=a01f8580-85f8-11f1-bda9-8632512a7a78
  → resolves to → /g/151efcca-e11b-4ad5-94b5-e8aeea2a7db0  (relative, broken)
```

Any subscriber clicking this CTA lands nowhere. The `txn` value (`a01f8580-85f8-11f1-bda9-8632512a7a78`) identifies the specific send batch — useful for isolating the misconfigured redirect in the ESP.

**[WARN] 23 tracking links not probed**
All click-redirect URLs route through `dv.adidas.com` and were skipped by the HTTP prober. Status of remaining CTAs is unconfirmed.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Two HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources**
Both will be blocked by Gmail, Outlook.com, and most modern clients that enforce mixed-content policies:

- `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` — referenced twice (arrow graphic)
- `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f8b02d9aa-7c0f1969-caea-4a2f-893a-a3420b2f8c70-000000/sV4XxKsqgfSLsaH2kT3D-9hjQ...` — tracking pixel

**[WARN] Two images missing `alt` text**
- The image linked to the broken redirect (`151efcca-e11b-4ad5-94b5-e8aeea2a7db0`) — likely a hero or CTA image; blank alt on a primary CTA breaks image-off rendering
- The tracking pixel (`sV4XxKsqgfSLsaH2kT3D...`) — tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly

**[INFO] Malformed `<meta>` tag**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Without the `http-equiv` attribute, this meta tag is inert. Charset declaration is not applied. Should be:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` declarations**
The same `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` face rules appear in two separate `<style>` blocks. No rendering breakage, but doubles CSS parse cost and signals a template assembly defect.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found in the visible HTML. Subscriber identifiers appear properly resolved in tracking parameters (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`). The truncated source prevents full-body merge token verification — recommend a pre-send seed test to confirm all body tokens render.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text MIME part absent**
QA confirms the text/plain part is 0 characters. CAN-SPAM does not mandate plain text, but its absence is a strong spam-filter signal and several major filtering systems (SpamAssassin, Proofpoint) penalize HTML-only messages. It also breaks delivery to clients configured for text-only mode.

**[INFO] `no-referrer` meta policy**
```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />
```
HTTP `Referer` headers will be suppressed on outbound clicks. UTM parameters still travel in the URL, so GA4/Adobe Analytics campaign attribution is unaffected — but any server-side system relying on the `Referer` header for channel attribution will see no origin.

Authentication headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) are envelope-level and not visible in the HTML source — not evaluated here.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**[FAIL]** The broken redirect (#2 above) means at least one CTA cannot be evaluated for UTM continuity — the destination is unreachable.

**[INFO]** The `cp_tp` parameter on `dv.adidas.com` links contains a base64-encoded payload — this is the ESP's internal campaign-tracking envelope, not standard UTM. Whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to the final destination URL cannot be confirmed without resolving the non-broken redirects manually.
