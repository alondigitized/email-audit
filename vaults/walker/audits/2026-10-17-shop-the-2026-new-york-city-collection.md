## Executive summary

- Well, here's another adidas email, and this one at least has shoes in it. Two of them, and they're real running shoes. The top photo is a couple running past a brick wall. - The text is large and easy to read on my phone. I didn't have to zoom.
- The black "Shop Now" buttons are big and easy to tap with my thumb.
- It's short. There are two products and two buttons, and I'm not scrolling through a maze.

- There's no price and no offer. Compare the 2026-10-15 "up to 50% off" email. That one gave me a reason to look, and this one doesn't.
