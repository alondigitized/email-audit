# SANDALS! Starting at $39.99
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Subject said adidas up to 40% off, but crack it open and it's an adidas swoosh — sorry, stripes — front and center, then straight into flip-flops and strappy sandals modeled by a woman in a red dress, and after that a whole "Sun's Out, Toes Out" wall of women's sandals. I've been sifting DSW emails all week looking for a men's walking shoe or a cushioned slip-on and this one, like the last six, just isn't built for me. The "SANDALS STARTING AT $39.99" banner near the bottom is at least a real number with a real price tag on it, and I do like a plain black mule-heel — for my wife, maybe.
- There's a men's link in the top nav, and eventually a "Sneakers" thumbnail with what looks like a red New Balance-style trainer, but I'd have to hunt for it. Nothing here talks about arch support, cushioning, or comfort — just style copy and "toes out." I'd skim past this one, not click, and definitely not forward it — unless it's to my wife, who might actually want those sandals.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF*" banner up top is big, bold, and easy to read without squinting.
- $39.99 sandals is a concrete price — I know exactly what I'd pay.
- "Shop By Size" grid near the bottom, including wide width and wide calf, is a nice touch — my feet aren't skinny anymore.
- Free shipping for VIPs and in-store pickup icons are clear and I can tap them fine on my phone.

## What's weak

- Every model in the hero and the sandal grid is a woman — nothing here says "this is for a 62-year-old guy."
- Subject promised adidas at 40% off; the email is 90% women's sandals with one small logo up top.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or all-day comfort anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to buy.
- Men's category is buried in a tiny nav link at the very top instead of getting its own section.
- "Everyday Essentials" headline runs sideways in orange text — had to tilt my head to read it, that's just annoying on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a men's comfort section — walking shoes, slip-ons, sneakers — right under the hero, not as an afterthought thumbnail three-quarters down.
- 2. Match the subject line to the actual content: if it's mostly women's sandals, don't lead with "adidas up to 40% off."
- 3. Add one line of comfort language — "cushioned footbed," "all-day support" — near the sneaker or men's thumbnails.
- 4. Fix that sideways orange text block; nobody wants to rotate their phone to read a headline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's sneakers & sandals, up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort you can wear all day — sale ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned sneakers, sandals, and more for him`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop wide widths, comfort styles, up to 40% off`
