# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Here it is again. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" — same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom that's been showing up in my inbox all week, this time with a "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button, like I've got something sitting in a cart. I don't. Never touched this shoe, never scrolled past it long enough to click anything. This is the sixth or seventh time flagging this exact sneaker and it's starting to feel like the algorithm's just stuck.
- The good news is scrolling further down actually gets somewhere useful — the Men's Slip-ins: Max Cushioning Arch Fit 2.0 catches my eye, and the 20% off member banner near the bottom is a real offer with a real date. But it's buried under a hero section that's talking to somebody else entirely.
- I'm not clicking the hero, I'm not forwarding this to anybody, and honestly if this keeps happening I'll mark it as read and move on without scrolling next time.

## What's working

- The Men's Slip-ins: Max Cushioning Arch Fit 2.0 - Azusah Sneaker in the grid actually fits what I look for — cushioned, arch support, slip-on.
- The 20% off Skechers Plus member banner is a concrete, dated offer (thru 9/7) that speaks to me as a member.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup/store finder at the bottom are useful, low-friction touches even if I don't need them today.
- No render bugs — everything loaded clean, text is legible, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- The hero "YOU LIKED THESE" plastered over a shoe I've never liked is now a running joke — this is the same D'Lites - Spring Bloom from at least five prior sends this week.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" implies an actual cart, and I don't have one — that's a false urgency claim, not a friendly nudge.
- The men's product I'd actually want (Max Cushioning Arch Fit 2.0) is buried three rows down in a small grid instead of leading the email.
- Subject line "Your cart expires soon!" is the same lie as the last send — there's no cart to expire.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the "you liked this" personalization for this shoe entirely — if the recommendation engine's this wrong, stop trusting it and lead with a real bestseller or the loyalty offer instead.
- 2. Swap the hero image for the Max Cushioning Arch Fit 2.0 or another men's comfort shoe — I've told this brand what I care about through six emails' worth of non-clicks.
- 3. Move the 20% off member banner up near the top — it's the one thing in this email that's actually true and actionable.
- 4. Stop using "cart" language unless there's a real cart — it erodes trust fast when it's wrong every time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: 20% off ends 9/7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Arch Fit favorites, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers Plus members save 20% on regular-priced styles, thru Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns on men's slip-ins and arch support styles.`
