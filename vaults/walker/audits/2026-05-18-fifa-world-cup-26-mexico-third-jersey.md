**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

It's a full World Cup soccer jersey pitch — a dark green Mexico national team uniform — with poetry about heritage and pride. Beautiful stuff if you're a soccer fan. I'm not. ## Technical Audit — adidas FIFA World Cup 26™ Mexico Third Jersey

### 1. Technical Summary

Two confirmed failures: a malformed relative URL causing a broken click destination, and a missing plain-text multipart body. Six arrow images load over HTTP and will be blocked by Gmail/Outlook with image proxy enforcement enabled.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken relative URL in click wrapper**

The QA checker reports `unknown url type: '/g/7bb8c724-4322-4313-a6b0-355ecaf8658c?txn=5a220a80-52b3-11f1-9bfa-6e9b95041fa3'`. This is a bare relative path being passed as a link href where an absolute URL is required. The associated click-tracked destination resolves to `dv.adidas.com/o/7bb8c724-...` — the `/g/` variant is likely an unresolved ESP template shortlink that was never expanded to a full URL before send.

**[WARN] 29 tracking links not probed**

All click-redirect domains were skipped during HTTP probing. No link rot confirmed, but dead links in this batch cannot be ruled out.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Six HTTP image loads — same asset, repeated**

`http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` appears six times as an arrow/UI element. Gmail's image proxy and Outlook's mixed-content blocking will substitute a broken image icon for any `http://` src. All six references should be `https://`.

**[WARN] Tracking pixel over HTTP**

`http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019e3afece37-.../TdUxv3B2...` — the open-tracking pixel is also HTTP. Same blocking risk as above; open-rate data will be undercounted wherever the proxy enforces HTTPS.

**[WARN] Two images missing `alt` text**

- The hero image served from `dv.adidas.com/o/7bb8c724-...`
- The tracking pixel at `click.comms.adidas.com`

The pixel can use `alt=""` (empty, decorative), but the hero image needs a descriptive alt value for screen readers and image-blocked clients.

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` declarations**

`AdihausDIN` (400 + 700) and `AdineuePRO` (400 + 600) are declared twice across two separate `<style>` blocks with identical `src: local(...)` values. No functional breakage, but it adds unnecessary parse weight and signals a template assembly issue.

**[INFO] `font-display: swap` has no effect in email**

`font-display` is a CSS property for browsers; no email client honors it. Harmless, but it clutters the stylesheet and indicates the CSS was copied from a web context without email-specific review.

**[INFO] `<meta charset>` tag is malformed**

```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```

Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Without that attribute the tag is ignored by all parsers. Should be:

```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text body is empty (0 chars)**

CAN-SPAM does not mandate a text/plain part, but all major ESPs (and RFC 2822 best practice) require a `multipart/alternative` with a non-empty text part. A 0-char text body means recipients on text-only clients or aggressive spam filters receive a blank email. Spam scoring tools (SpamAssassin, Barracuda) penalize missing text parts. This needs a plain-text fallback with at minimum the subject line, a primary CTA URL, and the physical mailing address.

**[OK]** `ROBOTS: NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW` and `referrer: no-referrer` are correctly set.

**[OK]** Sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com` — a dedicated ESP subdomain, consistent with DKIM/SPF alignment on a subdomain envelope.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The primary click destination (`dv.adidas.com/o/7bb8c724-...`) is an adidas ESP redirect with `cp_tp`, `cp_cid`, `mi_cid`, and `mi_mid` parameters present. Internal campaign tracking is wired. No standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible in the decoded payload — if the landing page relies on Google Analytics or any non-adidas analytics stack for attribution, those sessions will arrive untagged. Confirm whether UTMs are injected at the redirect layer or whether this is a known gap.
