- "Bad weather? No such thing." is huge and I can read it without squinting.
- The hero shoe is big and sharp, and the GORE-TEX waterproof pitch is a real benefit for a guy walking in fall rain.
- The six-shoe grid is simple. Each shoe is labeled Women or Men with the name underneath, so I can find the men's Clifton 11 GTX fast.
- The black "Shop GORE-TEX® Styles" button is wide and easy to tap with my thumb. It shows up twice.
- The HOKA logo is up top and the look matches what I'd see on their site, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- There's no price or discount anywhere on the shoes. I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- Nothing says cushioning, arch support or all-day walking. The copy talks about doing "as hard as you do on road or trail," which sounds like a runner.
- The Speedgoat and Speedgoat Hike shoes are rugged trail boots. They push me away, and so does the hiking photo.
- The free shipping offer is at the very bottom, and the text-signup condition is in tiny print. It reads like a catch.
- The footer text is thin and tiny, and I couldn't read the fine print without zooming.
- Only the logo shows what the shoes look like on a foot. There are no older models or everyday walking scenes.

- 1. Lead with the Clifton 11 GTX as the one hero shoe, and say "cushioned, waterproof walking comfort." Cut the trail shoes from a send that goes to a walking customer.
- 2. Put the free shipping offer near the top in plain words. Drop the text-signup condition, or spell it out clearly.
- 3. Add a price under each shoe, plus one short line like "Cushioned for all-day walks."
- 4. Add a few star ratings or a short review. That tells me it's comfortable for people like me.
- 5. Show a photo of someone my age walking in the rain, not climbing waterfalls.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The send has no plain-text part (0 chars) and no alt text on any image, and the head has several markup defects. All 12 probed click-tracking links returned 403 or 406, but this looks like the QA probe being blocked, not broken links (see §2). Tracking and UTM structure are consistent.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 links returned 406 and the Unsubscribe link returned 403.** All sit on the Cordial tracking domain `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`. Every failure is on one host and shows the same status pattern, so the likely cause is bot filtering of the probe's user agent or headers. These are not necessarily dead links.
  - The "26% pass rate" is therefore probably misleading. The data can't confirm whether real recipients reach the destinations.
  - The JWT payloads decode to valid `linkUrl` targets such as `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/hoka-x-gore-tex/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…`, so the wrapped URLs are well-formed.
  - Re-probe with a browser-like UA, or manually click-test the hero link and the unsubscribe link, before treating these as defects.
- **Unsubscribe 403 needs priority verification.** The target is `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast…`. A real failure here would be a compliance problem, not only a UX one.
- **Link text is blank or an image filename.** The first "HOKA" link has no resolvable text. Image links report text like `[image: US_20261015_GoreTex_a.jpg]` because there is no alt text (see §3).
- **Tracking pixels are present.** Two have no alt: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions. Otherwise this is low severity.
- **Two tracking hostnames are in use**, `e.emails.hoka.com` and `mg.emails.hoka.com`. Confirm both are covered by the authenticated sending domain's DNS and tracking-domain configuration.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **16 images have no alt attribute.** These include the hero and banner images `US_20261015_GoreTex_a` through `_e`, the six product images (Clifton 11 GTX, Speedgoat 7 GTX, Speedgoat 7 Hike GTX), `US_Q3_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`, and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - Most are wrapped in links, so those links have no accessible name. With images blocked, the email renders as empty boxes.
  - The product tiles are the only product identifiers, so they carry the most risk.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **The charset meta is malformed.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it doesn't declare an encoding. The encoding then depends on the MIME `Content-Type` header.
- **There are two viewport meta tags with conflicting values.**
  - One is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`.
  - The other is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`.
  - `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom on clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. Use one tag without `maximum-scale`.
- **`* { outline:none; … }` removes focus indicators**, which hurts keyboard and assistive-tech users in clients that allow focus.
- **Invalid CSS declarations:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values. They are ignored, but they suggest unrendered template variables in the CSS.
- **Custom fonts load from `hoka.com`.** `TheFutureHOKA` and `TheFutureHOKAMono` come from `https://www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…` with no fallback list visible in the truncated source. Most clients ignore web fonts, so confirm a web-safe fallback stack exists.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- Each wrapped URL carries a recipient identifier: `hmail=9153f33fe5cdf59baf088bb835d3b39ef90f188bd8540a4faba92899227ced00` and `cordial_id=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9`. These look like a hashed email and a contact ID, and they are populated, not blank.

- **Authentication could not be assessed.** `Authentication-Results` is absent because of the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. This is a QA-environment limit, not evidence of a defect.
- **An unsubscribe link exists in the body** (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`), but the probe returned 403 (see §2).
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are unverifiable.** No header data was provided. Check the raw message for RFC 8058 one-click support, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are consistent across the decoded links:** `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261015_GoreTex_STO_A60Day_270GTXTag_720GTXPurchasers_720HikePurchasers_AIAOVHigh`.
- **There is no `utm_content` or `utm_term`.** Hero, product tiles, returns link and logo can't be told apart in analytics. The `dwvar_<sku>_color=` parameter identifies the product only.
- **`utm_source=US_Broadcast` describes the send type, not a source.** This is a convention issue, not a defect.
- **The campaign string exposes audience-segmentation logic** (`720GTXPurchasers_720HikePurchasers_AIAOVHigh`) to anyone who reads the URL. The same applies to the unsubscribe URL, which carries the full UTM set.
- **The product link paths appear to match the image filenames:**
  - Clifton 11 GTX: `/en/us/womens-everyday-running-gear/clifton-11-gtx/1176575.html?dwvar_1176575_color=SZD`.
  - Speedgoat 7 GTX: `/en/us/womens-trail/speedgoat-7-gtx/1176677.html` and `/en/us/mens-trail/speedgoat-7-gtx/1176674.html`.
  - Speedgoat 7 Hike GTX: `/en/us/women-hiking/…/1176671.html` and `/en/us/mens-trail-hiking-shoes/…/1176638.html`.
  - One filename (`US_Clifton11GTX_TopRight_176574-VYBR`) is missing a leading "1" against SKU 1176574. This is cosmetic, but it suggests a manual naming step.
- **Landing-page availability can't be confirmed** because the probe was blocked.

1. **Re-probe with a browser UA, or click-test manually.** Make the unsubscribe link, the hero link (`/c2/…/b92982f6`) and the PDP links the priority. Treat the 406/403 results as unconfirmed until then.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to all 14 content and logo images.** Use `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels.
3. **Generate a plain-text part** from the HTML (Cordial supports multipart).
4. **Clean the head:**
   - Change the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />`.
   - Remove the duplicate viewport tag and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Populate `<title>`.
   - Remove or fix the `px`-only CSS rules.
   - Restore focus outlines for links.
5. **Verify raw headers** for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `emails.hoka.com`.
6. **Add `utm_content` per link or module.** Consider moving segment names out of the visible `utm_campaign` string.
