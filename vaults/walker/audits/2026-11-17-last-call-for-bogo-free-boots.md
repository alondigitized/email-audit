## What's working

- "THESE DEALS END TODAY" is big, red and plain. I know right away it's a one-day thing.
- The $10 off $74.98 / $25 off $149.98 coupon with code HOLIDAY is easy to read.
- The Skechers Hands Free Slip-ins tile is there, and the "$59.98" is large enough to read on my phone.
- The "Add $10 / Add $25" mobile wallet buttons and barcodes mean I could use the coupon in store without hunting for the code.
- The text is big and the sections are clearly separated, so I don't have to squint.

## What's weak

- The BOGO FREE hero is women's boots. Nothing at the top is aimed at a man my age.
- Four sales sit above the Slip-ins tile, so it sits near the bottom of a very long scroll.
- The Slip-ins tile shares a row with clogs, so the comfort shoe I want doesn't get its own spot.
- The subject's "LAST CALL‼️" in caps with double exclamation marks feels shouty. Under the same banner the email says "THESE DEALS END TODAY." The words differ but the pressure is the same.
- The fine print for BOGO and the coupon exclusions is tiny at the bottom. The "some exclusions apply" note is small, and I have to scroll down to learn the details.
- The email stacks the same HOLIDAY coupon, another Rudolph-themed holiday banner and another sale list. It's the same email I've seen all week.

- 2. Skip the repeat. If I got the HOLIDAY coupon three times this week, send me something new, or send less often.
- 3. Make the deadline say when. "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET" is more credible than "END TODAY," which I've now seen several times.
- 4. Put the BOGO terms near the hero, not in the footer. "Buy one, get one of equal or lesser value" matters before I click.
- 5. Segment by what I've bought or clicked. A man who opens men's comfort emails shouldn't get women's boots as the headline.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has no hard failures (QA: 86% pass, 0 issues, 1 warning). It does have two defects in the tracking pixels: an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` token and a malformed `t=` parameter. Authentication status and the footer/unsubscribe markup can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 30 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains). Destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261117_bogof_clogs_AM​`
  - A UTM string is embedded as the value of `t=`, so it is nested rather than a real query parameter.
  - The URL ends with an invisible zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. This can corrupt the campaign value when parsed.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the campaign token says `bogof_clogs`, but the subject line says "BOGO FREE Boots". This is a possible mislabel that would skew reporting. I can't confirm it from the source shown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - `.../m/1/fe4e293c-bccf-488a-bbf7-db06bf5745de.jpg`
  
  If these carry content, screen readers and images-off clients get nothing.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (2):** `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` and `0di86k1v.emltrk.com`. Low severity. These should carry `alt=""` so they are treated as decorative.
