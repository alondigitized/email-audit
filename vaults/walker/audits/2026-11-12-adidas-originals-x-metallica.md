# adidas Originals x Metallica
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- Another adidas email, and I've stopped counting them since the 15th. This one is a Metallica band tee. It has no shoe, no price and no deal. I'm 62 and I shop for cushion and arch support. A rock T-shirt from a tour collaboration doesn't speak to me.
- The black banner at the top says "Explore the latest adidas best sellers," and that's the most generic line in the whole email. Then comes a big paragraph about the "Trefoil" and a "Life Burns Fast residency." That reads like a press release. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on. Even if I liked Metallica, nothing here tells me what it costs.

## What's working

- The tee photo is crisp, and the red Metallica logo pops against the dark stage gear.
- The "Shop Now" button is a big black rectangle that's easy to tap with my thumb.
- The body text is a readable size without zooming.
- The layout is simple and uncluttered, with one hero image and one button.

## What's weak

- There's no shoe anywhere, which is the only thing I come to adidas for.
- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline, so I have no reason to act now.
- The paragraph about the Trefoil and the residency is marketing language that means nothing to me.
- There are two "Shop Now" buttons, one in the banner and one at the bottom, and I can't tell where either goes.
- The banner above the hero says "best sellers," which has nothing to do with the Metallica collection below it.
- The picture is a young rock-fan tee, not something aimed at a guy my age.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a comfortable walking shoe up top, or at least state a price or offer. Right now there's no reason to click.
- 2. Cut the paragraph down to a line or two, and say what's in the collection and what it costs.
- 3. Name the CTA, for example "Shop Metallica tees," and drop the generic banner so there's one clear button.
- 4. Send this only to people who have bought Originals or band-style apparel. Don't send it to comfort shoppers like me.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a missing plain-text part (0 chars, QA FAIL) and several non-HTTPS image sources, including the tracking image. The HTML is truncated, so body-level checks (unsubscribe link, UTMs, merge tokens) can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- Two images are served over plain `http://`:
  - `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` (the "arrow" image, flagged twice).

- Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or rewritten by clients, and they cause mixed-content warnings in webmail. A blocked tracking pixel also undercounts opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on two images:
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/65b3f1dc-b0d4-45c5-8a1a-a70d1fcdd7fc?cp_tp=…`
  - The `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…` image. This is likely the tracking pixel, which should carry `alt=""`. The `dv.adidas.com` image's role is unknown.
- The first meta tag is `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`, with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. It is therefore not a valid charset declaration. The charset has to come from the MIME `Content-Type` header, and the HTML-level fallback is ineffective.
- Custom fonts (`AdihausDIN`, `AdineuePRO`) are declared with `local('Arial'|'Helvetica'…)` sources and `mso-font-alt: Arial`. Fallback is handled, so this is not an issue.
- Alt text on the "arrow" image was present, so it passes.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. Unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) weren't flagged by QA, though the HTML is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback:** QA FAIL, with a text version of 0 chars. There is no `text/plain` part, which hurts spam scoring and accessibility for text-only clients. It also removes the plain-text fallback some filters expect.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, and this is a pipeline limitation rather than a confirmed sender fault.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers:** not visible in the truncated source or QA output, so they can't be verified. Use the full HTML and raw headers to confirm.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is present. It is harmless, but it can strip referrer data on click-throughs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page URLs can't be assessed. All 13 links are wrapped in click-redirect domains, and the probe skipped them.
- The redirect destinations need to be resolved to check UTM presence and landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` MIME alternative. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Serve `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com` assets over HTTPS. Update the `src` of the "arrow" image and the tracking pixel.
3. Add `alt` to the `dv.adidas.com/o/65b3f1dc…` image (descriptive if it is content) and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Replace `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` with `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`, and confirm the MIME header declares the same charset.
