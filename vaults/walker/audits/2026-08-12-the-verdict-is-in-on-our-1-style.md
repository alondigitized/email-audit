The "40% off select styles" banner up top grabbed me right away, and then the "Men's" section further down with a customer quote about wide feet and high arches — "It is so difficult to find shoes... these fit perfectly" — that's basically me talking.
- That said, I'm scrolling through a lot of clutter to get there. - Men's section has a real customer quote about wide feet and high arch, which is exactly my situation
- "Shop Wide Fit Arcade" button right up top under the hero — wide fit is a real thing I look for

## What's weak

- Women's gets the bigger, first billing with a five-star quote block before men's shows up at all
- A lot of dead scroll after the offers — "more casual favorites," apparel, then six plain text nav links (Women/Men/Kids/Clothing/New Arrivals/Sale) that feel like filler
