## What's weak

- Every hero product shot is a women's heel or strappy sandal — not one slip-on, sneaker, or men's casual shoe in sight.

- This is the same BOGO offer and same "not-for-me" grid I've seen daily all week from this brand.

- 5. Lead with a comfort/cushioning callout somewhere in the body — one line about arch support or all-day comfort would actually get my attention.
