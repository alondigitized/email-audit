# Today Only! Extra 10% off for Members
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- I'm going to be straight with you: I almost didn't open this one. After yesterday's "Going, going... shop before they're gone" and the day before that with the 25% off members pitch, my thumb was already hovering over the trash icon. But "Today Only" and "Extra 10% off" in the subject line — fine, Skechers, you got me one more time.
- Inside, the deal is real: stack a member-exclusive extra 10% on top of their 25% off $100+ offer. That's not nothing. But here's the thing that slowed me down — buried beneath that SHOP NOW button in small print: "Today only. Applicable on App." I have to download or open their app to get the full deal? I don't love that. I'm browsing on my phone in a normal browser, and now you're telling me the advertised price requires a detour.
- The other thing that got me was that the biggest shoe photo at the top — nice tan sneaker — is sitting under a SHOP WOMEN'S button. I'm a 62-year-old man. Put the men's shoe first. I almost scrolled past before I found my section. I'd mark this read and maybe circle back, but I wouldn't forward it to a friend — there's nothing here that screams "you need to know about this right now."

## What's working

- **Dual-offer stacking is clearly laid out** — 10% + 25% off $100, both codes (SAVE10 and SAVINGS) visible without hunting for them
- **"SKECHERS PLUS MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" banner** — I'm a member, so that felt like it was talking to me specifically
- **"Today Only" urgency is credible** — not a 5-day countdown clock, just a clean deadline that I actually believe
- **SHOP NOW button is prominent** — teal, centered, impossible to miss
- **No layout breakage** — text is readable, images loaded, nothing overlapping or broken

## What's weak

- **Women's shoe leads, men's is an afterthought** — the hero image is a tan women's sneaker; my section is buried further down
- **"Applicable on App" is a quiet gotcha** — that fine print below the CTA is the kind of thing I'd only notice after I click and get confused at checkout
- **No comfort language anywhere** — zero mention of cushioning, arch support, slip-ins, or walking comfort; this email could be selling dress shoes for all I know
- **Fourth day in a row of overlapping offers** — I've had this same member discount in my inbox since July 22nd; the freshness is gone
- **Preview text is broken JSON garbage** — before I even opened, the preview showed raw code. That's not a good look.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the men's shoe first** — if you know I'm a male member, the hero shoe should match. Not a small thing for a guy who's about to click "nope."
- 2. **Add one comfort cue to the hero** — even just "Hands Free Slip-ins included" under the offer stack would triple my interest. You know I click on those.
- 3. **Fix the app-only restriction language** — either make it bigger and honest ("Best price in-app only") or rethink whether app-exclusivity is worth the drop-off; don't hide it
- 4. **Rest the 25% off $100 for a day** — three days running, it's starting to feel like the price nobody actually pays
- 5. **Fix the preheader** — whatever is injecting JSON schema into the preview text needs to be killed immediately; it's a broken first impression in every inbox
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: Today Only, Stack 10% Extra on Top`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Member Price Just Got Better — Today Only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 10% stacks on 25% off — use both codes at checkout today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two codes, one day. Members save more today than any other day this week.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Today Only! Extra 10% off for Members" (2026-07-24)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders with a structurally sound table layout and correct responsive media queries, but has a systemic HTTP image-serving issue across all assets from `image.emails.skechers.com`, one broken tracking pixel with a missing URL protocol, and a malformed `<meta charset>` tag. No unrendered merge tokens detected in the visible source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Broken Return Path pixel — missing protocol**
```html
<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499" />
```
`src` has no `http://` or `https://` prefix. Email clients will treat this as a relative URL and the request will silently fail. Inbox monitoring data from Return Path will be missing for this send.

**26 click links not auditable**
All CTAs route through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect URLs. The QA probe correctly skipped these; destination URLs and UTM pass-through cannot be verified from the source alone (see §6).

**Third-party DMP beacons present**
Three Krux (`beacon.krxd.net`) pixels fire on open — one for user match (SHA-256 hashed UID) and one ad impression. These are expected for Salesforce Marketing Cloud + Audience Studio but represent PII-adjacent data sharing to a third party on message open.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Systemic HTTP image serving — 12 affected assets**
All images from `image.emails.skechers.com` are served over plain HTTP. Modern clients (Gmail, iOS Mail, Outlook.com) proxy or block mixed-content HTTP resources. Affected assets include:
- Skechers logo: `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png`
- App Store badge, Google Play badge
- Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find A Store icons
- Instagram, Facebook, YouTube social icons

This domain should be serving over HTTPS; all `src` values need the scheme updated to `https://`.

**ink1000 tracking pixel also HTTP**
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif
```
Same issue — will be blocked in security-conscious clients.

**Missing alt text — 2 images**
- `o.gif` (ink1000 open-tracking pixel) — `alt=""` is correct for tracking pixels; the QA warn is a false positive here since it's intentionally invisible.
- `49468f73-4651-4af3-bae2-61d1ae5db486.png` — purpose unknown from the truncated source but should carry descriptive alt text if it is a content image.

**Malformed charset meta tag**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
Missing required `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute. Should be:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
Without it, some Outlook and legacy clients may fall back to a default encoding.

**CSS display-toggle conflict**
`.mobile-content { display: block }` / `.desktop-content { display: none }` are set unconditionally in `<style>`, then toggled via `@media (min-width: 768px)`. Clients that strip `<style>` blocks (some Outlook versions) will show only mobile layout regardless of viewport.

**Sending domain:** `emails.skechers.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain — standard practice. DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment should be confirmed at the DNS level but is outside the scope of the HTML audit.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 26 CTAs pass through `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect URLs, which is the SFMC click-tracking layer. UTM parameters are typically appended by SFMC at redirect time, not embedded in the raw `href`. Without being able to follow the redirects (blocked by QA probe), UTM pass-through to `skechers.com` landing pages is unverified. Recommend spot-checking one live link post-send to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are present on the destination URL.

Campaign identifier `PRO_US_NONPUR_U_SPENDMIN_LOYL_EN_07242026` is consistent across all tracking pixels, which is correct.
