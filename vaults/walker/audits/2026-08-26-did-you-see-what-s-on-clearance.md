# Did you see what's on clearance???
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Same clearance email DSW's been sending me most of this week — I audited a nearly identical "up to 60% off" send on the 24th and again on the 25th, and this is basically that one with the shoe grid reshuffled. "UP TO 60% OFF CLEARANCE" in big bold letters, size grid right up top from 5 to 11 — that part I like, saves me the trouble of clicking in only to find out they don't stock my size. But the actual product grid is the same story every time: twelve pairs, and I count maybe two or three men's sneakers in there. Rest is women's heels, sandals, a leopard print bootie. That's not my clearance rack.
- I'd scroll past this one same as the last three. Not deleting it, not forwarding it to anybody — just marking it read and moving on. If DSW wants me to actually click, they need to stop sending me the women's aisle with a couple of sneakers sprinkled in for cover.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off" is a real number, not the usual "$10 off your purchase" coupon nonsense they've been running — that's honest and it caught my eye.
- Size grid (5–11) sitting right under the hero is something I actually use before I bother clicking further.
- "Clearance in stores is always 30% off" plus a "Find Your Store" button — I like knowing I can just walk in, I don't love ordering shoes online I haven't tried on.
- Clean layout, nothing broken or overlapping that I can see.

## What's weak

- Grid's mostly women's shoes again — heels, strappy sandals, a leopard bootie. Same imbalance as the last two clearance emails I looked at.
- "No, you're not dreaming... these are ALLLLL on clearance" — that stretched-out "ALLLLL" reads like it's trying too hard, feels like it's talking to somebody younger than me.
- Third clearance email with the same "up to 60%" headline this week — starts to feel like the same coupon just repackaged, not an actual new drop.
- Nothing points me to men's comfort or walking shoes specifically — no arch support callout, no cushioned sole mention, nothing that tells me this applies to my category at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the product grid with men's comfort/walking styles for subscribers tagged male, or at minimum balance it 50/50 instead of burying two sneakers among ten women's items.
- 2. Add a men's-specific CTA — "Shop Men's Clearance" — right next to the general "Shop All Clearance" button, not just as a small text link.
- 3. Drop the "no, you're not dreaming" line for something plainer — it's trying to sound clever and it just sounds like it's not for me.
- 4. Vary the headline from the last two sends — three "up to 60% off clearance" emails in four days makes the deal feel less special each time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's clearance: comfort sneakers up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size is in — clearance up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns in men's walking styles, sizes 8–13`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned comfort, clearance prices — shop before sizes go`
