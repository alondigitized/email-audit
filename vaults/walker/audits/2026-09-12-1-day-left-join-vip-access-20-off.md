# [1 Day Left] Join VIP -> Access 20% off
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW email I've been getting all week, just with a new subject line slapped on — "1 Day Left" this time instead of "VIPs only" or "Don't miss." I open it and it's the identical playbook: big blue "20% OFF" banner up top, then "BRAND NEW...AND SO YOU" followed by fifteen shoes in a grid. I count maybe two pairs in there I'd even glance at — some dark low-top sneakers and a pair of brown sneakers — everything else is heeled booties, ballet flats, leopard mules, wedge sneakers, and a yellow dress boot. That's not "so me," that's so not me.
- The "SHOP MEN" link sits up top in the nav, which tells me they at least have men's inventory — they just never bother showing it to me. If they'd swap even half that grid for men's walking shoes or slip-ons, I'd stop and look. As it stands, I'll skim for ten seconds and mark it read.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm sure not forwarding this to anybody — there's nothing here for me to pass along.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" banner is big, bold, and impossible to miss — that part always lands.
- "SHOP MEN" is right there in the top nav if I want to go find something myself.
- Shoe size chart later in the email is clear and easy to read on a phone.
- Free shipping and Afterpay mentioned near the bottom, which is a nice touch even if I have to scroll a mile to find it.

## What's weak

- Grid is almost entirely women's shoes again — booties, ballet flats, leopard-print mules, a yellow dress boot. Same story as the last five DSW emails this week.
- No men's comfort brands mentioned by name — no Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance callout, nothing that'd make me stop scrolling.
- Email is long — I had to scroll through fifteen product photos, a "shop by size" block, and a benefits row before hitting any real content for someone like me.
- "1 Day Left" urgency feels manufactured since I've seen "20% off" from them nearly every day this week — doesn't feel credible anymore.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if DSW knows I've never clicked a women's boot in five emails, swap that whole grid for men's comfort sneakers and slip-ons. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Lead the hero section with a men's shoe, not fifteen photos before I see anything relevant.
- 3. Name a comfort brand in the subject or hero — "Skechers, Hoka, New Balance — 20% off" would actually get my attention.
- 4. Ease off the fake urgency — I've seen this same "X days left" clock reset over and over; it's starting to read as noise, not a deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort sneakers — 20% off ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 20% off Skechers, Hoka & more for VIPs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ons, walking shoes, and arch support picks — all 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort brands, 20% off through tomorrow`
