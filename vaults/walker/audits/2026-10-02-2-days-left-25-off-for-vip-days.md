# 2 days left: 25% off for VIP Days
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this is the sixth email I've had from them in about two days. The purple "25% OFF*" banner is the same one I saw on 10/01 in "Happy VIP Days" and "Ooooh you get 25% off...", so nothing here is news. The only new part is the "2 days left" deadline, and that doesn't show up anywhere in the body I can see. The code VIPFUN is in small print under the number, and I'd have to hunt for it.
- The rest is a fashion spread: a girl in a Dodgers cap, ballet sneakers, animal print, "Y2K retro sneakers, or as we prefer to call it: millennial core." That isn't aimed at me. The New Balance retro shoe and the $64.99 Adidas-style court sneaker are the only things close to my speed. I'd want the Men's link up top, and nothing here talks about cushioning or arch support.
- I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF*" in huge white type on purple is easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- The Women / Men / Kids shop links sit right under the offer, so I can get to Men quickly.
- The New Balance retro sneaker is a brand I already cross-shop, and the $64.99 price tag on the court sneaker is the one concrete price in the email.
- The "Shop by Size" grid has big numbers that are easy to tap. Wide width and wide calf buttons are a good touch for real feet.
- The footer has free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and text signup, and all of it is easy to find.

## What's weak

- The "2 days left" from the subject never appears in the body. The email I opened doesn't match what the inbox promised, and that cuts my trust.
- The asterisk and the "regular-price styles only" line are right in the banner, and the real exclusions are in tiny gray text at the bottom. Skechers is on the exclusion list, and I wouldn't see that until I'd scrolled the whole thing.
- The imagery is young and trendy, with Y2K, "millennial core" and ballet flats. Nothing says comfort, cushioning, slip-on or arch support.
- Compared with 10/01, this is the same banner with a new coat of paint. I've now seen it five or six times, so the urgency feels manufactured.
- The VIP join block shows up twice, and the "Shop Athletic & Sneakers" and "Shop All Women" buttons are small next to the big banner.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline and the code in the banner itself: "Ends Sunday · code VIPFUN". The "2 days left" has to be in the email if it's in the subject.
- 2. Give men like me a comfort section: cushioned walking and slip-on shoes from Skechers, Hoka and Brooks, with their prices. Skip the millennial-core copy.
- 3. Stop sending the same banner every day. Combine the sends, or change the creative when you do send.
- 4. Move the exclusions up near the offer, in plain words, instead of burying them at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `VIP Days ends Sunday: 25% off, code VIPFUN`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last 2 days: 25% off comfort walking shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ballet sneakers, retro New Balance and court styles, plus free VIP shipping.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code VIPFUN at checkout through Sunday. Wide widths in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken link, the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned HTTP 400 in the automated probe. It also has a few minor markup and accessibility defects. The HTML source was truncated and only 12 of 66 links were probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (HTTP 400):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/5_VO85dPQQj1QEu2yIQc_Q~~/AAQRxRA~/KM86P4x7…`. The URL is a long (~900+ chars) tracked redirect token, and the visible link text is empty in the QA output.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking domain usually means the token is malformed or truncated, or the redirect rejects the probe's request. The QA data can't tell these apart.
  - Treat it as a real defect until someone confirms in a browser or mobile client that the link resolves.
- **Link coverage:** 54 of 66 links were skipped, mostly footer, utility and social links. Their health is unverified.
- **Plain-text weight:** `20945/25267` characters (83%) of the plain-text part are URLs. This is a consequence of wrapping every link in an `ablink.dsw.com` token. It is a spam-filter risk, not a functional break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - The tracked image `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/mpqgduCdHAPiRIyZ-1jAlw~~/…` has no alt. If it is a content image, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
  - The tracking pixel `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261002_P261394_V1_W-_Offer_Trend` has no alt. For a pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Ineffective head meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither does anything. The charset should be declared with `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Most clients fall back to the HTTP/MIME charset, so the impact is low.
- **Not verifiable:** the body markup (table roles, `lang` attribute, heading structure) was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or re-issue the SMS opt-in link** (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/5_VO85…`). Test it in a real browser and mobile client, and confirm the token isn't truncated or expired. This is the only confirmed functional defect and it is the 15% offer CTA.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel. Add descriptive alt to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image if it is visible content.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Correct the head meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
