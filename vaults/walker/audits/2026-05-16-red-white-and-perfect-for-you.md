# Red, White, and Perfect for You
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Seven emails in seven days from Skechers, and here we go again — Memorial Day patriotic push. I'll give them credit for the seasonal timing; I do need a new pair before the summer walking kicks in and a BOGO deal is hard to ignore. But I opened this email and the first thing I saw wasn't a shoe for me — it was a woman in a navy dress waving a flag. "Stars, Stripes & Style." Style. That word doesn't move me. Give me "Stars, Stripes & Arch Support" and now you're speaking my language.
- The mechanics here are solid. The offer is right at the top — BOGO 50% off, or 25% off a single pair — and I understood it in about two seconds, same as the May 14th send. They know how to put a deal front and center. There's a Men's section lower down with what looks like a nice navy casual sneaker, and that "Celebrate the long weekend in comfort" tagline almost redeemed the hero copy. Almost.
- The broken preview text in my inbox is becoming a pattern — same garbage JSON code I saw on May 15th. At this point I wonder if anyone's checking these before they go out. I'd click the Men's section if I was in a buying mood, but I wouldn't forward this to a buddy. I'd just scroll to the men's shoe and move on.

## What's working

- **BOGO front and center** — buy one, get one 50% off banner lands before the hero image; I see the deal before anything else.
- **"Celebrate the long weekend in comfort"** — that sub-tagline slipped in the word I actually care about; credit where it's due.
- **Dedicated "Shop Men's" CTA** — they didn't bury it; the Men's section has its own button and its own shoe image.
- **Seasonal timing** — Memorial Day framing is relevant and timely; I'm thinking about summer footwear right now.
- **Clean layout in the body** — no broken images, no overlapping text, visually organized with clear section breaks.

## What's weak

- **Female-first hero, again** — main model is a woman in a dress; I have to scroll past Women's entirely before I see anything for me.
- **"Style" is not my word** — "Stars, Stripes & Style" is fashion-brand language; I shop for fit, cushion, and support, not style cred.
- **No comfort specs anywhere** — Memory Foam, arch support, slip-resistant sole? Not a word. I can't tell if that navy men's shoe has any of the things that actually matter to me.
- **Broken preview text, again** — schema.org JSON code leaking into the inbox preview; this is the second send in two days with this bug and it makes the brand look careless.
- **Seventh email in seven days** — I'm not annoyed enough to unsubscribe, but the cadence is getting up there.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text immediately** — this is a technical bug that's been present since at least May 15th; a real preheader that previews the BOGO offer would lift open rate measurably.
- 2. **Open with a men's shoe hero or a mixed-gender visual** — Walker-aged men aren't going to self-insert into a woman in a flag dress; lead with the navy casual or show a couple walking, not posing.
- 3. **Swap "Style" for "Comfort" in the hero** — "Stars, Stripes & Comfort" or "Stars, Stripes & Step Easy" keeps the patriotic theme without alienating comfort-first shoppers.
- 4. **Add one comfort callout under the men's shoe** — a single line like "Memory Foam cushioning | Wide widths available" would make me click Men's with intent instead of curiosity.
- 5. **Add a specific expiration date** — "Through Monday, May 25" is three words and turns "long weekend" into real urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% Off — Comfort Styles for the Long Weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day Sale: Walk Into Summer for Less`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one, get one 50% off. Or take 25% off a single pair — through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Memorial Day comfort starts here — men's, women's, and apparel all on sale.`

## Technical Audit — Skechers "Red, White, and Perfect for You" (2026-05-16)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken experience (empty personalization token in greeting) and a systemic HTTP image-serving issue affecting 11+ assets. No link failures were confirmed by probe, but all 27 tracking links were unresolvable via HTTP check.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No issues found** with link destinations (all 27 click-redirect URLs passed format validation; HTTP probe was correctly skipped for tracking domains — expected behavior).

**Minor observation:** The open-pixel at `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?MGDMDJOU5PBENFCWYN263YKVIQ...` uses Salesforce Marketing Cloud's standard open tracking. No anomaly, but worth noting two third-party sync beacons also fire on open:

- `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` — Salesforce/Krux DMP sync, twice (once SHA-256 hashed UID, once raw subscriber ID `121173391`)
- `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_ad_impression.gif` — ad impression pixel

These are standard but represent three distinct third-party pings on open. No action required unless privacy policy doesn't disclose third-party pixel tracking.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources — affects 11 images.**

All images served from `image.emails.skechers.com` use `http://` not `https://`. Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook 2016+, Apple Mail) proxy or block non-HTTPS image loads. Affected assets:

```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png  (Skechers logo)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-...png  (no alt text)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-...png  (App Store badge)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/9bb508d0-...png  (Google Play badge)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/00100b23-...png  (Curbside Pickup)

http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif                                (secondary open pixel)
+ social icons (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube)
```

**Missing alt text — 2 images:**

- `o.gif` (ink1000 pixel): tracking pixel, `alt=""` is acceptable here
- `49468f73-...png`: decorative or content image with no alt — needs `alt=""` (if decorative) or descriptive text

**`<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` is missing `http-equiv`** attribute: should be `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />`. Some older clients may not apply the charset declaration.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** disables font scaling globally. This prevents visually impaired users from enlarging text on iOS. Should be `100%` not `none`.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**FAIL — Empty greeting slot (confirmed broken experience).**

QA detected `"Hi ,"` — a name merge token that resolved to an empty string. The subscriber record either lacks a first name value or the fallback default was not configured. Evidence from QA:

```
[FAIL] Empty greeting slot: Greeting has empty name field (e.g. "Hi ,")
```

This ships to any subscriber without a populated first name field. Requires either:
- A non-empty fallback (e.g., `%%=IIF(EMPTY(FirstName),"there",FirstName)=%%` in AMPscript), or
- Suppression of the name token when null
