# $15 off ends tomorrow!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same SOBRIGHT "buy more, save more" ladder I got yesterday in "Have you used your $15 off yet?" and earlier today in "Buy more = SAVE more." The $15 / $20 / $60 numbers are big and easy to read on my phone. The subject says it ends tomorrow, and the fine print agrees: valid October 26-28. So the deadline is real, but it's the third send on this one offer in two days.
- Then the email turns into women's booties. High-heel boots, a snakeskin heel, a woman in a red coat, all under "Bootie Reboot" and "Animal attraction." Nothing here is for a 62-year-old man. The only thing for me is a small "Shop Men" link under the banner. I want cushioned, slip-on, arch-supported walking shoes, and this email doesn't show one. The sneakers tile is a silver Mary Jane flat.
- I wouldn't click. I'd glance at the $15 off, see nothing for me, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either, unless my wife wanted the boots.

## What's working

- The $15 / $20 / $60 ladder is large and clear, and I can read it without zooming.
- "Ends tomorrow" matches the fine print dates, so the urgency is honest.
- The black "Claim $15 off" button is easy to see and easy to tap.
- The SOBRIGHT code is stated plainly, and the offer works in stores and online.
- The Shop by Size block with Wide Width gives me large tap targets. Wide fit matters to me.

## What's weak

- Almost everything is women's: heels, snakeskin, and a fashion model. I'm not the audience.
- "Shop Men" is a small underlined link, easy to miss, with nothing for men below it.
- The offer is a repeat. I saw SOBRIGHT yesterday and earlier today, and last week it was 20% off with JINGLE.
- Nothing mentions comfort, cushioning, or arch support. A heeled bootie is the opposite of what I look for.
- The fine print is tiny and the exclusions list is long. Skechers is excluded, and that's a brand I'd shop here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Send men like me a men's comfort hero, such as slip-on walkers or cushioned sneakers, with the same offer.
- 2. Make the CTA say what's behind it, like "Shop men's walking shoes," instead of three generic "Shop Women / Men / Kids" links.
- 3. Stop sending the same code three times in two days. Send one strong reminder, with the deadline in the subject.
- 4. Put the exclusions in plain words near the offer instead of far down the page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: $15 off at DSW`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$15 off walking shoes ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SOBRIGHT works in stores and online. Men's comfort picks inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save $15, $20 or $60. Wide widths and cushioned styles included.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent message with click tracking wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`. The one confirmed defect is a 400 on the SMS opt-in CTA. The remaining findings are low-severity accessibility and deliverability issues. The HTML was truncated and only 12 of 69 links were probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (400):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned HTTP 400.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/IuF92uXWl5W1LUYC-x99mQ~~/AAQRxRA~/D79kR66e…Q~~`. This is a tracked redirect, so the 400 came from the tracking or redirect layer or the destination.
  - A bot-filtered probe can cause this, so manually confirm the link in a real client before treating it as definitely broken. If it is real, it affects a revenue-bearing CTA.
- **Link coverage:** 57 of 69 links were skipped, so the rest are unverified. Only the 12 highest-priority links were probed, and the 400 above was the only failure among them.
- **Tracking pixels:** there are two. One is `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261027_P261498_V1_W-_Value_15Off`. The other is an `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` open pixel. Both are working as designed apart from the alt text issue below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. The charset then depends on the Content-Type header alone. Impact is low, but it is a clean-up item.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):** both are tracking pixels, `o.gif` and the `ablink.dsw.com/q/P_3jmTfRA…` pixel. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs are 22,002 of 26,254 characters, about 84%. That is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk. The long tracked URLs drive it.
- **Dark mode:** `.dm-bg-white` sets `#ffffff !important` inside the dark-mode media query. This is an intentional override and no defect was found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. `mi_u=225090073` is a resolved Movable Ink user ID. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** the QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping it, so the failure is in the test path and not necessarily at the sender. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` are unverified. Check the raw headers from a direct delivery or a seed-list tool.
- **Unsubscribe link:** the probe policy always includes it, and it is not among the failures. The footer markup, the physical address and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not visible in the truncated source or QA data, so they could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be assessed because every link is wrapped in an opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirect and the destination URLs weren't resolved in the QA data. Campaign attribution appears only in the pixel, as `mi_ecmp=20261027_P261498_V1_W-_Value_15Off`.
- Landing page alignment is unverified for the same reason. The one probed link that failed, the SMS opt-in, never reached a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **High:** reproduce the 400 on the SMS opt-in link manually. If it fails in a real browser, fix the Braze link template or the destination URL before the next send.
2. **Medium:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Medium:** add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. **Medium:** shorten the plain-text version, for example by using short link redirects or dropping the per-link tracking tokens from it, to bring the URL share well below 70%.
5. **Low:** correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. **Verification gaps:** re-run with the full HTML. Resolve the redirect chains to check UTMs and landing pages, and capture the raw headers to check SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`.
