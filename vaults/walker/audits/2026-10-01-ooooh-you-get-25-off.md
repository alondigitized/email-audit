# Ooooh you get 25% off...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and it's the second VIP Days email I've had today. The giant white "25% OFF*" on purple is easy to read on my phone, so I'll give them that. But I'd already seen this banner this morning in "Happy VIP Days 🎉 Get 25% off >>>", and with the clearance emails all week, the "it only happens twice a year" line doesn't land. If it only happens twice a year, why am I getting it twice in one day?
- Then I scrolled, and the shoes under "So new, so you" are a wedge sneaker with straps, a platform clog, a Mary Jane flat, a shearling bootie, an ankle boot and a leopard bootie. Every one of them is women's. Nothing here is slip-on, cushioned or arch-supported, and nothing is aimed at a 62-year-old man. "So you" is a stretch.
- I might tap "Shop Men" out of habit, since 25% off is real money. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd probably mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The 25% OFF number is huge and I can't miss it, even on a small screen.
- The code VIPFUN is right there under the number, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- There's a "Shop Men" link in the hero, so I can skip straight past the women's stuff.
- "Shop by Size" with "Shop Wide Width" buttons is a thoughtful touch. Wide feet are a real thing at my age.
- The fine print lists Skechers among the brands that qualify. That's good to know, since I cross-shop them.
- The layout is clean, with nothing broken or overlapping, and the button text is big enough to read.

## What's weak

- All six featured shoes are women's fashion styles. There's no cushioning, arch support or walking shoe anywhere.
- The "Shop Men" link is a small underlined text link sitting between three others. It's an awkward tap target for my thumbs.
- The big button says "Become a VIP," which isn't a shopping action. The offer needs me to be a VIP, and the deal is for "regular-price styles only," and I only find those catches by scrolling to the small print.
- "It only happens twice a year" feels hollow when the same banner landed in my inbox this morning.
- The dates, October 1–4, are buried in the legal text. A deadline up top would have given me a reason to act today.
- The "Extra 25% off for VIPs!" tag under each shoe is tiny red text. It's hard to read and repeats the banner six times.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show men's content when the reader has shown men's or comfort interest. Two or three walking or slip-on shoes (Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance) in the hero would give me a reason to tap.
- 2. Make the main button "Shop Men's 25% Off" instead of "Become a VIP." Give the audience links bigger tap targets.
- 3. Put the end date in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday, Oct 4," and say "regular-price styles" up front instead of only in the fine print.
- 4. Combine this send with this morning's. Don't send two VIP Days emails on the same day.
- 5. Add a comfort row, such as "Cushioned, slip-on, wide widths," to go with the size shortcuts.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off regular-price shoes, through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `VIP Days: 25% off men's comfort shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code VIPFUN works in stores and online, Oct 1–4. Wide widths included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Walking shoes, slip-ons and boots from Skechers, Clarks and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally standard (Braze-built XHTML table layout, dark-mode CSS, tracked links). The QA run found one confirmed broken CTA, the "Opt-In to Texts" link, which returned HTTP 400. It also found two minor accessibility warnings and a plain-text bloat warning. The HTML source was truncated and no headers were supplied, so several categories can't be fully assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (HTTP 400):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/SGe6YklaZvq9P4qAw39FEg~~/AAQRxRA~/heas0vmv…` (about 600 chars of tracking token). A 400 on the click-tracking redirect means the link may fail for recipients, not just for the probe.
  - Caveat: some ESP redirectors return 400 to non-browser user agents or to tokens tied to a specific recipient. Confirm with a real-client click-through before treating it as an outage.
- **Probe coverage is partial:** only 12 of 69 links were probed and 57 were skipped. Footer, social and utility links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/Z7BsSpn9u5ZhuDMLmm4FJQ~~/…` and `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261001_P261342_V4_WM-_Offer_Stan…` are two separate open-tracking pixels. Two vendors are tracking opens, which is redundant, and `mi_u` puts a recipient identifier in a URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text on 2 images:** the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` pixel and `mi.dsw.com/…/o.gif`. Both are tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them.
- **Meta tags (low confidence):** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute in the supplied source, so they would have no effect. The source may have been sanitized, so verify against the raw MIME.
- No other rendering issues are confirmed in the visible portion. Viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection`, and Outlook `mso-*` resets are present, and the web fonts have fallbacks to confirm in the truncated section.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}` or `{%…%}` appear in the visible source. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Unsubscribe:** the QA probe always includes the unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported for it.
