# Take 20% off + 4 new trends to try
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW newsletter I've gotten all week — big "20% OFF" banner, then four straight trend blocks that are tall suede boots, animal print booties, lace-and-bow heels, and a loafer worn with cropped pants and mismatched socks. Every single hero shot is women's fashion footwear, nothing I'd ever put on my feet.
- The one bright spot is buried two-thirds down: "Happy UGG Season" with a guy in a plaid shirt actually pictured. That's the first time all week I've seen a man in one of these emails, and it's still not my brand — Uggs aren't comfort-first arch-support shoes for me. Below that there's a "Shop By Size" grid and wide-width/wide-calf buttons, which at least acknowledges bigger feet exist, but there's no men's callout anywhere near it.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm sure not forwarding this to anybody. Straight to the "meh, mark as read" pile — same as the last five DSW emails this week (per my history, none has cracked a 5).

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" banner in blue up top is bold and easy to spot at a glance.
- "Shop By Size" and wide-width/wide-calf buttons near the bottom at least nod to practical fit needs.
- The UGG section finally shows a man in the imagery — small thing, but it's the first time this week.
- Clean white background and readable fonts — nothing broken or garbled.

## What's weak

- Four consecutive "trend" blocks — tall boots, animal print, bows, loafers with cropped pants — and every one is women's styling. Nothing hands-free, cushioned, or arch-supported in sight.
- No "Shop Men" link anywhere in the main nav row up top or the trend section, only "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN / SHOP KIDS" as tiny text — buried, not a real CTA.
- The UGG section is the closest thing to relevant and it's still not a comfort-walking shoe, just a seasonal boot push.
- Same "20% OFF" formula I've now seen four times this week from this sender — nothing new, no escalation, no men's offer carved out.
- "SHOP THE FALL LOOKBOOK" and "SHOP ALL WOMEN" buttons at the bottom double down on the same audience miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's comfort block — Skechers Arch Fit, Hoka walking shoes, whatever's on sale — right after the 20% off banner, not buried behind four women's trend blocks.
- 2. Make "SHOP MEN" its own real button with an image, not a text link lost in a row of five.
- 3. Swap at least one of the four trend spots for a men's-specific trend (slip-ons, cushioned walkers, casual sneakers) so it's not 100% women's fashion.
- 4. Segment the send — if DSW has my purchase history or browse data, don't show me tall suede boots at all.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, arch support — all 20% off for VIPs`
