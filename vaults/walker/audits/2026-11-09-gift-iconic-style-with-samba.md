# Gift iconic style with Samba
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- It's another adidas email, and I've lost count since the 15th. This one has a shoe up top, three Sambas stacked against a brick wall, and that's nicer than the apparel-only ones. But it's a gift pitch with no price, no percent off and no deadline. It tells me Samba is "a classic for a reason" and leaves it there.
- I'm 62 and I walk for comfort. The Samba is a flat soccer-style sneaker, and nothing here mentions cushioning, arch support or how it feels on the foot. The second half pictures two young people in jackets and a scarf, which has nothing to do with me. "Evo SL" gets a passing mention, and that's the racing shoe from the November 1 email.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on. A grandkid might get a Samba from me, but this email gives me no reason to pick it over Skechers or New Balance.

## What's working

- The three Sambas on the brick wall are a clear, handsome hero. I could see the shoe right away.
- The headline "GIFT A CLASSIC" is big and easy to read on a phone.
- The Shop Men, Shop Women and Shop Kids buttons are large black blocks. They're easy to tap, and they let me shop for the whole family.
- The copy is short and the layout is clean, with plenty of white space.

## What's weak

- There's no price, no discount and no deadline anywhere. After the 50% off emails earlier this month, this feels empty.
- Nothing speaks to comfort, cushioning or arch support. The Samba is a flat-soled sneaker, and I walk for comfort.
- The second image shows two young adults in jackets. The "Evo SL" running mention is buried in a sentence, with no image and no link of its own.
- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" bar at the top is vague, and it fights with the real hero right below it.
- The "Shop Now" button at the bottom goes nowhere specific, and the two sections say nearly the same thing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer in the hero, like "Samba from $X" or a gift-card bonus, with a ship-by date for the holidays. Right now there's no reason to act today.
- 2. Add one line on fit and comfort: cushioning, insole, how it wears for all-day walking. Show a men's walking or lifestyle shoe for an older buyer.
- 3. Drop the top "best sellers" bar. It competes with the hero. Link the shoe image straight to the Samba page.
- 4. Replace the young-couple image with a product grid showing prices and star ratings.
- 5. Make the final button say what it opens, such as "Shop Samba gifts."
