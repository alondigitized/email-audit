# adidas up to 40% off >>>
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Subject said adidas up to 40% off, and I open it thinking maybe there's a plain pair of Sambas or a cushioned trainer in there for me — nope. First thing on screen is "Creature Comforts," a leopard-print bootie on a woman in a red coat with baby blue socks, then a snakeskin loafer, a suede boat shoe, and a leopard crossbody bag. Not one adidas item is even pictured — I see the three-stripe logo up top and that's it. This is the same DSW playbook I've been getting all week, just now with an "adidas" hook slapped on the subject line to get me to open it.
- I'm not clicking this and I'm sure not forwarding it — there's nothing in here for a 62-year-old guy who wants a slip-on with arch support. Straight to read-and-delete, same as the last six from this brand.

## What's working

- "UP TO 40% OFF" is at least big, bold, and unmissable at the top — that part I can read fine without my glasses.
- Sizing chart near the bottom (5 through 11, wide width, wide calf) is genuinely clear and useful — if there were a men's shoe in here I could actually picture buying, that section would help.
- No layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping text that I could spot — it renders clean, I'll give it that.

## What's weak

- Subject promised adidas, but the entire visual is leopard-print booties, snakeskin flats, and a handbag — bait and switch, plain and simple.
- Every model and product is women's fall fashion — animal print, ankle booties, a purse — nothing hands-free, cushioned, or man-sized in sight.
- "40% off" has an asterisk and I have to dig to the gray fine-print block at the very bottom to find out what's actually excluded — feels like a game.
- CTA buttons say "Shop Animal Print" and "Shop All Women" — there isn't even a "Shop Men" button to tap.
- This is the same brand, same offer structure, different costume for the fourth or fifth day running — I called this out yesterday with the "Brands That Have Us" send, and here we are again.

## Recommendations

- 1. If the subject says adidas, the hero image better show adidas — a men's Samba or a cushioned walking shoe, not a leopard bootie. Match the promise or don't make it.
- 2. Add a men's category button next to "Shop All Women" — right now I have nothing to tap.
- 3. Put the "up to 40% off" exclusions in plain type near the offer, not buried in gray legalese at the bottom.
- 4. Swap out at least one hero slot for a men's comfort silhouette — Skechers, Hoka, whatever's on sale — so it's not five straight days of the same heels-and-handbags formula.
