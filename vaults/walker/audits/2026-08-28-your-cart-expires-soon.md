# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that same pink floral D'Lites shoe, "YOU LIKED THESE AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG," parked right at the top with a big "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button underneath. I did not like it the first time, I said so on the 26th, I said so again yesterday, and here we are on a fresh date with the same recycled guilt-trip. That's not my cart, Skechers, and pretending I "liked" a floral women's sneaker doesn't make it true.
- Buried way down past a wall of kids' basketball shoes and boys' Garage Sneakers is the one thing that's actually for me — a 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step banner with a real code. That's the email I wanted on the 26th ("August upgrade: 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step"), and it's sitting here as a footnote to someone else's cart.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this — there's nothing here that fits a 62-year-old guy shopping for himself. Marking as read and moving on, same as the last four times this week.

## What's working

- The 20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step banner near the bottom is a real, relevant offer with a clear code (UPGRADE) — that part I'd actually use.
- "Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit: Altman - Murdock Sneaker" showed up in the best-sellers grid, which is at least in my category.
- Fine print with dates and terms is spelled out honestly at the bottom, no hidden gotchas.
- Payment options (Afterpay, Klarna) and store locator are easy to spot if I did want to buy something.

## What's weak

- The hero is, once again, a pink floral women's D'Lites sneaker framed as "my" cart — sixth-plus time this week, and it's never been anything I put there.
- Best-sellers grid is mostly kids' basketball and boys' Garage Sneakers — not remotely my demographic.
- The one offer that actually applies to me (20% off Cozy Fit & Glide Step) is buried past a fake cart and a kids' shoe wall instead of leading the email.
- "You liked these and you weren't wrong" is presumptuous and, frankly, wrong — I never looked at that shoe.
- No comfort/arch-support callouts near the hero, no men's casual framing up top where it'd catch my eye.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending me a stranger's cart. If there's no real abandoned-cart item for this subscriber, don't fabricate one — lead with an actual offer instead. This alone would fix five of my last six reviews of this brand.
- 2. Move the Cozy Fit & Glide Step 20% off banner to the top, make it the hero, and pair it with a man's product shot like the "Cozy Fit Favorites" send from the 27th that scored well with me.
- 3. Swap the best-sellers grid to actually reflect the recipient's browsing — men's comfort styles, not kids' basketball shoes.
- 4. Drop the "you liked these" framing entirely when it's not backed by real behavior data; it just erodes trust.
