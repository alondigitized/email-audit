# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Same skull sneaker, again. The black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" is the one I've been nagged about on 11/05, 11/08, three times on 11/11, and all through 11/16 and 11/17. I have never put it in a cart. "Ready to check out?" and "Complete your order" talk to me like I abandoned something, and I didn't. A new customer might believe it. I just roll my eyes.
- The slip-ins at the bottom are the only part that's for me: the Murette slipper, the waterproof Orson boots, and the Contour Foam sneaker. That's hands-free comfort, which is what I buy. But they're small, shoved under a skull shoe that looks like Halloween, and the offer is buried. The fine print says the Buy One Get One 50% off expired 11/17, and today is 11/18. That's a stale promo, and I don't like it.
- Would I click? Maybe on a slip-in, but not through the "complete your order" button. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The slip-in lineup (Murette, Orson boots, Contour Foam) is exactly what I wear, and the product names say "Relaxed Fit" and "Arch Fit."
- The Black Friday banner is readable, with big white text on navy: "Buy One, Get One 50% Off, or 20% Off a Single Pair."
- The Skechers logo is clear, so I know who sent it.
- The category list (Men, Women, Kids, Sale) has big text and is easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- The skull sneaker is wrong for me for the seventh or eighth time. It's loud, young, and nothing like what I'd buy.
- "Ready to check out?" is false. I have no cart, and a false claim costs trust.
- The fine print says the offer ended 11/17, so the Black Friday banner is stale today.
- Two "Complete your order" buttons (black and orange) are nearly the same, and neither links to anything I chose.
- The best-sellers are small, the captions are tiny, and the long product names are hard to read without zooming.
- I see no loyalty points or "Hi, Walker" bar, though the 11/16 email had one. I get treated like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the cart-abandonment template to people who have no cart. Use a real browse-based or sale email instead.
- 2. Lead with the slip-in comfort lineup as the hero, with bigger images and one clear button.
- 3. Fix the offer dates so the banner doesn't advertise an expired deal.
- 4. Bring back my points balance and personal greeting.
- 5. Use fewer CTAs: one hero button and one "shop all" button.
