# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 3/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-14**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I got through the browsing part of this visit fine, but I could not buy anything. The homepage, the Men's menu, the Men's Shoes page and search all loaded and were easy to read on my phone. The login step showed nothing different from the homepage, so I can't tell whether I was logged in. The cart badge still said 0. On the product page I tapped Add to Cart with no size chosen and got a red "Please select a size" message. The cart page then came up completely white, with no items and no error. A shopper can't finish a purchase on that, however good the shoes look.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10.** Yesterday was also 3/10, so nothing has changed. Browsing works, but the cart screen is blank and I never got to see checkout.

## 3. What's Working

- **Readable header.** The Skechers logo, search, account, cart and MENU are clear and easy to tap. Everything fits without zooming, which my eyes appreciate.
- **Menu that fits a phone.** The Men's menu slides open with large, well-spaced lines: Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections and so on. There's a Back button and an X. I could use it without help.
- **Comfort words up front.** The homepage strip shows "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit" and "Max Cushioning." That is what I'm shopping for. On Men's Shoes, "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes" are the first two tiles.
- **Clear sizes.** The sizes (6.5 to 14) are big buttons I can tap, and the Add to Cart button is wide and obvious.
- **Search had plenty of results.** "comfort shoes" returned 1,592 results with the price and the 20% member discount shown on each.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Blank cart page (Step 8).** I got a completely white screen. I'd give up and go to Brooks or New Balance.
- **Add to Cart didn't work for me.** I pressed it before choosing a size and only got a red error. That's reasonable, but there's no message that tells me what to do next, and the cart still showed 0 afterward. The size picker also sits well down the page, so I only found it by scrolling.
- **Login looked like nothing happened (Step 3).** The screen is identical to the homepage. I saw no "Hi, Walker," no saved info and no points. I can't say the experience felt personal.
- **The popup step is also identical to the homepage.** Either no popup appeared, or it was gone before the screenshot.
- **Wrong first product.** The shoe I landed on was the Men's UNO - Compañero Eterno at $100. It's a black lace-up fashion sneaker with a graphic print, has no reviews and is "Excluded from promotions." That's the opposite of a slip-on, cushioned walking shoe, and the banner promising 20% off doesn't apply to it.
- **Search skews women's.** The first two results for "comfort shoes" were women's styles, a pink sneaker and a black wedge. I'm a man looking for men's walking shoes. It doesn't know that, and nothing lets me narrow it down quickly.
- **Mixed messages on price.** The top banner says "20% OFF Sitewide," while the product page says "Excluded from promotions." The shipping line says "Sign in for free shipping & returns." That's confusing and makes me doubt the banners.
- **Small, light text in places.** The "Free pickup" toggle, the Filter row and the gray product subtitles are small for tired eyes.
- **No comparison help.** I saw nothing that compares Skechers with Hoka or Brooks, and nothing that does the price-versus-quality math I care about.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the blank cart page first.** A white screen costs the sale. Show the cart contents, or at least a clear "Your cart is empty" message with a link back to shopping.
2. **Make the size error obvious.** Scroll the shopper to the size row and highlight it, or put the size chooser higher up the page.
3. **Show that I'm logged in.** Put a greeting or my name in the header, and show "Hi Walker" and any points on the first screen after signing in.
4. **Point comfort shoppers to the right shoes.** The first products I see in search or "Men's Shoes" should be men's slip-ins, walking shoes, or arch-fit styles that qualify for the promotions. Don't send me to an excluded fashion sneaker.
5. **Reconcile the promo messages.** If a shoe is excluded from the 20% off, say so in the banner or label it consistently on the page.
6. **Add a simple comfort filter.** A one-tap "Slip-ins," "Arch Fit," "Wide Fit" row at the top of search results would be a big help.
7. **Use larger text** for filters, prices and the pickup toggle.

## 6. Bottom Line

I'd browse Skechers again. The site is clean, the buttons are big, and the menu speaks my language: slip-ins, arch fit, wide fit, walking. But a blank cart is a dead end. If I can't tell whether my shoes are in the cart, I'm shopping somewhere else. Fix the cart and show me men's comfort shoes first, and this could be a much better experience.

**Compared with yesterday (3/10):** I see no real improvement or regression. The mobile layout, menu and search are good, and the blank cart and unclear login are still there. I don't have yesterday's step-by-step notes, so I can't say whether any single step changed.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

- **Data quality:** This run is only partly usable.
  - LCP and CLS are `?` for all 8 steps, so Core Web Vitals can't be assessed.
  - The captured URL for "Log In" and "Men category" is `https://www.skechers.com/`, the same as the homepage. Their HTML heads are nearly identical.
  - TTFB is exactly 509ms for both of those steps, and 28–34ms for steps 4–6 and 8. That points to soft navigations or stale `PerformanceNavigationTiming` entries, not real document loads.
  - The HTML samples are truncated inside `<head>`, so `<title>`, viewport meta, canonical and structured data could not be checked.
- **Bot mitigation:** Kasada (`x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.899`) returned HTTP 429 five times on its `/fp` fingerprint endpoint. `/cart/` also returned 429. The scripted browser was rate-limited or challenged, so the View Cart step probably captured an error or challenge page, not the real cart.
- **Main findings:**
  - Homepage landmark structure is broken: duplicate and nested `<main>`.
  - Unlabelled buttons and links on the homepage.
  - Heavy third-party tag load.
  - Several `unload` permissions-policy violations.

## Accessibility

18 axe violations across 3 pages: 2 critical, 3 serious, 11 moderate, 2 minor.

**Homepage (10 rules)**
- **Critical:** `button-name` (buttons with no accessible text) and `aria-required-parent` (ARIA roles such as `listitem` or `tab` outside their required parent).
- **Serious:** `link-name` (links with no discernible text).
- **Landmark structure:** `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region`. Together they indicate multiple `<main>` elements, some nested inside other landmarks. A second app shell or a third-party widget may be injecting its own `<main>`.
- **Other:** `heading-order` (moderate) and `aria-allowed-role` (minor).

**Search "comfort shoes" (4 rules):** `aria-allowed-role`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region`. These match the homepage, which suggests shared template markup in the header, footer or aside.

**View Cart (4 rules):** `document-title`, `html-has-lang`, `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one`. A page missing all four basics is almost certainly the 429 response body, not the cart template. This is a test artifact and shouldn't be filed as a cart defect. The Homepage `<html lang="en">` is correct.

## SEO

- **Not assessable:** Titles, meta descriptions, canonicals, hreflang and JSON-LD are not visible in the truncated samples.
- **Possible bot-gate effect:** The View Cart response had no `<title>` and no `<h1>`. If Kasada serves a similar shell to crawlers that get rate-limited, indexing could be affected. Verify that Googlebot is allow-listed.
- **Heading structure:** The homepage `heading-order` violation is a minor on-page SEO and semantics issue.

## Performance

- **LCP/CLS:** No data for any step.
- **TTFB:** Homepage is 426ms and Log In / Men are 509ms. All three are acceptable, below the 800ms "good" threshold. The 28–34ms values on later steps are likely not full document loads.
- **Third-party load on the homepage:**
  - Tag manager: GTM.
  - Ad and social pixels: TikTok, Pinterest, Bing, Yahoo, Taboola, Snapchat, Reddit, Facebook, Nextdoor, Impact, RTB House.
  - Analytics and personalization: Quantum Metric, Bloomreach, Tune/HasOffers, Pulse Insights, and Monetate (`custom.js` and `entry.js`).
  - The Men category page also loaded Taboola, Snapchat, Reddit, Stackadapt and Nextdoor.
- **Fonts:** Fonts load through `webfontloader.js` from cdnjs, set by `wf-mulish-*-active` classes. This adds a third-party dependency and a JS-gated font swap that can delay LCP text and cause layout shift.
- **Personalization:** Monetate client-side experiments can cause flicker and CLS. Measure this once LCP/CLS capture works.
- **Consent gating:** `vice-managed-src` attributes show consent-managed script blocking, which is correct. Check that scripts don't all fire once consent resolves.

## Mobile Optimization

- The `--vh` CSS variable is set via JS (6.64px, so a ~664px viewport). That's a workaround for mobile browser chrome, and it means layout depends on JS before first paint.
- No viewport meta or touch-target data is available.
- Search results show only the landmark and ARIA issues listed above.

## Console & Network Errors

**Network: 6 × HTTP 429**
- Five are on `/149e9513-.../fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.899`, Kasada's fingerprint endpoint. This is anti-bot rate limiting that the automation is triggering.
- One is on `https://www.skechers.com/cart/`, which blocks the View Cart step.

**Console: 10 listed of 26 reported**
- `429` resource failures, 3 times. These correspond to the network 429s.
- `Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed in this document`, 4 times. A script registers an `unload` handler. This is deprecated and blocks bfcache. Likely a third-party tag (candidates: Quantum Metric, Monetate or ad pixels).
- `Unsupported Summarizer API languages…`, 3 times. This comes from Chrome's built-in Summarizer API, called with unsupported language options. It is probably from a browser extension or the test harness, not site code. Confirm by running without extensions.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the audit harness first.**
   - Capture LCP and CLS with `PerformanceObserver` or the `web-vitals` library.
   - Wait for real navigations before sampling. The Log In and Men steps captured the homepage URL.
   - Retry or back off on 429, and use an allow-listed or authenticated test identity for Kasada, so View Cart and later steps measure the real pages.
2. **Fix the homepage landmarks.** Keep a single top-level `<main>`, unwrap nested `<main>` and `<aside>` elements, and give repeated `<nav>`/`<aside>` elements unique `aria-label`s.
3. **Fix the unlabelled controls.** Add accessible names to icon-only buttons and links (`aria-label` or visually hidden text), and correct the parent/child ARIA role structure.
4. **Fix the shared aria-allowed-role and heading-order issues.** Fix them in the shared template, since they repeat across Homepage and Search.
5. **Find the source of the `unload` listener** (DevTools, "Permissions policy violation" stack trace). Replace it with `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`, or ask the vendor to.
6. **Audit the third-party tags.** Load pixels via GTM after the interaction or consent trigger, drop unused vendors, and consider self-hosting the fonts to remove the cdnjs/webfontloader dependency.
7. **Re-run with the full HTML head** to cover title, meta, canonical, structured data and viewport.
