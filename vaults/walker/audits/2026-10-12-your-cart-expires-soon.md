# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. That's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom," a women's shoe I never put in a cart, and it fills the top third of my screen under "YOU LIKED THESE." I've now seen this cart nag on 10/10, twice on 10/11 ("Your Items Are Waiting!") and again today. The subject says my cart "expires soon," but nothing in the email tells me what expires or when.
- The one useful thing is a 25% off banner for Skechers Plus members, but it sits well below the fold. The grid under the hero has a men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman - Murdock sneaker and a men's Summits Contour Foam Cozy Fit, which are my kind of shoes. But they're tiny, and they're mixed in with boys' and girls' Garage sneakers and a waterproofer spray. I wouldn't click. I'd skim it, shrug, and swipe it away. I wouldn't forward it either.

## What's working

- The 25% off banner is clear. "2 DAYS ONLY," "Valid thru 10/13" and "Discount auto-applied at checkout" tell me what I get and when it ends.
- The grid includes two men's comfort shoes, the Slip-ins Altman and the Cozy Fit Summits. Those are the shoes I'd actually wear.
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is big and dark. I could tap it easily with my thumb.
- The category list (WOMEN, MEN, KIDS and so on) is in large text. It's easy to read on my phone.

## What's weak

- The women's pink floral sneaker is the hero again. It's not for me, and I've seen it five times now.
- "Your cart expires soon!" sets up urgency, but the email never shows a cart, a price or an expiry time.
- The best deal, 25% off, is buried halfway down the page. It's also for Plus members only, and the email doesn't say whether I'm one. My 1000 points were front and center on 10/10, and they're missing today.
- The men's shoes in the grid are small and have no prices. Kids' shoes and a spray crowd them. Nothing mentions arch support or cushioning.
- The fine print is tiny and says the offer is "online only" and excludes some items. That makes a 25% deal feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the items actually in my cart, with prices and the real expiry time. If it's a men's slip-on, lead with that.
- 2. Move the 25% off banner above the hero and say plainly whether I qualify as a Plus member.
- 3. Segment the grid by what I browse. Show men's Slip-ins and Arch Fit shoes, and drop the kids' shoes.
- 4. Cut the cart-reminder cadence. One reminder is enough, not five in three days.
- 5. Make the product photos bigger and add a price under each one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, your Slip-ins are still in your cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off ends tomorrow, Plus members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your cushioned slip-ons are saved. Take 25% off before 10/13.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members save 25% on comfort shoes through tomorrow only.`
