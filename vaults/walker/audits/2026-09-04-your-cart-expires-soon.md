# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom, front and center under "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG," with a "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button underneath. I did not like these, I never touched these, and this makes at least four times this week Skechers has told me I'm one click from checking out a sneaker I've never so much as looked at. Whoever's inbox they think this is, it isn't a 62-year-old guy's.
- Here's the thing though — scroll past the floral shoe and there's actually a decent little grid underneath: Men's Slip-ins, an Arch Fit 2.0, an Arcade sneaker, even a 20%-off-for-Plus-members banner. That's the stuff that would've gotten my attention if it had led the email instead of being buried under a shoe I'm never buying.
- I'm not clicking on this one. The hero is wrong for me, and after this many identical "you liked these" emails for a shoe I've never touched, I'm getting close to just unsubscribing rather than scrolling past it again.

## What's working

- The men's grid further down has real product — Arch Fit 2.0, Delson 3.0, Arcade — that's actually my lane.
- The 20% off for Skechers Plus members banner is a clear, honest, time-bound offer (thru 9/7).
- Layout is clean and easy to scroll on my phone, big enough text, no glitches I can see.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup options are visible if I ever did want to buy.

## What's weak

- Hero product is a pink floral women's D'Lites sneaker I've never clicked on — wrong gender, wrong style, wrong everything for me.
- "YOU LIKED THESE AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG" is just false. I didn't like it. This is the same shoe from at least three other emails this week.
- The men's Arch Fit/slip-in products that actually fit me are buried three sections down, after the fake cart and a "shop all best sellers" banner.
- No loyalty personalization up top even though I get emails constantly — no name, no points balance shown near the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the personalization engine — if I've never clicked or added a shoe, don't build a "you liked these" hero around it. Lead with what I've actually browsed (men's Arch Fit, slip-ins) or with the 20% off Plus banner instead.
- 2. Move the men's product grid up to the top — that's the stuff that would make me open the app or click through.
- 3. Put my name and Plus/points status near the top so it feels like they know who I am, not a template blast.
- 4. Stop repeating this exact "cart" creative — I've now gotten it enough times this week that it reads as spam, not personalization.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New Arch Fit styles just landed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your 20% off is waiting at checkout, Skechers Plus`
