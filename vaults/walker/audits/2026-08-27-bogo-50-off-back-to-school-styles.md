# BOGO 50% Off back-to-school styles!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we go again — BOGO 50% off, big and bold, right at the top where I don't have to squint for it. That's the third or fourth time DSW's run this exact deal on me this week, so at this point I could recite the fine print myself. Below the headline it's the same "So New, So You" wall of heels — wedges, mary janes, strappy little numbers with bows on 'em — nothing a 62-year-old guy is putting on his feet.
- The size grid's a nice touch and the shipping/pickup icons are handy, but there's not a single men's shoe pictured anywhere in this email. I'm not clicking through six screens of women's heels to find the one pair that might fit me. This gets a glance, maybe a "huh, BOGO again," and then archived. Not forwarding it — nobody in my house wants heels either.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is a real, easy-to-do-in-my-head deal — no funny math required.
- Size grid (5 through 11) is clean and easy to tap on a phone.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup, and VIP rewards blurb are all visible without digging.
- Barcode right in the email for in-store use — that's actually handy for someone who still likes to just walk into the store.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown is a women's shoe — heels, wedges, mary janes. Not one sneaker, slip-on, or anything resembling comfort footwear for a guy like me.
- "MEN" is just a nav link up top with nothing behind it in this email — feels like an afterthought, not a real category push.
- Same BOGO 50% offer I've now seen from DSW multiple times this week — the novelty's worn off.
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support, or hands-free comfort anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap at least half the hero grid to men's comfort styles — a slip-on, a walking shoe, something with visible cushioning — if you're going to have a MEN nav link, back it up with product.
- 2. Segment this send by browsing history — I've clicked on Skechers and Hoka-style shoes before if they're tracking that; don't show me heels.
- 3. Lead the copy with a comfort hook, not just "so new, so you," which reads like it's aimed at someone browsing for date-night shoes.
- 4. Vary the offer language — I've seen "BOGO 50% off" from DSW enough times this week that it's starting to blur together; even the same deal read fresher last time with the "gear up for school" framing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles: BOGO 50% off, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, half off the second pair`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ons, walkers, and more — buy one, get 50% off the next.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort shoes for men, priced two-for-less.`
