# ⏰ Reminder: 30% Off + FREE Shipping is happening!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is the same 30% off and free shipping I got yesterday, with "Reminder" in front of it. The message is clear enough. Big black letters on bright blue, one button, and I understood it in a couple of seconds. But it's the second send in two days with the same offer, and it feels like being nagged. "Your shopping cart is calling" is also odd, because I never put anything in a cart.
- The shoe is a plain black Baya clog, so at least that part is readable. It's the same clog that was 30% off on 9/27. Nothing here tells me why it's good for my feet, and I'm a guy who cares about cushion and arch support. There's no price, no reviews, and no mention of comfort at all. The deadline is only in the tiny print at the bottom, Wednesday, October 7th at 11:59pm MT. That should be up top.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I already wanted a clog. I wouldn't forward it. I'd read it and move on.

## What's working

- The headline is huge and I can read it on my phone without squinting. 30% off plus free shipping is plain talk.
- There's one big black "Shop the Sale" button, easy to tap with my thumb.
- The black clog is a simple, honest shoe photo. No cartoon monsters or camo this time.
- The bright blue background makes the offer stand out. It looks like the yellow 30% email from 10/2, so I recognized it as the same sale.
- The footer is easy to read. Unsubscribe and Update Your Preferences are right there.

## What's weak

- It's a repeat of the 10/2 "Limited-Time Offer: 30% Off + Free Shipping" email. Same deal, different color.
- The "Your shopping cart is calling" line makes no sense to me, since I have nothing in a cart.
- The end date is buried in the small print at the bottom. "Still time" doesn't tell me how much time.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the button, and "select styles only" is in the fine print. That makes me wonder which shoes actually get the discount.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning or support. The ghosted "SALE SALE SALE" behind the shoe is a bit cluttered, and the shoe itself is partly covered by it.
- The Refer a Friend and app download blocks are noise to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Wednesday, Oct 7," and drop the "cart is calling" line. Both changes would help.
- 2. Show two or three specific comfort shoes with sale prices, something like a men's slip-on with the arch support noted, instead of one clog.
- 3. Add a line about comfort, like "Cushioned, hands-free, all-day wear," plus a star rating or review count.
- 4. Don't send a near-identical reminder the day after the main offer. Wait until the last 24 to 48 hours, or send it only to people who didn't open the first one.
- 5. Say what's excluded right in the hero, not in the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off + free shipping ends Wednesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfy clogs, 30% off through Oct 7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on qualified orders. Sale ends Wed, Oct 7 at 11:59pm MT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Light, cushioned, easy to slip on. Shop men's styles now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound, with a responsive media query, `lang="en"`, and the Gmail and Apple client resets. Its confirmed issues are seven images served over plain `http://` and two `<meta>` tags that have no effect. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and header checks could not be done.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** the QA run skipped 20 tracking links because they use tracking or click-redirect domains such as `click.crocs-email.com`. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Missing alt on a tracking pixel:** the Adobe Audience Manager pixel is `<img style="visibility:hidden;display:none" height="1" width="1" src="https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…">` and has no `alt` attribute. It is hidden, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` would keep it consistent with the primary open pixel, which has `alt=""`.
- **Third-party pixel:** the demdex pixel passes `d_dpuid` in the query string. Many clients, including Apple Mail with Mail Privacy Protection, prefetch or block it. Its `c_emailopened=Yes` signal is therefore unreliable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources:** seven images load from `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`:
  - Download on the App Store (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Get It On Google Play (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)

  Mixed content can be blocked or trigger warnings in some clients and webviews. The same host serves other assets over HTTPS elsewhere in the email, so the HTTPS version is likely available.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so the browser ignores both. The charset is then declared only by the MIME `Content-Type` header, which I couldn't see.
- **QA output inconsistency:** the QA summary reports "0 issue(s), 0 warning(s)" while listing eight `[WARN]` entries. The automated check's counters or severity mapping appear wrong.
- **Alt text:** the seven images above have alt text. Beyond the demdex pixel, no other missing-alt images were reported.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and QA data show no unresolved tokens, such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
Not assessable from the data provided. The HTML is truncated before the footer, and no raw headers were supplied. I cannot confirm the physical address, the unsubscribe link or `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `crocs-email.com` is also unconfirmed. No violations were observed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links go through `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, which the QA run skipped. The final destination URLs and any UTM parameters are therefore unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the seven `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image sources to `https://`.
2. Fix the head tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
4. Fix the QA tool's warning counter. Also add a step that resolves redirect-domain links and checks the final destinations and UTMs.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and raw headers to cover compliance and authentication.
