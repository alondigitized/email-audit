# Your Items (+ Cyber Week savings) Are Waiting!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth cart nag since 11/20, and I'm tired of it. "We noticed you noticing us..." still sounds like someone watching me through the window. The hero shoe saves it a little. It's the same men's-looking GO WALK Glide-Step 2.0 Cozy Fit Walker slip-in in taupe and black that I saw in this morning's "Your cart is one click away!" It's cushioned and hands-free, which is what I shop for.
- The black Cyber Week banner at the top, "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE", does the heavy lifting. I could read it without squinting. Below it the email drops into a lot of small gray product captions, and the grid mixes in women's boots, a women's sneaker and a can of waterproofer spray. Only one of the six items is a men's shoe, the Garlan Pryor, and I'd have to hunt for it.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the hero shoe, since I did sit up for it this morning. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the cart nags to stop.

## What's working

- The "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner on black is big and red, and I could read it on my phone.
- The hero shoe is a men's-looking slip-in in a neutral color, and it's the right kind of shoe for me.
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is a clear navy block, easy to tap.
- The Skechers Plus "EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING" red box tells me there's a member perk.
- The WOMEN / MEN / KIDS / SALE list is big type and simple to navigate.

## What's weak

- "We noticed you noticing us..." is creepy. This is the tenth nag, so it also feels like pestering.
- The six-item grid has tiny captions I'd have to zoom to read. Four of the six are women's styles, and one is a spray.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is tiny. "Discount auto-applied at cart" is also small, and it's the line I care about most.
- The hero shoe shows no price, no sale price, and no mention of cushioning or arch support.
- The bottom third (app, Afterpay/Klarna, texting) is clutter I'd never act on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap the cart reminders. Send one a day and tie each to a different reason, like a price drop or low stock in my size.
- 2. Show the cart shoe with its sale price, e.g. "$XX now, was $YY", plus one comfort line like "Slip-in, cushioned, arch-supported".
- 3. Filter the "more options" grid to men's comfort and drop the spray. Make the captions bigger.
- 4. Move "discount auto-applied at cart" up next to the button in larger type.
- 5. Cut the creepy copy and the footer clutter.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-in comfort, now with Cyber Week savings applied at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders as a table-based, responsive layout with a mobile media query, but the QA run found a failing unsubscribe link and an empty plain-text part. It also found about 14 non-HTTPS image sources, several images with missing or poor alt text, and a malformed charset meta tag. The QA pass rate was 62%. The HTML source was truncated, so findings below cover only what the source excerpt and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400 (FAIL).** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…`. The `attn_macro` value base64-decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. A 400 may be caused by the probe, such as a missing browser context or a single-use token. It is still unverified, and it is the one link that must work.
- **Two CTAs returned 429 (WARN).** These are "Complete you Order" and "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting...", both on `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`. The server rate-limited the probe, so the links are unconfirmed rather than broken.
- **Opaque tracking redirects.** All links are Attentive redirect tokens. Only 12 of 42 links were probed, so the final destinations of the other 30 are unknown.
- **Very long URLs.** The CTA URLs are over 500 characters. That is normal for this tracking style but adds weight.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources (WARN).** At least 13 images load from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`. They include the logo `dde00662-…png`, the cart image `dfcc8fd7-…jpg`, the app-store badges, and the social icons. Clients may block or warn on them, so they should move to `https://`. The host already serves HTTPS, as the `.gif` shows.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif`
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`, which is also served over http
  - the tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` (low impact)
- **Poor alt text.**
  - `alt="Complete you Order"` has a typo.
  - `alt="Text"` on `49468f73-…png` is non-descriptive.
  - `alt="Here's some more great options we think you'll love:"` repeats body copy as alt.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This hurts accessibility and the web-version view.
- **Malformed charset declaration.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it declares nothing. The preheader's special characters depend on the MIME header charset instead.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta, and a `max-width:620px` responsive breakpoint are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`. The truncated portion, which holds the product and cart blocks, could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative (FAIL).** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". That hurts deliverability and accessibility. The cause could be the AgentMail relay stripping the part rather than the sender omitting it, so check the raw MIME.
- **Authentication unverified (WARN).** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for `msgs.skechers.com` could not be assessed. This comes from the relay path and is not evidence of failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not visible.** Headers were not provided, so one-click support (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed.
- **Unsubscribe link health.** See section 2: it returned a 400.
- **Physical postal address.** It was not in the truncated excerpt, so it is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Continuity can't be confirmed. The links are wrapped in `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirects, so the UTM parameters and landing pages can't be read from the source. The two CTA probes were rate-limited (429), so there is no evidence of where the cart-recovery links land or whether the cart state carries over. No mismatch was found, but nothing was verified either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check that the Attentive unsubscribe URL works in a real browser, with the single-use token fresh. If it fails, fix it first, since it is a compliance risk.
2. Confirm in the raw MIME that a `text/plain` part exists. If it doesn't, add one.
3. Change all `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` image sources to `https://`.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the gif and jpg that have none. Use `alt=""` for the tracking pixel and decorative images. Fix "Complete you Order" and replace `alt="Text"`.
5. Fill in `<title>`, and change the charset tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
6. Re-probe the two 429 CTAs with throttling. Follow the redirects to confirm the landing URL, UTM parameters, and cart restoration.
7. Capture the full headers to verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`.
