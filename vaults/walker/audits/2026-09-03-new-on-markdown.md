- The "$20 off $150" member offer up top and again at the bottom is clear and easy to read.
- "Shop Sale" and "Shop Now" buttons are big, black, easy to tap on a phone.

- No indication which of these shoes is the cushioned, arch-support one I actually care about — Arahi vs. Clifton means nothing to me without a line of copy explaining it.
- "Don't miss out" hero is just noise — no real urgency, no date, no specific reason to act now.

- 2. Fix that cut-off "Members Only" banner text — sloppy for a brand this size.
- 3. Swap "Don't miss out" for something concrete — tell me what's actually on markdown and why now.
- 4. Lead with the men's shoe next to the women's, not always women's-then-men's — small thing, but it'd catch my eye faster.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email is well-formed HTML with a proper preheader/nav/footer structure, but automated QA flagged two broken link probes (both returning 403) and a missing plain-text MIME part.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Broken link (403): CTA linking to `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` — the tracked redirect URL (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...`) returned a 403 when probed.
- Broken link (403): the Unsubscribe link — tracked redirect URL (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...`) also returned 403.
- Note: both 403s are on the Cordial click-tracking redirector (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/...`), not necessarily the destination itself — decoding the JWT payload shows the underlying `linkUrl` targets are `www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/` and `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`. A 403 at the redirector layer could indicate bot/automation blocking (e.g., WAF rule on the tracking domain) rather than a truly dead destination — recommend manual re-verification in a real client, but treat the unsubscribe failure as high priority regardless of cause (see Compliance).
- 12 material links were identified by click-likelihood ranking; all were probed, and remaining links were not flagged as broken.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text, including all primary promotional/product images (`US_20260903_LaborDayPromoKickoff_*.jpg`, `US_Arahi8_*`, `US_Clifton10_*`) and the footer logo (`LogoFooterPrimary.png`). This affects screen-reader users and clients with images blocked by default (common for cold-send recipients).
- Two tracking pixels also lack alt text (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`) — low impact since these are 1x1 opens trackers, not content images.
- CSS includes standard Outlook/Gmail reset patterns (`.ExternalClass`, `mso-table-lspace`, `x-apple-data-detectors`) and mobile breakpoints at 600px/480px — no structural rendering issues detected in the truncated source.
