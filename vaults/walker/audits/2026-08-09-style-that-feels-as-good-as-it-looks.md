## What's working

- "HANDS FREE SLIP-INS" is a direct hit — that's the exact comfort feature I shop for, no digging required.
- Named technologies (Arch Fit, Cozy Fit) give me something concrete to evaluate, not just marketing fluff.
- The lead sale banner (40% off) is right at the top, big and legible, no squinting needed.
- Product shots show the shoes at good angles with real detail on the soles — I can actually judge the cushioning from the image.
- Each section has its own clear CTA button, so I know exactly where to tap for what I want.

## What's weak

- That "ALL DAY" collage up top has more models doing yoga poses and gym stretches than actual comfort-walking scenarios — feels aimed at a younger, athletic crowd, not me.
- No mention of arch support specifically by name until you get into the product sections — took some scrolling to get there.
- Kids' back-to-school 25% off banner at the bottom is dead space for me — not relevant, and it's competing for attention with the styles I actually care about.
- No pricing shown on any specific shoe — I'd want to see a real number, not just "up to 40% off select styles" with an asterisk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "HANDS FREE SLIP-INS" section higher — that's my strongest hook and it's buried two-thirds down the email.
- 2. Cut one of the athletic/yoga model shots from the top collage and replace it with a guy my age out walking — right now the hero image skews younger than the product mix underneath it.
- 3. Put an actual price or "$/off" figure on at least the Arch Fit or Cozy Fit shoes instead of leaving it all to "see below for details."

# Technical Email Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an SFMC/Salesforce Marketing Cloud-sent promotional email using standard MJML-style responsive markup; the QA pass reports a 100% pass rate with 12 warnings, all related to insecure (`http://`) image sources and missing `alt` attributes.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 30 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for SFMC `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect wrappers — not itself a defect, but it means link destinations are unverified in this pass).
- Multiple tracking pixels present: SFMC open pixel (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?...`), two Krux/Salesforce DMP user-match beacons, one Krux ad-impression beacon, a Return Path deliverability pixel, and an `ink1000.com` engagement pixel — six total 1x1 tracking calls before any content renders.
- The `ink1000.com` pixel is served over **`http://`** (`http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?...`), unlike the other tracking beacons which use HTTPS. Mixed-protocol tracking increases the chance this specific call is blocked by mail clients enforcing TLS-only subresources.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text** on 2 images: `o.gif` (ink1000 tracking pixel — acceptable since it's non-content) and `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png`, which is a **content image** (per QA metadata, distinct from the labeled nav/social icons that do have alt text). This one should be fixed — it's not decorative.
- **Non-HTTPS image sources** (10 instances), all served from `image.emails.skechers.com` over `http://` rather than `https://`: main Skechers logo, a content banner, App Store/Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, AfterPay, Find a Store, and Instagram/Facebook/YouTube social icons. Many modern mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) will block or flag mixed-content/non-TLS images, meaning these assets — including the brand logo — may fail to render for recipients.
- Extensive responsive CSS (multiple breakpoints at 375px/480px/640px/680px/768px) with mobile/desktop content toggling via `.mobile-content`/`.desktop-content` — structurally standard for SFMC templates, no defects observed in the truncated markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, AMPscript `%%=...=%%`) are visible in the truncated HTML provided. No issues found — but note this section is not fully verifiable since the source was truncated before the body content and footer.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
The provided HTML is truncated before reaching the footer/unsubscribe block, so **physical address, unsubscribe link, and list-management footer cannot be confirmed** from this source — this is a coverage gap in the audit, not a confirmed violation. Authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) are not present in the provided data (headers weren't included) and cannot be assessed. Sending domain (`emails.skechers.com`) is a properly delegated subdomain, consistent with standard DKIM/SPF alignment practice, but this is inferred, not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Campaign identifier `mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NONPUR_U_BEST_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_08092026` is consistently applied across the ink1000 pixel and the Krux ad-impression beacon (`campaignid=MKG_US_NONPUR_U_BEST_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_08092026`), indicating consistent internal campaign tagging. However, since all 30 click-through links were skipped by the probe, **UTM parameters and landing-page destinations on actual CTAs could not be verified** in this pass.
