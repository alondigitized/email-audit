## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The provided source and QA data show no hard failures, but the tracking-pixel markup has real defects. One pixel URL has an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` token and another ends in an invisible character. The HTML was truncated and no headers were supplied, so compliance and most link checks can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe skipped:** 28 links on `*.emltrk.com` were not probed ("Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Their destinations and redirect health are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Malformed query:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261010_skechers_birkenstock_HEYDUDE_AM`
  - The value of `t=` is itself `utm_campaign=…`, so a nested `=` sits inside a single parameter. A strict parser reads `t` as `utm_campaign=SCA_…`, and the campaign value may not be recognized as UTM data.
  - The URL ends in a zero-width space (U+200B) after `_AM`. It is invisible in the markup but can corrupt the campaign value or break matching in the tracker.
- **Unresolved token:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]`
  - The literal `[UNIQUE]` was not substituted before send. Whether it is a per-recipient token or just a placeholder, the sent HTML still contains it, so the parameter carries no recipient or message value.
- **Campaign name:** `utm_campaign=SCA_20261010_skechers_birkenstock_HEYDUDE_AM` names other brands, while the sender is Shoe Carnival. This may be a template or campaign-ID carryover.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** QA flagged both `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` and `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` images as having no `alt`. If they are 1×1 pixels, they should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Neither tag takes effect as written. The charset attribute still works.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and self-closing tags. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Link styling:** the global rule `a, a:link, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` removes default link color and underline from all links. Unless the body re-styles them (not visible in the truncated source), links lack a non-color affordance.
- **Mobile font size:** `.responsive-text` is forced to `12px !important` at max-width 600px, which is small for body copy.
- **Media-query breakpoints:** the breakpoints (600px, 660px, 450px) overlap and are inconsistent. This is minor.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"` is set, and the viewport meta tag is present.

- The truncated source showed no other visible tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`). The rest of the body was not available to check.
