## What's working

- The hero shoe is big and clean, and the "Shop now" button is easy to see and tap.
- The eight shoes are in a two-column grid with large photos. I can read the names without squinting.
- The 1080v15 Pykrete is in there. That's a cushioned walker I'd actually look at.
- The look is clean and matches the brand I know, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- I came for walking shoes, and there's no word about cushioning, arch support or comfort anywhere.
- The shoes have no prices and no descriptions. I'd have to tap through every one to learn anything.
- Half the grid is flashy or fashion-y: pink, peach and a purple race shoe. The SuperComp Elite is a racing shoe, not something for my feet.
- The "Complete the look" block has two young models and graphic tees. That's not me.
- The 25% off is in tiny text up top and buried at the bottom. The fine print is also tiny and full of exclusions, so it doesn't feel quite honest.
- The "Visit a store" button next to "Shop now" splits my attention.

- 4. Cut the clothing and paper-bag blocks, or move them below a men's walking section.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The send has no QA failures (88% pass rate, 0 issues, 1 warning). The source does carry template leftovers (a placeholder background URL, a "New Balance Template" title) and invalid CSS/meta markup. Three images lack alt text, and the plain-text part is almost entirely tracking URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text bloat (QA WARN):** 12,658 of 14,519 characters (about 87%) of the plain-text part are URLs. This suggests long redirect-wrapped links (`link.email.newbalance.com/...`) with little readable text. That raises spam-filter risk and makes the text part hard to use.
- **Placeholder URL in CSS:** `.bgwhite {... background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` is an unresolved placeholder. It is also plain HTTP, so it can trigger a mixed-content request or a 404 in any client that applies the class.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 26 skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The QA reports no broken links among the probed set. The skipped 26 are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_..._23280554.gif` is the open-tracking pixel. It is expected, but it has no alt attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 3 images):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `egR1f966kc_818512772_12364579_2197562_23280554.gif`, the tracking pixel. It needs `alt=""`.
  - The two shipping images are content images that screen readers will read as filenames or skip. If they carry shipping-offer text, that text is lost when images are blocked.
- **Invalid `@font-face` rules:** Every `@font-face` block (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, etc.) has no `src` descriptor, so none can load a font. One block also repeats `font-family` inside itself (`proxima-nova ... font-style: italic;font-family: proxima-nova,sans-serif;`). Text falls back to Arial or Times New Roman unless the fonts are loaded elsewhere, and that part is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `.bg1` is defined twice with different values (`#ffffff`, then `#58546d`), and the later one wins. `.bgwhite` and `.bg1` are also redefined inside the dark-mode block.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an `xmlns` attribute and XHTML-style self-closing tags. It's tolerated, but it is inconsistent.
- **Dark-mode support:** `color-scheme` meta and `prefers-color-scheme` rules are present, and a dedicated dark banner asset (`ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`) exists. The truncated source doesn't let me check dark-mode contrast.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The only unresolved placeholder is `XXXXX` in the `.bgwhite` URL (section 2). The `<title>` is the template default, "New Balance Template", which is a leftover and not a merge issue.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found in the data provided. The QA reports 0 failures, and an unsubscribe link is always included in probing. The footer, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe`/SPF/DKIM/DMARC headers are not in the truncated source or QA output, so they are unverified rather than confirmed good.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found in the data provided. UTM parameters and destination URLs are hidden behind the `link.email.newbalance.com` redirect wrapper and are not visible in the supplied source. One minor observation: the image assets sit under `/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/`, a welcome-series path, in a "New arrivals" email. This points to reused assets, so it's worth confirming they are intended for this campaign.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or replace `http://image.XXXXX.png` in `.bgwhite`, and use HTTPS if a background image is needed.
3. Fix or remove the `@font-face` blocks. Add real `src` URLs, or drop them and rely on web-safe stacks.
4. Replace `<title>New Balance Template</title>` with the campaign title, and correct the `IE=edge` meta to `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
5. Deduplicate the `.bg1` rule.
6. Generate a purpose-written plain-text part, with readable copy and fewer wrapped URLs, to clear the 87% URL warning.
7. Verify the 26 unprobed links, the `List-Unsubscribe` and authentication headers, and the UTM structure behind the redirect wrapper.
