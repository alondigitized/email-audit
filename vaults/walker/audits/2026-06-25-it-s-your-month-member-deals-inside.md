# It's your month — member deals inside
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- I tapped open this one expecting something worthwhile — it's from Skechers Plus, which I've been a member of for a while, and "your month" in the subject made me think they were finally going to treat me like a loyal customer. And inside? The BOGO 50% off deal is real and legible, and there's a chance to win a $250 gift card. Fine. But here's my problem: I opened this email and the first thing I'm looking at is a woman in a flowy dress next to some sandals. I'm a 62-year-old man shopping for cushioned walking shoes, not a sundress catalog. The "SHOP WOMEN'S" button comes before the men's section — I have to scroll past ladies' styles just to get to whatever gray sneaker they threw in the men's slot.
- The offer itself is solid — I'm not going to pretend otherwise. A BOGO 50% off plus a 20% standalone discount is something I'd actually use, and Member Appreciation Month is a nice framing for a loyal subscriber. But Skechers keeps missing the easy layup: lead with comfort language, show me a male model in a pair of Go Walk's, and put men's first. I'm not looking to guess whether the deal applies to the style I actually want.
- And I've got to say it again because I've been saying it for eight straight emails now: that preview text is still broken JSON garbage. I recognize the sender, so I opened it anyway — but if I were a new subscriber, I'd have swiped it into the trash before I ever saw the BOGO deal.
- I'd clip the coupon. I wouldn't forward this to my buddy Dave — I'd just tell him about the sale if he asked.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off + 20% off deal is front and center, easy to read at phone-screen size — no squinting required.
- "Member Appreciation Month" framing is a nice loyalty hook; makes me feel like a VIP instead of just a name on an email list.
- The $250 gift card sweepstakes is a clever bonus — doesn't dilute the main offer, sits cleanly above it.
- "DEAL #2" badge suggests a series, which makes me wonder what Deal #1 was and whether I missed something — mild but effective engagement pull.
- Men's shoes section IS present, even if it's not lead position — the gray sneakers in that block look like a walking shoe I'd consider.

## What's weak

- Female model is the hero of this email. I'm a 62-year-old man. Wrong demographic front and center.
- "SHOP WOMEN'S" leads before "SHOP MEN'S" — I'm scrolling past stuff I'd never buy just to get to my aisle.
- Not one word about comfort, cushioning, arch support, or fit in the entire visible email — that's the only reason I'm looking at Skechers in the first place.
- That preview text is still broken JSON — eighth email in a row with this issue. I counted. Whoever's running their email tech has a real problem and nobody seems to be fixing it.
- No end date on the BOGO offer that I can see. "Your month" is vague — does this expire June 30? Midnight tonight? I don't like having to guess.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken preview text.** This has been wrong since early June. At this point it's costing them open rates from cold subscribers and it's embarrassing for a brand this size. It's the single highest-leverage fix in the whole operation.
- 2. **Lead with a male model in a comfort walking shoe.** Or at minimum, swap the section order: put SHOP MEN'S before SHOP WOMEN'S. I shouldn't have to scroll to find myself in my own member appreciation email.
- 3. **Add comfort copy to the hero.** Something like "Cushioning you can feel — BOGO 50% off" gives me a reason to care about the deal before I see the shoe.
- 4. **Give the BOGO offer a hard end date.** "Ends June 30" creates real urgency. "Your month" is soft and easy to defer.
- 5. **Make the sweepstakes CTA work harder.** "ENTER TO WIN" is fine, but what's the prize? "$250 to spend on the pair you've been eyeing" is one line of copy that makes the prize feel personal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: Buy One, Get One 50% Off — men's styles too`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your appreciation deal: BOGO 50% off this month only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, enter to win a $250 Skechers gift card before the month ends`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned styles, half off your second pair — shop men's picks first`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound with standard ESP table-based layout and responsive CSS, but has a widespread HTTP (non-HTTPS) image hosting problem affecting 13+ assets and a third-party tracking pixel loaded over plain HTTP — both of which will cause broken images in security-enforcing clients (Gmail, Outlook.com, Apple Mail with image blocking).

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **26 click links** route through `click.emails.skechers.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect). Normal for SFMC; cannot probe destinations, so final-URL UTM coverage is unverified (see §6).
- **Open pixel** at `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?33A6ZNIQTN5UFDPGB4WM5X7PNI...` — HTTPS, no issue.
- **Krux/Salesforce DMP match pixels** (`beacon.krxd.net`) — loaded over HTTPS, but present. These are identity-matching beacons (subscriber ID `121173391` is exposed in plain text in the source as `partner_uid`). Verify this is intentional and compliant with the privacy policy disclosed to members.
- **Return Path seed pixel** `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef...` — loaded over **HTTP** (no scheme prefix = relative, or implicit HTTP). This will be blocked by clients that enforce HTTPS-only mixed content rules.
- **Ink1000 tracking pixel** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif` — explicit **HTTP**. Will be blocked in Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail when image proxy enforcement is active.

### 5. Compliance

- **CAN-SPAM**: Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source — presence must be confirmed in the footer. The QA summary reports 100% pass, suggesting the checker found them, but evidence is not in the provided excerpt.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 26 destination links are behind the SFMC click-redirect domain (`click.emails.skechers.com`). UTM parameters on final landing pages cannot be confirmed from the source alone. Recommend spot-checking at least the primary CTA link to verify parameters like:

The campaign ID `PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_LOYL_EN_06182026` appears consistently across tracking pixels, which is a good sign for attribution consistency — but the SFMC redirect strips referrer, so UTM must be appended to final URLs, not just the redirect wrapper.
