# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom shoe, front and center, again. That's now seven, eight times this week I've flagged this exact sneaker, and I want to be clear one more time: I did not click on it, I did not linger over it, I have never once looked at a floral shoe with intent to buy. "We noticed you noticing us" — no, Skechers, you didn't notice anything, you just keep mailing me the same cart-abandon template with a shoe that was never in my cart to begin with.
- That said, buried down in the "more options" row there's actually something for me — the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman-Murdock and a men's arch fit option nearby. That's the first sign of life all week that somebody in that email system knows I exist as a 62-year-old guy who wants a slip-on with support, not a floral sneaker for my daughter. Too bad it's scroll number six on the page, after five other product tiles that have nothing to do with me.
- This is a mark-as-read, delete, and honestly getting close to unsubscribe territory. The 40% off banner at the bottom is the only thing worth a second look, and even that gets buried under a wall of unrelated inventory.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF SELECT STYLES" banner is bold, easy to read, and I know exactly what it means.
- Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman-Murdock shown in the grid — that's an actual slip-on comfort shoe in my size and style wheelhouse, first time I've seen my category surface all week.
- "SHOP NOW, PAY LATER" with Afterpay/Klarna and "FIND A STORE" — clean, simple footer options if I did want to go look in person.
- No broken images, nothing overlapping — it renders clean even if the content is wrong for me.

## What's weak

- That pink floral D'Lites is the hero image again — I've now seen this same shoe more times this week than I've talked to my grandkids, per my own count from Friday, Saturday, and every day since.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button sits under a shoe I never ordered — that's not a reminder, that's a made-up memory.
- The "more options" grid is mostly women's and kids' shoes — Women's Arch Fit Arcade, two boys' Garage Sneakers, women's BOBS Skip Cute — none of that is me, and it's five tiles before I get to one that is.
- No mention of arch support, cushioning, or hands-free fit anywhere in the copy — the one product that fits my needs gets a photo and a name, nothing else.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop leading with the floral D'Lites for a subscriber who's never clicked it — swap the hero for the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit or an Arch Fit men's style, since that's clearly in my browsing history somewhere.
- 2. Move the 40% off banner up near the top, right under the headline, instead of making me scroll past six product tiles to find the real offer.
- 3. Segment the "more options" grid by gender/interest instead of showing me kids' and women's shoes I'll never buy.
- 4. Add one line of comfort-focused copy under the men's shoe — "hands-free slip-on, built-in arch support" — since that's the actual reason I'd click.
