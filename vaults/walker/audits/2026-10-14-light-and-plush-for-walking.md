## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links, UTM tagging and Cordial/Mailgun infrastructure look structurally sound. Three problems are confirmed: no plain-text part, 13 images with no alt text, and head-level HTML defects. The 406/403 link failures are probably the QA prober being blocked, not broken links, but that isn't confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures, 12 of 12 links:**
  - 11 links return 406 and the Unsubscribe link returns 403. All are Cordial redirect URLs of the form `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6ac665d1cc1a0f11c209697f:ot:69e560485cc95da09e001cd9:1/<hash>?jwtH=…&jwtP=…&jwtS=…`.
  - Every probed link failed, and all are on the same redirect host. That points to bot or WAF filtering of the QA user-agent, not 12 independently broken links.
  - The jwtP payload decodes to valid destinations, for example `linkUrl=https://www.hoka.com/en/us/walking-shoes/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…`.
  - No real-browser or redirect-chain check was run, so the final landing status is unverified. The 26% pass rate overstates breakage.
- **Link identity:** several links are labelled by image filename, for example `[image: US_20261014_WalkingFeature_a.gif]`. Links `_a`, `_f`, `_g` and `_h` all resolve to the same `/walking-shoes/` URL, and `_i` adds `prefn1=width&prefv1=Wide|X-Wide`. Each has its own tracking hash, but none carries a `utm_content` to tell placements apart (see §6).
- **Link text:** the anchor text `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/.` includes a trailing period inside the link text.
- **Duplicate tracking pixels:** the source shows two open-tracking images, `https://e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/1664:…` (Cordial) and `https://mg.emails.hoka.com/o/eJyM…` (Mailgun). These double-count opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 13 images are flagged. Eleven are content images, such as `US_20261014_WalkingFeature_a.gif`, `_f`, `_g`, `_h`, `_i`, `US_Arahi9_TopLeft_1176570-ALK.jpg`, `US_Arahi9_TopRight_1176531-CRDS.jpg`, `US_Clifton11_BottomLeft_1176573-SLWL.jpg`, `US_Clifton11_BottomRight_1176572-GLCT.jpg`, `US_Q3_ShoeFinder_Blue.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. The other two are the tracking pixels.
  - Most of the content images are the only content inside their links. They have no accessible name, so screen readers and image-blocked clients fall back to the filename.
  - The two tracking pixels are harmless, but `alt=""` is still correct for them.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare the charset.
- **Conflicting viewport tags:** two `<meta name="viewport">` tags are present. One sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on iOS. The other sets `user-scalable=yes`.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values. They are ignored, so they have no effect.
- **Global reset:** `* {…border:none;outline:none…}` removes focus outlines for keyboard users.
- **Web fonts:** `TheFutureHOKA` is loaded from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…`. The font-family fallback stack was cut off in the truncated source, so I can't verify it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`. The source is truncated, so the footer and body weren't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This is a confirmed deliverability and accessibility defect. Missing multipart/alternative text is a spam-scoring signal.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `info@emails.hoka.com`, and tracking is on `e.`/`mg.emails.hoka.com`, which suggests aligned sending subdomains. That is unverified.
- **Unsubscribe link:** present, pointing to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`. The 403 is probably bot gating, so it needs a manual click test. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided, so one-click support is unverified.
- **Physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so it is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** consistent on all decoded links. `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261014_Walking_STO_A120Day_…` are present on the destination URLs, along with `hmail=<hash>` and `cordial_id=…`.
- **Missing `utm_content`:** it is absent, so the multiple links to `/walking-shoes/` can't be told apart in analytics.
- **Campaign slug:** it is about 200 characters long and embeds internal segment names (`Oct24AnniPurchasers_Oct25AnniPurchasers`, `AIRFMMedium`), which expose audience segmentation in the URL.
- **Product deep links:** these carry the right `dwvar_*` color parameters, for example `…/arahi-9/1176570.html?dwvar_1176570_color=ALK&dwvar_1176570_size=7&dwvar_1176570_width=Regular`. The women's Arahi 9 link hardcodes `size=7`, so check that this is intended.
- **Landing pages:** none could be verified. Every probe was blocked, so I can't confirm the pages load or that the products are in stock.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart plain-text alternative.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (product name and color for the product tiles, destination for the banners), and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Re-verify the 406/403 links with a real browser user-agent or a manual click test, including the Unsubscribe flow. Whitelist the QA prober if it is being blocked.
4. Fix the head. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, fill in `<title>`, remove the duplicate viewport tag and drop `maximum-scale=1`. Delete the empty-value CSS rules.
5. Restore focus outlines for links and buttons in the global reset.
6. Add `utm_content` per placement (hero, tiles, shoe finder, footer).
7. Shorten the campaign slug and move audience segment names out of the URL.
8. Check the raw headers for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). Also confirm the footer has a physical address.
9. Consider removing one of the two open-tracking pixels.
