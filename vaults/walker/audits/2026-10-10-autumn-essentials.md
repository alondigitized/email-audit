- The "up to 30% off select styles" line at the top, plus the "Shop the sale" block at the bottom, tells me there's money to save.
- The 9060 is in the shoe grid. That's the chunky cushioned one I liked in the October 5 email.
- The text is big and the layout is a plain single column. Easy to read on my phone.
- The "Shop clothing" button is black and clear, and I know what it does.
- The New Balance logo and the clean look feel like the same brand I've been getting mail from.

## What's weak

- The hero is a young man in clothes. I came for shoes, and the shoes are small and tucked at the bottom.
- The models are all young guys and teenagers. Nobody looks like me.
- The fine print says the sale ends October 13 and that exclusions apply. That's the only deadline, it's tiny, and I'd likely miss it.
- The shoe grid has names but no prices, and nothing about arch support or cushioning. "950" and "T500" tell me nothing.
- The subject "Autumn essentials" is vague. Fleece and vests aren't something I'd call an essential for my feet.

- 3. Add prices, or "from $X," under each shoe, plus one comfort word each, like "cushioned" or "all-day walking."
- 4. Add a "Shop walking shoes" button. There's a "Walking" link at the very bottom, and it deserves more room.
- 5. Show a few older faces, so a 62-year-old feels the email was meant for him.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The template has leftover placeholder code (`<title>New Balance Template</title>`, a `http://image.XXXXX.png` background URL), three images without alt text, and a plain-text part that is almost entirely tracking URLs. The visible source shows no merge-token or link failures. The HTML is truncated and no headers were supplied, so the compliance and UTM checks below can't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Placeholder asset URL:** `.bgwhite {… background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` is an unreplaced template stub. It is also non-HTTPS, so it would trigger mixed-content blocking in clients that load it.
- **Plain-text bloat:** the QA flagged "Plain-text is >70% URLs: 12116/13873 chars" (about 87%). The long click-tracking URLs dominate the text part, which is a spam-filter risk and makes the part hard to read.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 38 links were probed, and 26 were skipped because they were footer, utility or social links. No broken links were reported among the probed ones. The skipped links are unverified.
- **Asset path reuse:** images load from `.../custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`, a welcome-series folder, in an "Autumn essentials" send. This works, but it shows shared assets and may be a maintenance risk if that folder is changed or purged.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12411699_116614_23384876.gif`. This is probably the open-tracking pixel, so it should have `alt=""`. The other two are content images and need descriptive alt text.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="…">` has none, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE is paired with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute and self-closing tags. Most clients tolerate this, but it is inconsistent.
- **Empty `@font-face` rules:** the rules for `linotype-didot`, `proxima-nova` and others have no `src`, so they are no-ops. Text falls back to `Arial` or `Times New Roman`. `ITC Garamond Book Condensed` is also unquoted in `@font-face`.
- **Duplicate selector:** `.bg1` is defined as `#ffffff` and then as `#58546d`. The later rule wins and overrides the intended white.
- **Dark-mode link contrast:** `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { a {color:#cf0a2c !important;} }` on a `#151415` background works out to about 3.3:1, below the WCAG 4.5:1 minimum for body text. It also overrides link colors on any anchor, including white-on-dark CTAs. The truncated source doesn't show whether it hits buttons.
- **Generic `<title>`:** "New Balance Template" is a leftover from the template.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The only placeholder is the `XXXXX` image URL in section 2. The truncation means the body wasn't fully checked.

Not verifiable from the data provided:
- No headers were supplied, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` can't be assessed.
- The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and raised no failure, but the footer and physical address aren't in the truncated source.
- The sender `newbalance@email.newbalance.com` and the tracking domain `link.email.newbalance.com` share the `newbalance.com` organizational domain, which is good for DMARC alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible, because links go through the `link.email.newbalance.com` redirector. The QA reported no landing-page failures for the 12 probed links, but it didn't report destination alignment or UTM presence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace or remove `url('http://image.XXXXX.png')`. If a background image is needed, use HTTPS.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking GIF and descriptive alt text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, fix the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`, and set a real `<title>`.
4. Remove the duplicate `.bg1` rule and the empty `@font-face` blocks, or add `src` values for them.
5. Check the dark-mode `a {color:#cf0a2c !important}` rule. Scope it to text links and use a lighter red that reaches 4.5:1 on `#151415`.
6. Shorten the plain-text part with fewer or shorter URLs, or send a cleaner text alternative, to bring the URL ratio down.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers to check the footer, unsubscribe, physical address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and UTM parameters.
