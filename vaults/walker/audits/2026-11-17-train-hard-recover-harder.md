# Train hard. Recover harder.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- Another day, another HOKA email. This is my seventh in a row since the 9th, and I'm starting to feel it. It's a pretty picture, but I can't tell what the shoe is or what it costs.
- The middle is the useful part. "Call us soft" over four slip-on style shoes, including a men's Skyward Laceless. That's close to what I buy: hands-free, cushioned, easy on the foot. But no prices, no arch or cushion details, and the "Shop Now" button doesn't point to any one shoe. The bottom half is a free-shipping pitch that turns out to need a text-message signup. ## What's working

- The "Rest is half the job" headline is huge and legible on a phone, with no zooming.
- The laceless, slip-on shoes in the grid match how I shop: hands-free and soft.
- The men's Skyward Laceless is labeled "Men," so I know it's for me. The other three are all-gender or women's.
- The "Shop Recovery" button is a clear black pill, easy to see and easy to tap.
- Recovery and "soft" is a message I care about at 62, and it's a change from the running-shoe emails.

## What's weak

- The hero is a tight crop of a sole and mesh. I can't tell which shoe it is, and it doesn't link to one clear product.
- There are no prices, no cushioning or arch-support details, and no reviews. I'd have to tap through to learn anything.
- "Free Expedited Shipping*" is the loudest offer, but the fine print says it's only for new text subscribers on a first full-price order. That feels like bait.
- The "Shop Now" button covers all four shoes, so it's vague.
- This is my seventh HOKA email in nine days. Nothing here is urgent, and the cadence is wearing thin.
- The footer links ("Locate a Store," "Unlock Free Shipping," "FLY for 30 Guarantee") are in a different, plain font and look unfinished. The FLY for 30 guarantee is the most useful thing in the email, and it's buried down there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one real shoe, a Bondi or the Skyward Laceless, and put the price and a cushioning cue on it. "Easy on, easy on your feet" beats a close-up of mesh.
- 2. Link each shoe in the grid to its own page, and show the price and a star rating under each name.
- 3. Pull the FLY for 30 Guarantee up near the top. It's the trust signal that would get me to try a new shoe.
- 4. Drop the text-signup shipping offer, or make it honest and small. Don't make it the headline.
- 5. Ease off the cadence, or vary the content. Seven in nine days blurs together.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken hero link (a zero-width space in the URL path), no plain-text part, and widespread missing alt text. Three more links failed the probe (403/403/400), but those failures may be bot-blocking rather than real breakage. QA pass rate was 45%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed hero link (confirmed).** The `US_20261117_ActiveRecovery_c.jpg` link failed with "URL can't contain control characters". The JWT `linkUrl` payload decodes to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/recovery-comfort-shoes/ \u200b?utm_source=US_Broadcast&…`. There is a literal space and a U+200B zero-width space between the path and `?`. The authoring tool or template likely injected it. Clients may encode it as `/%20%E2%80%8B`, which would probably 404 or drop the UTM parameters. This is the "Recovery" CTA, so it is the highest-impact defect.
- **Unsubscribe link returned 403** (`…/ab2042a3`, redirecting to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`). Return-start (`…/bff8d3d8`, redirecting to `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`) also returned 403. Both are probably the prober being blocked by bot protection on the destination, because the Cordial tracking redirect itself is well-formed. I can't confirm this from the data. A manual check from a real browser is needed.
- **SMS CTA link returned 400** (`US_Q3_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`, redirecting to `https://hoka.attn.tv/p/ie7/landing-page?utm_…`). The cause is unclear. It may be Attentive rejecting the non-browser request, or the long UTM string. Verify manually.
- **Two tracking pixels** are present: `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` (Cordial) and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…` (a second sender domain). This is not a defect, but it shows two open-tracking systems in one message.
- **Coverage gap:** 13 links were skipped by the prober and are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images.** Ten are content images. Two are tracking pixels, where empty alt is acceptable.
  - Hero and content images: `US_20261117_ActiveRecovery_a/b/c.jpg`.
  - Product tiles: `CA_RestoreTC_TopLeft_1134532-CMCGR.jpg`, `US_ORAPrimo_TopRight_1141570-ATPR.jpg`, `US_SkywardLaceless_BottomLeft/BottomRight_*.jpg`.
  - Other images: `US_20261109_TripleBlackStyles_d.jpg`, `US_Q3_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`, `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - Several of these are linked images, so the links have no accessible name. The CTA text is baked into the images, so with images blocked the email is blank.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does not declare a charset.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags.** `maximum-scale=1` in the first blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). The second sets `user-scalable=yes`, and the two conflict.
- **Focus outlines removed.** The universal rule `* {…outline:none…}` hurts keyboard and assistive navigation.
- **Invalid CSS.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have no values. They are harmless but indicate an unfilled template.
- The HTML source was truncated, so the body markup could not be checked for alt attributes beyond the QA output.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA output. Tracking URLs carry resolved `hmail` and `cordial_id` values. Note that `CA_RestoreTC_*` (a Canada asset) and `US_20261109_TripleBlackStyles_d.jpg` (a Nov 9 asset) are reused in this Nov 17 US send. This is low confidence and worth confirming as intentional.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative** (0 chars). This is a deliverability and accessibility failure for a multipart message.
- **Authentication unverified.** `Authentication-Results` was not found because of the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a gap in the test setup, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe link is present** (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…`), but the 403 is unresolved (see section 2).
- **Not verifiable from the provided data:** the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address in the truncated footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** all links use `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261117_ActiveRecovery_STO_A30Day_HighestEngaged_365RecoveryTag_365RacePurchasers_270RaceTag_WishlistProductsTag_RecoveryPLPBrowsers_WishlistAdd`. There is no `utm_content`, so the hero, tile and CTA clicks can't be told apart in analytics.
- **Segment data in the URL:** the `utm_campaign` value embeds audience-segment tags such as `365RacePurchasers` and `WishlistAdd`. These are visible to the recipient and to any downstream analytics tool.
- **Hero landing page:** the recovery URL's malformed path (section 2) risks a broken landing or loss of attribution.
- Extra parameters `hmail` and `cordial_id` are appended to the destination URLs. Confirm they are intended to reach the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the hero link.** Strip the space and U+200B after `recovery-comfort-shoes/` in the Cordial template, and add a pre-send lint that rejects control or zero-width characters in hrefs.
2. **Add alt text** to all 10 content images, especially those carrying CTA text. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. **Generate a plain-text part** in Cordial.
4. **Manually verify** the unsubscribe, return-start and attn.tv links in a real browser. If the prober is being blocked, allowlist it or use a browser-like User-Agent so these checks stop producing noise.
5. **Confirm authentication.** Inspect raw headers on direct delivery to check SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`.
6. **Template cleanup:**
   - Fill in `<title>`.
   - Fix the charset meta (`http-equiv="Content-Type"`).
   - Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Remove the `outline:none` reset.
   - Fix the empty `px` CSS values.
7. **Add `utm_content`** per placement, and move audience-segment tags out of `utm_campaign` into internal-only parameters.
