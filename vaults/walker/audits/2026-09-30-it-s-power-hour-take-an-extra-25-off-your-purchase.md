## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a legacy table layout with mobile media queries. The automated QA reports 88% pass, 0 failures and 1 warning. The source shows two tracking-pixel problems: an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder, and a `utm_campaign` value that doesn't match this send.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved token in pixel URL:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still contains the literal `[UNIQUE]`. Opens may not be attributed per recipient.
- **Zero-width character in pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20260930_MWK_Best_sellers__AM​` ends with an invisible U+200B. Some clients or proxies may encode it into the query value or drop the request.
- **Malformed query:** `t=utm_campaign=...` nests a second `=` inside the `t` parameter value. It is unencoded, so a parser may read it differently than intended.
- **47 tracking links not probed:** all are `*.emltrk.com` redirects, so the QA could not check destinations, status codes or redirect chains.
- **Third-party click domain:** links and pixels use `emltrk.com` while the sender is `email.shoecarnival.com`. A branded tracking domain is not evidenced in the source I have.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on two images:** both `emltrk.com` tracking pixels have no `alt`. This is low impact since they are pixels, but `alt=""` is the correct form.
- **Links styled away:** the global rule `a, a:link, a:visited, ... { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` removes link styling. Any text link without inline colour or underline won't be distinguishable as a link. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Small mobile text:** `.responsive-text` is forced to `font-size:12px !important` at `max-width:600px`, which is below the usual 14px minimum.
- **Malformed meta tags:**
  - `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **Mixed doctype and namespace:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the `<html>` tag carries `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. It is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Inconsistent breakpoints:** the CSS mixes 600px, 660px and 450px. The 600px `.responsive-text` rule and the 660px layout rules leave a 601–660px band where they disagree.
- **Five-plus `<style>` blocks:** I can't assess Gmail's 102KB clipping because the HTML was truncated and its total size is unknown.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved placeholder:** `?d=[UNIQUE]` in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel URL (see section 2). No other unrendered tokens appear in the visible portion of the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found in the evidence provided. The QA raised no compliance failures. The unsubscribe link, physical address and SPF/DKIM/DMARC headers were not in the truncated HTML or QA output, so I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign tag mismatch:** the tag is `utm_campaign=SCA_20260930_MWK_Best_sellers__AM`. The subject is "IT'S POWER HOUR: Take an extra 25% off your purchase." The tag reads like a reused best-sellers AM template, so this send's revenue may be attributed to the wrong campaign.
- **Double underscore in the tag:** `Best_sellers__AM` suggests an empty segment or variable in the naming convention.
- **Destinations not verified:** click URLs are wrapped in redirects that were skipped, so I can't check whether UTMs survive to the landing page or whether the promo is on the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Resolve `[UNIQUE]` in the pixel URL at send time, and add a pre-send check that fails on any literal `[...]` or `{{...}}` in URLs.
2. Strip the U+200B from the `ieezzy5m` pixel URL and URL-encode the `t` parameter value (`t=utm_campaign%3D...`).
3. Correct `utm_campaign` to match the Power Hour send and check the naming template for the empty variable that produced `__`.
4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
5. Fix the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. Restore underline or colour on in-body text links, or set them inline, so the global `text-decoration:none !important` doesn't hide them.
7. Raise the mobile `.responsive-text` size to at least 14px and align the 600px and 660px breakpoints.
8. Re-run the QA on the full HTML, since it was truncated, to cover the unsubscribe link, headers, total size and click-through destinations.
