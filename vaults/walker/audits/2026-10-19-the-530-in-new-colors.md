## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (click-wrapped `link.email.newbalance.com` URLs) with no hard QA failures (0 issues, 2 warnings, 75% pass rate). It carries leftover template artifacts, three images without alt text, and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs. The HTML source is truncated, so body-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 of 42 links and skipped 30 (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). No broken probed links were reported, but the skipped links are unverified.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 13,348 of 15,240 plain-text characters (about 88%) are URLs. This comes from long wrapped tracking URLs, and a ratio that high can hurt spam scoring.
- **Placeholder URL in CSS:** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` is an unreplaced placeholder. It is also non-HTTPS, so it can trigger a failed request or a mixed-content warning wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12469089_868931_23513536.gif` is the ESP open pixel. This is expected, but it has no alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `egR1f966kc_...gif` (the tracking pixel, low impact, though an empty `alt=""` is still correct)
- **Reused asset path:** The two shipping images come from `/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`, a welcome-email folder. This suggests template reuse, and I can't tell from the truncated source whether the shipping content applies to this send.
- **Font declarations:** The `@font-face` blocks (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and others) have no `src` descriptor, so they load nothing. Rendering falls back to Arial or Times New Roman. One rule also has a malformed duplicate `font-family` declaration (`font-style: italic;font-family: proxima-nova,sans-serif;`).
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv` attribute, so it does nothing.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE is combined with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute and self-closing tags. This is not harmful in practice, but it is inconsistent.
- **Generic title:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is the template name, not the campaign. It shows in web-view and some client contexts.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags and a `prefers-color-scheme` media query are present. No defects were found in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The placeholder `XXXXX` in the CSS is a URL stub, not a merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a tooling limitation and not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA always probes an unsubscribe link, and none was flagged as broken. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not reported either way. I can't verify the physical postal address because the footer is truncated.
- **Sender:** The From address is `newbalance@email.newbalance.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, so the sender identity is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but I can't confirm UTM usage. No destination URLs or query parameters are visible in the truncated source, and all links go through the `link.email.newbalance.com` redirector. Whether UTM parameters survive the redirect to the landing pages needs a probe of the final destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the two shipping images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or replace the `http://image.XXXXX.png` background in `.bgwhite`.
3. Replace `<title>New Balance Template</title>` with the campaign subject.
4. Give the `@font-face` rules a `src`, or remove them and rely on the Arial fallback. Fix the duplicated `font-family` declaration.
5. Fix the `IE=edge` meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`, or drop it.
6. Shorten or simplify the plain-text part to reduce the URL ratio.
7. Rerun QA against the full HTML and the raw message headers. This would confirm `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, the postal address and the final UTM parameters, and would probe the 30 skipped links.
8. Confirm that the shipping banner belongs in this send and isn't a leftover from the `myNB_Welcome` template.
