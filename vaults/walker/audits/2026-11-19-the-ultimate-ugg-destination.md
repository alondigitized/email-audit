# The ultimate UGG destination ➡
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and UGG again. That's the third UGG send in four days, after "Oh joy! Up to 30% off UGG 🎁" and "Up to 30% off UGG is on!". The big coral "30% OFF*" is easy to read on my phone without my glasses, and I'll give them that. But this is the same banner I've been looking at all week. Nothing here is new.
- The subject promises "the ultimate UGG destination," and what I got is the same 30% off boots and slippers. The asterisk on that 30% sends me down to fine print at the bottom. All six products are fuzzy boots and slippers. The one thing I might wear is the slip-on scuff, and there's no price, no cushioning talk and no arch support anywhere. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward this to anyone. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 30% OFF*" is huge and I can read it at a glance, even at my age without reading glasses.
- The black "SHOP UGG" buttons are big and easy to tap.
- The size grid (5 to 11) and the "Shop Wide Width" button are a nice touch for someone who needs room in a shoe.
- The "Shopping Made Easy" row (free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup) is plain and useful.
- The DSW look matches what I see in the store and on the site, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- It's the same UGG pitch I've seen three times this week, so I'm tired of it.
- There are no prices on any of the six products. "Up to 30% off" doesn't tell me what I'd pay.
- Everything is women's. The grid has no men's styles, and the "Shop All Women" button sits right where a men's link should be. The size grid is all women's sizes too (5 to 11).
- Fuzzy boots and slippers aren't what I'm after. I want cushioning, arch support and slip-on walking shoes.
- The "Designer Shoe Wonderland" logo text looks jumbled, and it overlaps in the header.
- The 500 bonus points need a purchase of UGG or Birkenstock, and the exclusions are tucked into tiny type at the bottom. Points won't decide my purchase anyway.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and show men's styles to men. I'm a 62-year-old man, and a men's slip-on or a Tasman-type shoe up top would have gotten my attention.
- 2. Put real "now" prices on the products, like "NOW $89." That's what moved my score up on the 11-18 send.
- 3. Add a men's category link next to "Shop All Women."
- 4. Say something about comfort (cushioning, arch support, slip-on).
- 5. Space the UGG sends out. Three in four days trains me to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
One confirmed defect: the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" tracked link returned HTTP 400 in the QA probe. Two missing-alt warnings on tracking images and a link-heavy plain-text part are lower severity. The HTML source was truncated, so checks beyond the QA data are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400).** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/9Tfco_taYaSQBBpLB6GBiQ~~/AAQRxRA~/mFavN4fGk…` (Braze/SendGrid-style click wrapper).
  - The probe saw a 400 from the redirector, so the SMS opt-in CTA may not resolve for recipients.
  - The QA data can't tell a bot-specific rejection from a malformed or expired token. Manually click-test it from a real client.
  - The wrapped URL is about 1,000 characters long, which makes truncation or wrapping damage in the plain-text part plausible. This is unconfirmed.
- Only 12 of 59 links were probed (47 skipped by the click-likelihood ranking). The other links are unverified, not confirmed good.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 2 images.** Both look like tracking pixels, so the impact is low:
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/IM2ylss0gfKBJSYaY1rzSw~~/AAQRxRA~/RyLgYlWs…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261119_P261744_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_U`
  - Add `alt=""` (and ideally `role="presentation"`) so screen readers skip them.
- **Plain-text part is >70% URLs** (21,615 of 25,748 chars). The long tracked URLs dominate the text/plain alternative, which is a mild spam-filter signal and poor for text-only readers.
- **Viewport and dark mode.** The viewport meta, `color-scheme`, and dark-mode `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` blocks are present. I saw no defects in the visible markup.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv` either. Neither tag does anything as written. The `utf-8` declaration is not honored, though the transport charset normally covers it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`). The `mi_u=225090073` value in the pixel URL is a rendered numeric user ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status can't be verified. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it's a limitation of the check, not a sender defect. Verify with the raw headers.
- The QA harness always probes the unsubscribe link, and it reported no failure. The source was truncated before the footer, so the physical postal address and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`, so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters can't be seen in the source. UTM completeness and landing page alignment are unverified.
- The tracking pixel carries the campaign ID `mi_ecmp=20261119_P261744_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_U`. Check that it matches the destination-side campaign tagging.
- Based on the link text, the 400 link (SMS opt-in) is the only CTA confirmed not to land.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or replace the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/9Tfco_…`). Click-test it from a real mail client, then re-probe it. If it only fails for bots, whitelist the probe.

3. Shorten the plain-text version by using shorter link wrappers or fewer links. Add readable labels next to the URLs.

5. Pull the raw headers to confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` and the presence of `List-Unsubscribe`.
