# Only a few days left to BOGO…
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Same darn BOGO 50% off DSW's been running all week — I called it before, and here it is again, just with "urgency" bolted on this time ("Only a few days left"). I scroll down expecting maybe a men's shoe to show up somewhere in the mix, and no — it's clogs, mary janes, wedge sneakers, leopard print heels, a heeled boot, and one lonely pair of white sneakers that look like they belong to my granddaughter, not me. There isn't a single item in this whole email I'd put on my feet.
- The "SHOP BY SIZE" section at the bottom made me laugh a little — sizes 5 through 11, no half sizes past 11, no wide options up top before you scroll forever. That's not sized for a 62-year-old guy who wears an 11 or 12 wide. This is a women's shoe email wearing a "New Arrivals" costume, and I'm marking it read without a second thought.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off headline is legible and the deal itself is a real dollar-value offer, not a gimmick.
- "Shop by Size" and "Shop Wide Width / Wide Calf" buttons show DSW does think about fit, even if the product grid above doesn't reflect it for me.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup, and Afterpay icons are clear and reduce friction if I were actually going to buy.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown is a women's shoe — heels, clogs, mary janes, leopard print — nothing for a man, let alone a comfort-first guy like me.
- "Hey, what's new?" headline promises new arrivals, but it's the same BOGO wallpaper I've seen in "Weekend plans" (9/3), "Fall Lookbook" (9/3), and "STARTS NOW" (9/3) — just relabeled again.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, or slip-on ease anywhere — nothing that speaks to how I actually shop for shoes.
- The urgency in the subject ("only a few days left") isn't backed up by any visible countdown or date in the email body itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment men's vs. women's sends — if DSW has my browsing history, put men's comfort shoes (walking, slip-on, cushioned) in the hero, not clogs and leopard heels.
- 2. Put an actual expiration date on the BOGO deal in the body, not just vague "a few days left" language in the subject.
- 3. Cut the redundant fifth "SHOP BY SIZE" grid and instead show one men's hero product with real comfort details (arch support, cushioning).
- 4. Vary the creative — five near-identical BOGO sends this week is fatigue, not persistence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort shoes: BOGO 50% off ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: BOGO 50% off before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-on comfort, cushioned support — buy one, get 50% off the second`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends this weekend: mix and match any two pairs`
