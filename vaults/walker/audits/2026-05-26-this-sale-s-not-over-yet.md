# This sale’s not over yet
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Well, HOKA's in my inbox again — and now we're at "This sale's not over yet," which is exactly the kind of subject line that makes me roll my eyes a little. I've gotten — what — eight or nine of these in the last two weeks? The "Ending Soon Summer Kickoff Sale" headline is fine and the hero text is readable, but there's no shoe in the picture, no price, no percent off. Just legs and water and a "Shop Sale" button that sends me who-knows-where.
- The second block — "Free Expedited Shipping" — is actually the more interesting offer to me, but I have to sign up for emails to get it, and HOKA, buddy, I'm already getting your emails. That's the whole problem.
- I'm not clicking. I'm not forwarding. I'd mark this read and move on — and honestly, after this many sale reminders with no specific shoe and no specific discount, I'm one more send away from hitting unsubscribe.

## What's working

- The "Ending Soon" tag at the top creates a little urgency without screaming at me.
- "Summer Kickoff Sale" headline is big and readable on my phone without zooming.
- The free shipping callout is a real, concrete offer — I noticed it.
- Layout is clean, not cluttered, easy on the eyes.

## What's weak

- No shoe. No model name, no price, no "% off." Just a vague "snag savings" line — that's not how I shop.
- The hero image is somebody's legs in water — what does that have to do with walking shoes for a guy like me?
- The free shipping is gated behind a newsletter signup I'm already on. That's insulting.
- "Shop Sale" is the laziest CTA copy in the business — tell me what's on sale.
- This is the ninth-ish promo in two-plus weeks. I'm numb. The May 17th plated rundown and May 12th cushion email were the only ones that actually spoke to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a shoe in the hero — Clifton, Bondi, Transport — with a real price and a real % off. The May 21st "Clifton 10: Now on Markdown" send was your best one this month because it showed me a shoe I recognize.
- 2. Kill the newsletter-signup gate on free shipping. If I'm reading this email, I'm already signed up. Just give me the shipping.
- 3. Replace "Shop Sale" with something specific — "Shop Men's Walking Sale" or "See Clifton Markdowns."
- 4. Slow your cadence. Nine emails in two weeks for a guy who hasn't bought yet means I'm tuning out, not warming up.
- 5. Add a real end date. "Ending Soon" without a date is the boy who cried wolf.
