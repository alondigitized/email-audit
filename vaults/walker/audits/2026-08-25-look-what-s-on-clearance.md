#  Look what’s on clearance 👀
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Alright, "up to 60% off clearance" got my attention right off the bat — that's a real headline, not the usual "$10 off" coupon math these DSW emails keep serving up. And the size grid — 5 through 11 — sitting right under the headline, that's exactly what I want to see before I bother scrolling further. But then it turns into a whole different email: suede clogs, ankle strap heels, retro sneakers, tall boots, all on women's feet and legs. I don't wear any of that, and there's not a men's shoe in sight after the top nav.
- So it's a mixed bag — decent open-worthy headline, useless body. I'd click through to "shop by size" if I were already looking, but nothing after that first fold is going to make me tap anything else. Not forwarding this one, and honestly it's the same story as the last several DSW sends — good bones, wrong audience for the actual product shots.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off clearance" is a concrete, honest-sounding headline — no fine print buried in it that I can see.
- The size-by-number grid (5–11) right up top is genuinely useful — saves me a click to find my size.
- "Clearance in stores is always 30% off" tells me I can just walk in, which I appreciate more than any online-only gimmick.
- Nike callout at the very top ("Up to 30% off select Nike styles") at least mentions a brand I actually wear.

## What's weak

- Every product shot after the size grid is women's footwear — clogs, heels, suede sandals. Nothing for a guy like me.
- "One Word: Suede" fall lookbook has zero relevance to comfort/slip-on/arch support, which is the only thing that gets me to buy shoes.
- The "MEN" nav link up top is the only nod to me in the whole email — never referenced again.
- No mention of Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, or New Balance — brands I actually cross-shop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the send — a men's clearance version with men's comfort/walking shoes, not a one-size-fits-all lookbook that's 90% women's product.
- 2. Put a men's comfort brand (Skechers, Hoka, New Balance) in the hero instead of suede clogs — that's the shoe that gets me to tap.
- 3. Keep the size grid — that part's good — but link it to men's sizing, not a generic "shop by size" that dumps me into women's clearance.
- 4. Drop "fall trends" filler like retro sneakers/tall boots for my segment; swap in "men's clearance under $50" instead.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, 60% off: men's clearance is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers, Hoka & more — arch support included`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-on comfort, marked way down`
