## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders via standard responsive MSO/webkit table markup with mobile/desktop breakpoints, but ships a broken personalization token and nine HTTP (non-HTTPS) asset URLs, several missing `alt` attributes.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 30 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped automated HTTP probing (redirect domains not resolvable via static check) — manual click-through QA needed to confirm destinations resolve.
- Five tracking pixels fire on open: `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx` (SFMC open tracking, `d=100229`), two Krux/Salesforce DMP user-match beacons (`beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif`), one Krux ad-impression beacon (`campaignid=PRO_US_NA_U_SELECTEXTRA_FULL_EN_09142026`), and a Return Path deliverability pixel (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif`) — note this last pixel's `src` is protocol-relative/missing scheme (`pixel.app.returnpath.net/...` with no `http(s)://` prefix), which will fail to load in strict mail clients.
- Additional tracking pixel via `ink1000.com` served over **http://** (see Rendering section) — mixed-content risk and likely blocked in HTTPS-only mail clients (Gmail, Apple Mail).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **9 images served over `http://`** instead of `https://`, spanning the header logo, hero/product image, app-store badges, footer nav icons, and social icons (all under `image.emails.skechers.com` plus the `ink1000.com` tracking pixel). Non-HTTPS assets are commonly blocked or generate mixed-content warnings in Gmail/Outlook/Apple Mail, risking broken images across the template.
- **2 images missing `alt` text**: the `ink1000.com` open-tracking pixel and `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` (an unlabeled content image, likely hero/product art) — impacts screen-reader users and image-blocked fallback rendering.
