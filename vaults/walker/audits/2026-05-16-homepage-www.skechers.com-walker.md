# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Now I have a clear look at the page. Writing the review in Walker's voice.
- Right away I see Skechers is pushing their Hands Free Slip-ins — and I'll be honest, that product name catches my attention because that's exactly what my feet need after a long day. There's a solid BOGO deal sitting at the top of the page, which is hard to ignore. But those two models in the hero look like they're heading to a pool party at 28, not picking up something comfortable for a walk around the neighborhood — doesn't feel like they're talking to me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner:** That blue bar at the very top stops the scroll before it starts. Applied at cart means no code hunting.
- **"Hands Free Slip-ins / Glide-Step" headline:** This is the product I actually came here for. Hands-free entry is a real feature I care about, not marketing fluff.
- **SHOP NOW button:** Large, white, full-width — on a phone screen this is exactly the right treatment. Easy to tap with a big thumb.
- **Men / Women / Kids' / Recently Viewed nav strip:** I can get to Men's in one tap. No digging through a hamburger menu.
- **Spring Apparel Sale secondary banner:** Two deals stacked — BOGO on shoes AND 25% off apparel. Feels like a real sale day, not a placeholder.

## What's weak

- **Hero models:** A young guy in shorts and a young woman in a sundress. I get the lifestyle angle, but I don't see myself anywhere on this page. Skechers has older customers — show me one.
- **SHOP NOW goes nowhere specific:** That button sends me to… what? Men's Slip-ins? The whole site? Ambiguous destination makes me hesitate before tapping.
- **No loyalty hook:** I've bought from Skechers before. Where's my points balance, my member price, anything that says they recognize me? Not a single mention of Skechers Elite above the fold.
- **No free-shipping callout:** The BOGO is front and center but I have no idea what the shipping situation is. That matters when I'm comparing to ordering the same shoe off Amazon.
- **No trust signals:** No review counts, no "free returns," nothing that makes me feel safe about buying. This feels like it assumes I already trust them.

## Recommendations

- **Swap one of the hero models for someone 55+** doing something low-key — walking a dog, heading to a farmers market. Slip-ins don't scream "poolside in your 20s," they say "I want to leave the house without bending over." Show that.
- **Make the CTA destination explicit:** Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Men's Slip-ins" or at minimum split the button into Women / Men so I know where I'm landing.
- **Add a one-liner below the BOGO banner:** Something like "Free shipping on orders over $50 · Free returns" — two facts, one line, done.
- **Surface the loyalty program:** Even a small "Skechers Elite members earn 2X points this week" line in that secondary banner strip would signal that the brand knows its repeat customers.
