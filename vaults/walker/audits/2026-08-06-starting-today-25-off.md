# STARTING TODAY: 25% OFF!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- My eye went straight to that big "25% OFF" number — DSW knows how to make a percentage look impressive on a phone screen. But then I scrolled and every single shoe in that "So New, So You" grid is a woman's heel, wedge, or strappy sandal. Not one boot, slip-on, or walking shoe a guy my age would look twice at. This is the fourth day running DSW's hit me with some flavor of "up to X% off" — yesterday it was "40% off select Nike, adidas, Birkenstock," the day before was the FOMO alert, and now it's a back-to-school wrapper on basically the same brand list (Nike, New Balance, Crocs, adidas, Birkenstock again).
- The header banner even leads with "Birkenstock: Up to 30% off," then the hero says 25% off, then further down it's 40% off "big-deal brands" — three different numbers in one email is the kind of thing that makes me squint instead of trust it.
- I'm not clicking this one. It's a school-shopping email for moms buying wedges and sandals for their kids, not a retiree looking for something cushioned to walk the dog in. Straight to "mark as read," and I sure wouldn't forward it — nothing in here is worth telling a buddy about.

## What's working

- The 25% off number is huge and unmissable — no hunting for the deal.
- I recognize the sender immediately; DSW's black-and-white look is consistent with every email they've sent me this week.
- The "Shop by Size" grid and Wide Width / Wide Calf buttons are a nice touch for anyone who actually needs fit help.
- The DSW VIP loyalty box is clearly laid out — 5% back, free shipping, birthday gift, join free.
- No broken images or garbled layout that I can see — it renders clean top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Every product photo is a women's heel, wedge, or dress sandal — nothing here says "comfort" or "men's" to me at all.
- Three conflicting discount numbers in one email (30% header, 25% hero, 40% brands block) makes the offer feel muddled, not generous.
- This is at least the fourth "percent off" email in four days from this brand — after yesterday's 40% off Nike/adidas/Birkenstock pitch, today's just feels like a repackage.
- "Big-deal brands" list (Nike, New Balance, Crocs, adidas, Birkenstock) is the exact same lineup as yesterday's FOMO email — nothing new for me to consider.
- The subject line's all-caps "STARTING TODAY" plus the exclamation point reads a little spammy in my inbox list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the list — send me a version where the hero image is a men's comfort shoe, not six women's heels. I'll never click past that first grid.
- 2. Pick one number and stick with it. Don't run 30%, 25%, and 40% in the same email — it makes me trust the deal less, not more.
- 3. Ease off the cadence — four "% off" emails in four days is fatigue, not urgency. Space these out or vary the format.
- 4. If there's a men's angle at all, put it above the fold, not buried in a four-item nav bar.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New markdowns on men's comfort styles — 25% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort: 25% off starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, and on sale — see what's new for men`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `25% off men's walking shoes, in stores and online today`
