# Ends Tonight! 30% off select boots, slippers, & outdoor
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- Finally, something besides the skull sneaker. After a week of cart nags for a cart I never filled, this one at least has a real offer. It also has "Hi, Walker" and a member bar with a points balance at the top. That tells me they know who I am.
- The trouble is that there are two sales stacked on top of each other. The big green hero says 20% off sitewide for members. A little further down, a blue block says 30% off boots, outdoor and slippers. The subject only mentions the 30%. I had to scroll to figure out which deal was which, and I'd bet most people give up before that. The "Ends Tonight" subject also sits oddly with the fine print, which says the 20% runs through 11/26. The 30% runs only through 11/10, so the urgency is real for that one but not for the hero.
- Would I click? Probably, yes. Slip-ins and Cozy Fit are exactly my kind of shoe. I'd tap the slippers or slip-ins tile and see whether the 30% shows up in the cart. I wouldn't forward it, since it's a sale email and not something to pass along to a friend.

## What's working

- "Hi, Walker" with the member bar and the 1000 points ("enough for a $5 reward") treats me like a known customer. I don't let points make my decision, but it's a nice touch.
- The hero numbers are huge. "20% OFF" is readable on my phone without zooming.
- The Slip-Ins and Cozy Fit tiles speak to my hands-free, cushioned needs.
- The 30% block is easy to read, with big blue numbers and one "SHOP NOW" button.
- The men's size buttons (Under 7.5 up to 12+) are big enough for my thumb.
- The "Discount auto-applied at cart" line is honest and saves me from hunting for a code.

## What's weak

- The 20% and 30% offers compete. I can't tell which deal applies to what without reading the fine print.
- "Last Chance" in the hero and "Ends Tonight" in the subject don't match the 11/26 end date in the fine print. That feels like a pressure tactic, and I trust a brand less when the email contradicts itself.
- The photos show boots and slippers that look women's, plus a couple of unisex-looking shoes. Nothing here is clearly a man's walking shoe or slip-on.
- The hero gift art is cute, but it takes up a whole screen before I see a single shoe.
- The fine print is tiny, and I'd need to zoom to read the exclusions.
- The "20% members only" deal says I must be logged in to a Skechers Plus account, and that's easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Either put the 30% boots-and-slippers deal first, since it's the one with the deadline, or put the 20% in the subject and drop the "Ends Tonight" claim.
- 2. Put a men's comfort product up top, like a slip-in or a Cozy Fit walker, with the price and the sale price.
- 3. Add a men's category tile and a short line on arch support or cushioning, since that's what I shop for.
- 4. Make the deadline in the fine print match the headline, or only say "Ends Tonight" about the offer that actually ends tonight.
- 5. Move the size buttons under a specific product so they lead somewhere.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience bug (an empty greeting merge field) and several tracking-pixel defects visible in the source: a scheme-less returnpath URL, two `http://` images and missing alt text. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients will resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Return Path/Validity seed tracking never fires. It also has no `alt`.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_11102026` is plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by proxies and clients, which means lost measurement.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is plain HTTP and may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Unverified links:** 46 tracking links (e.g. `click.emails.skechers.com/…`) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity is unconfirmed.
- **Third-party beacons:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels are hidden with `display:none` and `width/height="0"`. Two of them carry identifiers, `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00` (a 64-hex value, likely a SHA-256 hash) and `partner_uid=121173391`. This is a privacy and deliverability consideration, not a functional break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (ink1000) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` have no `alt` attribute. For the content PNG, screen readers will announce the file name and image-blocking clients will show nothing.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declaration is valid or effective.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Text-size lock:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables iOS text-size scaling.
- **Language and roles:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute. The visible layout tables have no `role="presentation"`, though the HTML is truncated so later tables are unchecked.
- **Preheader:** The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none !important`), with no issue found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` because the first-name field is empty. This is a visible broken experience, and the template has no fallback value such as "Hi there,". The QA check does not say whether the cause is missing subscriber data or a missing default in the AMPscript/merge logic.
- No unresolved raw tokens (e.g. `%%first_name%%`) were found in the visible portion of the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a testing-path limitation, not a confirmed sender failure. Verify against the original message headers.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** The footer, unsubscribe link, List-Unsubscribe header and physical address are not in the truncated source, so they cannot be assessed.
- **From address:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address. That is not a CAN-SPAM violation, but it gives recipients no direct reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Links are wrapped in `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects, which were skipped by the probe, and the truncated source shows no resolved destinations or UTM parameters. The only campaign identifier visible is the pixel parameter `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_11102026`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Merge fallback:** Add a default for the first-name field, or suppress the greeting when it is empty, so `"Hi ,"` never renders.
2. **Pixel URL:** Change the returnpath pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…` and add `alt=""`.
3. **HTTPS:** Switch `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` assets to `https://`. If `ink1000.com` doesn't serve HTTPS, replace or remove that pixel.
4. **Alt text:** Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels and descriptive alt text to `dde00662-….png`.
5. **Meta tags:** Fix them to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
6. **Accessibility:** Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables, and reconsider `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`.
7. **Audit follow-up:** Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to check the unsubscribe link and address. Inspect the raw headers for SPF, DKIM, DMARC and List-Unsubscribe. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm landing pages and UTMs.
