# Skip the guesswork. Shop these best-sellers
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Skechers again — that's got to be the sixth email from them in my inbox this week, and I'm starting to feel like I'm on some kind of automatic drip instead of getting an actual sale notice. The "Skip the guesswork" line made me think they'd actually pick something for me, but what I got was the same three-way split I always get: comfort, fashion, performance, with a woman in a crop top and jeans leaning on a wall, a guy dancing under nightclub lights, and a woman jogging by the NYC skyline. Not one of those is me, and not one of those shoes gets more than a sentence of actual product info.
- The one thing that did catch my eye was the plain "UP TO 40% OFF SELECT STYLES" banner right at the top — that's a real number, no games, and I could see it before I even had to scroll. The "Shop Tax Free" banner further down was a nice practical touch too, that's the kind of thing that actually helps my wallet math.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already looking for shoes — the 40% off is real enough to pull my thumb. But forward to a friend? No chance. This is a catalog blast, not something that speaks to me, and after this many nearly identical sends this week I'm getting numb to the "Shop the Sale" button entirely.

## What's working

- The top banner leads with a real number — 40% off, no hunting for it.
- "Shop Tax Free" / "Find Your State" is a genuinely useful, practical call-out for someone watching the total price.
- Layout is clean top to bottom, no broken images or overlapping text that I could see.
- There's a dedicated "Men's Best Sellers" button up top, so at least they tried to route me somewhere.
- The comfort-tech shoe pair (burgundy and grey) actually looks like something I'd wear.

## What's weak

- Every hero model is a young woman or a guy dancing in a club — none of that is me, and it makes the whole email feel like it was built for somebody else's inbox.
- "Most Comfortable," "Most Fashionable," "Most Technical" — three vague buckets with no actual fit, cushioning, or arch-support detail, just "cozy" and "innovative."
- This is the same brand blasting me for the fourth or fifth straight day — after "2 for $100 Ends Tonight," the cart emails, and the back-to-school one, I'm tuning it out.
- No mention of my name, my size, or anything I've actually looked at — feels like a mass mailer, not a store that knows me.
- No urgency anywhere — no deadline, no "ends tonight," so there's nothing pushing me to act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the send frequency — I don't need six Skechers emails in a week; space it out and I'll actually open the next one instead of skimming past it.
- 2. Put a man my age, or at least a men's walking/comfort shoe, front and center instead of three lifestyle shots aimed at a younger crowd.
- 3. Add one real product detail per section — arch support, memory foam, slip-resistant sole — something I can actually weigh against a Hoka or Brooks.
- 4. Give the 40% off a deadline. "Ends Sunday" beats an open-ended "up to 40% off" every time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort favorites — up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, restocked — 40% off this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, arch support — the styles guys actually re-order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tax-free in participating states. Sale ends Sunday.`

# Technical Email Audit: SKECHERS "Skip the guesswork" Campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) email with standard multi-client responsive markup and heavy third-party tracking instrumentation; the primary technical issues are non-HTTPS image/pixel sources and two images missing alt text, while compliance and personalization elements fall outside the truncated HTML provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **31 tracking/click-redirect links were not probed** (QA skipped these as `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect domains), so destination URLs and final landing pages cannot be verified in this audit.
- Multiple tracking pixels fire on open, from at least 3 distinct vendors:
  - SFMC open tracking: `https://click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?AV63ZSUNS7VEFEOTD2EWU26LKY.100225&d=100225&bmt=0`
  - Oracle/Krux (krxd.net) — 3 beacons: 2 user-match pixels (`sha25` and `subid` partner keys) + 1 ad-impression pixel (`confid=so3yexmpm&advertiserid=100007688&campaignid=MKG_US_NONPUR_U_BEST_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_08062026`)
  - Return Path deliverability pixel: `pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499`
  - ink1000.com engagement pixel: `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NONPUR_U_BEST_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_08062026`
- **Bug not caught by QA:** the Return Path pixel `src` is missing a URL scheme entirely — `src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?..."` instead of `src="https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?..."`. Most email clients will attempt to resolve this as a relative path against the render context rather than an absolute host, meaning this tracking pixel likely fails to load in practice. Recommend adding the missing `https://`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Two images are missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed):
  - `o.gif` (ink1000.com engagement pixel — low impact, tracking-only)
  - `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` (content image, `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/.../m/11/...` — user-facing, should have descriptive alt text for screen readers and blocked-image fallback)
- Markup uses XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype and Outlook/legacy-client conditional CSS patterns (`.ExternalClass`, `mso`-style resets implied, `-ms-interpolation-mode`), consistent with standard ESP-generated multi-client compatibility — no issues here.
- Three overlapping responsive breakpoints are defined (`max-width:375px`, `max-width:640px`/`max-device-width:640px`, `max-width:680px`, plus a duplicate `max-width:480px` block in a second `<style>` tag) with duplicated selector rules (`.hidden`, `.legal`, `.mobile-pi`, etc. repeated verbatim across the 375px and 640px blocks). This is redundant but not functionally broken.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No AMPscript (`%%...%%`) or personalization strings are present in the provided HTML (head, preheader, and tracking-pixel block). The visible source is truncated before the main content/offer blocks, so merge-token usage in the body (e.g., name personalization, product recommendations) could not be verified — flagging as **unverifiable from available data** rather than confirming absence.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- No raw SMTP/MIME headers were supplied, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header presence cannot be assessed from this audit.
- **Recommendation:** re-run this audit against the full HTML payload (through `</html>`) and raw headers to confirm CAN-SPAM footer requirements and authentication compliance — these are currently open, not passed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identifier `MKG_US_NONPUR_U_BEST_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_08062026` is consistently used across the Krux ad-impression beacon and the ink1000.com pixel (`mi_ecmp` param), indicating consistent internal campaign tagging at the tracking-pixel level.

## 7. Recommendations

2. Convert all `http://` image/pixel sources to `https://` (9 flagged: 1 ink1000.com pixel + 8 `image.emails.skechers.com` assets including logo, nav icons, and payment/social badges) to avoid mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-rendered clients.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `49468f73-4651-4af3-bea2-61d1ae5db486.png` (content image); alt text on the tracking pixel is optional but harmless to add.
4. Consolidate the duplicate/overlapping `@media` breakpoint blocks (375px vs 640px vs 480px) to reduce redundant CSS.
5. Re-audit with full HTML (including footer) and raw email headers to verify CAN-SPAM unsubscribe/address compliance and DKIM/SPF/DMARC authentication — currently out of scope of the data provided.
6. Sample-resolve a subset of the 31 skipped `click.emails.skechers.com` redirect links to confirm final landing URLs carry aligned UTM parameters.
