# A lil something for you: 20% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this is the fifth 20% pitch in about a week. It's the same TINSEL code I got on the 5th and again earlier today in "Treat yourself to 20% off + top gifts >>". The "20% OFF*" up top is big enough to read without squinting, and I'll give them credit for that. Then the email gets cute with a free "Squishy Butter" for VIPs. I'm 62 and I had to read that twice to work out it was a squishy toy shaped like a stick of butter.
- I scrolled the whole thing and the shoes are sneakers, boots, slippers and heels. There's a woman's leg in a skirt and fuzzy socks. I didn't see a single man's shoe, and nothing about cushioning or arch support. The "Shop by Size" with Wide Width is a nice touch, but it's buried near the bottom.
- Would I click? Probably not. Forward it? No. I'd mark it read. If I were buying, I'd compare against Brooks and New Balance and I wouldn't need a code to do it.

## What's working

- The big "20% OFF*" and black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button are easy to read and tap on my phone.
- The TINSEL code is printed right in the hero, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons near the bottom tell me they know feet come in different sizes.
- The size grid is large, so I can tap a size without fumbling.
- The footer lists free shipping, in-store pickup and Afterpay clearly. The pickup is handy for me.

## What's weak

- No men's product at all. The top nav has MEN, but the body is almost entirely women's. It doesn't feel like it was sent to me.
- The Squishy Butter takes up a whole banner, and it's for a toy I'd never want. It pushes the real shoes down the page.
- It's the same 20% and the same TINSEL code I've seen all week. It's no longer a special occasion.
- There's no comfort language anywhere: no cushioning, arch support or slip-on. The adidas Adimule and the New Balance pairs are just pictures with no details.
- The legal text at the bottom is a wall of tiny print. The asterisk on "20% OFF*" sends me down there to find the exclusions, and the offer is in-store and online, not both for every item.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one men's comfort shoe in the hero with the discount. Show a walking shoe or slip-on with a line like "Cushioned all-day walkers, 20% off."
- 2. Cut the Squishy Butter banner, or move it below the shoes. Lead with shoes, not toys.
- 3. Spell out the exclusions in plain words near the offer ("Excludes Skechers, Clarks, Nike") instead of leaving them in the fine print.
- 4. Add a men's comfort row with "Shop Men's Walking" and "Shop Slip-Ons" buttons. Put it above the "More for You" women's styles.
- 5. Vary the pitch. If it's the same code all week, give me a reason, like a last-day deadline.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wide widths and cushioned walkers included. Ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Braze-built XHTML template with dark-mode support. The automated probe found one failing link, the SMS opt-in CTA, which returned HTTP 400. Authentication and unsubscribe compliance can't be confirmed from the data provided, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] SMS opt-in CTA returns 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/4FpwJn0ZtQHSjquISqFM2w~~/AAQRxRA~/t-rYUVV3Cmq9a4A2N67109V-…` (about 1,000 characters).
  - The 400 came from the Braze/SendGrid click-tracking redirect (`ablink.dsw.com`), not from the destination page.
  - It could be a truncated or malformed token, or the tracker rejecting a non-browser probe. The data doesn't say which, so confirm with a manual click on a real delivery.
  - If the cause is a malformed token, the SMS opt-in CTA is dead for every recipient.
- **Long tracking URLs.** The QA check reports the plain-text part is 22,396 of 28,434 characters URLs (about 79%), well over the 70% threshold. The cause is long `ablink.dsw.com` redirect tokens. This is a spam-filter signal for a plain-text body, and the multipart text could be trimmed.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 61 skipped, so most links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Images missing alt text (2):**
  - The image at `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/qT5_4D6Wy-HtJKxfvIjQNA~~/…`.
  - The tracking pixel `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif`. Adding `alt=""` here is the right fix, since the pixel is decorative.
- **Meta tags without `http-equiv`.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`. The charset declaration and compatibility directive are therefore ignored, though the charset is typically also set in the Content-Type header.
- **Generic title.** `<title>DSW</title>` is the same for every send. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appear in the truncated HTML. The tracking pixel carries `mi_u=225090073`, which appears to be a resolved recipient ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** The QA check found no `Authentication-Results` header, because the AgentMail relay strips or doesn't expose it. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` can't be confirmed. This is a limitation of the test setup, not a confirmed defect.
- **Unsubscribe and address unverified.** The QA data doesn't show the unsubscribe probe result, a `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header, or the physical postal address. The footer is past the truncation point, so these need a check against the full source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be evaluated.** Destinations are hidden behind `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the visible source doesn't expose them.
- **Campaign ID visible only in the pixel.** The pixel carries `mi_ecmp=20261106_P261661_V1_W-_Offer_Occas…`.
- **Landing-page alignment is unverified.** The probe ran only against the redirect hop for the failing CTA, so the "20% off" promise couldn't be compared with the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Click the SMS opt-in CTA from a real delivery. If it returns 400 there too, regenerate the Braze link or fix the token.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and a descriptive `alt` to the image behind the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` URL.
4. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge" />`.
5. Reduce the URL density in the plain-text part, for example by using shorter link-tracking tokens.
6. Re-run the audit against the full HTML and raw headers (not the relay copy). That will confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the UTM parameters on the resolved landing URLs.
