# Samba delivers timeless style for school days
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- "Back to school" — I retired eleven years ago, my kids are in their thirties, and this one's got legs in cutoff shorts standing on a stone step. Flat suede sneakers, no arch support in sight, no cushioning claim, nothing about how they feel on your feet after an hour on them — just "effortlessly cool." That's not a phrase that's ever made me buy a shoe in sixty-two years.
- There's no offer here at all — no percentage, no price, just "Shop Now" pointing at a "best sellers" banner that could be anything. Compare that to yesterday's gift card deal at least had a number I could do math on. This one's pure lifestyle mood board, and I'm not the mood.
- I'm not opening this one twice, and I'm sure not forwarding it. Delete and move on — same as most of what adidas has sent me this month.

## What's working

- The banner text is legible and the "SHOP NOW" button is a real button, easy enough to tap.
- No broken images or overlapping text — it rendered clean.
- The Samba is at least a recognizable, actual shoe, not a jersey or a backpack.

## What's weak

- "Back to school" doesn't apply to me at all — I've got no kids in school and no grandkids they're shopping for through this inbox as far as I can tell.
- Bare ankles, cutoff shorts, kids on a stoop — nothing about this models my life or my feet.
- Zero comfort language — no cushioning, no arch support, no all-day-on-your-feet talk, nothing I actually shop for.
- No price, no percentage off, no member pricing — just vibes and a "Shop Now" that could lead anywhere.
- Huge dead white space in the middle of the email before the footer — feels unfinished, like half the email didn't load.

- 1. Drop the back-to-school angle for a segment like me entirely, or if adidas insists on sending me Samba emails, pivot the copy to comfort and versatility for everyday wear, not "the halls."
- 2. Put an actual price or discount in the hero — right now there's nothing to click toward.

- 4. Fix that blank space in the middle before the footer — tighten it up so it doesn't feel broken.
