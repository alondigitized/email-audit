# Your size is back in stock! Buy it before it’s gone again
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- "Your size is back in stock" got my thumb moving faster than usual — that's the kind of subject line that actually sounds like they're paying attention to me instead of blasting the whole list. Then I open it up and... size 4.5? I don't know whose foot that fits, but it sure isn't mine. That's not a "back in stock" alert for me, that's a system that saved somebody else's browsing session under my email address.
- The shoe itself, the Slip-ins Contour Foam in white/navy, actually looks like something I'd wear — hands-free entry, chunky cushioned sole, looks comfortable. If the size lined up I might've tapped Buy It Now just to price-check it. But the second I saw "4.5" the whole urgency angle fell apart. It's the same problem as that cart-abandonment email with the Care Bears sneakers — the tracking is off, and it shows.
- I'd mark this one read and move on. Not forwarding it, not buying it, but I'll admit the layout and the single hero product were cleaner than a lot of what Skechers sends me.

## What's working

- Clean, single-product layout — no clutter, no five competing offers fighting for my eye.
- The hero shoe (Slip-ins Contour Foam, white/navy) is a legit comfort-casual style, cushioned sole, slip-in entry — right in my wheelhouse.
- "BUY IT NOW" button is big, black, easy to find and easy to tap on my phone.
- "Arch Fit" and "Slip-ins" language show up in the related products, which is the stuff I actually care about.

## What's weak

- Size 4.5 on the product — that's not my size, not even close, and it kills the entire "back in stock for you" premise.
- No price shown anywhere — I can't do my price-vs-quality math without opening the site.
- The related products grid is six more shoes with zero context on why they're relevant — no "customers who liked this also liked" reasoning I can trust.
- No loyalty/Skechers Plus points mentioned even though I'm a member — feels like a generic blast, not a message to a known customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the size match before sending "your size is back in stock" — if you can't confirm my actual size, don't put a number in there at all, it just exposes the mismatch.
- 2. Show the price and any member discount right under the product — I shouldn't have to click through to do basic math.
- 3. Add a line noting my Skechers Plus status or points if I'm a member — I've been buying from them long enough that this should be trivial.
- 4. Tighten the "You Might Also Be Interested In" section to 2-3 picks with a reason ("similar to items you've viewed") instead of six with no explanation.
