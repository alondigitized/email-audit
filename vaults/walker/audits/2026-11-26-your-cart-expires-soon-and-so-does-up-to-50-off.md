# Your cart expires soon! (and so does up to 50% off)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Twelfth cart nag since 11/20, and my eyes glazed over before I got to the shoe. The subject at least tells me the sale ends, which is more honest than "Did you forget something?" from yesterday. I opened it, and the big red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" on black is the first thing I read. That's the same banner I saw on 11/23 and 11/24.
- The hero shoe is the GO WALK Glide-Step 2.0 Cozy Fit Walker, taupe and black, a slip-in that looks like a man's shoe. That's the third day running I've seen it, so it's starting to feel like they only have one idea. The six shoes below are a mixed bag. The three on top are men's slip-in boots and a slipper. The three on the bottom are women's sneakers, and they're a miss for me.
- Would I click? Maybe, because the Glide-Step is the kind of shoe I'd actually wear. I wouldn't forward it. If I'm shopping, I'd also check Hoka and Brooks before buying.

## What's working

- The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner is big and easy to read without zooming.
- The Glide-Step slip-in hero is a men's-looking walker, and it fits what I want: hands-free, cushioned, easy on and off.
- The row of men's Slip-ins (the Tanzier boots, the Murette slipper, the Respected boots) shows comfort styles I'd look at.
- The Skechers Plus "Extra 15% off everything" box tells me what members get.
- The "Shop now" buttons are large and easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- "Your cart expires soon" is a pressure line I've now seen a dozen times this week. It reads like a nag, not a real deadline.
- The email says "You liked these" but never shows a price, a size, or the sale price on the hero shoe. I have to click to find out what 30-50% off actually gets me.
- The bottom row is three women's sneakers, and none of it is for me.
- The fine print is tiny, and the two offer codes (BF2026 and CYBER) are buried at the very bottom. I'd have to hunt for them, and that makes me trust the discount less.
- The bottom of the email has too many extras: app download, texting, Afterpay, Klarna, store finder. None of it helps me pick a shoe.
- There's nothing about arch support or cushioning, and no reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the sale price on the hero shoe, for example "Was $85, now $59," with a one-line comfort note like "Arch Fit, cushioned, no bending to put on."
- 2. Make the deadline real and specific: "Ends Monday 12/1 at midnight." Stop saying the cart "expires" unless it truly does.
- 3. Show only men's shoes to a man who has been browsing men's slip-ins. Drop the women's row.
- 4. Put the Plus member code (BF2026) next to the 15% button, not in the fine print.
- 5. Cut the cadence. One cart email a day is plenty.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Glide-Step walkers: sale ends Mon 12/1`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking about those slip-ins? 40% off ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same easy slip-on comfort, now at sale price. Members take an extra 15%.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size may sell out before the sale does. See what's left.`
