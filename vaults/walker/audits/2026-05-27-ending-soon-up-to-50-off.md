# Ending soon! Up to 50% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again — another Crocs email, and if I'm counting right, this is probably the seventh or eighth one this week. That said, when I open this one, I see two classic clogs staring back at me — one red, one navy — and not a platform wedge or a Jibbitz-covered foam boot in sight. That alone keeps my finger off the delete key.
- The offer is real: "Up to 50% Off!" with tonight as the hard stop. I can work with that. But the moment I scroll down, Crocs loses me — there's a whole section pushing $3 charms and $7 Jibbitz packs, which means absolutely nothing to me. I'm not decorating my shoes like a birthday cake. The "Buy 2, Take 30% Off!" below that is at least functional, but the email never gives me one clear tap to make.
- I'd call this a "mark as read." The classic clog hero earns a look, but the charm clutter and the missing CTA button mean I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- **Classic clogs in the hero** — red and navy, plain, no studs or accessories. These are shoes I'd actually wear to the store or on a walk.
- **"Ending tonight!" is a real deadline** — more credible than a vague "limited time offer." I notice it.
- **50% off is a real number** — not some buried "up to 5% on select clearance" sleight of hand. That gets attention.
- **Clean render** — nothing broken, images all loaded, text is legible on my phone screen. After the mess of a preview text, at least the email itself shows up properly.

## What's weak

- **Preview text is dead air** — inbox showed me nothing before I opened. Just Crocs' name and a wall of blank. That's a wasted second line in my inbox.
- **Jibbitz section kills the momentum** — right when the hero image had me thinking about clogs, I hit a charm sale aimed at a twelve-year-old. Completely irrelevant to me.
- **No visible CTA button** — I see the offer copy but there's no tap-able "Shop Men's Styles" button anchoring the hero. I'm hunting for what to press.
- **"Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply"** — that asterisk energy makes me wonder if the pair I want is even in the sale. Won't know until I click through, which is friction.
- **Email fatigue is real** — Crocs has been in my inbox every single day. Even a decent send gets a skeptical eye at this frequency. Compare this to May 22nd's email, which actually earned a 7 partly because the frequency hadn't numbed me yet; by now, they've spent that goodwill.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Jibbitz section entirely** — replace it with one clean line about which men's classic styles are actually in the sale.
- 2. **Add a visible CTA button under the hero** — "Shop Men's Classic Clogs" in a solid, tappable button. Don't make me guess.
- 3. **Fix the preview text** — that's valuable inbox real estate sitting empty. One line reinforcing the deadline would close more opens than invisible unicode.
- 4. **Give me a floor price, not just a ceiling** — "Classic Clogs from $24.99" beats "up to 50% off" + exclusions every time. Show me the number I'm working with.
- 5. **Pull back on send frequency** — one well-targeted email per week would make each one matter more.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight only: Men's clogs up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Classic clogs, half off — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's classic styles on sale — tonight's the last night`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 50% off classic clogs. Sale ends at midnight.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed `<meta>` charset tag.**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
The `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute is missing. The correct form is:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
Without `http-equiv`, some legacy parsers will not apply the charset declaration and may misinterpret character encoding.

**Open-tracking pixel lacks `alt` attribute.**
```html
<img style="visibility:hidden;display:none" height="1" width="1"
     src="https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?...">
```
Should carry `alt=""` for strict HTML validity. Low severity but triggers validators.

**Custom font loading.** `TT Crocs` is loaded via `@font-face` at weights 400/700/900 from `media.crocs.com` (Cloudinary). No fallback stack is visible in the truncated source — confirm a web-safe fallback (`Arial`, `sans-serif`) is declared on body/table elements, as font loading is suppressed in most email clients.
