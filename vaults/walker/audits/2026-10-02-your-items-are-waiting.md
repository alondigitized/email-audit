## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two QA failures: the probed unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400, and the plain-text MIME part is empty (0 chars). The HTML also has about 12 `http://` image sources and several rendering and accessibility defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The link text is "Unsubscribe" and the URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…&attn_sid=23d8f9d8-…`.
  - The probe was automated, and Attentive tokenized links often reject non-browser requests. A 400 therefore doesn't prove the link is broken for real recipients, but it can't be treated as passing. It needs a manual click-through.
- **Two CTAs returned 429 (rate limited), not a failure.** These are "Complete you Order" and "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting...", both on `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. The links are unverified rather than broken.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** Only 12 of 37 links were probed, and 25 were skipped.
- **Click tracking is opaque.** All observed links go through Attentive redirect wrappers (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<token>`), so the final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Tracking domains are mixed.** There are four hosts: `msgs.skechers.com` (sender), `skechers.e.attentivemail.com` (clicks), `skechers.attn.tv` (unsubscribe), and `image.emails.skechers.com` (images). Clicks and unsubscribe run on vendor domains, not the sender domain, which weakens alignment.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.**
  - Eleven or more images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`. These include the logo (`dde00662-…png`), the "Complete you Order" hero (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), the product-recommendation image (`737031d9-…jpg`), and the footer app-store, Afterpay, and social icons.
  - Gmail proxies these, but other clients may block or warn on mixed content. The same host serves `https://` for `021bd784-…gif`, so HTTPS is available.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif`
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`
  - The Attentive open-tracking pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`). This one is minor; the pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- **Typo in alt text.** The hero image alt "Complete you Order" is missing the "r" in "your". Screen readers and image-blocked clients will show it.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute, so it doesn't declare the charset. The preheader's heavy use of `͏` and soft-hyphen padding characters makes a correct declaration matter more.
- **Present and fine:** `<html lang="en">`, a viewport meta tag, `mso-` table resets, and a responsive stack at `max-width:620px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source was truncated, so the footer and later blocks were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Empty plain-text part.** The QA check reports the text version at 0 chars. A missing multipart/alternative text part hurts deliverability and fails text-only and accessibility clients.
- **Unsubscribe link needs manual verification** (see section 2). It is present and labeled, but unconfirmed.
- **Not assessable from the data provided:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and the physical postal address. The HTML was truncated before the footer, so I'm not flagging these as failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs can't be verified.** The click wrappers hide the destination parameters, and the two cart CTAs returned 429, so their landing pages weren't resolved.
- **Cart-link target is unconfirmed.** The email promises "Your Items Are Waiting" and a cart restore. Whether the "Complete you Order" link lands on a populated cart can only be confirmed by following the redirect with a session.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text alternative part. The abandoned-cart items, CTA URL, and unsubscribe URL are the minimum.
2. Manually click the unsubscribe link (`skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?…`) in a real client to confirm it resolves, and confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and one-click headers are present.
3. Change all `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` sources to `https://`. The host already serves HTTPS.
4. Add alt text to `021bd784-…gif` and `a6ec5198-…jpg`, set `alt=""` on the open pixel, and correct "Complete you Order" to "Complete your Order".
5. Fill in `<title>`, and change the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`.
6. Re-run the link probe with throttling or retries for the 429 links. Resolve the redirects with a session to confirm the cart destination and UTM propagation.
7. Consider a custom click-tracking and unsubscribe domain aligned with `msgs.skechers.com` instead of the `attentivemail.com` and `attn.tv` vendor hosts.
