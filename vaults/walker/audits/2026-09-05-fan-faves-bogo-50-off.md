# Fan faves: BOGO 50% off✨
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Same darn BOGO 50% off DSW's been running every single day this week — I saw it Tuesday, Wednesday, and now Friday, and this one's slapped with "Fan Faves" like that means something. I scroll down through two pages of tall boots, cowboy boots, strappy heels, and mary janes before I even hit a men's sneaker, and by then I'm already checked out. There's one row of New Balance-style sneakers buried in the middle that could've been for me, but nothing says "men's" anywhere near it.
- Honestly this is the same email I've reviewed four times now with a new coat of paint. I'm not opening this one either — mark as read, keep scrolling.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off is a real, legible offer right at the top — no hunting for it.
- One of the sneaker rows (white/gray pair, looks like a New Balance style) is at least something I'd wear if I saw it standing alone.
- "Shop By Size" and "Shop Wide Width" near the bottom is a nice touch for guys like me who need wide.
- Free shipping for VIPs and buy-online-pickup-in-store called out clearly in the footer icons.

## What's weak

- Every hero image is women's footwear — tall boots, cowboy boots, strappy sandals, mary janes. Nothing says this brand knows I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants a slip-on walking shoe.
- "FALL'S BEST" section is wall-to-wall boots and heels — zero relevance to comfort or arch support, my whole reason for buying shoes.
- I have to scroll past six product blocks before there's even a hint of a men's-adjacent shoe, and it's not even labeled men's.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — just style categories (boots, sneakers, dress shoes, casual).
- This is the same BOGO deal I've now seen close to five times this week under five different headlines — the novelty's gone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if DSW knows I've browsed or bought men's shoes before, lead with a men's comfort/walking row, not six boots and heels first. That's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. Add a men's comfort category button next to "Shop Boots / Shop Sneakers / Shop Dress Shoes" — something like "Shop Men's Comfort" so guys don't have to dig.
- 3. Drop or at least de-emphasize the "Fall's Best" boot wall for male recipients — swap in walking shoes or slip-ons up top instead.
- 4. Vary the creative shell — I can tell this is the same underlying BOGO email retitled all week. Give it a real reason to open, like a specific new arrival or a men's-only cut of this deal.
- 5. Call out comfort features (cushioned insole, arch support) somewhere in the copy — that's what actually gets me to click, not "Fan Faves."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, half off the 2nd pair`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals in men's walking shoes, BOGO 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned, wide-width options now in stock`
