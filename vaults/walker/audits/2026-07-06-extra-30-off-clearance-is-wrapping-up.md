# Extra 30% off clearance is wrapping up!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again. I've been getting this same "Extra 30% Off Clearance" email from Crocs since the first of July — five days of the same sale, just with a new subject line each morning. Today's version adds "is wrapping up!" to create urgency, but at this point it reads like the fourth time I've been told to hurry up and buy something I've already had five days to think about. The brand trained me that there's always another version of this email coming, so the urgency doesn't land.
- That said, I opened it, and the layout is clean. The Baya clog in that creamy white is exactly the kind of slip-on I might grab on clearance — no laces, easy on and off. But there is no button anywhere in this email. Just a picture of a shoe floating on a green background and some copy underneath. On my phone, I'm supposed to figure out that the image is tappable? That's asking too much from me.
- The accessories pitch halfway down the email is completely irrelevant to what I came here for. I'd mark this read and probably not tap anything — unless the shoe image happens to be clickable and I accidentally discover it.

## What's working

- The "ONLY HOURS TO SAVE" urgency line with the hourglass emoji is visible immediately — I don't have to scroll to understand the deal
- The Baya clog is a genuine hands-free slip-on, which is my category, and the clean product shot shows it clearly without clutter
- "Extra 30% Off Clearance" is a specific offer with a real number — I know exactly what I'm getting
- The fine print at the bottom confirms a hard deadline (Monday, July 6 at 11:59pm MT) — that's more concrete than most brands bother with
- Layout is clean, no broken images, text is big enough that I don't need to squint

## What's weak

- No CTA button anywhere — the only apparent tap target is the product image, which is not obvious on a phone; I should not have to guess how to shop
- This is the same 30% off clearance sale I've been getting since July 1st — "wrapping up" means nothing when the sale has stretched five days already
- The accessories section ("40% Off Accessories!") is irrelevant to me — I'm not here for Jibbitz or bag clips, I'm here for a comfortable shoe
- The preheader is blank from the inbox — it shows nothing useful, so that second line of real estate in my inbox is completely wasted
- No comfort language at all — cushioning, arch support, lightweight, ease of wear — nothing that speaks to why I'd actually want this shoe

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a real CTA button** — a large, high-contrast "Shop Clearance Now" button under the hero image would make this instantly shoppable for anyone on a phone. This is the single biggest fix.
- 2. **Cut the accessories section entirely** — it dilutes the message and Walker-type shoppers aren't cross-shopping accessories in a clearance email. Put a second shoe in that space instead.
- 3. **Add one comfort detail to the hero copy** — even one line like "Slip-on cushioned comfort, now 30% off" ties the offer to the reason I'd buy.
- 4. **Acknowledge the repeat** — a line like "This is your last chance, really" with a hard "Sale ends tonight at midnight" would feel honest given the repeated sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, clearance ends tonight — your size may still be there`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last hours: slip-ons, clogs & comfort shoes on clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 30% off — sale ends tonight at 11:59pm. Don't leave it in your cart.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your clearance window closes tonight. Slip-ons from $X.`
