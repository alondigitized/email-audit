# Avoid the holiday rush
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one is a gift email, and I'm not shopping for gifts yet. The top of my phone screen shows a gray strip with the preview text, then the New Balance logo and menu. After that comes a big photo of a family on a couch unwrapping red presents. It's a nice picture, but there's no shoe in it and no cushioning. After the 10/21 and 10/20 bag-and-shoebox emails, this is another send where the first screen has no shoe on it.
- The 25% off line is there, but it's a thin red strip above the photo, and I almost scrolled past it. The headline, "It's never too early," has a lot of white space around it, and the buttons are black and easy to see. Further down there's a grid of six shoes. I'd have liked that grid at the top.
- I'd probably skim it and close it. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. If it had led with the 1080 or a walking shoe, I might have.

## What's working

- The "Up to 25% off select styles. Shop now" strip is plain English with a clear number, and the link is underlined.
- The "Shop now" button is big, black and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The shoe grid (9060, 204L, 983, 950, 1080v15 Pykrete, AC Runner) has shoes on clean gray backgrounds and labels I can read.
- The page is clean, with no broken pictures or overlapping text.
- The trade-in voucher, up to 25% of MSRP, is a real value idea. It's the kind of price math I like.

## What's weak

- The first screen is a family photo with gift wrap and no shoes. The 25% off is in tiny type above it.
- The gift guides (Men, Women, Kids, All) show models in hoodies and jackets, not footwear. The "Men" tile is a young fella in a sweatshirt.
- Nothing here speaks to walking, cushioning, arch support or slip-on comfort. The 1080v15 is the only cushioned shoe, and it's buried in the middle with no price.
- The "Essential gifts" shoes have no prices, no sale marks and no reviews. I can't tell what 25% off does for me.
- The dates (through October 29) and "exclusions apply" are in tiny fine print at the very bottom.
- It's the sixth New Balance email I've seen this week. "Avoid the holiday rush" in the third week of October feels pushy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a shoe. Put the 1080 or a walking model at the top, with the 25% off written on it in big type, not in a strip.
- 2. Show prices and "was/now" on the product grid, and add star ratings or a "comfort pick" label.
- 3. Put the end date (10/29) next to the offer instead of hiding it at the bottom.
- 4. Replace "Shop now" with something more specific, like "Shop walking shoes" or "Shop 25% off shoes."
- 5. Add a men's comfort and walking gift guide for the dads and grandpas, not just the kids and the hoodies.
- 6. Send fewer emails this week.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned walking shoes and gifts for him, up to 25% off through Oct 29.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has template leftovers in the source (a placeholder asset URL, a "Template" `<title>`, `@font-face` rules that can't load fonts), 9 images flagged for missing alt text, and a plain-text part that is almost entirely URLs. The HTML was truncated and the link-probe results weren't included, so link, compliance and UTM checks can't be fully confirmed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- The QA check probed 12 links and skipped 26. No failures or redirect problems were reported, so none are flagged. Coverage is partial.
- The plain-text part is 12,476 of 14,182 characters URLs (88%). This is the `[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs` finding. Long tracking-wrapped URLs in the text part make it hard to read for text-only clients and can raise spam-filter scoring.
- The `.bgwhite` rule contains `background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png')`. This is an unresolved placeholder on a non-HTTPS URL, so it will 404 or trigger a mixed-content block. It is dead weight and probably unintended.
- `egR1f966kc_818512772_12490600_535068_23559598.gif` on `link.email.newbalance.com/mo/` is an open-tracking pixel. It is expected, but it has no `alt`, so it is the QA warning that comes from tracking infrastructure.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text on 9 images:**
  - `md_1750938.jpg`, `md_1750939.jpg`, `md_1750945.jpg`, `md_1750949.jpg`, `md_1750947.jpg`, `md_1750951.jpg`
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking pixel above
  - Screen readers will announce filenames. With images blocked, the shipping banner and icons will have no fallback text.
- **`@font-face` rules have no `src`.** Every rule (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and others) declares only family, weight and style. No font files load, so every text style falls back to `Arial` or `Times New Roman`.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is shown by some clients and in browser-view tabs.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it is invalid and does nothing.
  - The HTML 4.01 doctype is paired with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `.bg1` is defined twice (`#ffffff`, then `#58546d`). The later rule wins, so any element using `.bg1` outside dark mode gets the purple-grey, which may not be intended.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags and a `prefers-color-scheme` block are present. There is a dark-specific shipping banner (`ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`). This is handled, but the banner needs alt text.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The only placeholder is the `XXXXX` image URL, covered above. The rest of the HTML wasn't available, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA note says this is expected because of the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a limitation of the test setup, not a defect in the sender.
- The sender domain `email.newbalance.com` is aligned with the tracking domain `link.email.newbalance.com`, which is a good sign, but alignment isn't proof of authentication.
- The QA step says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the footer physical address weren't in the data provided. They can't be verified or flagged.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
No UTM parameters or final destinations were visible. Links go through the `link.email.newbalance.com` redirector and the probe results weren't included. No issues can be confirmed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the six `md_*.jpg` product images and to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or replace `background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png')` in `.bgwhite`.
3. Add `src` URLs to the `@font-face` rules, or delete them and rely on the `Arial` fallback.
4. Replace `<title>New Balance Template</title>` with the real subject, "Avoid the holiday rush".
5. Fix `<meta content="IE=edge">` (add `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` or remove it) and resolve the duplicate `.bg1` definition.
6. Shorten or restructure the plain-text part so it isn't 88% tracking URLs.
7. To close the verification gaps, re-run with full HTML and link-probe output. Also capture raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) from a direct inbox delivery rather than the AgentMail relay.
