# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- The pink flower sneaker is back again. It's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom," a women's shoe, and it fills the middle of my screen. I'm a 62-year-old man and I never put that in a cart. "Ready to check out?" and "Complete your order" are the same cart nag I got on 10/5, and I've now had several in a row. Skechers has this wrong, and it keeps getting it wrong.
- There is one useful thing further down. The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit "Altman – Murdock" is a tan hands-free shoe, and that is what I'd actually buy. It's one of six small thumbnails, though. It sits below the big pink shoe and a lot of kids' sneakers, and it has no price and no link of its own that I can tell.
- I'd mark this as read. The Skechers Plus banner for BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair, through 10/7, is the only reason I'd glance twice. I wouldn't forward it. I might click through to the Altman, but only because I already know the brand.

## What's working

- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman is in the grid. That's the hands-free comfort shoe I want.
- The dark blue Skechers Plus banner stands out. "Buy One, Get One 50% Off or 20% Off a Single Pair" is easy to read.
- The deal has a clear end date, "Thru 10/7."
- The Men / Women / Kids / Sale list is big, simple, and easy to tap on a phone.
- The layout isn't broken. Nothing overlaps and no images are missing.

## What's weak

- A women's pink floral sneaker is the hero for a man who has never shopped for one. It's the same one from 10/4 and 10/5.
- Three of the six grid shoes are kids' sneakers, and two others are women's. Only one is a men's shoe. That doesn't look like a cart of mine.
- The product captions are tiny, and I'd have to zoom in to read them.
- The offer is far below the hero. I had to scroll past the shoe and the grid to find it.
- The fine print is very small, and it excludes sale styles, new premieres, and more. That makes the discount feel less honest.
- There's no "Hi, Walker" and no member badge or points balance, unlike the 10/5 "Your fall footwear search ends here" email. This one treats me as a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a product I actually looked at, or a men's slip-in, as the hero. Don't show a women's floral shoe to a 62-year-old man.
- 2. Move the Skechers Plus BOGO banner above the product grid and put the deadline in the headline.
- 3. Cut the kids' shoes from a cart reminder for an adult-male browser. Add a men's comfort row with Arch Fit and Slip-ins, with prices.
- 4. Make the captions bigger. Add a short line on cushioning and arch support.
- 5. Say "Hi, Walker" and show the Plus member badge, so it feels like a known customer.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Did you forget something?" (SKECHERS, hello@msgs.skechers.com)

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an abandoned-cart email built on Attentive (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com` link wrapping). It has a failing unsubscribe link in the automated probe, no plain-text part, and about 12 images served over `http://`. The HTML has some further hygiene problems: an empty `<title>` and a malformed charset meta.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400** (QA `[FAIL]`).
  - Link text: "Unsubscribe".
  - URL: `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1...&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m48&externalId=2U3eG&attn_sid=cb205bf7-...`
  - A 400 on a tokenized URL may mean the token is single-use or bound to the recipient or session, or that it rejects the probe's request pattern. This could be a false positive, but it is the only link the checker always tests, and it needs manual verification with a real click.
- **Two links returned 429**, one on "Ready to check out? Complete your order" and one on the linked image `de642685-...jpg`. The QA marks these as "may be valid", so they are rate limiting by the Attentive redirect host, not confirmed breakage. Neither the primary CTA nor the hero link could be validated.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 36 links were probed and 24 were skipped, so the remaining links are unverified.
- All links are opaque click-tracking redirects (`/e/c/<token>`). The final destinations can't be read from the source, so redirect targets are unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.** At least 11 images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`. Gmail proxies these, but some clients may block or warn on them.
  - Affected images: Skechers logo (`dde00662-...png`), `a6ec5198-...jpg`, "Text" (`49468f73-...png`), App Store, Google Play, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook and Youtube.
  - The same host serves `de642685-...jpg` over `https`, so HTTPS is available and the templates just aren't using it consistently.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `de642685-77b3-46fa-950b-5bd9ee6800e1.jpg` is inside a link, so the link has no accessible name. The QA reports the link text as `[image: de642685-...jpg]`.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` also has no alt.
  - The tracking pixel `https://skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` has no alt. This is low impact, and `alt=""` would be the correct value.
- **Non-descriptive alt:** `49468f73-...png` has `alt="Text"`, which conveys nothing.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Malformed charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing. Rendering depends on the MIME header charset alone.
- **Positives:** `<html lang="en">`, a viewport meta tag, and a `max-width:620px` responsive breakpoint with `.stack .column` are present. The preheader is hidden correctly with `display:none` and padding characters.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncation means the cart contents and product block can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is present but failing the probe** (see section 2). A non-functional opt-out is a CAN-SPAM risk if it is real.
- **Plain-text alternative missing:** the QA reports "Text version is 0 chars" (`[FAIL]`). This is a multipart/alternative deliverability and accessibility issue, though it could also be a capture artifact.
- **Not verifiable from the supplied data:**
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
  - The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
  - The physical postal address, which would be in the footer and was cut off by truncation.
- These are listed as unverified, not as failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible in any `href`, because every link is wrapped in an Attentive redirect (`attentivemail.com/e/c/...`). Campaign attribution on the landing page depends on Attentive appending UTMs at redirect time, which can't be confirmed here.
- The "Ready to check out? Complete your order" CTA couldn't be resolved because of the 429, so cart-restoration continuity is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the unsubscribe link manually** by clicking it from a delivered message. If it returns 400 for real recipients, fix it in Attentive before the next send.
2. **Add a plain-text part** (multipart/alternative) with the cart CTA and unsubscribe URL.
3. **Switch all `http://image.emails.skechers.com` images to `https://`.** The host already serves HTTPS.
4. **Add alt text:**
   - Descriptive alt on `de642685-...jpg`, which is a linked image, and on `a6ec5198-...jpg`.
   - Replace `alt="Text"` on `49468f73-...png` with the actual text the image contains.
   - `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. **Populate `<title>`** and fix the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
6. **Confirm the headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment for `msgs.skechers.com`) and the footer postal address from the raw message.
7. **Re-probe the 429 links** (the CTA and the hero image link) with a slower rate to confirm they resolve, and check the final URLs for UTM and cart parameters.
