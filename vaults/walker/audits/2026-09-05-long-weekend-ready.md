## What's working

- Skechers logo shows up in the top third of the deal banner instead of buried at the bottom.
- "Lowest Price Guaranteed" and a real "up to 40% off" number both give me something concrete to weigh.
- $20 off $99.98 / $10 off $74.98 coupon with a code is easy to understand and stackable-sounding.
- Mobile wallet buttons are a nice touch for actually using the coupon in-store on my phone.

## What's weak

- Not one actual shoe pictured anywhere — just brand logos and bold sale banners, so I have no idea what's even on sale for guys like me.
- Zero mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, slip-on — nothing that speaks to how I actually shop for shoes.
- It's a pile of six or seven different promos stacked on top of each other (Labor Day sale, sandal blowout, buy-one-get-pink-sticker-shoe, $20/$10 coupon) — feels cluttered, hard to know what to focus on.
- That red banner near the bottom just cuts off mid-word ("LIMIT...") — looks like a broken or cropped graphic.
- "Sandal Blowout" and generic sale banners could be for anyone; nothing here is segmented to my age or style.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show me an actual shoe — a men's comfort walking shoe or slip-on, ideally the Skechers Arch Fit or similar, with a price. A logo isn't a product.
- 2. Cut this down to one hero offer instead of five stacked promos — pick the 40% off athletic brands and lead with it, move sandals/coupon/pink sticker deal further down or drop them.
- 3. Fix that cut-off red banner graphic near the bottom — it looks broken and makes the whole email feel sloppy.
- 4. Add one line of comfort-focused copy under the Skechers logo — "cushioned, arch-supported, all-day comfort" — since that's what actually gets my thumb moving.
