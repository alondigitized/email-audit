# Going, going...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least this one leads with an actual sale instead of another colorway blast — that's a step in the right direction after nine emails in two weeks. "Blink and you'll miss them" markdowns, three shoes shown right up top, "Shop Sale" button before I even have to scroll much. Problem is, I still don't know what the markdown actually is — no percentage, no dollar figure, nothing. And two of those three shoes on the sale rail are in pastel green and blush pink, which tells me this batch wasn't picked with a guy my age in mind.
- The real letdown is the bottom half, which pivots to some runner sprinting down a dark street to pitch me on signing up for text messages just to get free shipping on my first order. I'm not a first-time buyer and I'm not signing up for texts from a shoe company. That whole block is dead weight for someone like me.
- I'd click through on curiosity about the sale price, but I wouldn't forward this — nothing here is a "hey, check this out" moment, just a vague markdown pitch.

## What's working

- "Just hit the sale page" with real product photos front and center — no guessing what I'm clicking into.
- "Shop Sale" and "Shop Sale Styles" buttons are big, clear, and hard to miss on a phone screen.
- It's an actual sale email, not another colorway or collab push — different beat than the last several sends.

## What's weak

- No percentage or dollar amount anywhere — "markdowns" could mean $5 off or $50 off, and I'm not clicking blind to find out.
- Two of the three featured shoes are in colors (seafoam, blush) that skew younger and more feminine — not speaking to me.
- The whole bottom section is a text-signup pitch dressed up as "free shipping," aimed at new customers, complete with a runner in short-shorts that has nothing to do with sale shoes.
- No mention of wide sizing, arch support, or anything about fit — just "grab these before they're gone."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number on the offer — "Up to 40% off" or "$30 off select styles" — right in the hero, not buried behind a click.
- 2. Swap at least one of the three sale shoes for a neutral men's colorway — brown, black, gray — so it doesn't read as a women's sale email.
- 3. Cut the free-shipping-for-texting block entirely, or at minimum, offer it to everyone, not just new subscribers, since existing customers get nothing out of it.
- 4. Add one line about wide/X-wide availability on sale styles if it applies — that's the thing that actually gets me to click, per your own recent "Find the fit for your foot" send.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — HOKA "Going, going..." Email

### 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA returned a 60% pass rate with 4 failures: three tracked-link probe failures (return policy, SMS CTA image link, unsubscribe) and a missing/empty plain-text MIME part. Combined with universal missing `alt` attributes, this is a deliverability- and accessibility-risk email despite otherwise standard responsive markup.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed links route through the Cordial ESP redirector (`e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...`) with signed `jwtH`/`jwtP`/`jwtS` params — standard click-tracking wrapper, working as designed structurally.
- **[FAIL] 403** on the "return-start" CTA — decoded `jwtP` payload resolves to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260818_...&hmail=...&cordial_id=...`. The tracking wrapper itself rejected the probe (403), which could indicate bot/anti-scraping protection on the redirector rather than a truly broken destination — but it should be manually clicked to confirm live users aren't also blocked.
- **[FAIL] 400** on the image-only CTA (`US_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`), decoded destination `https://hoka.attn.tv/p/ie7/landing-page?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=...`. A 400 from the redirector suggests a malformed/expired token or an issue at the Attentive (`attn.tv`) landing page target — worth verifying directly, since this is the only click path on that image block.
- **[FAIL] 403** on the **Unsubscribe** link, decoding to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=...`. A broken unsubscribe link is the highest-priority fix in this report — see Compliance section.
- QA notes 12 material links were identified but only these 3 were probed (footer/social/legal deprioritized); no visibility into whether other CTAs share the same wrapper failure mode.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] 8 images with no `alt` text**, including the hero (`US_20260818_NewHero.jpg`), all three product tiles (`MC9GTX`, `C10`, `Skyflow`), the SMS CTA image (which is also a CTA link — doubles as a broken-link + accessibility failure), the footer logo, and two tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...`, `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`). Screen readers get zero context, and if images are blocked (common default in Outlook/Gmail), the email renders as a blank shell.
- CSS includes several malformed mobile declarations under the `480px` media query: `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` — missing numeric values make these rules invalid and they will be dropped by CSS parsers, silently failing on mobile clients that rely on them.
- Font loading uses external `@font-face` `.woff2`/`.woff` from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...` — most email clients (Outlook, Gmail app) strip custom `@font-face`, so this is a graceful-degradation-only concern, not a hard bug, provided a web-safe fallback stack is defined elsewhere in the body CSS (not visible in truncated source).

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No visible unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) in the truncated source.
- Personalization is present via query params (`hmail`, `cordial_id`) appended to tracking/unsubscribe URLs — these are populated (not literal placeholders), so token resolution appears correct.
- Full body beyond the truncation point wasn't available to confirm no unrendered tokens exist further down.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link returns 403** (see §2) — this is a CAN-SPAM risk if reproducible by real recipients, since a non-functional unsubscribe mechanism is a direct compliance violation. Recommend manual verification before next send.
- Unsubscribe URL correctly includes `cid` (contact ID) for one-click identification: `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9`.
- No authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) were present in the supplied data — this audit only had HTML source, not raw headers, so authentication cannot be assessed from what was provided.
- **Plain-text part is 0 chars** — CAN-SPAM requires the email be readable; an empty text/plain fallback fails for text-only clients and materially hurts deliverability/spam scoring (most filters penalize HTML-only or empty-multipart messages).

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All three decoded links share a consistent campaign tag: `utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20260818_Markdowns_HardCoded_STO_A60Day_365SaleTag_0521MarkdownOpeners_90DayOptIn_SalePLPBrowse` — consistent tagging across CTAs, good for attribution.
- Destinations span two domains: `www.hoka.com` (return policy) and `hoka.attn.tv` (Attentive SMS signup landing page) — the cross-domain hop to Attentive is expected for SMS opt-in flows but wasn't verifiable given the 400 probe failure.
- Cannot confirm landing-page content alignment (e.g., does `return-start` page match "Going, going..." sale messaging) since destination pages weren't rendered/fetched in this audit — only link probing was performed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority:** Manually load the unsubscribe URL in a browser to confirm it isn't actually broken for recipients — a persistent 403 here is a compliance blocker.
2. Investigate the 400 on the `hoka.attn.tv` SMS CTA link — check for a malformed/expired JWT or a config issue on the Attentive landing page.
3. Add `alt` text to all 8 images, particularly the SMS CTA image (functions as a link) and the hero/product images.
4. Fix the malformed CSS declarations (`padding-top:px`, `border-top:px solid`) in the `@media (max-width:480px)` block — supply actual pixel values or remove the dead rules.
5. Populate the plain-text MIME part; do not ship with a 0-char text fallback.
6. Re-run the link prober from a different network/UA to rule out the 403s being anti-bot blocking rather than genuine breakage, before treating them as confirmed dead links.
