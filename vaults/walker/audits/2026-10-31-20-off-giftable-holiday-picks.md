# 20% off giftable holiday picks >>>
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. After the "Become a VIP & get 20% off!" run all week, at least this one has a different angle: gifts. The "20% OFF*" up top is big and I can read it without squinting. But it's the same FABULOUS code and the same VIP pitch, now dressed up for the holidays. The asterisk and the "regular-price styles only" line told me right away there's a catch.
- The rest is a long scroll of boots, women's dress heels and rain boots. Most of that isn't for me. I did see a couple of men's sneakers, a New Balance and an Adidas-style pair, but nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-ons. I came for comfort and got a boot catalog. The shoes are clean and easy to see, and the buttons are big enough for my thumb. I'd probably give it a glance and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The giant "20% OFF*" is the first thing I see, and the code FABULOUS is spelled out right under it.
- "Become a VIP" is a plain black button, big enough to tap on my phone.
- The shop buttons (Boots, Sneakers, Casual Shoes) are wide and easy to hit.
- The size row near the bottom, with a "Shop Wide Width" button, speaks to people with real feet like mine.
- Free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and the Afterpay line are all laid out clearly at the bottom.

## What's weak

- It's the same VIP/20% pitch I've seen all week, so it doesn't feel like news. It's about the sixth or seventh version.
- The asterisk and the "regular-price styles only" line make it feel like fine print is hiding the real deal. The long disclaimer block at the bottom is tiny and I can't read it.
- It's mostly women's boots and dress shoes. Only two men's sneakers show up, with no comfort or walking message.
- "Shop Men" is a small text link in the hero, easy to miss. The big buttons go to boots, dress shoes and women's.
- I have to become a VIP before I see the discount, which feels like a hoop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's comfort row, like walking sneakers and cushioned slip-ons, and put "Shop Men" up top as a real button. This would help the most.
- 2. Say whether VIPs get the 20% automatically or whether I have to join first, and drop the asterisk from the headline.
- 3. Tie the gift angle to something real, like "gifts for dad" with comfort picks, instead of a pile of women's boots.
- 4. Cut the repeating 20% pitch to one send a week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Comfy gifts for dad, 20% off with VIP`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cushioned walkers + slip-ons, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free to join. Code FABULOUS takes 20% off regular-price styles through Nov 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's walking shoes and wide widths, ready to wrap.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Braze/SendGrid-style template with click-tracked links on `ablink.dsw.com`. One confirmed QA failure: the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned HTTP 400. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, headers and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** the link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400** when probed.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/5zuo-11FHUdJtaGGyqaAxA~~/AAQRxRA~/Y1agFr3m…`
  - A 400 from a redirect host can mean an expired or malformed tracking token, or that the host rejects the probe. The data can't separate the two, so confirm with a real-client click.
  - If it is broken, the SMS-acquisition CTA is dead.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 71 links were probed (59 skipped by priority ranking). All other links are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **URL-heavy plain text (WARN):** 27,301 of 31,923 characters (~86%) are URLs. This is a consequence of long wrapped tracking URLs and can add spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `src="https://ablink.dsw.com/q/6zQAbI75kTYR5J102d4-GA~~/AAQRxRA~/IjItoiI8dUWp…"`
  - `src="https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261031_P251284_V1_W-_Offer_MI_DY"`
  - `o.gif` is a tracking pixel and should carry `alt=""`. The first image is served from a `/q/` tracking path. If it is a content image, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Ineffective meta tags (from source):** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. Charset then relies on the HTTP/MIME headers.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the opening tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **`<title>`:** it is just "DSW". This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens appeared, but the source was truncated, so the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication headers:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is not evidence of a sender-side failure.
- **Unsubscribe:** the QA run always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address were not in the provided data, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Almost all links go through `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be read from the source. They are also not in the probe data beyond the one 400. The tracking pixel's `mi_ecmp=20261031_P251284_V1_W-_Offer_MI_DY` shows a campaign ID, but nothing links it to the destination pages. Nothing is confirmed either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts" link in a real browser or client. If the 400 is real, fix or regenerate the tracking token.
2. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`. Add descriptive alt text to the `/q/` image if it is visible content.
3. Change the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge">`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Include full raw headers in the next audit, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC and List-Unsubscribe can be checked.
6. Probe more of the 59 skipped links, or resolve redirects, to check UTMs and landing-page alignment.
