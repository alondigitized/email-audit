- DSW again. That's the same pink banner and giant black "30% OFF*" I've been deleting since the 20th. "ICYMI" is a fair label, because I've been told about this sale at least six times. Today's subject at least says the sale is live. The earlier ones kept asking me to join VIP first.

- The giant "30% OFF*" with the code BFDEALS is impossible to miss. I know the deal in two seconds.

- The sale dates (Nov 23–29) are in the fine print, so the deadline is real.

## What's weak

- It's the same pink banner and 30% pitch I've seen all week. This is about the sixth near-copy.
- The product grid is a white sneaker, a fur-lined boot and trendy Adidas-style shoes. Nothing is slip-on, cushioned or arch-supported, and nothing looks like it's meant for a 62-year-old.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny gray text. The exclusions are buried there, and that's where I find out which brands don't count (Skechers is on the list that qualifies, but Hoka and Brooks aren't mentioned).
- The "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" and asterisk make me wonder what's left out.
- The header ticker scrolls and gets cut off ("SEASON'S BIGGE"), and there's a second banner above it for clearance boots. It's cluttered before I reach the offer.

- 2. Put comfort brands and features in the product grid: slip-on, memory foam, arch support, wide widths.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a standard table layout with dark-mode CSS, but it has a 400 error on the SMS opt-in CTA and an unresolved `%%gender%%` merge token. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM status couldn't be confirmed from the truncated source and the QA data.

The QA output shows an empty target after "Broken link (400):", which suggests the redirect failed to resolve. A 400 from a tracking redirect can also mean the probe was rejected. I can't tell which from the data, so re-test it in a real client before treating it as confirmed.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 78 links were probed and 66 were skipped. The remaining links are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Plain-text part (WARN):** 27,662 of 33,284 characters (83%) are URLs. The long `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/…~~` tracking URLs make the plain-text alternative close to unreadable. This also adds a modest spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (WARN):** Two images have no alt attribute. One is `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/J264uWnRtI591JeFrFnm3Q~~/…`, which looks like an open-tracking pixel. The other is `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…`, which looks like a dynamic-content (`mi_*`) pixel. Both are likely non-visual, so they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. The charset declaration is ineffective, which risks encoding problems in clients that don't default to UTF-8.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility (WCAG 1.4.4) concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen readers.
- **Web fonts:** Futura_PT_* and HelveticaNow_* load as `.woff` and `.ttf` from `braze-images.com`. Many clients, including Gmail, ignore web fonts, so the fallback font stack matters. I can't verify the stacks from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Raw token (FAIL):** `%%gender%%` appears unrendered in the delivered message. It's a leftover personalization variable that wasn't substituted. The truncated source doesn't show where it sits, so I can't say whether it's visible text, a URL parameter or an attribute. If it's in a URL, that link may carry a literal `%%gender%%` string. This may be the cause of the 400 above, but that's unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header absent (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the message arrived via the AgentMail relay. This is a limit of the capture, not a confirmed sender failure. Check the headers at the original receipt.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** The QA data flags nothing here, and unsubscribe links are always probed. The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers or the postal address. No issues found in what's visible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Click-through links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/…` redirects whose destinations and UTM parameters aren't visible in the truncated source or the QA data. **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.**
- The `mi.dsw.com` pixel carries `mi_ecmp=20261123_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…`, which identifies the campaign, but there's no evidence of how it maps to site-side attribution.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the SMS opt-in link. Check its redirect target and make sure it returns 2xx or an `sms:` handoff.
2. Fix the `%%gender%%` substitution, or add a default fallback value. Check that the Braze template syntax matches the token format the sender actually processes.
3. Correct `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
5. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
6. Shorten the plain-text URLs, or use a shorter tracking domain path, to improve the text-to-link ratio.
7. Re-run the QA probe with a higher link limit, and capture the full headers and footer HTML to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe` and UTM parameters.
