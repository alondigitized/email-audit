## What's working

- The headline is big and easy to read without zooming, and the offer is stated clearly: "Up to 25% off select shoes."
- The code NOVEMBER and the end date, November 11th, are right under the headline, so I know what to do and when.
- The WX608v5 in the grid is a shoe I'd consider. It's a wide walking shoe with cushioning, and it's more my speed than the flashy ones.
- The dark "Shop now" button is large, and the footer has "Walking" next to "Running" and "Lifestyle."
- The price buckets of Under $50, $50-$100 and Over $100 let me shop by budget, which is how I think about shoes.

## What's weak

- The hero shows shopping bags and no shoes. It's a big picture that tells me nothing about comfort or fit.
- The grid doesn't say which items are on sale. There are no prices, no sale tags and no "was/now," so I can't do the price-versus-quality math I care about.
- Socks, a hoodie, joggers and a kids' shoe fill half the grid. The pink Rave Run is clearly not for me.
- The fine print is small gray text that's hard to read on a phone, and "Exclusions apply" with "select shoes" is vague. I don't know whether the 608 even qualifies.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or width, and the top menu is Men, Women, Kids and Holiday, with no walking option.

- 3. Fill the grid with walking and comfort shoes only. Cut the socks, hoodie and kids' shoe, or move them to a second section.
- 4. Make the "Shop now" button say what it leads to, like "Shop men's walking shoes."

- 6. Cut the doubled subject line.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, but it has template-hygiene defects (placeholder CSS, a conflicting `.bg1` rule, a default `<title>`) and three images missing alt text. The subject line is duplicated. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM compliance could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links are reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 27, so link health on the 27 skipped is unverified.
- The 1×1 open-tracking pixel `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12633262_623972_23860872.gif` is served from the ESP click-tracking domain. This is normal, but it has no alt text (see section 3).
- The shipping assets come from `/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`. That path belongs to a welcome-series template, so this promo reuses welcome-email assets. If that folder is retired, these images break.
- The plain-text part is 12,596 of 14,314 characters URLs (88%). This is the QA deliverability warning. It is probably long tracking-redirect URLs, and it can raise spam-filter scoring on the text/plain part.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 3 images):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `egR1f966kc_..._23860872.gif`, the tracking pixel, which should carry `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt on the shipping banner:** it likely carries informational text, so screen readers get nothing.
- **Placeholder CSS:** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}` is left in the `<style>` block. If the class is applied, it requests a non-existent, non-HTTPS URL. This is a 404 and a mixed-content risk.
- **Conflicting duplicate rule:** `.bg1` is defined twice, first as `#ffffff` and later as `#58546d`. The later rule wins, which is probably unintended.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Default title:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` was never replaced. Some clients and archive/web-view pages show it.
- **Ineffective `@font-face` rules:** the blocks for `proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot` and others have no `src`, so they load nothing. One block repeats `font-family` inside itself. Rendering falls back to Arial or Times New Roman.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with an `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`. This is harmless but inconsistent.
- **Dark-mode CSS:** it uses `prefers-color-scheme` with `!important`. Support is partial in Outlook and Gmail, and the source is truncated, so I can't assess it further.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source has no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`. The one concern is the subject line, which repeats the same phrase twice ("Up to 25% off select shoes ends soon, up to 25% off select shoes ends soon"). That looks like a subject and preheader concatenation or a dynamic-content bug.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header:** not found. QA notes it is "Expected via AgentMail relay", so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a limit of the capture, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe link:** QA says one is always included in link probing, but no result is shown. The truncated source also doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the visible unsubscribe link, the physical postal address, or the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
- **Sender:** `newbalance@email.newbalance.com` is aligned with the brand's sending subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows no destination URLs, so I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment. QA also reports no redirect-chain or landing-page results.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add alt text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and set `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixel.
2. Remove the `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder from `.bgwhite`, or replace it with an HTTPS asset.
3. Resolve the duplicate `.bg1` definition, and fix the meta tag to `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
4. Replace the `<title>` with the campaign name or subject.
5. Fix the doubled subject line. Check how the subject and preheader merge.
6. Make the plain-text part shorter and more readable, for example with less raw tracking-URL bloat.
7. Re-run QA with the full headers and complete HTML. That would let you verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address, and UTM parameters on CTA links.
