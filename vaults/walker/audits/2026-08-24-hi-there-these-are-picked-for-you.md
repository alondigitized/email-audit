# Hi there, these are picked for you!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- "Picked for you" and then it opens with a "Pop Quiz: What's Your #1 School Essential?" — I'm 62, nothing about that was picked for me. This is a "New Arrivals" grid-dump with boys' Nike Air Max, girls' New Balance 515s in pink, a Puma Speedcat, Converse high-tops — basically a garage sale of every category they carry, sorted by nothing in particular. I have to scroll through six product blocks of kids' and women's shoes before I hit anything I'd wear.
- To be fair, there is a genuine save at the bottom — Brooks Ghost 17 flagged "$40 OFF," a pair of Birkenstock sandals, and a real coupon banner ($20 off $99.98+ or $10 off $74.98+, code SAVEMORE). That's the same Ghost 17 deal I liked in Sunday's and Friday's Brooks emails, so at least it's consistent. But it's buried under a wall of kids' sneakers, and the subject promised personalization I didn't get.
- I'd scroll straight to the bottom for the coupon, maybe click Brooks, and mark it read. Not forwarding this one — nothing here says "for a guy like you," it says "for whoever opens this."

## What's working

- The Brooks Ghost 17 block near the bottom actually matches me — same shoe, same $40 off theme from recent sends, so it's at least a shoe I recognize and trust.
- Birkenstock Arizona sandals next to Brooks is a fair adjacent pick — comfort footwear I'd actually consider.
- The coupon banner is clear and usable: two real dollar thresholds, a plain promo code, barcodes included for in-store.
- No broken images or layout glitches — everything rendered clean, just poorly targeted.

## What's weak

- "Picked for you" leads with a back-to-school pop quiz and a wall of kids'/women's sneakers — nothing in the first six product blocks is remotely me.
- The category carousel dots (A/B/C/D) suggest this is supposed to be dynamically personalized, and whatever landed in my inbox missed badly.
- No men's comfort or walking shoe shows up until Brooks, five scroll-lengths in — Skechers, Hoka, New Balance men's are entirely absent even though this brand knows I cross-shop those.
- Small all-caps product labels under tiny thumbnails are a real strain to read on my phone without zooming in.

## Recommendations

- 1. If the subject says "picked for you," the first fold better actually be picked for me — lead with Brooks/Birkenstock/men's comfort brands instead of burying them under kids' back-to-school stuff.
- 2. Drop or de-prioritize the school-quiz banner for customers without kids in the household — that's a segment miss, not a content problem.
- 3. Move the $20/$10 coupon banner up near the top — it's the strongest, clearest offer in the email and it's stuck at the very bottom.
- 4. Add a men's/comfort-specific row (Skechers, Hoka, New Balance men's) since that's the brand mix I've actually shown interest in.

- **Subject Alt B:** `$20 off your next comfort shoe`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brooks, Birkenstock, and a $20 coupon inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The comfort shoes we think you'll actually wear`
