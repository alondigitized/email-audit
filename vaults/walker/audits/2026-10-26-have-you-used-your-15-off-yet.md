# Have you used your $15 off yet?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, but the pitch has changed. Last week it was 20% off with code JINGLE. Now it's a new "buy more, save more" ladder with code SOBRIGHT. The subject asks "Have you used your $15 off yet?", which made me think I'd missed something, and I hadn't. That's a little cheeky when this is the first time I've seen this offer.
- The pink banner is big and easy to read on my phone. $15 off $49, $20 off $99, $60 off $199 is plain math, and I like plain math. Then comes "So New, So You", and all six shoes are women's: a Mary Jane flat, a loafer, ankle boots, a clog and a fuzzy winter boot. There's no cushioned walking shoe or slip-on for a man my age. I'd skim it and probably not click.
- I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. I might tap "Shop Men" if I were already near a DSW, but nothing here pulls me there.

## What's working

- The three-tier discount ladder is huge and readable on my phone, with no squinting.
- The new offer is a break from the same JINGLE 20% I've been getting all week.
- The "Claim $15 Off" button is black on pink and easy to find.
- The "Shop Men" link sits right under the banner, so I can skip the women's shoes.
- The "Shop by Size" block has big numbers, and the Wide Width button is useful for my feet.

## What's weak

- Every shoe shown is women's. Nothing is hands-free, cushioned or arch-supported, and no brand is named, so I can't compare against my Hoka, Brooks or New Balance.
- The subject implies I already got a $15 coupon, but the email just introduces a new code. It feels a bit misleading.
- The logo's "Designer Shoe Warehouse" tagline overlaps and gets cut off at the top.
- The $60 off $199 line has an asterisk, and the real terms are in tiny print at the bottom. Brands like Skechers are listed there, but I can't tell what's excluded without reading it all.
- The "$15, $20, or $60 off!" label sits under only two of the six shoes, which looks inconsistent.
- The shop links under the banner are small, close together and hard to tap.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show men's comfort and walking shoes, with brand names like Skechers, New Balance or Hoka and a slip-on in the mix, or split the hero by men and women.
- 2. Make the subject match the email: say what the offer is, not a question implying I already have it.
- 3. Space out the Shop Women/Men/Kids/Everything links and make them bigger buttons.
- 4. Put the key exclusions in one line under the banner so I don't have to read the fine print.
- 5. Fix the logo tagline overlap.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's comfort picks: save up to $60`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SOBRIGHT works in stores and online through Oct 28.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a standard Braze/ablink table-based template with dark-mode CSS. The SMS opt-in CTA link returned HTTP 400, and the plain-text part and authentication results couldn't be validated. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what was visible and to the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed):** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **400** on `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/TEj5atW6GnEBpyPdydH7-A~~/AAQRxRA~/qmQ4W_Nv2mr…`. The probe recorded no link label before the URL, which suggests an empty or odd href target.
  - The probe can't tell whether the 400 is a genuinely dead redirect or a bot-blocked or personalization-dependent token. It should be re-tested from a real client.
  - This is the only hard failure in the run. It is a conversion CTA that promises a discount.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 24,945 of 29,377 plain-text characters (about 85%) are URLs. The long ablink wrapped URLs, around 500 characters each, drive this.
  - The spam-filter risk applies to the text/plain part.
  - The text version is also hard to read.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 links were probed and 57 were skipped. The clean result for the rest is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/QCCSTEs-TBOc6zcxLGTsBw~~/…`. This `/q/` path looks like a tracking image.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…`. This is an open-tracking pixel.
  - Both should carry `alt=""`. That is low severity, since they are decorative or tracking images.
- **Malformed meta tags in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare a charset.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
  - Both are inert.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` lacks `lang`, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- Web fonts load from `braze-images.com` (woff/ttf). Fallback stacks weren't visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appeared in the visible source. The pixel URL carries `mi_u=225090073` and a campaign id, `mi_ecmp=20261026_P261675_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…`. Both are populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication unverified:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay. The From domain `e.dsw.com` and the link domain `ablink.dsw.com` are both on `dsw.com`, so sender and link branding are aligned.
- **Unsubscribe:** The QA run always probes the unsubscribe link, and it raised no failure for it. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't included in the data provided, so one-click support is unconfirmed.
- **Footer address:** The physical postal address couldn't be checked because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All links go through `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be seen in the source. Resolve the redirect chains to check UTM presence and landing-page alignment.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261026_P261675_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` on the pixel. That is an indirect signal.
- The failing opt-in link couldn't be followed to any destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts" link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/TEj5atW6…`) from a real browser or device. If it fails there too, fix the destination or the Braze link template.
2. Fix the two meta tags with `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `/q/` tracking image and the `o.gif` pixel.
4. Shorten or trim the plain-text part, for example with shorter link wrappers or fewer repeated links, to bring URL density down from about 85%.
5. Capture the raw headers (Authentication-Results, `List-Unsubscribe`) and fully resolve redirect chains on a re-run. That covers the unverified compliance and UTM items.
