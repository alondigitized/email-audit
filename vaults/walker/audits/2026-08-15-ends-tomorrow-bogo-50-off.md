# ENDS TOMORROW: BOGO 50% OFF
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- Same BOGO 50% off DSW email I've been getting all week, and once again it's aimed at somebody who is not me. Twelve shoes in that grid and every last one is a woman's heel, strappy sandal, slingback, or pointy loafer — even the sneaker in there looks like a women's colorway. "School Supply Stock-Up" tells me this brand thinks its whole list is moms buying for kids, not a guy shopping for himself.
- The one part that actually caught my eye was the National Shoe Donation Day banner — give shoes, get $10 toward a VIP reward. That's a real, honest little offer and I like that it's tied to something with actual meaning (Soles4Souls), not just another markdown gimmick. Everything above and below it, though, is noise to me.
- I'm not clicking, not forwarding. This is a mark-as-read, and honestly after this many nearly identical BOGO sends from DSW this week, I'm getting close to just not opening the next one.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly up top with no funny business — same honest number I've credited this brand for before.
- The National Shoe Donation Day / $10 VIP reward section is a nice change of pace from pure markdown pitching.
- "Shop by Size" and the Wide Width / Wide Calf buttons are a smart, functional touch — that's the kind of practical detail that'd matter to me if the shoes themselves were in my category.
- Clean, simple layout, big readable text and buttons — easy enough to navigate on my phone even if the content isn't for me.

## What's weak

- Every single shoe in the "Brand New" grid is women's — heels, slingbacks, pointy flats. Nothing hands-free, cushioned, or arch-supported in sight.
- "School Supply Stock-Up" as the header context tells me I'm not the customer this was written for.
- The teaser up top ("Select Nike styles: up to 40% off") gets me to open and then never delivers — no Nike product shown anywhere in the body.
- No MEN's category products featured at all, despite a "MEN" nav link sitting right there unused.
- Sixth-plus BOGO email this week with the same format — the cadence is wearing thin regardless of who the audience is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender/purchase history — if DSW has ever seen me buy men's shoes, don't send me a grid of twelve women's heels. That's the single biggest miss here.
- 2. If leading with Nike in the subject/preheader-adjacent teaser, actually show Nike product in the email body — right now it's bait with no payoff.
- 3. Feature comfort/walking shoe styles (men's or unisex) if this is going to a mixed list — something with actual arch support, not just heels and sandals.
- 4. Cut back the send frequency — I've gotten near-identical BOGO emails almost daily this week; space it out or vary the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort BOGO: 50% off your 2nd pair`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Donate a pair, get $10 — plus BOGO 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals in your size, wide widths included`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Give shoes, get rewarded — details inside`
