# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- There it is again. That same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sneaker, front and center, big as life — the exact one Skechers has been hammering me with since at least the 11th. "We Noticed You Noticing Us..." Well I noticed *them* noticing *me* click on absolutely nothing, and yet here we are, cart still "waiting" on a women's shoe I never touched.
- Buried way below the fold, though, there's a men's Delson 3.0 - Roth in the "more options" grid, plus a 40% off summer styles banner — that's the kind of thing that'd actually get my attention if it led the email instead of playing backup to a shoe I don't want.
- I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding this to anybody, and honestly I'm getting close to just marking these as read on sight. Five days running with the same phantom cart item is starting to feel less like marketing and more like a broken record stuck on the wrong customer.

## What's working

- The 40% off summer styles banner near the bottom is a real, specific offer — if that had been the headline I'd have looked twice.
- "Complete Your Order" button is big, easy to tap, no squinting needed.
- Men's Delson 3.0 - Roth shoe shows up in the grid — that's actually in my lane, cushioned slip-on style.
- Payment options (Afterpay, Klarna) and curbside pickup are listed clearly if I did want to buy.

## What's weak

- Same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom hero shoe as the last several emails — I've never clicked it, never carted it, and it's still the star of the show.
- The "more options" grid is mostly women's and kids' shoes — Boy's Garage Sneaker twice, BOBS slip-ons — nothing that says "this brand knows I'm a 62-year-old guy."
- Headline "We Noticed You Noticing Us" is a stretch when I never noticed this shoe in the first place.
- The real offer, 40% off, is buried two-thirds down the page instead of leading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop the cart-abandonment emails for an item that's never actually been carted by me — if the system's guessing wrong, fix the targeting before sending a fifth "reminder."
- 2. Lead with the 40% off sale and a men's comfort shoe, not a floral sneaker I've ignored all week.
- 3. If the "more options" grid is supposed to be relevant to me, populate it with men's Arch Fit or slip-on styles, not boys' and women's product.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Still deciding? Here's 40% off to help`
