## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is stated plainly up top, no bait-and-switch headline this time like the last several DSW sends.
- The layout is clean, no broken images or overlapping text that I could spot.
- The "Shop by Size" grid is a nice touch for someone actually shopping this catalog — clear tap targets.
- Loyalty perks (DSW VIP, 3% back, free shipping) are laid out clearly near the bottom.

## What's weak

- Every single model shot is a woman — yoga poses, heeled booties, espadrilles, a dress. Nothing men's, nothing that reads casual/comfort.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or slip-on ease anywhere — the stuff I actually shop for.
- "Shop by Size" runs 5–11, which is women's sizing, not mine.
- The one arguably-unisex item (Frye boot) is buried halfway down after four screens of women's fashion.
- "2(!) days left" urgency in the subject doesn't even show up clearly in the email body until you're well past the fold.

- 2. Lead the men's version with a comfort brand (Skechers, Hoka, New Balance) and call out cushioning/arch support in the hero copy, not just "BOGO 50% off."
- 3. Move the urgency ("2 days left") into the hero, not just the subject line — right now it's easy to miss once you're scrolling.
- 4. Add a men's size grid instead of, or alongside, the women's one.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on styles from Skechers, Hoka & more`
