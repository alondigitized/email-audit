# Up to 50% off for Week of Deals last call
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- I've lost count of the adidas emails since the 15th, but this one finally gets the basics right. It has a big black banner that says "UP TO 50% OFF" and a countdown clock showing under 20 hours left. I don't need to squint to find the offer. The red Gazelle sneakers on the gift wrap are a handsome photo, and they're a real shoe, which the 10/31 jacket-on-wrapping-paper email wasn't.
- The trouble is that a Gazelle is a flat, skinny retro sneaker with almost no cushion. I'm 62 and I buy for arch support and cushioning. The copy names Gazelle, Soft Lux and Spezial, and none of those is a walking comfort shoe. I'd give it a quick look, maybe tap "Shop Men" to see what's marked down, and then probably leave. I wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The black banner is easy to read, and "UP TO 50% OFF" is the first thing I see.
- The countdown (00 days, 19 hours) makes the deadline feel real, and it matches the "last call" in the subject.
- The photo is a real shoe with good color, and the shoe is the focus. That's an improvement over the 10/31 email, which had no shoe at all.
- The "Shop Men / Women / Kids" buttons are big and black with white text. They're easy to tap on my phone and easy to read.
- The body text is large enough that I can read it without zooming.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is a flat fashion sneaker. There's nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-on comfort, which is what I shop for.
- "Up to 50%" is the usual fine-print trap. I don't know whether the shoe I'd want is 50% off or 15% off.
- None of the buttons goes to a specific shoe. "Shop Men" just dumps me in a big catalog.
- The "W E E K  O F  D E A L S" lettering is spread so far apart that it's hard to read as words.
- There's no review, rating or price shown. I can't do my price-versus-quality math, and I'd have to leave the email to do it.
- This is the same "Week of Deals" push I got on 10/31, only now it says "last call." It feels like the same email again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick a hero for the customer. For someone like me, show a cushioned walking or lifestyle shoe with a real sale price, like "Now $64, was $90." Show a few prices instead of only "up to."
- 2. Link the buttons to something specific, such as "Shop men's walking shoes," and put a few men's picks with prices under the hero.
- 3. Add a star rating or a line like "4.6 stars from 2,100 reviews" so I can judge a shoe without leaving the email.
- 4. Tighten the lettering in the photo so "WEEK OF DEALS" reads as three words.
- 5. Space these sends out. After a week of near-identical deal emails, "last call" doesn't feel urgent.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: Gazelle and Soft Lux up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off men's sneakers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends at midnight. See men's comfort picks from $45.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free returns on every pair. Today is the final day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive adidas campaign sent through a click-tracking redirect (`click.comms.adidas.com`). It has no plain-text alternative, and several images use insecure `http://` sources or lack alt text. The HTML supplied was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe block, and most link targets could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations are verified.
- **Insecure image sources, 5 occurrences:** the "arrow" image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`. Some clients block or warn on mixed content.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1ab9e0a7b-1a7ff67a-.../8mHTPj223vnQqxDzQfujeI2ju…`. It uses `http://` and has no alt text. The path pattern suggests it is the open-tracking pixel, though the truncated source doesn't confirm that. If it is blocked, opens go unrecorded.
- **Link targets and `<a>` hrefs:** not visible in the truncated source, so no further issues can be confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 3 images:**
  - `https://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe5c15707c63077e7013/m/1/eebc2368-b3ae-43d8-9596-f1f95c7eb473.jpg`
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/0b804007-6a7f-4481-a971-0976bd8228c1?cp_tp=…`
  - the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…` image above
- **Charset declaration is ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it isn't a valid declaration. The charset will come from the MIME header instead, and the header wasn't provided.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0" />` lacks `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may not scale correctly.
- **Plain-text fallback is missing (QA FAIL).** Text-only clients and accessibility tooling get nothing.
- **Positives:** `<html lang="en">` is present. Font stacks fall back to Arial and Helvetica via `local()`, with `mso-font-alt`. `-webkit-text-size-adjust` is set.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear, but the truncated body can't be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part is missing.** Multipart/alternative is the expected format, so this is a deliverability and compliance-hygiene gap.
- **Authentication-Results header was not found.** The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a gap in the data, not a confirmed failure. The sender `us-news.comms.adidas.com` should be verified against the original headers.
- **Unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source or the QA data, so I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. Destinations sit behind `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects that were not probed, and no final URLs were captured. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set, which is a common choice for privacy but can strip referrer data downstream.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative that includes the key offer copy and the main links (QA FAIL).
2. Serve `b0c011a2-….jpg` and the tracking pixel over HTTPS. Both hosts already serve HTTPS elsewhere, as `image.link.adidas.com/.../eebc2368….jpg` shows.
3. Add `alt` text to the 3 flagged images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones, including the pixel and the arrow.
4. Change the charset meta to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`.
5. Update the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.
6. Resolve the 22 redirect links in a follow-up run and record their final URLs and UTM parameters.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers to check `List-Unsubscribe`, the unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
