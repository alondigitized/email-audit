# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. That's the D'Lites "Spring Bloom," a women's shoe I never put in a cart, and it takes up the middle of my screen. I've seen it in "Your Items Are Waiting!" earlier today, in "Your cart expires soon!" yesterday, and in this same "Did you forget something?" subject on the 8th. This is the fourth or fifth nag in a row with the same shoe. It feels like a stranger's cart, not mine.
- The "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner is big and readable, and the orange "Complete Your Order" button is easy to tap. Below that, a grid of six random items shows two boys' sneakers, a girl's glitter shoe and a bottle of waterproofer. Only two are men's: a slip-on and a Summits Contour Foam sneaker. The slip-on is the one thing in here I'd look at, but it's tiny and buried.
- The "Members stack an extra 10% off sitewide" bar is the only real offer. It's well down the page, and it's a lot smaller than the 30% off I got on the 8th. Would I click? Probably not. I'd mark it read and wait for the next 30% email. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Ready to check out?" banner is huge, and I can read it without zooming.
- The orange "Complete your order" button stands out, and it's big enough for my thumb.
- The "Members stack an extra 10% off" bar is clear, and it gives a deadline (valid through 10/11).
- The Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit (Altman – Murdock) is in the grid. Slip-on, hands-free and relaxed fit is exactly what I shop for.
- Skechers branding at the top matches what I see on their site, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is a women's floral sneaker I never touched. "Did you forget something?" falls flat when I never had it in a cart.
- The email repeats nearly the same creative I got earlier today and over the past week. It's too much.
- Half the grid is kids' shoes and a spray bottle. That's irrelevant to me.
- The 10% off is buried below the product grid. I had to scroll a long way to find the only real incentive.
- There's no mention of cushioning, arch support or walking, and no prices or ratings on any shoe.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and hard to read. It says the offer excludes a lot of things, and it's only for Skechers Plus members, online.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop showing me a shoe I never carted. Use the men's slip-on I actually browsed, or lead with a men's comfort pick. That would lift this email the most.
- 2. Put the 10% member offer right under the hero, not below the grid.
- 3. Cut the kids' shoes and the spray from the grid. Show men's slip-ins, walking and arch-support styles with prices and star ratings.
- 4. Cap cart reminders at one or two a week. After that, send something new.
- 5. Add a short comfort line, like "Cushioned. Arch-supported. Slip right in," next to the men's picks.
