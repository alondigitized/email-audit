# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that pink floral D'Lites sneaker, chasing me around my inbox for what's now the fourth time this week. "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." — no, you didn't, I never clicked that shoe, never even looked twice at a women's floral sneaker. That's twice this week I've gotten this exact cart-abandon gimmick with the same picture, just different headline copy slapped on top.
- The one thing that saves this from the trash is scrolling past the fake cart pitch into the "here's some more options" grid — that's where the men's slip-ins with arch fit and the Contour Foam pair actually show up, which is more my speed. And the 20% off members code down at the bottom is a real, usable offer if I ever get around to buying anything.
- But leading with a shoe I never touched, for the fourth time, tells me nobody's actually looking at what I do — it's just an algorithm spinning the same wheel. I'm not clicking "Complete Your Order" on a shoe that was never in my cart. I might scroll to the men's section out of habit, but this one's getting marked as read, not forwarded.

## What's working

- The 20% off member code (EXTRA20, valid thru 9/14) near the bottom is a real, specific offer with a clear deadline.
- The secondary product grid finally shows men's styles — the Arch Fit Altman-Murdock slip-in and Contour Foam Cozy Fit leather sneaker are actually in my lane.
- Afterpay/Klarna and "Find a Store" options are visible for anyone doing price-vs-quality math before buying.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — nothing overlapping, images all loaded fine.

## What's weak

- Leading with that pink floral D'Lites cart-abandon nudge for the fourth time this week — I've never clicked it, never browsed it, it's not remotely my shoe.
- "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." is a presumptuous, slightly creepy line when it's flat-out wrong about my behavior.
- Hero section is 100% women's — nothing hands-free, cushioned, or arch-supported for a guy like me until deep in the scroll.
- The real offer (20% off) is buried two-thirds down the email instead of leading with it.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" implies I actually started an order — I didn't, which just erodes trust in the next five emails like this.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop the fake cart-abandon framing entirely when there's no real cart history — lead with the 20% off member offer instead, since that's the one honest, actionable thing in here.
- 2. Segment hero product by gender/browse history — if I've never looked at women's floral sneakers, don't put one front and center for the fourth time.
- 3. Move the men's Arch Fit / Contour Foam options up top where I'll actually see them before I decide whether to keep scrolling.
- 4. Drop "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" copy unless there's an actual cart — swap for something honest like "Still deciding? Here's 20% off."
