## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders structurally (table layout, `lang="en"`, mobile media query at 620px), but the QA run flagged a failing unsubscribe probe, no plain-text part, and about 12 images served over `http://`. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, headers and final landing URLs could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe returned 400** (QA `[FAIL]`): `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkz…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_pos=0&attn_lid=m48…`.
  - The `attn_macro` value decodes to `unsubscribeLink`, so this is the real unsubscribe URL.
  - A 400 on a tokenized per-recipient link may be an artifact of the probe, such as a missing UA or a consumed or replayed token. It is not confirmed broken for real recipients, but it should be verified manually.
- **429 on the primary CTA** ("Complete Your Order") and on "You liked these and you weren't wrong.": both are `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` links. QA marks these as "may be valid". They are rate-limit responses from the Attentive click tracker, not confirmed breakage.
- **All links are wrapped in Attentive redirects** (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`), including "web version". Final destinations were not resolved. 24 of 36 links were skipped by the probe, so coverage is partial (12 probed).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`**.
- **Malformed charset meta**: `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing. This is a real source-level defect.
- **Non-HTTPS images**: 12 images load from `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/…`. They include the logo, the "Complete Your Order" hero (`dfcc8fd7-…jpg`), App Store and Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, and the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons. Clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Missing alt text**:
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` (a content image, also `http://`).
  - The Attentive open-tracking pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`). This is low impact, since it is a tracking pixel.
- **Plain-text version is 0 chars** (QA `[FAIL]`), which also affects accessibility.
- The preheader is padded with `&zwnj;`/soft-hyphen filler and hidden with `display:none`, which is standard practice. No issue found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. I could not confirm that dynamic cart contents rendered, because the HTML is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is present** (link text "Unsubscribe") but failed the probe with a 400, as above. If it is genuinely broken, that is a CAN-SPAM violation, since the opt-out must work.
- **No text part (0 chars)**: this is a deliverability and multipart-compliance gap.
- **Not verifiable**: the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and the physical postal address were not in the data. The sender is `hello@msgs.skechers.com`, while the tracking domains are `attentivemail.com`, `attn.tv` and `emails.skechers.com`. This is typical for Attentive, but alignment should be checked in the raw headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. Every link is an opaque Attentive redirect, and the QA output does not include resolved destinations or UTM parameters. No mismatch is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click-test the unsubscribe link (`attn.tv/p/dvm?…attn_lid=m48`) from a real client. If it truly returns 400, fix it first. It is the only compliance-critical item.
2. Add a multipart plain-text alternative.
3. Switch every `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` image to `https://`. The image host needs a valid cert.
4. Fix the charset declaration with `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`, and fill in `<title>`.
5. Add alt text to `a6ec5198-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. Re-run the link probe with throttling or retry on 429, and resolve redirect chains to confirm the CTA destination and UTM params.
7. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe` / one-click, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
