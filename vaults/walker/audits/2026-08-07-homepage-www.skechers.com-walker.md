# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-08-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a brand chasing a much younger, streetwear-obsessed crowd than me — two twenty-somethings striking a pose on some rooftop steps, not a soul over 30 in sight. They're pushing "Up to 40% off Select Styles" and a kids' discount, which tells me the site's mind is on back-to-school shoppers today, not guys like me looking for something that won't kill my feet on a long walk.

## What's working

- The **"Up to 40% off Select Styles"** banner is easy to spot and gives me a reason to at least tap Shop.
- The category strip — **Slip-ins, Aero Burst, Arch Fit, BOBS** — is right there under the promo, and Slip-ins and Arch Fit are exactly the kind of thing I'd tap into for comfort and ease.
- Page loaded clean, nothing broken or overlapping.

## What's weak

- The hero photo is two young people in an urban fashion shoot — cargo pants, cropped tank top, beanie — nothing about cushioning, arch support, or comfort. It's a look, not a pitch to my feet.
- The top banner is **25% off kids styles**, which does nothing for me — I don't have little ones to shop for.
- No loyalty or rewards callout anywhere I can see, and I do pay some attention to that even if it's not the deciding factor.
- "LIMITED TIME ONLY" has no actual date or countdown, so it doesn't really push me to act today.

## Recommendations

- Swap the streetwear rooftop hero for a lifestyle shot of someone comfortably walking or standing — show the Arch Fit or Slip-in tech actually being used.
- Move "Arch Fit" or "Slip-ins" into the hero banner language itself instead of burying it in the small nav strip.
- Put an actual end date or countdown on the "Limited Time" offer so it feels real.
- Surface a rewards/loyalty teaser above the fold — even a small "Members save more" badge would catch my eye.
