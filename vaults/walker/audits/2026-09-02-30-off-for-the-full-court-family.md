- The $20/$10 storewide coupon up top is a real, clear number I can use no matter what I end up buying.
- Skechers gets its own dedicated block with a specific percentage — 25% off — instead of being buried in a family lineup like the 8/26 New Balance email did to itself.
- No broken images, no overlapping text, nothing glitchy — it all rendered clean on my phone.
- "In Store Only" and "Add Savings to Your Mobile Wallet" — I do go into the store sometimes, and that wallet button is a simple, big tap target I could actually use.

## What's weak

- "Full Court Family" with a young mom, dad, and kid at a basketball game — I'm 62, I'm not that family, and it's the biggest image in the whole email.
- The Skechers block shows zero product — just a logo on a blue background. I want to see the actual shoe before I click, not take it on faith.

- 4. Add one line of comfort language near the Skechers offer — "cushioned, hands-free, all-day comfort" — since that's what actually gets my attention.
