# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- Another "we saved these for you" nag, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. It's the same routine as "Your cart is one click away!" and "Did you forget something?" yesterday, and the "Your Items Are Waiting!" sends on 10/21, 10/23 and 10/28. This one at least shows me shoes, and two of the six are men's. Slip-ins, a Contour Foam sneaker and a waterproof boot are in my wheelhouse.
- The layout is clean and the text is big enough on my phone. But the lineup is half women's sneakers, so it doesn't feel like it was saved for me. The 20% OFF band for Plus members is the only thing that would move me, and it sits below the fold of the product grid. It's the same 20% I've seen since 10/23.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the Contour Foam sneaker or the waterproof boot. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The Skechers logo and blue headline make it easy to know who it's from. The email looks like the site.
- Two men's Slip-ins and a Contour Foam sneaker are right up my alley. "Arch Fit" and "Cozy Fit" in the names tell me it's about comfort.
- The 20% OFF band is big and easy to read. It has a date, "Valid thru 11/2."
- The big plain "SHOP NOW" button is easy to tap with my thumb.
- The shop-by-category list (Men, Sale and so on) is stacked simply. That suits how I browse.

## What's weak

- Three of the six shoes are women's sneakers. After a week of these I'd expect the email to know what I look at.
- There are no prices and no ratings. I can't do my price-versus-quality math from the email.
- The product names are tiny underlined links, harder to read than the rest of the text.
- The 20% offer is buried under the grid, and "for Skechers Plus members" is a hurdle. There's no "Hi, Walker" or points balance like the 10/23 and 10/24 sends had.
- The "No Tricks, Just Treats" Halloween line feels odd on October 30th. Fine print about exclusions is crammed at the bottom, so "20% off" may not cover what I'm looking at.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show only items I actually viewed, with price and a "Slip-in" or "hands-free" tag. Move the 20% Plus offer up under the headline.
- 2. Add "Hi, Walker" and my Plus status, as the 10/23 send did.
- 3. Make the product names bigger and put a "Shop" button on each.
- 4. Stop sending these every day. One reminder is plenty.
