- The shoe photo is big, sharp and easy to see on my phone.
- The body text is a readable size, and the black SHOP NOW button is large enough to tap without fumbling.
- The layout is simple and uncluttered. I know where to look.
- The adidas logo at the top matches the sender, so I trust it's the real thing.

## What's weak

- The shoe looks like a soccer cleat or court shoe, which doesn't match "comfort." I can't tell what the shoe is or what it's called.
- There's no price, no offer, no sizing and no mention of arch support or cushioning. "Softness you won't believe" is a claim with nothing behind it.

- The "Seamless. Sculpted. Climacool…" line at the top is small, gray and underlined, so it looks like a link. It's hard to read.

- 3. Drop the best-sellers banner, or move it below the hero so the email makes one point.
- 4. Give the button specific words, like "Shop men's walking shoes."
- 5. Give me a reason to act now, such as free shipping or a members' price.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound, with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta and responsive breakpoints. The QA run found one hard failure: the plain-text alternative is empty (0 chars). Several images also load over `http://` or have no alt attribute.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their destinations, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unverified.
- **Insecure image sources.** The `arrow` image is served over `http://`, and the same image is flagged twice:
  - `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`
  - The tracking image `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a104ad731f-e1746070-f4c5-4875-a70f-fb2779521c16-000000/lNw5nEide85XEMdSnHpd5y4mO…` is also `http://`. Its `…=452` suffix suggests it is the open pixel. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop it, so opens will be undercounted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/d48059db-99f5-4abd-9956-e59302126a7c?cp_tp=…`. If this is content imagery, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing.
  - The `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…` tracking image. If it is a pixel, it should carry `alt=""`.
- **Ineffective charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing to the parser. This only matters if the Content-Type header does not already specify UTF-8, which I can't confirm from the truncated source.
- **Fonts fall back to Arial.** The `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` `@font-face` rules use only `local('Arial'|'Helvetica'|…)` sources, so the brand fonts never load and rendering relies on the local fallback. This is a design-system choice rather than a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is empty.** QA reports `[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing or too short: Text version is 0 chars`. A missing text alternative hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only clients with nothing. It could be a capture artifact, so check the raw MIME for a `text/plain` part.
- **Unsubscribe, physical address and SPF/DKIM/DMARC could not be assessed.** The HTML is truncated before the footer and no headers were supplied. I am not flagging them.
- **Sender domain.** `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is a dedicated subdomain of the brand, which is the usual setup for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Link destinations and UTM parameters are hidden behind the 13 skipped click-tracking redirects, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` alternative.** Check the raw MIME first, and if it is genuinely empty, generate one from the HTML (QA FAIL).
2. **Serve the `arrow` image and the open pixel over HTTPS.** Use the `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com` hosts (4 of the 6 QA warnings).
3. **Add alt text.** Describe `dv.adidas.com/o/d48059db-…` if it is content, and use `alt=""` on the tracking image (2 QA warnings).
4. **Fix the charset meta.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`, or `<meta charset="UTF-8">`.
5. **Re-run the audit with the full source and headers.** Include the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`, the footer address, and a resolved redirect chain for the 13 tracking links. That would confirm compliance and UTM continuity.
