# ALL-NEW New Balance for the Family
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- New Balance is in the subject line and in the hero, and I do wear New Balance. The top banner is a big yellow block with three chunky sneakers, and I can read "NEW for the Crew!" without my glasses. That's a good start. But "ALL-NEW" oversells it. The email is really the same Nike 30% off and orange coupon stack I've been getting from Shoe Carnival all week. It didn't feel like a New Balance launch.
- Below the hero it turns into a catalog. Nike sale, a Family Athletics Sale, four shoes, then the $10/$25 coupon and wallet barcodes. It's a lot of scrolling for one idea. I'd say it's a decent send and I might click the New Balance hero. I wouldn't forward it. Nobody in my family needs a "Crew" email.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and the yellow banner is easy to see on my phone. The three shoes are big.
- "Shop New Balance" goes straight to a brand I wear, and the shoes look sturdy and cushioned.
- The "Cloud-like comfort" tag on the Skechers Max Cushion shoe is the kind of thing I look for.
- The coupon block is easy to read: $10 off $74.98+ or $25 off $149.98+ with code SAVEMORE. The prices make sense to me.
- Shoe Carnival is a store I know, and the logo and layout match what I'd expect.

## What's weak

- "ALL-NEW" isn't backed up. There's no model name, no price and no cushioning detail under the hero.
- The New Balance link is small, thin text under the image. It's hard to tap with my thumb.
- Nike gets a huge 30% off banner, and the family sale gets a second 30% banner. The offer repeats and crowds out New Balance.
- The product grid is mixed. Two of the four shoes are women's, and none are slip-on or hands-free. No prices are shown either.
- It's a long email, and the fine print and barcodes at the bottom are tiny.
- The preview text is a raw tracking link, so I see junk next to the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make New Balance the offer. Put a specific price or percentage on the hero, plus a comfort detail such as cushioning, wide widths or slip-on. Add a real button.
- 2. Cut the repeated Nike and Family 30% banners down to one.
- 3. Add prices and men's comfort or walking styles to the product grid, and tell me which shoes are cushioned.
- 4. Make every link a big tappable button.
- 5. Fix the preheader so it's real copy, not a link.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh cushioning styles plus $10 off $74.98+ with code SAVEMORE.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a table-based HTML 4.01 Transitional template with extensive client-specific CSS fixes. Two tracking-pixel images have defects: one carries an unresolved `[UNIQUE]` placeholder, and the other has a campaign tag that doesn't match the subject. The HTML was truncated and all 36 tracked links were skipped, so link destinations, the footer and the unsubscribe mechanism couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved placeholder:** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` contains a literal `[UNIQUE]` token. If this is the open-tracking pixel, it was never substituted, so opens are likely not attributed to a recipient.
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261027_nike_athletics_AM` embeds a `utm_campaign=` key/value inside the value of `t=`. The reported URL also ends in a zero-width space (U+200B). That character can break URL matching or parsing.
- **Link probing:** 36 links on `emltrk.com` redirect domains were not probed, so there's no evidence of redirect depth, final destinations, or 4xx/5xx status.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs make up 7,146 of 9,848 characters (73%), above the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk. It is consistent with long tracked URLs and is not a rendering defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `<img>` tags for `ieezzy5m.emltrk.com` and `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` have no `alt`. If they are 1×1 pixels, they need `alt=""`. This is low severity.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` lacks `http-equiv`, and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` lacks `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. The second is a no-op. The `charset` attribute on the first still declares UTF-8.
- **Link affordance:** the global rule `a, a:link, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` strips underlines and link color from every anchor. Unless the template restores styling inline, links lack a visual cue (WCAG 1.4.1).
- **Mobile text size:** `.responsive-text` forces `font-size: 12px !important` at ≤600px. That is small for body copy.
- **Language attribute:** `lang="en"` is present, which is good.
- **Truncation:** the source is truncated, so body structure, `alt` on content images and `role="presentation"` on layout tables could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[UNIQUE]` in the `0di86k1v.emltrk.com` pixel URL (see Section 2).
- No other tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `*|…|*`) were visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA check attributes this to the AgentMail relay. It is a test-harness limitation, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers could not be verified from the truncated source. Treat them as unverified, not as passing.
- The `From:` domain `email.shoecarnival.com` is a branded subdomain, which is appropriate.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign tag mismatch:** the subject is "ALL-NEW New Balance for the Family", but the tracking parameter reads `utm_campaign=SCA_20261027_nike_athletics_AM`. This suggests the template or tracking was cloned from a Nike Athletics send. It would misattribute this send's results in analytics.
- **UTM structure:** the UTM is nested inside `t=` on the tracking redirect rather than appended as standard query parameters on the destination. Whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` reach the landing page can't be confirmed, because the redirects were not followed.
- **Landing-page alignment:** not verifiable, since no destination URLs were resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the substitution for `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` so the token resolves per recipient, and add a pre-send lint that blocks literal `[UNIQUE]`.
2. Correct the campaign tag `SCA_20261027_nike_athletics_AM` to match the New Balance send. Strip the zero-width space from the `ieezzy5m` URL.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Restore visible link styling (underline or color) on text links, and raise the 12px mobile `.responsive-text` size to at least 14px.
6. Re-run QA on the full HTML and capture the raw headers to verify `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address and the unsubscribe link. Follow redirects on a sample of the 36 `emltrk.com` links to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
