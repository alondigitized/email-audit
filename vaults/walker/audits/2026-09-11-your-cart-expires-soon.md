# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- There's that pink floral D'Lites sneaker again — same flower-stitched shoe I've now seen chasing me around my inbox all week under "Did you forget something?" and "Your Items Are Waiting!" Today it's dressed up as "YOU LIKED THESE... AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG," which is a cute line, but I never liked that shoe. I've never clicked it, never looked at it twice. It's a women's floral sneaker and I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants something I can slip on and walk the dog in.
- Below the fold there's actually some relevant stuff — a men's slip-in Arch Fit and a couple of comfort-looking styles mixed in with kids' light-up basketball shoes and boys' Garage sneakers, which tells me nobody's sorted this by who I actually am. The one thing that would've gotten a real look from me, that Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit - Altman - Murdock Sneaker, is buried third row down, small, easy to miss on a phone.
- I'm not clicking this one. Between the wrong hero shoe and the grab-bag of kids' and women's product below it, nothing here says "we know what you shop for." Straight to read-and-ignore, same as the last two "cart" emails this week.

## What's working

- The 20% off member code (EXTRA20) is clear, has a real expiration date (9/14), and is easy to spot in that dark banner.
- There's a men's slip-in listed — "Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit: Altman - Murdock Sneaker" — that's actually in my wheelhouse, even if it's small and buried.
- Curbside pickup, Afterpay/Klarna, and store locator up front are nice practical touches I'd actually use.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe — that pink floral D'Lites — is the same one I've been served three times this week and I've never once engaged with it. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" doesn't land when I never liked it in the first place.
- The product grid mixes boys' light-up sneakers, kids' basketball shoes, and women's styles in with the one or two men's options — feels like a generic "here's everything" dump, not a cart reminder built around me.
- Subject says "cart expires soon" but I don't see an actual cart or item I put there — just a "you liked these" recommendation carousel, which reads like bait.
- Lots of scrolling past irrelevant kids'/women's product before I'd hit anything I'd actually consider.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the men's slip-in Arch Fit or the Altman Murdock slip-in as the hero image, not the pink floral D'Lites — if this is really a cart reminder, show me what I actually looked at.
- 2. Cut the kids' and women's product from a men's-targeted recommendation block, or at minimum segment it below a clearly labeled "for you" section.
- 3. If this is genuinely an abandoned-cart email, show the actual cart item with a price, not a "you liked these" style carousel — that mismatch makes me trust the email less.
- 4. Move the 20% off / EXTRA20 offer higher, above the product grid, since it's the strongest hook here.

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off ends Monday — your cart's waiting`
