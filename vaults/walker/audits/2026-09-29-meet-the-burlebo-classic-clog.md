## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx`) with a solid responsive scaffold, including Outlook and Gmail hacks and a hidden preheader. Two code-level issues are confirmed: seven images are served over `http://`, and the `<meta>` charset and compatibility tags look malformed. Most links, the footer and the headers could not be verified from the truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 24 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure image sources:** Seven images use `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/...`:
  - App Store (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Google Play (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)
  
  Clients that don't proxy images, or that block mixed content, may drop these. The same host is likely to serve HTTPS, so this is a one-line fix per image.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open pixel. It is hidden with `visibility:hidden;display:none`, so it will not fire in clients that block display:none images. Open-tracking data will be inconsistent across clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags:** The source shows `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` with no `http-equiv` attribute. As written, the charset declaration and X-UA-Compatible hint are non-functional. This is only reliable if the source shown is unmodified. Charset should also be declared in the `Content-Type` header.
- **Missing alt text:** The demdex pixel (`event?d_dpid=196107…`) has no alt attribute. It is a hidden tracking pixel, so impact is minimal, but `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is the same on every send, which is a minor issue for web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Working elements:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - The 1×1 open pixel has `alt=""`.
  - The viewport meta tag is correct.
  - The `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `u + .email-body` Gmail hacks are present.
  - The mobile breakpoint is 639px.
  - `font-display: swap` is set on the `@font-face` rules.
- **Font fallback:** The 'TT Crocs' web fonts load from `media.crocs.com`. Most clients ignore `@font-face`, so the fallback stack matters. It is not visible in the truncated source, so I can't assess it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated HTML limits this check.
