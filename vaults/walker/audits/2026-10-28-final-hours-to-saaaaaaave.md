# Final. Hours. to. SAAAAAAAVE.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the sixth or seventh send in about three days, and it's the same SOBRIGHT "buy more, save more" ladder I got in "$15 off...but only till midnight ⏳" and "$15 off ends tomorrow!". The subject, with its stretched-out "SAAAAAAAVE," reads like a shouting teenager. It didn't make me feel any urgency, just tired.
- Once I opened it, the pink banner with $15, $20 and $60 is big and easy to read, even on my phone. The rest is a long scroll of boots and flats, and it's mostly women's shoes. The "Weekly Holiday Recap" block says nothing, and "Shop Women" is the first button I'm offered. I didn't see anything for a man my age who wants a cushioned slip-on.
- I wouldn't click. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it either.

## What's working

- The $15 / $20 / $60 numbers in the pink banner are huge and easy for my eyes to read.
- The code SOBRIGHT sits right under the numbers, so I know how to get the deal.
- The "Shop Men" link is there in the banner, and the top nav has "Men."
- The "Shop by Size" block with a "Shop Wide Width" button is handy for a guy who needs room in the shoe.
- The footer has in-store pickup, free shipping for VIPs and the Rewards sign-up. That's a decent trust cue.

## What's weak

- The subject line is gimmicky. "Final. Hours. to. SAAAAAAAVE." looks like spam, and I've been told "ends tomorrow" and "till midnight" already this week.
- The "Weekly Holiday Recap" hero is vague. It has no offer and no product, and "Shop Women" is its only button.
- Nearly every product shown is a women's boot, flat or heel. I can't find a single men's shoe.
- Nothing in the body mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-on comfort, which is what I shop for.
- The offer is "$15 off $49" with a pile of exclusions in tiny fine print at the bottom. That's hard for me to read without zooming.
- The email is very long, and the tap targets in the size row are small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending this ladder three times a day. One clear final-day reminder would beat five near-identical ones.
- 2. Let me choose men's right away. Put a "Shop Men's Comfort" button in the hero for men on the list, and show men's walking and slip-on shoes.
- 3. Drop the empty "Weekly Holiday Recap" block and put the deadline and the code where my eye lands first.
- 4. Make the fine print larger and tell me up front which brands are excluded.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent, table-based XHTML template with dark-mode support. The automated QA found one broken CTA link (HTTP 400, the SMS opt-in) and a few minor accessibility and deliverability warnings. The 62% pass rate is mostly because only 12 of 83 links were probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA, 400 response:** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link failed its probe. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/GkBUVsNPceB4ZByaEdc3FQ~~/AAQRxRA~/dK0fVl_r69…` (very long encoded redirect token).
  - The QA output shows an empty label before the link text, so the link may not resolve to a valid destination.
  - A 400 from an automated probe on an SMS-opt-in redirect can be a false positive, for example if it expects a mobile user-agent or a deep link. Verify manually in a real client before treating it as a confirmed outage.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 31,999 of 36,951 characters (about 87%) are URLs. The long `ablink.dsw.com` wrapped tracking links bloat the text part and are a spam-filter risk.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 83 links were checked and 71 were skipped, so the other 71 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `o.gif`, src `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261028_P250961_V3_WM-_MI_BAU_15O`. This is a tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
  - The image with src `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/JU-qBbgpfHX-oJoyTMhJ9A~~/AAQRxRA~/XmQI-LBbb9Q65ScbjtVuYQn4VKjp-YIb4XC6PoxafSe6bF1z8BI9J520BX6CT`. It may be an open pixel, but its purpose isn't clear from the data. If it's visible content, it needs real alt text.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute. Both are ignored by clients, and the charset one is the one that matters.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>DSW</title>` is the same for every send.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`) appear in the visible HTML, though the source was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** The QA reported "Authentication-Results header not found". The cause is the AgentMail relay stripping or not exposing the header, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is not evidence of a sender-side failure. The From domain is `e.dsw.com`.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** The QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't shown. The footer and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers are not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. All links go through `ablink.dsw.com` redirect wrappers, which hide the final destination and UTM parameters, so alignment can't be assessed. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261028_P250961_V3_WM-_MI_BAU_15O` on the pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/GkBUVsNPce…`) on mobile and desktop. Fix it if it returns 400 for real users.
2. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif` and to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image if it's decorative or a pixel. Give it descriptive alt text if it's visible content.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Correct the meta tags to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge" />`.
5. Shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part, for example with fewer wrapped links or a short-link domain.
6. Re-run the audit with a full probe of the 71 skipped links, and capture raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) directly rather than through the relay.
