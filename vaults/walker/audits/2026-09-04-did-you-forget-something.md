# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Well, here we are again. Same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sneaker parked under "READY TO CHECK OUT?" like I'm one click away from buying something I have never so much as glanced at. This is the third or fourth time this exact scenario's shown up in my inbox this week, and I'm not tired of Skechers — I'm tired of this one shoe.
- The rest of the email actually isn't bad. Once you scroll past the phantom-cart nonsense, there's a real "Best Sellers" grid with a men's Arch Fit and a men's Delson slip-on sitting right there, plus a flat 20% off for Plus members. That's the stuff that should've been up top.
- I'm not clicking "Complete Your Order" on a shoe I never touched, and I'm sure not forwarding this to anybody. Mark as read, move on.

## What's working

- The 20% off Plus member banner near the bottom is a clean, honest offer with a real deadline (thru 9/7).
- The best-sellers grid has two shoes actually in my lane — Max Cushioning Arch Fit 2.0 and Delson 3.0 slip-ons.
- Layout's clean, nothing overlapping or broken, text is big enough to read on my phone without squinting.

## What's weak

- That pink floral D'Lites again — never been in any cart of mine, and it's the hero image for the fourth time this week.
- The whole top half is built around a purchase I'm not making, so the actual good stuff (best sellers, the 20% deal) is buried below the fold.
- "READY TO CHECK OUT?" assumes I was already halfway through a purchase, which just makes the whole email feel like it's talking to the wrong person.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake-cart framing entirely for a customer with no real cart activity — lead with the 20% off Plus deal and a men's shoe instead, that's the actual sellable moment here.
- 2. Swap the hero product to the Arch Fit or Delson from the best-sellers row — something in my size range, not a floral sneaker.
- 3. Pull the 20% off banner up near the top — it's the strongest offer in the whole email and it's stuck two-thirds of the way down.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus Members: 20% Off Best Sellers Ends 9/7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's Arch Fit — 20% Off for Plus Members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Discount applies automatically at checkout — no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort slip-ons, arch support, 20% off through Sunday`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Attentive-hosted (attentivemail.com) responsive cart-abandonment email; core structure renders correctly, but the unsubscribe link returns a 400 and no plain-text MIME part is present, both of which are compliance-relevant defects.

- 12 of 37 material links were probed per the QA tool's click-likelihood ranking; footer/utility/social links were deprioritized by design, not skipped due to failure.

- All probed marketing links route through Attentive's `ls/click?upn=...` redirect wrapper — consistent tracked-link pattern, no bare/untracked URLs observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Two `<img>` elements missing `alt` text: `de642685-77b3-46fa-950b-5bd9ee6800e1.jpg` and `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`. Screen readers will announce nothing (or filename) for these.
- The tracking pixel (`https://skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...`) also has no `alt`, but this is expected/inconsequential for a 1x1 open-tracking pixel.
- 9 images load over `http://` (non-TLS) instead of `https://`, e.g. `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115.../dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (main logo) and the app-store, payment, curbside, and social icon badges. Mixed content on `http://` may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing TLS-only image loading (Gmail/Outlook proxy this, but some corporate/webmail clients will block it outright).
- Responsive CSS (`@media (max-width:620px)`) and MSO-conditional handling for Outlook (`mso-hide`, `mso-table-lspace`) are present and structured correctly — no structural markup issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens found in the visible/truncated HTML. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken (400).** CAN-SPAM requires a functional opt-out mechanism in every commercial email; a dead unsubscribe link is a direct compliance violation, not just a UX bug. This should be treated as a release blocker.
- **No plain-text MIME part** (0 chars). CAN-SPAM doesn't strictly mandate multipart/alternative, but its absence increases spam-filter scoring risk and is a deliverability best-practice failure, not just cosmetic.
- Authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) cannot be assessed — the provided source is the HTML body only; no raw headers were included in this payload. Recommend pulling full headers separately to confirm alignment for `msgs.skechers.com`.
- Physical mailing address / footer compliance block is outside the truncated HTML snippet shown — cannot confirm presence; flagging as unverified rather than fabricating a pass/fail.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
All links use Attentive's `ls/click?upn=...` redirect wrapper rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs, so campaign attribution (source/medium/campaign) is handled server-side by Attentive at redirect time and isn't inspectable from the raw HTML. Cannot confirm final landing-page UTM alignment without following redirects — recommend a manual click-through to verify the destination URL carries expected `utm_source=attentive` (or equivalent) parameters and lands on a live, matching PDP/cart page rather than a 404.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe link immediately** — 400 response is a CAN-SPAM compliance risk, not just breakage.
2. **Add a plain-text alternative part** to the MIME message to improve deliverability/spam scoring.
3. **Add `alt` text** to the two flagged product/promo images for accessibility.
4. **Switch all `http://` image sources to `https://`** (logo, app badges, payment/social icons) to prevent blocking in TLS-strict mail clients.
5. Re-probe the two 429-rate-limited links (checkout CTA, hero image) manually to confirm they resolve correctly before sign-off.
6. Manually follow one tracked link end-to-end to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page validity, since this can't be verified from HTML alone.
