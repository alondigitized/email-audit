# Our biggest sale of the year is now live!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is the one I was waiting for after that week of cart nags. The big red "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" on black is impossible to miss. It also finally has no women's black Contour Foam shoe with rose-gold eyelets staring at me. Skechers actually greeted me by name, "Hi, Walker," and showed my 1000 points with a $5 reward. That felt like they know me.
- Then I scrolled and got a wall of banners: shoes, boots, apparel, sale styles, doorbusters, and a stack of fine print. The first two shoe tiles are women's, and the six doorbuster shoes are small with tiny captions I'd have to squint at. I'd click, mostly for the Plus member code and the $49.99 doorbusters. I'd probably still have to hunt for a men's walking slip-in. I wouldn't forward it. It's a decent sale email but not a personal one.

## What's working

- The black and red "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" hero is big, readable on my phone, and says "SHOP THE SALE" plainly.
- "Hi, Walker" with my 1000 points and the $5 redeem link makes me feel like a known customer.
- The Plus members' "EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING, CODE: BF2026" is clear, with the code in large type.
- The doorbuster banner with "STARTING AT $49.99" gives me a concrete price to judge against Hoka and Brooks.
- This one matches the Black Friday 30% early access I saw Saturday and earlier today, so it feels honest and consistent.

## What's weak

- Two stacked banners offer different things, 30%-50% off and Plus members' extra 15%, and I had to read twice to understand how they combine.
- Category tiles carry different discounts (30%, 40%, 50%, "up to 50%"). It's a lot of math for a quick phone read.
- The doorbuster shoes are tiny, with captions I can't read without zooming. There are no prices under them and no "slip-in" or "arch support" call-out.
- The fine print at the bottom is a very long block of tiny type with exclusions. That makes "biggest sale" feel a bit buried, and "up to" claims always make me suspicious.
- Only one men's shoe tile points at comfort; mostly it's apparel and women's shoes. Nothing about Hands Free Slip-ins by name.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the doorbuster shoes larger, with a price on each. Add a "Slip-ins" callout and make a men's walking tile first for male subscribers.
- 2. Merge the two offers into one line: "30-50% off, plus 15% more for Plus members."
- 3. Cut the fine print down to a readable summary with a link for the details.
- 4. Give 62-year-old shoppers a bigger "SHOP MEN'S" button near the top.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed merge-token failure (an empty greeting name) and one malformed tracking-pixel URL with no protocol. It also has insecure `http://` image sources, a broken charset/compat `<meta>` declaration, and accessibility gaps. Compliance and UTM checks can't be confirmed, because the HTML is truncated and 43 links were skipped.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Protocol-less pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1">` has no scheme. Clients will resolve it as a relative path, so the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking pixel will 404 or never fire.
- **Insecure image sources:**
  - `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026`
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`

  Clients that block mixed content may drop these. The second is a content image on the brand's own domain, which is likely to cause a visible broken image. The first is an open-tracking pixel, which would fail silently.
- **Unverified links:** 43 click-redirect links on `click.emails.skechers.com` were not probed, so their destination health is unknown. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** There are 5 pixels in the hidden preheader block: SFMC open (`click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx`), 3 × `beacon.krxd.net`, and Return Path. A sixth (ink1000) sits in a separate 1×1 table. The `krxd` `partner_uid=9153f33f…ed00` is a 64-hex value, which looks like a SHA-256 hash of an identifier. That is a third-party data-sharing point to review for privacy compliance.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed `<meta>` tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect. The charset is then left to the HTTP headers, which risks mojibake on `®` and similar characters.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - QA flagged `o.gif` and `dde00662-….png`.
  - The `pixel.app.returnpath.net` image and the three `beacon.krxd.net` images in the visible source also have no `alt`. The ink1000 and krxd pixels are decorative, so this is low severity. The content PNG needs alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`.
- **Layout tables:** The outer layout tables in the visible source lack `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Preheader:** The `div.preheader` hidden text is implemented correctly. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,` because the name field is empty. This is a broken experience. The fix is a fallback value in the SFMC `%%FirstName%%`/AMPscript logic, or a name-less greeting variant. If the seed address has no profile name, this is a seed-data artifact, but the template still has no fallback.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results missing:** This is expected via the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` could not be verified. This is not a confirmed sender defect.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** The footer, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are outside the truncated source and could not be assessed.
- **From address:** A `no-reply@` sender is not a CAN-SPAM violation. It only matters if no other reply or contact path appears in the footer, which I couldn't confirm.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** All 43 tracking links were skipped and the visible source has no CTA `href`s. UTM parameters and destination alignment are unverified.
- **Campaign ID:** `PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026` appears consistently in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels. That fits the Black Friday campaign and the "30-50% off sitewide" preheader.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `https://` to the Return Path pixel (`https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`) or remove it.
2. Switch `ink1000.com` and `image.emails.skechers.com` image sources to `https://`, and confirm both hosts serve valid TLS.
3. Add a fallback to the first-name merge so the greeting never renders as `Hi ,`.
4. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
5. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content PNG and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels. Also add `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on the layout tables.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML and raw headers to check UTMs, unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe`, the physical address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
