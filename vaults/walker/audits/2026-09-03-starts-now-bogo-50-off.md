# STARTS NOW 👇 BOGO 50% OFF!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off got my attention for half a second — that's real money, not a "spend $75 get $10" shell game — but then I scrolled and it's the same story as every other DSW email this week: six shoes up top and every single one is a women's clog, mary jane, or bootie. Nothing here says "hey Walker, here's a slip-on with some cushion in it." The "Back to School" banner at least has a sneaker on someone's foot, which is the closest thing to relevant real estate in the whole email.
- I wouldn't click. There's a MEN link in the nav bar, sure, but making me hunt for it while the whole hero rail is boots and clogs tells me exactly who they built this send for, and it isn't me. Straight to archived, no forward — my buddy Ron doesn't need to see mary janes either.

## What's working

- BOGO 50% off is a real, concrete offer — no vague "up to" language, it's right there in giant type.
- "SHOP MEN" is present in both the top nav and the black banner links, so at least the door is open even if nothing behind it is shown.
- Size grid (5-11) and wide width/wide calf options show they think about fit, which matters to me even if these are women's sizes.
- Shopping Made Easy icons — free shipping, in-store pickup, app, 15% off for texts — are clear and easy to scan without squinting.
- DSW VIP callout (5% back, free shipping, birthday gift) is a real loyalty benefit spelled out plainly.

## What's weak

- Every hero product image is a women's shoe — clogs, mary janes, suede booties. Zero comfort-brand or men's casual shoes above the fold.
- "So New, So You" — I don't know who "you" is here, but it's not a 62-year-old guy shopping for arch support.
- No mention of cushioning, arch support, or slip-on comfort anywhere — the stuff that actually gets me to click is just absent.
- Back to School section is a nice change of pace visually but still isn't a shoe category I'm shopping.
- This is the same dark-hero, small-grid layout DSW's run all week (the booties emails) — just swapped the product photos and the offer math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual men's comfort shoe — a walking shoe, a slip-on, something with a cushioned sole — in at least two of those six hero tiles. Right now the algorithm's picking for someone else entirely.
- 2. Add one line of copy under the BOGO banner naming a comfort feature ("memory foam," "arch support," "all-day cushion") so guys like me know there's something in this sale for us before we even scroll.
- 3. Swap "SO NEW, SO YOU" for something that doesn't assume a single shopper — or better, split it into "New for Her" / "New for Him" rails like the Back to School email semi-attempted.
- 4. Move the men's shortcut higher — right now it's a small nav link competing with five other categories; a dedicated men's tile would actually get tapped.
