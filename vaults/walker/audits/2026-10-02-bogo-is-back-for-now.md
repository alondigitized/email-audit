# BOGO is back...for now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, a plain BOGO with a deadline, and no pink flower sneaker. After a week of that thing following me around, I'll take it. "Hi, Walker" with my Member badge and 1000 points up top at least tells me they know me. The big orange 50% OFF is hard to miss, and I didn't need to squint on my phone.
- I still have to hunt for what I came for. The four boxes are Men's, Women's, Girls' and Boys'. The men's shoe is a gray-green Skechers Glide-Step type sneaker, so it's close to what I wear, but it's not slip-in. Nothing says "hands-free," "arch support" or "cushioned." Since I buy on comfort, I'd click through to see if the men's shoes qualify, but I'd go in skeptical. The fine print says "select regular-priced shoes."
- Verdict: I'd click once, check the price math against Hoka and Brooks, and not forward it. It's a fair email that's easy to read, but it never talks about my feet.

## What's working

- The 50% OFF is huge and easy to read without zooming. I get the offer in two seconds.
- "Hi, Walker," the Member badge and the 1000 points make me feel like a known customer, not a stranger.
- "Valid thru 10/7/26" is a real deadline. It's five days away, not a fake countdown.
- "Discount applied at cart" tells me I don't need a code.
- The SHOP NOW button is wide and green, easy to tap with my thumb.

## What's weak

- "Buy one, get one 50% off or 20% off a single pair*" has two offers in one line, and the asterisk makes me think there's a catch. The fine print says it excludes sale styles and "prior purchases."
- Nothing here mentions comfort, Slip-ins, arch support or cushioning, which is why I buy shoes.
- Only one of the four tiles is for me, and the pairs shown are a women's suede sneaker, girls' boots and boys' pixel shoes. Half of this email is aimed at people I'm not buying for.
- It doesn't name the shoes or show prices, so I can't do my own price-versus-quality math.
- The fine print is tiny, and the terms I'd need (exclusions, "must be logged in") are buried at the bottom.
- I keep getting emails from Skechers. This is the fifth or sixth since 9/17, so I'm tuning out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the men's tile bigger and show a specific Slip-ins model with a price, such as "Slip-ins: no bending, no tying. BOGO 50% off." That would lift this email the most for me.
- 2. Pick one offer for the hero. Lead with BOGO and move the 20% single-pair line into the small print or a second line.
- 3. Add a comfort line under the hero, like "Cushioned, arch-supported styles included."
- 4. Put the key exclusions in plain type near the button, not at the bottom.
- 5. Space out the sends, so I'm not getting one every day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering/personalization failure, an empty greeting (`Hi ,`). It also has a malformed Return Path pixel URL and several `http://` or alt-less images. The HTML provided was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe block and headers could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so it 404s or fails to load. Any seed or deliverability measurement that depends on it is lost.
- **Plain HTTP tracker:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_FULL_EN_10022026` (QA WARN). Gmail's image proxy and some clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS images, so this pixel is unreliable.
- **Unverified links:** 28 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA WARN). Their destinations are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party beacons:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` pixels (`1x1_usermatch.gif` ×2, `1x1_ad_impression.gif`) sit in a `display:none` div. The `partner_uid` on the first is a 64-hex value (`9153f33f…ed00`), consistent with a hashed email. This is third-party identity syncing, and it is a privacy-disclosure concern rather than a functional bug.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (QA WARN):** `o.gif` (ink1000) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The PNG is on `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`, so it is also a non-HTTPS image that may be blocked, and with no alt it is invisible to screen readers.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declares anything.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation profile.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," because the name field resolved empty. There is no fallback or conditional around the token, so a blank first-name value ships a visibly broken salutation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** the truncated HTML cuts off before the footer, and no SPF/DKIM/DMARC or `List-Unsubscribe` header values were supplied. I can't confirm or deny the unsubscribe link, physical address or `List-Unsubscribe-Post`.
- **From domain:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a subdomain used with the `click.`/`image.emails.skechers.com` hosts, which is consistent. Authentication results are not shown.
- **Privacy:** the hashed-identifier krxd beacons in section 2 may warrant disclosure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITEBUNDLE_FULL_EN_10022026` is consistently applied in the ink1000 and krxd pixels.
- **Link-level checks not possible:** the visible links are SFMC click-wrapped, and the probe skipped all 28. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified from the available data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the greeting merge, e.g. `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstname),"Hi there,",CONCAT("Hi ",@firstname,","))=%%`. Alternatively, suppress the salutation when the name is empty.
2. Fix the Return Path pixel to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. Move `ink1000` and `image.emails.skechers.com/…dde00662…png` to `https://`. Ask the ink1000 vendor for an HTTPS endpoint, or drop the pixel.
4. Add `alt=""` to the decorative tracking pixels and meaningful alt text to the PNG if it carries content.
5. Fix the meta tags (`http-equiv="Content-Type"`, `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`), add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, plus the raw headers, to check the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Resolve the click-wrapped links to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
