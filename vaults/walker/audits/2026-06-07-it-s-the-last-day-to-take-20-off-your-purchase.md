# It's the last day to take $20 off your purchase
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Opened it, saw the "$20 off $100" banner — and my first thought was, *didn't I just get this same deal this morning?* Because I did. Famous sent "Your closet is calling" with the exact same $20 off $100 offer earlier today, June 7th. So before I even get to the Skechers section, I'm already a little numb to it.
- That said, once I'm inside the email, it's one of the better ones they've sent me this week. The Skechers hero with a guy roughly my age, in khaki shorts and a casual plaid shirt, is about as on-target as this brand gets with me. And "Shop Men's Walkers" — that's my section, full stop. If I hadn't already seen this offer twice in 48 hours, this might've pulled me over to the site.
- The preview text is flat-out broken — it's showing a raw tracking URL in my inbox. That's the kind of thing that makes a 62-year-old like me wonder if the email is legitimate before I even open it. I'd probably still click because I know Famous Footwear, but it planted a seed of doubt. I'd mark this as read, maybe click the Men's Walkers section, but I'm not forwarding it to anyone.

## What's working

- **"Shop Men's Walkers" is dead center in the email** — that's my category, and seeing it called out by name is exactly what gets me scrolling.
- **The Skechers hero model looks like me** — middle-aged guy, relaxed fit, khaki shorts, walking shoe. That's not an accident and I appreciate it.
- **$20 off $100 is simple math** — I don't need to decode it. I understand immediately whether I have enough in my cart to use it.
- **"Last day" urgency feels credible** — knowing this deal has been running for days, "last day" actually lands. I believe it.
- **"Top rated" section below the fold** — social proof matters to me; I want to know what's working for other guys before I commit.

## What's weak

- **Preview text is a raw URL** — in my inbox it looks like a phishing link. Worst possible first impression before I've even opened the thing.
- **Same offer I got this morning** — Famous already sent me "$20 off $100" today. Seeing it again from the same brand on the same day makes it feel less like a deal and more like they're shouting.
- **CTA button copy is generic** — I can see buttons but the copy reads like "Shop Now" rather than something specific to me, like "Browse Men's Walkers."
- **Women's and kids sections mixed in below** — once I scroll past the men's area, it gets noisy fast. I lose the thread.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preview text immediately** — this is the single highest-ROI fix. It's currently showing a raw tracking URL; it should be something like "Final hours — Men's Walkers, Skechers, and more." A broken preheader makes even a loyal subscriber pause.
- 2. **Lead the subject with "Skechers" or "Men's Walkers"** — the $20 off is the mechanic; what I actually want to buy is a comfort walker. Give me the product, then the deal.
- 3. **Trim the offer cadence** — two "$20 off $100" emails in one day trains me to wait for the next one instead of acting now.
- 4. **Make the hero CTA specific** — swap generic "Shop Now" for "Shop Men's Walkers" so the button matches the section it leads to.
- 5. **Add one product callout with fit language** — even a single line like "memory foam insole" or "wide-width available" would seal the deal for me.

## Technical Audit — Famous Footwear "Last Day $20 Off"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but has two issues that warrant immediate attention before future sends: a preheader populated with copy from a different brand (Skechers), and a LiveContent block served over HTTP that will be blocked or flagged in modern clients. Accessibility coverage is near-zero across 30 images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken links detected,** but link validation was blocked for all 30 click-redirect URLs routed through `click.email.famousfootwear.com` — destinations unverifiable without following redirects.

- Open pixel correctly placed above fold: `https://click.email.famousfootwear.com/open.aspx?QFPG7SAGRAVE3KOGEGKXZ7FQKU.70253`
- All CTA links use the same redirect domain and include `qs=` encoded query strings — standard ESP wrapping, no evidence of raw untracked URLs

No issues found with link structure itself.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**High severity — HTTP image asset (will be blocked):**
```
src="http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5MDB9..."
```
This Salesforce Marketing Cloud LiveContent block is served over plain HTTP. Gmail, Outlook.com, and iOS Mail all proxy or block mixed-content images. The dynamic content panel (likely a loyalty/reward block) will render as a broken image for most recipients.

**Alt text missing on 30 of 31 images.** The logo image (`3b6a7dc1`) has `alt=""` (acceptable for decorative), but all product and content images omit alt entirely — non-decorative images require descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-blocked rendering.

**Viewport disables pinch-to-zoom:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
`user-scalable=0` / `maximum-scale=1` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Users with low vision cannot zoom.

**Malformed charset meta tag:**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />
```
Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute. Browsers tolerate this but it is technically invalid and may affect rendering in legacy clients.

**CSS typo in inline style:**
```html
style="...lineapacing:20px..."
```
`lineapacing` is not a valid CSS property (`line-height` or `letter-spacing` was likely intended). Silently ignored by all clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Preheader contains wrong brand name — likely a template reuse error:**
```html
<div class="preheader" style="font-size:1px;display:none !important">
  Skechers has the perfect gift for dad.

```
The send is from Famous Footwear; the preheader copy references Skechers. This text renders in Gmail and Apple Mail inbox preview rows. No unfired merge tokens (e.g. `%%firstname%%`, `{{name}}`) were visible in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance

Cannot fully assess without the email footer — the HTML was truncated before the unsubscribe block. From what is visible:

- Sender domain `famousfootwear.com` aligns with the From header `famousfootwear@email.famousfootwear.com` — no domain spoofing signal
- Physical mailing address and one-click unsubscribe link are required by CAN-SPAM; presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated source
- Authentication headers (DKIM, SPF, DMARC pass/fail) are not available in this audit — would require raw message headers

**Deliverability flag:** Plain-text alternative is 79.6% URLs (9,965 of 12,517 chars). Spam filters penalize plain-text parts that are mostly link strings; this pattern is consistent with an auto-generated plain-text version that converts every tracked redirect URL verbatim.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 30 destination URLs are wrapped by the ESP click-redirect and were not probed by QA. UTM parameter presence on final landing pages cannot be confirmed. If the ESP is not configured to append UTM params through the redirect, attribution to this send will be lost in GA4/analytics.

No issues found with the click domain itself (`click.email.famousfootwear.com`).
