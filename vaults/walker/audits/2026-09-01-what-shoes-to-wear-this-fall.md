# What shoes to wear this fall ✨🍂
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again — this is the same story as yesterday's bootie email, just with a trend-forecast bow on it. Booties, animal print flats, tall boots, "reimagined loafers" with a cropped denim leg and a cowprint purse — every single model in here is a woman. I scrolled all the way down hoping for a men's trend roundup and instead I got a "SHOP ALL WOMEN" button in big black letters. That's not even subtle, that's DSW telling me straight out who this email is for, and it isn't me.
- The one shoe in the whole thing that could pass for something I'd wear — those black-and-white retro sneakers — is modeled by a woman lying on the floor in a green sweatsuit, so even that gets filed under "not my section." I've got no interest in boot math, animal print, or size charts that top out at an 11 with a "wide calf" option. Not clicking, not forwarding — this one's getting archived same as yesterday's.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout — one trend per block, easy to scroll even for me on a phone.
- Booties starting "FROM $59.99" up top is a real, specific number, I'll give them that.
- Big legible fonts and buttons — no squinting needed here at least.

## What's weak

- Every model, every shoe, every "trend" is women's — third day running I've gotten an email like this from DSW.
- "SHOP ALL WOMEN" is the only shop-by-gender button on the whole page — no equivalent for men anywhere.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, or arch support — it's all about "vibes" and "boot math," not how the shoe feels on your feet.
- Size chart runs 5 to 11 with "wide calf," which tells me exactly who this is built for, and it's not a 62-year-old guy.
- Zero mention of Rewards points balance or anything personalized, even though I'm apparently a member based on the footer perk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender before sending — if DSW has men's inventory (they do, I've seen it in Tuesday's email), put a men's block in here too, even just one.
- 2. Swap "boot math" and animal-print copy for something that speaks to fit and comfort if it's going to a customer flagged male in their system.
- 3. Add a men's-specific CTA next to "SHOP ALL WOMEN" — right now there's no parallel option at all.
- 4. Lead with an actual dollar offer for guys' shoes, not just booties.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall's easiest slip-on styles for guys, from $59.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New fall arrivals: comfort styles for him`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, arch-support fall shoes just landed.`
