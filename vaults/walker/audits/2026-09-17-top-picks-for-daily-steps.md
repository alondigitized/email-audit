# Top picks for daily steps
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- Well, now, this one I can actually use. "Walking" is right there in the header — "Built to walk the walk" — and for the first time in a while HOKA's put a shoe front and center that's meant for a fella like me putting in his daily steps, not sprinting across a bridge or diving into a mosh pit. The hero shot's still got some young buck in neon socks striking a pose on a city street, but at least the copy underneath talks about support, cushioning, and a lightweight feel — that's the stuff I actually care about.
- Where it loses me a bit is the actual product lineup — every single shoe shown is a running shoe (Bondi, Clifton, Gaviota), not a dedicated walking shoe, and there's no mention of arch support specifically, which is the thing that makes or breaks it for my feet. But I'll give them credit for closing with "Walk far. Walk wide." and calling out wide and X-wide sizing — that's a real signal they know guys like me exist.
- Compared to that "Marathon Pack" nonsense from yesterday (2026-09-16) or the crowd-surfing fiasco from a week back, this is a real step in the right direction. I wouldn't forward it to a buddy, but I might actually click through to look at the wide sizes.

## What's working

- Header "Built to walk the walk" and body copy about support, cushioning, and lightweight feel speaks directly to what I'm shopping for.
- Wide and X-wide sizing callout at the bottom — that's rare and it matters to guys my age with wider feet.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — one shoe per row, big product shots, easy to read without squinting.
- Buy 2+, get 20% off member deal is clearly stated up top in a banner I can't miss.

## What's weak

- Every shoe pictured is a running shoe, not a walking shoe — the headline promises one thing, the product grid delivers another.
- Hero model looks like he's about to run a 5K, not stroll to the mailbox — still skews younger and more athletic than me.
- No mention of arch support anywhere, which is the single biggest factor in whether I buy.
- Six nearly-identical shoe photos in a row gets a little monotonous scrolling on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap at least one or two of the six shoes for an actual walking-specific model, or relabel the section so it's honest about what's being sold.
- 2. Call out arch support explicitly in the body copy — it's the single word that gets my thumb moving toward "Shop."
- 3. Put an older or more relatable guy in the hero shot, or at least someone just walking rather than posed mid-stride like a runner.
- 4. Move the "Walk far. Walk wide." wide-sizing message up higher — that's the strongest hook for me and it's buried at the bottom.
