# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — the pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom, front and center under a "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner, and I want to state for the record: that shoe has never once been in my cart. I don't own anything floral, I never clicked on it, and Skechers seems determined to pretend otherwise. This is now the seventh time this week I've flagged this exact sneaker chasing me around my inbox like it owes me money.
- Buried below it, though, is stuff I'd actually consider — the Arch Fit Arcade slip-ins, the Max Cushioning Arch Fit 2.0 running shoe, the relaxed-fit Altman. And there's a real offer this time: 20% off for Plus members, through 9/7. That's useful information, it's just three scrolls down from a lie about my shopping cart.
- I'm not clicking the "Complete Your Order" button because there's no order to complete. If the 20% off banner had been the headline instead of an afterthought, this would've been a keeper. As it stands, into the "seen it, ignore it" pile it goes — not deleting, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The 20% off member banner is a real, dated offer (thru 9/7) and it's easy to read.
- Men's Max Cushioning Arch Fit 2.0 and the relaxed-fit Altman are actual shoes in my category, cushioned and sensible.
- Afterpay/Klarna and "Find a Store" options are visible for anyone who shops that way.
- Layout is clean, big buttons, nothing overlapping or broken.

## What's weak

- The whole email is built around a "complete your order" hook for a shoe I never touched — that's the sixth or seventh time this exact bait has landed in my inbox this week.
- Hero image and top CTA are both wasted on a product with zero relevance to me — floral, women's, not remotely my style.
- The actual good offer (20% off) is stuck two-thirds of the way down instead of leading.
- No sign Skechers is tracking that I keep ignoring this shoe — feels like I'm shouting into a robot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the fake "abandoned cart" framing entirely when there's no real cart activity — lead with the 20% off Plus offer instead, that's the actual reason to open.
- 2. Swap the hero product to something from my browsing history, not a shoe I've never clicked.
- 3. Move the member discount banner to the top of the email, above the fold.
- 4. Rotate creative — I've seen "Ready to Check Out," "Your Items Are Waiting," and "Did You Forget Something" all push this same shoe this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 20% off ends 9/7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your arch support upgrade is 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No cart tricks — just 20% off regular-priced styles for Plus members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned. Comfortable. 20% off through Sunday.`
