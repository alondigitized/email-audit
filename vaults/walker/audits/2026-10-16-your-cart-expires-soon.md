It's close to the same cart nag as "Your Items Are Waiting!" and "Did you forget something?" from earlier this week, and the subject is the same one I got on the 14th. I'm getting tired of it.
- Below the hero there's a grid of six shoes. Only one is a men's shoe, the John Deere waterproof Slip-in boot. Two are boys' sneakers with toy cars built into the soles. The 20% off for members is a decent offer, but it's buried below all that. I'd skim it and move on.

## What's working

- The "Skechers" logo is at the top, so I know who it's from. It's an easy name to trust.
- The "20% OFF" banner for members is big and readable. The dates are there too: valid through 10/21.
- The men's Slip-ins John Deere boot is the one shoe I'd actually look at, since it's hands-free and waterproof.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals, Sale) is big and easy to tap on a phone.
- Both "Complete Your Order" and "Shop All Best Sellers" are clear buttons.

## What's weak

- The hero shoe is a women's floral sneaker I never looked at. "You liked these" feels wrong and a little creepy.
- The subject says my cart "expires soon." I don't see a cart, a price, or a deadline anywhere in the email.
- The grid is mostly women's and boys' shoes. Only one of the six is made for a man like me.
- The product captions are tiny, and none of the shoes shows a price. I can't tell what anything costs or what the discount saves.
- The 20% off sits below the grid and is only for "select full-priced styles." The fine print excludes sale items and more, so the offer isn't as good as it looks.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support, or comfort, which is why I buy shoes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a real cart if there is one: the actual item, its price, and its size. If there isn't a cart, drop the cart language. Send me a men's slip-on as the hero, not a shoe from a different category.
- 2. Move the 20% member offer to the top, next to the hero shoe, with a button that says "Shop Slip-ins, 20% off."
- 3. Put prices in the captions, and add a short comfort note such as "Arch Fit" or "Air-Cooled Memory Foam."
- 4. Cut the boys' sneakers from the grid unless I've shopped for kids.
- 5. Use bigger caption text for readers like me.
