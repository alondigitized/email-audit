# Walker Miles — Skechers.com Mobile Journey Review
**Date: May 18, 2026 | Yesterday's Score: 4/10**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Look, I came here ready to buy. Skechers put the right shoe — Hands Free Slip-ins — right on the front page, and I thought this was finally going to be a smooth trip. Got through the menu just fine, found the waterproof Glide-Step Pro, liked the price, went to check my cart... and got a completely blank white screen. Nothing. Not even an error message. My cart disappeared into thin air. On top of that, the login never actually registered — that "Members Only — Login" reminder kept mocking me on every single page. And when I tried searching "comfort shoes," the first two results were Women's shoes. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for cushioned walking shoes. That's not a small miss. The bones of this site are decent, but the critical moments — login, cart, search relevance — all failed me today.

---

## 2. Business Impact Score: 4/10

**No change from yesterday.** The homepage and navigation held steady, but a blank cart page is an outright abandoned transaction. The login failure and women-first search results compound the problem. If anything, the blank cart is a regression that could cost real revenue today.

---

## 3. What's Working

**BOGO deal is impossible to miss.** That bright blue "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart" banner rode along with me on every single page. I saw it nine times. For someone who watches price-vs-value closely, that kind of persistent deal visibility earns trust.

**Hero banner nailed the pitch.** Homepage leads with Hands Free Slip-ins / Glide-Step front and center — my exact category. Didn't have to hunt for it. "SHOP NOW" button is big, wide, easy to tap with a thumb. Good first impression.

**Men's menu navigation is genuinely good.** The flyout drawer (Step 4) is clean, full-width rows, large readable text, plenty of tap space between items. "Shoes," "Comfort Technologies," "Hands Free Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Extra Wide Fit" — all right there. A 62-year-old doesn't need to squint or guess.

**Hands Free Slip-ins category page (Step 5) is solid.** "281 Results," width filter chips (Wide Fit, Extra Wide Fit, Medium Fit) immediately visible at the top. Filter and Sort are accessible. Breadcrumb trail "Men > Shoes" confirms I'm in the right place.

**Product page text and pricing are readable.** $120.00 price in large font, deal message "Buy 1, Get 1 50% OFF! Applied at Cart" in red — clear. Product name is big and wraps cleanly. The Hands Free Slip-ins badge gives me confidence about the technology.

**Size selector is well-designed.** Large square tap targets for each size, runs from 7.0 to 14.0 — good range. The red "Please select a size" prompt (Step 7) with the warning triangle is the correct behavior; it didn't just silently fail.

**ADD TO CART button is excellent.** Full-width, navy blue, sticky at the bottom of the screen. Hard to miss, easy to tap.

**Search volume is reassuring.** "Comfort shoes" returned 1,452 results — plenty of inventory depth.

---

## 4. What's Weak

**Cart page is completely blank. (Critical — Step 8)** I tapped through to my cart and got a white void. No items, no message, no error, nothing. I don't know if my shoe is in there. I don't know if the BOGO deal is applying. I'm not checking out blind. This is a transaction-killer.

**Login never took. (Step 3 → all subsequent steps)** The "Members Only — Earn a $5 Reward on $75+ Login | details" prompt appeared on *every* page of my journey. After logging in, nothing changed. No name, no personalized greeting, no confirmation I was recognized. Every page treated me like a stranger. If I'm a loyalty member, I want to feel like one.

**"Comfort shoes" search served women's products first. (Step 9)** Both visible results — "Arch Fit Arcade - See Ya There" and "Contour Foam - Cozy Fit" — are labeled Women's. I searched without a gender filter because I assumed the site would use my account context or at least randomize gender fairly. It did neither. The results count (1,452) sounds great until you realize the top of the pile isn't for you.

**Product has zero reviews. (Step 6)** "No Reviews" on a $120 shoe is a red flag for me. I cross-shop Hoka and New Balance and those pages are full of real people telling me how their feet feel. No reviews means I have no social proof to close the deal. I'd lean toward checking DSW or Zappos to read what actual wearers say.

**Popup handling was invisible (Step 2).** There was no real popup to dismiss — the notification bar just cycled from "Members Only" to "Spring Apparel Sale 25% OFF Apparel." Not a bug exactly, but it means the journey step didn't test anything meaningful. If the site normally fires an email capture modal and it didn't appear today, that's either a miss or a cookie-based suppression that went unnoticed.

**No cushioning / arch support detail visible on product page. (Step 6)** I can see the shoe looks reasonable, it says "Waterproof" and "Slip-ins," but I can't see any callout for Arch Fit, Air-Cooled Memory Foam, or cushioning specs in this viewport. For my buying decision, comfort technology details are table stakes — if they're buried below the fold, I may not scroll far enough.

**Account icon shows no logged-in state throughout.** The person-silhouette icon in the nav never changed to indicate I was signed in. No name, no different icon color, nothing. Visually, the site looked identical logged-in and logged-out.

---

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page immediately.** A blank cart is lost revenue on the table right now. This needs to be the #1 priority — not a UX polish issue, a break-fix.

2. **Validate the login flow on mobile.** Something is broken between tapping "Login" and the session being established. The persistent "Login" prompt after authentication is either a session cookie failure or a rendering issue. Either way, fix it and show the user their name or a "Hi, Walker" somewhere obvious.

3. **Make search gender-aware.** When a logged-in male account searches "comfort shoes," men's products should default to the top. At minimum, add a visible gender toggle at the top of search results so I can fix it in one tap.

4. **Add comfort technology callouts above the fold on PDPs.** Put Arch Fit, Air-Cooled Memory Foam, or whatever tech this shoe has in a scannable badge or bullet list *before* the size selector. That's the deciding factor for buyers like me.

5. **Prioritize or solicit reviews for new product launches.** A $120 shoe with zero reviews is a conversion wall. Seed early reviews from loyalty members or add a "Be the first to review" CTA that makes it feel like a community act, not a gap.

6. **Confirm BOGO deal calculation in cart.** Even if the cart is fixed, show a line-item breakdown of the BOGO discount. "1 item at $120, BOGO discount applied: -$60" gives me confidence the deal is actually working.

---

## 6. Bottom Line

Skechers knows what I'm looking for — they put Hands Free Slip-ins on the front page and gave me wide-fit filters without asking. The navigation is genuinely good for older mobile users. But three of the four most important moments in a purchase journey — logging in, completing the cart, searching by intent — all failed today. I landed on a blank white cart page after picking out a $120 shoe I was ready to buy. That's the whole ballgame right there. Same score as yesterday: **4/10.** Fix the cart, fix login, fix search gender defaults, and this becomes a 7 without touching anything else.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

Platform: Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware), evident from `/on/demandware.static/Sites-USSkechers-Site/` paths. The stack carries 20+ third-party script tags injected via GTM and a consent manager (`vice-managed-src` attribute pattern = TrustArc/Vice). View Cart is the most broken page in this journey — a 429 on `/cart/` causes the page to return incomplete HTML, missing `<title>`, `lang`, and landmark structure. The Kasada bot-detection fingerprinting endpoint (`/149e9513.../fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.412`) is rate-limiting itself 5 times across the session, indicating a retry storm from the SDK.

---

## Accessibility

**15 violations across 3 pages.**

**Homepage (8 violations):**
- `aria-required-parent` [critical] — orphaned ARIA role elements; likely a carousel or tab component with broken DOM nesting.
- `aria-allowed-role` [minor] — element has a role value invalid for its tag.
- `heading-order` [moderate] — heading hierarchy skips levels (e.g., h1 → h3).
- `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-unique`, `region` [moderate] — multiple `<main>` elements present; content outside landmark regions. Consistent with Monetate/personalization injecting DOM fragments without landmark awareness.

**View Cart (4 violations, all severity serious/moderate):**
- `document-title` [serious] — `<title>` is empty or absent. The 429 on `/cart/` is almost certainly serving a Kasada challenge page instead of the real cart HTML.
- `html-has-lang` [serious] — same root cause; the challenge shell lacks a `lang` attribute.
- `landmark-one-main` [moderate], `page-has-heading-one` [moderate] — challenge page has no semantic structure.

**Search results (3 violations):**
- `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique`, `region` [moderate] — same Monetate/widget injection pattern as homepage.

## SEO

- **View Cart is serving a bot-challenge page** (`<title>` absent, no `lang`, no `<h1>`). If Googlebot ever hits `/cart/` in a crawl path (e.g., via sitemaps or internal links with session params), it receives a structurally empty document.
- **Duplicate GTM destination scripts**: `G-1234567890` appears to be a placeholder/test GA4 property ID loaded alongside the real `G-XZTV9LQ9DQ`. A second synthetic tracking ID firing in production contaminates GA4 data.
- **WebFontLoader** (`webfontloader.js` from cdnjs) loads 6 Mulish weights asynchronously, adding FOUT risk. Font display strategy not visible in truncated source.

## Console & Network Errors

**429 — Kasada fingerprinting endpoint (5 hits):**
```
/149e9513-01fa-4fb0-aad4-566afd725d1b/2d206a39-8ed7-437e-a3be-862e0f06eea3/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.412
```
This is the Kasada bot-protection SDK performing repeated fingerprinting POSTs and getting rate-limited by its own infrastructure. The SDK appears to be retrying without exponential backoff. This is a Kasada configuration/version issue — `j-1.2.412` may be outdated.

**429 — `/cart/`:**
The cart endpoint itself is rate-limited. This means the View Cart step is serving a Kasada challenge page to what appears to be a legitimate browser session. This is a false-positive bot detection event on the checkout funnel — a direct conversion risk.

**CORS block — Stylitics:**
```
https://web-assets.stylitics.com/style.css?base=hotspots&theme=skechers&version=8a49290ac7
```
Stylitics' CDN is not returning `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://www.skechers.com`. This blocks the "Shop the Look" / outfit widget CSS from loading. Root cause is on the Stylitics CDN configuration — Skechers needs to open a ticket with Stylitics to add their origin to the allowlist, or self-host the stylesheet.

**Duplicate `ytc.js` (Yahoo):**
```html
<script async="" vice-managed-src="https://s.yimg.com/wi/ytc.js"></script>
<script async="" vice-managed-src="https://s.yimg.com/wi/ytc.js"></script>
```
Same script injected twice via GTM or TrustArc. Duplicate pixel fires inflate Yahoo Ads attribution data.

## Recommendations

1. **[Critical] Investigate Kasada false-positive on `/cart/`** — a 429 on the cart page is blocking real users from reaching checkout. Audit Kasada sensitivity thresholds for session-authenticated users and the multi-step navigation pattern.

2. **[Critical] Fix Kasada SDK retry storm** — the `j-1.2.412` SDK version is making 5+ retries without backoff. Check for a newer Kasada SDK version or configure `maxRetries`/backoff in the integration.

3. **[High] Fix Stylitics CORS** — file a ticket with Stylitics to add `https://www.skechers.com` to their CORS allowlist for `web-assets.stylitics.com`. The outfit widget is silently broken.

4. **[High] Fix Homepage ARIA landmark structure** — the `aria-required-parent` critical violation points to a broken carousel/tab component. The duplicate `<main>` likely comes from a Monetate-injected personalization container; ensure injected content is wrapped in a non-main landmark.

5. **[Medium] Remove placeholder GA4 property** — `G-1234567890` is a test/placeholder property ID firing in production via GTM. Remove the tag or point it to a real QA property.

6. **[Medium] Deduplicate `ytc.js`** — remove the duplicate Yahoo pixel tag from GTM.

7. **[Low] Instrument LCP and CLS** — all 8 steps are missing LCP and CLS data. Given the third-party script density, add RUM (e.g., via Quantum Metric, which is already installed) to capture field Web Vitals across the full journey.
