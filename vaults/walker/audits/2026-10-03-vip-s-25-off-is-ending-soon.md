# VIP's 25% off is ending soon…
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count, but this is at least the seventh email in three days, and the giant white "25% OFF*" on purple is the same banner I got on 10/01 in "Happy VIP Days" and "Ooooh you get 25% off...". The subject says it's "ending soon," but the fine print says it runs October 1-4. So the real news is that it ends tomorrow, and the subject buries that. The number is easy to read on my phone, I'll give them that.
- Then I scroll and it's nine women's boots, heels and sandals, plus one sneaker. I'm a 62-year-old man looking for a cushioned slip-on, and I don't see a thing about arch support, walking or hands-free. There's a "Shop Men" link, but it's a small underlined line under the banner. Wide width is there, which is nice, but I'd never find a comfortable shoe from this email.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd forward it only to my wife, and only if she'd missed the last six. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The huge "25%" is readable on my phone without zooming. I know the deal in two seconds.
- "Shop by size" with big numbers and a "Shop Wide Width" button is useful. Wide fit matters to a guy my age.
- The "VIPFUN" code and the barcode for in-store use tell me how to actually get the discount.
- The price tags show the old price crossed out next to the new one, so the savings are plain.
- The "FREE SHIPPING FOR VIPS" and "In-store pickup" icons are clear and easy to read.

## What's weak

- The product grid is almost all women's boots, bootie heels and a strappy sandal. Nothing says "men's" or "comfort" to me.
- "Shop Men" is a small underlined link among four links. It's a tiny tap target for my thumbs.
- The headline offer has an asterisk, and the real terms sit in tiny gray text at the bottom, with a long list of excluded brands. I don't trust a deal that needs that much fine print.
- The "Become a VIP" button is the main call to action. That's a sign-up ask, not a shoe, so it competes with the shopping buttons.
- It's the same banner for the third day running. The "Priority Gifting" holiday block in October feels early and a bit pushy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender and show men's comfort shoes in the grid. Walking shoes, slip-ons and cushioned sneakers would speak to me. Right now I get women's fashion boots.
- 2. Put the real deadline in the subject, like "tonight" or "ends Sunday." "Soon" means nothing after six near-identical emails.
- 3. Cut the send frequency. Pick one VIP Days email and make it count.
- 4. Make the "Shop Men" and "Shop Women" links real buttons with room around them.
- 5. Move the discount terms higher up, in plain words, so I'm not hunting for them.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight — VIP 25% off walking shoes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code VIPFUN works through Oct 4. Slip-ons, sneakers and wide widths included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned walkers from top brands, 25% off for VIPs until midnight.`

# Technical Audit: DSW, "VIP's 25% off is ending soon…"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken link, the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA, which returned HTTP 400. It also has malformed `<meta>` declarations and missing alt text on a linked image. The HTML was truncated and only 12 of 74 links were probed, so several checks below couldn't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned **HTTP 400**: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/NeP5v2mhlKMwiFTVhZtebw~~/AAQRxRA~/eyCqLA1_YobSUQrjUHx7J...`
  - The failure is on the click-tracking redirect, so the final destination is unknown.
  - Possible causes are an expired or malformed token, or the tracker rejecting an automated probe. The data can't distinguish these.
  - If it fails for real recipients, the SMS-acquisition CTA is dead. Verify manually from a live send.
- **Link-wrapping opacity:** All probed links go through `ablink.dsw.com` tracking redirects, so destinations and UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- **Probe coverage:** 62 of 74 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized), so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked image missing alt:** `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/za2x3J3yl31v7bBmBL9F_A~~/AAQRxRA~/RgvzLREJ9zpKMyxz9K2rTYZ2q8lFS3l-...`
  - The QA flag says this is an image, and its src is an `ablink` click-tracking URL.
  - If it is wrapped in a link, the link has no accessible name for screen readers or images-off clients.
- **Tracking pixel missing alt:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?mi_u=...` has no `alt`. It should carry `alt=""`. This is low severity.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` are both missing `http-equiv`.
  - The charset declaration is therefore not valid, and the browser ignores the tag.
  - Charset is likely still correct via the HTTP `Content-Type` header, so this is a hygiene issue.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs make up 27,461 of 31,925 characters (86%), which exceeds the 70% threshold.
  - This is caused by very long tracking URLs.
  - The plain-text part is mostly unreadable and can add a small spam-filter signal.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (e.g. `{{ }}`, `${}`) appear in the truncated HTML. The remainder is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues found in the supplied data. QA always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` headers weren't provided, so they are unverified. The sender `dsw@e.dsw.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with normal ESP setup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues could be confirmed. Tracked redirects hide the final URLs, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The campaign identifier `20261003_P261342_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_D` appears in the pixel URL (`mi_ecmp`), but this audit has no way to check it against the click-through destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the SMS opt-in link.** Manually click the live link and re-issue the tracking token if it is expired. This is the only confirmed functional defect.
2. **Add `alt` text to the linked image.** Describe the destination or action. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
3. **Correct the meta tags** to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
4. **Shorten the plain-text part.** Use a shorter-link or non-wrapped-URL setting for the text version, or trim it to the primary CTAs.
5. **Re-run QA with the full HTML, headers and a wider probe set.** That would cover authentication, `List-Unsubscribe` and UTM continuity.
