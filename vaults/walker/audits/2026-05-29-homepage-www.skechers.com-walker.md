# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is pushing a soccer sweepstakes front-and-center, and that is not my world at all — I am not trying to win a prize package, I am trying to find a pair of Slip-ins that don't hurt my feet by noon. The BOGO deal at the top is the one thing that grabbed me right away, and the nav bar has exactly the categories I want (Slip-ins, Arch Fit), but everything in between those two good things is pointed at somebody twenty-five years younger than me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — top of the page, high contrast blue, impossible to skip. That's the kind of deal that makes me stop scrolling my email and actually come to the site.
- **Navigation tabs: Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit** — three of the four visible tabs are exactly what I shop. One tap and I'm in the right aisle.
- **"Spring into Summer Sale! 25% OFF Apparel"** — secondary banner is clean and readable. Even if I'm not buying a shirt, it signals the store is in sale mode, which puts me in a browsing mood.
- **Clean mobile layout** — large logo, readable type, no tiny text crushing my eyes. The page doesn't fight me.

## What's weak

- **The Skechers Plus soccer prize module** — teal banner, big bold "WIN THE ULTIMATE SOCCER FAN PRIZE PACKAGE." I don't follow soccer. This feels like a promotion for someone else entirely, and it's eating prime real estate between my nav tabs and the hero image.
- **Hero models are 25–35, not 60+** — the man walking the city block and the woman in white pants are both clearly young. There's nothing wrong with them, but they don't make me think "that shoe works for someone like me."
- **No comfort language above the fold** — cushioning, memory foam, arch support, wide width — none of those words appear anywhere I can see. That's the whole reason I'm on this site.
- **Apparel discount gets more copy than shoes** — the secondary banner pushes 25% off apparel. I came for shoes. Feels like the priorities are reversed.
- **No free-shipping threshold or returns mention** — I need to know shipping is free before I start adding things to my cart, and I can't see that promise anywhere yet.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the soccer sweepstakes module with a Slip-ins comfort story** — a lifestyle shot of someone my age walking a park path, copy like "No Hands. No Hassle. All Day Comfort." That converts a guy like me immediately.
- **Add a Men's / Women's split CTA button directly under the hero** — something like "Shop Men's Slip-ins" in a clear button. Right now I have to hunt the nav; a single tappable button is faster on a phone.
- **Surface a free-shipping callout in the sticky banner area** — even a small line "Free shipping on orders $75+" would lower my hesitation before I've looked at a single product.
- **Swap the apparel discount banner for a shoe-specific deal** — "25% OFF Select Arch Fit Styles" lands harder for me than a shirt discount I didn't come for.
