# Ends tomorrow: BOGO 50% off shoes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the sixth BOGO email in about four days, after "Don't miss BOGO 50% off shoes" earlier today and "BOGO 50% off shoes ends SOON" before that. The green banner is the same one I've seen all week. I get it: buy one pair, get 50% off a second. But by now it's wallpaper. The "Ends tomorrow" subject at least tells me the deadline, and the fine print says October 15–18.
- I scrolled down looking for something made for me. "Brand New…and so you" is a grid of fifteen shoes, and almost none of them are for a 62-year-old man. I see ankle boots, a stiletto, a wedge sandal, a mary jane and fuzzy slippers. The women's, men's and kids' links sit under the banner, so I could tap "Shop Men." Nothing in the picture tells me where to go for cushioned, slip-on comfort.
- I wouldn't click today. I've seen the deal five times already, and if I wanted it I'd have gone to the store. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and leave it.

## What's working

- The green banner says "BOGO 50% OFF*" in huge type, and I understood it in two seconds.
- "Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids" sit right under the button, so I can pick my own aisle.
- The "Shop Now" button is big and white on green. That's easy to tap on a phone.
- The barcode for in-store use is handy. I don't have to hunt for a code.
- The sizes are in large type, and there are "Wide Width" buttons. A man with wide feet notices that.

## What's weak

- Every shoe in the grid is women's. I don't see one man's shoe, one slip-on, or one walking shoe.
- It's the sixth BOGO send in four days, with the same banner and the same offer. The deadline feels less real when it's been "ending soon" all week.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text. It says the discount goes on the lower-priced item, and that Skechers is included.
- The grid has no prices, no names and no ratings. I'm looking at pictures, not shopping.
- The "Shop by size" row is a list of numbers that don't look tappable. The half sizes are crammed together on a small screen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me a men's version. I've shopped the men's side before, so show me cushioned slip-ons, walking shoes and comfort brands like Skechers, Hoka and Brooks. Don't show me a stiletto.
- 2. Stop sending the same email every day. One "ends tomorrow" send is plenty after a week of BOGO.
- 3. Put prices and a "Comfort" or "Arch support" tag under each shoe, so I can do the math on two pairs.
- 4. Say up top that the lower-priced pair gets the 50% off, in plain words, instead of burying it at the bottom.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned slip-ons and walkers included. Through Oct 18.`
