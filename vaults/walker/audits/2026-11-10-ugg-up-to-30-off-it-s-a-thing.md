# UGG. Up to 30% off. It's a thing.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this one's a nice change from last week's endless TINSEL 20% emails. Yesterday I got "Up to 30% off UGG?! (Spoiler: YES!)", and this is basically the same offer again. The "30% OFF*" on UGG is big and clear, and the slipper and boot photos near the bottom are the closest thing in here to what I'd actually wear.
- The email then turns into a Bombas gift guide, with socks, "For Her" and "For Him" boxes, and a lot of pink. I came for shoes, not stocking stuffers. The UGG boot in the hero is a fashion boot, not my kind of shoe. The slippers lower down are more my speed, but they're buried, and they have no price and no button of their own.
- Would I click? Probably "Shop UGG" if I were slipper shopping, but I wouldn't hurry. Forward it? No. Mark it read and move on. I might check the Bombas socks, since cushioned socks are something I'd buy.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF*" with the UGG logo is huge and easy to read on my phone.
- There's one clear black "SHOP UGG" button right under the offer.
- The slipper images in the "For Him" section feel closer to my comfort-first style.
- Bombas is a brand I trust for cushioned socks, and "One Purchased = One Donated" feels decent.
- The size row (5 to 11) and the "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons are a thoughtful touch for real fit needs.

## What's weak

- Half the email is Bombas and gift-box themes, so the headline offer gets diluted fast.
- The hero shows a women's fashion boot. Nothing says men's comfort, arch support or slip-on walking shoes.
- The size row stops at 11. I wear bigger than that, and it makes me feel like the email isn't for me.
- The fine print is tiny and dense, and the asterisk on "30% OFF*" makes me wonder what's excluded. "Online only. Details below." is a bit of a tease.
- It's still the same UGG sale as yesterday's email, with no new reason to act today. There's no deadline and no member perk. The VIP banner is at the very bottom.
- I'd like to see a men's slipper in the hero with a price on it. As it is, the "For Him" photo has no link or label I can tell is tappable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a men's slipper or comfort walking shoe, with a real price, next to the 30% off. Make the "For Him" section a clearly tappable shop block.
- 2. Pick one story. Either make this an UGG sale or a gift guide, not both.
- 3. Add a real end date for the sale.
- 4. Move the VIP points message up, and extend the size run to 12 to 14.
- 5. Make the fine print readable, or explain the exclusions in one plain line near the offer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's slippers and UGG boots, plus Bombas cushioned socks. Early Black Friday pricing.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide widths in stock. Shop slippers before the Black Friday rush.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent XHTML 1.0 Transitional message with click-tracking through `ablink.dsw.com`. The QA run found one failed link (the SMS opt-in CTA returns 400), two accessibility gaps and two malformed `<meta>` tags. The HTML was truncated and only 12 of 63 links were probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed, HTTP 400):** the link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/OYCak94Hco4bLIxENT6krQ~~/AAQRxRA~/K8dHdiQ1roA0…` (~700-character tracking path). The QA output shows an empty final URL after the 400, so the destination can't be inspected.
  - A 400 from a Braze `ablink` wrapper can come from a malformed or expired token, or from the probe's request fingerprint. The probe can't tell these apart.
  - Treat it as a real defect until it is re-tested from a normal browser or mobile client.
- **Overlong tracking URLs:** the plain-text part is 20,279 of 24,529 characters URLs (83%), well over the 70% warning threshold. The long `ablink` paths inflate message size and can hurt text-part spam scoring.
- **Coverage gap:** 51 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized), so their health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (linked image):** `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/QTyAQpj7VXi1Bz_XMc-JZw~~/AAQRxRA~/bL1y1VXIOi9K…` has no `alt`. If this image is the only content of its link, the link has no accessible name.
- **Missing alt text (tracking pixel):** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=…` has no `alt`. It should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` both lack `http-equiv`. Neither declares anything, so the charset and IE-mode hints are ignored. Fix by adding `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
- The rest of the `<head>` is fine: viewport, `color-scheme`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and the Outlook `mso-` resets are present, and the `@font-face` rules point at HTTPS Braze-hosted fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `${}`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The pixel's `mi_u=225090073` is a resolved per-recipient ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a verification gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the probe always includes an unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported for it. The postal address and the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers couldn't be checked because the source was truncated and no headers were provided.
- **Sender:** `dsw@e.dsw.com` is a branded subdomain that matches the tracking domains (`dsw.com`), so alignment looks plausible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`, so final destination URLs and any `utm_*` parameters can't be verified from the source or QA data.
- **Campaign ID:** attribution appears to rely on the pixel's `mi_ecmp=20261110_P261678_V1_W-_Offer_Brand`, not on UTM parameters.
- **SMS opt-in CTA:** the 400 means continuity is broken for this link. A subscriber tapping it doesn't reach a working opt-in page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link from a real client. If it still fails, fix the Braze link template or SMS landing URL and re-send or hotfix. This is the only high-priority item, since it is a broken conversion path.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…bL1y1VXIOi9K…` image and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
3. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two `<meta>` tags.
4. Shorten the plain-text part, for example by dropping tracking from the text version or using shorter link IDs, to bring URL density well under 70%.
5. Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) so SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the postal address and the UTM parameters can be verified.
