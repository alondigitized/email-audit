## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: New Balance, "Get top styles on sale now"

## 1. Technical Summary
The send is structurally sound. The automated QA shows 0 hard failures, and no broken links were reported among the 12 probed. The issues are template hygiene (placeholder and invalid CSS left in the `<head>`), three images without `alt`, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The HTML was truncated and the SPF/DKIM headers were unavailable, so several checks below could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 24 were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized by design). No dead or redirecting-to-error links were reported. The 24 skipped links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,140 of 13,893 characters (about 87%) are URLs. This is the QA warning. It points to long ESP tracking URLs (`link.email.newbalance.com/...`) in the text alternative, which spam filters can score negatively.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12748212_314832_24070939.gif` is a standard `/mo/` open pixel. It has no `alt`, and there is no evidence of a problem beyond that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (confirmed by QA):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking pixel `egR1f966kc_..._24070939.gif`

  Informational images should get descriptive `alt` text. The pixel should get `alt=""`.
- **Template placeholder left in CSS:** `.bgwhite {... background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}`. This is an unresolved placeholder over insecure `http://`. If the class is applied, it triggers a 404 and a mixed-content request.
- **Wrong page title:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is a leftover template name. It shows in some clients' tab and preview text.
- **Invalid meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv` attribute, so it does nothing.
- **Fonts will not load:** every `@font-face` block (`proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot`, and others) has no `src` descriptor. Clients fall back to `Arial`/`Times New Roman`, and these rules are dead code. `.fst10` and `.fst12` fall back to `Times New Roman`.
- **Conflicting class definitions:** `.bg1` is declared as `#ffffff` and later as `#58546d`. The later declaration wins, which looks unintentional.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute and self-closed tags (`<meta ... />`). This is benign in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Dark-mode assets:** `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` indicates dark-mode handling is in place. `color-scheme` metas are present and correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FirstName]`) were visible in the provided source. This is limited to the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a measurement gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed and raised no failure, but I can't confirm its presence, the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, or the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** can't be verified. Links are wrapped in the ESP redirector (`link.email.newbalance.com`), and the final destination URLs and their parameters weren't in the provided data.
- **Asset path:** images are served from `/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`, a welcome-series folder reused in a sale campaign. If that folder is pruned or re-versioned, the sale email's images will break.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to `ShippingIcons_06.png` and `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or replace the `.bgwhite` rule (`http://image.XXXXX.png`), and change the `<title>` to the actual campaign name.
3. Either add `src` to the `@font-face` rules or delete them. Fix `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">` or remove it. Resolve the duplicate `.bg1` rule.
4. Shorten the plain-text part. Use fewer links, or shorter tracking domains/paths, to bring the URL ratio well under 70%.
5. Move the sale images out of the `myNB_Welcome` folder into a campaign-specific path.
6. Re-run QA with full raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) and the full, untruncated HTML. This would let the compliance and UTM checks be completed. Probing the 24 skipped links would also help.
