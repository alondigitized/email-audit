# Storage that clears the clutter
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-five. That's the Pottery Barn count for July, and this is the third one today — I already opened the design services email this morning and the new arrivals send before lunch. I want to be upfront about that because it's the lens I'm carrying into this one, whether I like it or not.
- But here's the honest thing: "storage" is basically the word I've said most to contractors this month. The second home has a utility hallway that needs to work hard. The primary needs a mudroom that can actually absorb two people's worth of outdoor gear. So when Pottery Barn lands with a storage-focused email and a 70% off Summer Sale banner as the literal first thing I see below the logo — I'm more receptive than I was for the gift card sweepstakes or the new arrivals send. The hero is clean, the category tile navigation gives me four immediate paths without making me type anything, and "SHOP MODULAR FURNITURE" is exactly the right CTA for someone who hasn't finalized room dimensions yet.
- What kills the momentum is a "Recommended Just For You" section mid-scroll that appears to be surfacing beds. I'm in a storage mindset. Beds are not it. That's the algorithm misfiring in a way I notice and that erodes trust in PB's personalization claim fast. I'd click the modular furniture CTA, skip the beds entirely, and not forward this — it's personal-use territory, not something I'd send to the friend I'm co-renovating the second place with.

## What's working

- **70% off Summer Sale lives in the banner**, not buried in footer fine print — that's the only place I'll believe it's real, and PB put it there.
- **"SHOP MODULAR FURNITURE" is the right verb + noun** — "modular" signals flexibility, which matters when I'm still waiting on a contractor's final floor plan measurement.
- **Category tile navigation (baskets, bookcases, buffets & cabinets)** gives me multiple entry points without requiring search box intent — that's how I actually browse storage.
- **25X Rewards visible in footer** — I buy on whichever card runs the highest rewards, but PB stacking its own points on top of that is a real tiebreaker versus Wayfair.
- **Affirm callout is practical for furniture at this price tier** — Article did this clearly in their dining room email last week; PB is at least matching that expectation.

## What's weak

- **"Recommended Just For You" is surfacing beds in a storage email** — the algorithm misfired visibly, which immediately undermines the personalization premise.
- **No dimensions anywhere in the modular section** — I'm actively measuring a hallway. Crate & Barrel has started adding width callouts to their storage grid; PB skips this entirely and loses the conversion I was closest to making.
- **Third Pottery Barn email today** — even a well-executed send takes a hit when I've already been to this well twice before lunch.
- **"Discover New Arrivals" section at the bottom is a different email entirely** — a lifestyle living room shot with no connection to organization or the Summer Sale reads as filler bolted onto the real email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Strip the beds from "Recommended Just For You" or rename the section to "Storage Picks for Your Project"** and actually populate it with storage. This is the single change that protects the email's thematic integrity mid-scroll.
- 2. **Add one dimension callout to the modular furniture hero** — even a line like "Available in 30", 36" and 48" widths." That detail converts renovation-mode browsers. CB2's sale emails started doing this; PB should follow.
- 3. **One email per day, full stop** — policy not design, but the highest-leverage fix available. The cadence is eroding open rates from subscribers who are actually in-market.
- 4. **Put a deadline on the Summer Sale** — "Ends Sunday" or "3 days left" turns "I'll look later" into a click. CB2 shows their end date; PB doesn't, and it costs them urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your storage shortlist — 70% off starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Modular storage on sale: find the one that fits your space`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bookcases, buffets, and baskets — all Summer Sale pricing, ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 70% off the storage pieces that actually earn their square footage.`
