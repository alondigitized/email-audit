- The sofa is the first tile, so the subject isn't a total bait-and-switch.
- The purple "80% OFF / ENDS TONIGHT" banner is readable at a glance, and the Sealy badge gives it a brand anchor.
- The big category tiles (80% area rugs, 60% bedroom furniture, 70% bedding, 70% curtains) are tappable and match my renovation categories.
- Was/Now pricing with star ratings under each product gives me quick social proof and the discount math.
- The "Your Home, These Picks" section at the bottom (table lamps, comforters, area rugs) is closer to how I actually shop, by room and category.

## What's weak

- "SOFAS" in the subject is one product tile. There's no sofa section and no sofa-specific offer.
- "Picked just for you" is hollow. An ice maker, a sauna and deck tiles aren't picked for anyone.
- The product grid has no coherent look. Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel at least keep a color palette. Here nothing coordinates, and I can't tell what style Wayfair thinks I like.
- The credit card offer ($40 off after opening a Wayfair card) has dense fine print, and a hard-sell financing pitch in the middle of a sale email feels off. I buy on my highest-rewards card anyway.
- The email is very long. There's no sofa-specific shipping or delivery info, which is what I'd need before buying furniture, and the Wayfair Rewards block is just an icon at the bottom.
- I got four near-identical Black Friday blasts in two days.

- 1. Make the sofa subject real. Lead with a sofa hero and a short row of sofa tiles (fabric, leather, sectional), each with a delivery window. If the segment isn't sofa-focused, change the subject instead.
- 2. Cut the grid from about 20 tiles to 6-8 that share a theme. CB2 and Article put one clear hero and one CTA up top, and that's easier to scan.
- 3. Replace "ends tonight" with a specific, credible time, and don't repeat it across four sends.
- 4. Move Wayfair Rewards / member pricing up near the offer and shrink the credit card block.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Wayfair "SOFAS 🚩 BLACK FRIDAY ends TONIGHT"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules. QA gave it a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The substantive problems are widespread missing `alt` text, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and unconfirmed unsubscribe and authentication headers. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and body markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 material links were probed. Every flagged one returned 429, which is rate limiting by Wayfair's CDN and not a dead link. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, the `wayfair.com/?_emr=...` home link, and the product links (e.g. `...Daily-Nugget-Ice-Maker-Countertop-L1192-K~id-BQ12005656.html`).
- **Incomplete coverage.** 73 links were skipped by design. 4 more were unprobed after the 70s time budget:
  - `.../wayfair-product-L6087-K~id-BI84075700.html`
  - `.../wayfair-product-L6846-K~id-BL96081372.html`
  - the `12-In.W-X-12-In.-L-Outdoor-Striped-Pattern-Square-Plasti...` product link
  - `.../daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html?eid=635985`
  
  These are neither pass nor fail.
- **App deep-link fallback uses HTTP.** The OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Desktop and non-app users get a non-HTTPS landing URL that depends on a server-side redirect.
- **Possible campaign-label mismatch.** The same OneLink URL carries `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=2`. A triggered-campaign label on a batch send may skew attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **Tracking pixel load.** There are 21 pixels in total: 1 open pixel (`https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`) and 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019` (see Rendering).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images.** Affected images:
  - The `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` rating images appear about 14 times. Their links have no accessible name ("[image: stars_10.png]").
  - The `1113-US-BF-Email-ETn.gif` hero banner is wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales`, so the link has no accessible name.
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-BF-Doorbusters`, `...Skinny-FlashDeals.jpg`, and `...Skinny-WFCC.jpg`.
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`.
  
  With images off, or in a screen reader, promotional banners and the rating information are not conveyed. The 4.5-star rating (`stars_10`/`stars_11`) is conveyed only by the image.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** All 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=1241260xx&sz=2x1&m=false&p=30f21350-...` pixels are flagged as insecure. Gmail's image proxy upgrades some of them, but clients that block mixed content may drop them. The pixels also lack `alt`.
- **Ad-impression pixel count.** 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` calls, with a 2x1 `sz` parameter, add avoidable requests and weight.
- The CSS uses `@font-face` for "Sofia" (a `.woff` on `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`), with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` as an Outlook fallback. No issue there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The `_eml`, `_emr`, `p=` and `emlid` values are populated per recipient. The HTML was truncated, so the body and footer could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (WARN).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN). Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe from large senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be verified.
- All three warnings carry the QA note that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so they are unconfirmed and should not be treated as sender defects until raw headers are checked.
- The From domain `members.wayfair.com` is a subdomain, so authentication should be verified against that domain specifically.
- The body-level unsubscribe link and postal address are in the truncated section. The QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it was reported. Their presence is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses proprietary parameters on every link: `_emr`, `wfcs=cs7`, `source=batch`, `batchid=2`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_145483`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16853`, `skurectxid`. These are consistent across links, so tracking is coherent. It will not show up in standard UTM-based analytics.
- **Subject/landing mismatch (unconfirmed).** The subject leads with "SOFAS". The probed links go to `/daily-sales` and to unrelated products (a nugget ice maker, an infrared sauna, outdoor planters, area-rug clearance). No sofa-specific URL appears among the probed or unprobed links, but the truncated HTML may contain one.
- Product links include `sku=` and `mplid=` and resolve to PDP URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text** to all banner and rating images. Use `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` on decorative images and tracking pixels. Give the hero GIF linked to `/daily-sales` a descriptive `alt`.
2. **Serve all pixels over HTTPS.** Switch `li.wayfair.com/imp` to `https://`, and consolidate the 20 impression pixels into fewer calls if possible.
3. **Change the OneLink `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.** Check whether `c=Triggered` is correct for a `source=batch` send.
4. **Inspect raw headers** (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on `members.wayfair.com`.
5. **Re-probe with throttling or retries** to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed links, including the `area-rug-clearance~e408846` link.
6. **Verify the footer** for a working unsubscribe link and a physical postal address, using the full, untruncated HTML.
7. **Check that at least one CTA goes to a sofa category page** to match the subject line. Only the probed links were reviewed.
