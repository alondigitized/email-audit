# BACK IN STOCK. ON SALE.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Another day, another CB2. At this point I've genuinely lost count — this is easily the sixth or seventh from them this week, and I said the same thing yesterday about the "You want them, they're finally on sale" send. What's different here is the countdown timer up top — "SALE ENDS IN 04:07:20:30" — which is the first real urgency device they've used all week instead of just yelling "MORE" or "EVEN MORE" at me. The "BACK IN STOCK" hook is smart too, since half my problem with these emails is I've already scrolled past the same sold-out sofa four times.
- That said, this is still the exact same Labor Day hero banner, the exact same category grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor, Entertaining), and the exact same Double Rewards module I've seen from both CB2 and Crate & Barrel proper this week. Nothing here is actually "back in stock" specific — it's the generic sale template with a countdown bolted on top. Compare that to Article's "Up to 40% off ends Monday" send, which at least commits to one deadline banner and gets out of the way. This one buries the countdown, then makes me scroll through six more category tiles before I even get near the rewards pitch.
- I'd skim it because the countdown catches my eye for half a second, but I wouldn't click through, and I definitely wouldn't forward it — there's no specific "back in stock" item shown, just abstract lifestyle shots I've now memorized from prior CB2 sends.

## What's working

- The countdown timer is a genuinely new device for CB2 this week — first real deadline pressure I've seen from them, not just "up to 50% off" copy.
- "BACK IN STOCK" as the subject hook is a good instinct — it targets the exact frustration of chasing sold-out furniture, which is real for me right now.
- Extended holiday hours note at the top is a small but relevant touch since I might actually visit a showroom for the rugs I need.
- Clean category grid makes it easy to jump straight to Rugs or Lighting if I wanted to, without forcing me through unrelated categories like kids' furniture.

## What's weak

- Zero actual "back in stock" products shown — the hero and every category tile below are generic lifestyle shots, not the specific item that came back in stock. Total bait-and-switch on the subject line.
- This is the same "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" hero I've now seen from CB2 multiple times this week — no new creative, just a countdown slapped on.
- The Double Rewards block ("GET 10% 20% BACK") has a visible copy error — it literally shows both "10%" and "20%" stacked with no resolution, which reads broken, not enticing.
- Six full category tiles before any actual "back in stock" proof — if the hook is scarcity/restock, show me the restocked item in the hero, not sofa lifestyle shots I've already seen from Article and Pottery Barn this month.
- Cadence fatigue is real — after CB2's "EVEN MORE" (9/3), "You want them, they're finally on sale" (9/4), and the plain-text Nicole email (9/4), this is diminishing returns territory.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "back in stock" promise — show the actual restocked SKU (with a "sold out for 3 weeks, now back" badge) in the hero instead of a generic room shot. That's the whole reason I'd open this.
- 2. Fix the Double Rewards copy bug — "10% 20% BACK" needs to just say one number. Right now it looks like a template merge error, which kills trust instantly.
- 3. Move the countdown timer into the hero image itself so it's the first thing I see, not a thin strip above the nav — Article does this better with their bold "ENDS MONDAY" banner treatment.
- 4. Cut at least two of the six category tiles — I don't need Entertaining and Decor & Mirrors both getting full-width real estate when the hook is restocked furniture.
- 5. Give me one specific rug or lighting SKU with a price, since I'm actively buying both right now — a static category label isn't enough after seeing six CB2 emails this week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Restocked: the sofa/rug everyone wanted is back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus extended Labor Day showroom hours this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Timer's running — see what just came back in stock`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard MI/Cheetah-Digital-built email with clean DOCTYPE/meta setup, but compliance headers are unverifiable at the relay and the email embeds a legacy Dotomi ad-sync pixel chain served over unencrypted HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 64 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (standard for ESP click-tracking domains; not itself a defect, but means link-destination validity is unconfirmed).
- Embedded third-party ad-sync pixel chain via `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com` (`pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `cookieredir`), all served over `http://` rather than `https://`. Many modern mail clients (Outlook, Gmail image proxy) will block or fail to load mixed-content `http://` resources, meaning these pixels likely fail silently.
- First-party tracking pixel `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/...` are HTTPS — no issue there.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 content images (`7762f0ff...` through `00291240...`) are missing `alt` text — screen readers and images-blocked clients will show nothing in their place.
- The Dotomi tracking images also lack `alt` attributes, though as 1x1 tracking pixels this is functionally immaterial.
- HTML uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments and extensive inline `@media` targeting — standard defensive email-client coding, no defects found in the truncated markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders present in the visible/truncated HTML source. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay rather than genuinely absent).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe via mail client cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Visible footer unsubscribe link/physical address not present in the truncated HTML sample, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance cannot be assessed from available evidence.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Truncated source does not expose the primary CTA destination URLs (masked behind the 64 skipped tracking redirects), so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing page alignment cannot be verified from available evidence.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images for accessibility and images-off rendering.
- Migrate the Dotomi ad-sync calls (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com/*.php`) to HTTPS, or drop them if legacy/unused — HTTP pixels are likely already being blocked by major clients, wasting the sync opportunity.
- Verify with the sending ESP/relay that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (not just dropped by capture) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly at the sending domain (`mail.cb2.com`) since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
