# Double Rewards. Up to 50% off. The Fall Sales Event is on!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Crate & Barrel send in days where I'm the target. It's the main Crate & Barrel sender, not the Kids one that got a 5 from me on 11/08 with "I'm not buying a nursery." The hero is "The Fall Sales Event up to 50% off" over a red-and-black fall image. Beneath it is a dinnerware and drinkware block, a "guest ready" furniture block, and a row of in-stock sofas with a dollar amount of Reward Dollars under each. The "last Double Rewards this year" banner is on top again, with Nov. 17 as the deadline.
- The in-stock sofa row is the part I care about. I'm furnishing two houses and need to know what ships fast. The rewards math under each sofa is real, which is more than I usually get. But this is the twentieth family send in about four days, and I've seen the "last Double Rewards" line in at least three of them. The urgency has worn thin.
- Would I click? Yes, the sofa row. Forward it? No, but I'd save it to my inspiration folder. Neither the hosting guide nor the Gift Shop is for me.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "up to 50% off" in big type with a single "Shop Now" button, and the eye lands there first.
- The sofas show Reward Dollars per piece (Earn $209, $184, $160). That's the card-rewards math I want.
- "Get this in-stock furniture by next week" answers my shipping question for furniture.
- The "we love these for you" product grid with prices (stemware, a bowl) is easy to scan for lighting-and-dining people like me.
- The Nov. 17 deadline is stated more than once, so the urgency feels credible.

## What's weak

- The "last Double Rewards this year" banner is repeated three or four times down the email. It reads like padding, and I've now seen it across the whole week's sends.
- The Thanksgiving hosting guide has a dark photo with the headline text hard to read against it. It looks like a legibility bug.
- The Gift Shop block is a big maroon rectangle with a script logo. It uses a lot of space and sells me nothing.
- The fine print is tiny and dense in several places, and the Reward Dollars terms are hard to find. It undercuts the "honest offer" feel.
- There's nothing on lighting, my other big category. I get dinnerware, sofas and stemware, but no lamps or fixtures.
- The app and TheDesk (design service) promos at the bottom come after a very long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the repeated rewards banners to one and move the sofa row up under the hero. That's the strongest content and it sits too low.
- 2. Add a lighting or renovation module (pendants, sconces, swatches) for people with active projects, instead of the Gift Shop block.
- 3. Put the Reward Dollars terms in one short readable line next to the offer.
- 4. Fix the hosting guide's text contrast.
- 5. Vary the creative between sends this week. Wayfair at least swaps its hero between sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is well-formed for client rendering (`lang="en"`, MSO conditionals, mobile media queries). However, about 31 images are flagged for missing alt text, the viewport blocks zoom, and the visible source has template leftovers. The HTML is truncated and the 70 tracking links were not probed, so link, footer and UTM checks are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** The QA run skipped all 70 tracking/click-redirect links ("skipped HTTP probe"), so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unconfirmed.
- **Five third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp (rlcdn) pixels. Each is a separate request carrying a 64-hex `s=` value that looks recipient-derived (it shares a prefix with the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` values on the first-party pixels). This adds third-party calls and a privacy disclosure risk, and clients that proxy images may block or flag them.
- **Open pixels.** Two first-party tracking pixels also appear: `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/9c70ae59-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are normal, but they are flagged as alt-less images.
- **Encoded Scene7 URL.** The image `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/09%5FCore/…ThanksgivingGuide…` encodes `_` and `-` as `%5F` and `%2D`. It is valid but fragile, and the filename also suggests a September asset reused in a November send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (31 images, per QA):**
  - **Content images, about 24:** `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, the Scene7 Thanksgiving image, and seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` recommendation images. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, there is no text equivalent.
  - **Tracking pixels, about 7:** the `rlcdn` ones, `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel. These should carry an explicit `alt=""` instead.
  - The QA output doesn't say whether `alt` is absent or empty, so confirm which in the source.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`, `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{…none}` override the `100%` set elsewhere and prevent user text scaling on iOS.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses a raw `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Mixed and duplicated head markup:**
  - The XHTML doctype is combined with HTML5 `<meta charset="utf-8">` (unclosed) plus the `http-equiv` charset.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. The later rule wins at all widths of 640px and up, so the 600px rule is dead.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated per-recipient identifiers, not leaks. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. That is unverified, so treat it as "unconfirmed", not a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing,** also attributed to the relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the image and tracking subdomains are first-party, which is consistent with a properly configured ESP.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 70 click links were not resolved, and no UTM parameters or landing URLs appear in the available source or QA output. No issue is asserted.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw message headers (outside the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results before treating the compliance warnings as real.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 24 content images, especially the `mi.…/p/rp/*.png` product recommendations. Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations.
4. Change `<title>` to `Crate &amp; Barrel` and clean up the duplicate meta tags.
5. Review whether five LiveRamp pixels are needed, and confirm the `s=` value is covered by the privacy policy. Consolidate to one pixel if possible.
6. Resolve a sample of the click links, since the probe skipped them all, to confirm UTM consistency, landing-page match and no redirect loops.
7. Remove or fix the "Thanksgiving Guide" September-dated asset if it is stale.
