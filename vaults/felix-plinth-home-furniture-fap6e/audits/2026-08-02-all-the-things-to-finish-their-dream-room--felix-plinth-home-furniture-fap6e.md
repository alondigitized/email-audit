# All the things to finish their dream room
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — third time in one day now, and I already flagged the cadence problem on the "Free shipping on 100s of items" send a few hours ago. This one's "back to school, save up to 20% off finishing touches for their dream room," and the hero image is a nursery/kid dresser vignette. I don't have kids. My renovation is a primary and a second home, both very much adult spaces — swatches, sconces, dining sets — and no amount of "modern lighting looks" in a toddler palette is going to get me to stop scrolling.
- Structurally it's actually not a bad email — clear hero, clean section breaks (decor, rugs, lighting, bedding, baby bedding), a sweepstakes for $5,000, and a credit card pitch at the bottom. If I were actually shopping for a kid's room this is competent, low-friction, easy to scan. But I'm not, and that's the whole problem: this is the third near-identical Crate & Kids send today and none of them have adjusted after two ignores.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — this goes straight to the Pinterest-adjacent folder unopened-equivalent, or just gets left. If anything I'd forward it to my sister who actually has toddlers, not keep it for myself.

## What's working

- Clean, repeatable section rhythm — decor, rugs, lighting, bedding — each with its own hero shot and CTA, easy to skim on my phone without zooming.
- The dresser hero shot has real styling — plant, framed art, textured rug — it reads like a room, not just a product on white.
- "up to 50% off summer sale" further down is a real number, not vague "select styles" language.
- The $5,000 sweepstakes banner is at least a scroll-stopper if you're actually a Crate & Kids shopper.

## What's weak

- Wrong persona entirely — cribs, baby bedding, kids' rugs, none of it applies to a childless 36-year-old renovating two adult homes.
- This is the third Crate & Kids email in my inbox today. I flagged the "Free shipping on 100s of items" duplicate-feeling cadence a few hours ago and this is more of the same, just repackaged with different modules.
- Subject says "20% off" but the email itself buries that number under a wall of category modules before you hit the actual "up to 50% off summer sale" banner — inconsistent headline math between subject and body.
- No color-palette or swatch-and-sample content that would actually pull a home-renovation shopper in — this is 100% kid-room styling, zero crossover into adult furniture the way Crate & Barrel proper sometimes throws in.
- CB2 at least ran a designer-spotlight issue ("David Girelli, exclusively for CB2") to break up its promo fatigue this week — Crate & Kids just keeps stacking category blocks with no variation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix segmentation first — if I've never clicked anything Crate & Kids in three sends, suppress me from this list or route me back to core Crate & Barrel. This is the actual lift, not copy tweaks.
- 2. Reconcile the subject's "20% off" with the body's "50% off summer sale" — pick one headline number and lead with it consistently.
- 3. Cut the module count — five stacked categories (decor, rugs, lighting, bedding, baby bedding) is a lot of scroll for one send; Wayfair's 48-hour rug clearance email hit harder with one big offer up top instead of five smaller ones.
- 4. Move the sweepstakes higher if it's meant to be a hook — right now it's buried past four other sections.
