# Happening now! Early Black Friday + Furniture & more—minus the wait
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. By my count it's the thirteenth or fourteenth send since 11/16, and the same maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner opens it, the one I've now seen on 11/18, 11/19, 11/20 and twice on 11/21. This one has a new angle, though. "Delivered Fast / In 1-4 weeks" is the first time I've seen a delivery-time claim as a headline instead of a bury-it-in-the-footer line. The 11/21 "delivered in 1-4 weeks" send teased that and didn't pay it off. This one pays a little more, with a "Quick-ship seating" block and a "Shop all in-stock" button.
- The body is a clean room-by-room scroll: pillows and decor, lighting, bedroom, rugs, seating, dining, office. It's pretty and it's my catalog, so I'd probably click into the quick-ship seating or dining. But it's one very long scroll with no prices and no named products in the first half. "Up to 70% off" is the only number I get, and I can't tell what's actually 70% off versus what's just "in stock." "Recommended just for you" then shows stockings and a curtain rod, which doesn't look like it was picked for someone mid-renovation on two houses.
- I'd click once, into dining or seating, then mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. I'd Pinterest-folder the bedroom hero and move on.

## What's working

- The "Delivered in 1-4 weeks" band speaks to furniture lead time, the thing I care about when I'm furnishing two houses on a timeline.
- The "Quick-ship seating" and "Shop all in-stock" blocks give me a real path to things I can get fast.
- The room-by-room layout is easy to scan, and the photography is good enough to save to my inspiration folder.
- The maroon hero with a white SHOP NOW button is unambiguous, and the logo is instantly recognizable.
- The Pottery Barn Rewards card block (10% back) is visible, which matters because I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The maroon banner is the same creative I've seen all week, so my eye skips it before I read it.
- "Up to 70% off" has no product or price attached, so I don't know whether furniture is even included.
- The "Recommended just for you" block is stockings, a throw and a curtain rod. That's generic seasonal stuff, not personalized to a renovator.
- It's very long. The rewards card, Affirm, app download and sweepstakes blocks all stack up before the footer, and the legal text is microscopic.
- Several CTAs compete: Shop Now, Shop All In-Stock, Christmas Shop, Book a Free Appointment, Learn More. Nothing says what I should do first.
- Zero swatch or sample mention and no color-palette cohesion cues, which I'd want for a renovation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the delivery promise the hero with a real, specific claim ("Sofas and dining tables in your home by Dec 15"), and drop the banner I've seen six times. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put actual prices or named products in each room block, such as "Extendable dining table, was $X, now $Y," so the 70% claim becomes concrete.
- 3. Cut the lower stack. Keep one secondary CTA, probably the Design Crew appointment, since that's the showroom/design tie-in a renovator would use.
- 4. Use browse history for the "Recommended" block instead of defaulting to stockings.
- 5. Add a swatch/fabric-sample callout next to the quick-ship seating.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas & dining tables that ship in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off, and in your home by Christmas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quick-ship seating, dining and bedroom, in stock and priced for Early Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Filter by what's in stock now, plus 10% back with your Pottery Barn card.`
- Compared with the others in my inbox: CB2's "SO MUCH SALE TO SHOP" at least has a moody, differentiated hero. Crate & Barrel's "These 10 holiday favorites always sell out early" gives me ten named products, where this gives me categories. Wayfair's wine-refrigerator email shouts a category and a number at least, though I'm sick of Wayfair too.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a legacy XHTML 1.0 table layout with SFMC-style click wrappers. The main technical problems are about 50 images without `alt` text, three cleartext `http://` image sources (one carries the recipient's email address), and compliance headers that the QA run could not confirm. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click wrapping is opaque.** 58 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon with `alt="1"` and no visible dimensions in the QA output.
- **Stray PHP code in the CSS.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...`. This is invalid CSS from a templating leak. It comes after the last rule, so it is probably harmless, but it is sloppy.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sibling brand's CDN, which adds an unnecessary dependency.
- **Stale asset paths.** `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and `/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` look like reused legacy modules.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 50 images have none, per the QA output.
  - `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg`
  - The `F26_InStock_B8_InStockLivingFurnitureHero/slices_01–12.jpg` hero slices
  - The six product images, e.g. `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`
  - Bottom banners
  - The `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` slices
  - Several tracking pixels
  - Content images are missing alt text, so image-blocked and screen-reader users get no usable content. The pixels (`clickcp`, `turn.com`, `svopn`) should carry `alt=""`.
- **Insecure image sources.** Three images load over `http://`, which risks mixed-content blocking:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta.** This blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Layout tables.** Only some tables have `role="presentation"`. The outer wrapper tables and the 20px spacer table lack it.
- **Doctype and comments.** A `<!-- -->` comment precedes the `<!DOCTYPE>`, and `<!--V2-->` sits between the doctype and `<html>`. Comments before the doctype can trigger quirks mode in older Outlook/IE renderers.
- **Image-sliced layout.** The hero and footer are built from `slices_NN.jpg` images, which are fragile when images are blocked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** are visible in the available source.
- **Recipient email in URL query strings.**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The second is over cleartext HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit. Both URLs put PII in logs and referrers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, the email fails the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirement.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`, and the click domains are `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com`. That looks aligned, but it is unverified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated source does not include them, so they cannot be confirmed from the data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters can be confirmed.** Tracking is carried in opaque `qs=` blobs and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` pixel parameters, and the probe skipped the 58 click links.
- **Product images link to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`.** These are image assets, not landing pages. The product destination URLs were not visible in the truncated source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image (hero slices, products, banners). Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`. Remove the `sv_sveme=<email>` and `?email=` parameters, or replace them with an opaque subscriber ID.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, bypassing the relay. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the original headers.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block. Fill in `<title>`, drop `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Move the comments that precede the doctype so the doctype comes first.
6. Rerun the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve a sample of the click-wrapped links to check landing pages and UTM parameters.
