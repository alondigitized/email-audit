# Ends soon: Up to 50% off furniture!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kids sub-brand again, and I'm still not the target. It's the fifteenth Crate & Barrel send in about two days, and I'm renovating two houses with no nursery in either. Today's earlier "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+" email was Kids too. That makes this the same urgency beat with a different hook. The strip at the top says "final hours! free shipping on orders $99+", and then the hero says "ends soon! up to 50% off furniture."
- It's a decent-looking email. A big lifestyle hero, then bunk beds, a toddler bed, a dresser, and a glider, each with a price and a customer quote. I'd run my eye down the page and be done. The Redondo 54" oak 6-drawer dresser is the only thing I'd even pause on. It's a nice piece, and I could picture it in a guest room if it were sold as a plain dresser. As a "kids dresser" it doesn't fit what I'm buying.
- I wouldn't click, I wouldn't forward it, and I'd probably let it sit unread in the folder. "Ends soon" and "final hours" on the same page, three emails into the same day, undercuts the urgency. Pottery Barn's nightstand send worked because it matched something on my list. This one never gets the chance to.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Up to 50% off furniture" is a concrete offer, and the lifestyle image carries it.
- Each product block has a sale price with the regular price struck through, for example "Sale $1,199.00 Reg. $1,599.00" on the dresser. That's easy to scan and the discount math is visible.
- The customer quotes under each product ("I bought this for my daughter…") read as real social proof, not brand copy.
- The "we love these for you" grid and the Design Desk "100% free" appointment give me secondary places to land without a big scroll.
- The layout is clean and the type is readable on a phone, with no broken images or overlap that I can see.

## What's weak

- The sender name "Crate & Barrel Kids SALE" tells me it's not for me before I open it. I have no kids' furniture on my lists.
- The two urgency lines don't agree. The strip says "final hours," the hero says "ends soon," and the subject says "ends soon." I got "ENDS TODAY!" from the same brand this morning, so I don't believe any of it.
- Each product has two buttons, "Shop Now" and a second "Shop the Sale" or "Shop the Furniture Sale." That's redundant and splits the tap.
- "Up to 50%" is vague. The dresser I looked at is $1,199 against $1,599, which is about 25% off. The hero number made me expect more.
- The app block ("including your chance at $500") and the Scorecard card block feel bolted on. Nothing here shows me what the rewards math is, and I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I notice that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this. Send the Kids furniture push to people who've browsed nursery or kids' items, and send the dresser and glider to everyone else in a non-kids frame. Right now it reads like a bulk blast.
- 2. Pick one urgency line and one deadline, with a real time, and stop re-running it all day. Keep the free-shipping strip or the furniture sale, not both.
- 3. Use one button per product, and put the actual discount in the hero. If the average is around 25%, say that instead of "up to 50%."
- 4. Add delivery windows and shipping cost for large furniture. For a $1,199 dresser, that's the real purchase question.
- 5. Show the rewards or Scorecard math next to the offer for members, not as a separate block further down.

# Technical Audit: "Ends soon: Up to 50% off furniture!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a 460px mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed problems are many images with no alt text and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, because the relay did not capture them and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **82 links were not probed.** QA skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations are verified (dead links, redirect chains, wrong landing pages).
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs.** Several pixels carry a `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` parameter:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/dcfcc0eb-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` is five calls to the same third-party domain (rlcdn.com). Duplicated beacons add requests and are blocked by many clients. Whether this is intended is a decision for the sender.
- **Spacer image from another host.** `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` is served from a different host than the other images. It adds a dependency on Scene7 for a purely structural element.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA.** About 24 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` have no alt attribute. Examples:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `…/m/1/2dae08c2-87d6-446b-a381-301ff0b4a441.jpg`
  - `…/m/1/e802740f-354a-4a43-aedc-b46193d830e8.jpg`
  - `…/m/1/33367d69-2ba1-42e9-8d6a-e7f89e66318b.gif`
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these may be announced by filename or not at all.
  - The pixels and the spacer also lack `alt`. Those should carry `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on some clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-size control is disabled.** The global rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` turn off user text scaling. The universal `line-height:100%` also overrides inherited line-height for all elements.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, which is invalid. The document also declares both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>`.
- **Redundant `format-detection` tags.** There are four, with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`).
- **Overlapping image rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftovers.** The source contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` rule in the MSO block.
- **Mobile breakpoint.** The mobile styles only apply at `max-width:460px`, so viewports from 461 to 639px get the desktop layout. This is a risk for larger phones and small tablets, but I can't confirm the effect from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The tracking parameters are populated. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown.
- **These may be capture artifacts, not sender defects.** QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Treat all three as unconfirmed until the raw `.eml` headers are checked.
- **Footer not verified.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated portion, so they could not be verified.
- **Sender details.** The From address is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a dedicated mail subdomain. That is good practice, but alignment with DKIM/DMARC is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 82 outbound links go through tracking redirects that QA skipped, and the truncated source doesn't show the final URLs or any UTM parameters. The email claims "Up to 50% off furniture", and whether the landing page matches that was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image listed above. Use `alt=""` on all pixels and spacers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove the global `text-size-adjust:none`, and scope `line-height` to specific elements instead of `*`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Merge the four `format-detection` tags into one. Delete the stray template comment and the empty CSS rule. Fix the overlapping `img` max-width media queries.
4. Check the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If they really are missing, add them, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
5. Resolve the 82 tracking redirects with a redirect-following probe, and check the final URLs for UTM parameters and landing-page match.
6. Consider consolidating the five `rlcdn.com` beacons into one.
7. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer and unsubscribe link can be verified.
