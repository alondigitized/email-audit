# 2 DEALS & ONLY DAYS LEFT
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. By my count this is the ninth send in about four days, so I opened it already tired. The sender name "CB2 LONG WKND SALE" at least tells me something is happening. There is an actual sale this time. The "Fresh off a new season" and "Merriment is upon us" sends had free shipping or nothing at all.
- The email stacks four things: free shipping on $99+, up to 60% off, upholstery up to 25% off, and a $1,000 app giveaway. I'm renovating two houses and I do want a console and a sofa, so the dark marble console and the green velvet sofa are on target. But the 60% headline and the 25% upholstery number sit together, and the 25% is what I'd actually pay on a sofa. The copy says "up to," so I don't know what the real discount is on the pieces I'd buy. The hero overlay on the first banner also runs the headline text straight across a busy photo.
- Would I click? Probably one tap on "Shop the Sale," mostly to check whether the console is in the 60% group. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my inspiration folder only if the console were named and priced.

## What's working

- The sender name "CB2 LONG WKND SALE" gives me the event before I open it.
- The "Up to 60% off" hero is the biggest thing on the page, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The marble console and the green velvet sofa are my kind of pieces, and both photos are strong.
- The upholstery block has its own "Shop the Sale" button, which fits my sofa hunt.
- The Rewards Dollars block says "Earn 10% back" and mentions Crate & Barrel. That matters to a highest-rewards-card buyer like me.

## What's weak

- The free-shipping banner is the same offer I've seen in the last three sends. It's also the first thing on the page, so the new news is buried under old news.
- "Up to 60%" and "up to 25%" in the same email feels like bait. I can't tell which discount applies to what I'd buy.
- The "SALE ENDS SOON" line on the console image is tiny and has no date. The email's subject says "only days left," but I can't find a deadline above the fine print.
- The $1,000 app giveaway is a distraction from a sale email. I'm not downloading an app for a sweepstakes.
- The legal block at the bottom is a wall of tiny text, and the free-shipping exclusions are only in that wall. I want to know whether furniture ships free, and I can't tell from the top banner.
- Compared with Pottery Barn's "Holiday movie magic" send, there's no specific product here. Crate & Barrel's "A living room with legendary comfort" at least named sofas and sectionals.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the end date in the hero ("Ends Monday 11:59 PT") and put it above the fold instead of "Sale ends soon."
- 2. Drop the free-shipping banner on this send, or merge it into the sale hero. I've seen it too many times this week.
- 3. Pick one hero product and link it, with a price and a "was/now" figure, instead of an "up to" range.
- 4. Move the app giveaway and the 15% signup block out of this send. Anyone who's on the list already has the 15%.
- 5. Add a line on whether furniture ships free and what delivery costs, since that's what decides a furniture purchase for me.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, is in the HTML. Four content images lack alt text, and a chain of ~11 third-party pixels (Dotomi) loads over plain `http://`. The QA pass rate is 43%. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **58 tracking links** point to click-redirect domains, so the HTTP probe skipped them. Destination validity is unverified, not broken.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 11 Dotomi requests use `http://`, which clients may block or flag as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
- These are cookie-sync pixels. Most clients proxy or strip them, but any that load them will make 11 extra requests.
- **First-party open pixels** are HTTPS: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/79ac090f-…?mi_cid=…`. No issue beyond the missing alt (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images without alt text.** These are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `acb86340-41fe-4efe-8aea-73256b4615a4.jpg`

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, those blocks have no text equivalent.
- **Tracking and pixel images without alt:** `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/79ac090f-…` and the Dotomi pixels. This is low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct value for decorative or tracking images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%`** was left unresolved. It looks like a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `%%…%%` personalization string for a product-reviews/SKU-list block that didn't render. Possible effects:
  - The literal text is visible to the recipient.
  - A dynamic reviews or SKU block is empty or broken.
  - The token sits inside a URL, which would break that link.
- Check where it sits in the source to see which of those applies.
