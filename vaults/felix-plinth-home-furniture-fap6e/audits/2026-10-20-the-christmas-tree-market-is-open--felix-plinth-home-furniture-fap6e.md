# THE CHRISTMAS TREE MARKET IS OPEN >>> 🎄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that it's October 20th and Wayfair is already selling me a Christmas tree. I'm in the middle of two renovations and haven't touched a holiday decoration, so this one doesn't line up with what I'm buying. The hero is gorgeous, though: five lit trees under presents, and the whole top half reads cleanly. Then I scroll and there's no price, no percent off and no deadline anywhere. It's a catalog page, not a promotion.
- The bottom "more styles to scoop up" grid is where it falls apart for me. I count pergolas twice ("On sale now: pergolas" and "Pergolas for less"), comforter sets twice, and washable rugs twice. That's the same duplicated-lamp annoyance I get when three brands email the same product in one week, except Wayfair is doing it to itself inside one email. It's also the eighth-ish Wayfair send this week. On 10/20 they sent "COMFORTERS + SETS for less. *MAJOR* markdowns" and on 10/19 "OUTDOOR DECORATIONS 📣 OCT. 24–26". Wayfair keeps teasing Way Day and never shows me a number.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder only if I were shopping trees, and I'm not. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. Five styled trees on a dark navy background make the "Christmas tree market" idea land in about one second.
- The purple banner button, "Find your festive faves," is big and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The star ratings under every tree tile give me some social proof without any copy.
- The "fast & free shipping" badge is a real trust cue. Delivery is my biggest worry with big items, and Wayfair at least names it for the trees.
- The category pills (classic, pre-lit, frosted, "Verified" trees) are a smart way to filter by what I care about, which is flocked versus pre-lit.

## What's weak

- There's no offer anywhere. There's no percent off, no price and no deadline, only "Some exclusions apply" under a footnote. The pergola email last week at least had "$134 off" and a price.
- The "more styles" grid repeats itself: pergolas ×2, comforters ×2, washable rugs ×2. It reads like filler.
- The subject is all caps with ">>>" and an emoji, which is shouty. It also tells me nothing about price or urgency.
- None of the tree tiles show a name, size or price. I can't tell a 7-foot from a 9-foot, which is what I'd need to decide anything.
- The tree-pill labels are inconsistent. "Verified trees" is capitalized and the other three are lowercase. It's a small thing, but it looks sloppy.
- Nothing in it feels like it's for me. I'm renovating two houses, and Wayfair keeps selling me seasonal décor, not the sinks and rugs from my past browsing. Those only show up in the grid at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero, such as "Up to 40% off pre-lit trees" with a date. Right now the only urgency is the "Open" in the subject.
- 2. Cut the duplicate tiles. One pergola tile, one comforter tile and one rug tile is enough, and use the freed space for tree prices and heights.
- 3. Add a price and a size under each tree tile (e.g., "7.5 ft pre-lit, $249"). Article and Pottery Barn put the price in the email, and Pottery Barn's "Barstools… from $99" subject did that work before I even opened it.
- 4. Personalize the grid with what I've browsed. Kitchen sinks and washable rugs are the only tiles that fit my renovations.
- 5. Drop the ">>>" and the all caps, and name a benefit in the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pre-lit Christmas trees, up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Trees that ship free, in time for Thanksgiving`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `7.5-ft pre-lit, flocked and classic trees, with fast, free delivery`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated trees from $99, rated 4+ stars by other shoppers`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch send built on a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. It has no confirmed broken links or merge-token failures. Its confirmed problems are missing alt text on many images and about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified in this environment.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 material links were probed and 46 were skipped. Every probe failure was an HTTP 429 (rate limit), not a 404 or 5xx. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/m/christmas-trees?...` and three `/pdp/the-holiday-aisle-...` product links. These are probe throttling, not defects.
- **4 links were not checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out:
  - `/pdp/the-holiday-aisle-fir-christmas-tree-w006829595.html`
  - `/pdp/the-holiday-aisle-leterra-dunhill-pre-lit-fir-christ...`
  - `/pdp/the-holiday-aisle-slim-pre-lit-white-christmas-tree-...`
  - `/curated/classic-christmas-trees~ev562746.html`

  Their status is unknown, not failed.
- **Non-HTTPS app-link fallback.** The "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app are sent to an `http://` URL, which depends on a server-side redirect to HTTPS.
- **Per-link tracking IDs.** `_eml` differs on every link (for example `...8bfaa8be`, `...a101a421`, `...a50`). That is expected for per-link click tracking, but it makes URL-based link deduplication impossible.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
  - 6× `https://secure.img.wfrcdn.com/st4/stores/common/emails/assets/wayfair/review_stars/stars_10.png`
  - `WFNA_Winter2026_GTM_Email_Holiday_Ph2_Minibahn.png`
  - `WFNA_Winter2026_GTM_Email_Holiday_Ph2_MidEmail.gif`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_1_purple.png`
  - `WFNA_Winter2026_GTM_Email_SkinnyBanner_HolidayPh2_...`
- **Links with no accessible name.** The three product links (`/pdp/the-holiday-aisle-...-w100710957`, `-w004314038`, `-w004134899`) are probed with link text `[image: stars_10.png]`. The star-rating image is the only link content, so screen readers have nothing meaningful to announce. The links are also tied to the missing-alt images above.
- **20 tracking pixels are non-HTTPS and have no alt text.** They are `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=<recipient-id>`. Clients that block mixed content may not load them, so impression counts for those placements would be undercounted. The mobile CSS (`@media (max-width: 480px)`, `u ~ div`, `.u-FullWidth`) looks standard. Beyond that, the truncated source only supports the findings above.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS but also has no alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the supplied data.
- The recipient identifier `_emr=fa85246b-6af1-43b2-bff9-59142c2b7385` is resolved and consistent across all links. The same value is sent in the clear as `p=` in the `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels, so a stable user identifier travels unencrypted. This is a privacy and security observation, not a rendering bug.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA flags this as likely a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay. It is not evidence that Wayfair omitted them.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The cause is the same relay limitation. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none was reported as failing. The truncated HTML does not show the footer, so I can't confirm the link text or the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses proprietary parameters instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=19`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `refid=MKTEML_141821`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16535`, `mdlid=...`, `sltid` (slot position), `wfcs=cs7`. This works only if Wayfair's own analytics consume these parameters. Third-party analytics such as GA won't attribute the traffic by default.
- **Landing pages match the subject.** The hero image links to `/m/christmas-trees` and the PDP links are Holiday Aisle Christmas trees. The "Shop Sale" link goes to `/daily-sales`, which is generic and not tree-specific.
- **App deep link.** The OneLink URL uses `af_dp=wayfairapp://home`, so it lands on the app home screen rather than the Christmas tree collection.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, mid-email, banner and header images. Give the star images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`. This also stops sending the `p=` recipient ID in cleartext.
3. Make the product links accessible by putting product-name text inside the anchor, or an `aria-label`, instead of only the star image.
4. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw message headers from the original inbox. The relay-processed copy is not reliable for this.
5. Re-run link probing with a longer time budget or lower concurrency to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed links.
6. If third-party analytics matter, add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside the existing parameters.
