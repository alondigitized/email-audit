# New arrivals inspired by heritage design

## Executive summary

- Okay, "heritage design" — this one actually looks like *my* kind of Pottery Barn email. Indigo ceramics, warm wood furniture, a dining table with actual dimensions listed. No kids' dorm gear, no random Halloween skeleton parade dominating the top half. It's coherent and it's clearly built around the reno aesthetic I'm actually chasing for both houses.
- But I opened Pottery Barn's own "Shades of indigo—bedding, decor & more" email yesterday (8/1), which scored a 7 from me for basically the same blue-and-white color story. Today's hero copy literally says "grounded by deep indigo blues" again. That's two indigo pitches in 24 hours from the same brand — my thumb remembers this. And unlike the 8/1 "on sale & shipping free" send that actually told me a real deal was happening (8/10 from me), this one has zero discount anywhere. No %, no free shipping banner, nothing.
- Would I click? Maybe skim the "Recommended Just For You" grid for the Toscana dining table since I'm actually furniture shopping right now, but I'm not clicking with urgency and I'm definitely not forwarding this to anyone — there's no deal to brag about scoring.

## What's working

- The heritage/indigo aesthetic reads like an actual grown-up furnishing a house, not the Dormify-for-college-kids miss from earlier this week.
- "Recommended Just For You" surfaced real furniture — a Toscana Extendable Dining Table with dimensions listed (60"-124.5") and a Remmy Swivel Chair — genuinely useful when I'm outfitting two places at once.
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" callout is exactly my buying behavior — I run everything through the highest-rewards card, so this is the one section that actually speaks to me directly.
- "Design Crew — Free Design Services" is relevant to an active renovation in a way most PB emails aren't.
- Clean render, no broken images or overlapping text — product grid photography looks legit.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere in the body — compare that to PB's own 8/1 "on sale & shipping free" send, or Wayfair's "48-HOUR CLEARANCE UP TO 70% OFF" rug email that scored a 9 from me — those give me a reason to act today. This one gives me a mood board.
- The indigo color story is a rerun of yesterday's dedicated Indigo email. Between that, the florals/plaids NEW ARRIVALS send, and Dormify, that's four Pottery Barn-family emails in three days — my inbox is starting to feel like Crate & Barrel's bedding-family fatigue I flagged with Crate & Barrel itself this week.
- The Halloween Shop block wedged in the middle breaks the "heritage design" narrative — feels like a different email that got bolted on.
- "SHOP NOW" / "SHOP ALL NEW ARRIVALS" are generic CTA copy — CB2's designer-spotlight send at least told me a specific name (David Girelli) I could get curious about; this just says "shop."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer in the hero — even a modest one. PB already proved a real discount banner outperforms a mood-board send (their 8/1 "on sale & shipping free" scored highest of the week).
- 2. Cut or delay the indigo messaging — it was the entire subject of yesterday's email. Rotate to a different color story or skip color-story language entirely this send.
- 3. Pull the Halloween Shop into its own dedicated email instead of interrupting a furniture narrative.
- 4. Sharpen the CTA copy — "Shop the Dining Table" beats "Shop Now" for someone mid-scroll.

# Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "New arrivals inspired by heritage design"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table-based ESP template (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `click.e.potterybarn.com` / `image.e.potterybarn.com` hosts) with a leaked server-side code fragment in the `<head>` and widespread missing `alt` attributes; QA pass rate 57%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary CTAs route through ESP click-tracking redirects: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7...` (opaque token, destination not resolvable from source).
- Secondary click tracking via `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/10dedcde-cf99-436d-991e-f6eb367b96fb?mi_mid=019fc28f-5080-7000-81e8-3a4e54a07b79&cp_tp` — a second, distinct click-tracking vendor pattern from the primary `click.e.` domain, suggesting two separate tracking layers on the same send.
- Third-party ad-tech pixel present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Amobee/Turn conversion pixel).
- Open-tracking pixel served over **HTTP, not HTTPS**, with the recipient's email address embedded unencoded in the query string: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. The `@` is not percent-encoded (`%40`), and cleartext transport exposes the recipient identifier to any network intermediary.
- QA flagged 54 tracking/redirect links as skipped from HTTP probing (informational only — not evaluated for liveness).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- QA confirmed ~40 images missing `alt` text, including all product tile images (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg` across two grids, `farmhouse-platform-bed-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-l.jpg`, `hydrocotton-quick-dry-towel-l.jpg`, footer/banner assets, and tracking pixels). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or fall back to filenames.
- Four images load over `http://` instead of `https://`: `spacer_0.gif` (edm.westelm.com), `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` (edm.potterybarn.com), `slices_02.jpg` (edm.potterybarn.com/2022 brand banner), and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel — non-HTTPS assets risk being blocked or triggering mixed-content warnings in clients that render HTML email in a browser-like context.
- The primary spacer image (`spacer_0.gif`) is served from `edm.westelm.com` rather than a Pottery Barn asset host — cross-brand shared infrastructure (Williams-Sonoma family), not a functional bug but worth noting for asset-ownership tracking.
- Malformed embedded code: the second `<style>` block in `<head>` contains non-CSS server-side statements —
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  This is PHP `header()` output syntax that has leaked into the client-facing template inside a `<style>` tag. It's inert as CSS (browsers/mail clients will ignore it as invalid), but it indicates a template-build defect where server response code was concatenated into the HTML output rather than executed server-side.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No broken or unresolved merge tags (e.g. stray `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) are present in the truncated source. The recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` resolved correctly and consistently across the preference-center link (`coreemail.html?email=...`) and the open-tracking pixel (`sv_sveme=...`).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — may be a relay-capture limitation rather than sender omission; cannot confirm from HTML alone since unsubscribe is header-based, not body-based.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown via the AgentMail relay capture.
- A working preference-center/unsubscribe destination is present in-body: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, satisfying CAN-SPAM's requirement for a visible opt-out mechanism independent of header-level unsubscribe.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be fully verified: nearly all outbound links are wrapped in opaque ESP redirect tokens (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...?mi_mid=...`) rather than direct URLs carrying visible UTM parameters, so destination landing pages and UTM alignment are not inspectable from the static HTML. No UTM query parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, etc.) appear anywhere in the visible/truncated markup.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — indicates a build/templating defect that should be traced upstream.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all product and banner images for accessibility and to preserve context when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail).
3. Serve all assets over HTTPS — migrate the four `http://` sources (`spacer_0.gif`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel) to HTTPS equivalents.
4. Stop passing the recipient's raw email address unencoded over an HTTP tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`); at minimum percent-encode it, and move the endpoint to HTTPS to avoid cleartext PII transmission.
5. Confirm with the sending platform/relay whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are actually being sent but dropped by the AgentMail relay, versus genuinely absent — if genuinely absent, add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support.
