# ☀️ CLEARANCE ☀️ Get summer-ready ☀️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Wayfair email in roughly 72 hours. I know because I've been counting — and the kitchen mats send from earlier today was already sitting unread in my folder when this one landed. So I'm coming in with the native skepticism of someone who's been getting Wayfair'd twice a day like clockwork.
- That said — I'll give credit where it's due. This one actually has *content* worth looking at. The Memorial Day clearance hook is real, the offers are specific (40% off, 50% off, a BOGO on Adirondack chairs), and the "picked just for you" section at least gestures at personalization. If I were only seeing one Wayfair email this week, this would feel like a solid send. The problem is I've trained myself to archive first, ask questions later, and Wayfair is the reason I have that instinct.
- I'd probably open this one (Memorial Day clearance is exactly the kind of thing I'd save for a second-home outdoor space decision), but I'd do it later on a laptop, not by tapping through on my phone. It's a heavy email — too many competing tiles to navigate on mobile. Mark as unread, revisit. I wouldn't forward to a friend, but I might screenshot the Adirondack BOGO and text it to my wife.

## What's working

- **BOGO Adirondack chair is genuinely useful** — that's the kind of specific deal that lands differently when you're outfitting two outdoor spaces. Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel never get this concrete in the hero.
- **Memorial Day hook is timely and credible** — the clearance framing makes sense for the season, and the "doorbuster deals" label creates urgency without feeling manufactured.
- **"Picked just for you" section exists** — even if the personalization is algorithmic, the label signals effort. That's more than the kitchen mats email earlier today attempted.
- **Star ratings on product tiles** — social proof is visible in the grid and it's the kind of signal I actually check before committing to a furniture purchase.
- **Free delivery over $35 is prominent** — that's an easy threshold to clear on any home goods order and it removes a genuine purchase objection.

## What's weak

- **Three sun emojis flanking "CLEARANCE" in the subject is too much** — it reads like a liquidation email, not a curated clearance event. Article's welcome email, which I got recently, had zero emoji and felt more premium. Wayfair is competing with those brands in my inbox.
- **Second email today, fifth this week** — the cadence is doing active damage to the brand. Even a good email reads as noise at this frequency.
- **No explicit deadline visible** — "Memorial Day" implies urgency but nowhere in the scroll does a hard date appear. Crate & Kids said "happening now" which at least creates present-tense pressure; this email just gestures at a holiday.
- **The layout is a catalog dump, not a curated send** — eight+ offer tiles fighting for attention means I can't identify what Wayfair actually wants me to buy. That diffusion kills conversion.
- **"Shop all sales" as the primary CTA is maximally generic** — it's three words that point at a firehose. After the BOGO Adirondack moment in the body, the email should have a matching CTA anchored to that deal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the tile count in half and lead with the Adirondack BOGO** — that's the sharpest, most memorable offer in the email. Build the scroll around it, not buried in a doorbuster subsection.
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline** — "Ends Monday, May 26" or a countdown. Memorial Day is a holiday, not a date.
- 3. **Kill two of the three sun emojis in the subject** — one emoji as punctuation is fine. Three reads as shouting.
- 4. **Replace "shop all sales" CTA copy** with something tied to the hero offer — "Shop Outdoor Clearance" or "Grab the Chair Deal" would feel more intentional.
- 5. **Reduce send cadence to one email per day max** — the second send of the day actively devalues the first.

- **Subject Alt B:** `BOGO Adirondack chairs + big summer clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one outdoor chair, get one 25% off. Plus free delivery over $35.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance pricing on patio, rugs, and lighting — ends Memorial Day weekend.`
