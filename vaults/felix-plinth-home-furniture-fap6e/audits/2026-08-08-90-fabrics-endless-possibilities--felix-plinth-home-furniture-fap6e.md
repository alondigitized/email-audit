# 90+ fabrics. Endless possibilities.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually the CB2 send I've been low-key hoping for. No sale banner, no marble bit, no "AVAILABLE AGAIN" restock hook — just a straight custom-upholstery pitch with a genuinely nice fabric-swatch hero. "Custom is such a good look" is a decent line, and "Order 10 free swatches" as the first CTA is smart — that's a real furniture-shopper action, not a vague "shop now."
- The problem is I'm mid-renovation on two houses and I already have a swatch program going with Crate & Barrel-adjacent brands, so "90+ fabrics, endless possibilities" reads more like a catalog explainer than something urgent. It's well-made, it's on-brand, but there's no hook that makes me stop scrolling and act today. I'd read the whole thing on my commute, probably order swatches for the sectional I'm eyeing, and not forward it — this isn't share-with-a-friend content, it's bookmark-for-later content.
- Compared to the CB2 sends I've seen this week — "AVAILABLE AGAIN" (6/10, thin restock hook) and "THE AUGUST SALE" (6/10, plain seasonal) — this one's actually the strongest of the three because it commits to one story (custom fabric program) instead of half-doing a sale or a restock. It's the anti-Wayfair: no siren emoji, no 72-hour countdown, no five sales competing for my eyeballs.

## What's working

- The draped-fabric hero shot is genuinely premium-looking — leather, velvet, wool all stacked together, no stock-photo vibe.
- "Order 10 free swatches" is the single best CTA I've seen from CB2 lately — it matches exactly how I shop for upholstery right now.
- "Design experts at your service / book a free appointment" is a real value-add for someone renovating two houses at once, not filler copy.
- Single coherent narrative (fabric → possibilities → design help → new sofa collection) instead of the multi-sale chaos from earlier CB2 sends this week.
- Rewards blurb at the bottom (10% back, Crate & Barrel family) is a nice quiet reminder I'm leaving money on the table if I'm not in the loyalty program.

## What's weak

- Zero urgency or specific offer — no %, no $ off, no deadline. After Wayfair's countdown clock and Pottery Barn's 60%-off banner this week, this feels almost too polite by comparison.
- "More for you" product grid at the bottom is generic — beds, nightstands, a side table — none of it ties back to the custom-fabric story up top, so it dilutes the one clean idea the email had going.
- No mention of lead times beyond the opening line ("7 weeks custom, 1-2 weeks in-stock") — for someone juggling two renovation timelines, I'd want that surfaced again near the CTA, not buried in paragraph one.
- No visible loyalty-tier callout up top even though I might already be a Rewards member — the 10%-back mention only shows up in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real incentive tied to the swatch order — even something small like "free swatches + $50 off your first custom order" would convert this from "nice idea" to "act now."
- 2. Swap the generic "More for you" grid for fabric/upholstery-specific pieces only (sectionals, chairs, ottomans) so the whole email stays on-theme instead of trailing off into bedroom furniture.
- 3. Pull the lead-time line ("in as little as 7 weeks") down next to the "Browse Our Fabrics" button — that's the actual decision-driver for a renovation buyer.
- 4. Surface Rewards Dollars math near the top, not just in the footer — "earn 10% back on this order" next to the swatch CTA would nudge me harder than a buried mention.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free swatches, 90+ fabrics — pick yours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Custom sofas, delivered in 7 weeks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order 10 free swatches before you commit to a fabric.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Book a free design appointment — pick the right fabric the first time.`
