# Can’t wait to get home
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction scrolling through this thing: it's long. Like, "I have to stop and check my phone battery" long. Living room, entryway, dining room, kids' room, then a whole separate Design Desk mini-magazine with Lupita Nyong'o's L.A. patio, then a sweepstakes, then a product grid, then a credit card pitch, then the app pitch, then a store locator. It's less an email and more a home goods buffet, and by the time I hit "we love these for you" I've forgotten there was a 60% off sale at the top.
- The good news buried in there: the actual furniture is genuinely my taste — that wire-brushed dresser-nightstand hybrid and the weathered oak dining tables look like something I'd screenshot into my Pinterest-adjacent folder. But between the Lupita Nyong'o editorial (nice photo, zero relevance to me deciding on a dining table) and yet another "design kids spaces that grow with them" module — I don't have kids, and Crate & Kids has already sent me three of these this cycle — this email doesn't feel curated for me, it feels like every asset in the CMS got dumped into one send.
- I'm not clicking the hero. If anything I'll scroll straight to "we love these for you" because it's the only part that respects my time, and even then I'm doing it out of habit, not urgency. Not forwarding this — there's no single hero moment worth sending a friend. Marking read, moving on.

## What's working

- The "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" banner up top is clean and readable — no squinting required, unlike some of Wayfair's cluttered purple banner stacks.
- The "we love these for you" grid at the bottom is the best part of the email — actual dimensions in the product names ("Basque 82"-118" Extendable Dining Table," "Keane 32" Charging Nightstand") which is exactly the spec-level detail I need mid-renovation.
- Room-by-room CTAs (Shop Living Room, Shop Entryway, Shop Dining Room) are specific verb+noun buttons, not vague "Discover" nonsense.
- No broken images, no overlapping text — technically clean render even at this length.

## What's weak

- This is basically Article's room-rotation idea (living room, then dining room, days apart) except Crate & Barrel crammed four rooms into one scroll instead of pacing it — it dilutes every single room instead of giving me one clean reason to click.
- The Lupita Nyong'o / Design Desk feature is a nice magazine spread but it's competing with the actual sale for my attention, and it pushed the product grid — the part I care about — below the fold by a country mile.
- "Design kids spaces that grow with them" — no. I don't have kids. This is the same fatigue Crate & Kids gave me three times already this cycle with the crib-forward sends; seeing it wedged into the flagship C&B email too tells me segmentation isn't happening at all.
- The asterisk on "up to 60% off" with no visible terms anywhere in the email is the kind of soft fine-print flag that makes me trust the number less, not more.
- No urgency anywhere on the main sale — Wayfair's "ENDS TONIGHT" is annoying but at least it tells me why to act today; this just sits there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this email in half. Pick ONE room (dining room, since that's clearly the freshest merchandising push) and save entryway/living room/kids for separate sends — Article proved this works better as a rotation, not a dump.
- 2. Move "we love these for you" up near the top — that product grid with real dimensions is the strongest asset in the email and it's currently buried under a celebrity puff piece.
- 3. Drop the kids' furniture module from the flagship send entirely, or gate it behind actual purchase-history segmentation — I've now seen this beat five times across two different Crate brands this month.
- 4. Put real terms behind that 60%-off asterisk, or drop the asterisk — right now it just reads as bait.
- 5. Give the sale a deadline in the subject and hero, the way the Design Desk Sweeps at least does with its "through August 3" line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off ends soon — dining & bedroom picks inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your next room, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extendable dining tables, king beds, and nightstands — all on sale through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The pieces we'd put in a renovation-in-progress home right now.`
