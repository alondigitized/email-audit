# Just landed: The fall deals edit
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel family send I've gotten today, and the second "deals" roundup. The earlier one was "Inside: Our best deals of the season" (7/10). I almost archived it unread. It's the same bones: sale hero, sofas, storage, kitchen, clearance. I did get further than I expected, because this one leads with furniture ("up to 20% off bestselling sofas, sectionals & chairs" and "up to 25% off storage furniture & more"). That's what I'm actually buying across two houses, not another stroller or a celebrity nursery.
- It's a long scroll with a lot of "Shop Now" buttons and no single hero offer. Nothing is wrong, and nothing makes me stop. I'd click once, on the sofa block, to check whether the 20% stacks with my card's rewards. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder only if I saw a specific piece I liked, and the generic sofa photo doesn't give me one.

## What's working

- The furniture blocks come first: sofas and sectionals at up to 20% off, then storage at up to 25% off. That matches a two-house renovation.
- The sale prices on the kitchen products (knife set, Vitamix, Le Creuset dutch oven) are visible with strikethrough pricing and "+ Shipping Free" cues. That's rewards-math friendly.
- The "hand assembled in the USA" line is a real trust cue for upholstery. It's more useful than "bestselling."
- The 5% back in Reward Dollars block for the Crate card is something I'd use, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.
- The dark, moody photography reads as fall and looks good. It's cleaner than Wayfair's banner-stack emails.
- "The Design Desk" with a free appointment is a good touch. It echoes what Pottery Barn Design Services pitched me with "FREE design services."

## What's weak

- There's no single hero offer. I count about nine stacked blocks, each with its own "Shop Now" or "Learn More." My eye has nowhere to land.
- The CTA copy is generic: "Shop the Sale," "Shop Now." It doesn't say sofas, storage or coffee.
- I can't see a deadline, so I get no urgency. "Just landed" in the subject promises something new, but the content looks like the same sale I got earlier today.
- The Affirm "pay later" block and the app-download block with a "$500 chance" pull focus away from furniture. Neither helps someone ordering a sectional.
- There's no swatch or fabric-sample mention and no delivery-time info for the sofas. That's the first thing I'd want before buying large furniture.
- The footer is a wall of tiny legal text, and the CB2 and Crate Kids cross-promos add to my fatigue. I'm already tired of the whole family today.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one lead offer and let it own the first screen.** The sofa and sectional event is the strongest. Put a swatch-and-sample line and a delivery window next to it, such as "ships in 2-4 weeks, free fabric swatches."
- 2. Cut the Affirm, app and Design Desk blocks down to a single slim strip. Put the Reward Dollars line directly under the furniture offer.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop sofas up to 20% off," "Shop storage up to 25% off."
- 4. Add a real end date so the urgency is credible.
- 5. Suppress or consolidate the family sends. Two deals roundups in one day is the problem more than the creative.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall furniture deals, hand assembled in the USA`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink–assembled template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) with about 74 click-tracked links and a large number of images with no alt attribute. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the relay stripped the headers. The visible source also has a few real markup and accessibility problems.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations and UTM parameters are unverified.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe on all 74 links because they are click-redirect domains. No redirect chains, status codes or final URLs were checked.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` are third-party identity-sync pixels, and each carries the same recipient hash `s=56aaa278…cfd8`. They are extra third-party requests per open.
- **Open and personalization pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/9566117e-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are in the markup.
- **Per-recipient ID in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa278…` hash appears in the query string of the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` product images. Opening the email therefore also exposes that ID to the image host.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA.** About 38 images are flagged, and the list was truncated. They fall into two groups:
  - **Content images:** about 21 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif` images (for example `2749e9c3-…jpg`, `63920a83-…gif`) and 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`560d850e7e517ede.png` through `18f126218cc222ec.png`). With images off, or in a screen reader, this content has no text equivalent. If any of these are linked, the link has no accessible name.
  - **Tracking pixels:** the `rlcdn.com`, `o.gif` and `dv.…` images. Missing alt here is harmless, but `alt=""` would be correct.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing is suppressed.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is set on `body` and `*`.
- **Duplicate and conflicting `<meta>` tags.** `charset` is declared twice (`http-equiv` and `charset=`). `format-detection` is declared three times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. This is low severity, since clients tolerate it.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It has no render impact but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Desktop image cap.** The `@media (min-width:640px)` and `(min-width:768px)` rules set `img {max-width: 640px}` and `img {max-width: 600px}`, so the two rules conflict. The cascade makes the 768px rule win, and the 640px rule is dead code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and later modules were not reviewed.
- Personalization is done server-side by Movable Ink through the `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters. Whether those images actually rendered content is untested.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it. This is **unverified, not confirmed missing**.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. RFC 8058 one-click support can't be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data.
- The sender and tracking subdomains are aligned (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `image.mail.…`, `mi.…`, `dv.…`), which is consistent with a properly authenticated setup, but that is an inference.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Cannot be assessed.** The probes were skipped and the HTML was truncated. UTM parameters, landing-page alignment and the redirect target for "The fall deals edit" were not observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all ~28 content images (the `image.mail…/m/1/*` and `mi…/p/rp/*.png` assets). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Consider dropping `text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Collapse the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags. Escape the title as `&amp;`. Delete the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml`, or capture headers before the relay, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Until then, treat those three warnings as unresolved rather than failures.
5. Resolve a sample of the 74 tracked links, for example with a redirect-following probe, to verify final URLs, UTM parameters and status codes.
6. Review the necessity of the five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels. Each is an extra third-party call and a privacy and deliverability consideration.
