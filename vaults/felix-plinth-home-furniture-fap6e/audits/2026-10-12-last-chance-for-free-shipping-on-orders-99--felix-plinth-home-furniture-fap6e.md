# Last chance for free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was that this is the fourth Crate & Barrel family send today, and I nearly archived it. I opened it because the hero says "bestselling sofas & sectionals," and that's the one thing on my list right now. The hero image is a wallpapered vignette with a horse figurine, a lamp and a plant, and there isn't a sofa in it. The copy promises upholstery and the picture shows decor, so I had to work out what I was actually looking at.
- The email is also two promos fighting each other. The green banner says "final weekend: up to 20% off," and the next block says "ends today: free shipping on orders over $99." Which deadline is real? Then there's the Crate&kids tile, which I've now seen three times today. I have no nursery to furnish, and its pale gray text on beige is hard to read. The 10% back in Reward Dollars on the Crate Card is the only thing that speaks to how I shop, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.
- Would I click? Probably one tap on Shop Now, to see whether the sofas are actually 20% off. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it only if the sofa page showed real products and delivery timing.

## What's working

- The hero headline names sofas and sectionals, which matches what I'm shopping for across two houses.
- The dark green banner with a white "Shop Now" button is easy to find and easy to tap.
- The free shipping band has a clear $99 threshold and a tidy two-by-three grid of category links.
- The Reward Dollars block (10% back with the Crate Card) is the one thing aimed at my rewards-card habit.
- The "we love these for you" grid has prices under each item, which is how I screenshot things for my inspiration folder.

## What's weak

- The hero image is decor, not a sofa. The headline and the picture don't match, and "up to 20%" with an asterisk and "select styles" feels vague.
- "Final weekend" and "ends today" are two different deadlines in one email. It reads as stacked promos, and the urgency loses credibility.
- The Crate&kids block is irrelevant to me, and its light gray body text is hard to read on the beige background.
- The "for you" grid is glasses, bowls and a juice glass. That has nothing to do with the sofas, and nothing in it suggests the sender knows me.
- Furniture shipping is unaddressed. "Standard shipping; exclusions apply" doesn't tell me whether a $2,000 sectional ships free or when it arrives. The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text.
- The app, TheDesignDesk and store-locator blocks pile on below the fold. That's too many asks for one send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real sectional in the hero, with a price or "from $X" and the free-shipping line on the same banner. Pottery Barn's Thanksgiving send led with a recognizable product vignette and one dominant sale strip. Here the headline and picture disagree.
- 2. Pick one deadline and one offer. Either "20% off sofas" or "free shipping," not both with different end dates. CB2's "2 SALES END SOON" was cleaner. It stacked two offers but labeled each one clearly.
- 3. Say how furniture ships. Add "free delivery on sofas over $X" or a delivery window, and mention fabric swatches.
- 4. Cut the Crate&kids tile for anyone who hasn't browsed kids. I've had three Kids sends today, so the segmentation is clearly off.
- 5. Replace the glassware grid with products from the category in the hero (sofas, rugs, lighting). Wayfair at least tried "picked just for you," even if its pergolas were overkill.
- 6. Make the Kids tile text and the fine print larger and darker, and move the Reward Dollars block up near the offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a Movable Ink-driven template. QA shows 0 hard failures and 3 warnings, but the 57% pass rate is mostly missing alt text on about 26 images, and the three header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay. The source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and link `href`s could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation:** 60 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and UTM handling are unverified.
- **LiveRamp pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` are five repeated third-party pixels with no alt text. The `s=` value is a 64-character hash that matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` prefix used on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs. A per-recipient identifier is therefore passed to a third party (LiveRamp) through image URLs. Whether it is a hashed email is not confirmed. That depends on Crate & Barrel's privacy disclosures, so flag it for review.
- **Tracking pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7509dcee-…` are open/impression pixels. Both are missing alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (about 18):**
  - 10 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` and `/m/47/`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `d8b928f0-…gif`, `3c9b2911-…jpg`.
  - 7 dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`.
  - `s7d5.scene7.com/.../25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F50px%5FWhite`.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from `alt=""`. Any of these that carry offer or product content are a real accessibility gap, and the GIFs may carry the "free shipping" messaging.
  - For tracking pixels and spacers, `alt=""` is correct. The 7 `rp` images may need descriptive alt text.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Image width rules:** the `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` rule is overridden by the later `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`, which also matches at 768px and above. The 600px rule is dead code.
- **Head markup:**
  - Unescaped `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - There are duplicate `format-detection` metas and both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>`.
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
  - These are minor and unlikely to break rendering.
- **Global CSS:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` and `*{line-height:100%}` are broad resets that risk unintended inheritance. This is a risk only, not a confirmed defect.
- **Language:** `lang="en"` is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` tokens. Per-recipient content is delivered through Movable Ink `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` image parameters, which look populated. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Headers:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - The QA output attributes all three to the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed rather than proven defects.
  - Check the raw `.eml` from a direct inbox, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Body:** the unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated footer and were not verifiable.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. UTM parameters and landing pages sit behind the 60 skipped redirect links. The "$99+" free-shipping claim cannot be matched to a landing page from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the offer and product images (the `/m/1/*.gif|jpg` and `mi…/p/rp/*.png` images). Set `alt=""` explicitly on spacers and pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Re-test the three header checks against a raw message from a direct inbox, not the relay. If `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are really absent, add them.
4. Resolve the 60 redirect links with a follow-redirect probe to confirm UTMs and the landing page.
5. Review the repeated `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels and the identifier they carry against the stated privacy policy.
6. Clean up the template: escape `&amp;` in the `<title>`, remove duplicate metas and the `IMPUT HERE` comment, and delete the dead 600px `img` rule.
