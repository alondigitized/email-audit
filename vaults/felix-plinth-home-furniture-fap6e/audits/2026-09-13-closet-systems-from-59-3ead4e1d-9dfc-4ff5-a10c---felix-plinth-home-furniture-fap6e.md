But then I open it and it's the exact same "must-shop steals" garage-sale grid I've been getting for four straight sends now: closet systems, privacy screens, pergolas, Adirondack chairs, air conditioners, gazebo deals, frying pans, hot tubs, bird baths, wall clocks. That's not a closet systems email, that's a "we have a warehouse, please buy something from it" email with a closet system thumbnail bolted onto the subject line to get the open.
- The one thing I'll give it: no broken hero image this time. First three Wayfair emails this week (gazebo deals, area rug, pergolas) all had a blank white rectangle up top — this one actually renders. Small win, low bar.

## What's working

- Subject line hit an actual category I'm shopping for (closet systems) instead of pergolas or gazebos, which is a first this week.
- No render bugs this time — every hero tile actually loaded, unlike the last three Wayfair sends that opened on a blank broken-image box.
- 60% off banner up top is at least a real, specific number, not vague "save big" language.
- Rewards module ("5% rewards on every item, always") is a legit loyalty callout if I'm already a member.

## What's weak

- The subject promises closet systems, but the email is a 20-tile catalog dump — closet systems get one small thumbnail and then I'm buried in pergolas, AC units, hot tubs, and bird baths that have nothing to do with why I opened it.
- Zero product detail on the actual closet system — no shelving config, no sizing, no material, nothing a person actually renovating a closet needs to decide.
- "ends tonight" urgency banner paired with a grid that looks identical to every other "picked for you" grid Wayfair's sent me this week — the urgency doesn't feel real when the creative hasn't changed.
- This is the same purple "Last Call" banner treatment and same recently-purchased-style grid as the pergola and gazebo emails — total fatigue at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero actually about closet systems — a real photo of an organized closet system with a starting price and a "Shop Closet Systems" button, not a 20-category catalog dump. If the subject sells one category, the fold should deliver that category.
- 2. Cut the grid down to 3-4 categories max that are thematically related (closet systems, shelving, storage — adjacent renovation stuff), not pergolas next to bird baths next to cat trees.
- 3. Add real closet system product info — configuration options, price range, maybe a "shop by size" filter — since that's the actual decision I need help with.
- 4. Drop or substantiate the "ends tonight" urgency — if every email says that, it means nothing anymore.
