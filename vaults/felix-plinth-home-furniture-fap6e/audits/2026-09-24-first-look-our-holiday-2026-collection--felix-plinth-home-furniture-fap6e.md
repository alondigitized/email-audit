# First look: Our holiday 2026 collection!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, holiday wreaths in September — that's the first thing that hits me, and not in a good way. I get that retail runs six months ahead, but "first look! holiday 2026" on September 24th feels like Crate & Barrel skipped straight past Halloween and Thanksgiving to plant a flag. That said, the wreath wall photo at the top is genuinely gorgeous — moody green paneling, red bow, multiple wreath styles — and it's the kind of shot that would actually work on my Pinterest-adjacent folder if the timing weren't so aggressively early.
- The category grid (Christmas decor, ornaments, stockings, garlands & wreaths, tabletop, bedding) is clean and I like that it's not just "shop now" — it tells me what's actually in scope. But then the email sprawls: TheDesignDesk pitch again (third time this week per my own history — "Tricky entryway," "perfect rug," and now this), a Caraway coffee maker preorder that has nothing to do with holiday decor, then a random ornament/holiday-gift product grid, then the Crate Card rewards pitch, then an app download push with a $500 sweepstakes tease, then TheDesignDesk again. It's five different emails stapled together.
- Would I click? Maybe on the wreath hero out of curiosity since I do want the family room at the second house to feel finished for the holidays, but I wouldn't forward this — it's too unfocused and too early to be useful to a friend right now.

## What's working

- The wreath hero image is the strongest visual C&B has sent me this week — real product, real styling, matches the "decorate your halls" copy directly above it.
- The category tile grid (decor/ornaments/stockings/garlands/tabletop/bedding) gives me a scannable map instead of forcing me to scroll blind.
- "Shop All Christmas" is a clear, unambiguous CTA button, well-placed under the tiles.
- The Crate Card 10% back mention is genuinely useful to me — I run everything through my highest-rewards card anyway, so seeing that reinforced mid-email is a small win.

## What's weak

- Full holiday content on September 24 is premature — I haven't even done Halloween candy yet, and it makes the "best finds sell out fast" urgency line feel manufactured rather than credible.
- TheDesignDesk block appears twice in the same email, which after seeing it three times this week already (per "Tricky entryway" and "perfect rug" sends) is starting to feel like the brand is padding emails rather than segmenting them.
- The Caraway coffee maker preorder is a total non-sequitur dropped into a holiday-decor email — no color-palette or gifting frame to connect it to the "first look holiday" theme.
- No pricing or specificity on the actual holiday hero (the wreaths) — I see gorgeous styling but no "shop this look" link straight to the wreath wall, just the generic "Shop All Christmas" button.
- This is the second time this week I've seen C&B stack an app-download/sweepstakes pitch onto an otherwise unrelated email; it reads like leftover inventory from other campaigns rather than a coherent single send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this email in half — split the Caraway coffee maker, the Crate Card rewards, and the app/$500 sweepstakes into separate sends. One holiday-decor email should stay about holiday decor.
- 2. Add a direct "Shop This Wreath Wall" link under the hero image instead of only the generic "Shop All Christmas" button — I want to buy the exact styled look I just saw, the way CB2's best-sellers sale at least kept its CTA tied to what was pictured.
- 3. Push this send closer to early-to-mid October — Pottery Barn's "cozy season" bedding drop at least felt seasonally honest; this jumps the gun by weeks.
- 4. Drop the duplicate TheDesignDesk block — one mention per email is plenty, especially given how often I'm already seeing this pitch this week.
- 5. If the coffee maker has to stay, frame it as a hosting/holiday-gifting item, not an orphaned "only at Crate" callout with zero connection to the wreaths above it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Get ahead on holiday decorating (before it sells out)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New wreaths, garlands, and tabletop pieces — plus free design help.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the wreath wall above, then book a free Design Desk session.`
