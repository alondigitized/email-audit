# Don't miss The Warehouse Sale + Online-only deals on select furniture
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Up to 60% off" and "Online Exclusive" stacked in the top banner got my attention immediately — that's real number, not "up to great savings" fluff. Then it just... rolls into four collections (Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston, Farmhouse) each with a "NOW ON SALE" red tag, and that's actually useful for someone like me juggling two houses — I can scan collection names and know instantly which one might work for the guest room versus the home office. The Livingston desk shot especially caught my eye since I'm still hunting office furniture for the second house.
- Where it loses me: there's no actual percentage or price next to any of these four collections. "Up to 60% off" is sitting way up top disconnected from the products, so by the time I'm scrolling through Toscana dining or the Farmhouse bed, I have no idea if that piece is 10% off or 60% off. Crate & Barrel's kitchen electrics email at least showed me products with clear categorization; this just says "trust us, it's on sale" four times in a row.
- I'd click through to Livingston for the desk, mostly out of curiosity about the price, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone — it's a solid warehouse-sale reminder, not something worth sharing.

## What's working

- The top banner discount ("Up to 60% off") is bold and unmissable — better than Wayfair's emoji-cluttered subject that buried the actual sale.
- Four distinct collections shown as full lifestyle shots, not a cluttered grid — feels more like C&B's "Workspace Refresh" desk email than a generic sale blast.
- "Recommended Just For You" section actually mixes categories I care about (curtain rods, platform beds, linen) — feels less random than most retargeting blocks.
- Rewards card callout (10% back) near the bottom is relevant since I use a rewards card for every furniture purchase.

## What's weak

- Not one dollar amount or percentage on any of the four collections — I'm mid-renovation and price is the first thing I filter on, and this email makes me click through blind.
- "NOW ON SALE" red tag repeated four times with zero differentiation — no urgency, no "ends Sunday," nothing time-bound.
- The Halloween Shop banner wedged right after "Discover New Arrivals" feels like whiplash — I'm shopping for grown-up furniture, not porch skeletons.
- Legal fine print block at the bottom is dense and generic — no swatch/sample program mention, nothing about white-glove delivery timing which matters a lot for furniture this size.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put actual sale percentages or "from $X" pricing directly under each collection name — that's the single biggest lift here, since the headline promises 60% off but the body never delivers a number.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Sale ends Sunday 9/27") near the top banner — right now nothing signals urgency beyond the word "sale."
- 3. Move the Halloween Shop block to its own send or push it further down — it undercuts the "serious furniture sale" tone Aubrey/Toscana/Livingston are building.
- 4. Add shipping/delivery window info for furniture specifically — that's the #1 thing that stalls me on big-ticket purchases.

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Warehouse Sale" Email

**1. Technical Summary**
Email uses standard multi-client EDM markup with legacy `mso`/VML fallbacks and three separate tracking/pixel systems; it lacks required unsubscribe headers and has widespread missing alt attributes across ~40 images.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 49 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...`, `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` params) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — link destinations are unverified and should be manually validated before send.
- Multiple redundant open-tracking pixels present: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`, and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/5003d64d-...`. The `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel is served over **http://**, not https.
- Recipient email address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is exposed in cleartext in two URLs: the `coreemail.html?email=...` link and the `sv_sveme=` open-tracking pixel query param. This is a PII-in-URL exposure — these values will be logged by any intermediary proxy/CDN.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — expected for email but confirm intentional if this markup is ever reused on a web-hosted version.
- 40 `<img>` tags missing `alt` text (full list in QA output), spanning hero banners (`F26_Top_WarehouseSale2.jpg`), product shots (`remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, etc.), and footer slice images (`slices_01–18.jpg`). Screen reader users get no fallback content if images fail to load.
- Two images load over **http://** rather than https: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed-content policies in some mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) may block or flag these.
- Stray `<style>` block contains raw PHP-style `header()` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) as literal CSS text — these are inert (harmless as CSS, will just be ignored/invalid), but indicate a copy-paste error from server-side code into the client stylesheet.
- Media queries reference two different breakpoints (`min-width: 700px` and `min-width: 650px`) with overlapping `img {max-width}` rules — the 650px rule's `max-width:100%!important` will override the 700px rule's `max-width:650px` on viewports ≥700px, likely unintentional cascade conflict.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%NAME%%`) found in the truncated source.
- Personalization is implemented via tracking-pixel query params (`sv_sveme=`, `email=` in `coreemail.html`) rather than visible on-page tokens — no rendering risk from broken tokens, but see PII exposure note in §2.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules; may be present in full headers but not captured by the relay, or genuinely absent.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — without this, one-click unsubscribe cannot function even if `List-Unsubscribe` exists.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture.
- Unsubscribe/footer link mechanism not visible in the truncated HTML source provided — cannot confirm a working footer unsubscribe link exists in-body; recommend checking the untruncated footer.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
