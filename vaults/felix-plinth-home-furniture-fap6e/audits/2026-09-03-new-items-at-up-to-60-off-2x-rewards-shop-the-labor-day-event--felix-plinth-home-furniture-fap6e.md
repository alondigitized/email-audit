# New items at up to 60% off & 2x Rewards. Shop The Labor Day Event→
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Kids email, third or fourth one from this family this week according to my own inbox log, and it's the exact same "New Items Added! Up to 60% off" hero I already reviewed twice today from the "kitchen brands" send and the proper Crate & Barrel one. Nursery chairs, baby bedding, a Nuna car seat, a "Busy Baby" activity chair — I don't have kids, I don't have a nursery, I'm renovating two adult houses. This isn't a miss on execution, it's a miss on who's in the room.
- The "TheDesignDesk" $500 sweepstakes and the enter-for-appointment banner is actually the one thing that would be relevant to me regardless of category — free design consultation while I'm juggling two renovations is a real hook — but it's buried two-thirds down the page under a wall of crib furniture and baby carriers. Article's Labor Day email put its number at the top with zero scrolling; this one makes me scroll past six rows of nursery gear to find anything I'd actually click.
- Not opening again, definitely not forwarding — my Pinterest-adjacent folder is for lamps and consoles, not convertible cribs. Marking read and moving on.

## What's working

- The "Up to 60% off" hero banner is unmissable, bold red star badge, no ambiguity on the discount.
- Double Rewards banner (5-10% back in Reward Dollars on the Crate Card) is a real loyalty mechanic and I do use my highest-rewards card for purchases, so that's the kind of math I actually track.
- The Design Desk / $500 sweepstakes section is genuinely on-brand for someone renovating two homes — free designer help is the one thing here I'd consider.
- Layout is clean, no render bugs, no overlapping text — better production quality than the Wayfair cushion email that buried its offer under pergola clutter.

## What's weak

- The entire middle of this email — kids furniture, baby bedding, nursery chairs, "Busy Baby" activity chair, Nuna car seats — is aimed at parents of infants, not a 36-year-old dual-house renovator.
- I've now seen this identical "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" hero from Crate & Kids and Crate & Barrel proper today; the C&B family is running one campaign across five sub-brands and my inbox is the casualty.
- The "we love these for you" personalized row recommends a Jenny Lind dresser and a nursery glider — if this is meant to be personalized, it got my profile completely wrong.
- The Design Desk offer, the one thing worth clicking, is stranded below six rows of baby gear instead of leading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the list — if I don't own a Crate & Kids item or haven't browsed nursery categories, don't put me in this send at all; fold me into the general Crate & Barrel or CB2 blast instead.
- 2. Move the Design Desk / $500 sweepstakes up near the hero — that's the actual crossover appeal for adult-furniture shoppers like me, not buried after nursery rugs.
- 3. Cut the redundant hero — I already got this exact banner from Crate & Barrel proper today; if you're sending both, differentiate the creative instead of copy-pasting it.
- 4. Fix "we love these for you" so it reflects actual browsing/purchase history, not a generic nursery grid.
