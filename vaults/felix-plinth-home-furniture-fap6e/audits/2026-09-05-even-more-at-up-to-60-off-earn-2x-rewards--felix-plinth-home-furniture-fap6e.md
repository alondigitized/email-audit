# 🚨 Even more at up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — subject says "even more at up to 60% off," and yep, the hero literally confirms it with "New items added!! Double Rewards + up to 50% off during The Labor Day Event." This is nursery furniture, kid rugs with stars on them, a toddler-height table with a green step stool. I have zero kids and zero plans in that direction, so this is a flat-out swing and a miss for a 36-year-old dude renovating two houses' worth of adult living rooms and primary bedrooms.
- This is also, by my count, at least the ninth Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox this week — real Crate & Barrel, Crate & Kids, CB2, all blasting "up to 60% off" and "Double Rewards" on repeat. Wayfair at least varies its subject lines (even if "🔍 FURNITURE CUSHIONS 🔍" was its own kind of chaos), and Article's "Up to 40% off ends Monday" read cleaner and more urgent than anything in this whole C&B family tree. This one's an instant archive, no click, no forward — unless I know someone actually furnishing a nursery, in which case maybe I forward it to them instead of engaging myself.

## What's working

- The "Double Rewards" + "earn 10% 20% back in Reward Dollars" beat is at least consistent with the real Crate & Barrel sends I've been tracking, so the loyalty math isn't invented for this line.
- Photography is clean and well-lit — the striped nursery, the star rug, the little desk vignette all look like real staged rooms, not stock junk.
- "enter for a chance to win $500" and the shared card at the bottom ("next the card you love just got better") at least tries to bridge back to the adult Crate & Barrel brand.

## What's weak

- Wrong audience, full stop — cribs, kid step stools, star rugs, a toddler at a play table. Nothing here touches primary-bedroom or second-home renovation, which is the only thing I'm actually shopping for right now.
- This is the same "up to 60% off + Double Rewards" language I've now seen from real Crate & Barrel twice today and CB2 this week — total brand fatigue, and Crate & Kids piling on makes it worse, not better.
- The email is enormous — I counted something like nine separate "Shop Now" modules before hitting the footer. Pottery Barn's sixth-email-this-week fatigue was bad; this scrolls even longer with less relevance per inch.
- "we love these for you" personalization module shows nursery furniture and kid gear — there's no chance my browsing history actually generated that, so the "for you" framing rings hollow.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress this send entirely for subscribers with no kids-category browsing or purchase history — that single segmentation fix solves 90% of the problem, this shouldn't have hit my inbox at all.
- 2. Cut the email length by half. Nine "Shop Now" CTAs stacked vertically is Wayfair-level scroll fatigue, and Wayfair scored a 3 for exactly this kind of sprawl.
- 3. If Crate & Kids must mail non-parent subscribers, lead with the adult-facing crossover content (the $500 sweepstakes, the credit card) instead of burying it at the bottom under eight kid-furniture blocks.
- 4. Drop "Double Rewards" as a headline beat here — it's been the exact same hook in every C&B-family email I've read this week, it's lost all urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New nursery & kids styles just added — up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Double Rewards ends soon: shop kids' furniture sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, rugs & playroom furniture — free shipping on bedding`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Enter for a chance to win $500 toward your nursery`
