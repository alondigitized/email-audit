# NEW finishes for pieces you love
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-five. That's the Pottery Barn counter now. And I want to be honest: the June 17 wave — three emails in a single day, capped by "Shop the Summer Sale" — had me mentally writing off the whole brand folder until further notice. So this one had to earn its keep. Surprisingly, it mostly does. New finishes on a collection I've actually considered for the second home's primary bedroom is legitimately useful information. The Sausalito Collection is a multi-room line — bedroom, dining, storage — and if they're expanding the palette, that changes my shortlist calculus. The lifestyle photography is solid, the hierarchy is clean, the "White Rustic" / "Natural White" / "Sandrift White" language gives me enough to work with.
- But here's the problem: there's no discount, no deadline, and no reason to act today versus waiting for the next sale email (which, at PB's current cadence, is coming in approximately 36 hours). A new-finish announcement with zero price incentive is a "save for later" click, not a "buy now" click. I pined it mentally and kept scrolling.
- I'd mark this read and save to the folder. I would not forward it. I might circle back when I'm standing in a PB store this weekend.

## What's working

- **The Sausalito Collection is a real collection.** It spans bedroom, dining, and living — that's relevant when you're furnishing multiple rooms in parallel. PB correctly led with the breadth.
- **Finish names are visible and descriptive.** "Sandrift White brightens the space" is the kind of copy I actually use when talking to a contractor about paint anchors. It does the job.
- **Layout is clean and uncluttered.** No render issues, no broken images, no overlapping text — this is PB doing what PB usually does well visually. After Wayfair's chaotic outdoor-rug repeat sends, this felt calm.
- **"Recommended just for you" module at the bottom.** I know it's algorithmic, but it signals PB knows my browse history, which is more than Wayfair's generic rug blasts manage.

## What's weak

- **No offer.** CB2 dropped their "Floor Sample Sale" email the day after this one — real prices, real urgency, real reason to click. This is a brochure, not a commercial.
- **"Pieces you love" is presumptuous.** I haven't bought Sausalito yet. PB is assuming familiarity I haven't confirmed. CB2's "Now & Future Classics on Sale" at least acknowledged it was making a pitch, not assuming a relationship.
- **Volume fatigue is real.** Three emails the day before, now a fourth the day after — the cadence is actively training me to batch-skim the folder rather than engage. The June 17 "Shop the Summer Sale" (email #24) cannibalized whatever goodwill this one might have earned.
- **No room dimensions or finish swatch depth.** When I'm actually shopping for a bedroom dresser or dining table, I want to see actual dimensions and something resembling a finish comparison chip — not aspirational room styling. Article's home tour emails at least contextualizes scale. This doesn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a single price anchor or limited-finish launch offer.** Even "Free shipping through Sunday on new Sausalito finishes" gives me a reason to act today. Right now there's nothing tethering the decision to a timeline.
- 2. **Show a side-by-side finish comparison.** Two swatches, four pieces, three finishes. That's the one image I'd actually screenshot and send to my interior designer. Currently missing.
- 3. **Earn the "you love" line.** If this is going to customers who have Sausalito items in their cart or purchase history, say so — "You browsed Sausalito — it just got three new finishes." Otherwise, pull the assumption entirely.
- 4. **Consolidate the cadence.** If June 17 had three sends, June 18 should have had zero or one. The email itself isn't wrong; the timing is.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (will be blocked by Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail):**

| Image | src |
|---|---|

**Missing alt text — 40+ images:** Every content image in the Sausalito product grid (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_15.jpg`), all footer slices, both bottom banner images, all product recommendation images, and both tracking pixels have empty or absent `alt` attributes. With images off (the default in many corporate email clients), the email renders as a blank page with no text fallback. WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1 non-conformant.

**PHP `header()` calls in `<style>` block:**
```html
<style>
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache...");
```
Server-side PHP code was pasted raw into a CSS `<style>` block. This is a template authoring error — it is inert in the rendered email but signals a broken build/QA process where template fragments are assembled without validation.

**`<title>` is empty:** `<title></title>` — causes poor rendering in some preview panes and screen readers.
