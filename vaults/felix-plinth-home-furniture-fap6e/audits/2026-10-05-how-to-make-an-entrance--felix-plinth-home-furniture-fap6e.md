## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`), plus 11 content images with no alt text and 11 insecure third-party `http://` pixels. The QA run passed 43% of checks. Several compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **71 tracking links were not probed.** They go through click-redirect domains, so their final destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** These load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1`
  - Mail clients may block or rewrite them, and the cookie-sync calls will fail silently. They also expose requests in cleartext.
- **Many third-party calls.** The email makes 11 Dotomi (Epsilon) calls in addition to the first-party pixels `mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/3c45ef75-...`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 content images.** All are hosted on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `e2e2388e-...jpg`, `fe88f89a-...jpg`, `972aa036-...jpg`, `3c723f49-...jpg`, `67f2e534-...jpg`
  - `8d09e9e5-...jpg`, `2cd4907d-...jpg`, `9360acdf-...jpg`, `66a75060-...jpg`, `badb0ec1-...jpg`
  - `8e957a75-...gif`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, there is no text fallback.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** The Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/...` have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. The CSS also sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`, which blocks user text scaling in iOS Mail.

- **Font URLs.** `@font-face` rules load fonts from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, a different domain. Some `cb2.com//Styles/...` URLs have a double slash, which may fail on strict servers. I couldn't confirm that either one breaks. Most clients ignore web fonts anyway.
