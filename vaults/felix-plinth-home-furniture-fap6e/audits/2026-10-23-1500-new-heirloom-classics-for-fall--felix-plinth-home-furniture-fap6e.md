# 1500+ new heirloom classics for fall →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Crate & Barrel send with a reason to exist. After three Kids sends today, all the same "free personalization on Nod & Barrel Chairs" promo, a dark-green dining room with "up to 30% off" for Thanksgiving feels like a different brand. The moody English-heritage look is the stuff I save to my Pinterest folder. A wood-paneled room, a curved accent chair and a sage-and-brass kitchen are all things I could actually use in one of my two renovations.
- It's a very long scroll, though. Hero, dining, accent chairs, Sunday roasts, kitchen, "more newness," wallpaper, a tabletop-and-rug sale, a Design Desk sweepstakes, and a "we love these for you" grid. That's about nine pitches in one email, and the one number I care about (30% off) only shows up in the hero. I'd click through to the dining tables and probably the accent chairs. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to the folder and move on.

## What's working

- The hero says "up to 30% off" on the Thanksgiving dining event, and it sits right above a single "Shop Now" button. I know the deal immediately.
- The photography is dark, warm and wood-heavy, and it matches how I'm styling both houses. It also looks more grown-up than the pastel Kids creative from earlier today.
- "Estate-worthy accent chairs" and "the modern English-inspired kitchen" give each block a clear point of view. They read like a room I could copy, not a product dump.
- The Design Desk block offers a free appointment and a chance to win $5,000. I'd actually book the appointment, since I'm furnishing two places.
- The product captions under the images have names, so I can see which pieces are which.

## What's weak

- There are too many modules and too many "Shop Now" buttons. The 30% offer is gone by the second screen, and I can't tell if it applies to the chairs or the Sunday-roast tableware.
- Most of the blocks have no price. The only number I see outside the hero is "starting at $29.95" on a vague "more newness" grid, which is the least interesting block.
- "Up to 20% off" on accent chairs, tables and rugs is tucked in the middle, and it contradicts the 30% hero. That reads like fine print.
- The email has no shipping or lead-time info for furniture, no swatch or sample mention, and no rewards math. For a large dining table or sofa, those are the first things I check.
- The Design Desk block and the app footer ("including your chance at $500") pile sweepstakes on top of a sale email. It's noisy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to three modules: the dining event, accent chairs, and the Design Desk. Put the sale terms and end date in one strip under the hero.
- 2. Put "ships by" or "in stock" tags on the furniture. For tables and chairs that's what turns a click into a cart.
- 3. Show prices or "from $X" on the hero products, and drop the generic "more newness" grid.
- 4. Add a swatch or fabric-sample link under the accent chairs.
- 5. Add a cadence guard. Don't send Kids, C&B and CB2 on the same day. CB2's "Now & Future Classics on Sale" arrived the same week as the five other Crate & Barrel emails I'd already read.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Heirloom-look dining + accent chairs, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Thanksgiving-ready tables, English-inspired kitchens, and a free design appointment.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 30% off dining through the weekend. Plus 1,500 new pieces for fall.`

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "1500+ new heirloom classics for fall →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The ESP-built XHTML Transitional template (Movable Ink personalization) has no hard failures, but the QA run found 3 warnings and about 46 images missing `alt` text. Several header and tracking checks can't be confirmed because the HTML source was truncated and the AgentMail relay may strip headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **79 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **Five repeated LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` differ only in `n`. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string that looks like a hashed identifier.
- **Identifier in image URLs.** The same hash prefix (`mi_u=dbe8da718e29…`) appears on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` requests.
- **Pixels have no alt text.** The `rlcdn` and `o.gif` pixels, plus `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/cfb245fd-…`, are all flagged.
- **No broken links were confirmed.**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on about 46 images (QA output appears truncated).**
  - 31 are content images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `56be7a63-…jpg` and several `.gif`.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (Movable Ink dynamic content).
  - `971aa10f-…jpg` is also missing it.
  - If these images carry the offers or products, an image-blocked or screen-reader user gets no content. Pixels should use `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Text-resize overrides.** `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override iOS text sizing. The global `*{line-height:100%}` rule can also clip enlarged text.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate and conflicting declarations.**
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv Content-Type`.
  - Three redundant `format-detection` metas.
  - Conflicting `img{max-width}` rules at `min-width:768px` (600px) and `min-width:640px` (640px). At 768px and up, both match and the later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule is dead.
- **Leftover template text.** The source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Not verifiable.** The source was truncated, so `role="presentation"` on layout tables, the preheader and the Outlook VML fallbacks can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. Movable Ink dynamic images (`/p/rp/`) are present, but I can't confirm they rendered without the full HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (RFC 8058 one-click, QA WARN).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.
- **These three could be relay artifacts.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Check them against the raw message before treating them as sender defects. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this is worth confirming.
- **Not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address aren't in the truncated source.
- **Sending domain.** `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is generally aligned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 79 links were tracking redirects that weren't resolved, and the source shown is truncated, so I can't confirm UTM parameters or whether destinations match the "1500+ new … for fall" claim. This is a gap in the audit, not a confirmed defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 31 content images and the 7 Movable Ink images. Use `alt=""` for the `rlcdn`, `o.gif` and `dv.` tracking pixels.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message headers, not the relay copy.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `text-size-adjust:none` from `html` and `*`.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and remove the duplicate metas and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
5. Consolidate the conflicting `img{max-width}` media queries.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and follow the 79 redirect chains to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
7. Review whether the five LiveRamp pixels carrying a hashed email identifier fit the sender's privacy and consent posture.
