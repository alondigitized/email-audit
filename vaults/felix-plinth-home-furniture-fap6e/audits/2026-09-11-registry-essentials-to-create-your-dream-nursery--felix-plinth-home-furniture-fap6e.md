# Registry essentials to create your dream nursery →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again — I lost count somewhere around send number six this week from the Crate & Barrel family, and I don't have a kid, a kid on the way, or a nursery, so "your dream nursery starts here" is just noise scrolling past on my phone. This one's actually the most sprawling of the bunch — registry tool, design consultation, assembly service, brand grid (Nuna, UppaBaby, Stokke, Doona, Bugaboo, all present and accounted for like the last one), a sweepstakes, a credit card pitch, and an app download all crammed into one endless scroll. It's well-produced — the moody purple nursery shot and the herringbone-tile playroom are genuinely nice rooms — but production value doesn't fix targeting.
- I'm not clicking, not forwarding, not even reading past the first screen. This is straight to archived. If I had a friend who was actually pregnant I might forward it for the registry angle, but that's a "maybe someone else" pass, not a "this is for me" open.

## What's working

- The nursery hero room (moody plum walls, wood crib) is a genuinely aspirational shot — good styling, not stock-photo stiff.
- "let us assemble it all" is a real pain point call-out — furniture assembly is the one thing that actually makes me pause on baby/furniture emails, brand-agnostic.
- The brand logo strip (Nuna, UppaBaby, Stokke, Doona, Bugaboo, Nanit, Mushie) signals real curation, not white-labeled junk — same move CB2 makes when they want to feel credible.
- "15% off everything else" is a concrete, findable number, which beats Wayfair's vague "This Week's Best Deals" framing from their pergola send.

## What's weak

- I'm not a parent. This is the fifth-plus Crate & Kids nursery/registry email this week and it's zero-for-zero on relevance to me — worse than the "TV that blends in" Samsung/Frame send, which at least brushed my actual living-room shopping.
- The email is exhaustingly long — registry, design desk, assembly, brands, discount, sweepstakes, playroom, credit card, app download. Nothing is prioritized; it reads like every merchandising team got a slot.
- No single clear CTA — "Create My Registry," "Book a Free Appointment," "Shop Now," "Shop All Baby," "See All Perks," "Enter to Win," "Learn More," "Download Now" — eight-plus buttons competing for the same thumb.
- The credit card pitch buried near the bottom ("now the card you love just got better") feels bolted on, disconnected from nursery shopping entirely.
- $5,000 sweepstakes copy is small and crammed next to a playroom photo — easy to scroll right past even if I cared.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household data — if I've never clicked a Crate & Kids link or bought baby gear, suppress this send entirely and put me back in general Crate & Barrel/CB2 flow. That's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. Cut this down to one job per email. A registry-building email should stop at registry + brand list + maybe assembly. Split design services, sweepstakes, and the credit card into separate sends.
- 3. Pick one hero CTA and repeat it, not eight different buttons — "Create My Registry" should be the only ask above the fold.
- 4. Move the 15% off higher — it's currently buried mid-scroll after two soft-sell sections.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Build your registry with Nuna, Stokke & UppaBaby — 15% off everything else`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free nursery design help + 15% off your registry`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book a free design appointment, then save 15% on everything not on your list.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Assembly included. Top baby brands in one place. Registry perks inside.`
