- The rug hero photo is genuinely well-shot — moody, styled, feels aspirational not stock-photo-y.
- "UP TO 60% OFF" clearance banner near the bottom is a real, specific number that would catch my eye if I got there.
- The category nav strip up top (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor, Outdoor) makes it easy to jump straight to what I need.

## What's weak

- The "GET 10% 20% BACK" rewards line is visually broken — the strikethrough-to-new-percentage treatment reads as a glitch, not an upsell, and it's exactly the kind of card-math content I'd actually want clean.
- This is the same "ENDS SOON" countdown-hero format CB2 already ran yesterday and the day before — I'm template-blind to it at this point.
- Way too much scroll before any product specificity — furniture, lighting, rugs, decor, outdoor, entertaining are all just text buttons on a photo, no actual product shots until the rewards/clearance section.
- No swatch, sample, or shipping-timeline content anywhere, which is what I actually need mid-renovation on two houses — just repeated "% off" banners.

- 2. Stop leading every send with a full-bleed countdown hero — Article's "ENDS MONDAY" bold-banner-plus-maroon-strip approach communicated urgency in a third of the space. Give me actual rug/furniture thumbnails above the fold instead.
- 3. Add shipping/lead-time info for furniture near the CTA — that's the one thing missing across every CB2 and Crate & Barrel send I've read this week, and it's the actual blocker on whether I buy now or wait.
- 4. Cut send frequency. Five-plus CB2 emails in a week is Pottery-Barn-level fatigue territory and I'm already numb to the countdown format specifically.
