# Introducing our first-ever wallcovering collection
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the Crate & Barrel email I actually wanted. Wallcoverings are a live question across both my houses, and "first-ever collection" reads like news. The moody botanical and toile hero is the best creative I've seen from them this week. I only skimmed it at thumbnail size on my phone, and there's a lot of scroll.
- I'm still in Crate-family fatigue. This is at least the sixth send since yesterday, after the 10/07 furniture-on-sale and "NEW wallcoverings: Prints, patterns & murals" emails. That one covered the same launch, so "introducing" is a stretch. The banner at the very top shouts "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" over a wallpaper story, and that split focus bugs me.
- Would I click? Yes, on the wallcovering shop link, because I'm sampling patterns this week. Would I forward it? Maybe to my partner with a "look at the navy toile" note. It's a save-to-the-inspiration-folder email, not a buy-now one.

## What's working

- The hero is a moody floral wallcovering behind a navy chair and a dark bookcase. It shows the paper in a real room, which is how I shop.
- The "textures / prints / patterns" tiles let me pick my lane fast, and each tile has its own "Shop Now".
- The "before" and after pairing shows the room change, which sells the idea better than a swatch grid would.
- The Crate & Barrel Kids wallcovering block with the nursery shot is a smart cross-sell, since I get both brands anyway.
- The TheDesignDesk "free appointment" block gives a renovator a real next step. It's the showroom-style touch I look for.

## What's weak

- The 20% off sofas banner at the top competes with the launch story. It shows up again lower down, and the repeat feels like filler.
- Every button is a generic "Shop Now". I can't tell what I'm shopping without looking at the photo above it.
- There's no sample or swatch program, no peel-and-stick vs. traditional guidance, and no price. For wallpaper, I need those to commit.
- The email is very long. The "we love these for you" tile row of vases and bowls is the same decor every brand pushes. I saw the same lamp-and-vase carousel energy from Pottery Barn this week.
- No deadline on any of it, so there's no reason to act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with wallpaper only, and move the sofa offer to a thin strip at the bottom. One story per send.
- 2. Put sample pricing up top, something like "Order a free swatch" with a visible button.
- 3. Replace the "Shop Now" buttons with specific ones: "Shop botanical prints," "Shop grasscloth textures."
- 4. Cut the email by a third. Keep the three categories, TheDesignDesk, and the Kids block, and drop the product carousel.
- 5. Add a reason to act now, like a launch-week price or a sample-shipping cutoff.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Our first wallpaper is here: 3 ways to try it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wallpaper, finally: shop the new collection`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free swatches, designer prints, and a free pro to help you pick.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Textures, florals and murals, plus 20% off sofas this week.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Introducing our first-ever wallcovering collection" (Crate & Barrel)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with Outlook (MSO) fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed defect is missing alt text on about 40 images. The QA header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay and don't confirm a real defect. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination health, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not passed.
- Tracking pixels and beacons are present and load from several third-party and first-party hosts:
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with an identical URL.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` is the open pixel.
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4d0c37ad-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another beacon.
  - `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are 5 LiveRamp pixels, a third-party identity-sync domain.
- The repeated identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel adds 6 redundant requests. The 5 LiveRamp pixels differ only by `n`, so this is a deliberate multi-sync.
- One hero asset is hosted on a different CDN: `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026_0716_CB_BrandAnthemSecondary_01.gif`. Its path says 2026/07 (July 16), so it is a reused asset on a different host from the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` images. An asset-host outage or a path change would break only that image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA:** about 27 content images have no alt, such as `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/3654ac4f-….jpg` and the two `.gif` files `a42b3fc0-…` and `d14cdabb-…`.
  - With images off, or in a screen reader, these read as filenames or nothing.
  - Another 13 are tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""`.
  - Because the source is truncated, I can't tell whether any of these have `alt=""` that the check mis-flagged. The check reports them as missing.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Text resizing:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS text scaling. The global `*{line-height:100%}` can also clip multi-line text.
- **Media query cascade bug:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template residue:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Markup nits:**
  - There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).
  - The `format-detection` meta tag is declared three times.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML.
  - The `<meta charset="utf-8">` tag is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype.
- `lang="en"` is present on `<html>`, which is correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` / `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` values in the pixel URLs are per-recipient identifiers filled in by the sender's platform.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says this may be caused by the AgentMail relay, so it is **unconfirmed**. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules would be violated. Check the raw message headers at the source mailbox.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. Nothing here shows a failure.
- The footer, the unsubscribe link and the physical postal address fall in the truncated section of the HTML. I can't confirm or deny them.
- The `From:` address is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a branded subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Can't be verified. All 74 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't show the final URLs. That means UTM parameters, landing-page match to the "wallcovering collection" subject, and redirect depth are all unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive alt text to every content image (about 27).** Hero and product tiles should describe the item, and GIFs should describe the key frame or message. This is the highest-priority fix.
2. **Add `alt=""` to all 13 tracking pixels.** Consolidate the 6 duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests into one.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.** Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`, and scope `line-height:100%` away from the `*` selector.
4. **Fix the image `max-width` media query order or ranges** so the 600px and 640px rules don't overlap.
5. **Clean up the template:** delete the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment, de-duplicate the meta tags, and escape `&` in `<title>`.
6. **Re-audit with the raw `.eml`.** That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, give the full HTML including the footer, and let the redirect chains and UTMs be resolved.
7. **Re-run link probing with redirect following** for a sample of the 74 skipped links, to check landing-page health and UTM presence.
