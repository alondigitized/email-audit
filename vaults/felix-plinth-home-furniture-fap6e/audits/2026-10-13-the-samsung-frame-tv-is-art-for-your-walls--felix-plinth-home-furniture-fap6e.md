# The Samsung Frame TV is art for your walls  →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Crate & Barrel email today, and the first one where the main sender and my project line up. But the subject is about a TV, and the best part of the email isn't. The real offer is the small green banner at the very top: "ends tomorrow: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals." I'm doing sofas for two houses, so that's what I care about. The Samsung Frame is the big picture underneath it. A TV is a living-room purchase, but it isn't the one I'm shopping for.
- It's a decent send once you're inside it, and a better sofa email than the Halloween shop or the Nuna clothing sends from earlier. It still feels like four emails stacked together: TV, living room categories, design service, and a grab-bag grid. I'd skim it and click "Shop Sofas." I wouldn't forward it. Earlier today the "bestseller edit: our 4 most-loved sofas" send did the sofa job more cleanly and got a 7.

## What's working

- The "ends tomorrow" banner gives me a real deadline on a category I'm actively buying: 20% off sofas and sectionals.
- The Samsung Frame lifestyle shot, with art on the screen above a carved media console, is the kind of image I'd save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder.
- The "quality-crafted living room furniture" grid of sofas, media consoles, accent chairs, coffee tables, console tables and sleeper sofas is clean and shoppable. It's closer to my project than anything else in the email.
- The Design Desk block ("refresh your home with free design help… no purchase necessary") is a low-pressure offer. It's close to what Pottery Barn Design Services did with "RSVP for FREE design services."
- The layout renders cleanly. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with the TV and buries the 20% sofa deadline. The best hook isn't in the subject line at all.
- The hero banner is small, lowercase text on dark green. The giant TV image takes over the hierarchy, so the offer reads as an afterthought.
- The Design Desk appears twice, once mid-email and once at the bottom. The app download block repeats the same pitch a third time.
- The "we love these for you" grid is an espresso machine, a toaster oven, stainless pots, a wine glass and cookware. None of that is living room, so it doesn't feel personalized. The prices are also tiny and hard to read on my phone.
- The Design Rewards card block says "10% back in Reward Dollars." I already put everything on my highest-rewards card, so this doesn't change my math. There's also nothing on furniture shipping times, fabric swatches or showroom availability, which I'd want before clicking buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject and hero with the sofa deadline, which is the real offer. Put the Samsung Frame lower down, or send it as its own email.
- 2. Make the hero offer big, high contrast and bold, with a single "Shop Sofas & Sectionals" button. Right now it's a quiet banner above a TV photo.
- 3. Show the Design Desk once, not three times. Cut the app block or move it to the footer.
- 4. Replace the kitchen and glassware grid with sofas I've browsed, with readable prices. Add fabric swatch, delivery timing and showroom info, since those drive furniture decisions.
- 5. Show the cadence honestly. Six sends in one day (Halloween, Nuna, bestseller sofas, this one) trains me to ignore the brand. Consolidate them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tomorrow: 20% off sofas & sectionals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your living room, sorted: sofas + the Frame TV`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free design help from The Design Desk, no purchase needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bestselling sectionals, media consoles and a TV that doubles as art.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "The Samsung Frame TV is art for your walls →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink-driven template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) with sound responsive scaffolding. The automated QA pass rate was 57%, driven mostly by about 32 images with no `alt` attribute. The HTML was truncated, so footer, link, and UTM checks are only partly verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 65 tracking/click-redirect links, so destination status, redirect chains, and final landing URLs are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` calls come from LiveRamp, not the sender's ESP or Movable Ink. They add external requests, which is a deliverability and privacy consideration. The five identical calls differ only in `n`.
- **Per-recipient IDs in URLs:** `mi_u=56aaa278…` appears in every `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URL and in the `dv.` open-pixel URL (`?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`). Forwarding the email exposes that identifier. This is standard for the platform, so it is informational only.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA), 32 images:**
  - 17 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, e.g. `2c94bb7d-…jpg`, `80ff2d37-…jpg`, `5821f69c-…gif`, plus `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - 7 Movable Ink images, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - 1 more image, `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, the content is unusable or unlabeled. Any of these that carry the headline, product, or CTA need descriptive alt text.
  - The other 7 are tracking pixels (`rlcdn` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.…/o/71a91ac9-…`). These should carry `alt=""`, so the missing attribute is low severity.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is an accessibility issue.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is set in the `*` rule and in `html` and `body`. It disables user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Narrow mobile breakpoint:** The mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px` only. Devices between 461px and 639px get neither the mobile nor the desktop `img{max-width}` rules.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - The `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` ampersand is unescaped under an XHTML 1.0 doctype, so it should be `&amp;`.
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv Content-Type` declarations.
  - Redundant and repeated `format-detection` metas (`telephone`, `address`, `date` declared multiple times).
  - A template placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, shipped in production.
  - None of these breaks rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The remaining HTML was truncated, so this covers the visible portion only.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping it, so it is unconfirmed. If the header is truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured,** so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with aligned sending, but this isn't verified.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** These are in the footer, which was cut off by truncation, so they can't be assessed. Do not treat them as passing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. UTM parameters and landing-page URLs aren't visible in the truncated source, and the 65 tracking links were not resolved. Resolving the redirect chains would be needed to confirm landing alignment and UTM retention.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~25 content and Movable Ink images, starting with the hero and CTA images. Set `alt=""` on the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` so users can zoom and resize text.
3. Re-run QA against the raw MIME from the sending ESP, not the relay. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. If `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are really missing, add them.
4. Resolve the 65 tracked links with a redirect-following probe to check destination status codes and UTM retention.
5. Clean up the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, remove duplicate metas and the `IMPUT HERE` comment, and add a breakpoint covering 461–639px.
6. Review whether the five LiveRamp `rlcdn` pixels are needed, and consolidate them if not.
