# ON NOW! Double Rewards + up to 60% off The Labor Day Event!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I've now gotten this exact Labor Day push from Crate & Barrel twice in two days — this is the same "Double Rewards + up to 60% off" hero I flagged yesterday, just re-sent. My gut reaction scrolling through: it's a genuinely dense, well-organized send — living room, dining, lighting, rugs, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining, Crate&Kids, all with real prices and real percentages stacked under clean product photography. Nothing looks broken, nothing looks like filler.
- But this is where the fatigue sets in. I'm renovating two houses at once, so in theory this whole email is "my category" — sofas, rugs, chandeliers, outdoor furniture, all of it is relevant to what I'm actually shopping for right now. The problem is I already opened basically this same email yesterday. Crate & Barrel is starting to feel like Wayfair's clearance blast or CB2's "so much is on sale" cadence — good creative, repeated one day too often.
- I'd click into the sofas/sectionals section for the primary or the rugs section for the second home, since those are real needs, not impulse buys. I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's not distinctive enough to share, it's just a solid retail email doing its job. Compare that to Crate & Barrel's own "$1000 gift card" send from a couple days back — that one made me stop scrolling because it was a genuine hook tied to something I'd actually do (review a product I bought). This one is just... comprehensive.

## What's working

- The "Double Rewards + up to 60% off" hero is unambiguous — I know exactly what the deal is in one glance, no hunting.
- Every section pairs a lifestyle photo with a specific percentage and a "Shop Now" button — sofas/sectionals, furniture, rugs, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining. No vague "explore the collection" filler.
- The rewards math ("earn 10%-20% back in Reward Dollars") is spelled out right under the hero, which matters to me since I buy on the highest-rewards card and actually track this stuff.
- Crate&Kids and the Le Creuset $5000 sweepstakes at the bottom give this real breadth without feeling like a bait-and-switch — everything ties back to the same sale.

## What's weak

- This is essentially a repeat of yesterday's "ON NOW! Double Rewards" send — same hero copy, same structure. That's the fourth-plus Crate & Barrel/CB2-family email I've gotten this week and my brain is starting to do the C&B snooze I already flagged with CB2.
- It's a long scroll — living room, dining, lighting, rugs, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining, kids, sweepstakes — by the time I hit Le Creuset at the bottom I've lost the thread of what the actual offer was.
- No swatch/sample or fabric program callout anywhere, which is exactly what I need mid-renovation when I'm deciding on a sectional fabric or rug material sight-unseen.
- Two different price/reward tiers get shown across sections (up to 40% here, up to 60% there) without a single unifying banner reminding me which category hits which discount — makes we have to re-read each block.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the near-duplicate send.** If this is functionally the same email as yesterday's, collapse it — send one strong version with a day-2 "reminder" subject instead of a full re-blast. That's the single biggest lever here; repetition is costing more goodwill than the creative is earning.
- 2. **Lead with a personalized module for renovators** — a "shop by room" jump-nav at the top (Living / Dining / Outdoor / Kitchen) so I can skip straight to what I need instead of scrolling past sections I don't.
- 3. **Add a swatch/sample CTA** near the sofa and rug sections — "order a fabric swatch" is the single most useful thing C&B could add for someone furnishing two houses without seeing anything in person.
- 4. **Trim the tail.** Crate&Kids, kitchen ship-free deals, Ninja blender, and the Le Creuset sweepstakes could be a separate send — bolting all of it onto one Labor Day email dilutes the urgency of the main offer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop by room: living, dining, outdoor, kitchen — all in the Labor Day Event`
