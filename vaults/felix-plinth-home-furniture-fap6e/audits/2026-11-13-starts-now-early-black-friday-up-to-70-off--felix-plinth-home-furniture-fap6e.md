# STARTS NOW 📣 Early Black Friday UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday email I expected, and it's the first Pottery Barn send in two weeks where the hero is the offer. The Fall Sale strip that sat on top of the 11/11 Grinch send and the 11/12 gift guide is gone. In its place is a maroon "NEW DEALS ADDED / EARLY BLACK FRIDAY / up to 70% off" block with one Shop Now button. I could tell what this was in about a second.
- Below that it's an eight-tile grid with real was/now prices: the Toscana dining table at $1,599 (was $1,999) and the Remmy swivel at $849 (was $1,049). That's what I want when I'm furnishing two houses. But I'm the 15th-email-this-week guy, and "up to 70%" appears on five of the eight tiles. Mixed in are Furniture at "up to 60%" and Sofas and Gifts at "up to 50%". The real furniture deals are the weakest ones.
- I'd click, and I'd go straight to the dining table. I'd probably save it to my Pinterest folder rather than forward it.

## What's working

- The "Early Black Friday" hero is one block and one button. It's cleaner than the Fall Sale strip from earlier this week.
- The tiles show was/now prices (Toscana table $1,999 → $1,599, Remmy swivel $1,049 → $849, Vintage Santa Quilt $349 → $279). I can do the math without clicking.
- The "SHIPS FREE" badges on bedding, pillows and tabletop answer the shipping question up front.
- The "10% Back in Rewards" block is right there in the email. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I care about that stacking.
- The Design Crew "Book an Appointment" block is a useful callback to the 11/12 sweepstakes send, and it's relevant to my two renovations.

## What's weak

- The "up to 70%" headline is carried by decor, bedding and throws. The furniture I'd actually spend money on is only "up to 60%" and "up to 50%", so the hero oversells it.
- It's long. The "Recommended Just For You" block is stockings, a curtain rod, a bed and another dining table. The Toscana table is already in the grid above, so it's the same lamp-in-three-emails problem inside one email.
- A separate "Christmas Shop up to 50% off" banner follows the 70% hero. Two different headline numbers makes it feel like a promo pile-up.
- The footer fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the "Earning Rewards" and "Affirm" legalese is unreadable on a phone. I can't tell what the sale excludes.
- There's no end date. "Starts now" gives me no reason to act today rather than wait for the real Black Friday.
- The "Just For You" tiles don't look personalized, and it's the same grid anyone would get.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the furniture deals. Wayfair's pergola email put a single category up top and I knew immediately what was on sale. Put the Toscana table and the Remmy swivel in the hero with their real prices.
- 2. Add a deadline, like "through Sunday", so "early" means something.
- 3. Cut the "Just For You" block or make it real. It should reflect what I browsed, not repeat the grid.
- 4. Pick one headline number and stick to it across hero and tiles.
- 5. Put a rewards line next to the hero, like "plus 10% back with the PB card". Crate & Barrel leads with "2x Rewards" in its sender-line emails, and that's the number my card math cares about.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dining tables up to 60% off — Early Black Friday is on`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: furniture + bedding, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Toscana table now $1,599. Most bedding and decor ships free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New deals added today, plus 10% back with the PB card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, sliced-image build on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `image.e.potterybarn.com`). It has widespread missing alt text, three insecure `http://` image requests (one carries the recipient's email address), and stray non-CSS code in the `<style>` block. Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **54 tracking links were not probed** (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`). Their destinations and status are unverified.
- **The tracked links are opaque.** The logo link uses a base64-style `qs=` token, so no destination URL or UTM parameters can be read from the source.
- **Legacy and cross-brand assets.** `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm host inside a Pottery Barn send. `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset. Both are low-severity reuse risks.
- **Three tracking pixels are present:**
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`
  - `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/685d68e9-…?mi_cid=…`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 45 images**, including:
  - the hero slices `…/1113_FRI_AM_EBF_ZigZag1_NewDealsAdded/slices_01–10.jpg`
  - product images such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`
  - the banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`
  - the 18 footer slices `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`
  - With images blocked, the offer and CTAs are unreadable. The 1×1 pixels and `spacer_0.gif` should use `alt=""`, but that is not confirmed in the QA output.
- **Non-HTTPS images** (flagged by QA as possibly blocked): `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/…/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Stray code in a `<style>` block.** The second `<style>` contains PHP-style statements: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS and can break parsing of rules that follow in strict clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>`.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`.** The outer wrapper tables and the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lack it, while others have it.
- **`<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat…">`** is stripped or ignored by many clients, including Gmail and Outlook. The font will fall back.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated source.
- **The recipient's email address is exposed in URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- The `svopn` pixel sends the address in cleartext over HTTP, which leaks PII to network observers.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unverified rather than confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the tracking subdomains.
- **The footer is outside the truncated source.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified.** The click-redirect domains were skipped and the `qs=` tokens are opaque.
- Product images point directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which is the image CDN path and not a landing page.
- No continuity defects are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the hero, product, banner and footer slices, and `alt=""` to the spacer and tracking pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` assets, especially the `svopn` pixel, to HTTPS.
3. Stop passing the plaintext email in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque or hashed ID.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
5. Add `lang="en"` and a non-empty `<title>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
7. Capture the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay to confirm compliance.
8. Resolve the redirect chains with a real click-through to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
