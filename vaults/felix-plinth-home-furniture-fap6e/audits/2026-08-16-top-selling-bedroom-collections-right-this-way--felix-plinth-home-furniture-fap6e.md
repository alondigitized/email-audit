# Top-selling bedroom collections right this way →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Crate & Kids email in two days — I'm just going to say the number now because it's become the actual headline. This is "our favorite bedroom collections" and it's Jenny Lind, Hampshire, Classic, plus nursery gliders, backpack bundles, and a "cozy summer bedding" section stapled on at the bottom. I don't have kids. No nursery, no crib, no glider I need "comfy" — I have a primary bedroom and a second home I'm actually furnishing, and this brand keeps sending me the same three kid-bed collections with different wood tones.
- The scroll is enormous — I counted eight or nine distinct "shop now" sections before it even gets to the app download pitch — and none of it is mine. It's the Twin Tween Room problem and the Beatrix problem and the kitchen-sink problem all over again, just wearing a different subject line this time.
- Not opening past the first scroll if I were doing this live, not clicking, definitely not forwarding — unless it's to whoever's running Crate & Barrel's segmentation to ask why I'm still getting the Kids feed.

## What's working

- Product photography is clean and on-brand — the Jenny Lind spindle bed and Hampshire dresser shots are genuinely nice, showroom-quality.
- Layout has a clear, repeated pattern (headline → hero shot → grid → Shop Now) so at least it's not visually chaotic, just long.
- The "GREENGUARD Gold Certified" callout under the Classic Collection is a real trust cue for parents worried about sustainability/off-gassing.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — this entire email, wall to wall, is nursery furniture, kid beds, and backpacks. Zero relevance to a guy renovating a primary and a second home.
- It's the fourth Crate & Kids send in 48 hours (after "Their room isn't complete," Beatrix, and Twin Tween Room) — no attempt to vary the pitch or acknowledge the fatigue.
- No offer anywhere — no %, no $ off, just "Shop Now" over and over. Even Pottery Barn's "Curated bedding looks" send at least said "1600+ New Arrivals."
- The scroll length is punishing on mobile — nine-plus product sections before the app-download footer is a lot to thumb through for zero personal relevance.
- "we love these for you" is an especially hollow line given none of it is for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the list. If Crate & Barrel knows I don't have kids (no crib/nursery clicks, no baby registry), suppress Crate & Kids sends entirely and route me to Crate & Barrel proper — this is a segmentation fix, not a creative fix.
- 2. Add an actual offer. Every competitor I've seen this week — CB2's "Summer Clearance," the "up to 25% off bedroom" send from this same brand family — leads with a number. This one has none.
- 3. Cut the length. Nine product sections in one email is fatigue-inducing even for someone who IS the target; pick the top 2-3 collections and link the rest to a landing page.
- 4. If this has to go to non-parents, reframe as "gift guide" or "for the design-obsessed" rather than "our favorite bedroom collections" — right now it reads as parent-only content with no off-ramp for adult buyers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The nursery collection parents keep asking for`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Solid wood, GREENGUARD Gold Certified, built to last`
