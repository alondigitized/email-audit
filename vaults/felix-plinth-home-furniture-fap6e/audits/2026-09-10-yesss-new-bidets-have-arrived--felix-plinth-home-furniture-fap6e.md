# Yesss! New BIDETS have arrived →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "new bidets have arrived" as a subject line, and then the actual hero image slot is just... blank. White space where a product shot should be. That's the first thing I notice and it's not a good look — feels like the email broke on the way into my inbox. Then I scroll and it's the same "score styles you 💕" purple-header category grid Wayfair's been running all week, except this time bidets are duct-taped onto a shelf with pergolas, privacy screens, and — I kid you not — a chicken coop. I'm renovating two bathrooms right now, so bidets are genuinely relevant to me, but burying that under a random chicken coop hero image tells me nobody curated this for someone actually shopping bathroom fixtures.
- This is the fifth Wayfair email in my recap this week and the pattern is now impossible to unsee: same purple color-blocking, same "60% OFF warehouse clearout" banner at the bottom, same eight-tile grid of unrelated categories. Compare that to Crate & Barrel's actual Double Rewards send, which at least felt like a coherent single sale — this is a junk drawer.
- Marking as read, not clicking, definitely not forwarding. The blank hero image alone would've made me bail before the chicken coop even had a chance to confuse me further.

## What's working

- Bidets do appear twice in the grid ("Bidets You'll Love" x2) — so the subject isn't a total lie, unlike the Wayfair dining-chairs email that had nothing to do with dining chairs.
- The bottom "60% OFF Warehouse Clearout" banner is at least visually clean and legible, same treatment as the Sept 8 clearout send.
- Wayfair Rewards / Flexible Financing icons at the footer are a nice touch for someone financing two renovations — Pottery Barn doesn't surface anything like that.

## What's weak

- The hero image slot is completely blank — that's a real render bug, not just a boring layout, and it's the first thing my eye hits.
- A chicken coop with "people love this chicken coop" copy has zero business being in a bidet email — total category whiplash.
- It's the same purple category-grid template I've now screenshotted from Wayfair five times this week (bedding, clearout, dining chairs, and now this) — zero visual differentiation.
- No offer or price attached to the bidets themselves — just a product tile, no %, no member price, nothing that tells me why to click now.
- Two separate "Bidets You'll Love" tiles that link to the same generic category, not a specific model — no sizing, no install-type info (that matters a lot for a bathroom reno).

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the blank hero — put an actual bidet product shot up top since that's what the subject promised. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Cut the chicken coop, pergola, and privacy screen tiles from this send entirely — if the subject is bidets, the first fold should be bidets, sale price, and maybe a "which bidet fits your toilet" guide.
- 3. Add a concrete price or % off on the bidet tiles themselves, not just the generic 60% banner buried at the bottom.
- 4. Link each bidet tile to a specific model with install-type (bidet attachment vs. standalone) called out — that's the actual decision-making info I need.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New bidet arrivals for your reno list`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Attachment or standalone — find the right fit for your bathroom`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: warehouse clearout, up to 60% off`
