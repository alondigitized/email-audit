## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on all images** — 30+ image instances flagged, including primary content images (`bbf6f9d4-...png`, `b84ff384-...jpg`, product/hero images) and tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`).
