- 15% off member pricing is called out clearly — they know I'm in the rewards program.
- No render bugs I can see — clean layout, images loaded, no broken stacks.

## What's weak

- This is the *fourth* "Double Rewards" / "Memorial Day Event" mothership send in 48 hours. The earlier two on 2026-05-24 already covered Ninja/Ooni/Dyson, then the kids brand hit me four times — I'm fatigued.
- No single dominant offer. The countdown clock is louder than any actual discount. CB2's "Now & Future Classics" send last week made one promise; this email is making five.
- "Up to 35% off" is buried in tiny copy at the top — Wayfair would've made that the whole hero.
- Generic "Shop Now" CTAs everywhere — no verbs that match what I'm actually doing (sourcing, completing a room, etc.).
- No shipping clarity on the larger furniture pieces — beds and dining tables with no lead-time callout. For a renovation buyer, that's the single most important piece of info and it's nowhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero.** Either the John Boos board (concrete deal, real save) OR the living-room vignette as a room-builder. Right now neither wins.
- 2. **Surface delivery lead times** next to the bed and dining tables. "Ships in 2 weeks" converts a renovation buyer; ambiguity stalls us.
- 3. **Kill one of the four Memorial Day sends.** Cadence is the biggest drag on this brand right now — I would've scored this 7+ if it were the first one this weekend.
- 4. **Replace "Shop Now"** with "Add to my room" or "Check stock" — anything that respects that I'm building, not impulse-shopping.
- 5. **Put the rewards math up top.** "2x rewards on $500 = $50 back" beats "Double Rewards" abstraction.
