# It’s here! Earn DOUBLE REWARDS + Shop up to 60% off The Labor Day Event!

## Executive summary

- Okay, now this is a Crate & Barrel email actually worth opening. I've been drowning in Crate & Kids sends all week — four of them, same nursery gliders, same toddler beds, and I don't have kids — so seeing the actual grown-up account show up with a beach-sand hero image and a real category grid is a relief. Double Rewards plus up to 60% off is a legit hook, and they back it up immediately with a full grid: sofas, kitchen, tabletop, rugs, lighting, outdoor. That's the stuff I actually need for two houses right now.
- Where it gets me is the Dyson callout lower down — "Sale $849.99, Reg. $979.95" on a stick vacuum, up to 25% off select Dyson, plus a Sonos speaker deal. Specific prices, specific brands, real math. That's rare. Compare that to the Wayfair "UP TO 70% OFF" confetti banner I logged this week that just dumps you into category tiles with no actual price anchor — this beats it on specificity alone.
- I'd click. Probably the "Shop The Labor Day Event" button or straight into kitchen deals for that Dyson. Wouldn't forward it to a friend, this is still a mass sale blast, not something curated for me specifically, but it's earned an open-and-scroll instead of an instant archive.

## What's working

- Double Rewards banner up top gives me a concrete loyalty number (10-20% back) instead of vague "earn points" language.
- The category grid (sofas, kitchen, tabletop, rugs, outdoor, kids) covers both my primary and second-home renovation without forcing me into one room.
- Real prices on the Dyson ($849.99 vs $979.95 reg) and Sonos — most sale emails hide this behind "Shop Now" until you click.
- "top kitchen deals (they ship free!)" calls out shipping clarity for furniture-adjacent big items, which actually matters to me when I'm ordering across two addresses.
- The Design Desk / free design pro CTA near the bottom is a nice showroom-adjacent touch for someone mid-renovation.

## What's weak

- It's a long, long scroll — by the time I hit "we love these for you" I've already made my decision, that section is doing nothing extra.
- The rugs and decor tiles use the same handcrafted-rug photo style I've now seen from CB2 and Pottery Barn this same week, nothing distinguishes it visually.
- New card upsell ("the card you love just got better") buried near the bottom competes with the Double Rewards banner already up top — pick one loyalty message.
- No urgency deadline stated anywhere visible beyond "through September 8" tucked in small type up top — easy to miss on a skim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the Dyson/Sonos priced deals up higher — that's the strongest, most specific content in the email and it's currently buried past a full screen of category tiles.
- 2. Cut the "we love these for you" recommendation block — it's redundant after the Design Desk CTA and just extends an already-long scroll.
- 3. Consolidate the two loyalty pitches (Double Rewards banner + new card upsell) into one clear message so I'm not doing math twice.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or bolder "ends Sept 8" treatment near the hero, not just small print.
