- The hero offer, "up to 30% off" on the dining room event, is big and sits with a table-and-chairs photo, so I get the point fast.
- The four tiles (dining tables, dining seating, dining storage, select lighting) match how I shop, room by room.
- The "we love these for you" product row shows prices, including the Arne Black Wood Dining Chair, so I can price-check without clicking.

## What's weak

- The "Christmas decor ships free!" strip competes with the Thanksgiving hero, and the season is confused on Halloween. Ends tonight, but for which holiday?
- The "ends TONIGHT" urgency from the subject doesn't appear anywhere I can see in the body. The subject promises a deadline the email never repeats.
- The email is far too long. Cookware, holiday decor, Black Friday and the app all dilute a send that should be about one event.
- The fine print is tiny and dense, so I can't tell the exclusions without zooming. That hurts trust on "up to 30%."

- 3. Move the rewards card offer next to the hero, with a plain stack-it line like "30% off plus 10% back."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML table layout with a mobile media query at max-width 460px. The QA run found no hard failures. Its warnings are missing alt text on about 30 images, header checks that couldn't be completed, and 64 click-tracking links that weren't probed. The HTML supplied was truncated, so the footer, body links, and any unsubscribe markup could not be reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 64 links on click-redirect domains were skipped ("skipped HTTP probe"). Destination status, redirect chains, and expiry could not be confirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** 5 identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels fire per open. rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain. They carry a recipient-derived `s=` hash and add 5 extra third-party requests.
- **First-party pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/310e2822-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and tracking pixels.
- **Personalized images:** 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images look like per-recipient dynamic content. They depend on a live render service, so they could fail or blank if it is slow or down.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** about 30 `<img>` elements have no alt text.
  - 16 are content images, for example `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/9bc4943e-….jpg`, two GIFs (`c65ab55e-….gif`, `f3246943-….gif`), and the scene7 `2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide_Tertiary…` image. With images blocked or a screen reader, these show nothing.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, which likely show product or recommendation content.
  - 7 are tracking pixels (5 rlcdn, 1 `o.gif`, 1 `dv.…`). These should use `alt=""`.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Invalid `<title>`:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Template leftovers and duplicates:**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.
  - There are duplicate `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` tags.
  - There are three redundant `format-detection` metas.
  - `-webkit-text-size-adjust` is set to both `100%` and `none`.
  - None of these breaks rendering.
- **Image width rule:** The `@media (min-width:640px) { img {max-width:640px} }` rule and the `min-width:768px` rule with `max-width:600px` are applied in a confusing order. The 640px rule is later in source, so it overrides the 600px rule at widths of 768px and above. I can't confirm a visible effect from the truncated source.
- `lang="en"` is present, which is good.

No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens are visible in the supplied HTML. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid`, and `mi_mid` parameters are populated. The source is truncated, so the body and footer are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** The QA tool says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. It is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed absence.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support can't be determined. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for large senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain aligned with the brand domain.
- **Footer not reviewed:** The truncated HTML does not show the physical postal address or visible unsubscribe link, so those CAN-SPAM requirements were not verified.
- **Pixel disclosure (privacy):** The rlcdn.com pixels send a recipient-derived hash to a third-party data-onboarding provider. Confirm this is covered by the sender's privacy disclosures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 64 links are on tracking or click-redirect domains and were not resolved, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs. I can't confirm UTM parameters, whether the links reach the "Dining Room Event" landing page, or whether the "ends TONIGHT" offer is still live on the site.
