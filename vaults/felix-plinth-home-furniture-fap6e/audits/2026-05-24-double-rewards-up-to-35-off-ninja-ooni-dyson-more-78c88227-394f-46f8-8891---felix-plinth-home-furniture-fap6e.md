- The Ooni pizza oven shot is the strongest image in the email — it's aspirational and it's *me*.

- The credit card / rewards math callout near the bottom is at least *visible* — which is more than I can say for the last four sends from this family.

## What's weak

- "Up to 35% off" with no specific SKU price means I have no idea what the actual deal is on the Ooni or the Dyson. Pottery Barn at least tells me the table.
- Still no personalization. I'm clearly in-market for kitchen — they have the data — but I'm getting the same blast as everyone else.
- The "Double Rewards" badge is doing the heavy lift but the math isn't shown. What's my base rate? What does double get me on a $499 Vitamix?
- This is the *forty-second* C&B-family send since Sunday. Even when the creative is right, the cadence has me numb. Article landed *one* email yesterday and scored an 8 with me.
- Knife blocks and a Vitamix tile are tiny and crammed — I can barely read the brand chips on mobile without zooming.

- 1. **Put a real price on the hero.** "Ooni Koda 16 — $499 (was $599) + 2x rewards" beats "Up to 35% off" every time. Article and even CB2's app push were more specific than this.
- 2. **Segment me out of Crate & Kids and *into* the kitchen/appliance audience.** You clearly have the SKU intent data — use it. Forty-two sends in a week, zero personalization.
- 3. **Show the rewards math on the hero, not the footer.** "Spend $499, earn $50 back in rewards" is the line that justifies the Crate & Barrel Card vs. my Amex.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Broken webfonts (403)** — four `@font-face` resources return 403 from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`:
  - `1e07d600-3787-4517-bd3d-b140f9e54ec2.woff2`
  - `7ec25fd5-2752-4d84-b307-af3faabc21bb.woff2`

  - `fd5f8f50-d539-4a7d-b893-99501d746ef2.woff2`
  Most clients strip `@font-face` anyway, but webmail/Apple Mail users will fall back silently. CDN ACL likely blocks cross-origin font fetch — should be served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` or an open CDN path.
- **Missing alt text** — 45+ `<img>` elements lack `alt` attributes, including hero/product images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`. Image-blocking clients (Outlook default) will render empty rectangles. Provide alt text on content images; explicit `alt=""` on the 1×1 tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `cd1d1523-…`) to mark them decorative.
- DOCTYPE is XHTML 1.0 Transitional — outdated but acceptable for email. `meta name="color-scheme"` set to `light` only (no dark-mode support declared).
- Typo in HTML comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` — cosmetic, but indicates an untouched template placeholder.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No visible unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) in the captured source. Subject and visible copy use static promotional content.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header missing** in captured headers — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail). Verify at the SMTP source that both `List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:…>, <https://…>` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) are set. Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (Feb 2024) require one-click for senders >5k/day.
- **`Authentication-Results` header missing** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass state cannot be confirmed from this capture. Confirm `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned for DMARC against the From domain.

No UTM parameters visible on click-trackers themselves (UTMs typically appended post-redirect).
