- CB2 again. This is the ninth send in about six days, and the sender name "CB2 SALE" told me what was inside before I opened it. The hero is a tufted camel sofa under "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off." That's the same banner I saw on 10/1 ("UP TO 25% OFF CUSTOM & IN STOCK") and on the 10/2 "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" send. The design is gorgeous, but I'm in a permanent CB2 snooze.
- The real problem is that the email argues with itself. It gives me three offers: up to 25% off upholstery, a $5,000 refresh giveaway, and "Fall Design Sale, up to 50% off." I scrolled the whole render and never saw what the 50% covers. The fine print says "select furniture, lighting and more." The subject promised 25%, so the 50% block at the bottom reads like a different campaign stapled on.
- I'd click once, on Sectionals, because I'm furnishing two houses and upholstery is the expensive category. I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read, and probably file it in the Pinterest folder for the tufted-sofa photo only.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "Up to 25% off" is huge, and the tufted sofa photo is exactly the mood I save.
- The five stacked category buttons (Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs, Benches & Ottomans, Shop All) are big, easy to tap on a phone, and go straight to what I'd shop.
- "Book a free design appointment" fits a two-house renovation. It's a showroom-adjacent service, and the copy about custom options with the design experts is useful.
- The footer has the 10% Rewards Dollars card. That's what I'd check for my highest-rewards-card math, and it covers both CB2 and Crate & Barrel.
- The subject matches the hero, so there's no bait-and-switch like Wayfair's "free shipping over $35, all day, every day" subject, where I never found the promise in the email.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers (25%, $5K giveaway, 50%) dilute the hero. Pottery Barn's "Table Essentials Ship Free" put one offer over one image and was easier to read.
- The "rarely-on-sale" claim in the subject doesn't hold up. It's the same upholstery event I've seen all week, so "rarely" feels hollow.
- I can't tell what the 50% sale covers. The only explanation is tiny gray legal text at the bottom.
- The $5,000 refresh giveaway is a sweepstakes tied to booking an appointment. It's a distraction, and the moody dark-rug photo doesn't even show furniture.
- There's no shipping or lead-time info for custom upholstery. For sofas, that's the question I care about most.
- There are no swatches or sample program mentions, and no color-palette guidance, even though the hero is custom upholstery.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one offer. Lead with the 25% upholstery and drop or demote the "Fall Design Sale" block, or tell me which items it covers.
- 2. Show actual products with prices and a timeline, like "Ships in 4 to 6 weeks," instead of three mood shots.
- 3. Add a swatch-kit CTA ("Order free fabric swatches") next to the appointment button, which would speak directly to a renovator.
- 4. Put the sale end date (October 14) in the hero. It's only in the fine print.
- 5. Space out the cadence. Nine sends in six days teaches me to ignore you, and Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel are doing the same thing.
