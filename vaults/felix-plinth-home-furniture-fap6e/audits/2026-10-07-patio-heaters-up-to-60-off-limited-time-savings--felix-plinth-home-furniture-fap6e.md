## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Wayfair promo with heavy tracking instrumentation and consistent `_emr`/`batchid` parameters. The main issues are widespread missing alt text, about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and header data the relay didn't capture (so compliance can't be confirmed).

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 12 links were probed and 64 skipped. Every probed link returned 429 (rate limiting), so none was confirmed valid or broken. Affected URLs include `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...` and the product PDPs (e.g. `...Pyramid-Propane-Gas-Patio-Heater...~NNUG1188.html`). 429s are a probe limitation, not a defect.
- 4 links were not checked because the 70s probe budget ran out: `W115286860`, `W001540266`, `One-Way-Privacy-with-Heat-Control...` and `daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e390696.html`. They are unverified.
- The hero image link uses `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, not HTTPS. It depends on a redirect, which adds a hop and a downgrade risk.
- The `wayfairapp.onelink.me` link appears twice with different `_eml` values. This is per-slot tracking, so it is expected.
- The "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" link carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. The web fallback is also non-HTTPS.
- The email includes the open-tracking pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (no alt text). It also includes about 20 ad-impression pixels, `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019`, served over plain HTTP.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text, content images:** `5DaysofDeals_Fall2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`, `wfus_5daysofdeals_fall2026-skinny-flashdeals.png`, `WFUS_5DaysofDeals_Fall2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`. The hero image is also a link (`daily-sales`), so with images off or in a screen reader the link has no accessible name. The QA output shows the link text as `[image: ...]`, which is the filename.
- **Missing alt text, star ratings:** `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png`, about 14 instances in total. Ratings are conveyed only by images, so they are inaccessible. Several of them sit inside product links.
- **Stale asset:** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner` (May 2026, Memorial Day) is still in an October email. This is a template leftover, so confirm it is hidden or conditional.
- **HTTP images:** the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels may be blocked by clients that enforce HTTPS. They are 2x1 tracking images, so the user-visible impact is low.
- Responsive CSS is present: `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules and `u + .Gmail` / `body[data-outlook-cycle]` handling. The `.ProductRec-name` rule uses `word-break: break-all`, which can break product names mid-word on mobile. The HTML was truncated, so I could not check the body structure (`role="presentation"`, `lang`).

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens appear in the visible source. The `_eml`, `_emr` and `skurectxid` identifiers are populated.

**5. Compliance**
- QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` header and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The QA note says the relay may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed failure**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`; its alignment needs checking from the raw headers.
- The QA data doesn't confirm the unsubscribe link or the physical address. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed, and the source I have is truncated. I can't confirm either way.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters are absent. Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=5`, `varid=3`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_141458`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16482`. Analytics that depend on `utm_*` will need mapping, but the scheme is internally consistent across links.
- Landing pages align with the email: the patio-heater PDP (`NNUG1188`) and `daily-sales` match the "Patio Heaters" and sale subject. `Sasha-3-Drawer-Chest...HBXL1263` and `Farmhouse-Convertible-4-Piece-Nursery-Furniture-Set...UO10286` are nursery items that do not match the "PATIO HEATERS" subject. They may come from the recommendation engine (`skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`), so this may be a data-driven mismatch.
- Landing-page content could not be verified because of the 429s.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero and banner images. Use `alt=""` for decorative stars and pixels, or give stars a text equivalent such as "5 out of 5 stars".
2. Switch `li.wayfair.com/imp` and the hero link `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` to HTTPS.
3. Inspect the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment before treating them as failures.
4. Remove or conditionalize the Memorial Day banner.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links and the 429'd links with throttling.
6. Verify the nursery products in a "Patio Heaters" send are intentional.
7. Replace `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name`, which breaks words mid-letter.
