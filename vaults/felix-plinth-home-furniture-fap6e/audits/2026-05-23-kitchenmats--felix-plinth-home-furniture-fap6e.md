## What's weak

- This is email fourteen from Wayfair since Sunday. Even when the content hits my category, the cadence has done real damage to my click instinct.

- 5. **Give the rewards section a real number.** "You have 2,400 points — that's $24 toward this order" beats the current vague "get $X rewards on every item."
