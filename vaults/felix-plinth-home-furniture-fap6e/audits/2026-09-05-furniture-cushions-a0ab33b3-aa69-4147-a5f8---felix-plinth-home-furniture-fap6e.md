## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a fully HTML-table-based Wayfair marketing template with heavy click-tracking redirects on every link/image; automated probing hit rate-limiting (429s) on several material links, and multiple images lack `alt` text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs route through tracked redirect wrappers with `_emr`, `_eml`, `refid`, `maiid`, `mdlid` query params (e.g., `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=a0ab33b3-...&refid=MKTEML_121675...`) — consistent with expected ESP click-tracking, not flagged as an issue on its own.
- 7 of 12 probed material links returned **429 (Rate Limited)** during QA, including the app deep-link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`), "Shop Sale," the logo link, and a PDP link. QA notes these "may be valid" but could not confirm — recommend manual verification since rate-limiting could mask a broken redirect.
- **4 links were unprobed** due to the 70s time budget: two `daily-sales` filter links, the recliners category link, and one homepage link — status unknown, not pass/fail.
- 20 first-party pixel/impression beacons (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plaintext HTTP**, not HTTPS — see Rendering & Accessibility below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images missing `alt` text**, including content-bearing images (not just spacers/pixels): `0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, `stars_9.png`, `wfus_jt800_batch9_livingroom_recliners_ge...`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purpl...`, `wfus_jt800_minibahn_singleclass_tvstands_...`, `wfus_jt800_batch9_outdoor_chaiselounges_g...`, `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, plus the open-tracking pixel (`b.php`) and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons.
- One image src contains a **URL-encoding defect**: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` — the literal `%o` is not a valid percent-encoding sequence, which can cause the filename to fail to resolve/load in strict clients.
