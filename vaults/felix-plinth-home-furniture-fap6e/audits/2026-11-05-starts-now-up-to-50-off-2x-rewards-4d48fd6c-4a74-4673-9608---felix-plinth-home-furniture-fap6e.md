## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO conditional comments and mobile media queries. The automated QA scan found 0 failures and 3 warnings, plus about 40 images without alt text. Header-level checks (List-Unsubscribe, authentication) could not be confirmed because the relay did not expose them. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks (footer, unsubscribe link, UTMs) were not possible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Five third-party pixels fire per open.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2…&n=1` through `&n=5` are LiveRamp (rlcdn) pixels. Each carries a long hashed per-recipient identifier in the query string. They add five third-party requests and a privacy exposure. They are also sent without alt attributes.
- **First-party tracking is present.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/33d76386-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open-tracking pixels. They are expected, but the `dv.` URL has no file extension, so some clients may not treat it as an image.
- **Images are hosted on several domains.** They come from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `sr.rlcdn.com`. If any one fails or is blocked, part of the email breaks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** About 19 images are affected, including:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (about 17 files, e.g. `26171910-0ada-47d9-a83d-8056129f4c2f.jpg`)
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - the Scene7 GIF `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`
  - `…/971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`
  
  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show nothing or a filename.
- **Six more images lack alt text.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times with the same file. I can't tell from the truncated source whether it's content or a pixel.
- **Pixels without `alt`.** The rlcdn, `o.gif` and `dv.` pixels also lack `alt`. This is low impact, but `alt=""` would be correct for decorative images.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport tag is `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{… -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` setting declared earlier. This stops iOS text scaling.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses an unescaped `&`. A leftover template comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. The `format-detection` meta tag is declared three times.
- **Conflicting media queries.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and above both match, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the truncated HTML. The rest of the body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN].** The QA tool says the relay may not capture it. The header is therefore unconfirmed, not proven absent.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN].** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Body compliance elements not verified.** The truncated source did not reach the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are unchecked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 78 links go through tracking redirects that were not probed. The truncated HTML shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters, so landing page alignment with the "Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards" subject is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image. Use `alt=""` on pixels and decorative images.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` overrides.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original message, bypassing the relay. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the raw headers.
4. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. That covers the unsubscribe link, postal address, UTM parameters and any merge tokens.
5. Resolve a sample of the 78 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe. Check status codes and final landing URLs for UTM and promo-code carry-through.
6. Reconsider the five rlcdn pixels, or at least confirm they are consent-gated. Collapse the duplicate `format-detection` metas and fix the `&` in `<title>`.
