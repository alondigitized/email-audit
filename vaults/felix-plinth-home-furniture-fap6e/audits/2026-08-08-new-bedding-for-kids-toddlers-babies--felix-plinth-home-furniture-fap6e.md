# New bedding for kids, toddlers & babies →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — that's four of these in my inbox this week, and I'm not exaggerating, I actually went and checked. This is the fourth send in a category I have zero use for: kids bedding, baby bedding, toddler bedding, a "newborn shop," a stroller blanket collab with Nuna. I don't have kids. I have two renovations and a media wall that needs consoles, and this brand keeps mailing me nursery quilts like I'm going to suddenly need a crib rail cover.
- The one thing that stopped my thumb for half a second was "up to 50% off summer sale" buried two-thirds of the way down, and then immediately after it, "2 tween bedroom makeovers" from TheDesignDesk — that's actually adjacent to something I could use for the guest room in the second house, but it's crammed at the bottom after nine other scroll-stops of crib bedding I already scrolled past on autopilot.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and probably finally unsubscribing from Crate & Kids specifically while keeping regular Crate & Barrel. This is worse cadence than even Article's four-sale-emails-in-a-row stretch I flagged last week — at least Article was hitting the same category each time. This is hitting a category I'm not in, four times.

## What's working

- The "up to 50% off summer sale" banner is genuinely the strongest visual moment in the whole email — clean typography, clear number, no clutter around it.
- Photography is consistent and calm — muted sage, oat, terracotta tones — so at least it doesn't look like a discount blast even when it eventually is one.
- The TheDesignDesk "2 tween bedroom makeovers" module at the bottom is the one section that could theoretically apply to a non-parent doing a whole-house reno, if I'd made it that far.
- Product photography quality matches what I've come to expect from the Crate & Barrel family — no render bugs, images load clean, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids, and this is baby/toddler/newborn bedding stacked five sections deep before anything remotely adjacent to my actual project shows up.
- Subject says "kids, toddlers & babies" and the email delivers on that promise so literally that there's no room left for anyone outside that demo.
- The Nuna stroller collab in the middle makes this feel even more like I got cross-sold into a parenting newsletter I never signed up for.
- Compare this to Pottery Barn's "Warehouse Sale — Up to 60% off" send I scored 8 on — that led with the number in the subject line itself. Here the 50% off is invisible until you've scrolled past a newborn shop.
- Four Crate & Kids sends this week (this one, "Bestsellers for your nursery starting at $30!", "Nursery faves at prices you'll love," and now this) is worse fatigue than CB2 landing the same week as its own parent brand C&B — at least that's one instance of overlap, not a fourth consecutive miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely, or at least suppress it after I've ignored three straight sends — that's the single biggest lever here, not a copy tweak.
- 2. Pull the 50% off sale banner to the top of the email and into the subject line, the way Pottery Barn did with "60% off" — don't bury the one broadly-relevant offer under five kids-bedding modules.
- 3. If Crate & Kids and TheDesignDesk are going to share a send, lead with TheDesignDesk's tween/teen room content for recipients tagged as non-parents — that's the only section here that isn't baby-specific.
- 4. Cut the "newborn shop" and Nuna stroller cross-sell from any send going to a subscriber who's never clicked kids content — that's two full modules of zero relevance for me.
