## What's weak

- Zero relevance to my actual project — I'm mid-renovation on two homes and this is colanders and salad spinners.
- It's a kitchen-and-random-junk-drawer email masquerading as one send: kitchen tools, dish towels, a fall tablescape, back-to-school, a credit card upsell, and an app download all crammed into one scroll.

- 2. Move "the new fall table" section up top and make it the hero — vignette styling, room context, and a "book a design consult" CTA would actually get my click given I'm renovating two houses.
- 3. Cut the app-download and credit-card modules entirely from this send — they read as filler and dilute whatever focus the email had.
