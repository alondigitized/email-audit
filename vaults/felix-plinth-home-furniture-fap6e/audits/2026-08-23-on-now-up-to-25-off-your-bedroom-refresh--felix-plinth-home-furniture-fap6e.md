# On now: Up to 25% off your bedroom refresh!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the second bedroom-focused Crate & Barrel email today and it's basically a rerun of the "Up to 25% off bedroom furniture, bedding & more" one I already gave a 7 — same canopy bed hero, same category grid, same "up to 60% off clearance" beach photo tacked on for no reason. The bones are good: real bedroom furniture, real percentages, a dresser and nightstands and lighting all broken out cleanly. But the "$5000 Design Desk Sweeps" and the "new Crate Card" upsell and the app-download pitch in the back half feel like someone crammed three separate campaigns into one email because it was Tuesday.
- I'd click "Shop Bedroom Sale" — that Coveteur canopy bed at $1,899 (reg $2,299) actually stopped my scroll, and dressers/nightstands up to 25% off is exactly my lane right now with two houses mid-reno. But I'm not opening the Design Desk sweepstakes or the credit card pitch, and I'm definitely not forwarding this — it's a good-not-great repeat of a send I already saw today.
- Compared to CB2's "This sale ends tonight" (their fifth near-identical subject in six days, scored 5), Crate & Barrel is at least varying the hero product between sends. But this one's still cannibalizing its own sibling email from earlier today rather than doing something new.

## What's working

- The canopy bed hero with actual price-and-strikethrough ($1,899 vs $2,299) — real number, not vague "sale" language.
- Category grid (beds/dressers/bedding/decor) with a specific % per category — I can jump straight to dressers & nightstands, my current priority.
- "we love these for you" section mixes furniture with actual price drops (KitchenAid mixer at $100 off, dinnerware sets) — feels like a real personalization attempt, not just more beds.
- Bedroom demographic signal is right — canopy bed, walnut dresser, warm neutral bedding — this reads like a grown adult's primary bedroom, not Crate & Kids nursery furniture again.

## What's weak

- This is a near-duplicate of the other Crate & Barrel bedroom email already in my inbox today — same offer tier, same bed, same layout shape. Cadence is the problem CB2 has, and now C&B is doing it too.
- The $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes and "book a free appointment" block interrupts the shopping flow — I came for a dresser, not to enter a giveaway.
- Credit card cross-sell ("new! the card you love just got better") buried mid-scroll feels like a stranger's form letter, not a message to someone who's actively furnishing two homes.
- The clearance beach-photo banner ("up to 60% off clearance") has zero product shown — just a lifestyle shot and a button, no idea what's actually on clearance.
- App-download pitch this deep in the email is dead weight for me — I buy on desktop via my rewards card, not the app.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this from a today-send — it's competing with C&B's own earlier bedroom email for my attention and diluting both. Space bedroom promos out by at least a few days.
- 2. Move "we love these for you" (the personalized product mix) up higher, right after the hero — that's the section doing the actual work of feeling curated to me.
- 3. Kill or move the Design Desk sweepstakes and Crate Card pitch to a dedicated send — they're diluting a clean bedroom-sale email with unrelated asks.
- 4. Put a product or percentage on the clearance banner instead of just a beach photo and button.
