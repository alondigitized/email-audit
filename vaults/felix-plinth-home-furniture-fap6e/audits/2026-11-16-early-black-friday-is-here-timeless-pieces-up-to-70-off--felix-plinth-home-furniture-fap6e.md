# Early Black Friday is HERE + Timeless pieces UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Fourteenth Pottery Barn email in about two days, and my eyes slid right past the maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner. I've seen that exact banner in "⭐️ Our top Early Black Friday deals ⭐️" and "For the early shopper: UP TO 70% OFF." The subject says "Early Black Friday is HERE," but I've been told that since 11/14, so the news isn't new.
- The body is a real catalog, though. I can see a rug, a swivel chair, a jute rug, throws and a dining table, all with strike-through prices. For someone renovating two houses, the Reeva rug and Remmy chair are close to what's on my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I'd save this one and not click it right now. I wouldn't forward it. Sending a friend the 14th identical sale email would be weird.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Up to 70% off" is huge and there's one SHOP NOW button, so I get the offer in about two seconds.
- Real products with was/now pricing, like the $269 rug and $699 chair, make the discount feel concrete. That beats the "SALE" banners that Article sent.
- The category tiles (Bedding, Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Decor) let me skip to my lane. Furniture is exactly what I'm after.
- The Pottery Barn credit card block ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") speaks to my rewards-math brain. Crate & Barrel's 2X Rewards pushes are the closest comparison.
- The Design Crew appointment module gestures at showroom and design-service content, which I actually care about.

## What's weak

- Same banner, same offer, same week. CB2 at least changed its angle with "ends tomorrow." This one just announces "HERE" like it's a launch.
- The "Recommended just for you" block is stockings, a throw and a quilt. It doesn't read as personalized to a guy mid-renovation, and nothing shows I'm a known customer.
- The Christmas Shop block (up to 50% off) mixes holiday decor into a furniture-and-rugs sale. It dilutes the hero and repeats the Christmas pitch from the earlier sends.
- There's no shipping or lead-time info on the chair or dining table. That's the biggest question for furniture, and the email never answers it.
- The legal footer is a huge block of tiny text, and the 70% hero has a lot of exclusions behind it. "Up to" does a lot of work here.
- There's no swatch, sample or color-palette content, and no mention of quick-ship availability.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the cadence. Send one Black Friday email with a countdown instead of 14 near-copies. That would lift opens more than any creative change.
- 2. Segment by behavior. Show me furniture and rugs, and the dining table, based on my browsing. Drop the stockings.
- 3. Add ship-by dates and a "quick-ship" tag on the big pieces, and add swatch/sample links.
- 4. Move the rewards card pitch up near the hero as a stack, like "70% off + 10% back."
- 5. Cut the Christmas Shop block from a furniture-led send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs, chairs & tables: up to 70% off through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Remmy chair is $699 (was $899)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quick-ship furniture and rugs, plus 10% back with the PB card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your saved favorites are marked down. Sale ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email, 650px wide, with image-sliced content and footer. QA flags 4 warnings: two compliance headers, plain-text URL ratio, and the Authentication-Results header. The HTML source shows widespread missing alt text, 3 non-HTTPS images and leaked non-CSS code in a `<style>` block. The source is truncated, so findings below cover only what was visible or reported by QA.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 61 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Mixed-content tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is loaded over plain HTTP and may be blocked, which would undercount opens.
- **Plain-text URL ratio:** 12041/17043 chars (about 70.6%) are URLs, just over the 70% threshold. This is driven by the long encoded `qs=` tokens and is a mild spam-filter signal.
- **Additional third-party pixels:** `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/007328cf-...?mi_cid=...` are tracking images with no alt text (see Section 3). Neither was probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 44 images:**
  - Hero slices: `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_10.jpg` under `/pb/2026/US/H26_Sale_EBF_MINKStudio_WasNowPricing/`.
  - Product images: `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg` and the `open-box-cozy-cloud-...` image.
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg` and `_02.jpg`.
  - Footer: `/Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`.
  - With images off, the offer, product links and footer would be unreadable to screen-reader and blocked-image users. The `<img>` tag for `nav0.jpg` does have alt text (`POTTERY BARN`).
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`) blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility barrier.
- **Leaked server-side code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");`. It is invalid CSS and can break parsing of rules that follow in strict clients.
- **Stale and cross-brand assets:** a 2022 path (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`) and `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (West Elm domain) are still referenced. These are brittle dependencies if those hosts or paths are retired.
- **Spacer tables:** several (e.g. `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lack `role="presentation"`, though other layout tables have it.
- **Image-only footer:** 18 footer slices with no alt text. If the unsubscribe and legal links are inside these images, they disappear when images are blocked. The truncated source does not show the footer markup, so this is unconfirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Recipient email exposed in image URLs:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and the `svopn` pixel's `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` both carry the plaintext address in query strings, and the `svopn` pixel goes over HTTP. This is a privacy leak risk via proxy logs and referrers, though `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` reduces it.
- **Unresolved tokens:** none seen in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so they cannot be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing alignment:** cannot be assessed. All visible links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` tokens, and the probe skipped them. No UTM parameters are visible in the available source, and the final destinations are unknown.
- **Direct-site images:** `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` product images are loaded straight from the site, which is fine for continuity. The `coreemail.html` image URL is a site endpoint used as a tracking beacon.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels (`spacer_0.gif`, `d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`).
2. Switch the 3 `http://` assets to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, `svopn` pixel).
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block and fix the PHP template leak at source.
4. Populate `<title>` and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the raw recipient email in image URL query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use an opaque ID instead.
6. Make sure the unsubscribe link and mailing address are live text, not only inside the image-sliced footer.
7. Replace stale 2022 and `edm.westelm.com` asset references with current, same-brand hosted files.
8. For the audit pipeline, capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) before the relay. Resolve the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects with a HEAD or GET follow to check final destinations and UTMs.
