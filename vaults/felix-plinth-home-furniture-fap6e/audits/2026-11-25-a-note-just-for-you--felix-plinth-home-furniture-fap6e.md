# A note just for you!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Barrel family send. By my count it's the eleventh-plus in two days, and my C&B snooze is fully engaged. The "A note just for you!" subject is a fake-personal dodge, and the body is a mass-mailed letter. I'm renovating two houses, and this is the Kids line. I have no kid-furniture project, so a "wooden easel, workbench and screen-free toys" pitch is a miss for me.
- The format is odd for a retailer: a plain "Hi," letter, a five-item numbered gift list with underlined links, and one maroon "Shop the Sale" button. It reads like a note from a person, which I'll give it. But it buries the offer in prose, and "up to 50%* off" only shows up in the third line of a long paragraph. Pottery Barn and CB2 lead with a banner and a percentage. I'd rather get that than scroll a letter.
- I would not click, and I would not forward it. I'd skim and archive. It isn't going into my Pinterest folder either, since there's no visual to save.

## What's working

- The letter format breaks the pattern of the ten banner emails before it. It felt different from the "NEW ITEMS ADDED! Up to 50% off Black Friday" ribbon from earlier today.
- The numbered list gives me specific offers: 30% off toys, 50% off furniture and rugs, 30% off baby gear, 20% off Babyletto.
- Free shipping on $79+ is stated plainly, and the link text is bold.
- The Crate Card block mentions Reward Dollars. That speaks to how I buy, with the highest-rewards card.
- The footer nav (Furniture, Bedding, Decor, Storage) is clean and has big tap targets.

## What's weak

- There's no hero image and no product photo above the fold. Nothing to look at, nothing to save, nothing to pin.
- "Can you believe it's that time of year again?" and the Halloween aside are filler, and it takes too long to get to the offer.
- The "A note just for you!" subject promises personalization, but "Hi," has no name. It's the same letter everyone gets.
- The email stacks four more modules after the CTA: app download with a $500 sweepstakes, TheDesignDesk, the Crate Card, and a store locator. The email has no single job.
- Almost every offer has an asterisk or "up to" on it. The fine print at the bottom is a wall of text, and the sweepstakes language is dense.
- Kids' gifts and baby gear don't match a 36-year-old renovating two houses. This feels like it belongs to someone else's inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a real hero image and a single offer line, "Up to 50% off kids' furniture + free shipping $79+", and cut the intro paragraph to one sentence.
- 2. Segment this send. Send the Kids letter only to people who have bought or browsed kids' or baby products, and send me something on lighting or furniture. Better still, send me less often. CB2 and Pottery Barn also stack sends, but at least they stay in my category.
- 3. Collapse the footer modules (app, DesignDesk, Crate Card, store) into one secondary module, so the maroon button is the only real CTA.
- 4. Give the CTA a verb and a noun, like "Shop kids' gifts up to 50% off", instead of the generic "Shop the Sale".
- 5. Add a deadline. "Before it's over" isn't credible urgency, and "Ends Sunday 11:59 PT" would be.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: an empty first-name merge field ("Hi ,") and a 403 on the "Official Rules" link. Several images lack alt text. The HTML source was truncated, and the relay didn't capture the authentication or unsubscribe headers, so compliance and authentication can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (403):** The "Official Rules" link points to `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/app-sweepstakes`. This is a legal-disclosure link, so a failure matters more than usual. A 403 from an automated probe can be bot protection rather than a real outage, so verify it in a browser.
- **Unverified links:** 52 tracking or click-redirect links skipped the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Duplicate tracking pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` is requested 5 times (`n=1` to `n=5`) with the same `s=` value. That is redundant third-party calls. Other pixels are `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://s3enpwpv.emltrk.com/v2/s3enpwpv?i=99278410`.
- **Identifier in URLs:** The `s=` value on the rlcdn pixels and the `mi_u=` value on the `o.gif` pixel share the prefix `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f…`. This looks like one hashed recipient identifier shared with a third-party domain (`rlcdn.com`). Confirm this matches the privacy disclosures.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** Three images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` have no alt:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `2072d6b9-0ff6-4d6b-9743-38ba0fce3744.png`
  - `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`

  If these carry content, they are blank to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** The rlcdn, `o.gif` and emltrk pixels also lack alt. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.tPad-auto {padding-top:auto!important;}` is not a valid value for `padding-top`. It is harmless but dead code.
- **Redundant head markup:** There are three charset declarations (`http-equiv`, plus `<meta charset="utf-8">` twice) and three `format-detection` metas. This is harmless.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta and `color-scheme` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty name field (confirmed):** The QA check found the greeting renders as "Hi ," with an empty name slot. This is a merge-field failure with no fallback value. It matters more because the subject line "A note just for you!" promises personalization.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** The header is missing from the captured message. QA notes AgentMail may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be validated here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** These are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.
- **Official Rules link:** The 403 on this link is also a compliance risk if the email runs a sweepstakes.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing alignment:** The truncated HTML and the skipped tracking links show no destination URLs or UTM parameters, so this can't be assessed. The one probed destination, `/app-sweepstakes`, returned 403.

1. Fix the "Hi ," greeting by adding a merge fallback (for example "Hi there,") or suppressing the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Check `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/app-sweepstakes` in a real browser. If it is bot-blocked only, no action is needed. If it is truly 403, restore it before send.

4. Remove the duplicate `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls.
