# Black Friday deal: up to 50% off select furniture!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crate & Barrel Kids send I've had in two days with the same furniture hook. The 11/20 "Shop Black Friday: up to 50% off furniture!" was the one I actually wanted, and this is basically its Kids twin. At least this one is less nursery-only. Bedroom and workspace furniture get the first two blocks before the cribs show up.
- The layout is easy to skim: free-shipping strip, red Black Friday hero with a Shop Now button, then stacked category blocks with was/now prices and a "Shop Bedroom Furniture"-style button under each. I'd click once, probably the workspace block, since I'm outfitting an office in one of the houses. I wouldn't forward it. I don't have a nursery, and nothing here says "Felix."
- The email is long, and half the lower scroll is stuff I don't care about: stockings, play furniture, a sweepstakes, a credit card pitch, an app download. Pottery Barn would have kept this to one screen of offer.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: red Black Friday banner, "up to 50% off furniture," and a visible Shop Now button.
- Free shipping is in the top strip. For furniture that's a real purchase driver, and I want to see it before anything else.
- Each category block has was/now pricing on small tiles and its own button, like "Shop Workspace Furniture." That's closer to how I shop than a generic "Shop the Sale."
- The workspace block (desk, chairs, storage) is the one piece that fits my renovation.
- The lifestyle photos are well styled, and the holiday dressing makes it feel seasonal. Saving these to my Pinterest-adjacent folder is the right instinct.

## What's weak

- I'm not the Kids audience. The nursery block, the cribs, Babyletto and Namesake, and the baby bedding are dead weight for me.
- This is the same hook as "Top beds, cribs, dressers & more at up to 50% off!" earlier today and the 11/20 furniture send. After the "top 10" email, I'm in a Crate family snooze again.
- The email runs too long, and the sweepstakes, card promo, app download and "Design Desk" appointment all pile on after the real offer. Nothing ties the card to rewards math, which is the one place I'd have cared.
- The offer is "up to 50%" on "select" furniture, with no deadline and no sense of what's actually 50% off. The tiles also give me no dimensions, finishes or ship dates.
- Wayfair would at least show me a countdown. Here I have no reason to act now rather than Monday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by about half. Keep the hero, three category blocks and one CTA row, and drop the sweepstakes, app and card sections or send them separately.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, like "Ends Sunday 11/23," plus a ship-date or in-stock cue on the furniture tiles.
- 3. Segment by household. Someone buying home-office pieces shouldn't get a nursery hero. Lead with the bedroom and workspace blocks for non-parents.
- 4. Put the card offer next to the pricing with explicit math, such as "extra 10% back with the Crate card," since I'm on the highest-rewards card anyway.
- 5. Make this send different from the 11/20 furniture email. Name a hero product, not just a category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Desks, beds & dressers: up to 50% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free shipping + 50% off Kids furniture, 48 hrs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedroom, workspace and nursery picks, with free shipping through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Little Bear bed, Boerum desk and more, marked down for Black Friday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Marketo-style XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with working MSO/Outlook fallbacks, but about 65 images lack `alt` attributes. The viewport and text-size settings also hurt accessibility. The header-level compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay doesn't expose the headers. The source is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level findings are limited to what the QA run captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 123 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are not validated.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`) fire alongside the first-party open pixel (`https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`). Six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` images and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c84e8275-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` image also carry per-recipient identifiers. That is extra tracking surface, and the pixels add requests to the email.
- **Missing alt on tracking images:** All of these pixels lack `alt`. Tracking pixels should use `alt=""`, which keeps them out of the accessibility tree.
- **Possible empty spacer:** `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` is a layout image with no `alt`. Its URL-encoded filename (`%5F`) is harmless, but the spacer is another remote request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 65 images flagged, including roughly 50 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|.gif|.png`. With images blocked, the product grid renders as empty boxes. The animated `7cf1a4d1-ef06-4343-9ede-2424b6bd36a1.gif` has no `alt` either.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** The `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rules stop users from enlarging text in WebKit clients. They also contradict the `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` rule earlier in the same document.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` in an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Redundant or leftover markup:**
  - `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are both present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - A template placeholder comment ships in production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, which also has a typo.
- **Generic title:** `<title>` doesn't reflect the subject or campaign.
- **Lang attribute:** `lang="en"` is set, which is good.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` only, so there is no dark-mode handling. This is a limitation rather than a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the supplied source or QA data. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are populated. The body is truncated, so the footer and greeting weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (QA WARN). RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified for the same reason. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment couldn't be checked. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated subdomain, but alignment is unconfirmed.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 123 tracking links were skipped, so final landing URLs, UTM parameters and redirect behavior are unknown. No UTM parameters appear in the visible source. Click attribution appears to run through the Marketo/MI redirect domains instead.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (product name and price/offer). Use `alt=""` on spacers and on the pixels from `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Remove `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` and `html` rules, and keep a single consistent `100%` value.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it campaign-specific. Delete the duplicate meta tags and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
5. Verify the headers from a raw `.eml` captured outside the AgentMail relay: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in `Authentication-Results`.
6. Probe a sample of the 123 tracking links with a redirect follower. Record the final URLs and their UTM parameters, and check for dead destinations.
7. Audit the third-party pixels from `rlcdn.com`, and consolidate duplicates if the repeated `n=1…5` calls are redundant.
