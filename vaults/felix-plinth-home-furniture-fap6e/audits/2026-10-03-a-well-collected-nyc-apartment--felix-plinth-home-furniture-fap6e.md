## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `<title>`, Outlook conditionals, mobile media queries). However, it has 12 content images with no alt text and 11 third-party tracking pixels served over plain `http://`. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skips click-redirect domains, so link validity is unverified, not broken.
- **11 pixels load over `http://`** and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **The Dotomi pixels are third-party ad-network cookie-sync redirects** (`cookieredir`). They add external dependencies and can hurt deliverability and privacy posture. They also fail to load wherever images are proxied or blocked.
- **Two first-party pixels also lack alt text:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0a76448f-...?mi_cid=...`. These are expected open-tracking pixels. The `mi_u` parameter is a 64-hex-character per-recipient identifier.
- **Font URL typo:** `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` has a double slash in three `@font-face` rules. It probably resolves, but it's sloppy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 content images have no alt attribute**, all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `f340bec4-…jpg`, `e1fd183e-…jpg`, `268042c0-…gif`, `b741cc5c-…jpg`
  - `0dd08f59-…jpg`, `6adfc879-…jpg`, `7972a5cb-…jpg`, `a68a4f22-…jpg`
  - `aec4186f-…jpg`, `63ecbdaf-…jpg`, `89537ff7-…jpg`, `326a21c4-…jpg`
  
  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, this content is lost.
- **All 13 tracking pixels also lack alt.** They should have `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing is disabled:** the universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ... line-height:100%}` stops user text scaling and sets a tight line-height everywhere.
- **Template placeholder left in the head:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (misspelled, never filled in).
- **Web fonts load from another brand's domain:** the `HNW05` `@font-face` rules point at `crateandbarrel.com`. Fallbacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) exist.
