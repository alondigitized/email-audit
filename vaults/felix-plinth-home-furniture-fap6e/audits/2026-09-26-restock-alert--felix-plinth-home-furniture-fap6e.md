# Restock alert
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Restock alert" — this is CB2 telling me eight different categories are back "IN STOCK" (bedroom, coffee tables, sectionals, storage, sofas, lighting, wall art) stacked one after another like a category directory, not a curated email. That's genuinely useful information framed badly — I don't need to see every single department restocked, I need to know if the ONE thing I actually clicked on last month is back.
- By the time I scroll past seven near-identical "IN STOCK / [Category] / SHOP NOW" blocks, my eyes have glazed over, and then it pivots mid-email into the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event (25% off) — which I already got as its own dedicated send yesterday ("The Upholstery Event is on," 7/10). So now this is two emails smashed into one, and the actually-good upholstery deal gets buried under a wall of restock filler above it.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is the kind of email where I skim the top, register "oh, restocks," and archive. Compare this to Pottery Barn's towels email — narrow, single-purpose, exactly one thing I need mid-renovation. CB2 keeps trying to cram five campaigns into one send and it dilutes everything.

## What's working

- The hero "SEIZE the MOMENT" with the burgundy velvet chair is a strong, moody opener — genuinely my style aesthetic.
- Consistent CB2 photography — every room shot is styled and shoppable-looking, no stock-photo feel.
- The Upholstery Event banner (25% off) is at least visually distinct from the restock grid above it with the black background.
- "Sofas & Sectionals" and "More For You" at the bottom have real prices ($1199, $499, etc.) visible, which I actually want to see before clicking anything.

## What's weak

- Eight stacked "IN STOCK" category blocks is just a scroll marathon — no prioritization, no "here's the one thing you looked at."
- Restock alert + Upholstery Event + $5,000 Refresh sweepstakes + Best Sellers grid is four campaigns in one email; nothing gets to breathe.
- "IN STOCK" repeated as the header on nearly every section reads like a template that forgot to differentiate copy.
- No personalization at all — this isn't restocked based on anything I browsed, it's every category, which makes me treat it like a stranger-blast, not a "we know you looked at that sectional" email.
- The $5,000 Refresh sweepstakes squeezed in between upholstery and best-sellers feels like a tacked-on afterthought, not integrated into the renovation narrative I'm actually living.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails: a real restock alert (limit to 2-3 categories tied to my browsing/wishlist, not eight) and a standalone Upholstery Event send — don't recycle yesterday's promo into today's alert.
- 2. Lead with whatever I actually browsed — if I looked at sectionals, put sectionals first and drop bedroom/lighting/wall art entirely for me.
- 3. Cut the sweepstakes block or move it to its own email — it has nothing to do with "restock."
- 4. Add urgency to the restocks themselves ("back for a limited run" / stock counts) since restock alerts live or die on scarcity, and right now there's none.
- 5. Show prices on the restock hero items, the way the "More For You" grid does at the bottom — I shouldn't have to click through eight sections blind.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Restocked: 8 categories, including your saves`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The pieces you saved just came back — plus 25% off upholstery`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sectionals, coffee tables, and storage — back before they sell out again`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Email fails on personalization (unresolved merge token) and shows unresolved compliance/authentication header visibility issues; markup itself is otherwise a standard MSO/VML responsive template.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 73 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP validation (redirect domains), so their final destinations are unverified.
- Legacy Dotomi ad-sync pixels use plain HTTP: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Non-HTTPS resources may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, silently breaking these ad-sync calls.
- Mi.cb2.com and dv.cb2.com tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `/o/32f89755-...`) use HTTPS — no issue there.

- 15 content/tracking images are missing `alt` text, including all primary product images (e.g. `4ddadd7f-a573-48ad-912f-4e23a2a4b2df.jpg`, `fa96b5d7-c967-4bd6-8092-1830c7513292.jpg`, and 9 others under `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`). This degrades screen-reader experience and image-blocked fallback rendering.
- Markup includes standard MSO conditional comments and `@media` breakpoints (418px, 690px, 768px) for responsive/Outlook handling — no structural rendering issues detected in the truncated source.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL**: Raw unresolved merge token `%%reviews_skulist%%` present in the rendered HTML — indicates a failed merge/personalization step in the ESP send. This will display literally to recipients and likely breaks the associated review-content block.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support without header access.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — same caveat; if genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) is not supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC status) not detected — cannot confirm sending domain authentication from this data.
- No in-body physical address or unsubscribe link visibility could be confirmed from the truncated HTML source provided.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No issues found — insufficient data; the 73 tracking links were not resolved/probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from available evidence.

7. **Recommendations**
