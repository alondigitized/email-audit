## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a Movable Ink and Salesforce-style template with 84 click-redirect links, and about 45 images lack alt text. The HTML head has several template and accessibility defects. The QA run could not confirm authentication or unsubscribe headers because of the AgentMail relay, so those are unverified rather than confirmed failures. The HTML source was truncated inside the CSS, so the body, links and footer were not available to audit.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **84 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times. This is a LiveRamp pixel carrying a hashed identifier (`s=`), and it is duplicated. It adds request overhead and a third-party data-sharing surface.
- **Other tracking beacons:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/65e47ae1-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression beacons. Both are blocked by default in Apple Mail Privacy Protection and some clients, so open-rate data is unreliable.
- **Stale asset path:** the Scene7 asset `…/07%5FJuly/2026%5F0716%5FCB%5FBrandAnthem/2026%5F0716%5FCB%5FBrandAnthemSecondary%5F01.gif` is a July asset reused in an October send. It is not a defect by itself, but I can't confirm it still resolves because it was not probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 45 images** (QA WARN). This covers content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.png`, Scene7 images (`2026%5F1002%5FCB%5FTertiary%5FVF%5FOoni%5FSocial…`), and Movable Ink product images (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, 7 instances). With images off, or in a screen reader, product and CTA content is unreadable. The tracking pixels (`448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry `alt=""`. The QA tool can't distinguish a missing attribute from an empty one, so some of these may be false positives.
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing is disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` stop users from scaling text. The global `line-height:100%` with `mso-line-height-rule:exactly` also tightens readability.
- **Small mobile text:** `.f10{font-size:10px!important;line-height:12px!important}` is below common 11–14px minimums.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` is duplicated with `http-equiv Content-Type`.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
  - A template placeholder was left in: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Later source order wins, so the 600px cap is dead code at ≥768px.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto;text-align:center}`** forces centered text on all paragraphs and may override intended left alignment.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=` and `mi_cid=` parameters on the Movable Ink URLs are populated. The body was truncated, so subject and body tokens beyond the head weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN) and List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN, RFC 8058).** The QA note says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. I can't confirm this is a sender defect. If the headers are genuinely absent, this would fail Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, so alignment against that subdomain should be checked on the raw message.
- **Footer unverified.** The visible, working unsubscribe link and the physical postal address required by CAN-SPAM are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so they were not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 84 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated HTML exposed no final destination URLs. UTM parameters, landing-page match to the subject line ("coffee tech is on sale") and redirect behavior could not be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images (about 40). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels, including the five duplicate `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `text-size-adjust:none` to restore zoom and text scaling.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Dedupe the `charset` and `format-detection` metas. Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder.
4. Consolidate the conflicting `img` max-width media queries.
5. Cut the duplicate LiveRamp pixel fires to one, or drop them if they aren't needed.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`, not the AgentMail relay copy, to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also extend the HTML capture so footer, link and UTM checks can run on the full body.
7. Follow the redirect chains for a sample of the 84 tracked links, and check UTM presence and the landing-page match to the offer.
