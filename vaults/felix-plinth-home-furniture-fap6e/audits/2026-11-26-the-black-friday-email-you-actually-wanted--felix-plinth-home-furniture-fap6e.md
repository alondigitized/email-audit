## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a 57% QA pass rate with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main findings are about 14 content images with no alt text and 11 third-party tracking pixels loaded over plaintext `http://`. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links.** 64 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect health and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels.** 11 Dotomi (Epsilon) pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
  - Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources won't load them, so cookie-sync and tracking will silently fail for those recipients. Gmail's image proxy may upgrade or fail them.
  - Ten cookie-redirect pixels is a heavy third-party load for one email.
- **Open-tracking pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/be229c68-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` use HTTPS, which is fine. Both are missing alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** 14 images hosted at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt attribute. Examples are `e32ad699-…gif`, `3a2b32eb-…jpg`, `844a54c0-…jpg` and `fab87793-…jpg`. If images are blocked, or a screen reader is used, the content is lost. It's a real problem if these are the hero or product tiles, which can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Pixels without alt.** The 11 Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel also lack alt. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template leftover.** The source still contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Font URLs.** Some `@font-face` URLs contain a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`). One group references `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`, a different domain. Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway, so this is low severity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the tracking pixels are populated, not placeholders. The truncation means the full body couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** Both were not found. QA notes that AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results`.** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender uses `mail.cb2.com`, so alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer.** The unsubscribe link, physical address and sender identification are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. Links are wrapped in 64 click-tracking redirects that were not resolved, and the truncated HTML shows no UTM parameters or final landing URLs. Whether the destinations match the Black Friday offer is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch all Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop them. Consolidate the 10 `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 14 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Re-run the audit on a raw `.eml` source, or against the sending ESP, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
5. Follow the redirect chain on a sample of the 64 tracked links to confirm 200 status, landing URL and UTM carry-through.
6. Remove the template placeholder comment and fix the `//` font URL paths.
