# End of Summer Sale is over tomorrow
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2 again, this is now four sends in a row this week — "Just added to clearance," "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE," "Future Heirlooms Under $500," and now "End of Summer Sale is over tomorrow." At this point I've stopped reading these as distinct campaigns and started reading them as one long scroll from the same sale. That said, the deadline framing here actually does something the other three didn't: "Ends tomorrow" is a real, credible urgency beat, and the hero — that moody glassware shot in olive, blush, and amber — is genuinely the best-looking thing they've sent me all week.
- The category grid underneath (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) is clean and I can actually picture the green velvet sofa in my primary-home living room, but there's still zero dollar figure or percentage anywhere above the fold. I have to scroll to the legal fine print at the bottom to learn it's "up to 40% off." That's a real miss — Wayfair's Labor Day Clearance send led with "70% OFF" in the subject and the banner, no digging required.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the sofa image alone, since I've got both houses to furnish. Would I forward it to a friend? No — this is background noise at this point, not something worth interrupting someone's day for.

## What's working

- The hero banner is legitimately pretty — that pastel glassware still life with "END OF SUMMER SALE" in editorial type is the strongest visual CB2's sent me this week.
- "Ends tomorrow" is a real deadline, unlike the vague "Summer Clearance" language from their Aug 20 clearance email.
- The Furniture/Lighting/Rugs/Decor grid is scannable and room-relevant — I can actually place that sofa and those pillows in a space I'm renovating.
- The Rewards/App/Text-signup footer block is at least organized and legible, not just another banner slapped on.

## What's weak

- No percentage or dollar figure anywhere in the visible email — I have to scroll to the tiny legal text to learn it's "up to 40% off." Wayfair puts the number in the subject line itself.
- Fourth CB2/C&B-family email this week with the same "SALE" energy — my brain has fully entered snooze mode on this sender.
- "SHOP THE SALE" appears twice and just routes to a generic sale page, not a specific product — no single hero item I can click straight to, unlike Article's storage home-tour which at least links a specific bed and dresser.
- The wall art pillow feature at the bottom feels like a bolt-on, disconnected from the "end of summer" urgency above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual discount number in the hero — "Up to 40% Off" belongs under "END OF SUMMER SALE," not buried in an 8-point footer disclaimer.
- 2. Cut send frequency. Four CB2-family emails in four days is Wayfair-level spam, and my open rate for this sender is dropping fast because of it.
- 3. Link "SHOP THE SALE" to a real filtered landing page (e.g., "Furniture Up to 40% Off") instead of a flat sale homepage.
- 4. Move the Rewards 10% back / text-signup 15% off content higher — that's actual, personalized value for a repeat shopper and it's currently buried below three product categories.
