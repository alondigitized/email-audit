- Half this email didn't even load — every single hero shot is a broken blue-icon placeholder except one, "on sale now: pergolas." And the one image that DID render isn't even a gazebo, it's a pergola tile, in an email literally titled "Our Best Gazebo Deals." I've got two houses mid-reno right now, I would actually look at gazebo shots if you showed me any.
- This is the same pergola/gazebo overlap I called out in Wayfair's 8/8 "72 HOURS ONLY" clearout send and the "On sale now: pergolas" send from that same day — Wayfair's outdoor category emails are visibly cannibalizing each other, same tile recycled three sends running. - Would I click? No, there's nothing to click on — literally can't see the product. Mark as read and move on, not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- The pergola tile that actually rendered looks decent — string lights, styled patio, real lifestyle shot.
- Nav bar up top is clean, "Shop Sale" and "New Arrivals" are legible.
- The 5% Rewards banner is at least readable text, no image dependency, so that message survives the render failure.
- Section labels ("let's complete your space," "cool down... on any budget") are readable text, so I can tell the email's structure even where the images died.

## What's weak

- Nearly every hero and product image on the page is a broken blue-square placeholder — birdbaths, cat trees, wall decor, dining chairs, nightstands, ottomans, planters, bedding, all broken.
- The subject says gazebos, the one image that rendered is a pergola — same mismatch problem I flagged in the 8/8 pergola-heavy sends.
- No actual gazebo product, price, or dimension is visible anywhere in the email.
- The "cool down... on any budget" AC section at least has a rendered unit shot, but it's totally disconnected from the gazebo subject line — feels like three unrelated campaigns stapled together.
- Zero swatch/color-palette or shipping-window info for outdoor furniture, which is exactly what I need before I'll click into a gazebo purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image pipeline before this ships again — a marketing email where 90% of the product images are dead placeholders shouldn't clear QA. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Lead with an actual gazebo image and price, not a pergola. If gazebo inventory isn't ready, don't send a "gazebo deals" subject.
- 3. Cut the email down — birdcages, cat trees, bedding, and planters have nothing to do with "gazebo deals" and just dilute an already-broken send.
- 4. Add a real price/percent-off on the gazebo tile itself, not just "on sale now."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

Email renders on a legacy Unbounce/BEE-style responsive framework with standard `u-` mobile breakpoint classes. Automated QA returned a 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and non-HTTPS tracking pixels; no broken links were confirmed (all failures were rate-limit responses, not 404s/dead links).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 of 60 links were probed (click-likelihood ranked); all 12 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by Wayfair's edge, not confirmed broken) — includes the primary hero CTA `daily-sales/filters/our-best-gazebo-deals--se82152.html` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me` deep link.
- 4 links were left unprobed on time budget, including `on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm` and `our-best-narrow-nightstands-se68199.` — note the trailing `.` and `%3a` (URL-encoded colon) in these slugs, which look like malformed href construction rather than probe artifacts and are worth a manual check.
- 20 tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged by QA as "Image uses http://: non-HTTPS source may be blocked." In a TLS-enforcing mail client these impression pixels will be silently dropped, undercounting opens/attribution for this send.
- Standard `_emr`/`_eml`/`refid`/`maiid` click-tracking params are present and consistently applied across probed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- 15 images are missing `alt` text, including content-bearing creative (`0728_Outdo%orClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, three `wfus_jt800_batch10_majorappliances_airconditioners*` assets, `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`, `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`) as well as all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels and the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open-tracking pixel.
- Tracking/beacon pixels lacking `alt` is standard practice (decorative, zero-dimension), but the content images lacking `alt` (hero banners, product/appliance imagery, rewards disclaimer) will read as blank to screen readers and show broken-image placeholders with filenames when images are blocked — a real accessibility gap.
- One asset filename is truncated/malformed in the source: `0728_Outdo%orClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg` contains a stray `%` (likely an encoding artifact from `%23` or similar being partially decoded) — worth verifying the actual asset resolves correctly.
- Mobile breakpoint CSS (`@media max-width:480px`) is present and comprehensive (width/display overrides, font-size scaling, button padding) — no structural rendering issues found in the visible markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `*|...|*`) or personalization placeholders found in the visible HTML/link set. No issues found.

QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM/one-click compliance status from this data alone; needs verification against raw SMTP headers at the source MTA.

**Recommendation**: these three header findings should be re-verified against raw headers captured before the AgentMail relay, since relay stripping vs. genuine absence changes the severity substantially.
