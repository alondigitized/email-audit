# The Upholstery Event is on
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "The Upholstery Event is on" — this is the strongest email CB2 has sent me all week, and it's not close. Semi-annual upholstery sale, up to 25% off, hero image is a green velvet sectional in a moody styled room that actually looks like something I'd put in the second house's living room. This is the first CB2 send in days that isn't "collected feel" mood-board filler or an app ad disguised as a sale — it's a real category sale with a real product shot up top, and the swatch program call-out ("Order 10 free swatches now") is genuinely useful for someone renovating two houses at once and trying to nail down fabric before committing.
- That said, I've now seen "up to X% off upholstery/best sellers" from this exact family (CB2 + Crate & Barrel) like five times this month, so my enthusiasm is capped by fatigue, not execution. I'd click through to the sectionals section specifically — that's the room I actually need furniture for right now. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, but I'd screenshot the sectional for my own moodboard folder.

## What's working

- Hero copy is clear and category-specific — "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, Up to 25% Off" is a real, understandable offer, unlike CB2's "Only On The App" send from two days ago that buried the same sale behind an app pitch.
- The green velvet sectional hero shot is styled well — warm wood tones, textured rug, looks like an actual room, not a white-box product photo.
- Category breakdown (Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs, Benches and Ottomans) with real lifestyle shots for each — this is the swatch-and-sample, room-context content I actually want from a furniture brand and Wayfair completely fails at (their "ALL-WEATHER DECK BOXES" email had a broken image icon in the hero, this has none).
- "Order 10 free swatches" CTA is exactly the kind of tactile decision-support I need mid-renovation — nobody else in my recent reads (Pottery Barn, Article, Wayfair) called out a swatch program this directly.
- Rewards math is visible in the footer — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" — which matters since I'm optimizing every purchase through a rewards card anyway.

## What's weak

- Cadence fatigue is real: this is the fourth CB2/Crate & Barrel-family sale-structured email I've seen this week ("Don't miss these best sellers," "Popular for a reason," now this) — same layout, same "Shop The Sale" black button, different hero.
- The "25%" is enormous and eye-catching but the fine print (obviously, per the screenshot, not verified in code) isn't visible above the fold — I can't tell what's excluded until I scroll to the legal block at the very bottom, which is a lot of scrolling for a phone.
- A second "SALE ON NOW — Up to 50% off" banner appears right after the main upholstery push, which muddies the hierarchy — is the story 25% off upholstery or 50% off something else? Undercuts the one clear offer I just read.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the body — "it only happens twice a year" is a rarity hook, not a deadline, so there's no reason to act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill or clearly separate the "Up to 50% Off" banner from the upholstery story — right now it steps on the primary offer and makes me question which number is real. If it's a different category, header it distinctly ("Also on sale:") instead of stacking two competing percentages back to back.
- 2. Add a real deadline near the hero ("Sale ends Sunday") — "it only happens twice a year" is a nice rarity beat but doesn't create this-week urgency the way a date does.
- 3. Move the swatch-order CTA up next to the hero button instead of in a separate full-width block below — for someone mid-renovation, "order swatches" and "shop the sale" are both primary actions, not one primary and one secondary.
- 4. Since this is the fourth sale email from the C&B family this week, differentiate the subject line harder from CB2's own recent "Don't miss these best sellers" — the current subject is fine in isolation but reads as one more sale rerun in an inbox that already has three.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Custom and in-stock pieces rarely go on sale. This is one of two chances a year.`
