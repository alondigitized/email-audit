# Which TV STAND ARE YOU?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Talk of the Team" — this is Wayfair basically saying "here's what we personally like," and it opens with a genuinely nice hero: a dark cane-front sideboard styled as a TV console with a cocktail and cheese board on top, "TV time, in style." That's the first Wayfair email in a while that actually nails a lifestyle shot instead of a broken image or a bargain-bin banner. Problem is, that's the entire relevance budget spent in ten seconds, because right after that it's back to the exact same "Sales We Tell Our Friends to Shop" firehose grid I've seen from this brand every single day this week — deck boxes, dinnerware, wall sconces, bath towels, drinkware.
- I'd stop scrolling right after the hero. The subject "Which TV STAND ARE YOU?" promises a fun quiz/personality angle and the email doesn't even attempt to deliver that — no quiz, no "pick your style," just a static image and then generic categories. Not forwarding this, not clicking past the hero, marking as read.

## What's working

- The hero image is legitimately styled — moody dark green room, cane-front console doubling as a TV stand, drink and snack styled on top. That's aspirational, not stock-photo.
- "TV time, in style." + "SHOWSTOPPING STANDS HERE" is a clean, on-brand headline pairing, better copy than most of Wayfair's recent all-caps category dumps.
- No broken images this time — unlike the "ALL-WEATHER DECK BOXES" send from 2 days ago where the hero was just a broken icon, everything here actually rendered.
- Rewards banner (5% on every item, always) is visible and simple, which is a real trust/loyalty signal for anyone already Wayfair Rewards enrolled.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a personality-quiz gimmick ("Which TV Stand Are You?") and the body never follows through — it's just one static console image, then an unrelated 20-item grid. Total bait-and-switch on the one clever idea in the send.
- After the hero, this is the same undifferentiated grid I've gotten from Wayfair for four days running (bins & baskets, open box, deck boxes) — dinnerware, bath towels, patio cushions, mattresses, cookware, all mashed together with zero connection to "TV stand."
- If I'm actually shopping TV consoles/media furniture for either house, there's exactly one relevant tile buried mid-grid ("Blue Sideboards & Buffets") — no filtering by size, finish, or cord-management features I'd actually care about for a media console.
- No urgency, no price anchor on the TV stand category itself — CB2's "Upholstery Event... up to 25% off" gives me a number to act on; this gives me a vibe and then changes the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to the quiz premise.** If the subject is "Which TV Stand Are You?", build an actual 3-style picker (Modern / Farmhouse / Cane & Warm) that routes to filtered collections — that's the one idea here worth keeping, and right now it's abandoned after the subject line.
- 2. **Cut the grid down or segment it.** Nineteen unrelated tiles under one hero about TV stands is the same shotgun approach that's dragged every recent Wayfair send to a 3-4/10. Give me a media-furniture module (consoles, sideboards, cord management, mount hardware) and drop the rest into a separate send.
- 3. **Put a number on the TV stand offer.** CB2's semi-annual upholstery event ("up to 25% off") is the strongest thing in my inbox all week because it has a real percentage attached. This email has zero pricing on its own hero product.
- 4. **Add sizing/fit info near the console tile** — for media furniture I care about width vs. my TV size and cord routing, none of which is shown or hinted at.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Wayfair template renders with standard MSO/Gmail responsive CSS; QA pass rate 57%, with issues concentrated in tracking-pixel HTTPS/alt-text hygiene and unconfirmed unsubscribe/authentication headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links probed by CTA likelihood; several returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) rather than confirmed failures, including the primary CTA "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" (wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157) and "Shop Sale" (wayfair.com/daily-sales). These are marked as "may be valid" — not confirmed broken, but unverified.
- 4 filter/category links went unprobed due to a 70s time budget (e.g. `/daily-sales/filters/all-weather-deck-boxes-se16398.html`, `/daily-sales/filters/dinnerware-sets-from-%2425-se35945.h`) — status unknown.
- 50 lower-priority (footer/utility/social) links were deprioritized and not probed.
- All sampled links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_140172`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`) — tracking parameter structure is intact and consistent across CTAs.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 20 tracking/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) are served over plain HTTP, not HTTPS — likely to be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would break impression tracking silently.
- Same 20 pixels plus 4 content images (banner, TV stand product image, rewards banner, "sales-we-tell-our-friends" image) and the loyalty disclaimer image are all missing `alt` text — screen reader users get no fallback content, and no context is shown if images fail to load.
- No layout/DOM-breakage issues detected in the truncated source; media-query scaffolding (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, etc.) is standard and intact.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens detected in the source or QA output.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may simply be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than its absence at origin; cannot confirm compliance status either way.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) also not found — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — deliverability/authentication status cannot be verified from this relay capture.
- An in-body unsubscribe link was included per QA (always probed) but was among the deprioritized/unprobed set, so its destination status is unconfirmed.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair uses its own proprietary tracking schema instead of standard UTM params (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — e.g. `source=graymatter_batch&refid=MKTEML_140172&sltid=8&brcid=1`. This is consistent site-wide practice, not a defect, but standard UTM-based attribution tooling won't pick these up.

No mismatch detected.

7. Recommendations
- Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent silent tracking loss in TLS-enforcing clients.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 content images and the loyalty disclaimer image for accessibility/broken-image fallback.
- Re-probe the 429-rate-limited links (onelink.me app CTA, daily-sales, homepage) and the 4 unprobed filter links outside the rate-limit window to confirm CTA validity, given the primary CTA is among the unconfirmed set.
