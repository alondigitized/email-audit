# Move. Quick. SHOWER CURTAINS, in stock now >>
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing I notice: the hero is just... gone. Blank white space where the actual "shower curtains in stock now" imagery should be, with what looks like a broken-image placeholder. For an email whose entire subject line is built around urgency — "Move. Quick." — showing up to a dead hero is almost funny. It's the digital equivalent of a "back in 5 minutes" sign taped to a locked door.
- Once you scroll past the void, it's just... Wayfair being Wayfair. A wall of category tiles — shower curtains, bathroom stuff under $50, Adirondack chairs, wallpaper, home bars, daybeds, dog crates, bird baths, air fryers, grill covers. I'm mid-renovation on two houses right now and there's furniture in here I'd actually look at (the nightstands section is decent, the outdoor fireplace tile catches my eye), but it's buried in an inventory dump that has nothing to do with the subject line I clicked on.
- Verdict: I opened it because "shower curtains, in stock now" sounded like a real, specific need (I do actually need shower curtains for the guest bath in the second house), got a broken image for my trouble, then a generic 40-tile catalog. Marking as read, not forwarding. This is the fourth-ish Wayfair email this week and it's blurring into the last three.

## What's working

- The "bedside best friend" nightstand module is genuinely styled — real room shot, warm tone, clear "shop wooden picks / shop metal loves" split. That's the one section that feels like an actual campaign, not a spreadsheet.
- Wayfair Rewards module (5% on every item) is visible and simple — I know I'm a member, I know what I get.
- Category tile copy is at least specific ("In-Stock Shower Curtains," "Top-Rated Air Fryer Ovens") rather than vague "Shop Now" mush.

## What's weak

- The hero is straight-up broken — blank space, no shower curtain image, no product shot backing up the subject line at all.
- The subject promises shower curtains urgency; the body buries that single tile as one of 40+ generic category links (bird baths, dog crates, air fryer ovens?). Crate & Barrel's "Beautiful & bestselling for every room" send stayed disciplined across five categories — this just keeps scrolling with no editorial hand on it.
- "70% OFF EPIC FALL SALE" — the one real offer in this whole email — is crammed at the bottom next to a Delta co-brand card logo, which means it's probably card-gated, not a straight discount. That's the kind of buried/bait-y fine print Article and CB2 have both been guilty of too.
- Zero renovation-specific content I actually care about — no swatch/sample program, no shipping-time clarity for furniture, no showroom tie-in. Pottery Barn's dining table email at least segmented by seat count; this is just noise.
- ALL CAPS on "SHOWER CURTAINS" in the subject reads spammy for a brand I already trust — Wayfair doesn't need to shout.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix or replace the broken hero image before anything else ships** — right now the subject's entire promise (shower curtains, in stock, act fast) has zero visual payoff. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Cut the tile count by half and lead with what the subject promised — shower curtains and adjacent bath refresh — instead of drifting into dog crates and air fryers three tiles later.
- 3. Move the 70% off Fall Sale banner up top and clarify whether it's Delta-card-gated or sitewide — right now it reads like the good offer is hidden on purpose.
- 4. Borrow the nightstand module's format (one styled room shot, two clear sub-CTAs) for the shower curtain hero instead of a flat product grid.
