# ENDS TOMORROW ⏰ Up to 60% off the Labor Day Event →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Fourth one from this brand family today, and this is the one I actually clocked as "not for me" the fastest — it's baby dressers, cribs, and nursery gliders. I don't have a kid. This is Crate & Kids, the toddler-furniture spinoff of Crate & Barrel, and it landed in the same inbox folder where I'm saving my actual dining table and console picks. That's the whole problem in one sentence.
- The countdown timer up top ("01:07:28:53" — I've seen that clock face on three other Crate & Barrel/CB2 emails today) and the "Double Rewards" banner are the only things that feel like a repeat beat I recognize. Everything else — crib mattresses, changing tables, "nursery chairs" — is just noise scrolling past on my phone.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to razz a friend who just had a baby, and even then it'd be "lol Crate & Barrel thinks I have a kid now."

## What's working

- The purple dresser hero image is genuinely nice-looking product photography, if I were in the market.
- Price comparisons (sale price vs. reg price, e.g. "$799.20 Reg $999") are shown clearly under each product — that's honest math, better than Wayfair's vague "60% OFF" banner with no baseline.
- "Design Desk" free-consultation callout mid-email is a decent trust/service angle I haven't seen CB2 or Pottery Barn lean on this hard.
- Countdown timer creates some urgency, at least visually.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — nursery furniture, cribs, and gliders are 100% irrelevant to my renovation project.
- This is the fourth Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox today (I already flagged the "clock's ticking" swivel-chair send twice) — total fatigue, and now the family brand is spilling into a completely different life stage.
- No indication anywhere that this is a "sub-brand" send until you scroll and see the Crate&Kids logo — the sender name says it, but the countdown/rewards banner styling is identical to regular Crate & Barrel, so on a first glance I thought it was the same sale I already dismissed.
- Endless product grid (five separate "up to X% off" sections) makes we scroll forever for zero relevant categories.
- The persistent countdown timer gimmick is losing all urgency for me at this point — it's the same clock face I've now seen across two different sub-brands today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list. If Crate & Barrel/CB2 knows I've never clicked a nursery or kids product, suppress the Crate & Kids sends entirely — that's a targeting fix, not a copy fix, and it's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Stop reusing the identical countdown-timer red banner across every sub-brand — it's the fourth time today and it now reads as noise rather than urgency.
- 3. Lead subject lines with the sub-brand name so people without kids can filter/mute at a glance instead of opening to find out.
- 4. Cut the product grid from five sections to two — nobody scrolls through 20 SKUs from a phone.
