# Setting the standard since 2018
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. That's about the seventh send in five days, and I knew it before I opened it. The subject, "Setting the standard since 2018," has nothing to do with what's inside. The email is a Goop collaboration push, with a pink curved sofa under a big "STILL THE REFERENCE" headline. The subject reads like a brand-anniversary line, and 2018 isn't even a milestone worth celebrating. That's a miss on the one line that decides whether I open it.
- Inside, it's better than the last few. After the 9/30 "Holiday Collection" send and the 10/1 "New arrivals are here" send, which both led with the same "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off" banner, this one has no sale banner. It looks like an editorial moodboard: the blue Gwyneth swivel chair, Nadine bouclé chair, Melange sofa, and a Hemisphere lamp. It's the kind of thing I'd drop into my Pinterest-adjacent folder for the second home. But I'm not going to forward it, because there's no price on any Goop piece and no reason to act today. I'd skim it, save a screenshot of the Nadine chair, and archive it.
- I'd click once, on the swatch link, because I'm mid-renovation and fabric samples are the real friction. I wouldn't click the hero.

## What's working

- The "STILL THE REFERENCE" hero is clean, and the single "Shop the exclusive Goop collection" button is obvious.
- The "You won't find them anywhere else" line gives me a reason it's not the same lamp I saw at Wayfair.
- "Don't see your fabric of choice? Order 10 free swatches" is the first useful thing a CB2 email has told me all week. It's what I need for color matching across two houses.
- The Rewards callout ("Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars, valid at CB2 and Crate & Barrel") feeds my card-and-rewards math.
- The "More for you" grid has visible prices: Foley velvet counter stool, Vera fringe ottoman, Berkeley sofa. That's where my eye went after the Goop section.

## What's weak

- The subject has nothing to do with the email. A Goop collab is the story, and "since 2018" doesn't say so.
- Not one Goop piece has a price. For furniture that's a shipping-cost-and-budget decision, and I can't tell what a Nadine chair costs without clicking.
- The Goop section is long, and the hero and the second "Shop the Goop Collection" button repeat each other. After that comes an app download, then lighting, then "More for you." It's a scroll with five different asks.
- The "More for you" picks have nothing to do with the Goop story, and I can't tell what they're based on. Bed, bar stool, sofa and ottoman feel like random inventory rather than anything about me.
- Nothing about shipping or lead times. Upholstery is the longest wait in a renovation, and the email says nothing about it.
- The footer's "15% off when you sign up" is an odd offer to show a subscriber who's already on the list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject about the Goop collab, which is the actual content. Naming the collaboration and one piece gives me a reason to open it.
- 2. Put prices and "ships in X weeks" under each Goop piece. A $1,499 chair and a $3,999 sofa are different decisions. Pottery Barn's "Cozy Throws Ship Free" hero at least put the offer in the first line.
- 3. Move the swatch offer up, right under the hero. It's the strongest practical hook, and I'd like it before the third product.
- 4. Cut one of the two Goop buttons and the app block, so the email makes one ask.
- 5. Show a deadline or stock signal if there is one. Right now nothing says why I should act today and not next week.
- 6. Break up the cadence. Seven near-identical sends in five days is why I'm snoozing the whole Crate & Barrel family. Article's bedroom story felt like a considered send, not a daily one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has widespread accessibility gaps (24 images with no `alt`) and 11 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the QA run did not capture the headers and the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **72 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so the QA run skipped the HTTP probe. Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 11 images load over `http://` from Dotomi (a third-party ad/retargeting vendor):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and they leak the recipient's request in cleartext. They are also cookie-sync redirects, which some clients and privacy filters strip.
- The first-party pixels are HTTPS: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/46b6d99a-…?mi_cid=…`. Neither has `alt`, and both carry long identifying query strings (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **24 images lack `alt` text.**
  - 11 are content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`: `d0658b56…`, `c3ab335c…`, `fb4629e6…`, `4b9fa27a…`, `57fa3441…`, `c8390aba…`, `7ae5c4cf…`, `4d2665f0…`, `1f394f1c…`, `badb0ec1…` and `9685411b…`. With images blocked, or with a screen reader, these have no text equivalent.
  - 13 are tracking pixels (Dotomi ×11, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/…`). For these, `alt=""` is the right fix so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless to rendering but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Non-standard font hosting.** Several `@font-face` rules pull from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain. The `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/…` URLs contain a double slash. Fallback stacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present, so the impact is limited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL: raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is in the delivered HTML.** The `%%…%%` syntax looks like an ESP personalization string, probably a product-reviews or SKU-list block that didn't resolve. The recipient may see literal token text or an empty or broken module. I can't see the surrounding markup, because it falls in the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is inconclusive, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, again probably due to relay capture.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address are unverified.** The supplied source is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 72 click-redirect links were skipped and the HTML was truncated, so no final landing URLs or UTM parameters are visible. The only parameters observed are the `mi_*` tracking IDs on the pixels.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the reviews/SKU data source and add a fallback so the module is hidden if it can't resolve.
2. **Add `alt` text to the 11 content images.** Add `alt=""` to all 13 tracking pixels.
3. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`,** or confirm the vendor supports it. If it doesn't, drop the pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Re-run compliance checks against the raw message.** Use an original `.eml` or seed-list test to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, and the footer unsubscribe link and address.
6. **Resolve and probe the redirect chains** to confirm landing pages and UTM parameters.
7. **Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` template comment.**
