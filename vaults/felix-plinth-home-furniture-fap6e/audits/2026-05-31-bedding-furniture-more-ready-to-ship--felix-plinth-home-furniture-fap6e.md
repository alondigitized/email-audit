# Bedding, furniture & more ready to ship!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Four Pottery Barn emails in thirty-six hours. I'm saying that number out loud because I'm not exaggerating — I've already got the outdoor furniture sale, the Coastal Washed Quilt, the table linen refresh, AND the "All bedding ships free" send from earlier *this morning* sitting in my archive. This is now the second Pottery Barn email today alone. I noticed. My inbox noticed. My brain is filing everything with a PB header under a mental label called "do later," and "do later" is where clicks go to die.
- That said — strip away the cadence problem and there's a real email here. "Ready-to-Ship Furniture & More" is the right concept for someone like me who is mid-renovation on two properties and terrified of the six-week backordered-sofa situation I lived through in 2024. The split hero showing bedding on the left and a living room setup on the right is smart coverage — it's touching both active projects simultaneously. And the "Recommended Just For You" block mid-email actually shows sofas in colorways that track with things I've been looking at. That's CRM muscle at work. I see it, I appreciate it.
- But I'm marking this as read without clicking. Not because the email failed — because I opened the outdoor furniture sale two days ago, bookmarked three things, and still haven't converted. Sending me a fifth door-knock doesn't change my purchase timeline. What would move me today is a "your saved items are ready to ship" trigger. That email I'd click before finishing coffee.

## What's working

- **"Ready-to-Ship" hero concept is a real purchase trigger** — availability anxiety is real for furniture; this framing directly answers the one question I have before committing to big pieces.
- **Split lifestyle hero images** — bedding left, living room right; covers both renovation contexts without forcing me to dig. Efficient.
- **"Recommended Just For You" mid-email block** — actual sofa options in neutral colorways visible in the personalization row. This doesn't feel like a random product dump; the CRM is earning its keep here.
- **Loyalty reminder in footer** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" is a low-friction nudge that lands correctly for someone who routes every furniture purchase through their highest-rewards card.
- **Clean render, zero bugs** — compared to Wayfair's recent campus-dorm fiasco, this is technically airtight. Every image loaded, text is readable even at thumbnail size.

## What's weak

- **The second email today is one too many** — "All bedding ships free" already covered the bedding category this morning. When Crate & Barrel ran the same outdoor furniture email twice in one week I clocked it immediately; I'm doing the same thing here, except this is within *hours*, not days.
- **Subject line is a laundry list** — "Bedding, furniture & more" is three categories and an ampersand. That's the email copywriting equivalent of "misc." There's no editorial point of view in that subject; it's just a table of contents.
- **"Ready to Ship" needs a deadline** — is this window two weeks? All summer? Evergreen? The urgency implied by "ready to ship" has no hard date behind it. Article's "last chance" sends from last week did this correctly — a specific cutoff creates action. Vague availability phrasing creates a bookmark.
- **No pricing in the product grid** — I can see furniture silhouettes and lifestyle images but no "from $X" anchors anywhere above the fold. CB2 put pricing context front and center in their "Now & Future Classics on Sale" email. Without it, this is browsing bait, not a conversion driver.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress this send for anyone who already opened the bedding email from this morning** — two sends in three hours covering the same category is a suppression logic failure, not a creative problem. Fix the segment, not the template.
- 2. **Lead with my most-browsed category in the subject** — if the CRM knows I've been looking at sofas, "Your sofa is ready to ship" is a 9. "Bedding, furniture & more" is a 4. Use the data you clearly have.
- 3. **Add a soft deadline** — even "In-stock through June 15" creates friction reduction. "Ready to ship" without a window is catalog copy.
- 4. **Surface starting prices in the product grid** — one line per category block. Article does this; it removes the "click to find out" tax that kills conversion.
- 5. **Make the personalized block the hero** — the "Recommended Just For You" section is buried below the standard grid. Flip the order. Lead with what you know about me, then give me the full catalog below.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your sofa ships now — no backorder wait`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ready-to-ship furniture, in stock this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No six-week wait. These pieces leave the warehouse now — and free shipping always applies.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `In stock, ready to go. See what's shipping this week in furniture and bedding.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed content — 3 HTTP image sources** (will be blocked by most modern email clients on HTTPS-enforcing connections):
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...` (open-pixel tracker — also HTTP)

**Missing alt text — ~50 images** including all 17 main content slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_17.jpg`), all 18 footer slices, product images (beds, towels, curtain rod bracket, bedding), and bottom banner images. With images off (Outlook default, many corporate clients), the email renders as a blank white block.

**PHP header injection artifact in `<style>` block:**
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
Raw PHP `header()` calls appear inside a `<style>` tag. This is a server-side template rendering failure — the PHP was not executed and leaked into the HTML output. While it won't crash rendering, it is junk text injected into the style block and indicates a template compilation error.

**`<title>` is empty** — no email title set, reduces accessibility for screen readers.

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta** — suppresses pinch-zoom for accessibility users on mobile.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** — QA relay did not detect this header. If absent at delivery, this violates Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced Feb 2024) and is a CAN-SPAM risk. Needs verification against raw SMTP headers, not the relay snapshot.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) appears unsupported. Required for bulk senders to Gmail/Yahoo.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; authentication should be verifiable but was not surfaced through the AgentMail relay.

**Plain-text body is 71.5% URLs** (10,397 / 14,545 chars) — exceeds the 70% threshold that triggers spam filter heuristics. Plain-text version needs to be regenerated with meaningful content, not raw link dumps.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All navigation goes through `click.e.potterybarn.com` click-tracking redirects which may inject UTM on redirect — this is unverifiable without probing the redirect chain.
