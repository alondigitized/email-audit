# STEALS OF THE WEEK
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-seven. That's the CB2 count in my July inbox, and at this point I could write these reviews in my sleep because CB2 basically writes the same email every two days. This one is at least honest about being a roundup — four sale banners stacked (Furniture Event 40%, Bedroom & Bath 30%, Outdoor 50%, Clearance 60%) — but it's also literally the same categories I got in "UP TO 30% OFF BEDROOM & BATH" on 7/24 and "THE FURNITURE EVENT IS ON" on 7/23, just mashed into one scroll instead of four separate sends.
- The one thing that actually stopped my thumb was the Outdoor section — teal bistro chair, marble side table, that green foliage backdrop. I'm doing the patio at the second home and that's a real maybe-click. Everything else is wallpaper at this point; my eye just skims past "UP TO X% OFF [CATEGORY]" banners now because CB2 has trained me to.
- Would I click? Only on Outdoor, and only because I haven't sourced patio seating yet. Forward to a friend? No — nothing here is a "look at this" moment, it's a "here's the whole store on sale" moment, which is the opposite of what makes me forward something. Marking as read and moving on, same as the last three from this brand.

## What's working

- The Outdoor banner (50% off) actually hit — teal chair + marble side table is exactly my patio-furnishing itch right now, unlike C&B's endless bedroom/bath pushes.
- Clean visual hierarchy on each banner — big percentage, category label, hero photo, nothing competing for attention.
- "More For You" product grid at the bottom has real prices and real product names (Andes Acacia Bed, Foley Mink Stool) instead of vague category tiles — that's more useful than most of what Pottery Barn sends.
- The rewards note ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars... on all things CB2 and Crate & Barrel") is at least member-relevant since I know these two brands share a loyalty program — Article doesn't even have this lever to pull.

## What's weak

- Four sale banners in one email is the same fatigue problem as four emails in one week — it's just compressed, not fixed. I already saw "40% off Furniture Event" and "30% off Bedroom & Bath" as their own dedicated sends days ago.
- No swatch or fabric-sample CTA anywhere, and I'm mid-decision on upholstery for two houses right now — Article's living room email at least showed me tailoring/customization options, this doesn't.
- "Clearance up to 60% off" with a mustard sofa is the vaguest CTA in the email — no product name, no link path implied beyond "guess and click."
- The review-for-$1,000 CTA ("LEAVE US A REVIEW, WIN $1,000") reads like a sweepstakes bait tactic dropped mid-scroll — feels like filler between the sale banners and the product grid.
- Nothing here differentiates this from the last two CB2 sends other than the percentages moved around — same fonts, same crop style, same wood tones.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the multi-banner roundup format** — if Outdoor is the strongest hook for a furniture-renovating audience like me, lead the whole email with it instead of burying it third. One hero offer beats four diluted ones.
- 2. Cut the four-banner sale stack down to two max, and use the freed space for something CB2 never sends me: a shipping-timeline callout for furniture ("arrives in 2-3 weeks") — I have to guess this every time I order case goods.
- 3. Drop or reposition the $1,000 review sweepstakes — it's disconnected from the shopping flow and reads as desperate filler.
- 4. Give the Clearance sofa an actual product name and price like the "More For You" grid does — inconsistent treatment within the same email is a miss.
- 5. Space this out from the 7/23 and 7/24 sends — three CB2 emails in four days on overlapping categories is why I've stopped reading the banners at all.

- **Subject Alt B:** `This week only: furniture, bedroom, outdoor, all discounted`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New outdoor pieces, marked down through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your rewards stack with every sale this week`
