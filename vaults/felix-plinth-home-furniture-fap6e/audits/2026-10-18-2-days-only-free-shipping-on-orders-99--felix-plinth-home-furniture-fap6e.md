# 2 days only! Free shipping on orders $99+ 🏃‍♀️💨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel send in two days, and it's the Kids sender, so I almost didn't open it. The earlier "Free shipping starts today!" email already gave me the $99 threshold, and this one repeats it with a countdown timer. I'm renovating two grown-up houses, and the hero is a jack-o'-lantern pumpkin spilling plush toys. That's not my project.
- Past the hero, the email is a long scroll of bedding, toys, lighting, decor and storage tiles, then The Design Desk, a gift section, strollers and a credit card promo. The Design Desk and the lighting tile are the only parts that speak to me. I'd skim it, maybe save the Design Desk image to my Pinterest folder, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (03:15:44:38) under "The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event: Up to 50% off" creates urgency that reads as real.
- "2 days only! Free shipping on orders $99+" is a concrete offer, with "Standard shipping, exclusions apply" right under it.
- The Design Desk section ("our Design Desk pros can help, it's 100% free") is the one thing aimed at someone doing a full renovation. A free design consult is something I'd actually use.
- The bedroom photo in the Design Desk block is a warm, styled room with a rust and neutral palette. I'd pin that.
- The category tiles (bedding, lighting, decor, storage) are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero is Halloween plush toys and a pumpkin. It's a nursery and kids pitch, not for a 36-year-old furnishing two houses.
- The CTA buttons ("Shop All Free Shipping", "Shop Now", "Shop 300+ Gifts") are pale grey. They're low-contrast and easy to miss, and they blend into the page.
- It's very long and has too many competing sections. The free-shipping offer gets buried under the tiles, then the Design Desk, then the gifts, strollers and a card promo.
- The Design Desk appears twice, near the top and again near the bottom, which feels padded.
- Some of the heirloom/gifts banner text is hard to read over its background image.
- I get no furniture shipping clarity. Does free shipping at $99 cover a dresser or a bed, or only small items?

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send. Don't send the Kids-branded version to people who've only browsed grown-up rooms. Lead with the bedding and lighting tiles and drop the pumpkin and plush hero for non-parents.
- 2. Make the free-shipping offer the only hero, with a dark, high-contrast button.
- 3. Say what free shipping covers, such as "free shipping on furniture over $99" or "small items only."
- 4. Cut the page by half. One Design Desk block, one product row, one footer.
- 5. Add a swatch or sample link in the bedding section.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping on $99+ ends in 3 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bedding up to 50% off, plus free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Semi-Annual Bedroom Event: save on bedding, lighting and decor before Tuesday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free design help included. See what ships free over $99.`
- Compared with the others in my inbox: Pottery Barn's "Colorful pillows, shipping free" opened on one product category I could act on. Wayfair's pergola emails at least stayed in the same lane all week. CB2's "THE WAREHOUSE SALE IS BACK" put the offer front and center. Crate & Barrel Kids puts a pumpkin first.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with a responsive media-query layer, but about 19 content images have no alt text and the viewport and text-size settings block zoom. The compliance and authentication checks are unverified because AgentMail didn't capture the headers. The supplied HTML is truncated, so the footer and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking not verified.** 75 links were skipped because they are tracking or click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were probed. Broken redirects and expired links can't be ruled out.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five identical LiveRamp pixels load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. They differ only in `n`. Each is an extra third-party request, and the repeats look redundant.
- **Per-recipient ID in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` appears 6 times, and the same `mi_u` value is in the `o.gif` open pixel. This is expected for Movable Ink, but it means each image URL carries a recipient identifier.
- **Two open or measurement pixels.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/447f062b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are both present.
- **No broken links were confirmed.**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (about 19).**
  - 17 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `6400e4fb-…gif`, `51edcafd-…jpg`, `30cf0d67-…gif` and `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - 2 are on `s7d5.scene7.com`: `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - If these images are blocked, the email loses its content, and screen readers read out filenames.
  - The spacer should use `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixels without alt (about 13).** These are the `f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif` and `dv.…` images. They should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`), so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing disabled.** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` rule and the `html`/`body` `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules stop users scaling text. The global `line-height:100%` also forces tight leading on all elements.
- **Invalid markup.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - There are duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv Content-Type` declarations.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with partly conflicting values: one combined tag, then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and "IMPUT" is a typo. It is harmless to rendering but shows a template placeholder was not cleaned out.
- **Selector conflict.** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` is a global rule that can override paragraph alignment in clients that honor `<style>` blocks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the supplied HTML. The `mi_u` parameters are resolved values. The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, not confirmed defects.** The QA check flags these three:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found.

- All three are described by the checker as likely relay-capture limitations. This audit can't confirm that the sender lacks them.
- **Footer unverified.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML.
- **Sender domain.** The `From` address (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`) is a subdomain consistent with the image host (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`). That's a good alignment signal, but it doesn't prove authentication.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unable to verify.** All 75 links were skipped as tracking redirects. There is no resolved destination, UTM set or landing-page data to evaluate.
- **Redirect chain.** Destinations should be resolved so UTM persistence and landing-page alignment can be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 17 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images and the GiftGuide gif. Use `alt=""` on the spacer and all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`, and drop the global `*{line-height:100%}`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags, and delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Consolidate or justify the five repeated `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels (`n=1`–`5`).
5. Re-run the header checks on the raw `.eml` or the original message source. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) there.
6. Resolve the 75 tracking redirects, using a follow-redirects probe or the ESP's link report, to check for broken destinations and UTM carry-through.
7. Re-audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
