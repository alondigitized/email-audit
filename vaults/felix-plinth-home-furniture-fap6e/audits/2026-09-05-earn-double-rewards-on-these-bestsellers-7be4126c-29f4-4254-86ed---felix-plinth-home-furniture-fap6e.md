# Earn DOUBLE REWARDS on these bestsellers →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is at least Crate & Barrel proper, not another Crate & Kids nursery blast — after three of those today I'll take it. And honestly, this is the right category for me right now: sofas, dining tables, bedroom sets, a whole "Double Rewards + up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" banner running the length of the email. I'm mid-renovation on two houses, so a wall-to-wall furniture sale with a rewards multiplier is exactly the kind of email I should stop scrolling for.
- But it's SO long. I counted like eight separate "shop bestsellers" sections stacked on top of each other — living room, bedroom, dining room, kitchen, lighting, decor — before it even gets to the actual sale grid. It reads like they merged three separate campaigns into one email and just kept scrolling. By the time I hit the actual "up to 60% off" pricing tiles two-thirds of the way down, I've already thumb-scrolled past a design magazine's worth of styled room shots.
- Verdict: I'd open it (Crate & Barrel is a brand I actually buy from), I'd probably jump straight to the sale grid near the bottom instead of reading top to bottom, and I might click through if I see the dining table category tile. Not forwarding this one — nothing here is surprising enough to send to a friend, unlike if they'd led with something like CB2's "rugs on sale" hook, which is a one-beat message I could forward in two seconds.

## What's working

- Lead banner is unambiguous: "Double Rewards' + Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event," dated through September 9 — that's a real deadline, not vague urgency.
- The category mix (living room, bedroom, dining room, kitchen) actually maps to what I'm shopping for across two renovations — better hit rate than the last Crate & Kids sends which were all nursery gliders and cribs.
- Star ratings and quoted customer reviews ("the quality craftsmanship is amazing!", "beautiful, solid, durable") are woven into the product blocks — that's the social proof Article and CB2 usually skip.
- The sale grid near the bottom breaks it out by category with specific percentages (up to 40% off seating, up to 30% off bedding) instead of one blanket number, which is more honest than Pottery Barn's flat "Extra 20% off" I've now seen six times this week.

## What's weak

- It's exhausting — this is one of the longest promotional emails I've opened all week, easily double the length of the CB2 or Article sends, and the actual discount math doesn't show up until deep into the scroll.
- Redundant structure: "bestsellers made even better," then another bedroom bestsellers block, then dining room bestsellers, then kitchen — it's the same "Shop X Bestsellers" button repeated five or six times with different photos.
- The Double Rewards mechanic ("Earn $XX-$XXX in Reward Dollars") is teased at the very top and then not really explained again until way down — I still don't know the exact tiers without doing math myself.
- The Design Desk / $1,000 sweepstakes block at the very bottom feels bolted on, like a second unrelated email got appended to this one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to a third of its length — pick ONE hero category (with two renovations going, dining room or bedroom would hit hardest) and put the sale percentages directly under the hero banner, not eight scrolls down. CB2's "rugs on sale, so worth it" email works because it's one idea, well executed.
- 2. Move the Double Rewards tier breakdown ($X spend = $Y reward) up near the top banner so I don't have to hunt for the math — Article's "up to 40% off ends Monday" banner gets this kind of clarity right immediately.
- 3. Cut the sweepstakes/Design Desk block or split it into its own send — it dilutes the sale message and reads like clutter after an already-long scroll.
- 4. Add swatch/fabric or dimension callouts on at least the hero product — I'm buying furniture for two spaces right now and "beautiful, solid, durable" as a review quote doesn't tell me if the sofa comes in performance fabric.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bedroom & dining room upgrade: Double Rewards is on`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn $20-$300 in Reward Dollars now through Labor Day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, dining sets & bedroom favorites — up to 60% off inside.`
