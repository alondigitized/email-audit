# Time to shop: Up to 30% off top toys for Black Friday!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Kids again. This is the eighth Crate & Barrel Kids send in about three days, and I still don't have a nursery. I'm renovating two houses and shopping sofas, lighting and rugs. This one is a long column of toys: a ball pit, a wooden train set, an art easel, a play tent, a workbench and a rocking horse.
- The layout is clean. Each product gets its own block with a price and a single "Shop Now" button, and the "Black Friday is on! Up to 30% off toys" hero is clear. The rendered image is tiny, so I can only read the headlines, but the structure is easy to follow. It just isn't for me.
- I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. The only thing I might screenshot is the grid of "up to 50% off Black Friday deals" tiles near the bottom, because it hints at furniture and bedding.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: "Up to 30% off toys" with a Shop the Sale button, so I understood the offer in a second.
- Each product has its own block with a sale price over the regular price, so the discount math is visible.
- The product photography is warm and styled, with holiday-tree context. It reads like a real catalog.
- The "up to 50% off Black Friday deals" grid near the bottom is the first thing that felt in my lane: seating, bedding and rugs.
- The free-shipping banner at the top is a trust cue, and I like shipping clarity on big items.

## What's weak

- Almost every module is kids' toys. I have no kids in this renovation, and this is the eighth send in this stream.
- The email is extremely long, with six full product blocks before any furniture. Pottery Barn Sale's "Shop early, save BIG" puts the discount and the category up top and keeps it tighter.
- The best content is buried. The 50%-off furniture grid and the "sign up, save 10%" box sit far down the page. I'd never scroll there from the toy hero.
- It repeats the earlier "Black Friday: Up to 30% off toys that top their list!" send from the same day. It's the same toys with a slightly different subject.
- There's no rewards or loyalty angle and no swatch, sample or color-matching content. I buy on my highest-rewards card and want that math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by category. I've gotten seven kids sends and zero nursery purchases. Suppress me from kids, or lead with the furniture and bedding deals when I haven't engaged with toys.
- 2. Cut the toy blocks from six to three and move the 50% furniture grid up.
- 3. Collapse same-day Black Friday sends into one. The 30%-off toys email shouldn't go out twice in a day.
- 4. Add a cardholder or rewards line, plus a clear furniture delivery estimate.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style (MI) XHTML template with a valid `lang="en"` and a Outlook/VML fallbacks. The QA run scored 57% with 3 warnings and 0 failures. The main confirmed issues are about 49 images flagged as missing alt text and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **100 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations or HTTP statuses were verified.
- **Repeated pixels.**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with the same URL.
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` are 5 third-party (LiveRamp) pixels.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d24d5580-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are further tracking pixels.
  - Together these are about 13 tracking requests in one message. The duplicates add load and image-blocking exposure without adding signal.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in pixel URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa278…` and `s=56aaa278…` values are sent to a third-party domain (`rlcdn.com`). This is worth confirming against the sender's privacy disclosures.
- **Non-semantic spacer.** `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F50px%5FWhite` is a hosted spacer image on a separate CDN, which adds an extra request and an alt warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 49 images.** QA flagged content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, for example:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `d3119bec-89fb-46f7-89b2-61061734f3a7.jpg`
  - `c1cafb52-714b-4aa2-98ef-fb9aa59481cb.gif`
  
  If these images carry the product and offer content, the email is unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader. The check reports "missing", so I can't tell from this data whether the tracking pixels have `alt=""` or no `alt` attribute at all.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Generic `<title>`.** It reads `Crate & Barrel` with an unescaped `&`. It doesn't match the subject line, and the ampersand is invalid in XHTML.
- **Duplicate and leftover markup.**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values.
  - A template placeholder comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - There is an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!-- --><!--<![endif]-->` block.
- **Stylesheet bloat.** `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules are defined twice in separate `<style>` blocks. A global `*{max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` is also applied.
- **Narrow mobile breakpoint.** The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports between 461px and 639px get no mobile rules.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so I can't check the body for table semantics, button markup, `role="presentation"`, or preheader handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[[…]]`. The body was truncated, so the personalization check is incomplete.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them. It is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect. For bulk senders, Gmail and Yahoo require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain. Its alignment can't be verified here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address** are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm or deny them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 100 links are click-tracked redirects, so the final destination and any UTM parameters weren't resolved. No landing page URL is exposed in the captured data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text.** Add descriptive `alt` to every content image, and `alt=""` to decorative images, spacers and tracking pixels.
2. **Viewport.** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. **Tracking pixels.** Deduplicate the 6 `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests and the 5 `rlcdn.com` pixels, and confirm that sending hashed identifiers to LiveRamp is covered by the privacy policy.
4. **Head cleanup.** Escape `&` as `&amp;` in the `<title>` and make it meaningful. Merge the duplicate `<meta>` and `<style>` rules, and remove the placeholder comment.
5. **Headers.** Capture raw headers from the original delivery, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and align.
6. **Redirects.** Resolve a sample of the tracked redirects, for example with `curl -I -L`. This would verify the landing pages and UTM parameters, and check for broken or expired links.
7. **Source capture.** Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and body markup can be checked.
