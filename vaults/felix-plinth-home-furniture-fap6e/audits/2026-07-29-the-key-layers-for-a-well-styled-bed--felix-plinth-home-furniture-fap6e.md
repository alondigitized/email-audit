# The key layers for a well-styled bed
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "The Layered Bed" — this is at least the sixth or seventh Pottery Barn email in my inbox this week, right behind "Beautifully Crafted Quilts" from yesterday, and it's honestly hitting closer to what I actually need than most of the recent batch. My primary bedroom is one of the two active renovation zones right now, and a full "get the look" bedding layering guide — duvet, sheets, quilt, pillows, all shot on the same styled bed — is a real category for me, not filler.
- That said, it's also just... a lot of bed. Five nearly identical navy-and-white bedding shots stacked vertically before I even hit the cross-sell grid. Crate & Barrel's CB2 arm would've given me one hero shot and a "24 hours left" clock by now; this is pure catalog scroll with zero urgency anywhere until the Affirm financing blurb at the very bottom.
- I wouldn't click past a skim. I'd screenshot the ruffle-hem sheet shot for my wife's Pinterest-adjacent folder maybe, but I'm not forwarding this — there's no single decisive offer to rally around, just "up to 70% off, 100s of new deals" sitting in a gray banner I've seen phrased differently every time this brand emails me.

## What's working

- "Get the look: The Layered Bed" is a real content hook — it's the first PB send in a while that reads like a styling guide instead of a straight sale blast, and layering a guest bed is literally on my punch list.
- "Duvets Ship Free" as a standalone CTA under the hero is specific and useful — shipping cost is a real friction point for me on bedding-weight items.
- The Rewards module near the bottom (10% back, PB/Williams Sonoma/West Elm shared program) is genuinely relevant — I've got the PB card, and cross-brand point pooling is the kind of thing that actually nudges me toward one retailer over another.
- Product photography is consistent and clean across every module — no render bugs, no broken images, same navy/white/cream palette carried through six straight sections.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical dark bed vignettes in a row (Layered Bed, Ruffle Hems, Velvet Quilts, Antique Toile Duvets, Fringe Pillow) with no differentiation in layout — my eye stopped registering new information by shot number three.
- The top banner offer — "Summer Sale: Up to 70% off" — never resurfaces as a real number anywhere in the body. No "$X off duvets," no percentage tied to the actual products shown. Compare that to CB2's furniture event send I scored an 8 on — that one had a real countdown and a specific discount up top, not just a vague banner.
- "Recommended Just for You" is six SKUs — curtain rod, platform bed, quick-dry towel, area rug, linen curtain, duvet cover — with zero connective tissue to the bedding theme above it. Feels like a generic algo block bolted onto a curated email, the same whiplash I flagged on Wayfair's grab-bag kitchen mats send.
- No swatch or fabric-sample CTA anywhere, despite this being explicitly a "which texture/pattern" browsing email — velvet vs. toile vs. ruffle is exactly the decision where I'd want to order swatches before committing to a full duvet set.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the five sequential bed shots down to two or three max, and put a real number on the hero — "Duvets from $X" or "30% off bedding" — instead of the vague "up to 70% off" banner that's disconnected from the products below it.
- 2. Turn "Recommended Just for You" into an actual bedding cross-sell (nightstands, lamps, rugs that pair with *this* look) instead of an unrelated six-item grid — tie it back to the styling narrative you just spent five modules building.
- 3. Add a swatch/sample CTA on at least the velvet and toile modules — that's the natural next action for someone comparing four fabric options, and PB doesn't offer it anywhere in this send.
- 4. Move the Rewards/Affirm module higher — it's the most persona-relevant content in the whole email for someone mid-renovation financing two homes, and it's buried below three unrelated modules.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New bedding layers, up to 70% off duvets`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your guest bed needs this layering guide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Duvets ship free — plus 10% back with your Key Rewards card`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Velvet, toile, ruffle: shop the full layered-bed look`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "The key layers for a well-styled bed"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated HTML email (VML/MSO conditionals, table-based layout) sent via e.potterybarn.com/click.e.potterybarn.com infrastructure; core structure is sound, but automated QA shows a 50% pass rate driven by widespread missing alt attributes, mixed-content (http://) image sources, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers at the transport layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs route through the click-redirect wrapper `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7...` (opaque base64/encoded payload) rather than plain query strings — 58 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe, so final destination and status codes are unverified.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels present: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (Amobee/Turn data-management pixel) and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (open-tracking pixel) — both loaded over **http://**, not https.
- A second open-tracking beacon at `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/8f9c8470-...?mi_mid=...` uses https correctly.
- Plain-text alternative part is 71.7% URLs (10,470/14,609 chars) — indicates the plain-text version is likely just a flattened link dump rather than genuine text content, which can hurt spam-filter scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **50+ images missing `alt` text**, including all content images in the `slices_*.jpg` layout grids, product shots (bed, curtain rod, towel, rug, duvet), and footer banners. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will render this email as empty space with no fallback text.
- **3 images served over plain http://** (mixed content, may be blocked by mail clients enforcing https):
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- Malformed embedded code: the client `<style>` block contains literal PHP-style calls — `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...");` — sitting inside CSS. This is invalid CSS and a build artifact (looks like a leaked server-side snippet from an image-serving script that was pasted into the template); browsers will silently ignore it, but it's a template hygiene bug worth flagging to the ESP.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present and appropriate for a transactional/marketing email.
