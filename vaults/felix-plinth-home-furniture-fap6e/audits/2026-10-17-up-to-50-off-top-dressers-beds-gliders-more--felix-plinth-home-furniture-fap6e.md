# Up to 50% off top dressers, beds, gliders & more!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a nursery email sent to a guy who is furnishing two grown-up houses. The "up to 50% off furniture" hero is clear, and I'd probably click at least once if I had a kid's room on the list. But the whole thing is bunk beds, toddler beds, a glider recliner and a baby stroller. It's the third Crate & Barrel send in about 24 hours: the Samsung Frame TV and the dining-room send came earlier today, and the nightstand and Design Desk sends came yesterday. My brain is already in C&B snooze mode.
- The screenshot is tiny, so I'm going on structure and the headlines I can read. It is well built, though. There's a free-shipping-on-toys strip, a big offer headline, a sequence of single-product blocks with their own buttons, then clearance, wallcovering, baby gear, a Venus Williams collab, a "we love these for you" grid, the Crate card, an app promo and the Design Desk. That's a lot of modules for one send. Pottery Barn's holiday send had no offer at all, so at least this one leads with a number.
- Verdict: I'd skim and I wouldn't forward it. If the sale carried over to the Redondo 3-drawer dresser in natural oak, that's a possible guest-room piece and I'd screenshot it for the Pinterest folder. The rest is for someone else's life stage.

## What's working

- The "limited time only! up to 50% off furniture*" headline puts the offer first. The eye lands there before any product.
- Each hero product gets its own block with a name, a sale price and a "Shop Now" button. That's better than Wayfair's pergola wall, which was twelve near-identical photos in two grids.
- The Redondo natural-oak dresser block is the one I'd actually use. It's a warm wood tone that would sit fine in an adult bedroom.
- There are no render problems. Images load, nothing overlaps, and the buttons look tappable.
- The "up to 80% off clearance" block is a second, deeper-discount hook. It's a good fallback if the first offer doesn't land.

## What's weak

- The content is nearly all kid and baby: bunk bed, toddler bed, nursery glider, stroller. Nothing says it's for a renovator doing two houses.
- This is a third C&B send in a day or so. The 10/17 dining-room email was the first one that matched what I'm doing, and I scored it 8. This one gives that goodwill back.
- The "*" on the offer headline signals fine print, and "up to 50%" is carrying a lot of weight. I can't tell which of these items is actually at 50%.
- It's overloaded below the fold. Wallcovering, baby gear, Venus Williams, a recommendations grid, the credit card, an app promo and the Design Desk all follow the products. It reads like a catalog dump, not a send with one purpose.
- "Limited time only" has no date. I have no idea when this ends, so there's no real urgency.
- Nothing is said about furniture delivery windows or lead times. That's what I care about most for big pieces like beds and dressers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an end date on the offer ("ends Sunday") and spell out what the asterisk means. That would lift the most.
- 2. Cut the lower modules (card, app, wallcovering, Venus Williams) or move them into a separate send. Let the sale be the email.
- 3. Say in the body that the dresser and bed pieces work as guest or adult-room furniture. Or segment so I get the dining and bedroom furniture send instead of the nursery one.
- 4. Add delivery timing and a swatch or finish-sample link on the furniture blocks.
- 5. Space the sends out. Three C&B-family emails in about a day is too much.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The oak dresser, now on sale (plus 49 other picks)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on toys $49+, and clearance up to 80% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bunk beds, 3-drawer dressers and gliders, all marked down this weekend.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, Outlook-aware template (VML/MSO conditionals, `lang="en"`) built on Movable Ink. The main confirmed defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a few markup and CSS problems. Header-level compliance and authentication, link destinations and UTMs could not be verified from the data provided. QA reported a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **88 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **Five per-recipient pixels from a third-party ad-data domain.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp-style identity-sync pixels with a long `s=` token in the URL. They are also missing `alt`. They add third-party requests, and clients that block images or trackers will drop them.
- **Movable Ink and other tracking assets:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is an open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b3993086-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another tracking asset.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` repeats 6 times with the same `mi_u` token and filename. These may be dynamic images, and the data can't confirm what they render.
- **Image hosting is split across four domains.** These are `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`. It isn't a defect, but it is more points of failure.
- **A possibly stale asset.** `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` has a 0926 date in its filename. This email was sent 2026-10-17, so a reused creative is possible. I can't confirm it from the filename alone.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 45 images are missing `alt` (QA WARN).** The count is from my tally of the QA list.
  - About 22 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (for example `6cda691d-…jpg`, `478d5c14-…jpg`) plus `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`. If these are the product and promo images (likely, since the subject is dressers, beds and gliders), image-blocking clients and screen readers get no content.
  - The other `alt` warnings cover decorative and tracking images. These should carry `alt=""` to be explicit.
  - The scene7 image `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` appears 3 times as a spacer. CSS padding would avoid the extra image requests and layout shift when images are blocked.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom** (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Text-scaling is locked.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `body { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` both disable text-size adjustment. The universal selector also forces `line-height:100%` on everything.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.** That is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`. The title is also generic and doesn't reflect the subject.
- **Duplicate and conflicting head tags:**
  - `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" charset=utf-8>` is paired with `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
- **A template placeholder was left in production:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is a misspelled instruction comment, and the `<!----- MI styling end----->` comments are also malformed.
- **CSS ordering conflict.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code and images cap at 640px.
- **The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`.** Viewports from 461px to 639px get the desktop layout, with no image cap in that range. Examples are large phones in landscape and small tablets.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML was truncated, so heading semantics, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, link text and the footer could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source, and the `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are populated. The HTML is truncated, so tokens in the unseen portion are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found (QA WARN).** QA itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers. I can't confirm the sender omitted them. If they are absent at origin, this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement failure.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is unknown. This is a data-capture gap rather than a confirmed failure.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified** because the footer falls in the truncated section.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 88 click links are redirect-wrapped and were skipped, so final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "Up to 50% off dressers, beds, gliders" can't be checked. The visible source shows no UTM parameters on any URL. Any UTMs would be applied at the redirect layer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text to every content image** (the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/m/1/*.jpg` set), and add `alt=""` to the spacers, the `rlcdn.com` pixels, `o.gif` and the `mi.` and `dv.` assets.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and replace the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations with `100%`.
3. **Fix `<title>`** to `Crate &amp; Barrel` or a subject-aligned title, and remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` metas and the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. **Resolve the `img max-width` media-query conflict** by ordering the rules explicitly, and consider a breakpoint above 460px, such as 600px.
5. **Replace the repeated 40px white spacer images** with padding or height on the table cells.
6. **Re-run QA with the raw `.eml`** (full headers and untruncated HTML) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer unsubscribe link and address, and unwrapped link destinations with UTMs.
7. **Review the five `sr.rlcdn.com` per-recipient pixels** for necessity and disclosure, since they add third-party calls.
