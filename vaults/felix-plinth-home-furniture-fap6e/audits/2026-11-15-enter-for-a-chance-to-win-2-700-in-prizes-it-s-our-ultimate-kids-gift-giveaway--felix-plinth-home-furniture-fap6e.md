# Enter for a chance to win $2,700+ in prizes! It’s our Ultimate Kids Gift Giveaway →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- I'm 36 and renovating two houses, and I have no kids. This is a Crate & Barrel *Kids* email, so I'm not the audience. My gut reaction was "wrong aisle." It's also the third Crate & Barrel family send I've gotten today, after two kitchen emails, and yesterday's "last Double Rewards of the year" and dinnerware sends. My brain has C&B on permanent snooze.
- The hero is a gift-card giveaway, and I don't care about nursery brands. The best part for me is the bottom half. It has an "early black friday is here! up to 50% off*" block with furniture, bedding and rugs, plus the red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner. That's the one thing that matches how I shop, since I buy on the highest-rewards card. But it sits under a giveaway I'd never enter. I'd skim it, maybe tap into the rugs, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "$2,700+ in prizes" line and the "Enter Now" button are unmistakable, and the hero photo is warm and on-brand.
- The red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner with "Shop Top Categories" is the only loyalty and deadline cue that speaks to my buying habits.
- The Early Black Friday block is clean: four tiles with a plain "up to 50% off" for furniture, bedding and bath, and rugs, then one "Shop the Sale" button.
- The prize grid shows named brands (mushie, Super Smalls, bookroo, Banwood, Babiators) with dollar values. Concrete is good.
- The product rows ("we love these for you") show prices, so I know what I'm tapping into.

## What's weak

- The email runs very long, with giveaway, sale, rewards, Feeding America, nursery chairs, a product grid, a credit card, the app and DesignDesk. It's seven emails in one.
- The giveaway hero pushes the sale and rewards below the first screen, so the thing I'd actually act on is buried.
- Nothing here is for a renovator. The only furniture I see is nursery gear, and a stroller row is zero use to me.
- The small "up to 50% off*" asterisk and the dense legal block at the bottom make the offer feel less clean than it first looks.
- The top-strip boxes ("last Double Rewards this year!" and "Early Black Friday") are tiny at phone size and easy to miss. The red banner repeats the rewards message, so I get it twice.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sale and Double Rewards, with the giveaway as a secondary tile. The giveaway should be one send, not the headline over everything.
- 2. Cut the module count. Pick giveaway or sale as the primary and drop the nursery-chair, credit-card and app blocks.
- 3. Segment. Send this only to people with kids' or registry purchase history. Send me the home furniture version instead.
- 4. Dedupe cadence across the C&B family. Pottery Barn already hit me nine times in two days, and I don't need C&B at the same rate.
- 5. Put a deadline on the giveaway hero. The Nov 17 rewards date is the only clear deadline I can see.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards ends Nov 17 + a $2,700 giveaway`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Enter to win gift cards from 8 brands. Early Black Friday is on, up to 50% off furniture.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with `lang="en"` and mobile media queries. The QA run flagged about 45 images without alt text, header checks that couldn't be completed, and 85 tracked links that were not probed. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s could not be inspected directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** 85 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains, so none were checked for redirect chain, status code or final destination.
- **Third-party pixel (LiveRamp):** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are five separate 1×1 requests to a third-party domain, and none has alt text.
- **Shared identifier:** the 64-hex value in the `s=` parameter appears to match the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value on the first-party pixels (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`). If it is a hashed recipient identifier, it is being passed to a third party. I can't confirm what it encodes from the truncated data.
- **Duplicate pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` is requested 6 times with the same `mi_u`. There are also single open or tracking pixels at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/37bb43d1-…?mi_cid=…`.
- **Mixed image hosts:** most images are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, but one is on `s7d5.scene7.com` (`…2026%5F1104%5FCBK%5FQualityGifting…`). It uses a different domain from the sending domain and has a long, percent-encoded path.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 `<img>` elements were flagged. Roughly 28 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` (for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `3930ebb6-ec2c-4396-aa4c-fd7eb34265da.jpg`). Another is the scene7 image. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, those sections will have no text equivalent. The QA tool can't tell a missing `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`, so decorative images may be among them.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** the `rlcdn.com`, `mi.` and `dv.` pixels are flagged too. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity.
- **`<title>`:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype (it should be `&amp;`). It is also generic and does not match the campaign.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Leftover template markup:**
  - The placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was not removed.
  - Several meta tags are duplicated: `charset` appears twice, and `format-detection` appears three times with conflicting values.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, the Outlook VML/MSO conditionals and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `%%`, `{{ }}` or `[FNAME]` appear in the visible source. The footer and later body were truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed** and not a verified sender defect. Verify against the raw message from the original delivery.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.
- **Sender:** `From: crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain. That is consistent with the image and tracking hosts.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unable to assess. All 85 tracked links were skipped, and the `href`s and any UTM or `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters were not available. The only parameters confirmed in the source are the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` pixel parameters, which belong to the sender's tracking platform.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~28 content images, such as the 26 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Set `alt=""` on decorative images and tracking pixels.
2. Change `<title>` to `Crate &amp; Barrel`, or use the campaign title.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Remove the template placeholder comment and the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` meta tags.
5. Collapse the six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel requests into one. Review whether the five `rlcdn.com` pixels and the identifier shared with them are intended and covered by the privacy policy.
6. For the QA pipeline: capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay, and resolve tracking redirects to check destinations and UTMs. Otherwise the compliance and continuity checks remain unverifiable.
