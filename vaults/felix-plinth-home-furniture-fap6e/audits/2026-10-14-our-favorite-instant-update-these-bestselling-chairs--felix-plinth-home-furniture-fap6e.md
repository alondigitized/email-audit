# Our favorite instant update? These bestselling chairs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel email I've logged today. The main sender is back after sofas this morning, and the Kids sender has already given me the nursery event and two Doona sends. I'm renovating two houses, so chairs are on my list. But the subject says chairs and the banner at the top says "hours left: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals." That's the same deadline I got this morning. I opened expecting a chair email and got a sofa reminder first.
- Below the banner it turns into a good email. It's a long scroll of big, moody accent chairs, and each one has a customer quote ("already my family's favorite set of chairs", "we love it so much, we had to get a second one"). Social proof like that is useful when I'm picking between a boucle swivel and a leather lounge. I'd save this to my Pinterest folder. I wouldn't forward it, because the photos are the whole value and I'd rather send my wife a screenshot.
- Would I click? Probably one chair link, since the curvy swivel shots work on me. I wouldn't treat it as urgent, though. The urgency is about sofas, and the "fall deals edit" and the sale button near the bottom aren't tied to any specific price.

## What's working

- The chair-per-section layout gives me one product, one photo and one "Shop [Name]" button at a time. I can scan it on my phone without hunting.
- The customer quotes are a real trust cue. They read like reviews, not ad copy.
- The photography is warm and consistent: bouclé, leather, mohair-looking fabrics. It looks like a room I'd want, and the color palette stays coherent as I scroll.
- The product blurbs mention fabric and leather options (the "200+ fabrics" line), which matters when I'm matching two houses.
- The "10% back in Reward Dollars" card block speaks to how I shop. I always pick the highest-rewards card, so I did the math.

## What's weak

- The top banner promotes sofas and sectionals while the subject promises chairs. The ask and the offer don't match, and I can't tell whether the 20% applies to chairs.
- It's a very long email with around a dozen shop buttons and no single primary CTA. The scroll goes from chairs to a "fall deals edit," then to a cookware and wine glass grid, a card promo, an app promo and a design-desk plug. By the cookware grid I'd lost the thread.
- It's the fifth Crate & Barrel send today. The sofa deadline email this morning and this one share a banner, which feels lazy.
- There's no shipping or lead-time information anywhere. For furniture that matters more than a pretty photo, and it's the first thing I check.
- Some of the small type under each chair is hard to read without zooming, especially the product descriptions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the top banner match the subject. Put the chair offer and its dates in the first screen, and move the sofa message to its own send.
- 2. Add shipping and lead-time lines per chair, such as "In stock, ships in 2 weeks" and "Swatches available." That would do more for me than the cookware grid.
- 3. Cut the lower modules (cookware, wine glass, app promo). Keep chairs, the sale button, the card promo and the design appointment.
- 4. Make the body text larger and give each chair a price or a "from $X" figure.
- 5. Hold the cadence to one furniture send a day. Wayfair sent me sixteen this week and I've stopped reading it, and CB2 is at sixteen in six days. Crate & Barrel is heading the same way.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with `lang="en"`, a preheader-safe head and Outlook conditional CSS. The main confirmed problems are 29 images flagged for missing alt text, including product and content images, and a viewport tag that disables zoom. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because of the relay, not because they failed. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking not probed:** 62 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` beacons (LiveRamp) are embedded. Their `s=` value begins with the same hash as the first-party `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cc…` parameter used on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels. That suggests a subscriber-derived identifier is being shared with a third party. The data shows no consent or disclosure mechanism, and I can't confirm that from this source.
- **Redundant beacons:** The email carries five identical rlcdn pixels, plus `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/657f822c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. This adds requests and blocked-image failure points. The rlcdn pixels also hit a third-party domain that image proxies or privacy filters may block.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=dbe8da71…` is embedded in the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs (7 images). Because it is exposed in image URLs, it is forwardable and cacheable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (29 images flagged):**
  - 15 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `47f1832d-…jpg`, `35c4da66-…gif`, and `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`.
  - The rest are tracking pixels: 5 rlcdn, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/657f822c-…`.
  - The pixels are harmless if `alt=""` is set. The content images are the real issue, since with images blocked or a screen reader in use, linked images have no accessible name.
  - The QA check does not distinguish a missing attribute from `alt=""`, so some of the 29 may be intentional.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize concern.
- **Text size adjustment disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; }` conflict with the `body` declaration of `100%`. They override user text-size preferences on iOS.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `img {max-width: 600px}` at `min-width:768px` and `img {max-width: 640px}` at `min-width:640px` overlap. At 768px and wider both match, so the later rule (640px) wins, which is probably not what was intended.
- **Template leftovers:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. There are also duplicate `format-detection` meta tags: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` repeated. Neither affects rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The footer and body were truncated, so token coverage there is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated. This is unverified, not a failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified. All 62 tracking links were skipped, and no UTM parameters or landing URLs are visible in the truncated source. Tracking appears to go through redirect domains with identifiers such as `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`, not visible UTMs. Landing page alignment with the subject ("bestselling chairs") can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images (the 15 `image.mail…/m/…` assets and 7 `mi…/rp/*.png` images). Use `alt=""` explicitly on the pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` overrides.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the leftover template comment and duplicate meta tags.
4. Review the five rlcdn.com beacons and the hashed `mi_u` identifier shared with them, for consent and privacy disclosure. Consolidate the duplicate pixels.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw RFC 822 message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, which the relay may be stripping.
6. Re-run with the full HTML and with redirect-following enabled for the 62 tracked links, so the unsubscribe link, postal address, final landing URLs and any UTM parameters can be checked.
