## What's weak

- The whole email is built around kids' rooms — bedding, backpacks, lunch bags, Halloween decor — and I don't have kids. This is the exact cross-pollination problem I flagged on the last "Refresh their room" send.
- No hero copy or product anywhere references anything close to a full-home renovation, which is my actual mode right now.
- No product model or lifestyle shot signals an adult male buyer at all — every styled room reads preschool-to-elementary.
- Zero urgency that actually means anything — "before school starts" isn't a date, it's a vibe. Article's "Ends Sunday" at least gives me a real deadline to react to.
- This is at minimum the fourth Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox this stretch (Aspen dinnerware twice, the sofa email, now this) — cadence fatigue is real and I'm starting to tune out anything with that logo.

- 2. Add a real deadline to the back-to-school sale (a date or countdown), not just "before school starts."
