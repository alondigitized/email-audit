# Don’t miss these best sellers on sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Best Sellers, up to 40% off" — CB2 running a sitewide category sale is the most CB2 email possible, and I've now seen this exact structure from the C&B family so many times this month it's basically muscle memory: hero bed, "shop the best" button, then a category grid (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs, Tabletop), interior design pitch, app/rewards footer. It's clean, it's on-brand, the photography is genuinely nice — that console table and the pendant lamp both made me pause for half a second. But nothing here is new information. This is the same "best sellers up to 40% off" beat CB2/Crate & Barrel already ran on 9/22 ("Best sellers up to 40% off. Just one more day."), and I remember thinking then that the deadline language felt manufactured. Now it's back three days later with no urgency language at all, which almost feels like they got caught in their own fake deadline.
- I'd scroll it, maybe tap into Rugs since I'm actually shopping that category for the second house, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone — there's no single hero product worth sharing, just a category directory. Mark as read after a browse, not a real engagement.

## What's working

- The hero image (linen-look bed with layered neutrals) is genuinely aspirational and matches my taste better than most Wayfair sends ever will.
- "Up to 40% off" is a real, visible number, not buried — better than CB2's own designer-collab emails that skip the price entirely.
- The category grid is scannable — I know exactly where to tap if I want Lighting vs. Rugs vs. Tabletop, which beats Pottery Barn's wall-of-quilts approach from the 23rd.
- Rewards math is spelled out ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars") in the footer, which is the loyalty-member content I actually look for and Wayfair almost never bothers with.
- Interior Design Services block ("In Store | Virtually," free consult) is a real value-add for someone mid-renovation on two houses — Article's "organized entryway" email leaned into content this way too, and it worked there.

## What's weak

- I've now gotten near-identical "best sellers % off" sale framing from this brand family twice in four days (9/22 and 9/23) — the cadence problem I keep flagging with CB2/C&B is getting worse, not better.
- No swatch/sample program mention anywhere, which is the thing that actually gets me to commit to furniture and rugs online instead of bailing to a showroom.
- Shipping info for furniture is completely absent — for a console table or a bed, I need to know delivery windows before I click "shop," and this email just doesn't say.
- The design-services section uses white text over a dark, busy photo (green room, dark chair) that's noticeably harder to read at a glance than the rest of the email — mild hierarchy stumble.
- Zero urgency or end date on the sale, so there's no reason to act today versus next week — compare to the 9/22 send which at least gestured at "one more day."

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop running "best sellers % off" two sends apart — if it's genuinely the same sale, say "still 40% off" and change the creative; if it's a new sale, say when it ends. Repetition without acknowledgment is what's training me to skim past CB2 subject lines now.
- 2. Add a real deadline back in, but make it true this time — a decaying countdown or a stated end date would push me from "I'll browse later" to "I'll click now."
- 3. Put swatch/sample ordering and a one-line shipping estimate ("Ships in 2-3 weeks") directly under the hero CTA — that's the actual friction point for me on furniture.
- 4. Fix the design-services text contrast — bump to a lighter overlay or move copy onto the cleaner left third of that photo.
- 5. Personalize the category order to my browsing history — I've clearly been in Rugs and Furniture more than Tabletop; lead with what I actually buy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs, lighting, and furniture — all 40% off through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your best sellers sale ends this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on the console tables and rugs you've been eyeing`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: free design help, in-store or virtual`

- These dotomi.com pixels (cookie-sync/retargeting) add 11+ blocking or silently-dropped requests; worth confirming they're still needed vendor-side, as broken retargeting silently degrades attribution rather than causing visible failure.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
