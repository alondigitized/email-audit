# APP ONLY: FIRST LOOK AT SALE
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again — sixth send in six days, and this one's the most honest about what it's doing: "APP ONLY: FIRST LOOK AT SALE" is a gate, not an offer, and at least the hero owns that with a full-bleed shot of an iPhone showing their app UI over the actual planter/lounge scene. Up to 50% off Labor Day, up to 60% off clearance — the numbers are real. But I'm not downloading an app to unlock a preview of a sale that'll hit my inbox in full form in 48 hours anyway.
- The category grid below the fold — Furniture, Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining — is clean and I like that lamp tile, that carved-wood table lamp has a shape I haven't seen from Pottery Barn or Article this month. But this is the same six-tile sale-teaser layout CB2's been running since "Just added to clearance," just reskinned for Labor Day.
- Would I click? Only the tile grid, and only because the lamp caught my eye — not the app download, not the hero CTA. Wouldn't forward it. Marking read.

## What's working

- The hero photography is genuinely nice — warm stone/olive palette, real plant foliage framing the phone mockup, feels premium not stock.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" and "UP TO 60% OFF" clearance are both stated as concrete numbers, not vague "big savings" language.
- The six-tile category grid (Furniture/Outdoor/Rugs/Lighting/Decor/Entertaining) gives me an actual visual hierarchy to scan instead of one giant banner.
- The lighting tile — that sculptural stone-base lamp — is the one product shot that made me pause; better than anything CB2's shown me this week.
- Footer rewards math is spelled out plainly: "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" and "15% off" for texts — at least the loyalty stuff isn't buried.

## What's weak

- The entire top half is gated behind an app download — that's friction dressed up as exclusivity, and I'm not installing an app for a "first look."
- This is CB2's sixth distinct subject line since 8/19 ("Just added to clearance," "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE," "Future Heirlooms Under $500," "Designed by Andreas Hansen in Copenhagen," "This sale ends tonight," now "APP ONLY: FIRST LOOK") — I've completely stopped reading these as individually meaningful.
- "†" footnotes on both offers with no visible terms in the main body — I have to scroll to a wall of legal text to find out what's excluded.
- No product names, prices, or dimensions anywhere in the grid — just category labels, so there's zero reason to click through versus waiting for the real sale email.
- Crate & Barrel, Crate&Kids, and CB2 all stacked in the same footer logo row — confirms what I already suspected, that this is one company hitting me from three angles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the app-gate premise entirely — show me actual sale products with prices in this email instead of a locked teaser. Wayfair's "Black Outdoor Privacy Screens" send led with a real tile up top; that's the move.
- 2. Put one hero product with a price and the specific discount (not "up to") above the fold — Article's storage home-tour send at least showed real furniture without a countdown gimmick.
- 3. Cut the send cadence — six CB2 emails in six days is Pottery Barn-level fatigue and it's making me tune out the one lamp that's actually good.
- 4. Move the footnote terms into a single visible line under each offer instead of buried in footer legal copy.

- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Your first look — no app required`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, lighting and more — Labor Day pricing is live`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MI (Message Interactive)-templated CB2 promotional email; core mechanics render correctly, but the QA pass surfaces missing authentication/unsubscribe headers, unencrypted third-party pixel loads, and widespread missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 60 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect-domain links — not itself a defect, but means link destinations are unverified).
- Multiple Dotomi ad-tracking calls load over plaintext HTTP: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id` and ten sequential cookie-redirect pixels `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Non-HTTPS resources are frequently stripped or blocked by mail clients (Gmail image proxy, Outlook), which can break the tracking/attribution these pixels exist for.
- CB2's own tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) correctly use HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 of the content/product images are missing `alt` text (e.g. `4f518d08-5886-446f-a164-187ffa2c9aec.jpg`, `84e471bd-9a6c-4e01-baf3-d96aa577e29f.jpg`, `8b5bd3aa-0895-4f07-a44a-d459ebcf87d6.jpg`, `f4624093-0a3a-4fff-8e49-0f74878682c8.jpg`, `f6cce36f-4e38-43fb-b180-13bbba29da4d.jpg`, `c4d5a99f-5e44-4031-a025-70a015a1214d.jpg`, `65069ce7-cdc0-4698-86a7-6c0a7a15cf6f.jpg`, `4d540dc3-561d-4295-b49a-1f667d773eaa.jpg`, `51beedf3-4a60-44a8-b55b-e04023610f91.png`, `53c67fab-a66d-4816-930d-835edd7ce2aa.gif`). With images blocked by default in most clients, recipients get no fallback text, degrading both accessibility (screen readers) and comprehension when images don't load.
- Tracking pixels also lack `alt` (expected/non-issue for 1x1 beacons, e.g. `UCMController`, `pub1–10.php`, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`).
- Template includes standard MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and mobile media queries (`max-width: 418px`) — no structural rendering defects found in the truncated source.
- Custom `@font-face` declarations (Lineto Brown, GT Sectra, HNW05) have no web-safe fallback chain issues detected in the excerpt (`a` selector falls back to `Helvetica, sans-serif`), but most email clients strip `@font-face` entirely — not a bug, just expected degradation to fallback fonts.
