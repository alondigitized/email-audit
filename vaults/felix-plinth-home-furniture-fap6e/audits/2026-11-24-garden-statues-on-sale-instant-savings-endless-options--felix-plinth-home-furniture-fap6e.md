## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Wayfair "GARDEN STATUES *on sale.*"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a 480px mobile breakpoint and consistent per-link tracking parameters. Confirmed defects are about 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels and several linked images with no alt text. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, because AgentMail's relay didn't expose the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, preheader and `<head>` meta tags were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probe results:**
  - 12 links were probed and 47 skipped, so coverage is partial.
  - Nine probes returned 429 (for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links). A 429 is Wayfair rate-limiting the QA prober, not a broken link.
  - No 4xx or 5xx failures were reported.
  - Three links were not checked because the 70s probe budget ran out: `/daily-sales/filters/cozy%2c-thick-pile-washable-rugs-se7…`, `/california-king-bedding-sets-se74547`, and `/wayfair-rewards?_emr=…`. These are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 impression beacons use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=76423e48-…`.
  - They may be blocked or upgraded by clients, so impression counts could be undercounted.
  - Each is also missing alt text.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is HTTPS but has no alt attribute.
- **Per-recipient identifiers:** `_emr=76423e48-13c8-4d2a-ab37-05cfc1b59933` is on every link and in the `p=` param of each impression pixel. Each link also carries a unique `_eml` token. Forwarded emails will carry the recipient's identity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images without alt text.** These give the link no accessible name, and the QA tool fell back to the filename as link text:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-BF-AppOffer.jpg`, linked to the onelink app URL.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, linked to `https://www.wayfair.com/`.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_2_purple.png`.
  - `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24…`.
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`, which should have `alt=""` if it is decorative.
- **Disclaimer as an image:** `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg` has no alt text. Any disclaimer text it carries is unavailable to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Subject line:** it ends in `options!️`, which has a stray emoji variation selector (U+FE0F) after the "!" and no emoji. Some clients may render it as a blank box.
- **Responsive CSS:** the template has a Gmail `u ~ div` min-width fix, `u-FullWidthBlock` stacking classes and `!important` mobile overrides. No defects are visible in the portion provided.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FNAME]` appear in the visible source or the QA data. The `_emr`/`_eml` values are resolved GUIDs.

## 5. Compliance
- **Not verifiable:** the QA run found no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` headers. Its notes say the relay may be stripping them, so these are unconfirmed, not confirmed failures.
- **Sender:** `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign for alignment.
- **Body footer:** the footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) was cut off in the truncated source and can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking scheme:** the links use Wayfair's own parameters (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121286`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16805`). There are no `utm_*` parameters. This is consistent across links and works if Wayfair's analytics reads these, but it won't show up in a GA-style UTM report.
- **Header banner link:** `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png` (`sltid=5`) goes to the homepage `https://www.wayfair.com/`. The subject and the adjacent "On Sale Now: Garden Statues" link (`…se68290.html?eid=413515`, same `sltid=5` and `mdlid`) go to a specific sale event. That is a landing mismatch, though it may be deliberate.
- **App promo links:** the "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" onelink URLs deep-link to `wayfairapp://home`, with a web fallback via `af_web_dp`. The banner variant targets `/curated/app-extra-10%25-off~ev646886.html`.
- **Mixed content:** the `%25` double-encoding inside `af_dp` is valid but fragile. Check that it resolves in the fallback path.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""` plus explicit `width`/`height` to them and to `t.wayfair.com/b.php`.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the linked banners (app offer, header, rewards), so the link text isn't a filename. Put the text of the loyalty disclaimer in `alt` or, better, in live HTML.
3. Point the header banner at the garden-statues event URL, or confirm the homepage link is intended.
4. Remove the stray U+FE0F from the subject line.
5. To close the compliance gaps, check the raw message (not the AgentMail relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
6. Re-probe the three unchecked links and the 429-limited URLs with a slower or throttled crawler to separate rate-limiting from real failures.
