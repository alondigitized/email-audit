# Discover Early Black Friday deals + Online exclusive: Furniture ON SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the furniture email I actually wanted, and I'm annoyed it took a week of "Early Black Friday" blasts to get one. The maroon "up to 70% off" banner is the same one I've had since 11/16, but this send is built around "Select Furniture Now on Sale." That matters because I'm furnishing two houses. Four collection tiles follow: Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston and Farmhouse. They're big, readable and linked to specific collections, which is what I save to my inspiration folder.
- It falls apart after that. The "Recommended just for you" block is stockings, a faux fur throw, a robe and a curtain rod, so it's the usual Pottery Barn catch-all. The Christmas Shop banner and the rewards, app and Affirm blocks sit on top of that, and the footer is a wall of fine print. This is the fifth Pottery Barn send since 11/24, and the same banner has repeated through all of them.
- I'd click, probably the Toscana dining table, and I'd save it. I wouldn't forward it, because nothing here is new or exclusive enough to send a friend.

## What's working

- "Online exclusive: Select Furniture Now on Sale" is concrete and matches both renovations.
- Four named collections (Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston, Farmhouse) with large lifestyle photos give me something to pin.
- The red "NOW ON SALE" tags on each image make the sale status clear without reading copy.
- Two CTAs, "Shop Furniture Sale" and "Shop All In-Stock Furniture," are clear. The in-stock one speaks to my delivery-timeline worry.
- The "Earn 10% back in Rewards" card block matters to me, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The banner says "up to 70% off," but none of the furniture tiles shows a price or a percent off. I can't tell if my dining table is 20% or 70%.
- The recommended grid is stockings, a throw, a robe and a quilt. It has nothing to do with the furniture hero I just read.
- Two promos sit between the furniture and the footer: Christmas Shop "up to 50% off" and a Design Crew sweepstakes. Both dilute the message.
- Three loyalty and financing modules (app, rewards card, Affirm) stack at the bottom, and the tiny fine print dominates the last stretch. The "Design Crew" sweepstakes also overlaps with the "FREE design help in stores" send from 11/24.
- The collection tiles have no price, no ship date and no swatch or sample offer, which is what I need for furniture.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or percent-off on every collection tile ("Toscana dining, 30% off, ships by Dec 15"). Furniture buyers need the math and the delivery window.
- 2. Drop the generic "Recommended just for you" grid, or make it furniture-adjacent (rugs, lighting, side tables). Stockings after a dining set feels like a different email.
- 3. Cut the Christmas Shop and the sweepstakes from this send. One story per email.
- 4. Add a swatch and fabric sample link under the upholstery. Crate & Barrel and Article both lean on that more than Pottery Barn does.
- 5. Vary the top banner. It's the same maroon hero I've seen on every send since 11/16.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Furniture sale: Toscana, Aubrey & more, in stock`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Online-only furniture deals, up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining, desks and beds on sale now. See what ships before the holidays.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Four collections, in-stock pieces, plus 10% back in Rewards.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Discover Early Black Friday deals + Online exclusive: Furniture ON SALE"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px layout. It is image-heavy, with about 44 images missing alt text (per the QA scan). Three images load over `http://`, and the recipient's email address appears in plaintext in two tracking URLs, one of them over `http://`. The HTML was truncated, and the click-tracking links were not probed, so link destinations, UTMs and the footer could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click redirects, unverifiable.** All 53 tracking links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (an encoded Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style redirect). The QA skipped HTTP probing on them, so destination status and redirect chains are untested.
- **PII leaked in tracking URLs.** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext in:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
  
  The second is plain HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit. Both end up in server logs and in any proxy or referrer logs.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is a Turn/Amobee ad-tech beacon.
- **Open-tracking pixels.** There are multiple, including `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/ad13408c-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. These are normal, but they all rely on image loading.
- **Cross-brand asset host.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from a West Elm domain inside a Pottery Barn email. This is a minor dependency on another brand's host.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA flagged about 44 `<img>` elements, including:
  - the hero banner `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg`
  - all `slices_01–07.jpg` and `slices_03.gif` under `/2025/US/1016_THU_AM_DTCOnlyFurnitureSale/`
  - `slices_01–18.jpg` under `/2026/US/Email-Footer/`
  - the product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `waffle-weave-resort-robe-l.jpg`, etc.)
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg` and the `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_*.jpg` banners
  
  Because the sale content is sliced images, it is invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3).**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...`
  
  These may be blocked or may trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` with no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray server code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS and can break rule parsing in strict clients. It also exposes template or backend code.
- **Missing table roles.** The outer layout tables (`<table width="100%" ... bgcolor="#ffffff">` and the spacer tables) lack `role="presentation"`. Only the inner content tables have it.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` to Montserrat is stripped by many clients, including Outlook and Gmail. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The recipient address is resolved in the URLs (`...fap6e@etell.app`), and no unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[[...]]` appear in the visible source. The privacy concern with those resolved addresses is covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed** and not a verified sender defect. Check the raw original message to confirm.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated. Every CTA is wrapped in an encoded `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect, and the probes were skipped.
- Product images point directly at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which suggests live catalog assets, but landing pages weren't tested.
- Several creative assets sit in older campaign folders (`/2025/US/1016_THU_AM_DTCOnlyFurnitureSale/`, `/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`). These may be reused modules. Confirm the offer content still matches the current landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Stop putting the raw email address in URL query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use an opaque token or hash instead.
2. Move `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `slices_02.jpg` to HTTPS.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to all content and sale images (the sliced banners and product images). Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Add a `<title>` and `lang="en"` to the document.
6. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Add `role="presentation"` to the outer layout tables.
8. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC headers on the original message, outside the relay.
9. Re-run the link checks with the redirects resolved, so UTMs and landing-page status can be validated.
