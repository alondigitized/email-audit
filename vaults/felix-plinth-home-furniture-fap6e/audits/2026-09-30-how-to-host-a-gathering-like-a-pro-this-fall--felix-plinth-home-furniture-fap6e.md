## What's working

- The hero is on-season and on-topic, with a styled fall table and a single "Shop Now."
- The creator-led sections (@eddaniel_, @oliviaamcdowell, @alvinwayne, @ourtablecompany) feel editorial, so I don't get the usual "SALE" shout.
- Product tiles under each story carry Bestseller, New Arrival and Exclusive tags, which helps me skim.
- "Shop All Tabletop & Bar" is a specific CTA with a verb and a noun.
- The decor event (up to 30% off) and the coffee and espresso offer (up to $300 off) are real price hooks. The "we love these for you" grid shows prices.
- I can see the Crate Card 10% off block, which matters to me because I pick my card for rewards.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. The money offers don't show up until after all four creator stories, so someone skimming would miss them.
- "Watch on IG" sends me out of the inbox to social. That's friction when I'm trying to shop.
- There are too many competing CTAs: Shop Now, four Watch on IG links, Shop All Tabletop & Bar, two more Shop Now buttons, Learn More, Download Now and a design appointment. No single action stands out.
- Nothing addresses furniture shipping times, swatches or samples, or color-palette consistency. I'm mid-renovation, and this is all tabletop and party content.
- The "we love these for you" grid doesn't feel personalized to me.
- I've had about ten C&B-family emails this week, so I'm in a permanent snooze on this sender, as I was on the newborn-shop send earlier today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% decor offer and the $300 coffee offer up into or directly under the hero. Right now the content is competing with the offers instead of supporting them.
- 2. Cut the creator stories from four to two and link the tiles straight to product pages instead of IG.
- 3. Add one line on furniture delivery windows. Pottery Barn's "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner does this well, and CB2 led with its Upholstery Event numbers, so I saw the offer first.
- 4. Pick a single primary CTA color and treat the rest as secondary.
- 5. Fill the preheader, which currently looks empty or leaks junk.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "How to host a gathering like a pro this fall →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with Outlook/VML support. The main confirmed issue is missing alt text on 52 images. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the AgentMail relay drops those headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and link-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **88 tracking links weren't probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. That is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Plain-text part is 71% URLs** (17,846 of 25,047 chars). This is consistent with 88 wrapped links, and the QA check flags it as a deliverability signal. It is a real but low-severity issue. The text part is dominated by long tracking URLs, so it has little readable content.
- **Multiple third-party pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5`, which are LiveRamp beacons repeated with an incrementing `n`.
  - Six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...`.
  - One `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`.
  - One `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e061f04a-...?mi_cid=...`.
  - The repeated calls add request overhead. Whether they're intentional per-module beacons can't be confirmed from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **52 images are flagged as missing alt text.** Breakdown:
  - 35 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif`, e.g. `c17eb305-60f4-47b7-9ad8-33aa51279cc5.jpg` and `0424b473-...gif`. One more, `bbf6f9d4-...png`, sits under `/m/47/`.
  - 4 are Scene7 assets named `2026%5F0930%5FCB%5FFallGathering%5FInfluencers%5F...`.
  - 13 are tracking pixels (`f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, and the `dv.` beacon).
  - The 13 tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`. If the checker treats an empty alt as missing, those 13 are false positives. The 39 content and GIF images need real alt text, because images-off clients will show blank slots or raw filenames.
  - I can't confirm from the truncated source whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **`<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1 />`** blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid in XHTML (`&amp;` is required). The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Template debris in `<head>`:**
  - A leftover comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - An empty `<!---->`.
  - An empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.
  - These are harmless but show an unfinished template.
- **Redundant or duplicate meta tags:**
  - `meta charset` alongside `http-equiv Content-Type`.
  - Three `format-detection` tags (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` again). Client behavior with duplicates is undefined.
- **Conflicting CSS:**
  - `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` are both declared.
  - `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` is applied globally.
  - The single mobile breakpoint (`max-width:460px`) is narrower than typical phone widths (~375–430px). Devices at or just below it may get the `.mobile {width:460px !important}` rule, which could force horizontal overflow on smaller viewports.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens. The `mi_u=` and `mi_cid=` parameters in the pixel URLs look like resolved subscriber and campaign IDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) weren't found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. It can't be treated as a sender defect without the raw headers from the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, which is an expected limitation of the relay.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are in the footer, which was truncated, so I can't confirm them either way.
- **Sending domain alignment looks consistent:** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` (From), `image.mail.`, `mi.` and `dv.` are all on `crateandbarrel.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All 88 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so the landing URLs and any UTM parameters can't be evaluated. Nothing in the visible source shows UTM tagging.
- The Scene7 assets (`s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026_0930_CB_FallGathering_Influencers_*`) are named for the campaign, which suggests the landing pages align with the "Fall Gathering" theme. This is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all ~39 content and GIF images, and `alt=""` to the 13 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape `&` in `<title>` as `&amp;`, and use a title that matches the subject.
4. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas, the placeholder comment, and the empty conditional blocks.
5. Raise the plain-text/HTML ratio by shortening the tracking URLs in the plain-text part or adding more readable copy.
6. Inspect the raw original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, which the relay hides. Until then, treat those warnings as unverified.
7. Resolve a sample of the tracking links manually to confirm landing pages and UTM tagging.
