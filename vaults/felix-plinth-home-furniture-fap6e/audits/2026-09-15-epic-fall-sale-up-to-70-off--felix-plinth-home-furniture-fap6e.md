#  🍂 EPIC FALL SALE 🍂 Up to 70% OFF 🍂
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Same "70% OFF EPIC FALL SALE" banner, same purple-and-red grid I've been getting from Wayfair on repeat this week — this is basically indistinguishable from the "48-HR 70% OFF" send from yesterday and the "LIVING ROOM SETS" one from the day before that. The "picked just for you" section is the same trick, too: a wood drawer slide, a bathroom vanity, a sewing machine, a guy grilling, a dog trampoline, and a shoe rack. None of that reads as "picked for Felix" — it reads as "picked at random from the warehouse." I'm mid-reno on two houses and there's real category overlap here (lighting, curtains, cooling, kitchen faucets, home office furniture) but it's all buried in a wall of identical red-tag tiles with no hierarchy telling me which one actually matters today.
- The one thing that stopped my scroll for half a second was the Afterpay 0% APR banner — that's genuinely useful when I'm furnishing two houses at once — but it's crammed into a small strip between rug and bulb tiles instead of being a headline feature.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is the fourth near-identical Wayfair promo this week and my eyes have started to just skim past the red boxes. Mark as read, move on.

## What's working

- Afterpay "0% APR through Sept 24" is a real, concrete financing hook for someone dropping money on two renovations at once.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" banner is clear and relevant since furniture shipping cost/timing is always my top anxiety with Wayfair.
- Lighting, curtains & drapes, and home office furniture tiles land in categories I'm actually shopping right now.
- Category tiles at least have specific %-off numbers instead of vague "save big" language.

## What's weak

- This is the same red-tag-on-purple grid layout I've flagged from Wayfair four sends running — "picked just for you" hasn't felt personalized once.
- Zero swatch/sample program or color-palette-consistency content, which is exactly what I need when I'm trying to keep two houses' palettes from clashing.
- No showroom-integration or "see it in a room" content — CB2's "Fall Sale" send at least gave me one big moody styled bedroom hero; this is just product-tile Tetris top to bottom.
- The grid is so dense and repetitive that my eye doesn't land anywhere — no true hero, just tile after tile after tile.
- "Picked just for you" mixing a dog trampoline and a sewing machine into a furniture-reno email tells me Wayfair isn't actually reading my purchase history.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the generic "picked just for you" grid or actually personalize it — if I've been browsing lighting and rugs, show me lighting and rugs, not a dog trampoline.
- 2. Pick ONE hero category (lighting or area rugs, both at strong % off) and give it a full-width styled room shot like CB2 does, instead of 20 competing tiles.
- 3. Move the Afterpay 0% APR banner up near the top — that's a stronger differentiator than another "70% OFF" repeat.
- 4. Add a swatch/sample or "coordinate your palette" module — directly relevant to a two-house renovation and something no competitor email I've seen this week has done either.
- 5. Vary the subject line language — four "48-HR / 70% OFF" sends in five days is training me to ignore this sender.
