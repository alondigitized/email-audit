# New! Chandeliers & lamps for fall
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the one from Crate & Barrel today that actually makes sense to send me — "new! the lighting edit" with chandeliers, pendants, floor lamps, table lamps, and sconces all lined up in one email. I'm rewiring lighting in two houses right now, so unlike the pizza-oven grill guide or the plastic-free kitchen checklist from earlier in this same 24-hour C&B blitz, this one is actually shopping for my problem.
- That said, it's enormous. I scrolled forever — six or seven distinct lighting categories, then a "most-loved lights" reel, then a whole separate "Ceramicist Collection" launch, then a tableware sale, then a "we love these for you" personalized grid, then a credit card pitch, then an app download push, then a design-consultation CTA. By the time I hit the Aspen collection sale I'd forgotten this started as a lighting email.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the Gordon Chandelier and the Milbank table lamps are genuinely close to what I've been eyeing for the primary. Would I forward it? Only the top third, cropped, to my wife with "these two" — not the whole thing, it's too much of a scroll to make anyone else sit through.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — "new! the lighting edit" plus "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" gives me both a category and a number before I even scroll.
- Each lighting category (pendants, floor lamps, table lamps, sconces) gets its own clean section with a styled room shot plus a clear product callout — that's the kind of "shop by room function" structure Wayfair's "Fearless design, bold layers" send nailed too.
- The Gordon Chandelier and Milbank Marble table lamp shots are genuinely on-brand for a fall renovation — brass accents, warm wood tones, not the styled-for-Instagram staging that sometimes misses.
- "we love these for you" toward the bottom at least gestures at personalization instead of just another generic grid.

## What's weak

- This is way too long — it's basically five emails stitched into one (lighting, Ceramicist Collection launch, Aspen tableware sale, credit card offer, app download). CB2's "Two sales end tonight" annoyed me by stacking two heroes; this stacks about eight.
- The tableware section ("bestselling Aspen collection 10% off") and the credit card pitch have nothing to do with lighting and just dilute the one clear reason I opened this.
- No pricing anywhere on the actual lamps — "up to 60% off" is the only number in the whole email, and it's not attached to any specific product I'm looking at. Wayfair at least ties percentages to visible SKUs.
- Sconces section says "save space" but doesn't show dimensions or projection depth, which is exactly the kind of spec I need before I buy a wall light for a tight hallway.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — a tight lighting-only email (chandeliers → pendants → floor → table → sconces, done) and a separate one for the Ceramicist Collection/Aspen sale/credit card/app stuff. Right now the good content is buried under four unrelated pitches.
- 2. Put actual sale prices or "%off" tags on the hero products, not just a banner percentage — if it's up to 60% off, show me 60% off on the chandelier I'm already looking at.
- 3. Add a dimension or "fits X sq ft room" line under the sconces and floor lamps — I don't want to click through five product pages just to check projection depth.
- 4. Move "we love these for you" up near the top — that's the most relevant block in the whole email and it's stranded near the footer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Chandeliers, pendants, table lamps & sconces — all in one edit`
