# Seating updates, ready to ship
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the tenth Pottery Barn email this week, and it's actually the one I'd click. "Ready-to-Ship Seating" with a "1-4 weeks" delivery promise — that's the exact anxiety I have mid-renovation, where I don't want to order a sofa and find out it's a 16-week special order. The plaid armchair hero shot is genuinely nice, and they organized the whole thing by category — armchairs, sofas, sectionals, swivel, leather — like a real furniture buyer's mental model instead of a mood board.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid toward the bottom is where it falls apart a little — curtain rods, a dining table, a rug, a bed, duvet covers — none of that is seating, none of it is "just for me" in any way I can tell, it's just their catalog. And then the Halloween Shop banner and the Design Crew Sweepstakes stacked at the bottom feel like three different emails glued together.
- I'd click through to "Shop All In-Stock Seating" — that's a real, specific, useful CTA for someone furnishing two houses right now. I wouldn't forward it, but I'd bookmark it in my Pinterest-adjacent folder. Compare this to Article's "New for home office" send I read recently — that one committed to a single room story start to finish. This one starts great and then wanders.

## What's working

- "Ready-to-Ship Seating" + "delivered in just 1-4 weeks" directly answers the question I actually have when furniture shopping mid-renovation.
- Clean category breakdown — armchairs, sofas, sectionals, swivel, leather — makes it easy to jump to what I need instead of scrolling a generic grid.
- The plaid wingback chair hero photo is warm and specific, not generic catalog-white-room stuff.
- "Shop All In-Stock Seating" is a clear, unambiguous primary CTA button.
- "More to Round Out the Room" (pillows, coffee tables, floor lamps, rugs) is smart cross-sell — logical add-ons for someone who just bought seating, not random noise.

## What's weak

- "Recommended Just For You" is bedroom and dining stuff (dining table, platform bed, duvet cover) dumped into a seating email — zero personalization signal, just filler.
- Halloween Shop banner crammed in below all that furniture content feels like a totally different email got stapled on.
- Design Crew Sweepstakes at the very bottom (after I already saw this exact sweepstakes concept, per my note from earlier this week — "Book a FREE appt. for a chance to win $1500") is repeat creative, not new.
- This is Pottery Barn's tenth email to me this week — the cadence is genuinely exhausting, and this one doesn't acknowledge that I've seen eight others.
- No swatch/fabric-sample CTA anywhere, which matters a lot for a sofa or sectional purchase — I'm not buying a couch off a thumbnail without seeing the fabric in my house.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email at "Shop All In-Stock Seating" or the room-round-out grid — kill the Halloween Shop and Sweepstakes blocks entirely, they dilute a genuinely strong, focused seating pitch.
- 2. Replace "Recommended Just For You" with actual seating-adjacent picks (an ottoman, a throw, a slipcover) — if there's no real personalization data, don't fake the header.
- 3. Add a "Order a free swatch" or fabric-sample CTA near the leather/sectional section — that's the actual friction point for a seating purchase this size.
- 4. Lead with the 1-4 week delivery promise even harder — maybe a badge on each product tile, since that's the strongest differentiator in this send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In-stock sofas, delivered in weeks not months`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Seating that ships in 1-4 weeks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Armchairs, sectionals, and leather seating — all ready now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the special-order wait on your next sofa`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Seating updates, ready to ship"

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard PB/EDM legacy template with heavy MSO/VML conditional markup; passes basic structural checks but fails several deliverability and accessibility hygiene rules — 3 mixed-content (HTTP) image sources, near-universal missing `alt` attributes, and no visible unsubscribe/authentication header data in the captured payload.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 59 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) were skipped by the HTTP prober — expected for click-redirect domains, not a defect, but means link destinations are unverified in this pass.
- Two open-tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`. The svopn pixel fires over **plain HTTP**, exposing the recipient email address (`sv_sveme=`) in a cleartext query string — a PII-over-HTTP leak.
- Personalized email address is also embedded in a clear URL param on the customer-service image link: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (HTTPS here, so lower risk, but still logs PII in URL/referrer chains).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 44 of ~46 images are missing `alt` text — affects every content image (product shots, banners, footer slices, tracking pixels). Screen readers and images-off clients get no fallback text.
- 3 images load over `http://` instead of `https://`: `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed content on an otherwise-HTTPS mail template can trigger client-side blocking/broken-image icons.
- The `<style>` block includes literal PHP `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');` etc.) sitting inside a CSS `<style>` tag — dead/inert code that does nothing in an email client but suggests a copy-paste error from a server-side asset script into the template source.
- No `<img>` `width`/`height` mismatches or broken conditional blocks observed in the truncated source; VML/MSO fallbacks and Outlook `xml` blocks are present and well-formed.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

- Recipient identifier is populated correctly across tracking/link URLs (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`), confirming personalization merge succeeded — but see §2 for the plaintext-over-HTTP exposure this creates.

- No unsubscribe link/footer visible in the truncated HTML source provided; full source would need to be reviewed to confirm a body-level unsubscribe link and physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM requires both regardless of header state).
