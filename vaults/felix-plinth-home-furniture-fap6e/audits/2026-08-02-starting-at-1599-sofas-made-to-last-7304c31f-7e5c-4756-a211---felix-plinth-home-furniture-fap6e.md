I wouldn't forward this to anyone; it's a solid catalog browse, not a "you have to see this" moment. - Fifth Crate & Barrel-family send this week counting the two Halloween emails (8/1) and the Crate & Kids duo — my inbox has become a permanent C&B snooze zone, same fatigue I flagged on CB2's five-in-a-week run.
