# New deals UP TO 60% OFF + See what's inside our summer catalog
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn number nine for me this week — I'm not even exaggerating anymore, I've started a mental tally. "The Summer Catalog" is a genuinely nice hero shot — that bedroom vignette with the woven pendant lights actually made me pause for half a second — but then it's immediately followed by a wall of gray navigation tiles, a design-classes banner, a "Recommended For You" grid, ANOTHER hero, and then a Halloween shop teaser in the same breath as the summer catalog headline. That's the tell this was assembled from six different modules rather than written as one email.
- The "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" rug and dining table grid is honestly the best part — that's actual personalization, unlike Crate & Barrel's wine glasses send which was one clean product story top to bottom. But Pottery Barn buries it two-thirds down the page behind generic category tiles I've already tapped through six times this week.
- I'm not clicking this one. It's not bad, it's just diluted — too many offers competing (60% off warehouse sale, design classes, new arrivals, Halloween shop) for me to know what I'm actually supposed to do. Marking as read, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The 60% off warehouse sale banner up top is a real, specific number — that's the one thing that could've carried the whole email.
- The Summer Catalog hero photography is genuinely well-styled, not generic stock furniture.
- The "Recommended Just For You" rug/curtain-rod/dining-table grid is the closest thing to real personalization I've seen from this brand all week — feels like it's actually looking at what I've browsed.
- 10% Back in Rewards card callout near the bottom is relevant since I'm already running the highest-rewards-card play.

## What's weak

- Too many competing CTAs stacked in one scroll — Browse Now, Shop 1400+ New Items, Learn More (design classes), Shop Now, Enter If You Dare (Halloween) — my eye has nowhere to land.
- The Halloween Shop banner showing up under a "Summer Catalog" subject line is a seasonal whiplash moment — pick a season.
- This is the ninth-ish Pottery Barn email this week and there's zero acknowledgment of that cadence — no "in case you missed it," just another full catalog dump.
- The 60% off claim in the top banner never resurfaces as an actual product-level discount anywhere below it — no strikethrough pricing on the recommended items, so the offer feels disconnected from the products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two emails — one for the Warehouse Sale (lead with 60% off, show discounted SKUs), one for the Summer Catalog inspiration piece. Trying to be both dilutes both.
- 2. Move "Recommended Just For You" up near the top — that's the section doing the most work for a repeat shopper like me, don't bury it under six navigation tiles.
- 3. Kill the Halloween Shop module from this send entirely — it contradicts the "Summer Catalog" framing and reads like a leftover template block.
- 4. Show actual sale pricing (strikethrough + sale price) on at least a couple of the recommended products so the 60% off banner has something to point at.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard EDM-platform (potterybarn.com via Salesforce/Cheetah-style edm.potterybarn.com + click.e.potterybarn.com tracking domains) marketing email; automated QA shows no critical failures but flags missing alt text across nearly all images, several plain-HTTP asset/tracking URLs, and unverifiable compliance headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 57 tracking/click-redirect links detected (click.e.potterybarn.com, clickcp.potterybarn.com) — skipped by automated HTTP probe, so destination validity is unconfirmed; manual click-through testing recommended.
- Open/tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` served over plain HTTP (non-HTTPS), and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` third-party pixel.
- No issues found with link structure itself (standard redirect-domain pattern for ESP click tracking).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 40+ `<img>` elements missing `alt` attributes (banners, product slices, footer legal/social slices, tracking pixels) — see QA list; screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing.
- Two images served over `http://` instead of `https://`: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing TLS.
- `<style>` block contains stray PHP-style `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) embedded as literal text inside a CSS `<style>` tag — inert/dead code, harmless to rendering but indicates a templating/build artifact that leaked into the HTML source.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (XHTML 1.0 Transitional) and MSO/Outlook conditional comments present — standard for cross-client email compatibility, no issues found.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — same caveat.
