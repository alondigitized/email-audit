# Up to 40% off top kitchen brands!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is at least a real, specific email — "up to 40% off top kitchen brands" with actual named brands (KitchenAid, Vitamix, All-Clad, Fellow) stacked top to bottom. That's a different animal than the family blast avalanche I've been drowning in this week. But it's also the fifteenth-ish Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox in seven days, and my brain has fully entered snooze mode for anything with that logo.
- The KitchenAid stand mixer and the All-Clad cookware set are genuinely things I'd consider — I'm outfitting two kitchens right now, one of which needs actual cookware, not just throw pillows. So there's real relevant inventory in here. But it's buried under an absolutely endless scroll of "more can't-miss kitchen deals," then a hard pivot into furniture, rugs, bedding, and a DesignDesk sweepstakes that has nothing to do with kitchens at all.
- Verdict: I'd scroll the top third for the KitchenAid/All-Clad deals, maybe screenshot one for the Pinterest folder, then bail once it turns into the everything-bagel furniture section. Not forwarding this — nothing here is surprising enough to text a friend about.

## What's working

- Named-brand ladder up top (Fellow, KitchenAid, Vitamix, All-Clad, Crate & Barrel Silicone) reads like an actual curated sale, not just generic "shop now."
- KitchenAid stand mixer and All-Clad 10-piece cookware set are legitimately in my "need this for the second house" bucket.
- Specific dollar/percent-off callouts sit right next to each product (up to 15%, 20%, $50, $100, 30%) instead of one vague banner claim.
- "Ships free" flagged on the "more can't-miss kitchen deals" section — for appliances and cookware that's a real decision-driver, not filler.

## What's weak

- This is at minimum my fifteenth Crate & Barrel-family email this week — I've lost the ability to distinguish this from Tuesday's "Double Rewards" send or the CB2 sale blast.
- The email doesn't stay on-topic. Subject says "kitchen brands," but by the midpoint it's dining, then living room furniture, then rugs, then bedding, then a design-consultation sweepstakes. Total scope creep.
- Zero mention of my Double Rewards status or member pricing anywhere near the kitchen products — if I'm a rewards member, treat me like one on the offer I'm actually looking at, not just in a banner three screens down.
- It's an absurdly long scroll for a phone — I counted something like a dozen distinct product modules before it even gets to the "Double Rewards" event section. Article's Labor Day email got its "up to 40% off" hero across in one screen; this makes me work for it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Make this an actual kitchen-only send — mixers, cookware, small appliances — and split furniture/bedding/rugs into its own campaign. Right now it's diluting a genuinely strong kitchen lineup with unrelated categories.
- 2. Put my Double Rewards status/math next to the KitchenAid and All-Clad items specifically, not just as a banner buried mid-scroll. That's the difference between "known customer" and "mass blast."
- 3. Lead with the single strongest deal (the $100 off All-Clad set, arguably) as the hero image instead of a generic lifestyle kitchen shot — CB2's better sends do this: one clear hero, one clear number, above the fold.
- 4. Drop or heavily trim the "more can't-miss kitchen deals" repeat section — it's redundant with what's already shown above it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `$100 off All-Clad, 20% off KitchenAid — free shipping on kitchen orders`
