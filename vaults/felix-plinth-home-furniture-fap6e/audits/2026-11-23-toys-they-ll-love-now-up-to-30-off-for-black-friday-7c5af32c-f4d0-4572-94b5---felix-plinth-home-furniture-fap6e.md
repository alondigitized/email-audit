# Toys they’ll love—now up to 30% off for Black Friday!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- I'm not the audience, and this is the third Kids send from Crate & Barrel today. I'm renovating two houses and shopping for sofas, lighting and rugs. This email is ball pits, wooden train sets and a play tent. The Kids sender name tells me that before I open it, and I opened it anyway to see how the Kids list handles Black Friday.
- The layout is clean. There's a hero with "Black Friday is on! Up to 30% off toys*", then a long single-column run of products, each with a sale price, a was-price, a customer quote and a Shop Now button. The tall stack with one button per product is easy to scan. But my inbox already has the 11/23 gift-guide send ("How to hear 'I LOVE it' about every gift") and "Our 15 top gifts for everyone on your list" from the main brand. This one adds nothing for me.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. It goes to the Kids folder I never read. The one thing I'd screenshot is the rocking horse, as a design reference. That's the only item that overlaps with how I shop.

## What's working

- The "Up to 30% off toys" hero and the free-shipping bar at the top make the offer obvious in the first screen.
- Each product block has sale price, was-price, a short customer quote and its own Shop Now button. That's more useful than the "SHOP NOW" tiles Wayfair's grid uses.
- The warm, neutral photography (wood, linen, soft tones) looks like a Crate & Barrel catalog and matches how I'd want my own rooms styled.
- The "shop up to 50% off Black Friday deals" category grid gives me a way into furniture and bedding.
- The "we love these for you" strip near the bottom feels like curation, not just a firehose.

## What's weak

- Toys-only content does nothing for a 36-year-old renovating two houses.
- This is the third Crate & Barrel send today, after the Kids toys version, the gift guide and the top-15 gifts. Cadence is a problem. The 11/22 "Huge news: NEW ITEMS" email already felt like a repeat.
- The email is extremely long, around a dozen scrolls. The photos are lovely, but the useful content is buried.
- The 30% toy offer sits next to a 50% category grid with an asterisk. The exclusions aren't visible, so I'm not sure what the real deal is.
- Nothing mentions rewards, swatches, shipping times for furniture or showroom info. That's what I'd need to make a furniture decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment better. Don't send me the Kids version when my history is furniture and lighting. A household with no kids signal should get one consolidated Black Friday email per day.
- 2. Cut the length. Four products plus the category grid would do the job.
- 3. Put the exclusions and the end date next to the offer, not behind an asterisk.
- 4. Lead with the furniture and bedding grid for non-parent segments, since it's already there at the bottom.
- 5. Add a line on delivery windows for large items.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Toys they'll love—now up to 30% off for Black Friday!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout from a Movable Ink-style template. It has mobile media queries and Outlook (MSO) fallbacks. The main confirmed problem is missing `alt` text on about 50 images. Header-level compliance and authentication items could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated, so footer and link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 97 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not confirmed good or bad.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are five third-party (LiveRamp) pixels.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is an open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c5494bba-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another tracking image.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times.
  - These are not defects. They are a privacy and deliverability surface, since several third-party calls fire on image load.
- **Template leftover:** The source contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, which was never replaced. It has no functional impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA:** roughly 50 `<img>` elements have no `alt`.
  - About 35 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, for example `d3119bec-…jpg`, `c1cafb52-…gif` and `f8c48ff1-…gif`. Image-blocking clients and screen readers get no content from these.
  - The others are spacers and pixels: `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F50px%5FWhite` and `…40px…`, the `rlcdn` pixels, `o.gif` and `f3c0d…png`. These should carry `alt=""` so they are explicitly decorative.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with conflicting values: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
- **Global `p` rule:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` forces centering on every paragraph unless it is overridden inline. This is a fragile global.
- **Doctype and language:** The XHTML doctype and `lang="en"` are present, and MSO conditionals are included. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the visible source or QA output. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values in image URLs are populated, not placeholders. The source is truncated, so tokens later in the body are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** QA notes the relay may strip it. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified omission.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** The same caveat applies. For a bulk sender, one-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be checked through the relay. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, matching the image and tracking subdomains, which suggests alignment, but this is unverified.
- **Footer unverified:** The visible HTML is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
**Not assessable.** All 97 links were skipped from probing, and the truncated source shows no `href` values with UTM parameters. No evidence supports a defect or a pass. The QA run reports a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~35 content images. Set `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels. This is the highest-confidence fix.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Re-check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw SMTP message, not the relay copy, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from original headers.
5. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer and unsubscribe link. Probe a sample of the 97 tracked links by following redirects to the final URL, and check that UTM parameters survive to the landing page.
6. Consider whether five LiveRamp pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `n=1` to `n=5`) are intentional, given the privacy and load cost.
