# NEW MARKDOWNS ADDED: Up to 60% off clearance
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a real clearance email — "up to 60% off clearance" right in the hero, category grid, actual dollar amounts on the "we love these for you" products. That's a step up from the Crate & Kids nursery parade I've been wading through all week. My eye landed on the wave/beach hero photo first, which is a weird choice for a clearance sale — feels like leftover summer creative, not "everything must go" urgency.
- The problem is this email tries to be six emails at once: clearance, Clean Kitchen line, JURA espresso machine, fall tabletop, dining/bedding furniture picks, AND the Crate Card upsell, AND the app download push, AND TheDesignDesk consult pitch. By the time I hit the espresso machine I've forgotten this was supposed to be a clearance email. That's the same "which brand am I in" whiplash I got from the CB2 "goop palette" send yesterday — except here it's one brand doing it to itself in a single scroll.
- I'd click "Shop All Clearance" up top since I'm mid-renovation on two houses and clearance is exactly my lane right now, but I'm not scrolling through eight more sections to get there. Not forwarding this — it's not tight enough to send a friend, it's a catalog dump.

## What's working

- The clearance % is stated up front, unlike Wayfair's faux-plant email that buried the actual offer under a vague subject.
- Category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding) makes it easy to scan for what I actually need for the reno.
- Actual product photos with visible prices in the "we love these for you" section — dining table, bed frame, nightstand — that's the kind of specificity CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" send did well too.
- "Shop All Clearance" and "Shop Now" CTAs are legible, dark buttons that stand out against white.

## What's weak

- The hero image is a beach/wave shot with zero product in it — for a clearance sale that's supposed to feel urgent, it reads like stock summer photography, not "act now, inventory's going."
- Way too much crammed in: clearance → Clean Kitchen → JURA espresso → fall table → furniture picks → Crate Card → app → DesignDesk. Pick one job for this email.
- The JURA espresso machine and Clean Kitchen sections have nothing to do with clearance pricing shown anywhere near them — no discount badge, so it reads like a totally different campaign got pasted in.
- No urgency device anywhere — no countdown, no "ends Sunday," nothing that makes 60% off feel time-bound the way Pottery Barn at least attempts with seasonal framing.
- As a Crate Card / Reward member callout mid-email, "clean kitchen" and "Clean Kitchen icon" reads like it's talking to someone else entirely — no personalization, no "based on what you've browsed."

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: a pure clearance email (hero product, % off, deadline, straight to the sale grid) and a separate "new arrivals" email for JURA/Clean Kitchen/fall table. Trying to do both kills the urgency of the clearance message.
- 2. Swap the beach hero for an actual clearance product shot with a price slash — show me the 60% off item, not a wave.
- 3. Add a deadline. "Ends Sunday" or "while supplies last" next to the 60% off — right now nothing here feels finite.
- 4. Move "we love these for you" (the priced furniture picks) up near the top — that's the strongest, most specific content in the email and it's buried two-thirds down.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance just dropped: up to 60% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off clearance — new markdowns on furniture & decor`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New dining, bedding & lighting markdowns — shop before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, rugs & decor now up to 60% off clearance`
