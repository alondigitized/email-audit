It's pretty, and it's also a mood board with no deal in it.
- I'm renovating two houses, so the "In-Stock Furniture" and "Bedding Ships Free" captions got my attention. They're tiny, though, and they sit in italic script on top of photos. ## What's working

- The hero photography is strong. It reads as a real room, not a catalog cutout, and that's what I save.
- "Gather together · In-Stock Furniture" speaks to my timeline. I care about delivery when I'm furnishing two houses.
- "Create a cozy guest room · Bedding Ships Free" is the one concrete perk. It's tied to a room I'm actually working on.
- The "Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" block near the bottom matches the 11/03 "Book your FREE design services" send. It's a useful CTA for someone with two projects.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has real products: the Banks Extendable Dining Table, the Farmhouse Platform Bed and the Cozy Cloud Quilt. That's more specific than the hero.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere above the fold. The "Extra 20% off" strip is gone and nothing replaces it, so I have no reason to act today.
- Several tiles use light script text over busy photos. "Create a cozy guest room" and "Home for the holidays" are hard to read on a phone without zooming.
- The sub-captions ("In-Stock Furniture", "Bedding Ships Free", "Sofas & Sectionals") are tiny, and the actual benefit is the smallest text in the email.
- There's nothing on the Pottery Barn rewards card or earn rates up top. Rewards math decides where I buy, and the "Earn 10% Back" and Affirm blocks are buried at the bottom.
- It's long. The "More Holiday Essentials" and "Great Gifts" tiles repeat the same holiday-decor idea three or four times.

- 1. Put one clear offer in the hero. If the 20% is over, say what replaced it. A line like "Free shipping on bedding + in-stock furniture in 1-4 weeks" would work.
- 2. Make the captions bigger and put them on a solid band, not over photos.
- 3. Move "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" up near the first CTA.
- 4. Cut the repeat tiles. Four rooms plus the product grid is enough.
- 5. Add a "Book a design appointment" button next to the hero CTA, since that's the one thing that reads as personalized.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an image-sliced, table-based XHTML template with 53 opaque click-tracking links. Almost every content image lacks `alt` text, three assets load over `http://`, and the subscriber's address is embedded in tracking and pixel URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 53 tracking links were skipped by the probe (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). Destinations, redirect chains and link health are unconfirmed.
- **Mixed content:** Three images use `http://`, which clients may block:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- **Stale asset:** The `2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner` image is a 2022 asset still referenced in a 2026 send.
- **Multiple open-tracking pixels:** There are three, from different vendors: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`, `svopn.potterybarn.com`, and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/99501ae0-...`. The first is a third-party ad-tech domain.
- **Plain-text part:** It is 10,192 of 14,373 characters URLs (about 71%), which trips the >70% ratio warning. The plain text is mostly raw tracking URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text:**
  - About 45 images have no `alt`.
  - Examples are the `1104_WED_AM_HolidayBigBets/slices_02–13.jpg` set, `Email-Footer/slices_01, 03–18.jpg`, all product images (such as `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg` and `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`), `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`, and the animated GIF `db469eb4-...gif`.
  - With images blocked, most of the content, and possibly the footer links, would be unreadable to screen readers and image-blocking clients.
  - The tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`) are flagged too, but they are non-content and only need `alt=""`.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP-style text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...`). It is invalid CSS and could break parsing of the rules that follow it in strict clients.
- **Layout tables:** The spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;`) has no `role="presentation"`, unlike the others.
- **External font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so those clients use fallback fonts.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** The preheader text has a raw `&` ("favorites & more") in XHTML.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm the alt text on linked images further down, or whether the `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` selectors match any elements.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered tokens** are visible in the supplied source.
- **Address in URLs:** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=...`
- The second is over unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and can end up in logs and referrers. Hashing or opaque IDs would avoid that.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. QA notes AgentMail may not relay them, so this is inconclusive. It is a real gap if the headers are actually absent, since Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified. Because the footer is image-sliced (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, no alt), any footer links should be checked for a text fallback.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters:** Every link is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` token, and no UTM parameters are visible in the source.
- **Landing pages unverified:** The probe was skipped, so I can't check that landing pages match the subject ("New arrivals with timeless holiday charm") or the preheader ("Christmas essentials, in-stock favorites").
- **Product images:** They are hotlinked from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which at least suggests the product detail pages are the intended destinations.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm CDN reference inside a Pottery Barn send. It is harmless but a dependency on another brand's host.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to all content and product images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. This is the highest-impact fix.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS, and retire the 2022 brand banner.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block, fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Replace the plaintext email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=` with a hashed or opaque identifier.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the original message headers, not the relay.
6. Re-run link probing against the redirect endpoints, or resolve a sample of the 53 links, to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.
7. Consolidate the three open-tracking pixels, and check whether the `d.turn.com` third-party pixel is needed.
8. Rebuild the plain-text part with readable copy and fewer raw URLs.
