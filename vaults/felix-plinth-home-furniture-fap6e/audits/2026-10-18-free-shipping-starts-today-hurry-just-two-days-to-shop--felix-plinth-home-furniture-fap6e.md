# Free shipping starts today! Hurry, just two days to shop
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel finally sent me something with a real offer. This is the sixth send from them in two days. The first five were nursery, toys and football, and I was about to mute the whole thread. "2 days only! free shipping on orders over $99!" is a hook I can use. I'm furnishing two houses, and shipping on big-ticket stuff is where the money goes. Then I scrolled past the Dutch ovens and the offer turned into a glassware catalog.
- The hero is a stack of enamel Dutch ovens in grey, green and blue. They look great, but they have nothing to do with the offer or with renovating. The nav tiles (Tabletop, Kitchen, Decor, Bedding, Halloween, Thanksgiving) are a scattershot list, and there's no furniture, lighting or rugs. After that it's a cranberry spritz mocktail and six glasses and bowls at $5.95 to $12.95. Nobody is going to hit a $99 threshold on a $10 bowl in an email like this. I'd also guess "exclusions apply" covers the large furniture I'd actually be shopping for, but the email never says.
- Verdict: I'd click once, on "Shop All Free Shipping", and bounce if it lands on a generic page. I wouldn't forward it. The mocktail block is the only part I'd save, and it belongs in a different folder from the renovation stuff.

## What's working

- The headline "2 days only! free shipping on orders over $99!" is clear, with a number and a deadline, and it sits right under the hero.
- The Dutch oven stack is a gorgeous hero photo. It's the kind of image I'd save to my Pinterest folder.
- "Shop All Free Shipping" is a solid, dark, full-width button that's easy to tap with a thumb.
- The Reward Dollars card block is relevant to me because I pick cards by rewards. "10% back in Reward Dollars" is a number I can do math on.
- The nav tiles are big and tappable, with a clean two-column layout.

## What's weak

- The hero is cookware, not furniture, so it doesn't match what I'm shopping for. The offer and the imagery don't line up.
- The mocktail section is a total pivot. It takes up about a screen and a half before I reach any product.
- Every product tile is a glass, bowl or tumbler under $13. That's nothing toward a $99 threshold, and it feels like filler.
- "Standard shipping; exclusions apply" is vague. I can't tell whether sofas, rugs or lighting count, and that's what I care about.
- The footer fine print is a dense wall of tiny text. It's the only place the real shipping terms live.
- This is the sixth Crate & Barrel send in two days (see 10/17 "Holiday toys for babies, toddlers & kids!" and "Football essentials, from tailgate to coffee table"), so cadence is already burning goodwill.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put furniture, lighting and rugs in the hero and the nav. Show a sofa or an area rug with "Ships free over $99" on it. Pottery Barn's "Colorful pillows, shipping free" did this much better, with a product I could act on and shipping in the subject.
- 2. Say what's excluded right under the headline, like "Furniture included" or "Excludes large furniture". One line saves me the footer hunt.
- 3. Cut the mocktail block, or move it below the product grid. The email should earn the click on the offer first.
- 4. Add a $100 to $300 product tier so the $99 threshold feels reachable, and show the free-shipping math.
- 5. Use the Reward Dollars card higher up, paired with the shipping offer. That's the combo I care about.
- 6. Cut cadence. I got six sends in 48 hours. One strong send beats six mediocre ones.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a table-based XHTML Transitional template. The main confirmed problems are about 28 images flagged without alt text, including content images, and several template-hygiene defects in the head. Unsubscribe headers and SPF/DKIM could not be verified, and the QA run says this is probably a relay capture limit. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 60 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Redirect chains, status codes and final destinations are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) are in the markup. The `s=` value starts with the same `dbe8da71…` prefix as the `mi_u` value on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels, so it looks like the same per-recipient identifier.
- **Tracking pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a024d6c5-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` look like open or tracking pixels. The `dv.` URL has no file extension.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 28 `<img>`):** These split into two groups.
  - **Content images:** About 21 images appear to be content.
    - 13 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `fc2adc28-…gif`, `1c9c4d87-…jpg`).
    - 1 is on `s7d5.scene7.com` (`2026_1019_CB_Tertiary_VF_Aarke_Social…`).
    - 7 are on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`. These look like personalized or recommendation images (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`).
  - **Tracking pixels:** `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5), `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel. They need `alt=""` and a `width`/`height` of 1 to avoid affecting screen readers.
  - **Impact:** The QA tool reports "missing," so it cannot tell a missing attribute from an empty one. Content images with no alt leave image-blocked and screen-reader users with nothing in place of the offer.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype requires `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** There are three `format-detection` tags: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`. There are also two charset declarations, `http-equiv Content-Type` and `<meta charset>`. These are harmless but redundant, and clients may apply only one `format-detection` tag.
- **Leftover placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It is a misspelled template comment and ships in production.
- **Redundant CSS:** `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` are declared in two separate `<style>` blocks. `@media (min-width:768px) img{max-width:600px}` is overridden by the later, overlapping `@media (min-width:640px) img{max-width:640px}`, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Global line-height:** `*{… line-height:100%}` applies a global line-height that the `.f10`, `.f14` and `.f20` mobile classes then have to override.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this is not fully confirmed. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated, which indicates personalization is resolving.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limit.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. QA notes the relay is the likely cause.
- **Footer:** The body-level unsubscribe link and postal address are in the truncated section and could not be verified.
- **Identifier in third-party pixels:** The apparent shared identifier in the LiveRamp `rlcdn.com` pixels may be relevant to privacy disclosures. This is an observation, not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 60 tracking links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 21 content images, especially the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` recommendation images and the promo images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Set `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions on the tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.` pixel).
2. Re-run the audit against the raw MIME headers, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
3. Resolve the tracking links by following redirects (or use a seeded click test) to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.

5. Clean up the template: drop the `IMPUT HERE` comment, merge the duplicate `format-detection`, charset and `.ReadMsgBody` declarations, and fix the overlapping `img` max-width media queries.
