# Shades of indigo—bedding, decor & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Indigo" — a color-story email, and it's actually a decent one. Blue-and-white ceramics, chambray bedding, that grid of textured product shots up top all reads coherent and kind of aspirational without being try-hard. This is the kind of moodboard I'd screenshot for the guest room if I were doing a full palette rather than piecing rooms together across two houses.
- But then it does the thing Pottery Barn always does to me lately — it can't just be one email. After the lookbook, I get a "Recommended Just For You" product grid (fine, that's useful), then it face-plants into "The Halloween Shop" with a spooky front porch, then pivots to "Free Design Services," then the app download pitch, then the 10% rewards card pitch, then Affirm financing. That's five different campaigns stapled to one color story. I already gave PB's actual Halloween send ("A special Halloween sneak peek 👻," 7/31) grief for the tonal whiplash — this is the same problem in miniature, just crammed into the bottom half of one email instead of being the whole thing.
- I'd click into the Indigo lookbook, honestly — the Belgian Flax Linen Duvet and that handwoven rug caught my eye for the primary bedroom. I would not forward this to anyone; it's a catalog scroll, not a "look at this" moment. Marking it read after I click through the duvet.

## What's working

- The indigo palette is genuinely consistent from the hero banner through the product grid — denim, chambray, blue-and-white ceramics all cohere, unlike CB2's recent "Bedroom + Bathroom Event" grab-bag.
- "Recommended Just For You" actually shows relevant big-ticket furniture (platform bed, dining table, swivel chair) instead of random tchotchkes — feels tailored to someone mid-renovation like me.
- The Free Design Services banner is a repeat of the 7/31 "RSVP for FREE design services" send I liked (8/10) — consistent messaging that I actually need right now with two houses in progress.
- Two clear CTAs up top ("Shop New Arrivals," "Explore the Lookbook") without burying the offer.

## What's weak

- The Halloween Shop insert breaks the indigo mood completely — pumpkins and a haunted porch sandwiched between a linen duvet and a "Book Now" design banner. Crate & Barrel at least keeps its Halloween pushes ("Halloween's on the brain," 8/10) as their own dedicated send instead of tacking it onto a color story.
- No actual offer or discount anywhere in this email — just "shop," "explore," "book," "learn more." Compare to the 8/1 "On sale & shipping free✨" send that led with a real number; this one's pure aspiration with no price signal.
- Swatch/sample program is nowhere — if you're pushing an "Indigo" color story to someone renovating, a "order a fabric swatch" CTA writes itself and it's missing.
- Rewards card math ("up to 10% back") is vague and buried near the bottom, same complaint I'd have with Wayfair's rewards mentions — no real number, no visible current balance/tier.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two emails: an Indigo lookbook send and a separate Halloween send. Splicing seasonal horror into a "deep waters, soulful blue" mood is the same bait-and-switch energy as the 7/31 sneak-peek email, just less blatant.
- 2. Add a swatch/sample CTA directly under the hero — "Order Indigo swatches free" would convert better than "Explore the Lookbook" for someone actually renovating and needs to see the fabric in their light.
- 3. Put a real number in the subject or hero — even 15% off new arrivals would beat the current all-aspiration, no-offer approach.
- 4. Move Free Design Services higher, right after the lookbook — it's the strongest asset in this email and it's currently stuck below Halloween noise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Indigo, swatched: order your free samples`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: the Indigo collection (+ free design help)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Denim blues for bedding, dining & everything between.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free design services, on us — book a session this week.`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Shades of indigo—bedding, decor & more"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template (`click.e.potterybarn.com` / `image.e.potterybarn.com` link patterns) with standard MSO/Outlook conditionals; automated QA passed 57% of checks with 0 hard issues and 3 warning categories, concentrated in missing alt text, non-HTTPS assets, and unverifiable authentication/compliance headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 54 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe since they're opaque SFMC redirect tokens — destinations cannot be verified from source alone.
- Multiple open-tracking pixels present: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/2af5b981-...?mi_mid=...`, `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, and `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`.
- The `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel is served over **plain HTTP**, flagged by QA as "Image uses http://" — non-HTTPS image requests are commonly blocked or rewritten by mail client image proxies, which can silently break open tracking for this pixel specifically.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **44 images missing `alt` text** across hero slices, product shots (curtain rod, platform bed, dining table, swivel chair, duvet cover, rug), footer slices, and tracking pixels — confirmed by QA. This degrades screen-reader/accessibility fallback and image-blocked rendering (no alt copy shown when images fail to load).
- **3 image sources served over HTTP instead of HTTPS**: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed content on an otherwise HTTPS-served email risks client-side blocking or broken rendering in strict mail clients.
- **Malformed `<style>` block**: the client-styling `<style>` tag contains literal PHP `header()` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`) embedded as raw text inside the CSS. This is not valid CSS and indicates unprocessed server-side template code leaked into the compiled HTML — a build/templating bug in the ESP pipeline, not something that will execute, but it pollutes the style block and could confuse strict CSS parsers in some clients.
- Spacer/tracking `.gif` from `edm.westelm.com` appearing in a Pottery Barn email suggests shared cross-brand ESP infrastructure (West Elm/Williams-Sonoma family) — not a functional bug, but worth noting as a cross-brand asset dependency.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is correctly resolved (not a raw merge tag) in the customer-service link and both open-tracking pixels — personalization substitution appears to be functioning.
- No unresolved merge syntax (e.g. `%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `AMPScript` fragments) observed in the truncated source.
- No issues found in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI may be unavailable; relies entirely on in-body unsubscribe link (not visible in the truncated source, so presence/placement of a footer unsubscribe link and physical mailing address could not be verified here).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe (POST) not supported at the header level, increasing unsubscribe friction and Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance risk.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be confirmed via the AgentMail relay; sender domain alignment (`e.potterybarn.com` matches the `From` domain) is consistent with an SFMC-managed subdomain, but authentication cannot be confirmed from headers alone.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All primary CTA and product links route through the SFMC click-tracking redirector (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), so UTM parameters and final landing-page URLs are opaque/encoded and cannot be verified without following the redirect chain.
- Direct (non-tracked) asset references to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` are static product images, not navigational links, so they don't indicate landing-page alignment either way.
- No issues found in the visible, verifiable portion — continuity cannot be fully assessed due to link obfuscation, not due to a confirmed defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all 44 flagged images, particularly product shots (currently zero fallback text for accessibility/image-blocked rendering).
2. Convert the 3 HTTP-only image sources to HTTPS to eliminate mixed-content risk.
3. Serve the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel over HTTPS to avoid silent tracking loss from proxy/client blocking.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` calls from the `<style>` block — indicates a template-compilation leak that should be fixed at the ESP build step.
5. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers to support one-click unsubscribe and reduce spam-complaint risk with major mailbox providers.
6. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly with the sending ESP, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured via the relay used for this audit.
