# FULL MATTRESSES. ARE. HERE.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-three. That's the Wayfair count now, and this is the fifth one in what feels like 36 hours — outdoor picks, bath mats, warehouse clearout, outdoor rugs, and now this landing on June 3rd. I want to put that on the table before I say a word about the email itself, because cadence is the single most important fact about what I'm looking at.
- The subject — three words, period-separated, all caps — is doing something deliberate. "FULL MATTRESSES. ARE. HERE." snaps like fingers across a crowded room. It irritates me and it also works, which I resent a little. Mattresses are actually live for me: I'm in the middle of spec'ing the guest room at the second house and I haven't picked a platform yet. So the relevance is genuine. Opening it: the hero section shows a styled bedroom shot with the "shop before they're gone" urgency frame, then a "Got It Now, Pay Later" financing block, then the email just... dissolves. Fire pits. Outdoor chairs. A dining set. I came for mattresses and got a catalog page. The mattress premise held for maybe 15% of the scroll before Wayfair remembered it also sells everything else.
- No click. Definitely no forward. Marking it read.

## What's working

- The period-as-emphasis subject trick actually stops the inbox scroll — annoying but effective, which is more than most Wayfair subjects this week earn.
- The mattress category is genuinely in my purchase window for the second home right now; no other Wayfair send this week could say that.
- The "Got It Now, Pay Later" block is legitimately useful — I do think about protecting my rewards card limit on big-ticket buys, and financing an order makes that math work.
- Bedroom photography is styled and room-context, not white-void product shots. That's the right call for a premium-adjacent play.
- Visual structure is clean: hero → financing → product grid. No broken images, no overlapping text, no render weirdness.

## What's weak

- The email doesn't stay on mattresses. "Let's complete your space" pivots immediately to fire pits and outdoor dining — a completely different renovation project and a different budget headspace.
- "Shop before they're gone" is vague scarcity that I no longer believe. Article, when running low stock, names the specific product and gives a number. Wayfair just gestures.
- All-caps subject reads cheap. Crate & Barrel does "UP TO 60% OFF!" and I find it grating — same energy here, and it's a spam-signal flag in modern inbox clients.
- No price anchor anywhere near the hero. I need to know if we're talking $199 full mattresses or $799 before I commit to a click. Pottery Barn's bedroom edit this week put pricing in the second line of the hero; that's the bar.
- This is the fifth Wayfair send in 36 hours. At this cadence, every email is ambient noise no matter what the offer is.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lock the email to mattresses end-to-end.** Cut the outdoor and living room cross-sells entirely. Pottery Barn ran a focused single-category bedroom edit recently — deep product selection, one CTA — and it earned an 8 from me. That's the model.
- 2. **Put a price anchor in the hero.** "Full mattresses from $179" or whatever the honest entry point is. The financing block implies cost anxiety; resolve it before the scroll, not after.
- 3. **Replace vague urgency with an inventory signal.** "Under 200 left in full" or "selling fast in [city]" beats "shop before they're gone" because it's falsifiable, and falsifiable urgency is trusted urgency.
- 4. **Lowercase the subject.** At this send frequency, all-caps is eroding credibility faster than it's earning attention.
- 5. **Add a firmness or size callout to the hero.** Article does construction callouts in the email body — thickness, materials, who it's for. I'm buying a full mattress; I want to see "plush / medium / firm" before I click, not after I land on a category page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your next full mattress — from $179, ships free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The full mattress shortlist (if you're still shopping)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Top-rated picks in plush, medium, and firm — with next-day delivery options.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes, firmness, fast shipping. Here's where to start.`
