- My gut reaction: it's the same CB2 sale again, with a different caption. I've had the 10/30 "THIS IS YOUR CHANCE" email, the 10/31 "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" email, and now this one, and the Warehouse Sale is the common thread in all of them. I'm renovating two houses, so a real markdown on furniture and lighting should get my attention. But "up to 60% off" with no product on screen tells me nothing. Where's the sofa? Which lamp?

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" type is enormous and my eye lands on it instantly.
- It's clean and nothing is broken. Wayfair's recent blank-hero emails make this look better by comparison.
- The Furniture, Lighting, Decor, Rugs buttons map to what I'm actually shopping for.
- The "10% back in Rewards Dollars at CB2 and Crate & Barrel" block speaks to how I buy. I'm always doing rewards math.
- "So much ships free" is a useful beat for furniture and decor. Shipping cost is a real factor when I'm filling two houses.

## What's weak

- There isn't a single product in the sale hero, just a blurry taupe throw. Pottery Barn and Article at least show me chairs and tables.
- The deadline, "Ends November 4," is buried in the fine print. "Final days" is the only urgency in the subject, and the hero never says when it ends.
- The five identical white buttons are a menu, not a call to action. Shop All doesn't stand out from the rest.
- The free-shipping hero is a cocktail glass and glassware. It's pretty, but it's about holiday entertaining, not my renovation.
- Cadence fatigue. After the 10/29, 10/30 and 10/31 sends, this adds no new information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put three to five real sale products in the hero, such as a sofa, a lamp and a rug, each with the price shown as was/now. That's what Pottery Barn's "Extra 20% off" sends do, and it gives me a reason to click.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Nov 4 · 11:59 PM PT," ahead of the fine print.
- 3. Merge or space out the sends. Five emails in a few days with the same sale is why I'm in a permanent CB2/Crate snooze.
- 4. Swap the glassware shipping hero for furniture and lighting that ships free, or for a delivery timeline. It would also help to bring back the swatch program from 10/31 here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound (`lang="en"`, XHTML doctype, MSO conditionals), but it has 11 non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels, 7 content images with no alt text, and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. The automated 57% pass rate comes mostly from header checks that the AgentMail relay can't verify, not from confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **11 pixels load over plain `http://`:**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection may block or rewrite these, and some clients flag mixed content. Their data won't be recorded reliably.
- **Several pixels fire from one email.** There is `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/89a9b7f0-...` and the 11 Dotomi calls. This adds load time and failure points.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **7 content images lack `alt`.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`: `cddb5f0d-…`, `d8a6a421-…`, `0b39bba5-…`, `cf79ef63-…`, `51e3ac34-…`, `538b87c8-…`, `8087f594-…`. With images blocked, these render as blank space, and screen readers announce the filename.
- **Tracking pixels lack `alt` too** (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, `pub1–10.php`, `UCMController`). They should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **The viewport tag blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **`<title>` doesn't match the subject.** It reads "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", not the subject "Final days of the Warehouse Sale". This is minor and only matters in web-view or archive contexts.
- **A template placeholder was left in the production source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It's harmless but sloppy.
- **Web fonts load from two domains.** `@font-face` pulls from `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`). Clients that block remote fonts will fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif, which is the stated fallback, so this isn't a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` values in the pixel URLs are resolved. Because the HTML is truncated, the body copy and later links could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was missing,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be checked. The From domain is `mail.cb2.com`.
- **The body unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated excerpt.** They need to be checked in the full HTML.
- **The Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-sync pixels are cross-site tracking.** They are worth confirming against the sender's privacy disclosures. This is a note, not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 57 links are tracking redirects that were skipped, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. The QA can't confirm that the landing pages match the "Warehouse Sale" campaign or carry `utm_*` values.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all 11 Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop them. If the vendor can't serve HTTPS, remove the calls.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images, and `alt=""` to every tracking pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Fix the inspection gaps. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct send rather than the relay, and resolve the 57 redirects to check destinations and UTMs.
5. Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment, and set `<title>` to the campaign subject.
6. Consolidate the tracking pixels to cut the number of third-party calls per open.
