# UP TO 50% OFF + Double Rewards
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the thirteenth CB2 email since 10/30, and the "Long Weekend Sale" hero is still up on November 8th. Yesterday's "Show off your impeccable taste" and 11/06's "Five-Star Picks" carried the same banner. The countdown timer is the only new thing, and "Final Days" with a ticking clock at least looks like real urgency. The subject has an offer and Double Rewards, so I know what I'm getting before I open it.
- Once I'm in, the email is stacked with offers. There's a sale, a free-swatch offer, free shipping and a card promo. That's four messages and no hero product. The one thing I care about is the free swatches, because I'm matching finishes across two houses. It's buried in a thin banner halfway down. I'd skim it, maybe tap the swatches, and mark it read.
- I wouldn't forward it. A friend who isn't renovating wouldn't care about any of it.

## What's working

- The countdown timer and "Final Days" give the urgency a deadline I can see (03 days, 15 hours).
- "Up to 50% off" is large and readable over a sofa shot that suits my taste.
- The four category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors) are big tap targets and match what I'm shopping for.
- "Order 10 free swatches" is the most useful thing in the email for a two-house reno.
- The Double Rewards block says "through Nov 17th, 2026" and "20% back", and the dates are clear. I'd do the rewards math before buying.

## What's weak

- The "Long Weekend Sale" copy is stale. There's been no long weekend since the 11/05 "STARTS NOW" send, and the footer says the sale ends 11/11.
- There's no hero product. Nothing is named, priced or linked to a single item, so the email never gives me a reason to click one thing.
- The rewards block has a red strikethrough on "10%" and "20%" that looks like a rendering glitch. Because of it I'm not sure what the rate is.
- The card terms are in the grey footer fine print. The "free shipping" claim has an asterisk and no threshold, so I can't tell whether it covers furniture, and that is what I care about most.
- The footer packs in a lot: an app promo, 10% back, a gift list and 15% off texts. It reads as clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero product or room. Put a dining or bedroom set in the top slot with a real price, and drop the "Long Weekend" label.
- 2. Move the free swatches up under the hero and make them the main button, since swatch programs help with color matching.
- 3. State the shipping terms in plain language ("Free shipping on orders over $X, including furniture") and don't tuck them behind an asterisk.
- 4. Fix the strikethrough on the rewards rate and show one clear number.
- 5. Cut the footer promos down to the app and the rewards card.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last 3 days: 50% off + 20% back in Rewards`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free swatches + 50% off, ends Tuesday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, sideboards and lighting, plus free shipping on 1000+ items.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order 10 free swatches before the sale ends 11/11.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO/VML fallbacks and a single mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed problems are 9 content images with no alt text and 11 non-HTTPS third-party Dotomi pixels. The source is truncated and 61 tracking links were not probed, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link destinations could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not verified.** The QA probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so redirect chains, status codes and landing destinations are unconfirmed.
- **11 images load over plain `http://`**, which risks blocking or mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- These Dotomi (Conversant) requests look like cookie-sync pixels. Clients that proxy images over HTTPS (Gmail, Apple Mail privacy protection) may drop them. They also add 11 third-party requests to the load.
- **Tracking pixels are present and served over HTTPS:**
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...`
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/4171017a-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- Both lack alt text. This is a minor accessibility issue for pixels, and it is better fixed with `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Nine content images have no `alt` attribute.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `a78c5af8-...jpg`, `93c7b43a-...jpg`, `ce4e1b39-...jpg`
  - `568bb9f7-...jpg`, `93dba763-...jpg`, `64f0e585-...jpg`
  - `54836071-...jpg`, `55fbf30d-...jpg`, `345f2993-...jpg`
- With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show as filenames or nothing. If the offer text sits inside the images, it is lost.
- **The viewport meta tag has `maximum-scale=1`.** This prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **A template placeholder was left in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless to rendering but shows an unfinished template.
- **The `<title>` is generic:** "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME". It does not match the subject line, "UP TO 50% OFF + Double Rewards".
- **Font sources are inconsistent.** `@font-face` rules point at `cb2.com` for some fonts and `crateandbarrel.com` for "HNW05". The first "Lineto Brown Sub" block has `.eot` as its base `src` with no format hint. This is low impact because the web fonts fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif.
- No other structural problems are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` parameter on `o.gif` is a 64-character hash, not a raw email address. The rest of the body was truncated, so this only covers the visible portion.

- **Not confirmed as defects.** The QA flags the three items below as header-capture limits of the AgentMail relay, not as confirmed sender faults.

  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) is not found.

- **Sending domain.** The `From` domain is `mail.cb2.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment with the DKIM `d=` domain could not be verified.
