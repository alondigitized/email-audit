# AVAILABLE AGAIN
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "AVAILABLE AGAIN" — restock alert, not a sale. My gut reaction: fine, but this is basically a full catalog dump with a really thin hook. The hero says "Seize the Moment... it's back in stock" over a maroon velvet chair, and then instead of showing me *the thing* that's back, CB2 just marches me through Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage, Sofas, Lighting, Wall Art — every single category "IN STOCK" like it's their whole spring line, not a restock.
- This is the fifth CB2 email in my inbox this week (August Sale on the 6th, the marble bit on the 5th, the double "ends tonight" chaos also on the 5th, Bed & Bath on the 4th) and now they've pivoted from urgency-sale mode to "everything's back" mode. It's actually a nice change of pace tonally, but the execution buries the one thing a restock email needs — which specific SKU sold out and is now available. I scroll through nine product categories before I get any sense of what "available again" even refers to.
- I'm not clicking this one. It reads more like a monthly lookbook than a targeted alert, and I've already got CB2 fatigue from this week. Not forwarding it either — nothing here is specific enough to send a friend.

## What's working

- The maroon velvet swivel chair hero is genuinely nice-looking and on-brand for CB2's whole aesthetic — good photography, no complaints there.
- Category sections are clean and scannable — Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage, Sofas each get their own "IN STOCK" + Shop Now block, so if I *am* hunting for something specific I can jump to it.
- The "Interior Design Services" block with "expert design, tailored to you, free consultation" is actually relevant to me mid-reno on two houses — that's the one section that made me pause.
- "MORE FOR YOU" personalized product row at the bottom (Andes bed, Archer nightstand, Paley chair, Rondo table) is a decent touch — feels like it's pulling from browse history rather than generic bestsellers.
- $1,000 giveaway for downloading the app is a real, concrete incentive, unlike vague "join us" copy.

## What's weak

- The hero literally never tells me *what* is back in stock — "it's back in stock" with no product name is a wasted headline slot. Wayfair's "Fearless design, bold layers" send (8/10 in my inbox) at least anchored on a styled room; this doesn't even anchor on a product.
- Nine stacked "IN STOCK" category blocks is not a restock email, it's a full catalog re-send — by the third or fourth one (Sectionals, Storage, Sofas) my eyes glaze over exactly like they did during the CB2 "two sales end tonight" double-hero mess on the 5th.
- No prices shown until the very bottom "MORE FOR YOU" strip — everything above that is just "Shop Now," no anchor on cost, no sense of whether "back in stock" means back at full price or a special price.
- This is my fifth CB2 email in a week and there's zero acknowledgment of that — no "we know, we know" wink, no consolidation. Compare to Article, which at least alternates sale-mode and editorial-mode sends so it doesn't feel like the same button every day.
- The "$1,000" giveaway and "10% back" rewards blurbs are crammed into tiny footer tiles that are easy to miss if I'm not scrolling slow — CB2 buried its best hooks (real money) under nine product category blocks.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the hero with the actual restocked hero SKU and its price** — if the velvet chair is what's back, name it and price it right under "Seize the Moment." A restock email should feel like "the thing you wanted is here," not "here's our whole catalog."
- 2. **Cut category count from nine to three** — Bedroom, Sectionals, Sofas is plenty; Coffee Tables/Storage/Lighting/Wall Art can be one combined "Shop the Restock" grid instead of five separate full-width sections.
- 3. **Move the $1,000 app download and 10% rewards blocks up, not buried at the very bottom** — those are real incentives that get lost after a nine-section scroll.
- 4. **Show prices earlier** — at least on the hero and top two categories, not just the bottom "MORE FOR YOU" row.
- 5. **Acknowledge send frequency** — even a one-line "back by popular demand" note would land better than a fifth blast this week with no self-awareness.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back in stock: the chair everyone wanted`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Restocked: your saved favorites are back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The velvet swivel chair (and more) just came back — shop before it's gone again`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sold out fast last time. Now available again, starting at $399`
