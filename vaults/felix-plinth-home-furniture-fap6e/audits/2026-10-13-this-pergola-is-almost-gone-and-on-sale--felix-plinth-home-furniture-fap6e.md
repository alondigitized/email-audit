# This pergola is almost gone – and on sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- Thirteenth Wayfair email this week, and the hero is a blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon. The subject promised one pergola that's "almost gone," and the top of the email shows nothing. My eye went straight to the three pergolas under "on sale: from your browsing," which is the one block that rendered. It has "Sale" badges, "Now" prices and star ratings. That block is what the subject should have led with.
- Below it, the dining sets, bedding, soft pastels, cocktails, storage and deals-of-the-day sections are mostly lavender and blue boxes with product names in alt text. It looks like a wireframe. This is the same failure as the 10/12 "Your roundup of pergolas" send, which I scored 3. Crate & Barrel's "final weekend: up to 20% off sofas & sectionals" put a real sofa on screen. Pottery Barn's dark-green "Fall Sale" strip is repetitive, but it always loads.
- Would I click? Maybe the pergola block, because I did browse them. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read. I'm renovating two houses and have never put a pergola on a list, so "from your browsing" feels like the algorithm following me around.

## What's working

- The "on sale: from your browsing" block is the best part. Three pergolas have red "Sale" badges, "Now" prices and star ratings, so I could judge them in two seconds.
- Each section has a button with a verb: "Shop bedding essentials," "Shop storage," "Shop cocktail style." They're full-width and easy to tap on a phone.
- The orange-red "deals of the day" band breaks up the lavender and gives the page some rhythm.
- The footer's Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing and Exclusive Brands icons are a useful reminder. I'm a rewards-card guy, so financing on furniture matters to me.
- The "Big Help. Small Price Tags." wine-fridge banner is the only module outside the pergola block that loaded cleanly.

## What's weak

- The hero is a blank white box with a broken-image icon, so the "almost gone" pergola never appears.
- Roughly 80% of the product tiles are empty boxes with serif alt text like "Bergan Animal Print Comforter Set." I can't judge style or color from that.
- "Almost gone" has no stock count, no countdown and no deadline, so the urgency is vague.
- The sections (dining, bedding, pastels, cocktails, storage) have nothing to do with the pergola in the subject. It reads as a dump of everything.
- There's no swatch, sample or delivery-timing info anywhere. For furniture, that's what makes me click.
- This is the same pergola push I got on 10/11 ("Your pergola search is over!") and 10/12. The cadence is exhausting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix image delivery and send a fallback hero. If the hero image fails, the email should still show the pergola, its price and the percent off in live text. Wayfair has had three broken sends in a row.
- 2. Lead with the one pergola from the subject: a big image, "$XXX off," a "only 4 left" stock line and a single "Shop this pergola" button. Cut the five unrelated sections.
- 3. Cap the cadence. One personalized send a day, not 13 a week, and suppress categories I've never opened, like pergolas, cocktails and Libbey glassware.
- 4. Add furniture-buyer info: delivery window, free swatches or samples, and a rewards-math line such as "earn X points."

- **Subject Alt B:** `The pergola you viewed is on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ships by Oct 24. Free returns. Prices end tonight at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 5 more picks from your browsing history, all under $1,000.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links use consistent per-link IDs, and the QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). Its main technical problems are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, missing alt text on content images, one malformed image URL, and missing compliance and authentication headers. The headers may only be missing because of the AgentMail relay, so that is unconfirmed. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what the QA output and the visible markup show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe results are inconclusive, not broken.** 7 of the 12 probed links returned 429, for example:
  - `/curated/bedding-sets~ev623643.html` ("Shop bedding essentials →")
  - `/daily-sales/powdery-pastels~e314178.html`
  - `/daily-sales?_emr=…` ("Shop Sale")
  - `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` (twice)
- **The pergola links were never checked.** The subject line is about a pergola, but both pergola product URLs (`/Louvered-Pergola-with-Panel-PullDown-Screen-Outdoor-Alum…` and `/Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola-Aluminum-Pergola-With-Adjustabl…`) were among the 4 links unprobed after the 70s time budget. So the main link the email promises is unverified. The `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout` and homepage links were also unprobed.
- **Probe coverage is low.** 67 of 79 links were skipped by design (footer, social, utility).
- **The app link falls back to a non-HTTPS URL.** The onelink URL sets `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so non-app users get an `http://` destination that depends on a redirect.
- **The `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` image URL is malformed.** `%oo` is not valid percent-encoding, so the request may 400 or fail to load depending on the CDN. This was not verified by a fetch.
- **Open tracking relies on two kinds of pixel.** One is `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (HTTPS) and the other is 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` impression pixels. The same `p=ee92bf52-…` ID is on every `imp` request and on every link's `_emr`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Impression pixels are insecure and missing alt text.**
  - All 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels use `http://`. The QA check says non-HTTPS sources may be blocked, which would drop those impression counts in clients that block mixed content.
  - All 20 have no `alt`. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Content images are missing alt text.**
  - `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (twice) and `stars_11.png` are review-rating graphics. Without alt text, the ratings are inaccessible and nothing shows if images are blocked.
  - `72HR_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png` is a promo banner.
  - `small_appliances_01_gif.gif` and `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png` are also missing alt text.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The visible root is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang`.
- **No other rendering issues were found in the visible CSS.** It includes Gmail (`u ~ div`) and Outlook-app (`body[data-outlook-cycle]`) fixes and mobile breakpoints at 480px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or the QA output. The per-recipient IDs (`_emr`, `_eml`, `mdlid`) are all populated. The truncated body could not be checked for tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (WARN).** The QA note says this may be a relay capture artifact. Treat it as unverified, not confirmed absent.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. If it is truly missing, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` could not be checked.
- **Footer items not confirmed.** The QA output does not say whether the in-body unsubscribe link resolved, and the truncated source doesn't show the physical postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses Wayfair's own parameters instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=7`, `csnid=…`, `refid=MKTEML_121639`, `emlid=101` and `maiid=16435`. This is consistent across links and not a defect. It does mean third-party analytics (GA, for example) won't pick up campaign attribution without extra mapping.
- **Landing alignment is unverified for the subject's offer.** The pergola product URLs weren't probed, so the match between "This pergola is almost gone" and the destination is unchecked. The probed destinations are curated and category pages that returned 429.
- **Asset names look stale, but this is inferred.** `0728_Outd%oorClearance…` and `72HR_Spring2026…` suggest creative reused from earlier campaigns. Whether they match current offers can't be determined from the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to `https://` and add `alt=""` to them and to `t.wayfair.com/b.php`.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the star-rating images (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars"), the promo banners and the GIF.
3. Fix the `Outd%oorClearance` filename or URL encoding and confirm the image returns 200.
4. Re-probe the two pergola URLs, `/72-hour-clearout` and the homepage link with a slower rate or retry, to confirm 200 and landing alignment.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw headers from the original send, not the relay copy. Do the same for SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
6. Add `lang="en"` to the `<html>` element.
7. Change `af_web_dp` to an `https://` URL.
