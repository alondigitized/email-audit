# Sale on sale 🎉 EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Same burgundy banner, same "labor day sale," same "Extra 20% off" — this is the sixth Pottery Barn email I've catalogued this week, and at this point I could draw the header from memory. What's actually new here is the "Recommended Just For You" grid, and I'll give them credit — the Toscana Extendable Dining Table and the Banks Extendable Dining Table are exactly the kind of thing I'm actually shopping for on the second-home reno, not filler. But it's buried under a category nav grid and a "More Deals from Our Family of Brands" cross-sell block before I even get there.
- The Halloween Shop insert mid-email is the same jarring left-turn I flagged in yesterday's "front door Halloween-ready" send — porch ghosts and pumpkins wedged between dining tables and a design-services CTA. It doesn't ruin the email, it's just more proof nobody's coordinating what goes into these.
- I wouldn't click. I've already seen "extra 20%" three times this week and I'm numb to it. If the "Recommended Just For You" grid had been the hero instead of buried at position 5, I might have. Not forwarding — my wife would just say "another Pottery Barn one?"

## What's working

- The "Recommended Just For You" grid actually has relevant furniture — dining tables and a swivel chair, not random bath mats.
- "10% Back in Rewards" callout is real for someone financing two renovations at once — I filed that mentally even if I didn't click.
- Free Design Services block is a legit differentiator vs. Wayfair, which has never once offered me a designer.
- Clean burgundy/white palette stays consistent top to bottom, no render bugs, everything loaded.

## What's weak

- Sixth near-identical "Extra 20% off" burgundy banner this week — I'm fully desensitized to this discount tier now.
- The personalized product grid is buried under two rows of category nav and a brand cross-sell block before I get to it.
- Halloween Shop insert breaks the reno-focused flow again, same as yesterday's send.
- No swatch/sample program or fabric/finish content anywhere, which is what would actually move me on the dining tables shown.
- Generic "labor day sale" header text feels stale — Labor Day was days ago at this point in the cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with "Recommended Just For You" — move the personalized grid above the category nav. That's the only section here that felt like it was for me specifically, and it's currently five scrolls deep.
- 2. Retire the burgundy "Extra 20%" banner for at least one send — Crate & Barrel varies its hero enough that I don't feel like I'm reading the same email twice; Pottery Barn hasn't given me that break once this week.
- 3. Add fabric/finish/swatch info directly under the dining tables and bed — that's the actual blocker for me converting on furniture, not the discount.
- 4. Pull the Halloween Shop into its own send, not wedged between furniture recs and design services.
