# Shopping for something? Start with our bestsellers.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Crate again. By my count this is at least the fifth send from them in about 24 hours. My first reaction was "I already own this email." It's the same bestsellers-with-5-star-quotes layout as this morning's "Bestsellers made for childhood" and "The bestseller edit: Our 4 most-loved sofas," except it covers the whole store. That's weaker than the sofa one, which at least knew what I was shopping for.
- The "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" banner is the best thing in it, and it's a thin strip above the hero. Under it, the hero is a "So classic & will last us for years" review quote. Below that is a very long scroll: sofas, bedroom, dining, a KitchenAid mixer, tabletop, bedding, rugs, lighting, kitchen deals. I'm furnishing two houses, so I'd skim it. But nothing here asks me to act today.
- I'd click once on the sofa block, because the price break is real. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my Pinterest folder and mark it read.

## What's working

- The "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" banner is a concrete discount in my category.
- The customer-quote format ("Our forever couch." "Brings the dining room together.") with star ratings feels more credible than brand copy. It's good social proof.
- Each module has its own specific button ("Shop Sofas & Sectionals," "Shop Dining Furniture," "Shop Bedroom Furniture"). That's better than a single vague CTA.
- The small product rows under each photo (collection names, "Built-in Charging," fabric and finish options) give me real details to compare.
- The Crate & Barrel card block ("it all adds up to a home you love") shows rewards math, which matters to a highest-rewards-card buyer like me. The Design Desk appointment block near the bottom is a nice touch for renovators.

## What's weak

- Cadence is the main problem. This is the fifth Crate email today, and it overlaps almost completely with the earlier "4 most-loved sofas" send. The lamp problem I hate, but with a whole brand.
- There's no deadline. "It's on" tells me nothing about when it ends, so I have no reason to act today.
- The offer says "up to 20%" and covers only sofas and sectionals. Most of the scroll (mixers, bowls, bedding) has no visible discount, so the headline promise gets diluted.
- It's too long, and every module has the same weight. By the kitchen deals and the "we love these for you" grid I'd stopped reading. The app and Design Desk blocks at the bottom are buried.
- Nothing addresses furniture logistics: no delivery timing, no swatch or fabric-sample program, no showroom tie-in. That's what I'd need when buying across two houses.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job: the sofa and sectional sale. Add an end date next to the 20% banner, and drop the mixer and bowl modules. Alternatively, segment so a sofa browser like me never gets the kids and kitchen versions.
- 2. Cap sends. After four near-identical emails, this one should have been suppressed for anyone who already opened the sofa email.
- 3. Add a swatch-and-sample line and a delivery-window line under the sofa section, plus the card rewards math in the banner area instead of the footer.
- 4. Move the Design Desk appointment up. For a two-house renovator that's the actual hook.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order free fabric swatches before the sale ends.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML template with a `lang="en"` attribute, a viewport meta tag and MSO conditional comments. The main confirmed problems are about 53 images flagged for missing alt text and a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking not verified:** 92 links go through tracking or click-redirect domains, so the HTTP probe skipped them. Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Repeated third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5` is the same pixel fired five times (rlcdn.com belongs to LiveRamp). Each request adds load and sends the same hashed identifier (`s=`) to a third party. One call would do.
- **Per-recipient tokens in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c871d02b…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are personalized or dynamic content. Rendering depends on those endpoints staying live, and the token travels in the URL.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 18,655 of 25,838 characters (about 72%) are URLs. The tracking-link volume causes this, and it can raise spam-filter scores.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 53 images):** The QA check flagged these:
  - 38 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/.../m/1/*.jpg|png`, many of which are likely product or content images.
  - 7 Movable Ink images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - The tracking pixels: `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c871d02b-…`.

  The check doesn't say whether `alt` is absent or empty. The pixels should carry `alt=""`. The content and product images need descriptive alt text. If images are blocked, those sections will be blank.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Overlapping media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times with different values.
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - There is a duplicate charset declaration (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).

  None of these is likely to break rendering.
- **Cannot confirm:** the HTML is truncated, so I couldn't verify `role="presentation"` on layout tables, heading structure or CTA link text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncated body means this only covers the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't treat this as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers to confirm.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected with the relay and isn't evidence of failure.
- **Not verifiable:** the visible-footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are beyond the truncation point.
- The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent for alignment.

4. **Headers:** Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and run SPF, DKIM and DMARC checks on the original message rather than the relayed copy.
5. **Links:** Follow the 92 redirects to confirm final status codes, UTM parameters and landing pages.
