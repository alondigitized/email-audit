## What's weak

- Three stacked sales (40% furniture, 30% bedroom+bath, 50% outdoor) muddies which one is actually "the" event — Pottery Barn's quilts email at least stayed on one clear offer.
- "More For You" grid is decent but generic — a bed, a stool, a nightstand, a dining table — feels like inventory filler after three hero banners already did the heavy lifting.
- Price ranges are all over the map ($299 side table to $999 dining table) with zero clue if any of it applies to the "up to 40/30/50%" claims above it.
- This is at minimum my fourth CB2 email this week — the cadence is catching up with the quality even when the creative is good.

- 1. Pick one hero sale and lead with it — "Furniture Event, up to 40% off" as the whole subject, then push bedroom/bath and outdoor into a secondary block instead of three equal-weight banners.
- 2. Tag the "More For You" grid with the actual % off per item so it connects back to the sale banners instead of floating as generic inventory.
- 3. Put "ships in as little as one week" in the subject line or preheader — it's the strongest differentiator in the whole email and it's buried under the fold.
