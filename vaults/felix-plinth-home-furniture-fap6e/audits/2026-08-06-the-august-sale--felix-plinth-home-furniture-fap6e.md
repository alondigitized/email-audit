# THE AUGUST SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- "THE AUGUST SALE" — okay, CB2, we're doing a plain seasonal sale now instead of the marble bit or the "two sales end tonight" double-hero chaos from yesterday. Honestly this one's cleaner than what I've seen from them all week — one hero, one number (40% off), one CTA. But I've now gotten a CB2 email every single day since Sunday (marble Tuesday, two-sales Wednesday, bed & bath Thursday, Goop Monday, and now this), and my brain has fully filed CB2 under "ignore, they'll email again tomorrow." That's not this email's fault exactly, but it's the water it's swimming in.
- The reward-dollars block caught my eye more than the actual sale did — 10% back at CB2/Crate & Barrel/Crate&Kids/Hudson Grace plus a new 4% back on dining through year-end is a real, specific loyalty mechanic, and I've got the Barrel card sitting in my wallet already. That's more relevant to me than another "shop the sale" banner. But then it's immediately followed by an app-download push for a $1,000 sweepstakes and a "leave a review, win $1,000" ask — two separate sweepstakes asks back to back reads like they're padding the email, not talking to me.
- I wouldn't click past the hero. I've already got two mid-reno houses of furniture decisions open in other tabs from Crate & Kids and Pottery Barn today — I'm not opening a third catalog for "up to 40% off" with no category specificity. Not forwarding, not really reading past the fold.

## What's working

- Hero is dead simple — big "THE AUGUST SALE," "UP TO 40% OFF," one black "SHOP THE SALE" button, no competing hero stacked underneath like yesterday's email.
- Category tiles (Furniture, Decor, Lighting, Shop the Sale) let me jump straight to what I care about instead of a generic homepage dump.
- The Reward Dollars block is genuinely useful — specific percentages tied to specific stores I actually shop, not vague "member perks" language.
- The "New & Best Selling: Lighting" mini-feature with a single hero pendant is a nice change of pace from wall-to-wall sale banners.

## What's weak

- Fifth CB2 email this week and it's the second one today counting the marble send from yesterday — cadence is exhausting, and "up to 40%" with no anchor product makes it feel interchangeable with Tuesday's and Wednesday's sends.
- Two stacked sweepstakes asks (app download for $1,000, review for another $1,000) right after the loyalty block dilutes the actual reward message instead of reinforcing it.
- "More for you" personalization grid is just six random SKUs (a bed, a nightstand, a stool, a side table, a dining chair, another bed) — no shared style thread, doesn't read as actually personalized to me.
- Legal fine print at the bottom is a wall of gray text with no breathing room — feels like the one place CB2 didn't bother with design.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to three CB2 sends a week max — I'm now getting a marble bit, a double-sale, a bed & bath countdown, a Goop tie-in, and a plain August sale all in six days. Even Wayfair, who I complained about for volume before, doesn't hit me this hard.
- 2. Put a real number in the subject — "up to 40% off" belongs in the subject line, not just the hero. Pottery Barn's "Warehouse Sale" send worked because the number showed up early; do that here too.
- 3. Merge the two sweepstakes into one clear ask instead of two competing $1,000 pitches back to back — pick the one that drives more value (probably the review ask) and cut the other from this send.
- 4. Make the "More for you" grid actually feel curated — group by room or finish, the way Article's room-tour format at least implies a point of view, instead of six unrelated SKUs.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your CB2 Rewards + 40% Off This Week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New lighting, reward dollars that add up, and 40% off sitewide.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `10% back at CB2, Crate&Barrel, Crate&Kids — plus 40% off today.`
