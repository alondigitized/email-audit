# Get the look: The new traditional dining room
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Get the look: The new traditional dining room" — this is a good one, actually. I'm mid-renovation on two houses and dining rooms are exactly the kind of big-ticket, need-to-get-right room where I want inspiration before I drop real money. The hero shot of the traditional dining table with the blue-and-white china is doing real work, and the follow-through into "Heirloom Dining Essentials" dinnerware and the Toscana Extendable Dining Table with actual dimensions listed (60"-124.5") is the kind of specificity I complained was missing from a lot of these sends. This feels like the same team that made the 8/2 "heritage design" email I gave an 8 — indigo ceramics again, warm wood furniture again, actual product info again.
- Where it loses me: about two-thirds down, right after a genuinely useful "Complete the Look" module (lighting, rugs, table linens, glassware — smart, that's exactly how I actually shop a room), it face-plants into "The Halloween Shop" with a skeleton-and-pumpkin porch banner. I flagged this exact whiplash yesterday with the "Enchanting Front Door" send — Pottery Barn cannot decide if it's August or October, and stacking a genuine dining-room-renovation email with a skeleton porch is a tonal gut-punch. Crate & Barrel doesn't do this to me; when they push a collab they stay on-message start to finish.
- Net: I'd click "Shop Dining Tables" or the Toscana table link, no hesitation. I would not forward this — it's not weird or exciting enough to text a friend about — but I'd probably screenshot the indigo dinnerware for my own inspo folder. Mark as read after clicking through, not deleting.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — traditional dining table, "New Traditional" headline, "Shop Dining Tables" button, no scrolling needed to know what this email is about.
- "Heirloom Dining Essentials" dinnerware and the "Indigo Blues" lookbook module reuse the same blue-and-white palette from the 8/2 heritage email — consistent, not random, and it's a look I already responded well to.
- The Toscana Extendable Dining Table listing includes actual dimensions (60"-124.5") — this is the exact product-detail signal I said Crate & Barrel and CB2 sometimes skip, and Pottery Barn nailed it here.
- "Complete the Look" (Lighting, Rugs, Table Linens, Glassware) mirrors how I actually shop a room — one category at a time, not just "browse everything."
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, logo and nav crisp at the top.

## What's weak

- The Halloween Shop banner mid-email is the same complaint I had yesterday about the front-door email — it breaks a genuinely coherent dining-room narrative and makes the whole send feel like two different campaigns stapled together.
- No actual offer anywhere — no %, no $ off, no free shipping banner. Wayfair's "AIR PURIFIERS CLEARANCE ENDS TONIGHT" send at least gave me a number to react to; this is pure lookbook with zero urgency.
- "Recommended Just for You" throws in a farmhouse platform bed and a curtain rod next to dining-room product — if this is personalization, it's generic-catalog personalization, not "Felix is furnishing a dining room" personalization.
- Zero social proof — no star ratings, no "X people bought this," even though CB2's Goop collab emails at least lean on collab credibility to substitute for reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Halloween Shop module from this specific send entirely — it undercuts the whole "new traditional dining room" premise. Save Halloween for its own dedicated email like yesterday's, don't bolt it onto a furniture-focused one.
- 2. Add one concrete offer line near the hero — even "Free shipping on dining tables this week" would turn this from browse-only into an actual reason to act today.
- 3. Make "Recommended Just for You" actually category-matched — if I clicked into dining/dinnerware content, show me sideboards and rugs for a dining room, not a platform bed.
- 4. Add a review count or star rating under the Toscana table — I trust a $1,000+ table purchase more with social proof attached.

# Technical Email Audit: Pottery Barn — "Get the look: The new traditional dining room"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud/EDM template (SFMC-style `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect links, `image.e.potterybarn.com` CDN) with legacy XHTML doctype; core rendering scaffolding is sound, but the message ships with widespread missing alt text, several plaintext-HTTP image sources, and a leaked server-side code fragment sitting inside a `<style>` block.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (SFMC encrypted redirect format, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5NTd9...`). 57 such tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (by design — these are click-redirect domains, not final destinations), so final-URL validity is unconfirmed.
- Open-tracking pixel `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over plain HTTP and embeds the recipient's full email address as a plaintext query parameter — flagged again under Compliance below.
- Secondary tracking pixel `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn/Amobee) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/bea14ffb-...?mi_mid=...` (additional open/click tracker) are both present — three separate tracking mechanisms in one send.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is correctly set, which mitigates referrer leakage of the embedded PII to third-party landing pages when links are clicked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Leaked server code in `<style>` block**: the CLIENT styling `<style>` tag contains literal PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS and appears to be a backend template artifact that leaked into client output rather than executing server-side — indicates a bug in the template pipeline that produced this send, not a rendering risk to the user (browsers will silently ignore the invalid declarations) but worth a bug report to the ESP/template team.
- **41 images missing `alt` text** (per QA), including all hero/product slice images (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg`, dining product shots like `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-l.jpg`) and every tracking pixel. If images are blocked (default in Outlook/many mobile clients), the message renders as a near-blank page with no accessible fallback text for screen readers.
- External Google Font (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat`) is loaded via `<link>` in `<head>` — most major email clients (Outlook, Gmail) strip `<link>` tags and won't fetch this, so the font will silently fall back; not breaking, but dead weight in the payload.
- Media queries target both 650px and 700px breakpoints with overlapping `img {max-width}` rules — the 700px query sets `max-width:650px` while the 650px query (which fires at a narrower width) sets `max-width:100%!important` — the `!important` in the narrower query correctly wins, so no visible conflict, but the ordering is fragile.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient identity is passed as a literal email string rather than an AMPscript/Handlebars merge token in at least two places: `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. This is expected behavior for this test mailbox (etell persona alias) but confirms personalization is resolved server-side before send (no unresolved `%%EmailAddress%%`-style tokens leaked into markup).
- No broken/unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) were found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in message headers (QA WARN). Note: may be stripped by the AgentMail relay rather than genuinely absent at origin — cannot confirm from headers alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: also not found — one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found — authentication status of the originating send is unknown from available data (again, possibly a relay artifact rather than a true absence).
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer text in the (truncated) HTML sample provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link) without the full source.
- PII exposure: recipient email address transmitted in cleartext via GET parameters on two different HTTP(S) endpoints (`coreemail.html?email=...`, `svopn.potterybarn.com?...sv_sveme=...`), one of which is unencrypted HTTP — this is a plaintext PII-in-URL/in-transit concern rather than a CAN-SPAM item.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify UTM parameter presence/alignment — all outbound links use the opaque SFMC `qs=` encrypted token format rather than standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query strings, and the 57 tracking redirects were not resolved by the QA probe. Landing-page alignment is unverifiable without decoding the `qs` payload or following redirects manually.
- Product deep-links that do use plain URLs (e.g. `https://www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/ab/images/dp/wcm/202608/0021/toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-l.jpg`) are image assets, not click-throughs, so not applicable to landing continuity.
- One footer/legal asset is served from a sibling brand domain, `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (plain HTTP) — cross-brand infrastructure reuse; confirm this is intentional shared asset infrastructure and not a stale/broken reference.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Report the leaked PHP `header()` fragment inside the `<style>` block to the ESP/template team — indicates a template rendering bug in the send pipeline.
2. Add `alt` attributes to all content images (41 flagged), particularly the 18 dining-room "slices" images and named product shots, for accessibility and image-blocked rendering.
3. Upgrade the two plaintext-HTTP image/pixel sources (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/.../Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/.../slices_02.jpg`) and the HTTP open-tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking in strict mail clients.
4. Confirm with the sending platform/ESP whether List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and Authentication-Results headers are actually being generated at origin — if the relay is dropping them, get raw headers pre-relay to close out the compliance warnings definitively.
5. Avoid passing the recipient's raw email address as a plaintext URL parameter on an HTTP (non-TLS) endpoint (`svopn.potterybarn.com`); move this tracking call to HTTPS at minimum.
