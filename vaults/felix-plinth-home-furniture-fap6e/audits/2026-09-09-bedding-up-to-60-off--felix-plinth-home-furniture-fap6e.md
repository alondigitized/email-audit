# BEDDING up to 60% OFF ➡️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Bedding does appear in the top-left tile this time — small win, subject actually matches something on the page for once — but then it's the same purple-and-white Wayfair category grid I've screenshotted four times this week: pergolas, privacy screens, "Must-Grab Deals" with recently-purchased counts, rugs, grills, nursery stuff, gazebos. This is the "under $29.99" layout and the "WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT" layout wearing a different subject line. I'm two houses deep into a renovation and I actually need bedding right now, so the top module isn't wrong for me — it's just buried under six more categories I don't care about in this exact moment (grills, nursery, colanders?).
- Crate & Barrel's Double Rewards email at least led with a real percentage and a real deadline in the hero and kept the rewards math visible. This is scattered "up to 60%," "up to 40%," "up to 35%" across a dozen tiles with no single clear CTA — my eye doesn't land anywhere, it just scans. The big red price tag at the bottom saying "SALES, SALES & YES, MORE SALES... Now that we got our attention" is honestly the most self-aware thing in this email, and not in a good way — it's basically Wayfair admitting they know they've fatigued me.
- Verdict: I'd click the bedding tile specifically because I need it, but I wouldn't forward this — there's nothing here worth sharing, it's just Wayfair's evergreen grid again.

## What's working

- Bedding clearance is the actual first tile, top-left, so the subject line isn't a total bait-and-switch this time (unlike yesterday's dining chairs email).
- "up to 60% OFF" badge is bold and legible on the bedding tile specifically.
- "Recently Purchased" counts (4041, 18389, etc.) on the Must-Grab Deals are a decent social-proof touch I don't usually see from this brand.
- Clean purple/white color-blocking is consistent with every other Wayfair send, so at least it's on-brand even if repetitive.

## What's weak

- Six unrelated categories (privacy screens, pergolas, nursery, grills, colanders, gazebos) crowd out the one thing the subject promised — I have to hunt for bedding again after the hero.
- No single clear CTA — it's a dozen small "category »" links, so there's no obvious "click here for your offer" moment.
- The bottom red tag saying "Now that we got our attention... Snag 'Em ASAP" reads like Wayfair mocking its own fatigue-inducing cadence, which is a weird note to end on.
- This is functionally the same layout as "under $29.99" and "WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT" from earlier this week — zero visual differentiation in my inbox scroll.
- Nursery essentials and outdoor grills have nothing to do with bedding or my renovation — feels like a generic blast, not anything segmented to what I've clicked before.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the grid to 3-4 categories max and let bedding own the top half of the email — if the subject says bedding, give me a bedding-only hero with a real deadline, not a link buried in tile one of twenty.
- 2. Kill the self-aware "Sales, sales & yes, more sales" banner — it undercuts trust by admitting the fatigue instead of fixing it.
- 3. Add a real percentage/dollar figure per category instead of "up to X%" everywhere — Crate & Barrel's Double Rewards send beat this exact rubric by having one crisp number in the hero.
- 4. Segment by purchase/browse history — if I've never looked at nursery furniture, don't give it a tile.
