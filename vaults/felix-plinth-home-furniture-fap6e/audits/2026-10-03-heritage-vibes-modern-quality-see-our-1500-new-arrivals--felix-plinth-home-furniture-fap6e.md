# Heritage vibes. Modern quality. See our 1500+ new arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh Crate & Barrel-family send I've counted in about two days, so my first reaction was "oh, you again." In a normal week I'd have mass-archived it. But I'm renovating two houses, and this is a proper catalog-style send with furniture, lighting, rugs, bedding and a kitchen section. It's useful for the project board. The screenshot is a 12,000px scroll, and at that size I can read the headlines and button labels but not the fine print.
- Compared with the Design Desk sends from 10/02 (the bathroom one and the rug guide, both 7/10), this one says nothing about a project. It's a shelf of "new stuff." Pottery Barn's "All tabletop now ships free!" at least put one offer in the headline. Here the "up to 25% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" is in a thin banner above the hero. The hero itself is "1500+ heritage-inspired finds," which is a vibe and not a deal.
- I'd click once, probably the sofa/sectional tile or the lighting block, and save it to my inspiration folder. I wouldn't forward it. The lamp-in-three-brands problem is real here, because this is the same fall-moody palette CB2 and Article have been showing all week.

## What's working

- The 25% off sofas and sectionals banner is a real, specific offer, and it's in my renovation category.
- The moody, dark-wood, deep-green palette is consistent from top to bottom. That makes it easy to mentally board a whole room.
- The tiles are broken up by category (kitchen, rugs, tabletop, bedding, lighting), each with its own "Shop New ___" button. I can jump straight to what I need.
- The coffee and espresso block says "up to $300 off," which is a concrete number. Pottery Barn's tabletop send didn't give me one.
- The Design Desk footer ("book a free appointment") is the one thing that fits how I actually buy.

## What's weak

- The sofa discount is buried in a small top banner, while the hero sells "heritage-inspired." The hierarchy is backwards.
- The email is extremely long. By the time I reach "we love these for you" I've seen about fifteen CTAs and none of them feels like the main one.
- The "the holidays happen here" tile has text over a busy photo that's hard to read. It also feels early, since it's October 3.
- There are no swatch or sample offers, no delivery timing for the big furniture, and no rewards or card math anywhere. For sofas that matters more than "vibes."
- Kids and baby (500+ arrivals from $29) and the app promo ($500) aren't relevant to me. They add scroll with no payoff.
- This is the eleventh C&B-family send in two days, and nothing in it acknowledges that I've already been mailed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer. Make "Up to 25% off sofas & sectionals" the hero headline, with "new heritage-inspired arrivals" as the supporting line.
- 2. Cut the email to five or six modules. Merge the two-up tiles, drop kids and the app promo from the main send, and segment those to people who shop them.
- 3. Add decision-making info for furniture: delivery window, free swatches, and the Design Desk appointment moved much higher.
- 4. Collapse the sends. Bundle a week of C&B, CB2 and Kids into one weekly digest, or at least suppress duplicates.
- 5. Move the holiday tile to later in October, or give it a clear deadline.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Heritage vibes. Modern quality. See our 1500+ new arrivals"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Outlook/MSO fallbacks and a 460px mobile breakpoint. Its main confirmed defect is missing alt text on about 40 images. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of how the email was captured. The HTML was also truncated, so body-level checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **75 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them because they are click-redirect domains. Destination resolution, redirect chain health and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Third-party tracking pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `&n=5` requests fire (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain). They sit alongside first-party pixels at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/bda4b7de-…`. The third-party pixels are a privacy and deliverability consideration. Some clients and privacy proxies will block them.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 40 images.** This is confirmed by QA. It includes:
  - the hero (`2026%5F0711%5FCB%5FFallNewArrivals%5FHero…` on `s7d5.scene7.com`)
  - about 25 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`
  - 7 dynamic product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`
  - `…HolidayAwareness%5FAnthem…` on `s7d5.scene7.com`

  With images blocked, content images render as blank space with no fallback text. QA can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from an empty `alt=""`. The tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`) should carry `alt=""` and are fine if they do.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dead CSS rule.** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width:600px} }` is followed by `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width:640px} }`. Both match at ≥768px and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** `<meta charset>` is declared twice, and `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values. This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Template remnant.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in the head, and the title contains an unescaped `&` (`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`).
- `lang="en"` is present and the MSO/VML namespaces are declared correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear. The `mi_u=dbe8da71…` parameter on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images and the `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` values on `dv.crateandbarrel.com` resolved to per-recipient IDs. The truncated HTML means the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. It shouldn't be treated as a sender defect without checking the raw message. If they are really absent, that would conflict with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This is a capture limitation, not evidence of failure. The From domain (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`) aligns with the image and tracking subdomains.
- The footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. The 75 tracking links were not resolved, and the truncated source doesn't show the final URLs or any UTM parameters. Landing-page alignment with the "1500+ new arrivals" claim is not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the hero and the product tiles. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Re-check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before drawing any compliance conclusion. Consider capturing headers outside the relay.
4. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm the final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
5. Remove the duplicate meta tags and the dead 768px `img` rule, and escape the `&` in the title.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and address can be checked.
