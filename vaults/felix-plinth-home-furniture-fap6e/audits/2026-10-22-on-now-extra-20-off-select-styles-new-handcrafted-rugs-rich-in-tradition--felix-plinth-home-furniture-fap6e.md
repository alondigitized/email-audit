# ON NOW! Extra 20% off select styles + New handcrafted rugs, rich in tradition
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Pottery Barn send today, and my snooze finger was already moving. The maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is the same one from this morning's "STARTS NOW 📣" email and the "Holiday NEW ARRIVALS" email. I've now seen that banner three times before lunch.
- Then the rugs hero got me. "Handcrafted Heritage" with hand-knotted Persian-style rugs under a Williamsburg collab is a real product story, and I have two houses with bare floors. The moody antique photography is Pottery Barn at its best.
- The problem is that the two halves of the email don't connect. The sale strip says "select styles," and nothing in the rug section tells me whether the rugs are in the 20%. I'd click through to the rugs, but I'd forward this to nobody, and I'd fold it into the Pinterest folder.

## What's working

- The "Handcrafted Heritage" hero is a strong, moody image. It stands out against the generic sale emails I've been getting.
- The rug story has a real angle, a Williamsburg archive collab with hand-knotted construction. It reads like something worth saving, not a coupon.
- The "Ground any space" row (Area, Hallway, Custom, Outdoor) is a useful way to browse. Hallway rugs matter for the reno.
- The "Free Design Services / Book a free appointment" module is the best thing in the email. I'm doing two houses, and I'd want that.
- The "Earn 10% back in rewards" card block is on-brand for how I buy. I put everything on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The sale strip is the identical creative to the two earlier sends today, which is cadence fatigue.
- It's unclear whether the rugs are part of the 20% off. The offer and the hero never meet.
- The "Recommended just for you" block is a random mix of a curtain rod, a swivel chair, a rug, and two dining tables. It doesn't feel personal.
- The Christmas Shop banner is premature for me. I'm choosing floors and lighting, not trees.
- The footer is a wall of tiny fine print. I can't read it without zooming, and the 10% rewards terms are buried in it.
- Several tap targets are small, including the outlined "Shop all rugs" button and the text links.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tie the offer to the hero. Say "Rugs included in 20% off" or name the exact discount on the rug section, so I know what I'm getting.
- 2. Don't send the same sale banner three times in one day. Rotate the creative, or consolidate the day's sends into one.
- 3. Move Free Design Services higher, right after the rugs. It's the best offer for renovators.
- 4. Make "Recommended for you" follow my browsing. Show rugs and dining tables I've looked at, not a mixed bag.
- 5. Add a swatch or sample CTA to the rugs section. Rug color and size are the real friction, and Article does this well.
- 6. Shorten the fine print and make the rewards math visible above the fold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Hand-knotted rugs, now part of 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Williamsburg-inspired rugs + 20% off ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Persian-style, hand-knotted, and in the sale. Plus free design help.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order rug swatches, then take 20% off select styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from image slices, and most of its images have no alt text. It sends the recipient's address in cleartext over HTTP and has several template defects. The unsubscribe headers and authentication results could not be checked, and the HTML source was truncated. Automated QA scored 50% pass with 0 errors and 4 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 54 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo link. I cannot confirm that any destination resolves.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an ad-network beacon with no alt text. It adds a third-party data flow.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,386 of 14,579 characters (71%) are URLs. This is mostly the long `qs=` tokens, and QA flags it as a deliverability risk.
- **Cleartext pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an open-tracking pixel served over HTTP. It may be blocked by clients, and it leaks the address (see section 4).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 46 QA warnings. The affected images are:
  - The hero banner `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`.
  - The 12 rug slices `F26_Collab_B12_ObeeteeWilliamsburgRugs/slices_01–12.jpg`.
  - Six product images, e.g. `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg` and `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`.
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - The 18-slice `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Some of these are tracking pixels where an empty `alt=""` would be fine. The content images and the linked slices are real gaps.
- **Blocked images:** the entire layout is slice-based, so with images blocked the email shows little usable content. Linked images with no alt text also leave link text empty for screen readers.
- **Mixed content:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` use HTTP and may be blocked.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with leaked PHP text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. It is invalid CSS and a template bug. It is at the end of the block, so rules before it should be unaffected.
- **Duplicate meta tags and no lang:** the source has two viewport variants commented out, and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** I found none in the visible source.
- **Address in query strings:** the recipient's address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears as plain text in two URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - The HTTP `svopn` pixel above.
- **Why it matters:** the `svopn` URL sends the address unencrypted. Both URLs also put PII in query strings, which end up in server and proxy logs.
- **Cross-brand assets:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on West Elm's domain. This is template reuse that creates a dependency on another brand's CDN.
- **Stale asset:** the 2022 path (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`) is reused in a 2026 send.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA did not find either header. It notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer body:** the truncated HTML does not show the unsubscribe link or postal address in the body. The footer is 18 image slices with no alt text. I could not verify whether those elements are live text or baked into images.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, because links are wrapped in opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirects and probing was skipped. UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unverifiable.
- **Direct product images:** product image URLs point at `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` directly. Those are images, not click destinations, so they say nothing about continuity.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content and linked images, and use `alt=""` on tracking pixels and spacers. Prioritize the hero, the rug slices and the footer slices.
2. Move the `svopn` pixel and the two remaining `http://edm.potterybarn.com` images to HTTPS. Stop putting the raw address in URLs, or hash it.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block and fill in `<title>`. Drop `maximum-scale=1` and add `lang="en"`.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC against the raw source headers rather than the relay copy.
5. Confirm that the unsubscribe link and postal address in the footer are live text or have alt text.
6. Resolve the redirect chains out of band to check UTM tagging and destination health.
7. Replace the West Elm spacer and the 2022 banner asset with current, brand-specific files.
