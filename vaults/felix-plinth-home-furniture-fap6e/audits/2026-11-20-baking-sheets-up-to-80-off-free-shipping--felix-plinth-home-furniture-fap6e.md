# BAKING SHEETS ✨ UP TO 80% OFF ✨ FREE SHIPPING ✨ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again. By my count this is the sixteenth send since 11/09, and it lands the same day as "Black Friday is here" and "Pergolas for less." The subject promised baking sheets. The first tile is a sheet pan of roasted asparagus, with no price, no "was/now," and no label saying it's on sale. I had to guess it was the baking sheet. After that, nobody mentions baking sheets again. I'm renovating two houses, not stocking a kitchen, so I scrolled for anything useful.
- Further down I found a nightstand, a bench, a wine-rack sideboard, rugs, curtains, and the same pergola grid I've seen three times this week. The "80% off" in the purple banner is attached to a Sealy mattress logo and a tiny "up to." The subject's baking-sheet hook has little to do with that banner. This is a catalog dump with a kitchenware headline on top.
- Would I click? Maybe the sideboard or the bench. Would I forward it? No. I'd file the bench in my Pinterest-adjacent folder and archive the rest.

## What's working

- The "picked just for you" block opens with real product photography. The nightstand and the bench are the kind of pieces I'd use in a renovation.
- The "Was / NOW" pricing on the flash-deals tiles is clear. The sideboard at $159.99 is easy to read.
- "Everything ships free*" is a strong, visible band. Furniture shipping is my biggest worry, so I'm glad it's up front, even with the asterisk.
- The category strips (area rugs up to 80%, bedding up to 70%) are easy to tap and big enough for a thumb.

## What's weak

- The subject says baking sheets, but the body is a general sale. Almost nothing here is bakeware. Compare Crate & Barrel's sofa and dining send, which did what its subject said.
- The hero banner is a Sealy mattress badge with "up to 80% off." That's confusing. The 80% claim and the mattress sponsor aren't clearly connected.
- The pergola and gazebo grid at the bottom is the same one I got in "Pergolas for less" this morning. I'm annoyed when the same product shows up repeatedly. That's the third time this week.
- The email is very long. The flash-deals sale tiles have tiny price text, and the "*Some exclusions apply" fine print is buried under free shipping.
- Wayfair's cadence is worn out. CB2 BLACK FRIDAY sends often, but each send at least had a single hero offer. Pottery Barn repeats one banner but keeps it clean. Here I get ten competing offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. If baking sheets are the lead, make the hero a baking-sheet set with a real price. If not, change the subject.
- 2. Cut the length by half. Keep one deal block and one category block. Drop the repeated pergola section.
- 3. Separate the Sealy sponsor from the sitewide "up to 80%" claim.
- 4. Put the free-shipping terms (the minimum, and what's excluded) in one line.
- 5. Show each product's price directly under the photo, not in tiny type.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sheet pans from $12 + 24-hr flash deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bakeware, benches and sideboards, all with free shipping today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `24 hours only: flash deals on storage and furniture.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch send with per-recipient tracking parameters and a table-based, media-query-driven layout. The automated checks found no hard failures. The warnings are missing alt text on linked images (including tracking pixels), 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, and compliance headers that could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 7 probed material links that returned a status got HTTP 429, which is bot rate-limiting from Wayfair, not a dead link. This applies to `/daily-sales`, the homepage, three product PDPs and the onelink app link.
- **4 links were not checked.** The 70s probe budget ran out before they were tested: `…L6315-K~id-BV83034023.html`, `…Skylight-Collection-Handle-Pull…`, `…L256-K~id-AB17061619.html` and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html?eid=635985`. 68 links were skipped by design, so coverage is partial.
- **Insecure impression pixels.** There are 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `…019&sz=2x1` images.
  - They are non-HTTPS, so clients or proxies that block mixed or insecure content may drop them.
  - They also have no alt text.
  - If blocked, ad or impression counts will be under-reported.
- **Deep-link fallback is HTTP.** The onelink URL (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`) has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app land on an `http://` URL and depend on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Inconsistent attribution labels on the same link.** The onelink URL has `c=Triggered` but also `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch campaign is labelled as triggered in AppsFlyer, which will skew campaign reporting.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images with no accessible name.** The QA output shows these links with `[image: …]` as their only text, so screen readers get no link purpose:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jpg`, which links to `/daily-sales`.
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-FlashDeals.jpg`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-EverythingShips…`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`
- **Star ratings are image-only.** `stars_10.png` appears 10 times and `stars_11.png` twice, all without alt text. The rating is not exposed to assistive tech or to image-blocked clients, and these images are also wrapped in the product links, as the 429 entries show.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` has no alt, which is a minor issue. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Not assessable.** The source is truncated, so `lang`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and the body markup could not be checked. The visible head has an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype and `@font-face` for "Sofia" with an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback, which is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data. The `_emr` and `_eml` parameters are rendered as UUIDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) is also not found. The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture artifact, so I can't confirm it is missing from the sent message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is the same relay limitation. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe and postal address not confirmed.** The QA output says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for it. The footer is outside the truncated source, so the unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Proprietary tracking, no UTMs.** Links carry `_emr`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `refid=MKTEML_143896`, `emlid=101` and `maiid=16776` instead of `utm_*` parameters. This is consistent across all links, so I'm noting it for analytics mapping rather than flagging it as an error.
- **Product links are well-formed.** PDP links add `mplid` and `sku` (for example `sku=MBZI1018` for the Nordic Ware set), and sku and `mplid` match on each link checked.
- **Landing alignment not verifiable.** The subject says "BAKING SHEETS … UP TO 80% OFF", but the hero and banner links go to the generic `/daily-sales`, not a baking-sheets page. Because of the 429s, the landing page content was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` and explicit 1x1 dimensions.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the linked banners, for example "Shop Black Friday daily sales". Give the star-rating images text such as `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`, and give the open pixel `alt=""`.
3. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, and align `c=Triggered` with the batch send.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from raw headers at the source, not through the relay.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links and the 429 URLs with a longer time budget, backoff and a browser-like user agent.
6. Check that the footer has a working unsubscribe link and a physical address.
