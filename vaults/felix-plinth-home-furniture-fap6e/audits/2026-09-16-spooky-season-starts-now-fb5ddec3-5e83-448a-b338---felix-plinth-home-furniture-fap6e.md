- Zero relevance to two renovations in progress: no furniture, no room styling, no swatches, nothing tied to what I'm actually shopping for.
- Kids' costumes and toddler decor dominate — wrong demographic entirely for a 36-year-old renovating two houses with no kids mentioned.
- The one adjacent-to-me item (Tripp Trapp chair, design service) is stranded at the very bottom after nine screens of Halloween noise.
