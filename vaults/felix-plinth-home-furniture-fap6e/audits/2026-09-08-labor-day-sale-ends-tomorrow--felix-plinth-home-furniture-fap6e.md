# LABOR DAY SALE ENDS TOMORROW
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2, "ends tomorrow" — I've seen this exact countdown-clock-plus-green-velvet-headboard hero before, just yesterday actually, when it said "48 HOURS LEFT." Now it's down to "01:07:10:29" and the same category grid is marching underneath it: Furniture, Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting, Decor, Entertaining. I've clicked through this grid mentally so many times this week I could sketch it from memory.
- The one thing that actually made me pause was the "GET 10% ~~20%~~ BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" strikethrough down in the Double Rewards banner — that's a new visual trick from them, and for half a second I thought it was a typo like yesterday's "10% 20%" mess. It's not, it's an actual before/after gimmick, but it's buried below six rows of product categories so I'm not sure how many people scroll that far to see it.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I actually needed a rug — we're two renovations deep and rugs are genuinely on my list. Would I forward this to a friend? No. This is the fifth near-identical CB2/Crate & Barrel send this week and there's nothing here worth sharing versus, say, Pottery Barn's actual entryway styling guide from today, which at least gave me a reason to open it beyond "sale ends soon."

## What's working

- The green velvet headboard + white bedding shot in the Furniture row is a good demographic match — clean, modern, not fussy, the kind of look I'd actually put in the primary bedroom reno.
- Rugs row product shot is genuinely well-lit and the pattern is legible at thumbnail size, which matters since I'm shopping rugs right now.
- The strikethrough "10% ~~20%~~" on Double Rewards is at least a fresh creative beat compared to yesterday's flat-out typo version.
- Clear black "SHOP THE SALE" bar breaking up the grid gives my eye a resting point instead of just category after category.

## What's weak

- This is the same countdown-timer, same hero copy structure ("ENDS TOMORROW" / "THE LABOR DAY SALE" / "UP TO 50% OFF") I've now seen from CB2 three sends running this week — nothing new to justify opening this over the last one.
- Six back-to-back category rows (Furniture, Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting, Decor, Entertaining) with zero differentiation in offer or messaging — it's a scroll-fest, not a curated pick.
- The "up to 50%" dagger symbol at the top has zero visible context on where the terms actually live until you hit the tiny fine print at the very bottom — feels like standard exclusion-language cover.
- Double Rewards banner is a completely separate offer (credit card signup) crammed into the same email as the furniture sale — confusing which CTA I'm even supposed to care about.
- Crate & Barrel's own Labor Day send hit me with an actual coastline sunset hero and felt seasonal; this CB2 send is just green plant fronds and glass — same mood board they've used all sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the identical hero structure — I've gotten "ENDS TOMORROW" + countdown + "UP TO 50% OFF" from CB2 three times in four days. Rotate in something like Pottery Barn's editorial angle (style guide, room inspo) to earn the open.
- 2. Cut the six-category grid down to 2-3 hero categories tied to what's actually moving (rugs, lighting) instead of a full product-catalog dump.
- 3. Separate the Double Rewards credit-card pitch into its own send — it's diluting the furniture sale message and reads like two emails stitched together.
- 4. Surface the exclusions/dagger terms right next to the "50% off" claim, not six screens down in gray fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs + lighting, up to 50% off — ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: your CB2 wishlist is on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The pieces you've been eyeing, now up to 50% off through midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns on rugs and lighting — plus double rewards.`
