# 🎄 Save BIG on holiday decor 🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and I could have told you the creative before I opened it. It's the same maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner I got on 11/18 and earlier today in "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SAVINGS for every room." The only new thing is a Christmas Shop hero with a tree and a pile of plaid-wrapped gifts, and the subject's tree emojis are a very Wayfair move. I've lost count of the sends since 11/16.
- The email itself is decent. It's a clean stack of categories with a discount tier on each: stockings up to 20%, bedding up to 70%, candles and lit decor up to 30%, tabletop up to 70%. The "Ships Free" tags help. But it's holiday decor, and I'm renovating two houses. I'm buying a dining table and curtain rods, not stockings. The "In-stock furniture, delivered in just 1-4 weeks" block is the only part that speaks to me, and it sits near the bottom.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the dining table or the curtain rod, but I'd go there from the site, not this email. I'd file it in my Pinterest folder as "holiday inspo, maybe" and not forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The "up to 70% off" banner is unmissable, and each category tile has its own discount, so I can see where the deals are.
- The "Ships Free" badges on stockings, bedding, tabletop and throws take away the shipping question up front.
- The Christmas hero photo is warm and on-brand. It feels like a catalog page, which is why I save these.
- The "In-stock furniture, delivered in just 1-4 weeks" block answers my biggest furniture worry, which is lead times.
- The rewards strip ("earn up to 10% back") ties into how I buy, since I use the highest-rewards card. Having a Pottery Barn card tier for the family-of-brands (Williams Sonoma, West Elm) is a real hook.

## What's weak

- The Early Black Friday banner and the Christmas Shop hero stack on top of each other and compete, so there are two heroes and two "shop" buttons before I've scrolled once.
- The subject is party-emoji bait with no number. Wayfair's "🎉 BLACK FRIDAY IS HERE 🎉" had the same problem, and "Save BIG" says nothing about how big.
- The email is 7,000+ pixels tall. The discounts are tiny, and the fine print at the bottom is a wall of text I couldn't read without zooming.
- "Up to 70%" is vague. The stockings are only "up to 20%," so the headline number doesn't apply to the hero product category.
- It's all stockings, wreaths and throws. There's nothing for the renovation projects I'm in the middle of, like paint-adjacent palettes, swatches or showroom content, and the "Recommended just for you" tiles are generic holiday items.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one hero with one offer and one button. Use the 70% banner as the hero and drop the second "Shop now" band. Move the furniture and in-stock block up into the top third.
- 2. Personalize "Recommended for you" using what I browse. I've been looking at dining tables and rods, so show those first instead of stockings.
- 3. Put a real deadline and a real number in the subject and preheader. The point of the campaign is urgency, and "early" should mean something.
- 4. Cut the discount tiers to three and make the type bigger so they read on a phone.
- 5. Space out the sends. This is at least the seventh since 11/16, and Crate & Barrel and CB2 are doing the same thing to me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off bedding & tabletop, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `In-stock furniture in 1-4 weeks + 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on stockings, bedding and tabletop. Plus 10% back in rewards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dining tables and rods ship in 1-4 weeks. Early Black Friday prices end Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout, and the visible source has structural problems: no `<title>`, no `lang`, and a stray PHP snippet inside a `<style>` block. Almost every image is missing alt text, and the recipient's email address is exposed in plaintext in two image/pixel URLs. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 57 links went through click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`) and were not probed. Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure image sources:** three images load over `http://`, which risks blocking or mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (open pixel)
- **Third-party and cross-brand dependencies:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party tracking pixel. `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is an asset hosted on a sister brand's domain.
- **Stale asset paths:** `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` and `/pb/2025/US/H25_Bottom_InStock_V2/` are reused creative from earlier campaigns. They are a maintenance risk if those paths are purged.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images have no `alt` attribute, including:
  - all `slices_01`–`slices_12.jpg` in `/1119_THU_AM2_EBF_XmasShopSale/`
  - `slices_01`–`slices_18.jpg` in `/Email-Footer/`
  - the product images (`faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, etc.)
  - the banners `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`

  Because the email is built from image slices, a client with images blocked shows no readable content. Tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`) should carry `alt=""` rather than omit it. The header logo is fine (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is an accessibility issue.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. This is leaked PHP and may cause CSS parse errors for the rules after it.
- **Possibly dead mobile rules:** the rules `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` have no matching elements in the visible HTML, but the source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat) is ignored by many clients, including Outlook and Gmail.
- **Good practice:** layout tables use `role="presentation"`, and the preheader is hidden with `mso-hide:all`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Email address in URLs:** the recipient address is resolved and sent as a query parameter:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `...svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  This is PII in plaintext URLs, which can end up in server logs and referrer chains.
- **Unresolved tokens:** none were found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not necessarily the sender.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** not verifiable, because the HTML source is truncated before the footer.
- **Sender:** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain. No problems found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** these can't be verified. The wrapped `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` links hide their destinations, and none were resolved. The only visible direct URLs are product images and `coreemail.html`, which carry no UTM or campaign parameters.
- **Landing page alignment:** not testable, because no destination URLs were resolved.
- **Campaign naming:** the asset path `1119_THU_AM2_EBF_XmasShopSale` matches today's send, but the subject ("holiday decor") and the preheader ("Early Black Friday deals") describe different offers. That is a content point, so it is not assessed further here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all content images, and set `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels. The alt text on the sliced hero and footer images should carry the offer copy and CTAs.
2. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Move the three `http://` images, including the `svopn` pixel, to `https://`.
6. Stop passing the plaintext email in query strings (`coreemail.html`, `sv_sveme`). Use a hashed or opaque identifier.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, the footer unsubscribe link and the physical address.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm the final URLs, UTMs and HTTP status.
