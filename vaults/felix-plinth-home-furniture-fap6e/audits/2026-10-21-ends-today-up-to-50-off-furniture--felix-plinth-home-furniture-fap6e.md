# ENDS TODAY: Up to 50% off furniture!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crate & Barrel send I've logged today. The Thanksgiving dining event went out twice this morning and now there's a Kids SALE sender. The "ends today" urgency got my attention, but then I saw it's nursery and kids furniture: bunk bed, glider, dresser. I'm renovating two houses, but I'm not furnishing a nursery. The hero is a bed with a striped quilt and the offer is up to 50% off furniture, which is clear. It's just clearly for someone else.
- The layout is long and tidy. Big product blocks with price, strikethrough and a Shop Now button read cleanly, and the 50% headline is easy to find. But it's the same C&B family hitting me again, and Pottery Barn Kids would run exactly this play. I'd skim it, save a dresser to my Pinterest folder, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. No one in my circle has a baby on the way right now.

## What's working

- "Ends today! Up to 50% off furniture" is a clear offer with real urgency, and it sits right under the hero.
- Each product block shows a sale price against a struck-through regular price, so I can do the math fast.
- Every product gets a primary "Shop Now" button plus a secondary category link. That's easy to tap.
- The Redondo 6-drawer dresser in natural oak fits the neutral wood palette I'm using in both houses.
- The Design Desk block with "Book a Free Appointment" speaks to me as someone doing two renovations, and it says "it's 100% free."

## What's weak

- I've now had three C&B-family sends today, and the same Kids and Design Desk modules are repeating. It's the same fatigue I get when one lamp shows up in three brands' emails in a week.
- The whole email is nursery and kids furniture. Nothing in it is for a renovator doing living and dining spaces, so it's off-target for me.
- The email is very long, with two repeated Design Desk blocks, a "we love these for you" grid and an app promo. The sale gets buried.
- It never says whether the 50% is the whole furniture line or only select items. "Up to" is vague, and so is shipping and delivery timing on furniture that supposedly ends today.
- The member and rewards angle is weak. The credit card and app promo is a "$500" teaser with no clear tie to this sale, so I can't do the rewards math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Consolidate C&B sends. After two dining emails today, a third kids email feels like spam. Segment so non-parents don't get it.
- 2. Put the deadline and the real discount range in the hero, with delivery or in-stock status for furniture.
- 3. Cut the page to three products and one Design Desk block, and move the Design Desk up near the top for renovators.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample link and a shipping-timing line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: bunk beds, gliders & dressers up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: kids' furniture up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends at midnight. Free in-home design help on any room, plus delivery dates on every piece.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Redondo dresser, Babyletto glider and bunk bed are all marked down today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based email with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed problems are missing alt text on 39 `<img>` elements and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML was truncated, so link-level checks were limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing was skipped.** 84 links went through tracking or click-redirect domains, so none were checked for HTTP status, redirect chain or final destination. Broken links can't be ruled out.
- **LiveRamp pixel repeated 5 times.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` are the same pixel with the same `s=` hash. Each one adds a third-party request and no alt text.
- **MI pixel repeated 6 times.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with the same identifier.
- **Other pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/0eabadb8-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are further tracking images.
- **Recipient identifiers in image URLs.** `mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid` and `s=` are per-recipient values. They are also sent to a third-party domain (`rlcdn.com`), which matters for privacy disclosure. Whether the footer discloses it can't be checked from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **39 images were flagged as missing alt text.** Judging by filename and host:
  - About 24 look like content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (UUID-named `.jpg`/`.png`). With images blocked, these render as blank space and screen readers read the filename or URL.
  - 2 are spacers: `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - 13 are tracking pixels. These should carry `alt=""`.
  - I can't tell whether the attribute is absent or empty from the QA output, so the empty-alt cases may be fine.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text scaling is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `*`, `html` and `body` conflicts with the `100%` value declared elsewhere in the same stylesheet.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Duplicate and redundant meta tags.**
  - `format-detection` is declared 3 times: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, and `address=no`.
  - `Content-Type` and `<meta charset>` are both present.
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Mobile image widths.** `.showmobile` forces `width:414px` and `.mobile` forces `460px`, both above the 460px breakpoint's narrower viewports. Images could overflow on screens narrower than 414px. This is inferred from the CSS, not a rendered test.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML has no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. Truncation means the body and footer weren't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA flagged this as a warning, noting the relay may not capture it. It is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found.** The same caveat applies to RFC 8058 one-click support. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed.
- **Body-level items not checked.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were outside the truncated source.
- **Sender alignment looks consistent.** The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 84 tracked links were skipped, so UTM parameters, redirect destinations and landing-page status are unverified. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 24 content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and read SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original headers. The relay-captured data didn't include them.
4. Resolve a sample of the 84 tracking links to confirm destinations, UTM presence and HTTP status. Unwrapping the redirects would let the probe run.
5. Remove the duplicate `rlcdn.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixel fires and the redundant `format-detection` metas.
6. Escape `&` in `<title>` and drop the leftover template comment.
7. Re-run this audit with the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, unsubscribe link and address.
