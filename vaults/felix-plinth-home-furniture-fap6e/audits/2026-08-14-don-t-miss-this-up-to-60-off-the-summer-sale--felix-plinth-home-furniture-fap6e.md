# Don’t miss this: Up to 60% off the Summer Sale!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the same email I already logged this morning as "Up to 60% off The Summer Sale," and rereading it doesn't change my score much — it's still Crate & Barrel proper, still the beach photo hero, still the clearest single offer I've gotten from this brand family all week. But scrolling further down this time, I'm noticing how much is crammed into one send: a Levain bakery collab, a Howell x Harrier x AD sweepstakes, a rug launch, a "we love these for you" product grid, a Crate Card pitch, an app download push, and a Design Desk plug — all in one email. That's not a hero email, that's six emails wearing a trenchcoat.
- The "we love these for you" grid is actually decent — dining tables, a bed, a console, a nightstand — real furniture at real price points ($1,796, $2,199, $749) which is exactly my renovation-brain zone right now. But it's buried under three promotional modules before you even get there, and I've already seen near-identical console/nightstand pieces from Crate & Kids and CB2 this same week. My brain has officially entered C&B-family blur mode.
- Would I click? Maybe scroll to the furniture grid on my phone, but I'm not fighting through the sweepstakes and bakery collab to get there. Not forwarding this — nothing here is a "hey check this out" moment, it's just noise with an offer buried in it.

## What's working

- Top-of-email offer is real and specific: "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" with a clean beach hero — best single offer line I've gotten from this brand this week.
- Category nav grid (bed & bath, outdoor, kitchen, rugs, decor & lighting, furniture clearance, tabletop clearance) makes it easy to jump straight to what I care about instead of scrolling.
- The "we love these for you" product grid has real furniture with real prices — this is the part that actually matches my renovation shopping mode.
- Crate Card / Reward Dollars callout is relevant since I buy on the highest-rewards card anyway, so I clocked it even mid-scroll.

## What's weak

- Way too many competing CTAs stacked in one email — sale, bakery collab, AD sweepstakes, rug launch, product grid, credit card, app download, Design Desk. Pick one story.
- The Howell x Harrier x AD sweepstakes section reads like a completely different email that got spliced in; zero connection to "60% off sale" at the top.
- I've now seen a lounge chair / console / nightstand aesthetic almost identical to this in Crate & Kids' Finn Collection email and CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" email — same wood tones, same styling, all landing in the same week.
- No urgency or end date on the sale itself — "Summer Sale" with an asterisk but no actual deadline visible in the hero, so nothing pushes me to act today vs. next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two emails: one pure sale email (hero + category grid + product picks), one separate "collabs & content" email for the bakery partnership and AD sweepstakes. Trying to do both kills the urgency of the sale.
- 2. Move the furniture product grid up, right under the category nav — that's the highest-intent content and it's currently six scrolls deep.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero ("ends Sunday 8/17") so the 60% off actually feels time-bound instead of evergreen.
- 4. Since I'm clearly getting hit by the whole Crate & Barrel family (Crate & Kids, CB2) multiple times a week, dedupe the product styling across those sends or at least vary the assortment shown.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Summer Sale picks: dining tables, beds, storage`
