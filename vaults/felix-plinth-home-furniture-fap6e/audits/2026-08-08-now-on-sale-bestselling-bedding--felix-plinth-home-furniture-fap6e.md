# Now on sale: Bestselling bedding
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "The Warehouse Sale — Up to 60% off" — that headline number is the first thing that's actually stopped my scroll all week, and I've been scrolling past a lot of Pottery Barn subject lines lately. 60% is real money on a duvet, and bedding is legitimately on my list right now since I'm trying to make the primary and the second house not look like they were furnished by two different people. The three hero shots — Cloud Quilt, Belgian Flax Linen Duvet, Pick-Stitch Quilt — are clean, on-brand, and the kind of neutral layering I'd actually screenshot into my inbox folder next to the Crate & Barrel stuff.
- Where it loses me is the back half. By the time I hit "Recommended Just for You" it's basically a site-map dump — rugs, curtain rods, a dining table, towels — none of which I asked for, and then it nosedives into "The Halloween Shop" with a spooky porch photo. It's August 8th. I'm mid-reno on two houses and you're showing me carved pumpkins. That's the kind of seasonal whiplash Wayfair's pergola email at least avoided by staying in its lane.
- I'd click "Shop All Bedding" — the offer's good enough — but I'm not forwarding this to anyone, and this is easily the eighth-plus Pottery Barn email in my inbox this week. I'm marking it read and moving on.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off" in the top banner is a real, specific number — more concrete than the "Design Crew sweepstakes" or "coffee table styling" sends earlier this week.
- The three product blocks (Cloud Quilt, Belgian Flax Linen Duvet, Pick-Stitch Quilt) are shot consistently, same neutral palette, same styled bedroom — feels cohesive, not like three different catalogs mashed together.
- Bedding is genuinely relevant to me right now with two houses to outfit, so this lands better than the sweepstakes email or the cabinet-organization one from earlier this week.
- Clear top-of-email CTA ("Shop Now") right under the fold, no scavenger hunt required.

## What's weak

- The Halloween Shop module at the very bottom is a jarring seasonal mismatch on an August bedding-sale email — pumpkins and "enter if you dare" copy doesn't belong next to quilts.
- "Recommended Just for You" isn't personalized in any way I can tell — rugs, curtain rods, a dining table, towels is just a generic site cross-section, not anything tied to bedding or my actual browsing.
- This is at minimum my eighth Pottery Barn email this week (I've genuinely lost the exact count) and the fatigue is real — even a good offer stops landing at this cadence.
- The fine print block at the bottom (Affirm, rewards, sweepstakes legal) is a wall of gray text that makes the whole email feel heavier and more "corporate mailer" than the crisp hero up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Recommended Just for You" grid down to bedding-adjacent items only (pillows, throws, bed frames) — right now it undercuts the whole "bestselling bedding" premise by pivoting to rugs and dining tables.
- 2. Pull the Halloween Shop module entirely, or at minimum move it to its own send — it actively contradicts the "Now on Sale" bedding focus and reads like two campaigns stapled together.
- 3. Add a plain-language rewards line near the hero ("Members earn 10% back on this sale") instead of burying it near the footer — I'm the guy who buys on the highest-rewards card, put that math where I can see it fast.
- 4. Throttle send frequency — even strong creative like this drowns when it's the eighth email in a week from the same sender.
