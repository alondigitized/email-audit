# Have you shopped the Labor Day Sale?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- No hero image, no product shots, no lifestyle photo — just a plain-text-style letter from "Nicole" asking if I've shopped the sale yet. That's a wild choice eleven days into this streak. This is the tenth CB2 email I've logged this stretch, and it's the first one that reads like an actual person nudging me instead of a banner shouting at me — I'll give it points for tone, but zero points for effort on the visual side.
- The offer itself — up to 50% off, plus double rewards on the CB2 card — is the exact same stack I already saw in "DOUBLE REWARDS§ ON NOW" two days ago and the Labor Day banner yesterday. Nothing new, no product I can actually picture, just links buried in paragraph text for rugs, dinnerware, sheets, beds. Compare that to Pottery Barn's "Cozy Throws Ship Free," which at least put one clear thing in front of me. This one I'd skim in three seconds and archive — not annoyed, just unmoved. Not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- The subject is a plain, honest question — no ALL CAPS SUITE UPGRADES screaming, a nice break from CB2's usual register.
- "Nicole from the CB2 Team" signing off gives it a human voice instead of corporate blast copy.
- The free design-appointment line is genuinely useful for someone actually renovating two houses like me — that's a real service hook, not just a discount.
- Footer terms (end date, exclusions) are visible without digging, which I appreciate after CB2's past emails buried fine print.

## What's weak

- Zero images. No sofa, no rug, no lamp — for a furniture brand mid-renovation, that's baffling; I need to see the thing to want the thing.
- It's the same 50%-off-plus-double-rewards combo I've now read three times this week in three different wrappers.
- The body tries to cover rugs, dinnerware, sheets, beds, and design appointments all in one paragraph — nothing has visual priority, so nothing sticks.
- "See everything that's on sale right now" is a lazy catch-all CTA — no product, no category, just a generic link.
- Nine-plus emails in nine-plus days and this is the tenth subject variant on the identical Labor Day promo — the fatigue is real at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hero product back in — a rug or a sofa shot with the 50% badge on it. The plain-text-letter format might work for a single "did you forget" nudge, but not as one more entry in a ten-email cadence that's otherwise been all banners.
- 2. Cut the paragraph down to one category CTA, not five (rugs/dinnerware/sheets/beds/appointments all competing).
- 3. Actually segment — if I've already opened five of these this week, don't send a sixth "have you shopped yet" version of the same sale.
- 4. Give the CTA a verb and a noun: "Shop rugs up to 50% off," not "See everything that's on sale right now."

- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off rugs, beds & more — ends soon`
