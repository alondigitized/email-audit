# So many best sellers on sale for Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. This is the fifth or sixth CB2 Black Friday send I've gotten this week, and the hero is the same one from "NOT SOLD OUT YET." It's the oxblood panel with "Black Friday SALE," "UP TO 60% OFF," and a velvet green channel-tufted chair. The only new things are a thin "Extended Store Hours, Friday 11/27, 9AM–9PM" strip and a "Best Sellers" label. The subject promises best sellers, but the body is mostly a category grid.
- The layout is clean. The 60% number is huge and I can't miss it, and the accent chair module and the free shipping banner are easy to read. But I'm renovating two houses and keep a Pinterest-adjacent folder, so I want to see actual products with names and prices. This gave me moods and category tiles, with no SKU and no "was/now" price anywhere. I'd skim it, maybe tap Lighting, and archive it. I wouldn't forward it. I'm already getting the same lamp-adjacent content from Crate & Barrel, so the whole family blurs together.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" hero is impossible to miss, and the dark oxblood panel against white makes the offer land immediately.
- The category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath) matches how I shop room by room. The photography is moody and good enough to save to a board.
- The Accent Chairs module with a single burnt-orange chair and a SHOP NOW button is the one place with a clear, product-level focus.
- The free shipping banner for orders of $99 or more is clear. For furniture I'd still need to know what delivery costs on big pieces.
- The 10% back in Rewards Dollars on the CB2/Crate & Barrel card is useful for me, since I buy on the highest-rewards card. It sits near the bottom, though.

## What's weak

- The hero is a recycled creative. It's the same oxblood panel as the 11/26 "NOT SOLD OUT YET" send and close to the 11/25 "100+ more reasons" one, so this reads as a fresh subject line on old art.
- The subject says "best sellers," but the "Best Sellers" label in the hero only sits above a button. No best-selling product is named or priced.
- There are no prices and no "was/now" math. Pottery Barn's "up to 70% off" sends at least name specific deals, and Wayfair's "picked just for you" tiles show product prices.
- There's no swatch, sample, or delivery-date info, and nothing about color palette or showroom. That's what I'd need to decide on a sofa or a rug.
- The "SAVE 15% OFF in the New Year" banner undercuts the urgency of today's sale. It makes me wonder whether waiting is smarter.
- The fine print is a dense block at the bottom, with exclusions I can't read on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic category grid with 4–6 named best sellers, each with its sale price and its original price. That's what the subject line promised.
- 2. Put the Rewards 10% back and the free shipping threshold in a strip right under the hero. Rewards math is a purchase trigger for me.
- 3. Don't re-send the same hero. Rotate the creative, or lead with the Extended Store Hours as a "see it in person" showroom angle.
- 4. Drop or move the "15% off in the New Year" banner, since it competes with today's offer.
- 5. Add a line on furniture delivery times and a swatch/sample link on the accent chair.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The velvet chair is 60% off. Best sellers inside.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Named deals on chairs, lighting & rugs. Stores open 9AM–9PM tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping over $99 plus 10% back in Rewards Dollars.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML source has a valid doctype, `lang="en"` and a `<title>`, but the automated QA run shows widespread missing alt text (27 images) and 11 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks are limited to what the QA data covers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** 65 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Mixed content.** 11 pixels load over `http://`, which clients may block or flag:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The ten `cookieredir/.../pubN.php` requests look like ad-network cookie syncing (Dotomi). They add 10 extra third-party requests and cost load time. If the images are blocked, tracking data is lost.
- **Other tracking pixels.** Two more first-party pixels have no alt text: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/6a73165f-...?mi_cid=...`. The `mi_u` parameter looks like a hashed user identifier in a URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** 27 images lack alt attributes.
  - 14 are content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. Twelve are GIFs or JPGs (e.g. `978d7bb3-...jpg`, `e8ab22dd-...gif`) and two are further JPGs (`e68a523c-...jpg`, `20185973-...jpg`). With images blocked, these render as blank space for screen readers and image-blocking clients.
  - 13 are tracking pixels (`UCMController`, `pub1`–`pub10`, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`). These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility (WCAG 1.4.4) problem.
- **Text resizing disabled.** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none;...}` also prevents user text scaling.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled placeholder with a typo.
- **Cross-brand font hosting.** `@font-face` rules pull the `HNW05` fonts from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, a different brand domain. Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway. Because the family is defined in `<style>` and the `a{}` rule falls back to Helvetica, the effect is minor.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source or the QA data. The truncated HTML shows no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%` or `[[ ]]` tokens. The tracking URLs contain per-recipient parameters (`mi_u=`, `mi_cid=`, `mi_mid=`), and these were resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header** found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header** (RFC 8058). One-click unsubscribe is not detected. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, subject to the same relay caveat.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion of the HTML, so they could not be checked.
- **Sending domain.** The From address is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with aligned authentication. Alignment itself is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable.** All 65 click links go through redirect domains and were not probed, so UTM presence, landing URLs and 404s are unknown.
- **Attribution looks like it relies on redirect and pixel IDs rather than visible UTMs.** The `mi_cid`, `mi_mid` and `dtm_cid` parameters suggest this, but the visible evidence is limited to those pixel URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 14 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com`. Add `alt=""` to the 13 tracking pixels.
2. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS (`https://login.dotomi.com`, `https://ads.dotomi.com`), or remove the 10 `cookieredir/pubN.php` sync pixels. Do the same for the `UCMController` pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop the global `text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message headers, not the relay copy. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com`.
5. Resolve the 65 redirect links in a follow-up check to verify landing pages and UTM parameters.
6. Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` template comment and self-close the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tag.
