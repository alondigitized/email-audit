## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard, with a table-based XHTML layout and `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules. The main defects are about 20 HTTP-only impression pixels and widespread missing `alt` text on linked images. The link probe was inconclusive because of 429 rate limiting, so no links are confirmed broken. The compliance headers could not be assessed from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 63 skipped. Four more were unprobed after the 70s budget ran out, including `.../wayfair-product-L6114-K~id-BK81042267.html`, `.../FORIOUS-Pull-Down-Single-Handle-Kitchen-Faucet...` and `.../daily-sales/area-rug-sale~e404488.html`. They are neither pass nor fail.
- **The 429s are not evidence of broken links.** Nine probed links returned 429, including `/daily-sales`, `/`, three product URLs and both `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links. Wayfair is rate-limiting the QA prober, so the status of these links is unknown.
- **The hero link uses plain HTTP.** `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=...` (the `1105_HolidayFlashSale_SkinnyHero-General` image) adds a redirect hop and exposes the tracking parameters in transit. The app link's fallback is also HTTP: `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`.
- **There are 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels.** They are `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, with `sz=2x1&m=false`. Clients that block mixed or insecure content, or that proxy images, may not load them, so ad-impression counts could be undercounted. `QA: "Image uses http://"` flags all 20.
- **The open pixel is HTTPS.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (`eventType=EmailOpen`) does not have this problem.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images have no accessible name.**
  - The hero `1105_HolidayFlashSale_SkinnyHero-General.png` and the gift-card banner `WFUS_1105_HolidayFlashSale-Skinny-GiftCard.png` have no `alt`. The hero is the only content of its `<a>`, so screen readers announce a bare URL and the link text falls back to `[image: ...]`.
  - The star-rating images (`.../review_stars/stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png`) appear 12 times without `alt`. Each is the only content of a link to a product page (for example `Propane-Standing-Patio-Heater-L402-K~id-AI78025115.html`), so those links have no meaningful name.
  - The `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...` images also lack `alt`.
- **Tracking pixels have no `alt`.** This covers `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels. Use `alt=""` on them so assistive tech skips them.
- **Responsive CSS is present.** The media queries, the `u ~ div` Gmail fix and the Outlook `body[data-outlook-cycle]` fix are all in the source. The HTML was truncated, so the remaining body markup (table roles, `lang`, preheader) is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the probed URLs. The `_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid` values are all resolved. The `_eml` value is per-link for most links but shared (`2eaf8beb-...`) across the product links, which is consistent with per-module tracking.

## 5. Compliance
- **Header evidence is missing, not failing.** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were all absent from the captured data. The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe support are **unverified**. Do not treat them as failures.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical address are unconfirmed.** The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't listed in the findings. The source supplied is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or postal address.
- **The sender address is consistent.** The From is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, and I found nothing contradicting it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Campaign labeling is contradictory.** The app links carry `c=Triggered`, while every link carries `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch promotional send tagged as a triggered campaign could skew attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **No standard UTM parameters are used.** Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters: `refid=MKTEML_143021`, `csnid`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16689`, `wfcs=cs7`. This is consistent across links, but a UTM-based analytics setup would miss it.
- **Landing alignment can't be fully confirmed.** The subject is about patio heaters. The product links go to heater PDPs (`sku=BOWI1005` is a standing heater), but the hero and "Shop Sale" go to the generic `/daily-sales` and not a heater category. The landing page content wasn't probed (429).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all impression pixels over HTTPS by changing `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...` (×20) to `https://`. Change the hero link and `af_web_dp` to `https://www.wayfair.com/...`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero and gift-card banners, for example `alt="Holiday Flash Sale – shop daily deals"`. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Give the star-rating images a text alternative such as `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`, or move the product link onto the product name text so the link has a name.
4. Align `c=Triggered` with the `source=batch` send type so app-install attribution is correct.
5. Re-run the link checks with a rate-limit-friendly strategy (a slower cadence or a retry on 429). Include the four unprobed links, and capture the raw headers directly from the MX or SMTP path to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
