## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows a legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with MSO conditionals. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 errors, 3 warnings). The confirmed problems are missing image alt text, ten insecure `http://` third-party pixels, and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated. The source supplied was truncated, so anything past the `<style>` block is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **60 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure third-party pixels (confirmed).** 11 images load over `http://` and may be blocked or rewritten by mail clients and proxies:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` through `pub10.php`
- **Cookie-sync pixels.** The 10 `cookieredir/pub1–10.php` calls are Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-syncing beacons. They add 11 extra third-party requests per open and are unlikely to fire in clients that proxy images (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection). They also raise a privacy and consent concern.
- **Open-tracking pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/cf5b66c8-f82f-4421-b608-66f04cf4e871?mi_cid=…` are HTTPS and functional. Both lack alt text, and the `mi_u` value is a long hashed identifier in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 9 content images (confirmed)**, all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `558c00b9-…`, `4738d6a9-…`, `6d8af964-…`, `232f654f-…`, `454d1b65-…`, `4d5d7a32-…`, `604d471b-…`, `93958f7d-…`, `e68a523c-…`
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these render as empty or filename output.
- **Tracking and beacon images also have no `alt`.** That covers `UCMController`, `pub1–10.php`, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel. They should carry `alt=""` with 1×1 sizing and `display:none` or `aria-hidden`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` also stops iOS text scaling.
- **Template residue shipped in source.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in, with an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block beside it.
- **Font URLs.** Several `@font-face` sources use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Some fonts are pulled from `crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`). Web fonts have little client support and will fall back to Helvetica or sans-serif.
- The `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules and the `mso-*` fixes are present. `lang="en"` is set.
