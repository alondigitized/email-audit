## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with a dark-mode opt-out and mobile breakpoints at 460px. The automated QA flagged missing alt text on 35 images, and the header checks (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) came back empty. The header gaps are probably capture artifacts of the AgentMail relay. The HTML was truncated, so the body links, footer and merge tokens were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** 78 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations, redirect chains or 404s were checked.
- **Duplicate pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` appears 6 times with identical src. `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=...&n=1` through `n=5` appear 5 times. This adds requests without adding information.
- **Open pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/0d62136f-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are tracking images with no alt text.
- **Third-party pixel.** `sr.rlcdn.com` is a LiveRamp domain. It receives a subscriber-derived hash in `s=dbe8da71...` through five separate requests. This is a privacy and deliverability consideration, since third-party tracking domains can contribute to spam-filter scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 35 images.**
  - 22 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (for example `ec997ddd-...jpg`, `4f4a73dd-...jpg`, `0782bf25-...gif`).
  - Many of these are likely product or promo images. With images blocked, the "Double Rewards" and "ship quick" messaging may not render for those images.
  - The tracking pixels and `.gif` spacers should use `alt=""` explicitly. A missing attribute is read differently from an empty one by screen readers.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="...maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand in an XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed and duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. It is harmless, but it is a typo and adds weight.
- **Conflicting global image rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap. The cascade makes 640px images win between 640 and 767px, and the cap is 600px at 768px and wider. This is likely unintended.
- **Heavy global resets.** `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` can override inline styles in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{`, `%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` were visible in the truncated source. The only per-subscriber values are the `mi_u=` and `s=` hashes in pixel URLs, which look correctly populated. The footer and body were truncated, so this was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require this for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not in the truncated source, so they cannot be confirmed.

Do not treat the header findings as sender failures until the raw message headers are inspected.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The 78 click-tracked links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified.
- The tracking identifiers are `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` (Movable Ink / Cordial-style), not visible UTMs. Any UTMs are applied after the redirect.
- The subject promises "ship quick" and "Double Rewards". Whether the landing pages carry that offer cannot be checked from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML and raw headers, so the footer, unsubscribe, merge tokens and link inventory can be checked.
2. Follow the redirect chains on a sample of the 78 tracking links, using a HEAD or GET with redirect following. Confirm the final URL, status code, UTMs and offer messaging.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 22 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Set `alt=""` on the pixels and spacers.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Deduplicate the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `448796.gif` pixels, unless each is intentionally tied to a distinct module.
6. Fix the markup: escape `&` as `&amp;` in the title, drop the duplicate `format-detection` and `charset` metas, remove the placeholder comment, and reconcile the overlapping `img` max-width media queries.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message source rather than the relay copy.
8. Fix the QA scoring. A "57% pass rate, 0 issues, 3 warnings" summary is inconsistent with about 40 warnings in the list. Most of those are the repeated image warnings, so the scorer may be under-counting them.
