# New deals (with EXTRA 20% off) + The right bath vanity for your space (& routine)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Nine. I counted. Nine Pottery Barn emails in roughly seventy-two hours, and this one lands right after the outdoor-space email I actually clicked through yesterday. At this point my brain has developed an involuntary triage reflex: see "Pottery Barn," scan for the category, decide in under two seconds. The category here is bathroom vanities, which — I'll be honest — is live money for me right now. The primary bath in the second home needs a double vanity and I haven't pulled the trigger yet. So this hit at the right moment for the right reason.
- That said, the subject line is doing too much work. There's a plus sign in there connecting two different thoughts — new deals *and* bath vanity — and the parenthetical "& routine" at the end reads like it was written for a woman building a skincare shelf, not a guy trying to fit two undermount sinks into a 72-inch run. If I'm being honest, I almost didn't open it because the subject read like an email written for my wife's inbox, not mine.
- Inside, the three-way split between Double, Single, and Powder Room vanities is genuinely useful — that's actually how I'm thinking about this project (primary bath needs a double, powder room needs something small). The Memorial Day 20% off banner at the top holds up the offer clearly. I'd click to the Double Vanity section. I would not forward this; Crate & Barrel's concurrent Double Rewards offer might actually be a better deal if you're stacking points.

## What's working

- **Vanity segmentation by type** (Double / Single / Powder Room) maps directly to how I think about a renovation — I'm not shopping "vanities," I'm shopping by room. That structure respects my time.
- **The hero image is clean and aspirational** — warm wood tones, brass fixtures, looks like something from a Remodelista board. No clutter. Renders fast.
- **Extra 20% off is front and center** — I don't have to hunt for the offer; it's in the banner before I even hit the hero. That's the right call.
- **Memorial Day framing is seasonally coherent** — it's May, there's a real deadline implied, and the offer tracks with the broader campaign I've been watching across their sends this week.
- **"Recommended Just for You" section** at the bottom gestures toward personalization, which I appreciate even if I know it's algorithmic.

## What's weak

- **"& routine" in the subject line is female-coded** — that phrasing is spa-talk, not renovation-talk. Lost me for a half-second before I remembered I actually need a vanity.
- **The subject line is exhaustingly long** — two distinct value props stapled together with a plus sign, wrapped in parentheticals. On mobile the second half almost certainly gets cut.
- **Email nine in seventy-two hours is a problem** — the previous send ("outdoor space") scored high because it hit my exact project phase. This one is also relevant, but the cumulative cadence is eroding goodwill faster than any individual email can earn it back.
- **No explicit deadline date** — "Memorial Day Sale" is time-bounded in concept, but I don't see "ends Monday, May 27" anywhere in the render. Crate & Barrel's ongoing Memorial Day email at least signals a specific end-of-event framing more clearly.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split the subject line — pick one job.** Bath vanity is the winner here; drop "New deals" as the lead. It's vague and it buries the actual hook.
- 2. **Change "& routine" to "& renovation"** — one word swap, completely reframes the audience. Speaks to people like me, not just people prepping a beauty shelf.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline date** — "ends Monday" in the hero copy converts fence-sitters. Article does this better in their welcome cadence; Pottery Barn keeps leaving it implicit.
- 4. **Give the Double Vanity category its own CTA** earlier in the email — it's the highest-consideration, highest-ticket item and it's buried below two other categories before I can click into it.
- 5. **Surface a room-size callout** — something like "fits 60"–72" openings" next to the Double Vanity image would be the one product detail I actually need to self-qualify before clicking.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off bath vanities — Double, Single & Powder Room`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day: 20% off the vanity your renovation's been waiting for`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three layouts. One deadline. Find your fit before Memorial Day ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Double, single, or powder room — your 20% off window closes Monday.`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn: "New deals (EXTRA 20% off) + Bath Vanity"

Two hard-broken links (403s) will produce dead click destinations for credit card and spacer assets. Pervasive missing alt text across ~45 images creates a fully non-functional experience when images are blocked, which is the default in many email clients.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

**Missing alt text (45 images)** — this is the dominant accessibility failure. Key affected assets:

- All 15 vanity-size slice images (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_15.jpg`, path `S26_Bath_B5_VanitiesBySize/`)
- All 18 footer slice images (`Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg`)
- Hero banner: `S26_Top_MemorialDayNoUrgency.jpg`
- Product images: farmhouse-platform-bed, sausalito-dresser, toscana-dining-table, outdoor chaise, sheet set, curtain rod bracket
- Credit card banner: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`

With images off (Gmail Promotions, corporate firewalls), the email renders as a blank table with no navigable content.

**HTTP image sources (3 confirmed):**

```
http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif          ← 403 + HTTP
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg
http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg
```

Mixed content will be blocked in most modern clients/webmail.

**CSS anomaly in `<style>` block:**

```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...");
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```

PHP `header()` calls embedded inside a `<style>` tag — these are inert in email but indicate a template/copy-paste error from a server-side asset. No rendering impact but signals a broken build process.

**`<title>` is empty** — minor, but mail clients that display tab/window titles will show nothing.

**5. Compliance**

| Check | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | WARN — not captured | May be stripped by AgentMail relay; unconfirmed presence |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | WARN — not captured | One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) unverifiable |

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**

The 403 on the credit card CTA (`clickcp.potterybarn.com`) means that click path is entirely broken — users cannot reach the intended landing page. All other destination URLs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect chains which were not probed; UTM parameter presence on final landing pages is unconfirmed.

The West Elm spacer domain (`edm.westelm.com`) is a cross-brand asset leak — this asset should be served from `edm.potterybarn.com` or inlined.

**7. Recommendations**

1. **[P0] Fix the credit card CTA link** — `clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407...` returns 403. Regenerate or replace the destination URL before any resend.
2. **[P0] Replace the West Elm spacer** — `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a 403 cross-brand HTTP asset. Use `https://edm.potterybarn.com/...` equivalent or a 1×1 transparent GIF inlined as a data URI.
3. **[P1] Add alt text to all 45 images** — prioritize the hero banner, vanity-size section (slices 01–15), and all product images. These carry the email's navigational and commercial content.
4. **[P1] Upgrade all HTTP image sources to HTTPS** — `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` (2022 CC banner), and the open-pixel at `svopn.potterybarn.com`.
5. **[P1] Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers** — confirm these are present upstream of the AgentMail relay. Gmail/Yahoo bulk compliance requires both.
6. **[P2] Remove PHP `header()` calls from `<style>` block** — template build error; indicates asset-generation code leaking into email CSS. No client-side impact but signals a broken pipeline step.
7. **[P2] Confirm SPF/DKIM authentication** — `Authentication-Results` not captured; validate DKIM signing on `e.potterybarn.com` sending domain.
