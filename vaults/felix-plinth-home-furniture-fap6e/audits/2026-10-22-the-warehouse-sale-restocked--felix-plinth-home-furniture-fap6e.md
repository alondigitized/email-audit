# THE WAREHOUSE SALE, RESTOCKED
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This one is clean, and I stopped scrolling at the hero. "Warehouse Sale, up to 60% off" sits over a dark plum dahlia shot. It's moody and on-brand, and the offer reads in about a second. It's also the third time this week CB2 has run the warehouse sale. It was 10/20's "WAREHOUSE SALE: UP TO 60% OFF", and now it's "restocked" with a "new items just added" tag. I saw the 60% twice already. Restocked is the only news here, and the email never says what was restocked.
- Below the hero are four category tiles (furniture, lighting, rugs, decor), then an app-download sweepstakes, then a lighting feature. That's a lot of stacked ideas for one send. The sale is the one thing I care about. The $1,000 giveaway pulls against it, and I'm not downloading an app for a raffle ticket. With two houses in progress, I'd click through to lighting and rugs. But I'd do it from the tiles, since the hero shows me a flower and no product.
- Would I click? Probably a quick tap on Lighting or Rugs. Forward it? No. Nothing in it is specific enough to send to anyone, and the lamp at the bottom is one I'll probably see in Crate & Barrel's emails by Friday.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and readable: "UP TO 60% OFF" with a white "SHOP ALL" button. I get the deal without squinting.
- The four labeled tiles (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) let me jump straight to what I'm buying for the renovations.
- The rug and lighting photography is warm and consistent, with a rust, plum and brown palette. That matters when I'm trying to keep color across two houses.
- The loyalty block at the bottom is clear: "Earn 10% back" in Rewards Dollars. That's real rewards math for someone who optimizes cards.
- The lighting feature has a single product, a white lamp, and a "Shop Now" button. That's easy to act on.

## What's weak

- "Restocked" is never explained. There's no product, no price and no "back in stock" callout. The hero is a flower, not furniture.
- The $1,000 app giveaway sits in the middle of a sale email and splits my attention. It also reads like a sweepstakes with a lot of fine print.
- The bottom is crowded. App, rewards, gift list and text signup (15% off) all stack up as four small tiles, and the legal block under them is tiny and unreadable on my phone.
- Not one price, discount or product name appears anywhere in the email. "Up to 60%" could mean one rug.
- This is the third warehouse sale send in four days, and the second CB2 email today. I'm in a cadence rut.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual restocked product in the hero. Show a bed, a sofa or a lamp with the sale price and a "was/now" line. That would lift the email more than anything else.
- 2. Cut the app sweepstakes from sale sends, or move it to a separate send.
- 3. Add 3 to 4 priced products, such as a rug at $X or a lamp at $Y. Article and Wayfair use badges like "$134 off" and they work.
- 4. Add a shipping note, like "ships in 1 to 2 weeks" or "in stock now". That's what I need for furniture.
- 5. Trim the footer down to rewards and the text signup.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Warehouse Sale: rugs and lighting back in stock`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just landed, plus the lamp everyone wants is back.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token reached the delivered HTML. The QA run also found 6 content images with no alt text and 11 plain-HTTP tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, because the relay did not capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 62 tracking links were skipped (click-redirect domains), so none were probed. Link destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **HTTP tracking pixels:** 11 pixels load over plain `http://`, and all lack alt text. They are the Dotomi (Epsilon/Conversant) cookie-sync pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Consequences of the HTTP pixels:**
  - Clients that block non-HTTPS content will drop them, so the cookie-sync and retargeting calls will fail silently.
  - The 10-pixel cookie-redirect chain is a third-party tracking load that is not needed for rendering.
- **HTTPS pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/4e01ba53-…?mi_cid=…` are fine on protocol. Neither has alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (6):** These are hosted on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `80239223-e739-4d51-bc04-d32452126c21.jpg`
  - `8eebfac4-ed50-4d71-9ccf-cd4862ed3fdc.jpg`
  - `8dde80ba-f9d0-411e-a20e-0addd9980bb5.jpg`

  - `8e957a75-f2c0-405e-a28e-366aa14f3e65.gif`
  
  If any of these carry sale messaging or CTAs, that content is invisible with images off and to screen readers.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (about 14):** Pixels should use `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`). A missing attribute can make screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in both the global `*{}` rule and `body`. This disables user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Template residue (low severity):** The source still contains the unfilled placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Font URLs:** Some `@font-face` URLs have a double slash (`www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`). The HNW05 faces load from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`, a different domain. These are fragile, but the email falls back to Helvetica or sans-serif, so impact is low.
- **Limit of this review:** The HTML was truncated, so I could not check body structure, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw merge token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` was found unrendered. This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) style variable (`%%…%%`) that failed to resolve at send time.
  - It looks like a dynamic product-reviews or SKU-list block.
  - Because it is a SKU-list variable, the likely failure is a missing or empty data extension or content lookup. That would leave a blank or broken reviews or product module, or visible `%%…%%` text, in the delivered message.
  - I could not see where the token sits in the truncated source. Check whether it is in visible body text, an attribute (`href`/`src`), or a hidden or comment region.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are missing from captured headers. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender defect. Without them, there is no confirmed RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` has not been tested.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** I could not confirm either, because the HTML was truncated before the footer. Neither is flagged as missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They can't be assessed. All 62 links went through click-tracking redirects and were not resolved, so no landing-page URLs, UTM parameters or destination alignment are confirmed.
- **Other identifiers seen:** The only tracking identifiers visible are the Marketing Intelligence (MI) pixel parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) and the Dotomi `dtm_*` parameters. These are not site-continuity evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%` (priority):** Find the unresolved variable in the SFMC template and its data source, add a fallback or suppression for an empty value, and add pre-send validation that blocks sends with unrendered `%%…%%` tokens.
2. **Add alt text:** Add descriptive alt text to the 6 content images. Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/…`, the Dotomi pixels).
3. **Move Dotomi pixels to HTTPS:** Switch the 11 `http://` pixels to `https://` if Dotomi supports it, or drop the 10-pixel `cookieredir` chain.
4. **Re-enable zoom and text resizing:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
5. **Verify headers outside the relay:** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on a direct-to-inbox copy, such as a Gmail "Show original", before treating the missing headers as real defects.
6. **Re-run link and UTM checks:** Resolve the 62 redirect links (follow the redirects) to audit destinations and UTM parameters.
7. **Clean up the template:** Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment and the `//` double slashes in font URLs.
