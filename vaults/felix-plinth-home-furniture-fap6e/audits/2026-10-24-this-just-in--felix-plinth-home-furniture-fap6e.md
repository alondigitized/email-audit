- This is the first CB2 send in days that looks like a catalog and not a sale blast. That's the same Warehouse Sale I've now seen in three sends in two days. It reads like the sale banners got stapled onto a lookbook. ## What's working

- The "So much new" hero is a real room with a burl-wood table and a curved sofa. It sets a mood before it sells anything.
- The designer credits ("Designed by Mermelstein Estudio", "Studio ANANÓ") give me something to name-drop and a reason to care.
- Each product block has its own short hook, like "a best-selling collection takes new shape." Pottery Barn would have just stacked a grid.
- The product-tile section near the bottom has real prices. The Foley Mink Velvet armchair shows $319 against a struck-through $499, and the Bolla acrylic chair is $199.
- The rewards callout ("Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars on purchases at CB2 and Crate & Barrel") speaks to my highest-rewards-card habit.

## What's weak

- Two competing sales sit under the new-arrivals story, so I don't know what I'm supposed to do. This is the third Warehouse Sale appearance since 10/22.
- The "Semi-Annual Dining Event" is the same event as yesterday's "FINAL DAYS" send. A "final days" email followed by another one the next day makes the urgency feel fake.
- The "Shop" buttons are small and sit off to the side of each hero. On a phone that's a lot of thumb-hunting. Crate & Barrel's recent send at least put its top strip front and center.
- Nothing about shipping for furniture, lead times, or swatches. I'm buying a bed and a coffee table, and I have to leave the email to learn when they'd arrive.
- It's a long scroll to reach the actual price tiles, and the prices are the part that would make me click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Either run this as a new-arrivals lookbook, or lead with the dining event and drop the Warehouse Sale banner I've already seen three times.
- 2. Move the price tiles up and add a "swatches and ship dates" line under the bed and the dining pieces.
- 3. Make the "Shop" buttons full-width on mobile and name the product ("Shop the Davison bed").
- 4. Space out the sale reminders. Seeing the same hero repeatedly is what trains me to snooze the whole CB2 and Crate & Barrel family.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks. The QA run found no hard failures (57% pass rate, 0 issues, 3 warnings). However, 22 images have no alt text, 11 third-party tracking pixels load over `http://`, and none of the link, auth or unsubscribe checks could be verified. The HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **65 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 11 images load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  
  Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, so cookie-sync and attribution will silently fail for those recipients.
- **Heavy third-party pixel load.** Ten sequential Dotomi `cookieredir` pixels plus the UCM pixel add 11 extra third-party requests. They also expose the recipient's IP and client to that ad-tech domain.
- **Identifier in a pixel URL.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa278…` carries a hashed user parameter. This is expected for an open pixel but should be noted for privacy review.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 22 images.**
  - 9 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` (e.g. `f396aa5c-dea6-4426-b2c3-4dba3cfc8505.jpg`, `ff4e2783-…jpg`, `dbb13b0e-…jpg`). If these carry content or links, they are unreadable with images off or with a screen reader.
  - 13 tracking pixels (`UCMController`, `pub1–10.php`, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/b36f3d92-…`). These should have `alt=""` so they are explicitly decorative.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `body`.
- **Mobile breakpoint is narrow.** The `@media only screen and (max-width: 418px)` rule means phones wider than 418px, such as 428px-wide iPhone Plus/Max models, will not get the mobile classes (`.mobile`, `.f14`, `.p-sm-button`).
- **Generic `<title>`.** `CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME` does not match the subject line "THIS JUST IN".
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a placeholder left in the source. It has no functional impact.
- **Font loading.** `@font-face` files load from `www.cb2.com` and `www.crateandbarrel.com`. Some URLs have a double slash (`//Styles/CB2/…`). Most email clients ignore web fonts, so the fallback stack (Helvetica, sans-serif) is what most recipients will see.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated source. The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA flagged this and notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com` cannot be assessed from this capture.
- **Footer not verified.** The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance is not confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 65 tracked links went through redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked. The truncated source shows no direct `cb2.com` hrefs with UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all tracking pixels over HTTPS. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://` or drop them, and consider consolidating the 10 `cookieredir` calls.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com`. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and avoid `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
4. Raise the mobile breakpoint to about 480–600px so large phones get the responsive styles.
5. Re-run the capture with raw headers (not via the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Resolve a sample of the 65 tracking redirects to check final URLs, UTM parameters and HTTP status.
7. Make the `<title>` match the campaign and delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
