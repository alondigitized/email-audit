# ENDS TODAY: Last Double Rewards of the year!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- I'm a sucker for the highest-rewards card, so "double rewards" works on me. But this is the fourth or fifth Crate & Barrel email since 11/16, and the Kids arm is still in the mix. Today's earlier send, "New! A beautiful dining room, built for the way you live," already had me looking at dining tables. This one re-sells me the same dining category, plus sofas, rugs, a gift shop and kitchen electrics.
- It's a long scroll that tries to be everything. The mechanic is clear: every tile says "Earn $300 → $600 in Reward Dollars." What I never get is one hero product to anchor on. For a renovation buyer that's a catalog, not an offer. I'd probably click once on the dining section and the Axis sectional, then archive it into my Pinterest folder. I wouldn't forward it, because it's a points promo and not an inspiration piece.

## What's working

- The strikethrough on the old reward amount and the green new amount ("~~$300~~ $600") makes the doubling obvious. Wayfair would just say "up to X% off" and leave the math to me.
- The dining block fits my renovation. Portico Marble and Oak Dining Table, Broome chair and Oxley chair sit in a clean three-up with a "Shop Dining Furniture" pill.
- The Axis 3-Piece Sectional room shot is the best image here. It has the wicker pendant, burl stool and green velvet chair, which is the styled-room look I save.
- The brown header has a simple "ENDS TODAY" with two buttons. The deadline is credible, since it's "Last Double Rewards of the year."
- The KitchenAid line, "Save $170* + Earn $86 in Reward Dollars," is concrete. It gives me both a dollar discount and the rewards math.

## What's weak

- There's no single hero. It runs bedroom, dining, sofas, rugs, a Gift Shop banner, kitchen and cookware. Pottery Barn's "Up to 70% off" and Article's "UP TO 40% OFF" both hit me in two seconds. This takes about fifteen.
- It's too long, and the bottom half drifts to espresso machines and cookware. That's off-focus for someone furnishing two houses.
- The rewards earn is a dollar figure per item with a § footnote. I can't tell what it costs me or when the rewards expire without hunting for the fine print.
- I got two C&B sends earlier today ("Retreat collection" and the dining room one). Seeing dining tables again hours later feels like the CB2 loop I complained about.
- There's no shipping or lead-time info on the sofas and tables, and no swatch or sample link. For furniture, that's what actually decides whether I buy.
- The "Shop Top Categories" and "Learn More" buttons at the top compete with each other.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Use the Axis sectional with "2x Rewards on furniture, ends tonight 11:59 PT," and cut the kitchen and gift sections for furniture-engaged subscribers.
- 2. Segment. Anyone who clicked dining or sofa in today's earlier send shouldn't get the same categories again hours later.
- 3. Add a rewards-math line, e.g. "Spend $2,000 → $600 back," plus a quick-ship or delivery-window badge on the furniture.
- 4. Add a "Order swatches" or "Book a showroom visit" link under the sofa and rug blocks.
- 5. Cut the top CTAs to one button, and make the Learn More link a text link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double Rewards ends tonight at 11:59 PT`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn $600 back on a dining table. Tonight only.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, dining tables and rugs all earn 2x. Ends midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance to double rewards before Black Friday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard (`lang="en"`, XHTML doctype, MSO conditionals, a 460px mobile breakpoint). The main technical problems are widespread missing image alt text and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, because the QA harness didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed:** 136 links point to tracking/click-redirect domains and were skipped. Destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 26,924 of 36,671 characters (about 73%) are URLs, over the 70% warning threshold. The text part is dominated by long tracked links, which can add a mild spam-filter signal.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` to `n=5` beacons are present (LiveRamp). The `s=` value is a 64-character hex per-recipient identifier, and it matches the `mi_u=` prefix used on first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` assets. The same ID is therefore sent to a third-party domain on every open. This is an observation about data sharing, not a defect.
- **Remote-image dependencies:** the first-party tracking pixels are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/165f0a26-…`. Tracking is blocked when Apple Mail Privacy Protection or an image blocker is active.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** well over 80 `<img>` elements were flagged, and the list is truncated. Examples:
  - Content-style images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, e.g. `6e5bbd3c-5066-44d5-ba13-c00d5ec2f8e7.jpg` and `4fb62df8-4137-448f-91a5-fb4ec9bff4ee.jpg`, plus `.gif` files such as `03e2281b-f653-49be-92cf-3efa3fd90e6f.gif`.
  - Dynamic product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - Tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). These should carry `alt=""` rather than no attribute.
  - With images off, or in a screen reader, the missing alt text on linked images leaves the email blank or exposes raw filenames.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Conflicting `img` rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px or wider, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Duplicate and leftover head markup:**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
  - Four `format-detection` metas are present, with overlapping values.
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in. It is harmless but sloppy.
- **Title encoding:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML.
- **Not evaluated:** the HTML source was truncated, so the body structure, `role="presentation"` usage and link text are unchecked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Per-recipient `mi_u=` parameters resolved correctly.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found, and the QA note says AgentMail may not capture it. This is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the image hosts are on the same `mail.` subdomain, which is consistent.
- **Body footer:** the truncated source does not show a physical address or an unsubscribe link, so neither could be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. Links are wrapped in click-tracking redirects that were not probed, so final destinations, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Double Rewards" offer could not be checked. No evidence of a problem either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every linked content and product image. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels and spacers. This is the highest-volume issue.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta (and don't add `user-scalable=no`).
3. Confirm with raw `.eml` headers, not the AgentMail relay copy, that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and align.
4. Resolve the click-tracking redirects with a follow-redirects probe. Check destination status codes and UTM or campaign parameters, and confirm the landing page shows the Double Rewards offer.
5. Shorten tracked URLs in the plain-text part to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
6. Clean the template: remove the duplicate meta tags and the placeholder comment, escape `&` in `<title>`, and fix the conflicting `img` max-width media queries.
7. Review whether sending a per-recipient hash to `sr.rlcdn.com` matches the privacy-policy disclosures.
