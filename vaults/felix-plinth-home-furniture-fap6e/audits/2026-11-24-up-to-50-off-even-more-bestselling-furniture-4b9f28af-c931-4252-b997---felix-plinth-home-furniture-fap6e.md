# Up to 50% off even more bestselling furniture!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- I knew this was Crate & Barrel before I opened it. The sender line says Kids and Black Friday, and this is at least the ninth C&B-family send in two days. The hero is clean: "new deals added! up to 50% off furniture + free shipping" with a Shop Now button over a bedroom shot. It's the same promo as this morning's "Exclusive first look: NEW Black Friday deals added!" and the earlier "NEW DEALS just added" email. This one is just reworded around furniture.
- The furniture blocks help. Bedroom, nursery and a workspace desk each have a Sale/Reg price grid and a "Shop [category]" button. Then it goes off the rails. There's a baby-photo "holiday travel-ready gear" block, a Christmas collection block, an app download pitch with a "$500" chance, and a Design Desk pitch. At about 10,000px tall, I'd never scroll it on my phone.
- My verdict: I'd skim the hero and the desk block, and I might save the workspace desk to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I wouldn't forward it. I'm not buying cribs, and the Kids label makes me assume this isn't for me.

## What's working

- The hero states the whole offer in one line: "up to 50% off furniture + free shipping." Free shipping on furniture matters to me.
- Each category block shows Sale and Reg prices on three products, so I can do the math without clicking.
- The buttons are verb + noun, like "Shop Bedroom Furniture" and "Shop Workspace Furniture." That beats Wayfair's vague banner blasts.
- The Design Desk "Book a Free Appointment" block is a real service. It's the same idea as Pottery Barn Design Services' "FREE design help in stores," which I scored 7.
- Layout is clean, and I couldn't see any broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The sender name says Kids, and half the content is cribs, nursery and baby gear. I'm renovating two houses, so that's a mismatch.
- The email is far too long. After three furniture blocks it wanders into holiday travel gear, the Christmas collection and an app download. CB2 and Article at least keep their hero to one thing.
- It repeats the "new deals added" angle from earlier today with nothing new in it. I get a dozen C&B-family emails in two days.
- The "free shipping" claim sits in the hero with no deadline or delivery timeline. For furniture I want to know lead times, and nothing here covers that.
- There's no swatch or sample program, no rewards-card angle, and no countdown. Nothing says the sale ends tonight.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus the three furniture blocks, and move the gear, Christmas and app content to a different send. That would lift the email the most.
- 2. Add a real deadline, like "ends Monday 11:59 PT," and a furniture delivery window so the free shipping means something.
- 3. Segment by browsing. Send the desk and bedroom blocks to people who don't shop nursery, and the nursery blocks to the Kids list.
- 4. Put the Design Desk higher up. For people doing whole-house projects it's a stronger hook than another discount.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a large Marketo-style/Movable Ink build with 105 click-tracked links and about 50 images flagged for missing alt text. The truncated HTML and the AgentMail relay stripped the headers, so the compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive. They are not confirmed failures. QA result: 57% pass, 0 errors, 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 105 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** 5 LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5`) fire on open. Each carries a long hashed identifier in the query string (`s=56aaa2787a...`).
- **Other tracking images:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/71eb5ab4-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are also open or analytics beacons. The `dv.` URL has no file extension.
- **Repeated image:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` appears 6 times. These are probably dynamic-content (Movable Ink) renders. Check that each instance resolves, because the images are generated at open time.
- **Pixel count:** I can't tell whether the number of third-party pixels matters for deliverability or image-blocking behavior. The body past the truncation point wasn't visible.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flags about 50 `<img>` elements. Examples:
  - Content images under `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (for example `23c80033-9068-4aa7-b85b-ee3f61af397a.jpg`) and `m/47/bbf6f9d4-...png`.
  - Scene7 images, such as the `CK_H26_KidsHolidayMagic_24x13_Edit3_Tert` hero and the `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White` spacer.
  - With no alt text, the layout is unreadable when images are blocked, and screen readers fall back to filenames or URLs.
  - For the tracking pixels and spacers, an empty `alt=""` is acceptable. The QA output doesn't say whether these are absent or empty attributes.
  - Missing alt on the product and hero images is a real defect, especially if they sit inside `<a>` tags. Linked images with no alt produce unlabeled links.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on iOS, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype should be `&amp;`. Strict parsers will treat it as invalid.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared 4 times with overlapping values, and both `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset>` are present. This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Template residue:** A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. The `<!--V2-->` comment also sits before `<html>`, which can trigger quirks mode in some older clients.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, so the later 640px rule wins and the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Global resets:** `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are broad. Verify that text-heavy blocks aren't clipped or force-centered.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The rest of the body was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** QA notes the relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed. If it is really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for senders over 5,000 messages per day are not met.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so check that alignment holds there.
- **Footer unverified:** The visible-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed because the footer was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The 105 tracked links were not probed, so there is no evidence of UTM parameters, final landing URLs or destination alignment with the "Up to 50% off even more bestselling furniture!" subject. The link-wrapping format also hides the destination, which makes the UTM check impossible without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add meaningful `alt` to every linked content image, hero and product image. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels. This is the main confirmed defect.
2. **Viewport:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Title:** Change `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` to `&amp;`, and remove the stray placeholder comment and the duplicate `format-detection` and charset metas.
4. **CSS:** Reorder or merge the `img` max-width media queries so the intended 600px or 640px cap applies.
5. **Headers:** Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message from the original SMTP source, not the relay copy. Then capture `Authentication-Results` the same way.
6. **Tracking links:** Resolve a sample of the 105 tracked links with a redirect-following check to confirm destinations and UTMs.
7. **Footer:** Confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the untruncated HTML.
