## What's working

- **Category pivot paid off.** After days of Memorial Day furniture saturation, a bedding-specific subject line actually registered as new information.
- **Hero image is clean and masculine-neutral.** Navy bedding, layered texture — not precious, not overly styled. Works for my renovation mental model.
- **"Recommended Just for You" module exists.** Wayfair sends me the same emoji-stuffed grid every time with zero personalization signal; the fact that PB has individual product cards with what looks like browsing-based picks is legitimately differentiated.
- **"Extra 20% Off" stacking is visible.** If that discount layers on top of the existing sale, that's real money on a $400 duvet insert. The two-offer structure is actually useful for someone running rewards math.
- **"Shop Bedding Deals" CTA is specific.** Verb + noun, no waffling. Better than the "Discover" or "Explore" copy Wayfair defaults to.

## What's weak

- **Email 24 fatigue is real.** Even a category pivot can't fully reset the mental exhaust. The goodwill PB built with the subject line erodes the second I remember how many times I've been here this week.
- **"Just Added Handcrafted Rugs" feels tacked on.** I opened a bedding email. Mid-scroll rugs is a separate consideration and it breaks the category focus — it reads like someone couldn't let a send go out without hitting one more SKU bucket.
- **Family of Brands cross-promo block is filler.** PB Kids, PB Teen, Williams Sonoma — none of that is relevant to me, and it's a big visual block taking up prime scroll real estate. CB2 doesn't do this to me in their sends.
- **"Up to 20% off" is soft.** After seeing 40% and 60% off in the Crate & Barrel family this week, a 20% ceiling on the hero feels underwhelming. The "Extra 20%" secondary offer does some work to rescue it, but the stacking math should be front and center, not buried below the fold.
- **No shipping or lead-time language visible.** I'm renovating a second home — I need to know if a duvet ships in time to matter. Article always surfaces lead time; PB doesn't here.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the stacked savings, not the ceiling.** "Up to 20% off" as the hero is weak. If the extra 20% stacks, say "Up to 40% off when savings stack" in the hero. That's the number that makes me stop scrolling.
- 2. **Kill the Family of Brands block or move it below the footer.** It dilutes the bedding focus and wastes scroll depth. Reserve that real estate for a second personalization module.
- 3. **Add delivery timing.** One line under the hero CTA: "Order by [date] for pre-summer delivery." Article does this; it converts for anyone mid-renovation.
- 4. **Cut the rugs section or make it a single teaser link.** Don't split the category. If rugs are on sale too, that's a separate send — or at minimum a one-liner at the bottom.
- 5. **Surface reviews on the recommended products.** Even a star rating under each card in the personalization rail adds trust and specificity.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] PHP `header()` calls injected into `<style>` block**

```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```

Server-side PHP statements are present verbatim in a `<style>` block. This is a template rendering failure — the PHP was not executed and fell through as literal text. CSS parsers will ignore it, so no visual breakage, but it is evidence of a broken template pipeline for this module and will flag spam filters that inspect raw source.

Webmail clients (Gmail web, Outlook.com) display the tab as blank when the email is opened in a browser pane. Low severity but fixable.
