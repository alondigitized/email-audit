# Select styles are EXTRA 20% OFF + How we’re styling the fall season
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- I've been getting this maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip all week, so my first reaction was "oh, again." The 10/30 "FREE design help" send and the "Sooner is better! In-stock seating" send both opened with it. The difference this time is that the strip is only the top. Below it is "The Fall Lookbook," a moody, dark-wood, plaid-and-leather editorial. That's the Pottery Barn I like, and it's why I scrolled.
- The screenshot is shrunk to a long thin strip, so I can't read the fine print or product names. What I can see is a lot of email: three themed rooms (Rustic Lodge, Heritage Autumn, Indigo Blues), a "Recommended just for you" grid, the Christmas Shop banner, a Design Crew banner, app, rewards card, Affirm and a dense legal block. I'm renovating two houses, so the room-by-room styling is useful. I'd save this to my inspiration folder and probably not buy from it today. I'd skim it, skip the forward, and maybe click "Shop the Look" on Indigo Blues.

## What's working

- The lookbook photography is strong. The warm leather, plaid armchair and antler chandelier read as a point of view, not a catalog dump.
- Naming each room (Rustic Lodge, Heritage Autumn, Indigo Blues) with a short descriptor gives me a palette to react to. That's useful for coordinating two houses.
- The "Shop the Look" links under each room are the right idea for someone building a whole space, not buying one SKU.
- The 10% back in rewards card block is visible. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I did the math.
- The Design Crew banner with "Book a free appointment" ties into the showroom and service side that I want.

## What's weak

- The maroon sale strip leads again. It's the same opener as the last several sends, so I tune it out.
- There's no deadline anywhere I can see. "Select styles" with no end date and no list of which styles gives me no reason to act now.
- Too many CTAs compete: Shop All Sale, Get Inspired, three Shop the Looks, Shop All In-Stock Furniture, Shop Now on Christmas and Book a Free Appointment. Nothing is clearly the main action.
- The Christmas Shop banner and the "Recommended just for you" grid (curtain rod, dining tables, rug) feel bolted on after the lookbook. Fall styling and a Christmas tree in the same email is a mixed message.
- The legal and fine-print block at the bottom is huge. A "select styles" offer with that much exclusion text doesn't feel fully honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with the lookbook and make the sale a slim banner, or lead with the sale and cut the lookbook down to one room. Right now it tries to be both.
- 2. Put a real deadline and a visible exclusion hint in the sale strip, such as "Ends Sunday, excludes ___".
- 3. Make one primary button and demote the rest to text links.
- 4. Link "Shop the Look" to a bundle with a total price and in-stock or ship-date info. For furniture, ship dates decide whether I buy.
- 5. Move the Christmas banner into its own send. Crate & Barrel is already blasting me six times a day, and I'd rather Pottery Barn not do the same.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with SFMC-style `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects. The main technical problems are widespread missing alt text on image-sliced content, non-HTTPS tracking pixels that put the recipient address in the URL, and head/markup hygiene issues. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are unverified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **58 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im…` on the logo link. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is 72% URLs** (11091 of 15336 chars), which is above the 70% threshold. This comes from the long opaque `?qs=` tokens, and it can hurt the text-part spam score.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. It is requested over plain HTTP and may be blocked or upgraded by clients.
- **Stacked third-party beacons:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`, `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/e15516ce-…?mi_cid=…`, and the `svopn` pixel. Each adds a request and may be blocked by privacy proxies.
- **An `<img>` points at an HTML page:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. An image tag whose `src` is a `.html` page won't render as an image. It looks like a mis-wired beacon or a broken element.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on roughly 55 images.** The hero and offer banner `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, the lookbook slices `slices_01–16.jpg` (`/0725_SAT_AM_FallLookbook3Chapters/`), the footer slices `slices_01–18.jpg` (`/Email-Footer/`), and all product images (e.g. `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`) have none. With images blocked or in a screen reader, the offer and navigation are unavailable. The brand logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **HTTP image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">`** disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **PHP code leaked into a `<style>` block:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");` follows the `html {…}` rule. It is invalid CSS that parsers drop, but it could corrupt rule parsing in strict clients.
- **Layout tables missing `role="presentation"`:** the outer wrapper tables and the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) don't have it. Some other tables do.
- **Spacer image with no alt:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a cross-brand asset host, which is a dependency risk.
- **Mobile selector mismatch (low confidence):** the mobile rules target `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`, but the visible tables use `class="contenttable"`. The body is truncated, so I can't confirm whether those classes exist.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible HTML or QA data. The source is truncated, so coverage is partial.
- **The recipient email is exposed in plain text in URLs** (`sv_sveme=` and `coreemail.html?email=`), unhashed. One of these is over HTTP, so the address travels unencrypted.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so the finding is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that is a gap against Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- The visible HTML is truncated, so the footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** All links are opaque `?qs=` redirects, and the product image `src` URLs on `www.potterybarn.com` carry no campaign parameters. Landing pages and attribution depend on the redirector's resolved destination, which wasn't probed.
- The `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters on the `clickcp` pixel are the only visible campaign identifiers.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, especially the offer banner `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg` and all `slices_*` images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, the `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS. Stop passing the raw email address in query strings and use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID instead.
3. Fix or remove the `coreemail.html` image element.
4. Remove the PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block, populate `<title>`, drop `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
5. Re-run the QA against raw headers, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm landing pages return 200 and carry UTM parameters.
7. Cut the plain-text URL ratio below 70% by shortening the tracked URLs or adding more plain-text copy.
