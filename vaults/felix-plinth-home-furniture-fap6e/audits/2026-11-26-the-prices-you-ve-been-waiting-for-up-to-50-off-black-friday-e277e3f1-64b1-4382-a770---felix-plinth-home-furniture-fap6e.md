## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard table-based, Marigold/Cheetah-style template with VML/MSO fallbacks. The QA run flags about 27 images with no alt text, five duplicate third-party pixels, and header-level compliance checks (List-Unsubscribe, authentication) that it could not verify. The HTML source is truncated, so body-level checks (footer, links, UTMs) are not confirmable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **70 tracking links were not probed** because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, but no failure is confirmed.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels** load `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…cfd8&n=1` through `n=5`. They differ only in the `n` counter, which is redundant and adds third-party requests.
- **The same 64-hex value (`56aaa2787a67…cfd8`) appears** in the LiveRamp `s=` parameter and in the first-party `mi_u=` parameter at `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. A hashed recipient identifier is therefore being passed to a third-party domain (see Compliance).
- **Mixed image hosts.** Most images come from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, but one comes from `s7d5.scene7.com` with percent-encoded `%5F` underscores in the path. It still renders, but it bypasses the ESP image domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 27 images**, confirmed by QA. Examples:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `…/m/1/592f13e9-481c-4372-b648-5341189d5ae4.jpg`
  - `…/m/1/91ea068d-869a-42ec-b19e-06266e21f83f.gif`
  - the scene7 `2026%5F1105%5FCBK%5FPersonalizedGiftShop%5FSeconda…` asset
- **With images blocked, content is unreadable.** The alt-less images appear to be the content and CTA images, so this affects both screen readers and image-blocking clients.
- **Tracking pixels lack explicit `alt=""`.** `448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e7de2cc8-…` are flagged. Purely decorative pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `body`, which prevents user text scaling on iOS.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` are not self-closed.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
- **Weak `<title>`.** It is generic ("Crate & Barrel") rather than the subject line, which affects the web-view/archive title.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the shipped source.
- **Global resets are broad.** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` can cause unintended inheritance in nested content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML or the subject line. The only unresolved bracketed text is the template-instruction comment noted above, which is not a merge token. The body beyond the truncation point is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found.** This is a QA warning, and it may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping headers. It is unconfirmed for the original send, so it should be verified on a direct-delivery copy.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found**, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found**, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain whose alignment needs checking from a raw header.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified** because the source is truncated.
- **Privacy:** a hashed recipient identifier is sent to `sr.rlcdn.com` (LiveRamp) five times per open. Confirm this is covered by the sender's privacy disclosures and consent/opt-out handling (CCPA/GDPR). This is a compliance question, not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 70 tracked links were skipped as redirect domains, and no landing URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. The only first-party signal is `mi_cid=` / `mi_mid=` on the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` open-pixel, which suggests Marigold/MI-style tracking. Whether the final landing URLs carry UTMs, and whether they match the Black Friday "Up to 50% off" offer, remains open.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and CTA images, and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Verify the raw headers of a direct-delivery copy for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. The relay limitation makes the QA result inconclusive.
4. Consolidate the five `rlcdn.com` pixels into one, and confirm the disclosure for sharing the hashed identifier with a third party.
5. Escape the title (`&amp;`), set it to the subject or campaign name, fix the self-closing tags, and remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder comment.
6. Resolve a sample of the 70 redirect links manually or via the ESP's click-domain API to confirm 200 destinations and UTM presence.
7. Host the scene7 asset through the ESP image domain, or confirm its cache and availability.
