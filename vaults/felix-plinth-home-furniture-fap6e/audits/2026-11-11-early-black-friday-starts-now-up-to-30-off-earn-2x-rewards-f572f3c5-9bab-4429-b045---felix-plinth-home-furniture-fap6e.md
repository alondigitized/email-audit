# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW! Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth or seventh Crate & Barrel send in about three days, and the "last Double Rewards this year!" red banner shows up twice in one email. I've now seen Double Rewards on 11/10 ("Double Rewards on 100s of bestsellers") and earlier today, so "last" has stopped meaning anything. Crate & Barrel is clearly using the same urgency bucket every send.
- The hero, "starts today! up to 30% off Early Black Friday", is a clear offer over a decent living room shot. But once I scroll, it turns into a kitchen email. I get a Caraway/All-Clad/Dyson block and a Thanksgiving menu, and furniture only appears as a small tile. I'm renovating two houses, so I want sofas, rugs and lighting. I'd skim this and probably click once, into furniture, then archive it.

## What's working

- "Up to 30% off Early Black Friday" with a Shop Now button is a clear, single-offer hero.
- Reward math is stated outright: 2x back in Reward Dollars with the Crate Card, through 11/17. That fits how I buy, since I always go for the highest-rewards card.
- The 2x2 category grid (entertaining, furniture, kitchen, holiday decor) lets me jump straight to furniture.
- The "Book a Free Appointment" Design Desk module is the only showroom/design-service content, and it matches my two-house renovation.
- The bottom nav (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, Decor & Pillows) is easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards banner repeats twice, and "last" is not credible after the 11/10 "Double Rewards on 100s of bestsellers" send.
- Everything past the hero is kitchen: Caraway, All-Clad, Cuisinart, Dyson, Shun. Furniture, my actual category, gets one small tile.
- The tiles say "up to 30% off furniture" with no shipping or in-stock delivery info. Pottery Barn's "in-stock furniture delivery" angle in its 70% off email at least speaks to my timeline.
- The fine print under the hero is tiny and dense, which makes the offer feel less honest.
- I can't compare the rewards math against the sale price. Does 2x stack with the discount, or does the discount replace it?

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by browse history. Someone who has looked at sofas should see sofas and rugs, not Dyson vacuums. Wayfair's 5' x 8' rug email at least led with the category named in its subject.
- 2. Retire the second Double Rewards block, or put it at the very top and cut the fine print.
- 3. Add a furniture hero with "ships in X weeks" and a swatch/sample CTA.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Seven sends in three days is training me to ignore the brand. Article's "Black Friday Sale, Coming Soon" teaser was a single, calmer send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: sofas, rugs & lighting from 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-stock furniture ships fast. Earn 2x Reward Dollars with your Crate Card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last 2x Rewards window closes 11/17. Start with sofas and rugs.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML Transitional layout with `lang="en"` and VML/MSO fallbacks. The main confirmed problems are about 15 content images with no `alt` text and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 70 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) are present. The `s=` parameter looks like a hashed recipient identifier. Repeating the pixel five times is redundant.
- **Per-recipient tokens in URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` (7 images), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d498b6dc-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carry per-recipient identifiers. These are normal for ESP/personalization tracking, but they are unauthenticated identifiers in image URLs.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 29 images in total.** The 15 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (and one under `/m/47/`) have no `alt`:
  - `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `6100bbef-…jpg`, `d8683d75-…jpg`, `84b30380-…jpg`, `42cea11d-…jpg`, `d409bb7f-…jpg`
  - `43bbb542-…jpg`, `2808c76b-…jpg`, `b97c5525-…jpg`, `70a68d08-…jpg`, `826c4ae4-…jpg`
  - `24335f96-…gif`, `0df48a1a-…jpg`, `b37fe807-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`
  
  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show nothing or the raw filename.
- **Other missing alt:** the tracking and personalization images should carry `alt=""` and are lower impact. These are the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, 5 `rlcdn` gifs, `o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype (should be `&amp;`).
- **Template leftovers:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never filled in. There are also duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags (3 or more) and both `http-equiv` and `charset` declarations.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`) were visible in the truncated source. The only personalization seen is the `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` tracking identifiers, which resolved to values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** QA flagged it as likely not captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a proven defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (RFC 8058):** same caveat. Check the raw headers at the receiving MTA.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Not confirmed as a failure.
- **Footer not inspected:** the physical address and the unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 70 click links were not probed, and the visible source doesn't expose final landing URLs or UTM parameters. Tracking appears to go through the ESP redirect domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 15 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and `alt=""` to the pixel and tracking images.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment and the duplicate meta tags.
4. Deduplicate the five `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels, and confirm the LiveRamp hashed-identifier sharing is covered by the privacy policy.
5. Re-run the checks with raw headers (for example from a direct-to-mailbox seed) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Resolve a sample of the 70 click-tracking links to confirm landing pages, redirect depth and UTM or campaign parameters. Also run the footer through a full (non-truncated) check.
