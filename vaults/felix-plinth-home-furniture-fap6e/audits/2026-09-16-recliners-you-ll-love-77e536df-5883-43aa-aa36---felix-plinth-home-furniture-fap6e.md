## What's weak

- Half the product images are broken — blank white tiles with placeholder icons under headlines like "Just for You: Espresso Machines" and "Must-Have LED Lanterns." That's a rendering failure I can see with my own eyes, not a nitpick.
- Subject promises recliners; body buries the recliner category after one thumbnail and pivots into sheds, pergolas, privacy screens, toilets — a random-category dump, not a curated recliner send.
- No offer anywhere. No percentage off, no price, no "starting at $X." Just category links.

- Zero furniture-buyer basics I actually need: no swatch/fabric options on the recliner, no dimensions, no delivery/assembly info, no rewards math tied to my card.

- 1. Fix the broken image tiles before sending — this is the single biggest lift possible, a broken product photo kills trust instantly and this had at least six of them.
- 2. If the subject says recliners, make recliners the hero — one lifestyle shot, a price or % off, a "Shop Recliners" CTA — not a single thumbnail lost in a 20-category grid.
- 3. Cut the grid down to 3-4 relevant categories tied to an actual renovation stage (living room, primary bedroom) instead of dumping sheds and toilets next to recliners.
- 4. Add a real offer. Crate & Barrel's KitchenAid send and CB2's "Things of Quality" collab email both gave me a reason to click — a story or a price. This gave me neither.
- 5. Segment this against my browse history — if I've looked at recliners, show me recliners with swatches and fit info, not everything in the catalog.

1. Technical Summary
Uxbridge/Wayfair "Graymatter" batch template with standard tracking/personalization tokens; QA shows no unsubscribe/authentication headers captured, widespread missing alt text, and several HTTP (non-HTTPS) tracking pixels.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed material links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `refid=MKTEML_121694`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16287`, `mdlid`) — structurally consistent, no malformed params observed.
- 12 of 71 total links were probed (59 skipped by design/deprioritization); of those probed, 7 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by target server, not confirmed broken) and 4 were left unprobed due to time budget. No confirmed-broken (4xx/5xx non-rate-limit) links in the probed set.
- Tracking pixel `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...` (20 instances, `s=124126000` through `s=124126019`) is served over plain HTTP, not HTTPS — flagged by QA as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently break open-tracking for this batch.
- Open-tracking beacon `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` present and uses HTTPS — no issue.
