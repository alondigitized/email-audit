# Amazing Early Black Friday deals + Up to 70% off throws & pillows
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. This is at least the sixth send since 11/16, and it's the same maroon "Early Black Friday" pitch I got this morning in "Early Black Friday is UP TO 70% OFF" and "Early Black Friday deals to beat the holiday rush." I opened it knowing roughly what was inside. The hero is clean, though. It has a big "Up to 70% off Pillows & Throws" over a faux fur throw photo, with one SHOP NOW button. It's more focused than the generic banner sends.
- The body is a tidy pillow-and-throw grid, then a maroon category block, then "Recommended just for you." For a guy renovating two houses, the throws are a nice-to-have. The stocking row is not what I'm shopping for. The in-stock furniture strip, "delivered in 1-4 weeks," is the only part that speaks to my actual project. It's buried near the bottom.
- Verdict: I'd skim it and save it to my Pinterest folder, but I wouldn't click. I wouldn't forward it. The Mink Faux Fur throw is the one thing that might get a tap.

## What's working

- The hero names the product category, "Pillows & Throws," which is more specific than the usual maroon banner.
- There's one clear SHOP NOW button above the fold, and the up-to-70% offer reads in about two seconds.
- "These soft, cozy must-haves all ship free!" gives me a shipping hook that matters when I'm ordering for two houses.
- The "Earning Rewards" block mentions 10% back on the Pottery Barn card. That's relevant to how I buy, since I optimize for rewards.
- The product tiles are large, and the pillow and throw photography is good.

## What's weak

- The cadence is exhausting. The 11/16–11/18 run has used nearly the same maroon "Early Black Friday" language every time.
- The "Recommended just for you" row (stockings, curtain rod, quilt, platform bed) feels generic. I'm renovating, and nothing in it reflects that.
- The product tiles have no prices, no "was/now" and no star ratings. "Up to 70% off" doesn't tell me whether the Mink Faux Fur Pillow is 20% or 70% off.
- The "Book a free appointment" Design Crew sweepstakes with a $3,000 gift card is a distraction from the sale. The legal text under it is a wall.
- The in-stock furniture offer is a thin banner low in the email. It's the most useful message for me, and it's the least prominent.
- The footer is a block of tiny, dense legal text. I'd have to zoom to read it on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices on the tiles ("$79 · was $149") and add star ratings. That alone would raise my click odds.
- 2. Move "In-stock furniture, delivered in 1–4 weeks" up under the hero for a renovation audience, and segment it for people who've browsed furniture.
- 3. Cut the send frequency, or make each send distinct. Crate & Barrel's "Your holiday traditions, our festive new decor" at least varied the angle. CB2 and Wayfair have shown the same fatigue, so Pottery Barn isn't alone, but it's still a problem.
- 4. Drop the sweepstakes block from promo sends, or give it its own email.
- 5. Add a color or room filter, such as "Neutral throws for your living room." Pinterest-style inspiration fits how I save emails.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Image-slice email on an ESP click-redirect stack (SFMC-style `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=`). The main confirmed issues are ~55 images without alt text, 3 non-HTTPS image sources, and recipient email addresses in the query strings of tracking-pixel URLs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the relay dropped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** They are all on click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`). The QA run therefore can't confirm that any of them resolve or where they land.
- **Mixed-protocol tracking pixels:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is plain HTTP.
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party pixel (Turn).
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/0a6032e5-...?mi_cid=...` is a further open pixel.
  - So there are at least three separate open-tracking beacons, one of them unencrypted.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on West Elm's CDN. It is a dependency on another domain, and it has no alt text.
- **Stale assets in a 2026 send:** `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2025/US/H25_Bottom_InStock_V2/...` and `.../pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` are 2025 and 2022 assets reused in the footer. The 2022 one is also served over `http://`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 55 images.** This covers:
  - The two slice sets, `.../H26_Sale_EBF_HolidayDecor/slices_01–18.jpg` and `.../Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Product images such as `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`.
  - Banners `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg` and `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg`.
  - Because this is a sliced-image layout, with images blocked the email likely renders as blank space with no readable offer or CTA text. The QA flags the missing alt text but not the rendered result.
- **Pixels without `alt=""`:** `coreemail.html`, `d.turn.com/.../cat/1`, `svopn...` and `clickcp...` are flagged. For decorative or tracking images, an explicit empty `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel. These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings in some clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **`maximum-scale=1`** in the viewport meta disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Stray PHP in a stylesheet:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is server-side code leaking into CSS. It is harmless to render but sloppy, and it may corrupt the parse of the rule that follows it in some clients.
- **Plain-text part is >70% URLs** (12,431 of 17,434 chars). That is consistent with an image-heavy HTML part and gives the plain-text alternative almost no readable copy.
- **Web font:** `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat` is not supported in many clients, notably Outlook and Gmail. This is low impact if fallback fonts are declared; the truncated source doesn't confirm that.
- The rest of the body was truncated, so any `role="presentation"` or width issues further down are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the available source.
- **Recipient email in URLs:** the address is passed in clear text in query strings, as `?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (`coreemail.html`) and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (`svopn`). Putting the address in the URL exposes it to proxy and server logs. The `svopn` pixel is also plain HTTP, so the address is sent unencrypted.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so the result is **unverified** and not a confirmed sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this needs a check against the raw original headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Send domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the From address, but alignment alone doesn't confirm a pass.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not visible in the truncated source and can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable from this data.** Every destination link is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect and none were probed. The final URLs, UTM parameters and landing pages are therefore unknown.
- The only direct `potterybarn.com` URLs are image assets under `/pbimgs/...` and `customer-service/coreemail.html`, which is a tracking pixel.
- The `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` params on `clickcp.potterybarn.com` and `sv_cid` on `svopn` show a tracking ID scheme, but no UTMs appear.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC before treating them as failures.
2. **Resolve the redirect chains.** Follow a sample of the 63 `click.e.potterybarn.com` links with a redirect-following probe, then check destination status, UTMs and offer alignment.
3. **Add alt text** to all content images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
4. **Move the three `http://` images to HTTPS**, in particular the `svopn` pixel that carries the recipient address.
5. **Stop putting the recipient email in query strings.** Use an opaque token instead.
6. **Clean the markup.** Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block, set a real `<title>`, drop `maximum-scale=1`, and replace the 2022 and 2025 legacy banners and the West Elm spacer.
7. **Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML**, so the footer unsubscribe link and the physical address can be checked.
