# RUGS FOR EVERY ROOM
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Rugs. Just rugs, top to bottom, and honestly — this is the most competently-executed CB2 send I've seen all week. "Everything You Want In A Rug" hero, clean product grid, vintage-inspired/hand-knotted copy, no overlapping-text disaster like Tuesday's Upholstery Event banner. I'm mid-renovation on two houses and rugs are an actual line item on my list right now, so the category is right. But it's rug #4 or #5 pitch from the C&B family this week if I count the "New arrivals," "Restock alert," and "Things of Quality" sends, and there's a whole Upholstery Event banner shoved in the middle that has nothing to do with the subject line.
- Compared to Article's "organized entryway" email, which at least gave me a project to attach the products to, this is just a straight catalog scroll — six rug SKUs, then an app download pitch, then six MORE rug SKUs at the bottom. That's a lot of real estate for one category. Pottery Barn's soft-goods email at least led with a styled room shot and got out of the way; this one keeps going and going.
- Would I click? Probably, since I need a rug for the second house and a couple of these (Leonore, the leather chair one) actually caught my eye. Would I forward it? No — not distinctive enough to send to a friend, it's just "here are rugs."

## What's working

- Hero copy directly matches what I need right now — "designed for every room," "runners to 10x16'" is useful sizing info up front.
- Product grid is clean, well-lit, labeled by name (Lavish, Anders, Leonore, Eros, Arakati) — feels like a real catalog, not a jumbled promo.
- Two distinct "SHOP RUGS" CTAs (top and after the initial grid) so I'm not hunting for the button.
- No render bugs this time — a real relief after Tuesday's broken "SEMI-ANNUAL UPHOLSTERY EVENT" banner overlap.
- "More For You" section at the bottom has actual prices and sizes visible ($1099.00, 8'x10', etc.) — that's rewards-math-relevant info I actually check before buying.

## What's weak

- The Upholstery Event 25%-off banner is jammed in the middle of a rugs email — if I wanted upholstery news I'd open that email, this just dilutes the rug focus.
- Way too long — six rugs, an app pitch, then six more rugs. By the time I hit "More For You" I've already mentally checked out.
- No mention of swatch/sample ordering before I commit $1000+ to a rug sight-unseen — that's the one thing that actually reduces my hesitation on a big-ticket textile purchase.
- Fine print at the very bottom is a wall of gray legal text — nobody's reading that, and it makes the whole email feel heavier than it needs to.
- This is my fourth-plus C&B-family rug/home email this week; the brand isn't spacing sends out, and I'm starting to skim rather than read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — one hero, one 6-product grid, one CTA, done. Drop the second "More For You" block or save it for a follow-up send.
- 2. Pull the Upholstery Event banner out entirely — it's a different sale, different category, and breaks the rug narrative.
- 3. Add a one-line swatch/sample callout near the hero ("order a free swatch before you buy") — this is the single biggest lever for a $1000+ rug purchase from someone who's never seen it in person.
- 4. Move the app-download pitch to its own send or the footer — it doesn't belong sandwiched between two product grids.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The Rug Edit: Vintage-Inspired, Hand-Knotted`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `From 3x5 runners to 10x16 statement pieces — free swatches available`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Handpicked rugs for every room, ships free over $79`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email renders via a table-based MSO/Outlook-compatible template with standard client resets, but ships one unresolved merge token and several tracking/compliance gaps surfaced by automated QA (43% pass rate).

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 70 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect domains — not itself a defect, but means link destinations are unverified).
- Multiple legacy ad-tracking pixels load over plain HTTP instead of HTTPS:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 endpoints, same pattern)
  - Risk: mail clients that block mixed-content/non-HTTPS image loads (Outlook, some webmail) will silently drop these, breaking whatever tracking/attribution they're meant to capture.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 content/product images and 1 tracking pixel (`o.gif`) are missing `alt` attributes, e.g.:
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/732cf7e6-3cc2-4508-9ba2-fa607209dbe2.jpg`
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/a1f0160c-5c3a-4d7b-8704-4d47c7c5ceab.jpg`
  - (and 8 more with the same pattern) — screen readers will announce nothing for these when images are blocked/disabled, which is the default in most mail clients.
- Template includes standard MSO conditional comments, `@media` breakpoints at 418px/690px/768px, and Apple Mail reformatting disable meta tag — no rendering defects identified in the visible markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- [FAIL] Unresolved raw merge token in rendered output: `%%reviews_skulist%%`. This indicates a template/personalization-engine failure — the token was not substituted before send, and will display literally to recipients.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a sender-side omission; cannot confirm compliance status from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) header not detected — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome) not present in the captured headers — authentication status is unverifiable from this data; likely a relay/capture artifact rather than evidence of a missing DKIM/SPF setup.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues found — insufficient data to evaluate, since the 70 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe and their resolved landing-page destinations/UTM parameters were not captured.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the merge-token resolution pipeline so `%%reviews_skulist%%` renders as intended content (likely a product-review SKU list block) before send.
- Migrate the Dotomi ad-tracking pixels/redirects (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com` pub1–pub10) to HTTPS to prevent silent drops in mixed-content-blocking clients.
- Add `alt` text to the 10 flagged content images for accessibility and to preserve context when images are blocked.
- Re-run header capture through a path that preserves `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` to get a real compliance/deliverability read — current absence is inconclusive, not confirmed non-compliance.
- Re-run link resolution on a sample of the 70 skipped tracking links to confirm final landing-page URLs carry correct UTM parameters and match live site pages.
