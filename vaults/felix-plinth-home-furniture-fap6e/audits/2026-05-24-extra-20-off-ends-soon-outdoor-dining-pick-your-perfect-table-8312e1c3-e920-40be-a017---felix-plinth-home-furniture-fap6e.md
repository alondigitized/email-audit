# Extra 20% off ENDS SOON + Outdoor dining: Pick your perfect table
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-six. That's the count on Pottery Barn since Sunday, and we are *still* on Memorial Day. Yesterday it was bedding. The day before that it was a "FINAL WEEKEND" furniture push that I actually gave a 9. Today — Sunday afternoon — they've pivoted again to outdoor dining tables. Same red banner, same "Extra 20% off ENDS SOON" hammer, just a new product silo bolted on top. The hero is a long teak-ish dining setup under "Dining Alfresco" and honestly, that's relevant to me — I'm landscaping the back patio at the second house and an outdoor table is genuinely on the list.
- But the email itself is a tiny, narrow column of stacked product shots. On my phone everything renders postage-stamp size, the "Pick your perfect table" promise gets buried under a generic "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" rail and an Affirm block. There's no shipping clarity, no swatch/material callout for outdoor wood, no rewards math. Wayfair's $99 rug email beat this on relevance because it actually showed me product I could shop; this is more "everything is 20% off, here are some tables, also a lamp, also a sofa, also Affirm financing."
- Would I click? Maybe — the dining table is genuinely on my list. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read after a half-scroll? That's the more likely outcome.

## What's working

- The "Dining Alfresco" angle is finally a seasonal hook that fits Memorial Day weekend — patio, table, holiday cookout. That's a coherent beat, unlike yesterday's bedding pivot.
- The hero image actually shows a styled outdoor setting — table, chairs, plates — instead of a flat product silo. That gives me something to imagine.
- "Extra 20% off ENDS SOON" with a SHOP SALE button in the red banner is unambiguous. I know the offer in one glance.
- Affirm financing block is visible — useful when you're stacking a $2K+ teak table on top of a renovation budget.

## What's weak

- Twenty-sixth email since Sunday. I am genuinely fatigued. The "ENDS SOON" hammer has been swung so many times this week it's gone numb — CB2 manages four sends in six days and I notice every one; Pottery Barn does six in a weekend and I tune them out.
- "Pick your perfect table" is the promise, but the email shows me *one* table layout, then jumps to "Recommended Just For You" with a lamp, a sofa, a chair. Where's the table comparison? Article would have built a proper "round vs rectangle vs extendable" picker; this is a homepage link with hero dressing.
- No shipping clarity for outdoor furniture. A teak dining table is a lead-time question — when does it ship, white-glove or curbside? Wayfair's last email at least surfaced "under $99" with delivery context.
- No rewards-card or PB Key Rewards callout. I'm a known list-member; treat me like one.
- The "Recommended Just For You" rail is unrelated to outdoor dining. If you're going to promise "Pick your perfect table," follow through — don't show me an arc lamp.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make "Pick your perfect table" literal.** Three-up comparison: Round / Rectangle / Extendable, with seats-N callouts, material (teak / acacia / aluminum), and weather-resistance notes. That is the email this subject line promised.
- 2. **Add shipping/lead-time on every table tile.** "Ships in 2 weeks · White-glove delivery included." Outdoor furniture buyers stall on lead time, not price.
- 3. **Cut the unrelated recommendation rail or theme it to outdoor.** Replace lamp/sofa with umbrellas, planters, lanterns, outdoor cushions.
- 4. **Throttle the cadence.** This is send #26 since Sunday — even loyal subscribers tap out. CB2 paces every 2 days and I open every one.
- 5. **Add a Pottery Barn Key Rewards line.** "Members earn 10% back in rewards" turns a 20%-off email into an effective 30%.

# Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Extra 20% off ENDS SOON"

## 1. Technical Summary
HTML is a legacy table-based template with several real defects: stray PHP `header()` calls leaked into a `<style>` block, two broken asset/link URLs (403 + non-resolving spacer), and ~50 images missing alt text. Authentication and List-Unsubscribe headers were not observed via the relay, so compliance posture cannot be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (HTTP 403)**: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember…` — credit-card "apply and buy" CTA returns 403. Likely auth/cookie-gated endpoint but should not 403 from a cold click.
- **Broken asset (HTTP 403)**: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — cross-brand West Elm spacer referenced from a Pottery Barn template; also served over HTTP.
- 23 click-redirect URLs on `click.e.potterybarn.com` / `clickcp.potterybarn.com` were not probed; manual spot-check recommended.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray PHP in `<style>`**: the head contains literal PHP `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0"); …` inside a CSS block. This is invalid CSS and looks like server-side template bleed. Most clients will ignore it, but Gmail/Outlook clip detection and some sanitizers may flag it.
- **Mixed-content / HTTP images** (3): `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Will be blocked or warned in HTTPS-only clients.
- **Missing alt text** on ~50 images, including all 15 hero slices (`S26_B8_Out_OutdoorDiningTables/slices_01–15.jpg`), Memorial Day hero (`S26_Top_MemorialDayFinalWeekend.jpg`), all 18 footer slices, and product tiles (`farmhouse-platform-bed-l.jpg`, `westin-heavenly-bed-mattress-1325h-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-l.jpg`, etc.). Decorative slices/spacers should at minimum be `alt=""`; content images need real alt.
- Duplicate/conflicting `@media` rules: `min-width:700px` sets `img {max-width:650px}` while `min-width:650px` sets `img {max-width:100%!important}` — the second rule wins on 650–699px, making the first dead code.
- Commented-out viewport metas left in source (minor noise).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`) visible in the captured source.
- Recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is leaking into tracking pixel querystrings in plaintext (`coreemail.html?email=…`, `svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=…`). Functional, but exposes the address to any intermediary that logs URLs.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header: not found** in the captured headers (relay may have stripped it — cannot confirm RFC 2369 compliance).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post: not found** — one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 cannot be verified; required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results header: not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown from this capture.
- From-domain `e.potterybarn.com` and visible postal/unsubscribe footer content was not in the truncated body shown; not verifiable here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Click-redirect URLs (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`) are opaque tokens; no UTM parameters visible on the redirector entry. Final landing-page UTMs cannot be verified without resolving the redirects.
- Product tile images point directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` (canonical PDP image CDN) — consistent with the brand domain.
- The 403 on `clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/...` (member CC apply-and-buy) breaks the email→site path for that CTA.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Remove the PHP `header(...)` block** from `<style>` — it's server-side code that escaped templating; investigate the render pipeline.
2. **Fix the 403 on the credit-card apply CTA** (`clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-…`); verify endpoint accepts unauthenticated GETs from email clicks.
3. **Replace the West Elm spacer** (`http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`) with an HTTPS Pottery Barn-hosted equivalent; it's both broken and cross-brand.
4. **Convert all `http://` image sources to `https://`** (3 instances) to avoid mixed-content blocking.
5. **Add alt text**: real descriptions for hero/product images; explicit `alt=""` for decorative slices and spacers so screen readers skip them.
6. **Confirm List-Unsubscribe + List-Unsubscribe-Post headers** are being sent at the MTA (likely stripped by relay capture, but verify — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk rules).
7. **Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment** on `e.potterybarn.com` outbound; capture wasn't able to see Authentication-Results.
8. **Dedupe the conflicting `@media (min-width:700px)` img rule** — currently overridden by the 650px rule.
