# STARTS NOW: Black Friday deals + free shipping!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel send I've logged since 11/17, and it's the Kids sender this time. It's the same "Black Friday starts now" message I got earlier today under the main C&B name ("Our biggest sale of the year starts now!"). I'm renovating two houses, and a nursery isn't on either punch list. So my gut says this is C&B in a different hat, and I'm not the target.
- The hero is clear enough. It says "Black Friday starts now! Up to 50% off + free shipping" with one red Shop Now button. The scroll after that is enormous. It's a grid of "up to 50% off" tiles for kids furniture, baby furniture, holiday decor, bedding and rugs, then stockings, an Oilo collab, travel gear, a credit card pitch, an app download and a design appointment. I'd mark it read and move on. I won't click, and I won't forward it. There's nothing here for the house I'm actually renovating.

## What's working

- The hero headline puts the offer first: "Black Friday starts now! Up to 50% off + free shipping." I got it in two seconds.
- "Ships Free" labels sit on several tiles. That matters for furniture, where shipping cost is my first question.
- The Design Desk block ("your design pro is waiting, and it's 100% free") fits how I'm renovating two homes. It's the only thing in the email that speaks to me.
- The tiles have a consistent grid and lots of photography. It feels like a catalog, which I like.

## What's weak

- Nearly every tile says "up to 50% off" or "up to 20% off" with no real price. I can't tell which items are actually discounted.
- The email is far too long. On my phone it's a scroll marathon, and the product tiles are tiny and hard to read.
- The sale section has too many competing CTAs: Shop the Sale, Shop 300+ Gifts, Shop Kids Stockings, Shop Adult Stockings, Learn More, Download Now, Book a Free Appointment. There's no single clear next step after the hero.
- The credit card block ("the card you love just got better") appears low in the email and has no rewards math. I buy on my highest-rewards card, so I wanted to see the points. It's one more thing in a 7th send in two days.
- Free shipping has fine print buried in the footer, and I can't see the terms at a glance.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero plus 4–6 hero products with real sale prices. Right now it reads as a dump of the whole catalog.
- 2. Segment by household. I'm not a baby shopper, and the Kids sender should only reach people who've bought or browsed kids and nursery.
- 3. Put the Design Desk and shipping terms higher. Furniture buyers need both before they commit.
- 4. Cap the frequency. Three C&B sends in one day makes the "biggest sale" claim feel empty. Pottery Barn did the same with its repeated "up to 70% off" banner.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "STARTS NOW: Black Friday deals + free shipping!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and a responsive breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and header checks that couldn't be completed. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links are unverified.** 79 tracking links were skipped because they go through click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and HTTP status are unknown. No broken links are confirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times. `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times, plus `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/6a657a07-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. That is about 13 tracking images, each with a per-recipient hash in the query string. They add requests and weight, and they leak the recipient hash to `rlcdn.com`, a third party.
- **Possibly stale asset:** `s7d5.scene7.com/…/10%5FKids/2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide/…SecondaryStory.gif` comes from an October (1002) campaign folder in a Nov 18 send. It may be intentional reuse. It is worth confirming that the asset isn't outdated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA check flagged about 40 `<img>` elements. About 21 are content images:
  - 14 `.jpg` files under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (for example `1742130f-…jpg`, `5af5c4f3-…jpg`).
  - 4 `.gif` files under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/`.
  - Several hero and spacer images on `s7d5.scene7.com` (for example `2026%5F1118%5FCBK%5FEBF1%5FStartsNow…` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`).
  - With images blocked, or with a screen reader, the message may be unreadable or announce raw filenames. The `.jpg` and `.gif` images are the likely carriers of the offer copy.
- **Tracking pixels with no `alt`:** the remaining ~19 flagged elements are 1×1 pixels. Add `alt=""` to them so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is declared on `*`, `html` and `body`.
- **HTML validity:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`; it should be `&amp;`.
  - Several `format-detection` meta tags are repeated or conflicting (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then separate `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`).
  - Both `http-equiv` and `meta charset` are declared.
- **Template residue:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the markup. It is harmless, but it shows an unfinished template and adds bytes.
- **Title:** the `<title>` is generic and not tied to the campaign.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source or the QA output. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters resolved to real values. No issues found in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing** (QA WARN). The QA note says it "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so this is **unconfirmed**. It could be stripped by the relay rather than absent from the sender.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` missing** (RFC 8058 one-click) (QA WARN). Same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so it is worth checking against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the QA note attributes this to the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the sender's own domain, which is a normal setup.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they cannot be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 79 click links were skipped as tracking redirects, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the available source. No issues are confirmed, and nothing was fabricated.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to all ~21 content images, especially the `.jpg` and `.gif` files that carry offer messaging. Use `alt=""` on spacers and the ~19 tracking pixels.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. **Check the original message headers** (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Confirm that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
4. **Escape `&` in `<title>`**, remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and delete the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT…` comment.
5. **Reduce pixel redundancy.** Six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` calls and five `rlcdn.com` calls are likely unnecessary.
6. **Re-run the audit** with full HTML and a resolved click-redirect probe (following the redirects to the final URLs). That would let the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and UTM continuity be checked.
7. **Confirm the October scene7 GIF** (`2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide`) is still current.
