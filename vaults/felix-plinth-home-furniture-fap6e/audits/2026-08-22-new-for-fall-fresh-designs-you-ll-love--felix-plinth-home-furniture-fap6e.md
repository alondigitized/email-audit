# NEW for fall! Fresh designs you'll love
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Yeah, no. This is Crate & Kids, third or fourth send from this exact sub-brand into my inbox this week, and this one's a straight-up catalog dump — beds, bedding, lighting, nursery furniture, rugs, storage, backpacks, a stroller. Nothing here is for a 36-year-old guy juggling two reno projects who occasionally needs a bunk bed for a guest room. This is nursery-and-toddler-parent content top to bottom, and I don't have a kid in that age bracket.
- The "500+ new fall arrivals starting at $29!" hero is at least a real number and a real price, I'll give it that — better than CB2's vague "Floor Sample Sale" teasers I've been getting. But then it just... keeps scrolling. Kids furniture, kids bedding, lighting, nursery furniture, organic cotton baby bedding, kids rugs, storage, backpacks, a Nuna stroller. It's an entire department store crammed into one email with zero attempt to figure out what stage of parenting (or non-parenting) I'm actually in.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Marking as read and moving on — this is worse than even the weakest Pottery Barn sale blast because at least those are stuff I might buy for the house.

## What's working

- The "$29" starting price in the hero is a concrete number, which beats most of what I've been getting from CB2 lately.
- Product photography is clean and on-brand — muted pinks, warm woods, that Crate & Barrel color consistency carries through even in the kids sub-brand.
- The "we love these for you" product grid at the bottom at least attempts personalization structure, even if the products themselves miss.
- Layout is broken into clear tappable sections with individual "Shop New X" buttons — no ambiguity about where each CTA goes.

## What's weak

- Every single hero image is nursery/toddler gear — cribs, kids beds, baby bath, backpacks, a stroller. I have no kid needing any of this.
- This is the third-plus Crate & Kids email this week per my own read history; the brand is just not adjusting cadence even though I've never engaged.
- The "designers help for FREE" and now this generic arrivals dump both landed with zero attempt to route me toward the Crate & Barrel main brand where my actual reno spend would go.
- Way too long — I counted something like a dozen separate product categories before getting to the loyalty card pitch at the bottom. That's Wayfair-catalog-dump energy, not a curated fall preview.
- The Crate Card / Reward Dollars pitch at the very bottom is buried under all that kids content — if I were a Crate & Barrel loyalist that's actually relevant news, and it's getting no visibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids sends entirely, or at minimum lead with a "no kids yet? here's Crate & Barrel's fall arrivals" redirect at the top — this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Cut the email by half. Twelve categories in one scroll is Wayfair-tier catalog dumping; pick 3-4 hero categories and link a "shop all" for the rest.
- 3. Move the Crate Card / Reward Dollars callout up near the top for engaged Crate & Barrel account holders — that's actually a relevant hook for someone like me mid-reno on two houses.
- 4. If Crate & Kids insists on emailing non-parents, at least cross-sell furniture/storage pieces that work in a guest room or home office, not backpacks and strollers.
