# Starts now! Up to 20% off accent chairs, tables & rugs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Crate & Barrel send I've logged in two days, and my first reaction was "again?" It does have an actual sale in it: up to 20% off accent chairs, tables and rugs. I'm furnishing two houses, so that's my category. Then the email kept going. After the rug hero come a Design Desk sweepstakes, a Thanksgiving dining event, a Affirm "pay later" block, a product grid, a rewards card pitch, an app download and a second Design Desk ad. It reads like four emails stacked on top of each other.
- The offer is also weaker than the subject suggests. Yesterday's "ENDS TODAY: Up to 50% off furniture!" set the bar, and 20% isn't a reason to act. The hero says "20% off lighting, today only" while the subject says chairs, tables and rugs, so the two don't match. I'd save it to my Pinterest folder for the rug and the burgundy chair. I wouldn't forward it. I'd click once, on the rug or the chair.

## What's working

- The burgundy accent chair and the rug tile are good, specific products. They fit the renovation I'm planning.
- "Up to 20% off" in the subject is a concrete number, and "Starts now" is a clear time frame.
- The "Shop the Sale" button under the first headline is easy to find.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows real product names with struck-through prices. That's useful for comparison shopping.
- The Design Desk sweepstakes ($5,000 gift card) is a decent hook for someone doing two renovations.

## What's weak

- The top banner says "20% off lighting, today only", but the subject and hero say accent chairs, tables and rugs. I can't tell what the offer actually is.
- There are far too many modules: lighting, quick-ship furniture, Design Desk, Thanksgiving dining, Affirm, rewards card, app, Design Desk again. The sale gets buried.
- The Design Desk pitch appears twice in this one email. It was also the whole point of the 10/21 sends "Let's nail your holiday aesthetic →" and "Your most beautiful holidays yet start with The Design Desk →".
- The Thanksgiving dining module is "up to 30% off" and the main hero is 20%. Two different discount levels in one email is confusing.
- The Affirm "pay later" and rewards card blocks feel like filler. They don't help me compare furniture or math out rewards on my card.
- The fine print is tiny and dense at the bottom, and the promo exclusions are hard to read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it one offer. Pick the 20% off chairs, tables and rugs and cut the lighting banner, the sweepstakes and the Affirm block. Send the Design Desk and dining content separately.
- 2. Make the banner, the hero and the subject say the same thing.
- 3. Show the sale price on the hero rug and chair, and add the sale end date.
- 4. Cap it at one cadence per day. Six sends in two days is why I'm skimming.
- 5. Add swatch or sample links and a shipping window for furniture. I'm choosing a color palette and need to know when it arrives.
- 6. Show how rewards points apply to this sale, since I buy with my highest-rewards card.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with `lang="en"` and mobile media queries. The QA data flags about 32 images without alt text, header checks that couldn't be completed, and 67 tracked links that were never probed. The HTML supplied is truncated, so the body, footer and link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains, so none was probed. Destinations, redirect chains and broken links are unconfirmed.
- **Duplicate third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fires five times in one message. The same `s` hash is reused, and only `n` changes.
- **Recipient identifier in asset URLs:** `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…` is appended to the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs and the open pixel `o.gif`. A per-recipient token in image URLs works as intended for dynamic content, but it makes those images uncacheable and identifies the recipient to any image proxy.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/adce5a49-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are present. Clients that block images will not register opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 32 `<img>` elements are flagged. They fall into these groups:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`, including `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `1aadeb21-…jpg` and the animated `cd56cdc7-…gif` and `74e404dc-…gif`. Image-blocking clients show no text for these.
  - Dynamic product images `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images, `560d850e7e517ede.png` through `18f126218cc222ec.png`). These are likely product tiles, so missing alt is most harmful here.
  - Tracking and pixel images (`rlcdn.com/448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`). These need `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on iOS and some Android clients.
- **Dead CSS rule:** two `img` max-width rules sit in overlapping media queries. `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` comes before `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, and equal specificity means the later rule wins, so the 600px rule never applies.
- **Template residue:** the `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` placeholder comment (misspelled "IMPUT") is still in the head. It is harmless but adds bytes.
- **Duplicated head tags:**
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` appears three times with overlapping values: the combined `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` first, then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has a bare `&` under an XHTML doctype. This is a validation error and unlikely to break rendering.
- **Mixed approaches:** the XHTML doctype is combined with HTML5 `<meta charset>`. Global rules `p{margin:0 auto;text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` can override unstyled content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u` hash is populated. Only the truncated head was inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The envelope uses `mail.crateandbarrel.com` with a sender of `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is aligned by domain, but this is not a verification.
- The footer unsubscribe link and postal address were not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 67 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated HTML shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. Nothing here confirms or refutes campaign tagging, or that the landing page matches the "up to 20% off accent chairs, tables & rugs" subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `lib/.../m/1/*` content images and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` product tiles. Set `alt=""` on the pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls into one, unless the `n` values are intentional.
4. Delete the dead `max-width:600px` media query, consolidate the `format-detection` and charset tags, remove the placeholder comment, and escape the title as `&amp;`.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve a sample of the 67 tracked links through their redirects to confirm HTTP 200 on the final URL and UTM or campaign parameter presence.
