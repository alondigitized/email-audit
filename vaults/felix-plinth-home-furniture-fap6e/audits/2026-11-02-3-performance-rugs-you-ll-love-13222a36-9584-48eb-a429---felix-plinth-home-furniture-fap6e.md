# 3 performance rugs you’ll love!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Crate & Barrel Kids email in the same two days, and this is the first one I didn't write off right away. It's rugs. I'm renovating two houses, so rugs are relevant, and the hero is a textured rust rug with "kid-proof performance rugs" and a "starting at just $249" line. The problem is the Kids label. A "geo dotty ivory performance area rug" and a "daisy field kids performance area rug" look like nursery rugs. They'd also work in a den or a guest room, but nothing in the email says so. The rug images are big and nice, though, and I'd never go through a plain grid like this.
- The rest of the email is the usual Kids stuff. Halloween treat bags, a Nuna stroller, a glider and a Babyletto changing table are all below the rugs. I'm not buying any of that. The Design Desk block and the new Crate Card pitch are the parts I'd actually save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. That's a lot of modules for a send that promised three rugs.
- I'd click through on the ivory rug and look at the size options, but only if I'd forgotten about the other three Kids sends this week. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read, and maybe star it for the Design Desk piece.

## What's working

- The three rugs each get a full-width photo with a short name and one "Shop Now" button, so the layout is easy to follow.
- The hero says "starting at just $249", which gives me an actual price to react to.
- Calling them "performance rugs" and mentioning they "hold up to anything" fits how I shop. I want durability and washability, and I don't want to baby a rug.
- The Design Desk block says "100% free" and "no purchase required", and it mentions floorplans and 3D previews. That's useful for someone doing two renovations.
- The ivory and wheat-colored rugs match each other. The palette is calm and I could see them in a real room.

## What's weak

- I'm not in the Kids audience. Rug names with "kids" in them and the baby bedding tile, the Nuna stroller, the Babyletto changing table and the Halloween bag tell me this is a parent's email, not mine.
- It's too long. The three rugs end around the midpoint, then there are seven more modules, including an app download and a legal block.
- I can't see rug sizes, price ranges or material details. Only the hero has a price. The three rug tiles have no price.
- The Design Desk block appears twice. A second "Book a Free Appointment" button near the bottom is overkill.
- The "up to 60% off" clearance tile and the Crate Card pitch have nothing to do with rugs. They dilute the one message the email had.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the rugs, or at least keep it short. A send called "3 performance rugs" should be three rugs plus one Design Desk plug.
- 2. Put a price and a size range under each rug name, and say whether the rug is washable or stain-resistant.
- 3. Show a rug in a regular living room or hallway, not only in a kids' room, so people like me can picture it.
- 4. Segment. If I've bought furniture or browsed kitchens, send me the main Crate & Barrel rugs, not Kids. I've had three Kids sends from you since Monday ("EVERYTHING inside ships free →" and "100s of things that ship for FREE!" among them) and none of them were for me. CB2's "The past is present" and Article's "New arrivals: Seat selection" were both furniture-focused and clearly aimed at my kind of project. Pottery Barn's pillow email at least kept to one theme the whole way down.
- 5. Fix the cadence. Four sends in two days is too much.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs that survive pets, kids and red wine`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 washable area rugs, from $249`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stain-resistant, machine-washable, and in sizes up to 9x12.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: free design help for your next room.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's main technical problems are about 30 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute and no verifiable unsubscribe or authentication headers. Link and UTM validation was not possible because all 70 links go through tracking redirects and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links.** The QA run skipped HTTP probing for 70 links because they use tracking and click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` calls (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp) load alongside the first-party opens pixels `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/9be90604-…?mi_cid=…`. Each of these carries a long hash in the query string, and the same `dbe8da71…` prefix appears in `mi_u` and `s=`. That suggests a recipient-level identifier is shared with a third party. This is worth checking against the privacy policy, but the source doesn't show what the value is.
- **Repeated pixel.** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` from `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` appears six times. It is probably a per-module tracking image, and the repetition adds requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text.** The QA check flagged about 30 images with no `alt` attribute. This includes content images such as:
  - `rugs%5Fhero%5Fremix%5F640%5FMI%5F02.gif` (the hero)
  - `042025%5FCBK%5FPerformanceRugs1%5FPrimary%5FCopy`
  - `042025%5FCBK%5FPerformanceRugs1%5FSection%5F1%5FGeo`
  - the product images `4611c63f-….jpg`, `ed026fce-….jpg`, `ae8b8e5a-….jpg`, `3a02d248-….jpg`, `a1b45221-….jpg`, `ef6e5d28-….jpg`, `a54414f1-….jpg`, `971aa10f-….jpg`
  
  Several of these look like text-in-image, such as `…Primary_Copy` and `…Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_Tert`. With images blocked, screen readers and image-off clients get nothing, or the filename.
- **Spacers and pixels.** `…Rule_Spacer`, `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`, `o.gif`, `448796.gif` and the `dv.` pixel should have `alt=""` rather than no attribute.
- **Viewport.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Conflicting `img` rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. They have equal specificity, so the later rule wins at 768px and above and the 600px cap never applies.
- **`<title>` entity.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML 1.0 Transitional (should be `&amp;`).
- **Leftover boilerplate.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the template. The `<head>` also repeats `format-detection` meta tags: one combined, then separate `date`, `address` and `telephone`.
- **Asset naming.** Several assets are named with `042025_CBK_PerformanceRugs1` and `042025_CBK_KidsBeds1`, which is an April 2025 campaign. Another is named `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_Tert`. This suggests reused or stale modules, so confirm that the imagery and linked products match this send.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The tokens beyond the truncation point could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it. It is unconfirmed rather than proven missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (QA WARN). Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe isn't supported or visible. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the brand, but alignment can't be proven without the headers.
- **Footer.** The visible unsubscribe link and the physical address were not in the truncated portion, so they couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links are wrapped in tracking redirects and none were resolved, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs are unknown. The creative names also don't line up: the hero and copy are tagged April 2025 "Performance Rugs", while the tertiary module is a November 2026 "Fall Clearance" asset. This is the main reason to check landing alignment manually.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, especially the hero and text-baked images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relay copy, and re-run the compliance checks.
3. Resolve the tracking-link redirects, either through a follow-redirect probe or a seed-list click test. Record the final URLs, UTM parameters and status codes for all 70 links.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
5. Reorder or merge the two `img { max-width }` media queries so the intended cap applies.
6. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, remove the placeholder comment, and consolidate the `format-detection` metas.
7. Review what the `rlcdn.com` pixels and the `mi_u` / `s=` parameters transmit, and whether that matches the stated privacy disclosures.
8. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check merge tokens and the footer.
