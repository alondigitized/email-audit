# Get ready for Week 1 with the NFL Game Day Collection!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, an NFL-branded Crate & Barrel email — that's new territory for them, I'll give it that. Beer glasses etched with team logos, monogrammed candles, a kids' recliner in a team color, and a "cook like a pro" tailgate spread. My gut reaction is actually mild interest, because this is the first Crate & Barrel send all week that isn't Crate & Kids back-to-school or a generic dining room refresh — it's at least trying to be seasonal and topical.
- But it falls apart on relevance fast: I don't know if any of this is Packers, Chiefs, or Raiders gear until I squint, and I'm renovating two houses with a specific palette in mind, not building a man-cave shrine. The beer glasses are mildly funny, the robe is a swing and a miss, and there's zero indication of price, sizing, or which teams are even available without scrolling through six more product shots.
- Would I click? Maybe on the beer glasses out of curiosity, more to see if my team's even offered than to buy. Forward to a friend? Only ironically, to the buddy who has an actual dedicated football room. Mostly I'm scrolling past this one — it's cute, not a priority buy.

## What's working

- The lead visual — two logo beer glasses being clinked — is a strong, specific hero that actually tells me what's inside before I scroll.
- "Something for every fan" segments the collection into recognizable categories (entertaining, beer, kitchen, candles, kids), which at least organizes the chaos.
- The monogrammed candle set and the kids' team recliner are legitimately clever tie-ins I haven't seen from Pottery Barn or Wayfair in this category.
- No back-to-school/summer-sale banner collision this time — a relief after this week's run of Crate & Kids clutter.

## What's weak

- I can't tell which NFL teams are actually available from the hero images alone — I see Packers and what might be Chiefs/Raiders logos buried mid-scroll, but there's no team-selector or "shop your team" CTA up top.
- Zero price visible anywhere until the bottom "we love these for you" strip, and even those are generic dinnerware, not NFL product — so the pricing signal doesn't even match the hero offer.
- No discount, no urgency, nothing tying this to "Week 1" beyond the subject line — compare to CB2's "Now & Future Classics" send, which at least had a real sale mechanic; this is pure browse-mode.
- The robe/candle/recliner section drifts so far from "game day" that by the time I hit "little fans, big spirit" I've forgotten this was supposed to be about football.
- This is buried after an unrelated dining-room CTA logic-wise — it reads like six micro-campaigns stapled together, not one cohesive NFL push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a team-shop mechanism up top — logos or a dropdown for "Shop Packers / Chiefs / Raiders / etc." right under the hero, so I'm not hunting for my team six screens deep.
- 2. Add a real hook — % off, bundle deal, or at minimum ship-by-kickoff messaging — "Week 1" in the subject implies urgency the email never delivers on.
- 3. Cut the robe and candle sections, or move them to a separate "cozy season" send — they dilute the NFL focus and feel like inventory padding, not curation.
- 4. Show price on at least the beer glasses and jersey aprons — Pottery Barn's Design Crew email at least anchored value with a concrete number; this email never does.
- 5. Tighten the whole thing to 4-5 sections max — right now it's a dozen stacked modules, which is exhausting on mobile scroll.
