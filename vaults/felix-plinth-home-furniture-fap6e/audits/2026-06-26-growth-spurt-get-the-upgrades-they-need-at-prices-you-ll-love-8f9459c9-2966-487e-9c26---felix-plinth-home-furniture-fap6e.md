## What's weak

- "Growth spurt?" in the subject tells me immediately I'm in the wrong audience — I don't have a kid hitting a bed-size transition
- Three Crate & Barrel family sends on one day is cadence malpractice — I already burned my attention on the wine glasses and the "forever faves edit" before this hit

- 2. **Anchor it to the 4th of July sale.** Every brand in my inbox this week is doing it. Article stamped a date. Pottery Barn stamped a date. This email has the offer but not the deadline, which kills urgency.
- 3. **Cut the number of categories.** CB2's floor sample email worked because it went one concept deep. Four simultaneous categories reads like a flyer, not a curated edit.
- 4. **Cap the daily send rate.** Don't hit me three times in a day from sub-brands of the same parent. One of those sends absorbs the other two.
