# Two sales end tonight
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Two sales end tonight" — and this is CB2's move now, apparently, because the second I open it I'm staring at two nearly identical hero blocks stacked on top of each other, both screaming "ENDS TONIGHT," both dark moody bedroom-adjacent photography, both a white "SHOP THE SALE" button. I actually had to scroll back up to check if my email client had duplicated the render, because that's how repetitive the top of this thing is.
- This is the seventh CB2 send I've logged this week — after "BED & BATH ON SALE" yesterday, the Goop color studies, the Cubs sweepstakes, and the David Girelli spotlight — and the fatigue is real. That round dining table in the second hero is genuinely nice (I've got a spot in the new-house dining room that's been empty for a month), but I've now seen a version of "up to 40% off furniture and decor" from this brand so many times this week that my brain has started auto-categorizing it as noise before I even read the percentage.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the table specifically, but not from urgency — just because I happened to actually need one. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on, same as basically every CB2 email since Monday.

## What's working

- The "MORE FOR YOU" product grid at the bottom is the strongest part of this email — six real SKUs with real prices, strikethrough pricing on three of them (Andes Acacia bed $849, Archer nightstand $349, Welling chair $349), so at least there's honest discount math visible somewhere.
- That burl-wood dining table hero shot is genuinely well-styled — warm tone, brass legs, looks like something Article or Pottery Barn would be proud of, and it's the one image in this email that made me pause.
- The "EARN 10% BACK in Rewards Dollars... at CB2 and Crate & Barrel" module is relevant to me specifically since I've got the CB2 card — that's the one piece of this email that felt like it knew who I was.
- Clean footer brand lineup (CB2, Crate&Barrel, Crate&kids, H&G) at least shows me the family relationship instead of pretending they're unconnected, which is more honest than some of their other sends this week.

## What's weak

- Two "ENDS TONIGHT" hero banners back to back is a real hierarchy failure — I genuinely couldn't tell in the first two seconds if this was one sale or two, and the eye has nowhere confident to land.
- Zero mention of a specific discount code, minimum spend, or exclusions above the fold — the actual "up to 40% off" only shows once, buried in hero two, with a tiny superscript disclaimer I can't read at this size.
- The "LEAVE US A REVIEW, WIN $1,000" banner sandwiched between the sales and the product grid feels like a random interruption — it's not tied to anything I just looked at and breaks the flow.
- After yesterday's "BED & BATH ON SALE" and this being the same bedroom/bath framing again, the cadence is genuinely worn out — Wayfair's "Fearless design, bold layers" send at least tried a styling narrative instead of stacking two sale banners.
- No color-palette or room-styling story anywhere, which is what actually gets me to click mid-reno — Pottery Barn's Heritage Autumn / Indigo Blues / Rustic Lodge lookbook does this and it's the reason that one scored higher with me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill one of the two redundant hero banners — pick either the bedroom event or the 40%-off sitewide push, not both stacked. Two competing "ENDS TONIGHT" CTAs in the first scroll is losing me before I even get to the good product.
- 2. Move the burl dining table (or a single hero SKU) up top with a real price and the discount stamped directly on the image — that's the product that actually earned my attention, don't bury it as generic "40% off furniture" copy.
- 3. Cut the review-sweepstakes banner or move it to the very bottom near the footer — it interrupts the one part of the email (product grid) that's actually working.
- 4. Given this is CB2's sixth-plus send to me this week, start segmenting — I've already looked at a dining table category, lean into that instead of another generic bedroom+bath blast.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The burl wood table you'd actually keep — plus 10% back in Rewards Dollars.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bedroom + bath sale wraps tonight. New arrivals just dropped below.`
