# Our fave new nursery finds →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the twelfth Crate & Barrel family send in about three days, and it's the Kids arm again. The last one was "New collection alert: Oilo Studio is here!" That makes it the fourth Kids email in that stretch. The screenshot was shrunk a lot, so I can read the section headlines and layout but not the fine print.
- The email itself is well built. It's a clean catalog walk through nursery furniture: a glider, cribs, dressers, then a product grid with prices. That structure is how I like to shop, and I save emails like this to my inspiration folder. The problem is that I'm renovating two houses, and a nursery isn't on either list. The Double Rewards strip at the top, the only thing here that matters to my card math, is crammed into a thin banner above the logo. This is a mark-as-read for me. I'd click the rewards strip, but only because it's rewards.

## What's working

- The "first look / new / new / new" headline pattern makes the scroll easy to follow, and each section ends in its own button.
- The product photography is warm and styled. The glider and dresser rooms look like a catalog I'd save to Pinterest.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices under the products. I can judge value without clicking.
- The Double Rewards strip at the top is the kind of loyalty detail I care about, because everything I buy goes on the highest-rewards card.
- The Oilo block gives the Kids brand a premium feel, with a person and a lifestyle shot instead of just furniture.

## What's weak

- It's very long. There are about eight stacked modules, so the one message I'd care about gets lost.
- The "Fall Sale: up to 50% off" banner sits in the middle, below the first few sections. The first screen sells "new arrivals" instead of a deal.
- The Double Rewards and sale strips at the top are tiny and sit above the logo. They're easy to miss.
- There are too many CTAs: Shop All Nursery Furniture, Shop Nursery Seating, Shop Cribs, Shop Dressers, Shop All Baby Furniture, Shop All Gifts, Shop Now, Shop Now. The two "Shop Now" buttons tell me nothing.
- The footer is a wall of dense legal text, and a card promo and an app-sweepstakes promo are stacked right before it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer to the top. Lead with the sale strip or one hero, not three promos stacked in the header.
- 2. Cut it to three modules: seating, cribs, dressers. Drop the gifts block, the card promo and the app giveaway, or send them separately.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific verbs and nouns, such as "Shop Cribs up to 50% off."
- 4. Make the Double Rewards deadline legible in the body, not just in a thin banner.
- 5. Give me a reason to open that isn't a repeat. I've had twelve of these in three days, so cadence is the real problem.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Double Rewards on everything through the weekend.`

- Compared with the other senders in my inbox:
- Pottery Barn's "Selling fast: Open Box Outlet Deals" had one hook. This one has eight.

## Technical Audit

The 57% QA pass rate comes from missing alt text on about 40 images and three header warnings. The warnings are likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so link targets, footer content and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 79 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels with identifiers:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1..5` pixels (LiveRamp) carry what appears to be a per-recipient hashed identifier in `s=`. The same value appears in the `mi_u=` parameter on the first-party pixels `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances) and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/775e4367-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another open or tracking beacon. Sending a recipient-derived identifier to a third-party ad-data domain is worth reviewing for privacy disclosure. It isn't a rendering defect.
- **Redundant pixels:** `rlcdn.com` is requested five times with only `n` varying, and `f3c0d22992a01946.png` six times. This adds requests without apparent benefit.
