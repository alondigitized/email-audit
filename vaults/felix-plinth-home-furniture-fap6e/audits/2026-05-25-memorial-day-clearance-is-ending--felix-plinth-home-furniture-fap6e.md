## What's weak

- Eighteen emails in a week, and this one is functionally identical to the last four — same tile grid, same emoji-shouting subject, zero new information.
- "Picked just for you" is a lavender-bedroom + pink-vanity stack that has nothing to do with a 36-year-old guy renovating a second home. Pottery Barn's algo at least pretends to know my category; this is generic female-skewed inventory.
