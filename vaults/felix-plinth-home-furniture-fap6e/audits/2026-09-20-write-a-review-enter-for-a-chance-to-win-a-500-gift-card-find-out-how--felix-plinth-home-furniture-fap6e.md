# Write a review. Enter for a chance to win a $500 Gift Card. Find out how →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — I don't have kids, and this is a "rate and review for a chance to win $500" email, which I already scored a 5 on when it showed up as "We're giving away a $500 Gift Card!" — is this the same contest re-sent under a different subject line, or did I get two separate sweepstakes emails from the same brand in one week? Either way, my brain is already filing this under "nursery spam" before I even scroll.
- The Venus Williams tennis collab is actually kind of cute and at least reads like real creative work, not just another crib photo, but it's buried under a sweepstakes hero, a baby gear sale, a rewards card pitch, a design consultant pitch, and an app download push — this email is trying to be six emails at once. Compare that to the Crate & Barrel "Beautiful & bestselling for every room" send from 9/19 that scored an 8 with me — clean, one category, no kitchen-sink approach.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and moving on. If Crate & Barrel wants my attention they need to stop CC'ing me on the Crate & Kids list — I'm 36, no kids, renovating two houses, and none of this baby gear is relevant to a single line item on my Pottery Barn or Wayfair lists.

## What's working

- The Venus Williams x Crate & Kids collab shot is genuinely well-produced and feels like a real campaign, not filler.
- The two-star rating icon next to "want to win a $500 gift card?" is a nice small touch that signals this is review-driven, not just spam-a-sweepstakes.
- The upgraded Crate Card / Reward Dollars module is at least relevant to me since I already shop this family of brands with a rewards card.
- "TheDesignDesk" free design consultation call-out is the one thing here that could matter to someone mid-renovation like me, if it weren't stapled onto a kids' newsletter.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — this is the wrong list for me, full stop, and it's the same complaint I had about the "$500 Gift Card" send from the same day.
- Six different offers crammed into one email (sweepstakes, tennis collab, baby sale, product grid, credit card, app download, design service) with no clear hierarchy — nothing says "click here first."
- The "up to 20% off baby essentials" sale banner and the Nuna stroller/crib grid are 100% irrelevant to my renovation, my two houses, or anything on my Pottery Barn/Wayfair lists.
- The hero CTA buttons ("Write a Review," "Shop Bestsellers") are low-contrast gray-on-gray — easy to miss scrolling on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely and route me to Crate & Barrel proper or CB2 — I've now gotten multiple kid-focused sends this month and it's actively making me tune out the brand family.
- 2. Pick one offer per email. If it's the sweepstakes, lead with it and cut the sale banner, product grid, credit card pitch, and app download — that's an email for design pros, not a shopper mid-review-entry.
- 3. Fix the CTA contrast — dark charcoal or the brand green (like the collab banner) instead of washed-out gray buttons.
- 4. If TheDesignDesk service is worth promoting, give it its own send targeted at people actively renovating — that's an actual match for me, wasted here.
