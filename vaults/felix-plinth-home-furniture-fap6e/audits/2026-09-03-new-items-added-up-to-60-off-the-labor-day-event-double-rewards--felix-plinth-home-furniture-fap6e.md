# New items added! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event + Double Rewards→
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Kids email, and my inbox log doesn't lie — this is at least the fourth send from this family today, on top of yesterday's "registry faves" nursery roundup I already dinged them for. I don't have a nursery, I don't have a kid, and this whole email is nursery furniture, baby bedding, and convertible cribs top to bottom. It's not for me.
- That said, credit where due: the hero is clean — "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" with a "Double Rewards" callout, one clear "Shop the Sale" button, no render bugs. But then it just… keeps going. Category grid, "we love these for you" product carousel (all cribs and infant car seats), a $500 gift card sweepstakes for "TheDesignDesk," a Crate Card pitch, an app download push. It's five separate offers crammed into one scroll, and none of them are furniture I'd actually buy for my renovation.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — I'd swipe to archive. If I wanted rugs or storage furniture (which I do, for the second home), I'd go straight to Crate & Barrel proper, not wade through baby gear to find it.

## What's working

- Hero banner is legible and the offer is unmissable — "Up to 60% off" plus "Double Rewards" stacked cleanly at the top, no scrolling required.
- One unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop the Sale") sits right under the hero, red button, high contrast.
- No visual bugs — every product tile loaded, pricing is visible, nothing overlapping.
- The "study & storage furniture" and "rugs" tiles are the only two things in this whole email remotely relevant to my actual reno, and they're at least present in the category grid.

## What's weak

- This is a baby/kids brand email and I have zero kids — cribs, infant car seats, nursery chairs, baby bedding dominate the whole layout. Wrong list for me entirely.
- "we love these for you" personalization block recommends a Jenny Lind dresser and a Babyletto crib — that's not "for me," that's just the same catalog reshuffled.
- Way too many competing CTAs by the bottom third: sweepstakes entry, "Double Rewards" card sign-up, Crate Card upsell, app download — I've lost track of what I'm supposed to actually do.
- Fourth-plus Crate & Kids email today per my own log (following the "registry faves" nursery send yesterday) — this brand is in full inbox-fatigue territory, same as CB2 sending nineteen emails this month.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of kids/baby sends entirely — if Crate & Barrel has my purchase history (I'm buying rugs, storage, and lighting, not cribs), route me to the parent brand's Labor Day email instead of the Crate & Kids one. That's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. Cut this email down to one offer. Article's Labor Day send nailed this with "Up to 40% off" front and center and nothing else competing for attention — this one buries that discipline under five stacked asks.
- 3. Drop the "we love these for you" block if it's not actually personalized — showing me a crib is worse than showing nothing, it tells me the segmentation isn't real.
- 4. Move the study/storage/rugs tiles higher in the grid for recipients without kids' items in their purchase history — right now they're stuck at position seven of eight.
