# Our top 100 gifts 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this is a gift guide, and I'm renovating two houses, not shopping for gifts. It looks like Pottery Barn: moody neutral photography, cozy throws, a scent set. But I'd wallpapered over this inbox with PB all week. Yesterday it was the Halloween Shop, the free design help and the barstools. Now there's a Christmas Shop tile at the bottom, so I'm already getting the next holiday before I've cleared the last one.
- The best thing in here is buried. "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" is the line that matters for a renovation, and it's a thin strip above the hero. The Recommended Just For You block (curtain rod, Toscana dining table, Remmy swivel chair, Banks table) is the stuff I'd actually click. It sits below nine gift tiles I'll scroll past. The free design appointment near the bottom is the same offer that made yesterday's send my favorite. I'd mark this as read and maybe save the dining table to my inspiration folder.
- I wouldn't forward it. Nothing here is a deal. The "Ships Free" badges are on a few items, and the gift tiers ($30, $50, $100) are price buckets, not discounts.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and readable. "Our Most-Loved Gifts" with "Top 100 Gifts" and "All Gifts" buttons works in a second, with no broken images like Wayfair's empty-box hero on the Christmas tree send.
- The "Ships Free" tags on the throw, robe and cheese board are the one concrete perk in the gift section.
- The gifts under $30 / $50 / $100 tiles are a quick way to filter by budget.
- The Recommended Just For You block feels like it was pulled for me. The extendable dining tables, with the 60"-125" and 76"-128" ranges printed, are the sizing detail I actually look for.
- The free design services banner is a trust cue and fits my two-house situation. It echoes the 10/20 "FREE design help" send.

## What's weak

- The gift focus misses me. I'm buying lamps, tables and barstools, not eyelash chenille throws.
- There's no actual deal. Compare CB2's "WAREHOUSE SALE: UP TO 60% OFF" or Article's free-design-services email, where the offer was the headline. This one is browse-only.
- The cadence is exhausting. This is at least the fourth PB send since 10/19 (Harvest Decor, barstools, design help, Halloween Shop). Now there's a Christmas Shop tile, and the Halloween one hasn't even ended.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" strip is tiny and gets no follow-through. That's my number-one question on furniture, and it's not backed up with product.
- The footer is a wall of fine print, with the 10% rewards card, Affirm and the app all stacked in. Too much noise at the bottom, and the rewards math is vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with what I'm buying. For a furniture-in-progress segment, make the hero "In-Stock Furniture, Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" with a real room shot and a visible product row. Push gifts to the second fold. Crate & Barrel's SMEG send worked because it led with one hero product.
- 2. Give the gift guide a reason to exist today: a free-shipping threshold, a gift-by date, or a member price. Right now it's just a catalog.
- 3. Move the free design appointment up. It's the strongest offer for a renovator, and it's buried below the Christmas Shop tile.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Skip the Christmas Shop tile until the Halloween Shop is over, or send fewer emails.
- 5. Make the rewards card block simpler: one line stating "10% back on PB purchases" and a single link, with the legal text in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In-stock furniture, delivered in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free design help + our top 100 gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining tables, swivel chairs and curtains that ship in 1-4 weeks`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Book a free design appointment before the holiday rush`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout built from sliced images, with link wrapping through `click.e.potterybarn.com`. It has widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, and recipient email addresses in plain text in image and tracking-pixel URLs. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay did not pass the headers through.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links can't be verified.** 56 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are wrapped on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`. Destination, status codes and redirect chains are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over cleartext HTTP. Clients that block or upgrade mixed content can drop it, which undercounts opens.
- **Other open and tracking pixels:**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` is a third-party pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d6025af6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is an open pixel.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…` is a pixel.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain. If West Elm changes or removes it, the spacer breaks in this email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text is widespread.** There are about 55 `<img>` tags without `alt`:
  - Hero and product slices: `H26_Seasonal_B4_OFGiftIdeas/slices_01–14.jpg`.
  - Product images: `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, and others.
  - Banners and footer: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`, `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Because the content is image-sliced, a client that blocks images, or a screen reader, gets almost no content from the body. The only `<img>` confirmed to have alt text in the source is the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Tracking pixels without alt or dimensions.** The three pixels listed above have no alt text. They should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. The second path is dated 2022, so it looks like a stale reused module.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Stray server code in CSS.** The last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It sits after the final valid rule, so the impact is probably small. It is still invalid CSS and suggests a template-assembly error.
- **Possible dead mobile rules.** The responsive rules `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when `class` is exactly that value. The visible tables use `class="contenttable"`. The truncated source doesn't show whether any element uses `wrapper` or `photo`, so these rules may be dead.
- **Table roles.** Layout tables carry `role="presentation"` in places, but the outer wrapper and spacer tables don't. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens.** No `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` placeholders appear in the visible source.
- **Recipient email in plain text in URLs.** The address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is in the query strings of `coreemail.html?email=` and `svopn…&sv_sveme=`. It is unhashed. The `svopn` URL sends it over HTTP, so it is exposed to network observers and to any proxy or CDN logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From address `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is on a sending subdomain, so alignment should be checked on the raw message.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** both are likely in the footer, but the HTML was truncated before that point, so neither can be confirmed.
- **Plain-text part.** 10,651 of 14,857 characters (72%) are URLs, which is just over the 70% flag threshold. A heavily wrapped link structure is the likely cause. A URL-heavy text part can add some spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Direct product URLs.** The only first-party `potterybarn.com` URLs in the QA data are image and pixel URLs. They carry no campaign parameters apart from the `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` identifiers on the `clickcp` pixel.

7. Host the spacer GIF on `edm.potterybarn.com` instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
8. Capture raw headers outside the relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Fetch the untruncated footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
