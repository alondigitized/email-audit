# It’s here! Up to 60% off + Double Rewards→
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again. That's the fifth time this account has hit my inbox this week with nursery furniture and I don't have kids — I don't think I've ever clicked through on a single one of these. The hero at least states the offer plainly, "It's Here! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event," stacked with the same Double Rewards banner Crate & Barrel proper has been running non-stop, but every single grid tile under it — kids furniture, baby bedding, nursery furniture, changing tables, a "we love these for you" carousel of a diaper backpack and a baby swing — is stuff that has zero relevance to my life right now. I'm not opening this, I'm archiving it, and I'm definitely not forwarding it to a friend unless it's to complain about how Crate & Barrel's family of brands seems to have no idea I've only ever bought a dresser and a rug from them.
- The Design Desk free-consultation block halfway down is honestly the only thing in this email built for someone renovating two houses like me, and it's buried under six screens of onesie-adjacent inventory.

## What's working

- The top banner is unambiguous — "Up to 60% off," Double Rewards dates spelled out (Now–September 8, 2026), one red Shop the Sale button.
- The Design Desk section ("our Design Desk pros can help — it's 100% free," "Book a Free Appointment") is actually on-target for someone mid-renovation — that's the one block I'd stop and read.
- Clean grid layout, product photography is crisp, no broken images or overlapping text anywhere I scrolled.
- The clearance and "create a focus zone for every age" sections at least try to widen the net beyond nursery gear.

## What's weak

- This is Crate & Kids sending me a full catalog of kids furniture, baby bedding, nursery gliders, and a diaper backpack — I have no kids. It's the same misfire I flagged on both 2026-08-26 sends from this account.
- The "we love these for you" personalization carousel is baby gear top to bottom (Nuna stroller, Babyletto crib dresser, diaper backpack) — if that's supposed to feel personalized, it's aimed at the wrong person entirely.
- Way too long — I counted eight separate content blocks before I even hit the footer. Pottery Barn's "Cozy Throws Ship Free" send got in and out with one clear offer; this is a scroll marathon.
- The email is functionally identical to the Crate & Barrel parent-brand Labor Day sends I already logged today — same red banner, same Double Rewards math, just re-skinned for kids' furniture, which reinforces the sense that this whole family of brands is blasting the same campaign under five different sender names.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress me from Crate & Kids entirely** — I've never purchased baby or kids furniture from this brand; five sends in one week is a targeting failure, not a cadence choice. Route me to Crate & Barrel proper and CB2 only.
- 2. **Lead with Design Desk, not nursery gliders** — if Crate & Barrel's system genuinely can't tell I have no kids, at minimum put the free design-consultation CTA above the fold since that's the one universally relevant hook for a renovation household.
- 3. **Cut the email in half** — eight product blocks plus a second Double Rewards banner plus a clearance banner plus an app-download pitch is overkill for one send.
- 4. **Stop repeating Double Rewards twice in the same email** — it's in the header and again mid-scroll; once is enough.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Double Rewards on Crate & Barrel's Labor Day Event`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book a free room consult, or save up to 60% sitewide through September 8`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 2x Reward Dollars now through Labor Day`
