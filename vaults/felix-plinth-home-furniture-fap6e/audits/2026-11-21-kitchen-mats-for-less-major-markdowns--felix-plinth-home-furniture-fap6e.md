# KITCHEN MATS for less. *MAJOR* markdowns ➡
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and this is the twentieth-ish send since 11/09. It lands right behind yesterday's "POPCORN MACHINES / NUT ROASTERS ⚫ UP ⚫ TO ⚫ 80% ⚫ OFF" and "SHOWER FAUCETS & SYSTEMS 🟪 ⬛ 🟪". The subject promises kitchen mats, and for once the first tile delivers. It's a burgundy anti-fatigue mat with "Our Best Kitchen Mats for Less." That's more honest than the baking sheets email, where the first tile was roasted vegetables.
- Then the email wanders off. Pergolas show up three times, in tiles 2, 4 and 6. The lemon-print runner is the only other kitchen-mat tile. The "we think you'll ❤ these" grid is a random mix of bedding, Christmas trees, toilet seats and ottomans. I'm renovating two houses, so I can see some of this being relevant. A toilet seat tile next to a pink bedding set doesn't read as personalization. It reads as the whole catalog dumped into a template.
- Would I click? Maybe on the kitchen mats, since I have a primary kitchen I'm redoing. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read, and probably save the lemon runner to my Pinterest-adjacent folder.

## What's working

- The first tile matches the subject. A kitchen mat as the hero keeps the promise, unlike the baking sheets send on 11/20.
- The Sealy "Black Friday 80% OFF" banner at the top is large and obvious. I know the offer before I scroll.
- The "Kitchen Mats Under $30" tile has a concrete price anchor. It also looks decent, with a bright lemon print on warm wood flooring.
- Every tile has a plain-language label with a "»" arrow, so I know where each one goes.
- The bottom has Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing, Professional and the app QR. That's useful for someone who buys through a rewards card.

## What's weak

- Pergolas appear three times (tiles 2, 4 and 6). They look nearly identical, and it feels padded. It's also the same pergola I saw in "Best-selling pergolas" on 11/20.
- Only two of about six hero tiles are actually kitchen mats. The email breaks its own subject promise after the first tile.
- The "we think you'll ❤ these" grid says "Just for You" on bedding and toilet seats, but nothing connects it to what I've browsed. Pottery Barn would at least tie it to a collection.
- The "up to 80% off" is on the banner, but no tile shows a price or a percent off. I can't tell what is actually discounted.
- "Rewards math" is missing. No points, no cash back, nothing for me as a member. The Rewards icon is buried at the bottom.
- There's no shipping or delivery info. For bulky items like deck boxes, pergolas and armchairs, that matters.
- The "Wayfair Verified Christmas trees" tile and the bedding tile feel disconnected from a kitchen-mat message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it a real kitchen-mat email. Use four or five mat tiles with prices and sizes, and cut the pergolas. If I've browsed pergolas, give that its own send.
- 2. Put a price or percent on every tile, like "from $24" or "40% off." The banner's 80% claim should show up somewhere in the products.
- 3. Add a rewards line near the top, such as "Earn X% back with Wayfair Rewards."
- 4. Add delivery clarity on bulky items, like "Ships free in 3-5 days."
- 5. Make the "Just for You" grid tie to my browsing, such as "Because you looked at kitchen sinks," so it reads as personal. Don't mix in toilet seats and Christmas trees.
- 6. Cap cadence. Skip a day. Sending near-identical emails every day trains me to ignore the brand.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, table layout, `@media max-width:480px` rules) shows no confirmed broken links or unresolved merge tokens. The real issues are missing alt text on linked images and tracking pixels, 20 impression pixels served over `http://`, and a campaign label in the app deep link that doesn't match the send type. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 links were probed. 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting from probing, not dead links), including:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` ("Shop Sale")
  - `.../daily-sales/filters/our-best-kitchen-mats-for-less-se20587.html?eid=247958`
  - `.../on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.html?eid=603456`
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` (×2)
- **Unprobed links:** 4 links were not checked because the 70s time budget ran out: `popular-picks-se49538`, `pergolas-and-gazebos-from-%2450-se81`, `kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022`, `pergolas-for-less--se82165`. Their status is unknown. Two of them also appear truncated in the QA output, so the output cut them off rather than the source.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 ad-impression pixels use plain HTTP, `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=cc818fed-...`.
  - Some clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS images, so impressions are likely under-counted there.
  - Because these are impression beacons, the loss affects ad measurement rather than visible content.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (eventType=EmailOpen) is HTTPS and fine, but it has no alt text (see section 3).
- **Rate-limited CTAs:** the two "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links point to the same onelink URL with different `_eml` values. This is per-link tracking, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images without alt text:** the link name falls back to the filename in the QA output (`[image: ...]`). These are the highest-impact accessibility issues.
  - `1113-US-BF-Email-VP.gif`, wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales?...sltid=4`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`, wrapped in a link to `/?...sltid=5`
- **Other images missing alt:**
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_2_purple.png`
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`. The filename contains URL-encoded spaces and parentheses, and the image looks like a spacer or placeholder.
  - `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels. These are tracking pixels and should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Images off:** with images disabled, the header and hero links would have no accessible text.
- **Not assessed:** the HTML source was truncated, so the viewport meta tag, `lang` attribute, and dark-mode handling could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the captured source or URLs. Recipient and link IDs (`_emr=cc818fed-...`, `_eml=<uuid>`) are all populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw message as received before calling it a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for large senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent. The QA tool attributes this to the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. Nothing indicates failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** the QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the results don't show it. The footer was also outside the truncated HTML. These could not be verified either way.
- **From domain:** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a consistent sending subdomain. No mismatch is visible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters** on any probed link. Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_121290`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16802`, `mdlid`, `sltid`. This is consistent across links and likely intentional, but it won't register as campaign traffic in a generic analytics setup.
- **Campaign label mismatch on the app link:** it carries `c=Triggered` and `pid=Email`, while every other link carries `source=graymatter_batch`. A batch send tagged as "Triggered" in AppsFlyer will skew campaign reporting.
- **Insecure fallback:** the app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP, so it relies on a redirect for users without the app.
- **Landing alignment:** the "Our Best Kitchen Mats for Less" link (`se20587`, `eid=247958`) matches the subject's kitchen-mats theme. I could not confirm that the page content matches, because every landing page returned 429 and none was loaded.
- **Off-theme links:** the pergola links (`se82161`, `se82165`, `se81`) are not tied to the subject. This is a content matter and not flagged as a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the two linked hero and header images (`1113-US-BF-Email-VP.gif`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`) and to `sku_top_2_purple.png`. Add `alt=""` to `blankBanner`, `b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels.
2. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, or confirm the host redirects HTTP to HTTPS without dropping the beacon.
3. Correct the AppsFlyer `c=Triggered` value on the onelink URL to match the batch campaign, and change `af_web_dp` to HTTPS.
4. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff so the 4 unprobed links and the 8 rate-limited links get real status codes.
5. Inspect the raw delivered message, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating any of them as a defect.
6. Fix the QA tooling noise. A 57% pass rate with 0 issues comes mostly from warnings on pixels and rate limits. Suppress alt-text warnings for 1×1 or `imp` beacons and treat 429 as "inconclusive."
