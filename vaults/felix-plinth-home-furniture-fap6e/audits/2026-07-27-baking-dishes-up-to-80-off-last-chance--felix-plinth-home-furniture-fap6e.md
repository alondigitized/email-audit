# BAKING DISHES ⭕ UP TO 80% OFF ⭕ LAST CHANCE ⭕ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-something Wayfair emails deep into my inbox this week and this one somehow finds a new way to annoy me: the subject screamed "BAKING DISHES ⭕ UP TO 80% OFF ⭕ LAST CHANCE ⭕" and there is not a single baking dish anywhere near the hero. I open it expecting muffin tins and Pyrex, I get a crib, a yellow throw pillow, a shower door, and a purple "Black Friday in July" banner that's apparently still running from three emails ago. This is the exact same bait-and-switch move as the "AWNINGS" send that scored a 5 with me — subject promises one thing, hero delivers a random grab-bag.
- The "picked just for you" header is doing a lot of unearned work over a grid that includes a baby crib and a bathroom shower panel — I don't have kids and I'm not renovating a bathroom right now, so "for you" is generous. Bakeware only shows up once, buried mid-scroll as a "cookware & bakeware up to 60% OFF" category tile in the flash deals section — not even the same discount as the subject claimed.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is the same purple-banner, same-fonts, same-"ends tonight" template I've seen from Wayfair a dozen times this month, just with a different lie in the subject line this time. Marking read and moving on.

## What's working

- The "was/now" strikethrough pricing on every tile is doing real work — I can see the actual dollar savings without clicking, which is more than most brands bother with.
- Star ratings under each product give a quick trust signal even scrolling fast.
- Cookware & bakeware does exist as a 60%-off category tile in the flash deals grid, so the subject isn't a total fabrication — just wildly overstated.
- The Affirm 0% APR banner near the bottom is genuinely useful for me specifically, since I'm financing two renovations at once — that's the one line in this whole email that felt targeted.

## What's weak

- Subject promised baking dishes as the hero; the actual hero is "Black Friday in July" generic sale copy — this is the same bait pattern as the awnings send, and I'm starting to expect it from Wayfair now.
- The "picked just for you" grid has zero personalization logic I can detect — a crib next to a shower door next to a muffin tin isn't a curated pick, it's just inventory.
- A random Bedsure ad is wedged into the middle of a Wayfair email ("Bedsure — Up to 70% off bedding, blankets, and more") — feels like a sponsored insert, and it breaks the single-brand trust I'd expect from my own inbox.
- "Ends tonight" urgency has now shown up in back-to-back-to-back Wayfair sends this week — I don't believe it anymore, it reads like the boy who cried wolf.
- No swatches, dimensions, or material specs on any furniture tile — for stuff like the console table or shelving I'd actually need that before considering a click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. If baking dishes are the hook, show baking dishes first — a kitchen sale hero block, not a generic Black Friday banner. Pottery Barn's "Nightstands starting at $349" send at least led with the actual product it named.
- 2. Cut the "picked just for you" grid down to one coherent category instead of 20 unrelated SKUs — CB2's "Just in. Already trending." worked because it committed to one theme instead of a grab-bag.
- 3. Drop or clearly separate the Bedsure ad — right now it reads like a bait insert inside a Wayfair-branded email, not a partnership.
- 4. Retire "ends tonight" for at least a week. I've seen it on four separate sends now; it's lost all urgency.
- 5. Add real product specs (dimensions, material) to at least the furniture tiles — that's the actual blocker for me converting on nightstands/shelving.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bakeware & kitchen essentials, up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your kitchen upgrade list, on sale today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bakeware, cookware, and prep tools — all marked down`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Muffin tins to Dutch ovens, up to 60% off today only`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Email Audit: Wayfair — "BAKING DISHES ⭕ UP TO 80% OFF"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Wayfair XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with Outlook/Gmail CSS hacks and mobile media queries; the main technical gaps are 20 non-HTTPS tracking-pixel sources, widespread missing `alt` attributes on content images, and unconfirmed unsubscribe/authentication headers at the transport layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Mixed-content tracking pixels**: 20 impression beacons load over plain HTTP, e.g. `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000&sz=2x1&m=false&p=b48338f0-ecd4-465f-8f45-c60df4f1a3d4` (sequential `s=124126000`–`124126019`). Many mail clients block or strip non-HTTPS resources, which would suppress open/view tracking for this batch.
- **Rate-limited probes (429)**: 7 links returned 429 during QA probing (CTA "Get Exclusive Deals on the App", "Shop Sale", homepage, and 3 product/star-rating links). These are inconclusive — likely prober-side throttling, not confirmed broken — but warrant a manual re-check.
- **Unprobed (time budget)**: 4 links were not checked at all, including `.../Martha-Stewart-Gallen-10-Piece-Baking-Pans-Ceramic-PFA-F` and `.../daily-sales/bedding-sale~e363376.html` — status unknown, neither pass nor fail.
- **Deep-link fallback**: App CTA uses AppsFlyer OneLink (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`) with both `af_dp` (native scheme `wayfairapp://home`) and `af_web_dp` fallback to `http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app` — correctly configured deferred deep link. Note the fallback URL itself is HTTP, not HTTPS.
- Only 12 of 80 total links were probed (footer/social/utility links deprioritized by design); no broken (non-429, non-timeout) links were confirmed in the probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text** on ~24 flagged images, including customer-facing content (not just tracking pixels): `stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`/`stars_9.png` (rating icons, 8+ instances), `WFUS_0720_BlackFridayinJuly-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyTop`, `...SkinnyBan` banner images (3 instances), and promotional button images (`0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`, `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi`). Screen readers will announce nothing for these elements.
- Tracking pixels (`b.php`, `imp`) also lack `alt`, which is standard/acceptable for invisible beacons.
- CSS includes standard Outlook/Gmail mitigations: `u + .Gmail` padding hack, `body[data-outlook-cycle]` fix, `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for the custom Sofia webfont (`@font-face` woff-only — correctly paired with an MSO fallback since Outlook desktop won't render the woff).
- Responsive breakpoints present at 480px and 320px (`u-Title`, `u-Button`, `ProductRec-*`, `Footer-link` classes) — mobile scaling logic looks intact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIRSTNAME%%`) or personalization placeholders appear in the provided HTML or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in headers as relayed. QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than true absence — needs verification against raw SMTP headers from the original delivery.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed from available data.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM)**: not found — sender authentication status is unknown via this relay; cannot confirm pass/fail.
- A footer unsubscribe link is confirmed to exist per the QA prober's methodology ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in probing), but its destination/HTTP status was not independently verified in the provided findings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links use Wayfair's proprietary attribution schema (`refid=MKTEML_138002`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15852`, `batchid=2`, `varid=7`, `csnid=...`, `_emr`, `_eml`) rather than standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` params — consistent internal tracking, not a defect, but note this if any downstream analytics expect UTM-based attribution.
- Product-link SKU params are internally consistent: e.g. link for "JoyJolt-8Piece-Glass-OventoTable-Baking-Dish" carries `mplid=JYJT1157&sku=JYJT1157`, matching the destination path `L7097-K~JYJT1157.html`. Same pattern holds for the crib (`URE1242`) and third product (`WLRO1509`) links — no SKU/URL mismatches observed.
- Sale-page CTAs ("Shop Sale", banner image) correctly point to `/daily-sales` with matching tracking params.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels (and the `af_web_dp` fallback URL) over HTTPS — plain HTTP resources risk being blocked as mixed content in modern mail clients, undercounting opens.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to content-bearing images, particularly the repeated `stars_*.png` rating graphics and the two `SkinnyTop`/`SkinnyBan` promotional banners.
3. Independently verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` against raw SMTP headers from the original send (not just the AgentMail relay copy) to rule out a relay-side capture gap versus a true compliance gap.
4. Re-probe the 7 rate-limited (429) links and the 4 time-budget-skipped links outside the automated QA window to confirm they resolve correctly.
