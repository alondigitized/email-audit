# Your next great find is ON SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. By my count this is the eleventh send since 9/29, and the subject line is the vaguest of the bunch. "Your next great find is ON SALE" could be any retailer. I opened it hoping the sale was real, and the hero says "Now on Sale" with no percentage, no dollar amount and no end date. A sale with no number reads as a placeholder.
- Below the hero it's a decent category grid: furniture, tabletop, bedding, bath, outdoor, pillows & decor. Then a "Recommended Just For You" block, a Christmas Shop banner, a design services push, an app promo and the credit card pitch. It's a long scroll that tries to be everything. Compare this morning's "Ships FREE: Your new favorite throw," which had one offer I could act on. That one worked better.
- Would I click? Maybe into Furniture, since I'm furnishing two houses. But I'd have to scroll to the fine print to find out what the discount is. I wouldn't forward it. I'd file it in the Pinterest folder and move on.

## What's working

- The hero dining room shot with the wood chairs and rust rug fits the fall palette I'm planning around.
- The six-tile grid (Furniture, Tabletop, Bedding, Bath, Outdoor, Pillows & Decor) is easy to scan, and I can jump straight to the room I'm working on.
- "Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" is the best thing in the email for someone renovating two places. It's a real showroom-style benefit.
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" with the card pitch is something I'd do the math on. I buy through the highest-rewards card, so it got my attention.
- The "Shop In-Stock Furniture" link at the top addresses shipping timelines, which is the real pain point for big furniture.

## What's weak

- "Now on Sale" has no number. No %, no $, no deadline. It's the weakest possible way to state an offer.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block mixes a platform bed, a swivel chair, a curtain rod and a ghost collection. None of it reflects my browsing, so "just for you" feels false.
- The Lit Ghost Collection next to the Christmas Shop banner is a seasonal whiplash. It's October 2nd and I'm looking at Halloween and Christmas in the same scroll.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of exclusions, and it's the only place the real terms live. That feels buried.
- The Christmas Shop, design services, app download, credit card and Affirm sections all compete. There's no single focus after the hero.
- Tap targets in the lower section are small text links ("Learn More," "Download Now"), which will be fiddly on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero, for example "Up to 30% off furniture, through Sunday." That one change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Cut the lower stack to one secondary module. Keep Free Design Services and drop the app, Affirm and card blocks, or fold them into the footer.
- 3. Make the "Recommended" block real: show items I viewed, or at least keep it to one category.
- 4. Pull the key sale terms above the fold in plain language instead of leaving them only in the fine print.
- 5. Space the sends out. Eleven emails in four days trains me to ignore the sender, the same way CB2's near-daily sends and Crate & Barrel Kids' family emails have me filing everything under "snooze."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-heavy XHTML 1.0 layout with 52 click-redirect links. The QA data shows widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, and PHP code leaked into a `<style>` block. Unsubscribe and authentication status could not be verified because the headers weren't captured and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verifiable:** 52 links point at tracking/redirect domains (e.g. `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) and were not probed, so destinations and redirect health are unknown.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` loads over plain HTTP.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party ad-network beacon, and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/fa5448a4-...` is an open-tracking pixel. Both lack alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from a West Elm domain. It is a dependency on another brand's CDN.
- **Test/stale asset paths:** `S26_Sale_B3_MINKStudioTest_Clearance/` has "StudioTest" in the production path. `pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images, from the QA list. Examples:
  - `slices_01`–`09.jpg` in `S26_Sale_B3_MINKStudioTest_Clearance`
  - `slices_01`–`18.jpg` in `Email-Footer`
  - the product images `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `chunky-wool-jute-rug-l.jpg`, and others
  - the banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - With images off, the email may render as blank slices.
- **Footer as sliced images:** the footer is 18 sliced images with no alt text. Any footer text, including the unsubscribe and address lines, may be unreadable to screen readers and with images blocked.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel. These may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport restricts zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>` (the root element is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`).
- **Leaked server-side code:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...`. This is invalid CSS and can break the rules that follow it in some clients.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat...">` is ignored by most email clients. The fallback fonts are not confirmed.
- **Legacy selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are attribute selectors that only help if those exact classes exist in the body. This couldn't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Stray `img` rules:** `img {max-width: 650px}` at min-width 700px and `img {max-width: 100% !important}` at min-width 650px overlap and conflict.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the available source.
- **Recipient email in URLs:** the address is passed in plaintext in query strings, in the `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` pixel and in `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. This exposes PII in server logs and referrers. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` only partly mitigates it, and only in the browser.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA warn). The report notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA warn). One-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA warn). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown and can't be assessed from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated HTML. The footer is image slices, so I can't confirm a text-based unsubscribe link or physical address.
- **From domain:** `e.potterybarn.com` is consistent with the click domains `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `image.e.potterybarn.com`. No mismatch found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing alignment unverifiable:** the redirect URLs are opaque (`?qs=...`), so UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be confirmed.
- **Asset path vs. content:** `S26_`-prefixed asset paths mixed with `F26_` and `H26_` paths suggest reused modules. The links to those assets were not resolved, so possible stale destinations are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** add it to every content image, starting with the product images and the footer slices. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. **Footer:** make it live text for the unsubscribe link, address and legal copy.
3. **HTTPS:** move the three `http://` assets to `https://`.
4. **Style block:** remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. **Document head:** fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. **Test assets:** replace the `MINKStudioTest` path and the 2022 banner asset.
7. **Email in query strings:** use opaque IDs instead.
8. **Header QA:** re-run it against the raw message (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
9. **Link tracing:** resolve a sample of the 52 tracked links to verify landing pages and UTM parameters.
