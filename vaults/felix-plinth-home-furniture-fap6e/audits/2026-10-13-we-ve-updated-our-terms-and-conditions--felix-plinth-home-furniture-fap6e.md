# We’ve Updated Our Terms and Conditions
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, but this isn't a promo, and I'm glad. After the Fall Sale strip showed up on every send since 10/06 (10/12 alone had "Don't miss The Fall Sale," "Everything you need for Thanksgiving," and "Fall deals UP TO 70% OFF"), a plain legal notice almost felt like a break. The email is the PB wordmark, two short paragraphs about an updated arbitration clause and "new legal requirements," and a footer. Below that is a very long stretch of empty white.
- The "arbitration clause" wording made me pause. I'm mid-renovation on two houses with a lot of open orders, so I wondered whether this affects my right to dispute a delivery or a damaged sofa. But the email tells me nothing about what changed. It only gives a "here" link, and it says it's transactional, so it goes out even to people who've opted out of marketing. I would not click. I'd mark it read, and I might skim the terms later if a big furniture order goes sideways. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The email is short, and the two paragraphs are readable without zooming.
- The PB wordmark up top makes it clear who sent it. It's less noisy than the green sale strip I've gotten used to.
- It says outright that it's transactional and why I'm getting it. That's honest, and Wayfair never explains why I got its 13th email of the week.
- No discount bait. Nothing here pretends to be something else.

## What's weak

- The arbitration change is buried in a sentence with no summary of what's different. That is the one thing I'd want to know.
- The only CTA is a small underlined "here" link. It's a tiny tap target on a phone, and "here" isn't descriptive.
- There's a huge blank area under the footer. Whether that's the render or the email, it looks unfinished next to a tight Crate & Barrel send.
- The footer text is tiny and gray. The address and "this message was sent to" line are hard to read.
- Nothing connects to me as a customer: no account, order, or rewards reference. It reads like a broadcast to a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a plain-English summary of what changed, such as "We've updated our arbitration terms. Here's what it means for your orders." Then add a real button, "Read the updated terms," instead of an inline "here."
- 2. Add one line on what it means for me: whether it affects returns, delivery disputes, or Pottery Barn Rewards benefits. A note that nothing changes for existing orders would help too.
- 3. Trim the trailing blank space and make the footer legible.
- 4. Put the effective date in the preheader or hero so there's a clear deadline.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Quick update to your Pottery Barn account terms`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `What's changing in our arbitration clause, in plain English.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No action needed. Here's what's new and when it starts.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
This is a minimal, table-based transactional notice (terms update) with a single body link. It has one confirmed compliance failure (no unsubscribe link in the body), one non-HTTPS image, and a CSS typo in the footer.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All 4 links are ESP click-redirects (`https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=ABp7...`). The QA probe skipped 3 of them, so I couldn't verify where they resolve.
- The "here" link to the updated Terms points to an opaque `qs=` token. I can't confirm from the source that it lands on the Terms page.
- The open pixel `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/open.aspx?46WILIB3Q7QU3NKSB2HREZWBUQ.110060&d=110060&bmt=0` is a standard 1x1 with `alt=""`. No issue.
- The unescaped `&` in the pixel's query string is a minor HTML validity nit.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- The logo loads over plain HTTP: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2013/0801_nav/logo.gif`. Clients that block mixed or insecure content may drop it, and the `alt="POTTERY BARN"` text is then the only fallback. The path (`/2013/0801_nav/`) suggests a legacy asset.
- Footer CSS typo: `font-family:verdana,arial; font-size:9px; cold or:#bcbbbb;`. `cold or` is invalid, so the intended color declaration is ignored. The links carry inline `color:#bcbbbb`, but the footer text itself falls back to the inherited color.
- The footer text is 9px in `#bcbbbb` on white. That contrast is about 1.9:1, well below WCAG AA (4.5:1). It's small and hard to read.
- The body text is `#666666` at 12px, which passes AA at about 5.7:1.
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- No `lang` attribute on `<html>`, no `<!DOCTYPE>`, and no `<meta charset>` or viewport tag in the supplied source.
- The layout uses fixed `width="650"` tables with no responsive handling, so it will scroll horizontally on mobile.
- Layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.
- The `<style>` block sits inside `<body>`, which some clients strip.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. There are no unresolved tokens. The recipient address appears in the footer ("This message was sent to: lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app").

**5. Compliance**
- **[FAIL] No unsubscribe link in the body.** The email states it is transactional ("you are receiving it even if you have opted out of marketing emails"). That framing may exempt it from CAN-SPAM opt-out requirements, since a pure terms-change notice is a transactional or relationship message. The automated check flags it regardless, and it is a risk if the message is judged to have commercial content.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** weren't found. The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified defect.
- **Authentication-Results** wasn't captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not necessarily the sender.
- The physical address is present: "3250 Van Ness Ave., San Francisco, CA 94109".
- The privacy policy link is present.
- The sender domain `o.potterybarn.com` is a subdomain, consistent with a dedicated sending domain.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- No UTM parameters are visible. Campaign attribution sits inside the opaque `qs=` payload, so I can't confirm it.
- The landing destination for "here" can't be verified (see section 2).
- Nothing in the supplied source indicates a mismatch.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Confirm the "here" link resolves to the current Terms page by following the redirect chain manually, since the QA probe skipped it.
2. Serve the logo over HTTPS (`https://edm.potterybarn.com/...`), ideally from a current asset path.
3. Fix `cold or:#bcbbbb` to `color:#bcbbbb` and raise the footer contrast and size (at least 11–12px, with a darker gray).
4. Add `<!DOCTYPE>`, `lang`, `<meta charset>`, a viewport tag, a non-empty `<title>`, and `role="presentation"` on layout tables. Move the `<style>` block into `<head>`.
5. Consider a preference-center or unsubscribe link in the footer, even on transactional sends, to avoid a QA fail and complaints. Legal should confirm whether the transactional classification holds.
6. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and authentication headers on the raw message, bypassing the relay, to separate relay artifacts from real gaps.
