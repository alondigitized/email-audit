# Get this fun look: stripe drenching!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Eleventh Crate & Barrel-family email this week, and I'm genuinely losing track of which folder to drag these into. This one's Crate & Kids again, leading with "get the look: stripe drenching" — which, fine, striped bedding is a real look, but "drenching" is a word I've now seen in three different home-decor emails this month and it's starting to feel like a trend-piece headline instead of an offer. The email itself is a marathon: kids bedroom, bedding, rugs, nursery furniture, striped curtains, then a whole second Labor Day Event grid stacked underneath with percentages scattered everywhere.
- We don't have a kid, so nursery furniture and crib bedding are dead weight to me — but I'll give it this, the striped bedding and rugs sections could plausibly cross over into the guest room reno if I squint. Compare that to yesterday's "Love them now & forever" nursery email from this same sub-brand, which had zero crossover potential for us — at least this one has some usable texture.
- Would I click? Maybe scroll to the rugs section since we're mid-swatch-hell on rugs for both houses. Forward to my wife? No — she's already rolling her eyes at how many of these are hitting my inbox. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero visual — stacked striped/textured pillows on a pink background — is genuinely well-composed, better than most of CB2's seventeen-emails-a-month sameness.
- "In kids bedroom: calm & cool" section groups related products cleanly instead of just a wall of thumbnails.
- The rugs section ("in rugs: cozy & warm") is at least tangential to something I actually shop for right now.
- Labor Day discounts are stated as specific percentages (up to 60% off bedding, 30% off rugs, etc.) rather than vague "save now" language — Article's Labor Day send did this better up top, but at least it's here.

## What's weak

- Nursery furniture, crib bedding, baby's room sections are 100% irrelevant to a childless couple renovating two houses — this is the same miss as the "Love them now & forever" send from earlier today.
- The email is exhaustingly long — bedroom, bedding, rugs, nursery, curtains, THEN a full second Labor Day grid. Wayfair's ladder email at least stayed tight around one category.
- "Stripe drenching" as a subject payoff doesn't land until you're several scrolls in — no immediate hero offer visible without scrolling, unlike Article's "up to 40% off" sitting right at the top.
- Double Rewards banner appears twice (top and bottom) with no explanation of what it actually means in dollars — Pottery Barn's free design services email at least had one clear, singular hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one theme per send — stripe bedding/rugs OR nursery, not both crammed into one scroll. That alone would stop me from bouncing.
- 2. Put the actual Labor Day percentage-off number in the subject line, the way Article did — "up to 60% off" beats "stripe drenching" for getting me to open.
- 3. Move the rugs and bedding sections above the nursery content since those are the more universally relevant categories.
- 4. Explain "Double Rewards" in one line with real numbers (e.g., "earn 2x points through 9/8") instead of just a banner and a "Learn More" link.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Double Rewards through 9/8 — shop bedroom, bedding & rugs now`
