# 🚨Ends tonight: Earn 2x Rewards & get up up 60% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Kids blast, and it's the same "Ends Tonight" countdown clock I've now seen from this brand family basically every day this week — except this time the timer says 7 hours 20 minutes instead of the "00:16:08:41" one from earlier today. I don't have kids. I've said this in every review of this sub-brand and it keeps landing in my inbox because I'm subscribed to Crate & Barrel proper for my actual renovation. The crib, the toddler bed, the baby high chair — none of it is remotely relevant to two adult houses I'm furnishing.
- Buried in that endless scroll there's actually stuff I'd care about — rugs, decor, lighting, a desk — but it's wearing Crate & Kids branding and nursery styling, so my brain filters it as "not for me" before I even register the category. Compare that to CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send, which at least felt like a grown-up sale even sharing the same parent company fatigue. This one's an automatic scroll-past, not a delete only because I know Crate & Barrel proper sometimes rides shotgun in the same inbox week.

## What's working

- The countdown clock is a real, live-feeling timer, not just static "today" copy.
- "Earn 10% back in Reward Dollars" is a concrete, specific offer if you're already in the loyalty program.
- The rug and bedding shots (star pattern, plaid runner) are genuinely nice product photography — could work in a big-kid room, not just a nursery.
- Consistent red accent color and Crate&Kids logo placement, so I always know instantly who sent it.

## What's weak

- Wrong audience entirely — crib, high chair, baby swing content when I'm furnishing two adult homes.
- This is at least the fourth near-identical "Ends Tonight" / countdown email from the Crate & Barrel family this week; I could recite the section order from memory now.
- The "we love these for you" personalized product grid at the bottom is still baby gear (a nursery dresser, a toddler chair) — no actual personalization happening.
- Scroll length is brutal — nine or ten stacked offer blocks before you even hit the app download pitch.
- "up up 60% off" in the subject line is a typo I noticed before I even opened it — sloppy for a brand asking for my trust.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely and only send me Crate & Barrel proper — I've now flagged this exact mismatch across multiple emails and nothing's changed.
- 2. Fix the subject line typo ("up up") — it's the first thing anyone sees and it reads unprofessional.
- 3. Cut the email in half — nobody needs ten stacked category offers at the same discount tier; lead with rugs/decor/lighting since that's the only stuff overlapping with adult renovators like me.
- 4. If cross-selling to Crate & Barrel subscribers who don't have kids, drop the crib/high chair blocks and swap in the adult-furniture crossover items up front.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added across bedding, rugs, and decor.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `7 hours left to earn 2x Rewards on kids' furniture.`
