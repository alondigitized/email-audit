# Pillows in all your favorite hues (ship free!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the fifth send since 10/30 to open on the same maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip. After that strip, though, the email is a real pillow story. "Made for Mixing" with a stack of patterned pillows is a hero I'd stop on. Of all the recent Pottery Barn sends, including the 11/01 "Hosting soon? Quick-ship furniture inside," this one has the most styling content.
- The "ship free" in the subject is only a small "PILLOWS SHIP FREE" pill on the hero. It carries no threshold, no fine print and no deadline. The neutrals/blues/greens tiles are a good way to shop by color palette. That's how I'm buying for two houses at once. But it's a pillow email, and I'm mid-renovation on two houses. Pillows are a last-mile purchase for me, not a priority one.
- Would I click? Probably one tile, "shop blues," and then I'd save it to my inbox folder. Would I forward it? No. It's pleasant, but nothing in it is worth passing on.

## What's working

- The "Made for Mixing" hero is a strong stacked-pillow photo, and it lands as a styling idea rather than a discount banner.
- The neutrals, blues and greens tiles let me shop by palette, which matches how I plan rooms.
- "Our Favorite Pillow Pairings" with "Get Inspired" is the Pinterest-folder content I save emails for.
- The "More for your living room" row (Throws, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) lets me jump to adjacent categories quickly.
- The free design services and the rewards card (10% back) near the bottom feel relevant to a rewards-card buyer like me.

## What's weak

- The same maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles" strip opens this send again, as it has since 10/30. It's wallpaper by now, and it pushes the actual hero down.
- "Ship free" is vague. I see no minimum, no date and no exclusions in the body, and the fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text.
- "Recommended just for you" shows a dining table, a curtain rod, stockings, a rug, a bed and a mattress. It has nothing to do with the pillow story and looks like a generic grab-bag. It's unclear whether any of it is actually personalized.
- The Christmas Shop banner midway through dilutes the focus, and the email is very long for a pillow send.
- Nothing shows pillow prices, sizes or fills, and there are no reviews. There's no way to judge value without clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the repeat sale strip, or fold it into a thin line. Lead with the pillow hero so the creative isn't identical to the last five sends.
- 2. Put the actual offer in the hero: "Pillows ship free, no minimum, through Sunday" or similar. Right now it's a vague pill.
- 3. Show 3-4 hero pillows with prices and star ratings. Add "from $XX" to the color tiles.
- 4. Cut the unrelated recommendations, or tie them to the pillows (throws, sofas, poufs). Crate & Barrel's kitchen sends stayed on one topic, and that's why I stayed with them.
- 5. Shorten the footer. Crate & Barrel and CB2 manage a tighter close.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Pillows in all your favorite hues (ship free!)"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an image-sliced Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e.potterybarn.com`) with no alt text on most images, three `http://` image sources, and the recipient's address in plaintext in tracking pixel URLs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML was truncated, so footer and unsubscribe content wasn't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 58 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped as click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked, which would undercount opens, and it exposes the recipient address on the wire.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is a Turn (Amobee) ad-network beacon with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer served from the West Elm domain. A change on that host would break this email.
- **Stale assets:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 campaign path reused in a 2026 send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images have none. This includes all 16 `F26_Decor_B6_PillowPairings/slices_01–16.jpg`, the 18 `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, the `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` banners, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`, and six product images such as `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`. With images off or in a screen reader, the body and footer are effectively blank.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Missing language:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Mixed content:** three `http://` image sources are flagged: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` (2022 banner), and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Leaked server code:** PHP `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` calls sit as literal text inside the second `<style>` block. It is harmless but malformed CSS, and it will be parsed as a bad rule.
- **Web font:** the `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat `<link>` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook, so the fallback stack must carry rendering.
- **Hidden text:** the preheader contains an unescaped `&` in "mix & match".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the supplied source.
- **Recipient email in query strings:** it is exposed in `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and the `svopn` pixel's `sv_sveme=` parameter. Both are plaintext, unhashed PII. The first is also used as an `<img>` source, so it is a tracking beacon.
- **Beacon alt text:** the `coreemail.html` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/272bcb5a-...` beacons have no alt text, so the 1x1 pixels aren't marked as decorative.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The relay is the likely cause.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,162 of 15,399 characters (72%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold and a minor spam-scoring risk.
- **Footer content:** the physical address and in-body unsubscribe link weren't in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed. The 18 footer slices have no alt text, so any unsubscribe or address text in the images is invisible to filters and screen readers.
- **Sender:** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is a branded subdomain consistent with the click domain. No mismatch was observed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. Click URLs are opaque `qs=` tokens, and the redirect targets weren't probed, so UTM presence, landing-page alignment, and the "ship free" promise can't be verified from the data.
- **Tracking IDs:** `sv_cid=0053_00597` and `mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3` are in use, so attribution probably runs through those IDs rather than UTMs.
- **Referrer policy:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` may suppress referrer data on landing, which hurts analytics attribution.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the pillow-grid slices, footer slices and banners. Add `alt=""` to spacers and beacons.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Hash or tokenize the recipient email in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`, and stop sending it as plaintext.
4. Remove the PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
5. Host the spacer on `edm.potterybarn.com` instead of `edm.westelm.com`, and refresh the 2022 banner.
6. Inspect the raw message for List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, since the relay may have stripped them. Resolve a sample of the 58 click-tracking URLs to check for UTMs, 200 status, and landing-page match.
7. Rebalance the plain-text part so URLs are under 70% of its characters.
