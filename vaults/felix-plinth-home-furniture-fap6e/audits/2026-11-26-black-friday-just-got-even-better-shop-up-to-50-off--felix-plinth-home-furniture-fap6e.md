# Black Friday just got even BETTER! Shop up to 50% off →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel family send I've dealt with this week, and the second under the real "BLACK FRIDAY" sender name today. The first screen is fine: a red hero with "Biggest sale of the year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping*" and a Shop Now button. But "just got even BETTER" doesn't tell me what changed. It's the same 50% off I've seen since the "Happy Thanksgiving! 🦃" sends this morning.
- After the hero it turns into a very long scroll. There's a grid of "up to 40% off sofas," "up to 60% off kitchen brands," rugs, gifts and so on. Then comes a kitchen block (Shun, Ninja, Philips, Ooni, Cuisinart), a Gift Shop banner, "best kitchen gifts," "we love these for you," a credit card pitch, an app download and a design appointment. It's a catalog, not an email. I'd skim it, screenshot the sofa and rug tiles for my renovation folder, and probably not click today.
- I wouldn't forward it. It's useful for a quick price check, but nothing in it is a "you have to see this" moment.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer clearly: up to 50% off plus free shipping, with a single obvious Shop Now button.
- The tiles are sorted by category (sofas, kitchen, rugs, holiday décor). That fits how I'm furnishing two houses, since I can jump straight to the room I'm working on.
- The kitchen section shows named brands with specific percentages (Shun 25%, Ninja up to $100 off, Philips up to $400 off). Specific offers beat vague ones.
- The Crate Card block mentions Reward Dollars. That's the rewards math I care about, though it's buried near the bottom.
- The design appointment strip speaks to my renovation project more than anything else in the email.

## What's weak

- "Up to" is stacked on every tile, so I can't tell whether the sofas I want are 10% or 40% off.
- Free shipping has an asterisk. For furniture, that's the thing I actually need spelled out: white glove or curbside, and what's excluded.
- The email is far too long. Compared with it, Pottery Barn's "NEW BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" keeps one hero and a tighter set of modules.
- There are no deadlines. "Even BETTER" implies urgency, but nothing says when the sale ends.
- The kitchen gadget and gift content is aimed at someone shopping for presents, not someone outfitting a house. There's no swatch, sample or color-matching content for renovators.
- It's the same creative as Wayfair's and CB2's Black Friday sends this week, with a different subject line on top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to a hero and two or three category blocks, and put the furniture shipping terms next to the hero. Move the app, card and appointment pitches into a separate send.
- 2. Say what's new. If there's a new deal, name it with the dollar amount or percentage and the product category.
- 3. Add a real deadline, like "Ends Monday 11:59 PT," so the urgency is credible.
- 4. Segment by interest. Someone who browses sofas and rugs shouldn't see Ninja blenders first.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas & rugs now up to 40% off, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns added: furniture + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on furniture, plus design help for your next project.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra kitchen deals just dropped, and sofas are still up to 40% off.`

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel "Black Friday just got even BETTER!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has structurally sound XHTML with `lang="en"` and VML/MSO fallbacks, but at least 38 images have no alt text and the viewport meta blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the QA relay did not capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and link markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **79 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5`. They share one recipient-hash `s=` value and differ only in `n`. Each is an extra third-party request, and none has alt text.

- **Further tracking pixel:** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a268ceb3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (at least 38 images, the QA list appears cut off):**
  - 23 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/*.jpg|png|gif`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png` and `61581a40-…gif`.
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - 5 `rlcdn` pixels, the `o.gif` pixel, and the `dv.` pixel.

  The 23 content images are the real accessibility problem. With images blocked, the email will render as empty blocks and its links will have no accessible name.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Image-width CSS conflicts:** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` is followed by `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftovers:**

  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags: one combined and two separate.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 23 content images and the 7 `mi.../rp/*.png` images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries so the intended 600px or 640px cap applies.
