# The bestsellers customers "love, love, love!"
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Eighth Crate & Barrel-family email this week and this one is trying to do everything at once — living room, bedroom, dining room, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, bedding, mattresses — basically their entire catalog crammed into one scroll. "Bestsellers for a reason" with the star ratings up top is actually a smart trust play, and since I'm mid-renovation on two houses, a room-by-room breakdown isn't the worst structure. But by the time I hit the fourth "Exclusive / Customer Fave" product grid I've completely tuned out — it's the same layout repeated six times with different furniture in it.
- The thing that would actually get me to click is buried: "Double Rewards is on" plus "up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" is at the very top, which is good, but then it just... repeats category grids for what feels like a mile of scrolling with no reminder of the offer until way at the bottom. Compare that to CB2's "On sale now and twice the rewards" send I looked at — that one at least leads hard with the rewards math as the entire hook. This one buries "Double Rewards" as a small banner and hopes I remember it by the time I hit the kitchen deals section.
- Would I click? Maybe, straight to "Shop All Kitchen" since that's the room I actually need right now for the second house. Forward to a friend? No — this is a catalog dump, not a "hey look at this specific thing" moment.

## What's working

- The bestseller framing with star ratings at the top gives it social proof before I've even scrolled — none of the other C&B family sends this week led with that.
- Room-by-room sectioning (living room, bedroom, dining, kitchen, tabletop) actually maps to how I'm shopping right now across two renovations.
- The KitchenAid stand mixer callout in the kitchen block is a real, recognizable bestseller — not generic filler.
- "Top kitchen deals (they ship free!)" near the bottom finally gives me a concrete price comparison (reg price crossed out vs. sale price), which is the first hard number in the whole email.

## What's weak

- This is the same core problem CB2's "Double Rewards" email had and Wayfair's pergola email didn't — the offer gets stated once at the top and then the email just becomes an undifferentiated product wall for a full-screen scroll.
- Six nearly-identical "here's 3 products + a Shop button" grids in a row is exhausting — my eye has nothing new to land on after room three.
- No color-palette or finish consistency callout anywhere, which is exactly the kind of cross-shop pain point I actually have (matching wood tones between two houses) and this email does nothing to solve it.
- Mattress teaser tacked on at the very bottom feels like a random afterthought, not a real push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the room grids from six down to two or three and let "Double Rewards + 60% off" be restated as a sticky reminder every couple sections — right now the discount is out of sight for 90% of the scroll.
- 2. Lead with the kitchen deals block (the one with actual struck-through prices) — that's the only section with real urgency and math, and it's currently buried near the bottom.
- 3. Add a "shop by finish/wood tone" filter callout since cross-room color matching is my actual pain point, not just more SKUs.
- 4. Drop or consolidate the "finishing touches" (rugs/lighting/bedding) grid — it dilutes focus right before the actual sale block.
