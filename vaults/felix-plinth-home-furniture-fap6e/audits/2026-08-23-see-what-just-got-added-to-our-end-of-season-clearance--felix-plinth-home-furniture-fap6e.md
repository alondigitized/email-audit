# See what just got added to our end-of-season clearance
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- "Up to 60% off clearance" with a beach hero — fine, but I've now seen this exact clearance-plus-lifestyle-photo move from Crate & Barrel three times this week, and honestly this one at least leads with the offer instead of burying it under a celebrity home tour. But then it just... keeps going. Clearance grid, then NFL game day beer koozies, then a whole dining room pitch, then a credit card upsell, then an app download push, then a Design Desk booking CTA. That's five different asks in one email and I lost the thread by the third scroll.
- I'm mid-reno on two houses — I'd actually click into "rugs" or "bedding & bath" off that clearance grid since those are real category tiles with real photos. But the NFL koozie insert is completely random for a furniture email and the dining table SKUs at $1,799-$2,295 next to a "% off clearance" subject line just feels like bait-and-switch — those aren't clearance prices.
- Would I click? Maybe, straight to the clearance grid, ignoring everything after. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on — this is the fourth-plus C&B-family send this week and my patience for the parent brand is thinning same as it is for Crate & Kids.

## What's working

- The 60% off clearance headline is a real, specific offer up top — better than CB2's vague "over tomorrow" urgency-without-a-number play from earlier this week.
- Clearance is broken into recognizable categories (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, lighting, rugs, bedding) with clean product photography — I can scan and pick my lane fast.
- "Ships Free" callout on bedding & bath is a nice concrete detail buried in there.

## What's weak

- Way too many campaigns stitched into one email — clearance, NFL gameday, dining room, credit card, app, Design Desk. Pick one.
- The NFL koozie section has zero connection to "end-of-season clearance" and just feels like a random insert that dilutes the offer.
- Dining table/console prices shown ($1,799–$3,199) read full-price, not clearance — undercuts the headline offer and feels like a mild bait-and-switch next to the 60% off promise.
- This is at least the fourth C&B-family email in my inbox this week (plus the Crate & Kids barrage) — cadence fatigue is real and this one doesn't do enough to stand out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE email: clearance only. Drop the NFL section, dining room pitch, credit card, and app download into separate sends — they're diluting a genuinely good offer.
- 2. Show clearance pricing (strikethrough + sale price) on at least the hero products, not just a percentage badge — Wayfair's pergola email did this better by putting a real deal front and center immediately.
- 3. If dining room stays, label those prices clearly as "not included in clearance" so I don't feel misled scrolling from a 60%-off headline into $2,000+ tables.
- 4. Drop the credit card and app pitches to the footer only, not as full mid-email sections with their own CTAs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance just dropped: up to 60% off, new items added`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns: rugs, lighting, bedding all 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh clearance across furniture, outdoor, and bedding — while it lasts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just added to end-of-season clearance`
