# The best collab since 🍪 & 🥛 JUST hit our app
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "the best collab since 🍪 & 🥛" got me to open it, I'll admit — but then I'm staring at an app-exclusive Levain Bakery collab and immediately deflating a little, because I don't need cookies pushed to my phone, I need the Aspen collection restock tracked. This is Crate & Barrel doing the thing CB2 already did with the Goop bouclé chair tie-in (8/10, scored well because it felt like a real design collab) — except this one's a bakery, not furniture, so the "exclusive" framing doesn't land the same way for someone mid-reno.
- The good news is they don't bury the actual furniture pitch — Aspen collection at 15% off is right there below the fold, and the "Design Desk Sweeps" $5,000 gift card entry for booking a designer appointment is genuinely interesting to me given I've got two houses going at once. That's the section I'd actually engage with.
- Would I click? Not on the cookie app banner. I'd scroll past it to the Aspen sale and maybe the Design Desk sweepstakes. Wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's not embarrassing, it's just cluttered, three different offers competing for my attention in one email.

## What's working

- The hero illustration is genuinely well-designed — clean NYC storefront line art, on-brand navy, doesn't look like clip art.
- "bestselling Aspen collection 15% off" is a real, current furniture offer with visible pricing on the Le Creuset and KitchenAid items below it.
- The Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes is a strong hook for someone actually furnishing two homes right now — free designer appointment plus a real prize.
- Product grid shows actual prices and strikethrough sale pricing (Le Creuset Dutch Oven $460→$289.95) — that's the rewards-math transparency I look for before I click.
- The new Crate Card / Reward Dollars callout is relevant since I already buy on the highest-rewards card I have.

## What's weak

- Leading with a cookie bakery app collab instead of furniture is a swing-and-a-miss for my persona — I'm not opening this for pastries.
- Three competing CTAs (Shop the App, Shop the Sale, Book Appointment) with no clear priority — my eye doesn't land on one hero offer, it bounces.
- The product grid ("we love these for you") mixes cookware, utensils, and stand mixers — none of that is renovation furniture, so "for you" doesn't feel true given I'm mid-reno on two houses.
- Card offer shows up mid-email sandwiched between products and another app push — feels like four separate emails stitched together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Aspen collection sale or the Design Desk sweepstakes, not the Levain collab — that's the actual purchase intent for someone renovating, and it's currently buried two scrolls down.
- 2. Cut the redundant app push — it appears twice (hero banner and again near the bottom with "$500 chance"). Pick one placement.
- 3. Swap the "we love these for you" grid to furniture/decor items instead of kitchen tools if this is meant to be personalized — right now it reads like a generic best-sellers block, not "for you."
- 4. Give the Design Desk sweepstakes its own subject line treatment on a future send — that's a stronger persona match than a bakery collab.
