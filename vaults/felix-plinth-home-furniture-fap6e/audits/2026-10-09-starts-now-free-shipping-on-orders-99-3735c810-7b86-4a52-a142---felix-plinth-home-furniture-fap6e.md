# STARTS NOW: Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- Crate again, and this is the second sale-day template in two days. My thumb was already swiping. Between the 10/08 bathroom and "free design help" sends and the three "up to 60% off clearance" emails, I'd stopped reading the sender name. The subject promises free shipping, but the hero leads with "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals." So the email I opened isn't the one the subject described.
- It does have a real offer and a real project fit. Sofas are live for me because I'm outfitting two houses. But 20% off is thin next to Pottery Barn's "up to 70% off" Fall Sale, which I've now seen four or five times since 10/06. The free-shipping threshold is $99, and for furniture that threshold means nothing. Furniture shipping is what I actually worry about, and the footnote says "standard shipping, exclusions apply." That reads like the sofas might be excluded. I'd save this to the Pinterest folder for the sectional shot and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The sectional hero is a good room shot. Warm, neutral and styled, it's the kind of image I'd pin.
- The category grid (Tabletop, Kitchen, Decor, Bedding, Halloween, Thanksgiving) lets me jump to what I'm shopping for.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button is clear and easy to tap.
- The Design Desk block, "your design pro is waiting—and it's 100% free," is the best thing in the email for someone doing two renovations. It's buried near the bottom, though.
- The Reward Dollars card pitch ("Get 10% back") is a rewards-math hook. I'd still just use my highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The subject says free shipping, but the hero says 20% off sofas. The two lead messages don't match.
- The sectional appears twice, as a hero and again mid-email, with the same "up to 20% off" line. It feels like padding.
- The footnote ("standard shipping, exclusions apply") doesn't say whether sofas qualify. The legal block at the bottom is tiny and unreadable on my phone.
- The Wirecutter food processor and the Caraway coffee maker are kitchen items. They're irrelevant to my sofa and renovation interests and make the email feel like a grab bag.
- The product row (juice glasses, bowls, wine glasses) is low-ticket and generic. None of it is renovation-relevant, and the prices are too small to read without zooming.
- It's the same template as the 10/09 clearance sends. Cadence is the real problem.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero match. Either lead with the sofa sale or lead with free shipping, not both.
- 2. State furniture delivery terms next to the hero: white-glove delivery cost, lead time, and whether sectionals are eligible.
- 3. Move the Design Desk block up to second position and tie it to the sectional (free layout help for your room).
- 4. Cut the Wirecutter and Caraway blocks, or segment them to kitchen-affinity subscribers.
- 5. Add fabric swatches ("order free swatches") beside the sofa hero. Pottery Barn and Article both do this better.
- 6. Cut the cadence. One strong send beats a third near-identical promo in a week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas up to 20% off, plus free shipping on $99+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order free fabric swatches and see delivery dates before you buy.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Book a free design appointment with your sectional order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Mapp-style template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) with no confirmed hard defects. The QA run found 0 issues and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The open items are missing alt text on about 27 images, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, and header and link checks that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links.** 61 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed, not failing.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) are in the body. They are extra third-party requests with no `alt`.
- **Subscriber identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` parameter is on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif` URLs. It is a 64-character hash, so it is resolved, not a broken token. It does mean the image URLs are subscriber-specific and can't be cached across recipients.
- **Open pixel.** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/938b7052-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is the open-tracking pixel. It has no alt, which is acceptable for a pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images.** 13 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/...` have no alt, for example `bbf6f9d4-….png`, `d8b928f0-….gif`, `3c9b2911-….jpg` and `971aa10f-….jpg`. These are content images, so the missing alt matters if images are blocked.
- **Missing alt text, product images.** 7 images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png` and others) also have no alt. The `rp` path suggests dynamically recommended products, so they are likely informational images.
- **Missing alt text, pixels.** The 5 `rlcdn` GIFs, `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly. The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from an empty one.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-size adjustment disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` appear alongside `100%` overrides. The conflicting rules are inconsistent.
- **Invalid XHTML.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. It is harmless in most clients.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** There are three `format-detection` metas (`telephone=no,address=no,…`, `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`) plus both `http-equiv` Content-Type and `meta charset`. This is redundant but not breaking.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the shipped source.
- **Dark mode.** `color-scheme: light` and `supported-color-schemes: light` are set, so dark-mode inversion is intentionally suppressed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values are resolved. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** The QA tool did not find either header. It notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`.** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. From `mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the `crateandbarrel.com` image and tracking subdomains, domain alignment looks plausible but is unproven.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address.** These are not in the truncated source, so they can't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed. All 61 tracking links were skipped and the source is truncated before the link block. No UTM evidence is available, and none is flagged as missing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 13 `image.mail…/lib/…` images and the 7 `mi…/p/rp/*.png` product images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` with headers intact, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
4. Resolve a sample of the tracking links by hand to check final URLs, redirect depth and UTM parameters.
5. Escape the title (`&amp;`), dedupe the `format-detection` metas and remove the `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
6. Review whether five LiveRamp pixels per send are intended, since they add third-party requests and privacy surface.
