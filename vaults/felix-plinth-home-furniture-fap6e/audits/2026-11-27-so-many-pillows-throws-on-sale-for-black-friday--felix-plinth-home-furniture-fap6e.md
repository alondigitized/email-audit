- The hero finally matches the subject. Pillows and throws are front and center, with a clear 60% number.
- The extended store hours bar is useful for a showroom shopper like me. Neither Wayfair nor Article offers that.
- The category grid (lighting, rugs, mirrors) gives me a path to the stuff I'm actually renovating with.

- The oxblood and olive palette is consistent and moody. It looks like CB2.

## What's weak

- "It all ships free" in the hero, then "Free shipping on orders of $99 or more" lower down. Which is it? For furniture shipping, that matters.
- The sale is the same one I've been getting since 11/16. The "Black Friday SALE" lettering is spaced so wide it's hard to read quickly.
- "Save 15% off in the new year" reads like a bait-and-wait. It undercuts the urgency of buying today.
- The footer legal block is a wall of tiny gray text. The Crate & Barrel, CB2 and Kids logos remind me I'm in the same family-of-brands pile as the seven other sends this week.
- There's no swatch or sample push and no sign of the shipping timelines I'd want for furniture. The two "SHOP THE SALE" buttons are identical.

- 1. Resolve the shipping message into one clear line. Say what's free and what the threshold is.
- 2. Make the hero's discount legible. Drop the wide letter-spacing and put the offer in a button label like "Shop pillows & throws."
- 3. Link grid tiles to specific products or collections with prices instead of one generic "Shop the Sale" button.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample row and a delivery-date note for furniture.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is a templated XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with `lang="en"` and VML/MSO fallbacks. It has two confirmed code-level problems: 12 content images have no alt text, and 11 Dotomi pixels load over `http://`. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 11 Dotomi requests use plain `http://`: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. They can be blocked as mixed content, and they leak the recipient's request metadata in cleartext. Ten of them are cookie-sync redirects, which is a third-party ad-tech payload with no user-facing function.
- **Unverified links:** 62 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Redirect health and destination validity are unconfirmed.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/d1879d49-…?mi_cid=…` are first-party and use HTTPS. Both have no alt text, which is minor for 1×1 pixels (see Section 3).
