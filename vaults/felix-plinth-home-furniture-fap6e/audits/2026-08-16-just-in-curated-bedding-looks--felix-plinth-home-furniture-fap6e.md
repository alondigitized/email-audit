# Just in! Curated bedding looks
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Discover 1600+ New Arrivals" and a "New Bedding Inspiration" hero — this is Pottery Barn's fourth email to me this week if I'm counting the "Made to Match" beds-and-nightstands one from yesterday, the dining table drop the day before, and the design services RSVP before that. My brain has basically filed Pottery Barn under "check every day, act rarely" at this point. That said, bedding is actually relevant — we're doing the primary bedroom at the second house — so the "clean plaid in warm neutrals" and "antique-inspired indigo toile" duvet shots did make me pause and scroll instead of trashing immediately.
- The problem is there's no offer anywhere. It's four straight "Complete the Look" duvet vignettes with zero percentage-off, zero price anchor, nothing but "All Bedding Ships Free" as the closest thing to a deal. Compare that to Crate & Barrel's Gather Sectional email that actually made me want to screenshot it for my Pinterest folder — that one had a hero product I could picture in the room. This one's just a lookbook wall of duvets that all blur together by the third one.
- Verdict: I'd scroll it once on the couch, maybe screenshot the indigo toile bedding for my folder since it fits the second house's vibe, but I'm not clicking through and definitely not forwarding — nothing here is urgent or differentiated enough to send to my wife or a friend.

## What's working

- The "antique-inspired indigo toile" bedding actually stopped my thumb — good color story, feels like it fits an actual room, not just a catalog flat-lay.
- "All Bedding Ships Free" is a real, honest, no-asterisk shipping line — refreshing after Wayfair's slipcover email buried its terms.
- The category nav strip near the bottom (Sheets / Quilts / Duvet Inserts / Blankets / Pillows / Throws) is genuinely useful if I already know what I'm shopping for.
- "Recommended Just For You" at least gestures at personalization with curtain rods and rugs, even if generic.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, zero price, zero urgency anywhere in this email — for a brand sitting on my inbox four times this week, that's a missed lever compared to CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" which at least led with a number.
- Four nearly identical "Complete the Look" duvet blocks in a row is visual fatigue — by block three I stopped reading captions and just kept scrolling.
- The Halloween Shop banner and Design Crew Sweepstakes tacked onto a bedding email feel like a grab-bag, not a curated send — it dilutes the "new bedding" premise I opened for.
- No swatch/sample-ordering CTA anywhere, which is exactly the kind of decision-support I need mid-renovation and Pottery Barn has pushed in past emails.
- Rewards math ("Earn 30% Back in Rewards") is buried at the very bottom near the app download pitch — should be much higher if they want cardholders like me to notice it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero duvet and an actual price or % off — pick the indigo toile, the strongest visual, and anchor it with a number. Right now there's nothing to click toward.
- 2. Cut the four "Complete the Look" blocks down to two — the plaid and the toile were distinct enough to keep; the flannel charcoal one added nothing new.
- 3. Move the "Earn 30% Back in Rewards" line up near the hero — I have their card, and rewards math is one of the few things that gets me to actually click instead of just browsing.
- 4. Drop the Halloween Shop plug entirely from a bedding-titled email, or split it into its own send — it breaks the "just in" premise.
- 5. Add a swatch/sample ordering CTA under at least one duvet — I'm mid-renovation on two houses and that's the actual friction point for bedding decisions right now.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The indigo toile bedding we can't stop styling`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on every new arrival + order a swatch first`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4 new duvet looks, ready to ship this week`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Mostly clean B&B/EDM template; primary defect is compliance-header related — no List-Unsubscribe/List-Unsubscribe-Post found and widespread missing alt text on image-only content.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 64 tracking/click-redirect links (e.g. `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by HTTP probe per QA, so destination validity is unconfirmed.
- Open-tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (HTTP, not HTTPS) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/24b13fe2-...`.
- A third-party pixel `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is loaded (data/ad-tech tracker).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 40+ `<img>` elements missing `alt` text (product images, banner slices, footer slices, header nav) — screen readers will announce nothing for these; flag as accessibility gap.
- Two images served over plain `http://`: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content may be blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS.
- Stray `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` PHP-style function calls appear inside a `<style>` block (not valid CSS) — dead/leftover code, harmless but indicates template hygiene issue.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — appropriate for email, no issue.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email is hard-embedded in plaintext across several URLs rather than an opaque token, e.g. `...coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — no broken/unresolved merge tags (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) observed in the truncated source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not found (QA WARN) — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level; body-level unsubscribe link not visible in truncated HTML.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo requirement) unconfirmed.
- Authentication-Results header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Plain-text part is 70%+ URLs (11653/16607 chars) — spam-filter risk factor, may reduce inbox placement.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Primary click links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect domain rather than direct UTM-tagged potterybarn.com URLs — actual landing page/UTM parameters are not visible pre-redirect (skipped by QA probe), so continuity cannot be verified from source alone.

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` attributes to all content-bearing images, particularly the 18 `slices_*.jpg` promo images and product shots.
- Migrate the 3 remaining `http://` image sources to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking.
- Confirm List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually being sent by the originating ESP (may be a capture artifact of the relay rather than a true absence) and validate via a raw SMTP/header capture outside the relay.
- Remove dead `header(...)` PHP calls from the `<style>` block.
- Reduce plain-text URL density or add more descriptive text to lower spam-filter risk.
- Spot-check a sample of the 64 redirect links manually to confirm destination and UTM parameters land correctly on potterybarn.com.
