# BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW + 2X REWARDS
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 again, and it's the first of the 11/12 sends where the hero actually changed. The "Long Weekend Sale" banner I've been staring at since 10/30 is gone. It's now "The Early Black Friday Sale, up to 60% off," with "a sneak peek at our best sale of the year." That's a fresh beat, and I noticed it. The catch is that a preview of a sale is not a sale. I'm renovating two houses and I want to know what I can buy today and at what price.
- The email also stacks two different offers. A "Limited Time Now Up To 70% Off Clearance" strip sits above the 60% hero. Then comes a Double Rewards block below, where the "10%" is struck through and replaced with "20% back." That's the one I care about, since I buy on the highest-rewards card. I'd click the rewards block and the Furniture button. I wouldn't forward it. It's a retailer email, not something to send a friend.

## What's working

- The hero finally moved on from the Long Weekend Sale. Early Black Friday fits the date.
- The category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) are big and easy to tap with a thumb. I shop by room, so that works for me.
- The Double Rewards block has a strike-through on 10% and a bold 20%. It's clear, and it's the offer I'd do math on.
- The "So much ships free, 1000+ items" block answers the question I always have on furniture, which is shipping.
- The CB2 app, rewards card, and text-signup tiles at the bottom are tidy. The 15% off for texts is a real hook.

## What's weak

- Two competing percentages are stacked at the top: 70% off clearance, then 60% off the sale. I can't tell which one applies to the lamp I want.
- The hero is a dark bokeh photo with no product in it. It's mood only. Pottery Barn's gift guide at least showed me things.
- The "SHOP THE SALE" button and the five category buttons are the same pale yellow, so nothing leads. There are seven CTAs before the footer.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text. The exclusions look long, and "up to" discounts always make me suspicious.
- No countdown, no hero product, and no sizing or shipping dates. I can't tell what's actually available or when furniture arrives.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hero product with a real price at the top, e.g. "Peekaboo Acrylic Chair, now $X," instead of mood bokeh. That would lift this email the most.
- 2. Lead with one offer. Either the 60% preview or the 70% clearance should be the hero, not both.
- 3. Move the rewards offer up, since I'd act on 20% back immediately. Add the end date, Nov 17, to the headline.
- 4. Cut the five category buttons to two or three, and make the primary button a different color.
- 5. Add a short "what's in the preview" line, e.g. "Early access to 400 items," so the preview feels like a real offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals. It has missing `alt` attributes on 9 content images, 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels, and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. Compliance headers and link health couldn't be verified, because the relay stripped the headers and the 60 tracking links weren't probed. The source is truncated, so the footer and body links weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 60 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure pixels (11):** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1` all use plain HTTP.
  - Gmail's image proxy typically rewrites these, but other clients may block them as mixed content.
  - These are third-party cookie-sync pixels (Dotomi) that run only if the images load.
- **Other pixels over HTTPS:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/2c52dc5d-…?mi_cid=…` are fine on transport.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (9):** All are hosted at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `a3de039b-…jpg`
  - `5870f90f-…jpg`
  - `283f47c4-…jpg`
  - `8352ebb6-…jpg`
  - `02e1fa3e-…jpg`
  - `ef678a06-…jpg`
  - `0d4e3bd6-…jpg`
  - `6d4c8be5-…jpg`
  - `345f2993-…jpg`
  
  If these carry offer or product content, the email loses meaning with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (13):** Dotomi, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com`. These should use `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. A missing attribute can be announced as the filename or URL.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing:** The universal selector `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` disables text-size adjustment on iOS and Windows.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the production HTML (it has a typo, "IMPUT").
- **XHTML well-formedness:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, which is minor under the XHTML doctype.
- **Font URLs:**
  - Some `@font-face` sources use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`).
  - The `HNW05` font loads from `crateandbarrel.com`, a cross-domain dependency.
  - Both have fallbacks, so the impact is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The only substitution seen is the hashed `mi_u=` parameter on `o.gif`, which resolved correctly.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is **unconfirmed** and could be a capture artifact.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN):** The RFC 8058 one-click header wasn't seen, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` can't be confirmed as aligned with the `From` domain.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible in the truncated source. Destination URLs sit behind the 60 skipped click-redirect links, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Tracking identifiers:** The `mi_cid`, `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters identify the recipient and message in the pixel URLs, but nothing here shows whether they carry through to the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images and `alt=""` to all 13 tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or remove them. They're 11 HTTP requests, and many clients drop them anyway.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
4. Delete the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment from the template.
5. Re-run the check against the raw `.eml` from the origin MTA, or with a direct-to-inbox seed, to confirm:
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers
   - SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment
6. Resolve a sample of the 60 tracking links through the redirect chain to confirm HTTP 200 destinations and UTM carry-through.
7. Confirm the footer has a working unsubscribe link and a postal address. This needs the full, untruncated HTML.
