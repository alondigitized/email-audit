## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Up to 40% off faves from KitchenAid, Caraway & more + 2x Rewards!"

The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head, the QA output, and the URLs the QA listed.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML fallbacks and `lang="en"`. Its main confirmed defects are about 50 `<img>` elements without alt text, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and a plain-text part that is over 70% URLs. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture at the relay and are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not verified.** 100 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chain length and 404s were not tested.
- **Plain-text bloat.** 20,156 of 28,625 characters (70.4%) in the plain-text part are URLs. This is just over the 70% threshold and suggests long tracked URLs are printed inline. It can slightly raise spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.
- **Repeated third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are five copies of the same LiveRamp pixel with an incrementing counter. This adds redundant third-party requests, and the `s=` value is a recipient-specific hash.
- **Ad-hoc tracker domains.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com` (`/p/rp/*.png`, `/p/up/…/o.gif`) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c9d3dbe1-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are first-party tracking pixels. All three carry per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u=56aaa278…`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 50 images).** The QA flagged:
  - 36 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`, e.g. `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `24335f96-f5a7-410d-9964-4b9f706534b4.gif`.
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, 5 `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixel.
  - The content images are a real accessibility and images-off problem. If the product and promo images are not wrapped in text links, a screen reader or an images-blocked client gets nothing.
  - The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. The QA cannot distinguish a missing `alt` attribute from an empty one.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom on clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. It should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` are both declared.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with conflicting content.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a typo and unfilled placeholder shipped in production.
- **Empty `<style>` rule block** in `<!--[if mso]>`: `td,th,div,p,a { }` is a no-op.
- **Image width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap and are order-dependent, so the 640px rule can override the 600px one. This is a real inconsistency, but I can't confirm it affects rendering without the full source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source or QA output. The only per-recipient values are the resolved `mi_u=` and `s=` hashes in pixel URLs. The rest of the body was not available, so this category is not fully verifiable.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post are not found.** The QA itself says they "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed sender defect. If the sender really omits them, there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results is absent.** This is expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign but not evidence of a pass.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** They are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Insufficient evidence. All 100 tracked links were skipped, so UTM parameters, final destinations and landing-page alignment with the "up to 40% off KitchenAid, Caraway" offer and the "2x Rewards" claim could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to every content image (the 36 `image.mail…/m/1/*` assets) and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels (`mi.`, `dv.`, `sr.rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, de-duplicate the `charset` and `format-detection` metas, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Collapse the five identical `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels to one, or confirm that each `n` value is needed.
5. Shorten tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of links, to bring URL density below 70%.
6. To close the unverified items:
   - Re-capture the raw `.eml` with full headers to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
   - Run a redirect-following probe on a sample of the 100 tracking links to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
