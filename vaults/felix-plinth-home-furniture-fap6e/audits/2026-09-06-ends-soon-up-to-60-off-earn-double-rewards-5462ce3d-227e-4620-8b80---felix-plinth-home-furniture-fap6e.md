# ENDS SOON: Up to 60% off + earn Double Rewards→
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again. That's — I've lost count, but I'm pretty sure this is my fourth or fifth from this specific sub-brand this week, and my gut reaction is the same one-second delete I gave the last one: I don't have a baby, I don't shop for nursery furniture, and this whole email — kids dressers, crib bedding, "Babyletto bestsellers," strollers — is just not my life right now. The reno work I'm actually doing is on kitchens and living spaces for the second house.
- What almost saved it was the "Double Rewards" banner up top, since I do chase rewards math on my highest-cash-back card, but even that's wrapped around a nursery sale, so it doesn't pull me in. Compare that to yesterday's actual Crate & Barrel "Double Rewards on bestsellers" send — sofas, dining tables, bedroom sets — that one at least lived in my category.
- Verdict: delete, not even a skim-scroll. Definitely not forwarding this — the one friend I'd forward baby stuff to already has two kids and gets her own spam from this brand, I'm sure.

## What's working

- The hero banner is clean — "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" is legible at a glance, no clutter.
- Double Rewards callout is placed high, which is the one hook that could pull a rewards-card guy like me in regardless of category.
- Grid layout for the sale categories (kids furniture, bedding, nursery chairs, rugs) is organized and scannable, no broken images.
- "The Design Desk" free design consultation block is a nice trust-builder if you're actually furnishing a room — just the wrong room for me.

## What's weak

- Everything after the hero is nursery/kids-specific — cribs, strollers, car seats, "loved by parents" — zero overlap with a childless renovator like me.
- No kitchen, no living room, no general home category cross-sell at all, unlike the C&B kitchen brands email that at least put an espresso machine in front of me.
- The star rating icon in the "loved by parents" section reads as a mismatched font glyph, kind of a visual stumble.
- This is a segmentation failure at its core — Crate & Barrel clearly knows my order/browse history skews kitchen and living, yet Crate & Kids keeps firing at me like I'm a new parent.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the segmentation, not the creative.** If I've never bought or browsed a single kids item, stop sending me Crate & Kids entirely — this is the single biggest lever, bigger than any copy tweak. Pottery Barn's problem is repetition of the *same* email to me; this is worse, it's the *wrong* email category altogether.
- 2. Lead any cross-sell with the Double Rewards program itself, not the nursery sale — that's the one universal hook that applies to every Crate & Barrel family shopper, kids or not.
- 3. If Crate & Kids must email broad lists, add a "shop the rest of Crate & Barrel" module up top for accounts with no kids-category history.
- 4. Tighten the "loved by parents" star icon rendering — small polish issue but it's the kind of thing that undercuts trust at a glance.
