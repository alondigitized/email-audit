# Start nesting now with fast-ship nursery finds!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- I'm 36 and redoing two houses, and I don't have a baby on the way. This was never meant for me. It's the seventh Crate & Barrel send I've seen in two days, and the second one today from a different sub-brand. This morning's "Last call! Up to 30% off The Dining Room Event ends TONIGHT" actually matched my project. This one is a long scroll of cribs, car seats and strollers.
- The email itself is tidy. The hero is a warm, neutral nursery shot with a "Delivers as fast as 7 days" line. Below it is a clean two-column category grid, a Doona car seat feature, and a "we love these for you" product row. If I were nesting, I'd probably spend a few minutes here. As a guy picking out dining chairs and a dresser for the primary and the second home, I scroll past it. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it, unless a friend's expecting and I'm feeling generous.

## What's working

- The hero is calm and warm-toned. The "Delivers as fast as 7 days" line gives a real shipping promise, and I notice that for furniture.
- The category grid (chairs, cribs, dressers, changing tables) is easy to scan, and each tile says what it is. Crate & Barrel is more organized here than Wayfair, whose last blank-hero send had nothing to scan.
- "Ships free" labels on bedding, blankets and car seats cut shipping friction.
- The Design Desk "free" module and the "find your nearest store" link are a real bridge to the showroom. That matters to someone like me.
- The product row shows prices on actual items, so I can see what I'd pay.

## What's weak

- It's a nursery email, and I'm furnishing dining rooms and living rooms. The subject says "nesting," which isn't my focus, and nothing in the email reflects what I've been browsing.
- The "up to 60% off" clearance block sits more than halfway down, and it doesn't say what's actually discounted. Pottery Barn's maroon "Extra 20% off" strip puts its offer at the top, and that's better.
- This is the seventh Crate send in two days. The brand-family cadence is wearing thin, and CB2 was the same story with four sends in two days.
- The prices in the product row and the Doona feature copy are tiny on a phone. I'd have to zoom.
- There's a Christmas "magic they'll never forget" holiday block in a nursery email, and the whole thing runs very long. The app, the credit card and Design Desk modules all compete for attention near the bottom.
- The fine print is a dense wall of text, with several promo terms stacked together. That makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the clearance offer to a banner right under the hero and name the categories it covers. A specific "up to 60% off" is a stronger reason to click than a vague one.
- 2. Segment this. Send nursery content to people who've browsed nursery or registry pages, and send me dining, dressers and decor. I'm the guy who opened this morning's dining room send.
- 3. Cut the page to one hero, the category grid, one feature and one product row. Drop the card, app and Design Desk modules, or move them to a separate send.
- 4. Raise the price and product-name type size in the product row.
- 5. Cap Crate & Barrel family sends per day, so Kids, CB2 and the main brand don't all land on the same afternoon.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cribs, dressers & chairs, delivered fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on bedding and blankets, plus up to 60% off clearance.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fast-ship cribs, glider chairs and dressers, ready for the new arrival.`
