# Our most-loved bedroom collections
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Pottery Barn bedroom send in two days, and I liked it more than the last one. "Our Bestselling Beds" is a clear hero. Three beds sit one after another, each with its own finish swatches: Sausalito Cane, Farmhouse, Cayman. I'm putting beds in two houses, so I read it like a catalog page. The swatches let me compare wood tones without clicking, and I like that.
- There is still no offer. No percent off, no free-shipping line, no deadline. The only money talk is buried down in the "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" card block and the Affirm module. After the 10/16 "Love pattern?" send, which was the best of the week, and this morning's "Just in!" holiday arrivals email, I feel I'm getting the same catalog in a different order. The email is also very long. The bottom half is a dining table, a curtain rod, a rug, an app download and an Affirm pitch, and the bedroom story gets lost.
- Would I click? Probably one tap, on the Cayman bed, and I'd save the email to my inspiration folder. I wouldn't forward it. A bestseller list with no price anywhere gives me nothing to compare against Crate & Barrel or Article.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "Our Bestselling Beds" with a big SHOP NOW button over a warm bedroom shot. I understood it in about two seconds.
- Each bed has its own colour swatches, which is the closest thing to a swatch-and-sample experience I've seen from them. It helps me plan a palette across two houses.
- The one-bed-per-block layout is easy to scan on a phone, and the buttons are big enough to tap.
- "More for your bedroom" (nightstands, dressers, bedding, rugs) is a sensible cross-sell for someone furnishing a whole room.
- The photography is consistent and calm: wood, linen, neutral tones. It's the kind of thing I'd pin.

## What's weak

- There are no prices and no offer on any of the beds. "Bestselling" with no number is just a brochure. CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" at least gave me a reason to look.
- Furniture shipping is missing. This morning's send had the "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1…" line, and this one dropped it. For a bed, delivery time matters as much as the finish.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block is a dining table, a curtain rod and a rug, and none of them is a bed. It doesn't feel personal to me. It feels like filler.
- The email is very long, and the credit card, app and Affirm blocks at the bottom pile on. The legal and fine print is tiny and unreadable without zooming.
- The cadence is heavy. The 10/16 "Love pattern?" send, the 10/16 "living room" send, this morning's holiday arrivals and now this make four bedroom and holiday sends in two days. Wayfair does volume too, but at least its ice maker email had "up to 70% OFF" in the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real number in the hero, for example a bed event with a percent off or a "free shipping on beds through Sunday" line. That is the single biggest lift. Right now the offer lives in the rewards card block at the bottom.
- 2. Add a price and a delivery window on each bed block, as in "From $1,499 · ships in 2 weeks". I'd then compare it against Crate & Barrel without leaving the email.
- 3. Replace "Recommended Just For You" with items that fit the story: bedding, nightstands and a rug in the same finishes as the beds above.
- 4. Cut the bottom third. Pick one of the app, the credit card or Affirm, not all three. Move the 10% rewards line up near the beds, because that's the math I actually do when I choose a card.
- 5. Use a swatch-mailing link ("Order free swatches") next to the colour dots. It's a real benefit and it's missing here.
- 6. Space out the sends. Don't send me another bedroom email tomorrow.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Compare finishes and delivery times, then earn 10% back with the Pottery Barn card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sausalito, Farmhouse, Cayman: pick your wood tone before the holiday rush.`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Our most-loved bedroom collections"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email built from sliced images. It has widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, and the recipient's email address in cleartext in tracking-pixel URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, because the relay probably didn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 54 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...` on the logo. Destination, status and redirect chain are unverified.
- **Third-party and legacy pixels:**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is an open-tracking pixel over plain HTTP.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d8dd8140-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a second open pixel.
  - That makes three separate open-tracking pixels. Any of them blocked or slow adds no visible content but adds requests.
- **Stale or cross-brand assets:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's domain. If that host changes, the spacer breaks.
  - Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, the production site, instead of the EDM CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** roughly 55 images are flagged. Examples:
  - `F26_FURN_B12_CoreBedroomCollections/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_13.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`
  - `H26_Top_ChristmasShop.jpg`

  The core content is image slices with no alt text, so it is effectively invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader. Tracking pixels also lack `alt=""`, which should be explicit.
- **Mixed content:** three images use `http://`, which clients may block or flag:
  - `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. It is invalid CSS and can break rule parsing in strict clients.
- **Global CSS:** `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are overly broad.
- **Layout and external dependencies:**
  - Spacer tables lack `role="presentation"`, while others have it.
  - The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by many clients, so it is a dependency with no benefit there.
  - Media queries use attribute selectors (`table[class="wrapper"]`) and a `min-width: 700px` rule, `img {max-width: 650px}`. Support for these varies by client.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content slice and product image. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Move all `http://` assets to HTTPS, including `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_..._slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in URLs, and use an opaque or hashed subscriber ID instead. Do this first for the HTTP pixel.
4. Remove the stray PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block, set a non-empty `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Host all creative assets on the owned EDM CDN, not `edm.westelm.com`, the production site or the 2022 folder.
6. Consolidate the three open-tracking pixels, and drop the legacy `d.turn.com` one if it is unused.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message at the original receiving mailbox, not through the relay.
8. Follow a sample of the tracked links manually to confirm destination status and UTM parameters.
