- 2. If Design Crew and Halloween have to stay, move them into their own dedicated campaigns — don't tack a sweepstakes and a Halloween teaser onto a vase email, it splits my attention right when I'm ready to click.
- 3. Make "Recommended Just For You" actually personalized — if I've been browsing dining or bedding, fine, show me that, but label it or ditch the "for you" framing.
