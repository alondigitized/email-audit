# ENDS TODAY: Last Double Rewards of the year!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction is that the deadline is real this time. It's the last Double Rewards of the year, and I put everything on the highest-rewards card, so 2x back on the sofa and rug I'm already pricing out gets me to at least skim it. Today's earlier sends were the dining room and Retreat emails, which were actually for me. This one is the same stuff with an urgency label on it.
- Then I scroll, and keep scrolling. It runs about a dozen modules deep: kitchen, gift shop, bedroom, dining, sofas, rugs, bedding, lighting, espresso machines, cookware. Pottery Barn's maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner at least tells me the offer once and lets me leave. Here the offer is a rewards multiplier, so every module has to repeat it, and the repeats turn into small-print noise.
- I'd open it, and I'd probably click one of the furniture modules (sofas or rugs) before I hit the kitchen section. I wouldn't forward it. It's good for me but it isn't special, and I'm getting this much Crate & Barrel just from the past two days.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a maroon block with the logo, "ends today: last Double Rewards this year," and the 25%-back-in-Reward-Dollars mechanic.
- The "ends today" deadline is credible. It's the last rewards event of the year, not a fake countdown.
- The room-based modules (bedroom, dining, sofas, rugs) match how I'm shopping two renovations, one room at a time.
- Each module has a specific CTA like "Shop Bedroom," "Shop Dining Furniture," "Shop Sofas & Sectionals," or "Shop Rugs." Those are verb-plus-noun, not "Discover."
- The thin strip at the top about ordering by 11/18 to get it by Thanksgiving is useful, though I'd rather it said that about furniture.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. By the time I reach the espresso machines and cookware at the bottom, I've forgotten the hero offer.
- "Earn 2x / 25% back" repeats above module after module in tiny type. At phone size I'd have to zoom to read it, and the screenshot shrinks it further.
- Two buttons sit under the hero ("Shop Top Categories" and "Learn More"). "Shop Top Categories" is the vague kind of CTA I skip. I'd want a sofa, not a category.
- Furniture shipping and lead times aren't visible anywhere. For a $3k sectional, I need to know whether it lands before the holidays, or what the rewards math means on a big ticket.
- The kitchen block (KitchenAid, All-Clad, espresso machines, cookware) feels tacked on. I'm renovating, but I'm not buying a stand mixer from an email about rewards.
- It's the fifth Crate & Barrel send since 11/16. Even the good ones are blending together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to the hero plus three or four modules in the order I'm most likely to buy: sofas, rugs, dining, bedroom. Drop the kitchen and gift modules, or push them to a separate send.
- 2. Show the rewards benefit once, big, with a rewards-math line, e.g. "$3,000 sofa = $750 back." Rewards people like me do that math anyway.
- 3. Replace "Shop Top Categories / Learn More" with a single "Shop sofas, rugs & dining" button, or link the hero straight to a rewards-eligible product page.
- 4. Add shipping reality for furniture. "In-stock sofas ship by 12/10" would do more than any module headline.
- 5. Stop sending a rewards-deadline email and a Black Friday email on the same day. Merge them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 2x Rewards on sofas, rugs & dining`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight: 25% back in Reward Dollars`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture that ships before the holidays. Double Rewards end at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, rugs and dining sets all count. Earn 2x before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML Transitional email on an Oracle/Marigold-style tracking stack (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). The main confirmed issue is widespread missing image alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and are inconclusive. The HTML is truncated, so body links and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **136 links were skipped by the HTTP probe** because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains, and final URLs are unverified.
- **Five third-party pixels** point to `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. `rlcdn.com` is LiveRamp. Each pixel carries the same identifier token with no `alt`. This is third-party identity-sync tracking, which affects privacy and image-blocking behavior.
- **Recipient-identifying tokens in image URLs:**
  - `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`.
  - `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` on `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/165f0a26-…`.
- **Plain-text/URL ratio:** the QA check reports 26,924 of 36,671 characters (73%) of the plain-text part are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk. It likely comes from long tracked URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 85 images.** Roughly 70 are content images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (`.jpg` and `.gif`) plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`. Some are product or promo imagery, so with images blocked or a screen reader on, those blocks have no text equivalent. The check does not distinguish a missing attribute from `alt=""`. The remaining items are tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `/p/rp/*.png`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`), where empty alt is acceptable.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">`** disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`** appears on `*` and `body` and overrides the `100%` set elsewhere. It blocks text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **Unescaped `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. Browsers and clients tolerate it.
- **Template residue and redundancy:**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the source.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times, and `ReadMsgBody` and `ExternalClass` rules are duplicated across style blocks.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice, which is redundant but harmless.
  - These add bytes, and the email is already large: the plain-text part alone is about 36 KB.
- **Email size:** with about 136 links and 80+ images, the HTML is likely near Gmail's ~102 KB clipping threshold. This is an inference, since only the truncated source was available, so confirm it against the full message.
