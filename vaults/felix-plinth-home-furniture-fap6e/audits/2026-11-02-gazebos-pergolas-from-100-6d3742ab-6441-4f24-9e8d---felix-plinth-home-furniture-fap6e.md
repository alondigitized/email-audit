## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's links and tracking are structurally consistent: all 12 probed links returned 429 rate-limiting, which does not indicate a broken link. The confirmed defects are about 20 tracking pixels served over plain `http://`, widespread missing alt text, and one malformed image URL. Compliance headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No hard failures.** All 8 flagged probes returned 429, including:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se81255.html?eid=row_0_Grid-100&...`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`

  These are bot-throttling responses. Link validity is unconfirmed, not failed.
- **4 links were not checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/top-rated-kitchen-mats-se26540.html`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-nightstands-se44429.h…`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/top-brands%3a-kitchen-sinks-se16242.`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/quilt-sets-you%27ll-love-se56866.htm`

  Of the 61 links, 49 were skipped as low priority, so most of the email is unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 `<img>` tags (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, `sz=2x1`) use `http://`. Clients that block mixed content or rewrite images over a proxy may drop them, which would undercount impressions.
- **Insecure app-link fallback.** The OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. This is a non-HTTPS fallback and adds a redirect hop.
- **Malformed percent-encoding.** The image URL `.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not a valid escape. Some CDNs or clients may return an error or fail to load it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `refid=MKTEML_121730`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16640`, `csnid=…`, `varid=6`, `sltid=…`. This works if Wayfair's analytics reads them, but it is not interoperable with standard GA or UTM reporting.
- **Campaign-label mismatch.** The app link carries `c=Triggered`, while the same email is tagged `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=1`. A batch send labelled as triggered may skew campaign segmentation.
- **Landing-page match.** The subject, "Gazebos & pergolas from $100", matches the destination slug `gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se81255.html`, with `eid=row_0_Grid-100` and `unifiedeventid=569893`. I couldn't confirm the landing page's content because of the 429s.
- **Two app links duplicate the same destination.** Both "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links point to the same OneLink target and differ only in `_eml`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to `https://` and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the linked hero and banner images, and put the disclaimer in live text rather than `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`.
3. Fix the `%oo` in the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` URL by encoding it correctly or renaming the asset.
4. Change the OneLink `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`, and reconcile `c=Triggered` with `source=graymatter_batch`.
5. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that the proprietary parameters feed the analytics pipeline.
6. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message at the source or in a direct inbox. Don't rely on the relay copy.
7. Re-probe the 4 unchecked links and the 429 links with throttling or a longer budget before judging link health. The 57% pass rate is driven mainly by 429s and unprobed links, not confirmed breakage.
