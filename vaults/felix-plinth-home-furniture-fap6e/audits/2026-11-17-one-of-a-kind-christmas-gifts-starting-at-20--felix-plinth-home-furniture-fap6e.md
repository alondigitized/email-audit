## What's weak

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (XHTML doctype, `lang="en"`, MSO/VML fallbacks), but about 50 images have no alt text, and the viewport meta tag blocks zoom. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified. The QA checker reports a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The HTML source was truncated, so the body and footer were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **97 tracking links were not probed.** They all use click-redirect domains, so landing destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **Many third-party and first-party tracking pixels, all with no alt attribute:**
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5`: five LiveRamp pixels.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`: repeated six times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`: the open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/89133d9f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` token appears in the image query strings. This is a persistent per-recipient identifier, and the repeated pixels add load for no reported benefit.
- **Cross-environment assets.** Two images come from `s7d5.scene7.com` (the Scene7 CDN), not the ESP image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. One is `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`, whose filename carries an October (`1002`) date in a November send. This may be a stale or reused asset.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA output lists about 50 images. Most are `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/<uuid>.jpg` (for example `73298d08-…jpg`, `84df5f56-…jpg`). Some are animated GIFs (`8c4ce5d9-…gif`, `f8c48ff1-…gif`) and the Scene7 hero video poster `2026%5F1104%5FCBK%5FQualityGifting%5FHeroVideo%5F2`. With images blocked or a screen reader in use, the product grid is unlabelled. Pixels and spacers should use `alt=""`, and content images need descriptive alt text.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Markup errors.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times with conflicting values.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production, with an empty `<!---->` after it.
- **Generic title.** The `<title>` is the brand name only and doesn't reflect the email content.
- **Global resets.** `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are applied to every element and paragraph. These could cause unexpected rendering in some clients, but that can't be confirmed from the visible source.
- **No dark-mode support.** `color-scheme: light` is declared, so dark-mode clients may auto-invert instead of using a designed dark theme.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: no unresolved merge tokens (such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear. The truncated body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect. Check the original message headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be verified. The same relay caveat applies.
- **Footer not verified.** The footer (unsubscribe link, physical postal address) is past the truncation point, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unconfirmed.
- **Sender consistency.** The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains. No mismatch found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The destination URLs and any UTM parameters are hidden behind the 97 unprobed redirect links, and the visible source doesn't show the final landing pages. The subject ("starting at $20") could not be checked against landing-page pricing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text.** Give every content image a descriptive `alt` and every pixel or spacer `alt=""`. The `.gif` and video-poster images need alt text that conveys what they show.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Fix the template.** Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), deduplicate the `format-detection` metas, remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment, and use a descriptive `<title>`.
4. **Check the original message headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results to settle the three QA warnings.
5. **Consolidate the tracking pixels** (the six `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels and five `rlcdn` pixels), or confirm each one is needed.
6. **Review the stale October asset** (`2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide`) in this November send.
7. **Improve QA coverage.** Resolve redirect chains to the final URL and check UTM parameters. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer and merge tokens.
