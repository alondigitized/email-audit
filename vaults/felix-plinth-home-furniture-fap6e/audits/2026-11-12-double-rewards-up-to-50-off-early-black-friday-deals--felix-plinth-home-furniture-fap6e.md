# Double Rewards + Up to 50% off Early Black Friday deals!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Crate & Barrel family send I've gotten today. It's from "Crate & Barrel Kids SALE", so it's the kids' arm of a brand I shop for furniture. I'm furnishing two houses and neither one has a nursery, so my gut reaction was "wrong list, same promo." I also don't trust the hero. The "up to 50% off" headline sits on top of a dark crib photo with what looks like a second ghosted copy of the text behind it, so it's muddy. The red "Double Rewards" banner above it is the only clean piece of the offer.
- The body is a long scroll: category tiles, a Jenny Lind bed push, a repeated Double Rewards banner, a Feeding America block, a product grid of strollers and car seats, a Crate Card pitch, an app download, and a design-service ad. It's the same Early Black Friday story as 11/11 and the Hanukkah and "bestsellers" sends this morning. The only new angle is the kids-only skew, and I'm not the audience for it.
- I wouldn't click. I'd archive it, and I wouldn't forward it. If a friend with a baby were expecting, I might screenshot the Jenny Lind bed.

## What's working

- The "Earn 10% back in Reward Dollars" banner is clear and red, and it's the one readable offer. As someone who buys on rewards, I clocked it immediately.
- The four-tile "up to 50% off" grid (furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs) is easy to scan. Each tile carries the same number, so there's no math.
- The "Jenny Lind beds, up to 20% off" block is a real hero with a real product, a short line of copy and a single "Shop Now" button.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices ($599, $1,499 and so on) and named products. That's the kind of detail I'd actually compare.
- The Feeding America block is a nice human break from the sale stack.

## What's weak

- The top hero is a mess. The "Early Black Friday is here! Up to 50% off" text overlaps and looks doubled over a busy crib photo. The headline of the whole send is hard to read, and I only see that bug in this one block.
- The "Shop the Sale" button sits on that same dark photo and gets lost.
- It's a kids-only sender, so nothing here speaks to a guy renovating two homes. There's no sofa, dining table or lighting, only cribs, strollers and car seats.
- The rewards banner appears twice, and the Crate Card pitch is a third reward-related ask. The email is long and repetitive.
- The legal fine print blocks at the top and bottom are dense and tiny, and unreadable on a phone without zooming. It's the "honest offer" problem: 50% off, 20% off and 10% back are all carrying asterisks I can't read.
- Seven or eight C&B-family emails in three days, including the 11/12 Hanukkah and "bestsellers" sends, makes this feel like noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Put the "Up to 50% off" headline on a flat color block with a high-contrast button, not over a photo with ghosted text.
- 2. Segment. If I've never bought kids' items, don't send me the Kids sender. Fall back to the main Crate & Barrel furniture send, like the 11/11 "Up to 30% off furniture" one, which was the only one I wanted this week.
- 3. Collapse the repeated rewards banners into one, and put the earn math in plain words, for example "$100 spent = $20 back."
- 4. Cut the length by about a third. Drop the Feeding America block and the design-service ad from a sale send.
- 5. Move the fine print into a short summary line with a link, and make the deadline explicit.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double Rewards on cribs, rugs & bedding — through 11/17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off nursery + kids' furniture, plus 10% back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jenny Lind beds up to 20% off. Earn 10% back in Reward Dollars through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Strollers, rugs and bedding, all with Double Rewards. Ends soon.`
- Compared with the competition, Pottery Barn's gift-guide send at least put one clean green "Up to 70% off" strip at the top. Wayfair's 72-hour clearout banner is annoying, but I can read it. Article's black "Sale, Coming Soon" hero was empty, but it was legible. CB2's countdown clock is more urgent than anything here.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Double Rewards + Up to 50% off Early Black Friday deals!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is truncated, and 74 tracking links were not probed, so link destinations, the footer and the unsubscribe link could not be verified. The confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 33 images) and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 74 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so there is no HTTP status or final-destination data for any CTA.
- **Third-party beacons:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels load from LiveRamp. They carry a hashed per-recipient identifier in `s=`.
- **First-party beacons:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ff8d12b6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and tracking pixels. Neither is an error, but both need `alt=""` (see §3).
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, about 33 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `26171910-…jpg`, `af1c5ddd-…jpg`, `8c4ce5d9-…gif`) have no alt. Image blocking or screen readers will show nothing for them.
  - `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png` and `971aa10f-….jpg` are also missing alt.
  - Spacer `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` has no alt.
  - Pixels and beacons (`f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4. Combined with `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`, text cannot be resized.
- **Conflicting media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype.
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - The `<title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are populated, so personalization IDs are resolving. The truncated source means the footer and body copy could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check them against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` should be checked directly against its DNS and the raw headers.
- **Footer not verifiable:** the physical address and in-body unsubscribe link are beyond the truncation point.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 74 links are click-redirect URLs with no resolved destination, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Early Black Friday / Double Rewards" offer are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Add `alt=""` to the spacer, pixels and beacons.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Remove the conflicting `max-width:600px` or `640px` `img` rule so a single desktop cap applies.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message. Rerun QA with full source if the relay is dropping headers.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links in a follow-up run to check UTMs and landing pages.
6. Clean up the duplicate `format-detection` metas, the unescaped `&` in the title, and the stray "IMPUT HERE" template comment.
