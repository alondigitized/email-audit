# Selling fast: Open Box Outlet Deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Pottery Barn send in three days, and this one has a hook. I'm furnishing two houses, so open-box outlet deals interest me. Returned floor-model sofas and dining tables at a discount is how I'd actually save money. The red hero says "Open Box Outlet Deals" and "Save big with on-sale items that have been opened and returned," and I get it right away.
- Then the email lets me down. "Save big" has no number on it, and nothing in the hero shows an open-box product. Below it comes the Free Design Services banner, which is the same message as yesterday's "Free design appointments! Spots go fast." The "Recommended just for you" grid is stockings, curtain rods, a rug and a quilt, with a dining table as the only furniture. None of it looks like outlet or open-box stock. The Christmas Shop block that closes it out is a repeat of the 11/05 launch email.
- I'd tap through to see whether the outlet page has real furniture. I wouldn't forward it. It goes in the folder for later, and honestly I'd probably skim past it. I'd rather see the actual deals than be told they exist.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Open Box Outlet Deals" in big serif type on deep red, with one SHOP NOW button, is easy to read on a phone.
- "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" in the top bar speaks to my renovation timeline. Furniture lead times are my biggest worry, and PB at least addresses it.
- The Banks Extendable Dining Table (76"–128") is a real furniture item with a size range. It's the one product that looks like my project.
- The rewards and Affirm blocks are easy to find. The 10% back card offer does the rewards math I care about, since I buy with the highest-rewards card.
- The email follows its own hierarchy: hero, then services, then a product grid, then the Christmas Shop.

## What's weak

- "Save big" gives me no percentage, no dollar figure and no "up to X% off." Crate & Barrel's "Custom furniture made easy" email at least led with sofas I could act on.
- No open-box product appears in the hero or the grid. The grid looks like an ordinary recommendations module, so I can't tell what the outlet actually stocks.
- The Free Design Services banner repeats the 11/06 "Free design appointments" send. I've now seen that message twice in two days.
- The grid is mostly Christmas stockings and the Christmas Shop banner again. It dilutes the outlet message and pushes toward seasonal decor, which isn't what I came for.
- The product cards have no prices, no "was/now," and no condition notes. A deal email without prices isn't a deal email.
- The legal block at the bottom is dense and tiny. The outlet's "final sale" rules are probably buried in it. I'd want that stated up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number and real products in the hero, for example "Open-box sofas, tables & rugs, up to 40% off," with 2–3 outlet items and their prices shown.
- 2. Replace the stockings and curtain rods with open-box furniture cards. Show was/now pricing and a short condition line such as "Open box – like new."
- 3. State the return and final-sale terms in one line near the CTA so I don't have to dig for them.
- 4. Drop the repeated design-services banner and the Christmas Shop block from the same week's sends, or rotate them. This is the fourth PB email in three days.
- 5. Make "Selling fast" credible with a stock count or a visible deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open-box sofas & tables, up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Outlet finds: returned furniture, marked way down`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Like-new dining tables, rugs and beds. Once they're gone, they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Open-box furniture, plus in-stock pieces that ship in 1–4 weeks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-built (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style) HTML template with fully wrapped tracking links. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, cleartext HTTP assets, the recipient's email address exposed in pixel URLs, and leaked server-side code in a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified. QA: 57% pass rate, 0 errors, 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **43 links could not be probed.** They all use click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Mixed-protocol tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is loaded over plain HTTP, so some clients may block it and open tracking may be undercounted.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-tech pixel with no `alt`.
- **Cross-brand asset dependency.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from a West Elm host. If that asset is removed, the layout spacing breaks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 35 images.** This includes content images:
  - `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`
  - `slices_01.jpg` (Open Box Deals)
  - `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`
  - `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`
  - `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`
  - `open-box-cozy-cloud-...`
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`
  - 18 footer slices, `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`, under `/2026/US/Email-Footer/`

  With images blocked, the email is largely unreadable, and screen readers will announce filenames or URLs. The pixels and spacers (`spacer_0.gif`, `coreemail.html`, `svopn`, `clickcp`, `d.turn.com`) should carry `alt=""`.
- **HTTP image sources.** These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Leaked server-side code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS from a pasted PHP snippet, and parsers will discard it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Image-based layout.** The footer is 18 sliced images, which is fragile when images are off. From the truncated source I can't confirm whether it has live text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Email address exposed in URLs.** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second one is sent over cleartext HTTP. Neither is hashed, so it is a PII leak risk through server logs and proxies.
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source. The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo one-click unsubscribe requirements for bulk senders are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these sit in the truncated portion, so I can't verify them. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the tracking domain, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** Every CTA is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` token and the redirect targets were not probed. No UTM presence or landing-page match is confirmed.
- **Asset path dates.** Hero assets sit under `/2025/US/F25_Sale_B2_OpenBoxDeals/`, and a footer banner uses `/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`. Reuse of old assets is not an error in itself, but the 2022 banner is served over HTTP.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images and the footer slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Move all `http://` assets to HTTPS: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in the `coreemail.html` and `sv_sveme` query strings. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message headers, since the relay may be hiding them.
7. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 43 tracking links to verify the final URLs, UTMs and landing pages.
8. Host the spacer image on `potterybarn.com` rather than `edm.westelm.com`.
