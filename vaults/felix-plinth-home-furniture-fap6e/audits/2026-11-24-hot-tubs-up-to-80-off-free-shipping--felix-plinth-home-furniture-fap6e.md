# HOT TUBS ⚫ UP TO 80% OFF ⚫ FREE SHIPPING ⚫ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and I'm numb. The subject promised hot tubs, and I got exactly one: a gray tub in the top-left tile of a "picked just for you" grid. Next to it is a front door. Below that are a gazebo, a barrel sauna, a fridge and Christmas lights. This is the same Black Friday blast I've had since 11/09. It landed right behind "SHELVING & RACKS 🎉 UP TO 80% OFF 🎉" and "DURABLE RUGS worth the click" on 11/23, and "BARS & BAR SETS" on 11/22. The "just for you" label isn't true. It's the same pile of random tiles with a new headline on top.
- I'm renovating two houses, so some of this is almost useful. Area rugs at 80% off, bedding at 70% off and mattresses at 60% off are in my lane. The recliner is not. The massage chair is not. The "FREE SHIPPING*" banner with an asterisk is also not the same as a clear furniture delivery promise.
- I'd skim it, maybe save the rugs block to my folder, and mark it read. I wouldn't click through for the hot tub, and I wouldn't forward it. If I did forward it, it'd be as a joke.

## What's working

- The four-box "up to 80% / 45% / 70% / 60% off" grid (area rugs, Christmas trees, bedding, mattresses) is the cleanest part. The big percentages and red buttons are easy to read on a phone.
- The purple "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" band is hard to miss. Free shipping on big items matters when I'm furnishing two houses.
- The purple header with "80% OFF BLACK FRIDAY" and the Sealy co-brand tells me right away what the sale is.
- Product tiles show "Was / NOW" pricing and star ratings, so I can judge a deal without clicking.
- The footer has Wayfair Rewards, Pay Over Time and Wayfair Professional. That's the right kind of trade and financing signal for someone doing two renovations.

## What's weak

- The subject says HOT TUBS and the body doesn't deliver. One tub tile sits among doors, saunas, fridges, string lights and a massage chair. Compare my read of the 11/23 "DURABLE RUGS" send, which did the same bait-and-switch.
- The "picked just for you" and "Your Home, These Picks" headers don't match what I see. Pergolas appear twice and rugs appear twice, and none of it connects to my renovation.
- The credit card banner ("$40 off your qualifying first order of $100+ when you open a new credit card account") is a big, noisy block. It's irrelevant to me since I already buy on my highest-rewards card.
- The asterisks on "ships free*" and the "up to" percentages hide the real deals. I can't tell which tile is actually 80% off.
- The Wayfair Rewards icon is a small footer item. There's no member pricing and no rewards math anywhere above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. If the subject says hot tubs, give me a hot-tub grid with a "Was/Now" price, delivery timing and a visible "ships free" note. Don't hide a single tub in a mixed grid.
- 2. Move the category blocks (rugs, bedding, mattresses) above the product grid. They're the strongest part of the email and they sit in the middle.
- 3. Cut the grid down. Show one clear module per category, not three rows of unrelated products.
- 4. Drop or move the credit card banner. It breaks the flow and does nothing for existing cardholders.
- 5. Put a furniture delivery line in plain text: "Free delivery on furniture over $X, arrives by [date]". Show the exclusions up front.
- 6. Use my browsing history for real. If I looked at rugs, show rugs.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hot tubs, saunas and pergolas, delivered free. Black Friday pricing ends Monday.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "HOT TUBS ⚫ UP TO 80% OFF ⚫ FREE SHIPPING ⚫"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed broken links or hard failures, but the QA run only partly validated it. The 57% pass rate reflects 429 rate-limiting on every probed link and unverifiable headers. The confirmed defects are missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, a missing `lang` attribute and an `http://` web fallback in the app deep link.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity is unconfirmed.** All 8 flagged probes returned 429 (for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…` and `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=…`). A 429 is rate-limiting, not a broken link. Only 12 of 84 links were probed, and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget:
  - `.../Almost-Heaven-Saunas-Watoga-4-Person-Indoor-Outdoor-Trad…`
  - `.../Samsung-28-cu.-ft.-4Door-French-Door-Refrigerator-with-F…`
  - `.../wayfair-product-L7251-K~id-AY94006836.html`
  - `.../daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`
- **The OneLink app link has an insecure fallback.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...&af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` sends non-app users to `http://`, so they depend on a redirect upgrade to HTTPS.
- **The tracking-parameter scheme is non-standard.** Links carry `_emr`, `_eml`, `batchid`, `csnid`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`, `refid=MKTEML_143945` and `maiid`. `_eml` is unique per link, so it tracks per recipient and per placement. This is internally consistent and no defect is evident.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on content images.** These are `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`, `…Skinny-EverythingShips`, `…Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`, `…Skinny-WFCC.jpg`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the banner content, which is probably the offer, is lost.
- **Star-rating images are the only content of product links.** About 14 instances of `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` have no alt. The QA link text for those links reads `[image: stars_10.png]`, so the links have no accessible name. The product links in the QA output, such as `.../AquaRest-Discover-AR300…` and `.../80-In.-Entry-Door-Slab…`, are affected. The rating value is also not conveyed.
- **Tracking pixels are served over `http://`.** 20 pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…019` have no alt and use a non-HTTPS source, so mixed-content blocking may stop them loading or counting. They add 20 extra requests per open.
- **Open pixel has no alt.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is missing alt, which is minor.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The tag in the source reads `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="width:100% !important;…">`, with no `lang` or `xml:lang`. Screen readers will fall back to a default language.
- **The remaining markup is fine.** The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype and the `@media (max-width: 480px)` blocks are valid. The Sofia `@font-face` has an `mso-font-alt` fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the QA output or the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
Nothing here is confirmed as a violation:
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing them. Their absence from the sender can't be confirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the output. The footer is past the truncation point, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address are unverified.
- **The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.** The domain is a subdomain of the brand domain. Alignment can't be assessed without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are present on any link.** Attribution relies on the proprietary `refid`, `emlid` and `batchid` parameters. This can be a problem if the site's analytics expect UTMs.
- **The subject promises hot tubs, but the main CTAs go to a generic page.** The "Shop Sale" CTA and the top banner link to `/daily-sales`. The first product link (AquaRest hot tub) is the only visible hot-tub link, so the offer in the subject may land users on a non-hot-tub page. Landing-page content couldn't be verified because of the 429s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the banner images. Add alt to the star images (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars") or put the product name in the link text.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""`. Consider consolidating the 20 pixels.
3. Change `af_web_dp` in the OneLink URL to `https://`.
4. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff so the 429s and the 4 unprobed links get real status codes. Confirm the unsubscribe URL specifically.
6. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct send or the original `.eml` to settle the compliance and authentication questions.
7. Point the hot-tub CTAs at a hot-tub category page, or add UTM parameters, so the click-to-landing path can be checked.
