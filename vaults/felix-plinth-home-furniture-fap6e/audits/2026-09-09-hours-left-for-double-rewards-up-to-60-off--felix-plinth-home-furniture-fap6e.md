- Real hero product (Drewe swivel chair) with actual sale price and reg price, not just a vague "% off" banner.

- App-download push ("It's all on the app, including your chance at $500") feels bolted on and unrelated to the sale I opened this for.
- Two nearly-identical "Double Rewards" callouts (one right under the header, one again lower in a big navy block) — redundant, could've been one section.
