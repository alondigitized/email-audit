# Keep-forever decor. On sale for a moment.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Seventeenth CB2 email of the month — I'm not exaggerating, I've been counting because it's genuinely absurd at this point. This one at least leads with actual decor: "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF DECOR & MIRRORS" over a moody shot with a candle, some trays, a stack of books. Clean, on-brand, no complaints about the hero itself.
- But then it just... keeps going. Furniture, rugs, lighting, outdoor, entertaining, shop all — a full nine-tile category grid, then another "SHOP THE SALE" banner, then a full-width Double Rewards credit card pitch, then a separate "CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF" banner, then eight more product tiles under "MORE FOR YOU." This is basically four emails stapled together. Compare that to Article's Labor Day send, which put "Up to 40% off" front and center and didn't make me scroll through a department store to find the deal.
- I'm not clicking. I might glance at the candle holders in the second grid since a couple are genuinely my style, but the email is so overloaded I'd rather just go to the site directly than try to parse this. Not forwarding — my wife already thinks I'm subscribed to too many home brands as it is.

## What's working

- The hero image is genuinely nice — warm, styled, on-brand, and the "decor & mirrors" framing matches the subject line exactly.
- "MORE FOR YOU" candle/glassware tiles at the bottom are a decent product match for my actual taste (I've bought amber glass before).
- No render bugs — everything loaded, text is legible, no overlapping elements.
- Clearance banner is at least a clean, simple standalone callout with a specific number (60%).

## What's weak

- Way too much in one email — sale hero, 6-tile grid, second CTA, credit card pitch, clearance banner, another 8-tile grid, footer perks. It reads like three campaigns merged into one send.
- The Double Rewards credit card block is jammed in the middle with zero visual separation from the actual sale content — same beat as their "On sale now and twice the rewards" send from yesterday (2026-08-31), just relocated lower in the email instead of leading with it this time.
- Fine print on the discount ("Up to 50% off¹") with no visible asterisk explanation anywhere near the top — I'd want to know if that 50% only applies to like three mirrors before I click through.
- Nothing here differentiates it from the other sixteen CB2 emails I've gotten this month — same sale, same layout template, same "shop the sale" black button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this in half. Pick ONE offer to lead with — either the Labor Day decor sale or clearance — and send the other as a separate email later in the week instead of Frankensteining them together.
- 2. Move the credit card pitch to its own dedicated send or the footer only — burying a financial product mid-scroll in a decor sale email is jarring and I always skip past it anyway.
- 3. Add a specific fine-print callout right under "Up to 50%" (e.g., "on select mirrors & decor, through 9/9") so I don't have to hunt for the catch.
- 4. Trim the category grid from 6 tiles to 3-4 — furniture, lighting, entertaining are the ones with actual product photography that pulls me in; rugs and outdoor feel like filler here.
- 5. Given this is my seventeenth CB2 email this month, start segmenting sends by category interest instead of blasting the full catalog every time — I've clicked lighting and entertaining before, not rugs.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 68 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped from HTTP probing — unable to confirm final destination health; recommend manual crawl-through of redirects to verify no 404s/loops.

Non-HTTPS resources are frequently blocked by mail clients (especially on mixed-content policies) and may fail silently, undermining any tracking/attribution these pixels support.
