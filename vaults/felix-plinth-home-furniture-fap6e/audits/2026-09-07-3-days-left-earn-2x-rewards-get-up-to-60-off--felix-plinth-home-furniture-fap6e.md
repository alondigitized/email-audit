## Executive summary

- Third time today — third — that Crate & Barrel has sent me this exact "3 Days Left! Up to 60% off" Labor Day banner from the Crate & Kids sub-brand, and I flagged the wrong-Felix problem on the first two already. This one's slightly different only in that it leads with furniture and bedding categories instead of a crib photo up top, but scroll two inches and there's a nursery glider, a baby car seat, and a "Busy Baby Activity Chair" — I don't have kids, I have two houses mid-gut-renovation and a Crate & Barrel Rewards Card I use for actual furniture spend.
- What's mildly interesting: the "double Rewards" banner and the "new! the card you love just got better" upgraded Reward Card teaser are things I'd actually care about since I'm already a cardholder — but they're buried under baby gear I'll never click. The Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes and free design appointment banner is the one thing here that speaks to someone renovating, and it's stuck in the middle of the email like an afterthought.
- Judgement: not clicking, not forwarding — this is the same recycled Crate & Kids send I already dismissed twice today, just re-skinned with a beach hero. Marking read and moving on.

## What's working

- The hero beach photo and "3 Days Left" countdown language at least reads clean, no clutter, single CTA button up top.
- "Double Rewards" callout in navy is genuinely relevant to me as a cardholder — that's the one line that made me pause.
- The Design Desk banner (free appointment, $5,000 sweepstakes) is the single piece of content actually aimed at someone renovating, not someone with a baby.
- Product grid layout with clear "% off" labels under each photo is easy to scan even if the products themselves are wrong for me.

## What's weak

- This is baby/nursery furniture — gliders, cribs, baby car seats, a "Busy Baby Activity Chair" — sent to someone with zero kids, for the third time today from the same sender family.
- Zero furniture/renovation-relevant hero product despite that being clearly my actual purchase intent right now.
- "we love these for you" personalization block recommends a nursery glider and baby gear — that's not personalization, that's a template that doesn't know who I am.
- Cadence is broken — I've now gotten near-identical Crate & Kids Labor Day sends multiple times today, no new offer, no new angle.
- Jellyfish plush "back in stock" insert has nothing to do with anything else in this email or my life.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress me from the Crate & Kids list entirely, or at minimum de-prioritize baby/nursery content when a subscriber's browse/purchase history skews adult furniture — this is the actual fix, not a copy tweak.
- 2. Lead with the Design Desk / free design consultation offer since that's the one relevant hook for a renovator — put it above the fold, not mid-scroll.
- 3. Cap send frequency — three near-identical Labor Day emails from the C&B family in one day is how I stop opening any of them, including the ones I'd actually want (see: Crate & Barrel's countdown-timer send scored 7/10 earlier today, this one's a clear step down).
- 4. Move the Reward Card upgrade news higher — that's real news for existing cardholders and it's currently sandwiched below baby product grids.
