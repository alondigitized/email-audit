# LAST CALL FOR MEMORIAL DAY
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- Seven. We're at seven CB2 emails in ten days, and this one lands the same calendar day as "Final hours for up to 50% off" — which I had already mentally flagged as the closer. "Final hours" implies finality. "Last call" arriving six hours later means I now have to decide whether either phrase means anything from CB2 anymore. They don't.
- The creative itself is actually solid. Hero hammers SALE / MEMORIAL DAY, clearance is a real 60% off with "ENDS TONIGHT" anchored right in the module, and double rewards is surfaced mid-email. As someone buying furniture for two renovation projects simultaneously, I should be clicking. The offer is real — same pitch as Crate & Barrel's own "HOURS LEFT: Up to 60% off + Double Rewards" email I filed earlier this week from CB2's parent brand. So the CB2 family is now running the exact same value prop out of two sender addresses in the same inbox. That's not targeting. That's collision.
- Mark as read. The clearance math is genuinely interesting and I might circle back to the site directly, but I'm not clicking from this email because "last call" died the moment "final hours" preceded it from the same brand family.

## What's working

- **"ENDS TONIGHT"** in the clearance block is the sharpest time anchor in the whole email — more credible than the all-caps subject line screaming the same thing
- **Double Rewards section is visible and legible** — "GET UP 25% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" doesn't make me hunt for the loyalty perk, which is better than most Wayfair sends where rewards math is buried in the footer
- **Clearance at up to 60% off is a real number** — no "starting at 10% off select items" hedge, just 60% stated clean
- **Product grid gives me a fast visual triage** — I can scan in two seconds whether there's anything worth a click without committing to a landing page
- **Visual hierarchy works**: SALE hero → product grid → clearance block → rewards → more for you. Clean flow, no module fighting for attention

## What's weak

- **Two CB2 emails in a single day** — this one and "Final hours for up to 50% off" — completely nukes the urgency of both sends and trains me to wait for the actual final final call
- **Entertaining/tableware hero misses my renovation brief** — I'm buying dining chairs, case goods, lighting; a styled tablescape says "dinner party host," not "guy who needs to furnish a second home before summer"
- **"LAST CALL FOR MEMORIAL DAY" in all-caps is indistinguishable from the three other last-call subjects in my inbox today** — Pottery Barn, C&B proper, and Article all ran the same urgency beat; CB2's version blends into the pile rather than standing out
- **Loyalty double rewards is buried below the clearance block** — for a member, that's the highest-value offer in the email and it should hit above the fold or at minimum before clearance
- **Every CTA drops to a category page, never a specific product** — compare to Pottery Barn's Memorial Day closer, which surfaced specific items I could drop into a cart; this just sends me to `/sale` and hopes for the best

## Recommendations

- 1. **One send per day per brand family, maximum.** CB2 and Crate & Barrel are siblings in my inbox and they both fired "final" sends on the same day. The double-send is a quota problem masquerading as a marketing problem — fix the send calendar before fixing the creative.
- 2. **Combine clearance + double rewards into a single hero.** "60% off clearance + double rewards — ends tonight" is a sharper single-screen value prop than the current SALE hero → separate clearance module → separate rewards module sequence.
- 3. **Swap the hero from entertaining/tableware to furniture.** A sofa or a dining table hero converts a renovation buyer. A styled platter does not.
- 4. **Add one specific anchor product with a direct link.** Pottery Barn's version linked directly to specific pieces — CB2 needs at least one "here's the thing" CTA, not just a wall of category tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `CB2 clearance: 60% off + double rewards end tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Double your rewards before Memorial Day closes out`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 60% off clearance + 25% back in reward dollars. Offer ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at double rewards — plus clearance up to 60% off.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**High — viewport blocks user zoom:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is flagged by Apple's accessibility audits in Mail.app.

**Medium — mobile breakpoint is 418px, not the standard 480px:**
```css
@media only screen and (max-width: 418px) { .mobile { width:414px !important; } }
```
Devices between 419px–767px receive no responsive treatment (no mid-tier breakpoint). The 418px cutoff targets the iPhone SE footprint only; larger Android phones fall through unstyled.

**Low — no mid-range (tablet/large-phone) breakpoint.** The jump from `max-width: 418px` directly to `min-width: 768px` leaves a ~350px gap.

**Low — `format-detection` declared across three separate `<meta>` tags** instead of one combined tag (`content="date=no, address=no, telephone=no"`). Not a rendering bug, but redundant markup.
