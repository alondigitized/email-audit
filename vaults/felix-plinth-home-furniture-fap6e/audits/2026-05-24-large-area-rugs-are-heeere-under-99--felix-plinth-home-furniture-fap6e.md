# LARGE AREA RUGS are heeere! under $99
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Fifteen. I want that number lodged in your brain before you read another word. This is my fifteenth Wayfair email since roughly Sunday, and — I'll give credit where it's due — this one actually has *something* I need. I'm putting down LVP flooring in the secondary bedroom this month and I've been half-heartedly shopping 8×10s. "Under $99 for a large area rug" is a real hook. That's legitimately useful information.
- But the email doesn't trust the pitch. The rug hero dissolves almost immediately into "Open Box Deals" (a chair? okay?), then a coffee table, then a dining grid, then more scattered home goods. Every section undercuts the one before it. By the time I scrolled through the "let's complete your space" furniture grid, I'd forgotten why I opened in the first place. This is a rug email that forgot it was a rug email.
- I wouldn't click through on anything except maybe the rug grid at the top. I would not forward this. I'd probably mark it read and move on — which, for a product I'm actively looking for, is a failure.

## What's working

- **"Under $99" is a genuine anchor.** A specific price ceiling on large area rugs is more useful than a percent-off that requires math. I immediately knew whether that was my budget tier or not.
- **The rug hero loads up top.** The first scroll zone is actually about the thing the subject promised, which is more than I can say for most of the Wayfair sends this week.
- **Memorial Day banner is present and seasonally coherent.** Beats the zombie "DEALS DEALS DEALS" energy from the May 24th potting bench email, which felt like it was written by someone who lost track of the calendar.
- **No render bugs.** Clean load, images intact, layout held together on the screenshot. After the chaos of some of Wayfair's week-long flood, this one at least renders.

## What's weak

- **The email can't decide what it is.** Rugs → Open Box chair → coffee table → dining furniture → more home goods. Article ran a tighter brief this week with "The furniture you've always wanted" — one category, one voice, one scroll. Wayfair shoved four categories into one send and diluted all of them.
- **"heeere!" is doing too much.** The elongated spelling in the subject reads like someone got excited and forgot spell-check exists. It's not charming quirky; it's eBay listing energy. Crate & Barrel's "FINAL WEEKEND" subject from earlier this week was aggressive but at least dignified.
- **Open Box Deals section is completely off-brief.** I opened a rug email. Why am I being shown a closeout armchair in the middle? If Open Box is its own product, it deserves its own email — and Wayfair has enough send volume this week to justify that.
- **No sizing information visible.** I'm shopping 8×10s. If the hero showed the actual dimensions available, I'd click. "Large area rugs" is not a size, it's a vibe.
- **Cadence is broken.** Fifteen emails. CB2 has sent four in six days and I'm already fatigued by *that*. Wayfair's pace means this legitimately relevant email arrives with zero goodwill left in the tank.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email in half.** Rug section, price proof, one CTA. Kill the Open Box insert, the coffee table, the dining grid. Pottery Barn ran a focused bedding send while simultaneously running furniture — two separate briefs, two sends, each coherent. Do that.
- 2. **Lead with a size + price combination in the hero.** Something like "8×10 rugs under $99 — yes, really" turns browsing intent into click intent.
- 3. **Add a filter shortcut CTA.** "Shop by size" or "Shop 8×10 under $99" is the one button I'd actually tap. A homepage rug link is not that.
- 4. **Kill the Open Box section or make it a footer footnote.** It breaks the visual through-line entirely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Large area rugs under $99 — shop by size`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your next rug is under $99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `8×10s, 9×12s, runners — all under $99 while Memorial Day stock lasts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Filter by size, ship free, done before the weekend`
