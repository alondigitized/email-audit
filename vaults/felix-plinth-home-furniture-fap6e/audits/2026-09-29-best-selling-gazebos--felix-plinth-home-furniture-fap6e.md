# Best-selling gazebos
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Wayfair's fourth 72-hour clearout this week. The subject says "gazebos", but the banner at the top says "ENDS TONIGHT," and I stopped believing that countdown by the second send. Since 9/26 I've seen "Our 72-HOUR CLEAROUT is BACK," the "UP TO 60% OFF" one, and Monday's "peel-and-stick" one. The clock never runs out. The gazebo grid is at least on the subject, but it's a category tile grid, not a curated pick.
- I'm doing two houses, so a soft-top gazebo for the second place's yard isn't crazy. But I have no idea what a gazebo costs, how big it is, or whether it ships flat, and the email gives me none of it. Below the gazebos it turns into a general clearout: wall clocks, toilets, a cat tree. I don't own a cat. I'd skim it, maybe save the gazebo tile, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The subject and the first content block match. Gazebos, soft-top gazebos, sheds and pergolas all sit right under the banner.
- "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" is the only real price anchor in the email, and it's in the hero area.
- The "get 5% rewards on every item, always" block is a clean loyalty message. That's the kind of thing I'd factor into which card I put a big outdoor order on.
- The Garcelle Beauvais "Outdoor dining favorites" block is the best-looking part. The blue plates and yellow napkins are cohesive, and I'd pin it.

## What's weak

- There's a huge blank white gap near the bottom, after "even more to ❤️", with one tiny broken-image icon in the middle. It looks like the layout died. Crate & Barrel and Pottery Barn don't ship gaps like that.
- The "72-HR CLEAROUT ENDS TONIGHT" banner is small and buried at the very top. It's also the same creative as the last three sends, so the urgency reads as fake.
- There are about 20 tiles with labels like "Must-Have Black Outdoor Privacy Screens" and "Our Favorite Toilets," and no prices. It feels like a warehouse aisle, not a catalog.
- Nothing helps with furniture logistics: no delivery windows, no sizing, no swatches or samples, no color story. The Pottery Barn Cayman bedroom email at least had a cohesive palette.
- Wayfair sent me the same lamp-adjacent stuff (hall trees, benches, bedroom sets) in earlier sends this week. It doesn't treat me like a known customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the blank block and lead with one hero. Pick one gazebo, show its price and size, and put a real "Shop gazebos" button on it.
- 2. Stop reusing the clearout banner. If the sale is really ending, say a specific time. If it isn't, drop the banner and lead with the product.
- 3. Add prices and one-line specs to the tiles, such as "10x12, ships in 3-5 days."
- 4. Cut the tile grid to 6-8 items that match the subject, or segment by browsing history. The cat tree and the toilets shouldn't be in a gazebo send.
- 5. Turn the rewards block into math, for example "5% back on a $600 gazebo = $30."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free delivery on most, plus 5% back with Wayfair Rewards.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML with mobile media queries and per-recipient tracked links. The QA data shows widespread missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and unverifiable unsubscribe and authentication headers. No link was confirmed broken. The 12 probed links all returned 429 (rate limited), and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 reported probe results are `429`, which the QA tool itself calls "may be valid". Link validity is unverified, not failed.
- **Unprobed links.** 4 links were not checked because of the 70s time budget:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/sheds-for-less-se19265.html?eid=2358…`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se8…`
  - `/wayfair-rewards?_emr=…`
  - `/?_emr=…&wfcs=cs7&_eml`
- **Truncated URLs.** The unprobed URLs appear cut off in the QA output (e.g. `…-se8`, `…f514`). I can't tell whether that's a display limit or a malformed `href`. Check the raw source.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels.** 20 impression pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Clients that block mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions.
- **Onelink fallback is HTTP.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` on `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` forces an extra redirect hop on desktop.
- **Campaign parameter mismatch.** The same Onelink URL has `c=Triggered` alongside `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. That labels a batch send as triggered and may misattribute it in AppsFlyer.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
