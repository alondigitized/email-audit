And there's a broken image block sitting dead center in the email — a big empty white box with just a tiny broken-image icon in it.

- "Peel & Stick picks" hero copy is genuinely relevant to my ongoing renovation-on-a-budget problem, especially for the second home.
- The subway-tile-and-pitcher lifestyle shot is clean and on-trend, no complaints there.

- Broken image block with just an error icon, sitting in a big empty white rectangle — that's a real render bug, not a nitpick.

- 3. Drop or seriously slow the 72-HR CLEAROUT banner cadence — three sends in a week kills the urgency completely.
