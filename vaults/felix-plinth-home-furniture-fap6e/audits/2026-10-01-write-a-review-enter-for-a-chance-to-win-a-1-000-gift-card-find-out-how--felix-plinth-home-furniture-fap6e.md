# Write a review. Enter for a chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card. Find out how →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel send I've opened today, and the first one that asks me to do homework. The hero is a two-star graphic over a crib, with "want to win a $1,000 gift card?" and a "Write a Review" button. Under the hero banner, a muted green strip still says "Up to 50% off" on the Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event. That's the same event the Kids sub-brand pushed this morning. It's the third outfit for one sale.
- The rest is a catalog. There are washable rugs, Jellycat animals, a Nuna stroller and car seat, a glider, a dresser and a crib mattress. Then comes a Crate Card ad, an app download ad, a Design Desk appointment and a footer nav. I'm renovating two houses, so I do care about the dresser and the rugs. But I'm not writing a review for a $1,000 sweepstakes entry, and the product tiles are generic.
- Would I click? Maybe the Jellycat lion, if I were shopping for a gift. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and archive it, and I wouldn't even save it to my Pinterest folder.

## What's working

- The hero photo is clean. The crib sits against a dusty mauve wall, and it's calm and well lit.
- "Want to win $1,000?" is a concrete prize, and the Oct 31 deadline is stated right under it.
- The "Write a Review" and "Shop Bestsellers" buttons are clear and tappable. They're dark pill buttons that stand out on the pale background.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices and strike-through sale prices, like the glider at $849.95 down from $999.99. I can do the math at a glance.
- The dresser and glider are real products with real prices, which is more useful to me than a mood image.

## What's weak

- The email has two offers fighting each other: a sweepstakes and an "up to 50% off" sale. The sale banner is muted green and easy to skim past, so the eye lands on the contest.
- I'm not a reviewer for a product I haven't bought. The ask doesn't match how I shop.
- The "new! washable rugs" block has a headline but no price, no rug name and no size. For a renovation buyer that's a miss.
- The card ad, the app promo and the design appointment come in a row. It's three more asks stacked at the bottom.
- The product grid is baby-heavy (Nuna stroller, car seat, activity chair). That's off-target for a 36-year-old male doing home renovations with no stated nursery.
- Nothing on swatches, delivery timing on furniture or showroom availability, which is what I'd want for a dresser.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job. Either run the review sweepstakes or run the 50% off event, and don't put both in the hero. I'd lead with the sale and move the contest into a slim banner at the bottom.
- 2. Give the rugs real product detail: name, size, price and a swatch link. The headline alone does nothing for me.
- 3. Cut the card ad and the app promo from this send. Put them in a separate lifecycle email.
- 4. Add a delivery estimate and a "see it in a store" link on the furniture tiles. The store line right now is a throwaway.
- 5. Pull the Bedroom & Nursery event copy out of the muted band and put the percentage in the hero where I can see it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Win a $1,000 gift card — review your favorite piece`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off nursery & bedroom ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Review a product by Oct 31 and you're entered. Plus up to 50% off bedroom & nursery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Washable rugs, gliders and dressers from $849. Rate one for a shot at $1,000.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a clean template structure with no unresolved merge tokens visible. The confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, a zoom-blocking viewport meta, and leftover template cruft. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay capture limits, so they can't be confirmed as sender defects. The HTML is truncated and the 65 tracking links were not probed, so link, UTM and footer checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified redirects:** 65 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `&n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) pass a long hashed identifier in the query string. Other pixels include `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5c8cb637-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. They add many third-party requests, and the `s=` and `mi_u=` values look like the same recipient hash.
- **Stale asset path:** one image is served from `s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2025/05%5FKids/202505%5FCBK%5FWashablePerformance%5FRugs/…`. The path indicates a May 2025 asset in an October 2026 send. I can't confirm from the source whether it is reused deliberately.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA check flagged 24 `<img>` elements.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (the QA output also lists one under `/m/47/`): `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `eeb8adae-…jpg`, `82e1c4fe-…gif`, `74344650-…jpg`, `d7ea9922-…jpg`, `ea0160ba-…jpg`, `f28e7f40-…jpg`, `ed972f76-…gif`, `8727260f-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`
  - The scene7 `202505_CBK_WashablePerformance_Rugs` image
  - Tracking pixels: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6×), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5×), `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5c8cb637-…`
  - **Impact:** with images blocked or in a screen reader, the content images have no fallback text. Pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` override user text-size preferences in iOS/WebKit clients.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` doesn't match the subject line. The `&` is also unescaped, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags (3 variants).
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype and duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` shipped in production.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** forces centered text on every paragraph unless overridden per element. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Verify against the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay. The `From` domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** the HTML is truncated before the footer, so these can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links route through tracking redirects and were not probed. No UTM parameters, final destinations or landing-page status codes could be checked, so I'm not flagging a problem. The subject promises a review-and-win sweepstakes. Whether the CTA lands on a matching page is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images and the stale scene7 rugs image. Set `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the `rlcdn`, `mi.`, `dv.` and `o.gif` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `text-size-adjust:none` in favour of `100%`.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`. If the relay strips them, capture headers upstream or test with a direct seed inbox.
4. Resolve the 65 tracking links with a follow-redirect probe. Confirm final URLs return 200, keep UTMs and land on the review/sweepstakes page.
5. Remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder comment and the duplicate meta tags. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`, and set the title to something descriptive.
