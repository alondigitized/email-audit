# Black Friday: It’s go time
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Article finally says "go time," and the email is a clean, loud Black Friday announcement. It has a black banner, "UP TO 40% OFF," a giant "SALE," and one coral SHOP NOW button. I got the offer in about two seconds. That's better than the 11/08 "Black Friday's almost here" teaser, which was a big black hero with nothing to buy. This one at least has a number and a button.
- Then it falls apart for me. It's an eight-tile room grid and nothing else. There's no product, no price and no hero piece. I'm renovating two houses, so I want to see the Isla console or the walnut dresser at a real sale price. I got that on 11/13 ("Travertine: A natural showstopper") and 11/06 ("Real homes. Really good storage."), and those were Article's best sends this month. This one reads like a homepage nav bar turned into an email.
- Would I click? Probably once, on Shop Bedroom or All Sale, mostly because I already have a Pinterest-adjacent folder of Article pieces to price-check. I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read, and come back when I see an actual price.

## What's working

- "UP TO 40% OFF" sits above the fold in a black banner. There's no hunting for the offer.
- The coral SHOP NOW button is the only warm color in a black-and-grey hero, so it pops. It's also a decent tap target.
- The room tiles map to how I'm shopping: living room, bedroom, dining, office, outdoor. Two houses means I'm hitting several categories.
- The photography is consistent, with warm walnut, leather and olive tones. The palette feels like Article, and that matters to me for color consistency across rooms.
- The footer has a 30-day satisfaction guarantee and a shipping and delivery link. For furniture, I want both of those.

## What's weak

- No product, no price and no sale-price example anywhere. "Up to 40%" could mean one lamp or the whole catalog.
- The "SALE" hero is a dark gradient with no furniture in it. It's the same black Black Friday look as the 11/08 teaser, so it feels like a repeat.
- The white tile labels sit on mid-tone photos, and "Shop Outdoor" and "Shop Entryway" are low-contrast. I had to squint.
- There's no loyalty or rewards angle and no end date. Pottery Barn and CB2 are running "2X Rewards" and "ends tomorrow" countdowns, and Article gave me no deadline to act on.
- It says "Black Friday starts now" and then repeats it in the black strip below the grid. That's redundant, and the second line is a wasted slot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace at least four of the eight tiles with real products and sale prices, such as "Isla console, now $X" or "Walnut dresser, was/now." That one change would lift this the most. CB2 and Pottery Barn lead with products in their sends, even when I'm numb to them.
- 2. Add a deadline: "Ends Cyber Monday" or a countdown. Without one, I can put it off.
- 3. Fix the tile contrast with a darker overlay or a text pill behind the labels.
- 4. Cut the second "Black Friday is officially on" strip and use that space for swatch/sample or white-glove delivery info. Furniture buyers want to know when it ships.
- 5. Add a short "most saved" row from the lookbook sends, such as the travertine pieces. It would give me a reason to click on something specific.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with dark-mode support, but its dark-mode CSS has syntax errors, three images have no alt text, and the unsubscribe and authentication headers couldn't be verified. The QA run reported 0 hard failures and 4 warnings. The HTML I received is truncated, so body-level findings are limited to what the head and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 16 were skipped (footer, utility and social links). No broken links were reported, but the 16 skipped links are unverified.
- **Redirect-wrapped URLs:** Images and links go through third-party redirect hosts, `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...~~/AAQVbhA~/...` and `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`. This means two different tracking domains are in use, and neither is the sending domain `email.article.com`. I can't verify the final destinations from the source. If either redirect domain has a certificate or DNS problem, both images and links will fail.
- **Plain-text part:** 2367 of 3323 characters (about 71%) are URLs, which trips the QA ">70% URLs" warning. The cause is long tracking-wrapped URLs, so the plain-text alternative is mostly unreadable link strings. This is a mild spam-filter and readability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, from QA):**
  - `…B00IDbOpsaqZvEcjC1SyljjjNZhZKSpcRpxx-wypkO7RLGBKRp` (`post.spmailtechnol.com/q/lGVsBkmpQiBGIY1yj0nV0Q~~/…`)
  - `…26` (`links.email.article.com/s/eo/wA6A2Jxgf3T3ewfp2X2F7UcGAZKQWk4OumpUCx4LTv8a-…`)
  - `…8v94EmHTZRP3ybtm7KAgHfcyZZCrc_hnTjlN5Q1pK9Rx7Ncdfx` (`post.spmailtechnol.com/q/xiV48aWkK_Cd3OXL4DNbXg~~/…`)

  If any are tracking pixels, they need `alt=""`. If any are content images, they need descriptive alt text. The `/s/eo/` path may be an open-tracking pixel, but I can't confirm that from the source I have.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is present in `<head>`. This is minor, but some clients and assistive tech use it.
- **Dark-mode CSS errors** in the `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma in the selector. Strict parsers will drop the whole rule, so heading and paragraph colour overrides may not apply.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a misplaced `;`, so the `!important` is invalid.
  - The `@media` block is never closed. The block ends with `a .link{color:#fff!important}</style>`, and browsers will auto-close it.
  - `line-height:auto!important` in `.dark-img` is not a valid `line-height` value.
- **Dark-mode risk:** If the `p` and `a` rules are dropped, text may stay dark on the forced `.darkmode{background-color:#000!important}` background. That would be a contrast failure, but I haven't rendered it to confirm.
