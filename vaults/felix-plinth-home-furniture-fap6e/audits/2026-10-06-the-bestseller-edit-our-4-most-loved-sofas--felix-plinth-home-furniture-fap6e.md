# The bestseller edit: Our 4 most-loved sofas
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Crate again. This is the fourth one from them in about 24 hours, and the sofa follow-up to this morning's "Your perfect new sofa, starting at $1599." Normally I'd snooze it. But I'm furnishing two living rooms at once, and a "4 most-loved sofas" edit is a shortcut I'll actually use. The top banner says "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" with "starting at $1599" under it, so I know the deal before I scroll.
- It's a long scroll, but it holds together. Each collection (Axis, Oceanside, Lounge, Gather) gets a big room shot, a fabric name, fabric swatch dots, and its own Shop button. That's what I want when I'm comparing. It then drifts into pillows, rugs, kitchen appliances and an Affirm pitch. By then I'm skimming, not reading.
- I'd click, probably on Axis or Oceanside, and I'd save it to my sofa folder. I wouldn't forward it. My co-buyer already gets the same Crate blast, and the cadence is wearing me down.

## What's working

- The banner puts the offer first: "up to 20% off" plus "starting at $1599." I didn't have to hunt for it.
- Every collection has a swatch row and a spec line for fabrics, sizes and configurations. That's the comparison info I need when planning two rooms.
- "Get it in gorgeous leather" and "try a swatch first" cover the questions I have before spending $3K+ on a sofa. Swatches matter for color consistency across both houses.
- The room photography is warm and neutral, catalog-grade, and consistent from block to block.
- The rewards card block near the bottom is relevant to me, since I buy on the highest-rewards card. The Affirm "pay later" tile also speaks to big-ticket furniture.
- The TheDesignDesk "free appointment" CTA is a real showroom/service hook.

## What's weak

- I got the sofa email this morning and now this one. Same offer, same $1599, and probably some of the same sofas. This is the kind of repeat that makes me tune out Crate. Pottery Barn's plaid send at least felt like a different story.
- The back half drifts. Pillows, rugs, vases and the kitchen deals block (Breville, KitchenAid, "they ship free!") dilute the sofa message. The kitchen block feels like it belongs in another email.
- The text is tiny on a phone. The fabric descriptions and the product price lines in the "we love these for you" grid look like fine print without zooming. The product grid also repeats the Crate-family sameness.
- It never answers the furniture-shipping question. Delivery timing, white-glove and lead times are missing, and for a sofa that's the main blocker.
- The 20% off is "up to" and no end date is visible. I can't tell how urgent it is, so I'll put it off.
- The app download and footer category links at the bottom add more length without adding anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to the sofas. Drop kitchen and decor, or move them to a short row at the bottom. That makes it a real "bestseller edit" and not a mega-catalog.
- 2. Put the price and a deadline on each collection. "From $1,599, 20% off through Sunday" beats a vague "up to."
- 3. Add a delivery line, such as "Ships in 2–4 weeks, white-glove included," near the swatch CTA. Show the swatch program higher, not mid-email.
- 4. Show one rewards-math line at the top: "Earn X% back with the Crate card on sofas." That's the first thing I'd check.
- 5. Bump the body copy size and give the buttons more padding for thumb taps.
- 6. Space out the sends. Two sofa emails in one day is the real problem.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4 best-selling sofas, up to 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Axis, Oceanside, Lounge, Gather: pick your sofa`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fabric swatches, sizes and delivery dates for each, from $1,599.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Sunday. Order swatches free before you commit.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "The bestseller edit: Our 4 most-loved sofas"

**Scope note:** The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, the unsubscribe link, the physical address and the CTA hrefs could not be inspected. Findings below are limited to the visible `<head>` and the QA output.

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Mapp/MI-templated XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with 75 click-tracked links. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 issues, 3 warnings). The main problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (36 listed), a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom, and compliance headers the relay didn't capture, so they are unverified.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **75 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` are five separate third-party beacons. This adds requests and a third-party dependency, and image-blocking clients will not fire them.
- **Shared identifier in two places.** The same 60-character hex prefix `56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640` appears in the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` and `o.gif?mi_u=…` pixels. It also appears in the third-party `sr.rlcdn.com` `s=` parameter (`…640cfd8`, 64 chars). This looks like a per-recipient hashed ID shared with LiveRamp. I can't confirm what it hashes, but it should be checked against the privacy policy and consent language.
- **Open pixels in several forms:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/58dd95ad-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are normal, but they inflate Apple MPP open metrics.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 36 images listed.** The QA output looks cut off at the end, so the real count may be higher.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`, for example `d6d762e4-…jpg`, `0a6fbaa4-…jpg` and `5422078f-…gif`.
  - The scene7 hero `SP25_CB_UphBestsellers_leather_01`.
  - The seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, which are product/recommendation tiles.
  - Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `dv…`) should carry `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
  - If the sofa tiles are image-only, images-off clients will show no product names or CTAs.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. The CSS also sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `body` and `*`, which stops iOS text scaling.
- **Dead CSS rule.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match on desktop and the later one wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed and duplicates the `http-equiv` charset.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.
- **Generic `<title>`:** it doesn't match the subject line.
- **Outlook:** the VML and `mso` conditionals are present, and I found no issues there.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The footer was not visible, so this isn't fully verified.

### 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not captured.** `[WARN]` The header is absent in the relayed message.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured.** `[WARN]` Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay.
- **Not confirmed as defects.** QA flags that these headers may be stripped by the relay, so treat them as unverified, not as violations. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, so alignment should be checked with a direct DNS or raw-message test.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address** could not be checked because the source was truncated.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. CTA destinations are hidden behind tracking redirects that were skipped, and the truncated HTML doesn't show any UTM parameters. The email does not link to a visible landing page I can verify against the "4 bestseller sofas" subject.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv…`, `448796.gif`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Collapse the five identical `sr.rlcdn.com` beacons to one, and review what the shared `mi_u`/`s=` hash identifies.
4. Fix the markup: escape `&` in the `<title>`, de-duplicate the `format-detection` and charset metas, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Re-run QA against the raw message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for the 75 tracked links in a separate pass to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.
7. Fix the `img {max-width}` media-query ordering so the 600px desktop cap applies as intended.
