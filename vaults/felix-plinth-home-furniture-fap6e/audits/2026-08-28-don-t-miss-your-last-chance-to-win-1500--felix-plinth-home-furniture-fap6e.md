# Don’t miss your last chance to win $1500!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "last chance to win $1500" — a sweepstakes email, not a sale, and it's a book-a-designer funnel wearing a promo subject line. This is Pottery Barn's Design Crew RSVP pitch again, dressed up with sweepstakes urgency to get me to open it. I don't do sweepstakes emails — "no purchase necessary," odds are garbage, and I know it. What's actually underneath is a decent design-consultation pitch with real product tiles (curtain rods, a swivel chair, dining tables), but it's buried under a sweep banner and then a wall of design-service CTAs before I ever see a product I could buy today.
- I'm not clicking to enter a raffle. If anything I'd scroll past the sweepstakes banner to the "Recommended Just For You" grid at the bottom — that Toscana Extendable Dining Table is actually in my renovation lane. But that's six screens deep. Not forwarding this, not marking important, straight to read-and-archive.

## What's working

- The "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" product grid at the bottom has real SKUs with real names and dimensions — curtain rod, swivel chair, mattress, dining tables — that's the part of this email actually built for someone furnishing two houses.
- "Our designers can tackle transformations of any size" section has clean, consistent room photography — the plaid lamp shot and the gallery wall shot are genuinely nice and on-brand.
- Free in-store design class card (Sept 13, Styling Your Living Room) is a concrete, dated event — more credible than vague "book now" CTAs.
- 10% back in Rewards + Affirm financing blocks at the footer are relevant to how I actually pay (highest-rewards card mentality, and Affirm for furniture-size purchases).

## What's weak

- Leading with a sweepstakes ("last chance to win $1500 gift card") instead of an actual product or sale — I've learned to discount these on sight, and it undercuts trust more than it builds urgency.
- The whole middle of the email is designer-booking CTAs repeated five times ("Book Now," "Book Now," "Book a Free Appointment," "Book Your Free Appointment") before a single shoppable product appears — that's a lot of scroll for someone who just wants to buy a rug.
- No pricing or offer numbers on any of the "Decor touches," "Statement lighting," "Curtains," "Gallery walls" tiles — just moodboard copy and a repeated CTA, so I can't tell what anything costs until I click through.
- I already logged Pottery Barn hitting me with a moodboard-as-sale email on 8/26 ("Bedroom inspiration: A cozy retreat") — this is the same trick, sweepstakes flavor this time. Three different Pottery Barn beats in three days and none of them are a straight discount.
- Compare to CB2's "OUTDOOR ON SALE" send (7/10) — that one led with a hero I'd actually pin and a real sale. Article's Labor Day email finally broke its own "editorial mode" streak with a real discount. Pottery Barn just keeps recycling inspiration/service content as if it's a promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the sweepstakes as the hero — replace it with the highest-margin product from the "Recommended" grid (the dining table or swivel chair) as the lead image, with an actual price or rewards callout above the fold.
- 2. Cut the five "Book Now" repeats down to one strong CTA after the designer pitch, then get straight to shoppable product — I shouldn't have to scroll past four moodboard tiles to see something with a price.
- 3. Put the free in-store design class date/time near the top if that's the real intent — it's the most credible, specific thing in the email and it's buried.
- 4. Add actual prices or "starting at $X" under each lifestyle tile (curtains, lighting, gallery walls) so I'm not guessing before I click.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Book a designer, get 10% back in Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Complimentary styling help, plus new arrivals for fall`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spots are limited — book your free appointment today`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard SFMC-templated transactional/marketing email with heavy click-tracking redirection; QA surfaced no critical failures but several compliance-header and accessibility warnings worth fixing.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` SFMC click-tracking redirects (51 tracking links total, per QA — skipped for HTTP probing, so live destination validity is unconfirmed).
- Secondary tracking pixels/beacons present: `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...`, `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, and an open-tracking pixel at `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — note this pixel passes the recipient email address in a plaintext query parameter, which is a PII-in-URL exposure risk (logged in server/proxy logs, referrer headers, etc.).
- Malformed `<style>` block in `<head>`: raw PHP-style `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...");` statements are embedded directly inside a CSS `<style>` tag (not inside a script or server context). This is invalid CSS and will either be ignored or cause style-parsing errors in some clients — appears to be a copy/paste artifact from a server script, not functional code.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Every content image (37 instances) is missing `alt` text, including product images (`remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `westin-heavenly-bed-mattress-1325h-l.jpg`, etc.), hero slices, and footer legal/nav images. This breaks accessibility for screen readers and image-blocked clients (Outlook default).
- Two images load over `http://` instead of `https://`: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed-content over http:// may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing TLS.
- Media queries target both `max-width: 480px` and `max-width: 650px` with overlapping `.mobile-hide`/`.mobile-show`/`table[class="wrapper"]` rules — no functional conflict detected in the truncated source, but worth visual QA on mobile clients given the dual breakpoints.
- Standard Outlook/Apple Mail resets present (`mso-*`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `.ExternalClass`) — no issues found here.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No AMPscript/merge-token output is visible in the rendered source — the `<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot -->...<!-- end dynamic ampScript slot -->` is empty, meaning either personalization wasn't populated in this render/preview or no dynamic content was configured for this send. Cannot confirm personalization logic executed correctly from this source alone.
- Recipient identifier is passed in cleartext as `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (in the `coreemail.html` customer-service link) — functional for personalization/preference-center handoff, but same PII-in-URL exposure noted in Section 2.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in the message headers (QA WARN) — one-click unsubscribe via mail client UI (Gmail/Yahoo/Apple Mail) will not be offered, which is now effectively required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN) — no one-click POST unsubscribe support confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture; flagged as relay-dependent (AgentMail) rather than confirmed sender misconfiguration.
- No in-body unsubscribe link or physical mailing address was visible in the truncated HTML source provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance; likely present in the untruncated footer but unverified here.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Outbound links use SFMC `qs=` opaque tracking tokens rather than visible UTM parameters — campaign attribution is handled server-side via the click-tracking redirect, so UTM alignment with the landing page cannot be verified from this source alone.
- Customer-service link (`potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`) points to a real PB domain, consistent with brand domain; no cross-domain leakage detected in visible links (all resolve to `potterybarn.com`, `e.potterybarn.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com`, or known ad-tracking domains).

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` attributes to all 37 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for purely decorative slices; descriptive alt text needed for product images).
- Upgrade the two `http://` image sources to `https://`.
- Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP-style statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — invalid CSS with no functional purpose in-browser.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at the SMTP layer (may be a capture/relay artifact of AgentMail rather than a true absence — verify against raw SMTP headers from the sending MTA, not just this relay).

- Consider removing or hashing the plaintext recipient email from the `sv_emopen`/`coreemail.html` tracking URLs to reduce PII exposure in logs.
