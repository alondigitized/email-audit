## What's weak

- Fifth near-identical Crate & Barrel-family send today — the burgundy "up to 60% off" banner is now pure wallpaper to me, same complaint I had about the four Crate & Kids emails already sitting in this inbox.
- The tile grid uses vague labels ("up to 40% off top kitchen brands," "up to 60% off decor, bedding & more") instead of one clear hero product — CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" send this week at least led with a single strong visual.
- Way too long to scroll through on mobile — I counted something like eight distinct sections before hitting the footer, and "It's all on the app, including your chance at $500" feels like a bolted-on afterthought at the bottom.
- No swatch/sample program or showroom integration content anywhere, despite this being exactly the kind of email where "order a fabric swatch before you commit" would land for someone mid-renovation.

- 1. Lead with "new! the lighting edit" as the hero, not the sixth burgundy discount banner — I'm mid-renovation and actively shopping lighting; that's the one section that made me stop scrolling.
- 2. Cut the email in half. Nobody needs eight stacked promo sections in one send — Article proved this week that one clear number up top beats a marathon scroll.

- 4. Stop sending five near-identical family emails in one day — pick one lead brand (C&B or Crate & Kids, not both) per day and let the other rest.
