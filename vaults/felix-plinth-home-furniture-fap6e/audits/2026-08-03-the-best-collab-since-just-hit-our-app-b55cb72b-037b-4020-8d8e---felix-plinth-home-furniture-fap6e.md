# The best collab since 🍪 & 🥛 JUST hit our app
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, déjà vu — I'm pretty sure I already opened this exact email today. Cookie/milk emoji hook, Levain Bakery, "shop early on our app." Same subject, same navy hero with the little app-phone illustration. If Crate & Barrel is going to resend the identical bakery collab pitch to me twice in one day, that's exactly the kind of overlap that makes me start bulk-archiving a brand's mail without reading it — I already flagged this same subject line as a 7/10 shrug earlier, and seeing it land again does nothing for me.
- Buried under the app hype is actually the good stuff: 15% off the Aspen collection, and a Design Desk sweepstakes offering a free design appointment plus a shot at $5,000 — that's the one section that's actually built for someone like me juggling two gut renos. If C&B had led with "book a free designer, enter to win $5K toward your reno" instead of an app-exclusive cookie collab, I'd have clicked immediately.
- Verdict: I'm not clicking the hero CTA — I don't want app-exclusive anything — but I'd probably scroll to the Design Desk block and maybe book the free appointment. Not forwarding this to anyone; it's too scattered to be worth someone else's time.

## What's working

- The Design Desk sweepstakes ($5,000 gift card + free design appointment) is genuinely on-target for a guy mid-renovation on two houses — this is the one beat in the whole email that feels like it knows who I am.
- The Crate Card / Reward Dollars callout speaks directly to my highest-rewards-card habit — I always route big furniture spend through whichever card pays me back the most.
- The kitchen product grid (Le Creuset Dutch oven, KitchenAid stand mixer) shows real prices and piece counts, not vague "shop now" mystery-meat links — Wayfair's clearance email did the same thing right by showing an actual discounted item up top.
- Clean, on-brand visual execution throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent typography. CB2's "Goop color studies" send had that same polish; this one matches it.

## What's weak

- I've now seen this exact subject line and hero twice today — that's the same "same lamp, three brands" fatigue I get from Pottery Barn/West Elm pile-ons, except this time it's one brand emailing itself.
- The hero CTA gates the actual offer behind an app download ("Shop Early on Our App") — I'm not installing an app for a bakery collab, full stop. That's friction Article's "Last chance" sale email never made me deal with; it just took me straight to the sale.
- Way too many competing CTAs stacked top to bottom — app collab, Aspen sale, Design Desk, product grid, Crate Card, app download again, Design Desk again. By the third repeat ask I've stopped paying attention to any of them.
- Zero furniture/renovation content up top where it counts — no swatches, no room shots, no "your project" framing until you're deep into scroll territory. Pottery Barn's Halloween email fumbled for a different reason (stale seasonal push), but same result: the stuff I actually care about is an afterthought.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Design Desk sweepstakes, not the bakery collab — "free design appointment + $5,000 giveaway" is a far stronger hook for a renovating homeowner than a Levain cookie tie-in, and it's the one thing in this send built for me.
- 2. Kill the app-gate on the hero offer, or at minimum give a non-app path to shop the same collab — don't make me install something to see a product.
- 3. Cut the redundant CTA blocks — one app-download ask and one Design Desk ask, not two of each. Every repeat dilutes the one I'd actually act on.
- 4. Check send cadence/dedup logic — I should not be getting the identical subject line twice in a single day; that's a segmentation bug, not a feature.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Book a free designer, enter to win $5,000`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Aspen 15% off + a shot at $5K for your reno`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free design appointment, no strings — plus a $5,000 gift card giveaway through Aug 3.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Aspen dinnerware is 15% off, and our design pros are booking now.`
