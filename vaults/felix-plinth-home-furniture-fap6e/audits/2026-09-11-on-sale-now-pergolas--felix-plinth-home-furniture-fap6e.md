## Executive summary

- Okay, "This Week's Best Deals, Picked for You" — sure it is, Wayfair. This is the same "Last Call" purple banner, the same "recently purchased" social-proof grid, the same "Sales You *Almost* Missed" filler section I've scrolled past a dozen times this week. It's not broken this time, which after Wayfair's own gazebo email had a blank hero rectangle and the bidet email had a straight-up missing product shot, actually counts as a win — the pergola photo renders, it's crisp, no lorem-ipsum, no dead space.
- But the subject says pergolas and the hero delivers exactly one pergola shot before dumping me into bedding, area rugs, dart boards, foosball tables, and ride-on toys. That's not "picked for you," that's a category dump with a pergola thumbnail slapped on top for the subject line. I'm mid-reno on two houses — if you're going to lead with outdoor structures, give me pergola styles, sizes, install notes, something. Instead I get a "Shop All Picks for You" button that goes nowhere specific.
- I'd glance at the pergola image for two seconds, maybe click through out of curiosity since I actually do need patio cover ideas for the second home, then bail once I hit the grab-bag grid. Not forwarding this — nothing here is worth sending to anyone.

## What's working

- The pergola hero photo is a real render this time — no broken-image gap like the last two Wayfair sends I've seen, so at least the basics work here.
- "MUST-SEE PRICE" and "GRAB IT FAST" tags on the sub-tiles are at least specific about urgency without going full ALL-CAPS spam.
- Bedding clearance tile actually ties back to Wayfair's own cadence — they hit bedding again after Tuesday's "BEDDING up to 60% OFF" send, so at least it's consistent messaging, not random.
- Percentages are visible and legible (40% off, 60% off) without me hunting for fine print.

## What's weak

- Subject promises pergolas, hero shows one pergola, then it's immediately privacy screens, bedding, rugs, living room seating — total bait-and-switch on focus.
- Zero pergola-specific detail: no size options, no material (wood vs. aluminum vs. steel), no install/assembly note, no price. For a $500+ outdoor structure that's the stuff that actually gets me to click.
- "Sales You *Almost* Missed" with dart boards, foosball tables, and 2-seater ride-on toys has nothing to do with a guy juggling two house renovations — this is generic mass-blast filler.
- "Shop All Picks for You" CTA is vague — a verb-plus-noun like "Shop Pergolas" would at least match the subject line I opened for.
- This is the fifth-ish Wayfair email in my inbox this week and it's the same purple category-grid template I screenshotted for the bedding send two days ago — nothing here differentiates it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero section actually about pergolas — show 2-3 styles/sizes with prices, since that's what got me to open. Article's recent "Patio season's not over yet" send did this right: lifestyle-first, no countdown pressure, and it stayed on-theme instead of pivoting to bookcases by scroll three.
- 2. Cut "Sales You *Almost* Missed" entirely or replace it with outdoor/patio-adjacent categories only (furniture covers, outdoor rugs, fire pits) — the dart boards and ride-on toys read like they escaped from a different email.
- 3. Swap "Shop All Picks for You" for "Shop Pergolas" as the primary CTA directly under the hero, before the category grid even starts.
- 4. Add a price or price-range under the hero pergola image — CB2's recent "NEW TO COVET" send scored better with me specifically because it skipped urgency theater and let the product speak; a real price does more work than "On sale now."

# Technical Email Audit: Wayfair "On sale now: pergolas"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Unbounce/Bronto-style Wayfair marketing template with functional tracking pixels and links, but it fails several accessibility and delivery-header checks and serves 20 tracking pixels over insecure HTTP.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 70 links were probed (deprioritized footer/social links skipped by design); all 12 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited), which the QA tool notes as inconclusive rather than broken (e.g., `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`).
- 4 additional links were unprobed due to a 70s time budget cutoff and are neither pass nor fail.
- 20 tracking/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over plain **HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged by QA as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- All links carry consistent tracking parameters (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_122338`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16165`, `csnid`, `brcid`), indicating a coherent tracking scheme with no obviously malformed or truncated parameters in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 15 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including all content images (`wfus_0331_twbdpfy_2024_email_banner-gif.gif`, `...sku-rec-callouts-01/02`, `...shop-all-picks-for-yo`, `...sales-you-almost-miss`), all 6 review star-rating icons (`stars_10.png` ×5, `stars_11.png` ×1), the open-tracking beacon (`b.php`), and all 20 HTTP impression pixels — screen readers will announce nothing or fall back to filenames for these.
- Template uses standard Outlook/MSO and mobile media-query fixes (`@media max-width:480px`, `mso-font-alt`, `u-FullWidth` etc.), consistent with a mature responsive framework — no structural rendering defects observed in the truncated source.
- Custom web font (`Sofia`, via `@font-face` from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`) has an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for Outlook — correctly implemented.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are visible in the truncated source. No issues found in the visible portion; note that the HTML was truncated so later sections were not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in the captured headers — QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a confirmed absence.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status is unknown via the AgentMail relay.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA methodology ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in probing scope), but it was among the 58 deprioritized/unprobed links, so its destination was not validated.
- Sender domain (`members.wayfair.com`) and From name ("Wayfair") are consistent with a legitimate brand-owned sending domain — no spoofing indicators in the visible source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair uses proprietary tracking params rather than standard UTM (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`); instead: `_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid`, `source=batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `eid`/`unifiedeventid`. This is a template-consistent scheme, not a defect.

- App deep-link CTA ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App") correctly uses an AppsFlyer OneLink (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with both `af_dp` (native deep link) and `af_web_dp` (web fallback) params set — properly configured for cross-platform continuity.

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 15 content/UI images, or empty `alt=""` for the purely decorative tracking pixels/beacons, to resolve accessibility gaps.
2. Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients.
