# WAREHOUSE SALE: UP TO 60% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the first CB2 email in a week that gives me a reason to act. "Up to 60% off" in huge type over a moody bedroom shot is clear in about one second. It's also the 60% warehouse sale that was buried in the 10/18 "STEALS OF THE WEEK" send, now with its own email. The price is finally the headline.
- I'm still tired of CB2. This is the sixth or seventh send in about six days: "STEALS OF THE WEEK" on 10/18, "BACK IN STOCK" and "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED" on 10/19, and "Make your coffee table a moment" earlier today. A promo that is also a repeat doesn't feel urgent. "Last-chance markdowns" is also vague. I don't know if this is a real clearance or a rebrand of the same stock.
- Would I click? Probably yes, once, to see whether there's a rug or lighting piece I can use for either renovation. I wouldn't forward it. Nothing in it is specific enough to send to anyone.

## What's working

- "UP TO 60% OFF" is the biggest thing on the page. The eye lands on the offer first, which Pottery Barn's Halloween hero didn't manage.
- The five stacked category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Decor, Rugs, Shop All) are easy to tap on a phone and match how I shop room by room.
- The earthy olive, cream and warm-brown palette is calm and consistent. It looks like CB2 and it suits what I'm designing.
- The rewards block says "Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars… at CB2 and Crate & Barrel." I'm a rewards-card person, so that's the line I read twice.
- The 15% off for texts sign-up and the "Let's celebrate" gift list are useful extras, with no extra scrolling to find them.

## What's weak

- Nothing is a product. There's no lamp, table or sofa with a price, only a bedroom photo with the buttons on top. Article's free-design email at least showed me something I could do next.
- "A curated selection of last-chance markdowns" is soft copy. The scarcity is implied, but I don't see a date for the sale. The only date in the email is the fine print: "Ends November 4."
- The "UP TO 60%" footnote sends me to a wall of legal text. It can't be combined with other offers, and the credit-card block at the bottom is dense. I'd also like to know about furniture shipping and returns on clearance items.
- The $1,000 app sweepstakes banner shows a cropped phone and blurry background. It's a second ask that competes with the sale, and it's an app giveaway I don't care about.
- This repeats the cadence problem. After six sends I'm in the same "CB2/Crate & Barrel family snooze" I described last time.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the single bedroom hero with 3–4 priced, clickable products under the 60% banner, such as a rug, a lamp and a sofa, each with a was/now price. That matches how Wayfair's pergola email worked with its "$134 off" badge. It would also make this email feel different from the other CB2 sends.
- 2. Put the end date in the hero: "Ends Nov 4." Use the same date in the subject or preheader.
- 3. Give "Shop All" more weight than the four category buttons, and make the category buttons say what's on offer, for example "Rugs from $X."
- 4. Add a line near the top about furniture shipping and returns on clearance pieces.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Fold the sweepstakes into the next send and don't run a separate CB2 promo every day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Warehouse Sale ends Nov 4: rugs, lighting, sofas up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last-chance markdowns: up to 60% off furniture & lighting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends Nov 4. Plus earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars at CB2 and Crate & Barrel.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rugs, lamps and sofas, marked down while they last. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint. The QA data shows 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels and 7 content images with no alt text. Header-level checks (unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source is truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 58 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 third-party pixels load over `http://`, which many clients block (mixed content). These are the Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-sync pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Cookie sync in email:** Ten sequential `pubN.php` cookieredir calls make up a cookie-sync chain. Most clients strip or proxy these pixels, so they are unreliable and add third-party requests.
- **First-party pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0276f2a6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are served over HTTPS and look like open tracking.
- **Font URLs:**
  - Several `@font-face` URLs use a double slash: `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`.
  - The `HNW05` font is pulled from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain.
  - Most clients ignore web fonts, so these requests are likely wasted. They would only matter if the server rejects `//` paths.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 7 images served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`:
  - `69c963ab-…jpg`
  - `e40aedf1-…jpg`
  - `16721ae5-…jpg`
  - `bb2d7174-…jpg`
  - `113ebcbd-…jpg`
  - `fef911c0-…jpg`
  - `8e957a75-…gif`
  
  The image-heavy sale content is not available to screen readers or to clients with images off. Whether these images are wrapped in links is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** The Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel also lack alt text. These are decorative, so use `alt=""` (an empty alt) rather than omitting the attribute.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on both `*` and `body`, and `*{line-height:100%}` is set globally. This blocks user text scaling, and 100% line-height can clip wrapped text.
- **Template leftover:** The source still contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, the charset is declared and the VML/MSO namespaces are present.

- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain is `mail.cb2.com`.
- **Body-level items:** The visible unsubscribe link, physical postal address and sender identification could not be checked in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 58 tracking links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't show their destination URLs. UTM parameters, landing-page alignment and the offer match to the "UP TO 60% OFF" warehouse sale are unverified.
- The tracked links and pixels carry `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` identifiers (`mi_cid=678efa99bfad4da1`). Whether those identifiers survive the redirect to cb2.com could not be tested.

## 7. Recommendations

2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images. Use `alt=""` on all tracking and cookie-sync pixels.
3. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS or remove them. The `pubN.php` chain adds third-party requests and is mostly blocked anyway.
