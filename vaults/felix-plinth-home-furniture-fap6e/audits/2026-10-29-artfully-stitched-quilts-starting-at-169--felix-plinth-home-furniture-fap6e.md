# Artfully stitched quilts starting at $169

## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and the maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is on top for what feels like the seventh send in a week. It's the same opener as the 10/29 "More to love 🧡" email and the 10/28 "Take an EXTRA 20% OFF" send. I skimmed straight past it. The subject promised quilts starting at $169, and the quilt hero is a lot better than that strip.
- The moody bedroom hero with "Beautifully Crafted Quilts / Quilts Ship Free" is the kind of thing that ends up in my Pinterest folder. The six-quilt grid (Cozy Cloud, Velvet Scalloped Stewart Plaid, Heritage Medallion) is easy to browse. But I don't see "$169" anywhere in the image, and no quilt tile shows a price. The subject's one concrete hook never shows up in the body. I'm doing a bedroom refresh across two houses, so I'm mildly interested, but I'd click once and not forward it.
- Then it dilutes. "Recommended Just For You" is a dining table, curtain hardware, a rug and Christmas stockings. That's the third category in one email, and it's the same product-spray feeling I get when the same lamp shows up in three brands' emails. The Christmas Shop banner, Design Crew, the app, the card and Affirm pile on after that. It's a long scroll with a lot of footer.

## What's working

- The "Beautifully Crafted Quilts" hero is a rich, layered bedroom shot. It fits the Pinterest-folder behavior and sells the mood before the product.
- "Quilts Ship Free" sits right inside the hero. For bulky bedding that's useful, and I care about shipping clarity on big items.
- The two-column quilt grid with named products is clean and scannable. Nothing is broken or overlapping.
- The "Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" block speaks to my two-house renovation. It's a real showroom-style service.
- The Pottery Barn sender and branding are instantly recognizable, so I know exactly which catalog this is.

## What's weak

- There's no "$169" anywhere in the rendered email. The subject's only concrete number never appears, and no quilt has a price under it.
- The maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles" strip is the same opener I've seen all week (10/27, 10/28, 10/29). It's wallpaper now.
- It's unclear whether the 20% off applies to the quilts. "Select styles" and "Exclusions apply" make me doubt the offer.
- "Recommended Just For You" is generic and off-topic: a dining table, a rug, stockings. It feels like a spray of products, not something picked for me.
- Too many CTAs and a long footer. The Christmas Shop banner competes with the quilts, and the Affirm and rewards-card blocks make the email feel like a catalog wrapped in disclaimers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price in the body. Show "From $169" on the hero or the first tile, and a price under each quilt. The subject makes a promise the email has to keep.
- 2. Retire or collapse the maroon sale strip when the email is single-category. One offer, one clear message: quilts, shipping free, starting at $169.
- 3. Cut "Recommended Just For You" to bedding-adjacent items, like shams, duvets and bed frames, so it stays on theme.
- 4. Pull the Christmas Shop banner out of a quilt email. Competing seasonal creative weakens both. Crate & Barrel and CB2 at least keep a send to one idea, like the Nina Garcia collection launch.
- 5. Add a swatch or sample cue, such as "Order free fabric swatches" or color and size info, since I care about palette consistency across two houses.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style email (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`) that is mostly sliced images. The automated QA run shows a 50% pass rate with no hard failures. The real issues are widespread missing alt text, three plain-HTTP image requests, and the recipient's address leaking into tracking-pixel URLs. The header-related warnings are likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Opaque tracking links:** 51 links were skipped as click-redirect domains (for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im…`). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Plain-HTTP open-tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`.
- **Other tracking pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` (third-party ad-network pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/6322bd15-…?mi_cid=…`. Neither has alt text. Both are 1×1 pixels and should carry `alt=""`.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,804 of 13,963 characters (70%+) are URLs. This is the long tracking-URL problem and can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt text:** about 36 images are flagged. Most of the email is sliced images, so with images blocked it renders as blank space or filenames.
  - Hero and banner images: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `slices_01.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, `slices_09.jpg` (in `W26_Bedding_B12_MINKStudioTest_Bedding/`), `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.

These are linked images, so screen readers get no link name.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`, all unlabeled. These are likely navigation or links.
  - Spacer: `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` should have `alt=""`.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** these may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - The `svopn` pixel above.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains leaked PHP (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`). The browser ignores it, but it is a template defect, and it could corrupt the rule that follows it in some parsers.
- **Selector that may not match:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only apply to elements whose class is exactly that value. I couldn't confirm any such elements in the truncated source.
- **`<html>` lang:** no `lang` attribute is visible on `<html>`.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Alt text:** add meaningful alt text to every linked image (hero, product, footer slices) and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. **HTTPS:** move the three `http://` assets, including `svopn`, to HTTPS.
3. **Email address in URLs:** stop putting the plaintext email in pixel and image URLs. Use an opaque subscriber key.
4. **Template cleanup:**
   - Remove the PHP `header()` text from the `<style>` block.
   - Fill in `<title>`.
   - Add `lang` to `<html>`.
   - Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. **Headers:** verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message source rather than the relay copy, and confirm the unsubscribe link and address in the footer.
6. **Landing pages:** follow the redirect chain on a sample of links to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages, since the HTTP probe skipped all 51.
