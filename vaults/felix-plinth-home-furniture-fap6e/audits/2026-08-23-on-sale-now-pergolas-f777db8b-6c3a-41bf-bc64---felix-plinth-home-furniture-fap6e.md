# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the subject said "pergolas" and it actually delivers — "On sale now: pergolas" is the very first tile under the hero, right next to "Must-Have Black Outdoor Privacy Screens" and "Our best gazebo deals." For once a Wayfair subject line isn't a bait-and-switch — a real contrast to yesterday's "duvet fills and inserts" send that buried the actual duvet inserts under a propane fire pit and some lamps. That's the good news, and honestly it's the only news, because after that pergola row this thing turns into the same kitchen-sink scroll Wayfair always runs: recliners, a shoe bench, a window AC unit, ceiling fans, wall clocks, patio dining chairs, a fountain, vases — anything with a SKU.
- Two of the product tiles in the "fresh-picked favorites" and "Picks you'll love" sections are just blank white boxes with a tiny blue dot icon where a product photo should be — broken images, plain as day. That's not a taste thing, that's a bug, and it's the kind of thing that makes me trust the email less on principle.
- I'm not clicking past the pergola/gazebo row, I'm not forwarding this — my wife and I are mid-reno on both houses and pergolas are actually relevant, but a 70%-off banner with no expiration date and a half-broken product grid reads like every other Wayfair send I get three times a week. Mark as read, maybe tap the pergola link if I'm bored on the train.

## What's working

- The hero-to-first-tile handoff is honest: "pergolas" in the subject, pergola/gazebo shots right at the top — no scavenger hunt like the duvet-inserts send.
- Outdoor category is genuinely on my reno list right now, so the top row (pergola, privacy screens, gazebo) is the one section I'd actually stop scrolling for.
- Klarna/Affirm "Get It Now, Pay Later" callout is at least legible and upfront, not buried in fine print.
- Star ratings are visible on some tiles (recliner, shoe bench), which is a small trust cue Crate & Barrel's rug email also leaned on well.

## What's weak

- Two product tiles are just blank white boxes with a placeholder icon — actual broken images, not just weak photography. That's a real render bug, not a style complaint.
- "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" banner has zero deadline or date — no "ends Monday," nothing. CB2's "End of Summer Sale is over tomorrow" at least gives me a reason to act today.
- After the pergola row it's a total category dump — recliners, ceiling fans, wall clocks, a fountain, vases, patio dining — nothing organized by room the way Crate & Barrel's rug email was ("living, dining, bedroom, kids"). I have to do the sorting myself.
- The "fountain" tile is just a giant purple block with one word on it — no price, no product shot next to the actual fountain photo above it. Feels like an ad slot that never got filled in.
- This is the third pergola/gazebo/outdoor-adjacent Wayfair email I've gotten this week counting the fire-pit cameo in the duvet send — cadence is getting noisy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image tiles before this goes out — two blank boxes in a promotional email is an easy trust hit, and it's the first thing my eye catches after the pergola row.
- 2. Put an actual end date on "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" — even "Ends Monday 9/1" would beat an open-ended 70% claim.
- 3. Reorganize the mid-email grid by room/category like Crate & Barrel did with rugs — outdoor, bedroom, living — instead of alternating recliners, fans, and vases at random.
- 4. Cut the email down after the outdoor row — if the subject is "pergolas," give me two more outdoor add-ons (that privacy screen, patio dining) and stop there instead of sprawling into shoe benches and wall clocks.
- 5. Finish or drop the "fountain" purple placeholder block — right now it looks like an unfinished ad unit, not a merchandising decision.
