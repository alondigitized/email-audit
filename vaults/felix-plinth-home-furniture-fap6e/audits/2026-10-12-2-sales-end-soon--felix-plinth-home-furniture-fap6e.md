# 2 SALES END SOON
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. By my count this is the twelfth send in about five days, so I opened it already numb. Of the recent ones this is the most organized. It's the same Long Weekend Sale, but two clear offers are stacked: up to 60% off sitewide and up to 25% off upholstery. I'm furnishing two houses, so the upholstery block with the deep green velvet sofa got a real look from me.
- But I've now seen free shipping on $99+ in nearly every send since 10/10. Last night's "This sale pairs so well with free shipping" had the same banner, and this is the third time this week it's been a hero block. The subject also tells me nothing I couldn't guess from the sender name. "2 sales end soon" is accurate, but it isn't a reason to open.
- Would I click? Probably, but only on the upholstery block, and only because I'm in the middle of a sofa decision. I wouldn't forward it. I'd move it to the Pinterest-adjacent folder and keep going.

## What's working

- The "Ends tomorrow" tag over the 60% hero gives me a deadline I can believe. Two days ago the urgency was vague.
- The two-offer structure is easy to read. 60% sitewide and 25% upholstery each get their own full-bleed block, so I can see which discount applies to what.
- The green velvet sofa photo is the best image in the email. Moody, editorial, the kind of thing I'd actually save.
- The marble-and-glass sconce in the lighting block is a specific product. It's a real item I could picture in a hallway, not just a category banner.
- The Rewards footer ("Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars") speaks to how I buy. It's the one place the email touches rewards math.

## What's weak

- The free shipping block again. It's the same $99+ offer I've been shown all week, so it works as filler.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is a wall. The upholstery exclusions and the 60% exceptions are buried there, and "up to" does a lot of work. Wayfair at least puts a dollar-off badge on the product.
- The "Shop the Sale" buttons are small, thin white rectangles on busy photos. On my phone the tap targets feel tight.
- There are no product names, prices, or swatch and sample links. For a sofa or bed I want to see fabric options and ship times, and the email gives me neither.
- The lighting block isn't tied to the sale. I can't tell whether the sconce is discounted.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the shipping block or fold it into a thin banner. It has run in every send this week, and the sconce is a stronger fourth block.
- 2. Put real products under the upholstery hero: three sofas with sale prices and fabric swatch dots, plus a "Order free swatches" link. That's the biggest lift for someone like me.
- 3. Show the 60% exclusions in plain language right below the hero, so the fine print isn't doing the disclosing.
- 4. Make the CTAs larger and filled, with a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop upholstery sale".
- 5. Lead with the Rewards 10% back line. It's a real differentiator and it's buried.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email renders with one confirmed defect: an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has widespread missing alt text, plain-HTTP tracking pixels, and header checks that could not be verified through the relay.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 58 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed failure.
- 10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?...` through `pub10.php`. The UCM beacon is also on `http://`: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`. Clients and proxies that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them, so the retargeting and sync calls will silently fail for those recipients.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Three content images have no alt text:
  - `3e3d16d4-b941-44a6-8309-bb2eb5254612.jpg`
  - `844a5767-6c7f-4bb5-abde-10ad4a8c06f6.jpg`
