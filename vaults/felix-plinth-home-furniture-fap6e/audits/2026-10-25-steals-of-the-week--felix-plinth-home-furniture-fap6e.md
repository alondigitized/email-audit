# STEALS OF THE WEEK
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the "everything on one page" CB2 send. It's a roundup of three things I've already seen this week: the Semi-Annual Dining Event, the Warehouse Sale and the app sweepstakes. Nothing here is new to me. On 10/24 "FINAL DAYS: The Semi-Annual Dining Event" gave me the dining offer, and on 10/23 "WAREHOUSE SALE | UP TO 60% OFF" gave me the other one. This one stacks them again, and I recognize it as a recap. The big room photos are lovely, though. The green velvet headboard and the burnished-wood dining set are what I'd save to my Pinterest folder.
- "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" is a clear headline, and the numbers (30%, 60%) are big. But the strongest pitch in the email is the thin Thanksgiving strip at the top: "Order in-stock furniture by 11/17." I'm renovating two houses, so a delivery cutoff matters more to me than a $1,000 app giveaway. That strip is crammed into a tiny banner, and the giveaway gets a full-bleed hero. Backwards.
- Would I click? Maybe the dining hero, since I'm still shopping chairs. Would I forward it? No. Mostly this is a mark-as-read, and I'd keep the Thanksgiving date in my head.

## What's working

- The 30% and 60% numerals are huge and readable on a phone without zooming.
- The dining hero has a real category with a real room. Those curved velvet chairs are what I'm shopping for.
- The Thanksgiving delivery date (11/17) answers the furniture-shipping question I always have.
- The "NOT SOLD OUT, YET" bouclé sofa gives me a specific product and a little urgency.
- The footer covers the 10% Rewards Dollars card and the app. That's rewards math I care about, since I buy on my highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- It repeats the dining and warehouse heroes I got on 10/23 and 10/24. It's the same offers in a fresh layout.
- Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel at least vary the product by send. Here the same offers keep coming back, so I'm numb to them.
- The "$1,000" app giveaway has nothing to do with my renovation. The "DOWNLOAD THE CB2 APP" button is also hard to read over the busy photo.
- The "SHOP NOW" text on the dining and warehouse heroes is small and thin over the photos. These are weak tap targets on a phone.
- The "up to" language with a dagger and a wall of fine print makes me wonder what is actually excluded. I can't tell whether 30% covers the chairs or just the tabletops.
- The whole thing is very long. The sofa is the only specific product, and it sits near the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the delivery cutoff as the hero ("Order by 11/17 for Thanksgiving") and show the actual dining set. Cut the app sweepstakes or move it to the footer.
- 2. Show 3-4 named products with prices and "was/now" figures instead of just category percentages. Wayfair's price-forward grids do this better.
- 3. Spell out what the discount covers, such as "30% off dining tables and chairs," instead of leaving it to a dagger.
- 4. Give the CTA buttons solid backgrounds so they're visible over the photos.
- 5. Keep sends from repeating the same two offers more than twice in one week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dining sets 30% off — order by 11/17 for Thanksgiving`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The velvet dining chairs are on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 60% off the Warehouse Sale. In-stock pieces ship in time for 11/17.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a standard XHTML table-based template with an Outlook/VML fallback. The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The findings that matter are 11 plain-HTTP tracking pixels, about 20 images with no `alt` attribute, and missing unsubscribe and authentication headers that the AgentMail relay may have stripped. The HTML source is truncated, so the body, footer, and link destinations could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **55 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains, and dead links are unverified.
- **11 tracking pixels use plain `http://`.** All are Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-sync or UCM pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients and proxies may block or rewrite these. Gmail's image proxy fetches over HTTPS, so the pixels likely fail silently there, and some clients show mixed-content warnings.
- **Ten sequential cookie-sync pixels (`pub1`–`pub10`)** add 10 third-party requests on open. This is a load and privacy cost, and a deliverability risk on strict filters.
- **First-party tracking pixels** are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=<64-hex hash>&mi_...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/d1f3090c-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. Both are HTTPS. The `mi_u` value is a recipient hash embedded in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 20 images have no `alt` attribute.**
  - Four are content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`: `2a60c92d-…jpg`, `6f4a0ba7-…jpg`, `a3217878-…jpg`, `6c46a2ff-…jpg`. If these carry offers or copy, they are invisible with images off and to screen readers.
  - The rest are tracking pixels: Dotomi, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`. Add `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">`** blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Leftover template placeholder in the head:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is cosmetic but shows the template wasn't cleaned up (and "IMPUT" is a typo).
- **Font declarations are inconsistent.**
  - One `@font-face` for "Lineto Brown Sub" lists only an `.eot` plus `?#iefix`, and others use `//Styles/` double-slash paths (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`).
  - The "HNW05" font loads from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain.
  - Most clients ignore web fonts and fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif, so impact is low.
- **Media query gaps:**
  - The mobile breakpoint is `max-width: 418px`, which misses many larger phones, since common widths are 390–430px.
  - `min-width: 690px` and `768px` both set `img` max-widths, and the 690px rule is overridden by the 768px one above that width.
- **Global rules to watch:** `*{max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` and `p{padding:0 !important; margin:0 !important}`. They are intentional Outlook/Gmail fixes, but `line-height:100%` on `*` can clip text in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The only per-recipient data seen is the `mi_u` hash in the pixel URL, and the rest of the body was not visible, so tokens there are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe is missing** (QA WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this may be a capture artifact rather than a sender failure.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is missing** (QA WARN). Without it, Gmail and Yahoo's one-click requirement for bulk senders is not met. The same relay caveat applies.
- **Authentication-Results is missing**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a subdomain, so alignment is plausible but not shown.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** the visible unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and sender identification in the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 55 tracking links were skipped, so landing URLs, UTM parameters, and redirect targets are unknown. Nothing in the visible source (all within the head) shows `utm_*` parameters. Click-redirect wrapping hides the destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch all Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop them. Consolidate or remove the 10 sequential `pub1`–`pub10` cookie-sync calls.
2. Add `alt` to the 4 content images, and `alt=""` to every tracking pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the original headers. If the relay strips them, re-run QA against raw `.eml` to separate relay artifacts from real gaps.
5. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the raw message to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
6. Resolve a sample of the 55 tracking links (follow redirects) to check destinations and UTM parameters.
7. Clean the template: remove the "IMPUT HERE" placeholder, fix the `//Styles/` double slashes, and widen the mobile breakpoint.
8. Increase the HTML capture limit so the footer and body can be audited.
