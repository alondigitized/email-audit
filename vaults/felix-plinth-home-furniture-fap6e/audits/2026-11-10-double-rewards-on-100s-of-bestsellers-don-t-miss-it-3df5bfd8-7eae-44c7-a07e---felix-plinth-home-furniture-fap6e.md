# Double Rewards on 100s of bestsellers. Don’t miss it!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kids sender again, and I know this email already. By my count it's the fourth Crate & Barrel send in two days. I got "Don't miss out: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards!" earlier today, and "Dinnerware, serveware & more up to 30% off + 2x Rewards" yesterday. The red "don't miss out: the fall sales event: up to 50% off" strip and the "last Double Rewards this year!" block are the same pieces I've already seen, with a new subject line on top.
- Underneath, it's a competent merchandising email. The nursery hero, the bedroom bestsellers, the nursery must-haves and the playroom picks are all laid out in clean two-up product grids. But I'm renovating two houses, not furnishing a nursery. Crib, stroller and nursery-chair content isn't for me, and the "bestsellers for their bedroom" grid is the only part with any pull. The render is also so long that the offer repeats before I've finished scrolling.
- Would I click? Probably not from this one. I'd skim it, and the bedroom grid might go into the Pinterest-adjacent folder. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the main Crate & Barrel sender to send the same 2x Rewards deal with furniture I actually want.

## What's working

- The 2x Rewards offer is concrete. "Up to 50% off" and "last Double Rewards this year!" give me a real reason to time a purchase on the highest-rewards card.
- The product grids are clean and consistently structured, with a "Shop Bedroom Furniture" style button under each. The CTAs are specific and easy to tap.
- The "Shop Now / Apply Now" pair under the first rewards block makes the card pitch obvious.
- The section headers ("bestsellers for their bedroom," "must-haves for their nursery," "fun picks for their playroom") make the email easy to scan.
- The TheDesignDesk "Book a Free Appointment" block is the one thing here that fits someone doing two renovations.

## What's weak

- The kids and nursery focus misses me. The hero is a crib and the middle is strollers and nursery chairs.
- The rewards message appears at the top and again in the red block near the bottom, so the email repeats itself. The earnings fine print is small, and the legal footer is a dense wall of text.
- The cadence is fatiguing. This is the fourth send in two days, and "Don't miss it" is the same urgency line as the 11/10 "Don't miss it: Up to 50% off the Fall Sales Event!"
- The email is very long. The strollers, the app promo and the design-services pitch pile up near the end.
- The small type under the product tiles is hard to read at phone size. I can't compare the rewards math without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the repeat and consolidate.** Send one rewards email per 48 hours. If the Kids sender must go out, make it a genuinely different message, not a re-skin of the main sale.
- 2. **Lead with the offer, not the crib.** Put the "2x Rewards, ends [date]" message above or on the hero image. Show the exact earn math in readable text.
- 3. **Segment by behavior.** Someone who browsed bedroom and living furniture shouldn't get nursery as the hero. CB2 and Pottery Barn Design Services both at least varied their angle.
- 4. **Cut the page by a third.** Drop the app block or the stroller grid, and keep the design-services pitch higher.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last 2x Rewards of the year: bedroom furniture picks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2x Rewards ends soon: bestsellers up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn double on bedroom, nursery and playroom bestsellers. Last chance this year.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 50% off plus 2x Rewards on your Crate & Barrel card. Ends soon.`
- Competitor reference points:
- Wayfair ran the same clearout twice, but it at least counted down in the subject ("Less than TWO DAYS LEFT").
- Pottery Barn Design Services led with a real home-refresh hook, a $3,000 gift card, instead of another sale strip.
- CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" was at least a distinct angle.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed issues are about 45 images with no `alt` attribute, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and a link layer that is almost entirely click-redirects, so destinations could not be checked. The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and don't show a defect in the sender.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **112 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM survival are unverified. The HTML source I was given is truncated, so I can't confirm any link-level defect.
- **Five third-party pixels** load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp). These are extra third-party requests that add privacy and consent exposure and slow rendering.
- **First-party pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel), `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3a3da172-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`, and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, which appears 6 times.
- **Repeated image:** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` loads 6 times with the same `mi_u` token. This is redundant, or possibly a per-module tracking design.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on roughly 45 images.**
  - Content images: all `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, for example `a13bcc00-1484-457d-83fd-303c1441893b.jpg` and `ad301dcd-e18a-4821-a956-483003ba7759.gif`.
  - Animated GIF: `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide_SecondaryStory.gif`.
  - Tracking pixels (rlcdn, `o.gif`, `dv.` and `mi.` images) should carry `alt=""`.
  - If the content images are image-only links, screen readers and image-blocked clients get no link text.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" ...>` are unclosed under XHTML.
  - `format-detection` is declared 3 times with overlapping values.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production, and the `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` block is empty.
- **Weak preheader and title:** the `<title>` is generic ("Crate & Barrel") and doesn't match the subject line. Preheader presence couldn't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Possible CSS conflict:** the `img {max-width:600px}` (≥768px) and `img {max-width:640px}` (≥640px) media queries are ordered so the 640px rule wins at desktop widths. This is probably unintended, but I can't confirm it from the truncated source.
- **Size and plain-text part:** 22,788 of 32,356 plain-text characters are URLs (70%+), so the text alternative is mostly tracking links. Image-heavy content with missing alt text also leaves little readable content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) were reported by QA or visible in the HTML provided. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are resolved per-recipient identifiers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is consistent with the image and tracking subdomains.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 112 links point to tracking redirects, so UTM parameters and landing page content could not be checked. Image sources are first-party (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`), plus one off-brand CDN (`s7d5.scene7.com`, Adobe Scene7).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels, and consider `role="presentation"` and `width="1" height="1"`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, make the title relevant to the campaign, and close the XHTML void tags (`<meta … />`).
4. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` tags and the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
5. Re-run QA against raw SMTP headers, or enable header passthrough on the relay, to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve a sample of the 112 redirect links with a follow-redirects check to confirm landing URLs and UTM carry-through.
7. Review whether five LiveRamp pixels and six repeated `f3c0d…png` calls are all needed. Fewer calls reduce load time and consent exposure.
8. Add a meaningful plain-text version with readable copy and fewer raw tracking URLs.
