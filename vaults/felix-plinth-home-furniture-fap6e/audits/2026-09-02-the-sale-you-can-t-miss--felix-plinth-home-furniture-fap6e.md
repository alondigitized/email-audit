# The sale you can't miss
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again — this is nineteen or twenty emails from them this month by my count, and I opened this one mostly out of morbid curiosity at this point. Credit where it's due: the "LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" hero over the sunset horizon shot is genuinely nice-looking, moodier and more editorial than the flat burgundy banner Pottery Barn keeps reusing. But then it just... keeps going. Another sale banner, a runner rug, a six-tile category grid, a clearance banner, then app/rewards/text-signup footer blocks. It's the exact same skeleton as every other CB2 send this week, just reshuffled.
- I'm not clicking. Nothing here tells me anything new — no new product, no new price, just "sale" restated four different ways down the page. This is a mark-as-read, and honestly getting close to an unsubscribe candidate given the frequency. Not forwarding it to anyone; there's nothing here worth sharing.

## What's working

- The horizon/sunset hero for "THE LABOR DAY SALE" is the best-looking creative CB2's sent me this cycle — more atmosphere than Pottery Barn's flat "Up to 70% off" burgundy block I've now screenshotted twice.
- The double rewards banner up top ("20% back in Reward Dollars with the CB2 Visa card") is a real, specific offer with a clear date window (through Sept 9th) — more concrete than most of the filler CB2's sent this month.
- Category grid (Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Shop All) at least organizes the catalog visually instead of dumping everything into one collage.
- "UP TO 60% OFF" clearance banner near the bottom is a bigger number than the 50% hero, which is honestly a little disorienting but at least it's specific.

## What's weak

- I've now gotten so many CB2 emails this month that "Labor Day Sale, up to 50% off" has completely lost its urgency — it's the same beat as 2026-09-01's "Keep-forever decor" send and 2026-08-31's rewards-card email, just recombined.
- No product prices anywhere except the rug — the lamp, candle holder, coffee table, dinnerware are all shown with zero pricing, so I can't tell what "50% off" actually means for anything I'd buy.
- Two competing discount claims (50% off hero vs. 60% off clearance) in the same email with no explanation of which applies to what — that's the kind of buried-logic thing that makes me trust the number less.
- This is basically the same structure as CB2's 2026-09-02 "LABOR DAY SALE IS ON" send from earlier today — two near-identical Labor Day emails same day is Crate & Kids-level overkill (four sends in a day from that sister brand, and I still don't have a nursery).

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending near-duplicate Labor Day emails same-day — consolidate today's two into one send, that alone would do more for my opinion of CB2 than any copy tweak.
- 2. Put a price or a specific dollar range under at least the lamp and coffee table tiles — Article's "Up to 40% off" send this week at least led with a real number up top; CB2 needs that at the product level too.
- 3. Reconcile the 50% vs 60% off messaging — either the clearance section is separate and should say so explicitly, or drop one of the numbers.
- 4. Add a "recommended for you" module like Pottery Barn's latest send did — even a small personalized block would break up six generic sends in a row.
