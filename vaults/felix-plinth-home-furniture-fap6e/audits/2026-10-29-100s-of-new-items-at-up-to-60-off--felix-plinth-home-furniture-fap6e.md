# 100s of new items at up to 60% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Crate & Barrel send today, and this one comes from the Kids sender. I'm renovating two houses, not nursing a newborn, so my gut reaction was "wrong list." The hero copy, "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off*," is the biggest discount I've seen from C&B this week, and the "Shop Clearance" button is clear. But the rest is strollers, car seats, a crib mattress and a Halloween treat bag. None of that is my project.
- It also repeats what I got earlier today. The 10/29 "New markdowns added! Up to 60% off →" and "New markdowns on furniture, decor & more just landed 👀" already pushed the same clearance beat. The nav strip does include "Shop Furniture," "Shop Rugs" and "Shop Decor & Lighting," so there's a thin path back to my category. I would skim it, maybe tap Shop Furniture, and not forward it to anyone. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero headline and the "Shop Clearance" button are big and readable. The offer lands in about two seconds.
- The terracotta nav block with Shop Furniture, Shop Rugs, and Shop Decor & Lighting gave me a route into my category.
- The product grid has real prices, like the $1,499 Jenny Lind dresser and the $14.97 pumpkin bag with the old price struck through. That's useful for a price-tracker like me.
- The "Book a Free Appointment" design desk module speaks to a renovator. It's free, which I like.

## What's weak

- Most of the products are baby gear: Nuna PIPA strollers, a Doona car seat, a Babyletto recliner, a crib mattress. This feels targeted at new parents, not a 36-year-old furnishing two houses.
- The hero is soft. Autumn leaves under white text, a small "Selling fast! Shop now, while quantities last" line, and an asterisk on the discount with fine print at the bottom. Pottery Barn's maroon "Extra 20% off" strip is blunter.
- It's the fourth C&B send today, and the "up to 60%" clearance message is the same one from the 10/29 sends this morning. The cadence is noisy.
- The Crate Card block and the app promo ("chance at $500") pile on after the product grid. The email gets long, and the real offer gets diluted.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, including a "15% new mover discount" that I couldn't read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household. Send furniture, rug and lighting clearance to me, and strollers to new parents. That one change would lift this email most.
- 2. Put the markdown deadline in the hero. "Ends Sunday" would be credible urgency in place of "Selling fast!"
- 3. Cut the card and app modules from clearance sends, or move them to a separate email.
- 4. Show the discount on each product. "$1,499 → 40% off" is the math I want to see, and I want to know if it ships free.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a clean table-based XHTML structure with Outlook/MSO fallbacks and mobile breakpoints. The QA run flagged 3 compliance and deliverability warnings, which are likely relay artifacts. The one confirmed defect is missing alt text on many images, and the pinch-zoom block and leftover template text also come from the HTML source. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, CTAs and most link hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 65 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Redirect destinations and link health are unconfirmed.
- **Repeated pixels:** The same `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` image is requested 6 times. This is redundant and probably a template loop.
- **Third-party beacons:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are 5 LiveRamp pixels. Together with `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/9da421bf-…` they add up to many tracking requests. Image-blocking clients will suppress all of them.
- **Subscriber identifier in URLs:** The `mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `s=dbe8da71…` query values appear to be a 64-character hex hash tied to the subscriber. It is embedded in third-party image URLs. This is not necessarily a defect, but it is worth a privacy review.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 25 images. The content images are the real problem:
  - `…/m/1/f1bdc9e3-…jpg`, `67c84df5-…jpg`, `16d802ed-…jpg`, `5b49ac67-…jpg`, `59d3a675-…jpg`, `07e15d43-…jpg`, `eb8bcaba-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`
  - The hero `…scene7.com/…/20261101%5FCBK%5FFall%5FClearance%5FPM%5Fv3%5F100s…`
  - Two GIFs: `57ac4065-…gif` and `33367d69-…gif`
  - With images off, or in a screen reader, "100s of new items at up to 60% off" and the product tiles carry no text equivalent. The QA check cannot tell a missing `alt` from `alt=""`, so confirm each in the full source.
- **Tracking pixels:** The `rlcdn`, `mi.`, `dv.` and `o.gif` pixels need `alt=""`, not descriptive text. The flag on these is low-severity noise.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times, once combined and then separately for date, address and telephone. The combined tag sets `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`. This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Leftover template text:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It is unfilled and misspelled, and it adds nothing in rendering but leaks template scaffolding.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses a bare `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. Strict validators would want `&amp;`. The title also does not match the "Crate & Barrel Kids" sender or the subject. The source shown may be decoded, so this is low confidence.
- **Global rule:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` applies to every paragraph. Check that it doesn't override intended left-aligned copy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the portion provided. The subscriber hash in pixel URLs is resolved, not a raw token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` header. The tool itself notes that the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed, not a verified sender fault.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post:** There is no `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058). The same relay caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check it on a direct-delivery copy.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender uses the subdomain `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Validate alignment on a direct-delivery copy.
- **Footer:** The visible in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They could not be confirmed. All 65 links were skipped as tracking-redirect URLs, and the hrefs are not in the truncated source.
- **Landing page:** Landing-page alignment with the "100s of new items at up to 60% off" claim is unverified for the same reason.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, starting with the hero and the product tiles. Use `alt=""` explicitly on the tracking pixels.
2. **Viewport:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Duplicate pixel:** Dedupe the 6× `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel.
4. **Template cleanup:** Drop the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT…` comment and the redundant `format-detection` tags, and escape `&` in `<title>`.
5. **Headers:** Re-test with a direct-delivery copy, or one with full headers, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Treat the three WARNs as unresolved until then.
6. **Links:** Resolve the redirect chains on the 65 skipped links to check UTMs, destination status and landing-page match.
