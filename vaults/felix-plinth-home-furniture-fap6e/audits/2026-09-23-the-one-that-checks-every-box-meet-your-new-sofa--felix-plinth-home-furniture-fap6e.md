# The one that checks every box. Meet your new sofa →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a lot. "The sofa upgrade you've been dreaming of just arrived" up top is a decent hook, but then Crate & Barrel just... keeps going. Lounge collection, Cortina, Griffin, Serenity, Cosgrove — five different sofa collections stacked one after another like a catalog got dumped into an email. I'm renovating two houses right now so in theory I'm exactly who should want this, but even I can't process five near-identical sofa shots in one scroll.
- The free swatches call-out ("order 10 free swatches so you can... see what fits your space") is genuinely the most useful thing in here — that's the kind of low-commitment, high-utility CTA that actually matches how I shop for furniture, since I'm not buying a sofa off a JPEG. But it's buried two-thirds down, after I've already scrolled past what feels like the entire spring catalog. Article's "storage solutions" email got to the point in one scroll; this one makes me work for it.
- Would I click? Maybe the swatches link, if I'm patient enough to get there. Would I forward this to a friend? No — there's no single hero moment to forward, it's a wall of product. This gets marked read, swatches link bookmarked for later, rest ignored.

## What's working

- The swatch program ("order 10 free swatches") is a real furniture-shopper feature and speaks directly to my "will this actually match my floors" anxiety.
- "1000s of items ship free every day" up top removes a real friction point before I even start scrolling.
- The room photography is consistently moody/warm and on-brand — no jarring style shifts collection to collection.
- The Design Desk callout (book a free appointment) is a smart touch for someone juggling two renovations — I don't have bandwidth to pick every piece myself.

## What's weak

- Five sofa collections in one email (Lounge, Cortina, Griffin, Serenity, Cosgrove) with almost no differentiation in the copy — I can't tell why I'd pick one over another just from "new: Griffin collection."
- No offer. Crate & Barrel's own CB2 sibling sent me "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" a couple weeks back and at least had a price lever to pull — this is just product, product, product with zero %/$ off anywhere I can see.
- It's exhausting on mobile — this is the fourth or fifth Crate & Barrel-family email I've had to process this cycle (on top of the Texas-store-opening one I already flagged twice, and the Crate & Kids shared-room-guide emails), and none of them have had an actual discount.
- The bottom third pivots hard into kitchen tools and dining tables after five sofa sections — feels like three separate emails got merged into one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one hero sofa collection plus a single supporting product, and move the other four collections into a follow-up send or a "shop the full collection" link — right now it reads like a lookbook, not a targeted email.
- 2. Pull the free-swatches CTA up near the top — that's the actual differentiator versus Wayfair/Article and it's wasted at position six.
- 3. Add an actual price signal. Even "starting at $X" on the hero sofa would beat zero pricing info in a five-collection email.
- 4. Tighten the CTA copy — "Shop Now" appears at least twice and is generic; "Shop Griffin," "Shop Cortina" etc. are fine but a stronger single CTA on the hero would help more than six duplicate buttons.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free swatches, real fabric, zero guesswork — new sofas`
- **Subject Alt B:** `5 new sofa collections, 1 free swatch kit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order 10 free swatches before you commit to a color`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Design Desk appointments open — free room planning`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Standard ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud email template with heavy click-tracking wrapping and pixel-based analytics; core structure is sound but missing compliance headers and alt text across all images.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 68 tracking/click-redirect links present (`mi.crateandbarrel.com` link-wrapping domain), consistent with a Marketing Cloud/ExactTarget send — not independently probed as this is expected redirector behavior, not a defect.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels present: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/*/o.gif`, `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp/RLCDN, fired 5x with sequential `n=1`–`n=5` params), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...` (likely DV/attribution pixel). No issues with presence, but volume/redundancy of pixels (5x same RLCDN pixel) is worth noting for load performance.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- 33 images missing `alt` attributes, including all product/hero imagery (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/...`) and Scene7 assets (`s7d5.scene7.com/...`). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or fall back to filename/URL fragments.
- Tracking pixels (RLCDN, mi.crateandbarrel.com) also flagged for missing alt — these should carry `alt=""` (not omitted) to be explicitly ignored by assistive tech, but this is a minor/non-blocking pattern issue.
- Template includes standard MSO conditionals, Outlook font-fix classes, and mobile media queries (`max-width:460px`) — no structural rendering defects found in the visible source.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FIELD%%`, `{{token}}`) or broken personalization syntax visible in the truncated source.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe from the mail client's UI is unavailable; QA notes this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a sender-side omission, but should be confirmed against raw headers at the source MTA.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — cannot confirm authentication status from available data; flagged as unknown, not failed.
- No footer/unsubscribe link content was present in the truncated HTML to verify CAN-SPAM physical-address/unsubscribe-link requirements — cannot confirm compliance either way from available evidence.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Cannot assess — the 68 tracking links are wrapped through `mi.crateandbarrel.com` redirectors and were not resolved/probed, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible in the provided data.

7. **Recommendations**
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all product/lifestyle images; add explicit `alt=""` to tracking pixels and decorative images.
- Verify raw SMTP headers at the source (pre-relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) — current absence may be an AgentMail relay artifact rather than a true compliance gap.
