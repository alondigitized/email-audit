# COMFORTERS + SETS for less. *MAJOR* markdowns ➡
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was that this is Wayfair's Way Day teaser wearing a comforter subject line. The top banner says "80% OFF, WAY DAY, OCT. 24–26, SHOP EARLY," and that's the only real offer in the email. The "Comforters & Sets for Less" tile shows up second, in a two-by-two grid under a "score styles you ❤️" header. There's no price, no percentage and no "MAJOR markdown" anywhere near it.
- It also looks broken in several places. Six or so tiles are blank white boxes with a tiny broken-image icon: Makeup Vanities, Trellises, Wall Art Clearance, Patio Seating, Kitchen Sinks and Kitchen Mats. Near the bottom there's a huge empty white rectangle, roughly 700px tall, before the footer. This is the same problem as the 10/19 glassware email, where the hero was blank. Wayfair keeps shipping these, and a brand that big should catch it.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim it, notice the 10/24–26 date, and mark it read. I'd forward it to nobody. I'm renovating two houses, and a bedding tile with no price doesn't help me.

## What's working

- The purple Way Day banner is the one clear offer, with a date range. It's the only thing that told me why to come back.
- The comforter tile has a nice photo of a sage-green plaid bedding set in a calm room. That's the kind of image I'd save to my Pinterest folder.
- The "Recliners / Customer Favorites" block is bold and easy to tap. The big "Shop Now" button is the clearest CTA in the email.
- The Afterpay/Klarna "pay later" block is easy to read, and for big furniture purchases it's useful.
- The footer has Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing and Professional. That's the loyalty and financing material I'd care about, even though it sits at the very bottom.

## What's weak

- **The subject is mostly a bait-and-switch.** It says "*MAJOR* markdowns," but I can't find a single markdown in the body. There's no price, no % and no badge on the comforters.
- **The broken images are everywhere.** About six tiles are empty boxes with alt text, and the huge blank block before the footer looks like a failed module. It looks sloppy and I don't trust it.
- **The category mix is incoherent.** Makeup vanities, kitchen mats, trellises, patio seating and filing cabinets sit together in one email. It feels like a catalog dumped into a template, with no relevance to me.
- **There's no shipping info.** For furniture I want delivery windows and freight costs, and there's no swatch or sample mention either.
- **It's the Nth Wayfair send this week.** After 10/19's "Outdoor Decorations" and "New glassware," and the 10/18 tile and pergola sends, this feels like a fire hose.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken images first.** Add fallback images, or pull any tile that doesn't load. A blank 700px block is the biggest damage in this email.
- 2. **Put the comforter offer in the hero.** Show a real price ("Comforter sets from $39") or a "up to X% off" badge on the first tile, so the subject's promise is kept.
- 3. **Cut the mix to two or three coherent categories.** Bedding, rugs and recliners would work. Drop the makeup vanities and kitchen mats.
- 4. **Add delivery cues.** Something like "Ships free, arrives by Oct 27" or a swatch/sample line would help a furniture buyer.
- 5. **Move Rewards and financing higher.** Put one line above the fold, not buried under the grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Comforter sets from $39 + Way Day Oct. 24–26`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bedding for both houses: sets up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plaid, linen and washable sets, plus early Way Day access.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on comforters. Way Day starts Oct. 24.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules. QA found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The main problems are 28 images without alt text, 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, and a campaign-parameter mismatch in the app deep link. Header-based compliance checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 material links were probed. 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting by Wayfair), which is inconclusive rather than a failure. Affected URLs include:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=2`
  - `.../comforters-%26-sets-for-less-se30625.html?eid=306019...`
  - `.../cozy%2c-thick-pile-washable-rugs-se78341.html?eid=554386...`
  - both `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links
- **4 links were never checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out. They are neither pass nor fail: large-capacity shoe racks, lighted makeup vanities, `/buy-now-pay-later~b547.html`, and `/furniture/sb0/recliners-c413904.html`. 48 more links were skipped by ranking, so footer, social and utility links were not verified.
- **Mismatched campaign parameter in the OneLink app link.** It carries `c=Triggered`, but the same URL has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`, which marks a batch send. This will misattribute AppsFlyer campaign data.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` sends users without the app to `http://`, which depends on a redirect.
- **Insecure pixels.** 20 impression pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019`. They are 2x1 images and are likely blocked or rewritten by some clients, so impression counts will undercount. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS.
- **Malformed image URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is invalid percent-encoding. Strict parsers may fail to load it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **28 images have no alt text** (QA count, confirmed in the findings list):
  - 7 content or banner images:
    - `WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_SkinnyTop-Sale`
    - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`
    - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png`
    - `wfna_0331_bnpl_email_banner_01.png`
    - `image.jpg` (`.../118239328/image.jpg`)
    - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`
    - `bnpl_legal_disclaimer.png`
  - 20 `imp` pixels and the `b.php` open pixel. These are tracking images and should carry `alt=""`.
- **Linked images fall back to filenames.** The probe reported link text as `[image: WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skin...]` and `[image: wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purpl...]`. Screen readers will announce filenames, and the links go to `/daily-sales` and `/`.
- **`bnpl_legal_disclaimer.png` is legal text in an image.** Without alt text, the disclaimer is invisible when images are blocked.
- **Mobile CSS is in a `<style>` block.** Gmail clients that strip `<style>` will lose the responsive rules. The `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` hooks suggest Gmail is handled, but the truncated source gave me no way to check inlining.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible URLs contain resolved identifiers (`_emr=a572bbb8-...`, per-link `_eml=...`) and no unrendered `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens. The HTML source was truncated, so this covers only the portion provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed failures:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. QA notes these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status and one-click unsubscribe support are unknown. Re-check against the raw message from a direct mailbox.
- **Sender:** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain. Alignment can't be assessed without the headers.
- **Footer not verifiable.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears, and the truncated source doesn't show the footer or postal address. Neither can be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121677`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16546`, `mdlid`. This is consistent across all links and is fine for internal tracking. It will not map into GA or other third-party analytics without a custom mapping.
- **Landing alignment:** the "Comforters & Sets for Less" link goes to `/daily-sales/filters/comforters-%26-sets-for-less-se30625.html?eid=306019`, which matches the subject line. The final page content was not verified because the probe got a 429.
- **Redundant parameters:** `eid=306019` and `unifiedeventid=306019` carry the same value. This is cosmetic.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the 7 content images, and `alt=""` to the 21 tracking pixels. Give linked banners an alt that describes the destination, such as "Shop Wayfair daily sales".
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
3. Fix the `%oo` in `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` (probably `%25` or a stray character). Rename the asset and re-upload it.
4. Correct `c=Triggered` in the OneLink URLs to match the batch send, or confirm the AppsFlyer mapping is intentional.
5. Put the BNPL legal disclaimer in live text, or at least give the image a full-text alt.
6. Re-run the unprobed and 429 links with throttling or a longer time budget, and re-audit the headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) on a raw message that bypasses the relay.
7. Consider adding standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside the proprietary parameters if third-party analytics need them.
