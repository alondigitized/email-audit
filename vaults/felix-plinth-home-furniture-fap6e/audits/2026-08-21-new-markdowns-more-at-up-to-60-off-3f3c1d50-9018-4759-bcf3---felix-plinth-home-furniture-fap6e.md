# NEW markdowns! More at up to 60% off →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — my inbox is basically a permanent nursery-lighting subscription at this point. "Up to 60% off + new markdowns" against a daisy-field hero, and my first thought was: my kid's not in Nuna stroller territory anymore, so half this send is just noise scrolling past my thumb. It's the same brand-family drumbeat I flagged with the "Up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" send earlier today — except that one actually put a Coveleur canopy bed and a real price in front of me. This one's clearance-bin energy: no anchor product, no price, just a generic "shop clearance" button floating over flowers.
- The "we love these for you" grid at least tries to personalize — backpack, Nuna car seat, a dresser, a glider — but it reads like it was built for a parent of a toddler, not a guy mid-renovation on two houses. I'm not opening the $5000 DesignDesk sweepstakes or the "new Crate Card" upsell either; that's just cross-sell clutter stacked on top of an already-irrelevant send. Marking this read and moving on, not clicking, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The hero offer is at least legible — "up to 60% off + new markdowns" is a clear headline even if I don't care about the category.
- The category nav bar (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) is clean and scannable, no clutter.
- Product grid shows real prices ($1,499, $899, $1,450) — that's more honest than a lot of the vague "shop now" sends I get.
- The "TheDesignDesk" $5000 giveaway block is at least a distinct offer, not just another % off repeat.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to me — I don't have a baby, and this is the second Crate & Kids nursery send clogging my inbox this week; Crate & Barrel's own bedroom email today did the "real hero + real price" thing way better.
- No specific hero product under the daisy banner — just a generic "Shop Clearance" CTA, unlike CB2's "Floor Sample Sale" which at least anchored on named pieces.
- Too many competing CTAs crammed in one email: clearance, backpack bundles, DesignDesk sweepstakes, new credit card, app download — nobody scrolling on their phone is doing all five of those.
- The backpack bundle section has a "JB" monogram tag sitting oddly on a cork board image — reads like a placeholder personalization element rather than an actual product shot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — a Crate & Kids nursery/kids email for actual parents-of-littles, and a separate straightforward Crate & Barrel clearance email for everyone else. Bundling backpacks, cribs, and a credit card pitch in one blast is why I tune it out.
- 2. Lead with one specific clearance hero product + price, the way the bedroom-refresh email did with the Coveleur bed — a daisy field with no product is wasted hero space.
- 3. Cut the DesignDesk sweepstakes and credit-card blocks out of this one — they dilute the "60% off" offer and make the email feel like five campaigns duct-taped together.
