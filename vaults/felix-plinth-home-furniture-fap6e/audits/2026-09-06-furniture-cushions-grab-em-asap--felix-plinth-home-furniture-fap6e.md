# FURNITURE CUSHIONS | Grab ’em ASAP >>
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "FURNITURE CUSHIONS," and I scroll... a window seat cushion, then an air conditioner, then a pergola, then a black privacy screen, then headboards, then two sofas, then wall lights, patio dining chairs, wall sconces, peel-and-stick tile, bar stools, kitchen islands, fountains, gazebos, a hot tub, and a king headboard. That's fifteen-plus product categories under a subject line about cushions, and I count exactly one hero image — the little window-seat cushion thumbnail at the very top — that has anything to do with what got me to open this. This is now the fourth Wayfair subject-line-bait email I've caught this week (the dining table one, the privacy screens one, the actual cushions one two days ago), and it's the same kitchen-sink scroll every time, just reshuffled.
- The one thing I'll give it credit for: the 70% off Labor Day banner up top is a real, specific number, and I am mid-renovation on two houses, so an AC unit and headboards genuinely land in my zone. But Crate & Barrel's rewards email this week at least kept me in one furniture category the whole way down; Article's "Up to 40% off ends Monday" had one clear maroon deadline strip and one hero. This one buries a legit air conditioner pitch and some decent headboard deals under so much unrelated inventory that I've lost the thread by the third row.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is a mark-as-read-and-mentally-file-under-"Wayfair-is-spamming-me-again" email.

## What's working

- The 70% off Labor Day Clearance banner up top is a concrete, specific number, not vague "big savings" language.
- The window-seat cushion at the very top at least nominally matches the subject line, unlike some recent Wayfair sends.
- The AC section ("cool down on any budget") is timely for early September and relevant to a guy renovating two homes with rooms that need climate control.
- The "loved & lasting" sofa section with a star rating and two color options (green velvet, tan leather) is a legible standalone moment — I can actually picture those in my living room.

## What's weak

- Total subject-hero mismatch: "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" gets me a single small thumbnail, then AC units, pergolas, privacy screens, headboards, sofas, wall lights, dining chairs, tile, bar stools, kitchen islands, fountains, gazebos, and a hot tub — fifteen-plus unrelated categories.
- Zero rewards math or member pricing visible anywhere despite a Wayfair Rewards icon in the footer — just tap targets, no actual numbers.
- No swatch/sample program, no fabric or fit detail on the sofas or cushions I'd actually need before buying furniture.
- This is the fourth near-identical scroll-everything email from Wayfair this week — I audited the privacy screens send yesterday and the dining table one the day before, same layout, same footer, different header lie.
- There's a big blank white gap near the bottom before the footer icons — looks like a broken or missing content block.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE category and commit — if the subject says cushions, the first three rows need to be cushions, cushion covers, and outdoor cushion cushions on sale, not an AC unit by row two.
- 2. Cut the email in half. Fifteen product categories in one send reads as "we don't know who you are," which is the exact opposite of what a "picked for you" personalization program should feel like.
- 3. Add real rewards math — "Rewards members save an extra X%" with a number, not just an icon in the footer.
- 4. Fix or remove that blank white block before the footer — it reads as a broken render.
- 5. If AC and cushions are genuinely both worth pushing, split them into two separate sends with honest subject lines instead of merging into one grab-bag.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Outdoor cushions: up to 70% off, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your patio cushions are finally on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: window AC units starting under $150`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members save even more this week`
