## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a responsive XHTML table layout with a 480px breakpoint. The automated QA shows no hard failures, but it flags widespread missing alt text, 20 plain-HTTP impression pixels, and no `List-Unsubscribe` or authentication headers to verify. Several checks are inconclusive because of probe limits and the AgentMail relay.

- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 links were probed and 66 skipped. All 8 reported warnings were HTTP 429 rate limits, which the QA marks "may be valid". They are not defects. The affected URLs include:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`
  - the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links
  - the `bar-stools%3a-buy-one%2c-get-one-15%25-off~e411114.html` event page
  - the Simzlife and VIKIO product pages
- **4 links were not probed** because the 70s time budget ran out. These are `W009657036`, `W100548262`, `DeerValley-33L-x-20W-Farmhouse-Kitchen-Sink`, and `W011294897`. They are unverified, not failed.
- **Plain-HTTP links and pixels:**
  - The hero image link is `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`.
  - The app link's fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
  - 20 impression pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`. The QA flags each as "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Clients that block or rewrite insecure content will drop these, so impression counts will be undercounted.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is served over HTTPS. Only its missing alt is flagged.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - The QA output shows the hero and the star images as `[image: ...]` link text. Those links therefore have no accessible name beyond the filename, and with images off the hero CTA is empty.
- **Language attribute:** The visible `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="...">` tag has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Possibly stale assets:** Two asset filenames don't match the Oct 17 send date: `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner...` and `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnybanner-affirm.jpg`. No issues found. The truncated source and the QA data show no unrendered merge tokens. The per-recipient values (`_emr=a8930960-...`, per-link `_eml`) are populated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking scheme:** Links use Wayfair's own scheme (`source=batch`, `batchid=1`, `varid=9`, `csnid=...`, `refid=MKTEML_141189`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16577`) and no `utm_*` parameters. This is consistent across all probed links. It is an observation, not a defect, unless the downstream analytics expect UTMs.
- **Landing alignment:** The subject is "ICE MAKERS ... 48-HOUR CLEARANCE". The hero and "Shop Sale" links both go to the generic `/daily-sales` page, not an ice-maker category. Only one product link, the Simzlife nugget ice maker (`SMZL1040`), is ice-related among those probed. The `/daily-sales` target was rate-limited (429), so I could not confirm what it shows.
- **Redirect risk:** The hero is `http://` while the other links are `https://`, so it likely depends on a redirect that carries over the query parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the hero link over HTTPS.
2. Add `alt` text to the hero, the banners and the star-rating images, for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels, and make sure each linked image has descriptive alt.
3. Add `lang="en"` to the `<html>` tag.
4. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment against the original message, not the relay copy.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links and the 429 URLs with a slower, retrying probe to confirm they resolve.
6. Confirm the Memorial Day and 1013 holiday banner assets are not shown in this send.
7. Consider linking the hero to an ice-maker-specific landing page to match the subject.
