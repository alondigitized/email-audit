## What's weak

- **Zero offer.** No percent off, no free shipping threshold, no Key Rewards reminder. Just "look at the new finishes." I've got a cart sitting somewhere; this email didn't give me a reason to empty it.

- **Collection name is buried** — I had to squint to figure out what I was actually looking at. Lead with the collection name, not just the finish names.
- **The CTA buttons are small and low-contrast** — on a phone, I'd probably tap the wrong thing or miss them entirely.
