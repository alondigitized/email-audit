# Your favorite kitchen brands, now up to 40% off →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was that this is a real kitchen email, and I'm renovating two kitchens, so I stayed on it longer than I have on most of this week's Crate sends. The hero is a Dyson-style stick vacuum with a "2 days left! accent chairs, tables & rugs up to 20% off" banner over it. The subject is about kitchen brands, so the top of the email contradicts it. The actual kitchen offer, "up to 40% off top kitchen brands," sits one block below.
- Past the confusion, the body delivers: All-Clad, Breville, KitchenAid, Shun, and a JURA coffee machine. I know those brands and I buy them. The Sale prices and Reg. prices are shown side by side, which is what I want. But I've now had Crate in my inbox every day since 10/31, and the Kids sends were the worst. The email is also long. It stacks a Thanksgiving menu, a Black Friday signup, the credit card pitch, an app download, and a design appointment. I'd click one of the Breville or KitchenAid tiles and skip everything else. I wouldn't forward it, since nobody I know is buying an espresso machine this week.

## What's working

- "Up to 40% off top kitchen brands" is clear, and the brand names carry the offer. All-Clad, Breville, and KitchenAid are exactly what I'd add to a cart.
- Sale and Reg. prices are shown together, for example the Cookware Set at $899.95 against $1,429.70. I can do the math without clicking.
- The "we love these for you" grid at the bottom gives me a second chance to see products, including the Le Creuset Dutch oven and the Breville espresso machine.
- The Crate & Barrel Reward Dollars card block, "10% back in Reward Dollars," speaks to how I shop. I pick the highest-rewards card, so I read that part.
- The Thanksgiving menu hero is seasonal and well shot. It's the right time of year to be thinking about cooking.

## What's weak

- The hero is a vacuum and a furniture/rug banner, which has nothing to do with the kitchen subject. I had to scroll past it to find what I came for.
- The "up to 20% off" banner and the "up to 40% off" kitchen headline compete with each other at the top, so two different deals are fighting for attention.
- The email is far too long, with at least six separate sections. The Black Friday signup and app download feel like leftover blocks from a template.
- Product tiles use tiny text. I'd have to zoom to read the model names and prices on my phone.
- "Up to 40% off" with "Open Stock" and "Reg." labels and a block of fine print at the bottom makes me wonder which items actually get the full discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with the kitchen offer. Lead with the Breville or KitchenAid image, because the subject already promised it. Move the furniture banner lower or send it in a separate email.
- 2. Cut the section count in half. Keep kitchen products, one seasonal block (Thanksgiving), and one loyalty block. Drop the app download and design-appointment sections.
- 3. Make the product tiles bigger. Two per row at a readable size beats four tiny ones.
- 4. Label the real discount per item and say how long it runs. "2 days left" is attached to the wrong offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout with `lang="en"`, a Movable Ink (`mi.`/`dv.crateandbarrel.com`) personalization layer and 66 click-tracked links. The confirmed problems are missing alt attributes, a viewport/text-size setup that blocks zoom, and some invalid or redundant head markup. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** all 66 links point to tracking/click-redirect domains, and QA skipped the HTTP probe on them. Destination status, redirect chains and broken links are unknown.
- **Third-party identifier sharing:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` pixels are present. The `s=` value matches the `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…` token on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs. The same recipient-level hash therefore appears to go to a third-party data domain (`rlcdn.com`), five times in one send. This is a privacy and deliverability consideration, not a functional break.
- **Pixel count:** the email carries the open pixel `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, the `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e0779ace-…` pixel and the five `rlcdn` pixels, so seven tracking beacons in total.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 26 images. They fall into three groups:
  - **Content images:** about 11 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (e.g. `24dc83e8-…gif`, `f55586e1-…jpg`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`), plus the `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F1102%5FCB%5FKitchenPromos%5FSocial%5Fv2…` image. Any of these that carry an offer or kitchen-brand message are invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
  - **Dynamic product images:** 7 PNGs at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, such as `560d850e7e517ede.png`.
  - **Pixels:** the `rlcdn`, `o.gif` and `dv.` beacons. These should carry an explicit `alt=""`. I can't tell from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on some mobile clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on `*{…}` and on `body`. `*{…line-height:100%}` plus `.ExternalClass * {line-height:100%}` also sets tight global line height.
- **Invalid XHTML:** the doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, but `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Redundant or conflicting head markup:**
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type charset declaration.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, with values `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`. Clients may honor only one tag.
  - Two `img {max-width}` rules, 600px at `min-width:768px` and 640px at `min-width:640px`, have equal specificity. The later 640px rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px rule is dead.
- **Template residue:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and the empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block are unrendered but sloppy.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, MSO conditionals and `o:PixelsPerInch` are present, and `color-scheme` is declared as light only.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the supplied HTML or the flagged URLs. The `mi_u` / `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters are populated. The HTML is truncated, so the body copy and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA itself says they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed sender defect.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the same relay limitation.
- The visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address sit in the footer, which is cut off in the truncated source. They are **not verified**.
- The sending domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` are aligned subdomains of the brand domain, which is consistent with a properly branded setup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The tracked links are redirect wrappers and the truncated HTML shows no destination URLs, so UTM presence and landing-page match can't be confirmed. The creative filename `2026_1102_CB_KitchenPromos` is consistent with the "kitchen brands" subject, but that is circumstantial.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed above, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to restore zoom and text resizing.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
4. Collapse the four `format-detection` metas into one, drop the duplicate charset, and fix the competing `img max-width` media queries.
5. Review whether sending the `mi_u` hash to `sr.rlcdn.com` five times is intended, and cut it to one call if so.
6. For the next audit run, capture the full raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) and the untruncated HTML. Then the compliance and UTM sections can be confirmed.
7. Resolve the tracking redirects, for example with a headless follow, to check destination status codes and UTM carry-through.
