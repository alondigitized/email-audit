- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of exclusions and affiliate and card disclaimers. It's long for a "free" offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an image-sliced, table-based email with 47 opaque click-tracking links. The source has stray server-side code inside a `<style>` block, several images with no alt text or on `http://`, and the recipient's email address in plaintext pixel URLs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** All 47 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` and the QA probe skipped them. I can't confirm any destination resolves, or that any of them carries UTM parameters.
- **Stale commented-out block:** The header still contains a commented-out credit-card banner. Its link carries `cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`. It's harmless but dead markup.
- **Third-party pixels:** The email loads `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`. Both are ad-network retargeting pixels, and both lack alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on the West Elm domain. A failure there would affect this send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block begins with literal PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. This isn't valid CSS. Parsers will treat it as part of the next selector, so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {...}` rule is likely dropped. That rule is the one that stops iOS auto-linking.
- **Content before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot --> <!-- end dynamic ampScript slot -->` sits before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some clients, notably Outlook and some webmail, can fall back to quirks mode because of this.
- **Mobile CSS doesn't match the markup:** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. The visible tables are fixed `width="650"` with no `class` attributes, and the images have no `class="photo"`. The 480px rules therefore don't apply, and the layout won't scale on mobile. This is based on the truncated source, so confirm against the full HTML.
- **Missing alt text:** The QA run flagged about 40 images. These include:
  - `slices_01–08.jpg` in the body
  - `slices_01–18.jpg` in the footer
  - the product images (`toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, etc.)
- **Unnamed links:** The body slices carry `alt=""` and are each wrapped in an `<a>`, so those links have no accessible name. With images blocked, the email is blank except for the `nav0.jpg` banner.
- **Insecure images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` use plain HTTP and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content. The second is a 2022 asset in a 2026 template.
- **Markup:**
  - The layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.
  - The XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype conflicts with the `<center>`, `bgcolor` and `border` attributes.
  - The Montserrat Google Fonts `<link>` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address is populated in the pixel URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified defect. Check the raw message before treating it as a failure. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`.
- **PII in URLs:** The recipient address is in plaintext query strings:
  - `www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  These can leak through proxy logs and referrers.
- **Footer not verifiable:** The footer is 18 image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) with no alt text, and the source was truncated. I couldn't confirm a text unsubscribe link or a physical postal address. If either exists only inside an image, that is a CAN-SPAM and accessibility problem.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. UTM parameters and landing pages can't be evaluated, because every link is wrapped in an opaque `qs=` redirect and the HTTP probe was skipped. The only visible direct URLs are product image paths and the `coreemail.html` pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Delete the `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block. Move the AMPscript comments inside `<body>` or after the DOCTYPE.
2. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the tables and images, or rewrite the media query to match the real markup, and use fluid widths.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to every linked image, including footer and product slices. Mark the spacer GIFs and tracking pixels `alt=""`.
4. Move the two `http://` assets to HTTPS and replace the 2022 `BrandBanner` slice.
5. Confirm the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. The check needs the original message, not the AgentMail copy.
6. Confirm the footer has a live-text unsubscribe link and a postal address.
7. Stop putting the raw email address in pixel and query-string URLs. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
8. Resolve a sample of `click.e.potterybarn.com` links manually to check destinations and UTM tagging.
