# Holiday NEW ARRIVALS just dropped
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened this expecting new holiday product, and the top half is a sale banner. The maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is the same one that opened this morning's "STARTS NOW 📣" send. Under it is a moody sleigh bed with green bedding and a "New Arrivals" hero. That's a decent room shot, but I came for arrivals and got a discount reminder. It's also the fifth Pottery Barn email I've logged in about two days.
- The middle is a 3x3 mosaic of holiday vignettes: a lamp, a wreath on a door, a closet of coats, a reindeer. It's pretty, but nothing in it is labeled or priced, so I can't tell what's new. The "Recommended just for you" grid is the useful part: a curtain rod, a swivel chair, a rug, two extendable dining tables, and linen curtains. That's close to what I'm doing across two houses. The "In-stock furniture, 1-4 weeks" block is the first shipping-timeline info I've seen from them all week.
- Would I click? Probably the dining tables, since I'm sourcing for the second house. Forward it? No. I'd save it to my inspo folder and move on.

## What's working

- The "In-stock furniture, guest-ready pieces delivered in just 1-4 weeks" block answers my actual renovation question, which is when it arrives. Wayfair never gives me that clarity.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid has furniture and window treatments (Toscana and Banks extendable tables, Emery linen curtain, Reeva rug). It feels like someone noticed what I browse.
- The sleigh-bed hero is on-brand and moody, and the green and brown palette is easy to match against my color boards.
- The 10% back in rewards card block is visible. I buy with the highest-rewards card, so I read it.
- "Shop the Holiday Preview" is a clear dark button, and the CTAs are tappable on a phone.

## What's weak

- The sale strip fights the "New Arrivals" hero. Two offers and two messages in the first screen, and I can't tell which one this email is about.
- The mosaic has no product names, prices, or links I can identify. Nothing says what's new, so I'm guessing.
- The lamp I care about is the one that appears in three brands' emails the same week. CB2's "Now & Future Classics" and Crate & Barrel did the same thing, and this mosaic doesn't differentiate.
- Some of the lower promo blocks are a stack of cards (app, rewards, Affirm). The Christmas Shop banner is also a second holiday push in one email.
- The email is very long, and the fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny. "Extra 20% off select styles" doesn't say which styles, and the footer exclusions are buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the new arrivals and drop the sale strip, or move it below the hero. One message per send.
- 2. Label the mosaic with product names and starting prices, and link each tile to its product page.
- 3. Make the "just for you" grid the centerpiece, and put the in-stock 1-4 week delivery message right under it.
- 4. Cut the app, Affirm, and card stack down to one rewards line.
- 5. Cut send frequency. Five emails in about two days trains me to ignore the brand, which Article's single "free design help" email did better.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Holiday NEW ARRIVALS just dropped"

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a standard 650px table layout from an ESP template. The QA run shows 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The real findings are widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, recipient email addresses in image URLs, and leftover non-CSS text in a `<style>` block. The missing header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are probably capture limits of the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click-tracking was not verified.** 46 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` and similar redirect domains were skipped. Destination resolution, redirect chains and link health are untested.
- **Open-tracking pixels use a mix of protocols and hosts:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is plain HTTP.
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` is a third-party pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/cdf6c334-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a first-party pixel.
- **The `coreemail.html` request is an image.** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…` is loaded as an `<img>`, so it is a tracking or beacon call rather than content.
- **A cross-brand asset host is used.** The spacer image comes from `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`. If West Elm changes that path, it breaks silently.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on about 40 images, so the alt attribute is absent entirely.** Examples:
  - Hero and product images: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `slices_01–04.jpg` (`0913_SUN_AM_NewArrivals`), `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`.
  - The whole `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` set and the two bottom banners.
  - Images are sliced, and the content images are linked. With images blocked, the email renders as empty links with no text.
  - The tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`, `coreemail.html`) should carry `alt=""` instead of being omitted.
- **Three images load over `http://` and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/…`
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom.** This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is set on `*` and `html`.
- **The CSS block has stray non-CSS text.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This looks like pasted PHP. It is invalid CSS that parsers skip, but it is a template hygiene defect.
- **Some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.** The outer wrapper tables and the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) don't have it, though the content tables do.
- **The `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` media-query selectors** have no matching elements in the visible portion of the source. The HTML is truncated, so this can't be confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens appear** in the visible source.
- **The recipient's email address appears in plaintext in image URLs:**
  - `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The second one is over unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy or server logs. These URLs also appear in the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the relay may not capture them. Treat this as unverified. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo one-click unsubscribe requirements for bulk senders are not met.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is the same relay limitation. Sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` and click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` are aligned, which is a good sign.
- **The footer is truncated,** so the physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.** The links use opaque `qs=` redirect tokens and weren't probed, so UTM presence and destinations are unverified.
- **Some URLs show campaign identifiers** (`sv_cid=0053_00597`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`). These are ESP and analytics IDs, not UTM parameters.
- **Product image paths** are on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, but whether the product links resolve to matching PDPs is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and linked images, and use `alt=""` on tracking pixels and the spacer GIF.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS, especially the `svopn` pixel and `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`. Retire the 2022 banner path (`0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner`).
3. Stop putting the raw email address in image query strings. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block, fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Verify the List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers and Authentication-Results against the raw message at the receiving mailbox. Don't rely on the relay.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm UTM tagging and landing-page match. Host the spacer GIF on a Pottery Barn domain rather than `edm.westelm.com`.
