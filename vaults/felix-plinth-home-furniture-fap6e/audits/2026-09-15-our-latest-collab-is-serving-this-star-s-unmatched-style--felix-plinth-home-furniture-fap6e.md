# Our latest collab is serving this star’s unmatched style
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Venus Williams round four. I flagged this exact hero — the phone mockup, the pairing, the "Venus for Crate & Barrel" lockup — on 2026-09-14 twice in one day, once as "Shop early on our app" and once with the subject swapped. Today's version at least drops the app-exclusive gate and shows the actual product now: a "Lawn Club" sectional, a "Meadows" bed, a "Monte Carlo" dining table, a "Legacy" sideboard. That's real furniture I can evaluate, which is more than the last two sends gave me.
- But four Venus Williams emails in two days is a cadence problem no amount of good product photography fixes. I'm renovating two houses right now — a green velvet sectional and an oak dining table are genuinely relevant to me — but my brain has already filed "Venus Williams collab" under "seen it, scrolled past it" from yesterday. The quote block from Venus ("I hope this collection makes you think, 'this is the lifestyle I want'") is a nice touch I haven't seen from this brand before, but it's buried mid-scroll after three product blocks.
- Would I click? Maybe on the dining table or the sideboard specifically, since those match my reno. Would I forward it? No — not a fourth time. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- Actual named products with room-styled photography — the green sectional and the oak "Monte Carlo" table both look like real inventory, not just a lifestyle mood board.
- The Venus Williams quote humanizes the collab a bit more than the flat "Shop the Collab" buttons from yesterday's sends.
- Color palette is consistent — green, cream, black-and-white checkerboard flooring recurring across sections, which reads cohesive scrolling through.
- "TheDesignDesk" free design-help CTA near the bottom is a relevant touch for someone mid-reno like me, similar to what CB2 was pushing with its design-service email.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth Venus Williams send in two days from this brand — I already called the fatigue out on 9/14 twice, and nothing about the cadence changed.
- "New & exclusive" hero copy up top undercuts itself — if it's the fourth email about the same collab, it doesn't feel exclusive to me anymore.
- No pricing, no % off, no member/rewards math anywhere in this whole scroll — I shop on the highest-rewards card and this gives me zero reason to act today versus next week.
- Subject line "serving this star's unmatched style" is vague sports-metaphor filler that doesn't tell me it's furniture — Article's "Patio season's not over yet" at least implies category at a glance.
- Long, undifferentiated scroll — six or seven product sections with near-identical "new! [Product] — Shop the Collab" formatting makes it hard to tell what's actually new versus filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the standalone Venus Williams sends entirely and fold this into one weekly roundup — I've now seen this collaboration four times in 48 hours, and repetition is actively costing goodwill, not building it.
- 2. Put a real offer in the hero — even a modest "15% off the collection" would beat this content-only approach, especially against CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send that actually led with a deal.
- 3. Lead with the dining table or sideboard, not the sectional — those are the pieces with the broadest reno appeal, and the checkerboard-floor styling on the sideboard section is the strongest visual in the whole email.
- 4. Move the Venus quote up near the hero — it's the one piece of copy that differentiates this from a generic new-arrivals email, and it's currently buried.
