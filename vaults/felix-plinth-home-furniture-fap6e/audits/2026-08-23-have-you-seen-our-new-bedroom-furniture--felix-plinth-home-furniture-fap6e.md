- The Scarlett Collection's "NOW IN A NEW FINISH" callout gives me a reason to care if I've seen this dresser before — that's a real update, not a stale re-send.

- Four collections plus two grids plus a sweepstakes plus a Halloween plug plus a rewards pitch is just too much — nobody's finishing this scroll on a commute.
- No price or percent-off anywhere near the furniture, which is exactly what I'd need to decide if the primary-bedroom budget can absorb a new dresser.

- "SHOP NOW" / "Shop the Look" repeated five times with zero differentiation — no verb-plus-noun CTA like "Shop Sausalito Dressers."

- 1. Cut this to one or two collections max and kill the sweepstakes/Halloween/Affirm stack entirely — let the furniture actually breathe. Crate & Barrel's room-organized rugs email proves focus converts better than volume.
- 2. Put a price or starting-at price under at least the hero bed and the top dresser — furniture buyers need the number before they click.
- 3. Make each CTA specific: "Shop Sausalito Dressers" instead of five identical "SHOP NOW"s.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Have you seen our new bedroom furniture?"

Standard MSO/Outlook-compatible HTML email built on a legacy table-based template with third-party open/click tracking pixels; core rendering markup is sound, but compliance headers are unverifiable and accessibility/protocol hygiene has multiple gaps.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary nav CTA routes through a branded click-tracking redirector: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEs...` (Cheetah/CM Digital-style token). Not independently resolvable without following the redirect.
- 69 tracking/click-redirect links total were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (by design — redirect domains aren't followed), so destination validity for the bulk of links is **unconfirmed**, not verified-good.
- Open-tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` — the `svopn` pixel is served over **plain HTTP**, which many mail clients/proxies will block or strip, silently degrading open-rate data.
- Recipient PII is embedded directly in tracking pixel URLs in cleartext: `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. This exposes the recipient's email address to any intermediary logging these plain-HTTP requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **43 images with no `alt` text** flagged by QA (banners, product slices, footer slices, tracking pixels). Functional/content images (e.g., `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `sausalito-8-drawer-dresser-66-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-l.jpg`) losing alt text is the more significant a11y issue vs. decorative slice/spacer images.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (or confirm with the sending platform/relay why they aren't being captured) to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements and enable one-click unsubscribe.
2. Migrate the two remaining HTTP-only asset URLs (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey`, `.../0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner`) to HTTPS to prevent blocked images and mixed-content flags.
3. Add `alt` text to all product images at minimum (bed, dresser, table, sheet set) for screen-reader accessibility; decorative slice/spacer images can use `alt=""`.
4. Route the open-tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) over HTTPS to stop transmitting recipient email addresses in plaintext over an unencrypted connection.
5. Remove the stray literal `header(...)` PHP-style statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code with no functional effect but adds noise/confusion for future template edits.
6. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly with the sending ESP, since Authentication-Results couldn't be captured via this relay.
