# ONLY AT CB2
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Popular For A Reason" — this is a best-sellers roundup, and it's basically the CB2 version of the same email I already got from CB2 itself two days ago ("Don't miss these best sellers on sale," 7/10). Except that one had a real number attached — up to 40% off — and this one has nothing. I scroll past a mirror, a sectional, a pendant light, a bed, a rug, a dining table, a bowl... eight products, zero prices, zero discount, until I hit the app-only upsell for 25% off buried two-thirds down. That's a lot of scrolling for a "reason" that never actually gets stated.
- The Fiora Rug and the Davison Dining Table are genuinely nice-looking and relevant to what I'm doing in the second house right now, so I'm not mad at the merchandising. But the whole email reads like a catalog page, not an offer. Compare this to Crate & Barrel's Design Desk email I looked at recently — that one had an actual problem-to-solve angle (custom furniture help) baked into the send. This is just "here's stuff," which after CB2's last three emails this week is starting to blur together in my head.
- Verdict: I'm not clicking anything except maybe the rug out of genuine curiosity, and I'm definitely not forwarding this — there's no hook, no urgency, nothing worth texting a friend about. Marking as read.

## What's working

- The hero shot (mirror + bed styling) is clean and on-brand, no render bugs anywhere in this whole long scroll.
- Product photography is consistently well-lit and styled — the green Faible Sectional and the Fiora Rug both stopped my scroll for a second.
- The "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event" app-exclusive banner at least has a real number (up to 25% off) even if it's app-gated.
- Footer is straightforward with clear app/rewards CTAs (10% off) and social links.

## What's weak

- No price, no discount, no urgency anywhere in the main body — eight product blocks and not one dollar sign until deep into the footer promos.
- "Popular For A Reason" as hero copy tells me nothing; it's the vaguest possible framing for a best-sellers email.
- This is the third CB2/Crate & Barrel-family email in four days hitting my inbox with near-identical "best sellers/collected feel/moody lighting" structure — I flagged this exact fatigue on 9/23's "That Collected Feel" send, and it's happening again.
- The real offer (25% off upholstery) is app-only and buried below eight full-width product tiles — that's a lot of friction between me opening this and finding a reason to act.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the actual offer. If there's 25% off upholstery, that's the subject line and the hero — don't make me scroll past eight unpriced products to find the one thing with a number attached.
- 2. Add prices or "starting at $X" under each best-seller tile — Wayfair's Open Box email failed on substance but at least it's a price-forward brand; CB2 shouldn't cede that ground.
- 3. Cut the product count from eight to four and give the survivors more room to breathe — this email is exhausting to scroll on mobile.
- 4. Make the upholstery event accessible without the app download — a hard app-gate on the only real discount is going to lose people like me who aren't installing an app for 25% off a couch.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails on merge-token resolution and shows multiple deliverability/compliance gaps (missing unsubscribe headers, unconfirmed auth), alongside legacy accessibility and mixed-content issues in third-party pixel tags — 43% automated pass rate.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 69 tracking/redirect links were skipped by HTTP probing (click-redirect domains), so live-link validity is unconfirmed — recommend manual click-through QA before send.
- Multiple `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php`–`pub10.php` tracking pixels load over plain `http://`, not `https://` — flagged as non-HTTPS sources that may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` tracking call is also `http://`, same blocking risk.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 content images (`image.mail.cb2.com/lib/.../*.jpg`) are missing `alt` text — screen readers and image-blocked clients will render no fallback copy.
- Tracking/pixel elements (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, dotomi pixels, UCMController) also lack `alt` attributes, though this is lower-impact since they're 1x1 trackers, not content images.
- HTML uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype and heavy MSO/legacy conditional comments — standard for enterprise ESP templates, no defects observed there.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL]** Raw unresolved merge tag `%%reviews_skulist%%` found in the rendered HTML — this token did not resolve at send time and will display literally to recipients. This is the single confirmed functional bug in the email.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — QA notes this may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed absence; cannot confirm compliance either way from this data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat; one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — sender authentication status unknown via this relay; cannot confirm pass/fail.
- Recommend re-verifying all three at the raw SMTP/header level (outside the AgentMail relay) before treating these as real compliance failures.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found — no UTM/landing-page data was available in the provided source or QA output to evaluate continuity.

7. Recommendations
- **Priority 1:** Fix the merge-tag resolution pipeline so `%%reviews_skulist%%` populates before send — this is a visible, broken-experience bug for every recipient.
- **Priority 2:** Migrate the `ads.dotomi.com` and `login.dotomi.com` tracking calls to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients.
