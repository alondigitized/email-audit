## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed defect: an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has accessibility and mixed-content problems in the image markup. The HTML source is truncated and 72 tracking links were not probed, so link, UTM, and footer checks are incomplete. The 43% QA pass rate is mostly warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 72 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination, status codes, and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Mixed content:** 11 tracking pixels load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  
  Clients and proxies can block these, which would break the Dotomi cookie sync. Each also exposes the request to a downgraded connection.
- **Malformed font URLs:** several `@font-face` URLs have a double slash, e.g. `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2`. These may still resolve, but that depends on the server.
- **Cross-brand dependency:** the `HNW05` fonts load from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`, a different domain from the sender. That adds a dependency on another property.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (11):** these are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `47ee1413-…jpg`, `2118efc7-…jpg`, `67e5e043-…jpg`, `40ea3443-…jpg`
  - `6b5c88cb-…jpg`, `63867d17-…jpg`, `0c4854df-…jpg`, `05e98e66-…jpg`
  - `73f7ba0c-…jpg`, `edf27f62-…jpg`, `49efaabb-…jpg`
  
  If these carry content or are linked, they are invisible to screen readers and blank when images are blocked.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (13):**
  - `mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif`
  - `dv.cb2.com/o/093cf9b3-…`
  - the 11 Dotomi pixels listed above
  
  These should use `alt=""` explicitly. They are low severity.
- **Zoom and text-resize restrictions:**
  - `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom.
  - `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` (in the `*` and `body` rules) blocks iOS text resizing.
  
  Both are WCAG 1.4.4 concerns.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (note the "IMPUT" typo) shows the template placeholder was left unedited.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, a charset declaration, and VML/Outlook conditionals.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%`:** this is `%%…%%` personalization-string syntax, as used by SFMC-style platforms, and it did not resolve at send time. The truncated source doesn't show where it sits. If it is in visible content, subscribers see the literal string. If it is in a comment or a hidden block, it is cosmetic only. A reviews or ratings block that depends on a SKU list probably failed to populate.

## 5. Compliance
- **No issues confirmed.** These QA warnings are not defects:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unverified.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Not visible in the truncated source:** the unsubscribe link and the physical postal address. They need checking in the full body.
- **Needs checking on the original message:** the three missing headers, which matter for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Cannot assess.** The 72 tracking links are redirect-wrapped and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown.
- **Reachable but unaudited:** the `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels carry `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` identifiers. They are cb2.com first-party domains, so they should work.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the merge token:** find where `%%reviews_skulist%%` renders and add a fallback or suppress the block when the SKU list is empty. Add a send-time check for unresolved `%%…%%`.
2. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS:** change all 11 to `https://`, or remove them if the vendor doesn't support it.
3. **Add alt text:** write descriptive alt for the 11 content images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the 13 pixels.
4. **Allow zoom and text resize:** remove `maximum-scale=1` and change `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to `100%`.
5. **Clean the template:** remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment and collapse the `//Styles` double slashes. Host fonts on the sender's own domain.
6. **Verify on the original message:** check the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers, and the footer unsubscribe link and address. Resolve a sample of the 72 tracking links to audit UTM parameters and landing pages.
