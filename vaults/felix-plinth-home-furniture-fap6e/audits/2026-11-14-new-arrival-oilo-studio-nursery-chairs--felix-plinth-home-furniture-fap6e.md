## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The visible markup is a standard ESP-built XHTML table layout with no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The confirmed problems are about 35 `<img>` elements with no `alt`, a viewport tag that disables zoom, and several third-party tracking pixels. The source was truncated, so the footer and body links could not be inspected.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 79 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect/tracking domains. Link health and final destinations are **unverified**.
- Five identical third-party pixels fire from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `n=5`. The `s=` value looks like a hashed subscriber identifier sent to a third-party ad-data domain (rlcdn.com).
- First-party pixels carry per-recipient identifiers: `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/6d4311cf-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are standard open/engagement tracking.
- `f3c0d22992a01946.png` (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`) appears six times with the same `mi_u` token. This is repeated per-recipient dynamic image calls, not a broken link.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** the 21 content images below have no `alt` attribute.
  - These are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (and `/m/47/`), for example `3930ebb6-…jpg`, `1cce28bd-…jpg`, `e01ad581-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - With images blocked or on a screen reader, the new-arrival chair content is unavailable.
  - The animated GIFs (`e01ad581…`, `8c4ce5d9…`, `9c5d35f4…`) have no text fallback.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** `448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif`, the `dv.` pixel and `f3c0d…png` (×6) are also flagged. If they are decorative or tracking-only, they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Global line-height:** `*{… line-height:100%}` is applied to every element. This tightens line spacing and can hurt legibility for wrapped text.
- **Markup validity (minor):**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under that doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with overlapping values.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, remains in the source. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Subject line:** the 🤍 emoji in the subject is fine technically. No encoding issue is evident, and the charset is declared UTF-8.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are resolved, per-recipient values.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed sender defect. Check the raw original message headers before acting.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found. This is also a relay limitation, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, not failed. The envelope `From` domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper authentication, but that is not verified here.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section and could not be checked.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Cannot be confirmed. All 79 links are tracking redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment with "Oilo Studio nursery chairs" are unverified. No evidence of a mismatch was found.

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 21 content images, using the product name for the Oilo Studio chair images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking and decorative pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Review the five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels, which pass a hashed identifier to a third party. Confirm they are covered by the privacy policy and consent basis, and consider whether five calls are needed.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, de-duplicate the `format-detection` metas, and remove the leftover template comment.
