# Now and future classics
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2 again — this is the sixth send I've clocked from this brand alone this stretch, and "Now and Future Classics" is basically the designer-edit cousin of "MOST SOUGHT AFTER" hero copy they've run before. It's a long, moody, editorial scroll — Deseo sectional, Gwyneth chair, Macron console, Fitz chair, Davison bed, all shot in that same green-and-camel showroom lighting — and it genuinely looks good. Better than the sofa-photo-then-banner mess from two days ago. But it's a catalog, not an email: I have to scroll through five furniture hero shots before I hit an actual number, and by then I've mentally filed this as "browse later, never."
- The Labor Day 50% off banner buried two-thirds down is the whole ballgame and they know it — it's the same "UP TO 50% OFF" I've seen from this brand three times this week. CB2 vs. its own parent Crate & Barrel is starting to blur for me too; I flagged that exact confusion on the "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send. This is not a forward-to-a-friend email — it's a scroll-past, maybe-tap-if-bored one.

## What's working

- The Deseo sectional and Davison bed shots are genuinely good — moody, styled, the kind of thing I'd screenshot for my reno mood board.
- "Designed by [studio]" credits under the Gwyneth chair, Macron console, Fitz chair, Davison bed give it real designer-collab credibility — Pottery Barn's Molly Sims edit tried this same "credibility over discount" play and landed flatter because it had zero product photography this dense.
- The "NOW AND FUTURE CLASSICS" category grid (Sofas & Sectionals, Bedroom, Dining Room, Accent Chairs) is a clean way to route me by room, which none of the recent Wayfair cushion-bait emails bothered with.
- Rewards math is spelled out at the bottom — "Earn 15% back" — which at least answers a real question for me as a cardholder.

## What's weak

- Five straight product hero blocks before any offer — that's a lot of scroll for a guy checking email on his commute.
- The Labor Day 50% banner is generic and undifferentiated from every other CB2/C&B Labor Day banner I've seen this week — no specific category, no deadline visible.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom has real prices ($1129, $399, $579, $999, $449, $249) but they're crammed six-up with tiny type — not readable without zooming on my phone.
- Sender fatigue is real — this is CB2 email number six-plus, and the "designer edit" framing doesn't disguise that it's another catalog drop in a saturated week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Labor Day 50% off banner to the top, above the fold — right now the actual offer is buried under five product blocks, and that's the single biggest miss.
- 2. Cut the hero product sequence from five to two or three — Deseo sectional and Davison bed carry the "classics" story fine on their own.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the Labor Day banner ("ends Monday") — nothing here signals urgency, it just says "sale."
- 4. Enlarge the "MORE FOR YOU" price grid or drop it to four items so the numbers are legible without pinch-zoom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Labor Day: 50% off the sofa you keep saving`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New designer classics, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Deseo sectional and 4 more, now on sale`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the edit before Labor Day pricing ends`
