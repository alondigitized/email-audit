# The latest innovation from Doona
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Crate & Barrel send I've logged today. It came from the Kids sender, after sofas this morning and the "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event" earlier. My gut reaction was "I'm renovating two houses, why am I looking at a stroller?" A Doona travel system isn't my project. The email itself is tidy: a big lifestyle hero of a woman walking with the car seat/stroller, then a clean stack of feature modules.
- The top is confusing. A sage-green strip says "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off," and right under it the hero sells a single Doona product with no price and no discount. The 50% banner is about furniture and the hero is about a stroller, and I can't tell which one I'm meant to act on. It's also a long scroll, with five or six "Shop" buttons and no price until the very bottom.
- I wouldn't click. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it either, unless a friend with a newborn came to mind. The only thing I'd save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder is the nursery chair and dresser block near the bottom, and only barely.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The outdoor lifestyle shot with the teal Doona is clean and the "New! Doona X All in One Car Seat & Stroller" headline reads instantly.
- The feature modules ("3 comfy recline positions," "3 smart travel modes," "5 modern colors") are scannable. The color swatches are a nice touch.
- The "more to love" block with the adjustable height, added safety, folds fast and trunk space tiles is short and clear.
- The sage-green palette is consistent from the top strip through the section headers, so it feels like one brand.
- The Crate Card block mentions Reward Dollars, which is the one thing that speaks to how I shop, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The "up to 50% off" strip is about a different event than the hero. It makes me wonder whether the Doona is part of the sale, and the email never tells me.
- There's no price anywhere in the top half. For a $500+ item, I scroll a long way before seeing any numbers.
- The email is long, with repeated "Shop Now" buttons that all say roughly the same thing. That's tap fatigue on a phone.
- The audience is wrong for me. I'm 36, renovating, with no nursery, so the strollers, nursery chairs and cribs are noise.
- The cadence is bad. This is the fourth Crate send today, and this one sits right next to the earlier nursery event email, so it feels like the same send twice.
- Much of the body text is small and light grey. I'd have to zoom to read the feature descriptions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the top. Either lead with the Doona and drop the 50% strip, or tie them together by stating plainly whether Doona is included in the event. Put a price or "from $X" in the hero.
- 2. Cut the repeated "Shop Now" buttons down to one hero CTA and one "Shop all Doona" at the bottom.
- 3. Bring the price and the Reward Dollars earn rate up high instead of burying the Crate Card near the bottom.
- 4. Segment it. Send this to people who've browsed nursery or registry, and suppress it for the people in the middle of a home renovation. Wayfair sends me sixteen emails a week too, but at least its pergola email matched what I searched.
- 5. Use bigger body text and darker grey.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five colors, three travel modes, and it folds in seconds. See what's new.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has broad accessibility gaps: 33 images lack an `alt` attribute and the viewport blocks zoom. The automated checks also couldn't confirm the unsubscribe headers or SPF/DKIM, and all 73 links are redirect-wrapped, so the link destinations and UTM parameters are unverified. QA pass rate was 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on all 73 links ("Tracking/click-redirect domains"). Destination status, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` calls (LiveRamp). Five fires of the same pixel add third-party requests with no obvious need.
  - The `s=` value matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (six repeats) and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. The same recipient-level identifier is therefore passed to a third-party domain through image URLs.
- **Pixels in the DOM:** The redundant pixels (6× `f3c0d22992a01946.png`, 5× `448796.gif`) are extra requests that clients proxying or blocking images will fail.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (33 images, per QA):**
  - **Content images (20):** 16 under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `eeb8adae-….jpg`, `8964c2f1-….gif`), `971aa10f-….jpg`, `f9868e75-….gif`, `3195520b-….jpg`, and the `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` spacer. With images off or a screen reader, these may be unlabeled or read out by filename.
  - **Tracking/spacer images (13):** `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/f08d47ff-…`. They need `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` prevent user text scaling in some clients.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed under XHTML.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - A leftover template comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - Overlapping `img{max-width:600px}` (min-width 768px) and `img{max-width:640px}` (min-width 640px) rules mean the 640px rule wins at desktop widths, so the 600px rule is dead.
- **Rendering unverified:** The HTML was truncated, so I could not check the rest of the CSS or the body markup (e.g. table roles, link text).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible HTML. This covers only the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Neither was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not present, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is unknown (the relay is a likely cause).
- **Body-level compliance:** I could not check the footer unsubscribe link or postal address because the source was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified, not confirmed broken. All 73 links use redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the 20 content images and `alt=""` (with `width="1" height="1"`) to the 13 pixels and spacers.
2. **Viewport:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Text sizing:** Drop `text-size-adjust:none` from the universal and `html` selectors. Use `100%` as the `body` rule already does.
4. **Tracking pixels:** Collapse the repeated pixels (5× `rlcdn.com`, 6× `f3c0d22992a01946.png`) to one each, and review what identifier is passed to `rlcdn.com`.
5. **Headers:** Re-run QA against raw headers from the original SMTP message, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
6. **Link resolution:** Resolve a sample of the 73 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
7. **Markup cleanup:** Escape `&` as `&amp;` in the `<title>`, close the `<meta>` tags, remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment, and delete the dead 600px `img` rule.
