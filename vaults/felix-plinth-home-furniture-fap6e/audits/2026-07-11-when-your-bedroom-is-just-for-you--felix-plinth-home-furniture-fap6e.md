- 1. **Add one price anchor to the hero** — "From $899" or "Beds from $X" gives me a reason to click instead of admire-and-close; the photography is earning nothing commercially without a number attached
- 2. **Reframe the subject away from solitude** — the bedroom as sanctuary is a real aspiration, but it's alienating to couples and co-renovators; the room itself is the pitch, not who sleeps alone in it

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Replace with `maximum-scale=5` or remove the constraint entirely.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `*` selector (confirmed)**
```css
*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none; ...}
```
Suppresses OS-level font scaling for users with accessibility needs. Change to `100%` on the `body` rule and remove from the `*` wildcard.

**Mobile breakpoint set to `max-width: 418px` (confirmed)**
The `.mobile` class fires at ≤418px and forces `width: 414px`. Current flagship iPhones (15/16 series) have a 390–430px CSS viewport, making this breakpoint unreliable. Standard email practice is 480px or 600px.

**Duplicate `@font-face` declarations for `"Lineto Brown Sub"` (confirmed)**
Three separate `@font-face` blocks declare the same family name with different sources. While technically valid, this creates ambiguous resolution order across clients. Consolidate into one block with `src:` fallback list.
