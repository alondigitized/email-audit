# Starts now! Up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Crate & Barrel email this week that matches what I'm doing. I'm renovating two houses and need a sofa or two, so a sofa and sectional sale is the right category. The warm, dark-wood living room hero sits above "it's on: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" and a plain Shop Now button. I knew the offer within two seconds. The 9/30 hosting guide and the newborn-shop blasts were wallpaper by comparison.
- It's also a very long email. After the sofa block it turns into a full catalog: rugs, lighting, a swatch program, a performance-fabric story, TheDesignDesk, a dining room section, the lighting edit, a product grid, the credit card, the app and TheDesignDesk again. The sofa offer is strong enough that I'd click. I'd save the swatch and fabric section to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I would not forward it, because it's a sale email and not something to send a friend.

## What's working

- The offer is clear. Up to 20% off sofas and sectionals is the headline, with a Shop Now button right under it.
- The "10 free swatches" block speaks to how I actually buy upholstery. I match colors across rooms before spending four figures on a couch.
- The red-wine stain on cream fabric is a good proof point for performance fabrics, and I can read it at a glance.
- The two-up tiles, up to 20% off sectionals and up to 20% off sofas, make the category split obvious. "Shop Upholstery Sale" is a specific verb and noun.
- The Crate card block (the "it all adds up to a home you love" section) caught my eye because I buy on the highest-rewards card. I'd want the rewards math spelled out.

## What's weak

- No end date is visible, only "Starts now!" and "select styles." For a big furniture purchase I want to know how long the sale runs and which sofas are included.
- The email is too long. TheDesignDesk appears twice, and the app promo ("your chance at $500") dilutes the sofa message.
- There are no prices, delivery windows or lead times for the sofas. Furniture shipping clarity is the main thing I'm hunting for, and it's missing.
- The lighting edit and dining room blocks are pretty but unrelated to the sale. Same lamp-in-three-brands-this-week fatigue: Pottery Barn's Holiday Lookbook and CB2's sale sends cover the same ground.
- The footer is small and cluttered, and the fine print is hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the swatches, performance fabric and TheDesignDesk blocks, and show it once. Put a visible end date and a delivery estimate under the hero.
- 2. Link the hero to one named bestselling sofa with a price and review count, not a general Shop Now.
- 3. Put the Crate card savings math next to the sofa offer, for example "extra X% back on sofas".
- 4. Replace the app block with a free-swatch or showroom appointment CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas & sectionals up to 20% off, through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free swatches + 20% off sectionals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order 10 free swatches, performance fabrics and ships-by dates inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our bestselling sofas, now up to 20% off. Select styles, limited time.`
- Comparisons: Article's bedroom story was moodier, but it had no offer, so this one wins on clarity. CB2's "SALE'S ALMOST OVER" shouted urgency with a real deadline, which this email lacks. Wayfair's broken pergola email sent empty boxes, and this one at least renders cleanly. Pottery Barn would have led with a lookbook, while Crate & Barrel led with a price.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with a mobile media query at 460px. The QA run found 0 errors and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The main confirmed issue is 33 `<img>` elements flagged for missing alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings may come from the relay capturing headers poorly, so they are not confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and link markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are 7 tracking pixels, all with missing alt:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are five repeated third-party (rlcdn.com) pixel calls.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/56d90e87-cd81-4bee-9773-527a04493313?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Subscriber identifier in URLs:** The `mi_u=56aaa278…` value is repeated in image URLs and in the rlcdn `s=` parameter. It is a persistent subscriber identifier exposed in image requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (33 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, for example `def83198-…jpg`, `2a0bb8a6-…jpg`, and the `.gif` files `6039c57a-…gif` and `6b053e7a-…gif`.
  - The scene7 hero `2026%5F0923%5FCB%5FNewSofas%5FSectionals…`.
  - Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, such as `560d850e7e517ede.png`.
  - With images off or a screen reader, these are blank or announce filenames. The two `.gif` files may be animated, and their content would be lost.
  - The seven tracking pixels in section 2 should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem for low-vision users.
- **Text resizing disabled:** The universal selector `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` disable iOS text scaling. They also conflict with the earlier `body { -webkit-text-size-adjust:100% }`.
- **Duplicate and conflicting markup in `<head>`:**
  - Two charset declarations: `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
  - Four overlapping `format-detection` metas (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
  - `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules defined twice.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the production HTML. It is harmless but visible in the source.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype. This is invalid XML, though clients tolerate it.
- **Global paragraph centering:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` applies to every `<p>`. This is a rendering risk where left-aligned copy is intended.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the truncated source or the QA output. Personalization seems to be limited to per-recipient `mi_u`/`mi_mid` IDs. The unseen remainder of the HTML was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the relay likely rewrote the headers.
- **Footer:** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 67 links go through click-tracking redirects that were not resolved, and the source is truncated. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" offer are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add `alt` to all 33 flagged images. Use descriptive text for the content images, the scene7 hero and the `/p/rp/` images, and `alt=""` for the seven tracking pixels.
2. **Viewport and text sizing:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove the `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule.
3. **`<head>` cleanup:** Drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` metas and the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment. Change `&` to `&amp;` in `<title>`.
4. **Pixels:** Deduplicate the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels, which are identical apart from the `n=` counter.
5. **Compliance:** Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in the raw headers received from the sender. The relay is the likely cause of the warnings.
6. **Tracking links:** Resolve the 67 tracking links with a redirect-following probe, then check final URLs for UTM parameters and 200 status.
