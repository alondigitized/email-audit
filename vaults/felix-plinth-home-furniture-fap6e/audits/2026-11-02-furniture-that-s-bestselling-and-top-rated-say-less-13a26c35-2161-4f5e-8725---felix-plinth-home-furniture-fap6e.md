# Furniture that’s bestselling AND top-rated? Say less.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the best Crate & Barrel send I've had this week, and that's mostly because it finally isn't Kids. It's furniture, which is what I'm shopping for across two houses. I only got a thumbnail-sized view of the render, so I'm going by layout, the pull-quote headlines and the button labels. I couldn't read the fine print.
- The concept is clear: the reviews are the hero. Each section leads with a star rating and a big quote, like "The best couch we've ever owned" and "The bed of my dreams!". Under each quote are a few products with their options (depths, fabrics, sizes) and a "Shop Bestselling [Room]" button. It's a long scroll, but it's organized by room, and I can skip to whatever I'm actually renovating.
- I'd click, probably into dining or living. I wouldn't forward it, though. Nothing in it is a find I'd text a friend about. It goes in the Pinterest-adjacent folder.

## What's working

- The review-quote format is a good idea. Social proof as the hero beats "SHOP NOW" by a mile, and I trust a stranger's "solid, heavy and feels really well-made" more than catalog copy.
- It's organized by room (living, dining, bedroom, storage, outdoor), and every block has its own button. That matches how I shop two renovations: one room at a time.
- The product tiles list options such as depths, fabric choices and sizes, so I can see the configurable details before clicking.
- The Thanksgiving hosting guide and the Black Friday early-access signup fit the date. I'm furnishing for the holidays, so it feels timely.
- It's visually cleaner than Wayfair's "Gazebos & pergolas from $100", which had a blank lavender hero box where the photo should be. Here every image loaded.

## What's weak

- The only offer is a small top strip about accent chairs and tables up to 30% off, and it ends today. The sofas, beds and dining sets below don't obviously get a price. The urgency and the products don't line up.
- It's very long, and the Black Friday signup, app promo, Design Desk pitch and "we love these for you" grid all pile on at the bottom. That's five asks after the room sections.
- The pull quotes are good, but several photos feel like a generic catalog. I don't see swatches, sample offers or shipping times, which are what I care about on a sofa.
- I've had five or six Crate sends in a few days, including "Our #1 gift of the season is inside→" and "3 performance rugs you'll love!". Even a good one lands in a fatigued inbox.
- Pottery Barn's pillow send at least told one story. This one jumps across seven categories.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. "Accent chairs & tables up to 30% off, ends tonight" should be a real banner above the first image, not a strip.
- 2. Cut the bottom clutter to two asks, shipping/delivery and the Black Friday signup. Drop the app promo or move it to the footer.
- 3. Add delivery windows and a "order swatches free" line to the sofa and bed blocks.
- 4. Cap it at four rooms and link each hero straight to a specific product page.
- 5. Be careful with cadence. Skip a day after a Kids run, or suppress me from Kids sends entirely.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Furniture that's bestselling AND top-rated? Say less."

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with `lang="en"` and Outlook MSO fallbacks. About 50 images lack alt text, and the HTML head has several hygiene problems. The QA run (50% pass, 0 errors, 4 warnings) could not verify the unsubscribe headers or SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and it skipped all 84 tracking links. The source was truncated, so the body, footer and link targets were not inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** All 84 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 16,974 of 23,520 characters (72%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This is likely long tracking URLs inflating the text part. It is a minor spam-filter and readability risk.
- **Third-party pixels:** The following load from external domains and should be confirmed as intentional:
  - `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `n=5` (five repeated LiveRamp pixels)
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/72b0c68c-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- **Identifier in image URLs:** The `mi_u=dbe8da71...` parameter is carried on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs, so it is a recipient-level identifier.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 50 `<img>` elements were flagged. The scanner can't tell `alt=""` from an absent attribute, so decorative pixels may be false positives. The content images are real gaps:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (about 35 images) and `.../m/47/bbf6f9d4-...png`
  - `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary%2D...`
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images, likely dynamic product tiles)

  If these images carry text or links, image-blocking clients and screen readers lose the content. Tracking pixels should use `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Redundant meta tags:**
  - There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).
  - `format-detection` appears four times, once combined and three times separately, and the separate ones repeat the combined one.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Template placeholder left in source:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production.
- **Conflicting global CSS:**
  - `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ... line-height:100%}` and the global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` can override inherited styles.
  - `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` conflicts with `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`.
  - The `img{max-width:600px}` rule at min-width 768px and `img{max-width:640px}` at min-width 640px overlap, so the 640px rule can apply when a 600px cap was intended.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The media queries target `max-width:460px`. Viewports between 461px and 639px get no responsive rules, so large phones and small tablets may render the desktop layout.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. Dynamic content appears to come from Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/…`). Whether those images rendered correctly was not verified.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a proven violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, because the relay does not expose it. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be verified.
- **Sender domain:** The From domain (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`) matches the image host, which is consistent.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page destinations could not be assessed. The 84 links were not probed, and the visible URLs belong to the image and tracking hosts. The file name `2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide_Tertiary` suggests a September asset reused in a November send. That is only a naming observation, not a confirmed defect.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images, and `alt=""` to pixels and spacers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Consolidate the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags, escape the title as `&amp;`, and delete the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Resolve the conflicting `text-size-adjust`, `line-height` and `img max-width` rules, and add a breakpoint covering 461–639px.
5. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, outside the relay.
6. Shorten tracking URLs or add a real text alternative to bring the plain-text URL ratio under 70%.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, with link probing against the resolved destinations, to cover the footer, UTMs and landing pages.
