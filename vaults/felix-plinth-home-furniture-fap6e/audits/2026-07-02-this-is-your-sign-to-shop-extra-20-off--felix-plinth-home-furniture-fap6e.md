## What's working

- **Open Box Outlet section is the one real news item.** "Limited quantity + extra 20% off clearance" is a different card to play than the standard hero, and it's the kind of deal that moves a renovation-budget needle.
- **Category nav is exhaustive and scannable.** Outdoor, Furniture, Decor, Pillows & Throws, Rugs, Lighting, Bath — every tab I need across two house lists, laid out in one row.
- **"Recommended Just for You" delivers actual products.** I can see specific item images — looks like bedding/linens and possibly a rug — not just a generic homepage link. That earns a second look.
- **Visual hierarchy doesn't fight itself.** Logo → hero offer → categories → secondary sell-ins. My eye landed where it was supposed to, first pass.
- **West Elm is in the Family of Brands cross-sell.** That's the one PB sibling I legitimately browse independently, so surfacing it here isn't wasted ink.

## What's weak

- **"This is your sign" is cringe for this brand and this audience.** The 🔮 belongs on an astrology meme account, not on a premium home brand trying to sell me a $1,400 sectional. It signals someone's A/B test got too experimental and should've been killed in review.
- **Three sends today, same hero.** Crate & Barrel has hit me twice today on outdoor furniture too, but they rotated the lead category between sends. PB is just reprinting the same EXTRA 20% OFF stamp and calling it a campaign.
- **No pricing visible on the recommended products.** I can see the items but not what the 20% takes them to. CB2 at least shows me the sale price in-email; PB makes me click to find out if the math is even worth my time.
- **App download CTA buried in the body is friction I didn't ask for.** I'm here to buy furniture for two houses. The App Store badge mid-scroll breaks the purchase path in a way that should've been footer-only.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with Open Box Outlet, not the standard sale hero.** The Extra 20% off main sale is wallpaper at this point. The Outlet angle is differentiated, scarcity-signaled, and actually worth opening for — put it at the top.
- 2. **Show the math on recommended products.** "Was $349 → sale $279 → Extra 20% = $223." That's what makes someone reach for a rewards card. Numbers close emails.
- 3. **Kill the in-body app download CTA.** Footer only. It creates a dead-end detour in a purchase flow that should be linear.
- 4. **Fix the subject line.** The offer-math version earns the open in a way the crystal ball does not:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open Box + extra 20% off — limited clearance stock`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Outlet deals: extra 20% off what's left before the 4th`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clearance pieces up to 70% off — plus an additional 20% today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited-quantity Open Box styles. Priced to move before July 4th.`
- 5. **Rotate the hero or dial back frequency.** Five sends in three days with the same top-line offer trains subscribers to ignore the subject line. Article just closed a 4th of July send cycle with a single well-timed nudge and a concrete deadline. PB is doing the opposite.

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken CTA link (403) on the credit card apply banner, plus PHP server-side code leaked verbatim into a `<style>` block — both indicate build/template pipeline errors. The rest of the issues are accessibility gaps (57 images without alt text) and missing authentication/unsubscribe headers that may be relay artifacts but warrant verification.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[BROKEN — CONFIRMED]** The credit card apply-and-buy banner returns HTTP 403:
```
https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682
  ?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB
  &marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY
  &external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner
  &cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember
```
This is a revenue-bearing CTA (credit card acquisition). The `cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember` segment targeting is correct, but the destination is dead.

**[INFO]** 23 click-redirect links via `click.e.potterybarn.com` were not probed — standard ESP behavior, not an issue itself, but means destination health is unverified.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[CRITICAL] PHP code leaked into `<style>` block.** The final `<style>` tag in `<head>` contains:
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is PHP `header()` function calls — server-side code that should never appear in rendered HTML output. It is inert in email clients (parsed as malformed CSS, silently ignored) but is a clear build pipeline defect where a PHP image-caching script's output was concatenated into the email template without sanitization.

**[HIGH] 3 images served over HTTP** (will be blocked by Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail with image blocking or HTTPS enforcement):
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — also a cross-brand CDN domain (West Elm)
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — 4-year-old asset path

**[HIGH] Open-tracking pixel served over HTTP:**
```
http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app
```
Open-rate data will be undercounted in clients that block mixed content.

**[MEDIUM] 57 images missing `alt` text** — the entire hero grid (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_14.jpg` across two content blocks), all footer slices, and multiple product images. With images blocked (default in many enterprise mail clients), the email renders as a blank column.

**[LOW] `<title>` is empty.** Some clients surface this in the tab/preview bar.

**[LOW] `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** suppresses pinch-zoom on mobile — WCAG 1.4.4 violation.

**[LOW] `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` applies globally**, overriding OS-level accessibility font scaling.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent.** QA flags this as potentially a relay-capture gap, but Google and Yahoo have required this header for bulk senders (>5k/day) since February 2024. If the header is genuinely missing from the outbound message, this is a deliverability compliance failure.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent.** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe has been required by Google/Yahoo since June 2024 for bulk senders. Absence will increase spam folder placement rates.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the captured email. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; this should align with Williams-Sonoma's published DMARC policy.

**[INFO]** Plain-text body is 73.7% URLs by character count (11,950/16,207 chars). A near-empty plain-text alternative with dense URL content is a known spam-filter trigger for Spamhaus and Microsoft's SmartScreen.

**[INFO]** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are both present — correct for email.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link appear present in the footer (standard for PB), but the `List-Unsubscribe` header compliance is the priority item.
