# Your bedroom design checklist ✔️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and the maroon "Extra 20% off" strip that ran on every send from 10/30 through 11/02 is gone. The top banner says "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" instead. For someone renovating two houses, that's the first line in a week that tells me something useful. The email itself is a numbered five-item checklist: bedding, nightstand, pillows, table lamps, frames. It reads like a lookbook, and I'd drop it straight into my Pinterest-adjacent folder.
- It's also a plaid-and-Christmas bedroom, which isn't my aesthetic for a primary bedroom. There's no offer anywhere above the fold. The only money talk is "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" down by the credit card block. I'd save it and probably not click it.
- I wouldn't forward it. It's pretty and it's well organized, but nothing in it is urgent, and the whole thing runs about a screen and a half longer than it needs to.

## What's working

- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner answers the shipping question I actually have on furniture.
- The numbered 1-through-5 structure gives it a story and a rhythm. It beats the straight product grid I get from Wayfair.
- "Ships Free" tags on the Patchwork Plaid Sheet Set and the Stewart Plaid Pillow are small, concrete cues.
- Each numbered item has its own category link (Bedding, Nightstands, Pillows, Table Lamps, Frames), so I can click straight to the thing I care about.
- The "Book a Free Appointment" design services block is a nice reminder. It matches the earlier "Book your FREE design services" send from 11/03.

## What's weak

- No discount, no deadline and no price anywhere. After a week of "Extra 20% off," the silence on price makes me wonder what happened to it.
- The plaid and Christmas styling is narrow for a primary bedroom. The stocking, Christmas Shop and "ready for guests" lines pull this toward guest-room holiday dressing.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block is mostly stockings, a curtain rod, a dining table and a rug. That doesn't feel personal at all. I've been browsing furniture, not stockings.
- Too many CTAs: Shop Beds, five category links, In-Stock Furniture, the Christmas Shop, Book a Free Appointment, the app, the card and Affirm. The eye has nowhere to land.
- The fine print block at the bottom is a wall of tiny text, and the rewards and Affirm sections repeat what I already know.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer or deadline in the hero. The "Extra 20%" language, or a financing line, would do it. Right now the strongest hook is shipping time, and it's buried in a thin banner.
- 2. Trim the checklist to three items, and make the first one the bed or a big furniture piece, not a pillow.
- 3. Segment the "Recommended for you" block on furniture browsing, so I'm not getting stockings. Crate & Barrel's Ooni send was at least on-topic start to finish.
- 4. Move the card and Affirm blocks into a slim footer strip.
- 5. Show a swatch or sample link for the bedding. For colour-matching across two houses, that's what I'd actually click.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced build with correct basics (UTF-8, `role="presentation"` on the main tables, MSO conditionals). It has widespread missing alt text, stray non-CSS code inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in tracking and asset URLs. The QA run scored 50% pass (0 failures, 4 warnings). Most warnings concern headers that the relay probably stripped, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 61 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect URLs. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM handling are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` loads over plain HTTP. The open signal can be blocked or sent in cleartext.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on a different brand's domain. This is a minor dependency risk.
- **Stale asset path:** `/pb/2025/US/H25_TOP_InStockFurniture/...` sits in a 2026 campaign (`H26_...`), which suggests a reused module.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
