# Shop the look: beautiful kid & baby spaces →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kids sender for the third time today, and I'm snoozing it again. The top banner is the same "free personalization on Nod & Barrel Chairs" promo from the earlier two sends. The "Shop the look" idea is the part I'd actually save. Walnut-striped bedroom, a moody-wallpaper nursery, a sage-green study nook: that's Pinterest-folder material. But I have two houses mid-renovation and neither has a kid's room on the punch list.
- The format is good, but the email is very long. Four rooms each get a hero photo, a three-product strip and two buttons. After that come a Nod chair repeat, a bedding block, a "we love these for you" grid, a credit-card pitch, an app-download banner and The TradeDesk pitch. It reads like a catalog, which I like, but there's no clear offer or deadline. For me it's a mark-as-read, with a screenshot saved only if the green desk wall holds up.
- I wouldn't forward it. If I had a nursery in progress I'd probably click through.

## What's working

- The room-by-room "shop the look" layout is how I actually browse. I get a styled photo first, then the pieces.
- The photography is strong. The sage study and the dark-wallpaper nursery are the kind of images I'd save.
- Each room has "Shop [Room] Furniture" and "Shop by Room" buttons, so there are clear tap paths.
- Products are labeled "Bestseller" or "GREENGUARD Gold Certified." That's useful signal for furniture.
- The "design a calming kid's bedroom" and "create a cozy nursery" headers make the sections easy to skim on a commute.

## What's weak

- The "free today on our app" personalization banner is a repeat of the two earlier sends today, on the same Nod chair. I've now seen it three times.
- It's too long. Pottery Barn Kids' sends run shorter, and Article's "Give guests a cozy stay" had one hero and one idea.
- There's no hard offer on the products, and no price on most of the room strips. My rewards-card math has nothing to work with.
- The credit-card pitch, app download, TradeDesk and bedding blocks pile up at the bottom and dilute the "shop the look" message.
- The product text is tiny. I'd be zooming in to read names and prices on a phone.
- It's a kid/baby audience. Nothing here signals that I'm a dining-room and lighting shopper, which is what I clicked on earlier today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it one email with one hero, and cut it to two rooms. Put a single offer like "Free personalization today" at the top and don't repeat it mid-email.
- 2. Show prices, or a "from $X," under each product strip.
- 3. Move the credit card, app and TradeDesk blocks into a single footer module.
- 4. Throttle the Kids sender against the main Crate & Barrel sends. Two Kids emails plus a Thanksgiving dining email in one day is a lot.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Shop the look: beautiful kid & baby spaces →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML Transitional with Outlook (MSO) fallbacks and about 93 redirect-wrapped tracking links. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` text (about 38 content images), zoom-blocking viewport and text-size settings, and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be inspected. The QA run passed 50% of checks (0 failures, 4 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 93 links on tracking or click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. The QA run reported no broken links, but it also didn't test these.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` beacons (LiveRamp) are embedded. Each carries the same recipient-hashed `s=` value.
- **Duplicate first-party pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times with an identical `mi_u` value.
- **Other pixels:** an open pixel (`.../p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/90d1ba8e-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are also present.
- **Image load cost:** the duplicate pixels add about 12 extra requests for no added signal.
- **Plain-text part:** 18,840 of 26,866 characters (70.1%) are URLs. This usually comes from long tracking URLs. It can hurt the text/HTML ratio and spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text:** 51 `<img>` elements were flagged. About 38 are content images, for example `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `…/m/1/c6f4be4c-….jpg` through `…/m/1/971aa10f-….jpg`, plus the GIF `1646b818-….gif`. The other 13 are tracking pixels (see section 2). Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no content. If these images carry the product and room imagery, the email is unusable with images off.
- **Pixels without `alt`:** the tracking pixels should have `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. The decorative ones should use `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `body{… -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` conflict with the earlier `100%` declarations. The universal `line-height:100%` also overrides per-element line heights.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (the typo is in the source) is left in the markup.
  - An empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` rule sits inside the MSO block.
  - The `format-detection` meta tag is declared three times, once with a different value set.
  - The `<title>` contains a raw `&` (`Crate & Barrel`) instead of `&amp;`.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** the main breakpoint is `max-width:460px`, with `.mobile{width:460px!important}` and `.showmobile{width:414px!important}` forced. These fixed widths can overflow on screens narrower than 414px.
- **`img` max-width:** the `@media (min-width:640px)` rule sets `img {max-width:640px}` and the 768px rule sets `600px`. Because the 768px query comes first, the 640px rule wins on desktop, so the 600px value never applies.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. Per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) appear to be correctly resolved. The truncated HTML means body-level tokens can't be fully ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers, so it is **unconfirmed**, not a verified sender defect. Check the raw message or send a test through a direct mailbox before treating it as a failure.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** absent for the same relay reason, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the image and tracking hosts are on the `crateandbarrel.com` family. Alignment can't be confirmed here.
- **Footer and physical address:** the footer unsubscribe link and postal address are outside the truncated source, so they are **not assessed**.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, because the sampled links are redirect-wrapped (`mi.crateandbarrel.com` / `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) and 93 were not resolved. Final-destination UTMs and landing-page alignment **can't be verified**.
- **Link resolution:** the tracking-domain hop is the only visible link layer. Resolving those redirects would show whether the "shop the look" links land on kids and baby pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all about 38 content images, starting with the `…/m/1/*.jpg` set and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. Set `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and the global `line-height:100%`.
3. Remove the duplicate `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels, and check whether five `sr.rlcdn.com` beacons are needed.
4. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, via a direct-delivery test rather than the relay.
5. Resolve a sample of the 93 tracking links to confirm 200 responses, final UTM parameters and landing-page match.
6. Shorten tracking URLs or add more real text to the plain-text version to bring the URL ratio down from 70%.
7. Clean up the template leftovers: the `IMPUT HERE` comment, the empty CSS rule, the duplicate `format-detection` tags, and the unescaped `&` in `<title>`.
