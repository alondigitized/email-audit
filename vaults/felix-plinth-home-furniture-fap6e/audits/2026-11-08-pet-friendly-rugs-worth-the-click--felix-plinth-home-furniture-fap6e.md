On 11/05 it was patio heaters sitting next to a massage chair. I've stopped expecting the subject to match the body.

It's buried under shoe racks and a Christmas tree tile, though, and it has no product, price or clear button. The 7% back Wayfair card block is the only thing that touches my rewards math. I already run the highest-rewards card, so it's noise.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skip it, or save it to my inspiration folder if the king-bed photo caught me. I wouldn't forward it. Nobody wants a link to a 20-tile grid.

## What's working

- The "Must-Have Pet-Friendly Rugs" tile is first in the grid, so the subject isn't entirely a lie.
- The "fit for a king? Rug sizing solves" hero is the most useful content here. A walnut bed over a layered rug is the kind of image I'd pin.
- The purple "Black Friday Pop-Up Sale, up to 70% off, 48 hours only" banner is clear, and the urgency reads as credible for this week.
- Each tile has a plain verb-free label with a price anchor ("Under $75", "Under $25", "from $100"), which lets me scan fast.
- The Wayfair Credit Card block states a concrete 7% back with an "Apply Now" button.

## What's weak

- The subject promised pet-friendly rugs, but they get one small tile. That makes three Wayfair sends in a row (11/05, 11/07, now) where the subject hook is a single tile.
- Pergolas are back in the second slot. That's the fourth or fifth pergola appearance since 11/01, and I'm not buying a pergola.
- The 20-plus tiles span stockings, toilet seats, washing machines and teapot sets. "Picked just for you" doesn't feel personal at all.
- The rug sizing hero has no product, no price and no CTA. It's a nice photo with a dead end.
- The pet-friendly tile shows a classic traditional rug. Nothing visible says pet-friendly: no stain-resistant, washable or pile-height detail.
- The legal block at the bottom is tiny and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the email: a pet-friendly rug hero with 3-4 rug tiles, and drop pergolas, toilet seats and teapots. Pottery Barn's "Ships free" send at least built the whole email around one theme.
- 2. Turn the "fit for a king?" image into a real module with a size guide (5x8 vs 8x10 vs 9x12) and a "Shop rugs by bed size" button.
- 3. Put washable, stain-resistant and shed-friendly callouts on the rug tiles. Article's UGC-style send showed real rooms, and Wayfair could show real dog-on-rug photos.
- 4. Add a clear rug price, like "Washable rugs from $X".
- 5. Move the credit card block lower, or cut it for people who already hold it.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe results are inconclusive, not broken.** All 8 detailed probe results returned 429, including `/daily-sales`, `/`, the app OneLink and the two `/daily-sales/filters/...` links. A 429 is rate limiting, so I'm not treating these as dead links. 12 links were probed, 53 were skipped and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget:
  - `.../gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se8...`
  - `.../kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.htm...`
  - `.../wayfaircard?_emr=...`
  - `.../?_emr=...&wfcs=cs7&_eml`
  
  The QA output shows these URLs cut off. The last one ends at `&_eml` with no value, but the report may have truncated it, so I can't confirm a malformed link.
- **Insecure fallback in the app link.** The OneLink URL (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`) has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Any user without the app lands on an `http://` URL and depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Inconsistent campaign labeling.** The same OneLink URL sets `c=Triggered` but also carries `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labeled "Triggered" may skew attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **Unsecured tracking pixels.** There are 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019&sz=2x1` pixels, all flagged "Image uses http://". Clients that block mixed content, or proxies that don't rewrite it, will drop them, so impression counts will undercount. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 429 and unprobed links with throttling or a retry and backoff, then re-run QA. Don't treat the 429s as failures until then.
2. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't count the missing headers as a compliance defect until you've checked outside the relay.

4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two linked banners and the content image (`...rightsizerugforkingbed`). Use `alt=""` on pixels and spacers.
5. Align `c=Triggered` on the OneLink URL with the batch classification.
6. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters, or a documented mapping from `refid`/`emlid`, for downstream analytics.
