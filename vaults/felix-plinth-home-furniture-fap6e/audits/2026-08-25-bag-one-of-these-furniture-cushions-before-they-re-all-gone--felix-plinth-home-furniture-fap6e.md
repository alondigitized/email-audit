# Bag one of these furniture cushions before they’re all gone!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, subject says "cushions" and the hero image is actually a stack of cushions — green, rust, and cream ones piled up in a nice neutral room. That's more alignment than Wayfair usually manages, but then the copy under it just says "fresh-picked favorites" with zero specifics, no price, no "cushions" word anywhere in the actual text. Then it pivots hard into a full grab-bag: privacy screens, pergolas, gazebo deals, a copper kettle, porch swings, slipcovers. That's six unrelated categories under a "shop before they're gone" banner that never once mentions the thing that got me to open the email.
- There's also a straight-up broken render — a big blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon sitting right before the footer. That's the second Wayfair email this week with something visibly off (their "Patio Heaters" send buried the actual heater under a stockpot and a bathtub).
- I'm not clicking this one. The cushions I came for get one photo and zero follow-through — I'd have to guess which of six unrelated tiles might lead to cushion inventory. Not forwarding, not opening the next one from this sender for a few days.

## What's working

- The hero image genuinely matches the subject line — stacked cushions front and center, which Wayfair doesn't always pull off (their "Patio Heaters" and "Outdoor Furniture" sends both buried the headline product).
- "Get It Now, Pay Later" with Afterpay/Klarna badges is a real, clean financing callout — useful for the furniture-budget math I'm always running mid-renovation.
- Wayfair Rewards / Flexible Financing / Wayfair Professional icon row at the bottom is a nice touch for someone like me who's mid-reno and actually cares about rewards math.

## What's weak

- "Cushions" never appears anywhere in body copy — the subject promised something specific and the email delivers vague filler ("fresh-picked favorites") instead.
- The "shop before they're gone" grid is a dumping ground — privacy screens, pergolas, gazebos, a kettle, porch swings, slipcovers. None of it is cushions, and it's the same scattershot problem CB2's "Summer's calling" send avoided by actually staying in one lane.
- There's a broken/blank image block right above the footer — literal empty white space with a tiny error icon. That's a hard miss on QA.
- 70% off Labor Day clearance banner up top never reconnects to the cushion story below it — I can't tell if the cushions are even part of that sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "cushions" in the actual body copy and link the hero straight to a cushion collection page — right now the subject and the CTA are disconnected, and that's the single biggest leak in this email.
- 2. Fix the broken image block before the footer — a blank box with an error icon undercuts trust on every email after this one.
- 3. Cut the "shop before they're gone" grid to 2-3 truly related items (throw pillows, slipcovers, outdoor cushions) instead of six unrelated categories — Article's Miila launch proved a tight, on-theme grid beats a grab-bag.
- 4. Tie the 70%-off Labor Day banner explicitly to the cushions — "70% off cushions & throw pillows" would give me a reason to scroll instead of bail after the hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard Wayfair MKTEML batch send (refid=MKTEML_121355) built on a responsive Uniform-style ("u-") table framework. Automated QA found no confirmed broken links, but flagged missing List-Unsubscribe/Authentication-Results headers, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and pervasive missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 of 44 links were probed (32 deprioritized as footer/utility/social); of those probed, 8 returned **HTTP 429 (rate-limited)** rather than a pass/fail — includes the primary CTAs "Get Exclusive Deals on the App," "Shop Sale," the hero image link, and the featured PDP link (`.../pdp/hokku-designs-18-h-customizable-bench-cushion-...w113526282.html`). These are inconclusive, not confirmed failures — recommend re-probing outside the batch rate limit.
- **4 links were unprobed** due to a 70s time budget cutoff, including a Buy-Now-Pay-Later link (`wayfair.com/buy-now-pay-later~b547.html`) and two PDP links — status unknown.
- All probed/tracked links carry consistent tracking params: `_emr`, `wfcs=cs7`, `_eml`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `sm`, `refid=MKTEML_121355`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16016`, `mdlid` — no malformed or missing tracking params observed in the sampled set.
- **20 open/impression tracking pixels use `http://` instead of `https://`**: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019` (sz=2x1, m=false, p=c4951f73-...). Non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently degrade open-tracking accuracy.
- A separate open-tracking pixel via `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (base64-encoded payload decoding to `mcch=ECM&eventType=EmailOpen&source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15...`) is HTTPS — no issue there.

3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 19 flagged images, prioritizing the BNPL legal disclaimer image (compliance-relevant) and the hero/CTA images (conversion-relevant for images-off rendering).
