# HEADBOARDS at a great price.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- So the subject line says "HEADBOARDS at a great price," I open it expecting headboards, and the very first thing that hits me is a giant orange-and-red "OUTDOOR CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF ENDS TONIGHT" banner. I have officially lost count of how many times Wayfair has done this to me this week — this is the exact same subject/hero bait-and-switch I flagged on "BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS" (7/31 and again 8/1) and on "CAT TREES *on sale*" (8/1). At this point it's not a fluke, it's the house style.
- The actual headboards content shows up as one of four tiny tiles in a "score styles you ❤️" grid, no price, no percent off, just "Our Best Headboard Deals »." Then it's off to Klarna/Afterpay, a random garden gnome mascot pitching "the Wayborhood," and a second massive grid of completely unrelated stuff — dog beds, greenhouses, stove kettles, recliners — like they just dumped every open SKU category into one email. There's also a blank broken-image box with a stray little icon partway down, which is just sloppy.
- I'm not clicking on headboards from this — there's no price, no product shot I can actually judge, nothing to act on. This gets marked read and forgotten. Not forwarding it; if anything I'd screenshot the subject/hero mismatch and send it to a friend as a "look how bad Wayfair's email game is" joke, which is not the reaction they wanted.

## What's working

- Wayfair's purple branding is consistent and instantly recognizable, no confusion about who sent this.
- The "Get It Now, Pay Later" Affirm/Klarna block is genuinely useful info for anyone furnishing two houses at once — that's real utility, not filler.
- The headboard tile image itself (navy upholstered headboard) is decent — clean, on-trend, the kind of thing that would work in my primary bedroom.
- Wayfair Rewards and Flexible Financing get their own footer icons, so at least loyalty stuff isn't totally buried.

## What's weak

- The subject promises headboards; the hero banner sells outdoor clearance. Same trick as the privacy-screens and cat-trees emails I already dinged them for — I'm starting to feel like the subject line is just clickbait with zero connection to what's actually being pushed.
- Zero pricing or percent-off info anywhere near the headboards tile — just a bare "Our Best Headboard Deals »" link with no number to anchor on.
- There's a broken/blank image box with a tiny icon partway through the email — looks like a tracking pixel or ad slot that failed to load.
- The second grid is a random household grab-bag (greenhouses, dog beds, stove kettles, patio benches) that has nothing to do with headboards or even a coherent theme — it reads like they emptied a warehouse inventory feed into the template.
- The garden gnome "Wayborhood" mascot is bizarre and does nothing for a 36-year-old renovating two houses — it reads more like a kids'-brand gag than anything I'd associate with furnishing a primary residence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero actually be about headboards if that's the subject line — a real product shot, a real price or % off, one clear "Shop Headboards" button above the fold. Pottery Barn's "Shades of indigo" email (7/31, 7/10) at least kept its hero coherent with its subject; Wayfair should copy that discipline.
- 2. Cut the second unrelated mega-grid entirely, or split it into a separate, honestly-subject-lined email. Trying to cross-sell greenhouses and dog beds in a headboard email dilutes the whole thing.
- 3. Fix the broken image block — it's a visible defect that undercuts trust in an already trust-strained sender relationship (see: four mismatched sends in four days).
- 4. Drop or seriously reframe the gnome mascot — it doesn't match a renovation-focused adult buyer's mental model of a "great deal on a headboard."
- 5. Add specific pricing to the headboard tile ("Headboards from $89" or similar) so there's an actual reason to click instead of a bare category link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Headboards from $89 — this week only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New bedroom, new headboard: shop the sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Upholstered, wood, and platform styles — all on sale through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pair it with your Klarna checkout — 0% interest available.`

# Technical Audit: Wayfair "HEADBOARDS at a great price" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses a standard Uber-style responsive MJML/table layout with heavy mobile-media-query overrides; QA probing returned a 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and two unauthenticated-header warnings, with no confirmed broken links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 60 links were probed (footer/social/utility links deprioritized); of those probed, 7 returned **HTTP 429 (rate limited)** rather than a confirmed failure — includes the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Our Best Headboard Deals," and the Wayfair logo/header link. These are inconclusive, not confirmed broken.
- 4 links were left unprobed due to a 70s time budget (e.g. `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-dressers-se44425.html`, `/welcome-to-the-wayborhood~b786.html`).
- Every probed link carries a consistent tracking parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid=...`, `sltid=N`, `brcid=1`, `sm=1`, `refid=MKTEML_121679`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15891`, `mdlid=...`) — internally consistent across all sampled URLs, no mismatched or stale campaign IDs observed.
- 20 tracking/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS (see §3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20+ images missing `alt` text**, including primary content images (`0728_OutdoorClearance_Skinny-EndsTonight.jpg`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, `wf_email_displaybanner_outdoorentertainin[g].jpg`, `bnpl_legal_disclaimer.png`) and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels — screen readers/blocked-image clients will render this content as empty.
- **20 images use `http://` (non-HTTPS) sources** — all `li.wayfair.com/imp?...` beacons. Mixed-content HTTP resources are blocked by default in many mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy over HTTPS-only), which will silently drop these impression pixels and undercount opens for the affected inventory slots.
- One image filename contains a URL-encoded stray character: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` (literal `%o` in the path) — worth confirming this isn't a broken/mistyped asset reference vs. an intentional encoding.
- Extensive mobile media-query scaffolding (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidth`, `Button--mobileAlignTable`, etc.) is present and structurally standard for this ESP template; no rendering-breaking CSS issues found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`), or dynamic-content conditionals are visible in the provided HTML source. No issues found based on available evidence — note the HTML was truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in the captured headers. QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than it being absent from the original send — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance status from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — same relay-capture caveat applies. If genuinely absent, this email would not support Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe, which is a bulk-sender requirement as of 2024.
- **Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found — again potentially a relay artifact via AgentMail rather than a true absence. Cannot confirm authentication pass/fail status.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in the footer per QA (deprioritized from probing but included by policy).
- Recommend verifying these three header findings against the raw SMTP transaction (not the relay-forwarded copy) before treating them as compliance failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are used; Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`refid=MKTEML_121679`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15891`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`) substitutes for UTM and is applied consistently across all sampled links — no attribution gaps found.
- Landing destinations align with email content: headboard CTA → `/daily-sales/filters/our-best-headboard-deals-se18073.html` (matches subject line "HEADBOARDS at a great price"); general CTA → `/daily-sales`; app CTA → OneLink deep-link (`wayfairapp://home`) with correct `af_web_dp` HTTPS fallback to `wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
- No mismatched or off-topic destination URLs found among probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all content images and tracking pixels (20+ instances) — highest-volume finding.
2. Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent silent open-tracking loss in mixed-content-blocking clients.
3. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` directly against the raw send (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to distinguish a true compliance gap from a relay-capture artifact.
4. Re-probe the 7 rate-limited (429) links and 4 unprobed links outside the 70s budget window to confirm they resolve correctly, particularly the primary "Shop Sale" and headboard-deal CTAs.
5. Confirm whether `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` is a correctly encoded asset path or a broken filename reference.
