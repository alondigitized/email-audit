# Early Black Friday bedding deals + FREE SHIPPING

## Executive summary

- Tenth Pottery Barn email in about two days, and at least this one has a lane. Bedding is a real category for me. The maroon "UP TO 70% OFF BEDDING" banner is the same maroon Early Black Friday block I've seen in "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SAVINGS across the home" and "Early Black Friday is ON!", but it's now bedding-only. I'll give them credit for that.
- The body is a clean stack of products. Each has a "SHIPS FREE" tag and a tiered offer: 70% off quilts and duvets, 30% off velvet, 50% off holiday. The tiers are easy to scan. The Christmas-cheer quilt with Santa on it isn't for me. It's the kind of thing Wayfair would also push, and I'd have it in three brands' emails by Tuesday.

## What's working

- The hero is bedding-only with one big "SHOP NOW." It's a narrower promise than the nine home-wide sends before it.
- "SHIPS FREE" on each product tile answers the shipping question for bulky items before I ask.
- The tiered discounts (70 / 30 / 50) make it clear which categories are the real deal.
- The Belgian Flax Linen duvet and the Cozy Cloud quilt are the kind of neutral, textured, lived-in styling I buy.
- The 10% back in Rewards block near the bottom speaks to how I buy: highest-rewards card, and I do the math.

## What's weak

- The maroon banner is identical to the last several sends. I'm numb to it.
- The hero doesn't show a product. I have to scroll to see a bed.
- The "Santa" holiday bedding tile feels off-persona. I'm furnishing two homes, not decorating a Christmas display.
- After the bedding, the "Recommended Just For You" section drifts into stockings, a throw, a dining table and a curtain rod. That feels generic, not personal.
- The legal fine print is dense and tiny. It's a wall of text, and I can't read it without zooming.
- The "Book a Design Crew appointment" block shows a showroom/design-service angle, but it's buried at the bottom.

- 1. Put a hero bed photo behind the headline instead of a flat maroon block, and cut the repeated banner.

- 3. Show real "was/now" prices on every tile. Right now I see "Was $249" in small gray type and then percent-off ranges.
- 4. Move the Design Crew and Rewards modules above the "Recommended" row. Those are my decision drivers.
- 5. Add swatch and sample access to the bedding. For a color-palette decision, I want fabric swatches before I commit.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style, image-sliced, table-based email. The main confirmed issues are missing alt text on about 55 images, three `http://` image sources, a leaked PHP snippet in a `<style>` block, and the recipient's address exposed in tracking-pixel and image URLs. The header-based compliance checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified because the AgentMail relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 63 links go through click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7...`). The HTTP probe skipped them, so destination validity is unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` loads over HTTP, so it can be blocked and leaks the address in the clear.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with alt `"1"`. Several clients will block it.
- **Second tracker:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/ac475c4d-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is an open-tracking pixel with no alt.
- **Insecure assets:** two further images use `http://`: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed content may be blocked or flagged in some clients.
- **Stale or cross-brand assets:** `https://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2025/US/H25_Bottom_InStock_V2/H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg` is a 2025 asset in a 2026 send. `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on the West Elm domain. Both are low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 images are flagged, including all 18 hero slices (`.../1115_SUN_AM2_EBF_BeddingDeals/slices_01–18.jpg`) and 18 footer slices. The nav image does carry alt (`POTTERY BARN`), so the check is discriminating. With images off or a screen reader, the offer content (the "Up to 70% off" and free-shipping messaging) is unavailable. Product images such as `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg` also lack alt.
- **Leaked server code:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS. Parsers will drop it, but it looks like a template bug and it can corrupt the CSS rule that follows it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Layout tables:** the spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">`) has no `role="presentation"`, unlike the others.
- **Obsolete selectors:** `.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass` and `.yshortcuts` are legacy and harmless.
- **Dead code:** several commented-out viewport tags remain.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the supplied source. The HTML is truncated, so this is not a full check.
- The recipient email is embedded in plaintext query strings: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. The `coreemail.html` URL is used as an image src. This puts PII in URLs that will appear in proxy and CDN logs. Combined with `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`, referrer leakage is limited, but it is still a privacy weakness.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA reports both absent. The QA note says AgentMail may strip them, so treat this as unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.potterybarn.com` are unverified.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** the source is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm either.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,431 of 17,449 characters (71%) are URLs, which trips spam heuristics. This comes from long tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters can be verified, because all 63 links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects that were not resolved.
- Product images link to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` assets, but destination pages weren't probed.
- No continuity conclusion is possible without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all sliced hero and footer images, and `alt=""` to the spacer and tracking pixels.
2. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
3. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`.
4. Remove the viewport `maximum-scale=1`. Also add `lang="en"` and a non-empty `<title>`.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in image and pixel query strings. Use an opaque subscriber ID.
6. Fix the QA gaps: capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from the source message, and follow the redirects to check final URLs and UTMs.
7. Retire the 2025 and cross-brand (West Elm) asset references.
