# ON NOW✨ The Warehouse Sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Another Pottery Barn email in a week that's already given me the digital catalog, the GreenRow tease, the Fall Lookbook, and "1600+ new arrivals." "The Warehouse Sale" at least has a number on it — up to 60% off — which is more than I can say for that lookbook send. The swivel chair hero shot is genuinely nice, and I like that the category grid underneath (Furniture, Rugs, Bedding, Outdoor, Bath, all ten of them) basically maps to the rooms I'm actually staging across two houses right now.
- But then it does the thing every PB-family email does lately: it can't stop itself at one idea. Sale banner, then a "Recommended Just For You" grid that's actually useful, then a random "Discover New Arrivals" lifestyle banner, then — genuinely, in an August warehouse-sale email — a full "The Halloween Shop, Enter If You Dare" section with a fog-machine porch. That's a hard stop for me. I'm mid-reno, not shopping for skeletons.
- I'd scroll past the Halloween block, glance at the recommended tiles because the duvet cover and the wool rug are actually in my color palette, and probably close the email without clicking. Not forwarding this one — nothing here beats the Wayfair "Fearless design, bold layers" send for room inspiration, and it's not as tightly curated as PB's own "1600+ new arrivals" email from two days ago.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off" up top with a single Shop Now button — I don't have to hunt for the offer.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid actually reads like personalization: a platform bed, curtain hardware, a linen duvet, a wool rug — that's a real room, not a random SKU dump.
- Material call-outs on product names (Belgian Flax Linen, Hand-Tufted Wool, Hydrocotton) give me enough to know if it's worth clicking through without opening every tile.
- The 10% Back in Rewards credit card block landed — I already run everything through my highest-rewards card, so a loyalty tie-in like this is the one section I actually read closely.
- Clean render, no broken images, no overlapping text — better production quality than CB2's marble email, which felt like two campaigns fighting each other.

## What's weak

- The Halloween Shop insert makes zero sense in a warehouse-sale email in August — it reads like two different campaigns got stapled together, and it kills the momentum right when I'd be scrolling toward "Discover New Arrivals."
- "Up to 60% off" is a ceiling number with no per-category breakdown — by the time I hit the legal fine print block ("*Promotional Exclusions apply") I'm assuming the good stuff isn't 60% off.
- No deadline anywhere. "ON NOW" tells me it started, not when it ends — nothing forcing me to act today versus next week.
- This is at minimum the fourth Pottery Barn-family send in my inbox this week (catalog, GreenRow, lookbook, now this) — the cadence fatigue is real, and unlike the "1600+ new arrivals" send, this one doesn't earn its spot with fresh curation.
- No product pricing or dimensions on the recommended tiles — Wayfair and Crate & Barrel both do a better job surfacing at-a-glance price/size info before I have to click through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Halloween Shop block entirely from this send — it's off-topic for a furniture sale email and actively derails the scroll. Save it for its own campaign.
- 2. Put a real deadline on the sale ("Ends Sunday 8/9") next to the 60% off banner — urgency without a clock isn't urgency.
- 3. Break "up to 60% off" into category-specific numbers (e.g., "40% off rugs, 60% off outdoor") so it doesn't read as bait once you hit the fine print.
- 4. Add price or "starting at $X" under each Recommended tile — I shouldn't have to click through six products just to know if they're in budget.
- 5. Drop the emoji from the subject line — it reads more spam-adjacent than PB usually does.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Warehouse Sale: up to 60% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off rugs, bedding & more — Warehouse Sale is on`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns across furniture, bedding, and rugs — while they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your recommended picks are included in the sale.`

# Technical Audit — "ON NOW✨ The Warehouse Sale" (Pottery Barn)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard SFMC/ExactTarget-style MSO-compatible table email. Three code-level issues stand out: leftover PHP `header()` calls embedded in a `<style>` block, four assets/pixels served over plain HTTP (one leaking the recipient's email address in cleartext), and missing `alt` text on nearly every image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 56 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`, etc.) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — validity/destination not confirmed.
- Header CTA routes through an obfuscated ESP redirect: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5NjB9AAsAAAAAA57D7J5P0aFonuv9Y-eRjHPWwZZmhumUWzWI5oP_PlhJj_3l_XrVl_DJhnC-fYy9NyeJ8KKHSS9zZhJbvAXlPjOPjOvgXslnmfA-v5z72KYkOtVB` — actual landing page cannot be verified without following it live.
- Third-party ad-tech pixel present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn Inc./Amobee) — shares open/engagement signal with an external ad platform.
- Open-tracking pixel is loaded over **HTTP** and carries the recipient's full email address as a plaintext query param: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 39 of ~40 `<img>` elements have no `alt` attribute (full list in QA output), e.g. `slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `custom-capitola-hand-tufted-wool-rug-l.jpg`. With images blocked (Outlook/Gmail default), the email renders as unlabeled blank tiles.
- `<title></title>` is empty — no inbox tab/preview title.
- Four images load over `http://` instead of `https://` (see §2 and §7), risking mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-only mail clients.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) and Gmail/Apple resets (`.ExternalClass`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]`) are present and correctly structured — no issues found here.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tags (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) appear in the source. The recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` resolves consistently across the customer-service link and open-tracking pixel, indicating personalization is functioning (transport of that value over HTTP is flagged separately in §2/§5).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers not detected — QA tooling itself notes this may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than a confirmed sender-side omission; recommend verifying at the source MTA.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — same relay caveat applies; authentication status is unknown, not confirmed failing.
- The truncated HTML provided does not include the footer/unsubscribe block, so CAN-SPAM body requirements (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) cannot be verified from this excerpt.
- Separately: transmitting the recipient's email address over unencrypted HTTP (`svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel, §2) is a PII-handling concern worth flagging alongside compliance review.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible on raw hrefs; all primary CTAs route through ESP click-redirects (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...`) that presumably append campaign params server-side — cannot confirm without following the redirect.
- Direct (non-redirected) link `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is HTTPS and domain-consistent — no issue found.
- Landing-page alignment for the Warehouse Sale CTA cannot be confirmed since the destination is obfuscated behind a tracking redirect that was skipped during probing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");` PHP calls sitting inside the `<style>` block in `<head>` — inert as CSS today, but evidence of server-side template code leaking into compiled output; fix at the build/compile step.
2. Add `alt` text to all 39 flagged images, prioritizing product/CTA images.
3. Move the four HTTP resources to HTTPS — especially `svopn.potterybarn.com`, which currently exposes the recipient's email address in a cleartext query string.
4. Retire the stale 2022-dated asset (`edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`) and confirm the cross-brand `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` reference is intentional.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` directly at the sending MTA rather than relying on relay capture, since QA flagged these as relay-side unknowns.
6. Confirm the `d.turn.com` third-party pixel's data use is covered by current privacy disclosures.
