# Open Box steals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Seventy-two. That's where my Wayfair count sits, and "Open Box steals" lands the same day they already sent me "AREA RUGS." — so I'm getting two Wayfair emails on June 26, following "MAILBOXES" and "BIRDBATHS" on June 25. The cadence is starting to feel like spam.
- That said — I actually paused on this one before deleting, because open box is the one Wayfair format that genuinely fits my situation. I'm furnishing two homes simultaneously, and I'm not precious about whether the floor lamp had a previous owner if it's 55% off and arrives without a dent. The premise is correct. The execution is lazy. Seventeen category rows with "up to X% OFF" next to each is the same wall of purple text I've seen a dozen times. No specific products. No hero items I can actually go look at.
- The blue KitchenAid-looking mixer in the hero imagery is what kills me. I'm shopping for end tables and dining room sets and you've put a countertop appliance front-and-center in a furniture sale email. I'd click. I wouldn't forward it to anyone. I'd just quietly filter it into my deal folder and come back later.

## What's working

- **Premise is dead-on for my use case.** Open box savings on furniture makes genuine sense when you're furnishing two properties at the same time — I'm not just a Wayfair browser, I'm actively buying.
- **Category list with percentage ranges is actually scannable.** I can immediately see "Dining Room Sets | up to 65% OFF" and "End Tables | up to 65% OFF" — two things I'm literally shopping right now — without having to read a paragraph.
- **"SHOP ALL SALES" button is visually unambiguous.** Purple, centered, high contrast. I know exactly what it does.
- **Wayfair Rewards visible in footer.** I'm a member and I do track the points math, so seeing it is a small reassurance.
- **Clean render, no broken images.** The email actually displays. Given how often Wayfair fires these, it's nice that this one didn't show a gray box where the hero should be.

## What's weak

- **Blue countertop mixer in the hero.** This is a kitchen appliance in a home goods / furniture email. I understand Wayfair sells everything, but the creative choice sends the wrong signal. Article's 4th of July email showed an actual sofa hero — I knew exactly where I was landing.
- **"Up to X% off" on 17 categories is a promise I don't fully trust.** The 70% off bar stools headline is doing work, but without a single specific product linked, this reads as a max-ceiling teaser, not a real offer. Pottery Barn's sale emails at least show me specific heroes.
- **No deadline.** This is open box inventory — it should feel urgent by nature. There's no "while supplies last" framing, no end date, nothing that makes me act now versus tomorrow.
- **Two Wayfair emails in one day.** The "AREA RUGS." send already hit me this morning. My thumb is getting trigger-happy on the unsubscribe link.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with one or two specific open box hero products, not a category menu.** Show me the $400 sectional that's now $190. Show me the walnut credenza. CB2's editorial approach — "It's back. And now it's on sale." — makes you feel like you're discovering something. This email makes me feel like I'm at a warehouse liquidation.
- 2. **Remove the mixer from the hero creative.** Replace with a furniture or lighting piece that signals "home renovation" clearly. The yellow accent chair that IS in the image is the right direction — lead with that.
- 3. **Add urgency language.** "Open box inventory clears fast" or even "XX items left" on one hero product would change my reading of this entirely.
- 4. **Deduplicate send days.** If "AREA RUGS." already went out this morning, this shouldn't send until tomorrow.
- 5. **Preheader fix.** The nav bar text ("Shop Sale | Wayfair | New Arrivals") appears to be leaking as the inbox preview. That's throwing away real estate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open box furniture: up to 70% off while it lasts`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Someone returned a great sofa. Want it?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining sets, end tables, rugs — all returned, all discounted.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Real savings on real inventory. No waiting for a sale.`
