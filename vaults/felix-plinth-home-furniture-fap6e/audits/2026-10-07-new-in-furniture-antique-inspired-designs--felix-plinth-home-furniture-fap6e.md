# New in furniture: Antique-inspired designs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the fourth or fifth send in two days. My first thought was "didn't I just see the Christmas shop?" It has. It's at the bottom of this email, and it was the hero of an earlier one. The hero is a big Rigato Bed shot with "New in Furniture" in a serif face, and it looks good. But it's a catalog page with no offer. I'm renovating two houses and I like the antique-inspired angle, with brass, wood grain and a wine-barrel curve. That fits what I'm sourcing for the second home.
- The email then buries me. There are three lookbook blocks, a "Discover more new arrivals" grid, a six-item "Recommended just for you" section, the Christmas shop, Free Design Services, the app, rewards and Affirm. It's about eight different emails stacked together. I'd save it to my Pinterest folder for the room shots and not buy from it today. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.
- Crate & Barrel has already sent me six or seven emails in 24 hours, so I'm in catalog fatigue. Of this week's Pottery Barn sends, the 10/06 "Norman Rockwell x Pottery Barn" email was better because it had one idea.

## What's working

- The Rigato Bed hero is warm and aspirational, and "New in Furniture" is readable on a phone.
- The "Antique-inspired" theme is a real point of view. It's more curated than Wayfair's blank-hero "FLOORING, HARDWARE & MORE" send.
- The "Shop the Look" tags on the room shots work like my Pinterest folder. I can see a whole room, not just a product.
- Each block names a collection (Campaign, Summerville, Vintners) with a one-line material story. "Hand-finished with a timeworn patina" is the kind of detail I care about.
- "Book a free appointment" for Free Design Services is a service I'd use. It's the same idea that made the 10/06 RSVP email and CB2's "Things of quality. Expert design." work.

## What's weak

- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline. Article's "Clearance Event: Get up to 50% off" at least told me what I'd save.
- The email has too many jobs. The Christmas shop, design services, the app, the card and Affirm all compete with the furniture story.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid is mostly curtain rods, a Halloween ghost collection and a dining table. It doesn't track with the antique furniture hero, and it doesn't feel like it knows me.
- Furniture shipping, lead times and swatch/sample info are missing. For a bed or a dining table that's a big gap.
- The rewards line ("Earn 10% back") is buried near the bottom. That's the math I care about, and it should sit higher.
- The body text is small and "Shop Now" links are tiny tap targets on a phone.
- The "Dining table" appears again in the recommendations after the lookbook. That's the same repetition I get from Crate & Barrel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Keep the antique-inspired furniture edit and move the Christmas shop, app and Affirm blocks into the footer or a separate send.
- 2. Put one concrete benefit in the hero, such as the 10% rewards back, a free-shipping threshold or a "ships by" date.
- 3. Make the Recommended grid reflect the furniture theme and my browsing, such as bed frames and tables, not curtain rods and a ghost.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample line and a delivery estimate under each collection.
- 5. Make the CTAs bigger and more specific, like "Shop the Rigato Bed" instead of "Shop Now."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Antique-inspired beds & tables, just in`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Brass, wood grain, patina: new furniture`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rigato bed, Summerville linens, Vintners tables. Free design help too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hand-finished pieces, plus 10% back in rewards on your first order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 650px fixed-width tables. It has widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, a leaked code fragment in a `<style>` block, and an empty `<title>`. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 59 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Imdi...`). Destinations are unverified, and I can't confirm any of them resolve or land correctly.
- **Plain-text bloat:** the QA check found 11,356 of 15,590 plain-text characters (73%) are URLs. This comes from the long `qs=` redirect tokens and can hurt the text-part spam score.
- **Mixed-content tracking pixels:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is a plain-HTTP open pixel and may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (a third-party ad-network pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/5a5d660d-...` are additional pixels.
  - Three open or tracking pixels is redundant.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 images are flagged, including all 16 `slices_01–16.jpg` under `1007_WED_AM_NewFurnitureCollections/` and 18 footer slices under `Email-Footer/`.
  - The product images have no alt text, including `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`.
  - Both bottom banners (`H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`) have no alt text either.
  - The sliced layout means that with images blocked, the email renders as blank space.
  - Tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif` should use `alt=""`. The logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **HTTP image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`**, and no `lang` attribute on `<html xmlns=...>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Leaked code in the CSS:** the final `<style>` block contains literal PHP text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...`. It is harmless to parsing but is a template or build error.
- **Table roles:** several layout tables lack `role="presentation"`. For example, the `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` spacer after the logo and the top-level wrapper tables don't have it.
- **Stale and cross-brand assets:**
  - `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm asset in a Pottery Barn email.
  - A 2022 banner path, `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`, is reused.
  - Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` rather than the email CDN.
- **Webfont:** the `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat `<link>` is unsupported in many clients, and I saw no declared fallback stack in the visible source.
