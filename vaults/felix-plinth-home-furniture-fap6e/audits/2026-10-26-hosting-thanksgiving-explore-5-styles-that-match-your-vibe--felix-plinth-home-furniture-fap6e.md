# Hosting Thanksgiving? Explore 5 styles that match your vibe
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Thanksgiving tablescapes are the Crate & Barrel email I actually want in my Pinterest folder. I'm hosting at one of the two houses, and the 5-styles idea (autumn botanical, modern heritage, warm & elegant, moody & textural, artisanal & casual) is a good one. It's catalog-grade styling. I'd save it.
- But the screenshot is a very long scroll, and the 30% off line sits in a thin banner above the hero. The "5 styles" headline is the hero, so the offer reads as an afterthought. After the 10/26 "3 DAYS LEFT for up to 30% off The Dining Room Event!" send, I've now seen this same event pitched twice in one day. Add the nursery, organization and Design Desk sends and that's five C&B emails in a day.
- Would I click? Probably one "Shop This Look" button, then I'd keep scrolling. I wouldn't forward it. I'd screenshot the moody & textural look for my designer.

## What's working

- Five named style palettes let me pick the one that fits my color scheme. That beats a generic dinnerware grid.
- Each look has its own "Shop This Look" button, so the CTA copy is specific and repeated.
- The photography is moody and editorial. The dark stoneware shots feel like real catalog work, not stock.
- The Thanksgiving menu/recipes block and the hosting guide go beyond selling plates. They give me a reason to keep the email.
- The "we love these for you" grid near the bottom is a quick way to scan individual pieces.

## What's weak

- The 30% off offer is a small banner above the hero. I had to look for it.
- It's a huge scroll. There are five style blocks, then dinnerware, entertaining, the menu, dining furniture, the hosting guide, the holiday decor launch, a product grid, Design Desk and an app promo. Too many asks for a single send.
- A few "Shop This Look" buttons look faded and low-contrast, especially in the warm & elegant block. On a phone those are weak tap targets.
- The Design Desk and app promos at the bottom feel bolted on. This is the same free design help pitch from the earlier kids email.
- There's no price, no shipping or delivery info for furniture, and no rewards or member benefit anywhere I can see.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Up to 30% off dining" next to the 5 styles headline, with a deadline. Right now the offer and the creative don't connect.
- 2. Cut the second half. Stop after the five looks, then one furniture CTA and one product grid. Move Design Desk and the app promo to their own sends.
- 3. Fix the button contrast so every "Shop This Look" is as visible as the first one.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample cue ("order a free napkin swatch") and delivery timing, so I know I can get it before Thanksgiving.
- 5. Space out the dining event sends. Wayfair did the same thing with its Way Day countdowns, and it wore me out. CB2's "Most sought after for a reason" worked because it was one idea, one hero and four rooms. Pottery Barn's recycled maroon "Extra 20% off" strip is the repetition I'd rather C&B not copy.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout (MI/Movable Ink templating). The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 48 images), a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and a heavy tracking footprint of 78 redirect links plus several third-party pixels. The header-level compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links skipped.** The HTTP probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel repeated 5 times.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp) all carry the same hashed `s=` identifier. Five calls to one endpoint add requests and a privacy surface for no obvious benefit.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` and `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` embed `mi_u`, the same token as the `s=` value in the LiveRamp pixels. The recipient's identifier is therefore shared with a third party via the pixel URLs.
- **Multiple open-tracking pixels.** There are three beyond the LiveRamp ones: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/.../o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7139c9c6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`, and the LiveRamp set. Image-blocking clients will not load them.
- **Scene7 assets are hotlinked.** For example, `s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/09%5FCore/2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide/…`. This is fine functionally, but it makes rendering depend on a second CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 48 images.** The QA check flagged both content and tracking images.
  - Content images are under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `56be7a63-…jpg`, `0e6a84bb-…gif`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`) and `s7d5.scene7.com/…` (`2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide_Tertiary…`, `2026_1011_CB_HolidayDecorating_Secondary…`).
  - Dynamic product images are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images).
  - Tracking pixels (`rlcdn` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry `alt=""`. Missing alt can cause some screen readers to announce the filename or URL.
  - With images blocked, any content image without alt renders as blank space, and the email's content and links are inaccessible.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which should be `&amp;`.
- **Redundant and duplicate meta tags.**
  - `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` both appear.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values.
  - `<title>` is generic ("Crate & Barrel") and does not reflect the subject.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px) { img {max-width:600px} }` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px) { img {max-width:640px} }`. The later rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The source still contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, which indicates an unfinished template.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so body-level checks (button VML fallbacks, `role="presentation"`, hidden preheader, link text) could not be done.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear. The only unreviewed material is the truncated remainder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes the headers "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". Treat this as **unconfirmed**. If the headers are really absent, the sender is out of line with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are **unknown**. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Plain-text part is 71% URLs** (16,020 of 22,532 characters). That URL density is a spam-filter signal. The plain-text alternative exists but is bloated by raw tracking URLs.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address** are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 78 links point to tracking or redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Thanksgiving / 5 styles" subject could not be confirmed. No issues are asserted.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` on every tracking pixel, and add `alt` to the dynamic Movable Ink images with fallback text.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the ampersand in `<title>` and set a meaningful title. Remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags, the dead `img` max-width rule, and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder.
4. Consolidate the five `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls into one, and review whether sharing `mi_u` or `s=` identifiers with LiveRamp is intended and disclosed.
5. Shorten or restructure the plain-text version to bring the URL ratio down.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to settle the open items:
   - Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`.
   - Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
   - Resolve the 78 tracking links and check their UTM parameters.
   - Check the body for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
