# The ELECTRIC & ALTERNATIVE FUEL FIREPLACES we can barely keep in stock
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Twelve. This is my twelfth Wayfair email since roughly Sunday and I know because I specifically noted eleven on yesterday's clearance blast — which itself was the send right after the potting bench one. So before I even looked at the body, I was already in the red. That said — the subject actually stopped me for a second. I am literally mid-spec on a fireplace for the second home's living room right now, flipping between an electric insert and a bioethanol console. If any email from any brand was going to cut through the cadence noise, a specific fireplace send was it.
- Then I opened it. What I got was a Memorial Day clearance banner, a fireplace somewhere in the hero zone, and then a full scroll of completely unrelated categories: beds, outdoor stuff, a "spotless solves" cleaning section, small-space furniture. Wayfair built the subject around fireplaces and then sent me their weekly digest with a fireplace image bolted to the front. The promise and the delivery don't match, and at this frequency I notice that disconnect immediately.
- I would not click. I would not forward. I would mark as read and go back to the tab I already have open on their site — which is where I do my actual research because the email experience has trained me that it's unreliable.

## What's working

- **Subject line was genuinely relevant** — "electric & alternative fuel fireplaces" is word-for-word the search I've been running; the specificity earned the open.
- **Memorial Day Clearance banner is visible and timely** — I can place this in context immediately; the seasonal hook is at least honest.
- **Fireplace appears in the hero area** — they didn't completely bury the lede; the product that earned the open is at least visible above the fold.
- **Render appears clean** — no broken images, no overlapping text; the template does its job technically.

## What's weak

- **The email body is a multi-category dump** — beds, outdoor furniture, cleaning, small-space storage. The subject sold me one thing and the body sold me twelve. CB2's "Find your favorite new thing" send from earlier this week had the discipline to hold a single visual idea; this doesn't.
- **No fireplace-specific offer is legible** — Memorial Day Clearance is a category-level banner, not a fireplace offer. What's the actual discount on the hero unit? I can't read it at any reasonable zoom.
- **"Barely keep in stock" is tired FOMO language** — I see this in every third furniture email. Pottery Barn used the same move on their throw pillows send. It reads manufactured, and at email twelve from the same sender in a week, I'm immune.
- **Zero personalization for a known customer** — I have a Wayfair account, I've ordered from them. Article's "the deals are in full swing" send felt more like it was talking to someone who'd been on the site; this treats me like a cold prospect.
- **Subject line is 73 characters** — not mobile-friendly; the fireplace promise is truncated on my phone before I even get to "barely keep in stock."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Send a single-category fireplace email** — dedicate the full scroll to electric and bioethanol options, show the discount clearly on the hero unit, link directly to the fireplace category page. Crate & Barrel ran "Hundreds of new items added" and still kept a clearer category hierarchy than this.
- 2. **Surface the actual % off on the fireplace hero** — "Memorial Day Clearance" means nothing without a number. Pottery Barn's "Take an EXTRA 20% OFF" is more actionable even when I don't love the category.
- 3. **Cut the subject line for mobile** — lead with the scarcity claim and let the preheader carry the category.
- 4. **Show a specific model with price, dimensions, and BTU/heat-output spec** — that's what makes me click on appliance-adjacent furniture. I need to know if it fits the wall and the room before I care about the discount.
- 5. **Throttle the cadence** — this is not a content problem at this point. No single email will fix twelve sends in seven days. The damage is accumulative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Electric fireplaces: up to X% off this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The fireplace inserts going fast for Memorial Day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Electric and bioethanol styles — see what's actually left in stock`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance pricing on console and wall-mount fireplaces, through Monday`
