# ☀️ Sunny savings UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-four. That's roughly where my Pottery Barn counter sits now, and this one drops two days after four separate Summer Sale emails — June 20 gave me "On sale & ready-to-ship!" and the rugs/70%-off blast with the blue heart emoji; June 21 gave me nightstands and the first-day-of-summer banner. Today, June 22, the subject swaps category anchors for "sunny savings," which is the most generic hook they've run yet. It's not a product. It's not a room. It's a season and a sun emoji.
- What actually saves this send from the trash: the "Recommended Just For You" section. I can see product cards — beds, some upholstered pieces, what looks like curtain rod hardware — and if those are pulling from my browsing history across the two renovation projects, that's the only personalization Pottery Barn has shown me in five emails this week. The Design Services block with the "BOOK NOW" CTA is also legitimately on-point for someone running concurrent renovations. I've been meaning to book a consult and this is a clean reminder.
- I'm not clicking through to the sale itself. The 70%-off headline has lost all signal at this point — it's ambient noise, like a "sale always on" tag on a clearance rack. Mark as read. Screenshot the Design Services section for later. Forward to no one.

## What's working

- **"Recommended Just For You" product cards** — the only moment in this email I actually stopped scrolling. Beds and interior pieces that feel adjacent to what I've been browsing. Article pulled this off with the Cillian Collection send by leading with specific products; PB buries it below the fold but at least it's there.
- **Category nav bar** — FURNITURE | OUTDOOR | BEDDING | RUGS all in one scannable row is the fastest architecture path I've seen in this category. Wayfair's large-area-rugs email made me dig for the sub-navigation; PB's single-row layout is cleaner.
- **Design Services callout** — "Free Design Services. Any project big or small." with a hard BOOK NOW button earns its spot in a sale email aimed at someone doing room-by-room work. Not expected. Appreciated.
- **Rewards visibility** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" is in the body, not just the footer. I track rewards math, and having it surfaced mid-email rather than buried is the right call.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout collapse. Seems small, but Wayfair occasionally blows this on mobile-width reflows.

## What's weak

- **Fifth "UP TO 70% OFF" email in 72 hours** — the hero and offer are word-for-word identical to the June 21 nightstands email and the June 20 rugs email. "Sunny savings" is fluffier than both of those. Crate & Barrel stamped their Summer Sale to a specific event (4th of July Warehouse Sale) with a real date; that creates a deadline. PB's running an indefinite soft banner with a rotating adjective.
- **No deadline anywhere in the email** — "Summer" is a three-month window. Article's Cillian spotlight implied urgency through a new collection drop. PB implies urgency through nothing.
- **Affirm feels list-spliced in for a different segment** — I pay on the highest-rewards card I have. BNPL is irrelevant to me and signals that this email wasn't segmented by spend behavior.
- **"Sunny savings" subject is weaker than recent PB subjects** — the June 20 rugs email at least told me what category was featured and why. This one tells me there's a sale, which I already knew from the prior four emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rotate the hero or retire the 70% banner** — on the fifth send, "UP TO 70% OFF" is not a hook. Lead instead with what's actually new: "100s of new items added" is real news that's currently buried in subtext below the headline.
- 2. **Promote "Recommended Just For You" above the fold** — that's the highest-value block in the email and it's sitting below the category nav. Swap it up; let the sale header be secondary.
- 3. **Add a deadline** — "Sale ends July 4" or "This weekend only" gives me a reason to click now rather than next time I think about rugs.
- 4. **Suppress Affirm for Key Rewards cardholders** — easy segmentation win that cleans up the email for the high-LTV segment.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `New to the Summer Sale — 100s of items just added`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Your picks are on sale (70% off through the 4th)`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Beds, lighting, rugs — fresh drops in the Summer Sale`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: free design consults for any project size`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Sunny Savings UP TO 70% OFF"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has one broken cross-brand asset (403), three HTTP image sources that will be blocked by modern clients, and PHP template code leaked verbatim into a `<style>` block. Authentication headers are absent from the relay capture, and pervasive missing alt text affects every product image.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Cross-brand broken asset**
`http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` returns 403. This is a West Elm CDN domain referenced inside a Pottery Barn email — likely a copy-paste template artifact. Will render as a broken image.

**[WARN] 25 tracking links unverified**
All CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects; HTTP probing was skipped. Destination URL health and UTM integrity cannot be confirmed from this audit pass alone.

**[WARN] Open-pixel URL is HTTP and exposes recipient address in plaintext**
`http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
— unencrypted transport for a URL containing the recipient email. Most clients will block the HTTP pixel anyway; the exposure is a secondary concern.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] PHP header code leaked into `<style>` block**
The following appears verbatim inside a `<style>` tag in `<head>`:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
This is a template/CMS artifact — PHP server-side code that was never executed. Some email clients will render it as visible text.

**[FAIL] 43 images missing `alt` text**
Every product slice image and all footer images lack `alt` attributes. Affected examples:
- `slices_01.jpg` through `slices_12.jpg` (main content)
- All 18 `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg`
- Product images: `farmhouse-platform-bed-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-l.jpg`, `hydrocotton-quick-dry-towel-l.jpg`, etc.

Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, Gmail app with data saver) will produce a blank email with no scannable content.

**[WARN] Three image sources are HTTP — will be blocked**
1. `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (also 403)
2. `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
3. `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy or block mixed-content HTTP images.

**[WARN] `maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta blocks accessibility zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents users with low vision from pinching to zoom on iOS/Android.

**[WARN] Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — no content. Screen readers announce this as the document title in some clients.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens visible in the provided source. The open-pixel confirms email-level personalization is being injected at send time (`sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`), so the merge pipeline is functioning. No issues found beyond the HTTP exposure noted above.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent from relay capture**
Required by CAN-SPAM and enforced by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules (>5k/day). May be set at the ESP level and stripped by the AgentMail relay — needs verification at the raw MTA layer, not just relay headers.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe is now mandatory for bulk senders under Gmail/Yahoo 2024 requirements. If the ESP is injecting it, confirm it survives the relay.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` absent**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; authentication chain should be verified at the MTA hop before AgentMail.

**[WARN] `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`**
Suppresses the HTTP `Referer` header on all outbound link clicks. If landing pages rely on `document.referrer` for attribution or A/B test targeting, this will break it. Likely intentional for privacy, but worth confirming it's deliberate.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 25 CTAs go through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects — UTM parameters on final destination URLs are not visible in source and were not probed. The `no-referrer` meta (noted above) means server-side Referer-based attribution on potterybarn.com will not receive the email signal. If PB's analytics rely solely on UTM params in the redirect chain this is acceptable; if any Referer-based logic exists it will see no referrer from these clicks.
