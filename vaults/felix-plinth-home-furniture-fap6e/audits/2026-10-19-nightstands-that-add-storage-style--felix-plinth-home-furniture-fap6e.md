## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, 650px email with a stray server-side code fragment in its `<style>` block, extensive missing alt text, and several insecure or PII-bearing tracking pixels. The unsubscribe and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed as sender defects. The source was truncated, so footer and body links are only partly visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 61 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`). Destinations, redirect health and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is an open-tracking pixel loaded over plain HTTP. Clients may block it, which would undercount opens.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` also use `http://`. These risk mixed-content blocking.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` is a third-party ad-network beacon. It adds an external dependency and is a privacy surface.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer in a Pottery Barn send. Its availability depends on a different brand's CDN.
- **Stale asset path:** the `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` path suggests a reused footer module from 2022.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,850 of 16,101 characters (about 74%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This is driven by the long tracking-link query strings.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **PHP code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0"); header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is leaked server-side code. It is invalid CSS and can break parsing of the rule that precedes it in strict clients.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=... >` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing alt text on content images:** the QA check flagged about 55 images. These include:
  - the hero and sliced content images `slices_01.jpg`–`slices_21.jpg` under `/W26_Furniture_B11_NightstandRoundUp/`
  - the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-...`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, etc.)
  - the footer slices (`/Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`)

  Because the email is image-sliced, with images off or a screen reader the content and links are unreadable. The logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Tracking pixels:** the alt warnings on `coreemail.html`, `d.turn.com/.../cat/1`, `svopn...` and `clickcp.../o/80ef...` are pixels, so these should have `alt=""` rather than meaningful text. This is a low-severity hygiene issue.
- **Layout tables:** the spacer tables (e.g. `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lack `role="presentation"`. Other tables have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source.
- **Email address in URLs:** the recipient address is in plain text in image and pixel query strings (`coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`). Query strings are logged by proxies and image caches, so this exposes PII. The `coreemail.html` URL is served from `www.potterybarn.com`, and the address is also sent over HTTP in the `svopn` pixel.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **not confirmed as a sender defect**. Check against raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed.
- **Sender identity:** `From: "Pottery Barn" <PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com>` is consistent with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, with no mismatch seen.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible. Every link seen is an opaque ESP redirect (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), so campaign attribution and landing-page alignment with "Nightstands that add storage & style" can't be verified from the source.
- The only direct product-site URLs are image `src` values on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. Their filenames (curtain rod bracket, dining tables, towel, platform bed, curtain) are unrelated to nightstands. That is product-module content, not a link-integrity issue.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is in the source. It only has effect in web-view contexts and may strip referrer data on click-through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content and slice image. Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels and spacers.
3. Move the `svopn` pixel and the two footer images to HTTPS.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Add `lang="en"` and a populated `<title>`.
5. Stop putting the recipient email address in image and pixel URLs. Use an opaque token instead.
6. Replace the West Elm-hosted spacer with a Pottery Barn-hosted asset, and retire the 2022 footer banner path.
7. To close the unverified items, resolve a sample of the redirect links and capture raw headers from a non-relay delivery.
