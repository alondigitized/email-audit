## What's weak

- The green "Up to 70% off" strip is the same banner I've seen since 10/06. It's the only offer in the email, and it's vague and labelled "Fall" while the body is Christmas.
- The email is long. The furniture I care about doesn't show up until roughly two-thirds of the way down, in a small grid with no prices.
- The Christmas Vacation, Elf and Peanuts collabs are novelty items and don't help a renovation. The email also gives no shipping or lead-time info, which matters for anything bulky.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML Transitional with a 650px layout and hidden preheader text. The main technical issues are about 40 images with missing alt text, three `http://` image sources (one of them a tracking pixel that puts the recipient's email address in the URL), and stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 47 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe. The sample is `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im…`, which is an encoded redirect, so destination, status and redirect chain are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network (Turn) pixel with no alt text. Confirm it is intentional.
- **Open pixels:** there are three: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/1cd61567-…?mi_cid=…`, `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`, and `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`. The `sv_` pixel is insecure (see section 3).
- **Cross-brand asset:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on West Elm's domain. It adds a dependency on another brand's host and has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 40 images.**
  - Hero and banner images: `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, `F26_Collabs_B11_HolidayXMASCollabRoundUp/slices_01–06.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg`.
  - Product images: `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `sausalito-8-drawer-dresser-66-l.jpg`, `york-slope-arm-deep-seat-slipcovered-sofa-60-108-l.jpg`, `chunky-wool-jute-rug-l.jpg`, `lit-ghost-collection-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`. If the footer text (links, legal) is baked into these images, it is unreadable with images off.
  - Tracking pixels and spacers: these should carry `alt=""`. The QA check can't tell empty alt from absent alt.
- **Mixed content (`http://`):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  These may be blocked or upgraded by some clients. The `svopn` pixel also sends the recipient's address in cleartext.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Leaked server code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS. Clients' error recovery should ignore it, but it is dead code in the template.
- **Zoom and text scaling:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom. `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable user text scaling.
- **Layout tables:** several layout tables (outer wrapper, spacer table) lack `role="presentation"`, though the main content tables have it.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** the preheader text `National Lampoons, Peanuts, Elf & more` has a bare `&`. It is minor and is tolerated by clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient email is injected into URLs (`?email=…`, `sv_sveme=…`). The HTML is truncated, so tokens further down can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified violation**. Check the raw headers before concluding.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`.
- The visible footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) is in the truncated portion and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible. Every link is wrapped in the `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirect, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be verified from this source.
- The only visible direct site URLs are product image hosts under `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`. These are image assets, not landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the hero, product and footer images. Set `alt=""` on spacers and pixels. If the footer is image-only, add real-text legal and unsubscribe links.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS. Stop sending the plain email address to `svopn.potterybarn.com`; use a hashed or opaque ID.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block, set a non-empty `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` and `text-size-adjust:none`.
4. Verify the headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) in the raw message at the source mailbox rather than the relay copy.
5. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links with a redirect-following fetch to confirm destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link and any remaining tokens.
