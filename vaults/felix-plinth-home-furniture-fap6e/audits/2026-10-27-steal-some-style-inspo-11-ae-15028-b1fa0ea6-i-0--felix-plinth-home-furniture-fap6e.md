## What's working

- The terracotta and rust palette in the hero carries through the whole email, which is the color consistency I look for when planning two houses.
- "Your space, solved." paired with the flat-lay of the bed, rug and bench shows me the room as a set of products.
- The "Real homes. Real inspiration." row with the @livingwithsid-style handles is real social proof. It reads more honest than a studio shot.
- The named bundle caption (Ashford dining table, bench and Morton chairs) tells me exactly what's in the set. That's useful for someone buying a dining room in one go.
- The footer has Help Center, Satisfaction Guarantee, and Shipping and Delivery. For furniture, I want those three visible.

## What's weak

- There's no offer anywhere. Pottery Barn's "Extra 20% off" strip is annoying, but at least I know what the deal is. Here I can't tell what I'd save.
- There are six or seven CTAs: Get Inspired, Explore Curated Rooms, See Article in the Wild, Shop Bundles, Set Up a Consultation, Explore What's New and Shop Best Sellers. Nothing stands out as the main one.
- The free design service, the strongest hook for me, sits in a dark, low-contrast banner with small text. I almost missed it.
- No prices, no lead times and no sample or swatch mention. Furniture shipping time is my first question. Pottery Barn at least leads with "Delivered Fast."
- There are no people or lifestyle cues, so nothing says it's for me. It could go to anyone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the free design consultation to the hero and make it a solid button. It's the one real offer. Pair it with a concrete line like "Free 30-min consult, swatches shipped to you."
- 2. Cut the CTAs to one main button and one secondary link. Merge "Explore Curated Rooms," "Shop Bundles" and "Explore What's New."
- 3. Put a price or savings number on the bundle: "Ashford dining set from $X." CB2's countdown email scored 8 because I knew the deadline and the discount before I scrolled.
- 4. Add shipping and lead-time language, such as "Ships in 2 weeks," next to the bundles.
- 5. Fix the preheader so it adds new information. Right now the first visible line just echoes the "curated rooms" theme.

## Technical Audit: "Steal some style inspo" (Article)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed link failures: 12 material links were probed with no errors reported. The confirmed problems are malformed dark-mode CSS and 3 images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The headers aren't visible through the AgentMail relay, and the HTML was truncated.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links found.** The QA run reported 0 issues across 12 probed links. 16 lower-priority links (footer, utility, social) were skipped, so their status is unknown.
- **Two third-party tracking domains.** The images use `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...` and `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`. The first is not on the sender domain (`email.article.com`). This is normal for an ESP, but it means the click and open paths depend on separate infrastructure.
- **The email-open pixel is probably one of the images flagged for missing alt text.** I infer this from the `/s/eo/` path pattern. It is not confirmed.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images** (QA warnings):
  - `https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/l5aSv_Wbw87w…`
  - `https://links.email.article.com/s/eo/bFxki5n6…`
  - `https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/GzQa-WHrxkvP…`
  
  If any of them is content rather than a pixel, it has no text alternative. The tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Invalid dark-mode selector.** The CSS has `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}`. The trailing comma makes the whole selector list invalid, so browsers drop the rule. Headings and paragraphs get no forced white text in `prefers-color-scheme: dark`.
- **Malformed declaration.** The CSS has `a{color:#fff;!important}`. The stray `;` before `!important` is a parse error, so the declaration is dropped. This is a typo for `color:#fff!important`.
- **Unbalanced braces.** The `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block ends `…a .link{color:#fff!important}</style>` with no closing brace for the media query. The `[data-ogsc]` block ends with an extra `}}`. Each `<style>` block is parsed separately, so browsers recover, but strict parsers may not.
- **Invalid value.** `line-height:auto` in `.dark-img` is not a valid `line-height` value and is ignored.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is low severity for email.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, viewport meta, `color-scheme` meta, and the Outlook (`mso`) fallbacks.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The footer was truncated and not checked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA states the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Nothing can be concluded.
- **Footer not inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no failure.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows only the ESP-wrapped tracking URLs, and the destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible. QA reported no failing landing pages among the 12 probed links.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the dark-mode CSS:
   - Change `h1,h2,p,{` to `h1,h2,p{`.
   - Change `a{color:#fff;!important}` to `a{color:#fff!important}`.
   - Close the `@media` block properly and remove the stray `}`.
   - Drop `line-height:auto`.
2. Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to any of the 3 flagged images that carry content.
3. Fill in the `<title>`.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from a direct send (for example, seed to Gmail and use "Show original"), not via the relay.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. That would cover the footer compliance elements, UTM tagging, and the 16 skipped links.
