# TUB FAUCETS ⭕ UP TO 70% OFF ⭕ ENDS TONIGHT ⭕ 
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Ninth, tenth — I honestly don't know anymore. Wayfair has been in my inbox every single day this week and each one promises one very specific thing in the subject line and delivers something else in the body. "TUB FAUCETS" caught me because I am — genuinely, actively — looking for a tub faucet for the second house bathroom remodel. That subject line should have been money. Instead the hero I land on is a "North of July Clearance" banner with "picked just for you" grid thumbnails so small I'd need to lean into my screen on desktop, let alone tap on my phone.
- Scroll down and the category links are: living room seating, bedding, patio essentials. Not a bathroom section in sight above the fold. Compare this to Crate & Kids' "ENDS TOMORROW! Up to 50% off beds, rugs & more" — that one actually delivered beds and rugs when you clicked in. Or Article's July 4th countdown that linked directly to the product category it promised. Wayfair keeps doing the opposite: a laser-specific subject that widens into a general clearance dump the moment you open it.
- The 24-hour flash deals section has star ratings visible, which is a genuine trust signal, and Wayfair Rewards is at the bottom — both things I notice. But I'm not clicking to "shop all sales" looking for a tub faucet. I'd mark this as read and go directly to wayfair.com/keyword/tub+faucets myself, which means Wayfair lost the attribution on their own sale. Wouldn't forward this to anyone.

## What's working

- **Concrete offer**: 70% off + ENDS TONIGHT is unambiguous — I know exactly what the deal is and when it dies.
- **"Picked just for you" framing**: I know it's probably algorithmic but I'll take the personalization signal over a flat "Shop the Sale" hero any day.
- **Flash deals with star ratings**: The 24-hr section has visible review stars on products — that's a click-stabilizer, especially for a brand where I've returned items before.
- **Wayfair Rewards visible**: I have points sitting there, and the reminder that I'm a member with benefits is the right thing to show at the bottom.
- **Subject length**: 44 characters — tight, mobile-friendly, doesn't get clipped on my lock screen.

## What's weak

- **Classic bait-and-switch**: "TUB FAUCETS" in the subject, zero prominent faucet imagery in the hero. I scrolled the whole email and the category callouts are living room, bedding, patio — the July 3rd "AIR FRYERS" send did the exact same thing.
- **No bathroom section or anchor**: Even a "Bathroom Sale" category link would have saved this. Crate & Kids managed to anchor their email to the exact categories they named in the subject; Wayfair can't seem to do this.
- **Triple ⭕ emoji pattern**: Three identical emoji in a row in the subject reads like a spam template, not a brand sending to a valued customer.
- **"Shop all sales" CTA is the laziest possible button copy**: I came in looking for one thing. Don't send me to all 14 million SKUs.
- **Cadence is at the breaking point**: This is approximately the ninth Wayfair this week. The "ENDS TONIGHT" urgency reads hollow now — it ended last night too, and the night before.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put one tub faucet hero in the email body** — not in a 6-thumbnail grid, a real hero product shot with a price and a direct link to the faucets category. That's the entire ask. Subject says faucets, email shows faucets, email wins.
- 2. **Add a "Bathroom" category link** alongside living room and bedding — the user who opens "TUB FAUCETS" is in bathroom mode.
- 3. **Swap "shop all sales" for "Shop Tub Faucets"** as the primary CTA copy, with a direct URL. Let the secondary CTA be the general sale.
- 4. **Drop two of the three ⭕ emoji** — one is punctuation, three is inbox spam filter bait.
- 5. **Vary the "ENDS TONIGHT" cadence** — consider "72-hour window" or a specific timestamp one day, a hard product count another. Pottery Barn's "extra 20% off select styles" with a LAST DAY flag felt more earned because it's not the daily default.
