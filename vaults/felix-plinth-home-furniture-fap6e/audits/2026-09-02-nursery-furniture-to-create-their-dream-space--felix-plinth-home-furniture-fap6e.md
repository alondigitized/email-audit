# Nursery furniture to create their dream space →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids strikes again — third one from this family in my inbox and same story as the Jenny Lind and "1,000 hour chair" emails: this is not for me. I'm renovating two houses for grown adults, not doing 3am feeds. "Everything you need for your nursery" over a moody, well-styled crib shot — fine, it's pretty, but I have zero use for cribs, bassinets, or dressers with "changing table ready" call-outs.
- That said, credit where due: this is a genuinely well-organized catalog email. Cribs, chairs, dressers, on-the-go gear, room accessories, all clearly sectioned with "shop" CTAs under each. If I had a kid on the way this would be a legit one-stop planning email. But I don't, so it's scroll, note the Double Rewards banner up top, and archive.
- Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward — unless I know someone actually expecting. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- "Double Rewards is on" banner at the top is the one thing that's actually relevant to me as a cardholder — rewards math I track.
- The product photography is genuinely nice — warm, styled nursery scenes, not stock-y.
- Clear category segmentation (cribs, chairs, dressers, gear, room accessories) makes this scannable even for someone skimming past it.
- "FSC-Certified," "GREENGUARD Gold Certified" badges are a real trust cue for anyone actually furnishing a nursery.

## What's weak

- Wrong audience, full stop — no kids, no nursery, no reason to open this beyond brand-recognition.
- This is the third Crate & Kids send I've gotten in what feels like one week — same cadence problem I flagged with CB2 hitting me five times off the C&B parent list.
- Bottom section stacks in even more offers (Design Desk consult, credit card push, "it's on the app") — by the time I'd get there I've long since checked out.
- The hero copy doesn't even try to bridge to a non-parent audience — no "gift for a friend" angle that could've pulled me in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list. If Crate & Barrel knows I've never clicked a Crate & Kids email, stop sending nursery furniture to my inbox — that's the single biggest lift here, more than any subject line tweak.
- 2. If they must send to the broader C&B list, lead with a "shopping for a shower gift?" angle instead of assuming everyone opening this has their own nursery to furnish.
- 3. Cut the send frequency — three sends this fast, even from a genuinely relevant sub-brand, would fatigue me; for someone I actually am a nursery match for, this would already feel like a lot.
- 4. Move the Double Rewards banner higher or make it its own send — that's the one universal hook and it's buried as a small top strip.
