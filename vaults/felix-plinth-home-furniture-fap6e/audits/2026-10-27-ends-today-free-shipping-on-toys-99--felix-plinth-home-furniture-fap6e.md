## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a Mobile Inbox (MI) tracking and styling layer. It renders defensively for Outlook and mobile. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and unverifiable header-level compliance. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and header checks are partly blind.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations.** 76 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are tracking or click-redirect domains. Final destinations, redirect health and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five LiveRamp pixels (`sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`) are embedded. Each carries the same hashed recipient identifier that appears in the first-party `mi_u=dbe8da71…` parameter. Five calls to the same endpoint look redundant. They also add third-party requests that image-blocking clients and privacy tooling may flag.
- **First-party pixels.** These are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (six instances) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/720e3f6c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. They are expected tracking, not defects.
- **Template leftover.** The source contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned before send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA, about 37 flags).** These fall into three groups:
  - **Content images:** many UUID-named images, e.g. `…/m/1/d37b3d6a-9c10-4721-aa1f-7348a9f44f3e.jpg` and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`. These likely carry the offer, product and CTA content. If any are linked or contain text, image-blocked clients and screen readers lose that content.
  - **Scene7 banners:** `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`, `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif` and `CK%5FH26%5FKidsHolidayMagic…`.
  - **Spacers and pixels:** `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px`, `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` and the tracking pixels. Decorative images should use an explicit `alt=""`. The QA output can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject or the kids brand.
- **Redundant head tags.** There are duplicate `charset` declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`). There are four separate `format-detection` meta tags, two of which conflict with the first combined one.
- **Fixed-width mobile images.** `.mobile { width:460px }` and `.showmobile { width:414px }` are fixed widths. They may overflow narrow viewports (below about 414px), but this can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Present and fine.** `lang="en"` is set, the VML and Office namespaces are declared, and Outlook conditionals are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncation means the remainder wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN ×2). The QA note says AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a proven sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so the headers should be checked on the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a sending subdomain, but alignment can't be verified here.
- **Footer not verified.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) fall in the truncated portion. They are neither confirmed nor flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 76 skipped tracking links hide the final URLs, so UTM parameters, landing-page alignment with the "Free shipping on toys $99+" offer, and any expired-promo landing state could not be checked. The subject says "ENDS TODAY", so a click-time check of the landing pages would be worthwhile.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content and CTA image. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Capture the raw RFC 822 headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat the WARNs as sender failures until then.
3. Resolve a sample of the redirect links through the tracking domain to check final URLs, UTMs and the shipping-promo landing page.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Escape the title as `&amp;` and align it with the brand and subject.
6. Dedupe the `charset` and `format-detection` meta tags.
7. Strip the `[IMPUT HERE…]` template comment.
8. Rerun the audit with the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, merge tokens and the rest of the links can be checked.
