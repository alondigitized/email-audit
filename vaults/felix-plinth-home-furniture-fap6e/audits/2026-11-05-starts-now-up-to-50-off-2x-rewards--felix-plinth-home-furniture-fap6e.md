# Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- Third Crate & Barrel Fall Sales Event send today, and the first one wearing the Kids logo. The hero is the same plaid "the fall sales event: up to 50% off" with a Shop Now button, so I know the offer in two seconds. The strip above it says "last Double Rewards this year! Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars with your Crate Card now–Nov. 17, 2026." That's the line I care about, because I buy through the highest-rewards card.
- The rest is a nursery. Strollers, baby gear, kids bedding, playroom furniture and a Nuna car seat all show up in the grid. I'm renovating two houses and I have no baby, so about 80% of this is wasted on me. The 11/05 main-brand send had the same hero and was relevant to me. This one is the same send reskinned for parents. I'd skim it and not click.
- I wouldn't forward it. I might screenshot the Crate Card strip and move on.

## What's working

- The plaid hero with "up to 50% off" is unmistakable. It's the same strong beat as the red hero in the earlier 11/05 send.
- The "last Double Rewards this year" strip has a real deadline (Nov. 17) and a real rate (20% back), so the rewards math is easy for me.
- "Up to 50% off furniture" and "up to 50% off rugs" in the first grid are the only tiles that could apply to a renovation.
- The "Black Friday deals first" signup block is a decent early-access hook.
- Section labels are readable at phone size, and the Shop Now button is a clear tap target.

## What's weak

- Almost every product is for babies or kids: strollers, a Wonderfold wagon, a Nuna car seat, a Babyletto glider. Nothing here is aimed at a 36-year-old renovating two homes.
- The Double Rewards message shows up three times (top strip, a tile, a mid-email block), and the new Crate Card pitch repeats it. It feels padded.
- The email is very long. Between the app download, TheDesignDesk, the footer category buttons and the fine print, my eye gives up before the end.
- "We love these for you" is a claim of personalization, but it's all baby gear. That feels like a mismatch, not a segment.
- The subject says "Starts NOW" while the same event started earlier today in two other sends. I've now seen three versions of one sale, which is the repeat-send annoyance I hate.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress or segment this Kids version for people with no kids signal. Send those subscribers the main-brand version.
- 2. Lead with the Rewards deadline in the subject for rewards-driven buyers. Put the rewards math in one place, not three.
- 3. Move the furniture and rug tiles above the baby gear and trim the email by a third.
- 4. Promote TheDesignDesk higher. A free design appointment is the one thing here that fits a dual renovation.

CB2's "FINAL HOURS" hero was tighter and carried real urgency, where this one buries it in a strip. Wayfair at least keeps one category per send, while this one tries to cover everything.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings, and the main code-level issue is widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 36 flagged images). The auth and unsubscribe header checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture them, not because they were confirmed absent.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **77 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains, and broken links can't be confirmed from this run.
- **Open and render pixels** (the last two are listed without "pixel" in the QA data, but their URLs show their role):
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/33d76386-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5`
- **Third-party pixel:** `sr.rlcdn.com` is a LiveRamp domain, and its `s=dbe8da71…5a8` value appears to be a recipient-level hashed identifier. The `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value starts with the same characters, but both are truncated in the QA output, so I can't confirm they match. If they do, the same ID is going to a third party five times. I can't tell from this data whether the privacy policy covers that.
- The "Image missing alt text" warning counts both content images and tracking pixels, so it overstates the number of real content images affected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 36 `<img>` tags.**
  - About 19 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`).
  - One is `s7d5.scene7.com/…/2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  - Six are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`, the same file repeated.
  - The rest are tracking pixels.
  - With images blocked, or for screen readers, any text baked into the images is lost, and the sale terms may be among it. The "up to 50% off" and "2x Rewards" offer is in the subject line, but I can't tell from the truncated HTML whether it also appears as live text.
  - Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type tag.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
- **Template residue:** the leftover comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and the empty `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` block are harmless but show an unfinished template.
- **Global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}`** overrides text-size adjustment everywhere and may hurt text scaling.
- **Unverified:** the HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the rest of the body for dark-mode handling or `role="presentation"` on layout tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. Personalization appears only as per-recipient `mi_u` parameters on Movable Ink image URLs. The truncated source means the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Inconclusive, not confirmed defects.** The QA warnings say the relay may not capture these headers:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- The sender is a branded subdomain (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`), which fits proper authentication, but there's no evidence either way here.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm the CAN-SPAM body requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 77 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so final destinations, UTM parameters, and whether the landing pages show the "Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards" offer are unknown. A follow-up run that resolves the redirects is needed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 19 content images, including the Movable Ink `f3c0d22992a01946.png` placements and the Scene7 GIF. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` tags, and strip the leftover template comments.
4. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If they are missing, add them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
5. Follow the redirect chains on a sample of the 77 tracked links to check UTM consistency and offer alignment on the landing pages.
6. Make sure the third-party LiveRamp pixels (`sr.rlcdn.com`, five of them) are disclosed in the privacy policy.
