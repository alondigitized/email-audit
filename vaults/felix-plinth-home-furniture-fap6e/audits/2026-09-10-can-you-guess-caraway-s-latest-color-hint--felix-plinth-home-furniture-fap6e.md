# Can you guess Caraway’s latest color? (Hint: 🌊🧘🌴)
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is basically a rerun of the "Trying to get back to your cooking routine" email I audited earlier today — same blender hero, same everyday-kitchen framing — except this time the subject line teases a Caraway color reveal ("Hint: 🌊🧘🌴") and then buries it about two screens down under a Vitamix blender pitch. That's a bait-and-switch on the subject line and it annoys me. If I actually clicked because I wanted to see the new Caraway color, I'd be scrolling past a completely unrelated appliance push to get there.
- The stuff that IS relevant to me — Caraway cookware, the new sage/spa-toned color, the "just landed" GreenPan pieces — is genuinely in my kitchen-renovation wheelhouse, more so than the fourth Crate & Kids blast. But the email just keeps going and going: blender, cookware, more cookware, another appliance, a countertop oven, an ice cream maker, "even more new arrivals," a clean-kitchen section, more new arrivals again, then an app download pitch. It's an everything-bagel email dressed up with a curiosity-gap subject line.
- Verdict: I'd open it because the subject actually got me curious (unlike the nursery spam), but I wouldn't click past the first fold, and I definitely wouldn't forward it — there's no single hero offer worth sharing with a friend.

## What's working

- The subject line's color-guessing hook is legitimately more creative than anything CB2 or Pottery Barn has sent me this week — a little playful curiosity instead of another "ENDS TONIGHT" banner.
- The Caraway section itself, once I got to it, has a clean single-product focus: "only at Crate: a calming new color from Caraway" with a clear "Shop Now" button.
- The GreenPan "cleaner cooking originals" block calls out nonstick/non-toxic materials, which is exactly the kind of ingredient/material detail I actually care about when buying cookware for a remodel.
- "Even more new arrivals that ship free" — free shipping called out plainly, no fine print games.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a Caraway color reveal, but the email opens with a Vitamix blender ("make your meal prep easier") — that's the CB2-style trick of teasing one thing and leading with another, and CB2's actual fall sale email this week didn't need a gimmick like that.
- The email is just too long — blender, cookware, more cookware, toaster oven, ice cream maker, "even more arrivals," clean kitchen, "we love these for you," then an app pitch. Article's patio email this week was tight and single-themed; this one has zero editorial discipline.
- No visible discount or rewards math anywhere — no %/$ off, no My Rewards tie-in, nothing that reflects that I'm a highest-rewards-card shopper who wants to see the math before I click.
- The Caraway "hint" color itself is never named — I still don't know if it's sage, sand, whatever. If you're going to tease a color, actually show me the name/swatch, not just a sage-toned product photo I have to guess at.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Caraway color reveal since that's what the subject promised — put that hero block first, name the actual color, and cut straight to "Shop Now." Right now the payoff is buried under an unrelated blender pitch.
- 2. Cut this email in half. Pick either "new Caraway color" or "kitchen refresh roundup" — not both plus an oven, an ice cream maker, and an app download pitch.
- 3. Add an actual number somewhere — a %-off, a rewards multiplier, anything. Right now there's no reason to act today versus next week.
- 4. Drop the app-download CTA at the bottom entirely from this send — it dilutes the one thing (Caraway color) that made the subject line worth opening.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Caraway's new color is finally here 🌊`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New from Caraway: the calming color you've been waiting for`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A spa-inspired sage, exclusively at Crate & Barrel.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cookware that matches your kitchen reset — shop the new shade first.`
