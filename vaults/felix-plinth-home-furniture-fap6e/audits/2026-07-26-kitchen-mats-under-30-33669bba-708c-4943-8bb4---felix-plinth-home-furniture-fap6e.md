# KITCHEN MATS under $30 ✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Twelve emails deep into Wayfair's July inbox takeover and this is the one where I stopped scrolling to actually count how many product tiles are stacked in a single send: benches, birdbaths, bathroom storage, bread pans, ping-pong tables, landscape lighting, bedding, serving bowls, door mats, table lamps, window ACs, drying racks, an 8ft pool table. That is fourteen-plus unrelated categories under a subject line that promised me exactly one thing — kitchen mats under $30 — and delivered it as a single 200px thumbnail in the second row, indistinguishable from wall art clearance next to it.
- The "80% OFF — BLACK FRIDAY IN JULY — ENDS TOMORROW" banner is the same banner I screenshotted from Sixteen and Seventeen this same week. At this point "ends tomorrow" has ended tomorrow about five separate tomorrows in a row, and I don't believe it anymore — that's not urgency, that's wallpaper.
- I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding. Article's dining-room-to-living-room editorial rotation earned a bookmark from me four sends ago because it respected that I'm mid-decision on furniture. This is the opposite move — a shotgun blast disguised as a kitchen mat email. Archive.

## What's working

- The kitchen mats tile itself is genuinely cute — the yellow botanical print would've caught my eye on its own in a dedicated email.
- "get 5% rewards on every item, always" is a real, ongoing loyalty mechanic and I do have a Wayfair card, so that section at least speaks my language.
- The purple "top picks. low prices." and "even more to ❤" section dividers give the eye brief landing spots in an otherwise endless scroll.
- No render bugs — images loaded clean, nothing overlapping, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- Subject says kitchen mats, hero banner says Black Friday/Bedsure, first section says "must-shop steals" — three different messages before I've scrolled once.
- Kitchen mats appear exactly once, at 200px, sandwiched between wall art and area rugs — not remotely the star of the email its subject line promised.
- Fourteen-plus categories crammed into one send with zero attempt to group by room or project — benches next to birdbaths next to bread pans is just noise for a guy trying to finish two renovations.
- "80% OFF" up top and "65% off" bedding sale lower down with no percentages tied to the kitchen mats I actually opened this for — the one offer I care about isn't quantified anywhere.
- Same "ends tomorrow" urgency claim I've now seen on back-to-back sends this week — it's lost all credibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject line true — if it says kitchen mats, open with a kitchen mats hero block with an actual price point ($19.99, $24.99, whatever), not a Black Friday banner for a mattress brand.
- 2. Cut this to one or two adjacent categories (kitchen + bath, or kitchen + dining) instead of a whole-store catalog dump — Pottery Barn's "on sale & ready to ship" at least stays in one lane even when it's generic.
- 3. Retire "ends tomorrow" for a week until it can mean something again, or swap to a real countdown timer.
- 4. Move the 5% rewards module up near the top — it's the most honest, most differentiated content in the email and it's buried below two full rows of unrelated tiles.
- 5. Give kitchen mats a bigger hero treatment with a second angle/room shot since that's the one category with actual demand from me right now.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New kitchen mats, $30 and under`
