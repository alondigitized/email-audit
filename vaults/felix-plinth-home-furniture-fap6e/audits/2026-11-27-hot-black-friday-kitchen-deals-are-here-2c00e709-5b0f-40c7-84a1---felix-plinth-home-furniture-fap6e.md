# 🔥 Hot Black Friday Kitchen deals are HERE!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the Crate & Barrel send that finally speaks to my actual project. I'm renovating two kitchens' worth of house, and this is espresso machines, stand mixers, Dutch ovens and cookware, with the Breville hero right up top. It's still the seventh C&B family email this week, so my brain started to file it under "didn't I just get this?" The red "Biggest Sale of the Year!" ribbon is the same one from "Our biggest sale of the year is HERE!" earlier today.
- The kitchen section is genuinely useful. Each product block has a dollar-off figure ($200 off Breville, $170 off Le Creuset, over $600 off All-Clad), so I can do the rewards-card math in my head. The back half turns into a generic "up to 50% off Black Friday" collage of rugs, furniture and decor, which is the Pottery Barn "everything is on sale" move. I'd click, but only on the kitchen blocks. I'd forward the Le Creuset deal to my contractor's wife, who is also doing a kitchen, and nothing else.

## What's working

- The hero is on-topic: "kitchen deals" with a big Breville espresso machine. It matches the subject, which is more than I could say for the "dessert" subject on Pottery Barn's 70% send.
- Dollar-off callouts ($200, $170, $300 off) are easier to compare than a vague "up to 40%."
- Each brand gets its own block with its own Shop Now button (Breville, KitchenAid, Le Creuset, All-Clad, Fellow). That's one product, one click, and I like it.
- "Lowest price all year" on the All-Clad set is the only line that made me want to check my cart. It reads as a credible claim.
- The big white logo header and generous spacing are easy to scan on a phone.

## What's weak

- The top banner mixes two offers. "Today Only!" applies to wreaths and garlands, and "up to 50% off" is the sitewide line. The kitchen hero says "up to 40% off premium brands," so I get three different numbers before I've scrolled.
- It's the seventh near-identical send this week. CB2's pillows-and-throws email was cleaner and more specific, and Article's repeated black "SALE" banners are at least consistent.
- The back half drifts into rugs, furniture, decor and a gift guide. That's Wayfair-style scatter, and it dilutes the kitchen focus I opened for.
- The long scroll ends in a $5,000 TheDesignDesk sweepstakes and a "we love these for you" block that feels like filler. Most of the tiny text is hard to read without zooming.
- Almost every CTA is a plain "Shop Now," so I can't tell where a button goes without reading the headline above it.
- There's no shipping clarity for heavy appliances and no rewards or card-benefit angle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the kitchen story and drop the generic Black Friday collage. A focused send would beat Wayfair's loud 80%-off blast.
- 2. Put one offer and one deadline in the top banner. Don't mix "Today Only" wreaths with kitchen deals.
- 3. Change the CTA copy to something like "Shop Breville deals" or "Get the Le Creuset Dutch oven."
- 4. Add a free-shipping and delivery-timing line for heavy items like stand mixers and Dutch ovens.
- 5. Add reviews or a bestseller tag to the product blocks.
- 6. Cap the cadence. Seven sends in a week trains me to snooze this brand.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$200 off Breville + 30% off KitchenAid today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kitchen deals: Le Creuset, All-Clad, Breville`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lowest price all year on the All-Clad 10-piece set. Free shipping.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Espresso machines, stand mixers and Dutch ovens, all marked down.`

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel "Hot Black Friday Kitchen deals are HERE!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML fallbacks and a `lang="en"` attribute. The main confirmed defects are about 63 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute and viewport and text-size settings that restrict user scaling. The header-based compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay did not capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 111 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels are third-party (LiveRamp) and fire in one send.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8c9fd3d6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are first-party open and view pixels.
  - The pixels carry per-recipient tokens (`mi_u=56aaa2787a67…`, `s=56aaa2787a67…`), so the same identifier is sent to a third-party domain (rlcdn.com).
- **Broken links:** No confirmed broken links, because none could be probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, about 63 images):**
  - 49 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (e.g. `7c847c23-48cd-41fa-b19f-95972268fa99.jpg`, `581a68f8-…jpg`) and `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - 7 images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - 5 `rlcdn` pixels and 2 first-party pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8c9fd3d6-…`).
  - The 56 content and `rp` images are real defects, since screen readers announce the filename or nothing and images-off clients show a blank block. The 7 tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and are lower priority.
- **Pinch zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` disables user text resizing in WebKit and Edge.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. Clients tolerate it.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `<meta charset>` appears alongside `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, and `format-detection` is declared four times. This is harmless redundancy.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, `color-scheme` meta tags, and MSO `PixelsPerInch` and line-height fixes are present.
- **Limit:** the HTML was truncated, so the body markup (headings, button `role`s, link text) could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied portion of the source. The truncated HTML means the body was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found (WARN), with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 111 links go through tracking or redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified. No UTM parameters are visible in the supplied source. The 57% QA pass rate comes largely from the skipped probes and the alt-text warnings.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** add meaningful `alt` text to the 49 `image.mail…/m/1/…` and 7 `mi…/p/rp/…` images, and set `alt=""` on the 7 tracking pixels.
2. **Zoom and text size:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop the blanket `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule.
3. **Title:** change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
4. **Headers:** inspect the original message headers (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
5. **Link resolution:** follow the redirect chains for a sample of the 111 tracked links, and confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM presence.
6. **Third-party pixels:** reduce the five repeated `rlcdn.com` pixels to one if possible, and review the privacy-policy disclosure for the per-recipient tokens sent to that domain.
