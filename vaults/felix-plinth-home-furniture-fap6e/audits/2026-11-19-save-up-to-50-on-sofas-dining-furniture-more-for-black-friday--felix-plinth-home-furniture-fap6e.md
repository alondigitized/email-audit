# Save up to 50% on sofas, dining furniture & more for Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the Crate & Barrel email I've been waiting for. After the Babyletto nursery send and the two toy sends today (the 8th-plus Kids email since 11/17), this one is the main "SALE" sender with sofas and dining tables. It's the first C&B email this week that fits two houses mid-renovation. The red "Black Friday is on! Up to 50% off + free shipping" banner is the first thing I see, and the offer is clear straight away.
- The email is very long. It runs from the furniture hero through a category grid, a bestseller sofa, kitchen deals, a "up to 60% off" grid, clearance, a $5,000 sweepstakes and a "we love these for you" block. That's a lot of scrolling on a phone, and the small text is hard to read at inbox size. I'd click once, on the furniture CTA, and save the rest to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I'd only forward the sofa section, and only to my partner.

## What's working

- The banner puts "up to 50% off + free shipping" with a Shop Now button at the very top, before any scrolling.
- The hero is a dark green mantel with garland. It feels like a real room, not a catalog cutout, and it fits my renovation mindset.
- The category tiles (dining, bedroom, sofas & sectionals, storage, accent tables, home office) let me jump straight to what I'm shopping for.
- The single "save on this bestseller" sofa block gives me a specific product to look at.
- The Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes is a nice touch for a design-minded buyer.

## What's weak

- There are too many modules and too many Shop buttons. The page tries to be furniture, kitchen, decor and clearance all at once, so the offer fragments into 20%, 30%, 35%, 40%, 50% and 60% off.
- The kitchen and cookware blocks (Le Creuset, Always Pan-style cookware, espresso makers) feel off-target for my furniture-focused list.
- The small text on prices and "ends" notes is unreadable on a phone without zooming.
- It's heavily discount-driven. There's no swatch or sample cue, no shipping-timeline clarity for big furniture, and no rewards or showroom angle.
- Under the offer, "up to" language with exclusions makes me assume the best sofas aren't 50% off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the furniture story: banner, hero, category grid, one bestseller, then a single footer CTA. Move kitchen and clearance to a separate send.
- 2. Add furniture-specific trust cues: delivery windows, a free swatch offer, and a "see it in a showroom" link.
- 3. Name the real deadline, such as "ends Sunday", in the hero instead of burying it in the small top line.
- 4. Make price text and tap targets bigger for mobile.
- 5. Show the real sale price on one or two hero pieces, not just percentages.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday furniture list, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on furniture + free swatches. Order by tonight for holiday delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best sofas, tables and storage, now with Black Friday pricing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has solid fundamentals (`lang="en"`, `<title>`, MSO/VML conditionals, 460px mobile breakpoint). It has widespread missing `alt` attributes and a viewport/text-size setup that blocks user zoom. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the headers weren't captured through the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity unverified.** 102 tracking links were skipped for HTTP probing because they sit on click-redirect domains. QA gives no evidence of broken links, and none of the 102 destinations were resolved.
- **Third-party pixel fired 5 times.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` appear five times, with only `n` changing. This is a LiveRamp (rlcdn) tracker. The `s=` value is a 64-hex-character string that shares its leading characters with the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images. A recipient-specific identifier is therefore being passed to a third party. I can't confirm from the source whether it is a hashed email.
- **Pixel count.** Besides the five rlcdn pixels, there is a first-party open pixel (`https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`) and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3f343803-…` beacon. All of these are `<img>` tags without `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 55 content images.** Examples are `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/f62f4e33-3599-46c5-9b3d-0d263bbfcb5a.jpg`, `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, and seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. With images blocked or a screen reader in use, the sale content and CTAs are unreadable. The alt-less images include `.gif` files (`7914a9b8-….gif`, `07b7830d-….gif`).
- **Alt text missing on tracking pixels.** The 5 rlcdn pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon lack even `alt=""`. This is low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct fix for decorative or tracking images.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` also disable iOS text resizing.
- **Template leftovers.** The source still contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->`.
- **Markup nits.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens, such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The rest of the body wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing them. That is unconfirmed, so this is not a confirmed sender defect. It matters in practice because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` aligns with the image and tracking subdomains, but alignment can't be confirmed without the headers.
- **In-body footer is unverified.** The truncated source doesn't reach the unsubscribe link or the physical postal address, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. QA did not resolve the redirect destinations, and the visible source contains no UTM parameters or landing-page data. The `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters on the `dv.` beacon suggest that Movable Ink handles tracking and personalization. They don't tell us where the CTAs land.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all product and promo images. Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels (rlcdn, `o.gif`, `dv.` beacon).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `html` and `*`, and keep `100%`.
3. Re-run the capture against the raw message headers. This confirms `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), instead of relying on the relay's view.
4. Resolve a sample of the 102 tracking redirects, checking the final URL, status code and UTM parameters. This covers Sections 2 and 6.
5. Collapse the five identical rlcdn pixel calls to one, if the template allows it. Confirm what the `s=` parameter contains, and whether it is disclosed in the sender's privacy policy.
6. Clean up the template: escape the `&` in `<title>`, dedupe the `format-detection` metas, and remove the placeholder comment.
