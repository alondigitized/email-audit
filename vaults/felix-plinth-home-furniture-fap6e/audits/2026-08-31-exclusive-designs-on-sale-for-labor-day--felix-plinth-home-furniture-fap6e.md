- This is at minimum my sixteenth CB2 Labor Day-adjacent email this month — the "UP TO 50% OFF BEST SELLERS" headline is nearly identical to the "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send and the lighting one from yesterday. I'm numb to it.

- The App/Rewards/Text-club footer block eats a ton of scroll real estate for people who are already subscribed to the email — I don't need four more sign-up asks below the fold.
- Rugs tile photo is dim and cluttered enough that I can't tell what pattern it actually is without clicking — worse than Wayfair's pergola tile which at least made the product obvious at a glance.
