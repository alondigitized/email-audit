# NEW Venus Williams kids collab 💜 Only at Crate & Kids!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Venus Williams, round six, and now she's crossed over into the *kids* line. I've flagged this exact "Venus for Crate & Barrel" beat four times this week already — the yellow dress, the tennis-adjacent color story, the same design-partner lockup — and now Crate & Kids is running the junior version of the identical campaign. My gut reaction is "okay, I see the org chart now," not "ooh, new."
- To be fair, this one's actually a real catalog send unlike the app-gate nonsense from earlier in the week — room-by-room kid spaces, bedroom, playroom, study nook, with product shots I can actually evaluate. But I don't have a kid in the target age range for this line, and nothing here is furniture I'm buying for either of my two renovations. This is a hard scroll-past.
- I'm marking as read, not clicking, and definitely not forwarding — the only person I'd forward Venus-for-Crate&Kids to is someone who's already gotten four Venus emails from me complaining about fatigue, so that joke's dead.

## What's working

- The room-by-room structure (bedroom → playroom → study space → "championship-worthy decor") is a legible catalog format, same trick that redeemed the 9/14 "clean home dream" storage send.
- Product photography is clean and the color palette (deep green, navy, cream) is consistent across every room, which at least reads as a coherent collection rather than a grab-bag.
- The closing "we love these for you" strip and Design Desk card-offer block at the bottom are on-brand cross-sell, not random noise.

## What's weak

- Venus Williams as hero, again — this is the fifth-plus time this exact partnership has anchored a Crate & Barrel family email in my inbox this week, and now it's stretched into the kids sub-brand too. The well is dry.
- I don't have a kid in the crib-to-tween range this line targets, so the entire email — bedroom sets, playroom furniture, study desks — is a zero-relevance send for a guy renovating two adult homes.
- No pricing or offer anywhere in the rendered image. Not one %, not a "starting at," not a member-price callout — just "Shop the New Collab" repeated eight times with no reason to click now versus next week.
- The card-promo block at the bottom ("the card fee you love just got better") is generic financial cross-sell bolted onto a kids-furniture email — feels like filler, not personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the repetition fatigue** — if Crate & Barrel and Crate & Kids share a subscriber list (which clearly overlaps with mine), stagger the Venus creative across the family instead of running it in five sends in one week. CB2 already made this mistake this week with six Crate-family emails hitting one inbox.
- 2. **Put a real offer in the hero** — Wayfair's "48-HR 70% OFF" banner is obnoxious but at least it's a number; this email has zero pricing anywhere, which kills urgency entirely.
- 3. **Segment by household composition** — if I've never bought from Crate & Kids, don't lead every subject line with "kids collab"; lead with the design story and let me self-select in.
- 4. **Add a "new parent / new grandparent" gifting angle** — the entire send reads as if every subscriber has a toddler; a gifting frame would at least give childless-in-my-age-range subscribers a reason to open.
