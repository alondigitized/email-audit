# The bath towels everyone loves
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Our Most-Loved Towels" — this is exactly the kind of email I actually want from Pottery Barn right now, since I'm mid-renovation on two houses and towels are a real, boring, necessary line item I haven't checked off yet. The hero shot is clean — stacked white/cream towels, free shipping banner, starting price right there — and it doesn't waste my time before getting to the point.
- But then it just... keeps going. Cloud, Dream, Hydrocotton, Addison Striped — four near-identical towel lines stacked vertically, then bath vanities, then hardware, then bath mats, then a "Recommended Just For You" grid that's clearly generic (a Halloween ghost bed collection and a dining table have nothing to do with towels), then a full Halloween shop, then a Design Crew pitch, then the app, then Affirm financing. By the time I hit "Follow us" I've forgotten this was a towel email.
- I'd click the "Shop All Bath Towels" button near the top and bounce — I'm not scrolling through six more modules to compare four towel lines' worth of swatches on my phone. Wouldn't forward it, wouldn't need to; my wife already has this same towel obsession in her own inbox.

## What's working

- The $24.50 starting price and "TOWELS SHIP FREE" badge are both visible above the fold — that's a real, honest offer signal.
- Product photography is genuinely good — the folded stacks read as soft and give me actual texture/color info, which matters for towels specifically.
- Four material/style call-outs (cotton terry, silk-cotton blend, "ultra-absorbent in 15+ hues," textured stripe) at least differentiate the SKUs instead of just saying "towels are great."
- Monogram "W" on one towel stack is a nice subtle touch — reads as a personalization option without shouting about it.

## What's weak

- The email is way too long for its subject — "bath towels" turns into vanities, hardware, mats, then a totally unrelated furniture/Halloween/Design-Crew/app/Affirm dump. Crate & Barrel's clearance emails have the same "just keep scrolling" problem, but at least those are consistently about deals, not four different departments.
- "Recommended Just For You" shows a platform bed, a wool rug, and a dining table — nothing here reflects that I'm looking at towels. It's not personalization, it's just the generic homepage grid with a personalized label slapped on it.
- No rewards/loyalty math tied to this purchase — the 10% back in rewards mention is buried all the way at the bottom in tiny app-promo text, not anywhere near the actual towel offer.
- Zero swatch/color-count clarity up top — I only find out Hydrocotton comes in "15+ hues" three modules in; that's the single most useful buying detail for towels and it's buried.
- Halloween shop and holiday decor showing up in a towel email is the same brand-cadence issue I flagged in "Nostalgic holiday collabs are back!" (5/10) — PB can't resist cross-selling seasonal even when the subject line promised something specific.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this email in half — kill the vanities/hardware/mats/Halloween/Design-Crew stack, or move it to a second send. A towel email should end after towels.
- 2. Pull swatch/color-count info (like Hydrocotton's 15+ hues) up into the hero, not three scrolls down — that's the actual decision-driver for towel buyers.
- 3. Make "Recommended Just For You" actually be towel-adjacent (bath rugs, robes, monogramming) instead of a random bed and dining table — right now it reads as a template bug, not personalization.
- 4. Surface the 10% rewards-card math near the CTA, not buried in the footer — that's a real incentive for a guy who buys on the highest-rewards card.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Hydrocotton towels in 15+ colors`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on our best-selling bath towels, from $24.50`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cloud, Dream, Hydrocotton — which one's your bathroom?`
