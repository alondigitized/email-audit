# ON NOW: Up to 60% off decor, bedding & more!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Finally, an actual Crate & Barrel email — not the Kids spinoff. No baby cribs, no Jellycat lion, no nursery furniture. This is the eighth email from the Crate & Barrel family in my inbox today, but it's the first one that's actually for my living room, not a toddler's. The category grid up top — lighting, bedding, rugs, wall decor, home accents, curtains — is exactly the stuff I'm actually shopping for while juggling two renovations.
- That said, it's a monster of a scroll. Hero, category grid, a second "Double Rewards + up to 60% off" banner, another product grid, a Design Desk sweepstakes pitch, a "Double Rewards is on!" repeat, "we love these for you" curated picks, an app download push, then a footer with CB2 and Crate&Kids cross-sell. It's the full kitchen-sink treatment I've been logging from Wayfair and Pottery Barn all week, just with better photography.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — "up to 60% off decor, bedding & more" and the rugs/wall-mirror tiles are things I need right now for the second house. Would I forward it to a friend? No — nothing here is surprising enough to share, it's just a solid, on-brand sale email.

## What's working

- The hero banner leads with "up to 60% off decor, bedding & more" over a styled living room shot that actually looks like a room I'd want — moody, layered, adult.
- The category icon grid (lighting, bedding, rugs, mirrors, home accents, curtains) lets me jump straight to what I care about instead of scrolling blind.
- "Double Rewards is on!" is called out twice with the specific mechanic (Earn 15% back in Rewards when you use a Crate&Barrel Card) — real loyalty math, not vague "member perks."
- Free shipping threshold is spelled out ("$149.95 & ship free") — that's the kind of concrete shipping clarity that furniture emails almost always bury or skip.
- The Design Desk section with a $5000 sweepstakes and "book your appointment" is a genuinely relevant showroom/design-service touchpoint for someone mid-renovation like me.

## What's weak

- This is stupidly long — by my scroll count there are at least six distinct sale/reward callouts stacked on top of each other before you even hit the footer. CB2's "LONG WKND SALE" send (7/10 in my history) made its point in half the real estate.
- "Double Rewards is on!" appears twice, nearly identically, a few screens apart — redundant within the same email, on top of being the fourth "up to X% off" message from this brand family today.
- The app-download push and the CB2/Crate&Kids cross-sell footer feel like tacked-on filler after I've already scrolled through two full product grids.
- No swatch/sample-ordering callout anywhere, which is the one thing that would've actually moved me on rugs and upholstery — Article's emails at least occasionally mention swatches, this one doesn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one clear offer stack: hero + category grid + one Rewards callout + one CTA. Kill the duplicate "Double Rewards is on!" banner — say it once, say it well.
- 2. Move the Design Desk / showroom appointment section higher, right after the hero — that's the most differentiated, renovation-relevant content in the whole email and it's buried two-thirds down.
- 3. Add a swatch/sample-order CTA near the rugs and upholstery tiles — I'm not buying a rug sight-unseen for a house I haven't finished renovating.
- 4. Trim the footer cross-sell (CB2, Crate&Kids) — given how many Crate&Kids emails I've already gotten today, seeing that logo again at the bottom just reinforces the fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off decor & bedding — plus Double Rewards`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rugs, lighting & mirrors: up to 60% off, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the Labor Day Event — 15% back with your Crate&Barrel Card`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping over $149.95 on decor & bedding`
