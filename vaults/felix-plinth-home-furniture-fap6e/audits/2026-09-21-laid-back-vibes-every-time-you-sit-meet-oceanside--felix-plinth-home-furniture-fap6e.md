- Starting price ($1,899) is right there under the headline — no hide-the-ball pricing games like Wayfair's rocking chair fiasco where I scrolled forever and found nothing.

- "Assembled by hand in the USA" is a trust cue that actually matters to me for a sofa I'm keeping for a decade.
- The "shop more dream sofas" bestseller grid (Lounge, Gather, Monterey, Axis) gives me alternatives without forcing a second email.

## What's weak

- This is at minimum the third sofa-centric email I've gotten from the Crate & Barrel family this week (plus CB2's "Steals of the Week" and "Now & Future Classics" sales) — the cadence is bleeding together and I'm losing track of which sofa was in which email.

- The Venus Williams collab banner feels randomly inserted between sofa content and design-help content — no connective tissue to why I'd care mid-scroll.
- "Available in 2 depths and 2 heights" is good info but it's buried as small text under the fabric grid instead of being a clear spec callout.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split this into two emails.** The Oceanside launch + swatches is strong enough to stand alone. Bundling Venus Williams, TheDesignDesk, and the Crate Card into the same send dilutes the actual news (new sofa, new fabric).
- 2. **Pull the starting price and dimensions into a single spec strip** right under the hero — depth, height, price, fabric count — so I don't have to reconstruct it by scrolling.
- 3. **Cut the sibling-brand fatigue** — coordinate send calendars with CB2 and Crate & Kids so I'm not getting three furniture pitches from the same corporate family in one week.
- 4. **Move "try a swatch first" higher**, maybe right under the hero CTA — for someone furnishing two homes, the free-swatch offer is more decision-critical than the bestseller grid.
