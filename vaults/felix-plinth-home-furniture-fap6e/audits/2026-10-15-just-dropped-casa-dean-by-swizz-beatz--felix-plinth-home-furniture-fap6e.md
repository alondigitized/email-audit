## What's weak

- No prices, product names or dimensions anywhere in the collection. It's about 4,000 pixels of story with nothing to click through to a specific piece.
- The two "SHOP THE NEW COLLECTION" links are small, thin text. On my phone that's a tight tap target for the only real CTA.
- The body copy under the hero is too long and too light-grey for a phone, and I'd have to zoom to read it.
- There are no shipping or lead-time cues for furniture and no swatch or sample mention. Pottery Barn's recent dinnerware send at least anchored the visuals to named items.
- Four sections (collab, sale, lighting, app and rewards) are stacked in one email, so I don't know the single ask. The Crate & Barrel sister brands in the footer make it feel like more of the same family spam.

- 1. Tag three to five hero pieces with name, price and a "Shop the chair" link. Article's "Fall is for full tables" at least sold a styled scene with items attached.
- 2. Make the CTA a full-width button that says something like "Shop the Casa Dean lounge chair."
- 3. Cut the lighting and warehouse blocks, or move them to a separate send. Fewer than seventeen emails a week would also help.
- 4. Add ship dates and a sample or swatch link for upholstery.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`) leaked into the output. It also has 12 content images without alt text, 10 insecure HTTP tracking pixels, and no unsubscribe or authentication headers visible to the QA tooling. The QA pass rate was 43%.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 66 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, so this is not a confirmed defect.
- 10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` through `pub10.php`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`

  Clients may block or rewrite non-HTTPS images. Blocked pixels mean lost tracking, and mixed-content warnings are possible in web clients.
- No issues found with the first-party pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/8f64aeea-...`. Both use HTTPS, but both lack alt text.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 12 content images served from `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt` attribute. Examples are `bb60d6c1-e2df-4d86-b024-7bd3dc32ecad.png`, `c42bc1b4-...jpg`, `77838c2b-...gif` and `ad709095-...jpg`. With images blocked, or with a screen reader, the content is lost.
- Tracking and cookie-sync images (`pub1`–`pub10.php`, `UCMController`, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) also lack `alt`. Use `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"` or `display:none` so screen readers skip them.
- The visible head markup has a leftover template placeholder: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is cosmetic, but it shows the template was not cleaned.
- `@font-face` rules import fonts for `crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`) alongside the CB2 fonts. This is likely a shared-template artifact. The email does not rely on them, because the declared stacks fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif.
- The source was truncated, so I could not check the body structure, such as role attributes, a preheader, or button markup.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears in the delivered HTML. It looks like a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style personalization string that did not resolve. Depending on its location, it may render as visible text or break an attribute or URL.
- No other unresolved tokens were flagged.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool itself notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a limit of the relay, not evidence of failure. The sender domain `mail.cb2.com` is aligned with the image host `image.mail.cb2.com`, which is a good sign.
- The visible unsubscribe link and the physical address footer are outside the truncated source, so I cannot confirm them.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- I can't assess this. All 66 tracked links were behind redirects and were not resolved. The final destinations, UTM parameters, and landing-page alignment with "Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz" are unverified.
- Resolving a sample of the redirects (hero CTA, product links) would close this gap.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%` (high).** Add a fallback or suppress the block when the data is missing. Add a pre-send check that fails on `%%...%%` patterns.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS (medium).** Use `https://` for `ads.dotomi.com` and `login.dotomi.com`. If they don't support it, ask the vendor or remove them.
3. **Add `alt` text to the 12 content images (medium).** Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
4. **Check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message (medium).** Test from the original send, not through the relay.
5. **Resolve a sample of the 66 tracked links (low).** Confirm that they return 200 and that UTM parameters survive to the landing page.
