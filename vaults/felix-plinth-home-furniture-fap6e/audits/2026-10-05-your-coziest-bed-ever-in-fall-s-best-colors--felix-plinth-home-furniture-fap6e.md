# Your coziest bed ever in fall’s best colors
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifteenth Crate-family email in about three days, and the sixth from the main sender today. I'd already mentally filed it as "Crate, again" before it finished loading. It's a long, polished scroll and I can't fault the product photography. But I got the bedroom-refresh send ("The 1 thing that totally refreshes your bedroom") a few hours ago, and this is the same bedding story with new colorways. The first thing I see is a banner for up to 20% off sofas and sectionals. Then the hero pivots to bedding, so the two don't connect.
- I'm renovating two houses, so the Celeste, waffle and Serene sateen sets do sit near my list. Mostly I'm scanning for what's new versus what I've already seen. The kitchen block is a mild bonus, because a "they ship free" Breville and KitchenAid grid is the kind of thing I'd stash in my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I'd click once, maybe, on a bedding collection. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The banner's "up to 20% off" on sofas and sectionals is a real, quantified offer. It's the only one that matters to me during a renovation.
- Each bedding collection gets its own big photo and its own "Shop Now" button. The tap targets are easy to hit on a phone.
- "It all ships free" and "(they ship free!)" address furniture and big-ticket shipping anxiety directly.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows actual prices, so I can judge value without clicking.
- The Crate Card block ("It all adds up to a home you love") is the only rewards cue. As someone who optimizes for the highest-rewards card, I noticed it.

## What's weak

- The banner is about sofas, the hero is about bedding, and the middle is kitchen. There's no single story, so I can't tell what the email is for.
- The headline "new! the softest layers in fall's best colors" is lowercase and soft. It doesn't tell me how much I'd save on bedding, or whether bedding is even part of the sale.
- This is the same lamp-in-three-emails problem, but with comforters. Pottery Barn's Holiday Catalog drop at least felt like a distinct event.
- The email is very long. The app promo ("chance at $500"), The Design Desk and the footer links all sit below the useful content.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop Now". Nothing tells me which collection or product it's taking me to.
- There's no swatch, sample or color-matching content, even though the whole pitch is "fall's best colors."

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with the 20% sofa event, or make this a pure bedding send with the offer in the hero. Don't split the top of the email between the two.
- 2. Put the deadline and the discount in the hero image, not in a thin banner above it.
- 3. Cut the send to four modules (hero, three collections, one deal grid, Crate Card). Move the app and Design Desk blocks out of this send.
- 4. Name the CTAs, for example "Shop Celeste bedding" or "Shop waffle sets", instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Offer free swatches or fabric samples for the fall colorways. For a renovator, that's the real click driver.
- 6. Suppress or slow the cadence to people who got the bedroom-refresh send earlier today.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three new organic cotton colorways, and everything ships free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup looks standard for a Marketing Cloud-style template, but about 35 images are flagged for missing alt text. The viewport and text-size settings also block zoom and text resizing. The source was truncated and the relay dropped some headers, so unsubscribe, authentication, UTM and footer checks could not be completed. QA pass rate was 57% (0 failures, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 75 tracking links were skipped from HTTP probing because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and landing-page status are unverified.
- Third-party pixels are present: `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `n=5` (five LiveRamp beacons) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/efe9e66e-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`.
- `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=<hash>` is an open pixel. Six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` images share the same `mi_u` identifier.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 35 `<img>` elements were flagged.
  - About 21 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, plus `m/47/bbf6f9d4-...png` and `971aa10f-...jpg`. These are likely product or tile images.
  - The hero image `2026_0703_CB_NewBedding_HeroVideo_v2-o` on `s7d5.scene7.com` has no alt text, so it has no text fallback when images are blocked.
  - The tracking and pixel images (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...`) should carry `alt=""` explicitly. They are flagged as missing the attribute.
- **Viewport.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text sizing.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;...}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the later `body{...:100%}` rule and prevent text resizing on iOS.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;`. The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, so this is invalid markup.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later 640px rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Template leftovers.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is unreplaced placeholder text with a typo.
  - `format-detection` meta tags are duplicated (3 separate tags plus the combined one).
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
- **Hero video.** The hero asset name says "HeroVideo". The truncated source doesn't show whether a poster image or fallback is used, so this is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]`. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender failure.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay.
- The visible footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) was beyond the truncated source and could not be verified.
- The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 75 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the source shows no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters. Whether campaign parameters survive the redirect to crateandbarrel.com is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the scene7 hero. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `mi.`, `dv.`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder and the duplicate meta tags.
4. Remove the conflicting `img{max-width}` media-query pair, or reorder it so the intended cap applies.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` with full headers, or against the untruncated HTML, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the footer.
6. Resolve a sample of the tracking links (follow redirects) to confirm final landing URLs, status codes and UTM retention.
