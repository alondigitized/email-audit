# BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. I've lost count of the Black Friday sends, and this one is the fullest version of the same pitch. It opens with a free-shipping strip at $99, then the sofa hero with "Up to 60% off," then a room-by-room grid, then a "Save 15% off in the new year" block, then a $5,000 design-consult sweepstakes. The subject says "biggest sale of the year," but "Up to 60%" is the same number I saw on the earlier oxblood blasts, so nothing about it feels new.
- The leather sofa hero is a good shot. The category grid is also easy to scan on a phone. But the email tries to be four campaigns at once, and the CTA copy is mostly just "Shop the Sale." I'm running two renovations, so the furniture tile and the "meet with a designer" offer are the parts that speak to me. The free-shipping threshold doesn't. I'm not buying a sofa at $99.
- I'd open it. I'd click through to furniture, probably once. I wouldn't forward it. It's the same sale I've seen sixteen times, so I'd file it in the Pinterest folder at best.

## What's working

- The leather sofa and stone coffee table hero looks like a real room. It matches the moody, grown-up look I'm after for the second home.
- "Up to 60% off" is large and centered, with a clear SHOP THE SALE button right under it.
- The category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Tabletop, Bedding) lets me jump straight to what I'm sourcing. This is cleaner than Wayfair's wall of tiles.
- The designer appointment offer is the one thing here that fits how I shop. It's a showroom-style service for someone doing two houses.
- The footer has the CB2 app, 10% back in Rewards Dollars, and a "get 15% off" signup. The rewards math is at least mentioned, and I always pick the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- Free shipping is the first thing I see, but at "$99 or more" it's meaningless for furniture. I can't tell what freight costs on a sofa.
- Three different offers compete: 60% off now, 15% off in the new year, and the $5,000 sweepstakes. Nothing tells me which one wins.
- "Up to 60%" with a dagger and a wall of fine print below it. I can't read that text without zooming, and it feels like the real terms are buried.
- This is the same sale as "BLACK FRIDAY, BETTER THAN EVER" and "SO MUCH SALE TO SHOP." Pottery Barn did the same thing with its maroon "up to 70%" banner. Crate & Barrel's biggest-sale send at least led with sofas and sectionals, which are the things I actually need.
- Nearly every CTA says "Shop the Sale" or "Shop Now." None names a product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one hero offer and cut the other two, or send them separately. The sweepstakes and the new-year coupon are muddying the main message.
- 2. Link the hero to one or two specific products, such as the leather sofa, with a price. Right now it goes to a generic sale page.
- 3. Replace the $99 free-shipping line with furniture delivery terms: a flat rate, white-glove, and lead times.
- 4. Add swatch or sample ordering next to the furniture tile. Color matching is what holds me back on big pieces.
- 5. Reduce the number of sends. Let me cap at one or two Black Friday emails a day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a `lang="en"` attribute, a `<title>`, and mobile media queries. Automated QA found 3 warnings. The main code-level issues are 14 content images with no alt text, 11 third-party tracking pixels loaded over `http://`, and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. Header-level checks were inconclusive. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **64 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **11 insecure tracking pixels (`http://`).** The images below are served over plain HTTP, and clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may drop them. All are also missing alt text.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`, and `pub2.php` through `pub10.php` with the same query string.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The `cookieredir/…/pub1–10.php` calls are Dotomi (Epsilon) cross-site ID-matching beacons. Ten sequential requests add load and a privacy and deliverability footprint. They can also trigger tracker-blocking in privacy-focused clients.
- **Open-tracking pixels are present.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/b013c31f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are both HTTPS and have no alt text. The `mi_u` value is a 64-character hex string, which looks like a hashed user ID.
- **Font URLs.**
  - Some `@font-face` sources use a double slash, for example `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2`. This works on most servers but is sloppy.
  - The `HNW05` family loads from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`, a different domain than the sender's.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **14 content images have no alt text.** They are all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `e68a523c-…`, `81908876-…`, `913148b6-…`, `f9424ebe-…`, `b27f0d41-…`
  - `af5f88f5-…`, `b93574bb-…`, `cd7074e5-…`, `588dfbc6-…`, `00040af3-…`
  - `2c5ed016-…`, `80bf0ce4-…`, `20185973-…`, `e4289cf9-…`
  
  With images off or a screen reader, the email's content is likely inaccessible.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks user scaling, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10 on mobile.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the `<head>`. It is a typo in a comment and has no functional effect.
- **Empty conditional block.** `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` contains nothing.
- **Mobile breakpoint is narrow.** The query `@media (max-width: 418px)` applies `.mobile {width:414px}`, so it fires only on narrow phones. Wider phones and small tablets get the desktop layout. The 690px and 768px `img` rules are also inconsistent: one sets `max-width: 100% !important`, the other a fixed 690px.
- **Strengths.** `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, MSO conditional comments, and VML/Office namespaces are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` appear in the truncated portion. The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA warning). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same relay caveat applies. If the header is really absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, which Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed through the relay. From `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Footer not verified.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section. Neither can be confirmed or denied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The 64 links are wrapped in click-tracking redirects, and the QA tool skipped them. The visible source has no UTM parameters or final destination URLs. Landing-page alignment with the "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" subject cannot be checked from the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 14 CDN images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones. Add `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS or consolidate them. Ten `pub*.php` cookie-sync calls are heavy and mostly blocked by modern clients.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers, not the relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Resolve a sample of the 64 tracked links through their redirect chain. That would verify final URLs, UTM parameters, and landing-page status.
6. Clean up the `//Styles/` double slashes and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment. Host the `HNW05` fonts on the sender's own domain.
