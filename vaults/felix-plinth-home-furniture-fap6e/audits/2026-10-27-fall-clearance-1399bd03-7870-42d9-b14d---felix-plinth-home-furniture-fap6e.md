# 🍂 🍁 🍂 FALL CLEARANCE 🍂 🍁 🍂
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was that this is Wayfair on autopilot. The purple "60% OFF FALL CLEARANCE" banner at the top is clear enough. Then it turns into a wall of percent-off tiles and a "picked just for you" block that is twelve pergolas and gazebos. I'm renovating two houses, but I have never shopped for a pergola. If that's my "just for you," Wayfair doesn't know me.
- This is the same Wayfair I've been skimming since 10/25. The Way Day sends (10/26: "AREA RUGS. You *gotta* see these." and "INTERIOR DOORS ❗ ❗ LAST CHANCE ❗ WAY DAY ❗ ❗") were already a blur. Now the clearance email shows up with the same tiles. The "Fall" theme is mostly the subject line. Nothing visible in the body is autumn except the Christmas tree and nutcracker tiles near the bottom.
- Would I click? Maybe on area rugs or bedding, only because I'm furnishing two houses. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread in the folder, not the Pinterest-adjacent one.

## What's working

- The 60% off banner sits right under the nav, so I know the offer in about one second.
- The big percent tiles (60% area rugs, 50% mattresses, 40% kids bedroom furniture) are easy to scan on a phone.
- Star ratings under the product photos give me social proof without a tap.
- The Affirm "as low as 0% APR" strip speaks to someone furnishing two houses at once. Big-ticket math matters to me.
- The footer shortcuts (Rewards, Pay Over Time, Professional) are useful. Wayfair Professional is the one I'd actually use.

## What's weak

- The "picked just for you" and "free & easy delivery" grids are all pergolas and gazebos, with no prices or product names. They look personalized and aren't.
- Same-looking products appear twice, once in each grid. That's the "same lamp in three brands' emails" annoyance, except it's within one email.
- The "Fall Clearance" theme is thin. There is no deadline, no "ends Sunday," and no real fall product like throws, rugs or lighting. It reads as a perpetual sale.
- Nearly every tile says "Select items only" or "up to," so I don't trust the real discount.
- The CTAs are small lowercase labels like "area rugs →" with no verb. Nothing says "shop" or "save."

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the pergola grid with products tied to what I browsed or bought, with prices and the discount shown. Wayfair knows I'm furnishing rooms, not yards.
- 2. Add a credible end date next to the 60% banner. Compare CB2's "SALE ENDS IN 01:11:52:19" countdown from this week. It earns its place, and this email has nothing like it.
- 3. Lead with one hero product and its price, not a wall of category percentages.
- 4. Cut the repeated pergola block so the email isn't a third longer than it needs to be.
- 5. Write CTAs with a verb ("Shop rugs up to 60% off").

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with 480px responsive breakpoints. The QA data shows widespread missing alt text and about 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The source was truncated, so the footer and body markup were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing was incomplete.** Only 12 of 65 links were probed. Eight returned HTTP 429, for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…` ("Shop Sale") and `…/area-rug-clearance~e403624.html`. A 429 is rate limiting, not a broken link, so these are unverified rather than failures. Four more links were unprobed after the 70s budget (the bedding and pergola URLs). Neither group can be called pass or fail.
- **Non-HTTPS impression pixels.** There are 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=…` images, each with no alt text. Clients that block mixed content or upgrade requests may drop them, which would undercount impressions.
- **Open pixel without alt.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (the `eventType=EmailOpen` beacon) has no alt text.
- **Attribution parameters conflict.** The app-download links (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…`) carry `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=1`. A batch send tagged as a triggered campaign can misattribute in AppsFlyer.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is the web fallback for the app link. It depends on a redirect to HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA flagged:
  - hero `1029_FallClearance_SkinnyHero-General.png`
  - banners `WFUS_1029_FallClearance-Skinny-BAU_Financing.png` and `WFUS_1029_FallClearance-Skinny-FlashDeals.png`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - about 15 `stars_9/10/11.png` rating images
- **The hero link has no accessible name.** It is an `<a>` wrapping an image with no alt text, so its link text falls back to the filename, as the QA shows: `[image: 1029_FallClearance_SkinnyHero-General.pn]`. The star-rating images also convey ratings with no text equivalent.
- **Stale asset.** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-…` appears in a Fall Clearance send. The filename suggests a May 2026 Memorial Day asset, so check that it is the intended creative and not a leftover module.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="…">`.
- **Mid-word breaks on mobile.** `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; … height: 48px !important }` inside the 480px media query will break product names mid-word and may clip them.
- **Fonts and viewport.** The `@font-face` for Sofia falls back through `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`. I saw no viewport meta in the truncated `<head>`, but the source is cut off, so this is unconfirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source or in any probed URL. Per-recipient values (`_emr`, per-link `_eml`, and `p=` in the pixels) all resolved to UUIDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header.** The QA reports it was not found, and it may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so treat this as unverified.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header.** The RFC 8058 one-click header was not found, with the same relay caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so neither can be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Links use proprietary tracking instead: `source=batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_142510`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `wfcs=cs7`. This is consistent across links but incompatible with standard UTM-based analytics.
- **Landing URLs match link text.** `/daily-sales/…~e410310.html` ("nightstands: buy 2+, get 10% OFF") and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e403624.html` fit their labels. The `daily-sales` paths were not fetched, so the landing pages are unverified (429s).
- **Encoded characters in the path.** The nightstand URL has `%3a`, `%2b`, `%2c` and `%25` in the path, which depends on correct server-side decoding.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, banners and stars (for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`). Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixels.
2. Serve all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
3. Re-run the link probe with throttling or retries to clear the eight 429s and the four unprobed links.
4. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message instead of the relay copy.
5. Confirm that `c=Triggered` on the app links is intended for a batch send.
6. Remove or verify the Memorial Day banner asset.
7. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and replace `word-break: break-all` with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` or `break-word`.
8. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, the unsubscribe link and the physical address.
