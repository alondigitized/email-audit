## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 57% QA pass rate comes almost entirely from missing `alt` attributes on 45 images. The truncated source and the relay-limited header capture leave the compliance, authentication and UTM checks unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Several third-party and recipient-keyed pixels are in the body.**
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`: five repeated LiveRamp pixels.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`: an open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3a5c3660-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`: a second tracker.
  - Five identical rlcdn calls add requests and are redundant. The `s=` and `mi_u=` values are recipient-identifying tokens in image URLs.
- **The `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images (7) have an unclear role.** The source shown doesn't say whether they are content or tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **45 images have no `alt` attribute**, which is the bulk of the QA warnings.
  - About 31 are content images, mostly `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`. Examples: `932c2760-…jpg`, `a3f7737e-…jpg` and `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - Others are the hero `2026%5F1104%5FCB%5FGiftGuide%5Fhero%5FV2%2Dezgif…` on `s7d5.scene7.com`, `bbf6f9d4-…png` and the Thanksgiving tertiary banner.
  - About 14 are tracking pixels (the rlcdn, `o.gif` and `dv.` images above). These need `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`, not descriptive text.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render as filenames or UUIDs. If product tiles are image-only links, the links are unnamed.
- **The hero filename contains `ezgif`, which suggests an animated GIF.** Outlook desktop shows only the first frame, and with no alt text there is no fallback copy. I couldn't verify the frame content from the truncated source.
- **The viewport meta tag sets `maximum-scale=1`.** This disables pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, an accessibility issue.
- **Markup validity is minor.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand under an XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under that doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times.
- **Two `img` max-width rules conflict.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` comes before `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match at equal specificity, so the later 640px rule wins and the 600px rule never applies.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated, so personalization on those links appears to have resolved. The truncated source limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** The same relay caveat applies. If it is genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo one-click unsubscribe requirements for bulk senders are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the image and tracking hosts are on `crateandbarrel.com` subdomains, which is consistent with aligned branding. That is not proof of authentication.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source.** I couldn't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing-page destinations could not be verified.** The 81 click-redirect links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't expose final URLs.
- **Image asset paths are dated.** The Thanksgiving tertiary banner (`…/2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide/…`) comes from a September campaign folder. Reusing an older asset in a Nov 4 gift-guide email isn't a defect by itself, but check that its link target is still live.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all about 31 content images, including the hero and product tiles. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels and give them explicit 1×1 dimensions.
2. Add a text or live-HTML fallback for the animated hero, and confirm the first frame carries the key message.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
4. Deduplicate the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls into one.
5. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, self-close the `<meta charset>` tag and drop the duplicate `format-detection` tags. Remove the dead 600px `img` max-width rule or reorder the two media queries.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw original message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve a sample of the 81 redirect links to verify UTMs and landing pages.
