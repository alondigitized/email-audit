# Growth spurt? Get upgrades fast →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Wrong list, wrong day, wrong Felix. I'm renovating two houses and I don't have a kid — this is a Crate & **Kids** send with a nursery, a stroller sweepstakes, and a "Nuna" baby gear line, and none of that touches my project. The "Growth spurt? Get upgrades fast" subject implied furniture that scales with a room, which almost fooled me into thinking it was a general home upgrade play, but the second the hero loads it's a crib-adjacent green playroom and I'm out.
- This is also the third or fourth Crate & Barrel-family email clogging my inbox this week — I already gave the actual Crate & Barrel bedroom sends 7/10 on 2026-08-23, and now the sister brand is spamming me with baby registries. If Crate & Barrel's data team can't tell the difference between "guy remodeling two houses" and "expecting parent," that's a segmentation failure, not a creative one.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — unless it's to razz a friend who actually just had a baby, this is a straight delete.

## What's working

- The hero photo (green cabinetry, gallery wall, kids' table) is genuinely well-styled — Crate's photography team never misses on room composition.
- "new faves in days, not weeks" is a decent hook if I were furnishing a nursery on a deadline — fast-ship messaging always lands for renovation timelines.
- The category grid (bedroom, nursery, study, playroom, rugs, lighting) is at least organized and scannable, same clean grid discipline as the Crate & Barrel bedroom email.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to me — no kids, not on this list intentionally, and the email doesn't even try to hedge with "shop for a gift" framing.
- The $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes buried mid-email requires booking an appointment just to enter — that's Article-level friction ("Home tour" email had the same "no real offer, just browse" energy) dressed up as a giveaway.
- Way too long — I counted eight+ distinct sections before the footer. CB2's five-emails-in-six-days problem, but compressed into one scroll.
- The Paperless Post partnership banner for "baby shower invites" is the moment I fully checked out — this brand thinks I'm expecting.
- "we love these for you" personalization grid is showing me a stroller and a nursery glider — the personalization engine is confidently wrong.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix list hygiene first** — if I've never bought baby gear, don't put me on Crate & Kids. This is the single biggest lever; no subject line rewrite saves a mistargeted send.
- 2. Cut the email in half — one hero, one grid, one CTA. Right now it reads like four emails stapled together (new arrivals, Paperless Post, stroller launch, sweepstakes, "we love these," card upsell).
- 3. Make the $5,000 sweepstakes the actual hero if it's the best offer in the email — right now it's stranded two-thirds down.
- 4. Drop the app-download CTA at the very bottom — nobody scrolls that far, and it's a wasted send if they do.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nursery to playroom, furnished in days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New stroller just landed + $5,000 giveaway inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop nursery-to-big-kid furniture that ships in as little as 7 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: enter to win $5,000 toward your Design Desk appointment.`
