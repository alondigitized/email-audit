# For the early shopper: UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the twelfth Pottery Barn email in about two days, and I'm numb to it. It's the same maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner I got in "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS + Up to 70% off for your cozy home" and "Early Black Friday bedding deals + FREE SHIPPING." The subject adds "for the early shopper," which is a bit of a joke when I've been getting this offer since Friday.
- The layout is clean. The banner, one SHOP NOW button, and a category grid with Furniture, Bedding, Rugs and Tabletop all land fast. I'm doing two houses, so the grid is the useful part. Below it, "Recommended just for you" is stockings, a throw, a quilt and curtain rods, and none of that is personal. The dining table is the only real hit. Nothing tells me what ships when, or whether any of it is in the 70%.
- I'd mark this as read and keep moving. I wouldn't forward it. If I click, it's the Furniture tile or the Toscana table, and I'd get there faster from the site.

## What's working

- The "up to 70% off" number is huge and sits first. The eye lands on it with no hunting.
- The category grid (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, Curtains, Tabletop) gets me into my renovation lanes in one tap.
- The Toscana Extendable Dining Table is the one product I'd actually bookmark for the Pinterest folder.
- The Pottery Barn Rewards card block (up to 10% back) matters to a points-chaser like me.
- No broken images or overlapping text. It renders clean.

## What's weak

- It's the same maroon banner as every other send this weekend. By email twelve I've stopped reading it.
- "Recommended just for you" isn't personal. Stockings and a Christmas tree for a guy renovating two houses reads as a generic merchandising block.
- "Up to 70%" says nothing about which items. The recommended products have no prices and no sale tags, so I can't tell what the discount is.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text and the rewards math is buried. There's nothing on furniture shipping or lead times.
- The Design Crew sweepstakes and the Christmas Shop banner crowd the bottom. They are three offers fighting for one click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show real prices on the products: "Toscana table, was $X, now $Y." Cut the "just for you" label unless the picks are based on what I browsed.
- 2. Make the hero specific. Name one category and one deadline instead of a repeat banner.
- 3. Add shipping info for furniture and a mention of the Design Crew in the top third. That's what a two-house buyer cares about.
- 4. Cap the cadence. Twelve sends in two days is the real problem. Crate & Barrel's "ON NOW! Up to 30% off Early Black Friday deals + Earn 2x Rewards!" at least gives me a rewards angle. CB2's "Made to linger longer + 2X rewards is ending soon" had a dining-chair lookbook I'd actually read. Wayfair's "END TABLES" subject at least matched its hero tile.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "For the early shopper: UP TO 70% OFF"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a hidden preheader. Its main technical problems are about 46 images without alt text and the recipient's email address sitting in plaintext query strings, including over HTTP. Header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the AgentMail relay stripped the headers. The HTML source was also truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains. The visible example is the logo link at `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`, and the other link destinations are opaque. Their redirect health is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party tracking pixels.**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` is a Turn/Amobee ad-network beacon.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` is an open-tracking pixel served over plain HTTP.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/2d605d30-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a second open pixel.
- **Three open-tracking pixels.** That is redundant, and the ad-network beacon adds a third-party data flow.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 46 images flagged for missing alt text.**
  - Hero slices: `H26_Sale_EBF_GraphicHeroLinkfarm/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_12.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`.
  - Product images: `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg` and others.
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg` and `_02.jpg`.
  - Other: `3ceb0c96-…jpg`, `spacer_0.gif`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`.
  - Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`. The content images need descriptive alt text.
  - Because the hero and footer are sliced images, a client with images blocked shows blank space and no readable link labels.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport meta disables zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3).**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/…` (the pixel)
  - These may be blocked or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Stray PHP in `<style>`.** The lines `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")` ended up inside the second `<style>` block. They are harmless but leaked server-side code, and they can invalidate the rule that follows.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain, so it breaks if that path is retired.
- **Hotlinked product images.** They load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` rather than the email CDN, which couples rendering to the site's availability.
- **Minor markup issues.** The spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lacks `role="presentation"`, and the Google Fonts `<link>` is ignored by most clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** in the visible source.
- **Recipient email in plaintext in two URLs (privacy issue).**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The second goes over unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed to anyone on the network path. Both URLs also land in access logs and referrer data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool says this is likely because the relay doesn't capture them. This can't be confirmed from the data, so verify it on a direct-to-mailbox copy. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be assessed from this capture.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address.** These are normally in the footer, which was truncated, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** The links are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects. The `qs` value is an opaque token, and the destination URLs and UTMs were not resolved.
- **Direct links have no UTMs.** The two direct-to-site URLs in the data (`coreemail.html?email=…` and the product images) show none.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the hero and footer slices. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Remove the recipient email from URL query strings, or replace it with an opaque ID. Stop sending it over HTTP.
3. Move the three HTTP assets to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, `svopn` pixel).
4. Fill in `<title>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Delete the stray `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
6. Capture a raw `.eml` from a non-relay inbox. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
7. Resolve the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects out-of-band to confirm destinations and UTM coverage.
8. Re-run the QA on the untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and physical address can be checked.
