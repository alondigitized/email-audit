# COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED + August Sale ends tonight
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "ENDS TONIGHT" up top, up to 40% off — that's the most direct urgency signal CB2 has thrown at me all week, and it actually got my eye before the "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED" tagline registered. But then the email immediately pivots into another decor-catalog scroll: table jewelry, handblown glass, Italian ceramics, candle holders, porcelain vases, one styled vignette after another. It's gorgeous, moody, on-brand — but I'm renovating two houses right now and there's not a stick of furniture in the first two-thirds of this. It's all vases and candle holders.
- This is a sharper send than the David Sirelli designer-profile email from yesterday (8/11) or the Andreas Hansen one from 8/10 — those had zero discount, this one leads with a real number and a real deadline. It's closer in energy to the "WIN A CUBS GAME" email from 8/9, which also got me to open faster than a straight product dump. Compared to Wayfair's privacy-screen bait-and-switch or Pottery Barn's sixteenth entryway listicle this week, CB2 at least respects that I know their brand and doesn't waste my scroll on stuff I don't need explained.
- Would I click? Yeah, actually — "SHOP THE SALE" at the top is enough to get a tap, mostly to see if the 40% touches anything I actually need (rugs, lighting, an accent chair) rather than another hurricane candle holder. Wouldn't forward it to a friend — too generic a sale banner for that, no single product worth sending someone a link to.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" + "UP TO 40% OFF" right at the top is the clearest offer CB2 has sent me all week — no hunting for it.
- The dark ceramic vase hero shot photographs beautifully and matches the moody, elevated aesthetic CB2's been running (consistent with the Sirelli and Hansen sends).
- "MORE TO COVET" section finally surfaces furniture categories I actually care about — entryway furniture, coffee tables, accent chairs, lamps — even if it's buried mid-scroll.
- Second urgency beat ("ENDS TOMORROW: Download the app, win $1,000") is a nice structural move — most brands only hit urgency once.
- Clean footer cross-sell into Crate & Barrel and Crate & Kids without feeling like spam.

## What's weak

- The sale banner says "up to 40% off" but then six straight sections are vases, candle holders, and glassware — none of it visibly tagged with a price or percentage, so I can't tell what's actually on sale versus just featured.
- Way too much decor-accessory real estate before I get to anything renovation-relevant; I'm hunting for furniture and it's vase after vase after vase.
- Two competing CTAs fighting for my attention near the bottom — "$1,000 app download" and "leave a review, win $1,000" — that's the same prize twice in two different asks, which reads a little scattershot.
- No swatch/sample or shipping-timeline content anywhere, which is the thing I actually need before I click "shop" on furniture — CB2 never runs this, and it's the one thing that would separate them from Article, who at least frames new collections around sizing/finish options.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the vase/candle-holder gauntlet down to one section and pull "Entryway Furniture," "Coffee Tables," "Accent Chairs" up near the top, right under the hero — that's the stuff worth my 40% off, not glassware.
- 2. Put an actual percentage or price callout on at least the hero product so "up to 40% off" feels connected to something specific, not just a banner.
- 3. Kill one of the two $1,000 CTAs — pick either the app-download or the review-for-a-chance-to-win, not both, it dilutes urgency instead of stacking it.
- 4. Add one line of shipping/delivery-window copy near the furniture section — that's the actual friction point for someone mid-renovation, and CB2 never addresses it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: Up to 40% Off the August Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Accent chairs, coffee tables, and lighting — all included tonight only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hours left to save before the August Sale ends.`
