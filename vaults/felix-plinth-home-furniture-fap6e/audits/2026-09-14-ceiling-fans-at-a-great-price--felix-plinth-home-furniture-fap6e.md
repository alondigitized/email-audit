# CEILING FANS at a great price.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- "Ceiling Fans at a great price" is a lie — or at least a bait-and-switch, because the actual ceiling fan section is buried about two-thirds of the way down, and by then I've already scrolled past pergolas, privacy screens, an AC unit, recliners, birdbaths, greenhouses, and a daybed. This is the same "48 HR 70% OFF" clearance banner I got yesterday on "ADJUSTABLE BEDS 🚩 UP TO 60% OFF" and the day before on "IF YOU WERE AN AREA RUG" — at this point I could set my watch by it. And it's got the exact same broken-image problem I flagged on the area rug email: at least eight blank white boxes with the little blue-dot placeholder icon where product photos should be, including one in the "fresh-picked favorites" section that's supposed to be personalized to me.
- I'm mid-renovation on two houses and I do actually need ceiling fans for the primary — so there's a version of this email that gets a click. This isn't it. I'm marking it read and moving on. Not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- The subject line is at least specific and on-category for once — ceiling fans is a real line item for me, unlike the random gazebo/bidet grab-bag subjects this brand's been running.
- The "48 HR 70% OFF" badge up top is legible and the urgency is at least plausible, if repetitive.
- There is an actual ceiling fan hero photo lower in the email (dark wood-blade fan in a bright room) that looks decent — good lighting, real room context, not stock-y.
- Wayfair Rewards "5% back on every item, always" is a real loyalty hook I recognize and actually value as a high-rewards-card shopper.

## What's weak

- Ceiling fans, the whole premise of the subject line, don't show up until deep in the scroll — everything above the fold is pergolas, privacy screens, and generic "must-shop steals."
- At least eight product image slots are broken/blank placeholder boxes — this is now the third Wayfair send this week with this exact bug (rugs, adjustable beds, now this).
- The "fresh-picked favorites" section, which should feel personalized, is half broken images and generic categories like "Best-Selling Recliners" and "Birdbaths" — nothing in there reads as picked for a guy renovating two houses.
- It's a wall of unrelated categories — AC units, recliners, daybeds, office chairs — with zero curation logic tying it together beyond "clearance."

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image renders first — this is the third time this week and it's actively making the brand look janky, not just this one send.
- 2. Lead with the ceiling fan hero and a real price/percentage off a specific fan model, not a generic 70% clearance badge that applies to everything in the warehouse.
- 3. Cut the grab-bag categories (birdbaths, greenhouses, daybeds) out of a ceiling-fan-subject-lined email — if it's a clearance blast, call it that in the subject instead of promising one category and delivering ten.
- 4. Make "fresh-picked favorites" actually reflect browsing history — right now it reads as randomly assigned SKUs, same complaint I had on the "picked just for you" section in the adjustable beds email.
