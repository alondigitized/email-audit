# Earn 2x Rewards & get up to 50% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- Nineteenth Crate & Barrel family send in about four days, and it's the Kids sender again. My gut reaction was "I'm not buying a nursery." I'm renovating two houses, so I need kitchen, dining and lighting. This email leads with a plaid hero ("the fall sales event: up to 50% off") and then spends most of its length on cribs, strollers and baby gear. I'm not the audience for any of that.
- The Double Rewards deadline of Nov. 17 appears three times, and it's the only part that speaks to how I shop. I run everything through the highest-rewards card, so 2x matters. But it's buried in repeated red banners. The same "last Double Rewards this year" line was in my earlier Kids sends today ("Get it fast AND earn Double Rewards?" and "These ship quick AND earn you Double Rewards"), and it's getting stale.
- I'd mark this as read. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. The only thing I'd save to my Pinterest folder is the arched floor lamp tile under "decor & storage", and that tile is tiny.

## What's working

- The "up to 50% off" hero is a clear, single number, and the Shop Now button is visible.
- The Double Rewards deadline of Nov. 17 is specific and credible. That's the one thing that registers with me.
- The tile grid of furniture, bedding, rugs and decor gives me a quick category scan, and the lamp and rug tiles are nice.
- "Ships quick" cues on some tiles help, because furniture lead times are my biggest worry.

## What's weak

- The content is nursery-heavy: baby gear, a crib, strollers, "we love these for you". Nothing is aimed at a 36-year-old renovating two houses.
- The Double Rewards message repeats in three separate red and tan banners, and none of them says how many points I'd earn on a typical purchase.
- The email is extremely long. By the time I reach the app promo, the TheDesignDesk block and the legal fine print, I've lost interest.
- The "we love these for you" product grid is all strollers and car seats. If it's personalized, it's wrong for me.
- This is the same family send I've seen all week. CB2 and Pottery Barn do the same, but Crate & Barrel is by far the worst offender on cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by category. Send me the main Crate & Barrel kitchen, dining or lighting content, not Kids, since my browsing history is clearly home renovation.
- 2. Show the rewards math in one place, for example "$500 spent = $X back in Reward Dollars". Then cut the repeated banners.
- 3. Cut the length by half. Lead with one hero product and a single clear CTA.
- 4. Keep the product grid to items I've actually browsed, with price and shipping dates.
- 5. Cap frequency across the Crate & Barrel, Kids and CB2 senders so the same promotion doesn't hit me three times in a day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard table layout with a Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) dynamic-content stack. The confirmed problems are missing alt text on about 21 content images and several markup defects. The header and tracking-link warnings are unverified, because the relay drops headers and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **80 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM survival are unverified.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with identical URLs. Each one is an extra request, and the repeats may cause double-counting.
- **Five third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp pixels. They send a hashed identifier to a third party on every open. That is a privacy-disclosure point, not a rendering bug.
- Other tracking beacons present are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/32e26386-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (about 21).** The QA tool flagged:
  - 20 images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `26171910-…jpg` and `8c4ce5d9-…gif`.
  - The spacer `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - The QA tool can't tell an absent `alt` attribute from `alt=""`. If these images carry the offers, clients with images blocked will show nothing and screen readers will announce filenames or nothing.
- **The remaining ~13 flagged images are tracking pixels** (`mi`, `rlcdn`, `dv`, `o.gif`). They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, but this is low severity.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` is followed by `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`. Both rules have equal specificity and the later one wins, so the 600px cap never applies at 768px and wider.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed.
  - The charset is declared twice.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times, with a conflicting combined value.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (also misspelled) is still in the source.
- **Empty rule in the MSO block:** `td,th,div,p,a { }`.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}`** apply to every element, so any text without an explicit override inherits them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`). The `mi_u` / `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` warnings are not confirmed defects.** The QA output itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so the original headers need checking.
- **The unsubscribe link and postal address can't be verified.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so I won't call them present or absent.
- The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain and consistent with the From name. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. All 80 links go through redirect wrappers and were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing destinations can't be assessed from the available data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~20 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images. Give the spacer and all tracking pixels `alt=""`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Collapse the 6 repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels into one. Review whether 5 `rlcdn.com` pixels are needed and whether the privacy policy discloses them.
4. Fix the CSS cascade. Reorder or merge the two `img{max-width}` media queries so the intended cap applies.
5. Clean the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, de-duplicate the meta tags, and delete the `IMPUT HERE` comment and the empty MSO rule.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and the raw headers. Check that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are present and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. Then confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to verify landing-page status and UTM carry-through.
