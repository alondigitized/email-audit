# STARTS NOW: Black Friday deals + free shipping!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel send I've logged since 11/17, and the Kids sender is just the same "Black Friday starts now" message I got earlier today as "Our biggest sale of the year starts now!" under the main C&B name. I filed it as "C&B again" before I read anything. The hero offer is clear: up to 50% off plus free shipping. But the text sits on a busy photo of wrapped gifts and is hard to read. At phone size the shrunken screenshot makes the whole thing feel like a long catalog scroll.
- I'm renovating two houses, so kids furniture, bedding and playroom pieces aren't my category. A few pieces like the nod chairs and the rugs are borderline useful, but this is a kids-and-baby send. The "we love these for you" strip is all strollers, a glider and a baby activity chair, which tells me nothing about my renovations. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim and mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "up to 50% off + free shipping," is a specific number, and the Shop Now button is clear.
- The "Ships Free!" tags on the bedding tiles answer the furniture shipping question right where I look for it.
- The product grid is tidy and easy to scan, with a short offer line under each tile ("up to 50% off baby furniture").
- The Crate Card block and the app download block both mention rewards. I care about that math, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The hero headline is low contrast on a textured photo, so I had to squint at the free-shipping line.
- It's the seventh C&B-family send in about a day. "Starts now" is stale when I got the identical "starts now" email from the main sender hours ago.
- It's a kids-and-baby send with stroller and glider picks, so it doesn't reflect that I'm furnishing adult homes. It reads as if I'm a stranger.
- The email is very long, with many CTAs: Shop the Sale, Shop 300+ Gifts, Shop Kids Stockings, Shop Adult Stockings, Shop Baby Gear, Learn More, Download Now, Book a Free Appointment. No single clear next step stands out.
- Product prices under the "we love these" tiles are tiny and hard to read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the cadence. Send one Black Friday email per day across the C&B family, or suppress the Kids sender for people who haven't browsed kids.
- 2. Fix the hero. Put a solid color block behind the headline, or use a plainer image, so the offer is readable in a thumbnail.
- 3. Personalize the product picks to what I've browsed, such as rugs and shelving, rather than strollers.
- 4. Collapse the CTAs to one primary button and one secondary button.
- 5. Show the Crate Card rewards math in the hero, for example "extra 10% back + free shipping."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, a mobile breakpoint at 460px and 79 tracked links. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are about 21 content images with no alt text and a leftover template placeholder comment. The header warnings are unverified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **79 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not confirmed good.
- **Tracking pixels are repeated:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` appear 5 times (RampLive/LiveRamp).
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/6a657a07-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are one open pixel each.
  - These are about 13 beacon requests per open, and the `mi_u` value is a long per-recipient identifier.
- **Stale asset path:** `https://s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif` comes from an October (`2026_1002`) gift-guide campaign folder. It is reused in a 11/18 send, so it may carry stale content. Whether it is stale can't be confirmed from the source.
- **Mixed image hosts:** images are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com` and `sr.rlcdn.com`. A failure on any one of them breaks part of the render.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 21 content or spacer images are flagged for missing alt text** (WARN). Examples:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - the hero `2026%5F1118%5FCBK%5FEBF1%5FStartsNow%5FEcomm%5FHer…`
  - eleven `…/m/1/*.jpg` product or category tiles
  - five `…/m/1/*.gif` files
  - `971aa10f-…jpg`
- **Alt text and image blocking:** with images off (Outlook default, some corporate clients), the hero and tiles render blank, and screen readers announce the filenames. Alt text is the only fallback for the hero and tile content.
- **Pixel alt:** the 13 tracking pixels also lack `alt`. A spacer or pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so they are not announced.
- **Spacer alt:** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` is a decorative spacer and should use `alt=""`.
- **Placeholder comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in the production `<head>`. It is harmless but shows a template step was skipped.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Pixel-size CSS:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` disables text-size scaling and sets a tight 100% line-height globally. `.f10` sets a 10px font size on mobile.
- **Duplicate meta:** `format-detection` is declared 4 times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`). The later tags are redundant, and parsers may handle conflicting tags inconsistently.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses a raw `&` (invalid in XHTML; it should be `&amp;`). It also doesn't reflect the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The HTML was truncated, so the footer and later modules could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are flagged "not found" (WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **not confirmed as a sender defect**, but one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) support can't be verified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are **unknown**. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, and `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a separate host. Alignment can't be checked from this capture.
- **Footer:** the footer (physical address, in-body unsubscribe link) is cut off by truncation, so those CAN-SPAM elements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** the 79 links are tracking redirects that weren't probed, so UTM presence and campaign naming can't be verified. The final destination URLs and landing-page match for "Black Friday deals + free shipping" are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Per-recipient tokens:** the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values on the pixels identify the recipient and message (Movable Ink / MI). Whether they are carried into the click URLs is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all hero and tile images, for example the sale hero and product or category names. Use `alt=""` on spacers and the 13 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace the global `text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, make it match the subject line, and remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder comment. Deduplicate the `format-detection` metas.
4. Confirm whether the reused October gift-guide GIF (`2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide`) is intended in this send.
5. For the QA pipeline: capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message instead of the relay copy, so these warnings can be settled. Resolve the 79 redirect links with a HEAD or GET `Location` trace, without following to the end, to check UTM parameters and landing pages. Reconcile the 57% pass rate against the 0 issues reported.
