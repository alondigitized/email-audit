# Starts TODAY! Open to shop Double Rewards →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crate & Barrel send today, and the Kids logo is the only new thing. I opened it expecting the same Double Rewards pitch I'd already seen, and that's what I got. The headline, "last Double Rewards this year!", sits right at the top with a Shop Now button, so the offer lands fast. The rest is a long scroll of bedroom, nursery and playroom grids.
- Kids furniture isn't what I'm shopping for. I'm renovating two houses, so the cribs, nursery chairs and strollers are noise. The "up to 50% off" Fall Sales Event banner is buried mid-email. The Double Rewards math is the part I'd actually care about, since I buy on the highest-rewards card. I'd mark this read and keep the earlier send if I needed the offer. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- "last Double Rewards this year!" is a clear, urgent hook at the top with a visible Shop Now button.
- Each product tile has a small "Earn 2x/4x Reward Dollars" label, so the rewards math is visible per item. That's the detail I care about.
- Sections are cleanly split into bedroom, nursery and playroom, each with its own CTA. The layout is easy to scan.
- The photography is warm and well styled, with deep green and plaid tones. It's the Pinterest-folder material I save.
- The Design Desk block at the bottom is a real showroom-style service pitch. That's closer to my renovation than anything else here.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical send today, after "The Fall Sales Event starts TODAY!" and "Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅". It's cadence fatigue.
- The Kids focus misses me completely. Cribs, strollers and playroom tables are irrelevant to a 36-year-old furnishing two houses.
- The 50% off banner sits mid-email as a thin strip. The bigger deal is hidden below a bedroom-set hero.
- The email is extremely long. It runs through the app promo, a $5,000 sweepstakes, a card pitch and the Design Desk. That's too many asks for one send.
- At phone size, the small text under each tile, including the reward labels and prices, is hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 50% off and 2x Rewards offer in one big hero. Move the Fall Sales Event strip to the top instead of the middle.
- 2. Cut the email to one theme. A kids-only send should be segmented to people who've shopped kids.
- 3. Add a rewards math line, such as "Spend $X, earn $Y back," so I can compare against my card.
- 4. Drop the sweepstakes and app promo, or move them to a separate send.
- 5. Make the product text bigger for mobile.
