# Time to shop: Up to 30% off top toys for Black Friday!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "wrong inbox, and I've already seen this one." The subject says toys, but this is the eighth Crate & Barrel Kids send I've logged since 11/17. I got the Babyletto nursery furniture email and the "toys that top their list" email earlier today. I'm renovating two houses and there's no playroom in either one. This email is a long scroll of ball pits, easels, a play tent and a rocking horse, and none of it helps me finish a kitchen.
- I do give it credit for execution. The "Black Friday starts now! Up to 30% off toys" hero is clear. The product blocks are clean, and each one has a price, a review quote and a Shop Now button. It reads like a proper catalog. But I get the same C&B family through the main brand, CB2 and Kids all week, and my brain has filed this under "snooze."
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to a friend with a toddler. I'd save the wooden workbench to my Pinterest-adjacent folder as a gift idea at most, then mark the rest as read.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "Up to 30% off toys," is stated plainly with one Shop the Sale button, so I know what's on offer right away.
- Each product block pairs a photo with a sale price versus regular price, a real customer quote and a Shop Now button. That's good merchandising.
- The "free shipping starts now" strip at the top answers a question I always have about furniture-size items.
- The lifestyle photography is warm and consistent, and the wood and neutral palette fits the C&B look I recognize.
- The "up to 50% off Black Friday deals" grid near the bottom gives me a quick way to jump between categories.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is aimed at a 36-year-old renovating two homes. It's all toys and nursery, and there's no sign they know who I am.
- It's the eighth send in three days, and the Babyletto furniture email earlier today covered some of the same product. Seeing the same product more than once in a week is the kind of repetition I hate.
- It's far too long. There are five hero products, then a deal grid, then a collection banner, an Oilo partner block, a sweepstakes and a "we love these for you" strip. The Oilo block and the sweepstakes dilute the offer.
- The small text is tiny on a phone, especially the price lines, the review quotes and the grid captions. I'd have to zoom to read them.
- "Up to 30% off" in the hero and "up to 50% off" in the grid make the offer fuzzy. I can't tell what's actually 30% and what's 50%.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household. Send this to people who've bought kids' items and send me the home and kitchen version. Fewer sends to non-parents would help more than any creative tweak.
- 2. Cut the body to two or three products plus one deal grid, and drop the partner and sweepstakes blocks.
- 3. State one number in the hero. Either "30% off toys" or "50% off select," not both.
- 4. Make the text bigger on mobile and move the free-shipping detail into a visible line near the hero button.
- 5. Consolidate the Black Friday cadence. Wayfair is the benchmark for what not to do here. I've had about fifteen sends from them since 11/09, and Crate & Barrel is heading the same way.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off toys, ships free through Black Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts for the kids: wooden toys, 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wooden workbench, art easel and play tent, all on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on furniture-size gifts, today through Cyber Monday`
