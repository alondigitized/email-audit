# Elite-level design: See our NEW Venus Williams collab
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Elite-level design: See our NEW Venus Williams collab" — a tennis legend fronting a kids' furniture line, that's a genuinely different beat than the fifth "cozy season" or "olive sofa" email this month. The hero shot with her in the neon-green box motif is sharp and the copy "inspired by the game, designed by the GOAT" is a fun hook. But I'm the wrong recipient entirely — this is Crate&Barrel Kids, and I don't have kids, I'm mid-renovation on two adult houses. It's a well-made email for someone else's inbox.
- The products themselves — winged bed, green velvet accent chair, dresser, lamp, tennis-motif bedding and throw pillows — are genuinely cute and cohesively styled around one color story (that green/cream/burgundy palette repeats through every module), which is more disciplined than the usual C&B grid dump. If I had a kid's room to do I might actually click through. As it stands: not opening a second time, not forwarding to a friend (unless one of my friends has a kid, in which case maybe), straight to archived.
- Compared to the regular Crate & Barrel sends I've been getting — "Your favorite sofa in olive," "Tricky entryway," "The Design Desk" rug email — this one at least has a real celebrity-collab hook, more than CB2's umpteenth "best sellers on sale" rerun. But it's simply not aimed at my life stage.

## What's working

- The Venus Williams collab angle is a real point of difference — not another generic seasonal push, unlike C&B's "olive sofa" or "holiday 2026" sends.
- Tight, consistent green/cream/burgundy color story running through every single module — beds, chairs, dresser, lamp, bedding all visually match, better palette discipline than the usual C&B scattershot grid.
- Clear one-product-per-module layout with a visible "Shop Now" button under each — no ambiguity about what to click.
- Registry callout near the bottom (nicole brydon bloom & justin theroux's registry) is a nice touch of social proof/relevance for anyone actually shopping for a nursery.

## What's weak

- Wrong list for me entirely — I don't have kids, so this whole send is dead weight in my inbox regardless of execution quality.
- It's a long, long scroll — probably 10+ full modules before you hit the Design Desk and app-download pitch; on mobile that's a lot of thumb-swiping for a single collab drop.
- No price anywhere in any module I can see — every button just says "Shop Now," no "from $X" anchor, so there's zero purchase-intent signal before you click.
- The tennis racket lamp and neon "ACE" sign are fun but read more "novelty gift" than "elite design" — undercuts the premium framing in the subject line a bit.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this — if C&B knows I've never bought anything from the Kids line (or clicked a Kids email), don't send it, or at minimum lead with something that could cross-sell into "gift for a friend's kid" framing instead of assuming I'm a parent.
- 2. Add a price or "from $X" anchor on at least the hero bed and chair — right now there's no rewards-math or price signal for someone deciding whether to even scroll.
- 3. Trim the email — six-plus product modules deep is too long for a single collab reveal; cut to hero + 3 bestsellers + shop-the-collection CTA.
- 4. Move the Design Desk / registry / app content into a separate follow-up send rather than tacking it onto the same email — it dilutes the single "Venus Williams collab" story.
