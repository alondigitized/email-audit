# New backpacks in every size, now starting at $39!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, another Crate & Kids drop in the same week I already reviewed the nursery bait-and-switch ("Shop furniture you'll love now & forever" that turned out to be cribs and a monogrammed "WILL" backpack) and two separate Ninja Midnight Mocha sends. At this point my inbox folder for this stuff is basically a Crate & Barrel family reunion. This one's at least honest — subject says backpacks, hero says backpacks, and it delivers backpacks, organized by kid size with a "what fits inside" diagram for each tier. That's a real improvement over the furniture email that lied to me.
- That said, I'm renovating two houses right now, not outfitting a kindergartner — this whole email is dead on arrival for my actual purchase intent. I get why it's landing (that "WILL" monogram backpack a few days back tells me they've profiled me as a parent), but nothing here touches paint, fabric swatches, or furniture financing, which is the stuff I'm actually shopping for this month.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Maybe to my sister who's got two in elementary school — the size guide is genuinely useful, not just decorative. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The size-guide concept is legitimately smart — small/medium/large backpacks each get a "what fits inside" visual so a parent isn't guessing whether it holds a binder or just crayons.
- Subject and hero copy match for once — no bait-and-switch like the furniture send from three days ago.
- "$39" starting price is a concrete, specific number right up top, not a vague "new arrivals."
- The bundle CTA ("make it a bundle... shop back to school with a lunch box & pencil case") is smart cross-sell logic that mirrors how a parent actually shops before school starts.
- Product photography is clean and consistent — kids in real backpack-wearing scenarios, not staged mood shots.

## What's weak

- I've now gotten a Crate & Kids or Crate & Barrel email nearly every day this week — this is brand fatigue, not a single bad send. Compare to CB2's family snooze problem I flagged a few days back; the whole Crate & Barrel portfolio is spamming the same households right now.
- Zero relevance to my actual shopping mode (renovating two homes) — no swatch program, no fabric samples, no furniture financing, nothing that ladders up to what I'm actually buying this month.
- The email is a marathon — I count something like nine or ten distinct sections (size guide, small/medium/large, bundle, sweepstakes, backpack cloud, summer sale, "we love these for you," credit card, app). By the time I hit the credit card pitch near the bottom I've mentally checked out.
- The "up to 50% off summer sale" banner buried two-thirds down feels like a completely different campaign stapled onto a backpack email — no visual through-line to the back-to-school theme above it.
- Credit card cross-sell ("new card, low APR just got better") at the very bottom is the same beat Crate & Barrel hits in nearly every one of their emails — I've seen it enough times now that it reads as filler, not a benefit.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment this out of my inbox entirely if I don't have school-age kids in my profile.** This is the single biggest lever — a renovation-focused shopper getting a back-to-school backpack size guide is a targeting miss, not a creative miss.
- 2. **Cut the email in half.** Small/medium/large backpacks, bundle CTA, done. The sweepstakes, backpack cloud, unrelated summer sale banner, and credit card pitch should be separate sends — Article and Pottery Barn keep their promo emails to one clear offer, and it reads faster.
- 3. **Move the size guide higher and make it interactive if this goes to web** — right now it's a static image; a real "tap your kid's grade" tool would beat every competitor doing static back-to-school banners.
- 4. **Drop or relocate the summer sale banner** — it has nothing to do with backpacks and breaks the narrative I'm following through the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Backpack size guide: find their perfect fit, from $39`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Small, medium, or large? The backpack sizing cheat sheet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A size for every grade, plus lunch boxes and pencil cases to match`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off back to school — sized right the first time`
