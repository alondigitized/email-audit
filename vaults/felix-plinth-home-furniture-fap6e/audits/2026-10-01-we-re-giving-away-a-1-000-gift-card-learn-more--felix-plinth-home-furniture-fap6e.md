# We’re giving away a $1,000 Gift Card! Learn more →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel email I've opened today, and it's the weakest one. The subject promises a gift card giveaway. The hero does the same: two empty stars over a crib, "want to win a $1,000 gift card?", and a "Write a Review" button. That's homework, not a deal. I'm renovating two houses and I'm not going to review a crib I haven't bought for a sweepstakes, and the header banner still says "Up to 50% off."
- The rest of the email is a catch-all. It has a 60% clearance block with fall leaves, a Jellycat lion, strollers, a dresser, a glider, a crib mattress, a credit card promo, an app download and a TheDesignDesk plug. Nothing is edited down. I'd save it to my Pinterest folder only for the Jenny Lind dresser and the Babyletto glider, and I'd forward it to nobody. I'd skim it and mark it read.

## What's working

- The Kids logo and the "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, Up to 50% off" banner are at the very top. I know who sent it and there's a real discount.
- The sale prices are clear: the Babyletto glider is $849.95 (was $999.99) and the crib mattress is $314.95. I can do the math.
- The Reward Dollars card block ("the card you love just got better") speaks to how I buy. I'm always on the highest-rewards card.
- TheDesignDesk, "100% free," fits someone doing two renovations at once. It's buried, though.
- The product grid is clean, with decent photography and visible prices.

## What's weak

- The hero sells a sweepstakes that requires effort, and the contest ends Oct 31, so there's no urgency. It also fights with the 50% off banner directly above it.
- There are two equal-weight pill buttons, "Write a Review" and "Shop Bestsellers". I can't tell what the main action is.
- The fall-leaves clearance block has no product in it. Pottery Barn's lookbooks at least show me the room.
- It's very long, with six or seven different ideas stacked up, and the strollers have nothing to do with my renovation.
- The nursery and baby products don't fit a 36-year-old renovating a second home. The Jenny Lind dresser is the only item I'd keep.
- It's the fifth C&B send today, after the Cotswolds Collection email and the sofas and sectionals email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sale and not the sweepstakes. Use a hero product with its price, and move the giveaway to a slim banner.
- 2. Use one primary CTA per block, and name what it links to.
- 3. Cut the page by about half. Keep the product grid, the rewards card and TheDesignDesk, and drop the app promo or the Jellycat block.
- 4. Cap the cadence. Five emails in one day will get this sender muted.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Marketo-style/MI-templated HTML build (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, MSO conditionals, responsive at 460px). The automated QA run found 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are about 10 content images with no alt attribute and zoom restrictions in the head. The missing-header warnings are probably capture artifacts, not real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 65 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they go through click-redirect domains. Link health and destination status are **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a67…&n=1` through `n=5` requests (LiveRamp) are embedded. The `s=` value is the same recipient hash as the `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` parameter on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels. The email therefore shares a per-recipient identifier with a third party. This is an observation for privacy review, not a rendering defect.
- **Repeated asset:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. The repeated per-recipient request is likely a dynamic/personalized content block, but the truncated source doesn't confirm that.
- **Tracking pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5c8cb637-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open pixels. They are expected, but Apple Mail Privacy Protection will inflate their opens.

- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` also appears in `html`, `body` and `*` rules, which disables text-size adjustment.
- **Invalid XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`. This is minor, but it is a validation error.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the shipped source. It is harmless but sloppy.
- **Redundant markup:** `format-detection` is declared 4 times, and there is a duplicate `<meta charset>` alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type. This is benign, and the second `format-detection` declaration may be ignored.
- **Image width rules:** `img {max-width:600px}` at ≥768px and `img {max-width:640px}` at ≥640px both apply. The 640px rule is overridden at ≥768px, so the width cap is inconsistent between those ranges.

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `*|…|*`) are visible in the available source. The per-recipient `mi_u` / `mi_mid` / `mi_cid` values are populated. The HTML was truncated, so tokens in the footer or body below the cut-off could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for large senders, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** It was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are **unknown**. It is likewise probably stripped by the relay, not a sender defect.
- **Sender domain:** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a consistent branded subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This **cannot be verified**. All 65 tracked links were skipped by the probe, and the truncated source shows no resolved landing URLs or UTM parameters. Redirect chains and destination alignment with the sweepstakes subject line ("$1,000 Gift Card") are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~10 content images, including the scene7 clearance GIF. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Revisit `text-size-adjust:none` on `*`.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>` to `&amp;`, and remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
4. Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating those warnings as defects.
5. Re-run the QA with a click-through resolver on a sample of the 65 tracked links to check destination status, UTM params and the sweepstakes landing page.
6. Review whether sharing the `mi_u` hash with `sr.rlcdn.com` (5 pixels) is intended, and whether the footer discloses it.
