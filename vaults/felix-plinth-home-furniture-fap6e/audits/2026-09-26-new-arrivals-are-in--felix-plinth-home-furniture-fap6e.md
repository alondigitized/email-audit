# New arrivals are in
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "New arrivals are in" — and CB2 leads with the Upholstery Event banner up top (the one I already gave 7/10 to yesterday), then pivots into "Just In / Already Trending" with a moody rug hero, then just... keeps going. The piece of design history, the onyx table, the green console, the tufted sofa, the fluted bed — this is six individual product hero shots stacked end to end, basically a lookbook disguised as an email. It's gorgeous to scroll through, I'll give it that, the styling is consistently good and every shot looks like a real room I'd want.
- But my thumb gets tired around shot four. There's no single offer, no urgency, nothing telling me why today specifically — this reads like CB2 batched their whole new-collection catalog into one email instead of picking a hero and running with it. Compare this to yesterday's Upholstery Event send: that one had ONE sofa, ONE offer (25% off), ONE reason to click. This one has six pieces of "design history" content and zero pricing until the "More For You" grid at the very bottom.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Onyx table or the tufted leather sofa, both genuinely caught my eye for the second-house living room. Would I forward it? No — this is a "save to the folder for later" email, not a "hey check this out" email. Read and archived.

## What's working

- The rug hero under "Just In / Already Trending" is a strong scroll-stopper — warm tones, close crop, feels editorial not stock.
- Naming each piece with a designer credit ("MEDA by Gianfranco Frattini," "RIO by Studio Anaysi") is a nice touch — Article does something similar with maker stories and it builds trust that these aren't disposable pieces.
- The "More For You" grid at the bottom finally shows actual prices ($499, $399, $299) — that's the first concrete number in the whole email, and it's buried.
- No render bugs, images all loaded clean, fonts consistent — better production quality than Crate & Barrel's bait-and-switch text-signup email from a couple days back.

## What's weak

- Six full-bleed hero product sections back to back with zero differentiation in CTA — it's "Shop New Arrivals" repeated, not six distinct reasons to click.
- No pricing anywhere near the hero pieces I actually want (the onyx table, the sofa) — I have to scroll all the way to the bottom grid to see any dollar figure, and even then it's for different products.
- This is the second CB2 email this week leaning on "design history" / vintage-reissue framing (their "Italian vintage reissues" send on 9/25 did the same designer-credit thing) — I'm starting to feel the formula repeat.
- No swatch/sample CTA anywhere, which matters a lot for a leather sofa or a lacquered console — Pottery Barn at least gestures at "shop the look" categorization even when it's cluttered.
- Loyalty math ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars") is stuck at the very bottom next to app-download — for a big-ticket furniture buy that's exactly the info I want up near the product, not in the footer graveyard.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to 2-3 hero pieces max, not six — pick the onyx table and the sofa as leads, cut the rest to the bottom grid where price is already showing.
- 2. Put a price or "starting at $X" under every hero shot — a $3,000 dining table and a $500 side table shouldn't get the identical treatment with zero price signal until the footer.
- 3. Move the "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" strip up near the top, right under the Upholstery Event banner — that's a real incentive for someone already in from an upholstery cart.
- 4. Add one swatch/material CTA ("Order a leather swatch") next to the tufted sofa — high-consideration furniture buyers like me want to touch the material before committing.

- **Subject Alt B:** `6 new pieces, starting at $299`

- **Preheader Alt B:** `See prices on this week's new arrivals`
