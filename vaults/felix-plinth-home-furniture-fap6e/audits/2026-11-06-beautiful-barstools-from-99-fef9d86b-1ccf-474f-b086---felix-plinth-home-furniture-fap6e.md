# Beautiful barstools, from $99
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Pottery Barn email about actual furniture. After four days of pillows, quilts and outlet deals, a barstool send is the first thing this week that fits my kitchen reno. The top banner says "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks", which is the shipping clarity I want on big pieces. The render is tall but organized: Upholstered, Leather and Wood barstools each get a hero and two products.
- I'm still annoyed by cadence. This is the fifth Pottery Barn send in about three days, and the bottom half goes back to filler: Christmas Shop, stockings, a design services banner, the app, the card and Affirm. The "from $99" in the subject is also small and hard to find in the hero. I'd click through for the barstools, and I'd save it to my kitchen folder. I wouldn't forward it, because nothing in it is a deal worth sharing.

## What's working

- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner at the top answers the first furniture question I have.
- Splitting barstools into Upholstered, Leather and Wood makes it easy to find my style. I'm leaning toward the wood Folsom or Summerville.
- Each category has a lifestyle kitchen photo followed by two product shots with names (Layton, Montclair, Baldwin, Skylar). That works like a mini-catalog.
- The "Shop All Bar & Counter Stools" button is clear, and the "More Kitchen Updates" row (Lighting, Runners, Shelves, Baskets) fits a reno.
- Free Design Services with "Book a Free Appointment" is useful for my two-house project. It's also a better CTA than the 11/06 send had.

## What's weak

- The "from $99" price is only a small badge on the hero. Per-product prices aren't visible, so I can't compare.
- The fifth Pottery Barn send in three days is too much. Crate & Barrel Kids and CB2 have me in the same snooze.
- The Christmas Shop and stocking tiles in "Recommended Just For You" are off-topic. The dining table and bed are closer to my project, but they feel like generic filler.
- The footer stack of the app, the 10% back card, Affirm and the legal fine print is long. The rewards math is buried instead of tied to a barstool purchase.
- There are no swatch, sample, dimension or seat-height details, which matter for counter versus bar stools.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put per-stool prices and counter/bar height options under each product, and make "from $99" the headline.
- 2. Show a clear fabric or finish swatch row, with sample ordering, to match the palette consistency I care about.
- 3. Replace the Christmas filler with project-relevant items: counters, pendant lights and island pieces.
- 4. Spell out the card rewards for this purchase (for example, 10% back on a four-stool set).
- 5. Pace sends. After the 11/07 outlet email, this should have waited.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Beautiful barstools, from $99"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px sliced-image layout with 59 click-redirect links and many `<img>` elements missing alt text. Three images load over `http://`. The recipient's email address appears in plain text in tracking-pixel URLs, one of them over cleartext HTTP. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and later content could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 59 links on tracking or redirect domains were skipped. The header logo link is `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`, an opaque token, so the destinations cannot be verified from this data.
- **Tracking pixels are not marked as pixels.**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` has alt text `1`.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/36290d5b-…` has no alt text.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…` is loaded as an image.
  - The checker flags all three as images missing alt text. If the pixels have no explicit 1×1 dimensions or `display:none`, they may also render visibly.
- **Cleartext pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` is an open pixel over HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients, which would undercount opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** About 50 images have no alt text. The `slices_01`–`17` barstool images and the footer `slices_01`–`18` images are sliced images that are likely wrapped in links. Image-blocking clients and screen readers will get no content or link purpose. Product images are also affected, for example `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`. A few hero and banner images lack alt too, such as `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`. The header logo is the exception, with `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Mixed content:** Three images use `http://`: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg` (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`) and the `svopn` pixel. These may be blocked or show warnings in some clients.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **Stray code in a style block:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP-style statements: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, …"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. These are template leakage and invalid CSS. Parsers will ignore them, but they add bytes and can break the rule that follows in strict clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Layout tables:** Only some layout tables have `role="presentation"`. The outer wrapper tables and the spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) do not.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `spacer_0.gif` is hosted at `edm.westelm.com`, which makes this email depend on another brand's domain.
- **Dead CSS:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are in the stylesheet, but no matching elements appear in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** are visible in the truncated source.
- **Email address in URLs:** The recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is passed unhashed as a query parameter in two places:
  - `coreemail.html?email=…`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=…`

  The second is over HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy and server logs. This is a privacy and security concern.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both headers were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the sender does omit them, there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`. This is a relay limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not verifiable, because the source is truncated before the footer.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,356 of 15,607 characters (about 73%) are URLs, over the 70% warning threshold. Long tracking URLs inflate the text part and can raise spam-filter scores.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Landing destinations could not be checked. All content links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`, and the redirects were not followed. The few direct URLs are product images on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which are assets rather than landing pages. The QA data has no evidence of UTM parameters, so they are neither confirmed nor flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, including the sliced images and any in-link text. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Move all `http://` images, including the `svopn` pixel, to `https://`.
3. Stop passing the plain-text recipient email in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque or hashed ID.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block and fill in `<title>`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw headers of the original message, outside the relay. Then check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to confirm landing pages and UTM parameters. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and physical address.
