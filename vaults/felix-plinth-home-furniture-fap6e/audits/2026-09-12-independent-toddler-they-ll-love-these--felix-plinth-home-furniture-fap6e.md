- Wrong persona entirely — I'm 36, renovating two houses, not shopping toddler furniture; this belongs in my wife's inbox, not mine.
- Zero pricing or offer anywhere in the whole scroll — no %, no $, no "starting at" — just product after product with "Shop the ___" buttons.
