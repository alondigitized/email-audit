# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW! Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth or seventh Crate & Barrel send I've had in about three days. I've seen "Double Rewards" in some form at least four times, going back to the 11/10 "Double Rewards on 100s of bestsellers. Don't miss it!" blast. My gut reaction was to snooze it. The red strip at the top says "order by 11/19 at 11:59 pm PT to get it in time for Thanksgiving," and that's the most useful line in the email. It's the only thing that treats me like someone with a deadline.
- The hero is "starts today! up to 30% off Early Black Friday" over a living room photo. Two weeks ago Pottery Barn was running "up to 70% off across the home," so 30% looks timid next to it. Below the hero it's the usual stack of kitchen and entertaining tiles, then a Thanksgiving menu, then a Double Rewards repeat. I'm renovating two houses and want furniture and lighting. Furniture only gets one small tile.
- I'd open it out of habit, maybe tap into furniture, and not forward it. It goes in the Pinterest folder only if the sofa tile is worth saving, and I doubt it is.

## What's working

- The "order by 11/19 to get it in time for Thanksgiving" strip is a real, credible deadline, not fake urgency.
- "Up to 30% off" in the hero is easy to read, and a single "Shop Now" button sits right under it.
- The "last Double Rewards this year" callout is the one thing that matters to a rewards-card guy like me. It's stated twice, with the Nov. 17 end date.
- The "top kitchen deals (they ship free!)" section shows real prices, like Sale $149.95 against Reg. $199.95. That's more concrete than the tiles above it.
- The "Book a Free Appointment" design desk module is a good touch for someone doing two renovations.

## What's weak

- Two of the four category tiles, "up to 30% off furniture" and "up to 30% off holiday decor," have text that's hard to read against the busy photos. The furniture label especially looks washed out.
- Six of the main sections are kitchen or cooking. That does nothing for a lighting-and-furniture renovation buyer.
- "Double Rewards" shows up in the red hero and again mid-email with the same "last Double Rewards this year" headline, and I've seen it in four earlier sends. It's repetitive.
- The fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny. I'm not going to zoom in to figure out the exclusions, and it makes me doubt the "up to 30%" claim.
- The email is very long, and the "we love these for you" recommendations are small and low on social proof. They look generic, not tailored to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with furniture and lighting, not cookware. Give me one hero product with a real sale price, as Wayfair does with its rug grid, and fewer tiles about everything.
- 2. Cut the repeated Double Rewards blocks to one, and put a rewards-math line beside it: "Spend $2,000 on a sofa, earn $X."
- 3. Add free-shipping and delivery timing for big pieces. Pottery Barn's "in-stock furniture delivers by..." is exactly what I look for.
- 4. Shrink the fine print into an expandable "details" link and make the tile labels readable.
- 5. Send this less often. After five or more sends in two days, a promotion this size loses its meaning.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style template (`<!--V2-->`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com`) with 70 redirect-wrapped links. The automated QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The main confirmed problems are about 30 images with no `alt` attribute, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and header checks that could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 70 links were skipped because they are tracking or click-redirect domains, so none of the final landing URLs were checked for 404s or redirect chains. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) load in the body. They carry a per-recipient `s=` hash.
- **Other tracking images:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d498b6dc-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are the open pixels. Several `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images are per-recipient dynamic content.
- **Per-recipient parameters:** `mi_u` and `s=` appear in image URLs. If the email is forwarded, those identifiers go with it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 30 `<img>` elements with no `alt`.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` include `6100bbef-…jpg`, `d8683d75-…jpg`, `84b30380-…jpg` and `24335f96-…gif`.
  - Dynamic product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/…` include `560d850e7e517ede.png` and `789e7381ff44be74.png`.
  - Blocked-image clients and screen readers will get nothing for these.
  - The pixel images (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`) should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so the empty alt is deliberate.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `body`. This prevents user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times, and both `http-equiv` and `charset` content-type declarations are present. This is redundant but harmless.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source (typo in the original).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are resolved. The remainder of the HTML was not available to check.

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA did not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **not confirmed** as a sender defect. Check the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The QA note attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.
- **From domain:** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image domain `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. This looks consistent.
