- **Five identical third-party pixels** load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…1015f5a8&n=1` through `n=5`. This is a LiveRamp domain. Five copies of the same pixel add requests without adding signal.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 37 `<img>` elements have no alt text.** These are the QA warnings on this email:
  - 22 hosted on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (plus one `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`).
  - 7 dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, likely product recommendation tiles.
  - The remaining 7 are tracking pixels (5 × `rlcdn` `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv…/o/1d45dad2-…`). These should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
  - The QA output appears cut off mid-URL, so the real count may be higher.
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing is blocked:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on `html`, `body` and `*`, and `*{ line-height:100% }` is applied globally. This hurts users who rely on text scaling.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has a raw `&`, which should be `&amp;` under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
- **Duplicate and redundant meta tags:**
  - `charset` is declared twice (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).
  - `format-detection` is declared four times.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the markup, with a typo.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap, so 600px wins at 768px and up only through source order.
