## What's working

- The Double Rewards deadline (Nov. 12, 2026 end date in the red banner) is clear, and rewards are how I choose which card to use.
- The "up to 50% off furniture" and "up to 50% off bedding & bath" tiles are concrete and tappable.
- The red color blocks break up the long scroll, and the repeated "Shop the Sale" buttons are easy to hit with a thumb.
- "We love these for you" shows real products with names and prices (Nuna PIPA, Babyletto Kiwi chair), which is the product detail I want.
- The TheDesignDesk appointment is a useful service angle for someone doing two renovations.

## What's weak

- The sender is Kids, so the whole thing is nursery-and-stroller. It misses my renovation focus.
- The hero headline is low-contrast and small, and the offer text is buried in the photo.
- There's too much happening: furniture, baby gear, rugs, clearance, gifts, a giveaway, the credit card and the app. Nothing leads.
- This is a near-duplicate of "Double Rewards + Up to 50% off" sends I've seen all week from the same brand. Same offer, different outfit.
- Fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. The "up to" offers aren't honest-feeling when the exclusions run that long.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Either Double Rewards or the 50% off, not both plus clearance plus a giveaway. Pottery Barn's "Our favorite EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" at least has one grid and one idea.
- 2. Segment. I've bought furniture, not baby gear. Send me the main C&B line, not Kids. CB2's "UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS" lets the headline carry the offer, and that's the cleaner approach.
- 3. Put the rewards math in the top strip as a number, like "$20 back per $100", not as a phrase.
- 4. Make the hero headline larger and put it on a solid background.
- 5. Move TheDesignDesk higher for the renovation crowd and cut the app and card blocks.
