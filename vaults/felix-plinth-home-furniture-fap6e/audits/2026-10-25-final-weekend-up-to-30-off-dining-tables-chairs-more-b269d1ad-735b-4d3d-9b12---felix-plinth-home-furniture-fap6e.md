## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a Movable Intelligence template on an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It has 30 images without alt text and several template and markup defects. The automated compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay. Only the head of the source is available, so body links, footer and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing:** 67 tracking links were skipped (click-redirect domains), so none were checked for resolution, redirect chains or destinations.
- **Shared identifier:** the `mi_u` value on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `.../up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` is `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`. The same 64-character hex string is passed as `s=` to `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, a LiveRamp domain. A per-recipient identifier is therefore going to a third party from the email body.
- **Duplicate pixels:** the LiveRamp pixel fires five times (`n=1` to `n=5`) with the same `s=`. This adds requests with no apparent benefit.
- **Open pixel:** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1510ebef-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a separate open-tracking pixel with no extension.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (30 images):** the QA check flags 30 images, which is the main accessibility finding.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` and `/m/47/` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `ef861136-...jpg`) have no alt text. These need real descriptions, especially any that carry offer text.
  - The scene7 asset `2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide_Tertiary-` also has no alt text.
  - The seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images are dynamic modules, probably product or recommendation content. They also have no alt text.
  - The five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel are tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - The check cannot tell a missing `alt` attribute from `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, and the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in several places. Both hurt low-vision users.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - The `<title>` contains an unescaped `&` (`Crate & Barrel`) under an XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed alongside the XHTML-style `http-equiv` tag, so the head mixes HTML5 and XHTML syntax.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with overlapping values.
- **Conflicting rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The body is not visible, so this covers the head only.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN, unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says the relay may strip them, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw message headers at the receiving mailbox before treating it as a failure.
- **WARN, unverified:** `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. The `image.` and `mi.` hosts are separate subdomains, which is normal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. Of the 67 tracking links, none were resolved, and no landing URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated source. The subject promises "Up to 30% off dining tables, chairs & more", but landing-page alignment cannot be checked without resolved destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit against the full HTML and raw headers. This is needed to verify the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, destination URLs and UTMs, and the real `List-Unsubscribe` and `Authentication-Results` values.
2. Resolve a sample of the 67 redirect links with a followed-redirect probe, and confirm the final landing pages and UTM parameters.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the content images (`/m/1/*.jpg`, `/m/47/*.png`, scene7 asset). Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels, plus explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
5. Drop the redundant `format-detection` metas, the dead `max-width:600px` rule and the `[IMPUT HERE...]` comment. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in the `<title>`.
6. Review the repeated LiveRamp pixel and the `mi_u` value passed to `sr.rlcdn.com`. Deduplicate the pixel calls if they are redundant, and confirm the third-party sharing is covered in the privacy disclosure.
