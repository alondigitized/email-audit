## What's working

- The Doona X hero is clean. One product, one "Shop Now" button, and the eye lands there first.
- The banner says "The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, Up to 50% off", so there's a concrete offer at the top.
- The three feature blocks are readable. They cover recline positions, travel modes and five colors, with a swatch row at the end.
- The "more to love" grid explains height, safety and trunk space in short sentences.
- The Crate Card block and "The Design Desk, book a free appointment" are real trust and service cues. The Design Desk is the one thing in here that fits how I shop.

## What's weak

- The subject promises news about a stroller. The offer in the banner is about bedroom and nursery furniture. The two don't connect, and the 50% doesn't clearly apply to the Doona.
- This is the second Kids-sender send today, with a nursery event send before it. That's cadence fatigue, and I'm in a permanent C&B family snooze. Wayfair has also been in my inbox sixteen times this week.
- The email is very long. The hero is baby gear, and the first thing that applies to a home renovation, the Design Desk, is near the bottom.
- Every button says "Shop Now", "Learn More" or "Shop All Doona". None of them tells me what I'm getting.
- There's no deadline. The earlier sofa send said "ends today", but this one has no urgency.

- 1. Segment this. Send the stroller email only to people who've browsed baby gear or have a registry. Everyone else should get the furniture and the Design Desk.
- 2. Put the offer in the hero and tie it to the product. Say what the Doona costs or what the 50% covers.
- 3. Cut the email down. Keep the Doona, one nursery block and the Design Desk.
- 4. Make the buttons specific, like "Shop Doona X" and "See nursery dressers". Add a deadline for the event.
- 5. Add a way to suppress the lookalike sends. If I got the nursery email this morning, don't send me another from the same sender.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Doona's travel system just got even better!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed issue is missing alt text on about 35 images. Header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and link hrefs are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **73 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Repeated beacons:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` is a LiveRamp pixel firing 5 times.
  - These add third-party requests and a per-recipient identifier (`mi_u`, `s=`) in URL query strings. They are not broken, but the redundancy is unnecessary overhead.
- No broken links were reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 35 images flagged.** The content-image candidates are:
  - `…/m/1/eeb8adae-5b4e-4a30-bd6d-f75109be3722.jpg`
  - `…/m/1/a609fd03-9bac-4f06-857a-10af8d1f76c2.jpg`
  - `…/m/1/8964c2f1-1720-4ec6-958f-02a8eac1b096.gif`
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - About 15 more `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` assets.
  - If these carry the product or CTA content, image-blocking clients and screen readers get nothing. The QA output doesn't distinguish an absent `alt` attribute from `alt=""`.
- **Decorative and tracking images flagged:**
  - The spacer `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - The pixels `f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/f08d47ff-…`.
  - These should carry an explicit `alt=""` (plus `role="presentation"` for the spacer). They are low severity.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility regression.
- **Markup validity:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, which is invalid in an XHTML doctype (it should be `&amp;`).
  - Duplicate `format-detection` metas.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - All of these are cosmetic and unlikely to break rendering.
- **CSS:** the universal selector `*{… line-height:100%}` and the global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` can cause unintended overrides in some clients. The `img{max-width:600px}` rule (min-width 768px) is overridden by the later `img{max-width:640px}` rule (min-width 640px), so it never takes effect. No rendering failure is evidenced.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found**, and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so it's unconfirmed. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements can't be verified for this send.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status are unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the From address.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they're **unverified**.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No hrefs were visible in the truncated source, and the 73 tracking links weren't resolved. Visible URLs use Movable Ink parameters (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_u`) rather than UTM. Whether UTMs survive the redirect to the landing page is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/m/1/*.jpg` hero and product assets. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Re-capture the raw message, not the relay copy, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC).
3. Resolve a sample of the 73 tracked links to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Escape the ampersand in `<title>`, and deduplicate the meta tags and repeated beacon pixels.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer address, the unsubscribe link, and the hrefs.
