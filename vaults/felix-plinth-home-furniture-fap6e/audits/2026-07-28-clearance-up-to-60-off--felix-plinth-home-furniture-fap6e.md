# 👉 Clearance 👉 UP TO 60% OFF 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- So this is at least an "outdoor clearance" theme, which is a real seasonal moment in late July — better than another baking dishes or awnings word-salad. But it's still Wayfair being Wayfair: a wall-to-wall grid of category tiles (rugs, planters, lighting, fire pits, storage, dining, grills) each shouting its own percentage off, with zero actual product detail. I renovate two houses at once and I'd genuinely consider outdoor furniture and lighting right now, but this email doesn't sell me anything — it sells me a directory.
- The "picked just for you" block briefly got my attention with a real lantern and a fire pit photo, but then it's immediately undercut by the fact that literally everything below it is "picked for you" too, apparently, since the whole email is just more grid tiles. I'm not opening this on my commute and I'm sure not forwarding it — there's no single hero item worth sending a friend. This gets skimmed for two seconds and archived.

## What's working

- "60% OFF Outdoor Clearance" is at least a real seasonal category, not the manufactured "sale within a sale" branding Wayfair loves (see 7/27's "Baking Dishes ⭕ Up to 80% Off ⭕").
- The lantern, fire pit, and outdoor lounge chair photos in "picked just for you" are decent lifestyle shots — better production value than the flat product-on-white tiles further down.
- Klarna/Affymo/Afterpay financing bar is relevant — I'm mid-renovation on two properties, so 0% APR messaging on furniture-scale purchases actually lands for me.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" is a clear, honest threshold, not buried fine print.

## What's weak

- Every single CTA tile is the same orange-on-cream template repeated a dozen times — rugs, planters, lighting, fire pits, storage, dining, grills. My eye has nowhere to land because everything is visually equal weight.
- There's no single hero SKU with a price, dimensions, or material — for furniture-scale purchases I need to know if a rug is 8x10 or a fire pit is propane vs. wood before I'll click anything.
- A ride-on kids' Jeep toy and a chicken coop show up in the same grid as fire pits and rugs — I don't have kids or chickens, and it makes the "picked just for you" labeling feel like a lie.
- No color-palette or material consistency shown for the rugs/furniture — CB2's "SO MUCH NEW" (my 9/10) actually shows fabric texture and finish close-up; this is all thumbnail-sized product-on-white.
- This is at least the fifth or sixth Wayfair send I've caught this week (after "Baking Dishes," "Kitchen Mats," "Awnings," "Wall Décor"), and the layout template hasn't changed once — it's the same purple-banner, orange-tile scaffolding with a new word swapped in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero product (the lantern or the fire pit from "picked just for you") and lead the email with a real price, dimensions, and a single clear CTA — stop treating every category as equally important.
- 2. Cut the grid from ~14 tiles to 4-5 max. Article and CB2 both prove a tighter, curated send outperforms a directory dump.
- 3. Remove the kids' Jeep and chicken coop from "picked just for you" — if this is supposed to be personalized, it should reflect an outdoor/renovation shopper, not a random assortment.
- 4. Show one swatch or material close-up (rug weave, fire pit finish) — I can't judge outdoor furniture quality from a 100px thumbnail.
- 5. Vary the send template — five nearly identical purple-banner layouts in one week is training me to stop opening them at all.

# Technical Email Audit: Wayfair "Clearance Up to 60% Off"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Wayfair batch marketing template (Unbounce/ESP-style responsive HTML) with heavy tracking pixel usage and multiple accessibility/compliance gaps in the automated scan; 57% of automated checks passed with 3 warning categories and 0 hard failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 67 total links were probed (55 deprioritized as footer/utility/social); of those probed, 6 returned **HTTP 429 (rate limited)** rather than a confirmed pass/fail, including primary CTAs: `Shop Sale` → `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, the hero image link, and both app-download links (`https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`). These are inconclusive, not confirmed broken.
- **4 links were never probed** due to the 70s time budget, including a product link (`wayfair-product-L6114-K~W100203863.html`) and a grill product page — status unknown.
- All probed/tracked links carry consistent campaign params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs=cs7`, `refid=MKTEML_138186`, `maiid=15872`, `emlid=101`), suggesting a coherent single-send tracking scheme with no mismatched IDs observed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 tracking pixels** (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) are served over **plain `http://`**, not `https://` — these will likely be blocked or stripped by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies (Gmail, Apple Mail), silently degrading open/impression tracking.
- **~24 images missing `alt` text**, including the primary hero banner (`0728_OutdoorClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`), all star-rating icons (`stars_9.png`/`stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`, appearing ~13×), promo banners, and the beacon/pixel images. This degrades the experience for screen-reader users and for anyone with images blocked (a common default in Outlook/Gmail), since no fallback text renders.
- Responsive CSS is present and reasonably thorough (`@media max-width: 480px` rules for buttons, titles, product rec grid, footer links), including a `min-width: 100vw` Gmail/Outlook mobile fix — no structural rendering issues found.
- Uses a custom `@font-face` (Sofia, served via HTTPS from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`) with an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for Outlook — correctly implemented.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens were found in the visible source or flagged by QA. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** in the captured headers — QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence, but as observed it cannot be confirmed present.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 cannot be confirmed supported from available headers.

- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in the footer/utility link set), satisfying the baseline CAN-SPAM body requirement even though the header-level mechanism is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All primary CTA and product links resolve to `wayfair.com` (e.g. `/daily-sales`, `/daily-sales/indoor%2foutdoor-rug-clearance~e369270.html`) — consistent with the sending domain (`members.wayfair.com`), no domain mismatch.
