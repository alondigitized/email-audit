- The "48 HR" countdown-style hero at least gives me a deadline, which is more than some of Wayfair's recent sends had.
- Free & easy delivery over $35 is a real, relevant trust signal for furniture-weight purchases.
- 0% APR financing through 9/24/2026 is genuinely useful for someone financing two renovations, if they'd surface it.
- No broken images this time — first Wayfair email this week I've opened where the hero graphic actually rendered instead of a blank white box.

- Zero personalization — this reads like a category dump, not "picked for Felix." Pottery Barn's "holiday preview" send at least felt art-directed toward a lifestyle; this is a spreadsheet with photos.
- The category I actually need (rugs, closet storage) is stuck at the very bottom, below fitness equipment and tableware I'll never click.

- Star ratings with no review counts or product names attached — just decoration, not proof.
- This is the same purple-banner, same-skeleton structure as three other Wayfair emails I've gotten in the last three days. The fatigue is real.

- 2. Move rugs, closet storage, and furniture categories to the top; cut fitness/tableware/cooling entirely from a "picked for you" targeting this deep into a renovation.
