# Your 2 fave words: FREE SHIPPING!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — third or fourth send I've clocked from this sub-brand this stretch — and this one's just a straight free-shipping flex: "it. all. ships. free." over a nursery/kids linen shot. No percentage off, no dollar figure, just shipping. I don't have kids, so nursery gliders, baby car seats, and "Nuna PIPA" travel systems are a hard scroll-past no matter how clean the grid looks. This is the wrong list for me, full stop.
- The "we love these for you" personalization block is almost funny given it's a $1,599 dresser and a $499 nursery chair — nothing about that reads like it was built from my browsing history, it reads like it was built from someone else's registry. I'm not clicking, not forwarding (unless it's to my buddy who just had a kid), marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The "it. all. ships. free." hero headline is at least unambiguous about the one offer in play.
- The 8-tile grid (bedding, gear, cuddles, curtains, throw pillows) is genuinely clean layout work — better organized than the sprawl I've seen from some CB2 sends this week.
- Product photography is consistent, good color grading, on-brand with the rest of the Crate & Barrel family look.
- The card upsell ("Reward Dollars") at least signals a loyalty program exists, even if it's buried near the bottom.

## What's weak

- This is a Crate & Kids nursery/baby email, not Crate & Barrel proper — I'm mid-renovation on two houses with zero kids in the picture, so backpacks, car seats, and a "baby registry" footer link are just noise.
- No percentage or dollar offer anywhere — "free shipping" alone doesn't move me the way Crate & Barrel's "up to 60% off clearance" sends (which I audited twice yesterday) at least tried to.
- "we love these for you" personalization is a swing and a miss — a $1,599 dresser and a nursery glider are not "for me" in any real sense.
- Way too many CTAs stacked (Shop All Free Shipping x2, Shop Bundles, Shop Now, Learn More, Download Now, Book a Free Appointment) — by the time I hit "TheDesignDesk" appointment booking I've lost the plot on what this email even wants me to do.
- Sender name "Crate & Kids" buried the real story — I almost swiped past thinking it was spam from an unfamiliar sender before recognizing the parent brand in the logo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids sends entirely — this is a targeting failure, not a creative one. If Crate & Barrel knows I'm doing a whole-home renovation with no baby registry signal, this shouldn't have landed in my inbox at all.
- 2. Cut the CTA stack from six to two max — pick "Shop Free Shipping" and one secondary, drop the appointment booking and app download asks from a shipping-promo email.
- 3. If this must go to non-parent segments, lead with a category that applies (curtains, throw pillows, bedding fabric finder) instead of burying it as tile 7 of 8.
- 4. Fix "we love these for you" — either make it real personalization or relabel it "trending now" so it doesn't read as a broken promise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping on everything for the nursery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Zero shipping cost, all week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New bedding, gear, and cuddly favorites — all ship free`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Backpack bundles, bedding fabrics, and more — free shipping sitewide`
