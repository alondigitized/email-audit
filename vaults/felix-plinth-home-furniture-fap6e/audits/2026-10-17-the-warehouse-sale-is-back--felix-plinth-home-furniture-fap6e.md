# THE WAREHOUSE SALE IS BACK
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a CB2 email with a real offer in the hero. "Warehouse Sale, up to 60% off" is the clearest headline I've had from them this week. It beats 10/16's "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE," where the subject promised a sale and the hero showed a woman carrying a tiger painting over a "FIND A STORE" button. Here the subject and hero match. The category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Decor, Rugs, Shop All) are stacked and easy to hit with a thumb.
- Then the email loses focus. After the hero there's a second 30%-off dining banner, a Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz sweepstakes, and a four-up grid for the app, rewards, gift list and texts. I got the "semi-annual dining event" on 10/15, so that banner is a rerun. It also undercuts the 60% headline. I'm furnishing two houses and I'd tap Furniture or Rugs. I'd also want to know what's actually in the sale, and there isn't a single product in the whole email.
- I'd click once, probably Furniture, and then bounce to see if there's anything real. I wouldn't forward it. If CB2 sent this and stopped after the hero, I'd be happier. Pottery Barn's rug email today at least showed me a rug.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Up to 60% off" in huge type with "last-chance markdowns" under it. I get it in one second.
- The five stacked category buttons match how I shop. I go to Furniture and Rugs first for the bare floors.
- The "Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars at CB2 and Crate & Barrel" tile speaks to my card-stacking habit.
- The gift list tile ("weddings, house warmings") is relevant, since I'm doing two houses.
- The sale email arrives after the Floor Sample Sale confusion, so at least the offer is now visible.

## What's weak

- There's no product anywhere. "Curated last-chance markdowns" is a promise with nothing to see, and it's a weak spot next to Wayfair, whose clearance email puts the ice maker tile right under the hero.
- The dining banner is a rerun of the 10/15 "Dinner party must-haves" send. The 30% figure after the 60% headline muddies the offer, and the "SHOP THE SALE" button doesn't say which sale.
- The Swizz Beatz Casa Dean sweepstakes takes a big chunk of the email. It's a texting-signup giveaway for a collection I haven't seen anything from.
- The legal fine print is tiny and dense. It says 60% applies to "select" items, and it mentions Oct 28 and Nov 4 dates. There's no visible deadline up top.
- I'm in a permanent CB2 and Crate & Barrel snooze. This is the 20th-ish CB2 email this week, and the "Holiday, meet high design" send was only hours ago.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put four or five real sale products under the hero, each with a struck-through price and a sale price. Furniture and rugs should lead.
- 2. Put the end date in the hero ("Ends Oct 28"), not in the fine print.
- 3. Drop the dining banner or change it, since I've already seen it. Move the sweepstakes and the app tiles below the products.
- 4. Make the buttons say what's behind them, like "Shop sofas up to 60% off" and "Shop rugs."
- 5. Space out the sends. Three CB2 emails in two days dilutes every one of them.

# Technical Audit: CB2, "THE WAREHOUSE SALE IS BACK"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has accessibility gaps (7 content images with no alt text, `maximum-scale=1`) and 10+ insecure `http://` Dotomi tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers were not captured. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what the source and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 62 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains, and 404s are untested.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** Eleven Dotomi (`dotomi.com`) pixels load over plain `http://` and have no alt text:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Mail clients that proxy images, or that block mixed content, can drop these. Tracking and cookie-sync would then fail silently.
- **Many third-party beacons:** The email also carries `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/3f009f7d-...?mi_cid=...`. Both lack alt text. The user-level hash in `mi_u=` is expected for this kind of pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (7 or more):** All are hosted at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `7f459882-…jpg`

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`** before send. Check the dynamic-content or data-extension mapping, and add a fallback so an empty value suppresses the block.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to the eight hosted `.jpg` content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
