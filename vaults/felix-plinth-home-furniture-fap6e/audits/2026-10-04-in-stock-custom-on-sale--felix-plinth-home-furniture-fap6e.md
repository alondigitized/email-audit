# IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and this is the twelfth send in about six days. The sender name "CB2 SALE" at least tells me what's coming. The hero is a green sectional under "The Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off." I already got that on 10/3 ("Up to 25% off rarely-on-sale upholstery"). So the subject's "IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE." is a new headline on the same creative. I felt like I was getting a rerun.
- Underneath, the email does real work for someone renovating two houses. Ten free swatches is the best thing in it. Five named sectionals and sofas are lined up with swatch dots and designer credits. Pottery Barn's quilt email on the same day had one idea and sold it cleanly. This one has four or five ideas. After the upholstery run there's a $5,000 design-services refresh, a Fall Design Sale up to 50% off, and a lighting tile.
- Would I click? Maybe the swatch button, since I'm actually buying sofas. Would I forward it? No. I'd file it in the Pinterest folder at best, and only because the room shots are nice. I'm not going to forward the fifth CB2 upholstery email this week.

## What's working

- "Order 10 free swatches" sits right under the hero. Swatches are how I de-risk a sectional, and it sits right next to the offer.
- The "In stock and rarely on sale" line answers my biggest furniture question, which is lead time, before I ask it.
- Each product tile has the name, designer credit and fabric swatch dots. That tells me custom options exist without me clicking.
- The room photography is moody and well lit. It reads like a catalog, not a clearance bin, and it beats Wayfair's thumbnail grid.
- The free design appointment block is a real service for someone doing two houses at once.

## What's weak

- The offer is the same up-to-25% upholstery event I got on 10/3. I'm not seeing anything new except the headline.
- "Up to 25%" doesn't tell me which sectionals are discounted or by how much. No prices anywhere, so I can't tell if the Faible is a $3K or $6K sofa.
- The Fall Design Sale (up to 50% off) and the lighting tile near the bottom compete with the sofa story, and the page feels long.
- The "$5,000 refresh" block has text over a busy photo and is hard to read at phone size. I'd have to zoom.
- No shipping or lead-time detail for custom pieces. "In stock" and "custom" are in the subject, but I don't see delivery dates in the body.
- The footer rewards and app blocks are generic and add length. They also tell me nothing about my card math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one story: upholstery plus swatches. Drop the Fall Design Sale and lighting tiles, or send them separately. Pottery Barn's single-idea quilt send is the model.
- 2. Put a price on every product tile ("from $2,499") and the sale amount. I make decisions on numbers.
- 3. Add a lead-time line per piece, for example "ships in 2 weeks" for in-stock and "6-8 weeks" for custom. That's the real furniture question.
- 4. Vary the creative from 10/3. If it's the same event, change the hero or tell me what's new, such as new fabrics or limited in-stock quantities.
- 5. Make the design-services block legible with a solid text panel instead of text over the photo.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sectionals in stock, 25% off, ship fast`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10 free swatches + up to 25% off sofas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Faible, Berkeley, Aldine and more. Order swatches before the sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Custom or in stock, see prices and delivery dates inside.`

# Technical Audit: CB2 "IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE."

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, is left unresolved in the output. It also has widespread missing alt text and about 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi pixels. The compliance and authentication findings are mostly unverifiable because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 65 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** 11 third-party pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients that don't proxy images may block these as mixed content, and `pub1–10` are cookie-sync calls to ad-tech endpoints.
- **Possible template error:** `@font-face` URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). This is probably harmless, but it depends on server normalization.
- **Cross-brand font hosting:** The `HNW05` fonts are hosted on `crateandbarrel.com` (a different domain from the sender).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 10 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt attribute:
  - `0f72982f-…jpg`, `d0f18edd-…jpg`, `f51d611c-…jpg`, `8d55d508-…jpg`, `b9c08168-…jpg`
  - `f4f8cd5c-…jpg`, `a644744c-…jpg`, `34604254-…jpg`, `326a21c4-…jpg`, `fe23441b-…png`
  - With images off, or for screen readers, the content is invisible or announced as a filename.
- **Pixels without alt:** `mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/78488f97-…` and the Dotomi pixels also lack alt. For tracking pixels, `alt=""` is the correct value, and its absence still gets flagged.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Font stack:** `@font-face` for "Lineto Brown Sub" lists the `.eot` source before the `woff2` sources in the first declaration. Custom fonts are ignored in most email clients anyway, so the fallback is `Helvetica, sans-serif`.
- **Template residue:** The leftover placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` shows the template was not cleaned up.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, the VML/MSO conditionals and the 418px mobile breakpoint are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` rendered literally. It looks like an unresolved dynamic reviews or SKU-list block from the ESP or Movable Ink. Depending on where it sits in the markup, it could display as visible text or leave an empty module. The truncated source doesn't show its location.
- No other unresolved tokens were reported.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. Sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, with images on `image.mail.cb2.com`. Alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated HTML doesn't reach the footer, so these can't be verified.
- **Third-party tracking pixels:** The Dotomi cookie-sync pixels (`pub1–10.php`) are ad-tech tracking embedded in the email. This is a privacy-disclosure concern, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified. All 65 tracking links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't include the href values.
- The only visible tracking parameters are Movable Ink (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) on pixels and Dotomi (`dtm_cid=2437`). No UTMs were observed in the available data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`:** Check the ESP/dynamic-content block, add a fallback for empty data, and add a pre-send check that fails on `%%…%%` patterns.
2. **Add alt text** to the 10 `image.mail.cb2.com` images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`, Dotomi).
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS.** Confirm the 11 cookie-sync calls are needed, because they add third-party requests on every open.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta to allow zoom.
5. **Re-run the header checks on the raw message**, not through the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. **Resolve the 65 tracking links** with a redirect-following probe to verify destinations, status codes and UTM presence. Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and address.
7. **Clean up the template:** remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment and the double-slash font paths.
