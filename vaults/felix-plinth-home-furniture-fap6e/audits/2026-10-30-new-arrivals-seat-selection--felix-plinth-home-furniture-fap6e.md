## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard QA failures (0 issues, 4 warnings, 50% pass rate). The warnings are three images missing alt text, a plain-text part that is mostly URLs, and missing `List-Unsubscribe` and authentication headers. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links and images go through ESP redirect wrappers. These are `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...~~/AAQVbhA~/...` and `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`. That is two different tracking domains in one message.
- Only 12 of 32 links were probed, and 20 were skipped as footer, utility or social links. The QA output reports no broken links among the probed set, so destinations for the skipped 20 are unverified.
- The plain-text part is 2494 of 3481 characters URLs (about 72%), which is over the 70% threshold. The long encoded tracking URLs make this worse.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images.** Their src values are:
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Z-r0d7wE-eTzR-yq7xqeGg~~/AAQVbhA~/sViLi4PE...`
  - `links.email.article.com/s/eo/dqdR9TIgcw1Q...` (reported as "26")
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/ENk1BRaMFzsmAWk7URd-Kg~~/AAQVbhA~/lMHCaTpA...`

  Images with no alt attribute are announced by screen readers by filename or URL. They also render as blank space when images are blocked.
- **Invalid dark-mode CSS in the `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma in the selector list. Browsers drop the whole rule, so heading and paragraph colors won't invert.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a stray `;` before `!important`. The color applies, but without `!important`, so inline link colors may override it.
  - The block's closing `}` is missing before `</style>`. Parsers usually recover, but strict clients may not.
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- The `.light-img` rule is paired with a `.dark-img` toggle. This is not an error, but the dark-image variants depend on `prefers-color-scheme` support, which is limited in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The HTML is truncated, so this covers only the portion provided.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) is also not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that is a Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirement miss.
