# Pinning down your vibe for the holidays? Try these looks →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the thirteenth Crate & Barrel email in about two days, and the "hours left" free-shipping banner at the top is the same urgency stunt as this morning's "holiday aesthetic" send. I'm mid-renovation on two houses, so my mantel is currently a pile of drywall. Still, the structure here is better than most of their blasts. It's five named looks (modern heritage, timeless evergreen, classic cheer, frosted sage, winter harvest), each with a big room shot, a small product collage and a "Shop the Look" button. That's how I think about decor, and it's the kind of thing I'd drop into my Pinterest-adjacent folder.
- The problem is that it's long, and it repeats the premise of the 10/19 "But what's my holiday aesthetic??" send. That one asked what my aesthetic is, and this one answers it. The two should have been one email. If the second house is going to have a tree, I'd click through to look. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd probably file it and move on.

## What's working

- The five looks have names and numbers, so I can scan to the one that matches my palette. That's the color-consistency framing I actually want.
- Every look has a big, styled room photo. Those are the images I'd save.
- The "Shop the Look" buttons are green, the same on every block, and easy to tap on a phone.
- The free-shipping-over-$98 banner sits at the very top, so the offer is clear before I scroll.
- The "furniture that arrives before your holiday guests do" block with "Shop In-Stock Furniture" speaks to my real concern, which is delivery timing on big pieces. It's the most relevant thing in the email.

## What's weak

- It's one very long scroll. By look #4 I'm skimming, and by look #5 it all blurs together.
- The product collages under each hero are tiny. I can't see names, prices or sizes without clicking, and I can't tell what I'm buying.
- The banner says "hours left" with no clear deadline. After the 10/18 "Buy once, love for decades" send, this feels like manufactured urgency.
- The TheDesignDesk block is a lot of space for a service pitch, and I only see it after five looks. The app-download block and the last design-desk repeat feel like filler.
- The "we love these for you" tiles are glassware and wine glasses. They have nothing to do with the holiday looks above them, and they don't read as personalized to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to three looks, or lead with a single "pick your palette" row at the top. Then put the in-stock furniture and delivery-date block directly under it.
- 2. Put prices and one hero SKU in each look, so I can see what the room costs.
- 3. Replace "hours left" with a real end time.
- 4. Merge this with the earlier holiday-aesthetic send instead of running both a day apart. Wayfair did the same thing to me with the pergola emails, where the one with a clear price badge and a single hero won.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 61 click-tracking links and several tracking pixels. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The main findings are about 20 content images with no `alt` attribute, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and an unverified `List-Unsubscribe` header. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links unverified:** all 61 links go through tracking/redirect domains and were skipped by the HTTP probe. Destination status, redirect chain depth and final URLs are unverified.
- **Identifier shared with a third party:** the same hashed ID (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…`) appears in the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs (`mi_u=`) and in the `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…` URLs (LiveRamp). The pixel fires 5 times (`n=1` to `n=5`) with the same ID. This is a privacy and deliverability concern, because the extra third-party requests can be blocked or flagged by clients.
- **Redundant pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c48c3e55-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` look like two separate open-tracking pixels.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images (high impact):** these are about 14 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…` (for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `0f34b30c-…jpg`, `a1330778-…jpg`) and 7 on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`. The `rp` images appear to be recommended-product tiles. With images blocked or a screen reader, these come through as unlabeled blanks.
- **Missing `alt` on tracking pixels (low impact):** `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c48c…` should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Text resizing disabled:** the global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` rule overrides user text scaling.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`) and duplicate `format-detection` meta tags.
  - An HTML5 `<meta charset>` sits under an XHTML doctype.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source, and the `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated. The HTML is truncated, so body copy was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this should be checked against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is the correct pattern.
- **Footer unverified:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are outside the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 61 links are wrapped in redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages could not be checked. No `utm_*` values are visible in the supplied source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, including the `/p/rp/*.png` product tiles. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` selector, and keep it only on the elements that need it.
3. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't rely on the relay-stripped copy.
4. Resolve the tracked links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations and UTM parameters.
5. Review whether the same hashed ID needs to be passed to `sr.rlcdn.com`, and why it fires 5 times.
6. Clean up the template debris: the `IMPUT HERE` comment, the duplicate meta tags, the conflicting `img` max-width rules, and the unescaped `&` in `<title>`.
