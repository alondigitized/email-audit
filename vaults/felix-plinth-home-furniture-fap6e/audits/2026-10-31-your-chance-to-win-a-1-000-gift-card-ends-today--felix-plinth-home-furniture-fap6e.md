- This is the third Crate & Barrel Kids send I've seen today, after "Start nesting now with fast-ship nursery finds!" and "Your dream nursery starts right here →". The 60% clearance block is buried under the contest hero, and the bedding block is the only thing that even loosely touches my renovation. ## What's working

- The hero is clean and readable. The crib photo, the big "want to win a $1,000 gift card?" and the stars all land immediately.
- Two buttons, "Write a Review" and "Shop Bestsellers", are large white pills that are easy to tap on a phone.
- The "up to 60% off" markdown block gives me a concrete number, and "Shop Clearance" is a clear verb plus noun.
- The product grid shows prices, with a strikethrough on the Halloween treat bag ($34.95 down to $14.97), so I can see savings at a glance.
- The images all loaded. Wayfair's recent sends had blank heroes, so this at least renders properly.

## What's weak

- The whole email is nursery and baby gear: Nuna strollers, a glider, a crib mattress. I'm 36, renovating two houses, with no baby on the way, so none of it is for me.
- It's the third Kids email today, and the eighth or so Crate & Barrel family send in two days. The cadence is exhausting.
- The "ends today" urgency is about a sweepstakes entry that requires me to write a review. That's more work than a coupon, and I don't care about winning a gift card.
- The "Selling fast! Shop now" text on the markdown block sits on busy autumn foliage and is hard to read.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny legal text, and the app block says "your chance at $500", which muddles the $1,000 headline.

- 1. Segment by household. Send nursery content only to people who have bought or registered for baby gear. For me, lead with dining, lighting or bedding and keep the crib out of it.
- 2. Make one offer the hero. Either the contest or the 60% markdown, not both fighting for attention. A clean "up to 60% off, ends tonight" works better than a sweepstakes with a review requirement.
- 3. Fix the contrast on the clearance block, with a solid band behind the text.
- 4. Move "TheDesignDesk" up. A free design consult is the one thing here that fits a renovator, and it's currently buried near the footer.
- 5. Consolidate the fine print and keep the $1,000 and $500 contest messaging consistent.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard ESP table layout (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, VML/MSO fallbacks). The QA run found no hard failures, but 22 images have no alt attribute, the header-based compliance checks are inconclusive, and 63 click-tracked links were never probed. Pass rate was 57%, with 0 issues and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links unverified:** 63 links point to tracking/click-redirect domains and were skipped by the HTTP probe. Destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain) load on open.
  - The `s=` value shares its leading characters with the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` parameter on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, which suggests one subscriber identifier is passed to a third party. The truncated source doesn't show what that identifier is, so confirm this.
  - Five identical-purpose calls differing only in `n` add request overhead.
- **First-party tracking:** the open pixel is `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`. There is also `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/cf081457-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
- **Repeated image:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with the same URL. It looks like a per-block tracking or recommendation image.
- **Asset hosts:** content images come from three hosts: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. Any one failing to resolve breaks part of the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** 22 `<img>` tags have no `alt` attribute.
  - Content images affected: `bbf6f9d4-….png`, `e1277dff-….gif`, `1a008c44-….jpg`, `87907b24-….jpg`, `fc3f77e8-….jpg`, `18cc53f1-….jpg`, `ca6f2fd1-….gif`, `971aa10f-….jpg`, and the scene7 `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_Tert` image.
  - Tracking pixels are also affected: the `rlcdn`, `o.gif` and `dv.` images. They need `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
  - With images blocked, any of the content images that carry offer or CTA text will render as blank space.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
- **Duplicate meta tags:**
  - `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are both present.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times: once combined, then separately as `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, along with empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` blocks.
- **Conflicting media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap at 768px and above. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Title:** it is the generic brand name, not the subject or campaign.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The footer and body past the truncation point were not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** neither header was found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo's one-click requirement for bulk senders can't be verified.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and alignment with the visible From domain can't be confirmed.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not in the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing pages:** UTM parameters and final landing URLs can't be verified because all 63 links go through click-redirect domains and were not followed.
- **Asset naming:** the hero asset is named `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_Tert` (2026-11-01). The email was received 2026-10-31 with an "ENDS TODAY" subject, so confirm the offer end date matches the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run the 63 tracked links through a redirect-following check and record final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Escape the `&` in `<title>` and drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` tags. Delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Delete the dead `img{max-width:600px}` rule, or give the two media queries non-overlapping ranges.
6. Capture the raw message headers directly to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
7. Check what the `rlcdn.com` `s=` value is, and why five pixels fire.
