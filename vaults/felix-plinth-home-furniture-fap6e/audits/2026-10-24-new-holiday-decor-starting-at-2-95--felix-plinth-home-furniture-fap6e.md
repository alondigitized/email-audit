The 2x3 category grid (garlands, stockings, ornaments, candlelight) is easy to skim. It's also a long scroll, with at least six "Shop" buttons and a stack of unrelated modules: TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, accent chairs, glassware, the Reward Dollars card and an app download.

- The mantel hero sells a feeling, not a discount. It's the kind of image I save.
- The six-tile grid (garlands & wreaths, stockings, decorative objects, ornaments, pillows & throws, candlelight) lets me pick a lane fast.
- The "upgrade your garlands with lifelike beauty" band, with a "sells out early" line, is a credible nudge on a category I actually need.
- The "what's your holiday aesthetic?" module, with a numbered style guide, speaks to my palette-matching obsession.

## What's weak

- The subject's "$2.95" has no visible payoff anywhere in the render. The only offer I can see is the 30% off dining strip.
- The top strip is about Thanksgiving dining, but the hero is Christmas. It reads as two campaigns stapled together.
- It repeats the same CTA, "Shop All Christmas Decor," twice in a row, which feels like padding.
- The TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, the accent chairs, the glassware and the app download dilute a decor email into a catalog dump. The accent-chair block ("30% off" tables and chairs) repeats what that other send covered earlier today.
- No shipping or delivery cues for furniture, and no swatch or sample mention, even though the DesignDesk module could have carried both.

- 1. Put the $2.95 item in the hero, with a price tag on it. Make the subject's promise visible within the first screen.
- 2. Cut the dining strip from this send and let the holiday decor own the top.
- 3. Collapse the duplicate "Shop All" buttons into one. Trim the lower modules to one cross-sell, such as accent chairs or TheDesignDesk, not both.
- 4. Add real product tiles with prices under the grid. Pottery Barn's Grinch send at least shows products I can price-compare, and CB2's dining event named a category and committed to it.

The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path (the AgentMail relay), so they can't be treated as sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- Per-recipient identifiers are embedded in image URLs:

  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 33 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute.
  - Content images: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif` (for example `a6067ac2-…jpg`, `0fef3c31-…gif`).

- **Text resize:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on `*` and `body`, and `html` and `*` also set it. This prevents text resizing in Safari/iOS.

- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand. It is also generic, with no subject-specific text.
