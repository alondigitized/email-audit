# HOT TUBS ✨ Deals You'll Love ➡
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "HOT TUBS," and I'll give it credit — the hero banner actually does open with a hot tub on a deck, so at least it's not the faux-plant bait-and-switch from earlier today. But that's basically all the hot tub content I get before it dissolves into the usual Wayfair "everything under one purple roof" scroll: reclining sofas, vanity lighting, dog beds, a Keurig, garden statues, chicken coops, air conditioners, bar stools. By the time I hit "Chicken Coops in Every Size" I've completely forgotten this was supposed to be about hot tubs.
- The one thing that made me pause: "Must-have inflatable hot tubs" buried in the "Scores in Your Style" grid, third row down. That's actually relevant to the renovation — I've genuinely looked at inflatable spas for the second-home deck — but it's competing for attention with garden statues and toilet storage, so it doesn't land the way a focused hot tub email would.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding. This is a scroll-and-close. If I wanted hot tubs I'd search Wayfair directly rather than hunt for the one relevant tile in a 15-category grab bag.

## What's working

- Hero banner does deliver an actual hot tub image tied to the subject line, unlike yesterday's plant/lamp mismatch.
- "Must-have inflatable hot tubs" tile is genuinely relevant to my current renovation project.
- No render bugs — every tile loaded, no blank boxes like the "Bookcases" email from a couple days ago.
- Clean purple/white Wayfair branding throughout, consistent with every other send from them.

## What's weak

- The subject promises hot tubs, but hot tubs are maybe 5% of the actual email — this is a category-dump, not a hot tub sale.
- "These Top Steals Are Going Fast" section has zero hot tub content — recliners, vanity lights, dog beds, pet beds.
- No price, no percentage off, no discount visible anywhere near the hot tub content — just "Shop Now."
- Chicken coops, garden statues, and toilet storage in the same email as hot tubs tells me this is algorithmic filler, not a curated send — I feel like a stranger being fed whatever's in the warehouse.
- "Sales, Sales & Yes, More Sales... Now that we got your attention" is a tone-deaf admission that the email itself knows it's noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. If the subject says hot tubs, lead the entire email with hot tubs — at minimum 3-4 tiles of actual hot tub/spa products with prices, not one banner and one buried grid tile. Article's recent "Clocking in" home office email nailed this kind of single-focus story; Wayfair should try it here.
- 2. Put an actual price or % off on the hot tub hero — "Deals You'll Love" with no number attached is the same fine-print dodge CB2's "Summer Clearance" pulled with no dollar figure.
- 3. Cut the unrelated categories (chicken coops, toilet storage, garden statues) out of a hot-tub-titled send — save them for the next generic "scores in your style" blast.
- 4. Drop the self-aware "now that we got your attention" red banner — it undercuts trust by admitting the email is just noise for noise's sake.
