# Get your Memorial Day deals ASAP!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Seventeen. I am fairly confident this is the seventeenth Pottery Barn email since Saturday, and the subject line literally says "ASAP" — as if the deals are about to evaporate at any second, despite the fact that the exact same "Extra 20% off" offer has been in my inbox every day this week. My finger was on the archive button before the email fully loaded.
- That said, when I actually looked at it, the layout held up. The hero is clean, the category nav tiles are organized in a way I actually use — Sofas & Chairs, Rugs, Bedding, Outdoor — and the "Recommended Just For You" block in the middle showed products that looked like they were pulled from something I'd actually browsed. The Affirm section at the bottom was a nice gesture for someone financing two simultaneous renovations, though I'm buying on my travel card for the points, not splitting into installments.
- I marked it read. Didn't click. Definitely didn't forward. At this send volume, this email has to be extraordinary for me to engage, and it was solidly ordinary.

## What's working

- **Category nav tiles are genuinely useful** — Outdoor, Sofas & Chairs, Rugs, Lighting as quick jumps is the right UX for someone shopping by room, not by campaign.
- **"Recommended Just For You" block** — the presence of personalized product pulls signals they know something about me, which Wayfair almost never does this cleanly in a single email view.
- **15% Back in Rewards is visible** — I'm a Key Rewards member and the callout near the bottom is there; it's below the fold, but it's there.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, nothing broken, images loaded, text isn't overlapping anything. Basics, but CB2's "Welcome to CB2" email on May 18 couldn't even get sequencing right.

## What's weak

- **"ASAP" is exhausting at volume 17** — this urgency device lands very differently on email one than it does on email seventeen. It reads like crying wolf.
- **"Select Styles" qualifier buried in small type** — the hero says Extra 20% off, but the asterisk universe underneath that offer is doing a lot of work I can't read without zooming.
- **"Recommended Just For You" products were bedding** — I'm renovating a second home; I need outdoor furniture and a sectional, not more pillow shams. The personalization engine is close but not dialed.
- **No specific deadline** — "ASAP" is not a date. Article's Memorial Day email this week said "ends Monday." That's the kind of thing that actually prompts me to act before the weekend.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give ASAP an actual expiration date** — "Ends Sunday at midnight" does more work than three exclamation points. Article and Wayfair both did this better this week; Pottery Barn keeps leaning on vague urgency.
- 2. **Lock the hero offer to a specific category I care about** — I've been browsing outdoor sectionals and rugs. The "Extra 20% off" hero should surface the category I clicked last, not generic Select Styles.
- 3. **Move the Rewards callout above the fold** — 15% back is a real reason to click; hiding it three scrolls down is leaving money on the table.
- 4. **Suppress this send to anyone who opened an identical "Extra 20% off" email in the last 48 hours** — that's not a creative fix, that's a suppression rule, but it would save the relationship.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Memorial Day: Extra 20% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your rewards + 20% off — last 48 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs, outdoor — sale prices drop before Monday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your Key Rewards on top. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Memorial Day" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard-broken links (403) will produce dead ends for a subset of users, and three HTTP image sources risk silent blocking in privacy-conscious clients. Compliance header capture is unconfirmed via the AgentMail relay.

**FAIL — Two 403 links:**

- `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-...` — The Key/credit card banner CTA returns 403. Likely an expired or misconfigured campaign token in the `cp_tp` payload.

**INFO — 24 click-redirect links skipped by probe.** All route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` — cannot confirm destination health without following redirects. No action needed if ESP click-tracking is standard.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (3) — risk of client-side blocking:**

| Filename | Source |
|---|---|
| `spacer_0.gif` | `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` |
| `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` |
| `slices_02.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` |

Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook will either block or show a broken image icon for plain-HTTP assets. The `westelm.com` asset is also a cross-domain load — unusual for a PB send.

**Missing `alt` text — 44 images.** The entire `S26_Sale_MemorialDay_9GridHeroLinkFarm` slice grid (`slices_01` through `slices_14`), all footer slices, and several product images have empty `alt`. When images are blocked, the email renders as a blank wall with no text fallback.

**PHP-style header calls inside `<style>` block (dead code / noise):**

```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, ...");
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```

These are PHP statements copy-pasted into a CSS `<style>` block. They do nothing in an email client but add byte weight and could confuse CSS parsers in strict clients.

**`<title>` tag is empty.** Minor — some clients use this for tab/preview labels.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — unconfirmed (WARN):** The QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. If the header is present upstream but stripped by the AgentMail relay, this is a monitoring gap, not a sender defect. If genuinely absent, this violates RFC 2369 and Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced for >5k/day senders).

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) — unconfirmed (WARN):** One-click machine unsubscribe requires this header in addition to `List-Unsubscribe`. Same caveat — relay may be stripping it.

**Authentication-Results header — not captured (WARN):** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Pottery Barn sends from `e.potterybarn.com` (ESP subdomain); if DMARC alignment on the `From: PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` header is misconfigured, inbox placement degrades. Cannot confirm from this data.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism** appear present in the footer based on the HTML structure (standard PB footer template). No issues flagged from visible source.
