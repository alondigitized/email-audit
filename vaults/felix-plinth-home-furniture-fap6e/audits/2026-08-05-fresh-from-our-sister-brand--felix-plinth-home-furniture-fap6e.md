# Fresh from our sister brand
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Fresh from our sister brand" — and I know exactly what this is before I even scroll, because I got the near-identical GreenRow tease bundled into last week's Pottery Barn sends too, just with different subject dressing. This isn't Pottery Barn emailing me about Pottery Barn. It's Pottery Barn using my inbox as a billboard for a brand I never signed up for, and the second I see "GreenRow" up top with the little "sustainably sourced, made for life" tagline, I know I'm being cross-sold, not served. That 3x3 grid of quilts, wicker baskets, and moody farmhouse lamps is genuinely pretty — I'll give them that, it's got a real folkloric/cottage vibe going — but it's competing for headspace with an actual Pottery Barn dining set I'm mid-decision on from three emails ago.
- The kicker is the "Dream Vacation Giveaway" banner sitting right in the middle of a shopping email — a coastal escape sweepstakes with a $1,000 gift card, no purchase necessary, wedged between product grids. That's not what I opened this for. I'm two houses into renovation, I need furniture decisions, not a raffle entry.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Also no — this reads like the retail equivalent of a store clerk handing you a flyer for the shop next door while you're mid-checkout. Marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The GreenRow product grid is genuinely well-shot — warm, lived-in room scenes with quilts, wicker, and that lamp in the bottom row caught my eye for about two seconds.
- "Sustainably sourced, made for life" is a message I actually care about when I'm buying furniture that needs to survive two households of renovation chaos.
- The category nav grid (Furniture, Bedding & Bath, Pillows & Decor, Table Settings, Lighting, Rugs, Curtains, Sale) at the bottom is clean and I know exactly where each link goes.
- Credit card rewards callout (10% back with the Pottery Barn Visa) is relevant — I already shop on the highest-rewards card I've got, so seeing this reminds me the card exists, even if it's buried at the bottom.

## What's weak

- This is a sister-brand ad wearing a Pottery Barn subject line — I didn't sign up for GreenRow, and burying that pivot in vague "family of brands" copy at the top feels like a bait-and-switch before I've even scrolled past the header.
- The Dream Vacation Giveaway sweepstakes mid-email is a hard stop for me — it has nothing to do with furniture shopping and just adds noise between two product sections.
- No price, no discount, no specific SKU anywhere in this email. "Shop Our Collection" is the vaguest possible CTA — compare that to Wayfair's "Fearless design, bold layers" send, which at least pointed me at a styled room concept I could picture in my own space. This just points me at... a whole brand.
- "Join The Journey" sign-up module for "limited-edition home collections" is basically asking me to opt into a THIRD mailing list from the same parent company. I'm already drowning in Williams-Sonoma family emails — this is the fourth "sister brand" beat I've seen from them this month between Pottery Barn, West Elm, and now GreenRow.
- Zero loyalty/rewards math tied to an actual purchase — just a generic card ad at the very bottom, disconnected from anything above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into its own GreenRow-branded send with a real reason to open — a specific new-arrival hero product, a price, or a launch discount. Don't dress it up as a Pottery Barn email; I'll feel tricked the moment I realize it's not.
- 2. Cut the sweepstakes entirely from a product email, or move it to its own dedicated send. It dilutes the "Fresh for Fall" narrative and reads like a distraction, not a bonus.
- 3. Give me one hero product with a price and a room context — like the CB2 "classics on sale" grid does with actual $ callouts — instead of a lifestyle collage with no numbers anywhere.
- 4. Move the Pottery Barn Visa 10% rewards block up near the top if you want it to actually convert — nobody scrolls past a giveaway and two nav grids to find their card perk.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Fresh from our sister brand" (GreenRow cross-promo)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard MSSQL/ExactTarget-style HTML table layout with heavy tracking/pixel instrumentation, but it contains a corrupted `<style>` block with leaked server-side PHP code and ships several unencrypted/inaccessible image assets that will trigger client-side warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All navigable links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` opaque tokens (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEs...`). QA confirms 57 tracking/redirect links were present and skipped by HTTP probing — destination URLs cannot be verified from source alone.
- Multiple third-party ad-tracking pixels are embedded as `<img>` tags rather than standard 1x1 tracking pixels tied to the ESP, including:
  - `https://secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501&srvc=1&betr=30802=711754[8760]`
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  These are functional but represent external ad-network beacons firing on open, worth noting for deliverability/privacy review.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Critical: corrupted `<style>` block.** The second `<style>` tag in `<head>` contains literal PHP `header()` calls before the CSS rules:
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  This is invalid CSS (not comments, not valid selectors/declarations) and indicates server-side code was accidentally pasted into the template. Most clients will silently drop these as parse errors, but this points to a corrupted or mismanaged template source and should be scrubbed.
- 39 images are missing `alt` text per QA findings (all GreenRow footer/nav images, PB footer slices, and tracking pixels). With images blocked by default in most mail clients, the email will render as largely blank/unlabeled content on first open.
- Two image sources use plain `http://` instead of `https://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  Mixed-content/non-HTTPS sources are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) and should be updated to HTTPS.
- Mobile media queries target `max-width: 480px` and `max-width: 650px` separately (`.wrapper`, `.photo`, `.mobile-hide`/`.mobile-show`) — no conflicts observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No broken/unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) observed in the visible source — all personalization tokens appear resolved.
- The recipient's raw email address is embedded in cleartext in two tracking URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  PII-in-URL exposes the address to referrer headers, proxies, and link-scanning/logging systems. Flagging as a privacy-adjacent technical concern rather than a functional bug.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance either way from available data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat as above.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via this relay.
- No in-body unsubscribe link/footer was visible in the truncated HTML provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link) from the available source; flagging as unverified rather than failing, since the source was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All click-through links are wrapped in opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=` tokens rather than exposing readable UTM parameters, so UTM presence/consistency cannot be verified from source — this is a redirect-obfuscation limitation, not a confirmed defect.
- Sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com` while creative assets are served from `image.e.greenrow.com` / `edm.greenrow.com` (a different brand's email-asset domain), and footer assets pull from both `edm.potterybarn.com` and `edm.greenrow.com`. This cross-domain asset sourcing is consistent with the described "sister brand" cross-promo and not itself an error, but any landing-page alignment check would need the resolved (non-tracking) URLs, which are unavailable here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray PHP `header()` statements from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — likely a copy/paste error from an image-serving script into the CSS template.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 39 flagged images, particularly the GreenRow hero banners which currently rely entirely on `alt` for pre-image-load context.
3. Update the two `http://` image sources to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM) are actually present at the SMTP layer — re-test outside the AgentMail relay to rule out a header-capture artifact vs. a true compliance gap.
5. Consider tokenizing/hashing the recipient identifier in `coreemail.html` and `svopn.potterybarn.com` tracking URLs instead of passing the raw email address in the query string.
