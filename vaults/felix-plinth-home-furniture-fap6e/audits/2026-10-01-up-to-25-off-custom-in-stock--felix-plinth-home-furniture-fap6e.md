# UP TO 25% OFF CUSTOM & IN STOCK
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth CB2 send in about four days, and the same sale again. The 9/30 "Holiday Collection is here" email had the same "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off" banner, and "SALE'S ALMOST OVER" was the same promo in a different outfit. I knew what this was before I opened it. The plum channel-tufted chair in the hero is gorgeous, but I've seen it.
- I'll give it credit because I'm actually in the market for sofas and sectionals. With two houses under renovation, a green velvet sectional and "10 free swatches" are the right things to show me. Swatches are the best idea in the email. The page then stacks a second sale on top: "Fall Design Sale, up to 50% off," plus a "New & Best Selling" candle. Which discount applies to what? I'm not doing that math on my phone.
- Would I click? Probably once, on swatches or sectionals, and I'd do it from the Pinterest folder, not the inbox. I wouldn't forward it. I'd archive it with the other four.

## What's working

- The "Order 10 free swatches" banner is the best thing here. It lets me check color against my paint before I commit, which is how I actually shop.
- The hero is clean. "Up to 25% off" is big, the plum chair is the focus, and "Custom, in stock and rarely on sale" explains why the discount is notable.
- The sectionals, accent chairs and benches tiles are easy to scan. They let me jump to the category I need instead of one generic shop link.
- The CB2 App, Rewards ("earn 10% back") and Crate & Barrel cross-links at the bottom speak to my highest-rewards-card habit.

## What's weak

- Two sales stacked in one email, 25% and 50%, with a mess of fine print below. I can't tell which one applies to my sofa.
- It's the fifth near-identical send in four days. The upholstery event is the same creative as the 9/30 "Holiday Collection" email, which I already ignored.
- The swatch banner is a dark strip with thin white text over a busy photo. It's hard to read at phone size, and it's the best offer.
- The "Fall Design Sale" and candle blocks are decor-lite. They pull me away from furniture and feel like a second email glued on.
- Nothing on shipping, lead times or custom-order timelines. For a custom sofa, that's the first thing I need to know.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send one offer per email. Lead with upholstery and cut the 50% Fall Design block, or save it for a separate send.
- 2. Put lead time and delivery next to "custom, in stock." Something like "In stock ships in 2 weeks" would help.
- 3. Make the swatch offer a proper block with a bold, high-contrast button. It's the highest-intent action in the email.
- 4. Cap the cadence. Alternate the sale with a design-story email, like the 9/29 "Color Is the Word" send, which I liked.
- 5. Show a price or a "from" price on the sectionals and chairs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Velvet sectionals up to 25% off, plus 10 free swatches`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Order swatches before the upholstery sale ends`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Custom and in-stock sofas rarely go on sale. Try 10 swatches free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sectionals, accent chairs and benches, with swatches shipped free.`
- Compared with other senders: Crate & Barrel's Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery send had the same overload problem, but it at least stayed on one event. Pottery Barn's Holiday Lookbook is moodier but also over-sent. Article's bedroom refresh was a clean single story, and that's the structure I'd copy. Wayfair's broken-image pergola email shows how bad it gets when the hero fails. CB2's rendering is solid, so that's not the issue here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a well-structured XHTML template (lang set, MSO/VML namespaces, mobile media queries). It ships with one confirmed broken merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`), a zoom-blocking viewport tag, missing alt text on content images, and 10 insecure `http://` third-party pixels. The QA pass rate is 43%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** There are 10 `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` to `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1` images and 1 `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` image. These non-HTTPS sources can be blocked as mixed content or stripped by image proxies. Some clients, such as Gmail's proxy, may also upgrade or fail them. The cookie-sync calls will fail silently.
- **Third-party pixel load:** The Dotomi pixels are a cookie-sync chain. They add 11 extra requests with no `alt`, width or height, and no visible purpose to the recipient.
- **Unverified links:** 63 links were skipped from the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not confirmed good.
- **First-party open pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/8bda8587-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` work as intended. Neither has an `alt` attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (8):** These are all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`: `1d1df03c-…jpg`, `9ad60c3b-…jpg`, `ccffc352-…jpg`, `5955bc60-…jpg`, `eb7c4c78-…jpg`, `c1ee9bca-…jpg`, `f3370c11-…jpg`, and `46f342ff-…png`. If any carry offer or product text, that content is invisible with images off and to screen readers. Decorative images should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Missing alt on tracking and pixel images (~13):** This covers `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com/o/...` pixel, and all of the Dotomi pixels. These should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Non-self-closed meta:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. Parsers tolerate this, but it is invalid.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in `<head>`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Font URLs:** Several `@font-face` rules use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). The `HNW05` faces load from `www.crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain. Both are fragile if paths are normalized or CORS is enforced.
- **Title mismatch:** `<title>` is the generic "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", not tied to this campaign. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw merge token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` rendered unresolved in the delivered HTML. This is Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) personalization-string syntax. It indicates a failed substitution, most likely a dynamic reviews or SKU-list block that returned nothing for this recipient or was never populated. Depending on its position, it may appear as visible literal text or inside an attribute or URL, which would break a link or image. Where it sits in the markup couldn't be checked, because the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. QA notes this may be a limitation of the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified sender failure. If the headers really are absent, the sender fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is **unknown** for the same relay reason. No conclusion can be drawn.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** These could not be verified because the supplied HTML is truncated. They are not flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. All 63 links are routed through click-tracking domains and were not resolved, and the HTML is truncated before the body links. No confirmed issues.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`** (high priority). Check the SFMC data extension or AMPscript source, add a fallback or suppress the block when the value is empty, and run a send-preview with an empty-data subscriber.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://ads.dotomi.com/...`, `https://login.dotomi.com/...`), or drop them if the cookie sync is no longer needed. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
3. **Add alt text** to the 8 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
5. **Verify headers at the MTA.** Check the raw message in a seed inbox or at the sender for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The relay-dependent QA warnings can't settle this.
6. **Clean the template:** remove the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT...]` comment, self-close the meta tag, and normalize the `//Styles` font paths.
7. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML and link resolution** to cover UTMs, the footer and the 63 tracked links.
