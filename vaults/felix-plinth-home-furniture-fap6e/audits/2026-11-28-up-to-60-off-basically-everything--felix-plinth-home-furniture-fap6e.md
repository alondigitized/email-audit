# UP TO 60% OFF (BASICALLY) EVERYTHING
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. I've lost count of the Black Friday sends this week. The 11/27 "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE," "UP TO 60% OFF HOSTING FAVORITES" and "THE SALE OF THE YEAR IS HERE" all used the same "Black Friday SALE, up to 60% off" panel. This one swaps in a plate-and-bowl hero under the "DINNERWARE (IT ALL SHIPS FREE)" line. The subject says "basically everything" and the hero is dishes, so I'm not sure what I'm getting.
- I'm renovating two houses, so I'd rather see furniture, lighting and mirrors than a place setting. The six-tile grid below the hero is the useful part, because the green velvet chair and the arched mirror are what I'd actually click. But I've seen this scroll-and-grid layout five times this week and it's all one campaign. I'd skim it, maybe tap Lighting, and file the rest under "C&B family snooze." I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Extended store hours, Friday 11/27 9AM-9PM" strip is a nice touch. It's a showroom cue that fits how I shop.
- The six-tile grid (Furniture, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Bedding & Bath) lets me jump straight to my categories.
- The free shipping band ("on orders of $99 or more") and the "Save 15% off in the new year" band are both clear. Furniture shipping clarity matters to me.
- The product photography is moody, warm and cohesive. It suits a palette-consistency person like me.
- The Rewards Dollars block ("Earn 10% back") speaks to my highest-rewards-card habit.

## What's weak

- The hero is dinnerware, but the subject promised "basically everything." That mismatch is the same problem as the 11/26 "NOT SOLD OUT YET" send.
- The same "Black Friday SALE" block again. Pottery Barn and Article do the same thing: Article's black "SALE" banner has run all month, and Pottery Barn's plaid maroon block is on its fifth send today. CB2 is now doing it too.
- The "More for you" row is glassware and a serving bowl at $9-$32. It feels generic, not personalized to someone mid-renovation.
- The legal text at the bottom is a wall of tiny type, and the "Sale ends 11/30" deadline is hard to find.
- Only the Muse wine glasses show a was/now price ($43.80 → $32). The other products show a single price, so the "up to 60%" isn't backed up with numbers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: use a furniture or lighting collage, not dinnerware.
- 2. Put a hard deadline ("Ends Monday 11/30") in the hero, not the fine print.
- 3. Cut the send frequency, or vary the creative by category so each email isn't the same panel.
- 4. Make the "More for you" row product-aware, with lighting, rugs and seating for people who browse those categories, and show was/now prices on every item.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has accessibility gaps and a set of insecure third-party tracking pixels: 25 images lack alt text, and 11 Dotomi pixels load over `http://`. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, because the relay didn't capture the headers and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so the destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **11 images load over insecure `http://`.** All are third-party Dotomi (Conversant) ad-sync pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` through `pub10.php`
- **Impact of the `http://` pixels.** Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will likely block or rewrite them, so the pixels probably won't fire. Where a client does load them, they trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Privacy exposure from the pixels.** They are a cookie-sync chain. Ten sequential `pub*.php` calls add 11 third-party requests per open and expose the recipient to ad-tech partners.
- **The first-party pixels use HTTPS.** `mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/872769f5-...` load correctly, apart from the missing alt text below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **25 images have no alt text:**
  - 12 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` (e.g. `978d7bb3-...jpg`, `f64bfcd3-...jpg`, `20185973-...jpg`).
  - 11 Dotomi pixels (`UCMController`, `pub1`–`pub10`).
  - 2 first-party pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/872769f5-...`).
- **Content images need alt text.** The 12 content images are the real accessibility problem. If they carry the offers, image-blocked and screen-reader users see nothing.
- **Tracking pixels need empty alt.** The 13 pixels should use `alt=""` or be hidden with `aria-hidden`.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks it on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **`@font-face` rules conflict.** `"Lineto Brown Sub"` is declared three times with different sources and weights (an `.eot`/`.woff2` block with no weight, then 400 and 600 `.woff2` blocks pointing at the light and regular files). Several URLs also contain a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...`). This can cause inconsistent font fallback.
- **Template placeholder left in.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head, and the `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block is empty.
- **Rendering features that are fine:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - A `<title>` is present.
  - The Outlook (MSO) conditional blocks and VML namespaces are in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated HTML means this is not a full-body check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (QA WARN).** The QA note says the relay may strip it, so presence is unverified.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` was not found (QA WARN).** RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com` is unknown.
- **Footer elements are unconfirmed.** The visible footer unsubscribe link and postal address can't be checked, because the HTML is truncated before the footer.

None of these three header results is a confirmed failure. They are verification gaps from the AgentMail relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. All 68 tracking links were skipped, so UTM parameters, final destinations and the offer match between email and landing page could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Content image alt text (high).** Add descriptive `alt` to the 12 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images.
2. **Insecure Dotomi pixels (high).** Remove the 11 `http://` Dotomi pixels. If they are needed, switch them to HTTPS, or move that tracking server-side or to an ESP-supported pixel.
3. **Pixel alt text (low).** Add `alt=""` to the remaining tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`).
4. **Viewport meta (medium).** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Font declarations (low).** Consolidate the duplicate `"Lineto Brown Sub"` `@font-face` rules, fix the `//Styles` double-slash URLs, and delete the leftover `[IMPUT HERE ...]` placeholder.
6. **Header verification (QA pipeline).** Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) directly instead of through the relay, so the compliance and authentication checks can be settled.
7. **Redirect resolution (QA pipeline).** Follow the tracking-redirect links to their final URLs, so UTM and landing-page continuity can be audited.
8. **Source truncation (QA pipeline).** Run the audit on the full HTML. The footer, the unsubscribe link and the physical address were not available to check.
