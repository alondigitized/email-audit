# PERGOLAS 🛒 🌲 📅 Up to 70% OFF your holiday haul 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- Eighteenth Wayfair email this week, and it's pergolas again. The pergola wall is back, with six tiles up top and six more mid-email. The images loaded this time, which is more than I could say about the 10/15 "Just dropped: Pergolas on sale now" send with the blank hero. I still don't need a pergola. I'm doing two interiors, and I've never clicked one of these.
- The email works better than that last one. The "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner is clear, and the "free & easy delivery over $35*" band is useful. But the body is a grab bag of unrelated 40% and 50% off tiles for rugs, bedding, curtains, fitness, fireplaces and appliances. The subject promised pergolas and the email sells everything. The pergola grid is a wall of near-identical gray-and-white structures, and the price text is tiny. Pottery Barn's free design help email at least told me one clear thing I could do.
- I'd skim it and probably file it. I'd click the area rugs tile because I'm furnishing two houses. I wouldn't forward it. My buddy would ask why I'm sending him a pergola.

## What's working

- The images rendered. All twelve pergola tiles show, with star ratings and "Was/NOW" pricing. That's better than the blank-hero 10/15 send.
- The "up to 70% OFF" banner at the top is readable and tells me the offer immediately.
- The "free & easy delivery over $35*" band answers a question I always have about furniture shipping.
- The category tiles (area rugs, curtains & drapes, bedding) are big and tappable. The purple-and-pink blocks are easy to scan on a phone.
- The "Wayfair Rewards," "Pay Over Time" and "Professional" icon row near the bottom hints at member perks, though it's buried.

## What's weak

- Pergolas are in the subject, then split across two grids with a mess of unrelated categories between them. It feels like a catalog dump.
- The "picked just for you" header isn't believable. I've gotten the same pergola wall for a week, so it feels like a stranger's email.
- The price text under each pergola is tiny. I'd have to zoom to read "Was $1,799 NOW: $999."
- There's no hero product and no single clear CTA. "shop all sales" at the bottom is vague, and the rest are category labels with arrows.
- Nothing speaks to my renovation: no swatches, no color story, no showroom or design-service content. The bar stools "buy one, get one" tile is the only specific offer outside the percent-off tiles.
- Cadence is exhausting. This is the same pergola wall as 10/13 ("PERGOLAS 🎄 HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK 🎄 Up to 70% OFF") and 10/11 ("CLEAROUT ENDS TOMORROW").

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it one story. Either a tight pergola email (one hero, three or four tiles, one CTA) or a mixed deal-week email without the pergola subject. Don't do both.
- 2. Suppress repeat sends. After a week of pergolas with no clicks from me, send me rugs, lighting or something related to the renovation. Crate & Barrel and CB2 at least vary their heroes.
- 3. Make the prices bigger. Put the sale price in bold, show the percent off, and keep the star ratings.
- 4. Replace "picked just for you" with something honest, or personalize it from what I browsed.
- 5. Move the member perks up. "Wayfair Rewards" and delivery terms should be near the top. I buy through the highest-rewards card, so give me the math.
- 6. Give me a CTA with a verb and a noun, like "Shop pergolas up to 70% off."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free delivery over $35, plus rewards on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `12 top-rated pergolas, marked down for holiday deal week.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

The email's HTML is mostly sound, but it has accessibility and tracking-pixel gaps: many images have no alt text, and 20 tracking pixels load over plain HTTP. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The automated QA pass rate was 57%, with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Rate-limited probes (inconclusive):** 8 links returned 429. Examples are the wayfairapp.onelink.me app-download link ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App", two instances), `/daily-sales/bar-stools%3a-buy-one%2c-get-one-15%25-off~e411114.html`, `/daily-sales` ("Shop Sale"), the `https://www.wayfair.com/` logo link, and two pergola product links (`PAUR1264`, `PAUR1193`). A 429 is bot throttling, not a broken link, so none of these is a confirmed defect.
- **Incomplete coverage:** Only 12 of 75 links were probed (63 skipped). Probing stopped at the 70s budget, leaving 4 pergola product URLs unchecked (`JLYD1476`, `20-Ft.-W-x-12-Ft.-D…JLY`, `Louvered-Pergola-with-Panel-PullDown-Screen…`, `LeanTo-WallMounted-Pergolas…`). Their status is unknown.
- **Unique per-recipient tokens:** Each link carries a different `_eml=` GUID. The shared `_emr=54ec6b14-…` ID is the recipient. This is normal ESP tracking, but it means the URLs can't be compared directly across sends.
- **Mixed protocol:** The hero banner link is `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?…` while the other links use `https://`. It is probably redirected, but it adds an unnecessary hop.
- **Encoded characters:** The bar-stools URL contains `%3a`, `%2c` and `%25`. This is valid, but the lowercase hex and the encoded commas in the path are uncommon.
- **Truncated product URLs:** Several product paths are clipped. The `…-L7053-K~PAUR1193.html` slug starts with a hyphen (`/-Aluminum-Pergola…`), which suggests the product name was truncated or left blank in the template. The SKU after `~` should still route correctly.
- **App deep link:** The `onelink.me` link sets `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` with an `af_web_dp` fallback to `http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. That fallback uses HTTP.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt text:** About 20 images are flagged. This includes:
  - The tracking pixel `t.wayfair.com/b.php`.
  - The hero banner `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnytop-general.jpg`, which is the image inside the first "Shop Sale" link.
  - 10 review-star images (`stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png`, `stars_11.png`). These are linked, so screen readers will announce the filename or the bare URL as link text.
  - The skinny banners `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner…`, `wfus_5daysofdeals_fall2026-skinny-flashdeals.png`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`.
- **Stale banner assets:** The filenames `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_…` and `0125_48hourclearance…` point to old campaigns (Memorial Day, a January clearance) inside an October holiday send. The QA data shows the files are referenced. It doesn't show whether they are visible or hidden fallbacks. This needs a source check before it's treated as a defect.
- **Insecure image requests:** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=…` ad impression pixels load over HTTP. They also lack alt text. Clients that block mixed content, or proxy images over HTTPS, may drop them and under-count ad impressions. They are 2x1 pixels, so there is no visible effect.
- **Structure:** The email uses an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype and responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules. It also has a custom `@font-face` for "Sofia" with an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback. No rendering defects are confirmed from the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The personalization IDs (`_emr`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`, `csnid`) are populated.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture this header. This is a capture limitation, not confirmed absence. The headers need checking in the raw message.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN):** The same limitation applies. Without it, one-click unsubscribe isn't confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address:** The QA report says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set, but the excerpt doesn't show it. Confirm the footer link and the postal address in the full source.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **No UTM parameters:** None of the links use `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters (`source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=12`, `csnid=…`, `refid=MKTEML_141673`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16510`). That is internally consistent, but standard analytics tools won't recognize it without custom mapping.
- **Consistency:** The parameters are the same across links, so attribution is uniform.
- **Landing alignment:** The subject promotes "PERGOLAS … Up to 70% OFF". The pergola product links (`PAUR1264`, `PAUR1193`) match it. The primary CTAs ("Shop Sale", the hero banner) go to the generic `/daily-sales` page, not a pergola category. The mismatch can't be checked, because the 429 responses blocked content verification.
- **Final landing state:** No redirect chain or landing-page content was captured for any link, because probing was rate limited.

7. Recommendations

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all linked images. Use `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"` for the review stars and `alt=""` for decorative banners.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels, and the app-link fallback `af_web_dp`, over HTTPS. Give the pixels `alt=""`, `width` and `height`.
3. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
4. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with a slower, throttled crawler before treating them as healthy.
5. Fix the product slugs that begin with a hyphen (`/-Aluminum-Pergola…`).
6. Add standard `utm_*` parameters, or document the mapping from `source`/`batchid`/`refid`.
7. Make sure no stale campaign banners (Memorial Day, 48-hour clearance) are rendered in the live email.
