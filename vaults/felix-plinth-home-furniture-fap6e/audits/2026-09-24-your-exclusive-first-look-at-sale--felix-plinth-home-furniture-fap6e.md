# Your exclusive first look at sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the hero here isn't even a product shot, it's an ad for the CB2 app — "Only On The App," a phone mockup with the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off. That's a weird way to open, because the thing I actually care about (upholstery, since I'm mid-renovation on two houses) is buried behind an app-download pitch instead of just being shown to me. Then it pivots into a straight product grid — New Arrivals, Furniture, Decor, Lighting, Rugs — which is fine, competent CB2-house-style photography, but I've now seen this exact "moody amber-lit vignette" aesthetic in basically every CB2/Crate & Barrel send this month, including the "Now & Future Classics" email and "That Collected Feel."
- The one thing that actually lands: that walnut-and-black-leather sofa+coffee table vignette midway down is genuinely nice, and the rolled rug shot in the Rugs tile has good texture detail. But by the time I hit "SALE ON NOW — Up to 50% Off" near the bottom, I'm annoyed that the top of the email teased "up to 25% off" upholstery and the actual sitewide deal is double that — so why wasn't 50% off the headline?
- Verdict: I'm not clicking. This reads like three emails stapled together (app ad, category directory, sale banner) with no single clear offer up top. Not forwarding it, not saving it to my Pinterest-folder — I'd scroll past this the way I did CB2's "Moody Lighting" send two days ago.

## What's working

- The upholstery event art direction (dark green velvet sofa, phone mockup, warm lighting) is genuinely premium-looking, matches CB2's brand voice.
- The sofa/coffee-table vignette photo has real detail I'd zoom into — good wood grain, styled shelf, that's the kind of image that gets saved to a moodboard.
- Category navigation up top (Furniture, Decor, Rugs, Lighting) is clean and lets me skip straight to what I need without scrolling.
- The 10% Rewards Dollars callout at the bottom is relevant since I already use a rewards card for all my furniture buys.

## What's weak

- The actual best offer (50% off) is buried at the bottom instead of leading the email — the top fold wastes real estate on an app install pitch.
- "Up to 25%" vs "Up to 50%" in the same email is confusing — which discount applies to what, and why show me the smaller number first?
- Nothing here is upholstery-specific beyond the hero graphic — no swatch/fabric program content, no fit-and-sample callout, even though "Upholstery Event" is literally the headline.
- This is the same moody-neutral-palette formula I've seen from CB2 three times in the last week (Moody Lighting, That Collected Feel, Now & Future Classics) — creative fatigue is real.
- Asterisks everywhere ("25%*", "50%*") with fine print pushed to the very bottom footer — feels like there's a catch I have to hunt for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the actual sale headline (50% off sitewide) instead of the app-download pitch — that's the strongest offer in the email and it's currently third-billed.
- 2. Cut the "Only On The App" hero entirely or move it to the footer with the other app/rewards blocks — it's not why people open a sale email.
- 3. Add real upholstery-shopping content since that's the named event: swatch/fabric-sample ordering, lead-time/shipping estimates for custom pieces, since that's the actual friction point when buying a sofa online.
- 4. Consolidate the two competing discounts into one consistent message, e.g. "Upholstery: 25% off. Everything else: up to 50% off" stated plainly near the top, not split across two banners.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Upholstery event: up to 25% off sofas & chairs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sitewide sale is on — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, sectionals & accent chairs — limited time`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus new arrivals in rugs, lighting & decor`
