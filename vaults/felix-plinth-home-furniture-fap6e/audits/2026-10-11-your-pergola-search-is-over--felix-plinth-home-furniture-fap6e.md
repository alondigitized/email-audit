# Your pergola search is over!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- I've logged this as the eighth Wayfair email this week, and it's the first one that actually delivers what the subject promises. The top has one pergola with a red "$134 off" badge, $745 against a struck-through $879, and a Shop Now button. That's clean. Then I get ten more pergola photos in a grid, and I read it as the same wall of purple pergolas from the 10/10 "72 hours only: UP TO 60% OFF" send, only less purple.
- The retargeting copy ("We didn't want you to miss out on this recent pick") is honest, and I did look at a pergola. But I'm renovating two houses, and the punch list is sofas, bookcases, and lighting. A pergola is a one-time browse, and in mid-October it's off-season. Between yesterday's bookcases email, where I got one bookcase, and this one, Wayfair has now missed on both ends of the specificity scale. Would I click? Maybe once, to see the hero price. Would I forward it? No. I'd archive it.

## What's working

- The hero card is clear. One product, real dimensions (10 Ft. W x 10 Ft. D, louvered, adjustable roof), a sale price against a struck-through one, and a red "$134 off" badge. I can read the deal in two seconds.
- The "Don't Forget This Find" framing is honest retargeting. It tells me why I'm getting this, unlike the 10/09 dresser email that opened with a generic "Last Call" banner.
- The Shop Now button is big, purple, and has clear contrast. It's an easy tap on a phone.
- The photography is strong. The string-lit patios and dusk shots sell the lifestyle better than the product-on-white tiles in Wayfair's other sends.
- No render bugs. Everything loads, and nothing overlaps or breaks.

## What's weak

- The "Top Picks For You" grid is ten pergola photos with star ratings and nothing else. There are no prices, names, or sizes, and no way to compare them. Crate & Barrel at least gives me product context in its sofa sends.
- The Rewards banner pitches "5% back on holiday must-haves" with a "Join now" button. That has nothing to do with a pergola, and I already optimize for rewards, so a generic join prompt reads like I'm a stranger.
- The "Shop Limited-Time Deals" tiles say "Pacific Northwest outdoor essentials" and "Midwest outdoor essentials" with no price or deadline. The urgency is just a label. CB2's "2 DEALS & ONLY DAYS LEFT" at least named a sale and a clock.
- I'm furnishing indoor rooms, and there's nothing on swatches, delivery windows, or assembly for a 10x10 metal structure. That's the first thing I'd ask before spending $745.
- The footer is dense and tiny. The rewards and membership fine print is hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices and short names under each "Top Picks" tile, and cut the grid from ten to six. Adding a price range or "from $X" would do the most for this email. Right now it's a photo wall.
- 2. Replace the generic Rewards banner with a message that matches the product: "Earn 5% back on this pergola" for members, with a join prompt only for non-members. Article's clearance emails keep the loyalty hook tied to the offer.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Price ends Sunday," and make the deals tiles say what the deal is.
- 4. Add one line on delivery and assembly for large items, like "Ships in 5-7 days, assembly guide included."
- 5. Check the cadence. Eight sends in a week with the same category repeated is the real problem. One personalized send beats three generic ones.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking about that pergola?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Louvered roof, $745 today. Ships in about a week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Price drops end Sunday, plus 5% back for members.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair triggered PDP-abandonment send, per `templateName=PDP+Abandonment+Reminder+-+WFUS+Active` and `c=Triggered`. Its tracking and link structure is coherent. The confirmed technical issues are 20 non-HTTPS ad-impression pixels and widespread missing alt text. Link validity, the unsubscribe headers and SPF/DKIM could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity is unverified, not broken.** 9 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 429. These include `/daily-sales`, the homepage, `/curated/the-holiday-shop~ev632090.html`, both `erommy-louvered-pergola…etrs1219` PDP links, and `mirador-8088…vve10006`. This looks like Wayfair rate-limiting the QA prober, and no 404s or 5xx responses were reported.
- **Coverage is thin.** Only 12 of 65 links were probed and 53 were skipped. 3 more links were cut off by the 70s time budget (`mirador-8088…`, and two `polar-aurora-aluminum-pergola…` URLs). Those 3 are neither pass nor fail.
- **Empty `SKU=` parameter** on the `mi.wayfair.io/p/cp/9f67e77b485f8e52/c` redirect. The SKU is only carried in `mi_skus=JLYD1804`. This is low severity, but an empty param can break strict parsers.
- **Double-redirect wrapper.** `mi.wayfair.io/.../c?...&url=https%3A%2F%2Fmi.wayfair.io%2Fp%2Frp%2F...` nests a second `mi.wayfair.io` URL. The extra hop adds latency and a failure point.
- **Per-link `_eml` GUIDs.** These are unique per link, but the title and image links to the same PDP share `_eml=610bfab9…`. The star-rating link uses a different `_eml` (`0839a160…`), so click attribution is consistent within each element type.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 12 star-rating images.** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` at `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/` have no alt. The `stars_10.png` image inside a link to the PDP (link text `[image: stars_10.png]`) leaves that link with no accessible name. The rating information is lost to screen readers.
- **Missing alt on linked images.**
  - `819e8b231debd6c3.png` is the product image linking to the `mi.wayfair.io` redirect.
  - `WFNA_Winter2026_GTM_Email_Holiday_Ph2_SkinnyBanner` links to `/curated/the-holiday-shop~ev632090.html`.
  - Both are clickable with no alt, so they have no accessible name. If images are blocked, the link is blank.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** `t.wayfair.com/b.php?...eventType=EmailOpen` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels (`s=124126000` through `s=124126019`) have no alt. The QA check can't distinguish a missing alt from `alt=""`, so confirm they use `alt=""`.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** All 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...&sz=2x1` pixels use plain HTTP. They may be blocked or rewritten by clients, and they can cause mixed-content warnings in some webmail. If they are blocked, impression tracking undercounts.
- **Truncated HTML.** The visible head has a valid XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, 480px media queries and a Gmail `u ~ div` hack. The rest of the markup was not available, so no further rendering findings are made.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible content shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`. Recipient and message identifiers (`_emr`, `csnid`, `_eml`, `mdlid`) are all populated. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers. It is unconfirmed as a sender defect. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe would not be met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain, `service.wayfair.com`, can't be checked against DKIM from this data.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but the footer was truncated from the HTML. I can't confirm either one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses its own scheme: `source=graymatter`, `refid=EML_110731`, `wfcs=cs7`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `treatmentGuid`, `mdlid`. This is consistent across links, but downstream analytics that depend on UTMs will not pick up campaign attribution without mapping.
- **Subject-to-landing alignment is good for the PDP links.** The subject "Your pergola search is over!" matches the PDP links (`erommy-louvered-pergola…`, `mirador-8088-metal-frame-pergola…`, `polar-aurora-aluminum-pergola…`), each with `sku` and `mplid` set.
- **Non-pergola destinations.** The `/daily-sales` link and the holiday-shop link (with `action=openenrollmentmodal`) lead away from the pergola intent. This is a destination mismatch, not a technical fault.
- **App link.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` has `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and `af_web_dp=…/the-wayfair-app`, so the deep link lands on the app home rather than a pergola page. This was not verified because of the 429.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
2. Add alt text: descriptive for `stars_N.png` (e.g. "4.5 out of 5 stars") and the linked product and banner images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Re-probe the 12 material links with a throttled or allow-listed client. Check the 3 unprobed `polar-aurora` and `mirador` PDPs, then re-run to separate real 4xx/5xx from 429s.
4. Check raw `.eml` headers at the source mailbox, before the relay, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass for `service.wayfair.com`.
5. Capture the full HTML and verify the footer unsubscribe link and physical address.
6. Fix the empty `SKU=` parameter on the `mi.wayfair.io` redirect.
7. Map `source`, `refid` and `wfcs` to UTM fields in analytics if cross-tool attribution is needed.
