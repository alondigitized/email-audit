## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout (`lang="en"`, VML/MSO conditionals). The QA run found 0 hard failures, 3 warnings and a 57% pass rate. The missing authentication and unsubscribe headers are probably relay artifacts, not sender defects. The confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes and viewport and text-size settings that hurt accessibility.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 74 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect health are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` to `n=5` beacons are in the body. They are third-party (LiveRamp-style) tracking calls, and the QA data shows no consent or disclosure mechanism for them.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs:** the same 64-hex value is in the rlcdn `s=` parameter and the start of the `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1…` parameter on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances) and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. This looks like a hashed recipient ID, possibly a hashed email address, passed to a third party. I can't confirm what it hashes from the truncated source.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It's harmless but sloppy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 35 `<img>` warnings, in two groups.
  - **Content images** (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/.../*.jpg`, e.g. `73fcbea2-…jpg`, `d450dcd2-…jpg`, and the `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F0924%5FCBK%5FVenusWilliams%5FEliteDesign…` assets) have no text equivalent when images are blocked.
  - **Decorative and tracking images** (`25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` ×3, `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px`, the `o.gif` and `448796.gif` pixels) should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. Whether the attribute is absent or empty isn't clear from the QA output.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents zooming (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` setting declared earlier, which blocks text resizing on iOS.
- **Duplicate and conflicting metas:** `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`).
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`. The title also doesn't match the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible truncated source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. I can't check the part that was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Re-check against raw headers before treating it as a sender defect.
- **Footer not verifiable:** the truncated HTML doesn't show the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 74 tracking links were not resolved, so I can't see UTM parameters or landing pages. No issues found in the visible source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** add descriptive `alt` text to the content images and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. **Viewport:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Text sizing:** drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `*` and `body`, and keep `100%`.
4. **Headers:** capture the raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) in the QA pipeline to confirm or clear the three compliance and deliverability warnings.
5. **Redirects:** follow the tracking redirects with a HEAD or GET request to capture final URLs, status codes and UTMs. This is the only way to assess continuity.
6. **Third-party pixels:** disclose the five `rlcdn.com` beacons and the hashed `mi_u`/`s=` identifier in the privacy policy, and confirm the opt-out path.
7. **Cleanup:** consolidate the `format-detection` metas, escape the `&` in `<title>`, and remove the template placeholder comment.
