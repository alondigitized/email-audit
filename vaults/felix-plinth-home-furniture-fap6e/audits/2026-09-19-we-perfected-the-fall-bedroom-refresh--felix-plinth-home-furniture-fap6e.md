# We perfected the fall bedroom refresh
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "We Perfected the Fall Bedroom Refresh" — bold claim, and I'll give CB2 this: the hero shot with that camel-leather headboard and sheepskin throw is genuinely gorgeous, and "bedroom refresh" is exactly my current headspace with two houses mid-reno. But then it just... keeps going. Davison Bed, Rio Nightstand, Rio Bed, Isobel Nightstand, Russell Bed — five beds and nightstands in a row with no pricing differentiation upfront, no "which one is actually on sale," just a scroll-forever catalog dump.
- This is CB2 running the exact same designer-collab playbook I called out on 9/18 with "Floor Sample Sale" and "Go Bold or Go Home" — another product name in small caps under a hero shot, no urgency, no clear number. By the time I hit "Best Sellers Up to 40% Off" two-thirds down, my thumb's already tired. Compare that to CB2's own 9/16 "Last Call for the Fall Sale" — that one led with "30% Off" huge on a couch. This one buries the actual discount below five furniture pieces I have to scroll past first.
- Verdict: I'd stop at the hero image, maybe glance at the Davison Bed, then bail before I hit the Best Sellers section. Not clicking, not forwarding — this is a scroll-and-archive.

## What's working

- The hero shot is genuinely on-brand for me — warm camel leather, sheepskin throw, moody lighting, reads like a real bedroom not a showroom.
- "Fall Bedroom Refresh" is a real seasonal hook that matches what I'm actually doing in the second house right now.
- The Interior Design Services block ("in-store or virtually," free) is smart — I'm mid-renovation and would actually consider that, unlike Wayfair's fountain-to-trash-can whiplash email.
- Rewards math is spelled out at the bottom — "Earn 15% Back in Rewards Points" — that's the kind of concrete loyalty number Pottery Barn's desk email also nailed.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical bed/nightstand product blocks before any discount appears — this is worse pacing than CB2's own "Best Sellers" send on 9/17, which at least organized by category grid instead of one-at-a-time scroll.
- No price is visible on a single one of those five hero furniture pieces — just product names in tiny caps. I have to tap through to even see if it's in my budget.
- "Up to 40% Off" only shows up after I've scrolled past what feels like the whole bedroom catalog — total buried-lede problem, the opposite of the 9/16 "Fall 30% Off" email that led with the number.
- The "More For You" grid at the bottom does show prices ($499, $599, $349, $399, $299) but it's generic algorithmic filler, not tied to the bedroom refresh narrative at all — feels bolted on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the discount, not the designer names — move "Up to 40% Off" or a specific bedroom-category discount into the hero banner itself. CB2 proved on 9/16 this works better.
- 2. Add prices under every hero product (Davison Bed, Rio Bed, etc.) — I shouldn't have to click through five times to find out what's actually in my range.
- 3. Cut the product count from five sequential beds to two or three, and use the space saved to move Best Sellers and the design-services CTA higher.
- 4. Tie the "More For You" grid back to bedroom category specifically — right now it reads like an unrelated afterthought.
