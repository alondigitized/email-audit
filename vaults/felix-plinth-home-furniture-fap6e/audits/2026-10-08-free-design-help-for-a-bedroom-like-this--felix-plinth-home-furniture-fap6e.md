## What's working

- The before / vision / finished-space structure shows me the result instead of just claiming "design help." I get a real sense of what the service does.
- Three named looks (luxe & nature-inspired, chic & serene, relaxed & modern) give me a style I can pick. That's how I think about palette when I'm sorting two houses.

## What's weak

- The top "20% off sofas & sectionals" banner fights the bedroom story. The hero says one thing and the discount says another.
- The sofa offer appears twice and the Design Desk ask appears twice. Combined with the Affirm, credit-card, app and "we love these for you" blocks, the email is far too long.
- The "we love these for you" section looks random: an espresso machine, flatware and kitchen gadgets. It reads as filler, not as bedroom or renovation products, and it doesn't feel personalized to me.
- There are too many CTAs: Shop Now, Book Your Free Appointment, Shop Bedroom Furniture, Shop Bestsellers, Learn More and Download. There's no single "do this" button.
- I can't see any shipping clarity for furniture, swatch or sample info, or lead times. For a big-ticket order those are the details that decide whether I buy.
- This is the fifth Crate-family send today. The cadence is exhausting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job. Either lead with the Design Desk and drop the sofa banner, or send a sofa-sale email. Right now the strongest asset, free design help, is buried under a 20% strip that has nothing to do with the subject.
- 2. Cut the back third: the generic product grid, the app promo and one of the two Affirm and credit-card blocks. End on a single "Book your free appointment" button.
- 3. Add a "shop this look" link under each finished space, with the actual bed, bench and lighting. I'd click that immediately, and it solves my complaint about not seeing what's in the room.
- 4. Add logistics. Show the appointment length, whether it's virtual or in-store, and a swatch or sample mention. For furniture, add delivery windows.
- 5. Cap the frequency. After three clearance emails, one Santa email and one nursery email today, this should have been held for another day. Pottery Barn's quilt send and CB2's bed email at least gave me one clean focus.

## Technical Audit: "Free design help for a bedroom like THIS?"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Movable Ink and ESP template and has no confirmed hard failures. The QA run scored 57% pass with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are about 45 images flagged as missing alt text, an unescaped `&` in `<title>`, and a `maximum-scale=1` viewport that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings may be artifacts of the capture relay. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and body links could not be inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page health are unverified.
- **A recipient identifier appears in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1…` carries it, and `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8` shares the same prefix. That suggests one hashed ID is being passed to a third party (LiveRamp, `rlcdn.com`). It is probably a hashed email, but that can't be confirmed from the source.
- **The LiveRamp pixel fires five times.** `448796.gif` loads with `n=1` through `n=5`. These are redundant third-party requests that add weight and privacy exposure.
- **Several tracking pixels are in the body.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e35bb580-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression pixels. Image-blocking clients will skip them, so open data will be incomplete.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 45 images were flagged for missing alt.**
  - Most are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, such as `32048dff-…jpg` and `a8e1c417-…jpg`.
  - Others are the dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - The check can't tell a missing `alt` from an empty `alt=""`. If these are meant to be content images, there is no fallback with images off and screen readers will read filenames or nothing.
  - The pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `dv…`) should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is set.** It appears on `*{}`, `html` and `body`, and it blocks text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **The `<title>` is invalid XML.** It reads `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, and the `&` should be `&amp;`.
- **There are leftover template artifacts.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled placeholder with a typo.
  - There are empty `<!---->` blocks.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - These are harmless but sloppy.
- **Broad global CSS.** `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are risky for inherited styling. The `img{max-width:600px}` and `640px` rules inside `min-width` media queries are inconsistent with each other.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. The truncated HTML means this is not fully verified.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so treat this as unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, that is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement failure.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign, but nothing is verified.
- **The footer could not be inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section, so they are neither confirmed nor flagged.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 78 links go through tracking redirects and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "bedroom design help" offer can't be checked. No issues are confirmed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** add descriptive `alt` to content images and `alt=""` to pixels, and confirm the dynamic Movable Ink images get alt at render time.
2. **Viewport:** remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `text-size-adjust:none` declarations.
3. **Title:** change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and clean out the template placeholder comments and duplicate meta tags.
4. **Pixels:** deduplicate the five `rlcdn.com` calls, and review whether passing the `mi_u`/`s` hash to a third party is covered by the privacy policy.
5. **Our pipeline:**
   - Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relay copy, so these warnings can be settled.
   - Resolve the redirect chains in a follow-up probe to check UTMs and the landing page.
   - Raise the HTML truncation limit, or extract the footer separately, so unsubscribe and address checks can run.
