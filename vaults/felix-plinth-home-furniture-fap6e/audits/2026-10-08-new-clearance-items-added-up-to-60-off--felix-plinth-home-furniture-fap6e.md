# NEW clearance items added: up to 60% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crate send today with the same "up to 60% off clearance" hero. I got "Up to 60% off NEW markdowns! Don't miss out →" and "New markdowns on furniture, decor & more just landed 👀" earlier. Now Crate & Barrel Kids sends the same pitch under a "NEW clearance items added" subject. I don't have a nursery. I'm renovating two houses, so a kids-brand version of a sale I've already seen twice today is easy to ignore. Crate's wallcovering launch on 10/07 felt like news. This one is a markdown loop.
- The layout itself is clean. The amber fall-leaf hero says "up to 60% off clearance" in big type, and there's one "Shop Clearance" button. It's easy to read on a phone, and it's honest about the offer. The "furniture in as fast as 7 days" line is the only thing that spoke to my actual renovation problem. After that it turns into a long scroll of strollers, a glider, a crib mattress, a credit card pitch, an app pitch and a design service pitch. I'd skim it and not click. I wouldn't forward it, because nobody I know is buying a Nuna travel system.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "up to 60% off clearance" in large type with a single "Shop Clearance" button leaves no doubt about the offer.
- The amber autumn-leaf imagery fits the season, so it doesn't feel like a stale promo.
- "furniture in as fast as 7 days" tells me delivery timing, which I always want for furniture. Wayfair's blank-hero ceiling fan email gave me nothing like it.
- The product grid shows real prices with strike-throughs (the Babyletto glider at $764.15, the Naturepedic mattress at $314.95), so I can see the discount math.
- Nothing is broken. No empty image boxes and no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- It's the same 60%-off message I've already seen twice today. The "NEW" in the subject doesn't match what I'm seeing. Pottery Barn at least gave me a new angle with "Up to 70% off" for their Fall Sale.
- It's far too much for one email. There's a clearance hero, a category block, a Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event at 50% off, a product grid, a card promo, an app pitch, TheDesignDesk and a store locator. Article's clearance email had one job and did it.
- The offers compete with each other. The hero says 60%, the green block says 50% on select styles, and the category block has its own "40%–60% Off Final Sale" tile. I can't tell which discount applies to what.
- The product picks skew to strollers, car seats and a baby activity chair. That is invisible to a guy furnishing living rooms and primary suites. Several of the picks, like the Nuna strollers and the dresser, show no discount at all.
- The footer fine print is a dense wall of exclusions, and I can't read it without zooming. It undercuts the "up to 60%" headline.
- The card, app and design-service blocks bury the sale. The "chance at $500" app promo has no explanation I can see.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse to one offer and one hero. Pick either the clearance hero or the Semi-Annual Event, not both stacked.
- 2. Cut the send count, or make each send genuinely different. Three near-identical clearance emails in one day drives unsubscribes.
- 3. Segment kids and nursery content to people who've bought or browsed it. Send me the home furnishings clearance instead.
- 4. Show a few real clearance items in the first screen, with a "was/now" price, instead of a generic hero.
- 5. Move the card, app and TheDesignDesk blocks into one slim strip, and trim the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids clearance: 60% off, in-stock furniture ships in 7 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final-sale nursery & kids' furniture, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, dressers and rugs marked down, with in-stock pieces delivered in as fast as 7 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New clearance added today. Prices are already marked, and the best sizes go first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a mobile media query. Its main confirmed problems are about 26 images flagged for missing alt text and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication headers could not be checked because the relay doesn't expose them, and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links sit behind click-redirect domains, so the HTTP probe skipped them. The QA data can't confirm that any CTA resolves, so treat link health as unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` are five separate LiveRamp pixels. Five identical-purpose pixel calls add third-party requests and may be redundant.
- **Repeated first-party pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` appears 6 times, plus `.../p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8b62532c-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. These look like open or impression trackers. The six identical calls inflate counts and add load.
- **Image blocking:** The tracking images are not dependent on remote image loading for content, but any blocked-image client will miss them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 26 `<img>` tags.
  - **Content images (13):** the hero `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FHERO.gif`, `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `971aa10f-...jpg`, and ten `.../lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif` assets. Screen-reader and images-off users get no content, including whatever the "up to 60% off" hero conveys.
  - **Tracking pixels (13):** the `rlcdn`, `mi.`, and `dv.` images. These should carry `alt=""` and are lower priority.
  - **Limit of the data:** The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from an empty one, so confirm in the full source.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype (it should be `&amp;`).
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" ...>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are declared.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
- **Template leftover:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed. It's harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Global CSS:** `*{... max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` applies a universal `line-height:100%`, which can hurt text readability. Mobile breakpoints (`max-width:460px`) are present.
- **Not verifiable:** The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the body for `role="presentation"` on layout tables, heading structure, or link text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u=` / `mi_mid=` values in the image URLs look correctly rendered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Verify against the raw message. If they are genuinely absent, that's a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement miss.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` aligns with the images served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. That is circumstantial and doesn't prove authentication.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** These fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** Not verifiable. All 66 links are redirect-wrapped and unprobed, and the truncated source doesn't show destination URLs.
- **Landing-page match:** Whether the subject's "NEW clearance items… up to 60% off" reaches a matching clearance page can't be determined from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers. If they are present, fix the relay capture so the QA check stops reporting false warnings.
2. Capture `Authentication-Results` or inspect the raw message to verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 13 content images, starting with the hero GIF. Add `alt=""` to the 13 tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Fix `Crate & Barrel` to `Crate &amp; Barrel` in `<title>`, drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` meta tags, and remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment.
6. Rerun the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the redirect-wrapped links in a follow-up pass to check UTMs, destination status, and the footer compliance elements.
7. Review whether six repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` calls and five `rlcdn` pixels are all intentional.
