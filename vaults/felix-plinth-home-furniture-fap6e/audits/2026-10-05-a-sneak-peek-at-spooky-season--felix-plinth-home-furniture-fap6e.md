## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style template (Movable Ink `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` plus click-redirect links) that passes 57% of automated QA with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are missing alt text on 12 content images and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. The compliance warnings are unverified because the relay may have stripped the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, preheader and merge tokens could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **65 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 4xx/5xx status are unverified.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `n=5`. The `s=` value is 64 hex characters, which looks like a SHA-256 hash, possibly of the recipient's email address. I can't confirm this from the truncated source. If so, it is a third-party identity-sync pixel that makes five requests per open and exposes a hashed identifier to a data broker. It should be covered in the privacy policy and consent language.
- **Six identical Movable Ink calls:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…`. These are dynamic-content image requests, each carrying a per-recipient `mi_u` hash. If the service is slow or blocked, those slots will render empty.
- **Open and view pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/112ad0df-68d8-4bbf-b3dc-54966bc75bcc?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Neither has `alt`, and the first-party `dv.` pixel has no file extension. These are expected tracking beacons, not defects. They only add noise to the alt-text warnings.
