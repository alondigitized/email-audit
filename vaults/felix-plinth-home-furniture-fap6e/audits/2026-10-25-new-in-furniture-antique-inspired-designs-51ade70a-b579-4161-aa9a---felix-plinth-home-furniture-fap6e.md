# New in furniture: Antique-inspired designs
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- Same maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is on top again. I've seen it since 10/22 and I skim past it. Under it, though, this is the Pottery Barn I actually want. The Rigato Bed hero, three antique-inspired room scenes, and the brass-pull and wine-barrel-finish copy are Pinterest-folder material. Earlier today's "Online SALE exclusive: Bestselling furniture" was a bestseller dump. This one has a point of view.
- The email is long, and the story gets diluted by the time I scroll to the recommendations, the Christmas Shop banner and the app and rewards blocks. The three collections (Campaign, Summerville, Vintners) are the right idea for two renovations. They only get "Shop Now" links, with no prices, dimensions or lead times beyond a buried "1-4 weeks" in the in-stock band. I'd click, mostly on the dining table and the Campaign pieces. I'd save it before I'd forward it.

## What's working

- The Rigato Bed hero with "New in Furniture" reads clearly, and it's a real product, not a generic sale banner.
- The "Shop the Look" room scenes show how pieces sit together in a space. That helps when I'm furnishing two houses and want a consistent palette.
- Naming the collections (Campaign, Summerville, Vintners) with one-line finish descriptions, like "brass details and rich wood grain", gives me something to save and compare.
- "In-stock furniture, guest-ready pieces delivered in 1-4 weeks" answers my biggest furniture question, which is when it arrives. It's low in the email, though.
- The 10% back rewards card block and Affirm are there. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I notice the rewards math.

## What's weak

- The maroon sale strip is stale and fights the "new arrivals" story. The "Select Styles" fine print makes me wonder whether the Rigato Bed is even included.
- There's no pricing anywhere on the hero collections. "Recommended Just For You" shows product names and sizes but no prices.
- The email runs about 7,800px tall. By the bottom I've hit the Christmas Shop banner, the app download, the card pitch and Affirm. That's four asks after the furniture.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid is curtain rods, a rug and a pendant. It doesn't connect to the antique-furniture theme above it, and it feels generic rather than personalized.
- The same lamp-and-linen sameness from other brands is here, and I'm numb to the sale framing after the seventh send in four days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the maroon strip on new-arrival sends, or put one concrete line in it: "Extra 20% off applies to the Rigato Bed." That tells me whether the offer reaches the hero.
- 2. Add starting prices and a swatch or finish-sample link under each collection. For furniture, a "Order swatches" CTA would win me over.
- 3. Move the 1-4 week in-stock delivery band up under the hero. Make the "Shop in-stock furniture" button more prominent.
- 4. Make the recommendations match the antique theme: dining chairs, consoles, bed frames. Show prices.
- 5. Cut the app, card and Affirm blocks down to one line each, and drop the Christmas Shop banner from a furniture send.
- 6. Space out the sends. Crate & Barrel and CB2 land the same week and compete for the same attention. CB2's "Feels like a splurge" lighting email worked because it had one category and one idea. Wayfair's blank hero box was worse, but Pottery Barn's seventh sale strip is just as numbing.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
A Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style email with 58 opaque tracked links and a sliced-image layout. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing alt text, HTTP image/pixel requests that put the recipient's email address in the URL, and stray non-CSS code inside a `<style>` block. The HTML is truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects, unverified:** 58 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` were skipped by the QA probe, so none were checked for destination, status or redirect chain.
- **Email in plaintext in tracking URLs:** the recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`
- **Unencrypted PII:** the `svopn` pixel is served over HTTP, so the address is sent in cleartext.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with `alt="1"`. A third party receives opens.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from the West Elm host. This is low severity, but it is a single point of failure.
- **Stale assets:** `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg` (`/pb/2025/…`) and `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (`/pb/2022/…`) are reused from prior campaigns. Confirm they are still current.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed), roughly 55 images:**
  - Hero slices `slices_01–16.jpg` under `/1007_WED_AM_NewFurnitureCollections/`.
  - Footer slices `slices_01–18.jpg` under `/Email-Footer/`.
  - Banners: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg`.
  - Product images, including `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, the sliced layout is likely to render blank and give screen readers nothing. The offer and CTA content appears to be image-only.
- **Mixed content:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_…/slices_02.jpg` use HTTP and may be blocked.
- **Stray code in `<style>`:** the source has `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …"); header("Pragma: no-cache");` inside the second `<style>` block. This is PHP leaked into CSS. Parsers may drop or mis-parse the rules that follow it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>` in the visible source.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Web font:** the `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/…Montserrat">` is ignored by Gmail and other clients that strip `<link>`. Confirm the fallback stack.
- **Global rule `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}`** overrides alignment for all paragraphs.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `img {max-width:650px}` at min-width 700px and `img {max-width:100%!important}` at min-width 650px both apply at 700px and wider. The `!important` rule wins, so the 650px cap is ineffective.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA data.
- The recipient email is injected correctly into `coreemail.html` and `svopn`. The exposure issue is covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC could not be assessed because of the relay. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` are aligned subdomains.
- **Footer content not verifiable:** the body unsubscribe link and the postal address (CAN-SPAM) fall in the truncated section or in image-only footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18`). If the footer text exists only as images, with no alt text, it is invisible when images are blocked.
- **Plain-text part is 72% URLs** (11,162 of 15,405 chars). This is a spam-score risk and a poor text fallback.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** no `utm_*` parameters could be seen. Click URLs are encoded `qs=` blobs, so tracking parameters are not visible and the final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Product image links:** product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which suggests the destination pages are on potterybarn.com. This is not confirmed because the redirects were skipped.
- **Campaign tag:** the folder `1007_WED_AM_NewFurnitureCollections` implies an Oct 7 send, but this copy was received Oct 25–26. Check the send date and whether the offer banners are stale.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the hero, offer banners and footer slices. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html` and `svopn` URLs. Use an opaque subscriber key, and serve all pixels over HTTPS.
3. Move the `header(...)` PHP out of the `<style>` block, which most likely points to a template or build bug.
4. Fill in `<title>` and `<html lang>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Replace the HTTP image URLs with HTTPS and retire the 2022 and 2025 assets.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, along with SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Also verify the footer unsubscribe link and physical address as live text.
7. Rebalance the plain-text part so it is not mostly URLs.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to confirm landing pages and `utm_*` parameters.
