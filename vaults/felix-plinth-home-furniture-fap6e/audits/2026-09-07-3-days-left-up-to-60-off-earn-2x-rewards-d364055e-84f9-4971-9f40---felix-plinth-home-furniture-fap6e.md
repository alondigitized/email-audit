# 3 days left! Up to 60% off + earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — same sub-brand, same day even, and I already flagged this exact "wrong Felix" problem this morning with the "3 days left! Up to 60% off + earn 2x Rewards!" send. This is the third near-identical Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel Labor Day email I've gotten today, and this one doubles down on the baby stuff — cribs, high chairs, a literal infant sitting in a green high chair, nursery gliders, "Design Desk" nursery consultations. I don't have kids. I have two houses mid-gut-reno and a growing folder of sofa and rug screenshots that has nothing to do with any of this.
- The countdown timer up top (02:15:38:41) is the one thing that actually works — it's the same beat Crate & Barrel's main-brand emails have been running all week, and it's the only element that would make me glance twice before scrolling past everything else. But the second I hit the crib and high chair sections, I'm done. Not clicking, not forwarding — unless it's to my sister who's actually pregnant, and even then I'd feel weird forwarding a marketing email instead of just texting her "hey Crate & Kids is having a sale."

## What's working

- The countdown timer (02:15:38:41) at the top is sharp and creates real urgency, same as what worked in the main Crate & Barrel Labor Day sends I saw today.
- Category segmentation (bedding, rugs, decor & lighting, clearance, kids furniture) is clean and mirrors the smart breakdown Crate & Barrel's adult-line email used — good structure, wrong audience.
- The "Design Desk" nursery consultation panel is a genuinely nice value-add if you're actually furnishing a nursery — free planning help is a real differentiator over Wayfair or Pottery Barn, who don't offer anything like it.
- Reward Dollars callouts ("earn 20% back," "earn 40%," "earn 50%") are specific and give a concrete reason to buy now if you're in-category.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — full stop. Cribs, high chairs, baby gliders, nursery gear top to bottom. This is the same targeting miss I called out this morning on the earlier Crate & Kids send.
- No adult furniture, no living room, no anything that maps to what I'm actually shopping for right now (sofas, rugs, lighting for two renovations).
- This is the third Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox today between the main brand and this sub-line — cadence is brutal, and CB2 already piled on this same week too.
- The infant-in-a-high-chair hero photo near the bottom is a hard stop for someone outside this life stage — it's not "annoying," it's just a signal to skip immediately.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids sends entirely — I've never bought anything baby-related from this brand family, and hitting me with three of these in one day (plus the main-brand emails) is actively burning trust. Use purchase history, not just "subscribed to Crate & Barrel."
- 2. If cross-sell is the goal, lead with the "Design Desk" nursery consultation as a standalone low-commitment CTA for people who might be gifting or just renovating a spare room — that's more universally relevant than the product grid.
- 3. Cut the send frequency — one consolidated Crate & Barrel family email covering main + kids + CB2 categories beats three separate near-identical countdown emails same day.
- 4. Drop the redundant subject language — I already got "3 days left / up to 60%" this morning; this one needs to differentiate or it reads as a duplicate send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New parents: 2x Rewards on cribs & gliders — 3 days left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Design Desk consults, plus 60% off kids furniture & bedding`
