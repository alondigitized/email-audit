# 🩵 Shop UP TO 70% OFF + Celebrate summer with SAVINGS
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Nineteen. I've been keeping count since the July 10th "FREE design help" send — that was the one that felt like a genuine break in the sale-hammer pattern, and it bought Pottery Barn maybe three emails of goodwill. That goodwill is gone. This one dropped on the same day as "Get the look: The layered bed," which I hadn't fully processed yet, and here's the thing: the email itself is fine. The blue summer sale hero is clean, the 70% off number is legible from six feet, the category grid is actually useful navigation. Pottery Barn can still execute.
- But I'm numb. Two homes in renovation mode means I'm genuinely in the market for furniture, rugs, and lighting right now — I should be the warmest lead on their list. And yet I have to burn through eighteen prior emails this month to even see this one with fresh eyes. There's a "Recommended Just for You" section that has my attention for about two seconds until I spot what looks like a sports hydration product in the carousel, which either means the algorithm had a bad night or I accidentally clicked something at 11pm on Wayfair.
- Mark as read. Not unsubscribe — there are two houses to furnish and I can't afford to — but mark as read.

## What's working

- The summer sale hero is clean and immediate — "Up to 70% off / 100s of new styles added" requires zero decoding
- The category navigation row (Furniture, Outdoor, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Decor, etc.) is genuinely the most useful thing in this email; it works like a shortcut menu
- "Recommended Just for You" is the right structural instinct — personalization in the home category actually matters because my two-home shopping list is specific
- The Rewards earn section is placed where I want it; I check my reward rate before every purchase and this keeps it top of mind
- Zero render bugs, clean image rendering, consistent type — this is a well-assembled email

## What's weak

- Email #19 in 12 days; the cadence is doing more damage than any individual creative decision could repair
- "Up to 70% off" with no deadline reads like a permanent markdown — Crate & Barrel's same-week sends have the same problem, but at least their July 8th send felt like an event; this feels like a fire that's been burning so long I've stopped smelling the smoke
- Subject line is 53 characters, includes 🩵, and caps "SAVINGS" — it trips every spam-feel threshold before I open; compare to Article's "New arrivals just landed" from July 8th, which is 20 characters and no emoji
- At least one recommendation in the "Recommended Just for You" grid looks like a sports hydration product — wrong enough to undermine the entire personalization signal
- No single anchoring product with a price; Article gives me an object to want; this gives me a sale to browse

## Recommendations

- 1. **Drop one email per week, minimum.** No creative execution survives 19 sends in 12 days. This is the change with the largest ROI by far.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the sale.** "Ends Sunday" or "3 days left" turns perpetual discount into an event. Crate & Barrel's volume is just as punishing but their urgency language at least creates a decision moment.
- 3. **Lead with one hero product and its price.** The category grid is useful nav but desire requires specificity — a specific sofa, a price, one link. Article does this every time; it converts harder.
- 4. **Audit the recommendation algorithm.** A sports hydration product in a home furnishings carousel signals something is miscalibrated. Wrong item in the personal grid erodes trust in the whole send.
- 5. **Subject: drop the emoji, lose the plus sign, pick one value prop.** The 🩵 + "UP TO" + "SAVINGS" stack is too much.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer Sale: Up to 70% off furniture + outdoor`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New to sale this week — 100s of styles up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs, lighting and outdoor — new sale arrivals added today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends soon: shop furniture, bedding, and outdoor before it's gone.`

Two CTA/asset links return 403, three image assets are served over HTTP (no HTTPS), and 43 of ~45 images lack alt text. A PHP `header()` code artifact is embedded inside a `<style>` block, indicating a template injection error.

`https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/c/da01b407-b21c-4618-a71b-f83bef0dd682?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&...`

`http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`

**[INFO] 25 tracking links skipped**

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**PHP `header()` calls inside `<style>` block**
```css
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, ...");
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are server-side PHP statements accidentally rendered into the CSS block. All browsers will ignore them silently, but they indicate a template/preprocessor pipeline failure — the email was likely rendered from a dynamic image-serving script and the PHP header calls leaked into the HTML output.

**43 images missing `alt` text**
Includes content images (12× `slices_*.jpg` product grid, product PDPs, hero banners), footer images, and tracking pixels. Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, Gmail app on mobile data) produces a completely unreadable email. Tracking pixels missing alt is low-risk but adds noise.

**3 image assets served over HTTP (not HTTPS)**
| File | URL |
|---|---|
| `spacer_0.gif` | `http://edm.westelm.com/...` |
| `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` |
| `slices_02.jpg` | `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` |

Gmail and Apple Mail will flag or block mixed-content images. Note: `slices_02.jpg` references a 2022 asset path — likely stale footer template code.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**
`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`
Prevents user zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4). Should be removed.

**Empty `<title>` element**
`<title></title>` — no impact on inbox delivery but will show blank in browser-view fallback.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
Not captured by the AgentMail relay. If the header is missing at the MTA level (not just relay-stripped), Gmail and Apple Mail will not display the native "Unsubscribe" link in the header UI, and the email may be deprioritized by spam filters.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policies for >5k/day senders since Feb 2024) is not confirmed present. If Pottery Barn sends at bulk volume, this is a deliverability risk.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed through the relay. Sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` should have aligned DKIM signatures — unverifiable from this capture.

**`meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"`** — Suppresses HTTP Referer on any non-tracked link clicks. Low compliance risk but means any fallback landing page analytics lose referrer signal.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism appear present in the footer (footer HTML is truncated but structure is visible). No issues confirmed.
