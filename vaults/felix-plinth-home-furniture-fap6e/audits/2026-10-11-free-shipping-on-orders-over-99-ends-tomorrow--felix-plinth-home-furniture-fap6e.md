# Free shipping on orders over $99 ends TOMORROW!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- Finally, the main Crate & Barrel brand instead of Kids. A sectional is exactly what's on my list for the second house, so I paid attention. But the subject promised free shipping and the first thing I saw was "final weekend: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals." The shipping message doesn't show up until the second block. I got "Ends tomorrow: Free shipping on orders $99+" earlier today, so this is the same deadline with a new hat on it.
- The email itself is clean. The Lounge 3-piece sectional with the $4,024 price is the one thing I'd actually click. Everything else is cadence fatigue. This is roughly the sixth C&B-family send in two days, and the glassware and coffee maker at the bottom are filler. I'd click the sectional, then drop it in my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I wouldn't forward it. Nobody I know wants six emails about one sale.

## What's working

- The sectional tile shows a real sale price against a struck-through price. That's the only product block where I could do rewards math.
- The Tabletop, Kitchen, Decor, Bedding, Halloween and Thanksgiving grid under the free-shipping banner makes the offer easy to browse by room.
- "Standard shipping; exclusions apply" sits right under the headline instead of being buried. That's more honest than the dense footer fine print.
- The 10% back in Reward Dollars card block is a real reason to care for someone who optimizes card rewards like me.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. It's easier to scan than Wayfair's pergola wall of twelve tiles.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with free shipping, but the hero is the sofa sale. The mismatch is jarring.
- There are two competing offers, free shipping and 20% off sofas, and the sofa sale appears twice. It reads like a stacked, repetitive template.
- "Shop Now" appears over and over without saying what it opens. Pottery Barn at least names the sale in its CTAs.
- The "Select styles" and "exclusions apply" caveats stay vague, and the shipping fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. I'd have to zoom in on my phone.
- The glassware, coffee maker and app-download blocks have nothing to do with sofas, lighting or rugs. Nothing here helps with swatches or samples, color palettes, delivery windows for large furniture, or showroom tie-ins. The "see it at your nearest store" strip is the only nod to that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject, or the reverse. If shipping is the deadline, put the shipping banner first and one hero sectional right under it. Cut the duplicate sofa block.
- 2. Say what free shipping means for furniture. A $99 threshold sounds great until large items ship separately. Clarify delivery cost and timing for sofas.
- 3. Put a real deadline in the hero ("ends Monday 11:59 PM PT"), not just "tomorrow".
- 4. Segment by browse behavior. I clearly look at sofas, so show me sectionals and swatches, not glassware.
- 5. Replace the generic "Shop Now" with specific copy like "Shop sectionals" and "Shop free-shipping picks."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping ends Monday — sectionals up to 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: free shipping on $99+ and 20% off sofas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bestselling sofas & sectionals are up to 20% off through Monday. Select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by 11:59 PM PT Monday. Free standard shipping on $99+, exclusions apply.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on an XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with Outlook (MSO) conditionals. QA passed 57% of checks, with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (27 images) and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The authentication and unsubscribe-header warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed as sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 61 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Destination health and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` to `n=5` beacons are present (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain).
  - The `s=` value is the same 64-character identifier as the `mi_u=` parameter on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images. The same subscriber ID is therefore passed to a third-party domain.
  - Five identical-purpose beacons are redundant. They add requests, and each lacks `alt`.
- **First-party tracking:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/52cfade4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open-tracking pixels. They are expected, but they carry no `alt` and no explicit zero dimensions in the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 27 images are flagged.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`: 12 files, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `d8b928f0-…gif`, `3c9b2911-….jpg`, `971aa10f-….jpg`.
  - One Scene7 image: `s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/10%5FCore/2026%5F1011%5FCB%5FHolidayDecorating…`.
  - Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, which look like dynamic recommendation images.
  - Tracking pixels (listed in section 2) account for the other 7 flagged images; they should carry `alt=""`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the content images have no readable fallback, including any that carry the offer text.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust:none` are set on `*`, which disables user text scaling.
- **Duplicate and conflicting meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times with different values:
  - `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`
  - `date=no`
  - `address=no`
  - `telephone=no`
- **Charset and markup:**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. `<meta charset="utf-8">` isn't self-closed under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML.
- **Leftover template text:** The source contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Conflicting `img` rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both headers were not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown and cannot be assessed from this capture. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the image and tracking subdomains (`image.`, `mi.`, `dv.`) are on `crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so they cannot be confirmed or flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. All 61 tracking links were skipped, so final destinations and UTM parameters are unverified. The visible URLs show only `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` (the MI/Movable Ink platform), with no `utm_*` parameters. Landing-page alignment with the "Free shipping on orders over $99" offer was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 `image.mail…` content images, the Scene7 image and the 7 `mi…/rp/*.png` recommendation images. Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Collapse the four `format-detection` metas into the single combined tag. Drop the redundant charset meta, escape `&amp;` in the title and delete the template placeholder comment.
4. Reduce the five LiveRamp `448796.gif` beacons to one if possible, and confirm that `s=` is a hashed identifier and that sharing it is covered by the privacy policy.
5. Inspect the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` rather than relying on the relay capture.
6. Resolve a sample of the 61 redirect links to confirm 200 responses, correct destinations and UTM carry-through.
