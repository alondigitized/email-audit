# Selling fast: up to 60% off clearance!

## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again. That's four sends from this sub-brand in my inbox this stretch — I flagged the same thing on "The birthday shop is OPEN" and both baby-essentials sends: I don't have kids. I'm two grown houses deep in a Pottery Barn/Crate & Barrel/Wayfair furniture project, and this is the kids offshoot serving me a crib, a stroller, and a "Nuna PIPA" car seat like I'm about to be a dad. Nothing here is for me.
- The "up to 60% off + new markdowns" hero is at least a real number, and the category strip (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) is clean — but every single tile is baby/kid gear. The Venus Williams tennis collab is a genuinely interesting creative beat, more interesting than anything CB2 or Article sent me lately, but it's still a kids tennis outfit on a kid model. Not clicking, not forwarding — straight to archived, same as the last three from this sender.

## What's working

- The 60% off clearance banner is a real, specific number up top — no vague "sale" language.
- Category nav bar (Furniture / Rugs / Kids Bedding / Baby Bedding / Decor & Lighting / Final Sale) is legible and well-organized, more scannable than Wayfair's litter-box wall of text from earlier this week.
- The Venus Williams collab photo is genuinely well-shot and distinctive — better creative than the generic lifestyle shots CB2 leans on.
- Clean render, no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Every hero and every product tile (car seat, glider recliner, crib, dresser, activity chair) is baby/kid furniture — zero adult or general-home crossover, unlike Pottery Barn's "Rejuvenation" cross-promo which at least had adult-relevant home goods.
- "we love these for you" personalization line is flatly wrong — nothing here reflects my actual project (two-house furniture renovation).
- "limited time only! up to 20% off the best baby essentials" is the exact banner language from the baby-essentials email I already scored a 5 on twice this month — pure repeat creative, zero new offer.
- Crate Card / app download / TheDesignDesk blocks bloat the email further with content I'll never use for a brand I'm not even shopping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress me from the Crate & Kids list entirely and route Crate & Barrel main-brand furniture sends instead — I'm demonstrably not converting on four straight kid-gear emails.
- 2. Cut the "we love these for you" personalization module unless it's actually driven by browse/purchase history — right now it reads as a lazy template, not real segmentation.
- 3. If cross-selling Crate & Barrel main brand is the goal, lead with that (the Design Desk / Crate Card content already assumes I'm a broader Crate shopper) rather than burying it under six kid-product tiles.
- 4. Retire the "20% off baby essentials" banner — it's third use this month with identical copy.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Final sale alert: 60% off before it's gone`
