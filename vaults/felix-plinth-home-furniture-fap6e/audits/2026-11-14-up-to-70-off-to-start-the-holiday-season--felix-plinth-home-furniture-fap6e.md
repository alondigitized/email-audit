# Up to 70% off to start the holiday season 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth Pottery Barn email in about two days, and it's the same maroon "Early Black Friday up to 70% OFF" banner I got in "Early Black Friday is ON! Save up to 70%" this morning. The subject adds a gift emoji and "holiday season," but the hero hasn't changed. I'm numb to it. The one new thing is the "Recommended just for you" block. I appreciate that, but it's mostly stockings, so it doesn't feel very personal.
- I'm renovating two houses, so the Remmy swivel chair, the Toscana dining table and the Reeva rug are things I'd actually look at. The "In-stock furniture... delivered in just 1-4 weeks" strip is the best thing in here for me. Shipping lead time is my biggest furniture worry, and Pottery Barn is putting it in a box. I'd probably save this one to my Pinterest-adjacent folder for the table and the chair, and I wouldn't click through today.
- I wouldn't forward it. Nobody sends a friend "70% off" for the eighth time in 48 hours.

## What's working

- The "up to 70% OFF" is huge and I can read it instantly. The offer is unambiguous.
- The eight-category button grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Lighting, Rugs and so on) lets me jump straight to what I'm shopping for. That's better than Wayfair's 20-tile grab bag.
- "In-stock furniture, 1-4 weeks" answers the furniture shipping question directly.
- The Remmy chair, Toscana table and Reeva rug are real products with clean, shoppable photography.
- The "Earn 10% back in rewards" credit card block speaks to how I shop, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The hero is a repeat of today's earlier send. After "Our favorite EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS ❤️" and "Just in! NEW Early Black Friday deals," there's no news here.
- The "Recommended just for you" label is overpromising. Two of the six tiles are stockings and one is a curtain rod, so it looks like generic seasonal merchandising.
- None of the product tiles show a price, a sale percentage or a rating. For a "70% off" email, I can't see a single discounted price.
- The Design Crew sweepstakes, app promo, card promo and Affirm blocks pile on at the bottom. The legal copy is a wall of tiny text.
- The 10 category buttons are all the same weight and the same dark red, so nothing stands out. The pillows & throws, tabletop and bath tiles get the same treatment as furniture.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put real sale prices on the product tiles. For example, show the Remmy chair with a "was/now" price. Crate & Barrel and CB2 both lead with the offer and their rewards math, and I can't tell what 70% off means here.
- 2. Collapse the eight sends into one or two a day, and make this one different. The "In-stock in 1-4 weeks" strip should be the hero of a separate send, since that's a real angle.
- 3. Make "Recommended for you" actually personal. Use my browsing data (dining, seating, rugs) and drop the stockings.
- 4. Cut the footer clutter. Pick one of the app, card or Affirm blocks.
- 5. Add a color or swatch link ("order swatches") near the upholstery. Article does this well with its lookbook-style sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Remmy chair is up to 70% off (ships in 1-4 wks)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `In-stock furniture, delivered in 1-4 weeks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, dining tables and rugs ready to ship, plus 10% back with the PB card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday pricing on pieces that arrive before the holidays.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with about 50 images missing alt text. It also has three plain-HTTP image sources, one of which sends the recipient's email address in cleartext, and several markup defects in the `<head>`. The header-level compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay did not capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links, not probed:** 57 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains such as `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations and redirect health are unverified, so there are no confirmed broken links.
- **HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded over plain HTTP. Clients that block non-HTTPS images will drop the open pixel and undercount opens.
- **Other pixels:**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/5390e92f-...?mi_cid=...` is a second open pixel.
  - The email carries three or more separate tracking beacons.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. This is a dependency on another brand's CDN.
- **Stale asset:** `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg` sits under a `/2025/` path inside a 2026 campaign. It may be a reused module rather than an error, so verify it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 50 images):**
  - All 12 `slices_01–12.jpg` images in `H26_Sale_EBF_LinkfarmOverSolidColor/`.
  - All 18 `Email-Footer/slices_*.jpg` images.
  - Product images such as `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`.

  Many of these appear to be sliced images that carry the offer text, so with images off the email will show little or no readable content.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains literal `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` text. This is leaked server-side code inside the CSS. It is harmless in most clients but sloppy, and it can invalidate the rule that follows it in strict parsers.
- **HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Layout markup:** some tables carry `role="presentation"` and others, including the bare spacer tables, do not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{ }}` appear in the truncated source or the QA output.
- **Email address in URLs:** the recipient address is injected into two image URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second goes out over HTTP, so the address is exposed in cleartext and could land in proxy logs. Pairing `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` with these URLs is the only mitigation visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them, so they may exist upstream.
  - `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is a branded subdomain that matches the `click.e.potterybarn.com` link domain, which is consistent with aligned authentication but not proof of it.
- **Footer unverified:** the physical address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source, so they cannot be checked.
- **Hidden preheader:** the hidden `<div>` contains "Shop EARLY BLACK FRIDAY" in a block with `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`. This is standard preheader markup. The visible pre-text may not match the subject's "Up to 70% off".

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The links are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` tokens, so UTM parameters cannot be confirmed from the HTML. They would only appear after the redirect resolves, and the HTTP probe skipped these links.
- Product image URLs point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is a CDN asset path and not a landing page.
- No landing-page alignment issues can be confirmed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all sliced offer images, and use `alt=""` only for true spacers and pixels. Add `alt="" width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixels.
2. Switch the `http://` assets and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS. Stop putting the recipient email in the pixel query string and use an opaque ID instead.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP from the `<style>` block, and populate `<title>`.
4. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Host the spacer GIF on the sender's own CDN, and confirm whether the 2025 `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2` module should be in this send.
6. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` or the original delivery headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also resolve the redirect chains with a GET follow to check UTM parameters and landing-page status.
