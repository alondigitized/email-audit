# Calling all bakers: you have to see this 🍪
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, cookies. Crate & Barrel wants me to get emotionally invested in a Le Creuset x Levain Bakery collab, and honestly the bakeware itself is gorgeous — that blue-and-white cookie jar, the enamel Dutch oven, the cookie cutting board — but I'm two houses deep in a full gut renovation and this is a lot of scroll for cookies. I don't bake. My wife bakes maybe twice a year and even then it's a box mix.
- The redeeming stuff is buried way down: $135 off a Le Creuset 3.5-qt Dutch oven and 15% off the Aspen collection are real, dollars-and-cents offers I'd actually consider — I'm always shopping cookware and dinnerware for whichever kitchen isn't finished yet. And "TheDesignDesk... free design appointment" at the very bottom is genuinely the most relevant line in the whole email for someone mid-reno, and it's an afterthought.
- Would I click? Maybe, but only if I scroll past the entire cookie narrative to get to the Dutch oven deal — most people won't. Wouldn't forward this to a friend unless that friend is a baker, and mine aren't. This gets marked as read, not deleted, because the Aspen collection tile caught my eye on the way down.

## What's working

- The hero shot is clean and the "Shop the Collab" CTA is right up top, no scrolling needed to find an action.
- $135 off the Le Creuset Dutch oven is a real, specific number — that's the kind of offer that gets my highest-rewards card out of my wallet.
- The cookware styling (navy/cream palette, marble counters) is consistent top to bottom — no jarring creative swaps like CB2's Goop tie-in had.
- "We love these for you" personalized rec block near the bottom actually mixes in flatware and a Dutch oven — closer to my actual list than the five screens of cookies above it.

## What's weak

- This is a bakery/cookie email dressed up as a home-goods send — I don't have a Levain Bakery-shaped hole in my life, I have a rug swatch problem.
- It's the same Levain Bakery collab Crate & Barrel already pushed me yesterday ("The best collab since 🍪 & 🥛 JUST hit our app," 2026-08-03) — different subject line, same product story, back-to-back days. That's the exact cadence fatigue I flagged with Crate & Kids running "20% off school gear" twice in one day.
- Five full screens of cookie jars, cutting boards, and "how to bake the perfect cookie" steps before I get to a dollar-off offer I'd act on — Pottery Barn's dining room email put me in "get the look" mode in one screen; this buries its best asset.
- No renovation-relevant content at all until the very last unit (the free design appointment) — should've been up near the fold for someone on their loyalty list.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere on the Dutch oven or Aspen offers, so nothing pushes me to act today versus whenever.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Le Creuset Dutch oven deal and the Aspen collection discount, not the cookie jar — put dollars-off up top where Wayfair puts its clearance banner, then let the Levain tie-in be a secondary "fun collab" module further down.
- 2. Move the TheDesignDesk free-appointment CTA up near the top third — that's my actual purchase trigger, not cookie-baking tips.
- 3. Cut at least two of the five "more from our new collab" product tiles — the spatula/bench-scraper/cutting-board wall is redundant once I've seen the cookie jar.
- 4. Space this collab out from yesterday's near-identical send, or at minimum vary the offer between the two rather than repeating the same products under a new subject line.
- 5. Add a specific deadline to the Dutch oven and Aspen discounts to create real urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your kitchen upgrade: 15% off Aspen + a Levain collab`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Le Creuset deals inside, plus Levain Bakery's cookie jar we can't stop staring at`
