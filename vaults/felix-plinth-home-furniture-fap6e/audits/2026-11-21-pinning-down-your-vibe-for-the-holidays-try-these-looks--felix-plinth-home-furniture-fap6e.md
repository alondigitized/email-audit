# Pinning down your vibe for the holidays? Try these looks →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the one I wanted from Crate & Barrel today. After four Kids sends in two days, a holiday-styling email in the main brand lane finally shows up. The five "looks" (modern heritage, timeless evergreen, classic cheer, frosted sage, winter harvest) are exactly how I think about decorating, as a palette first and individual SKUs second. It's the closest thing in my inbox to a Pinterest board, so it goes in the saved folder.
- It's also a very long scroll with three jobs: holiday inspiration, Design Desk services, and a Black Friday kitchen sale. The "Shop the Look" buttons sit under big lifestyle photos with no prices, so I can't tell whether the styling is part of the 50% off. The 11/21 "Santa's fave deal" Kids send at least led with one clear thing. I'd click, but I wouldn't buy tonight.
- I'd forward this to my wife for the mantel ideas. I wouldn't forward it to a friend.

## What's working

- The red "Black Friday is on! Up to 50% off + free shipping" banner is right at the top, and the Shop Now button is clear.
- Each look has a name and a short palette description, which is how I plan a room. Color consistency is the thing I care about most.
- The fireplace hero and the numbered looks scan fast. I can skip to whichever look I like.
- The Design Desk block with a free appointment is a showroom-style service nudge. That's the integration I want from a brand like this.
- The "get it by Thanksgiving, order by 11/24" line at the top gives me a delivery deadline I can plan around.

## What's weak

- None of the "Shop the Look" sections show prices or percent-off, so the sale and the inspiration feel like two separate emails.
- The kitchen section (Staub-style cookware, coffee makers, knives) is a hard pivot from holiday decor. It's not what the subject promised.
- Three offers stack up: up to 50% off at the top, up to 40% off kitchen, and up to 50% off at the bottom. I can't tell which one applies to what.
- There's no swatch, sample, or furniture lead-time info, even though the top says 2-day shipping. I can't tell whether that covers anything larger than decor.
- The email runs about 12,000px long. On a phone, I'd quit before reaching the personalized-gifts and final sale blocks.
- It's the fifth Crate send in about two days, with the same Black Friday banner on top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one price or percent-off tag on every look, ideally "Shop the look, from $X." This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Cut the kitchen block, or give it its own send. Keep this one about decor.
- 3. Show 3 to 4 hero products per look as tiles, so one look can be added to a cart.
- 4. Say which items are covered by the "get it by Thanksgiving" 2-day shipping.
- 5. Cap the email at about three sections and one offer, and suppress it for subscribers who just got a Kids send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `5 holiday looks, up to 50% off + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pick your holiday palette: 5 styled looks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the full look and order by 11/24 for Thanksgiving delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Modern heritage to winter harvest, with free shipping on all of it.`
- Compared with the others:
- **Pottery Barn** ran "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS delivered in 1-4 weeks" and at least tried a lead-time story. Crate gives me a deadline but not a lead time.
- **CB2 BLACK FRIDAY** leads with one olive velvet hero and one % off. This email has three offers.
- **Article** gives a room-by-room grid. Crate's look-by-look format is more inspiring but has no prices.
- **Wayfair** would have sent this as 20 emails by now, so Crate is doing better on volume.
