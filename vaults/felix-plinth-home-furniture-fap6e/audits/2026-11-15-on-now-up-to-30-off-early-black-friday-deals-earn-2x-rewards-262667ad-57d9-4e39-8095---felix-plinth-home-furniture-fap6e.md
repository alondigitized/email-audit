# ON NOW! Up to 30% off Early Black Friday deals + Earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel family send in about two days. It's the main brand again, not the Kids arm, so at least I'm the audience. The red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner and the "up to 30% off Early Black Friday" hero tell me the deal in a couple of seconds. That is more than I can say for most of these.
- My problem is that I've now seen this same banner and offer on four or five sends since 11/14. When the 11/15 "Ends soon" Kids email and this one share a hero offer, I'm not discovering anything new. Then the body drifts into espresso machines, Wüsthof knives, stockings and a Gift Shop block. I'm renovating two houses, and I wanted sofas, rugs and lighting up top. A living-room hero opens the email, but the tiles below it lead with entertaining and kitchen brands.
- Would I click? Probably once, on the hero, to check whether the 30% covers furniture. I'd forward nothing. It goes into the Pinterest-adjacent folder only if the Double Rewards window is worth screenshotting for my card math.

## What's working

- The "last Double Rewards this year!" banner is bold and unmissable. It also gives me the card-rewards math I care about, with the Crate Card and a clear end date.
- The living-room hero image with "up to 30% off Early Black Friday" and a single "Shop Now" button makes the primary action obvious.
- The kitchen section shows real prices and discounts (Philips espresso, Nespresso, cutlery, Made In cookware) plus "they ship free!". Free shipping is what I look for on bulky items.
- The top bar, "final weekend to get it by Thanksgiving, order by 11/16", gives the urgency a believable deadline.
- The footer category nav (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, Decor & Pillows, Kitchen) lets me jump straight to my category.
- The Design Desk "free appointment" block fits how I plan two renovations.

## What's weak

- The 2x2 tile grid has "up to 30% off" labels sitting on busy photos. At inbox size they're hard to read, and "furniture" is only one of four tiles.
- The email runs long. Kitchen, stockings, Gift Shop, "we love these for you", app promo and Design Desk each get a block before the footer, so the offer gets diluted. The Gift Shop and stockings blocks are filler for me.
- The fine print and rewards terms are tiny at the bottom, and "up to 30%" never says which categories qualify.
- I've seen the same banner and nearly the same hero on five-plus emails in two days. It's fatigue, not enthusiasm.
- Nothing here says I'm a known customer. There are no rewards balance, no past categories, and no "your wishlist item is on sale".

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one furniture or lighting hero with a real price drop at the top. Make "30% off" specific to the category and tell me what's excluded.
- 2. Cut the stockings and Gift Shop blocks, and send those to a holiday-gifting segment. Lead with whichever category I last browsed.
- 3. Make the tile labels high-contrast, with solid text bars instead of text over photos.
- 4. Add the member's Reward Dollars balance, or a "your Double Rewards ends Nov 17" countdown, to personalize the banner.
- 5. Suppress sends to anyone who has already got the same offer from a sibling brand (Kids, CB2) the same day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off sofas, rugs & lighting + 2x Rewards, ends Mon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of 2026: ends Nov 17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs and kitchen up to 30% off. Order by 11/16 for Thanksgiving delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 2x Reward Dollars on your Crate Card through Nov 17. Free shipping on select kitchen deals.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template (Movable Ink/"MI" styling) with a 57% QA pass rate: 0 failures, 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom, and leftover template artifacts. The header-based compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, not failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity is unverified.** 73 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect chains were not checked.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five identical LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`) fire on open, and each also lacks `alt`. These are redundant calls to a third-party domain; they are a deliverability and privacy surface, not a rendering bug.
- **Other tracking pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7ec6e46e-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are first-party open/dynamic-content beacons, also without `alt`.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0…` value is repeated across the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs. This is a per-recipient hash and is expected for Movable Ink, but it means any forwarded copy carries that identity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 29 images (confirmed by QA):**
  - 16 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (jpg, png, 2 gifs), including `bbf6f9d4-…png` and `6100bbef-…jpg`.
  - 7 dynamic images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png` and others).
  - 6 tracking pixels (the `rlcdn` and `o.gif` beacons above, plus the `dv.` image).
  - The QA output was cut off after the `dv.` entry, so the real count may be higher.
  - With images blocked, content images such as the 16 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the 7 `mi.` images will render as blanks, and screen readers will announce filenames or nothing. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the later `body{…100%}` and block user text scaling in iOS Safari/Mail.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype, which should be `&amp;`.
- **Redundant meta tags.** The `charset` and `format-detection` metas are declared two to three times each.
- **Template leftovers.** The source still contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->`.
- **Responsive coverage.** The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports between 461 and 639px get the desktop layout.

## 3a. Not assessable
The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer, and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].**
  - The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is inconclusive. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders of this volume.
  - Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. This is an artifact of the relay, not evidence of failure. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` aligns with the image domains `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed**, because the source is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 73 links were skipped, and the truncated source shows no landing-page URLs or UTM parameters, so UTM consistency and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed. Click tracking via redirect domains also hides the final URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 16 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and set `alt=""` on the 6 tracking pixels. Set `alt` text on the dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images through the Movable Ink template.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, de-duplicate the meta tags, and strip the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment from the template.
4. Consolidate the five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels into one, or confirm the repetition is intentional.
5. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct, non-relayed delivery, so the WARNs can be confirmed or cleared.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, and resolve the redirect chains for the 73 tracked links, to verify unsubscribe, footer address, UTM parameters, and landing pages.
