- The "Exclusive" tags on tiles make the "only at Crate" claim feel backed up, so it isn't just a subject-line gimmick.
- The Thanksgiving delivery-cutoff strip at the top is a credible deadline, and for furniture shipping that matters.

## What's weak

- It's heavy on kitchen. About two-thirds of the tiles are cookware or small appliances, with only bedding and a rug for the house. The furniture-led send on 11/20 was a better fit for me.
- The closing "Shop top Black Friday kitchen deals" block repeats the cookware from the top 10. It's the same Dutch oven in a new frame.
- The Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes and the "we love these for you" grid at the bottom feel tacked on. Nothing in the grid tells me why it's "for me".
- I see no rewards or card-member angle, no swatch or sample mention, and no showroom content. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I get nothing from that.
- Too many "Shop Now" buttons. Specific CTA copy like "Shop the rug" would help.

- 1. Lead with furniture and rugs, since that's what a renovator is buying, and move the appliances below. Wayfair would segment this by browse history. Crate is blasting everyone with the same kitchen list.
- 2. Add a loyalty and shipping line near the top: "Free shipping + extra rewards on your Crate card". This is where Article and CB2 have been weak too, so there's room to win.
- 3. Cut the duplicated kitchen block at the bottom. Replace it with a "ships by Thanksgiving" furniture row.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel "The top 10: Black Friday deals you'll ONLY find at Crate →"

The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link markup were not available. Several categories below are limited to what the QA data shows. The automated QA reports 0 hard failures and 3 warnings, with a 57% pass rate.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard Marigold/Sailthru-style XHTML table template with MSO conditionals and a 460px mobile breakpoint. Its main verified defects are about 46 images flagged by the QA for missing `alt` attributes and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **99 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are five separate LiveRamp beacons. Each is an extra third-party request on open and has no alt.
- **Per-recipient token in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` and `…/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` carry an identifier in the query string. This is normal for open and personalization tracking, but the token will appear in any forwarded copy and in cached images.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the `<head>`. It is harmless but shows the template was not cleaned.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 46 images.** Content-image examples:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - roughly 33 more under `/m/1/*.jpg|gif`
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a25fbe80-…`
  
  The checker cannot distinguish a missing attribute from a deliberate `alt=""`. For the tracking beacons (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`), `alt=""` is correct, so those are low severity. For the roughly 33 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` images, an empty alt is only acceptable if they are decorative. If product or deal images are image-only, images-off clients and screen readers get no content.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` does not match the subject line. It also contains an unescaped `&` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, which is invalid XML (it should be `&amp;`).
- **Redundant head markup:** `<meta charset>` duplicates `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">`. `format-detection` is declared three times with differing values.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}`:** both apply to all elements and can override inherited alignment and line-height in client-specific edge cases. This is unconfirmed without the body markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. The `mi_u` values are resolved hashes, not raw tokens. The truncated source cannot confirm the body or footer.

- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header not found** (QA WARN).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML. Confirm the body unsubscribe link, postal address, and each CTA's resolved destination.
2. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, `Received-SPF`, `DKIM-Signature`). If the AgentMail relay strips them, mark these checks as "not testable" rather than WARN.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Use `alt=""` explicitly on tracking pixels and decorative images.

5. Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel` (preferably matching the campaign), drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` tags, and remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` template comment.

7. Reconsider the five `rlcdn.com` LiveRamp beacons if load time or privacy exposure matters.
