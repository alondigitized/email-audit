# Don't miss your chance at $1500 for your registry!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Forty-seven. That's Pottery Barn now, and this one at least isn't a bedding email — it's a wedding registry sweepstakes, which doesn't apply to me at all since I'm already deep into two renovations, not planning a wedding. I'm not the newlywed demo, and my wife would notice if I forwarded her a "say I do to your dream registry" email a decade late. The "Recommended Just for You" section at the bottom is the only part that's actually mine — a farmhouse platform bed and a Sausalito dresser I've genuinely seen bouncing around my inbox from three different brands this month.
- The sweepstakes hook itself is fine copywriting — "$1,500 Toward Newlywed Must-Haves" is clear and the hero shot of the bed with the linen bedding is on-brand and calm, same visual language as the "New quilts for all-season comfort" send from a few days back. But it's a swing-and-a-miss on targeting. I'm not registering for anything, and the registry-tools middle section (discount, app, checklist, expert advice) is dead weight for me. This gets skimmed and archived, not clicked, not forwarded — unless I send it to my actually-engaged coworker as a "hey did you know about this" tip, which is a stretch.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — "Win $1,500 Toward Newlywed Must-Haves" tells me exactly what the deal is in one line, no guessing.
- The bedroom shot is genuinely nice — warm wood tones, linen bedding, matches the aesthetic I'm chasing for both houses even if the registry angle doesn't apply.
- "Recommended Just for You" at the bottom actually does personalize — the platform bed and dresser are things I'd click on if they'd led with that instead.
- Fine print is upfront and reasonably scannable ("*$1,500 worth of items. No purchase necessary. Ends 8/3/2026, 9pm PT. See official rules") — not buried like some sweepstakes emails bury the catch.

## What's weak

- I'm not a newlywed and I'm not registering for anything — the entire top two-thirds of this email (sweepstakes, registry discount, checklist, app) is irrelevant to a guy mid-renovation on two houses.
- "Start Building Your Registry" with six product tiles — dining essentials, bedding, furniture, entertaining — none of it framed for someone furnishing a home, only for someone building a gift list.
- The Sausalito dresser and farmhouse bed at the bottom are the same pieces I keep seeing recycled across Pottery Barn, Crate & Barrel, and West Elm sends this month — CB2's "Just in. Already trending." email at least felt like new merchandise, this feels like the same six SKUs on rotation.
- Nothing here reflects that I'm a repeat browser — no "welcome back," no cart reminder, no acknowledgment I've clicked bedding and furniture links in five other emails this month.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this out entirely for non-registry subscribers — if I've never touched a registry link or the wedding category, don't send me sweepstakes copy at all. Lead with "Recommended Just for You" instead; that's the only section earning its space.
- 2. Move the loyalty/rewards content ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") higher — that's more relevant to actual repeat furniture buyers like me than registry checklists.
- 3. Cut the middle "Your Registry Made Easy" block for non-registry segments — four tiles of pure dead weight for anyone not planning a wedding.
- 4. Add shipping/lead-time info near the furniture recs (bed, dresser) — Crate & Barrel's "On sale & ready to ship!" send at least told me delivery windows; this one shows me a $1,500+ dresser with zero shipping context.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: the platform bed everyone's asking about`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shipping in 2-4 weeks — order before it's backordered`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard MSO/Outlook-compatible HTML template from Pottery Barn's ESP (Cheetah/Bronto-style infrastructure on `e.potterybarn.com`/`edm.potterybarn.com`), heavily instrumented with click- and open-tracking. Automated QA passed at 50% with 4 warnings, driven mainly by missing alt text, non-HTTPS image sources, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 53 tracking/redirect links (e.g., `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7...`) use opaque, encoded query strings — actual destination URLs cannot be verified from source; QA skipped HTTP probing on all of them.
- Open-tracking pixel exposes the recipient's raw email address in a **plaintext, non-HTTPS** URL: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. Sending PII (email address) over unencrypted HTTP in a query string is a data-exposure risk (visible to any network intermediary, likely to be logged in plaintext by proxies/CDNs).
- Second personalization instance of the same PII pattern: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — this one is at least HTTPS.
- Third-party ad-tracking beacon present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Turn/Amobee ad-exchange pixel) — not a Pottery Barn/ESP domain, indicates cross-party audience tracking embedded in the message.
- Additional click-tracking beacon: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/0c470a58-e786-4e38-92f8-864e2b6a6eab?mi_mid=019fa0df-0c00-7000-b37e-d39ab5ecc832&cp_tp` — truncated query string in source (`cp_tp` with no value), can't confirm it's well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **40+ images missing `alt` text** across hero slices, product images, and footer banners (full list in QA output) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback states will show nothing.
- **3 images served over plain HTTP**, which will be blocked or flagged mixed-content by most modern mail clients:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- Malformed template artifact in `<head>`: a literal PHP snippet is embedded as text inside a `<style>` block rather than being processed server-side:
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  This is inert (ignored as invalid CSS by renderers) but indicates a broken include/templating step upstream in the ESP build — worth flagging to the email production team even though it has no rendering impact here.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{token}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible source — personalization appears to have resolved correctly. The only personalization-related concern is the PII-in-URL exposure noted in Section 2 (recipient email address passed as a raw query parameter on two tracking endpoints, one over HTTP).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: **not present** (per QA) — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): **not present** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) is not confirmed functional via headers.
- `Authentication-Results` header: **not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture (may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than the sender's actual auth posture).
- Body-level unsubscribe link/physical mailing address: not visible in the truncated HTML source provided, so compliance cannot be confirmed or denied — flagging as unverified rather than a defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All outbound links route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect domain with opaque encoded payloads (`qs=ABB7...`), so no UTM parameters are visible in source — landing-page attribution alignment cannot be verified without following redirects.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` strips referrer data on click-through, which combined with the redirect-obfuscated links means downstream analytics on potterybarn.com cannot rely on document referrer for attribution; all tracking depends entirely on the redirect service's own query parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the two personalization/tracking pixels carrying the raw email address (`svopn.potterybarn.com`, `coreemail.html?email=...`) to a hashed/tokenized identifier instead of plaintext PII, and force both to HTTPS.
2. Fix the 3 HTTP image sources to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking in mail clients.
3. Add `alt` text to all content-bearing images (hero/product slices); decorative-only images (spacers, pixels) can use `alt=""`.
4. Remove or clean up the dead PHP `header()` snippet leaking into the `<style>` block — likely a build/include bug in the ESP template.
5. Confirm with the ESP/deliverability team that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay used for this audit) — required for Gmail/Yahoo 2024+ bulk sender compliance.
6. If feasible, verify a sample of the 53 redirect links resolve to `potterybarn.com` pages with consistent UTM tagging, since source-level inspection can't confirm destination or attribution alignment.
