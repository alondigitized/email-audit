# JUST got bigger! Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-nine. That's where the Crate & Barrel family tally sits since Sunday, and *this* is the second Crate & Kids send in the same window — to a 36-year-old guy renovating two houses without a nursery in either of them. The Memorial Day machine just kept escalating: "FINAL WEEKEND" three times in a row, then a graduation to Crate & Kids, and now Crate & Kids *again* with a "JUST got bigger!" upgrade. Bigger than what — the email you sent me yesterday I didn't open?
- The creative itself is fine. A dresser, some wallpaper swatches, a couple of cribs and changing tables, a clean enough hierarchy. But none of it is for me. I'm shopping dining chairs and bedroom lamps for the second place; I am not shopping a sloped-back crib. Pottery Barn at least had the decency to push *bedding* at me earlier this week — adjacent to my actual project. Article's brand-building send on Sunday felt like an adult talking to me. This feels like a list-hygiene failure.
- I'm not clicking. I'm not forwarding. I'm marking it read and seriously considering finally unsubscribing from the Kids list — except I don't even remember opting in.

## What's working

- Hero offer is loud and specific: "up to 50% off" + "2x Rewards" sits at the top, hard to miss.
- The dresser hero shot is genuinely nicely styled — warm, light, looks like a real room.
- "TheDesignDesk" service card at the bottom is the only piece that feels like it belongs in an adult catalog — that's a real differentiator the parent brand under-uses.
- Visual hierarchy is clean: hero → category tiles → service → footer. No render bugs I can see.

## What's weak

- I'm not the target. Cribs, changing tables, kids' wallpaper — none of this matches a 36-year-old renovating two adult houses. This is the second Crate & Kids send in two days to the wrong list.
- "JUST got bigger!" with no reference to what changed from yesterday's send. Bigger discount? More categories? I can't tell, and I'm not going to investigate.
- "2x Rewards" is dangled but with zero math. CB2's recent send at least felt like it knew who its member was. Here, my Rewards balance, tier, none of it shows.
- Cadence is the real story. Thirty-nine emails from this family in five days, and a Crate & Kids subline I never asked for is now hitting twice. Wayfair's rug send last week earned its open because it surfaced something I needed — this earns nothing.
- Subject line is generic enough it could have come from any of the Memorial Day blasts this week. No segmentation, no name, no project context.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress me from Crate & Kids entirely.** If I've never opened a Kids send and have a long browse history on dining/lighting/upholstery, stop sending. This is the single biggest fix and it's a segmentation problem, not a creative one.
- 2. **If you must send, lead with what's actually new in "JUST got bigger."** Yesterday's email was up to 50% — so what changed? Add a category? A tier? Tell me in the subject and the hero, or don't bother.
- 3. **Show me my Rewards balance.** "2x Rewards" is meaningless without "you'll earn ~$X on a $200 order." CB2 hinted at this better last week.
- 4. **Cut the Kids creative for cross-list recipients.** If you absolutely must email me, show me the *adult* C&B sale with a small Kids strip below — not the reverse.
- 5. **Kill the "FINAL WEEKEND → JUST got bigger" pattern.** It reads as bait-and-switch. If the sale extended, say "extended through Tuesday" — don't pretend it grew.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Rewards 2x — sale extended through Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extended: up to 50% off + your Rewards earn double`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dressers, lighting, and rugs — your saved categories included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One more day to double Rewards on the pieces you've been browsing.`
