# BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS 🔎 Find one that's all you →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- So the subject promises "BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS" and there's technically one photo of a privacy screen up top next to a pergola — and then the email just... disintegrates into the same "everything Wayfair sells" grid I called out on the "RECLINERS you'll love!!" send. Charcoal grills, toilet seats, rain barrels, mini fridges, massage chairs, bar stools — this isn't a privacy screen email, it's the full warehouse catalog with a privacy screen thumbnail bolted onto the header as a fig leaf.
- I'm mid-renovation on two houses right now, and outdoor privacy screens is genuinely a category I'd click into — I've got a deck at the second house that's exposed to the neighbors. But Wayfair buries that one relevant item after two seconds of scroll and pivots to "shop. save. repeat." like a slot machine. Compare that to Article's home office tour I flagged at a 6 — that one stayed on one room, one vibe, one reason to click. This is the opposite: zero curation.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Marking as read and mentally docking Wayfair another point for cadence — this is the fourth or fifth near-identical "grid of everything" send from them this week alone.

## What's working

- The hero privacy screen photo is genuinely well-shot and it's a category I actually need right now for the second-house deck.
- "get 5% rewards on every item, always" is a real, ongoing loyalty mechanic — I'm a rewards-card guy, so a always-on rewards line at least registers.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Subject says privacy screens, hero shows privacy screens, and by the third row it's already pergolas, shower doors, flatware, and LED lanterns — total bait-and-switch on focus.
- The lower two-thirds is an undifferentiated grid: grills, rugs, toilet seats, rain barrels, freezers, playhouses — nothing here says "we know you're renovating," it says "we're dumping inventory."
- "big deals" as the hero banner has zero specificity — no percentage, no dollar figure, nothing I can act on before I scroll forever.
- This is the same structural complaint I had about the "LIVING ROOM SETS" send two days ago — a subject that promises one thing, a body that's a warehouse-wide grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to privacy screens and maybe 2-3 truly adjacent outdoor-privacy items (planters, fencing, outdoor curtains) — if the subject says privacy screens, show me privacy screens, not a toilet seat six scrolls later.
- 2. Put an actual number on "big deals" — 30% off, $50 off orders over $200, something concrete instead of a purple banner with no math.
- 3. Move the rewards module up near the hero — 5% on every item is a decent hook, wasted at scroll depth four.
- 4. Kill the generic mid-funnel grid entirely for a category-specific send like this — that's what turned Article's office-tour email into a 6 for me and what's tanking this one.
