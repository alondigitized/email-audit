# 🚨 Even more at up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again — same day, same brand, and I already dinged the sibling send this morning for the exact same crime: this is a nursery/kids furniture blast landing in a house where I'm renovating a primary and a second home for adults. I scroll and it's crib rugs, star-print bedding, a toddler at a play table, a "10s of items ship free" banner over a highchair. None of it maps to a single line item on either of my renovation lists. The one thing that stops my thumb for half a second is the "enter for a chance at $5000" banner near the bottom — that's real money, but it's buried under nine other sections by the time I get to it.
- This is a scroll-and-close, not a scroll-and-click. Compare this to Article's Labor Day send, which I gave a 7 — bold "ENDS MONDAY" banner, one clean hero, done in three sections. Crate & Kids instead stacks section after section (bedding, rugs, decor & lighting, clearance, highchair, sweepstakes, "we love these for you," app download) like they're trying to hit every SKU in the catalog in one email. Wayfair's "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" email got a 3 from me for exactly this kind of scroll-fatigue whiplash, and this one's giving me the same feeling, just with a baby theme instead of a search-term-salad theme.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — I don't have a kid, I don't shop this sub-brand, and I've now seen this same "Double Rewards + up to 60% off" framing from Crate & Barrel proper three times this week. Straight to read/archive.

## What's working

- The rewards math is stated plainly — "earn 10% 20% back in Reward Dollars" tiers show up repeatedly, which is at least honest math, not a vague "earn rewards" tease.
- Real product photography throughout, no broken images or lorem ipsum — the crib, the rugs, the reading nook shots all render clean.
- The $5,000 sweepstakes banner is a genuine attention-grabber if I'd gotten that far — free money beats another 20%-off banner.
- "Ships free" is called out explicitly on a couple of sections, which answers a real shipping-clarity question before I'd have to click through and find out.

## What's weak

- This is Crate & Kids, not Crate & Barrel — I don't have kids, so literally every hero image (crib, star rug, toddler at a play table, highchair) is irrelevant to my inbox.
- Nine-plus stacked product sections before I hit anything resembling a single clear CTA — no hierarchy, just scroll fatigue.
- Same "Double Rewards + up to 60%" framing I've now seen from Crate & Barrel's main list multiple times this week — brand-family fatigue is real and this doesn't do anything to differentiate itself.
- The subject's siren emoji and "even more at up to 60% off" reads like they're straining for urgency on a discount tier I've already seen twice today.
- The sweepstakes and best offer ($5,000 chance to win) is buried near the very bottom instead of leading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if I've never bought anything nursery/kids-adjacent, don't send me Crate & Kids at all; fold my Labor Day activity into Crate & Barrel's main furniture send instead.
- 2. Move the $5,000 sweepstakes to the top — that's the one section with real stopping power, and it's wasted at the bottom.
- 3. Cut this down to 3-4 sections max — bedding, rugs, decor, clearance can be one scrollable carousel instead of five separate full-width blocks.
- 4. Drop the redundant "up to 60% off" repetition — state the offer once clearly, then let the products carry the rest.
