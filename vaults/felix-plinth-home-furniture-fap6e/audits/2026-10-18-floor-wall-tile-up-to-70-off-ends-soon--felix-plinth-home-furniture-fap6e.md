# FLOOR & WALL TILE ⌛ UP TO 70% OFF ⌛ ENDS SOON ⌛ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- The subject promised floor and wall tile, and I got no tile. The closest thing is a shower-wall photo with a black fixture, about two-thirds of the way down the product grid, with no tile label. The hero says "48-HOUR CLEARANCE, ends tonight." The subject said "ends soon." That's the same bait-and-switch as the 10/18 pergola send, where the pergolas ended up as one tile in a grid. Wayfair keeps writing a category subject line and then sending its one generic clearance template.
- I do like some of the products. The black boucle-style bar stool is my kind of thing, and the shower system is relevant because I'm mid-renovation on two houses. But it's the same grab bag I've now seen about twenty times this week: a grill island, a fireplace TV wall, a hot tub, a ping-pong table, a patio heater. Almost none of it matches how I shop. When I buy for a reno, I build a room around a color palette. This doesn't do that.
- Would I click? Maybe the stool, if I had a free minute. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and go back to the Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel emails I'm actually tracking.

## What's working

- The "48-HOUR CLEARANCE, up to 70% off, ends tonight" banner at the top is loud and clear about the offer and the deadline.
- Struck-through "Was / NOW" prices with star ratings under each tile give me the price anchor and social proof fast. CB2's "Steals of the Week" didn't have that.
- The "bar stools: buy one, get one 15% off" strip is a concrete offer in a category I care about.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35*" is shown prominently. Furniture shipping is always my first question.
- The bottom "Wayfair Rewards" and "Pay Over Time" icons are a nod to the loyalty and financing angle I care about for rewards math.

## What's weak

- Floor and wall tile, the whole subject, isn't in the email. The only vaguely related image is the shower wall, and nothing is labeled tile.
- The deadline conflicts: the subject says "ends soon" and the banner says "ends tonight." Which is it? That weakens the urgency.
- The grid is random: a grill island, a fireplace wall, a hot tub, a ping-pong table. There's no color or style cohesion. Article's Melo sofa send at least styled one room I could pin.
- The category strips (curtains, mattresses, TV stands) are text-only, small, and low-contrast lilac on lilac. They're easy to skim past on mobile.
- "Picked just for you" feels hollow when nothing here reflects my tile or reno search.
- The subject has three hourglass emoji and all caps. It reads like spam, and it's the same format as the ICE MAKERS and WASHABLE RUNNERS subjects from earlier this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Lead with a tile hero and 4-6 tile product cards (floor, wall, backsplash), and put the generic clearance grid below that.
- 2. Use one deadline in both the subject and the hero.
- 3. Add swatch or sample-ordering and square-footage or shipping info on tile cards. That is what a renovator needs.
- 4. Group products by room, such as bath, kitchen, and entry, so I can pin them.
- 5. Cut the hourglass emoji and the all caps.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Floor & wall tile, up to 70% off tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bathroom and backsplash tile: clearance ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shower, backsplash and floor tile marked down, plus order samples before the 48 hours are up.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free delivery over $35 on tile and bath fixtures. Clearance closes at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is the main problem. About 36 images have no `alt` attribute, and 20 impression pixels load over plain `http://`. Link health and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, because the probe was rate-limited and the relay may have dropped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is inconclusive.** Of 12 links probed, 8 returned HTTP 429, including `/daily-sales`, `wayfair.com/`, the bar-stools event, the Tilefy and `W009657036` product links, and the two `wayfairapp.onelink.me` app links. A 429 doesn't show a broken link, but those 8 are unverified. 69 links were skipped and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget ran out (DeerValley sink, Metal privacy fence, `L167-K~W004780728`, Primed Frosted Glass door).
- **Impression pixels use plain HTTP.** 20 pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` (`sz=2x1`) are non-HTTPS. Clients that block mixed or insecure content may not fire them, which would undercount ad impressions.
- **App-link campaign label looks wrong.** The OneLink URLs carry `c=Triggered` while the same URLs carry `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labeled "Triggered" would skew AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Open pixel** is `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`. It is HTTPS but has no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
The QA run flagged missing `alt` on the images below. The counts come from the QA output; I could only inspect the truncated HTML.
- **Content images (4):**
  - `48HR-Clearance_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`
  - `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner…`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wf…`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`
- **Star-rating images (12):** `stars_10.png` ×9 and `stars_11.png` ×3. These are inside product links, such as `…/Tilefy-Trace-2X18-Porcelain-Subway-Tile-L2970-K~JLEF1511.html`. With no `alt`, those links can have no accessible name, and the ratings are unavailable to screen readers or with images off.
- **Tracking images (21):** the `imp` pixels and `b.php`. These need `alt=""`, though this is minor.
- **Linked banners:** the `/daily-sales` banner link has the image as its only content and no `alt`, so the link also has no accessible name.
- The `<style>` block uses media queries, `@font-face` with an Arial fallback (`mso-font-alt`), and the `u + .Gmail` selector. I saw nothing broken in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or the probed URLs. The `_emr` value is constant across links, and `_eml` is unique per link, which is expected.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`. The relay likely strips this header, so it is not a confirmed defect.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical address are unconfirmed.** The probe says an unsubscribe link is always included, but none appears in the probed results or the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses its own scheme: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=9`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_141222`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16579`, and `skurectxid` / `event_rec_txid`. This is consistent across links. Attribution depends on Wayfair's internal parameters rather than standard UTM tags.
- **Landing targets seen in the probe don't match the subject line** ("FLOOR & WALL TILE"). The banner and "Shop Sale" link to the generic `/daily-sales`, and one event link goes to `bar-stools%3a-buy-one%2c-get-one-15%25-off~e411114.html`. Only one tile product link (Tilefy subway tile) is visible. The truncated source may hide a tile-specific event link.
- **Banner filenames suggest older creative.** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH` and `Spring2026` are dated in the filenames. If this email went out in October, those assets may be stale or reused, but I can't tell from the HTML.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 content images and the star images (for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS.
3. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited links, and the 4 unprobed ones, with throttling or a retry.
4. Capture the raw headers at the source (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay, to confirm compliance and authentication.
5. Correct `c=Triggered` on the OneLink URLs to a batch/campaign label.
6. Point the subject-line CTA at a tile-specific landing page, and add standard `utm_*` tags if cross-tool attribution is needed.
