# Shop Early Black Friday deals in stores!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this time it comes from "Design Services." That's the first send in this run that isn't just a maroon banner with a percentage on it. The hero is "Free Design Services" with a $3,000 gift card sweepstakes up top. I'm renovating two houses, so a free designer is the one thing here that's actually relevant to me.
- The offer is buried, though. "Early Black Friday up to 70% off" doesn't show up until after a big block of what/when/where copy and a "Book a free appointment" button. And it's the same early-BF story I've seen in "Up to 70% off for EARLY BLACK FRIDAY" and "UP TO 70% OFF cozy pillows & throws" over the last two days. I'd click through to the design services page, since the design crew is the new angle. I wouldn't forward it. I'd file it in my Pinterest folder only if the "Recommended just for you" grid had anything I needed, and it's stockings and a throw.

## What's working

- "Free Design Services" is a genuinely different hook from the sixteen straight discount blasts, and it fits someone doing two renovations.
- The what / when / where block tells me how it works: in store, virtual, or in my home.
- The category tiles carry their own discounts: sofas and chairs up to 50%, bedding up to 70%, furniture up to 60%. That's easy to scan.
- "Ships free" tags on a few of the tiles help, because furniture shipping is the thing I'm always worried about.
- The 10% back in rewards card block is useful. I do put everything on the highest-rewards card, so the math matters to me.

## What's weak

- Two "Book a free appointment" CTAs plus "Find your store" and "Learn more & RSVP" make about six asks. The primary one isn't clear.
- The sale is below the fold. The headline "Early Black Friday" is a tiny script line inside a design-services panel, so the offer is hard to see.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid is stockings, a faux fur throw and a curtain rod. It reads generic, not like it knows I'm buying for two homes.
- The sweepstakes and promo fine print is huge, and the "up to" percentages look like bait. Nothing here helps me with swatches, samples or delivery timing.
- It's still the same cadence. I got a Pottery Barn email an hour ago, and "Shop early Black Friday deals in stores" doesn't tell me anything new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Open with the sale and put the designer offer second. A single hero such as "Free design help + up to 70% off" with one button would do it.
- 2. Cut the CTAs to one primary and one secondary, and drop the repeated "Find your store" links.
- 3. Personalize the "Recommended" row from my browsing, or at least use furniture and lighting instead of stockings.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample line and delivery windows for the big pieces. Wayfair and Crate & Barrel at least show the "ends today, 2x rewards" deadline, and nothing here tells me when this ends.
- 5. Pull the sweepstakes out of the main flow and make the disclosure shorter.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Book a free Design Crew visit before spots go`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early Black Friday pricing, plus a free design consult for your next room.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas to 50% off, bedding to 70% off, and a $3,000 gift card giveaway.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an SFMC-sent, image-slice template with 54 opaque tracked links. Its confirmed defects are stray PHP code in the `<style>` block, missing alt text on content images, two `http://` images, and recipient email addresses in tracking-pixel URLs. Compliance headers and authentication couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** All 54 links use `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...`, an encoded SFMC redirect. The QA run skipped the HTTP probe for these, so destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Mixed content:** Two images load over `http://`, which clients may block or rewrite:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Third-party and cross-brand dependencies:**
  - Ad pixels: `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`.
  - Cross-brand asset: `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`.
  - Stale asset path: `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`, a 2022 campaign folder.
- **External font:** `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat` is loaded via `<link>`. Most clients strip it, and no fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** The second `<style>` block begins with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` and so on, directly before `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {...}`.
  - Parsers will treat this text as part of the next rule's selector.
  - The `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule is therefore likely discarded, so Apple data-detector link styling isn't neutralized.
- **Missing alt text:** `slices_01`–`slices_12` of the hero (`.../1117_TUE_AM2_EBF_DC_TIHT/`) and `slices_01`–`slices_18` of the footer (`.../Email-Footer/`) have `alt=""` or none.
  - The hero images are the only carriers of offer content, so with images off or in a screen reader the email is blank.
  - Six product images also lack alt (for example `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`).
  - The footer is also built from image slices, so any footer links or text inside them are inaccessible as text.
- **Mobile CSS looks inert:** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`.
  - The visible source has no elements with those classes. All tables are fixed `width="650"` and the images are `width="650"` with no class.
  - The layout probably won't scale on mobile. The `.mobile-hide`/`.mobile-show` breakpoint is also 650px, versus 480px in the first block.
- **Structure:** The `<html>` tag has no `lang`, there's no `<title>`, and the layout tables have no `role="presentation"`. The ampScript comments sit before the DOCTYPE, which can trigger quirks mode in some clients. The XHTML 1.0 Strict DOCTYPE conflicts with the `bgcolor`, `align` and `border` attributes used throughout.
- **Tracking pixels:** The QA alt-text warnings on `coreemail.html`, `lb`, `1` and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...` are tracking pixels, so empty alt is appropriate there. Their `alt` should be `""` explicitly, since a missing `alt` can leak the URL as fallback text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** No `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `${...}` tokens appear in the visible source.
- **Recipient address in pixel URLs:** The address is passed as plaintext in the query string of two pixels. Both are https, but the address will land in third-party and proxy logs.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- **Not verified:** The HTML is truncated, so tokens in the footer can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verified, not confirmed defects:** The relay didn't capture the following, so the QA data can't distinguish a missing header from a stripped one.
  - `List-Unsubscribe`
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click)
  - `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)
- **Footer risk:** The footer is 18 image slices without alt text, and the truncated source doesn't show a text unsubscribe link or physical postal address. If those exist only inside images, that is a CAN-SPAM usability and accessibility problem. Check this in the full source.
- **Sender:** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not observable:** The `?qs=` redirects hide the destination URL, so UTM parameters can't be confirmed.
- **Commented-out link:** The only visible direct URL is inside an HTML comment: `https://www.potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?...marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`. It uses brand-specific params (`cm_ite`, `external_id`) rather than UTM, and it isn't live.
- **Landing alignment:** Not assessable without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `header(...)` lines from the second `<style>` block so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule parses.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all hero slices (offer, dates, CTA) and the product images. Set `alt=""` explicitly on pixels and spacers.
3. Switch `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` to `https://`, or remove the stale 2022 banner.
4. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the tables and images, or rewrite the media query so it matches the markup. Use fluid widths, for example `max-width:650px; width:100%`.
5. Add `lang`, `<title>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables. Move the ampScript comments after the DOCTYPE.
6. Stop passing the plaintext email in pixel query strings. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
7. Make the footer unsubscribe link and postal address live text.
8. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to capture `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve a sample of `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check destinations and UTMs.
