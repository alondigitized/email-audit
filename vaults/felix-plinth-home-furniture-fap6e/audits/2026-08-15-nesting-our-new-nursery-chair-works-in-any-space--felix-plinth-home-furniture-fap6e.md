# Nesting? Our new nursery chair works in any space!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Kids email, another wall of nursery product, another reminder that I don't have a nursery. This is the same mailbox where "New & bestselling bunks and loft beds" landed today too — that's twice from this sub-brand in one day, and my honest reaction both times is the same: not my life right now. "1 nursery chair, 4 ways" with a fabric mood board is a genuinely well-produced piece of content — the styling, the moodboard graphic, the "start with a mood board" concept — someone on their design team is good at their job. But it's aimed at someone furnishing a baby's room, and I'm two houses deep in tile and paint swatches for grown-up spaces.
- I'm not opening this one twice. Mark as read, scroll past, move on — no click, no forward.

## What's working

- The "start with a mood board" concept with the swatch-and-fabric-square graphic is a genuinely nice merchandising idea — it's basically a mini design consult in an email.
- Consistent, clean product photography — the styled nursery vignettes look real and cohesive, not stock-y.
- "New Arrival / Best Seller / Restock" labeling under each SKU is useful shorthand if you're actually shopping this category.
- The "Book a Free Appointment" CTA shows up repeatedly, which at least signals a showroom/design-service angle rather than pure hard-sell.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to me — no kids, not the persona this was built for, and I already got a bunk-bed email from this same list today.
- No offer anywhere. No %, no free shipping, nothing — just five fabric variations of the same chair.
- It's a chair. One chair, four fabrics, endless scroll — compare that to CB2's "Now & Future Classics" sale email that at least had a real percentage-off hook.
- "Book a Free Appointment" repeated 4-5 times feels like it's trying too hard to convert a browse into a design consult I never asked for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if Crate & Kids knows I've never clicked a nursery item, stop sending nursery-specific product drops and fold me back into general Crate & Barrel content.
- 2. Lead with something concrete if you're going to send this to a broad list — a fabric-and-frame customization angle needs a hook (e.g., "swatches ship free") the way CB2's floor sample or sale emails at least try to.
- 3. Cut the CTA repetition — one "Book a Free Appointment" at the top and one at the bottom is plenty; five is noise.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Design your nursery chair — free swatches`
