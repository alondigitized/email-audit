# GOING FAST: Up to 60% off the Summer Sale!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually got me for a second — "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" with the campaign filatan dresser hero is a clean, honest open, and the category grid right underneath (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining, decor, rug, bed & bath, clearance) is exactly the kind of scannable menu I want when I'm juggling two renovations. But then it just... keeps going. A wedding sweepstakes for a $23K Jamaica honeymoon I have zero use for, a fall tablescape pitch, a credit card push, an app download nudge — by the time I hit "we love these for you" with a $2,899 dining table I've already mentally checked out.
- The "in-stock furniture ships in as fast as 7 days" section is the one line in this whole email that speaks directly to a guy mid-reno who's sick of 12-week lead times — that should've been higher up, not buried after a honeymoon sweepstakes. Compare to CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send I gave a 7 — that one felt like ONE idea executed cleanly. This is Crate & Barrel doing what Wayfair does (the "everything and the kitchen sink scroll" I dinged them for yesterday) but with nicer photography.
- Verdict: I'd click "Shop Furniture Sale" if I were already in dresser-hunting mode, which today I sort of am, but I wouldn't forward this — nothing here is surprising enough to send a friend. Skims to "read," not delete.

## What's working

- The hero dresser shot with strikethrough pricing ($1,499 vs $1,995) is a real, specific offer right up top — no scavenger hunt needed.
- The category tile grid (furniture/outdoor/kitchen/entertaining/decor/rug/bed & bath/clearance) lets me jump straight to furniture sale without scrolling through kids' stuff — a relief after this week's nonstop Crate & Kids star-emoji spam.
- "Ships in as fast as 7 days" is a genuinely relevant trust signal for someone mid-renovation who's been burned by backorders — this is the one beat none of my other catalogs (Pottery Barn, Wayfair, CB2) have hit lately.
- The product grid at the bottom shows real prices ($1,799–$3,499) next to real product names, not vague "shop now" mystery boxes.

## What's weak

- The honeymoon sweepstakes with TheKnot/WeddingRegistry is completely off-target for me — I'm renovating houses, not planning a wedding, and it eats prime real estate right after the sale CTA.
- Way too many competing CTAs stacked vertically — Shop Now, Enter Now, Shop Now, Shop Now, Learn More, Download Now — by the fourth one I've stopped reading copy and I'm just scrolling past buttons.
- No mention of Design Desk swatch/sample ordering or showroom tie-in until the very bottom, small and easy to miss, when that's exactly the kind of "help me actually pick a finish" content that would move me on a $2,000+ purchase.
- The new Crate Card push feels tacked on — I already got hit with three C&B/Crate&Kids emails this week, and now there's a credit card pitch wedged in before the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Sale hero → category grid → in-stock/fast-ship section → done. Everything after that (honeymoon sweepstakes, fall table, credit card, app download) should be its own send or its own week.
- 2. Move "ships in as fast as 7 days" up to sit right under the hero — that's the strongest differentiator versus Pottery Barn and Wayfair's generic sale banners I've seen this week, don't bury it.
- 3. Drop the honeymoon giveaway from a furniture sale email entirely — it reads like two different marketing calendars got merged by accident.
- 4. Add swatch/sample ordering or a "book a Design Desk appointment for this dresser" link directly under a hero product, not just in the generic footer banner.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer Sale: 60% off furniture, no 12-week wait`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Campagne Flatiron dresser now $1,499 — shop the full sale`
