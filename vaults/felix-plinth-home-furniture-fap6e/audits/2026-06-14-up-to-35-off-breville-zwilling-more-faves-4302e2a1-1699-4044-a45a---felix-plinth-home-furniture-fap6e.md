## Executive summary

- Fifty. That's the number I'm holding walking into this one. And to be fair: the deals are real. I know what a Breville Smart Oven costs at Williams Sonoma. I know what Zwilling 7-piece sets go for. Thirty-five percent off either of those is not a throwaway promotion.
- My problem is context. Right now my brain is running two renovation projects in parallel — I'm actively choosing between sectionals, fighting with a lighting vendor over a lead time, and deciding whether the second house gets rugs before or after the furniture arrives. Kitchen appliances are a third-tier purchase at best. Breville is on the list for "someday soon," not for this weekend. So even though the offer is credible and the email is clean, it's the right deal at the wrong moment. I'll archive it without clicking.

## What's working

- **Breville and Zwilling are named in the subject** — these are premium brand anchors I recognize cold, and naming them beats "kitchen sale event" by a mile. I opened this.
- **35% is a number** — after the outdoor sale on June 12 (30% off) and three furniture promos on June 13, a specific discount on appliances I can price-check is a genuine refresh of signal.

- **Product grid stays on-theme** — knives, cookware, appliances below the fold. No random pivot to accent chairs halfway through the kitchen scroll. Focused.

## What's weak

- **Wrong category for where I am right now** — Wayfair nailed this last week with "The BEDS we can barely keep in stock" because beds are literally on my active purchase list for both houses. Breville is not. Category relevance is the whole game, and C&B swung at the wrong pitch for me.
- **No deadline anywhere in the email** — "Up to 35% off" with no expiry is ambient noise. CB2's Warehouse Sale had urgency I could anchor decisions to. This floats.
- **Fifty emails** — The frequency has completely eroded the signal value of anything C&B sends me. Even a legitimately good offer arrives pre-fatigued. The June 13 rugs email was #49, this is #50. At some point the inbox relationship is mathematically damaged.
- **Furniture content buried below the kitchen modules** — I could see living room imagery lower in the scroll, which is actually what I want. But the subject trained my brain to "kitchen," and I'd be gone before scrolling that far.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment kitchen offers away from active furniture buyers** — I'm tagged as a renovation/furniture-mode customer. Send Breville/Zwilling to the kitchen-purchase-history segment. Send me the sofa sale.
- 2. **Add an expiry date** — "Ends Sunday" transforms a deferral decision into a real one. Article does this every single send. C&B keeps leaving it vague.
- 3. **For furniture-segment subscribers, lead with the furniture modules** — if the email has both categories, let purchase history determine which hero renders first.
- 4. **Pull back on frequency** — This is the blunt note. Fifty sends in roughly five weeks. Something has to give before unsubscribes become the feedback mechanism.

## Technical Audit — Crate & Barrel "Up to 35% off Breville, Zwilling & more faves!"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has four hard infrastructure failures on webfont delivery and two missing compliance headers that violate 2024 Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements. Accessibility coverage is near-zero: 33 images lack `alt` attributes, including both product images and tracking pixels.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Four webfont assets return HTTP 403**

```
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/1e07d600-3787-4517-bd3d-b140f9e54ec2.woff2
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/7ec25fd5-2752-4d84-b307-af3faabc21bb.woff2
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/aaa41944-25b3-4265-80b2-5564efe9b2d7.woff2
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/fd5f8f50-d539-4a7d-b893-99501d746ef2.woff2
```

The font files are hosted on `crateandbarrel.com` with what appears to be referrer or origin restrictions. Email clients do not send a `Referer` header matching the web origin, so the request is rejected and the email falls back to system fonts in every client that respects `@font-face`. Fix: serve webfonts from a CDN or subdomain with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` and no referrer guard, or use a third-party font host (e.g., Adobe Fonts, Google Fonts) with email-safe delivery.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Template placeholder comment left in production HTML**

```html
<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->
```

A build-time placeholder (with typo "IMPUT") was shipped verbatim. This is harmless to rendering but indicates the send was not run through a pre-flight template linting step.

**[FAIL] 33 images missing `alt` attributes**

All 33 flagged images have no `alt` attribute at all (not even `alt=""`). This affects two distinct categories:

- **Product/content images** (22 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` JPGs + 1 GIF, plus 1 `971aa10f` JPG): render as broken images in image-blocked environments; screen readers have nothing to announce.
- **Tracking pixels** (6× `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`, 5× `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, 1× `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/o.gif`, 1× `dv.crateandbarrel.com` impression tracker): must carry `alt=""` explicitly to suppress screen-reader announcement.

**[WARN] `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta**

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```

Disables user zoom on mobile — a WCAG 1.4.4 failure. Remove `maximum-scale=1` or replace with `maximum-scale=5`.

**[WARN] Global `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`**

```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }
```

Applied to all elements, not just `body`. This suppresses OS-level text scaling for users who need it. Scope to `body` only.

**[INFO] Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags**

The head contains one combined tag and three additional single-attribute tags covering the same directives (`telephone=no`, `address=no`, `date=no`). Redundant but not rendering-breaking.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**

QA reports the header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by CAN-SPAM (indirect, via best practice) and is a hard requirement under Google and Yahoo's February 2024 bulk-sender policy for lists >5,000/day. If the ESP is injecting it downstream, confirm it survives the relay. If not, it must be added at send time.

**[FAIL] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent**

One-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 is mandatory for Gmail/Yahoo bulk senders. Without `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, Gmail will show its own unsubscribe UI rather than the brand's managed flow, and Yahoo may apply deliverability penalties.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured**

SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown through the current relay path. Cannot confirm the email authenticates as `crateandbarrel.com`. If DMARC is in `p=reject` or `p=quarantine`, a relay that breaks DKIM alignment will cause inbox-level failures.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 CTAs use click-tracking redirect URLs and could not be probed. UTM parameter coverage and landing page alignment cannot be confirmed from this audit. A manual spot-check of two or three redirect chains (e.g., "Shop Breville," "Shop All Sale") is needed to verify:
