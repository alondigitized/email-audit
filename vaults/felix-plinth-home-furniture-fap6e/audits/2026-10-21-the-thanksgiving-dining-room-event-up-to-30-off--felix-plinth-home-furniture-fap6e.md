# The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event: Up to 30% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Crate & Barrel send today pushing the same 30% off dining furniture. This morning's was "Hosting Thanksgiving? Up to 30% off dining furniture →." Same offer, same round wood table, new subject line. I'm renovating two houses and a dining room event is on topic, so the pitch lands. But a second email with the same hero within hours reads like a mail merge nobody checked. It's the same problem as the Philips and SMEG sends on 10/20, and I'm already in a C&B family snooze.
- The hero is clear in about a second: a big "up to 30% off" over a nice table shot, with a plain "Shop Now" button. Then the email keeps going. It adds a hosting guide, two more buttons, a Design Desk sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" strip of air fryers and cookware, a rewards card ad, an app ad, a second Design Desk block and a store locator. By the end it's a catalog, not an event email. CB2's "up to 60% off" warehouse sale did the one-offer thing much better.
- Would I click? Yes, on the table or the dining tables tile, because I'm shopping for a dining room. I'd save it to my inspo folder, not forward it. The Design Desk free appointment is the one thing I'd mention to a friend. I'm not forwarding a discount I've already seen once today.

## What's working

- "Up to 30% off" in big type over a dining table photo is unambiguous, and it matches the room I'm working on.
- The four tiles (tables, seating, storage, lighting) let me jump straight to the category I need. The chandelier tile also shows the 20% off lighting offer.
- The Design Desk free appointment fits how I shop. Article's free interior design pitch got my attention the same way, and having it here with a $5,000 gift card sweeps is a reasonable hook.
- The rewards card block (10% back in Reward Dollars) speaks to my highest-rewards-card habit, even though it's buried.
- The photography is warm and neutral, so the palette stays consistent for someone matching a color scheme across two homes.

## What's weak

- It duplicates this morning's send almost exactly. Same table, same 30%, new copy. Pottery Barn at least changes the story between sends.
- Too many competing blocks after the hero. Three shop buttons in a row, then a sweepstakes, products, a card, an app and a second Design Desk ad. The offer gets diluted.
- The "we love these for you" row has stainless steel bowls, an air fryer and cookware, so it feels generic for a dining room event. The sofa and plates are closer, but none of it is dining furniture.
- Prices on the product captions and the sale-price line under the hero are tiny. I'd have to zoom on my phone.
- I see no clear deadline or ship-date promise in the body. It's a Thanksgiving event, and I need to know if a table orders and arrives in time. Furniture shipping clarity is missing.
- The "Thanksgiving Hosting Guide" tile has text sitting over a busy photo with a thin button. It's lower contrast than the top hero.
- There's no swatch or sample mention anywhere, which is a big miss for someone matching finishes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send the same offer twice in one day. Fold this into the morning send, or make the second one different, such as a dining-room-only ship-by date or a "last chance" version.
- 2. Cut the body to hero, four tiles, one Design Desk block and the footer. Drop the app ad, the card ad and the second Design Desk block.
- 3. Add a ship-by-Thanksgiving message ("order by X for delivery before the 26th") and a swatch or sample link in the dining section.
- 4. Replace the generic product row with dining items: chairs, sideboards, a chandelier, with sale prices set larger.
- 5. Put a real preheader in. Right now I see nothing to complement the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Order by Nov 5 for Thanksgiving: dining tables up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tables, chairs, sideboards: up to 30% off, delivered in time`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free swatches and a free design appointment, plus ship-by dates for every table.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 20% off select lighting. See which pieces still arrive before the holiday.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event: Up to 30% off!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout from an ESP template. Its main confirmed issue is missing alt text on 31 images, about 12 of them content images. The compliance and authentication WARNs are most likely capture artifacts of the relay, not confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated and 59 links were not probed, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 59 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. I can't confirm destinations, redirect health or UTM handling.
- **Open and tracking pixels (7):**
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp). These are 5 beacons carrying the same hashed subscriber identifier.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e5ccb949-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Identifier exposure:** The same `mi_u=56aaa2787a…` hash is appended to the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs. These are dynamic recommendation images, so every image request is tied to one recipient. This is a design observation, not a defect.
- **Pixel count:** Five LiveRamp pixels with sequential `n=` values in one send is unusually heavy. It adds request weight and blocked-image fragility.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 31 images):**
  - 12 content images are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (e.g. `ef861136-…jpg`, `38f614bb-…jpg`) and `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`. One more is the scene7 asset `2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary…`.
  - 7 recommendation images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` are likely product images.
  - 7 are tracking pixels (`rlcdn` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv…`). They should carry `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`. Missing the attribute entirely makes screen readers announce the filename or URL.
  - The QA check can't distinguish an absent `alt` from `alt=""`. If the content images are the hero and promo tiles, images-off clients will show no readable offer text.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-resize blocking:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` value. Together they disable user text scaling on iOS.
- **Title and markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML, and doesn't match the subject.
- **Template leftovers:** The `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` placeholder comment is still in the source. There are also duplicate `format-detection` meta tags and an empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` rule in the MSO block. These are cosmetic.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The only media query is `max-width:460px`, which is narrower than the common 480–600px range. Large phones and small tablets between 461px and 639px get no responsive rules. I haven't rendered it to confirm.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"` is set and VML/Office namespaces and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present for Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML has no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The identifiers in image URLs are resolved. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Header WARNs (not confirmed defects):** The QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` header. The check itself says these may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so their presence at the sender is unknown. Re-check against raw source headers before calling it a failure.
- **Sender:** The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a brand-owned subdomain, consistent with the image hosts `image.`, `mi.` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Not verifiable:** The footer, unsubscribe link and physical address are beyond the truncation point.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 59 tracking links were skipped, and no final landing URLs or UTM parameters were resolved. Whether the "Dining Room Event" CTA lands on a matching page is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 content images and the 7 recommendation images.
2. Set `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions on the 7 tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` overrides.
4. Escape the title (`&amp;`), use a subject-aligned title, and strip the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder and duplicate meta tags.
5. Consolidate the 5 LiveRamp pixels into one if they serve the same purpose.
6. Re-run QA with full-source and raw-header capture, outside the relay, to verify the unsubscribe headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer and the 59 redirect chains with their UTM values.

**QA context:** 57% pass rate, 0 hard failures. Of the 3 warnings, the two compliance ones and the authentication one are likely relay artifacts. The alt-text warnings are the only items confirmed as real defects.
