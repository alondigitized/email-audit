# TREE SKIRTS are heeere!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Tree skirts in the subject, and I got a tree skirt in exactly one tile. The hero is a red Santa-sleigh skirt under a "Just Dropped / New Price Cuts" banner, and it's the only holiday thing in the whole email. Below it is a scroll of fireplaces, pergolas, frying pans, bidets and refrigerators. That's the same furniture catalog dump I complained about in the 9/30 "HOLIDAY ACCENTS" send, and it's the sixth Wayfair email since 9/26.
- There's no offer either. "New Price Cuts" names no percent, no dollar amount and no deadline. The only specific number is "Gazebos & pergolas from $100," which has nothing to do with the tree skirt I was promised. I'm renovating two houses, and a peel-and-stick tile tile or a fridge tile might get a glance. Nothing here gets a click.
- Verdict: skim, don't click, don't forward. It gets archived, not saved to the Pinterest folder.

## What's working

- The hero tree skirt is clean and readable. The red Santa-sleigh skirt under the tree matches the subject.
- The two-column tile grid is tidy, with big images and tap targets that are fine on a phone.
- The Wayfair Rewards and Flexible Financing icon row is a decent trust cue, and the rewards math is something I care about.
- The kitchen tile and refrigerator shots (the sage-green fridge and the peel-and-stick backsplash) are the closest thing here to my two renovations.

## What's weak

- "New Price Cuts" has no number. The brand's own 9/26-9/29 "72-HOUR CLEAROUT, 60% off" emails at least stated the discount. This one is vaguer.
- The tree skirt is the only holiday item, yet it's the whole subject line. Pottery Barn's "season of gathering" send and CB2's "Holiday Collection" actually built a holiday story.
- Bird baths, bidets, frying pans and laundry hampers have no cohesion. I can't tell what the email is about, and there's no palette or room story for someone matching two houses.
- The Wayfair credit card "Apply Now" block in the middle interrupts the browsing. The financing footnote is tiny, and the red "SALES, SALES & YES, MORE SALES" tag adds noise with no offer behind it.
- Cadence fatigue. It's the same lamp-in-three-brands problem, just with pergolas. I've seen outdoor fireplaces and pergolas in prior Wayfair sends this week.
- No delivery-window or swatch/sample info, which I'd expect for furniture.

## Recommendations

- 1. Deliver on the subject. Make the top half a real holiday block: 3-4 tree skirts, stockings and garland, with a named price ("Tree skirts from $24").
- 2. Put a real number in the banner, like "Up to 40% off through Sunday," with a real deadline instead of a recycled countdown.
- 3. Cut the grid to 4-6 tiles that share one theme, and drop the bidet and hamper tiles.
- 4. Move the credit-card block to the bottom, and lead with Rewards instead.
- 5. Segment by browsing history. Someone who's looked at tile and fridges should get a kitchen-renovation email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tree skirts from $24 — new today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your holiday tree, finished: skirts + stockings`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new price cuts on kitchen tile and fridges through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sleigh prints, plaid and faux fur, all in stock and shipping fast.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "TREE SKIRTS are heeere!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block and per-recipient tracked links. The QA run reported 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main gaps are missing alt text on many images, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 of the 12 probed material links returned 429 (rate limited), which the probe flags as "may be valid". These cover the app CTA, both "Shop Now" and "Shop Sale" links, the logo, and the banner images. They are unverified, not failures.
- **4 links were not probed** (70s time budget):
  - `.../on-sale-now%3a-outdoor-fireplaces-se`
  - `.../popular-medium-sized-dog-products-se`
  - `.../10%27-w-x-10%27-d-pergolas--se82167.`
  - `.../steel-pergolas-you%27ll-love-se76757`
  - The first two end in `-se` with no numeric ID, unlike the `-se24347` and `-se76757` slugs. The pergolas URL ends in a trailing `.`. These may be truncated in the QA output, but if they are the real hrefs they would likely 404. Verify them against the raw HTML.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` impression pixels. Clients that block mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is HTTPS.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback:** the app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the OneLink app URL has `c=Triggered`, while every other link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labeled as triggered will misattribute in AppsFlyer.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 6 content images and 2 icon or disclosure images:**
  - `wfna_0331_new-markdowns_2024_email_banner-02.jpg`
  - `Must-Have+Tree+Skirts.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_new-markdowns_2024_email_top-steals-goin…`
  - `wfna_0331_new-markdowns_2024_email_scores-in-your-…`
  - `wfna_0331_new-markdowns_2024_email_sales-sales-mor…`
  - `WFCC2%402x.png`
  - `WFCC%202%20Disclosure.png`
  - The `Must-Have+Tree+Skirts.jpg` image sits inside a linked element, so its accessible name falls back to the filename or URL (the probe reported the link text as `[image: Must-Have+Tree+Skirts.jpg]`). The same applies to the banner links to `/daily-sales`.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Responsive CSS:** the `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` and `u + .Gmail` hacks are present for Gmail. No rendering defects were identified in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the rest of the body was not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data. Each link carries a unique `_eml` value plus a shared `_emr`, so personalization resolved at send time. The source was truncated, so tokens further down the body can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` is not found. QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed omission.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) is not found, with the same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` is not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- An unsubscribe link was included in probing per the QA note, but its result isn't shown. The footer and physical postal address are outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters** on any link. Attribution relies on proprietary params (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_137249`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16360`). This is consistent and fine for internal tooling, but GA-style campaign reporting won't pick it up.
- **Landing alignment:** the two "Shop Now" and tree-skirt image links go to `/daily-sales/filters/must-have-tree-skirts-se24347.html`, matching the subject. They share `eid`/`mdlid`, and only `eid` differs (`row_0_Grid-100` vs `row_0_column_2_items_0_image`), which allows per-element click tracking. The banner and "Shop Sale" links go to `/daily-sales`. The landing pages returned 429 and could not be verified.
- **Stale asset names:** several banners are named `wfna_0331_new-markdowns_2024_…`. If the creative is reused, confirm the content is current. This can't be determined from the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and banner images. Give each linked image alt text that describes its destination, for example "Shop must-have tree skirts".
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to them and to `b.php`.
3. Check the 4 unprobed URLs, especially the `-se` slugs without IDs and the trailing-dot pergolas URL, against the raw HTML.
4. Correct `c=Triggered` on the OneLink app link to match the batch campaign, and change `af_web_dp` to HTTPS.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the original message headers, not the relay. Until then, treat compliance as unverified.
6. Re-probe the 429 links with throttling or a longer budget to confirm they return 200.
7. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters alongside the existing params if cross-channel analytics matter.
