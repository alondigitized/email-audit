# You need to see this designer’s coastal bedroom
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Crate & Barrel again with a design-story email — and it's the second one this week after yesterday's Lisa Pollock coastal bedroom send. Wait, actually, checking the subject: this IS "You need to see this designer's coastal bedroom" — so this is a near-duplicate of yesterday's "How designer Lisa Pollock crafted the perfect coastal bedroom." Same designer, same canopy bed, same "slowing down influenced every choice" quote block, same rattan dresser shot. That's a real problem — I remember this exact bed from yesterday's audit, and now I'm getting round two of it in my inbox 24 hours later.
- The email itself is well-shot — that white canopy bed with the linen drapes is genuinely nice, and I can see myself considering it for the primary bedroom refresh. But it just goes on forever: mattress upsell, rattan dresser, "up to 25% off bedroom," "new markdowns up to 60% off clearance," a Design Desk sweepstakes, a credit card pitch, a "we love these for you" product grid with a KitchenAid mixer randomly thrown in. It's not one story, it's six different asks stapled together under a coastal-bedroom banner.
- Would I click? Maybe on the hero "Shop the Look" if I hadn't already seen this bed yesterday. Forward to a friend? No — this is starting to feel like C&B is repeating creative, and that's not a good look to pass along. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero canopy bed shot is genuinely aspirational and on-brand for a coastal bedroom refresh — good staging, natural light, no clutter.
- The "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" banner gives me one concrete number tied directly to the category I'm shopping.
- The rattan dresser and console vignettes are exactly the "swatch-and-sample," texture-driven content I'd want when picking finishes for a room redo.
- Clean visual hierarchy at the top — bed photo, headline, CTA, no clutter in that first fold.

## What's weak

- This is a near-repeat of yesterday's Lisa Pollock coastal bedroom email — same bed, same designer quote treatment, same rattan pieces. I already saw this.
- Way too much stapled on after the design story: mattress ad, clearance markdown banner, Design Desk $5000 sweepstakes, credit card pitch, random product grid with a KitchenAid mixer that has nothing to do with a bedroom.
- No clear single CTA — by the time I hit "new markdowns added! up to 60% off clearance" I've forgotten this was supposed to be about a coastal bedroom.
- The 25% off banner doesn't say what qualifies as "bedroom" (furniture only? bedding too?) — feels like the honest-offer fine print is just missing rather than buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send the same designer/bed content twice in two days — if Lisa Pollock's coastal bedroom already ran yesterday, this send needed genuinely different creative, not a rehash.
- 2. Cut this email in half — pick either "design inspiration story" or "bedroom sale," not both plus a sweepstakes plus a credit card plus a random product grid.
- 3. Put the 25% off bedroom offer right under the hero, with a plain-language qualifier (furniture, bedding, or both) instead of burying it two-thirds down.
- 4. Drop the KitchenAid mixer from a coastal-bedroom email entirely — it breaks category focus and reads like a generic filler module.
