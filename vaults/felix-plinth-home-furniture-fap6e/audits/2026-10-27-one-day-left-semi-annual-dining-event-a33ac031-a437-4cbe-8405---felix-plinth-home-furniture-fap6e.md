## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also has missing alt text on content images and 10 non-HTTPS third-party pixels. QA pass rate is 43%. The HTML is truncated and the headers weren't captured, so compliance and link-level checks are mostly unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **64 tracking links were skipped** because they are click-redirect domains, so no destination, redirect chain or 404 was verified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels use plain `http://`.** They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`. Clients and proxies may block them as mixed content, and HTTP requests expose the `d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2` identifier in cleartext.
- **Unneeded third-party calls:** the email loads 11 Dotomi requests on top of the first-party pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0040b932-…?mi_cid=…`. This is a deliverability and privacy-footprint concern. Image proxies such as Gmail's will fire or strip these inconsistently.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images are missing alt text.** Eight images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt` attribute: `2a60c92d-…`, `0e75b0b4-…`, `d992c912-…`, `d4ddcdc1-…`, `fad212c9-…`, `aa3c1482-…`, `a84a5116-…` and `ad0b5473-…`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these may be unreadable, and some may carry the offer content.
- **Tracking pixels have no `alt` at all.** This applies to the Dotomi, `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels. Pixels should carry `alt=""`, not omit the attribute.
- **Zoom is restricted.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. That is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- Text-size adjustment is also disabled (`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `body` and `*`). This can hinder users who rely on larger system text.

- **Not confirmed as defects:** the checks found no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` headers. The tool notes these may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
