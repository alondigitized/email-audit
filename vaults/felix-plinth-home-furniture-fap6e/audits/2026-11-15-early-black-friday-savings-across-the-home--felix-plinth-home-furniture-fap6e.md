# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SAVINGS across the home
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- Ninth Pottery Barn email in about two days, and I'm numb to it. It's the same maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner from "Up to 70% off to start the holiday season 🎁" and "Early Black Friday is ON!" yesterday. This one has no new hook. The subject is all caps and generic. It does at least do some work in the body: living, dining and bedroom sections, with a Whitaker chair, a Portola bed and an extendable dining table priced out.
- I'm renovating two houses, so the room-by-room layout fits how I shop. It's still a long scroll that ends in a Christmas shop, a sweepstakes, an app promo, a credit card and Affirm. I'd skim it, maybe save the dining section to my Pinterest-adjacent folder, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably click once, on the dining table.

## What's working

- The "up to 70% off" banner with a SHOP NOW button is clear and sits at the top.
- Room-based sections (living room, dining room, bedroom) match how I'm furnishing two houses.
- Whitaker chair, Portola bed and the dining table show "Was / Now" pricing, so I can judge the discount.
- "Ships Free" tags on the throws and bedding callouts help, since delivery is a big deal for furniture.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has named items, so it feels slightly personal.

## What's weak

- The subject is generic and in caps. It repeats "Early Black Friday" for the ninth time, with nothing new to open on.
- Several tiles (Throws, Pillows, Rugs, Lighting, Bedding, Decor) have no product names or prices. It reads as a category wall.
- The Christmas Shop banner, the Design Crew sweepstakes, the app download and the credit card pitch all compete with the sale. It's the Crate & Barrel "can't decide what it's about" problem again.
- The legal fine print is huge, tiny and dense. I can't read it on my phone without zooming.
- Nothing about swatches, samples, color matching or delivery timing for big furniture. "Up to 70%" with "Select Fabric" on the chair makes me wonder what actually qualifies.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one concrete, time-boxed hook, such as a specific price on a hero piece or an end date. Don't repeat the banner I've seen for two days.
- 2. Cut the body to the three room sections and one recommended row. Move the app, Affirm, credit card and sweepstakes into a footer strip.
- 3. Put product names and sale prices on every tile, and say which furniture ships free and when.
- 4. Add a swatch-and-sample link near the furniture sections, and shrink the legal text.
- 5. Make the preheader do real work by saying what's new in this send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dining tables up to 70% off, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your living room, bedroom & dining picks: up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on throws and bedding, plus order fabric swatches before you buy.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Whitaker chair and Portola bed are marked down. Sale ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout with opaque ESP click-wrapping. Its main code-level problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 50 images), three `http://` image sources, and the recipient's email address sent in plain text to third-party endpoints. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The automated pass rate was 57%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links not probed.** They all point at click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Recipient email in cleartext over HTTP.** The open pixel is `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. The address travels unencrypted, and the pixel may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Email in a query string on an image.** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is used as an `<img>` source. The address will appear in server logs.
- **Third-party tracker.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-tech pixel (Turn). The QA scan also flagged `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/5e807323-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` as a pixel.
- **Cross-brand asset.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister-brand domain. It works, but it is a dependency on another property.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 50 images:**
  - `slices_01`–`slices_12.jpg` in `/pb/2026/US/1115_SUN_AM_EBF_SaleArossTheHome/`.
  - `slices_01`–`slices_18.jpg` in `/Email-Footer/`.
  - Product images such as `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - Banners such as `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`.
  
  Because the layout is sliced images, it will be blank or unreadable with images off and unusable for screen readers. Some of the flagged items (`spacer_0.gif`, the Turn, svopn and clickcp pixels) are tracking or spacer images, so for those an empty `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Insecure image sources.** Three images load over `http://`: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the svopn pixel. Clients may block them.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag prevents pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The `<html>` tag has none.
- **Stray PHP in a `<style>` block.** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` appears there. It's harmless but invalid CSS and suggests a templating leak.
- **Attribute-selector media queries.** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match if those exact class values exist. This can't be confirmed in the truncated source.
- **Web font.** Montserrat is loaded via a Google Fonts `<link>`, which many clients (Outlook, Gmail) ignore. The fallback stack in the visible source is only `verdana,arial`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The recipient address is populated in the tracking URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip these, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing. The affected senders are Gmail and Yahoo bulk senders, who require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from this capture, again likely a relay artifact.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated portion, so they can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Inconclusive. Visible links are wrapped by `click.e.potterybarn.com`, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs are encoded in the `qs=` token and weren't resolved. Product images do point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is consistent with the stated sale content, but landing-page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices and product images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. Change the three `http://` assets to `https://`, especially the svopn pixel, which carries the recipient's email address.
3. Stop putting the recipient's email address in image URLs, or use an opaque hashed ID.
4. Add `<title>`, `lang="en"`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove the stray PHP code from the `<style>` block.
6. Re-run the audit with raw headers to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Resolve the click-tracking redirects to check the final URLs and UTM parameters.
