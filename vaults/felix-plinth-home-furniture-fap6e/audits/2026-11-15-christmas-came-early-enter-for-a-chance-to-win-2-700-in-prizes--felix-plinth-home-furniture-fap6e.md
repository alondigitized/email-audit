# 🎁 Christmas came early! Enter for a chance to win $2,700+ in prizes →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Crate & Barrel family send I've dealt with in two days, and this one is the Kids arm running the same giveaway as this morning's "Enter for a chance to win $2,700+." The new part is a 🎁 and "Christmas came early." I have no kids, so the hero (a baby in a round ball-pit crib) isn't aimed at me. My gut reaction was "wrong aisle, again."
- The screenshot is a long scroll with a lot going on. The giveaway sits on top, then an "early black friday is here! up to 50% off" block with furniture and bedding, then a red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner, a Feeding America block, gifts, nursery chairs, strollers, an app download and a Design Desk plug. The Double Rewards banner and the Black Friday sale are the only things I'd act on. Both are buried below a prize-draw hero for a baby gear brand pack.
- Would I click? Probably the "Shop Top Categories" button at the Double Rewards banner, if I were already shopping for the renovation. Would I forward it? No. It would make a decent "this is what Kids email looks like" screenshot, but that's it. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The Double Rewards banner is a clean red block with a clear "Shop Top Categories" CTA. That's the one place the email speaks to how I buy, because I'm on the highest-rewards card.
- The giveaway tile grid (Petite Kenzy, Mushie, Super Smalls, BabyBjorn and the rest) is easy to scan. Each tile shows its gift card amount.
- "Up to 50% off furniture / bedding & bath / rugs" is a concrete offer, and the rugs and bedding tiles are things I'm shopping for.
- The Crate & Barrel Kids header and logo are clean, so I know who's sending it.

## What's weak

- The hero is a baby product. I'm 36, renovating two houses and have no kids, so the giveaway is irrelevant to me.
- The "Christmas came early" subject has nothing to do with the body. The email opens with a giveaway and a Black Friday sale, and nothing says Christmas.
- There's too much in one email. It has a giveaway, a sale, Double Rewards, a charity block, gifts, nursery chairs, strollers, app promo, credit card promo and Design Desk. No single hero wins.
- The Double Rewards block repeats the thin "Earn 2x" line from the header strip and from yesterday's "last Double Rewards of the year" send. Same beat, fourth time.
- The bottom of the email is a wall of tiny legal text. I wouldn't read it, and it's unreadable on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one job. Either run the giveaway as a short, clean send, or run the sale. Don't stack both with the Double Rewards message.
- 2. Segment. Send the Kids giveaway to people who've bought nursery or kids items. Send me the main Crate & Barrel sale with the rugs and bedding blocks. Pottery Barn Kids already splits this properly, and Wayfair's "Table Lamps" and "Area Rugs" sends at least name a category I care about.
- 3. Make the subject match the body. If "Christmas came early" stays, the hero needs to show it. Otherwise lead with the prize.
- 4. Move the Double Rewards CTA up. It's the one thing that converts someone like me.
- 5. Cap frequency. Four family sends in two days is a lot. Pottery Barn's ninth email in two days is the same problem.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with Movable Ink dynamic image blocks and 84 click-tracked links. QA shows no hard failures (57% pass, 0 issues, 3 warnings), but the source has widespread missing image alt text, five third-party tracking pixels, and zoom-blocking viewport settings. The HTML is truncated, so anything below the head and the footer is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** QA skipped the HTTP probe on all 84 tracking links ("Tracking/click-redirect domains"). Redirect targets, 404s and redirect chains were not checked.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` are the same pixel fired five times, differing only in `n`. They are third-party data-sync beacons and add 5 extra requests.
- **Hashed identifier in image URLs.** The `s=` value on `sr.rlcdn.com` starts with the same hash as `mi_u=` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. A recipient-level identifier is therefore passed to a third-party domain on every image load.
- **Movable Ink image.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with identical URLs. Check whether the repeats are intentional or a template-loop duplicate.
- **Open pixels.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/37bb43d1-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are two separate open/view trackers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 42 `<img>` tags (QA).** These include:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, for example `3930ebb6-…jpg` and `8c4ce5d9-…gif`.
  - The Scene7 image `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F1104%5FCBK%5FQualityGifting…`.
  - The Movable Ink dynamic images.
  - Tracking pixels (`448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv.…/o/37bb43d1…`). These should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Alt text may be absent rather than empty.** The checker doesn't distinguish the two, so confirm against the full source.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks user scaling, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Fixed pixel widths inside the ≤460px media query.** `.mobile{width:460px!important}` and `.showmobile{width:414px!important}` can overflow on 320–390px viewports. This is a potential issue, not confirmed.
- **Conflicting desktop image rules.** The `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` rule is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At equal specificity the later rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px cap is dead.
- **Unescaped ampersand in `<title>`.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Leftover template artifacts.** These include the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, an empty `<!---->`, and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->`.
- **Duplicate head tags.** There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `meta charset`). `format-detection` is declared 3 times with overlapping values.
- **Global `*{line-height:100%; -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`.** This can clip text and disables text-size adjustment. `.f10` forces 10px text on mobile.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers (QA warnings).** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was not found. QA itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so these may be capture artifacts rather than sender defects. They can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer not verified.** The visible HTML is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.
- **Sender alignment.** The From address is on `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown for the reason above.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 84 links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are present in the data provided. No issues are claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Consolidate the five identical `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels into one, and review the hashed `s=` parameter against the privacy policy.
4. Escape the title (`&amp;`), delete the template leftovers and duplicate meta tags, and fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries.
5. Re-run QA with direct access to the original headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`). Resolve a sample of tracking links to verify landing pages and UTMs.
