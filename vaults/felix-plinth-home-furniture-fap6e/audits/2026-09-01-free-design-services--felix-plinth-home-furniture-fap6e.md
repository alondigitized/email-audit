- The "Free Design Services" hook is genuinely different from the last three Pottery Barn sends and speaks directly to my two-house renovation situation.
- "Choose a time — but spots go fast" gives real urgency to a real service, not just inventory.
- The what/when/where breakdown (in-home, virtual, in-store) removes the "is this even for me" friction — I'm not near a store much, so knowing virtual is an option matters.
- Product photography is warm and on-brand — that living room shot up top could be my actual mood board.

## What's weak

- The sale grid underneath is a straight copy-paste of what I got in "It's ON 🎉 The Labor Day Sale" (08-31) and "100s of NEW Labor Day deals" (08-31) — same categories, same percentages, same burgundy "SHOP ALL SALE" button.
- "Recommended Just For You" shows curtain rods, a dining table, a platform bed, a swivel chair — no sizing, no swatch/finish options, nothing that reads as actually tailored to what I've browsed or saved.
- The in-store design class (Sept 13, 10AM) has no location listed in the visible screenshot — if I don't know which store, that's dead weight for me.
- Rewards card pitch ("Earn 10% Back") is buried way at the bottom after two other footer blocks — for someone who buys on the highest-rewards card like me, that should be higher.

- 1. Split this into two emails or lead harder with design services only — burying a genuinely differentiated offer (free design help) under a sale grid I've already seen three times this week kills its impact. CB2's been doing the same over-mailing thing and it's numbing me to the whole Crate & Barrel family too.
- 2. Add the store location/city to the in-store design class block — as-is it's unusable for anyone not already local.
- 3. Make "Recommended Just For You" actually personalized — tie it to browsing history or the design appointment intake, not a generic furniture grid Wayfair-style.

1. Technical Summary

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTA and hero links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` (SFMC click-tracking redirects) — 50 tracking links were skipped by automated HTTP probing since these are redirect endpoints, not final destinations; link validity beyond the redirect layer is unconfirmed.
- Open tracking pixel present: `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — recipient email address is passed in plaintext as a URL query parameter (`sv_sveme=`).
- Third-party ad/retargeting pixels present: `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (Turn/Verizon Media conversion pixel) — third-party tracking beacons embedded in the email body.
- One legacy insecure image reference: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (also flagged in Rendering section).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 27 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, including all primary content slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_08.jpg`) and product images (curtain rod bracket, dining tables, platform beds, swivel chair) — screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or fall back to filenames.
- Tracking/pixel images also lack `alt=""` — while non-critical visually, unlabeled 1x1 pixels are lower priority than content-image alt gaps above.
- Two images served over plain HTTP instead of HTTPS: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing HTTPS.
- Stray malformed `<style>` block content: literal PHP `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');`, `header("Cache-Control: ...")`, `header("Pragma: no-cache")`) appear inside a `<style>` tag as plain text rather than valid CSS — this is invalid CSS that will be ignored by renderers but indicates a template-authoring error (PHP code leaked into a CSS block).
- Media query breakpoint mismatch: one `@media` block targets `max-width: 480px` (targeting `table[class="wrapper"]`, a class not present in the visible body markup) while a second `@media` block targets `max-width: 650px` (`.mobile-hide`/`.mobile-show`) — inconsistent breakpoints across the same document.

- Personal email address is embedded in cleartext in two tracking/link URLs: `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and the open-tracking pixel `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — PII exposed in URL query strings, which may be logged by intermediate proxies/CDNs.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance status from headers alone. No unsubscribe link visible in the truncated HTML body either.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) header not detected — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — sender authentication status cannot be verified from available data.
- Given the relay-capture caveat, these should be flagged as **unconfirmed**, not confirmed violations.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
