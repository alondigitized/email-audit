# 🏆 Winning styles from Venus Williams’ new collab
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "winning styles from Venus Williams' new collab" — I already saw this exact collection yesterday with a different subject line ("Elite-level design"), so my first reaction is déjà vu, not excitement. It's the same tennis-court hero, same green velvet accent chair, same dresser, same striped bedding — just repackaged with a new headline. The kids' room stuff is genuinely well-styled (that green velvet chair and the tennis-ball throw blanket are cute details), but I'm not shopping for a kid's room in either house right now, so this is a scroll-past for me even before the repeat-send fatigue kicks in.
- This wouldn't get a click from me, and I definitely wouldn't forward it — there's nothing here relevant to my actual renovation (primary + second home, adult spaces). Compare this to Crate & Barrel's "olive sofa" email from the same day, which I gave 7/10 because it was an actual sectional I'm considering for the second house's family room — that's the bar for what gets my attention from this brand family, and Kids doesn't clear it.

## What's working

- The tennis-court hero with Venus Williams in the doorway is a strong, distinct visual — better than another generic "cozy season" banner.
- Product photography is clean throughout — no broken images, no render bugs.
- The green velvet accent chair and matching upholstered bed are genuinely nice pieces with clear "Shop Now" CTAs under each.
- "Design Desk" section with the free interior-design consultation offer is a nice trust/service touch, even if it's buried mid-email.

## What's weak

- This is the second Venus Williams email from Crate & Barrel Kids in two days ("Elite-level design" on 9/24, and this one same-day) — that's not cadence, that's spam-adjacent repetition.
- Entirely irrelevant to my persona — I'm renovating grown-up spaces in two homes, not a kid's bedroom. Zero swatch/sample program, zero showroom integration, zero adult furniture.
- No concrete offer anywhere I can see — no %/$ off, no free shipping threshold, just "shop the collection." Compare to Wayfair's bins-and-baskets email (3/10, sure, but at least it led with "$20").
- Long, scrolly, multi-section grid — by the fourth near-identical bedroom vignette I've mentally checked out.
- No urgency, no loyalty/rewards messaging, nothing that treats me as a repeat customer versus a cold subscriber.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop resending the same collection under a new subject two days in a row — pick one angle and let it breathe, or this trains me to skip C&B Kids emails on sight.
- 2. Add a real offer — a %/$ threshold or bundle deal would give this a reason to click beyond "nice photos."
- 3. Segment: I don't have kids' room needs on file with this brand (if they track that) — this should never have hit an inbox that's mostly bought adult/second-home furniture.
- 4. Tighten the email length — cut two of the four near-identical bedroom vignettes; lead with the single best room and one CTA.
