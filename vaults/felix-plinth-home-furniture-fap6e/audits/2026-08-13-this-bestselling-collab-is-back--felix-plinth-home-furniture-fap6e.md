# 🎃 This bestselling collab is back
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a Peanuts x Pottery Barn collab — that's the first email all week that's actually made me stop scrolling instead of auto-archiving. I recognize this collab, my wife had the mugs from it a couple years back, and "bestselling collaboration is back" is a real hook, not filler copy. That said, this is squarely Halloween/fall seasonal decor — pumpkin plates, nostalgic dining essentials, whimsical bedding — and I'm mid-renovation on two houses right now, so cute Snoopy sheet sets aren't solving my problem. It's a nice change of pace creatively but not where my budget's going this week.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block near the bottom is actually the more relevant section for me — curtain rods, a dining table, an area rug, towels — that's renovation-adjacent stuff, though it's buried under four screens of Peanuts merchandise first. I wouldn't click into the Halloween collection, but I might scroll straight to that recs grid if I open it on a slow moment. Not forwarding this to anyone, not deleting on sight either — probably a mark-as-read.

## What's working

- The Peanuts collab hero is genuinely distinct — first non-generic creative I've seen from Pottery Barn all week, versus the fifteenth "in stock & on sale" banner from 2026-08-11.
- "Ships Free" callout on the bedding section is clean and answers a real question upfront, same beat as the 2026-08-13 quick-ship email that scored well for exactly this kind of clarity.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid at the bottom actually maps to renovation categories — curtain rods, dining table, rug, towels — closer to my actual shopping list than the seasonal stuff above it.
- Design Crew Sweeps (free design appointment, $1500 gift card entry) is a legit loyalty-adjacent hook I haven't seen in prior sends this week.

## What's weak

- It's four full screens of Peanuts pumpkin plates and tablecloths before anything renovation-relevant shows up — I'm not decorating for Halloween, I'm hanging drywall.
- No discount code or percentage anywhere in this whole email — CB2's "Now & Future Classics" send at least had a real sale headline; this is pure collection-launch mode with zero price anchor.
- Rewards/credit card math is nowhere near the top — the Pottery Barn credit card push is crammed at the very bottom next to Affirm and the app download, easy to miss on a phone.
- Three near-identical product-and-CTA blocks stacked in a row (Whimsical Bedding, Nostalgic Dining Essentials, Shop the Full Collection) start to blur together — feels like padding once you're past the first "ooh, Snoopy" reaction.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Recommended Just For You" personalized grid up to right under the hero — that's the section actually built for repeat renovation shoppers like me, don't bury it after four Halloween screens.
- 2. Put an actual number on this collab — even "Peanuts collection, prices from $X" would beat pure lifestyle copy with no anchor.
- 3. Pull the Pottery Barn credit card rewards callout (10% back) up near the top or into a persistent footer band — that's the one thing that'd actually make me click through given how I shop.
- 4. Trim the collab to two product blocks instead of three — bedding + dining is enough, the third "shop full collection" banner is redundant with the "Shop All Halloween" button right above it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MSO/Outlook-compatible HTML email with responsive media queries; QA pass rate is 57% driven almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and two non-HTTPS legacy asset domains, plus unverifiable auth/unsubscribe headers at the relay layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary hero link routes through a click-tracking redirect: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEs...` (Ensighten/Bronto-style tracking domain) — functional, not flagged as broken.
- 55 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect domains — not a confirmed defect, just unverified).
- Open-tracking pixels present and functioning as expected: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` — both are 1x1 tracking beacons, correctly recipient-personalized.
- **Issue:** The `sv_emopen` open-tracking pixel is served over `http://`, not `https://` — see Rendering section below for blocking risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **41 images missing `alt` text** across hero banners, product "slices" images, footer navigation slices, and the customer-service/open-tracking pixels. This affects screen-reader users and image-blocked inboxes (no fallback text renders). Examples: `F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg` (product/footer nav images), `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-5-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-l.jpg`.
- **3 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`**, which many mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) will block or flag as mixed content:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - The open-tracking beacon `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...` is also HTTP, meaning open tracking may fail to fire in clients that block mixed HTTP content.

- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) and Apple Mail reformatting disable (`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`) are correctly present.
