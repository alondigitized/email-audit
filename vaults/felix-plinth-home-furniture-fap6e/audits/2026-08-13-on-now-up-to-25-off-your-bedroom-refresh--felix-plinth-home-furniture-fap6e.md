# On now: Up to 25% off your bedroom refresh!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one I'd actually read. The canopy bed hero with "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" and a real price — Sale $1,899 Reg. $2,299 on the Cofeuur Upholstered Canopy Bed — is exactly the kind of anchor that gets me to stop scrolling. I'm juggling two renovations right now, and a category grid breaking out beds/dressers/bedding/lighting by discount tier is basically a shopping list laid out for me.
- Where it loses me is the back half. After four screens of legit bedroom merchandising, it swerves into an AD Magazine sweepstakes with a fashion-editorial photo that has nothing to do with furniture, then a rug launch, then a generic "we love these for you" grid with dinner plates and cereal bowls mixed in with a sofa and a dresser — that's not "for you," that's just inventory. Compare that to the C&B send from yesterday (8/13, "Up to 25% off bedroom furniture, bedding & more," 7/10) which stayed tighter on bedroom the whole way through; this one starts stronger but sprawls.
- Would I click? Yes, on "Shop Bedroom Sale" right up top, before it derails. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is gift-worthy or a "hey look at this" moment for a friend, it's just my own to-do list. Mark as read after clicking through the hero, basically.

## What's working

- The hero bed + explicit sale/reg pricing is the clearest offer any C&B email has shown me all week — most of their sends bury the number.
- Category tiles (beds 30% / dressers 25% / bedding 60% / lighting 60%) let me triage by what I actually still need for the second house.
- Two clean CTAs stacked ("Shop Bedroom Sale," "Shop All Bedroom") right after the grid — no hunting for the button.
- Rewards card teaser ("new! the card you love just got better... Reward Dollars") is relevant since I run everything through my highest-rewards card anyway.

## What's weak

- The AD/Howell x Harlier sweepstakes block is a hard topic break — fashion photography, no product, no price, just "enter to win a $1,000 Gift Card." Feels bolted on.
- "We love these for you" mixes dinnerware and cereal bowls into a bedroom-refresh email — that's the exact "same lamp in three brands' inboxes" fatigue I already have, except here it's category fatigue in one email.
- App download push with "$500" teased vaguely at the bottom — no context on what that even is, reads like filler.
- By the time I hit TheDesignDesk and the social icons, I've forgotten this started as a bedroom sale email — hierarchy just gives up two-thirds down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the bedroom category grid + rugs. Everything from the AD sweepstakes onward should be its own send — it's diluting a genuinely strong offer.
- 2. Replace "we love these for you" with actual bedroom cross-sell (nightstands, lamps, mirrors) instead of dinnerware — if it's going to claim personalization, make it true.
- 3. Give the Crate Card teaser a number (X% back, $X welcome bonus) — "just got better" with no math doesn't move a rewards-card guy like me.
- 4. Move rugs up or cut it — it's tonally fine but breaks the bedroom narrative right after the strongest CTA block.
