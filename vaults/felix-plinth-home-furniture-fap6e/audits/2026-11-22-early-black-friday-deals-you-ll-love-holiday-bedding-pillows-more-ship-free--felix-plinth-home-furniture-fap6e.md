**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. It's the same maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner I've been getting since 11/16, including the 11/22 sends "Shop early, save BIG" and "UP TO 70% OFF for every room." I scrolled past the hero on muscle memory. What got my attention was the "Festive Favorites Ship Free" tile grid under it, because that's new. That's the only reason this isn't a pure delete.

The Toscana Extendable Dining Table and Banks Extendable Dining Table are what I actually shop for, and the Cozy Cloud bedding is a reasonable fit. ## What's working

- The "Ship Free" tile grid is clear. Bedding, pillows, throws and table linens each get their own tap target, and "All Free Ship" sits at the bottom.
- The dining tables in "Recommended Just for You" match what I'm buying. Banks and Toscana both have size callouts (76"–128", 60"–125"), and I need those for furniture.
- The Design Crew Sweepstakes with the $3,000 gift card has a clear "Book a Free Appointment" button. It ties into showroom and design services, which suits a two-house renovation.
- The 10% back rewards card block is clear. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I do the math on stuff like this.
- The layout is clean, with white-on-photo labels that I can read on a phone.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is a repeat. I've seen this exact maroon creative on 11/18, 11/19, 11/20, twice on 11/21 and again today. At this point I read it as wallpaper.
- The subject says "Holiday bedding, pillows & more SHIP FREE." The hero says up to 70% off. The Christmas Shop says up to 50% off. That's three offers competing for the same eye.
- The recommendations lean holiday: stockings, a faux fur throw, ornaments. Only two of the six products are furniture, and there's no price on any of them.
- The "Just for You" label feels thin. Nothing in it shows that Pottery Barn knows I've browsed extendable tables.
- The fine print under the rewards block is dense and tiny. The Affirm, sweepstakes and exclusions text reads like a wall.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the maroon hero for a day and lead with one product-led story. For me that's extendable dining tables with a visible sale price and ship dates.
- 2. Show prices and percent-off on the "Just for You" products. Without them, I can't tell whether anything is actually on sale.
- 3. Cut the stockings from the personalized block. Use browse data to show me furniture and bedding.
- 4. Move the Design Crew sweepstakes higher. It's the one fresh idea in the email.
- 5. Space out sends. Six near-identical emails in a week trains me to ignore the whole brand.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout. 45 images lack `alt` attributes, 3 resources load over `http://`, and the recipient's address is exposed in cleartext pixel URLs. The HTML is truncated and the click links are opaque redirects, so footer compliance, UTM and landing-page checks can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 56 links were skipped by the QA probe because they sit on tracking domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjgs…`. The click endpoint's availability and its destinations are untested.
- **Stale and cross-brand assets:**
  - `https://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2025/US/1110_MON_AM_FreeShipHolidayTTOP/slices_01–11.jpg` is a 2025 campaign path (Nov 10) reused in a 2026 send.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a cross-brand dependency.
  - If any of these paths are retired, the email breaks.
- **Third-party and multiple trackers:** three beacons are present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`, `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/4193589a-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`, and `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&…`. The `coreemail.html` pixel is also a beacon.
- **Leaked code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is server-side PHP pasted into the CSS. It is invalid CSS and should be removed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (45 images):**
  - Hero and banner slices: `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg`, `slices_01–11.jpg` in `1110_MON_AM_FreeShipHolidayTTOP`, and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`.
  - All six product images, such as `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - The footer slices: 18 in `Email-Footer`, plus `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`.
  - Because the content is sliced images with no alt text, a client with images blocked shows a blank body with no usable links.
  - Tracking pixels need `alt=""` rather than no attribute. Only the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`) is correct.
- **Insecure images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/…/slices_02.jpg`, and `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/…` can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom.
- **Layout tables:** the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` after the logo lacks `role="presentation"`. The other tables have it.
- **Web font:** the external Montserrat `<link>` is stripped by Gmail and others. The fallback stack should be confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens, such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`, were seen in the visible source.
- **Email address in cleartext:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` puts the address in a query string.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` sends it over plain HTTP.
  - Both are PII in URLs, and the second is also exposed in transit.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** the QA check did not find them. It notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, which is consistent for alignment.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and postal address are outside the truncated HTML, so they can't be verified. The footer is built from image slices, so these must also exist as live text links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All 56 CTAs are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`. The final URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible and were not probed. No UTM or landing-page alignment issue can be confirmed or ruled out.
- Product images are hosted on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, so the asset path matches the destination domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content slice and product image, and `alt=""` to pixels and spacers. Also add live text or bulleted fallbacks for key offers.
2. Move the three `http://` resources to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and `svopn.potterybarn.com`).
3. Stop putting the recipient address in plain URL query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use an opaque ID instead.
4. Delete the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Add a `<title>` and `lang="en"`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the spacer table.
6. Replace the reused 2022 and 2025 asset paths and the `westelm.com` spacer with current assets for this campaign.
7. Re-run the checks against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer unsubscribe and address. Resolve a sample of tracking redirects to check UTMs and landing pages.
