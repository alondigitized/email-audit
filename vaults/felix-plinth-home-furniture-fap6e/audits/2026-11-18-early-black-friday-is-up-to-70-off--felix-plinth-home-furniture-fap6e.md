# Early Black Friday is UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. This is my fifth or sixth send since 11/16, and "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" is the same pitch I got this morning in "Early Black Friday deals to beat the holiday rush." The sender name is now "Pottery Barn Sale," so I'd already filed it as the sale email before opening. The hero is a dining table with taper candles and a big "Up to 70% off" over the photo. The offer reads in about a second. The headline is thin script laid over a busy photo, though, and it's harder to read than the offer deserves.
- The body has more going on than the maroon-banner-only sends I've been seeing. It has category tiles, a "Recommended just for you" grid, a Christmas Shop block, a design appointment pitch, the app, the card and Affirm. That's a lot of modules for a 70%-off email. The best part is the deadline: "Order by Fri, 11/20 at 12 PM PT" for Thanksgiving delivery. That one line is a real reason to act. I'd probably click through to Decor or Sofas, since I'm in the middle of two renovations. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- "Up to 70% off" with a SHOP NOW button is clear right at the top.
- The Order by Fri, 11/20 at 12 PM PT line gives me a credible deadline. It also settles whether it ships before Thanksgiving, which matters for furniture.
- The category tiles show tiered numbers (Sofas & Chairs 50%, Decor 70%, Gifts 50%). That tells me where the deepest discount is.
- The "SHIPS FREE" tags on the Tabletop & Bar, Bedding and Pillows & Throws tiles answer the shipping question without a click.
- The photography is classic Pottery Barn, with a fireside chair, ochre vases and green bedding. It matches the palette in my home-project folder.

## What's weak

- The hero script is thin and sits on a cluttered shelf and candle background. It's the weakest part of the email, and it's the part that should hit hardest.
- It's a repeat. This is the same Early Black Friday message as 11/17 and this morning, with a new subject line. Compare Article's "Black Friday: It's go time," which was one banner, one button and one number. That one landed faster.
- The Recommended just for you grid is stockings, a throw, a curtain rod, a quilt and a Cayman bed. It feels like a generic bestseller list, not something chosen for me. The products show no prices and no sale badges.
- The Christmas Shop banner is a separate "up to 50% off" offer, and it competes with the 70%.
- The footer fine print is a dense, tiny wall of text. The exclusions are buried in it, and "up to 70%" is a headline that mostly applies to decor.
- Several tiles say "up to 10% off" or "up to 20% off," so the 70% promise is thin on the page.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero text legible by putting it on a solid or darkened panel. Then cut the lower half of the email down to two or three modules. A repeat send should be shorter than the first one.
- 2. Show prices on the "recommended" items, with was/now pricing, so it reads as a sale and not a lookbook.
- 3. Name the exclusions next to the headline, for example "Decor up to 70%, furniture up to 50%." That makes the offer more honest.
- 4. Put the rewards pitch up higher, where it hits me before the last scroll. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so "10% back" matters to me, and it's buried near the bottom here. Wayfair and CB2 don't lead with it either, but Pottery Barn actually has the card to push.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Even Crate & Barrel, which has sent me seven this week, varies the creative more than this.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Decor up to 70% off — order by Fri for Thanksgiving`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas 50% off, decor 70% off, ships before Thanksgiving`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 12 PM PT Friday 11/20 and it arrives in time. Free shipping on select bedding & tabletop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your saved sofas and decor are part of the sale. See what's marked down.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout sent through Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style click tracking (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=`). The main problems are widespread missing `alt` text, three cleartext `http://` images, and the recipient's address passed in plaintext in pixel and image URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified.** 55 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and HTTP status are unconfirmed. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjQs…`.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over HTTP and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Several tracking beacons.**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` is a third-party pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/cabae602-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a first-party open pixel.
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com` is a further open pixel.
  - All three carry no alt text, and `d.turn.com` is a cross-domain dependency.
- **Leaked server-side code in CSS.** The last `<style>` block contains literal PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS, and some clients may drop the rule that follows it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 44 images.**
  - Hero slices: `…/1118_WED_AM2_EBF_InStockSaleThanksgivingCutoff/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_10.jpg`.
  - Product images: `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg` and others.
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg` and `_02.jpg`.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`.
  - If the offer or CTA text is baked into the sliced images, the email is unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** Three images use `http://`: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Spacer tables.** The `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` spacers lack `role="presentation"`, which the other layout tables have.
- **Legacy asset paths.** The email still loads assets from `/pb/2016/nav/` and `/pb/2022/…`. It also loads `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, a cross-brand dependency.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and most clients, so it adds a request without benefit.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the supplied source or QA output. The recipient address resolved in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and in `sv_sveme=`.
- **PII in URLs.** The plaintext email address appears as a query parameter in an image URL (`coreemail.html?email=…`) and in an HTTP pixel (`sv_sveme=…`). That exposes it in proxy logs, referrers and third-party image-proxy logs.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set, but it does not apply in most email clients.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, which is consistent.
- **Footer not verified.** The supplied HTML is truncated, so the unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked. The footer is 18 unlabelled image slices, so if those details are image-only, the link would be inaccessible with images off.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot assess.** All 55 links are opaque `qs=` redirect tokens that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified.
- The visible product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` assets, so the product CDN is consistent with the destination brand.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image (hero slices, products, banners). For slices that carry text, put the offer copy in the `alt` or in live text.
2. Switch `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Remove the leaked PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque subscriber ID or a hashed token.
5. Set a meaningful `<title>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw MIME headers, not the relay copy. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC in the original message.
7. Follow the redirect chains with a real click-through to check UTM tags, 200 status on landing pages and offer alignment. Also check that the unsubscribe and address text in the footer is present.
8. Retire the stale `/2016/` and `/2022/` assets and the cross-brand West Elm spacer.
