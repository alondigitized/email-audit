# Get your living room ready for guests
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn doing its job without doing anything memorable. The "A Place to Gather" hero is a warm, sunlit living room with a linen sofa and a leather chair, and I liked it right away. It's a lifestyle send, not a coupon send. After a week of the green "Fall Sale" strip, that's a relief. It's closer to the 10/15 Kravet and dinnerware emails than to the sale blasts.
- Then I scrolled and it turned into a catalog index. There's a 2x3 grid of Pillows, Armchairs, Sofas, Lighting, Rugs and Coffee Tables, then a "Recommended Just For You" row, "Shop our Latest Designs," Free Design Services, the app, the credit card and Affirm. That's about eight emails stacked in one. There's no offer in the hero and no deadline. "Get your living room ready for guests" implies a Thanksgiving timeline, but nothing tells me what ships in time.
- Would I click? Probably one tile, and the Sofas one. I'm furnishing two houses, so a sofa is the item with the long lead time. I'd save this to my inspo folder. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero is gorgeous and on-brief for a living room refresh. It reads as design, not discount.
- The six-tile grid (Pillows, Armchairs, Sofas, Lighting, Rugs, Coffee Tables) lets me jump straight to a category.
- The "IN-STOCK LIVING ROOM FURNITURE" button is the best thing in the email for me. Ship date is my real question with furniture.
- The "Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" block repeats what the 10/15 "FREE (yes, free!) design help" send did. That one scored 8 with me, and the reminder is welcome.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" card block speaks to my rewards-card habit. It's just buried at the bottom.

## What's weak

- There's no offer anywhere above the fold. No % off, no free shipping, no member price. Pottery Barn's own Fall Sale strip at least gave me a number.
- The "Recommended Just For You" row is a curtain rod, a dining table, a bed, a swivel chair, a rug and a curtain. That's not living-room-for-guests, and it doesn't read as personal. A dining table and a bed are different rooms.
- It's far too long. Seven or eight modules are competing, and the app, Affirm and credit card pitches all sit after the design services block.
- The small grey legal copy at the bottom is tiny. The rewards and Affirm fine print is unreadable on a phone without zooming.
- The text on the grid tiles ("Pillows," "Sofas") is thin white serif over busy photos. "Rugs" and "Coffee Tables" are hard to read.
- Nothing says whether the "guests" deadline is Thanksgiving. There's no delivery timing anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a concrete reason to act now in the hero. For example, "Sofas and sectionals in stock, delivered before Thanksgiving," with the real cutoff date. That one line would do more than the whole recommendation row.
- 2. Cut the module count by half. Keep the hero, the category grid, one recommendation row and one footer service block. Drop the app, Affirm and credit card pitch or make them a one-line footer strip.
- 3. Make "Recommended Just For You" actually match the living-room theme, or say what it's based on ("Because you looked at sofas").
- 4. Add swatch or fabric-sample access to the Sofas and Armchairs tiles. That's the real friction for me when I'm matching colors across two houses.
- 5. Put solid-color label bars under the grid tiles instead of white text on photos.
- 6. Move the 10% rewards card up as a loyalty line near the hero, so it isn't buried under the app pitch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In-stock sofas that arrive before Thanksgiving`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Living room refresh: what ships in time for guests`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, armchairs and rugs ready to ship, plus free design help.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Nov 10 for delivery before the holiday. Swatches are free.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Get your living room ready for guests"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout. Its main problems are about 60 images with no alt text, three non-HTTPS images (two content images plus a tracking pixel), and the recipient address placed in plain-text tracking URLs. The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results checks could not be confirmed because of the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and body links were not fully inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable destinations:** 53 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they go through click-redirect domains. Examples are `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/efad7506-...`. Their destinations, status codes and UTM parameters can't be confirmed from the source.
- **Recipient address in tracking URLs:** the address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in these URLs:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`, which is plain HTTP.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network beacon.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading from the West Elm CDN. This is a minor dependency risk.
- **Stray code in the CSS block:** the second `<style>` block ends with PHP-style text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS. It is parsed as a malformed rule at the end of the block, so it is probably harmless in practice, but it is a templating bug.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 60 `<img>` elements have none (QA flagged each one). Affected groups:
  - Body content slices: `F26_XCAT_B12_GuestReadyLivingRoomChecklist/slices_01–11.jpg`.
  - Product images, such as `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`.
  - Banners: `H26_Top_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - With images off or a screen reader, the body, CTAs and footer links, which sit in sliced images, carry no text. The header logo has `alt="POTTERY BARN"`, but that is the only one in the visible source.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** these may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...` (open pixel)
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No lang attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom.
- **Layout tables:** the spacer tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) lack `role="presentation"`. The main tables have it.
- **Large HTML:** the plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (10,290 of 14,422 characters). This points to many slice and tracking links and a weak plain-text alternative.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) in the visible source. The recipient address is populated correctly in the pixel and `coreemail` URLs, though the privacy concern for it is noted under Link & Tracking Issues.

### 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are absent from the captured headers. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Without them, one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058, required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders) can't be verified.
- **Authentication-Results:** missing. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.potterybarn.com` can't be confirmed from this capture.
- **Footer unverified:** the visible footer is image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) with no alt text. The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address (CAN-SPAM) can't be verified from the truncated source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing alignment:** can't be assessed. All links go through opaque redirects (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), so landing URLs and UTM parameters are hidden.
- **Product images:** they are hosted on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. Their click targets are among the skipped links.
- **Unresolved:** whether the links land on the pages the email promises, such as a guest-ready living room collection.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content image, especially the checklist slices and footer slices. Add `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel). Stop putting the recipient email in URL query strings, and use an opaque ID instead.
3. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block and add a `<title>`, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on all layout tables. Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Re-run the audit on the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also resolve the tracking redirects to check the final URLs and UTM parameters.
5. Rebuild the plain-text alternative with readable text rather than mostly URLs.
