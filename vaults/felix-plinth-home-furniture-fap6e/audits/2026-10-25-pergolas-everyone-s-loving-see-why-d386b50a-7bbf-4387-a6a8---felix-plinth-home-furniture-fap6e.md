# Pergolas everyone's loving. See why →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and I'm numb to it. The top of this email is a big blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon where the hero should be. Nothing tells me what the sale is. When the first screen is empty, I'm one swipe from archiving.
- Below the gap it's a wall of pergolas on purple. That does match the subject, so at least it's honest. But I've had six days of pergolas, Way Day countdowns, and the popcorn-machine and dining-chair sends, and this is the same product pile again. Two more tiles in the grid are blank, and another big white block sits near the bottom. No price, no percent off, no deadline anywhere I could find. The "5% rewards on every item" strip is the only real offer.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd save it only if a pergola happened to match the patio in house number two. I wouldn't forward it. Pottery Barn at least leads with a clear "Extra 20% off" strip, and CB2's "jewelry of the room" lighting email gave me something to pin.

## What's working

- The subject and the products agree. Pergolas in the subject, pergolas everywhere in the body, unlike the bar stools send on 10/24 that had no bar stools.
- The pergola photos are good. Lifestyle shots with pools and decks, the kind I'd pin to a patio board.
- The star ratings under each tile give me some social proof without a click.
- The 5% Wayfair Rewards strip is the one concrete benefit. I pick my card by rewards math, so I noticed it.
- The footer is tidy: Rewards, Financing, Professional, and the app QR code. It's easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero is a blank white box with a broken-image icon. The top screen of my phone has no offer and no headline.
- Two product tiles in the first grid and one in the second are empty purple or white squares. It looks half-built.
- There's another large empty block near the bottom, right above the footer, so the layout feels broken twice more.
- There's no price, percent off, or deadline on any pergola. The "shop before they're gone" header has no number behind it, so it reads like a bluff.
- It's the same pergola story I got on 10/24 and 10/25. Fatigue is real, and the 10% off app email earlier was a clearer offer than this.
- The tile labels like "Pergolas for less »" are vague. I can't tell whether it's a sale, a price drop, or just a category link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken images, or give the hero a real fallback. A solid-color banner with live text like "Pergolas up to X% off" would have saved this send.
- 2. Put one number on the page: a price-from or percent-off on the hero and on the tiles. Pottery Barn's maroon strip does this and I can read it in half a second.
- 3. Cut the grid to 4 working tiles with prices, not 8 with three holes.
- 4. Add a real deadline or shipping promise, since I'm buying big outdoor pieces. Tell me about delivery time, assembly, and whether there's a swatch or sample.
- 5. Cap the pergola run. After three days of the same category, rotate in something different, like the bedding or rugs already sitting at the bottom.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules and per-recipient tracking parameters on every link. The QA data shows no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The real defects are missing `alt` attributes, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and one malformed image URL. The header and authentication warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** All 8 probed warnings are HTTP 429 rate limits from `wayfair.com` and `wayfairapp.onelink.me`. They are probe-side throttling, not dead links.
- **Incomplete coverage.** Only 12 links were probed and 52 were skipped. Four product links (`Erommy-Louvered-Pergola…` ×2, `12-ft.-W-x-10-ft.-D-HeavyDuty-Outdoor-Metal-Pergola…` ×2) were unprobed after the 70s budget. Their destinations are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` … `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=d386b50a-…`) use `http://`. Clients that block mixed content may drop them, and impression counts would then undercount. They also have no `alt` attribute.
- **Insecure app fallback.** The OneLink URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app land on an `http://` URL, which adds a redirect hop before HTTPS.
- **Malformed image URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-…/0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is invalid percent-encoding. Strict URL parsers or proxies (e.g. Gmail's image proxy) may fail to load it. The filename prefix `0728` also suggests a reused July asset.
- **Open pixel is unusually shaped.** The open tracker is `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=<base64>` and has no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images.** Flagged images include:
  - The hero and banner: `WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyTop-Ends…`, `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_2_purple.gif`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png`, `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24…`.
  - The disclaimer: `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`.
  - The review-star images: `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png`, at least 12 occurrences.
- **Effect on linked images.** With images off or a screen reader, the star ratings convey nothing. Linked images such as `WFUS_…SkinnyTop-Ends` and `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` have no accessible link name. The QA tool falls back to `[image: filename]`.
- **Disclaimer is image-only.** `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg` has no `alt`. If it carries legal terms, they are inaccessible.
- **Markup is mostly sound.** The `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` selectors cover Gmail and iOS quirks, and the `body[data-outlook-cycle]` fix covers the Outlook app. The HTML was truncated, so I did not check beyond that.
- **Tracking pixels need `alt=""`.** For `imp` and `b.php`, an explicit empty `alt` is the correct fix. A missing attribute is not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No issues found.** Every captured URL has resolved per-recipient identifiers: `_emr=d386b50a-…`, a unique `_eml` per link, and `p=d386b50a-…` on the pixels. No unrendered `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens appear in the data provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects.** Three warnings are unverified. The QA notes say the relay may strip the headers:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` was not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer not inspected.** The tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the output, and the HTML is truncated. I can't confirm the body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address from the data provided.
- **Sender domain looks fine.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Links use Wayfair's own scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=1`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121271`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. Standard analytics tools won't attribute these without a custom mapping, but the tagging is internally consistent.
- **Inconsistent campaign label on the app link.** The OneLink URL has `c=Triggered` while `source=graymatter_batch`. A batch send tagged as a triggered campaign can skew attribution.
- **Link-to-link continuity looks right:**
  - The pergola product link goes to `Outdoor-Louvered-Aluminum-Pergola-L7053-K~JLYD1476.html` with `sku=JLYD1476`.
  - The image link `stars_10.png` goes to the same PDP.
  - The "Shop Sale" link goes to `/daily-sales`.
  - The Wayfair header link goes to `/`.
- **Unconfirmed alignment.** The subject ("Pergolas everyone's loving") has no pergola-specific category link in the probed set. Two banner images link to the generic `/` or `/daily-sales`. Landing-page alignment for the 52 unprobed links is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"` style text for the `stars_*.png` images, and put real text on the banners and the disclaimer image.
2. Add `alt=""` to `b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels, and switch `li.wayfair.com/imp` to `https://`.
3. Fix `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` (`%oo` should be a valid escape or removed), and replace the stale asset.
4. Change `af_web_dp` to an `https://` URL.
5. Correct `c=Triggered` to match the batch send.
6. Check the raw message at the final inbox (not the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`, and confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Re-probe the four unprobed product URLs with a longer time budget or with throttling to avoid 429s.
