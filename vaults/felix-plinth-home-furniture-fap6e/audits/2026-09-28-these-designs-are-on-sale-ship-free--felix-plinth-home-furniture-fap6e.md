# These designs are on sale & ship free!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Save on Easy Updates" — sale on pillows, throws, sheets, all the soft-goods stuff, and it opens with an actual hero: a neutral sofa styled with ivory and tan pillows, natural light, "on sale & shipping free" right above it. It's a clean, calm open and I'll give them that. But then it scrolls into a Halloween ghost display, two dining tables, a curtain rod, and a "Design Crew" pitch, and by the time I hit the app download banner I've forgotten what the actual sale was. This is the fourth Pottery Barn email this week and it's blending into the last three in my head.
- The core problem: "Save on Easy Updates" and "on sale & shipping free" never tells me what the discount actually is. Compare that to their own "Online SALE exclusive: Bestselling furniture" send from yesterday — that one at least named real collections (Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston) right in the hero. This one is just categories in a grid — Pillows, Throws, Quilts, Duvets — with zero percentage anywhere I can see.
- I'm not clicking this one. It's soft-goods, not furniture, so it's not touching my two-house renovation budget right now, and there's no number to anchor on. Not forwarding it either — nothing here is interesting enough to send to my wife or my Crate & Barrel-obsessed friend.

## What's working

- The hero photo is genuinely nice — warm neutral palette, real room styling, no lorem-ipsum or render bugs.
- "Recommended Just For You" actually surfaced two furniture pieces relevant to my renovation — the Toscana Extendable Dining Table and the Farmhouse Platform Bed — that's the one section that felt like it knew who I was.
- Free shipping is called out clearly in the hero, no fine-print hunting required for that one claim.
- Layout is clean and scannable, no overlapping text, images all loaded fine.

## What's weak

- No percentage or dollar amount anywhere in the sale section — "on sale" could mean 10% or 40%, I have zero way to judge if this is worth my time, same complaint I had about their "Rugs for a Colorful Foundation" send on 9/27.
- This is soft-goods (pillows, sheets, towels), but the "Recommended Just For You" block is furniture and curtain hardware — the email doesn't actually connect its own hero offer to what it thinks I want.
- The Halloween Shop and Design Crew blocks turn this into a category dump by the third scroll — same complaint I had about "Just In! 1400+ New Arrivals," it stops being one email with one point.
- Nothing here reads as "known customer" — no rewards balance, no "welcome back," no reference to anything I've bought or browsed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the hero — "30% Off Pillows, Throws & Bedding + Free Shipping" beats "Save on Easy Updates" every time. Crate & Barrel's fall cozy email worked because it committed to a mood AND a reason to act; this commits to neither a number nor a mood.
- 2. Cut the email in half — sale categories, then stop. Halloween and Design Crew are separate campaigns wearing this one's skin.
- 3. Move "Recommended Just For You" to actually reflect the sale category (soft goods), not furniture that has nothing to do with the hero.
- 4. Add a rewards nudge near the credit card block — I'm exactly the target for that 10% back pitch, don't bury it at the bottom under Affirm.
