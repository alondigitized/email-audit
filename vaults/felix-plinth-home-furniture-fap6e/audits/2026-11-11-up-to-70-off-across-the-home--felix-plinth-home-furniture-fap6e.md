# Up to 70% off across the home
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the 13th or so Pottery Barn email in about a week, and at least this one is about furniture. "Savings Across the Home" with "UP TO 70% OFF" is the kind of hero I'd actually stop for. The "in-stock furniture delivered in 1-4 weeks" strip at the top is the first time in days a PB send has talked about furniture lead times. I'm doing two houses, so that matters to me.
- Then it turns into a long scroll of room vignettes: bedroom, living room, dining room. Each one has a "Savings for a…" headline and a category label, and none has a price. "Up to 70%" is a ceiling, not an offer. The nightstand send on 11/10 ("Nightstands starting at $349") gave me a number I could act on, and this one doesn't. I'd open it and scroll, but I probably wouldn't click. I might save it to my inspiration folder for the dining table and the Cayman bed.

## What's working

- The "in-stock furniture delivered in 1-4 weeks" banner answers the shipping question I always have about furniture, and Wayfair rarely does that in the hero.
- "SHIPS FREE" tags on bedding, throws and pillows make the free-shipping offer visible without any fine print.
- The room-by-room layout (bedroom, living room, dining room) matches how I'm planning two renovations. It's clear and the photography is good.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has real furniture: the Banks Extendable Dining Table, the Toscana table, the Cayman Platform Bed. That's closer to my actual shopping than another Christmas hero.
- The 10% back in Rewards card block is a rewards-math cue I notice, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- "Up to 70%" has no anchor. Not one product in the email shows a sale price or a percent off, so I can't tell whether the sofa is 70% off or the candles are.
- The Christmas Shop banner and the stocking tiles in "Recommended Just For You" pull away from the furniture pitch. Last week was all plaid ribbon, and it's still here.
- It's very long, and the legal block at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. The sweepstakes ($3,000 gift card, Design Crew) and the affirm, app and credit card blocks pile on more asks after the "SHOP ALL SALE" button.
- The tiles have only category names ("BEDDING", "DECOR", "THROWS", "PILLOWS") and no product names or prices, so it reads like a category page rather than a sale. The decor and pillow tiles are the same filler I see in every home brand's email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put real prices or a percent off on at least one hero product per room, like "Banks dining table, was $X, now $Y." This would lift the email more than anything else.
- 2. Cut the Christmas Shop block and the stockings from this send, or move them to a separate send, so this one stays about the furniture sale.
- 3. Move the "Recommended Just For You" furniture up above the bedding and decor tiles, since the furniture is what I'd click.
- 4. Pull the sweepstakes, affirm and app promos into one footer strip so the sale gets the attention.
- 5. Add a sale end date. "Up to 70%" with no deadline feels permanent, because the same fall sale banner has been on every send since 10/06.
- Compared with Crate & Barrel's "Psst… something special" send, which at least had a slate hero with up to 60% off, this one isn't clearer on the deal. CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" at least gave a countdown for urgency. Pottery Barn does have the better delivery message, and Wayfair's 72-HR CLEAROUT banner has more urgency but less taste.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a 650px fixed-width, table-based XHTML layout with a large number of image-only slices. The main code-level problems are widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, and the recipient's email address in cleartext in image URLs. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 57 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`). Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- Three third-party or secondary tracking beacons load alongside the main redirect domain:
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
  - `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/5f3ce264-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- `www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` is loaded as an `<img>`, so it is a tracking pixel pointing at an HTML page.
- Mixed-domain assets: `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer in a Pottery Barn send.
- Stale asset path: `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset reused in a 2026 send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images are flagged.
  - The hero slices `slices_00–11.jpg` under `/1111_WED_AM_SaleTheHome/` have no alt text, so the offer is invisible with images off.
  - Product images have none either: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings-2-l.jpg` and `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`.
  - The banners `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg` have none.
  - The footer slices `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` have none.
  - The tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif` also have no alt text. These should use `alt=""`.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `.../0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel use `http://`. These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. This server-side code leaked into the template. It sits after the valid rules, so it is probably harmless, but it is a template defect.
- **Unmatched mobile selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` don't match anything in the visible source. The `.contenttable{width:320px}` rule is commented out, and the tables are fixed at `width="650"`. Mobile scaling therefore relies on the `img{max-width:100%}` rule and the `mobile-hide/show` classes. This can't be fully confirmed because the HTML is truncated.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** the preheader text contains a raw `&` ("bedroom, dining room & more").

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible.
- **Cleartext email in image URLs:** the recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is in plain text in two image URLs:
  - `coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=...`
  
  The `svopn` pixel is also `http://`, so the address is sent unencrypted. Forwarded copies and proxy or log caches can expose it.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. QA notes the relay may have stripped them, so this is unverified, not a confirmed violation.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm or deny them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible links are opaque redirect URLs (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), so UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be verified. Tracking is done through redirect and pixel parameters (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `sv_cid`), not visible UTMs.
- The product image hosts (`www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`) are on the main site domain. The redirect destinations still need checking against the sale landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero slices (the offer copy) and the product images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets to `https://`. Stop passing the plaintext email in the `svopn` and `coreemail` pixel URLs, or hash or token-ize it.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Replace the stale 2022 banner asset and the cross-brand West Elm spacer.
6. Re-test with raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also resolve the 57 tracked links to confirm their destinations and UTM handling.
7. Verify the mobile layout. Either restore a working responsive rule for the 650px `contenttable` or remove the dead `wrapper` and `photo` selectors.
