# New furniture added! Earn 2x Rewards & up to 50% off!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Kids blast, and by my count this is now the sixth one sitting in my inbox from this brand family today. I don't have kids, I don't need a nursery chair or a crib, and no amount of "up to 50% off baby furniture" is going to change that — this is pure list-scrubbing failure. The one thing that snags my eye is the "Double Rewards is on!" banner up top, because I do carry the Crate & Barrel card and rewards multipliers are the one lever that actually gets me to click, but it's wrapped around a hero image of a nursery, not a sofa or a dining set.
- Compare this to today's actual Crate & Barrel send ("Up to 40% off bestselling sofas—and it's Double Rewards time!") — same Double Rewards mechanic, same day, but that one is at least selling me furniture for a house I'm renovating. This Crate & Kids version is the same promo engine pointed at the wrong catalog. It's the same mismatch I flagged with the two nearly-identical "NEW ITEMS ADDED" Crate & Barrel sends yesterday — this brand family cannot stop repeating itself within a 24-hour window.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and moving on. If anything I forward this to my wife with a "why do we still get these" caption, not because it's worth shopping.

## What's working

- The Double Rewards banner at the very top is legible and the offer math (up to 60% off + Rewards) is at least stated plainly, no hunting required.
- Product grids are clean — clear price/strikethrough pricing per item (bedroom, nursery, storage, chairs), no layout breakage.
- The "$500 credit / win $5,000" contest banner and TheDesignDesk consult CTA at the bottom are somewhat interesting as a hook, even if buried after nine screens of crib furniture.

## What's weak

- Wrong catalog entirely — I'm renovating two adult houses, not a nursery. Every hero image (crib, glider, changing table) is demographically off for me.
- This is the sixth Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel email in one day per my log — the cadence alone would get most brands unsubscribed.
- Massive scroll depth: bedroom furniture, then nursery furniture, then storage, then nursery chairs, then a sweepstakes, then "we love these for you," then a credit card pitch, then an app pitch. No single clear CTA — it's nine offers stapled together.
- The card offer at the bottom ("get $500 just for opening a card") is squeezed in as an afterthought instead of leading, when that's honestly the only line in this whole email relevant to how I actually shop (highest-rewards card).

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely — if I've never clicked a nursery/kids product, stop sending this line. That's the single biggest lift possible here.
- 2. Cut this to one hero offer and one CTA. Right now it's five product carousels stacked vertically with no prioritization — pick the best margin category and lead with it.
- 3. If Double Rewards is the real hook, put it on Crate & Barrel adult furniture sends only, the way today's sofa email did — don't reuse the badge across every sub-brand indiscriminately.
- 4. Move the $500 credit card offer up near the top if this email must go to non-parents — it's the one piece of copy that could earn a click from someone like me.
