# This best-selling lamp is on sale right now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the actual "lamp" email the subject promised — and the hero delivers, mostly: "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF LIGHTING" over a moody staged shot with an actual lamp in frame. That's the fourteenth CB2 send I've clocked this stretch, and it's finally doing the thing the subject line said it would do, which after "So Much Is On Sale Right Now" and "Now and Future Classics" feels almost novel.
- Where it loses me is everything after the hero. I scroll past a furniture/outdoor/rugs/entertaining grid, then landmine into a "Double Rewards" card promo I don't have, then an "800+ New Arrivals" bowl-of-olives shot that has nothing to do with lighting, and only THEN do I hit actual lamp SKUs with prices — six of them, buried two-thirds down. If I clicked from "best-selling lamp," I want the best-selling lamp in the first screen, not after a card-signup pitch.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the hero CTA alone, since 50% off lighting is a real number and I do need a floor lamp for the second-home reading nook. Would I forward it to a friend? No — this is diffuse enough that nobody scans it twice. Marking it "seen, revisit if I need a lamp."

## What's working

- Hero copy is honest and on-category — "UP TO 50% OFF LIGHTING" is a specific, credible number, unlike Wayfair's "you *gotta* see these" cushion bait-and-switch I got burned by last week.
- The staged lamp shot in the hero actually looks like something I'd pin — warm wood tones, natural light, matches my second-home reno palette.
- The product grid near the bottom has real prices and strikethrough comparisons (Raw Marble Table Lamp $199.00→$158.00, Pavo Black Marble Floor Lamp $499.00→$399.00) — that's the rewards math I actually want to see before I click through.
- "800+ New Arrivals" and the rewards card section are clean, readable blocks — no overlapping text, no broken images anywhere in this render.

## What's weak

- The lamp content — the whole reason for the subject line — is stranded below furniture, outdoor, rugs, entertaining, decor, a credit card pitch, AND a new-arrivals filler shot. That's five sections of noise before I get what was promised.
- "Double Rewards" card promo mid-email is dead weight for me — I already pay with my highest-rewards card, I'm not opening a CB2 store card for 20% back in reward dollars I'll never fully redeem.
- This is the same "MORE SAVINGS THIS WAY" category-grid beat CB2 has run in nearly every send this month — I said it about "Now and Future Classics" three days ago and I'm saying it again. It's autopilot creative, not a fresh Labor Day push.
- No swatch/sample or shipping-timeline info anywhere for the lamps themselves — for a $399 floor lamp I'd actually want to know lead time before I click, and neither Article nor CB2 has given me that yet this month.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the actual best-selling lamp SKUs (with prices) directly under the hero, above the category grid — the subject sold me a specific product, not a store directory.
- 2. Cut the Double Rewards card block entirely from lighting-focused sends, or move it to the footer where Crate & Barrel and Crate & Kids already tuck theirs — it's stealing space from the thing I opened for.
- 3. Swap "800+ New Arrivals" (bowls, no lighting relevance) for a second lamp cross-sell or a "complete the look" shade/sconce pairing — keep the whole email on-theme.
- 4. Add a one-line shipping/lead-time note near the lamp grid — furniture/lighting buyers like me stall out without it.
