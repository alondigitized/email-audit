# Ends today! Up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This one finally speaks to my project. Last week was Nuna clothing, Halloween and a Samsung Frame TV from the Kids and main senders. Now I get a lounge sectional in a dark, moody room, with "hours left" and "up to 20% off" on top. I'm furnishing two houses, so this is the first C&B send in days that I'd keep.
- The email then runs long and starts to blur. The sofa offer is clear. Below it come rugs, lighting, decor and mirrors, then free swatches, a stain demo, TheDesignDesk, custom furniture, a product grid, the Reward Dollars card, the app and a second TheDesignDesk plug. By the second TheDesignDesk block I was skimming. The 10/13 "bestseller edit" email pointed at four specific sofas. This one points at a category page, so it's weaker for a shopper who already knows what they want.
- I'd click, probably on "Shop Upholstery Sale" or "Order Free Swatches," and I'd save it to my inspiration folder. I wouldn't forward it. I've already gotten sofas from C&B twice in two days, so the freshness is gone.

## What's working

- The hero is a real sectional in a styled room, with "hours left: up to 20% off" right under it. I know the deadline and the offer in two seconds.
- The "10 free swatches" block hits how I actually buy. I won't commit to a $4K sofa without matching fabric to paint and rugs.
- The stain demo ("our performance fabrics really work") answers a real worry about big furniture purchases.
- The rugs, lighting, decor and mirrors tiles are what I'm shopping for next anyway. The chandelier shot is nice.
- "Exactly the look you want. Custom options" speaks to people with two houses and different rooms to fill.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. Pottery Barn's Grinch email had one hero and one SHOP NOW. This one makes me scroll through about nine sections, and the offer gets buried.
- TheDesignDesk shows up twice, and "Book a free appointment" is repeated. CB2 Free Design Services made the same pitch better, with a sender name and copy that addressed two houses.
- "Up to 20% off" is vague, and the hero fine print is tiny. There are no sofa prices visible next to the sectionals. Wayfair at least puts a "$134 off" badge on a product.
- The "we love these for you" grid has an espresso machine, steel cookware, wine glasses and plates. That's a kitchen mix in a sofa email, and it reads as generic.
- The Reward Dollars card promo, the app promo and the "$500" app line are all small and crowded at the bottom. I'd want rewards math up high. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so a 10% back offer matters to me.
- I'm in a C&B family snooze: Kids, main, CB2 and now the SALE sender. This is the second sofa email in two days, so the cadence is the problem.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, swatches, one design-service block and one product row. Merge the two TheDesignDesk blocks.
- 2. Put named sofa SKUs with prices in the hero. Use "Lounge 93" Sofa, was $X, now $Y" instead of a category link.
- 3. Move the Reward Dollars offer up next to the sale so I can do the math.
- 4. Pull the kitchen items out of the "for you" grid. Show sofa-adjacent items like rugs, coffee tables and lighting.
- 5. Don't send a second sofa email to someone who opened yesterday's. Suppress it, or send a different angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off sectionals + free swatches`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lounge sectionals, performance fabrics and 10 free swatches, all through midnight.`

# Technical Audit: "Ends today! Up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional template with no confirmed critical defects. The main confirmed issues are about 33 images flagged for missing `alt` text and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits in the AgentMail relay, not from the email itself. The source was truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe block were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 64 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none were checked for destination status or redirect chains.
- **Per-recipient identifiers:** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` parameter appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif`. The same hash appears in `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. That ties a recipient identifier to a third-party pixel (rlcdn.com, a LiveRamp domain). This is a privacy observation, not a rendering defect.
- **Pixel count:** There are 5 identical `rlcdn.com` pixels (`n=1` to `n=5`), plus `o.gif` and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` beacon. Many clients that block remote images will block all of these.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA, 33 images):**
  - Content images, where missing `alt` is a real accessibility defect:
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, about 14 files, including `def83198-…jpg`, `2a0bb8a6-…jpg` and `4d0545c3-…jpg`.
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/cf4a7ed2-….gif`.
    - The Scene7 asset `2026%5F0923%5FCB%5FNewSofas%5FSectionals%5FSpillTe…`.
  - The seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images are probably product or recommendation tiles, which makes this more likely a real defect. The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`.
  - Tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry `alt=""`. The check flags them, but the impact is low.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` also disables text scaling on iOS.
- **Dead CSS:** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`, because the 640px rule matches every width at or above 768px.
- **Markup hygiene (minor):**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` are both declared.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values.
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `color-scheme: light`, and Outlook VML/MSO conditionals.

Resolve tracking redirects with a HEAD/GET follow to record final URLs and UTMs. Run the checks against the untruncated HTML so the footer and unsubscribe link are covered.
