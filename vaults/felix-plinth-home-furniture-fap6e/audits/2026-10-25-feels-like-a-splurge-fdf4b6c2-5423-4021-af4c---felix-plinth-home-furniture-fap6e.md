## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with VML/MSO fallbacks. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The main problems are 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels, missing alt text on 9 content images, and header checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) that couldn't be confirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are limited to what the QA data covers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links were skipped from HTTP probing because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chain length and 404s are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
- These may be blocked or rewritten by image proxies (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) and can trigger mixed-content or "not secure" handling in webmail. They are also third-party cookie-sync calls (Dotomi/Conversant) with no first-party relationship to the sender domain. Ten sequential `pub*.php` cookie-redirect pixels are heavy and add load time and privacy exposure.
- **Other pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/1e325a27-…` are HTTPS and first-party, so they are fine apart from the missing alt text.
- **Font URLs:** CB2 pages reference `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...` with a double slash, which can break on stricter servers. Fonts are also pulled from a different brand domain (`www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (9):** all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `33a9c22b-…jpg`

  With images off or a screen reader, these content images, likely linked, have no text equivalent.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (13):** the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/…` have no alt attribute. Low impact, but use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `*{... line-height:100%}` disable user text scaling and force tight line height globally.
- **Title:** `<title>CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME</title>` is generic rather than describing this email.
- **Leftover template text:** the source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (and the typo "IMPUT").
- **Breakpoints:** the media query is `max-width: 418px`, so mid-width phones and small tablets (about 419–689px) get no responsive rules.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=56aaa278…` parameter on `o.gif` is a hashed identifier, not a raw email address. Merge tokens in the truncated portion are unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe (WARN):** the header was not found. QA notes it may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (WARN):** the RFC 8058 one-click header was not found, with the same relay caveat. If it is truly absent, Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** the header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `mail.cb2.com`.
- **Body footer:** the physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 66 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated source doesn't show final destinations or UTM parameters. Resolve the redirects manually or in a follow-up run to check landing-page alignment with the "Feels like a splurge" subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or drop the 10 cookie-sync pixels, which carry the most deliverability, privacy and load cost.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to restore zoom and text scaling.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw SMTP headers from the sender side, since the relay may be the cause of the missing headers.
5. Capture `Authentication-Results` from a direct inbox (not the relay) to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
6. Follow the 66 redirect links to check final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
7. Clean up the template: the leftover comment, the `//Styles` double slash, and a descriptive `<title>`.
