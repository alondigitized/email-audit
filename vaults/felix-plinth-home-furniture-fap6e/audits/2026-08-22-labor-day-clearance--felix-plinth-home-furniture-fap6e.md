## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-generated (Bronto/Wayfair) transactional/marketing template; automated QA found no hard failures but flagged compliance-header visibility, widespread missing alt text, and mixed-content tracking pixels.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 63 links were probed (51 deprioritized as footer/utility/social); of those probed, 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by Wayfair's edge, not necessarily broken) including the primary hero CTA (`.../daily-sales/labor-day-sale?...`) and app-download deep link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/...`).
- 4 links were unprobed due to the 70s time budget (product detail pages, e.g. `/wayfair-product-L2049-K~W002728195.html`, `/Freddie-Round-Placemat-L7327-K~VJE4709.html`) — status unconfirmed.
- All probed/observed links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_139246`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16031`), indicating uniform click-tracking wrap across CTAs.
- Tracking pixel `imp` (impression beacon, `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000...019` — 20 sequential instances) is served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS. Modern mail clients (Gmail, Apple Mail) commonly block or strip non-HTTPS image loads, which would suppress open/impression tracking for this campaign.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 24+ `<img>` elements are missing `alt` attributes, including the primary hero (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`), review-star rating icons (`stars_10.png`, `stars_11.png` — 10 instances), the financing banner, and all 20 `imp` tracking pixels. Screen readers relying on alt text get no fallback for the hero/CTA imagery.
- Template uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with extensive `!important`-laden inline/embedded media queries (Unbounce/Bronto-style `.u-*` utility classes) — standard for ESP-rendered HTML, no structural errors observed.
- Custom `@font-face` (Sofia, `.woff` via `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`) has an `mso-font-alt` fallback to Arial for Outlook — correctly implemented.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization fields found in the visible source/QA output. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance status from available data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — authentication status unknown via this relay.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA methodology notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), though its exact URL wasn't in the probed sample.
- Recommend re-verifying headers directly against raw SMTP/MIME source rather than relying solely on the AgentMail-relayed copy, since all three flags stem from the same capture gap.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair does not use standard UTM params; instead uses proprietary tracking (`_emr`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_139246`, `maiid=16031`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`). This is consistent across all links — no mismatched or missing tracking params observed.
- Hero image CTA and "Shop Sale" CTA both route to `/daily-sales/labor-day-sale` and `/daily-sales`, consistent with subject line/creative theme (Labor Day Clearance). No content/landing mismatch found.
- Category CTAs (`bedding-clearance`, `area-rug-clearance`, `kitchen-&-dining-furniture-clearance`) route to matching category-specific clearance pages with `eid`/`unifiedeventid` params — alignment confirmed for probed items.

7. Recommendations
- Migrate the `imp` impression pixel from `http://li.wayfair.com` to HTTPS to prevent silent tracking loss in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to hero/product imagery at minimum (star-rating and tracking-pixel images can use empty `alt=""` to mark them decorative, which is valid and avoids screen-reader noise).
- Re-run header capture directly against raw MIME/SMTP source to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and Authentication-Results are genuinely present — current absence is flagged as a likely relay-capture limitation, not a confirmed compliance gap.
- Re-probe the 8 rate-limited (429) links and 4 time-budget-skipped links outside the 70s window to confirm final link health, particularly the hero CTA and app deep link.
