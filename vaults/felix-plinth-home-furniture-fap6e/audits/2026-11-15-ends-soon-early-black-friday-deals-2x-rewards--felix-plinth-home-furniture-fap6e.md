# Ends soon: Early Black Friday deals + 2x Rewards
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel family send I've had in about two days, and it's the Kids arm again. I'm 36 with no kids, and I'm renovating two houses. The "last Double Rewards this year" banner got my attention for about a second. Then I saw cribs, Nuna strollers and a baby activity chair, and I was out. This is the same early Black Friday and 2x Rewards pitch as the "ON NOW! Up to 40% off top kitchen brands" email from earlier today, which was the one I'd actually click.
- The rewards math is the one thing I care about. 20% back in Reward Dollars through Nov. 17 is real money on a big furniture order, and I do buy with the highest-rewards card. But it's the same message twice, once in the top banner and again in the big red block mid-email. The "up to 50% off" hero also has text stacked on top of the crib photo, and the banner copy crowds the headline. That made it harder to read than it should be.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to a friend with a newborn. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The rewards banner at the top states the deal and the deadline (Nov. 17, 2026) right away. I get the point in one glance.
- The 2x2 category grid (furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs) is easy to scan. Each tile has its own "up to X% off".
- The "up to 60% off clearance" block gives a second, bigger discount number for people who want to dig.
- The product row ("we love these for you") shows real prices, like the Nuna strollers and the Jenny Lind dresser. That's useful for comparing across brands.
- The Crate Card block makes the rewards angle concrete for anyone who isn't already on the card.

## What's weak

- I'm not the audience. Everything is cribs, strollers and nursery rugs, and nothing is aimed at someone furnishing two homes.
- The rewards message appears twice, in the top banner and again in a full red block, and the hero adds a third mention. It reads as padding.
- The hero text overlaps the crib photo, and "Last Double Rewards this Year!" sits on top of the "early black friday" line. I'd call that a visible layout problem.
- Mid-email there's a $2,700+ giveaway, a "shop Black Friday deals first" signup block, an app promo and a Design Desk pitch. Too many competing asks dilute the main offer.
- The fine print at the bottom is a long wall of tiny type. "Up to" discounts with all those footnotes make me doubt the headline numbers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this. Don't send a Kids email to people who have never bought kids' items. Send me the main Crate & Barrel version with furniture and kitchen.
- 2. Cut the duplicated rewards blocks down to one. Put the deadline in the hero and drop the second red block.
- 3. Fix the hero so the headline, offer and CTA sit on a clean area. They shouldn't overlap the photo.
- 4. Remove the giveaway, the app promo and the Design Desk block from this send. Keep one offer and one CTA.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific, like "Shop nursery furniture", instead of repeating "Shop the Sale".
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% back in Rewards ends Nov. 17 + up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day for 2x Rewards on nursery furniture`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off furniture, bedding and rugs, plus 20% back on your Crate Card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday pricing and Double Rewards both end Nov. 17. Shop the sale.`
- Compared with the other brands I've audited:
- Pottery Barn's "Early Black Friday bedding deals + FREE SHIPPING" at least named a category I buy, and it led with free shipping.
- Wayfair's "END TABLES" email had a subject that matched what I wanted to buy.
- CB2's "WHY WAIT FOR BLACK FRIDAY?" was also repetitive, but its products were in my lane.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Movable Ink-built, XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with 75 tracked links and many images, mostly hosted on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Confirmed defects are missing `alt` text on about 30 images and a few head-level markup problems. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture relay, so they are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links are unverified.** The QA run skipped 75 tracking and click-redirect links (HTTP probe skipped). Link validity, redirect chains and final destinations were not tested, so I can't confirm or rule out broken links.
- **Third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` is the same LiveRamp pixel fired 5 times with a hashed identifier in the query string. The 5 `n` values suggest one pixel per section.
- **Movable Ink beacons.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/844e9f2b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression trackers.
- **Dynamic image calls.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. These are server-rendered personalized images, so a failure in that service breaks rendering.
- **Mixed image hosts.** Images load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `s7d5.scene7.com`, e.g. `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
- **Possibly stale asset.** The scene7 path suggests a September 2026 clearance creative (`2026_0926_CBK_Clearance`) in a mid-November send. I can't confirm from the truncated source that it is stale.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 30 images (confirmed by QA).**
  - Content-bearing images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (about 17 `.jpg` files, e.g. `26171910-…jpg`, `4edeeb9c-…jpg`).
  - Content-bearing images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - Content-bearing images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/8c4ce5d9-…gif`.
  - The scene7 `…Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif`.
  - The 6 dynamic `f3c0d22992a01946.png` images.
  - Spacers and pixels (`25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`, the `rlcdn` and `o.gif` beacons) should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
  - The QA tool doesn't distinguish an absent attribute from an empty one. Decorative images with `alt=""` are fine, but the content images need descriptive alt text.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid XHTML in the title.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Duplicate and conflicting meta tags.** There are 4 `format-detection` tags: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, plus separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags. Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production. It is harmless but sloppy, and it is misspelled.
- **Limited dark-mode support.** `color-scheme` is set to `light` only.
- **Conflicting text-size rules.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust` is declared as both `100%` and `none` across the style blocks. The later `none` can win and block text resizing in WebKit clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. Personalization is done via Movable Ink image URLs (`mi_u=` parameter). The body was truncated, so tokens further down can't be assessed.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both are reported as not found. The QA tool itself says AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is not a confirmed defect. It needs verification against the raw message at the source.
- **Authentication-Results.** It is missing from the captured message, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The same relay caveat applies. The sending subdomain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` should be checked directly via DNS or a raw-header test.
- **Footer and physical address.** These were not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't verify the in-body unsubscribe link or the postal address.
- **Identifier privacy.** `sr.rlcdn.com` carries hashed identifiers in the URL (`s=dbe8da71…`). Disclosure in the privacy policy is a compliance consideration, though not a CAN-SPAM violation by itself.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 75 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't expose final destination URLs or UTM parameters. No continuity defect is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all content images, especially the promo and offer graphics, since the sale and 2x Rewards message may live only in images. Use `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. Escape the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it descriptive.
4. Deduplicate the `format-detection` metas, drop the redundant charset tag and delete the leftover template comment.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw original message. Don't rely on the relay-captured copy.
6. Re-run the link check with redirect-following enabled for the 75 tracked links, and capture the full HTML so UTMs, the footer and the unsubscribe link can be validated.
7. Consider consolidating the 5 identical `rlcdn` pixels into one. If they are intentionally per-section, confirm that.
