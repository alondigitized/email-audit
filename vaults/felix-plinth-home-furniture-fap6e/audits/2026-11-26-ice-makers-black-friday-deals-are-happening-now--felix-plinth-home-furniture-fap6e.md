## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
No hard failures: the automated QA run reports 0 issues and 3 warnings. The code-level problems are 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and a `c=Triggered` campaign label on a batch send. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 links were probed and 72 skipped. Several probed links returned 429 (e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...`, three product URLs). These are rate limits, not dead links.
- **Unprobed links:** 4 links were not checked because the 70s probe budget ran out, so their status is unknown. They include the `NineStars-13.2-Gallon...Trash` product link and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`.
- **Insecure impression pixels:** 20 pixels use plaintext HTTP, `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Clients or proxies that block mixed or insecure content may drop them, which would undercount ad impressions.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the app link `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` carries `c=Triggered`, while the same URL has `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch send tagged "Triggered" will skew attribution reporting in AppsFlyer.
- **Insecure app fallback:** the same link has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app land on an HTTP URL, which relies on a redirect to HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images:**
  - 13 or more `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` rating images (`secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`). Each is also wrapped in a product link, so the link text becomes `[image: stars_10.png]` and screen readers get no useful name.
  - Banner images `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`, `..._Email_Skinny-EverythingShips`, `..._Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`, `..._Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`.
  - The `WFUS_..._SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg` banner is itself a link to `/daily-sales`, so it is a link with no accessible name.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` (open pixel) and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels. Low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct attribute for decorative images.
- **Other checks:** the visible source has no unclosed-tag or structural errors. The HTML was truncated, so full rendering could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) appear in the QA data or the visible HTML. Per-recipient identifiers (`_emr`, `_eml`) are populated in every captured URL.

- **Unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not a confirmed defect.

- **From address:** `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain separate from the primary domain. Alignment cannot be assessed without headers.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the QA run states an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported. The footer postal address was not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters.** Links use proprietary tracking instead: `refid=MKTEML_143968`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16798`. This is consistent across all captured links, but GA-style source and medium attribution would depend on Wayfair's own parameter mapping.
- **Landing pages:** link text "Shop Sale" goes to `/daily-sales`, the product links go to product pages, and the app CTA goes to the onelink deep link. The URLs match their link text.
- **Landing page content:** not verified, because the 429s blocked content checks.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the banner images (they are links). Use `alt=""` on the star and tracking images, or give the star images meaningful text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars".
3. Correct `c=Triggered` to a batch or campaign label on the onelink URL.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the original message headers. The relay likely hides them, so treat them as unverified rather than failed.
5. Re-run link probing with a longer time budget or slower pacing to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed links.
