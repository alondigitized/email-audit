# For your fall gatherings: SO much dining room newness
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth Crate-family email I've had in about three days. After the 10/3 sofa blitz and the 10/4 "markdowns" double-tap, I was ready to snooze. This one is at least on target. I'm renovating two houses and I need dining room pieces, so a dining-room-only send beats the Kids sends I got on 10/4.
- The scroll is long and editorial, and it's pretty. The hero is a warm, moody dining room, and then comes a run of collections with a table, chairs, and lighting for each. The module for each collection is a lifestyle shot, a name, a short blurb, two small product thumbnails, and a button. It feels like a mini catalog. That's how I plan a room, so I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. The text is small, though. At the size this renders, I can't read the blurbs or prices without zooming.
- Would I click? Probably one collection, and likely the lighting. Would I forward it? Maybe to my spouse, with the Bilbury or Sullivan block flagged. I'd mark it read and file it, not buy off it today.

## What's working

- The dining room theme matches what I'm doing. After the Kids sends, this is the first one that fits my renovation.
- The "up to 25% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" strip at the top gives me a concrete number right away. It's the same Upholstery Event CB2 SALE pushed ("IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE."), so I've now seen it from both brands.
- Each collection gets its own lifestyle shot, a named set (Sullivan, Bilbury and others), and a "Shop [collection]" button. That helps me build a coordinated palette instead of hunting piece by piece.
- Lighting, rugs and the espresso machine sit under the dining content, so it works as a one-stop room plan. The espresso block says "up to $300 off top coffee & espresso machines," which is another real number.
- The Affirm "pay later" block and the "we love these for you" product grid near the bottom are both useful. The grid gives me a quick scan if I don't want to read the story.

## What's weak

- Only the top strip and the espresso block show a real discount. The dining collections themselves show no price or offer, so I can't tell if the table I like is in the 25% off.
- The email is very long, with around ten modules, and the CTAs are thin buttons. On a phone I'd get fatigued before reaching the lighting or rugs.
- The hero reads "life happens in your dining room." It's soft and gives no urgency or deadline for the sale.
- The "SO much newness" subject suggests new arrivals, but the body mixes new collections, a sale on sofas, and espresso machines. That muddies the message.
- Nothing speaks to rewards or loyalty, and there's no shipping or lead-time info for furniture. Pottery Barn's holiday catalog email at least gave me a clear lane (browse the catalog).
- Tiny body copy and thumbnail text are hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a single dated offer in the hero, such as "Dining tables up to 25% off through Sunday." Put the discount on the collections too, so the offer and the room I'm shopping match.
- 2. Cut the email to the top four dining collections, then lighting, then one sale block. Drop the espresso machines and the Affirm block, or send them separately.
- 3. Add price and "in stock / ships by" lines under each collection's thumbnails. For furniture, lead time is the deciding detail.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample CTA ("order fabric swatches") to the upholstered chair blocks. That's what I do before I buy.
- 5. Make the button copy specific, e.g. "Shop Sullivan dining tables" instead of just "Shop Sullivan."
- 6. Space out sends from the Crate family so I'm not getting the same lamp-and-table lineup from three senders in a week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New dining rooms for fall hosting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five new collections, plus lighting and rugs to match`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sullivan, Bilbury and more, with free swatches`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and a responsive breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 48 images) and a viewport meta tag that disables zoom. The header-based compliance warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 83 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of their destinations or redirect chains were validated. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Duplicate third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5` are five separate LiveRamp pixel requests with the same `s` value. Only `n` differs, so one email open fires five third-party calls.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/0b799024-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are both present. These are standard, but they are among the images the QA check flagged for missing alt text.
- **Template leftover:** the source contains an unfilled placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless in rendering but shows the template wasn't cleaned before send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, ~48 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` (for example `042259ed-…jpg`, `d32c317c-…gif`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`).
  - The hero on `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F0811%5FCB%5FDining%5FRugs%5FLighting%5FHero`.
  - Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - If these images are also used as links, the links have no accessible name. With images blocked, the content collapses entirely.
  - The QA tool doesn't say whether `alt` is absent or empty (`alt=""`). The tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately). `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are both present. This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Not checked:** the HTML was truncated, so the body (semantic structure, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, link text) could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- The `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8` parameter is a per-recipient hash on image URLs. It is resolved, not a leaked token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports both headers not found. The QA note itself says the relay may not capture them. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Check the raw message headers before concluding the sender lacks RFC 8058 one-click support.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It is expected to be stripped by the relay. The sender (`crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`) is a subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the visible HTML is truncated before the footer, so these couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Can't assess. All 83 tracked links go through redirect domains that weren't probed, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible, and no landing-page alignment could be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and hero images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Remove or consolidate the five identical `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels unless the `n=1..5` sequence is intentional.
4. Remove the dead 768px `img` rule and the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and delete the `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
5. For future audits, capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from the original message, not the relay copy. Also resolve a sample of the tracking links to check destinations and UTMs.
