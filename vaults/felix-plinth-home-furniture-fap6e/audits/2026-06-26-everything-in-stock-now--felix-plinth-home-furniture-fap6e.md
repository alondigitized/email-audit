# Everything in stock now
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Forty-two. Give or take. That's where I am with the CB2/Crate family this month, and this one drops on June 26 — the same day I've already been hit by Crate & Barrel's "BIGGER" warehouse sale update and, the day before, CB2's own "It's back. And now it's on sale." send. So "Everything in stock now" shows up in my inbox and my brain files it immediately under *more of the same* before I've even opened it.
- Here's what's frustrating: the content inside is actually defensible. I'm furnishing two houses simultaneously, lead times are killing me, and in-stock availability is something I genuinely track. The 50% off storage signal is real money. The category tiles — accent chairs, bedroom, storage — are three things actively on my list. But the subject line buries all of it. It reads like a logistics update, not a sale. Article's "4th of July Sale starts now" at least told me there was a reason to open. Wayfair's "Open Box steals" told me what I was getting. This tells me the warehouse has product. Thanks, I assumed.
- Won't click. Won't forward. I'll mark it as read and maybe loop back to the storage section via direct navigation if I'm already on the site for something else.

## What's working

- **In-stock framing is actually useful to me** — when I'm watching 12-week lead time estimates blow past my renovation deadlines, "in stock" is a real differentiator that earns a scan.
- **50% off storage is a hard number** — unlike Pottery Barn's "Amazing 4th of July Sale finds" from the same day, which buried actual percentages, CB2 put the discount in the body where I can see it.
- **Category tiles are clean entry points** — three clear buckets (accent chairs, bedroom, storage) without hunting through a wall of lifestyle copy.
- **"SHOP EVERYTHING IN STOCK NOW" CTA is specific** — verb + noun, no vague "Discover" or "Explore," button is visible without excessive scroll.
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum. CB2 is consistent here.

## What's weak

- **Subject line wasted the setup** — "Everything in stock now" has zero offer signal and zero discount hook. The 50% off is buried in the body. This is the same structural mistake CB2 made on the June 25 send, just with a different vague headline.
- **"SEIZE THE MOMENT" earns nothing** — aspirational brand copy as a hero headline when the entire email is about a warehouse sale is a mismatch. I've seen this tone in every CB2 send since April.
- **Hero image is a decorative sideboard, not a sale** — if the hook is 50% off storage, show me storage at 50% off above the fold, not a credenza styled for a catalog spread.
- **No end date anywhere visible** — "now" is not urgency. Without a "Sale ends July 6" or visible low-stock signals, this lands as another indefinite promo, indistinguishable from last week's.
- **Cadence is destroying open rates on my end** — this is the second CB2-adjacent email today and roughly the fourth in three days. I'm not unsubscribing but I am desensitizing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the number** — the 50% off is the only reason to open; it needs to be in the subject, not hidden two scrolls down.
- 2. **Replace "SEIZE THE MOMENT" with the actual deal** — "50% OFF IN-STOCK PICKS" or even just "WAREHOUSE SALE: 50% OFF" does more than the current hero copy.
- 3. **Add a visible sale deadline** — even "Sale ends July 6" closes the loop for someone like me who is triangulating between this and the simultaneous Crate & Barrel warehouse sale.
- 4. **Surface the rewards/loyalty angle** — I buy on the highest-rewards card I have, and CB2 never tells me if there's a loyalty multiplier running. Wayfair at least reminds me consistently. If CB2 has something, say so.
- 5. **Suppress the near-duplicate if one sent yesterday** — two warehouse-adjacent sends in 24 hours from the same brand trains me to ignore the second one automatically.

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with VML/MSO conditional blocks for Outlook; head section contains a confirmed developer artifact and two accessibility/rendering defects. Body-level link, tracking, and compliance elements cannot be fully audited due to HTML truncation.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully assess** — HTML is truncated before body links appear. The following is confirmed from the `<head>`:

- **Cross-domain font dependency:** `HNW05` font family loads exclusively from `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…` (four `@font-face` declarations). If that domain has CSP restrictions, CDN rules, or goes down independently of cb2.com, CB2 emails silently fall back to system fonts with no CB2 control.
- **Double-slash path bug in font URLs:** Four `@font-face` src attributes use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/…` (double forward slash), e.g.:
  ```
  url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2")
  ```
  Most servers normalize this, but it is a malformed URL and should be corrected.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue 1 — Zoom restricted (WCAG 1.4.4 violation):**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-to-zoom on iOS/Android. This fails WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is flagged by accessibility scanners. Remove `maximum-scale=1` or change to `maximum-scale=5`.

**Issue 2 — Non-standard mobile breakpoint:**
```css
@media only screen and (max-width: 418px) { .mobile { width:414px !important; … } }
```
The breakpoint is 418px but the forced width is 414px (iPhone SE width). Standard practice uses 480px or 600px. At 419px–479px viewport widths, the mobile layout never fires, potentially causing layout overflow on mid-size Android devices.

**Issue 3 — Duplicate/conflicting `@font-face` declarations:**
`"Lineto Brown Sub"` is declared four times — once with a single `.eot` src, then again for `woff2` at `font-weight: 400`, then for `woff2` at `font-weight: 600`. The first declaration (no weight specified, `.eot`-only) will win in non-MSO clients for weight 400, overriding the `woff2` version intended for that weight.
