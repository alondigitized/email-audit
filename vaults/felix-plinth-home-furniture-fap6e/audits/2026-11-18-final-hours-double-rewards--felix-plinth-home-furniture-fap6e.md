# FINAL HOURS: DOUBLE REWARDS§
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth CB2 email in about four days, and I filed it as "CB2 again" before I read it. The sender name is "CB2 Credit Card," and "FINAL HOURS" is the third "ends tomorrow/tonight" beat since "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY ENDS TOMORROW" on 11/16. The same urgency, with new wording, has now run all week.
- The hero itself is clean. "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" sits over a dining table and chairs, with a struck-through "10%" and "20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" under it. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so the math is the part I care about. But the hero is a card-acquisition pitch with a "LEARN MORE" button, not a shopping hero. Below it are six category tiles, then two sale banners (up to 60% and up to 70% off) stacked under the rewards message. That's three offers competing in one scroll.
- Would I click? Probably a tile like Lighting or Rugs, since I'm furnishing two houses. I wouldn't click "Learn More" because I already know the card. I wouldn't forward it. It's a reminder, not a find.

## What's working

- The struck-through "10%" next to "20%" makes the doubling obvious at a glance, which is good rewards math.
- The hero photo (a dining table with a sculptural lamp and a pendant) looks like my renovation board. It's the first thing I see, so the hierarchy is clear.
- The six-tile grid (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs) lets me jump to the room I'm working on. The photography is moody and consistent, so it matches my color palette.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" in the hero is a credible deadline for a final-hours send.
- Nothing looks broken. Images load, type is legible, and the buttons are decent tap targets.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete: 20% back, up to 60% off, and up to 70% off clearance. I can't tell which one matters, and the sale banners come across as an afterthought.
- "LEARN MORE" is a weak hero CTA. It sends me to terms, not to product.
- The urgency is stale. "Ends tonight" shows up in the hero and again in the Black Friday banner, after a week of "ends tomorrow" from CB2 itself.
- The footer fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the rewards terms I'd need to verify the 20% are buried in it. That undercuts how honest the offer feels.
- There's nothing tied to my context: no showroom, swatch or sample info, no shipping or delivery timing for furniture, and nothing that says CB2 knows me as a customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero a product-led shopping hero with a verb CTA ("Shop 2x rewards"), not "Learn More." Keep the card pitch in the lower module.
- 2. Pick one offer. Make the rewards the headline and demote the 60% and 70% banners, or the reverse.
- 3. Collapse the "ends tonight" repetition into a single countdown.
- 4. Add one line of furniture logistics, such as "Ships in X weeks" or "Order swatches free," and put the rewards cap and exclusions near the hero.
- 5. Suppress or consolidate sends. Nine in four days is why the urgency doesn't land.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 2x rewards on lighting & rugs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off clearance. Rewards math inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your reward dollars double until midnight. Shop the room you're building.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 64 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 pixels load over plain `http://` and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling:
