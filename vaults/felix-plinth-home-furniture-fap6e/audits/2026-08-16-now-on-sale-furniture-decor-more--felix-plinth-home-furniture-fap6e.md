# NOW ON SALE✨ Furniture, decor & more
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Now on Sale ✨ Furniture, decor & more" — this is the fifth Pottery Barn email in my inbox this week, and it's the least specific one yet. No percentage off, no dollar figure, just "Now on Sale" sitting on top of a leather chair I've already half-seen in three other emails. It's not broken, it's not offensive, it's just... the catalog dump version of the brand, which is exactly the one I don't need when I've already gotten the dining table email, the beds-and-nightstands email, and the bedding email this same week.
- The category grid — Furniture, Tabletop, Bedding, Bath, Outdoor, Pillows & Decor — is basically a sitemap in photo form. It's not wrong for a mid-renovation household, but it's the least targeted send in the run because it's targeting everyone at once. Then there's a "Recommended Just For You" block with a curtain rod, a swivel chair, a jute rug, linen curtains — actual personalization signal, more than most of what I've gotten from this brand lately — but it's buried past the fold under a generic "Shop All Sale" banner that has no dollar amount attached to it either.
- Would I click? Maybe on the curtain rod, since I'm actually mid-window-treatment-shopping for the second house. Would I forward it? No — there's nothing here specific enough to send a friend. This one's getting a scroll-and-archive, not a scroll-and-click.

## What's working

- The "Recommended Just For You" section with the curtain rod, swivel chair, and rugs is legitimately closer to my actual cart than the generic hero — that's the one part of the email doing its job.
- Clean product photography, consistent PB style, no render bugs — text is crisp, images load fine, nothing overlapping.
- The Rewards ("Earn 10% Back") and Affirm blocks at the bottom are relevant to how I actually pay for this stuff — highest-rewards-card guy appreciates seeing loyalty math spelled out.
- Halloween Shop insert is at least seasonally timely, even if it's off-category for me.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete offer. "Now on Sale" and "Deals on Furniture, Decor & More" never once state a percentage, dollar amount, or even "up to X% off" — Crate & Barrel's "Get & stay organized" send at least named bestseller categories, and CB2's "Summer Clearance" led with "up to 60% off" right in the subject. This one gives me nothing to anchor on.
- This is a straight sitewide catalog dump — Furniture, Tabletop, Bedding, Bath, Outdoor, Pillows & Decor is basically every category PB sells, which is the opposite of the dining-table-specific send from 8/14 that actually worked on me.
- Cadence fatigue is real: fourth-plus PB email this week, and this one's the most generic of the bunch — worse than yesterday's "Made to Match" beds-and-nightstands send which at least had three named collections.
- The personalized "Recommended Just For You" row — the actually-relevant part — is stuck below a wall of category tiles and the "Shop All Sale" CTA, so most people bail before they see it.
- Design Crew Sweepstakes ("book a free appointment for a chance to win a $1500 gift card") is a nice touch for a renovation but it's competing for attention with everything else on the page instead of getting its own moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the "Recommended Just For You" products, not generic category tiles — that's the section with actual signal (curtain rods, swivel chairs, rugs) that fits someone renovating right now. Put it right under the hero.
- 2. Put an actual number in the subject and hero. "Up to 30% off furniture & decor" beats "Now on Sale" every time — compare to CB2's "up to 60% off," which at least gives me a reason to open.
- 3. Cut the category grid down to 2-3 tiles tied to what I've browsed (furniture, outdoor) instead of all six PB categories — I don't need a bath towel pitch mixed in with my dining table search.
- 4. Move the Design Crew Sweepstakes and Rewards content higher — those are stronger hooks for a two-house renovator than "Shop All Sale."
- 5. Vary the creative — the leather chair hero has shown up adjacent to too many recent PB sends; give it a rest for a cycle.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTAs route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirect wrapper (Salesforce Marketing Cloud tracking format) — consistent, expected pattern for this ESP.
- QA skipped HTTP probing on 55 tracking/click-redirect links (expected behavior for redirect-domain links, not itself an error).
- Two open-tracking beacons present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`. The `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel is served over **plain HTTP**, which mail clients commonly block, causing this open-tracking pixel to silently fail.
- Secondary click tracker `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/7dbc40f6-69ed-4669-885a-c27c52e27d29?mi_mid=...&cp_tp` present and well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **34 images missing `alt` text**, including all navigation/banner creative (`F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg`, `F26_Top_DesignCrewSweeps_01a/b.jpg`), full product slice sets (`slices_01–18.jpg` in both the sale and footer modules), and product photography (`balboa-swivel-chair-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`, `belgian-flax-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, `400-thread-count-organic-percale-sheet-set-l.jpg`, `custom-capitola-hand-tufted-wool-rug-l.jpg`, `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`).
