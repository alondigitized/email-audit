## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML fallbacks. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most hrefs could not be inspected. The main confirmed issue is missing alt text on 39 images, and the header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **70 tracking links not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations or HTTP status codes were verified. Dead links and redirect chains can't be ruled out.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` requests fire for LiveRamp (rlcdn.com), a third-party data-matching domain. Five calls with an incrementing `n` is redundant. This is a deliverability and privacy-surface concern because it adds a third-party host to the email.
- **Subscriber identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` token is appended to `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif`. These are dynamic recommendation images and an open-tracking pixel. The identifier is also re-used as the `s=` value in the rlcdn pixels, so the same subscriber token is passed to a third party.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **39 images lack alt text** (QA WARN):
  - 25 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, which appear to be dynamic product-recommendation images.
  - 7 are tracking pixels: 5 `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1e45b96e-…`.
  - The pixels should carry `alt=""` with width and height of 1. The 32 content and recommendation images need descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. Without alt text, screen readers read out filenames or URLs, and images-off clients show nothing.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the declared XHTML doctype.
- **Duplicate and conflicting meta tags.** Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. `format-detection` is declared three times: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, then `address=no`, then `telephone=no`. This is redundant and harmless in practice, but it is template cruft.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is unfilled and misspelled. It has no rendering impact but adds payload.
- **Global `p` rule.** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` applies to every paragraph. Any paragraph without its own override will center, which is a risk worth checking.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The personalized `mi_u` identifier resolves to a populated value. The rest of the body was truncated, so this is not fully confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN).
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.

All three warnings are flagged by QA as likely relay-stripping by AgentMail, so they are unconfirmed rather than proven defects. The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the source is truncated. Verify them against the raw message headers and the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. All 70 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so final destinations, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified. Check the post-redirect URLs for `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** add descriptive alt text to the 25 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` images and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` recommendation images. Set `alt=""` on the 7 tracking pixels, with explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
2. **Viewport:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Third-party pixels:** consolidate or remove the five duplicate `sr.rlcdn.com` calls. Confirm that sharing the `mi_u` token with a third party is intended.
4. **Template hygiene:** escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags, and delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
5. **Re-verify the inconclusive items:**
   - Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the original message, not the relay copy.
   - Resolve the 70 redirect links to confirm 200 responses and UTM parameters.
   - Audit the full, untruncated HTML for the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
