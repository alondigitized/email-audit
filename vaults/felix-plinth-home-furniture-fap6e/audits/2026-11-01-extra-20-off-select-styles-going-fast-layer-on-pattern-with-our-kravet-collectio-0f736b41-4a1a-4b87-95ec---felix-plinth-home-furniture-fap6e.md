# EXTRA 20% OFF select styles—going fast! + Layer on pattern with our Kravet Collection
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. The maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is on top for what feels like the sixth send in a row. I've seen it on 11/01's "A little holiday cheer!", 10/31's "Happening now!" and 10/31's "NEW collections for your bedroom". My first reaction was "this again." But the Kravet collaboration underneath is the best creative I've had from them all week. It's moody, layered, and looks like a real room, not a clearance rack.
- The patterned bedding, the "start small, think in layers" pull-quote and the dark dining table styling are right up my alley. I'm redoing two houses, and a primary bedroom refresh fits. The offer is the weak part. "Select styles" doesn't say whether the Kravet pieces are included, so I can't tell if the 20% applies to what I'm looking at. I'd save this to my Pinterest-ish folder, and I probably wouldn't click through for the sale. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The Kravet × Pottery Barn lockup and the "Layer pattern like an interior designer" line are specific. It reads like a design story, not another sale blast.
- The room photography is strong. The tonal bedding, the hallway shot and the candlelit table give me inspiration I'd actually save.
- The pull-quotes ("Patterns tell the story of a home…", "Start small, think in layers") break up the scroll and teach me something.
- "Recommended just for you" includes the Banks Extendable Dining Table and the Reeve Rug. Dining and rugs are on my list, and the products are named.
- The 10% back in Rewards block and the Free Design Services appointment fit how I shop. I'd run this through the rewards card anyway.

## What's weak

- The sale strip is identical to the last five or six sends, so it's banner blindness at this point. Crate & Barrel's Axis sofa email gave me a different hook instead of recycling one.
- "Select styles" is vague, and the fine print is a wall of tiny text. I can't tell whether the Kravet collection qualifies.
- There's only one hero CTA, "See the full collection," for a long scroll of lifestyle imagery. None of the room shots link to a specific product I can see.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid feels generic. A Westin mattress, holiday stockings and a swivel chair don't add up to anything I'd searched for.
- There's no swatch or sample mention for the patterned fabrics, which is the first thing I'd want before buying pattern online. There's also no shipping or lead-time info for furniture.
- It's a lot of cross-promo at the bottom (Christmas Shop, App Store, Affirm, the brand family) after an already long email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the sale from the story. Drop the maroon strip on Kravet sends, or say clearly whether the 20% covers the collection. Right now two messages fight each other.
- 2. Make the room shots shoppable. Add "Shop the bedding" and "Shop the table" links, and show a price or a "from $X" under each.
- 3. Add a swatch and sample offer for the pattern pieces, plus a shipping or lead-time note for furniture.
- 4. Personalize the "Recommended" grid with categories I've browsed. Wayfair at least tries to tie recs to behavior.
- 5. Cut the stacked footer promos down to one: rewards card or design services.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced build behind a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style click-tracking domain. The main findings are widespread missing alt text, cleartext `http://` assets, and the recipient's email address in image URLs. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified from the relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed.** 58 links on click-redirect domains were skipped, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo. Destinations are unverified, so QA cannot confirm there are no dead or mismatched links.
- **Third-party tracking pixels.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (Turn/Amobee ad-tech), `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true...` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/03856028-...?mi_cid=...` are all open-tracking pixels. They have no alt text, and the `svopn` one is served over HTTP.
- **Cross-brand asset.** The spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain inside a Pottery Barn send. It's low risk, but it adds a dependency on another property's CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 60 images).** Examples are `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `slices_01–16.jpg` in the `0814_FRI_AM_FallKravetLaunch` folder, `slices_01–18.jpg` in the `Email-Footer` folder, and the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg` and others). The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from `alt=""`, so some may be intentional decorative images. The sliced hero and footer images carry offer and navigation content, so with images blocked they will render as blank space. The logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Mixed content.** Three images use `http://`: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel. Clients may block or rewrite these.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some web-view and accessibility contexts need a title.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile clients that honor it.
- **Server code in the stylesheet.** The second `<style>` block ends with stray PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...`. This is invalid CSS. It has no visible effect here because it sits last, but it suggests a template or build leak.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`.** The outer layout tables (`<table width="100%" ... bgcolor="#ffffff">` and the spacer table with `<td height="20">`) lack it, although the inner content tables have it. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Unsupported web font.** The `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat">` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The text then falls back to the declared Verdana/Arial.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** are visible in the captured source (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`).
- **Recipient email in plaintext URLs (PII exposure).**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  
  The second one is sent over HTTP, so the address travels in cleartext in a query string. It will also appear in proxy and server logs, and in any image-proxy logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` share an organizational domain, which is a good sign, but there is no evidence either way.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address unverified.** The HTML is truncated before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address can't be confirmed.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,162 of 15,393 characters (about 72.5%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** Links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects, and no destination URLs were resolved. The only direct `potterybarn.com` URL in the capture, `coreemail.html?email=...`, has no UTMs.
- **Landing-page alignment is unverified** for the same reason. The offer ("EXTRA 20% OFF select styles") and the Kravet Collection links could not be checked.
- **Stale asset paths.** Image paths such as `/2026/US/0814_FRI_AM_FallKravetLaunch/` (August 14) and `/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` are reused in a send dated November. This is only a staleness signal to check, not a confirmed error.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the sliced hero and footer images, and `alt=""` to decorative images and tracking pixels.
2. Move the `http://` assets (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel) to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque subscriber ID or hashed token.
4. Check the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and read SPF/DKIM/DMARC from a direct (non-relay) capture.
5. Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block, add a real `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the outer layout tables.
7. Rebalance the plain-text part so it has more readable copy and fewer bare URLs.
8. Resolve the redirect chains in a follow-up run to verify destinations and UTMs. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address from the full, untruncated HTML.
