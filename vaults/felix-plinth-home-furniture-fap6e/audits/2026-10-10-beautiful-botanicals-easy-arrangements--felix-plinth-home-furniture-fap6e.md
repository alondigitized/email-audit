# Beautiful botanicals, easy arrangements
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. The green "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" strip is on top, same as nearly every send since 10/06. After that the email does something different: a botanicals story with a dark, moody hero ("The Botanical Shop"), then faux stems, hydrangeas, wreaths and garlands in tidy sections. It's a better product story than the 10/10 "NOW ON SALE: Bestselling bedding" send, which repeated the 70% headline twice on the first screen. As a 36-year-old furnishing two houses I can see styled mantels and consoles in here, so it reads as a real send.
- It's also a long scroll for something I don't urgently need. Faux hydrangeas are a finishing touch, not a punch-list item, and I'm still on lamps, bedding and big furniture. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder, because the mantel with the candles and garland is pretty. I probably wouldn't click through today. I wouldn't forward it either, unless my partner is staging the second house.
- The "Recommended just for you" block near the bottom lands closer to my actual list: curtain rod, swivel chair, dresser, sofa, jute rug. Those items should have been higher up.

## What's working

- The "Botanical Shop" hero is moody and seasonal, with deep burgundy and rust that match fall. It looks like a magazine spread, not a coupon.
- The sections are clearly labeled (Floral Stems & Branches, Floral Arrangements, Wreaths & Garlands), so I can scan to what I care about.
- The styled room shots (mantel with candles, console with a branch arrangement) show how to use the product. I save emails like that.
- "Recommended just for you" gives me something to click that fits two renovations, and the "Shop all botanicals" button is easy to find.
- The Pottery Barn Rewards card callout ("Earn 10% back in rewards") speaks to someone who buys through the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The green "Up to 70% off" strip is the same one I've seen since 10/06. The botanicals story never says which items are on sale, so the offer feels generic and I can't tell if it applies here.
- Every product tile is captioned with a name only, such as "Faux Chocolate Leaf Branch" and "Faux Hydrangeas". I can't see prices, sale prices or ratings.
- The email has two "Shop Now" CTAs before any content, plus Discover New Arrivals and The Christmas Shop at the bottom. That's a lot of competing campaigns in one send.
- The Christmas Shop banner pulls the email in a different direction. It's October 10th and I'm still deciding on a sofa.
- The legal and fine print block at the bottom is dense and tiny on a phone. It makes the "up to 70%" feel conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one visible, specific offer on the botanicals. For example, "Faux stems 30% off through Sunday" would turn a mood board into a reason to act.
- 2. Add prices, with the strikethrough and the sale price, under the product tiles, and star ratings on the top two.
- 3. Cut the bottom campaign stack (New Arrivals plus Christmas Shop) to one CTA, or move "Recommended for you" up above the wreaths.
- 4. Add a shipping line. For a catalog like this I want to know what ships free and when it arrives.
- 5. Link the room shots to shop-the-look pages, so the mantel photo goes to the actual candles, garland and stems.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, image-sliced email with 62 redirect-wrapped links. It has widespread missing `alt` text, several non-HTTPS image sources, and the recipient's email address in tracking-pixel URLs. The truncated source and missing relay headers mean unsubscribe and authentication can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** All 62 links use click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. The QA probe skipped them, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** Three images load over `http://`, which can trigger mixed-content blocking or proxy rewriting:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- **Stale and cross-brand assets:** The `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` path points to a 2022 asset. `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on a different brand's domain, so the email depends on that host staying up.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text.
- **Multiple open-tracking pixels:** There are three: `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/64f75086-...`, and `d.turn.com`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text:** About 70 images have none. This includes every `F26_Decor_B6_FallBotanicals/slices_01–20.jpg`, `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, all product images (e.g. `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `sausalito-8-drawer-dresser-66-l.jpg`), and the top and bottom banners. The email is almost entirely sliced images, so with images off or in a screen reader it is largely empty and unlabeled. The only `alt` seen in the visible source is on the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."`) after the `html {...}` rule. Some clients may drop or misparse the rule that follows it.
- **Unsupported attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` aren't supported in some clients (e.g. Gmail app variants). The `.wrapper` and `.photo` classes may also not exist in the visible markup.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is ignored by most major clients, so it falls back. This is minor.
- **Mobile layout:** `maximum-scale=1` and `.showmobile{width:414px}` are fixed pixel values. The layout relies on `<table width="650">`, so narrow screens depend on `img{max-width:100%}`, which applies only at `min-width:650px`. Below 650px the images may not scale. Verify this in a mobile render.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** appear in the visible source.
- **Email address in URLs:** The recipient's address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears in plaintext query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=...`
  
  The `svopn` one is unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit. This is a privacy and leakage risk, since URLs end up in logs and referrers. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` partly mitigates it.
- **`coreemail.html` as an image:** It is listed as an image with no alt text, so it is likely a tracking pixel.

- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the tracking domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` do align.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be verified here.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,045 of 16,309 characters (about 74%) are URLs. This can look spam-like and hurts readability.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`:** Harmless in email, but irrelevant.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible in the source. All links are opaque `?qs=` redirects, so UTM and campaign tagging can't be confirmed. The QA run skipped the HTTP probes, so landing-page alignment with the "Beautiful botanicals" and Fall Botanicals content is untested.
- **Footer banners:** The `HolidayBanners/H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg` banners can't be checked against their destinations.

4. Set a non-empty `<title>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in query strings (especially over HTTP). Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
6. Re-run QA against the full raw `.eml` (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. Also resolve a sample of the 62 redirects to verify UTMs and landing pages.
