# Rugs that give any room a fresh POV
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Colorful Foundations" as the hero with that living room shot — brass table, that layered blue rug, the credenza with books — is actually a room I'd screenshot for my own reno mood board. Rugs are a real category for me right now, I'm mid-renovation on two houses and floors are one of the last things you nail down, so the "Set the tone with a color you love" color-sorted grid (Blue, Neutral, Green) is a genuinely smart way to let me self-select instead of making me scroll a wall of product.
- Where it loses me is right after: it pivots into "Recommended Just For You" with a curtain rod, a swivel chair, two beds, towels, and sheets — none of that is rugs, and it dilutes what started as a focused category email into another everything-store dump. Then it tacks on a Halloween shop and a Design Crew pitch, which just feels like Pottery Barn cramming every current campaign into one send.
- I'd click through on "Shop Blue" or "Shop Green" for the rugs specifically — that part is genuinely well done. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, and I'm not opening the recommended-for-you section at all.

## What's working

- The color-first browse pattern (Shop Blue / Neutral / Green) matches exactly how I actually shop rugs — by palette, not by SKU.
- Hero room photography looks like an actual finished room, not a catalog cutout — that's the same quality bar Crate & Barrel hit with its Caraway email pull-quote, just applied to styling instead of a review.
- "Colorful Foundations" is a decent hook that ties subject to hero without just repeating it.

## What's weak

- "Recommended Just For You" throwing beds, towels, and a curtain rod into a rugs email breaks the category focus completely — Article's storage email at least stayed on-topic the whole way through.
- The Halloween Shop and Design Crew blocks feel bolted on — three unrelated campaigns stacked in one send is worse sprawl than even the CB2/Crate & Barrel "same-week fatigue" problem I already have.
- No pricing, no sale %, no rewards-card math anywhere near the rugs — Wayfair at least slaps "70% OFF" on its cutting board tile even if the rest of that email was empty.
- No swatch/sample-order CTA for rugs, which is the one thing that actually gets me to buy a rug sight-unseen online.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to rugs only — kill "Recommended Just For You," Halloween, and Design Crew, and use that space for more rug looks or a swatch-sample CTA. That's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. Add a real offer line under the hero — even "Rugs from $X" or a %-off band would beat the Wayfair last-call flag-emoji approach while still creating urgency.
- 3. Put a "Free swatches" or "Order a sample" CTA next to Shop Blue/Neutral/Green — rugs are a texture/color risk purchase, and PB's own bedding emails (like the 9/21 duvet send) lean on fabric/weight detail that rugs are missing here.
- 4. If cross-sell must stay, cap it to one adjacent category (pillows/throws) that pairs with rugs, not beds and towels.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rugs to ground your fall refresh`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop by color — plus free swatches before you commit`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Blue, neutral, or green — find your foundation piece`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Rugs that give any room a fresh POV"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses standard legacy table-based HTML with MSO conditionals and third-party ESP tracking (Bronto/Oracle-style `svopn`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`, `d.turn.com`); no unsubscribe headers or authentication headers were captured, and nearly all images lack alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 54 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) were skipped by HTTP probing — normal for redirect-wrapped ESP links, not itself a defect, but unverified.
- Two 1x1/tracking pixels use plain `http://` instead of `https://`:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — open-tracking pixel with a raw email address in the query string, sent over unencrypted HTTP.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- The recipient's plaintext email address is exposed in cleartext in the open-tracking pixel URL and in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — both transmitted (the pixel over HTTP) in ways that could leak PII to network intermediaries.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 46+ images missing `alt` text across product slices (`slices_01–18.jpg`), banners (`nav0.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_HalloweenShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`), and product photography (rugs, beds, towels, sheets). This breaks screen-reader and images-blocked fallback rendering.
- 3 tracking pixels (`svopn`, `d.turn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`) are also flagged as "missing alt text," which is expected/non-issue for 1x1 pixels — no fix needed there.
- Mixed-content risk: `http://` image sources may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading (Gmail/Outlook proxy will still typically rewrite these, but native HTTP requests from other clients could fail silently).
- Deprecated DOCTYPE (XHTML 1.0 Transitional) and heavy MSO conditional bloat — functional but adds weight; not a defect per se.
- Odd artifact: a `<style>` block contains raw PHP-style `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) inside CSS — these are inert as CSS (browsers/mail clients will ignore invalid declarations) but indicate a code-generation/templating leak from the ESP build pipeline worth flagging to the sender's dev team.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the truncated source.
- Recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is correctly interpolated into tracking/service links (`coreemail.html`, `svopn` pixel), confirming personalization tokens rendered successfully for this send.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in captured headers — QA notes this may be an artifact of the relay (AgentMail) not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm root cause from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe compliance (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules since Feb 2024) cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; same relay-capture caveat applies.
- No visible physical mailing address or unsubscribe link was present in the truncated HTML sample provided (footer content was cut off before capture ended), so CAN-SPAM footer compliance cannot be assessed from this excerpt.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)
- Cannot assess: the truncated HTML source ends before content/product CTA links are visible, and the 54 destination links were skipped rather than resolved, so no landing-page URLs or UTM parameters are available to verify against.
- The one non-tracking direct link visible, `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`, points to a real potterybarn.com path with no UTM parameters — can't confirm campaign attribution tagging without seeing more of the link set.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` attributes to all content-bearing images (rug/product/banner slices) — currently ~40+ are missing, which fails accessibility and produces a blank experience with images disabled.
2. Migrate the two remaining `http://` image/pixel URLs to `https://`, especially `svopn.potterybarn.com`, which also carries the recipient's raw email address over an unencrypted connection.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP-style statements from the `<style>` block — dead code from the template pipeline, currently rendered inert by the browser but a sign of unsanitized build output.
4. Re-run QA with full headers captured directly from the sending MTA (not via the AgentMail relay) to get a real reading on `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` — current absence is inconclusive, not confirmed non-compliance.
5. Re-run the audit against the full (untruncated) HTML to verify CAN-SPAM footer elements (physical address, working unsubscribe link) and to check destination-link UTM tagging for site continuity.
