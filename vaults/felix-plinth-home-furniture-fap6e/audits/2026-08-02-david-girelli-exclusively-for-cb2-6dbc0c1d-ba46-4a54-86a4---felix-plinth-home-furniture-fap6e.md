# David Girelli, exclusively for CB2
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "David Girelli, exclusively for CB2" — my first thought was "who?" This is CB2 doing a designer-spotlight issue instead of another discount blast, which after five-plus sends from them this week (the "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" pile, the "final weekend for 30% off" bedroom push) is honestly a decent palate cleanser. The Provence studio shot, the sketch-book textures, "Swiss engineering with the romance of Italian soul" — it reads more like a shelter-mag feature than a sales email, and I'll admit I scrolled further than I usually do for CB2.
- But then I hit the actual sale — same semi-annual bedroom + bath event, same 30% off, same "ends soon" energy I already saw yesterday in "These bedrooms are so good + final weekend for 30% off" — buried two-thirds of the way down under a designer bio I don't have context for. It's the same move Wayfair pulled with the headboards email that ambushed me with an outdoor clearance banner: subject promises one thing, the real offer is a different email wearing a costume.
- Would I click? Maybe the "Shop the Collection" button on the David Girelli pieces out of pure curiosity about the sconce — that brass one is genuinely nice. Would I forward it? No. Would I open the next one from CB2 this week without some fatigue? Also no.

## What's working

- The Aldine Sofa / Aldine Chair / Turno Sconce trio is shot cleanly and the designer quotes give it actual point-of-view instead of generic "new arrivals" copy.
- "Shop the Collection" is a clear, unmissable CTA right under the hero — no hunting for it.
- The CB2 Rewards card callout ("Earn 10% back") in the footer is the kind of loyalty math I actually track since I buy through my highest-rewards card anyway.
- No render bugs, no lorem ipsum, no broken images — clean production top to bottom, unlike some of the sloppier retail sends I get.

## What's weak

- The hero collection pieces — the actual sofa and chair I'm supposed to want — don't show a single price. I have to scroll to "More For You" at the bottom to see numbers on completely different products.
- The 30% off bedroom + bath event is the second time this week CB2's run this exact same promo (7/31's "final weekend for 30% off" already covered it), and it's crammed in mid-scroll instead of leading with it.
- This email is trying to be four emails at once — designer spotlight, sale, review giveaway, rewards card pitch, app download, text-signup discount. That's a lot of competing CTAs for one send.
- Subject says nothing about a sale at all, so if I'd opened this expecting "who's David Girelli," the 30% off ambush two-thirds down feels tacked-on, same bait-and-switch feeling Wayfair gave me with that outdoor-clearance banner under a headboards subject line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — a pure editorial "Designers to Know" feature and a separate sale email. Trying to do both dilutes the story and buries the discount.
- 2. Put a price under the Aldine Sofa and Aldine Chair. If you're going to feature them as the hero, let me know what they cost before I click through.
- 3. Reference the "ends August 5" deadline near the actual 30% off banner, not just in six-point fine print at the bottom.
- 4. If you keep the combined format, move the sale banner up to right after the designer collection so it doesn't feel like a footnote.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New from David Girelli — plus 30% off bedroom & bath`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Meet David Girelli, and save 30% before Aug 5`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Swiss-Italian design meets our biggest bedroom sale of the season.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Aldine collection just launched — plus up to 30% off bedding.`

- 72 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (opaque redirect domains) — link destinations were not independently verified and should be manually checked before send.
- Multiple third-party ad-tracking pixels load over plain `http://` instead of `https://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`
