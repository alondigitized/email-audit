# Starts now! Bestselling Aspen dinnerware 15% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Same Aspen dinnerware drop I already opened once this week, just with a different subject line slapped on it — "Starts now! Bestselling Aspen dinnerware 15% off" instead of whatever the last one called itself. Plates, a highball glass, cereal bowls, a martini glass, mugs — I scrolled through the whole thing and there's not a single item here that touches either of my two gut jobs right now. I've got two houses mid-renovation and Crate & Barrel wants to talk to me about a $5 salad plate.
- The one thing that stopped my thumb was "we love these for you" — a Le Creuset Dutch oven and a KitchenAid stand mixer showed up, which at least feels like it's trying to read my Wayfair-and-Pottery-Barn-adjacent browsing instead of just pushing whatever's on sale. But that's buried two-thirds of the way down, way past the dinnerware I don't need.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is a mark-as-read-while-waiting-for-coffee email. If anything, it's reinforcing the "why does the same lamp — or in this case the same plate — show up three times a week" fatigue I already have with this brand.

## What's working

- The hero shot is clean — actual styled tablescape, pomegranates and a napkin, not a stock product grid, so at least it doesn't look cheap.
- Pricing is dead simple: sale price crossed against "Reg." price on every single item, no math required.
- "we love these for you" section actually pulled in categories I touch — cookware, small appliances — even if it's stapled onto a dinnerware email.
- Two clear CTAs stacked ("Shop the Sale" / "Shop All Bestselling Tabletop") so I'm not hunting for the button.

## What's weak

- Zero renovation content. Compare this to Pottery Barn's "elements of a welcoming entryway" send I gave a 6 to last week — that one at least tied itself to a room I'm actively working on. This is just tableware.
- The "in season now: summer produce" corn banner in the middle has nothing to do with anything above or below it — feels like a filler module someone dropped in to break up the page.
- This is the second time I've seen "Aspen collection 15% off" essentially reskinned — first as "starts now," now presumably again. CB2 already burned me on this same pattern with "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" right after a Crate & Barrel send, and I flagged the family-wide snooze fatigue then too.
- New Crate Card plug midway down is just noise for me — I already shop on a fixed rewards card and I'm not switching cards for 5.90% back on cereal bowls.
- No mention of a showroom or in-person swatch/sample option anywhere, which for tabletop specifically I'd actually want — is white porcelain going to look right in a mid-century kitchen or a farmhouse one? No way to tell from a thumbnail.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of tabletop-only sends entirely, or bundle dinnerware into a broader "finish your renovation" email with furniture/lighting up top and tableware as a secondary module — right now I'm getting the whole catalog carved into single-category blasts and it's exhausting.
- 2. Cut the corn/produce banner — it does nothing for conversion and just adds scroll fatigue before I even get to the stuff I might buy (cookware, appliances).
- 3. Move "we love these for you" to the top, above the dinnerware hero — that's the only section actually aimed at someone mid-reno, don't bury it.
- 4. Add a room/style filter chip ("Modern," "Farmhouse," "Coastal") on the tabletop grid so I can see how Aspen actually reads in a space instead of a bowl on white seamless.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Dutch oven and stand mixer we think you'll actually want.`
