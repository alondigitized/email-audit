# Things of quality. Expert design.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, the fifteenth or so send in about a week. This one finally does something different. There's no sale and no countdown, just a free design services pitch, and after "WE RARELY DO THIS" earlier today I was braced for another fake deadline. It's a quiet, moody, well-photographed email. The brown and cream palette, the marble globe lamp and the olive sofa are exactly my taste. I'm furnishing two houses, so a free designer is a real hook for me.
- The problem is that the email asks for a lot and gives me almost nothing to act on. Two "Book your free design appointment" buttons, and that's the whole ask. I can't tell what an appointment involves, how long it takes, or whether it's a video call or a showroom visit. The middle of the email is four big lifestyle photos with no captions, no prices and no product names. I can't even find that lamp without hunting.
- Would I click? Maybe, later. I'd save it to my inspiration folder, but I wouldn't book today. I'd forward it to my partner, since we're stuck on the lighting for the primary. I wouldn't forward it as an offer.

## What's working

- The palette is consistent: warm browns, cream and olive. Pottery Barn's Norman Rockwell send looked good, but this feels more like my actual renovation.
- "Free" is in the sender name, so I knew the offer before opening. That's more honest than "WE RARELY DO THIS."
- The "2 WAYS TO MEET: In store or online" block explains the service. Mentioning showroom and remote options matters for someone doing a whole-home project.
- The "90+ FABRICS. 10 FREE SWATCHES." banner is the best practical hook in the email. Swatches are exactly how I de-risk a sofa order.
- The Rewards footer says "Earn 10% back" in Rewards Dollars on CB2 and Crate & Barrel. That's the card math I care about.
- Category tiles (Sofas, Dining, Bedroom, Lighting, Rugs, Mirrors) give me a way to shop the rooms I'm doing.

## What's weak

- Nothing is shoppable. The big lamp and sofa photos have no product names, no prices and no links I can see. For someone who hates seeing the same lamp in three brands' emails, I'd love to know which lamp this one is.
- The two identical "BOOK YOUR FREE DESIGN APPOINTMENT" buttons are the only primary CTAs. They're separated by a long scroll of photos.
- The email is very long, and the swatches banner is buried near the bottom. That's the most useful thing in it.
- The hero text "HOME IS EVERYTHING" and "FURNISH IT WELL" is pretty but doesn't say what's on offer. I had to read down to "Interior Design Services" to get the point.
- It's another CB2 email on top of the Crate & Barrel flood, and the footer even shows Crate & Barrel, Crate & Kids and H&G. My brain files it with "CB2 family snooze."
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and unreadable without zooming. I can't tell if the appointment is really free.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the swatch offer and the Rewards benefit up top, next to the design appointment CTA. Right now the most practical value sits at the bottom.
- 2. Add product names and prices under the hero lamp and sofa, and link each photo. The globe lamp image needs a "Shop the Alba lamp" style label.
- 3. Spell out the appointment: how long, in store or video, what I walk away with (mood board, floor plan, sourcing list).
- 4. Add a second CTA with a verb and noun, like "Order free swatches" or "Shop sofas", so there's an option besides booking.
- 5. Give me a break in the cadence. After the sale-heavy week, a service email is welcome, but combining it with the next sale event dilutes it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free designer for your whole-home reno`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10 free swatches + a designer, on us`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book a free in-store or online session. Plus 10 free fabric swatches.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, lighting and rugs, planned with a CB2 designer. No cost.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a 57% QA pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The confirmed problems are missing alt text on 11 content images, 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi ad-network pixels, and a viewport/text-size configuration that restricts zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **60 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** Eleven `http://` images load from Dotomi (Conversant):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1`
  
  Mail clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop these. The `cookieredir/pubN.php` pattern is ad-network cookie syncing, which adds ten extra third-party requests per open.
- **First-party open and view trackers** are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=<64-hex>&mi_...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/196a6a05-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. The 64-hex `mi_u` value looks like a hashed subscriber identifier, but the source is truncated, so I can't confirm that. Both pixels lack `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** Eleven images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`, so they will render blank or show filenames when images are blocked. Their filenames start with:
  - `cee2c9a2-…`
  - `9a84fadd-…`
  - `6ffc0701-…`
  - `c2be15fd-…`
  - `14a2ff3d-…`
  - `28417d40-…`
  - `77909a02-…`
  - `2de6e60c-…`
  - `3b60715d-…`
  - `2f604a89-…`
  - `3a05caa8-…`
- **Pixels without `alt=""`.** The 12 tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, `UCMController`, `pub1`–`pub10.php`) also lack `alt`. Screen readers may announce their filenames. Add `alt=""` to each.
- **Zoom restrictions.** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. The `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` rule also disables user text scaling. Both are WCAG 1.4.4 concerns.
- **Font loading.** Web fonts load from `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com`, and the declared `.eot` and `.woff` sources won't load in most clients. Fallbacks are `Helvetica, sans-serif`, which is acceptable. Some `@font-face` URLs contain a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...`).
- **Truncated source.** I can't assess table structure, `role="presentation"`, or bulletproof buttons beyond what is visible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or subject line.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Neither header was found. QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed rather than proven missing**. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe are not met.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer.** The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.
- **Sender domain.** `mail.CB2.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with branded sending.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. All 60 links are wrapped in click-redirect domains and were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment could not be checked. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the pixels suggest campaign and message IDs are tracked on the sender side.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 11 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Replace the `http://` Dotomi pixels with HTTPS endpoints, or remove the ten `cookieredir/pubN.php` syncs.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `text-size-adjust:none`, or limit them to specific Outlook and Apple quirks.
4. Re-run header checks against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy, to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
5. Resolve a sample of the 60 redirect links in a real browser to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
6. Run the QA pass on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer address and unsubscribe link.
