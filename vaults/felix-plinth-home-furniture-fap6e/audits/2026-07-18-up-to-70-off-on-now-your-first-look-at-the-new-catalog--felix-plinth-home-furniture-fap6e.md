# Up to 70% off ON NOW + Your first look at the NEW catalog
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-nine. I'm at twenty-nine Pottery Barn emails in eighteen days. I checked. Yesterday brought two — the Chris Loves Julia collab and the indigo color story, both of which were legitimately good sends. So when this lands, I'm already carrying a mild resentment balance before I even open it.
- But here's the thing: "up to 70% off" actually works on me even at email twenty-nine. I'm in the middle of furnishing two houses at the same time — there's a dining set I've been stalking and a bedroom refresh for the second home that keeps getting pushed because nothing hits the right price point. Seventy percent off on Pottery Barn furniture is real money.
- The catalog reveal is the smarter hook. PB's physical catalog is legitimately one of the things I pull out when I'm trying to explain a room direction to a contractor. Getting the digital first look framed as access — not just "browse more" — is a genuine value-add. I'd open this. I wouldn't click immediately. I'd park it in my "buy later" folder alongside the July 16 artisanal vases send and probably return to it once. I wouldn't forward it — nothing here is surprising enough to share.

## What's working

- **The 70% off lead is doing real work.** When you're pricing out full-room refreshes across two properties simultaneously, that number stops the scroll.
- **"The Summer Catalog — Your first look" framing is differentiated.** It positions the email as an access moment, not just another browse nudge.
- **Recommended Just For You section is visible.** Personalized product rows are what move me toward a click — if those suggestions are actually based on my browse history, that's the right move.
- **20% Back in Rewards is visible at the bottom.** I buy on my highest-rewards card, but PB's own stacking rewards are relevant math when a single purchase crosses $400.
- **Clean render, zero layout problems.** No broken images, no placeholder text, no overlapping copy. After the IKEA OTP disaster that hit my inbox earlier this week, I appreciate basic competence.

## What's weak

- **"ON NOW" reads like a Wayfair tactic, not a Pottery Barn tone.** PB should lean into craft and aspiration; that copy sounds like clearance rack urgency. Yesterday's Chris Loves Julia send did aspiration meaningfully better.
- **No end date on the sale.** "ON NOW" means nothing without stakes. CB2's Floor Sample Sale email gave me a reason to act; this just says something is happening.
- **The New Arrivals grid is a category dump.** Pillows & Accents, Lighting, Bedroom, Outdoor Furniture, Tabletop — that's the entire store with no editorial angle. Article's home tour send picks one room, tells one story, and earns the scroll. This throws the sitemap at me.
- **"Shop Now" is doing zero work.** Identical CTA copy from yesterday's "Discover New Arrivals" send. When I get back-to-back emails from the same brand with the same button label, I notice — and it lowers my trust that anyone curated this for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with one specific hero product at a specific sale price.** Even a single styled room shot with a "Sofa, now $X" callout beats the catch-all percentage. The catalog browse is the secondary story — let the sale conversion carry first.
- 2. **Add a hard end date.** "Sale ends Sunday" is the difference between clicking now versus filing this in my revisit-later folder and never coming back.
- 3. **Collapse the New Arrivals grid into one editorial category.** Pick the single most compelling new arrival. PB has the inventory to tell one sharp story; Article does it every send and it's the reason I actually browse their emails instead of just filing them.
- 4. **Differentiate CTA copy from yesterday's email.** "Browse the Summer Catalog" and "Shop 70% Off" are two distinct actions — name them separately and don't recycle "Shop Now" across back-to-back sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer Sale is live: up to 70% off your shortlist`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New catalog drop + 70% off — ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `First look at the Summer Catalog — deep discounts on furniture, rugs, and lighting now live.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Sunday. New catalog just dropped. Two reasons to open this one.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — PHP code injected into CSS block:**
The following server-side directives appear verbatim inside a `<style>` tag:
```
header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
header("Pragma: no-cache");
```
These are invalid CSS. They will cause CSS parser errors in strict email clients and represent a template assembly failure — server-side code that was not stripped before rendering.

**Missing `alt` text:** 39 images across the email have no `alt` attribute set. This includes all content slices (`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`), hero banners (`F26_TOP_SummerSalePartC_NoUrgency.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals.jpg`, `S26_Bottom_UpTo60OffOutdoor.jpg`), and product images (bed, rug, towel, curtain, curtain rod). Renders as a blank email when images are blocked.

**HTTP image sources (3 assets):** Will be blocked by Gmail, Outlook 365, and Apple Mail with image proxying active:
- `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

**Open pixel tracker also HTTP:**
`http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` — open tracking may fail in clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading.

**Viewport `maximum-scale=1`:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents user zoom on mobile. Accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

**Empty `<title>` tag:** `<title></title>` — affects web-view rendering and any browser tab display.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header:** Not captured by relay. Cannot confirm CAN-SPAM/GDPR-required header is present. If absent from the actual SMTP envelope, this is a compliance failure.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (>5k/day) as of 2024. Absence risks deliverability suppression.

**Authentication-Results:** Not captured via AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown. Cannot confirm sender authentication for `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`.

**`meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"`:** Suppresses HTTP Referer header on any web-view link clicks. Will break attribution in any analytics system that uses Referer as a fallback when UTM params are absent.
