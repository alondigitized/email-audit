# ⭐️ Our top Early Black Friday deals ⭐️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the thirteenth Pottery Barn email in about two days, and I'm numb to it. It's the same maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner I've now seen in "For the early shopper: UP TO 70% OFF" and "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS + Up to 70% off for your cozy home." The subject says "top deals," and that's a fair description of the body. It's a clean grid with real products and real was/now prices, not just a banner.
- What keeps it from being a throwaway is that it's built for someone like me. I'm renovating two houses, so the Toscana dining table at $1,599 was and 60% off lands. So does the sofa and chair block, with the Cole Swivel Chair at $774. The "In-stock furniture, delivered in 1-4 weeks" strip answers my biggest furniture worry, which is shipping lead time. The 10% back in rewards block also speaks to my highest-rewards-card habit.
- Would I click? Probably, once, on the dining table or the sofa, then leave it for the Pinterest-adjacent folder. I wouldn't forward it. Nobody I know wants their fourteenth Pottery Barn email.

## What's working

- The 70% hero with a single SHOP NOW button is instantly readable. I know the offer in half a second.
- Product tiles show was/now pricing, like the Toscana Dining Table at $1,599 and the Cole Swivel Chair at $774. I can do the math without clicking.
- The "SHIPS FREE" tags on the bedding and pillows tiles are useful, and they're specific.
- The "In-stock furniture… delivered in 1-4 weeks" strip speaks directly to furniture shipping anxiety. Wayfair almost never answers that in its emails.
- The category tiles (Tabletop & Bar, Bath, Rugs, Art & Mirrors, Lighting) fit how I shop room by room for two houses.

## What's weak

- Cadence is brutal. This is the same offer, the same banner, and the same up-to-70% claim I've seen since 11/14, just reshuffled.
- "Up to 70%" is doing a lot of work. The furniture tile is only 60% and the sofas are 50%. The big numbers are on pillows and decor, which are not what I'm spending real money on.
- The "Recommended just for you" block is stockings, a throw, and a quilt. That's generic holiday filler, not personalization. They don't seem to know I've been looking at dining furniture and rods.
- The legal and fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of tiny text. I can't read it on a phone without zooming. The exclusions are buried.
- The Design Crew sweepstakes, the app promo, the Affirm block and the credit card pitch are stacked at the bottom, so the page runs very long. Crate & Barrel keeps its rewards messaging up top instead.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap the cadence. Send one flagship Early Black Friday email, then trigger follow-ups from browse behavior. Thirteen sends in two days is training me to ignore the sender.
- 2. Make the "recommended" block real. If I clicked a dining table, show dining tables and a matching rod or lighting, not stockings.
- 3. Move the rewards line (10% back) and the in-stock strip higher, since those are my deciding factors on a furniture purchase.
- 4. Lead with the best furniture deal instead of the blanket "up to 70%," and state the real range, like "Furniture 40-60% off."
- 5. Cut the fine print into a short expandable "exclusions" line with a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dining tables up to 60% off, in stock in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Early Black Friday picks: sofas, tables, rugs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Was $1,599, now less. Plus free shipping on bedding and pillows.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `In-stock furniture ships in 1-4 weeks. Earn 10% back in rewards.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with an SFMC-style click wrapper (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`). The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main code-level issues are widespread missing image alt text, `http://` image sources that pass the recipient's address in the URL, an empty `<title>`, and a viewport that blocks zoom. Header-based checks (unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links unverified:** 59 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. The destinations, redirect health and UTM params are unknown. All visible hrefs are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` tokens (for example the logo link).
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with alt `"1"`. `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…` is used as an `<img src>`, and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/4c2f4e86-…?mi_cid=…` is another open pixel. That makes at least four open or tracking beacons (SFMC `svopn`, Turn, `coreemail.html`, `clickcp`).
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is server-side code leaked into CSS. It is harmless to render but invalid, and it suggests an unsanitized template.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 55 images flagged:**
  - `slices_01`–`slices_14.jpg` in `/1116_MON_AM_EBF_ZigZagGrid/`.
  - `slices_01`–`slices_18.jpg` in `/Email-Footer/`.
  - Product images such as `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - Banners such as `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg` and `H25_Bottom_InStock_V2.jpg`.

  With images blocked, the sliced hero grid and footer render as blank space and the links in them have no accessible name. Only the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`) is covered.
- **`http://` image sources, mixed content:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&…`

  These may be blocked or rewritten by clients and proxies.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom on clients that honor it, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stale or cross-brand assets:**
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm host used as a spacer.
  - A `/pb/2022/` banner and an `H25_` (2025) asset are still in a 2026 send.
- **Spacer table:** `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"`, no width and no `border="0"`. This is minor, and the other layout tables do set `role="presentation"`.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by many clients, so it falls back to the inline stacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were found in the visible source or QA output.
- **Recipient email in URLs:** the address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext query strings (`coreemail.html?email=…` and `sv_sveme=…`). The `svopn` beacon sends it over unencrypted HTTP. This leaks PII to network observers and to server and proxy logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing from the captured message. QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed. If it is real, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. These are likely stripped by the relay, so this is a gap in the data, not a confirmed defect.
- **Footer:** the source is truncated, so the unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked.
- **Sender:** `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` is a consistent subdomain of the brand domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable:** no UTM parameters are visible in any href, since all are opaque `qs=` tokens. Attribution probably happens in the redirector, which can't be confirmed here.
- **Landing pages not probed:** the 59 click links were skipped, so landing-page alignment with the "Early Black Friday / up to 70% off" offer is unchecked.
- **Product image URLs:** images from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` are hosted on the main site. Their query params are not shown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all sliced hero and footer images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels. Make sure sliced images that carry offer text have alt text that states the offer.
2. Move the three `http://` images to HTTPS, and stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
3. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Set a meaningful `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Retire the 2022 and 2025 banners and the West Elm spacer from the shared module library.
6. To close the QA gaps, capture raw headers and verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original delivery. Resolve the click wrappers with a redirect-following probe to check destinations and UTMs.
