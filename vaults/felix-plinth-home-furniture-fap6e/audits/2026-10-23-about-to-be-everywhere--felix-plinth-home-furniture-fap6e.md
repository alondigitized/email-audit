# ABOUT TO BE EVERYWHERE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened this one because it's CB2, but the subject gave me nothing. "About to be everywhere" could be a lamp, a rug or a collab. It's also the same all-caps tease as "IT'S SO BACK" yesterday. Inside, the email is better than the envelope. The hero is a curvy bouclé lounge chair and a chenille sofa, with a designer story about 70s silhouettes and the line "exclusively available at CB2." That's a real product story. I'm renovating two houses and a deep, soft, brown-toned sofa is exactly what I'd screenshot into my Pinterest folder.
- It still runs long, and the bottom half goes generic. After the Aldine section there's a "Shop New Arrivals" grid, an interior design pitch, then two stacked "More for you" blocks that are basically a catalog dump. By then I'm scrolling on autopilot. Compare Article's "free interior design" email, where the design-services offer was the whole point. Here it's wedged into the middle.
- I'd click the Aldine collection button. I wouldn't forward it, because there's no price or deal I could point a friend to. It stays in the folder as inspiration, not a purchase trigger.

## What's working

- The hero is moody and warm, and "Everything Elevated" over a sculptural sofa tells me the vibe immediately.
- The Aldine chair gets its own spotlight, with "in textured bouclé" under the name. Material callouts are what I actually care about.
- The designer feature adds a reason to care beyond "new thing." CB2's "exclusively available" line is a stronger hook than anything Wayfair has sent this week.
- The "Shop the Aldine collection" button appears twice and is clearly a button. I always know where to tap.
- Free interior design is a CB2 offer I'd use for a second home. It's the same idea Article led with.
- The product grid shows prices in plain view, with no hunting.

## What's weak

- The subject line is pure vibes. "About to be everywhere" with no product or offer is how you get snoozed, especially after "IT'S SO BACK" and "THE WAREHOUSE SALE, RESTOCKED" in the last two days.
- No deal. The only money mention is a small "15% off" for signing up and a rewards-style "10% back" block near the bottom. My highest-rewards-card brain wants the math up front.
- The email is too long. The two "More for you" sections stack nightstands, beds and dining tables with no clear logic. Pottery Barn does the same catalog-dump thing, and it's why I tune out.
- The interior design banner is a thin, low-contrast block. Its "Book your free appointment" CTA gets lost in the middle.
- There's no mention of shipping, lead times or swatches. For a sofa and chair that's a big gap. Nothing says whether Aldine ships soon or in 12 weeks.
- The footer cross-promotes Crate & Barrel and Crate & Barrel Kids. I already get those in separate inboxes, and the same lamp shows up in three brands' emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a concrete reason to act in the subject and hero: the product name plus a benefit such as swatches, ship date or member pricing. The Aldine story is strong enough to carry it.
- 2. Cut the second "More for you" block and make the first one actually personalized. I've browsed furniture, so show me the sofas and chairs I looked at.
- 3. Add a line under the Aldine sofa with fabric swatch availability and estimated delivery. That would turn inspiration into a purchase.
- 4. Move the interior design block up and give the button real contrast.
- 5. Put the loyalty and sign-up perks in one clearly visible strip, not scattered at the bottom.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Designed by David Girelli. Order free swatches today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Exclusive to CB2, in bouclé and chenille.`

# Technical Audit: CB2 "ABOUT TO BE EVERYWHERE"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads about 10 plain-HTTP third-party pixels and has no alt text on its content images. The HTML source was truncated, so findings below are limited to what the QA data and the visible head section show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link verification not possible:** 77 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** 10 Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-sync images load over `http://`:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?...` through `pub10.php`.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - Clients that block insecure content, or proxy images, will drop them. Where they do load, they trigger third-party cookie-sync requests.
- **Other tracking beacons:**
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` (Movable Ink).
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/af08f251-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - Both are served over HTTPS.
- **Repeated dynamic image:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/rp/3e7bfd3585a0a926.png?mi_u=...` appears 6 times with an identical URL. If it is meant to render per-slot personalized content, check that it isn't a fallback or placeholder. I can't confirm either way from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (11):** all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, with filenames starting `e914561c…`, `ab2c66cf…`, `9d7b9da6…`, `8286904c…`, `8b5a3af9…`, `06749d2e…`, `c4205037…`, `f287c9b2…`, `196e8f96…`, `8f6270a0…` and `d208e96e…`. With images off, or in a screen reader, this content is invisible.
- **Missing alt on the 6 `mi.cb2.com/p/rp/3e7bfd3585a0a926.png` images:** these may be content too, so they need real alt text.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the Dotomi, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels have no alt. These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{}` and in `body`. That blocks user text-size adjustment on iOS.
- **Template leftover:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It's harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned up.
- **Broken font fallback:** the `@font-face` for "Lineto Brown Sub" lists `.eot`, `.woff2` and `.woff` in the first rule, and two more rules also use that family name at weights 400 and 600. The `.eot` is listed alone as `src` in the first declaration, which is fragile in modern clients. Fallback stacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present, so impact is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token in output: `%%reviews_skulist%%`**
  - This is the Salesforce Marketing Cloud personalization-string syntax. It was not resolved at send time.
  - Likely cause: a dynamic reviews/SKU-list block failed to populate. Depending on where it sits, it will show as literal text, or break a reviews module or link.
  - I can't determine its location from the truncated HTML. The QA flag alone is enough to treat it as a broken-experience defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **not confirmed as a sender defect**. Check the raw message at the original receiving mailbox.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool again attributes this to the relay. The `mail.cb2.com` and `image.mail.cb2.com` subdomains do suggest an aligned ESP setup, but that is inference, not evidence.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No assessment is possible. All 77 links were skipped by the HTTP probe, and the visible source contains no landing URLs or UTM parameters.
- The only parameters visible are the vendor tracking IDs (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `dtm_*`). These are not campaign UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the dynamic reviews block's data source and fallback. Add a conditional so the module hides itself when the list is empty.
2. **Add alt text** to all 11 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com` and the 6 `mi.cb2.com/p/rp/…png` images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. **Move Dotomi pixels to HTTPS,** or drop them. Confirm the vendor supports `https://ads.dotomi.com` and `https://login.dotomi.com`.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** and change `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to `100%`.
5. **Verify headers at the source.** Inspect the original message, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML** and an unrestricted link probe. That lets you check the unsubscribe link, the physical address, UTM parameters and landing destinations.
7. **Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT…]` template comment.**
