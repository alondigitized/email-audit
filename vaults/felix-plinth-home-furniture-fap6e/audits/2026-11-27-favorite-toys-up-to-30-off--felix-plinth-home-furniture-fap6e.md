## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 57% QA pass rate comes from 3 warnings, none of them hard failures. The main confirmed issues are about 34 images flagged without alt text, a viewport meta that blocks pinch-zoom, and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be checked. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, UTM and merge-token checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 tracking links were not probed** because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels are embedded**: `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. They are third-party tracking beacons with no alt text.
- **Recipient-hash identifiers appear in image URLs**:
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…`
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c54813a5-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`

  These are Movable Ink dynamic-content and open-tracking calls. The same `mi_u` hash appears in the rlcdn `s=` parameter, so one recipient identifier is shared across two domains.
- **No broken links were confirmed.** The QA run had no probe results to confirm them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 34 `<img>` elements have no alt text.** They fall into three groups:
  - **Content images (about 20)**: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`, for example `27e4cabc-…jpg` and `bbf6f9d4-…png`. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the product and promo content is lost.
  - **Animated GIF**: `Video%5FEdit%5FBF3%5FToys%5FFinal.gif` on `s7d5.scene7.com` has no alt text, so no fallback text carries the promo.
  - **Dynamic Movable Ink images**: `f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times with no alt text, so blocked images leave nothing to read.
- **Decorative and tracking images** (`25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, `o.gif`, `448796.gif`, the `dv.` pixel) should carry `alt=""`. Having no `alt` attribute at all is treated differently by screen readers.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **The `<title>` is generic and not escaped.** It reads `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`, which has a bare `&` and doesn't reflect the email.
- **Minor hygiene in the markup**:
  - Duplicate `format-detection` metas.
  - Both `http-equiv` and `charset` content-type declarations.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - `-webkit-text-size-adjust` is declared as both `100%` and `none`.
- `lang="en"` is present, which is good.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved merge tokens or placeholders appeared in the truncated source. The rest of the body wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so I can't confirm the sender omitted them. Check the raw message headers before treating this as a defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sending domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is visible, but alignment can't be verified.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked**, because they fall in the truncated portion of the HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 74 tracking links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters were visible in the truncated HTML. Resolve the redirects to check UTM consistency and landing-page alignment with the "Favorite toys up to 30% off" promo.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images.** This covers the ~20 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/.../m/1/*` images, the scene7 GIF and the 6 Movable Ink `f3c0d22992a01946.png` placeholders. Give the GIF's first frame the key promo message.
2. **Set `alt=""` on tracking pixels and spacers.** This applies to the `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.` and spacer images.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta** so users can zoom.
4. **Change the title** to something like `Favorite toys up to 30% off | Crate & Barrel Kids`, with `&amp;` escaped.
5. **Verify the headers from the raw `.eml` or the original delivery.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` rather than relying on the relayed copy. The same applies to the footer unsubscribe link and address.
6. **Re-run the QA with the full HTML and with tracking redirects followed.** This would cover UTM parameters, destination status codes and merge tokens.
7. **Clean up the duplicate metas and the leftover template comment.**
