- "Up to 60% off" in big white type is readable and leads the email.
- The sectional module has a moody, real-room photo with a sale price shown. That's the one that spoke to my renovation.
- The Reward Dollars card module is useful for me, since I pick my card by rewards math. The "get 10% back" line is the kind of detail I notice.
- The category links (Furniture, Rugs, Tabletop & Bar, Decor) are easy to tap and sit right under the hero.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows was/now pricing on a few items.

## What's weak

- The "up to 20% off sofas" banner and the "up to 60% off clearance" hero compete. I don't know which deal I'm getting.

- The holiday 2026 tabletop module drops in right after a clearance push. The pitch is mixed, and it's early for me to care.
- It's very long, and the legal fine print is tiny and dense. I can't tell which exclusions apply to the 60%. The app and appointment modules at the bottom feel like filler.

- 2. Put a real product or room in the hero, with the discount stated once.
- 3. Cut the email by a third. Remove the app promo and the coffee modules, and add furniture delivery windows and swatch or sample info.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Technical audit of the Crate & Barrel "Up to 60% off NEW markdowns" email. The QA run found no hard failures, but it flagged 3 header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and about 29 images missing alt text. The HTML was truncated and 63 tracking links were not probed, so link, footer and continuity checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 63 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM pass-through are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five identical LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`) carry a hashed recipient identifier in the `s=` parameter. These are extra third-party requests, and several clients will block them.
- **Own-domain tracking:** The email also uses first-party tracking beacons: `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/30cafb78-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are extensionless or GIF pixels with no alt text, which is harmless for pixels.
- **No broken links:** None of the findings show broken links. No link errors were reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 29 images have no `alt` attribute (per QA):
  - 15 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (jpg/png/gif), e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png` and `0350abec-…gif`.
  - 7 images from `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`). The `rp` path suggests these are dynamic product-recommendation images.
  - 7 tracking pixels, where a missing alt is low-impact: the 5 `rlcdn.com` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon.
  
  The content images matter. With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, the offer and product content may be unreadable. Add `alt=""` to pixels and descriptive `alt` to the content images.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` turn off iOS text-size adjustment. `max-height:1000000px` is a deliberate Gmail font-boosting workaround.
- **Markup validity:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It is also a generic title.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting combined and separate forms.
- **Placeholder comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a leftover template comment in production HTML. It is cosmetic.
- **Outlook support:** The VML and `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` namespaces and MSO conditional blocks are present, so Outlook support looks intentional.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** No unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `${…}` appear in the visible truncated source.
- **Per-recipient parameters:** Per-recipient identifiers are present as resolved parameters (`mi_u=dbe8da71…`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`). These look like normal Movable Ink personalization.
- **Not verified:** Personalization in the truncated remainder of the body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe (WARN):** Not found. QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so it is **inconclusive**, not a confirmed violation.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (WARN):** Not found, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unconfirmed. This is also likely a capture limitation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so verify against the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay strips or does not expose the header.
- **Footer not inspected:** The in-body unsubscribe link, physical postal address and sender identification could not be checked because the HTML was truncated.
- **From domain:** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host subdomain (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`), which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot assess:** The 63 tracking links were not probed, and the visible truncated source shows no final destination URLs or UTM parameters.
- **Not confirmed:** Whether links land on the markdown/sale pages promised in the subject ("NEW markdowns", "up to 60% off") is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 15 `image.mail…` content images and the 7 `mi…/p/rp/` images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against raw headers, since the relay may be hiding them. Do the same for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolve the redirects, to check the footer unsubscribe link and address and to capture the UTM parameters and landing pages.
5. In the QA tooling, downgrade or tag header warnings caused by relay stripping so they are not read as sender defects. Also count only content images, not pixels, in the alt-text check.
6. Minor cleanup: escape the `&` in `<title>` to `&amp;`, make the title descriptive, deduplicate `format-detection`, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
