## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and this is the third blank hero I've gotten in three days. The "CEILING FANS" subject promised a fan story. What I got was a giant white rectangle with one tiny broken-image icon in it. The patio benches send (10/06) and the flooring/hardware send (10/07) did the same thing. By now I expect the hero to be missing.
- Below the hole, a ceiling fan does show up. It's the first tile in the "inspo for your space" grid, a black-bladed fan with a light kit, but nothing labels it as the subject. It's sitting next to dining chairs, a coat stand, a coffee table and a sideboard. After that it's the usual Wayfair pile: pergolas, hot tubs, kitchen sinks, gas grills, trash cans, wall décor. I'm renovating two houses, so I could use fans, lighting and hardware. The email never says "fans" again.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim it, notice the fan tile is unlabeled and unpriced, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I'm on Pinterest and want a fan, I'll search Wayfair directly.

## What's working

- The first product tile is a real ceiling fan with a light kit, so the subject is at least partly honored.
- The "up to 60% OFF" band with the Delta presenter tag is the one clear offer, and it's easy to find near the bottom.
- The financing block (up to 60 months, Wayfair card) is relevant to me. I'm funding two renovations and care about the card math.
- The star ratings under the "inspo" tiles give me some social proof.
- The purple headers, "inspo for your space" and "score styles you," break the long scroll into sections.

## What's weak

- The hero is a blank white box with a broken-image icon. It's the biggest element, and it's empty.
- The subject says "our top picks" for ceiling fans, but I count one fan. The rest is patio and kitchen stuff.
- The fan tile has no price, no name and no sale tag. The discount only appears in the banner near the bottom.
- The product grid is a catalog dump. Tiles like "Hot Tubs Under $700" and "Coffee Makers, Grinders & More" have nothing to do with my renovations.
- The fine print under the footer is a wall of tiny text about APR and financing terms, and I can't read it without zooming.
- I got three near-identical Wayfair sends in 48 hours, which feels like spam.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. A blank hero ruins a send, and this is the third in a row. Put a real fan image there with alt text and a background color. The page can't look empty when images fail.
- 2. Make the email actually about ceiling fans. Show 6 fans in a grid with a name, price and sale tag on each. Add a size or room filter (e.g. "for 12x12 rooms"), since fan sizing matters and I'd care about it.
- 3. Cut the unrelated categories (hot tubs, trash cans, coffee makers). Crate & Barrel's wallcovering send worked because it had one clear story. Wayfair should stay on theme.
- 4. Put the offer next to the products instead of in a banner at the bottom. Say what's on sale and by how much.
- 5. Add shipping and delivery information for bulky items, such as "Ships in 3 days," since that's a real concern for me.
- 6. Cap the cadence, with no more than one Wayfair send per day.

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "CEILING FANS — our top picks."

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source was truncated, so findings come from the QA output and the visible URLs. The main problems are about 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels with no alt text, many linked images with no accessible name, a stale or test-named asset in production, and a ceiling-fan header image that links to the homepage. The authentication and unsubscribe-header warnings can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** Eight probed links returned 429 (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157…`, `/daily-sales`, `/`, and the `L230-K~W009861949` product page). That is rate limiting from the probe, not a dead destination. Four product URLs were not probed because of the 70s time budget (`L168-K~W111651355` ×2, `Yamazaki-Home-Plain-Freestanding-Coat-Hanger-Rack…` ×2). Only 12 of 75 links were probed.
- **Non-HTTPS impression pixels.** 20 `<img>` tags use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` (`sz=2x1`). All are flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Clients that block mixed content or proxy images may drop them, which undercounts impressions.
- **Missing alt on the open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` has no alt text.
- **Non-HTTPS app fallback.** The OneLink URL's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses `http://`, so desktop fallback goes through a redirect.
- **Inconsistent campaign labelling.** The OneLink URL has `c=Triggered` but also `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is labelled as triggered in the attribution campaign field, which will skew AppsFlyer reporting.
- **Stale or test asset names.** The image `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty…` carries a Labor Day (0824) name in an October send. Its filename also contains an unencoded apostrophe (`De4'als`) in the `src` path. `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` is a test-named asset in production.
- **Per-recipient `_eml` tokens.** These are unique per link, so link-level dedup and caching are not possible. This is expected for tracking and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt:** 10 content images in the QA list: `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-…`, `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, `WFCC1%402x.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, `5daysofdeals_fall2026_skinnyhero-vp.png`, and `image%20%283%29.png`. - **Linked images with no accessible name.** The probe recorded link text as `[image: stars_10.png]`, `[image: email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg]`, and the Labor Day filename. The `stars_10.png` link points to the same product URL as the "Kemp 52" W LED Standard Ceiling Fan" text link, so it adds a redundant, unnamed tab stop.
- No viewport or media-query problems are visible in the truncated source. The `@media (max-width: 480px)` block and the `u ~ div` Gmail fix look intact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible URLs contain no unresolved tokens (no `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]`). `_emr=f7d96494-…` is a populated recipient ID and is consistent across links. This is limited to the visible portion of the source.

2. Add descriptive alt text to the 10 content images. Give the star images alt text like "4.5 out of 5 stars", or put the rating in live text.
3. Remove duplicate linked star images, or give them `alt=""` with the link name carried by the adjacent text link.

5. Rename the test and stale assets (`email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-…`). Remove the apostrophe from the filename or percent-encode it.
6. Change the OneLink `c=Triggered` to a batch campaign value, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
7. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Re-run link probing with throttling or retries to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed product links.
