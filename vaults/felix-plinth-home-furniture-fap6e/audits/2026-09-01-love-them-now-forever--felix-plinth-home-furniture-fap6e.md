# Love them now & forever ♥️
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Crate & Kids, not straight Crate & Barrel — but let's be real, it's the same family that's already sent me nine emails this week, now just wearing a nursery-colored hat. My wife's not pregnant and we don't have toddlers, so "nap-ready cribs" and "toddler-to-teen beds" are an instant scroll-past for me. This one's actually well-built as an email — clean hierarchy, one clear "Double Rewards" offer up top — but it's aimed at a completely different shopper than me.
- I'm not the audience here, full stop. If I had a kid this age it'd probably be a decent, well-organized send; as the guy renovating two houses with zero kids in the picture, this is an instant archive, not even a real read.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — unless I've got a friend with a newborn, in which case maybe. This is the first Crate-family email this week that's actually mis-segmented rather than just repetitive, which is almost worse.

## What's working

- The "Double Rewards" banner up top is a real, specific offer — 20% back in rewards through 9/8, no vague language.
- Layout is clean and scannable — each category (cribs, beds, dressers, bookshelves, rugs) gets its own hero shot and CTA, no visual clutter.
- The "up to 60% off" grid partway down actually stacks specific category discounts (30% off cribs, 40% off rugs, etc.) instead of one vague blanket number.
- Kid-room styling looks legit — soft palettes, actual nursery/kid furniture, not just adult stuff repainted pastel.

## What's weak

- Wrong persona entirely — I'm renovating two adult homes, not a nursery. Nothing here maps to my actual project.
- This is at least the ninth or tenth Crate-family send clogging my inbox this week (following "wine glasses," "bestsellers," Apartment Collection, dining furniture) — my folder-dragging fatigue is now cross-brand, not just cross-email.
- The bottom "we love these for you" grid is trying to recommend based on browsing, but it's still kid-furniture-only — no bridge back to anything I'd actually shop.
- App download pitch at the very bottom feels tacked on and generic, no reason for me specifically to install it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment this out of my inbox entirely.** If Crate & Barrel knows I've never clicked a kids' product, Crate & Kids shouldn't be firing at the same cadence as the parent brand — Article at least seems to only send me stuff in my actual size/style lane lately.
- 2. **Cut send frequency across the whole Crate family.** Ten emails a week from one company (even across sub-brands) is CB2-credit-card levels of overkill — I flagged the same thing with the CB2 card email.
- 3. Lead with the Double Rewards banner as its own send to loyalty members, not bundled under a nursery-furniture wall of scroll.
- 4. If they insist on cross-selling non-parents, at least segment toward "gifting" — grandparent/aunt/uncle framing — rather than assuming everyone opening this has a baby.
