# NEW Labor Day savings ☀️ UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Same burgundy "Up to 70% off" banner I've now seen five times this week, but this one at least breaks the pattern by the second scroll — the category breakdown (Bedding, Sofas & Chairs, Furniture with actual percentages) is more useful than the last few sends, and the "Recommended Just for You" module with the Cayman Platform Bed and Reeva rug is the kind of thing that could actually get me to click since we're mid-renovation on both houses right now.
- But it's still buried under the same structure as every other Pottery Barn email this month — hero banner, then a scroll-fest of category tiles, then the personalized stuff way down where most people have already bailed. I'm not forwarding this to anyone. I'll skim the "Recommended" row on my phone, maybe tap the platform bed since we need one for the second house, then archive.

## What's working

- The category-specific percentages (70% bedding, 30% sofas & chairs, 60% furniture) are more concrete than the generic "Up to 70% off" blast I got yesterday and the day before.
- "Recommended Just for You" actually shows furniture relevant to a renovation — curtain rods, a platform bed, a rug — not just random home decor filler.
- Ships Free badges on the quilt and curtains are a real decision-driver for me since furniture shipping costs are the thing that kills a cart.
- The Affirm "Buy Now, Pay Over Time" module is smart placement for someone financing two renovations at once.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth near-identical burgundy "Up to 70% off" hero from Pottery Barn this week — after 2026-09-02's bedding email and 2026-09-01's "End-of-summer savings" send, my brain has fully pattern-matched this banner and stopped reading it.
- No loyalty/rewards math visible until literally the last screen — I have a rewards card and would want that "10% back" number near the offer, not after the Halloween shop and New Arrivals modules.
- The Halloween Shop and "Discover New Arrivals" inserts in the middle break the Labor Day sale narrative — feels like three emails stapled together.
- Zero mention of swatches, samples, or fabric/finish options anywhere, which is the one thing that actually gets me from "browsing" to "buying" on upholstered furniture.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move "Recommended Just for You" and the rewards card math up near the hero — that's the only section with real relevance signal, and it's currently the 8th scroll.
- 2. Cut the Halloween Shop and New Arrivals blocks out of a Labor Day sale email entirely — they dilute the offer and this isn't the first time Pottery Barn has crammed unrelated campaigns into one send.
- 3. Add a swatch/sample CTA on at least one furniture tile — Article and CB2 both lean on "see it in your space" messaging better than PB does right now.
- 4. Vary the hero creative — even a different crop or color would help after five straight burgundy banners this week.
