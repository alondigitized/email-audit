# Find it here: up to 60% off clearance!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel Kids, again. This is the fifth one of these I've had to sort through this week and I still don't have kids, so the second I see "clearance" and "best baby essentials" in the hero I'm gone — this whole email doesn't exist for me. What's mildly interesting further down is the Venus Williams tennis-champ kids collab (twice, same creative, stacked back to back like they forgot they already sent it) and the Nuna stroller/car-seat travel system, but none of that saves an email whose entire top third is baby gear.
- The autumn leaves hero is genuinely a nice visual — moodier and more premium than the flat product-grid stuff I usually get from this house — but it's decorating an offer I have zero use for. I'm not opening this on my phone twice; first glance is enough to know it's a mark-as-read.
- Compared to Pottery Barn's "Our Most-Loved Towels" send I actually engaged with this week — a boring, real line item I need for the renovation — this is the opposite: a well-produced email for a life stage I'm not in. CB2's "Restock alert" at least sorted by furniture category so I could scan and bail on the ones I didn't need; this one just leads with baby before anything for me.

## What's working

- The fall-leaves hero photography is a step up in production value from the usual C&B product-tile grind.
- "up to 60% off clearance" is a clear, specific number right in the hero, not buried.
- The category nav row (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor, Final Sale) is a legit way to self-select out fast, which I appreciate even as a non-target.
- Nuna travel system block has real pricing and struck-through comparison pricing ($1450 → $1085), which is honest and concrete.

## What's weak

- Baby essentials and clearance up top — I have no kids, this is dead on arrival for me the same way it's been every time this week.
- The Venus Williams tennis collab literally repeats twice in a row, same image, same copy, same CTA — looks like a template error, not intentional.
- Nothing here speaks to furniture renovation, swatches, fabric samples, or showroom/Design Desk content until way down at the bottom, and even then it's a generic "book a free appointment" tile with no relevance hook.
- Zero adult-furniture presence until the "we love these for you" grid, and even that's still nursery gliders, cribs, and dressers — not living room or dining room, which is what I'm actually shopping for right now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if I've never clicked a kids/baby CTA across four-plus sends, stop leading with baby essentials in my hero slot; swap in adult furniture clearance instead.
- 2. Delete the duplicate Venus Williams block — one is a bug, not a feature.
- 3. Move the "we love these for you" personalization block up, and make sure it's actually personalized to browsing history, not another baby/nursery dump.
- 4. Pull swatch/sample-program and showroom content out of the footer and make it a real mid-email module — that's the one thing that would make me stop and look, and no competitor in my inbox this week (Article, CB2, Pottery Barn) has nailed that either.
