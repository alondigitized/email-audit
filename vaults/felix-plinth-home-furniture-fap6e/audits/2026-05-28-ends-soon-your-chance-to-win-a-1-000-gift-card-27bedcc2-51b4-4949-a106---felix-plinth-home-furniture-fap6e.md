# ENDS SOON! Your chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Ten. That's the Crate & Barrel family count in my inbox now, and this isn't just another one — it's a *duplicate*. Same sender, same subject line, same day. I already reviewed "ENDS SOON! Your chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card" earlier this morning and gave it a 7. Now the exact same email showed up again, presumably from a list misfire or a broken send scheduler. Whatever the cause, the only thing "ENDS SOON" communicated to me just now is that someone's ESP is misfiring.
- Setting aside the duplicate problem: this is a children's email. The hero is a kids' bedroom. The second section is "finishing touches for their dream room" with a nursery scene. The "we love these for you" product grid at the bottom is serving cribs and toddler furniture. I'm 36, renovating two adult homes, and I have browsed exactly zero cribs on crateandbarrel.com. Wayfair at least uses my actual browse history to populate "recommended" grids — their "American Summer Shop" email this week pushed outdoor furniture and rugs, categories I actually shop. This is the wrong catalog delivered to the wrong address.
- I'm not clicking anything. I'm not forwarding. If there's a third one today I'm unsubscribing from Crate & Kids specifically and staying on C&B proper.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly — the bedroom photography is attractive and the gift card callout lands without visual clutter
- "Gear up for summer" section at least reflects the season, even if the products are children's outdoor gear
- App download prompt mid-email is logically tied to the sweepstakes mechanic ("your gift card entries") — that's a sensible hook
- No layout breaks or broken images — clean execution at a technical level, which is more than I can say for some Pottery Barn sends this month

## What's weak

- Exact duplicate of a send I already received today — kills all urgency credibility in "ENDS SOON" instantly
- Children's and nursery content throughout; I have no kids and have never browsed that category — the "we love these for you" grid should know this
- Sweepstakes is not a real offer — I cannot route a chance-to-win into any line item in a renovation budget; Article and CB2 both ran concrete dollar-off offers this same week
- "ENDS SOON" with no visible deadline date in the email body is a half-measure; Wayfair consistently puts a specific cutoff date in the hero when they run urgency plays
- Five competing asks in one email: enter sweepstakes, shop nursery, shop summer, get the card, download the app — none of them win

## Recommendations

- 1. **Deduplicate before send** — a single suppression check on "sent within 24 hours" would have caught this; there's no campaign goal served by landing in my inbox twice in one day
- 2. **Segment off children's content** — CB2 uses purchase-category signals to gate who sees what; Crate & Kids should suppress the nursery track for accounts with zero children's category history
- 3. **Replace sweepstakes with a real offer** — even a modest "20% off your next order" outperforms a 1-in-N chance at a gift card for someone actively spending on renovation
- 4. **Put the deadline in the email** — "ENDS SOON" without a date is a trust deduction; pick a day and say it out loud in the hero
- 5. **Consolidate to one CTA** — sweepstakes entry should own the email; the card and app sections should be footer-level, not mid-email sections competing for attention
- **Subject Alt A:** `Win $1,000 toward your next big room — entry closes [date]`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $1,000 gift card entry is waiting (closes Friday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One entry, real furniture money. Deadline: [date].`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The upgrade your rooms have been waiting for — enter now.`
