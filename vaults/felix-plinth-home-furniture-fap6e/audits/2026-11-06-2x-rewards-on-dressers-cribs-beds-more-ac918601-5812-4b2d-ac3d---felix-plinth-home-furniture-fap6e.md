# 2x Rewards on dressers, cribs, beds & more!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Crate & Barrel Kids send I scored on 11/06, so I'm judging it again on the layout. The "Last Double Rewards this year!" block is the clearest thing in it. I'm a rewards-card guy, so a hard "last chance" on 2x earnings is a real reason to move a purchase. The dresser, the walnut bed and the oak pieces work for me. I'm furnishing two houses, so a "kids" bedroom piece still lands as an adult one.
- The cribs and the nursery glider are noise, same as last time. The email also stacks too many pitches under the offer. It has the fall sales strip twice, a holiday gifts banner, a Design Desk sweepstakes, an app download pitch and a credit card pitch. That's the sixth or seventh C&B-family send in two days, so my brain files it as "the same email again." I'd click the dresser and the bed. I wouldn't forward it, because nobody in my life is shopping for a crib.

## What's working

- "Last Double Rewards this year!" sits right under the hero, with Shop Now and Learn More buttons. That's the one offer I'd screenshot.
- Each product block has its own "Earn 2x Reward Dollars" tag, a named item (the Thurloe oak dresser, the Jenny Lind spindle bed) and a category button like "Shop All Dressers" or "Shop All Beds." That's easy to scan.
- The photography is warm and styled, in oak, walnut and cream. It fits my renovation mood-board habit, and I'd save this one to my Pinterest folder.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices, so I can sanity-check the rewards math without clicking.
- The Design Desk appointment and the store-finder strip are a nod to showroom integration, which I care about.

## What's weak

- Half the hero blocks are nursery (crib, glider recliner). Cribs and gliders aren't for me, and they push the beds and dressers further down.
- The fall sales strip appears twice, and a holiday gifts banner follows. They compete with the rewards message. Pottery Barn does the same thing when its Fall Sale strip rides on every send.
- The holiday gifts banner and the Design Desk sweepstakes image have text sitting over busy photos. It's low-contrast and hard to read on my phone.
- The credit card, app and sweepstakes blocks at the bottom make this feel like a utility closet. The footer is a dense wall of legal text, so the rewards terms are buried.
- Nothing here shows swatches, finish samples or delivery timing for furniture. Article's UGC bedroom send gave me more of that "will this match my room" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job. Keep the rewards hero and the four product blocks, and move the card, app and sweepstakes pitches to a separate send.
- 2. Segment the product blocks by browsing history. A renovator who has looked at beds and dressers shouldn't get the crib and glider blocks.
- 3. Show one dated rewards line near the top, for example "Earn 2x through Nov. 17," and put the terms in a visible line instead of the footer.
- 4. Drop the duplicate fall sales strip.
- 5. Add a delivery estimate or a "swatch / sample" link to each furniture block.
- 6. Fix the contrast on the text-over-photo banners.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Double Rewards of the year: beds & dressers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn 2x on bedroom furniture before it ends`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walnut, oak and spindle beds all earn double Reward Dollars. Ends Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at 2x on dressers and beds this year.`
