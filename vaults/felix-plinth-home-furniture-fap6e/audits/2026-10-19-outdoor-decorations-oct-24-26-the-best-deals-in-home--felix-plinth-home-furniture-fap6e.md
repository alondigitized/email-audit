- The Affirm "as low as 0% APR" strip is useful for the big-ticket furniture I'm buying.
- The Wayfair Rewards and Wayfair Professional icons at the bottom are a nice nod to repeat buyers like me.

## What's weak

- The subject says outdoor decorations, but the first tile is a Halloween spider and the real outdoor items sit near the bottom.
- The top product tiles have no labels or links I can read. The mattress protector, the tray and the sofa don't say what they are.
- The 80% off headline is for an event days away. I can't tell if anything is discounted today.
- It's another near-identical grid after the 10/19 glassware, 10/18 tile and 10/17 ice maker sends. The cadence is exhausting.
- Pergolas appear twice in the lower grid ("On sale now" and "Pergolas for less"), which feels like filler.
- The footer is dense, with a wall of Affirm fine print. The tiny type isn't readable on a phone without zooming.

- 2. Label every top tile with a product name and a price or % off, as the lower grid does.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The Wayfair email is built on a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with consistent tracking parameters. QA found no hard failures, but it flagged ~20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, missing alt text on 25 or more images, and compliance headers that couldn't be verified. The 57% pass rate comes mostly from unprobed or rate-limited links and warnings, not confirmed defects.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Probing was incomplete.** 12 links were probed and 59 were skipped, and probing stopped after 70s. Four links were never checked, so they are neither passes nor failures:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/must-have-lighted-makeup-vanities-se…`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-bar-stools-se73979.html`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/black-filing-cabinets-se70982.html?e…`
- **429 responses are probable bot throttling, not broken links.** 8 probed links returned 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=…`, `/curated/24-hour-deals~ev641886.html`, `/affirm`, and two `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links. They are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Two "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links differ only in `_eml`.** That is per-link tracking, which is expected.
- **`https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` is used for several images and the "Shop Sale" CTA.** Each has a unique `_eml` value, so click attribution is possible per placement.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** About 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=…` pixels use plain HTTP. Some clients may block or rewrite them, so impression counts could be undercounted. The pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (open tracking) is HTTPS.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text on content images (6):**
  - `US-WD26-Preview-Email.gif`
  - `WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-DotD.jp…`
  - `WFCA_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-Affirm…`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png`
  - `WFCA_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-GiftCa…`
  - `b.php` (the open-tracking pixel)
- **Why this matters.** The first four of these are linked images, so the link text in QA is the image filename (for example "[image: US-WD26-Preview-Email.gif]"). With images blocked, screen readers and clients will expose a filename or nothing. These are also the primary clickable promos.
- **Missing alt on all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels.** This is low impact for tracking pixels, but `alt=""` would be correct markup.
- **Web font.** `@font-face "Sofia"` loads a `.woff` from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`, with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` as an Outlook fallback. The HTML doesn't show a fallback stack, because the source was truncated.
- **Mobile CSS.** There is a `max-width: 480px` media query set. One rule, `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; }`, can break words mid-character on product names. That is a rendering risk.
- **Truncated source.** I can't confirm the doctype, `lang` attribute, or table `role="presentation"` usage beyond the `<html>` tag, so I'm not flagging them.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, and `skurectxid` values are populated rather than raw placeholders.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe not found** (`[WARN]`). The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (`[WARN]`). The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders at Wayfair's volume, so the header should be checked against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `members.wayfair.com` can't be assessed from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result for it appears in the output. The footer content was truncated, so I can't confirm the postal address or the unsubscribe URL.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **No standard UTM parameters.** Links use Wayfair's own scheme: `_emr`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=15`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_142080`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16560`. This is internally consistent across all links, but `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` are absent. Third-party analytics such as GA will not attribute by default unless Wayfair maps these server-side. This is an observation, not a defect.
- **App links.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me` carries `pid=Email`, `c=Triggered`, `af_sub5=AppEmail`, and `af_dp=wayfairapp://home`, with a web fallback `af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. The campaign is labeled `c=Triggered`, though this is a batch send (`source=batch`), so campaign attribution in AppsFlyer may be mislabeled. The fallback is also plain `http://`.
- **Landing page alignment.** I couldn't verify it, since the pages returned 429. The subject says "OUTDOOR DECORATIONS," while the unprobed links point to filter pages for pergolas, vanities, bar stools and filing cabinets. Whether the landing pages match the subject can't be confirmed from the data.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-probe the 4 unchecked and 8 rate-limited links with a slower rate or browser-like headers. Confirm the `/daily-sales/filters/*` URLs resolve (200, not 404 or an expired sale).
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 linked promo images. Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
3. Switch the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `af_web_dp=http://…` fallback to HTTPS.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message headers. If the relay stripped them, check an unrelayed copy.
5. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` from the raw headers.
6. Review `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name`. `overflow-wrap: anywhere` or `break-word` is less destructive.
7. Check whether `c=Triggered` on the onelink URL should be a batch/campaign value.
