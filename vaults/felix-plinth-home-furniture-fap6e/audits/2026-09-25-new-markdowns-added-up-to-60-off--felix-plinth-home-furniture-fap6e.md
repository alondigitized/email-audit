# New markdowns added! Up to 60% off →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, up to 60% off clearance again — this is the same headline number C&B hit me with two days ago in "New markdowns on furniture, decor & more just landed" (6.00/10), so my thumb didn't stop this time, it just kept scrolling because I've already seen this exact camo-green moody hero move. The "we love these for you" grid is actually decent — a driftwood dresser and a couple of sofas that could genuinely work for the second house — but nothing here says "Felix, we know you're mid-reno," it's just generic best-sellers.
- Then it veers into "so scary, it's chic" Halloween decor and pumpkins, which is a hard tonal left turn from a clearance email, and by the time I hit the Crate Card / TheDesignDesk / store locator stack at the bottom I'm just scrolling to get to unsubscribe-adjacent territory. Not clicking, not forwarding — this is a mark-as-read, and honestly it's starting to feel like C&B is emailing me every single day this week.

## What's working

- The 60% off clearance number is still the one thing that'll stop me — CB2's "Upholstery Event" (7/10) used the same trick with a specific 25% and a strong hero, and it worked there too.
- "we love these for you" personalization framing is a nice touch even if the products themselves feel generic-catalog rather than actually personalized.
- Product grid has real prices visible ($2599, $2249, etc.) — no hidden-cost friction, which Pottery Barn's Design Services email also got right.
- Clean single-column mobile layout, nothing overlapping or broken.

## What's weak

- I already got the near-identical "up to 60% off" clearance beat from C&B two days ago — this is starting to feel like the Crate&Kids "FINAL WEEKEND" emails that also showed up twice in my inbox this week.
- The Halloween decor section completely breaks the clearance narrative — it's a different email stapled onto this one.
- Nothing here is furniture-reno specific — no swatch/sample callout, no "still renovating? here's what pairs with your last order" — just a generic best-seller grid.
- The bottom third (app download, Crate Card, TheDesignDesk, store locator, social, footer nav) is a wall of unrelated CTAs competing with the actual offer.
- "Select styles. While quantities last." under the hero is the kind of vague exclusion language that makes me trust the 60% less.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails — clearance and Halloween don't belong together, and diluting the 60% off with pumpkins kills momentum.
- 2. Cut the CTA pile-up at the bottom to one — either the app push or the Crate Card, not both plus TheDesignDesk plus store locator.
- 3. Make "we love these for you" actually personalized — pull from my browsing/cart history (I've been circling dining tables and sectionals) instead of generic top-sellers.
- 4. Add a specific expiration date/time to "while quantities last" — CB2's sale copy felt more honest because it didn't hide behind vague scarcity language.
