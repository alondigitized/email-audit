# NEW indigo blue details for every room
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-eight. I checked the count. This is the twenty-eighth Pottery Barn email and we're seventeen days into July, and I'll say this: a color story email is actually a smart reset after back-to-back sale sends. The INDIGO hero is genuinely beautiful — rich ceramics, layered textiles, deep blues — and it's the kind of moodboard image I screenshot and drop into my notes app to reference when I'm at the showroom. For someone coordinating two interiors simultaneously right now, knowing that Pottery Barn has a dedicated indigo moment in stock is useful context.
- Here's the problem: the Autumn Sale banner is sitting right at the top shouting 30–60% off, and then the email pivots immediately into a full editorial mood piece with no price anchor. CB2 sent "New chairs. Instant character." last week — four words, specific product category, clear buy signal. Article ran an entryway editorial the week before that with no discount and the clarity of that choice read clean. PB wants to straddle both lanes simultaneously and I'm left wondering whether I should be inspired or purchasing.
- I'd open the lookbook. I wouldn't transact tonight. I'd save this one to my folder alongside the rugs guide from the 16th.

## What's working

- The INDIGO hero is genuinely editorial — that ceramics-and-textiles collage is exactly the palette calibration image I keep in a running folder for the second house
- "EXPLORE THE LOOKBOOK" alongside "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" is smart dual-CTA structuring — browse path for me, buy path for someone further down the funnel
- "Recommended Just For You" is surfacing sofas and dining chairs, which I'm actively pricing right now — that section is doing real work
- 40% Back in Rewards is prominently placed; I run everything through rewards math before I buy and seeing it right there matters
- The Free Design Services block is genuinely useful for where I am — I could book that call for the primary right now

## What's weak

- "AUTUMN SALE SAVE 30-60%" and an editorial indigo mood piece coexist without connecting — there's no price on a single blue item in the hero section
- "for every room" is the emptiest possible claim — I have a specific dining room, a specific living room, and specific problems; this helps none of them
- It's July 17 and the word "AUTUMN" is already in the banner — PB always runs the calendar early but it trains me to expect permanent sale conditions
- The product grid below the hero is a dense thumbnail row at a scale where I genuinely can't read product names without zooming; compare that to how Article presents individual pieces with room to breathe
- Twenty-eight emails and the subject still doesn't know I have two projects going simultaneously, while the Recommended section clearly does — that personalization gap is jarring

## Recommendations

- 1. Anchor one indigo hero product to a real price — "The Indigo Table Lamp: $89, 30% off today" beats a color mood board with a floating sale banner
- 2. Drop "for every room" — replace with the actual room the recommended section knows I need (dining room, living room) — the data is there, use it
- 3. Collapse the product grid to four items max, larger images, one price each — the Wayfair grid approach of cramming in twelve SKUs at thumbnail size is a mistake here
- 4. Move "Autumn Sale" language out of the hero banner and into a single sentence at the top of the product section — keep the editorial story clean
- 5. Promote the Design Services block above the product grid for subscribers who've been on the list long enough to see twenty-eight emails

This is a template rendering bug. The directives do nothing in a `<style>` block but expose internal implementation detail.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — not found**

The AgentMail relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Without it, Gmail and Yahoo (per 2024 bulk sender requirements) cannot surface the one-click unsubscribe UI. If the header is present upstream but stripped by the relay, that needs relay configuration review; if it is genuinely absent, this is a deliverability and compliance risk.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header — not found**

RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is absent or not captured. Required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders since February 2024.

**Authentication-Results — not found**

SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. The relay did not surface an `Authentication-Results` header. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain); alignment should be verified directly against DMARC records for `potterybarn.com`.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**

Not verifiable from truncated source — assumed present in footer (standard for Pottery Barn sends). Confirm unsubscribe link is in the rendered footer and resolves.
