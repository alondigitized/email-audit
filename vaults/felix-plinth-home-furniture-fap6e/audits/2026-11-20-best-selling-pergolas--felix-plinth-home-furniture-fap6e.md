# Best-selling pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again. This is the seventeenth-ish send since 11/09, and it's the same day as "Baking sheets up to 80% off" and "Black Friday is here." The subject says "Best-selling pergolas," and the first tile is a gray pergola. It's one of twenty-odd tiles in a grid, though, so the pergola is a headline for what's basically a catalog dump. I scrolled a long way and found bedding, a Christmas tree, wall art, kitchen mats and deck boxes. I counted three pergola or gazebo tiles in total.
- The purple "Up to 80% off Black Friday" banner at the top is the real offer. It's a thin strip with a Sealy logo and a small orange arrow, so it reads as an ad slot and not a hero. There's no price on any tile. Every product is just a label and a "»" link.
- I wouldn't click. I'm renovating two houses, and bar stools and curtains are things I actually need. But with no prices and no way to tell which of the three pergolas is the best-seller, I'd just open Wayfair in a tab and search. Not forwarding. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The purple "80% OFF BLACK FRIDAY" banner at the top is clear, on-brand and seasonal.
- The "score styles you ❤️" and "savings too good to pass up" headers split the grid so it's easier to scan.
- Categories like bar stools, curtains and accent pillows are relevant to a two-house renovation.
- The footer rewards row (Wayfair Rewards, Professional, Exclusive Brands) is a useful reminder, since I care about rewards math.
- The Affirm, Afterpay and Klarna strip is a legitimate nod to big-ticket furniture buyers.

## What's weak

- No tile shows a price, a percent off or a "from $X." "Pergolas for less" and "Curtains from $9.99" are the only numbers in the body, and that's weak for 80% off.
- Pergolas appear three times ("Best-selling pergolas," "Pergolas and gazebos from $50," "Pergolas for less"). The subject promised one thing and the body gives me three overlapping versions of it.
- The 80% banner is small and has the Sealy logo crammed into it. I can't tell whether Sealy is the sale or just an ad.
- There's no hero and no single CTA, just about twenty text links with tiny "»" arrows that are awkward to tap on a phone.
- The legal block is huge and runs to a third of the scroll. Nothing here gives me shipping clarity for furniture, swatches or samples.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Show the best-selling pergola with a real price, a was/now and a "Shop pergolas" button. Then trim the grid to six tiles, not twenty.
- 2. Put prices or "up to X% off" on every tile. Pottery Barn's "up to 70% off" banner at least tells me the depth.
- 3. Cut the duplicate pergola tiles. Crate & Barrel's sofas-and-dining email was far better targeted, and it was what I wanted that day. CB2's "Free shipping, orders of $99 or more" is a single clear hook, and Wayfair has no equivalent.
- 4. Add furniture-shipping and delivery-date info, plus a swatch or return note, near the big-ticket items.
- 5. Make the Rewards and financing blocks specific, for example "earn X back" or "as low as $X/mo."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links are well-formed and consistently parameterized. All 8 probed material links that returned a status code got HTTP 429, so none could be verified. The main confirmed defects are missing alt text on 27 images and 20 `http://` impression pixels. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture in the AgentMail relay and are unverified, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429 (e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-pergolas--se82159.html?eid=603454…`). 429 is rate limiting on the QA prober's side, so these are unverified, not failed. 47 links were skipped and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget, so link coverage is partial.
- **Insecure fallback in the app link.** The Onelink URL (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…`) carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app land on an `http://` URL and depend on a server redirect to HTTPS.
- **Campaign label mismatch.** The same Onelink URL has `c=Triggered`, while every other link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is tagged as a triggered campaign in AppsFlyer, which will skew attribution.
- **Likely QA output truncation, not email defects.** The `…se81`, `…%2430-se10022.htm` and `…?_emr=…&` URLs are cut off in the QA output. The HTML source is also truncated, so I can't confirm they are broken in the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **27 images are missing alt text.** They fall into three groups:
  - **Content images with no alt, 6 total.** `1113-US-BF-Email-General.gif`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_2_purple.png`, `MultiPartnerBAU-WFUS_Email_Skinny-LivingRoom.png`, `blankBanner%20%281%29.png` and `image%20%283%29.png`. The probe lists the first and the top header as link text (`[image: 1113-US-BF-Email-General.gif]`, `[image: wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purpl]`), so those links have no accessible name. Wherever images are blocked, the email renders blank for those links.
  - **Open pixel, 1.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?...` should have `alt=""`.
  - **Impression pixels, 20.** The `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` images should have `alt=""`.
- **20 impression pixels use `http://`.** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` are non-HTTPS and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients, so impressions may go unrecorded. The pixels are 2x1, so this is a measurement issue only.
- **`blankBanner%20%281%29.png` and `image%20%283%29.png`** have default-export filenames, which suggests leftover CMS assets. Check whether they are meant to be in the send.
- No other rendering issues are visible in the source I received. The responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block is present, and the `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` client hacks are in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible, and the HTML source is truncated, so this is not a full check. The per-recipient `_emr=0962113b-ba41-49d6-9388-a97287788b4d` appears in every link, and `_eml` is a unique link-level ID. That matches the personalization pattern, and the pixel `p=` value uses the same ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. I can't confirm they are absent at the sender, so this is unverified, not a confirmed failure.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address.** The QA data says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the output, and the footer is outside the truncated HTML. I can't confirm either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_121286`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16799`, `mdlid`, `sltid` and `brcid`. It is consistent across links, but it won't feed a standard GA UTM report without mapping.
- **The subject lines up with the landing page.** "Best-selling pergolas" links to `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-pergolas--se82159.html` with `eid=603454&unifiedeventid=603454`, which match each other. The page content itself couldn't be checked because of the 429.
- **Header and banner images route generically.** They go to `https://www.wayfair.com/` and `/daily-sales`, not to a specific event, which is expected for the shared header modules.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the 6 content images, especially the linked `1113-US-BF-Email-General.gif` and the top header, so those links have an accessible name. Set `alt=""` on the open pixel and the 20 impression pixels.
2. Move the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to `https://`, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
3. Correct `c=Triggered` on the Onelink URL to match the batch send, or confirm it is intentional.
4. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff, plus a longer budget, to verify the 8 links that returned 429 and the 4 unprobed ones.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message headers, since the relay may be stripping them. Confirm the footer unsubscribe link and address from the full HTML.
6. Raise the QA tool's HTML capture limit so merge-token and footer checks cover the whole message.
