# PERGOLAS ❗ WAY DAY ❗ TOMORROW ❗ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Wayfair pergola email in four days, and I'm tired of it. The 10/21 "PERGOLAS 🟣 🟣 WAY DAY STARTS OCT. 24" send already told me Way Day starts the 24th. Today's "TOMORROW" version is the same news with a shorter countdown. The images loaded this time, which beats the blank heroes on 10/21 and 10/22. After that I'm back to scrolling a wall of gazebos.
- The top is a purple "80% OFF STARTS TOMORROW, WAY DAY" banner, then eight pergolas with was/now prices. Midway there's an "EARLY WAY DAY TODAY ONLY" strip with code APP10 for an extra 10% off in the app. That's the only reason to act today, and it's buried under the fold. A "but wait, there's more…" section then runs bar stools, makeup vanities, shoe racks and filing cabinets. Two homes mid-renovation doesn't make me a pergola buyer. I'd skim it and probably save nothing.
- Would I click? Only if I were already hunting a pergola, and I'm not. I wouldn't forward it. If I came back tomorrow, it would be for the app code.

## What's working

- The purple "80% OFF / STARTS TOMORROW / WAY DAY" banner is the first thing my eye hits, so the event is clear immediately.
- Every pergola tile shows a was/now price, like $896 down to $788. That's easy to compare at a glance.
- The "EARLY WAY DAY TODAY ONLY, code APP10, extra 10% off in the app" strip is a real, time-bound hook.
- The Affirm 0% APR block is useful for a big-ticket buy like this. Furniture shoppers think in monthly payments.
- Images actually rendered, unlike the blank 10/22 "Best-selling pergolas" send.

## What's weak

- Eight pergolas in a row is too much of one category. My Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel lists are indoor, so none of it fits my renovations.
- The "up to 80% off" claim sits next to prices that look like roughly 10 to 30% off. The offer doesn't feel honest.
- The app-code strip is mid-scroll, in small print. It's the best offer and it's easy to miss.
- The "but wait, there's more…" grid is a random mix of vanities, freezers, bar stools and filing cabinets. It's a catalog dump, and none of it is curated.
- The Affirm copy is tiny and confusing, with a struck-out "24" before "36 months". Fine print runs to the footer.
- The subject's ❗ emoji stacking feels spammy. Wayfair sends the same shape every day, and Pottery Barn's "extra 20%" sends have the same fatigue problem.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the real discount range, then the app code, then the products. One clear offer beats eight tiles.
- 2. Cut the pergola run to 3 or 4 hero items and spread the rest across categories matched to what I browse, like lighting, rugs and sofas.
- 3. Make the APP10 code a button with a visible deadline, not a strip.
- 4. Stop sending the same pergola theme four days running. CB2 varies its heroes, and even that "ABOUT TO BE EVERYWHERE" send felt fresher.
- 5. Clean up the Affirm block with a single readable line, like "from $X/mo".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Way Day starts tomorrow: extra 10% off in the app today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early access: pergolas up to 80% off + code APP10`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use APP10 today for 10% off, before Way Day opens to everyone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop early deals on outdoor, bar stools and more. Ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Link probing was inconclusive: 9 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 429, so none of those could be confirmed. The most concrete defects are 5 linked banner images with no `alt` text and 20 tracking pixels served over plain `http://`. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, and the automated checks attribute that to the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **9 of 12 probed links returned 429 and are unverified.** They are not confirmed broken. This is bot throttling on `wayfair.com` and `wayfairapp.onelink.me`. Affected links:
  - `/daily-sales`, in the "Shop Sale" button and 2 image links
  - `/` ("Wayfair")
  - `/affirm?...isApplicationOpen=true`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-pergolas--se82159.html?eid=603454...`
  - both `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links
  - `.../AppPromoEmail?af_dp=https://wayfair.com/curated/app-extra-10%25-off~ev640199.html`
- **3 links were not probed** because the 70s time budget ran out: `.../on-sale-now%3a-makeup-vanities-se606`, `.../our-favorite-bar-stools-se73977.html` and `.../our-best-freezer-deals-se36776.html?`. In the QA output the first and third URLs end without `.html` or after a bare `?`. That is probably truncation in the QA report, but I can't confirm it.
- **Coverage is low.** 60 links were skipped as low priority, so the email is only about 17% probed (12 of 72 links).
- **Campaign label mismatch on the app links.** Both OneLink URLs carry `c=Triggered` while also carrying `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch send is labelled as a triggered campaign, which can misattribute AppsFlyer reporting.
- **Insecure fallback URL.** The OneLink `af_web_dp` parameter is `http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback starts on plain HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **5 linked banner images have no `alt` text.** Each is wrapped in an `<a>`, so the link has no accessible name and the probe records the filename as the link text:
  - `US-WD26-Preview-STm-Email.gif` → `/daily-sales`
  - `WFUS_261024_WayDay-APPEARLYACCESS_Email_SkinnyBann…` → OneLink
  - `WFCA_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-Affirm.` → `/affirm`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png` → `/daily-sales`
  - `WFCA_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-GiftCar…`
- **Tracking pixels have no `alt`.** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` (open pixel) and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` (2x1) are missing it. Add `alt=""` to these.
- **20 impression pixels use `http://`**, for example `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000&sz=2x1&m=false&p=ffd356ec-...`. Clients that block mixed content will drop them, which undercounts impressions. They are also an unencrypted request carrying the recipient identifier `p=`.
- **Missing language attribute.** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="width:100% !important;...">` with no `lang` or `xml:lang`.
- The HTML was truncated, so table structure, `<title>`, role attributes and the footer could not be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. Per-recipient identifiers (`_emr`, `_eml`, `p=`) are populated. This check only covers the truncated portion of the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`, and tracking runs on first-party `wayfair.com` subdomains.
- **The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address were not visible** in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses proprietary parameters: `_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs=cs7`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=15`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_142115`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16566`. This is consistent across links, so it is only an issue if downstream analytics expect UTMs.
- **The pergola link matches the subject.** `best-selling-pergolas--se82159.html?eid=603454&unifiedeventid=603454` is consistent, but the landing page was not verified because of the 429.
- **Most banner and CTA links go to the generic `/daily-sales`.** Only the pergola link is category-specific.
- **The Affirm banner creative is named `WFCA_...`** (Canada), but its link goes to `www.wayfair.com/affirm` (the US domain). Verify the locale targeting.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 linked banners, and `alt=""` to the 21 tracking pixels.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`. Align `c=Triggered` with the batch send, or confirm it is intentional.
4. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Re-probe the 9 links that returned 429 and the 3 unprobed links, using throttled requests or a longer time budget.
6. Pull the raw `.eml` straight from the sender or an MTA. Use it to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
7. Confirm the `WFCA` Affirm banner is meant to link to the US `/affirm` page.
