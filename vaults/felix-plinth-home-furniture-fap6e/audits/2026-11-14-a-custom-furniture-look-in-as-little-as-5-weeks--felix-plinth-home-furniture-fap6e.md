# A custom furniture look in as little as 5 weeks
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is a better Crate & Barrel send than the six I archived yesterday. It's a sofa email, and sofas are what I'm shopping for right now. The "exact right sofa made easy" hero and the "try a swatch first" and "order free swatches" blocks are for someone furnishing two houses at once. After the 11/13 pile-up of Nod Chair, Double Rewards, Le Creuset and three Kids sends, plus the "Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards!" blast, I'm already numb to this sender. The sofa content is the first thing in days that isn't a rerun.
- The email is a very long scroll. Lounge, Retreat, Contour, Axis, Serenity and Cosgrove each get a full-width room shot and a button. Then it pivots to accent chairs, the Design Desk, kitchen deals, cookware and a second Early Black Friday banner. That's three emails stapled together. The sofa section is the good part, and I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. The cookware tail is noise for me.
- One caveat: the render I looked at is heavily downscaled. The small print in the top strip and the per-collection copy was too small to read, so I'm judging structure and layout, not fine print. I'd click through to the sofa collections and swatches. I wouldn't forward it, because there's nothing in it for a friend who isn't mid-reno.

## What's working

- The sofa-first hero ("the exact right sofa made easy") with a "Start Customizing" button matches what I'm doing right now.
- Each collection gets its own room shot and a "Shop [name]" button. That reads like a catalog I can scan, and I can tell Crate & Barrel from Pottery Barn at a glance.
- "Try a swatch first" with an "Order Free Swatches" button hits swatch-and-sample, which is how I actually buy upholstery.
- The Design Desk block with "Book a Free Appointment" is the showroom-style service content I want from a furniture retailer.
- The top strip carries the Double Rewards and Early Black Friday offer. That matters, because I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. Six sofa collections, then chairs, the Design Desk, kitchen deals, cookware and a second Early Black Friday banner is a lot of scrolling.
- The subject says "custom look in as little as 5 weeks." I couldn't find a clear 5-week lead-time callout or a cutoff date that backs it up.
- The offer is a small strip at the top while the room photography dominates. The visual hierarchy lands on the sofa, not the deal.
- The kitchen and cookware blocks at the bottom are off-topic for a sofa email. They look like leftovers from the earlier Le Creuset send.
- This is again the same sender, the same promo and the same "Early Black Friday" language as 11/13. I'd tune it out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Keep the hero, three or four sofa collections, swatches and the Design Desk, and send the kitchen content as its own email.
- 2. Make the 5-week promise a real, visible headline element. Show a lead-time badge on the hero and on each collection card.
- 3. Pull the offer up into the hero. "Up to 30% off + 2x Rewards" should sit next to "Start Customizing", not in a small top strip.
- 4. Add price-from, fabric names and dimensions under each collection button, so I know what each sofa costs before I click.
- 5. Add a clear order-by date for delivery. Crate & Barrel is already testing "order by 11/19" language, and it should lead.
- Compared with the competition: Article's travertine send makes one hero product earn the whole email, and it shows more discipline than this one. Wayfair's "Area Rugs. People. Are. Loving." is a 20-tile grid, so at least Crate's collection-by-collection layout is easier to scan. Pottery Barn's "Just in! NEW" was the fifth send in two days with no reason to open. CB2's "UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS" contradicted its own strip. Crate has the same offer-clarity problem here, just quieter.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with about 86 click-tracked links and 45 images. The QA run flagged 3 warnings (57% pass rate), but 2 of the 3 are header-capture limits of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a zoom-blocking viewport meta tag.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links unverified.** 86 links were skipped because they are on tracking/click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Destination validity, redirect chains and broken links can't be confirmed from this run.
- **Heavy third-party tracking.** There are 5 identical LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`). Each one fires a separate third-party request.
- **Other tracking assets.** The email also includes:
  - an Oracle/Responsys-style open pixel, `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - a second beacon, `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/06dc6493-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images carrying the same per-recipient `mi_u` identifier
- **No broken links detected.** Nothing could be tested, so this is not a finding.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA).** Of the 45 flagged images:
  - 31 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `bf3053a9-…jpg`). The hero and product imagery have no accessible name and show nothing when images are blocked.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - 7 are tracking pixels or beacons (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, the `dv.` image). These should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Text-resize override.** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` rule and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` also stop users from enlarging text on iOS.
- **Markup hygiene.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
  - `format-detection` is declared 3 separate times (once combined, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` individually), which is redundant.
  - The doc mixes `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` with `<meta charset>`.
- The source was truncated, so body-level semantics (heading structure, table `role="presentation"`) could not be reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. The `mi_u` values are rendered per-recipient identifiers, not broken tokens. Because the source is truncated, this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN).** QA notes the header may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe support can't be confirmed, and Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown because the relay doesn't expose the header. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` aligns with the image and tracking subdomains, which is a positive sign but not proof.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The footer (physical address, in-body unsubscribe link) falls beyond the truncated HTML, so CAN-SPAM body requirements couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 86 links are wrapped in tracking redirects and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "custom furniture in as little as 5 weeks" subject can't be assessed. This is a data gap, not a detected defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 31 content images and the 7 `mi.…/p/rp/` images. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` and `html` rules.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>` and remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` template comment and the duplicate `format-detection` metas.
4. Reduce the 5 duplicate `rlcdn` pixels to one, or confirm each is needed.
5. For the audit pipeline, capture raw headers (or use a direct-MX capture) to verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results. Also follow redirects on the tracking links, or resolve a sample, so UTM and landing-page continuity can be checked.
