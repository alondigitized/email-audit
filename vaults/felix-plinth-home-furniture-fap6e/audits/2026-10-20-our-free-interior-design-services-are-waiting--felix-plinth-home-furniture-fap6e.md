Article's version is self-serve, which suits how I shop.

## What's working

- The swatches, fabric samples and tablet design proposal in the hero show me exactly what I'd get.
- The three-step structure (STEP ONE/TWO/THREE) is easy to scan and reads like a real process.
- "And the best part? It's free." puts the value up front in a dark brown block with high contrast.
- The 3D layout and room plan visuals in step two are what I'd actually want to see before committing.
- The customer quote and "30-day returns" block near the bottom reassure me about a furniture purchase.
- It's a service email, not a coupon. That's a nice break from Wayfair's Way Day teaser and CB2's sixth send in six days.

## What's weak

- The hero button says "Learn more" while every other button says "Start your project." That makes the primary CTA unclear.
- There are three identical "Start your project" buttons. I'd want each to say something different, like "Pick your style" or "See a sample plan."
- There's no time-bound reason to act today and no stated turnaround, so it's easy to put off.
- It doesn't say whether the free service requires a purchase, which makes me skeptical.
- The shop-the-styles photos have Instagram handles but no product names or prices, so I can't tell which desk or chair I'm looking at.
- It has no rewards or loyalty tie-in, and there's no mention of fabric swatches being mailed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero CTA match the rest ("Start your free project") and state the terms right under it: "No purchase required, plan in 3 days."
- 2. Show one real before-and-after room with the actual products and prices, so I can picture the outcome.
- 3. Add a line about free fabric and material swatches. That's the sample program I care about.
- 4. Give each step button its own copy, and add a lead-time note for furniture shipping.
- 5. Add a deadline or a limited perk, like a design-fee credit toward the first order.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP template (tracked links on `links.email.article.com` and `post.spmailtechnol.com`). QA found 0 hard failures. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from the data. The confirmed issues are malformed dark-mode CSS, an empty `<title>`, and three images without alt text. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Probing covered 12 material links and skipped 18 (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). The QA output reports no broken or redirecting links among the probed set. Skipped links are unverified.
- Image and link tracking uses two different hosts: `links.email.article.com` (branded) and `post.spmailtechnol.com` (ESP-owned, unbranded). Examples are `https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Ai9kdwWlvESDpFJuTnPZXw~~/AAQVbhA~/...` and `https://links.email.article.com/s/eo/YTnlmsr8-...`. Serving images through the unbranded redirect domain is a minor reputation and consistency risk. A block on that domain would also break those images. Neither is confirmed as a failure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `src=https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Ai9kdwWlvESDpFJuTnPZXw~~/AAQVbhA~/sJM65b03...`
  - `src=https://links.email.article.com/s/eo/YTnlmsr8-...` (the QA labels it "25"). The `/s/eo/` path may be an open-tracking pixel. If so, an empty `alt=""` is the correct fix, not descriptive text.
  - `src=https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/PBpqpzxEdeT20SZukfcl0Q~~/AAQVbhA~/qB5LPfrv...`
  - With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, any of these that are content images will have no fallback.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. Some clients and web-view versions use it, and accessibility tools flag it.
- **Malformed dark-mode CSS** in the first `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma, so parsers treat the selector list as invalid and drop the whole rule. Heading and paragraph colors won't be forced in native dark mode.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a stray `!important` outside a declaration. The `color:#fff` still applies, but without `!important`, so it can lose to inline styles.
  - That `@media` block closes only the last rule (`a .link{color:#fff!important}`) before `</style>`, with no closing brace for the `@media` itself. Browsers recover, but strict parsers may not.
  - The `[data-ogsc]` (Outlook.com dark mode) block is well-formed (`...}}</style>`), so it likely works, but it duplicates the broken rules.
- `lang="en"`, `color-scheme` meta and the viewport meta are present. No issues there.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA itself says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unverified, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at volume, so verify them against raw headers from a direct (non-relay) copy.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay and is not a confirmed defect. The From domain is `email.article.com`, so check alignment with the ESP's signing domain.
- The QA states an unsubscribe link was included in probing. The footer, physical address and unsubscribe markup are not in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.
