# FOUND: Bar Stools!
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- So the subject is "FOUND: Bar Stools!" — like Wayfair's algorithm remembered I looked at bar stools and is delivering the goods — and then I scroll through eight-plus screen-lengths of email and count maybe two working images total. Everything else, including the bar stools that got top billing in the subject line, is a blank white box with a tiny blue dot where a photo should be. This is worse than the "New score alert: Bookcases" send from 2026-08-19, which at least had a coherent single blank hero — this is a graveyard of broken tiles stacked one after another: mattresses, dining tables, desks, AC units, closet systems, spice racks, nightstands, gazebos, faux plants, recliners, fireplaces, bath towels, cat trees. It reads like the entire "you might also like" engine misfired and nobody caught it before send.
- The one thing that did technically render — two little rattan bar stools with a star rating — is buried above the fold in a tiny thumbnail before the email face-plants into broken image after broken image. There's zero attempt at furniture-shopping basics I actually care about: no dimensions, no "ships free," no swatch options, nothing about lead time on that gazebo or recliner. Compare that to CB2's "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" send, which at least had crisp product shots and real pricing — this is Wayfair actively working against itself.
- Judgement: not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and moving on. If a friend saw this they'd assume my email client was broken, not that Wayfair sent it that way.

## What's working

- The subject line promise (bar stools) technically appears as the very first product tile, with a legible star rating.
- The purple "but wait, there's more..." and "style" section dividers are at least visually clean and on-brand.
- The recliner section headline ("recliners in every style") is one of the few spots with real copy instead of just a broken tile.
- Footer trust badges (Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing, Wayfair Professional) render fine and are recognizable.

## What's weak

- The hero banner space at the very top is a totally blank white box — no image, no headline, nothing.
- The actual bar stools promised in the subject are a tiny two-inch thumbnail, not the hero — total mismatch between subject and visual hierarchy.
- The overwhelming majority of product tiles (mattress, desk, AC, closet systems, spice racks, nightstands, gazebo, faux plants, fireplace, bath towels, cat trees) are blank boxes with a lone blue dot — a near-total image rendering failure.
- No pricing, no percent-off, no free-shipping badge anywhere in the body — I have zero idea what any of this actually costs.
- Category sprawl is out of control — bar stools, AC units, spice racks, and cat trees in one email tells me this is a generic "browse abandonment" blast, not a targeted send, despite the personalized subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the image pipeline before this ships again — a subject-line promise ("Bar Stools!") followed by a wall of broken placeholder boxes is the single biggest trust-killer here; nothing else matters until images actually load.
- 2. Make the bar stools the actual hero — full-width image, price, star rating, "ships free" badge — since that's what got me to open.
- 3. Cut the scope down to 3-4 related items (bar stools, counter-height dining, maybe a bar cart) instead of the current AC-unit-to-cat-tree grab bag; it reads as spam-y "recently viewed" sprawl, not curation.
- 4. Add real pricing and a shipping/lead-time callout on at least the hero product — I don't buy furniture without knowing the delivery window.
- 5. Add a clear single CTA button ("Shop Bar Stools") near the top instead of relying on tiny "›" links buried under broken tiles.

## Technical Audit

Technical Summary
This transactional/promotional email (Wayfair "Bar Stools" campaign, batch 15) shows standard ESP-generated markup with 20 unlabeled 1×1 tracking pixels served over unencrypted HTTP, systemic missing alt text on all content images, and unverifiable authentication/unsubscribe headers due to relay limitations.

Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 72 links were probed (link-likelihood ranking); 60 skipped as low-priority footer/utility/social — an unsubscribe link was confirmed present among probed links.
- 8 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) rather than a clean pass/fail, including the primary CTA "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`), "Shop Sale" (`/daily-sales`), the homepage link, and a product link for the Kymberlynn Bar Stool (`/wayfair-product-L59-K~W100391215.html`). Server rate-limiting prevents confirming these are not broken; QA notes link may still be valid.
- 4 additional product links (2 duplicate instances of `/wayfair-product-L291-K~W114854683.html`, plus `L59-K~W100391215` and `L441-K~W006989343`) went unprobed after the 70s time budget expired — status unknown, not a confirmed pass.
- 20 tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over plain HTTP, not HTTPS — flagged as "Image uses http://: Non-HTTPS source may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently drop open-tracking fidelity for this batch (s=124126000–019).

Rendering & Accessibility
- 23 images are missing `alt` text, including all product/hero creative (e.g., `wfus_jt800_batch9_livingroom_recliners_everystyle_`, `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDeals-Skinny-Loyalty-N`), 7 star-rating icons (`stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`), and all 20 HTTP tracking pixels. With images blocked (the default in most mail clients), recipients get no fallback text — this affects both content comprehension and screen-reader accessibility.
- One image filename contains a raw unencoded `%`: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` — worth confirming this isn't a URL-encoding artifact from the source asset name, though the QA tool did not flag it as a broken fetch.
- Standard Uniform/MJML-style responsive media-query scaffolding (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, Outlook `data-outlook-cycle` fix, Gmail `u-P--5` hook) is present and structurally normal — no rendering-engine-specific issues surfaced by QA.

Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens were flagged in QA output or visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe as of their 2024 bulk-sender rules) also not found — same relay caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — pass/fail of sender authentication is unknown from this capture, not confirmed as failing.
