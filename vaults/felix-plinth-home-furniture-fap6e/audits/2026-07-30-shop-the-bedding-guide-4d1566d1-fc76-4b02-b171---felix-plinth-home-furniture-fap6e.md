# Shop The Bedding Guide
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, kid bedding, "back to school." I don't have kids. This is the fourth or fifth send from this sub-brand I've had to sit through, and I said as much on "Meet Beatrix" and the newborn shop email — I'm just not the audience here, and no amount of nice organic-cotton photography changes that. The fabric swatch grid up top is honestly a decent hook for about two seconds, then it's toddler duvets, a girl in a crib photo, and a "back to school savings" banner, and I'm out.
- To be clear, this isn't a bad email on its own terms — the "stay cool," "supersoft," "cozy cloud," "comfy tee" sections are organized cleanly, the material/breathability/feel ratings are a nice touch I haven't seen Pottery Barn or Wayfair bother with, and the photography is soft and consistent. But I'm on lists for two home renovations, not a nursery. Crate & Barrel's main list at least stays in my lane (dinnerware, furniture); Crate & Kids keeps landing in my inbox like it doesn't know that.
- Not opening again if I get a sixth one this week, not forwarding — the only friend I'd forward this to doesn't have kids either.

## What's working

- The "search for wildcard sale up to 25% off" hero copy at least leads with a real number instead of vague "shop now" fluff.
- Each fabric section (percale, gauze, washed cotton, jersey) has a material/breathability/feel rating strip — genuinely more product detail than Wayfair or Pottery Barn usually bother to show me.
- Photography is consistent and calm — soft neutral tones, no jarring color clashes across sections.
- Star ratings under each product name are a trust cue I don't usually get from these mid-funnel category emails.

## What's weak

- Wrong persona, full stop — this is a nursery/kids bedding drop and I'm renovating two adult homes, not a kid's room.
- It's the same "we've been sending you Crate & Kids constantly this week" pattern I flagged on the newborn shop and Beatrix emails — the cadence problem is getting worse, not better.
- Email is enormous — five nearly-identical fabric sections stacked before I even hit "back to school," which is exhausting to scroll on a phone.
- "Back to school" savings banner at the bottom feels bolted on and unrelated to the bedding guide framing above it.
- Zero adult/home-office/second-home framing anywhere — nothing here acknowledges I'm not shopping for a kid.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids sends entirely and route me to Crate & Barrel main-brand bedding/home content instead — that's the single biggest lift possible here, this is a targeting problem, not a creative problem.
- 2. Cut the email in half — five fabric-type sections with near-identical layouts is redundant even for someone who IS shopping for kid bedding; two or three with a "see all fabrics" link would convert just as well.
- 3. Drop the "back to school" savings block or move it to its own send — it reads like two campaigns stitched together.
- 4. If Crate & Kids must go to adults without kids, add a "shop for a gift" framing so at least the send has a reason to exist for someone like me.
