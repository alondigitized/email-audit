# ENDS TODAY: Up to 60% off + earn 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again. That's the fourth or fifth time this brand family has hit my inbox this week alone, and I only actually renovate for adults — the nursery/kids furniture is a hard scroll-past for me. "Ends Today" on a coastline hero, then straight into a full grid of kids furniture, baby bedding, nursery chairs — none of it is my category. I'm juggling a primary and a second home renovation, both adult spaces, so this whole send is dead air for me.
- The one thing that stopped my thumb was "TheDesignDesk" — free interior design consults with no purchase required is actually relevant to someone doing two houses at once. But it's buried below six product tiles I don't care about. I'm not clicking, not forwarding, and this is getting archived without a second look — same fate as the Crate & Kids sends earlier this week.

## What's working

- The countdown-free "Ends Today" urgency at least keeps things simple compared to Crate & Barrel's live ticking clock I've seen three days running.
- TheDesignDesk free-consult callout ("11 min with a pro, no pressure") is genuinely useful for someone mid-renovation — that's the one block that speaks to my actual project.
- Layout is clean, no render bugs, images all loaded fine, and the "up to X% off [category]" labels under each tile are specific and scannable.
- The "new! the card you love just got better" Crate Card teaser at least signals a rewards angle, which I do care about since I chase the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- This is Crate & Kids, not Crate & Barrel — the sender name buried that distinction, and I almost skipped it entirely because I don't have kids' furniture needs.
- Every single hero product tile is nursery/kids: cribs, baby bedding, nursery chairs, kids furniture. Zero adult-home relevance despite the "renovating two houses" reality this brand's own CRM should know from my purchase history.
- The Jellycat stuffed animal restock block is even further from my world — that's a baby-shower-gift email, not a home-renovation email.
- "we love these for you" personalization block is still 100% baby gear (strollers, car seats, gliders) — if this is meant to be personalized, it's personalized to the wrong customer.
- Compare this to CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" send I logged this week — CB2 at least stayed in adult furniture lane the whole way through. This one never pivots to anything I'd buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment kids-free households out of Crate & Kids sends entirely, or lead adult-relevant households straight to TheDesignDesk consult offer instead of six kids-furniture tiles first — that's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Move the free design consultation block up near the hero — it's the only genuinely cross-category hook in the email.
- 3. Drop or de-prioritize the Jellycat restock for non-baby-registry subscribers; it reads as noise to anyone not actively expecting a baby.
- 4. Make the sender name and subject clearer that this is the kids-specific sub-brand so I don't have to open it to find out it's not for me.
- 5. If cross-selling into Crate & Barrel proper, put an actual adult-furniture CTA in this email rather than assuming I'll dig for it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Today: nursery deals + free design help`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book a no-pressure appointment — 3D previews included`
