# ORNAMENTS & TREE DÉCOR ✨ UP TO 80% OFF ✨ FREE SHIPPING ✨ 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Eight. That's where the Wayfair counter sits in my inbox as of this morning, and I'm putting that number first because it is the only honest frame for this send. I already reviewed the "Black Friday in July" send from them today, I got the outdoor organization email this morning before that, the end-tables-80%-off teaser dropped yesterday — and now I'm staring at an email with a subject line about ornaments and tree décor that, the moment I open it, shows me rugs. Bedding. Outdoor furniture. Not a single ornament or Christmas tree product anywhere in the scroll.
- That subject/body mismatch is the story of this send. The body is actually a decent Black Friday in July home furnishings campaign — categories I genuinely have browser tabs open for right now. But someone put the wrong subject line on this template, or deployed the wrong template against this subject, and the result is that I opened it feeling tricked. That's a harder hole to climb out of than a boring subject line.
- Mark as read, no click. If Pottery Barn had sent me this same body content with an accurate subject, I'd probably have tapped through to rugs. Instead I'm here writing a frustrated review. Not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- The 80% OFF hero banner reads in under a second — big, high-contrast, instant signal.
- "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE" is a real motivator for furniture buyers; free shipping on big-ticket items moves me more than most % discounts.
- The category grid — rugs, bedding, outdoor furniture — is legitimately relevant to my current shopping list across both houses.
- Wayfair Rewards badge in the footer is a trust anchor; I'm running this against my highest-rewards card either way, but seeing it confirms there's a loyalty stack.
- The layout renders cleanly; no broken images, no overlapping text, no Lorem Ipsum disasters.

## What's weak

- The subject says ORNAMENTS & TREE DÉCOR and the email has zero Christmas content. This isn't a minor misfire — it's a bait-and-switch at the inbox level, and it immediately erodes whatever goodwill the offer had.
- Email number eight in approximately 72 hours. Article sent me one well-timed dining room email this week and nearly had me on dining chairs. Wayfair sent eight and I'm routing them on autopilot.
- "Picked just for you" over a grid of rugs, air quality products, kitchen mats, AND entryway furniture simultaneously is not personalization — it's a warehouse.
- "Shop all sales" is the laziest CTA in home retail. CB2's "THE FURNITURE EVENT IS ON" linked me directly to the sale. Pottery Barn's coffee table send went straight to that category. Wayfair is dumping me on a landing page and calling it a call to action.
- The mid-email BedSure branded module reads like a sponsored insertion — disrupts the flow and reminds me I'm looking at an aggregator, not a curated store.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject line immediately and send a correction.** This email has nothing to do with ornaments. Sending a follow-up with "we sent the wrong subject" transparency actually builds goodwill — more than pretending it didn't happen.
- 2. **Cut to four emails per week maximum.** Eight in 72 hours has moved Wayfair into the same mental bucket as daily deal spam. Article is earning more engagement per send with a fraction of the volume.
- 3. **Swap "shop all sales" for split category CTAs** — "Shop Rugs," "Shop Outdoor Furniture," two buttons side by side. CB2's event email did this and it worked; so did Pottery Barn's product-specific send.
- 4. **Lead with the category most relevant to my browsing history.** If Wayfair knows I'm a logged-in renovation shopper (and they do), outdoor furniture and rugs should be at position 1, not bedding.
- 5. **Remove or clearly label the BedSure module** as a partner placement — mid-scroll brand drops with no context feel like native ad deception.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday in July: up to 80% off rugs, furniture & outdoor`
- **Subject Alt B:** `80% off + free shipping — your home list, this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The categories you've been browsing are on sale right now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Everything ships free. Pick what's already on your list.`
