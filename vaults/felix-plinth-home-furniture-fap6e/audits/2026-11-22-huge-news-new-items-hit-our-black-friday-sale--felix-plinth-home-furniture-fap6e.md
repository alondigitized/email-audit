# Huge news: NEW ITEMS hit our Black Friday sale!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: finally, a main-brand Crate & Barrel email, and it still isn't built for me. The 11/22 Kids sends ("Time to shop: Up to 30% off top toys", "Black Friday: Up to 30% off toys that top their list!") were the ones I was dreading. This one at least opens with a hero for the whole store, "Up to 50% off + free shipping." But the hero is an espresso machine. The "huge news" is "even more furniture," and I have to scroll past a tile grid to get to it.
- It's a very long scroll. I count about eight sections: the hero, the new-items strip, a 12-tile category grid, clearance, kitchen electrics, EvenCook cookware, a Thanksgiving menu, personalized gifts and a "we love these for you" row. Then come a rewards block, an app-download block, The Design Desk and a wall of footer fine print. Pottery Barn's "Shop early, save BIG" got one maroon banner and one idea across. This email makes me hunt.
- Would I click? Probably once, into sofas and sectionals, since "up to 60% off" is the best number I can see in my category. Would I forward it? No. It's a catalog dump, and I'd just see the same offer in Pottery Barn's and CB2's sends anyway. I'd skim it and save it to the inspiration folder at most.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Up to 50% off + free shipping." I know the deal in one second.
- The "Big news: New items…even more furniture" strip is the right idea for me, because it's about furniture and not toys.
- The sofas and sectionals tile (up to 60% off) and the furniture and rugs tiles map to my renovation list.
- Sale vs. Reg. pricing is visible on the Ninja and EvenCook items ("Sale $319.95 Reg. $399.95"). I can do the math without clicking.
- Real deadline cues, like the 2-day shipping cutoff up top and the personalized-gifts date of December 15, make the urgency believable.
- This is the first send in a while that isn't a Kids takeover. The photography is warm and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The "new furniture" news is buried below the fold, behind a hero that sells a coffee machine. The subject promised furniture and the top of the email didn't deliver.
- Nearly every button reads "Shop Now," or the tiles carry no verb at all. None of them tells me which room or category I'm going to.
- The email is far too long. Kitchen electrics, a Thanksgiving menu and personalized gifts all repeat the offer in different wrappers.
- Almost every number is "up to." I can't tell which sofa is actually 60% off.
- There's no free-shipping or white-glove delivery detail for furniture. That's what I actually need to know on a big order, and it's missing.
- It's Crate & Barrel's fifth or sixth send in four days. Wayfair and Pottery Barn have me similarly numb, but they at least change the creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the new furniture in the hero. Show the sofa, name the discount and add delivery timing. Cut the kitchen items to one strip lower down.
- 2. Cut the length by half. Drop the Thanksgiving menu, the app block and either the gifts or the clearance section. Keep one product row.
- 3. Write CTAs with a noun, such as "Shop sofas," "Shop rugs" or "Shop new arrivals."
- 4. Add furniture-specific trust cues: the delivery window, swatch or sample availability, and the reward-dollars math in plain numbers.
- 5. Space out the cadence, and stop sending the same Black Friday template with a new subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New sofas just joined the 60%-off Black Friday sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New furniture added: up to 60% off + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sectionals, rugs and lighting, plus delivery dates that still beat the holidays.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Just added: more furniture at up to 50% off. See what's new before it sells out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard table-based ESP template (Marketo-style `mi.` tracking) with `lang="en"` and a Gmail/Outlook/Apple-Mail compatibility scaffold. Confirmed issues are a large number of `<img>` tags without `alt`, a viewport that blocks zoom, and leftover template and markup errors. The compliance and authentication warnings come from missing relay headers, not from the email itself. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped the click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Third-party pixel repeated 5 times.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp) is requested five times with a 64-hex `s=` identifier. This looks like a hashed user identifier, but the source doesn't show what was hashed. If it is derived from the recipient's email, it is a privacy and data-sharing concern. The repeats look redundant (one pixel per `n`).
- **Tracking identifiers in image URLs.** `mi_u=dbe8da71…` is appended to the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif` URLs. A recipient-level ID in image URLs is expected for this ESP, but it will leak if the email is forwarded or rendered in a public archive.
- **Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/<uuid>?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` beacon** are first-party tracking and personalization assets. They depend on image loading, so Apple MPP or blocked images will distort metrics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images.** The QA output lists about 25 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif` images with no alt text. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `5d342883-…jpg` and the animated `56526c31-…gif`. The list is cut off, so the true count may be higher. If these are product or promo imagery, they are invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
- **Pixels flagged as missing alt.** The `rlcdn` GIFs, `o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon are flagged too. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`. The QA check can't tell a missing alt from an empty one, so some of these may be false positives. Verify in the source.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Markup errors.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which should be `&amp;`.
  - Duplicate declarations: `<meta charset>` alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type, and `format-detection` repeated three times.
  - The leftover template placeholder `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production.
- **Global CSS reset.** `*{… line-height:100%}` overrides line-height everywhere, so it needs per-element overrides. `.f10`, `.f14` and `.f20` set px line-heights only inside the 460px media query.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"`, `color-scheme: light` and MSO conditional blocks are present, and there is a mobile breakpoint at 460px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) in the truncated HTML, subject or From. Per-recipient values appear only as `mi_u` / `mi_mid` / `mi_cid` parameters, which are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support, so check the raw `.eml` from the original delivery.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. No finding either way.
- **Footer not visible.** The physical postal address and an in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated HTML, so they are unverified.
- **Sender domain.** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain and matches the image and tracking hosts. No misalignment is visible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 81 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't expose any final URLs or `utm_*` parameters. Attribution runs through the ESP redirect layer rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`, `mi…/rp`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Collapse the five identical `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls into one, and confirm the `s=` value isn't a raw or reversibly hashed email.
4. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), deduplicate the meta tags, and strip the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT…` comment.
5. For the audit pipeline itself, the missing headers are a capture gap that makes the compliance and deliverability checks uninformative. Preserve the original headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`) before relay, or mark those checks "not assessable" instead of WARN. Also resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 81 skipped links so UTM and landing-page continuity can be checked.
