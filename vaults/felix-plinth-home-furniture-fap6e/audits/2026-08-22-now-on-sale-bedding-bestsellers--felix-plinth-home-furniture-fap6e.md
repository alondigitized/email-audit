# Now on sale 🚨 Bedding bestsellers
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Bedding Bestsellers — Now on Sale" — this is at least a legible premise, and if I'm being honest, bedding is closer to my actual reno checklist than the fortieth lamp email. The quilts and duvets look good in isolation — that Cloud Duvet in the chambray blue is genuinely a piece I'd screenshot for my Pinterest-adjacent folder. But my brain immediately flags this as the same aisle Pottery Barn already sent me this week with "on sale AND shipping free" — soft goods again, just a narrower cut of the same inventory.
- What actually stops the scroll is the Halloween Shop banner shoved in the middle of a bedding sale email, right before a sweepstakes for a design consultation. That's three different asks in one send, and none of them are "buy this quilt at this price." No percentage, no dollar figure, nothing — just "Now on Sale" as a vibe.
- I'd scroll through it on the toilet, maybe tap the Cloud Duvet product shot out of habit, and not forward it — there's nothing here surprising enough to send a friend, unlike Crate & Barrel's "300+ new rugs" email which at least organized by room and felt like a shopping tool.

## What's working

- The three hero product shots (quilt, cloud quilt, cloud duvet) are styled consistently and photograph well — feels like actual product, not stock imagery.
- "Recommended Just For You" section has real variety across categories (dresser, dining table, sheet set, platform beds) — reads like it's trying to be useful, not just another single-SKU push.
- Rewards card callout ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") near the footer is relevant to how I actually buy — I'm a highest-rewards-card guy, so that's the one line in this email actually built for me.

## What's weak

- No actual price or percentage anywhere in the hero — "Now on Sale" with zero number is the same soft-goods sale beat as yesterday's email, just less specific.
- The Halloween Shop banner and Design Crew Sweepstakes both crash the party mid-email — this is a bedding sale, not a decor variety pack, and it kills the hierarchy.
- Category nav grid (Furniture / Lighting / Pillows & Decor / Bath) is just links to more browsing, not more clarity — feels like filler between the hero and the "recommended" block.
- I've now gotten five-plus Pottery Barn emails this week and this is the second bedding-flavored one; the cadence is genuinely wearing me down more than any individual send's execution.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the hero — "30% off bedding bestsellers" beats "Now on Sale" every time; CB2's recent "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send scored higher with me specifically because the discount was visible up top.
- 2. Cut the Halloween Shop and Design Crew Sweepstakes blocks entirely from a bedding-sale email — split them into their own sends so this one stays on-message.
- 3. Move the rewards card callout up near the hero — that's the actual reason I'd click, not buried past three product blocks.
- 4. Add fill/weight or material specs (down-alternative? organic cotton fill weight?) under at least the hero quilt — I'm renovating two houses and buying sight-unseen, I need the spec not just the photo.
