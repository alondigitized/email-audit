# ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- I opened this one on autopilot, and the sender line is why I almost didn't. It's Crate & Barrel **Kids** again, not the main brand. My lists are sofas, lighting and rugs for two houses, and I don't have a nursery to furnish. Yesterday's "Free shipping on orders over $99 ends TOMORROW!" was the first send that matched my lists, so this feels like a step backward. It's also the third C&B-family send I've had today, after the 10/12 coffee and espresso machine email and this one.
- The free-shipping deadline is clear, but I had to scroll past a huge Halloween pumpkin bag with a plush toy pile to find it. The "Up to 60% off" bedroom and nursery event banner sits above the shipping offer, so two deals compete for the headline. The rest of the email is a long scroll: a six-tile category grid, a Halloween shop, bestsellers, clearance, and a "we love these for you" block with car seats, a stroller system and a crib mattress. Nothing in it speaks to a guy renovating two houses.
- I wouldn't click, and I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either, unless a friend with a newborn is buying a stroller. Then the Nuna travel system alone might earn a forward.

## What's working

- "ends today! free shipping on orders $99+" is a clear, credible deadline with one threshold. Yesterday's version was muddier.
- The category grid (bedding, toys, lighting, decor, storage, halloween) is clean and tappable. The "shop lighting" and "shop decor" tiles at least overlap with my lists.
- The product block shows real prices and named items, like the Jenny Lind dresser and the Naturepedic mattress. That's more concrete than most promos.
- The "loved by parents" five-star bestseller block gives some social proof. The soft, neutral nursery photography is polished and on brand.
- The app download callout ("including your chance at $500") and the TheDesignDesk consult are service touches. I'd actually use the design-help idea, just not for kids' rooms.

## What's weak

- Two offers fight for the hero: "Up to 60% off" for the Semi-Annual Event and free shipping at $99+. I don't know which one is the point.
- The hero image is a Halloween pumpkin bag. It's seasonal, but it has nothing to do with the free-shipping message or with me.
- The email is very long. By the time I hit clearance, the "we love these for you" block and the credit card pitch, I'm scrolling past three or four promos.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of tiny text with exclusions. It undercuts the "honest offer" feel, and I can't read it on a phone without zooming.
- The "for you" block is baby gear and a toddler dresser, with no sign it knows I'm not a parent. I'm a stranger to this sender.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer for the hero. Put the free-shipping deadline in the first screen and move the Halloween bag lower, or tie it to the offer ("Free shipping on pumpkin season").
- 2. Cut the repeating CTA blocks (clearance, Halloween shop, app, TheDesignDesk) to two. Shorten the email by about a third.
- 3. Segment the "for you" block by recent browsing. A non-parent who browsed lighting and decor shouldn't see car seats.
- 4. Make the fine print legible, or link to it in one line, instead of a gray wall.
- 5. Send a single "ends today" to the segment that hasn't ordered, not a third C&B-family send in one day.
- For comparison, Pottery Barn runs the same dark-green "Up to 70% off" block on every send, but at least it's one block and one message. CB2's "This sale pairs so well with free shipping" was cleaner and shorter. Wayfair's blank-hero pergola emails are worse, so this isn't the bottom of the pile. ## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based XHTML template with the usual Outlook/MSO fixes and a responsive breakpoint at 460px. The automated QA found 3 warnings (57% pass rate). Most of the findings are missing `alt` attributes on about 33 images and header checks that were inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **71 tracking links** went through click-redirect domains, and the HTTP probe skipped them. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` is five LiveRamp pixels. The `s=` value is the same hash as the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` parameter on the first-party pixels. The recipient's identifier is therefore being passed to a third-party data-matching domain. This is a privacy note, not a rendering defect.
- **Duplicate pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times, plus `o.gif` and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` tracker. That is 13 or more tracking images, which adds requests and may slow loading when images are enabled.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images:** Many images served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` have no `alt`. These include `eeb8adae-…jpg`, `fa956f91-…jpg` and `f76e182b-…jpg`, among others. The same applies to the `s7d5.scene7.com/…/2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` image. If images are blocked, or a screen reader is used, the content is lost. This may include the "Free shipping on orders $99+" offer, if it is rendered as an image. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm that.
- **Spacer images:** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` appears twice and has no `alt`. It should use `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixels:** `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `f3c0d…png` and `dv.` also lack `alt`. These are decorative, so `alt=""` is enough. The checker flags them because the attribute is absent, not because they matter to users.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Markup validity:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype. The document also has both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>`, and the latter is not self-closed. Neither is likely to break rendering.
- **Title:** The `<title>` is a generic brand name, not the subject line.
- **CSS conflict:** `img {max-width:600px}` at `min-width:768px` is overridden by `img {max-width:640px}` at `min-width:640px`, because the later rule wins at equal specificity. The 600px cap therefore never applies.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the footer and body past the cut-off were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** were not found (RFC 2369 / RFC 8058). The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. This is **inconclusive**. Check the raw headers at the sending side before treating it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay, not the sender. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is the correct setup.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section and could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 71 links are redirect-wrapped, and their targets and UTM parameters are not exposed in the available source or QA data.
- The `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance` asset name suggests a creative first built around Sept 26 and reused in a "ENDS TODAY" send. That could not be confirmed from the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image. That includes the `/m/1/*.jpg` set and the Scene7 clearance GIF. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Check the raw send headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment.
4. Re-run link validation with the redirect resolver enabled, and record the final URLs and UTMs.
5. Reduce the duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels from 6 to 1. Review whether the LiveRamp pixels, which carry a hashed recipient ID, are covered by the privacy policy.
6. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`, and set the title to the subject line.
