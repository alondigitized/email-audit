# Happy Thanksgiving! 🦃
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel again, and this time it's the actual main brand, not the Kids arm. That's a small win. The top banner says "Biggest Sale of the Year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + Free Shipping*". The Thanksgiving greeting under it, "happy Thanksgiving, from our home to yours," over a warm table shot, is the nicest thing C&B has sent me all week. I've had 5-6 C&B-family sends a day since 11/25, including "First turkey, then shopping (at up to 50% off!)" this morning. So the greeting reads as a thin wrapper around the same Black Friday sale.
- Below it, the email is a very long scroll. It covers knives, Ninja and Philips espresso machines, Oven-style pizza ovens, rugs, ornaments, an NFL "game day" block, a Design Desk promo, an app download and a store locator. For someone renovating two houses, the rugs, furniture and decor tiles are relevant. I'd still tap through only if I weren't already snoozing the C&B family. Verdict: skim, maybe save the rug and sofa tiles to my Pinterest folder, don't forward.

## What's working

- The "Happy Thanksgiving" hero with the styled table is warm, on-brand and doesn't scream at me.
- The headline offer is clear: "up to 50% off + free shipping" is visible right at the top.
- The category grid ("up to 40% off sofas & sectionals," "up to 60% off rugs," "60% off clearance") hits my renovation categories directly.
- The Design Desk block ("book a free appointment") is the only thing that speaks to someone furnishing two homes.
- The Rewards/credit card callout ("earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars") is the only thing here that touches my card-points math.

## What's weak

- The tiny asterisk on free shipping isn't explained near the banner. For furniture, shipping terms matter, and I don't see them until the fine print at the very bottom.
- The email is far too long. Knives, espresso machines, a pizza oven, ornaments, an NFL block and an app promo get roughly equal weight. There's no single hero for me.
- The tile text is small, and several offers are stacked ("up to 30%," "up to 40%," "up to 60%"). Nothing tells me what actually costs what.
- I've now gotten this same creative in several subject lines this week. "Happy Thanksgiving" is a thin relabel, and sending it on the holiday itself feels tone-deaf.
- There's no swatch or sample program and no showroom or delivery-date info, which is what I'd actually want for big-ticket furniture.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero (sofas/rugs/decor) plus one offer line, and move the kitchen-appliance block to a separate send.
- 2. Put the shipping terms next to the headline instead of only in the footer.
- 3. Add free swatches or a delivery-by-Christmas date next to the furniture tiles.
- 4. Segment: I buy furniture and decor, not baby gear or knives.
- 5. Respect the holiday cadence. One send today, not six.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with `lang="en"` and Outlook (VML/MSO) fallbacks. The QA run found no hard failures (57% pass rate, 0 issues, 3 warnings). The main findings are about 38 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute, a pinch-zoom restriction in the viewport meta, and compliance and authentication headers that could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 78 links were skipped because they are tracking or click-redirect domains, so there was no HTTP probe. Destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unconfirmed. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Redundant third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are five requests to the same LiveRamp pixel, differing only in `n`. This adds requests and a third-party dependency for no clear technical need.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` (Movable Ink) carries a hashed recipient ID. It appears to be intentional and working. The same ID is reused in the rlcdn `s=` parameter.
- **Open tracking:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ce0c03f6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are tracking pixels. Both are flagged for missing alt. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 38 images were flagged. Content images such as `…/m/1/1c52bbbc-857d-4c0b-90f6-634c843930d7.jpg` through `…/m/1/971aa10f-….jpg`, the `.gif` files `2c51d2e6-….gif` and `93a7ca28-….gif`, and the scene7 video asset `2026%5F1126%5FCB%5FHappyThanksgiving%5FVideo%2Dopt` have no alt text. If images are blocked, those sections show nothing, and screen readers announce filenames or nothing. Some of the flagged images are pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, the `dv.` pixel) that should have `alt=""`. The QA output doesn't show whether those have an attribute that is present but empty, or none at all.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - The title also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Duplicate and leftover markup:**
  - `format-detection` appears three times in `<head>` (a combined value, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately). The later tags conflict with or duplicate the first.
  - A template comment is left in the source: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px) { img {max-width:600px} }` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px) { img {max-width:640px} }`. The 640px rule also matches viewports of 768px and wider, and it comes later, so it wins. The 600px rule is dead code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The visible portion shows only the hashed `mi_u` recipient ID, and nothing about the truncated remainder can be confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA note says the relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw headers at the origin before treating it as a defect. For a large retail sender, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** It was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Verify alignment with a direct-delivery test instead of through the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Neither could be confirmed because the source was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The HTML was truncated and all 78 links are tracking redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. No evidence either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image (hero, product tiles, video poster) and `alt=""` to the tracking and spacer pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`) and align it with the subject. Remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` template comment.
4. Drop the duplicate `format-detection` tags and the dead 600px `img` rule.
5. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` pixels into one, or confirm all five are needed.
6. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the original message headers, outside the AgentMail relay.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolve the tracking redirects to verify landing URLs and UTM parameters.
