# BOO! Shop Halloween before it sells out this year →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "BOO!" hero shot — the wreathed front door with the pumpkins on the stoop, moody twilight lighting — is genuinely a nice piece of seasonal photography, and at least the subject line and the hero agree with each other for once, unlike the "Shop furniture you'll love now & forever" nursery bait-and-switch I called out from this same sender back on 8/1. But this is now the fifth-ish Crate & Barrel-family send in my inbox this week — two Crate & Kids "learning fun" emails, the backpack drop, the nursery furniture one, and now a full Halloween catalog. My inbox folder for this stuff is getting comically fat.
- The problem is where it goes after the hero: pumpkins, witch's-brew cocktail glasses, "cauldron mac & cheese" recipes — cute, but none of it touches the two houses I'm actually renovating. It's not until you're five screens deep that you hit the Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes and the Aspen collection 15% off, which is the actual relevant content for someone like me mid-reno on a primary and a second home.
- Verdict: I'm not clicking off the hero. I might scroll far enough to register the Design Desk appointment offer since I've been meaning to book one, but this reads as a seasonal catalog blast, not something targeted at my renovation. Not forwarding — my friend doing her own reno would roll her eyes at another Halloween pumpkin email the same as me.

## What's working

- The hero photography is legitimately well-shot — that wreathed door with pumpkins actually makes we want to see the wreath SKU, even if I'm not buying it.
- "The Design Desk" $5,000 sweepstakes plus free design appointment is the one section that's actually on-brief for a guy renovating two houses — CB2's recent "bedrooms" send hooked me the same way with real design content instead of just product tiles.
- Aspen collection at 15% off is a clean, specific discount with a name I can search for later.
- No render bugs — everything's crisp, no overlapping text or broken image tiles, which the last Crate & Kids nursery email also got right.

## What's weak

- The whole top two-thirds is pumpkins, cocktail glassware, and Halloween appetizers — zero renovation relevance, and I have to scroll past six sections before hitting furniture.
- Cadence fatigue is real: this is at least the fourth or fifth Crate & Barrel-family email this week between Crate & Kids and now Crate & Barrel proper. Article did the same thing to me two weeks running with sale emails and I'm just as tired of it there.
- The "Shop Now" CTA is repeated on nearly every module with zero variation — no specific product or category named, unlike Pottery Barn's recent "on sale & shipping free" send which at least told me what was on sale in the button context.
- New Barrel Card pitch buried near the bottom after I've already scrolled through Halloween decor, cocktails, and recipes — if the card math (points, $170 off a Le Creuset) is the hook, lead with it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this list — Halloween shoppers get the pumpkin/cocktail/recipe email, renovation-stage subscribers like me get an email that leads with Design Desk + Aspen collection + the Barrel Card math. Right now I'm getting the full catalog dump.
- 2. Move the Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes up near the hero — it's the single highest-intent offer in this email for someone renovating two properties, and it's currently six scrolls down.
- 3. Give the "Shop Now" buttons actual product nouns ("Shop Wreaths," "Shop Aspen Dining," "Book Design Appointment") instead of repeating the same generic CTA ten times.
- 4. Throttle send frequency across the Crate & Barrel / Crate & Kids family — I'm getting hit multiple times a week from what's clearly one parent company's list.
