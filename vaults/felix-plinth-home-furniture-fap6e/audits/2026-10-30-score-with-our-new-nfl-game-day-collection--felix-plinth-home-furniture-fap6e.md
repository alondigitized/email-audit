# Score with our NEW NFL Game Day Collection!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel Kids send I've gotten today, so I was already tired of them. This one is NFL Nod Chairs in Eagles and Ravens-style colors, plus beer-glass-style pint glasses with team logos. I'm renovating two houses and I'm not buying team-branded toddler furniture. It reads like it was built for a sports-fan dad with a toddler, and that isn't me.
- Past the hero, it turns into a catalog dump: clearance, Christmas, 10% signup, a product grid, a credit card ad, an app ad, and a design desk pitch. Compared with the Beatrix send earlier today, which at least had a real room scene, this one has no point of view. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it, because the one friend with a toddler and an NFL habit isn't on my list.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. A big headline, a short "pick your color" line, and a "Shop Nod Chairs" button sit right under the chair photo.
- The three personalized Nod Chairs tiles show the color options (indigo, iceberg green, yellow) with names, which helps if you actually want one.
- Prices are on the "we love these for you" grid, like $16.97 on the pumpkin treat bag and $1,350 on the stroller. I like seeing them without a click.
- "Hundreds of new markdowns up to 60% off" is a concrete number, even if it's buried mid-email.
- The Christmas collection block is on-season and the button is clear.

## What's weak

- Nothing here speaks to someone doing two renovations. There are no sofas, lighting, rugs, swatches, or showroom content.
- The 60% off clearance tile sits over a busy autumn photo, and the "selling fast" line is hard to read without zooming.
- It's too long and tries to do everything. The NFL hero, clearance, Christmas, signup, a card ad, the app, and the Design Desk all compete, so there's no single takeaway. Pottery Barn Design Services' recent send was one idea (free design help) and I remembered it.
- The "sign up, save 10%" block is a bit insulting when I'm an existing subscriber getting my fifth send of the day.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. It makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap Kids sends per day. Five in one day, with the Venus Williams collab, Beatrix, birthday gifts and now NFL, is what makes me tune out. Wayfair at least spaces its Thanksgiving blasts out.
- 2. Cut the second half of the email. One hero, one secondary offer and one footer would beat this long scroll.
- 3. Lead with a real reason to buy now, such as a game-day deadline or a stock-by date, instead of just "NEW".
- 4. Segment by household. If I've been browsing sofas and dressers, don't send me toddler chairs. CB2's wallpaper swatch email was targeted and read that way.
- 5. Put contrast behind the text on photo tiles so the clearance offer reads on a phone.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style (MI) XHTML Transitional template. The truncated source and QA data show a markup problem with image alt text (24 flagged images) and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed as real defects. The 67 tracked links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Tracking pixels:** these are present and expected.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` (×6)
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/61921bd4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp third-party sync pixels)
- **Third-party pixels:** the five `rlcdn.com` pixels add third-party requests, and they will fail silently under image-blocking or privacy proxies. This is a privacy and deliverability consideration, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 24 images are flagged with no `alt` attribute.
  - Content images:
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (about 8 files)
    - `.../m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
    - Scene7 `20261101%5FCBK%5FFall%5FClearance%5FPM%5Fv3%5FTert`
    - Scene7 `CK%5FH26%5FKidsHolidayMagic%5F24x13%5FEdit3%5FTert`
  - Spacer: `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - Tracking pixels: these should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - Effect: with images blocked, or in a screen reader, the content images have no text fallback and may be announced by filename.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. It is also a generic title that doesn't match the subject.
- **Global resets:** `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` override defaults for every element and can compress text and force centering.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the production source.
- **Truncated source:** the rest of the markup was not available, so I can't comment on `role="presentation"` on layout tables, headings or link text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated per-recipient identifiers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both were not found in the capture. The QA note says AgentMail may strip them, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw message headers before treating it as an issue.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper setup, but nothing here verifies it.
- **Body compliance elements:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated footer, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The truncated source doesn't show campaign link parameters, and all 67 tracked links were skipped. Nothing was confirmed, so no issue is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 or so content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>` as `&amp;`, and use a descriptive title.
4. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` (not the relay copy) to confirm the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers.
5. Resolve the tracking redirects with a followed-redirect probe to check final landing URLs and UTM carry-through.
6. Remove leftover template comments from the production build.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer and layout-table accessibility.
