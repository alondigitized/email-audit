# Up to 50% off rugs
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is my fourth CB2 email on July 2nd alone. I already processed "New items added. One week left to shop." earlier today, and the two July 1st sends before that. So my first reaction when this landed was muscle memory: *another one?* The subject is the most specific of the bunch — rugs, not just "furniture & more" — which is the one thing that kept me from reflexively archiving it. I'm actually mid-decision on two area rugs right now (one for the primary, one for the second home's living room), so the category hit at exactly the right moment. That's the only reason this gets a real look.
- But the email itself buries a plot twist: there's a "UP TO 60% OFF WAREHOUSE SALE" banner below the fold that outperforms the hero's 50% lead. I shouldn't have to scroll past furniture and lighting thumbnails to find the better number. Pottery Barn managed to hold its "Extra 20% off" headline consistently across three consecutive sends this week — CB2 is undercutting its own hero right here. The "MORE FOR YOU" product grid at the bottom is genuinely interesting (there's a floating shelf and a bar chair I want to look at), but the thumbnails are too small to read at a glance.
- I'd probably click the rug section, honestly, but more out of need than because this email earned it. I would not forward this — I'd share an individual product link if something stuck.

## What's working

- **Category specificity in the subject line paid off.** "Rugs" is a noun I'm actively shopping, and after three days of "Warehouse Sale" blasts, the narrower angle finally cuts through.
- **Hero to CTA flow is clean.** The overhead room shot → "50% OFF RUGS" → SHOP NOW reads in under two seconds. No mystery about what I'm supposed to do.
- **Loyalty footer is visible.** The "EARN 10% BACK" line is there — I'm a Crate/CB2 rewards member and I actively run the math before clicking. Good that it's present.
- **Free swatch program callout.** The "GET 5 SWATCHES" block in the footer is exactly the kind of friction-reduction I need when buying rugs remotely for a second home I can't always be at. Wayfair doesn't offer this; it's a real differentiator.
- **No render bugs.** Clean layout, images loaded, text wasn't overlapping anything.

## What's weak

- **The better offer is buried.** "UP TO 60% OFF" appears below the fold while the hero leads with 50%. That's a self-inflicted wound — either lead with 60% or don't show it.
- **Fourth CB2 email today, probably eight-plus from the C&B family this week.** Crate & Kids' "Tween dreams" celebrity collab, yesterday's "MORE ADDED TO SALE," the earlier July 2nd "New items added" — my inbox is a CB2 graveyard. This cadence is doing real damage to the brand's perceived urgency.
- **"MORE SAVINGS THIS WAY" row dilutes the rug angle.** If you sent me a rugs email, show me rugs. The three-tile row for furniture, outdoor, and lighting immediately tells me this is just the warehouse sale with a different subject line hat on.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the laziest CTA they could write.** Article ran "Shop 4th of July Sale" in its hero last week — a verb plus a specific event. "SHOP RUGS" would be a 10-second fix that makes the button do work.
- **"More for you" product tiles are too small.** On mobile those thumbnails are unreadable without zooming. If that bench or floating shelf is supposed to convert, I need to actually see it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with 60%, not 50%.** Make the hero honest about the best available discount. "Up to 60% off rugs" is a stronger subject AND a stronger email.
- 2. **Cut the three-tile furniture/outdoor/lighting row from the top half.** Save the category browse for below the rug feature. Let the first scroll be all rugs.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA.** "SHOP RUGS ON SALE" > "SHOP NOW." Five characters of work.
- 4. **Add one featured rug with a name, dimensions, and the sale price.** Wayfair anchors every email to a specific price point; CB2 should do the same to give me something concrete to react to.
- 5. **Hold one send per day during the sale.** Three CB2 emails on July 1st and now this on July 2nd — the cadence is burning goodwill faster than the discount is creating it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New styles added. Free swatches on any rug you're considering.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table-based template with responsive CSS breakpoints. ---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully audit** — HTML source is truncated before body links appear. The following applies to visible font asset URLs:

- **Double-slash paths on font URLs** (confirmed): Multiple `@font-face` declarations use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` (double slash). This will produce a `301` redirect on each font fetch, adding latency and potentially failing in strict email client sandboxes that block redirected resources.
  - Affected: `lineto-brown-light.woff2`, `lineto-brown-regular.woff2`, `GT-Sectra-Display-Regular.woff2`

- **Cross-brand font origin**: Three `@font-face` rules load from `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/` (family `HNW05`). No issue per se, but if the sister brand rotates those assets, CB2 emails break silently.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **`maximum-scale=1` on viewport** (confirmed): `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` — disables user-initiated zoom on mobile. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Should be removed or changed to `maximum-scale=5`.

- **Aggressive global `line-height` reset** (confirmed): `*{ line-height: 100% }` overrides inherited line-height on all elements. This collapses readable body text and can cause overlapping descenders on taller glyphs in Outlook.

- **Mobile breakpoint is narrow** (confirmed): `@media only screen and (max-width: 418px)` with `.mobile { width:414px }`. Devices with viewport width 419–689px (e.g., folded foldables, landscape phones) fall into no responsive bucket — neither the `<418px` mobile styles nor the `>690px` desktop `img` cap applies.

- **`font-display: swap` in email** (confirmed): Declared on three `@font-face` blocks. This property is unsupported in email clients; it is inert but adds dead code. Not a bug, but worth noting for template hygiene.

- **Alt text**: Cannot audit — image tags not present in truncated source.
