# On sale now: pergolas

- Okay, this one actually gets it right for once — "On sale now: pergolas" and the hero image is an actual pergola, over a real outdoor living setup with a sectional, side table, and lantern styling. I'm mid-renovation on the second house and a covered outdoor space is legitimately on my list, so credit where due. But then it does the exact thing Wayfair does in every single one of these — "outdoor fountain goals" turned into a trash can and AC dump on 9/19, "cat litter boxes" on 9/17 — it pivots hard into a full-catalog fire sale: privacy screens, area rugs, living room seating, a mysterious "Moody darks" dutch oven, nursery essentials, vacuums, bar carts, laundry hampers. ## What's working

- Real hero product match — pergola image actually shows a pergola in a styled outdoor room, subject line isn't a bait-and-switch like the fountain email was.
- "LAST CALL" banner up top gives it urgency without being ALL-CAPS screaming.
- Social proof numbers ("4127 Recently Purchased," star ratings) on the mid-catalog grid — that's a trust cue Article and CB2 usually skip.
- Single clear purple CTA button ("Shop All Picks for You") breaks up the grid nicely.

## What's weak

- Subject promises pergolas, but pergolas get one image and then the email turns into the same random full-inventory dump as the fountain and litter box emails — I'm noticing the pattern now, Wayfair does this every time.
- Zero pergola-specific info I'd actually need to click through — no price, no size range, no material/warranty callout, nothing about assembly or shipping timeline for a big structural item like this.
- "Sales You *Almost* Missed" section is nursery items, dressers, vacuums, mattresses, bar carts — I don't have a kid, and this is the third time this month a Wayfair email has assumed I do (litter boxes for a cat I don't own, now a nursery for a kid I don't have).
- No pricing shown on the hero pergola tile at all — CB2's bedroom refresh at least put a real product name and material callout front and center; this just says "On sale now: pergolas »" with no number.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "% off" badge directly on the pergola hero — right now it's the one product in the whole email with zero pricing, which is backwards for the thing you led with.
- 2. Cut the email after "Shop All Picks for You" or replace the random inventory dump with actual outdoor/patio adjacent categories — privacy screens and patio heaters fit the theme, nursery and vacuums don't.
- 3. Add a second pergola-style shot or a size/material chip (aluminum vs. wood, dimensions) since this is a big-ticket item people research before buying — Pottery Barn's desk email at least implied real product depth.
- 4. Drop the nursery and litter-box-style irrelevant segments entirely for known non-parent, non-pet subscribers — this is the third Wayfair email this month assuming a life I don't have.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Wayfair batch-marketing template (Sailthru/ESP-style tokenized links) with widespread missing alt text and multiple insecure tracking pixels; no fatal rendering or compliance blockers identified from available data.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 70 total links were probed (58 skipped per click-likelihood prioritization); of those probed, 7 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by origin, not confirmed broken) and 4 were left unprobed due to a 70s time budget — none confirmed as dead links.
- 20 tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) load over plain HTTP rather than HTTPS, flagged by QA as "Image uses http://" — non-HTTPS resources may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, silently degrading open-tracking accuracy.
- Standard multi-parameter tracking scheme present on all sampled links (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `csnid`, `varid`, `batchid`, `sm`, `refid=MKTEML_122338`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16211`) — consistent across links, no malformed or truncated parameters observed.

- All 20 open-tracking pixel images (`li.wayfair.com/imp?...`) also lack `alt` text (expected/acceptable for 1x1 tracking pixels, not a real accessibility concern on its own, but flagged since combined with HTTP delivery above).
