# 3 DAYS LEFT for up to 30% off The Dining Room Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the dining-event email I wanted, and I've now seen it too many times. The hero is a round pedestal table with upholstered chairs and a clear "up to 30% off" line. Below it is a tidy 2x2 grid for tables, seating, storage and lighting. I'm furnishing two houses, so I can see right away what's on sale. The "3 DAYS LEFT" in the subject is a real deadline, and it earns the open more than the 10/26 Dining Room Event send did.
- Then the email loses focus. The Thanksgiving Hosting Guide block shows up twice. After that come a $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" product row, a Reward Dollars card pitch, an app sweepstakes, a second Design Desk plug and a store locator. It's a long scroll, and the dining offer is in the first screen and a half. I'd click the hero and the grid, then mark the rest as read. I wouldn't forward it. If anything, I'd screenshot the pedestal table for my Pinterest folder.

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer first. "Up to 30% off" sits directly over a dining table photo, with a clear Shop Now button.
- The four-tile grid (tables, seating, storage, lighting) is how I actually shop a dining room. I can go straight to the category I need.
- The CTA under the Hosting Guide, "Shop The Dining Furniture Event", is specific. It says what I'll get.
- The Thanksgiving framing is current. It's late October, so it matches what I'm planning.
- The product row shows prices, including a struck-through price on the cookware set. That makes the discount easy to see.

## What's weak

- The Hosting Guide block repeats almost identically. It reads like a template bug or padding, and it pushes the sale content down the page.
- The Hosting Guide title looks like it collides with the glass-and-table photo behind it. It's hard to read.
- Too many offers compete: up to 30% off, a $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes, a $500 app sweepstakes and 10% back in Reward Dollars. Nothing wrong with any one of them, but together they dilute the dining deadline the subject promised.
- The fine print is a dense block of tiny type. "Up to" and the exclusions are buried there, which weakens my trust in the 30%.
- The "we love these for you" row (cookware, a sofa, a dresser, an air fryer) isn't dining and isn't personalized to me. It's a generic bestseller shelf.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the duplicate Hosting Guide block and move the Design Desk and app sweepstakes below the sale. The email should be the hero, the grid, then one hosting-guide strip. That alone would shorten the page by about a third.
- 2. Put the actual end date next to the hero offer, for example "Ends Thursday 10/29". "3 DAYS LEFT" in the subject needs a date in the body to back it up.
- 3. Show 3-4 specific dining pieces with sale prices instead of the generic bestseller row. Pottery Barn's strip is lazy, but Wayfair's string-lights email put a $16.99 tile first and delivered on the subject. Do the same here with a table price.
- 4. Move the Reward Dollars card pitch up to the offer area. "10% back" is the rewards math I care about, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and drop the all-caps urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dining Room Event ends Thursday: up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tables, chairs & lighting up to 30% off. 3 days left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pedestal tables from $___, plus free shipping on dining. Ends 10/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hosting Thanksgiving? Extra 10% back in Reward Dollars with your Crate card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. Its main confirmed problems are about 30 images with no alt text, a few markup defects, and third-party identifier pixels. The header and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML supplied was truncated, so body-level checks such as footer, unsubscribe link and UTMs could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation not performed:** 62 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations were verified.
- **Third-party pixel:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a67…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels load from rlcdn.com. They carry the same hashed recipient identifier as the sender's own `mi_u` parameter, so that identifier goes to a third party, and they fire five times per open.
- **Per-recipient tokens:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa2…` (7 images), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/639262fa-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are tracking or open pixels. They will fail or be blocked when images are proxied or blocked.
- **Mixed image hosts:** images load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. The two `scene7.com` images share the filename stem `2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary%2D`. These are the only images hosted off the sender's mail domains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 30 images, confirmed by QA):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, for example `ef861136-….jpg`, `38f614bb-….jpg` and `64e9a7b1-….jpg`.
  - One content image at `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
  - Both `scene7.com` Thanksgiving Guide images.
  - The seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, which look like dynamic product or recommendation tiles.
  - The pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry `alt=""`. This is minor, but the QA tool flags them.
  - With images off or a screen reader, the offer content in these images is inaccessible. This is the highest-impact item.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Markup defects:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand inside an XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed and duplicates the `http-equiv` charset.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values.
  - A leftover template placeholder remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Global CSS rules:** `*{max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply to every element, and `html` sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`. This prevents text resizing on iOS. I didn't check rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or QA output.
- The visible HTML is truncated, so I can't rule out tokens in the body or footer.
- Per-recipient `mi_u` values are embedded in image URLs, which is expected for dynamic content, but they are also passed to rlcdn.com (see Section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). One-click support is unconfirmed, with the same caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, an artifact of the AgentMail relay. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Body-level checks not verifiable:** the visible-footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified. All 62 tracking links were skipped and the truncated source doesn't show final URLs. Click-redirect domains conceal the destinations.
- The email promotes "The Dining Room Event" with up to 30% off. Whether the landing page matches that offer is not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/m/1/*.jpg`, `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`, both `scene7.com` Thanksgiving images, `mi…/p/rp/*.png`). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Fix markup: `&amp;` in the `<title>`, remove duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` metas, delete the leftover placeholder comment.
4. Review the five `rlcdn.com` pixels and whether sharing the hashed recipient ID with LiveRamp is intended. Consolidate if redundant.
5. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relay to settle the compliance and authentication warnings.
6. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML. Resolve tracking-link redirects to check UTMs, landing-page alignment, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
