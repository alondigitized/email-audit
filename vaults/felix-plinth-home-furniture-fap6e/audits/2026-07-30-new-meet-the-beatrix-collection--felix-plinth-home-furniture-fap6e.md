## What's weak

- This is nursery and kids' bedroom furniture. I don't have a kid, and nothing in this email speaks to my actual project (primary + second home renovation). Wayfair's "fifteen things before coffee" email at least tried to sell me stuff I could plausibly buy — this one's targeting is off by a life stage.

My inbox now has C&B, CB2, and Crate & Kids all in rotation, and I can't tell anymore which one I'm supposed to care about.
- Bottom of the email pitches me a new store credit card ("the card you love just got better") — I already shop on my highest-rewards card, so a store-specific card pitch is dead weight, not a benefit.
- No swatches, no dimensions, no material callouts anywhere — if I were actually furnishing a nursery, I'd still need to click through blind on fit and finish.
- Nine — NINE — distinct CTAs stacked in one email (bedroom, nursery, fall arrivals x6 subcategories, sale, lookbook, sweepstakes, "we love these," credit card, app download). That's Wayfair-level kitchen-sink energy in an email that should've been one clean collection launch.
