# Your most beautiful holidays yet start with The Design Desk →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Crate & Barrel email today. I opened it, and it was the Design Desk pitch I already half-saw this morning in "Let's nail your holiday aesthetic →." It's the same free design appointment with new hero copy. I'm renovating two houses, so a free designer is the one thing in this inbox that's relevant to me. I just don't need it pitched twice in one day.
- The email is long and soft. A tiny "Thanksgiving dining room event, up to 30% off" strip sits above the logo hero, and that's the only price hook. Below it are four or five "Book Your Free Appointment" buttons, a $5,000 sweepstakes, an Affirm banner, and a product grid. The product grid is where I'd actually click. The Breville Smart Oven and the white plates are fine, but they don't connect to any room or project. Wayfair would have shown me the price and the badge right at the top.
- Verdict: I'd save it to the Pinterest folder for the room photography. I'd click once, on the dining room shot. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The room photography is gorgeous. The dining room with the greenery and the red pillow set is exactly the Pinterest-folder material I save.
- "Book Your Free Appointment" is an unambiguous CTA, and a free designer fits my two-house situation.
- The "more ways The Design Desk can help" 2x2 grid is scannable. Entryway, mantel, tablescape and refresh are each concrete ideas.
- The Crate Card block mentions earning back in Reward Dollars. That's the rewards math I care about, even though it's buried low.
- The 100% free line and the $5,000 sweepstakes give the appointment an extra reason to act.

## What's weak

- The Design Desk CTA repeats at least five times: the hero, the 2x2 grid, the sweepstakes, the app block and the closer. It reads like nagging.
- The 30% off dining event is a thin strip above the fold, and the headline never mentions it. The only real discount is easy to miss.
- It's the fifth Crate & Barrel send today, and it overlaps with this morning's "Let's nail your holiday aesthetic." That's the repeat-lamp annoyance, but from one brand.
- The Affirm "pay later" banner and the app download block feel like filler. Neither helps me decide on a sofa.
- The "we love these for you" product grid has no tie to the Design Desk story or to any swatch or sample program, and there's no shipping or lead-time info for furniture.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the CTA repeats to two and merge them with this morning's design-consult send. One appointment email per week is plenty.
- 2. Put the 30% off dining offer into the headline and the preheader, and give it a real deadline.
- 3. Replace the generic product grid with items from the featured rooms ("shop this room"), with prices.
- 4. Add swatch and sample info plus furniture lead times near the appointment CTA, since that's what I'd actually ask a designer about.
- 5. Drop the Affirm and app blocks, or move them below the footer links.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free designer + up to 30% off dining, through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Book your free holiday design consult`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 30% off dining furniture, plus a free 1:1 with a designer. Pick a time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Swatches, a room plan and a $5,000 giveaway. Book in 2 minutes.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel "The Design Desk" email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a mobile media-query layer. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (31 images flagged) and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The header-related warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as sender faults. The HTML is truncated, so body links, footer and unsubscribe markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **No broken or malformed links are confirmed.** The visible source is truncated before the body links.
- **Tracking pixels are present.** These are the only items in this section that need action, and only if you care about tracker footprint:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/cfbcaf45-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - 5 × `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5`
- **The five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels are third-party.** `rlcdn.com` is LiveRamp's domain. They are identical except for `n=1..5`, so the same ID sync fires five times, which adds redundant third-party requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **31 images are missing `alt` text**, per QA. They fall into three groups:
  - **17 content images** on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`. These include `.jpg`, `.png` and animated `.gif` files such as `01659cba-…gif` and `a610131e-…gif`. With images off or a screen reader, these blocks have no text equivalent. If any are CTAs or headlines baked into images, that content is lost.
  - **7 images** on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`. These look like dynamic recommendation images, which would make the missing alt text more significant.
  - **7 tracking pixels** (the `o.gif`, `dv.` and `rlcdn` URLs above). These should use `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom.** This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{…}` and `body`.** This blocks text resizing in WebKit clients, a minor accessibility issue.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.** It is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Duplicate and redundant head tags.**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under XHTML.
  - These are harmless in practice but add noise.
- **A leftover template placeholder is in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is an HTML comment, so it doesn't render, but it shows the template wasn't cleaned up.
- **Positives:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - Outlook VML and MSO conditionals are included.
  - `color-scheme` is pinned to light.
  - A responsive breakpoint exists at 460px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` are visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` parameters are resolved per-recipient identifiers, not leaked tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers.
  - Treat this as unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender defect.
  - A large retailer on a dedicated `mail.` subdomain very likely sends them. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA again attributes this to the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link are unverified.** The footer is outside the truncated HTML. I can't confirm or deny CAN-SPAM body requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 66 tracking links route through click-redirect domains and weren't resolved. The source is truncated before the anchors, so I can't assess UTM parameters or the landing page. Nothing is flagged as broken.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text to all content and recommendation images** (17 on `image.mail…/m/…` and 7 on `mi…/p/rp/…`). Use `alt=""` on the 7 tracking pixels. This is the highest-value fix and would lift the 57% QA pass rate.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.** Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
3. **Escape the `&` in `<title>`** (`Crate &amp; Barrel`) and make the title descriptive.
4. **Deduplicate the head.** Keep one charset declaration and one `format-detection` meta, and delete the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder.
5. **Consolidate the five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels** into one request if the tag manager allows.
6. **Re-run the audit** on a full, untruncated message, ideally with raw headers. That would confirm:
   - `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
   - `Authentication-Results`
   - the footer unsubscribe link and postal address
   - UTM parameters on the destination URLs, via a redirect-following probe of the 66 tracking links
