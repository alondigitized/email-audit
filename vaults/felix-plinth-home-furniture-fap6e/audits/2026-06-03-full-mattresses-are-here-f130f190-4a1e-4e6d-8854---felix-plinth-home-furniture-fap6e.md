# FULL MATTRESSES. ARE. HERE.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-three. That's the Wayfair count in my inbox now, and before I say anything else I want to put that number on the table because it's the lens through which I'm reading every word of this email. I've had warehouse clearout, bath mats, outdoor picks, outdoor rugs — all inside roughly 36 hours — and now "FULL MATTRESSES. ARE. HERE." lands on June 3rd. I'm renovating two houses at the same time. By all rights, I should care about a mattress email. Wayfair has just burned through all the goodwill that should have bought them that care.
- The subject line is doing one thing and the email is doing something else entirely. "FULL MATTRESSES. ARE. HERE." — punchy, specific, declarative — I'll give it that. And then I open it and land on "BIG-TIME BARGAINS" as the hero with a product grid that includes rugs, ceiling fans, outdoor furniture, and what I think might be a mattress somewhere in the first row. That bait-and-switch move is exactly what makes me stop trusting Wayfair's targeting. Compare this directly to CB2's "New collab. Only on the app." from yesterday — that email said one thing, delivered one thing, scored an 8. Article's kitchen email this week had a specific promise in the hero. This one has all-caps amplitude and zero signal.
- I'd mark it read. Maybe reflex-tap "Open Box Deals" on the way by. I'm not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- **The subject line's punctuation rhythm.** "FULL. MATTRESSES. ARE. HERE." is genuinely scroll-stopping on mobile — the period breaks create staccato urgency and it reads differently from every other send this week.
- **"Open Box Deals →" is the one CTA worth keeping.** Verb, noun, directional arrow — that's the entire formula, and it's the only place in this email where I feel like Wayfair knows what it's asking me to do.
- **Star ratings on product tiles.** Social proof embedded in the grid matters for tactile categories. I notice it and it adds credibility.
- **Wayfair Rewards visible at the bottom.** I run my purchases through a rewards card and I track Wayfair points. Good to see it, even buried.
- **No render bugs.** Layout held, images loaded. That's the floor, not the ceiling — but at this cadence it's worth noting when something doesn't break.

## What's weak

- **Subject says mattresses, body says BIG-TIME BARGAINS.** I opened for mattresses and landed in a general bargain dump. That's a bait-and-switch and it costs trust every time it happens.
- **"BIG-TIME BARGAINS" as the hero copy is doing no work.** No percentage, no dollar anchor, no category hook — just maximum amplitude with no information. Article put a specific product and a specific promise in its hero. This one has all caps and vibes.
- **The product grid is a throw-everything approach.** Rugs, fans, outdoor furniture, lamps, what I think are mattresses — there's no editorial point of view. CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" at least had an aesthetic organizing principle. This has none.
- **Five emails in 36 hours.** My thumb has developed a swipe reflex before my eyes have processed the sender name. The cadence is destroying whatever targeting intelligence Wayfair has.
- **No size, firmness, or spec callout anywhere.** The subject promised a mattress email. I need "full size," "plush vs firm," "open-box inventory count." None of that is visible.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make this email actually about mattresses.** If the subject says mattresses, the hero should show mattresses — with size availability, firmness options, and a price anchor. Not a general bargains roundup.
- 2. **Anchor the hero with a specific offer.** "Open-box full mattresses from $299" or "Up to 40% off this week only" — something that earns the subject line rather than hiding behind BIG-TIME BARGAINS.
- 3. **Cut the product grid to one focused cluster.** Two or three mattress options, one secondary category max. The scatter-shot approach trains me to stop reading.
- 4. **Throttle the cadence.** One email per day maximum. At this rate I'll unsubscribe before I finish the renovation.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Full mattresses in stock — open box from $X, free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The full mattress drop you've been waiting for`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plush, firm, hybrid — all sizes, all at clearance pricing through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Open box options too. Compare firmness and get it by next week.`
