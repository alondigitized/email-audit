# Don't miss: Up to 60% off more clearance items!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel Kids email since 10/31, and I still don't have a kid. The hero says "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off" over a blurry autumn photo, and right under it the email repeats the "20% off top play furniture, today only" banner from the send earlier today. I got that one this morning, so the repeat is wasted space.
- The body is a stack of unrelated asks: a clearance banner, the app download, the DesignDesk $5000 sweepstakes, the "we love these for you" product grid, the Crate card, the app again, and DesignDesk again. It reads like a catch-all. I'm renovating two houses, so the Jenny Lind dresser and the nursery glider caught my eye for a second. That's a stretch for me, though. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The headline "up to 60% off" is large and legible, with a clear white "Shop Clearance" pill under it.
- The category buttons (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Decor & Lighting) in the terracotta block are easy to tap on a phone.
- Some product tiles show real before/after pricing, like the pumpkin treat bag at $14.97. That's an honest offer.
- The DesignDesk free design appointment is the one thing relevant to a two-house renovator. It's free and bookable.
- The brand look is consistent and trustworthy. It looks like Crate, not a knockoff.

## What's weak

- The "20% off play furniture, today only" message shows up twice, and I already got it as its own email this morning.
- The hero photo is a soft, blurry fall shot, and the white text sits over a busy background. The product isn't shown.
- Too many competing CTAs: Shop Clearance, Download Our App, Enter Now, Learn More, Download Now, Book a Free Appointment. Nothing leads.
- The "we love these for you" grid is baby gear (car seats, strollers, nursery glider). That's wrong for a 36-year-old with no kid, and "for you" is a stretch.
- The "Enter Now" button is pale grey on blue-grey and barely reads as a button. The $5000 sweepstakes also needs a design appointment to enter, and the fine print is tiny and buried.
- The product list prices (strollers at $350 and $1,050) don't show the clearance discount, so "60% off" isn't visible where I'd buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one offer. Lead with the clearance hero, show a real product with a visible markdown, and drop the repeated play furniture banner. Don't send a second email on the same day with the same banner.
- 2. Segment the grid. Show non-baby clearance (dressers, rugs, lighting) to people with no kid signals. Show me the Jenny Lind dresser at its clearance price, not a car seat.
- 3. Cap it at one primary CTA. Give the DesignDesk sweepstakes its own send, with a real button.
- 4. Use a sharper hero image. Pottery Barn's maroon "Extra 20% off" strip at least has a clear banner. Wayfair's "Black Friday Pop-Up Starts Now" is loud but unmistakable. Here the offer gets lost in the photo.
- 5. Cap the cadence. CB2 ran the same Warehouse Sale across 10/30, 10/31 and now. Crate Kids is doing the same thing, and the whole C&B family is on my snooze list.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Marigold/Sailthru-style template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com` tracking, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `s7d5.scene7.com` image hosts). It has no hard QA failures, only 3 warning categories (57% pass rate). The main findings are header visibility gaps, missing alt text on 27 images, and accessibility-hostile viewport and text-size CSS. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link targets could not be fully inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were not HTTP-probed** because they go through click-redirect domains. Link health and final destinations are unverified.
- **Five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a67…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels** load a third-party (LiveRamp) pixel. Its `s=` value matches the `mi_u=` subscriber token on the first-party pixels, so a recipient-linked hash is passed to a third party. This is not a defect, but it is worth disclosing in the privacy policy.
- **Six identical first-party pixels** (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`) are redundant. Together with `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e7e3e52c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`, that makes 13 tracking pixels in total, which adds weight and extra requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 27 images.**
  - 14 are content or spacer images, for example `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `…/m/1/038b0feb-5641-4d84-9f6b-529ab47352b9.jpg`, `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_100s` (scene7) and `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`.
  - 13 are tracking pixels (`f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv…/e7e3e52c-…`).
  - If the content images lack alt text, the clearance offers are invisible with images off and to screen readers.
  - The pixels and spacers need `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`. The check may not distinguish an absent `alt` from an empty one, so confirm in the full source.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}`** and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable text resizing. They also conflict with the later `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` rule.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags** appear three times with differing values: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`. Behavior is undefined when they conflict.
- **Leftover template placeholder comment** in the head: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is cosmetic only.
- **Responsive and Outlook handling is present:** a `max-width:460px` breakpoint, MSO conditional comments and `lang="en"`. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source, though the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN), and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Check the raw message at the original receiving mailbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status cannot be determined from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment is unverified.
- **The footer and physical-address block are outside the truncated source.** CAN-SPAM unsubscribe-link and postal-address checks could not be performed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source, and the 68 tracked links were not resolved. The only visible parameters are the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` pixel identifiers.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the unsubscribe headers and authentication** against the raw original message. Ensure `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC align on `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to the 14 content images. Mark spacers and pixels `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and drop the `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
4. **Escape the title** as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and align it with the subject line.
5. **Consolidate the six duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels** into one and merge the `format-detection` metas.
6. **Resolve the 68 redirect links** with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations and UTMs. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, merge tokens and landing-page alignment.
