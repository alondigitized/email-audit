# See our new & exclusive Nuna clothing collection!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel send today, and the second from the Kids sender. My gut said mark it read. I'm renovating two houses, not dressing a baby. A cashmere-and-Supima clothing line from Nuna has nothing to do with sofas, rugs or lighting. Earlier today the 8/10 sectional email ("Your living room makeover starts now with up to 20% off") actually spoke to me. This one spends its whole 11,000-pixel length on baby sweaters and strollers.
- To be fair, the layout is tidy. It runs hero, then fabric story, then babies, then toddlers, then strollers, in clean tan and cream blocks with a clear "Shop Now" under each. The tiny "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 20% off" strip is the only thing that touches my world. It's buried in a thin banner above the hero and reads like an afterthought.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it to a friend. If I had a friend with a newborn, maybe, but nothing about this is aimed at me.

## What's working

- The hero reads "new & exclusive: Nuna Wardrobe," so the one idea of the send is obvious right away.
- The sections are clean and repeatable, with "made from ultra-fine cashmere" and "made from 100% Supima cotton" each getting its own block and a real fabric story.
- The "Shop Now" buttons sit under every block. Nothing looks broken, overlapped or missing.
- The Crate Card and TheDesignDesk "Book a Free Appointment" blocks near the bottom are a good rewards and showroom cue. They're the only parts I'd actually use.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows prices, so at least there's something concrete to scan.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is aimed at a 36-year-old furnishing two houses. The whole email is nursery and kids' apparel, plus a couple of strollers.
- The 20% off is a thin strip at the very top. The hero itself has no offer, so the discount doesn't carry the email.
- It's long. There are five or six near-identical "shop" blocks before the product grid, so by the time I scroll to the "$5000 giveaway" I've stopped caring.
- The cadence is bad. Five C&B emails in one day, plus CB2 sending roughly fourteen in five days, trains me to ignore the whole family.
- The Crate Card, app and design-appointment blocks are tacked on at the end, after the stroller and giveaway modules, with no connection to the Nuna story.
- The scene images and small type don't invite a tap. The captions under the product tiles are tiny on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this. Send the Nuna drop to people with kid or registry signals, and keep it away from me. That one change lifts it more than any creative tweak.
- 2. If it has to go wide, move "Up to 20% off" into the hero. Pottery Barn Design Services leads with a clear free-design offer and a Design Crew badge. Wayfair at least puts "$134 off" on the product.
- 3. Cut it to three modules: hero, one best-seller grid, and the card or app strip. Article's "Fall is for full tables" story email works because it's one idea, not seven.
- 4. Put the Crate Card and design-appointment strip higher, since that's the rewards math I'd care about.
- 5. Give the CTA a real verb and noun, like "Shop cashmere sets," instead of a generic "Shop Now."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO/VML fallbacks. It has a high volume of images without alt text (about 38 flagged), and the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided, and the HTML was truncated, so footer and link-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 links not probed.** QA skipped them because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` requests (LiveRamp) fire in addition to the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 copies), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a5080397-…`. Together these are 13 tracking images. The 6 copies of the same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` URL look like a template loop emitting duplicates rather than distinct measurements.
- **Recipient identifiers in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa278…` and `s=56aaa278…` values are per-recipient hashes embedded in pixel URLs. This is not a defect, but it is the personalization data sent to third parties.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 38 images.** Of these, 25 are content or layout images:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (jpg/gif, about 20), for example `86f61730-…jpg` and `f1db6dd8-…gif`.
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - Scene7 `2026%5F0311%5FCBK%5FNunaWardrobe%5F1.gif` and `…%5F4.gif`.
  - Scene7 `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  
  The other 13 are tracking pixels (listed above). In clients that block images, the content images will render blank with no fallback text.
- **Spacer and pixel images** should carry an explicit `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template cruft.** The unresolved authoring comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** There are three `format-detection` metas, two of them redundant, and both `http-equiv` and `charset` declarations.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid XHTML, which the doctype claims to be.
- **Conflicting CSS.** `@media (min-width:768px) img{max-width:600px}` and `(min-width:640px) img{max-width:640px}` overlap. The later rule wins for ≥768px, so the 600px cap is overridden.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** forces centering on any paragraph without an inline override.
