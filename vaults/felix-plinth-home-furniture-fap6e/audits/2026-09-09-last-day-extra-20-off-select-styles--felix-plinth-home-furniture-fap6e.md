- "Free Design Services" and "Design Crew" block is a nice touch but generic — no mention of swatches, samples, or showroom integration, which is what I'd actually need mid-renovation on two houses.
- No urgency or exclusivity cue beyond "Labor Day Sale" — feels stale for a September 9th send, same complaint I had about the 2026-09-08 "48 Hours Left" email.
