## What's weak

- I've now seen this near-identical velvet-chair-on-green hero from CB2 twice in three days (8/31 "Exclusive designs on sale for Labor Day" was the same chair-hero-clean-CTA formula). It's blending into wallpaper.

- This is CB2's Nth email hitting the same "up to 50% off" ceiling I've seen from Pottery Barn and Article this week too — the whole category is starting to sound identical.

- 2. Cut the send frequency. Two near-identical chair-hero emails in 72 hours is CB2's real problem, not the creative.
- 3. Simplify the rewards card banner — just say "Get 20% back in Reward Dollars," drop the crossed-out 10% since it reads as a mistake, not a win.
