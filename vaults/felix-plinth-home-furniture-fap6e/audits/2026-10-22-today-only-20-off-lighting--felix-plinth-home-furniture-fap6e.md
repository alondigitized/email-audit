# TODAY ONLY: 20% off lighting!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Crate & Barrel send in two days that's actually about my house. I've been snoozing the Kids sender (the Doona travel system and the nursery glider), and this one is main-brand lighting. I have two houses mid-renovation and fixtures are on both lists, so I paid attention. Then I hit the hero. It's a phone mockup, and the offer is "now in our app: 20% off lighting, today only!" So the deal is app-gated, and I'm not sure the web-order discount applies at all. That's annoying.
- The body is a mixed bag. The top strip pushes the Thanksgiving dining room event at up to 30% off, then the lighting hero, then a fireplace and stockings shot for "holiday-ready styles," then a rug and accent furniture block at up to 20% off. The lighting I came for (floor lamp, pendants, chandelier) is buried in a "we love these for you" grid way down the page. I'd click through to the lighting grid, but only if I could confirm the discount applies on desktop.
- Would I forward it? No. Would I save it to my lighting inspo folder? Maybe the chandelier. Net, it's a decent email with a muddled offer.

## What's working

- Lighting is my category, and the "today only" deadline is credible and concrete.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows real prices with struck-through originals, like the Lia Pendant at $343.20. I can do the rewards math in my head.
- The Reward Dollars card block ("Get 10% back in Reward Dollars across all our design forward brands") speaks to how I buy, with the highest-rewards card.
- The TheDesignDesk "100% free" appointment block is a real draw for a two-house renovation, same as Article's free interior design send.
- The chandelier, pendant and lamp photography is clean and the products are easy to read.

## What's weak

- The hero is a phone mockup with an app-download button. The offer is gated behind the app, and I came for lighting, not an install.
- Three offers compete: the 30% Thanksgiving dining event, 20% lighting, and up to 20% accent chairs and rugs. No single hierarchy.
- The fireplace and stockings block has nothing to do with the lighting subject. It's a bait-and-switch feel.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are generic, and the fine print at the bottom is tiny. I can't tell whether "20% off" has exclusions without squinting.
- It's another C&B-family send. I've seen the Kids sends all day, and this is the second app push I've gotten from them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the lighting products, not a phone mockup. Put the lamp grid at the top with the 20% badge.
- 2. Say plainly whether the offer is app-only. If it works online with a code, give me the code.
- 3. Cut the competing offers (the dining event, rugs, stockings) from a lighting-themed send.
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop 20% off lighting."
- 5. Move the fine print exclusions up near the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off pendants & lamps, today only`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Chandeliers, pendants and floor lamps, priced for today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The main findings are about 20 images flagged for missing alt text, an unverified unsubscribe and authentication state, and some template hygiene problems in the `<head>`. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **52 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Redirect resolution, destination status and UTM handling are therefore unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are the same pixel with only the counter changed. This adds third-party requests and load time with no apparent benefit.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs.** Many image URLs carry `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…`. This looks like a hashed subscriber ID. The same hash prefix appears as `s=` in the `rlcdn.com` pixels, so the identifier is shared with a third-party domain.
- **Open-tracking pixels.**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/df1f12ff-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images with no alt text (QA warning).** The check can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from `alt=""`.
  - **Likely content images (about 14):**
    - 6 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…` (`bbf6f9d4-…png`, `56be7a63-…jpg`, `18887a19-…jpg`, `91daf28b-…gif`, `5c7b78d8-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`).
    - 7 dynamic product-recommendation images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede`, `789e7381ff44be74`, `5ec241ce0b852c66`, `00423cc9007b550c`, `f5ca909404423879`, `45e8c84f5a9185ff`, `18f126218cc222ec`).
    - If these are the promo or product imagery, the image-blocked experience is empty.
  - **Tracking pixels:** `448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif`, and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixel. They should carry `alt=""`.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` stops zooming on some mobile clients.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on `*` and `body`, which blocks user text-size scaling in iOS Mail.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
  - `<meta charset>` is duplicated alongside `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Conflicting image sizing.** `img {max-width:600px}` (min-width 768px) is overridden by `img {max-width:640px}` (min-width 640px), so the 600px rule is dead.
