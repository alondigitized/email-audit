## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is a Marketing Cloud-style (Movable Ink) XHTML table layout with a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, and an MSO fallback. The automated QA scored it 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 35 images) and missing unsubscribe and authentication headers in the captured data. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link markup were not available to verify.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA skipped the HTTP probe on 68 tracking links because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- Movable Ink dynamic-content hosts are in use: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8bc0a004-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These resolve per recipient at open time. If the Movable Ink service is slow or unavailable, the images fail or render blank.
- There are five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels. This is a third-party (LiveRamp/RLCDN) tracking pixel that fires 5 times. The `s=` parameter is a long hash identifier, which is a privacy and compliance consideration (see section 5). Some clients and privacy tools block this host.
- `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` is an open-tracking pixel. Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens from it.
- The `mi_u` and `s=` values are the same recipient-specific hash. Forwarding the email exposes that identifier.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (about 35 images, confirmed by QA).** Content images lack `alt` on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif`, plus `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. Examples: `b85bfaed-….gif`, `1b45d1b2-….jpg`, `2f7ba79b-….jpg`, and so on through `971aa10f-….jpg`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, those blocks have no text equivalent. Since the visible content is image-based, it is lost entirely.
- The Movable Ink product images (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, 7 images) also lack `alt`.
- Tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) lack `alt`. These should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. Without `alt`, some clients show a broken-image icon or a filename.
- `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility issue.
- The `<title>` contains an unescaped ampersand: `Crate & Barrel` (should be `&amp;`). The document is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, where this is invalid.
- Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` and again as separate `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`. This is redundant, not harmful.
- There is a leftover template placeholder comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (also a typo). It is harmless but unclean.
- The CSS rule `*{ ... line-height: 100% }` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` are global overrides that may cause unexpected spacing and alignment.
- The `img {max-width: 600px}` and `img {max-width: 640px}` media queries conflict (the 640px rule wins at ≥768px, since it's later and also matches).
- `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">` means no dark-mode support. This is a deliberate choice, not an error.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the truncated source. The rest of the body could not be checked.
- Personalization is carried in the Movable Ink `mi_u` and `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` URL parameters, which are rendered as resolved values.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). The note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this may be a capture artifact. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for senders above 5,000 messages a day. Unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, and the images and tracking use the same parent domain, which is consistent. This could not be validated here.
- The in-body unsubscribe link, physical postal address and footer were not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements cannot be confirmed either way.
- The `rlcdn.com` pixel (LiveRamp) carries a hashed identifier to a third party. Depending on the brand's privacy disclosures, this may need CCPA/GDPR consideration. This is a risk to review, not a confirmed violation.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- UTM parameters could not be verified. All 68 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated source contains none of the `<a href>` values.
- The links go through click-redirect domains, so final landing URLs and any UTM or campaign parameters are not visible. Whether the landing pages match the "cozy-season kitchen" subject is not verifiable.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image (the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/...` set and the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` product images). Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on all tracking pixels.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message at the sending MTA, not only through the AgentMail relay. Also check SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the original headers.
3. Reduce the `rlcdn.com` pixel to one call, or remove it if it isn't needed. Five identical-host pixel requests add load and third-party exposure.
4. Escape the title ampersand (`Crate &amp; Barrel`) and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
5. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the leftover placeholder comment. Resolve the conflicting `img max-width` media queries.
6. For the next audit, capture the full HTML (the footer, the `<a href>` values and the UTM parameters) and resolve a sample of the redirect links, so the link, tracking and compliance sections can be checked properly.
