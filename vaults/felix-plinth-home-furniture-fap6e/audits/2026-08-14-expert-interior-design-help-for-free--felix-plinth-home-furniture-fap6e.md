# Expert interior design help, for FREE
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's actually about design *services*, not another sale — "RSVP for Your Free Design Services," a Design Crew sweepstakes for a $1,500 gift card, plus a "Decorating for Halloween" in-store class on August 23. That's a different beat than the last four Pottery Barn emails I've opened this week, and given we're mid-renovation on the second home, a free design consult is genuinely something I'd consider — my wife's already fielding quotes from a designer for the primary, so "free" is the magic word here.
- The lifestyle shots are doing real work — that dark wood cabin-lodge living room with the charcoal sectional and antler chandelier is exactly the "rich brown and warm charcoal" fall palette pitch, and it actually looks like a room I'd want in the second home, not the beach house. But then it dissolves into the standard "Recommended Just For You" grid — curtain rods, a bed, sheets, a mattress — which feels bolted on, like two different emails got merged.
- Would I click? Maybe on "Book a Free Appointment" just to see what it actually involves — a real designer or an upsell funnel. Wouldn't forward to my wife, but I'd screenshot the Halloween class date since we're local to a store. Not archiving immediately, which for Pottery Barn this week is already a win.

## What's working

- "Free Design Services" + a $1,500 gift card sweepstakes is a real, distinct offer — not the fourth restatement of "in stock & on sale."
- The lodge-style living room hero photo matches my actual renovation aesthetic, not generic staged catalog beige.
- "In your home, virtually, or in-store" gives me three ways to take the offer, which respects that I don't live near a flagship store necessarily.
- The Halloween in-store class has a specific date and time (August 23, 10AM) — concrete, not vague "join us soon."

## What's weak

- The email runs long — hero, then a full styled-room photo dump, then a second CTA, then a class promo, then a six-item product grid, then app download, then rewards card pitch. By the time I hit "Recommended Just For You" I've mentally checked out.
- Zero mention of design-service specifics that'd close the deal for me — no "how long does a consult take," no swatch/sample program, no showroom tie-in, just "styling tips, room refreshes, total makeovers, and more."
- The product grid at the bottom (curtain rods, sheets, a mattress) has nothing to do with the design-services hook up top — feels like a generic module tacked on because the template requires one.
- "Legal Rock 'N Roll" fine print blob at the bottom (sweepstakes rules, rewards card disclosures) is the usual wall of gray text nobody reads, but it does confirm this is a real sweepstakes with real odds/eligibility, which is at least honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — kill the six-item recommended-products grid entirely. It undermines the "we're a design service, not a sales feed" positioning of the top two-thirds.
- 2. Add one line of proof near the CTA — "average consult takes 30 minutes" or "150,000 rooms styled" — something that de-risks booking an appointment with a stranger.
- 3. Move the Halloween class up near the top CTA since it's the most concrete, dated, local action in the whole email — right now it's buried mid-scroll.
- 4. Tie the design-service CTA to the renovation-in-progress angle directly: "Mid-reno? Get a free plan for the room you're stuck on" — speaks straight to someone like me instead of generic "ready to get started?"

# Technical Audit: "Expert interior design help, for FREE" (Pottery Barn Design Services)

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard SFMC-generated marketing HTML with a functional tracking/click layer, but it ships broken/leftover ampScript-adjacent debug code, several unauthenticated header signals, and widespread missing accessibility attributes.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 51 tracking/click-redirect links detected (`click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...`), consistent with SFMC (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) click-tracking wrappers — not individually probed by QA (expected/by design for redirect domains).
- Third-party pixel/beacon tags present: `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?...`, `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (AOL/Verizon Media ad retargeting pixels), and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=<recipient-email>` — this last one embeds the recipient's raw email address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) in a plaintext query string on an open-tracking pixel, which is a PII-in-URL exposure pattern (logged in server/proxy logs, referrer headers, etc.).
- `coreemail.html?email=<recipient-email>` — same pattern: full recipient email passed in a clear-text query parameter.
- Stray malformed `<style>` block contains raw PHP-style `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`) inside the CSS `<style>` tag — these are inert as CSS (browsers will ignore/fail to parse them) but indicate leftover/misplaced server-side code accidentally pasted into the template. Not functionally harmful to rendering but is dead/broken code that should be removed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 28 images missing `alt` text across hero slices, footer slices, product images, and even a 1x1 tracking pixel/spacer (`spacer_0.gif`) — impacts screen-reader users on all primary content images (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_09.jpg`, product shots, footer slices_01–18).
- Two images served over plain `http://` instead of `https://`: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed content risk; many mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) will block or flag non-HTTPS image sources.
- Preheader text includes a promotional line ("Book for a chance to win $1500!") padded with a long block of zero-width joiner characters (`͏`) and soft hyphens (`­`) for length-hiding — a standard technique, functioning as intended, no issue.
- No native `<img alt="">` fallback strategy for image-blocked clients — combined with the missing alt attributes, recipients with images disabled will see a table of blank content with no textual context (no accessibility issue found for text areas; not evaluated for content copy per scope).

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`) found in the truncated source — personalization appears to be handled via the `sv_sveme`/`email` query params on tracking/pixel URLs rather than inline body merge fields.
- The leading `<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot --> <!-- end dynamic ampScript slot -->` comment markers are present but empty — either the dynamic content block failed to populate for this recipient, or this is a static/fallback send with the ampScript slot intentionally left blank. Cannot confirm intent from source alone; flagging as worth verifying against a populated/test send.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm from HTML body alone (unsubscribe footer link is likely below the truncation point).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat as above.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — again, may be a relay-capture limitation rather than an actual authentication failure by Pottery Barn's sending domain (`e.potterybarn.com`).
- **Recommendation**: these three findings should be treated as inconclusive pending a raw-header capture outside the AgentMail relay, not as confirmed compliance violations.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All primary CTA and hero links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` (opaque SFMC tokenized redirect, no visible UTM params) rather than direct `potterybarn.com` URLs with UTM parameters — this is standard for SFMC click-tracking but means UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified without following the redirect chain (out of scope for static HTML analysis).
- Header logo click-through and hero image click-throughs use the same `click.e.potterybarn.com` wrapper pattern consistently — no broken or malformed href attributes found in the visible source.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Remove the orphaned PHP `header(...)` statements sitting inside the `<style>` block — dead code with no functional effect but signals a template hygiene issue.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 28 flagged images, or at minimum `alt=""` for purely decorative slices to satisfy accessibility linting explicitly (currently attribute is absent, not empty).
3. Upgrade the two `http://` image sources to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in Outlook/Gmail.
4. Review whether raw recipient email addresses need to be passed in cleartext query params (`sv_sveme=`, `?email=`) on the open-pixel and coreemail links — consider hashing/tokenizing if this is sent over non-TLS-guaranteed paths or logged by third parties.
5. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` against raw SMTP headers captured outside the AgentMail relay before treating as compliance gaps.
