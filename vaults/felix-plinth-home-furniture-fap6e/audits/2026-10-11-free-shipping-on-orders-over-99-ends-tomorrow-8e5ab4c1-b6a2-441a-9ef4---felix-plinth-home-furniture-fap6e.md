# Free shipping on orders over $99 ends TOMORROW!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Crate & Barrel send today that matches my lists. It's the main brand, not Kids, and the sectional is what I'm shopping for the second house. My gut reaction was still "wait, which deadline is it?" The subject sells free shipping over $99 ending tomorrow. The first thing I see is a green banner saying "final weekend: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals." Then a second headline says "ends tomorrow: free shipping on orders over $99!" Two sales, two urgency claims, and no clear winner.
- I'm also fatigued. The earlier "Ends tomorrow: Free shipping on orders $99+" and "Final weekend! Up to 50% off bedroom & nursery" were both in my inbox today, and this one repeats the same deadline. The sectional gets a real product tile with a sale price, which I appreciate. The "up to 20%" headline is weak, though. Pottery Barn is running "Up to 70% off" right now.
- Would I click? Yes, on the sofa tile, probably once, and I'd add it to a list before buying. Would I forward it? No. I'd save it to my inspo folder, and only for the sectional.

## What's working

- The sectional tile is the first thing aimed at my renovation. A named product with a visible sale price beats a generic "shop sofas."
- "Ends tomorrow" free shipping is a credible deadline. Shipping cost matters on big orders.
- The six shop tiles (Tabletop, Kitchen, Decor, Bedding, Halloween, Thanksgiving) are easy to tap. "Shop All Free Shipping" is a clear anchor button.
- The 10% back in Reward Dollars card block speaks to my rewards-math habit.
- TheDesignDesk "100% free" appointment fits a two-house project. It's the only showroom-adjacent content, and it's useful.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with shipping but the hero leads with a sofa discount. I read the subject, then the banner, and the two don't match.
- "Up to 20% off… select styles" is soft. The fine print is dense and tiny. I can't tell at a glance whether the sectional is in the 20%.
- There are too many "Shop Now" buttons. The holiday collection, the coffee maker and the app download each compete with the sofa.
- The Caraway coffee maker preorder, the glassware row and the app/$500 giveaway feel like filler. None of it helps with sofas, lighting or rugs.
- There's no swatch or sample link, no delivery window for furniture, and no color-palette guidance. All of those matter on a $4k+ sofa.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Either lead with sofas and sectionals with the shipping offer as a subhead, or the reverse. Make the subject match the hero.
- 2. Move the sectional tile above the fold. Add fabric options, a "free swatches" link and an estimated delivery window.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific verbs and nouns, like "Shop sectionals" or "Order free swatches."
- 4. Cut the glassware row and the coffee maker, or segment them to buyers who have bought tabletop.
- 5. Space out the sends. Four or five in two days teaches me to skim.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sectionals up to 20% off, shipping free through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: free shipping + sofa savings end tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bestselling sofas and sectionals, plus free shipping on $99+. Swatches ship free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final weekend on sofas, rugs and lighting. Free shipping ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a mobile breakpoint at 460px, built on a Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) and image-CDN stack. QA flagged 3 warnings. Most are header-capture gaps from the AgentMail relay, plus about 27 images without alt text. The HTML supplied is truncated, so the footer, body links and unsubscribe markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8&n=1` repeats with `n=1` through `n=5`. This is LiveRamp, and the `s=` value starts with the same hash as the `mi_u=56aaa278…` parameter on the Movable Ink URLs. Five identical calls with only `n` changing look redundant, and a recipient-linked hash is being sent to a third party.
- **Movable Ink pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/52cfade4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and dynamic-content trackers. They are expected, but they will fail if images are blocked.
- No broken links were reported. None could be confirmed either, because of the skipped probes.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 27 images (QA-confirmed).**
  - *Content images:* the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` files (for example `3c9b2911-…jpg`, `8101387b-…jpg`, `27ffff48-…jpg`), `.../m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`, `.../m/1/d8b928f0-….gif`, and the Scene7 `2026%5F1011%5FCB%5FHolidayDecorating%5FSecondary` image. These likely carry meaning, and screen readers or image-blocked clients will get nothing.
  - *Dynamic-content images:* the seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` files, which are probably product tiles.
  - *Tracking pixels:* `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/52cfade4-…`. These are not real issues, but they should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so the QA check passes cleanly.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` is followed by `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`. At the same specificity the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies at ≥768px.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - The title `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - The `<meta name="format-detection">` tag is declared three times, with conflicting content.
  - The source still contains the template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - The global `*{… line-height:100%}` rule can crush line spacing in clients that honor it, though Outlook is handled with `mso-line-height-rule:exactly`.
- **Good practice:** `lang="en"`, the VML/Office namespaces, the Outlook DPI fix and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` overrides are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens appear. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are resolved. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (≥5k/day) require them.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown** because of the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a branded subdomain, which is a good sign, but alignment is unverified.
- The footer unsubscribe link and postal address are in the truncated section and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 61 links were skipped and the visible source contains no resolved destination URLs. Whether UTM parameters survive the click-redirect, and whether the destinations match the "free shipping over $99" offer, is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content and product images listed above. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Remove the duplicate `rlcdn.com` pixels, or confirm that five calls are intended, and review what hashed identifier is being passed to the third party.
4. Fix the title entity (`&amp;`), the duplicate `format-detection` tags, the conflicting `img max-width` media queries and the leftover template comment.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the `Authentication-Results` headers against the raw message, not the relay copy, before treating them as failures.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and a redirect-following probe, to confirm the footer compliance elements and UTM and landing-page continuity.
