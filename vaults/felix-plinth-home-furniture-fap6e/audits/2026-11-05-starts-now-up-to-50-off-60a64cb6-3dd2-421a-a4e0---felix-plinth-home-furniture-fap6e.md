# STARTS NOW: UP TO 50% OFF
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth CB2 email since 10/30. My first reaction was "Long Weekend Sale?" It's a Thursday in November, and the subject says "Starts Now" while the hero says "One Week Only." The 11/05 "Material matters" email I opened earlier today said Warehouse Sale final hours. So the 'new' sale arrives the same day the old one dies. It's a clean swap, but it still feels like a treadmill.
- The creative itself is good. Each product gets its own big tile: the Corvina chandelier, Davison dining table, Aldine sofa, Roza rug and Diana bed. That's the kind of thing I save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. The "Double Rewards" block lands well for me because I buy through the highest-rewards card. I'd probably click the chandelier or the Aldine sofa. I would not forward it, because it's the same week-long CB2 and Crate & Barrel drumbeat.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "Up to 50% off" with category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors) right under it. I can jump straight to what I'm renovating.
- Every product tile has a name, a designer credit and its own Shop Now. That's a lookbook feel with real click targets, which Pottery Barn's "On sale & delivered for free" didn't give me.
- The "Double Rewards is on" block with the 20% back figure is the kind of rewards math I actually do. It's a strong card-stacking trigger.
- The palette is consistent: warm neutrals, walnut and cream. It matches a renovation mood board.
- The "On Sale" tags on each tile make it clear what's discounted.

## What's weak

- The sale name changed again. Warehouse Sale, then Guest Ready, then Long Weekend. That's three promos in a week, and I'm numb to all of them.
- The hero says "Up to 50%" but the products show no prices or percent per item. I can't tell if the Aldine sofa is 20% or 50% off.
- No shipping or delivery timing anywhere. For furniture across two renovations, I need to know lead times. Pottery Barn at least says "Delivered in 1-4 Weeks."
- The "10%" in the rewards block appears struck through next to "20%." At this size it reads a bit messy, and I had to squint.
- The footer is long and has dense fine print. The app, Rewards and "15% off" cards stack on top of the main offer, so it feels like too many asks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the sale price or percent on each product tile (for example "Aldine sofa, 35% off, ships in 2-3 weeks"). Naming the discount per item is the biggest lift.
- 2. Add delivery timing and a swatch/sample link for the sofa, bed and rug.
- 3. Don't send a new sale email the same day the last sale ends. Space the sends, or tell me this one replaces the earlier one.
- 4. Move the rewards offer up, near the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Aldine sofa + 49 more picks, up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Long Weekend Sale: 20% back in rewards too`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One week only: chandeliers, rugs and beds, plus double Rewards through 11/17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, tables and lighting marked down. Rewards Dollars on top.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 418px. Two things are confirmed: 15 content images have no alt text, and 11 third-party Dotomi tracking pixels load over plain `http://`. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 66 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 images use `http://` (QA flags "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked"):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Gmail's image proxy and Apple MPP typically rewrite or block these. Where they're blocked, the cookie-sync pixels silently fail. They also add 11 extra third-party requests and are mixed content in HTTPS contexts.
- **Cross-brand font hosts:** the `@font-face` rules for `"HNW05"` load from `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, a different brand domain from the sender's `cb2.com`. Other rules use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` with a double slash. The double slash resolves for now but is fragile.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 15 content images** (all `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/*.jpg`). Examples: `cb450f85-e2ff-4712-843e-daf0d972b6cd.jpg`, `decff57a-37e4-42f5-9d70-520fe38c9634.jpg`, `9685411b-425d-4865-a92f-a520df64bb18.jpg`. If images are blocked, or a screen reader is in use, the offer and product content is lost.
- **No alt on tracking images:** `UCMController`, `pub1.php`–`pub10.php`, `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/cb9e7a04-...` have none. For 1x1 pixels, `alt=""` is the correct empty value.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** the universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ... line-height:100%}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` set earlier on `body`. This stops iOS text scaling. The blanket `line-height:100%` can also clip wrapped text.
- **Template residue:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in the head. It's harmless but sloppy, and the typo suggests no template QA.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, a descriptive `<title>`, MSO conditional comments and `format-detection` metas are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=56aaa278...` parameter on `o.gif` is an opaque hashed identifier, not a broken token. The source was truncated, so the body copy past the head wasn't inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, probably a capture artifact:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` is not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. These are not confirmed defects. A sender of this scale probably sends them, but this audit can't verify it.
- **Not verifiable:** the visible in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated part of the source.
- **From domain:** `cb2@mail.cb2.com` is a branded subdomain of the sender's own domain, which is consistent with proper alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 66 click-tracking links were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be checked. The click redirects need to be resolved to confirm UTM/campaign parameters and that the destinations match the "UP TO 50% OFF" sale.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 15 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or remove them. They are `http://` today, and 10 of them are cookie-sync calls.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `*` and `body`.
4. Self-host the `HNW05` fonts on `cb2.com` and fix the `//Styles` double slash.
5. Remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` placeholder comment.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Resolve the 66 tracked links to verify UTMs and landing pages.
