- The "70% OFF" banner up top at least gives me a number without hunting, which is more than some of CB2's recent sends manage. ## What's working

- "UP TO 70% OFF" banner is unmissable right under the header, no scrolling needed.
- The cushions tile does exist and does loosely match the subject, unlike some sends where the hero and subject diverge completely.
- Member-rewards module ("5% in rewards + more perks") is a real loyalty hook, something Pottery Barn's last send skipped entirely.
- Clean four-column layout keeps individual tiles legible even if the whole thing is bloated.

## What's weak

- Cushions — the whole reason I opened this — are buried in row four of nine, way below pergolas, hall trees, and birdbaths that have nothing to do with the subject.
- No urgency cue anywhere near the cushions tile itself (no "low stock," no counter) despite the subject explicitly promising scarcity.
- Big empty gray block near the bottom of the email, right before the footer — looks like a broken or unloaded module.
- This is basically the same "score styles you 💜" grid I've now seen three sends running (pergolas, dog beds, this one) — just the top-left tile swapped to match whatever the subject says that day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero grid with the cushions tile, not pergolas — if the subject says cushions, the first thing I see should be cushions with a real markdown, not scrolling to find them in row four.
- 2. Add a visible urgency signal on the cushions tile itself — "only a few left," a stock counter, something that earns the "selling out" claim in the subject.
- 3. Fix or remove the blank gray block before the footer — it reads as a rendering failure, not a design choice.
- 4. Stop reusing the identical 17-tile grid across sends; rotate real content instead of just swapping the top-left image to match the subject line du jour.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

Batch email built on a standard Unlayer/Wayfair ESP template; core links resolve but tracking pixels use plaintext HTTP, most images lack alt text, and unsubscribe/authentication headers were not captured by the relay.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 of 65 links were probed (link-likelihood ranking); the primary CTAs ("Shop Sale", "Wayfair" nav, product PDP link) all returned 429 (rate-limited, not confirmed broken) — evidence: `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...&refid=MKTEML_121356&emlid=101&maiid=16079`.
- 4 links were unprobed due to the 70s time budget, including `wayfair.com/wayfair-rewards` and two `daily-sales/filters/...` category links — status unknown, not pass/fail.
- 20 open/click tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plaintext HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged by QA as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently drop attribution data.
- All probed and skipped links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_121356`, `maiid=16079`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `mdlid`) — no malformed or missing tracking params observed in the sample checked.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- 10 content/creative images are missing `alt` text, including the hero (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`), product image (`email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`), review-stars graphic (`stars_9.png`), loyalty banner, event header banner, and the loyalty disclaimer image (`loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`) — screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or skip them entirely, and if images are blocked (common default in Outlook/Gmail), no fallback text renders.
- The 20 HTTP-only tracking pixels (`imp?s=...`) also lack `alt` text, though this is lower-impact since they're 1x1 trackers, not content.
- One image src contains an unencoded `%` character: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` — malformed URL encoding that may fail to load in strict URL parsers.
- CSS is heavy Outlook/mobile-client conditional styling (`u-MobileHide`, `data-outlook-cycle`, MSO-pattern classes) consistent with standard ESP boilerplate — no structural rendering issues identified in the visible source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible HTML or QA output. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a relay capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than confirmed absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe) also not detected — same capture caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC status) not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail cannot be verified from available data.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA note ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in probing), satisfying the visible CAN-SPAM footer-link requirement, but its target URL was not in the probed sample provided.
- **Caveat:** all three header-related warnings stem from relay capture gaps, not confirmed violations — flag for re-verification against raw SMTP headers before treating as compliance failures.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`refid`, `maiid`, `emlid`, `_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `mdlid`) rather than standard UTM params (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — not a defect, but means GA-style UTM continuity checks don't apply; continuity should instead be verified against Wayfair's internal `maiid=16079`/`refid=MKTEML_121356` campaign IDs.
- Destination domains (`wayfair.com`, `wayfairapp.onelink.me`) are consistent with the sender domain (`members.wayfair.com`) — no domain-mismatch risk observed.
- Deep-link app CTA (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?af_dp=wayfairapp%3A%2F%2Fhome...`) correctly includes both `af_dp` (app deep link) and `af_web_dp` (web fallback) params for cross-platform continuity.
