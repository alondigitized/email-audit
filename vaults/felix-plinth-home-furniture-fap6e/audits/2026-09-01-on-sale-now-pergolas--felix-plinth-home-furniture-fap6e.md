# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "pergolas," and the top-left tile actually is a pergola on sale — so credit where it's due, that's the third send in a row now where Wayfair's subject line matches what's actually in the hero grid. But that's about the only compliment I've got, because two clicks in this thing turns into the same sprawling category dump I've been getting from them all week: gazebos, dog beds, toilets, hall trees, faux florals, window AC units. It's not a pergola email, it's a "everything in the warehouse" email with a pergola photo bolted onto the top.
- The middle section is a hard scroll-past for me — a window AC unit and a "cool down on any budget" banner has nothing to do with a guy mid-renovation on two houses, and there's a blank white gap near the bottom that looks like a broken module. I'm not clicking past the top row, and I'm definitely not forwarding this to anyone — my Pottery Barn/Crate & Barrel lists exist specifically so I don't have to sift through toilets and dog beds to find the outdoor furniture I actually want.
- Compare this to Crate & Barrel's "Apartment Collection" send I looked at — that one led with one hero product in my exact use case and held that line. Article's Labor Day email did the same: one sale, one clear CTA. Wayfair still can't resist cramming fifteen unrelated categories into a single scroll.

## What's working

- Subject-to-hero match: "pergolas" and the actual top-left tile is a pergola — Wayfair's now 2-for-2 on this after ignoring it for weeks.
- "UP TO 70% OFF" banner up top is unmissable and unambiguous — no hunting for the offer.
- Rewards module ("get 5% rewards on every item, always") is a real loyalty hook, which I care about since I stack cards for renovation spend.

## What's weak

- The pergola promise dies after one tile — by row three I'm looking at dog beds, toilets, and a window AC unit, none of which belong in a "pergolas" email.
- There's a blank white block with a lone dot near the bottom — looks like a broken/empty content module, not intentional whitespace.
- No price, no discount %, no dimensions on the pergola tile itself — just "On sale now: pergolas »" with zero specifics.
- Visual hierarchy collapses fast — after the 70% off banner, it's an undifferentiated wall of same-size tiles with no clear second CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one category. If the subject says pergolas, the whole email should be pergolas, gazebos, and outdoor structures — not a toilet and a dog bed. Article and Crate & Barrel both prove a single-focus send performs better with someone like me.
- 2. Put a real number on the pergola tile — "$399, was $650" beats "On sale now »" every time.
- 3. Fix or remove that blank white block near the footer — it reads as a rendering bug, not a feature.
- 4. Move the Rewards module up near the hero if it's meant to drive urgency — right now it's buried between two unrelated category grids.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your pergola, on sale now — $399+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shade structures, screens & vertical gardens — Labor Day pricing`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Wayfair promotional email built on the Bee/Unbounce-style responsive framework (`u-*` MSO/Gmail media-query classes); QA probing shows a 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by rate-limited (429) link checks and widespread missing `alt` attributes, plus absent authentication/unsubscribe headers at the relay level.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 9 of 12 probed material links returned **429 Rate Limited** during automated checks (e.g. `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`, `wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `wayfair.com/home-improvement/sb0/air-conditioners-c420527.html?...`). These are inconclusive (link may still be valid) but could not be confirmed reachable.
- 3 links were **unprobed due to a 70s time budget**, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm` and `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/garden-beds-se81925.html?eid=602156&` — status unknown.
- All checked/tracking links carry a consistent parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_121694`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16077`, `mdlid=...`) suggesting a unified tracking schema — no malformed or truncated tracking params observed in the sampled URLs.
- Open-tracking pixel (`t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`) is present and correctly base64-encoded.
- **20 impression-tracking pixels use `http://` instead of `https://`** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000...` through `...019`), flagged as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies. This is a real infrastructure defect — all impression pixels in this batch are non-secure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **21 images missing `alt` text**, including the primary hero (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`), header banner (`wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`), product images (`wfus_jt800_batch10_majorappliances_airconditioners` ×3), rating graphics (`stars_9.png` ×2), and all 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels (these last 20 are non-content and low-impact, but the hero, header, and product images lack alt text for screen readers/image-blocked clients).
- Media-query CSS uses standard Bee/Unbounce mobile scaffolding (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, Outlook `data-outlook-cycle` fix, Gmail `u + .Gmail` hook) — no structural rendering defects identified in the truncated source provided.
- `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout: none)` fallback present for iOS Mail `u-HalfWidthBlock` image display — standard mitigation, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags, empty personalization tokens, or broken dynamic-content blocks found in the provided HTML/QA output.

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) support unconfirmed.
