# Snuggle up! Their coziest bed awaits ☁️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — third time in a week hitting my inbox with baby/toddler stuff, and I don't have kids. "Layer a cozy bed," bedding sets, quilts, sheet sets, "bestselling baby bedding," swaddle blankets — it's all nursery and toddler-room content, wall to wall. This is a well-produced email in isolation — the quilts look nice, the flat-lays are clean, there's a limited-time 20% off banner up top — but it's simply not for me.
- The Jenny Lind send on 2026-09-21 already told me this arm of Crate & Barrel doesn't know I'm childless, and this one doubles down harder, going deeper into cribs, swaddles, and star-shaped throw pillows for a toddler's room. I'm not opening this on my commute a second time, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — there's nothing here for the second house or the primary reno.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and mentally filing "Crate & Kids" as noise from a brand I otherwise like.

## What's working

- The photography is genuinely nice — soft neutral palettes, good styling on the flat-lays of quilts and sheet sets.
- 20% off clearly flagged at the very top, so at least the offer isn't buried.
- Multiple clear "Shop" CTAs throughout, so if I were the target customer, navigation would be easy.
- The "make it extra special — Personalize It" section is a nice loyalty-adjacent touch for actual parents.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to me — no adult bedding, no living/dining room furniture, nothing for a reno of two houses.
- This is at least the third Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel-family email this week (following the Jenny Lind send and the two Oceanside sofa sends) — my inbox is basically subscribed to this family twice over now.
- The email is enormous — I scrolled through what feels like ten separate product modules (quilts, sheets, baby bedding, blankets, pillows, "shop the app") before it ended. Way too long for a phone read.
- No color-palette or swatch-and-sample callout for grown-up furniture, no shipping-timeline info for furniture — because none of this applies to my actual purchase category.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely.** Crate & Barrel already has my browsing/purchase history — if I've never touched nursery furniture, stop cross-selling it. This is the single biggest lever.
- 2. **Trim the email length.** Even a parent-subscriber is scrolling through eight-plus modules; cut to the top 3 offers and link out to a full collection page.
- 3. **If Crate & Barrel insists on emailing me from this sub-brand, lead with something crossover-relevant** (e.g., "furnishing a home? here's what's new across Crate & Barrel + Kids") rather than 100% nursery content.
- 4. **Cap cadence** — three sends in a week from one brand family is what pushed CB2 down to a 5 in my recent scores; Crate & Kids is heading the same way.
