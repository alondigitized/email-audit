# 6 fall baking recipes you’ve got to try →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Six recipes deep and I'm renovating two kitchens, not auditioning for the Great British Bake Off — this one's a swing and a miss for me. I get why Crate & Barrel is running fall baking content (it worked when I read the almost-identical send on 2026-09-15, "You need to see the latest designs from KitchenAid," at 7/10, because that one led with hardware), but this version buries the bakeware under six full recipes — apple spice cake, pumpkin cheesecake bars, apple pie bars, chocolate swirl pumpkin bread, maple pumpkin cupcakes — before it even shows me a "more fall baking must-haves" product grid. I'm not baking pumpkin cheesecake bars, I'm trying to figure out if the range hood clears code in the second house.
- And then, halfway down, there's Venus Williams again — same tennis-collab banner I already flagged as a 4/10 drag in the Crate & Kids "Ace their space" send. Seeing her pop up in a baking recipe email is genuinely disorienting; it reads like two different marketing teams' assets got stapled together.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Pottery Barn's "Save up to 60% across the home" warehouse email (6/10) at least organized by room, which is how I actually shop. This one organizes by recipe.

## What's working

- The hero shot — cast iron, stoneware, that copper-ish bakeware stack — is genuinely nice photography and reads "kitchen renovation," not just "recipe blog."
- "more fall baking must-haves" section finally gets to product photography (stoneware, cast iron, KitchenAid stand mixer) with visible star ratings, which is the part of this email actually built for someone like me.
- The layout is clean — no overlapping text, no broken images, everything renders crisply.
- Seasonal timing is honest — mid-September fall baking push isn't stale.

## What's weak

- Six recipe blocks before the actual gear pitch is backwards for a home-renovation buyer — I don't need the pumpkin cheesecake bar recipe, I need to know the Staub cocotte comes in the color I already picked for the kitchen.
- Venus Williams' tennis collab banner shows up again, completely off-theme in a baking email — it's the same asset I already dinged in the Crate & Kids send this week, now recycled into a context where it makes even less sense.
- No CTA button variety — it's "Get the Recipe" over and over; there's no single clear "Shop Bakeware" button until deep in the scroll.
- Zero rewards math, swatch/sample info, or shipping-clarity content for furniture — which is what actually gets me to click from this brand.
- "we love these for you" personalization grid at the bottom is generic — nothing here signals they know I've been eyeing kitchen renovation gear specifically.

## Recommendations

- 1. Flip the structure — lead with the bakeware/hardware grid (stoneware, cast iron, stand mixer) and demote the recipes to thumbnails linking out, the way the 2026-09-15 KitchenAid send did when it scored 7/10.
- 2. Cut the Venus Williams banner from this email entirely — it doesn't belong in a baking context and I've now seen it twice this week in unrelated sends.
- 3. Add one clear top-of-email CTA button ("Shop Bakeware") instead of six repeated "Get the Recipe" links.
- 4. Segment this differently for reno-stage subscribers — someone furnishing a kitchen from scratch wants SKUs and finish options, not maple pumpkin cupcake instructions.
