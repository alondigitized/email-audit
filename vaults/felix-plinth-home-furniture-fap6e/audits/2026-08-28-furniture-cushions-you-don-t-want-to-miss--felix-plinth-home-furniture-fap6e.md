# FURNITURE CUSHIONS you don’t want to miss
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Here we go again — another Wayfair "cushions" email, and I already flagged this exact bait two days ago with "FOUND: Furniture Cushions." This one at least opens with an actual cushion (a window-seat bench cushion with a cat sitting on it, cute), but by the second scroll it's window AC units, then pergolas, gazebos, recliners, wall sconces, wine glasses, bar stools, cat trees, doormats, slipcovers, rug pads, ice buckets, and a shed. That's not a cushion email, that's the entire Wayfair warehouse wearing a cushion hat.
- Worse, a big chunk of the lower grid is just blank white tiles with a tiny blue square icon where a product photo should be — legitimately broken images, not me nitpicking. I'm renovating two houses right now and cushions/accent chairs are genuinely on my list, but I'm not digging through a broken, unrelated grid to find them.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Marking as read and moving on — this is the fourth or fifth near-identical Wayfair grab-bag I've logged this week alone.

## What's working

- The hero cushion shot is a real, on-topic product image with a 5-star rating badge visible — good start that the rest of the email abandons.
- The "70% OFF" red-and-white banner up top is at least a bold, unambiguous headline offer.
- "Your counter, completed." bar stool module has a clean, isolated three-product shot that's actually well composed.
- Category link density (pergolas, sconces, recliners, gazebos) means there's technically something for a renovator like me — just buried.

## What's weak

- Subject promises cushions, hero delivers one cushion, then the email pivots to air conditioners, gazebos, a shed — total bait-and-switch, and I called this same move out on the "FOUND: Furniture Cushions" send from 2026-08-26.
- Multiple broken image placeholders (blue square icons on blank tiles) in the lower half — wine glasses, cat trees, mats, slipcovers, accent chairs, rug pads, ice buckets all show as empty boxes, not actual products.
- Zero pricing/percentage-off info on almost every individual tile except the top banner — "Sheds From $399" doesn't tell me anything useful.
- This is at least my fifth Wayfair email this cycle with the same kitchen-sink layout; Crate & Barrel's Labor Day send at least kept its hero image intact and Pottery Barn's "Ready-to-Ship Seating" email stayed on one category the whole way through — Wayfair can't manage that discipline.
- No color-palette or shipping-timeline info for the furniture pieces, which is exactly what I'd need to actually buy an accent chair or gazebo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image tiles before this ships again — a marketing email with a dozen blank placeholder boxes is a QA failure, not a style choice.
- 2. Make the subject match the content: either send a real cushions-only email (like Pottery Barn's single-category "Ready-to-Ship Seating" send) or rename it "Everything's on sale" and stop overpromising.
- 3. Cut the grid down to 3-4 categories max and add real price/percent-off text on each tile — right now only the top banner has a number on it.
- 4. Since this is a near-repeat of "FOUND: Furniture Cushions" from two days ago, space out the cushion-themed sends or actually vary the creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cushions, restocked — plus 70% off everything else`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your accent chair search ends here (70% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New cushion colors just landed, Labor Day pricing inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the 70% off clearance before it's gone`

# Technical Audit — Wayfair "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Wayfair batch-template email (graymatter_batch/batchid=15) with heavy tracking-pixel usage and template-generated markup; automated probing hit rate-limiting on most CTAs, and multiple images lack alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 58 links probed, 46 skipped** (deprioritized footer/utility/social) — coverage is partial by design, not a defect.
- **9 material links returned HTTP 429 (rate limited)** during probing, including primary CTAs ("Shop Sale," "Get Exclusive Deals on the App," hero image links, PDP link for the bench cushion). These are flagged as "may be valid" — server-side rate limiting on the prober, not confirmed broken links. Recommend re-probing at lower concurrency/slower rate before treating as pass/fail.
- **4 links unprobed** due to 70s time budget exhaustion (pergola/gazebo filter pages, homepage, one PDP) — status unknown, not failures.
- **20 first-party tracking-pixel requests to `li.wayfair.com/imp`** (sequential `s=124126000`–`124126019`) served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — see Rendering & Accessibility below.
- Link query strings carry a consistent, well-formed tracking schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_121675`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16067`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `csnid`) — no malformed or truncated tracking parameters observed in the sampled links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **21 images missing `alt` text**, including primary content images (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, review-stars asset `stars_9.png`, multiple product/category images) and 20 tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=...`). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or skip them; product/hero images should carry descriptive alt text at minimum.
- **20 tracking pixels loaded via `http://` (not `https://`)** — e.g. `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000&sz=2x1&m=false&p=f3b16466-...`. Mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients/webmail may prevent these from firing, undercounting opens; also inconsistent with the rest of the template's HTTPS assets (`assets.wfcdn.com`, `secure.img1-fg.wfcdn.com`).
- One image filename contains a literal `%` character un-encoded in the path segment (`0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`) — likely a typo in the source filename (`Outd%oor` instead of `Outdoor`) rather than encoding corruption, but worth flagging to the sender since it could 404 depending on CDN handling.
- Media-query CSS block is standard Unroll/BEE-style boilerplate (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidth`, `Text--fontSize*`, etc.) — no malformed rules or unclosed braces observed in the truncated source provided.
