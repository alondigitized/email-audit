## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Vertical Response-templated Wayfair marketing email; core delivery infra (auth headers, unsubscribe headers) could not be verified via the relay, and several tracking pixels use insecure HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed against a total of 84 links found; most returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) rather than a confirmed pass/fail, including primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" (appears twice), and the top nav "Wayfair" logo link — these are unconfirmed, not failed.
- 4 links were left entirely unprobed due to the 70s time budget, including `wayfair.com/wayfair-product-L13-K~W110095045.html` and a Labor Day decor clearance link — status unknown.
- 20 first-party impression-tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over plain HTTP rather than HTTPS, which mail clients commonly block, silently degrading open/impression tracking.
- All probed/unprobed links carry consistent `_emr`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_139340`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16045` tracking parameters — no malformed or truncated tracking URLs observed in the sampled set.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 24 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including the hero banner (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`), the financing banner, multiple seasonal skinny banners, and all 8 product star-rating icons (`stars_9.png`/`stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`) — screen reader users get no fallback content for these.
- The 20 HTTP-only tracking pixels (`imp?s=...`) are also flagged as missing `alt` text, though as 1x1 impression pixels this is immaterial to accessibility.
- Head contains extensive `@media max-width` rules and a `mso-font-alt` fallback for the custom "Sofia" webfont, indicating standard mobile/Outlook responsive handling is in place; no broken selector nesting observed in the truncated source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
