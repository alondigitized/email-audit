# ENDS TODAY! Up to 20% off the best baby essentials  →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth Crate & Barrel-family send I've waded through this week, and it's a baby-gear sale that isn't for me. I'm renovating two houses, not registering for a stroller. The "ENDS TODAY" urgency also lands right after this morning's "20% off exclusively in the app" and the "Step into cozy season" send. When a family of brands sends nine emails in a week, "ends today" stops sounding like a deadline.
- The email itself is competent. The top is clean: a bold headline, a "Shop the Sale" button, then a run of premium brands (Nuna, Stokke, Mushie, Maxi-Cosi, Cybex, Thule, Dagne Dover). It's a long scroll, though, and by the third brand block it reads like a catalog dump.
- I wouldn't click. I might scan for the lookbook and nursery furniture section near the bottom, but that's about it. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it to anyone unless a friend has a baby coming.

## What's working

- The headline is huge and plain, and the "up to 20% off" number is the first thing I see.
- The brand-by-brand blocks (Nuna, Stokke, Mushie, Maxi-Cosi) each carry a percent off and a button, so a parent could shop by brand they already trust.
- Product photography is clean and consistent, with neutral sage and beige tones that look nice in a Pinterest folder.
- The nursery furniture and "fall 2026 lookbook: storybook style" blocks near the bottom are the only part that connects to how I actually shop.
- The footer has app-download and Design Desk modules, which is a useful reminder that the loyalty and design-service pieces exist.

## What's weak

- "ENDS TODAY!" in caps with an exclamation mark reads as spam-adjacent, and I've seen too many "sale ends" emails from this family to believe it.
- The email is very long, and I can't tell which brands need a promo code and which don't. The small code text under some tiles is hard to read on a phone.
- Nothing here is for me. There's no room, furniture or color story until the very bottom.
- Pottery Barn's "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner at least tells me about delivery. Nothing here mentions shipping timelines for big items like strollers and cribs.
- The kids banner and the sale branding repeat the same 20% message about six times. Repetition doesn't add anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send this only to people with baby or registry signals. For someone like me, the family-wide blast is wasted, and it hurts the send-to-open ratio for the next email.
- 2. Put the code-required brands and the automatic-discount brands in separate, labeled sections, so the rewards math is obvious.
- 3. Add delivery windows and a "free shipping" note for the large items (strollers, cribs, high chairs).
- 4. Cut the tile count by about half and lead with the strongest brand (Nuna).
- 5. Drop the caps and the exclamation mark, and put the deadline time in the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Baby sale ends tonight: up to 20% off Nuna, Stokke & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 20% off strollers, car seats and nursery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends at midnight. Code BABY unlocks extra savings on select brands.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nuna travel systems, Stokke high chairs and Mushie feeding sets, all up to 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style (`mi.`/`dv.crateandbarrel.com`) table-based XHTML template with MSO fallbacks. The QA data shows about 45 images flagged for missing alt text and 81 tracking links that were not probed. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay capture gaps rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 81 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity, redirect chains and expiry for a "ENDS TODAY" promo are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) sit alongside first-party pixels `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8d30676d-…`. Five near-identical calls to one third-party endpoint is redundant.
- **Identifier sharing:** the `s=` value appears to be the same 64-hex per-recipient identifier as the `mi_u=` value on first-party URLs. If that hash is derived from the recipient's email, it is being passed to a third party.
- **Repeated asset:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` is requested 6 times (probably per-module tracking or product recommendation, but not confirmed from the truncated source).
- **Retry cost:** `s7d5.scene7.com` spacer images use `%5F`-encoded names such as `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`. Encoded underscores resolve fine, but each spacer is an extra HTTP request that could be CSS padding.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 `<img>` elements are flagged. Examples:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.gif|jpg`, for example `c383e650-…jpg`, `be09c538-…jpg`, `2ac4d074-…jpg`.
  - The lookbook asset `2026%5F0710%5FDTP%5FKids%5FStorybookLookbook%5FSec…`.
  - Spacers and tracking pixels that should carry an explicit `alt=""`: `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer…`, `o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`.
  - Caveat: the checker may also be counting `alt=""`, which is valid for decorative images. Confirm per image.
  - If the content images are linked without alt text, screen readers announce the raw URL or filename.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Duplicate and invalid markup:**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The title is also generic and identical to the brand name.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, along with an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.
- **Dead CSS:** at ≥768px both `img {max-width:600px}` (`min-width:768px`) and `img {max-width:640px}` (`min-width:640px`) match. The later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule never takes effect.
- **Breakpoint:** the only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports of 461–639px get the desktop layout.
- **Global rule:** the universal selector `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` is heavy-handed. It also disables text autosizing in WebKit.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) are visible in the truncated source. The per-recipient `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are populated. The remainder of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are reported not found. The QA note attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so this is inconclusive rather than a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The envelope is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com` and images are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is aligned on the same subdomain. That is a good sign but not proof.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link:** the footer is not in the truncated source, so it cannot be verified.
- **Privacy:** see the LiveRamp identifier sharing under section 2 if the hash is derived from the email address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 81 click links go through tracking redirects that were not resolved, and no `utm_*` parameters are visible in the truncated HTML. The final landing pages, their UTM preservation and their offer consistency ("up to 20% off … ENDS TODAY") were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and lookbook images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), replace it with a campaign-specific one, and drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` metas and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Delete the dead 600px `img` rule, or reorder the media queries so both take effect.
5. Consolidate the 5 `rlcdn.com` pixels into one call, and confirm that hashed identifiers shared with LiveRamp are covered by the privacy policy and consent.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be verified.
7. Resolve a sample of the 81 tracking links and confirm the final URLs, UTM parameters and that the offer is still live for the "ENDS TODAY" send.
