# Hand-knotted rugs you’ll treasure for years
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Pottery Barn send in about two days, and it's the one I'd actually save. It's a rug email. I have two houses with bare floors, so a hand-knotted rug story is the first thing from them this week that matches what I'm doing. The hero is a big antique-looking rug under "Hand-Knotted Rugs" with a clear Shop Now button. The rest is a tidy catalog tour. It breaks the rugs into Traditional Motifs, Geometric Patterns and Neutral Textures, so I can browse by style instead of scrolling a grid.
- It's still a lookbook with no offer. I can't see a price, a sale, or even a "from $X" anywhere. The one part that helps a renovator is "Order Free Swatches" in the design-resources block, and it's buried near the bottom. This also lands right after the 10/17 "Our most-loved bedroom collections" send and the "Just in! New arrivals" send, which had the same "Recommended Just For You" strip. I'd click through and drop the rugs into my Pinterest folder. I wouldn't forward it, because there's nothing in it that's actually a deal.

## What's working

- The hero is a single idea. "Hand-Knotted Rugs" with Shop Now is one clear action, and the rug looks like something I'd use in a bedroom.
- Grouping by Traditional Motifs, Geometric Patterns and Neutral Textures matches how I pick a rug. I choose a pattern family first, then a size.
- The room shots with rugs under beds and tables show scale and how the rug works with a palette. That helps when I'm matching two houses.
- Named products (Arlet, Tyson, Paloma, Merrin, Jameson) can go straight into my notes. Pottery Barn also has a tighter product focus here than Crate & Barrel's tailgating send.
- "Order Free Swatches" and the rug buying guide are the right content for someone doing a renovation.

## What's weak

- There's no price or offer anywhere in the rug section. The brand is asking me to commit to a hand-knotted rug on looks alone. Wayfair at least puts a "$134 off" badge on the pergola.
- The swatch link is tiny and sits below the fold, even though it's the most useful thing for me.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block (Cayman Platform Bed, Toscana Dining Table, Banks table, curtain rod) is the same filler as the last two sends. It dilutes the rug story.
- The email is very long, with a rewards card, an app promo, Affirm, and a "1400+ New Arrivals" banner. It feels like a template stacked on a story.
- The text over the hero is thin and sits on a busy rug image, so the headline is harder to read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete reason to act in the hero, such as "Free swatches, free shipping on rugs" or a "from $X" price.
- 2. Move "Order Free Swatches" up under the hero as a real button. Do it before the first product row.
- 3. Show prices and sizes under each named rug (e.g. 8x10 from $X), plus a "handmade in India" or wool-fiber detail.
- 4. Remove the "Recommended Just For You" beds and dining tables from a rug send, or swap in rug-adjacent items like rug pads.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from sliced images, and nearly every image lacks alt text, so the rug content is unreadable with images off. The footer has three non-HTTPS images. One of them, an open-tracking pixel, puts the recipient's address in a plain-HTTP query string. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The QA run reported 50% pass, 0 issues and 4 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 65 links were skipped from HTTP probing because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`). Destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,630 of 16,922 characters (about 75%) are URLs, mostly tracking redirects. This is a spam-score risk and makes the text alternative hard to read.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text.
- **Open pixels without alt:** `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d978b90a-…` and `www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…` are tracking pixels with no alt attribute.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain and has no alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 60 images are flagged, including:
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_23.jpg` under `/pb/2025/US/H25_Rugs_B13_HandKnottedRugs/`, which carry the main body content.
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg` under `/pb/2026/US/Email-Footer/`.
  - The banners `H26_Top_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Six product images, e.g. `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`.
  
  With images blocked, the message is effectively blank to screen readers. The logo does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** these may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no text.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains literal `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is invalid CSS, apparently leaked server-side code. It can break parsing of the rules after it in strict clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when the class attribute is exactly that value. The `.wrapper` table in the truncated source was not confirmed.
- **Webfont:** the Google Fonts `<link>` to Montserrat is stripped or ignored by Gmail and Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The tokens that did resolve populate the recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) in two URLs:
  - `…/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`
- The `svopn` pixel sends the plaintext address over unencrypted HTTP, a privacy and leakage risk.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, also possibly stripped by the relay.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer:** the truncated HTML doesn't show the physical address or the unsubscribe link, so I can't confirm or flag them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** all links go through opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects and weren't resolved. I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment with the "Hand-knotted rugs" subject.
- **Landing pages:** none were verified.
- **Stale assets:** the rug body uses `/pb/2025/…/H25_Rugs_B13…` assets while the header and footer use `/pb/2026/…`. The page is mixed-vintage, but I found no evidence it breaks anything.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content slice, banner and product image. Add `alt=""` to pixels and spacers.
2. Serve all images over HTTPS, and stop putting the recipient's address in an HTTP pixel URL. Hash or tokenize it, or use HTTPS.
3. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Set a meaningful `<title>` and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Replace the attribute-equality selectors with class selectors, or confirm that the classes match.
6. Reduce the URL ratio in the plain-text part. Include real copy rather than only link lists.
7. Capture raw headers to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Resolve a sample of the tracking links to check destinations and UTMs.
