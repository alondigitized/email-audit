- The photography is warm, neutral and Pinterest-ready, with oak, sage green and cream. The palette is consistent from tile to tile.
- Each product block has a "Shop Now" button and a secondary link. The tap targets look big enough on a phone.
- The "instant inspo: shop the look" block is the right kind of content for my Pinterest-style folder.
- The app promo with the $500 hook and the "TheDesignDesk" appointment block are real value-adds. Showroom and design-service content is what I look for.

## What's weak

- The top banner is garbled, with "that hours!" reading as a truncated "hours left." The free-shipping message also competes with the furniture offer right below it.
- Everything is for kids and babies: bunk bed, toddler bed, nursery glider, crib. There's no signal that I'm a known customer or that the email is relevant to two renovations.

- The email is very long, and the repeated "Shop Now" plus "Shop the Sale" pairs dilute the hierarchy.
- No shipping or lead-time clarity for big furniture, no swatch or sample cues, and no rewards math. That's what I need before buying a $1,000 dresser.

- 2. Fix the top banner and pick one offer per send, either free shipping or 50% off.
- 3. Add white-glove delivery and lead-time info on furniture tiles.
