# The holiday decor shop is now open 🎄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Crate & Barrel email that isn't a nursery. After three Kids sends this morning (the "$1,000 Gift Card ENDS TODAY" one was the last), this is the first one that fits two houses mid-renovation. The fireplace hero with stockings and garland is pretty, and holiday decor is something I'll actually buy for both places.
- The problem is that the banner at the top says "last chance! the Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off," which has nothing to do with a holiday decor launch. It's also the eighth C&B send in two days. The email is long, with lots of lifestyle tiles, and I never saw a single clear offer for decor. I'd click through to garlands and probably save it to my Pinterest folder. I wouldn't forward it, because nothing here is a deal.

## What's working

- The fireplace hero with the red stockings and a "Shop Holiday Decor" button is a strong, seasonal image that tells me the topic right away.
- The category tiles (garlands & wreaths, stockings, ornaments, candleholders) are easy to browse and match how I actually shop.
- The "what's your holiday aesthetic?" block, with five curated styles, feels like real design help instead of just a sale.
- The Reward Dollars card callout ("Get 10% back in Reward Dollars") is the one thing that speaks to my rewards math.
- The Black Friday signup (10% off) gives me a reason to hand over my info again.

## What's weak

- The "last chance! Thanksgiving dining room event" banner is leftover from another campaign and clashes with the holiday decor message.
- The "up to 30% off" in that banner doesn't clearly apply to decor, and the "*Select styles" fine print makes it feel buried.
- The email is very long, with two "Shop All" buttons and a repeated hero CTA. Pottery Barn's strip emails at least make the offer obvious.
- The "we love these for you" section is mostly glassware and has nothing to do with decor or the holiday theme.
- The app, TheDesignDesk and store-finder blocks at the bottom pile on. I'm tired of C&B, CB2 and Kids all landing in the same week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Take the leftover Thanksgiving banner off a decor send and replace it with one decor-specific offer, or say clearly that there isn't one.
- 2. Cut the length and keep one hero CTA and one "Shop All" button.
- 3. Make the product picks match the topic: garlands, stockings and candleholders, with prices, instead of glassware.
- 4. Move the Reward Dollars and shipping details higher.
- 5. Add a delivery cutoff date, which is the urgency I'd believe. Wayfair does that well with "Fall Clearance Ends Tonight."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML source has code-level hygiene problems: 32 images with no `alt` attribute, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and a leftover template placeholder. The header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) came back inconclusive because of the relay, and 65 tracked links were not probed. The QA run reports 0 hard failures and a 57% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 65 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so none of their final destinations or status codes were checked. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Per-recipient identifier in asset URLs:** The `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da718e29…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/59bc3b93-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` URLs carry a 64-hex recipient token. If the email is forwarded or archived, the token travels with it.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1…5` beacons (LiveRamp) load on open, alongside the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` open pixel. They add extra third-party requests per open. No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (32 images):** The `alt` attribute is absent entirely, not just empty.
  - Content and hero images: 17 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/*.jpg|png|gif`, plus the hero GIF `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F1016%5FCB%5FHolidayDecor%5FGrid%5Fhero.gif`. With images blocked, these render as blanks, and screen readers announce filenames or nothing. This is the most significant finding.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`: 7 images.
  - Tracking pixels: `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/59bc3b93-…`. These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed.
  - Two `<meta http-equiv>` / `<meta charset>` declarations duplicate each other.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, with conflicting values on the first one.
- **Template residue:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (misspelled) shipped in production.
- **Global resets:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` are applied globally. They can override per-element styling in some clients, but I can't confirm a visible effect from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[[…]]`. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are populated, not placeholders. The HTML is truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing, but the QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unconfirmed, not a defect. Mailbox providers such as Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** Absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment couldn't be assessed. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** These fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. All 65 tracked links went unprobed, so I can't see UTM parameters or landing pages. The visible source also doesn't show whether the redirect chain preserves campaign parameters.
