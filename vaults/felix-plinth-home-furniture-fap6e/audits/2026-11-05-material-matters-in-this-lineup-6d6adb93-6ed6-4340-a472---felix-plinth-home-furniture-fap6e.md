# Material matters in this lineup
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh CB2 email since 10/30, and I'm tired. The subject is "Material matters," which sounds like a lookbook. But the hero is "FINAL HOURS" with Warehouse Sale up to 60% and Guest Ready up to 40%. It's the same sale story as the 11/04 "WAREHOUSE SALE ENDS TONIGHT" and the 11/03 "2 SALES END TOMORROW." The subject and the body don't match.
- Once I scrolled, it got better. The product run is the part I'd save: the Ambroise chair, the Davidson bed, the Norrie chair, the Enyo coffee table and the Scatola coffee table. They're moody walnut, travertine and velvet, and they photograph well. This is what goes in my Pinterest-adjacent folder. The "So much ships free" band and the "More for you" grid with prices are useful too.
- Would I click? Probably, on the Davidson bed or the Scatola table, since I'm furnishing two houses. Forward it? No. Mark it read, then save it to the folder.

## What's working

- "Final Hours" with 60% and 40% in huge type is instantly readable. The offer lands before I scroll.
- The product cards give a name, a designer credit and a one-line hook, like "Proof that lilac looks good in any season." That's real curation, and Article's "Fresh for fall" lookbook doesn't do it as well.
- Room-scene photography in warm browns and amber stays consistent across the whole email. That matters when I'm matching a palette across two houses.
- "So much ships free" answers the furniture shipping question. Wayfair buries that.
- The "More for you" grid shows real prices, like $399 and $1,199. A strike-through price on the counter stool ($499 down to $424) gives me the math.
- The footer has the app and the Rewards line, "Earn 10% back." That's the card-points angle I care about.

## What's weak

- The subject says "Material matters in this lineup." The body is a clearance countdown. I opened for a materials story and got a sale email.
- "Final Hours" is the third "ending" email in three days, after "ends tomorrow" and "ends tonight." The urgency has stopped working on me.
- The email is very long. By the Scatola table I'm skimming, and the repeating "Shop Now" links are small tap targets on a phone.
- Mid-email I get "Shop New Arrivals" twice plus two sale buttons. That's four competing CTAs and no clear lead.
- The "More for you" items (Foley, Andes, Journey, Viggo) don't match the products I just scrolled past. They feel like a generic recommendation block, not anything tied to what I've viewed.
- Nothing about swatches or samples. CB2 pushed that on 11/03, and it would have fit a "material matters" theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the hero. Either lead with the sale or build the email around materials. Don't send one and deliver the other.
- 2. Don't send a seventh sale reminder. Skip the "final hours" push, or send it only to people who haven't opened this week's emails.
- 3. Cut the middle product run to three items and use one clear CTA.
- 4. Put a swatch or sample link beside the materials story, like the walnut, travertine and velvet.
- 5. Make the "More for you" block use my browsing, not a generic pick.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walnut, travertine, velvet: 60% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: Warehouse Sale up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on glassware, pillows and holiday décor.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Davidson bed and Scatola table, before the sale closes.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard table-based, Marketing Cloud-style template with a `lang="en"` attribute and MSO conditional comments. The QA run found 10 content images and 13 tracking pixels missing `alt`, 11 third-party pixels loaded over `http://`, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The compliance headers and authentication results could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are untested.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 Dotomi (Conversant) pixels use plain `http://`.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Gmail proxies these through HTTPS, but other clients may block or warn on them. The `cookieredir/pub1–10` pattern is a cookie-sync chain that fires 10 extra third-party requests per open.
- **Identifier in URL:** the open pixel `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa2787a...cfd8&mi_...` carries a 64-hex-character `mi_u` value. It looks like a SHA-256 hash of a user identifier, probably the email address, in a URL query string.
- **Other pixel:** `https://dv.cb2.com/o/b17bc8e3-ccca-455e-ac39-f4d34f677f60?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a second tracking beacon alongside `o.gif`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** 10 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`.
  - `04404684-b4f7-4b14-b829-7fbbb4cd6205.jpg`

- **Pixels missing alt:** the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` beacon all lack `alt`. These should use `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.

Most email clients ignore `@font-face`, so these fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif. The `<title>` ("CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME") also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so body structure, button markup (VML/bulletproof) and dark-mode handling could not be checked.
