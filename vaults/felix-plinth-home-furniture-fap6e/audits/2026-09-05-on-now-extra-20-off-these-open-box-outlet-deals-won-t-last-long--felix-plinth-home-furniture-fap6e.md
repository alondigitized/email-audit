# ON NOW! Extra 20% off + These Open Box Outlet Deals won’t last long!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Same burgundy banner, same "Extra 20% off," same "starts now" energy I've now seen six times this week from Pottery Barn — I genuinely could sketch this header from memory at this point. The one new beat is "Open Box Outlet Deals," which is actually the first genuinely different hook I've gotten from them all week, but then it's immediately buried under a "Recommended Just For You" grid that's a total miss for a guy renovating two houses in a consistent style — a curtain rod, a swivel chair, an executive desk, and a farmhouse platform bed with zero visual thread between them. Then it pivots to a Halloween haunted-porch spread, which, fine, seasonal, but it's competing with three other messages in the same email for my attention.
- I'm not clicking this one. It's the sixth near-identical Pottery Barn send this week and my eye just slides past the burgundy banner now — CB2's "rugs on sale" send at least led with something I'm actually shopping for, and Article's "Up to 40% off ends Monday" had one clean deadline instead of four competing offers. This gets skimmed and archived, not forwarded.

## What's working

- "Open Box Outlet Deals" is a genuinely new hook this week — discounted returns is a real value prop I haven't seen from them yet.
- The "Free Design Services" banner with the moodboard-and-hardware photo is on-brand for someone mid-renovation like me — that's actually relevant content, just underweighted.
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" callout matches how I actually shop — I run everything through the highest-rewards card, so that's a real signal.
- Product photography is clean and consistent (same styled-room aesthetic across the grid).

## What's weak

- Four separate CTAs — sale, outlet, design services, Halloween — fighting for the same scroll, no clear hierarchy on what they actually want me to do.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid feels like nothing was actually personalized: curtain rod, dining table, swivel chair, desk, bed — no cohesive style or room story, just inventory.
- This is the same burgundy Labor Day banner and "up to 70% off" language I've already catalogued from four Pottery Barn sends this week — there's zero creative refresh.
- Halloween porch decor dropped into a furniture-sale email is jarring — I'm picking out sofas for a primary and a second home, not string lights for a stoop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with Open Box Outlet — it's the one thing that's actually different this week; give it the hero slot instead of burying it under the same recycled Labor Day banner.
- 2. Cut this down to one offer, one CTA. Split "Design Services" and "Halloween Shop" into separate sends so they don't dilute the sale message — Article's single-deadline "ends Monday" approach converts better than this grab-bag.
- 3. Make "Recommended Just For You" actually mean something — filter by category I've browsed, not a random furniture assortment.
- 4. Move the 10% Rewards callout up near the hero — that's a real motivator for me and it's currently buried below three other sections.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email renders via a legacy table-based HTML structure with heavy tracking pixel/redirect usage; automated QA found no functional link/CSP failures but flagged compliance header gaps, missing alt attributes across all images, and two insecure (http://) asset URLs.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 47 tracking/click-redirect links (e.g., `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by HTTP probing per QA output — validity unconfirmed, not necessarily broken.
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/27b58625-f4ad-4fb6-869c-6eaeaa5ebb87?mi_mid=...`, and an open-tracking beacon at `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`.
- The open-tracking pixel above transmits the recipient's full email address as a plaintext GET parameter over unencrypted HTTP (`sv_sveme=...@etell.app`) — this is a data-exposure issue (PII in cleartext over the wire), distinct from general "http:// blocked" concerns.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Every content and tracking image in the QA scan (32 instances) is missing `alt` text, including product images (e.g., `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`) and promotional banners (`F26_Top_LaborDayNoUrgency.jpg`). This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades the experience for screen-reader users and when images are blocked.
- Two images load over plain HTTP: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Mixed content on an otherwise HTTPS-served email may be blocked or flagged by mail clients.
- Head contains non-functional PHP-style `header(...)` calls inside a `<style>` block (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) — dead code with no effect in an email client context; harmless but indicates copy-paste artifact from server-side code.
- Uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype and MSO/Outlook conditional comments — standard for retail ESPs, not a defect.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient identifier is present in cleartext across multiple tracking URLs as `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (in `coreemail.html?email=...` and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel) — appears to be a resolved value, not an unresolved merge tag, so no broken-personalization defect. No `{{...}}` / `%%...%%` / unresolved token artifacts found in the visible source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found by automated QA — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed from headers as relayed; note QA caveats this may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmation the sender omitted it.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header also not found — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — authentication status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Visible unsubscribe/customer-service link present in body: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` — an in-body mechanism exists; this is separate from the missing header-level unsubscribe confirmation above.
- Cannot confirm physical mailing address in the truncated source provided (footer not included in excerpt) — not flagging as a defect, just noting it's unverified from available evidence.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- CTA and header links route through the `click.e.potterybarn.com` ESP redirect domain rather than direct UTM-tagged potterybarn.com URLs — standard ESP click-tracking pattern; cannot verify final landing-page UTM alignment since redirect targets were not resolved (skipped per QA note).
- Product image `src` attributes point directly to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` (not the redirect domain), consistent with the redirect domain being reserved for click destinations, not asset hosting — no inconsistency found here.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all content/product images (32 flagged) for accessibility and blocked-image fallback; decorative/tracking pixels can use `alt=""`.
- Migrate the two http:// image sources to https:// to avoid mixed-content blocking.
- Move the open-tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) to HTTPS to stop transmitting the recipient email address in cleartext.
- Remove the inert `header(...)` PHP snippet from the `<style>` block — copy/paste leftover with no function in email.
- Verify with the sending ESP that `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) are actually present at the MTA level, since this capture's relay may simply not have surfaced them.
