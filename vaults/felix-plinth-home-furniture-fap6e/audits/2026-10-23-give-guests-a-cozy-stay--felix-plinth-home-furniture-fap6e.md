## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an Iterable send with wrapped tracking links. The primary CTA, "Start your project", failed the QA link probe. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible portion.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Primary CTA "Start your project":** the probe raised `unknown url type: '/c/free-interior-design-services?utm_source=Iterable&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=10_26&_u_id_=7db6b71b…'`.
  - The tracker URL is `https://links.email.article.com/s/c/-wG1-zYPRvi2c…/25`.
  - The error shows the probe received a relative path (no scheme or host) as its redirect target. Either the Iterable redirect returned a relative `Location`, or the destination was stored without a domain.
  - If it's a real redirect fault, the CTA may not resolve correctly in some clients. If it's a probe artifact, the link is fine. This needs a manual click-through to settle.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 28 links were probed, and 16 were skipped. Footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** `links.email.article.com/s/eo/…` is an Iterable open pixel, and the two `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/…` URLs are also redirector-hosted. Their purpose can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images:**
  - `…/s/eo/x-YKF7-q285…` has `alt="25"`. This is almost certainly the tracking pixel, with its path suffix leaking into the alt text (the QA tool's alt-text parse appears to have picked up the URL tail). If so, it should be `alt=""`.
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/bCnEUpYX…` and `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/teakShoP…` have no alt. If they're content images, they need descriptive alt text. If they're pixels, they should have `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Malformed dark-mode CSS in the first `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma, which makes the selector list invalid. The rule is dropped, so headings and paragraphs won't be forced to white in dark mode.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a stray `;` before `!important`, so the color applies without `!important`.
  - The `@media` block is closed by `</style>` with no closing brace. Browsers recover from this, but strict parsers may not.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, Outlook `mso-` resets, `color-scheme` meta tags and a `[data-ogsc]` fallback are present.
