# GARDEN STATUES just for you
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and the subject promised garden statues "just for you." I got a bronze bear holding a welcome sign and, further down, two heron statues. That's two tiles out of roughly twenty. The rest is pergolas (twice), jute rugs, a bedding set, kitchen mats, table lamps, shoe racks, ornaments and a Santa-face light string. I'm renovating two houses, and I don't buy bear statues. Nothing here says Wayfair knows me. It reads like a catalog dumped into an email.
- The one thing I'd look at is the Pottery Barn-style bedding photo with the sage tufted duvet, and maybe the jute rug. The banner says "80% OFF, Black Friday, Ends Tonight," but it's a thin purple strip with a tiny Sealy logo. It has no product, no price and no button. Today I've already had Wayfair's red-flag "ENDS TONIGHT" send, the kitchen sinks send, the outdoor rugs send and the meat slicer send. This is the fifth. I'd skim it, maybe save the bedding to my Pinterest folder, and not click.

## What's working

- The photography is good. The sage duvet and the jute rug are styled well enough to go in my inspiration folder.
- The "Wayfair Rewards" and "Flexible Financing" icons at the bottom are the only member-benefit cues. I care about rewards math, so I noticed them.
- Every tile has a short label like "Pergolas for Less" or "Budget-Friendly End Tables." That's easy to scan.
- The "we think you'll ❤ these" grid has the home categories I actually shop: lamps, end tables, curtains and wall art.

## What's weak

- The subject says garden statues but the email is a general product dump. Statues get two tiles, and the first is a bear.
- There's no price, no percent off and no deadline on any tile. The only offer is the thin "80% OFF" banner at the top, and it has no CTA.
- The hero tiles are huge and take up most of the scroll. The useful grid sits far below the fold.
- Pergolas and statues appear twice each, so the layout looks padded.
- Nothing is relevant to renovation. There's no swatch or sample program, no furniture shipping or delivery info, and no color-palette thread.
- The images carry no shipping or availability detail. Everything is a lifestyle shot with a small label.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject-to-body mismatch. Either build the email around statues and garden decor, or drop "just for you." Right now "just for you" reads as untrue.
- 2. Put one real offer in the hero, with a number, a deadline and a button. For example: "Up to 80% off, ends tonight, shop the sale." The thin banner doesn't do this.
- 3. Cut the duplicate tiles and tighten the email to six to eight products with prices. CB2's "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" send at least gave me one clear hook, a credenza with its offer.
- 4. Add a renovation or home-project angle: delivery windows, free swatches and room bundles. Article at least sells me on a single big idea.
- 5. Dedupe across the day. One Black Friday send with segmented content would beat five near-identical ones.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, with a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block and a table-based layout. The automated QA did turn up real gaps: linked banner images with no alt text, about 20 plain-`http://` tracking pixels, and an app link whose campaign tag doesn't match the email type. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture in the relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 links were probed and all 8 non-OK results were HTTP 429, which is rate limiting and not a failure. This applies to `/daily-sales`, the `wayfair.com/?_emr=…` home links, the garden-statues and pergola filter pages, and the app link.
- **Campaign tag mismatch:** the app link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…`) has `c=Triggered`, while every other link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=2`. A batch send tagged as triggered will skew AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback:** the same link has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Desktop and non-app users go through an insecure hop, which is then redirected.
- **4 links were not probed (70s time budget):** `…must-have-jute-rugs-se78343.html?eid`, `…comforters-%26-sets-for-less-se30625`, `…our-favorite-garden-statues-se19051.` and `…pergolas-for-less--se82165.html?eid=`. They appear cut off in the QA output, which may only be display truncation. Verify them against the full HTML, because a real truncation would break the destination.
- **Possible duplicate destination:** two "garden statues" filter IDs appear, `se73878` (probed) and `se19051` (unprobed). I can't tell from the data whether this is intended.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked banner images with no alt text.** Each of these is wrapped in a link, so the link has no accessible name and screen readers will read the filename or URL:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-EndsTonight.jpg` (links to `/daily-sales`)
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png` (links to `/`)
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_2_purple.png`
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`
- **Tracking pixels flagged for missing alt:**
  - `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…`
  - 20 × `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1`
  - These are 2x1 beacons, so alt text is cosmetic. Adding `alt=""` would still clear the warnings.
- **Insecure image source:** all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels use `http://`. Some clients block or rewrite mixed-content images, so impression counts will be undercounted, and Gmail's image proxy may flag them.
- **Not verified:** the HTML was truncated, so I can't comment on a `lang` attribute, a preheader, or Outlook conditional comments.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible data. The `_emr`/`_eml` values are per-recipient and per-link IDs, as expected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, probably a capture artifact:** QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe`, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and no `Authentication-Results`. It notes these may not be preserved by the AgentMail relay, so I can't call them real defects. For a bulk sender at this scale the headers are very likely present, and checking raw headers on a direct-to-mailbox copy would settle it.
- **Footer not assessed:** the QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't in the output, and the footer is past the truncation point. I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Tracking uses proprietary parameters instead (`source`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_121290`, `mdlid`, `sltid`). This is fine if the analytics stack reads those, but it means no UTM-based attribution in GA.
- **Landing alignment:**
  - "Our Favorite Garden Statues" goes to `/daily-sales/filters/our-favorite-garden-statues-se73878.html`, which matches the subject line.
  - The primary "Shop Sale" CTA and banner go to the generic `/daily-sales` page, not a garden-statues-specific page.
- **Consistent parameters:** `csnid`, `refid` and `maiid` are the same across all links, so campaign identity is intact.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the four linked banners, for example "Black Friday ends tonight – shop deals", and `alt=""` to the pixels.
2. Move the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Change `c=Triggered` on the app link to the batch campaign value, or confirm the mismatch is intentional.
4. Confirm the 4 unprobed URLs are complete in the source HTML.
5. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a non-relay copy, and check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters alongside the proprietary ones if cross-channel reporting needs them.
7. Re-probe the 429 links with throttling to confirm they resolve.
