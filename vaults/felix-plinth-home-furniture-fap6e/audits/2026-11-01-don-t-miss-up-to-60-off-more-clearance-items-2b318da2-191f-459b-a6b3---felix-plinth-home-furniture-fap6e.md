# Don't miss: Up to 60% off more clearance items!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- I'm tired of this stream. This is the fifth Crate & Barrel Kids email since 10/31, and I still don't have a kid. The "Today only: 20% off top play furniture!" send went out earlier today. This one repeats that same banner, and the hero then says "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off" on a brown-orange panel. At a glance it reads as a clearance blast, not something chosen for me.
- The body is a bit of a mess. The play-furniture banner shows up twice, once at the top and again as a second hero. Then there's a $5000 Design Desk sweepstakes, a $500 app giveaway, a credit card ad, and a second Design Desk block. I count four different asks before the footer. Compare the 11/01 "The look you're envisioning, with 100% free design help" send, which was the only recent C&B email that fit two houses mid-renovation. This one is the Kids version of that, and I'm not the audience.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim the product row for a dresser, see "Jenny Lind 54" Deep Brown Wood Wide 7 Drawer Adult Dresser" at $1899, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and consider muting Kids.

## What's working

- The "up to 60% off" hero is big and clear. I know the offer in one second.
- A single "Shop Clearance" button sits right under the hero, and it's easy to tap.
- The category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting) gives me a fast way in.
- The "Jenny Lind 7 Drawer Adult Dresser" at $1899 is the one item that's close to my world.
- Prices with strikethroughs (Halloween bucket $14.97, Nuna car seat $1050) make the discounts concrete.

## What's weak

- Subject says "more clearance items", so this is a repeat of the last clearance send. It's the 4th/5th Kids email in about three days.
- The play-furniture banner appears twice in the same email, once as the top strip and again as a full hero.
- There are too many competing CTAs: Shop Clearance, Download Our App, Enter Now, Learn More, Book a Free Appointment. Nothing wins.
- The product picks are strollers, car seats and a swivel glider. I get a nursery stream with no kid on my profile.
- The credit card block is a mid-email ad that interrupts shopping, and the $5000 and $500 sweepstakes lines feel like fine print I'd have to decode.
- The footer disclaimer is a wall of tiny gray text I can't read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same offer three days running. Pick one hero and let the other creative rest.
- 2. Cut the duplicate play-furniture block and one of the two Design Desk blocks.
- 3. Use one primary CTA and push the sweepstakes and app promo below the products.
- 4. Segment by household. If I've been browsing sofas and dressers, show me the adult dresser, not a Nuna car seat.
- 5. Move the card ad to the bottom. Pottery Barn runs its savings strip on top and keeps the rest focused, and Wayfair's Black Friday Pop-Up banner is just as loud but only has one job.

- **Tracking pixels:** these image beacons are present:

  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
