# DOUBLE REWARDS | Up to 35% off Ninja, Ooni, Dyson & more!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Forty-three. That's where the Crate & Barrel family tally sits since Sunday, and this is the *second* copy of the "Double Rewards | Up to 35% off Ninja, Ooni, Dyson & more" send to hit my inbox — I already audited the first one a couple hours ago. Same subject, same sender, same hero. If there's a difference, the rendered email doesn't telegraph it. From a guy renovating two kitchens simultaneously, the *category* still hits — Ninja, Ooni, Dyson is exactly the appliance shortlist I'm building — but the duplicate send guts the credibility.
- The layout itself is fine. Clean grid, recognizable brand pillars stacked in the body, the "Up to 35% off" hero does its job, and the 2x Rewards bug in the corner is the one thing keeping this from feeling like background noise. Pottery Barn ran a similar "Extra 20% + Outdoor dining" send earlier in the weekend and at least *rotated* hero categories between drops; Crate is just retransmitting.
- Would I click? Maybe — but on the *first* copy, not this one. The Ooni in particular is on my list. Would I forward? No. Mark as read after a five-second skim and move on.

## What's working

- Brand names in the subject — Ninja, Ooni, Dyson — are the exact tier I'm shopping, so the inbox preview earned an open even on a duplicate.
- "Up to 35% off" + 2x Rewards is a real, stackable offer; the rewards math actually compounds for a Crate cardholder like me.
- Visual hierarchy lands the eye on the kitchen-appliance hero first, which matches the subject promise (unlike that Wayfair "WALL MOUNTED SHELVES" send that bait-and-switched me earlier this week).
- Multiple price points down the page — small appliances, cookware, knives — give me a clear "what's actually on sale" answer without me hunting.

## What's weak

- This is a *duplicate send*. Same subject line, same creative, same day as one I already opened. That's the single biggest miss.
- Forty-three emails from the C&B family in five days. The brand has lost the right to my undivided attention even when the offer is good.
- No segmentation: I'm a registry-active, two-house renovation customer and I'm getting the same blast as a first-time browser. CB2's "Now & Future Classics" felt curated; this feels mass.
- No showroom or sample/swatch hook anywhere — for furniture-adjacent shoppers like me, that's a missed cross-sell. Article does this beat better with their brand-build sends.
- "Up to 35%" without a single anchor product showing a real before/after price near the hero. I want to see "Ooni Karu 12 was $X, now $Y" — make me do less math.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Dedupe.** Do not resend an identical creative to the same address within the same 24-hour window. This is the highest-leverage change by a mile.
- 2. Lead the hero with *one* anchor SKU and its actual sale price — pick the Ninja Creami or the Ooni Karu — instead of a generic "up to 35%" claim.
- 3. Segment cardholders: show me my actual rewards balance and what 2x earns me on a $400 Dyson. The math is the moat.
- 4. Add a "shop by brand" row of logos directly under the hero — Ninja / Ooni / Dyson tiles, tap-targets sized for thumbs. The subject already promised it; deliver it above the fold.
- 5. Suppress this send for anyone who opened the morning's identical copy. That's a frequency-cap rule, not creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ooni & Dyson, 35% off + 2x Rewards through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your kitchen shortlist is 35% off (and earning 2x)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ninja Creami, Ooni Karu, Dyson V15 — sale prices inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cardholders earn double on every appliance this weekend.`

# Technical Audit — Crate & Barrel "DOUBLE REWARDS" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML scaffolding is solid (proper DOCTYPE, MSO conditionals, responsive media queries), but it suffers from systemic accessibility gaps (40+ images lacking `alt` attributes) and four broken webfont URLs returning 403. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified through the AgentMail relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 broken webfont resources (HTTP 403)** — non-fatal for email rendering (clients fall back), but indicates a misconfigured CDN ACL on `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`:
  - `1e07d600-3787-4517-bd3d-b140f9e54ec2.woff2`
  - `7ec25fd5-2752-4d84-b307-af3faabc21bb.woff2`
  - `aaa41944-25b3-4265-80b2-5564efe9b2d7.woff2`

- 22 click-redirect/tracking links present (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) — skipped by the probe, so final destinations were not verified.
- Multiple pixel/beacon images detected: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/...o.gif`, `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...` — expected for tracking but worth confirming `mi_u` token is per-recipient.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **40+ `<img>` tags missing `alt` text** — major WCAG 2.1 (1.1.1) failure and a deliverability signal for spam scoring. Includes hero/category images such as:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `1ffc8c7e-6902-43c3-91e3-f879b96ab994.jpg`
  - `df918262-0a82-4899-b3d9-8c178acad4f5.jpg`
  - (full list of 40+ image assets across `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/...`)
- Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`) should carry `alt=""` (decorative) — currently no attribute at all.
- Webfont 403s will cause fallback to system fonts; the `mso-font-fix-*` classes mitigate this on Outlook, but visual font in webmail/Gmail mobile may differ from design intent.
- HTML uses XHTML 1.0 Transitional DOCTYPE — acceptable for email, no issues with the table-based layout.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `%%firstname%%`, `{{...}}`) visible in the captured HTML source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header: NOT CAPTURED** — likely stripped by AgentMail relay, so RFC 2369 compliance cannot be confirmed from this audit.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): NOT CAPTURED** — one-click unsubscribe support unknown; required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (Feb 2024+).
- **Authentication-Results header: NOT CAPTURED** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unverifiable. From-domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a properly delegated subdomain (correct pattern), but actual auth pass/fail unknown.
- Physical address and unsubscribe link presence in the footer cannot be confirmed from the truncated source provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot verify — all CTAs route through `mi.crateandbarrel.com` click-trackers which were skipped by the probe. UTM parameter consistency and landing-page alignment would need the final resolved URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` attributes to every `<img>`** — descriptive alt for product/hero images, `alt=""` for tracking pixels and decorative spacers. This is the highest-impact fix (a11y + spam score).
2. **Fix the 4 webfont 403s** — check CORS/ACL on `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`; either restore public read or remove `@font-face` declarations referencing them to avoid console errors in webmail previews.
3. **Re-capture headers outside the AgentMail relay** to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC=pass) — these are required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
4. **Resolve a sample of the 22 tracking links** end-to-end to confirm UTM tagging, no redirect chains >2 hops, and HTTPS throughout.
5. Consider adding `role="presentation"` to layout `<table>` elements for screen-reader cleanliness (currently absent).
