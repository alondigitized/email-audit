# Just for you: a holiday sneak peek, only on our app!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — third "holiday sneak peek, only on our app" nursery blast I've scrolled past today, and this one's even less for me than the last two since I don't have kids. The hero is a pretty forest-green kids' bedroom, sure, but the whole email is nursery gliders, Jellycat stuffed animals, and baby car seats stacked $1,350–$1,450 a pop — that's not my renovation, that's someone else's registry.
- What actually stops my scroll is buried two-thirds down: "new! the card you love just got better" — a Crate Card upgrade for Reward Dollars. I'm the guy who runs everything through the highest-rewards card, so that's the one line in this whole email I'd actually read twice. Everything else — the $5,000 sweepstakes, the app download push, the Jellycat restock — is noise for my profile.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is the same "download the app for a holiday sneak peek" beat Crate & Barrel proper already ran on me today (5.00 and 6.00/10 sends), just repackaged under the Crate & Kids sub-brand with a nursery product grid bolted on. Straight to archived.

## What's working

- The hero bedroom shot is genuinely well-styled — deep green walls, canopy bed, warm lighting — the kind of room photo that would work in a Pottery Barn send too if it weren't a kid's room.
- The Crate Card "Reward Dollars" callout is relevant to how I actually pay for furniture, even if it's buried.
- Clean visual hierarchy, no render bugs — images loaded, no overlap, no lorem ipsum.
- The "we love these for you" product grid at least shows real prices, which Wayfair's blank-hero bidet email last week couldn't even manage.

## What's weak

- I'm not the customer. Nursery gliders, baby car seats, Jellycat toys — none of it maps to a childless Millennial doing a primary-plus-second-home renovation.
- "Holiday sneak peek" on September 10th is premature for the third time today — Crate & Barrel's own "Something festive just landed" send hit me with the identical framing twice already this morning.
- The primary CTA is "Download Our App," not a shoppable link — friction for someone who just wants to browse and buy, not install another app.
- Two competing offers (app download vs. $5,000 sweepstakes vs. Crate Card) fight for attention with no clear single ask.
- Zero color-palette or cross-collection tools — no swatch/sample program, no room-planner tie-in, nothing that speaks to my actual "match the lamp across three catalogs" problem.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending me Crate & Kids — segment by household composition. If I've never clicked a nursery or kids item, suppress this sub-brand entirely and route me to Crate & Barrel proper or CB2.
- 2. Lead with the Crate Card Reward Dollars offer instead of burying it — that's the one thing in here with cross-persona pull, put it up top with actual dollar/point math.
- 3. Kill the app-download CTA as the primary ask; if there's a $5,000 sweepstakes, let me enter from the browser, not from an app store detour.
- 4. Drop "holiday sneak peek" language until at least October — three sends of this exact beat in one day is starting to feel like the brand doesn't know what it already mailed me.
