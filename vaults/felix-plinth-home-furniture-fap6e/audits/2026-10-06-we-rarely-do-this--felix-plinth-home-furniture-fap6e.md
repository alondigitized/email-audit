# WE RARELY DO THIS
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and "WE RARELY DO THIS" is a lie. This is the semi-annual upholstery event I already got on 10/04 ("IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE."), now stacked on top of the Fall Design Sale from yesterday's "TIME'S RUNNING OUT". The subject promises something rare, and the body is the same two sales in a new order. The email itself is clean and well shot. It just isn't what the subject said it was.
- I'm renovating two houses, so a sectional at 25% off is a real prompt for me. The green velvet sectional hero is great, and "Up to 50% off, ends tonight" is the only urgency that has felt believable all week. But this is roughly the fifteenth CB2 send in a week. I'd open it only because I want to see what's "rare," and the answer is nothing.
- Would I click? Probably, but through my saved CB2 tab, not this email. Forward it? No. Mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero is a moody velvet sectional with a huge "UP TO 25% OFF" that reads at thumbnail size. I knew the offer in one second.
- The "Ends tonight" tag over the Fall Design Sale is credible urgency. It matches yesterday's "ends tomorrow" countdown.
- The sectionals, sofas, accent chairs and benches/ottomans grid is how I actually shop. I'm comparing categories across two houses.
- The free design appointment with the $5,000 refresh giveaway is a nice showroom-adjacent touch. That's the kind of thing I want from a furniture brand.
- The "Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars" block with the Crate & Barrel cross-credit speaks to how I buy. It's the rewards math I care about.

## What's weak

- The subject says "rare," but I've seen this upholstery event twice in three days. It's clickbait that I clocked immediately.
- Two sales fight for the top slot: 25% off upholstery and 50% off "select" fall design. It's not clear whether they stack, and the fine print says the 50% is limited to select furniture, lighting and more.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny. There are three separate legal blocks (upholstery exclusions, sweepstakes, sale terms), and none of it is readable on a phone without zooming.
- There's no price, no named product and no swatch or fabric detail. "Sectionals" tells me nothing about lead time or custom versus in-stock. Last week's "IN STOCK. CUSTOM." was more useful on that.
- The footer is crowded: app, card, text signup, and a toast graphic. It's a lot after a long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the email. This is a recurring upholstery sale, so say so, and name the deadline.
- 2. Pick one hero offer per send. Run the upholstery event and the Fall Design Sale on separate days, or say clearly how they combine.
- 3. Show two or three real products with prices, and flag in-stock versus custom with delivery dates. Pottery Barn's plaid email at least sold me a specific pattern, and Article's clearance email led with a hard "up to 50% off."
- 4. Cut the footer to one or two modules. Move the sweepstakes terms behind a link.
- 5. Fix the cadence. Wayfair's blank-hero patio email wasn't a good send either, but it wasn't the fifteenth in a week.
