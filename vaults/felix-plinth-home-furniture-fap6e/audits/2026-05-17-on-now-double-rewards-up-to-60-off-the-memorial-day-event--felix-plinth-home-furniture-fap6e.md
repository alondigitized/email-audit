# ON NOW! Double Rewards + up to 60% off The Memorial Day Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I've been counting. This is the sixth Crate & Barrel family email in roughly 48 hours, and by now I have a dedicated folder for these. That said — this one actually has the most aggressive offer in the cycle. Double Rewards *plus* up to 60% off is the clearest value proposition they've put in front of me all week, and the hero communicates it in under two seconds, which earns some goodwill back.
- Then the email keeps scrolling. Past the furniture grid, there's a full Crate & Kids section dropped right in the middle — and I do not have children. I'm in the middle of furnishing two houses simultaneously, not a nursery. That section burned my patience. Below it was something called "TheDesignDesk," which is editorial content I didn't ask for when I'm in sale-mode. Pottery Barn's Memorial Day send this week stayed tight — outdoor furniture, clear deadline, one lane. This email tries to be three emails.
- I'd mark it as read and come back before the event ends because the deal is real. I would not forward it to a friend. The Crate & Kids detour would confuse literally anyone I know who doesn't have a toddler.

## What's working

- **Hero offer is fast and legible** — "It's on! Double Rewards" and "up to 60% off The Memorial Day Event" lands in the first glance
- **Memorial Day timing is genuine** — I'm actively purchasing furniture for two properties; seasonal alignment is as good as it gets
- **Double Rewards loyalty hook lands here** — this is the most specific rewards pitch in this send cycle and it feels earned rather than tacked on
- **Clean render** — layout holds, images load, no overlapping text or broken grid
- **Coastal lifestyle photography** in the hero matches the season and brand register

## What's weak

- **Crate & Kids section is a targeting miss** — I'm 36 and buying dining tables; segmenting this out costs nothing and tightens everything
- **"ON NOW!" reads a bit desperate** — coming after five prior C&B family emails in 48 hours, the exclamation-point urgency feels like crying wolf
- **"TheDesignDesk" module mid-email** — editorial content kills conversion momentum in a sale send; Article's welcome email this week handled editorial *and* commerce cleanly, this one doesn't
- **No specific hero product with a price** — Pottery Barn's Memorial Day send showed an actual sectional with a struck-out price; this stays at the lifestyle-image level and makes me do extra work
- **"Up to 60% off" language** — classic qualifier; if three obscure items are 60% off, the headline is doing sleight of hand

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment Crate & Kids out for non-parent accounts** — this alone removes the biggest scroll-stopper
- 2. **Kill TheDesignDesk for this specific send** — editorial belongs in a nurture email, not a sale-event blast
- 3. **Add a hard deadline** — "Ends Monday at midnight" would make the "ON NOW!" urgency credible instead of ambient; Article had this nailed in their welcome cadence
- 4. **Lead with a named hero product + crossed-out price** — a specific item beats lifestyle photography in a sale context every time
- 5. **Trim the subject and move the number forward** — the discount percentage should be the lead, not the exclamation
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off + Double Rewards — Memorial Day, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Double your rewards all weekend: up to 60% off now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, lighting — all on sale through Memorial Day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn double points on every purchase. Shop the Memorial Day Event.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**FAIL — Zoom blocked:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-to-zoom. WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 violation.

**FAIL — Global text-size override:**
```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -ms-text-size-adjust: none; }
```
Applied on the wildcard selector — overrides user OS accessibility font-size settings across all elements, not just the body.

**WARN — Dark mode suppressed:**
```html
<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">
<meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light">
```
Explicitly disables dark mode rendering. Background `#e6e7e8` set on `body` will remain in dark-mode clients that respect these declarations; images without dark-mode variants will likely clash.

**WARN — Conflicting `img` max-width breakpoints:**
```css
@media only screen and (min-width: 768px) { img { max-width: 600px } }
@media only screen and (min-width: 640px) { img { max-width: 640px } }
```
The `640px` rule is declared *after* the `768px` rule. On screens ≥768px, both fire and the later cascade wins (`640px`), making the `768px` rule a dead rule. Likely unintentional; images on large viewports get 640px cap instead of the intended 600px.

**WARN — Global paragraph center-align:**
```css
p { margin: 0 auto; text-align: center; }
```
Forces center alignment on every `<p>` in the document. Any left-aligned body copy in `<p>` tags will be overridden unless explicitly overridden inline.

**INFO — Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags:**
`telephone=no`, `address=no`, and `date=no` each appear twice (once in the combined declaration, once standalone). Harmless but indicates copy-paste without cleanup.

**INFO — Vestigial MSO conditional block:**
```html
<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->
```
Empty no-op conditional. Dead code from template.
