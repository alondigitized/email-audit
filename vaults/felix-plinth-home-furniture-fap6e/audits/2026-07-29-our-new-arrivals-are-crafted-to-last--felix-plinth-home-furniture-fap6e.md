# Our new arrivals are crafted to last→
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, back again. This is the eighth-ish of these in my inbox this stretch and I still don't have kids, still don't want a crib, still don't want a "GREENGUARD Gold Certified" changing table. The headline hasn't even changed language from last time — "500+ new arrivals starting at $29" is the exact same hero I already saw and scored a 5 on. I scrolled past a crib, a nursery, a playroom, a $500 sweepstakes, and a "50% off summer sale" banner all in one email, and none of it moved the needle because none of it is furniture for grown-ups.
- The one thing that made me pause for half a second was the "new! the card you've been waiting for" credit card teaser near the bottom — rewards cards I actually pay attention to — but it's buried under six other modules and it's a kids-brand card, not something I'd apply for. Compare that to CB2's "24 hours left for the Furniture Event," which I gave an 8 because it led with real urgency and an actual room shot I could picture in my own place.
- Verdict: archive without opening the linked pages. Not forwarding this — the only person I'd forward a Crate & Kids email to is a friend who just had a baby, and I don't have one of those right now.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text, no broken images, easy to scan even if none of it applies to me.
- The nursery and playroom room-shots are genuinely well-styled — deep green walls, warm wood tones — Crate & Barrel's design team clearly touches this sub-brand too.
- "$500 sweepstakes" and "50% off summer sale" banners at least break up the product-grid monotony instead of it being wall-to-wall cribs.
- Consistent brand chrome top to bottom (logo, footer, social icons) — feels like a real, trustworthy sender, not spam.

## What's weak

- Wrong list entirely — I'm on Pottery Barn, Crate & Barrel, and Wayfair lists for my own house, not Crate & Kids. Nothing here is for a childless 36-year-old renovating two homes.
- Same exact hero copy ("500+ new arrivals, starting at $29") I already saw and rated 5/10 on a prior send — zero creative refresh.
- Six-plus product modules (bedroom, nursery, playroom, sweepstakes, sale, "we love these for you") is a wall of scrolling with no single clear CTA — nothing like CB2's one-offer-one-deadline approach.
- The credit card pitch is the only relevant-adjacent thing in here and it's crammed at the bottom after I've already checked out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress me from Crate & Kids sends entirely and cross-sell me into Crate & Barrel or CB2 adult-furniture lists instead — that's the single biggest lift, since no subject line fixes a fundamentally mistargeted send.
- 2. If Crate & Kids must keep emailing broad lists, lead with one hero offer and one deadline (steal CB2's "24 hours left" playbook) instead of six stacked product grids.
- 3. Move the credit-card teaser up near the top if it's meant to reach non-parents — rewards cards are the one thing in this email that could hook someone like me.
- 4. Vary the hero copy send-to-send; reusing "500+ new arrivals starting at $29" verbatim reads as stale even to a parent, let alone to me.
