# ON NOW: Extra 20% off select styles
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Pottery Barn email forty-three for me, and it's the **second one today** — June 26 already started with "Our 4th of July Sale STARTS NOW 💥" and now this lands a few hours later with a slightly different headline. At some point the inbox starts looking like a fire hose, and this is the second burst.
- That said, when I open it there's actually more useful content here than a carbon-copy sale reminder. The Open Box Outlet module is legitimately interesting — I'm furnishing two houses at the same time on a budget with real caps, and open-box furniture at an extra 20% off is the kind of deal that actually moves me. The "Recommended Just For You" tiles are a nice touch too. So the email earns some credit; the problem is structural, not conceptual.
- Would I click? Yes — once, on the Open Box section, and maybe a second time on the personalized picks. Would I forward it to a friend? No. Would I have preferred it be the one email instead of the second email today? Absolutely.

## What's working

- The **Open Box Outlet module** is the single best content decision in this email — Wayfair ran a full "Open Box steals" send on the same day (which I'd score an 8) and it dominated because it led with that angle; PB has the same ammunition here.
- **"Recommended Just For You"** with actual product tiles beats a generic category grid — I can see specific items that presumably reflect my browse history, which saves me from doing manual triage.
- The **category nav row** under the hero (Furniture, Outdoor, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs, etc.) is practical — for someone renovating two homes it functions as a quick-filter shortcut rather than filler.
- The **Rewards section and Affirm module** are both present — I run the rewards math on every big purchase, and BNPL matters when a single furniture order can hit four figures.
- The **4th of July holiday anchor** makes the urgency feel earned rather than manufactured — it gives the "ON NOW" language a credible expiry frame.

## What's weak

- **Two Pottery Barn emails in one calendar day is one too many** — the "STARTS NOW 💥" send already established the sale; this feels like an anxious noon-hour retrigger when conversions didn't hit by 10am.
- **"Select styles" is the phrase that will cost them a click** — I've been burned enough times at checkout discovering the sectional I wanted is excluded that I now treat this qualifier as a yellow flag, not a green one.
- The **Open Box section is buried below the fold** — that's the highest-differentiation content in this email and it's sandwiched between the main sale hero and the personalized picks. Article's "4th of July Sale starts now" send (cleaner, one module, one CTA) made me want to click faster than this kitchen-sink layout.
- **"Discover New Arrivals" has no business being in a sale email** — it's off-message and fractures the hierarchy. CB2 keeps promotions and new arrivals in separate sends; PB is mashing them together.
- **No explicit end date visible** anywhere — "ON NOW" implies urgency without a deadline, which I've learned to distrust.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with Open Box** — make that the hero module, not a secondary section. "Returned pieces, extra 20% off" is a real click trigger for renovation-budget shoppers and it differentiates from every other brand running a straight 4th of July sale.
- 2. **Suppress one of the two daily sends** — retarget non-openers of the morning email, don't blast the full list again by afternoon.
- 3. **Kill the New Arrivals module** — wrong email, wrong message, wrong timing; it belongs in a dedicated send next week.
- 4. **Resolve "select styles"** — either name what's included prominently ("extra 20% off furniture and outdoor") or acknowledge upfront what's excluded. Vague qualifiers at the subject line level are the surest way to lose trust at checkout.
- 5. **Add a count to the Open Box section** — "142 pieces available" creates real scarcity without manufactured urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open Box + Extra 20% off — furniture that came back at clearance prices`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The returned pieces. Now with an extra 20% off.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open box outdoor, sofas & rugs at up to 70% off — 4th of July sale ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance pricing + your personalized picks, all in one place`
