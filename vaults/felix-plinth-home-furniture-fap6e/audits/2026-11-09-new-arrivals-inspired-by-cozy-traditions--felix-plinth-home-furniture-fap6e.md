# NEW ARRIVALS inspired by cozy traditions
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh Pottery Barn email in about five days, and my eye is twitching again. The 11/09 "New arrivals await in The Christmas Shop" send and the "Now shipping free 🎄 Holiday essentials" send went out the same day. The hero is the same cozy fireplace, plaid pillows and tree story. The only real change is the stack of category tiles underneath.
- The category-tile layout is actually useful for me. I'm renovating two houses, so I scan for furniture, lighting and tabletop, not stockings. I did like "Recommended Just For You." That block has the Toscana Extendable Dining Table, the Banks Extendable Dining Table, a curtain rod and a mattress. It's the first time this week anything looked like it knew I'm mid-renovation.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the Banks dining table, but I'd already be in a Pottery Barn tab from the last ten emails. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder and not forward it. Nothing here is new to me.

## What's working

- The category tiles (Furniture, Armchairs, Lighting, Bedding, Decor, Tabletop & Bar) let me skip the Christmas stuff and go straight to what I'm shopping for.
- The "Ships Free" badges on Pillows, Bedding and Tabletop make the shipping clear. That matters for furniture-adjacent buys.
- The lighting tile with the two table lamps is clean and catalog-quality.
- "Recommended Just For You" has dining tables and a mattress, which matches my renovation shopping.
- The Design Crew "Book a Free Appointment" block is a real showroom and design-service hook, and it's what I'd want from a brand like this.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" card callout fits my rewards-card habit.

## What's weak

- The Fall Sale strip, "Up to 70% off," is still at the top in mid-November. It has been on every send since 10/06, and it fights the Christmas imagery below it.
- "NEW ARRIVALS" with a "Shop Now" button is generic. I can't tell which of the 70% off items are actually new.
- The Christmas Shop, Pillows & Throws and the Decor tile are the same plaid-ribbon story I've seen in at least six sends this week.
- The legal and fine-print block is long and dense, and it's tiny on a phone. That hurts the "honest offer" read.
- There are no prices or discounts on the category tiles, so I don't know what the offer is until I click.
- There are no swatch or sample callouts, and no color-palette guidance. For a two-house reno I want to see fabric and finish options.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap the cadence. Two Pottery Barn sends a day is the real problem. Crate & Barrel's "Double Rewards + Up to 30% off drinkware & more!" gave me one clear offer and a reason to open. Pottery Barn gives me ten near-identical ones.
- 2. Pick one offer. Drop the stale Fall Sale strip or make it the hero, and state the discount in the hero so I'm not guessing.
- 3. Segment by intent. I've been looking at dining tables, so lead with them. Move "Recommended Just For You" up to the top.
- 4. Add swatch or sample links under Furniture and Bedding. CB2 and Article both lean on this kind of detail.
- 5. Put prices or "from $X" on each tile.
- 6. Shrink the legal block or collapse it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extendable dining tables, table lamps, and free shipping on bedding.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with about 54 ESP-wrapped click links. Its main technical problems are widespread missing image alt text, three `http://` image sources, and the recipient's address in cleartext in tracking and image URLs. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, not failed, because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 54 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/…`). Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network beacon with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from the West Elm CDN. This is a dependency risk, not a defect.
- **Second open-tracking image:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/f634186a-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a separate open pixel from the `svopn` one.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - QA flags about 46 images. These include all `slices_01–11.jpg` under `/H26_NewArrivals_B6_NewArrivalsGridCottageChristmas/`, the 18 `slices_*.jpg` under `/Email-Footer/`, `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, and the product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, and others).
  - The QA output does not distinguish an absent `alt` from an empty `alt=""`. Only the tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif` should legitimately be decorative.
  - Sliced image grids with no alt text have no text equivalent for screen readers or for images-off clients.
- **Mixed content:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` can be blocked or rewritten by some clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** This is an accessibility and metadata gap.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP-style text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`). It is invalid CSS and leaked template code. It may be ignored, but it can break parsing of the rules that follow it.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"`, unlike the other layout tables.
- **Legacy attribute selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` only match when the class attribute is exactly that value. The `@media (max-width:480px)` rules will not match elements with extra classes.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so the fallback font stack matters.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the truncated source. The footer and the remainder of the body were not inspected.
- **PII in URLs:** The recipient's address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears in cleartext in two places:
  - the `sv_sveme=` parameter on the HTTP `svopn` pixel;
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, which is loaded as an image.
- The HTTP request exposes the address in transit and in server logs. Neither URL is hashed.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool says the relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`. Alignment cannot be assessed from this data.
- **Footer:** The visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.
- **Hidden preheader:** The preheader uses `display:none` with `mso-hide:all`, which is standard and not an issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All 54 click links go through the opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirector. UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be confirmed because the probe skipped them.
- The product image sources are direct `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` URLs, but their click targets are unknown.
- Several creative paths look stale or reused: `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` and `/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/` (non-member banner). These are worth checking for current relevance, but they are not confirmed errors.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, starting with the product tiles and the `H26_NewArrivals…/slices_*.jpg` grid. Use `alt=""` explicitly on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in the pixel and `coreemail.html` query strings. Use an opaque or hashed ID.
4. Remove the PHP `header()` text from the `<style>` block, fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the spacer table.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
7. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 54 tracking links with a follow-redirects check, and record the final URLs and UTM parameters.
8. Inspect the footer HTML for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
