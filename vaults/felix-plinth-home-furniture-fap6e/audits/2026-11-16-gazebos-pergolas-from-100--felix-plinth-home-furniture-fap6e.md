# Gazebos & pergolas from $100
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and I'm numb. This is the eighth send since 11/09. The subject promised gazebos and pergolas from $100, and the hero is a gazebo. That's fine, but I'm renovating two houses, and a $100 gazebo is not what I'm shopping for. Then the email wanders off into kitchen mats, Christmas trees, deck boxes, kitchen trash cans and a bath mat. It's the same everything-bagel as "Popular Picks" from 11/15 and the pergola grid from 11/14.
- The "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner at the top is real, but it's a small strip and it's buried under a purple "score styles you ❤️" header. Pottery Barn's maroon banner leads with the sale, and Wayfair doesn't. The email has an accent chair and a table lamp I'd actually look at, but they sit well down the page.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim the accent chair, maybe save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero photo is a clean, big gazebo shot. The subject and hero match, so I know what I opened.
- The Sealy-sponsored "Early Black Friday up to 70% off" strip is a clear, current seasonal hook.
- "Get 5% rewards… on every item, always" is a plain loyalty line, and I'm a rewards-card guy, so I noticed it.
- The accent chair under $200 and the table lamp tile are on target for a renovation shopper.
- The tiles have short, specific price labels ("Quilts Under $75", "Bath Mats & Rugs Under $25"), so I know the price tier without clicking.

## What's weak

- The "from $100" and "from $50" gazebo and pergola tiles repeat each other. The hero, "On sale now: pergolas" and "Pergolas and gazebos from $50" are three tiles of the same thing.
- The grid is scattered: Christmas trees, deck boxes, kitchen sinks, trash cans. I can't tell what Wayfair thinks I want.
- "picked just for you" is a false promise. Nothing here was picked for someone renovating two houses, and it's the same grid everyone got.
- The Black Friday banner is small, and the "up to 70% off" has no named products or end date.
- There are no swatch or sample cues, no shipping info on the furniture, and no color-palette cohesion. It's a catalog dump.
- Wayfair's table lamp tile has a lot of competition. I've seen the same lamp in three brands' emails this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the grid from 20+ tiles to about 8, and make them one category: outdoor, or accent chairs and lighting. Pick one.
- 2. Make the Black Friday banner the hero, with a real deadline and the discount tied to a product.
- 3. Drop the duplicate pergola tiles and use the room for the accent chair as a second hero.
- 4. Add shipping clarity for large furniture ("Free shipping, arrives by Dec 5"). Crate & Barrel and CB2 lead with Double Rewards, and Wayfair's 5% could be the same kind of hook.
- 5. Replace "picked just for you" with something honest, like "Chairs and lamps for your reno list", or actually segment on browsing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Accent chairs under $200 + 5% back`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pergolas from $100, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early Black Friday: up to 70% off outdoor, seating and lighting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `5% back on every order, plus quick-ship deck boxes and lamps.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a mobile media query and the `u ~ div` Gmail fix. Its main code-level problems are missing alt text on several content images and 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed failures. The HTML source was truncated, so only the head and CSS were inspected directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 links were probed. Every flagged result was a 429 (rate limit), which means "unverified", not "broken". Affected URLs include `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=2` and the gazebo event link `.../gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se81255.html?eid=569893`.
- **4 links were not checked** because probing stopped after 70s:
  - `.../kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.htm`
  - `.../popular-picks-se49538.html?eid=35623`
  - `.../pergolas-and-gazebos-from-%2450-se81...`
  - `.../wayfair-rewards?_emr=...`

  The last two are truncated in the QA output, so I can't tell whether the links themselves are truncated.
- **Per-link unique `_eml` tokens.** Each link carries its own `_eml` GUID, for example `_eml=18173392-...` on the gazebo link and `_eml=aa5cd798-...` on the pergola link. The `Get Exclusive Deals on the App` link appears twice with different `_eml` values (`85b0010b-...` and `441c6455-...`). That is expected for per-placement click tracking.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3Blbi...`. It is HTTPS and decodes to `eventType=EmailOpen`.
- **Campaign label mismatch in the app deep link.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me` URL uses `c=Triggered`, but the same URL carries `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A "Triggered" campaign label on a batch send will misattribute in AppsFlyer reporting.
- **Insecure web fallback.** The deep link's `af_web_dp` is `http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, which is `http://`. It relies on a redirect to upgrade to HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Impression pixels use `http://`.** 20 images from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1` have no alt text and a non-HTTPS source. Clients that block mixed or insecure content may drop them, so ad-impression counts will be under-reported. They also add 20 extra requests per open.
- **Missing alt on content images.**
  - `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-VP.jpg` is wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales`. The QA tool reports the link text as `[image: WFUS_1112...]`, so the link has no accessible name.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png` is also linked, with the same problem.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_1_purple.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_3_purple.png`
  - `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24...`
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`
  - `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`. If it carries legal or terms text, that text is invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
- **Image-only content:** several of these are banners that probably carry the offer text, so with images blocked the offer is lost.
- **`blankBanner (1).png`** is a placeholder-style asset. It likely needs `alt=""` or removal.
- **Doctype:** XHTML 1.0 Transitional with the `xmlns` attribute is standard for email. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the HTML provided or in any probed URL. The `_emr` and `_eml` values are fully resolved GUIDs. The body was truncated, so this covers only what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) was not found. The QA tool itself attributes these to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so don't treat them as sender failures.
- **From domain:** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a subdomain, so SPF/DKIM alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer not seen.** The physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but no result for it is listed in the output I have.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary params (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121289`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`). That is consistent and well structured for Wayfair's own system. Third-party analytics such as GA will see these sessions as direct or unattributed unless Wayfair maps the params server-side.
- **Subject alignment:** the subject "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" matches the link to `/daily-sales/filters/gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se81255.html?eid=569893`. The URL slug carries the same $100 price point, so the targeting is consistent.
- **Landing page validity:** this could not be confirmed because the probe was rate-limited (429).
- **Event params:** `unifiedeventid` and `eid` match on the two event links (569893 and 603456).

2. Add descriptive alt text to the linked banners (`SkinnyTop-VP`, `event_top_1_purple`) so the links have accessible names.
3. Add alt text to the disclaimer image, or move its legal text into live HTML.
4. Fix the `c=Triggered` label on the app deep link, or document why it is correct.
5. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
6. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers, not the relay copy, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Re-probe the 4 unchecked links and the 429'd links with throttling or backoff so they get a pass/fail result.
8. Consider adding `utm_*` params for third-party analytics, if cross-tool attribution matters.
