# Don’t miss out: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Crate & Barrel send in two days, and this one comes from the Kids sender again. My gut reaction was "I know this one already." The red-and-plaid hero says "don't miss out! the fall sales event: up to 50% off," which is nearly word for word what I got earlier today as "Don't miss it: Up to 50% off the Fall Sales Event!" The main difference is the sender name. I'm renovating two houses, and not one of them has a nursery.
- The email isn't bad. The offer is clear, and the 2x Rewards banner is the one thing that makes my rewards-card brain pay attention. But a long scroll of strollers, a Wonderbold baby gear promo, toddler dressers and a baby registry block is aimed at someone else. Kids furniture, rugs and decor are the only parts that even loosely apply to me.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim it, notice it's a repeat, and file it. I wouldn't forward it. Anyone I'd send it to would be a parent, and I'm not one.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and readable: "up to 50% off" in big lowercase type, with a clear "Shop Now" button underneath.
- The "last Double Rewards this year!" banner is real urgency. It gives a deadline (Nov 17) and a loyalty hook, and the rewards math is the only thing that moves me.
- The 2x3 category grid with "up to 50% off" labels (furniture, rugs, decor & storage, clearance) is scannable and easy to tap on a phone.
- The "new! the card you love just got better" block explains the Crate Card clearly, and I'm a rewards-card person.
- The photography is consistent and well styled, and the Crate palette stays on brand.

## What's weak

- It's a repeat. I've now had "Double Rewards + Up to 30% off drinkware & more!" on 11/09, the Fall Sales Event send earlier today, and this one. That's the same hero again.
- Most of the email is stroller and baby gear. The Nuna and UPPAbaby products and the "up to 20% off select baby gear" tile mean nothing to me, and the registry block is worse.
- The "we love these for you" product row isn't personalized. It's strollers and a toddler dresser for a guy who has been browsing lamps and nightstands.
- The "The Design Desk" free-appointment pitch and the $5000 sweepstakes feel bolted on. They pull focus from the offer.
- The footer fine print is a wall of tiny text. The exclusions on "up to 50% off" are buried down there.
- The red rewards banner is repeated twice and the email is very long, so the second half feels like padding.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by buyer. A home-renovation shopper shouldn't get a Kids-sender email. Send me the main Crate & Barrel version with furniture, lighting and rugs, and drop the strollers.
- 2. Put a real subject-line deadline in. "Don't miss out" is the same line Crate used earlier today, so it needs something new.
- 3. Cut the email to a single hero, one rewards banner, and one product grid. Move the Design Desk and sweepstakes into a separate send.
- 4. Show the rewards math. For example: "Spend $1,000, earn 20% back in Reward Dollars." That's the kind of number I'd actually calculate.
- 5. Stop sending the same hero repeatedly in one week. Pottery Barn's "Nightstands starting at $349" had one specific product category in the subject line and a clean grid, and that kind of focus is what pulls me in. Wayfair's pergola emails prove that repeating one hero all week trains me to ignore you.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of 2026, 7 days left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars on sofas, rugs and lighting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale prices plus 2x Rewards. Ends Nov 17.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style table layout (XHTML 1.0 Transitional with VML/MSO fallbacks) with 78 click-tracked links. The automated QA pass is 57%, with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The main code-level problems are about 19 content images with no alt text, a recipient-identifying hash sent to a third-party pixel, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The header-level warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** All 78 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status codes, redirect chains and broken links are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels are embedded in the body.**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels (LiveRamp).
  - Six `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` pixels.
  - One `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` open pixel.
  - One `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/07117397-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` pixel.
- **The same per-recipient hash appears in two places.** The `rlcdn.com` `s=` value (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8`) matches the `mi_u=` prefix on the first-party pixels. A persistent recipient identifier is being passed to a third-party ad-data domain. This is a privacy and disclosure concern, and it is only confirmed as far as the URLs show.
- **Redundant pixels.** Duplicating the same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` six times and `448796.gif` five times adds requests with no evident purpose. The `n=1…5` parameter suggests deliberate repetition, but the purpose can't be determined from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA data flags about 19 content images with no alt attribute:
  - 17 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` or `/m/47/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `26171910-…jpg`, `8c4ce5d9-….gif`).
  - Two on `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  
  With images blocked or a screen reader in use, these will read as filenames or be skipped. If they carry offer or CTA text, that content is lost.
- **Tracking pixels flagged for alt.** The 13 pixel images listed in section 2 have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 sizing so assistive tech ignores them.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Markup hygiene** (minor):
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed under an XHTML doctype, and the Content-Type meta is declared twice.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the source.
- **Good.** `lang="en"`, Outlook VML/MSO conditionals, and the `@media (max-width:460px)` responsive overrides are all present.
