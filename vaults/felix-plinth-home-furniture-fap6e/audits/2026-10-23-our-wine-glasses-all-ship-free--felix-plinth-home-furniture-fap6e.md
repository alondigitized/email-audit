- The subject promises "all ship free", but I couldn't find a free-shipping line in the body. That reads like a bait-and-switch.
- The email is long and has no single focus. Glassware, cocktails, furniture, a $5,000 sweepstakes and an app download all compete for attention.

- There are many tiny "Shop Now" buttons, and no price or product name sits next to most of them.

- 2. Cut the email to one theme, either "set the table" with glasses and dining furniture, or the in-stock furniture story. Move the sweepstakes and app promo to the bottom or into a separate send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout from the Movable Ink / ESP stack. It is image-sliced, and the only confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes and a few markup and accessibility problems in the `<head>`. The automated header and link checks were inconclusive because of relay and tooling limits, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 66 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Final destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times. The `s=` value looks recipient-specific. These are LiveRamp-domain pixels, and some clients and privacy tools block or flag them. The repeated calls add no value, since a single call would do the same job.
- **Personalized Movable Ink assets:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` (7 images) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/cfb245fd-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are dynamic. They need a fallback if the MI service is slow or blocked, and the QA data doesn't show whether one exists.
- **Tracking pixel:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` is present. Missing `alt` on it is a flag only; the pixel itself is not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 34 `<img>` elements have no `alt` attribute.
  - Content images with no `alt`: the 12 Scene7 slices (`202504_CB_EntTurnaround_Wine_Slice1_HERO` through `Slice12_…`), plus the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…` images (`bbf6f9d4-…png`, `56be7a63-…jpg`, `a061e037-…jpg`, `5c7b78d8-…gif`, `2f3f07fc-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`).
  - The hero slice carries the "ships free" offer. With images off or a screen reader, none of the message is available.
  - Pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`) should carry `alt=""`.
- **Image-sliced layout:** the file names show a 12-slice build from a single design. Slice 7 (`Recipe_RULE`) and Slice 12 (`FinalRule`) are decorative rules and should be `alt=""`. This build has a greater risk of broken-image gaps, and image-off rendering is worse as a result.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-resize blocking:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and the `html`/`body` rules `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` disable text-size adjustment on iOS.
- **Markup validity:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv Content-Type` tags.
  - Redundant `format-detection` metas, repeated three times.
  - A leftover template comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Conflicting desktop rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` conflict. The 768px rule overrides the 640px one only through source order.
- **Language and dark mode:** `<html lang="en">` is present, which is good. `color-scheme: light` is declared, so there is no dark-mode adaptation.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) in the visible source, and the `mi_u`/`mi_mid` parameters are resolved. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are flagged as not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so it is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Without them, Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (RFC 8058 one-click) can't be verified from this sample.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Body-level compliance:** the footer was truncated, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 66 links are behind tracking redirects and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "wine glasses ship free" offer can't be checked. No evidence of a mismatch was found.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the Slice 1 hero (it carries the "ships free" message). Set `alt=""` on decorative slices (7 and 12) and on tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), and remove the duplicate meta tags and the template placeholder comment.
4. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` pixel calls to one, and confirm they are covered by the privacy disclosure and consent basis.
5. Test with a direct-from-ESP capture, not the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
6. Resolve the redirect chains with a browser-based or authenticated probe to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
7. Confirm the Movable Ink blocks have a static fallback when the personalized image fails to load.
