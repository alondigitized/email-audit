# Early Black Friday Starts TODAY! Up to 50% off + Earn 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the Crate & Barrel **Kids** sender, and it's the seventh or eighth C&B-family email I've had in about three days. My gut reaction was "oh, another one." The hero is a red block that says "last Double Rewards this year!", then "starts today! up to 50% off Early Black Friday." That's contradictory. "Last" and "starts today" in the same breath, and the "last Double Rewards" line shows up a second time further down. It reads like a stack of promos rather than one clear message.
- I'm renovating two houses, not buying nursery gear. The grid is cribs, kids bedding, baby bedding, rugs and a Wonderful stroller wagon. None of that is for me. The kitchen collection tile ("Our Kids Kitchen Collection") and the Black Friday deals sign-up are the only things that come close to my world. I'd skim it for the 2x Rewards mechanic and move on. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it.
- Compared with Pottery Barn's "Up to 70% off across the home," which at least leads with furniture I'd buy, this feels like it's for a different customer. Wayfair's 72-hour clearout banner is repetitive, but it's at least on my categories.

## What's working

- The red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner is the first thing my eye lands on. As a rewards-card guy, 2x with a hard "Nov 17" date is the one credible deadline here.
- "Up to 50% off" is a single clear number, and the white "Shop Now" button on the hero is easy to find and tap.
- Each product tile is labeled ("up to 50% off furniture," "kids bedding & bath"). "Ships free*" on bedding is a useful shipping cue.
- The Design Desk "free design appointment" block is a nice touch and speaks to the showroom/design-help side of how I shop.
- The "we love these for you" product block has actual prices ($1,469 and $1,559 for the Nuna strollers), so I can compare at a glance.

## What's weak

- The "last Double Rewards" message is repeated almost word for word in the hero and again in the second red block. It's the same offer twice, and that's padding.
- "Starts today!" next to "last Double Rewards" is confusing. Is this the beginning or the end of something?
- The kids and baby focus is irrelevant to me. Nothing here relates to my living room, dining or kitchen renovation, and nothing about swatches, delivery windows or color coordination.
- The "Shop the Collection" button in the kitchen block is pale gray on a light background and looks disabled. It barely reads as a button.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. The exclusions are buried there, not near the offers, which makes the "up to 50%" feel less honest.
- The email is very long. Between the app banner, the new credit card pitch, and the Design Desk, the CTAs compete and no single one wins.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one message. Pick either "Black Friday starts today" or "last Double Rewards," not both, and drop the duplicate red block.
- 2. Suppress or segment the Kids send for people who clearly shop furniture, lighting and kitchen. I've gotten the same Double Rewards message from the main C&B list all week.
- 3. Fix the low-contrast "Shop the Collection" button so it's clearly tappable.
- 4. Move the key exclusions up under the offer instead of burying them in the footer.
- 5. Cut the app download and card-launch sections, or move them to a separate send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off kids' furniture + 2x Rewards through Nov 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of 2026 ends Nov 17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, bedding and rugs up to 50% off. Free shipping on bedding.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 2x Reward Dollars back on your Crate Card, today through Nov 17.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with `lang="en"`, an MSO/VML fallback and a mobile media query. The QA pass flags about 31 images without alt text, three header checks that could not be run, and 75 click-tracking links that were never probed. The source supplied is truncated, so body-level checks (links, tokens, footer) are limited to the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **75 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels are embedded.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…1015f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` are third-party pixels. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string, which looks like a hashed subscriber identifier. This is a third-party data-sharing vector that should be covered by the privacy policy and consent.
- **Open and render pixels are present** on first-party hosts: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/373b80e1-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Each carries a per-recipient `mi_u` token.
- **Six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` images use an identical URL.** This looks like a dynamic recommendation or personalization render. If the same image is served to six slots, either the slots are intentionally identical or the content is not differentiated per slot. The truncated source doesn't let me tell which.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 31 images have no alt text** (the list may be cut off after the last entry).
  - 18 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (the `/m/47/` path appears once). Examples: `26171910-0ada-47d9-a83d-8056129f4c2f.jpg`, `3e0bfc48-…jpg`, `8c4ce5d9-…gif` and `de9c8f78-…gif`. With images blocked, or for screen readers, these are blank or announced by filename.
  - The six `f3c0d22992a01946.png` images are probably product or recommendation tiles, which also need alt text.
  - The seven tracking pixels (5× `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv…`) should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Unusual mobile breakpoint.** All responsive rules sit at `@media (max-width: 460px)`. Viewports between 461 and 640px get the desktop layout.
- **Conflicting image caps.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. The later rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template residue and invalid markup:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in production.
  - The `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` is unclosed in an XHTML document.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - The MSO block contains an empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` rule.
- **Global resets are overly broad.** `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` can cause inconsistent text spacing in clients that honor them.
- **Good practice present:** `lang="en"`, `color-scheme: light`, and the Outlook `PixelsPerInch` fix.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. I can't check the truncated remainder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). Same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders of this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From address `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is on a subdomain of the organizational domain, which is compatible with relaxed alignment but unverified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address** are not visible in the truncated source and could not be confirmed.
- **Third-party hashed-ID pixels** (see §2) should be reflected in the privacy disclosure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 75 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and no resolved destinations or UTM parameters are visible. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the `dv.` pixel suggest campaign and message IDs are tracked through the vendor redirect rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 18 content images and the six `f3c0d22992a01946.png` tiles, and `alt=""` to the seven tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` or original headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't treat the relay-capture warning as a pass or a fail.
4. Resolve the tracking redirects (follow the 75 links with a redirect-following probe) to verify the destinations, the HTTP status, and whether UTMs persist on the landing page.
5. Confirm whether the six identical `rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` URLs are intentional.
6. Clean up the template. Remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment, escape `&` in `<title>`, de-duplicate the `format-detection` metas, and fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries.
7. Document the LiveRamp `rlcdn` pixels and the hashed identifier in the privacy policy.
