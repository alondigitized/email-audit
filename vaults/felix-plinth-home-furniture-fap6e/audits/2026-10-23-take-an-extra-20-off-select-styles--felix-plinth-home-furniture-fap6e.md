# ❤️ Take an EXTRA 20% OFF select styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Pottery Barn "extra 20%" send I've seen since 10/22, so my first reaction was a tired "yes, I know." The maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is the same one that opened "STARTS NOW 📣" and "ON NOW!" yesterday. The ❤️ in the subject line is the only thing that's new, and it has nothing to do with the email. The body is a plaid and Christmas showcase.
- The plaid hero is gorgeous and the Christmas photography is on brand. But nothing tells me which "select styles" the 20% covers, so the offer and the pictures never connect. Crate & Barrel's "TODAY ONLY: 20% off lighting!" told me exactly what was discounted in the subject line. Here I'd have to click "Shop All Sale" and hunt.
- Would I click? Probably not from the top. The "Recommended Just For You" block is the only part that speaks to me, with a dining table, a pendant, a swivel chair and a rug, which is basically my punch list. I'd save it to the Pinterest folder, not click. I wouldn't forward it, because nobody needs a fourth sale email this week.

## What's working

- The maroon sale strip with a "Shop All Sale" button is the first thing my eye lands on. The 20% and 70% are easy to read on a phone.
- The Plaid Shop hero is a strong visual and fits the season. It's the kind of image I'd pin.
- "Recommended Just For You" has furniture, lighting and a rug in a clean two-by-three grid. It's the only part that feels like it knows I'm renovating.
- The "Design Crew / Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" block is a real offer for someone doing two houses. Article's "free interior design" email hit me the same way last week.
- The "More for the Holiday Home" row (Bedding, Decor, Furniture, Rugs) gives me a quick way into my category.

## What's weak

- The offer and the content don't match. The 20% is for "select styles," but the plaid and Christmas sections never say which products qualify. There's no price, no strikethrough and no percent badge anywhere in the body.
- The subject line and the repeated sale strip make this the same promo for the fourth time in about 36 hours. That's the exact "same thing again" fatigue I get when the same lamp shows up in three brands' emails.
- The "Christmas Shop" appears as a button and then again as a big red box later. That's redundant, and it adds length without adding a reason to click.
- The recommended products have no prices. Wayfair at least puts the "$134 off" badge and a price on its hero. I can't tell whether that dining table is in the sale.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the rewards, app and Affirm blocks pile up at the bottom. The 10% back rewards card is the math I'd actually care about, but it's buried under the app download.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the discount and show prices. Put "20% off" badges and sale prices on the recommended products, and say which categories are included. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Don't send the same maroon strip four times. Rotate the lead with the category that's actually discounted (lighting, rugs, dining), so each send has its own reason to open.
- 3. Cut the second Christmas Shop banner and move the Design Crew block higher. It's the only thing that's genuinely different from the other sends.
- 4. Bring "Earn 10% back in rewards" up near the offer so I can do my card math without scrolling to the fine print.
- 5. Add a real end date. "Extra 20% off" with no deadline reads as permanent.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The plaid shop is here (and 20% off applies)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off. See which styles made the cut.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tables, pendants, rugs and swivel chairs, all in the sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard table-based XHTML. It has a stray server-side code fragment in a `<style>` block, many images without `alt`, and three plain-HTTP image requests. Two of those requests expose the recipient's address. The header-level checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be evaluated, because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 57 links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo. Destinations, redirect health and UTM parameters are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Recipient email in a cleartext URL:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an open-tracking pixel that sends the recipient address over unencrypted HTTP.
- **Recipient email in an `<img>` URL:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an image, and the address is in the query string.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-tech pixel, and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/eea06183-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a tracking pixel. Both are expected for this kind of email but add third-party requests.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from a different brand's domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images:** About 50 images are flagged. These include the plaid story slices (`1023_FRI_AM_PlaidStory/slices_01–15.jpg`), the hero banner (`H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`) and the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, etc.). The footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) are also flagged. If these carry text, such as the offer or footer links, they are unreadable with images off or in a screen reader. The `alt` flags on tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif` are benign.
- **Plain-HTTP images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Stray code in CSS:** The last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...`. This looks like leaked server-side code, and clients just discard it as invalid CSS.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Layout tables:** The spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"`, unlike the other tables. I can't confirm the others in the truncated source.
- **Font link:** The Google Fonts `<link>` is ignored by many clients, so the layout depends on fallbacks. This is minor.

3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP fragment from the `<style>` block.
4. Set `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
