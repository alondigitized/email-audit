# CHANDELIERS ✨ Up to 70% OFF for MEMORIAL DAY ✨ 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Nine. Pretty sure this is nine Wayfair emails since roughly Sunday, and I know because I checked after the kitchen mats one — which itself followed the low-pile rugs send, which followed the clearance blast, which followed the bedding sets omnibus. And now it's chandeliers. Which would actually be a relevant subject for me right now: I'm mid-spec for the dining room in the second house, I have a shortlist of three fixtures bookmarked, and "up to 70% off" on lighting is exactly the kind of line that breaks through fatigue.
- Except I open it and there are no chandeliers. The hero is outdoor furniture and some kind of wire rack. The category callouts are curtains, dining furniture buy-one-get-one, storage and organization. There's a "doorbuster deals" section showing patio seating and what appears to be gym equipment. I scrolled through the whole thing looking for the lighting feature that was promised in the subject and it simply isn't there in any prominent way. That's not a miss — that's a bait-and-switch. Article's "deals are in full swing" email scored a 9 from me last week partly because the subject and the hero were the same conversation. This email is two different emails stapled together.
- Mark as read. Maybe tap through if I'm desperate and have thirty seconds. I would not forward this.

## What's working

- **The subject line actually has teeth.** "CHANDELIERS ✨ Up to 70% OFF for MEMORIAL DAY" — that's a specific product category with a specific discount percentage tied to a real date. That's more than the bedding sets omnibus managed.
- **"Free & easy delivery over $35" is a solid persistent trust bar.** Wayfair has always been good at making shipping terms visible without burying them, and that stripe in the middle earns its keep.
- **Memorial Day framing is at least seasonally coherent.** Pottery Barn's "The Memorial Day Sale is calling ☎️" from yesterday felt phoned in (literally); this at least commits to the event.
- **The category discount callouts are scannable.** "curtains & drapes | up to 50% OFF" — I read those fast and that format works on mobile.

## What's weak

- **The subject and the body are different emails.** CHANDELIERS is in the subject; chandeliers are not in the hero, not in a category callout, not in the doorbuster section. This is the single largest problem in the whole send.
- **"Picked just for you" is doing no work.** Outdoor furniture, hardware, a tent, a weight rack — that's not a persona, that's a clearance warehouse. CB2's "Find your favorite new thing" (which I scored a 9) got away with a vague subject because the body had editorial curation. This has neither.
- **Nine emails in five days has crossed from cadence to noise.** I said this after the kitchen mats one, and I'll say it again: the marginal send is now costing more in trust than it's earning in clicks.
- **No chandelier product shown, no price point anchored.** If you're going to promise 70% off a specific category, show me a $400 chandelier marked down to $119. That's what would make me tap.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a chandelier in the hero.** Full stop. If the subject says "CHANDELIERS," the first thing I see below the fold should be a chandelier with a crossed-out price. This is a production/QA issue before it's a marketing issue.
- 2. **Anchor a specific price.** "Up to 70% off" is legally safe but psychologically lazy. Show one hero fixture: "Was $349 → $104." That's what converts someone who is actually shopping for lighting.
- 3. **Cut the email in half.** Pick one story — lighting, or outdoor, or storage — and tell it. The bedding sets omnibus from two days ago tried to do everything and scored a 7 from me. This one is trying to do the same and delivering less.
- 4. **Drop the send frequency or add a preference center CTA.** One "shop by room" preference nudge somewhere in this email would let me self-select out of the gym equipment sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Chandeliers 70% off — Memorial Day only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The dining room chandelier you bookmarked is on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off pendant lights, flush mounts & more through Monday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping over $35 — lighting deals end Memorial Day`
