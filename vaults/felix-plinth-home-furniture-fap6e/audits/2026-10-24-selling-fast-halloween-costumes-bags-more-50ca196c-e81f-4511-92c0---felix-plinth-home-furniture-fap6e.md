# 🎃 Selling fast: Halloween costumes, bags & more →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel send I've counted in two days, and I almost snoozed it. The image rendered very small, so I'm judging layout, headlines and structure, not fine print. The subject says "selling fast" and "costumes, bags & more," but what I actually get is a very long Halloween catalog. It runs from trick-or-treat baskets to bedding, costumes and festive decor, then adds a "cozy winter edit" and an app promo.
- The Halloween imagery is charming, with the black-cat basket, the pumpkin pillows and the wizard-costume kid. It's also a 12,000-pixel scroll for a holiday that's about a week away. I'm renovating two houses, not buying baskets. The only thing near my lane is the festive plates and decor, and that's a stretch. Would I click? No. Forward it? No. I'd mark it read.
- Compare it to Wayfair's Way Day send, which opened with "up to 80% off" and dates. Here the offer is a thin "free shipping on toys $99+" strip, and it appears twice. CB2's "Final Days: The Semi-Annual Dining Event" at least named a category I'm shopping for. Pottery Barn's "Extra 20% off" block is repetitive, but it's a number. This email has a Halloween theme and a shipping threshold, and that's all it has.

## What's working

- The black-cat treat basket and pumpkin-face pillow photography is playful, and the images read well even at a small size.
- Each section has a clear lowercase headline, such as "boo-tiful bedding & more" and "scary-cute halloween costumes," with its own "Shop All" button, so the structure is easy to scan.
- Small badges like "Selling Fast" and "Ships Free" sit on individual products. They give some urgency and shipping clarity at the product level.
- The "up to 50% off clearance" block is the one real discount in the email, and it's easy to spot.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. By the time I reach "we love these for you" I've scrolled past seven or eight themed sections.
- The hero says "selling fast" but gives no stock count, deadline or price. The urgency isn't believable when it's the same line they ran in "Heads up: These holiday bestsellers always sell out →" earlier today.
- There are two competing offers, free shipping on toys $99+ and up to 50% off clearance, and the headlines don't say which one matters.
- The bottom half drifts into a cozy winter edit, a credit card promo and an app download pitch. It reads like three emails stacked together, which echoes the stacking I saw in the wine glasses send on 10/23.
- Nothing here is for me. There are no furniture, lighting or swatch cues, and no rewards math beyond a card pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one hero and two or three product rows. Pick either costumes or treat baskets, not all five categories.
- 2. Put the price or the deadline in the hero. "Selling fast" with nothing behind it doesn't convince anyone.
- 3. Move the app and card promos into a footer strip, and drop the winter edit from a Halloween send.
- 4. Suppress this send for people who already got three Kids emails in two days.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Movable Ink-driven template with 97 tracked links and an `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` image host. QA shows a 57% pass rate with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The confirmed issues are widespread missing alt text, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and several third-party and per-recipient tracking pixels. The compliance headers could not be checked.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Link destinations unverified.** 97 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Five third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp (rlcdn) pixels. Five sequential sync calls add load and expose the recipient to a third party.
- **Possible shared recipient identifier.** The `s=` value in the rlcdn URLs begins with the same hex string as `mi_u=` in the Movable Ink URLs (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…`). It is a 64-character hex token, the length of a SHA-256 hash. I can't tell from the source what it encodes, so check whether it is a hashed email address.
- **Per-recipient first-party pixels.** These include `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c0b988b6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are expected for Movable Ink but are recipient-identifying.
- **Six identical dynamic images.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times. It is likely one render fetched repeatedly.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Alt text is missing on about 60 images.** Examples:
  - **Content images.** About 35 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, for example `6cda691d-b295-47d6-aa7c-8c52d1eec48a.jpg`. If these carry the product or promo message, image-blocking clients and screen readers get nothing.
  - **Spacers.** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` (4 times) and `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` (2 times) should use `alt=""`.
  - **Tracking pixels.** The rlcdn, `mi.` and `dv.` pixels should also use `alt=""`.
  - **Missing vs. empty.** The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty. If some are already `alt=""`, those are false positives.
  - **Animated images.** `b8e4ab5a-….gif`, `e7fbcb9b-….gif` and `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` are animated or video-poster images, and the text they carry is invisible to assistive tech.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Duplicate meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared four times, with one combined tag plus separate `date`, `address` and `telephone` tags. This is harmless but unclean.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a typo'd placeholder and adds bytes.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under the same doctype.
- **Dead CSS rule.** `img {max-width: 600px}` at `min-width:768px` is overridden by the later `img {max-width: 640px}` at `min-width:640px`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unverified, not a confirmed defect. Check the raw message in the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.

The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain and matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. That is good for alignment, but I can't confirm authentication from this data.
