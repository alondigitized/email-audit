# Our fan-fave kids’ chair = the perfect present
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the third or fourth Crate & Barrel family send I've had in two days, and this one is from the Kids sender. I'm renovating two houses and neither has a kid's room, so it's mostly noise to me. The top is a loud "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off" banner with a ticking countdown. Under it is a monogrammed armchair, then NFL bean-bag chairs, baby activity mats and cribs. The two houses don't need any of that.
- It's well built. The offer and the clock sit above the fold, and the chair photography is pretty. Pottery Barn's barstool email last week got my attention because it matched something I'm pricing. This one sells me nothing I'm pricing. Back on 10/20 the Le Creuset and salad-spinner emails at least had a kitchen angle for me.
- I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't click or forward it. I'd only forward it to a friend with a toddler, and the sender doesn't know I'm not that person.

## What's working

- The countdown timer and "up to 50% off" banner are impossible to miss. The deadline feels real, which is more than Wayfair's vague "MAJOR markdowns" managed.
- The hero chair photo is warm and styled, with a tartan backdrop and a monogram. It's good-looking content.
- "Add their name or initials with custom embroidery" is a specific, personal hook. It tells me what the product does.
- "Shop All Soft Seating" and "Shop Clearance" are clear, readable buttons.
- The free-shipping-over-$99 strip is a clear shipping rule. I wish furniture emails were this plain.

## What's weak

- None of it is for me. Nursery, cribs, kids' chairs and NFL bean bags assume a household I don't have, and the sender isn't segmenting me.
- The email is very long. It has about six offer blocks: the event, the chair, clearance, shipping, Christmas, and the credit card. Nothing tells me which one matters.
- I'd get a Pottery Barn Kids or Crate Kids feed with this much overlap. The same product appears again in the "we love these for you" grid. It's the same-lamp-in-three-emails problem, inside a single email.
- The Crate Kids card promo and the app download pitch sit at the bottom as extra asks with no rewards math. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I'd want to see the numbers.
- Section subheads like "create their perfect chair" and "we love these for you" are small and low-contrast on a phone. The product tiles at the bottom have tiny text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment it. Send this to people who bought kids' or nursery items and keep the rest of us out. Kids-sender sends to non-parents are the biggest lift available here.
- 2. Cut it to one offer and one hero. Pick either the chair or the event, and drop the stacked clearance, Christmas, shipping and card blocks.
- 3. Put the actual price on the hero chair. "Our #1 holiday gift" with no number makes me scroll.
- 4. Move the Crate Kids card to its own send, or add the real rewards math (X% back) if it stays.
- 5. Enlarge the grid text and subheads for mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off kids' bedroom & nursery — ends in 1 day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The monogrammed kids' chair, from $XX`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free embroidery on our #1 gift chair. Sale ends Thursday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft seating, nursery and bedroom, all up to 50% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). Most findings are missing `alt` attributes (about 35 images) and unverifiable headers and links. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 78 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. I could not verify their destinations, status codes or redirect chains.
- **Third-party pixel repetition:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are five separate LiveRamp pixel calls in one message. The `n` values are sequential, so these look like repeated module-level fires.
- **Other per-recipient pixels:**
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with identical URLs.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` is the open pixel.
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c34fbb05-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another tracking image.
  - Identical duplicate requests add load and can inflate open and impression metrics.
- **Template leftover:** the HTML contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, an unremoved template placeholder with a typo.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 35 images were flagged. Content images include:
  - `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `b369fa96-…jpg`, `bf533f54-…jpg`, `e7f469bf-…jpg`, `b236db57-…jpg`
  - `2026_1020_CBK_ExclusiveSoftSeating_ezgif2…` (animated, on scene7)
  - `2026_0926_CBK_Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif`
  - `CK_H26_KidsHolidayMagic_24x13_Edit3_Tert`
  - These need descriptive alt text, because with images blocked or in a screen reader the content is lost.
- **Decorative spacers and tracking pixels:** these should carry an explicit `alt=""`. Affected images:
  - `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White` (2×)
  - `042025_CBK_KidsBeds1_Rule_Spacer_40px` (2×)
  - the `rlcdn`, `mi.` and `dv.` pixels
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` restricts pinch-zoom in some clients, which is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`; it should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed and duplicates the `http-equiv` charset declaration.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
  - These are low-impact but redundant.
- **Title:** `<title>` is the generic "Crate & Barrel" and does not match the subject line "Our fan-fave kids' chair = the perfect present".
- **Image delivery:** assets come from two hosts, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `s7d5.scene7.com`. Two animated GIFs are included (`024feb9f-…gif`, `2417c77e-…gif`, plus the scene7 GIFs). Weight and first-frame fallback for Outlook were not verified from the data.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u=`, `mi_mid=`) are populated in the pixel URLs. The truncated portion was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** the QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed rather than a verified absence. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN]:** RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and alignment with the visible From domain could not be tested.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 78 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated source does not show final destinations or UTM parameters. The click-redirect wrapper hides them. No landing-page alignment or parameter-persistence conclusions can be drawn.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (hero, product and animated modules), and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title ampersand (`&amp;`), make it descriptive, and remove the duplicate `format-detection` and charset tags and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
4. De-duplicate the repeated `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/…` and `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels, or confirm each is intentional.
5. Re-run the QA against raw headers, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect-wrapped links (follow the redirects) to check final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page status codes.
7. Capture the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address can be audited.
