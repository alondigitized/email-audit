## Technical Audit: Wayfair, "Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS"

**1. Technical Summary**
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 480px media queries, and its tracking is well formed. The QA run could not verify any link as a 200 or confirm the compliance headers. Eight of 12 probed links returned 429, 4 were never probed, and the unsubscribe headers and Authentication-Results are missing from the relay capture. The HTML source was also truncated, so only what the QA data exposes is assessed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **No link verified:** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429 and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget, so no link was confirmed live.
  - The 429s are most likely Wayfair rate-limiting the QA prober, not broken links. This includes `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` and the `.../our-favorite-8%27-x-10%27-outdoor-rugs-se78336.html?eid=row_0_Grid-100...` "Shop Now" link.
  - A re-probe with throttling is needed to confirm.
- **Truncated URLs, unconfirmed:** Four unprobed URLs end mid-token, for example `.../best-selling-garden-statues-se73905.`, `...must-have-pet-doors-se74775.html?eid` and `...kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.htm`. This is probably the QA tool's display truncation, so check the raw `href`s.
- **Campaign label conflicts with the send type:** The app link has `c=Triggered`, but every link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labelled "Triggered" will skew attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback:** The app link has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. It depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **20 insecure tracking pixels:** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`) are all plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed content or insecure images will drop these impression beacons and undercount them. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS and fine.
- **Per-recipient IDs in URLs:** `_emr=7c92fe8d-...` appears on every link and as `p=` on the pixels. It is standard but worth noting for privacy and link-forwarding.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Content images missing alt text:**
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-EndsTomorrow.jpg`
  - `Our+Favorite+8%27+x+10%27+Outdoor+Rugs.jpg`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png` and `..._top_3_purple.png`
  - `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24...`
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`
- **Linked images have no accessible name:** The skinny banner, rugs hero and header image are wrapped in `<a>`. The QA log shows their link text as `[image: filename]`, so screen readers get a filename or nothing.
- **Disclaimer text may be an image:** `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg` has no alt, so any legal copy in it is inaccessible and is lost when images are blocked.
- **Tracking pixels:** The 20 `imp` pixels and `b.php` are flagged for missing alt. Use `alt=""` and `width`/`height` attributes on them.
- **Gmail and mobile handling:** `u ~ div { min-width:100vw }` and `u + .Gmail` are present, so Gmail and mobile handling exists. Nothing else can be verified from the truncated source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible data shows no unresolved tokens. The `_eml` and `_emr` values are populated UUIDs.

**5. Compliance**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA tool itself says AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, so check the raw message headers.
- **In-body unsubscribe and postal address:** The probe always includes an unsubscribe link, and none appears among the failures. The footer was cut off, so the unsubscribe URL and the physical address could not be checked.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- **Tracking scheme:** There are no `utm_*` parameters. Attribution uses Wayfair's own `source`, `batchid`, `csnid`, `refid`, `sltid` and `eid`. This is consistent across links but invisible to standard UTM-based analytics.
- **Landing alignment:** The subject and hero link to `.../our-favorite-8%27-x-10%27-outdoor-rugs-se78336.html`, which matches the subject. "Shop Sale", the skinny banner and the header logo go to generic `/daily-sales` or `/`.
- **Landing page not verified:** The 429s mean the rugs landing page content could not be checked.

2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""` to all pixels.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the hero, banner and disclaimer images. Give each linked image meaningful alt text as its link name.
4. Fix `c=Triggered` to match the batch send, and change `af_web_dp` to HTTPS.
5. Inspect the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be dropping them. If the relay is the cause, treat the compliance checks as unverified, not failed.
