# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Free shipping over $35, all day, every day. Cool, Wayfair. That's been your standard policy for as long as I can remember — you've never charged me shipping on anything that clears $35, and I've never once thought "wow, thank you for that." The subject line is the email equivalent of a hotel card that says "we have Wi-Fi." It's table stakes dressed up as a headline, and it's the reason I almost didn't open this.
- What actually made me glad I did: chandeliers. I've been circling the lighting decision for the dining area at the second home for two months. "Let's Talk: Chandeliers" as the hero is genuinely on my list — I just pinned a half-dozen options last week. The 72-HR clearout banner at the top is a real offer (60% off), and the dark-studio chandelier photo earns its placement. This could have been a genuinely useful email.
- But Wayfair keeps doing this thing where the subject and the body belong in different emails. Yesterday's clearout send — the purple wall of percentage tiles — at least made the urgency legible from the subject line. This one buries the 72-hour deadline inside the body and leads with a shipping policy I don't find interesting. I'd mark it read, maybe bookmark the chandelier link, and definitely not forward it. Pottery Barn's Chris Loves Julia send from earlier this week had three times the editorial pull with a cleaner subject.

## What's working

- **Chandelier hero is genuinely on my list** — a dark, moodily lit pendant over what reads as a dining context is exactly the visual I need to start a tab.
- **72-HR clearout is a real offer** — the orange banner reads clearly and "UP TO 60% OFF" is credible given what I've seen move in their clearance section.
- **Affirm financing callout** — for a light fixture that can easily run $300-800, 0% APR framing is useful. CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" email last week didn't offer this at all.
- **Brand family footer** — Birch Lane and Perigold listed means I know where to go if the Wayfair price point isn't right.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, images loaded, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is an evergreen policy, not an offer** — "Free shipping over $35. All day, every day." is true every single day. It doesn't earn the open on its own.
- **Three competing hooks in the first scroll** — clearout banner, Affirm financing strip, chandelier hero. My eye couldn't settle. Article's "Home tour: A warm welcome" editorial send picked one lane and stuck to it; this doesn't.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is a category link, not a product** — if I'm clicking on chandeliers, I want to land on a sorted-by-discount chandelier page, not wherever the generic handler drops me. This is the same friction I hit on the sofa tables email from yesterday.
- **Katherine from NC testimonial is essentially meaningless** — "It was easy to install and looks fantastic" could be the review for literally any light fixture in any catalog. Crate & Barrel's floor sample sale email had a much stronger specificity pull.
- **Fourth email in two days** — at this cadence, I'm already developing a reflex to skip without reading.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the actual urgency** — the clearout with a deadline is news; shipping policy is not. Reframe around what's time-sensitive.
- 2. **Kill the Affirm strip as a standalone section** — surface it inside the chandelier card as "or finance at 0% APR" so it adds value without splitting attention.
- 3. **Link the chandelier hero to a curated, clearance-price-sorted page** — not a general category dump. Give me "$149–$499, rated 4.5+, ships in 3 days."
- 4. **Replace the Katherine testimonial with something specific** — model number, room size, mention of the shade material. Generic five-word reviews do not move me.
- 5. **Drop the app QR block from the main email** — save it for a dedicated re-engagement send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Chandeliers on clearance — 72 hrs, up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The lighting you've been putting off: 60% off ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Over 500 pendants, sconces, and chandeliers — clearance pricing today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dining room, entryway, bedroom — find it before the 72-hour window closes.`
