# PERGOLAS 🟣 🟣 WAY DAY STARTS OCT. 24 🟣 🟣 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Wayfair email in days where the images actually load, and it's a wall of pergolas. Twelve of them, stacked in a grid, with a purple "80% OFF WAY DAY OCT. 24–26" banner on top. The subject promised pergolas and the body delivers them, so that's a point in its favor. I'm renovating two houses, so an outdoor structure isn't crazy for me. But it's late October and I'm not shopping for patio gear right now.
- The sale is also three days away. "SHOP EARLY" is the only button in the banner, and nothing tells me what I'd get by shopping early. The prices are visible under each tile ("Was $1,999.00 NOW: $1,844.54"), but the type is tiny and I'd have to squint on my phone. Then it runs on for ages. After the pergolas there's a second pergola block, a "but wait, there's more…" section, and a gift card and a credit card offer. It's the ninth Wayfair email I've logged this week, and the fatigue is real.
- Would I click? Maybe once, on a pergola, out of curiosity. Forward it? No. I'd save it to my Pinterest folder at best.

## What's working

- The hero banner is clear: "80% OFF, WAY DAY, OCT. 24–26" in purple and white. I know the event and the dates right away.
- The subject and the body match. After the empty-hero email on 10/21 ("If christmas trees could win awards…") and the glassware send on 10/19, seeing the images load is a relief.
- Every pergola tile shows a was/now price and some have a "Sale" badge, so I can compare without clicking.
- The "$40 off your first order of $100+" credit card strip is easy to spot. I'm a rewards-card guy, so I noticed it, even though it's tied to opening a new Wayfair card.
- The "but wait, there's more…" grid has a few categories that fit my renovation, like bar stools, filing cabinets and area rugs.

## What's weak

- Twelve near-identical pergolas with no names, sizes or materials. I can't tell an aluminum louvered pergola from a wood one. For a big purchase, dimensions and shipping info matter, and none of it is here.
- The "Way Day deals of the day" yellow banner sits on top of tiles that look the same as the ones above it. I can't tell what the deal is, and the section feels repeated.
- The email promotes Way Day starting Oct. 24, but all the prices are already marked as "NOW." Is the 80% off real today or on Saturday? The messaging is muddled.
- The length is exhausting. Gift cards, the credit card, and an app QR code all come after a 20-tile scroll. No clear single CTA.
- The tile captions are in tiny type with no buttons, so the tap targets are just the images. On a phone that's fussy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the pergola grid to 4–6 tiles and put real details on each: size, material, free-shipping or delivery window. A big-ticket item needs more than a price.
- 2. Make the CTA concrete. "SHOP EARLY" should say what early gets me, such as "Preview Way Day pergola deals" or "Set a reminder for Oct. 24."
- 3. Remove the repeated "deals of the day" block, or label what's actually different about it.
- 4. Move the credit card offer lower or drop it. It's the most promotional thing in the email and it competes with the product.
- 5. Compare with CB2's "Up to 30% off things for hosting" (8/10). CB2 gave me one headline and one table. Pottery Barn's "Our top 100 gifts" at least curated. Article's free design services email gave me one clear action. Wayfair gave me forty.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch send (`source=batch`, `refid=MKTEML_142103`) with consistent first-party tracking, but link validity could not be confirmed (every probed link returned 429). The source also has 5+ content images with no alt text and 20 insecure `http://` tracking pixels. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be assessed because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified, not broken.** All 10 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting), including:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-for-less--se82165.html`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/curated/24-hour-deals~ev641886.html`
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`
  
  That means 0 links were confirmed 200. 60 links were skipped by design and 2 were unprobed after the 70s budget (`our-favorite-bar-stools-se73977.html`, `large-capacity-shoe-racks-se73165.ht…`). A re-probe with throttling is needed.
- **Insecure link:** the Wayfair Card image link uses `http://wayfair.com/wayfaircard?...`. It relies on a redirect to HTTPS. The OneLink web fallback is also `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the OneLink URL sets `c=Triggered`, but the send is `source=batch&batchid=3`. The app-install attribution will be bucketed as a triggered campaign.
- **Shared `_eml` token:** the "Pergolas for less" and "On Sale Now: Makeup Vanities" links both carry `_eml=2f17152a-c9e4-42ff-97f2-0a6be7dde0aa`. This is minor, but per-link click attribution may be coarser than the other links, which each have a unique `_eml`.
- **Duplicate destinations:** `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` is the target of at least 3 separate links ("Shop Sale", the GIF, and the header image), each with its own `_eml`. This is fine for tracking, but it is redundant.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images with no alt text.** The QA tool shows the filename as the link text, which is what screen readers and image-blocked clients will present:
  - `US-WD26-Preview-Email.gif` (links to `/daily-sales`)
  - `WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-DotD.jp…`
  - `WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-Affirm-…`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png` (links to `/daily-sales`)
  - `WFCA_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skinny-GiftCa…`
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (open pixel) and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019` impression pixels. These are decorative and low impact, but an empty `alt=""` is the correct form.
- **20 impression pixels over plain `http://`** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=…&sz=2x1`). Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS content will drop them, so impression counts will be under-reported. Some clients may also show mixed-content warnings.
- **Locale asset mismatch (plausible):** `WFCA_2026_1024_…Skinny-GiftCa…` is a Canadian (`WFCA`) asset in an email whose other assets are `WFUS`. The truncated source can't confirm whether it renders for US recipients.
- The CSS includes mobile media queries at 480px and 320px, Gmail and Outlook-app fixes, and an `mso-font-alt` fallback for the Sofia web font. No rendering defects are visible in the truncated source.
