## What's weak

- The sender is "Kids" and the hero is "love now & forever" over a baby foot. I'm 36 with no nursery, so this reads as the wrong audience.
- The email is very long. Cribs, beds, dressers, bookshelves, rugs, a promo grid, Olio, strollers, a food brand, a recommendations row, an app promo and TheDesignDesk all stack up. Nothing leads.
- The hero is a mood shot with no price. The offer sits in a small strip above it.
- The category blocks have no price anchors, so I can't tell if the bestsellers are $400 or $1,400.
- Cadence is bad. This is at least the tenth Crate family send today (11/12 already had eight and nine of them).

- 2. Cut the email down to the three best-selling categories, each with a "from $" price.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with VML/MSO fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing image alt text and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path, not from evidence about the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 85 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1..5` beacons (LiveRamp) carry a long hashed identifier in the `s=` parameter. 6 `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` pixels and one `o.gif` open pixel are also present. These are standard ESP tracking, but they add 12+ extra requests and a third-party data share.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML source was truncated, so link-level `href` problems such as empty or `#` hrefs could not be checked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 45 images are flagged, which is the largest confirmed issue.
  - **Content images:** About 30 are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif` or `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. This also includes the animated `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F0408%5FCBK%5FMadeforMemories%5FMain1.gif`. If images are blocked, the recipient sees nothing, and screen readers get only a filename or nothing.
  - **Tracking pixels:** The `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels were also flagged. These should carry `alt=""` and are lower priority.
  - **Limitation:** The QA check can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from an intentional `alt=""`, so some content images may only need verification.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 DOCTYPE. It should be `&amp;`.
  - The `format-detection` meta tag appears three times with overlapping or conflicting values.
  - `charset` is declared twice (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).
- **Template leftover:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the production HTML.
- **Global CSS:** `*{… line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` apply globally. This risks unintended overrides in nested content.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, the MSO and VML namespaces are declared, and Outlook fallbacks and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are resolved per-recipient identifiers. The source was truncated, so the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Header warnings:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool itself says these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. **They are not confirmed defects.** SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are unknown and need verification against raw headers.
- **Body content:** The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link were in the truncated portion and could not be verified.
- **Sender:** The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is consistent with the image and tracking subdomains, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this could not be fully assessed. The 85 links were not probed, and the source shows only `mi_u` and `mi_cid` on the pixels. Whether click URLs carry UTM parameters and whether the landing pages match could not be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, using `alt=""` for decorative images and tracking pixels. Add `width="1" height="1"` and `alt=""` to the pixels.
2. **Remove zoom block:** Delete `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Clean the head:**
   - Escape the title ampersand.
   - Remove the duplicate `format-detection` and `charset` tags.
   - Remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. **Verify authentication and unsubscribe headers:** Check raw headers at the receiving MTA to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. The relay-capture warnings alone don't prove a problem.
5. **Resolve skipped links:** Resolve a sample of the 85 tracking links to confirm the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing page status.
6. **Re-run on full source:** Run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, address and any remaining tokens.
