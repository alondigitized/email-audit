## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink–driven (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) XHTML table layout with 74 redirect-wrapped links. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The header-based compliance warnings can't be confirmed from this capture, and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links unverified:** 74 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are untested.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0…` (7 images) and `o.gif?mi_u=…` carry a recipient token. This is expected for dynamic content, but the images are not cacheable or shareable.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1..5` requests (LiveRamp) carry the same recipient hash. They are a privacy and deliverability consideration, since the five pixels are separate requests. They are also unlisted in the visible source.
- **Truncated source:** only part of the link set could be inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** about 30 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` have no `alt`. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `56be7a63-ec91-4fed-b133-e9563a2fbb5f.jpg`, and animated `504f24e6-…gif` and `8b714568-…gif`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these will be read as filenames or skipped. If they carry product, offer, or CTA content, that content is lost.
- **Missing alt, dynamic and tracking images:** the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5d8508ee-…`, `o.gif`, and the `rlcdn` pixels also lack alt. The pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly. The `rp/*.png` images appear to be dynamic content and need real alt text. The QA check can't distinguish an absent `alt` from `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Malformed XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand (should be `&amp;`) under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Duplicate and leftover markup:** `<meta charset>` is duplicated, `format-detection` is declared three times, and there's a template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. None of these affect rendering.
- **Global overrides:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` apply to every element. `*{line-height:100%}` can make wrapped text tight in clients that ignore the more specific overrides. No client-specific rendering failure is confirmed.
