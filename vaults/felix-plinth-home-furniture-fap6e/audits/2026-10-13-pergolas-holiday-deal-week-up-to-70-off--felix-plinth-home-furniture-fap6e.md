# PERGOLAS 🎄 HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK 🎄 Up to 70% OFF
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourteenth Wayfair email I've logged this week, and it's the pergola email again. Twelve pergola tiles, six up top and six in the middle. That's the same wall I counted on 10/11 ("CLEAROUT ENDS TOMORROW"). I'm renovating two houses and have never put a pergola on a list. Today's earlier send, "This pergola is almost gone," had a blank hero with a broken-image icon. This one renders cleanly, so that's an improvement.
- The useful part starts below the pergolas. Rugs at 70% off, curtains and drapes at 50% off, bedding at 50% off and entryway furniture at 40% off are all things I'm shopping for. They sit under a purple block I have to scroll past first. Nothing shows a price. Every pergola tile has a "Sale" tag and five stars, but no number. I can't tell if a pergola costs $400 or $4,000.
- I'd skim it, tap "area rugs," and mark the rest as read. I wouldn't forward it. Article's "Fall is for full tables" gave me something I'd save to my Pinterest folder. This gave me a clearance rack.

## What's working

- The red "Holiday Deal Week / up to 70% OFF" banner at the top is readable at a glance. I know the offer before I scroll.
- Everything loads. After this morning's blank-hero send, a clean render counts as a win.
- The category tiles have big percentages and one label each ("area rugs," "bedding"). They're easy to tap on a phone.
- The Affirm 0% APR block is a real furniture-buying cue. If I'm financing a patio set, I want to know that.
- The Wayfair Rewards and Pay Over Time icons near the bottom are the only nod to how I buy, which is the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- Twelve near-identical pergola tiles with no price, no name and no discount amount. "Sale" doesn't tell me what I'd save.
- The email sells outdoor structures under a "Holiday" banner. Nothing in the pergola grid says holiday.
- The order is backwards for me. The pergola block is first, and the rugs, drapes and entryway furniture I want are buried below it.
- The "Holiday Decor: buy 2+, get 10% off" tile is tiny compared with the 40–70% tiles. It's also the only holiday-relevant offer in the email.
- The cadence is exhausting. 10/11, 10/12, 10/13 morning and now this, all with the same pergola creative. It reads like I'm being mailed a feed, not curated for me.
- There's no swatch, sample or showroom content, and no furniture shipping detail beyond "free delivery over $35*." That doesn't help me plan two renovations.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with my actual purchase signals, not twelve pergolas. Show two or three pergolas at most, then the rugs, drapes and entryway tiles. If I haven't clicked a pergola in thirteen sends, stop sending them.
- 2. Put prices on the product tiles: sale price, strikethrough and "% off." The "Sale" tag alone is wasted space.
- 3. Use fewer, bigger tiles. A hero product and a single CTA would work better than a grid of identical shots.
- 4. Make the "Holiday" framing real, or drop it. Show a Holiday Decor hero if that's the theme.
- 5. Add a dated deadline ("ends Sun 10/19") so the urgency feels credible.
- 6. Cap frequency or suppress recent repeats. Crate & Barrel's family sends have the same problem, but CB2's countdown timer at least gave me a reason to look.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch send whose links all carry consistent first-party tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_141655`). Its main code-level issues are about 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and a campaign-label mismatch on the app deep link. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 failures, 3 warnings). The header warnings are most likely relay artifacts, and the HTML source is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 material links were probed. Every probed warning was a 429 from wayfair.com or onelink.me, which is rate limiting and not a dead link.
- **4 PDP links were not probed.** The 70s time budget ran out before the links starting `/-Aluminum-Pergola-with-Adjustable-Louvered-Roof-Waterpro`, `/111S-Aluminum-Pergola…`, `/Aurora-10%E2%80%98-X-12-Louvered-Pergola…` and `/Aluminum-Outdoor-Pergola-with-Independent-Louvered-L7053…` are unverified. The Aurora URL contains `%E2%80%98` (a curly quote) in the path, which is worth a manual check.
- **Mixed HTTP and HTTPS in the app link.** The onelink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback is plain HTTP and depends on a redirect.
- **Campaign label contradicts the send type.** The same link has `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch promo is labelled as a triggered email, which will skew AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Two kinds of tracking pixel.**
  - `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is an open pixel with no `alt`.
  - `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` are 20 impression pixels, all over HTTP. They can be blocked or rewritten by clients, so impression counts will be unreliable. They also add 20 extra requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`: about 40 images.**
  - 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels: use `alt=""`.
  - 13 `review_stars/stars_9/10/11.png` images: these carry the rating, so a screen reader gets no rating. The `alt` should be something like "4.5 out of 5 stars".
  - 3 banner images: `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnytop-vp.jpg`, `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnybanner-affirm.jpg` and `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - The top banner is wrapped in a link whose accessible name is only `[image: wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnytop-vp.j]`. The star images are linked to PDPs in the same way.
- **Stale asset.** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-…` (`assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/971f7a84…`) is a Memorial Day asset in an October holiday send. `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` is a January 2025 asset. If these are visible, they are leftover module content. If they are hidden, they are dead weight.
- **Mobile CSS.** `.ProductRec-name` uses `word-break: break-all`, which can split product names mid-word on mobile. This is a minor readability risk.
- **Web font.** The `@font-face` for "Sofia" is a `.woff` with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, so fallback is handled.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `_eml` and `_emr` values are resolved.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer, physical address and unsubscribe link** are not in the truncated HTML. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set but reports no failure for it, so the visible-link requirement is not confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Tracking is entirely proprietary (`source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=14`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_141655`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16508`). That is fine for Wayfair's own analytics, but GA/UTM-based channel reporting would not see this as email without a mapping.
- **Parameter consistency.**
  - The shared context params are `skurectxid=439b0860…` and `event_rec_txid=c5eb0000…`.
  - Product links add `mplid` and `sku` (for example `sku=ETRS1419` and `sku=JLYD1804`), and the SKU matches the PDP slug.
  - The `_eml` value is shared by two PDP links (`b15fff35…`) but differs on other links, so it looks like a per-module ID and not a per-recipient one.
- **Landing alignment.** "Shop Sale" and the top banner go to the generic `/daily-sales` and `/daily-sales/`, while the subject is pergola-specific. The "holiday decor: buy 2+, get 10% OFF" link goes to a different event, `~e409446.html?eid=636468`. The destination URLs are valid, but only the PDP links match the subject's pergola theme.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""`. Consider collapsing them into fewer calls.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the star-rating images (rating value) and to the three banners.
3. Change `c=Triggered` to a batch or promo campaign value on the onelink URL, and make `af_web_dp` HTTPS.
4. Remove or replace the Memorial Day and January 2025 assets.
5. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't treat the missing headers as defects until you've seen them.
6. Re-probe the 4 unprobed PDP links, especially the Aurora URL with `%E2%80%98`. Increase the probe time budget or retry the 429s with backoff.
7. Consider adding `utm_*` or a mapping so web analytics can attribute these clicks to email.
