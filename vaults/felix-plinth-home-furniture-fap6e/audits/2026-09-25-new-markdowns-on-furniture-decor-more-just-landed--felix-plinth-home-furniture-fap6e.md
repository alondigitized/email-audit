# New markdowns on furniture, decor & more just landed 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, up to 60% off clearance — that number actually stopped my thumb mid-scroll, which is more than I can say for the last five Crate & Barrel emails sitting in my inbox this week. But then I get to the "we love these for you" grid and it's a dresser, two dining tables, and three sofas — fine, that's actually my lane for once since I'm furnishing two houses right now, not the baby gear disaster from the last two sends. The Keane dresser and the Basque extendable table are genuinely things I'd screenshot for my reno folder.
- That said, this is clearance-first, personalized-recommendations-second, and the two don't talk to each other — none of the "we love these for you" pieces show a sale price, so I have no idea if they're even part of the 60% off. CB2's "Upholstery Event" email last week did this way better: one sale, one hero product, one price with the discount baked in. This one makes me do math.
- Would I click? Probably tap into clearance just to see what's actually 60% off, since that's real money on furniture. Forward to a friend? No — nothing here is surprising enough, and I've already sent my wife the CB2 sectional pic this week, that's my one furniture-forward per week budget.

## What's working

- The 60% off clearance hero is a real number with real urgency ("while quantities last") — that's rarer than it should be from this brand.
- The clearance category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) is genuinely useful for someone renovating two houses — I can jump straight to what I need.
- "We love these for you" finally shows product that's demographically on-target: dressers, dining tables, sectional sofas — not kids' bunk beds or baby bouncers like the last two sends.
- The Reward Dollars / Crate Card mention is exactly the kind of rewards-math content I actually read — I run everything through my highest-rewards card anyway.

## What's weak

- The "we love these for you" section has zero sale pricing or badges — if this is a personalization block, I can't tell if any of it overlaps with the 60% clearance up top, so it reads like two unrelated emails stapled together.
- Six products, zero swatch/sample or color-palette info — I'm juggling finishes across two houses right now and picking a dresser off one photo with no fabric/wood options shown is a non-starter.
- The Halloween "so scary, it's chic" block is a hard tonal swerve off the clearance urgency — CB2 stayed on-message with one sale, one CTA; this one splits attention three ways (clearance, Halloween decor, "shop the app").
- The $500 app-exclusive sweepstakes teaser is vague bait — "your chance at $500" with no rules or odds shown reads like the buried-fine-print flag on the rubric.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a sale price or "up to X% off" badge directly on the "we love these for you" products — right now the hero offer and the recommendation grid don't connect at all, which is the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Cut the Halloween decor block or move it to its own send — it dilutes the clearance urgency and this isn't a seasonal-decor persona moment for me.
- 3. Add a swatch/material call-out on at least the dresser and dining table — "available in 3 finishes" would move me from browsing to clicking.
- 4. Give the $500 app teaser a real hook (entry deadline, odds, or "one winner per week") or drop it — vague sweepstakes copy is worse than no CTA at all.
