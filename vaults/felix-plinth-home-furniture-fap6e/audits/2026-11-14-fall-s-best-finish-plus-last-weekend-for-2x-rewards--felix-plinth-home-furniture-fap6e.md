# FALL’S BEST FINISH Plus, last weekend for 2X rewards
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. The subject says "Fall's Best Finish" and I had no idea what that meant until I saw the hero. It's "Why we're loving lacquer," and it's a lookbook. That's a pun, and I'm only half impressed. The big dark-lacquer hero is gorgeous and it's my aesthetic for the primary house. Then the Tairo dining table, Hudson nightstand, Kirnan credenza, Isobel nightstand and Scatola coffee table run down the page, each with a designer credit. That's the Pinterest-folder content I actually save.
- The offer hierarchy is a mess, though. The green strip up top has the "Early Black Friday Sale, up to 60%" lockup. Way down the page there's a "Now up to 70% off clearance" block, and the subject says nothing about either. That's the same 60%/70% muddle I flagged in the "UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS" send earlier today. I'd click through to the lacquer collection and I'd save it. I wouldn't forward it, because the "last double rewards" line is tiny and the email never makes me feel the deadline.

## What's working

- The lacquer hero is a real point of view. It's one material story, not a 20-tile grid like Wayfair's "Area Rugs. People. Are. Loving." send.
- Every product has the designer's name under it ("Designed by Ross Cassidy"). That's the showroom-catalog feel Crate & Barrel Kids and Pottery Barn have dropped in their sale blasts.
- "Shop Lacquer" is a clear verb-plus-noun button, and there's a second one after the product run, so I don't have to scroll back up.
- The "More for you" grid shows prices ($319, $1199, $299, $1799), so I can sanity-check budget without a click.
- The CB2 Visa rewards callout in the top strip is the kind of thing I plan card spend around.

## What's weak

- The 2X rewards deadline is in a small top strip. The actual rewards and deadline copy is hard to read on a phone without zooming.
- It gives me two sale numbers, 60% and 70%, in two places. Neither one is tied to the lacquer collection I'm being sold.
- The "Not sold out, yet" green sofa block and the 70% clearance block feel bolted on after the lacquer story. It's three emails stacked into one long scroll.
- The product names under each image are small, light-gray text and the "shop now" links are tiny tap targets.
- This is the fourth CB2 send in about 48 hours (11/12, 11/13 twice, now 11/14). I'm fatigued, and Pottery Barn's "Our favorite EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS ❤️" gave me the same reaction.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one sale number and put it in the first visible band with the rewards deadline. Make "Ends Sunday" explicit, not "last weekend."
- 2. Say in the hero that the sale applies to lacquer, or name the discount on those pieces. Right now I can't tell whether the Kirnan credenza is on sale.
- 3. Cut the clearance block, or move it into its own send. Article's travertine email works because it stays on one story.
- 4. Make the product names and "shop now" links bigger. Add swatch or finish-sample and delivery-timing notes, since I'm furnishing two houses and need lead times.
- 5. Space out the cadence. Skip a day so the sends don't blur together.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, and the source has no hard errors. The QA run scored 57% with 3 warnings. The main findings are 9 content images without alt text, 11 insecure `http://` third-party pixels, and header checks that could not be verified. The HTML source is truncated, so the body, footer and link markup are not covered.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **67 links were not probed.** They are all on tracking or click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels.** 11 `http://` requests to Dotomi (Epsilon/Conversant):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Mail clients that block or upgrade mixed content, or proxy images (Gmail, Apple MPP), may drop these.
- **Cookie-sync pixels in email.** The 10 `pubN.php` cookieredir pixels are ad-tech cookie syncing. Most clients strip or proxy cookies, so they add little. They add 11 third-party requests, privacy exposure and a deliverability or content-filter risk.
- **First-party pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=<64-hex>&mi_...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/cd92d813-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are open and view-tracking pixels. They are fine in themselves, but they have no alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** 9 images on `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`: `5c26e5b4-…`, `da74b07c-…`, `acd1efa1-…`, `3993c036-…`, `9ac17f46-…`, `101d5c48-…`, `855217de-…`, `583d81b4-…`, `2c12b294-…`. With images blocked, or for screen readers, these will read as filenames or nothing. If any are linked, the link has no accessible name.
- **Pixels missing alt.** The Dotomi, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels have no `alt`. Use `alt=""` plus explicit `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in `*{}` and `body` also stops text from resizing.
- **Template leftovers.**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a placeholder comment left in production.
  - `td,th,div,p,a { }` is an empty rule.
  - `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` is an empty conditional.
  - `src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/…")` has a double slash.
  - Some `@font-face` fonts load from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain from the sender.
- **Likely fine.** `lang="en"`, `<title>` and the VML/Office namespaces are present, and the MSO fixes are standard.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- The `mi_u=56aaa278…cfd8` parameter in the `o.gif` URL is a hashed recipient identifier. It was populated, so personalization rendered, but a persistent per-user hash in a pixel URL is a privacy point.
- The rest of the body was not available, so token coverage there is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes AgentMail may not relay this header, so it is not confirmed absent at the sender.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058).** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` and the image domain `image.mail.cb2.com` look aligned, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer not seen.** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM and tracking parameter consistency cannot be verified, because the 67 links sit behind redirectors and were not followed.
- Landing page alignment with the "FALL'S BEST FINISH" and "2X rewards" messaging is untested.
- The 2X rewards promo is time-limited ("last weekend"), so landing pages should be checked for promo carry-through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, and `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove the `http://` Dotomi pixels or move them to HTTPS. Drop the 10 `pubN.php` cookie-sync pixels unless they clearly earn their cost.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` so users can zoom and resize text.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` at the origin, and capture raw headers to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
5. Clean up the template: remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment, the empty CSS rule and the empty MSO conditional, and fix the `//Styles` double slash.
6. Re-run the QA with the full HTML and with redirect resolution, to cover the footer, unsubscribe link, UTMs and landing pages.
