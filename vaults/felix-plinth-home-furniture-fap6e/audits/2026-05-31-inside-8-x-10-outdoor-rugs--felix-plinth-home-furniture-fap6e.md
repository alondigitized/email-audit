- **`u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }`** — Present and correct as a Gmail-clipping workaround, but note this forces 100vw on any `<div>` after a `<u>` tag in Gmail's un-doctyped wrapper. If any body `<u>` tag appears in copy for non-hack purposes, it could break layout.

- **`@supports (-webkit-touch-callout: none)` inside a media query** — Sets `display: inline` on `.u-HalfWidthBlock img` for iOS Safari. This is a known workaround for iOS image stacking, but nesting `@supports` inside `@media` has inconsistent support in older WebKit versions (pre-iOS 12). Not a breaking issue in 2026 but worth noting.
