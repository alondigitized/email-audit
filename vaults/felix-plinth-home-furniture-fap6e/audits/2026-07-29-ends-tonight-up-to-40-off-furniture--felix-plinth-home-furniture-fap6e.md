# Ends tonight: up to 40% off furniture
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "ends tonight" — that's a real deadline for once, and it's landing right on top of "24 hours left" from yesterday, which I gave an 8. So CB2 is now two-for-two on actual urgency instead of the vague "limited time" nonsense brands love to hide behind. That mauve velvet swivel chair up top is genuinely nice — I'd stop scrolling for that.
- But then it just... keeps going. Furniture sale, then a whole separate bedroom + bath event at 30% off, then a sofa push, then a full rewards-card pitch with four different cashback tiers, then a "leave a review, win $1,000" sweepstakes, then six more products, then app download, then a gift registry callout. This is four emails stapled together. Compare that to Article's "Summer Sale starts now" — one offer, one hero, done in three scrolls. CB2 buries its own urgency under so much stuff that by the time I hit the rewards card section I've forgotten what "ends tonight" even referred to.
- I'd click the chair. I would not forward this — there's no single clean thing to forward, it's a catalog dump with a deadline slapped on the front.

## What's working

- The velvet chair hero is a real product shot with good styling (dark backdrop, single bloom prop) — this is the same photography quality that earned CB2 a 9 on 7/27's "Just in. Already trending."
- "ENDS TONIGHT" + "up to 40% off" + "ships in as little as one week" stacks urgency, offer, and the furniture-shipping-anxiety answer all in one hero block — that shipping line is exactly what I need before I'll buy a $1,500 chair.
- The rewards card math (10% back at CB2/Crate & Barrel/Crate & Kids, no annual fee) is laid out cleanly with real numbers, not vague "earn points" language.
- Product grid at the bottom shows strikethrough pricing (Andes bed $999→$849, Archer nightstand $399→$349) — actual markdowns, not just a banner claim.

## What's weak

- Way too much crammed in one send — furniture sale, bedroom/bath sale, sofa spotlight, credit card pitch, sweepstakes, product grid, app download, gift registry. Pick one story.
- The second banner ("SEMI-ANNUAL BEDROOM + BATH EVENT — up to 30% off") directly competes with the hero offer and dilutes the "ends tonight" urgency — is everything ending tonight or just furniture?
- "Leave a review, win $1,000" sweepstakes block feels bolted on and has nothing to do with the furniture event — pure clutter this deep into an already-long email.
- No color-palette or fabric-swatch content anywhere, which is the one thing that would actually move me on a $1,500+ item I can't touch first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one offer. Lead with the furniture event, kill the bedroom/bath banner, sweepstakes, and gift registry from this send — save them for separate emails. CB2's 7/27 and 7/28 sends worked because they stayed focused; this one regresses.
- 2. Move the rewards card content to its own send or a footer mention only — it's competing for attention with the actual sale.
- 3. Add a "shop by room" or swatch/fabric CTA under the hero chair — I'm renovating two houses, I need material info before I click through.
- 4. Trim the product grid to 3 items max, all tied to "the furniture event," not a random sofa + nightstand + dining table + side table grab bag.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 40% off furniture, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Few hours left — up to 40% off the furniture event`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `That velvet chair you keep re-adding to cart. Tonight only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ships in a week. Discount's gone by morning.`
