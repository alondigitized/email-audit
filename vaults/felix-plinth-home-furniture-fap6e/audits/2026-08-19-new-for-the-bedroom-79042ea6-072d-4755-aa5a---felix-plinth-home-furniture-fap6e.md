# NEW FOR THE BEDROOM
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2 again — this makes it seven or eight sends from the Crate & Barrel family this week alone (I audited the "goop" palette one and the Paige DeSorbo apartment one just yesterday), and this time it's a straight bedroom furniture drop: "JUST IN — New for the Bedroom, Only at CB2." No gimmick this time, no celebrity tie-in, no sale countdown — just four new pieces, moody warm-toned room shots, "designer" credits under each item. Honestly, this is the most CB2 has felt like itself all week.
- The Jackson Bed in that lush cognac velvet actually stopped my scroll for a second — that's a genuinely nice hero shot, and I'm mid-renovation on the primary bedroom so a statement bed is not irrelevant to me right now. The Hawk Nightstand and Rio Dresser both have "designed by [studio]" callouts which is a trust signal I don't see enough of — Article does the "meet the maker" thing occasionally but CB2's leaning into it harder here.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Jackson Bed specifically, out of curiosity for pricing. Would I forward it? No — there's no offer here to make it feel urgent, and my wife and I already have a running Pinterest-adjacent folder for bedroom stuff, this doesn't clear the bar to get added to it. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The Jackson Bed hero shot in cognac velvet is a genuinely arresting image — warm light, textured headboard, looks like something I'd screenshot for a mood board.
- "Designed by [Studio Name]" under each product (Rio Dresser, Jackson Bed, Hawk Nightstand) gives it a design-credibility angle that CB2's competitors mostly skip — Pottery Barn and Wayfair don't do this.
- Material specificity is there — "FSC-certified oak," "lush cotton-blend velvet," "faux mohair" — which matters to me when I'm actually furnishing two houses and comparing quality tiers.
- Clean category nav strip (Rugs, Mirrors, Lighting, Wall Art) at the bottom gives me an easy lane into adjacent categories if the bedroom stuff doesn't land.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, no broken image boxes, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing anywhere in this email until the "More For You" carousel at the very bottom — I have to scroll past four full product blocks before I see a single dollar figure. Pottery Barn's outdoor email at least front-loaded "up to 60% off" in the hero.
- No offer, no urgency, nothing time-bound — this is a pure catalog drop dressed as "just in." Fine for browsing, does nothing to make me act today.
- The "More For You" recommendation carousel at the bottom is a grab-bag — a bed, a dining table, an accent chair, a side table, a coffee table, a dining chair — none of it bedroom-related, which undercuts the whole "New for the Bedroom" premise the subject promised.
- Rewards math is buried at the very bottom in tiny text ("Earn 10% Back" / "Get 15% off") — CB2 knows I might be a rewards person and hides the one thing that'd actually move me.
- This is CB2's seventh-ish email to me this week — the "JUST IN" framing doesn't acknowledge that I've already seen three of these pieces styled in other room shots this month.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a price or a reason to act now — even "Bedroom refresh, from $399" in the hero would beat a bare "JUST IN." Right now there's no reason to open the email today versus next week.
- 2. Fix the "More For You" carousel to actually be bedroom-adjacent (nightstands, dressers, bedding) — right now it torpedoes its own subject line by showing dining tables and accent chairs.
- 3. Surface the CB2 Rewards / Barrel Rewards line higher up, not buried in 8pt text at the footer — that's the one lever that'd actually get me to click "Shop Now" over just browsing.
- 4. Add a swatch/sample CTA near the velvet and mohair pieces — I'm choosing fabric and finish across two houses right now, and "order a swatch" is the single most useful button CB2 could add for someone like me.

# Technical Email Audit — CB2 "NEW FOR THE BEDROOM"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard MI (Message Impact/MessageGears)-templated CB2 promotional email built on legacy MSO/VML table markup; it relies on third-party ad-redirect pixels served over plaintext HTTP and is missing standard compliance/authentication signals in the delivered headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 67 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped from HTTP probing (redirect domains not resolvable via automated check) — cannot confirm destination validity or redirect chain integrity.
- Multiple Dotomi ad-redirect endpoints are loaded over **unencrypted HTTP**, which mixed-content policies in modern mail clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) will likely block or strip:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 sequential cookie-sync pixels, all HTTP)
- These 11 HTTP-only pixel/redirect calls are a functional deliverability risk, not just a security nit: if blocked, downstream ad-network cookie sync and any attribution tied to it silently fails with no error surfaced to the sender.
- Legitimate first-party tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) are correctly served over HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 content images (`image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/*.jpg`) are missing `alt` text — screen readers and clients with images-off will render blank space with no fallback context.
- The 11 ad-redirect/tracking pixels are also missing `alt` attributes, though this is standard/expected for 1x1 tracking pixels.
- Template uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with heavy MSO/VML conditional comments — appropriate for Outlook desktop compatibility, no issues found in the conditional structure itself.
- `@font-face` declarations reference multiple external font hosts (`cb2.com`, `crateandbarrel.com`) — fonts will fail to load in most clients (Gmail, Outlook) since `@font-face` support is inconsistent; this is a graceful-degradation case (fallback stack `Helvetica, sans-serif` is present on at least the `a` selector), not a hard rendering bug.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`, `[FIELD]`) found in the truncated source.
- No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may exist in untruncated body via footer link, but header-level mechanism is absent or not captured).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe (POST) is not confirmed supported.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
- Caveat: all three are flagged as WARN, not confirmed failures — the QA tooling notes these may not be captured by the relay rather than confirmed absent from the original send. Recommend verifying against raw headers from the original ESP delivery, not just the relay capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot assess — the 67 tracking/redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing, so destination URLs, UTM parameter structure, and landing-page alignment are unverified in this pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Convert the 11 Dotomi HTTP endpoints (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/.../pub1–10.php`) to HTTPS, or confirm with the ad-network partner whether HTTPS endpoints are available — plaintext HTTP resources are increasingly blocked client-side.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/*.jpg`.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present in the actual ESP-delivered message (not just the relay-captured copy) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
4. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results directly from the sending ESP's logs, since the relay-side `Authentication-Results` header was not captured.
5. Re-run link validation directly against the 67 skipped tracking-redirect URLs (bypassing the skip filter or resolving the true destination) to confirm landing-page and UTM continuity before send.
