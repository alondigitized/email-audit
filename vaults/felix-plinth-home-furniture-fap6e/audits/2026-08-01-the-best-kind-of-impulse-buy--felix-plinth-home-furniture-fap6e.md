## Executive summary

- Okay, "The best kind of impulse buy" — CB2's back in my inbox again, and at this point I've graded five of their sends this week alone. This one's a best-seller roundup dressed up as a design showcase: green velvet sectional, that blue Gwyneth chair from Goop (of all people), a walnut bed, a console. It's pretty to scroll through, I'll give it that — the photography is doing real work, and I'm mid-reno on two houses so furniture in general is not something I'm ignoring.
- But the subject line oversells it. "Impulse buy" implies something snackable and specific — instead I get five hero products with zero prices attached, then the actual "SALE UP TO 40% OFF" banner buried more than halfway down after I've already scrolled past a "meet our best sellers" CTA twice. And a couple of the product blurbs read like they got cut off mid-sentence — "The it chair your closet. Never seen anywhere." isn't English, it's a copy bug.
- I'm not clicking this one. I'd scroll it on the toilet, maybe screenshot the Fable sectional for my Pinterest-adjacent folder, and move on. Not forwarding it — nothing here is urgent or personal enough to hand to a friend.

- Naming the designers (Studio Vitali, Goop, Mermelada Estudio) is a good touch — feels editorial, like the 7/30 home office send I gave an 8 for the same reason.
- "MEET OUR BEST SELLERS" CTA is repeated twice so I'm never far from a jumping-off point.
- The rewards footer ("Earn 10% Back... across CB2 and Crate & Barrel") is useful info I actually track since I've got both brands on my list.

## What's weak

- Two of the five hero product blurbs are visibly garbled/truncated — "A pop base for shelter, style. flexibility from all angles" and "Statement at its finest. In a new class of its own" don't parse as sentences. That's a proofing miss, not a style choice.
- Zero prices on any of the five hero pieces. I have to scroll to "MORE FOR YOU" at the bottom to see a single dollar sign, which is backwards — Pottery Barn's "Shades of Indigo" send at least kept price visibility close to the hero.
- The actual "40% off" sale — the one concrete reason to click today — is buried below six product blocks. Compare that to Article's "Ends Sunday: Shop Summer Sale," which leads with the deal instead of making me earn it.
- This is the fifth CB2 email in my inbox this week (7/30 home office 8, 7/31 steals 6, 7/31 mood board 6, 8/1 bedroom event 7, now this). The cadence is genuinely wearing me down — I'm starting to auto-triage anything from cb2@mail.cb2.com.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "40% off" offer into the hero block, above the fold — right now the strongest CTA is stranded two-thirds down.
- 2. Fix the truncated copy on the Gwyneth chair and Macron console blocks before this goes out again.
- 3. Put a starting price under each of the five hero pieces — even "from $2,499" beats nothing.
