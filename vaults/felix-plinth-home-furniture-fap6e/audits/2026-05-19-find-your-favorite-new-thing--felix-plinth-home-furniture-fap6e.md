# Find your favorite new thing
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Third CB2 in three days. I barely got through the sale blast on the 17th, woke up to a baffling "Welcome to CB2" yesterday on a list I've been on for years, and now this — with a subject line that could have been generated by literally any brand in any category on any given Tuesday. A mattress startup could send "Find your favorite new thing." A luggage company could send "Find your favorite new thing." CB2 specifically? Impossible to tell from the inbox.
- Inside the email, though, it calmed down. The hero lifestyle shot is legitimately warm and well-composed — layered textures, natural light, the kind of room I'm actually trying to build in both homes. The category grid (Furniture & Rugs, Bedding & Bath, Decor & Dining, Lighting) is useful when you're actively furnishing two properties and making a dozen purchasing decisions a month. I scrolled further than I expected to.
- But I didn't click, and I won't forward it. No Memorial Day hook despite every competitor in my inbox hammering MDW week. No personalization. No urgency. It's a catalog send dressed as a discover invite, and in a week where Article delivered a tight, time-bounded offer that actually earned a 9 from me, CB2 is the friend who shows up to the party without a bottle.

## What's working

- The hero shot earns its real estate — warm, tactile, lived-in; it looks like a CB2 store window, not stock photography recycled from three other brands
- "LUXURY LIES IN THE DETAILS" as a section header is on-brand and resonant — it's the exact pitch CB2 should be making against IKEA and Wayfair in my head
- "EVERYDAY FREE SHIPPING" is well-placed mid-scroll and answers the friction I always carry with furniture pricing
- The "EARN 10% BACK" loyalty block near the footer actually reminded me I should be stacking that against my rewards card math
- The app download + 10% off is the one clean, concrete ask in the whole email — simple and credible

## What's weak

- "Find your favorite new thing" is the vaguest subject CB2 could have written — Article sent "The deals are in full swing" this week and felt more purposeful with less creative effort
- Zero Memorial Day tie-in while Crate & Kids and Wayfair are running full MDW scaffolding; CB2 feels like they're deliberately looking the other way during the biggest home-goods promotional window of the spring
- The interior design appointment block ("THE INTERIOR DESIGN MAGAZINE") is buried below the app section and completely undersold — Pottery Barn Design Services devoted an entire email to a free design appointment offer and I opened it; CB2 has the same card and hid it
- Three emails in three days, with a "Welcome" email in the middle, makes the sequencing feel broken rather than deliberate
- No personalization anywhere — CB2 knows I browse furniture and lighting; "bring your heart's desire home" is a stranger-to-stranger line

## Recommendations

- 1. **Tie to Memorial Day with a concrete offer** — Even a banner "Memorial Day: Free Shipping + 10% Back" would make this feel intentional. Right now it feels like the campaign team was on vacation while everyone else ran MDW.
- 2. **Move the design appointment CTA to hero position or panel 2** — Pottery Barn made a standalone email out of free design appointments and it was the most relevant thing they've sent me in weeks. CB2 buried the equivalent offer below the app download.
- 3. **Fix the automation sequence** — A welcome email one day after a sale email signals broken logic. Either suppress welcome sends for existing subscribers or space them by a week minimum.
- 4. **Write a subject with a category or offer hook** — Anything more specific than "thing" would outperform this send.
- 5. **Link one hero product, not just categories** — Article linked to specific pieces this week; CB2's browse tiles are fine but one standout item with a price would have given me a concrete reason to click.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Our best new arrivals — before Memorial Day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping + 10% back while you browse`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Book a free design consult — on us`
