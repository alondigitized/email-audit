# New! Exclusive designs for Hanukkah

- I opened this expecting another "Early Black Friday" blast, and the Hanukkah angle was a pleasant surprise. It's the most editorial C&B send I've had since 11/11. - I'm renovating two houses and I'm not hosting Hanukkah. The gift-and-hosting content mostly doesn't apply to me. ## What's working

- The product photography is excellent. The navy runner, the Star of David plates and the red wine glasses with brass candlestick all share one palette, which is how I think when I'm styling a room.
- Each product gets a tag: "Bestseller," "Exclusive," "Personalize It!". That's quick social proof, and the monogrammed napkin is a nice touch.
- The "Exclusive" label on the plates reads as C&B-only, so I won't see the same piece in three other brands' emails.
- The kitchen section has real numbers: "Up to 61% off KitchenAid," "30% off select Le Creuset," and "free shipping."
- The CTA buttons are navy pills with legible white text. They're large enough to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- It's too long. Around a dozen sections for one sale, and the "Shop Hanukkah" button appears over and over.
- The Rewards and Early Black Friday message is a small strip at the top. After seven or eight C&B-family emails this week, that's the part I care about, and it's buried.
- Two stacked buttons ("Shop Linens" and "Shop Hanukkah") under the linens row are redundant and make me choose between nearly identical options.
- The subject promises "exclusive designs," but the email then pivots to a Black Friday and kitchen sale. The two halves feel stitched together.
- There's nothing for a renovator: no furniture, lighting or rugs until the sale module, and no swatches or shipping lead times.
- "Tour Red Wine Glasses" is a vague, tour-style label instead of a clear shop link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Early Black Friday and Double Rewards offer into the hero. Keep Hanukkah as the second act, not the opener.
- 2. Cut the email to five modules: hero, menorahs, table, one kitchen deal row and one furniture row. Use one button per module.
- 3. Replace "Tour Red Wine Glasses" with "Shop Wine Glasses".
- 4. Segment: send this Hanukkah version to people who've browsed tabletop or gifts. Send furniture buyers the 11/11-style version.
- 5. Add a single "ships by Dec 18" line for the personalized items.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Movable Ink–templated email with about 111 click-tracked links and 60+ images missing `alt`. It also has some markup and template-hygiene problems. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, because the relay didn't capture them and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations not verified.** The QA run skipped 111 links as tracking or click-redirect domains, so none were probed for status or final destination. Whether the redirect targets resolve is unknown.
- **Third-party pixels.**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests go to a third-party domain. I believe rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's, but that is from memory, not from the email.
  - The `s=` value looks like a 64-character hex hash. It matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels and images. Each fires a separate request.
- **Tracking pixels.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/9e3d8e7b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression pixels. Neither has `alt`. A missing `alt` attribute fails validation; `alt=""` is the correct value for pixels.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 21,996 of 30,347 characters (72%) are URLs, above the 70% threshold. Long tracking URLs in the text alternative can hurt deliverability scoring and readability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 65 images.** Examples:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `…/m/1/bf3053a9-…jpg`
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/560d850e7e517ede.png`, plus six more `/p/rp/*.png` images from `mi.crateandbarrel.com`
  
  Most are content images, and the `/p/rp/*.png` ones look like dynamic product tiles. With images blocked or a screen reader in use, the email loses that content.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **iOS text resizing disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` conflict with `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` later in the source.
- **Markup hygiene.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` is duplicated alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type tag.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` shipped to production.
  - The Outlook block has an empty rule, `td,th,div,p,a { }`.
- **Dead CSS.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, because both match on desktop at equal specificity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this can't be confirmed as a sender-side defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (QA WARN). RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders, so it is worth checking at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown; this is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` aligns with the image hosts (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`).
- **Footer not verified.** The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 111 tracking links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked. No UTM parameters are visible in the available source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images, and use `alt=""` on the `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, de-duplicate the meta tags, delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment, and remove the dead `min-width:768px` img rule.
4. Capture raw headers directly (for example by seeding a Gmail inbox) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links with a browser-like client to check landing pages and UTM carry-through. Also fetch the full HTML to check the footer.
6. Shorten the tracking URLs in the plain-text part to bring the URL ratio below 70%.
