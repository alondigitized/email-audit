# BOO! Shop Halloween before it sells out this year →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, at least this one's a change of pace from the twelfth dinnerware email and the ninth Crate & Kids send — this is straight-up Halloween decor: black pumpkins, moody porch shots, a witch's brew cocktail spread. It's genuinely the best-styled email I've gotten from this brand family all week, and the "can't wait for Halloween" hero with the lit porch actually stopped my scroll for a second.
- But then it does the exact same thing every other C&B email this week has done — buries the actual dollars-off math under seasonal styling, then pivots into a full second email about the Labor Day sale with "Double Rewards" and a totally different visual language. By the time I hit the Labor Day block I've forgotten this was supposed to be a Halloween email. It's two campaigns stapled together, and neither one gets my full attention.
- I'd open it (Halloween decor is a real category for us since we're doing the front porch at the second house), but I'm not clicking past the hero pumpkins, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — this is the kind of email you skim and archive, not send to a friend.

## What's working

- The hero shot — carved pumpkins, lantern glow, wreath on a dark door — is genuinely seasonal and well-art-directed, better than anything CB2 or Wayfair has sent me this week.
- "Love at first fright" and "no tricks, all delicious treats" section headers are on-brand and mildly clever, not just generic promo copy.
- The witch's brew cocktail glassware section is a smart cross-sell — I could actually see picking up a set of those glasses for a party.
- Product photography throughout is clean and consistent, no render bugs or broken images.

## What's weak

- No actual Halloween discount anywhere I can see — the only numbers on the page ("up to 60% off," "Double Rewards") belong to the Labor Day sale, not Halloween, so the "shop before it sells out" urgency in the subject has zero price backup.
- The email tries to be two campaigns (Halloween decor + Labor Day furniture sale) and the seam between them is jarring — I'm in spooky-cocktail mode and then suddenly staring at a rug and lighting grid.
- "Shop Now" appears like six times with zero differentiation — I have no idea which button actually takes me to Halloween stuff versus the general sale.
- This is now the thirteenth Crate & Barrel-family email in my folder this week — even a genuinely nice hero image can't fight that fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — a tight Halloween decor email with a real offer, and keep Labor Day separate. Article's Labor Day email led with "up to 40% off" and nothing else competing for attention; that focus is why it scored well with me.
- 2. Put an actual number on the Halloween half — "20% off Halloween decor" or similar — the styling alone won't beat CB2's blunt "50% off decor & mirrors" on price-motivated shoppers like me.
- 3. Label the CTAs distinctly ("Shop Halloween," "Shop Glassware," "Shop Labor Day") instead of six identical "Shop Now" buttons.
- 4. Cut the send cadence — thirteen emails in a week from one brand family is training me to archive on sight.
