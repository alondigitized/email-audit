## What's working

- The hero chair against a dark backdrop is a strong first impression, and "MOST SOUGHT AFTER" tells me this is curated.
- Naming the pieces ("The Deseo Sectional," "The Macron Console") and crediting designers like Studio ANANSI makes it feel like a catalog. I'd save this.

- Sections are clearly separated: hero, the four featured pieces, "Now and Future Classics," the dining event, lighting and the product grid. Navigation stays simple.
- The two "Shop Best Sellers" buttons are clear and sit in the right places.

## What's weak

- No price or offer appears until well past the halfway point. For a "most sought after" send, I want at least a "from $X."
- The dining event 30% block repeats 10/24's "FINAL DAYS: The Semi-Annual Dining Event." It feels like filler, and it clashes with the luxury tone above it.
- The email is far too long. The lighting block and the product grid add the same "new arrivals" energy as 10/24's "THIS JUST IN."
- The buttons and credit lines under each product are tiny, and the dark photos make white text hard to read. I'd miss these taps on my phone.
- The footer, with the app promo, Rewards at "earn 5% back" and the Crate & Barrel family links, is cluttered. I'm already tired of seeing the same Crate & Barrel family sale in my inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or a "from $X" under each named product, and link each tile to its own product page. That's the single biggest lift for someone shopping two houses.
- 2. Cut the email by a third. Drop either the lighting block or the product grid, because they duplicate the room shots.
- 3. Move the dining event out of this send, or give it a distinct deadline. Crate & Barrel's "3 DAYS LEFT" dining email at least has a countdown. This one just says "ending soon."
- 4. Make the credit lines larger and brighter so they read on dark photos.
- 5. Add a swatch or fabric-sample link and delivery windows on the sectional and bed. For furniture, that's what stops me from buying.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with Outlook conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 418px. QA flags 25 images without alt text, 11 third-party tracking pixels loaded over plaintext `http://`, and three compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified (QA pass rate 57%, 0 errors, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing was skipped.** 67 links were skipped because they are on tracking or click-redirect domains. Final destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **11 pixels load over plaintext HTTP.** All are on Dotomi (Epsilon):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients that block mixed content or proxy images (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may drop them, so cookie-sync and attribution can fail silently.
  - These are third-party cookie-sync pixels. They add 11 external requests per open and are a privacy and deliverability surface.
- **Two first-party open pixels have no alt text:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/5a277eb4-b81d-4529-9f70-34e72f7579d0?mi_cid=...`. They are probably hidden. If they aren't also sized 1x1 or hidden, they could render as broken-image artifacts.
- **Font hosting:** Some `@font-face` rules point at a different brand's domain (`https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`). Some CB2 font URLs have a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Both are fragile if the paths change. Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway, and the fallback is `Helvetica, sans-serif`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **25 images are missing `alt`:**
  - 12 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` (for example `a979b0e9-0721-4ca9-84bb-5e2bc0874756.jpg`, `1cb65792-...`, `5c0a7324-956a-498f-849d-8794a08519a1.jpg`).
  - 11 Dotomi pixels.
  - 2 first-party tracking pixels.
  - The 12 content images matter most. With images blocked, or for screen readers, the body content is unavailable. If these carry the offers and calls to action, the email loses its message and links.
  - For the pixels, add `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` disables pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility regression.
- **Dead markup:** The head contains a placeholder comment (`<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`) and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` block. This is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Doctype:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed in an XHTML doctype. Rendering is minor, but it is a validity issue.
- The remaining HTML is truncated, so I can't verify the images' `width` and `height` attributes, the use of `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the footer markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The sections that would contain tokens, such as the greeting and footer, are truncated, so unrendered merge tokens can't be ruled out. The `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` parameters on the tracking pixels are populated (hashed), not raw placeholders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`: not found.** QA notes the relay may not capture it. The header is likely sent but unverified. If it is truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found.** One-click unsubscribe is required for bulk senders (Gmail and Yahoo, 2024+). It is unverified for the same reason.
- **`Authentication-Results`: not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Sending from `mail.cb2.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer:** I can't confirm the visible unsubscribe link, the physical postal address, or the sender identification, because the body is truncated.

These three warnings reflect what the relay captured, not confirmed defects. Verify against the raw `.eml` headers before acting.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** Not verifiable. The 67 tracking links go through a redirect domain, so the destination parameters are hidden. No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source, only `mi_*` (Marketing Cloud-style) identifiers on the pixels.
- **Landing page alignment:** Not verified, because link probing was skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text.** Give every content image on `image.mail.cb2.com` a descriptive `alt`, and set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (or remove the 10 cookie-sync `pubN.php` pixels) to avoid mixed-content blocking and reduce third-party requests.
3. **Verify headers from the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com`.
4. **Resolve a sample of tracked links** (follow the redirects) to confirm the destination URLs, the 200 status and the UTM parameters.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
6. **Clean the template:** remove the placeholder comment, self-close the meta tag, fix the `//Styles` double-slash font URLs, and host the fonts on CB2's own domain.
7. **Pipeline note:** capture the full HTML (not truncated) and the raw headers so the footer, merge-token and compliance checks can be done.
