# STILL GOING STRONG: 2X THE REWARDS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and this time it's the credit card sender. It's the same "Long Weekend Sale, up to 50% off" banner I've been looking at since 10/30. My count is the sixteenth CB2 send in about two weeks. Yesterday's "DAYS LEFT TO SAVE + Double Rewards" and "Things of quality + Double rewards is on" had the same banner. The only new thing is the 20% back line, which sits under a struck-through "10%". That is a real offer, but the layout makes me work to find it.
- The subject says "still going strong," which tells me nothing new. It reads as a brand talking to itself. I'm renovating two houses, so a 20% rewards-back offer should matter to me, and I do pay with the highest-rewards card. But it only counts if I open a CB2 Visa. The email never says what I'd have to do, how fast approval is, or what 20% actually works out to on a $4,000 sofa.
- Would I click? Probably not today. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and file it with the Pinterest-adjacent folder only for the dining table photo.

## What's working

- The hero dining scene (table, sculptural pendant, chairs) is a good room shot. It's the one image that made me stop scrolling.
- "20% back in reward dollars" with the 10% struck through shows the upgrade at a glance. That's real rewards math.
- The category tiles (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) are clean, and the photography has a consistent warm palette. That suits someone matching colors across two houses.
- The footer cross-promotes Crate & Barrel and Crate & Barrel Kids, and the "10% back at CB2 and Crate & Barrel" block is useful if you shop both.
- The deadline, "Now through Nov 17th, 2026," is a real date.

## What's weak

- The sale banner is stale. I've seen it for two weeks, and it competes with the rewards hero for the top spot.
- The CTA is "Learn More," which is weak. It sounds like I'm signing up for homework, not saving money.
- Two offers are stacked: up to 50% off and 20% back. It's unclear whether they combine. The fine print, which is tiny, says sale pricing and exclusions apply.
- "Scroll to shop and start earning rewards" is filler. The tiles below it have no prices and no product names, so I can't tell what anything costs.
- The "10%" in the footer and the "20%" up top confused me about which rate applies to what.
- There's nothing about shipping on furniture, lead times, or swatches.
- Pottery Barn's nightstand email at least showed nine priced products. This one shows zero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Put "20% back with the CB2 Visa" at the top, drop the sale banner or shrink it, and say plainly whether the two stack.
- 2. Change the CTA from "Learn More" to "Apply for the CB2 Visa" or "Shop and earn 20% back."
- 3. Replace the category tiles with four or five priced hero products. Wayfair's pergola sends were repetitive, but they at least named products and dimensions.
- 4. Add a worked example: "$3,000 sofa = $600 back."
- 5. Refresh the creative. Fifteen-plus sends with the same banner trains people to ignore the brand.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, an unresolved merge token `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also has widespread missing alt text and about 10 insecure third-party tracking pixels. The 43% QA pass rate is inflated by header checks that the AgentMail relay can't evaluate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 63 links were skipped because they use click-redirect or tracking domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Insecure pixels:** 10 Dotomi pixels load over `http://`.
  - Hosts: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or proxied in some clients, and can trigger mixed-content warnings in web clients. The cookie-sync matching they do will silently fail when blocked.
- **Other tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/7926cfb6-...?mi_cid=...` are HTTPS. Both lack alt text.
- **Template leftover:** The source contains a template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 8 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `e6081688-…jpg`
  - `fb97ec32-…gif`
  - `2400587f-…jpg`
  - `b2c3126c-…jpg`
  - `8727844f-…jpg`
  - `b5514231-…jpg`
  - `a117864e-…jpg`
  - `7cec3000-…jpg`

  If these carry offer or CTA content, the email is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The 12 pixel images have no `alt`. They should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, there is a non-empty `<title>`, and MSO conditional comments are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` is in the delivered HTML. This is SFMC-style syntax that failed to resolve, probably a dynamic product-reviews block.
  - The recipient may see the literal string.
  - Or the block may render empty, leaving a gap.
- No other tokens were detected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers not confirmed:** The `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` headers were not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing them, so these are unverified, not confirmed defects.
  - The sender should verify the headers on direct delivery. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Footer:** The HTML provided is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM evidence:** The truncated HTML and the QA output show no UTM parameters. Because links go through click-redirect domains, any UTMs would be applied at the redirect, which is invisible here.
- **Landing pages:** Not verifiable, since the 63 tracked links weren't probed.
- **Tracking IDs:** The pixel URLs carry `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` identifiers. These show per-recipient tracking is in place.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` resolution at the ESP, and add a fallback so the block hides when no data is returned.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop them if the endpoints don't support it.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against raw `.eml` headers (direct SMTP capture) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
6. Resolve a sample of the 63 redirect links to confirm status codes and UTM or landing-page alignment.
7. Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment from the template.
