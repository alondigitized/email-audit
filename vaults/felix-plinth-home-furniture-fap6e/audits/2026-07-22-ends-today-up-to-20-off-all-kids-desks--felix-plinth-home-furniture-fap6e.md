# ENDS TODAY: Up to 20% off ALL kids desks!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-nine. That's where the C&B family lands in my July inbox after this one, and I want that number sitting right on top of my reaction because it's impossible to separate. When "Crate & Kids SALE" hit my inbox, I clocked it before I even read the subject — the sender name has a Pavlovian exhaustion attached to it at this point in July. The subject itself reads fine: clear offer, real urgency, specific category. But then I opened it, and the $1,000 sweepstakes module is sitting right there in the scroll — the *exact same one* from the Crate & Kids email that landed in my inbox yesterday. Same graphic, same copy, same entry mechanic. That's not a campaign. That's a copy-paste.
- The deeper problem is that I don't have kids. My renovation projects are adult spaces — living rooms, primary bedrooms, a home office setup I've been circling for months. Kids desks are a zero-relevance category for me right now. This is Crate & Barrel's database treating me like a generic homeowner rather than the specific buyer I actually am. Compare that to the Article dining room email I reviewed a few days ago, which hit me at exactly the right moment because it read like they knew I was mid-decision on chairs. This reads like they knew nothing about me.
- Mark as read. No forward. Absolutely no click.

## What's working

- The offer is immediately legible — "20% off all kids desks" is zero-ambiguity, zero-scroll needed to understand the deal.
- Visual hierarchy is clean: logo → hero image → offer → CTA. The eye lands where it should.
- The email renders without bugs — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds on mobile.
- "ENDS TODAY" in the subject creates genuine time pressure that at least makes the send feel purposeful rather than evergreen filler.
- The storage secondary section ("up to 20% off select storage") extends the value prop beyond just desks without muddying the main message.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — zero segmentation effort here. Pottery Barn sent the Chris Loves Julia collab to me and it at least mapped to my aesthetic. This maps to a parent I'm not.
- The $1,000 sweepstakes module is recycled from yesterday's Crate & Kids send. Within 24 hours. On the same sublist. That is genuinely embarrassing creative cadence.
- ALL CAPS on "ENDS TODAY" and "ALL" in the subject feels like a 2014 email blast. CB2 managed a "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send with no caps abuse and it still read urgent.
- "We love these for you" personalization module at the bottom shows kids products regardless — the personalization layer didn't do any heavy lifting.
- Twenty-nine emails from the C&B family in July. This one had to earn its keep. It didn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress non-parents from this send entirely.** If purchase or browse history doesn't include kids furniture, this email shouldn't land. Article managed to feel surgical; Crate & Kids should too.
- 2. **Retire the $1,000 sweepstakes module or give it a 72-hour cooldown minimum.** Seeing the same module two days running trains subscribers to ignore it permanently.
- 3. **Kill the ALL CAPS.** "Ends today" does the same job without the broadcast-radio energy.
- 4. **Surface a specific product in the hero**, not a lifestyle room shot — Article linked directly to a chair I could picture in my space. This links to a category page.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Your kids' desk upgrade — 20% off, today only`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Desks for the study room: 20% off ends tonight`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 20% off kids storage — sale wraps at midnight.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the full sale before it's gone — desks, storage, and more.`

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**

Not captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the unsubscribe affordance in the UI. Its absence increases friction for recipients attempting to unsubscribe and raises spam-filter risk on high-volume sends.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**

One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender policy for >5k/day senders) cannot be confirmed. If the header exists at the MTA level but is stripped by the relay, this needs to be verified against raw SMTP headers from Marigold/Sailthru.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` header absent**

SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. The sending domain (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`) appears correctly aligned with the From header, but authentication cannot be confirmed from available data. Recommend pulling raw headers from a delivered sample to verify.
