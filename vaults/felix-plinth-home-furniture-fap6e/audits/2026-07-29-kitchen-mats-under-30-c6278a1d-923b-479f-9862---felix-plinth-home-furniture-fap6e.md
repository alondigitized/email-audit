- Outdoor planter clearance tile has a real number on it — 40% off, orange badge, easy to spot.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- Section dividers (purple banners) at least break up the wall of tiles so it's not one undifferentiated scroll.

## What's weak

- Subject promised kitchen mats; kitchen mats is one tile out of twenty-plus, not even first — CB2 doesn't pull this bait-and-switch, when they say "Furniture Event" the hero is furniture.
- Zero furniture-renovation relevance for someone mid-reno on two houses — no swatches, no showroom integration, no "complete the room" bundling like Pottery Barn at least attempts with its bedding sets.

- Financing pitch (Afterpay/Klarna) is irrelevant to me — I pay off in full every month for the points, so "no interest" isn't a hook, it's noise.
- No loyalty/rewards math anywhere despite a "Wayfair Rewards" icon at the bottom — just a logo, no actual member benefit surfaced.

- 1. Pick ONE hero and build the whole email around it. If it's kitchen mats, lead with kitchen mats big at the top with the $30 price visible — don't bury the subject-line product in a four-tile grid.
- 2. Cut the grid down from 20+ tiles to 6-8 max. CB2's "24 hours left" send worked because it committed to a theme; this is the opposite.
- 3. Move the AC module up top — it's the most seasonally credible thing in here and it's currently three scrolls down.
- 4. Drop or personalize the financing banner — segment it out for people who've actually used Afterpay/Klarna before, don't blast it to everyone.

# Technical Email Audit — Wayfair "KITCHEN MATS under $30" (maiid=15860)

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Wayfair batch marketing template (Unbounce/BEE-style responsive grid, `graymatter_batch` source) with heavy proprietary click-tracking wrapping every link and pixel. Automated QA could only confirm 12 of 60 links due to rate limiting/time budget, and found a pattern of missing `alt` attributes plus insecure (`http://`) tracking pixels.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All content links are wrapped in Wayfair's internal tracking schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_121678`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15860`, `batchid=15`, `varid=3`, `csnid=...`, `sltid=...`, `brcid=1`, `mdlid=...`) — consistent and well-formed across sampled links (e.g., `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.html?eid=63966&...`).
- 12/60 links were probed by QA; all 7 that returned a status were rate-limited (HTTP 429), so link validity is **unconfirmed, not failed** — this reflects Wayfair's probe defenses, not a confirmed broken-link defect.
- 48 links were skipped (deprioritized footer/utility/social) and 4 more (wall-art clearance, area rugs, pool tables, BNPL page) were unprobed due to a 70s time budget — no pass/fail data available for these.
- 20 tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) use plain **HTTP**, not HTTPS. Non-secure image requests are frequently blocked by mail clients (Gmail/Outlook proxy over HTTPS), which would silently suppress open/engagement tracking for these placements.
- One additional non-pixel tracking beacon, `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (base64-encoded `EventType=EmailOpen`), is present and correctly served over HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 15+ `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including content-bearing images, not just decorative ones:
  - `0728_OutdoorClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg` (hero banner)
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple`
  - 3x `wfus_jt800_batch10_majorappliances_airconditioners` (product images)
  - `wfna_0331_bnpl_email_banner_01.png`, `bnpl_legal_disclaimer.png`
  - One `<img>` with an empty `src` attribute
  - 20x `imp` tracking pixels (expected to have empty/decorative alt, but currently have none set — best practice is `alt=""` explicitly rather than omitting the attribute)
- Media-query CSS (`max-width: 480px`) is present and comprehensive (Outlook/Gmail-specific fixes, `u-MobileHide`/`u-MobileShow`, `-webkit-touch-callout` fallback for half-width blocks) — no structural mobile-responsiveness issues found in the visible markup.
- Doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, consistent with legacy ESP table-based templates — no issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge/personalization tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIRSTNAME%%`) are present in the visible subject line, from-name, or truncated HTML body.
- From-name is the static string `"Wayfair"` — no dynamic personalization observed.
- No issues found in the sampled markup, though the HTML was truncated — a full-source pass would be needed to rule out personalization logic further down in the template.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found by QA. Caveat: QA notes this **may be a relay artifact** ("Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay") rather than a confirmed absence in the original send — cannot be treated as a confirmed CAN-SPAM violation without checking raw source headers pre-relay.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe): not found, same relay caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — same caveat; SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from this data.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is present per QA's probing methodology note ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), though its specific URL was not surfaced in the sampled findings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair uses a proprietary attribution schema instead of standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` are absent everywhere sampled) — not a defect, but worth noting if this email is being benchmarked against a UTM-based analytics pipeline, since standard UTM parsing tools won't pick up attribution here.
- The primary CTA ("Kitchen Mats Under $30") correctly deep-links to a matching landing page: `/daily-sales/filters/kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.html?eid=63966` — subject-line-to-landing-page alignment confirmed for the hero offer.
- Secondary content (air conditioners, BNPL, app install, daily sales) links to correspondingly relevant category/feature pages (`/home-improvement/sb0/air-conditioners-c420527.html`, `/buy-now-pay-later~b547.html`, app onelink) — no mismatches found in the sampled set.
- App-install CTA uses a OneLink deep-link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with both `af_dp` (in-app deep link) and `af_web_dp` (web fallback) params correctly set — no issues found.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the 8 content images missing it (hero, SKU, appliance, BNPL banner images); explicitly set `alt=""` on the 20 tracking pixels and the empty-`src` image to satisfy accessibility linting cleanly rather than omitting the attribute.
2. Migrate the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels to HTTPS to avoid silent blocking by mail clients that require secure image loading — this directly affects open-tracking accuracy.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` against raw pre-relay headers rather than the AgentMail-relayed copy, since the current WARNs are inconclusive, not confirmed failures.
4. Re-run link probing with a longer time budget or reduced request rate to get pass/fail confirmation on the 12 rate-limited links and the 4 unprobed links, particularly the BNPL page given its regulatory/legal-disclaimer content.
