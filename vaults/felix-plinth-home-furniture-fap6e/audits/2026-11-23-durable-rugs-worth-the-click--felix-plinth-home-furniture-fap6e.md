# 👀 DURABLE RUGS worth the click
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and the subject promised DURABLE RUGS. The only rug I can find is one small tile in the top-left, "Durable Rugs Just For You," squeezed into a grid with pergolas, kitchen mats and garden statues. I opened this expecting a rug email and got a catalog dump. It's the same "Black Friday 80% OFF" banner I've seen on every send since 11/09. Yesterday's "5x8 shag rugs under $75" at least stayed on topic. This one doesn't.
- The AllModern block in the middle is the best part. A mid-century dining room with navy chairs is the kind of thing I'd actually pin. But it sends me to a different site, and with two houses to furnish I'm already tracking AllModern separately. Below it the email keeps going: bathroom vanity, comforter sets, bar stools, shuffleboard, Christmas trees, a Santa light string. It reads as a 20-tile scroll with no point of view.
- I'd save the AllModern image to my Pinterest folder and archive the rest. I wouldn't forward it. If I click, it's for the rug tile, and I'm not confident it lands on durable rugs.

## What's working

- The AllModern mid-century hero is a real room with a real palette. It's the one image that made me stop, and it matches the Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel look I already save.
- The Sealy / 80% OFF banner is readable and the offer number is large.
- The bathroom vanity image under "ready for a refresh?" is clean. It's relevant to a renovation, since I'm doing a primary bath.
- The footer links for Rewards, Financing and Professional are easy to find. Rewards math matters to me, even though nothing here shows what I'd earn.

## What's weak

- The subject says DURABLE RUGS, but rugs get one tile out of about 20. That's a bait-and-switch on the subject.
- Nothing has a price or a deal attached. "Up to 80%" is generic, and only the kitchen-mat and drapes tiles ("Under $20") show any number.
- The pergola, garden statues and Santa lights have nothing to do with a home renovation. It feels like a random assortment.
- The AllModern "Shop This + More" button takes me off Wayfair mid-email, and the same mid-century look will probably hit my inbox from AllModern itself this week.
- Many tiles are labelled "Just for You," but nothing here reflects what I've browsed. It still treats me like a stranger.
- The tile labels are tiny and the tap targets are cramped on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. Lead with a full-width hero of four or five durable rugs, with sizes and prices, and cut the unrelated categories.
- 2. Put one concrete offer in the hero, such as "Rugs from $59, 5x8 and 8x10 in stock." Show the performance details I'd actually care about: pile, washable, pet or kid friendly.
- 3. Take out the AllModern block or move it to the bottom. Show it only to people who've browsed that brand.
- 4. Add shipping and delivery-date info for large items, and rewards or financing math on the hero.
- 5. Cut the tile count by more than half. Group by room, not by random category.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Low-pile, washable, and delivered before the holidays.`

- **3 links were never checked.** The probe hit its 70s budget, so these are neither pass nor fail:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/our-best-kitchen-mats-for-less-se205`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-garden-statues-se73905`
  
  The report shows these URLs cut off mid-path, so they should be re-checked in full. Only 12 of 60 links were probed in total (48 skipped).
- **Mixed content: 20 tracking pixels use `http://`.** They are `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`, `p=d19b41d5-...`). Clients may block or proxy them, which would under-count impressions.
- **The app deep-link's web fallback is also non-HTTPS:** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` on the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` link.
- **Campaign labeling is inconsistent on the OneLink URL.** It has `c=Triggered` but `source=graymatter_batch` and `batchid=15`. A batch send is labeled as a triggered campaign, which will skew attribution in AppsFlyer.
