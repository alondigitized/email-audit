# Our favorite EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS ❤️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Pottery Barn send in about two days, and I'm numb to it. After "Just in! NEW Early Black Friday deals" yesterday, "Our favorite" with a heart emoji feels like the same email in a new outfit. The hero is at least honest. It's a maroon block with "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" and a Shop Now button, so I know what the email is about in about a second.
- The body is a tidy stack of category tiles, each with a price. Banks Dining Table is Was $2,199, Now $2,519. Cole Swivel Chair is Was $1,749, Now $874. Those prices make me do math instead of dream. I'm doing two houses, so I'd skim it. I'd probably click through once to check the Sofas & Chairs tile, then go back to the folder where I save these. I wouldn't forward it. A friend can open their own Pottery Barn inbox.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Up to 70% off" is huge, with one Shop Now button. It beats Wayfair's 20-tile rug grid, where the rug was one tile among pergolas.
- Every category tile has a product name with a Was/Now price. The Cole Swivel Chair at $874 and the Corinna Reversible Quilt at $174 are the kind of anchors I can use.
- "Ships Free" badges on the Bedding, Pillows & Throws, Tabletop and Gifts tiles. For furniture shipping, that's a trust cue.
- "In-stock furniture, delivered in 1-4 weeks" speaks to my reno timeline. C&B's bed email never gave me that.
- The Design Crew, 10% back in rewards and Affirm blocks all speak to how I buy. I pay with the highest-rewards card, so the rewards line gets a look.

## What's weak

- The Banks Dining Table says "Was $2,199, Now $2,519". That's a price increase, or a typo. As a render, it's a bug I can see, and it kills my trust in every other number.
- Cadence. This is the sixth send in two days, and it repeats the structure of 11/13's "Just in! NEW Early Black Friday deals" and "100s of NEW Early Black Friday furniture deals." "Our favorite" isn't a new reason to open.
- The "More Early Black Friday Deals" tiles for Rugs, Lighting and Bath have no prices or offers. They're pretty but thin.
- "Recommended just for you" shows holiday stockings, a curtain rod and a velvet stocking again. It doesn't read as personal to someone shopping for sofas, tables and rugs.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is long and tiny. The offer is clear up top, but I can't read the exclusions without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the Banks Dining Table price. A Now price higher than the Was price is the single biggest credibility hit in the email.
- 2. Name the hero reason. "Our favorite" isn't one. Lead with a specific deadline or a best-seller, such as "Sofas up to 50% off, ends Sunday."
- 3. Add prices to the Rugs, Lighting and Bath tiles so they aren't dead ends.
- 4. Use real personalization in the recommendation block. Show furniture or rugs based on what I browsed, not stockings.
- 5. Space out the sends. Two a day all week is what pushes me toward unsubscribing or filtering.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with many accessibility and hygiene gaps: about 50 images lack alt text, three assets load over `http://`, and the `<title>` is empty. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay doesn't expose those headers. The HTML supplied was truncated, so the footer and any unsubscribe link couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 60 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped as redirect domains. No link validity or destination data exists for this email.
- **Mixed-content tracking pixels over HTTP:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is a non-HTTPS open pixel and may be blocked.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is non-HTTPS.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is non-HTTPS.
- **Extra pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/e3a1f571-...?mi_cid=...` are additional tracking pixels with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer inside a Pottery Barn send. It is a shared-template dependency and has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images are flagged.
  - Content images: `slices_01–15.jpg` in the `1114_SAT_AM_EBF_ZigZagGrid_WasNow` grid, the product images (`remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and others), and the `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg` banners.
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, the email has little or no readable content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this affects the web-view and accessibility name.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility (WCAG 1.4.4) concern.
- **Stray server code in the CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...` PHP text. It is invalid CSS and can break parsing of the rules that follow in some clients.
- **Wrong meta tags for email:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` have no effect in most email clients. `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so the font falls back.
- **Outlook-incompatible CSS:** `*{max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` and the `table[class="wrapper"]` / `img[class="photo"]` attribute selectors are fragile in some clients.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,846 of 16,828 characters (70%) are URLs, mostly long tracking strings. That hurts readability and spam scoring.
