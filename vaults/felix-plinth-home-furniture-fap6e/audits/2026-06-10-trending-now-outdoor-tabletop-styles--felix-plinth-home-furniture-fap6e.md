# TRENDING NOW: outdoor tabletop styles 🍽️ ☀️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- Forty-four. That's the Wayfair count now, and I'm leading with it because I already opened an outdoor rugs email from Wayfair this morning — June 10 — and here they are again with a second outdoor send in the same day. Before I even get to the creative, that cadence is doing real damage to how I receive this one.
- Inside the email itself, the "what's your tabletop style?" quiz framing is honestly not bad for me as someone who actually does save these things to a Pinterest-adjacent folder and is trying to figure out whether the second house goes modern coastal or something warmer. Four style lanes — modern coastal, farmhouse charm, cool & classic, the new brights — with a small product grid under each. It's editorial, it's organized, and the hero image of a styled outdoor table is genuinely nice. But there is zero pricing anywhere in this email. Not a sale, not a percentage, not even a "from $X" anchor on the category grids. This is a pure inspiration send, and inspiration sends need to earn that position in my inbox — especially as #44.
- I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't click, wouldn't forward. If anything I'd pull up the Pottery Barn site myself, because at least they told me their bedding was on sale when they sent me an editorial email last week.

## What's working

- **The editorial format is right for the category.** Tabletop styles organized by aesthetic is how I actually shop — I need to know if I'm buying for the coastal second home or the cleaner primary before I pick a plate.
- **Four style lanes with visual separation** make it easy to scan without committing. I spent two seconds on "modern coastal" and skipped "farmhouse charm" instantly, which is exactly how I'd browse Wayfair anyway.
- **Hero image is well-styled and seasonal.** Outdoor table, warm light, the suggestion of a meal — this actually lands for June.
- **Clean render, no layout breakage.** After the broken images I've seen in some other sends this week, a clean two-column grid feels like a low bar but I'll take it.
- **"The new brights" section** with the colorful vase and plate caught my eye — I'm considering an accent palette for the back patio and that yellow-mustard hit registered.

## What's weak

- **Zero pricing, anywhere.** This is a style guide masquerading as a shop email. Article sent me an outdoor living piece on June 9 that had editorial framing *and* anchored prices on specific items — that's the move. This email gives me mood boards with no path to the cart.
- **Second outdoor send in one day.** I already got the 8×10 outdoor rugs email earlier this morning. Two outdoor emails in a single calendar day from one brand is a scheduling failure, full stop.
- **"TRENDING NOW" in all caps** in the subject line feels like a shout, not a curated recommendation. CB2 ran a warehouse sale with "WAREHOUSE SALE IS ON" and earned the caps. "TRENDING NOW" on tabletop styles is just noise.
- **"Find your style" as the hero CTA** is Pinterest-brained in a way that doesn't close the loop. I want to buy plates, not take a style quiz. The sub-section CTAs ("Shop Coastal," "Make it Homey") are better but buried under the hero.
- **No Wayfair Rewards tie-in to the actual offer.** The Rewards badge is down in the footer boilerplate. If I'm going to buy a $180 outdoor dinnerware set, tell me how many reward points I'd earn — that's the buy trigger for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a price anchor in the hero.** Even "outdoor tabletop from $24" changes the email from inspiration to intent. Pottery Barn put "now on sale" in their subject line and that's why I clicked through. This email has no equivalent hook.
- 2. **Separate the two outdoor sends.** Don't put outdoor rugs and outdoor tabletop in the same morning. One goes Tuesday, one goes Thursday.
- 3. **Swap the hero CTA from "find your style" to the highest-conversion category.** If "modern coastal" outperforms on click data, lead with "Shop Coastal" as the hero button and let the quiz live lower.
- 4. **Add reward points math to the section CTAs.** "Shop Coastal — earn 5% back in rewards" is one line and it converts me.
- 5. **Drop "TRENDING NOW" from the subject.** It's the loudest thing in my inbox and it says nothing.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Match your outdoor plates to the vibe. Styles starting at $24.`
