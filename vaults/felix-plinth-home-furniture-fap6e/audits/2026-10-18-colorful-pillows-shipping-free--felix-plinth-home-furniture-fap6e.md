# Colorful pillows, shipping free
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- Pillows are the first thing Pottery Barn has sent me this week that I can act on. I'm styling sofas in two houses, so "Made for Mixing" with a neutrals, blues and greens split works for me. It's the fifth Pottery Barn send in about three days, though, and the free shipping on pillows is the only offer I can find. "Pillows Ship Free" is a small button under the headline, so I nearly missed it. I'd say it's a decent, pretty email with a weak hook.
- The color-family tiles (neutrals, blues, greens) are the best part. That's how I actually shop when I'm trying to match a palette across rooms. Below that, the "Recommended Just For You" grid undercuts the pillow story. It shows a curtain rod, two dining tables, a platform bed and a rug, then drops into a Christmas Shop banner. Crate & Barrel did the same thing to me yesterday, with football gear in the middle of a kids' run. I'd probably click through to the blues, then save the email to my Pinterest folder. I wouldn't forward it, because it's a pillow email and nothing in it is urgent.

## What's working

- The "Made for Mixing" hero is a real styled stack of patterned pillows. It's the kind of image I save to my inspiration folder.
- Sorting by "shop neutrals / shop blues / shop greens" fits how I coordinate colors across two houses.
- "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" at the top is useful for a renovation timeline.
- The "Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" block speaks to what I'm doing. It's a showroom-style touch that Wayfair doesn't have.
- The 10% back in rewards card block is clearly laid out. I'm a rewards-card person, so I noticed it.

## What's weak

- "Pillows Ship Free" sits in a small outlined button. The offer is easy to miss, and there's no minimum or deadline.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has nothing to do with pillows. Curtain rods and dining tables read as filler.
- The Christmas Shop banner pulls focus away from the pillow story. It feels like a second email stapled on.
- The footer fine print is a dense wall of tiny text. I'd have to zoom to read the exclusions.
- The send is the fifth in three days (10/16 bedding, then bedroom collections twice, rugs, and now pillows). Wayfair's pergola run did this to me too, and this feels the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free shipping the headline. "Free shipping on pillows" should be in the hero text with a deadline, not just in a small button.
- 2. Replace the generic "Recommended" grid with pillow-adjacent items. Show throws and the sofas the pillows sit on, with swatch or sample info.
- 3. Pull the Christmas banner from this send. Give it its own email so each one has a single job.
- 4. Add one rewards line next to the offer, for example "Earn 10% back on these pillows with the Pottery Barn card."
- 5. Space out the cadence. A rug send, a bedding send and a pillow send should be spread across the week.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a sliced-image, table-based XHTML template and has no hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 4 warnings). It has widespread missing alt text on image slices, three non-HTTPS image sources, and email-address-bearing URLs in tracking pixels. The compliance headers and the click destinations couldn't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click redirects:** 58 links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified. The logo link is one example.
- **Plaintext email in pixel URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded as an `<img>`.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is also loaded as an `<img>`.
  - Both put the recipient address unhashed in a query string. The `svopn` one is also unencrypted `http://`, so the address is exposed in transit and in proxy logs.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv.../cat/1` is an ad-network pixel with no alt text.
- **Other tracking pixel:** `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/a0629ba0-...?mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3&mi_mid=...` is an open-tracking image.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a shared spacer hosted on a sister brand's domain. It is a minor dependency risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images have no alt text. They include:
  - the hero `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`
  - `slices_01`–`slices_16.jpg` under `F26_Decor_B6_PillowPairings/`
  - the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and others)
  - `slices_01`–`slices_18.jpg` under `Email-Footer/`

  The content is sliced into linked images, so with images off the email is blank and unreadable to screen readers.
- **Mixed content:** three images use `http://` and may be blocked: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
- **Empty title:** the document has `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Stray code in CSS:** the last `<style>` block ends with PHP text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...`). It is invalid CSS. It is probably harmless because it sits after the last rule, but it is a template leak.
- **Table roles:** some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`, such as the spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">` and the outer wrapper tables.
- **Plain-text part:** 11265 of 15481 characters (73%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. That comes from long tracked links and is a minor spam-filter risk.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation. Without both, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is absent. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay doesn't expose it. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com` are aligned.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both fall in the truncated portion, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Landing pages:** alignment with the "Colorful pillows" and free-shipping messaging can't be checked from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every image slice, and use `alt=""` on decorative spacers and pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` images to `https://`, or drop the legacy `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and 2022 `slices_02.jpg` assets.
3. Stop putting a raw email address in pixel URLs (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`). Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, fill in `<title>`, add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables, and delete the PHP `header()` text from the `<style>` block.
5. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also follow a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links to check destinations and UTM parameters.
