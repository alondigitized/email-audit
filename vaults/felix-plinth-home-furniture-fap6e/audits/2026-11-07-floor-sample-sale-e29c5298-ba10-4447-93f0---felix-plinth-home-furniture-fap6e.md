- "Floor Sample Sale" is a concrete, different angle from the 50% blasts. Floor-model deals are how I'd save real money.

- Button contrast is strong. The black "Find a Store" and "Learn More" buttons are easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- The floor sample hero has no offer. There's no percentage and no price, and the button points at a store locator, not at product.
- The Long Weekend Sale banner is stale. It's Nov 7 and this is the third send in a row with "Long Weekend" language. My 11/06 "FIVE-STAR PICKS" review flagged the same mismatch.
- "Long Weekend Sale" and "Double Rewards" both fight the floor sample hero for attention. I can't tell which offer is the main one.
- The sideboard has no name or price and no link to it. I can't tell if it's a floor sample or just a lifestyle shot.
- The legal block is a wall of tiny type. It has stacked exclusions that make me doubt the "up to 50%" claim.
- The same offers keep coming back. "Double rewards" and the sale banner have appeared in several sends this week.

- 2. Cut the Long Weekend banner and the Double Rewards block from this send. A floor-sample email should only be about floor samples.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly sound, but the automated checks flag missing alt text on content images and 11 pixels loaded over plain `http://`. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The source was truncated and 54 tracking links were not probed, so the footer and link destinations are unchecked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 54 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations, status codes or redirect chains were validated. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 11 pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1`
- **Likely blocked:** Non-HTTPS images are commonly blocked or rewritten by Gmail and Apple Mail proxies, and can trigger mixed-content warnings in webmail. The Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-sync pixels would then silently fail.
- **Pixel volume:** There are 10 sequential `pub*.php` cookie-redirect pixels. That is a lot of third-party requests for one email, and a possible spam-filter and privacy concern.
- **Other tracking images (HTTPS, fine):** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/d2553681-...?mi_cid=...`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (3):**
  - `3f069f22-9591-4c0b-ad71-1ac928423461.gif`
  - `5fedf75e-1e0b-4750-8463-93155769fdcd.jpg`
  - `345f2993-963b-4783-9afc-927a05cfc0e8.jpg`
  - All three are served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, these render as empty or as the filename. These are the QA warnings that matter most.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (about 13):** The Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel have no alt text. These should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech (`width="1" height="1"`, `aria-hidden`). Low severity, but it adds noise to the accessibility report.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head (a typo, and unfilled). It's harmless to rendering but shows incomplete template cleanup.
- **Font loading:** `@font-face` for HNW05 points at `crateandbarrel.com` and the others at `cb2.com`. Most email clients ignore web fonts, so fallback stacks matter. The `a` rule falls back to `Helvetica, sans-serif`, which is acceptable.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width: 418px`, so phones wider than that (many current devices are 390–430px) may miss some of the responsive classes (`.mobile`, `.hide`, `.block`). I couldn't confirm the effect on the full body because the source was truncated.

No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%first_name%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FNAME]` appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=` hashed identifier and `mi_cid` on the Movable Ink pixels are populated. The truncated source limits this check to the head and style block.

Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. The From domain is `mail.cb2.com`, a subdomain, so verify alignment on the raw message.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 54 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated HTML doesn't show landing-page URLs or UTM parameters. Click-redirect wrapping hides the final destination. Nothing confirms a mismatch.

1. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the three content images listed above. This is the highest-value fix.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`, `https://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/...`), or drop the ten `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels if they aren't needed. Give any remaining pixels `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag to allow zoom.
4. **Check the raw message headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com`. Do this outside the relay, since the relay probably stripped them.
