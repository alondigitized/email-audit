# A little midcentury. So now.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- "Instant Character" and that velvet Belmond sectional in the warm, rust-toned living room actually got a half-second of my attention — it's a genuinely nice hero shot, moody lighting, looks like a room I'd screenshot for the second house's living area. But then this thing just keeps going. Berkeley Sectional, Eastman Sofa, Oriole Sectional, a rugs section, a sweepstakes, a review-request ask, an app download pitch, a credit card pitch — by the time I hit "MORE FOR YOU" I've forgotten what the actual email was about. This is CB2 treating my inbox like a full catalog dump instead of a single decision.
- No price on the hero piece (Belmond), no price on Berkeley, Eastman, or Oriole either — I only get numbers way down at the bottom on random accent pieces like the $1,199 bed and $899 dining table. For someone actually furnishing two houses right now, that's backwards. I want to know if the sectional I'm looking at is $3K or $8K before I click anything.
- Compared to yesterday's Onyx & Walnut send (7/10, which I gave credit for restraint and a genuinely fresh collection name), this one's a step back toward the kitchen-sink format that's been plaguing my inbox all week — this is the sixth CB2/Crate & Barrel-family email since Monday. Not clicking, not forwarding. Marking as read and moving on, same as the Floor Sample Sale and End of Summer sends.

## What's working

- The Belmond hero shot has real atmosphere — warm rust palette, midcentury lines, looks like an actual styled room, not a product cutout.
- "Customizable in 60+ fabrics and multiple configurations" is a real, specific detail — that's the kind of spec I actually care about for a sectional.
- The three-sofa "More Now and Future Classics" block at least groups by category cleanly, so if I were shopping sectionals specifically it's not hard to scan.
- Loyalty content is present near the bottom (CB2 Rewards, 10% back) — acknowledges I might already be a member instead of ignoring it entirely.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing on every single hero product (Belmond, Berkeley, Eastman, Oriole) — I have to scroll to the very bottom before any dollar figure shows up.
- This email tries to be five emails at once: new arrivals, sofas, rugs, a sweepstakes, a review ask, an app pitch, and a credit card offer. Wayfair's outdoor privacy screen email (5/10) at least stayed roughly on-theme; this one sprawls way past that.
- "MORE NOW AND FUTURE CLASSICS" is vague filler copy — doesn't tell me anything about what's actually new versus what's just been sitting in the catalog.
- The sweepstakes block ("YOU CAN WIN IT") is visually disconnected from everything around it — different tone, breaks the flow, feels bolted on.
- Sixth CB2-family email this week in my inbox. I'm fatigued on this brand specifically right now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price under every hero product shown above the fold — Belmond, Berkeley, Eastman, Oriole all need a number. Pottery Barn's towel email this week scored better than most partly because it led with "$24.50" — a real number changes the whole read.
- 2. Cut this down to one collection per send. Pick Belmond, give it the full email — swatch options, configuration diagram, starting price — and hold Berkeley/Eastman/Oriole/rugs/sweepstakes for separate sends.
- 3. Move the CB2 Rewards / 10% back callout up near the top if I'm a known member — right now it's buried after the app pitch and credit card ask, which feels backwards for someone you're trying to retain.
- 4. Drop or relocate the sweepstakes block — it doesn't belong wedged between rugs and "more for you," it just adds noise.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (opaque ESP redirect domains) — not independently verified as functional; recommend manual click-through QA before send.
- 10 sequential Dotomi pixel calls load over **plain HTTP**: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`.
