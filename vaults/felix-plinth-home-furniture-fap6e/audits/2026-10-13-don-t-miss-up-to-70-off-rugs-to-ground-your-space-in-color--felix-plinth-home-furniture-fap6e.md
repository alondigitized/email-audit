# Don't miss UP TO 70% OFF + Rugs to ground your space in color
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the Pottery Barn send I've seen nine times this week, wearing a rug costume. The dark-green "Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" strip has been on top since 10/06. This time a "Grounded in Color" rug story sits right under it. I like that, because I'm doing two houses and the rugs in the primary are still bare floor. The email is long, but it's organized by color family. Pottery Barn's own "RSVP for FREE design services" send earlier today was more useful.
- The subject is working against it. "Don't miss" is the same line as the 10/12 "Don't miss The Fall Sale" send. The rugs only show up after the "+", so it reads like two emails stapled together. I'd open it because I'm shopping rugs, not because the subject earned it.
- Would I click? Yes, into the Classic Blues or Shades of Green tiles. Would I forward it? Maybe to my partner, since we're arguing about the primary living room. I'd skip the 70% banner, because "up to" never means rugs.

## What's working

- Sorting by color family (Classic Blues, Shades of Green, Warm Neutrals) matches how I actually pick a rug. I start with the palette, not the SKU.
- The big "Shop Rugs" button sits right under the "Grounded in Color" hero, so there's no hunting for it.
- The room-scene photos show scale and style. I can picture the rug under a coffee table.
- "Make It Yours" for custom sizes, plus "Order Free Swatches" and the rug buying guide, are the swatch-and-sample help I want before spending four figures.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" card block is there for a rewards-stacker like me.

## What's weak

- The "Up to 70% off" banner is vague. I can't tell if any of these rugs are actually discounted. No price or percentage appears on any rug tile.
- The email is very long, and the Christmas Shop, App, Affirm and "Discover New Arrivals" blocks pile onto the end. It feels like three emails in one.
- The rug names are tiny captions (Gabriele, Pajaro, Tyson, Merrin, Zoila, Violetta). I couldn't read them without zooming, and there are no prices or sizes.
- There's no deadline. "Don't miss" implies urgency, but I never see an end date.
- Fall Sale and Christmas Shop in the same email muddle the season, and the Christmas section pulls me away from the rugs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real rug price in the hero, for example "Rugs from $X, up to 30% off through Sunday". That would do more than anything else here. One honest number beats "up to 70%".
- 2. Make the subject about rugs only. Drop the stale sale line.
- 3. Add price and size to each rug tile, and a "Free swatches" link on every color row.
- 4. Cut the Christmas and app blocks from this send and keep it to rugs plus rewards.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Blue, green or neutral? Rugs by color`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rugs up to 30% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order free swatches before you commit to a size.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hand-knotted wool, custom sizes, and 10% back in Rewards.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based, with a hidden preheader and tracked links. Its main technical problems are missing alt text across about 55 images, three plain-HTTP image sources (one carrying the recipient's email address in the URL), and compliance headers that couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 60 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations are unverified. Example: the logo link `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwMjgs…`.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten, which would undercount opens.
- **Other pixels:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` (third-party ad pixel) and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/812a3ed4-…?mi_cid=…` (open pixel) have no alt attribute. They should use `alt=""`.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer hosted on the West Elm domain. It has no alt and adds a dependency on another brand's host.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,653 of 15,897 characters (73%) are URLs, which trips the >70% warning. The long tracking URLs inflate this.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 images have no alt attribute. These include:
  - Banners: `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, `F26_Bottom_NewArrivals_V1.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`.
  - Sliced assets: `W26_Rugs_B3_RugsColorStory/slices_01–18` and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18`.
  - Product images, e.g. `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg` and `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`.
  - Images-off clients will show blank slices, and screen readers will read filenames or nothing.
- **Insecure image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` may be blocked as mixed content.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Missing language:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Layout table without role:** the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` containing `<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"`, unlike the other layout tables.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the last `<style>` block ends with pasted PHP, `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …")`. It is the last thing in the block, so it is probably harmless, but it is malformed CSS.
- **Web font:** `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat` is a render-blocking external stylesheet that many email clients strip.
- **Responsive rules:** the `img {max-width:650px}` and `max-width:100%!important` rules are present and parse normally.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** appear in the visible source (no `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]`).
- **Recipient email in plaintext URLs:** `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in two image URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`, an `<img>` with no alt.
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=…`, which is plain HTTP.
- The HTTP one exposes the address in transit and in proxy logs. Both leak it to image-proxy and referrer paths.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com`, and alignment can't be checked without the headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.
- **Referrer meta:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is present. It does not affect compliance.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not verifiable:** every CTA goes through an opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` token, and 60 of them were not probed. UTM parameters and landing pages could not be confirmed.
- **Direct product images:** product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` (e.g. `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`), so those assets exist on-site.
- **Likely stale assets:** the rugs module path is `/pb/2025/US/W26_Rugs_B3_RugsColorStory/`, while other modules use `/2026/`. Confirm those assets are current.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels (`spacer_0.gif`, `d.turn.com`, `clickcp`, `svopn`, `coreemail.html`).
2. Move the three `http://` images to HTTPS (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, the `svopn` pixel).
3. Stop putting the raw recipient email in image URLs. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Fill in `<title>` and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Delete the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the spacer table.
7. Re-run with full raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Resolve the redirect chain on a sample of the 60 tracked links to check UTMs and landing pages.
