# AQUARIUMS & BOWLS ⭕ Hurry – UP TO 70% OFF IS ENDING ⭕ 
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- So the subject line is "AQUARIUMS & BOWLS ⭕ Hurry – UP TO 70% OFF IS ENDING ⭕" and I scroll through this entire email — bathroom scale, a step ladder, a dog getting bathed in a utility sink, dining chairs, a vanity, bedroom lighting, a hanging egg chair, kitchen mats, rugs — and there is not one single aquarium or fish bowl anywhere. Not one. This is the same trick Wayfair pulled on me with "RANGE HOODS" last night and got zero range hoods, and it's the same trick as the "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" send that turned into a pergola-and-headboard grab bag. At this point I'm convinced Wayfair just writes a random product category in the subject line and fills the body with whatever's in the flash-deal bin that day.
- The "picked just for you" section is laughably generic for someone renovating two houses right now — a bathroom scale and a step ladder are not "picked for me," they're picked for literally everyone on the list. The one thing that did catch my eye was the dining chairs (I've actually got a set on my list) and the vanity, but I had to dig for them.
- Would I click? Maybe on the dining chairs since I recognize that exact silhouette from a Pottery Barn email two days ago — but the subject-body mismatch means I'm opening this with zero trust, and that kills momentum before I even scroll. Would I forward it? No, unless it's to a friend as a "look how bad this is" joke. Marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" purple banner is genuinely the clearest urgency element in the whole email — bold, high-contrast, easy to spot.
- The dining chairs and the mirrored vanity are actual furniture I'd consider, and the strikethrough pricing (Was $264.99 Now $183.99) is legible and does the job.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" is a real, honest, useful line for someone ordering furniture — that's the kind of shipping clarity I actually want to see and other brands often bury it.
- Wayfair Rewards / Pay Over Time / Wayfair Professional icon row at the bottom is a nice touch for a repeat furniture shopper like me — Crate & Barrel doesn't surface a "Pro" tier like this.

## What's weak

- Zero aquariums or bowls anywhere in the email. This is now the third Wayfair send this week where the subject line has nothing to do with the contents (range hoods, furniture cushions, now aquariums) — I'm starting to auto-discount their subject lines entirely.
- "Picked just for you" is not personalized at all — a bathroom scale and a fold-out ladder next to living room furniture tells me this is a random SKU pull, not anything based on my browsing.
- The layout is a wall of disconnected categories — rugs, mattresses, bedding, flash deals, doorbusters, "your home these picks" — with no narrative or hierarchy pulling my eye anywhere specific. Compare to Article's "Up to 40% off ends Monday" send, which had one clean maroon deadline strip up top; this just keeps throwing more blue-and-purple banners at me until I give up scrolling.
- The egg chair and dog-bathing photo feel randomly slotted in with no category label tying them together — I can't tell if I'm looking at outdoor furniture or pet supplies.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject-to-content mismatch first — this is the single biggest problem across every recent Wayfair send I've reviewed. If the hero deals are furniture and rugs, say that in the subject, not a random category that doesn't appear once.
- 2. Actually personalize "picked just for you" — if you know I've looked at dining chairs and vanities, lead with those, not a bathroom scale.
- 3. Cut the number of banner sections in half — rugs/outdoor/mattresses/bedding pricing bar, then flash deals, then doorbusters, then shop all sales is banner fatigue; consolidate into one clear offer ladder.
- 4. Give the flash deals section a visible countdown timer instead of just "24 HRS ONLY" text — CB2's countdown-clock banner at least creates real urgency I can act on.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The chairs and vanity you've been eyeing, up to 70% off through tonight only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Flash deals refresh in 24 hours — grab dining and bedroom furniture before they're gone.`
