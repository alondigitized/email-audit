# TODAY ONLY: 20% off lighting!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this one is mine. It's main-brand Crate & Barrel, not the Kids sender I've been snoozing (the Doona and nursery-glider emails earlier today). Lighting is on both of my punch lists, so the subject gets a real look. Then I opened it and the hero says "now in our app: 20% off lighting, today only!" and the button is "Download Our App." So the sale I was promised sits behind an install. That's a bait-and-switch feeling on a "TODAY ONLY" send.
- Under the hero, the email works against itself. A Thanksgiving dining event at 30% off sits on top of the hero. Below it is a mantel with stockings, which isn't lighting. Then rugs and accent chairs at 20%, a lamp grid, a credit card pitch, a second app pitch and a design appointment. That's six asks in one scroll. The lamp grid is the useful part, but it comes after all of that.
- Would I click? Probably one lamp, maybe the Ilona chandelier or the Lia pendant, and only to save it to my Pinterest-style folder. I wouldn't forward it. It's also the 11th C&B-family email in two days, so my snooze reflex is strong.

## What's working

- "Today only: 20% off lighting" is a real, specific offer in my category, and it's the only C&B send this week about the house itself.
- The lamp grid names the products with sizes (Weave floor lamp 47", Lia pendant 30", Ilona chandelier 35") and shows struck-through prices next to sale prices. I can shortlist from that without clicking.
- The Reward Dollars block with the Crate Card is the right pitch for someone who optimizes card rewards. I'd actually do that math.
- TheDesignDesk "100% free" is a good renovation-friendly hook, and it sits right where I'd want it, near the bottom.
- The layout renders clean. Images load, nothing overlaps, and the footer nav is tidy.

## What's weak

- The hero's only button is "Download Our App," not "Shop lighting." The offer shows up as in-app, but the subject never says so.
- The top banner pushes a Thanksgiving dining event at "up to 30% off." That competes with the lighting offer before I've read it.
- The "5 holiday-ready styles" tile uses a fireplace mantel and stockings. I can't tell what's for sale, and it isn't lighting.
- The fine print is a wall of exclusions: "select styles," "cannot be combined," outlet carve-outs, and three different promos. That makes the 20% feel less honest.
- Everything is the same size, so nothing leads. The product text and prices are tiny on a phone.
- Cadence is bad. The 10/22 clearance, glider and Doona sends already used up my patience.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero button "Shop 20% Off Lighting" and link it to a lighting page that works on web. Keep the app as a secondary option, or say "in-app" in the subject.
- 2. Cut the page to one offer. Drop the Thanksgiving banner and the rugs and chairs block, and let lighting own the email.
- 3. Replace the mantel tile with a real lighting scene, such as a pendant over a dining table. Tag the product so I can tap straight to it.
- 4. Lead with the lamp grid and add a "20% off" badge on each item. Put the final price in bold, since that's what I'm comparing.
- 5. Move the Reward Dollars line closer to the grid. Show the actual math, like "20% off plus 10% back."
- 6. Send less often, or send each persona the right sender. I don't need the Kids sender at all.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off pendants & chandeliers, today (in-app)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your lighting picks, 20% off until midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Floor lamps, pendants and chandeliers. Stack it with 10% back in Reward Dollars.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Designer-worthy lighting for the holidays. App-only price ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The automated QA run shows no hard errors, but 20 images lack alt text, and the header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, the unsubscribe link and the link-level UTM parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified.** The QA run skipped 52 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixel repetition.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` fire the same pixel five times, differing only in `n`. This adds 5 external requests to a third-party domain.
- **Open and tracking pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/df1f12ff-a554-419d-9d7d-97dc20de5ab0?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are 1x1-style trackers. They are expected, but they stop working if images are blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 20 images.** In QA's order:
  - Six content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`:
    - `bbf6f9d4-….png`
    - `56be7a63-….jpg`
    - `18887a19-….jpg`
    - `91daf28b-….gif`
    - `5c7b78d8-….gif`
    - `971aa10f-….jpg`
  - Eight `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, such as `560d850e7e517ede.png`. Their purpose is unclear from the data given. If they are visible product or recommendation images, they need alt text.
  - Six tracking pixels (the five `rlcdn` pixels, `o.gif` and `dv…/df1f12ff-…`). These should carry `alt=""` and are low-impact.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks user zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }`, `body { … -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;…}` stop iOS text scaling.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses a raw `&` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`. The title is also generic and does not match the subject line.
- **Template leftovers and redundancy.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the source.
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` plus three separate tags).
  - `.ReadMsgBody` is defined twice.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
  - These are harmless but add bloat.
- **Positive.** `lang="en"` is set, the MSO/VML namespaces and Outlook fallbacks are present, and there is a 460px responsive breakpoint.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The rest of the source was truncated, so this is not fully verified.
- The `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` recipient identifier is resolved and appended to image URLs. The `rlcdn` pixel `s=` parameter shares the same prefix. If this encodes a hashed email or user ID, it is passed to a third party (`sr.rlcdn.com`). I can't confirm what it encodes from this data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** QA says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers (RFC 8058) for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and again this is likely a relay limitation.
- **Visible unsubscribe link and postal address.** These could not be checked because the footer was cut off in the truncated source.
- **From address.** `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is on a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with proper alignment. Alignment was not tested.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment **could not be verified**. All 52 links are click-tracking redirects that QA skipped, and the visible source doesn't show final destinations. No continuity defect is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images and to the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images if they are visible.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv…`).
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `html`, `body` and `*`.
4. Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it descriptive.
5. Collapse the five `rlcdn` pixel calls into one if the extra calls aren't required, and review what `s=` carries.
6. Clean up the duplicate `format-detection`, `.ReadMsgBody` and charset declarations and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` (or untruncated HTML) to verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, the `Authentication-Results` headers, the footer unsubscribe link and address, and the UTM parameters after redirect resolution.
