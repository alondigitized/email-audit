## What's weak

- It's the same layout, same hero language, same category order as the 09-04 email I already reviewed — Article at least changes up its "ends Monday" banner treatment week to week; this brand just recompiles the same template.
- Crate&Kids sneaks back in halfway down anyway, so even the "not for me" complaint from my last three reviews still applies, just delayed instead of avoided.

- "up to 60%" and "up to 40%" repeated across five different modules with no differentiation in visual weight — my eye doesn't land anywhere specific, it just scrolls.

- 2. Cut the module count in half. Nobody needs sofas, furniture, decor/bedding, and rugs as four separate blocks with near-identical copy — collapse into two hero categories per send.
- 3. Pull the Crate&Kids module out entirely and route it to parents on a separate list — I don't need "final weekend" pressure applied to a product line I've told this brand three times this week I don't want.
- 4. Put a real countdown or hard deadline near the top — "final weekend" appears in the subject but nothing in the visible hero reinforces urgency with a date.
