## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **93 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so link validity and destination status are unverified.
- **Third-party identity pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times. That is the rlcdn.com domain, which I believe belongs to LiveRamp. The source doesn't say what is hashed, but the shared value suggests one subscriber identifier is passed to a third party.
- **Repeated first-party pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times, alongside `…/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5a499a0d-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Duplicate fires may inflate open or impression counts.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (QA-confirmed):** about 35 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif` have no alt. Examples are `ec997ddd-48db-4d16-bffd-48b5b834d0f5.jpg` and `4f4a73dd-3948-4972-a45b-5c58c53bbc44.jpg`. Others are `s7d5.scene7.com/.../CK%5FH26%5FKidsBedding%5F4x5%5FPrimary.gif` and `bbf6f9d4-…png` on the `/m/47/` path. If these carry product or offer content, the email is unreadable with images off or with a screen reader.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `448796.gif` (×5), `f3c0d22992a01946.png` (×6), `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel. Add `alt=""` explicitly to the 1×1 pixels. The QA tool may not distinguish a missing attribute from an empty one, so check the source.
- **Spacer image:** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` has no alt. It should be `alt=""`.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text-size lock:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable user text scaling on iOS.
- **Template debris:**
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags appear three times.
  - `charset` is declared twice, in an `http-equiv` meta and a `<meta charset>`.
  - A leftover placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the head.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and doesn't match the Kids sender or the subject.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both have equal specificity, so the later rule wins at 768px and above and the 600px cap never applies.
