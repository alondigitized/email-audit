# Up to 40% off KitchenAid, Made In & more top kitchen brands!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the fifth Crate-family email in my inbox this week, but at least this one's got a different angle — it's not furniture, it's a straight-up kitchen appliance dump. Teal KitchenAid stand mixer front and center, then a scroll-forever grid of Vitamix blenders, All-Clad cookware, Le Creuset-style bakeware. My wife handles most of the kitchen registry stuff but I do the coffee and grill gear, so the "$50 off Vitamix" and the All-Clad cookware set actually made me pause for half a second.
- Where it loses me: by the time I'm four screens deep, it's pivoted into furniture and rugs ("Up to 60% off The Labor Day Day Event," "bedding & more") which has nothing to do with the subject line I clicked on. That's the exact same bait-and-switch problem I flagged on the 2026-08-30 Crate & Barrel furniture send — brand can't keep its lanes straight. And this is now the second time this week I've gotten a "$50 off Vitamix blenders" pitch, which feels like the same block getting recycled across sends.
- Verdict: I'd skim the top third for the mixer/cookware deal, maybe click through on the All-Clad set since I've been eyeing new pots, but I'm not scrolling to the furniture section and I'm definitely not forwarding this — nothing here is gift-worthy or renovation-relevant enough to send to a friend.

## What's working

- Real KitchenAid stand mixer as the hero image, matches the subject line exactly — no bait-and-switch at the top, unlike some Crate & Kids sends I've gotten this week.
- Clean stacked layout: mixer → cookware → blender → All-Clad set, each with its own "Shop Now" button so I don't have to hunt for the CTA.
- The All-Clad "$100 off curated ceramic nonstick 10-piece set" is a specific, real number I can do rewards math on.
- Double Rewards banner up top is consistent with every other Crate & Barrel send I've seen this week, so at least the loyalty messaging isn't inconsistent.

## What's weak

- Email drifts hard off-subject into "Labor Day Event," furniture, rugs, and outdoor sections — this is supposed to be a kitchen brands email and it's maybe 30% kitchen by the time I've scrolled through it.
- "$50 off Vitamix blenders" is a near-copy of a deal block I've already seen from this brand this week — feels recycled, not fresh.
- Way too long — by comparison, Article's Labor Day send was tight and stayed on-theme; this one just keeps adding sections until it becomes a catalog dump.
- No mention of ship times or delivery windows for the big-ticket stuff (stand mixer, cookware sets) — as someone juggling two home renovations, appliance lead time actually matters to my buying decision and it's just not there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email at the kitchen section — everything below "more can't-miss kitchen deals + they ship free" should be its own send. Pottery Barn's Labor Day email scored well with me specifically because it stayed on one clear offer instead of sprawling.
- 2. Lead with the All-Clad or KitchenAid deal as ONE hero offer with a big percentage/dollar number, not five different sub-deals competing for attention.
- 3. Add a one-line shipping/delivery note near the big appliances — "ships in 3-5 days" would move me from browsing to clicking.
- 4. Drop the repeated Vitamix block or bump the discount — reusing the same $50-off pitch two sends in a row reads as lazy, not urgent.
