# Sofas, sectionals and more up to 20% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Crate & Barrel email aimed at what I'm actually doing. I'm renovating two houses, and this one leads with sofas and sectionals at up to 20% off, plus free fabric swatches and a free design appointment. It's the first one this week that wasn't a nursery or a bunk bed. That said, this is at least the fifteenth or sixteenth C&B-family send in about two days, and it comes right after "The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event" went out twice today. My first reaction was "you again," softened a little because the content is relevant.
- The email is long, and I'd say it's too long. The top is strong: a big living room hero, "it's on: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals," and a Shop Now button. Then it keeps going through rugs, lighting, decor, mirrors, swatches, performance fabrics, The Design Desk, dining, a holiday section, a product grid, Reward Dollars, an app ad and a footer. Some of the text sits over photos and is hard to read, especially the Design Desk block and the "the holidays happen here" block.
- I'd open it and click, probably "Order Free Swatches" or the sectionals. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder only if the sectional pricing holds up. Pottery Barn Design Services did the free design help angle more cleanly earlier this week.

## What's working

- The hero is a big living room with a sofa and an upholstery offer, "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals." That matches my renovation, and the offer is easy to see.
- The "10 free swatches" block is what I actually want. Swatches are how I check color consistency across two houses before I spend real money.
- The "red wine? No problem" performance fabric section answers a real furniture-buying concern with a stain photo.
- TheDesignDesk gives me a free design appointment, which fits someone renovating two homes.
- The "Reward Dollars" block mentions 10% back, which matters to me because I shop around rewards math.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. Rugs, lighting, decor, mirrors, dining, holiday and a product grid all dilute a clean sofa sale.
- White text over busy photos in the Design Desk and holiday blocks is hard to read on a phone.
- "Select styles" and "up to 20%" are vague. Up to 20% is also a modest offer. CB2 is running up to 25% off upholstery right now.
- The product grid at the bottom has tiny text and prices I can't read without zooming.
- Cadence is the real problem. This is the fifteenth or sixteenth C&B-family send in two days, and it makes me want to unsubscribe from some of them.
- There's nothing about furniture shipping cost or lead times, which is the first thing I check on a sofa.
- The app download block reads like filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to three modules: sofa hero, swatches, Design Desk. Move the rugs, decor and dining to a separate send. That would lift this more than anything else.
- 2. Put the sale end date and shipping or lead time near the hero. I need to know whether I'm waiting 12 weeks for a sectional.
- 3. Use dark text on solid color blocks instead of text on photos.
- 4. Say "up to 20% off" once and name a specific sectional with a price.
- 5. Coordinate cadence across the C&B, Kids and SALE sender names. Don't send me three in a day.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Sofas, sectionals and more up to 20% off"

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a Movable Ink-built XHTML table layout with working responsive CSS. QA found about 33 `<img>` elements without alt text, including several tracking pixels. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link targets could not be inspected, and the header checks are inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination health and redirect chains are unverified, not broken.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1..5` pixels (LiveRamp) are in the body.
  - The `s=` value is a 64-hex string, SHA-256 length.
  - It is identical to the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images, so one per-recipient identifier goes to a third-party domain.
  - I can't confirm from the source whether it is an email hash.
  - Five identical pixels with only `n` varying look redundant.
- **Pixel alt text:** `o.gif` (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…`) and the `rlcdn` GIFs have no alt attribute. Decorative or tracking images should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 33 images are flagged, including:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (for example `def83198-…jpg`, `2a0bb8a6-…jpg`).
  - Scene7 hero and banner images (`2026_0923_CB_NewSofas_Sectionals_…`, `2026_0926_CB_HolidayAwareness_Anthem_…`).
  - Dynamic Movable Ink images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (seven flagged).
  - If the images are blocked, screen readers and image-off clients get nothing for them.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from `alt=""`, so some decorative hits may be intentional.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom on some mobile clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` also prevent text resizing, and they conflict with the later `body{…:100%}` rule.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate and leftover markup:**
  - `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - A template comment is still present: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Conflicting image sizing:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider, images cap at 640px, not 600px.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Clients between about 461px and 640px get no responsive rules. This is a risk inferred from the CSS, not confirmed by rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The personalized or dynamic blocks (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`) are image-rendered at open time. Their content can't be verified statically, and they have no alt fallback if they fail to load.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. It is not evidence that the sender omitted them.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is again the likely cause.
- **Body footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are beyond the truncation point and could not be checked.
- **Sender:** From `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a branded subdomain, consistent with the `image.mail.` and `mail.` hosts used in the body.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. The destination URLs sit behind tracking redirects that were not resolved, and the truncated source shows no UTM parameters. Resolving a sample of the 66 links would show the landing pages and campaign tagging.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Capture headers:** Pull the raw `.eml` straight from the sender (not via the relay) to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
2. **Alt text:** Add descriptive alt text to the content and Scene7 images, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels such as `o.gif` and the `rlcdn` GIFs. Add a fallback alt to the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` dynamic images.
3. **Viewport and text size:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
4. **Markup cleanup:** Escape the `&` in `<title>` and remove the duplicate meta tags and the leftover template comment.
5. **Image sizing:** Reconcile the two `img max-width` media queries.
6. **Link checks:** Resolve the redirect links in a full-source pass to confirm destinations, UTMs and the footer unsubscribe link.
7. **Pixel review:** Check what identifier the `rlcdn` pixels carry and whether five of them are needed.
