This version just has a better subject line. "Hosting Thanksgiving?" speaks to what I'm planning, which beats a generic "Black Friday deals first" text-signup pitch. ## What's working

- The subject and hero line up. "Hosting Thanksgiving?" followed by a 30% off dining headline gives me one clear reason to open it.
- The hero price callout (Sale $1,693, Reg. $2,499 on the Roland Walnut Dining Table) shows me real numbers. That's more than a bare percentage.
- The four category tiles (tables, seating, storage, lighting) let me jump straight to the piece I'm hunting for.
- The warm, moody photography fits the Pinterest-folder aesthetic I save these emails for.
- The "we love these for you" grid has prices and strikethroughs, so I can price-check against Pottery Barn and CB2 without leaving the email.

## What's weak

- The mirror banner ("only in our app: 20% off mirrors, today only") sits above the dining hero and splits my attention. It's also the same mirrors deal from this morning's send.
- Two Design Desk blocks and a $5,000 sweepstakes drag the email on. I'm not booking an appointment to win a gift card.
- Several CTAs say the same thing: "Shop The Dining Furniture Event" and "Shop All Dining Furniture" sit side by side. A "Shop Thanksgiving" button on the hosting guide adds to the pile.
- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel send today, counting the mirrors, kids' chair, text-signup and the earlier Thanksgiving dining email. The 30% off dining event I saw in the earlier send is back with a new subject. That's cadence fatigue.
- The legal fine print is tiny, and I can't read the offer exclusions without zooming. Dining furniture often has exclusions, and I want those up front.
- The category tile text overlays the images. The "Thanksgiving Hosting Guide" overlay text is hard to read against a busy photo.
- No delivery lead-time or swatch/sample info is visible. For a big table I need to know if it arrives before the holiday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half. Lead with the dining hero and the category tiles, then the product grid. Drop the second Design Desk block and the app banner, or make the app banner relevant to dining.
- 2. Add a delivery promise near the hero, like "Order by Nov 8 for Thanksgiving delivery". That answers my biggest question about buying furniture this late.
- 3. Merge the duplicate dining buttons into one primary CTA and make it specific: "Shop dining tables".
- 4. Show the Rewards math. Say what 10% back means on a $1,693 table, since I buy with the highest-rewards card.
- 5. Suppress sends from the same brand within a day, or consolidate them into one.

- **Third-party pixels:** 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp pixels. They carry a long `s=` hash, which is likely a per-recipient identifier. They are also loaded from a domain separate from the sender's own `mi.crateandbarrel.com` tracking.

- **Open tracking:** `o.gif` is a standard open-tracking pixel and has no alt attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 27 images are flagged.
  - 12 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, for example `bbf6f9d4-….png` and `cdd300d7-….jpg`.
