# A dining room worth gathering in—no matter the size
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Crate & Barrel send in days that matches what I'm doing. I'm outfitting two dining rooms, so I'd open it. The screenshot is tiny, so I'm going on structure and the headlines I can make out. The banner says "the Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off." Under it, "dining furniture that fits your life" leads into a stack of tables: Lakin, Edendale banquette, Dakota, Basque, Terra. Each gets its own hero and its own "Shop" button. That's a real catalog, and it's the kind of thing I'd save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder.
- It's a long scroll, though, and the offer gets buried. Up to 30% off is only a banner, and none of the product blocks show a price. Then it wanders into a Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes, Affirm financing, an app promo and a Kids/Sound cross-sell. I'd save it. I'd click once, probably the extendable Lakin table, since extendable matters in a second home. I'd forward it to my partner and nobody else.
- Pottery Barn's "Just in! New arrivals for the holidays" had no offer at all, so this beats it on clarity. Wayfair's pergola emails were a wall of near-identical photos. This one at least has a different room in each block. It's still the same family-of-brands pile-on I've been logging all week. Yesterday's nightstand email did the focused job better.

## What's working

- The Thanksgiving dining room event banner with up to 30% off sits at the very top, so the offer and the season are clear right away.
- Each table gets its own hero, a one-line benefit ("extendable," "solid oak," "built-to-last") and a "Shop [name] Table" button. Nothing is a generic "Discover."
- The "no matter the size" angle and the extendable and small-space callouts speak to someone furnishing a smaller second-home dining room.
- The lifestyle photography is warm and styled. It's the same moodboard material that Article's Melo email had, and I'd save it.
- Supporting categories (rugs, lighting, sideboards, bar carts) are a useful "complete the room" block. That's how I actually shop.

## What's weak

- No prices appear on any of the five hero tables. "Up to 30% off" doesn't tell me whether the Lakin is $1,200 or $3,500.
- The email is far too long. After the tables come the sweepstakes, Affirm, the app, the Kids/Sound block and the footer, and every one of them dilutes the dining pitch.
- Five heroes with five identical black buttons give me no clear primary action. Where do I go first?
- There's no shipping or delivery timing anywhere I can see. For furniture before Thanksgiving, that's the thing I need to know.
- There are no swatch or sample cues, no color-palette guidance and no rewards or member math. The $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes feels like a different email glued on.
- This is another Crate & Barrel family email in a week of them. Same-week repetition with CB2 and the Kids sender wears me out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the three best sellers and put the price, with the strike-through, on each. Move the sweepstakes, Affirm and app promo into one slim footer strip.
- 2. Add a delivery-by-Thanksgiving line near the top, such as "order by 11/5 for delivery before the holiday." That would do more for me than any beauty shot.
- 3. Add a fabric or finish swatch link (free swatches) under the upholstered pieces, like the banquette and chairs.
- 4. Collapse the five "Shop" buttons into one clear primary CTA plus small text links.
- 5. Coordinate sends across C&B, CB2 and Kids so I don't get three in a day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extendable dining tables, up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Thanksgiving-ready dining tables, delivered in time`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lakin, Dakota and Basque, with delivery dates. Event ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Small room or big one, here are 5 tables that expand when you host.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with Movable Ink dynamic content and third-party pixels. The automated QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and zoom/text-resize restrictions. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 71 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so no HTTP probe was run. I can't confirm any destination resolves, and the truncated source doesn't let me inspect the `href`s.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp) fires five times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (Movable Ink) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1d45dad2-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are also present.
  - All of these are 1×1 trackers with no `alt`. They are intentional, but they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Identifier in URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa278…` value is repeated across the Movable Ink image URLs (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`). It is a per-recipient hash in the query string and could end up in proxy or cache logs. This is a design observation, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA (about 40 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`. Examples: `56be7a63-ec91-4fed-b133-e9563a2fbb5f.jpg`, `e92eca67-f059-4912-ae8d-db3c02b187f8.gif`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - Seven Movable Ink render images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the email is unreadable if these carry the content. The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from `alt=""`, so some of these could be decorative.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resize disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `html` and `body`. The `*{… line-height:100%}` rule also affects readability.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype (should be `&amp;`).
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times and `charset` twice.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the source.
- **Conflicting image rules:** the `min-width:768px` rule sets `img{max-width:600px}` and the `min-width:640px` rule sets `img{max-width:640px}`. The later rule wins at every width of 640px and up, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, the VML/MSO namespaces and the Outlook `PixelsPerInch` fix are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The Movable Ink `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated. I could not review the body past the truncation point.

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect it.
- **Authentication-Results is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated subdomain.
