# Win a $5k design refresh
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's actually interesting — not because I'm going to win a $5k gift card (I never do, and neither does anyone I know), but because "meet with a designer, get a chance at $5k toward your refresh" is a real hook for someone in my exact situation. I'm mid-renovation on two houses right now, so a free design consultation isn't fluff to me, it's a service I've genuinely considered paying for. CB2's been in my inbox constantly this week — I already flagged 8/11's designer-profile send and 8/12's sale-ends-tonight — and this is the first one that isn't just "buy this," it's "we'll help you plan," which is a different pitch entirely.
- That said, the sweepstakes framing undercuts the real value underneath it. The actual service — mood boards, color palettes, fabric swatches, in-store or virtual — is buried under "WIN A $5,000 REFRESH" branding that reads like a promo I'll scroll past assuming it's a low-odds raffle. And then it pivots hard into a full catalog dump: six room categories, eight "more for you" products, an app pitch, a rewards pitch, a referral pitch. By the time I hit the footer I've forgotten this was supposed to be about talking to a designer.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the "Book a Free Design Appointment" button specifically, because the underlying service is relevant to me right now. Would I forward it? No — this is a "bookmark and maybe act on it later" email, not a "hey check this out" email.

## What's working

- The hero photo — deep green sofa, warm stone coffee table, natural light — looks like an actual finished room, not a staged product shot, which is exactly the "after" I'm picturing for my own renovation.
- Leading with a real service (design consultation) instead of another discount code is a nice break from CB2's own cadence this week — 8/11 was a designer profile, 8/12 was 40% off, this connects the two ideas into something usable.
- The "ABOUT CB2 DESIGN SERVICES" section spells out exactly what I get — mood boards, color palettes, fabric swatches, product selections, in-store or virtual — which answers the swatch-and-sample question I actually care about before I'd ever book.
- Two "Book a Free Design Appointment" CTAs (top and mid-email) means I don't have to hunt for it if I scroll past the first one.

## What's weak

- The sweepstakes framing on the hero reads like bait — "WIN A $5,000 REFRESH" in giant type, then fine print at the very bottom about official rules and odds. I've been burned by enough "you could win!" emails that this softened my trust in the actual free-consultation offer underneath it.
- After the second CTA, the email turns into a straight-up catalog: 6 room category tiles, then 8 "More For You" products with prices. None of that has anything to do with booking a designer — it's just CB2 doing its normal product-dump thing, the same move I called out in 8/11's "Prepare to want it all."
- No dates or deadline visible on the sweepstakes itself beyond "August 13 - September 13" buried in the subhead — easy to miss, and there's no countdown urgency like 8/12's "ends tonight" banner had.
- Rewards program teaser ("Earn 10% Back") and app pitch are crammed into the footer as afterthoughts instead of being tied to the design-service offer, where a "book now, and rewards members get X" line would've actually mattered.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the sweepstakes framing from the hero and lead with the service instead — "Free design help, $5k on us if we pick your project" undersells nothing and reads more honest. Right now the hero over-promises a lottery and under-sells a genuinely useful consultation.
- 2. Move the appointment date window ("August 13 – September 13") into the hero subhead in real type size, not buried copy — that's the actual urgency mechanism and it's nearly invisible.
- 3. Cut the "More For You" product grid entirely from this send. This email should be about booking a designer, not competing with CB2's own sale emails for shelf space in my head.
- 4. Tie the rewards program directly to the design service — "Rewards members get priority booking" or similar — instead of listing it as an unrelated footer perk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free design help + a shot at $5,000 off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Book a CB2 designer before Sept 13`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mood boards, swatches, and a plan — virtual or in-store.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One appointment, zero cost, real design help.`
