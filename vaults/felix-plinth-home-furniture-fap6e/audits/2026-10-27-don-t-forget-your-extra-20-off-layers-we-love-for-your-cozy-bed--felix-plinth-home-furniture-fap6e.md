# Don't forget your EXTRA 20% OFF + Layers we love for your cozy bed
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and the same maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is on top. I've been skimming it since 10/22. It's the fifth send in a week with that opener, and it was already on the "Free design help!" send earlier today. My eyes skipped it before I'd read it.
- Under the strip, this one has a real point of view. "Cozy is Calling" is a moody plaid and flannel holiday bedding story, and it's built as three "Shop the Look" rooms with named pairings. Velvet Scalloped Plaid Quilt paired with Medallion Matelassé Duvet and Merry Fair Isle Lumpar Pillow is the kind of thing I'd pin. Since I'm doing a primary and a second home, a layered bed I can copy wholesale is useful. I'd click through to one of the looks and skip the rest. I wouldn't forward it. It's a nice bedding lookbook sitting under a discount banner I've stopped reading.

## What's working

- The three "paired with" looks give me a complete bed recipe: quilt, duvet, lumbar pillow. That saves me from assembling it myself.
- "All bedding ships free" is a clear, concrete perk. It matters for bulky items.
- The "In-stock furniture, guest-ready pieces delivered in 1-4 weeks" block speaks to my renovation timeline. It's the delivery answer I actually need.
- The Rewards card callout ("Earn 10% back in rewards") fits how I buy, since I use the highest-rewards card. The Affirm option is a bonus.
- The room photography is warm and consistent, with a green, plaid and cream palette. That's easy to save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder.

## What's weak

- The maroon "Extra 20% off" strip is the same one from 10/22, 10/26 and 10/27. The "Don't forget" subject also reads like a nag, because I've been told this five times.
- "Select Styles" is vague, and I can't tell whether the quilts and duvets shown are included. The fine print is a dense block at the bottom, so the offer doesn't feel fully honest.
- The "Recommended just for you" block is a curtain rod, a dining table, a platform bed, a rug and a Christmas tree. It has nothing to do with the bedding story and reads as generic filler.
- It's very long, and the Christmas Shop banner, app download, rewards and Affirm blocks pile on at the bottom and dilute the story. Prices aren't visible on any of the featured products either.
- The product captions are small, and I can't tell if the lumbar pillow and duvet are sold as a set or separately.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the maroon strip for a few sends. Make the discount one line inside the hero, and name the exact eligible bedding styles.
- 2. Show prices, or a "now $X" tag, under each paired item. Add a "shop the whole look" button that adds all three to the cart.
- 3. Cut the "Recommended just for you" block, or make it bedding-only, so it matches the story.
- 4. Move the in-stock/delivery promise up near the top. For furniture buyers it's a bigger draw than another percent-off line.
- 5. Add swatch or sample access for the quilt and duvet colors. Crate & Barrel and Article both do a better job showing color and fabric choices.
- For comparison, CB2's "ONE DAY LEFT" countdown earned its place because the deadline was visible immediately. Pottery Barn's "Don't forget" gives me no real date. Wayfair's "Fall Clearance" send was a wall of percent-off tiles, and this one is more curated than that, though its bottom third drifts the same way. Article's "Steal some style inspo" made me care about the rooms without asking for a discount, and this email should lean harder on that.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px template with no unresolved merge tokens in the visible source. The main technical problems are missing alt text on about 60 images, three `http://` image sources, recipient email addresses in plaintext in tracking and pixel URLs, and unverifiable unsubscribe, authentication and UTM data. The HTML source is truncated and 64 tracking links were not probed, so those areas can't be fully confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 64 links were skipped as click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im…`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`). Destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1` is a Turn/Amobee ad-network tracking image. It is served alongside the ESP's own tracking.
- **Insecure open pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and it exposes the recipient address in transit.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,326 of 16,611 characters (about 74%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk.
