# New & bestselling bunks and loft beds →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, not Crate & Barrel — different mailbox subscription, same fatigue. I don't have kids, so "two kids, one room: find their sleep solution" and a wall of bunk beds and loft beds is just not my life right now. My renovation is a primary home and a second home, both adult-occupied — I'm buried in tile and paint decisions, not sleep solutions for two kids sharing a room. This is a clean, well-shot email — I'll give it that — but it's aimed at a completely different household than mine.
- The "exclusive!" bunk bed callouts and the "starting at $34" bedding module are nicely organized, and I actually like that they show three distinct bed styles (Grayson storage loft, Hampshire bunk, Finn bunk) instead of one hero and a wall of clutter like the C&B "bestselling for your living room" send I logged this morning. But it's irrelevant to me specifically, so I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — unless I know a friend with two kids sharing a room, in which case maybe. Marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- Three named bed styles (Grayson, Hampshire, Finn) each get their own hero shot and price anchor — more organized than C&B's category-tile dump I saw earlier today.
- The room styling photos are genuinely nice — warm wood tones, natural light — this brand knows its aesthetic.
- "Starting at $34" on the coordinating bedding/storage module gives a real price anchor, which most of these sends skip.
- Rewards card teaser near the bottom ("new! the updated Crate Card") is relevant to me since I already shop this rewards ecosystem for the renovation.

## What's weak

- Nothing here applies to my house — no kids, no shared bedrooms, no bunk bed need. Wrong list entirely for my current project.
- It's yet another Crate & Barrel family email today — between this, the flatware guide, and two living room sends, that's my fourth-plus touch from this family in 24 hours. Total fatigue.
- "personalize it" section and "we love these for you" feel like generic modules bolted on, not actual personalization — nothing in "we love these for you" reflects anything I've browsed.
- Long, long scroll (app download, TheDesignDesk consult, social follow, footer) before you even get to anything I'd care about, and even then it's not there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if Crate & Kids has zero signal I have children, don't send kids'-bedroom content at all; fold me back into core Crate & Barrel sends only.
- 2. If segmentation isn't feasible yet, lead with the cross-brand rewards/card content instead — that's the one thing here that's actually relevant to every subscriber, kids or not.
- 3. Cut the send frequency across the Crate family — I'm getting hit from C&B proper, CB2, and now Crate & Kids in overlapping windows. CB2 already gets docked for this same "family fatigue" in my head.
- 4. Trim the bottom half — app download, TheDesignDesk booking, social follow-all stacked as separate CTAs dilutes the one useful message (the Crate Card).
