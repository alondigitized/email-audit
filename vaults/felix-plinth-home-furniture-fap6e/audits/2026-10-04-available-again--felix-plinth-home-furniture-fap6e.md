## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, is left in the output. It also loads 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels and has widespread missing alt text. The 43% QA pass rate reflects these issues and the checks that couldn't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 71 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and link rot can't be confirmed from the data given.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  
  Clients may block these, and they can trigger mixed-content handling in webmail. If they are blocked, the Dotomi cookie-sync and retargeting calls fail silently. If they load, they add 11 extra third-party requests.
- **Pixels without alt:** The `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` open pixel and the `dv.cb2.com/o/6b41dbb4-...` pixel are over HTTPS but have no alt attribute. This is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 12 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt attribute, for example `4ddadd7f-a573-48ad-912f-4e23a2a4b2df.jpg` and `fa96b5d7-c967-4bd6-8092-1830c7513292.jpg`. With images off, or in a screen reader, these render blank or read out the filename. The other 10 are `c1960820-…`, `b6549606-…`, `ea79e7d9-…`, `4a4ff8d5-…`, `6ff4d19c-…`, `90915ec9-…`, `7d159c70-…`, `b1521c18-…`, `badb0ec1-…` and `962dd823-…`.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **Text-resize overrides:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `body`. This also prevents user text scaling.
- **Not assessable:** The source was truncated, so the remaining markup, such as table roles, heading structure and contrast, couldn't be reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` is present unresolved. It looks like a Marketing Cloud-style personalization string for a reviews or SKU module. Either the data source returned empty or the substitution never ran.
- **Unconfirmed impact:** The truncated source doesn't show where the token sits, so I can't say whether it is visible text, inside a link `href`, or in a hidden block. If it is in a link, that link is broken.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Neither header was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender defect. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (RFC 8058 one-click) wouldn't be met.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sending domain is `mail.cb2.com`.
- **Footer:** The visible unsubscribe link and postal address couldn't be checked because the source was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 71 tracked links point to redirect domains and weren't resolved, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the data provided. The only parameters visible are the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` identifiers on the open and tracking pixels. `mi_u` is a hashed per-recipient value.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the data feed and the substitution step, add a fallback or conditional block so the module drops out when empty, and confirm where the token lands in the markup.
2. **Add alt text** to the 12 content images, and use `alt=""` on decorative images and tracking pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com`). Failing that, remove them, since they are likely blocked anyway.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag, and reconsider `text-size-adjust:none`.
5. **Verify the headers at the source.** Check the raw message at the receiving MTA rather than through the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. **Resolve the 71 tracked links** in a follow-up run (follow the redirects) to check destinations and UTM consistency.
