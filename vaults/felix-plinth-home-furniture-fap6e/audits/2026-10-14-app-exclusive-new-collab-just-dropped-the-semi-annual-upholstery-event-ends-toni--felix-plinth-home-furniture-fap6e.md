## What's working

- The upholstery hero is the strongest block. "FINAL HOURS," "UP TO 25% OFF" and a white SHOP THE SALE button make a clear offer and deadline.
- The moody green sectional photo fits what I'm shopping for.
- The rewards strip near the bottom ("EARN 10% BACK" in Rewards Dollars at CB2 and Crate & Barrel) speaks to me, since I buy on whichever card earns the most.
- The category grid (New Arrivals, Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs) is well styled and easy to scan.
- The 15% off for texts offer is a concrete incentive I could take up.

## What's weak

- The "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS, AND MORE / BEST SELLER" header has overlapping text. It looks broken in the render.
- The app takeover, "A NEW COLLECTION IS HERE / SEE IT FIRST ON THE APP," pushes the sale below the fold and sends me away from the upholstery deadline. The Casa Dean collab is not for me.
- The subject says "ENDS TONIGHT," but the hero only says "Final Hours." The two deadlines don't quite match, and the exact time is buried in fine print.
- There are no prices, swatches, fabric options or delivery windows anywhere. For upholstery that matters a lot, because swatches and lead times drive the decision.
- The fine print is huge and tiny. "UP TO 25%" has so many exclusions that the offer feels less honest.
- It's the 16th send in six days. The 10/13 "ONLY HOURS LEFT TO SAVE" was also a countdown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make upholstery the whole email. Put the sale first, then a row of 3-4 sofas with prices, a "free swatches" link and delivery times. Move the app promo to a thin banner at the bottom.
- 2. Fix the overlapping "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS, AND MORE / BEST SELLER" header.
- 3. Match the deadline in the subject, the hero and the button, and put the actual cutoff time in the hero (for example "ends 11:59 PM PT").
- 4. Put the rewards earn rate higher up, with an example of what a sofa purchase would earn.
- 5. Use fewer sends, or at least don't run two different countdowns in two days.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, leaked into the output. It also has about 20 images with no alt text, 11 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The QA run passed 43% of checks. Several compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Skipped probes:** 62 tracking links were not checked because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels (11):** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` are all non-HTTPS. Clients and proxies may block or rewrite them, which causes mixed-content warnings and lost tracking. These are third-party cookie-sync pixels, and the 10 `pub*.php` calls add load and privacy exposure.
- **Other tracking pixels (HTTPS):** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/6a70fca6-30e6-459e-8dcd-c7046f791d5b?mi_cid=…` are fine on protocol. Both lack alt text (see section 3).
