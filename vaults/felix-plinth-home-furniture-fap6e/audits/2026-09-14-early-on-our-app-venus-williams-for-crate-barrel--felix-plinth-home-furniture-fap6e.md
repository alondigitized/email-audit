# Early on our app: Venus Williams for Crate & Barrel!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the third Venus Williams send I've now sat through — I flagged it yesterday as an app-only phone-mockup gate, and today's version is exactly the same creative, just retitled "Early on our app" instead of "Shop it first." Same tennis-dress hero shot, same green banner, same "Download the Crate App" button with zero web option in sight. My gut reaction hasn't changed: I'm not downloading an app to look at a collab, full stop.
- Where this email actually earns something is below the fold — "we love these for you" with six real products (sofas, a dresser, a dining table, an accent chair) and actual prices right there on the image. That's more useful to me than anything CB2 or Crate & Kids has sent this week, because I can see the number without clicking through. But then it undercuts itself by dangling "your chance at $500" behind the app download again, and pushes the Crate Card 10% rewards pitch, which — fine, I already have a high-rewards card doing that job.
- I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding. If a friend asked "did you see the Venus Williams thing," I'd say "yeah, they keep making you download an app for it," and leave it there.

## What's working

- Real prices on the product grid ("we love these for you") — Pottery Barn's Kravet collab email made me guess at cost; this one doesn't.
- The Barrett II sofa and Keane dresser shots are clean, well-lit, on-brand neutral palette — no clashing swatches like the Wayfair ceiling fan disaster.
- TheDesignDesk free-appointment CTA is a genuine value-add for someone mid-renovation like me, and it's repeated later so I'm not likely to miss it.
- Footer category nav (Furniture, Bedding, Decor, Rugs, etc.) is clean and gives me a real path if I do want to browse without the app.

## What's weak

- The entire hero — the actual "Venus Williams for Crate & Barrel" reveal — only unlocks behind an app download. Same complaint I had yesterday on the Venus Williams email, and it's still not fixed.
- "$500" chance is another app-gated incentive; I can't tell if it's a sweepstakes or a discount, and the vagueness reads like bait.
- This is now the fifth-plus Crate & Barrel family email (counting Crate&Kids) I've gotten this week — the C&B family snooze reflex is fully activated.
- No swatch/sample program mention anywhere, and I'm mid-renovation on two houses — this is exactly where CB2's "Grand entrances" email beat it by staying tightly scoped to one room/category instead of scattershotting sofa, dresser, table, chair all at once.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual Venus Williams collection images in the email body — not gated behind an app download. Let me see three product shots before you ask me to install anything.
- 2. Replace "chance at $500" with a real number or drop it — vague sweepstakes language behind an app-only wall reads as bait-and-switch, same issue I flagged on Wayfair's ceiling fan email.
- 3. Cut the product grid from six to three and tie them to the collab's aesthetic (rattan, tennis-club neutral) instead of a generic assortment — right now it reads like unrelated inventory, not a curated Venus Williams edit.
- 4. Space this out from the Crate&Kids sends — five emails in a week from the same house is fatigue, not frequency.
