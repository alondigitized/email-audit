## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has real CSS defects in its dark-mode blocks, an empty `<title>`, and 3 images flagged for missing alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the AgentMail relay didn't expose the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM details are unconfirmed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Two click-tracking domains are in use. `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...~~/AAQVbhA~/...` is a third-party domain that doesn't match the sender's domain. `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...` is on the brand's own domain.
- This split is a deliverability and brand-alignment concern. The third-party domain is a reputation risk for link-rewriting filters.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 15, and reported no broken links. The skipped links, including footer and social links, are unverified.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (QA, 3 images):**
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Pki1pMy6YriMRPt0Nyq7gw~~/AAQVbhA~/BxjxKGvs...`
  - `links.email.article.com/s/eo/HyGg6PxDWqFcUtaZfgqiSn9aoYKwcPR3...`
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/kTzEHOymrud408Hs_gLvyA~~/AAQVbhA~/2OKvYZCb...`
  - Some of these may be tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""`. I can't tell which from the truncated source.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is present. Screen readers and some clients show nothing.
- **Invalid dark-mode CSS:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma before `{`. The selector list is invalid, so browsers drop the whole rule and headings and paragraphs won't be forced white in dark mode.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a misplaced `!important`, which is parsed as a junk declaration. The colour applies without `!important` and can be overridden.
  - The `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark){` block is never closed in its own `<style>`. The next `<style>` ends with an extra `}`. Browsers auto-close at the end of the style element, but this is fragile.
  - `line-height:auto` in `.dark-img` is not a valid value.
- Positive: it declares `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes`, uses `[data-ogsc]` selectors for Outlook.com, and includes the `mso` line-height resets.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.

**5. Compliance**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay and says one-click unsubscribe "may not be supported". This is unconfirmed, not a verified failure.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA also expects this from the relay.
- The visible HTML doesn't show a footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical address couldn't be checked.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated. Links are opaque redirect tokens, and the QA data doesn't include their destinations.
- No landing-page mismatch was reported by the probes.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the dark-mode CSS:
   - Change `h1,h2,p,{` to `h1,h2,p{`.
   - Change `a{color:#fff;!important}` to `a{color:#fff!important}`.
   - Add the missing `}` to close the `@media` block.
   - Remove `line-height:auto`.
2. Set a meaningful `<title>`. Use the subject, "Home tour: A space fit for fall".
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to content images, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels and decorative images.
4. Use one tracking domain aligned with `email.article.com`, or confirm that `spmailtechnol.com` is set up as a branded link domain.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` with full headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and would show the footer and UTM parameters.
