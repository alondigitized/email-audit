## What's weak

- The top banner is the exact same "UP TO 40% OFF THE FURNITURE EVENT" strip I've now seen four days running — Wayfair spamming "80% OFF" in ALL CAPS is more annoying, but CB2 repeating the identical banner daily is its own kind of numbness.

- Subject line ("the most important room in the house") tells me nothing concrete — I had to open it to learn it's about the entryway.

- 2. Put a price or "from $X" under each console table shot. I'm mid-decision on a foyer piece; make me do less work to compare.
