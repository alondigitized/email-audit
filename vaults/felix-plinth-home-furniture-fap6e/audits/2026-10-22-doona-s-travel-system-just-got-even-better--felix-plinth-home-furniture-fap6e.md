# Doona’s travel system just got even better!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids again, and I'm not the buyer. I'm renovating two houses, and a car seat/stroller combo isn't on either punch list. My gut reaction was "wrong sender, wrong life stage." It's the tenth C&B-family email in two days, counting the glider send earlier today and the "60% off" clearance blasts before it.
- As a stroller email it's well built. The Doona X hero is big and clean, and the model on the sidewalk sells the product. The feature blocks (three recline positions, three travel modes, five colors) are easy to scan. But there's no price or percent-off anywhere in the top half, and the top CTA is just "Shop Now."
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. A friend with a newborn might get it, but I'd just send them the link myself. It gets marked as read and filed with the rest of the C&B pile. I do notice that someone gave this email real attention. It just wasn't aimed at me.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "New! Doona X All in One Car Seat & Stroller" sits over a clear product shot, and I get what it is in a second.
- The feature stack is easy to read. Recline, travel modes and colors each get a short caption and an image, and I never had to hunt for the point.
- The color swatch row is a nice touch. It's the same color-palette thinking I want when I'm picking finishes, and I wish C&B main did it for rugs.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows prices, so there's at least some commerce in the bottom half.
- The Venus Williams collab banner is a stand-out visual. It's the only thing here with a real brand story.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the top half is for me. The hero, the feature blocks and the Doona sub-catalog are all about newborns and travel. I'm furnishing rooms.
- There's no offer anywhere in the hero. No percent off, no free shipping, no member pricing. The main CTA is a grey "Shop Now" button.
- Too many things compete for the click. After the Doona section come the Venus Williams collab, a "we love these for you" grid, the credit card promo, the app promo, a CB2 and The Design Desk block, and a store locator. It reads like four emails stacked together.
- "Shop the Collab" and "Learn More" are vague. I can't tell where either one lands.
- The footer is dense and the legal text is tiny. The email is very long, and on my phone I'd never reach The Design Desk "Book a Free Appointment" block, which is the one part I'd actually care about.
- The "we love these for you" grid has a Pottery Barn Kids-style nursery chair next to a dresser and a mattress. It doesn't feel personalized to me at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send. Anyone who has bought furniture and not baby gear shouldn't get a Doona email. A kids sender mailing a home-renovation shopper is the cadence problem I keep running into.
- 2. Put one concrete offer in the hero, such as a price, a member deal or free shipping. Right now the hero only describes the product.
- 3. Cut the lower half down. Keep the Doona section, the product grid and one CTA, and move the card, app and Design Desk blocks to a separate send.
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific, like "Shop the Doona X" instead of "Shop Now," and "See the Venus Williams collection" instead of "Shop the Collab."
- 5. For a home-renovation shopper, lead with The Design Desk and the free appointment. Article's "free interior design" email did that better last week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Doona X: 3 recline positions, 5 colors`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Doona X is here. Ships free?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Car seat, stroller and travel system in one. See the new colors.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fold it in seconds. Fits in your trunk. Shop Doona X.`
- Pottery Barn's "Holiday NEW ARRIVALS" at least put a sale banner on top. CB2's "Warehouse Sale" nailed the offer in one image. This one makes me work to find the point.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink–driven template with 70 click-tracked links and about 34 images flagged for missing alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and link destinations were not available for inspection.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** The QA run skipped 70 tracking links as redirect domains, so none were probed for status codes, redirect chains, or landing-page health.
- **Third-party pixel with a hashed identifier.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels load from sr.rlcdn.com. The `s=` value matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value in the first-party Movable Ink URLs (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `o.gif`). The same per-recipient hash appears to be passed to a third party. This is a privacy and disclosure concern, not a rendering fault.
- **Duplicate pixel requests.** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` is requested 6 times and `448796.gif` 5 times. These are probably intentional repeats, but they add requests.
- **Identifiers in image URLs.** `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` are in the image URLs (e.g. `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d4272d5d-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`). A forwarded email exposes them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 34 `<img>` elements were flagged.
  - Content images: roughly 17 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif`, for example `a609fd03-…jpg`, `8964c2f1-…gif`, `c6e9b7e8-…jpg`. With images blocked, these render as blanks, and screen readers get no description.
  - Spacer and pixel images: `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` and the tracking pixels. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly. The QA check can't tell a missing attribute from an intentionally empty one, so this may overstate the count.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing blocked.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable text-size adjustment. They conflict with `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` earlier in the same sheet.
- **Invalid markup.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` are both present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
- **Template residue.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the source.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` and `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` give a larger cap at 640–767px than at 768px and above.
- **Title.** The `<title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The rest of the markup was not available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found.** QA reports "[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found". It notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** The RFC 8058 header is also missing. This matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, but the same relay caveat applies.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The sender is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated section. Neither can be confirmed or denied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 70 links go through click-tracking redirects and weren't resolved, and the final URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible. The Doona travel-system landing page, which the subject line promises, was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** add descriptive alt text to the ~17 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images. Set `alt=""` explicitly on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. **Viewport:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Text sizing:** drop the `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule.
4. **Markup hygiene:**
   - Escape the `&` in `<title>`.
   - Remove the duplicate `meta charset` and `format-detection` tags.
   - Remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
   - Reconcile the conflicting `img max-width` media queries.
5. **LiveRamp pixel:** review the `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels and confirm the privacy policy and consent cover sharing the hashed identifier.
6. **Headers:** re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Do not treat the current warnings as confirmed defects.
7. **Link checks:** resolve the 70 tracking redirects with a follow-redirect probe and inspect UTM params and landing-page status.
8. **Footer:** re-run with untruncated HTML to check the unsubscribe link and postal address.
