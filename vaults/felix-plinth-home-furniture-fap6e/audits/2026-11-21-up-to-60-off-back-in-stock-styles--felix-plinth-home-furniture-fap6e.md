# UP TO 60% OFF BACK-IN-STOCK STYLES
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and by my count this is somewhere around the fifteenth send since the Black Friday run started. The sender name is still "CB2 BLACK FRIDAY," so I knew what it was before I opened it. The hero is an olive velvet sectional with a big "UP TO 60% OFF," and it's a good-looking shot. The "In-stock favorites, ready to ship for instant gratification" line is the first thing that's spoken to me in a week. I'm renovating two houses and waiting on lead times, so "ready to ship" matters.
- Then it falls apart. After the hero, it's a six-tile category grid, a free shipping banner, and a "Save 15% off in the New Year" banner. That's four different offers and no product. Nothing here says which "back-in-stock styles" are back in stock, and the subject is the only place that phrase appears. This is the same template as yesterday's free-shipping send and the 11/21 "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" email, with the hero swapped.
- I'd open it again out of habit, but I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted that sectional I'd go to the site and look. I'll file the hero in the Pinterest folder and move on.

## What's working

- The hero's olive velvet sectional with the huge "60% OFF" reads in one second. The offer is the first thing my eye lands on.
- "In-stock favorites, ready to ship" is the one line that matches how I'm shopping right now, with a renovation deadline.
- The category tiles are photographed well. The lighting and décor/mirror tiles are the kind of thing I'd pin.
- The "Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars" block is a nod to my rewards-card habit, and it's the only loyalty hook in the email.
- The dark maroon and cream palette is consistent and clearly CB2.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "back-in-stock styles" and the body shows none. I got a sectional and a grid of generic categories, with no named products and no prices.
- There are four offers stacked together: up to 60% off, free shipping on $99+, 15% off in the new year, and 10% back. It feels like a pile, not a hierarchy.
- The sale tag on the hero is "up to 60%," but the footer fine print says up to 50% off select furniture. I noticed, and it makes the headline feel a little less honest.
- Both "SHOP THE SALE" buttons go to the same place and are small and thin. On a phone they'd be a skinny tap target.
- The fine print is a wall of microscopic text. I can't read it without zooming, and that's where the exclusions are buried.
- The "Save 15% off in the New Year" banner is a promise for next year in an email meant to get me to buy now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Show four to six actual back-in-stock pieces with names and sale prices, and a "Ships in 3-5 days" tag. Right now the email doesn't deliver what the subject promises.
- 2. Cut the stacked offers down to one primary offer and one secondary. Free shipping and the 15% banner can share a single line.
- 3. Reconcile the 60% and 50% claims so the headline and the fine print agree.
- 4. Give the sectional a product-specific CTA like "Shop the Lyric sectional," not another generic "Shop the sale."
- 5. Add swatch or sample and delivery-window info for furniture. Someone buying a sectional needs to know when it arrives.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In stock + ships fast: CB2 sofas, up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The velvet sectional is back (and 60% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ready to ship before the holidays. Sectionals, lighting, and mirrors in stock now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Back-in-stock styles, most delivering in under a week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML layout with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint. The confirmed defects are 11 content images with no alt text, 11 insecure `http://` Dotomi tracking pixels, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The unsubscribe and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML is truncated, so the body, footer and link markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure tracking pixels (11):** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1`. Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or proxied, and they trigger mixed-content warnings in some clients. Cookie-sync pixels that are blocked silently stop working.
- **Pixel volume:** 10 sequential cookie-redirect pixels (`pub1`–`pub10`) plus the UCM pixel is heavy third-party fan-out for one email. It adds load time and a larger privacy and deliverability surface.
- **Click tracking not verified:** 61 links were skipped as tracking or redirect domains (HTTP probe skipped). Link validity, redirect destinations and 404s are unknown.
- **Open pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/3536b007-...?mi_cid=...` are HTTPS and first-party. The only issue is the missing `alt` (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on 11 content images** hosted at `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `0d2b4415-...gif`
  - `35160d4e-...jpg`
  - `4b72079a-...jpg`
  - `c8040201-...jpg`
  - `e663ddd5-...jpg`
  - `c2386fbf-...jpg`
  - `fdbe20e7-...jpg`
  - `df0b893d-...jpg`
  - `7db2cdab-...jpg`
  - `e68a523c-...jpg`
  - `20185973-...jpg`

  With images off, or for screen readers, the offer content (such as the "UP TO 60% OFF" text) may be unreadable. Whether the text is in an image or in live text could not be checked in the truncated source.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`** (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, all Dotomi pixels): these should carry `alt=""` so they are explicitly decorative.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `body`. Combined with `*{line-height:100%}`, this undermines text scaling and legibility. The `.f10`/`.f14` mobile classes also use 12px and 16px line-heights.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` placeholder comment (misspelled "IMPUT").
  - Empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.
  - Malformed `<!----- MI styling end----->` comments.
- **Font URLs:**
  - Several `@font-face` URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`).
  - Some fonts are loaded from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain.
  - The `.eot` `src` has no `format()` hint, and the `.woff` rule has no weight or style descriptor.
  - Web fonts are unsupported in many clients, so the fallback is Helvetica/sans-serif (acceptable).
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, `charset=utf-8`, the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, and the `format-detection` metas.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay. The sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is consistent with aligned authentication, but it was not verified.
- **Footer not inspected:** the body-level unsubscribe link and the physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) fall in the truncated section. These need a check against the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 61 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the data. Nothing is flagged for or against the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 11 content images listed above, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixels (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/.../pub1–10.php`) to `https://`. Consider dropping the sequential cookie-sync pixels or moving them to server-side tagging.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Revisit `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and the global `line-height:100%`.
4. Clean up the template: remove the "IMPUT HERE" placeholder, the empty `!mso` block, and the double-slash font paths. Add `format()` hints for the `.eot`.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC on a direct-to-inbox copy rather than the AgentMail relay. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and address, merge tokens, UTMs and the 61 tracked links.
