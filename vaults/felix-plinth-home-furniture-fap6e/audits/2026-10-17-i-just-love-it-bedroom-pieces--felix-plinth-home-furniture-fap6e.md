## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML and has the standard responsive and Outlook fallbacks. The automated QA passed 57% of checks. The main confirmed issues are about 55 images without alt text, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and a third-party pixel sending an identifier in its URL. The header and tracking checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 92 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination health and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` fires five times. That domain is LiveRamp's. The same 64-character hex value appears in the first-party URLs as `mi_u=dbe8da718e29…`, so one identifier is being passed to a third party. The source doesn't show what it is derived from.
- **Repeated beacon:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. Some of these may be redundant tracking requests, though they could also be intentional per-module tracking.
- **Other pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (open pixel) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/bd550ffa-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are present as expected. Neither has alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 `<img>` warnings were flagged. The QA output can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from an empty `alt=""`.
  - **Content images:** about 30 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` have no alt text (e.g., `6e5c890c-e2e3-4278-a27b-31f628d3e2ab.jpg`, `feddbbd3-6727-4e10-9bb1-5536526d99ac.jpg`, `ed972f76-…gif`, `a4c4b37d-…gif`). If these are product or hero images, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. This is the largest accessibility issue.
  - **Spacers:** `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` and `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` should have an explicit `alt=""` (decorative). This is low severity.
  - **Tracking pixels:** these should also carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and is generic. The tag doesn't match the subject line.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately). Duplicates are harmless but add bloat.
- **Conflicting CSS:** two desktop media queries (`min-width:768px` sets `img{max-width:600px}` and `min-width:640px` sets `img{max-width:640px}`) both match at 768px and wider. The later one wins, so the 600px rule never applies.
- **Line height:** the global `*{... line-height:100%}` rule may cramp text in some clients.
- **Not assessable:** the HTML was truncated, so the body markup (role attributes, heading structure, live text vs. image-only text) wasn't reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so confirm against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 92 tracking links were not resolved, so I can't see final URLs, UTM parameters or landing pages. Tracking appears to be handled through the `mi.`/`dv.` redirect domain with `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Fix the `<title>` (escape `&` as `&amp;`, make it descriptive).
4. Confirm the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) by inspecting the original message outside the relay.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to check the destination URLs and UTM/campaign parameters.
6. Review whether the identifier shared with `sr.rlcdn.com` (LiveRamp) is a hashed email. If so, check that this is covered by the privacy disclosures. Also consider whether five duplicate calls are needed.
7. Clean up the duplicate `format-detection` metas, the dead `max-width:600px` rule and the stray template comment.
