# DINING TABLE SETS 🚩 Hurry – UP TO 70% OFF ends soon 🚩 
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "DINING TABLE SETS," "picked just for you" is the second header — and then the actual hero grid is a dining set, a poolside table, a bar cart, patio furniture covers, a nightstand, and a wall clock. One out of six tiles is a dining table. This is the same move Wayfair pulled with "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" and "BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS" earlier this week — subject promises one thing, the email delivers a kitchen-sink scroll of everything in the warehouse. I've caught this pattern from them four times now and I'm done being surprised by it.
- The one thing that's real: 70% off area rugs banner up top, "up to 70% off" category strip further down, free delivery over $35. Those are legit, specific numbers. But by the time I hit "doorbuster deals" with a weight bench, a playground swing set, and a sewing machine, I've completely forgotten this was supposed to be about my dining table. Compare that to Article's "Up to 40% off ends Monday" — one maroon strip, one deadline, done. Wayfair just can't resist showing me the whole store.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Marking as read and moving on — same verdict as basically every other Wayfair send this week.

## What's working

- The 70% off banner and the striped red/white "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" header is bold and easy to spot at a glance.
- The dining set and poolside table tiles at the very top actually do relate to a Millennial renovating two homes — real "before/after" price strikethroughs (Was $620/Now $560) give it some credibility.
- Free & easy delivery over $35 is a genuinely useful line for someone furnishing two houses at once — that's a real decision factor for me.
- Star ratings visible under products, at least giving a hint of social proof.

## What's weak

- Subject promises dining table sets; the hero grid gives me a bar cart, patio covers, a nightstand, and a wall clock instead — classic bait-and-switch that Wayfair has now run four times this week alone.
- "Doorbuster deals" section is total category chaos: gym equipment, a swing set, a kitchen faucet, ceiling tiles, and a mint sewing machine — none of that is dining, none of it is even furniture half the time.
- No swatch/sample program, no room visualizer, nothing that helps me actually picture this table in either of my two renovation projects — Crate & Barrel and CB2 at least show styled room shots.
- Zero personalization — no "based on your browsing," no rewards-member callout despite a "Wayfair Rewards" icon buried in the footer. Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel at least try segmenting by category.
- This is the fourth near-identical Wayfair kitchen-sink scroll I've screenshotted this week — cadence is exhausting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to just dining — tables, chairs, sideboards, bar carts if you must — and kill the doorbuster grab-bag entirely. If the subject says dining tables, the whole email should be dining tables, the way Article stays locked to one sale message.
- 2. Move the 70% off rugs banner or replace it with a dining-specific offer — right now the top banner doesn't even match the subject's category.
- 3. Add a "Complete the room" swatch or fabric-sample CTA under the dining set tile — that's the one thing that would actually get me to click, since I'm mid-renovation and comparing finishes.
- 4. Add a rewards-points callout near the dining hero tile instead of burying "Wayfair Rewards" in the footer icons.
- 5. Slow the cadence — four subject-mismatched sends in a week is training me to stop opening these at all.
