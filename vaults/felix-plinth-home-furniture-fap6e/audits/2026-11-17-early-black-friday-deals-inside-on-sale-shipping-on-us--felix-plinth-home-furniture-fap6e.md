# Early Black Friday deals inside + On sale + shipping on us!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. I've lost count since 11/16, and the maroon "Early Black Friday up to 70% off" banner is the same one I've now seen in "UP TO 70% OFF cozy pillows & throws 🧸" and the other sends. My eyes slid past it. This one does have a real hero, though: pillows and a throw on a sofa, "Save on Easy Updates," and "on sale & shipping free." Free shipping is the most useful thing in this email, and it's only a small italic line.
- Below that there's a clean eight-category grid, and the "In-stock furniture, delivered in 1–4 weeks" strip is the one block that speaks to my renovation timeline. Two houses and a contractor don't wait on 12-week lead times. The stockings and the Design Crew sweepstakes are filler. Earlier today, the Design Services email at least made free design help the hero. This one buries it as a small sweepstakes footer.
- Would I click? Probably into Throws or the in-stock furniture, and only because I'm already shopping. I wouldn't forward it. Crate & Barrel's "Double Rewards" email at least gave me rewards math to screenshot.

## What's working

- The pillow-and-throw hero is warm and on-brand. It looks like a room I'd want, and it's the only image here that feels like inspiration.
- "On sale & shipping free" sits right above the headline, so the shipping claim is at least in the hero.
- The category grid (Pillows, Throws, Quilts, Duvets, Table Linens and so on) is easy to tap and lets me skip straight to what I need.
- "In-stock furniture, delivered in just 1–4 weeks" is a concrete delivery promise for furniture buyers, which is what I care about.
- The 10% back in rewards card block gives me rewards math, although it's far down the email.

## What's weak

- The maroon banner is the same creative I've seen all week. Not new, so it doesn't register.
- The free-shipping line is tiny and italic. The subject line leads with it, but the body doesn't.
- "Recommended just for you" shows Christmas stockings, a faux fur throw, a curtain rod, a quilt and a platform bed. That's a generic mix with no price, no discount and no rating on any item.
- The Design Crew sweepstakes is a "chance to win" with long legal copy, and I'd rather have the free design consult itself as the offer. It's also a weaker repeat of today's "Free Design Services" send.
- The legal fine print is a long block. The exclusions weren't visible near the offer, so "up to 70%" feels vague.
- The email is very long. The App Store, card and Affirm blocks pile on after the furniture strip.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero one claim: "Free shipping + up to 70% off," in big type. Drop the separate maroon banner so I'm not seeing two heroes.
- 2. Put price tags and "was/now" figures on the recommended products. Show the actual savings on the throw, the quilt and the bed.
- 3. Move the in-stock furniture strip up under the category grid, with swatch or fabric-sample info, since I'm matching palettes across two houses.
- 4. Cut the sweepstakes to one line, or make the Design Crew free appointment the CTA. Drop the sweepstakes language.
- 5. Show rewards math near the top: "10% back with the PB card, stacks with sale."
- 6. Space out the sends. I got a lot of near-identical Pottery Barn emails in two days.

- **Subject Alt B:** `In stock, ships in 1–4 weeks: furniture on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on sale items, plus guest-ready furniture in stock now.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Early Black Friday deals inside + On sale + shipping on us!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a hidden preheader and VML/MSO conditionals. It has widespread missing `alt` text, three non-HTTPS image sources, and the recipient's email address sent in plaintext in tracking-pixel URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most link targets could not be inspected. The 57% QA pass rate comes mostly from the alt-text warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **56 links not probed.** They go through the click-redirect domain `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`, so destination status codes are unverified. The example is the logo link: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjMs…`.
- **Three or more third-party or secondary tracking beacons**, all missing `alt`:
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEv…/cat/1`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/f7e74d96-…?mi_cid=677b44e6831b79f3&mi_mid=…`
- **`http://svopn.potterybarn.com` is plain HTTP.** Clients that block mixed content will drop this open pixel, so opens will undercount.
- **Possible stale assets (not confirmed as defects).**
  - Image paths include `/pb/2025/US/0822_FRI_AM_LTOSale_FreeShip1/slices_01–12.jpg`, which looks like an August 2025 campaign.
  - Image paths also include `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, a 2022 asset.
  - Both are probably reused modules, but they are worth checking for outdated content.
- **Cross-brand asset dependency:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from a West Elm host. If that host changes, the layout spacer breaks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on about 50 images.** Examples:
  - Hero: `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg`
  - Product tiles: `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`

  This is the most serious accessibility issue. Image-blocking clients and screen readers get no content or link labels from the sliced images. Many of these images are also linked, so the links are unlabeled.
- **Insecure image sources.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/…/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.**
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and is probably a template artifact. It is harmless in most clients, but a strict parser may discard the rule that follows it.
- **Some layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.** The 20px spacer table after the logo header is one example, while other tables have it.
- **`<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/…Montserrat">`** is unsupported in Gmail and Outlook, so it falls back to the system font. This is a minor point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the available source.
- **The recipient's email address appears in URLs in cleartext:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second is sent over unencrypted HTTP, so the address can be seen by anyone on the network path and may appear in proxy or server logs. This is a privacy and PII-hygiene concern.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are really absent, the sender fails Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status cannot be assessed from this data. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the tracking domains `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `image.e.potterybarn.com`, but that alone proves nothing.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source.** They could not be verified, so no finding is made.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be verified.** Links are wrapped in opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` tokens, and the tracking links were not resolved.
- **Product image `src` URLs** (`www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`) are served from the site domain, so they are hot-linked from production. They are not landing-page links.
- **Landing-page alignment** to the "Early Black Friday / shipping on us" offer was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all linked and content images, starting with the hero `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg` and the 12 product and footer slices. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets, including the `svopn` pixel, to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw recipient email in the `coreemail.html` and `sv_sveme` query strings. Use an opaque ID or hashed token.
4. Check the real message headers (from the origin, not the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
7. Audit the reused 2022 and 2025 asset paths and the West Elm spacer dependency for staleness.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to confirm UTM parameters, 200 status and landing-page match.
