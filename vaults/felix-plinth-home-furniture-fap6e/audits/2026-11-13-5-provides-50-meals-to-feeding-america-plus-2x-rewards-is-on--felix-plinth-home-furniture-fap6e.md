# $5 provides 50 meals to Feeding America® Plus, 2X rewards is on
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and this is the first send in two weeks where I had to read the hero twice. The "Long Weekend Sale" banner I've been staring at since 10/30 is gone. In its place is a Feeding America donation story: "$5 provides 50 meals." It's a nice gesture and it's on-brand for the holidays. But I opened this email for a sale, and the first screen and a half is a donation ask with two buttons.
- The actual commerce is there if I keep scrolling. The Early Black Friday Sale is up to 60% off, double rewards runs through Nov 17th, and clearance is up to 70% off. That's a lot of promos stacked in one email, so my eye has nowhere to land. Pottery Barn's "Open Box Outlet Deals" hero sold me one thing in one block. Wayfair's "70% OFF EARLY BLACK FRIDAY" at least leads with the discount. I'd click the rewards block, since I put everything on the highest-rewards card, but I wouldn't forward this. It's a skim, and I'd save it for the Black Friday timing.

## What's working

- The "Double Rewards Is On" block is the strongest thing here. "Get 20% back in Reward Dollars" and a Nov 17th deadline are exactly the rewards math I care about.
- The Gianna dish tie-in ("$5 from every special edition Gianna dish sold") gives the charity angle a real product. That's better than a donation ask on its own.
- The Early Black Friday Sale block is clean. "Up to 60% off" is big and there's one clear Shop the Sale button.
- The moody, warm photography and the oversized type feel unmistakably CB2. It doesn't look like the Crate & Barrel Kids sends I keep getting.
- The footer is tidy. App download, 15% off for texts and the Crate & Barrel cross-link are all easy to find.

## What's weak

- The hero is a donation, not a sale. Two stacked buttons, "Donate Today" and "Learn More About CB2 and Feeding America," push the discounts below the first screen. This is a furniture-and-rewards shopper's inbox, not a charity drive.
- Four separate offers fight for attention: charity, 60% off, double rewards and 70% off clearance. Crate & Barrel's "Dinnerware, serveware & more up to 30% off + 2x Rewards" subject put one offer first and the rewards second. This one tries to do everything.
- The rewards headline says "10%" struck through and "20%," with a dagger after the number. I'm squinting at a tiny asterisk to find out what "20% back" requires.
- The fine print is a wall of text at the bottom. "Up to 60%," "up to 70%," "select furniture" and the exclusions all sit down there, and I can't tell what's actually included.
- There's nothing on shipping, sample or swatch programs, or showroom stuff. For furniture, I need to know what lands before the holidays. Article's Black Friday teaser didn't have that either, but it was also a one-note email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sale. Put the Early Black Friday Sale with "up to 60% off" and the 2X rewards deadline in the hero, and demote the Feeding America block to a mid-email feature. Donors and shoppers can both be served, but not in the same first screen.
- 2. Pick one primary CTA per block and make the copy specific, like "Shop furniture 60% off" instead of "Learn More" or "Shop Now."
- 3. Put the rewards math in a single readable line: "Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars on CB2 Visa purchases through 11/17."
- 4. Add a furniture-specific module covering the shipping cutoff for holiday delivery, the swatch program and the showroom locator.
- 5. Don't repeat the same hero across the week. Rotate between a product story, the sale and the rewards message.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early Black Friday: up to 60% off + 20% back`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2X rewards ends 11/17 (plus $5 = 50 meals)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off clearance, and every Gianna dish gives $5 to Feeding America.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% back in Reward Dollars on your CB2 Visa. Ends Nov 17.`

# Technical Audit: CB2, "$5 provides 50 meals to Feeding America® Plus, 2X rewards is on"

The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, link hrefs and UTM parameters could not be inspected. Findings below are limited to the visible source and the QA output.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with MSO conditional comments. Its main problems are 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi tracking images and incomplete alt text. The header-based compliance and authentication checks came back empty, which looks like a capture limitation rather than a confirmed defect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **56 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **11 images load over plain `http://`.** All come from Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-sync pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Impact of the http:// pixels:** HTTPS-only clients, such as proxied Gmail and Apple Mail with Mail Privacy Protection, may block or rewrite these requests, and some clients flag mixed content. The ten `pub*.php` calls are third-party cookie-sync redirects. Most email clients will ignore them, so they add payload and an external-domain dependency without reliable benefit.
- **First-party pixels** (`https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/1e7d0a73-...`) use HTTPS, so there is no protocol issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 content images**, all from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `f7012b47-...jpg`
  - `b5016d5d-...jpg`
  - `a160119b-...jpg`
  - `345f2993-...jpg`
  - `2c12b294-...jpg`

  If these carry content or links, they will be unlabeled for screen readers and when images are blocked. The QA output doesn't show whether they are decorative.
- **Tracking pixels lack `alt` attributes.** This affects `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel and all Dotomi pixels. Add `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden="true"` so they are skipped cleanly.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Text scaling is disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` prevent user text resizing. This conflicts with the `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` rule earlier in the same stylesheet.
- **Markup validity:** the doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, but the `<meta>` tags `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` are not self-closed. This is minor and clients tolerate it.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. It is harmless but shows an unreviewed template.
- **Font URL inconsistency:** some `@font-face` sources use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...` with a double slash, while others use a single slash. The fonts also load from the sister domain `crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`). All fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif, so impact is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated portion. The `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` parameters on the pixels look like resolved identifiers, not literal tokens. The remainder of the body is unverified.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat. If it is truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe would not be met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` is plausible, but nothing was verified.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so not assessable.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** the Dotomi pixels place identifiers through a third party. Check this against the sender's privacy disclosures and consent basis. This is a review note, not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 56 tracking links were skipped and the hrefs are not visible, so UTM parameters, landing-page alignment and redirect behavior are unverified. The email references the Feeding America donation offer and 2X rewards, so a landing-page match should be tested manually.

2. **Priority 2:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking and cookie-sync pixels.
3. **Priority 3:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Reconcile the conflicting `text-size-adjust` rules.
4. **Verify manually:** Pull the raw message headers from the original delivery, not the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
