# COFFEE MAKERS 📢 LAST CALL for UP TO 60% OFF ❗ ❗ ❗ 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- Sixty-five Wayfair emails — or close to it. And this one opens with the single most infuriating move in email marketing: the bait-and-switch. The subject screams "COFFEE MAKERS" and when I open it, I get a 70+ category sale hero banner, four category discount strips (mattresses, rugs, home office, bedding), a "picked just for you" product grid that appears to contain bookshelves, outdoor chairs, and colorful textiles — and zero visible coffee makers. Not one. The subject was a lie, or at best a rounding error.
- I'm renovating two homes right now. I spent last week looking at area rugs and home office furniture on Wayfair. A well-executed category sale email would actually hit — the June 20 72-hour sale landed an 8 from me because the offer architecture was honest. This one took the same bones and stapled a misleading appliance headline on top of it.
- Mark as read, immediately. Not clicking. Not forwarding. Actively reconsidering whether 65 Wayfair emails in my inbox is a life I want to keep living.

## What's working

- The red "ENDS TONIGHT" hero creates real urgency — same structure that worked on the June 20 send, and it still has pull even through my fatigue.
- Four concrete category discounts are visible and scannable: mattresses up to 80% off, rugs up to 80% off, home office up to 40% off, bedding up to 40% off. That's a useful cheat-sheet for someone with an active shopping list.
- "shop all sales" is an unambiguous purple CTA button — no "Discover," no "Explore the Magic," just a direct verb-noun action.
- Wayfair Rewards and Pay Over Time are both visible in the footer, which I appreciate — I'm running large furniture purchases through a rewards card right now, so the reminder to stack rewards matters.

## What's weak

- The subject promised coffee makers; the body has none. This is the bait-and-switch that erodes trust every time Wayfair does it — Pottery Barn sends a rugs email, I see rugs. Article sent me a Cillian Collection spotlight, I saw the Cillian Collection. Wayfair says coffee makers, I get mattress banners.
- "COFFEE MAKERS 📢 LAST CALL for UP TO 60% OFF ❗ ❗ ❗" is stacking every spam signal at once — ALL CAPS, megaphone emoji, "LAST CALL," percentage stinger, and three exclamation emojis. CB2 would never write a subject this desperate, and they're running the same category of sale this week.
- The "picked just for you" grid shows what looks like outdoor chairs, colorful towels, and garden items — nothing that maps to my browsing history of rugs and home office pieces. If the personalization engine ran my data, I can't find the evidence.
- This is at minimum the fourth promotional email from Wayfair in three days. "LAST CALL" urgency doesn't register when last call has been happening continuously since Monday.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the body match the subject** — if coffee makers are the hook, put coffee makers in the hero image and the "picked for you" grid. One hero product, one featured deal, then the category banners below. The June 20 send showed Wayfair can do focused offers; apply the same discipline here.
- 2. **Cut the spam stack to one signal** — "LAST CALL" alone is enough. Drop the 📢, the triple ❗, and the ALL CAPS product name. Article uses lowercase subject lines and I open them faster than I open this.
- 3. **Fix the "picked just for you" section** — surface home office and rug options since that's what I was browsing. The outdoor/garden picks visible here suggest the algorithm isn't firing correctly.
- 4. **Let the cadence breathe** — one email per day on a sale week is already aggressive. Wayfair has sent more email in the last 72 hours than CB2 sent in two weeks, and CB2's "What's old is new again" still got more consideration time from me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: coffee makers up to 60% off, tonight only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sale ends tonight — coffee makers + 70 more categories`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 80% off rugs, mattresses, and home office.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop your saved items before midnight — rewards points stack.`
