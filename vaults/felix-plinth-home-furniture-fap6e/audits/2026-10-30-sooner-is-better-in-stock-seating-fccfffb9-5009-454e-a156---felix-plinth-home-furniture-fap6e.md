# Sooner is better! In-stock seating
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and the maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is on top for what feels like the eighth send this week. It was the opener on the 10/29 "Artfully stitched quilts" email and the 10/29 "More to love 🧡" email too. I stopped reading the strip days ago. What I actually care about is the hero below it: "In-Stock Seating" with a "Skip the Wait" button. I'm furnishing two houses, so delivery in 1-4 weeks is the one useful fact in this email.
- The body is a clean grid of swivel chairs, leather, sectionals, armchairs and sofas. After that comes a "welcoming room" row, a "Recommended Just For You" block, a Christmas Shop banner, Design Café, the app, the rewards card and Affirm. It's polished, but it's a lot of stacked modules. The in-stock message gets diluted by the time I'm down at curtains and rugs. Article's "Seat selection" send did the same job (dining chairs, banquette, bench) with less clutter.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on the sectionals or the in-stock shop-all button, since I'm shopping for sofas now. I'd save it to my inspiration folder. I wouldn't forward it. There's no price, no sofa name and no fabric detail, so there's nothing to send a friend.

## What's working

- "Skip the Wait" is a strong hook. For furniture, speed is the pain point, and I like having it stated up front.
- The "deliver in just 1-4 weeks" copy gives me a real shipping timeline, which is rare for furniture emails.
- The category tiles (Swivel Chairs, Leather, Sectionals, Armchairs, Sofas) let me jump straight to what I'm buying.
- The styled holiday room photography is very Pinterest-folder-worthy and matches my taste.
- The 10% back in Rewards card block matters to me because I pick my card by rewards math.
- The Design Café "Book a Free Appointment" is the kind of showroom/design integration I look for.

## What's weak

- The maroon sale strip is the same opener I've seen all week, so I skim past it.
- There are no prices, product names or fabric callouts on the seating tiles, only category labels. I can't tell what's in stock.
- The email stacks too many modules: curtains, rugs, stockings, the Christmas Shop, Design Café, the app, rewards and Affirm. It reads like the whole site, not a focused in-stock send.
- "Recommended Just For You" shows a curtain rod, a dining table, a rug and stockings. None of it relates to seating, so it doesn't feel personal.
- Nothing mentions fabric swatches or samples, which I'd want before committing to a sofa.
- The "select styles" exclusions are small, and the fine print at the bottom is dense and hard to read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the in-stock story. Make the hero a specific sofa with a price, a fabric and a ship date ("Ships in 2 weeks"). Drop the stacked secondary modules.
- 2. Retire the maroon strip for a week or fold it into the hero. Repeating it in every send is training me to ignore it.
- 3. Put a swatch CTA next to the seating grid: "Order free swatches." That's how I'd actually decide.
- 4. Make the recommendations about seating: coffee tables and rugs that go with the sofas shown, not unrelated curtain rods.
- 5. Put the rewards message above the footer, with the earn-rate math spelled out.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas that ship in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `In stock now: sectionals, swivel chairs & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Leather, linen and performance fabrics, ready to deliver before the holidays.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus extra 20% off select styles. Order swatches free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based, with a hidden preheader and an SFMC-style click domain (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`). Its main technical problems are about 50 images without alt text, three cleartext `http://` image sources (one carries the recipient's address in its query string), and viewport and document-level accessibility gaps. The unsubscribe and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 58 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination health, redirect chains and 404s are untested. Example: the logo link `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded over HTTP. It may be blocked, and it exposes the recipient address in cleartext.
- **Address in other URLs:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is used as an `<img>` src and also carries the address in the query string.
- **Other non-HTTPS images:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Third-party and cross-brand dependencies:**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1` is an ad-network pixel.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain.
  - Failure of either host affects rendering or tracking.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,162 of 15,399 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 `<img>` elements have no alt, confirmed by QA. Examples:
  - The full `H26_FURN_B5_QuickShipSeating/slices_01–16.jpg` hero set. If the content is sliced images, image-blocking clients show nothing.
  - Product images such as `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg` and `belgian-flax-linen-curtain-1-l.jpg`.
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - The banners `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - The tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`), which should carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No language:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing table roles:** the outer layout tables (`<table width="100%" …>`) and the `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">` spacer tables lack `role="presentation"`. Some inner tables have it.
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with leaked server code: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS, probably harmless, but it signals a template build error.
- **Fragile selectors:** the `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` rules match exact class strings only. I saw no elements using those classes in the truncated source.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is stripped by many clients, so check the fallback stack.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was truncated, so the body below the header was not inspected.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The recipient address is resolved in the pixel URLs, and no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens appear in the visible source. The truncated body can't be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. QA flags that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that breaks Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the click and image domains, but alignment is not verified.
- **Footer:** the footer was truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All CTAs are wrapped in opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects, and the probe skipped them, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are unknown. The product image URLs point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…` (e.g. `…/202634/0007/toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`). These are product-image CDN paths, not landing pages. The cross-brand `edm.westelm.com` asset is the only continuity oddity visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all content images, especially the sliced hero and footer images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to HTTPS, and move the `svopn` pixel off cleartext so the recipient address isn't exposed.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and fill in `<title>`.
4. Remove the leaked PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers (or a seed to a mailbox that preserves headers) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
6. Resolve the click-tracking redirects (follow them without probing) to verify landing URLs, UTMs and 200 status.
7. Re-audit with the full HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and the rest of the body for unresolved tokens.
