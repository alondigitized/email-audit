# Now on sale & shipping free!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the 12th Pottery Barn email in about six days, and it's a pile of everything they had lying around. It has a Fall Sale banner, a "Save on Easy Updates" pillow hero, a "Recommended Just For You" grid, the Christmas Shop, the sweepstakes again and the credit card. I counted five different campaigns in one scroll. Earlier today I got "Nightstands starting at $349", which was one idea, clearly executed, and I scored it an 8. This one is the opposite.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block did earn a second look. The Banks Extendable Dining Table (76"-128") and the Cayman Platform Bed are the kind of things I'm shopping for across two houses. But "Just For You" sitting next to holiday stockings and curtain rods doesn't feel like it knows me. It feels like a bestseller list.
- Would I click? Maybe into the dining table, since I'd want to check the extension math. Would I forward it? No. It goes to the Pinterest folder at best, and only the table makes it there.

## What's working

- The "Up to 70% off" banner at the top is a real, specific number, and it's the first thing I see.
- The hero is a clean pillow-and-throw shot with a big "SHOP NOW" button. Nothing is broken or overlapping.
- The category list (Pillows, Throws, Quilts, Duvets, Sheets, Curtains) is easy to tap on a phone. It's more useful than the hero for someone outfitting two bedrooms.
- Naming the dining table with its extension range (76"-128") gives me a real spec to react to. Wayfair rarely does that.
- The 10% back credit card block is the only place the rewards math shows up, and I'd want it. I'd run this through my highest-rewards card anyway.

## What's weak

- The banner says Fall Sale while the same email pushes The Christmas Shop. Both are on screen on November 10, and it feels stale and confused.
- "Now on sale & shipping free!" doesn't say what's on sale or what the free shipping covers. The legal copy at the bottom suggests there are exclusions, and that buried fine print makes me distrust the offer.
- The "Design Crew Sweeps" $3,000 gift card is the same sweepstakes from this morning's "Want a $3,000 gift card for a home refresh?" send. Repeating it within hours is lazy.
- I get no sense of a deadline. "Up to 70% off" has no end date, so there's no reason to click today.
- There are no swatches, no fabric-sample info and no showroom or design-appointment framing for the textiles being pushed. For a bedding hero, I'd want to see color families.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Lead with either the sale or the Christmas Shop, not both. Crate & Barrel's "Psst… something special just hit up to 60% off!" at least commits to one clearance message.
- 2. Make "Just For You" actually personal. I've browsed nightstands and dining furniture, so show me those, not stockings and a curtain rod.
- 3. Say what the shipping offer covers and give a real end date. Article's "Black Friday's almost here" teaser was weak, but it at least had a single clear message.
- 4. Drop the sweepstakes from sends within hours of its first run. Move it to one dedicated email.
- 5. Put the 10% back card in the first screen. For a big furniture order, I'd run the numbers on that before anything else.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Dining tables & beds, now up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extendable dining tables, platform beds and more. Free shipping on sale items through 11/16.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 10% back with the Pottery Barn card. Sale ends Sunday.`

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Now on sale & shipping free!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout with fully wrapped click tracking. The automated QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). Alt text is largely missing and some assets use insecure HTTP. The HTML has stray non-CSS code in a `<style>` block, and the recipient address is exposed in plaintext tracking-pixel URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 56 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destinations, redirect chains and UTM parameters could not be verified.
- **HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=<email>` is non-HTTPS and may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- **Third-party pixels without alt:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/0e73fa15-...?mi_cid=...` are missing alt text. This is minor, but `alt=""` is the convention for pixels.
- **Mixed hosts:** the spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. It is not broken, but it adds a cross-domain dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 48 images are flagged, including:
  - `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and the `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_*` banners.
  - The `slices_01–07, 10–12.jpg` series under `/2025/US/0822_FRI_AM_LTOSale_FreeShip1/`.
  - `slices_01–18.jpg` under `/2026/US/Email-Footer/`.
  - Product images such as `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`.

  Because the email is image-sliced, all of the content and CTA text becomes invisible when images are blocked or read by a screen reader. Only the header logo has alt text (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`).
- **HTTP image sources:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the svopn pixel are non-HTTPS and may be blocked.
- **Stray code in `<style>`:** the second `<style>` block contains PHP-style text (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`). It is invalid CSS that some parsers may treat as an error, and it is sloppy template residue.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Missing document metadata:** `<title></title>` is empty and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Table roles:** the outer layout tables (`<table width="100%" ...>`) lack `role="presentation"`, while the inner tables have it.
- **Font loading:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is unsupported in Gmail and Outlook. No fallback stack is visible in the truncated source.
- **Stale assets:** the paths `/pb/2025/US/0822_FRI_AM_LTOSale_FreeShip1/` and `/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` are dated 2025 and 2022 and sit in an email whose other assets are under `/2026/`. This is likely template reuse, but the HTML cannot confirm the assets are current.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** are visible in the truncated source.
- **Email address in URLs:** the recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears in plaintext query strings:

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** were not found. QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`** was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` and the tracking domain is `click.e.potterybarn.com`, so the domains are aligned.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the source is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. All 56 tracked links are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, so final URLs and campaign parameters could not be checked.
- **Direct product links:** the product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, but the link targets behind them are hidden by the redirects.
- **Landing pages:** none were verified because the probe skipped all tracked links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content image, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels. Where text is baked into slices (`slices_*.jpg`), put that text in the alt, or use live HTML text for the CTAs.
2. Move the three HTTP assets to HTTPS: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the svopn pixel.
3. Stop putting the recipient email in plaintext query strings, especially on HTTP. Use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, set `<html lang="en">`, and add a real `<title>`.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the outer layout tables.
7. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy, to settle the compliance and authentication warnings.
8. Resolve the redirect chains with a followed-redirect probe to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
9. Check whether the 2025 and 2022 asset paths are intentionally evergreen.
