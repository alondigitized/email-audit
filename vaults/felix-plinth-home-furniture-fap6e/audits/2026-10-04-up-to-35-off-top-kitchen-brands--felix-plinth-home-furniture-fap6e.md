# Up to 35% off top kitchen brands!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the second kitchen send in two days, and it's another Crate & Barrel email I'm tempted to snooze on reflex. But it's better than the sofa carpet-bombing I got on 10/3. The hero is a Breville espresso machine with "up to 35% off top kitchen brands," and the "up to $300 off select coffee & espresso machines" line is a real offer. The screenshot is tall and clean, and the product photos are big.
- It does sit right on top of yesterday's "Our favorite coffee tech is on sale!" send. That one was the same Breville, the same 35%, and a near-identical subject. Same hero, one day later. I'm renovating two kitchens' worth of decisions, so Le Creuset and Shun matter to me. But I'd click once, save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder, and ignore the next three variants.

## What's working

- The hero names the offer: "up to 35% off top kitchen brands" with a Breville photo. I got the point in two seconds.
- Each product block has its own price: Le Creuset 6.5-qt Dutch oven at $170 off, Shun 20% off, De'Longhi $300 off. That's real math I can use.
- "Ships Free" tags show up in several places. That matters when I'm buying heavy cast iron.
- The "more can't-miss kitchen deals + free shipping" grid works as a second pass for people who didn't bite on the hero.
- The Le Creuset and Shun picks are brands I'd actually buy. They beat Wayfair's random pergola and pet-gate sprawl.

## What's weak

- It's the same Breville hero as yesterday. I saw "Our favorite coffee tech is on sale!" 24 hours ago. This is cadence fatigue, not a new campaign.
- The pumpkin-spice-bagels block is a swerve. It's a recipe video in the middle of a sale email, and it kills the momentum.
- The NFL game day section comes right after the bagels. Nothing in it connects to a renovation.
- The email is very long. The "we love these for you" product grid, the credit card block, the app download banner and TheDesignDesk all stack below the offer. I'd never scroll that far on my phone.
- The bottom-of-email block is tiny fine print, and the tap targets in the category nav look small.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are generic. They don't say what I'm shopping.
- Nothing here helps with kitchen renovation: no cabinet colors, no finish matching, no swatches. Yesterday's sofa sends at least offered free swatches and a design appointment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the tail. Drop the bagel video and the NFL section, and send me a shorter email that stays in the kitchen lane.
- 2. Don't run the same Breville hero two days in a row. If it's the same promo, send a "last chance" reminder with a new hook, not a re-skinned launch.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop espresso machines" and "Shop Le Creuset," not "Shop Now."
- 4. Add a renovation angle. Pair the cookware with a "kitchen finish guide" or free design appointment, like the sofa sends had.
- 5. Say the sale end date in the hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink-driven build (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) that uses table layout and VML/MSO conditionals. About 40 images are missing alt text, and the unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay dropped them. No hard failures were reported: the QA run shows 0 issues and 4 warning categories, with a 50% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 83 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status, redirect chains and landing pages are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests fire per open. The `s=` value looks like a hashed subscriber identifier, and `rlcdn.com` is a third-party (LiveRamp) domain. This is a privacy and deliverability concern, and it adds five extra image requests.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` is requested 6 times with the same `mi_u` value. That is redundant, and the repeats could be consolidated.
- **Identifier in URLs:** `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…` is carried in image URLs. This is a per-user identifier in URL query strings, and these URLs may end up in proxy logs.
- **Mixed image hosts:** One asset is served from `s7d5.scene7.com` (Adobe Scene7) instead of the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` CDN used elsewhere. Its path contains URL-encoded `%5F`, which is harmless but inconsistent.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged roughly 28 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `*.gif`, for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `0946a75b-efad-486c-bffe-ca1b923f1128.jpg` and `7c8c141b-bd61-4b1d-a124-15539fb14edf.gif`. It also flagged the Scene7 image (`2026%5F1002%5FCB%5FTertiary%5FVF%5FOoni%5FSocial…`).
  - With images blocked, the email would show as blank blocks and nothing a screen reader can read.
  - The truncated source doesn't show whether `alt=""` was set deliberately.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels also lack alt. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable text-size adjustment and set a tight global line-height.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - The `<title>` contains an unescaped `&` (`Crate & Barrel`), which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice, once as an unclosed tag in an XHTML document.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - A template placeholder comment is left in production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - Both `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules are duplicated across two `<style>` blocks.
- **Plain-text part:** 17,062 of 24,057 characters (about 71%) are URLs. This is mostly long tracking links, which makes the text alternative close to unreadable and can hurt spam scoring.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source, and the `mi_u` values are populated. The source is truncated, so the later sections weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping it, so it is unconfirmed rather than proven missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058):** One-click unsubscribe support is unverified, on the same relay caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, so a check against raw headers is needed.
- **Footer not reviewed:** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided. All 83 tracked links were skipped, and the visible source shows no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters. The email-to-site UTM mapping and landing page alignment should be checked by following the redirects in a later pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels. Start with the hero and product images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Capture the raw `.eml` headers (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
4. Resolve the 83 redirect links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations, UTM parameters, and any broken links.
5. Deduplicate the six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel calls and review the five `rlcdn.com` pixels for privacy and deliverability impact.
6. Clean the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, remove duplicate meta and style rules, and delete the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder comment.
7. Shorten the plain-text version's URLs so they make up less of the part.
