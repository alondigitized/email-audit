# ENDS SOON: Up to 20% off the BEST baby essentials →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Crate & Barrel Kids, which I honestly forgot was even a sub-brand I'm subscribed to — my inbox has been all C&B main and CB2 this week, so seeing "Kids" show up feels like a different list I didn't realize I opted into. We don't have a baby. We're renovating a second home and the primary at the same time, so a wrap-carrier hero image with a mom and infant does absolutely nothing for me. That said, I'll give it this: the layout itself is clean, organized by brand (Stokke, BABYBJÖRN, Nuna, Maxi-Cosi, Mushie), which tells me whoever built this actually knows their nursery-furniture landscape.
- But it's not my landscape. I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding — unless it's to my brother whose wife is pregnant, which, fine, that's a real use case for this email, just not for me. This is the classic "same parent company, wrong sub-list" problem, exactly like I flagged with CB2 riding C&B's coattails a few emails back. Straight to read, no action.

## What's working

- The brand-by-brand structure (Stokke, BABYBJÖRN, Nuna, Maxi-Cosi) is genuinely well organized if you're actually shopping for baby gear — no wall of random SKUs.
- "up to 20% off the BEST baby essentials" in the subject is a real, specific number, not vague sale language.
- The car seat / stroller travel systems section has a clean, styled lifestyle photo, not just a product-on-white cutout.
- No render bugs I can see — everything's laid out cleanly top to bottom, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to me — I have no kids, this is Crate & Barrel Kids not the home-goods C&B I actually want, and there's no "not you? update preferences" escape hatch visible.
- It's a genuinely long email — I count at least ten separate product sections before I hit the footer, which is a lot to scroll through for a non-parent.
- The "Crate&kids x Properties" clearance banner near the bottom is confusing — I have no idea what that partnership even is from the visual alone.
- Percentages vary wildly by section (20% off here, 15% off there, up to 60% off clearance there) with no single headline offer — it's scattered instead of one clear hero deal like Article's clean "3 Steps" structure I liked recently.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if C&B knows I've never clicked a baby/kids item, don't send me this at all. That's the single biggest lift here, more than any copy tweak.
- 2. Pick one hero offer and lead with it big instead of stacking six different %-off tiles — Pottery Barn's recent "on sale & ships free" hero was cleaner for exactly this reason.
- 3. Shorten the email — cut it to top 3-4 brands instead of ten scrolling sections; most parents aren't reading past the fold anyway.
- 4. Clarify what "Crate&kids x Properties" actually is with one line of context, not just a banner.
