# Love a good deal? Up to 60% off clearance is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is finally the actual Crate & Barrel mothership, not the Crate & Kids sub-brand that's been carpet-bombing my inbox all week (the Halloween shop, the $4,199 sweepstakes, the "bedroom furniture for their dream space" — none of which applied to a childless guy mid-reno). Beach hero, "up to 60% off clearance," category grid — this is at least talking to an adult who buys furniture for himself.
- But it's also a kitchen sink of an email. Clearance grid, then a bakery collab I don't care about, then a $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes, then a "we love these for you" product grid, then a credit card pitch, then an app download push. By the time I hit the actual furniture recommendations two-thirds down, I've scrolled past three different asks that have nothing to do with the 60% off headline I opened for.
- Would I click? Maybe the "Shop All Clearance" button up top, since that's honest and matches the subject. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is a specific enough find to send to a friend, it's just a scroll of categories.

## What's working

- The hero is clean — one clear offer ("up to 60% off clearance"), one clear CTA button, no clutter competing for attention.
- Category tiles (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding) are genuinely useful for someone furnishing two houses at once — I can scan and know instantly which section applies to me.
- "Ships Free" badge on bedding & bath is a real, concrete signal — I care about delivery cost/timing for anything home-related.
- The "we love these for you" grid at the bottom has actual SKUs with prices (Basque table $1,799, Anneli bed $2,299) — that's the kind of specificity that gets me closer to a decision versus another vague lifestyle shot.

## What's weak

- This reads like four emails stapled together — clearance, bakery collab, sweepstakes, credit card, app download. Compare to CB2's 8/8 fabric-swatch send, which I actually liked because it stayed on one idea. This one has no restraint.
- The Loveramics bakery collab is a total non-sequitur in a "60% off clearance" email — cute mugs, wrong context, kills momentum right after the hero.
- The Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes is the same beat Crate & Kids ran with its $4,199 prize email — I already flagged that as inbox fatigue from this family of brands, and here's the adult list doing the same trick.
- Credit card pitch buried mid-scroll with no rewards math shown ("earning Reward Dollars" — how many, what rate?) is a wasted opportunity; Pottery Barn's sale emails have the same problem, vague loyalty mentions with zero specifics.
- Clearance items themselves have zero prices or percentage-off shown in the grid — I have to click through blind, which is worse than Wayfair's slipcover email that at least put "Best-Selling" tags directly on the tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one job: clearance. Kill or move the bakery collab and app-download push to their own sends — they're diluting a genuinely strong offer.
- 2. Put price/percentage-off directly on clearance category tiles, not just in the hero banner — give me a reason to click each specific tile.
- 3. If the credit card pitch stays, show the actual reward rate (e.g., "earn 2X points") — a naked "better than ever" claim isn't a reason to apply.
- 4. Move the "we love these for you" product grid up, right under clearance — that's the strongest content in the email and it's stranded near the bottom.
- 5. Drop the sweepstakes or clearly separate it from clearance messaging — right now it looks like a distraction tactic, and I've now seen this exact device twice this week from this brand family.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off clearance — furniture, rugs, lighting & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just dropped: up to 60% off, while it lasts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns across furniture, decor, and bedding — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save up to 60% sitewide on clearance picks, today only while supplies last.`
