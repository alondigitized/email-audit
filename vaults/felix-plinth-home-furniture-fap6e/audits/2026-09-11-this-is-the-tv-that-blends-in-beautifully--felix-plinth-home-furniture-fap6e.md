# This is the TV that blends in beautifully →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, a Samsung Frame TV partnership — that's a new beat for Crate & Barrel, and I'll admit it caught my eye for half a second because I'm actually in the market for living room furniture for the second house. But the second I registered "TV" my brain filed this under "not what I signed up for from a furniture retailer." I'm not shopping electronics through C&B, I'm shopping consoles and sofas, and this feels like a brand extension nobody asked for.
- The good news is it doesn't stay on the TV forever — it pivots into "quality-crafted living room furniture" with a real category grid, which is the actual reason I open these emails. But between the Frame TV, the app-exclusive "our latest collab" teaser, The Design Desk $5000 giveaway, and the pillow sale, this is five different offers crammed into one send and none of them get room to breathe. Compare that to CB2's "THE FALL SALE" email I scored an 8 on — one hero, one offer, done. This is the opposite of that discipline.
- I'm not clicking the TV CTA. I might scroll to the furniture grid if I'm bored on the train, but nothing here is urgent enough to pull me off my phone. Not forwarding this one — there's no single clean idea to forward.

## What's working

- The "quality-crafted living room furniture" grid with sofas, consoles, accent chairs, and coffee tables is exactly my renovation category and it's laid out cleanly with real prices visible on some items further down.
- The $69.85 pillows with free shipping is a concrete, specific offer — that's rare in this email and it stands out because of it.
- Photography is consistent, warm, styled-room shots throughout — same visual language C&B always nails, no stock-photo feel.

## What's weak

- Opening with a Samsung TV partnership is a genuine miss for a furniture email — I came here for a console table, not a smart TV, and it buries the actual product category below the fold.
- Way too many competing CTAs stacked top to bottom: Shop Samsung Frame, Shop Media Consoles, Download Now (app), Book Your Free Appointment (Design Desk), Shop Now (pillows), Learn More (rewards) — six asks in one email is Wayfair-homepage levels of scattered, not the tight CB2 single-offer approach.
- The Design Desk $5000 giveaway section reads like a completely separate campaign bolted on — no visual thread connecting it to the TV or furniture content above it.
- This is the fourth-plus C&B send I've gotten this week, and now it's diluted further by chasing a TV tie-in instead of doubling down on the furniture categories I'm actually shopping in.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Samsung TV section entirely, or move it to the very bottom as a footnote.** Lead with the living-room furniture grid — that's my actual purchase intent and it's currently buried two scrolls down.
- 2. **Pick one offer and commit.** The pillow deal at $69.85 + free shipping is concrete and should be the hero, not an afterthought after four other asks.
- 3. **Consolidate the app/Design Desk/rewards blocks into one "more ways to shop with us" footer section** instead of three separate mid-email interruptions — CB2's cleaner sends don't make me stop and re-orient five times per email.
- 4. **Add prices to the furniture grid where they're missing** — some items show price, some don't, and consistency there builds trust the way Article's cleaner grids do.
