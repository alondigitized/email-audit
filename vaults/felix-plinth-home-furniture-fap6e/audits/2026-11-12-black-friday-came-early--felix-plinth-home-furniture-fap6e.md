## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "🎉 BLACK FRIDAY CAME EARLY 🙌"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive media queries and no unresolved merge tokens in the visible source. It has two confirmed issues: 20 non-HTTPS ad-impression pixels and widespread missing alt text. It also has one likely stale asset, and the QA run couldn't verify its compliance headers or most link health.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified, not broken.** All 8 probed failures are HTTP 429 (rate limiting), for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `.../area-rug-sale~e408698.html?eid=635911...` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app link. 429 is not a dead link, but these URLs got no valid verification.
- **4 links were never probed** because the 70s time budget ran out. They include `.../mattresses-%26-more-on-sale~e408728.html`, the two pergola product URLs and one more `/daily-sales?_emr=...` URL. Only 12 of 64 links were checked.
- **Mixed content:** 20 impression pixels load over plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS images will drop them. Ad-impression counts for these placements will be undercounted.
- **Non-HTTPS deep-link fallback:** the app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses `http://`. It depends on a server-side redirect to HTTPS.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the OneLink URL carries `c=Triggered`, but the same URL has `source=batch&batchid=2`. A batch send tagged as a triggered campaign will skew AppsFlyer attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA:**
  - Content images: `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-General.jpg`, `..._Skinny-Financing-Affir...`, `..._Skinny-Sponsor.jpg` and `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`. The first is a likely hero or linked banner, and the QA probe shows it is wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales/`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients get only the filename.
  - Star ratings: 11 `stars_9/10/11.png` images carry no alt, so the review score is not conveyed.
  - Tracking pixels: `b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` to these.
- **Stale asset:** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-...` is a Memorial Day (May 2026) banner in a Nov 12 Black Friday send. Whether it renders or is hidden can't be confirmed from the truncated source. If it is visible, it is a template carry-over.
- **Mobile CSS:** `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; ... height: 48px !important }` at `max-width: 480px` breaks product names mid-word and clips them at a fixed height.
- Everything else visible (`@font-face` Sofia with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, the Gmail `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` hacks, the Outlook app `body[data-outlook-cycle]` fix) is sound.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. `_emr` is constant across links (`ab0ac559-...`) and `_eml` is per-link, so the values are rendered and not left as literal tokens. The source is truncated, so tokens in the footer or later modules weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Not verifiable:** the QA run found no `List-Unsubscribe`, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and no `Authentication-Results` header. The QA tool says AgentMail may strip these headers, so treat these as unknowns, not defects. Check them against the raw `.eml` or a direct-delivery test.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link fall in the truncated HTML. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no probe result for it appears.
- The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, on a subdomain. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** there are no `utm_*` parameters. Attribution relies on Wayfair's own params (`refid=MKTEML_143498`, `csnid`, `batchid=2`, `varid=7`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16744`). This is consistent across links, but standard analytics won't pick it up without custom mapping.
- **Destination alignment:**
  - "Shop Sale" and the skinny banner go to `/daily-sales` and `/daily-sales/`. The trailing-slash inconsistency is minor.
  - The category links ("area rug sale", "indoor seasonal decor sale", "Christmas trees & garlands on sale") go to matching `/daily-sales/<name>~e<id>.html` pages. Link text and URL slug agree.
  - Whether those pages resolve and show the right content is unverified because of the 429s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to `https://` and add `alt=""` to them and to `b.php`.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the content banners. Give the star images ratings alt text (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars"), or `alt=""` if the number appears in adjacent text.
3. Remove or verify the Memorial Day banner (`WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_...`).
4. Replace `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name` with `overflow-wrap: break-word` and a `line-clamp`.
5. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`, and set `c=` to the actual batch campaign name.
6. In our QA tooling:
   - Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with backoff or a longer budget.
   - Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay before judging compliance.
   - Consider downgrading the relay-missing header checks from `[WARN]` to `[INFO]`.
