# NEW DEALS just added: up to 50% off Black Friday →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "another one." Yesterday I got six Crate & Barrel sends, and this is the "NEW DEALS just added" follow-up with the same "up to 50% off" number as the 11/23 "biggest sale of the year" email. The hero is a decked-out holiday mantel with a "Shop Now" button, and I don't see anything new in it. The only real change is the "HOURS LEFT to get it by Thanksgiving" strip at the very top.
- The screenshot is shrunk down to a thumbnail, so I can't read the fine print or the small product captions. What I can see is a grid that finally speaks to someone renovating two houses: sofas and sectionals, furniture, rugs, lighting and decor, plus a "up to 60% off clearance" block. Then it drifts into a long kitchen and Thanksgiving-table run (Moccamaster, cookware, drinkware), a TheDesignDesk sweepstakes and an app-download block. That's a very long scroll, and by the bottom I've lost the thread.
- Would I click? Probably once, on the sofa and sectional tile, to see if anything is worth saving to my inspiration folder. I wouldn't forward it, because nothing here is new enough to share. I'd mark it read and wait for the Pottery Barn and CB2 versions to arrive.

## What's working

- The urgency strip at the top ("HOURS LEFT to get it by Thanksgiving") gives a credible deadline right away.
- The hero is clear: a big "up to 50% off + free shipping" headline over a single "Shop Now" button.
- Sofas and sectionals, furniture and rugs have their own tiles. That's the first Crate & Barrel send in a while that feels built for a home-furnishing buyer, not just Kids.
- The "up to 60% off clearance" block gets its own full-width callout. A bargain hunter like me will go straight to it.
- The "ends tomorrow" label on the drinkware and dinnerware section is a real deadline, not generic urgency.

## What's weak

- It's the seventh Crate & Barrel send in about a day, and it repeats the 11/23 "Up to 50% off our biggest sale of the year!" message. "NEW DEALS just added" over-promises when the hero looks the same.
- Every "up to X%" label is a ceiling. The sofa tile says 60% and the hero says 50%, so I can't tell which number to believe.
- The email is about 12,000 pixels long. Thanksgiving tableware, coffee makers, a sweepstakes and an app promo all dilute the furniture pitch.
- The lead CTA is a generic "Shop Now" and the tiles show no products or prices. I can't see a single lamp or sofa I'd actually buy.
- Nothing speaks to rewards math, swatches, delivery windows for large furniture, or showroom visits. Free shipping is mentioned, but "get it by Thanksgiving" doesn't tell me whether that applies to a sectional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one furniture story and drop the Thanksgiving-table and gadget blocks. Show 3 to 4 real products with sale prices and a "ships by" date on the sofas.
- 2. Make the hero genuinely new: either name what was just added or put one real piece of furniture in the hero image instead of the mantel.
- 3. Pick one headline discount number and let the tiles back it up, instead of mixing 50%, 60%, 40% and 30%.
- 4. Use specific CTAs like "Shop sofas & sectionals" instead of a bare "Shop Now," and add a line about Crate Card rewards math.
- 5. Add a short delivery-by-Christmas note for furniture, plus a swatch or showroom link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas & sectionals up to 60% off — ends Cyber Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns on furniture + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order sofas by Dec 1 for holiday delivery. Fresh markdowns just landed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rugs, lighting and sectionals added to the sale. Free shipping on $79+.`
- For comparison, CB2's "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" opened with a $99 free-shipping strip and a sofa hero, so it got to furniture faster. Pottery Barn's maroon "Early Black Friday" banner has also hit my inbox repeatedly since 11/16, so Crate & Barrel isn't the only one repeating itself. Article at least kept its Black Friday sends to a couple of room-by-room grids, which is easier to scan than this scroll.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a heavily tracked Marketo-style/Movable Ink build (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) on an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed defect is about 46 `<img>` elements without alt text. The header, tracking-link and footer checks could not be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 83 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** The QA output shows these tracking beacons:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`: five identical LiveRamp pixels differing only in `n`.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b25ba48f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Identifier in pixel URLs:** The `s=` and `mi_u=` values are the same 64-character hex string (`dbe8da718e29…`), which looks like a per-recipient hashed identifier. Clients that proxy images or forward the email will expose it. Whether it is a hashed email address can't be confirmed from the data.
- **Pixel overhead:** Five LiveRamp pixel requests in one send add load and failure points.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 46 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, including `608f2d80-…jpg`, `4449d313-…jpg`, `9847b0f2-…jpg` and the GIFs `32d7f83e-…gif`, `631a8d87-…gif` and `07b7830d-…gif`.
  - Movable Ink images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png` and five others).
  - If these images carry the deal copy, images-off clients and screen readers get no content.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The `rlcdn.com`, `o.gif` and `dv.` pixels should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so they're treated as decorative. This is low severity.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template leftovers and markup:**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (typo, unfilled placeholder) is still in `<head>`.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared four times with conflicting values.
- **Global CSS:** The `*{… line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` rules are broad and can override inline text styling in some clients. This is a risk, not a confirmed bug.
- **Truncation:** The HTML source was truncated, so the body markup (roles, table semantics, VML/bulletproof buttons) couldn't be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the supplied source or QA output. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated, which suggests the Movable Ink dynamic content resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header:** Not found. The QA note says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` should be checked directly.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Neither is in the truncated HTML, so neither was verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 83 tracked links were not resolved and the supplied HTML is truncated, so no UTM parameters or landing-page destinations were observed. No continuity issues are claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 46 flagged content images, especially anything carrying offer text. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original message, outside the relay. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
4. Reduce the five duplicate `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels to one, or document why `n=1…5` are needed.
5. Clean up the template: remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment, escape `&` in `<title>`, and dedupe the `format-detection` metas.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and a redirect-following link check, so UTM and landing-page continuity and the footer unsubscribe and address can be assessed.
