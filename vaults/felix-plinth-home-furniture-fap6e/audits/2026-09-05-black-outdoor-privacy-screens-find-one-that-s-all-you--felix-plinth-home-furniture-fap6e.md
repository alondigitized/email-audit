## 1. Technical Summary

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 `<img>` tracking pixels use `http://` instead of `https://`** (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`). Non-HTTPS image requests are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients on HTTPS-only policies, silently degrading open/impression tracking.
- **26 images missing `alt` text**, including content-bearing creative (`wfus_0331_twbdpfy_2024_email_banner-gif.gif`, `...sku-rec-callouts-01/02`, `...shop-all-picks-for-yo`, `...sales-you-almost-miss`) and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels, plus the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open-tracking pixel. Missing alt on creative images degrades the experience for screen-reader users and when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail).
