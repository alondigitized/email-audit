# STARTS NOW! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a real Labor Day sale email, not another rerun of that Crate & Kids nursery furniture nonsense from yesterday — "Growth spurt? Get upgrades fast" and "The best of the best for their bedroom" both landed in my inbox with zero relevance to two houses I'm actually renovating. This one at least opens with real numbers: double Rewards, up to 60% off. But it's also just... a scroll. Sofa, dinnerware, dining, lighting, decor, kitchen, outdoor, rugs, a Crate & Kids section wedged back in halfway down, a bakery collab, a sweepstakes for The Design Desk. It's the whole catalog in one email.
- The chandelier shot mid-scroll actually caught my eye — that's the kind of piece I'd screenshot for my reno folder — and the "up to 60% off handcrafted rugs" section is legit useful since I need rugs for both houses. But there's no single hero offer I can act on fast; it's a wall of categories each with their own generic "Shop Now" button, and I have to do the work of figuring out what applies to me.
- Would I click? Maybe scroll to the rug or lighting section and tap through, but I'm not opening this on my commute and reading top to bottom. Wouldn't forward it — nothing here is a "hey check this out" moment, it's a sale blast.

## What's working

- Double Rewards + up to 60% off in the hero is a real, concrete offer stacked right at the top — better than CB2's vague "on sale" framing.
- The lighting/chandelier shot mid-email is genuinely aspirational and on-brand for a reno list like mine.
- Rugs section ("up to 60% off handcrafted rugs") is directly useful — I'm sourcing rugs for two houses right now.
- Sale badges (the little red ribbon icon) repeat consistently next to each category, so I always know I'm still inside the promo, unlike some emails that lose the thread halfway down.

## What's weak

- It's category soup — sofas, dinnerware, dining, lighting, decor, kitchen, outdoor, rugs, kids, bakery collab, sweepstakes. No prioritization for someone furnishing a home vs. someone buying a gravy boat.
- Crate & Kids gets its own section again, right after I flagged two straight days of misfired kids emails from this brand — I don't have a kid, drop it from my send or at least don't cross-post it into the flagship sale.
- "we love these for you" personalized product row at the bottom is a nice idea but it's five nearly-identical white ceramic/wood pieces — doesn't read as actually personalized to my style or my two-house project.
- The Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes and bakery collab feel bolted onto an already-long email — dilutes the urgency of the Labor Day offer itself.
- No credit card or loyalty-tier math shown — just "earn 10%-20% back in Reward Dollars" with an asterisk, no actual number I can bank on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with ONE hero category based on browsing history — for me that'd be lighting or rugs — instead of a flat list of eight categories in sale order. Article's Miila bed launch worked because it committed to one story; this email refuses to commit to any.
- 2. Cut Crate & Kids from this send entirely. Two kids-focused misfires this week already burned trust — don't compound it by folding kids content into the main sale too.
- 3. Move the Reward Dollars math up and make it concrete ("spend $500, get $50-$100 back") instead of a vague asterisked percentage — that's the number that actually gets a rewards-card guy like me to buy now vs. later.
- 4. Cut the bakery collab and sweepstakes, or push them to a separate send — they're diluting the actual sale urgency.
- 5. Make the "we love these for you" row genuinely personalized (browsing/cart history) instead of generic bestsellers — right now it doesn't feel different from the rest of the email.
