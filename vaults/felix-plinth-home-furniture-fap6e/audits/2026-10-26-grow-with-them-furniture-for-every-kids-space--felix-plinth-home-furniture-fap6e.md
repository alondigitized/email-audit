# Grow-with-them furniture for every kids’ space
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This one is a furniture catalog in email form, and I like it more than the last few Crate & Barrel Kids sends. The hero is a nursery photo, and the first thing under it is three named collections: Bodie, Finn and Hampshire. Each one has a lifestyle shot and a tidy grid of cribs, dressers and bunk beds. I'm renovating two houses, so a "pick a collection and build the room" layout is what I save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. It beats the 10/25 "Sunday plans: Bake up these easy fall recipes" send, which was a recipe magazine with a KitchenAid sale attached.
- The problem is that it's very long and the real offer is thin. "Free shipping on toys $99+" is the only banner at the top, and I don't buy toys. The "up to 60% off clearance" block sits near the bottom after a lot of scrolling. This is also the sixth or seventh Crate & Barrel Kids send I've logged in two days, after the Jenny Lind pair and the shared-room design guide this morning. I'm in permanent C&B snooze mode.
- Would I click? Probably one collection, to check the finishes and the GREENGUARD badges. I wouldn't forward it, because nobody I know needs a convertible crib right now. I'd save it to the folder and move on.

## What's working

- The collection-by-collection structure (Bodie, Finn, Hampshire) matches how I plan a room: one palette, matched pieces.
- Each collection has a big room shot followed by a clean product grid, so I can see the finish and the scale of the furniture.
- The GREENGUARD Gold Certified labels are a trust cue, which matters for anything going in a kid's room.
- The "Shop Top Furniture Collections" and "Shop All Furniture" buttons give me a clear next step after the collections.
- The Design Desk "Book a Free Appointment" block is the closest thing to showroom integration, and it's the right service for someone doing two renovations.

## What's weak

- The only banner offer is toy shipping at $99+. Nothing for furniture, which is the whole point of the email.
- The "up to 60% off clearance" block is buried below all three collections, and I'd have to scroll past most of the email to reach it.
- The email is very long for something with no deadline. Compared with Wayfair's string-lights Way Day send, which led with a $16.99 tile and a clock, this has no urgency.
- Furniture shipping, lead times and swatch or sample options aren't visible anywhere. For a crib or a bunk bed, that's the first thing I'd want to know.
- The app promo, the credit card block and "we love these for you" all pile on after the main content, so the email loses focus. It also feels like the same C&B template I've already seen all week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the clearance offer or a real furniture incentive into the hero. If it's "free shipping on toys $99+," it shouldn't be the lead in a furniture email.
- 2. Cut the length. Lead with one or two collections and drop the app, card and recommendations blocks, or put them in a separate send.
- 3. Add furniture shipping and lead-time info and a swatch or sample CTA under each collection.
- 4. Add rewards and member math, such as card or loyalty savings, near the product grids.
- 5. Cap the cadence. Fewer sends with one clear idea each will do more than another sibling of the same template.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bodie, Finn or Hampshire? Pick your kids' room`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cribs, beds and dressers that grow with them`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three top-rated collections, GREENGUARD Gold certified. Plus clearance up to 60% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See the finishes side by side, and book a free Design Desk appointment.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) XHTML table build with a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA data shows about 45 images flagged for missing alt text, plus a few boilerplate and markup defects. The header and tracking-link findings are inconclusive because of the relay and the truncated HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 80 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified, and no broken links are confirmed.
- **Tracking pixels:** The HTML contains these hidden tracking images:
  - 5 `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `n=5`. These are LiveRamp pixels, and the same `s=` hash repeats five times.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7ad65760-...`
  - These add third-party requests and may be blocked by image proxies or privacy features. They are not defects in themselves.
- **Repeated image URL:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` appears 6 times with the same `mi_u` token. That looks like a dynamic or recommendation image slot, but I can't tell from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - About 27 content-type images are flagged, mostly `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/.../m/1/*.jpg` and `.png`.
  - Two `s7d5.scene7.com` images are also flagged: `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` and `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  - Most of the remaining ~18 flags are tracking pixels, and these should carry `alt=""`.
  - The checker may be reporting `alt=""` as missing. If the content images are linked, screen readers and image-blocked clients get no usable link text.
- **Spacer image:** The `Rule_Spacer_40px` image is decorative and should have `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Markup defects:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, so it should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice, once as `http-equiv` and once as the HTML5 form.
  - `format-detection` meta tags are declared three times, with conflicting combined and individual values.
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in production.
- **Generic title:** The `<title>` doesn't match the subject line "Grow-with-them furniture for every kids' space".
- **Global CSS rules:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` sets `line-height` on every element. This is a known Outlook and Gmail workaround, but it may override intended line heights.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated, so this isn't a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool itself attributes this to AgentMail relay header stripping, so these are not confirmed sender defects. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Check the raw `.eml` headers before acting on this.
- **Sender domain:** `From: crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a branded subdomain, consistent with proper authentication alignment, but this is unverified.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 80 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't expose final URLs or UTM parameters. No mismatch is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to all linked content images. Use `alt=""` on the spacer and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it descriptive.
4. Remove the duplicate `meta charset` and `format-detection` tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
5. Review whether five identical LiveRamp pixels (`n=1` to `n=5`) are intended.
6. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers on the raw message, since the relay likely stripped them.
7. Rerun the audit with the full HTML and resolved tracking redirects. That would let you check the footer unsubscribe link, UTMs and landing pages.
