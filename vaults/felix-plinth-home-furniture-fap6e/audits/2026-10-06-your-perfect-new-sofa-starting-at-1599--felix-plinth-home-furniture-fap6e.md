## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint. The QA run shows 36 images without alt text and five third-party tracking pixels carrying a recipient-level identifier. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and body checks are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 75 tracking links were skipped from HTTP probing (click-redirect domains). Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party identifier sharing:** the same hashed value, `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`, appears in the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` URLs and in five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels. `rlcdn.com` is LiveRamp's domain. A per-recipient identifier is being passed to a third party on image load.
- **Redundant pixels:** five identical `448796.gif` pixels differing only by `&n=`, plus `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (open pixel) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/58dd95ad-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Together they add roughly 7 non-content requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 36 `<img>` elements. 29 are content images, mostly on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`. Others are `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/SP25_CB_UphBestsellers_leather_01` and seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. The other 7 are tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. If the content images carry the offer or product names, the email is unreadable with images blocked.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` set on `body`. The duplicated, conflicting rules suppress user text scaling on iOS.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` charset declaration.
  - Three redundant `format-detection` metas conflict with or duplicate the first.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is in the shipped source. It's harmless to rendering but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Narrow responsive breakpoint:** all mobile rules are under `@media (max-width:460px)`. Viewports of 461–640px get the desktop layout. This is inferred from the CSS and was not render-tested.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are resolved, so personalization appears to be working in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found. QA notes they may not survive the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for high-volume senders.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is a consistent sending subdomain.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section and could not be checked.
- The LiveRamp pixels with a recipient-level hash may need disclosure in the privacy policy (CCPA/GDPR). That is a legal check beyond what the source shows.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and final landing URLs can't be assessed. All 75 tracking links were skipped and no resolved destinations were provided. The only visible parameters are Movable Ink's `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`, which are personalization and analytics IDs, not UTMs. The subject's "starting at $1599" can't be matched to a landing page without resolved URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 29 content images, and `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions to the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. Deduplicate the five `rlcdn.com` pixels to one, and confirm the identifier sharing is covered in the privacy policy and consent.
4. Escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate `meta` tags and the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Capture the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) directly, not through the relay, to settle the compliance and authentication questions.
6. Resolve a sample of the 75 tracking links with a follow-redirect probe to check destinations, UTMs and the price match.
7. Test rendering at 461–640px widths, since mobile styles apply only at 460px and below.
