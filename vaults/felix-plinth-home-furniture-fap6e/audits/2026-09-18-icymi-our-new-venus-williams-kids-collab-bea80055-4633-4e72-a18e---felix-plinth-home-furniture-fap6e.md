# ICYMI: Our new Venus Williams kids collab! 🎾
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the fifth Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox this cycle, and I already reviewed this exact send once before today — same subject, same Venus Williams collab, just a different message ID. So either their ESP double-fired or I'm now auditing a duplicate, and either way that's a bad look. Setting that aside: it's Crate & Kids, not Crate & Barrel, so it's immediately off-target for me — I don't have kids, I'm renovating two adult houses. The "championship-worthy decor" and "powerhouse playroom" copy is genuinely clever wordplay for a kids' tennis-star collab, and the styling is gorgeous — that green gingham, the tennis ball accents, the varsity stripe bedding — but none of it is going in my primary or my second home.
- The one thing that did catch my eye scrolling down: "our Design Desk pros can help — it's 100% free." That's the same beat Pottery Barn hit with their "RSVP for Your Free Design Services" email, which I actually stopped for. Crate & Kids buries it at the very bottom after nine product blocks, though, where Pottery Barn led with it. Not clicking, not forwarding — this is a mark-as-read, and honestly I'm getting close to unsubscribing from the whole Crate & Barrel family list given the volume this week.

## What's working

- "the room I always dreamed of as a kid" is a strong emotional hook even for a non-parent like me — nostalgia sells
- The styled vignettes are cohesive — green, navy, and cream repeat across every room shot, so it reads like one collection, not a product dump
- Free Design Desk consultation at the bottom is a real value-add, same beat Pottery Barn used to good effect
- Venus Williams' own headshot and quote at the bottom adds legitimate celebrity credibility over a generic model shot

## What's weak

- This is Crate & Kids, and I have no kids — completely off-persona, same miss as the last two Crate & Kids sends I've read this week
- No visible discount, no urgency, no price anywhere in the nine product blocks — pure lifestyle browsing with zero reason to buy now
- Fifth near-identical Crate & Barrel-family email this cycle — my inbox is in permanent C&B snooze mode at this point
- The Design Desk offer — the most useful thing in the whole email for someone like me juggling two renovations — is buried at the very bottom, same mistake CB2 sometimes makes when it leads with color-story over utility

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this out of adult-furniture subscriber lists entirely — I've gotten this Venus Williams collab twice now, and if I don't have kids in the house it should never have landed at all
- 2. Move the free Design Desk consultation up near the top — Pottery Barn proved that's a stronger opener than another product grid
- 3. Add a real offer — even 15% off the collab for a limited window would give people like me a reason to act instead of just admire
- 4. Cut send frequency across the C&B family — five emails in one cycle is CB2/Crate & Barrel/Crate & Kids all cannibalizing each other's attention
