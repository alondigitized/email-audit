# New arrivals are here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 for the sixth time in about four days, and it's the same sale again. The "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off" banner sits at the top, just like the 9/30 "Holiday Collection" send and this morning's "UP TO 25% OFF CUSTOM & IN STOCK." The subject says "new arrivals," but the first thing I see is a sale banner. That's a bait-and-switch on the subject line.
- Once I scroll, the design is good. It reads like a mood board: the Loop chair, Scatola table, Muir chair and Wolcott bed, each with a designer credit. I'd save this to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. But it's very long, and a second sale block ("Fall Design Sale, up to 50% off") shows up near the bottom and contradicts the 25% banner at the top. Which one is it? Pottery Barn is at nine sends in four days and I've stopped reading them. CB2 is heading the same way.
- Would I click? Probably not on this send, because I've seen the offer five times already. I'd file it as a reference for the Wolcott bed and the Deseo sectional. I wouldn't forward it. If I were buying a sofa I'd already know about this sale.

## What's working

- The editorial layout with designer credits (Andreas Harlen, Mermelada Estudio, VUUE) feels like a catalog, not a flyer. That's the CB2 voice I'm paying for.
- Product names are labeled directly on the lifestyle shots (Loop Chair, Scatola Table, Wolcott Bed). I can screenshot these straight into a room board.
- The warm walnut, camel and cream palette holds together across every image. That matters when I'm matching two houses.
- The "More for You" grid has prices ($1,199, $899, $1,614) and clear product names. It's the only place I get price anchoring.
- The Rewards "Earn 10% back" tile speaks to me. I pick my card on rewards math.

## What's weak

- The subject promises new arrivals but the hero is a sale banner. This was the same pattern in the 9/30 "Holiday Collection is here" send.
- Two sales run in one email: up to 25% off upholstery at the top, up to 50% off "Fall Design Sale" lower down. I can't tell what actually applies to the Muir chair.
- None of the lifestyle imagery shows a price or a "shop this" cue. The Loop chair, Scatola table and Wolcott bed are all just pictures with a generic "Shop the Latest" button.
- The email is very long, with at least four "shop" buttons. The CTAs blur together and nothing stands out.
- The footer carries a wall of tiny fine print. It's unreadable on my phone, and I want shipping and lead-time info for furniture instead.
- The cadence is the real problem. Fifth or sixth CB2 send in four days, and the Crate & Barrel family sends on top of that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match its subject. If it's new arrivals, lead with the arrivals and move the sale to a thin banner. If it's a sale, say so in the subject.
- 2. Pick one sale message. Use a single offer with one deadline and drop the overlapping 25% and 50% claims.
- 3. Tag each hero product with a price and a link straight to its product page. Skip the "Shop the Latest" catch-all.
- 4. Cut the length by about a third and keep one strong CTA per section.
- 5. Add furniture-specific info like ship windows, swatches and the showroom link. That's what I need for a renovation.
- 6. Throttle the cadence. Send one email a week, not six in four days.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The CB2 "New arrivals are here" email has a visible raw merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`) and about 10 third-party pixels (Dotomi) loaded over plain `http://` with no alt text. Most of the other automated warnings (missing auth and unsubscribe headers) come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this source.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 69 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified.
- The Dotomi cookie-sync pixels are served over insecure HTTP:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- Clients that block non-HTTPS content will drop these 11 requests, and the cookie-sync they exist for will silently fail. Some clients may also show mixed-content warnings.
- The first-party tracking pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/a2e9b2a3-...?mi_cid=...` use HTTPS, so there is no transport issue there.

- 9 content images have no alt text. All are on `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `48a7e47c-…jpg`
  - `c144ba07-…jpg`
