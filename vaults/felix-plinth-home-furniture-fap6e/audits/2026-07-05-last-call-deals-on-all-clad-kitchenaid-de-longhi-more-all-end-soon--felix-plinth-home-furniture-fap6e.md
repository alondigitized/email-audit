## Technical Audit — Crate & Barrel "Last Call" Sale Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Functionally deliverable email with four confirmed broken webfont assets (403) and pervasive missing alt text across all product images. Compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are not captured by the relay, creating unverifiable SPF/DKIM state.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Broken webfont assets (403) — confirmed by QA probe:**
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/1e07d600-3787-4517-bd3d-b140f9e54ec2.woff2`
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/7ec25fd5-2752-4d84-b307-af3faabc21bb.woff2`
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/aaa41944-25b3-4265-80b2-5564efe9b2d7.woff2`
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/fd5f8f50-d539-4a7d-b893-99501d746ef2.woff2`

All four return 403. Clients that attempt to load these will fall back to system fonts silently — no crash, but type rendering will differ from intent. The 403 indicates the assets exist but access is blocked (likely a missing CORS/CDN allowlist entry for email clients or a moved asset path).

**22 tracking links skipped:** All click-redirect URLs were excluded from HTTP probing. Destination link health (e.g., whether sale landing pages are live) is unverified.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text: 44 images total**, including every product image hosted at `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, all pixel trackers, and third-party beacons. Notable groups:

- **Product images** (`/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`) — 34 images, zero alt attributes. Images-off rendering (common in corporate email clients, Outlook default) produces a blank email.
- **Pixel trackers** (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` × 6, `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` × 5, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/...o.gif`) — should have `alt=""` to suppress broken-image icons when blocked.
- **Dynamic image** (`dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c90595c9-...`) — no alt, likely a personalized or countdown element.

**HTML comment artifact in `<head>`:**
```html
<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->
```
This is an unfilled template placeholder left in production. Not a rendering defect but indicates the template was not fully cleaned before send.

**Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags:** `telephone=no`, `address=no`, and `date=no` each appear twice in `<head>`. Redundant but harmless.

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta:** Prevents user zoom on mobile — an accessibility regression (WCAG 1.4.4).

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header: not captured** by AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header for native unsubscribe UI; its absence (or relay stripping) means one-click unsubscribe is unavailable to subscribers on those clients, which is a Gmail bulk sender requirement (>5k/day threshold).

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): not captured.** One-click POST unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policies. Cannot confirm whether this is a relay capture gap or a genuine omission.

**Authentication-Results header: not captured.** SPF and DKIM pass/fail status for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is unknown from this relay path. Sending domain is `crateandbarrel.com` via `mail.crateandbarrel.com` — alignment cannot be verified from available data.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link are expected in the email footer; not visible in the truncated source but assumed present given sender sophistication.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

22 click-redirect links were not probed — UTM parameter presence and landing page health are unverified. The redirect domain (not shown in truncated source) would need inspection to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are appended before the final destination URL. Given the sale urgency ("last call"), a broken or expired landing page is a high-impact risk that should be spot-checked manually.
