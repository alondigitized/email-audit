# Something festive just landed on our app!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- So this is a phone-in-a-wreath hero telling me to download the app for a "holiday sneak peek" — on September 10th. I already flagged this exact framing as too-early when it hit my inbox the first time, and seeing it again just confirms Crate & Barrel decided the calendar doesn't matter this year. The actual furniture further down — that walnut dresser, the Barrett sleeper sofa, the swivel chair — is genuinely stuff I'd screenshot for the second-home mood board, and the "we love these for you" grid at least *looks* like it's trying to be personalized even if I doubt it actually is.
- But the whole email is doing three jobs at once — download the app, enter the $5000 Design Desk sweepstakes, shop pillows at $69.95 — and none of them win. There's no single offer I can act on in the ten seconds I'm giving this before my train stop. Compare this to Pottery Barn's "Seating, decor & more for a cozy living room" send, which at least led with one clean hero and no countdown-clock theatrics — this one buries actual product under an app-download CTA and a sweepstakes pitch.
- I'm not clicking. I'm not forwarding. This gets archived into the Pinterest-adjacent folder for the dresser and nightstand photos alone, and that's the only value I'm extracting from it.

## What's working

- The "we love these for you" grid has real product shots with prices visible — that's useful, unlike Wayfair's bidet email where the hero image was just blank white space.
- The Keane dresser and nightstand actually match my walnut/driftwood renovation palette — those two I'd genuinely screenshot.
- Rewards messaging ("10% back in Reward Dollars") is at least visible near the bottom, not buried in fine print.
- Clean grid layout, no overlapping text or broken images — technically solid render.

## What's weak

- Leading with "holiday sneak peek" on September 10th is the same premature-holiday move I called out on their last send — it's not urgency, it's just off-season noise.
- Three competing CTAs (Download App, Book Free Appointment, Shop Now) with no clear hierarchy — my eye doesn't know where to land first.
- The Design Desk sweepstakes ("$5000 Gift Card") reads like filler dressed up as an offer — no real discount attached to it.
- Pillows "$69.95 & ship free" is the only concrete price/offer in the whole email and it's stuck in the middle, easy to scroll past.
- This is the third Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox this week (on top of the Crate & Kids barrage) — cadence fatigue is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero: either the app download or an actual product offer — not both fighting for the top slot. Kill or demote the holiday-sneak-peek framing until at least October.
- 2. Move the $69.95 pillows-and-free-shipping deal into the hero slot — it's the only real, specific offer in the email and it's currently buried.
- 3. Cut the Design Desk sweepstakes down to a single line near the footer instead of a full section with its own CTA — it's diluting the actual shopping message.
- 4. Add a swatch/sample CTA near the furniture grid — for someone mid-renovation, "order a fabric swatch" beats "download our app" every time.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New in walnut & driftwood: dressers, sofas, nightstands`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: enter to win a $5000 Design Desk makeover`
