## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style template (`<!--V2-->`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` tracking) with 63 click-tracked links. QA data shows about 30 images without alt text and no captured compliance or authentication headers. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` are five identical LiveRamp pixel calls. They add redundant third-party requests and need a privacy and consent review.
- **Other tracking beacons:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` is the open pixel. `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c83ed945-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a second beacon. Both are expected, but each carries an `mi_u` or `mi_mid` identifier in the URL.
- **Mixed image hosts:** Most images are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. One hero image is on `s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/10%5FCore/20261027%5FCB%5FNSO%5FCherryCreek%5FPostOpening/...`, which is a second CDN dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 30 images** (QA WARN). This count includes the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif` ×5, the `dv.` beacon).
  - Content images (`bbf6f9d4-...png`, `42a98eb8-...gif`, `e5297c7c-...jpg`, `77d36002-...jpg`, and others on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`) will render blank with images off, and screen readers will read out the filename.
  - The Scene7 hero (`20261027_CB_NSO_CherryCreek_PostOpening`) has no alt text, and so do the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - For decorative or tracking images, use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables zoom on some devices. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice (once as `http-equiv` and once as `charset`).
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
  - These are low severity, because clients tolerate them.
- **Template remnant:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unresolved placeholder comment that ships in production.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** forces centered paragraphs everywhere and relies on inline overrides. This is a maintainability risk, not a confirmed bug.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The `mi_u=` values are resolved hashes.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN), and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Treat both as unverified, not confirmed missing. For bulk senders, Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the From address and with the image and tracking hosts, but alignment alone does not confirm authentication.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these were not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified. All 63 links go through click-tracking redirects that QA skipped, and the truncated HTML exposes no final `utm_*` parameters or landing URLs. The Scene7 filename (`CherryCreek_PostOpening`) suggests a store-opening landing page, but nothing in the data confirms it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images, including the Scene7 hero and the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. Use `alt=""` on the pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, the `dv.` beacon).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Deduplicate the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls, or confirm they are intentional.
4. Clean up the markup: escape `&` in `<title>`, drop the duplicate `<meta charset>` and `format-detection` tags, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy. That would verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
6. Follow a sample of the tracking links, with redirect resolution enabled, to confirm UTM parameters and landing page status. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full, untruncated HTML.
