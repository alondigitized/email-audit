# Starts today: Up to $300 off top coffee & espresso machines!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the second time in as many days C&B has hit me with the exact same "Coffee & Espresso Event" — I gave the 09-27 morning version of this a 7 already, and this looks like it's just... continuing? Same event, same banner, now with a scroll-forever wall of machines: Breville, Miele, a $2,000+ Jura, KitchenAid, even a Caraway one at the very bottom. It's not bad, it's just a lot — by the fourth "shop the Coffee & Espresso Event" module I'd already tuned out.
- The good news is the kitchen in the second house is genuinely getting redone, so a coffee machine upgrade is a real live decision for me right now, not a hypothetical. The Breville "$300 off a do-it-all fave in an exclusive color" caught my eye because it's specific — dollar amount, feature claim, and now a color hook too. But this email just never lands on ONE hero. Compare that to Pottery Barn's Thanksgiving Shop send I looked at yesterday — one clean seasonal hero, moody table, done. This is nine machines deep before I even get to "the ultimate espresso machine matchmaker" and a pecan crunch iced latte recipe.
- Net: I'd scroll it on my phone during commute, maybe screenshot the Breville and the sub-$1000 exclusive-sale one for my "reno inbox" folder, and move on. Not forwarding this — nothing here is funny or surprising enough to send to a friend, it's just a well-stocked catalog page.

## What's working

- The top banner is honest and matches the subject exactly — "up to $300 off top coffee & espresso machines," no bait-and-switch like the Wayfair cookware email that lied about its own subject line.
- Multiple real price points visible (the exclusive-color Breville at $300 off, the $200 off Miele-style automatic, sale prices called out like "Sale $749.99, Reg. $999.99") — that's rewards-math-friendly, I can actually compare against my card's cashback.
- Product photography is consistent, moody, on-brand — same styling C&B used in the SMEG kettle email I liked, dark countertop, warm light, feels premium not clip-art.
- The "we love these for you" strip near the bottom is a decent personalization gesture, even if it's generic.

## What's weak

- Way too many hero-level offers stacked — by the time I hit the third "Shop Now" button I'd lost track of which machine was actually the best deal.
- No mention of shipping specifics for appliances this heavy/fragile — for something like a $2,000 machine I want to know delivery timeline or white-glove options, and it's just not there.
- Zero loyalty/rewards-member callout — I'm assuming there's a C&B rewards tier and this doesn't reference it once, unlike a well-run program email would.
- The "Thanksgiving Hosting Guide" and Caraway teaser crammed at the bottom feel like a different email got welded onto this one — dilutes the coffee focus I actually opened for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to ONE hero offer above the fold (the Breville $300-off-exclusive-color is the strongest specific claim) and move the rest into a "browse more" carousel — CB2 has this exact bloat problem right now and it's not working for them either.
- 2. Add a rewards/loyalty line near the top if members get extra points on appliance purchases — that's a real lever for me.
- 3. Add a one-line shipping/delivery note for large appliances (e.g., "free white-glove delivery on machines over $500") — critical for anything this size going into a reno house.
- 4. Move the pecan latte recipe and Thanksgiving guide to a separate send — they're diluting a strong, focused sale email.
