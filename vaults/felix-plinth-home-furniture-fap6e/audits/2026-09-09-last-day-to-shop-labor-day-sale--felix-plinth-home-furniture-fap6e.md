# LAST DAY TO SHOP LABOR DAY SALE
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Same countdown clock, same "sale ends" hero, same category-button stack I catalogued from CB2 yesterday twice — "48 HOURS LEFT" became "01:07:10:29" and now it's "00:17:19:18" and "ENDS TONIGHT." I don't need a screenshot comparison to know this because I've literally already seen this template three times this week from this one sender. The sunset-gradient hero with the vertical button list (FURNITURE, OUTDOOR, LIGHTING, RUGS...) hasn't changed once.
- The one new thing is the credit card block underneath, and it's got the exact same "10% ~~20%~~" strikethrough typo-gimmick I flagged in yesterday's send — so at least that's consistent, if consistently sloppy. Between this and Pottery Barn's "LAST DAY" burgundy banner I've now seen a dozen times, and Crate & Kids' beach-sunset "Ends Today," my whole inbox is one long-weekend fire drill with different color grading.
- I'm not clicking. Not forwarding. This is the fourth near-identical CB2 send this week and my eyes have gone numb to "ends tonight" language from this brand specifically.

## What's working

- The countdown timer is legible and the "ENDS TONIGHT" urgency is at least honest — it really is the last day.
- Category buttons (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors) map directly to what I'm actually shopping for across two house renovations.
- No render bugs, no broken images — the sunset photography is clean and on-brand.
- Rewards program info is visible and relevant since I do use a high-rewards card strategy.

## What's weak

- I've seen this exact hero and button stack from CB2 at least twice already this week — "48 HOURS LEFT" → "ENDS TONIGHT" is not a new campaign, it's a clock ticking down on the same creative.
- "10% ~~20%~~ BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" strikethrough gimmick is the same one from yesterday's send — feels lazy, not fresh.
- No specific product shown anywhere — just category buttons and a lifestyle vase photo. Wayfair's dining-chair miss was worse (wrong product entirely), but CB2 isn't showing me anything concrete to click toward either.
- "Up to 50% off" with a dagger symbol and buried exclusions in 8-point gray footer text — same "honest offer" gap I dinged Pottery Barn and Crate & Kids for this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop resending the identical countdown-hero template — rotate in an actual product shot (a specific sofa, rug, or lighting piece) so it doesn't read as the same email I deleted twice already this week.
- 2. Fix or drop the strikethrough "10% ~~20%~~" gimmick — it reads like a typo, not a deal, and I've now seen it unfixed across two sends.
- 3. Lead with one hero category tied to what a renovation shopper actually needs this week (e.g., "Rugs — up to 50% off") instead of seven undifferentiated buttons.
- 4. Move the exclusions/financing fine print closer to the "50% off" claim so it doesn't feel buried at the bottom.
