# Double Rewards + Up to 30% off drinkware & more!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Crate & Barrel send today, and I've stopped reading these as separate emails. The Double Rewards and Fall Sales Event pitch from the earlier "Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅" and "Starts TODAY! Open to shop Double Rewards →" sends is back. The only new thing is a drinkware and entertaining skin on top. My gut was "I already saw this," and I was right.
- The hero, "host & toast: up to 30% off drinkware, dinnerware & more," is clean. The plated table and plaid-free styling beat the earlier plaid hero. But the rewards math is split across three places. There's a dark red banner at the top, a red block mid-email and a Crate Card tile near the bottom. For someone who buys on the highest-rewards card, I like the offer. I don't like being told three times.
- Would I click? Maybe, but only if I were already hosting. I'm renovating two houses, so a flatware and wine glass restock is plausible. I'd save it to my inbox folder rather than forward it. Nothing here is a product I'd text a friend about.

## What's working

- The hero reads in two seconds: "up to 30% off drinkware, dinnerware & more" with a clear Shop Now button.
- The 2x6 category grid (dinnerware, wine glasses, serveware, drinking glasses, table linens, flatware) lets me jump to what I need. Each tile has its own percentage.
- The "5 styles for your Thanksgiving table" block is real inspiration content, the kind I'd pin to my Pinterest-adjacent folder.
- The Crate Card tile and "Earn 10% back in Reward Dollars" speak to my rewards habit.
- The "we love these for you" product row has actual prices on specific glassware.

## What's weak

- I'm getting the Double Rewards message three times in one email, and for the fourth time today. It feels like noise, not urgency.
- The offer stack is muddled. The top banner says "up to 50% off The Fall Sale Event," the hero says 30% off and the tiles say 20% and 30%. Which is it?
- The email is very long. The Thanksgiving menu recipe block and the app download pitch dilute the offer, and the hero doesn't reach the product row until deep in the scroll.
- The fine print is tiny, and the rewards terms are buried in it. At phone size I can't read it without zooming.
- There's nothing on color palette, swatches or showroom beyond a "love it? see it at your nearest store" line. TheDesignHub gets a block but no real pull.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap Crate & Barrel at one send a day, and merge today's Double Rewards messaging into one email. This is the biggest lift, and cadence is the issue.
- 2. Pick one headline discount and make the top banner match the hero. Right now there's a 50% claim sitting over a 30% hero.
- 3. Show the rewards offer once, high in the email, and cut the repeats.
- 4. Drop the recipe block and app promo from a promo email, or move them to a separate send.
- 5. Show the rewards earn as dollars on a sample cart, for example "spend $200, get $40 back."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Double Rewards ends 11/17. Dinnerware, glassware and linens, all in one place.`

CB2 has the same over-frequency problem, and its eight sends since 10/30 are what I'm already tuning out. Crate is repeating that mistake today.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Marketing Cloud Intelligence-templated (MI) HTML email from `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`. QA gave a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 35 images), a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and template leftovers in the source. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay artifacts. The HTML was truncated, so footer and link-level checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp (rlcdn.com) pixels. They add a third-party request on top of the first-party tracking, and the recipient hash `s=` goes to a third party.
- **First-party tracking beacons.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c0f7d973-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (a second open or view beacon) are both present, so opens are double-counted.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` carries a recipient hash. These seven images appear to be dynamically generated per recipient. Check they resolve reliably, since they are rendered at open time.
- No broken links were confirmed, but none could be verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (confirmed).**
  - 18 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `296bfc38-…jpg` and `f2caf774-…gif`.
  - 7 images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, which are likely product or recommendation images.
  - With images off, or in a screen reader, these render as blank or as filenames.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels.** The five `rlcdn.com` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` beacon should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid or sloppy markup (confirmed in source).**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under the XHTML doctype, and it duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, with conflicting values.
  - A template placeholder was left in production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Global resets.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply to every element. They can override inline styles in some clients, and `text-size-adjust:none` conflicts with the `100%` set on `body`.
- The responsive breakpoint is `max-width:460px`, which is narrow. Widths of 461–639px get desktop layout rules.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` recipient hash resolves properly in the URLs. Coverage is limited by the truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN].** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it. It is unconfirmed, so verify against the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** The same relay caveat applies. If it is genuinely missing, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so alignment should be checked from the raw headers.
- The footer's unsubscribe link and physical address could not be confirmed because the HTML was truncated. This is not a confirmed failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 68 links are click-redirect URLs that were not followed, so the final destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Double Rewards + Up to 30% off drinkware" offer could not be checked. No issues are asserted.

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 25 content images, and `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), remove the duplicate `format-detection` and charset metas, and delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
4. Consolidate the duplicate open beacons (`o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon), and review whether the five LiveRamp pixels are needed.
5. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the original message headers, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
