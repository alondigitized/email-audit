## What's working

- The hero shot (pergola, string lights, sectional) is genuinely aspirational and matches my actual patio-reno headspace.
- "Keep it light" / sconce section gives me three finish options (bronze, black, cream) side by side — that's the kind of comparison shot that helps me actually decide.
- Furniture covers section is a smart practical add — most patio emails don't bother telling me how to protect the stuff I just bought.
- Clean visual hierarchy — each module is one idea, one CTA, no clutter.

## What's weak

- Zero discount or price anywhere in the body — after two weeks of Article hammering "40% off," a no-offer send feels like a letdown, not a palate cleanser.
- "Layer up" section has no visible CTA button at all — I'd have to guess I'm supposed to click the image.
- The "Furnish your space in one easy, stylish go" bundle teaser is vague — "Shop Outdoor Bundles" doesn't tell me what's actually in a bundle or what I'd save.
- No swatch/sample callout and no shipping-timeline info for furniture, which is exactly the stuff I care about mid-renovation on two houses at once.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero — even a modest "10% off outdoor" beats zero pricing signal, especially this soon after a 40%-off blitz trained me to expect one.
- 2. Add a CTA button under "Layer up" — right now it's the one section with no clear next step.
- 3. Make the bundle section concrete: name what's bundled (sofa + cushions + side table) and the bundle discount, not just "shop bundles."
- 4. Add a one-line shipping/lead-time note near the furniture cover or bundle section — that's a real purchase-blocker for me on furniture specifically.
