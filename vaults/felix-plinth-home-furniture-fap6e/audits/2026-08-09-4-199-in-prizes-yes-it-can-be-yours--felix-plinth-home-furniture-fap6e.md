# $4,199 in prizes?! Yes, it can be yours…
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Kids send, and this is the second one today alongside the "Shop furniture you'll love now & forever" email — my inbox is basically getting DoS'd by this sub-brand this week. It's a sweepstakes email: $4,199 in prizes, a shark-print backpack hero, a giveaway with a dozen partner logos crammed into a grid. I don't have kids. I have two houses mid-reno and a Pinterest folder full of dressers and lamps, not backpacks and Nuna strollers.
- The "$4,199 in prizes" hook is at least a real number, not vague marketing fluff, and I'll admit it caught my eye for half a second before I registered it's a baby-gear sweepstakes. Buried a few scrolls down is "up to 50% off summer sale" on beds/cribs/storage — that's the only line in this whole email remotely adjacent to something I'd buy, and it's competing with stroller car seats and lunch boxes for my attention.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — unless it's to my sister who actually has a toddler. This gets archived in about four seconds.

## What's working

- The $4,199 prize number is concrete and specific, not "amazing prizes!" filler — good instinct even if it's not my category.
- The shark backpack hero photo is genuinely well-shot and would stop a parent's scroll.
- The partner-brand grid (Bumbleride, Nuna, Oeuf) signals real value if you're in the market for nursery gear — feels legit, not spammy.
- "up to 50% off summer sale" on beds/cribs/storage is at least tucked in for someone renovating a nursery, which is the one beat that touches my world.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — this entire email, structurally, is invisible to me. Backpacks, lunch boxes, strollers, a "Baby Registry" footer link, none of it lands.
- This is the second Crate & Kids email in my inbox today, on top of the six Crate & Barrel sends I've been getting all week — my "C&B family" fatigue that I flagged on the CB2 "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" send is now compounding with a sub-brand I never asked for.
- The furniture content that could actually pull me in — the "up to 50% off summer sale" banner — is stacked below a giant sweepstakes block and a "Selling Fast! back-to-school gear" banner, so by the time I'd get there I've already checked out.
- Compare this to the Howell x Harrier designer collab send from C&B proper that actually got me to stop scrolling (7/10) — that one led with something aspirational and design-forward. This leads with a giveaway for toddler gear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely, or at minimum stop stacking Kids sends on top of Crate & Barrel proper — I've now gotten two kids-brand emails and multiple main-brand emails inside a week, and it's actively fatiguing me on the whole family of brands, including the parts I do shop.
- 2. Lead with the furniture sale, not the sweepstakes — if you're going to email someone with a reno/furniture purchase history, "up to 50% off beds, cribs, storage" should be above the fold, not scroll #4.
- 3. If the sweepstakes stays as the hero, at least caption it so a non-parent instantly self-selects out instead of scrolling four screens hoping for something relevant.
- 4. Kill the redundant double CTA — "Enter Now" appears twice back to back with the same partner grid; pick one.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your chance at $4,199 in kid-gear prizes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: summer furniture sale ends soon`
