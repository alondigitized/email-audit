# Up to 30% off The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Crate & Barrel email that's about my house. A dining room event with a 30% off headline is in my wheelhouse. I'm gutting two homes, and a dining table is exactly the kind of big-ticket item I'm tracking. The hero is a warm, moody dining room with a Sale $1,693 / Reg. $2,499 table callout. The price is specific, and that's what I want to see.
- Then the email keeps going, and it gets long. The sweepstakes, the app push, the credit card and the DesignDesk appointment all pile on after the event section. The screenshot is about seven screens tall on my phone. The "Shop Now" button and the four category tiles are strong. Everything past the hosting guide is competing for my attention. This is also the fifth C&B send I've seen today, after the mirrors, kids' chair, "Quality collections" and the Black Friday text-signup email. I'd click once, then mark the rest as read.
- I'd click the dining tables tile. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero names the event, the discount and a real table with a sale and regular price. I can do the math without clicking.
- The four-tile grid (dining tables, seating, storage, lighting) matches how I actually shop a dining room, one category at a time.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows individual pieces with struck-through prices, so I can compare before I click.
- The Rewards card block says "10% back in Reward Dollars". That's rewards math, and I like seeing it. I'll still use my highest-rewards card.
- The photography is consistent and warm. It looks like my Pinterest folder.

## What's weak

- The email is far too long. The offer is clear at the top and diluted by the bottom.
- The top banner pushes "only in our app: 20% off mirrors" above the actual event. I got the mirrors email earlier today, so this is a repeat.
- The "Thanksgiving Hosting Guide" tile has white text over a busy photo, and "Shop Thanksgiving" is hard to read against it.
- The DesignDesk sweepstakes and the "design pro is waiting" block appear twice. The $5000 chance and the app $500 chance are both noise next to a dining sale.
- No shipping or lead-time info for furniture anywhere. On a Thanksgiving-timed event, I want to know whether a table arrives before the holiday.
- The "20% off select lighting" tile quietly undercuts the 30% headline. That reads like fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the product grid except one block. Keep the card or the app, not both, and drop the repeated DesignDesk.
- 2. Put delivery cutoffs for Thanksgiving next to the hero. Furniture shoppers need a date, not just a discount.
- 3. Make the hosting guide tile legible with a darker overlay or a solid text panel.
- 4. Hold the app banner on days when the main event is a different category. Don't send me mirrors twice.
- 5. Space out sends. Wayfair and Pottery Barn run the same overkill cadence, and it trains me to skim. CB2's "One day left" dining email earned its place with a countdown. Crate could borrow that.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a lang attribute and MSO fallbacks, but it ships about 27 images with no alt attribute and several template-hygiene defects. The source is truncated and the relay stripped the auth and unsubscribe headers, so compliance and tracking could not be verified. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 60 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel repeated:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fires the same LiveRamp pixel five times with incrementing `n`. Each is an extra third-party request per open and has no alt attribute.
- **Identifiers in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and the `rlcdn` `s=` parameter carry the same 64-hex string. It appears to be a hashed recipient identifier. If it's a hashed email, it is exposed in image URLs that proxies and third parties can see.
- **Other trackers:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ece037ea-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open-tracking pixels. Neither has alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (~27 images):**
  - About 14 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `cdd300d7-…jpg`, `ef861136-…jpg`) and `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - One Scene7 image, `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary…`.
  - The 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - Tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""`.
  - With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, the content images have no text equivalent.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Redundant head tags:**
  - `<meta charset>` duplicates `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times, two of them with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` alongside separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`).
- **Leftover template text:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->` remain in production.
- **Overlapping media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider, the 600px cap never applies.
- **Dead CSS:** The `.mso-font-fix-*` and empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` MSO rules do nothing.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered merge tokens in the visible source. Only the unsubscribe and footer sections could hide any, and the truncation cuts them off.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found (WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post:** The RFC 8058 one-click header was also not found (WARN), with the same caveat. It is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a limit of the capture path.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML is truncated.
- **Sender domains:** The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` match, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. The 60 skipped links are click-redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs are not visible. Nothing in the data shows a mismatch with the subject, "Up to 30% off The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event".

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to every content image on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and Scene7, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
4. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` and charset metas, the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment, and the empty MSO rules.
5. Reduce the 5 duplicate `rlcdn` pixel calls to one, and check that the hashed identifier in `mi_u` and `s=` is not a reversible email hash.
6. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also resolve the redirect chains offline to check UTMs and landing pages.
