# Spotlight on Nuna: our #1 gear brand →

## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel family send I've logged today, and it's another one for a house with a baby in it. I have no baby. I'm renovating two houses, so a Nuna stroller hero and a car seat grid don't touch my to-do list. I'd skim it for the design layout and move on. Crate & Barrel Kids is a recognizable sender, but I'm not the audience.
- The email is clean and well organized. The "new & bestselling from Nuna" hero is clear, and the stroller, car seat and at-home sections each have their own shop button. Further down, "up to 60% off clearance" and a TheDesignDesk block are the only things that overlap with my projects. The design appointment is the one thing I'd actually care about, and it's buried near the bottom. I wouldn't click, I wouldn't forward it, and I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "free shipping on toys $99+" strip at the top is a clear, specific offer, even though toys aren't my category.
- The Nuna hero gives me a single focal point, so I know what the email is about right away.
- Each section pairs a short headline with its own shop button, which makes the structure easy to scan.
- The "Ships Free!" tags on the stroller tiles are useful. As a furniture buyer, shipping clarity is the first thing I look for.
- The TheDesignDesk "book a free appointment" block is a real service pitch. Pottery Barn Design Services did the same with "Free design help!" and I scored that 7.

## What's weak

- Strollers and car seats have nothing to do with a two-house renovation. The demographic signals are aimed at parents of infants, and I don't fit.
- The email is a very long scroll. The Nuna spotlight, the winter bedding, the clearance and the "we love these for you" section all compete, and none of it is anchored to a deadline.
- The "up to 60% off clearance" banner is the best offer in the email, but it sits halfway down and is lost under the Nuna content.
- I can't see any swatch, sample or color-palette content, and no showroom integration beyond the design desk block.
- The footer is a wall of tiny legal text and is unreadable on a phone without zooming.
- It's the fifth Crate & Barrel send today, and the second one from the Kids sub-brand. The earlier "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on toys $99+" sends carried the same free-shipping strip, so the cadence is exhausting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "up to 60% off clearance" block up to the hero, or lead with the offer, so someone who isn't a Nuna shopper still sees a reason to engage.
- 2. Cap the number of sends per day per household. Cadence is the biggest problem here, and Wayfair's "FALL CLEARANCE" email was repetitive too, but at least it was one email, not five.
- 3. Segment by household. Someone who has browsed accent chairs and rugs, like me, should get furniture content, not infant gear.
- 4. Raise the design appointment block higher up, with a concrete hook such as a free 30-minute room plan. CB2's countdown on its dining event email earned its place, and a similar clear deadline would help here.
- 5. Shorten the footer legalese, or collapse it behind a link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template (Marigold/"MI" platform) with VML/MSO fallbacks. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 48 images flagged) and a zoom-blocking viewport meta. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. QA scored 57% pass, with 0 hard issues and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped the click-redirect domains, so destination status codes, redirect chains and landing-page health are unverified.
- **Repeated tracking pixels.**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` fires 5 times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ef032a69-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are further pixels.
  - None of these has alt text, and they add 13 or more extra requests per open.
- **Third-party pixel receives a recipient identifier.** The `s=` value on `sr.rlcdn.com` (a LiveRamp domain) matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` token on the first-party pixels. A persistent per-recipient hash is therefore passed to an external domain.
- **Template leftovers in the head.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (note the typo) is still present.
  - There is an empty `<!---->` and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 48 images flagged:**
  - Content images: about 28 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|.gif|.png`, for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `e54a93c8-7d2a-47db-a056-2da30d46377c.gif` and `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`.
  - Scene7 assets: `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` and the `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` spacer.
  - Pixels and spacers should carry `alt=""` explicitly. If the content images are linked tiles, screen readers and image-blocked clients get no label for them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`** disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`** appears in `html`, `body` and `*{…}`, which blocks text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **Duplicate and redundant `<meta>` tags.**
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - The unclosed `<meta charset="utf-8">` is inconsistent with the XHTML doctype.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`.
- **Generic title.** It doesn't match the subject ("Spotlight on Nuna…").
- **Global `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}`** is a broad reset that can override intended line-heights, though `.f10`/`.f14`/`.f20` partly compensate on mobile.
- **Image width rules conflict.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap, and the later rule wins in the 768px+ range, so the 600px cap is overridden. The `max-width` cap also applies to every `img`, including the pixels.
- The HTML was truncated, so the body markup (headings, link text, tap-target sizes) could not be assessed.

- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain.
- The footer's physical address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source and could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 81 tracked links were skipped as redirect domains, and the truncated source doesn't show final URLs or `utm_*` parameters. Tracking relies on the redirect layer plus the `mi_*` identifiers, so UTM presence on the destination URLs is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `sr.rlcdn.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`, which also blocks text scaling.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and set it to the campaign subject. Delete the duplicate `format-detection` and charset metas and the `IMPUT HERE` leftover comment.
4. Collapse the 6 duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels and 5 `448796.gif` pixels to one each. Review whether passing the `mi_u` hash to `rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy policy.
5. Re-test with raw headers from the original SMTP message, not the relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the `Authentication-Results` pass/fail.
6. Run a redirect-resolving check on the 81 tracked links to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
