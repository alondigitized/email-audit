- **Subject line price anchor is real.** "Under $30" is a concrete ceiling — I actually opened this because I need affordable utility mats for both kitchens and that number scans instantly.

## What's weak

- **Bait-and-switch between subject and hero.** I opened for kitchen mats; the email leads with Open Box Deals. That's a broken promise in the first scroll.
- **"Open Box Deals" hijacks the entire hierarchy.** It's the biggest element in the email — larger than any product image, larger than the mat grid — and it has nothing to do with the subject line.
- **Category sprawl destroys focus.** Recliner chairs, ceiling fans, outdoor furniture, kitchen mats, open box items — every category manager apparently gets a row. Compare that to Article's dining room send from last week: one room, one editorial point of view, products that made sense together. Bookmarkable. This is not.
- **The "ready to relax?" recliner section is a complete non-sequitur.** I'm here for $30 kitchen mats. A green upholstered recliner in what reads as a standalone content block is jarring — it doesn't tie back to anything.
- **Cadence is at crisis level.** Twelve emails in roughly 48 hours. Pottery Barn sent forty this month but paced them; this Wayfair run feels like a fire sale panic.

- 1. **Make the kitchen mats the hero, full stop.** A grid of three or four specific mat images with actual prices under $30 visible in the email — not just "80% OFF" — would make this a genuinely useful email for someone in my situation.
- 2. **Kill the Open Box Deals section or save it for its own dedicated send.** It doesn't belong here. CB2's "AVAILABLE AGAIN" send from earlier this week worked because it did one thing; this should do the same.

- 4. **Show at least one real price in the hero.** "Under $30" in the subject is great — put a "$14.99" or "$22" on a product image in the body and I'm far more likely to click.
- 5. **Add a "ships by" date or stock note.** If these are open box items, scarcity framing ("limited quantities") would give me a real reason to act now instead of visiting the site on my own terms.
