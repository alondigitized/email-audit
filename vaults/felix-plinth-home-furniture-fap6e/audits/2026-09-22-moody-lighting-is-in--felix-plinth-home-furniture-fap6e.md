# Moody lighting is in
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the sixth CB2/Crate & Barrel-family send I've had to process this month, and for once it's not another "steals" or "sale-stacking" email — it's a designer collab drop, "A Whole New Mood" with Kara Mann lighting, and that's a genuinely different beat than the last four. The hero shot of five lamps staged on plaster/marble pedestals is moody and well-lit, and I actually like that they named the designer and gave her a bio blurb instead of just slapping "NEW" on product shots. That said, this is pure lighting — no color-palette guidance, no swatch program, nothing that speaks to someone mid-renovation trying to coordinate a whole room, which is exactly what I'm doing right now on both houses.
- It's well-produced, better than CB2's "STEALS OF THE WEEK" (5/10) from two days ago, but I'm not going to click today. I've got two houses of finish decisions pending and a single $650 table lamp isn't the unlock I need — it's the kind of thing I'd screenshot and drop in my inbox folder next to the Pottery Barn pillow email for "maybe later," not something I act on now.
- Would I forward it? Maybe to my wife if we're actually debating a reading lamp for the primary bedroom, but not to a friend as a "look at this" — it's not surprising or urgent enough. Mark as read, keep the folder tag, move on.

## What's working

- The "cult favorite designer" framing with Kara Mann's headshot and quote gives this actual editorial weight instead of feeling like another discount blast — a step up from CB2's usual "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" (7/10) approach.
- Moody, consistent lighting across every product shot — plaster, marble, walnut side tables — it reads like one photoshoot, not a stitched-together catalog.
- "Exclusively at CB2" gives a reason to care beyond price, which none of last week's C&B-family sale emails bothered to do.
- The "Don't see your fabric of choice? Order free swatches" banner is a good trust cue for a furniture buyer like me — Article's storage email (5/10) never bothered with anything like that.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing visible until you scroll way down to "More For You" — the hero lamps (George, Sandy, Bandit, Bonne) have no price tags at all, which is a hard miss for someone comparing against Pottery Barn's "$29.50 starting price" hero that landed cleanly last week.
- Nothing here acknowledges I'm mid-reno on two properties — no "shop the room," no bundle-and-save, no showroom/design-consult CTA beyond the tiny "Designed by Kara Mann" bio card.
- Six nearly-identical hero product blocks in a row (lamp, quote card, lamp, lamp, lamp) is a lot of scrolling before you hit any real CTA diversity — Wayfair's Halloween email (5/10) was scattered but at least varied the layout.
- The rewards callout ("Earn 10% Back") is buried at the very bottom below social icons — as a rewards-card-only shopper, that should be much higher up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put at least one price on the hero lamp — "$650" next to "Exclusively at CB2" would instantly tell me if this is impulse-buy territory or a considered purchase, and right now I have no idea.
- 2. Surface the "Earn 10% Back" rewards line near the top CTA, not buried under app-download and social icons — that's the single biggest lever for someone like me who buys on the highest-rewards card.
- 3. Add a "Shop the Look" or room-bundle CTA — lamp + side table + throw pillow — since I'm furnishing full rooms, not single fixtures.
- 4. Trim the repeated lamp-hero-lamp-hero sequence to 2-3 products max with a "See full collection" link instead of showing every SKU inline.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Moody lighting, exclusively at CB2`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plaster, marble, and glass — shop the collab before it sells out.`
