# Up to 60% off the Summer Sale, only for a limited time
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually made me stop scrolling — the hero shot with that round mirror over the Campagna Rattan dresser is genuinely the kind of image I screenshot and drop into my "reno inspo" folder before I even think about buying anything. That's real furniture in a real styled room, not a lifestyle stock photo of someone laughing at a salad. Compare that to Wayfair's "POOL TABLES under $800" nightmare from a couple weeks back where fifteen categories were screaming at me before coffee — Crate & Barrel knows how to let one hero product breathe.
- Where it loses me is right after: the sale-category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining, decor, rug, bed & bath, clearance) is fine but generic, and then it just... keeps going. New arrivals, Design Desk sweepstakes, "we love these for you" with six products, a credit card pitch, an app download pitch, a store locator. By the time I hit the Crate Card ad I've mentally checked out. This is the same sprawl problem I dinged CB2's parent-company sibling for — my brain is already in C&B-family fatigue mode after "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" scored a 7 from CB2 the same week.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the hero dresser is genuinely close to what I need for the primary-home reno, and "Shop Furniture Sale" is a real button. Would I forward it to a friend? Only the hero image, cropped, with "this dresser though" — not the whole email, which is way too long for that.

## What's working

- The hero image is the strongest single asset I've seen from this brand this stretch — styled room, coastal-neutral palette, an actual price ($1,500, reg $1,999) right in the shot.
- "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" is a real number, not vague "big savings" copy.
- The category grid under the hero (furniture/outdoor/kitchen/etc.) means I can jump straight to furniture sale without scrolling through kids' cribs I don't need — unlike the Crate & Kids emails that keep hitting my inbox regardless of relevance.
- "we love these for you" product grid actually shows dressers, nightstands, and dining tables — stuff a guy mid-renovation on two houses is realistically shopping for, with visible prices on every item.

## What's weak

- This email just does not end. Hero → category grid → new arrivals banner → Design Desk sweepstakes → six-product grid → credit card pitch → app download → store locator → footer nav. That's eight distinct asks in one send.
- The "new! 1500+ new heritage-inspired finds" banner and the Design Desk sweepstakes both compete with the 60%-off headline for my attention — I don't know which offer I'm supposed to care about.
- "we love these for you" implies personalization but reads like a generic bestseller rail — nothing here reflects that I'm mid-renovation on a primary AND a second home, which C&B could absolutely infer from my order history by now.
- The Crate Card pitch mid-email is pure friction — I did not open a sale email to apply for a store credit card.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one offer per email. Lead with the hero dresser and the 60%-off sale, kill the Design Desk sweepstakes and the credit card block entirely — those are their own sends. Article figured this out; their "Summer Sale starts now" send didn't try to also sell me a credit card.
- 2. Move "Shop Furniture Sale" / "Shop All Sale" buttons up near the hero — right now they're buried under the category grid instead of immediately below the dresser shot that actually earned the click.
- 3. Make "we love these for you" actually personalized — pull from my browse/purchase history (I've clearly bought casegoods before) instead of a generic six-pack.
- 4. Drop the sweepstakes banner below the fold or cut it — "$5000 Gift Card" sweepstakes copy this early reads like a distraction from the real sale.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The dresser you've been eyeing: 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer Sale: furniture up to 60% off, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on dressers, dining sets, and bedroom furniture`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Campagna dresser now $1,500 — plus 1500+ new arrivals`
