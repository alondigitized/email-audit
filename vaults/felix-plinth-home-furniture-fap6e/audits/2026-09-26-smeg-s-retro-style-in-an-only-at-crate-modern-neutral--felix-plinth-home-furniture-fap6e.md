## What's weak

- The email sprawls — SMEG hero, then fall decor event, then dining furniture, then random home goods, then credit card pitch, then app download. By the time I hit "It all adds up to a home you love" I've forgotten this started as an appliance drop.

- "we love these for you" grid (cutting board, wine glasses, dutch oven, mixing bowls) reads like an algorithmic filler block, not a curated pick — no rationale for why these specific six items.

- 2. Add price under at least the hero toaster/kettle — I shouldn't have to click to find out if this is a $60 toaster or a $250 one.
