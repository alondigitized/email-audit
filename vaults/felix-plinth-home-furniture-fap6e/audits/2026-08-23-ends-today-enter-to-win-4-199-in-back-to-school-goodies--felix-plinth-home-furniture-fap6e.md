# ENDS TODAY: Enter to win $4,199 in back-to-school goodies!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Second Crate & Kids email in my inbox today, and it's the exact same story as this morning's "vintage vibes" send: wrong list. I'm 36, mid-renovation on two houses, and a $4,199 giveaway stuffed with strollers, nursery gliders, and bento boxes tells me Crate & Barrel's segmentation still thinks every subscriber has a toddler. The backpack/back-to-school hook could've landed if I had school-age kids, but I don't, so it's dead on arrival.
- The one flicker of relevance is the "3 room themes" module halfway down — a bedroom and dresser tile that could've been dressing-adjacent to my actual project — but it's buried under a giveaway grid of nine baby-and-kid brands and a nursery glider chair. That's not a swap, that's a miss.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, not entering a giveaway for gear I'll never use. This one gets archived faster than CB2's "This sale ends tonight" did.

## What's working

- Clean masthead, single accent color, no render bugs — the layout itself is competent, unlike Wayfair's patio-heater email that buried its own subject.
- The prize grid (Crate&kids, Bumbleride, Nuna, etc.) is legible and the $4,199 headline number is a real, specific hook if you're the right parent.
- "New Crate Card" reward-dollars module at the bottom is a decent loyalty nudge — more concrete than most of what I've seen from this sender this week.
- App download CTA and "book a free design appointment" are reasonable secondary paths for someone who is actually shopping this brand.

## What's weak

- Nine of the ten product/prize tiles are strollers, car seats, nursery gliders, and baby feeding gear — I don't have a baby, I have a punch list.
- The "3 room themes" bedroom/nursery module is the only thing close to my world, and even that's captioned "woodland nursery," which just confirms the whole send is aimed at new parents, not renovators.
- Giveaway mechanic ("Sponsored in part by MOTHER") reads like sweepstakes spam bait, not a real offer — no discount, no percentage off, nothing I can act on today.
- Zero swatch/sample content, zero shipping-timeline info for furniture, zero showroom tie-in — the stuff I'd actually want from a Crate & Barrel family email isn't here.
- This is Crate & Kids again, same day as "Meet Beatrix" — that's twice from this sub-brand in one day, which is worse cadence than CB2's five-in-six-days streak.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely — I've now gotten two kids' emails today from a brand I only follow for adult furniture. That's the single biggest lift available.
- 2. If Crate & Barrel wants to cross-sell me from the parent list, lead with the bedroom/room-theme module (the one thing on-target) instead of burying it under a nine-tile baby gear giveaway.
- 3. Swap the giveaway mechanic for an actual offer — Pottery Barn's "Beautiful Foundations" email at least sold real bedroom collections instead of a sweepstakes.
- 4. Add furniture-specific trust content (shipping windows, swatch/sample ordering) since that's what actually moves someone renovating two homes.
