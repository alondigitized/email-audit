## What's working

- The "Way Day Oct. 24–26" banner with "up to 80% off" gives me a credible, bounded deadline at the very top.
- The category tiles read fast. Percentage, category and arrow, with large type that's easy to tap on a phone.
- Rugs, bedding, mattresses, curtains and entryway furniture match what I'm buying for both houses.
- The Affirm strip ("as low as 0% APR... through 11/18/2026") and the Wayfair Rewards link speak to how I shop, since I always check the rewards math.
- The "deals on big-name brands" list puts concrete dollar thresholds in text: "Delta upgrades: spend $250, save $50," "Sealy: up to $400 off." That's more specific than the tile percentages.

## What's weak

- The two pergola grids take up roughly a third of the email. I got "PERGOLAS 🟣 🟣 WAY DAY STARTS OCT. 24" on 10/21 and "PERGOLAS ❗ WAY DAY ❗ TOMORROW ❗" on 10/23. Same product, fifth send in about five days.
- "Up to" is on every tile. Without knowing which items hit 80%, those numbers don't tell me what I'll actually pay.
- Nothing shows a price or a specific product in the tiles. Pottery Barn's "Extra 20% off select styles" is at least one clear number, and CB2's dining event named a category and showed furniture.
- The brand deals are plain text with "Select items only" under each line. They feel like fine print stacked on fine print.
- The pergola photos have star ratings but no prices, so I can't tell what's discounted.
- The email is very long with many sections. The call to action is spread across more than 15 tap targets.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the pergola grids to one block, or replace them with tiles for the categories I actually browse (rugs, lighting, sofas). Segment pergola buyers out of the other sends.
- 2. Replace "up to 80% off" tiles with a real product and price for one or two hero items per category.
- 3. Put the Wayfair Rewards and Affirm offers in the first screen, since that's where the rewards math gets decided.
- 4. Group the brand deals into one visual block with a single "select items only" footnote.
- 5. Keep one Way Day send a day, not a pergola send and a Way Day send on the same day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a well-formed XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive media queries and per-recipient tracking parameters. The QA run found 0 hard failures. The warnings are missing alt text on many images, 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, and header checks the relay may have prevented.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** 12 links were probed and 60 skipped. Every probed link that returned a result returned 429 (rate limiting), which is a probe artifact and not a defect. These links include `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `/daily-sales/curtain-%26-drape-clearance~e403364.html`, and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me` app links. 4 links were not probed because of the 70s time budget (mattresses clearance and 3 pergola PDPs), so their status is unknown.
- **20 impression pixels use plain HTTP.** They run from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000&sz=2x1&m=false&p=35872c78-...` to `s=124126019`. Clients that block mixed content or HTTP images may drop them, which would undercount ad impressions. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS.
- **The app deep link carries an HTTP fallback.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me` URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Non-app users are sent to an HTTP URL, which then has to redirect to HTTPS.
- **The hero link has no usable link text.** The `US-WD26-Launch-ETm-Email-Desktop.gif` link to `/daily-sales/` appears in the QA output as `[image: US-WD26-Launch-ETm-Email-Desktop.gif]`. The anchor has no text and the image has no alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on about 35 images.** The affected images are:
  - the hero GIF `US-WD26-Launch-ETm-Email-Desktop.gif`;
  - 12 review-star images (`stars_9/10/11.png`), so star ratings have no text equivalent;
  - skinny banners (`WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-*`, `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH-Skinny-BigBrandNames.jpg`);
  - event buttons (`WFUS_261024_WayDay-LAUNCH_Email_Events_1x-Button-3/4`, 6 instances);
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
- **Some of these may be decorative.** The checker can't tell a missing `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`. That is expected for the tracking pixels (`b.php`, `imp`). It is not acceptable for the hero, banner, button and star images, which are linked or carry information.
- **Imagery-dependent content is at risk.** The hero and event buttons are image-only links, so with images blocked the email conveys little.
- **Structure looks sound.** The XHTML doctype, the `@font-face` for Sofia with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, and the 480px/320px breakpoints are present. The source was truncated, so the body markup, `lang` attribute and `role="presentation"` usage could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens are visible in the supplied source or in the probed URLs. Per-link `_eml` values and the `_emr=35872c78-...` recipient ID are populated. The source was truncated, so the body copy could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** Both headers are absent from the captured data. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unverified and not a confirmed sender defect. Check the raw message to confirm. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for one-click unsubscribe are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`. This is a relay limitation and not an evidenced failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA engine always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The footer was cut off in the supplied source, so the unsubscribe link and postal address were not directly inspected.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are present.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters instead: `refid=MKTEML_142223`, `emlid=101`, `csnid`, `batchid`, `varid=11`, `wfcs=cs7`, `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid`. This works if Wayfair's analytics ingest them, but the campaign can't be attributed in third-party analytics. For example, `af_sub5=AppEmail` on the app link is the only source tag visible outside Wayfair's own parameters.
- **Landing targets are consistent with the "Way Day" send.** The hero and "Shop Sale" go to `/daily-sales`, and the event tiles go to `/daily-sales/*-clearance~e4033xx.html` with matching `eid` and `unifiedeventid` (e.g. `eid=633054` and `unifiedeventid=633054`). One hero link uses `/daily-sales/` (trailing slash) and "Shop Sale" uses `/daily-sales`. A redirect would be harmless, but none was verified.
- **Probe results can't confirm the landing pages.** The 429 responses mean page content and status were not validated.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero GIF and the banner and event-button images, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels. Give the star images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", or move the rating into text.
3. Inspect the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
4. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links and the 429'd links with throttling or retries to confirm status 200.
5. Consider adding `utm_source/medium/campaign` alongside `refid` to improve cross-tool attribution.
