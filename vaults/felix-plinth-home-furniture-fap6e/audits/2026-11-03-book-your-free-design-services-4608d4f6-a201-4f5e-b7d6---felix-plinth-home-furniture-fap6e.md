# Book your FREE design services
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the first one in a week that isn't led by the maroon "Extra 20% off" strip. After the 11/02 "Don't miss your chance for EXTRA 20% OFF" send, a free design consult is a relief. I'm renovating two houses at once, so "RSVP for Your Free Design Services" speaks to me. The hero is clean: Design Crew, what/when/where, and one maroon "Book a free appointment" button.
- It then turns into a catalog. Free in-store classes, a "Recommended just for you" grid, the credit card, Affirm and the app. Stockings show up twice in the grid. The "just for you" grid is mostly holiday stockings, a curtain rod, a dining table, a desk and a rug. None of it comes with a price or a deal.
- Would I click? Yes, probably the booking button. I'd also save it to my Pinterest folder for the Livingston desk. I wouldn't forward it. A free design service is useful, but there's no example of what a consult gets me, so I can't tell a friend why to bother.

## What's working

- The hero is one idea with one button. "Book a free appointment" is clear and the maroon stands out against the soft toile image.
- What/when/where answers my three questions at once, and "in your home, virtually, or in-store" fits my two-house schedule.
- The class schedule has real dates, like Nov 8 Cozy Living Room and Nov 22 Trim a Tree. That's credible urgency without a fake countdown.
- No "Extra 20% off" strip this time. After four sends of it, this feels like a different brand.
- The rewards card block with "up to 10% back" is there. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I noticed it.

## What's weak

- "Recommended just for you" doesn't feel personal. Two stocking tiles, a curtain rod, a rug. Nothing shows my renovation or the Banks table I'd actually look at.
- No prices anywhere in the product grid. Crate & Barrel and Article at least show a number.
- Nothing about what design services includes. There's no sample room, no designer, no "what I'd get", and no mention of swatch or sample programs, which matters for a renovation.
- The bottom third is a wall of legal and card fine print. It's tiny and I can't read it without zooming.
- The classes are in-store only on Sunday mornings. If I'm not near a store, that whole section is wasted on me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show a result. Put a before/after room or a designer card with a name under the hero, so "free design services" becomes something I can picture.
- 2. Make the grid actually personal. Use what I browsed (dining, desk, rug) and add prices and a sample or swatch link.
- 3. Cut the app, Affirm and legal blocks down, or move them to a footer link. Keep the rewards line, since that's my buying logic.
- 4. Add a "how it works" line: book, consult, get a shoppable room plan. Include a shipping or lead-time note for large furniture.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based SFMC send. Its link plumbing is opaque but consistent. Its main defects are about 30 images with missing or empty `alt`, stray non-CSS text inside a `<style>` block, two `http://` image sources, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in pixel and image URLs. Authentication and one-click unsubscribe headers could not be verified from the relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 41 tracking links (`https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=ABp7Im…`) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Third-party pixels are embedded as `<img>` tags:
  - `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501…`
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true…`
  - These add ad-network requests to the send and are likely blocked by Apple MPP and privacy clients.
- A commented-out block still ships in the HTML. It holds a `potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?…external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner` link. It is inert but dead payload.
- `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a cross-brand asset host. A West Elm CDN dependency in a Pottery Barn send is a fragility risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray code in CSS.** The second `<style>` block starts with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …"); …` before `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {…}`. This is not valid CSS. Per CSS error recovery it is parsed as part of the next selector's prelude, so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset is likely dropped. Apple Mail would then auto-style detected links.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - The QA check flags about 30 images, including all `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`, product images such as `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and `3ceb0c96-….jpg`.
  - The hero images `slices_01.jpg` and `slices_02.jpg` have `alt=""` inside `<a>` tags. The links have no accessible name, and the offer is unreadable with images off.
  - Tracking pixels without `alt` are low impact.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** Two images load over `http://`: `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `…/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Both may be blocked or rewritten by client proxies. The second is a 2022 asset, so it may be stale.
- **Document structure.**
  - There is no `<title>` in `<head>` and no `lang` attribute on `<html xmlns=…>`.
  - Most layout tables lack `role="presentation"`. Only the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` table has it.
  - Comments precede the DOCTYPE (`<!-- begin dynamic ampScript slot -->`), which can trigger quirks mode in older Outlook and IE-based renderers.
- **Responsive rules may not apply.** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`, but no visible table or image carries those classes. The hero tables are fixed `width="650"`. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed for the rest of the email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens or AMPscript strings were found. The AMPscript slot is empty and rendered cleanly.
- **PII in URLs.** The recipient's email address appears in plaintext query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The first is fetched on open and the second goes to a third-party-style open-tracking host. Both expose the address to proxies, CDNs and logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`, consistent with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`.
- The footer is built from 18 unlabeled image slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). The truncated source does not show a text unsubscribe link or a physical postal address. If they exist only inside the images, they are invisible with images blocked and inaccessible to screen readers. This needs verification against the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every destination is wrapped in an opaque `qs=` token, so UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be verified from the source or the probe. The only plain destination, in the commented-out block, uses `cm_ite` and `external_id`, not `utm_*`.
- Nothing contradicts the subject ("Book your FREE design services"), but the click-through is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule applies.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked hero images (e.g. "Book your free design services") and to the product and footer slices. Use `alt=""` only on spacers and tracking pixels.
3. Move the two `http://` images to HTTPS and replace the 2022 banner asset.
4. Hash or token the recipient email in the `coreemail.html` and `svopn` URLs instead of passing it in plaintext.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message, not the relay copy. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the raw headers.
6. Make sure the unsubscribe link and postal address exist as live text in the footer, not only inside images.
7. Add `<title>`, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on layout tables. Move the AMPscript comments after the DOCTYPE.
8. Check the `wrapper` and `photo` class hooks against the full HTML. Add them to the tables and images, or drop the dead media-query rules.
9. Extend the QA probe to resolve the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects with a GET so destinations and UTMs can be checked.
