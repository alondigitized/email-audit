# Favorite toys up to 30% off 🧸🪁🦄🏰🚂→
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel Kids again. By my count this is the tenth C&B-family send this week, and the sender line says "Kids" in all caps next to "BLACK FRIDAY." I'm renovating two houses and I don't have kids. The subject's row of toy emoji told me before I opened it that this wasn't for me.
- Once I opened it, the email is actually better built than the title suggests. The hero says "up to 30% off toys + free shipping" and is followed by a tidy list of sale categories. The category list includes Furniture Sale, Rugs Sale and Finishing Touches Sale, and that's the first thing in here that applies to my project. It's still a kids-first email, and I've now seen four C&B sends today. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Biggest sale of the year! up to 30% off toys + free shipping" headline states the offer plainly.
- The category link grid (Furniture Sale, Rugs Sale, Finishing Touches Sale, Holiday Sale) lets me skip the nursery stuff and go straight to what I'd shop.
- The "Up to 50% off rugs" block with a "Shop the Sale" button is the one thing I'd click. I'm buying rugs for two houses.
- The "Crate&Barrel Kids" logo and the "Don't miss out: 677,678 people are shopping Black Friday now" banner at the top make the urgency feel real.
- The Reward Dollars credit card block fits how I shop. I always pick the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- Almost every product is a nursery item: a baby play mat, a stuffed elephant, a kids' coat. None of it is for a guy furnishing two houses.
- The hero is a red-ribbon gift photo with no product in it. I have to read the copy to learn what's on sale.
- The email is long, and the legal and fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. The "free shipping" and "up to" claims come with a lot of exclusions, and I'd have to dig for them.
- It's too much at once: an app download, TheDesignDesk, a store locator, a credit card ad, and two sale blocks. That dilutes the rug offer.
- The "we love these for you" picks aren't personalized to a renovator. It treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send. People with no kids' purchase history should get the rugs, furniture and finishing-touches blocks first, not a kids' hero. For people like me, the C&B parent brand should send one email, not four.
- 2. Put the product in the hero. A rug photo with "up to 50% off" would beat a ribbon.
- 3. Cut the app, TheDesignDesk and card blocks down to one secondary module. Move the legal text behind a "see details" link.
- 4. Say the shipping terms up front. For furniture, I need delivery windows more than a toy percentage.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and `lang="en"`. The automated QA found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The confirmed problems are missing alt text on about 34 images and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 74 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are tracking or click-redirect domains. Destination health, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical LiveRamp beacons are present, differing only in `n=1` to `n=5`: `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1`. They are extra third-party requests, and they have no alt text.
- **Other tracking images:** the Movable Ink pixel `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c54813a5-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carry per-recipient identifiers in the URL. They are standard for an ESP and not defects.
- **Repeated image:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. It is likely a dynamic Movable Ink module, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 34 `<img>` elements were flagged. About 21 are content images, such as `…/m/1/27e4cabc-2ec5-4213-a5bc-05a2f323a573.jpg` and the animated `Video%5FEdit%5FBF3%5FToys%5FFinal.gif`. The `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` spacer is also flagged. About 13 are tracking pixels.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`. The pixels and spacer should have `alt=""`, and the content images need descriptive alt text.
  - If the content images are also the promotional text, the offer is invisible to image-blocked clients and screen readers.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Template leftovers:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block remain in the source. `format-detection` is declared three times, once combined and twice separately.
- **Conflicting CSS:** the `img {max-width}` rules for `min-width:640px` and `min-width:768px` conflict (640px vs 600px).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both headers were not found. QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so I can't call this a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this needs checking against raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It is likely stripped by the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** both are outside the truncated source, so I can't verify them.
- **Sender domain:** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with the `image.mail.` and `mi.` hosts used for assets.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 74 tracked links were skipped, so no destination URLs or UTM parameters were observed. The sample shows only redirect-wrapped links, and the final landing pages are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all content images and `alt=""` to pixels and spacers. Priority: the 21 listed in the QA output.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and delete the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment and duplicate `format-detection` tags.
4. Re-run QA against the raw MIME to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Until then, treat those three warnings as unconfirmed.
5. Resolve the 74 redirect links in a follow-up crawl to check destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Capture the full HTML, because the truncation blocks checks on the footer, merge tokens and link markup.
