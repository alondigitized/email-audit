## What's weak

- I'm not a parent — this is Crate & Kids content landing in an inbox that should know I'm mid-renovation on two adult homes, not furnishing a nursery.
- Five straight emails now from the Crate & Barrel family this week (kitchen appliances, hosting recipes, bathroom tips, dining furniture, now baby bedding) — my brain hit permanent C&B snooze exactly like it did with the CB2 "Now & Future Classics" send.
- The rewards card pitch and "$500 chance" sweepstakes mention are crammed into tiny type at the very bottom where nobody scrolls.
- No shipping/delivery-timeline info anywhere, which matters way more to me on furniture-adjacent purchases than on a pillow insert.
- Zero showroom/design-consult integration despite "The Design Desk" branding showing up at the footer — feels bolted on, not tied to the content above it.

- 2. Lead with the OEKO-TEX/organic cotton certification higher up if you're going to use it — it's the only line in this whole email that reads like a real differentiator versus Pottery Barn or Wayfair's baby lines.
- 3. Pull the rewards card offer out of the footer and give it its own real estate — a "$500" chance is a strong hook getting buried.
