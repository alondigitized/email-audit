## Executive summary

- "Floor Sample Sale — In Stores For A Limited Time" and then the CTA is "FIND A STORE." I'm not driving to a CB2 showroom to poke at a dinged-up velvet chair — that's a very specific ask, and it's the very first thing this email leads with. My gut reaction is "not for me," followed immediately by the actual hook buried right below it: "END OF SUMMER SALE — UP TO 40% OFF," which is the online-shoppable version of basically the same pitch CB2 sent me yesterday in "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED + August Sale ends tonight." Except now "ends tonight" apparently didn't end — it's still running today, which makes that urgency copy from yesterday feel like it was fake.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" grid is where this actually gets useful for me — dining table, dining chairs, a coffee table, a bed — because I'm mid-renovation on two houses and that's genuinely my problem to solve. Pottery Barn's "Pull up a chair—new dining tables are here" email did this same dining-table beat better by leading with it instead of burying it under a floor-sample pitch I can't act on from my phone.
- Judgement: I'd scroll past the hero, skim the product grid for the dining table and chairs, and probably not click anything today. Not forwarding this — nothing here is exciting enough to send a friend, and the "ends tonight" repeat from yesterday makes me trust the urgency less, not more.

## What's working

- The product grid is genuinely relevant — dining table, dining armchairs (two colorways), a bed, a coffee table — that's my exact renovation shopping list.
- Prices are shown plainly next to every product, no "starting at" games.
- "10% Back in Rewards Dollars... at CB2 and Crate & Barrel" — I use the highest-rewards card for this stuff, so cross-brand rewards actually register with me.
- Clean single-column layout, easy to skim on my phone without pinch-zooming.

## What's weak

- Leading with "FIND A STORE" as the primary CTA when I'm shopping from my inbox is a mismatch — I want to click and buy, not plan a showroom trip.
- "Up to 40% off" with zero indication of which of the six products below it actually qualify — I have to guess or click through blind.
- "Ends tonight" ran yesterday and it's still going today — that's the second email in two days claiming false urgency, and now I don't believe the next deadline either.
- No fabric/finish swatch or sample-ordering link anywhere, which is a real miss on a "Floor Sample Sale" — that's exactly the moment to plug their swatch program if they have one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero CTA from "FIND A STORE" to something shoppable like "SHOP FLOOR SAMPLES ONLINE" — if floor samples aren't available online, then lead with the 40%-off banner instead and demote the store-locator chair to a secondary panel.
- 2. Tag which of the six grid items are part of the 40% sale — right now the discount and the product grid feel like two unrelated emails stapled together.
- 3. Fix the urgency math — don't reuse "ends tonight" language two days running; if the sale extended, say "extended" and give a real new deadline.
- 4. Add a swatch/sample-ordering link near the floor-sample hero — that's the natural CTA for someone renovating who wants to see the velvet in person before committing.
