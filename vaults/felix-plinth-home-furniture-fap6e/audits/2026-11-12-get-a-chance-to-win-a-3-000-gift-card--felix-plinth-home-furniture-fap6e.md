# Get a chance to win a $3,000 gift card
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Pottery Barn email that isn't the Fall Sale strip again. This is the Design Services sweepstakes send, and I'm honestly intrigued. I'm renovating two houses, so a free Design Crew appointment is the most useful thing PB has offered me in the roughly 15 sends this week. The catch is that "Win $3,000 Toward Your Home Refresh" is a sweepstakes. The real ask is an in-home or in-store appointment, so I'd call it a lead-gen play dressed up as a giveaway.
- The hero is clean and the WHAT / WHEN / WHERE layout is easy to follow. But the "chance to win" framing makes me cautious. Then the email drifts into free in-store classes (mantel, tree, table, bar) and a "Recommended Just for You" grid of stockings, a curtain rod, and dining tables. That grid is the only part that speaks to what I actually buy. The Toscana and Banks extendable dining tables are relevant. The stockings are not.
- Would I click? Probably "Book a Free Appointment," out of curiosity, since I need help matching finishes across two houses. Would I forward it? No. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder at most.

## What's working

- The hero offers a clear, concrete action: a "Book a Free Appointment" button in dark red that stands out against the photo.
- The WHAT / WHEN / WHERE block is scannable and sets expectations fast. "In your home, virtually, or in-store" fits two houses.
- Design Services is a real differentiator. Wayfair and CB2 are pure sale-banner shops and can't do this.
- The Toscana and Banks extendable dining tables in the grid are on target for me.
- The Rewards card callout ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") is the one piece of rewards math here. As a highest-rewards-card guy, I noticed it.

## What's weak

- The sweepstakes framing feels gimmicky. The asterisk fine print about the 1/2/27 deadline is tiny, and the full rules are buried at the bottom.
- The "Free In-Store Classes" banner competes with the hero. It's a second big CTA about holiday styling (stockings, tree, mantel), so the email has no single focus.
- The class dates are Sunday mornings at 10 AM, which isn't useful if I'm on a job site or at the second house.
- "Recommended Just for You" is mostly stockings. The curtain rod and dining tables are fine, but it doesn't feel personalized and has no prices or offers.
- Everything is crammed into the bottom third: app download, rewards card, Affirm, social, and a wall of legal text. It's long and slow on a phone.
- There's no swatch or sample program and no furniture shipping or lead-time info, which I'd want before booking anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero about design help for a renovation. Lead with the appointment benefit (finish matching, swatches, floor plans) and demote the sweepstakes to a sub-line.
- 2. Cut the in-store classes banner, or move it below the product grid. One hero, one CTA.
- 3. Make the grid actually personalized, with furniture and lighting, and show prices or in-stock furniture delivery dates.
- 4. Move the fine print up to a readable size and shorten the legal block.
- 5. Add a swatch-and-sample CTA and a mention of Rewards earning on design-service purchases. Crate & Barrel pitches 2x Rewards in its emails, and PB's equivalent is buried here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced layout sent through Salesforce Marketing Cloud click wrappers. The confirmed problems are a malformed `<style>` block, linked images with no accessible name, two `http://` images, and recipient email addresses in third-party pixel URLs. The HTML is truncated, so the unsubscribe link, footer and destination URLs could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click URLs can't be validated.** All 46 links use `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` with opaque tokens. The QA probe skipped them, so destinations, redirects and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party ad pixels** are present:
  - `https://secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501&srvc=1&betr=30802=711754[8760]`
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1`
- **Malformed tracking URL:** the leadback URL has `betr=30802=711754[8760]`, which is a double `=` plus unencoded brackets.
- **Dead code shipped in the HTML:** a commented-out header block contains a live-format link (`potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?...cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`). It is harmless but adds weight and exposes internal campaign parameters.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Broken CSS.** The second `<style>` block begins with PHP-style statements: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ..."); ...`. In a stylesheet these merge with the next selector into one invalid prelude, so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors] {...}` rule is likely discarded. That rule is meant to stop iOS auto-linking. It's a template-leak bug.
- **Empty alt on linked images.** The hero slices (`slices_01`–`04.jpg`) and the footer slices have `alt=""` inside `<a>` tags. The links have no accessible name, so screen readers announce a bare URL. The QA flagged about 30 images, and most are these linked slices.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** both are flagged by QA and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (a 2022 asset reused)
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels** (`svopn.potterybarn.com`, `d.turn.com`, `coreemail.html`, `leadback`) is low severity. They should use `alt="" width="1" height="1"`, but screen readers aren't affected much if they are hidden.
- **Responsive CSS mismatch.** The mobile rules target `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`, but the visible tables are fixed `width="650"` with no `class="wrapper"`, and the images have no `class="photo"`. The media query likely has no effect, so the email probably won't scale on mobile. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed for the rest.
- **Missing document basics:** no `<html lang>`, no `<title>`, and no `role="presentation"` on layout tables. The XHTML Strict doctype is also used with deprecated attributes (`<center>`, `bgcolor`, `border`), and ampScript comments precede the doctype, which can trigger quirks mode in some clients.
- **Preheader:** it uses a 9px font and an unescaped `&` ("in-store & take it home").
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's CDN, which is a dependency risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source. However, the recipient's email address is embedded in plain text in query strings:
- `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

This is PII in URLs, which are logged by proxies and CDNs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. The Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements make both headers mandatory.
- **Authentication-Results** is absent for the same reason, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC are unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the click domain.
- **Footer and unsubscribe link:** not visible due to truncation, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs:** none can be assessed, because the click wrappers hide the destinations.
- **Visible destinations:** the only unwrapped URLs are in the commented-out block (`marketingChannelCode`, `external_id`, `cm_ite`), so the brand uses its own parameters rather than UTMs.
- **Landing page alignment:** unverifiable without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Remove the PHP `header()` lines** from the `<style>` block so the `x-apple-data-detectors` rule applies.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to every linked image (hero slices and footer links). Set `alt=""` only on the decorative spacers and pixels.
3. **Move the two `http://` images to HTTPS.** Replace the 2022 banner asset or confirm it is intentional.
4. **Fix the mobile CSS.** Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the layout tables and images, or switch to fluid/hybrid patterns.
5. **Add `lang`, `<title>`, and `role="presentation"`.** Strip the commented-out code before send.
6. **Stop putting raw email addresses in tracking-pixel URLs.** Use hashed or opaque IDs, and fix the malformed `betr=` parameter.
7. **Verify the headers on the original MIME.** Use a direct-to-inbox seed, because the relay may have dropped List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
8. **Resolve the click-wrapper destinations** in a follow-up check to confirm UTM and landing-page continuity.
