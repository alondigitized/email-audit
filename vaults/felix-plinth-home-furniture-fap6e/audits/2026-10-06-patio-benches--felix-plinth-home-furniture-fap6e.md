## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based layout with 480px responsive CSS and per-recipient tracking tokens. The QA data shows several real defects: 30 images with no alt text, 20 impression pixels loaded over plain `http://`, and one image URL with a malformed percent-encoding. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified (see sections 3 and 5). QA pass rate was 57%, with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **The 429 responses are not confirmed breakage.** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links. These are rate limits from Wayfair's edge, so link validity is **unverified**. No 404 or 5xx responses were reported.
- **4 links were not probed.** Probing hit its 70s budget. The URLs in the QA output are cut off mid-string (`...se80398.h`, `...c419242.ht`), so that is likely QA output truncation, not broken `href`s.
- **Insecure app fallback.** The OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. It is an `http://` fallback and relies on a server-side redirect to HTTPS.
- **Campaign type mismatch.** The OneLink URL has `c=Triggered`, while every other link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch marketing send labelled as a triggered campaign will mis-segment attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **Malformed asset URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not valid percent-encoding. Strict decoders or CDNs may reject it. The QA run did not probe image fetches, so whether it renders is unconfirmed.
- **Heavy tracking footprint.** There are 20 ad-impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` to `...019`, `sz=2x1`) plus the open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`. These add 21 extra requests per open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 10 content images:**
  - `5DaysofDeals_Fall2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`
  - `small_appliances_02_gif.gif`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_3_purple.png`
  - 4× `wfus_jt800_minibahn_singleclass_barstools...`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_1_purple.png`
  - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`
- **Empty link names.** At least four of these are wrapped in links, including the "Shop Sale" hero and the "Just for You: Patio Benches" banner. QA reports the link text as `[image: filename]`, so screen readers get an empty accessible name for those links.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** The 20 `imp` pixels and `b.php` have no alt attribute. This is low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct form for decorative images.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** All 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels may be blocked or flagged as mixed content by some clients and proxies. Blocked pixels mean under-reported ad impressions.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML is truncated, so I can't assess table nesting, dark-mode handling, or Outlook conditional comments. The visible CSS includes `u ~ div`, a `.u-MobileHide` pattern and 480px breakpoints, and shows no defects.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. Per-recipient `_eml` and `_emr` values are populated on every URL, with no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens in the visible data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. It is **unconfirmed** whether the original message had them. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The sending domain is `members.wayfair.com`. This is a relay limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it does not report which URL or its status. The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm either element.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters: `source`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid` and `mdlid`. This is internally consistent, but the traffic won't auto-attribute in GA-style tools unless they are configured to read these parameters.
- **Subject-to-link alignment looks consistent.** The subject "PATIO BENCHES." matches `.../filters/just-for-you%3a-patio-benches-se46654.html?eid=349666`, with `unifiedeventid=349666` matching `eid`.
- **Landing pages not verified.** Landing page status and content could not be checked because the probes were rate limited.
- **Generic destinations.** The hero image and "Shop Sale" go to `/daily-sales`, and the logo and header image go to the `/` homepage.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 10 content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels. Make sure linked images have meaningful alt text.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Fix the `%oo` in `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` by renaming the asset or encoding it as `%25oo`.
4. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`, and correct `c=Triggered` to a batch campaign value.
5. Pull the raw `.eml` to confirm the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Re-probe the 429 links with throttling or backoff. Check the 4 unprobed links with full, untruncated URLs.
7. Add `utm_*` parameters if downstream analytics expect them.
