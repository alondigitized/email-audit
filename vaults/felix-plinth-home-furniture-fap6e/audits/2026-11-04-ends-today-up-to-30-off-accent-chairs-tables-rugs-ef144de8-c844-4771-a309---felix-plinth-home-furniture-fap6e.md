# ENDS TODAY: Up to 30% off accent chairs, tables & rugs!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the twelfth or so Crate & Barrel family send since 11/1, so I was already in snooze mode. This one at least has something I care about. A wool rug hero and "accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" are the pieces I'm shopping for across two houses. "ENDS TODAY" gets me to open it, but the body then forgets it's a one-day sale.
- After the hero it turns into a catalog of everything. I get rugs at up to 60% off, "quick ship furniture", the 2026 holiday decorating collection, a Black Friday signup, two store openings (La Cantera and Corte Madera), a rewards card pitch, the app and The Design Desk. Pottery Barn's "New arrivals with timeless holiday charm" was a clean lookbook. This is a flyer.
- Would I click? Yes, once, on the rug or the chairs, because "ends today" is credible and I'm actually in the market. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read, and probably save the rug to my Pinterest folder.

## What's working

- The hero rug (Brighton Wool Ivory) shows a real sale price against the regular price, $2,070.25 vs $3,099. That's a concrete number I can react to.
- "Accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" is in the headline, and it matches the subject line.
- The three-tile grid (tables, chairs, rugs up to 60% off) lets me see the categories at a glance, and the photography is on-brand.
- The "quick ship furniture" tile speaks to my real furniture question, which is how fast I can get it.
- The rewards card block ("it all adds up…", 10% back in Reward Dollars) is the one place the email talks to how I actually buy.

## What's weak

- "Ends today" isn't repeated anywhere in the body that I can see. I can't tell when today it ends or which items it covers beyond "select styles".
- There are too many competing asks: Shop The Sale, Shop All Sale (twice), Shop Now, Store Details, Get Directions, Learn More, Download Now, and Book a Free Appointment. Every button is a different weight and none of them says which chair or table it goes to.
- The store-opening sections take up a huge amount of space. I'm not in Texas or Corte Madera, so that's wasted scroll.
- The 10% off Black Friday signup is a green block mid-email. It competes with today's 30% off, and I'd rather wait for Black Friday than act now.
- The "we love these for you" product grid has tiny captions and prices. It looks personalized, but I can't read the details without zooming, and the same dining table and chairs appeared in other recent sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one story: ends-today furniture and rugs. Remove the store openings and the app promo, and move the Black Friday signup to the footer. Put a visible deadline ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PM CT") right under the hero.
- 2. Give the grid tiles specific CTAs ("Shop accent chairs", "Shop rugs up to 60% off") and make each tile link to its own category.
- 3. Make the product row readable, with larger type, one price per item, and a "ships in X days" tag on the quick-ship pieces.
- 4. Add the rewards math to the hero, something like "10% back in Reward Dollars on today's sale pieces", so the card block isn't buried at the bottom.
- 5. Send fewer of these. The C&B family cadence this week has been relentless.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 30% off accent chairs & tables`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: rugs up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brighton wool rug now $2,070. Quick-ship pieces included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends at midnight. Chairs, tables and rugs, plus 10% back in Rewards.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with `lang="en"`, an MSO/Outlook fallback, and a mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed defects are about 31 images flagged for missing alt text and a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom. Compliance headers and link destinations could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party tracking pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` to `n=5` beacons.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a7934bb4-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`.
- **Possible identifier sharing:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964...` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images matches the `s=56aaa2787a...` value on `sr.rlcdn.com`. That suggests one recipient-level hash is sent to a third-party domain. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 31 images.** The QA check can't distinguish an absent `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`.
  - **Likely content images (17):** 16 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/...` (for example `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `cd56cdc7-...gif`, `056e0bf8-...jpg`) and one on `s7d5.scene7.com` (`2026_1011_CB_HolidayDecorating_Secondary`).
  - **Seven images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`** (for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`). These look like dynamically rendered content.
  - **Tracking pixels (7):** the `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, and `dv.` beacons above. They need `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, but should not be treated as content defects.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing is suppressed.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` values.
- **Markup nits:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times and `charset` is declared twice (an http-equiv tag and `<meta charset>`).
  - There is a leftover placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Truncated source.** The rest of the document (body markup, footer) wasn't available, so there are no further findings.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 17 content images, including the `scene7` banner. Use `alt=""` on decorative images and tracking pixels, with explicit `width`/`height`.
2. Confirm that the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images carry alt text from the template. Dynamic content blocks often lose it.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Drop `text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the duplicate `meta` tags and the placeholder comment.
5. Re-run QA with redirect-following enabled for the click domain. Check final URLs, status codes, and UTM presence, and confirm the landing pages match the offer.
6. Obtain raw headers from a non-relay delivery to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Also run a full-source check for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Review whether sharing the `mi_u` hash with `sr.rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy disclosure.
