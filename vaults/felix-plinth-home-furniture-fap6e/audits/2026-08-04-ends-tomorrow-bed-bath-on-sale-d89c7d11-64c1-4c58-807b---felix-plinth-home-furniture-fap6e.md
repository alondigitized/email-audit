# ENDS TOMORROW: BED & BATH ON SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Six CB2 emails deep this week and I'm officially numb to the pattern — I open this one and my eye goes straight to the "60% off" clearance block before it even registers that the hero above it is a *different* sale at 30% off. That's a self-inflicted wound: two competing bedroom sales stacked on top of each other in the same email, and my brain just defaults to the bigger number and tunes out the first banner entirely. The bedroom is genuinely nice — warm wood tones, that canopy bed is doing real work — but I've seen this exact styling language from CB2 all week (the Girelli spotlight, the impulse-buy roundup, now this) and it's starting to blur together like one long scroll.
- Compare this to Crate & Barrel's Levain Bakery cookie jar email from yesterday — that one had a hook I hadn't seen before. This is CB2 running the same "up to X% off, shop the sale" playbook for the fourth or fifth time, and the two-sales-in-one-email thing actually makes it worse than a plain discount blast, not better. I'd scroll past the top two banners, maybe pause on the wall art vase because it's genuinely a nice piece, and bail before the product grid. Not clicking, not forwarding — this is a "skim and archive."

## What's working

- The canopy bed in the Summer Clearance banner is a strong hero image — warm, styled, makes me want that room.
- "Bedding ships free" under the 30% off headline is a real, concrete perk I care about mid-renovation — no guessing on shipping costs for bulky stuff.
- The "More For You" grid at the bottom has actual strikethrough pricing (Andes Acacia bed $999→$849, Archer nightstand $399→$349) — that's honest, visible math I can act on.
- Rewards callout ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars... at CB2 and Crate & Barrel") is relevant since I already shop both banners on my rewards card.

## What's weak

- Two competing sales (30% off bedroom+bath vs. 60% off summer clearance) stacked back to back confuses the hierarchy — I don't know which one is the "real" offer or if they overlap.
- "Bedding ships free" only appears once, tucked under the smaller sale — the bigger 60% off banner has zero shipping or fulfillment info, which matters a lot for a canopy bed frame.
- No swatch/sample program mention anywhere, and this is a bedding-and-bath-focused send — that's exactly the category where fabric feel and color-match matter and CB2 skips it entirely.
- This is the sixth-ish near-identical CB2 promo email I've graded this week; the "ends tomorrow" urgency reads more like recycled copy than a real deadline at this point.
- The vase in "Wall Art & Decor" has no name or price on the card itself — just "Shop Now" — so it's an aspirational image, not a shoppable moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one sale and lead with it.** Running 30% off and 60% off in the same email undercuts both — merge into a single tiered banner ("Up to 60% off bedroom + bath, bedding ships free") so I'm not doing math on which discount applies to what.
- 2. **Put shipping/fulfillment info on the big-ticket clearance banner too.** If the canopy bed frame ships free or has a delivery window, say so right there — that's the actual blocker for me buying furniture online.
- 3. **Add a swatch/sample CTA for the bedding sale specifically.** "Order fabric swatches" next to the shop button would close the trust gap on a category I can't touch before buying.
- 4. **Cut send frequency or differentiate harder.** Five-plus CB2 emails a week is training me to skim, not read — space these out or make each one clearly a different job (inspiration vs. discount vs. loyalty).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bedroom + bath: up to 60% off, ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bedding order ships free — sale ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Swatches available. Canopy beds, linens, and bath now up to 60% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on all bedding — last day for summer clearance pricing.`
