## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a large, image-heavy Crate & Barrel send with 80 tracked links. The QA data shows widespread missing alt text, plus unverified unsubscribe and authentication headers. Those header gaps probably come from the AgentMail relay, not the sender.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 80 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Several third-party pixels are embedded:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `n=5` (five LiveRamp/RampID syncs).
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` (open pixel).
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4afcd7c2-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` (tracking image).
- The `mi_u=` token is a long per-recipient identifier carried in image URLs. The same ID appears in the `sr.rlcdn.com` `s=` parameter, so it is shared with a third party. This is a privacy note, not a functional bug.
- The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (16,230 of 22,612 chars). That is a spam-scoring risk and is probably caused by long tracking URLs.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- About 45 images are flagged as missing alt text. Examples:
  - The hero image `2026%5F1013%5FCB%5FSenseSensibility%5F1%5FHeroVide...` on `s7d5.scene7.com`.
  - The `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` product and content images.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images).
- Most of these are content or linked images. With images blocked, the email will have no readable fallback for the hero or the product tiles.
- The 1x1 trackers (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv...`) should carry `alt=""`. The check cannot tell whether they do, so I can't confirm those are real issues. The other images do need descriptive alt text.
- The hero asset name ends in `HeroVide`, which suggests a video or animated asset. The truncated source doesn't show whether it has a static fallback, so I can't confirm one.
- The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is an accessibility concern.
- `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` is a global rule, so it centers all paragraphs by default. That is a maintainability risk and may misalign body copy. I can't confirm it from the truncated source.
- The `*{... line-height:100%}` rule in the global style block is aggressive. It can compress multi-line text.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`. The `<title>` is the generic "Crate & Barrel" rather than the subject line, which is minor.

**5. Compliance**
- The QA check found no `List-Unsubscribe` header, and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header. Both findings come with the QA caveat that AgentMail may strip the headers, so they are unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- The visible footer unsubscribe link, physical address and sender identity are not in the truncated source, so I can't verify them.
- The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment is not verifiable.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters can't be assessed. The 80 tracking links are redirect-wrapped and were not resolved, so the final destination URLs and any UTM tagging are unknown.
- The subject refers to the "Sense & Sensibility" movie tie-in. The landing page for the hero link can't be matched to it without resolving the redirects.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-run the audit with redirect resolution on, or follow a sample of the 80 tracking links, to check destinations, UTMs and 404s.
2. Pull the raw message headers outside the AgentMail relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Don't score these as failures until then.
3. For the sender, add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the hero and product tiles. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta to allow zoom.
5. Check that the hero asset has a static fallback if it is a video or animated file.
6. Shorten the tracking URLs, or add more real text to the plain-text part, to bring the URL ratio below 70%.
7. QA tooling: tag the 1x1 tracking pixels as decorative so they don't inflate the missing-alt count. About 6 of the flagged images are pixels.
