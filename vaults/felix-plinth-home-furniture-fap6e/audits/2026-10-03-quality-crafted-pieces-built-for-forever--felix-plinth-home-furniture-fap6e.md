## Technical Audit: "Quality-crafted pieces built for forever →"

### 1. Technical Summary
The source follows a standard Marigold/Cheetah-style table layout with MSO fallbacks. The automated QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings), driven mainly by about 40 images without alt text. The HTML was truncated and the headers were not captured, so the compliance and link findings below are unverified.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** QA skipped the HTTP probe for 79 tracking links ("Tracking/click-redirect domains"), so I can't confirm redirect targets, status codes or broken destinations.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `&n=5` beacons (LiveRamp) sit alongside the first-party pixels. The first-party ones are six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/49f71024-…`.
- **Duplicate pixels.** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `448796.gif` repeat with the same `mi_u` or `s` value. This adds requests without adding data.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa278…` and `s=56aaa278…` tokens are the same value across hosts. It is a per-recipient identifier passed to a third-party domain, which is worth confirming against the sender's privacy disclosures.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 40 images (the QA warnings).**
  - Content-sized assets on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (jpg and gif) have no alt text, for example `eeb8adae-…jpg`, `4377034b-…gif`, `cca69649-…jpg` and `bbf6f9d4-…png` (path `/m/47/`). If these carry content or link to products, the email is unreadable with images off.
  - Spacers `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` (scene7) and all the tracking pixels have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resize disabled.** The `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{… -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rules override the `100%` values declared earlier.
- **Markup validity.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has a raw `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - There are redundant duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags and both `Content-Type` and `charset` metas.
  - A template placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, was left in the source. It is not rendered.
- **Not assessable.** I can't evaluate the body markup because the source was truncated.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear. The only recipient-specific values seen are the `mi_u` and `s` tracking tokens, which resolved.

### 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN].** QA notes the header "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". This is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN].** Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so it should be checked on the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be verified from this capture. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` is on the same parent.
- **Footer not verified.** The body footer wasn't in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or the physical postal address (CAN-SPAM).

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Can't be assessed. The 79 tracking links were not resolved, so I can't check UTM parameters, final landing URLs, or whether the destinations match the "built for forever" kids-furniture content. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values on `dv.crateandbarrel.com` suggest campaign and message IDs are carried through the tracker, but the landing URLs are unseen.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/.../m/1/*`, `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`). Add `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` overrides in favor of `100%`.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw message headers. Don't rely on the AgentMail-relayed copy.
4. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the original delivery.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML with redirect-following for the 79 tracking links. Verify the unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the UTM and landing-page alignment.
6. Fix the `&` in `<title>`, de-duplicate the meta tags, and remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
7. Deduplicate the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `448796.gif` pixels.
