## What's weak

- Zero pricing or offer until "BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF" shows up after nine scroll-stops — that's a lot of real estate with no reason to act now.

- "NEW & BEST SELLING" section shows one dark stone-look bench with literally no name, price, or link context visible in the tile — feels like an unfinished module.
- No loyalty/rewards-tier messaging until the very footer ("Get 15% off," "earn 10% back") — should be higher if they want CB2 members like me engaged early.

- 2. Add price ranges directly on the category tiles (Table Jewelry from $X, Handblown Glass from $X) so I can gut-check budget before clicking through.
