# Introducing the new Dormify✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn drops this in my inbox and the first thing I register is "dormify" — lowercase, pastel-adjacent, very clearly not for me. I'm mid-renovation on two houses, deep in trade pricing spreadsheets, and PB has my purchase history. They know I bought a sectional. They know I'm not eighteen moving into a dorm. This email reads like it landed in my folder by accident — the copy literally says "Study smarter." I don't study. I tile bathrooms.
- The execution itself is clean — PB always delivers on layout and photography. But clean garbage is still garbage if you're not the intended recipient. The lifestyle shots are soft, airy, and aimed squarely at the incoming-freshman demographic. Nothing in here maps to what I'd actually click for: lead times on large furniture, trade pricing, swatches, or whether the sectional I favorited ships in time for the August move-in at the second house.
- Mark as read, don't click, definitely don't forward. If anything, this slightly erodes my confidence that PB is using their list data well.

## What's working

- **Brand recognition is instant** — PB logo, clean type, their signature neutral palette. Even though the content is wrong for me, I trust the sender in under a second.
- **Visual hierarchy is solid** — the vertical stacking of lifestyle shots with bite-size copy ("Sleep better / Study smarter / Feel at home / Stay fresh") is easy to scan even on a small phone screen.
- **$25 Earn in Rewards is a concrete hook** — tucked in the lower section, it's at least a specific number, which is more than most footer boilerplate gives you.
- **No render issues** — everything loaded cleanly. The photography looks good. Tap targets in the footer look reasonable.

## What's weak

- **Wrong customer entirely** — I'm renovating, not moving into a dorm. PB has my transaction history and still sent me freshman bedding copy. That's a list-segmentation failure, plain and simple.
- **"Study smarter" and "Feel at home" are the wrong emotional beats** — those speak to a 19-year-old moving away from parents for the first time. I own the home. I'm already at home.
- **No offer tied to the hero** — the scrolling copy teases the Dormify brand but never gives me a reason to act now. A % off launch discount or a limited-time bundle would have changed the math.
- **Hero feels like a collab announcement, not a campaign** — "Introducing the new Dormify" is an internal milestone, not a customer benefit. Lead with what I get, not what you launched.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment this off my list entirely.** If Dormify is genuinely a college-focused sub-brand, it should mail to customers who have browsed or purchased youth/college bedding, not every name on the PB file. This is the single highest-leverage fix.
- 2. **If you must send to the full list, dual-path the hero** — show me a "furnishing a second space?" angle. Dormify styling translates fine to a guest room or a first apartment for a kid. Reframe the hero copy for that use case.
- 3. **Add a launch offer to the above-fold section** — "20% off Dormify for the next 48 hours" would turn a brand announcement into a reason to click.
- 4. **Replace "Introducing the new Dormify" with something that sells a benefit** — the subject does the brand a favor, not me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Guest room refresh starts here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dormify is here — 20% off launch weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clean lines, easy layering — works in dorms or that spare room you keep ignoring`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pottery Barn's new sub-brand is live. Here's what it looks like in a real space.`

## Technical Audit — Pottery Barn "Introducing the new Dormify✨"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email contains two critical code-level defects: PHP `header()` calls rendered verbatim inside a `<style>` block, and a second nested `<!DOCTYPE html>` / `<html>` / `<body>` declaration mid-document. These are structural errors that degrade rendering across clients. Compliance headers were not captured by the relay, requiring direct sender-side verification.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken link (403) — commented-out block**
URL: `https://www.potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB&marketingChannelCode=APPLYANDBUY&external_id=PB_Email_MemberBanner&cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`

The link is wrapped in an HTML comment (`<!-- ... -->`), so it is not currently live. However, the destination returns 403. If the comment block is ever re-enabled, every click on the credit card banner will hard-fail. The URL should be verified and fixed before any reactivation.

**[WARN] 23 click-redirect links unverified**
All CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com`. Final destination URLs and UTM propagation cannot be confirmed without following redirects. No issues assumed, but see Section 6.

**[INFO] Non-standard tracking params on the credit card link**
Uses `cm_ite=` and `external_id=` rather than standard `utm_*` params. Inconsistent with any UTM-based analytics if that link is ever re-enabled.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[CRITICAL] PHP `header()` calls inside `<style>` block**
```html
<style>
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ...
</style>
```
These are PHP server-side directives that were not executed — they were written literally into the CSS block. Any email client that strictly parses CSS will treat these as invalid declarations; permissive parsers will silently ignore them. This indicates a template rendering failure where PHP source code was included unexecuted. No functional impact on sent mail, but this is a build/rendering pipeline defect that needs a root-cause fix.

**[CRITICAL] Nested `<!DOCTYPE>` and `<html>` declaration inside `<td>`**
A second `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC ...>`, `<html xmlns=...>`, and `<body>` tag appear mid-document, nested inside a table cell (the body content injection point). Spec-compliant parsers will ignore or error on the second declaration; some email clients may enter quirks mode or drop subsequent content. The outer and inner document structures should be consolidated into a single valid HTML document.

**[WARN] 18 footer image slices missing alt text**
`slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg` (all from `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2026/US/Email-Footer/`) have no `alt` attribute. When images are blocked — default in Outlook, Gmail, and Apple Mail with remote image loading off — the footer renders as a blank region with no fallback text.

**[WARN] 2 images served over HTTP (not HTTPS)**
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
- `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`

Gmail will proxy-fetch HTTP images; Outlook desktop may block them entirely. Both should be upgraded to `https://`.

**[WARN] Tracking pixel images missing alt text**
`coreemail.html`, `lb` (leadback.advertising.com), `1` (d.turn.com), and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel all lack `alt=""`. These should have `alt=""` (empty) to be explicit that they are decorative/invisible.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected**
QA flagged this as possibly a relay-capture gap rather than sender omission. Cannot confirm presence or absence from available data. Sender must verify that the `List-Unsubscribe` header is present on the actual SMTP delivery — required for CAN-SPAM compliance and for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policies.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders since Feb 2024) depends on this header. Same caveat — relay may have dropped it, but must be verified at the MTA level.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the captured headers. Sending domain is `e.potterybarn.com`; DKIM alignment should be verified against the `From:` domain (`potterybarn.com`).

**[INFO] Physical address and unsubscribe link**
