# Up to 50% off. Don't miss this.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2, again — this is Labor Day countdown-timer send number... I don't even know anymore, but at least this one's got a strikethrough gimmick I haven't seen from them this week: "GET 10% ~~20%~~ BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" for the co-brand card pitch, which reads like a deliberate before/after crossout rather than yesterday's flat-out "10% 20%" typo. Small mercy. But it's still the same sage-leaf hero, same beige palette, same "SALE ENDS IN" clock, same category button grid I've screenshotted about five times this week between this account and Crate & Barrel.
- The furniture sale itself — up to 50% off, then a second "CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF" banner stacked right under the credit card pitch — is honestly the strongest part of this email, and it's dead simple to parse: category buttons for Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Outdoor, then Shop All. That's more legible than Wayfair's chaos or Pottery Barn's eleventh identical burgundy banner this week. But between the countdown timer, the rewards card push, the second clearance banner, the app download pitch, the "get 15% off for texts" pitch, and the wedding-registry pitch all crammed into one scroll, it's trying to sell me four different things and I'm only here for the sofa.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. I already flagged CB2's near-identical "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send at 7/10 last week because it at least felt like a distinct drop — this one's just Labor Day noise stacked on Labor Day noise, and I've got three tabs open from this brand's parent company alone.

## What's working

- The category button grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Outdoor) is genuinely the cleanest piece of nav I've seen from any home brand this week — Wayfair buries its categories, this doesn't.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" is huge, unambiguous, above the fold — no scrolling gymnastics needed like some of Pottery Barn's sends.
- The strikethrough "10% ~~20%~~" at least reads as an intentional emphasis device rather than a straight-up proofreading fail like yesterday's version.
- Clean, consistent beige/sage palette across every banner — no jarring color clash between sections, unlike some Wayfair sends that mash five colors together.

## What's weak

- Four separate asks stacked in one email — furniture sale, credit card rewards, clearance, app download, texting signup, gift registry — nobody's reading past banner two on a phone.
- The countdown timer ("01:17:10:29") is the exact same lever CB2 ran yesterday and the day before — I'm numb to it at this point.
- No actual product shots of furniture — it's all lifestyle leaves-and-light-fixture mood photography, zero specific SKUs, no price points, no swatch or fabric-sample callout, which is exactly what I actually want before I click into a sofa or rug purchase.
- The rewards card pitch ("GET 10% 20% BACK") demands I read fine print about "First Bark & Trust" financing terms to even understand the offer — that's friction I'm not doing on my commute.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE offer per email. Lead with the 50% off furniture sale, kill the credit-card and clearance banners from this specific send — save them for a separate day so I'm not skimming past three offers to find the one I care about.
- 2. Show me actual furniture — a chair, a sectional, a rug close-up with texture visible. Article's "Last call" send scored 8/10 with me partly because the hero image did the selling without me needing to click through five categories first.
- 3. Kill the countdown timer or vary it — CB2 has now run some version of this clock in every single email I've read from them this week and it's lost all urgency.
- 4. If keeping the rewards card push, simplify it to one line: "10% back at CB2, 20% for new signups" — no strikethrough gimmicks, no need to parse it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Furniture up to 50% off — final markdowns`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The sofa you've been eyeing, 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance just dropped on rugs, lighting, and outdoor.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `48 hours left before prices go back up.`
