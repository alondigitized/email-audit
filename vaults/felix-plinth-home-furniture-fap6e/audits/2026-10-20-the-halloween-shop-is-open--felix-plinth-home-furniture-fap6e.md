# The Halloween Shop is OPEN
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Pottery Barn send I've logged today, after the barstools and the free design help. It opens with a dark, moody Halloween hero: a plush ghost, a bat, a trick-or-treat pillow and a pumpkin. I'm renovating two houses and I'm not decorating for Halloween, so I'd skim it. The hero is well made, but it's the wrong category for me. It also repeats 10/19's "The Harvest Decor Shop is open" almost word for word, only with ghosts instead of pumpkins.
- The email does get better further down. The "Recommended Just For You" block has the Toscana and Banks extendable dining tables, which I could use, plus a platform bed and curtain rods. Near the bottom is a Free Design Services banner with fabric swatches, which I'd already seen in today's dedicated send. It's a good email stacked in the wrong order. Mark as read, and maybe come back for the dining tables.

## What's working

- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" bar at the top gives me shipping clarity on furniture. That's rare, and it's what I actually care about.
- "Order by Tue 10/27, 6 AM PT with standard shipping" is a credible deadline with a specific time. Wayfair's "Christmas Tree Market" blast had nothing like it.
- The hierarchy is clean. There's one hero, one "UP TO 50% OFF" badge, and one solid black "SHOP ALL THINGS HALLOWEEN" button.
- The dining tables come with dimensions in the captions (60"-125", 76"-128"), so I can size them without clicking.
- The swatch and brass-hardware photo in the Design Crew banner hits the color-palette side of my renovations. It's close to what Article did with its free-design email.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the hero relates to a renovation. I have no mantel to style, and this is the second seasonal shop in two days after the Harvest Decor Shop.
- A "Christmas Shop" tile sits in the same email, 11 days before Halloween. It dilutes the urgency of the Halloween deadline. Wayfair at least committed to one holiday.
- "Up to 50% off" is vague, and the long exclusions block at the bottom reads like a legal wall. It's too small to read on a phone without zooming.
- The Design Crew banner is buried near the bottom. It's the strongest offer for me, and I already got it as its own send earlier today. That's the same repetition I get from CB2's six sends in six days.
- The bottom third is Affirm, the app and the rewards card pitch. That's three financing and app asks, none of them tied to a product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Design Crew offer, or with in-stock furniture, for anyone who has been clicking furniture. Keep Halloween as a secondary band. Segment on behavior, since I've clicked barstools and nightstands and nothing seasonal.
- 2. Pick one season per send. Drop the Christmas tile from the Halloween email.
- 3. Make the offer concrete. Name the best deal ("Plush ghosts from $X") instead of "up to 50%."
- 4. Collapse the Affirm, app and card blocks into one slim footer row. Show a real rewards number for card holders.
- 5. Stop re-sending the design appointment. If it's in the main send, skip the standalone one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Halloween decor up to 50% off, order by 10/27`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day to order Halloween in time: ships by 10/27`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus in-stock furniture delivered in 1-4 weeks and a free design appointment.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Tue 10/27, 6 AM PT. Dining tables, beds and more are in stock too.`

The main problems are widespread missing `alt` text, three `http://` image or pixel requests, and the recipient address placed in plain-text query strings. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 46 links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...` on the logo link. Destinations and link health can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. It is non-HTTPS, so it may be blocked and the open may not register. It also sends the recipient address unencrypted.
- **Recipient address in URLs:** `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is an `<img>` request. The plaintext address in a query string can end up in server and proxy logs.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-tech beacon on an external domain.
