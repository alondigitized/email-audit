# Quality-crafted pieces built for forever →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh Crate-family email in about three days, and the second one today from the Kids sender. Earlier today I got the Bodie "grow-with-them" send, and this is basically the same nursery furniture with a new subject line. I'm renovating two houses and I don't have a nursery to furnish, so my first reaction was to skip it. I scrolled it anyway, and it's well put together: a clear event banner, one collection per block, and star ratings and parent quotes throughout.
- It's a long, polished catalog scroll for someone who isn't me. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on. Pottery Barn's holiday catalog email at least gave me something for the primary house, and CB2's "IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE." talked to my actual project. Crate has sent me a sofa quote, dining newness and markdowns in the last few days, and this one isn't any of those.

## What's working

- The green "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, Up to 30% off" banner sits at the very top. I get the offer before I scroll.
- The hero is a white spindle bed in a styled room. It's pretty, and the brand voice is clearly Crate.
- Every collection block (Bestra, Jenny Lind, Bridgeman bunk, Bodie) has a short quote or star rating and its own "Shop…" button. The rhythm is easy to skim.
- The "we love these for" grid has prices on each item, so I don't have to guess.
- Repeating the event banner mid-scroll is smart. I see the offer again without scrolling back up.

## What's weak

- It's the wrong room for me. Cribs, bunks and nursery bedding don't help with two renovations.
- The cadence is rough. This is the second Kids send today, after the Bodie email, and the same Crate family has mailed me over and over since 10/3.
- It's very long. The card promo, app promo, TheDesignDesk appointment and legal footer all pile up after the product content, and the footer is a wall of tiny text.
- I couldn't see a clear end date on the event. "Up to 30% off" with no deadline doesn't give me a reason to act now.
- I didn't see any shipping or lead-time info for the big furniture pieces. That's what I'd want before I'd pay for a bunk bed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send the Kids sender only to people with kids, or people who've browsed nursery furniture. For someone like me, one nursery email a week is plenty, not two in a day.
- 2. Cut the email in half. Keep the banner, the hero and two collections, and drop the card, app and design-desk blocks, or move them to their own send.
- 3. Add an end date to the event banner, plus a line on delivery windows for furniture.
- 4. Put a shopping-by-room or "bedroom for any age" angle up top, so non-nursery shoppers like me have a way in.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bedroom & nursery event: up to 30% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bunk beds, cribs, and a lot of 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our Bestra, Jenny Lind and Bridgeman collections, plus free design help.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See what parents rate highest, and when furniture ships.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with roughly 35 images flagged as missing alt text, including content images, third-party pixels and open-tracking pixels. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay didn't capture the headers. The source is also truncated and all 75 links are click-tracked, so link, footer and landing-page behavior can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 75 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp) are five identical pixel calls that differ only in `n`. Together with `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2277c0de-…`, these are five or more tracking requests per open.
- **Identifiers in URLs:** The `s=` and `mi_u=` values are 64-character hex strings, which look like hashed recipient identifiers. I can't confirm what they hash.
- **Duplicate image:** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`) appears 6 times with the same `mi_u` value. It may be a dynamic recommendation image or a repeated pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 35 `<img>` elements are flagged. The list contains these types:
  - 22 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `eeb8adae-…jpg` and 8 `.gif` files. With images blocked or for screen readers, these show no text.
  - 6 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`.
  - 5 `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, 1 `o.gif` and 1 `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2277c0de-…`. These are tracking pixels that should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Template markup problems:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype.
  - There are duplicate `<meta charset>` and `Content-Type` declarations.
  - Three `format-detection` meta tags overlap. One combined tag plus two more is redundant.
  - The leftover placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Zoom and text sizing:**
  - `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` can block pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
  - `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{}`, `html` and `body`, which disables text resizing on iOS.
- **Breakpoint:** The only responsive breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports from 461px to 600px get the desktop layout.
- **Language:** `<html lang="en">` is present.
