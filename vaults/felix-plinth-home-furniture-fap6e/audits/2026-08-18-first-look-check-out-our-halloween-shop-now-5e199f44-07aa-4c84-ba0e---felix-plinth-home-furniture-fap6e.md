# First look: Check out our Halloween shop now →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again — same brand family that already sent me this exact Halloween shop teaser today, apparently, because this is the second one in my inbox with a pumpkin treat bag and a "Grayson" name-tag I don't recognize. I don't have kids, I don't have a nursery, and yet here I am looking at a personalized "Jackson" pumpkin bucket and a crib nursery lifestyle shot for the second time in one day. This is Crate & Barrel's kids sub-brand, and it's just not my account.
- The photography is genuinely nice — that felted black cat, the ghost bucket, the bunting banner — if I had a five-year-old I might actually screenshot this. But I'm mid-renovation on two houses, both fully adult spaces, and the one thing buried here that's remotely relevant to me — "new! the card you love just got better... Reward Dollars" — is stuck two-thirds of the way down under a pile of nursery furniture and Halloween throw pillows.
- Not opening this again, not forwarding it, marking as read and moving on. Crate & Kids needs to figure out list hygiene before it burns more of my attention.

## What's working

- The Halloween hero banner and bunting are well-styled — genuinely festive, not cheap-looking.
- "500+ new arrivals, starting at $29" is a clear, specific price anchor, even if it's for a crib.
- The Crate Card / Reward Dollars callout is the one section that's actually relevant to me as a Crate & Barrel household — I do care about rewards math.
- Clean footer navigation (Furniture, Bedding, Decor, Storage, Toys & Gifts, Gear) shows this is a real, structured retailer, not spam.

## What's weak

- This is a kids/nursery/Halloween email and I have no children — total demographic mismatch, same complaint I had on this exact "First look" send earlier today.
- The crib nursery photo and "we love these for you" grid (kids backpack, gold bow knobs, haunted house dinner plates) reads like it was built for a completely different subscriber segment than mine.
- Way too long — I'm scrolling through six distinct sections (Halloween shop, nursery arrivals, personalized picks, Crate Card, app download, design desk) before I even get to anything I might use.
- The one thing I'd actually want — Crate & Barrel Reward Dollars — is crammed near the bottom instead of leading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress me from Crate & Kids sends entirely, or at minimum stop leading with nursery/kids content — if there's cross-sell for adult Crate & Barrel shoppers, lead with that, not treat-bag personalization.
- 2. Move the Crate Card / Reward Dollars block to the top — that's the only genuinely relevant hook for someone like me on this list.
- 3. Cut this email in half — six stacked sections is a lot to scroll for someone who's already skimming on a commute.
- 4. If Crate & Kids wants to cross-sell parents-who-also-renovate, show adult-space nursery furniture (the crib is fine) paired with matching adult bedroom pieces, not throw pillows shaped like cats.
