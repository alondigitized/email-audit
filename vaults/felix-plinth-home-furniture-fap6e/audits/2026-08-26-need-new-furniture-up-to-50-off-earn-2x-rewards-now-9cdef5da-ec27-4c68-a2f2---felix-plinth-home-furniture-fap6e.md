# Need new furniture? Up to 50% off & earn 2x Rewards now!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids again. This is the fourth one of these I've logged this week — "up to 50% off baby & kids furniture," kids bedroom sets, nursery gliders, toddler beds, storage cubbies — and I still don't have kids. I renovate two houses and shop the highest-rewards card, not a crib registry. I scroll straight past.
- The one thing that snags my eye is "500+ new fall arrivals, starting at $29!" buried two-thirds down — that's actual furniture-adjacent, budget-friendly copy that could apply to me, but it's wedged between a nursery chair grid and a credit card pitch, so it reads like an afterthought. Everything else — the crib, the changing table, the "Nursery Chair" carousel — is dead air for my inbox.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and half-tempted to actually unsubscribe from this sub-brand the way I never bother to from the main Crate & Barrel list.

## What's working

- "Double Rewards is on!" banner up top is legible and at least ties to a program I might actually be enrolled in via the main C&B account.
- Product cards are clean — price, strike-through original, swatch/size labels all present, no broken images.
- The "$500 sweepstakes" module is a nice pattern-break in an otherwise wall-to-wall product-grid email.
- "TheDesignDesk" footer teaser (free design help, up to 50% off room refresh) is at least adjacent to my actual renovation project.

## What's weak

- Entire hero and first four scroll-screens are nursery/kids-bedroom furniture — cribs, gliders, toddler beds. Zero relevance to a childless 36-year-old furnishing two adult homes.
- This is the same "up to 50% off baby & kids furniture" beat I already scored 5/10 on this exact date — no new offer, no new angle, just reshuffled cards.
- "Enter for a chance to win $5000" sweepstakes module has no visible deadline or entry mechanism beyond a button — feels like filler between product blocks.
- The one line that could hook me ("500+ new fall arrivals, starting at $29") is buried below six screens of cribs instead of leading.
- Card pitch ("get the card, get $500... plus 5% back") is the third rewards-card ask I've seen from this C&B family this week, layered on top of Double Rewards — messaging is crowded, not clarifying.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill this send for subscribers with no kids-category engagement — Crate & Barrel's own sister sends (the "up to 40% off bestselling sofas" ones I logged today) prove they can segment by category interest; Crate & Kids clearly isn't doing that math.
- 2. Lead with the fall-arrivals-at-$29 line instead of burying it — that's the one piece of copy in here that isn't kid-specific and could pull an adult-furniture shopper in.
- 3. Cut the module count in half — six product carousels plus a sweepstakes plus two rewards-card pitches in one email is Wayfair-grab-bag energy, not the tighter single-offer approach Pottery Barn used in its "Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" send.
- 4. Consolidate the rewards messaging — Double Rewards banner at top and the credit-card pitch near the bottom are competing CTAs for the same wallet action.
- **Subject Alt A:** `500+ new fall arrivals from $29 — plus Double Rewards`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Not kids furniture: fall arrivals under $29`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New fall pieces just landed, starting at $29 — Double Rewards through Sept 5`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard ESP-templated (Cheetah/MessageGears-style "MI" markup) promotional email from Crate & Barrel's Crate & Kids list, built on nested table layout with heavy inline tracking pixels; core rendering scaffolding is sound, but authentication/compliance headers could not be confirmed and nearly all images lack alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 120 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect domains, not directly resolvable) — destination URLs cannot be verified as live from this data set alone.
- Multiple tracking pixels/beacons detected: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5 sequential instances, `n=1` through `n=5`), and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v` view-tracking call. The repeated LiveRamp (`rlcdn.com`) beacons firing 5x with incrementing sequence numbers is typical of multi-vendor data-onboarding/retargeting sync, not a bug, but worth flagging for anyone auditing third-party data sharing.
- No broken/malformed `href` syntax observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 57 of ~57+ images/pixels flagged with missing `alt` text in the QA scan — this includes both content images (e.g., `bbf6f9d4-...png`, product photography `.jpg` assets) and tracking pixels. Content images lacking alt text is a genuine accessibility gap (screen readers get no fallback if images are blocked); tracking-pixel alt-text warnings are not meaningful (pixels are 1x1 and non-content) and can be disregarded.
- Head contains legacy multi-client compatibility shims (`ReadMsgBody`, `ExternalClass`, MSO conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection` meta tags) — consistent with a template built for broad Outlook/Apple Mail/Gmail support, no issues found here.
- Responsive breakpoints defined at `max-width:460px` (mobile) and `min-width:640px`/`768px` (desktop image scaling) — standard, no issues found.
- `<title>` in `<head>` reads "Crate & Barrel" rather than matching the send ("Crate & Kids") — minor/cosmetic, not a functional defect (title tag isn't typically rendered in inbox clients), but noted since it's a code-level inconsistency.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge-tag syntax (e.g., `{{`, `%%`, `[%`, `##`) was observed in the visible/truncated source — no evidence of broken personalization fields.
- Subscriber identifiers are passed via hashed `mi_u` query params on tracking pixels/links (e.g., `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101`) and `mi_mid` on the view-tracking call — consistent hashed-ID pattern across all tracking calls, no mismatches found.

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — if genuinely absent, this email doesn't support one-click unsubscribe per Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements, which is a compliance risk for high-volume senders as of 2024+ deliverability rules.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found — authentication status unknown from this capture; cannot confirm the message passed sender authentication. This is a relay-visibility gap rather than a confirmed failure.
- No in-body unsubscribe link/footer was visible in the truncated HTML provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer requirements (physical address, unsubscribe link) are met from available data.
