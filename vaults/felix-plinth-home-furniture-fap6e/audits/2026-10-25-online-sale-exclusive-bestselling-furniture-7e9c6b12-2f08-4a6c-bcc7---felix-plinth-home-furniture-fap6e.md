# Online SALE exclusive: Bestselling furniture
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Pottery Barn send I've been getting since 10/22. The maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is on top again, and I'm numb to it. This time, though, the body is furniture, and that's the category I'm actually shopping. Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston and Farmhouse are four named collections, each with a big room shot. I can use that.
- The email does something the Crate & Barrel recipe send didn't. It gives me something I can click to compare. But it never tells me what the furniture discount is. "Select Furniture Now on Sale" has no percentage and no price anywhere. I'm renovating two houses and need to know whether Toscana is 15% off or 40% off before I tap anything.
- I'd click, probably on Toscana, since the extendable dining table shows up again in the recommended grid. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my Pinterest folder and move on.

## What's working

- Four named collections (Aubrey, Toscana, Livingston, Farmhouse) with big room scenes. That's how I shop, by collection and by matching finishes across two houses.
- The "NOW ON SALE" red tags on each image tell me which collections are actually discounted.
- "Shop All In-Stock Furniture" is a smart CTA for me. Shipping timelines are my biggest worry with furniture.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" card block and the Design Crew "Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" block are both relevant to my renovation and my rewards-card habit.
- The layout is clean. The hero reads quickly and the serif type feels like Pottery Barn.

## What's weak

- No furniture-specific offer. "Select Furniture Now on Sale" has no percentage, so I don't know what the discount is.
- The maroon 20% strip leads again. It's the same header I saw on 10/24's "Save MORE with EXTRA 20% OFF select styles", and it pushes the real hero down.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has no prices and no ratings. A curtain rod, a rug and a pendant are all there with nothing to compare.
- The Christmas Shop banner and the App, Affirm and card blocks make the bottom long and cluttered.
- The fine print is a dense wall of text. "Exclusions apply" appears twice, and I can't tell what's excluded without reading all of it.
- Nothing mentions swatches, fabric samples or delivery lead time, which is what I care about on upholstery and tables.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real furniture offer in the hero, for example "Up to X% off Aubrey, Toscana & more." Cut the stacked strip down to a thin line.
- 2. Add a price or "from $" and a ship window to each collection tile. Use a line like "In stock, ships by Nov 10."
- 3. Add prices and star ratings to the Recommended grid, and link each item to its product page.
- 4. Add a swatch and sample callout on the upholstered pieces.
- 5. Cut the lower stack to Design Crew plus the card. Move Affirm and the app into the footer.
- 6. Vary the creative. I've seen the same strip in at least seven sends since 10/22.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Toscana, Aubrey & Livingston: furniture on sale now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bestselling tables & beds, now with extra savings`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-stock pieces that ship before the holidays. See the sale prices.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Four collections, one design appointment. Book yours free.`

## Technical Audit: "Online SALE exclusive: Bestselling furniture" (Pottery Barn)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout that is mostly image slices. It has widespread missing alt text (about 41 images), three insecure `http://` images, and customer email addresses in the query strings of tracking pixels. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the relay may have stripped the headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **49 tracking links were not probed.** They are `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirects, so their final destinations are unverified. The logo link is one example.
- **Three third-party or beacon pixels are in the body:**
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…`
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/13eec539-…?mi_cid=…`
- **Stale or cross-brand asset paths** are hotlinked:
  - `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2025/US/1016_THU_AM_DTCOnlyFurnitureSale/slices_01–07` is an October 2025 campaign folder.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset.
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` comes from a different brand's host.

  If any of these are removed or changed, the email breaks.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 41 `<img>` elements.** The hero and body slices (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_07.jpg`) and all 18 footer slices (`…/Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) have none. So do these product images:
  - `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`
  - `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`
  - `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`
  - `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`
  - `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg`

  The result is a blank email with images off, and screen readers get nothing. The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Three images use `http://`:** `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `…0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn` pixel. They risk being blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`**, and the `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute.
- **`viewport` sets `maximum-scale=1`**, which disables pinch-zoom.
- **PHP code leaked into the `<style>` block:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …")`. This is invalid CSS and can break the rule that follows it in strict parsers.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.** This includes the outer wrapper and the `<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` spacer table. The other content tables do have it.
- **The Google Fonts `<link>` in `<head>`** is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so the Montserrat fallback relies on inline font stacks.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** were found in the visible source.
- **The recipient email address is exposed in plain text in URLs:**
  - `…/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The `svopn` request is cleartext HTTP, so the address can be seen in transit and ends up in proxy and server logs.
- `coreemail.html` is loaded as an `<img>` and returns a page URL, not an image. It may be a mis-typed pixel or may not render.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are missing at the source, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules for one-click unsubscribe are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown.
- **The footer could not be checked.** It appears to be built from `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` (all without alt), and the truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link or postal address. If either exists only inside an image, it is unreadable with images off. Verify against the full source.
- The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the link and image subdomains, which is good for alignment.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters can be verified.** Destinations sit behind `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` and were not resolved, and the source is truncated. Reading `qs` and the final URLs would confirm the campaign tags and landing pages.
- Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which matches the site domain. Whether those links land on the right pages is unconfirmed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images and the product shots, and use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels and spacers. Make sure the unsubscribe and address text in the footer exists as live text, not only as image slices.
2. Serve the three `http://` images over HTTPS. Move the email address out of the `svopn` and `coreemail` query strings, or hash it.
3. Delete the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from `<style>`. Add `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
4. Replace the 2022 and 2025 hotlinked assets and the `edm.westelm.com` spacer with current assets on the sender's own host.
5. Check the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Resolve the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects to confirm UTM tags and landing pages.
