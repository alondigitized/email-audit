# NEW ITEMS ADDED! Don’t miss our 4th of July Warehouse Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Fourteen. That's my Crate & Barrel family inbox count over the last four days — and this one is the SALE sub-brand sliding back into my inbox with the exact same subject line I already saw on June 25th. Word for word. "NEW ITEMS ADDED! Don't miss our 4th of July Warehouse Sale." This is a resend to non-openers, and I was an opener, which means their suppression logic either failed or doesn't exist. That's the first thing I noticed before I even looked at the content.
- The email itself is actually fine. Clean grid layout, a KitchenAid mixer front and center, cookware, some furniture, sale percentages hanging off the products. The kitchen appliances block is the strongest section — I've had a stand mixer on the list for the second house since March and the De'Longhi pitch from the June 24th kitchen-electrics send (scored it 8/10, the best of this run) already warmed me up. So there's product relevance here. But this is furniture/kitchen soup rather than a tightly curated "new items" reveal. I expected a focused drop of what was actually added. I got a recycled sale layout.
- Would I click? Maybe on the mixer link, same as I would have two days ago. Would I forward it? No. The cadence is burning whatever trust the brand had built over a genuinely good product run this week.

## What's working

- **KitchenAid mixer hero** — visible product, visible savings callout, right at the eye-line. That's the only thing that almost got me to click.
- **Sale percentages anchored to products** — "Up to X% off" attached to specific product rows is better than a vague hero banner.
- **4th of July framing is clean** — seasonal urgency reads as honest rather than manufactured. I know the sale ends around the holiday.
- **No render bugs** — layout held, no broken images, text didn't overflow. Technically clean send.

## What's weak

- **Exact same subject line as two days ago** — this is the cardinal sin for a subscriber like me who actually opens. Resend logic should suppress openers. Full stop.
- **"NEW ITEMS ADDED!" is doing too much work** — if you're going to lead with new items, show me the new items in a dedicated grid. This email doesn't isolate the additions; it just re-serves the whole warehouse sale layout with the same product mix I already scanned.
- **ALL CAPS subject opener** — kills any premium positioning. CB2's "PRIVATE SANCTUARIES" from June 24th (also in my inbox this week) signals something. ALL CAPS signals outlet.
- **No loyalty or member pricing angle** — Pottery Barn has been hinting at member-only pricing in this same cycle. Crate & Barrel SALE just keeps saying percent-off like anyone can get it, which makes it feel less like a subscriber perk.
- **Kitchen and furniture are competing** — the mixer and the dining table are fighting for the same click. Article's "Bedroom storage essentials" from this week picked one job and did it. This email is trying to be every department at once.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress openers from the resend** — this is an operations fix, not a creative fix, but it's the highest-leverage change. If I opened it Tuesday, don't send me the same subject line Thursday.
- 2. **Create an actual "New Items" grid** — if you're claiming items were added, front-load a 3-4 product row of genuinely new additions with a "Just added to sale" label. Make me feel like I got early access.
- 3. **Split kitchen from furniture** — two targeted sends would outperform one sprawling one. The mixer deserves its own moment.
- 4. **Add a deadline line in the body** — "Sale ends July 4th" should be a visible text callout, not just implied by the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Just added to the sale: mixer, cookware + more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New warehouse finds before July 4th`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The items that just landed — and the ones going fast.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 25% off select kitchen and furniture. Ends July 4.`

## Technical Audit — Crate & Barrel "4th of July Warehouse Sale"

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Broken webfont assets (403):**
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/1e07d600-3787-4517-bd3d-b140f9e54ec2.woff2`
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/7ec25fd5-2752-4d84-b307-af3faabc21bb.woff2`
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/aaa41944-25b3-4265-80b2-5564efe9b2d7.woff2`
- `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/fd5f8f50-d539-4a7d-b893-99501d746ef2.woff2`

All four return HTTP 403. The fonts load via `@font-face` in the `<head>` CSS; clients that attempt to fetch them (primarily webmail like Gmail) will silently fall back to the `font-family` stack. This is not a click-path breakage but degrades typographic rendering.

**22 click-tracking links skipped:** All CTAs route through a redirect domain — probing was skipped. Destination URL integrity (landing pages, UTM passthrough) is unverified by the QA run.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**35 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Notable categories:

| Type | Count | Example src |
|---|---|---|
| Product/hero images | 25 | `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` |
| Open-pixel trackers (mi.crateandbarrel.com) | 6 | `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` |
| Third-party trackers (sr.rlcdn.com, dv.crateandbarrel.com) | 4 | `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string) to suppress screen reader announcement. Product/hero images need descriptive alt text for image-blocked inboxes (Gmail clips, Outlook default-off images).

**CSS media query breakpoint mismatch:** Two `@media` blocks define `img { max-width }` rules — one at `min-width: 768px` (600px cap) and one at `min-width: 640px` (640px cap). The 640px rule overrides the 768px rule at viewports 640–767px, producing an unexpected 640px image cap at tablet widths. Order should be reversed or consolidated.

**Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags:** `telephone=no`, `address=no`, and `date=no` are each declared twice — once in a combined meta and once as separate metas. Redundant but harmless; indicates template hygiene issue.

**Left-over template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is present in the `<head>`. Placeholder comment was not removed before send.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe-Post absent** (WARN): RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not confirmed. Required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements as of February 2024.

**Authentication-Results header absent** (WARN): SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown due to relay capture gap. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. This is a relay instrumentation gap, not necessarily a sender-side failure — but it must be verified at the MTA level.
