# SO MUCH NEW
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "SO MUCH NEW" is CB2 in a good mood again — this is the same energy as the 7/27 "Just in. Already trending." send that I gave a 9, and it earns it for similar reasons: real new-arrival photography (that camel velvet sofa in the hero is genuinely nice), designer credits on almost every piece, and then it doesn't stop at inspiration — it backs into an actual "ends tomorrow" 40% off furniture event and a 30% off bedroom+bath event with free bedding shipping. That's the CB2 playbook when it's firing on all cylinders, and this is roughly CB2 email number 39-ish in my July inbox by my count, so at minimum they're consistent.
- Where it loses me a little is the subject line itself — "SO MUCH NEW" tells me nothing concrete, no % off, no category, nothing that would make me open this over the other four CB2 emails sitting in my inbox this week. Compare that to Wayfair, which at least screams the category and discount right in the subject (even if it's obnoxious about it with the emoji spam). CB2's body copy does the heavy lifting here, not the subject.
- I'd click — the Furniture Event banner and the "MORE FOR YOU" personalized rec grid with real prices (that $849 platform bed, marked down from $999) are exactly the kind of thing that gets me to a product page. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, this is just a solid regular send, not a special one. Mark as read, maybe click through to the bed later tonight.

## What's working

- The hero sofa shot is genuinely scroll-stopping — warm wood tones, good styling, doesn't look like stock photography.
- Designer name-drops (David Girard on the Aldine Sofa, Studio Anansi on the Rio Dining Table, David Aguirre on the Teya Pendant) give this a showroom-credibility feel that Article's editorial sends chase but don't always land as cleanly.
- "ENDS TOMORROW — THE FURNITURE EVENT — UP TO 40% OFF" is a real deadline with a real number, not the vague "LAST CHANCE" nonsense Wayfair pulls.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom shows actual products with actual strikethrough prices ($999→$849 on the bed) — that's the kind of personalization Pottery Barn's "table essentials" send didn't even attempt.
- Rewards math is spelled out plainly: "Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars every day at CB2 and Crate&Barrel" — I know exactly what I get for using my card here.

## What's weak

- Subject line "SO MUCH NEW" is filler — zero specificity, no hook, easy to skip past when I've already got three other CB2 emails sitting unread.
- No preview/preheader text came through distinct from the subject, so the inbox line adds nothing extra to make me tap in.
- The two sale events (Furniture Event, Bedroom+Bath Event) are buried about two-thirds of the way down — if I don't scroll past six product blocks, I never see the actual discount.
- Cadence is the real killer here — this is at least the fifth-ish CB2 send in my inbox this stretch, and "SO MUCH NEW" doesn't do anything to differentiate itself from "STEALS OF THE WEEK" or "Just in. Already trending." three days ago.
- No dimensions, materials, or lead-time info on any featured piece — for a renovation-mode buyer like me deciding between a sofa and a dining table, that's the info I actually need before I click through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to carry the actual offer — "up to 40% off" or "ends tomorrow" belongs in the subject, not buried six scrolls down. This is the single biggest lever here.
- 2. Move the Furniture Event banner up near the top, right under the hero — lead with urgency + discount, then let the new-arrivals showcase follow as browsing content.
- 3. Add one line of product specs (dimensions or "ships in 2 weeks") under at least the hero sofa and dining table — renovation shoppers are comparing real fit, not just vibes.
- 4. Space out sends — five CB2 emails in a week trains me to skim past all of them, including the good ones like this.
- 5. Add a preheader that previews the sale, not just repeats "so much new."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off — new arrivals just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tomorrow: 40% off the Furniture Event`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New sofas, dining tables + lighting — plus 40% off before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Rewards Dollars are waiting. Shop new arrivals + today's sale.`
