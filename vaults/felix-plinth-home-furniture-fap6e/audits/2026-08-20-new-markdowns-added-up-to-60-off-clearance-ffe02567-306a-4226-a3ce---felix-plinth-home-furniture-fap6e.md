# NEW MARKDOWNS ADDED: Up to 60% off clearance
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the second Crate & Barrel clearance email I've gotten this cycle (I audited "up to 60% off clearance" yesterday too), and creatively it's basically the same skeleton — beach hero shot, "up to 60% off" banner, the same category grid of furniture/outdoor/kitchen/tabletop/lighting/decor/rugs/bedding. But then it just... keeps going. It tacks on a Clean Kitchen icon push, a JURA espresso machine ad, a "new fall table" tablescape, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download nudge, and a Design Desk appointment CTA. That's seven or eight different offers crammed into one scroll.
- The dining table and bed products in the "we love these for you" grid actually land close to home — I'm mid-renovation on both properties and a $1,799 extendable dining table with real pricing shown is exactly the kind of thing I'd screenshot for my wife. But the clearance hook from the subject line gets buried after one category grid and never really comes back with specifics — no dollar amounts, no "starting at," just a beach photo and a banner.
- Verdict: I'd scroll past the first two screens, actually pause on the "we love these for you" table/bed grid since real prices are visible, and probably not click through the whole thing. Not forwarding this one — too scattered to send a friend a clean link.

## What's working

- The hero banner is unambiguous — "up to 60% off clearance" with a Shop Now button, no guessing required.
- The category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop & bar, lighting, decor & pillows, rugs, bedding & bath) is genuinely useful for someone renovating two houses at once — I can see myself tapping "outdoor" for the second home.
- The "we love these for you" section shows actual dollar amounts ($1,799, $2,299, $2,159, $1,995, $749, etc.) — that's the honest-pricing signal Crate & Barrel's Design Desk section and yesterday's clearance send both lacked until this point.
- "Ships Free" tag under bedding & bath is a small trust cue that matters when I'm comparing shipping costs across Pottery Barn, Wayfair, and this.

## What's weak

- Way too many offers stacked in one email — clearance, Clean Kitchen, JURA espresso, fall table, product picks, credit card, app download, Design Desk. It's the CB2 "seven sends this week" problem but compressed into a single scroll.
- The subject promises clearance specifics ("up to 60% off") but the body never shows a single markdown price next to a clearance item — no before/after pricing, no "now $X" tags on the category tiles.
- The JURA espresso machine and Clean Kitchen inserts feel like a totally different email that got pasted in — zero connective tissue to "clearance," and neither is furniture/renovation relevant to me.
- Credit card pitch ("the card you love just got better... Reward Dollars") this deep in a clearance email is noise — I'm not opening a store card mid-scroll for a sale email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two sends: a lean clearance email (hero + category grid + actual markdown prices) and a separate "new arrivals / fall table / JURA" lifestyle email. Right now it's diluting its own subject line promise.
- 2. Put real before/after pricing on at least 3-4 clearance category tiles instead of a single generic banner — that's the difference between "believe the 60%" and "vague sale language."
- 3. Move the "we love these for you" product grid up near the top, right after the hero — that's the strongest, most specific content in the email and it's buried two-thirds down.
- 4. Drop the credit card and app-download blocks entirely from clearance sends, or push to a dedicated footer-only mention.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off — new clearance prices just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real markdowns on furniture, lighting, and bedding — while it lasts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dining tables from $1,799, beds from $2,299 — clearance refreshed`
