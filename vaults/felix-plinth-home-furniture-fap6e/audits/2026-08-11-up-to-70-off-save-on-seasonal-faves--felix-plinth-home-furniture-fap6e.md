# 😎 UP TO 70% OFF 👉 Save on seasonal faves 👉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the classic Wayfair category-grid email — a wall of "up to X% off" tiles for every room in the house, no hero product, no story. My gut reaction: it's a spreadsheet dressed up as a marketing email. Bedding, mattresses, curtains, rugs, fireplaces, mirrors, grills, wall art — twelve different tiles all shouting a percentage at me before I've even scrolled past the fold. Compared to yesterday's chandelier email (2026-08-11, "People ♥ THIS CHANDELIER," 6/10) which at least had one gorgeous hero shot to anchor the eye, this one just fires a shotgun of discounts and hopes something sticks.
- The "picked just for you" section is the one interesting beat — a corgi at a front door, a dining set, a lantern on a coffee table, a dresser — but nothing there screams "we know Felix is mid-renovation on two houses." It's generic aspirational-home content, the same stuff CB2's "Prepare to want it all" dumped on me (5/10) minus even the discount banner CB2 skipped.
- I'm not clicking. I might scroll for ten seconds looking for the rug or curtain tile since I'm actually curtain-shopping right now, but this is a mark-as-read, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — there's nothing here specific enough to send a friend.

## What's working

- The top banner is clear at a glance: 70% off summer markdowns, no ambiguity about what this email is.
- Category tiles (curtains & drapes 50%, area rugs 70%) are relevant to my actual renovation shopping list right now.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" is a real, useful trust signal for furniture buyers worried about freight costs — something Article and CB2 rarely lead with.
- Pay-over-time financing logos (Affirm, Afterpay, Klarna) are visible and relevant since I'm juggling two home budgets simultaneously.
- Star ratings under the "picked just for you" tiles add a hint of social proof missing from most of these grid emails.

## What's weak

- Zero hero product — every prior Wayfair send at least had one anchor image (yesterday's chandelier, the slipcover grid); this is discount tiles top to bottom with nothing to fall in love with.
- No personalization signal at all — this could've landed in anyone's inbox, renovator or not, no "based on your curtain browsing" callout.
- Rewards math is invisible — I use my card specifically for stacked rewards and this email never mentions Wayfair Rewards until the footer icon row, buried after four screens of tiles.
- Repetitive percentage-off tiles (50%, 70%, 40%, 55%, 40%, 50%, 70%, 40%, 50%, 40%) blur together — by tile six I've stopped reading the numbers.
- The exact same lamp/furniture problem I complain about every week — this dresser and lantern shot could be lifted straight from a Pottery Barn or CB2 email; nothing here is distinctly Wayfair.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero product tied to a real season/room story (like Article's Ashford dining launch) instead of opening with a wall of percentage tiles — give my eye somewhere to land first.
- 2. Cut the tile count in half. Ten-plus discount blocks is decision fatigue, not merchandising — pick the 4-5 categories most likely to convert and cut the rest.
- 3. Move Wayfair Rewards messaging up near the top banner, not buried in the footer icon row — if I'm a rewards member, tell me my points math up front.
- 4. Add one line of real personalization ("Because you viewed: Curtains") to the "picked just for you" section so it earns its name instead of being generic stock photography.
- 5. Swap the redundant %-off badges for one categorized module — "Living Room," "Bedroom," "Outdoor" — so scanning takes seconds, not a full scroll.

- 20 open/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 22 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, including the hero (`0811_SummerMarkdowns_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`), the financing banner, the Memorial Day skinny banner, product rating stars (`stars_9/10/11.png`, 8 instances), a promo button image (`0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`), and the open-tracking pixel `b.php`. Screen readers will announce these as blank/filename, or skip silently.
- 20 tracking-pixel `<img>` elements load over `http://` (non-TLS) — flagged as potentially blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading (Gmail proxy, Outlook), which would also break open tracking for those events.
- Extensive `@media` mobile-responsive rules and MSO/Outlook-specific conditional CSS present (`u-FullWidthBlock`, `data-outlook-cycle` fixes, Gmail-specific padding hacks) — indicates deliberate multi-client rendering support; no structural rendering defects identified from the available source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) observed in the visible/truncated HTML. No issues found in the reviewed portion — note the source was truncated, so tokens later in the document could not be assessed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
