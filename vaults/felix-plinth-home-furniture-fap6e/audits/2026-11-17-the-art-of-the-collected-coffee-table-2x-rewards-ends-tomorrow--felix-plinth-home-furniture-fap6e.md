# The art of the collected coffee table + 2x rewards ends tomorrow
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh CB2 email in about four days. Earlier today I got "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY ENDS TOMORROW," and now it's "2x rewards ends tomorrow." My inbox is running on a CB2 loop. The hero is a statement coffee table in a "Statement Table" lockup, and it's a good one. It's moody and warm, and it looks like a Pinterest board. That's the folder I'd actually save this to.
- The body is a "One table, three looks" lookbook: Minimalist, Collector, Decorator. I'm renovating two houses, so a styled-vignette email like this is useful to me. The problem is the ending. After the nice editorial, it dumps a "Now up to 70% off clearance" banner, an Interior Design Services block, and a product grid. It reads like two emails stitched together. The 2x rewards deadline is the thing that makes me act, and it sits in a tiny dark strip at the top.
- Would I click? Probably one tap on "Shop coffee tables." Would I forward it? Maybe to my partner, for the Collector vignette. Honestly I'd save it and not buy today.

## What's working

- The "One table, three looks" structure is a real idea. I get styling direction, not just SKUs.
- The photography is warm and consistent. The amber and bronze palette (the Via knot sculpture, the Bangle bowl) holds together the way I want when I'm matching two houses.
- "Shop coffee tables" appears twice, with a clear black button each time.
- The rewards strip ("Earn 20% back in rewards dollars") speaks to how I buy. I pick whichever card has the highest rewards.
- The "More for you" grid shows prices with markdowns, e.g. the Foley armchair at $319.00, so I can do the math at a glance.

## What's weak

- The "2x rewards ends tomorrow" urgency is in the subject, but the in-email version is a tiny dark band at the top. It's hard to read on a phone.
- The email stacks three competing messages: the Early Black Friday banner, the 70% off clearance block, and the design services block. I couldn't tell what the main offer was.
- The Black Friday header and the clearance "70% off" repeat what this morning's send said. I've seen this exact promo today.
- The coffee table hero has no price and no sale framing. I can't tell whether the table is part of the discount.
- The fine print at the bottom is long and dense. I can't read it without zooming, and I don't know which exclusions apply to the 2x rewards.
- The shipping and lead-time info I need for furniture isn't anywhere. A table that "works in any style" doesn't tell me whether it arrives in two weeks or twelve.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the rewards deadline the second hero element, directly under the coffee table image. Give it a big number, "2X rewards, ends tomorrow," with one button.
- 2. Cut the email to one story. Either run the coffee table lookbook or run the clearance, not both. Wayfair does a better job here. Its interior doors send at least stayed on doors.
- 3. Put prices on the lookbook items (the Semele bowl, the Iris candle holders, the Emani sculpture) so it's shoppable.
- 4. Add a shipping or lead-time line to the coffee table hero. Crate & Barrel does this better on large-furniture sends.
- 5. Add a free swatch or sample link, plus a pointer to the design services showroom.
- 6. Space out the cadence. Pottery Barn's 70% off pillows send also flooded my inbox this week, so a quieter CB2 would stand out.
