## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's links carry per-recipient tracking, and the sampled links appear to point to appropriate destinations. QA confirmed no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The real defects are missing alt text on linked images (including two that are the only content of a link), 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and an `http://` app fallback URL. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 52 skipped. 8 of the probed links returned 429 (rate limited), so they are unverified, not broken. These include the `daily-sales`, homepage, pergola event and rugs event URLs.
- **4 links were never probed** (70s time budget), so they are neither pass nor fail:
  - `.../california-king-bedding-sets-se74547`
  - `.../kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.htm`
  - `.../pergolas-for-less--se82165.html?eid=`
  - `.../wayfair-rewards?_emr=...`
- **Possibly truncated URLs.** The pergolas-for-less URL ends in a bare `?eid=`, and the rewards URL ends mid-token (`...e5f9ec7aa`-style cutoff). This is probably truncation in the QA report, not the source. Confirm against the raw HTML.
- **Mismatched campaign label on the app link.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` has `c=Triggered`, while the rest of the email is a batch send (`source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`). This mislabels app-install attribution.
- **Insecure fallback URL.** The app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP and depends on a redirect.
- **Banner destination differs from the CTA in the same module.** `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple` links to the homepage `wayfair.com/?...`, but the "On sale now: pergolas" link in the same module goes to the pergola event. Both share `sltid=5` and `mdlid=e71f1500-...`. This could be a deliberate brand header, but the image click doesn't reach the subject's event.
- **Open tracking pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=<base64>` decodes to `mcch=ECM&eventType=EmailOpen&source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15...`, which is expected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images with no alt text.** These have no accessible name, and the QA link text falls back to `[image: …]`:
  - `1113-US-BF-Email-VP.gif`, linking to `/daily-sales?...sltid=4`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, linking to `/?...sltid=5`
- **Other content images with no alt text:**
  - `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24...`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_1_purple.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_3_purple.png`
  - `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`, a disclaimer rendered as an image, so its text is unavailable to screen readers and with images off
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`, a placeholder-named asset served in production
- **20 `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…019&sz=2x1` pixels.** All lack alt, and all use `http://`, which Gmail and Apple Mail may block or proxy-rewrite. Impressions may be under-counted. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML is truncated, so `lang`, the viewport meta, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and the dark-mode CSS can't be verified. The visible CSS has a 480px breakpoint and a Gmail `u ~ div` fix.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the supplied source or URLs. Per-recipient identifiers are populated: `_emr=f36d1b00-...` is constant across links, and `_eml=` is unique per link.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe is absent from the captured headers.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this isn't confirmed as a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is absent**, with the same caveat. It is required for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results is absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` is unknown.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and none was flagged, but its URL isn't in the output. The footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any URL.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters (`source=graymatter_batch`, `refid=MKTEML_121289`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16806`, `mdlid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `varid=4`). This works for first-party analytics but won't map into GA-style reporting without custom handling.
- **Subject-to-landing alignment is good.** "On sale now: pergolas" links to `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.html?eid=603456&unifiedeventid=603456`, and the event ID is consistent across both parameters. The landing page was not verified because of the 429.
- **Deep link.** `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` opens the app home, not the pergola event, which is the same generic-landing issue as the banner link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked images (`1113-US-BF-Email-VP.gif`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`). Use `alt=""` on the 20 `imp` pixels and `b.php`.
2. Move the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Replace `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg` with live text. Remove or replace `blankBanner (1).png`.
4. Fix `c=Triggered` to a batch campaign value, or confirm that value is intentional.
5. Point the header banner at the pergola event, or confirm it is meant to go to the homepage.
6. Re-run the link probe with throttling and retry/backoff on 429. Probe the 4 skipped links and verify the `?eid=` and `_emr` URLs against the raw HTML.
7. Capture raw headers from the original MX or a direct inbox to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat these as sender failures until then.
8. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` if cross-tool attribution is needed.
