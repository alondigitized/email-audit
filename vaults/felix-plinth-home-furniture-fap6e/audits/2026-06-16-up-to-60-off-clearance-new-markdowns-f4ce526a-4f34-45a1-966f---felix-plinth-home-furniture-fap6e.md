# Up to 60% off clearance + new markdowns!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-16**
## Executive summary

- Sixty-seven. That's where the C&B family counter sits in my inbox after this one lands, and the reason it matters is that I already got a C&B clearance email this morning — "Selling fast: up to 60% off clearance!" — and gave it a 7. Same clearance event. Same day. Different sub-brand sender. I understand the ESP logic: Crate & Kids is a distinct list, Crate & Barrel is a distinct list, and somewhere in a campaign calendar someone checked both boxes. But on my end it's two tabs in the same inbox folder, and the second one reads like a mistake.
- The Crate & Kids brand at least has a clear identity — I know exactly what it is and what it sells, which puts it ahead of some of the vague umbrella sends I've been wading through. The layout is clean. The hero lands immediately on the discount. The "lilac color of the year" section actually surfaced a color story I hadn't seen in the main C&B send, and I've been thinking about accent colors for the secondary home's spare room. That's a real moment, even if it's buried.
- But "we love these for you" showed me a stroller and a glider. I'm 36, not a new parent shopping a registry. The algorithm has no idea who I am here. Mark as read.

## What's working

- **Offer in the hero, no scroll required**: "up to 60% off + new markdowns" is the first thing you see — no hunting for the discount underneath lifestyle photography.
- **Clean render, nothing broken**: layout reads correctly, no overlapping text, no broken image gaps.
- **"Lilac color of the year" is a genuine sub-brand differentiator**: Pottery Barn would have made this a whole send — a color story with specific SKUs is more useful than a generic clearance sweep, and C&B main didn't do this today.
- **Navigation is specific to the sub-brand**: Furniture / Toys / Bedding / Baby Furniture tells me exactly what I'm in — it's not pretending to be a general home store.
- **Credit card section registers**: I buy on whichever card earns the most, so seeing the C&B card promo is at least a relevant signal, even if it's not my highest earner.

## What's weak

- **Second clearance email today from the same parent brand**: Pottery Barn sent me a beautifully targeted bedding email on June 14 and hasn't followed up with a near-identical twin from Pottery Barn Kids the same afternoon. The C&B team did exactly that.
- **"We love these for you" is a stroller and a glider**: If the algorithm knows I'm a C&B subscriber browsing adult furniture, it failed completely here. The recommendations feel like defaults, not personalization.
- **No deadline, no urgency**: "Shop Clearance" with no end date is how you lose a click to "I'll look at this later." Wayfair — even though I find them annoying at volume — at least committed to a specific product dimension ("8' x 10' outdoor rugs") in their last subject. This is just a portal.
- **TheDesignDesk is a footer whisper**: CB2 has been leading with their free design consult as an actual value prop. A nursery design service could be genuinely useful to someone furnishing a kids' room, but it's buried below the app download.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Hold the sub-brand send 24 hours after the parent brand clearance fires**: If C&B main already hit the list today, Crate & Kids should either queue for tomorrow or explicitly frame the distinction ("Crate & Kids clearance is a separate inventory pool — different items, same event").
- 2. **Add a hard deadline**: "Sale ends Sunday" or "New markdowns added — shop before they sell out" is the minimum I need to click now instead of later.
- 3. **Show one hero product with real numbers**: A crib marked down from $799 to $349 beats a floral hero image with a percentage. Wayfair overdoes dimensions but they commit to specifics — this email could learn that.
- 4. **Move the design desk above the fold**: "Free nursery design consult" as a top-of-email hook would give this send a reason to exist that the C&B main send doesn't offer.
- 5. **Tighten the behavioral targeting**: If my C&B browse/purchase history is adult furniture, don't fire the Crate & Kids list until I've shown a kids-category signal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Crate & Kids clearance: up to 60% off — new items added`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Lilac is the color. 60% off is the reason.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nursery furniture, kids beds, toys — prices just dropped again.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns added. Shop before sizes sell out.`

**Conflicting `max-width` media queries** — two desktop rules are defined in order:
