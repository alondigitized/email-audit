# Thoughtful gifts for everyone on your list 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the 15th or so Pottery Barn email in about a week, and it's the gift guide I knew was coming. It opens with a green "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" strip, and that's the only offer I can find. Then comes a big "GREAT GIFTS" hero in a dark, moody photo. The gift guide below is pretty. It's also aimed at a different shopper than I am this week, because I'm buying a sofa and a dining table, not stocking stuffers.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid at the bottom is where I perked up. Barkley Extendable Dining Table, Toscana Extendable Dining Table, a swivel chair. That's my actual project, and it's buried under six gift tiles and a price-tier row. Compared to the 11/11 "Up to 70% off across the home" send, which led with furniture and in-stock delivery, this one feels like a step backward. Would I click? Probably the dining tables, and only because I scrolled. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to the Pinterest folder only if the photography were better.

## What's working

- The "Up to 70% off" strip is a real, specific offer, and it sits right at the top where I see it first.
- The photography is well styled. The monogrammed gifts and the olive-wood serving board look like what Pottery Barn does best.
- "Gifts under $30 / $50 / $100" is a clean way to browse, and it's easy to tap on a phone.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid put dining tables in front of me. That's the one segment of the email that read as personalized.
- The Design Crew Sweeps, the 10% back in Rewards card and Affirm financing are all useful for me. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so the rewards line matters.

## What's weak

- The sale strip and the gift guide don't match. "Up to 70% off" says home sale, while "GREAT GIFTS" says gifting. I can't tell whether the discount applies to any of it.
- Every gift tile uses the same script-font overlay ("our top 100 gifts", "curated just for them"). The labels blur together, and none of them has a price.
- The best furniture content is in the second half of a very long email. Crate & Barrel's "up to 30% off furniture" send led with exactly that.
- There are about eight "Shop Now" or "Shop All" CTAs competing, so no single action stands out.
- The legal fine print is dense and tiny. It's unreadable on a phone without zooming, and that makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by browse behavior. I've been viewing dining tables and bedding, so lead my version with that and put the gifts below it.
- 2. State the offer once, clearly: what "up to 70%" covers and when it ends.
- 3. Put prices on the gift tiles and cut the script-font labels to one style.
- 4. Move the furniture block up, with in-stock delivery dates like the 11/11 send had.
- 5. Cut the CTA count to one primary button per section.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dining tables, up to 70% off, in stock now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts under $100 + up to 70% off home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extendable tables and swivel chairs ship before the holidays. Plus gifts by price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your picks are on sale, and Rewards members earn 10% back.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Thoughtful gifts for everyone on your list 🎁"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from sliced, image-heavy tables (SFMC-style `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects) and has widespread alt-text gaps, three non-HTTPS image sources, and subscriber email exposed in tracking URLs. The header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed, because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 59 links point to tracking/redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), so the QA probe skipped them. Final landing URLs, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Mixed content:** three images load over `http://`, which some clients may block or rewrite:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (open pixel)
- **Stale assets:** the footer banner is served from a 2022 path (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`). A cross-brand spacer, `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, is also still referenced.
- **Pixels:** several tracking pixels are present:
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (third-party)
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/13036fcd-...`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains leaked PHP (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`). It is invalid CSS and may break parsing of rules that follow it in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 55 images have none. Examples:
  - `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`
  - `slices_01`–`slices_13.jpg` in `/1102_THU_AM_GiftGuideLaunch/`
  - `slices_01`–`slices_18.jpg` in `/Email-Footer/`
  - product images such as `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`

  Most of the content is sliced images, so with images off or a screen reader the email is largely unreadable. The tracking pixels and spacer can keep an empty `alt=""`, which is acceptable.
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (a WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Webfont:** Montserrat is loaded via a `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com`. Many clients ignore `<link>`, so there is no guarantee it applies.
- **Preheader:** the hidden preheader ("For him, for her, for the host (& more!)") is present and padded correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were detected in the visible source or QA output.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears in plaintext in these query strings:
  - `coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com/?...sv_sveme=...`

  This is an unhashed identifier that can leak through logs and referrers. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` reduces the leakage but does not remove it from server logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found. The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed sender failure. Without them, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules) cannot be verified.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured. Authentication status is unknown.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the HTML source was truncated before the footer, so neither could be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable:** every link is wrapped in a click-redirect (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) with an opaque `qs` payload. The destination URLs, any UTM parameters and landing alignment cannot be confirmed from the source or the skipped probe. The only visible direct links are product images on `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which are assets rather than landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content slices, hero banners and product images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Change the three `http://` image sources to `https://`, including the `svopn` open pixel.
3. Remove the leaked PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add a `<title>` and `lang="en"`.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in query strings. Use a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw original message rather than the relay copy. Check SPF/DKIM/DMARC the same way.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links in a follow-up run to check destinations and UTMs, and retire the 2022 and West Elm-hosted assets.
