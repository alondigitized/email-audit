## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair marketing send (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, table-based, with responsive media queries) with no confirmed broken links. The main code-level issues are missing alt text on the images, about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and compliance headers the capture pipeline couldn't verify.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 of 85 links were probed and 73 were skipped by design. All 8 reported issues were HTTP 429 rate-limiting from Wayfair and the AppsFlyer OneLink, which the QA tool itself marks "link may be valid". Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` and `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...`.
- **4 product and category links were never checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out (shower door, Tilefy tile, NineStars trash can, `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`). Their status is unknown, not failed.
- **Mixed-content tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels use plain `http://`: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=33b672c2-…`. Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS images (some webmail proxies and privacy modes) will drop them, so impression counts for these placements may be undercounted.
- **Open-tracking pixel** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3BlbiZ…` has no alt attribute. This is minor, since pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Conflicting campaign parameters on the OneLink URL.** `c=Triggered` appears alongside `source=batch&batchid=3`. Attribution may classify a batch send as triggered.
- **The OneLink web fallback is not HTTPS.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images have no accessible name.** The QA tool derived link text from filenames: `[image: WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jp]` (links to `/daily-sales`) and `[image: stars_10.png]` and `stars_11.png` (each links to a product page). Screen readers get a filename or nothing for these links.
- **Alt text is missing on content images.**
  - Banners: `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`, `…Skinny-EverythingShip…`, `…Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`.
  - Promo images: `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`, `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`.
  - Star ratings: about 14 `review_stars/stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` instances, so the rating value is not conveyed.
  - If these banners carry promo text, that text is invisible when images are blocked.
- **Not confirmed.** The HTML source was truncated, so I can't assess the body structure, `lang` attribute or `role="presentation"` usage. The media queries and `@font-face` rule for "Sofia" include an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback, which looks fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) were found in the visible source or QA output. A recipient identifier is embedded in every URL and pixel: `_emr=33b672c2-61b1-4158-b0dd-1cc1a71db499` (also `p=` in the `li.wayfair.com` pixels). Together with per-link `_eml` values, it makes forwarded links trackable to the original recipient. This is standard ESP behavior and not a defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` can't be assessed from the data provided.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated footer. Per the QA note, an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the output, so I can't confirm it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are present.** Wayfair attributes with proprietary parameters instead: `refid=MKTEML_143901`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16780`, `csnid`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`. This is consistent across links (the `_eml` value differs per link by design), but it won't feed GA-style UTM reporting.
- **The subject is category-specific, but the visible CTAs ("Shop Sale", the top banner) land on the generic `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales`.** Product links go to individual SKU pages (for example `NST1031`, the Nostalgia popcorn cart). A popcorn or nut-roaster category landing page is not confirmed in the sampled links. This is only a code-level observation, since the landing target isn't aligned to the subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to all linked banners and product images, for example `alt="Rated 4.5 out of 5 stars"` on the star images. Use `alt=""` on the pure tracking pixels.
2. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Reconcile `c=Triggered` with `source=batch` in the OneLink campaign parameters.
4. Re-probe with a slower, throttled crawl (or retry after the 429s) to cover the 4 unprobed links and the rate-limited ones.
5. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy, before treating compliance as pass or fail.
6. If UTM-based analytics matter to the sender, add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside the existing `refid` parameters.
