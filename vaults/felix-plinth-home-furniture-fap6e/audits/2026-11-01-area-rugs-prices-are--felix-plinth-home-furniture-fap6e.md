## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from nested tables with a responsive media-query block and mostly HTTPS assets. It has widespread missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, and a few tracking-parameter inconsistencies. The header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 reported probe failures are HTTP 429 rate limits (onelink.me, `/daily-sales`, `/`, ottomans category, `on-sale-now:-area-rugs-se44339.html`). These are bot throttling, not dead links. They also mean those 8 destinations went unverified.
- **Probe coverage was thin.** Only 12 of 71 links were probed (59 skipped). 4 more were not checked because the 70s time budget ran out: the pergolas, outdoor-coverings and kitchen-mats `daily-sales/filters` URLs, and `/daily-sales/clearance`. Treat these as unknown.
- **Pergolas URL ends in `.htm`.** It is `...on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`, while sibling event URLs end in `.html` or have no extension. This may be truncation in the report. Verify it.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback in the app link.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` link has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Desktop users who fall through to the web will get an HTTP URL, which is an extra redirect hop.
- **Campaign label looks wrong.** The same link has `c=Triggered`, but the other params show a batch send (`source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`). This will misattribute AppsFlyer campaign data.
- **Unusual filename in an image URL.** The banner asset `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty` contains a raw apostrophe in the path. That risks inconsistent handling by proxies and clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** This affects the banner and header images (`WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`, `WFUS-OpenBox-2025-HVA-1.jpg`, `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, `1029_FallClearance_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`) and the four `wfus_jt800_batch9_livingroom_ottomans_general_layo…` images. Several of these are linked images. Their link text falls back to `[image: <filename>]` (for example `[image: wfus_jt800_batch9_livingroom_ottomans_ge]`), so image-blocked and screen-reader users get no usable link name.
- **Rating-star images have no alt.** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (×7) and `stars_11.png` (×3) are missing alt text, so the product ratings are not conveyed to assistive tech.
- **Test asset name in production.** `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` suggests a test asset reached the send.
- **Tracking pixels:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and `li.wayfair.com/imp` have no alt. For 1×1 or 2×1 pixels this is low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct form.
- **Responsive CSS is present.** I saw an `@media (max-width:480px)` block and a Gmail `u ~ div` fix. The source was truncated, so I can't assess the body markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and links contain no unrendered merge tokens. The `_emr` and `_eml` params are populated, not templated placeholders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed and shouldn't be read as a sender failure. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address.** The probe notes say an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but neither it nor the address appears in the truncated source. This is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses first-party params: `_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `source`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid` and `mdlid`. This is consistent across links and not an error. It does mean GA-style UTM reporting won't work without mapping.
- **Subject-to-landing alignment looks fine.** The "On Sale Now: Area Rugs" CTA goes to `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-area-rugs-se44339.html?eid=343332&unifiedeventid=343332`.
- **Several links go to generic destinations.** The "Wayfair" and header-image links go to `/`, and "Shop Sale" goes to `/daily-sales`. Landing-page content couldn't be checked because of the 429s.
- **Possible stale creative.** The banner `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-…` is named for an August Labor Day promotion, and `1029_FallClearance_Skinny-EndsTonight.png` is dated 10/29 in a 10/31 send. The asset names suggest reuse, but the image content can't be confirmed from the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the linked banners and ottoman images, and use `alt=""` on tracking pixels and decorative images.
2. Give the star-rating images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars".
3. Switch the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` impression pixels to HTTPS. Otherwise clients that block insecure content will undercount impressions.
4. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`, and correct `c=Triggered` to match the batch campaign.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or retry, and confirm the `.htm` pergolas URL.
6. Check the raw message headers at the source, outside the relay, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
7. Remove the test asset `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` from the production template and check that the Labor Day and 10/29 banners are intended.
