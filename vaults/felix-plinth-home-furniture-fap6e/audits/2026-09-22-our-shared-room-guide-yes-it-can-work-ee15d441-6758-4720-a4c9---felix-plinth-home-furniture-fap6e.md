# Our shared room guide (yes, it can work!) →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually the first Crate & Kids email this week that's got a wedge into my actual life — I've got two kids and a second house I'm renovating, and "shared room guide" is a real problem I'm solving right now, not aspirational nursery content. The bunk-bed hero and the "go vertical" storage section are genuinely useful for the second-house layout I'm chewing on. But then it just... keeps going. And going. Nursery rockers, a baby registry card pitch, "we love these for you" with cribs and gliders — none of that's me, and by the time I hit the app download banner I've completely lost the thread of why I opened this.
- Compare this to Article's "Storage solutions for every room" send I read recently — that one stayed disciplined, one hook, one payoff. This one starts strong then sprawls into every sub-category Crate & Kids sells like it's afraid to leave anything out. I'd click through on the bunk/shared-room content specifically. I would not scroll to the bottom, and I definitely wouldn't forward this — there's no single clean asset here I'd send a friend, it's too much of a catalog dump.
- **Verdict: partial click on the top module, mark as read after that, no forward.**

## What's working

- "our shared room guide" hero copy is the most targeted subject+hero combo I've gotten from this brand all week — actual signal, not just "cozy fall" filler.
- The bunk bed lifestyle shot at top is a real design vignette I could screenshot for my second-house kids' room board.
- "go vertical" storage module speaks directly to a real constraint (small shared room, need drawers/storage) — same beat Article hit better with their office nook storage email, but at least it's relevant here.
- Color-cohesion module ("keep color cohesive") actually maps to my second-home-plus-primary-home color-matching headache — rare for this brand to hit that.

## What's weak

- It sprawls into nursery gliders, baby registry cards, and "new! the card you just got better" halfway down — I don't have a baby, so this feels like the same category-bleed as the "Montessori toddler space" and "responsible design" sends from earlier this week.
- No concrete offer anywhere I can see — no %, no $ off, just "Book a Free Appointment" repeated three separate times. Pottery Barn's Design Services email led with the free-design hook once and got out; this repeats it like a nervous tic.
- The email is enormous — by the time I hit "it's all on the app" I've forgotten this started as a shared-room guide.
- "we love these for you" personalization block shows cribs/gliders, which tells me this isn't actually personalized to my browsing/purchase history — it's just generic Crate & Kids inventory.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this email in half — ship the shared-room/bunk-bed/storage content as its own tight send, and spin nursery/registry content into a separate campaign for people who've actually shown nursery intent.
- 2. Drop two of the three "Book a Free Appointment" CTAs — one strong ask beats three repeated nudges.
- 3. Add an actual offer — even a modest "15% off bunk & storage collection" would beat zero numeric offer in a 10-screen-long email.
- 4. Fix the personalization block — if "we love these for you" is going to show cribs to someone who bought bunk beds, don't call it personalized.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Two kids, one room, zero drawer fights →`
