# NEW ITEMS ADDED: Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox today — I already did the "kitchen brands" one and the proper Crate & Barrel Labor Day banner send, and now there's this one that's basically the same email again, just with "new items added" bolted onto the subject. My gut reaction: I've seen this hero, this rewards banner, and this scroll sequence before, just with slightly different row ordering. It reads like the exact same campaign re-triggered because a merchandiser added a rug SKU.
- The good news buried in here is that it's actually a decent renovation email if I pretend I haven't already opened two of its siblings — sofas, furniture, decor, rugs, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining, all with real percentages stacked top to bottom, no fluff. That's more relevant to me than 90% of what's landing this week. But the redundancy kills it. I'm not clicking this one — I already clicked (or didn't) the identical email a few hours ago, and my brain has filed "Crate & Barrel today" as done.
- Would I forward it? No. Would I mark as read and move on? Yes, immediately, probably without opening.

## What's working

- The percentage-off ladder down the scroll (40% sofas, 40% furniture, 60% decor, 60% rugs, 50% outdoor, 40% kitchen, 60% entertaining) is genuinely useful for someone furnishing two houses — I can scan and pick my lane.
- The "Double Rewards + Up to 60% off" hero banner is clear and the CTA buttons are consistent and thumb-friendly.
- Rugs and outdoor furniture sections are directly relevant to my second-home renovation — that's real targeting, better than Wayfair's cushion-bait subject from earlier this week.
- The Reward Dollars module up top ($20 back per $100, expiring 9/8) is a concrete, time-bound number, not vague "earn rewards" copy.

## What's weak

- This is a near-duplicate of the Crate & Barrel send I already reviewed today ("NEW ITEMS ADDED" is doing a lot of work to justify a re-send) — same hero language, same rewards banner, same category cascade.
- Crate&Kids shows up again mid-scroll with nursery furniture — that's the fourth Crate&Kids touch in my inbox today and I still don't have a nursery.
- The "Design Desk" sweepstakes and "15% off Beautyrest mattresses" blocks feel like filler tacked onto an already-long email — by that point I've scrolled past ten CTAs and stopped reading.
- No swatch/sample program mention anywhere, which is the one thing that would actually move me on a sofa or rug purchase I can't feel through a screen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress same-day re-sends to openers/non-openers of the earlier identical email — this exact duplication is the single biggest problem, bigger than any copy tweak.
- 2. Cut the Crate&Kids nursery block from a non-parent segment — I've now seen it four times today across three different emails.
- 3. Move rugs and outdoor furniture higher since those are my stated categories — right now they're rows 4 and 5 of ten.
- 4. Add a swatch/sample CTA near the sofa and rug sections — "order a swatch" would do more for me than another "Shop Now."

## Technical Audit — Crate & Barrel "NEW ITEMS ADDED" Email

- 77 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for redirect-wrapped links; not itself a defect, but means link-destination validity is unconfirmed for this run).
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels present: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 instances), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/*/o.gif` (open pixel), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp/RampID, fired 5x with incrementing `n=` param), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...?cp_tp=v` (Movable Ink dynamic content beacon). No broken or malformed tracking URLs identified.
- No issues found with primary CTA link structure in the visible source.

Content images without `alt` fail WCAG 1.1.1 and degrade the experience for screen-reader users and image-blocked clients (tracking pixels should use `alt=""` rather than an omitted attribute, but the content images need descriptive text).
- Template uses standard MSO conditional comments, Outlook `mso-*` fixes, and `@media` breakpoints at 460px/640px/768px — no structural rendering defects detected in the truncated source.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` present; no missing `<!DOCTYPE>` or malformed head tags observed.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

- Note: all three are flagged WARN, not confirmed failures — the sending domain (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`) is a mature ESP-managed domain where these mechanisms are typically present; the gap is most likely header capture at the AgentMail relay, not sender misconfiguration.
