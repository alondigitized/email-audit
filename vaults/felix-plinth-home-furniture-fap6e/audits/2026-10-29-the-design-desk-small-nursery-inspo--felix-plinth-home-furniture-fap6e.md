# The Design Desk: small nursery inspo →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel send today. Like the "100s of new items at up to 60% off!" email earlier, it comes from the Kids sender, and I'm renovating two houses, not setting up a nursery. My gut reaction was "wrong list again." The Design Desk framing is a nice editorial idea, and the sage-green nursery rooms are well shot. But it's built around babies, and I have no use for that.
- The most useful content for me is buried. The "Book a Free Appointment" design service is the only thing that fits a renovator, and the 60% clearance block sits in the lower third. That's the same markdown promo I already got in the morning sends, so it's a repeat. I'm also tired of the same lamp and the same clearance banner showing up across a brand family in one day.
- Would I click? No. Forward it? No. I'd skim the room photos, maybe save one image to my Pinterest folder, and mark it read.

## What's working

- The room photography is calm and editorial, with sage walls, soft drapery and a round mirror. It feels like a design magazine, closer to what Article does than to a coupon blast.
- The sections are clear ("maximize every inch," "conquering awkward corners," "looking up"). Each pairs a room scene with a small row of products.
- "Book a Free Appointment" is repeated as a button. A free design consult fits how I shop for a renovation.
- The product grid near the bottom shows names and prices, so I can judge things at a glance without clicking through.
- The layout rendered cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text that I could see.

## What's weak

- The whole concept is nursery-focused. Nothing signals that I'm a home renovator, so I feel like a stranger on the list.
- The 60% clearance block is a repeat of today's earlier promo. It's too far down to work as a hero, and it dilutes the editorial story.
- It's overloaded. Design editorial, a clearance event, a Halloween shop, a "we love these for you" grid, a card promo and an app pitch all share one email. Pottery Barn's maroon "Extra 20% off" strip is at least one consistent message.
- The "$500 chance" app promo and the credit card push feel tacked on. I'd want to see rewards math, and there isn't any.
- It's very long to scroll, and the small product captions would be hard to read on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send. Show nursery editorial only to people with baby or kids signals, and send me a living or dining version of the Design Desk instead.
- 2. Lead with one hero idea, either the editorial or the clearance event. Don't run both in the same email.
- 3. Cut the app, card and Halloween modules, or move them to their own send. That would make the appointment CTA the clear primary action.
- 4. Add swatch, sample and shipping-timeline info. For furniture, delivery lead times matter to me more than another inspiration shot.
- 5. Give the product grid larger type and a "Shop the room" link on each scene. CB2's Nina Garcia drop worked because every image tied back to a collection.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "The Design Desk: small nursery inspo →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML with a 460px mobile breakpoint, 87 redirect-wrapped links, and about 46 images flagged for missing alt text. It also loads several third-party and ESP tracking pixels. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be checked directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** all 87 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests (LiveRamp) load on open. The `s=` value matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier used in the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels. That suggests the same recipient-derived hash is passed to a third party.
- **Redundant pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` repeats 6 times with the same URL. There is also `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/636b3148-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. The repeats add requests with no benefit.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 46 `<img>` elements are flagged. Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, plus `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png` and `2605c227-….gif`, have no alt. The same applies to the Scene7 images `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_Tert` and `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`. Tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `mi`, `dv`) and spacers also lack `alt=""`, so screen readers may announce filenames or URLs.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resize blocked:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop user text scaling.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, so the later rule wins and the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Single breakpoint:** the only responsive breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports from 461px to 639px get no responsive rules.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared three times, once as `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` and again as separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags.
- **Template leftover:** the HTML contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML under the declared doctype. It also doesn't match the subject line.

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. Only the truncated portion could be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content and banner images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop the global `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. Remove the duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `rlcdn` pixel calls, keeping one of each.
4. Delete the conflicting `img{max-width}` media query, and fix the `&` in `<title>` with `&amp;`.
5. Add a mid-range breakpoint such as 600px, and remove the `IMPUT HERE…` template comment.
6. For the audit pipeline, capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relay copy. Also resolve the redirect chains with a `HEAD`/`GET` follow so UTM and landing-page checks can run.
