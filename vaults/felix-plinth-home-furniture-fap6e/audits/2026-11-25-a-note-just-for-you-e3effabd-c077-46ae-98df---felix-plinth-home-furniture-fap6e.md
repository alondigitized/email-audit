# A note just for you!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel again, and this time it's the Kids arm. By my count that's the eleventh-plus send from the C&B family in two days. "A note just for you!" is a fake-personal subject, and the body is a mass-mailed letter. It opens with "Hi," and no name. The "note" is a five-item numbered list of kids' gifts: wooden easel, bedroom makeover, personalized Nod chair, baby gear, Babyletto furniture. I don't have kids and I'm renovating two houses, so this is aimed at someone else.
- The "Shop the Sale" button is a muted red pill sitting under a wall of grey-on-grey text. It reads like a school newsletter, not a Black Friday send. Compare Pottery Barn's maroon "up to 70% off" banner, which at least puts the offer at the top. Here I have to read four paragraphs to find "up to 50% off." I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The numbered gift ideas are scannable, and each one links to a specific category (easel, rugs, Nod chair).
- "Free shipping on orders of $79+" is clearly stated, and for furniture shipping is what I care about.
- The Crate Card block mentions Reward Dollars. I'm a rewards-math guy, so that line got my attention.
- The "see it at your nearest store" strip with a map is a nice showroom tie-in.
- The footer category grid (Furniture, Decor, Storage) gets me to the main catalog quickly.

## What's weak

- There's no hero image or product photo above the fold. It's a wall of text on a grey background, and the offer is buried in the third paragraph.
- "A note just for you!" is a personalization bait-and-switch. It says "Hi," with no name and has the same body for everyone.
- There's one CTA, "Shop the Sale," and it's generic. It doesn't say which sale or what category.
- The "(and somewhere in there we celebrated Halloween)" intro is filler.
- The legal footer is huge and hides the actual terms. "Up to 50%*" and "up to 30%*" in a single email are hard to trust. Four more CTAs follow (app, Design Desk, Crate Card, store), and they compete with each other.
- It has no relevance to my renovation. I'm shopping furniture, lighting and decor, and nothing here covers that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a hero image and a single clear offer ("Up to 50% off kids' furniture + free shipping") above the fold. Cut the letter down to two sentences.
- 2. Replace the generic button with a category-specific one, like "Shop Kids' Furniture Deals."
- 3. Drop the fake-personal framing, or segment so it actually reaches parents. Don't send it to everyone on the C&B family list.
- 4. Cut the cross-promo blocks to one. The app, Design Desk and Crate Card pitches are too many CTAs.
- 5. Tell me about the cadence. After eleven sends in two days, a "this is your one Kids email this week" frame would be better.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's head is well-formed (lang, viewport, color-scheme, VML namespaces). The QA run flags two confirmed defects: an empty-name greeting ("Hi ,") and a 403 on the sweepstakes rules link. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the head, the QA data and the image inventory.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (403):** `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/app-sweepstakes` ("Official Rules"). A 403 may be bot-blocking of the QA probe rather than a user-facing failure. It still needs a manual check, because sweepstakes rules are legally required to be accessible.
- **Unverified links:** 51 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped, so their destinations and redirect chains are unvalidated.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are five requests to one LiveRamp endpoint. The only difference is the `n` counter, which suggests redundant firing. The `s=` value is a 64-hex string, consistent with a hashed email identifier, and the same hash appears in `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa278…`.
- **Other tracking pixels:** `https://s3enpwpv.emltrk.com/v2/s3enpwpv?i=99520999` is an open-tracking pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (real issue):**
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `…/m/1/2072d6b9-0ff6-4d6b-9743-38ba0fce3744.png`
  - `…/m/1/971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`

  If any of these carry content or links, they are inaccessible with images blocked or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (low impact):** `448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif` and the `emltrk.com` pixel should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Head markup is sloppy but harmless:** `<meta charset>` appears twice, and `format-detection` is declared four times with overlapping values. `.tPad-auto {padding-top:auto!important}` is invalid CSS, because `auto` isn't a valid padding value, so it is ignored.
- **Not assessable:** The body is truncated, so table structure, tap-target sizes and dark-mode handling can't be judged. The only responsive breakpoint seen is `max-width:460px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed defect:** The greeting renders with an empty name field ("Hi ,"). The fallback for a missing first name is absent or failed. This is a visible merge-token failure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. The QA tool notes AgentMail may not relay them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are unmet.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, which is consistent with standard ESP setup, but there's no evidence either way.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed or faulted.
- **Hashed identifier shared with a third party:** The `rlcdn.com` pixels pass what looks like a hashed email in the URL. That is worth confirming against the sender's privacy disclosures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. Landing-page and UTM alignment can't be evaluated, because 51 links were skipped and the truncated source shows no hrefs. The one probed destination, `/app-sweepstakes`, returned 403.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting fallback (e.g. "Hi there,") so a blank name never renders "Hi ,".
2. Verify `/app-sweepstakes` manually in a normal browser and from a non-bot client. If it is really 403ing for users, restore it before the sweepstakes runs.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` by checking raw headers at the receiving MTA, not through the relay. Do the same for SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three content images and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Dedupe the five `rlcdn.com` pixel calls unless the `n=` variants are intentional.
6. For future audits, capture the full HTML and resolve redirect chains on tracking links, so UTM and landing-page checks can run.
7. Clean up the head: remove the duplicate `meta charset` and `format-detection` tags and the invalid `padding-top:auto`.
