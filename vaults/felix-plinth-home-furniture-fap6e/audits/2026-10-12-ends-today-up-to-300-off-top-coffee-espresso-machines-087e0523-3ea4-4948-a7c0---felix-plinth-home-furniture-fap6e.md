# ENDS TODAY: Up to $300 off top coffee & espresso machines!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- I opened this because of the "ENDS TODAY" urgency, not because I want an espresso machine. My lists are sofas, lighting and rugs for two houses. This is the second Crate & Barrel send that's missed me today. The first-glance read is fine: a clean white layout, a big "up to $300 off" headline, and Shop Now buttons stacked down a very long scroll. It's tidy and easy to skim. It just isn't mine.
- The scroll is endless. Hero, then four or five product blocks, then a grid of "more from the Coffee & Espresso Event," a De'Longhi feature, a Caraway block, a "we love these for you" row, and finally an app promo and a design-desk pitch. I got the point after the first screen. Compared with 10/11's "Free shipping on orders over $99 ends TOMORROW!", which at least touched the sectional I'm shopping for, this is a category pivot with no link to my lists.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim to the bottom to see if any furniture showed up, find the design-desk CTA, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, because I have no friend shopping for a Le Creuset and an espresso machine this week.

## What's working

- The "ends today" headline plus "up to $300 off" is concrete, and the deadline reads as real.
- The product photography is big and clean, with one product per block and a Shop Now button under each. Nothing is broken or overlapping.
- Each block shows a sale price against a struck-through original, so the discount math is visible without clicking.
- The TheDesignDesk block at the very bottom ("your design pro is waiting") is the only thing that speaks to a renovator like me.

## What's weak

- Coffee and espresso machines have nothing to do with my sofa, lighting and rug lists. Nothing here signals that Crate & Barrel knows who I am.
- It's too long. At least five full-width product blocks, a grid and several side promos bury the one idea.
- The pitch overlaps with itself. The "ends today" free-shipping bar at the top and the "up to $300 off" headline fight for first place. This is the same "which deadline is it?" confusion I flagged on 10/11.
- The Shop Now buttons look identical every time and give no product detail like capacity, finish or ship date. I can't tell what I'd be buying without tapping.
- The cadence is heavy. Between the "Final weekend!" send and the free-shipping send, this is the third Crate & Barrel promo in about 48 hours.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus two or three products. Kill the repeated Shop Now blocks and the grid. A single decisive scroll beats a 12,000-pixel one.
- 2. Pick one deadline. Either lead with the $300 off or lead with free shipping, not both stacked.
- 3. Segment it. Send this to people who've browsed kitchen or small appliances, and give me a furniture or lighting version. Pottery Barn's Fall Sale at least keeps the same dark-green block, so I know what the send is about in half a second.
- 4. Add decision details under each product: finish, color options and ship timing.
- 5. Move the design-desk offer up. For a renovator that's the real hook.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML email with Movable Ink dynamic content. The QA run shows 57% pass, 0 failures, and 3 warnings. The main findings are about 45 images flagged for missing alt text, 88 tracking links that were not probed, and header checks that couldn't be verified because of the relay. The HTML given to me is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and link `href`s were not available to check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **88 tracking links were not HTTP-probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Per-recipient identifiers appear in image URLs.**
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` is a Movable Ink dynamic image carrying the recipient token.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e2f479a3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` look like open or view trackers.
  - If images are blocked, the dynamic modules won't render. I can't confirm from the truncated source whether fallbacks exist.
- **Five third-party pixels are present.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp (RampID) syncs. The `s=` value is the same token as `mi_u`, so the identifier is passed to a third party. This is a privacy and deliverability consideration, since extra third-party domains can add spam-filter weight.
- **One image is hosted on a different domain.** The `s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/…/2026%5F1012%5FCB%5FCoffeeEspressoEventEnds…` image (the `Socia…` filename suggests a social/share asset) comes from Adobe Scene7, while the rest come from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. That is not a defect, but it's an extra dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is flagged missing on about 45 images.**
  - This covers nearly every content image under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `*.gif`, plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
  - It also covers the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` dynamic modules and the scene7 image.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from `alt=""`. The 1×1 trackers (`rlcdn.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv…`) should be `alt=""`. The product and promo images need descriptive alt text. With images blocked, the coffee/espresso offer is unreadable if it is image-only.
- **The viewport meta disables pinch-zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Text scaling is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{…}`, `html{…}` and `body{…}`, so it overrides the `100%` set elsewhere. This blocks iOS text resizing.
- **The global `*{ line-height:100% }` rule hurts readability.** It is a very tight line height for any wrapped copy.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.** This is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, though clients tolerate it. The title also doesn't reflect the subject line.
- **There are markup errors:**
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` isn't self-closed.
  - Duplicate and redundant `format-detection` metas are present.
  - The comment `<!----- MI styling end----->` is malformed, with extra hyphens.
  - A leftover template comment reads `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]`.
- **Two `img { max-width }` media queries conflict.** `min-width:768px` sets 600px and `min-width:640px` sets 640px. The later rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Layout class names do not match their rules.** The `.mobile` class is `width:460px`, which is fixed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. Personalization appears to be handled by Movable Ink (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`). The truncated HTML means the body copy wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host, which suggests a consistent sending subdomain.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.** They would be in the truncated footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Link `href`s are truncated and the 88 tracked links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "up to $300 off coffee & espresso machines" offer can't be confirmed. The tracking domains suggest all clicks go through a redirect layer first.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Set `alt=""` explicitly on the tracking pixels (`rlcdn.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, self-close the meta tags, and remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas and stray template comments.
4. Resolve the conflicting `img max-width` rules. Replace the global `line-height:100%` with explicit per-element values.
5. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` or SMTP-level capture so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be verified. Also re-run with the full HTML so the footer and UTMs can be checked.
6. Resolve the 88 redirect links by following the chain, and verify the final URLs and UTM parameters.
7. Review whether the five LiveRamp pixels, which pass the shared recipient token, are intended and covered by the privacy policy.
