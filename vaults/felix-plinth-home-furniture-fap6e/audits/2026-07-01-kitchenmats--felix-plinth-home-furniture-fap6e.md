## What's weak

- Five modules, zero cohesion — kitchen mats, open box, personalized picks, patio furniture, and an app download is not a send, it's a sitemap dump.
- The Open Box section appears to feature outdoor/garden product visually, which is tonally miles from the kitchen category I thought I came here for.

- This is Wayfair email roughly number 79 in my inbox. Five sends in three days. The cadence has become a running joke — CB2's "Up to 50% off furniture & more" last week came in once and felt premium; this one feels like a drip sequence that forgot to stop dripping.

- 1. **Commit to the kitchen mats concept end-to-end.** If the subject is "#kitchenmats," the email should be entirely kitchen mats — three to five hero styles, a sizing guide for galley vs. island layouts, a reason this send exists beyond "we have mats." The hashtag format sets up an editorial promise the body doesn't keep.
- 2. **Pin the 70% OFF offer to the kitchen mat category.** Right now the discount banner and the subject line live in separate universes. One line of copy — "Kitchen mats from $19, up to 70% off" — bridges both and gives me a reason to click.
- 3. **Cut three modules.** Open Box Deals OR personalized picks OR future faves — not all three. One supporting hook is editorial; three is clutter.
- 4. **Replace "Shop Now" with destination-specific copy.** "Shop Kitchen Mats," "See Open Box Prices," something that tells me where I'm going.
