# On now: Up to 25% off your bedroom refresh!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this actually landed well for me. "Up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" with that Coveleur canopy bed front and center, real price stamped right under it ($1,899, reg. $2,299) — that's the second bedroom-focused Crate & Barrel send I've gotten this stretch (the "bedroom furniture, bedding & more" one hit yesterday too), and I'm not mad about it because I'm literally mid-renovation on both houses and bedrooms are on the list.
- The category grid under the hero — beds, dressers & nightstands, bedding, decor & lighting, each with its own %-off — is exactly the kind of scannable breakdown I want. It reads like a merchandiser who actually knows bedrooms, not a generic "shop now" wall. Compare that to Pottery Barn's "Layering With Light" send, which was their fourteenth lighting email in 48 hours and told me nothing concrete — this one gives me a real bed, a real price, real percentages by subcategory.
- Where it loses me is length and repetition-fatigue. There's a clearance banner, a sofa brand story, a "we love these for you" product wall with an air fryer randomly dropped into bedroom furniture, a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a Design Desk appointment CTA — all after the bedroom sale already closed its case. I'd click "Shop Bedroom Sale" off the hero and bail. Not forwarding this one — it's solid, not share-worthy — but I'd mark it read rather than delete.

## What's working

- Hero offer is immediate and specific: 25% off, real bed, real markdown price visible without digging.
- Category grid (beds/dressers/bedding/decor & lighting) with individual %-off numbers is genuinely useful for someone comparing across rooms mid-reno.
- Two clear CTAs right after the grid ("Shop Bedroom Sale," "Shop All Bedroom") keep the primary action obvious.
- Demographic fit is right — canopy bed, warm neutral styling, this is a couple's primary-bedroom aesthetic, not a nursery ambush like the Crate & Kids emails clogging my inbox lately.
- Unlike Wayfair's "FOUND: Bar Stools!" send, which buried actual product across eight screen-lengths with broken images, everything here renders clean and loads.

## What's weak

- The email just keeps going — clearance banner, sofa brand story, "we love these for you," credit card, app, Design Desk — by the time I hit the fifth CTA I've mentally checked out.
- An air fryer and a countertop oven show up in the "we love these for you" grid on a bedroom email. That's a targeting miss, not a bedroom item.
- The sofa "Our sofas are famous" module has zero connection to the bedroom theme — feels like a leftover block from a different campaign template.
- Credit card and app-download pushes this deep into a sale email add friction I don't need when I already know what I want to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Bedroom" — everything past that (clearance, sofa story, unrelated product wall, card, app) dilutes a genuinely strong hero and category grid. Save those for a separate send.
- 2. Pull the air fryer/oven out of "we love these for you" — cross-sell logic is broken if it's showing kitchen appliances on a bedroom-refresh email.
- 3. If clearance has to stay, move it to its own dedicated send rather than tacking it onto a focused bedroom sale — it undercuts the urgency of the 25% bedroom offer.
- 4. Add a swatch/fabric-sample CTA near the canopy bed — I'm choosing finishes for two houses right now and that's the kind of tool that'd actually get a click from me.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real markdowns on beds, nightstands, and bedding — plus new clearance drops.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The canopy bed everyone's asking about, now $1,899.`
