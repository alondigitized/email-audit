# How to choose the best rug materials for your space →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Fifteen. That's the C&B family count in my July inbox now, and this is the second rug email from them in as many days — yesterday was "Find the right rug for every room," today it's a full materials breakdown by fiber type. At least this one has actual substance behind it: performance vs. premium wool vs. viscose vs. sisal & jute, laid out with real product shots and swatch options. If I'm being honest, this is closer to a buying guide than a blast, and it's landing while I'm still not settled on the living room rug for the second home.
- The problem is length and repetition, not relevance. This thing scrolls forever — rug performance, then wool, then designer tips, then viscose, then sisal, then a kids' rug detour, then a "rug swatches" pitch, then an unrelated coffee-and-tea routine ad, then a heritage collection, then a Lupita Nyong'o design story. By the time I hit the celebrity home tour I've completely forgotten this started as a rug materials guide. Article's dining-chair emails stay tight and single-topic; this one tries to be six emails stapled together.
- I'd scroll the top half for the swatch program — that's a real, useful CTA for someone furnishing two houses — but I wouldn't make it to the bottom, and I definitely wouldn't forward it. Mark as read, maybe click "Order Swatches."

## What's working

- The fiber breakdown (performance, premium wool, viscose, sisal & jute) is genuinely useful category education for someone still deciding on rugs across two renovations.
- "Rug swatches" as a standalone CTA near the bottom is the single most actionable thing in here — Article and CB2 don't offer anything like this.
- Designer tips section ("always go for the larger size," "keep a bigger rug," "don't forget the rug pad") is practical, not just filler copy.
- Room photography is consistent and on-brand — warm, neutral, livable spaces, not overly styled showroom shots.

## What's weak

- It's way too long — by the time I hit the coffee/tea routine ad and the Lupita Nyong'o home tour, I've lost the thread entirely; this reads like three emails merged into one.
- "Up to 60% off The Summer Sale" in the hero banner never gets a specific number or category attached to it — CB2's "UP TO 30% OFF BEDROOM & BATH" at least tells me what's discounted.
- Kids rug section is a dead zone for me — no kids, not relevant, and it breaks the flow between sisal/jute and the swatch pitch.
- No urgency anywhere — no end date on the "Summer Sale," so nothing here makes me feel like I need to act today versus bookmarking it for later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two sends: a tight "rug materials 101" guide (performance → wool → viscose → sisal/jute → swatches, with the designer tips) and a separate email for the unrelated stuff (coffee routine, heritage collection, Lupita Nyong'o feature). Wayfair's trash-can email was one-note and annoying, but at least it didn't make me forget what I opened it for.
- 2. Put the swatch CTA right after the hero, not buried two-thirds down — that's the actual conversion path for someone like me who's still deciding.
- 3. Attach a real number to "up to 60% off" in the hero — even "up to 60% off select rugs" beats a bare percentage with no category.
- 4. Drop the kids rugs and coffee/tea sections from this send entirely — they dilute a genuinely useful guide.
