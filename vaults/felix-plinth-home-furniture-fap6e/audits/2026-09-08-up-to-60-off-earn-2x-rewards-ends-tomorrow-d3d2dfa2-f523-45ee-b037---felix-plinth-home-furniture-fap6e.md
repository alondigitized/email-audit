# Up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards ends tomorrow!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Yep, there it is again — same red countdown banner, same "up to 60% off" and "2x Rewards," this is literally the same email I already tore apart an hour ago, just re-rendered. Crate & Kids, for the third time today, still landing in the same inbox folder where I'm saving actual primary-home and second-home renovation stuff. I don't have kids. I have never once clicked through to a crib or a "Baby Registry" banner, and yet this brand keeps mailing me nursery furniture like I'm about to become a Design Desk client for a nine-month-old.
- The one thing that mildly catches my eye scrolling down is the actual Crate & Barrel "Design Desk" section near the bottom — free design help is something I'd care about for the renovation — but it's buried under six kids'-furniture modules I'm scrolling past on autopilot. Compare that to Article's "Last call: Up to 40% off ends today" send, which I scored an 8 because the hero was legible in one glance with no scroll-hunting. This is the opposite: a wall of nursery rugs, star-print decor, and a toddler in a highchair before I get to anything remotely adult.
- Not opening, not clicking, definitely not forwarding — unless it's to a friend who just had a baby, as a joke about how Crate & Barrel apparently thinks everyone on their main list is expecting.

## What's working

- The countdown timer up top is at least legible and creates real urgency (00:15:38:39) without looking broken.
- "Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars" is a concrete, specific loyalty hook — I do have a Crate & Barrel card, so this line at least applies to me even if the products don't.
- The Design Desk section ("our Design Desk pros can help — it's 100% free") is genuinely relevant to a guy mid-renovation, if only it weren't stapled to the bottom of a baby-furniture catalog.
- Reviews/social proof ("loved by parents," star ratings on the dresser) show up late in the email — nice touch, wrong audience for me.

## What's weak

- Every hero image is a nursery, a crib, a highchair, or star-print kids' bedding — zero relevance to a childless 36-year-old renovating two adult homes.
- This is the same "Up to 60% off + 2x Rewards ends tomorrow" creative I already flagged twice today from the main Crate & Barrel brand — total sender fatigue, and now the toddler spinoff is doing it too.
- No sizing, swatch, or shipping-timeline info for furniture I'd actually buy (dressers, rugs, decor) — just "ships free" as a blanket claim with no color-palette or fabric-sample callout.
- The email is enormous — I counted at least eight separate product modules before reaching anything I'd act on, way past Article's one-scroll hero standard.
- "hundreds of items ship free" next to a toddler photo is the kind of copy that makes me feel like a stranger, not a known customer, despite the loyalty math up top being personalized to my card.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment by household, not just email address.** If I've never clicked a kids' product in months of Crate & Kids sends, suppress this list entirely and route me to Crate & Barrel main + CB2 + Design Desk content only — that's the actual behavioral signal here.
- 2. **Lead with Design Desk, not cribs.** For a renovation-focused subscriber, "free design help" is the hook — move it to the hero, not module #9.
- 3. **Cut the email length by 60%.** Eight product modules for a non-buyer is pure scroll fatigue; Article proved a single clean hero + one CTA converts better for urgency sends.
- 4. **Stop reusing the exact same countdown-timer template across three sends in one day** — even loyal subscribers notice, and it reads as spam-adjacent regardless of the honest discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Reward Dollars are stacking — see what's eligible`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free design help + up to 60% off, ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `20% back in Reward Dollars on Design Desk projects too`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, decor, and clearance — all earning double rewards`

# Technical Email Audit — Crate & Kids SALE (2026-09-08)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Cheetah/Bronto-style responsive email template with heavy MSO conditional CSS; automated QA shows 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and unverifiable compliance/auth headers rather than structural HTML defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 89 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (redirect-domain links, not directly resolvable) — destination validity is unconfirmed and should be manually click-tested before send.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels are firing: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6x), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, and `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5x, LiveRamp/RLCDN identity pixel) plus a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...` open-tracking pixel. Volume of duplicate pixel calls is normal for MI (Bronto/Cheetah) but worth confirming isn't unintentional duplication in the template.
- No broken/malformed `href` markup found in the visible source; full link-by-link audit wasn't possible due to HTML truncation.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 37 images flagged with missing `alt` text, spanning hero/product images (`.jpg`), spacer/divider GIFs, and tracking pixels. Decorative spacers and tracking pixels should use `alt=""`; content-bearing product/offer images (e.g., `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `4e3f4804-...jpg`, `972ca7ba-...jpg`) should carry descriptive alt text — currently none do, which fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades screen-reader/blocked-image fallback.
- Template includes standard MSO/Outlook conditional fixes (`mso-table-lspace`, `xml:namespace`, VML) and Apple Mail reformatting suppression (`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`) — no defects observed in these blocks.
- `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` both fixed to `light` only — no dark-mode variant defined; not an error, but no dark-mode-safe fallback exists if a client forces dark rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%`, `{{`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) visible in the truncated source.
- No issues found based on available HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by automated check. Per the QA note this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than true absence — needs confirmation against raw SMTP headers at the ESP.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe)**: not found — if genuinely absent, this is a compliance gap for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (mandatory since Feb 2024 for senders of 5,000+ msgs/day).
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found/unknown, same relay-visibility caveat as above.
- Unsubscribe footer content itself is not visible in the truncated HTML sample provided — cannot confirm a working footer unsubscribe link or physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM requirement) from this excerpt.
- **Recommendation**: verify these three items against raw headers captured outside the AgentMail relay, since the current tooling cannot distinguish "missing" from "not captured."

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot confirm UTM parameter presence/consistency — the 89 tracking links were skipped by the prober and the raw destination URLs aren't visible in the truncated source.
- No landing page destinations were resolvable from the provided HTML excerpt.
- **Recommendation**: re-run with full (untruncated) HTML or resolved click-through URLs to verify UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to content images (product/offer creative); set `alt=""` explicitly on spacer GIFs and tracking pixels to satisfy accessibility checkers without adding noise.
2. Confirm with the ESP/raw SMTP capture whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are truly absent or just not passed through the AgentMail relay — the latter is a monitoring gap, the former is a compliance gap.
3. Capture raw `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) outside the relay to confirm deliverability posture.
4. Re-audit with full/untruncated HTML to validate the 89 skipped tracking links (UTM params, redirect targets) and confirm unsubscribe footer/physical address are present per CAN-SPAM.
