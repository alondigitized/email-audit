# Layer your bed in color
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Bedding is a real need for me. I've got a primary and a second home both getting furnished, so "Layers of Color" is the first Pottery Barn send in a while that fits. It's the Belgian Flax Linen sorted by color family: blues, pinks, greens, neutrals. That's how I actually shop, since I pick a palette first and then buy. The 11/10 "Nightstands starting at $349" email worked for the same reason. It was a category I could act on.
- Then it gets noisy. The Fall Sale banner is stuck on top again, the "Recommended Just For You" grid has Christmas stockings in it, the Christmas Shop banner is below that, and the sweepstakes and the card pitch are below that too. It's the 13th Pottery Barn email this week and the footer has more legal text than the bedding section has product. The color idea is good, but it's buried in clutter.
- Would I click? Yes, probably into the greens or neutrals. I'd save it to my inspo folder. I wouldn't forward it. I'd check the linen price, then compare it against Crate & Barrel and Article before buying.

## What's working

- Four color tiles (blues, pinks, greens, neutrals) match how I plan a room. I choose a palette first, so this is useful.
- "(It all ships free!)" in the subhead answers my shipping worry. Wayfair never says that clearly on furniture-adjacent items.
- The hero is clean and shows stacked linen in real colors. It also has one concrete offer: "Up to 15% off Belgian Flax Linen Bedding."
- The "More Bedding Essentials" row (duvets, sheets, comforters, pillows, duvet inserts) is easy to tap and lets me go straight to a sub-category.
- The "Shop Bedding by Color" button is a clear primary CTA.

## What's weak

- The top banner says "Up to 70% off," but the hero offer is "up to 15% off." The headline makes a promise the body doesn't keep.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid is stockings, dining tables, curtain hardware and a platform bed. It isn't personalized to what I've been browsing, and the stockings are off-topic for a bedding email.
- The Christmas Shop banner sits right under the recommendations and competes with the bedding story.
- The "Design Crew sweeps" block, the app download, the 10% back card pitch and the Affirm block make the email feel like a catalog stapled to an ad network. The legal copy at the bottom is tiny and hard to read on a phone.
- This is the same stack as the 11/10 "Now on sale & shipping free!" send, and it looks like a template with a different hero dropped in. Same sale strip, same rec grid, same Christmas block.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below "More Bedding Essentials" except one rewards line. A bedding email should be about bedding.
- 2. Make the top banner match the hero. If the real discount is 15%, say that, or show which bedding is actually in the 70% group.
- 3. Replace the rec grid with bedding-adjacent items such as nightstands, bed frames and rugs. Better yet, base it on my browsing.
- 4. Add swatch or sample access, plus fabric weights and color names, in the color tiles. I want to know what "sage" really looks like before I order 2 beds' worth.
- 5. Add a rewards line for bedding, like "earn X% back with the PB card on linen," so I can do the card math.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Belgian linen, sorted by color: blues, greens, pinks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pick your palette: 15% off Belgian Flax Linen`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop by color. Everything ships free through the weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Four palettes, one linen. Up to 15% off, free shipping.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Layer your bed in color"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with 65 click-tracked links and about 60 sliced images. Nearly all of them lack alt text, three images load over plain `http://`, and one HTTP pixel puts the recipient's address in its query string. The HTML shown is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **65 tracking links not probed.** The QA run skipped them because they are redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. Destination status codes are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 12,706 of 17,754 characters (71.6%) are URLs, which is just over the 70% threshold. This comes from long redirect URLs, and spam filters can penalize that ratio.
- **No broken links were confirmed.** The one real finding here is that the check is incomplete.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** About 55 images are affected. Most are slices: `…/0127_TUE_AM_BeddingColorStory1/slices_01–19.jpg` and `…/Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`. Also affected:
  - `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`
  - `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`
  - `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - Six product images under `potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, e.g. `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`
  - `3ceb0c96-….jpg` and `spacer_0.gif`

  Many of these are linked images, so with images off or a screen reader the links have no accessible name. The logo `<img>` does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Tracking pixels flagged for alt text.** `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`, `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=…`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/56ba87ae-…` and `coreemail.html?email=…` are pixels. They should have `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions or `display:none`, not be left without the attribute.
- **Insecure image sources.**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&…`

  These can be blocked or rewritten by clients. A blocked open pixel will undercount opens.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Server code leaked into CSS.** The final `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")`. It is harmless but invalid CSS, and it suggests a template or build artifact.
- **Image-only layout.** Content is sliced images, so image blocking leaves the email largely blank. Only the preheader text ("Plus, all bedding ships free") is real text in the source seen.
- **Stale asset paths.** One asset is from a 2022 template path (`/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/`). The body folder is named `0127_TUE_AM_…` but is sent alongside 2026 holiday banners. This looks like reused template assets, which risks stale content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens appeared in the visible source. The recipient address is populated in `coreemail.html?email=…` and `sv_sveme=…`.
- **PII in a plaintext HTTP URL.** The `svopn.potterybarn.com` pixel appends `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` over `http://`. The raw email address is exposed in transit and in proxy or server logs. The `coreemail.html` pixel also carries it in the query string, but over HTTPS.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are absent at the source, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. The relay is the expected cause, so this is not a sender defect.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destinations are hidden behind opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` tokens, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment **cannot be verified**.
- Some product images link directly to `potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`. Those are image sources, not click destinations.
- Product images in the email (curtain rod bracket, dining tables, stockings) don't obviously match the "bedding color story" subject. This is only observable from the filenames, so confirm it against the rendered email.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all linked content images. Use `alt=""` and 1×1 sizing on tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`. Remove the recipient email from the `svopn` pixel URL, or move it to a hashed or opaque ID.
3. Populate `<title>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and delete the stray `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
4. Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC, on the raw message at the origin rather than through the relay. Confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
5. Resolve the redirect chain for a sample of the 65 tracking links to check landing pages and UTM parameters.
6. Reduce the plain-text URL density with a shorter text part or shorter tracking URLs.
7. Audit reused template assets (the 2022 banner and the `0127_` folder) for stale content.
