## What's working

- The cabinet vignette photo is a straight-up mood-board shot — warm wood, ceramics, good styling, on brand with what I liked in the 09-22 "Free Design Services" credenza shot.
- The brass candle holder pair is a clean, giftable, low-price-point image that's easy to click into.
- The rewards card callout (10% back across the whole C&B/Williams Sonoma family) is genuinely relevant to how I actually buy — highest-rewards-card guy checking in.

## What's weak

- The entire hero is "sign up for texts," which is a house-list-growth ask, not a shopping incentive — it's not why I opened a Pottery Barn email.
- No actual offer or discount percentage anywhere in the primary content — "Savings across the home" links to "Shop Sale" but never says how much, so I can't gauge if it's worth a click.
- The App Store / Affirm / credit card block reads like three separate marketing teams' assets stapled to the bottom of one email, with zero visual hierarchy tying them together.
- After three PB sends this week (holiday bestsellers on 9/23, pillows and design services on 9/22, rugs on 9/22), this is the weakest one — it's a list-building email wearing a merchandising email's outfit.

- 2. Pull the 10% rewards card callout up near the top — that's the one piece of content that's actually persuasive for a repeat, high-spend shopper like me.
- 3. If the goal really is texts, incentivize it — "Text SIGNUP for 15% off" beats a bare CTA with nothing attached.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based HTML render from Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Pottery Barn's `e.potterybarn.com` domain), with functional click-tracking and open-pixel infrastructure, but several images lack alt text and two legacy footer assets still load over unencrypted HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` (SFMC CloudPages tracked-link format) — consistent and expected for ExactTarget/SFMC sends.
- Open tracking present via `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — note the recipient email address is passed in plaintext in the pixel URL query string.

- QA skipped HTTP probing on 31 tracking/redirect links (expected behavior for click-redirect domains, not a defect).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 24 of 24 content/footer images are missing `alt` attributes (slices_01–slices_18, Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg, and others) — screen readers and blocked-image states will show nothing.

- Mobile responsive rules target `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`, but no elements in the visible source actually use `class="wrapper"` or `class="photo"` — the media query may be dead code for this send.
- Outlook/Apple Mail resets (`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `ExternalClass`, `-webkit-text-size-adjust`) are present and standard.
