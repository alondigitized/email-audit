# ROCKING CHAIRS 📢 🍂 Don’t forget our EPIC FALL SALE 📢 🍂 
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- So the subject line screamed "ROCKING CHAIRS" at me with two megaphone emojis, and I scrolled this entire email looking for an actual rocking chair — didn't find one. There's a pink accent chair up top that's cute for the second house's reading nook, but it's not a rocker. That's the kind of bait-and-switch that makes me trust Wayfair's subject lines less every time, and honestly it's the same complaint I had about "OUTDOOR FOUNTAIN GOALS" a couple days back — two fountains, then a total pivot into trash cans and A/C units.
- The "picked just for you" grid is a total grab bag — kitchen utensils, a bathroom mirror, gym equipment, a closet system, a cutting board — none of it reads as personalized to a guy renovating two houses on Pottery Barn/Crate & Barrel/Wayfair lists. Compare that to Pottery Barn's "Cozy throws, pillows & more" send, which at least committed to one cohesive autumn palette and felt like an actual room. This is just inventory dumped into a template with "EPIC FALL SALE" slapped on top.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. I'll skim the flash deals section because the hot tub and greenhouse actually caught my eye for renovation #2, but the subject-to-content mismatch and the generic grid kill this one. Marking read, moving on.

## What's working

- The purple "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" banner has real urgency and a clean visual break from the rest of the email.
- The hot tub and greenhouse in the flash deals section are genuinely relevant to a second-home renovation — that's a real "for me" moment.
- Afterpay 0% APR call-out is useful for someone financing two renovations at once — that's a concrete, honest perk.
- Strikethrough pricing (Was/Now) is visible and consistent across every product tile, so the math is at least easy to do at a glance.

## What's weak

- Zero rocking chairs anywhere in this email — the subject line promised the one thing the body doesn't deliver.
- "Picked just for you" is a random mix of kitchen tools, gym gear, and a mirror — nothing here signals Wayfair knows I'm mid-renovation on two color palettes.
- The category deal bars (mattresses, curtains, cooling, wall art) are just a wall of text with no imagery — easy to scroll past without registering any of them.
- Same over-stuffed structure as the last few Wayfair sends — this is basically "OUTDOOR FOUNTAIN GOALS" and "SHOWER CURTAINS in stock" wearing a fall-sale costume.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject-to-hero mismatch first — either lead with an actual rocking chair hero image, or drop "ROCKING CHAIRS" from the subject entirely. This is the single biggest trust-killer here.
- 2. Replace the generic "picked just for you" grid with actual browsing-history-based picks — if I've been eyeing rugs and outdoor seating, show me that, not a bathroom mirror.
- 3. Turn the four category deal bars into small image tiles — right now they're invisible text rows competing with nothing.
- 4. Move the greenhouse/hot tub/pergola-style items higher — that's the content that actually pulls a renovate-two-houses guy like me in.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Unbounce/ExactTarget-style Wayfair batch email with functioning primary links, but multiple accessibility gaps, missing auth/compliance headers at the relay layer, and legacy insecure pixel trackers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Core CTAs ("Shop Sale", "Wayfair" logo, app deep link, product links) all carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `batchid`, `varid`, `refid=MKTEML_140402`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16213`) — parameter structure is internally consistent across links.
- QA probe returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) on 7 material links, including the primary "Shop Sale" CTA and the app deep link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`). This is a probing artifact, not a confirmed link failure — flagged as inconclusive, not broken.
- 4 links went unprobed due to a 70s time budget cutoff, including `1200LBS-Workout-Bench...` and `SuiteSymphony-60...Closet-System` — status unknown, not pass/fail.
- 12 of 86 total links were probed (rest skipped per click-likelihood ranking); an unsubscribe link was confirmed present per probe methodology.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 25+ `<img>` tags missing `alt` text, including the hero (`0915_EpicFallSale_SkinnyHero_TEST-VP.jpg`), flash-deals banner, financing banner, and all review-star icons (`stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png`, `stars_11.png`) — screen readers will announce nothing for these content/rating images.
- Tracking pixel `b.php` (open-tracking beacon) also missing `alt`, which is expected/benign for a 1x1 pixel.
- Media query blocks are well-formed and cover standard Litmus/Outlook/Gmail hooks (`u-FullWidthBlock`, Outlook `data-outlook-cycle`, Gmail-specific `.Gmail` padding fix) — no structural CSS issues found in the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) present in the visible/truncated HTML — no issues found in the reviewed excerpt.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than a sender-side omission; cannot confirm compliance status from this evidence alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected for the same reason — recommend re-verifying against raw SMTP headers rather than the relayed copy.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not present in the captured headers — cannot confirm authentication passed; this is a QA/relay visibility gap, not evidence of an auth failure.
- In-body unsubscribe link presence confirmed by QA probe methodology (always included in probe set), satisfying CAN-SPAM's visible opt-out requirement at the content level.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair uses proprietary tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `refid`, `maiid`, `emlid`, `csnid`, `skurectxid`, `brcid`) rather than standard `utm_*` params — internally consistent naming convention, not a UTM-based scheme, so no UTM mismatch to evaluate.
- CTA destinations align with subject/content intent: "Shop Sale" → `/daily-sales`, app CTA → `wayfairapp.onelink.me` deep link with `af_web_dp` fallback to `/the-wayfair-app`, product image links → specific SKU PDPs (`wayfair-product-L282-K~W116559807.html`, etc.) — destination-to-CTA mapping is coherent.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to hero, banner, and product images at minimum (currently ~25 missing); star-rating icons should get `alt="X out of 5 stars"` equivalent or `alt=""` if decorative-redundant with adjacent text.
- Replace `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...` pixels (20 instances, `s=124126000`–`124126019`) with `https://` equivalents — non-HTTPS resources are blocked by default in Gmail/Outlook and by browser mixed-content policies, silently killing that impression tracking.
- Re-run header capture directly against the originating MTA (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually present/passing — current absence is inconclusive, not a confirmed violation.
- Re-probe the 7 rate-limited (429) links and 4 unprobed links with a longer time budget or backoff/retry logic before treating them as pass/fail.
