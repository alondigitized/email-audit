# EXTRA 20% OFF is calling + Ships free: Dinnerware, linens & more

## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and the same maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip is sitting on top. I've seen it since 10/22 and I'm numb to it. This is at least the seventh send in four days, so my thumb was already on archive.
- The part that earned a second look is the "Table Essentials Ship Free" hero and the grid under it. It has table linens, throws, towels and pillows, with dinnerware over a rustic wood backdrop. That's holiday-hosting content, and I'm mid-renovation on two houses, so it's relevant. The "Recommended just for you" block is where it gets interesting for me. Curtain rod, extendable dining table, rug, pendant: that's what I'm actually shopping for. But there are no prices, so it's pure catalog browsing.
- Would I click? Maybe once, on the Banks Extendable Dining Table, but only because I'm already in dining-room mode. I wouldn't forward it. I'd drag it into my Pinterest-adjacent folder at best and then forget it. Crate & Barrel and CB2 are running the same holiday-hosting beat, and CB2's "This Just In" at least looked like a catalog.

## What's working

- The "Table Essentials Ship Free" hero is concrete and seasonal. Free shipping on dinnerware reads as real value when I'm outfitting two homes.
- The stacked white dinnerware over weathered wood is the kind of image I'd save to a board.
- The category grid (table linens, throws, towels, pillows) is easy to scan, and the large labels double as tap targets.
- "Recommended just for you" shows real furniture, which is my actual shopping list. Wayfair would have buried this below a countdown banner.
- The free design appointment and the 10% back in Rewards card callout are useful, and the rewards line speaks to my card-math habit.

## What's weak

- The maroon "Extra 20% off" strip is a repeat of every send since 10/22 (including 10/24's "Save MORE with EXTRA 20% OFF select styles"). It has stopped registering as news.
- The "Recommended just for you" tiles have no prices, no sale marks and no review stars, so I can't tell what the 20% actually applies to.
- Fine print says "Select Styles" and "Exclusions apply" twice, and the legal block at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. It undercuts the honesty of the headline offer.
- Free shipping is on "dinnerware, linens & more", but there's no deadline and no mention of furniture shipping, which is what I actually need to know about.
- The email is very long. The Christmas Shop and design services blocks come after the main pitch, and the page feels like several emails stacked together.

- 1. Retire the maroon strip and put the one best offer in the hero. Pick either free shipping or 20% off, and give it an end date.
- 2. Add prices and sale prices to the recommended products, plus a "ships in X weeks" line on the furniture.
- 3. Add a swatch or sample link on the linens and rugs, since I'm matching colors across two houses.
- 4. Cut the sections to three: hero, curated grid, rewards. Move the Christmas Shop and design services further down.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with image-sliced content, and about 50 images have no alt text. The HTML also contains a leaked server-side code fragment and a few non-HTTPS assets. The compliance and authentication headers could not be checked because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 53 links go through click-redirect domains such as `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. The QA run skipped probing them, so destination validity and status are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` is loaded over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten, which would skew open tracking.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 10,192 of 14,368 plain-text characters (71%) are URLs, which trips the QA ">70% URLs" warning. The long tracking URLs are the cause, and they are a mild spam-filter risk.
- **Insecure images:** two content images use `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  
  The second is a 2022 asset path, so it may be stale.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a West Elm domain. It adds a dependency on another brand's CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images flagged by QA have none. The main offender is the sliced creative, whose offer copy is presumably baked into images:
  - `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`
  - `1003_SAT_AM_FreeShipDinnerware/slices_01–11.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`
  - the product images (`banks-extendable-dining-table…`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and others)
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`
  - `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  
  With images off or a screen reader, the offer and CTAs are not conveyed.
- **Pixels without alt:** `coreemail.html`, `d.turn.com/r/dd/...`, `svopn…` and `clickcp…` are tracking pixels, so they are low severity. They should still have `alt=""`.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility problem.
- **Leaked code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with PHP-style text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. It is invalid CSS and suggests a template or build error. It sits after the last real rule, so it is probably harmless, but it should be removed.
- **Fragile selectors:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are exact-match attribute selectors that break if classes change. The `wrapper` and `photo` class names are not visible in the truncated source, so they may not match anything.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Tokens resolved:** no unresolved merge tokens are visible in the supplied source.
- **Address in URLs:** the subscriber address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is passed in plaintext query strings:
  - `coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `sv_sveme=...`
  
  This exposes it in server logs and to anyone who forwards the email.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer unsubscribe and address:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are outside the truncated HTML and could not be verified.
- **Sender:** the From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the tracking domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`, which is consistent for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not visible:** all destinations are encoded in the `qs=` redirect tokens, so UTM parameters and landing URLs cannot be verified.
- **Product image hosts:** the product images come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, which is the correct brand domain.
- **No continuity check:** the QA run did not follow any redirects, so there is no continuity evidence either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content slice, with the offer text ("Extra 20% off", "Ships free") and CTA labels. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Move all `http://` assets to HTTPS (`svopn` pixel, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`). Replace the stale 2022 banner.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>` and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in query strings (`coreemail.html`, `sv_sveme`). Use an opaque ID.
6. Re-run the QA against the raw message so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be checked. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm landing pages and UTMs.
7. Shorten or consolidate the tracking URLs to bring plain-text URL density under 70%.
