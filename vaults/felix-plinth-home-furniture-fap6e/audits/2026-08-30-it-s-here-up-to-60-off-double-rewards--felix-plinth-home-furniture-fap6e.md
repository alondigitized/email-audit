# It’s here! Up to 60% off + Double Rewards→
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Third time this week Crate-family emails have hit my inbox, and this one's the same story as the last Crate & Kids send I flagged — "Crate&kids" logo, a kid on a rug in the hero, nursery furniture and rugs stacked six tiles deep. I don't have kids, so my eyes just slide right off the whole thing. It's the identical "It's Here! Up to 60% off + Double Rewards" hero copy Crate & Barrel proper has been running, just reskinned onto the kids' catalog.
- What actually stopped my scroll for half a second was the "we love these for you" personalized row near the bottom — a backpack, a stroller travel system, a crib mattress, a glider recliner. That's Crate & Kids trying to do recommendation targeting, and it's aggressively wrong for me. If anything it confirms I'm not who this list is for.
- Instant scroll-past, no click, definitely not forwarding — unless it's to razz a friend who actually has a toddler. Marking as read and moving on, same as the last Crate & Kids send.

## What's working

- Layout is clean and legible — no render bugs, images all loaded, nothing overlapping.
- The Double Rewards banner (navy block, "10% back... 20% back") is at least a clear, single-focus CTA if I were a cardholder.
- "$5000 Design Desk" sweepstakes tile is a decent hook in theory — free money is free money — but it's bundled with "book a free appointment for your dream space," which again skews toward someone furnishing a nursery, not my two reno projects.

## What's weak

- Wrong catalog, again — Crate & Kids, not Crate & Barrel. Cribs, changing tables, nursery furniture, baby bedding — none of it maps to either of my two houses.
- The "we love these for you" personalization block is actively off — a stroller and a crib mattress are not products for a 36-year-old guy without kids. If this is meant to feel like they know me, it backfired.
- Same "Up to 60% off + Double Rewards" hero language I've now seen from Crate & Barrel and Crate & Kids both, twice this week — the brand's cannibalizing its own urgency by recycling the exact promo copy across sub-brands.
- No swatch/sample program, no color-palette guidance, no shipping-timeline clarity for furniture — the stuff that'd actually get me to click on a Crate & Barrel send isn't here because this isn't that catalog.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely — if Crate & Barrel has purchase/browse history showing no kids' items, don't cross-sell the sub-brand under a shared domain. This is the single biggest lever; nothing else matters if the email shouldn't have reached me.
- 2. Kill the copy-paste hero. Crate & Barrel and Crate & Kids running identical "It's Here! Up to 60% off + Double Rewards" language in the same week reads like one campaign blasted twice, not two curated sends.
- 3. If Crate & Kids must email adjacent Crate & Barrel subscribers, lead with something crossover-relevant — nursery furniture styled as "guest room" pieces, or storage/study furniture that isn't kid-specific — instead of cribs and strollers up top.
- 4. Fix "we love these for you" — that block should never surface baby gear to an account with zero kids'-category browsing/purchase signal.
