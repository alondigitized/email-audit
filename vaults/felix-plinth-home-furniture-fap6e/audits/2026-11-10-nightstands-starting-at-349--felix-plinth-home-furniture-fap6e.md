# Nightstands starting at $349
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- Nightstands, finally a send that's for a person with two bedrooms to furnish. I'm doing the primary and the second home at the same time, so a grid of nine nightstands is useful to me. The hero is clean: a walnut stack with a plant on top and "Starting at $349" in the corner. I'd click through, but not because the email earned it. The category happens to be on my list.
- The email buries its own subject. The top banner says "The Fall Sale: Up to 70% off," and the nightstand hero comes second. Then the bottom half turns into a catalog dump: stockings, a dining table, curtain rod, Christmas Shop, sweepstakes, app, affirm. This is roughly the twelfth Pottery Barn email in five days. After the 11/09 "NEW ARRIVALS inspired by cozy traditions" and "New arrivals await in The Christmas Shop," I'm wary of the plaid-and-stockings creative. I'd click once, save it to my renovation folder, and archive it. I would not forward it.

## What's working

- The nightstand grid is easy to scan. Nine styles (Sausalito, Farmhouse, Cayman, Oakleigh, Reed, Rylee, Calistoga, Summerville) are named under clean product shots on a consistent background.
- "Starting at $349" is a real price anchor. I can tell right away whether this fits my budget.
- Hero copy is plain. "Do-It-All Nightstands" tells me what I'm looking at.
- "Complete Your Retreat" with Beds, Dressers, Mattresses, Sheets, Duvets, Quilts matches how I actually shop. I'm furnishing whole bedrooms, not one piece at a time.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" block is useful for me. I buy through the best rewards card, so I care about that math.

## What's weak

- The Fall Sale banner conflicts with the nightstand hero. I can't tell if the 70% off applies to nightstands or to something else. Nightstands at "starting at $349" don't read as 70% off.
- The "Recommended Just For You" block is stockings and a Christmas tree. That is not personalization for a guy buying bedroom furniture. It's the same holiday stuff I got in the 11/08 "Inspired by your favorite holiday movies..." send.
- There are no prices under the individual nightstands, no reviews, and no sizes. I can't compare Cayman and Oakleigh without clicking through.
- Nothing about shipping time or the design appointment for furniture, which is the thing I'd care about before spending $349 or more. The Design Crew sweepstakes is pushed instead.
- It runs very long. The footer legal copy is a wall of tiny text, and the Affirm and app blocks are clutter at that depth.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Complete Your Retreat." Drop the stockings, tree, Christmas Shop and sweepstakes. A bedroom-furniture send should stay a bedroom-furniture send.
- 2. Put real prices, and a star rating where possible, under each nightstand.
- 3. Reconcile the sale banner with the hero. Say which nightstands are on sale and by how much.
- 4. Add a furniture-specific trust line near the top: delivery windows, white-glove delivery, free swatches or design appointment.
- 5. Show the 10% back in rewards next to the nightstand grid, not at the bottom.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walnut, oak and painted finishes. Compare sizes and ship dates.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 10% back in rewards on bedroom furniture this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 email that renders through standard ESP click-tracking, but with about 54 images missing alt text, three non-HTTPS images, and the recipient's address exposed in plaintext in two image URLs. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking:** 65 links go through click-redirect domains and were not probed, e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It may be blocked, and the open event is lost when it is.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is an ad-network beacon.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sister brand's domain, which adds a dependency on a separate host.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,706 of 17,754 characters (about 72%) are URLs. The long `qs=` tracking strings inflate the URL share and can raise spam-filter scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 54 images are flagged. This includes:
  - the entire nightstand slice set (`W26_Furniture_B11_NightstandRoundUp/slices_01–09, 12–21`);
  - `H26_TOP_FallSale.jpg`, the three `H26_Bottom_*` banners, and the six product images (e.g. `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`);
  - the 16 footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_03–18`).
  
  With images blocked, the email is unreadable, and a screen reader gets no link context for the sliced hero. Pure tracking pixels (`coreemail.html`, `clickcp…`, `d.turn.com`, `svopn`) should carry `alt=""` rather than none.
- **HTTP images:** `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel use `http://`. Clients may block or warn on them.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern on mobile.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the final `<style>` block contains PHP `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` text. This is invalid CSS. It doesn't break the rules before it, but it can cause the parser to drop any rules placed after it.
- **Outdated markup:** the layout uses legacy `.ReadMsgBody`/`.ExternalClass` hacks and an old 2022 asset, which suggests a stale template.
- **External font:** `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat` is an external stylesheet. Many clients strip it, and it costs an extra request.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens were seen in the visible source.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient email is embedded unencrypted in query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  
  The second is over HTTP, so the address is sent in cleartext and can appear in proxy and server logs.
- `<title>` is empty, so there is no personalised or descriptive title.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported missing. The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for large senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be verified from the supplied data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the HTML is truncated, so neither could be checked. No violation was found.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is harmless.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters can't be verified. Destinations are hidden in encoded `qs=` payloads on `click.e.potterybarn.com`.
- Product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, so image hosting is on-brand.
- Landing-page alignment with the "Nightstands starting at $349" subject can't be checked because the redirects weren't resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image: the nightstand slices, banners, product shots and footer slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in URL query strings. Use an opaque token or hashed ID.
4. Remove the PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block and fill in `<title>`.
5. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Re-test against a direct-from-ESP copy to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Resolve the tracking redirects to audit UTM parameters and landing pages.
7. Shorten the plain-text part, or reduce the `qs=` payload length, to lower the URL ratio.
