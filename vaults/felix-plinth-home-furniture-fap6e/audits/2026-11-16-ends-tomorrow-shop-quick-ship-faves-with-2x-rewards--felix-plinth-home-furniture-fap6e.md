## Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "ENDS TOMORROW: Shop quick-ship faves with 2x Rewards!"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email's header and link checks were largely inconclusive. The automated QA ran against an AgentMail-relayed copy, so header and redirect results cover only part of the picture. The confirmed defects are about 50 images with no `alt` attribute, a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom, and an unescaped `&` in `<title>`. The QA pass rate was 57% with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **88 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status codes, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **A recipient identifier appears in third-party pixel URLs.** The same hashed prefix `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8` appears in both:
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8…`
  - `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8…&n=1` through `&n=5`
- `sr.rlcdn.com` is a third-party ad-data domain. Five pixels (`n=1..5`) carry this identifier, and so does the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` open pixel. If the email is forwarded or archived, the identifier travels with it.
- **A tracking pixel is repeated.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times in one message. That is redundant, and each copy adds an HTTP request.
- **The video poster looks like a stale asset.** `2026_1002_CBK_CozyWinterEdit_TertiaryVideo` on `s7d5.scene7.com` has a filename dated 2026-10-02 in a 2026-11-16 send. I can't tell from the data whether it is actually stale.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 50 `<img>` elements have no alt text.** The QA flagged these on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`. Examples:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `0782bf25-0357-43a5-8ed2-cca28dcd0d82.gif`
  - `cb526cf0-1b08-4fe3-b985-9eb76106162a.jpg`
  - `8c4ce5d9-cf7b-4fbe-ba17-b9e2435a0e16.gif`
- Content images with no alt text read as filenames in screen readers and show nothing when images are blocked. Many are likely product or hero images that carry the offer.
- **Spacer and tracking images are missing `alt=""`.** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` (2×), the `rlcdn` pixels, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a050242b-…` should have an empty `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. A missing attribute is announced differently from an empty one.
- **The viewport meta blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops users from pinch-zooming, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **The `<title>` is invalid.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Minor source hygiene.**
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags.
  - Both `http-equiv` Content-Type and `<meta charset>` are declared.
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is a typo and not rendered, but it shows the template was not cleaned up.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or similar tokens. The tail of the HTML wasn't available, so this isn't a full check.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) was found.** The QA itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header was found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This is an artifact of the relay, not evidence of a failure.
- **The footer isn't verifiable.** The truncated HTML doesn't reach the physical address or unsubscribe link, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 88 tracking links were skipped and the final destinations are hidden behind redirects. Neither UTM parameters nor landing-page alignment with the "quick-ship / 2x Rewards / ends tomorrow" offer can be confirmed from this data.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels (about 50 images in total).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, and remove the duplicate meta tags and the leftover template comment.
4. Reduce the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel to one copy, and review the 5 `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels for privacy and consent.
5. Re-run the audit against a raw `.eml` or the original delivery headers to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Follow the redirects on the 88 links to confirm HTTP 200s and UTM or campaign IDs, and check that the landing page matches the offer.
7. Confirm the Oct-dated video poster asset is intended for this send.
