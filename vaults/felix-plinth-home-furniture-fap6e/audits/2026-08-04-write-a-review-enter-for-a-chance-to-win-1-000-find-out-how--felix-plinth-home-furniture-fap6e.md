# Write a review. Enter for a chance to win $1,000. Find out how →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Crate & Kids / Crate & Barrel family send in my inbox today, and this one's got the most going on — a $1,000 gift-card sweepstakes tied to leaving a review, a back-to-school sale, a summer sale, AND a full lineup of Nuna car seats, cribs, and a baby activity chair. Same problem I flagged on the "New, new & more new nursery picks" email from earlier today: I don't have a baby. Two houses mid-renovation, zero nurseries.
- The hero bed is fine — teal wood frame, decent styling, could pass for general kids/guest room furniture. But by the time I hit the Nuna convertible car seat and "up to 50% off" on cribs and gliders, I've mentally filed this under "not me" again. It's the same segmentation gap Crate & Kids has had all day, just dressed up with a sweepstakes hook this time.
- I'm not clicking "Write a Review" — I don't own any Crate & Kids product to review, which makes the whole premise a swing and a miss. I'd mark this as read and move on. Not forwarding it, though the twin bed might get a screenshot to my partner just for the color.

## What's working

- The $1,000 gift card hook is a strong scroll-stopper — bigger and more concrete than Pottery Barn's $1,500 gift card email, which buried its ask further down.
- "Write a Review" and "Shop Bestsellers" are both clean, unambiguous buttons — no vague "Discover" copy.
- The Crate Card / Reward Dollars callout mid-email is a real loyalty touch — CB2 and Article rarely bother surfacing card perks this directly.
- Layout is clean start to finish — no overlapping text, no broken image blocks, consistent Crate & Kids branding top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The whole middle section — Nuna car seat, cribs, gliders, baby activity chair, stroller — is nursery gear I have zero use for, same miss as the "New, new & more new nursery picks" send I already flagged as a data problem.
- This is my fifth Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel-family email today. CB2 already burned me out on the sibling-brand fatigue front this week; Crate & Kids doubling down on cadence makes it worse.
- The sweepstakes premise requires me to already own a Crate & Kids product to review — I don't, so the entire CTA is dead on arrival for me.
- Subject line is 64 characters, runs long on mobile, and leads with "write a review" instead of the actual $1,000 hook.
- Fine print at the bottom is dense and stacked — feels like the contest terms are doing a lot of quiet work.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this send by purchase history — anyone without a Crate & Kids order shouldn't get "write a review" as the lead CTA. Swap in "Shop Bestsellers" as primary for non-owners.
- 2. Cut the nursery/baby gear block for households flagged without kids — same fix I asked for on the earlier nursery emails today, still not applied three sends later.
- 3. Lead the subject with the dollar amount, not the ask — "$1,000 gift card, one easy way to enter" tests better than opening with "write a review."
- 4. Move the contest deadline ("ends August 31!") into the subject or preheader — right now it's buried mid-body where it does nothing for open rate.
- 5. Trim the fine print block or link out to full terms instead of stacking it visually at the footer.
