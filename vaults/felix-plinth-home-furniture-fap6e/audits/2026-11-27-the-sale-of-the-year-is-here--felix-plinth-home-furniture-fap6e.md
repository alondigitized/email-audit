# THE SALE OF THE YEAR IS HERE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Same CB2 Black Friday campaign, a different day. The subject says "the sale of the year is here," but the hero is the same "Black Friday SALE, up to 60% off" panel I've seen since 11/26. This time the dark green background has a coupe glass with sugared lemons. It looks nice, but I've now seen this sale about six times this week. "Here" is also odd. It's day two of Black Friday, and the sale has been running all week.
- I did like the layout better than the last few sends. The category grid under "TOO GOOD TO LAST" is clean and tappable. The "Save 15% off in the new year" bounce-back coupon is the only new idea, and it's buried under the free shipping banner. I'd skim it and probably save it to the Pinterest folder. I wouldn't click. I might forward the "Earn 10% back" rewards block to my wife as a reminder to put the renovation spend on the right card.

## What's working

- The hero is big and legible: "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" and "UP TO 60% OFF" are the first things I see, and the glass image is classy.
- The category grid (Living Room, Dining Room, Lighting, Rugs and so on) lets me jump straight to what I'm buying. Lighting and rugs are my renovation shopping.
- The "Extended store hours, Friday 11/27 9AM–9PM" strip is useful and credible, and it's the one genuinely time-specific element.
- The "Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars at CB2 and Crate & Barrel" block speaks my rewards-math language.
- "Free shipping on orders of $99 or more" is clear and matters for furniture.

## What's weak

- It's the same creative as 11/26's "NOT SOLD OUT YET" and "So many best sellers on sale." The subject is new, the email isn't.
- "Sale of the year" is generic, and nothing visible proves it. Also, my 11/27 pillows-and-throws email had an actual product hero. This one has a cocktail glass.
- The 15% off for January is buried mid-page and the fine print is a wall of tiny text. The coupon is only redeemable Jan 7–31 and needs a "first-time" signup. That feels like a bait-and-switch.
- No products, prices or reviews. I can't tell what's 60% off. The "up to" is doing a lot of work.
- The sender "CB2 BLACK FRIDAY" next to the Crate & Barrel and Pottery Barn sends all week blurs together in my inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the mood hero with 3–4 real products at real sale prices (a sofa, a lighting pick, a rug) so I can see the deal.
- 2. Pick one offer and lead with it. Either the 60% off or the 15% new-year coupon, not both stacked.
- 3. Add a swatch or sample link and a furniture delivery estimate. For a renovation buyer, ship dates matter more than a coupe glass.
- 4. Make the subject reflect something new, such as the extended hours or a last-chance deadline.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Skip a day instead of resending the same panel.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open till 9 tonight: up to 60% off at CB2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas, rugs & lighting up to 60% off, ships free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping over $99, plus 15% off in January.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stores open 9–9 today. Best sellers going fast.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly sound (`lang="en"`, XHTML doctype, MSO conditional blocks). The confirmed defects are 15 content images with no alt text and 11 Dotomi tracking pixels loaded over plain `http://`. The automated QA pass rate was 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The header-based compliance checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** 65 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so no destination status codes are available.
- **11 insecure tracking pixels.** All are `http://` (non-HTTPS) and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (`?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`)
- **Cookie-sync pixels.** The ten `pub*.php` calls are third-party cookie-redirect syncs. They add load and third-party exposure with no user-visible function.
- **Per-recipient tracking pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/baa2f664-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carry per-recipient `mi_u` and `mi_mid` identifiers. This is expected for the sender's tracking setup, but note that those parameters are visible in the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **15 content images lack alt text** (`image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/…jpg`). Examples:
  - `978d7bb3-f2b5-4308-9463-2825395a35c9.jpg`
  - `c222de5f-d7b3-4800-9af2-c5a2df5d9e1d.jpg`
  - `20185973-3ca0-4455-9e19-a5471f2ecc5a.jpg`
  
  With images blocked, or for screen readers, the sale content is unavailable.
- **Tracking pixels lack alt text.** These are `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/…` and all Dotomi pixels. It's low impact, but an empty `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom in some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never replaced.
- **Font URLs.** One `@font-face` uses a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`). The `HNW05` faces load from `www.crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain from the sender. Fonts fall back to Helvetica or sans-serif in most clients, so impact is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The only token-like string is the template comment above, which is a placeholder rather than a merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects.** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool itself notes these headers may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so treat this as a capture limitation. SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are unknown.
- **Body checks not possible.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link or postal address, so those can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 65 click links were skipped as redirect domains, and the visible source doesn't expose final URLs or UTM parameters. Tracking looks to be via Movable Ink (`mi_*` parameters) and click wrappers rather than UTM tags on visible hrefs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 15 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS (`https://login.dotomi.com/…`, `https://ads.dotomi.com/…`), or drop the ten `pub*.php` sync pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Delete the stray `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` comment and fix the `//Styles` double-slash font paths.
5. Re-run the audit with raw `.eml` capture, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the in-body unsubscribe link.
6. Resolve the 65 tracked links with a redirect-following probe, to confirm landing pages and UTM presence.
