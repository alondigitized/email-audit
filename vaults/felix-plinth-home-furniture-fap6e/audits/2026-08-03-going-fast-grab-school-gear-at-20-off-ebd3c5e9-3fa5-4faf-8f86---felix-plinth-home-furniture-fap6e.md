# Going fast: grab school gear at 20% off →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids is back with the exact same pitch I would've swiped past yesterday too — backpacks, lunch boxes, "grab school gear before it's gone." I don't have a single school-age kid in either house I'm renovating, so this lands the way it always does: like mail for the previous owner. The design feels tight and the offer is legible, but none of that matters when the entire hero half of the email is a wall of backpack patterns I will never buy.
- What actually stops my scroll is buried two sections down — the "TheDesignDesk" banner offering a free design consultation and a shot at a $5000 gift card. That's the one beat in this whole send that's actually aimed at someone mid-gut-reno on two properties. Everything above and below it is Crate & Kids doing Crate & Kids things.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. I'd scroll straight to the design-consult banner, maybe screenshot that part for my Pinterest-adjacent folder, and archive the rest. This is the same subject line Crate & Kids already ran on me — didn't even bother rotating the creative.

## What's working

- The hero banner is clean — "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off" is immediately legible, no zooming required.
- "Shop Kids Gear" and "Shop All Kids Gear" are unambiguous buttons, not vague "Discover" copy.
- The nursery furniture block (dresser, glider, crib) at least has adult-adjacent styling I could imagine in a guest room — better than the backpacks.
- TheDesignDesk section with the $5000 sweepstakes and free design appointment is genuinely on-brief for a renovator like me — Crate & Barrel's design-pro angle is the strongest thing in this email by a mile.
- No render bugs — images loaded, no overlapping text, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The entire top third — backpacks, duffle bags, lunch boxes, water bottles — is zero-relevance to a childless 36-year-old mid-renovation. Wayfair's 48-hour rug clearance (9/10 last week) at least led with something I'd actually buy the second I opened it.
- This is the same "Going fast: grab school gear at 20% off" subject Crate & Kids already sent me — no new creative, no new hook, just a rerun.
- It's the fifth-ish Crate & Barrel-family send hitting my inbox this stretch (sofas twice, bedding, now this) — my inbox is basically on C&B autopilot at this point, same fatigue CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" email triggered.
- The Crate Card "Reward Dollars" callout is dropped in with zero specifics — no earn rate, no dollar threshold — Article's "Last chance" sale at least backed up its urgency with a visible badge; this just says "Learn More" and moves on.
- "Selling Fast!" urgency has no actual deadline attached — no date, no countdown, unlike Wayfair's literal "48HR" giant-type badge.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely, or lead with TheDesignDesk instead.** If Crate & Barrel already knows I've never clicked a kids' product, put the $5000 design-consult offer at the top of the email, not buried after two backpack CTAs.
- 2. **Stop rerunning the identical subject line.** I've already seen "Going fast: grab school gear at 20% off →" — if the offer's the same, at least vary the hook so it doesn't read as a duplicate send.
- 3. **Give the Reward Dollars program actual numbers.** "The card you love just got better" with no earn rate is a wasted CTA slot — tell me what "better" means.
- 4. **Attach a real deadline to "Selling Fast."** A date or countdown (like Wayfair's 48HR badge) would make the urgency credible instead of decorative.
- 5. **Cross-sell the nursery/design content to non-parent renovators specifically** — the dresser, glider, and design-desk content could be reframed as "guest room" or "whole-home refresh" copy instead of being trapped under the Crate & Kids banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free design consult + a shot at $5,000`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off beds, cribs & storage — plus a free design session`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book with a Crate & Barrel designer, no cost to you`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save on furniture the whole house needs, not just the kids' room`
