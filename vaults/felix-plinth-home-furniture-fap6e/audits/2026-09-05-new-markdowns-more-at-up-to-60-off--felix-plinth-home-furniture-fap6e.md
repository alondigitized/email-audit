# NEW markdowns! More at up to 60% off →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Crate & Kids email today — I already dinged the "5-star bedroom faves" send this morning, and the double-rewards blast right after it, and now this one shows up with basically the same nursery gliders, cribs, and "up to 60% off" banner just reshuffled. My inbox is not a nursery. I'm gutting a primary and a second home right now — I need rugs, lighting, storage furniture for adult rooms, not a $299 glider and a stroller travel system. The one thing that made me pause for half a second was the rugs and decor/lighting tiles buried in the middle grid, because that's actually stuff I'm shopping for, but it's surrounded by so much kid gear that I scrolled right past it.
- This is going straight to "mark as read, don't click." I'm not forwarding this to anyone — the only person I'd forward a Crate & Kids email to is a friend who just had a baby, and that's not the renovation crew I'm coordinating with.

## What's working

- The rugs and "decor & lighting" tiles in the "up to 60% off" grid are genuinely relevant to my two-house renovation, unlike everything else in this send.
- Clean product photography, no broken images or layout glitches — at least it renders properly, unlike some of the janky Wayfair emails I get.
- The "Double Rewards" banner is at least a concrete, specific mechanic (10%/20% back) rather than vague language.

## What's weak

- This is nursery/kids furniture — cribs, gliders, "Babyletto bestsellers," strollers — none of which applies to me, and Crate & Kids has now sent me three near-identical "up to 60%" emails today alone.
- Subject says "NEW markdowns" but the hero and product grid are the same Labor Day sale assets I've seen twice already today under different subject lines.
- Way too long a scroll for a promo email — CB2's "Rugs on sale. So worth it." gets to the point in one screen; this one buries anything I'd actually want six sections down.
- The "we love these for you" personalized-feeling section recommends a glider, two stroller travel systems, and a bassinet — if this is meant to feel personalized, it's personalized for the wrong customer entirely.
- Fine print risk: "up to 60% off" in the subject but most tiles show 30-50% — same bait-and-switch pattern Pottery Barn pulled with its "Extra 20% off" banner that undersold what was actually available.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress me from Crate & Kids nursery sends entirely and route me to Crate & Barrel's adult-furniture list — sending the same person three baby-furniture emails in one day is a segmentation failure, not a creative one.
- 2. If cross-sell to a &Kids subscriber who's also flagged as doing whole-home renovation, lead with the rugs/decor/lighting items instead of burying them mid-scroll.
- 3. Cut this email's length by half — one hero, one product grid, one CTA. Article's "Up to 40% off ends Monday" gets the same sale message across in a third of the scroll.
- 4. Fix the "up to 60%" headline math so it reflects the actual majority discount range shown in the grid, not just the ceiling number.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall refresh: rugs + lighting markdowns inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New rug and lighting markdowns just landed — plus 2x Rewards through Labor Day`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

Standard ESP (SFMC/Movable Ink-style) template using redirect-tracked links and pixel-based tracking; automated checks show 57% pass rate driven mostly by widespread missing `alt` attributes and unconfirmed authentication/unsubscribe headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 86 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped from HTTP probing — actual destination URLs behind these redirects were not verified for validity (could not confirm no 404s/broken redirects).
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5 instances, sequential `n=1`–`n=5` params suggesting LiveRamp/RLCDN identity resolution calls), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v` (view-through conversion pixel). No functional issues found in these, but volume (12+ tracking-only requests) adds load overhead.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- All content images are missing `alt` attributes — QA flagged 38 distinct image warnings, including product imagery (e.g., `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `68910f1a-626e-4b12-8004-76fbb6577154.jpg`) and the Scene7-hosted clearance banner (`2026_0315_CBK_Clearance_01_05.gif`). This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades the experience for screen-reader users and image-blocked clients (default in Outlook).
- Tracking pixels (`f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`) also lack `alt=""`, which is correct to leave empty but they should still carry an explicit empty `alt` attribute rather than none, for stricter HTML validation.
- Head contains legacy/defensive boilerplate (XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, MSO conditional comments, multiple redundant `format-detection` meta tags) consistent with broad legacy client support (Outlook desktop); no errors found in this markup itself.
- Media query breakpoints at 460px, 640px, and 768px are present and structurally valid; no malformed CSS detected in the visible source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (CAN-SPAM requires a functioning unsubscribe mechanism, which may still exist as an in-body link not captured in the truncated source, but the header-level mechanism is unconfirmed).

- Caveat: all three warnings are flagged by QA as possibly artifacts of the AgentMail relay not preserving/capturing these headers, rather than confirmed absence at the originating MTA. Treat as "unconfirmed," not "confirmed missing."
