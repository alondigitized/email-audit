## What's weak

- I have no children. This is the third Crate & Kids send this week and it's 100% baby gear, top to bottom — cribs, quilts, activity chairs, a rocking chair, a diaper caddy.
- The "we love these for you" personalization block is just car seats and strollers — still nursery gear, so the "for you" framing is a lie for someone in my segment.
- "The Design Desk" pitch at the bottom (free design appointment) is actually relevant to a renovation guy like me, but it's buried under 15 nursery product tiles before I'd ever scroll that far.
- Subject line "prices you'll love" is generic filler — doesn't even hint this is a kids-only send, so I open expecting maybe something crossover and get pure nursery.

- 2. If Crate & Kids must email non-parent subscribers, lead with the Design Desk / broader home content and cut the crib inventory dump — Article does this better with room-story sends instead of raw catalog scroll.
- 3. Move The Design Desk appointment CTA up near the top for the segment of your list mid-renovation, since that's the one piece of this email actually built for someone like me.
