# JUST DROPPED: Nina Garcia Collection
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the CB2 email I'd actually save to my Pinterest folder. It's a long, editorial collection launch: moody tablescapes, burgundy and bone marble, candlelight, and a Nina Garcia pull-quote. After four days of dining-event countdowns ("ONE DAY LEFT" on 10/27, "FINAL HOURS" yesterday), a send with no clock on it feels like a relief. It's pretty and it has a point of view.
- The rendering is very tall, and I see three different emails stacked together. First comes the collection story. Then there's an Interior Design Services plug. Last is a Warehouse Sale banner ("Up to 60% off") with a "More for you" grid of glassware. That last section undercuts the launch. I'm in a renovation, so the idea of styling a table is fine. But the price-led glassware grid pulls me out of the editorial mood and back into promo-email mode.
- Would I click? Probably once, on "Shop the new collection," just to see prices. I would save it to my inbox folder. I'd forward it to my partner for the dining room, because the checkerboard and the stemware are on-palette for the second house. I'd skim the rest and mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero is a styled table with glassware, candles and florals. It sets the mood without a headline fighting it.
- "ALL DRESSED UP" with Nina Garcia's photo and the pull-quotes gives the launch a reason to exist. The "extension of you" line makes it feel curated, not just inventory.
- The photo grid of stemware, the marble board, and the checkerboard is all in one burgundy, bone and smoke palette. As someone who colour-matches across two houses, I noticed.
- "Shop the new collection" appears twice, as a clear dark button. It sits after the hero and again after the checkerboard shot.
- The design services block, with "Book your free appointment," is useful for someone renovating. It's a rare showroom-integration touch.

## What's weak

- I can't see a single product name or price in the collection section. It's mood with no merchandise, so I have to click blind.
- The Warehouse Sale banner ("Up to 60% off") sits right under the launch and clashes with it. A new designer collection next to markdowns undercuts the premium feel.
- The "More for you" glass grid is the same Marta/Neat glassware I'd expect in a generic send. It has nothing to do with Nina Garcia, and the sale prices read as filler.
- The email is very long. The services block, app download, gift and signup tiles, and the footer all pile up after the point is made.
- The paragraph of small print near the checkerboard is tiny. I couldn't read it on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Warehouse Sale banner and the generic glassware grid. Replace them with 4 to 6 named Nina Garcia pieces with prices, such as the stemware, the marble board and the checkerboard. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Add one line about shipping and lead time (in stock or ships by...) near the collection CTA. That's the question I always have with furniture and decor.
- 3. Show rewards or card math, or the 15% sign-up offer, once near the top instead of down in the footer tiles.
- 4. Move the design services block up as a "style it with us" step after the product grid.
- 5. Give the CTAs specific copy, like "Shop the glassware" or "Shop tabletop", instead of repeating "Shop the new collection."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with VML/MSO fallbacks. The QA run found 0 hard failures. The main issues are 11 insecure (`http://`) Dotomi tracking pixels, many images with no alt text, and a viewport that blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **67 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations or HTTP statuses are verified.
- **10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain HTTP:**
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` through `pub10.php`
- **One more Dotomi pixel is also `http://`:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - Clients that block mixed content or proxy images can drop these requests.
  - Those clients won't record the cookie sync.
  - Some security scanners flag non-HTTPS resources.
- **Three first-party open-tracking pixels have no alt text:**
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/b771210c-6c84-40e5-970d-970d3cd9c85c?mi_cid=…`
  - The Dotomi pixels above also have no alt text.
  - Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` along with explicit `width="1" height="1"`. The captured source doesn't show whether those attributes are present.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 content images** hosted at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. Examples: `974f50f3-8733-4249-a0aa-b8129b872199.jpg`, `005bf537-3038-4161-a692-db80054a37df.jpg`, `55879f10-3a3a-429d-af35-089896da5954.jpg`.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from an intentional `alt=""`.
  - If these images are the product and collection imagery, the email has no text equivalent when images are blocked.
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME` doesn't match the subject line, "JUST DROPPED: Nina Garcia Collection". This is minor.
- **Font URLs:**
  - Several `@font-face` URLs have a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`).
  - The `HNW05` family is loaded from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain.
  - The first "Lineto Brown Sub" face offers only `.eot` plus `?#iefix` fallbacks.
  - Web fonts are ignored by most clients, so the impact is limited to the clients that do load them.
- **Template cleanliness:**
  - A leftover placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the head.
  - An empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block is also left over.
  - Neither one affects rendering.
- **`*{line-height:100%}` with `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`:**
  - Both are in the global CSS.
  - The second disables text resizing in WebKit, which compounds the zoom restriction above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The captured HTML shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]`. The `mi_u=<hash>` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters on the tracking pixels are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not present in the captured data.** The QA tool itself suspects the AgentMail relay stripped them. Treat this as unverified rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from this capture.
- **The footer's unsubscribe link and physical postal address aren't visible in the truncated source.** I can't confirm or deny them. Re-run the check against the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 67 links are wrapped in click-redirect URLs that were not followed. The final landing URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "Nina Garcia Collection" are therefore unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS.** Switch all 11 `http://ads.dotomi.com` and `http://login.dotomi.com` URLs, or drop the sync pixels if they aren't needed.
2. **Fix alt text.** Add descriptive `alt` to the 11 `image.mail.cb2.com` images, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. **Change the viewport meta.** Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and remove `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` if it isn't required.
4. **Resolve redirect chains.** Follow a sample of the 67 tracked links with a redirect-following probe to confirm final status codes and UTM parameters.
5. **Check headers on the raw message.** Pull the raw message from a direct-to-mailbox seed, not the relay, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. **Clean up the template.** Remove the `IMPUT` placeholder comment, fix the `//Styles` double-slash font URLs, and align the `<title>` with the subject line.
