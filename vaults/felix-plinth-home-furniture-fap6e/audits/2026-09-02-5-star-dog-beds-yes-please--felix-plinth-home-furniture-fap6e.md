# 5-star DOG BEDS? Yes, please 🙋
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "5-star DOG BEDS? Yes, please 🙋" — subject checks out, top-left tile is literally a dog on a green bed. But then I keep scrolling and it's the same kitchen-sink grid Wayfair sends every single time: pergolas, privacy screens, console tables, air conditioners, recliners, planters, shed doors. There's a dog bed tile up top AND another "Popular Medium-Sized Dog Products" tile AND a "Dog Beds for Less" tile at the very bottom — three separate dog placements in one email, which feels less like a curated pick and more like they just dumped the whole catalog and hoped one dog photo would carry the subject line.
- I'm mid-renovation on two houses, so console tables and privacy screens and recliners are actually relevant to me — but the layout gives every category equal visual weight, so nothing pops. And there's a straight-up blank white gap near the bottom of the email, right before the rewards icons section, like an image failed to load or a module got left empty.
- I'd mark this as read without clicking. It's not offensive, but it's the fourth or fifth near-identical Wayfair grid I've seen this cycle, and the actual "must-shop steals" banner up top buries the dog beds under pergolas and privacy screens instead of leading with them.

## What's working

- Subject-to-hero match is real this time — dog bed tile is first in the grid, continuing the streak I noted on the ladders and pergola sends.
- The purple "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" banner up top is at least a clear, specific number, unlike CB2's vaguer "$5k design refresh" pitch.
- Member-only kitchen staples callout with a red "Member Deals" flag is a nice loyalty touch — I'm in Wayfair Rewards, so that's actually for me.
- Second dog tile ("Popular Medium-Sized Dog Products") shows a decent-sized dog on a teal bed — matches my dog's size, so someone's at least segmenting loosely.

## What's weak

- Three different dog-bed placements in one email dilutes the "yes please" subject line instead of delivering on it — feels like the subject was bolted onto a generic grid rather than the grid built around the subject.
- Blank white space near the footer, right before the rewards icon row — looks like a broken or missing image module.
- Zero renovation-specific content despite me having two houses in progress — no swatch/sample program, no "complete the room" bundling, nothing tailored to someone furnishing multiple properties.
- Visual hierarchy is flat — recliners, air conditioners, shed doors, and dog beds all get identical tile treatment, so my eye doesn't land anywhere specific.
- This is basically the same multi-category grid format I've now seen from Wayfair four sends running; at some point "must-shop steals" stops meaning anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the grid down to one theme per send — if the subject is dog beds, lead with dog beds and 2-3 complementary pet items, not pergolas and bathroom vanities crammed in around it.
- 2. Fix the blank white gap above the rewards footer — that's a real render bug bleeding trust.
- 3. Since I'm a Rewards member, surface my cashback %/points balance instead of a generic "5% + more perks" pitch — Wayfair already knows I'm enrolled.
- 4. Add a size/breed filter CTA on the dog bed tiles ("Shop by size") since that's the one purchase decision that actually matters for this category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your dog's next favorite spot: 5-star beds`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Medium dog? These beds have 5,000+ reviews`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Chew-resistant, machine-washable, rated 5 stars by real owners`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: 70% off Labor Day clearance sitewide`
