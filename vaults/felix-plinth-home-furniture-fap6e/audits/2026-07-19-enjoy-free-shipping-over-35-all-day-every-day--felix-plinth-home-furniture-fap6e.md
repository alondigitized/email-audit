# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- I did a double-take on the date when this landed, because I opened a Wayfair email with this exact subject line yesterday. Same sender, same "FREE shipping over $35, all day, every day" — a policy I've known since at least 2023 and have literally never thought about consciously because Wayfair has charged me zero shipping on anything over $35 for as long as I've been on the list. That's the subject line. That's the pitch. Open me.
- When I do open, the hero is a 72-HR CLEAROUT at 60% off, which is a genuinely different email. That clearout is relevant — I've got wall art, side tables, and accent pieces on the list for both homes. But the subject gave me no reason to open. The email and the subject line are describing two different sends, and one of them is a send that already went out yesterday.
- I won't click through. I scrolled, I registered the clearout exists, I moved on. I'd never forward this — if a friend asked me about Wayfair shipping I'd just answer them directly.

## What's working

- The 72-HR CLEAROUT hero is visually dominant and reads fast — 60% off on a red banner lands even on a small screen
- Wayfair Rewards callout ("get 5% rewards... on every item, always") is the one piece of copy that speaks to how I actually buy — I route Wayfair purchases through a specific card precisely because of this stacking
- Clean render, no broken images, legible text at thumbnail scale — no layout drama
- The sub-brand lineup at the bottom (AllModern, Birch Lane, Joss&Main, Perigold) is a useful reminder of where to scope when I want a specific aesthetic register

## What's weak

- Subject and email are describing two completely different things — the subject is a shipping policy memo, the email opens on a clearance event; that mismatch is disorienting
- This is the second consecutive day this exact subject line landed in my inbox — I checked because I had to — back-to-back resends with identical subjects feel like an ops accident, not a campaign
- "Take a look →" for the rewards section isn't a CTA, it's a suggestion from someone who wasn't sure whether to include it
- The clearout hero has zero product specificity — 60% off *what exactly*? Article put editorial product context behind their sale emails; Pottery Barn names the category in the subject line; Wayfair just says the number
- Multiple competing modules (clearout → rewards → benefits grid → app QR code) with no clear primary action — this email doesn't know what it's asking me to do

## Recommendations

- 1. **Match subject to email.** If the hero is 72-HR CLEAROUT at 60% off, the subject should say that. Free shipping is footer material.
- 2. **Pick one thing.** Lead with the clearout; let rewards and the app download drop to secondary. One email, one ask.
- 3. **Name the category in the hero.** "60% off sofas & accent tables" tells me in four words whether I need to pay attention. "60% OFF" tells me nothing actionable.
- 4. **Stop resending identical subjects on consecutive days.** If this is a different email, it needs a different subject. Non-negotiable.
- 5. **Put one hero product in.** One room scene, one linked item, one specific price — that's what moves me from open to click. Article has been doing this for two months straight and I've clicked every one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `72-HR Clearout: 60% off. Ends Sunday.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas, tables, rugs — 60% off for 72 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5% rewards stacks on everything — including clearance items`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping always. This week, stack 60% off too.`
