# OUR BIGGEST BLACK FRIDAY SALE IS HAPPENING NOW!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Same Crate & Barrel send, third time today. It's the one I've already seen under this subject on 11/27, and the sender line "BLACK FRIDAY" at least stops pretending it's something new. The red ribbon hero says "Biggest Sale of the Year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + 1-day-only deals + free shipping!" I get the offer in two seconds. Then comes a very long scroll of tiles, each with its own "Shop Now" button. It reads like a catalog dumped into an email.
- For my two renovations, the useful parts are sofas and sectionals (up to 40% off), furniture (up to 50% off), rugs, decor, and the kitchen block at the bottom. That block has the Breville espresso machines and Le Creuset, the same stuff that made the 11/27 "Hot Black Friday Kitchen deals" send my favorite. Kids and holiday stockings are noise to me. I'd skim it, maybe tap sofas, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: up to 50% off, 1-day-only deals, free shipping. I don't have to hunt for it.
- Each tile has a % off label and a "Shop Now" button, so every tap target is obvious and thumb-sized.
- "Sofas & sectionals up to 40% off" and "furniture up to 50% off" are in my lane. The photography is calm and neutral, and I'd save those images to my Pinterest-adjacent folder.
- The kitchen block at the bottom has real prices: Breville $799.95, "$200 off select espresso machines," Le Creuset "$170 off." That's the concrete detail I care about.
- "Free shipping on orders $79+" is spelled out. Shipping clarity matters when I'm ordering a sectional.

## What's weak

- It's about twelve tiles with the same button, so there's no hierarchy after the hero. Pottery Barn's "TODAY ONLY DEALS" block at least pulled one urgent thing to the top.
- The "today only: 40% off wreaths, garlands" tile is the only real urgency, and it's for wreaths. The furniture is the big-ticket item and it gets no deadline.
- Crate & Barrel Kids is in the middle. I don't have kids, and the sender has been showing me kids content all week.
- The best content, the kitchen deals with actual prices, is buried at the very bottom. I'd put it right under the hero.
- There's no rewards or card angle, no swatch or sample mention, and no delivery timing for furniture. For sofas, that's what decides whether I buy.
- The long page is mostly lifestyle photos with small captions. On my phone the tiny text under the photos is hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the product categories I actually shop for, with one hero and a deadline. Move the kitchen deal block up under the hero, since it's the only section with real prices.
- 2. Cut the 12 tiles to about five. Merge holiday decor, hosting, and wreaths into one tile. Drop Kids unless the list is a parent.
- 3. Put a delivery-by-Christmas note and a swatch/sample link on the sofa tile.
- 4. Add a countdown or "ends tonight" tag to the furniture offer, not just the wreaths.
- 5. Let me opt out of the family sends. This is my third C&B family email today and I'm in snooze mode, same as with CB2.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: sectionals, rugs & espresso deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture up to 50% off. Free shipping, 1-day-only deals inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Breville espresso, Le Creuset & sectionals, all marked down.`

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "OUR BIGGEST BLACK FRIDAY SALE IS HAPPENING NOW!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with Outlook (MSO) conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes on about 40 images (the QA list is truncated, so the real count may be higher) and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed from this data. The source provided is truncated, so findings are limited to the visible portion and the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them because they are on click-redirect domains, so destination status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2…&n=1` through `&n=5` are five identical LiveRamp pixel calls, differing only in `n`. They add third-party requests and are a privacy and deliverability consideration.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` and `…/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` carry a recipient hash. The same value appears in the rlcdn `s=` parameter, so it is shared with a third party.
- **Open-tracking beacon.** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4210b1c8-ab68-4a1b-a928-c57038137235?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` has no extension and no `alt`.
- No broken links were reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 40 images.** This is confirmed by QA. Examples:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - 25+ `…/m/1/*.jpg` and `*.gif` files, such as `3a6e0878-…jpg`, `77b4f73e-…gif` and `971aa10f-…jpg`
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images
  - The content images and GIFs need descriptive `alt`. The tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`) should carry `alt=""`.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, content images have no fallback text.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid XHTML.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses an unescaped ampersand under an XHTML 1.0 doctype and should be `&amp;`.
- **Template leftover.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a typo'd placeholder comment left in the shipped source.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared three times, with `telephone/address/email/date/url` in one tag and again as separate tags. This is harmless but redundant.
- `lang="en"` is present.
- The mobile CSS includes `.mobile {width:460px}` and `.showmobile {width:414px}` fixed widths. These could cause horizontal overflow at viewports narrower than 414px, but that can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** `[WARN]`.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** `[WARN]`.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** `[WARN]`, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown.
- The QA notes say these may be relay artifacts, since AgentMail may strip headers. These are not confirmed failures. Verify against raw headers before treating them as defects. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it matters if the headers really are missing.
- The visible portion of the source doesn't show the footer, so a physical address and an in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be verified.
- The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, which is consistent with good practice, but alignment is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 81 tracking links were skipped, so the final landing URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page content can't be compared to the email. Destination URLs behind the click-redirect wrappers should be resolved in a follow-up.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment and the duplicate `format-detection` tags.
4. Consolidate the five identical `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls into one, or confirm they are intentional.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message at the receiving MTA rather than the AgentMail relay.
6. Resolve a sample of the 81 tracked links with redirect-following to check destination status and UTM consistency.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and physical address can be checked.
