# 1 new nursery chair, customized 4 ways
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that I'm not having a baby and I'm not shopping for a nursery. I'm renovating two houses, so this is the Kids sender again, and it's the eighth Crate & Barrel family email I've logged since yesterday. I'll give it credit for the execution. The email is a long, calm scroll of four fabric stories: field green stripes, oat tan plain weave, denim blue stripes and linen natural stripes. Each one pairs a styled room with a swatch tile and three product picks.
- The swatch-first structure is what I wish Pottery Barn did. PB's "A Place to Gather" send was pretty, but it never showed me a fabric. This one shows the fabric, then the room, then the SKUs. The "Start with a mood board" block is the kind of thing I'd save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. But the category is wrong for me, I can't find a dollar or percent offer anywhere, and the whole thing is very long. I'd skim it, maybe save one image, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The swatch-led layout matches how I plan a room. I pick the fabric first, then see it in a room, then see the furniture.
- Each fabric section repeats the same pattern: lifestyle photo, swatch tile, then three products tagged New Arrival, Bestseller or Exclusive. It's easy to scan.
- The "Book a Free Appointment" button shows up repeatedly. The Design Desk gives it a showroom-and-service angle that Wayfair's "Shop Now" grab bags don't have.
- The colour palette is soft and cohesive: sage, oat, denim and linen. It looks like a catalog and not a flash sale.
- The Affirm "pay later" block at the bottom is a clear financing cue for a big furniture spend.

## What's weak

- It's a nursery story and I have no nursery. Same Kids sender as the "“I can do it myself” toddler-approved picks" and "The handcrafted furniture you’ll pass down someday" sends.
- I can't see a price, a percent off, free shipping or a rewards hook. Wayfair's "Your pergola search is over!" at least put "$134 off" on a badge. I'd earn nothing extra on my rewards card here.
- It's far too long. Four near-identical fabric blocks plus the "more ways The Design Desk can help" block make the email feel like a catalog, and the lower sections get thin.
- The product captions and the secondary buttons are small and low-contrast on a phone. The pale buttons under some sections nearly disappear. The shrunken screenshot doesn't let me read the exact product names.
- This is another Crate & Barrel-family send in a week when I've already had CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" and "Popular for a reason". I'm in the "same lamp in three brands" fatigue zone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to the hero chair plus two fabric stories, and put one concrete reason to click up top: an offer, a swatch deadline or free shipping on the chair.
- 2. Show lead time and shipping for the chair above the fold. Furniture buyers care about that, and fabric-heavy sends like this can raise the question.
- 3. Offer "Order free swatches" as a primary button next to "Shop Nursery Seating". It matches how I shop, and it gives people not buying a nursery chair a way in.
- 4. Make the secondary buttons high-contrast and bigger for thumbs.
- 5. Segment. I'm not the nursery audience, and a home-renovation list would be better served by the main Crate & Barrel sender.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Crate & Barrel Kids send through Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) and a click-tracking layer. It has widespread missing alt text and a heavy tracking footprint (98 tracked links, 5 `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels). The compliance header checks came back inconclusive because of relay capture limits. The QA pass rate was 57%, with 0 failures and 3 warnings.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 98 tracking links were skipped for HTTP probing because they sit on click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and final landing URLs are unverified.
- Five identical LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`) fire from one message. The `s=` value is the same hash that appears in the Movable Ink `mi_u=` parameter, so a recipient identifier is passed to a third-party data-onboarding domain. This is not a defect, but it is a privacy and deliverability consideration. Several third-party pixel domains can also add to image-blocking and spam-filter exposure.
- `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times. These are repeated Movable Ink dynamic-content images. The same `mi_u=` identifier is also in the `o.gif` open pixel (`/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2358d13b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. The identifier appears in URLs as plain query parameters. This is by design for Movable Ink, but the URLs carry it in the clear.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm broken hrefs, empty hrefs, or `http://` (non-TLS) links in the body.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- About 55 images have no `alt` attribute. Examples:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, such as `ce06de22-0d24-4f24-b798-a5dffbfe3cc6.jpg` and `aa5b6e5b-42f0-491f-8b43-b0401ad2e403.jpg`.
  - Animated GIFs, such as `a62b6734-eb51-40b6-87f2-e73a75d9cacb.gif` and `8751568e-…gif`.
  - Scene7 images, such as `2026%5F0902%5FCBK%5FBabyAwarenessCTV%5FSecondary.g…` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
- Because many of these are likely linked product or CTA images, screen-reader users and users with images blocked get no usable content or link labels. A missing `alt` attribute is worse than `alt=""`, because some readers announce the filename.
- Spacers and tracking pixels (`25_MI_Bottom_Spacer`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`) should carry `alt=""` so they are explicitly decorative. The QA tool flags them as noise.
- Positives: `<html lang="en">` is set, and the Outlook conditionals (`mso`, `OfficeDocumentSettings`) and viewport meta tags are present. `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` contains an unescaped `&`. This is minor and tolerated by clients, but invalid XHTML given the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
- The meta tags have duplicates and conflicts: `format-detection` is declared four times, and `meta charset` is declared twice. This is harmless but sloppy.
- `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production HTML.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`). The per-recipient `mi_u=` and `s=` values were substituted. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may be stripping these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, and alignment could not be checked.
- The visible HTML source was truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm an in-body unsubscribe link or a physical postal address. Check the full source before drawing a CAN-SPAM conclusion.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No UTM parameters could be confirmed. All 98 links are on click-redirect domains and were not resolved, so the final URLs and any `utm_*` or campaign parameters are unknown.
- Landing page alignment with the "nursery chair, customized 4 ways" subject could not be verified without following the redirects.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to every content and CTA image. Use `alt=""` on spacers, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, and the Movable Ink pixels.
2. **Viewport:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` so users can zoom.
3. **Markup cleanup:**
   - Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`.
   - Remove the duplicate `meta charset` and `format-detection` tags.
   - Strip the `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
4. **Headers:** Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers from the original message, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`.
5. **Link verification:** Resolve a sample of the 98 tracking links with a redirect-following probe, and check the final URLs for `utm_*` parameters and for 200 status.
6. **Source truncation:** Run the audit on the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, and merge tokens can be checked.
7. **Third-party pixels:** Consider consolidating the five LiveRamp pixels into one, and review whether sharing the hashed identifier with them is intended.
