- "Recently Purchased" counts under the green dresser (4427) and round coffee table (1199) are a real trust signal — Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel almost never show social proof like that.
- The green dresser and round coffee table shots are genuinely styleable — that's the kind of statement furniture photo I'd screenshot for my reno folder, similar to how that chandelier email caught my eye Monday.

## What's weak

- After the rug hero, it's back to the exact same "Must-See Price" / "Grab It Fast" module structure I got in the privacy-screens and covers-and-bags emails this week — slipcovers, pergolas, cookware, none of it related to rugs.
- "Sales You *Almost* Missed" is seven unrelated clearance categories — bedding, mattresses, dining furniture, coat racks, nursery — classic spreadsheet-dressed-as-email, same complaint I had about Sunday's category-grid send.
- Nursery essentials in a rug email makes zero sense for my profile — I'm renovating two houses, not a nursery, and CB2 and Article don't pad their sends with irrelevant categories like this.
- No swatch/sample program, no room-visualizer, no shipping-timeline callout for rugs specifically — for a category where size and pile matter this much, that's a real miss compared to Article's collection pages which at least show dimensions up front.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to rugs and adjacent categories only (pads, room refresh) — kill the pergolas/cookware/nursery detour entirely. A focused rug email would out-perform this grid every time, the way Article's Ashford dining launch beat this same day's category dump.
- 2. Add a size/pile-height filter or "shop by room size" CTA under the rug hero — rugs are dimension-driven purchases, the current CTA just says "Shop All Picks for You" with zero rug specificity.
- 3. Move "Recently Purchased" counts onto the rug hero itself, not just the dresser/table — that's Wayfair's strongest trust signal and it's buried mid-scroll.
- 4. Drop "Sales You *Almost* Missed" as a module entirely or trim it to 2-3 genuinely adjacent categories — right now it reads like an inventory clearance dump, not a curated pick.
