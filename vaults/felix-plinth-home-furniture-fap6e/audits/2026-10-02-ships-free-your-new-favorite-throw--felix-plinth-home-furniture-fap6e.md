# Ships FREE: Your new favorite throw
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the tenth send I've counted since 9/29. The hero is a stack of chunky knit throws on a rustic bench with "Cozy Throws Ship Free." It's warm and it's clear, and the offer is right there. That's better than the "Layer your bed" email on 10/1, which was basically a mood board.
- The trouble is that the "free shipping" promise gets stretched across pillows, table linens, flatware and curtains. The throw in the subject line gets one hero and then disappears. The "Recommended Just for You" block has a bed, a swivel chair, a jute rug, a curtain rod and ghost decorations. That feels like a generic carousel, not something that knows I'm renovating two houses. The Christmas Shop banner shows up on October 2nd, and the footer is a wall of fine print.
- Would I click? Maybe the throw hero, if I'm already in the market for textiles. Would I forward it? No. I'd file it in my Pinterest folder and move on. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The hero is clean: "Cozy Throws Ship Free" with a visible SHOP NOW button. The offer is the first thing my eye lands on.
- The throw photo is gorgeous and matches the textured, neutral palette I'm buying into.
- The 2x3 category grid (Pillows, Sheets, Duvets, Quilts) is easy to scan and tap on a phone.
- The free design appointment banner is actually useful for someone doing two renovations.
- The credit card block shows 10% back in rewards. I do the rewards math on every purchase, so I noticed it.

## What's weak

- The throws are the subject line and the hero, then they vanish. There's no product, no price and no reviews. I can't tell which throw I'd be getting.
- "Ships Free" gets stretched across everything from flatware to curtains. It reads like a shipping promo, not a throw promo. I don't see a threshold or a deadline.
- The "Recommended Just for You" block is a random mix: a platform bed, a swivel chair, ghost decorations. It's not personalized in any way I can see, and Wayfair at least tries to stay in one category.
- The Christmas Shop banner sits right in the middle of a cozy-textiles email. The two messages compete.
- The footer is a wall of tiny exclusions and fine print, which makes "free shipping" feel less honest.
- It's the tenth send in four days. The 9/30 "season of gathering" email, the Big Daddy's Antiques collab and the Holiday Lookbook already covered this moody, cozy look.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the throws the whole email. Show four or five specific throws with names, prices and star ratings, and drop the unrelated categories.
- 2. Say what "free" means. For example: "Free shipping on throws, pillows and bedding. Ends Sunday." Put the threshold and deadline in the hero.
- 3. Cut the Christmas Shop and ghost decorations from this send. Save them for a holiday email.
- 4. Segment the recommendations by browsing history so they track what I actually looked at, and cut the number of sends. CB2 has the same frequency problem, and it's burning my attention too.
- 5. Move the 10% rewards card and affirm blocks below the products, and tighten the fine print.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus pillows, duvets and quilts. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft, heavy and neutral. Free delivery through 10/6.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Ships FREE: Your new favorite throw"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with wrapped, redirected links (54 tracking links, all skipped by the HTTP probe). The confirmed problems are widespread missing alt text, three plain `http://` image sources, stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient address exposed in tracking-pixel URLs. The HTML was truncated, so the unsubscribe link and the footer address could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not verifiable:** 54 links go through redirect domains such as `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`. The QA probe skipped all of them, so destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed. This is a limit of the audit, not a defect.
- **Insecure image sources:** Three images load over `http://` and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` (open pixel)
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on a sibling brand's domain. This creates a dependency on another property's CDN.
- **Multiple third-party pixels:** The email loads `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…`, `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/…`, and `coreemail.html?email=…`. Several of these are open-tracking beacons. This is not a defect, but it adds image requests and failure points.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 50 images have no `alt`. This includes every sliced image in the main banner (`F26_FreeShip_B7_FreeShipThrows/slices_01–11.jpg`), the product images (`cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `chunky-wool-jute-rug-l.jpg`, and others), and the footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`). With images blocked, the email would render blank or unreadable, and screen readers would announce filenames. The 1x1 tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`, `coreemail.html`) are expected to lack alt and should get `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The `<html>` tag only declares `xmlns` attributes.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** The last `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…")` and similar lines. This is invalid CSS from a template leak. It probably does no harm, but it can break parsing of any rules that follow it in strict clients.
- **Image-only construction:** The main content is sliced images, so text is not live. This is consistent with the missing-alt problem above.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,738 of 13,860 characters (70%) are URLs, which is a deliverability signal. That is mostly the tracking-URL length. The plain-text part is not link-lean.
- **Layout and robustness:** `role="presentation"` is present on the layout tables, and MSO conditional comments and `mso-hide` are used correctly. The `Montserrat` Google Fonts `<link>` is stripped by many clients and has no fallback beyond the inline stacks (this is minor).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **PII in URLs:** The recipient email address appears in plain text in the query strings of `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `svopn.potterybarn.com/?…sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. These are `http`/third-party-visible requests, so the address is in server logs and unencrypted for the `http://` pixel. Hashing or opaque IDs would be safer.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are missing (RFC 8058 one-click). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed** and not a verified sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require these for large senders, so check the original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay and can't be assessed here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not present in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed. The footer is built from `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`. If the unsubscribe and address text are baked into the images, which have no alt, they would be inaccessible with images off. This needs verification against the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All visible CTAs use opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` redirect tokens, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment **cannot be verified** from the source or the QA data. Product images point to `potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which only confirms the asset host.
- No issues can be confirmed in this category.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images, especially the free-shipping banner slices and the footer slices. Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Switch the three `http://` image sources to `https://`. This includes the `svopn` open pixel.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Stop passing the plain-text recipient email in `coreemail.html` and `sv_sveme`. Use a hashed or opaque identifier.
6. Host `spacer_0.gif` on the brand's own CDN rather than `edm.westelm.com`.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC against the raw message, bypassing the AgentMail relay. Also confirm the unsubscribe link and physical address exist as live text in the full HTML.
8. Re-run the audit with the untruncated HTML and resolve the redirect chains offline to check UTM parameters and landing-page destinations.
