- The red 72-HR CLEAROUT banner is a recycled hero. I saw "72 hours only" on 11/09 and "Less than TWO DAYS LEFT" on 11/10. The urgency isn't credible when the clock never runs out.
- The rug is one of four tiles, and then pergolas take over. Eight or nine pergola and gazebo shots fill the middle of the email. That's a fourth or fifth pergola send this week, and I don't have a patio project.
- No product detail on the rug itself: no price, no material, no pile height, no shipping window. For furniture and rugs I need delivery timing and cost, and the email gives me neither.
- The footer fine print is huge and dense, and the "up to 60% off" hero doesn't say which items it covers.
- Wayfair doesn't show a swatch or sample program, no color-palette guidance and no showroom content. Crate & Barrel and Pottery Barn at least lean on design services and a "Recommended Just For You" grid. Here there's no sign the sender knows me.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch send with all links on `wayfair.com` and a single OneLink app-deep-link. The main confirmed defects are about 20 non-HTTPS `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and no `lang` on `<html>`. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified rather than confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Of 12 probed links, every failure was a 429 (`/daily-sales`, `/`, `/furniture/sb0/armoires-wardrobes-c45981.html`, `/daily-sales/filters/must-have-5%27-x-8%27-area-rugs-se68106.html`, `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`). These are bot rate-limiting responses, so the status of those links is unknown, not failed.
- **Coverage gap:** 59 links were skipped and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget ran out. These include `/wayfair-rewards`, the `gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100` filter, `kitchen-mats-under-%2430` and `on-sale-now%3a-pergolas`. The "57% pass rate" is therefore not a reliable link-health figure.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=d74c7b15-…`) load over plain `http://`. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, which under-reports impressions. They also have no `alt`.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is the `eventType=EmailOpen` beacon and has no `alt`. It is HTTPS, so there is no transport issue.
- **Per-link unique IDs:** each link has its own `_eml` value, such as `_eml=85b0010b-…` versus `_eml=441c6455-…` on the same OneLink CTA. This looks like intentional per-link click tracking, but it makes the URLs non-cacheable and non-deduplicable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text** on at least 24 images (the scanner reports each of these as missing `alt`):
  - Content images: `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`, `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`, `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`, `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24…`.
  - Four `wfus_jt800_batch10_bedroom_armoires_smallspaces_la…` images, one of which is linked.
  - Rating images `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` (8 occurrences). The star rating has no text equivalent, so screen-reader and image-blocked users lose it.
  - Several of these are linked images. The scanner's link text for them is `[image: <filename>]`, which means the link has no accessible name. Examples are the hero linking to `/daily-sales` and the header image linking to `/`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`. This affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Source truncated:** `<title>`, the preheader, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and the footer could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` appear in the visible source or the QA data. Recipient and campaign identifiers (`_emr`, `_eml`, `mdlid`, `maiid`) are resolved to concrete values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool itself notes this may be a relay artifact, so this is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements need both headers, and Wayfair's sender domain would be expected to have them. Check the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` cannot be assessed from this data.
- **Footer:** the physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source or the QA output. The tool states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for one appears. This is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters.** Links use a proprietary scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121690`, `emlid=101` and `maiid=16712`. This is consistent across links, but a standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is absent. Third-party analytics that rely on UTMs will classify the traffic as direct or unattributed unless Wayfair maps these parameters server-side.
- **Subject-to-landing alignment:** the subject's "5' x 8' Area Rugs" matches the CTA "Must-Have 5' x 8' Area Rugs", which goes to `/daily-sales/filters/must-have-5%27-x-8%27-area-rugs-se68106.html?eid=413331`. The apostrophes are correctly percent-encoded.
- **Module mismatch:** the header image `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple` (`sltid=5`, `mdlid=9614f7ff-…`) links to the homepage `/`. The text CTA in the same module, with the same `mdlid`, links to the rug event, so the image click lands on the homepage instead.
- **App link:** the OneLink URL carries `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and `af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. The web fallback is `http://` rather than HTTPS, though it will likely redirect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to every content image, including the `stars_*.png` images. Use a text equivalent such as "4.5 out of 5 stars". Use `alt=""` on decorative images and tracking pixels, and give linked images alt text that describes the destination.
2. Move the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, or confirm they are redundant with the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open beacon.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links, plus the 429'd ones with a throttled or retried request. Raise the probe budget so the pass rate reflects actual link health.
6. Point the header-image link at the rug event URL so it matches the text CTA, or confirm the homepage destination is intended.
7. Decide whether to add `utm_*` parameters or document the server-side mapping of `source`, `batchid` and `refid` for cross-tool attribution.
