# THIS BEST SELLING BED
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. I've lost count, but it's easily the seventeenth send in about ten days. The subject line says "best selling bed," and the hero delivers a moody, dark, cinematic Davison bed by Studio ANANSI under a "MORNING MUSE" banner. It looks good. I still stopped at the hero because there's no price, no percentage, and no deadline anywhere near the top. It's a mood board with a button.
- Further down the email turns into a grab bag. There's a bedroom edit (nightstand, lamp, bedding), then the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event at up to 25% off, then a Reward Dollars block with a 4% back promo, then another lighting hero, then a "More for You" product grid. That's five emails in one. I've already seen the upholstery event twice this week ("WE RARELY DO THIS" on 10/06, "IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE." on 10/04). Seeing it as a mid-email afterthought makes the "rarely" framing look even sillier.
- Would I click? Probably the Davison bed, since I have a primary bedroom and a second-home guest room in play. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder instead of buying today. I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read, file the bed.

## What's working

- The dark, moody Davison hero is the best creative CB2 has sent me this week. It's cohesive and it looks like a room I'd live in.
- The bedroom story is coherent: bed, nightstand, lamp, bedding, then "SHOP ALL BEDROOM." That's how I actually plan a room.
- The Reward Dollars block spells out the math: 10% back at CB2 and Crate & Barrel, 4% at home and garden, 1% everywhere else, no annual fee. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I read every line.
- The product grid shows real prices with the markdowns struck through, for example the Fiji velvet swivel chair at $879.
- The big "SHOP THE DAVISON COLLECTION" buttons are easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- The hero has no price and no offer. "Best selling" is a claim, not a reason to click today.
- The upholstery event is recycled. I've seen it twice this week. At "up to 25%" it's also a much weaker number than the "up to 60%" Long Weekend Sale from 10/07.
- The email is far too long. Wallpaper, bedroom, upholstery, rewards, lighting, grid, app, and gift registry all fight for attention. Pottery Barn's "Fall Sale is ON, up to 70% off" opened with one number and held that line. This doesn't.
- Lighting appears twice, in the bedroom section and again as "NEW & BEST SELLING LIGHTING." That's the same-lamp-everywhere annoyance I mentioned, inside a single email.
- The legal and fine-print block is tiny and grey and I can't read it. The 4% back promo ends December 31 but is barely flagged, and no shipping or lead-time info appears anywhere for a bed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer in the hero, such as a price or "free white-glove delivery on the Davison." Even the bed's price, on the first screen, would help.
- 2. Cut the email to one story. Make it the bedroom, drop the repeated lighting block, and move upholstery and rewards to their own sends.
- 3. Add swatches or sample links for the Davison upholstery and a delivery window. As a two-house renovator I need to know when it arrives.
- 4. Stop re-running the upholstery event as new news. Give it one clear send and a deadline.
- 5. Make the legal text readable, or shorten it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Davison bed: our #1 seller, now in walnut`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The bedroom edit: bed, nightstand, lamp`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ships in 2 weeks. Free swatches on every Davison finish.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Build the whole room: bed, nightstand, lamp, bedding.`

# Technical Audit: CB2 "THIS BEST SELLING BED"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also has widespread missing alt text and 11 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The HTML source is truncated and 74 links were not probed, so link, footer and unsubscribe checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **11 tracking pixels use plain `http://`:**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - Clients that proxy images over HTTPS or block mixed content may drop these. Gmail and Apple Mail privacy proxying are the likely cases. The cookie-sync calls would then fail silently.
- **Many third-party pixels.** There are 10 Dotomi cookie-redirect calls, plus `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/3f042043-...`. This adds image requests and third-party exposure. I can't tell from the evidence whether it affects deliverability or load time.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 content images** hosted at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `8829d162-…jpg`
  - `1b940baa-…jpg`
  - `ace2ae49-…jpg`
  - `dc0ab560-…jpg`
  - `d8f3c695-…jpg`
  - `d7298fcf-…jpg`
  - `12f46e36-…jpg`
  - `d3573149-…jpg`
  - `aae42603-…jpg`
  - `89537ff7-…jpg`
  - `d4ac8a16-…jpg`
  
  With images off, or in a screen reader, the content will be blank or read as filenames. This is the most significant accessibility issue found.
- **Tracking pixels with no `alt`.** These are `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, the 10 `pub*.php` images and `UCMController`. They should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta is `content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1"`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Leftover template comment.** The source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It isn't visible to recipients, but it indicates an unfilled template placeholder.
- **Malformed font URLs.** Several `@font-face` URLs have a double slash, such as `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/...`. Some `@font-face` rules also point at `crateandbarrel.com`. Either is a latent failure point if the host rewrites or blocks the path.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, MSO conditional blocks, and a media query at 418px are all present. I found no structural issues in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is in the rendered output.** The `%%var%%` syntax looks like Salesforce Marketing Cloud personalization-string syntax. It was not resolved at send time, probably because a dynamic reviews or SKU block failed to populate. Recipients may see literal text, or an empty or broken reviews module, depending on where it sits in the DOM.
- The `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` parameters on `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com` appear to be populated. They look like Movable Ink dynamic-content calls, though that is my inference from the domain and parameter names.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header: not found.** **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found.** **`Authentication-Results`: not found.**
- The QA tool attributes all three to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers. These are unconfirmed, not confirmed failures. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify these in the raw `.eml` or at the sending ESP.
- The visible HTML did not show the footer, so I could not verify the physical postal address or the unsubscribe link.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not assessable.** The 74 links were skipped and the truncated HTML doesn't show href values, so I can't confirm UTM parameters, landing-page alignment or SKU-level deep links.
- The subject refers to a specific bed, and the unresolved reviews token suggests a product-level module. Verify that the hero CTA lands on the matching PDP.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` resolution**, or add a fallback so the module is suppressed when the data is missing. Confirm there are no other unresolved tokens in the full source.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the 11 content images**, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://`**, or confirm with the vendor that http endpoints are intentional. Consider whether all 10 cookie-sync calls are needed.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
5. **Check the raw message** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment) outside the AgentMail relay.
6. **Re-run the link and UTM checks** against the full HTML with tracking-domain resolution, to cover section 6.
7. **Clean up the template**: delete the `IMPUT HERE` comment and the `//` in the font URLs.
