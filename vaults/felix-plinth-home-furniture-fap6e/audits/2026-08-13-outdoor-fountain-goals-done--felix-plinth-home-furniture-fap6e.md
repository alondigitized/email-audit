- The hero fountain + hose reel photo actually matches the subject line — rare for this brand's recent sends.
- "get 5% rewards on every item, always" is a clean, honest loyalty callout with a clear icon and no fine-print maze visible.
- Star ratings show up consistently on the "your home, these picks" grid, which is a trust cue Pottery Barn's vase email skipped entirely.
- The 70% off banner near the bottom is at least bold and legible, even if it's buried.

## What's weak

- The "outdoor" theme evaporates after two tiles — air fryers, wall art, bed skirts, china cabinets have zero connection to a fountain or an outdoor project.
- No price, no percentage off, no timer anywhere near the hero — I have to scroll past six sections to find any actual discount number.
- This is architecturally identical to "AREA RUGS up to 70% OFF" and the privacy-screens email from the same week — same purple banner blocks, same "shop before they're gone" section, just reskinned. It's fatiguing.
- Nothing here reflects that I'm mid-renovation on two houses — no swatches, no room-package bundling, no "complete the look" tied to the fountain itself.

- 1. Keep the whole email built around the fountain/outdoor water-feature theme — related pieces (planters, landscape lighting, pond pumps, patio furniture) instead of pivoting to air fryers and wall art by section three. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Move the 70% off banner and rewards callout up near the hero — right now the real offer is buried below eight rows of scroll.
- 3. Add a price or percent-off directly under the fountain hero photo so I don't have to hunt.
- 4. Cut the redundant category grids — I don't need "shop before they're gone" and "your home, these picks" as two separate near-identical purple-banner sections in the same email.
