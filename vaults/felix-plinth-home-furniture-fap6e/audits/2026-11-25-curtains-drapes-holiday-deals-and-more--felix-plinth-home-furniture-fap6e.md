## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with mobile media queries. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The substantive findings are widespread missing `alt` text and about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 links were probed and 70 skipped. The 429 responses on `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157…`, `/daily-sales`, `wayfair.com/` and the product links are rate limiting from the prober, not defects.
- **4 links were not probed** because of the 70s time budget, so they are neither pass nor fail:
  - `wayfair-product-L505-K~id-BV41098627.html`
  - `wayfair-product-L509-K~id-BY25010277.html`
  - the `Charbroil-Medallion-Series-2.7-Cubic-Feet…` product link
  - `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html?eid=635985`
- **Campaign labeling mismatch on the app link.** It carries `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=3`, so a batch send is labeled as a triggered campaign in AppsFlyer. This is inferred from the URL parameters.
- **Per-link `_eml` tokens.** Each link has a unique `_eml` value, for example `_eml=58e16ccb…` and `_eml=48a4ec74…` on the two "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links. This is expected per-link tracking. It does mean click data can't be aggregated by URL without stripping the token.
- **Tracking pixels.** There is one `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` open pixel. There are also 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` impression pixels (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images.** All 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=1241260xx` pixels are flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Gmail's image proxy and some clients will block or rewrite them, so ad-impression counts will be under-reported. Because they are HTTP, they may also trigger mixed-content or security warnings in some clients.
- **Missing alt text on content images.** These are the real accessibility failures:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`, which is also wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales`. The link has no accessible name beyond the filename.
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-EverythingShips…`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`
- **Star-rating images.** 11 `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` images have no alt text. They sit inside product links (for example `wayfair-product-L6260-K~id-BL74011295.html`), so rating information and the link's accessible name are lost to screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** The `b.php` and `imp` pixels have no `alt`. This is low impact if they are 1×1 or hidden. Setting `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Image blocking.** The banners are image-only with no alt text. If images are blocked, the promotional content disappears.
- **No other defects confirmed.** The source shows `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules, a Gmail-specific `u + .Gmail` selector, an MSO `mso-font-alt` fallback for the Sofia webfont, and an Outlook app fix. The truncated source doesn't allow further checks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{ }}` or `%%` appear in the visible source or QA output. The `_emr` and `_eml` parameters are populated. The body past the truncation point wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverifiable.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA tool states these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so they are not confirmed defects.
- **Sender.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain. Its alignment can't be checked without headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no unsubscribe result is listed. A physical address in the footer is not confirmed in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Links use proprietary parameters instead: `_emr`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_143963`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16794`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`. These are consistent across links, so first-party attribution is intact. Standard analytics tools such as GA won't see utm-based campaign data from this email.
- **Landing pages.** Every probed link was rate limited, so landing-page behavior and redirect chains are unconfirmed. All hrefs target `wayfair.com`, and the product `mplid` and `sku` values are present.
- **Subject and landing alignment.** The subject mentions curtains and drapes. The probed top links point to `/daily-sales` and the homepage, with no curtain-specific URL among the probed set. This could not be fully assessed because 4 links were unprobed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all banner images, especially the linked ones such as `SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`. Give the star ratings alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Fix the `c=Triggered` label on the onelink URL so it matches `source=batch`.
4. Check the raw message headers, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links with a longer time budget or lower concurrency, and retry the 429 links with backoff.
