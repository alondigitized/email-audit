# Our biggest Black Friday sale is happening now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel family send I've gotten this week. My first thought was "didn't I just get this?" The red-ribbon hero says "biggest sale of the year, up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping." That's the same hero as the 11/27 "Our biggest sale of the year is HERE!" send, and the same sale as the earlier "Black Friday just got even BETTER!" one. The sender name is Crate & Barrel **Kids**, which tells me this one isn't built for me. I'm renovating two houses, not nurseries.
- The body is a long column of category tiles, each with a "Black Friday" tag and a Shop Now button. Furniture, toys, bedding, play furniture, rugs, baby gear, decor/storage/lighting, holiday, Babyletto. The decor/storage/lighting tile with the floor lamp and the rug tile are the only things I'd glance at. The rest is toys, stockings and a baby high chair. I'd skim, maybe save the lamp, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.
- One caveat: the render came through very small (680x12000 scaled down), so the fine print and the preheader aren't legible to me.

## What's working

- The "up to 50% off + free shipping" hero is one clear offer with a big red ribbon. The eye lands on it first.
- Each tile has a consistent "up to X% off [category]" headline and a Shop Now button, so the scan is easy.
- The lifestyle photography is good. The lamp tile, the rug and the holiday stockings look like Crate & Barrel.
- The TheDesignDesk block at the bottom is the one thing that speaks to a renovator: a design appointment CTA.

## What's weak

- It's the Kids sub-brand, so most of the tiles (toys, baby gear, Babyletto, play furniture) have nothing to do with me.
- It repeats the hero from this week's other C&B sends. Pottery Barn at least added a "TODAY ONLY DEALS" block to its refresh, and this one adds nothing new.
- There are ten-plus tiles at roughly equal weight and no hero product. It's a catalog dump.
- Every tile says "select styles" in tiny gray text, and I can't tell what's actually discounted. The "free shipping" line is buried near the bottom.
- There's no urgency or deadline I can read, and nothing about rewards or card math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by interest. Send me the decor, lighting and rug tiles, not baby gear. A Kids brand sending to a non-parent is a wasted send.
- 2. Lead with one hero product, like the floor lamp, with a real price and a real end time.
- 3. Cut the tile count to 4–5, and put the free-shipping terms and the deadline in the hero.
- 4. Move the TheDesignDesk and design-appointment block higher for people on the home/renovation track.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Free shipping + 50% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Floor lamps, rugs and storage, plus free shipping on everything.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditional comments and about 80 tracked links. The QA run flags 38 images with no alt text. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the relay did not capture the headers and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 80 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` beacons (LiveRamp/RapLeaf) are in the body, with a hashed identifier `s=dbe8da71…` in the query string. The same hash appears in the first-party pixel `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=dbe8da71…`. This is a per-recipient identifier exposed in image URLs and shared with a third party.
- **Open pixel:** `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/474990b1-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a tracking image with no alt attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 38 `<img>` elements are flagged. 30 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (`.jpg` and `.gif`) or `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. One is the `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026_1105_CBK_PersonalizedGiftShop_Seconda…` image. The remaining 7 are tracking pixels.
  - If these images carry the offers or CTAs, the message is unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader.
  - The 7 pixels should have `alt=""`, not be treated as content.
  - The QA output may be cut off at its last entry, so the count could be higher.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks zoom on some mobile clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and the `html`/`body` rules do the same on iOS. The `body` rule sets `100%`, but the later `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` overrides it.
- **Markup hygiene (minor):**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type tag.
  - Three `format-detection` metas are redundant.
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Title:** `<title>` is the generic "Crate & Barrel", not the subject or the Kids brand.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML was truncated, so unrendered tokens could not be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports both missing. It notes the AgentMail relay may have stripped them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a proven defect. If they are really absent, there is no RFC 8058 one-click support. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so alignment would need a separate check.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The links are all click-tracking redirects and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 31 content images. That covers the 30 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` plus the scene7 `PersonalizedGiftShop` image. Set `alt=""` on the five `rlcdn` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` pixel.
2. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. This avoids acting on relay artifacts.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
4. Resolve a sample of the 80 redirect links with a HEAD/GET follow, to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
5. Clean up the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, drop the duplicate metas, and delete the placeholder comment.
6. Review whether sharing the hashed recipient ID with `rlcdn.com` is covered by the sender's privacy disclosures.
