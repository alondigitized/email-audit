## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run found no broken links and no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The warnings are mostly missing alt text and 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting), which is a probe artifact and not a defect. They are inconclusive, not failed. Examples:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`
  - the product links under `/Primed-Pantry-Door-Slab-...`, `/24-Inch-20-Bottle-...` and `/Challenger-Hathaway-Games-Shuffleboard-Table-...`
- **4 links were never probed** because the 70s time budget ran out, so their status is unknown: `L306-K~id-AC70038374`, `L3763-K~id-BP10012263`, `L59-K~id-BR13008642` and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`.
- **HTTP link in the skinny banner.** The banner link uses `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` while the other CTAs use `https://`. It depends on a redirect to HTTPS, which adds a hop and exposes the first request unencrypted.
- **HTTP fallback in the app link.** The OneLink URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback is also non-HTTPS.
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (open tracking) is HTTPS. 20 impression pixels from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` (`sz=2x1`) are plain HTTP. Clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading may block them, which would undercount impressions.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 6 banner and promo images have no alt text, and the QA output falls back to the filename as the link text.
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-EndsTomorrow.jpg`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-EverythingShips...`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`
- **The first banner is a linked image.** Its link text resolves to `[image: WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-EndsT...]`, so with images blocked or a screen reader the link has no meaningful label.
- **Star-rating images have no alt text.** 13 `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` images (`review_stars/`) are missing it, so the rating is not conveyed to screen readers. Those product links are labelled `[image: stars_10.png]`.
- **Tracking pixels have no alt text.** `b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels lack alt, which is low impact because they are non-visual. An empty `alt=""` is the usual convention.
- **Responsive CSS looks sound in the visible portion.** It has `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules, an `mso-font-alt` fallback for the Sofia web font, and a Gmail `u + .Gmail` hack. The truncated HTML prevents any further rendering review.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible data shows no unresolved merge tokens. Per-recipient identifiers (`_emr`, `_eml`) are populated in every URL.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may be stripping them, so this is unverified, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders of this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be assessed. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`. A relay limitation is the likely cause.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical address were not verified.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the results, and the footer is cut off in the supplied HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTM parameters are used.** Tracking runs on Wayfair's own parameters:
  - `source=batch`
  - `batchid=3`
  - `varid=7`
  - `csnid=e15faabd-...`
  - `refid=MKTEML_145351`
  - `emlid=101`
  - `maiid=16818`
  - `wfcs=cs7`
  - `brcid=1`
  - `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid`
- This is consistent across all links. Because there is no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`, third-party analytics tools may classify the traffic as direct or unattributed.
- **The OneLink app link** uses `pid=Email`, `c=Triggered`, `af_sub5=AppEmail` and `af_dp=wayfairapp://home`. The `c=Triggered` value on a `source=batch` send is probably a campaign-labelling inconsistency, which can skew AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Landing pages could not be checked.** The landing-page status of the 8 rate-limited links and the 4 unprobed links is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change the skinny-banner link and the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 6 banner images, and `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"` or similar to the 13 star images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Confirm that the sending path adds `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Check the raw message headers outside the AgentMail relay.
4. Check the raw headers for `Authentication-Results` and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed URLs with throttling or a longer time budget to confirm they resolve.
6. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that Wayfair's own parameters are mapped in analytics. Review `c=Triggered` on the batch send.
7. For the QA tool: pull the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and physical address can be verified.
