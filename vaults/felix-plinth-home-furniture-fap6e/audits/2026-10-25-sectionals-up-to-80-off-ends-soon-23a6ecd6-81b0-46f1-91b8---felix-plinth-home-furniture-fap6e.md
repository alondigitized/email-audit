## What's weak

- The sectional isn't priced. It has stars and a "Sale" tag but no was/now, which is the one item the subject promised.
- The mix is random: a trash can, a ping pong table, a vent cover and a bar fridge. It reads like an algorithm, not a design edit. Crate & Barrel's recipe email at least had a theme.
- There are too many CTAs: eight or more category bars, a doorbuster banner, an Everstein banner, "shop all sales," and pergola and wall art tiles. Nothing is the one thing to click.
- "Up to 80% off" is on a banner and in the subject, but I can't tell which sectionals qualify. The asterisk on shipping adds doubt ("Some exclusions apply").
- Nothing here helps a two-house renovation: no swatches, no color coordination, no rewards math, no showroom tie-in. The same pergolas from the 10/25 send are back at the bottom.

- 1. Make the email actually about sectionals. Lead with 4–6 sectionals, each with a was/now price, a fabric or color name and a delivery estimate. Cut the trash can and the ping pong table.
- 2. Show a clear deadline. "Ends soon" with hourglass emojis isn't credible. Put the actual end time and a countdown in the hero.
- 3. Collapse the 8 category bars into one row of 3–4. Keep one primary button: "Shop sectionals."
- 4. Add a furniture-specific trust line: free shipping, white-glove delivery, a 30-day return window, and fabric swatches.
- 5. Stop repeating the same pergola and wall art tiles across sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no unresolved merge tokens and no hard QA failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The real defects are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, widespread missing alt text, a non-HTTPS CTA link, and a missing `lang` attribute. The header-based compliance checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Non-HTTPS hero link:** the hero image CTA points to `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`. Every other CTA uses `https://`. The link depends on a server redirect to reach HTTPS.
- **Non-HTTPS impression pixels:** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=…` images. Clients may block or proxy them, so impression counts are likely undercounted.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (`eventType=EmailOpen`) is HTTPS but has no alt text.
- **App deep link:** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/...` carries `c=Triggered`, but the same URL has `source=batch&batchid=1`. A batch promo is labeled as a triggered send, which would skew AppsFlyer campaign attribution.
- **Probe coverage:**
  - The 429s on `wayfair.com` and `onelink.me` are rate limiting from the probe. They don't show broken links.
  - 4 links were not probed (time budget): `.../wayfair-product-L6085-K~W111312161.html`, `Charbroil-Medallion-Series...`, `Samsung-28-cu.-ft.-4Door...` and `/daily-sales/wall-art-%26-wallpaper-clearance~e403402.htm`. Their status is unknown.
  - Only 12 of 77 links were probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - Hero GIF `US-WD26-Launch-Hero-Email-Desktop.gif`.
  - Three skinny banners (`WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-*`).
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_...`.
  - 12 instances of `stars_10.png` (the rating image).
  - The open pixel and all 20 `imp` pixels.
- **Unnamed links:** the hero and the product links use only a non-alt image as link content, so the QA tool read their link text as `[image: stars_10.png]`. A screen reader has no accessible name for them. With images blocked, the hero CTA shows nothing.
- **Hero GIF:** if the hero is the sale message, Outlook desktop shows only the first frame. Whether the first frame carries the offer can't be confirmed from the source.
- **Missing `lang`:** the root `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="...">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Decorative pixels:** the tracking pixels should use `alt=""`, ideally with `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]` tokens. The per-recipient parameters (`_emr`, `_eml`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`) are populated in every link sampled.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so this is unconfirmed and not a verified sender defect. It needs a check against raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** the QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown for it. The HTML provided is truncated before the footer, so I can't verify the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters:** there are no `utm_*` parameters on any sampled link. Attribution relies on proprietary parameters: `refid=MKTEML_142295`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16606`, `csnid=…`, `wfcs=cs7`, `varid=13`. This is consistent across links, so it is not broken, but tools that expect UTMs will see the traffic as direct.
- **Generic landing page:** the subject line says "SECTIONALS … UP TO 80% OFF", but the hero and "Shop Sale" CTAs both go to the generic `/daily-sales`, not a sectionals category page.
- **Off-topic product links:** some product links are unrelated to sectionals (Charbroil grill, Samsung refrigerator, wall-art clearance). The lines connecting the subject to the destination can't be verified from the truncated HTML.
- **Protocol downgrade:** the hero link uses `http://`, so the first request is unencrypted before any redirect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the hero link and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to `https://`.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the hero, banners and product images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels, and give the star-rating image a value such as "4.5 out of 5 stars".
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Correct the `c=Triggered` campaign value on the app link for batch sends.
5. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` if downstream analytics needs them, or document `refid` as the canonical key.
6. Link the sectionals CTAs to a sectionals category page so the destination matches the subject.
7. Re-run the audit with raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-probe the 4 unchecked links with throttling or retries to get past the 429s.
