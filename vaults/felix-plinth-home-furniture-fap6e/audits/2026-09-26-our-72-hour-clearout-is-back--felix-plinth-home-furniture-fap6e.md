#  📅 🔔 Our 72-HOUR CLEAROUT is BACK 🔔 📅
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Wayfair's back with another "72-HR CLEAROUT" and my first reaction is déjà vu — I feel like I just closed one of these. The top of the email is actually doing real work for me: curtains, flooring, bedding, area rugs all stacked with clean percentage-off tags, which hits my actual renovation list for both houses. But then I hit the "picked just for you" section and it's the same broken-image problem I flagged on "Hall trees" a couple days ago — there's a big blank white gap with just a tiny broken-image icon sitting in it, right where a hero product shot should be. That's now three or four sends in a row with this bug.
- I'd scroll past the clearout, tap through to "bedding" or "flooring" since those are real needs right now, but I wouldn't forward this to anyone — it's just Wayfair being Wayfair, loud and category-dumping. Compare that to Pottery Barn's "Our Most-Loved Towels" email, which felt like it was actually curated for a person mid-renovation; this one feels like it's throwing the entire catalog at the wall.
- Verdict: I'd probably click through on one category tile (bedding, most likely, since I'm actively shopping that), mark as read, and move on. Not forwarding, not saving to the Pinterest folder.

## What's working

- The four category tiles up top (curtains 55%, flooring 40%, bedding 60%, rugs 60%) are specific, scannable, and actually match my renovation categories.
- Klarna 0% APR up to 24 months callout is genuinely useful for me — I'm buying furniture for two houses, financing math matters, and this is clearly labeled with a real term (10/12/2026 deadline).
- Star ratings under the "picked just for you" tiles are a nice trust cue, even with the broken images around them.
- Category breadth toward the bottom (kitchen & dining, pet essentials, storage & organization) means there's a decent chance something in here is relevant to whichever house I'm actively working on that week.

## What's weak

- That blank white block with the tiny broken-image icon in the middle of the email — same bug I called out on "Hall trees" (4.00/10) and the "ALL-WEATHER DECK BOXES" send (3.00/10, broken hero same problem). This is now a pattern, not a one-off.
- "Picked just for you" is doing a lot of lifting on personalization claims but the mix (patio fence, dresser, ottoman, blue locker) doesn't read as picked for me specifically — it reads like a generic best-sellers grid.
- The email is exhausting to scroll — I counted something like 14 separate category promo blocks. CB2's "Restock alert" (6/10) had the same category-dump problem, but at least CB2 kept it to eight categories in one clean stack instead of sprawling across the whole length of this thing.
- No sense of urgency reinforcement after the top banner — "72-HR CLEAROUT" is in the hero once and then never mentioned again, so by the time I'm scrolling through pet essentials I've forgotten there's a clock running.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image render before this goes out again — this is the fourth Wayfair email in a row with a blank/broken hero image. That's a bigger lift on trust than any copy tweak could fix.
- 2. Cut the email in half. Lead with my top 4-6 categories (curtains, flooring, bedding, rugs, home office, outdoor) and drop the long tail (pet essentials, surplus sale) into a "see more deals" link instead of full tiles.
- 3. Repeat the 72-hour countdown at least once mid-email — a small "ends in 47 hours" banner before the second half would keep urgency alive instead of front-loading it once and forgetting it.
- 4. Make "picked just for you" actually feel picked — pull from my renovation categories (flooring, bedding, entryway) instead of a generic best-seller grid with a patio fence and a blue locker.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 0% APR financing on furniture through 10/12`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
This is a Rebuild-templated (Unbounce/uEditor `.u-*` classes) Wayfair marketing email using tracked redirect links, pixel-based tracking, and standard responsive/Outlook CSS hacks; QA scan shows a 57% pass rate driven mainly by missing alt text and unauthenticated compliance headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed material links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_140866`, `maiid=16310`) — consistent, no mismatches found.
- 8 of 12 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by Wayfair's server during probing), including the primary CTAs "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" (×2), "Shop Sale", "Wayfair" logo link, hero image link, and 3 clearout category links. These are inconclusive (server-side rate limiting, not necessarily broken) but could not be verified as functional.
- 4 links were unprobed due to a 70s scan time budget: area rug clearout, and 3 PDP links (Zephyr Presrv, Hashtag Home bistro set, Birch Lane vanity) — status unknown.
- 20 tracking/impression pixels to `li.wayfair.com/imp` (sequential IDs `s=124126000`–`124126019`) all use `http://` instead of `https://`, which mixed-content-blocking mail clients may drop, silently losing open/impression attribution for those pixel slots.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 22+ `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including the primary hero (`72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`), the open-tracking beacon (`b.php`), multiple secondary banner images (`WFUS_0929_BigFurnitureSale...`, `-WFUS_260511_MemorialDay...`), and repeated star-rating images (`stars_10.png`, `stars_11.png` ×~10). Screen readers will announce nothing or filenames for these.
- Two `<img>` tags have empty `src` attributes (blank `src: `), which can trigger broken-image icons or, in worse cases, redundant requests to the current page URL.
- CSS uses standard Outlook/Gmail/mobile hacks (`u-MobileHide`, `u-OutlookAppStackingFix`, `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout: none)` for iOS Mail image fixes) — no issues found in the visible markup.
