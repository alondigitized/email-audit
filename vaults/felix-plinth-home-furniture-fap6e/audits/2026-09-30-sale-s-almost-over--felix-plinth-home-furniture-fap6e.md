# SALE’S ALMOST OVER
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. Fourth send in three days, and I knew what it was before I opened it. This is the fifth or sixth version of the same sale. "Last Chance, Up to 50% Off" sits over a blurry out-of-focus flower photo with no furniture in it. Under that is a stack of six full-width buttons. Further down is the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event at 25% off, which is the same module I got in this morning's "The Holiday Collection is here" send.
- It does the job as a last-call reminder. It's clearly a sale, it clearly ends, and the rewards card is in there. But it's a category menu, not a pitch. I have two houses to furnish, and nothing here tells me which sofa or rug to care about. I'd skim it and move on. I wouldn't forward it or save it to my Pinterest folder. It's a reminder, not inspiration.
- Other brands handle this better. Pottery Barn's Big Daddy's Antiques collab at least had a story and a face. Crate & Barrel's "host like a pro" email was a magazine spread I could save. Article's bedroom refresh gave me a room to copy. CB2 gave me a flower and a list of departments.

## What's working

- "Last Chance" plus "Up to 50% off" is big and readable, and the urgency is clear at a glance.
- The upholstery module is the strongest part. A moody green velvet sofa with "Up to 25% off" and a "Shop the sale" button is what I'd expect from CB2.
- "Order 10 free swatches" is the most useful thing in the email. Fabric is how I actually decide on a sofa.
- The 10% back in Rewards Dollars at CB2 and Crate & Barrel speaks to how I buy. I put everything on the highest-rewards card, and I'd do the rewards math on it.
- The category buttons (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs) are easy to tap with a thumb, and the departments I'd shop are all there.

## What's weak

- The hero shows a blurry flower and no product. "Up to 50% off" of what? I can't see a single item.
- Six identical stacked buttons push the upholstery module far down the page. That's a menu, not a hierarchy.
- The deal is split. The hero says 50% and the sofa module says 25%. The fine print (visible only as tiny text at the bottom) says the 50% covers "select" furniture, lighting and more. That reads like a bait-and-switch.
- The upholstery module is recycled from this morning's send, and the "SALE ENDS TOMORROW" send on 9/29 said the same thing. This is the third "ending" notice in two days. My trust in CB2's deadlines is gone.
- No deadline date appears anywhere above the fine print. "Last chance" is unverifiable. The date ("Ends September 30") is down in the footnote.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the flower with a real product. Show a specific sofa or a lit room with a price, like "Was $X, now $Y." A hero I can shop beats a hero I have to interpret.
- 2. Cut the six buttons to two or three, and put the upholstery module directly under the hero. Or use one button, "Shop the sale," plus a line saying "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM CT."
- 3. Put the real deadline in the hero, not in the footnote.
- 4. Move the swatch offer up. It's the best conversion tool in the email and it's hidden near the bottom.
- 5. Do a "last call" send with a short list of best sellers, not a category directory. Stop sending four versions of the same sale in three days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off CB2 furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: sofas 25% off, 10 free swatches`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends 11:59 PM CT. Upholstery, lighting, rugs. Plus 10 free fabric swatches.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tonight only: earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars at CB2 and Crate & Barrel.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token in the delivered HTML. It also has widespread missing alt text and 10 non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels. The header-level compliance and authentication warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not real failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **10 Dotomi pixels load over plain `http://`.** The URLs are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`. They are third-party cookie-sync calls. Clients that proxy images over HTTPS, or that flag mixed content, will block or fail them, so the sync is unreliable. They also add 11 extra third-party requests on open.
- **Two first-party pixels are present.** These are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/f288a3e3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are HTTPS, but neither has alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Nine content images have no `alt` attribute.** All are hosted on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `4509ae92-…jpg`
  - `6a13aff7-…jpg`
  - `b0947991-…jpg`
  - `6ee98185-…jpg`
  - `66580753-…jpg`
  - `7a2e60b0-…jpg`
  - `a9a1429f-…jpg`
  - `89537ff7-…jpg`
  - `badb0ec1-…jpg`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, there is no fallback text. Some of these may be functional or promotional images carrying the sale message.
- **The 13 tracking pixels also have no `alt`.** They are the 11 Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel. These should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom.** This is an accessibility problem on iOS and Android clients that honor it.
- **A template placeholder is still in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned up.
- **Font URLs are inconsistent.** Some `@font-face` sources use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/…` with a double slash. Several load from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`, a different domain from the sender.
- **I can't assess the body markup.** The HTML was truncated, so table structure, MSO fallbacks for buttons and mobile behavior past the `<style>` blocks are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears in the HTML.** This `%%…%%` syntax is an ESP personalization string (Salesforce Marketing Cloud style) that was never resolved.
  - A recipient could see it as literal text, or it could break the layout or links where it sits. I can't tell which from the truncated source.
  - The likely cause is a reviews or SKU-list content block that failed to populate at send time.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip or not capture these headers. That makes this inconclusive, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support, so check it against a direct-delivery copy.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be determined from this capture, for the same relay reason.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed.** They would be in the truncated footer. Nothing in the available data shows a problem.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found in the available data. The 63 click-tracked links were not resolved, and the visible source doesn't show final URLs or UTM parameters. Destination alignment and campaign tagging can't be assessed from this capture.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` block.** Check the data extension or dynamic content feeding it. Add a fallback so the token can never be delivered raw, and add a pre-send check that scans for `%%…%%`.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the nine content images.** Add `alt=""` to all 13 pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or remove them.** If the partner lacks HTTPS endpoints, the `http://` pixels give little value and add privacy exposure.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag, and delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
5. **Re-verify compliance on a direct-delivery test send.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com`. Don't count the relay-related warnings as confirmed failures.
6. **Follow the tracked links through to their landing pages** to check UTM tagging and destination health. The probe skipped them.
