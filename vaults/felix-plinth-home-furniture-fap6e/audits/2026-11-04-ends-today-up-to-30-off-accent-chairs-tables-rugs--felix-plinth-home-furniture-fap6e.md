# ENDS TODAY: Up to 30% off accent chairs, tables & rugs!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was "another one." This is the eleventh or so Crate & Barrel family send since 11/1, after the Ooni pizza email and that ice cream maker one that never showed me an ice cream maker. The sender name says "SALE," so I knew what I was getting. But this is actually in my lane: accent chairs, tables and rugs. For two houses mid-renovation, that's the right category, and the best one they've sent me this week.
- The hero is a Brighton wool ivory rug with the price shown ($2,070.25, down from $3,099). Underneath is a 2x2 grid of a table, a rounded boucle-looking chair and a rug. It's clean and I get the offer in two seconds. Then the email runs on forever: holiday decor, a Black Friday signup, two store-opening blocks for La Cantera and Corte Madera, a card promo, an app download and a design desk pitch. Eight modules for one "ends today" message.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on the rug or the chair, to check the sale price. Would I forward it? No. The next-door Crate & Barrel Kids email isn't relevant to me, and this one only matters if I'm shopping this week.

## What's working

- The hero names the category and shows a real product with a real sale price, Brighton Wool Ivory Rug at $2,070.25 against $3,099. That's the Pinterest-folder candidate I'd save.
- The "Shop The Sale" button sits right under the headline in a dark maroon pill, so there's no hunting for it.
- The accent chair, accent table and rug tiles are exactly what I'm shopping for.
- The "ends today" urgency in the subject lines up with the sale copy, so it feels real.
- The "quick ship furniture" tile speaks to my actual pain point, which is how long furniture takes to arrive.
- The product row near the bottom shows prices, so I can compare without clicking.

## What's weak

- The offer is muddled. The headline says "up to 30% off" but the rug tile says "up to 60% off rugs." Which number is it? That reads like fine print doing a bait-and-switch.
- This is the eleventh send in four days. "Ends today" loses credibility when the brand mails me every few hours.
- The back half is a pile-on of the holiday collection, Black Friday signup, two store openings, the card, the app and The Design Desk. None of it supports the one-day sale.
- The store-opening blocks (La Cantera, Corte Madera) are irrelevant to me unless I happen to live there. They eat two screens' worth of scroll.
- The product row at the bottom has small text, and the sale prices are hard to read at phone size.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Keep the hero, the three category tiles, the quick-ship tile and one product row. Move the store openings, the card and the app to a different send.
- 2. Pick one number. If rugs are 60% off, lead with that, because it's the strongest offer.
- 3. Add a countdown or an explicit "ends 11:59 PM PT" near the button to make the urgency credible.
- 4. Put the rewards math up front. Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel both lose me on card perks, so show me "10% back in Crate Dollars" next to the offer, not buried at the bottom.
- 5. Give me swatches or free shipping. I'm comparing fabrics across three catalogs, and a swatch CTA would beat "Shop All Sale."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs up to 60% off, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: chairs, tables & rugs on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brighton wool rug now $2,070. Quick-ship chairs included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends at midnight. Free swatches on select upholstery.`
- Comparisons:
- Pottery Barn's "New arrivals with timeless holiday charm" was just a lookbook with one clear idea. That's easier to read than this multi-module email.
- CB2's "HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RESTOCK + 2 Sales Ending Soon" also stacked two jobs in one subject, but at least the restock hero was a single idea.
- Wayfair's Holiday Flash Sale is loud, but it keeps one offer on screen. Crate & Barrel is trying to say six things.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard MI (Marigold/Selligent-style) table layout. The QA run found 0 hard failures, 3 warnings and a 57% pass rate. The main confirmed problems are about 24 content images without alt text, a viewport that blocks zoom, and an unverifiable header and unsubscribe posture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 63 tracking links went through click-redirect domains and were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and expiry are untested. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e…&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times. Each has no alt text, and the five requests add load.
- **Recipient hash in third-party URL:** the `s=` value on rlcdn.com starts with the same hash as `mi_u=dbe8da718e…` in the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs. This suggests the same recipient identifier is passed to a third party (LiveRamp). Confirm that is intended and disclosed.
- **Other trackers:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (open pixel) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a7934bb4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are both present.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (about 24):**
  - 16 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (jpg and gif, e.g. `bbf6f9d4-….png`, `cd56cdc7-….gif`).
  - 1 image on `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F1011%5FCB%5FHolidayDecorating%5FSecondary`.
  - 7 images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, which look like dynamic product-recommendation images.
  - With images blocked, or under a screen reader, these show nothing or the filename. If the CTAs are image-only, those links have no accessible name.
- **Tracking pixels without alt (7):** these should use `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. The warning is low severity.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1 />` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid markup (minor):** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. `format-detection` is declared four times (once in combined form, then `date`, `address` and `telephone` separately).
- **Template leftover (minor):** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in the production source.
- **Good:** `lang="en"`, the MSO conditional blocks (`o:PixelsPerInch`), `color-scheme` meta and a responsive breakpoint at 460px are all present.
- **Not assessed:** the source was truncated, so layout beyond the `<head>` could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. Per-recipient values appear only as the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` URL parameters, which look correctly rendered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** the QA tool says the AgentMail relay may be stripping it. This is inconclusive, not a confirmed failure.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN):** the same limitation applies. RFC 8058 one-click support (required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders) can't be verified here.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so check alignment on the raw message.
- **Footer not verified:** the truncated source doesn't show the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address. Neither can be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No evidence of landing-page continuity or UTMs:** all 63 links were skipped, so none were followed to their destinations. UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) can't be assessed because the links are click-redirect wrappers.
- **Time-sensitive subject:** the subject line says "ENDS TODAY: Up to 30% off". Whether the landing pages show the same offer is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 24 content images, especially any that carry a CTA or the offer. Give the 7 tracking pixels `alt=""`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Check the raw message headers outside the relay for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
4. Resolve a sample of the 63 redirect links manually or with a redirect-following probe. Confirm landing-page status, UTMs and that the offer matches the subject.
5. Confirm the `rlcdn.com` recipient-hash sharing is intentional and disclosed.
6. Clean up the template: escape `&` as `&amp;` in the title, drop the duplicate `format-detection` tags, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
7. For this QA tool, retain raw headers from the relay, or fetch the headers separately, so the header checks stop returning inconclusive warnings.
