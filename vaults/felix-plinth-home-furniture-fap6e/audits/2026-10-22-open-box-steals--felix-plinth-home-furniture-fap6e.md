# Open Box steals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- The images loaded this time, and that's about the best I can say. After the blank heroes on 10/21 and 10/22 ("Best-selling pergolas"), a big lavender "open box deals" wordmark with a Dutch oven in the "o" is a relief. It looks designed. But then I scroll and it's eleven identical purple bars with no product photos. It's a text menu with a brand logo on top.
- I'm renovating two houses, so end tables, lighting, rugs, mirrors and vanities are all on my list. Open-box is a decent angle for me. But I can't tell what's actually discounted. "Up to 70% off" on lighting could be one sconce. The hero is a Dutch oven, which has nothing to do with any of those categories. I'd skim this and move on. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd probably only click if I was already shopping for a bar stool.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and on-brand. The "open box deals" wordmark reads in about a second, and the purple is consistent with Wayfair's 10/21 Way Day email.
- The category list matches my renovation needs: lighting, end tables, mirrors, vanities, rugs.
- It has a clear "Shop All Sales" bar at the bottom, and the Rewards and Financing icons are there if I want them.
- No render bugs this time. Nothing is broken or blank, unlike the last two sends.

## What's weak

- There isn't a single product photo below the hero. Every row is a purple bar, so I can't judge style, finish or size. Article's swatch-and-tablet hero showed me something. This shows me a menu.
- The hero's one visual is a Dutch oven. It doesn't match any category in the list.
- The offers are all "up to" numbers, from 45% to 70%, with no anchor item. There's no price, no savings badge and no hero SKU. Wayfair's own "Your pergola search is over!" email had a $745 vs $879 badge, and that one actually made me look.
- There's no explanation of what "open box" means. I don't know the condition, the return policy, or whether I get free shipping on bulky furniture. For a bar stool or vanity, that's what I'd need to know.
- The red arrow under the hero is the only hero CTA, and it's small. The rest is a stack of identical-weight bars with no priority.
- It's the ninth Wayfair email this week and the third time Wayfair has swapped its creative approach. The cadence is exhausting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put 2–3 real open-box products in the hero with the original price, the open-box price and a condition tag, like Pottery Barn's sale blast does with a strong hero offer. One concrete $ off beats eleven vague "up to" lines.
- 2. Replace the text bars with a tile grid of photos, one tile per category with a real product. Keep the percentage as a badge.
- 3. Add one line on open-box condition, return policy and shipping. I'd also lead with Wayfair Rewards math, since I buy on the highest-rewards card.
- 4. Make the hero image match the offer. Use a lamp or a stool instead of the Dutch oven.
- 5. Cut the category list to the top 4–5 and send the rest through "Shop All Sales."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Like-new for less: open-box deals ending soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Inspected, discounted, and still covered by Wayfair returns. See today's open-box finds.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `End tables up to 65% off, lighting up to 70%. Open-box stock goes fast.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules and per-link tracking parameters. The confirmed technical issues are 20 plain-HTTP impression pixels, missing `alt` attributes, and an unverifiable compliance and authentication posture. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and body markup could not be fully inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** All 7 flagged links returned 429 (rate limited), which means the probe was throttled, not that the links are dead. Affected URLs include `wayfair.com/daily-sales`, `wayfair.com/daily-sales/clearance`, and `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`.
- **Coverage gap:** 12 links were probed and 23 were skipped. Four more links were unprobed when the 70s budget ran out. Those are the `daily-sales/clearance/filters-masterClID~1227`, `~2243`, `~6260`, and `~442` URLs. Their status is unknown.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixels:** 20 `<img>` tags point to `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=29ad8e36-…`. Clients or proxies that block or upgrade non-HTTPS images can drop these, which would undercount impressions. The `p=` value is the same `_emr` ID used in the click URLs.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3Blbi…` is HTTPS, but it has no `alt`.
- **Per-link tracking IDs:** Each link carries a unique `_eml` value, for example `c87afe23…` on "Shop Sale" and `7bf20182…` on "Wayfair". The "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" link appears twice with different `_eml` values (`85b0010b…` and `441c6455…`). That is consistent with per-slot tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked image with no accessible name:** `wfus-openbox-2025-minibahn-02.gif` has no `alt` and is the sole content of the link to `/daily-sales/clearance?...sltid=3`. The QA link text for it is `[image: wfus-openbox-2025-minibahn-02.gif]`. Screen readers and images-off clients get no label, so this is the main accessibility defect.
- **Second content image without `alt`:** `new_shop_all_sale_2024.jpg` also lacks `alt`.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** The 20 `imp` pixels and `b.php` have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` or the `2x1` dimensions with `style="display:block"`.
- **Responsive CSS:** The visible CSS looks sound. It includes `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` for Gmail and an Outlook app stacking fix. The CSS was truncated, so full rendering was not verified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The `_emr` and `_eml` parameters are populated with UUIDs, and no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the supplied data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed defects:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. `Authentication-Results` was also missing, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so they may be present at the sender.
- **Sender identity:** `From: "Wayfair" <editor@members.wayfair.com>` is a consistent subdomain of the brand domain.
- **Footer not inspected:** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion of the HTML. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it was reported. It could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** The links use Wayfair's own scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_141511`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16539`, `mdlid`, `wfcs=cs7`, `pid=Email`. This is internally consistent, but it won't register in a standard UTM-based analytics setup.
- **Landing alignment:** Destinations are consistent with the subject "Open Box steals". The hero image goes to `/daily-sales/clearance`, and the category links go to filtered clearance pages (`masterClID~1221`, `~167-masterClID~197`, `~1318`). Whether those pages actually show open-box inventory could not be checked because the pages returned 429.
- **App deep link:** The `onelink.me` link sets `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and falls back to `af_web_dp=…/the-wayfair-app`. Its `c=Triggered` campaign label doesn't match a batch send (`source=graymatter_batch`). This is a possible attribution inconsistency, not a break.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a descriptive `alt` to `wfus-openbox-2025-minibahn-02.gif`, for example "Shop open box clearance". This is the highest-priority fix, since it is the only accessible name for that link.
2. Add `alt` to `new_shop_all_sale_2024.jpg`, and add `alt=""` to the `b.php` and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels.
3. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
4. Re-probe the 4 unprobed `filters-masterClID~…` URLs and the 429'd links with throttling or backoff. Check the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
5. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message at the receiving mailbox, since the relay may be stripping them.
6. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that the `graymatter` and `refid` scheme is mapped in analytics. Check whether `c=Triggered` on the app link is intended for a batch send.
