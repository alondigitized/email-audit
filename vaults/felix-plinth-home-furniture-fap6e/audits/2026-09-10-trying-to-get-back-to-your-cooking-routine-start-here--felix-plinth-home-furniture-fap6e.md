# Trying to get back to your cooking routine? Start here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's actually for me. Kitchen appliances, blenders, cookware sets — this is the "renovating the kitchen" wave I've actually been shopping in, unlike the fourth Crate & Kids nursery blast I've had to scroll past today. The "make your meal prep easier" hero with the Vitamix-style blender is a legit hook, and the Caraway cookware section right under it ("only at Crate: a calming new color from Caraway") is smart — that's a brand collab I'd actually stop and look at since Caraway's been all over my Pinterest-adjacent folder already.
- That said, this is a LONG scroll. Blender, then cookware, then more cookware ("GreenPan Originals"), then another blender shot, then a toaster oven, then a Ninja CREAMi, then "even more new arrivals," then a bed, then a mixer, then the app pitch. By the time I hit the fifth "Shop Now" button I've lost the thread on what this email actually wants me to do. It's less "here's one offer" and more "here's our whole kitchen catalog, good luck."
- I'd click on the Caraway section specifically — that's the one genuinely new thing here (an exclusive colorway I haven't seen at Crate & Barrel, CB2, or Pottery Barn). I wouldn't forward it to a friend since there's no urgency or deal to share, but I'd probably screenshot the Caraway pans into my inbox folder and move on.

## What's working

- The blender hero photo is clean and appetite-appealing — smoothies actually look cold and fresh, not stock-photo fake.
- "Only at Crate: a calming new color from Caraway" gives me a reason this is Crate & Barrel-specific and not just generic cookware filler — exclusivity beats CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" email that just felt like a warehouse dump.
- The GreenPan "cleaner cooking originals" section has real product callouts (Reserve Nonstick Ceramic Pan, Crepe Pan) instead of vague "shop our collection" copy.
- "Ships free" language near the countertop/coffee section is the kind of concrete shipping clarity I actually look for before I click on furniture-adjacent appliances.

## What's weak

- No actual discount anywhere. Compare that to CB2's fall sale email that scored an 8 with me because the offer was front and center — this is just merchandising, no price incentive, so my highest-rewards-card instinct never kicks in.
- Way too many product sections stacked vertically with near-identical "Shop Now" buttons — by the Ninja CREAMi section I've already mentally checked out, and there's still a bed and a stand mixer after that, which don't belong in a "cooking routine" email at all.
- The bed showing up mid-kitchen-email is a hard swerve — feels like they just tacked on "we love these for you" cross-sell filler that has nothing to do with the subject line's promise.
- The app pitch at the bottom ("It's all on the app, including your chance at $500") buries what's actually a real incentive under six other sections — that should've been near the top, not the last thing before the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an actual offer or the $500 app sweepstakes up top — right now the strongest incentive in the whole email is buried at the very bottom where nobody's still reading.
- 2. Cut this down to 3 sections max: blender, Caraway cookware, one more hero. Right now it's trying to be a full catalog instead of a "cooking routine" story, and I lose interest by section four.
- 3. Drop the bed and mixer cross-sell entirely, or move them to a separate "new arrivals" send — they undercut the cooking-routine focus of this email.
- 4. Add a swatch/color callout for the new Caraway colorway by name (not just "a calming new color") — I want to know if it's sage, cream, whatever, before I click through.
