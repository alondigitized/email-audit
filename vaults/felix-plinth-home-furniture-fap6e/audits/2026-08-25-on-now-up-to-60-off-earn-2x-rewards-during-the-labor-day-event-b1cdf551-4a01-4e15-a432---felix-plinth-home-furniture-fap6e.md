# 🚨On now: Up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards during The Labor Day Event!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Third Crate & Kids email in my inbox this week, and I still don't have kids. This is the same nursery-furniture, kids-bedding, toddler-bed catalog I already saw in "Growth spurt? Get upgrades fast" and "The best of the best for their bedroom," just re-skinned with a Labor Day banner slapped on top. The 20% Rewards Dollars offer and 60% off headline are legible enough, but every single hero image after that — cribs, bassinets, changing tables, a baby swing — is for a life stage I'm nowhere near. I'm buying dining tables and lighting for two houses, not a nursery glider.
- Meanwhile Crate & Barrel proper sent me an actual Labor Day sale email today too ("STARTS NOW! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event + 2x Rewards!") that at least stacked sofas, dining, lighting, and rugs — stuff I can use. This Crate & Kids version is the same promo math wearing a onesie. I'm marking as read, not clicking, definitely not forwarding — unless it's to a friend who just had a baby, and even then I'd rather just tell them "Crate & Barrel's doing 60% off + double rewards right now" verbally than forward this specific send.

## What's working

- The "Double Rewards + Up to 60% off" banner up top is at least legible and matches what Crate & Barrel's main brand is running today, so the sitewide event math is consistent across their family of brands.
- Section headers ("50% off kids & baby furniture," "60% off bedding + ships free," "60% off rugs," "60% off decor & lighting") are clean and scannable if you're actually in the market.
- The nursery/kids room styling is genuinely well-shot — soft blues, warm woods, the kind of color-coordinated room-story photography Pottery Barn's Halloween email did well with too.
- Free shipping called out clearly on bedding, which removes friction for the shopper this is actually built for.

## What's weak

- Every hero image is a crib, bassinet, changing table, or infant swing — zero crossover to my actual renovation (dining, seating, lighting, outdoor).
- This is the third near-identical Crate & Kids send this week, and cadence fatigue is real — CB2 at least varied its "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" creative from Crate & Barrel's parent-brand spam, this doesn't.
- No swatch or fabric-sample program visible anywhere, no color-palette continuity tool for matching a piece across rooms — something I'd actually want as a two-house renovator.
- The "we love these for you" personalized module at the bottom is still baby gear (stroller, glider, crib mattress) — so whatever personalization engine picked those clearly hasn't clocked that I've never clicked a kids product.
- Long, repetitive scroll — module after module of the same 50-60% off framing with no variation in offer structure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress me from Crate & Kids sends entirely, or at minimum stop cross-selling kids furniture to accounts with zero kids-category browsing/purchase history — that's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. If Crate & Barrel wants to cross-promote sister brands during Labor Day, do it as one module inside the main Crate & Barrel sale email, not three standalone Crate & Kids sends in a week.
- 3. Add a real swatch/sample ordering CTA for the furniture and rugs modules — CB2 and Article are both doing better here.
- 4. Vary offer creative between sends instead of repeating the same "up to X% off [category]" block six times in one email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Labor Day: 2x Rewards on nursery must-haves`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off cribs, bedding & kids furniture — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Double Rewards Dollars on every kids category, now through Monday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New parents: this is the sale to wait for`
