# ⭐ OUR BLACK FRIDAY POP-UP STARTS NOW ⭐
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again. This time the images loaded, which is a relief after the blank hero on "Area rugs – prices are ⬇️ ⬇️ ⬇️" yesterday and the run of broken heroes before it. The banner is loud and clear: "Black Friday Pop-Up Sale, up to 70% off." Then the email loses me. Six pergola and gazebo photos under "picked just for you," then a second block of six more pergolas after the Affirm strip. I'm renovating two houses, and I have not shopped for a pergola.
- The category tiles are the useful part. Area rugs at 70% off and bedding, mattresses and mirrors at 50-55% are all things I'd actually browse. They sit above a wall of outdoor structures, though, and nothing in the email tells me the "just for you" claim is real. Would I click? Probably one tile, the area rugs. Would I forward it? No. I'd save it only if I were already building a rug list.

## What's working

- The "Black Friday Pop-Up Sale, up to 70% off" banner is the first thing my eye lands on, and the offer is specific.
- The two-by-two tile grid (rugs, bedding, wall art, mattresses) is scannable. Each tile has a big percentage and a labeled button.
- "Everything ships free*" is a good line for furniture, where freight costs are my biggest worry.
- The Affirm strip shows 0% APR for up to 36 months. For big-ticket furniture that's useful, even though I pay with my highest-rewards card.
- The flash deals section has a "24 hrs only" label, and the Black Friday strip says "Starts now." Both give some credible urgency.

## What's weak

- "Picked just for you" is twelve near-identical pergolas and gazebos, and none of them has a price. That's the same Wayfair pergola loop I've been getting for days.
- There's no color, material or style guidance anywhere. For a two-house renovation I'd want swatches or room-based curation, and this has none.
- The tiles show only a percentage and a category. Nothing says which rugs or what size, and nothing is a hero product I could commit to.
- Wayfair Rewards is buried in the footer, so I can't see any member math.
- The email is very long. The fine print (the Affirm terms, the "exclusions apply" notes) is tiny and hard to read on a phone.
- The tile discounts read "up to," so I can't tell what a typical rug would actually cost.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the pergola blocks to one short row, or drop them. Put something from my own browsing history there (rugs, lighting, bedding) with actual prices.
- 2. Lead with one hero product or room under the 70% banner instead of five category tiles.
- 3. Show a rewards or member price, plus a clear end date for the pop-up sale.
- 4. Make the fine print readable, and state the free-shipping exclusions up front for large items.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on most orders, plus 24-hour flash deals on decor.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday pricing on rugs, bedding and wall art. Ends Nov. 16.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with responsive media queries and no unrendered merge tokens in the visible source. The real defects are missing alt text on content images (hero, banners, 12 star-rating images) and 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The 429 responses and missing auth/unsubscribe headers are probably probe or relay artifacts, not email defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 probed links returned 429, including `/daily-sales`, `/daily-sales/area-rug-sale~e408298.html`, `/bedding-sale~e408302.html` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me` app links. A 429 means the server rate-limited the probe, so these are unverified rather than failed.
- **4 links were never probed** because the 70s budget ran out. They are the `mattresses-%26-more-on-sale~e408328.html` page and 3 pergola product URLs (`-Aluminum-Pergola-…`, `Aurora-10%E2%80%98-X-12-Louvered-Pergola…`, `All-Weather-Pergola-With-Curtains…`). The QA output cut these URLs short, so I can't tell whether the source is also truncated. The Aurora URL has an encoded curly quote (`%E2%80%98`) in the path, which is valid encoding but fragile if the product slug changes.
- **Hero link has no accessible name.** The `/daily-sales/` link's text resolves to `[image: WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale-Skinny]` because the image has no alt.
- **Tracking pixel at `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…`** is the open pixel (`eventType=EmailOpen`). It is HTTPS and has no alt.
- **20 impression pixels use plain HTTP.** They run from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000&sz=2x1…` through `s=124126019`, all with the same `p=7a67e927-…` ID. Clients that block or upgrade mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions. They also have no alt.
- **The `af_web_dp` fallback in the onelink URL is `http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.** It is plain HTTP, so desktop users go through an extra redirect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images with no alt text:**
  - `WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale-SkinnyHero-Gener…` (hero)
  - `…Skinny-FlashDeal`
  - `…Skinny_Affirm.jpg`
  - `…Skinny-FreeShipp…`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`

  If images are blocked or a screen reader is used, the offers, financing and shipping messages are lost.
- **12 rating images have no alt** (`stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` under `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`). The rating is conveyed only visually.
- **The 21 tracking pixels also lack alt.** Use `alt=""` on these so they are explicitly decorative.
- **`.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; … height: 48px }` in the 480px media query** can split product names mid-word on mobile, and the fixed height can clip long names.
- The `@font-face` for "Sofia" has `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, so there is an Outlook fallback. The visible portion shows no other rendering defects. The source is truncated, so the body markup could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or link URLs. Per-recipient IDs (`_emr=7a67e927-…`, per-link `_eml=…`) resolved correctly. I could not check the greeting or body copy because the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Re-check against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is expected for the relay and is not an email defect.
- The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears, and it isn't in the truncated HTML. I can't confirm that an in-body unsubscribe link or a physical postal address is present.
- The From address `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain of the sender's domain, which is fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses Wayfair's own scheme instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=1`, `varid=8`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `refid=MKTEML_142737`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16659`, `wfcs=cs7`. These are consistent across all links, so this is a gap only if the analytics tooling needs UTMs.
- **Inconsistent trailing slash.** "Shop Sale" links to `/daily-sales?…` and the hero links to `/daily-sales/?…`. This probably redirects to the same page, but it was not verified.
- **Landing page alignment is unverified.** Every probed destination returned 429, so I couldn't check that the page content matches the "Black Friday Pop-Up" offer.
- **The app link uses a deep link** (`af_dp=wayfairapp://home`) with a web fallback. This is correctly built, apart from the `http://` fallback noted above.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, banners and star images, for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited links and 4 unprobed links with lower concurrency or a longer budget, and confirm the 4 truncated product URLs against the raw HTML.
4. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, an in-body unsubscribe link and a postal address. Treat the QA warnings as unconfirmed until then.
5. Review `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name`. `overflow-wrap: anywhere` or `break-word` would avoid mid-word splits.
6. Treat the 57% QA pass rate as inflated by relay artifacts and 429s. The actionable items are the alt text and HTTP pixels.
