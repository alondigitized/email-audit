# First turkey, then shopping (at up to 50% off!) →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids again, and I've now gotten several C&B-family sends in a row. My snooze on that family is still engaged. The top banner says "Don't miss out: 686,267 people are shopping Black Friday now →". That's a weird number to flash, and it reads as manufactured urgency. Under it, the hero is "happy thanksgiving, from our home to yours" over a toddler on a floor with a dollhouse. It's a pretty photo, but it's a holiday greeting, not an offer.
- The real offer is further down: "Biggest sale of the year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping." Then comes a long grid of cribs, kids' furniture, stockings and a playroom rug. I'm renovating two houses, and nobody in either one needs a crib, a Nuna stroller or a baby activity chair. The Kids arm is the wrong arm for me. I'd mark this as read and move on. I wouldn't forward it. The only person I'd send it to is a friend with a newborn.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" headline is clear, and the red type stands out against all the white.
- The "Select styles. Exclusions apply. Orders $75+" line sits right under the offer, so the fine print isn't buried.
- Each grid tile has its own "up to X% off" label, so I can scan the discounts without clicking.
- The $2,700+ prize sweepstakes block and the Feeding America block add some variety. They also feel on-brand for Thanksgiving.
- The "we love these for you" product row shows actual prices, with strikethroughs, which I appreciate.

## What's weak

- The kids focus misses me completely. Cribs, strollers, stockings and baby bedding have nothing to do with my two renovations.
- The hero is a greeting card with no offer, and the 50% off headline doesn't show up until the second screen of scrolling.
- The "686,267 people are shopping" banner feels like fake scarcity, and it's the first thing I read.
- There are too many CTAs: Shop the Sale, Shop 300+ Gifts, Enter Now, Shop Kiwi Striped Glider, Shop the Sale again, Learn More, Download Now and Book a Free Appointment. No single one clearly leads.
- The Crate Card ad and the app download block push me toward things I didn't come here for, and the email feels long and cluttered because of them.
- Nothing about color matching, swatches or samples, and nothing on shipping times for furniture. The grid says "Ships Free", but there are no delivery windows.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. Make the first screen "Up to 50% off + free shipping" with a single button, and move the greeting to a small line above it.
- 2. Segment by household. If I've never bought kids' items, send me the main Crate & Barrel or Kids-adjacent furniture, not cribs and strollers. Pottery Barn did a better job of keeping its "Black Friday, up to 70% off" hero on a single clear offer, even though I've seen that creative since 11/16.
- 3. Cut the CTAs down to two or three. CB2 kept one oxblood hero with one message, and it was easier to act on.
- 4. Drop the live-shopper counter, or replace it with a real deadline like "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM".
- 5. Add furniture shipping windows next to the big pieces, and a swatch or sample link. Wayfair at least labels the exact product in its tiles, so I know what I'm clicking.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday at Crate & Barrel Kids: 50% off nursery & playroom`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $75+ orders. Cribs, bedding and playroom furniture, all marked down.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Sunday. Shop top-rated picks before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with Outlook/VML scaffolding and 82 redirect-wrapped links. The QA run passed 57% of checks, with 3 warnings and 0 errors. The only issues confirmed in the evidence are missing alt text on about 37 images and a few markup and accessibility problems in the head. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed as defects. The HTML source was truncated, so anything in the body or footer is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** QA skipped HTTP probes on all 82 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains. Link health, redirect targets and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are five LiveRamp sync pixels carrying a hashed subscriber identifier. They also lack alt text.
- **First-party pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/41c690cc-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are also present.
- **Repeated pixels:** the same recipient-hash URL repeats across the 6 `rp` pixels. This is likely redundant, but I can't tell from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (about 24):**
  - These are mostly `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`. Examples: `78b594c4-…jpg`, `4949d5f7-…jpg` and `bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - Two animated GIFs on `s7d5.scene7.com` are also affected: `2026_1126_CBK_HappyThanksgiving_03.gif` and `2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide_SecondaryStory.gif`.
  - The `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White` spacer is flagged too.
  - With images blocked or in a screen reader, these image blocks have no text equivalent. If the headline and offer text is baked into images, that content is lost.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (about 13):** These should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`. The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom in some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` prevent text resizing in iOS. The `*{… line-height:100%}` rule also applies globally.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" …>` are unclosed.
  - `format-detection` is declared 3 times with conflicting content.
  - The title is generic and does not reflect the Kids send or the subject.
- **Template leftover:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->` were left in production HTML. The comment is harmless but is a template-hygiene marker.
- **Not assessed:** I can't judge the dark-mode, VML or `.mobile{width:460px}` handling. The breakpoint is 460px, which is narrower than typical, but I can't confirm any actual rendering fault.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens were visible in the portion of HTML provided. Recipient identifiers (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `s=` hash) are embedded in image URLs and appear resolved. The remainder of the source was truncated, so I can't verify tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. QA notes these may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed, not a proven defect**. Bulk senders are expected to support one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements). Re-verify against raw headers at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment status is unknown. This is an expected relay artifact. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain that would need its own SPF and DKIM alignment.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.
- **Third-party pixel disclosure:** The LiveRamp pixels (`rlcdn.com`) are a privacy-policy consideration, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 82 links are redirect-wrapped and unprobed, and no final destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the visible source or QA output. The Scene7 GIF named `…2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide_SecondaryStory.gif` is an October asset reused in a November 26 send. This is an asset-naming observation only, and I can't confirm any content mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to the roughly 24 content images, starting with any that carry the offer text. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the roughly 13 tracking pixels and the spacer.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and drop the global `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. **Fix the head markup:**
   - Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it descriptive.
   - Close the meta tags.
   - Deduplicate the `format-detection` metas.
   - Strip the `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
4. **Verify raw headers at the origin** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't treat the relay-based warnings as findings until then.
5. **Expand tracking-link QA** to resolve one redirect hop per link, so the final landing URL and UTM parameters can be checked.
6. **Consolidate the six repeated `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/…` pixels** if they are not intentional, and confirm the five `rlcdn.com` pixels are covered by the sender's privacy disclosures.
