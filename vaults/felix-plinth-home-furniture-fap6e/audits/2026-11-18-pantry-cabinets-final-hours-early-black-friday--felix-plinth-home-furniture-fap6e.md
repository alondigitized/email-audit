## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with consistent per-recipient tracking parameters and no hard link failures. Its main code-level issues are 20 plaintext `http://` tracking pixels, many images without alt text (including image-only product links), and an `http://` web fallback in the app deep link. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** 12 links were probed and 8 returned 429 (rate limited). These are inconclusive, not failures. Affected links include:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...`
  - the two `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links
  - the `accent-pillows` sale link
  - the two product links (`L6382-K`, `L608-K`)
- **4 product links were never probed** because the 70s time budget ran out (`L6164-K~id-AB17066085`, Cosmo microwave drawer, Lifetime tetherball, 24" beverage refrigerator). 72 links were skipped as low priority, so most of the email is unverified.
- **Insecure fallback in the app link.** The OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Desktop and non-app users are sent over plain HTTP, which adds a redirect hop and a downgrade risk.
- **Attribution parameters conflict.** The same OneLink URL carries `c=Triggered&pid=Email`, while the rest of the email uses `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch promo labelled as a triggered campaign will skew campaign-level reporting.
- **Tracking domains are on the sender's own domain.** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` (open pixel) and `li.wayfair.com/imp` (impression pixels) are first-party and aligned, so there are no third-party redirect concerns.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels load over plaintext `http://`, from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, which undercounts impressions. The same 20 pixels also lack alt text.
- **Content images with no alt text:**
  - `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-EndsTonight...` (promo banner)
  - `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_Skinny-Financing-Affir...`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`
  - Without alt text, the message is lost when images are blocked.
- **Image-only product links have no accessible name.** The product anchors (e.g. `.../wayfair-product-L6382-K~id-QC74102670.html`) use `stars_10.png` or `stars_11.png` as their link content. Those images have no alt text, so screen readers announce a bare link. This repeats about 14 times.
- **Possible stale asset.** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner` is referenced in a November BFCM send. It has no alt text, and the truncated HTML doesn't show whether it is visible. Worth checking that it isn't an unintended leftover.
- **Template structure itself shows no defects.** It has the Gmail/Outlook-app fixes (`u + .Gmail`, `body[data-outlook-cycle]`), `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` for the Sofia web font, and `max-width: 480px` responsive rules.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA output. Per-recipient `_eml` values differ per link, which is expected.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. It needs checking on a raw message from a direct inbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain (`members.wayfair.com`) can't be assessed from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address are unconfirmed.** The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the output. The footer is beyond the truncated HTML, so the physical address can't be confirmed either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No standard `utm_*` parameters.** All links use Wayfair's proprietary set (`refid=MKTEML_143524`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16752`, `csnid`, `varid=7`, `batchid=3`, `skurectxid`). Attribution therefore depends on Wayfair's own analytics, and the email can't be tied to the same campaign in GA or other third-party tools.
- **Landing pages match their link text.**
  - "Shop Sale" and the banner go to `/daily-sales`.
  - The pillows link goes to `/daily-sales/accent-pillows...~e410010.html` with `eid=636769` and `unifiedeventid=636769`, so the two IDs agree.
  - Product links carry `sku` and `mplid` matching the product IDs.
- **The subject line is not reflected in the links.** The subject advertises "PANTRY CABINETS", but no probed or visible link targets a pantry-cabinet category or event. Most links go to generic `/daily-sales`. Landing pages weren't fetched, so this is evidence from URLs only.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`. Do the same for the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open pixel.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the banner and financing images. Add `alt=""` to decorative images. For the star-rating images, use something like `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`, and add a text label to the product links.
3. Change `af_web_dp` in the OneLink URLs to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. Reconcile `c=Triggered` with `source=batch`.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw headers received directly, not through the relay.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or backoff.
6. Confirm the Memorial Day banner is not rendering in this send.
7. Consider appending standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside the proprietary parameters, so campaigns can be attributed outside Wayfair's own tooling.
