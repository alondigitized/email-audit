## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a well-formed, table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with per-recipient tracked links, but it has two kinds of technical debt. About 20 open-tracking pixels are served over `http://`, and most images have no alt text, including the images that are the only content of a link. Several link targets could not be verified because the host returned 429 (rate limiting).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links (429).** Eight links returned 429 and cannot be called valid or broken: `wayfair.com/daily-sales`, `wayfair.com/`, three product URLs, and two `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links. 429 is consistent with bot throttling, so this is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Incomplete probing.** Only 12 of 85 links were probed, and 4 more were not checked after the 70s budget ran out. These include `/Nolan-74-Tall-Arched-Storage-Display-Cabinet-L170-K~id-A…` and `/daily-sales/area-rug-sale~e408698.html?eid=635911`.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=4ab3072c-…`. Clients or proxies that block mixed or insecure content can drop these, which undercounts impressions.
- **Open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3Blbi…` (base64 payload decodes to `mcch=ECM&eventType=EmailOpen…`) is on HTTPS, so it is fine apart from the missing alt.
- **Stale creative filenames.** Three banner assets carry old campaign names while this is a Nov 15 BFCM send (`WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_*`):
  - `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-…`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`

  These may be reused slots, but I can't confirm what they render because they have no alt text and the HTML is truncated. Check that they aren't showing outdated offers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA run flagged 12 `stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png` rating images and 5 banners. The banners are `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-General.jpg`, `…Skinny-AppEarlyAccess`, `…Skinny-Financing-Affirm`, `…SkinnyBanner`, and `0125_48hourclearance…`.
- **Image-only links with no accessible name.** The QA link labels show these as `[image: …]`, for example:
  - `[image: WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-Ge…]` → `/daily-sales`
  - `[image: stars_10.png]` → `wayfair-product-L442-K~id-QC81546290.html`
  - `[image: stars_11.png]` → Le Creuset casserole and Loon Peak butcher block

  Screen readers will read the filename or nothing, and with images blocked these CTAs have no fallback text.
- **Tracking images without alt.** `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels have no alt. This is low impact, but `alt=""` would be cleaner.
- **Mid-word wrapping on mobile.** `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; … height: 48px !important }` inside `@media (max-width: 480px)` breaks product names mid-word and clips them at a fixed height.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML is truncated, so `lang`, viewport meta, and the body structure could not be checked. The visible CSS (the `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` hooks, `mso-font-alt`, and the `max-width:480px` breakpoints) shows deliberate client handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the supplied source or link data. Each link has a unique `_eml=` ID, and `_emr=4ab3072c-aa69-4d8c-afdb-21036b882bc4` is constant across all links.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. That means this is unconfirmed, not a defect. Check the raw message headers, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be assessed through the relay.
- **Footer not visible.** The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated excerpt. The QA notes an unsubscribe link was always probed, and none appears among the failures.
- **Sender.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain, with no mismatch evident.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Proprietary tracking, no UTM.** Links carry `source=batch&batchid=3&varid=7&csnid=e15faabd-…&refid=MKTEML_143518&emlid=101&maiid=16748` and no `utm_*` parameters. This is fine if it is consistent internally, but it needs a mapping to GA-style reporting.
- **Campaign-label mismatch on the app link.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…&c=Triggered&…&source=batch&pid=Email`. The campaign is labeled `Triggered` while the same link says `source=batch`, so AppsFlyer attribution may be miscategorized.
- **Insecure web fallback.** The app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses HTTP. This is a minor point, since it will likely redirect to HTTPS, but it adds a redirect hop.
- **Landing targets.** `/daily-sales` and the product PDPs are consistent with a sitewide sale email. Landing-page content could not be verified because the probes were rate-limited.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch all `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS. Add `alt=""` to the pixels and `b.php`.
2. Give every content image meaningful alt text, and give image-only links an accessible name. Use `alt=""` for decorative star images or add rating text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars".
3. Confirm the three stale-named banner assets render the intended current offers.
4. Fix the `c=Triggered` versus `source=batch` mismatch in the OneLink URL, and move `af_web_dp` to HTTPS.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC from the raw headers (outside the relay), and confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with backoff or a longer budget.
7. Replace `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name` with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` or `break-word`, with ellipsis clamping.
