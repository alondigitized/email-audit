# Our Holiday Headquarters is OPEN
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Holiday Headquarters" — three shops stacked in one email (Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas) with actual seasonal styling behind each one, not just a logo slapped on a generic banner. This is a step up from the "Holiday Movie Magic" collab email Pottery Barn sent on 9/24 — that one felt like licensing filler; this one at least has real hero photography for each holiday tier.
- But three holidays in one scroll is a lot, and by the time I hit "Recommended Just For You" I'd already forgotten what the actual CTA was supposed to be. This reads like a category-index email, not an offer email — no percentage off, no free shipping line, nothing time-bound. Compare that to Crate & Barrel's "Quilts Ship Free" send on 9/23, which had one concrete hook I could act on immediately.
- Verdict: I'd skim it, maybe tap through to the Christmas shop since stockings are relevant to the second-house setup, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone and I'm not clicking with urgency. Marking as read.

## What's working

- The three-tier structure (Halloween → Thanksgiving → Christmas) is a smart way to acknowledge the whole holiday runway without pretending it's all one moment.
- Each shop has genuine seasonal lifestyle photography — the Thanksgiving tablescape and the mantel with stockings both look like real rooms, not stock renders.
- "Recommended Just For You" grid actually reflects stuff I'd browse — curtain rods, a swivel chair, a dining table, jute rug — this feels like retargeting off my actual browsing, not random SKUs.
- The rewards card callout ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") is relevant to me specifically since I buy on the highest-rewards card anyway.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete offer anywhere in the hero or shop sections — no %, no $, no free shipping, no deadline. It's all "shop now" with nothing behind it.
- Three holidays crammed into one email means none of them get to be the hero — my eye doesn't land anywhere specific, it just keeps scrolling.
- This is the same lamp-in-three-catalogs problem in miniature: I already got a Halloween-decor push and a Thanksgiving-tablescape push from Pottery Barn this week in separate emails (9/24 quilts, 9/23 quilts), and now they're all mashed back together here too. Feels like I'm getting the same inventory recycled across sends.
- "Design Crew — Free Design Services" and the Affirm/App Store blocks at the bottom are just clutter by that point — I'm two houses deep in renovation and a real design-desk pitch (like Crate & Barrel's "The Design Desk" email, which scored a 6 with me because the timing was right) would've landed better than this generic banner.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE holiday to hero per send and put an actual offer on it — the "three shops" approach dilutes urgency. If it has to stay three-in-one, at least give one of them a real percentage-off or ships-free hook like C&B's 9/23 send did.
- 2. Move "Recommended Just For You" up near the top — that's the most personalized, most relevant content in the whole email and it's buried after three generic hero shops.
- 3. Cut the Halloween section entirely for a guy renovating two houses for furniture/holiday decor — or at least segment it out for people who've bought seasonal Halloween decor before.
- 4. Turn the Design Crew banner into something with substance — a "book by [date] for holiday-ready rooms" hook would actually pull me in given my two-house timeline.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real hosting picks, no fine print — shop before it sells out`
