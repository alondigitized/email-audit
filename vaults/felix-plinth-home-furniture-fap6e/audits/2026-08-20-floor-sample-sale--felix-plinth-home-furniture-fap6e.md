- No percentage, no dollar amount, no date range beyond "limited time" — I have literally nothing to price-compare against my Pottery Barn or Wayfair carts.

- No indication of which categories are on floor sample (rugs? sofas? case goods?) or which stores near me even have inventory.
- Zero personalization — no rewards balance, no "items near you," nothing that says CB2 knows I've been buying from this family repeatedly this week.

- 3. Use store locator personalization — show me my nearest store's inventory highlights, not a generic locator link.
