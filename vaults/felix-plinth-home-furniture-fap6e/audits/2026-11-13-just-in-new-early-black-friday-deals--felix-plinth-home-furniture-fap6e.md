# Just in! NEW Early Black Friday deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Pottery Barn send in two days, and I felt it before I opened it. After "STARTS NOW 📣 Early Black Friday UP TO 70% OFF" and "100s of NEW Early Black Friday furniture deals" earlier today, "Just in! NEW" is the same email with a fresh label. The hero is the same maroon block, "up to 70% off", and a Shop Now button. The "NEW DEALS ADDED" line above it is the only thing that tells me anything changed.
- Below the hero there's a category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Rugs, Decor and so on) that I can actually shop from. The product picks are a mixed bag. The Cole Swivel at $874 down from $1,749 is a real furniture deal. The faux fur pillow and the Christmas duvet are filler. The "Recommended Just For You" block is just stockings, a curtain rod and a dining table, with no prices and no sign it knows I'm furnishing two houses.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the Cole Swivel, but I'd be going in from the Pinterest-adjacent folder, not from this email. I wouldn't forward it. Crate & Barrel already sent me six emails today, and I'm tired of being re-pitched the same sale.

## What's working

- The "up to 70% off" hero reads in about a second, and the Shop Now button is big and obvious.
- The category grid is useful for someone working through two houses room by room. Rugs, Curtains, Bedding and Decor are one tap each.
- The Cole Swivel at $874 vs $1,749 shows the math clearly. That's the one deal I'd actually look at.
- "SHIPS FREE" tags on the pillow and duvet matter to me. Shipping clarity is half the battle on furniture.
- The Design Crew sweepstakes module ($3,000 gift card) is a good tie-in. Free design help is useful to me, and it's a better pitch than another discount.

## What's weak

- It's the fifth send in two days with essentially the same hero. The 11/13 "100s of NEW Early Black Friday furniture deals" send looked almost identical, and "NEW" doesn't feel earned.
- "Recommended Just For You" has no prices and no offer. Stockings and a curtain rod aren't what I'm shopping for.
- The Early Black Friday banner shows "up to 70% off", but the best featured item is only about 50% off. The Christmas Shop banner says "up to 50% off". That feels like a bait-and-switch.
- The legal block is huge and tiny-type. The card rewards, Affirm and sweepstakes disclaimers pile up, and the "earn 10% back" card offer sits under them.
- It's very long. The rewards and card math I care about is buried near the bottom, while CB2 leads with "double rewards" at the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the cadence. One Black Friday send per day, and make this one a clear "what's new since this morning" digest with 3-4 named new items.
- 2. Put real prices on the "Recommended" block. Pull from furniture I've browsed (swivel chairs, dining tables) rather than stockings.
- 3. Move the 10% back in rewards up under the hero. I buy on the highest-rewards card, and Crate & Barrel is already leading with "Earn 2x Rewards".
- 4. Show one real hero product with a real discount instead of the generic "up to 70%".
- 5. Add delivery windows to the furniture items, since that decides whether I order.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on select items. Plus 10% back with the PB card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an SFMC-style, table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email built mostly from sliced images. Most images have no alt text, three assets load over plain `http://`, and the recipient address appears in plaintext in tracking URLs. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the relay did not capture the headers and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 64 tracking links on `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status are unconfirmed. The logo link is one example. Its `qs` token is opaque.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 12,626 of 17,652 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This points to long redirect URLs in the text alternative and can add spam-filter weight.
- **Three tracking beacons:** `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/d2a714ce-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` and `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`. All three lack alt text, which is acceptable for pixels. The `svopn` one is insecure, and `d.turn.com` is a third-party ad-tech domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text missing on about 60 images.** Examples:
  - `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2026/US/1113_FRI_PM_EBF_BannerOver6Grid_NewDealsAdded/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_19.jpg`
  - `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - Product images such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`
  - `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg`/`_02.jpg`

  Because the offer content is in these sliced images, the email shows blank space with images blocked and gives nothing to screen readers. The logo link does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Non-HTTPS images:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...`

  These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>`.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag** (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`) disables pinch-zoom on supporting clients.
- **Stray server-side code in the CSS.** The last `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...")`. This is invalid CSS, so parsers will discard it, but it is leftover code in the output.
- **`role="presentation"` is missing on some layout tables.** The spacer table (`<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`) and the outer wrapper tables don't have it. The `contenttable` tables do.
- **Possibly dead selector:** `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]` are targeted in the media query. No matching elements appear in the visible, truncated HTML, so I can't confirm they match anything.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens were observed in the visible HTML.
- **Plaintext email in URLs:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The second is sent over unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed in transit, and both can end up in server logs.
- **Referrer policy conflict:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set, but the pixels still carry the email in the query string.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `PotteryBarn@e.potterybarn.com` and the click domain is `click.e.potterybarn.com`, so they align on the subdomain, but alignment can't be verified without headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these were not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the extracted URLs. Tracking is carried by the opaque `qs=` token and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` (the `clickcp` pixel's Marigold/Sailthru-style IDs), so UTM parameters can't be assessed. Destination alignment is unverified, since all 64 redirect links were skipped.
- **Direct product image links:** product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. These are static assets, not click targets, and no landing pages could be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all sliced offer images, and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels (`spacer_0.gif`, `d.turn.com`, `clickcp`, `svopn`). Use `title` text and a live-text fallback for key offers.
2. Move `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel to HTTPS.
3. Stop putting the plaintext recipient email in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Check the raw MIME from the sending MTA, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
5. Shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part to bring URL content below 70%.
6. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and remove the stray `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
7. Re-run link validation using a follow-redirect probe on `click.e.potterybarn.com` URLs. This would let you confirm landing pages and UTM parameters.
