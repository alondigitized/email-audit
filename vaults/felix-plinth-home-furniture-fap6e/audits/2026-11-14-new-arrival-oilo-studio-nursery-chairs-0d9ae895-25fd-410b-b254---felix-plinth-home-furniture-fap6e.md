# New arrival: Oilo Studio nursery chairs! 🤍
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kids sender again, and it's the tenth Crate & Barrel family email since yesterday. I'm renovating two houses and I have no nursery. A mom holding a baby on a plaid recliner is a Pinterest save at best. It isn't something I'd buy. The email is long and has the usual C&B polish, but it's a nursery-chair launch for someone who isn't shopping for one.
- The subject sells the new Olio brand arriving at C&B Kids. The body is half that and half generic "Early Black Friday, up to 50% off, Double Rewards" filler. The Double Rewards banner is the only thing that speaks to how I shop. I'd earn on the card either way, but I already know about that promo from the nine sends before this one.
- I'd mark it read and not click. I wouldn't forward it either. If a friend had a baby coming, maybe. Pottery Barn Kids would get the same treatment, but at least PB doesn't send me ten of these a day.

## What's working

- The Double Rewards banner at the very top, plus "up to 50% off Early Black Friday," puts the offer above the hero. That's where I look first.
- Each chair gets its own block with a swatch row and a Shop Now button: the Flynn, Olie and Teddy recliners. That's the swatch-and-fabric info I care about when I'm matching a palette.
- The four-tile "up to 50% off" grid (furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs) is easy to scan, and the Shop the Sale button is clear.
- The "we love these for you" product row, with names and prices, is the most useful part of the email.
- The Design Desk "Book a Free Appointment" block is a real showroom-style service, and it's the one thing here I might remember.

## What's weak

- The email is about three screens of nursery chairs, and I have no nursery. Nothing in it is targeted to me.
- The Double Rewards message shows up twice, in the top banner and again as a mid-email block, and the deal itself isn't explained. That's repetition, not hierarchy.
- It's a long scroll for a launch email, and the footer is a wall of tiny legal text I can't read without zooming.
- The Feeding America block, the holiday travel gear, the new card and the app download all pull in different directions. I can't tell what the one ask is.
- The mom-and-baby imagery doesn't match a 36-year-old guy renovating two houses. It reads as a different customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment it. Send the Olio launch to people who browsed nursery or kids' furniture, and send me something else. A subscriber with zero kids-category activity shouldn't get this at all.
- 2. Cut it to one hero, one offer, one button. Lead with the Olio launch, put the Early Black Friday and Rewards message in a single strip, and drop the charity, travel gear, card and app blocks.
- 3. Show the rewards math. Something like "Earn 2X, here's what that's worth on a $1,500 order" would help the highest-rewards-card shopper.
- 4. Cap the cadence. Ten sends from one family in two days is why I stop opening.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 79 click-tracking links. The only QA warnings are missing alt text on about 40 `<img>` tags and header checks that could not be run. The HTML shown is truncated, so the footer, link targets and body markup are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **79 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped the HTTP check for these click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels fire on open.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a…&n=1` through `n=5` differ only in `n`. The long hex `s=` value looks like a hashed subscriber identifier, which I infer from its length and format. This is third-party syncing that adds requests and a privacy surface.
- **Six identical Movable Ink pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times, along with `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/6d4311cf-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are personalization and open-tracking calls.
- **Leftover template comment.** The source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, an unfilled template placeholder.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed).**
  - About 25 content images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (`.jpg`/`.gif`), plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`, have no alt text.
  - The pixels listed above are also flagged. Those need `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, not descriptive text.
  - The QA output does not say whether `alt` is absent or empty, so check that for the content images. Where alt is absent and the images are blocked, screen-reader and image-off users get nothing.
- **Zoom and text scaling blocked.**
  - The viewport meta tag has `maximum-scale=1`.
  - The CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust:none` on `*`. That prevents user zoom and text resizing.
- **Raw ampersand in `<title>`.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;`, since the doctype is XHTML.
- **Duplicate and redundant `<head>` tags.**
  - `meta charset` appears twice (once as `http-equiv`).
  - The `format-detection` meta tags are declared three times.
  - The `<meta charset>` tag is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype.
  - These are low-severity validity issues.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** Under `min-width:768px`, `img{max-width:600px}` is overridden by the later `min-width:640px` rule with `640px`. This is low risk but unintended.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear. Personalization looks like Movable Ink (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) and could not be inspected because the source is truncated.

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Treat them as unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unverified.** The visible-unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be checked because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 79 links go through tracking redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be confirmed. The only parameters visible are the Movable Ink `mi_*` ones on pixels and assets, not on destination links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit against the raw message with full headers (bypassing the relay) to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images. Give the tracking pixels `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`.
