- The hero is empty and takes up the whole first screen. I see no pergola, no offer and no CTA before I scroll.
- The subject promises best-selling pergolas, but most of the email is sinks, rugs, bar stools and filing cabinets. It reads like a catalog dump.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracking is consistent (per-recipient `_emr`/`_eml` tokens, first-party tracking domains, and a pergola CTA that matches its landing page). The confirmed problems are 20 impression pixels served over plain `http://` and missing alt text on every flagged image, including the linked banners. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** Of 61 links, 12 were probed and 8 returned HTTP 429. That is rate limiting from the probe, so those links are unverified rather than failed. Examples:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-pergolas--se82159.html?eid=603454&…`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?…sltid=2`
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…`
- **49 links were skipped and 4 timed out after 70s without a result.** These are the comforters, shoe racks, on-sale pergolas and rugs filter URLs. The QA output shows them cut off (`…se73165.ht`, `…se82161.htm`, `…se7`). That looks like truncation in the report, but it can't be confirmed from this data. Neither group counts as a pass.
- **Two app CTAs have identical text.** Both read "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" and point to the same OneLink URL, differing only in `_eml`. That is expected per-link tracking, not a defect.
- **The OneLink web fallback is not HTTPS.** It is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so it depends on a redirect to HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 impression pixels use `http://`.** They are `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Mail clients may block or proxy them as mixed content, which would undercount ad impressions. All 20 also lack `alt`.
- **The open-tracking pixel has no alt text.** It is `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` and uses HTTPS.
- **Four content images lack alt text, and two are inside links.** Link text for those two is reported as `[image: …]`, so screen readers get no accessible name:
  - `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty-…` (linked to `/daily-sales`)
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png` (linked to `/`)
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_2_purple.png`
  - `WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_SkinnyTop-Sale…`
- **One asset filename contains an apostrophe.** `LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-…` has a raw `'` in the `src` path, which risks encoding problems in some clients. The `0824` and "LaborDay" naming also suggests a reused asset, but that is inferred from the filename only.
- **Responsive CSS is present.** The `@media (max-width:480px)` block has `.u-FullWidth`, `.u-Block` and `u ~ div` Gmail fixes. The truncated source shows no defects.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the QA data. The source is truncated, so the footer and body copy were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified omission. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and postal address are unconfirmed.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for it. The footer is outside the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are on any link.** Tracking uses proprietary params instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121727`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. This only matters if downstream analytics expect UTM.
- **The OneLink campaign label is inconsistent.** It sets `c=Triggered` and `pid=Email`, while the same URL has `source=graymatter_batch`, which indicates a batch send. Attribution in AppsFlyer would label this as a triggered campaign.
- **The primary CTA matches the subject.** "Best-selling pergolas" goes to `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-pergolas--se82159.html?eid=603454…` with `unifiedeventid=603454`. Page content was not verified because the probe returned 429.
- **The header image links to the homepage.** The `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple` banner (`sltid=6`) goes to `https://www.wayfair.com/` with no landing context.
- **A recipient identifier is in every URL.** `_emr=94daf24f-…` and `p=94daf24f-…` on the impression pixels are the same value. Forwarded emails will carry the original recipient's ID.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, or consolidate the 20 `imp` calls, and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four banner images, especially the two linked ones (`/daily-sales` and `/`).
3. Rename the asset with the `'` in its path and confirm the Labor Day banner is intended for this send.
4. Check the raw message in the original inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results before treating either as a real defect.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited links and the 4 unprobed filter links, using a slower rate or a different user agent.
6. Use `https://` in the OneLink `af_web_dp` fallback, and align `c=Triggered` with the batch send, or confirm it is intentional.
