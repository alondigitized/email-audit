# Starts now! Up to 20% off accent chairs, tables & rugs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- Seventh Crate & Barrel send in two days, and my first thought was "I already have this one." The hero is a Brighton Wool Ivory Rug with a banner above it for 20% off lighting, "today only," in the app. Then the headline below says up to 20% off accent chairs, tables and rugs. Two different offers in the first screen is muddled. This is the same sale as this morning's sixth send, with a new subject line and a different layout.
- It's a long email, and most of it is not the sale. After the sale blocks I get a Design Desk sweepstakes for $5,000, a Thanksgiving dining room event at up to 30% off, Affirm financing, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card promo, an app download and a store locator. I'm renovating two houses, so the sweepstakes, the free design appointment and the dining room section all appeal to me. It reads like a catalog, though, and I never found the one thing I was meant to do.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the rug or the dining tables. I wouldn't forward it. I'd probably file it in my Pinterest-adjacent folder and move on.

## What's working

- The sale is real and stated plainly: "up to 20% off" accent chairs, tables and rugs. It's easy to see what's discounted.
- The burgundy accent chair is a good lifestyle shot. It reads as design-forward and not like a clearance flyer.
- The Design Desk appointment plus a $5,000 gift card sweepstakes is relevant to someone furnishing two houses.
- The Thanksgiving dining room block is specific and seasonal. Dining tables are something I'm actually shopping for.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices and product names, so I can judge without clicking.

## What's weak

- Two offers compete in the first screen: 20% off lighting "today only" in the app, and 20% off chairs, tables and rugs. I can't tell which deadline applies to what.
- It's far too long for one send. The sweepstakes, Affirm, credit card, app download and store locator all dilute the sale.
- The hero rug image has tiny price text under it ("Sale $2,279.00 Reg. $3,099"). I'd have to zoom to read it on my phone.
- The Design Desk pitch is now the third time I've seen it in two days, including the 10/21 "Let's nail your holiday aesthetic →" and "Your most beautiful holidays yet start with The Design Desk →."
- Nothing mentions rewards, shipping timelines for furniture, swatches or color matching. I buy on my highest-rewards card and need delivery dates, and this email gives me neither.
- The "Shop the Sale" and "Shop All Sale" buttons are generic and show up several times.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one offer and one deadline. Lead with the accent chairs, tables and rugs sale, or with the lighting offer, but not both. Move the Design Desk, Affirm and app pieces to their own sends.
- 2. Cap the cadence. Seven sends in two days is the real problem. A single send with the sweepstakes folded in would beat three near-duplicates.
- 3. Make the hero price text larger and add a clear sale end date.
- 4. Replace "Shop All Sale" with specific buttons such as "Shop accent chairs" and "Shop rugs."
- 5. Add a delivery line, like "in-stock furniture ships in 2 weeks," and show the color or material options on the chairs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Accent chairs, tables & rugs: 20% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The burgundy chair is 20% off right now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a chance to win a $5,000 Design Desk gift card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rugs from $X, tables and chairs ship in 2 weeks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold-style (`mi.` / `MI styling`) template with 67 redirect-wrapped links and a lot of third-party tracking. The QA run had 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are about 25 content images with no `alt` attribute and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. The authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be checked, because the relay did not capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 67 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so no destination, status code or redirect chain was checked. The truncated source also doesn't show the final landing URLs.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - Five identical LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`) differ only in the `n` counter. The 5× repetition is redundant.
  - The `s=` value is a long hex token, probably a hashed subscriber identifier. It is sent to a third-party ad-sync domain on every open.
  - All five have no `alt`.
- **Other identifier-bearing URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/adce5a49-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carry subscriber/message identifiers. They appear to be the open-tracking pixels, and both lack `alt`.
- **Image hosts:** Content images load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the recommendation images from `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…`. These are different hosts, but both are first-party subdomains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (QA-confirmed):**
  - 18 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` (for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `1aadeb21-…jpg`, `cd56cdc7-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`).
  - 7 recommendation images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` (for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - If these are the hero, category and product images, a screen reader or an image-blocked client gets no content. The QA tool can't tell a missing attribute from an intentional empty `alt=""`.
  - The tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`) should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `table td{…}` rules combine with `*{line-height:100%}`, which overrides user text scaling in iOS Mail.
- **Markup problems:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times with different values.
  - A leftover template comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules are declared in two separate `<style>` blocks.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, and there are MSO conditional blocks and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.
- **Not checked:** The source is truncated, so table-role markup, button fallbacks and dark-mode behaviour can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check this against a raw copy of the message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** It is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is consistent with the image and tracking subdomains, but alignment is unverified.
- **Footer content:** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated part of the HTML, so they can't be confirmed here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 67 links were not resolved and the truncated source shows no `utm_*` parameters. Tracking appears to rely on the redirect wrapper and `mi_u` / `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` identifiers instead. I can't confirm landing-page alignment with the "up to 20% off accent chairs, tables & rugs" subject line.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 18 content images and 7 recommendation images, and `alt=""` to the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` where it isn't needed.
3. Remove the 4 redundant `rlcdn.com` pixels (`n=2` to `n=5`). Review whether sending a hashed identifier to a third party is covered by the privacy policy.
4. Capture the raw `.eml` and verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat the missing headers as sender faults until then.
5. Re-run QA against the full HTML and the resolved click-through URLs, to check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address and the UTM and landing-page alignment.
6. Clean up the markup: escape `&` in `<title>`, collapse the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and remove the template placeholder comment.
