# Coffee table styling 101
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "A Curated Table" — I'll give Pottery Barn this: it's a legit styling guide, not another sale wrapper. Round pedestal coffee table, tapers, faux botanicals, vases, decorative objects, bowls and trays — this is the "how do I not have a bare table" content I've actually gone looking for on Pinterest at 11pm. The round-table-with-candles hero shot is doing real work, and "Layer tapers in varied heights for warmth and dimension" is a genuinely useful styling tip, not just ad copy.
- But this is also the seventh-ish Pottery Barn email in my inbox this week, and by the time I hit "MORE WAYS TO ACCENT THE ROOM" into "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" into a rug, a bed, a curtain rod, a duvet cover, and a dining table, it's stopped being "coffee table styling" and turned into the general merchandising scroll I've already seen from the digital catalog and the 1600+ new arrivals send. Crate & Barrel's August kitchen roundup stayed locked on one room and one use case the whole way down and scored an 8 from me for it — this one starts tight and then sprawls.
- Would I click? Yeah, actually — "Shop Coffee Tables" or "Shop the Look" both get a tap from me, that pedestal table is nice. Forward to a friend? Maybe the styling tips section specifically, to my wife, not the whole email. Mark as read after skimming past the sweepstakes and rewards blocks at the bottom.

## What's working

- The hero — round pedestal coffee table styled with tapers and a runner — is genuinely aspirational and matches exactly what I'm shopping for mid-reno.
- Breaking styling into discrete categories (Candles, Faux Botanicals, Vases, Decorative Objects, Bowls & Trays) each with a one-line "why" is smart, scannable content, not just product tiles.
- "Ships Free" badge on the candles tile answers a real furniture/decor shipping question before I have to ask it.
- The Design Crew Sweeps section (free design appointment + $1500 gift card chance) is a legit loyalty-adjacent hook I haven't seen from C&B or CB2 this week.

## What's weak

- The content drifts off-topic fast — by the time I hit a farmhouse bed, curtain rods, and a dining table under "Recommended Just for You," this isn't "Coffee Table Styling 101" anymore, it's the general catalog again. Wayfair's "Fearless design, bold layers" send stayed in its inspiration lane longer than this one does.
- This is at least my sixth or seventh Pottery Barn email this week (Warehouse Sale, digital catalog, GreenRow, Art of Organization) and there's zero acknowledgment of that — no "since you loved X" personalization, just another full-catalog cascade.
- No pricing or specific product name/dimensions on the hero coffee table itself — I want to know if that's the table I'm eyeing or a different SKU before I click through.
- The bottom third (app download, Affirm BNPL, rewards card) is pure boilerplate I've scrolled past in every PB email this month — it's dead weight by email #7.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email off after "SHOP ALL DECOR." The styling-guide format is the whole value prop — tacking on beds, rugs, and dining tables at the bottom undoes the focus and makes it feel like every other PB send.
- 2. Put a price or "starting at $X" on the hero coffee table — I'm mid-reno with a budget in my head, and styling inspiration without a price anchor just sends me to search instead of click.
- 3. Reference my actual behavior — if I've opened the Warehouse Sale or GreenRow emails, say "since you're into the round table look" instead of running the identical merchandising cascade everyone gets.
- 4. Move Design Crew Sweeps up higher — a free design appointment is more relevant to "styling 101" than three tiles of unrelated furniture.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free design help, plus the pieces to pull it together`

# Technical Email Audit: Pottery Barn — "Coffee table styling 101"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget-style `click.e.potterybarn.com`) template with heavy tracking instrumentation; automated QA passed at 50% with no hard failures but multiple compliance-header and accessibility warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 67 tracking/redirect links (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — destination URLs cannot be verified from this pass.
- Recipient PII is embedded in plaintext in tracking URLs:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — open-tracking pixel, served over unencrypted **http://**, with the full recipient email address in the query string.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` — same pattern over https.
- Third-party audience/tracking pixel present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (Amobee/Turn — data co-op tracking).
- Additional open-tracking beacon: `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/c9be04e6-f433-4dcf-8d1f-a501c65ff1d0?mi_mid=...`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **40+ images missing `alt` text** across hero banners, the "slices" spacer/divider set, product images (farmhouse-platform-bed, reeva-handwoven-rug, banks-extendable-dining-table, etc.), and footer assets — confirmed by QA.
- **Mixed-content images (http:// on an https page)**, which many clients will block or flag:
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`, disabling pinch-zoom — an accessibility regression (WCAG 1.4.4 reflow/zoom).
- Malformed content in `<head>`: a `<style>` block contains literal PHP function calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...");`) — invalid CSS, dead code, likely an accidental copy-paste from a server script into the template. Not functional but risks CSS-parser errors in stricter clients (e.g., Outlook's Word engine).
- Preheader hidden-text block uses a very large run of soft-hyphen/whitespace padding characters, consistent with standard preheader-truncation control but on the heavier end of typical usage.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{FirstName}}`, `%%field%%`) observed in the visible source.
- Personalization is resolved correctly into tracking URLs (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`), but as noted in §2, this exposes the full recipient email address in cleartext GET parameters, including one over unencrypted HTTP — a data-handling concern more than a rendering one.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: **not present** (QA WARN) — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI not available.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058): **not present** (QA WARN) — cannot confirm one-click POST unsubscribe support.
- `Authentication-Results` header: **not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be determined via the AgentMail relay.
- In-body unsubscribe link/physical mailing address could not be confirmed — the footer was outside the truncated HTML window provided; this is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All body/nav links route through the ESP click-tracking domain (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`), which is expected behavior for SFMC-based sends, but it means UTM parameters and final landing-page URLs are opaque at this layer — the 67 skipped links prevent verification of landing-page alignment in this pass.
- Product deep-links to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` (image CDN) resolve on-domain, consistent with the brand's primary site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to support one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements).
2. Move the open-tracking pixel (`svopn.potterybarn.com`) to HTTPS and confirm whether embedding the raw recipient email in tracking URLs is intentional — consider hashed/tokenized identifiers instead of plaintext addresses.
3. Fix the two remaining `http://` image sources (`edm.westelm.com`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022`) to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking.
4. Add `alt` text to all decorative/product images for accessibility and to preserve context when images are blocked by default.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag to restore pinch-zoom.
6. Remove the stray PHP `header()` calls from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code with no effect that should be cleaned from the template source.
7. Re-run the tracking-link probe with redirect-following enabled to verify final landing pages and UTM continuity, since 67 links were skipped in this pass.
