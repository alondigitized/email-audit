# DUVET FILLS AND INSERTS  🔴 CLEARANCE ⚪ UP TO 70% OFF 🔵  
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "duvet fills and inserts" and then the hero banner is just "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE UP TO 70% OFF" — no duvet insert in sight until I've scrolled past a random propane fire pit, a fence planter, some lamps, wood slat panels, and a swing set. This is the exact bait-and-switch move I called out on "FOUND: Bar Stools!" (2026-08-21, 2/10) — subject promises one thing, grid delivers a Labor Day dump of whatever's in the warehouse. At least this isn't as bad as that one; there IS a duvet-looking product in slot one ("Was $199.99 Now $100.64"), so technically the subject line has a fig leaf of truth.
- The bigger issue is I've now gotten two Wayfair Labor Day clearance emails back to back — this is basically the same red-white-blue flag banner as yesterday's "🔴 ⚪ 🔵 LABOR DAY CLEARANCE 🔴 ⚪ 🔵" (5/10). Same creative, same emoji-flag treatment, different subject wrapper. That's lazy segmentation, not a real "duvet fills and inserts" campaign — CB2 got dinged for this exact pattern of recycling the same sale under different subject headlines four times this week, and now Wayfair's doing it too.
- I'm not clicking this one. I already gave the near-identical send credit yesterday; this is just the same clearance grid reshuffled with a duvet product bumped to slot one to justify the subject line. Not forwarding — my wife would ask why I'm sending her a swing set and a Sealy mattress ad when she asked about duvet inserts specifically.

## What's working

- Hero banner is legible and the 70% off number is bold and unmissable at the top.
- The duvet product actually IS in the first grid slot with a real price cut ($199.99 → $100.64), so the subject isn't a total lie.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" and "Shop all sales" bars are clear, high-contrast CTAs.
- Star ratings are visible on every product tile, which is a trust cue Crate & Barrel's "300+ new rugs" send also got credit for.

## What's weak

- Subject says duvet fills/inserts, but the grid is a general clearance dump — fire pits, swing sets, lamps, wood panels — almost nothing else is bedding.
- This is the second near-identical Labor Day clearance banner from Wayfair in two days; the flag-dot creative from 2026-08-22 is essentially unchanged.
- No color-palette or swatch/sample content for a "duvet" pitch — if I'm shopping bedding I want fill material (down vs. down-alternative), fill weight, thread count, not a stock product photo and a star rating.
- The mid-email banner grid ("area rugs," "bedding," "kitchen & dining," "mattresses") buries bedding as one line among four unrelated categories instead of leading with it.
- Email is enormous — I counted 8+ screen-lengths before hitting the footer, same complaint I had about "FOUND: Bar Stools!"

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero image an actual duvet insert/fill comparison (down vs. alternative, fill weight chart) instead of the generic flag banner — if the subject says duvet fills, open on duvet fills.
- 2. Cut the unrelated category tiles (fire pit, swing set, wood panels) from the first fold — save them for a general clearance send, not one titled around bedding.
- 3. Differentiate this from yesterday's flag-banner email — reusing the identical red/white/blue creative two sends running reads as the same email twice.
- 4. Add real bedding spec info near the duvet tile: fill type, fill weight, size (Twin/Queen/King), material — that's what actually moves a bedding purchase decision.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Down vs. down-alternative duvet inserts: up to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Duvet insert clearance ends soon — up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fill weight, size, and material — all up to 70% off through Labor Day`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Restock your bedding before the sale ends`
