# In stock & on sale! Save and refresh fast ⚡
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Fifteenth Pottery Barn email this week and I'm officially numb to the "on sale & in stock" banner — I've seen this exact canvas bed with the green gingham duvet before, just in a different crop. It's a solid, competently shot hero (that bedroom actually looks like something I'd want in the primary), but there's no discount number anywhere near it, just "on sale" as a vibe, which after two weeks of this brand in my inbox reads as background noise, not an offer.
- What actually stopped my scroll was the Halloween Shop banner sandwiched between furniture categories — August 11th and we're doing "enter if you dare" pumpkin porch decor. That's a jarring seasonal whiplash next to "delivered in 1-4 weeks" furniture copy, and it tells me this is a generic weekly blast, not anything built around my two-house renovation. The Design Crew sweepstakes for a $1500 gift card is the one beat here I haven't seen — that's at least a fresh mechanic compared to yesterday's identical version — but a sweepstakes isn't a reason to buy today.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding. I'll skim the category grid for two seconds, maybe register "Toscana Extendable Dining Table" as a thing that exists, then archive. This is a mark-as-read email, and given the cadence, close to an unsubscribe candidate.

## What's working

- The canyon/gingham bedroom hero is genuinely nice — clean styling, matches my actual "second home reno" search behavior.
- Category grid (Bedding, Bedroom, Outdoor, Living Room, Dining Room, Lighting, Rugs) is scannable and I can jump straight to what I need without scrolling through story copy.
- "Recommended Just For You" row has real dimensions listed (60"-124.5" dining table, specific rug/curtain names) — that's the kind of spec-first info that actually helps me compare against my Crate & Barrel and Wayfair tabs.
- Credit card 10% back callout is relevant since I already run everything through the highest-rewards card — that's the one section that felt like it knew who I am.

## What's weak

- No actual percentage or dollar figure anywhere in the hero — "on sale & in stock" with zero number is weaker than even Wayfair's blunt "up to 70% off" wall of tiles, and Wayfair's email annoyed me for being a spreadsheet. At least it had a number.
- Halloween Shop banner mid-email is a total tonal break — I'm mid-renovation shopping for furniture and suddenly there's a haunted porch with pumpkins. Feels like two different marketing calendars got mashed into one send.
- This is the same "Save on Furniture & More" / neutral bedroom hero format I've now seen three times this week from this brand — CB2 at least varied its "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS" framing when it was fighting sibling-brand fatigue; Pottery Barn isn't even trying to differentiate sends.
- "Delivered in 1-4 weeks" badge sits right on the hero — for someone juggling two active renovations with contractor timelines, that's a soft deterrent, not a selling point, and nobody addressed it.
- Design Crew sweepstakes fine print (spend $0 to enter, no purchase necessary) undercuts its own urgency — it reads like a compliance afterthought bolted onto the bottom half.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero. "Save on Furniture & More" with no digit is the single biggest miss — even "up to 30% off" would beat vague "on sale" copy after two weeks of this brand training me to ignore soft language.
- 2. Cut the Halloween Shop banner from a furniture-restock email or give it its own send — it breaks the "in stock, ship it now" narrative the rest of the email is built on.
- 3. Reframe "delivered in 1-4 weeks" as a positive if possible ("ships this week" on in-stock items) or drop the badge from the hero entirely — for renovation shoppers, a vague window reads as a red flag, not reassurance.
- 4. Rotate the hero product — three sends this week with variations on the same neutral bedroom shot is exactly the fatigue Crate & Barrel and CB2 are both fighting against as sibling brands; Pottery Barn is doing it to itself within one line.
- 5. Move the credit-card rewards block higher — it's the most personally relevant line in the email and it's buried past the Halloween banner and the sweepstakes.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard MessageGears/Cheetah-style HTML table layout with heavy tracking pixel/redirect infrastructure; automated QA passed at 57% due to widespread missing `alt` attributes, several HTTP (non-TLS) image sources, and undetectable unsubscribe/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTA links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` — an opaquely encoded redirect param, not raw UTM tags. 56 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected for click-tracking domains, not itself a defect).
- Multiple open/tracking pixels present: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (third-party behavioral pixel), `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=...&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` (open tracking, served over **HTTP**), and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/32ec206a-...?mi_mid=...&cp_tp` (MessageGears open pixel).
- `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel and `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` (spacer) are both loaded over **plain HTTP**, which mixed-content-blocking mail clients may drop, undercounting opens.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` in `<head>` strips the `Referer` header on all outbound clicks — any downstream analytics relying on document referrer for attribution will not receive it (redirect-chain tracking via `click.e.potterybarn.com` is unaffected since it uses its own query param, not referrer).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 40+ `<img>` tags are missing `alt` text (product shots, banners, footer slices, and even functional/tracking images) — confirmed by QA output. This affects screen-reader users and image-blocked inboxes, where no fallback text renders.
- Two image sources use `http://` instead of `https://`: `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` — mixed-content warnings/blocking possible in strict mail clients (e.g., Outlook via Office 365 hardened rendering).
- The `<style>` block in `<head>` contains literal PHP code (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...");`) pasted directly inside CSS. This is inert (browsers ignore invalid CSS tokens) but indicates leftover server-side/template debug code shipped in production markup — should be removed as dead code.
- `<meta name="viewport" ...>` and responsive `@media` breakpoints (480px/650px/700px) are present and structured normally; no rendering-breaking markup found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Personalization tokens resolved correctly at send time — no unrendered merge tags (e.g. `{{FIRSTNAME}}`, `%%EMAIL%%`) found in the visible source.
- Recipient email is embedded in cleartext across multiple URLs: `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. This is standard ESP practice for one-click preference/unsub links but is worth noting as PII exposed in plain query strings (no hashing/tokenization).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- QA could not detect a `List-Unsubscribe` header — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed from available data (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence).
- QA could not detect a `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058), meaning one-click (POST-based) unsubscribe compliance is unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome) was not captured, so sender authentication status is unknown from this data.
- The visible HTML truncates before the footer, so an in-body unsubscribe link/physical address (CAN-SPAM body requirements) could not be verified directly — only the `coreemail.html` customer-service link was visible in this excerpt.
- **Recommendation-relevant gap**: none of the above are confirmed *failures* — they are confirmed *unknowns* due to instrumentation limits (per the QA warnings). Treat as needing header-level verification, not as proven violations.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No raw `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are present on any visible link; all destination tracking is opaque (`qs=` encoded blob via `click.e.potterybarn.com`), so landing-page UTM alignment cannot be verified from the HTML alone — would require following the redirect chain.
- Two links point directly at `www.potterybarn.com` (non-tracked): `/customer-service/coreemail.html` and product image references under `/pbimgs/...` — these bypass the click-tracking layer entirely, so any campaign-attribution reporting will not capture clicks on these specific assets if they're also used as link hrefs (only confirmed as image `src`s here, not verified as `<a href>` targets in the truncated excerpt).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray PHP `header()` calls from the `<style>` block — dead code with no function in email markup.
2. Convert the two/three HTTP image sources (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/.../Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/.../slices_02.jpg`) to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking, especially the HTTP open-tracking pixel which risks undercounting opens.
3. Add `alt` text to all `<img>` tags, prioritizing hero/product banners for accessibility and image-blocked rendering.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers directly at the SMTP/MIME level (outside this HTML-only capture) to close the compliance-confirmation gap flagged by QA.
5. If UTM-based attribution is needed for landing-page analytics, confirm the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect chain appends UTM params before hitting `potterybarn.com`, since none are visible in the pre-redirect href.
