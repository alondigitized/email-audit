# New lamps for a little extra light✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, table lamps — "Illuminate Every Corner" — and this is actually a decent-looking hero shot, that mixed grouping of brass table lamps with the pleated linen shades. But let's be honest, this is Pottery Barn's eighth email to me this week, and lamps is at least a new category after four straight days of bedding, towels, and Halloween dinnerware. I clicked into it mentally for about three seconds because I do need something for the entryway console at the second place.
- Then it just... keeps going. "Trendspotting" petite lamps, then a full lighting-by-type grid — chandeliers, floor lamps, pendants, sconces, picture lights, outdoor — which is fine as a catalog page but is a lot of scroll for an email with zero percentage off, zero dollar figure, nothing. Compare that to the Pottery Barn "Bath towels from $24.50" send from yesterday — that one at least had a number I could do math on. This one's all vibes.
- Then the "Recommended Just For You" section pivots hard away from lighting into curtain rods, a dining table, a rug, a platform bed, duvet covers — none of which is a lamp, all of which reads like generic algorithmic filler slapped under a lighting email. I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding. Marking as read.

## What's working

- The hero shot is genuinely nice — warm brass tones, good product variety, looks like a table I'd actually screenshot.
- "Petite table lamps are having a moment... perfect for dressers, entryways, or kitchen counters" is a real styling tip, not just sale copy — that's the kind of context I actually want.
- The lighting-by-type grid is organized and scannable if I were actually in "furnish a room" mode.
- No render bugs — everything's aligned, images loaded, text is legible.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere. Not a percent off, not a dollar figure, not free shipping tied to lamps specifically — CB2's "Now & Future Classics on Sale" from a few days back at least led with a real sale banner.
- "Recommended Just For You" is a mess — curtain rods, a dining table, a rug, a bed, duvet covers under a *lighting* email. If this is "for you," it doesn't feel like it, it feels like a generic module that didn't get swapped out.
- This is the eighth-ish Pottery Barn email this week and the cadence is genuinely wearing on me — my inbox has become a Pottery Barn feed, not an inbox.
- The Halloween Shop banner and Design Crew Sweeps block feel bolted onto the bottom of an unrelated lighting email — scattershot, not curated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an actual offer on lighting — even "15% off lamps this week" beats zero numbers. Crate & Barrel's apartment-collection email scored well with me specifically because it felt purpose-built, not padded.
- 2. Cut "Recommended Just For You" down to lighting-adjacent items only (lamp shades, side tables to put lamps on) — mixing in a dining table and duvet covers under a lamp email kills the "for you" credibility.
- 3. Drop the Halloween Shop and Design Crew Sweeps blocks from this send entirely — they dilute the one clear message (lamps) into three unrelated messages.
- 4. Pull back the sending cadence — I'm getting Pottery Barn fatigue, and a tighter weekly rhythm would make me actually open instead of skim-and-archive.
