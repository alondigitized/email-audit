# Introducing The Christmas Shop 🎄✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the first send in a week with a real reason to exist. The Christmas Shop launch is a lookbook, not a promo, and that suits me. The hero is a lit tree and stockings over a "Deck the Halls" button. I'm renovating two houses, so I'm browsing the holiday catalog with a Pinterest board open anyway.
- There's no number in it, though. Yesterday's "On sale & delivered for free" had a price hook and the 11/04 "New arrivals with timeless holiday charm" did not. This one is pure mood, and I can't tell whether anything is on sale. CB2 opened its "STARTS NOW: UP TO 50% OFF" with a percentage in the hero, and Crate & Barrel Kids put "2x Rewards" right in the subject. Pottery Barn wants me to scroll and guess.
- I'd save it to the inspiration folder and probably not click. The 11/05 West Elm Kids spotlight was irrelevant to me, and this is at least adjacent to what I'm doing. I might forward the dining table to my partner, but the email wouldn't be the reason.

## What's working

- The hero reads clearly. "The Christmas Shop" with "now open" and a Deck the Halls button tells me what this is in one second.
- Four tiles (stockings, bedding, tablescape, pillows) each carry a "Shop Now" link. They're category-level, but they're easy to skim and each has its own link.
- The photography is consistent. Everything is red, green and cream, which matters when I'm matching a palette across two homes.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has furniture in it: the Banks and Toscana extendable dining tables and the Farmhouse platform bed. That's the part that actually speaks to my renovation.
- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner is still on top. It's the best shipping cue in the category.
- The free Design Crew appointment and the 10% back card are both present, and I like the rewards math there.

## What's weak

- There's no offer anywhere above the fold, and I couldn't spot a headline discount in the body. Is anything discounted, or is this just new stock?
- The tile CTAs are small underlined "Shop Now" text on dark photos. They'd be hard to tap on a phone.
- The header is a vague launch announcement. It says nothing about furniture, even though that's what I'm buying.
- The email is very long. Design Crew, the app, the credit card and Affirm are stacked at the bottom, and the dining tables and bed are buried under stockings.
- It's stockings and ornaments again, a few days after the 11/05 West Elm Kids spotlight and the 11/04 holiday lookbook. The cadence feels heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer in the hero, such as a percent off, free shipping, or a member price. A launch needs a reason to click today.
- 2. Move the furniture tiles (dining tables, beds) above the stockings. Lead with what a renovator buys.
- 3. Turn the "Shop Now" text links into full-width buttons.
- 4. Cut the footer stack down to rewards plus Design Crew. Skip Affirm and the app promo.
- 5. Back off the send cadence. This is the fourth holiday-themed send in a week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Christmas Shop is open: free shipping on holiday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stockings, bedding & tablescapes, now in stock`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 1-4 week furniture delivery and 10% back in rewards.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout built from sliced images, and its click links go through an opaque ESP redirect domain. The confirmed code-level problems are widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, an empty `<title>`, and stray PHP text inside a `<style>` block. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, so they don't show a defect. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 failures, 3 warnings).

- **Opaque click tracking:** 47 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use tracking or redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNTEs...` on the logo. Destination validity, redirect chain and final status for those 47 links are **unverified**.
- **Recipient email in tracking-pixel URLs:** the plaintext recipient address appears in query strings:
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

  The address is unhashed and travels in a URL, so it can end up in proxy and server logs. The svopn pixel is also over HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 40 images are flagged. This covers the main creative (`F26_Seasonal_B10_XmasShop/slices_01–06.jpg`), all product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, and others), and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
