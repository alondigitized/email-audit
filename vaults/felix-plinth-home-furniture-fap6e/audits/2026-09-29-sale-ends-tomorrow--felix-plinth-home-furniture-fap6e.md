# SALE ENDS TOMORROW
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- CB2 is running an "Ends Tomorrow" sale, and I'm not sure I believe it. I got "Color Is the Word" earlier today and the David Girelli designer send on 9/28. The 9/28 rugs email had a sale feel too. A sale that ends tomorrow, announced the same day as a color story, reads like a calendar-driven blast. The hero is a moody blurred branch with "Ends Tomorrow, Up to 50% Off!" over it. The deadline is clear, but the image tells me nothing about what's on sale.
- Below the hero it's a clean four-tile grid: furniture (a plum velvet sofa), rugs, lighting (an alabaster lamp) and decor. Then a green "Upholstery Event, up to 25% off" block. It's tidy and nothing is broken. But it's category-level, so I have to click through to find out what's actually discounted. I'm furnishing two houses and I want a SKU, a price and a ship date.
- Would I click? Maybe. The alabaster lamp and the sofa tempt me, and the deadline is a real prompt. But I'd probably save it to my folder and check the site later. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- "Ends Tomorrow, Up to 50% Off!" is a plain, honest headline, and the deadline is obvious at a glance.
- The four tiles with category labels (Furniture, Rugs, Lighting, Decor) let me jump straight to what I'm shopping for.
- The alabaster table lamp and the plum velvet sofa are lovely. They fit the renovation mood and they're what I'd pin.
- The Upholstery Event block is a second, separate offer with a clear "up to 25% off" figure and its own button.
- The 10% Rewards Dollars callout on CB2 and Crate & Barrel purchases speaks to my card-stacking habit.
- Nothing is broken or overlapping. That's a step up from the garbled "Two sales are on now" hero last week.

## What's weak

- The hero image is a blurry stem and petals. It doesn't show a single product, so the offer has no visual anchor.
- "Up to 50% off" is vague. Which items, and how many? The upholstery block adds another "up to" figure, so two percentages compete.
- There's no product, price or "was/now" anywhere. It's four moody room shots with no products I can tell apart.
- Two identical "Shop the Sale" buttons plus a third in the upholstery block don't tell me anything new about where I'm going.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny gray text at the bottom. Exclusions are buried, and nothing about furniture shipping or lead times is visible, which matters a lot to me.
- Fourth CB2 send in three days. Between "Things of Quality", "Rugs for Every Room" and "Color Is the Word", urgency is hard to take seriously.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real product in the hero, such as the velvet sofa with its sale price, instead of a blurred branch.
- 2. Make the offer specific: "Up to 50% off sofas, rugs & lighting" with named hero items and prices, and one primary discount instead of two competing "up to" figures.
- 3. Show ship-by dates or in-stock status for the furniture. Pottery Barn's sconce send at least showed me finished, ready-to-hang product.
- 4. Use category-specific buttons like "Shop sale lighting" and "Shop sale rugs" instead of repeating "Shop the Sale".
- 5. Add a countdown or an exact time, like "Ends 9/30 11:59 PM PT," so the deadline feels real.
- 6. Suppress or consolidate sends when the same week already had three CB2 emails. Wayfair's 72-hour clearout banners taught me to ignore this pattern.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sale ends 9/30: sofas, rugs & lighting up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: that alabaster lamp is 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off sofas, rugs, lighting & decor, plus 25% off upholstery. Ends tomorrow at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free returns aren't a thing on furniture. Sale prices are. Last call.`

## CB2 "SALE ENDS TOMORROW": Technical Audit

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering bug: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads 10 non-HTTPS Dotomi cookie-sync pixels and has several accessibility gaps. The QA run passed 43% of checks. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified because of the relay, and the HTML source was truncated.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed** because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** Ten `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` to `pub10.php` pixels, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`, use plain HTTP. Clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading will block them. They can also trigger mixed-content warnings in webmail.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The 10 `pub*.php` calls are cookie-sync redirects to an ad-tech vendor. They add load and privacy exposure, and they are likely to be blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and similar features.
- **Cross-brand font hosting.** The `@font-face` "HNW05" sources load from `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`. That creates a dependency on a sister-brand domain, and hotlinked fonts can break if the path or CORS policy changes.
- **Malformed font URLs.** The font URLs `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` contain a double slash. It probably resolves, but it is fragile.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** Seven images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`: `accae064-…`, `e284a3e0-…`, `dbed4336-…`, `6aadc53c-…`, `a6d04091-…`, `44fe4db1-…` and `89537ff7-…`. If these are linked or carry content, they will render blank with images off and are unannounced or read as filenames by screen readers.
