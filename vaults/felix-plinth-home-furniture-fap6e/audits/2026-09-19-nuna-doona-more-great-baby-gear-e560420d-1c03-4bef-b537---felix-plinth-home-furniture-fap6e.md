# Nuna, Doona & more great baby gear
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- Yeah, no. This is Crate & Kids, not even Crate & Barrel proper, and it's wall-to-wall car seats, strollers, and "must-have high chairs." I don't have kids. Nothing about Nuna infant seats or Doona strollers or a Stokke high chair moves me one inch when I'm buried in swatches and paint chips for two houses right now. I opened my last audit of this exact list on 2026-09-19 and it was the same story — this is basically a repeat send from that same day, just recirculating the identical baby-gear catalog dump.
- The thing is, the production quality here is actually solid — clean product photography, a logical flow from strollers to car seats to high chairs to nursery furniture — but craftsmanship doesn't save an email that's aimed at a completely different life stage than mine. Compare that to the Pottery Barn "Workspace Refresh" email I scored 7/10 — that one showed me a desk I could actually picture in my second-house office nook. This is the exact opposite: total demographic mismatch.
- Verdict: not opening past the first scroll, not clicking, definitely not forwarding — unless it's to a friend who just had a baby, as a joke about how Crate & Barrel's algorithm apparently thinks everyone on their list is expecting.

## What's working

- The hero banner is clean — "the very best baby gear" with a lineup of car seats reads clearly even without zooming.
- Product photography throughout (strollers, car seats, the green high chair) is genuinely well-shot and consistent with Crate & Barrel's usual aesthetic.
- Sectioning is logical — strollers, then car seats, then high chairs, then nursery furniture, then "brands you love" (Nuna, Bugaboo, Stokke, Doona) — easy to scan even scrolling fast.
- The "20% off best-selling bath essentials" banner near the bottom at least has a concrete, time-bound offer, which is more than most of this email has.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — this entire email is irrelevant to me, full stop, same problem I flagged in my 2026-09-19 review of this identical send.
- No unifying discount or hero CTA up top — I have to scroll through five product categories before hitting an actual offer (the bath essentials sale), which buries the one concrete deal.
- It's an absurdly long scroll — car seats, strollers, high chairs, cribs, "brands you love," "shop the new collection," a bath sale, "we love these for you," and an app download pitch. That's not an email, that's a catalog.
- The brand lineup section (Nuna, Bugaboo, Stokke, Doona) reads like a wholesale directory, not a targeted pitch — zero personalization signal that Crate & Kids knows anything about me.
- Ending with "It's all on the app" and a Design Desk consultation pitch feels like the email ran out of ideas and started stapling on unrelated CTAs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress me from this list entirely, or at minimum segment by purchase history — if I've never bought baby gear from Crate & Barrel, don't send me the Crate & Kids catalog. This is the single biggest lever; no subject line rewrite fixes a targeting failure.
- 2. Lead with the one real offer (20% off bath essentials) instead of burying it two-thirds down — front-load the deal, not the product tour.
- 3. Cut this from nine sections to three — strollers, car seats, the sale — and drop the "brands you love" wholesale-directory block entirely.
- 4. Drop the app-download and Design Desk pitches from the bottom; they dilute an already unfocused send.
