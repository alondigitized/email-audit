# HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RESTOCK
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and "Highly Anticipated Restock" sells this one short. I don't know what was anticipated or by whom. The body is a plain "Restock Alert" with a sectional hero, and that's easier to read than the subject. I'm doing two houses, so in-stock beds, sectionals and coffee tables are the right categories for me. It's the fourth CB2 send in about three days, though, and I'm tired of it.
- The top of the email stacks three messages. There's a green band for the last double rewards, a "Early Black Friday Sale up to 60% off" block, and a "Restock Alert" hero. Near the bottom there's a separate "Now up to 70% off clearance" banner. That's the same 60-vs-70 muddle I flagged on 11/14 ("UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS"). I'd click into Sectionals, then lose interest. I wouldn't forward it. It's a good catalog, but the email can't decide what it's about.

## What's working

- The restock framing gives me a real reason to look. Availability matters when I'm furnishing two houses on a deadline.
- The category tiles (Beds, Dining Chairs, Sectionals, Coffee Tables, Storage) are big, moody photos. They're easy to scan and look like Pinterest-folder material.
- The Thanksgiving delivery-date strip (11/17, 11/19, 11/23, 11/24) is the shipping clarity I want on furniture. Most brands skip it.
- The rewards card callout with "last double rewards this year, now to Nov 15" is credible urgency. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so I notice it.

## What's weak

- The subject says "restock," but the first thing I see is a rewards band, then a Black Friday sale. Three competing headlines.
- The top band says up to 60% off and the bottom banner says up to 70% off clearance. It's the same mixed-offer problem as 11/14.
- The restock tiles show no prices, product names or "only X left" counts. "In-stock" with nothing specific isn't a reason to click now.
- The 11/13 and 11/14 sends already showed lacquer and lighting. This one has no new news and no reason to open it today.
- The "Shop Back In Stock" button appears twice, plus a button on every tile. That's a lot of tap targets pointing at the same place.
- The legal fine print is tiny and not readable on a phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline. Lead with the restock and push the rewards and clearance messaging below the fold. Or put the real hook, "Last day, 2X rewards," in the subject.
- 2. Name the products. Show 4-6 specific restocked pieces with price and a stock cue, e.g. "Peekaboo sectional, back, ships 11/19." The fine print also needs a mobile-friendly size.
- 3. Resolve 60% vs 70%. I'd rather see one number than two.
- 4. Space out the cadence. Four sends in three days makes me reflexively skip. Wayfair at least switched to a first-purchase offer, and Pottery Barn's updated terms email read as a break from the promo wall.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back in stock: the sectional, bed and coffee table you wanted`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Restocked + 2X rewards ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sectionals, beds and dining chairs are back, and most ship before Thanksgiving.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last day to earn 20% back at CB2 on in-stock favorites.`
- Compared with competitors: Article's "Travertine: A natural showstopper" led with one hero product and one theme, and it was cleaner. Crate & Barrel's "ON NOW! The last Double Rewards of the year!" also stacked too many blocks, and CB2 is repeating that mistake here. Wayfair's lamp email promised table lamps and delivered one tile, so CB2's tiles at least match the "restock" promise.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: CB2 "HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RESTOCK"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard ESP template (Marketing Cloud-style `mi_` tracking) with outlook/MSO fallbacks. QA passed 57%, with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The confirmed issues are 14 content images with no alt text, 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi tracking images, and a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The HTML source was truncated, so many checks can't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **65 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP check because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed good or bad.
- **Insecure tracking pixels (confirmed).** Eleven images load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Why it matters.** Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop these requests. The `cookieredir/pub1–10` calls are a 10-pixel third-party cookie-sync chain, which adds extra requests on every open.
- **Other tracking pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=<64-hex>…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/a422bffa-…?mi_cid=…` are HTTPS. The `mi_u` value looks like a SHA-256 hash. The source doesn't show what it hashes, so this is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 14 content images (confirmed).** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, e.g. `5c26e5b4-1736-4057-8954-c07f9606422e.jpg` through `2c12b294-00b8-4f4d-8993-bc26ec4083b8.jpg`. If these are product or hero images, images-off and screen-reader users get no content or link label.
- **Tracking pixels without alt (11 Dotomi, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`).** This is low impact for 1×1 pixels. Add `alt=""` to mark them decorative.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Text-size lock.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the `*` and `body` rules and prevents text resizing on iOS.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Font URLs.** One `@font-face` uses `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/…` with a double slash. Others load from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`. Fonts have fallbacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`), so impact is minor.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set, and there is a `<title>` and a `format-detection` meta. The title ("CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME") is generic and doesn't match the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. Later content was not available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than proven missing. A relay that strips headers also means this test can't confirm one-click unsubscribe support.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` and the image domain `image.mail.cb2.com` are consistent, which is a mild positive.
- **Footer.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 65 tracked links were skipped, and no final destination URLs or UTM parameters are in the data provided. Tracking appears to run through ESP redirects and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` identifiers rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** add descriptive alt text to the 14 `image.mail.cb2.com` images. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. **HTTPS pixels:** move the Dotomi `UCMController` and `pub1–10.php` pixels to HTTPS, or drop the cookie-sync chain from email. Most clients won't run it reliably anyway.
3. **Zoom:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. **Text size:** replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
5. **Unsubscribe headers:** verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message, not the relayed copy.
6. **Authentication:** check SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the original message to settle the unknown deliverability status.
7. **Re-run:** run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve a sample of the tracked links to confirm destinations, UTMs and the footer unsubscribe link.
8. **Cleanup:** remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT…` placeholder and the double-slash font URLs.
