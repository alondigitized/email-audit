## What's weak

- **Same campaign territory as three days ago** — the June 23rd Ninja/Fellow email already covered this; the cadence is starting to feel like the same promo just reshuffled with different thumbnails.
- **No deadline anywhere in sight** — Pottery Barn's June 26th send had an actual "through Sunday" hook buried in the fine print; this has nothing. For a "sale" email, that's a material miss.
- **Grid-dump structure kills hierarchy** — below the hero it's just row after row of small product tiles with no editorial voice or prioritization; my eye doesn't know where to land.
- **No brand callouts in the hero** — if KitchenAid or Le Creuset is in here, that's a name worth putting in the hero or sub-headline; vague "kitchen brands" undersells it.

- 1. **Put a brand name in the headline** — "KitchenAid, Le Creuset & more, up to 35% off" beats generic "kitchen brands" every time. Article does this with collection names; C&B should too.
- 2. **Add a deadline** — even "through Sunday" or "this week only" creates the close. The June 23rd Ninja/Fellow email also skipped this; the pattern needs to break.
- 3. **Editorialize the grid** — pick two or three hero SKUs and give them a visual moment before dropping into the product dump. The Finn Collection email from June 22nd did this right; borrow that approach.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text: 52 images missing `alt` attributes** (all QA WARN entries). This breaks two things simultaneously:
- Screen reader experience is completely absent for image-heavy content
- When images are blocked (default in many corporate/Outlook environments), recipients see a wall of empty boxes with no fallback text — no offer, no product names, no CTA context

Notable omissions include what appear to be product images (`.jpg` assets), animated GIFs (likely used for hero/banner), and all tracking/pixel images. Tracking pixels should have `alt=""` explicitly set (empty, not absent) to suppress screen-reader noise — the current absence is technically different from `alt=""`.

**Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags:** `telephone=no`, `address=no`, and `date=no` each appear twice in `<head>`. Redundant but harmless; indicates template assembly without deduplication.

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta:** Prevents user zoom on mobile. This is an accessibility regression (WCAG 1.4.4) and increasingly flagged by accessibility auditors.

**MSO conditional comments:** Present and correctly structured for Outlook VML rendering. No issues.

**Media query breakpoint gap:** Responsive styles kick in at `max-width: 460px`, but `desktop` styles apply at `min-width: 640px` and `min-width: 768px` (conflicting). Viewports 461–639px receive neither mobile nor desktop overrides — likely unintentional.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header: NOT DETECTED**
The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (>5k/day) under their 2024 enforcement policy. If genuinely absent from the envelope, this is a deliverability risk that can suppress inbox placement at Gmail.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): NOT DETECTED**
One-click unsubscribe (the machine-readable POST mechanism) was not found. Required alongside `List-Unsubscribe` for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance. Note: both headers may have been stripped at the relay layer rather than genuinely absent — this must be verified against raw envelope headers.

**CAN-SPAM physical address:** Not verifiable from the truncated HTML, but standard for C&B sends — confirm footer contains mailing address.

**Authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** `Authentication-Results` header not captured. Sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`; DKIM alignment requires a `d=crateandbarrel.com` or `d=mail.crateandbarrel.com` signature. Cannot confirm from this data — verify against raw headers in ESP dashboard.
