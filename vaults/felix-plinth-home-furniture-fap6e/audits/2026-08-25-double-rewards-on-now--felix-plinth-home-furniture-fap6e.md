# DOUBLE REWARDS§ ON NOW
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is CB2 email number eight in eight days, and this one's actually a credit card pitch dressed up as a sale email — "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON," 20% back in reward dollars, but only if you have (or sign up for) a CB2 Visa Signature card. I already flagged their "This sale ends tonight" send on 8/23 and the "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" before that — at this point I'm basically getting a CB2 email every single morning, and this one just adds a financial-products angle to the pile.
- The category grid — Living Room, Lighting, Dining Room, Decor & Mirrors, Bedroom, Rugs — is clean and the styling shots are genuinely nice, especially that walnut dining table and the green pendant lamp. But burying "Labor Day Sale, up to 50% off" below six category tiles, after leading with a credit card pitch, is backwards. Crate & Barrel's 8/25 "Earn DOUBLE REWARDS + Shop up to 60% off" email did this exact same rewards-plus-sale combo and led with the bigger number and the actual sale — this CB2 send buries its own Labor Day sale under a credit card ask.
- Not clicking this one. I don't want a store card, and if I did, this isn't the email that sells me on it — it's a discount card pitch with a strikethrough 10%→20% that reads more like fine-print math than an offer. Marking as read, moving on.

## What's working

- The category grid (Living Room through Rugs) is genuinely well-shot — that walnut dining table and the black horse decor vignette are exactly my renovation-project taste.
- "NOW THROUGH SEPT 9TH, 2026" up top gives a real, credible deadline instead of vague urgency.
- Clear brand nav bar (New Arrivals, Furniture, Decor, Rugs, Lighting) so I know where I'd land if I did click.
- The Labor Day banner at the bottom at least states "up to 50% off" plainly, no games.

## What's weak

- Leading with a credit card offer instead of the actual Labor Day sale is a swing and a miss — I don't want a new card, I want the furniture discount.
- The strikethrough "10%" to "20%" in the hero reads like a rate table, not an offer — it's confusing at a glance, not exciting.
- This is my eighth CB2 email in eight days. Fatigue is real. Crate & Barrel's sister-brand overlap makes this worse — I'm getting hit from both directions.
- Two competing offers in one email (card rewards vs. Labor Day sale) splits attention and neither one wins.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Labor Day sale (the thing everyone actually wants) and move the credit card pitch to a secondary block or a separate, less frequent send — Crate & Barrel proved this combo works when the sale headline comes first.
- 2. Cut send frequency. Eight emails in eight days is why I've started skimming CB2 mail instead of reading it.
- 3. Simplify the rewards math — "Get 20% back in reward dollars" without the crossed-out "10%" visual clutter.
- 4. Add a hero product link instead of routing everything to generic category tiles — give me one specific dining table or lamp to click on.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
CB2's "Double Rewards" HTML email uses legacy DTM/dotomi ad-tracking pixels served over plain HTTP and ships with no unsubscribe or authentication headers captured, alongside widespread missing alt text on product imagery.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure tracking pixels**: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` and 10 sequential `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` requests are all served over unencrypted HTTP. Most mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) block or strip non-HTTPS image/pixel loads, meaning this ad-tracking/cookie-sync chain likely fails silently for most recipients.
- 60 additional tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect domains — not itself an issue, but not independently verified as functional).
- Standard MI (mi.cb2.com) open-tracking pixel (`o.gif`) and a `dv.cb2.com` pixel are present and use HTTPS — no issue there.
