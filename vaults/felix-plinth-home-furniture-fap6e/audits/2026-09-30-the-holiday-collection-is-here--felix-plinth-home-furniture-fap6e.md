# The Holiday Collection is here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and this is the fourth send in three days. My first reaction was that the hero is a sale banner and the "Holiday Collection" header sits underneath it. The top is "The Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off." That's the only concrete offer in the email, and it has nothing to do with the subject line. I opened for holiday and the first thing I got was a sofa sale. Since I'm furnishing two houses, the sofa sale is what I care about. But the subject promised one email and the render delivered two.
- Below that it turns into a long, moody gift-guide scroll: dining, gifts, entertaining, barware, decor, ornaments. The photography is lovely, and it's the kind of thing I'd file in my Pinterest-adjacent folder. But I'm not shopping for cocktail glasses and a velvet-bow throw. I'm picking upholstery and lighting. The product grid near the bottom is all candle holders and a faux eucalyptus stem. I'd skim it, save it, and not click.
- Judgement: I'd click the upholstery banner if I hadn't already been to CB2 this week. I wouldn't forward it. It's a mark-as-read for me, and I'd only keep it if I were actively shopping for a sofa.
- Compared with the rest of my inbox, Pottery Barn's "New arrivals for the season of gathering" led with in-stock furniture and delivery times, which is what I actually need to decide. Wayfair's "HOLIDAY ACCENTS" send promised holiday and didn't deliver it. CB2 does deliver holiday here, but the sale headline fights it. Compared with Crate & Barrel Kids' "ENDS TODAY" baby blast, this is at least on-category. It's also less urgent than CB2's own "SALE ENDS TOMORROW" from yesterday, so I'm not sure how to read the deadline on this one.

## What's working

- The upholstery banner puts a real number (up to 25% off) at the very top, and I can see it without scrolling.
- The holiday photography is cohesive and warm, with a consistent palette. It reads as styled rooms, which is what I save for the renovation.
- The category tiles ("Shop Dining," "Shop Gifts," "Shop Entertaining," "Shop Decor") are easy to scan, and each has its own button.
- The rewards and app block near the bottom ("Earn 10% back") speaks to how I buy. I put everything on the highest-rewards card, so I noticed it.
- Nothing looks broken. I see no overlapping text and no empty image slots.

## What's weak

- The subject says holiday but the hero is a sale event. Two campaigns are fighting in one email, and neither wins.
- The email is very long and mostly mood imagery. There are a lot of "Shop ___" buttons, and I can't tell which one matters.
- The product grid at the end is small candle holders and faux greenery. There are no sofas, no chairs, and no lighting. That doesn't match the upholstery event I was pitched at the top.
- Text is small in places, especially the app and rewards block and the legal footer. I'd be pinching to zoom on my phone.
- CB2 is the fourth send in three days after "Color Is the Word," "Designer to know: David Girelli," and "SALE ENDS TOMORROW." Add the Crate & Barrel and Crate & Kids logos in the footer, and my inbox has the whole family piling on.
- I can't see any sample or swatch offer, delivery timing for furniture, or any showroom tie-in. The upholstery sale would need all three before I'd order a sofa.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Either lead with the upholstery event and make the holiday collection a small secondary module, or lead with holiday and drop the sale banner to a thin strip. Don't split the top of the email.
- 2. Under the upholstery banner, show three actual sofas with prices, fabric swatch chips, and a "free swatches" link. Add delivery windows for in-stock pieces, the way Pottery Barn did.
- 3. Cut the gift-guide section to about half its length and give each category one product with a price.
- 4. Attach a real deadline to the sale so the urgency is credible and doesn't clash with the "ends tomorrow" send from 9/29.
- 5. Move the rewards line up near the offer, with the math spelled out.
- 6. Use the preheader to say something the subject doesn't.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 25% off upholstery, plus the holiday edit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas up to 25% off. Holiday's here too.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Semi-annual upholstery event: swatches free, in-stock pieces ship in weeks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 10% back with the CB2 card on holiday decor and gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has widespread missing alt text and 11 non-HTTPS tracking images. The HTML source was truncated and the relay captured no headers, so compliance and authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 77 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Mixed content:** 11 tracking images load over `http://`, which clients may block. That would silently drop the Dotomi cookie-sync and retargeting pixels.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party pixel load:** The 10-pixel `cookieredir` chain adds 10 extra third-party requests per open.
- **Cross-domain font hosting:** Some `@font-face` sources point to `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, while the rest point to `www.cb2.com`. If the sister-brand host changes or restricts hotlinking, HNW05 falls back to system fonts.
- **Malformed font URLs:** Several `@font-face` sources use a double slash, e.g. `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Unresolved merge token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears raw in the source. See Section 4.
- **Missing alt text on content images (16):** All are hosted on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, including:
  - `48a7e47c-d164-4121-a8d9-5965174d8df2.jpg`
  - `391ada82-1f0e-4abe-8d3f-d7556f4bf4b8.jpg`
  - `af3c04f2-9174-4363-aa76-293eb9d58848.gif`
  - `3e248843-d819-4df7-8089-3f0278b34b1c.gif`
  - `9685411b-425d-4865-a92f-a520df64bb18.jpg`

  With images off or a screen reader, these are unlabeled. If any are linked, they have no accessible name.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** `mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/925f7948-...`, and the Dotomi images have no `alt`. These are non-visual pixels, so `alt=""` is the correct fix. Screen readers may otherwise announce the filename or URL.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, an accessibility issue for low-vision users.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a placeholder that was never removed. It is harmless but adds bytes.
- **Text-size scaling disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` (global `*` rule and `body`) stops iOS from scaling text. That may hurt readability on small-font elements such as `.f10`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tooling notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check them against the original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, again likely due to the relay. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is consistent with proper alignment, but this is not confirmed by the data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not assessable because the HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 77 click-tracked links were not resolved, and the visible source is truncated. UTM parameters and landing page alignment therefore **cannot be verified**.
- The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the pixel URLs suggest first-party tracking through the `cb2.com` subdomains, but that says nothing about link-level UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the data extension or dynamic-content source, and add a fallback so an empty value hides the block instead of printing raw text. Add a pre-send test that fails on `%%...%%` patterns.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS.** Use `https://` for `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com`, or drop the 10-pixel `cookieredir` chain if it isn't needed.
3. **Add `alt` to all images.** Use descriptive text for the 16 content images and `alt=""` for tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag to allow zoom.
5. **Verify the headers on the original message.** Confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment outside the relay.
6. **Clean up template debris.** Remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment, fix the `//Styles` double slashes, and host fonts on a single domain.
