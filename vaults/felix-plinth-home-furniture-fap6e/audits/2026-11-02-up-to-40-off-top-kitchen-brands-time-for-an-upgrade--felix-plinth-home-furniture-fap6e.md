# Up to 40% off top kitchen brands! Time for an upgrade →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the second kitchen send in a row from Crate & Barrel, and I'm renovating two kitchens, so I stayed with it. It's a repeat of yesterday's "Your favorite kitchen brands, now up to 40% off" with a different subject line. Same Dyson-style vacuum hero, same "2 days left!" banner, and the same Breville, KitchenAid and Caraway grid. I feel like I've been served the same lamp three times, only this time it's the same stand mixer twice in one email. The KitchenAid Artisan shows up in the product grid and again in "we love these for you."
- The kitchen grid is useful. It has real sale prices (Caraway, Breville Smart Oven, Shun knives) and a "$300 off Breville espresso machines" line I'd actually screenshot. But the hero is a vacuum with an "accent chairs, tables & rugs up to 20% off" banner above it, and that has nothing to do with the subject. The email is also very long, and the Black Friday signup, the app pitch, the design service and the Reward Dollars card all pile on. It's a catalog, not an email.
- Would I click? Probably the Breville espresso tile, since I'm shopping for that. Would I forward it? No. I'd save it to my kitchen folder and move on.

## What's working

- The 40%-off headline is big, clear and sits right under the hero, with a visible "Shop Now" button.
- The product grid shows named brands with actual sale and original prices, like Caraway at $399 vs $499. That's what I need for price comparison.
- "Up to $300 off Breville Espresso Machines" is specific and matches a real purchase I'm weighing.
- The Thanksgiving menu block with the Le Creuset Dutch oven feels seasonal and ties to hosting in a renovated kitchen.
- The Crate Card block ("10% back in Reward Dollars") speaks to my highest-rewards-card habit, even if the math is vague.

## What's weak

- The vacuum hero with an unrelated 20%-off furniture banner above it muddles the message. Pottery Barn's maroon "Extra 20% off" block has the same problem.
- The KitchenAid mixer repeats, and the grid overlaps with yesterday's send. That's the lamp-in-three-brands problem inside one brand.
- It's too many asks: Black Friday signup, app download, design appointment, store locator and credit card. Nothing carries the email.
- The "romanticize your fall mornings" JURA coffee block has no price and no offer, and the watch-the-video button is a tangent.
- The legal footer is dense and small, and the rewards terms are buried in it. I can't tell the real offer from the small print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the kitchen hero, such as a Breville or KitchenAid image, and drop the furniture banner. The subject promises kitchen and the top of the email should deliver it.
- 2. Cut the repeat products and trim to one grid plus one editorial block, then a single CTA.
- 3. Put one clear ask where the Black Friday signup and app pitch are, instead of four.
- 4. Add a delivery or in-stock line on the appliances. For a renovation I need to know when a range or mixer lands.
- 5. Space out sends. Two near-identical kitchen emails in two days is a lot, even for someone in the category.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a reasonable responsive and Outlook scaffold. The main confirmed problems are about 26 images flagged with no alt text and a viewport that disables zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified from this capture, and the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as tracking or click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations, redirect chains or status codes are verified.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `&n=5` requests (LiveRamp) are in the body. There are also two Movable Ink pixels: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e0779ace-…`. These are intentional tracking beacons, not defects.
- **Identifier in URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa278…` value on the Movable Ink URLs shares its prefix with the `s=56aaa278…` value on the rlcdn pixels. That points to the same recipient-level identifier being passed to a third party. It's a privacy-review point, not a rendering bug.
- No broken links are confirmed in the visible data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 26 images.**
  - 19 are content or dynamic images:
    - 11 static images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (e.g. `f55586e1-…jpg`, `67069d24-…jpg`) and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
    - 1 hero from `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F1102%5FCB%5FKitchenPromos%5FSocial%5Fv2…`.
    - 7 Movable Ink `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - The other 7 are tracking pixels: the 5 rlcdn GIFs, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e0779ace-…`.
  - The pixels should carry `alt=""` and are low severity. If the content images have no alt, image-blocked clients show nothing for them and screen readers read the filename.
  - The QA check doesn't distinguish a missing attribute from an empty one, so the dynamic `rp` images may already have `alt=""`. That should be confirmed.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is also set in several places.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - There are duplicate `<meta charset>`/`http-equiv Content-Type` declarations and duplicate `format-detection` metas.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the head.
- `lang="en"`, the Outlook VML/MSO namespaces and the mobile breakpoint at 460px are present, so no issue there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The truncated HTML means the body wasn't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a proven sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are outside the truncated source. They can't be confirmed or faulted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No UTM parameters or landing URLs can be assessed. The 66 click-tracked links were skipped, and the visible URLs are images and pixels. Landing-page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 19 content and dynamic images, and `alt=""` to the 7 tracking pixels. Confirm whether the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*` images already have an empty alt.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` where it isn't needed.
3. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` from the original mailbox. That should show the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers that the relay may have dropped.
4. Resolve the tracking redirects with a follow-redirect probe, to capture final URLs, UTM params and status codes.
5. Run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address and any merge-token leaks.
6. Cleanup: escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate metas and delete the stray `IMPUT HERE` comment.
