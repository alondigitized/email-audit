# 💙 Up to 70% off + Labor Day savings ⭐️ Bedding & more
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Pottery Barn email this week and it's the same burgundy "Up to 70% off" banner I already screenshotted twice, but this one's a little more useful because there's an actual "Recommended just for you" module halfway down with a curtain rod, a rug, two beds, a swivel chair, and a dining table — that's the first personalized-feeling content out of this whole run. Still, the top third is identical to Monday's send: same crop of the bedding shot, same maroon category grid, same "Shop Now" button placement. I'm renovating two houses right now, so a platform bed and a curtain rod actually land, but I've seen this exact "70% off" banner enough times that my eye just slides past it now — banner blindness is real.
- The Halloween Shop insert threw me — I'm mid-renovation on bedding and beds, not decorating porches with ghosts, and it eats real estate that could've stayed on furniture. I'd open this one over the identical Monday/Tuesday sends purely because of the recommended-products block, but I'm not clicking the hero — I'd scroll straight to the beds. Not forwarding this to anyone; my wife already gets the same five PB emails I do.

## What's working

- The "Recommended just for you" section with the Cayman and Farmhouse platform beds actually matches what I'm shopping for right now.
- Category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs, etc.) is a fast way to jump straight to what I need without scrolling the whole email.
- "10% Back in Rewards" with the Pottery Barn credit card is relevant — I run everything through my highest-rewards card, so this is the one email element actually built for someone like me.
- Layout is clean, no broken images or overlapping text — better production quality than some of the CB2 sends I've gotten this month.

## What's weak

- Same burgundy "Up to 70% off" hero as 2026-09-02's "Labor Day deals" email and 2026-09-01's "End-of-summer savings" send — literally the same font and banner treatment three sends running.
- The Halloween Shop banner is a jarring detour in an email otherwise about bedding and furniture sale — feels like two campaigns stapled together.
- "100s of new deals added!" is vague filler copy sitting right under the hero — doesn't tell me what's actually new.
- No swatch/sample-ordering CTA anywhere, which matters when I'm trying to match finishes across two houses — Crate & Barrel and West Elm at least gesture at fabric/finish info on some sends, this one doesn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the repeat hero. Lead with the "Recommended for you" beds and rug instead of the fourth identical burgundy banner this week — that's the section actually doing work.
- 2. Cut the Halloween insert entirely or move it to a separate, clearly-seasonal send — it doesn't belong wedged between a furniture sale and rewards messaging.
- 3. Replace "100s of new deals added!" with a real number or category callout tied to what I've browsed (bedding, beds).
- 4. Surface the 10% rewards card offer higher — that's genuinely differentiating and it's buried near the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns on the beds you've been eyeing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cayman and Farmhouse platform beds, now in the sale`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 10% back in rewards on furniture this week`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud template with heavy tracking instrumentation; core rendering markup is sound but header-level authentication/unsubscribe signals were not captured, alt text is absent site-wide, and two legacy assets still load over plain HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary nav link and 58 total tracking/click-redirect links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` (ExactTarget click-tracking) — not individually probed per QA config, but structurally consistent with standard redirect wrapping.
- Multiple parallel tracking pixels/beacons present: `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1`, `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/fc71bff5-e6cf-44c5-a67b-0e873d74473d?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=...`. The `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel is served over **http://**, not https.
- Recipient email address is exposed in plaintext in tracking URLs (`sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, and in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — a PII-in-URL pattern worth flagging even though it's vendor-standard for ExactTarget.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 0 of the identified `<img>` elements carry `alt` text — 40+ instances flagged by QA, including all hero/product slice images (`slices_01.jpg` – `slices_18.jpg`), product photography (e.g. `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`), and tracking/spacer pixels. Screen readers will announce nothing for meaningful product imagery.
- Two images load over insecure HTTP, which mixed-content-aware mail clients may block or flag: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`.

- Stray `header()` PHP-style function calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg')`, `Cache-Control`, `Pragma: no-cache`) appear inside a `<style>` block in `<head>` — these are inert as CSS/will be ignored by mail clients, but indicate leftover/misplaced server-side code accidentally baked into the template output.

- Personalization is present via recipient-specific tracking identifiers embedded in pixel/link URLs (`sv_sveme=`, `email=` params, `mi_mid=01a0626b-...`) rather than in on-page copy.

- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat.
