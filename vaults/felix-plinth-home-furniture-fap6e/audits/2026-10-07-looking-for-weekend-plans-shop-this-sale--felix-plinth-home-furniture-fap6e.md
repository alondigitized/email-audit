# Looking for weekend plans? Shop this sale.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. I've lost count, but it's easily the sixteenth send in about a week. The hero is a big "THE LONG WEEKEND SALE IS ON, UP TO 60% OFF" over a bed with a velvet headboard, and I got the point right away. The problem is that it's the same sale. On 10/05 it was "TIME'S RUNNING OUT" for the Fall Design Sale. On 10/06 it was "WE RARELY DO THIS," which was this same semi-annual upholstery event. Now the upholstery event is the second panel again, with a new holiday name on top. I bought none of it the first two times, and a weekend label doesn't change that.
- The email looks good. The bedroom hero is the kind of thing I'd screenshot into my Pinterest folder. But "Looking for weekend plans? Shop this sale" is a weak line for someone who is renovating two houses. Painting a guest room isn't a weekend plan, it's a project. I'd also like to know if the 60% is on the bed pictured or on a few clearance rugs. The only info I get is a footnote I'd have to squint at.
- I'd skim this, save the bedroom hero image, and not click through today. I'd forward it to nobody. My contractor doesn't care about a CB2 bed.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "UP TO 60% OFF" is huge, and the "Yes, really." line has some personality.
- The bedding and velvet-headboard photo is strong, and it matches the moody, textured look I'm going for in the primary.
- Each section has its own "SHOP THE SALE" button, so the CTA is easy to find.
- The upholstery panel with the green velvet sofa is the most useful block for me. It's a real category, it has a real number (25%), and the sofa is the kind I'd actually buy.
- The footer mentions 10% back in Rewards Dollars on CB2 and Crate & Barrel purchases. As someone who optimizes card points, I noticed it.

## What's weak

- This is the same sale for the third time this week. The upholstery event is a straight repeat of 10/06's "WE RARELY DO THIS," so the urgency doesn't feel real.
- "UP TO 60%" tells me nothing. Is it on beds, rugs, or leftover decor? There's no product, no price, and no sale tag on any item.
- The free shipping panel is hard to read. "SHIPPING ON US" is thin white text over a busy photo of cocktail glasses, and it overlaps the glasses. The "$99 or more" line is easy to miss. That's also a bad fit for furniture, where I want to know about white-glove delivery and lead times.
- The 60% and 25% offers sit one on top of the other with no explanation of which products qualify. I can't tell if they stack.
- The fine print is a huge block of tiny text at the bottom, and I can't read it without zooming. Nothing about it makes me trust the offer more.
- Nothing in it speaks to a renovation: no swatches, no room planning, no free design service. The 10/06 "Things of quality. Expert design." email actually did that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put real products in the hero or right below it, each with a was/now price. A sale without a price isn't believable to me.
- 2. Make the three offers clearly different (bedroom, upholstery, shipping), or send only one per email. I can't tell which of the three I'm supposed to act on.
- 3. Cut the send frequency, or at least don't repeat the same event under a new name. Pottery Barn did the same thing with its Christmas shop this week, and Crate & Barrel Kids sent three emails in two days. Article is better here. Its Clearance Event email gave me one straight offer after weeks of quiet mood boards.
- 4. Fix the shipping panel with solid text on a clean background, and say what it covers for large items.
- 5. Add a swatch-and-sample or design-services link, which would be useful for the two renovations.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The CB2 email is a standard XHTML table layout with a raw `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token that wasn't resolved, which is the one hard failure. The rest are mostly warnings: heavy third-party tracking pixels served over `http://` and missing alt text. The QA pass rate is 43%.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 57 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status are unverified.
- Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` through `pub10.php`. The UCM pixel `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...` is also `http://`. Clients that block mixed content or insecure sources won't fire them, so tracking is lost there. Apple Mail and Gmail proxy or rewrite them anyway.
- Two first-party beacons are present: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/d624dc41-...?mi_cid=...`. Both lack alt text and are expected to be 1x1 pixels.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Three content images on `image.mail.cb2.com` have no alt text: `d040ff78-e974-4883-a321-20a08e5e14c3.jpg`, `89537ff7-1e36-4326-8b49-b933154f98a9.jpg`, and `acb86340-41fe-4efe-8aea-73256b4615a4.jpg`. With images blocked or a screen reader, that content is lost. If these are hero or promo images, the sale message disappears.
- The tracking pixels (Dotomi, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`) also lack alt text. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so they're marked decorative.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm `role="presentation"` on layout tables or the dark-mode handling.
- What I could see in the visible markup is fine: `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and MSO conditional blocks.
- The `@font-face` rules point at `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com` web fonts. Most clients ignore them, so the fallback is Helvetica or sans-serif.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** a raw `%%reviews_skulist%%` token shipped unresolved. It looks like an Oracle Responsys or Marketing Cloud style product-reviews placeholder. The dynamic-content call failed or was never populated, so the token is likely visible text or sits inside an attribute or URL. Confirm where it appears in the source. If it's in a link, that link is broken.
- No other unresolved tokens were detected.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a problem. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. This is also likely a relay artifact. The sender domain `mail.cb2.com` is a subdomain, which is a good sign, but nothing here proves alignment.
- The visible footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) is not in the truncated source, so I can't verify CAN-SPAM body requirements.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
No issues found that I can confirm. All 57 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so I can't check UTM parameters or whether the landing pages match the sale. Resolve the redirects to verify them.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge failure before the next send. Add a fallback and a pre-send check that blocks any message containing `%%...%%`.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop the legacy cookie-sync calls (`pub1`–`pub10`) if they're no longer needed.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the three `image.mail.cb2.com` images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
