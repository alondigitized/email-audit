# HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RESTOCK + 2 Sales Ending Soon
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth CB2 send since 10/30, and the subject line is doing two jobs. "Guest Ready Sale, up to 40% off, ends tomorrow" sits at the top. Right under it is a big "Restock Alert" hero. I came for the restock, because for a two-house renovation, in-stock sectionals and beds matter more to me than a discount. But the sale banner got there first and the restock hero has to share the space. It feels like two emails stacked on top of each other.
- The restock content itself is good. Beds, dining chairs, sectionals, coffee tables and storage each get a tall, moody lifestyle image. It's a Pinterest-folder email, and CB2 is better at that than the discount shouting I got on 11/01 ("Final days of the Warehouse Sale") and 11/03 ("2 SALES END TOMORROW"). Still, the tight cadence wears me out. The "ends tomorrow" line is the same urgency they ran yesterday, so I stop believing it.
- Would I click? Probably once, on the sectional, to check lead time. Would I forward it? No. I'd screenshot the canopy bed for my partner and nothing else.

## What's working

- The "Restock Alert / Your favourites, back in stock for a limited time" hero is exactly what I want. A sectional that's actually available beats a hypothetical one.
- The category tiles (Beds, Dining Chairs, Sectionals, Coffee Tables, Storage) each have a separate SHOP NOW button. I can go straight to what I'm sourcing.
- The photography is warm and consistent. The walnut, tan and bouclé palette helps me plan a cohesive room.
- "So much ships free, 1000+ items at your doorstep" speaks to my furniture shipping worry. It's a bit vague, but it's there.
- The footer has the CB2 app, 10% back in Rewards Dollars on the card, and 15% off for signing up. For someone who optimizes card rewards, that math catches my eye.

## What's weak

- The "Guest Ready Sale up to 40% off" banner and the "Restock Alert" hero compete. I couldn't tell which one the email was about.
- The "Ends Tomorrow" urgency is stale. It's the fourth sale email in a week, and 11/03's "2 SALES END TOMORROW" already said the same thing.
- The in-stock tiles show no prices, no lead times and no "ships in X weeks." Pottery Barn's "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner does that job better.
- The white text on the beige and cream image tiles is hard to read, especially "DINING CHAIRS" and "COFFEE TABLES" at phone size. The small SHOP NOW buttons are also tiny tap targets.
- There's no mention of swatches, which CB2 pushed on 11/03, and no showroom content.
- The 15% sign-up offer is aimed at new subscribers, and I'm already a member. The email treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Lead with the restock, and make the sale a slim strip above it. Or run them as two separate sends.
- 2. Put a lead-time line on each in-stock tile, for example "Sectionals: ships in 2-3 weeks."
- 3. Add one price anchor per tile ("from $X") so I can compare it to Article or Wayfair.
- 4. Use dark or solid-background text on the tile titles, and make the SHOP NOW buttons bigger.
- 5. Give the sale's end date a specific time, not just "ends tomorrow."
- 6. Remove the new-subscriber 15% block for people who are already subscribed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back in stock: the sectional, beds & dining chairs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `In stock now, ships in weeks. Sale ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sectionals, canopy beds and coffee tables, all ready to ship. Up to 40% off ends tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorites are back. Free shipping on 1000+ items.`

## Technical Audit

## CB2: "HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RESTOCK + 2 Sales Ending Soon"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering-breaking defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads 11 cleartext `http://` Dotomi tracking pixels and has 13 content images with no alt text. The HTML was truncated and the click links were not probed, so the unsubscribe link, UTM parameters and landing pages could not be checked.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
  - Clients and proxies may block or rewrite them, and they can trigger mixed-content warnings. The Dotomi cookie-sync and retargeting pixels will probably not fire.
- **Unverified links:** 67 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains (HTTP probe skipped), so none of their destinations were checked. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Other pixels:** `mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/0b89d77c-…` are HTTPS and carry `mi_u` and `mi_cid` params. No issue other than the missing alt text below.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (13):** all are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. The file names are:
  - `27a7a977-…png`
  - `61ac2c43-…jpg`
  - `8438a788-…jpg`
  - `c179bd8b-…jpg`

  - `55fbf30d-…jpg`
  
  If these are linked product or promo images, images-off clients and screen readers get no label for them.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (13):** the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/…`. These should carry `alt=""` and `width=1 height=1`. They are lower severity than the content images.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing blocked:** the global rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` disables text-size adjustment.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** the head still contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, an XHTML doctype, VML/Office namespaces and MSO conditional blocks are present.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL:** raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is in the delivered HTML. This looks like a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style personalization string that never resolved. A dynamic product-reviews block is probably empty or showing literal text, and the token may also be exposed in an attribute or URL. The cause is likely a failed data extension or content lookup at send time.

### 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers at the source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured, so authentication is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` is aligned with the From address.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated. Nothing is flagged.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 67 click-redirect links were skipped and the truncated HTML shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. No issues are claimed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%` (high):** check the data extension or content block. Add a fallback so the reviews module is suppressed if the lookup fails, and add a pre-send check that fails on any `%%…%%` token.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS (medium):** switch all 11 to `https://`, or drop the redundant `pub1`–`pub10` cookie-sync pixels.
3. **Add alt text (medium):** give the 13 content images descriptive alt text, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` (low):** also stop disabling text-size adjustment globally.
5. **Confirm the headers (medium):** verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from a direct-to-inbox test, not through the relay.
6. **Extend the QA run (low):** resolve the click-tracking redirects to check UTM parameters and landing pages, and run QA on the full, untruncated HTML.
