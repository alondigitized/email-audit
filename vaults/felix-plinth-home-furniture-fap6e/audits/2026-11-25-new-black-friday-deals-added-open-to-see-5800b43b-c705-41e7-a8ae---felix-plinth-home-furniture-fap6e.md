## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template from the Movable Ink/Salesforce-style stack (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). It renders defensibly, but about 37 images have no alt text, and several head-level choices hurt accessibility. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Untested links:** 79 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are five LiveRamp pixels. Each carries a long hashed identifier in `s=`. This is a privacy and deliverability surface, since some filters score third-party pixels.
- **Repeated tracking images:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with the same `mi_u` identifier. Only 1 request is needed per open.
- **Other tracking images:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1cc80cf1-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are further open and personalization pixels.
- **Image hosting:** Content images load from two hosts, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` (ESP CDN) and `s7d5.scene7.com` (Adobe Scene7). Two hosts add points of failure.
- **Encoded filenames:** Scene7 paths use `%5F` for underscores, for example `2026%5F1124%5FCBK%5FBF%5FNewDealsAdded%5F02.gif`. They are valid but unusual. The email also references `2026%5F1105%5FCBK%5FPersonalizedGiftShop…`, a 1105 (Nov 5) asset in a 1124 send. That is probably a reused module rather than an error.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 37 `<img>` elements were flagged. Examples:
  - Content-bearing JPGs and GIFs, such as `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `d3119bec-…jpg` and `f8c48ff1-…gif`. These need descriptive alt text. With images off, or for screen readers, the deal content is lost.
  - The animated GIF `2026%5F1124%5FCBK%5FBF%5FNewDealsAdded%5F02.gif`. It needs alt text carrying the offer, and its first frame must carry the key message.
  - The spacer `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, and the pixels `rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `o.gif` and `dv…/1cc80cf1…`. These should be `alt=""` so they are skipped by assistive tech. It is unclear from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or only empty.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{…}` rule and on `body`. This disables iOS text scaling.
- **Duplicate and conflicting meta tags:**
  - `format-detection` appears in 4 declarations, one combined (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`) and three separate ones.
  - `charset` is declared twice (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`), and the second is unclosed in an XHTML doctype.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype and not descriptive of the content.
- **Leftover template text:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled placeholder, and "IMPUT" is misspelled.
- **Duplicated CSS:** Rules for `.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass` and `table td` are defined in two `<style>` blocks. This adds weight but causes no failure.
- **Strengths:** `lang="en"` is set, and the VML/Office namespaces and MSO conditional blocks (`PixelsPerInch`) are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the provided HTML. The only personalization is the `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` identifiers in the pixel URLs, and they are populated. The truncated portion cannot confirm the rest of the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** Not found. The QA check notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Not found, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, and alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Body unsubscribe and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so they cannot be verified.
- **Recommended re-test:** Capture raw headers directly, not through the relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 79 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated HTML shows no final destinations or UTM parameters. The subject promises "New Black Friday deals added", but no landing page was checked against it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the animated GIF and the hero JPGs. Use `alt=""` on the spacer and all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and avoid `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Collapse the four `format-detection` metas into one, drop the duplicate charset meta, and escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`). Delete the leftover "IMPUT HERE" comment.
4. Consolidate the 6 identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels and review whether the 5 `rlcdn.com` pixels are needed, given the privacy and spam-filter exposure.
5. Re-run QA with raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Re-run the link probe against the final redirect destinations. Verify UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the Black Friday offer.
