# Tricky entryway? We’ve got solutions
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually lands for me. "Tricky entryway? We've got solutions" is the exact problem I'm dealing with right now — the entry at the second house is a mess of coats, boots, and nowhere to put keys, and I'm actively shopping for it. The Design Desk framing continues to work for me the same way it did on the rug email from this same brand three days ago — a design pro walking me through a real project beat, not just another product grid.
- That said, by the time I scroll past the fourth "shop this room" module I start losing the plot. Entryway furniture, then decor, then a rug (again — didn't I already get "picking the perfect rug" from this same Design Desk three days ago?), then wall art, then a "performance rug" for high-traffic areas, then a moodboard pitch, then three ways to book an appointment, then reviews, then a random "we love these for you" grid, then a rewards card pitch, then an app download push. This is five emails smashed into one.
- I'd click into the entryway furniture section specifically — that's the actual hook and it's honest to the subject line. I wouldn't forward this to my wife, though; it's too sprawling for that, and half of it isn't about the entryway at all by the back half.

## What's working

- The subject line and hero "create a warm welcome" + entryway furniture CTA is directly on-target for a real project I'm mid-decision on right now.
- The Design Desk positioning (design pro help, free appointment, moodboard tool) keeps showing up as this brand's strongest differentiator — Article's "organized entryway" email touched the same nerve but didn't offer a human on the other end like this does.
- The "3 ways to meet with a design desk pro" section is genuinely useful — home visit, in-store, online, all laid out clearly with icons.
- Product photography is consistent, warm, editorial — feels like a real house, not a catalog dump.
- Star ratings on hero pieces (Antique White Terracotta Vase, etc.) give me quick trust signals without digging.

## What's weak

- This is way too long for one email — entryway furniture bleeds into rugs, wall art, "performance rugs," moodboards, appointments, reviews, a totally unrelated "we love these for you" grid, then rewards, then app download. Pick one job and do it.
- The rug section ("roll out a performance rug") is the same beat as the "Picking the perfect rug" Design Desk email I got three days ago — I already made a rug decision off that send, I don't need it recycled into this one.
- "we love these for you" at the bottom has zero connection to entryways — looks like a leftover template block, not curated for me.
- No visible discount or offer anywhere in this whole email — it's all full-price browsing, which is a stretch when Wayfair and CB2 are both running actual sale headlines this same week.
- The rewards blurb ("it all adds up... $500") is buried at the very bottom after I've already scrolled through eight sections — should've been higher if they want me to notice it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut this to one job.** Lead with entryway furniture + the Design Desk pro pitch, kill the rug/wall-art/moodboard sprawl, and save those for their own sends — you're already sending me a rug email this week separately.
- 2. **Move the rewards card math up**, not buried below reviews — that's a real lever for me since I chase points.
- 3. **Add a real offer.** Even 15% off entryway furniture or free swatches would separate this from pure inspiration content — right now there's nothing time-bound to act on.
- 4. **Drop "we love these for you"** entirely or actually personalize it to entryway-adjacent categories — as shown it reads like an unrelated insert.
- 5. **Cross-check cadence against the rug email** — don't repeat the same rug hero within a 3-day window from the same Design Desk franchise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fix your entryway before winter — meet a Design Desk pro free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your entryway, solved: furniture picks + free design help`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book a free appointment or shop entryway pieces we picked for tight spaces`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Storage, seating, and a mirror that actually fits — start here`
