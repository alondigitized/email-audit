# Labor Day ENDS SOON: Up to 60% off + earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Delete, immediately. This is the third or fourth Crate & Kids send I've logged today, and this one's not even trying to be different — same double-Rewards banner, same crib/bassinet grid, same "up to 60% off" scattered across nine different product categories. I don't have a baby, I'm not shopping for a baby, and no amount of rewards math changes that. Kelly Wearstler bedding and rugs mixed into a kids catalog just tells me this list is undifferentiated — they're blasting anyone with a Crate & Barrel account regardless of household stage.
- Nothing here touches what I'm actually doing right now, which is furnishing two houses' worth of adult rooms. Compare that to the Crate & Barrel kitchen email that scored a 7 with me — espresso machine hero, real category match, felt like it was built for someone renovating a home. This one feels like it got auto-generated from a template and mail-merged to my whole address book.
- Wouldn't forward it, wouldn't click, mark as read and move on — like I did with the last two nursery blasts this week.

## What's working

- The "we love these for you" grid at the bottom at least attempts personalization with a mix of nursery gear and non-baby furniture, even if it's clearly not dialed in.
- Product photography is clean and consistent — the chandelier and rug shots look like they'd sell fine to the right buyer.
- The $500 sweepstakes entry is a mildly interesting hook I haven't seen from this brand's other sends this week.

## What's weak

- Baby furniture, crib bedding, and bassinets — zero relevance to my life, same as the last two Crate & Kids emails I deleted today.
- "Up to 60% off" repeated across seven separate section headers with no single clear hero offer — my eye doesn't land anywhere specific.
- Double Rewards banner appears twice, top and mid-email, which just reads as filler when the actual content underneath doesn't apply to me.
- Nothing about swatches, samples, shipping timelines, or showroom pickup — the stuff that'd actually move me on furniture.
- This is a straight rerun of the "Ends soon! Double Rewards + up to 60% off" subject I already saw twice today from this same sub-brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household signal — if I've never clicked a kids item, stop sending me the kids catalog and route me to Crate & Barrel proper.
- 2. Pick one hero offer and lead with it instead of stacking six "up to 60%" claims across categories — Article's "up to 40% off ends Monday" banner is a cleaner model of this.
- 3. If cross-selling adult furniture inside a kids email, put that section at the top, not buried two-thirds down.
- 4. Vary the subject line — I've now seen near-identical "ends soon / double rewards" copy from this brand three times in one day, which just trains me to ignore the whole list.
