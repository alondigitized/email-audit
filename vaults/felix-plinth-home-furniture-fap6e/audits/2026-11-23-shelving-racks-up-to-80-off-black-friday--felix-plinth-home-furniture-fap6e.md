# SHELVING & RACKS 🎉 UP TO 80% OFF 🎉 BLACK FRIDAY 🎉 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and I'm numb. This is the same Black Friday blast I've been getting since 11/09, and it came in right behind this morning's "DURABLE RUGS worth the click" and yesterday's "BARS & BAR SETS." The subject promised shelving and racks. I opened it, and the first tile is a black metal storage rack, so that's a small win. After that the email drifts into a bunk bed, a shower, a wine fridge, a recliner and a Sealy banner. It's a catalog dump.
- It does have things I'd use. There's a free-shipping bar, a code for 10% off in the app, and category tiles up to 80% off. But I'm renovating two houses and I need a coordinated room, not 40 unrelated products. The pitch of "picked just for you" doesn't hold when the wine fridge appears twice and the bunk bed is in there. I'd skim it and archive it. I might save the faucet and sink tiles to my Pinterest-ish folder, since I'm doing kitchens. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The shelving rack is the first product I see, so the subject isn't a total bait-and-switch.
- "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE" is a clear purple band. For furniture shipping that matters to me.
- Each product tile shows "Was / NOW" pricing and star ratings, so I get social proof and a real discount at a glance.
- The faucet, sink and farmhouse-sink tiles speak to my kitchen renovation.
- The "EXTRA 10% OFF IN THE APP, code APP10" band is a concrete stackable offer I can do the rewards math on.

## What's weak

- "Picked just for you" is hollow. It's the same wine fridge, bunk bed and recliner the whole list gets, and the shelving I was promised is one tile.
- The hierarchy is flat. Eight category tiles with equal weight, all saying "up to X% off," mean no single offer wins.
- The tiles have no room-level coordination. There's no color palette, no swatch or sample content and no showroom tie-in, which is what a two-house renovator wants.
- The footer "Your Home, These Picks" repeats pergolas and rugs from this morning's "DURABLE RUGS" send. It feels recycled.
- The product price text is tiny at phone width, and I'd have to zoom to read the "Was/NOW" lines.
- The Wayfair Rewards icon is a footer afterthought with no points math, and I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject deliver. If it says shelving and racks, fill the top two rows with shelving and racks, with real price points and one hero.
- 2. Replace "picked just for you" with an honest label like "Storage & shelving" and a tight grid of 6 products.
- 3. Cut the category-tile wall to 2-3 tiles and give one a bigger offer.
- 4. Put the free shipping and the APP10 code in the first screen, not midway down.
- 5. Add reviews counts, dimensions and a "ships by" date to the hero shelving tile.
- 6. Cap cadence. I get a send every day, so suppress repeats of products I've already seen this week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Heavy-duty racks from $89 + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 10% off in the app with code APP10`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shelving, storage and kitchen finds that ship free`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive `@media` rules and per-recipient tracking parameters on every link. The QA run found no hard failures. The issues are missing alt text, about 20 plain-HTTP tracking pixels, and header checks the relay may have hidden. The HTML provided was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** 12 links were probed and 67 skipped. Eight probed links returned 429 (rate limiting), for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale") and `https://www.wayfair.com/?...` ("Wayfair"). A 429 means the server throttled the probe, so these links are unverified, not failing. Four product and clearance URLs were not probed because the 70s time budget ran out, so they are also unverified.
- **Plain-HTTP links and pixels:**
  - The banner link is `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`. It depends on a redirect to HTTPS, which adds a hop and exposes the tracking parameters on the first request.
  - 20 open and impression pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=4456ea32-…`. Clients may block or proxy-rewrite these, which would undercount impressions.
  - The app deep-link's fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, also HTTP.
- **Tracking pixel at `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`** (the `eventType=EmailOpen` pixel) is HTTPS, but it has no alt text (see section 3).
- **Inconsistent campaign parameters on the OneLink URL.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/...?c=Triggered&...&source=batch&batchid=3`: `c=Triggered` conflicts with `source=batch`. This is a batch send labelled as triggered, which will misattribute AppsFlyer reporting.
- **Two OneLink URLs for the same CTA ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App")** differ only in `_eml` (`edc125de-…` vs `c92ca695-…`). Per-link `_eml` values are expected, but the two copies split click attribution for one CTA.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 19 of the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` pixel.** The same warning appears on 11 `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` review-star images, and on these content banners:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jpg`, which is also the banner's only link content (the probe reported link text `[image: …]`).
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-BF-AppOffer.jpg`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-EverythingShip…`
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`
- **Linked images with no alt text are the real accessibility defect.** Screen readers and image-blocking clients get no link text for the banner or for the star images that link to product pages (for example `wayfair-product-L136-K~id-AS71028556.html`).
- **Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`** (decorative), not no alt attribute at all.
- **Custom font:** `@font-face "Sofia"` loads a `.woff` with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`. Most clients ignore web fonts, so a fallback stack is required. I couldn't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **The HTML wrapper sets `width:100% !important; margin:0 auto !important` on `<html>`.** I found no rendering failure in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the probed URLs. The recipient and send IDs (`_emr`, `_eml`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`) are all populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the relay may not be capturing them. The missing headers can't be confirmed as a sender-side defect. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders, so this needs a check against raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a capture limitation, not a finding about the sender. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified** because the HTML was truncated. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it is listed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are used on any link.** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `refid=MKTEML_143933`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16788`, `wfcs=cs7`. It is consistent across links, so this is an observation about analytics compatibility, not a defect. Any GA-style reporting on the destination would need the `refid` and `csnid` mapped.
- **Banner links go to the generic `/daily-sales`,** not a shelving or racks landing page. I couldn't verify whether `/daily-sales` shows shelving. The subject line promises "SHELVING & RACKS".
- **Product links don't match the shelving theme.** The probed URLs include a wall-mount rainfall shower system (`…L6123-K~id-AS83018705.html`, `sku=EEBS1007`) and refrigerator and ice-maker listings (the Frigidaire and Simzlife URLs). Whether the email's products are shelving can't be confirmed from the truncated HTML.
- **The app deep link falls back correctly** (`af_dp=wayfairapp://home`, `af_web_dp=…/the-wayfair-app`) but over HTTP (see section 2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the linked banner and CTA images and to the star-rating images (for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`). Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Serve `li.wayfair.com/imp`, the banner URL `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` and `af_web_dp` over HTTPS.
3. Fix `c=Triggered` on the OneLink URL so it matches the batch send.
4. Merge the duplicate app CTAs, or confirm that the separate `_eml` values are intentional.
5. Re-run the link probe for the 4 unprobed URLs and the 8 that returned 429, with a slower rate or a longer budget.
6. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, address and any fallback font stack.
