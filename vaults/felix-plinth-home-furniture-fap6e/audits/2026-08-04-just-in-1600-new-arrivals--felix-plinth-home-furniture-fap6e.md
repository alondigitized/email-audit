# Just in! 1600+ new arrivals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- "1600+ new arrivals" is the retail equivalent of someone dumping their entire closet on the bed and saying "pick something." I'm two houses deep into renovation and I need curation, not volume — the whole reason I bookmarked Pottery Barn's "Get the look: the new traditional dining room" send from yesterday (8/10) is that it told me a story about one room. This is the opposite: furniture, armchairs, pillows, bedding, lighting, decor, home office, tabletop, baskets, all crammed into one scroll with zero narrative thread except "here's plaid now."
- The hero shot is genuinely nice — warm, styled breakfast nook, moody window light, the kind of image that makes me want to screenshot it into my Pinterest-adjacent folder. But then three screens later I hit "The Halloween Shop — Enter if you dare" with a skeleton propped on a porch, and it's the same whiplash I flagged on the actual Halloween send yesterday (5/10). You can't sell me cozy heathered-chenille fall styling and skeleton decor in the same breath.
- I'd skim the categories, maybe tap "Shop Bedding" since that plaid quilt caught my eye for the guest room, but I'm not clicking "See What's New" — that's a homepage dump, not a decision. Wouldn't forward it. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero room scene (dining nook, natural light, styled table) is on-brand and matches the room-story format that scored well for PB yesterday.
- "Recommended Just For You" is a real personalization module with actual product tiles, not filler — Wayfair and Crate & Barrel rarely bother with this.
- Zero render bugs — every image loaded, no overlapping text, no broken swatches.
- The rewards credit card block (10% back) and Affirm financing are relevant to someone financing two renovations at once, even buried near the footer.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere — no %, no $, no free shipping threshold. Compare that to CB2's "60% off clearance" block or Crate & Barrel's gift-card sweepstakes hook; this email gives me nothing to act on beyond curiosity.
- "1600+ new arrivals" is a number, not a reason to click — it signals "we didn't have time to curate this week," the opposite of the dining-room story send.
- The Halloween Shop insert sandwiched between "Shop Tabletop" and "Design Crew" breaks the cozy-fall mood I was just sold on two scrolls up — same complaint I had about yesterday's dedicated Halloween send, now recycled inside a different email.
- Zero pricing or dimensions on any product tile — for someone furnishing two houses, "Standard Curtain Rod & Wall Bracket" with no price is a dead end, I'd have to click through just to know if it's in budget.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the 1600+ dump framing entirely — split this into 3-4 curated sends by room ("New fall bedding," "New for the home office") the way yesterday's dining room email worked. Volume isn't a hook, specificity is.
- 2. Pull the Halloween Shop into its own dedicated send (they already have one) instead of stapling it mid-catalog — it undercuts the fall/traditional mood the rest of the email just built.
- 3. Add price or "starting at $X" under at least the hero and Recommended tiles — I need a budget signal before I click, not after.
- 4. Give me one actual offer — even a modest "free shipping over $99" would move this from "browse if bored" to "act now."

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn — "Just in! 1600+ new arrivals"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via standard table-based HTML with Bronto/Bluecore-style tracking infrastructure; core structure is sound, but authentication/unsubscribe headers weren't captured, alt text is absent on all content images, and one CSS block contains leaked server-side PHP code.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 56 tracking/redirect links detected (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/{{uuid}}?mi_mid=...`) — all skipped by automated HTTP probe as expected for redirect domains; not independently verified as resolving to live pages.
- Open-tracking pixel via `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over **http://**, not https — mixed-content risk and may be blocked by mail clients that enforce TLS.
- Second open-tracking beacon via `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` (Amobee/Turn) — https, no issues.
- **PII exposure in URL query strings**: the recipient's full email address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) is passed in cleartext as a GET parameter on multiple third-party tracking/pixel URLs (`svopn.potterybarn.com`, `coreemail.html?email=...`). Any of these domains' server logs, CDN logs, or referrer headers on a subsequent redirect could leak the address.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **34 images missing `alt` text** across hero slices, product grid tiles, footer banners, and the credit-card/footer modules (full list in QA output). Screen readers and image-blocked clients (default in Outlook) will show nothing in place of these.
- **3 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`**: 
  - `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — note this is a **West Elm domain spacer asset inside a Pottery Barn email**, likely a stale shared-template artifact rather than a live rendering bug, but worth flagging as cross-brand leakage in the codebase.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` (dated 2022 asset, still in rotation)
  
  Non-HTTPS images are commonly blocked or flagged by Gmail/Outlook image proxies.
- **Leaked server-side code inside `<style>` block**: the CLIENT styling block contains literal PHP, not CSS:
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Cache-Control: post-check=0, pre-check=0", false);
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
  ```
  This is invalid CSS and inert in rendering, but its presence indicates a template-generation bug — a PHP image-serving script's source was pasted/concatenated into the email's `<style>` tag rather than executed server-side.
- Standard MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media` breakpoints (480px, 650px, 700px) are correctly present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email merge token resolved correctly and consistently across tracking pixels and the customer-service link (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — no unresolved `{{token}}` or `%%FIELD%%` placeholders found in the truncated source.
- No other visible first-name/loyalty-tier personalization tokens present in the truncated HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — could be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a true absence; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay; cannot verify sender authentication from available data.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — not a compliance requirement but reduces referrer leakage on outbound clicks (mitigates part of the PII concern in §2, though it doesn't prevent it since the address is in the URL itself, not just the referrer).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All primary CTAs route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrapper rather than direct `potterybarn.com` links — expected for an ESP click-tracking setup, but means UTM/campaign parameters on the final landing page cannot be verified without following the redirect chain (skipped by design in this probe).
- One direct (non-redirected) link present: `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` — reaches the brand domain directly with the email param appended, consistent with a preference-center/customer-service deep link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` attributes to all 34 flagged images (empty `alt=""` acceptable for purely decorative slices; descriptive text for product images).
2. Migrate the three `http://` image sources to `https://`, and confirm whether the West Elm–domain spacer (`edm.westelm.com`) is intentional or a template cross-contamination artifact.
3. Remove the leaked PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block — confirm the template pipeline that generates this email isn't accidentally inlining server script source.
4. Confirm with the ESP/relay (AgentMail) whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are actually being sent but dropped at the relay, versus genuinely absent — the latter would be a CAN-SPAM/deliverability risk requiring immediate fix.
5. Consider tokenizing or hashing the email address used in tracking-pixel query strings rather than passing it in cleartext, to reduce PII exposure via third-party log retention.
