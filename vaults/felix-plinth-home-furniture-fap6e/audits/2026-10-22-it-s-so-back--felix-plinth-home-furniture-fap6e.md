# IT’S SO BACK
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- "IT'S SO BACK" tells me nothing in the inbox, and the email itself doesn't fix that. The hero is a burgundy lounge chair with "SEIZE the MOMENT" over it and a tiny "It's back in stock" button. Then it's five stacked "IN STOCK" room tiles: Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage, Sofas. After that come Lighting, Rugs and Wall Art tiles, the Warehouse Sale, the design-services block and a product grid. It's a lookbook, not an offer.
- This is also CB2's fourth sale-adjacent send in five days. The 10/20 "WAREHOUSE SALE: UP TO 60% OFF" and this morning's "THE WAREHOUSE SALE, RESTOCKED" already told me about 60% off. Here it's buried halfway down the scroll. My C&B-family snooze reflex is already on, with Crate & Barrel's "TODAY ONLY: 20% off lighting!" and Pottery Barn's third send of the day in the same inbox.
- I'd open it only because I'm renovating two houses and CB2 is on my list. I'd click the free interior design block or one of the "More For You" items. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder, because the room shots are pretty, and I wouldn't forward it. If I'm being honest, I'd scroll to the sale and the design appointment and ignore the rest.

## What's working

- The room photography is moody and consistent: plum, walnut and travertine. The color palette matches what I'm trying to build across both houses.
- Organizing by category (bedroom, sectionals, storage, sofas) matches how I shop when I'm furnishing whole rooms.
- "Warehouse Sale, up to 60% off" reads in about a second. The "last-chance markdowns" line is honest about what it is.
- The free interior design appointment is a real draw. Article's "Our free interior design services are waiting" email got the same reaction from me, and the CTA here is clearer: "Book your free appointment."
- The product grid shows real prices with strike-throughs, like the Foley Mink Velvet Dining Armchair at $499 down to $319. The Reward Dollars block, "Earn 10% back," is the only part of this email that speaks to my card-stacking brain.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer and no urgency. "Seize the moment" and "it's back in stock" tell me neither what's back nor why I should care.
- "In stock" is a weak hook. Nothing says what was sold out, how limited it is, or how fast delivery is. For furniture, I need a ship date, not a badge.
- The 60% offer sits about two-thirds of the way down, below five full-bleed room tiles. Pottery Barn's maroon "Extra 20% off" strip at least goes up top.
- The CTAs are small "Shop Now" buttons, repeated on every tile. They're tiny tap targets on a phone and none of them say which product I'm shopping.
- This is the same warehouse-sale creative I've seen all week (10/20, 10/22 morning). Wayfair's "Open Box steals" at least gave me something different.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Warehouse Sale to the hero and put the 60% in the headline. Keep "back in stock" as the subhead and name the actual categories.
- 2. Cut the five room tiles to two or three, and put a visible CTA with a specific noun on each, like "Shop sectionals."
- 3. Add a delivery or ship-by line and a stock-limited cue to the in-stock tiles. That's the friction I care about.
- 4. Put Reward Dollars and the free design appointment higher, as one slim strip under the hero.
- 5. Don't send the 60% warehouse message a third time this week. Fold it into one send and give the others a different job.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off, plus sectionals are back in stock`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas & sectionals back in stock — ships fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Warehouse Sale markdowns, last-chance pieces, and free design help.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bedroom, dining and storage restocked. Earn 10% back in Reward Dollars.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-built (Marigold/Sailthru-style `mi_` tracking) XHTML table layout with no confirmed critical defects. The QA run flagged 12 content images with no alt text, 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels (Dotomi), and header checks that could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing URLs are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure pixels (mixed content).** 11 images load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients such as Gmail proxy or rewrite these. Others may block them, so the cookie-sync calls will fail silently.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The 10 `pubN.php` cookie-redirect calls plus `UCMController` add roughly 11 external requests per open. This adds render latency and third-party exposure for little email-side value.
- **Own tracking pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/c0f20c4c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are HTTPS and on the sender's domain, so they are fine. Both lack `alt` (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 content images** from `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. The filenames are `d0a0521a-…jpg`, `b126e58e-…jpg`, `6ca19922-…jpg`, `76f553fe-…jpg`, `693da144-…jpg`, `a41be9a8-…jpg`, `c7cbdddf-…jpg`, `8ebb14e6-…jpg`, `0d320a0e-…jpg`, `e76cba64-…jpg`, `8436ffd4-…jpg` and `75898014-…jpg`.
  - If images are blocked, or a screen reader is used, those blocks carry no content. A missing alt attribute also makes some readers announce the filename or URL.
  - If these are the main product or hero images, the alt gap is the most significant accessibility issue in the email.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel and the Dotomi pixels should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` or `display:none`) so readers skip them.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` (on `body` and the `*` selector) also disables iOS text resizing. Both fail WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Markup hygiene (minor).**
  - The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, but `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed.
  - A template placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, was left in the source.
  - Several `@font-face` URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`). The `HNW05` fonts are loaded from `crateandbarrel.com`.
  - None of these breaks rendering, because web fonts fall back to `Helvetica, sans-serif`.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set, `<title>` is present, and MSO conditional styles and `format-detection` metas are in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=56aaa278…` parameter is a 64-character hex value (likely a hashed recipient ID), so it is populated. The source was truncated, so tokens further down the body were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Bulk senders to Gmail and Yahoo are required to include both, and CB2 likely does. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The same relay caveat applies. The `From` domain is `mail.cb2.com`, matching the image and tracking subdomains (`image.mail.cb2.com`), which is consistent with a properly aligned sending setup.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address** were not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 68 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. No mismatch was observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 `image.mail.cb2.com` images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking and cookie-sync pixels.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS or drop them, especially the 10 `pubN.php` cookie-sync calls.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to restore zoom and text resizing.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the original message, not the relay copy.
5. Resolve a sample of the 68 tracking links in a follow-up pass to check final URLs, redirect depth and UTM consistency.
6. Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment and fix the `//Styles` font paths.
7. For the QA tool itself, re-run with full (untruncated) HTML so the unsubscribe link, footer address and merge-token checks can complete.
