# New! Just in from Staub, Breville & more →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- Eighth Crate & Barrel send I've logged in two days, so my first reaction was fatigue. The 10/09 "Just landed: The fall deals edit" and "Inside: Our best deals of the season" were still sitting in the folder. This one is different, though. It's a real product-led email, kitchen first, and I'm mid-renovation on two houses, so I actually stayed for it.
- The Staub Dutch oven and the Breville espresso machine are both things I'd put on a list. The "up to $300 off top coffee & espresso machines" band and "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" are concrete numbers I can act on. But it's long. It starts as a new-arrivals story, drifts into a sale email and then a lighting and dining mood board, and ends with a credit card pitch and an app download. I'd save the Staub and Breville modules to my Pinterest-adjacent folder and probably not buy today. I'd forward the espresso section to a friend.

## What's working

- The "2 days left: free shipping on orders over $99*" bar up top gives me a real deadline before the first image.
- The Staub and Breville call-outs are specific brands I recognize. "Just in from Staub, Breville & more" is the sort of brand-name subject I'll open.
- The "up to $300 off" coffee and espresso band and the "up to 20% off" sofas band put actual numbers in front of me.
- The "just in from your favorite brands" grid shows individual products with names, which beats a lifestyle shot with no product behind it.
- The kitchen photography is warm and consistent, with green and plum tones that pair well with each other. That helps when I'm trying to match a palette across two houses.

## What's weak

- Every CTA is a generic "Shop Now" or "Shop New Arrivals". Nothing says "Shop Staub" or "Shop espresso machines".
- It's very long, with nearly a dozen modules. By the lighting and dining section I'd lost the plot. This is the one-lamp-in-three-emails problem, but inside a single email.
- The new-arrivals story and the sale bands send two different messages. I couldn't tell whether this was a launch or a promotion.
- Café & Caraway and Staub get hero treatment, but the copy under each is small and light gray. On a phone I'd have to zoom to read it.
- The tail end (credit card, app download, TheDesignDesk) is a lot of asks after a long scroll. Nothing here tells me how the card math or the design service would help my renovation.
- The footer legal text for the "*" free-shipping terms is dense, and there's no furniture delivery timing anywhere. For a $2,000 sectional that matters more than the free-shipping bar.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split it. Send a kitchen "new arrivals" email on its own and a separate sofas and sale email. One email, one job.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific. "Shop Staub", "Shop Breville machines" and "Shop sofas up to 20% off" all beat "Shop Now".
- 3. Add furniture shipping and delivery timing near the sectional band, plus a swatch and sample link.
- 4. Cut the tail to one ask. Pick the rewards card or the app, not both plus the design service.
- 5. Increase body text size and contrast so I don't have to zoom.
- 6. Compare with the others. Pottery Barn's "Up to 70% off" strip repeats too much, and CB2's "These pieces are sooo back" turned into an eight-tile catalog. Crate & Barrel has the same sprawl problem here. Article keeps one big offer front and center. Wayfair's ceiling-fan email promised fans and delivered one. This one at least delivers the Staub and Breville it names.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Marketing Cloud/Movable Ink-style table layout with a 460px mobile breakpoint. The only confirmed defects are about 35 images with no alt text, and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. The source I received is truncated, and the 69 tracking links were not probed, so links, footer and UTMs can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 69 links were skipped by the QA probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are unconfirmed. This isn't a defect, just a gap in coverage.
- **Duplicate pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` are the same LiveRamp pixel fired five times, differing only in `n`. This adds requests without adding information.
- **Identifier shared across vendors:** the `s=` value on the rlcdn pixels (`dbe8da718e29…5a8`) starts with the same string as `mi_u=dbe8da718e29…` on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images. The pixel to LiveRamp, a third-party data vendor, therefore carries what looks like a persistent recipient identifier. I can't tell from the source whether it's a hash of the email address. It is worth a privacy review.
- **Open pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8bc0a004-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are two separate open-tracking beacons.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (35 flagged by QA):**
  - 21 are content images. Twenty are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` or `/m/47/…`, and one is `971aa10f-…jpg`. Examples: `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `b85bfaed-…gif`, `2f7ba79b-…jpg`. With images off or a screen reader, these give no text.
  - 7 are Movable Ink dynamic images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`, and others). These are the personalized blocks. If they fail to render, nothing shows in their place.
  - 7 are tracking pixels (5 rlcdn, `o.gif`, the `dv.` beacon). Missing alt is low-impact here, but they should carry `alt=""`.
  - The QA check doesn't say whether `alt` is absent or empty. Empty `alt=""` is correct for the pixels.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` conflict with the later `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`. They also disable iOS text scaling.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand under an XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared alongside `http-equiv`.
  - A leftover template comment reads `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]`.
  - None of these should break rendering.
- **Breakpoint:** the only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. The `min-width:640px` and `min-width:768px` rules set conflicting `img{max-width}` values (640px and 600px).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The personalization is Movable Ink (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid` parameters). The rest of the HTML is truncated, so I can't rule out tokens further down. The only confirmed issue is the missing alt text on the `mi.…/p/rp/` images (see §3).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. I treat this as unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check it against the raw message as originally delivered.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. The QA note says this is expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these fall in the truncated portion, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found that I can confirm. All 69 links were skipped by the probe and the source is truncated, so I can't check UTM parameters or landing-page alignment with the "Staub, Breville & more" subject line.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 21 content images. Give the 7 Movable Ink images static fallback alt text. Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop the `text-size-adjust:none` declarations.
3. Collapse the five rlcdn pixels into one, and review whether sending the shared identifier to LiveRamp fits the privacy policy.
4. Check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results against the raw message from the sender, since the relay may have stripped them.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, and resolve a sample of the redirect links, to cover the footer, unsubscribe link, address and UTMs.
6. Minor cleanup: escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and delete the template placeholder comment.
