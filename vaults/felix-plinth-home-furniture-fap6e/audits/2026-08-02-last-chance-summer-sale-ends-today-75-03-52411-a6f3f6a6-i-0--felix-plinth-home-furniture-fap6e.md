The last three sends (dining room, living room, home tour) each gave me a reason to actually stop and look at a room — this one is just the sale banner plus a four-square category grid I've basically memorized at this point: Outdoor, Bedroom, Living Room, Dining Room. ## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" in a white pill against the yellow gradient is impossible to miss — genuine urgency, not manufactured.
- 40% off is a real, specific number, not "up to great savings."
- The four-category grid (Outdoor, Bedroom, Living Room, Dining Room) lets me skip straight to Dining Room without scrolling past irrelevant stuff.
- 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee called out twice (mid-body and footer icon) — that's the kind of reassurance Wayfair's clearance-banner chaos never bothers with.
- Clean brand shutdown at the bottom — Help Center, Shipping and Delivery, socials — feels like a real retailer, not a fly-by-night sale blast.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth Article email in two weeks and the third one that's fundamentally "sale ends soon" — the dining room and living room sends had actual editorial hooks; this one's just recycled category photography I've now seen in three separate emails.
- No mention of swatches, fabric samples, or showroom pickup — for furniture I'm not buying without touching a swatch first, and Article never brings this up.
- The room images repeat photography I've already seen from the dining room and living room sends — nothing new to look at.
- "Deals so good we can't let you miss them" is filler copy sandwiched between two CTAs — it's not telling me anything the ENDS TODAY badge didn't already say.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap one of the four category tiles for a single hero product with a price and a strikethrough — right now the offer is abstract ("up to 40% off") with zero proof of an actual discounted item. Show me a dining chair at $199 (was $329) and I click immediately.
- 2. Since this is send #4 in the sale cycle, add a countdown timer or literal hour ("Ends tonight at midnight PT") instead of just "ENDS TODAY" — after three sale-mode emails, vague urgency stops working on me.
- 3. Add a one-line swatch/sample callout near the guarantee icons — "Order swatches free" would close my biggest hesitation on the dining chairs I'm deciding on.
- 4. Retire the category grid photography — I've seen the outdoor chairs and the bedroom shot in prior sends. Fresh angles would make this feel less like a rerun.
