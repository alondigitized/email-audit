# Nothing beats the original. Shop Jenny Lind →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids landing in my inbox is just a reminder that I get swept up in the whole Crate & Barrel family blast even though this one's not really for me — no kids, just two houses I'm trying to furnish for grown adults. "Nothing beats the original. Shop Jenny Lind" sounds like a vintage furniture callout, so I half-expected a heritage sideboard or an original Eames-style piece, and instead it's crib rails and toddler bunk beds. That's a subject-line bait I didn't sign up for.
- That said, credit where due: the layout itself is clean, better organized than the chaos-grid Wayfair usually sends, and it's clearly segmented by room (bedroom, nursery, playroom) instead of just dumping fifty SKUs at me. But this is now the fifteenth-plus Crate-family email this week between C&B, CB2, and now Crate & Kids, and my brain filed it as "more of the same" before I even got past the hero banner.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Straight to the "maybe reno" folder where the rest of this week's Crate haul is quietly rotting.

## What's working

- Room-by-room sectioning (bedroom → nursery → playroom → collections) is a smarter structure than the kitchen-sink grid Wayfair runs every send.
- Product photography is genuinely nice — warm, styled nursery shots that feel aspirational rather than catalog-flat.
- The "up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" banner mid-email is a real, specific number, unlike CB2's vaguer "Now & Future Classics On Sale" language.
- Loyalty tie-in ("Double Rewards," "Crate Card," "TheDesignDesk" app perk) at least acknowledges I might already be a cardholder from the C&B side.

## What's weak

- Wrong persona entirely — I'm renovating my primary and second home, not a nursery. Jenny Lind cribs and bunk beds have zero relevance to me right now.
- Subject line ("Nothing beats the original") reads like heritage/vintage furniture copy, not a kids' crib collection — mismatch between promise and payoff.
- Cadence fatigue is real: this is on top of the "Double Rewards, up to 40% off furniture" email and the Halloween one from the same family this week. My inbox is basically a Crate & Barrel newsletter now.
- The hero product (a small writing desk) doesn't even match "Jenny Lind" bedroom/crib styling shown right after — visual hierarchy sends me one message, then pivots.
- No swatch/finish or sizing detail visible for the furniture — for a crib or bunk bed, dimensions and safety cert info matter and I don't see it up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send Crate & Kids to subscribers with no kids-category signal — this is a segmentation miss, not a creative miss, and it's the single biggest lever here.
- 2. Fix the subject/hero mismatch — either lead with "the original Jenny Lind crib, reimagined" so it's clear this is kids' furniture, or use a hero image that matches the headline.
- 3. Consolidate cadence across the Crate family — I don't need C&B, CB2, and Crate & Kids all hitting my inbox in the same 48 hours with overlapping "up to X% off" banners.
- 4. Surface dimensions/safety certification badges (GREENGUARD Gold is mentioned in small text but easy to miss) higher up for anyone actually shopping nursery furniture.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Jenny Lind crib, reimagined for your nursery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off Jenny Lind cribs, bunks & bedroom sets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `GREENGUARD Gold certified. Free shipping on orders $199+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the icon parents have trusted for 20+ years.`
