# FOUND: Furniture Cushions
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "cushions," inbox shows a small stack of cushions up top... and then it's a Wayfair grab-bag of window AC units, pergolas, gazebos, doormats, ceiling fans, chicken coops, and dressers. Not cushions. This is the same bait-and-switch move as the "Bag one of these furniture cushions" send I logged yesterday — Wayfair puts a cushion in the subject and hero, then buries it under a wall of unrelated category tiles. At least three of the tiles have flat-out broken image placeholders — the little blue "?" icon where a product photo should be, for Adirondack chairs, dresser sets, and drinkware. That's not a rendering nitpick, that's a busted email.
- My gut reaction: I don't click, I don't forward, I archive. I'm mid-renovation on two houses right now — if this had actually led with cushions or even patio furniture and looked finished, I'd have at least scrolled. Instead it reads like Wayfair fired off the weekly clearance template and forgot to swap in real assets.
- Compare this to Crate & Barrel's Labor Day Event email I opened this week — that one led with one clear offer (double rewards, up to 60% off) and didn't ask me to parse eleven category tiles to find something relevant. Wayfair keeps doing this "everything at once" thing and it's exhausting.

## What's working

- The cushion hero image at the very top is genuinely well-shot — matches the subject line for once, unlike the "70% OFF" banner noise below it.
- "Must-Have Black Outdoor Privacy Screens" and "Black Adirondack Chairs" tiles are at least on-theme for outdoor/patio, which fits what I'm shopping for on the second house right now.
- Wayfair Rewards / Flexible Financing icons at the footer are a nice touch for someone actually financing furniture across two renovations.

## What's weak

- Subject and preview promise cushions; the body pivots almost immediately to air conditioners, gazebos, doormats, and chicken coops. Zero cushion follow-through past the hero.
- Multiple broken image placeholders (blue "?" icons) on the Adirondack chairs, dresser, and drinkware tiles — this just looks unfinished going out the door.
- No pricing or specific offer tied to any individual tile besides the generic "70% OFF" banner at the top — I can't tell what's actually on sale versus just "browse this category."
- This is the fifth-ish Wayfair send I've logged this cycle hitting the same "Labor Day catch-all" structure — confetti banner, category grid, no real hierarchy.
- "the warmest welcome" doormat section with a dog silhouette is a weird tonal detour in an email that opened on cushions and AC units.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image tiles before this goes out — that's the single biggest lift here, a wall of "?" icons kills trust instantly.
- 2. Make the body match the subject: if you're going to promise cushions, give me a cushion category block with actual SKUs and prices, not one hero photo and then a pivot to unrelated categories.
- 3. Cut the grid down to 3-4 tiles max and put a real discount percentage on each one — right now nothing but the top banner has a number attached.
- 4. Drop the doormat/dog section entirely or move it to its own themed send — it has nothing to do with cushions or the cooling theme running through the rest of the email.
