# NEW LOOKBOOK! Storybook Style
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again — this is the seventh time this account has hit my inbox this week, and I don't have kids. This one's dressed up as a "New Lookbook: Storybook Style" instead of another sale blast, but scroll past the "an enchanted nursery" / "a magical bedroom" copy and it's the identical rotation of cribs, dressers, and canopy beds I logged yesterday for the Labor Day sale email and the day before for the "It's here!" email. Swapping "up to 60% off" for storybook whimsy doesn't change what's in the boxes.
- Nothing here is for me. I'm renovating two houses full of adult furniture — dining tables, sectionals, my own bedroom sets — and this is nursery gliders and toddler beds under twee section headers. I'm not opening this on my commute, I'm not forwarding it (unless it's to mock the volume with a friend who also gets buried by this account), and it's getting archived unread like the last six.

## What's working

- The room vignettes are genuinely well-styled — sage green, blush pink, warm wood tones read cohesive across all six room shots.
- Layout is clean, no overlapping text or broken images that I can see.
- Each section has its own "Shop the Look" CTA, so at least the click path is unambiguous if you're the right shopper.
- The Labor Day discount tiles near the bottom ("up to 50% off kids bedding," "free shipping") are legible and grouped clearly by category.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to me — this is a nursery/kids lookbook and I don't have kids, full stop.
- Six-plus sends this week from the same account with the same core products just re-skinned under a new headline ("Storybook Style" today, "Labor Day Event" yesterday) — that's fatigue, not a campaign.
- The offer is buried: I have to scroll past six lifestyle rooms before I hit the actual discount tiles at the bottom.
- The sweepstakes tile ("enter for a chance to win $5000") at the very bottom feels like a last-ditch hook after the lookbook narrative already lost me.
- Compare this to Article's actual Labor Day sale send — clear discount up front, not four rooms of mood-board copy before the ask.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment me out.** I've never clicked a Crate & Kids email in this whole logged history — stop sending nursery content to an account with zero kids-category engagement. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. **Lead with the offer, not the fairy tale.** If there's up to 60% off buried at the bottom, put it above the fold like Pottery Barn did with "Cozy Throws Ship Free" — real offer, real hero, no scroll tax.
- 3. **Cut the send frequency.** Seven sends in a week from one account trains me to ignore the sender name entirely, which kills open rate on every future email including ones I might actually want.
- 4. **If this is meant to be a design-inspiration play (not a sale), differentiate it visually from the six promo emails already sent this week** — right now it reads as the same product photography with new adjectives.

- **Subject Alt B:** `6 room looks from our new kids lookbook`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the Beatrix Collection + up to 60% off through Labor Day`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New room ideas for nurseries, big-kid beds, and shared spaces`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated (Movable Ink/Salesforce-style) marketing email; core technical issues are missing unsubscribe headers and near-universal missing alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
97 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains not resolved for liveness) — not a confirmed failure, but link destinations are unverified. Multiple tracking pixels present: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5 sequential instances, `n=1`–`n=5`), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...`. No broken/malformed `href` or `src` attributes found in the sampled QA output.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
39 of 44 QA-flagged issues are missing `alt` attributes on `<img>` tags, including all product/lifestyle imagery (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/...`, `s7d5.scene7.com/...`) and tracking pixels. Product/content images lacking alt text fail WCAG 2.1 (1.1.1 Non-text Content) and degrade the experience for screen-reader users and when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail). Tracking pixels missing alt text is standard practice and not a defect.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) observed in the truncated source. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true sender omission, but as observed it fails RFC 2369 one-click unsubscribe support.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (POST) unsupported as observed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
Note: all three warnings may reflect the AgentMail relay not surfacing/forwarding headers rather than the sender's actual configuration; cannot confirm true CAN-SPAM violation without raw source headers from the originating MTA.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Truncated HTML did not include full CTA `href` values in the provided excerpt, so UTM parameter presence/consistency could not be verified. No issues found in available data (insufficient evidence to assess, not a clean bill of health).

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 39 content/product images; leave tracking-pixel `alt=""` (already effectively empty of content value).
- Verify with the sending platform (Movable Ink/Salesforce Marketing Cloud based on domain patterns) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at the SMTP layer, independent of the AgentMail relay.
- Re-run the QA check against raw MTA headers (bypassing the relay) to get a definitive `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) read.
