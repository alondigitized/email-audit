# Now on sale: Our bestselling Cozy Cloud Quilt
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the third bedding-and-pillows send in two days. On 11/05 I got "Colorful pillows, shipping free" and "On sale & delivered for free," and now it's quilts. The hero is a stack of quilts in muted greens and greys with a "now on sale" script line over "The Cozy Cloud Quilt." It's pretty, and I'd pin it. But I'm renovating two houses and I'm mostly shopping for dining tables, lighting and window hardware. A quilt doesn't move my project forward.
- The body does better than the hero. The "Recommended Just For You" block has an extendable dining table, curtain rod hardware and a platform bed, which is much closer to what I actually buy. It sits near the bottom, though, below three full bedding scenes. The sale itself is vague. "Now on sale" and "Quilts ship free" don't tell me a percentage.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the dining table, and archive it. I wouldn't forward it. If I were buying a quilt, the free shipping and the 12 colors would be enough to get me to click.

## What's working

- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" strip at the top is the kind of shipping clarity I want for furniture. Wayfair rarely gives me a window that clear.
- The Winter Whites, Festive Greens and Bold Reds sections are a palette-first layout. I can pick a color story fast, and that's how I plan a room.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has dining tables and curtain hardware. It feels like someone noticed my browsing.
- The Earn 10% Back in Rewards block is visible, and I do shop with the highest-rewards card.
- The "Free Design Services / Book a Free Appointment" band is a showroom-style service I'd actually use for two houses.

## What's weak

- There's no number on the sale. "Now on sale" with no % off feels like a bait-and-switch, and Crate & Barrel at least leads with "Up to 30% off" in its sends.
- The hero copy is small and hard to read over the busy quilt photo. The gray CTA buttons look washed out.
- I got a pillow free-shipping send yesterday and now a quilt free-shipping send, so the creative repeats and the cadence is tiring.
- The page is very long. The Christmas Shop banner, the Design Crew band, the app promo, the Affirm block and the rewards card all pile up at the bottom, and I lose interest before I reach them.
- The Christmas Shop banner is the same launch from the 11/05 lookbook email. Repeating it adds nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer in the hero, such as "Up to 25% off bedding, ends Sunday," so the sale is a number and not a mood.
- 2. Move "Recommended Just For You" above the bedding grids, or cut the bedding to one section. Tables and hardware are what pull me in.
- 3. Make the CTA buttons bolder and bigger so they're easy to hit on a phone.
- 4. Cut the footer promos, or put the rewards card and Design Services side by side higher up.
- 5. Space out the free-shipping sends so I'm not getting the same promise three times in two days.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, image-sliced build. QA found about 50 images with missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, and the recipient's address in cleartext URLs. The sandbox could not confirm the unsubscribe headers, authentication results, or link destinations, and the HTML source was truncated, so the footer was not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking is opaque.** 60 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Example: `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination status and 404s are unverified.
- **Open and tracking pixels on non-HTTPS or third-party hosts:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` (third-party ad-tech pixel)
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/8664d5b1-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- **Stray cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a West Elm host. This is low severity.
- **Leftover code in the CSS:** the second `<style>` block contains PHP `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` text after the rules. It is harmless to most renderers but shows the template wasn't cleaned.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** QA flagged about 50 images with no alt text, including:
  - All hero slices `1106_FRI_AM_CozyCloudQuiltSpotlight/slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg`
  - All product images under `potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` (for example `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`)
  - Banners such as `H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - The footer slices

  With images blocked, the offer, product, and CTA content would be invisible. Some of these may be tracking pixels where empty alt is acceptable, such as the `coreemail.html?email=...` and `d.turn.com` images.
- **Non-HTTPS images (3):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...`

  These may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Layout tables:** some spacer tables lack `role="presentation"`, for example `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td></tr></table>`. Others do have it.
- **Font:** Montserrat is loaded through an external `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/...">`. Many clients strip this, so a fallback stack is needed. None was visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** were found in the visible HTML.
- **Plaintext address in URLs.** The recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears unencrypted in:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...&sv_sveme=...` (sent over HTTP)

  This is PII in query strings, and the `svopn` one is sent over unencrypted HTTP.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. This may be the AgentMail relay dropping them, so it is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated section, so they couldn't be verified.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,550 of 15,804 characters (about 73%) are URLs, which is over the 70% threshold. This is a minor spam-filter risk.
- **Robots and referrer meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present. This is informational and not a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, because the links go through the `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirector. Whether UTM or campaign parameters survive the redirect is unverified.
- **Landing pages:** the 60 skipped links mean none of them could be checked against the subject ("Cozy Cloud Quilt"). Product images point at `potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` assets, but the product page destinations weren't confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content image (the 18 hero slices, product images, banners, and footer). Use `alt=""` only for true spacers and pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` image sources to HTTPS. Stop passing the plaintext address in `sv_sveme` and `coreemail.html?email=`, and use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, bypassing the relay, and check SPF, DKIM, and DMARC on the original headers.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` text from the `<style>` block.
6. Resolve a sample of the tracked links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destination status and UTM or campaign parameters.
7. Reduce the URL density of the plain-text part by trimming tracking-heavy links.
