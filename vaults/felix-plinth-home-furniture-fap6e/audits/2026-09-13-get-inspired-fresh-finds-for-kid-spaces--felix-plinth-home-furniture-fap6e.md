# Get inspired: fresh finds for kid spaces
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Third Crate & Kids email I've had to sort through this week — after "back-to-school lineup" and "washable rugs" — and it's the same story: not applicable to me. This is nursery furniture, bunk beds, playroom storage, a "Caroline" name embroidered on a quilt like it's from a registry catalog. I don't have kids. I'm not on a registry. This is a hard scroll-past, and by this point in the week it's basically inbox noise from a brand family (Crate & Barrel) I actually do shop.
- The photography is genuinely nice — that dark oak bunk bed and the moody green nursery wall both look like something out of a design magazine, not a catalog. But nice photography doesn't fix targeting. Compare this to what Pottery Barn sent this week with "The holiday preview" — that one at least had "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" front and center, which is a real signal for someone mid-renovation like me. This has nothing for my actual house.
- Marking this as read, not clicking, definitely not forwarding — unless I know someone who's actually decorating a nursery right now, and even then this isn't distinctive enough to be worth passing along.

## What's working

- The bunk bed and nursery photography is well-lit and aspirational, not stock-catalog flat.
- "Shop New Arrivals" and section-specific CTAs ("Shop All Bedroom," "Shop All Nursery," "Shop All Playroom") make navigation clear if I were in the market.
- The credit card teaser near the bottom ("the card you love just got better," Redeem Dollars) is at least a category I'm already engaged with via my rewards-card habit.
- GREENGUARD Gold Certified badges are a real trust cue for anyone buying kids' furniture — that's a legitimate differentiator, not fluff.

## What's weak

- Zero relevance to my life — no kids, no nursery, no playroom. This is the third Crate & Kids send this week and I still don't have kids.
- It's an enormous email — bedroom, nursery, playroom, "we love these for you," app download, credit card pitch, all stacked. Way more scroll than the offer justifies.
- There's no actual discount or urgency anywhere I can see — just "new arrivals" and product callouts. Compare to Wayfair's "48 HR 70% OFF" banner this week — obnoxious, but at least it's a concrete offer. This has nothing to grab me.
- The "we love these for you" personalization section is showing strollers and toddler chairs — that's not personalized to me at all, it just reinforces that I'm the wrong audience.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely — if my order/browse history shows zero kids' items across multiple sends, stop routing this sub-brand to my inbox and let Crate & Barrel proper carry the relationship.
- 2. Cut the email length by half — pick one hero category (nursery or playroom, not both) instead of a five-section scroll.
- 3. Lead with an actual offer if there is one — right now this reads like a lookbook, not a marketing email with a reason to click today.
- 4. If this must go to broader C&B subscribers, add an explicit "shopping for a kid's space?" gate at the top so non-parents can self-select out gracefully instead of scrolling six sections of nursery furniture.
