# So much is on sale right now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2 again — day thirteen at this point, I've genuinely lost count of how many of these I've opened this month. "So Much Is On Sale Right Now" as a subject is basically CB2 admitting it's out of ideas, but I'll give the actual email credit: it's the most useful *layout* they've sent me all stretch. It's just a clean category grid — Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor, Outdoor, Entertaining — up to 50% off, no gimmicks, no forced lifestyle story.
- The stuff that actually stopped my scroll: that marble-and-brass table lamp is a straight swap-in for what I'm hunting for the primary's office, and the vintage-look rug tile has real texture to it — that's the kind of thing I'd screenshot and text my wife. But there's zero pricing anywhere in the grid, just category names and arrows, which is a step backward from what Pottery Barn did with "starting at $349" printed right under each nightstand tile a couple days back.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably into Lighting for that lamp. Would I forward it to a friend? No — this is a "browse when bored" email, not a "look at this" email. Mark as read, maybe tap through once.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and on-brand — "THE LABOR DAY SALE, UP TO 50% OFF" is unambiguous, unlike Wayfair's china-cabinet email where I couldn't even find the product in the subject line.
- The two-column category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor, Outdoor, Entertaining) gives me a real reason to click into my specific renovation needs instead of one generic CTA.
- That table lamp tile and the vintage rug tile are genuinely giftable/pinnable images — first CB2 send in a while I'd actually screenshot.
- Double Rewards callout ("10-20% back in Reward Dollars," through Sept 9) is a real, dated loyalty perk, not vague point-speak.
- "800+ New Arrivals" section at the bottom with a single credenza hero is restrained — no clutter dump.

## What's weak

- No prices anywhere in the category grid — Pottery Barn's nightstand email beat this on the exact same beat by printing "starting at $349" under every tile.
- "So Much Is On Sale Right Now" is the laziest subject line CB2's run on me yet, and I've read "OUTDOOR ON SALE," "BACK IN STOCK," and "Now and Future Classics" from them in the same two weeks — total fatigue.
- Category tiles have arrows but no product name or spec — I still have to click blind to find out if the rug's in stock in my size.
- The rewards card CTA ("Double Rewards") is competing with the sale CTA for my attention right in the middle of the scroll, splitting focus instead of reinforcing one action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "starting at $X" under at least the top 3 category tiles — Pottery Barn already proved this works for exactly my persona this week.
- 2. Kill this subject line pattern. I've now seen four CB2 sends in two weeks that are functionally "everything's on sale" — pick one hero product (that lamp) and lead with it by name.
- 3. Move the Double Rewards block either above the fold near the hero or way down after New Arrivals — right now it interrupts the shopping flow.
- 4. Add a one-line delivery/lead-time note near Furniture — for renovation buyers like me, "ships in 2 weeks" beats another arrow icon.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Labor Day: 50% off furniture, lighting, rugs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% back in Reward Dollars through Sept 9`
