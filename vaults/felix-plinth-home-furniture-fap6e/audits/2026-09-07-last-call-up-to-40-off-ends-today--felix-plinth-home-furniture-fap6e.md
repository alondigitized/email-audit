# Last call: Up to 40% off ends today
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the Article send I've actually been waiting for — "ENDS TODAY" in that pale blue banner up top, then a genuinely massive "LABOR DAY SALE" hero with "40% off" spelled out clearly, no scrolling gymnastics required. This is basically the same beat as the 9/4 "Up to 40% off ends Monday" send, just with the deadline pulled forward to today, which makes sense as the closer in the sequence — but that also means I've now seen "up to 40% off" from this exact brand three times in a week and a half (9/1, 9/4, now 9/7).
- The room-category grid (Bedroom, Dining, Living Room, All Sale) is the same photography-driven layout Article always uses, and it's still their strongest asset — it looks like a real house, not a stock-photo showroom, which is more than I can say for half the Crate & Barrel family emails clogging my inbox this week. I'd click through to "Shop Living Room" since that's literally the room I'm mid-renovation on right now.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the urgency is credible since it's genuinely the last day, not a fake countdown timer like CB2's "48 hours left" nonsense. Would I forward it to a friend? No, this is just a normal last-chance sale email, nothing forward-worthy. I'll open it, glance at Living Room, and probably let it sit as the deadline nudge it's designed to be.

## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" plus a maroon strip repeating "last call... up to 40% off... ends today" gives me real, credible urgency — better than CB2's ticking countdown clock gimmick, which feels manufactured by comparison.
- The four-room grid (Bedroom, Dining, Living Room, All Sale) is genuinely useful — I can jump straight to the room I care about instead of landing on a generic homepage.
- "30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee" is called out twice (mid-email and in the icon row) — that's the kind of purchase-confidence signal that actually matters when I'm buying big furniture pieces sight-unseen.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text, no broken images, easy to scan on a phone without zooming.

## What's weak

- This is the third "up to 40% off" Article email in a week and a half (9/1, 9/4, now 9/7) — I already flagged the cadence issue on the 9/4 send, and now I'm just watching the same sale get "last-called" over and over.
- No specific product shown — it's all lifestyle room shots and category buttons, so I have no idea if there's an actual piece I want on sale versus just browsing blind.
- "Shop Living Room" and "Shop All Sale" both funnel to browse pages, not a specific hero product — Pottery Barn does this too and it's equally lazy across both brands.
- Bottom nav ("Explore What's New," "Shop Best Sellers") competes with the sale CTAs above it — muddies what I'm actually supposed to click if I've scrolled that far.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap one of the four room tiles for an actual "here's a specific piece marked down" callout — a sofa or bed frame with a struck-through price. Right now it's all mood, no proof there's a deal on something I'd buy.
- 2. Cut the redundant "Explore What's New" / "Shop Best Sellers" buttons from this specific send — they dilute the last-call urgency the top of the email is trying to sell.
- 3. Since this is the third "40% off" send in ten days, differentiate the framing — call out that inventory is shrinking or specific categories are almost sold out, not just repeat the same percentage a third time.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedroom, dining, and living room favorites — before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends at midnight. Shop your room while it's still 40% off.`
