# When it’s up to 60% off & 2x Rewards, you’ve gotta shop!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Crate&Kids again — different sub-brand than the "Just for you: a note about Labor Day" wall-of-text sends I flagged twice already today, and honestly this one's a night-and-day improvement over both. It actually leads with a product shot (a nursery dresser styled with a changing pad and folded onesies) instead of pretending to be a personal note, and it's got a real grid of category tiles below the fold. But my kid isn't a baby anymore, so a full email built around cribs, nursery gliders, and baby bedding just isn't for me right now — I already said this about the "1,000 hour nursery chair" send earlier today, and the targeting hasn't caught up.
- The "Double Rewards" banner is the fourth time I've seen that exact navy module this week from the parent brand — I called this cadence yesterday and here it is again, verbatim style. It's not annoying exactly, just numbing at this point.
- I'm not clicking. Nothing in this email applies to what I'm actually shopping for (I'm renovating two houses, not stocking a nursery), and I've hit rewards-program fatigue from Crate & Barrel's family of brands this week. Marking as read, not forwarding — my sister-in-law is pregnant, maybe I'd forward it to her, but that's a stretch, not a real send.

## What's working

- The hero actually shows a product (a green dresser with styled changing-table props) instead of the text-only "personal note" trick C&B proper keeps running.
- The category grid below the sale banner is genuinely useful — eight tiles, each with its own discount ("up to 50% off nursery furniture," "up to 60% off decor & lighting") beats CB2's old scattershot approach.
- "TheDesignDesk — 100% free" callout with real room photography is a nice trust cue, similar to what Pottery Barn tries with its Design Crew push, except this one doesn't bury it behind a sweepstakes gimmick like PB's "$1500" email did.
- Clear, unambiguous "Shop the Sale" and "Shop Now" buttons — no vague "Discover" copy.

## What's weak

- Entire email is baby/nursery-first — cribs, gliders, baby bedding, "Reward Dollars" — zero relevance to a guy renovating a primary and a second home with no baby in the picture.
- Double Rewards banner is a near-exact repeat of what I've seen from Crate & Barrel proper three days running; this is the fourth iteration and it's starting to blur together.
- "we love these for you" personalization row is stocked entirely with baby gear (bassinet, stroller travel system, nursery glider) — if this is meant to be personalized, the algorithm has me pegged wrong.
- Long, cluttered footer stack (app download, design desk repeat CTA, social icons, legal disclaimers) pushes the email length way past what I'd scroll through on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix segmentation — if I've never clicked a Crate&Kids link, stop sending me nursery-first sends; pivot me to Crate & Barrel or CB2 general furniture instead, the way Article does when it actually reads intent signals.
- 2. Cut the Double Rewards banner duplication — I've seen this exact module 3+ times this week from sibling sends; rotate creative or drop it for recipients who've already seen it twice.
- 3. Move "TheDesignDesk" free consultation higher for non-baby households — it's a stronger hook for someone like me renovating two properties than nursery gliders are.
- 4. Trim the footer — app download + design desk CTA + social + legal is too much stacked at the bottom; pick one closing CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Renovating? Design Desk help is free (and up to 60% off nursery)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off kids furniture — plus 2x Rewards through 9/8`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free 1:1 design appointments, no purchase required.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nursery, bedding, decor — all on sale through Labor Day.`
