# Extra 20% off select styles & more deals + Why you need an extending dining table
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Same maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip as the 10/31 "NEW collections for your bedroom" send, the 10/31 "Happening now!" send and the 10/30 "Sooner is better!" send. My first reaction was "again." I'm tired of that banner. But the body is different this time. It's a real dining table story, and I'm redoing two houses, so I stayed.
- "Make Room for More" with extending tables is the first Pottery Barn pitch this week that matches a problem I actually have. The Toscana, Vintners and Monroe tables each have an In-Stock tag and a "Shop the Look" cue. That tells me I can get one without waiting 14 weeks. The In-Stock tag is the most useful thing in the email.
- I'd click once. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder for the dining room and move on. It's a decent send, held back by the banner and the long tail of module clutter at the bottom.

## What's working

- The "Make Room for More" hero with the extending table is a specific product story. It's a real use case, not another sale strip.
- In-Stock tags on Toscana, Vintners and Monroe answer the furniture lead-time question up front.
- Each table gets a short description with finish and size options (3 sizes and 6 finishes, 2 rich finishes). That's the swatch-level detail I care about.
- "Recommended Just for You" mixes the extendable table, a pendant, a rug and a mattress. It reads as a sign that they know I'm furnishing a whole house.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" and Affirm modules speak to my rewards math.

## What's weak

- The maroon sale banner is the same one I've seen all week. It's stale, and it sits above a hero that has nothing to do with it.
- It never says which dining tables are in the 20% off. "Select styles" plus a tiny fine print block makes me hunt for the offer.
- The back half is too long. A chair module, a guest-ready table row, Christmas Shop, Design Crew, the app, a credit card and Affirm all pile in. It feels like three emails stacked.
- The Christmas Shop and Design Crew blocks pull me away from the dining story. Design Crew was a whole send on 10/30.
- Fine print is tiny and dense. I can't read it on a phone without zooming.
- The "Recommended Just for You" curtain rod and bed mattress feel random next to a table pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic banner with a dining-specific offer, such as "Extra 20% off extending tables, ends Sunday." Tie the sale to the hero.
- 2. Cut the second half. Keep the three tables, the chairs row and one rewards line.
- 3. Show the price or the savings on each table card. Right now I can't tell what 20% off does to a Toscana.
- 4. Add shipping or delivery timing for in-stock tables, such as "Ships in 2 weeks."
- 5. Move Design Crew and Christmas Shop to their own sends. Crate & Barrel's "The holiday decor shop is now open" got that right by keeping it one idea.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extending dining tables, in stock + 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The dining table that seats 10 when you need it`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Toscana, Vintners and Monroe are in stock now. Extra 20% off ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Drop-in leaves, 6 finishes, and delivery dates you can plan a holiday around.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px layout with no broken merge tokens visible in the truncated source. It has three technical problems: the recipient's email address is put in plaintext URLs (one over HTTP), about 60 images have no alt text, and unrelated server-side code is left in a `<style>` block. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 4 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links can't be verified.** 58 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` and the QA probe skipped them. I can't confirm they resolve, and I can't check the destinations or UTM parameters.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is a plain-HTTP open pixel. It can be blocked, and it sends the address unencrypted.
  - `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` is a third-party ad-network pixel.
  - `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/de0ff7ef-...?mi_cid=...` is another open pixel.
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is used as an `<img>`. It points at an HTML page, not an image, and also carries the address.
- **Mixed content:** these three images use `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel above
  
  Clients may block them, which would leave broken images or missing banners. The first two are on the same host that serves other assets over HTTPS.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is loaded from another brand's host. It adds a dependency, but it is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 60 `<img>` elements have none. The QA warnings list them by filename:
  - Content images: `slices_01–16.jpg/gif` under `H26_Furn_B4_ExtendingDiningTables/`.
  - Banners: `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`.
  - Product shots: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, and others.
  - Footer: `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  
  With images blocked, the offer, the product names and the footer navigation disappear. The QA output says nothing about alt text on the logo/nav link, which does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`. Screen readers will read the filenames for the others.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns=...>`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` stops pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Stray code in a stylesheet:** the second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This looks like server-side code that leaked into the template. It is invalid CSS and can break the rule that follows it in some parsers.
- **Layout table without a role:** the spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` has no `role="presentation"`, though the other layout tables do.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat">` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook, so those clients fall back to system fonts.
- **Fixed width:** the layout tables are a fixed `width="650"`. The mobile rules are `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`, but none of the visible tables use those classes. I can't tell whether the rest of the email scales, because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source.
- The recipient's email address is resolved into URL query strings (`email=` and `sv_sveme=`). This is a privacy issue, not a token failure. Anyone who sees the URL (proxy logs, forwarded emails, link scanners) gets the address. The `svopn` pixel sends it over HTTP.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing in the captured headers. QA says AgentMail may have stripped them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, that falls short of the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** I can't check these, because the source is truncated before the footer.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,162 of 15,428 characters (about 72%) are URLs. This can look spammy to filters, and it is unreadable to anyone using the plain-text version.
- **Robots meta:** `noindex, nofollow` and `referrer no-referrer` are present. These are fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- I can't check UTM parameters or landing page alignment. All CTA links are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` redirects, and the QA run didn't resolve them.
- Product images come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. Some are inconsistent: `open-box-cooper-ribbed-glass-pendant...` is an open-box item, and `standard-curtain-rod-wall-bracket-l.jpg` is a curtain rod bracket that doesn't fit an extending-dining-table feature. Without the link destinations I can't tell whether these are real product mismatches.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, especially the offer banner `H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg` and the dining-table slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Take the recipient's email address out of the `coreemail.html` and `svopn` URLs. Use an opaque ID instead.
3. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS.
4. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
6. Check the raw source for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
7. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm UTM parameters and destinations.
8. Cut the plain-text URL ratio, for example by shortening tracking URLs or dropping per-slice links.
