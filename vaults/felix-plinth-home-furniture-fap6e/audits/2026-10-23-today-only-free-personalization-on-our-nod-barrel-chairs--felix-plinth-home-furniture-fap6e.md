# Today only: FREE personalization on our Nod & Barrel Chairs!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kids sender again. I've been snoozing it all week for the Doona and the nursery glider, and nothing here changes that. I don't have a kid's room on either punch list. The hero is a pile of pastel nod chairs with "ELLIOT" embroidered on one, and I can see the free personalization pitch. But the whole email is built around a nursery, and I'm doing dining rooms and lighting.
- The offer is also muddled. "Download our app" is the only button under the hero, so the free personalization looks like an app-gated perk. Then free shipping on toys, 500+ new arrivals, a credit card pitch, the app again, and a design appointment all pile up below it. The hero offer isn't even the first thing the grid sells.
- Verdict: I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't click it and I wouldn't forward it. The one thing that made me stop was the free design appointment near the bottom. That's the only line that fits two renovations.

## What's working

- The hero photo is warm and tactile. The monogrammed chair makes the personalization offer easy to understand without reading any copy.
- "Free today only" gives me a clear deadline on the personalization offer.
- The sage green block under the photo keeps the headline readable. Nothing overlaps or looks broken.
- "TheDesignDesk – your design pro is waiting, and it's 100% free" is a useful hook. Article's "Our free interior design services are waiting" hit me harder, but this is the same idea.
- The product grid shows prices, so I can judge it quickly.

## What's weak

- The only button under the hero says "Download Our App." The headline promises free personalization, but the CTA sends me to an app store. That's a bait-and-switch feeling.
- Nursery cribs, a glider and a kids' dresser are irrelevant to me. This sender has no idea what I'm renovating.
- The app is pushed twice, plus a Crate Card pitch and a "chance at $500" tease. Too many asks and no single clear one.
- The "we love these for you" grid isn't personalized. It's six nursery items, and I haven't looked at any of them.
- The fine print at the bottom is long and dense. The free shipping on toys ($99+) has asterisks and exclusions I can't read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero button "Personalize a Nod Chair" and link it straight to the chair. Keep the app as a secondary link.
- 2. Cut the email to one offer, personalization, and drop the toys shipping, the new arrivals and the credit card block.
- 3. Segment the "for you" grid by what the subscriber actually browsed. Don't show every subscriber the same six nursery items.
- 4. Move the free design appointment up. It's the strongest offer in the email and it's buried near the bottom.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional build with Outlook/VML fallbacks and mobile media queries, served from Crate & Barrel's own subdomains. QA reported 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The main confirmed issues are about 22 images flagged for missing alt text, a viewport that blocks zoom, and header checks that couldn't be completed. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations unverified:** 63 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status (200, 404 or expired promo) is unknown for all of them.
- **Tracking infrastructure seen:**
  - Click and open tracking via `mi.crateandbarrel.com` (e.g. `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/af6935d4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
  - Five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` third-party pixels (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain).
- **Image hosts are split:** `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` (ESP CDN) and `s7d5.scene7.com` (Adobe Scene7).
- **Filename may be stale (low confidence):** the Scene7 asset `2026_0729_CBK_WholeHomeNewArrivals` is dated 07/29, but the email was sent 10/23. The source may be a reused asset, but I couldn't confirm that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 22 image warnings (about 14 unique images). Two groups matter:
  - **Content images:** `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `6ad8edc9-…gif`, `bbe70b96-…jpg`, `f554a182-…jpg`, `8af1ce55-…jpg`, `cc93fd89-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`, `2026%5F0729%5FCBK%5FWholeHomeNewArrivals%5F01%5F08` and `f3c0d22992a01946.png`. The last is repeated 6 times from `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`, which is likely a recommendation module. If these carry content or links, they need descriptive alt text.
  - **Pixels and spacers:** `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv…/af6935d4-…`, and the `…Rule%5FSpacer%5F40px` image. These should use `alt=""` rather than no alt attribute. The QA check can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile. This is an accessibility problem under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resize disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` are set. This also blocks text scaling on iOS, and it conflicts with the `100%` values set elsewhere in the same stylesheet.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are declared. `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values.
- **Template leftovers:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and the `<title>` doesn't match the subject line.
- **CSS conflict:** `img{max-width:600px}` at `min-width:768px` and `img{max-width:640px}` at `min-width:640px` overlap, so 640px wins at desktop widths.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA could not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not inspected:** the visible in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm or deny them.
- **Hashed identifier in third-party pixels:** the same 64-character hex value appears in `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` and in the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a67…` URLs. It looks like a hashed recipient identifier shared with LiveRamp. Confirm it is covered by the sender's privacy disclosures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. All 63 links are redirect-wrapped (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) and weren't resolved, so the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page match to "FREE personalization on Nod & Barrel Chairs" are unknown. No issues are confirmed here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Set `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Check raw SMTP headers outside the relay for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
4. Resolve a sample of the 63 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe. Confirm that the Nod & Barrel chair CTA lands on the right page, the promo is still valid, and UTMs are present.
5. Escape the title (`&amp;`), remove the duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags, and delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
6. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and merge tokens can be checked.
