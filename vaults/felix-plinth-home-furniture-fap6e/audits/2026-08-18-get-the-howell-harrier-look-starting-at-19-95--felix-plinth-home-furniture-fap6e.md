# Get the Howell & Harrier look, starting at $19.95
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Howell & Harrier" — so this is a designer collab, and I'll give it credit for actually looking like one. That hero shot with the sculptural vases and the pop of dried florals has a genuinely different vibe than the usual Crate & Barrel room-shot — it doesn't look like the fifteenth "apartment collection" email I got yesterday. But then it just... keeps going. And going. Vanity mirror, lighting, a whole "clean kitchen" SMEG toaster detour, then a full Design Desk sweepstakes pitch, then a "we love these for you" product grid, then a credit card pitch. By the time I hit the Klarna-style financing banner near the bottom I'd forgotten what I opened the email for.
- The $19.95 starting price in the subject is a decent hook, but I scrolled through this whole thing and never actually found a product tagged at $19.95 — the "statement pieces" hero button just says "Shop the New Collab" with no price shown on-image. That's the kind of bait that gets an email marked read and forgotten, not clicked. Compare that to yesterday's "Meet the apartment collection" send (8/10 from me) which actually named my situation — furnishing two places at once — in the first screen. This one buries any specificity under six unrelated sub-promos.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the collab hero, out of curiosity about who Howell & Harrier even are. Would I forward it? No — there's nothing here focused enough to send a friend. This reads like C&B crammed three separate campaigns (designer collab, kitchen, Design Desk services) into one email because someone missed a send date.

## What's working

- The hero image has actual art direction — moody florals, sculptural vessels, not another beige sectional in a sunlit living room.
- "statement pieces starting at $19.95" in the subject is a real, specific price point, which is rare for this brand's subject lines lately.
- The Howell & Harrier x Crate & Barrel lockup and the model shot below it signal this is a legitimate designer collab, not just another private-label refresh.
- Product tile grid near the bottom ("we love these for you") at least shows real furniture with visible names and looks personalized-ish, even if it's generic.

## What's weak

- The $19.95 price from the subject never resurfaces anywhere in the body that I can see — no price tag on the hero, no callout tile. Total bait-and-switch feeling once I scrolled.
- This email tries to be four emails: designer collab, "clean kitchen" SMEG toaster, Design Desk $5000 sweepstakes, and a credit card pitch. None of them get room to land.
- Zero mention of my actual situation — I'm furnishing a second home right now, and yesterday's "apartment collection" email nailed that. This one is generic "shop the collab" with no small-space or dual-reno angle at all.
- The Design Desk sweepstakes and credit card blocks feel bolted on — different visual language, different CTAs ("Book Your Free Appointment," "Learn More"), and they dilute the one thing (the collab) that had a shot at getting my attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Lead with Howell & Harrier collab only, put an actual $19.95 (or whatever) price tag directly on a hero product, and stop there — save the toaster, sweepstakes, and credit card pitch for separate sends. CB2's "midcentury" send at least stayed on one theme; this one doesn't.
- 2. Put a real price or "from $19.95" badge on the hero shot or the first product tile — right now the subject line promise is invisible once you open it.
- 3. If you're going to run a designer collab, say something about the pieces — dimensions, materials, ship times — the way a $19.95 accent piece implies "small item, easy to add to a room," which is exactly what I need for the second place. Zero product detail shown here.
- 4. Drop the Design Desk sweepstakes and credit card blocks from this send entirely — they read like an entirely different marketing team's afterthought.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Howell & Harrier: statement pieces from $19.95`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New designer collab — small pieces, big impact`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Vases, frames & decor from the Howell & Harrier collection — shop the collab.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Designed to be jaw-dropping. Priced to be easy.`
