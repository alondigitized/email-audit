# All eyes are on this sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is CB2 email eleven — ELEVEN — in eleven straight days, and "The Labor Day Sale, Up to 50% off" is at least a real hero image this time (that outdoor sofa/travertine setup is genuinely nice), which is more than I can say for yesterday's plain-text "Nicole" letter asking if I've shopped the sale yet. But then it's the exact same stack I've seen all week: category nav bar, "Clearance up to 60% off," the Double Rewards credit card pitch again, "800+ New Arrivals." I've now memorized this template better than my own kitchen remodel timeline.
- Nothing here is bad exactly, it's just completely undifferentiated from the nine sends before it. Compare that to Article's actual "Labor Day Sale starts now" send, which at least came after a run of non-discount editorial content so the discount felt like an event — here the discount is the only mode CB2 has, day after day, so it means nothing anymore.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Absolutely not, unless I'm forwarding it to a friend as a joke about how CB2 has lost its mind with cadence. Marking as read and moving on — this is fatigue, not a sale.

## What's working

- The hero shot is a real outdoor scene with an actual product (that woven-look sofa) instead of another all-text banner like yesterday's.
- "Shop the Sale" button is clean, high-contrast, easy to tap on mobile.
- Category nav (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Outdoor) up top is useful if I actually wanted to browse.
- Double Rewards card math is spelled out clearly this time — "10% → 20% back in Reward Dollars," with a real end date (Sept 9).

## What's weak

- This is day eleven of essentially the same promo stack — Labor Day, Clearance, Double Rewards, New Arrivals — just reshuffled. I could recite the order from memory at this point.
- No product category speaks to me specifically — I'm mid-renovation on two houses and there's zero rug/lighting/furniture pairing content, no "complete the room" cross-sell, nothing that reads like CB2 knows what I'm shopping for.
- The green velvet sofa under "800+ New Arrivals" gets one static product shot with no price, no dimensions, nothing — compare that to Pottery Barn's ready-to-ship chair send that at least gave me a "1-4 week" shipping window I could act on.
- "Prepare to want it all" is a groan-worthy CTA — vague, no verb-noun clarity, doesn't tell me what happens when I click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the daily cadence or at minimum stop repeating "Labor Day Sale" as the hero every single day — pick one big Labor Day send and let the rest of the week be genuinely different content (new arrivals only, or a rewards-focused send, not both crammed into one email again).
- 2. Put real numbers on the new-arrival sofa — price, dimensions, ship date — the way Pottery Barn does with its ready-to-ship messaging. That's the one thing that would actually move me toward a click on furniture I'm renovation-shopping for.
- 3. Cut "Prepare to want it all" for something with an actual verb and noun.
- 4. Add a rewards-member callout at the top if I'm already a CB2/C&B rewards member — right now this treats me like a cold lead every single day regardless of purchase history.
