# Our Labor Day Event: up to 60% off + Double Rewards→
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again. That makes it six times this week — I checked, and yeah, this is the same nursery glider/dresser rotation I logged yesterday for "It's here! Up to 60% off + Double Rewards." I don't have kids. I've never had kids. At this point I'm fairly sure this account just doesn't know who's on this list, and it's starting to feel less like marketing and more like static.
- The layout itself is fine — clean grid, a rocking chair hero, a tidy little "shop by category" wall of nursery furniture, bedding, rugs, lighting. If I were actually furnishing a nursery this is a perfectly serviceable email. But I'm renovating a primary and a second home, tracking real furniture across Pottery Barn, Crate & Barrel proper, and Wayfair — none of which is baby gear. This isn't irrelevant in a "meh" way, it's irrelevant in a "you have the wrong customer entirely" way.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and mentally filing this brand's kids account as noise I should probably just unsubscribe from.

## What's working

- The hero is legible at a glance — red banner, bold "60% off," clear "Shop the Sale" button, no ambiguity about what the offer is.
- Double Rewards messaging is repeated twice (top banner and mid-email card) so if I *were* a Crate Card holder I wouldn't miss it.
- The category grid (kids furniture, bedding, nursery furniture, rugs, lighting) is genuinely well organized — good product photography, consistent white backgrounds, prices/percentages layered cleanly on each tile.
- "Design Desk" free consultation block is a nice trust/service play — free room planning is a real value-add if you're actually renovating a kid's space.

## What's weak

- Wrong audience, full stop — every hero product (glider, crib bedding, nursery dresser) assumes a baby in the house.
- This is the sixth near-identical Crate & Barrel family send this week. My Crate & Barrel proper snooze from the countryside email yesterday is now bleeding into Crate & Kids too — the whole family of accounts is just spamming the same Labor Day + Double Rewards stack under different skins.
- The "we love these for you" personalized product picks (backpack, stroller, dresser, high chair) is exactly the kind of "known customer" signal that's completely wasted on me — it reads like fake personalization since none of it applies.
- Long, dense email — by the time you hit "it's all on the app" and the app-download QR/CTA near the bottom, I've long since stopped scrolling.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the list. If I've never clicked a Crate & Kids email and I'm on Crate & Barrel's furniture/renovation list, suppress the kids account entirely — this is a data hygiene problem, not a creative one.
- 2. Stop stacking Labor Day + Double Rewards across every sub-brand in the same week. Wayfair's "Adirondack chairs are here" send at least felt like one clear seasonal moment; this feels like the fourth rerun of the same stack.
- 3. If Crate & Kids must email adults without kids, lead with something crossover-relevant (storage, rugs, lighting that works in any room) instead of gliders and crib bedding up top.
- 4. Trim the email — six sections plus an app pitch plus a Design Desk pitch plus social icons is a lot of real estate for someone who bounces after the hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Double Rewards + 60% off kids' furniture — ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, dressers, rugs and more — plus 2X Reward Dollars through Labor Day`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the nursery sale before Double Rewards ends 9/8`
