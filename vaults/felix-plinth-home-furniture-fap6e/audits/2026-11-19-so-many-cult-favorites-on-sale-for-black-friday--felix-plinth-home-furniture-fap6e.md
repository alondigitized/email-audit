# So many cult favorites on sale for Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. I've lost count, but the brand history says this is at least the eleventh send in about five days, and the sender name is still "CB2 BLACK FRIDAY." I knew what this was before I opened it. The hero is a velvet green channel-tufted armchair under a giant "60% OFF," and it's a good-looking chair. It's also the same Black Friday story as "STARTS NOW: THE BEST SALE OF THE YEAR" yesterday, just with a new subject line.
- The offer is clear in about two seconds: 60% off, one SHOP THE SALE button. Below it, though, it turns into a pile. Six category tiles, a second SHOP THE SALE, free shipping at $99, a 15% off new-year offer, an app promo, a rewards promo and a text-signup promo. That's seven or eight asks in one email.
- Would I click? Maybe, because I'm renovating two houses and that chair is my lane. I wouldn't forward it. I'd file it in the Pinterest folder and move on.

## What's working

- The 60% OFF is huge and sits over a moody, well-styled chair. I got the offer before I scrolled.
- The dark burgundy header with spaced-out "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" reads as seasonal and premium. It looks like CB2, not like Wayfair.
- The six category tiles (furniture, lighting, décor & mirrors, bedding, pillows & throws) are good mood-board material for how I shop. The mirror and lamp tiles are the ones I'd save.
- "Earn 10% back" in Rewards Dollars is visible. I pick cards on rewards math, so that's a reason to care.
- The tile imagery is consistent in palette: olive, cream, walnut. That's what I want when I'm matching rooms.

## What's weak

- The subject says "cult favorites," but the hero shows one chair with no name or price. I can't tell what's actually on sale at what price.
- "UP TO 60% OFF" with a tiny asterisk and a wall of fine print isn't a clean offer. The 15% new-year offer, the free shipping and the 60% all sit in one email, and I can't tell whether they stack.
- Four stacked CTA blocks (Shop the Sale, Shop Now, Shop Now, then app, rewards and gift list) dilute the hero. It reads like a flyer.
- Nothing on furniture shipping or lead times, or swatches and samples. That's what decides a sofa order for a renovation.
- The cadence is exhausting. Pottery Barn's "Amazing Early Black Friday deals" and Crate & Barrel's festive-decor send were at least varied. CB2 has run one pitch since the 14th.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero one named product with a real sale price, like "Peekaboo Chair, now $X." Then link the button to that product's page instead of the generic sale page. Article's "Black Friday: It's go time" had a single banner and a single coral button, and it was easier to act on.
- 2. Cut the lower stack to two blocks: free shipping and rewards. Drop the app promo and the text-signup push for existing subscribers.
- 3. State clearly whether the 15% new-year offer stacks with the sale.
- 4. Cap sends per week and vary the creative. I'd rather get three good CB2 emails than eleven.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks. The QA data flags missing alt text on 11 content images, plus 10 `http://` Dotomi tracking pixels that clients may block. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so redirect targets, status codes and UTM propagation are unverified.
- **Mixed-content pixels.** 11 Dotomi pixels load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1`
  
  Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection and most webmail clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources, so these probably won't fire. Any cookie-sync or retargeting they were meant to do is lost for those recipients.
- **Cookie-sync pixels in the body.** `pub1.php` to `pub10.php` under `/cookieredir/2437/` are third-party cookie-sync calls. Email clients rarely honor them, and a large count of third-party hosts can add spam-filter risk.
- The first-party pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0816f7c1-...?mi_cid=...` load over HTTPS, so there is no issue with the transport.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images.** 11 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt` attribute. They are `6865bebf-...gif`, `b9413d2a-...jpg`, `33cc28c7-...jpg`, `33c17252-...jpg`, `8611f971-...jpg`, `87499f2d-...jpg`, `ea340882-...jpg`, `1e2a6467-...jpg`, `2998840d-...jpg`, `65d9a6b8-...jpg` and `20185973-...jpg`. With images off, screen readers and some clients read out the filename or show nothing. The QA output doesn't show whether these are inside linked CTAs, which would make the problem worse.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel and all Dotomi pixels also lack `alt`. These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. They are lower severity than the content images.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text scaling disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` turn off iOS text-size adjustment.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The source contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Font URLs.** Some `@font-face` sources use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Others load from `crateandbarrel.com`. Web fonts are ignored by Gmail and Outlook, so the fallback is `Helvetica, sans-serif`. That is acceptable, but I couldn't verify the full font stack because the HTML was truncated.
- **Generic title.** `<title>` is "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", not the email-specific subject. This is a minor issue that only matters in web-view contexts.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set and the Outlook DPI settings are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so unrendered tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) later in the body couldn't be checked. The `mi_u` value in the `o.gif` URL is a hashed identifier, not a broken token.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the headers. This is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers (RFC 8058 one-click) for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers at the source before treating it as a defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.cb2.com` is a subdomain, so alignment should be verified from raw headers or a seed test.
- **Footer not visible.** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and sender identification are in the truncated part of the HTML and could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 61 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so there is no resolved destination and no UTM data. The final-URL check is needed for the Black Friday promo landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text to the 11 content images**, using the sale or product description. Use `alt=""` on every tracking pixel.
2. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`**, or remove the `pub1` to `pub10` cookie-sync pixels from the email.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust:none` where it isn't needed.
4. **Check the raw message headers** for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't rely on the relay-processed copy.
5. **Resolve the 61 tracking links** with a redirect follower to check for 200 responses, UTM retention and landing-page alignment.
6. **Clean the template**: remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment, fix the double-slash font URLs, and set `<title>` to the subject.
7. **Fix the QA tooling.** The checker should read headers from the original message or a seed inbox, and it should resolve redirect-domain links instead of skipping them.
