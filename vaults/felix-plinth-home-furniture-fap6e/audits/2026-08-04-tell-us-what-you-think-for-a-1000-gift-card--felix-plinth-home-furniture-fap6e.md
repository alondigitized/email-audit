# Tell us what you think for a $1000 gift card
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Crate & Barrel's fifth send to hit my inbox today — I flagged the $1,000 gift-card sweepstakes angle already on the "Your chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card" send earlier, and now here's basically the same review-to-win mechanic wearing a different hero shot. The living room hero at the top is genuinely nice — warm neutral sofa, that sculptural coffee table, a chandelier that actually looks like something I'd screenshot for my Pinterest-adjacent folder — but then it nosedives into corn. Actual corn on the cob. "In season now: summer produce" has nothing to do with furniture, nothing to do with my two renovations, and it's wedged right between the sweepstakes CTA and a legit 15% off Aspen collection sale.
- This is a scroll-and-skim, not a click. The living room hero earns a glance, but by the time I hit farmers-market corn and then a credit card pitch, I've mentally filed this as "C&B just fires everything in one email today." Wouldn't forward it — there's nothing here I haven't already seen from them this week, just remixed. I'd mark it read and move to the next one.
- The one thing that'd actually get me to open a browser tab is the Aspen 15% off block three sections down — that's a real number on real furniture — but it's buried under a produce ad, which is a genuinely strange sequencing choice for a home goods retailer.

## What's working

- The hero living room shot is on-brand and matches my aesthetic — warm wood tones, layered textures, exactly the kind of room I'm trying to recreate in the second house.
- The Aspen collection 15% off block has a real, specific discount and its own clean CTA — that's the strongest offer in the whole email.
- Product grid at the bottom ("we love these for you") is genuinely relevant — dining tables, nightstands, a media console, king beds — all stuff I could plausibly be shopping for mid-reno.
- Star rating icons (★★★) next to the header are a nice visual anchor that reads as "quick, low-effort ask" before I even process the copy.

## What's weak

- The corn/produce block is a total non-sequitur — Wayfair's "Fearless design, bold layers" send stayed on-category the entire way down; this one derails into a farmers-market recipe pitch for no reason I can figure.
- This is the fifth C&B-family email in my inbox today (counting the Crate & Kids sends), and the sweepstakes mechanic is now the second time I've seen "$1,000 gift card" framing — CB2's "ENDS TOMORROW: BED & BATH ON SALE" at least varies its hook email to email; C&B's just repeating the review-to-win beat.
- Credit card pitch ("new! the card you love just got better") this deep in a review-request email is pure clutter — I'm not deciding on a store card while trying to figure out if I should write a review.
- Visual hierarchy is scattered — sweepstakes CTA, then produce, then a real sale, then products, then a credit card, then an app download, then a design service pitch. Seven different asks in one scroll is exhausting compared to how tight Article's room-tour sends stay.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the produce/recipe block entirely — it has zero relevance to a furniture retailer's core list and actively breaks momentum right after the hero. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Move the Aspen 15% off sale above the sweepstakes ask, or at minimum right after the hero — that's the actual purchase-intent offer, and it's currently the third thing I see.
- 3. Drop the credit card and app-download blocks from this specific send — save them for a dedicated email. A review-request email should end after the product grid, not turn into a five-part catalog.
- 4. Lead the subject/hero with the Aspen discount instead of the sweepstakes — "15% off bestselling Aspen" is a stronger open driver for me than another chance-to-win pitch I've already half-tuned-out from CB2 and C&B this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Aspen collection: 15% off bestsellers, ends soon`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: enter to win a $1,000 gift card when you review your favorite piece`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bestselling Aspen dining and bedroom, 15% off through this week`

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
