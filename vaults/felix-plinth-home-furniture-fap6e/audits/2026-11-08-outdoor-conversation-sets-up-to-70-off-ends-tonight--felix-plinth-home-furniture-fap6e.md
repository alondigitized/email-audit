## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with media queries, and the link structure looks sound: no confirmed broken links and no hard QA failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The main defects are missing alt text on about 25 images, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 probe warnings are HTTP 429 rate limits, not failures. They hit `/daily-sales`, the homepage, three `wayfair-product-*` URLs, and both `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links. Treat them as unverified.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 66 skipped. 4 more timed out at the 70s budget and are neither pass nor fail:
  - `.../5660-W-x-76-H-Single-Sliding-Frameless-Shower-Door-%7C-3`
  - `wayfair-product-L306-K~id-QC91583059.html`
  - `wayfair-product-L505-K~id-BV41098627.html`
  - `daily-sales/area-rug-sale~e408298.html`
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=b7dcd7e9-...`.
  - Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS images will drop these, which undercounts impressions.
  - Some clients will also show a mixed-content warning.
- **Open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (eventType=EmailOpen) is HTTPS, but it has no alt attribute.
- **Insecure app fallback.** The `onelink.me` URLs set `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback starts on HTTP.
- **Per-link tracking IDs.** `_eml` differs on every link, e.g. `fcd7a9cd-...` on "Shop Sale" and `cd6e107f-...` on the logo. The duplicate "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links also have different `_eml` values (`b78d9767-...` and `0013d860-...`). That's consistent with per-link click tracking and isn't a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (25 flagged):**

  - The financing banner `WFUS_1029_FallClearance-Skinny-BAU_Financing.png`.

- **Links with no accessible name.** The QA tool reports the `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` images as link text for product URLs such as `wayfair-product-L132-K~id-BY09007654.html`, `Samsung-28-cu.-ft.-4Door-French-Door-Refrigerator...L881-K~id-BQ23006477.html`, and `Bond-Tile-Auburn-Ribbon...BP66055215.html`. Without alt text, screen readers announce these links by URL or filename. The same applies to the `WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale_Skinny` banner, which links to `/daily-sales`.
- **Images off by default.** The hero banner has no alt text, so with images blocked the main promotional message is lost.
- **Markup baseline.** The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, the `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` client hacks, and `mso-font-alt` are present. The HTML was truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the structure (VML fallbacks, `lang` attribute, `role="presentation"` on tables).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the QA data or the visible source. Recipient identifiers (`_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`) all render as resolved values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found.** The QA tool says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found.** The same caveat applies. If it's truly absent, one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) isn't supported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be determined from this capture.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA data says an unsubscribe link is "always included" in probing, but the footer is outside the truncated source. I can't confirm either one.
- **Sender domain.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain, which is fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters.** None of the sampled URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Attribution uses first-party params instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=9`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_143029`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16695`, `wfcs=cs7`. That works internally, but it won't register in GA or other third-party analytics without a mapping layer.
- **App link.** `c=Triggered` and `pid=Email` on the OneLink URL label a batch send as "Triggered", which could mislabel the campaign in AppsFlyer reporting.
- **Landing alignment.** The primary links I can see go to `/daily-sales`, not an outdoor conversation sets collection, although the subject line names that category. Probed product links also include a Samsung refrigerator, tile and a shower door. The truncated source may contain an outdoor-specific CTA, so this is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the hero and banner images, `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"` (or similar) to the rating images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Serve all `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Check the raw message headers at the source (not the AgentMail copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links and the 429 links with throttling or retry backoff.
5. Add UTM params, or confirm that the proprietary params are mapped into analytics.
6. Confirm that at least one prominent CTA lands on an outdoor conversation sets collection.
7. Review the unsubscribe link and physical address in the untruncated footer.
