# Our Early Black Friday deals are on! Shop up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Crate & Barrel send from the main sender line instead of Kids. It's still the sixth one today, after the Nod Chair emails, Double Rewards, the dinnerware sale, Le Creuset and the three Kids sends. My brain is on full C&B snooze. The hero is a living room with a coffee table and the headline "up to 30% off Early Black Friday." That's a real offer, but it isn't a deep one.
- The email is a very long scroll, and most of it is kitchen: espresso machines, KitchenAid, Le Creuset, cookware. I'm renovating two houses and shopping lighting, tile and furniture right now, so that's the wrong aisle for me. The Gift Shop banner and a "we love these for you" block make it feel like a catalog dump. I'd click once for the furniture tile and bounce.
- I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it only if I were hunting for the Double Rewards deadline, and that deadline appears twice.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. A real room image, "up to 30% off," and a visible Shop Now button.
- The Double Rewards strip (earn 2x back in Reward Dollars with the Crate Card) matters to me, because I buy through the highest-rewards card. It's the one reason I'd stay.
- The 2x2 category grid (entertaining, furniture, kitchen, holiday decor) lets me jump straight to furniture.
- The kitchen deals show specific "Sale $429.99, Reg. $699.99" prices. That's honest, concrete pricing.
- The TheDesignDesk free-appointment block fits my two-house renovation. It's the most relevant thing in the email.

## What's weak

- The email is about 9,000px tall. I'd bail well before the footer.
- The kitchen content swamps everything else. I get no lighting, rugs or sofas, even though furniture is the category I'm shopping.
- "Up to 30% off" is weak next to Pottery Barn's "UP TO 70% OFF" Early Black Friday send. It doesn't feel like a doorbuster.
- The 2x Rewards deadline shows up in the subject, the top strip and mid-email. That's repetition, not urgency.
- The small gray product captions and the dense legal footer are hard to read on a phone without zooming.
- I got no sign of personalization. I felt like a stranger, not a known cardholder.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero, one rewards strip and one category grid. Segment kitchen content away from people who browse furniture and lighting.
- 2. State the rewards math. Say "2x = X% back on a $2,000 sofa."
- 3. Put one big furniture and lighting row near the top, as CB2 did with "Layer on the lighting." Article's travertine send also shows how to curate a focused hero.
- 4. Show the Double Rewards deadline once, with a clear time.
- 5. Increase the product-caption font size and trim the legal text.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, lighting & rugs on sale, plus double Reward Dollars with your Crate Card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive CSS and Outlook (MSO) fixes. QA scored 57% pass, with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The weak spots are widespread missing `alt` text (30+ images) and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and CTA hrefs were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **72 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Per-recipient identifiers appear in image URLs.** Examples: `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/560d850e7e517ede.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`.
- **Third-party pixels are embedded.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `&n=5` requests (LiveRamp) carry the same hashed value as `mi_u`. That ties the recipient identity to a third-party domain. This is a privacy and disclosure consideration, and it adds external requests that can slow rendering.
- **No broken links were confirmed.**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on 30+ images, including content images.** Examples:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/6100bbef-…jpg`
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`
  - `…/m/1/07b7830d-…gif`
  - `…/m/1/70061691-…gif`
  - the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` set (likely product or recommendation images)
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a17cec0f-…`

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these blocks are blank or announced as file names. The `rlcdn.com` and `o.gif` pixels are tracking beacons, so they need `alt=""` and are lower priority.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.** That is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`.
- **Head markup is redundant.**
  - It has both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times, once combined and then as `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
- **A template placeholder was left in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (note the typo).
- **A universal selector and overlapping media queries may cause inconsistent sizing.**
  - `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` applies to every element.
  - `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` and `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` overlap. At 768px and wider the later rule wins, so the cap shrinks from 640px to 600px.
- **The `color-scheme` meta declares light mode only**, so dark-mode clients may apply their own inversion.
- `lang="en"` is set correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the captured portion. The `mi_u` values are resolved hashes, not broken tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN).** QA notes the relay may have stripped it. It can't be confirmed from this capture.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found (WARN).** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements expect it.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it isn't evidence of failure.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked** because the HTML was truncated before the footer.
- The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` aligns with the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The truncated source and the skipped link probes mean no CTA hrefs, UTM parameters or landing pages were checked. Nothing is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` recommendation images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, deduplicate the charset and `format-detection` metas, and remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Fix the overlapping `img` `max-width` media queries and drop the universal `*{line-height:100%}` rule.
5. Confirm from raw SMTP headers (not the relay copy) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. That should cover the footer unsubscribe link and address, and the 72 tracking links by following redirects to check destinations and UTM carry-through.
7. Review whether the five `rlcdn.com` pixels are covered by the privacy policy and consent language.
