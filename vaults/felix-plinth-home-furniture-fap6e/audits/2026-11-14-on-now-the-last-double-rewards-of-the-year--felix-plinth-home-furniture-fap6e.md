## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "ON NOW! The last Double Rewards of the year!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a standard table-based ESP build (Mapp/MI-style, per the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` hosts). The main confirmed issues are about 41 images with no alt text and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated, so the footer and link targets were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **87 tracking links were skipped** by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not passing.
- **Five identical third-party pixels** load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5`. This is the LiveRamp domain. Repeating the same pixel five times is redundant and adds third-party requests that images-off clients and privacy tools may block.
- Per-recipient identifiers are embedded in image URLs, for example `mi_u=56aaa278…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif`. This is expected for open and recommendation tracking, but those images are personalized and not cacheable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 41 images** (QA list, possibly truncated). Examples:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`, such as `bf3053a9-…jpg` and `70061691-…gif`.
  - Recommendation images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7).
  - Tracking pixels: `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5), `mi…/o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/226ed0f0-…`.
  - The QA check does not distinguish an absent `alt` from `alt=""`. Pixels should carry `alt=""`. The ~27 content and product images need descriptive alt text, and if they are linked, they leave screen-reader users with no link name.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />`** disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **`*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}`** disables text-size adjustment globally.
- **Wrong font fallbacks in the MSO block:** `.mso-font-fix-georgia` and `.mso-font-fix-times_new_roman` fall back to `sans-serif` when they should use `serif`.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - A template placeholder comment was left in production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - There is an empty `<!---->`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncation means the body was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may be stripping these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are present in the original message, no issue exists. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and relay-captured headers cannot confirm alignment.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 87 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so final destinations and any UTM parameters were not resolved. The truncated source also limits link inspection.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~27 content and product images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Deduplicate the five `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels to one, if the vendor allows.
4. Fix the MSO fallbacks to `serif`, escape `&` as `&amp;` in the `<title>`, and strip the leftover template placeholder comment and the duplicate meta tags.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message, not the relay copy.
6. Rerun QA with an untruncated body and a redirect-following probe on the 87 tracking links. That will let the footer, unsubscribe link, UTMs and landing pages be checked.
