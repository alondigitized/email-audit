# The 4th is gone but our sale is STILL ON ❤️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- My sixth Pottery Barn this week — I counted. After "Happy 4th" with extra 20% off, "Sunny savings," the countdown hourglass, and "Ends tomorrow," this one shows up the day after the holiday to tell me the sale is *still* on. The subject line is self-aware enough about the awkward timing that it almost earns it — almost. Compared to CB2's "July 4th Sale. 3 days left." which landed clean and quiet with a specific three-day window, this feels like someone covering their tracks after missing a flight.
- The email itself is competent. Clean render, clear 20% off hero, the Open Box Outlet module is a genuine add for someone like me who knows outlet pricing is real. The "Recommended Just for You" grid actually has bedroom and living room pieces that look close to what I've been browsing — so the personalization engine is at least awake. The rewards callout at the bottom ("Earn 40% Back") matters to me because I track those points.
- But I've opened this exact email five times already in spirit. I won't click today. I'll mark it read and wait for the final-hours push which, at this cadence, arrives in about eleven hours. I would not forward this — Crate & Barrel's "Last call: All-Clad, KitchenAid, De'Longhi" email from today was a much stronger pass-along because it named products I'd actually text my wife about.

## What's working

- **Open Box Outlet module** — "Save big on our famous styles at unbelievably low prices" + its own "SHOP NOW" CTA is the most actionable thing in the email. Outlet pricing hits different when you're furnishing a second home on a budget.
- **Rewards callout at the bottom** — "Earn 40% Back in Rewards" reminds me there's a reason to buy here vs. Article or CB2, and it's visible without hunting for it.
- **Clean render, no friction** — No broken images, no lorem ipsum ghosts, no layout collapse. The hierarchy reads hero → category nav → secondary offer → recs → rewards. That's the right order.
- **"Recommended Just for You" grid** — The bedroom and sofa shots actually match my browsing pattern. It's not random inventory dumps like Wayfair pulls sometimes.

## What's weak

- **Cadence is completely blown** — This is number five in forty-eight hours. The "STILL ON" subject reads as apologetic panic, not confident urgency. Article ran three emails over eight days and it felt like a considered arc; this is the same arc compressed into a weekend bender.
- **No deadline visible anywhere in the email body** — "The sale is still on" begs the question: until when? The hero, the Open Box module, the category tiles — none of them anchor a close date. That's what CB2's "3 days left" did so cleanly.
- **"SHOP SALE" CTA is a dead end** — It's not a specific product, not a category, not even a landing page hint. After five emails, "SHOP SALE" feels like a shrug.
- **Family of brands cross-sell is clutter here** — Williams-Sonoma, Rejuvenation, Mark & Graham row takes up real estate when I came here for furniture. The C&B parent brand pulled something similar and it felt like they ran out of their own deals.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the deadline in the hero** — "Sale ends Sunday at midnight" or "48 hours left" anchors the call to action. Right now the urgency floats.
- 2. **Rename the CTA to something category-specific** — "Shop Living Room Sale" or "Shop Beds & Sofas" for my segment. "SHOP SALE" is a missed segmentation opportunity given they clearly know my browse history.
- 3. **Cut the family-of-brands row** — Or collapse it below the footer. I came for Pottery Barn furniture. The cross-sell dilutes the message on email six of the week.
- 4. **Throttle back to one more email this cycle** — One final-hours send, then silence until fall. The brand equity loss from fatigue is real.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your picks are still on sale — but not for long.`

The `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` is the credit card footer banner — doubly broken (also references a 2022 asset path).

**PHP `header()` calls in CSS block** — the `<style>` block contains literal PHP header directives:
```html
<style>
  ...
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');
  header("Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0");
  header("Pragma: no-cache");
</style>
```
These are server-side PHP statements rendered verbatim into the HTML. They do nothing in an email client but indicate a template rendering failure — a dynamic asset CDN snippet was pasted into the wrong block. No functional breakage in current form, but signals a template hygiene issue.

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>` renders as blank in clients that display it (some desktop clients show the title in tab/preview).

**Plain-text body is 74% URLs** (12,195 of 16,472 chars). Spam filters that score plain-text/HTML ratio will flag this. The plain-text version appears auto-generated from click-redirect URLs rather than readable copy.
