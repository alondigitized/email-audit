## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 12 probed links returned only HTTP 429 (bot rate limiting) and no hard failures. The main confirmed code-level issues are 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, many images without alt text, and `http://` links. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because AgentMail did not capture the headers. The HTML source was also truncated, so footer content could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **429 responses, not broken links:** All probed links returned 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale"), `https://www.wayfair.com/?...` ("Wayfair"), the three product links and both `wayfairapp.onelink.me` links. The probe can't confirm whether they resolve. This is a probe limitation, not a defect.
- **Coverage gaps:** 68 links were skipped, and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s time budget (`BY39013871`, `BP69028635`, the BCANHOME bidet product, and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`). Treat their status as unknown.
- **`http://` link targets:** The banner link is `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`. The app link carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Both depend on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels:** 20 impression pixels are served over `http://`, from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`. Each is flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Blocked pixels mean under-counted impressions.
- **Parameter label mismatch:** The `onelink.me` URLs carry `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labeled "Triggered" may skew campaign attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **Duplicate app link:** "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" appears at least twice with identical parameters except `_eml`. This is consistent with per-link tracking and is not an error.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flagged these images:
  - The primary banner `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jpg`, which is also a link to `/daily-sales`. Its accessible name is empty, so screen readers will announce the link with no label.
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`, `..._Skinny-EverythingShips...`, `..._Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`.
  - 12 instances of `stars_10.png`. Each is wrapped in a product link (the `[image: stars_10.png]` links), so each product link has no accessible name beyond the filename.
  - The open pixel `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels. For pixels, `alt=""` is acceptable, but an absent attribute is not.
- **Missing `lang`:** The visible `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="...">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Mobile word breaking:** `@media (max-width:480px){.ProductRec-name{word-break:break-all;overflow:hidden;display:block;height:48px !important}}` breaks product names mid-word. The fixed 48px height can clip long names such as the Nostalgia popcorn cart title.
- **Not assessable:** The HTML is truncated, so the remaining structure and the `<title>` could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or in the probed URLs. Because the source is truncated, the body and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Both were not found. The QA tool notes this may be a relay capture limitation. These headers are unverified, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is normal.
- **Footer elements:** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address couldn't be verified because the source was truncated. The probe says an unsubscribe link is always included, but no result for it was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters:** None appear on any URL. Attribution uses Wayfair's own scheme: `refid=MKTEML_143924`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16784`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `source=batch`, `_emr` and `_eml`. This is internally consistent, but a third-party analytics tool that expects `utm_*` won't attribute the traffic.
- **Variant identifiers:** `varid=7` and `batchid=3` suggest a test or variant send.
- **Product links:** Each carries `mplid` and `sku` (for example `mplid=RWFE1163&sku=RWFE1163`). The product-link URLs are consistent with the beverage-refrigerator subject line. Landing-page content couldn't be verified because of the 429s.
- **Redirect dependency:** The `http://` banner link and the `af_web_dp` fallback add a redirect hop that can drop or alter parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels, `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and the banner links over HTTPS.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the banner images. Use `alt=""` on the pixels. Give each star-rating image alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", or an `aria-label` on the wrapping link.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Remove `word-break:break-all` from `.ProductRec-name`. Use `overflow-wrap:anywhere` or `line-clamp` instead.
5. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present in the original send, outside the relay. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
6. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links and the 429 links from a throttled or allowlisted client.
7. Align the `onelink.me` `c=Triggered` value with the actual batch send, or document why it differs.
8. Add `utm_*` parameters if the downstream analytics stack relies on them.
