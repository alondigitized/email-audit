# New score alert: Bookcases
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- So the subject is "New score alert: Bookcases" — like Wayfair's algorithm flagged something specifically for me — and then I open it and the hero image is just... blank. A white box with a tiny blue dot where an image should be. That's not a great first impression on a "we picked this for you" email. Then it launches into "inspo for your space," a scroll-forever grid of a bookcase, a console table, a standing desk, a teal sofa, a lounge chair, a red stool, a green dresser, air conditioners, a pergola, slipcovers, recliners, sheds, mattresses, Adirondack chairs, sheets, fountains, dinnerware, more dressers, more console tables, a sectional, and console tables again. The bookcase is literally the first tile and then never mentioned again.
- This is the same move Wayfair pulled with "DINING CHAIRS | Shop Now >>" two days ago — subject promises one category, email delivers the entire catalog. At least this time the first tile is on-theme, which is more than I can say for the dining chairs disaster. But "New score alert" implies personalization, some kind of algorithmic match, and instead I get the exact same kitchen-sink grid Wayfair sends every day. I'm not clicking this, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — there's nothing here specific enough to send a friend.

## What's working

- The very first product tile actually is a bookcase, so the subject isn't a total lie like the dining chairs email was.
- Star ratings are visible under most tiles, which at least gestures at social proof.
- The "48-HR ENDS TONIGHT" clearance banner near the bottom has real urgency if I scroll that far.
- Section like "Finds for every budget" and "Just for You: Hybrid Mattresses" nod at personalization language even if the content doesn't back it up.

## What's weak

- The hero image at the very top is straight-up broken — blank white space with a tiny stray blue dot where a lifestyle image should be. Bad first impression before I even scroll.
- Subject promises "Bookcases" and a "score," implying a personalized match; the actual email is the same undifferentiated everything-grid Wayfair always sends — AC units, sheds, fountains, dinnerware, none of which is a bookcase.
- No pricing or percent-off visible on the bookcase tile itself — I can't tell if this is even a deal.
- Total information overload — I count something like 24+ product tiles before I even hit the clearance banner. My eye has nowhere to land.
- "total sellouts" for sheds is a random non-sequitur wedged into a supposedly bookcase-focused email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken hero image — that blank box with the floating dot is going to tank open-to-scroll rates before anyone even reads the first line.
- 2. If the subject says "Bookcases," the first screen needs to be bookcases — 3-4 options with price, wood tone, and shelf count, not one tile buried in a 24-item grid.
- 3. Cut the grid down hard. Pottery Barn's "Ready-to-Ship Seating" email worked because it picked one category and one anxiety (delivery time) and stuck with it — this email tries to be everything and lands as nothing.
- 4. Show an actual "score" or match reason ("87% match based on your recent browsing") if you're going to use that language in the subject — right now it's just marketing copy with nothing behind it.
- 5. Move the clearance countdown up near the top if it's real urgency — burying "ENDS TONIGHT" at the very bottom of a mile-long scroll wastes the best CTA in the email.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Solid wood, ladder, and modular styles — ready to ship`
