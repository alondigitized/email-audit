# Colorful pillows, shipping free
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the second "free shipping" send today. "On sale & delivered for free" went out earlier with a pillow-and-throw hero, and now I get a pillow hero with "Pillows ship free." I read this as the same email with a different crop. The Christmas Shop launch landed in the same inbox hours ago, and it's down in the footer here like an afterthought.
- The email itself is nice. "Made for Mixing" is a clean stack of patterned pillows. The three swatch tiles (neutrals, blues, greens) match how I shop, because I pick a palette first and then buy. It's what I'd drop in my Pinterest-adjacent folder. But there's no number anywhere. Free shipping on what, and at what threshold? Crate & Barrel's Double Rewards banner and CB2's "Up to 50% off" at least gave me a number to react to.
- I'd save it and probably not click today. I wouldn't forward it. A pillow-pairing lookbook isn't something I send a friend, and I'm not renovating around throw pillows.

## What's working

- The "Made for Mixing" hero is a clear single image with one pill button, "Pillows Ship Free," so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The shop blues / neutrals / greens tiles are palette-first browsing, which is how I plan rooms across two houses.
- "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" in the top bar answers the question I actually care about with furniture, which is when it arrives.
- The "Our Favorite Pillow Pairings / Get Inspired" block feels like a real lookbook. Article's "Fresh for fall" hero worked the same way.
- The Free Design Services "Book a Free Appointment" tile is the only showroom-style content in the email, and it's useful for a two-house project.

## What's weak

- "Ships free" has no threshold and no end date. The fine print at the bottom is the only place the conditions might live, and it's tiny.
- This repeats today's earlier "On sale & delivered for free" send, which had the same free-shipping hook and a nearly identical hero. That's two shipping-and-pillows emails in one day.
- The Christmas Shop tile is buried near the bottom, even though that's the day's actual news. It's a red block with no photo of the product.
- "Recommended just for you" shows stockings, a curtain rod, a dining table, and a bed. That reads as random, not personal, and it doesn't acknowledge what I've browsed.
- The footer is dense and hard to read on a phone. The App Store, rewards card, and Affirm blocks stack up before the legal text.

- 1. Put the offer in the hero with a number and a deadline, for example "Free shipping on all pillows through Sunday," so the promise has terms I can see.
- 2. Don't send this the same day as another free-shipping pillow email. Combine them, or space them a day apart.
- 3. Move the Christmas Shop up and give it a photo. Right now it's the most interesting thing in the email and it's an afterthought.
- 4. Make "Recommended just for you" actually follow browse history, or label it as bestsellers.
- 5. Link a specific pillow pairing with price and reviews, not only the category.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 58 opaque redirect links. It has about 55 images without alt text and three non-HTTPS image sources. The recipient's email address is also passed in plaintext in image URLs. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and physical address could not be inspected. Header-level compliance and authentication results are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click redirects:** All 58 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, an encoded redirect. The QA probe skipped them, so destinations and HTTP status are unverified.
- **No UTMs visible:** The visible redirect URLs carry no `utm_*` parameters. They may be encoded inside `qs=`, which can't be confirmed.
- **Mixed content:** Three images load over `http://`:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- **Stale or cross-brand assets:**
  - The footer banner path is dated `2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner`.
  - A top banner path is dated `2025/US/H25_TOP_InStockFurniture`.
  - `spacer_0.gif` is hosted at `edm.westelm.com`, which makes this send depend on another brand's asset host.
- **Third-party trackers:** The email loads pixels from `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1`, `svopn.potterybarn.com` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/0316695b-...?mi_cid=...`. Several tracking vendors fire on open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 55 `<img>` elements have no `alt`. This covers the hero slices (`F26_Decor_B6_PillowPairings/slices_01–16.jpg`), the footer slices (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) and the product images (`classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and others). With images off or a screen reader, the sliced hero and footer are unreadable. The tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`) should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The document has `<title></title>`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Stray code in `<style>`:** The second `<style>` block contains PHP text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and could break parsing of rules that follow it in strict clients.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat">` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. Without a fallback stack you can't rely on the font rendering.
- **Hidden preheader:** The text "We make it easy to mix & match like a pro" has an unescaped `&`, which is minor.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 11,091 of 15,338 characters (72%) are URLs, so the plain-text alternative is mostly tracking links.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** None were found in the visible source.
- **Recipient email in URLs:** The address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears as a plaintext query parameter in:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=...`

  The `svopn` request is unencrypted `http://`, so the address is exposed in transit. It can also leak through proxy logs and referrer data. `coreemail.html` is also used as an `<img>` source and returns an HTML page, not an image, which suggests a misused tracking endpoint.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both headers were not found. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are genuinely absent, the email lacks one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058), which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact, not a sender fault.
- **Body unsubscribe and address:** The visible HTML is cut off before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot verify:** The redirect wrappers hide destinations and the probe skipped all 58 links, so landing-page alignment with "Colorful pillows, shipping free" can't be checked.
- **Mismatch in assets:** The assets referenced include holiday and stocking creative (`H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `holiday-icons-crewel-stockings`) and an in-stock furniture banner (`H25_TOP_InStockFurniture.jpg`), alongside the pillow hero.
- **Expired-link risk:** The `2025` banner path and a `qs=` token with fixed IDs (`"d":5051,"t":5308`) mean these links and assets may expire or change after the send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (`slices_*`) and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS, especially the `svopn` pixel.
3. Remove the recipient email from `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use an opaque hashed ID instead.
4. Delete the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and add a real `<title>`.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers from the origin. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment outside the relay.
7. Resolve the `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirects in a follow-up pass. Verify destinations return 200 and carry consistent UTMs.
8. Replace the 2022 and 2025 asset paths and the `edm.westelm.com` spacer with current assets from the same host.
9. Shorten the plain-text alternative, or render it as readable text with fewer raw tracking URLs.
