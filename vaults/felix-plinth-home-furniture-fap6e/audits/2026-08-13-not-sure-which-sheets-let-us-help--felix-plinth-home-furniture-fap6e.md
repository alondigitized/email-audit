# Not sure which sheets? Let us help.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Yep, this is Crate & Kids again — same brand that hit me yesterday with the "enchanted nursery" fairy tale lookbook that had nothing to do with the canopy bed I've been pricing out. This one's at least trying to be useful — "find their perfect bedding fabric" with a fabric comparison guide (percale, gauze, washed cotton, jersey) is a genuinely smart content format. But it's still kids' sheets: toddler sizing, a baby in an onesie on cotton jersey, a kid peeking out from under a duvet. None of that is my registry. My registry is a primary bedroom canopy bed and a guest room refresh, not a nursery.
- The fabric-guide format is the best idea I've seen from this brand all week — swatch language, "crisp and cool," "soft and cozy," material/breathability/feel ratings — that's the kind of decision-support content that actually works on me for adult bedding. It's just aimed at the wrong room in my house. I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding — unless a friend has a toddler, in which case, sure, this one's actually decent.

## What's working

- The fabric-comparison format (percale vs. gauze vs. washed cotton vs. jersey, each with material/breathability/feel + a customer quote) is smart, useful content — better than a flat sale banner.
- Star ratings under each fabric name are a real trust cue, not just decoration.
- "2 Ways + Free Shipping" messaging is visible and clear under each collection.
- Organic cotton called out consistently — that's a real spec, not vague marketing fluff.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text or broken images, easy to scroll even if I'm not the audience.

## What's weak

- This is kids' bedding, full stop — a baby in a onesie, a toddler under a duvet, "supersoft" and "cozy cloud" language pitched at a nursery, not my primary or second-home reno.
- No price is visible anywhere on the fabrics themselves — I have to click through to even know what "perfect bedding fabric" costs me.
- "fall 2026 lookbook: storybook style" at the bottom doubles down on the kid-brand identity — reinforces this isn't for me.
- Zero mention of loyalty points or rewards — I'm shopping on my highest-rewards card and this gives me nothing to work with there.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely.** I don't have kids' furniture on any list — Crate & Barrel clearly knows my browsing history includes a canopy bed and bedroom refresh (their "up to 25% off" send nailed it at 8/10). Crate & Kids needs to stop landing in this inbox, or at minimum needs an adult-bedding cross-sell block for subscribers who've never bought kids' product.
- 2. **Put a price on the hero fabric callouts.** "stay cool organic cotton percale" with no dollar figure next to it is a missed CTA — Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel both learned that a real number (Sale $1,899 Reg. $2,299) is what gets me to actually read past the hero.
- 3. **If this does get sent to adult subscribers, reframe as "which sheets do YOU need" with grown-up sizing/imagery**, not a baby in a onesie — the format is good, the casting is wrong for me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `A fabric guide to help you pick the right sheets`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Percale, gauze, or jersey? Here's the difference`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Compare feel, breathability & durability across our best organic cotton bedding`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Real ratings, real fabric swatches — find your match in 30 seconds`
