# UP TO 70% OFF new sale styles + New arrivals with classic farmhouse style
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- Fourteen. I want to say that number out loud before I get into anything else, because this is — by my count — the fourteenth Pottery Barn email since Tuesday. The June 12 "NEW DEALS ADDED" send was thirteen. The "FREE design help" was also June 12. And now here's a third email on the same calendar day, with a subject line that's actually two emails stapled together: a 70% off sale push AND a new arrivals farmhouse pitch. Neither one gets room to breathe.
- I'll give Pottery Barn credit where it's earned — the rendered email is visually clean. The hero lifestyle shot is the kind of sun-drenched, linen-and-rattan living room that I genuinely want to reference when I'm furnishing the second property. The "New Arrivals" section scrolls into a tidy bedroom vignette and a product grid that covers rugs, accent chairs, and bedding. The "Recommended Just for You" block actually shows pieces that track with things I've been looking at. All of that is fine. Good, even.
- But I've opened thirteen of these emails already this week. The goodwill burned through. I mark this as read and move on. I don't forward it. I don't click unless one of the recommended items happens to be something I've been sitting on.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is solid** — "Summer Sale Up to 70% off" reads immediately in the top third without hunting for it; the offer lands before the lifestyle imagery competes.
- **New arrivals farmhouse vignette** — that bright, natural-light living room shot with the layered neutrals is exactly what I'm pulling inspo from for the second home; someone in product selection is earning their salary.
- **"Recommended Just for You" block** — this is the one section that treats me like a known customer, not a cold list hit; the product tiles look calibrated to prior browsing, which is more than the Crate & Barrel family sends do.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, images load, text doesn't overlap; this is table stakes but it's worth noting because it's not always a given.

## What's weak

- **Cadence is broken at this point** — three emails on June 12 from the same brand is not marketing, it's noise; Article has sent me four times in twelve days and I still open them because there's a gap between sends; Pottery Barn has eliminated any sense of occasion.
- **Double-barreled subject dilutes both messages** — a 70% off sale and a new arrivals launch are two different intents for two different moods; forcing them into one subject line means neither one punches through.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs are everywhere and specific nowhere** — the grid has at least four "Shop Now" buttons and I can't tell which category each links to without hovering; CB2's "ALMOST OVER" email last week put the collection name on each button and it felt intentional by comparison.
- **No deadline visible** — the 70% off offer has no end date I can see; if the sale is almost over, say so; the urgency that's doing real work here is purely manufactured by the subject line copy, not by any structural signal in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one message per send and commit** — either this is a sale email or it's a new arrivals email; this specific combination makes me feel like someone hit "send" twice and missed one merge.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the sale** — a visible "Sale ends Sunday" line under the hero 70% off claim would have made me tap; right now I assume the sale runs forever, so there's no reason to act today over next week.
- 3. **Label the "Shop Now" buttons by category** — "Shop Outdoor" / "Shop Bedding" / "Shop Rugs" so the grid reads like a menu, not a wall of identical buttons.
- 4. **Rest the cadence** — one email June 12, one June 13, one June 14; this is a fixable problem that's destroying deliverability trust faster than any render bug ever could.
- 5. **Lean harder into the personalized block** — the "Recommended Just for You" section is buried below two full-height lifestyle spreads; move it up to position two, right after the hero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh styles in living, bedroom, and outdoor — sale prices, limited time`
