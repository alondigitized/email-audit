## What's working

- The marble slab hero is genuinely striking — moody, editorial, not another stock-lifestyle shot with a family I don't relate to.
- No countdown clock. After three straight CB2 sends this week with the same ticking timer, dropping the gimmick actually stands out.
- "EARN 10% BACK in Rewards Dollars every day" hits me directly — I run everything through my highest-rewards card, so loyalty math like this is the one thing that actually moves me.
- The category button stack (Furniture / Lighting / Pillows / Decor & Mirrors) is a clean, scannable way to skip straight to what I'm shopping for instead of hunting through a wall of product tiles.
- Clear hierarchy in the bedroom section — one hero shot, one CTA, no clutter.

## What's weak

- "Up to 30% off" with a dagger footnote is the same soft-ceiling language every one of these sends leans on — I never know what I'm actually getting until checkout.
- The $5,000 Refresh sweepstakes runs "August 13 – September 13" — today's September 10, so this is buried mid-scroll with barely three days left and zero urgency flagged about it.
- It's a long email — hero, sweepstakes, bedroom collection, app pitch, rewards pitch, text-signup pitch — by the bottom I've lost track of the original 30% off hook.
- Nothing here differentiates "Fall Sale" from any other seasonal CB2 blast — no new-for-fall framing, no "renovation season" angle that'd actually land with someone mid-reno like me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the specific number, not "up to." If the real average discount is closer to 20%, say 20% and let 30% be the ceiling in fine print — it reads more honest and I trust it more.
- 2. Flag the sweepstakes deadline explicitly ("3 days left to book your design consult") since it's actually expiring — right now it just looks like evergreen filler.
- 3. Cut the email by a third. Hero + one collection + rewards callout is plenty; the app pitch, text-signup pitch, and rewards pitch stacked at the bottom dilute the one offer I came here for.
- 4. Give the "Fall Sale" a reason to exist beyond the season name — tie it to a "refresh before hosting season" angle the way Pottery Barn's cozy-living-room send did, which felt more like a moment than a markdown.

- Legacy Dotomi ad-sync pixels loaded over **plaintext HTTP** (not HTTPS): `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, and `pub1.php` through `pub10.php` at `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pubN.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`.
