# Up to 50% off beds, cribs, storage & more!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the sixth-ish Crate & Barrel-family email hitting my inbox today, except this one's from the kids offshoot — Crate & Kids — and honestly my first reaction is "wrong list." I'm mid-reno on two houses right now, dining rooms and lighting and rugs, not nurseries and crib sets. The 50% off headline grabs the eye for half a second, but the second I register "cribs" and see the nursery hero shot, my brain checks out — this isn't my project.
- To be fair, buried in the "select styles" fine print and the backpack/dresser grid further down, there's some crossover — a dresser, some storage pieces — but the email leads with baby gear, not general home furnishing, so it reads like a segment-fail rather than a targeted send. Compare that to today's earlier Crate & Barrel dining room email, which I actually gave an 8 because it was useful to me specifically — this one's the opposite: on-brand execution, wrong audience.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. If anything I'd forward it to my sister who actually has a toddler — not to a friend who's furnishing a house like I am. Marking read and moving on.

## What's working

- The 50% off banner is bold and impossible to miss at the top — clean visual hierarchy, no hunting for the offer.
- "Shop the Sale" and "Shop New Arrivals" buttons are big, high-contrast, easy tap targets on mobile.
- The "we love these for you" product grid actually shows real pricing with strikethrough discounts ($54 → $43.20), which is more concrete than a lot of the vague "new arrivals" blocks Crate & Barrel proper has been running lately.
- Layout is clean start to finish — no overlapping text, no broken image slots, consistent Crate & Kids branding throughout.
- The new Crate Card / Rewards Dollars teaser is a nice loyalty touch if you're already in that ecosystem — Wayfair's "Fearless design" email didn't even bother with a rewards hook.

## What's weak

- Wrong sub-brand for me entirely — cribs, nursery bedding, strollers, a "Baby Registry" nav link. I don't have a baby. This should never have hit my inbox given what I actually shop for.
- The hero mixes "summer sale" with "500+ new fall arrivals" two scrolls down — pick a season, I'm already tired of every C&B email today jumping between clearance and new drops.
- No specific hero product — it's a banner, not an anchor item, so there's nothing to click through to directly the way CB2's marble email at least had one clear obsession.
- Zero size/fit/availability info on any product tile — just photo and price, which doesn't help anyone actually furnishing anything.
- This is the fifth-plus Crate & Barrel family send today between C&B, Crate & Kids, and whatever else — my inbox fatigue with this brand family is real at this point, same complaint I had about the sofa-raves email yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely.** If purchase history shows zero baby/kids category activity, this email shouldn't exist for me — that's the single biggest lift here, bigger than any copy tweak.
- 2. Pick one season and commit — either "summer clearance" or "fall arrivals," not both stacked in one send; it muddies the offer.
- 3. Lead the hero with one specific hero product (a crib, a dresser) linked directly, the way Article's room-tour format anchors on a specific piece instead of a generic banner.
- 4. Add basic product info (dimensions, ships-free flag consistency) to the "we love these for you" grid — right now it's just floating price tags.
- 5. Cap send frequency across the C&B family — five-plus emails a day from one parent brand is the fastest way to get the whole family filtered.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Furnishing a nursery? Up to 50% off ends soon`
