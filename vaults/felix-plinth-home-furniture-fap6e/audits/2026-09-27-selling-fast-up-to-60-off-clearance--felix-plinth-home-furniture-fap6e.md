# Selling fast: up to 60% off clearance!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel Kids. Again. This is the third one of these in my inbox this cycle, and I've said it before — I don't have kids, this is not my house, not my aesthetic, not my problem. "Up to 60% off + new markdowns" is a decent clearance hook, but it's buried under a whole subsection of a brand I'm not part of, followed by a Venus Williams kids collab I scrolled past twice thinking it was the same image duplicated by a render glitch.
- Then, buried way down, is the actual thing that would've gotten me: a Nuna PIPA travel system and — more relevantly — a Jenny Lind 7-drawer dresser and a Naturepedic crib mattress. If I had a kid on the way, sure. I don't. What I do have is a kitchen and a mudroom mid-renovation in the second house, and nothing here — no rugs, no lighting, no furniture that isn't juvenile — speaks to that.
- Not clicking. Not forwarding — unless it's to my sister who actually has a toddler, in which case, sure, I'd screenshot the dresser. Straight to archived.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off + new markdowns" is a real, specific number up top — that's the one line that could've pulled a non-parent like me in if the rest of the email backed it up.
- The clearance hero photo (amber floral, soft neutral tones) is genuinely pretty — on-brand with the Pottery Barn Thanksgiving Shop email that scored a 7 with me this week for similar mood lighting.
- The Jenny Lind dresser and the Naturepedic mattress under "we love these for you" are legit product picks with visible prices — that's more concrete than most of what C&B Kids sends me.
- Nuna travel system section has a clear single hero product and one CTA, no clutter.

## What's weak

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids sending to someone who has zero kids — same complaint I logged on 09-27's "EVERYTHING inside ships free" email. The targeting hasn't improved in two weeks.
- The Venus Williams tennis collab section appears TWICE, back to back, with nearly identical imagery — that's either a render bug or somebody fell asleep on the template. Either way it reads broken.
- Category nav up top (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, 40-80% Off Final Sale) is the one spot that could serve me, but it's six tiny text links with no visual weight — my eye skips right past it to the giant kid-photo hero.
- The "up to 60%" headline and the "up to 20% off baby essentials" banner further down are two different offers stacked in one email — feels like two campaigns duct-taped together, not one coherent send.
- Nothing here — no swatches, no shipping-time clarity for furniture, no showroom tie-in — despite this being a "renovation" household's actual want list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Kids entirely, or lead a "shared household" version with the Furniture/Rugs/Decor links up top where the tennis collab currently sits twice — that's the single biggest lift since I'm clearly on this list for the parent brand, not the kids sub-brand.
- 2. Fix the duplicate Venus Williams block — it's identical content rendered twice in a row, which looks like a QA miss, not a feature.
- 3. Split the two offers ("60% off clearance" vs. "20% off baby essentials") into two sends or clearly subordinate one — right now they compete for the same eyeball space.
- 4. Pull "Shop Furniture" and "Shop Rugs" into a real visual module (photo + CTA) instead of a plain text link row — CB2's "New arrivals" email at least gave its category links imagery.
- 5. If clearance is the real headline, show 2-3 actual clearance SKUs with strikethrough pricing near the top, the way the Nuna/Jenny Lind block does further down — don't make me scroll past two kids-collab blocks to find real inventory.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Final markdowns: up to 60% off, going fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance drops across furniture, rugs, and lighting — while it lasts`
