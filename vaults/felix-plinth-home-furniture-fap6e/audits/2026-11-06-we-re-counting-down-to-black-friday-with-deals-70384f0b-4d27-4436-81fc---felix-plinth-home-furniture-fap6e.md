# We’re counting down to Black Friday with deals!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Crate & Barrel family send in about two days, and it's the same email I got at 8 AM wearing a different subject line. The "Countdown to Black Friday: up to 60% off clearance" hero is clear enough, and the Double Rewards strip at the top is what I care about as a points-card guy. But I've already seen that strip in the 11/06 "2x Rewards on dressers, cribs, beds & more!" send and the 11/06 "Countdown to Black Friday" send. Same red "last Double Rewards this year!" block, same Jenny Lind bed, same strollers.
- The body is a nursery catalog. Nuna strollers, a Babyletto glider, a baby activity chair, and a baby bedding tile. I'm renovating two houses, so a Jenny Lind dresser and a dark-green bedroom shot are the only parts that apply to me. The rest is clutter. It's also a very long scroll, and the fine print at the bottom is a wall of text.
- I'd skim it and maybe tap "shop furniture" because of the rewards. I wouldn't forward it. I'd file it with the others I've saved to the folder and move on.

## What's working

- The "last Double Rewards this year!" block gives me a real deadline, Nov 17, and tells me the rewards math is on.
- The 2x2 category grid at the top (furniture, rugs, kids bedding, decor & storage) is easy to scan and tap.
- The green "Black Friday deals first" sign-up block is a good early-access hook, and it's one I'd actually use.
- The dark green bedroom lifestyle shot is moodier and more grown-up than most kids' content. It's the one image that felt like my renovation.
- The new Crate Card announcement is relevant to someone who optimizes card rewards.

## What's weak

- The "we love these for you" section is strollers, a glider and an activity chair. None of it fits a two-house renovation.
- It repeats the other sends this week: the same rewards strip, the same Jenny Lind bed, the same "countdown" language. Seven in two days is a lot.
- "Up to 60% off" and "up to 20% off" are stacked in different blocks, and I can't tell which applies to what. The fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense.
- There are too many CTAs: Shop Now, Shop All Clearance, Sign Up Now, Shop Top Categories, Learn More, Download Now, and Book a Free Appointment. No single action stands out.
- The email shows no sign it knows me. It treats me like a stranger on a general kids list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap the cadence and consolidate. One Black Friday email a day, with the rewards strip only in the first one.
- 2. Make the body respond to behavior. If I've been browsing dressers and beds, lead with those and drop the nursery gear.
- 3. Pick one offer for the hero. Either the clearance discount or Double Rewards, not both in competing blocks.
- 4. Cut the CTAs to one primary and one secondary.
- 5. Compare with Pottery Barn Design Services' "Free design appointments! Spots go fast," which has one clear idea and one button. This email buries TheDesignDesk near the bottom, even though it's the most interesting thing for someone doing two houses.
- 6. Wayfair at least segments ("just for you"). Article's UGC bedroom send last week showed real rooms with dressers I could use. This one shows strollers.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure is mostly sound (table layout, MSO conditionals, `lang="en"`). The confirmed problems are widespread missing alt text, a viewport that blocks zoom, and several third-party tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay did not capture them. The source was truncated, so the footer and most link `href`s were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect chains are unverified. No broken links were confirmed.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels** (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…5a8&n=1` through `n=5`) fire from the body. They have no alt text.
- **One hashed identifier is shared across domains.** The 64-hex value `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8` appears in the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com/...?mi_u=` URLs and in the third-party `sr.rlcdn.com` `s=` parameter. It looks like a hashed subscriber ID, though the source doesn't say what it is. Its presence in a third-party request is a privacy and deliverability consideration.
- **Redundant pixels.** There are six identical requests to `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`. There are also an open pixel (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5e081145-…?mi_cid=…`. Together that makes 12 or more tracking requests, which adds load and can hurt image-blocking clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 33 images** in the QA output, which may be truncated:
  - 20 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
  - The tracking pixels listed above.
  - Content images with no alt text give image-blocked and screen-reader users nothing. Pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** Both `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` appear in the CSS.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 DOCTYPE.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. The head also has duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv` declarations, and three redundant `format-detection` metas.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was not removed.
- **Conflicting image rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **The title doesn't match the subject.** `<title>` is generic ("Crate & Barrel") rather than the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The truncated source means I can't rule them out further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` is not found** (QA WARN). The QA tool says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) is not found** (QA WARN), so one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer content is not checked.** The body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM compliance.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Because all 78 tracking links were skipped, UTM parameters, final landing URLs and whether they match the "Black Friday countdown" content could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 20 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust:none` where possible.
3. Escape the title as `&amp;`, set it to match the subject, and deduplicate the head metas. Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Reduce the pixel stack, in particular the six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests. Confirm that sending a hashed ID to `sr.rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy policy.
5. Fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries (600px against 640px).
6. Re-run QA with raw headers from the original message, not the relay copy. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
7. Re-run the link check with redirect following enabled. Confirm UTM values and landing pages for a sample of the 78 tracked links.
8. Run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address can be checked.
