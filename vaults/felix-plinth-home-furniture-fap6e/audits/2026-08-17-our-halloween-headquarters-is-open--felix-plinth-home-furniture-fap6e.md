# Our Halloween Headquarters is open!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Halloween Headquarters is open" — this is Pottery Barn's seventh email to me this week, and it's the first one to actually pick a lane. It's Halloween decor, full stop: haunted-house dinnerware, lit-up porch decor, skeleton-in-an-armchair vignettes. It doesn't help me finish either renovation, but I'll give it credit for being visually cohesive and knowing exactly what it is, unlike Tuesday's "Now on Sale ✨ Furniture, decor & more" which had no idea what it was.
- The styling is genuinely good — that haunted-house china pattern on the hero table is the kind of thing I'd screenshot for my wife even if I have zero intention of buying Halloween plates. But there's no offer anywhere until you hit "Design Crew Sweeps" a giant scroll down, and by then I've mentally filed this as "seasonal lookbook, not a sale." I'm not the target for spooky tableware — I'm mid-reno on two houses and my Pinterest-adjacent folder is full of rugs and curtain rods, not skeleton platters.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Maybe to my wife just for the haunted-house dishes, but that's a "look at this" text, not a purchase intent. This gets marked as read and scrolled past for the "Recommended Just For You" block at the bottom, which is honestly the only section that felt like it was actually looking at my purchase history.

## What's working

- The haunted-house china hero shot is legitimately striking — moody, high-production, the kind of thing Crate & Barrel nailed with its apartment-collection hero last week.
- "Recommended Just For You" at the bottom (Cayman Platform Bed, Reeva rug, Belgian Flax Linen Curtain) is real personalization — those are actual furniture categories I shop, not costumes.
- Three clear category CTAs (Lit Decor, Tabletop, Decor) under a consistent black-and-white "SHOP" button style — easy to scan even if I'm not buying.
- No render bugs — every section rendered clean, images loaded, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Zero dollar figures or percentages in the whole Halloween section — same complaint I had about Tuesday's "Now on Sale" email, PB keeps doing hero-only, no-number sends.
- I'm not the Halloween-tableware demo — a guy renovating two houses simultaneously wants curtain rods and rugs, not skeleton platters, and this buries that content below five screens of spooky decor.
- The one real offer, the $1,500 Design Crew Sweepstakes gift card, is stuck two-thirds down the email in a plain black box — that's actually relevant to a renovator like me and it's the last thing I'd ever scroll to.
- 10% rewards card callout is also buried near the footer — for someone who "buys via the highest-rewards card" like me, that should be much higher up, not after the app download pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the send — Halloween tableware and reno-relevant "Recommended Just For You" shouldn't share one email. Article's "Good changes" send stayed on-theme the whole way through; this one has two different emails glued together.
- 2. Move the $1,500 Design Crew Sweepstakes above the Halloween product grid — that's the one thing in here that speaks to someone actively renovating, don't make me scroll past three "SHOP" buttons to find it.
- 3. Put a real number on the Halloween shop — "20% off Halloween decor" beats "Halloween Headquarters is open," same fix Pottery Barn already found for the towel email ("Bath towels from $24.50").
- 4. Pull the 10% rewards card callout up near the hero — Crate & Barrel and CB2 both lead harder on loyalty math than PB does here.

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Halloween Headquarters" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Marketing HTML email built on legacy table-based layout with multiple tracking pixels; automated QA flags missing unsubscribe headers, one deliverability header gap, and widespread missing `alt` attributes plus a handful of insecure `http://` image sources.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 52 tracking/click-redirect links (e.g., `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`) were skipped from HTTP probing by design — no failure confirmed, but unverified.
- Multiple open/tracking pixels present: `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/19deadda-...`. These are standard email open-tracking beacons, not a defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixel**: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is served over plain HTTP — may be blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading, causing open-tracking undercounting.
- No broken/malformed href syntax found in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **29 images missing `alt` text** (QA-confirmed), including primary content banners (`F26_Top_NewArrivals.jpg`, `F26_Top_DesignCrewSweeps_01a/b.jpg`) and all footer/border "slices" decorative images. Screen readers and blocked-image fallback states will render empty.
- **3 images loaded over insecure `http://`**: `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Outlook/mixed-content-strict clients may block these, leaving visible gaps.
- Head contains a stray `<style>` block with PHP-style `header(...)` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); ...`) embedded as literal text inside a CSS `<style>` tag — this is dead/invalid code with no effect in an email client, but indicates a templating error (PHP snippet leaked into static markup) worth flagging to the ESP/template owner.
- Uses `mso-*` conditional comments and standard Outlook/`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` resets — no client-compatibility issues found beyond the above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email is embedded in plaintext in tracking URLs (e.g., `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`, `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) — functioning merge substitution, no unresolved `{{token}}` or `%%FIELD%%` placeholders found in the truncated source.
- No broken personalization tokens detected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA-confirmed) — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than sender omission; cannot confirm true CAN-SPAM compliance from headers alone. Recommend checking raw source at the receiving MTA.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) unconfirmed for the same reason.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via this relay; not conclusive evidence of an authentication failure.
- In-body unsubscribe/footer link presence could not be confirmed from the truncated HTML provided — flag for full-source review.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- CTA/nav link uses opaque tracking redirect (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7...`) rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs — standard for this ESP (Cheetah/Bronto-style), but means UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified without following redirects (these were skipped by the automated probe).
- Customer-service link resolves to a real, direct (non-redirected) URL: `potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...` — consistent domain, no mismatch found.
- No mixed-domain anomalies beyond the expected `westelm.com` asset reference (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`), likely a shared-infrastructure legacy spacer image from a sibling Williams-Sonoma brand — not a broken link, but a cross-brand asset worth flagging.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all 29 flagged images, prioritizing primary content banners over decorative footer slices.
2. Convert the 3 `http://` image sources to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking.
3. Remove the leaked PHP `header(...)` snippet from the `<style>` block in `<head>` — indicates a template rendering bug upstream.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) directly at the MTA/raw-source level, since the AgentMail relay may be stripping them rather than the sender omitting them.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 52 tracking redirects to confirm they resolve to correctly UTM-tagged, live landing pages.
