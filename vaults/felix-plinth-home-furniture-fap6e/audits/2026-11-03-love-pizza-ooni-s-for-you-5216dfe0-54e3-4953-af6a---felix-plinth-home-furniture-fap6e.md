# Love pizza? Ooni's for you →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth Crate & Barrel family send since 11/1, so I was already in snooze mode. This one at least keeps its promise. After the "trending ice cream maker" email earlier today that never showed me an ice cream maker, I did see pizza ovens here. The hero is a Koda 2 Pro oven in a backyard with a "Shop Ooni" button. I'm renovating two houses, so an outdoor kitchen is not a crazy thought. The "ends today! accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" strip above the hero is what caught me, because that's my actual project and it has nothing to do with the pizza oven.
- The render is a very long strip squeezed down to thumbnail size. I can read headlines and button labels. I can't read the body copy or the prices under the products. It's an ad for one brand's gear, a partner-brand takeover that ends with a Black Friday signup, the Reward Mastercard pitch and an app download. I'd skim it, maybe Pinterest-folder the Koda hero, and move on.
- Would I click? Probably not today. Would I forward it? No. If a buddy had a backyard project I'd send him the Koda hero, not this email.

## What's working

- The Ooni hero is a clean, aspirational backyard shot with a plain "Shop Ooni" button, so I knew where to look first.
- The "ends today!" strip on accent chairs and tables is the only thing here that speaks to my renovation, and it's at the top.
- Star ratings and "Ships Free" labels sit under the Koda and Karu ovens. That's the social proof and shipping clarity I want on a big-ticket item.
- The "the essentials to get you started (+ they ship free!)" row bundles accessories like the peel and the stand mixer, so it feels like a real outdoor-kitchen setup.
- The Reward Mastercard "10% back in Reward Dollars" block ties into how I buy. I'm on the highest-rewards card, so that registers.

## What's weak

- The top strip pushes furniture and the hero sells pizza ovens. Two unrelated stories fight each other, and the furniture offer is the one I care about.
- The email runs very long with many stacked sections: two ovens, a viral video, essentials, a Black Friday signup, a "we love these for you" grid, rewards, an app download and The Design Desk. I can't read the small text at this size.
- The "we love these for you" grid has the Le Creuset, Breville, KitchenAid and All-Clad. I've seen this same kitchen lineup in the baking and ice cream sends since 11/1. It's the same-lamp-in-three-emails problem, just with cookware.
- The Black Friday teaser ("be the first to know about our Black Friday deals") asks me to sign up for something I'm already subscribed to. It reads like a stranger email.
- The Design Desk "Book a Free Appointment" is buried at the bottom. That's the one piece relevant to a renovator.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one story. Either lead with the furniture sale ("ends today") or run a dedicated Ooni send, but don't mix them. Pottery Barn's "Book your FREE design services" email had one idea and I liked it more than this.
- 2. Move The Design Desk up, or make it the hero for people who've browsed furniture. A renovator with two houses should see that first.
- 3. Cut the length. Drop the "we love these for you" grid or make it actually personalized to me. Wayfair's flash sale was loud, but one clear purple offer beats ten sections.
- 4. Drop the Black Friday signup for existing subscribers.
- 5. Show a price on the Koda and Karu ovens at a readable size and add one line on delivery timing.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The automated QA shows 0 failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are about 37 images without alt attributes and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path and can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and body links were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 71 links were skipped from HTTP probing because they are tracking or click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are therefore unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** these are present and expected, but they lack alt attributes (see section 3):
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `n=5` (5 pixels, a third-party LiveRamp domain)
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1946939b-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- **Third-party calls:** 5 separate rlcdn.com calls add third-party requests. Image-blocking clients will not fire them.
- **Hostname split:** images load from four hosts: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `sr.rlcdn.com`. This is normal for the setup, but it is more hosts than needed.
- **Video assets:** two Scene7 images have names like `...Ooni_2_Koda_Video_` and `...Ooni_9_Social_video%`. They are probably video-poster stills, and the URLs appear truncated in the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 37 images in the QA output have no alt attribute.
  - About 21 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/...`, for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `8322f5f6-0be8-4f4c-8397-57e0f6d57837.jpg`.
  - 2 are Scene7 images.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, which look like dynamic product-recommendation images.
  - The rest are tracking pixels.
- **Effect of missing alt:** with images blocked, content images collapse to nothing and screen readers announce filenames or URLs. For the content and recommendation images, this likely means the body is unreadable with images off, because there is no text fallback.
- **Tracking pixels:** these should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on some mobile clients. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the XHTML doctype.
- **Doctype mix:** the XHTML doctype is paired with HTML5-style `<meta charset="utf-8">` and a duplicate `Content-Type` meta. This is harmless in practice.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared 4 times with overlapping values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately). This is redundant.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production, with a typo. It is cosmetic.
- **Conflicting image max-width rules:** both `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` and `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }` apply at 768px and above. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Scoping:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` is a global rule that affects every paragraph. Whether that causes layout problems can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the portion of the source I received. The remainder was truncated, so this isn't a full clearance. Per-recipient values appear in pixel URLs (`mi_u=56aaa2787a67...`, `mi_mid=...`), and these look like correctly rendered hashed IDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** both are flagged as not found. The QA notes say these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. That makes this an unverified capture limitation, not a confirmed sender defect. The sender has the header set if the real message carries it, which can't be seen here.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** it is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The `From` domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` shares a subdomain with the image host, which is consistent with a configured ESP setup, but that is only inference.
- **Footer:** the physical address and the unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. The 71 tracking links were skipped, so the destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing page state are all unconfirmed. The only visible identifiers are the ESP's own `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` call. These are tracking IDs, not UTM parameters. Scene7 asset paths (`2026_0515_CB_VF_Ooni`) include a May 2026 date, but the email was captured in November 2026. This may be reused creative, though the path alone doesn't prove staleness.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text to all content images.** Use descriptive alt for the product and hero images, including the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` recommendation images. Use `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions on all tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta to restore pinch-zoom.
3. **Escape the title:** change it to `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
4. **Clean up the template:** remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` comment, the duplicate `format-detection` and `Content-Type` meta tags, and the dead 600px `max-width` rule.
5. **Verify headers on a direct-delivery copy.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` in a copy that bypasses the relay, since this QA path can't see them.
6. **Fix the audit tooling gaps:**
   - Capture the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and merge tokens can be checked.
   - Resolve tracking redirects (follow one hop) so the UTM and landing page checks can run.
   - Treat tracking pixels as a separate class so they don't inflate the missing-alt count.
7. **Review the Scene7 creative path** (`2026_0515_...`) to confirm the assets aren't stale for a November send.
