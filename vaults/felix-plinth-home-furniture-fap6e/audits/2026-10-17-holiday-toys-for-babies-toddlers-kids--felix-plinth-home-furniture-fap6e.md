# Holiday toys for babies, toddlers & kids!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crate & Barrel Kids send I've gotten today, and it's the same nursery and kids' pitch with gifts on top. I'm renovating two grown-up houses, so a kids' gift guide isn't for me. The render is tiny and I'm working from structure and headlines. The hero is "heirloom-quality gifts made for wish lists" with a "Shop Gifts + Toys" button. It's a long gift-guide scroll with a "Shop" button after every block. It looks nice but it's generic.
- It does one thing well: it's organized. Sections include "our begged-for, bestselling gifts," "our perfectly personalized presents," "our #1 grow-with-them gift," "our guaranteed-to-love gift bundles," and "our fave gifts for every age." They run in a sensible order. I couldn't find a single dollar-off or percent-off number I could read at this size, though. The 10/17 "Up to 50% off top dressers, beds, gliders & more!" send at least had an offer. This one has none that I can see.
- I wouldn't click. I'd mark it read, though if I were shopping for a niece I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. I'd only forward it to a friend with a new baby.

## What's working

- The sections are clearly labeled and ordered by gift type, so I can scan quickly.
- The personalized-gift section (monogrammed items, chair with a name) feels premium and giftable.
- Each section has its own "Shop" button, so there's always a next step.
- The photography is warm and consistent with the Crate brand look, and the product tiles show names and prices.
- The "grow-with-them" chair block is a specific hero rather than a vague category.

## What's weak

- It's the third Crate Kids email today, after the "50% off dressers, beds, gliders" sends. That cadence is the annoying part.
- No offer is visible. I can't find a % off, a free-shipping line or a member perk.
- Nothing speaks to me as a 36-year-old renovating two houses. It's kids' gifts and nursery furniture.
- The email is extremely long. Product tiles and body text look small and would need zooming on a phone.
- There's no deadline or shipping-cutoff info, so I get no reason to act now.
- The bottom "we love these for you" tile row is a generic recommendation block that doesn't reflect anything I've browsed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer or a gift-shipping cutoff in the hero, such as "Order by Dec 15 for delivery by Christmas," so the email has a reason to be opened now.
- 2. Cap the cadence. Fold nursery furniture and gifts into one send per day, or suppress the second and third sends for people who didn't click the first.
- 3. Cut the length by dropping two or three sections and keeping the bestsellers, personalized gifts and bundles.
- 4. Segment on browsing history so I get the home-furnishings email I actually care about rather than a kids' list.
- 5. Make product tiles and prices larger on mobile.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Template-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional email (Movable Ink-style `mi.crateandbarrel.com` dynamic images) with 101 click-tracked links. The QA run shows 0 failures and 4 warnings, and the main confirmed issue is widespread missing image alt text. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be inspected directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 101 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe ("Tracking/click-redirect domains"). Destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain).
  - One `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`.
  - One `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/bd550ffa-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
  - The five rlcdn pixels are a request-count and third-party-dependency cost. They are not a defect.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs:** `mi_u=56aaa278…` appears in the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs and `s=56aaa278…` appears in the rlcdn pixel URLs. The values match, so the same recipient identifier is passed to a third party.
- **Repeated asset:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` loads 6 times with the same `mi_u`. This is probably per-slot dynamic content, but it is redundant requests if the slots return the same image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Roughly 60 QA warnings, covering most product and hero images. Examples:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (the bulk of the list)
  - `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5F1.gif`
  - `ed972f76-….gif` and `a4c4b37d-….gif`
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`
- **Alt text vs. empty alt:** The check doesn't distinguish a missing `alt` from an empty `alt=""`. For the pixels and spacers (`448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px`), `alt=""` is correct. For product and hero images, a missing `alt` leaves the image unnamed for screen readers and blank-looking when images are blocked.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resize disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the later `100%` declarations and block user text scaling in iOS Mail and Safari.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype, so it should be `&amp;`. The `<head>` also has duplicate `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` tags, plus three overlapping `format-detection` metas.
- **Dead CSS:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, since both match at 768px and up and the later one wins.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. It's harmless but sloppy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the truncated portion. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are resolved values. Tokens further down the body, in the footer or in href attributes could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers. This is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, and the sender is a high-volume marketer.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverifiable here. The envelope sender is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is aligned with the From domain, but alignment can't be confirmed without the headers.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.
- **Plain-text part is 71% URLs:** 20,136 of 28,360 characters are URLs, above the 70% threshold. The wrapped tracking URLs are what inflate it. This is a minor spam-filter heuristic risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. All 101 tracking links were skipped, so the QA run never resolved the final destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page alignment with the "Holiday toys for babies, toddlers & kids" subject. The `GiftGuide` asset names suggest a gift-guide landing page, but that is not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all product and hero images, and `alt=""` to the pixels and spacers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and remove or fix the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, dedupe the head metas, and delete the dead `max-width:600px` rule and the placeholder comment.
4. Re-run the audit with a source that preserves raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before treating the compliance warnings as real.
5. Add a probe that follows redirects on a sample of the tracking links, to capture the final URLs and UTM parameters.
6. Reduce the plain-text part's URL density by using shorter tracking URLs, or drop the plain-text links that duplicate the HTML.
