# Your chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card ENDS TODAY →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Third Crate & Barrel Kids email today, and I still don't have a baby. The hero is a crib with a half-filled star rating and "want to win a $1,000 gift card?" I'm 36 and renovating two houses, so a nursery crib doesn't reach me. The "ENDS TODAY" subject made me expect a big countdown. The body is a review contest, which means homework before I get a lottery ticket. Then it turns into a catalog of strollers, a glider and a crib mattress.
- The one part I'd look at is the "TheDesignDesk — your design pro is waiting, and it's 100% free" block near the bottom. That's the only thing here that fits two renovations. It sits below the credit card pitch and the app download, so I'd have to scroll about 80% of the page to reach it. I'd mark this as read. I wouldn't forward it. The only reason to open it is to check whether a gift card sweepstakes is worth the effort, and for me it isn't.
- Pottery Barn's "NEW collections for your bedroom" at least gave me a category I'm actually shopping. This is the third Kids send today, after "Start nesting now with fast-ship nursery finds!" and "Your dream nursery starts right here →". Wayfair's "Fall Clearance Ends Tonight" put a deadline on a real discount. Here the deadline is on a contest.

## What's working

- The "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off" block is the one concrete money offer, and it has a clear "Shop Clearance" button.
- The TheDesignDesk block, "100% free" with "Book a Free Appointment," is the one thing relevant to my renovations.
- Prices are visible on the product grid, with the Halloween bag's $34.95 struck through to $14.97. That is easy to scan.
- The footer category nav is clean: Furniture, Decor, Storage and so on. I can skip the nursery stuff and go there.
- The cobranded card block shows Reward Dollars. I use the highest-rewards card, so I noticed it, even though it's low in the email.

## What's weak

- The hero image and copy are for nursery shoppers. The 2/5-star graphic is odd, because it looks like a bad review.
- "Ends today" is about a sweepstakes that needs a product review to enter. The fine print adds a purchase requirement and a legal block, so the offer doesn't feel honest.
- Strollers, car seats, a crib mattress and a glider fill the "we love these for you" section. Nothing in it looks like it was chosen for me.
- The email has too many competing asks: review, bestsellers, clearance, bedding, a credit card, the app, a design appointment, a store locator. There's no single clear hierarchy.
- The second and third sends today repeated the same theme, so the cadence feels spammy. CB2's "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" had the same repetition problem.
- There's no shipping clarity or swatch and sample info for furniture. Only the bedding block says "Ships Free."
- The fine print is a wall of tiny type. It was unreadable on my phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send contest emails only to people who have bought from Kids or who have a registry. Everyone else should get a suppression or a different segment.
- 2. Put the single deadline and the single action in the hero: "Review a product by midnight, win $1,000." Drop the two-button stack.
- 3. Move TheDesignDesk up for non-parents. Article's "New arrivals: Seat selection" worked because it was one idea, and this should be too.
- 4. Add swatch, sample and ship-date info to the furniture products.
- 5. Cut the grid to four products, and make them match the segment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Review one item, win a $1,000 gift card`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$1,000 gift card entry closes at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Takes 2 minutes. Plus up to 60% off markdowns.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Enter by tonight, then shop clearance before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a mobile breakpoint at 460px. It has 63 click-tracked links, several third-party tracking pixels, and about 8 content images with no `alt` attribute. The automated pass rate is 57% (0 failures, 3 warnings). The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and UTM checks are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 63 links were skipped because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains, and landing-page parity are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** The source contains five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels (LiveRamp). It also has `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (open pixel) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/cf081457-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
  - Five identical LiveRamp calls per message add requests with no apparent benefit. The `n=` parameter suggests one pixel per variant or section.
  - The `?s=` value is a long hashed identifier (`56aaa278…`), so the recipient's identity travels in image URLs.
- **Repeated image URL:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times with the same `mi_u` token. If these are per-slot dynamic images, they share one image ID and may resolve to the same content.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images** (the QA tool flagged these, and they are not tracking pixels):
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `e1277dff-8f28-4978-988d-73df5db08f5a.gif`
  - `1a008c44-…jpg`
  - `87907b24-…jpg`
  - `fc3f77e8-…jpg`
  - `18cc53f1-…jpg`
  - `971aa10f-…jpg`
  - `ca6f2fd1-…gif`
  - The Scene7 asset `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_Tert…`
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these show nothing or the filename. Image-only CTAs would be unreadable.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** The `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, and `dv.` pixels are flagged too. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. That is low priority.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue on mobile.
- **Text-size CSS:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` disables text resizing on iOS.
- **Markup validity:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed under that doctype.
  - Three redundant `format-detection` meta tags are present: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, and `telephone=no` again.
- **Template leftover:** The source still contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but sloppy and adds bytes.
- **Conflicting `img` rules:** `img {max-width:600px}` (min-width 768px) and `img {max-width:640px}` (min-width 640px) both match at 768px and above. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never takes effect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appeared in the QA output or the truncated HTML. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid`, and `mi_mid` parameters look like resolved per-recipient values.

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found. The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found, with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support is needed for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be verified here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 8 or so content images listed in section 3. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop the `text-size-adjust:none` wildcard.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, close the `<meta charset>` tag, and remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be stripping them. If they are really missing, add them.
5. Consolidate the five `rlcdn.com` pixels into one, if the data-sharing design allows.
