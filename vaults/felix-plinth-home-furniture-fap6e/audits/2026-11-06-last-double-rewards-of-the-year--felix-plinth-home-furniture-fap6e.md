# LAST DOUBLE REWARDS§ OF THE YEAR
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth CB2 email since 10/30, and the first one that isn't a sale blast. It's a credit-card email, so the "Double Rewards" pitch is aimed at someone I'm not yet. I do put purchases on the highest-rewards card, and 20% back on a CB2 spend does get my attention. But I'm a Crate & Barrel Rewards person already, and the "Double Rewards" line is the same one I saw five times from Crate & Barrel proper. My first thought was that this is the same banner with a different logo.
- The hero is clean and handsome: a green velvet sofa, a big headline, and "20% back" in a readable size. Then it falls apart. The "10%" is struck through and "20%" is next to it, which looks like a pricing mistake until I work it out. Below that is a category grid, then a "Long Weekend Sale up to 50% off" banner. That banner is the same sale I've been told about all week, and it has nothing to do with the card.
- I'd skim it, save the sofa to my Pinterest folder, and not click. I wouldn't forward it either. Pottery Barn's "Pillows ship free" at least gave me one concrete reason to open it.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Double Rewards is on" with "20% back in Reward Dollars" reads in two seconds, and the dark green sofa is on brand and looks good.
- The "Now through Nov 17th, 2026" line gives a real deadline, about 11 days. That's more credible than the "ends tonight" emails earlier this week.
- The category tiles (new arrivals, furniture, lighting, rugs) are pretty and easy to tap. They're exactly the kind of thing I'd save to my inspiration folder.
- The footer strip with the CB2 App and "earn 10% back every day on purchases at CB2 and Crate & Barrel" explains the rewards structure. That's the part I actually care about as a points guy.

## What's weak

- The strikethrough "10%" followed by "20%" is confusing. On first read it looks like a mistake, not a promo.
- The "Long Weekend Sale up to 50% off" banner is stale. It's a Thursday, and I've heard about this sale in nearly every send since 10/30. It also competes with the rewards message, so I don't know which offer is the real one.
- "Learn More" is a weak CTA. It's a vague button that goes to an explainer page, not a product page, and I don't want to read terms.
- The sender is "CB2 Credit Card," which I don't recognize. Nothing about it says I'm a cardholder, and nothing says it knows me. I feel like a stranger who's being marketed a card.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, and it matters here. I can't read the exclusions, the cap or the limits without zooming, and for a rewards offer that's where the catch is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one message. Either run a card-only rewards email or fold it into the sale email. Don't run both at once. Right now it's two emails stacked into one.
- 2. Fix the "10% → 20%" treatment. Say "Earn 20% back, double the usual 10%" in plain words, with no strikethrough.
- 3. Put a worked example near the hero, like "Spend $2,000 on a sofa, get $400 back." Rewards math is what sells me.
- 4. Change the CTA to "Earn 20% back" or "Shop with your card," and link it to a product page, not an explainer.
- 5. Cut the Long Weekend banner. Crate & Barrel's Double Rewards sends at least kept a single story.
- 6. Say who it's from and who it's for. A "CB2 Rewards" sender name and a cardholder-aware line would help.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn 20% back at CB2 through Nov 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Double Rewards: 20% back on your next sofa`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $2,000, get $400 back in Reward Dollars. Ends Nov 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your CB2 Visa earns 2x on everything through Nov 17.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering failure: an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`) in the delivered HTML. It also has widespread missing alt text, plain-HTTP third-party tracking pixels, and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. The QA run scored 43% (1 failure, 3 warnings). The header-based checks (unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be evaluated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so link destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain `http://`.** The pixels are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`. A further `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` pixel does the same. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop these, and the tracking they carry won't fire. Dotomi is a third-party ad-sync vendor, and this many cookie-sync pixels in one send adds load.
- **Open-tracking pixels** (`https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/d68e4d11-…?mi_cid=…`) are served over HTTPS. The only finding on them is the missing alt attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Raw merge token.** See section 4. This is the only hard FAIL in the run.
- **Missing alt text on content images.** Eight images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt:
  - `1f6a4517-…jpg`
  - `fac2228e-…jpg`
  - `6b4bb56d-…jpg`
  - `f5f66ea8-…jpg`
  - `f7c2dbd3-…jpg`
  - `e0e52e3f-…jpg`
  - `19afaa4d-…jpg`
  - `5fedf75e-…jpg`

  With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, these render blank or read out filenames.
- **Missing alt on tracking and pixel images.** This covers the 11 Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel. They should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so assistive tech skips them. The QA can't tell `alt=""` from a missing attribute, so this may be a false positive for some of them.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Minor.** Several `@font-face` URLs have a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). They resolve on most servers but are fragile. The HTML was truncated, so the table structure and any further rendering issues beyond the head weren't assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Unresolved token `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** It was found raw in the delivered message. The `%%…%%` syntax suggests a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style personalization string that did not resolve at send time. The likely cause is a missing or empty product-reviews data feed. Depending on where it sits, recipients see literal token text or a broken/empty module. The truncated source doesn't show its exact location.
- **Subject line character.** `LAST DOUBLE REWARDS§ OF THE YEAR` contains a stray `§` mid-phrase. It looks like a mis-encoded or misplaced symbol (probably a footnote marker or ®). It isn't a merge token, but it is a likely subject-line encoding or authoring defect. Confirm the raw `Subject:` header bytes to rule out an RFC 2047 encoding problem.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Verify against the original message headers.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA attributes this to the relay.
- **Visible footer elements.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link, the physical postal address or the sender identification, so I can't confirm or deny them. The From domain (`mail.cb2.com`) is a sending subdomain and looks consistent with the image and tracking hosts (`image.mail.cb2.com`, `mi.cb2.com`).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment couldn't be verified because the 63 links are behind click-redirect domains and the QA skipped them. The only visible destination parameters are the vendor identifiers on the tracking pixels (`mi_cid`, `dtm_cid`), which aren't campaign UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` token (blocker).** Check the reviews data extension or feed, add a fallback for empty values, and add a pre-send raw-token check.
2. **Add `alt` text** to the eight content images. Add `alt=""` to all pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (or drop them), and reduce the ten cookie-sync pixels if the vendor allows it.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Check the stray `§` in the subject line** and the header encoding.
6. **Re-run the header checks** against the original `.eml` to confirm the following (the AgentMail relay is the likely reason they were missing):
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
   - SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment
7. **Resolve a sample of the redirect links** to confirm the landing URLs and UTM parameters, since the automated QA skipped them.
