# It’s here! Earn DOUBLE REWARDS + Shop up to 60% off The Labor Day Event!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a different animal than the Crate & Kids flood I've been logging all week — this is the actual main Crate & Barrel account, and it's noticeably better executed. The double-rewards banner up top plus the beach-photo hero for "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" is at least a coherent seasonal moment, not another toddler bunk-bed sweepstakes I have to scroll past. The grid underneath — sofas, kitchen brands, tabletop, rugs, outdoor furniture — is actually my category, and the Dyson stick vac at $849.99 (reg $979.95) is the first thing in days from this brand family that made me pause.
- That said, it's long. Really long. By the time I hit "we love here for you" with a random dresser, bed, and cocktail table, I've mentally checked out — this reads like Crate & Barrel just crammed every merchandising module they own into one email because it's Labor Day. Compare that to CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send, which felt tighter and more curated even though it's the same parent company hitting my inbox in the same week.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably on the kitchen deals section — the Dyson and the Sonos speaker discount are real, specific, dated offers. Would I forward it? No. Would I mark as read and maybe circle back for the vacuum? That's the honest outcome.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" with a clear "Shop Now" button, no guessing games.
- Double Rewards banner at the very top gives me a loyalty hook immediately, which Wayfair's confetti-blast clearance emails never bother with.
- "top kitchen deals (they ship free!)" section has real prices, real % off, and a name-brand callout (Dyson, Sonos) — that's the strongest single beat in the whole email.
- Category grid (sofas, rugs, outdoor furniture, decor) actually maps to my renovation, unlike five straight Crate & Kids sends I don't need.
- "TheDesignDesk" free design appointment tease at the bottom is relevant to someone renovating two houses at once — I noticed it, even buried that low.

## What's weak

- It's exhausting — hero, then an 8-tile grid, then kitchen deals, then a double-rewards repeat, then "we love these for you," then a credit card pitch, then an app download, then TheDesignDesk. Pick a lane.
- The double-rewards module repeats almost verbatim mid-email — I already saw it at the top, no need to hit me twice before I've even scrolled halfway.
- "we love these for you" personalization module shows a dresser, a bed, dinnerware, a cocktail table — no evident logic connecting these to anything I've browsed. Feels like generic filler dressed up as "for you."
- Product tile copy under the deals ("up to 40% off bestselling sofas, sectionals & more") is vague on percentage specifics compared to the kitchen section, which names exact prices — inconsistent trust signal.
- Credit-card upsell ("the card you love just got better") sandwiched in after I'm already fatigued is a hard sell with no dollar amount attached — feels like padding, not a real reason to act.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Lead with hero + one strong deal grid + the Dyson/Sonos kitchen block, then stop. Everything after "we love these for you" is diminishing returns.
- 2. Kill the duplicate Double Rewards module — one mention at top, one CTA, done.
- 3. Make "we love these for you" actually personalized or drop the "for you" framing entirely — right now it reads as random inventory, which erodes trust in the rest of the email.
- 4. Move the Dyson/Sonos kitchen deals block higher, right under the hero — that's the best offer in the email and it's currently buried past two full grids of furniture.
- 5. Give the furniture tiles the same specificity treatment as kitchen ("up to 40% off" → name the actual top seller and its price like they did with the vacuum).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off + Double Rewards — Labor Day starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dyson up to 25% off is live for Labor Day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: free shipping on top kitchen deals through 9/8`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, rugs, outdoor — up to 60% off sitewide`
