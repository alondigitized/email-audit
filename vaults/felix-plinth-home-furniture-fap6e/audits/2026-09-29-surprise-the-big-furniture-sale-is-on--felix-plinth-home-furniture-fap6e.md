# 🎉 Surprise! The BIG FURNITURE SALE is on 🎉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Surprise!" It isn't a surprise. This is the fifth Wayfair blast since 9/26, and I've already sat through the "72-HOUR CLEAROUT" banner three times this week, including the 9/28 "peel-and-stick" send and the 9/29 gazebos one. The "BIG FURNITURE SALE" header is basically the same clearout with a new name and a Klarna badge. The confetti emoji subject reads like a coupon flyer, not something a guy juggling two renovations wants to open.
- Once I scrolled, it's a wall of purple tiles. Sofas and sectionals at 40% off, mattresses at 50%, bedroom furniture at 50%, then "picked just for you" with a mirror, a bird cage, pet bowls, a floor lamp and a recliner. The mirror and the lamp are the only things I'd actually save. The bird cage and pet bowls make me think "just for you" is a lie. Crate & Barrel and Pottery Barn send me one room story at a time. This is a warehouse aisle.
- Would I click? Maybe the sofas & sectionals tile, if I were shopping that day. I wouldn't forward it. It goes in the read pile, not the Pinterest-adjacent folder, because there's no point of view to save.

## What's working

- The "up to 40% / 50% off" tiles are big and easy to read on a phone, and the category labels sit right underneath them.
- "sofas & sectionals" leads the grid. That's my category with two houses in progress.
- The Klarna 0% APR up to 24 months offer, through 10/12, gives me real financing math on big-ticket furniture.
- "free & easy delivery over $35*" answers a shipping question I always have on large items, even with the asterisk.
- The star ratings on the product tiles give me some social proof, which CB2 skips.

## What's weak

- It's the same countdown-clearout energy Wayfair ran on 9/26, 9/27, 9/28 and earlier today. I've stopped believing any urgency from this sender.
- "Picked just for you" mixes a bird cage, pet bowls and a laundry organizer with a mirror and a lamp. That doesn't read as personalized. It reads like a random bestseller feed.
- The tiles are all the same purple, so nothing tells me what the hero is. CB2's "Color Is the Word" gave me swatches and room shots, and there's zero palette or style guidance here.
- There's no rewards or card-points angle, no swatch or sample program, no showroom content, and nothing about my cart or past browsing. I'm treated like a stranger.
- It's very long, with kids, nursery and pet tiles that have nothing to do with me. The footer, the app QR code and the fine print at the bottom are too small to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with sofas & sectionals and a single real deadline. Pottery Barn's nightstands email at least stayed on one room.
- 2. Make the "just for you" grid match my browsing. If I've been looking at lighting and mirrors, show only those and drop the bird cage.
- 3. Cut the send count. Four near-identical clearouts in a week trains me to ignore the next one.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample CTA and delivery-time clarity on the large pieces.
- 5. Cut the length by half and drop the kids and pet tiles unless I've shown interest in them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive media queries, and no hard failures were detected (0 issues, 3 warnings). The real findings are in the images: missing alt text, roughly 20 plain-HTTP tracking pixels, and a stale Memorial Day asset. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 of 63 links were probed and 51 were skipped by ranking. 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget ran out (e.g. `/daily-sales/bedroom-furniture-sale~e400622.html`, `wayfair-product-L6117-K~W119832484.html`, `wayfair-product-L1174-K~W001269876.html`, `Wrought-Iron-Elevated-Feeder-L1006-K~IDPT1017.html`). Their status is unknown.
- **All 8 probe warnings are HTTP 429.** These cover the app-download links, `/daily-sales`, the homepage, and the sofa, wall art and mattress sale links. That is bot rate-limiting, not a broken-link finding, and no link returned 4xx or 5xx.
- **The app link's campaign tag conflicts with the send type.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` carries `c=Triggered` while the same email's URLs carry `source=batch&batchid=2`. A batch promo is attributed as a triggered campaign in AppsFlyer.
- **The web fallback is plain HTTP.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` forces a redirect hop for users without the app.
- **Two different `_eml` values appear across links.** `_emr` is constant (`7b18cd63-…`), so it is likely the recipient ID. The `_eml` value differs per link position (`743b80e0…`, `6b6ce840…`, `df3e053c…` for the sale links). This looks like per-link click tracking and is not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images are missing alt text.**
  - The hero banner `0929_bigfurnituresale_skinnyhero-general.jpg` is also a link. The link text falls back to `[image: 0929_bigfurnituresale_skinnyhero-general]`, so its accessible name is the filename.
  - The same problem affects `WFUS_0929_BigFurnitureSale-Skinny-BAU_Financing.jpg`, `WFUS_0929_BigFurnitureSale-Skinny-Sponsor-WFUS.jpg` and `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - 12 star-rating images (`stars_9`, `stars_10`, `stars_11.png`) have no alt, so the rating values are inaccessible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
- **A stale asset is in the send.** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-…` is a May 11, 2026 asset in a Sept 29 email. Confirm it isn't rendering.
- **Tracking pixels lack alt.** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels have none. Adding `alt=""` is best practice, but the impact is low.
- **The stylesheet was cut off at the truncation point** (`text-align: righ…`), so the rest of the CSS and markup can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. This is unverified and not a confirmed sender defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain, so alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **The footer was not visible in the truncated source.** The physical postal address and the unsubscribe link couldn't be verified, though the QA note says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are used.** Attribution runs through proprietary parameters: `refid=MKTEML_141056`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16362`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `batchid=2`, `varid=6`, `wfcs=cs7` and `source=batch`. This is consistent across all links and won't flow into a standard GA UTM report without mapping.
- **Sale links are consistent.** They resolve to `/daily-sales/<event>~e<id>.html?eid=…&unifiedeventid=…`, and `eid` matches `unifiedeventid` (631514, 631511, 631505). They pass `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid`.
- **Landing page content wasn't verified.** The 429s blocked the probes, so I can't confirm the pages match the email.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the hero banner, financing banner, sponsor banner and the 48-hour-clearance button image. Add `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`-style text to the 12 rating images. Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stale Memorial Day banner asset from the template.
3. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
4. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`, and reconcile `c=Triggered` with the batch send type.
5. Re-run the link probe with lower concurrency or a longer budget to cover the 4 unprobed links and the 429 links.
6. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message (or directly in the inbox) rather than through the relay. Confirm the footer address and unsubscribe link in the full HTML.
