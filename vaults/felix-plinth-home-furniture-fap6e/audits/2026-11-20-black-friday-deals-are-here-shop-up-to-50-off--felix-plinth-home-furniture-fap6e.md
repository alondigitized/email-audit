# Black Friday deals are here! Shop up to 50% off →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday sale email from a retailer I actually shop, and it's the first Crate & Barrel send this week where the sale leads. The hero is a stand mixer on a holiday-styled counter, with big "Black Friday is here" copy, a free-shipping line and a Shop Now button. It reads as a sale email within a second. I like the sofas and sectionals tile near the top because that's what I'm buying for two houses.
- The rest is a long scroll: a 10-tile grid, a clearance banner, a Le Creuset block, a Thanksgiving menu, a TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, a product grid and an app-download pitch. It's a catalog stapled to a promo. I've seen Crate & Barrel's 11/19 "Save up to 50% on sofas, dining furniture & more" send, and this is the same sale with more clutter. I'd open it. I'd probably click the sofas tile, then skim the rest and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero is instantly legible: big headline, free shipping, one Shop Now button, and a lifestyle photo that looks like a kitchen I'd want.
- The sofas & sectionals tile is first in the grid. That's my category, and it's better placement than the Kids and toy sends from 11/19.
- The category tiles are labeled with a specific "up to X% off" each. That's easier to scan than Wayfair's "Best-selling pergolas" grid, where I had to guess what was on sale.
- The kitchen block shows real products with was/now pricing, such as the Le Creuset cookware and the espresso machine. That's concrete.
- The TheDesignDesk appointment offer ties into the renovation I'm doing. It's the only showroom/design-service content in here.

## What's weak

- The offer isn't consistent. The subject says "up to 50%," the hero reads as "up to 60%," and the tiles say 40–60%. A sale email shouldn't make me work out the real number.
- The email is too long. It carries at least six competing ideas (sale grid, clearance, kitchen deals, Thanksgiving menu, sweepstakes, app). Pottery Barn's "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" sends have one maroon banner and a clear focus, even though I'm tired of them.
- Several things feel off-category: the Thanksgiving menu, the "$5000 sweepstakes" and the app-download block. I'm buying furniture, not planning a menu.
- The "we love these for you" product grid is mostly cookware and a stand mixer. That's not personalized, and it repeats the hero.
- The footer is a wall of tiny legal text. I couldn't read it without zooming, and the offer exclusions are buried there.
- There's nothing about rewards, card math, swatches or samples. Furniture shipping and delivery timing aren't addressed either.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero, the 8–10 category tiles and one featured block. Move the Thanksgiving menu, sweepstakes and app pitch to a separate send. That change would lift it the most.
- 2. Make the headline and subject match: one honest "up to X%" figure, with the exclusions as one visible line near the hero.
- 3. Add a furniture-specific block with delivery windows, swatch/sample ordering and a link to book a design appointment. CB2 and Article both handle shipping clarity better.
- 4. Segment the product grid by what I've browsed. Show me sofas and dining tables, not a mixer I already saw in the hero.
- 5. Use a more specific CTA than "Shop Now," like "Shop sofas up to 50% off."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas, dining tables & rugs up to 50% off through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday furniture picks, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $79+, plus sectionals, rugs and dining sets marked down.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Monday. Order by 11/23 for Thanksgiving delivery on select items.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Marigold-style (`mi.`/`dv.` hosts) table layout, with `lang="en"`, an XHTML doctype and MSO conditionals. It passed 57% of automated checks (0 errors, 3 warnings). The only confirmed defects are missing alt text on about 38 images and a few template and markup leftovers. The header-based compliance findings can't be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 80 links were skipped because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical beacons, `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`, load from a different domain (rlcdn.com). Their `s=` value matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier on the first-party tracking images, so a subscriber-level hash is passed to that third party. Five fires of the same beacon is redundant.
- **First-party pixels:** the open pixel `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/380ab5d0-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` beacon are present. Both lack alt, which is harmless for tracking but is flagged below.
- The HTML is truncated, so I can't audit every `href`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** about 38 `<img>` elements have no alt attribute. This counts only the entries the QA output lists, and the list may be cut off.
  - 24 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…`. Examples: `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `fa2c110e-…jpg`, `95cc86a5-…jpg`.
  - 7 are on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`.
  - 5 are the rlcdn beacons.
  - 1 is `o.gif` and 1 is the `dv.` beacon.
  - The content images are the real accessibility problem, because with images blocked the email is unreadable. The beacons should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` stop users from resizing text in some clients.
- **Template residue:** the source still contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (the typo is in the source). It also has an empty rule in the MSO block (`td,th,div,p,a { }`) and a `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` pair with nothing in it.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values. The first, `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, already covers the later `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is technically invalid. Browsers and mail clients tolerate it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` appear in the visible source or QA output. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are resolved per-recipient values. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and body copy are not covered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are reported missing. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Re-check against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy before treating it as a real RFC 8058 gap. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is also a relay artifact. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either way.
- **Third-party hashed ID:** the rlcdn pixels (section 2) send a per-recipient hash to an ad-data vendor. That is worth checking against the sender's privacy disclosures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. The 80 tracking links were not followed and the HTML is truncated before the CTA hrefs, so there is no evidence of UTM presence or landing-page match.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/.../m/1/*.jpg` set and the `mi.../p/rp/*.png` set). Use `alt=""` on the tracking beacons (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, the `dv.` beacon).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and the `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. De-duplicate the five identical rlcdn beacons and check the hashed-identifier sharing with privacy/legal.
4. Clean up the template: remove the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT…]` comment, the empty `<!--[if !mso]>` block and the empty MSO rule, and escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`.
5. Re-run the compliance and auth checks against full raw headers. Without the relay, confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML with redirect resolution, so the 80 tracked links can be checked for UTM params and landing-page status.
