# LAST CHANCE for today-only deals ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and I'm tired of it. This is at least the fifth "today-only" send I've had from them in 24 hours. I've also had the 11/27 "HOURS LEFT" and "New deals for TODAY ONLY, leftovers unlikely" versions of this exact plaid-bordered maroon "Black Friday, up to 70% off" block since 11/16. "LAST CHANCE" on the 28th, for deals that were "today-only" on the 27th, doesn't read as urgency. It reads like the clock is fake.
- To be fair, the layout is clean. The hero is a big SHOP NOW, then a four-tile "Today Only Deals" row, then category blocks for Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Decor and Pillows. That's my renovation shopping list. But I'm still looking at the same dining table and the same chair I've already seen and saved. I wouldn't click, and I'd leave it in the Pinterest folder only if the table were new. It's not. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The "Today Only Deals" row shows Was/Now prices on each tile, like the $449 Whitaker chair. I can do the math without clicking.
- The category blocks (Furniture up to 60%, Bedding up to 70%, Sofas & Chairs up to 50%) match what I'm buying for two houses.
- "Ships Free" badges sit on a few blocks, which matters when I'm pricing out furniture and linens.
- The "Earn 10% back in rewards" card block is visible. I put everything on the highest-rewards card, so I read it.
- The in-store "Find Your Pottery Barn" block is a decent nod to showroom shopping.

## What's weak

- The "today-only" urgency is stale. The hero doesn't say what's actually ending, and there's no time or date.
- The hero is the same maroon plaid "Black Friday up to 70% off" creative from 11/16. Nothing says "last."
- The page is very long, with roughly 7 category blocks, a "Recommended Just For You" grid, a family-of-brands strip and a Christmas Shop banner. The deal gets buried, and the long fine-print footer is dense and small on a phone.
- The offers are all "up to." The deepest discount is on small items like towels and vases, not the furniture I care about.
- No swatch or sample cues, no color-palette guidance and no delivery timing on furniture. For a big-ticket renovation buy, that's the missing info.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero tell me what's actually ending, with a specific deadline like "Ends tonight 11:59 PT" and a countdown. Drop the recycled Black Friday plaid on a day-after send.
- 2. Lead with the one or two furniture deals that really are last-chance, not a generic category grid.
- 3. Add furniture shipping windows and a fabric swatch link next to the sofa and bed blocks.
- 4. Cut the "Recommended" section or make it genuinely personal. I got generic stockings and a robe.
- 5. Compare with the others. CB2's "UP TO 60% OFF (BASICALLY) EVERYTHING" at least has some voice. Crate & Barrel's "TODAY ONLY! 40% off wreaths, garlands & luxe Monochrome candles!" names real product. Article's "ENDS MONDAY" gives a real date. Wayfair's home theater seating subject is at least specific, even if the body didn't follow through. Pottery Barn's is the vaguest of the lot.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: dining tables & sofas marked down`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale prices end at 11:59 PM PT. Free shipping on select bedding.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an SFMC-sent, table-based, image-sliced layout. The QA run found no errors, only 3 warnings, a 57% pass rate, and a large block of alt-text and HTTP-image warnings. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified from this capture. The HTML source also has one confirmed CSS defect and several hygiene issues. The source was truncated, so the footer and later modules weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 68 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=…`, an SFMC click-wrapper. The probe skipped them all, so no destination, status code or redirect chain was verified.
- **Third-party pixels:** the QA list shows these beacons alongside the first-party `svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel:
  - `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501…`
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/…/cat/1`
- **Dead markup:** a commented-out credit-card banner is still in the source. It links to `potterybarn.com/pages/credit-card/?…cm_ite=PB_header_nonmember`. It's inert but ships to every recipient.
- **Cross-brand and stale assets:**
  - `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is hosted on another brand's domain.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is a 2022 asset.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS (confirmed):** the second `<style>` block begins with PHP statements, not CSS:
  `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: …"); … a[x-apple-data-detectors]{…}`.
  A parser reads everything before `{` as one invalid selector and discards the whole rule. That drops the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset, so Apple Mail may auto-link addresses and numbers in the default blue.
- **Mixed content:** two images use `http://`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `…/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. Clients that block or upgrade insecure content may show broken images.
- **Missing alt text:** about 60 images are flagged. Two points narrow that:
  - The visible source shows `alt=""` on `slices_01` and `slices_02` of the hero. These are linked images with empty alt, so their anchors have no accessible name.
  - The same pattern appears across the `Email-Footer`, `H26_xBrand_Bottom_Banner_Red` and product-image slices, so most of the flagged images are probably also linked.
- **Pixel alt text:** the tracking pixels are also flagged, but 1×1 beacons should carry `alt=""`.
- **Mobile CSS:** the 480px rules target `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. The tables and slice images in the visible source have fixed `width="650"` and no `class` attributes. Those rules may match nothing in the truncated source, which would leave the layout fixed-width on mobile. I can't confirm this without the rest of the HTML.
- **Markup:** an XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype is paired with `<center>`, `bgcolor` and `border` attributes. Two AMPscript comments sit before the doctype, which can trigger quirks mode in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens or AMPscript were found in the visible source. The recipient address is in plaintext in query strings:
- `potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- `svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`

The address is unhashed. It can end up in proxy logs, image-proxy caches and referrer data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found. QA notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` is a dedicated subdomain.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and postal address weren't in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Landing pages couldn't be verified. The click-wrapped links hide their destinations, and the only plain `potterybarn.com` URL in the visible source (the commented-out one) uses `cm_ite` and `external_id`, not `utm_*`. SFMC likely appends the campaign parameters at redirect time, so attribution can't be confirmed from the HTML alone.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines** from the `<style>` block so the Apple data-detector reset applies.
2. **Set `alt` on linked slices** to the offer or destination text, and give tracking pixels `alt=""`.
3. **Serve the two `http://` images over HTTPS** and retire the 2022 and West Elm-hosted assets.
4. **Strip the commented-out HTML block** from production sends.
5. **Check the mobile rules** by adding `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` where intended, or by moving to fluid widths.
6. **Run the QA pass on the raw MIME message**, not the relay copy, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. **Follow a sample of the redirects** to confirm landing-page URLs and `utm_*` parameters.
8. **Replace the plaintext email in pixel and URL query strings** with a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
