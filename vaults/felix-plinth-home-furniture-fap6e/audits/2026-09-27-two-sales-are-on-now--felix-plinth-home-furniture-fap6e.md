# Two sales are on now
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Two sales are on now" — and the first thing that hits me is that hero banner is broken. The "SEMI-ANNUAL UPHOLSTERY EVENT" text is literally overlapping itself — half the words are cut off mid-letter, "-ANNUAL" is floating on its own line over the image, and "UPHOLSTE" just trails off into nothing. I've seen CB2 send some genuinely good creative this week — the 09/25 "Upholstery Event is on" email I gave 7/10 nailed this exact same sale with a clean green velvet sectional hero — so seeing the follow-up send with a rendering bug on the headline offer is a step backward, not forward.
- Past the glitch, this is the same upholstery sale stacked with a second "up to 50% off furniture, decor and more" banner, a $5K design refresh sweepstakes, an app download pitch, and a rewards footer. It's a lot of real estate for something that reads like CB2 just mashed two prior sends together. I'm not clicking on a hero I can't read, and I'm definitely not forwarding this — if anything I'd screenshot the broken text and send it to a friend as "lol CB2's email broke," which is not the kind of forward CB2 wants.

## What's working

- The second banner ("UP TO 50% OFF FURNITURE, DECOR AND MORE") is clean and readable with a nice wood dining table hero — no complaints there.
- "SHOP NOW" buttons are simple, high-contrast, easy to tap on mobile.
- The $5,000 Refresh sweepstakes with "Book a free design appointment" is a genuinely different CTA than the usual "shop now" loop — I gave Article's entryway how-to a 6/10 for similar reasons, offering something beyond a straight sale pitch.
- Rewards footer (10% back at CB2 and Crate & Barrel) is useful since I'm in the reno mindset across two houses and actually track this stuff.

## What's weak

- The top hero is flat-out broken — overlapping text, clipped words, a percentage I can't even confirm (looks like "25% off" but it's obscured). That's the headline offer and I can't read it.
- This is the second email in three days pushing the same Upholstery Event (09/25 and now 09/27) — the cadence is getting repetitive, and CB2's sibling brand Crate & Barrel Kids has already burned me on this pattern with three near-identical clearance sends this cycle.
- "This week's steals" up top doesn't match "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event" below it — feels like two different email templates got smushed together.
- No swatch/sample program mention despite this being an upholstery-specific sale — Pottery Barn's Thanksgiving Shop send (7/10) at least felt cohesive around one seasonal idea instead of bolting three offers together.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the hero render bug first** — that's the single biggest lift here. A broken headline offer tanks everything below it regardless of how good the rest is.
- 2. Pick one primary offer per email instead of stacking Upholstery Event + sitewide 50% off + sweepstakes + app download + rewards — Wayfair's bait-and-switch banner stacking already cost them a 5/10 from me, don't repeat that.
- 3. Space out the Upholstery Event sends — two in three days is Crate & Barrel Kids-level repetitive.
- 4. Add a swatch/sample CTA near the upholstery hero — "Order Swatches" is buried on the right side of the broken image; make it a real, separate line item.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Upholstery Event: up to 25% off, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sofa refresh starts here — 25% off upholstery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Semi-annual upholstery sale plus sitewide furniture deals`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Book a free design consult, win a $5K refresh`
