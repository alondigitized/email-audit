## Executive summary

- Okay, THIS is the Crate & Barrel email I've been waiting for — not the Crate & Kids nonsense that's been clogging my inbox all week. "Up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" with a canopy bed front and center, actual bedroom furniture pricing right under it ($1,699 reg. $2,299), and a clean grid breaking out beds, dressers/nightstands, bedding, and decor/lighting separately. This is exactly the kind of email that should be hitting my inbox given I've got two houses mid-renovation.
- That said, it sprawls. By the time I hit "TheDesignDesk $5000 sweepstakes," a clearance beach-photo banner, a "we love these for you" product grid with dinner plates and a KitchenAid mixer, a credit card pitch, and an app download push, I've scrolled through what feels like four different emails stapled together. - Verdict: I'd click the top "Shop Bedroom Sale" button without hesitation — that hero and the sub-grid are genuinely on-target. I would not scroll further, and I wouldn't forward this to a friend; it's not tight enough to be worth someone else's time. Mark as read after the click.

## What's working

- The hero canopy bed with visible sale pricing ($1,699 vs $2,299) is the first thing my eye lands on — real product, real discount, no guessing.
- The four-tile breakdown (beds, dressers/nightstands, bedding, decor/lighting) matches exactly how I shop a renovation — category by category, not "browse everything."
- Two clear CTA buttons ("Shop Bedroom Sale" and "Shop All Bedroom") right where I need them, not buried at the bottom.
- The "Coveteur Upholstered Canopy Bed" product name and specific price beats the vague "Discover" copy CB2 and Article keep leaning on.

## What's weak

- The email doesn't stop being a bedroom email — it turns into a sweepstakes pitch, a clearance blowout, a kitchenware cross-sell (dinner plates, a KitchenAid mixer??), a credit card ad, and an app download push, all stacked below the fold. Pick a lane.
- "We love these for you" is fake personalization — a stand mixer and beverage tub have nothing to do with "bedroom refresh," so the algorithm clearly isn't looking at my actual browsing.
- The $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes echoes the same "enter to win" beat Crate & Kids ran twice today ($4,199 back-to-school giveaway) — I'm now seeing sweepstakes fatigue across the whole Crate & Barrel family in one afternoon.
- New Crate Card pitch mid-email is pure friction — I'm trying to look at a dresser, not open a store credit card.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the bedroom sale grid and CTA. Everything from "TheDesignDesk Sweeps" down (clearance, kitchenware cross-sell, credit card, app download) should be its own send or dropped — this is doing the job of three emails.
- 2. Drop the "we love these for you" grid entirely or make it actually bedroom-relevant (a runner, a lamp, a headboard accessory) — a KitchenAid mixer under a bedroom sale header just tells me the personalization is fake.
- 3. Lead with the discount range in the subject the way the hero does — "up to 60% off bedding & decor" is a bigger number than 25% and should probably be up front for scroll-stopping power.
- 4. Space out the sweepstakes messaging — Crate & Kids already ran this exact beat twice today under the same parent brand; running it a third time same-day reads as spam even though it's a different sub-brand to me as a reader.
