# Get your nursery must-haves FAST!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Crate & Kids email this week, and honestly this is the one that finally hits the mark — sort of. It's a straightforward category grid: nursery chairs, cribs, bedding, dressers, changing tables, strollers, car seats, all with "delivate as feed as 7 days" (yeah, I can see the typo right in the hero) promising fast shipping. That's actually useful for me since I'm racing to get the nursery-adjacent room in the second home done before we lose the contractor window. No sweepstakes headline shoved at the top this time, no "quality craftsmanship" quote wall — just product, which I appreciate after slogging through the $5,000 sweepstakes-and-credit-card pitch from earlier today's "Finn Collection" send.
- That said, it still tries to cram in a backpack bundle push, a DesignDesk $5,000 sweepstakes entry, a credit card upsell, and an app download pitch — all before the footer. It's the same everything-bagel format Crate & Barrel family emails always run, just with a cleaner top half this time.
- Would I click? Maybe on "Shop All Nursery" if I'm actually in nursery mode, but I'm not furnishing a nursery — I'm renovating two houses for us as adults, no kid signals here. Not forwarding this one; it's competent, not exciting.

## What's working

- The hero typo aside, "get their nursery ready now" with a Shop Now button is a clean, single-minded CTA — no ambiguity about what to click.
- The 4x2 category grid (chairs, cribs, bedding, dressers, changing tables, decor, blankets, car seats, strollers) is genuinely useful browsing — reminds me of how Article's editorial layouts organize by room, except this organizes by product type, which is faster for a targeted shop.
- "Ships free" tags on select categories are a real, visible signal — more concrete than Wayfair's vague "Find one that's all you" subject line I read the other day.
- Product photography is consistent and neutral (cream/wood tones) — no jarring color clashes like I've flagged in past Crate & Kids sends.

## What's weak

- Hero copy has a visible typo — "Delivate as feed as 7 Days" — that's sloppy for a brand at this price point; CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send never had render bugs like that.
- Zero demographic match for me — this is 100% nursery/baby-gear targeting, and I'm not a new parent shopping cribs and car seats; feels like the same broad-blast targeting Crate & Kids has hit me with all week.
- The backpack bundle and DesignDesk sweepstakes blocks are pure filler bolted onto a product email — Article's "Good changes" send stayed disciplined and single-themed; this one can't resist tacking on three more asks.
- No discount or price anywhere in the main grid — just categories, so there's no urgency or savings hook the way CB2's sale email at least gave me a number to react to.
- The Crate Card "Earning Rewards Dollars" pitch at the bottom is another card upsell I've now seen twice today from this family of brands.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero typo immediately — "Delivate as feed as 7 Days" reads broken and undercuts trust on a brand this premium.
- 2. Cut the backpack bundle and DesignDesk sweepstakes blocks from this specific send — let the nursery-shop email be a nursery-shop email, then run those as separate, focused sends.
- 3. Add at least one price point or % off to the hero — right now there's zero urgency or savings signal, unlike CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" which at least gave me a number.
- 4. Segment me out of nursery/baby-gear sends entirely if the account picked up my activity on non-baby categories — five baby-focused emails in one week from this family with no kid activity from me is a targeting miss, not a cadence problem.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop chairs, bedding, dressers & more — free shipping on select items`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your nursery checklist, one page, fast shipping included`
