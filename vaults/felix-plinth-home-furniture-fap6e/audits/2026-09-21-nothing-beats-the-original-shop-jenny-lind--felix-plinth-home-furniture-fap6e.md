# Nothing beats the original. Shop Jenny Lind →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- Nothing beats the original, huh? Except that I don't have kids, so "nursery" and "for their playroom" isn't beating anything — it's just not for me. This is Crate & Kids, the baby/toddler furniture arm of Crate & Barrel, and it landed the same week I've already been buried under Crate & Barrel's own Oceanside sofa campaign (both the 09/21 "Laid-back vibes" send and the 09/21 "260+ ways" follow-up, both of which I scored a 7). Now I'm getting cribs and bunk beds from the sister brand. My inbox has basically become a Crate & Barrel family subscription I never explicitly signed up for across three different sub-brands.
- The Jenny Lind collection itself is nice-looking — that warm brown wood tone, the vintage spindle styling, decent room styling shots — but nothing here maps to what I'm actually doing right now, which is renovating a second home and my primary. I need sofas, dining tables, lighting, maybe a home office setup. Not a "Jenny Lind Deep Brown Bunk Bed" or a toddler play table. This would get archived into my Pinterest-adjacent folder for approximately zero seconds before I move on, and I definitely wouldn't forward it — I don't know anyone with a baby on my list right now.
- Would I click? No. Would I open it again? Also no — I probably only opened it because "Crate & Kids" still has "Crate" in the name and my eye skimmed past that before registering "Kids."

## What's working

- The Jenny Lind furniture photography is genuinely well-lit and the wood tone is consistent across bedroom, nursery, and playroom sections — good visual cohesion.
- "20% off the best baby essentials" is a clear, specific offer up top, even if it's not my essentials.
- The "500+ new fall arrivals, starting at $29!" section at least introduces a price anchor, which none of the hero sections have.
- The room vignettes (striped nursery wall, playroom rug) are styled like something out of a design catalog, similar to how Pottery Barn's "Cozy layers" send handled its bedding grid — that part translates even if the category doesn't.

## What's weak

- Wrong audience entirely — I'm not a parent, and there's no swap-in content here for someone like me who's on a Crate & Barrel-adjacent list for grown-up furniture, not cribs.
- The brand-fatigue problem is real: this is now my third Crate & Barrel-family email this week (two Oceanside sends plus this one), and CB2's "STEALS OF THE WEEK" makes it four counting the corporate siblings. At some point that's not marketing, that's spam by proxy.
- Zero shipping/delivery clarity for furniture — no assembly info, no lead-time estimate, nothing about how a crib or bunk bed actually gets to my door, which is the first thing I check on big furniture buys.
- No loyalty/rewards messaging tied to my card — I buy furniture on whichever card gives the best rewards multiplier, and there's nothing here about points or member pricing.
- The mid-email "TheDesignDesk" app pitch and the co-branded credit card CTA feel like they were bolted on from the main Crate & Barrel template without editing for a Crate & Kids context.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids sends entirely — if I've never bought nursery furniture, don't put me in this list. That's the single biggest lift here; no subject line rewrite fixes a targeting miss.
- 2. If Crate & Kids must email non-parent subscribers, lead with the fall arrivals/$29 section instead of Jenny Lind — broader price-point content has a better shot at relevance than a crib collection.
- 3. Add real shipping/assembly info for the furniture pieces — even a one-line "free assembly included" or lead-time badge would help credibility for big-ticket items like the bunk bed.
- 4. Cut the redundant "Shop Now" CTAs — I count at least five near-identical buttons; consolidate around the top offer and the fall arrivals.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Jenny Lind is back: 20% off nursery & kids furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The crib everyone still asks for — 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handcrafted spindle furniture for nursery, bedroom & playroom`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited time: 20% off Jenny Lind, starting at $29`
