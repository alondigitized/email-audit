# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and the subject is a promise the email never keeps. It says free shipping over $35, all day, every day. I scrolled the whole render and never saw free shipping mentioned. What I got was a "Big Furniture Sale, up to 50% off" banner with a Klarna tag, then a "5% rewards on every item, always" block. The headline offer and the body are two different emails. On a furniture renovation, a $35 threshold is also a non-event. I assume the sofa and the vanity ship free anyway.
- The middle is a decent six-tile "your home, these picks" grid with star ratings. There's a kitchen island, an entry bench and a lantern sconce, and those are things I'd really look at. Then it falls apart into a catalog dump of dorm storage, greenhouses (twice), pet crates and faux florals. Under that is a huge empty white box with one tiny broken-image icon. That's the same failure as the 10/2 dinnerware send, just lower down this time. It's also the fourth Wayfair email I've been sent in about three days. I'd skim it, maybe save the island tile, and not click through today.
- I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted to show my partner a kitchen island, I'd send them the Crate & Barrel page.

## What's working

- The 5% rewards line is the one thing that speaks to how I shop. I pay with the highest-rewards card, so I care about stacking.
- The "your home, these picks" block has star ratings under every tile. It's the only part that looks like it was chosen for a home-reno person: island, bench, sconce, nightstand.
- The pink-bedroom and mustard-chair hero is a nice mood image. It's the closest thing to the Pinterest-folder aesthetic I save emails for.
- The "Big Furniture Sale / up to 50%" banner sits at the very top, so I get an actual discount number before I scroll.
- The footer is clean and trustworthy: Rewards, Financing, Professional, and the app QR.

## What's weak

- The subject's free-shipping offer appears nowhere in the body. I felt a small bait-and-switch.
- A giant blank white box with a broken-image icon takes up about a screen of scroll. It looks like a bug and it pushes the footer far down.
- The "let's complete your space" grid is a random mix: greenhouses twice, dorm storage, pet crates, mattress pads, faux florals. None of it fits a two-house renovation, and none of it carries a price.
- There's no real button anywhere. "Take a look →" is just a text link, and the tile captions all end in "»". Nothing says "Shop kitchen islands."
- The rewards line has a superscript footnote, and the fine print about membership and Reward Dollars is buried in the footer. I'd want the math before I trust "5% always."

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and the hero match. If free shipping over $35 is the offer, put it in the hero with a real button. If the furniture sale is the offer, put that in the subject.
- 2. Fix or remove the empty module. A broken image gap is the most damaging thing here.
- 3. Cut the grid to one theme. Pottery Barn's Open Box email at least stayed on one idea. CB2's floor sample email kept a tight, on-brand hero and no filler. Give me kitchen and entry pieces, with prices, dimensions and ship dates.
- 4. Put prices on the tiles and use real CTAs, like "Shop kitchen islands" instead of "Take a look."
- 5. Show the rewards math in the hero, for example "5% back on a $2,000 order = $100."
- 6. Back off the cadence. Four Wayfair sends in three days is too many. I got candles, dinnerware, Adirondack chairs and pergolas, and the repeats are what make me tune out.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Furniture up to 50% off + 5% back on every order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kitchen islands, entry benches & more: free shipping over $35`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5% Wayfair Rewards on every item, and pay over time with Klarna.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated picks for your kitchen, entry and living room, ready to ship.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with a 480px responsive media query and no confirmed broken links. The real defects are widespread missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, one malformed image URL, and an HTTP fallback in the app deep link. Header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 of 63 links were probed and 8 returned 429 (rate limited). These were:
  - `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` (twice)
  - `/daily-sales/filters/find-your-perfect-greenhouse-se38081.html`
  - `/daily-sales` (twice)
  - `/wayfair-rewards`
  - `/` (twice)

  A 429 is not evidence of failure.
- **Coverage gap.** 51 links were skipped by design. 4 product links (`wayfair-product-L56-K~W100661138.html` ×2 and `wayfair-product-L66-K~W005270572.html` ×2) were unprobed after the 70s budget. Their status is unknown.
- **Insecure app-link fallback.** The OneLink URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Desktop and non-app users are sent to an `http://` URL, which adds a redirect hop and an insecure first request.
- **Malformed image URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is invalid percent-encoding because `o` is not a hex digit. Strict parsers or proxies may reject or mangle the URL and break the image. The `0728_` prefix also suggests a stale asset.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels.** 20 impression pixels use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1`. Clients or proxies that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, so impression counts will be undercounted.
- **Test asset names in production.** `email-sku-headers_test-01.jpg` and `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner` are served live. This is a hygiene signal and not a functional failure.
- **Empty parameter.** The rewards link carries `loyalty_hva=` with no value.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on linked images.** These images are wrapped in links, so the links have no accessible name. The QA link text shows `[image: …]` for each:
  - `0929_BigFurnitureSale_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` (links to `/daily-sales`)
  - `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24` (links to `/wayfair-rewards`)
  - `email-sku-headers_test-01.jpg` (links to `/`)
- **Other images missing alt:**
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png`
  - `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`, which appears to carry disclaimer text, so images-off clients and screen readers lose legal copy.
  - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`
- **Rating images without alt.** `stars_10.png` ×5 and `stars_11.png` convey review ratings and have no alt text.
- **Tracking pixels.** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels are flagged for missing alt. If they are not `alt=""`, they will be read out by screen readers.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML is truncated, so I can't evaluate the body markup, such as `role="presentation"` on layout tables or the lang attribute. The head shows a 480px breakpoint and a Gmail `u ~ div` fix.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the supplied URLs or QA data. `_emr` (recipient ID) is consistent across links, and `_eml` is unique per link.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both were not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed and not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC.** Authentication-Results was absent, so status is unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address.** The probe policy includes an unsubscribe link, but no result for it appears in the output. The footer is beyond the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121358`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. This is consistent across links. Whether analytics maps these to a campaign is unknown from the data here.
- **Landing alignment is unverified.** The subject promises free shipping over $35, and CTAs land on `/daily-sales`, `/`, `/wayfair-rewards` and a greenhouse sale page. Because those pages returned 429, I can't confirm they show the shipping offer.
- **Redirect hop on the app CTA.** It goes through OneLink and then falls back to `http://` (see section 2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all linked banners, the rating images (for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`), and the disclaimer image. Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Fix the `%oo` in the `0728_Outd%oorClearance…` URL, either by correct encoding or by renaming the asset. Remove or replace the stale asset.
4. Change `af_web_dp` to an `https://` URL.
5. Remove the empty `loyalty_hva=` parameter. Rename or retire test-named assets in production sends.
6. Verify the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment outside the relay.
7. Re-probe the 4 unprobed product links, and re-run the 429 links with throttling or a longer time budget.
8. Confirm the body has a working unsubscribe link and postal address. Confirm the destination pages state the free-shipping threshold.
