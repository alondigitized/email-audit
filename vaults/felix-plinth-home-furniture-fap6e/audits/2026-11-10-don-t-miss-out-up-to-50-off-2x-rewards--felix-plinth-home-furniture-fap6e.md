# Don’t miss out: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kids sender again, and it's the fourth Crate & Barrel send in two days. Earlier today I got "Don't miss it: Up to 50% off the Fall Sales Event!" and yesterday I got "Double Rewards + Up to 30% off drinkware & more!" and "Dinnerware, serveware & more up to 30% off + 2x Rewards." Same plaid hero, same "Last Double Rewards this year!" banner, same percentages. I'm furnishing two houses, not a nursery. The grid is mostly kids' bedding, playroom furniture, baby gear and strollers.
- The offer is easy to read. Up to 50% off is in the hero, and the 20% back in Reward Dollars is clear. Some of the grid is usable for me: rugs, decor and storage, and a floor lamp. But the product mix is a mismatch. Pottery Barn's nightstand grid last week was built for my situation. This one is built for someone with a toddler.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap "up to 50% off rugs," and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I saw that floor lamp in Pottery Barn or CB2 emails the same week, I'd be annoyed.

## What's working

- The hero is clean: "up to 50% off" in big type on the plaid, with one "Shop Now" button.
- The "Last Double Rewards this year!" banner with the Nov 17 date gives the 2x Rewards a credible deadline. That matters to me because I buy on the highest-rewards card.
- The tile grid is easy to scan. "Up to 50% off rugs" and "decor & storage" are categories I'd actually shop.
- The Crate Card callout near the bottom ties the rewards to a card, which fits how I buy.
- The TheDesignDesk free-appointment block is a good fit for a two-house renovation, even though I'd have wanted it higher.

## What's weak

- The sender is "Kids SALE," and the grid is kids' furniture, baby bedding, strollers and a changing-pad-type roundup. Nothing here addresses a primary-plus-second-home buyer.
- The "we love these for you" block is four strollers and a kids' dresser. That "for you" is wrong for me.
- The Last Double Rewards banner appears twice, and the hero and mid-email CTAs overlap. The email is very long (about 8,900px), so the repetition feels like padding.
- The "$5000 sweepstakes" and app-download blocks pull attention away from the sale.
- The tiny fine print at the bottom has several asterisked exclusions. I can't read it without zooming, and that makes "up to 50%" feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the sender. I shouldn't get Kids-branded sends when my history is home furnishings and tableware. Send me the main Crate & Barrel version, not the kids one.
- 2. Cut the repeated banner and consolidate to one rewards block. This email needs to be half as long.
- 3. Put the rewards math right in the hero, with an example like "spend $500, get $100 back in Reward Dollars."
- 4. Move TheDesignDesk higher for renovators. Add a shipping-time line for furniture.
- 5. Replace the stroller block with a recently-browsed or rugs, lighting and storage block for my segment.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard Marketo-style/MI template with proper `lang="en"` and mobile media queries. The confirmed problems are about 32 images flagged for missing alt text, a viewport meta that blocks zoom, and template cruft in `<head>`. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so link validity and destinations are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests (LiveRamp). The `s=` value is a 64-hex string, which looks like a hashed recipient identifier. The same value appears as `mi_u=dbe8da71…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`. This is recipient-linked data going to a third party, which is a privacy and disclosure consideration rather than a defect.
- **Repeated pixels:** the five rlcdn calls are identical except for `n`. Six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` images are also identical.
- **Mixed image hosts:** one image loads from `s7d5.scene7.com` (`2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`), while the rest use `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Hosting is split across domains, and the scene7 file is a GIF.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (32 warnings).**
  - About 19 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, for example `26171910-…jpg`, `068fa9a5-…jpg` and `8c4ce5d9-…gif`. Those need descriptive alt text.
  - The rest are tracking or beacon images: the rlcdn GIFs, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/07117397-…` and probably the `f3c0d22992a01946.png` files. These should carry `alt=""`. The check can't tell whether the attribute is empty or absent.
  - With images blocked, any linked image lacking alt text will render as a blank or filename link.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` set elsewhere. `.f10` also sets text to 10px on mobile.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. The title also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Conflicting rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, since both match at 768px and above.
- **Template cruft:**
  - A leftover placeholder comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - An empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.
  - Duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The rest of the body wasn't available, so this is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN, unconfirmed:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool itself notes AgentMail may not preserve these headers, so this is not evidence the sender omitted them.
- **WARN, unconfirmed:** no `Authentication-Results` header was captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- The footer, with its unsubscribe link and postal address, was in the truncated portion and could not be checked.
- The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is consistent with the image and tracking subdomains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links go through `mi.crateandbarrel.com` / `dv.crateandbarrel.com` redirects, and none were resolved. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards" offer are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~19 content images, and `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove `text-size-adjust:none` or set it to `100%`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the placeholder comment and duplicate meta tags.
4. Fix the conflicting `img` max-width media queries.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve the 78 tracking redirects to check destinations and UTM parameters.
7. Confirm the rlcdn/LiveRamp hashed-identifier sharing is covered in the privacy policy.
