# Barstools to complete your kitchen, from $99
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- I'm pricing barstools for two kitchens, so this is the first Pottery Barn send in a week that matches something I'm buying. Two kitchens means two islands, and I've been staring at counter heights. The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner at the top is the line that got me. For a renovation, a delivery window matters more than a discount. After the 10/19 Harvest Decor email, which I'd call the sixth send in three days, this one finally earns its space.
- Past the hero, it's a long scroll. There are three sections (Quality-Crafted, Upholstered, Leather) plus Wood, and each has its own "Shop Now." The "Starting at $99" badge is small and sits at the bottom corner of the first photo. Below that come the Christmas Shop tile and a pile of "Recommended just for you" items, including a bath towel and a curtain rod. I'd click through to the stools, but I'd skip the rest.
- Verdict: I'd click, then save it to the Pinterest folder. I wouldn't forward it. There's no price on any individual stool, so I'd have to leave the email to find out what a Montclair actually costs.

## What's working

- The "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" banner answers the first question I have about furniture, which is when it arrives.
- The hero shows two upholstered stools at a real island, with fall styling. That's the kitchen I'm picturing.
- The sections are split by material (upholstered, leather, wood) and each stool is named: Layton, Montclair, Baldwin, Skylar, Summerville, Folsom. I can make a shortlist from names alone.
- "Shop All Bar & Counter Stools" is a clear, category-specific closer, not a generic "Discover."
- "More Kitchen Updates" (lighting, runners, shelves, baskets) is a useful cross-sell for a kitchen reno.
- The 10% back in rewards on a new Pottery Barn credit card is visible near the bottom. I run the highest-rewards card, so I did the math.

## What's weak

- "Starting at $99" is tiny and tucked in a corner. It's in the subject line but barely visible in the hero.
- None of the six stools has a price, a seat height, or a counter-vs-bar label. I can't tell what fits my island.
- Four "Shop Now" buttons stack up over similar photos, and there's no clear single next step.
- "Recommended Just for You" didn't feel personal. A towel and a curtain rod have nothing to do with barstools.
- The Christmas Shop tile and Design Crew promo are clutter for me. Crate & Barrel's free-shipping email on 10/19 kept one offer on top.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense block that reads as "read this later."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "from $99" and the delivery window together in the hero, and add a price under each named stool, along with counter or bar height. Wayfair's pergola email ("$745 against a struck-through $879") put the price and the badge in the first screen. That's what I want here.
- 2. Cut the four "Shop Now" buttons down to two. Use one hero CTA and one "Shop All Bar & Counter Stools."
- 3. Move "Recommended Just for You" up only if it's relevant. Show matching counters, pendants, or runners, not towels.
- 4. Add a "Counter vs. Bar height" quick guide, and a swatch-request link for the upholstery colors.
- 5. Drop the Christmas tile from kitchen-category sends. CB2's "Collected, Not Expected" was editorial with no offer, but at least it was consistent.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Barstools in stock, delivered in 1-4 weeks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Counter & bar stools from $99 (in stock)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Upholstered, leather & wood. Layton, Skylar, Folsom and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Counter or bar height. Ships in 1-4 weeks. Earn 10% back with the card.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Barstools to complete your kitchen, from $99"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based, image-sliced ESP email (Salesforce Marketing Cloud click tracking plus Movable Ink dynamic content). It has widespread missing alt text, three plain-HTTP asset requests (one carries the recipient's email address), and an empty `<title>`. The compliance headers could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No link probing was possible.** 59 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, such as `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Mixed content on three assets.** These load over `http://` and may be blocked or rewritten by clients:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- **Several tracking systems are layered:**
  - `click.e.potterybarn.com`
  - `clickcp.potterybarn.com` (Movable Ink, `mi_cid`/`mi_mid`)
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com`
  - `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`
  - Each adds a failure point and a request. The `svopn` pixel is also plain HTTP.
- **Cross-brand asset dependency.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from a West Elm host. If that path is retired, the spacer breaks.
- **Stale asset paths.** `/pb/2022/...` and `/pb/2025/US/H25_TOP_InStockFurniture/...` are reused in a 2026 send. They load today but are a maintenance risk.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,460 of 15,691 characters (73%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk and makes the text part hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on about 55 images** (QA data). Examples:
  - Hero and barstool slices: `H26_Furn_B3_Barstools/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_17.jpg`
  - Footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - Product tiles: `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, `farmhouse-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `emery-linen-curtain-l.jpg`
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - Only the header logo has `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Image-sliced layout.** With images off or blocked, the barstool content has no text fallback because the slices have no alt.
- **Tracking pixels lack alt.** Examples are `d.turn.com/.../cat/1`, `svopn...`, `clickcp...`, and `coreemail.html?email=...`. Decorative or 1×1 images should carry `alt=""`.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.**
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Leaked server-side code inside a `<style>` block:**
  ```
  header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...
  ```
  It is invalid CSS and sits at the end of the block, so impact is likely nil. It still points to a template-hygiene problem.
- **Google Fonts `<link>` (Montserrat).** Many clients strip it, so confirm the fallback font stack.
- **Only some layout tables have `role="presentation"`.** The sampled ones do, but the outer wrapper tables do not. The source is truncated, so this can't be confirmed for the whole email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) are visible in the captured source.
- **Recipient email is in image and pixel URLs in cleartext:**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
- The second one is the real problem. It sends the address unencrypted over HTTP, where it is exposed to network observers and proxy logs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **not confirmed as a sender defect**. Re-check against raw headers from a direct-delivery source.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.potterybarn.com`.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified.** The HTML source is truncated before the footer, so I'm not flagging them as missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be evaluated.** Destinations are hidden inside the opaque `qs=` token on `click.e.potterybarn.com`, and the links weren't resolved.
- **Product images come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`**, which is the same origin as the site. Product tiles appear to point to the same catalog.
- **Landing page alignment is unverified.** Whether the barstool CTAs land on a barstool category page needs a link-resolution step.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `svopn` pixel.** Serve it over HTTPS and stop putting the plaintext recipient email in `sv_sveme` and `coreemail.html?email=`. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
2. **Add alt text to the content slices and product tiles.** Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels.
3. **Upgrade the two `http://edm.potterybarn.com` images to HTTPS.** Refresh or remove the 2022 brand-banner asset.
4. **Set a `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`.** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. **Remove the stray PHP `header()` lines from the `<style>` block.**
6. **Shorten the plain-text part** by trimming or consolidating tracking URLs to get under 70% URL content.
7. **Verify the compliance items** against raw headers and the full HTML:
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
   - SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment
   - Footer unsubscribe link and physical address
8. **Resolve the redirects** with a follow-redirect probe to confirm destination URLs, UTM parameters, and landing-page match.
