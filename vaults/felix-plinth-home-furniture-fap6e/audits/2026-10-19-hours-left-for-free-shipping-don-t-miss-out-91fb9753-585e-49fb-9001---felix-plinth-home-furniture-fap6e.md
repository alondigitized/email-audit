# Hours left for free shipping! Don’t miss out
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the same free-shipping blast I've been getting for two days, and it's the main Crate & Barrel send, not the Kids one. That's a relief, because I'm renovating two houses and neither has a nursery. It's also the sixth or seventh C&B-family email I've deleted or skimmed this week, so the "hours left" urgency doesn't land. Last time it was "Hours left for free shipping! Don't miss out" on 10/19 too, and the earlier "ENDS TODAY!" version used the same urgency.
- The Dutch oven stack up top is pretty, and the Thanksgiving dining event further down is the first thing that matches a purchase I'm making. I'd skim down to the dining table, glance at the 30% off, and probably save it to my Pinterest folder instead of buying. I wouldn't forward it. If Alon Tsang's friend were buying a dining set, maybe, but the buried 30% banner doesn't make me want to.

## What's working

- The free shipping headline is big and plain. "$99+" is the one number I need.
- The dining room event is the right category for me, and the sale-vs-reg price on the table gives me real math.
- The Reward Dollars card block ("10% back across all our design forward brands") speaks to how I buy. I pick the highest-rewards card every time.
- The Design Desk "100% free" appointment is a good renovation-stage hook. It's the closest thing here to showroom or sample integration.
- The product grid shows prices ($7.95 to $19.95), so I can scan it without clicking.

## What's weak

- The "Standard shipping; exclusions apply" line is tiny, and the real fine print is a wall of grey text at the bottom. Furniture shipping is exactly where I need clarity, and this doesn't give it.
- The "we love these for you" glasses and bowl feel like generic filler, not picks based on me. Nothing here shows swatches, samples or color consistency, which matter for a renovation.
- The top shipping offer and the dining event compete. Two headlines and a holiday collection tile make it hard to tell which is the main offer.
- The Thanksgiving dining text overlays a busy photo, and the small sale price caption sits right under the table. It's readable, but only just on a phone.
- The app promo and "chance at $500" feel tacked on after a very long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the dining event and make free shipping a slim banner above it. The shipping offer is generic, and the dining event is the one that moves real money.
- 2. Add furniture-specific shipping info, such as delivery windows and whether the free shipping applies to big items. Put it in the hero, not the footer.
- 3. Cut the length. The glasses grid, app block and store locator belong in a separate send.
- 4. Tie the rewards card to the dining event with a line like "extra 10% back on the dining table".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping ends tonight + dining sets up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Thanksgiving table, shipped free ($99+)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining tables, chairs and sideboards, up to 30% off through tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by midnight for free shipping. Rewards members earn 10% back.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Crate & Barrel's "Hours left for free shipping" email is a Movable Ink-driven template. The automated QA found about 26 images without alt text, and the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay not exposing headers, so they can't be confirmed from this capture. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body links were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking not verified:** 59 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains and were not probed. Their destinations and status are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` requests (LiveRamp) fire in addition to the first-party open pixels. They are expected for the ESP/ad stack, but they are third-party calls with no alt text.
- **Identifier in image URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` parameter appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and the `rlcdn.com` `s=` value. It is a per-recipient hash and is not a defect.
- **No broken links confirmed:** no 4xx/5xx results were reported for links that were probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** about 19 content images have no alt text.
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `1c9c4d87…jpg`, `386f64d2…jpg`, `fc2adc28…gif`)
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4…png`
  - The Scene7 asset `2026%5F1011%5FCB%5FHolidayDecorating%5FSecondary…`
  - Seven dynamic product images, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/560d850e7e517ede.png` and six others

  Image-blocking clients and screen readers will get no content for these. If the free-shipping offer or CTA is image-only, it is lost. The 7 tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b5e0fd92…`) should use `alt=""`, and the QA tool treats them the same as content images.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resize blocked:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` in the first style block conflicts with the later `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`. The universal `none` rule defeats user text scaling in WebKit.
- **Invalid markup:** the source has `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` with an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It also has an unclosed `<meta charset="utf-8">` and duplicate `format-detection` metas. These are low severity and render fine in practice.
- **Template remnant:** the source still contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless.
- **Outlook:** VML/MSO conditionals are present (`xmlns:v`, `o:OfficeDocumentSettings`), so Outlook support looks handled.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. The `mi_u` hash is a resolved dynamic-content identifier.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported missing. The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Large retailers normally send both for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header is absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, and alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** these were not available. The HTML was truncated before the footer, and no finding can be made either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No click URLs appeared in the truncated source or in the QA output, because all 59 tracking links were skipped. UTM parameters and landing page alignment with the "free shipping" offer are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive alt text:** do this for the ~19 content images and `alt=""` for the 7 tracking pixels. Fix the dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` product images in the Movable Ink template.
2. **Fix the viewport meta:** remove `maximum-scale=1`, and remove or scope the `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule.
3. **Escape the title:** change it to `&amp;`, and remove the duplicate meta tags and the template comment.
4. **Check the relay capture:** inspect the raw message to confirm the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers before treating them as defects.
5. **Improve QA coverage:** capture the full HTML, including the footer, and resolve a sample of the 59 tracked links to check UTMs and landing pages. Classify tracking pixels so they don't count as alt-text failures.
