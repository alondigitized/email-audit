# Your 2 fave words: FREE SHIPPING!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, again — this is the fourth send I've clocked from this sub-brand in this stretch, and my brain immediately filed it under "nursery parade, part four" before I even scrolled. It's the same "it. all. ships. free." hero I audited yesterday, just re-shuffled into a longer grid of kids bedding, strollers, and a backpack bundle. There's no percentage off, no dollar figure, nothing that actually moves the needle for someone mid-reno who's trying to hit free-shipping thresholds on real furniture, not a $54 backpack.
- The "we love these for you" module with the $1,499 dresser and $1,400 stroller travel system is the only part that made me pause — that's actual money, and if I had a kid I'd care. But I don't have kids on this account, and there's zero signal here that Crate & Barrel knows that. It's the same "spray and pray" approach I called out on the C&B clearance email from yesterday — just aimed at a different room of a house I'm not furnishing.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding. This gets the same treatment as the last Crate & Kids send — skim, close, move to archive. If anything, this one's worse than "up to 60% off clearance" (6/10 yesterday) because at least that one had a real number attached.

## What's working

- The hero shot — sage green gingham crib bedding, soft natural light — is genuinely well-styled, on brand with the "cozy lodge" aesthetic Pottery Barn's been running too.
- Clean grid layout, no overlapping text, no broken images — technically flawless render.
- The Crate & Barrel credit card teaser ("Reward Dollars") is a real loyalty hook buried mid-email, which is more than most of these sends bother with.
- "TheDesignDesk" free design pro CTA at the bottom is a nice differentiator vs. Wayfair, which has never once offered me a human in an email.

## What's weak

- Zero dollar or percent offer anywhere — just "free shipping" repeated three separate times (hero, mid-body button, footer language), which is the same beat as yesterday's Crate & Kids send almost verbatim.
- Nothing here matches my life stage — I'm not shopping cribs, strollers, or "back to school backpack bundles." This is a straight demographic miss for a 36-year-old dual-reno guy.
- The offer feels like padding — eight product tiles just to restate "ships free," when CB2's "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" (7/10) at least anchored around an actual sale event.
- Cadence fatigue is real: three or four Crate & Kids emails this cycle with the same "it. all. ships. free." skeleton is worse than the C&B family's usual overlap problem.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress me from Crate & Kids entirely** — I've got no baby/kid signals on this account; keep me on core Crate & Barrel and CB2 only. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. **Lead with an actual number.** "Free shipping" isn't an offer, it's a policy. Pair it with a real threshold or percentage the way the clearance emails did.
- 3. **Cut the send frequency** — four near-identical Crate & Kids emails in one stretch trains me to stop opening, full stop.
- 4. **If you must cross-sell adults into Crate & Kids, do it through the gift-registry angle** ("shopping for a shower gift?") rather than assuming I'm the parent.
