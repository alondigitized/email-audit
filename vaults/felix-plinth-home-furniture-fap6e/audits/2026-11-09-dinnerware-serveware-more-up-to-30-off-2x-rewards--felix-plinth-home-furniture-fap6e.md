# Dinnerware, serveware & more up to 30% off + 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Crate & Barrel send today that's actually for me. The earlier ones were all the Kids sender: toy sales, the Newborn Essentials Shop, the Finn Collection. I'm furnishing two houses, not a nursery. This one is the main-brand SALE sender, and the hero is dinnerware, serveware and wine glasses with "up to 30% off." I'm hosting Thanksgiving in the renovated place, so it lands.
- It's also long, and it says the same thing three times. The 2x Rewards offer shows up in the hero strip and again in a big red "last Double Rewards this year!" block. A third mention sits in the Crate Card pitch near the bottom. The Gift Shop, the 03 styles module, the app download and the DesignDesk all pile on after that. The first screen is strong, and then it turns into a scroll. Compare CB2, which hit me with a single "Double Rewards" line and one sale hero. I'd click the dinnerware hero. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd save the Thanksgiving table module to my Pinterest-adjacent folder.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a dark table setting, "host & toast: up to 30% off drinkware, dinnerware & more*," and a single "Shop Now" button.
- The sale is split by category (dinnerware, wine glasses, serveware, drinking glasses, table linens, flatware), each with its own percentage. That's the sort of detail I shop by, and it beats Wayfair's blanket "up to 60% off."
- The rewards math is stated: 2x Rewards, plus "Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars" on the Crate Card through Nov. 17. That's the highest-rewards-card logic I use.
- "The countdown to Thanksgiving is on" at the top gives me a credible reason to buy now.
- The "5 styles for your Thanksgiving table" module is curated and editorial. It feels like the showroom content I save.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards message repeats three times. By the second red block I'd already got it.
- The 2x Rewards in the subject doesn't match the "Earn 20% back" copy in the body. The Reward Dollars wording leaves me guessing what 2x actually means.
- There are too many CTAs: Shop the Sale, Shop All Tabletop & Bar, Shop The Styles, Shop the Gift Guide, Shop Top Categories, Learn More (twice), Download Now and Book a Free Appointment. The primary action gets diluted. Pottery Barn does the same thing in its Christmas Shop sends.
- The "we love these for you" product grid is small and has no ratings or review counts. The prices are tiny and hard to read on my phone.
- The Gift Shop block is a big burgundy banner with the headline in script and nothing to buy. It pushes the product grid further down.
- The fine print runs long and dense, and the "up to 30%" claim carries an asterisk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it by about half. Keep the hero, the category grid, the Thanksgiving styles module and one rewards block, and drop the second and third Double Rewards mentions. Move the Gift Shop and app promo to a different send.
- 2. Put one rewards line in the hero strip and spell out the math: "2x Rewards or 20% back on your Crate Card, through Nov. 17."
- 3. Add star ratings and "ships by" dates to the product grid. Furniture and tabletop buyers need delivery clarity before Thanksgiving.
- 4. Make the final "Learn More" a single card-signup CTA, and drop the duplicate.
- 5. Say what the colour story is. Name the palette ("matte black and warm oak") so I can match it to what I'm planning.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) and Salesforce-style template (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`) with 71 tracked links. The automated QA found no hard failures, but the 57% pass rate reflects mostly unverifiable checks. About 36 images lack alt text, and the template markup has several hygiene and accessibility problems.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 71 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel:** 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels load from LiveRamp. They are repeated with an incrementing `n` and are all missing alt.
- **Subscriber-level identifier in URLs:** The same 64-hex value (`dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`) appears as `mi_u=` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images and as `s=` on the `rlcdn.com` pixels. It looks like a hashed subscriber ID. It is shared with a third-party ad-data domain, which is worth confirming against the privacy policy.
- **Link bloat:** The source contains 71 tracked links and over 35 images.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA flagged about 36 images. The list is truncated, so the real count may be higher.
  - 22 are content images, such as `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`. This includes animated GIFs (`d21416db…gif`, `f2caf774…gif`, `70061691…gif`, `24335f96…gif`). If the offer text is baked into these images, images-off clients and screen readers lose it.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` dynamic images. These are likely personalized product content and need alt text.
  - 7 are tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`). These should have `alt=""` explicitly. I can't tell from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Viewport zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch zoom, an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;…}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` rules and disable text scaling in WebKit.
- **Small mobile text:** The `.f10` class sets `font-size:10px` on mobile.
- **Invalid markup:** The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is paired with an HTML5 `<meta charset="utf-8">`. `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand. `format-detection` is declared 4 times and `Content-Type` twice.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `img {max-width:600px}` at `min-width:768px` and `img {max-width:640px}` at `min-width:640px` overlap, so the 640px rule applies from 640–767px and the 600px rule above that.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It is harmless but shows an unfilled placeholder.
- **Good practice:** `lang="en"`, `color-scheme` meta, VML/MSO namespaces and Outlook conditionals are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source, but the body wasn't available to scan.
- The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters indicate Movable Ink dynamic content. It resolved to image URLs here, but whether the rendered content is correct can't be verified from source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header:** Not found. The QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is likely a capture limitation rather than a sender defect. It is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. One-click support is unverified. It is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found because of the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Neither is visible in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.
- **From address:** `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is on a branded subdomain, which is fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed. The 71 links were not probed, and the visible source shows no destination URLs.
- The subject promises "Dinnerware, serveware & more up to 30% off + 2x Rewards". Whether the landing page carries the same offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add meaningful `alt` text to the 22 content images and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp` images. Use explicit `alt=""` on the 7 tracking pixels.
2. **Viewport and text sizing:** Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations that override the `100%` rules.
3. **Markup cleanup:** Escape the `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate meta tags and the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment, and fix the overlapping `img{max-width}` media queries.
4. **Headers:** Check the raw message at the sending side or via a seed inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. The relay may be stripping them.
5. **QA coverage:** Re-run link validation with a redirect-following resolver for the tracking domains. That would verify the 71 skipped links and let the UTM and landing-page checks run.
6. **Tracking review:** Confirm that sharing the hashed identifier (`mi_u` / `s=`) with `rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy policy and consent.
