# NOW OPEN: The Newborn Essentials Shop!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel Kids again, and it's the fourth Crate-family send I've gotten in two days. The sofa carpet-bombing on 10/3 and the two kitchen emails earlier today at least matched what I'm doing. I'm renovating two houses, and a newborn shop isn't part of that. My gut reaction was to snooze it, which is what I do with anything from this sender family now.
- The email itself is well built. It's a long, calm scroll in beige, cream and warm wood, with a slim "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, Up to 50% off" banner on top. It then has a "Now Open" hero, then cribs, glider chairs, bedding, a keepsake section, gifts, finishing touches, a toy shipping offer, and a product grid. It looks like a catalog page, and I do like a catalog page. But I'm not shopping for a nursery, so I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. If a friend with a baby on the way were in my contacts, I might. Otherwise it gets marked as read.

## What's working

- The 50% off event banner sits above the hero, so I got the offer before I scrolled. That's honest and easy to find.
- The palette is consistent from top to bottom. Cream, oatmeal and wood tones, no clashing colors. That's how I judge a catalog, and it reads as a real room, not a coupon.
- Each section has a short lowercase headline and its own button ("quality-crafted cribs", "organic cotton bedding", "nursery finishing touches"). The sections are easy to scan.
- The "we love these for you" product grid at the bottom has strollers, a glider and a crib, with visible prices. That's the only spot where I can see what something costs.
- The "free shipping on toys $99+" callout is a clear threshold. I'd like to see that same shipping clarity on the furniture.

## What's weak

- The "Now Open" newborn shop doesn't match my situation. Nothing here speaks to a guy furnishing two houses. Pottery Barn's holiday-movie collab on the same day at least gave me something to save to my folder.
- It's too long. The same 50% event is announced at the top and again halfway down, and most sections are lifestyle photos with a bare "Shop Now". It's about a dozen CTAs, so there's no single hero action.
- The buttons say "Shop Now" or "Shop Sale", which is a generic verb with no noun. Compare Wayfair, whose "COFFEE TABLES we HAD to share" subject at least named the product, though its body didn't deliver.
- I can't see a deadline for the event. "Up to 50%" with no end date doesn't make me act now.
- The back half is an app download, a credit card pitch and a "chance at $500" tease. That's a lot of extra asks after a long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send. Show it to people with a registry or baby browsing history, and keep it away from the 10/3 sofa and 10/4 kitchen clickers. Send the C&B family something different on cadence, because four emails in two days trains me to ignore all of them.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four sections and lead with the best-selling crib and glider with prices. Right now the photos are doing more work than the product.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, like "Shop cribs" or "Shop gliders", and add an event end date next to the 50% banner.
- 4. Put swatch, sample and delivery-window info for the big furniture pieces near the crib and glider. That's what a buyer like me needs before spending money.
- 5. Move the app and card pitch into a short footer strip.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "NOW OPEN: The Newborn Essentials Shop!" (Crate & Barrel Kids)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with 81 click-redirect links, about 45 images missing `alt` attributes, and several third-party tracking pixels. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified, because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click destinations unverified.** The QA run skipped all 81 tracking links (`Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe`). It did not check redirect chains, final destinations, or 404s.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5` are the same pixel with only `n` incrementing. They carry a per-recipient identifier in `s=` and are third-party data-matching beacons. This is a privacy and deliverability surface, and a failing third-party host can delay rendering.
- **Six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` is requested six times. It is likely a dynamic-content or personalization image, repeated per module.
- **Open-tracking pixels and per-recipient IDs.** These are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/72af0c93-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. The `mi_u` value is a per-recipient identifier embedded in image URLs. It will leak if the email is forwarded or archived.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 45 images** (QA WARN). This includes:
  - The hero image `2025%5F0105%5FCBK%5FNewBornCapsuleLaunch%5FHERO%5F…` (`s7d5.scene7.com`).
  - About 25 content images from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`.
  - Spacer images `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - Tracking pixels.
  - With images blocked, the hero and product modules render as blank space for screen readers and in no-image clients. Decorative spacers and pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly, not omit the attribute.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. The `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules also disable text scaling in WebKit.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width media queries.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and wider, so the later 640px rule wins and the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the XHTML doctype (should be `&amp;`). The title also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Template residue.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a leftover placeholder with a typo.
- **Redundant meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared four times, and both `http-equiv` and `meta charset` are present.
- **Fallback stack.** `.mso-font-fix-georgia` uses `Georgia, sans-serif`, so a serif font falls back to sans-serif.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters resolved to concrete values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may have stripped it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN). One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not auditable.** The visible HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or physical postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. Because all 81 links go through redirect domains and weren't probed, I can't confirm UTM parameters, landing page alignment, or whether the "Newborn Essentials Shop" CTA reaches the intended page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text** to all content images, starting with the hero and product modules. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag and drop the duplicate `format-detection` tags.
3. **Fix the `img` max-width queries** so the 600px and 640px rules don't conflict, and escape the `&` in `<title>`.
4. **Remove the template placeholder comment** and correct the Georgia fallback stack.
5. **Deduplicate tracking pixels.** Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` beacons and six `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests into one each.
6. **Verify headers against raw `.eml`.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` outside the relay before treating them as failures.
7. **Resolve redirect chains** for the 81 tracked links in a follow-up check, to validate final URLs and UTM parameters.
