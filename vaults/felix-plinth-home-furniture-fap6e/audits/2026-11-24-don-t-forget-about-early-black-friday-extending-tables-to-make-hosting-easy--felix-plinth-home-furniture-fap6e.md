# Don't forget about EARLY BLACK FRIDAY + Extending tables to make hosting easy
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this one is actually useful to me. The maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner is still on top, the same one I've had since 11/16. Under it, though, is a real editorial idea: "Make Room for More," built around extendable dining tables. I'm hosting at the second house this year, so that landed. It's more focused than the earlier blasts that day. "FREE design help in stores" was at least a different angle, but this one gives me actual products with specific finishes.
- It still falls apart in the back half. After three table heroes, the "Recommended Just For You" grid is stockings, a faux fur throw, a robe and a quilt. That's generic, and it doesn't read as me. Then the sweepstakes, the app promo, Affirm and the rewards card stack up in a long tail. The email is a dining table pitch for the first third and a catalog dump after that.
- Would I click? Yes, probably the Toscana table, and I'd save it to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. Would I forward it? Maybe to my partner, for the Monroe table, but not for the Black Friday banner.

## What's working

- "Make Room for More" with the extendable-table story ties to a real hosting need, and I'm renovating two houses.
- Three named tables (Toscana, Vintners, Monroe), each with a one-line description and specific finishes. "3 sizes and 6 finishes" is the detail I actually care about.
- The "In-Stock" tags on the table photos matter. Furniture lead times are my biggest pain point.
- "Shop the Look" on the room shots sells the full tablescape, not just one piece.
- "Set a Guest-Ready Table" with dinnerware, linens, glassware and candles is a smart cross-sell for hosting.
- The 10% back in Rewards card callout speaks to how I shop, since I buy through the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- The "up to 70% off" banner is the same creative I've seen all week. It says nothing about which tables are discounted, so I can't tell if my table is actually on sale.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid (stockings, throw, robe, quilt) feels generic. It has nothing to do with dining or renovating.
- The tail is crowded: Design Crew sweepstakes, app download, Affirm, rewards and the follow-us block. The fine print is a dense wall of text, and the "Promotional Exclusions" blurb sits right above the recommendations. That undercuts the honesty of the offer.
- There's no price on any table, so I have no way to judge the deal. Tables are a considered purchase, and I need to see the sale price before I click.
- The "Design Crew Sweeps" and "Book a free appointment" blocks compete with the main offer. Yesterday's "FREE design help in stores" send already covered the design-help angle, so this repeats it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices in the table modules, for example "Toscana from $1,299, was $1,799." That single change would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Cut the back half. Keep the tables, the guest-ready table cross-sell and one rewards line. Drop the sweepstakes and app promo, or move them to a separate send.
- 3. Replace the generic "Recommended Just For You" grid with chairs and extension leaves that match the table I was just shown. Wayfair at least tries to tie recommendations to browsing. This one isn't tied to anything.
- 4. Add a swatch or sample CTA ("Order a free wood swatch"). For a furniture buyer, color and finish matching is the real barrier.
- 5. State shipping clearly for the in-stock pieces, for example "Ships in 2 weeks."
- 6. Cap the cadence. I've had roughly ten Pottery Barn sends in a few days. Crate & Barrel is doing the same thing, and my "C&B family snooze" is already on. I'm about to apply it to Pottery Barn too.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Host more: 3 extendable tables, ready to ship`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Toscana, Vintners and Monroe: leaves, finishes and sale prices inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `In stock now, up to 70% off. See which tables ship in time for the holidays.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from table-based, image-sliced HTML. It has widespread missing `alt` text, three cleartext `http://` image sources, and the recipient's email address in plain text in image URLs. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the truncated source doesn't reach the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** The QA run skipped 62 tracking links as click-redirect domains, so none were checked for destination status or redirect chains. The sample link `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` is an opaque wrapped redirect.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from a sister-brand host. It is harmless, but it adds a dependency on another domain.
- **Tracking pixels (`d.turn.com`, `svopn.potterybarn.com`, `clickcp.potterybarn.com`):**
  - Three third-party or secondary tracking pixels load, and `d.turn.com` is an ad-tech domain.
  - They have no `alt`. They should use `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 55 images:**
  - These include the hero banner `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg`.
  - All 16 `H26_Furn_B4_ExtendingDiningTables/slices_01–16` images are affected.
  - All 18 `Email-Footer/slices_01–18` images are affected.
  - So are the product images, e.g. `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg` and `classic-velvet-stockings-l.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the offer and the product content would be missing or unreadable. Only the logo (`alt="POTTERY BARN"`) is covered.
- **Cleartext image sources (mixed content):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&...`
  - Clients that force HTTPS or proxy images may block these, which would leave gaps in the footer and brand banner.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>`.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta** blocks pinch-zoom.
- **No `role="presentation"` on the outer layout tables.** Only the inner `contenttable` tables have it.
- **Stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block:** `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. This is invalid CSS from a template leak. It's harmless at the end of the block, but it's sloppy and could break parsing of any rules placed after it.
- **Web font dependency:** The `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat `<link>` is stripped by many clients, and no fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,236 of 17,251 characters (about 71%) are URLs. This is a deliverability and readability warning.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` are visible in the source or subject. The recipient address is resolved in the image URLs listed in section 5.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, QA flagged:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect.
  - `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
  - Check the raw `.eml` from the original delivery.
- **Footer not visible:** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.
- **PII in cleartext URLs (confirmed):**
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`
  - The raw, unhashed address is in image-source query strings. The second one is sent over unencrypted HTTP, so the address is exposed on the wire and in proxy logs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs and landing pages not verifiable:**
  - The visible links use opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects.
  - No `utm_*` parameters are visible, and the redirects weren't resolved.
  - Whether the destinations match the Early Black Friday and extending-tables messaging is unknown.
- **Product image hosts:** Product images load from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`. This is consistent with the brand's own domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, product and slice images. Use `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS. Start with `svopn.potterybarn.com`.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in `coreemail.html?email=` and `sv_sveme=`. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
4. Add `<html lang="en">`, a populated `<title>`, and `role="presentation"` on the outer tables. Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
6. Pull the raw message headers to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML. That would check the footer unsubscribe and address. Resolve the 62 tracking links to confirm their destinations and UTMs.
