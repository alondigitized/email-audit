# ONLY HOURS LEFT TO SAVE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again, and this is roughly the fourteenth send in about five days. The all-caps "ONLY HOURS LEFT" subject made me roll my eyes before I opened it. Once I did, the email has a lot going on. A countdown timer sits at the top ("Sale ends in 01:15:19:28"), then a Semi-Annual Upholstery Event at up to 25% off. Below that are a Long Weekend Sale at up to 60% off, free shipping on orders of $99.99+, a $1,000 app giveaway and a lighting block.
- The upholstery event is the part that matters to me. I'm buying sofas and accent chairs for two houses, and the sofa, accent chair and bench tiles are exactly my category. But "up to 25%" is a weak hook when the same email says "up to 60%" lower down. The countdown reads days:hours:minutes:seconds, so it shows more than a day left. That doesn't match "only hours left," and I noticed. Compared with 10/12's "Your perfect green. Or pink. And taupe.", which spoke to my swatch problem, this is a generic stack of everything.
- Would I click? Maybe once, on Sofas, if I were actively shopping. Forward it? No. Mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The upholstery hero is clean. Big "25% OFF" type over a curly boucle sofa is easy to read at a glance.
- The category tiles for Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs and Benches & Ottomans are what I actually shop. They give me a direct path in.
- The black "Shop the Sale" buttons are clear and consistent, so I always know where to tap.
- Free shipping on $99.99+ is a real trust cue for furniture.
- The rewards and app block ("Earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars") speaks to my highest-rewards-card habit.

## What's weak

- The "only hours left" subject conflicts with a countdown showing a day-plus. That kills the urgency.
- "Up to 25%" and "up to 60%" compete. I can't tell which sale applies to the sofa I'd buy.
- Too many offers are stacked: upholstery, long weekend, shipping, a $1,000 app giveaway and lighting. No single hero wins.
- The "More for you" credenzas (Alpena, Kinzie, Cairo) aren't what I browsed. They also feel like a different project from the upholstery push.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense block of tiny type. I'd have to squint to find out whether the sofas are actually in the 25%.
- This is the same Long Weekend Sale I saw on 10/12 ("2 SALES END SOON") with the same 60% off. It's recycled urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make it upholstery and say what the 25% applies to, with one named sofa and its price.
- 2. Make the countdown and the subject agree. If it's days, don't say hours.
- 3. Cut the $1,000 app giveaway and the lighting block from sale-end sends.
- 4. Show swatch ordering and delivery windows for upholstery. Furniture buyers like me need that.
- 5. Cap the cadence. Crate & Barrel Kids and Pottery Barn both hit me on the same days. Wayfair's thirteen emails this week is the cautionary tale.

# Technical Audit: CB2 "ONLY HOURS LEFT TO SAVE"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads about 11 insecure `http://` third-party ad-sync pixels and has many images with no alt text. The QA pass rate was 43%. Most compliance and continuity checks couldn't be completed because of the relay and the truncated HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 72 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so no destination, redirect chain or status was checked. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`, are embedded as images.
  - They are plain HTTP, so clients that block mixed content will drop them, and the tracking will be incomplete.
  - Eleven extra third-party requests per open add latency and privacy exposure.
- **First-party open pixels** are on HTTPS and look intentional: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/53b7f803-…?mi_cid=…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (9):** all on `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `a56a45bd-…jpg`, `3fdf15ba-…jpg`, `55c86a33-…jpg`, `f6a602ed-…jpg`, `71bbd2a7-…jpg`
  - `1aa79a45-…jpg`, `cbac302a-…jpg`, `acb86340-…jpg`, `8e957a75-…gif`
  - With images off or a screen reader, these blocks are blank or announced by filename.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** the dotomi, `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels have no alt. This is low severity, but `alt=""` is the correct value for decorative images.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` also blocks text resizing in iOS clients.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in `<head>`. It has no functional impact.
- **Not assessable:** the source is truncated, so the body markup, the MSO/VML fallbacks and any HTML size or Gmail-clipping risk are unchecked.

- **[FAIL]** The raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears in the delivered HTML. The `%%…%%` syntax and the `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/` path suggest Salesforce Marketing Cloud, where this is an unresolved AMPscript or personalization string variable.
