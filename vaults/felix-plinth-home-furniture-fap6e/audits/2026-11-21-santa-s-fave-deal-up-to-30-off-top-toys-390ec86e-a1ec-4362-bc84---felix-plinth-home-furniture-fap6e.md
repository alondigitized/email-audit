# Santa's fave deal: Up to 30% off top toys!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Crate & Barrel Kids email in about two days, and I'm still not the audience. I'm renovating two houses. I don't have a toddler. The hero is a kid on a pouf with "Black Friday is on! Up to 30% off toys," followed by a ball pit, a wooden train set and an easel. I scrolled it like a catalog, which is the only reason I got as far as the 50%-off furniture grid near the bottom.
- That furniture grid is the only part I care about. It's buried under five toy hero tiles. The 11/20 "Shop Black Friday: up to 50% off furniture!" send from the main Crate & Barrel list put that offer first. Here it's an afterthought. Pottery Barn Kids and Wayfair's kids' lines would probably get the same shrug from me. But at least Wayfair doesn't repeat one creative four times in a weekend.
- Verdict: I wouldn't click and wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and probably filter "Kids SALE" to a folder. If I did click, it would be the "Shop Furniture" button, because that's the one thing I'd actually buy.

## What's working

- The "free shipping is on" strip with the $49 threshold is clear. Shipping cost is my biggest friction on furniture.
- Each product tile has a was/now price and a short customer quote, which feels like real social proof.
- The 2x4 grid of "up to 50% off" categories is easy to scan on a phone.
- The red Black Friday tags and the Crate & Barrel Kids logo make the brand obvious right away.
- It's seasonal and credible. It's the weekend before Black Friday and the toys look gift-ready.

## What's weak

- Toys are the hero for a 36-year-old renovating two houses. The furniture offer, my category, sits well below the fold.
- Each tile repeats a "Shop Now" button, so the CTAs don't differ. Tapping through gets me nothing specific.
- The sweepstakes ("$2,750+ in prizes") and "personalization" banners are filler. Both seem to be sent to everyone.
- The subject says 30% but the body says 50% for furniture, so the offer feels muddled. Pottery Barn's "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS delivered in 1-4 weeks" at least gave me one clear promise.
- The send cadence is relentless. Four earlier Crate Kids sends in two days, plus CB2, and that's before Wayfair.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the single strongest offer for a mixed audience. For people who've browsed furniture, put the 50%-off furniture and bedding hero first and toys second.
- 2. Give each tile a specific CTA: "Shop the Ball Pit" instead of "Shop Now."
- 3. Cut the five-tile hero stack to three, and move the sweepstakes and personalization banners to the footer.
- 4. Suppress the second and third Kids sends in a 48-hour window for people with no kids' purchase history.
- 5. Add delivery-window info on furniture, since I'm planning two renovations around lead times.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a large, table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template (about 100+ tracked links) with broad Outlook/MSO handling. The confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, and the HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 102 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of their destinations, status codes or redirect chains were validated. The QA data doesn't show whether any are broken.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` appears on `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`, on `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and on the `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` pixels. It also appears in `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c7ac8c1b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are identifying open and ad-sync pixels.
- **Third-party pixel:** `sr.rlcdn.com` (LiveRamp) is fired 5 times (`n=1` to `n=5`) with the same hashed identifier. This is redundant and adds third-party request overhead.
- **Repeated pixel:** The `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel is included 6 times with identical URLs, which is likely a template loop artifact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text (about 55 images flagged):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `d3119bec-…jpg`, `15ada461-…jpg` and `c1cafb52-…gif`) and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - Scene7 assets, including `2026%5F1105%5FCBK%5FPersonalizedGiftShop%5FSeconda…`.
  - Two animated GIFs (`c1cafb52-…gif`, `7cf1a4d1-…gif`), which also have no text fallback.
  - If these images are linked or carry content, the email is unreadable to screen readers and with images blocked. Tracking pixels without alt are lower severity, but they should use `alt=""`.
- **Spacer images:** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F50px%5FWhite` and `…40px…` have no `alt`. They are decorative and should have `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). The CSS also sets `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`, which block text resizing.
- **Template hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML, and it doesn't match the subject line.
  - Leftover placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - Duplicate `format-detection` metas (`telephone`, `address` and `date` are declared twice).
  - Duplicated `.ReadMsgBody` and `body` rules across the two `<style>` blocks.
  - A global `*{max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` rule.
- **Image sizing:** Media queries set `img{max-width:600px}` at ≥768px and `640px` at ≥640px. The ≥768px rule comes first, so the ≥640px rule wins on desktop. The cascade is confusing and the effective width is probably 640px rather than the intended 600px.
- **Doctype:** XHTML 1.0 Transitional with `<meta charset>` declared twice. This is harmless but redundant.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`). The HTML was truncated, so tokens further down the body can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay. Check DNS and a raw-header test directly (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`).
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated source doesn't reach the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address couldn't be checked.
- **From domain:** `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be verified. All 102 links go through click-redirect domains and were skipped, so final landing URLs and UTM parameters are not visible. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on `dv.crateandbarrel.com` suggest Movable Ink dynamic content, whose rendering is not captured in the static HTML.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images and to the two GIFs. Use `alt=""` for spacers and pixels. This clears about 55 of the QA warnings and should lift the 57% pass rate substantially.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`, so users can zoom and resize text.
3. Deduplicate the repeated pixels (the 6× `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and the 5× `rlcdn` calls).
4. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>` and align the title with the subject line. Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment and the duplicate meta tags.
5. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message at the ESP, because the relay may be dropping them. Then confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirected links with a browser or an unwrapping probe to check the landing pages and UTM parameters.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. The truncation prevented checks of the footer, remaining links and any merge tokens.
