# So many snuggles. Make your nursery chair time cozy →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, nursery chairs — that's not a "someday" category for me right now, that's literally an open tab on the second house's second-floor room I haven't finished. So credit where due: this landed better than most of what hits my inbox this week. Four glider/recliner options stacked top to bottom (Soosae, Babyletto Kiwi, Rory swivel, Milo swivel), each with its own lifestyle shot and a "Shop Nursery Seating" button — that's an actual product lineup, not a vague lifestyle mood board.
- But it's also relentless. Four nearly-identical chair blocks in a row, then a "3 types of nursery chairs" recap, then "nursery chair necessities," then a whole second promo for the C&B app, then "we love these for you" cross-sell — this email just does not end. By the time I hit the app pitch I'd already mentally clicked out. Compare that to Crate & Barrel's own "olive sofa" send from earlier this week (7/10, my highest score for this brand) — that one had one hero product and stopped. This one is four products trying to be one email.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably on the Babyletto Kiwi block — that's a name I actually recognize from baby-gear research. Would I forward it? To my wife, maybe, just the one chair screenshot, not the whole email.

## What's working

- Nursery seating is a real, current need for me — this isn't a stretch to my persona like some of the C&B Kids sends I've seen.
- Each chair has its own lifestyle photo with an actual parent-and-baby-in-chair shot, which sells the "cozy" promise better than a plain product cutout would.
- "Shop Nursery Seating" is a clear, repeated, unambiguous CTA — I always know what tapping gets me.
- The "3 types of nursery chairs" educational block (rockers/gliders/recliners) is a smart touch for someone like me who doesn't know the category jargon yet.
- Top banner gives a real number — up to 20% off — so there's an actual offer anchoring the whole email, unlike Wayfair's vague "Open Box steals" send that never surfaced a number.

## What's weak

- Four chairs in a row before you even get to "necessities" is just too much — CB2's sale email at least led with one clear hero before diversifying.
- No prices anywhere that I can see — not on the hero banner beyond "20%," not on any individual chair. I'm comparing four recliners and I have zero idea if we're talking $400 or $1,400 each.
- The email drags forever — by the time I hit the app download pitch near the bottom I'd already checked out, and that section feels bolted onto an already-long email.
- No fabric/color swatch options visible on any chair — for a chair I'm going to sit in for months of late-night feedings, "olive or cream?" matters and I can't tell if there's a choice.
- This is basically the third or fourth Crate & Barrel Kids email this week (Venus Williams collab twice, the holiday-in-September one) — my inbox fatigue with this sender specifically is real at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one hero chair (Babyletto Kiwi, since it's the recognizable brand) with the other three demoted to a small "also available" strip — that alone would fix the fatigue problem.
- 2. Put a price or starting-at price on every single chair block. I shouldn't have to click through four times to find out if this is in budget.
- 3. Kill the mid-email app download pitch entirely — it breaks the shopping flow and reads like a different email got pasted in.
- 4. Add a swatch/fabric strip under each chair (boucle, velvet, leather-look) since color match to the nursery is exactly the kind of decision I'm stuck on right now.
- 5. Trim "nursery chair necessities" and "we love these for you" down to one combined cross-sell block near the very end, not two separate ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nursery chairs, ranked: glider vs. recliner vs. rocker`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off nursery seating — the one parents actually love`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `4 chairs, real price tags, zero guesswork`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Glider, recliner, or swivel — find your fit`
