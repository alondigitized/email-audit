# Hours left for up to 40% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Thirty-two. That's the CB2 count for July, and I'm opening with it again because it is the essential context for this email. I already reviewed a CB2 "UP TO 40% OFF ENDS TODAY" earlier this same morning — scored it a 6 — and now here's an afternoon follow-up with slightly warmer subject copy and the same exact sale. "Hours left" is genuinely more conversational than the all-caps bark from the morning send, and for half a second I thought this was different creative. It isn't. The Summer Edit hero, the outdoor collection callout, the same percentage, same structure. CB2 is running two clocks on the same sale in the same inbox on the same day.
- That said, the email itself is clean. The visual hierarchy is immediate — offer lands first, zero confusion about what they want you to do. The outdoor collection at 50%+ is actually the sharpest line in here; I'm furnishing a second property and that number got a real look. The $5,000 Refresh section with the free design appointment is the most genuinely useful thing CB2 has put in front of me all week — two active renovations, limited time — and they buried it third in the scroll. The one offer that would have made me click is below where most people stop reading.
- Mark as read. I'd save the Refresh section if it were the hero. It's not.

## What's working

- **"Hours left" subject beats "ENDS TODAY" all-caps** — it reads as human, not panicked, and the 29-character length lands clean on mobile
- **Outdoor collection at 50%+ is specific and relevant** — for a second home build, that number earns a second look; more useful than the flat 40% general banner
- **$5,000 Refresh + free design appointment is genuinely differentiated** — this is the offer CB2 should be leading with; it's the only thing in here I haven't seen from Pottery Barn or Wayfair this week
- **Clean visual hierarchy, no render bugs** — offer is hero, everything below it ladders down correctly; 32 emails in and the layout still holds

## What's weak

- **This is the second CB2 "40% off ends today" email today** — the morning send was identical in substance; this reads like a scheduling error, not a deliberate cadence
- **"Up to 40% off" credibility is spent** — after yesterday's identical "ends today" send that apparently wasn't the last day, the urgency is purely decorative at this point
- **The Refresh is buried at scroll position three** — the one truly differentiated CTA in this email is below where most mobile readers tap out; Pottery Barn's new arrivals send last week at least kept its primary CTA in the first third
- **Zero personalization visible** — CB2 has my purchase history; "More For You" at the bottom looks like a generic grid, not a curated picks section; Article's dining room email earlier this month felt targeted because it was, and the contrast stings

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the $5,000 Refresh the hero** — a sale-tied design appointment is the one offer in this email that CB2's competitors aren't running; it deserves the top slot, not the third
- 2. **Collapse the morning and afternoon sends into one** — two same-day emails on the same sale is the fastest path to an unsubscribe; one well-timed 8am send with the outdoor deal AND the Refresh would outperform the double-tap
- 3. **Put a hard timestamp on "hours left"** — "Ends at midnight ET" beats the vague countdown; Article set a clean deadline in their last send and it felt trustworthy compared to this
- 4. **Personalize the product grid** — if the algorithm has my data, show me floor lamps and dining chairs adjacent to what I've browsed, not a random furniture set
- 5. **Pull the outdoor 50%+ up to the hero secondary callout** — it's the most specific offer in the email and it's not visible until you scroll past the main Summer Edit banner
