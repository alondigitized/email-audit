- Okay, "Open Box Deals" — at least this one has a real, honest premise: this is discounted-because-it's-a-return/floor-model inventory, not another fake-flash-sale wrapper. But then it's straight into the grid-of-category-tiles format Wayfair runs every single time, 20 tiles deep, "up to X% off" on rugs, bar stools, desks, mirrors, cabinet pulls — no hero product, no price you can actually anchor to, no photo of a single piece of furniture after the opening banner.
- This is basically the same skeleton as yesterday's "FOUND: Bar Stools!" disaster (2.00/10) — different headline, same 20-tile grid, same nothing-actually-shown problem — except at least nothing's visibly broken here, no missing images, no truncated bar stool that quietly disappeared. I'm not clicking. This gets archived to the Pinterest-adjacent folder for the dutch oven graphic alone and nothing else.

## What's working

- The "open box deals" wordmark with the pot wedged into the "o" is a legit clever visual — better than CB2's straight banner text this week.
- Concept itself is relevant to my reno budget — open-box/returned furniture at discount is exactly the kind of deal I'd actually take seriously, more than a vague seasonal sale.
- Everything renders clean — no broken images, no lorem ipsum, no missing tiles like the bar stool email had yesterday.
- Rewards/Pay Over Time/Wayfair Professional/Exclusive Brands icon row at the bottom is a nice touch acknowledging I might be a repeat/rewards shopper.

## What's weak

- Not one actual open-box product is shown — no "was $400, now $180, one available" listing, which is the whole appeal of an open-box sale.
- The tile grid is identical in structure to "FOUND: Bar Stools!" and "HOT TUBS" from the last two days — I'm now pattern-matching Wayfair emails by their layout, not their content.
- "Up to 70%" repeated across a dozen categories with zero specifics reads like a template fill, not a curated open-box drop.
- No urgency or inventory signal — open-box deals are supposed to feel finite ("only 3 left"), and this has none of that scarcity.
- Bottom "Family of Brands" (AllModern, Birch Lane, Joss & Main, Perigold) plus app QR code is just more real estate not spent on an actual product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show 4-6 real open-box items with before/after price and "1 left in stock" tags — that's the entire point of this offer type and it's completely missing.
- 2. Cut the 20-tile category grid down to 6 max, and only categories with confirmed open-box inventory, not every furniture category Wayfair sells.
- 3. Add a countdown or "inventory refreshes weekly" line — open-box only works as urgency-driven, and right now it reads evergreen.
- 4. Lead the hero with an actual open-box photo (scuff, box-damage callout, whatever) instead of only stock imagery — it'd make the discount feel more credible.

- 20 open/click tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are loaded over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — see Rendering & Accessibility below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 20 tracking pixels served via `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...` (sequential IDs 124126000–124126019) use non-HTTPS `src` — many mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) will block mixed-content HTTP image requests by default, silently degrading open-tracking accuracy.
- 23 images are missing `alt` text, including the primary open-tracking beacon (`b.php`) and the hero product image `wfus-openbox-2025-minibahn-02.gif`. Missing alt text on the hero image degrades the experience for image-blocked/screen-reader clients since no fallback text is rendered.

- `@font-face` reference to `sofia_reg.woff` has an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for Outlook — correctly implemented.

No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization syntax found in the truncated source. Tracking-parameter values (`_eml`, `_emr`, `p=`) appear to be populated per-recipient GUIDs rather than left as literal placeholders.
