- The swatch section is still useful to me. I'm furnishing two houses and ordering 10 free swatches before committing to a sofa is how I actually shop. The Faible and Belmond sectional photos are good and look like the Pinterest-folder material I save. But the email has no prices, no Black Friday terms, and no early-access mechanic. Pottery Barn's "Extra 20% off" strip is repetitive, but at least it states a number tied to the promo.

## What's working

- The "Swatch now. Shop later." hero fits my situation. I'm comparing fabrics across two renovations.
- Two "Order free swatches" buttons, one up top and one lower down, are clear black buttons I could tap with a thumb.
- The Faible and Belmond sectionals are labeled by name, so I can look them up. That's more useful than Wayfair's anonymous grid.
- The "Last Chance Warehouse Sale, up to 60% off" strip is a single concrete number, and it sits at the very top.
- The 10% back in Rewards Dollars box speaks to my highest-rewards-card habit.

## What's weak

- "Black Friday Head Start" in the subject doesn't match the body. I can't find a Black Friday offer, early-access perk or date.
- The Warehouse Sale is the same message I've had since 10/30. "Last chance" has no credibility now.
- In the interior design block, the headline "Expert design, tailored to you" has a pendant light cutting through the text. The line looks partly obscured and hard to read.
- There are no prices, no shipping or lead-time info for the sectionals, and no color-palette guidance. I need delivery timing on furniture before I'd commit.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of tiny text. That's a trust problem for an offer billed as "last chance."

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero match. Either lead with the actual Black Friday early-access offer (dates, percent, who qualifies) or drop the phrase. Right now the subject promises something the email doesn't deliver.
- 2. Pick one story per send. Swatches and a sale make two competing CTAs, plus design services and the app. Lead with one.
- 3. Put prices and lead times under the Faible and Belmond sectionals so the images become shoppable.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the design-services headline by moving the text off the pendant light.
- 5. Retire "last chance" until the sale actually ends. Show a real date.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

It also has several accessibility gaps and ten insecure `http://` third-party tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay doesn't expose the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Ten insecure pixels.** These load over plain HTTP and may be blocked, or trigger mixed-content warnings, in clients that proxy images:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
- **Heavy third-party pixel load.** Ten cookie-redirect calls plus the UCM call fire on open. Each is an extra network request that no `https` fallback covers.
