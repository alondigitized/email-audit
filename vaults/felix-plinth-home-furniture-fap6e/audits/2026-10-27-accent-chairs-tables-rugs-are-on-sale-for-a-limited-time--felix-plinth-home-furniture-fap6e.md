- The hero says Thanksgiving dining room, the headline says accent chairs and tables, and the subject adds rugs. That's three framings in the first screen.
- "Select styles" and "up to 30%" show up with no deadline. The subject says "limited time", and Pottery Barn at least tells me "not for long" with a delivery angle.
- The Design Desk pitch appears twice. A free design appointment is a good hook for two houses, but the same ad twice feels padded.
- I count five different "Shop" buttons (Shop The Sale, Shop All Sale twice, Shop Now, Book Now). Most of them go to the same sale page, so they don't tell me what I'll land on.
- The footer is a wall of fine print on exclusions, so the offer feels less honest than the hero suggests. There's also no shipping or lead-time info for the furniture, which is what I need before I commit to a chair.

- 3. Cut the second Design Desk block and the app block. Keep one design-help CTA.
- 4. Make the buttons say what's behind them: "Shop rugs up to 60% off", "Shop accent chairs".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style template (`<!--V2-->`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` personalization) with no hard failures: the QA run reports 0 issues and 3 warnings, at a 57% pass rate. The real problems are about 30 images with no alt text, viewport and text-size settings that block zoom, and header-level compliance checks that couldn't be verified because of the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **64 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are LiveRamp (rlcdn) syncs. They add third-party requests and carry the same hashed identifier `s=dbe8da718e29…`.
- **Identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a…` value is repeated across the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images and the `o.gif` open pixel. It is a per-recipient token embedded in every image request, so forwarding the email or caching its images exposes it.
- **Open pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/679d81f6-d247-432f-8e8d-c67700da20fb?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are standard, with no defects found.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 30 images (QA WARN).**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`: 17 images with no alt, such as `2d3d8b72-….jpg` and `cd56cdc7-….gif`. With images blocked or a screen reader, these are blank or announced by filename.
  - Dynamic images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images, e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`). These look like personalized recommendation tiles, so they likely need real alt text.
  - Tracking and sync pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv…/679d81f6-…`). These should carry `alt=""` and are lower priority.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS text scaling. The later `body` rule sets `100%`, so the rules conflict.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Template residue:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled placeholder comment.
  - There are two `<meta charset>` declarations (the `http-equiv` one and `<meta charset="utf-8">`).
  - The `format-detection` meta tag is repeated three times.
  - These are harmless but add weight.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` because the later rule wins. The 600px cap never applies.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `color-scheme: light`, and MSO conditional comments with `PixelsPerInch`.
