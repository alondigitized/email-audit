# Introducing the Ashley Stark collab
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn finally sent something that isn't the dark-green "Fall Sale: Up to 70% off" strip. That strip has topped every send since 10/06, and I was ready to ignore it. This one opens on a clean Ashley Stark × Pottery Barn lockup, a portrait, and a SHOP NOW button. It looks like a design story, not a coupon blast, and that's the version of this brand I'll save to my Pinterest folder.
- I'm renovating two houses, so a "creative director's take on old and new" collection is in my lane. The warm, moody palette (brown velvet, brass, a big round mirror) feels designed for a living room I'd actually build. The weakness is that it's long and has no price or deadline. I like it and I'd probably click once, but I'd read it as a lookbook. It's not a reason to buy today. I'd forward it to my partner for the mirror, not to a friend.

## What's working

- No Fall Sale strip on top. After the 10/13 "Rugs to ground your space in color" send and the 10/14 Grinch collab, this one resets the tone.
- The Ashley Stark × Pottery Barn header is clean, and SHOP NOW sits right under it. I know what this is within a second.
- The pull quotes ("I love the tension between old and new," "Texture was a huge inspiration…") give the collection a point of view. That's the voice-driven storytelling Article did in "Fall is for full tables."
- The room shots (bedroom, mirror vignette, dining table) show a consistent palette: warm neutrals, brass, burl and marble. That helps me judge what goes with what.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid has named products (Banks Extendable Dining Table, Remmy Swivel Chair, Cayman Platform Bed). The Earn 10% Back in Rewards card is there too, and that matters since I buy on the highest-rewards card.

## What's weak

- No offer, no price, and no deadline anywhere I can see. Crate & Barrel's "hours left: up to 20% off sofas" at least gives me a reason to act today.
- It's very long. Between the collab story, the grid, a Christmas Shop banner, Free Design Services, an app promo, the rewards card and Affirm, the collab story gets buried.
- I can't tell which pieces are actually the collab. The grid below looks like generic bestsellers (a curtain rod, a rug, a platform bed), not the Ashley Stark items.
- The Christmas Shop banner in mid-October, right after the Grinch send yesterday, feels like holiday creep and dilutes the collab.
- The footer is dense with legal and promo exclusion text at tiny size. For a furniture buyer, nothing tells me shipping time or whether swatches are available.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the grid collab-only. Show six Ashley Stark pieces with prices and "ships by" windows, and drop the generic bestsellers.
- 2. Add one honest hook, such as a rewards-point multiplier, free swatches, or a design-services booking tied to the collection. Right now there's nothing time-bound.
- 3. Cut the Christmas Shop, app, and Affirm modules. This should be a collection email, not a catalog.
- 4. Move the price range and shipping clarity next to the SHOP THE COLLECTION button.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout sent through an ESP click-redirect domain. The main issues are widespread missing `alt` text, three plain-HTTP image sources, the recipient's email address exposed in tracking-pixel URLs, and stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of relay capture. The HTML source was truncated, so footer content wasn't available.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** 56 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…`. The HTTP probe skipped them, so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=…` is served over HTTP. Clients may block it, which would undercount opens.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an external ad-network pixel (Turn).
- **Cross-brand dependency:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` loads from a different brand's host.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** about 50 images are flagged. The hero and content slices (`…/1014_WED_PM_AshleyStark/slices_01–13.jpg`) and the footer slices (`…/Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) are the main groups. Product images are also affected, for example `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg` and `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`. With images off or a screen reader, the email is effectively blank. Several of the flagged URLs are 1x1 tracking pixels, where empty `alt=""` is acceptable.
- **HTTP image sources:**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - These are mixed-content risks and may be blocked.
- **Stray code in CSS:** the second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store…"); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is leaked PHP and can break CSS parsing in strict clients.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Web font:** the `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat` is stripped by many clients, so fallback fonts must be defined.
- **Tables:** only some tables have `role="presentation"`. The spacer tables, such as `<td height="20">&nbsp;</td>`, lack it.
- **Markup validity:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens were seen in the visible HTML.
- **Email address in URLs:** the recipient's address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears in plaintext query strings:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=…`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?…&sv_sveme=…` (over HTTP, so it is exposed in transit)
- That is a privacy and PII-leakage risk, and it can end up in server logs and referrers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` can't be assessed from the data provided.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** can't be verified because the source was truncated. The footer is image-sliced (`Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`) with no alt text, so any text-based unsubscribe or address inside those images is not accessible. This should be checked in the full HTML.
- **Plain-text part:** 10,875 of 15,075 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which is a minor spam-filter signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs are hidden behind `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=…` tokens, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the HTML or the QA data.
- The visible product image hosts are `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/…`, which is consistent with the brand domain.
- No continuity defect is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content, hero, product and footer images, and use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` image URLs to HTTPS, especially the `svopn` pixel.
3. Remove the leaked `header(...)` PHP lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Stop putting the raw email address in query strings (`coreemail.html?email=`, `sv_sveme=`) and use a hashed or opaque identifier.
5. Fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message at the origin. The relay may be hiding them.
7. Check the full HTML for a text-based unsubscribe link and a physical postal address, and don't rely on image-only footer slices.
8. Host the `spacer_0.gif` asset on the sender's own domain instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
9. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page destinations.
