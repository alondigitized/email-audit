- The entire top half — hero, nursery collection, crib photography, "we love these for you" baby dresser and glider picks — is a nursery/kids pitch, and nothing in my shopping history says I need any of it.
- A stroller ad ("now boarding: Nuna's new carry-on stroller") buried mid-email has zero business being here if I'm not a parent-shopper — it's the kind of irrelevant tile that makes me tune out the rest of the send.

- 2. Move "more new fall arrivals" (furniture, rugs, lighting, storage) up top — that's the section doing real work for a reno-focused adult buyer, not the nursery block.
