# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and the subject line made a promise the render never kept. I opened it for "FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day." After scrolling the whole thing, I saw no free-shipping banner, no threshold, and no fine print. The top of the email is a giant blank white box with one tiny broken-image icon where the hero should be. The 10/3 send had the same problem, with the subject promising free shipping and the body never mentioning it.
- The rest is mostly empty boxes with blue alt-text links. I can read "Wooden Patio Rockers," "Chicken Coops in Every Size," and "Top-Rated Freshwater Aquariums," but I can't see a single product. Three of the four big image slots are blank, and so are almost all the grid tiles. Only the Queen Box Springs tile and the air fryer banner loaded. I'm renovating two houses. Chicken coops and foosball tables tell me this was blasted to everyone, not to me.
- I wouldn't click, I wouldn't forward it, and I'd swipe it away. Wayfair has now sent me a broken or mismatched email several days in a row.

## What's working

- The Wayfair logo and the "Shop Sale / New Arrivals" nav at the top render cleanly, so I know who sent it.
- The air fryer banner, "Mix, Crisp & Chill – for Less," is a clear, readable block with a real button, "Shop Appliances." It's the only part that works as intended.
- The "let's complete your space" purple bar is a nice touch for someone furnishing rooms.
- The Queen Box Springs tile has a real bedroom photo. It's the one grid tile that looks like a product I might consider.
- The footer has the Rewards, Financing and Professional icons plus an app QR code. Those are trust cues I'd actually use for a big furniture order.

## What's weak

- The subject promised free shipping over $35, and I never saw it anywhere in the email.
- The hero and the other large image slots are blank white boxes with a broken icon. A phone user would see a huge empty first screen.
- Almost every grid tile is blank. All I get is blue link text, with no images and no prices.
- The assortment is random: aquariums, chicken coops, foosball tables, clothing racks and ceiling fans. None of it fits two home renovations. Pottery Barn's "Heritage holiday quilts" send had one clear idea. This has about twelve.
- No sale percentage or price appears anywhere I can see. The "INSIDER SALE 20% OFF" in the app promo is too tiny to read.
- It's the same repetitive, broken pattern as the 10/2 dinnerware email and the 10/4 coffee tables send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the free-shipping offer in the hero as live text, not an image, so it still shows when images fail. Include the threshold and the "ships free over $35" line.
- 2. Add a bulletproof fallback. Use background colors and styled alt text so a blocked image doesn't leave a white void.
- 3. Narrow the grid to my categories, like sofas, rugs, lighting and tabletop. Put a price or "% off" on each tile.
- 4. Cut the 14 tiles to 4–6 and group them by room. CB2's "IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE." at least gave me one coherent upholstery event.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is sound, with direct tracked links and no visible merge-token leaks in the captured source. It has 12 missing-alt images, 20+ non-HTTPS impression beacons, one malformed image URL, and compliance headers that could not be verified through the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Seven probed links returned 429, e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=2` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app link. That is rate limiting, not a failure, so these are unverified rather than broken.
- **4 links never probed** (70s time budget). Their URLs look truncated or malformed, but this may be the QA output truncating them, so check them against the raw HTML:
  - `.../queen-box-springs-you%27ll-love-se71` (no extension)
  - `.../wooden-patio-rockers-se78268.html?ei`
  - `.../chicken-coops-in-every-size-se38165.`
  - `.../in-stock-filing-cabinets-se60408.htm`
- **Malformed image URL:** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`. `%oo` is not a valid percent-escape, so some clients or proxies may fail to load it.
- **Inconsistent campaign labeling in the app link:** `c=Triggered` appears alongside `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is labeled as a triggered campaign in AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback in the app link:** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Non-app users get an extra http→https redirect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - `small_appliances_02_gif.gif`
  - `verified-email-standardcopy-minibahn.jpg`

  The first four are wrapped in links. The link text falls back to `[image: <filename>]`, so those links have no accessible name for screen readers. They also show nothing when images are blocked.
- **Missing alt on 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons** (`s=124126000` to `s=124126019`). Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, since these are decorative.
- **20 beacons load over `http://`** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=1241260xx&sz=2x1...`). They can be blocked as mixed content or rewritten by image proxies, which would undercount ad impressions.
- The responsive CSS (`@media (max-width: 480px)`, `u ~ div`, `.u-FullWidthBlock`) is well formed. The rest of the HTML was truncated, so I can't audit further.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the captured source. The `_emr` and `_eml` values are resolved per-recipient and per-link IDs. The source is truncated, so the body copy and footer are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified, not confirmed absent. Check the raw message headers at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` can't be assessed from this capture.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link was always probed, but no result is listed. The footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses proprietary parameters (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `refid=MKTEML_121679`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16388`, `mdlid=<per-module>`). That is consistent across links, but it won't populate GA `utm_` dimensions unless Wayfair maps these server-side.
- **Subject-to-landing alignment:** the subject promises "FREE shipping over $35". The hero image and "Shop Sale" both link to `/daily-sales`, and the logo and a banner link to `/`. No link in the probed set goes to a shipping-specific page. The subject offer can't be confirmed on the landing pages from this data.
- **Deep-link handling:** the app link sets both `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and `af_web_dp`, which is correct fallback behavior.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the original headers, bypassing the relay. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
2. Fix the `Outd%oorClearance` image URL to a valid encoding such as `Outdoor` or `%6F`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four linked images so those links have an accessible name. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to the tracking pixels.
4. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons and the `af_web_dp` fallback to HTTPS.
5. Re-check the four unprobed `/daily-sales/filters/...` URLs against the raw HTML for truncation. Re-probe the 429 links with backoff.
6. Align the AppsFlyer `c=Triggered` value with the batch-send naming, or confirm it's intentional.
7. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that `source`, `batchid` and `refid` are mapped into analytics.
