- **11 non-HTTPS tracking pixels** are loaded over `http://`. Mail clients and image proxies may block or rewrite them, which would silently drop the conversion and retargeting data. The evidence is:

  - `6047cf9d-01b5-44fe-bdd5-ce345cd10f96.jpg`
