# Earn 2x Rewards & save up to 60% on bedding →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids. Again. This is the sixth email from the Crate & Barrel family sitting in my inbox today, and this one's the most irrelevant yet — nursery cribs, toddler quilts, a stuffed Jellycat lion, a "design your dream nursery" sweepstakes. I don't have kids. I have two houses mid-renovation and zero baby registries to fill, so this whole email is dead on arrival for me personally.
- That said, credit where due: the "up to 60% off bedding + ships free" hero is clean, the crib/nursery styling shot actually looks nice, and the Double Rewards banner up top is legible. But it's a kids' bedding sale wrapped in the same "Double Rewards" language I already saw twice this morning from this same sender, just aimed at a completely different shopper than me.
- I'm not opening this again, not forwarding it, and honestly I'm getting close to unsubscribing from the whole Crate & Kids sub-brand — Crate & Barrel proper I'll keep (their actual sofa sale from earlier scored a 7 from me), but this one's noise.

## What's working

- The hero banner clearly states "up to 60% off bedding + ships free" — no digging required.
- Double Rewards callout ("Earn 15% back in Reward Dollars") is bold and easy to spot at the top and again mid-email.
- Product tiles show real prices with strikethrough (Sale $79.99 / Reg $99.99 style) — that's honest pricing, no bait-and-switch.
- The nursery/kids room styling shots are genuinely well-photographed, on-brand with the C&B aesthetic I already trust from the parent brand.

## What's weak

- I'm not a parent shopper — cribs, toddler quilts, and a Jellycat stuffed lion have nothing to do with a guy renovating two adult homes.
- This is the sixth Crate & Barrel family send today; my brain is fully in snooze mode for anything with "Double Rewards" in it at this point.
- The email is enormous — I scrolled forever past crib bedding, baby bedding, a "win $5000" sweepstakes, and a credit card pitch before hitting anything remotely adjacent to my world.
- Zero segmentation — nothing here suggests they know I've never bought a kids' item from them, unlike Article or CB2 who at least stay in adult furniture lanes with me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by purchase history — if I've never bought nursery/kids product, don't send me Crate & Kids at all; fold me into Crate & Barrel-proper sends only. This is the single biggest lever here.
- 2. Cut the email length by half — five separate promo blocks (bedding, baby bedding, Jellycat restock, sweepstakes, credit card) in one send is exactly the kitchen-sink scroll I already called out Wayfair for.
- 3. Stop reusing "Double Rewards" copy across every sibling brand in the same week — Crate & Barrel, Crate & Kids, CB2 all ran near-identical rewards messaging today.
- 4. If Crate & Kids must go out to non-parent subscribers, lead with something universal (the Jellycat restock has broader appeal) instead of crib bedding.
