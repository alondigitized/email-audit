# 100s of NEW Labor Day deals up to 70% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is basically the same "Up to 70% off" burgundy hero I already saw from Pottery Barn yesterday for "100s of New Labor Day Deals" — same headline, same category grid, same font. My brain barely registered it as new before I'd already scrolled past. Where it actually earns its keep is the "Recommended Just For You" block right under the fold: a Farmhouse Platform Bed, extendable dining tables, a swivel chair, a wool rug — this is the first Pottery Barn send all week that feels like it's looking at my actual reno list instead of just blasting a discount number at me.
- Then it falls apart. Halfway down I hit "The Halloween Shop — Enter If You Dare" with ghost decor on a porch, immediately followed by a generic "Discover New Arrivals" sofa banner that has nothing to do with Labor Day or the sale at all. That's three different campaigns crammed into one email, and it kills the momentum the recommended-products block built. I'd click through to the recommended dining table, sure, but I wouldn't forward this — it's too scattered to be worth someone else's time.

## What's working

- The burgundy "Up to 70% off" hero still lands fast and reads clean at a glance, consistent with yesterday's send.
- The category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Outdoor, Rugs, etc.) covers my whole punch list in one scroll, better than Wayfair's messier layout.
- "Recommended Just For You" actually shows relevant SKUs — extendable dining tables and a platform bed are things I need for the second house, not filler.
- Product cards show real names and dimensions ("60"-124.5"", "76"-128"") right under the image, which I don't get from CB2 or Crate & Kids.

## What's weak

- This is nearly a repeat of yesterday's "100s of New Labor Day Deals" email — same hero copy, same visual treatment, back-to-back days. That's Crate & Kids-level cadence fatigue creeping in.
- The Halloween Shop insert has zero business being in a Labor Day sale email — it's a jarring tonal and seasonal whiplash mid-scroll.
- "Discover New Arrivals" sofa banner right after Halloween is a third, unrelated CTA competing for the same click — no clear single hierarchy by that point.
- No specific % off shown on any of the recommended products — I have to click through blind to find out if the bed or table is actually part of the 70% off claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Halloween Shop and New Arrivals banners out of this specific send — a sale email should stay a sale email start to finish. Article's Labor Day send didn't dilute itself like this.
- 2. Put a discount badge or "Now $X" directly on the recommended product cards — that's the single easiest lift for click-through.
- 3. Vary the hero creative from yesterday's send even if the offer is the same — a different photo or headline treatment so it doesn't read as a duplicate in my inbox.
- 4. Move "Recommended Just For You" up above the category grid — it's the strongest, most relevant content in the whole email and it's currently buried.
