# NEW clearance items added: up to 60% off!

## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Barrel Kids send, and the brand history says the same thing. This is the eighth C&B-family email in two days. I'm renovating two houses and nobody in mine is under four feet tall. A kids sender is the wrong door for me, and the subject line doesn't hide it. "Up to 60% off clearance" is a real number, but I can't tell from the hero what's actually marked down. It's a headline over autumn leaves with a "Shop Clearance" button.
- Scrolling down, it's nursery stuff: stroller systems, a bassinet-style sleeper, a crib mattress, a Halloween treat bag. The one thing that made me pause was The Design Desk block with the free design appointment. That's the same pitch I saw in the 10/21 "Your most beautiful holidays yet start with The Design Desk" email, so it isn't new either. I'd mark this read and move on. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it to anyone who isn't expecting a baby.

## What's working

- The "up to 60% off clearance" headline is a concrete, plain number. I know what the deal is.
- The four-link nav grid under the hero (Furniture, Rugs, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) is the only part that speaks to my renovation.
- Free shipping on toys over $99 is stated clearly with a visible button.
- The Design Desk block, with a designer photo and a free appointment, is the one real service offer. It's closest to what Article pitched me in "Our free interior design services are waiting."
- Product cards show names, prices and a struck-through price on the Halloween bag, so the sale math is visible.

## What's weak

- The kids framing locks me out. Strollers, a crib mattress and a nursery recliner are nothing I'm shopping for.
- The hero doesn't show any clearance product, only a leaf photo and a button. Wayfair's pergola email at least led with one item and a "$134 off" badge.
- The Design Desk pitch appears twice, once mid-email and again near the bottom. It's also a repeat of the 10/21 send.
- The 60% headline is followed by a long wall of fine print, and the "$500 chance" app promo adds to the clutter.
- Most items shown are full price, not clearance. Only the Halloween bag has a visible markdown, so the "60% off" claim isn't backed up in the grid.
- The credit card block and app download block make this feel like a stack of unrelated asks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with three real clearance items and their actual markdowns above the fold, so the 60% claim is proven right away.
- 2. Segment me out of nursery content. I've clicked rugs and lighting, not strollers.
- 3. Cut the Design Desk to one placement and show something new each time, such as a real before-and-after.
- 4. Drop the card and app blocks on a sale send.
- 5. Cap cadence. Eight C&B emails in two days is a deliverability and unsubscribe risk.
