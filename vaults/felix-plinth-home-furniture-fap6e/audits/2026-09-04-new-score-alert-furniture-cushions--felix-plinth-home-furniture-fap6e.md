# New score alert: Furniture Cushions (!!!)
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- I actually laughed out loud at this one. The subject says "New score alert: Furniture Cushions (!!!)" — like my Wayfair account has some kind of algorithm that just found me a deal — and then I scroll through the entire email top to bottom and there is not a single cushion anywhere. Doormats, pergolas, privacy screens, a teal sofa, air conditioners, fountains, gazebos, an accent cabinet, wall art, a sectional, recliners, an outdoor playhouse. No cushions. Not even buried in row six like yesterday's send.
- This is the fifth Wayfair email in a row in my inbox and it's the worst offender yet on the subject-matching-content front. At least the pergola one had a pergola up top, the dog bed one had a dog bed, the ladder one had a ladder. This one just picked "Furniture Cushions" at random and slapped it on a completely unrelated kitchen-sink grid. Compare that to Article, which put "Up to 40% off" front and center with zero scrolling, or even Crate & Barrel's fifth-send-of-the-week email, which at least stayed on-theme with Labor Day.
- Deleting this one without opening — wait, I already opened it and I still can't find what it promised me. Not clicking, not forwarding, this is going straight to "why do I still get these."

## What's working

- The 70% off Labor Day banner up top is at least a real, visible number I can act on.
- "The warmest welcome" doormat section has decent lifestyle styling with the dog — mildly charming.
- Air conditioner section ("cool down on any budget") is seasonally relevant for early September.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Furniture Cushions" — I scrolled the entire email and never found a single cushion, image or text mention. That's a first even for Wayfair.
- It's the same sprawling everything-grid Wayfair sends every day this week — pergolas, privacy screens, sofas, fountains, gazebos, wall art, recliners, playhouses — nothing curated to what I've browsed.
- There's a big blank white gap near the bottom with what looks like a stray blue dot — looks like a broken/missing image block.
- "but wait, there's more..." and "these" and "SEE WHY" are filler section headers that tell me nothing about what's actually being sold.
- Zero mention of Wayfair Rewards points or member pricing despite the footer icons touting Rewards — feels like a missed chance to make me feel like a known customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match the subject to actual hero content — if you're sending a kitchen-sink grid, don't invent a fake "score alert" for a category that isn't in the email at all. This is actively worse than burying it in row four.
- 2. Fix or remove the broken image block near the bottom before the footer — it renders as an empty white gap with a stray dot.
- 3. Lead with the 70% off banner as the hero, then have every section below actually reinforce that number instead of vague headers like "but wait, there's more..."
- 4. If cushions are genuinely the intended focus, put a cushion tile in the top row with a price, the way the pergola and dog bed emails did.
- 5. Surface Wayfair Rewards points earned on this sale in the body, not just as a footer icon.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day cushion picks are here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on outdoor cushions plus pergolas, sectionals & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `70% off Labor Day Clearance — shop cushions before it ends`
