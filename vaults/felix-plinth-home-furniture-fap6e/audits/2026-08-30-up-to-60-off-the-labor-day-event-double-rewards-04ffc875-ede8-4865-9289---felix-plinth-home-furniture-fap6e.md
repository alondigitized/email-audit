# Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event + Double Rewards
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Same "Double Rewards + up to 60% off" hero I've now seen from Crate & Barrel twice this week — except this one's actually Crate & Kids, which I clocked about two seconds in from the crib in the hero shot. I don't have kids, I'm not shopping for kids, and nothing here — bassinets, changing tables, kids' bedding, a rocker with a "hundreds of items ship free" callout — touches either renovation. It's the same rewards math and the same "It's here!" energy as the parent brand's send, just reskinned for a different room I'll never furnish.
- I scrolled the whole thing out of morbid curiosity and there's genuinely nothing for me — decor and lighting sections show grown-up-looking pendant lights and a chandelier, which almost got me, but then it's back to nursery gliders and toddler bedding. This is an instant archive, not even a real read.
- Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward — unless it's to a friend who just had a kid, and even then I'd feel weird forwarding a promo email instead of just texting "hey C&B has a sale."

## What's working

- The chandelier and pendant-lighting shot in the "decor & lighting" section is genuinely on-brand for my style and could pass for a Crate & Barrel adult-home shot.
- Layout is clean and scannable, same visual polish as the main C&B sends — no render bugs, images load fine.
- Rewards math ("earn 10%-20% back in Reward Dollars") is stated clearly up top, consistent with what Crate & Barrel itself is pushing this week.

## What's weak

- Hero image is a crib and a bassinet-adjacent nursery scene — zero ambiguity that this isn't for me, unlike Crate & Barrel's own Labor Day send which at least opens on adult furniture.
- Entire middle of the email — kids bedding, baby furniture, rugs sized for a nursery, a rocker/bassinet — is 100% outside my two renovation projects.
- "Win $1000" sweepstakes and app download push feel like filler once you've already lost the reader on relevance.
- This is the third near-identical "Double Rewards + up to 60% off" Labor Day hero I've gotten from the Crate & Barrel family this week (C&B twice, now Crate & Kids) — the repetition itself is annoying regardless of category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household composition — if I'm not flagged as a parent/registry customer anywhere in Crate & Barrel's system, I shouldn't be on the Crate & Kids list at all. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. If cross-sell is unavoidable, lead with the one section that's actually adult-relevant (decor & lighting) instead of burying it under three baby-furniture blocks.
- 3. Differentiate the subject line from the parent brand's — right now it's a copy-paste of Crate & Barrel's own Labor Day subject, which just reads as inbox clutter once you've already opened that one.
- 4. Drop the sweepstakes/app-download tail — it's noise on a list that's already irrelevant to me.
