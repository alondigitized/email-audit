# Open Box Deals are here 📦
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Wayfair is now sending me two emails in one day — this "Open Box Deals" send lands the same 7/31 as the "BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS" one I already gave a 6. That's not a subscriber, that's a firehose, and I'm mid-renovation on two houses so my patience for Wayfair specifically is already thin.
- The hero is fine at a glance — orange armchair, green lamp, mustard pillow, "Open Box Deals" in that punchy red type. But then I scroll and it's just... eighteen identical purple pill buttons stacked top to bottom. Wall Art, Area Rugs, Bar Stools, Coffee Tables, all the way down to Cabinet Pulls, each one a different "up to X% OFF." Nothing here is actually an open-box item — no "was $400, now $211, one left in warehouse" — it's just the same category-sale format Wayfair runs every single week, with "open box" slapped on top as a headline that never gets paid off.
- I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding this to anyone. This is a skim-and-archive, and honestly it's starting to make me want to mute the whole list for a week.

## What's working

- The hero banner is legible and on-brand — purple/red palette reads as Wayfair instantly, no confusion about who sent this.
- Product cutouts (chair, lamp, pillow) at least gesture at furniture/home goods, which is in my wheelhouse right now with two renovations going.
- Wayfair Rewards icon is present in the footer block, so there's a nod to loyalty even if it's not doing anything for me here.
- Zero render bugs — no broken images, no overlapping text, everything's crisp on the scroll.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero promise "Open Box Deals" — actual clearance, one-off returned items — and then delivers eighteen generic category sale buttons. That's a bait-and-switch, same move Pottery Barn pulled with its "Halloween sneak peek" that was actually a summer sale.
- Eighteen purple buttons in a row with no hierarchy is exhausting — I did this exact complaint about the 7/30 "POOL TABLES" send with its wall of fifteen categories before I'd had coffee. Wayfair just keeps doing this.
- Not one specific product or price shown — every offer is "up to X% off," which I've learned by now means almost nothing lands at that top number.
- No room shots, no styling, nothing that shows me how any of this looks assembled. CB2's 7/30 home-office tour and even C&B's Heritage Maximalism hero (6/10, but at least it had a styled room) beat this cold on visual merchandising.
- Two Wayfair emails same calendar day is a cadence problem, full stop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE open-box hero item — an actual returned/discontinued piece with a real before/after price — and lead with it. That's the entire premise of the subject line; deliver on it.
- 2. Cut the eighteen-button wall to four or five categories max, or group them into a scannable grid instead of a vertical stack of near-identical pills.
- 3. Add urgency that's real — open-box inventory is inherently limited ("only 3 left"), so use that instead of generic "up to X% off."
- 4. Space this out from the privacy-screens send — don't hit me twice in one day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open Box: This armchair is $211, not $420`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Warehouse clearance — one of each, gone fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Returned, inspected, still under warranty — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited open-box stock across furniture & decor`
