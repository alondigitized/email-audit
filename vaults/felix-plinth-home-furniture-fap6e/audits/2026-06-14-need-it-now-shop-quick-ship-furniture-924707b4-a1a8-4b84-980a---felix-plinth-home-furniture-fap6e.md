### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

All four `.woff2` files are hosted directly on `crateandbarrel.com` (not a CDN) and return HTTP 403. The server is likely blocking cross-origin requests from email clients. Webfonts are only supported in Apple Mail and a small subset of clients; the 403 means those clients also fail, leaving the full send on system-font fallback.

**Tracking links:** 21 click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (expected behavior for wrapped redirects). No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but destination integrity cannot be asserted from this data.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 30 images flagged, zero exceptions:**

Every content image and every tracking/pixel image lacks an `alt` attribute. Notable affected images:

- Hero/product images: `bbf6f9d4-...`, `0b65340e-...`, `12438085-...`, scene7 product shots
- GIF animation: `905bce5c-...`
- Tracking pixels: `f3c0d22992a01946.png` (appears 6×), `448796.gif` (appears 5×), `o.gif`, `49ad91b9-...`

Impact is twofold: (1) screen readers receive no image context; (2) in image-blocking environments (Outlook default, Gmail mobile on poor connection), the entire visual content disappears with no fallback text. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.

**Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags:** `format-detection` appears three times — once as a combined value and once each for `date` and `address`. While not rendering-breaking, it signals copy-paste template hygiene issues.

**Left-in template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` — a developer placeholder is present in the `<head>`. Not a rendering issue but should be stripped before send.

**`max-width` media query conflict:** Two `@media` blocks targeting `min-width: 768px` and `min-width: 640px` both set `img { max-width: ... }`, with the 768px rule overriding the 640px rule on larger screens. This can cause images to exceed their container at the 640–767px breakpoint range.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header:** Not captured at the relay layer. CAN-SPAM and especially Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements mandate this header. If it is present upstream and stripped by the AgentMail relay, that is a relay configuration issue. If it is genuinely absent, this is a deliverability and legal compliance failure.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click):** Not present. Gmail and Yahoo have required one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders (>5K/day) since February 2024. Absence means mail may be routed to spam or have unsubscribe demoted to a manual flow.

**`Authentication-Results` header:** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown due to relay interception. Cannot confirm the send domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is properly authenticated. This is a relay-layer gap, not a confirmed failure.
