# 20% off ALL kids desks!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Seven. That's my running count of Crate & Barrel family emails since yesterday morning, and this one — kids' desks at 20% off — is actually the most legitimately useful thing they've sent me all week. I have a room in the second house that needs a desk. I've been loosely circling this. And yet my first instinct when this landed was mild exhaustion, because yesterday alone I sat through a fairy-tale lookbook, The Crib Guide, and two separate Breville/Le Creuset sale emails from what is technically the same parent company. My goodwill budget for the C&B sender address is running a deficit.
- The email itself is solid. The offer is front and center — no asterisk, no "starting from" sleight of hand — and pairing desks with storage as a secondary offer is smart, because that's how I actually shop. Where it loses me is the stroller/travel gear module that shows up partway down the scroll. I came in for a desk and suddenly I'm looking at a travel system. That's two different shopping modes in one send.
- I'd save this to the folder rather than click today. Would not forward — I already texted the same link from the website to my partner last week.

## What's working

- "20% off all kids desks" is in the hero, first thing I see — zero burial, no scrolling required to find the offer
- Storage at up to 35% off as a secondary module is the right companion — that's the actual purchase pair, desk plus organization
- Clean render, no broken images, readable text, clear product tiles with images; nothing embarrassing happening visually
- Back-to-school timing in mid-July is smart — I'd rather buy before the rush than scramble in August
- C&B brand trust is real; I've bought from this family before and returns have been painless

## What's weak

- The stroller/travel gear section mid-email is a different shopping trip entirely — it muddies what this email is for
- No furniture dimensions anywhere visible — I cannot evaluate a desk without knowing if it's 36" or 48" wide; Article and CB2 both surface specs closer to the product cards than this
- Zero deadline anywhere I can see — "20% off" with no end date means I defer indefinitely, and I will
- "ALL" in all caps in the subject reads slightly panicked; Pottery Barn's indigo color story yesterday made a clean case without raising its voice
- This is the seventh C&B family email in under 24 hours; cadence fatigue is real and it's working against even a legitimately relevant send

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the stroller module** or send it separately — one job per email; the desk message is genuinely strong on its own and doesn't need baby travel gear dragging it off-topic
- 2. **Add a hard end date** — "through Sunday" or "ends July 20" would close me off the fence; right now there's no reason not to procrastinate
- 3. **Put at least one dimension on the hero product or a card** — even "fits most standard alcoves (40"–48")" does the work; I'm not clicking to a PDP to find out a desk won't fit
- 4. **Pull back the cadence** — one Crate & Kids desk email is a campaign; seven C&B family emails in a day is noise; the Pottery Barn indigo send (single, purposeful, scored 8) showed what happens when a brand picks its moment
- 5. **Separate the storage sale** from the desk sale if the discounts are different mechanics — "up to 35% off select storage" vs. "20% off ALL desks" are different offers and conflating them in one email invites fine-print suspicion
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back-to-school desks, 20% off — all week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The desk that actually fits (20% off, this week only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 35% off storage. Sale ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Every kids desk, 20% off. No exclusions. Ships free over $49.`

## Technical Audit — Crate & Kids "20% off ALL kids desks!"

**1. Technical Summary**

Four web font requests return 403, degrading typography for all recipients. No authentication headers were captured by the relay, leaving SPF/DKIM status unverifiable and one-click unsubscribe non-functional.

---

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**

| Severity | Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FAIL | 4 web font files return HTTP 403 | `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/1e07d600-...woff2`, `7ec25fd5-...woff2`, `aaa41944-...woff2`, `fd5f8f50-...woff2` |
| INFO | 22 click-redirect URLs not probed | Tracking domain wraps prevented HTTP status verification — destination link health is unconfirmed |

The 403s on font files mean all clients that attempt to load custom fonts (primarily Apple Mail, Outlook on Mac) will fall back to system fonts. The font `@font-face` declarations are present in `<style>` but the CDN is blocking the fetch — likely a missing `Origin` / `Referer` header policy on the font CDN, or the files have been moved/deleted.

---

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

**Alt text:** 30 images are missing `alt` attributes, including all product imagery, both open/read tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6), and third-party retargeting pixels (`sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` ×5). This breaks the email entirely in image-blocking clients (Outlook default, many corporate environments) and fails WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1.

Notable missing-alt images:
- Hero/product shots: `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `2856f177-...jpg` through `971aa10f-...jpg`
- Animated GIF (likely promo banner): `d99d15dc-...gif`, `30cf0d67-...gif`
- Scene7 animated GIF: `CK%5FF26%5FBackToSChool%5F4x5%5F...gif` — product content, needs descriptive alt

**CSS conflicts:** Two `@media` blocks both target desktop but with different breakpoints (`min-width: 768px` sets `img { max-width: 600px }` while `min-width: 640px` sets `img { max-width: 640px }`). Because 640px is declared after 768px in source order, the 640px rule wins for viewports 640–767px — the narrower cap will clip images on mid-size tablets.

**Duplicate `format-detection` meta:** `format-detection` appears three times in `<head>` (consolidated value first, then individual `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no` declarations). Redundant but not harmful.

**`<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` placeholder** is present in production HTML — unfilled template comment left in source.

**5. Compliance**

| Severity | Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| WARN | `List-Unsubscribe` header absent | Not captured by AgentMail relay — one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 non-functional |
| WARN | `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent | Required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click compliance (2024 bulk sender rules) |
| WARN | `Authentication-Results` header absent | SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown — relay may have stripped headers |

The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` is the highest-risk compliance gap: Gmail and Yahoo now require `mailto:` + `https:` one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders (>5k/day). If the header is genuinely absent (not just dropped by the relay), this email is non-compliant with current inbox provider requirements and risks deliverability suppression.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**

The 22 tracking links were not probed so destination URLs cannot be confirmed. No UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source — cannot confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` tagging on CTA links. This should be validated against the full HTML before send.

The `<title>` tag reads `Crate & Barrel` rather than `Crate & Kids` — minor brand inconsistency, not a functional issue.

**7. Recommendations**

**P0 — Fix before send:**
1. **Web fonts 403:** Audit CDN access policy on `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/` — add `Content-Type: font/woff2` and permissive `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header, or rehost fonts on the email service's CDN.
2. **List-Unsubscribe headers:** Confirm with ESP (MessageIgnite / MI platform, per `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixel domains) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are being injected. If relay is stripping them, configure relay passthrough.

**P1 — Fix before next campaign:**
3. **Alt text on all images:** Add descriptive alt to product images; use `alt=""` (empty) for tracking pixels and decorative spacers so screen readers skip them cleanly.
4. **Probe the 22 tracking links:** Run a redirect-following HTTP check against click-wrap URLs to verify destination pages return 200 and are not 404/301-looping.
5. **Confirm UTM tagging:** Validate `utm_source=email&utm_medium=crate-kids&utm_campaign=<campaign_id>` (or equivalent) on all CTA links in the full HTML.

**P2 — Cleanup:**
6. Remove the unfilled `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` comment from production templates.
7. Consolidate duplicate `format-detection` meta tags to one line.
8. Reconcile the conflicting desktop `@media` breakpoints (640px vs 768px) for `img { max-width }`.
