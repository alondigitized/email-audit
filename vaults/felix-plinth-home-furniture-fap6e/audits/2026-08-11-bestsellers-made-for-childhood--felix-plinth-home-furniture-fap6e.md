# Bestsellers made for childhood →
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Another Crate & Kids email — I lost count around number six this week, but who's counting (me, apparently). "Bestsellers made for childhood" with a header banner shouting "back to school sale up to 25% off" — I don't have kids, I'm renovating two houses full of grown-up furniture, and none of this converts to anything I need. It's the beatrix collection, the jenny lind collection, bunk beds with sale stars carved into headboards — cute if you're furnishing a nursery, completely irrelevant if you're me.
- The one thing that made me pause for half a second was the "enter for a chance to win $500" banner near the bottom — free money always gets a flicker of attention — but then it's immediately back into kids' furniture and a $500 gift card only useful for buying more kids' furniture. Compare this to the "New! A beautiful dining room" email I got from Crate & Barrel proper today (5/10, but at least it was in my category) — that one at least respected that I'm an adult without children. This one's an easy archive, no click, not forwarding it to anyone unless I know a friend with a nursery to furnish.

## What's working

- The product photography is genuinely well-styled — the jenny lind daybed shot with the round rug and glider looks like a real room, not a catalog dump.
- Review stars are visible under a few items ("Absolutely love the excellent make" quote under the jenny lind collection), which is a trust cue even if the category isn't mine.
- The layout is clean and scannable — clear section breaks between collections, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- Zero targeting for a childless 36-year-old — bunk beds, cribs, nursery gliders, none of it applies to me or my two renovations.
- The "up to 25% off" sale banner up top gets buried by an unrelated hero bed image before I even see a real offer.
- The "$500 sweepstakes" and "new cardholder $500" offers are two different $500 mentions on the same email — reads muddled, not like one clean incentive.
- This is the sixth Crate & Kids-adjacent send this week per my own tracking — the fatigue is real and this brand keeps hitting the same beat with different SKUs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids sends entirely — I've never bought children's furniture and this is at least the sixth near-identical email this week. Send me Crate & Barrel or CB2 instead.
- 2. Lead with the actual 25% off number and a deadline, not a lifestyle bed shot — right now the sale banner and the hero image fight each other.
- 3. Consolidate the two $500 mentions (sweepstakes vs. card signup) into one clear CTA so it doesn't read like two separate half-offers.
- 4. If Crate & Kids must reach non-parent subscribers, pivot copy toward "gifting" (baby shower, grandkid) rather than assuming I'm a parent.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The bed everyone's asking about — 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beatrix, Jenny Lind & Bridgerton bunk — all on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: enter to win $500 toward your next room`
