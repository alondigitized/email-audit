# CRIBS ✨ UP TO 80% OFF ✨ FREE SHIPPING ✨ 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Thirteen. That's the Wayfair counter in my inbox as of this morning, the 25th, and I'm leading with it because it's the frame for everything. Yesterday I sat through kitchen mats. Before that, weight benches and outdoor privacy screens. Today: cribs. A full crib collection email, "picked just for you" in the hero, landing in the inbox of a person who does not have, has never had, and is not expecting a child. I am renovating a guest suite and a primary bedroom simultaneously. Neither room has an infant in it.
- The offer mechanics are legitimately sharp — 80% off during Black Friday in July is the loudest number Wayfair has run in this thirteen-email marathon, and the free shipping callout is the specific line item I always scan before choosing between Wayfair and a direct competitor like Article. But the category targeting is so wrong it poisons the well from the first pixel. There's a "Your Home, These Picks" block buried three scrolls below — rugs, what looks like a bed frame — content I'd actually act on. It's just trapped under changing tables.
- I'm marking this read. If a college friend just had a kid, I'm forwarding it — the 80% off is genuinely sharp for new parents on a budget. For my own two open renovation tabs? Nothing competes with what CB2 was running this week.

## What's working

- **80% off is the loudest number in this Black Friday in July stretch** — and it's a real offer, not a "select styles, while supplies last, excluding X Y Z" shell game.
- **Free shipping is prominently and repeatedly surfaced** — exactly right for heavy furniture where shipping is often the reason I choose Wayfair over a competitor.
- **"Your Home, These Picks" at the bottom** does have rugs and bedroom pieces that would actually pull me in — the personalization data exists somewhere in Wayfair's stack, it's just loaded into the wrong section.
- **Clean render throughout** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum. Thirteen emails in and they haven't shipped a broken one yet; that's worth noting.

## What's weak

- **"Picked just for you" above a crib carousel is actively insulting** — I have never bought, browsed, or lingered on nursery furniture in my Wayfair history. This is the personalization engine misfiring in a way that's hard to ignore.
- **ALL CAPS subject line with three ✨ clusters** — CB2's bedroom/bath sale email this week had none of that and outscored this on open likelihood. The spam-adjacent formatting is doing real damage.
- **No Wayfair Rewards callout anywhere visible** — I'm enrolled, I buy on the highest-cashback card I can find, and Wayfair has a loyalty program that should be the hook converting fence-sitters. It's absent.
- **Four separate discount tiers in one scroll** (80%, 65%, 60%, 50%, then 60%, 40%, 60%, 60% in the doorbuster block) creates decision paralysis — this feels like markdown on slow-moving inventory, not a curated event. Article's living room editorial from two weeks ago made choices; this email dumps the warehouse.
- **Cadence.** This is email thirteen. The Crate & Barrel coffee table guide was the email I actually bookmarked this week — partly because it was the only one in that stretch that felt deliberate.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the personalization engine before the next send** — if I've never clicked nursery, don't headline with it. Wayfair has my entire purchase and browse history. Lead with the bedroom and living room categories surfaced in the lower section, and bury the crib block for subscribers who actually have kids.
- 2. **Replace "picked just for you" with something honest** — "Your July clearance: rugs, beds, and case goods up to 80% off" is both accurate and actionable.
- 3. **Kill the ALL CAPS and reduce to one emoji in the subject** — Crate & Barrel doesn't emoji-blast to move furniture and they're outperforming this on open signals.
- 4. **Surface the Wayfair Rewards tie-in above the fold** — "This order counts toward your next $10 reward" is the email I click without scrolling.
- 5. **Collapse the doorbuster and brand-deal sections** — four distinct discount tiers in two adjacent blocks creates noise, not urgency. Pick one organizing principle per email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `80% off during Black Friday in July — free shipping included`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your July clearance: beds, rugs, and case goods up to 80% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedroom furniture, area rugs, and living room picks — your sale selections`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on everything, plus Black Friday in July pricing through the month`
