- The subject promises an ice cream maker. I couldn't spot one, so the hook and the content don't match.
- Roughly 80% of the email is Ninja small appliances: espresso, air fryers, slushies, blenders. I'm renovating, not outfitting a coffee bar.

- 2. Move "up to 30% off accent chairs & tables" out of the thin strip and into a real second module for people like me who shop the furniture categories.
- 3. Cut the email to three or four modules. The Ninja stack is repetitive.
- 4. Segment by browse behaviour. I've been on furniture and lighting, not kitchen electrics.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "The trending ice cream maker that sold out in preorders"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML Transitional with `lang="en"`, a mobile media query at 460px, and Outlook MSO conditionals. Automated QA gave a 50% pass rate with 0 errors and 4 warnings. The main findings are about 47 images without alt text, an unescaped `&` in the `<title>`, and header checks that could not be run through the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so only the head and the QA output could be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 81 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect/tracking links (the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pattern). Destination status, redirect chains and 404s could not be checked.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five identical LiveRamp pixels appear, `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. They carry a hashed subscriber identifier in the query string and are repeated five times with only `n` changing.
- **Other tracking images:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1946939b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images. The `/p/rp/` path suggests these are also tracking images, but I can't confirm that.
- **Identifier in image URLs:** The `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cc…` value appears across many image URLs, so the same subscriber identifier is exposed in all of them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 47 images with no alt attribute. Examples:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `…/m/1/7e06c5cf-e724-4b33-b6e5-8a3a597d6300.jpg`
  - Two `s7d5.scene7.com` Ninja REMIX images (Mocha and SlushiMax)

  About 7 of the 47 are clearly tracking pixels (5 `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv…`), which should carry `alt=""`. The remaining ~40 include the product and creative images. With images blocked, the email may render with little or no readable content.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicates:** There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`). `format-detection` is declared three times, and the values conflict (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no` separately).
- **Leftover template text:** The head contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->`. Both are harmless but should be removed.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 16,584 of 23,112 characters (about 72%) are URLs. The text alternative is mostly raw tracking links and is hard to read.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appeared in the truncated HTML. The tail of the document was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA did not find the header. QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find it, so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. This has the same caveat.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA expects this through the relay.
- **Body footer:** The footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) is outside the truncated source. I can't confirm or rule out a CAN-SPAM problem there.
- **Sender domain:** The From address is on `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and image hosts use `image.`/`mi.`/`dv.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent with a branded sending domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 81 CTAs go through redirect tracking domains, so the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters weren't visible and weren't probed. Nothing in the visible source shows a mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (about 40). Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv…`).
2. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Remove the duplicate charset and `format-detection` tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Consolidate the five repeated `rlcdn` pixel calls into one.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, bypassing the relay, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
7. Generate a cleaner plain-text part with readable text and fewer raw URLs.
8. Follow the redirect chains in a real browser or with an unrestricted probe to check landing pages and UTMs.
