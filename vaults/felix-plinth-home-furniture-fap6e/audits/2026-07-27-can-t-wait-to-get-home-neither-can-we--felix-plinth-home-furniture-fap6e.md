# Can’t wait to get home? Neither can we →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's a beast. I scrolled and scrolled and at some point checked if I'd accidentally opened three emails stitched together — living room, entryway, dining room, kids' furniture, then a whole Design Desk mini-magazine with Lupita Nyong'o's L.A. house, THEN a sweepstakes, THEN a product grid, THEN a credit card pitch. It's basically Crate & Barrel's entire summer catalog compressed into one scroll.
- The good news: the "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" banner up top is exactly the kind of concrete number I actually stop for, and the living room and entryway shots are legitimately the kind of rooms I'd screenshot and text to my wife about the second home. The bad news: by the time I hit "design kids spaces that grow with them" with a crib front and center, I'm reminded this is the same brand that's sent me three Crate & Kids blasts this cycle (per the "Bestsellers made for childhood" and Nuna sends) and I still don't have a kid. Article's living-room send four days after their dining-room one felt like a curated decision — this feels like C&B just mailed me their entire site map.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably on "Shop Entryway" specifically, because that console table is doing something for me. Would I forward it to a friend? No — it's too sprawling to forward as-is, though I might crop just the Lupita section and send that to my wife since she likes the Design Desk stuff. Mostly I'd open it on the couch, scroll past the kids and sweepstakes parts, and bookmark the entryway shot.

## What's working

- The "up to 60% off" banner at the very top gives me a real number before I even scroll — better than Wayfair's vague "FLASH DEALS" banner-with-no-percentage approach.
- The entryway shot (wood console, mirror, plant) is the exact aspirational-but-attainable styling that gets me to actually click "Shop Entryway" instead of scrolling past.
- TheDesignDesk section with a real celebrity home (Lupita Nyong'o) and "100% free design help" is a legit trust/differentiation play — Pottery Barn's quilt email had none of that kind of editorial anchor.
- Each room section has its own specific CTA ("Shop Living Room," "Shop Dining Room") instead of one generic button repeated five times.

## What's weak

- The kids' furniture section is dead weight for me — same fatigue I already flagged on the last two Crate & Kids sends this cycle, and it's now bleeding into the main C&B email too.
- This is genuinely too long. Article proved you can do one room per send and I'll actually engage; C&B crammed five rooms plus a sweepstakes plus a credit card pitch into one scroll, and by the credit card section I've mentally checked out.
- The sweepstakes ("enter for a chance to win $500!") feels bolted on — it's competing for attention with an actual 60%-off sale in the same email, which muddies which offer I'm supposed to care about.
- "Up to 60% off" never gets specific anywhere in what I can see — no callout of what's actually 60% off vs. 10% off, so it reads like the vague "up to" framing Wayfair and CB2 both lean on too.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two or three sends — one for the sale, one for the Design Desk/Lupita editorial content, and drop the kids' section from my segment entirely since I've shown zero engagement with Crate & Kids content three sends running.
- 2. Anchor the top banner with a real number tied to a real category ("Up to 60% off outdoor + entryway" beats generic "up to 60% off The Summer Sale") so I know if it applies to what I'm shopping.
- 3. Move the sweepstakes out of the sale email — it's diluting the actual discount message, put it in its own send or the footer only.
- 4. Lead with the entryway or living room section since those are my strongest engagement signals, not force me to scroll past the intro copy first.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off — the rooms you're mid-renovation on`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your entryway called. It wants this console.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Living room, entryway, dining room — plus free design help from TheDesignDesk.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `60% off select pieces, no kids' furniture in sight.`
