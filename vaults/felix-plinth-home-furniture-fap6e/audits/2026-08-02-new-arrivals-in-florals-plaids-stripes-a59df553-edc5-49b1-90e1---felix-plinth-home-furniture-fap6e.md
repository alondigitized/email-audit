# NEW ARRIVALS in florals, plaids & stripes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Print & Pattern" — this is actually one of the more coherent Pottery Barn sends I've gotten lately. Heritage florals, ticking stripe, that plaid throw pillow grid up top — it's a legit editorial moment, not another sale-blast dressed up as content. The blue toile bedding shot and that curtain/dining room vignette with the floral drapes are genuinely the kind of thing I screenshot for the "second home" folder.
- But there's zero offer anywhere in this email. No %, no dollar figure, nothing time-bound. And then right after all that curated pattern-story content, I hit "Recommended Just For You" — a farmhouse platform bed, a rug, a dining table — which feels like a completely different email got stapled to the bottom of this one. Then "The Halloween Shop" shows up again, same beat as the "Welcome to Holiday HQ" send I already flagged on 7/31 for jumping seasons too early. It's August 2nd.
- Would I click? Maybe, just to scroll the lookbook on desktop — the curtains genuinely caught my eye for the primary. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on — no offer means no urgency to act today.

## What's working

- The heritage floral/plaid/stripe framing is a real point of view, not just "new stuff, buy it" — feels more like Crate & Barrel's better content sends than PB's usual sale spam.
- Blue toile bedding shot with "instant visual interest" copy is genuinely well-styled and on-brand for a fall bedroom refresh.
- The floral curtain + dining vignette photo is the strongest single image in the email — that's a real room, not a product-on-white shot.
- No fake urgency or bait-and-switch banner up top, which is a win compared to the Wayfair headboard email that buried its own subject under an outdoor clearance banner.

## What's weak

- There is no offer. Not a %, not free shipping, nothing — compare that to "On sale & shipping free" from 8/1 which I gave an 8 because promise matched payoff. This one promises pattern inspiration and delivers exactly that, but gives me zero reason to buy today.
- "Recommended Just For You" personalization block feels bolted on — a bed, curtain rod, rug, chair, dining table, towels — none of it ties back to the floral/plaid/stripe story above it. Whiplash.
- The Halloween Shop banner again. Same complaint as the 7/31 "Holiday HQ" send — it's August, I'm mid-renovation on two actual houses, and seasonal-shop modules keep interrupting relevant content.
- No swatch/sample-ordering CTA anywhere, which is a miss for a fabric-pattern-themed email specifically — this is exactly the email where "order a swatch" would've made sense.
- Rewards card math is buried at the very bottom in small print ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards") — should've been closer to the top if they wanted me to act.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer in the hero — even 20% off the new bedding/pillow collection would turn this from "nice lookbook" into "reason to buy this week." Right now it's all inspiration, no incentive.
- 2. Add a "Order a Swatch" CTA next to the toile bedding and floral curtain shots — I don't buy $200 curtains sight-unseen, and this is the one email where fabric samples are on-theme.
- 3. Cut the "Recommended Just For You" grid or make it thematically consistent (patterned pieces only) — right now it reads like a mis-merged template.
- 4. Drop the Halloween Shop banner from this send. It doesn't belong in a florals/plaids/stripes email and it's the second time this week I've flagged PB for seasonal whiplash.
- 5. Move the rewards card math up near the CTA buttons — "Earn 10% Back" is a real incentive buried where nobody scrolls.

- Primary nav/header link is wrapped through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` (SFMC click-tracking redirect) — opaque, could not be resolved by automated probe (51 tracking/redirect links skipped per QA, expected for ExactTarget wrapped links).
- Multiple open/pixel trackers present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` (served over **http://**, not https), `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (Turn.com/Amobee data pixel), and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/b02761e4-fabf-4fbc-908f-ea109566020b?mi_mid=...` (open-tracking beacon).
- `spacer_0.gif` loads from `http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` — a West Elm (sister-brand) asset domain referenced inside a Pottery Barn email; served over plain HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **41 images missing `alt` text** per QA scan (product shots, banner slices, footer slices, tracking pixels) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback states will show nothing.
- **4 image sources use `http://`** instead of `https://` (`spacer_0.gif`, `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `slices_02.jpg`, the `svopn` open-tracking pixel) — likely to be blocked or flagged as mixed content by mail clients that enforce TLS.
- Malformed `<style>` block in `<head>`: raw PHP `header()` calls (`header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`) are embedded as literal text inside a CSS `<style>` tag rather than executed server-side. This is invalid CSS — most renderers will silently ignore it, but it indicates a broken template merge (server-side cache-busting code leaked into client markup) and should be removed.
- Table-based layout with MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) and Apple Mail overrides (`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]`) are correctly structured — no issues found there.
