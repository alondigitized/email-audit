- Twelve pergola photos across two grids is repetitive. It's the same near-identical dark gazebo with gray curtains, over and over.

- 2. Add an end time to the hero, like "Ends Thursday midnight." Put a real price on the product tiles.
- 3. Lead with a specific hero image of a room instead of a wall of percentages. Pottery Barn and CB2 sell a look. Wayfair sells a spreadsheet.
- 4. Add a delivery-date or "ships by Dec 10" cue for furniture, plus a rewards or financing line near the top.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch promo (`refid=MKTEML_143003`, `batchid=3`, `varid=8`). Every probed link was either rate-limited (429) or unchecked, so none was confirmed broken. The main confirmed problems are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and a campaign-label mismatch on the app link. The header findings are unverified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** All 8 probe warnings are 429 responses (e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…`, `…/area-rug-sale~e404488.html`). These come from rate limiting, not dead URLs.
- **Coverage is incomplete.** 12 links were probed and 49 skipped. 4 more were not checked because the 70s budget ran out: `indoor-seasonal-decor-sale~e404550`, and three pergola product URLs (`Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola…`, `Aurora-10%E2%80%98-X-12-Louvered-Pergola…`, `111S-Aluminum-Pergola…`). Their status is unknown.
- **The app deep link has an insecure fallback.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…&af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses `http://`, so non-app users go through an extra redirect hop. The same link appears at least twice with different `_eml` values.
- **There are 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels.** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`) all load over `http://`. Clients that block or rewrite mixed content may drop them, which would undercount ad impressions.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (`eventType=EmailOpen`) is flagged for no alt. Expected for a pixel, but it is an `<img>` with no `alt` attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the hero image.** `1105_HolidayFlashSale_SkinnyHero-VP.png` is wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales/`, and the probe recorded its link text as `[image: …png]`. With images off or a screen reader, that link has no accessible name or sale message.
- **Missing alt on review stars.** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` appear about 14 times and carry no alt, so the ratings can't be read to screen-reader users.
- **Missing alt on two banners.** `WFUS_1029_FallClearance-Skinny-BAU_Financing.png` and `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` have no alt, so any text baked into them is inaccessible.
- **Mobile CSS breaks product names mid-word.** `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; … height: 48px !important }` inside `@media (max-width:480px)` will split words at arbitrary characters.
- **Limited by truncation.** The HTML source was cut off, so I can't assess the body markup, `role="presentation"` usage, or preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source or the QA data. The per-recipient parameters (`_emr`, `_eml`) are populated with UUIDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may have stripped them. This is unverified, not a confirmed sender failure. Check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **No confirmed unsubscribe link or postal address.** The footer was cut off in the source I was given, and the QA report says an unsubscribe link was probed but gives no result. I can't confirm or deny either from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters: `refid=MKTEML_143003`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16687`, `csnid`, `source=batch`. This is a problem only if you expect GA-style UTM reporting.
- **Campaign labels conflict on the app link.** It carries `c=Triggered`, but the other links carry `source=batch&batchid=3`, which means a bulk send. The label may misclassify this campaign in AppsFlyer reporting.
- **Trailing-slash inconsistency.** The "Shop Sale" button goes to `/daily-sales` and the hero goes to `/daily-sales/`. Both likely resolve to the same page, but they could be counted as separate URLs.
- **Event links look aligned.** Each event link has a matching `eid` and `unifiedeventid` (e.g. `633648` for the area rug sale, `633677` for Christmas trees, `633653` for curtains).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as sender defects.
2. Re-probe the 4 unchecked pergola and seasonal-decor URLs, and retry the 429 links with backoff.
3. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change the app link's `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the hero and banner images, and meaningful alt (e.g. "4.5 stars") on the review-star images. Use `alt=""` on the purely decorative tracking pixels.
5. Replace `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name` with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` or `break-word`.
6. Confirm that `c=Triggered` on the app link is intentional for a batch send.
