# Biggest sale of the year! Open for up to 50% off Black Friday!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel Kids again, and I don't have kids. I'm renovating two houses. The "Kids BLACK FRIDAY" sender line told me what this was before I opened it. It's the same "Biggest sale of the year" send I already audited at 6/10 earlier today, and I've lost count of the C&B-family emails this week. The hero is a plaid-bordered ribbon with "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" over a pile of nod chairs, and it reads clearly.
- Almost everything below is nursery and playroom: baby bedding, toys, stockings, a crib, a kids' rug. The one thing that pulled me in was the Kiwi Striped Glider, "New Price! Sale $825 Reg. $1,099," and I'd only look at it if I were furnishing a nursery. It wouldn't go in my Pinterest folder. I'd mark this read and move on, and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable. "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" is in large type with a single "Shop Now" button right under it.
- The "Exclusions apply. Orders $79+" line sits directly under the headline. The threshold isn't buried in the footer, which I appreciate.
- The 2x4 product grid is clean. Each tile has one short caption like "up to 30% off toys," so I can scan it in seconds.
- The "Ships Free" tags on the bedding and rug tiles answer the shipping question on bulky items, and that's what I care about for furniture.
- The upgraded Crate Card block mentions Reward Dollars. As someone who buys on the highest-rewards card, I noticed it.

## What's weak

- Almost nothing is relevant to me. It's crib, toys, stockings and kids' bedding, and nothing for a living room, kitchen or primary suite.
- The sender line has "Kids" and "BLACK FRIDAY" crammed together. It reads like a subject line, and I already knew this was another sale blast.
- It repeats the earlier 11/27 sends. Same "biggest sale of the year" framing, same ribbon, same chairs. That's the fourth C&B-family send I've logged today.
- The bottom is a pile of competing CTAs: the card, the app, TheDesignDesk, the store locator, then a long legal block. The Learn More, Download Now and Book a Free Appointment buttons dilute the sale.
- The "It's all on the app, including your chance at $500" block is a sweepstakes teaser with no detail. I won't download an app for that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by household. Send the Kids version only to people who've bought kids or baby items. A renovator like me should get the main C&B or CB2 send.
- 2. Stop sending the same creative multiple times a day. Pottery Barn did the same thing with its plaid maroon block, and I'm tuning it out too.
- 3. Lead with one hero product and a price, like the glider at $825. Article's coral "SHOP NOW" with an "ENDS MONDAY" deadline gives a clearer urgency cue than this does.
- 4. Cut the bottom stack to one secondary CTA. TheDesignDesk is a good free service, but it's lost down there.
- 5. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at midnight."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run found 0 hard failures. The real defects are 18 content images with no alt attribute, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and leftover template artifacts. The header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) most likely come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and body links weren't visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Link health, redirect destinations, and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8&n=1` through `n=5`. This is the same pixel fired five times with an incrementing `n`. It is a LiveRamp domain, and `s=` is a 64-hex value that looks like a SHA-256 hash.
- **Open pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa2787a…` carries a hashed user identifier. The same hash appears in the `rlcdn` URLs.
- **Beacon:** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/96e0d6b9-a20b-4dd9-8904-96ff7b48dae1?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a second tracking beacon with no file extension.
- **Template leftover:** The source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, an unfilled template placeholder.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (content images):** 18 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` and `/m/47/` have no alt attribute. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `592f13e9-…jpg`, `2635bc5e-…jpg`, and `ac698ed7-…gif`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the content is unreadable. Because the images are probably the promotional content, this is the highest-impact finding.
- **Missing alt (pixels):** 7 tracking images also lack alt: 5 `rlcdn` pixels, `o.gif`, and the `dv.` beacon. These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** The CSS sets `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`. This conflicts with the `100%` setting declared elsewhere in the same stylesheet.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, so the later 640px rule wins and the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Source hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice, once as `http-equiv` and once as `charset`.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - `td,th,div,p,a { }` is an empty rule.
  - The `<title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens, and the `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are populated. The body past the truncation point wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. The QA tool says this may be a relay capture limit, so it is unconfirmed. If it is really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post:** The RFC 8058 one-click header was not found, with the same caveat.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be assessed. The relay stripped it, so this is unverified, not failed.
- **Hashed identifiers to third parties:** A hashed identifier (`s=56aaa278…`) is passed to `sr.rlcdn.com` in 5 requests. This is a privacy and consent consideration for the sender. I'm flagging it as a factual observation, not a violation.
- **Footer:** The physical address and the visible unsubscribe link couldn't be checked because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 66 links are redirect-wrapped and were not followed, so UTM parameters, final landing URLs, and whether the destination matches the "up to 50% off" offer are all unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 18 content images, and `alt=""` to the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Re-run the QA with redirect-following enabled for a sample of the 66 links. Check final URLs, status codes, and UTM params.
4. Capture the raw `.eml` headers directly, not through the relay, to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Fetch the untruncated HTML to check the footer, with its unsubscribe link and physical address.
6. Clean up the template artifacts: the `IMPUT HERE` comment, the duplicate meta tags, the empty CSS rule, and the unescaped `&` in `<title>`.
7. Collapse the five identical `rlcdn` pixel calls into one, or confirm that five fires per open is intentional.
