# The edit: Molly Sims’ favorites
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Molly Sims — that's the sixteenth-ish Pottery Barn email in my inbox this week, and at least this one has a different premise than "buy this lamp." It's an influencer edit: beauty founder/podcast host/mom of three curates her favorites, styled bookshelf, striped chair, the whole cozy-editorial vibe. I get why they're doing it — it's a break from the relentless lighting and pillow drumbeat (yesterday was lamps, day before was pillows) — but I'm not the audience for "mom of three's beauty picks" framing, even if the actual furniture (swivel chair, extendable dining table, platform bed) is squarely in my wheelhouse.
- The "Recommended Just for You" block below is honestly more useful to me than the Molly Sims section — curtain rod hardware, a swivel chair, a dining table, a platform bed, sheets, duvet cover. That's real reno-relevant inventory. But then it immediately pivots into a Design Crew sweepstakes for a $1500 gift card (the same beat as yesterday's "win $1500" email) and a Halloween Shop pitch, so the email can't decide if it's an edit, a sweepstakes, or a seasonal push.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. I'd scroll past the celebrity framing straight to the recommended grid, and even that doesn't have enough urgency or a specific price to make me act today.

## What's working

- The styled shelf/chair vignette photography is genuinely nice — better composition than most of C&B's recent product-grid sends.
- "Recommended Just for You" surfaces categories I actually care about right now: dining table, platform bed, curtain hardware — that's targeting, even if buried mid-email.
- The 10% Rewards callout at the bottom is relevant since I use a high-rewards card for this stuff — that's the one line that speaks directly to how I actually buy.
- No render bugs — layout is clean, images loaded, text is legible.

## What's weak

- Molly Sims as the hero has zero signal that matches me — a 36-year-old guy renovating two houses doesn't care whose "beauty founder" favorites these are, I care about SKU, price, and shipping window.
- Zero prices anywhere in the entire email. Not on the Molly Sims picks, not on the "Recommended Just for You" grid. Wayfair's Open Box email at least led with real discounted numbers — this is just imagery with no purchase information.
- Design Crew sweepstakes is the same $1500 gift-card mechanic as yesterday's dedicated sweepstakes email — repeating the offer two days running reads as filler, not a real promo.
- Halloween Shop tacked on at the bottom feels like a random append — no connection to "Molly Sims' Favorites" or the recommended furniture, just another shelf item crammed in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices on the "Recommended Just for You" grid — that's the section with actual purchase intent for someone like me, and it's the one place in the email with zero dollar figures.
- 2. Drop the celebrity-edit framing for a segment like mine, or replace it with a "how a renovator styled this room" angle — Article's "Home Tour" format with An Trieu at least ties the influencer to a concrete room problem (storage) rather than generic "favorites."
- 3. Cut the Design Crew sweepstakes repeat — running the same $1500 mechanic two sends in a row burns credibility; save it for a standalone cadence, not a bolt-on.
- 4. Lead with the dining table or platform bed as the hero if this is meant for a renovation-stage buyer — those are my actual open items, not curated lifestyle picks.
