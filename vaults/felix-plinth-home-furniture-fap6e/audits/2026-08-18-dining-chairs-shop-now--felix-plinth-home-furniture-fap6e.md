# DINING CHAIRS | Shop Now >>
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- So the subject says "DINING CHAIRS | Shop Now >>" and I open it expecting, I don't know, an actual dining chair spread — swatches, wood tones, something I can picture next to the table I'm still shopping for at the second house. Instead I get the same "48-HR CLEARANCE 70% OFF" grab-bag Wayfair always sends: a recliner, storage jars, a gazebo, outdoor privacy screens, bar stools, a chaise lounge. There's exactly one photo that has dining chairs in it — buried third row down, and it's actually a whole dining set, not a chairs callout.
- This is the same bait-and-switch move Wayfair pulled on me yesterday with "Are these the SLIPCOVERS you wanted?" (3/10, no real slipcover section) and the day before with the ottoman email that had zero ottomans. I'm starting to think their subject lines are just a random category generator that has nothing to do with the email body. Compare that to Crate & Barrel's "Small-space dwellers, this furniture's for you" (8/10) — when they say apartment furniture, the whole email is apartment furniture, hero to footer.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding. Marking read and half-tempted to unsubscribe at this point — three strikes on subject/content mismatch in one week is a pattern, not a fluke.

## What's working

- The "48-HR / 70% OFF" banner up top is at least a clear, legible offer — no ambiguity on the discount itself.
- Wayfair Rewards "5% on every item, always" section is a nice trust cue since I do have a Wayfair card in rotation.
- Star ratings under every product give a little social proof at a glance.
- The dining set photo (round table, four chairs) that IS in there is genuinely a decent shot — warm wood tone, would've worked as a hero if they'd built around it.

## What's weak

- Subject promises dining chairs, email delivers a random clearance grid — jars, gazebos, recliners, privacy screens, a chaise. This is the third straight Wayfair send this week with this exact mismatch.
- No dedicated dining chair section, no swatch/finish options, no "shop the collection" CTA anywhere near the one dining photo that exists.
- Visual hierarchy is a wall of near-identical purple-banner-plus-grid sections — my eye doesn't land anywhere specific, it just scrolls.
- Generic CTAs like "Take a look →" and "Top-Rated Recliners »" — nothing that says a verb + a specific dining product.
- There's a blank gray gap near the bottom of the email (right before the Wayfair Rewards icon row) that looks like a broken/missing image block.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the body — if this is a general clearance email, subject it as clearance, not dining chairs. If you want a dining chairs email, actually build one: 6-8 chair styles, price, one CTA.
- 2. Fix the blank image gap near the footer — looks broken on render.
- 3. Give the one dining photo you have an actual hero treatment with a specific CTA ("Shop the Round Dining Set") instead of burying it in a generic grid.
- 4. Cut down the section count — five purple-banner grids in one email is fatigue, not merchandising.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Wayfair dining chairs promo built on the Bee/Unbounce-style responsive email framework; core links resolve correctly where probed, but tracking-pixel delivery uses insecure HTTP and image accessibility is broadly non-compliant.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 64 links were probed (52 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); of those probed, 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by target server, not necessarily broken) — includes the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Get Exclusive Deals on the App," and the "Vannie Upholstered Solid Wood Side Dining Chair" product link.
- 4 links were left unprobed due to a 70s time budget, including both `wayfair-product-L443-K~W113370810.html` and `Jax-Glass-Highball-14-oz-L1235-K~RXK10071.html` occurrences (2 instances each) — status unconfirmed, neither pass nor fail.
- 20 tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently degrade open/impression tracking.
- Consistent query-param structure (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_138962`, `maiid=15996`, etc.) across all sampled links indicates functioning tracking-token injection at the platform level.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 22+ `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, spanning hero creative (`48HR-Clearance_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`), SKU header art, star-rating icons (`stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`, repeated 10+ times), the loyalty disclaimer image, and all 20 HTTP tracking pixels.
- One asset filename contains a literal `%` character (`0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`) — unencoded `%` in a URL path is invalid per RFC 3986 and may fail to load in strict clients.
- Responsive framework (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, etc.) and Outlook-specific fixes (`data-outlook-cycle`, `u-OutlookAppStackingFix`) are present, indicating standard cross-client scaffolding — no structural rendering defects observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe): not found — same caveat.

1. Convert the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels to HTTPS to prevent mixed-content blocking and tracking loss in strict mail clients.
2. Add `alt` attributes to all content and decorative images (use `alt=""` for pixels/decorative stars, descriptive `alt` for hero/product images) for accessibility and text-only rendering fallback.
3. Fix the unencoded `%` in the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` filename/URL.
