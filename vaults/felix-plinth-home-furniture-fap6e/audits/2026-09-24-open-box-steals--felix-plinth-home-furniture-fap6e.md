# Open Box steals
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "open box deals" — that's actually a phrase that gets my attention, because I'm renovating two houses right now and open-box furniture is legit money I'll take. But then I scroll and it's just... the entire Wayfair category directory dumped into purple pill buttons. Cabinet Pulls, Bar Stools, Wall Art, Desks — twenty-plus links with no actual open-box product shown anywhere. There's a Dutch oven in the hero graphic, which is the only concrete "steal" I see in the whole email, and it's decorative, not linked to anything.
- This is the fourth Wayfair email in my recent memory dump (bins & baskets, changing table pads, farmhouse decor) and at least this one didn't have broken images — small win after two sends in a row with blank hero space. But "open box" implies specific discounted individual items with maybe a scuff or a returned unit, and instead I get a generic sitewide sale wall. I'm not opening a single one of these links, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — there's nothing here worth sharing with a friend, it's just Wayfair's whole catalog with percentage signs slapped on.

## What's working

- The Le Creuset-style Dutch oven in the hero is a nice touch — it actually signals "used/returned item, real savings" even if it's just decoration.
- No broken images this time, unlike the farmhouse decor and bins-and-baskets sends — that's a real fix from recent history.
- Category list is at least organized alphabetically-ish and scannable if I already know what room I'm shopping for.

## What's weak

- Zero actual open-box products shown — no photos, no "was $400, now $180," no condition notes. The whole premise of the email is a bait-and-switch into a generic sale page.
- Twenty-plus purple buttons in a row is visual fatigue, not merchandising — my eye has nowhere to land after the hero.
- No mention of Wayfair Rewards points or my account status anywhere in the offer itself, just generic footer icons for "Wayfair Rewards" and "Pay Over Time" with no numbers attached.
- This feels like the same firehose approach as "Bins & Baskets from $20" and "Changing Table Pads under $30" — different subject, same button-wall body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show me 4-6 actual open-box items with real before/after pricing and a photo of the actual (possibly imperfect) unit — that's the entire appeal of "open box," and it's completely missing.
- 2. Cut the category list to my likely renovation categories (furniture, lighting, rugs) instead of everything from cabinet pulls to bathroom vanities — Article's "organized entryway" email worked for me specifically because it stayed narrow and relevant.
- 3. Put a real expiration or "while supplies last" note near the top — open-box inventory is inherently limited, and that urgency isn't being used at all.
- 4. Tie in Wayfair Rewards points earned on open-box purchases specifically, since that's the kind of math I actually check before buying.
