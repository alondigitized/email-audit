# More added to Labor Day Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Another CB2, and honestly at this point I've stopped being annoyed and started being weirdly impressed by the volume. This is at least their twentieth send this month per my count, and "EVEN MORE / UP TO 50% OFF" over a beach-sky hero is barely a variation on the "LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" one I got yesterday. The category grid underneath is genuinely useful, though — Furniture, Outdoor, Entertaining, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs, all with real product photography — and that stone coffee table and the green outdoor sofa actually made me pause for half a second since we're mid-renovation on both houses right now.
- The card-rewards block ("DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON... GET 10% 20% BACK") is where it falls apart for me — that's a visible strikethrough/overlap glitch on the percentage, and it made me trust the email less, not more. I'm not opening a CB2 store card when my Chase Sapphire is already stacking better cash-back on furniture spend across every brand I shop.
- I wouldn't click, and I definitely wouldn't forward this one — my wife already thinks I'm being dramatic about how many CB2 emails hit my phone a day. Mark as read, move on.

## What's working

- The category grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Entertaining, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs) is genuinely more useful than a single hero shot — it's basically a mini-catalog I can scan in five seconds.
- Real product photography, no lorem-ipsum, no obvious broken images in the main grid.
- Two "SHOP THE SALE" CTAs bookend the grid so I don't have to scroll back up.
- The rugs and lighting shots (that lamp base, the woven throw) are exactly my aesthetic lane right now — better fit than the Wayfair "cushions selling out" email I read that buried actual furniture under three rows of pergolas and gazebos.

## What's weak

- "10% 20% BACK" — that's a rendered overlap/strikethrough glitch on the one number that's supposed to be the hook of the whole rewards section. That's a real bug, not me nitpicking.
- Zero new information versus yesterday's "More added to Labor Day Sale" and "The sale you can't miss" — same 50% off ceiling, same beach hero palette, no new products flagged as actually new.
- No swatch/sample program mention anywhere, no shipping-timeline callout for furniture — CB2's furniture ships on trucks with real lead times and this email pretends that doesn't exist, same gap Article at least partially closes with clearer freight language.
- The CB2 credit card pitch feels like a bait-and-switch dressed as a "reward" — pushing a co-brand card instead of just applying the discount at checkout.
- Cadence is genuinely broken at this point — twenty-ish sends this month makes every individual email worth less, the same problem I flagged with Crate & Kids sending four times in one day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the reward-percentage render bug first — "10% 20% BACK" undermines the one CTA in that section that's supposed to close a card signup.
- 2. Cut send frequency. Twenty CB2 emails in a month trains me to skim past the subject line entirely — consolidate into fewer, higher-signal sends (e.g., once when a new SKU actually drops in the sale, not every 18 hours).
- 3. Add a shipping/lead-time line near the furniture category tiles — for a $2k sofa, "arrives in 6-8 weeks" is more persuasive than another discount tier.
- 4. Differentiate this send from yesterday's — if nothing new is actually added to the sale, don't title it "More added."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The green outdoor sofa is back in the Labor Day sale`
