# Sale on sale! Extra 20% off + Open Box Outlet Deals—selling fast!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and my first thought was "I know, 20% off." This is the fifth send since 10/22 with the same maroon "Extra 20% off Select Styles / Plus savings up to 70% off" strip on top. Earlier today the "Take an EXTRA 20% OFF" email led with it too. I didn't need a fifth copy.
- The Open Box Outlet block is the new part, and it's a decent hook for someone renovating two houses. The trouble is that it's just a headline and a button. There's no product, no price and no sense of what "returned items" means. I'm saving the wine-glass kind of email for later, but I can't tell if this one has a $400 sofa or a lamp with a dented shade. Below it, "Recommended just for you" looks like my taste: dining table, curtain rod, pendant, linen curtain, swivel chair, rug. But there are no prices, so I'd have to click through for anything.
- Would I click? Maybe the Banks dining table or the Remmy chair, since that's exactly what I'd pin. I wouldn't forward it. I'd file it in the Pinterest folder and move on.

## What's working

- The "Open Box Outlet Deals" headline has a clear red-outlined frame and a "SHOP EXTRA 20% OFF" button. The eye lands on it right after the banner.
- The "Free Design Services" block with fabric swatches and "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" fits how I shop. Swatches and design help matter when you're doing two houses.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid feels like it knows me. The dining table, pendant and swivel chair are all things I'd actually put on a list.
- The rewards card block mentions up to 10% back across Pottery Barn, Williams Sonoma, West Elm and Rejuvenation. That's the rewards math I care about, since I pay with the highest-rewards card.
- The layout is clean, with readable type and big buttons that are easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- The maroon "Extra 20% off" strip is the same one I've seen on 10/22 and again today. It's wallpaper at this point.
- The Open Box section has no product images and no prices, so "unbelievable prices" is just a claim.
- Two of the three CTAs are sale buttons ("SHOP ALL SALE" and "SHOP EXTRA 20% OFF"), and they say nearly the same thing, so the email feels repetitive.
- The recommended grid has no prices, ratings or availability. For furniture I want lead time and shipping info, and none of it is there.
- The Christmas Shop banner is a different message again. The email tries to do sale, outlet, design services, recommendations and holiday all at once.
- The fine print is a wall of dense text at the bottom, and "Exclusions apply" shows up twice near the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with real open-box products: three or four items with the original price struck through and the open-box price. That's what makes the hook believable. Add a "limited quantity" line that's specific to the items.
- 2. Drop the repeated 20% strip, or make it one thin line. I've seen it four times already.
- 3. Put prices and a lead-time or "ships in X days" note under the recommended items.
- 4. Merge "Shop all sale" and "Shop extra 20% off" into one clear CTA.
- 5. Give design services a stronger hook, such as "Free swatches shipped with your appointment."

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Pottery Barn "Sale on sale! Extra 20% off + Open Box Outlet Deals"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with widespread missing alt text, three plain-`http://` image sources (one a tracking pixel that puts the recipient's email address in a cleartext URL), and stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block. Header-level compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The source was also truncated, so the footer and most body links couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** The QA run skipped 43 links because they are tracking/click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...`). It never probed their destinations, so redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Cleartext tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over HTTP. Clients may block it, which skews open tracking, and it exposes the recipient address in an unencrypted request.
- **Email address in pixel URLs.** The recipient address is also in the query string of `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`. These URLs can appear in proxy and server logs.
- **Other tracking pixels.** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` and `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/68b0a8e4-...?mi_cid=...` are third-party and first-party pixels with no alt text. The `turn.com` pixel is a third-party ad-network beacon.
- **Mixed-protocol assets.** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` use HTTP, although the same host serves HTTPS elsewhere in the email.
- **Cross-brand asset host.** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer. This is a minor dependency risk if that path changes.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 35 images.** Affected images include:
  - the hero and banner images (`H26_TOP_Extra20CLR.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`);
  - every product image (`banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, and others);
  - all 18 footer slices (`.../Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`).

  With images blocked, the banners, product images and footer navigation all render empty. Some of the footer slices are likely links, which makes them unlabelled controls for screen readers. The tracking pixels and spacer GIFs should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` with no `lang`.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`.** For example, the spacer table `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td></tr></table>` has no role, although other tables do.
- **Stray PHP in the stylesheet.** The second `<style>` block ends with `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. This is server-side code leaking into the CSS. It is probably ignored by parsers, but some clients may mishandle the block.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** The preheader text reads `Shop outside the box & save BIG`, and the bare `&` is invalid XHTML.
- **Likely stale image paths.** `2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner` and `2025/US/F25_Sale_B2_OpenBoxDeals` are reused in this send. These aren't defects themselves, but they raise the risk of broken assets.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%name%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the visible source. The recipient email is populated correctly in the pixel URLs. The truncated source means the body copy wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both are absent from the captured headers. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it should be checked against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the same relay limitation. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is a sending subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address.** These are not visible in the truncated source and are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are opaque ESP redirects (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=<encoded>`), and the destination URLs and campaign parameters aren't exposed. They weren't probed either, since the redirect domains were skipped.
- **Product images.** These come from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, but their link destinations weren't visible.
- **Cross-brand assets.** The email contains no additional continuity evidence beyond the West Elm-hosted asset above.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, banner, product and footer images, and `alt=""` to the spacers and tracking pixels. This is the highest-volume issue.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS. Remove the email address from the pixel and `coreemail.html` query strings, or replace it with an opaque ID.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Delete the PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
5. Add `lang="en"`, a populated `<title>`, `role="presentation"` on all layout tables, and `&amp;` in the preheader.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw `.eml`, and don't rely on the relay-captured copy.
7. Resolve the redirect destinations of the 43 skipped links, then check UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
8. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
