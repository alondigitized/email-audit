# Up to 60% off clearance + new items added!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a step up from the last two Crate & Kids emails I flagged — no "Hi friend," no bulleted text wall. It's a real product grid with a floral hero, a clear "up to 60% off + new markdowns" headline, and a Labor Day sale block that's actually organized by category (kids bedding, nursery furniture, rugs, decor). That's the layout I want from this brand.
- But my nursery's already furnished — I'm not shopping cribs and swaddles, I'm doing dressers, lighting, and rugs for the primary and the lake house. So half this grid (kids bedding, nursery chairs, baby bedding) is just noise I'm scrolling past to get to the furniture tile. And I've now caught this brand five times this week between the two houses — the Jenny Lind push at the bottom is the exact same "our #1 furniture collection" beat I saw earlier today, just recontextualized into a bigger clearance email.
- Would I click? Maybe — the dresser tile and the "up to 30% off study & storage furniture" line are actually relevant to my renovation. Would I forward it? No. This is a "skim, maybe click furniture, archive" email, not a share-with-a-friend email.

## What's working

- The "up to 60% off + new markdowns" hero is a real, specific offer — no vague "shop now" language.
- Category subnav (Furniture / Rugs / Kids Bedding / Baby Bedding / Decor & Lighting / Final Sale) lets me skip straight to furniture instead of scrolling the whole grid.
- Product tiles show actual prices and specific discount tiers per category (50% kids furniture, 30% study/storage) instead of one blanket number — that's more honest than Wayfair's vague "CHINA CABINETS" clearance banner that didn't even show a china cabinet.
- Double Rewards callout ("earn 10% back in Reward Dollars") is a real loyalty hook, and I'm a Crate & Barrel cardholder so that lands.
- Jenny Lind section has a star rating visible — actual social proof, which most of these furniture emails skip entirely.

## What's weak

- I've seen "our #1 furniture collection: meet jenny lind" from this brand today already — same asset, same section, just bolted onto a different subject line this time.
- The grid is still majority baby/kids product (bedding, nursery chairs, backpacks, strollers) — I don't have an infant, so a big chunk of this email isn't for me even though I'm clearly a furniture/decor shopper based on what I've clicked before.
- No swatch or fabric-sample CTA anywhere, which is the one thing that would actually move me on furniture — I'm not buying an upholstered chair sight-unseen off a thumbnail.
- The "TheDesignDesk" free design-pro CTA is buried at the very bottom under the app-download pitch — that's actually a stronger offer for someone mid-renovation than half this email, and it's getting the least real estate.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead furniture and decor higher for known non-baby shoppers — if this brand knows I've only ever clicked dressers and lighting, put the furniture/decor grid before the kids bedding tiles, not mixed in.
- 2. Pull TheDesignDesk (free design consultation) up near the top — that's a genuinely differentiated offer for a renovation shopper like me, and it's currently dead last after an app download pitch.
- 3. Add a "shop by swatch/sample" CTA next to the furniture tiles — this is the single missing piece for converting furniture browsers into buyers.
- 4. Stop re-running the Jenny Lind hero block verbatim across sends this week — rotate the creative even if the collection stays the same, so it doesn't read as the same email twice.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dressers, rugs, and lighting just got marked down for Labor Day.`
