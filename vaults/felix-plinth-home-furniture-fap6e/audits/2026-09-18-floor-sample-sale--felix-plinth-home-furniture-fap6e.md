## What's working

- "Floor Sample Sale" + "limited time" is a real urgency hook that doesn't feel manufactured — floor samples are genuinely scarce.
- The hero styling shot (console, lamp, brass accents) is on-brand and photographs like something I'd actually put in my renovated living room.
- Clean top nav (New Arrivals / Furniture / Decor / Rugs / Lighting) — no clutter, easy to scan on mobile.
- The rewards/app/text-signup module at the bottom is well-organized into a clear 2x2 grid, easy to tap.

## What's weak

- Zero specifics on the floor sample sale itself — no discount %, no store list, no dates beyond "limited time." I can't judge if it's worth a drive.
- Two competing offers in one email (Floor Sample Sale vs. Best Sellers 40% off) dilutes which one I'm supposed to act on.
- "FIND A STORE" as the only CTA for the hero offer is friction — I want the sale details before I commit to finding a location.
- No mention of showroom-specific pricing, sample condition (scratches, floor wear), or whether furniture is final-sale — matters a lot when I'm buying secondhand-adjacent goods for a full house reno.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual discount or price range on floor samples right in the hero — "Floor Sample Sale: Up to 50% Off One-of-a-Kind Pieces" beats a vague mood shot every time.
- 2. Cut it down to one primary offer per email — either lead floor samples or best sellers, not both stacked as competing heroes.
- 3. Add a store locator with real inventory hints ("23 pieces at your nearest CB2") so this feels personalized, not generic.
- 4. Disclose sample condition/final-sale policy up front — I'm renovating two houses and need to know if this is returnable.
