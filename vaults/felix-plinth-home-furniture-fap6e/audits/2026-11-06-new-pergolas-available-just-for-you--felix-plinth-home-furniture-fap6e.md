# 📩 New pergolas available just for you 📩
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and this is the third pergola send in six days. I got "New Pergolas for YOU" on 11/01, "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" on 11/02, "Pergolas for less" on 11/05, and now this. The images loaded this time, so it's better than the blank lavender boxes on 11/01 and 11/02. The top is eight pergola photos in a grid under "inspo for your space." It looks good, but nothing in it tells me what anything costs.
- Then the email loses the plot. Under the pergolas there's a dresser collage with a purple tile that says "you?" and a "MEET YOUR MATCH" link. I can't tell what that's for. After that comes "but wait, there's more…" with kitchen mats, Christmas trees, sinks, quilts, advent calendars and bath mats. That's a catalog dump. The only real offer is the "UP TO 70% OFF, HOLIDAY FLASH SALE ENDS TOMORROW" banner near the bottom, and the same banner was in my 11/03 send.
- I wouldn't click. I'm renovating two houses, and a pergola isn't on my list. If it were, I'd go to the product page and find the price myself. I wouldn't forward it. I'd archive it, same as the last two.

## What's working

- The eight pergola tiles with star ratings are clean. Wayfair gets credit for showing social proof on the hero grid.
- The images actually rendered, with no blank boxes like on 11/01 and 11/02.
- The "Ends Tomorrow" flash sale banner is the one clear urgency cue, and "up to 70% off" is a real number.
- The "On sale now: pergolas »" and "Pergolas and gazebos from $50 »" tiles do tie back to the subject line.
- The footer has Rewards, Financing and Wayfair Professional. As someone doing two renovations, I notice those.

## What's weak

- The subject says "just for you." The email is the same pergola grid from earlier this week with a random furniture mix attached. Nothing here is personal.
- None of the hero tiles has a price or a discount. Pottery Barn's "Pillows ship free" at least gives me one concrete hook.
- The dresser collage with "you?" and "MEET YOUR MATCH" is confusing. I don't know what I'm matching to.
- The "but wait, there's more…" section runs about 16 tiles across kitchen mats, Christmas trees, sinks and bath mats. It reads as a scroll-a-thon with no hierarchy.
- The 70% banner sits at the bottom, about 80% of the way down. I'd never see it on my phone without a long scroll.
- Tile captions are tiny. I can't read them at a glance without zooming.
- There's no shipping info for furniture, no swatch or sample program, and no room-pairing guidance. That's what I'd actually want from a big-ticket outdoor purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Up to 70% off, ends tomorrow" banner to the top, above the grid. The offer is the hook, and right now it's buried.
- 2. Put a price on every pergola tile, e.g. "from $499". Show one hero, not eight.
- 3. Cut the dresser collage and the "but wait" dump. Keep one adjacent category, such as outdoor seating.
- 4. Add one line on delivery for large items, like "Ships free, arrives by Nov 20."
- 5. Don't send a third pergola email in six days. Rotate categories, or segment out people who didn't open the first two.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Pergolas from $499: free delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Louvered, steel and cedar pergolas, rated 4+ stars and priced to move.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Flash sale ends tomorrow. Plus free shipping on large items.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with an `@media (max-width: 480px)` responsive layer. The QA run found 0 hard failures, but the automated evidence leaves link validity and unsubscribe compliance unverified. The confirmed code-level issues are widespread missing `alt` text and 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, preheader and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity is unconfirmed, not broken.** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=2` and both product URLs for `8088-Metal-Frame-Pergola-with-Adjustable-Louvered-Roof-L7053-K~id-BT29012672.html`. 4 more pergola PDP links went unprobed after the 70s budget, and 58 links were skipped. Coverage is thin: 12 of 70 links were probed.
- **Insecure fallback in the app link.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, which is plain HTTP. It depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **20 HTTP pixels.** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (2x1, `p=d93bdfdd-...`) are all non-HTTPS and have no alt text. Clients that block or proxy non-HTTPS images will drop them, so impression counts will be under-reported.
- **Open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS but has no alt. The open pixel is separate from the 20 impression pixels.
- **Recipient ID in every URL.** `_emr=d93bdfdd-6dc4-4292-acae-9a5fd7a435bd` is in every link and matches `p=` in the impression pixels. A forwarded email would expose it.
- **Asset naming.** The `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` filename suggests a test asset reached production. That header image links to the bare homepage `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=6`, not a product or category.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png` is a linked image, so the link `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=5` has no accessible name.
  - `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` is also a linked image, with the same problem.
  - `1105_HolidayFlashSale_SkinnyHero-EndsTomorrow.png` has no alt, and its filename suggests it carries "Ends Tomorrow" text.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png` and `wfus_jt800_minibahn_singleclass_dressers_style_lay...` have no alt.
- **Star ratings.** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (several times) and `stars_11.png` have no alt, so the rating is inaccessible. One `stars_10.png` is wrapped in a link to the pergola PDP (`...sltid=6&mplid=VVE10006`), leaving that link nameless.
- **Possible mismatch to check.** The `...dressers_style_lay...` filename appears in a pergola-themed email. This can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Layout.** The visible CSS (`u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }`, 480px breakpoint, `.u-MobileHide`) shows standard Gmail and mobile handling. No rendering defects are confirmed in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens appear in the visible source or QA output, though the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, and the cause is the relay, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer not verifiable.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the findings. The physical postal address and unsubscribe link could not be checked in the truncated source.
- **Sender.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, and the tracker `t.wayfair.com` is first-party. Both are consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121671`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16681` and `mdlid`. Third-party analytics will classify this traffic as direct or unattributed unless it is mapped server-side.
- **Conflicting campaign labels.** The app link has `c=Triggered&pid=Email`, but `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15` marks a batch send. This will mislabel the campaign in AppsFlyer.
- **Landing alignment.** The subject promises pergolas, but the non-product CTAs go to generic pages. "Shop Sale" and the hero banner point to `/daily-sales`, and "Wayfair" and the header image point to `/`. Only the product links go to pergola PDPs, and no pergola category link is visible.
- **Unique `_eml` per link.** Even duplicate CTAs such as "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" get different `_eml` values, so link-level reporting is possible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image. Give linked images such as the banners and star ratings meaningful alt, for example "Rated 5 out of 5 stars". Use `alt=""` on the open and impression pixels.
2. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, or consolidate them. Change `af_web_dp` in the onelink URL to `https://`.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers from the sending ESP, not the relay. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
4. Add standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` parameters, or confirm server-side mapping. Reconcile `c=Triggered` with the batch send.
5. Replace the `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` asset and point its link at a relevant destination. Link a pergola category page for pergola-themed sends.
6. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or a longer budget. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer and unsubscribe markup.
