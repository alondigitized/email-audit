# TIME’S RUNNING OUT
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- CB2 again. This is the thirteenth send in about a week, and "CB2 SALE" in the sender slot at least told me what I'd get. This is the one that was supposed to be the "last chance." The hero says "ENDS TOMORROW" over a rug photo with "FALL DESIGN SALE, up to 50% off." The rug is the only thing I really see, and I couldn't tell you what product it is.
- It stacks three offers: 50% off, 30% off on the category buttons, and 25% off upholstery. It then adds a $1,000 app sweepstakes, 10% back in Rewards Dollars, and 15% off for a text signup. That's a lot of numbers for an email whose whole pitch is urgency. The green sectional is the same upholstery event I got on 10/3 and 10/4, so the "time's running out" framing feels manufactured. If I'm in the middle of the renovation I'd skim it, but I wouldn't forward it.
- I might click Furniture, but only because I'm already shopping for a sofa. Nothing in the email makes me do it today.

## What's working

- "ENDS TOMORROW" sits right on the hero, so the deadline is visible immediately. That's more honest than the earlier sends that only implied urgency.
- The rug hero is moody and well shot. It looks like CB2 and doesn't read as generic discount clutter.
- The four category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Pillows, Decor & Mirrors) are big white blocks. They'd be easy to tap on my phone.
- The upholstery section has a clear offer and one button, "SHOP THE SALE." The sofa photo gives me a mood and a palette to react to.
- The Rewards callout ("Earn 10% back") is the one thing that speaks to how I buy. I put everything on the highest-rewards card, so I notice it.

## What's weak

- **Too many offers:** 50%, 30%, 25%, $1,000, 10% back and 15% off. By the bottom I don't know which number applies to me. Wayfair's "FREE shipping over $35" email had a similar clarity problem, but at least that was one promise.
- **The hero doesn't match the buttons.** The headline says up to 50% off, but the button block underneath says "SHOP UP TO 30% OFF." That reads like bait-and-switch, and the footnotes confirm it's "select" items only.
- **The same upholstery creative is back.** I've now seen the sectional hero on 10/3, 10/4 and today. Pottery Barn's holiday catalog drop at least gave me something new.
- **The app sweepstakes banner is cut off.** The phone image bleeds off the right edge, so the $1,000 section looks a bit broken.
- **The fine print is tiny and endless.** I can't read it without zooming, and it holds the real exclusions. Free shipping and delivery for furniture aren't mentioned anywhere.
- **There's no product, price or swatch.** Nothing tells me what the rug is or lets me order a fabric sample.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. If it's the last day, make the hero the single sale with one price anchor and one button. Move the app, texts and gift list into a short footer strip.
- 2. Make the hero and the buttons agree. Either say "up to 30% off" in the buttons or show 50% items.
- 3. Show real products with sale prices, like the green sectional with its before and after price, instead of just a mood photo.
- 4. Add furniture-specific reassurance: delivery windows, a free swatch or sample link, and how the Rewards 10% back works on a big order.
- 5. Stop sending near-identical upholstery creative three days in a row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: up to 50% off, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sofa deal ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture up to 30% off, upholstery 25% off, plus 10% back in Rewards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free swatches on every sofa in the sale. Ends October 6.`
