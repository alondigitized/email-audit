# Don't miss Early Black Friday + UP TO 70% OFF to make the season bright
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and it's the same maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner I've now seen on 11/18, 11/19 ("Save BIG on holiday decor") and earlier today in "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS that do it all ✔️". The only new thing is the "Recommended Just For You" block and the Design Crew Sweeps strip, which also ran in the "RSVP for your FREE design appointment" send this morning. I could have told you the creative before I opened it.
- The category grid (Furniture, Sofas & Chairs, Bedding, Rugs and so on) is clean and I could shop from it. But the "just for you" picks are stockings, a faux fur throw, a curtain rod and a mattress. That's generic holiday filler. Nothing reflects that I'm renovating two houses and keep a Pinterest-style folder of saved emails.
- Would I click? Maybe the Toscana dining table, if I wasn't already numb. Forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for Crate & Barrel's "up to 50% off furniture" send, which was furniture-led and actually concrete.

## What's working

- The "up to 70% off" hero is huge and unambiguous, with a clear SHOP NOW button. The eye lands there first.
- The category tiles are tappable and big enough for a thumb. Furniture and Sofas & Chairs sit at the top, which is where I shop.
- The Toscana Extendable Dining Table and the Westin Heavenly mattress are real furniture pieces. That's closer to my renovation than the stockings.
- The Pottery Barn Credit Card block shows "10% back in rewards", which feeds my rewards-math brain.

## What's weak

- It's the same banner for the fifth time in five days. The cadence is exhausting, and Wayfair's eighteen-ish sends since 11/09 are the only thing that makes it look restrained.
- "Up to 70% off" doesn't say which items. The Christmas Shop says "up to 50% off", so the two offers are muddled.
- There's no shipping or delivery-timing info for furniture, no swatch or sample program, and no showroom or color-palette content. CB2's "Things of Quality" editorial feels more like a renovator's inspiration board.
- The sweepstakes fine print and legal block run very long, and the Design Crew prize reads as a gimmick. It's the same strip as this morning's send.
- The product picks have no prices or sale markers, so I can't tell what 70% off actually means.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one furniture hero, like Crate & Barrel did, with a real price, such as "Sofas from $X, free delivery by Dec 15". Replace the repeated banner.
- 2. Make "Recommended" behavior-driven: dining, rugs, lighting and swatch-ready fabrics for renovators. Add sale prices.
- 3. Add a furniture shipping-date and free-swatch line near the top.
- 4. Dedupe against this morning's Design Crew send. Cap the sale banner to one send per day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas, tables & rugs: up to 70% off through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your furniture picks, now up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free swatches + delivery dates for sofas and dining, plus 10% back with the PB card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dining tables, rugs and lighting: see sale prices before they sell through.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an XHTML 1.0 table-based layout with sliced image banners. QA passed 57% of checks, with 0 errors and 3 warnings, but the HTML has widespread accessibility gaps (about 47 images flagged for missing alt), 3 non-HTTPS image sources, and the recipient address written into image/pixel URLs. The source was truncated and the compliance headers were not captured, so footer and header checks are inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not verified:** 58 links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, for example `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABp7Im...` on the logo. Destination, status and redirect chain are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` loads over plain HTTP. It can be blocked and exposes the open event and recipient address in cleartext.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` is an ad-network (Turn) pixel with no alt attribute.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a spacer served from the West Elm domain. It is a dependency on a sister-brand host and has no alt.
- **Stale asset paths:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` are legacy 2022-era assets still served over HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 47 images):**
  - Hero banner `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2026/US/H26_Sale_EBF_BannerOver6Grid/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_13.jpg`.
  - Six product images from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/.../`, for example `faux-fur-ruched-throw-l.jpg` and `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-125-l.jpg`.
  - Three bottom banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop_50off.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01.jpg` and `_02.jpg`.
  - Footer slices `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`, plus `3ceb0c96-...jpg`.
  - The sliced banners are likely the main carrier of offer content, so image-blocked or screen-reader users get no offer information.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt=""`:** `coreemail.html?email=...`, the `d.turn.com` pixel, the `svopn` pixel and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/40a8426a-...` should use `alt=""` with explicit 1x1 dimensions. They are flagged only because the attribute is absent.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is present in `<head>`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure.
- **Invalid CSS:** the last `<style>` block contains stray PHP text: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store, ...`. It is invalid CSS and appears to be a template-authoring leak.
- **Layout tables without roles:** the outer layout tables and the spacer `<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">&nbsp;</td>` lack `role="presentation"`. Other tables have it.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` for Montserrat is dropped by many clients, so a fallback stack should be confirmed. This is low severity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens were found in the visible source or QA output.
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient address appears unencoded in query strings: `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. These appear in image `src` attributes, so the address is sent to the server in request logs and proxy logs, and over HTTP for the `svopn` pixel.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing from the captured headers. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Do not treat it as a sender defect without the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the tracking domains `click.e.potterybarn.com` and `image.e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they cannot be confirmed. The footer is image-sliced (`Email-Footer/slices_01-18.jpg`, no alt), so if the legal text is baked into images, it is unreadable with images off.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page URLs cannot be verified. All 58 CTAs are opaque `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` redirects that were not resolved.
- The product images link to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` assets directly, which is expected for images and not an issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the banner and footer slices (priority: `H26_Sale_EBF_BannerOver6Grid/slices_01-13` and `Email-Footer/slices_01-18`). Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Move the three HTTP sources to HTTPS: `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel.
3. Stop passing the plaintext email in image and pixel query strings, or use a hashed or opaque identifier.
4. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block and fix the template.
5. Add `lang="en"` and a populated `<title>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables. Replace the West Elm-hosted `spacer_0.gif` with a same-brand asset.
7. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.potterybarn.com` links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
