## What's weak

- The subject says "less than 24 hours" and the banner says "ends tonight." Same send, two different clocks. That erodes my trust.
- Only one tile is string lights. The rest is a generic grid, so the subject oversold the email.
- "Picked just for you" is just popular stuff: a wine fridge, a patio set, a pool table. Nothing here knows I'm doing a renovation.

- The pergola and wall-art tiles at the bottom are the same ones from the 10/25 pergola email. Nothing is new here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with consistent per-link tracking parameters. The main code-level defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 36 images) and about 20 `http://` tracking pixels. Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because of relay capture limits.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage was partial.**
  - 12 links were probed and 69 skipped.
  - 8 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...` and the 3 product links. Rate limiting is not a defect, but these links are unverified.
  - 4 links were not probed because of the 70s time budget (e.g. `.../Metal-72-x-47-Privacy-Metal-Screen-Fence-Panel-L7552-K~D`, `.../wall-art-%26-wallpaper-clearance~e403402.htm`).
  - Neither probed nor unprobed links should be counted as pass or fail.
- **Mixed protocols in click URLs.**
  - The hero GIF link uses `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, while the "Shop Sale" link uses `https://`.
  - The app deep-link fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
  - Both `http://` URLs depend on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Onelink parameters conflict.** `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...&c=Triggered&...&source=batch&batchid=1` labels a batch send with the campaign name "Triggered". This looks like a mislabel that would skew AppsFlyer attribution. It is inferred from the parameters, not confirmed.
- **Duplicate CTA.** "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" appears twice with different `_eml` values (`efd1f95d-...` and `163af80d-...`), which is consistent with per-placement tracking.
- **Tracking pixels.**
  - `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (the open pixel) has no `alt`.
  - 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=86693ccc-...` impression pixels use plaintext HTTP.
