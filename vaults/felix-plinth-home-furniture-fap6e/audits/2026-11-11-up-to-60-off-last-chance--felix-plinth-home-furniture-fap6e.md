# 🚩 UP TO 60% OFF 🚩 LAST CHANCE 🚩 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and this is the fourth day running of the same 72-hour clearout. On 11/09 it was "72 hours only," on 11/10 "Less than TWO DAYS LEFT," and today it's "ENDS TONIGHT" on the same red banner. The countdown is honest, but I've seen this banner so many times that I read it as wallpaper. That's the problem with a clearout that never seems to end.
- This one is better than the pergola and rug sends because the "picked just for you" grid is actual furniture and appliances. There's a banquette dining set, a wine fridge, bar stools and a built-in-looking bookcase. Those fit a guy renovating two houses. But then it slides into the usual pergola and gazebo block at the bottom, which is the fifth pergola module I've scrolled past this week. I get it, Wayfair, I have a patio.
- Would I click? Maybe the wine fridge or the bar stools, but I'd probably screenshot them into my Pinterest-adjacent folder and price-check them against CB2 and Pottery Barn later. I wouldn't forward it. It's a long, undifferentiated catalog dump with no reason to act today beyond "ends tonight."

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" on the hero is a clear, credible deadline. It's a real improvement over the repeated "72-HR" language.
- The top grid shows was/now pricing with star ratings under each item, so I can see the discount math without clicking.
- The wine fridge, bar stools and banquette set are relevant to renovating a kitchen and a second home.
- The red category bars (area rugs up to 60% off, bedding, curtains, seasonal decor) are big, tappable, and easy to hit on a phone.
- The "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" purple band gives a second hit of urgency and breaks up the scroll.

## What's weak

- The "ENDS TONIGHT" hero is a thin strip, and the "Shop Now" button inside it is tiny. Pottery Barn's "Up to 70% off across the home" hero is much bolder and easier to act on.
- Two deadlines, "72-HR CLEAROUT ENDS TONIGHT" and "24 HRS ONLY flash deals," compete with each other. Which one is real?
- The flash-deals grid is random. Holiday string lights sit next to a TV stand and a fireplace insert, so nothing feels curated for me.
- The bottom half is pergolas, gazebos, rugs and kitchen mats again. It repeats the 11/10 pergola send and the 11/11 rug email.
- Nothing helps me compare or decide. There's no swatch or sample program, no delivery windows for big furniture, and no sign that Wayfair Rewards earns me anything. Crate & Barrel at least pushes "Earn 2x Rewards," and I buy on my highest-rewards card.
- The email is very long, with product tiles but no copy. Several tiles have no price at all, like the bar stools and the area-rug grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the top 60% of the email into a single clear hero and one lead category. Show the sale and the "ends tonight" deadline once, at a size I can read.
- 2. Stop repeating the pergola and rug modules across consecutive days. Rotate by what I've browsed, such as bar stools or wine fridges, and drop the categories I've already ignored.
- 3. Add a furniture-delivery line, something like "In-stock furniture delivers by [date]," to match what Pottery Barn does.
- 4. Add a rewards tie-in, like "Earn X on this sale," so my card math has something to work with.
- 5. Put a price on every tile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 60% off furniture + wine fridges`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: bar stools, dining sets & more up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Picked for your kitchen refresh: banquette sets, bar stools, wine fridges.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The 72-hour clearout closes at midnight. Here's what's still in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair promotional send with first-party tracking and resolved per-recipient tokens. The confirmed defects are about 20 tracking pixels served over plain `http://`, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and no `lang` attribute on `<html>`. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 links were probed and 69 skipped. Seven probed links returned 429 (rate limiting), which is not a failure. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale") and the product links for SKUs RDBT2633, NPUS1022 and FNRE1521.
- **4 links were never checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out. They include `.../Assembled-UniversalReversible-Bookcase-Hidden-Door-with-...` and `.../daily-sales/area-rug-clearout~e417428.html?eid=640626...`. Their status is unknown.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 beacons load over HTTP, `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=390ba739-...`. Clients that block mixed or insecure content can drop them. Impression counts for those placements would then be under-reported.
- **Campaign label conflict on the app link:** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...&c=Triggered&...&source=batch&batchid=3`. The `c=Triggered` value contradicts `source=batch`, so app-install attribution for this batch send may land in the wrong AppsFlyer campaign bucket.
- **The `[image: stars_10.png]` links** are product URLs wrapped around the rating image. The accessible link name is therefore empty or just the filename.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text** is confirmed on:
  - Content images: `72HR_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`, `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-FlashDeals.jpg`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`. These are linked banners, so the link has no accessible name.
  - 12 `stars_10.png` review-star images. They carry the rating and have no text equivalent.
  - The open-tracking pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="...">`. Screen readers will fall back to their default language. The HTML was truncated, so I could not check `<title>` or `role="presentation"` on the layout tables.
- **Mid-word breaking on mobile:** `@media (max-width:480px) { .ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; height: 48px !important; overflow: hidden } }`. Product names can break mid-word and be clipped at a fixed height.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. `_emr=390ba739-...` (recipient) and `_eml=...` (per-link) are resolved UUIDs. No unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `*|...|*` appear in the visible source or probed URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Authentication-Results** was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `editor@members.wayfair.com` is unknown.
- **Footer and physical address:** the visible HTML was truncated and I could not confirm them. The QA run says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result is reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters** appear on any link. Attribution uses proprietary parameters instead: `refid=MKTEML_143119`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `emlid=101` and `maiid=16705`. This works for Wayfair's own analytics but won't populate standard GA source/medium reports.
- **`sm=1`, `brcid=1`, `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid`** are the same across all links, which is consistent.
- **Product deep links** carry `mplid` and `sku` that match, for example `mplid=RDBT2633` with `sku=RDBT2633`.
- **Stale-looking creative filenames:** the banner `72HR_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png` sits in a send whose other banner is `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2...`. The asset name suggests a Spring 2026 creative. I can't confirm from the source whether the content matches the "LAST CHANCE" framing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to them and to `t.wayfair.com/b.php`.
2. Add meaningful `alt` to the banners (for example the offer text) and to the star images (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars"), or give the star images `alt=""` and put the rating in adjacent text.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Fix the `c=Triggered` value on the OneLink URL to the batch campaign name.
5. Drop `word-break: break-all` from `.ProductRec-name`, or use `overflow-wrap: anywhere` with a line-clamp.
6. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` to verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results. Also re-probe the 4 unchecked links and the 429'd links with throttling.
7. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` mapped from `refid` if GA attribution matters.
