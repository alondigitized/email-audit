# Are these the SLIPCOVERS you wanted?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- So the subject asked if these were "the slipcovers I wanted," and yeah, sure enough — top-left tile, first thing in the grid, "Best-Selling Slipcovers." That's a for-once honest subject line out of Wayfair, and I'll give credit where it's due. But then it's immediately buried under "BIG-TIME BARGAINS" hero copy and a pergola tile sitting right next to it, and from there it's the exact same kitchen-sink scroll I've come to expect from this brand — rugs, lighting, wreaths, dinnerware storage, hot tubs, sheds. This is basically the "Open Box Steals" send from 8/7 wearing a different banner.
- My gut reaction: mild recognition ("oh yeah, I did look at slipcovers"), immediately drowned by generic clearout noise. I'm not clicking the slipcover tile with any urgency — there's no price, no % off, nothing that tells me this is actually a deal versus just "here's a category you looked at once." I'd scroll past this in about four seconds and mark it read. Not forwarding it — there's nothing here worth sending a friend, unlike, say, if they'd actually shown me styled slipcovers with a real discount.

## What's working

- The subject-to-hero match is real — slipcovers are actually the first tile, which is more restraint than the 8/9 corner-bookcase email that buried its own subject line four items deep.
- "SHOP. SAVE. REPEAT." section with star ratings and "X Recently Purchased" counts is a decent trust signal — social proof I don't usually get from Pottery Barn's warehouse-sale blasts.
- Layout is clean edge-to-edge, no overlapping text or broken images that I can see.

## What's weak

- The slipcover tile has zero price or discount — just "Best-Selling Slipcovers »." Compare that to CB2's 8/8 "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" send, which at least paired hero items with actual percentages.
- It's the same shotgun-blast structure as every other Wayfair email this month — pergolas, hot tubs, sheds, dinnerware storage, wall art — none of that is "renovating two houses and needs a slipcover" relevant.
- Two purple CTA pills ("Closeout Deals," "Open Box Deals") that go nowhere specific — just funnel back into another generic sale page, same move as the 8/7 "Open Box Steals" email I already dinged for this.
- "Family of Brands" footer with AllModern/Birch Lane/Joss & Main/Perigold logos feels like filler — I'm not going to click a logo strip.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number on the slipcover tile — "up to 40% off slipcovers" beats "Best-Selling Slipcovers »" every time. That's the single biggest miss since the subject already got my attention.
- 2. Cut the email in half. I don't need pendant lights, ranges, wall art, and sheds in the same send — segment by what I've actually browsed (furniture/renovation stuff) like the subject line promised.
- 3. Move "Best-Selling Slipcovers" up into its own hero block instead of sharing a two-tile row with pergolas — right now my eye reads them as equally important, which they're not to me.
- 4. Add a rewards-math callout near the slipcover tile since I shop the highest-rewards card — even "Rewards members: extra 10% back" would pull me in.
