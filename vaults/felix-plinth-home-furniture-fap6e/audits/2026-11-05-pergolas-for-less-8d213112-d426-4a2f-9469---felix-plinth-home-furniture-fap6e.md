## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Structurally sound, table-based, responsive email with no confirmed broken links. Problems found: about 20 non-HTTPS, alt-less tracking pixels, linked banner images with no accessible name, and no `lang` attribute on `<html>`. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because of relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 links were probed. All 8 reported warnings were HTTP 429 rate limits on wayfair.com and onelink.me, which is bot throttling and not a dead link. For example, `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-for-less--se82165.html?eid=603460&...` returned 429.
- **4 links were never checked** because of the 70s probe budget. They are the kitchen-mats, vintage-rugs, small-appliances and wall-shelves URLs. They are neither passing nor failing.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** There are 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=8d213112-…` impression pixels. They use plain `http://`, so they may be blocked or rewritten as mixed content. Impression counts could be undercounted for that reason.
- **Insecure app fallback.** The onelink.me link carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. The web fallback is `http://`, so it adds a redirect hop.
- **Attribution mismatch.** The app link has `c=Triggered`, but every other link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labelled as a triggered campaign will skew AppsFlyer campaign reporting.
- **Per-recipient link IDs.** `_eml` is unique per link position. For example, `…_eml=b456a570-…` on "Shop Sale" differs from `…_eml=d9544e50-…` on the logo. That is expected and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png` appears twice.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png`
  - `small_appliances_01_gif.gif`
  - `wfus_jt800_batch10_d&f_wallshelves_smallspaces_lay…`
- **Linked images with no accessible name.** Three of these images are the sole content of links. The QA probe reports their link text as `[image: blankBanner…]` and `[image: wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purpl…]`. The links go to `/daily-sales` and `/`. Screen readers and images-off clients get no label for them.
- **Pixel alt attributes.** The 20 `imp` pixels and the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open pixel also lack alt. That is low impact if they are hidden or 1px, but `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Missing `lang`.** The visible source has `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang` attribute.
- **Gaps in the check.** The HTML was truncated, so body structure, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and the viewport meta could not be checked. The `<style>` block shown contains a 480px mobile breakpoint and a Gmail/Outlook-app fix (`u + .Gmail`, `body[data-outlook-cycle]`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) were evident in the supplied source or QA output. Recipient IDs (`_emr`, `p=`) are resolved UUIDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are absent.** The QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation, so it is unconfirmed. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address.** An unsubscribe link was included in the probe, but no result was shown. The truncated source also hides the footer, so the physical address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTMs.** No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters appear on any link. Wayfair uses a proprietary scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121677`, `emlid=101`, `maiid` and `mdlid`. This is consistent across links. Third-party analytics will lump the traffic under direct or email unless it is mapped.
- **Landing alignment.** The "Pergolas for less" link goes to `/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-for-less--se82165.html?eid=603460`. Both pergola links resolve to `daily-sales/filters/` event pages (`eid=603460` and `eid=569893`). Nothing indicates a mismatch, but 429s prevented destination content from being verified.
- **Non-event links.** The header and logo links go to the homepage (`/?_emr=…`), and "Shop Sale" goes to `/daily-sales`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all impression pixels over `https://` (`li.wayfair.com/imp`) and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to the linked banners and product images. Use `alt=""` for the tracking pixels.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Correct `c=Triggered` on the onelink.me app link so attribution matches the batch campaign.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from the raw message, not the relay copy.
6. Re-probe the 4 unchecked links with throttling or retry-after handling. Treat the 429s as inconclusive, not as passes.
7. Consider mirroring standard `utm_*` parameters for downstream analytics tools.
