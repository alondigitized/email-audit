# What to buy: back-to-school bundles
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids, fourth or fifth time this week, and this one's even more of a swing-and-a-miss than the last two. Backpack bundles, then it pivots into a whole "registry faves" section with a Nuna car seat and a nursery glider, name-dropping some celebrity couple's baby registry. I don't have kids. I don't have a kindergartner starting school. This is a household running two home renovations, not a nursery.
- The one thing that stopped my thumb for half a second was the "new! the card you love just got better" Crate Card teaser near the bottom — I do have a Crate & Barrel card on my rewards rotation for the primary house furniture spend, so that's the only line in this whole email that's actually mine. Everything else — backpacks, lunch boxes, "create a focus zone for every age," a literal baby registry — is not.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, straight to archive. If anything I'd forward it to my sister who actually has a toddler, not because it's good marketing but because it's finally relevant to somebody.

## What's working

- The 4-in-1 backpack bundle diagram (backpack, lunch box, water bottle, pencil case labeled on one flat-lay) is clean, clear visual merchandising — I get exactly what's in the bundle at a glance.
- "Ships free" is stamped on every single product tile, so at least the fulfillment terms are honest and repeated, unlike Wayfair's ottoman bait-and-switch I got burned by earlier this week.
- The Crate Card rewards teaser at the bottom is the one relevant hook for me as an existing cardholder — earning Reward Dollars ties back to my actual spend on the main brand.

## What's weak

- I don't have kids — this is the fourth-plus Crate & Kids send this week and it's back-to-school gear, a nursery glider, and a celebrity baby registry, none of which applies.
- The "nicole brydon bloom & justin theroux's registry faves" section is a hard stop — celebrity baby registry content has zero relevance to a childless 36-year-old renovating two houses.
- "create a focus zone for every age" teases a study-nook/desk setup that could've been an actual crossover product (desks, storage, lighting apply to home offices too) but it's framed entirely as kid-room content, so I scroll past it.
- Compare to CB2's midcentury sectional email or even Pottery Barn's towel send this week — those at least stayed in adult-furniture territory. This is the wrong sub-brand entirely for my inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of Crate & Kids entirely — I've now gotten five of these in a week with zero kid-relevant purchase history. This is a suppression/targeting fix, not a creative fix.
- 2. If Crate & Barrel insists on cross-selling Crate & Kids to non-parents, lead with the crossover content (the "focus zone" desks/storage) reframed as home-office, not kid-room, and drop the registry section for non-parents entirely.
- 3. Move the Crate Card rewards teaser higher — that's the only asset in this email actually built for an existing multi-category cardholder like me.
