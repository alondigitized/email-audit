# New to sale & UP TO 60% OFF + New ways to bring print & pattern home
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Eleven Pottery Barn emails this week and this one's actually the first to lead with a design *story* instead of a discount banner — "PRINT & PATTERN," a striped wingback chair, a pile of floral and paisley pillows. That's a real point of view, and it's the kind of thing that would've caught my eye if my inbox wasn't already a Pottery Barn crime scene. But the "Warehouse Sale — Up to 60% off" bar sits right above it like a tic they can't drop, and by now I've seen that exact banner in nine other subject lines this week alone.
- The middle of this email is genuinely well-composed — blue toile bedding, a matching dinnerware spread, then a room shot tying the drapery pattern back to the pillows up top. That's the "collected" narrative Pottery Barn's actually good at when they're not just doing warehouse-sale spam. Then it face-plants into a generic "Recommended Just For You" product grid that could've been auto-generated for any customer, followed by a Halloween shop pitch in the same email as toile bedding and print pillows — total tonal whiplash.
- I'm not clicking. I might glance at the drapery room shot for two seconds because it's honestly a nice photo, but nothing here is specific enough — no % off called out on an actual product, no "these fabrics restock fast" urgency — to get me off my thumb. Not forwarding it either; my partner's already seen six of these this week from me complaining, not sharing.

## What's working

- The "PRINT & PATTERN" hero with the striped wingback chair is a real editorial angle, not just another sale banner — first fresh creative concept from PB in days.
- The toile bedding → dinnerware → drapery sequence tells one coherent "collected pattern" story, which is more than Crate & Barrel's beach-hero-then-random-grid send managed on 8/8.
- "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" section near the bottom at least surfaces the credit card loyalty math I actually care about — CB2's "STEALS" send never once mentioned rewards.
- Product photography is consistently clean and well-lit throughout, no render bugs I can spot.

## What's weak

- "Up to 60% off" is stapled onto the top banner of literally every PB email I've gotten this week — it's wallpaper at this point, not an offer.
- Zero specific price or percentage tied to any actual product in the whole email — Wayfair's slipcover send at least put its offer on one identifiable item.
- The "Recommended Just For You" grid feels bolted on and generic, like it was templated in rather than curated to match the print-and-pattern story above it.
- Halloween shop CTA at the bottom completely undercuts the toile-and-heritage-floral mood the rest of the email built — pick a season and commit.
- This is the eleventh near-identical PB email this week; my brain has fully stopped registering the subject lines as distinct offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the generic "Recommended Just For You" grid entirely, or actually tailor it to the print/pattern theme (more toile, more floral, not a random rug and curtain rod).
- 2. Put a real number on one hero product — "20% off this bedding set" beats "up to 60% off" sitewide banner fatigue every time.
- 3. Drop the Halloween shop teaser from this email; it belongs in its own send, not spliced into a floral/toile story.
- 4. Slow the cadence — eleven emails in a week means even a good creative concept like this one gets buried and ignored.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) template with functioning link/tracking infrastructure, but missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and widespread missing alt attributes on content and tracking images.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 53 tracking/click-redirect links (click.e.potterybarn.com, clickcp.potterybarn.com) were skipped from HTTP probing per QA policy — not independently verified as functional, but this is expected/standard for SFMC click-tracking wrappers.
- Two open-tracking pixels present: `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...` and `https://d.turn.com/r/dd/id/L21rdC84NjEvY2lkLzE3NDc3ODk3MzMvdC8y/cat/1` — both function as expected 1x1 open trackers, no issue.
- The `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel is served over `http://`, not `https://` — flagged below under Rendering.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `spacer_0.gif` (http://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif) served over HTTP — mixed content, may be blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading.
- `Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` (http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg) served over HTTP — same mixed-content risk, located in footer credit-card banner include.
- `slices_02.jpg` (http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg) served over HTTP, and references a 2022-dated asset path in a 2026 send — stale/legacy include.
- `svopn.potterybarn.com` open-tracking pixel served over HTTP (see above).
- 40+ images across the message (banners, product shots, footer slices, tracking pixels) are missing `alt` attributes, including all customer-facing content images (F26_TOP_WarehouseSale.jpg, slices_01–08.jpg, product images for reeva-handwoven-rug, farmhouse-platform-bed, belgian-flax-linen-duvet-cover, etc.). Screen readers and image-blocked clients will render no fallback text for any of these.
- Header contains an invalid embedded `<style>` block with raw PHP-style `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg');` and cache-control header() calls — these are not valid CSS and will be ignored by mail clients, but indicate a copy-paste artifact from server-side code into the template's CSS block.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Personalization is implemented via query-string email embedding rather than AMPscript/Handlebars merge tags visible in the truncated source: `email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears in the customer-service link and both open-tracking pixels (`sv_sveme=`).
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[[...]]`) observed in the visible source.
- The recipient identifier `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is a routed alias, not a raw personalization failure — confirm this is intentional for the receiving environment rather than a leaked internal address format.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (QA WARN). Note this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing/preserving the header rather than the sender omitting it; unsubscribe link presence in the HTML body itself was not confirmed in the truncated source provided.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058) not detected — same caveat as above; if genuinely absent, Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe) would not be met.
- Authentication-Results header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data; same relay-visibility caveat applies.
- Recommend re-checking headers against the raw SMTP transaction (not just the relay-forwarded copy) before concluding these are actually missing at the source.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot confirm UTM parameter presence/consistency — all outbound content links route through `click.e.potterybarn.com` redirect wrappers, which were skipped by the HTTP prober (tracking-domain exclusion), so final landing-page URLs and query parameters are not visible in the provided data.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (banners, product shots); leave `alt=""` only for the two open-tracking pixels and spacer gif, which are purely functional.
- Migrate the three HTTP-only image sources (`edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/.../slices_02.jpg`, and the `svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel) to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
- Retire the 2022-dated `CC_Banner` footer include in favor of the current-year asset path already used elsewhere in the template.
- Remove the orphaned PHP `header()` calls sitting inside the `<style>` block in `<head>` — dead code with no effect but worth cleaning from the template source.
- Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results directly against the originating SMTP headers (not the AgentMail-relayed copy) before treating these as confirmed compliance gaps.
- Resolve final destination URLs behind the `click.e.potterybarn.com` wrappers to confirm UTM parameters are present and land on pages matching the promotion (sale, print & pattern).
