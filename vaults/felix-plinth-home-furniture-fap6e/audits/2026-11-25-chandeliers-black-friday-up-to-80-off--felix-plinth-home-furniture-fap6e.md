## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with responsive media queries. Its main confirmed defects are about 20 plain-`http://` tracking pixels with no alt text, and missing alt text on every content image. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of how the relay captures headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 links were probed. The 7 that came back with 429 are rate-limiting by wayfair.com and onelink.me, not dead links. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…` ("Shop Sale") and the product links with `mplid=JYLA1013`, `HBL10130` and `SRNZ1023`.
- **Unprobed links.** 4 links weren't checked because the 70s time budget ran out. They are neither passed nor failed, and include `…/Tramontina-Gourmet-Aluminum-Stock-Pot-with-Lid-L781-K~id…` and `…/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html?eid=635985…`. 65 links were skipped as low priority.
- **Non-HTTPS links.**
  - The top banner link uses `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…`, which depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
  - The app deep-link fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels.** There are 20 impression pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Mail clients that block mixed content, or proxy-rewrite images, may drop these, which would undercount impressions.
- **Open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (eventType=EmailOpen) has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed in source):**
