## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a conventional XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with MSO fallbacks. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 31 images) and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. Header-based compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, because AgentMail may have stripped those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not probed:** 63 links went through tracking or click-redirect domains, so the QA probe skipped them. Destination validity, redirect chains and HTTP status are unverified.
- **Repeated third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` appears five times, differing only in `n`. This is a third-party (LiveRamp) pixel firing five times per open. Each hit carries what appears to be a hashed recipient identifier (`s=`).
- **Identifier in image URLs:** the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` parameter is on every `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` image, and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` are on `dv.crateandbarrel.com`. Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs are normal for this setup, but they make these URLs unique to each recipient.
- **Link-level defects:** none confirmed. The source was truncated, so I could not inspect the `href` values.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 31 flagged images).** The QA check does not say whether the attribute is absent or just empty.
  - **Content images (likely the product and hero imagery):** 17 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/...`, for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `5422078f-….gif` and `d6d762e4-….jpg`. Also `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/SP25_CB_UphBestsellers_leather_01`.
  - **Recommendation-block images:** 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - **Tracking pixels:** 5 `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/71a91ac9-…`. These are not content, so they should carry `alt=""`.
  - **Effect:** with images blocked or read by a screen reader, the "4 most-loved sofas" content has no text equivalent.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-size adjustment disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in the `*{}` rule and in `html`/`body` stops iOS text resizing, though it is overridden to `100%` on `body`.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared three times, once as the combined string and twice individually, and the combined string repeats the individual ones. Harmless but untidy.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a placeholder left in the production source. It has no rendering impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source, and the subject line renders cleanly. The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** the QA check says AgentMail may not capture it, so this is **unconfirmed** as a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, but that does not prove authentication.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are outside the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 63 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated HTML exposes no final landing URLs or UTM parameters. Nothing here confirms or rules out continuity problems.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, such as sofa name and key attribute. Use `alt=""` on the five `rlcdn.com` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixel.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the duplicate `format-detection` tags and the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Review whether five identical `rlcdn.com` pixel calls are needed. Reduce them to one if the repeats are redundant.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be checked. Also resolve the tracked links to verify landing pages and UTM parameters.
