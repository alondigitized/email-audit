- This is the best-organized CB2 email I've had all week. It's the same semi-annual dining event that was a thin strip on 10/19's "BACK IN STOCK." Now it's the headline: "One week left, up to 30% off" over a dining table with velvet chairs. That table is the right room for me. I'm furnishing two houses, and a dining set is on both lists.

Both offers are live, and the emails never say how they relate. I'd also like to see price, dimensions, or lead time on any piece, and there's nothing like that here. ## What's working

- "One week left" and "up to 30% off" in the hero give me a credible deadline and a number. I get that in about one second.
- The four category tiles (dining furniture, tabletop, lighting, rugs) match a renovation shopping list. The pendant and rug shots are good for my saved-emails folder.
- A second "Shop the dining event" button after the tiles means I don't scroll back up to act.
- The footer has the CB2 app, 10% back in Rewards Dollars on CB2 and Crate & Barrel purchases, and 15% off for texts. The rewards line matters to someone who picks cards for points.
- The layout is clean and renders fine. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The 10% rewards perk is buried at the bottom in small type. It should be near the offer.
- The Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz sweepstakes block takes up a big chunk of the middle of the email. It's a $5,000 gift card giveaway and a text-signup push. It competes with the sale and doesn't speak to my project.
- The "up to 30%" has no product, no price, and no "from $X." Article and Wayfair at least put a specific dollar-off on a specific item. Wayfair's pergola email showed $745 against $879. I don't get that here.
- The tiles don't tell me about shipping, delivery windows, or fabric swatches. I'm not ordering a $3k dining table without knowing lead time and being able to get a swatch.
- The fine print is a dense gray block at the bottom, and it's hard to read on a phone.
- The "SHOP THE SALE" and "SHOP THE DINING EVENT" buttons are small and low-contrast for tapping.
- "Hosting" in the subject only loosely matches the dining and tabletop content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hero product in the top section, with a price. "Dining table, $X, now $Y" beats a mood shot with a percentage.
- 2. Give the 30% event and the 60% warehouse sale one clear relationship. Either say the warehouse sale ends first, or point me to it. As is, two competing sales across two emails makes me wait.
- 3. Move the Rewards Dollars perk up under the hero, and add a line on delivery windows and swatches.
- 4. Move the sweepstakes below the product tiles, or cut it. At this cadence I'd rather see one clear message per send.
- 5. Make the buttons larger and darker. Clean up the legal block.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also carries a large number of tracking-pixel and image hygiene issues. The QA pass rate is 43%. The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results headers could not be verified, because the relay did not capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 62 tracking links went unprobed because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, so the QA data can't confirm whether any are broken.
- Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plaintext `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?...` through `pub10.php?...`
- Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS content (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may drop these pixels. They can also trigger mixed-content warnings.
- The pixels have no alt text.
- The tracking beacons `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/4397f0c5-...` also have no alt text.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Seven content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt text:
  - `0ac086b5-…jpg`
  - `a59e6571-…jpg`
  - `211b33c1-…jpg`
  - `6c910fb4-…jpg`
  - `8220ba3b-…jpg`
  - `5ebc1a2c-…jpg`
  - `5b8313e0-…jpg`
- With images off or a screen reader, these are blank or announced by filename.
- The tracking pixels should use `alt=""`, and ideally `width="1" height="1"` with `aria-hidden="true"`.
- The head sets `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag. This blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility concern.
- The head also includes the template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, which was never removed. It has no functional impact.
