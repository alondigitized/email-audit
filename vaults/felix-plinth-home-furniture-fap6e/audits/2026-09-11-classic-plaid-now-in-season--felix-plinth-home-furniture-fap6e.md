# Classic plaid, now in season
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, no burgundy "extra 20% off" banner today — that's the first thing I notice, and honestly a relief after screenshotting that same strip fourteen times this week. This is "Classic Plaid," a whole lifestyle-first drop: plaid bedding, plaid throws, plaid dinnerware, a "Heritage Autumn" moodboard panel. It's genuinely pretty — moody, warm, tailored — and it's exactly the kind of thing that gets saved to my inbox folder even if I don't buy today.
- But there's zero discount anywhere in this email. No %, no $ off, nothing. That's a big swing from the "Quick ship" and "extra 20%" sends I've been getting all week, and it means this is a browse email, not a buy-today email — fine as a category, but it changes what I'll do with it. I did notice "Recommended Just For You" further down actually has real product variety (curtain rods, a dining table, a swivel chair, a rug) that feels like it's pulling from stuff I've actually looked at, which is more than I can say for most of these.
- Verdict: I'd scroll the whole thing on my commute because the plaid styling is genuinely nice, but I'm not clicking to buy anything — there's no urgency and no price signal. I might forward the plaid bedding shot to my wife since we're doing the guest room in the second house, but that's it.

## What's working

- The plaid theme is cohesive top to bottom — bedding, throws, dinnerware, even the velvet pillow all read as one seasonal story, not a grab-bag grid.
- "In-Stock Furniture Delivered in 1-4 Weeks" sitting right at the top is the one line I actually care about — Article and CB2 bury shipping timelines, PB puts it front and center.
- "Recommended Just For You" section has actual product diversity (rod hardware, dining table, rug, curtains) instead of the same three SKUs repeated — feels more personalized than usual.
- Loyalty section down low ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards... Williams Sonoma, Reyounme") plus the Affirm "Buy Now, Pay Over Time" line — as a rewards-card guy, that's relevant real estate, even if it's buried at the bottom.
- Design Crew "Free Design Services" call-out is a nice touch for someone mid-reno — I've actually looked into stuff like this for the primary house.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere. Compare that to CB2's "THE FALL SALE" email that scored an 8 with me this week — that one led with a real number. This one leads with mood, which is fine for browsing but gives me zero reason to act today.
- The Halloween Shop insert ("Enter if You Dare") feels tonally jarring dropped right after a moody autumn plaid story — whiplash between "heritage tailored menswear vibes" and spooky porch decor.
- "Heritage Autumn" panel text is small and low-contrast (cream on dark brown) — I'd have to squint on my phone to read the CTA link.
- Nothing here says "for your second home" or "reno project" specifically — it's a generic seasonal drop, not segmented to people mid-renovation the way the Design Crew block almost gets there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer in this send — even a modest one. Right now it's all vibes; CB2 and Crate & Barrel both know a lifestyle hero still needs a number somewhere above the fold.
- 2. Cut the Halloween Shop insert from this email entirely — it breaks the plaid/heritage mood completely and belongs in its own send (which PB is already doing).
- 3. Bump contrast/size on the "Heritage Autumn" CTA text — dark-on-dark reads as an afterthought on mobile.
- 4. Move the Design Crew and rewards-card blocks higher — those are the two things in this email actually relevant to a renovation-heavy household, and they're stuck below the fold.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard MI-generated multipart email with a tracking pixel array, 52 click-redirect links, and CSS-only responsive breakpoints; automated QA could not confirm unsubscribe/authentication headers because they weren't captured by the ingesting relay, and nearly every image lacks alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 52 tracking/click-redirect links (e.g., `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`, `https://clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/cff0ccbf-...`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe since they are redirect endpoints, not final destinations — not independently verified as live.
- Multiple non-HTTPS (`http://`) resources present: `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`, and `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`. These may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- Multiple open-tracking pixels present (`svopn.potterybarn.com` open pixel, `d.turn.com/r/dd/...` third-party pixel, `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/...`), consistent with standard MI engagement tracking — no anomaly, noting for completeness.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — expected for email HTML, not an issue.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 39 `<img>` tags are missing `alt` attributes, including all content slices (`slices_01.jpg`–`slices_18.jpg` across two modules), product images (toscana-extendable-dining-table, remmy-swivel-chair, reeva-handwoven-rug, balboa-swivel-chair, belgian-flax-linen-curtain), and the header nav image (`nav0.jpg`). Screen readers and images-disabled clients (Outlook default) will render this email with no usable text content.
- Stray `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")` PHP-style function calls are embedded as literal text inside a `<style>` block in `<head>`. These are inert as CSS (browsers will ignore them as invalid rules), but their presence indicates a template artifact/leftover server code that wasn't stripped from the source.
- Conditional MSO blocks (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso 16]>`) and Outlook font-fix classes are correctly structured.
- Responsive breakpoints rely on two overlapping/conflicting media queries (`min-width: 700px` sets `img{max-width:650px}`, `min-width:650px` sets `img{max-width:100%!important}`) — the `!important` in the second will win at ≥700px, making the 650px cap in the first query dead code. Not a functional bug but redundant/contradictory rules.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient email is hard-coded/merged into tracking URLs as plain text, e.g. `sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`. No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%EMAIL%%`) were found in the truncated source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header: not found by automated check. QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than confirmed absence — cannot confirm compliance status from available data.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe): not found — same caveat as above.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — same relay-capture caveat; sender authentication status is unverified, not confirmed failing.
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer text was present in the truncated HTML provided — full footer beyond the truncation point was not available to confirm physical address and unsubscribe mechanism required by CAN-SPAM.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Header nav link uses an MI tokenized redirect (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=ABB7...`) rather than a directly inspectable UTM-tagged URL — final landing page and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without following the redirect (skipped by probe per QA findings).
