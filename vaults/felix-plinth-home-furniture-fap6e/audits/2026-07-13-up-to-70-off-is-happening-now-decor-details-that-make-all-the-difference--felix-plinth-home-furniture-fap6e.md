# Up to 70% off is happening now + Decor details that make all the difference
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-one. That's the July tally after this one lands. I started counting after the July 10 "FREE design help" send — the email that actually felt different, the one that bought Pottery Barn a few sends of goodwill. That goodwill is gone. Because yesterday I got "🩵 Shop UP TO 70% OFF + Celebrate summer with SAVINGS," and today I'm getting "Up to 70% off is happening now." The subject line has basically been cloned. The hero image — a nicely styled bed in navy and white — is beautiful in the way Pottery Barn photography always is, but the styling story is pillows, throws, and vases. I'm managing two homes in active renovation. I need sofas and case goods. I don't need a third email this week suggesting I buy another decorative pillow.
- What rescues this from total ignore is the "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" module sitting about halfway down — room scenes with seating that actually match where my renovation budget is pointed right now. That section is the most useful thing in the email. The problem is I have to scroll past pillows, throws, bedding, and vases to get there. If I'm on my phone between meetings, I've already put it down.
- Mark as read, no click. Definitely not forwarding it — I'd be embarrassed sending a friend what is functionally the same Pottery Barn sale email I forwarded two days ago.

## What's working

- **Photography is still doing the heavy lifting.** The hero bedroom scene in navy/white/cream is genuinely aspirational — Pottery Barn still shoots rooms better than Wayfair, which tends to flatten everything to white-background product shots with no context.
- **"RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" module.** Personalized room scenes buried mid-email are the most relevant thing here. If PB's algorithm knows I've been browsing seating, this section is pointing in the right direction.
- **"SHOP ALL DECOR" CTA is clean.** Verb plus noun, visible button, no "Discover the magic of summer" nonsense. Functional.
- **Rewards and Affirm are both surfaced.** As someone who runs rewards math on every purchase, seeing the PB Rewards callout matters — even if I have to scroll all the way to the bottom to find it.

## What's weak

- **"Up to 70% off" has lost all meaning.** This is the fourth consecutive email in roughly 72 hours with this headline. At this point it's a background pattern, not an offer. Crate & Barrel sent me "$170 off select Le Creuset Dutch ovens" this same morning — specific product, specific dollar amount. That's a real offer.
- **Subject is a double-header doing two jobs badly.** "Up to 70% off is happening now" is the sale hook; "Decor details that make all the difference" is the editorial hook. Neither lands because they're in competition. Article's "New arrivals just landed" from a week ago — ten words, one clear expectation — beat this on focus.
- **Bedroom/pillows hero is a persona miss.** I'm two homes deep in renovation mode. Throw pillows are not my bottleneck. CB2's "GET IT ON SALE" that same week led with room scenes that included actual furniture. PB is burying the furniture lower.
- **Nine modules is too many.** Pillows → Throws → Bedding → Vases → Shop All → Recommended → New Arrivals → App → Rewards → Affirm → footer. On a phone, this email doesn't end. It just keeps going until my thumb gives up.
- **No deadline.** "Is happening now" is not a deadline. It's a sentence. A Monday cutoff, a 48-hour counter — anything would make the urgency real.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero module for the "Recommended Just for You" content.** If the algorithm knows what I've been browsing, that belongs at the top, not row five. Lead with my data, not a generic bedroom shot.
- 2. **Give "up to 70% off" a specific product anchor or a hard end date.** "Up to 70% off sofas — ends Sunday" is worth opening. "Up to 70% off is happening now" is not.
- 3. **Cut this email in half.** One story per send: either the sale, or the style editorial, or the new arrivals. Running all three means none of them converts.
- 4. **Collapse the subject to a single lane.** The "+" construction is trying to A/B test inside a single subject line. It just reads as indecision.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer sale: select sofas up to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off — ends this Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, bedding, and decor — the biggest sale of the season is on.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your picks are in the sale. Don't scroll past them.`
