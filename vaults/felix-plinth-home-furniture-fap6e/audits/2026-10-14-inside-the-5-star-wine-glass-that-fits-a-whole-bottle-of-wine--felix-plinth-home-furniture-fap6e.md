- The 5-star review callout ("almost 330 reviews") on the bestselling glass is credible social proof.

## What's weak

- The sofa deadline banner sits above a wine glass story. It splits my attention and repeats this morning's sofa send.
- Too many modules compete. Recipes, Fall Sale, credit card, app, design appointment and the product grid all come after the main story.
- The two CTAs at the top, "Shop Now" and "Shop Durable Wine Glasses," have no clear priority.
- The small gray text under the images is hard to read on a phone without zooming. The product grid captions are tiny too.
- Nothing here speaks to someone renovating two houses. There's no color palette, sample, shipping or showroom content.
- The $500 app chance and the 10% back credit card pitch feel bolted on.

- **Third-party pixels:** Five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278...&n=1` through `&n=5` beacons (LiveRamp) are present.
