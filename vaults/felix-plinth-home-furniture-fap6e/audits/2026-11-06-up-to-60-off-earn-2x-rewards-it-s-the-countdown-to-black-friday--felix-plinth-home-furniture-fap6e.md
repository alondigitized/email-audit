# Up to 60% off + Earn 2x Rewards! It’s the countdown to Black Friday →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Crate & Barrel send in two days, and the countdown-to-Black-Friday hero is the first one that feels like a real event. The red strip at the top still repeats the "last Double Rewards this year" and "Fall Sales Event" pitch I got five times on 11/05. The "up to 60% off" hero, with a warm oak cabinet, does stand out from those. I only half-clicked on the 11/05 "Up to 30% off holiday hosting essentials + Double Rewards!" send. This one gives me a bigger number to act on.
- The email is long, and the same two offers (Double Rewards and the Fall Sales Event) show up three or four times. Pottery Barn's "Cozy Cloud Quilt" send on 11/06 picked one product and one price and was done. This one makes me scroll through a category grid, a clearance strip, a Gift Shop block, a Design Desk sweepstakes and an app promo.
- Overall, I'd click once, into rugs or sofas and sectionals, since I'm furnishing two houses. Then I'd file it in the Pinterest folder and mute the rest of the day's sends. I wouldn't forward it. Nothing in it is specific enough to send to a friend.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off" over the hero is the biggest number I've seen from them this week. It sits right next to a single Shop Now button.
- The category grid has labels I use: sofas & sectionals, rugs, lighting, tabletop & bar. I can jump straight to the room I'm working on.
- The clearance strip shows three real items with struck-through prices (a sectional, a chair, a bed). That makes the discount feel real instead of "up to."
- The Rewards callout is on-brand for someone who picks the highest-rewards card. "Earn 15% back in Reward Dollars with your Crate Card" is the one line I did the math on.
- The Design Desk block fits my two-house situation. A free design appointment with a $5,000 sweepstakes is a good hook for a renovator.

## What's weak

- The offer stack is confusing: up to 60% off, up to 30% off, up to 50% off, 2x Rewards, 15% back and a $5,000 sweepstakes. I can't tell which discount applies to what.
- Double Rewards appears again lower down as a second red banner. I'd already read it at the top.
- Almost no people or room scenes. It's all product tiles and flat-lays, so nothing tells me this was meant for me.
- The CTAs are generic: "Shop Now," "Learn More," "Shop All Clearance." Nothing names a category or an end date I can act on.
- There's no shipping or lead-time info, and no swatch or sample mention. That's what I need to know when I'm ordering furniture for a renovation. Wayfair has the same gap, and its pergola sends at least lead with "from $100."
- It's very long and ends in a wall of fine print. A phone user will give up well before the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one hero offer and one secondary offer. Move Design Desk, the app promo and the Gift Shop block to a different send.
- 2. Show the end date and the rewards math ("2x = 15% back through 11/11") once, in the hero.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones: "Shop sectionals up to 60% off," "Book a free design appointment."
- 4. Add one line on furniture delivery windows and free swatches near the sofa and sectional tiles.
- 5. Give me a reason this isn't the fifth near-identical send. CB2 and Article both send fewer, more distinct emails.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off sectionals & rugs, ends Black Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 15% back in Reward Dollars ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs, lighting and more, plus 2x Rewards on your Crate Card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free design appointments still open, and clearance just got deeper.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated HTML source is structurally sound, with a table-based layout, MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run found 3 warnings and no hard failures. The main confirmed problems are about 42 images with no `alt` text and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. The header warnings and the 83 unprobed links are unverified, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 83 links were skipped because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fires the same pixel five times. These requests go to the LiveRamp (rlcdn) domain, separate from the sender's own `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` tracking. All five carry the same subscriber-level hash in `s=`, so the repeat calls add no information.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f…` is a per-recipient identifier in image URLs, including `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif`. The `rp/*.png` images are probably personalized or recommendation content. If so, they depend on that ID resolving at open time.
- **Open pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4c60d1dd-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` look like open or tracking pixels. The `dv` one has no file extension.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 42 images are flagged, which is confirmed by the QA data.
  - 28 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `296bfc38-….jpg`.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, which are likely product or recommendation tiles.
  - 7 are tracking pixels (5× `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv…`).
  - The pixels should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`. The content images need real alt text, because with images blocked the email will have no readable content.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 problem.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` turn off text-size adjustment.
- **Small text:** `.f10{font-size:10px!important}` is used at mobile widths. Where it is applied is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Markup validity:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` appears alongside `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, with conflicting values in the first declaration.
  - The unfilled template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in. It does no harm but shows the template was not cleaned.
- **Fine as is:** `lang="en"` is set, and `color-scheme` is pinned to light.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tail of the HTML was truncated, so this is not checked for the full email.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are reported not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. The sender (Crate & Barrel) may well send them. Checking raw headers at delivery would settle it.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Neither is visible in the truncated source, so neither is confirmed.
- The sending domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a branded subdomain. This is consistent with proper alignment, but it is not proof.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 83 tracking links were not probed, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. The subject promises "Up to 60% off + Earn 2x Rewards", and landing-page alignment for that offer cannot be checked from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 35 content and recommendation images. Give the 7 tracking pixels `alt=""`.
2. Change the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
3. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` and `body` rules. Keep `100%`.
4. Collapse the repeated `rlcdn` pixel to one call, and review whether it is needed.
5. Escape the title as `&amp;`, and remove the duplicate `meta charset` and `format-detection` tags and the leftover template comment.
6. Re-run the audit on the raw message instead of the relay copy, so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be verified. Resolve the tracking redirects to check UTM and landing-page alignment.
