# Up to 50% off clearance styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- Article again, and this is basically the 10/05 "Clearance Event" send with a fresh coat of paint. Same coral gradient, same giant "Up to 50% off." I felt déjà vu before I finished the headline. Four days apart is fast, and the offer hasn't moved at all. It's the same hero, which is exactly the thing that annoys me when the same lamp shows up in three brands' emails in one week. Here it's one brand repeating itself.
- It's clean and I can read it in two seconds. The offer is huge, the button is obvious, and the four category tiles (living room, bedroom, decor, outdoor) at least let me jump to the room I'm working on. But there's no product, no price, and no "this sofa went from X to Y." I'm renovating two houses and I need to see an actual sectional and its number before I click. Today's inbox is already CB2's "So we're doing a little thing," plus Pottery Barn's Fall Sale at up to 70%, so a bare "up to 50%" with no hero item doesn't stand out. I'd skim it, maybe tap Living Room out of habit, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% off." headline is huge and the eye lands on it first. Nothing competes with it.
- A single white "SHOP NOW" pill sits right under the copy, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- The four room tiles are a useful shortcut. Bedroom and Living Room are the two I'd actually tap.
- The 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee gets a mention in the hero and again in the footer. For big furniture buys, a return window is a real trust cue.
- It's short and renders cleanly, with no broken images and no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- It's a near-duplicate of the 10/05 "Clearance Event: Get up to 50% off" send. I've now gotten the same offer twice in four days with no new reason to care.
- There's no product, no price and no before/after. "Up to 50%" could mean one throw pillow. Pottery Barn and CB2 at least show me specific pieces.
- The tile labels are white text on busy, darkened photos. "Shop Decor" over that rug pattern is harder to read than it should be.
- There's nothing on shipping or delivery timing for furniture, nothing on swatches or samples, and no sign of what's actually in stock. The footer just has a generic "Shipping and Delivery" icon.
- I don't know what "last chance" is tied to, and there's no end date. "Before they're gone" is soft urgency. The coral banner repeats the hero line, which is wasted space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic hero with 2–3 named clearance pieces showing the sale price next to the original. Do that for the sectional and the walnut bed, which Article already showed me in earlier sends.
- 2. Add a real deadline, like "Ends Sunday," and say which categories go deepest, such as "Sofas up to 50% off."
- 3. Brighten or darken the tile images consistently, or put the labels on a solid bar so they're legible.
- 4. Add a line on in-stock clearance delivery windows and swatch availability. That's the friction point for furniture.
- 5. Don't re-send the 10/05 creative. Rotate it so the second send gives a reason to come back.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is a standard responsive XHTML table layout with `lang="en"` and dark-mode support. The main code-level defects are malformed dark-mode CSS, an empty `<title>`, and 3 images flagged for missing alt text. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because of the relay, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links are wrapped in ESP redirect domains: `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...` and `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`. Final destinations can't be read from the source.
- Only 1 of 12 material links was probed (11 skipped, per QA). Link health is largely **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- The QA run found 0 broken links among the links it probed.
- Two tracking-wrapper domains are in use (`spmailtechnol.com` and `email.article.com`). If they are different ESP layers, that is an extra redirect hop and a point of failure. This is only an observation; I can't confirm it from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/q8qv9_.../Pep5WB-f6VFg6YG...`
  - `links.email.article.com/s/eo/1g5oIP1t...` (identifier "25")
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Nb1n-IQ_.../IodUesqu3TSk...`
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this hurts accessibility and the "view in browser" tab label.
- **Malformed dark-mode CSS:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma that leaves an empty selector. Strict parsers may drop the whole rule, so headings and paragraphs may not get the dark-mode color.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a misplaced `!important`. It is invalid, so the declaration is ignored and the link color is not forced.
  - The first `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark){...}` block ends at `a .link{color:#fff!important}</style>` with no closing brace. The `[data-ogsc]` block that follows ends with `}}`, so it carries a stray extra brace. Browsers usually recover, but behavior is parser-dependent.
- **Dark-mode risk:** `a` is set to `#fff` in dark mode while `.left-sidebar a{color:#85ab70}` and `.right-sidebar a{color:#70bbd9}` use fixed colors. Because the `a` rule is invalid, that override may not apply.
- `.dark-img` / `.light-img` swaps depend on `display:none!important`. This is a common Gmail/Outlook pitfall, but I can't confirm a failure from the source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens such as `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the full body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA flags that the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain, `email.article.com`, is a subdomain, so alignment can't be verified here.
- An unsubscribe link was included in the probed set, but the QA output doesn't show its result. The footer's physical address and unsubscribe text are outside the truncated source.

Not verifiable. UTM parameters are hidden behind redirect wrappers. 11 of 12 links weren't resolved, so landing-page alignment with the "up to 50% off clearance" subject couldn't be checked.
