# NEW prints & patterns with a storied past
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Kravet x Pottery Barn: New Print & Pattern" — this one actually stopped my scroll for a second. A designer archive collab is exactly the kind of thing that makes a renovation guy like me feel like I'm buying into something with a story instead of just another quilted duvet with a made-up "Heritage Medallion" name slapped on it. The moody navy floral bedding hero looks genuinely expensive, and the grid of pillows/drapes/lamps styled into real rooms gives me actual ideas for the second house's guest room.
- Then it just... keeps going. Curtain rods, a swivel chair, a dining table, a jute rug, a Halloween ghost display, Free Design Services, the app, the credit card, Affirm — by the time I hit "RECOMMENDED JUST FOR YOU" I'd already mentally closed the email. This is the same problem CB2's "Upholstery Event" (7/10) avoided by staying tight on one sale and one hero image — this Kravet email had a genuinely strong top and then buried it under five more asks.
- Would I click? Yes, on the Kravet collection itself, within the first two screens. Would I forward it? Maybe to my wife if we're still deciding on the guest room bedding — the archive angle is a good conversation starter. But I'm not scrolling to the bottom, and Pottery Barn's Halloween Shop pitch here feels like whiplash after "New Print & Pattern" — it's the same shop-fatigue problem as their "Holiday Headquarters" email from two days ago (5/10), just condensed into one send instead of three shops.

## What's working

- The Kravet collab name and "hand-selected from the archive" copy gives this real provenance — more credible than Crate & Barrel's generic "up to 60% off" markdown emails I've seen twice this week.
- Hero bedding shot is styled, moody, and on-brand — no stock-photo staging.
- The pillow/drape/lamp grid actually shows the print in multiple applications (pillow, drapery, upholstered chair) which helps me picture it in a real room, something Wayfair's "Which TV Stand Are You?" never bothered doing for its hero product.
- Clear single CTA ("Shop the Collection") appears twice for the hero section, so I'm not hunting for it.

## What's weak

- The email doesn't stop after the Kravet drop — it tacks on curtain rods, a chair, a dining table, a rug, Halloween décor, Design Services, app download, and a credit card pitch, all in one scroll. That's five different asks competing with the one good idea.
- Halloween ghost decor showing up directly under "Recommended Just For You" reads like a merchandising afterthought, not curation — nothing here is "just for me," it's just their catalog.
- No specific % or $ off anywhere in the whole email — for a "new arrivals" premium collab that's fine, but then slapping a rewards-card and Affirm pitch at the bottom undercuts the design-forward vibe.
- Zero mention of swatches or fabric samples, which is the first thing I'd want before committing to a $200+ pillow in a print I've only seen on a screen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the Kravet collection and the "Recommended Just For You" grid — kill the Halloween Shop, Design Services, app, and credit card blocks entirely, or move them to a separate send. Right now this is four emails stitched into one.
- 2. Add a "Order a swatch" or "Request a fabric sample" CTA right under the hero — that's the actual friction point before I'd drop real money on a print-heavy pillow.
- 3. Put a real incentive on the archive collection itself (even "New — limited run" framing) since there's no price signal anywhere in the hero.
- 4. Trim "Recommended Just For You" to 2 items max that actually relate to the print/pattern theme (the swivel chair fits, the dining table and curtain rod feel randomly pulled).

- **Subject Alt B:** `New from the Kravet archive — shop the print collection`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hand-selected patterns, now on pillows, drapery, and more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order a swatch before you commit to the print`
