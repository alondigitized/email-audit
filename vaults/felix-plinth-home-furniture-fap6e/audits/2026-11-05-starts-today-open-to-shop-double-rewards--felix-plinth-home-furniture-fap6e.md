# Starts TODAY! Open to shop Double Rewards →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crate & Barrel send today, and the Kids logo is the only thing that's new. My gut reaction was "I already saw this." It's the same Double Rewards and Fall Sales Event message as the 11/05 "Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅" and "The Fall Sales Event starts TODAY!" sends. This one has a bedroom hero on top and then crib, nursery and playroom modules, which is a kids' furniture pitch. I'm renovating two houses, not furnishing a nursery.
- The screenshot is very small and tall (680x12000), so I can't read the fine print. I can see the structure. There's a headline about the last Double Rewards of the year with Shop Now and Apply Now buttons. Below that come three product grids ("bestsellers for their bedroom," "must-haves for their nursery," "fun picks for their playroom"), then a plaid 50%-off banner, a sweepstakes block, stroller and baby-gear tiles, a card promo and an app promo. It's about 12 screens of scrolling for one idea.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim the hero and archive it. If I did click, it would be for the rewards math, not the cribs.

## What's working

- The "last Double Rewards this year" headline gives me a real deadline. It's more credible than the usual "ends soon."
- The hero photo is a moody green bedroom with good styling. That's the kind of image that goes into my Pinterest folder.
- The product tiles carry their own "Earn 2x Rewards" labels, so the rewards math sits right on each item.
- The plaid "up to 50% off" banner is the clearest, most price-driven block in the email.
- The Shop Now and Apply Now buttons are obvious and easy to hit on a phone.

## What's weak

- Every product module is for kids: nursery, crib, stroller, playroom. Nothing speaks to a renovation, like lighting, rugs or dining.
- It's the third C&B send today with the same offer. Pottery Barn's "Introducing The Christmas Shop" at least gave me a new reason to open it. This one gives me only the old reason.
- It's far too long. A single message has seven or eight stacked sections, including a sweepstakes, a credit-card pitch and an app download, and they dilute the offer.
- The Apply Now credit-card button beside Shop Now pulls the hero in two directions. I already use my highest-rewards card.
- The photos are large, and the text and prices at the bottom are small enough that I'd have to zoom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send the Kids logo only to people who have shown kids' browsing. For everyone else, fold it into one main Crate & Barrel send instead of a third message the same day.
- 2. Cut the module count to three: the hero, one product grid, and the 50% off banner. Move the card, the sweepstakes and the app promo to a separate send.
- 3. Put the rewards math in the hero. For example, say "2x Rewards = X% back on a $2,000 order," so a rewards-driven shopper can do the arithmetic at a glance.
- 4. Show the end date on the hero instead of "this year."
- 5. Sort the products by what the reader actually browses, whether that's furniture or décor.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link targets could not be checked. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 113 tracking links, which are click-redirect domains. Redirect chains, 404s and final landing URLs could not be confirmed.
- **Repeated third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times in one message. This is a LiveRamp domain. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string, which looks like a hashed identifier. `mi_u=dbe8da71…` on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels carries the same value.
- **Duplicate first-party pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears six times with identical src.
- **Other tracking pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d4029a0b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` look like open or tracking pixels.
- **Image hosts:** Content images load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `s7d5.scene7.com`. Scene7 is a separate host from the sending domain and could not be probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 50 `<img>`, flagged by QA):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, for example `20815667-626a-48ff-a000-53d635c39432.jpg`, `7a8a3e3d-…jpg`, and `bbf6f9d4-…png` under `/m/47/`.
  - The Scene7 GIF `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  - With images blocked, or for a screen reader, those sections would be empty or announce a filename.
  - The tracking pixels (`f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv…/o/…`) should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. I can't tell from QA output whether the attribute is absent or empty, so treat those as lower priority.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{…}` and in `body`.
- **Page title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and is generic, with no subject-specific text.
- **Leftover template markup:**
  - The placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still present.
  - `meta format-detection` is declared four times and charset twice (`http-equiv` plus `<meta charset>`).
  - `*{… line-height:100%}` is a universal selector that can override per-element line-heights in some clients.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider, both match and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead.

- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link, physical address and sender identification are in the truncated portion, so they could not be verified.
