## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed, because the AgentMail relay may have stripped those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA check skipped 87 tracking links, because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are all unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 17,670 of 24,290 characters (about 73%) are URLs. This is a consequence of the long tracked links. It is above the 70% threshold and is a minor spam-filter risk.
- **Third-party pixels:** `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are five separate LiveRamp pixel requests. There are also pixels on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` (`f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `o.gif`) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e061f04a-…`. Each one is an extra request, and some clients or privacy proxies will block them. They carry per-recipient `mi_u`/`s` tokens in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA list shows about 52 images with no `alt`.
  - About 39 are content images, served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif` and `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F0930%5FCB%5FFallGathering%5FInfluencers...`. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `0424b473-3342-45c7-9138-e3a2b6d997de.gif`. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these give no text.
  - The remaining ~13 are tracking pixels, where a missing `alt` is low impact. They should still carry `alt=""`.
  - Because the QA output may be truncated, treat 52 as a lower bound.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times (combined, plus separate `date`, `address` and `telephone` tags), and both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - A template placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, was left in the source.
  - None of these affect rendering.
- **Global reset:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` are applied globally. Any element without an explicit override inherits them, which is a fragility risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The only per-recipient values seen are the `mi_u`/`mi_mid` tracking identifiers in the pixel URLs. The HTML was truncated, so the body was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post:** The RFC 8058 header was also not found. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so confirm this on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain aligned with the brand, which is consistent with proper setup. Alignment is not verified.
- **Footer:** The visible footer, postal address and unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source, so they were not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 87 links are click-tracked redirects that were not probed. The truncated HTML does not show final URLs, so UTM parameters, redirect targets and landing page alignment could not be checked. There is no evidence of a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/.../m/1/*` and `scene7` assets. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Change the viewport to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1` and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
3. Re-check the raw `.eml`, outside the relay, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as defects.
4. Escape the ampersand in `<title>` (`&amp;`), remove the duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags, and delete the leftover placeholder comment.
5. Reduce the plain-text URL weight. Shorten the tracking redirects, or build the plain-text part from key links rather than every link.
6. Audit the five `rlcdn.com` pixels and consolidate them if the duplication is not intentional.
7. Run a follow-up check that resolves the redirect chains for a sample of the 87 links and records the final URL and UTM parameters.
