- "Up to 60% off clearance" is a concrete offer, and the "Shop Clearance" button is clear.
- The four quick-link buttons (Furniture, Rugs, Decor & Lighting, 40–60% Off Final Sale) let me skip the kids' stuff.
- "Free shipping on toys $99+" is simple and easy to scan.
- The Crate Card block mentions Reward Dollars. That's the kind of thing I'd do the rewards math on.
- Product tiles show prices cleanly. The Halloween treat bag shows a struck-through $34.95 against $19.97.

## What's weak

- The hero text sits on a busy leaf photo, and the "Selling fast! Shop now" line under it is small and gets lost.
- Strollers, car seats, nursery chairs and cribs aren't for me. The email gave me no way to tell it knew that.
- The Design Desk block appears twice, near the top and again near the bottom. It's also a repeat of yesterday's sends.
- The Halloween shop banner lands on the 22nd. That feels late, and there's no sale percentage on it.
- It's too long. There are about nine sections before the footer, and the legal text is a wall of tiny print. I couldn't tell what the clearance discount excludes.
- Most of the "we love these for you" tiles show no sale price, even though the headline says 60% off. The clearance items aren't visible, so it feels like bait.

- 1. Put the clearance products in the hero instead of a seasonal photo. Show 3–4 items with before and after prices, so the 60% claim is backed up on screen.
- 2. Drop the second Design Desk block, and ideally the first too. I've gotten this pitch on 10/21 already.
- 3. Add a ship-date line for large items. That's what I need for furniture, and Article does it better.
- 4. Make the hero text bigger, and put it on a solid band the way Pottery Barn Sale's maroon "Extra 20% off" box does.
- 5. Give the three or four mailings that day one clear reason each. Wayfair's pergola email with the "$134 off" badge states its case in one tile. This email makes five separate pitches.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the product and hero images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. This is the main confirmed defect.

4. **Unsubscribe headers:** Check the raw message source or the sending platform for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture rather than reporting a sender issue.
