## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Wayfair's Black Friday email (`editor@members.wayfair.com`) is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive `@media (max-width:480px)` rules. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The main problems are 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, widespread missing `alt` text, and several checks that came back inconclusive (429 responses, unprobed links, headers not captured).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe results are inconclusive, not failures.** 7 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...`, and the product links (`Karran-Dropin-Quartz...`, `Challenger-Hathaway-Games-Shuffleboard-Table...`, `11-Pieces-Cast-Iron-Enameled-Cookware-Set...`). The cause is rate limiting, which is expected when probing a retailer. Link validity is unconfirmed.
- **4 links were never checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out: `.../wayfair-product-L2960-K~id-AR93095935.html`, `.../Croll-Modern-Fireplace-Mantel-L1337-K~id-BL09066974.html`, `.../wayfair-product-L168-K~id-AV22046375.html`, and `.../daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html?eid=635985`. 72 links in total were skipped by the ranking heuristic.
- **Non-HTTPS app deep-link fallback.** Both "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" OneLink URLs carry `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app get sent to an `http://` URL, which depends on a server-side redirect to HTTPS.
- **Inconsistent `_eml` tokens.** The three product links in the evidence share `_eml=74721401-fe19-4eae-bf2e-bb526d5fc85c`. The nav, hero and CTA links each carry a different `_eml`, so link-level click attribution is not uniform.
- **Image-link wrapping.** Star-rating images (`stars_10.png`) are wrapped in the product anchors, so the link text the QA tool saw is `[image: stars_10.png]`. Screen readers will announce the filename.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on about 25 content and decorative images:**
  - Hero GIF `1113-US-BF-Email-General.gif`
  - Banners `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-EverythingShips`, `...Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`, `...Skinny-WFCC.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`
  - `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (×12) and `stars_11.png` (rating images, so the review score is lost with images off)
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (open pixel) and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp?...` pixels have none. These should carry `alt=""`.
- **20 `http://` image sources.** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`) are not HTTPS. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will suppress them, and some clients show a warning. Impression tracking is lost for those recipients.
- **Remote web font.** `@font-face "Sofia"` loads from `https://secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../sofia_reg.woff`. `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` is declared, so fallback is handled for Outlook.
- The HTML source was truncated, so structural checks (`lang`, `<title>`, preheader, `role="presentation"` on layout tables) could not be verified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `[[`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Recipient identifiers are injected as URL parameters (`_emr`, `_eml`, `emlid`, `maiid`) and resolved correctly.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). The QA tool notes this may be a relay-capture artifact, so it can't be confirmed as missing from the sender.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (RFC 8058 one-click). The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `members.wayfair.com` is a subdomain, so alignment should be checked from the raw headers.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the HTML is truncated. The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any observed link.** Wayfair uses first-party parameters instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `refid=MKTEML_145356`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16820`. This works if Wayfair's own analytics ingests them, but it is not compatible with GA-style channel attribution.
- **Campaign parameter mismatch on the app link.** The OneLink URL has `c=Triggered` and `pid=Email`, but the other parameters say `source=batch`. A batch promotional send labelled "Triggered" will skew AppsFlyer campaign reporting.
- **Landing alignment can't be confirmed.** The "Shop Sale" and hero links go to `/daily-sales` and the logo goes to `/`. Because of the 429s, the destinations and their promo messaging could not be checked against the email.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all tracking pixels over HTTPS (`li.wayfair.com/imp` ×20) and add `alt=""` to them and to `t.wayfair.com/b.php`.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the hero GIF and banners. Give the star images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", or make them decorative with `alt=""` and move the rating into text.
3. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, and use a distinct `c=` value for batch sends instead of `Triggered`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) from the raw MIME headers, along with SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
5. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff to clear the 429s, and extend the time budget to cover the 4 unprobed links.
6. Make `_eml` link tokens consistent per link, or document that they are shared per module.
7. In the QA pipeline, flag header-based compliance checks as "unverifiable via relay" rather than WARN, so they stop inflating the warning count.
