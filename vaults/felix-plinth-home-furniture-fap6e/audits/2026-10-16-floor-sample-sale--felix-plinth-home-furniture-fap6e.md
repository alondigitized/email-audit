## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, a raw merge token `%%reviews_skulist%%`, and a tracking and pixel setup with many accessibility and HTTP-image problems. The QA pass rate is 43%. Several compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 57 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified.
- Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels (`http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) are embedded as images. A further pixel, `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`, loads over plaintext HTTP. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy or block these, and any client that blocks mixed content will drop them. The cookie-sync only works where they load.
- The tracking pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/daab9815-...?mi_cid=...` carry hashed or identifying query parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`). I can't verify from the truncated source what they encode.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- 17 images have no `alt` attribute. Three are content images:
  - `70ce435a-0eee-4f96-9654-326c8063c7d9.gif`
  - `219caa71-114c-46c7-bc03-5c395d9b37e6.jpg`
  - `8436ffd4-f83c-4d1b-83e1-4a1d789552c3.jpg`
  
  The rest are tracking pixels (the Dotomi `pub1` to `pub10` and `UCMController` images, `o.gif`, and the `dv.cb2.com` image). Pixels should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. The content images need real alt text, and without it image-blocking clients show nothing.
- 8 of the 11 `http://` images are Dotomi pixels. `UCMController` and `pub1` to `pub10` are the full set of non-HTTPS images flagged.
- The `<title>` is a generic brand string (`CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME`), not the subject. This is minor.
- The `@font-face` rules load fonts from `www.crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`) and `www.cb2.com`. Outlook and many clients ignore web fonts, and the fallback stack is `Helvetica, sans-serif`. This is acceptable fallback behavior, not a defect.
- The CSS uses `p{margin:0 auto}` and later `p { padding:0 !important; margin:0 !important }`, which is redundant but harmless. The `*{... line-height:100%}` universal selector is aggressive and could override intended line heights in clients that honor it.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** The raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is in the delivered HTML. This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) style personalization string that was not resolved at send time. It likely sits in a dynamic reviews or SKU-list block. Recipients may see the literal token or a broken block, and it may be passed to a downstream URL or script.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found. The QA flags this as possibly an AgentMail relay capture limitation, so I can't confirm it is a real omission. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers at the source.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` has to be checked directly (`dig TXT _dmarc.cb2.com`, DKIM selector lookup).
- The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the physical address or the visible unsubscribe link.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All 57 tracking links were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be verified from the data given. Nothing was confirmed either way.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token. Add a fallback or suppress the block when the data is empty, and add a pre-send check that fails on any `%%...%%` string.
2. Add `alt` text to the three content images, and `alt=""` to all pixels.
3. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or remove them if the cookie-sync is no longer needed. Ten sync pixels per email adds load and privacy surface.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers from the origin MTA, not through the relay.
5. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com` directly.
6. Resolve a sample of the 57 tracking links and confirm the UTM parameters and landing pages.
