# Just in: 300+ new rugs
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "300+ new rugs" and this is a genuinely useful email for once — the actual reno reason I'm subscribed. It's organized by room (living, dining, bedroom, kids) with new-arrival/exclusive callouts and little "Designer Tip" nuggets, which is a format I don't hate. But it's also enormous — this thing scrolls forever, room by room by room, then a "bestseller spotlight," then a "rug shopping made easy" module, then a bedroom sale, then "new fall table," then a random "we love these for you" grid at the bottom. It's like three emails duct-taped together.
- I'd actually scroll through this one on my phone during the commute, which is more than I can say for the last four Crate & Kids sends this week. But I'm not clicking past the first section or two — by the time I hit "bedroom refresh" and "new fall table" I've mentally checked out, because now it's just becoming another everything-catalog like the "NEW for fall" dump from earlier this week. Wouldn't forward it to my wife, but I might screenshot the dining room rug section since we're actually shopping for that room right now.

## What's working

- Room-by-room structure (living/dining/bedroom/kids) actually maps to how I shop — I've got multiple rooms in flux across two houses right now.
- "Designer Tip" callouts (rug size under dining table, layering in bedrooms) are the kind of practical content I actually want, not just product photos.
- The rug swatch grids showing pattern/texture up close are genuinely helpful for a category where fit-and-feel matters and you can't tell from a thumbnail.
- "Bestseller spotlight: Monaco collection" is a decent trust signal — social-proof-adjacent without needing a review count.

## What's weak

- This is way too long — by module six or seven I've completely lost the thread, and it dilutes what was a strong, focused "rug guide" concept into a generic catalog blast.
- No actual offer up top — I have to scroll deep to find "up to 25% off bedroom refresh," and even that's bolted onto an unrelated section, not the rug story I opened for.
- Zero price anchoring anywhere in the rug sections — no "starting at $X," no sale badge, nothing. For a big-ticket item like an area rug I want at least a ballpark before I click through.
- The kids' rugs section blending into the main flow with no clear visual break makes the whole email feel like Crate & Kids and Crate & Barrel proper are one undifferentiated firehose — which, after this week, they basically are.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one focused send: pick ONE room (dining, since that's clearly the strongest visual block) and turn the rest into a "shop by room" follow-up series instead of cramming everything into one scroll.
- 2. Put a price or "rugs from $X" anchor in the hero — right now there's zero reason to click versus just bookmarking the email.
- 3. Move "rug shopping made easy" (swatch/design help/trade program) up near the top — that's a genuine differentiator versus Wayfair or Article and it's buried past the fold.
- 4. Drop the unrelated bedroom-sale and fall-table modules entirely, or split into a separate email — they undercut the "rug guide" premise this was sold on.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Living, dining, bedroom, kids — plus sizing tips from our designers`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals + the Monaco bestseller, room by room`
