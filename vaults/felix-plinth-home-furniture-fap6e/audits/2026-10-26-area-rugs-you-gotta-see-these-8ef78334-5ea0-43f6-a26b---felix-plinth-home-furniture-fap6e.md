# AREA RUGS. You *gotta* see these.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair again, and the subject promised area rugs. The first thing I see is a purple "Way Day ENDS TONIGHT" banner with 80% off, then a grid of mostly empty lavender boxes. Only one product shows, a jute rug on the top left, and a Morrell Corduroy Modular Ottoman caption is floating over a blank tile. The rest are broken-image placeholders with star ratings under nothing. Below that there's a huge white void, and another broken tile sits in the "Eco-Friendly Area Rugs" slot.
- I did scroll, because the lower "score styles you ❤️" grid does load: pergolas, comforters, a shag rug, patio sets, kitchen sinks. But it's a catalog dump, not a rug email. I'm renovating two houses and I do want rugs, but I can't tell what the sale is or which rugs are in it. This is the seventh Way Day send I've logged since 10/25, and my brain is in snooze mode.
- I'd mark it read. I might click the washable rug tile. I'm not forwarding it.

## What's working

- The "Way Day ENDS TONIGHT" banner with 80% off is the one clear urgency cue, and it reads at a glance.
- The jute rug at the top left is a good room shot with a natural texture, and it's what I'd save to my Pinterest folder.
- The "Cozy, Thick-Pile Washable Rugs" tile is a real rug product with a specific benefit.
- The lower grid has clean photography and short captions like "Curtains from $9.99," so I can scan quickly.
- The footer has the Wayfair Rewards and Flexible Financing icons, plus the 0% APR fine print. That's useful for how I buy on a rewards card.

## What's weak

- Most of the "inspo for your space" grid is blank lavender boxes with broken-image icons, so the hero section fails to deliver.
- There's a huge white gap under that grid, a whole screen of nothing before the content resumes.
- The subject says area rugs, but the body is pergolas, freezers, kitchen sinks and shoe racks. Only two or three tiles are rugs, and there's no rug hero or rug-specific offer.
- The "Eco-Friendly Area Rugs" tile is broken, and the caption sits alone with no image.
- The 80% off headline has no category attached, so I can't tell what's actually 80% off. Nothing about shipping on big rugs or sizes either.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the image rendering and the white void first. A broken hero costs more than any copy change. This is the second Wayfair send in a row, after the 10/25 pergola email, where the top of the email was blank.
- 2. Make it an actual rug email. Put one hero, such as "Area rugs up to 80% off, ends tonight," with sizes and free shipping, then 4 to 6 rug tiles with prices. Crate & Barrel's Dining Room Event email did this well: one clear hero, a tidy 2x2 grid and a single "up to 30% off" line.
- 3. Cut the unrelated tiles (freezers, sinks, shoe racks) from a send with a category subject. CB2's "STEALS OF THE WEEK" had the same "everything on one page" problem.
- 4. Put real prices and a color or size filter on the tiles. Pottery Barn's maroon strip is stale, but at least it names one offer.
- 5. Stop sending a near-identical Way Day email every few hours.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Area rugs up to 80% off — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Washable rugs from $__ (Way Day ends tonight)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jute, shag and washable styles, plus free shipping on big sizes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call: 5x8 and 8x10 rugs at Way Day prices until midnight.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429 (rate limiting), which the QA tool flags as "may be valid". Examples are `wayfair.com/daily-sales`, `wayfair.com/?_emr=…`, the `Alegre Bordered Indoor/Outdoor Rug` PDP, and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app link. These are inconclusive, not failures.
- **4 PDP links went unchecked** because probing stopped after the 70s budget: `wayfair-product-L310-K~W111781281.html` (twice) and `wayfair-product-L256-K~W118352394.html` (twice).
- **Non-HTTPS app fallback.** The OneLink URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback is plain HTTP and depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Redundant links.** The "Alegre Bordered Indoor/Outdoor Rug" text link and its `stars_10.png` image link go to the same PDP with the same `mplid=MCCY8001`. They differ only in `_eml`, which is per-link tracking and harmless.
- **Malformed asset URL.** `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is invalid percent-encoding (it should be `%XX`). Strict parsers or CDNs may reject or mis-decode it. The `0728` prefix is also a stale July campaign asset in an October 24 send (`WFUS_2026_1024`).
- **Test asset in production.** `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` has a "test" filename and is used as a linked hero image, going to `wayfair.com/?…sltid=6`. That link goes to the homepage instead of a rug landing page.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Subject-to-landing mismatch.** The subject is "AREA RUGS", but several primary links go to generic destinations: `/daily-sales` ("Shop Sale", banner `sltid=5`) and `/` (the `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` header and "Wayfair" logo). Only the product cards go to rug PDPs (`MCCY8001`).

4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, against the raw original message, not the relay copy. Treat them as open items until then.
5. Re-probe the 4 unchecked PDP links and the 429'd links with a slower rate or retry, to confirm they resolve.
6. Point the rug-focused header and banner links at a rug landing page, and correct the `c=Triggered` tag on the app link.
