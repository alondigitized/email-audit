# Early Black Friday deals going fast + Now open 🎁 The Christmas Shop
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again. I've lost count since 11/16, but it's at least a dozen sends. The maroon "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner is the same one I got on 11/18, 11/19 and both sends on 11/20. I scrolled past it before I registered it. The real news is below the banner: the Christmas Shop is open, with a tree, stockings, plaid bedding and a holiday table. That is the first fresh creative in days, and it's why I kept scrolling.
- The email is also too long. It runs hero, Christmas Shop, four tiles, a "Recommended just for you" grid, an "In Stock & On Sale" tile, Design Crew Sweeps, the app, Rewards, Affirm, and a wall of fine print. Crate & Barrel's "These 10 holiday favorites always sell out early" gave me one idea and let me go. This one makes me dig. Would I click? Probably the stockings or the bedding, since I'm doing two houses and the plaid duvet fits the second home. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my inspiration folder and move on.

## What's working

- The Christmas Shop hero photo, with the tree and wrapped gifts, is gorgeous and clearly seasonal. It reads as Pottery Barn and not as another sale blast.
- The lifestyle tiles (stockings, cozy bedding layers, vintage-inspired tabletop, festive pillows) are catalog-quality. They're exactly what I'd pin.
- The "Up to 50% off" label on the Christmas Shop is a concrete offer, and it sits right under the hero.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" block speaks to how I shop. I'd run it through the rewards card math.
- "In Stock & On Sale" is a good availability cue for furniture. I'd like to see more of it.

## What's weak

- The top banner is the same maroon "up to 70% off" I've seen about ten times this week. It makes the whole send feel recycled.
- Two headline offers (70% and 50%) are stacked back to back. I don't know which one applies to the stocking I'm looking at.
- The "Recommended just for you" grid shows plain stockings, a throw, curtain rod hardware and dining tables. There are no prices, no sale callouts and no reviews, so "just for you" feels generic.
- The footer fine print is a wall of tiny text. I can't read it on a phone without zooming, and the 70% exclusions are buried there.
- Nothing about furniture delivery windows, swatches or the design appointment until the Design Crew block near the bottom. For a renovator, that's backwards.
- Sweeps, app download, rewards and Affirm all pile up at the end. It reads as clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Lead with the Christmas Shop hero and drop the recycled maroon banner. Move the 70% line into a thin strip.
- 2. Put prices and "was/now" on every product in the recommended grid. Add a star rating or a "bestseller" tag.
- 3. Show a delivery cue on furniture tiles, like "Ships in 1–4 weeks" or "In stock, ships by 12/10." I'd click that.
- 4. Move the Design Crew appointment higher and make it the one secondary CTA. Drop the sweeps and app blocks.
- 5. Make the fine print collapsible, or link it out.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Christmas Shop is open: stockings to bedding, up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Plaid bedding + stockings now open, plus Early BF up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New holiday stockings, tabletop and bedding. Several styles in stock and ready to ship.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 10% back in Rewards on holiday decor. Sale ends soon.`

## Technical Audit: Pottery Barn, "Early Black Friday deals going fast + Now open 🎁 The Christmas Shop"

The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe block and most of the body links could not be inspected directly.

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 650px XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout that renders, but it has widespread missing alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources and recipient email addresses exposed in plain-text tracking URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay appears to have stripped the headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking is opaque.** 52 links go through `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` and were skipped by the HTTP probe. Final destinations, redirect health and UTM presence cannot be verified from the HTML.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixel.** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is loaded over `http://`. Clients that block mixed content or HTTP will drop the open-tracking beacon.
- **Third-party pixels.** `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` (Turn/Amobee) and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/87774d2e-...` are additional tracking pixels. The QA flagged them as images with no alt text.
- **Cross-brand asset.** The spacer `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is served from a West Elm host. It works, but it creates a dependency on another brand's CDN path.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA, about 40 images).**
  - Hero and banner images: `H26_Top_EBF_NoUrgency.jpg`, `H26_Bottom_InStockAndOnSale.jpg` and `H26_Bottom_DesignCrewSweeps_01/02.jpg`.
  - Sliced image sets, so the text content is likely invisible to screen readers and to users with images off: `F26_Seasonal_B10_XmasShop/slices_01–06.jpg` and `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg`.
  - Product images: `crewel-stockings`, `velvet-stockings`, `faux-fur-ruched-throw`, `curtain-rod-wall-bracket`, and the `toscana-` and `banks-extendable-dining-table` images.
  - Tracking pixels and spacers (`d.turn.com`, `svopn`, `clickcp`, `coreemail.html`, `spacer_0.gif`) should carry `alt=""` rather than none. This is lower severity.
  - The logo image does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
- **Insecure image sources over `http://`.** These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - the `svopn` pixel above
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is an accessibility and context gap in clients that surface it.
- **Zoom is restricted.** The viewport meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Stray server-side code inside a `<style>` block.** The text `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");` appears after the CSS rules. It is invalid CSS and looks like PHP copied in by mistake. It is probably harmless to rendering but is sloppy and can confuse some parsers.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.** The spacer table (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"><tr><td height="20">`) and the outer wrapper tables have no role, while other tables do. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Web font dependency.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat...">` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. This is a fallback concern only.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Recipient email address in plain-text URLs.** The address is embedded in `coreemail.html?email=lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` and in `sv_sveme=` on the `http://` svopn pixel. The second one is sent unencrypted. This exposes PII in URLs, which can be logged by proxies and intermediaries.
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available HTML.

### 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA attributes this to AgentMail relay capture, so it is unverified rather than confirmed absent. It needs a check against raw headers from the origin.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, for the same relay reason. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` is aligned with the click domain `click.e.potterybarn.com`.
- **Footer not inspectable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified because the source is truncated. The `Email-Footer/slices_01–18.jpg` sliced images with no alt text could mean footer legal text is image-only. If so, that is an accessibility and compliance concern.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters not verifiable.** All CTAs are wrapped in `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=` tokens, and the QA skipped probing them. Landing page alignment with the "Early Black Friday" and "Christmas Shop" promises is unconfirmed.
- **Product images hotlink the live site.** Examples are `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/ab/images/dp/wcm/2026xx/...`. This is fine for continuity, but the images depend on the product CDN and can break if assets are rotated.
- **Stale 2022 asset.** `pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg` is reused in a 2026 send, and it is served over HTTP.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, banners and sliced images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Switch all `http://` sources to `https://` (`Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`, the 2022 `slices_02.jpg` and the `svopn` pixel). Stop putting the recipient email in URL query strings, or use an opaque ID.
3. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block. Populate `<title>`, drop `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
4. Verify the raw headers from the origin: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC). Don't treat the relay-stripped result as a pass or a fail.
5. Confirm that the footer unsubscribe link and address exist as live text, not only in the sliced images. Resolve a sample of the `qs=` links in a browser to check UTM tagging and landing pages.
