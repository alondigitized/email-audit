Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping." The offer is clear. It's also aimed at a different household than mine.

## What's working

- The offer is plain. "Up to 50% off + free shipping" sits right under the hero.
- "Ships free" is tagged on individual tiles, which matters for bulky furniture.
- The two-column grid with percent-off captions is easy to skim.
- The Crate Card block mentions Reward Dollars. That's the rewards math I care about, and I use my highest-rewards card.
- "Book a Free Appointment" with The Design Desk is the closest thing to showroom or service content, and it's actually relevant to my renovations.

## What's weak

- Almost everything is for babies and kids. Nothing is aimed at a 36-year-old furnishing two homes.
- The top banner claims "686,267 people are shopping Black Friday now." I don't buy it. It reads like fake social proof.
- The email is very long. The things that matter, like the rewards card, the Design Desk and the store locator, are buried at the bottom.
- The $2,700 prize giveaway and the "download the app" block dilute the sale message.
- The "we love these for you" tiles aren't personalized to me. They show a stroller, a baby chair and a toy.
- No swatch or sample program is visible, and nothing says anything about color palette consistency.
- This is the sixth C&B-family send in two days. Pottery Barn and Article repeat creative too, but at least their cadence is a bit slower.

- 1. Segment by household. Send a customer who has bought adult furniture a Crate & Barrel main-line mailer, not the Kids arm.
- 2. Cut the length by half. Move the Design Desk and rewards card up to the top third.
- 3. Replace the live-shopper counter with a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT."
- 4. Put one hero product with a price and a "ships free" tag ahead of the grid.
- 5. Cap how often the family sends to one person per day. Wayfair at least keeps one clear tile for one clear subject, and CB2 sends one oxblood hero instead of six sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink / Oracle-style template with 82 click-redirect links, many images that QA flags as missing alt text, and no `List-Unsubscribe` or `Authentication-Results` headers in the captured data. QA scored it 57% pass, with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The HTML is truncated, so body-level checks (footer, unsubscribe link, UTM params) could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** 82 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` beacons (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp) fire on open. They are extra third-party requests and a privacy-disclosure consideration. The `n=1..5` sequence suggests repeated syncs.
- **First-party tracking.**
  - Six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` images.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`.
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/41c690cc-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
  - Each carries a per-recipient `mi_u` hash in the URL, which is an identifier embedded in image requests.
- **Stale asset reference.** `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif` is a Scene7 path from the October (`10%5FKids/2026%5F1002`) campaign, reused in a Nov 26 send. It may be intentional, but it points at an older asset path.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 40 images.** QA flags these image types:
  - Hero and content images, e.g. `2026%5F1126%5FCBK%5FHappyThanksgiving%5F03.gif`, `78b594c4-….jpg`, `4949d5f7-….jpg`, and about 20 more `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` files.
  - Animated GIFs: `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif` and `9c5d35f4-….gif`.
  - The spacer `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - Tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `mi.`, `dv.`).
  - Content images with no alt are inaccessible to screen readers and show nothing when images are blocked. For pixels and spacers, `alt=""` is correct. QA can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one, so the pixel and spacer hits may be false positives.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Generic `<title>`.** It reads `Crate & Barrel` with an unescaped `&` (`&amp;` is the valid form) and doesn't match the subject.
- **Minor markup issues.**
  - There are three duplicate `format-detection` meta tags: one combined, plus separate `telephone`, `address` and `date`.
  - A stray `<!--V2-->` comment sits before `<html>`.
- **Mobile layout.** Breakpoints use `max-width:460px`, which is narrower than the common 480px and 600px thresholds. Devices at 461–600px fall back to the desktop layout.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source. The only personalization is the `mi_u` hash in image URLs. Remaining source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). Without it, one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, so alignment is plausible but unverified.
- **Caveat on all three.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so they may not be real defects. Verify them against the raw message.
- **Footer not auditable.** The physical address and body unsubscribe link fall beyond the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. Links are click-redirect URLs that were not probed, and the visible source has no UTM data. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values on the `dv.` tracker are the only campaign identifiers seen.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, especially hero GIFs and product tiles. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results to confirm or clear the three compliance warnings.
4. Resolve a sample of the 82 redirect links and confirm the destination URLs and UTM parameters.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link and merge tokens.
6. Fix the `<title>` (escape the `&`, match the campaign) and remove the duplicate `format-detection` tags.
