## What's weak

- The send is almost entirely baking and kitchen, so nothing in the hero speaks to renovation or furniture.
- The 30% off furniture offer is buried in a tiny strip and then contradicted by an hour of cookie scrolling.
- Six recipes, each with its own button, is too many equal-weight CTAs, so nothing stands out as the one thing to click.
- The Crate & Barrel Kids block appeared yet again. I've had that brand in my inbox since 11/1 and I don't have a kid.
- The email runs long, and I only reach the "we love these" product row and the app promo after a lot of scrolling.

- 2. Make the offer part of the hero. If 30% off accent chairs and tables is the real news, put it at the top, not in a strip.
- 3. Cut it to three recipes plus one "shop the baking edit" button.
- 4. Drop the Kids module for anyone with no kids-category history.
- 5. Add a rewards or card-math line, such as the Crate & Barrel card perk for holiday orders. I pay with the highest-rewards card, so that's what would tip me over.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 37 images** (QA listing, which was itself truncated):
  - 23 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`, e.g. `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`.

  - `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` beacon.

- **Duplicate and redundant markup:**
  - Two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `meta charset`).
