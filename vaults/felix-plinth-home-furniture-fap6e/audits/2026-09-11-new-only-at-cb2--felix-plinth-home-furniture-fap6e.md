# NEW & ONLY AT CB2
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually stopped my scroll for a second — "NEW TO COVET" as the hero, no countdown clock, no "ENDS TONIGHT" screaming at me. After watching CB2 tick that same timer down from "00:17:19:18" to "00:07:30:24" across three straight sends this week, a clean editorial hero with a chunky wood accent table feels like a different brand entirely. This is the CB2 I actually subscribed for — new collection, custom sectionals, lighting, rugs — not the fifth "hours left" panic email.
- That said, it's a LOT of scroll. Custom sectionals, accent chairs, lighting, rugs, decor, bestsellers by room, a fall sale banner buried two-thirds down, then an app download pitch, then a "more for you" product grid, then a rewards footer. It reads like four separate emails Frankensteined into one. I'd scroll past the first three modules on my phone commute and probably bail before I hit "FALL SALE UP TO 30% OFF," which honestly should've been way higher if that's the real hook.
- Would I click? Maybe on "CUSTOM IS THE ULTIMATE LUXURY" — that green sectional actually looks like something for the primary home reno. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read and maybe come back for the sale banner specifically? Yeah, that's the honest answer.

## What's working

- The "NEW TO COVET" hero photography is genuinely nice — warm, moody, showroom-quality, not another stock white-box render.
- No countdown clock, no fake urgency — a real break from CB2's own cadence this week (compare to 2026-09-09's "HOURS LEFT FOR LABOR DAY SALE").
- "Best Sellers You Can Only Find at CB2" with room-category tiles (Accent Furniture, Bedroom, Dining Room) is useful wayfinding I don't get from Pottery Barn's plaid-lifestyle send.
- Rewards footer ("EARN 10% BACK," "GET 15% OFF") is visible, which Wayfair's broken-hero gazebo email didn't even bother with.

## What's weak

- Way too long — this is basically CB2's entire catalog crammed into one scroll, unlike Article's tighter "patio season" lifestyle send which stayed focused on one story.
- The Fall Sale offer — the one concrete number, 30% off — is buried past six other modules. That should be at or near the top if it's the real driver.
- No shipping-timeline or lead-time messaging anywhere, which matters a lot when I'm mid-renovation on two houses and trying to plan around delivery windows.
- App-download pitch feels like a non-sequitur wedged between furniture and more furniture — CB2 hasn't earned that real estate from me yet.
- Zero swatch/sample-program mention despite an entire module about "Color Is the World" — that's the exact moment to plug fabric swatches and they skipped it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Fall Sale banner (30% off) at the very top, then follow with the "New to Covet" editorial story — right now the strongest offer is the last thing I'd see before giving up.
- 2. Cut this to 2-3 modules max for the promotional version; split "New Collection" editorial and "Bestsellers" into a separate send instead of one mega-scroll.
- 3. Add a one-line shipping/lead-time note near the sectional and bed modules — that's the CB2-specific friction I actually worry about before I click "Shop Now" on big furniture.
- 4. Turn "Color Is the World" into an actual swatch-order CTA, not just a mood shot.
- 5. Move the app download and rewards footer into a dedicated lifecycle email, not stapled to a product drop.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email is a well-formed MSO/Outlook-hybrid HTML template with functioning tracking infrastructure, but ships one unrendered merge token and multiple legacy ad-network (Dotomi) pixels served over plain HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 tracking/click-redirect links present but skipped by automated HTTP probe (redirect domains not resolvable via static probe) — not confirmed broken, just unverified.
- Legacy Dotomi ad pixels served over `http://` (non-TLS): `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`, and `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 sequential beacons). Mixed-content HTTP images inside an HTTPS-loaded email may be blocked by mail clients (Gmail/Outlook proxy image loading, some clients silently drop `http://` resources).
- First-party tracking pixel present and HTTPS: `mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/...` — no issues.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 14 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, including 3 content-bearing product/lifestyle images (`cdb98e88...`, `60f54a3d...`, `6e458f4e...` etc.) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing.
- The 10 Dotomi `pub*.php` and 1 `UCMController` beacon are also missing `alt`, but as 0/1px tracking pixels this is cosmetically irrelevant (still flagged by QA as a blanket rule).
- Template includes standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments, `-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets, and mobile media queries at 418px/690px/768px breakpoints — no structural rendering issues detected in source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- [FAIL] Unresolved raw merge token: `%%reviews_skulist%%` — this token rendered literally instead of being replaced with dynamic review/SKU content, meaning at least one module (likely a "customer reviews" or recently-viewed SKU block) will show broken/literal text to recipients.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- [WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in the captured headers — could be a genuine omission or an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving it; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM compliance from this data alone.
- [WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat.
- [WARN] `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — inconclusive on relay vs. sender-side omission.
- Note: these three are relay-side visibility gaps, not confirmed sender violations — flag for header capture at the source (or check raw headers pre-relay) before concluding CB2 is out of compliance.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not verifiable from truncated source/QA data provided — none of the 74 tracking links were probed, and no destination URLs with UTM parameters were included in the excerpt. No issues found based on available evidence (cannot confirm alignment either way).

7. Recommendations
