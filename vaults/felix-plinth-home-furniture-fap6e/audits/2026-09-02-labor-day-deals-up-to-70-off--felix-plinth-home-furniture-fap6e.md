# Labor Day deals UP TO 70% OFF
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Same burgundy banner, same "Up to 70% off," same font — this is the fourth Pottery Barn email in my inbox this week and I've now literally screenshotted it enough times to notice they didn't even change the crop on the hero. I saw this exact "Labor Day Sale / Up to 70% off / 100s of new deals added!" treatment yesterday in "Just in! NEW DEALS UP TO 70% OFF" and the day before in "⭐️ Amazing Labor Day Sale finds ⭐️." At this point my brain doesn't even register it as new information, it's just visual wallpaper.
- That said, the category grid underneath is actually decent for me specifically — bedding, furniture, sofas & chairs, lighting, tabletop & bar, rugs, outdoor, all with real lifestyle shots and a rattan swivel chair that I'd genuinely stop scrolling for. The "Recommended Just for You" section with the platform beds and extendable dining table is closer to what I'm actually shopping for right now between the two houses. But I've seen Pottery Barn's own "FREE design services" email do a better job of standing out from the pack this week by leading with something other than the discount banner.
- Verdict: I'm not clicking off this one. I might glance at the category grid because we still need a dining table for the second house, but the hero itself gets an instant scroll-past. Not forwarding — nothing here is remarkable enough to send to my wife or the group chat. This gets archived, maybe with a mental note to actually go check the dining table on the site directly instead of through the email.

## What's working

- The category grid (bedding, furniture, sofas & chairs, lighting, outdoor) is genuinely relevant to a two-house renovation and gives me real product shots, not stock photography.
- "Recommended Just for You" with the Cayman Platform Bed and Banks Extendable Dining Table feels like it's actually pulling from browsing history, not just generic bestsellers.
- The "Earn 10% Back in Rewards" credit card callout at the bottom is the kind of rewards math I actually care about since I run everything through my highest-rewards card.
- Clean, readable layout on mobile — no overlapping text, no broken images, tap targets on "SHOP NOW" are big enough.

## What's weak

- Fourth near-identical "Up to 70% off" burgundy hero this week — I'm now pattern-blind to it, same complaint I had about "⭐️ Amazing Labor Day Sale finds ⭐️" and "Just in! NEW DEALS UP TO 70% OFF."
- The Halloween Shop insert ("ENTER IF YOU DARE") crammed between the sale grid and "Discover New Arrivals" feels like three different emails stapled together — no seasonal focus.
- "Up to 70% off" with no specific category or dollar amount is the same vague framing CB2 and Crate & Barrel keep leaning on too — Article's "Up to 40% off Labor Day Sale" this week at least felt like a real number I could act on.
- No swatch/sample program or fabric/finish info visible anywhere, which matters a lot when I'm actually furnishing two houses and need to know if the oak on that dining table matches what's already in my primary.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the repeat-hero problem — rotate in the design-services angle or a specific category spotlight instead of the fourth "Up to 70% off" banner this week. That's the single biggest lift since fatigue is clearly setting in.
- 2. Lead with the "Recommended Just for You" section instead of burying it below seven generic category tiles — that's the part actually tailored to me.
- 3. Cut or relocate the Halloween Shop insert; it breaks the renovation-shopping mindset mid-scroll.
- 4. Add a swatch/finish note or "matches your recent order" tag near the furniture recs — I'm juggling two houses and need continuity cues, not just pretty photos.
- 5. Put a real number in the subject line instead of "up to 70%."
