# LAST WEEK TO SHOP
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "End of Summer Sale" from CB2 — again. This is the fourth CB2/Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox this week if I'm counting right (I audited the Floor Sample Sale and Onyx & Walnut ones just yesterday and the day before). The hero shot is genuinely nice — that stone villa terrace with the pool and dusty mountains in the background has real travel-brochure energy, way better than the flat product-on-white stuff most of these brands lean on. But "Up to 40% off" with a dagger symbol immediately makes me squint for the asterisk, and by the time I hit the app-download banner and the "win $1,000" review sweepstakes stacked on top of a loyalty pitch and a text-signup pitch, I'm scrolling past on autopilot.
- This is a mark-as-read. Nothing here is urgent or specific enough to make me click, and I've already seen three near-identical CB2/C&B sends this week hitting the same "everything's on sale" beat. Wayfair's hall tree email yesterday at least led with an actual product I was shopping for; this one leads with a building.

## What's working

- The hero image is legitimately good — moody, aspirational, looks like something I'd screenshot for the renovation mood board, not just inventory-on-white.
- The category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) is clean and scannable, and that lighting shot with the marble table lamp actually caught my eye given I've got fixtures on my mind for both houses right now.
- Two clear "SHOP THE SALE" CTAs, one right under the hero and one after the category grid — I don't have to hunt for the button.
- Loyalty math is spelled out plainly ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars") which is more transparent than most of these emails bother to be.

## What's weak

- "Up to 40% off†" is doing a lot of lifting with that dagger — no rug, decor, or lighting-specific price band anywhere in the body, so I have no idea what's actually 40% off versus 10%.
- This is the same "storewide sale" format CB2/C&B has run all week — Floor Sample Sale, Onyx & Walnut, the standalone End of Summer Sale two days ago. The subject line difference is basically nothing.
- The bottom third is cluttered — app download, $1,000 review sweepstakes, rewards pitch, "get started" gifting pitch, and a 15% text-signup offer all crammed into one scroll. It reads like four different marketing teams each got their box checked.
- No swatch/sample program mention and no shipping-timeline callout for furniture, which is exactly the kind of practical info I actually need before I click "shop" on a sectional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number next to each category instead of one blanket "up to 40%" — even "Rugs: 30% off" under that tile would beat the vague dagger-footnote approach and match what CB2 did better in the Onyx & Walnut send.
- 2. Cut the bottom-of-email pileup to one offer, not four — app download, sweepstakes, rewards, and text signup competing for the same real estate kills all of them.
- 3. Add a shipping/delivery-window line near the furniture tile — for someone mid-renovation, "arrives in 2 weeks" or "in-stock, ships now" is the actual decision-maker, not the discount.
- 4. Swap the villa-terrace hero for a styled room shot that ties back to a specific in-stock item, so the click lands somewhere with a price instead of a general sale page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs & lighting, up to 40% off — 5 days left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `End of summer: your room, 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on furniture, lighting, rugs, and decor — through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The sectional you've been eyeing just dropped in price.`

# Technical Audit — CB2 "LAST WEEK TO SHOP"

**1. Technical Summary**
Email is a standard MSO/Outlook-conditional HTML template served from mail.cb2.com with heavy third-party ad-tracking pixels; primary compliance risk is unverifiable unsubscribe/authentication headers and several insecure (HTTP) tracking calls.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains not resolvable via static check) — link destinations are unverified and should be manually clicked through to confirm they don't 404 or loop.
- Multiple third-party ad pixels load over plain HTTP, not HTTPS:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 identical-pattern pixels)
  - Non-HTTPS image sources are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Gmail image proxy, Outlook), meaning these tracking pixels likely fail to fire for a meaningful share of opens.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 8 content/product images missing `alt` text (e.g. `cca6653a-b292-4520-b564-8b8d6fe676dc.jpg`, `16bc5205-...jpg`, `8add8946-...jpg`, `18eae94a-...jpg`, `b9eeca94-...jpg`, `d15f6a9d-...jpg`, `c9787622-...jpg`, `b3987305-...jpg`) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing.
- The 10 dotomi tracking pixels and the UCM controller pixel also lack alt text, but as 1x1 tracking pixels this is not user-facing (low priority vs. content images above).
- Template includes standard MSO conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media` breakpoints at 418px/690px/768px — no structural rendering defects detected in the visible markup.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge tokens or personalization fields (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{}}`) are visible in the truncated source. No issues found based on available markup.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe is present (may be a relay artifact rather than a true absence; flag for manual header inspection at the source ESP).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — one-click unsubscribe compliance (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules since Feb 2024) cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — sending domain authentication status is unverified via this relay.
- Note: all three warnings are qualified by the QA tool itself as possibly a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed absence — recommend verifying via raw headers from an inbox provider (Gmail "Show original") rather than treating as confirmed failures.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the probe, so destination UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified in this pass. No confirmed issues, but this category is effectively untested — flag as a gap, not a pass.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Migrate the 11 HTTP-only dotomi/UCM tracking pixels to HTTPS to prevent silent tracking failure in modern mail clients.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images for accessibility and text-only/blocked-image fallback.
3. Manually pull raw email headers (Gmail "Show original" or equivalent) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually present — current WARNs may be a relay-capture gap, not a real compliance failure.
