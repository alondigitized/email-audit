## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was that this is a broken email. The hero is a giant empty white box. Every tile underneath is a grey placeholder with blue link text where the photo should be. I'm looking at "10' W x 10' D pergolas »" in a serif font on an empty lavender square. Nobody opens this and gets excited.
- It's also repetitive. On 9/29 I got "Best-selling gazebos," and this one lists "Hardtop gazebos," "On sale now: gazebos" and "Gazebos & pergolas from $100." That's the same category four or five times in one send. I'm renovating two houses and I'm not buying a pergola. Add the 72-hour clearout cadence I've been getting since 9/26 and this is more noise from Wayfair. I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it.
- Pottery Barn's Big Daddy's Antiques email at least gave me a mood and a point of view. Crate & Barrel's "host like a pro" email was a magazine spread I'd save to my Pinterest-adjacent folder. This one gave me nothing I could save.

## What's working

- The Wayfair wordmark and purple header make it obvious who sent it, even with the images dead.
- "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" is one concrete price point. It's the only offer I can read.
- Some tiles do match my renovation project: "Our Best Peel & Stick Kitchen Tile," "Our Best Refrigerator Deals" and "Find the Perfect Fire Pit."
- The footer has Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing and Wayfair Professional. Rewards math matters to me, and that's the only place I saw it.

## What's weak

- The hero image is blank white and takes up the whole top of the screen. I never see an offer, a product or a CTA.
- Every tile is a broken image with alt text in default serif blue. It looks like a 2004 webpage, not a brand I trust.
- There are about 18 tiles and no hierarchy. Pergolas, refrigerators, frying pans, birdbaths and blankets all get equal weight. This is a product dump, not a campaign.
- Gazebos and pergolas show up in at least five tiles. This is the same lamp-in-three-brands problem, just inside one email.
- There's no deadline, no member pricing and no shipping info. For big furniture, shipping and delivery windows are exactly what I want to see.
- I can't tell what "from $100" is, or whether it's actually a deal. This is the brand that's run "72-HOUR CLEAROUT" four times this week.

- 1. Fix the image rendering and add proper alt text and a fallback background color. If the hero doesn't load, the email is dead, so the headline and CTA should be live text, not baked into an image.
- 2. Cut it to one hero and one button, like "Shop pergolas from $100," with real dates on the offer. Drop the 18-tile "but wait, there's more" grid.
- 3. Pick a single category per send. Gazebos were already the 9/29 send, so don't repeat them. Space out the clearout framing so "ends tonight" means something again.
- 4. Put Wayfair Rewards or card-linked savings near the top. Show a delivery timeline for large items.

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "10' W x 10' D pergolas"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block. The main defects are missing alt text on linked images, about 20 plain-HTTP tracking pixels, and an HTTP fallback URL in the app deep link. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed here. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 probed links returned 429 (rate limiting), which the QA tool itself marks as "may be valid". Affected links:
  - the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app link, twice
  - `/daily-sales`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/find-the-perfect-fire-pit-se19079.html`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/10%27-w-x-10%27-d-pergolas--se82167.html`
  - the `wayfair.com/` homepage link
- **4 links were not checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out. Their status is unknown, not a pass:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/outdoor-fireplaces-by-real-flame-se7`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se8`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-birdbaths-se26208.html?`
  - `/wayfaircard?_emr=…`
  - The first three URLs appear cut off in the QA report (for example `…-se7`, `…-se26208.html?`), so this may be a truncation in the report rather than in the email. Verify against the full source.
- **Malformed image URL:** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-…/0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is invalid percent-encoding (`%` must be followed by two hex digits). Strict parsers or CDNs may reject it. It was not probed, so a render failure is possible but unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=d44e6c92-…` pixels load over plain HTTP. These may be blocked, mixed-content flagged, or stripped by clients, which would undercount impressions.
- **Insecure fallback in the app link:** the `af_web_dp` parameter on the onelink URL is `http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback starts on HTTP and relies on a redirect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images with no accessible name.** The QA tool reports their link text as `[image: filename]`:
  - `0929_BigFurnitureSale_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` links to `/daily-sales`.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png` links to `/`.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png` is also missing alt text.
  - Screen readers will read the filename or the raw URL. If images are blocked, the link is empty.
- **More missing alt text:**
  - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`
  - `WFCC2%402x.png`
  - `WFCC%202%20Disclosure.png` (the credit card disclosure image)
  - The disclosure is legal text rendered as an image with no text alternative, which is both an accessibility problem and a content-loss problem when images are off.
- **Tracking images without alt:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php?…` (the open pixel) and the 20 `imp` pixels have no alt attribute. Add `alt=""` and set explicit `width`/`height` of 1–2px, so they are skipped by assistive tech and don't show broken-image icons.
- The responsive CSS is reasonable, with the `u ~ div` Gmail fix and `body[data-outlook-cycle]` handling for Outlook. No rendering defects are confirmed in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or in the link text. Per-recipient `_eml` and `_emr` values are populated on every link, and `_emr=d44e6c92-…` is consistent throughout.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed because the relay removes this header. The envelope is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, which is a subdomain of the brand domain.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none was reported as failing. The footer is outside the truncated HTML, so the postal address and in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=13`, `varid=2`, `csnid=…`, `sltid=…`, `refid=MKTEML_121343`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16381`. That works inside Wayfair's stack, but it won't register in standard GA UTM reporting without mapping.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the app link uses `c=Triggered` while the other parameters mark the email as a `graymatter_batch` send. This is a promotional batch, so AppsFlyer will attribute it to the wrong campaign type.
- **Landing alignment:** the subject and the `10' W x 10' D pergolas` link point to `/daily-sales/filters/10%27-w-x-10%27-d-pergolas--se82167.html?eid=603462`, which matches the subject. Its 429 response is not evidence of a mismatch. The hero image and `Shop Sale` link go to the generic `/daily-sales` page rather than a specific landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels, and the `af_web_dp` fallback, over HTTPS.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to every linked content image, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels. Put the WFCC disclosure text in live HTML or in a full alt.
3. Fix the `%oo` encoding in `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, and confirm the image loads.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw sent message, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in headers captured before the relay.
5. Re-probe the 4 unchecked links and the 429'd links with a slower rate or a retry, and compare against the full, untruncated URLs.
6. Correct `c=Triggered` on the onelink URL to match the batch campaign, and add `utm_*` or a mapping to standard analytics.
7. Re-run QA on the full HTML to check the footer address, the unsubscribe link and any hidden preheader.
