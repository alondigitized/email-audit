# Bright ideas: Lighting upgrades
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "On the Bright Side" — this is Pottery Barn's eleventh email to me this week, and it's a lighting roundup organized room-by-room: table lamps for the bedroom, floor lamps for the living room, pendants for the dining room, sconces for the bathroom. I flagged their actual lamp email yesterday ("New lamps for a little extra light✨") and this is basically the same category wearing a different structure — instead of one hero product grouping, it's now a full-house lighting tour.
- The room-by-room format is genuinely useful for someone renovating two houses at once — I can mentally slot "Payton floor lamp" into the primary's living room and "Elizabeth pendant" into the second home's dining nook without much friction. But there's zero offer anywhere in this email. No percentage off, no free shipping threshold, nothing — just "shop the look" links stacked five sections deep, then a sweepstakes teaser, then a Halloween shop pivot, then a credit card rewards pitch at the bottom. It reads like a catalog scroll, not a campaign.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I'm actually in the market for sconces this week — the bathroom vanity shot with the Palmer/Hayden sconces is clean and the mirrors-plus-vanity styling sells the "shop the look" idea well. But I wouldn't forward this to a friend; there's nothing surprising or offer-driven enough to share. I'd scroll past into the archive folder.

## What's working

- The room-by-room structure (bedroom → living room → dining room → bathroom) matches how I'm actually shopping mid-renovation, house by house.
- The bathroom vanity "Shop the Look" shot is the strongest visual in the email — warm wood vanity, twin mirrors, sconces flanking them, feels like a real room I'd want.
- Product names and prices-free "swatch-style" thumbnails at the bottom (Bailey dining table, Remmy swivel chair) give me quick browsing without forcing me into a subcategory page.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, no broken image boxes like the blank Wayfair bookcase email I flagged.

## What's weak

- No offer, discount, or urgency anywhere in the lighting section — after CB2's "Now & Future Classics on Sale" hit me with an actual percentage, this feels flat by comparison.
- Five distinct "Shop the Look" CTAs before you even reach a real call to action is decision fatigue, not hierarchy.
- The email tries to do three things at once — lighting catalog, Design Crew sweepstakes, Halloween Shop — and none of them land because it's stitched together instead of committed to.
- This is the same lamps I already saw yesterday, just resliced by room. My brain has officially entered Pottery Barn snooze mode, same as my CB2/Crate & Barrel fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lane and put a real offer behind it — "20% off lighting this week" would do more for click-through than five product groupings ever will.
- 2. Cut the Halloween Shop and Design Crew sweepstakes blocks out of a lighting email entirely — they dilute the one message this send could actually own.
- 3. Lead with the bathroom vanity shot — it's the best styled image in the whole send — instead of burying it fourth.
- 4. Consolidate the five "Shop the Look" CTAs into one clear "Shop All Lighting" button up top, matching the one that already exists lower in the email.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Room-by-room lighting refresh (starts here)`
