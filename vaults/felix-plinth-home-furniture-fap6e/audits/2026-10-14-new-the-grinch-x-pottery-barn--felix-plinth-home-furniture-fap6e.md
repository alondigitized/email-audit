## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a hidden preheader and ESP click-tracking (`click.e.potterybarn.com`). The main problems are widespread missing alt text, three `http://` image sources, and unverified compliance and authentication headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 51 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). Their destinations are unverified, so QA shows no confirmed broken links.
- The recipient address (`lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app`) appears in plain text in image and pixel URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- Third-party tracking pixels: `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...` and `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/abe30e52-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`.
- The `svopn` pixel loads over `http://`, so it may be blocked or stripped. Open tracking would then undercount.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set. It has no effect in most email clients.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images flagged, including:
  - Hero slices: `H26_Collab_B2_Grinch/slices_01.gif` through `slices_09.jpg`
  - Product images: `banks-extendable-dining-table-76-128-1-l.jpg`, `remmy-swivel-chair-1-l.jpg`, `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `reeva-handwoven-rug-9-l.jpg`
  - Banners: `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg`, `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`
  - 18 footer slices: `Email-Footer/slices_01.jpg` through `slices_18.jpg`
  - With images off, or in a screen reader, the Grinch campaign content is effectively invisible.
- **Alt-text exceptions:**
  - The nav image does have `alt="POTTERY BARN"`.
  - The 1x1 tracking pixels and `spacer_0.gif` are decorative, so they only need `alt=""`. The QA tool lists the pixels with a garbled alt ("1", or the URL).
- **Non-HTTPS images:** `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg` and `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`. These may be blocked or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Empty `<title>`.**
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** a `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: ...")`. It is not valid CSS and could break parsing of the rules that follow it.
- **Cross-brand asset:** `https://edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a West Elm-hosted spacer inside a Pottery Barn email.
- **Image-slice layout:** the email is built from image slices (`slices_01`–`09`, footer `01`–`18`), so content depends on images loading. The `img {max-width: 650px}` media query is for desktop.
- **Mobile-width rule:** `.showmobile` sets a fixed `width:414px` inside a 650px layout.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>`.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) are visible in the supplied source. The recipient email is correctly substituted into the `coreemail.html` and `svopn` URLs. The source was truncated, so the rest of the body was not checked.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe` header not found. QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- `[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- `[WARN]` `Authentication-Results` not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the relay.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified in the truncated source.
- The sender domain `e.potterybarn.com` and the tracking domains `click.e.`, `image.e.` and `clickcp.` are consistent subdomains.
- The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (10,095 of 14,260 characters). That is a spam-filter risk and a poor plain-text experience.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destinations are hidden behind `click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...` tokens, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified from the source or QA.
- Product images point to `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...` (for example, `banks-extendable-dining-table`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table`). Whether those products appear in the Grinch campaign or are generic "new arrivals" is unverifiable.
- The 2026 asset paths (`F26_Top_NewArrivals+InStock`, `H26_Collab_B2_Grinch`) match the Grinch subject line.
- Resolving the redirects would be needed to confirm landing pages and UTMs.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image. That means the Grinch hero slices, product images and footer slices. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Switch the three `http://` assets, including the `svopn` pixel, to `https://`.
3. Remove the stray PHP `header(...)` lines from the `<style>` block.
4. Add `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original send, bypassing the relay. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.potterybarn.com`.
6. Follow the click redirects to verify final URLs, UTMs and landing pages. Skipping 51 links leaves a coverage gap.
7. Avoid putting the plaintext recipient email in query strings on pixel and image URLs. Hash it or use an opaque ID.
8. Improve the plain-text part with more readable text and fewer raw URLs.
9. Replace the West Elm-hosted spacer with a Pottery Barn asset.
