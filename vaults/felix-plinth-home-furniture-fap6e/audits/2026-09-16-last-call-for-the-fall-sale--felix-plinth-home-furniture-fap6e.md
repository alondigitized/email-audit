# Last call for the Fall Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Ends Today" and "Fall 30% Off" plastered huge over a couch — this is CB2 finally, finally just leading with the deadline and the number, no designer name-drop, no "Things of Quality" wordplay to decode first. I called out two days ago that "The Fall Sale is over tomorrow" was the version of CB2 that actually gets me to click, and this is basically that email again, just one day closer to the wire. My brain registers it as the third CB2/Crate family send this week doing some version of "sale ends soon," so there's fatigue creeping in even though the execution here is clean.
- The category buttons — Furniture, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Tabletop, Shop the Sale — are honestly the smart part. I'm not hunting through a wall of product cards like the "Things of Quality" send made me do; I can jump straight to Rugs since that's an active decision for me right now (Pottery Barn's jute rug email is still sitting in my queue for the same reason).
- Would I click? Yeah, probably Rugs or Shop the Sale, mostly out of "last chance" instinct. Forward to a friend? No — this is routine sale mechanics, not the kind of designer-collab or room-tour content I'd actually share. Mark as read after a scroll, most likely.

## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" banner up top is unambiguous urgency — no guessing when this expires.
- "Up to 30% off" stacked right into the hero is the clearest single offer CB2's run in any of the sends I've seen this week.
- The category button stack (Furniture / Rugs / Decor & Mirrors / Tabletop) beats the single-CTA laziness of Wayfair's "we bet will sell out" email, which never even names a product.
- 10% back in Rewards Dollars callout is relevant — I've got a rewards card habit and CB2 ties it to Crate & Barrel too, so that's real cross-brand value for me.

## What's weak

- This is déjà vu from "The Fall Sale is over tomorrow" two days ago — same beige couch aesthetic, same deadline structure, just tightened from "tomorrow" to "today." That's the fourth CB2/Crate & Barrel-family email in my inbox this week.
- The hero image is just a couch cushion close-up — no room styling, no context for how this fits a space, which is weaker than Article's "Home tour: A welcoming workspace" that actually showed me a finished room.
- "App download" and "text signup for 15% off" blocks below the fold feel like filler — I've got the app already flagged in a prior CB2 email, this isn't new.
- No mention of showroom pickup or delivery timing for furniture, which matters a lot when I'm renovating two houses at once and need to know if a couch actually arrives before a deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't run "ends tomorrow" and "ends today" as near-identical sends 48 hours apart — collapse into one urgency arc or add new visual content (a styled room, not just fabric texture) so it doesn't read as a rerun.
- 2. Move the category buttons above or into the hero fold on mobile — right now they're solid but come after a big empty scroll of just "FALL SALE" typography.
- 3. Add furniture-specific shipping/delivery window info near the CTA — for someone furnishing two homes, "will it arrive by X" is the actual blocker on clicking "Shop the Sale."
- 4. Swap the couch-cushion hero for a styled room shot so the sale visual actually differentiates from the last CB2 send.
