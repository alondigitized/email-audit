# Up to 40% off faves from KitchenAid, Caraway & more + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the Crate & Barrel send I actually wanted, but it's the same Early Black Friday and 2x Rewards message I've been getting for days. "SALE" is in the sender name, and it's the main brand, not the Kids arm. My kitchen in the primary is next on the renovation list, so a KitchenAid hero with a Le Creuset Dutch oven, Caraway and a De'Longhi espresso machine is on target for me. The "last Double Rewards this year!" banner at the top is the right hook for someone who buys on the highest-rewards card.
- The email is a long stack of brand blocks, though. KitchenAid, Le Creuset, Caraway and De'Longhi each get their own full-width module, then "more can't-miss kitchen deals," then a rerun of Early Black Friday tiles, then stockings, then the Rewards banner again, then "we love these for you," then the app and TheDesignDesk. By the time I'm at stockings I've forgotten what the point was. The first screen works. The rest is the 2x Rewards message repeated.
- I'd click, probably the Le Creuset block or the Caraway block, and I'd save it to my inbox folder. I wouldn't forward it. Nothing in it is new if you've seen the last seven sends.

## What's working

- The hero is a big KitchenAid mixer shot with "up to 40% off top kitchen brands." The offer and the category read in about a second.
- The Rewards banner at the very top is the right place for it. A rewards-first buyer like me sees it before anything else.
- Each brand block gives a concrete "up to $170 off select Le Creuset" or "up to $500 off select De'Longhi." Dollar-off figures are easier to act on than "up to 30%."
- The product tiles show sale price, regular price and "+ Ships Free" lines. That's how I decide, even though the text is small.
- The photography is clean and consistent. Cookware in muted greens and creams fits the palette I'm building across two houses.

## What's weak

- It's far too long. Pottery Barn's "Our top Early Black Friday deals" email at least ranks its deals. This one gives every brand an equal-weight slot, so nothing leads.
- The Early Black Friday and "up to 30% off" tiles repeat offers already shown above. That's the same stacking I complained about in the 11/15 "ON NOW!" send.
- The buttons are mostly a generic "Shop Now" repeated down the page. "Shop All Le Creuset" is the only one that tells me what I'm tapping.
- The stockings block and the holiday decor tile are a left turn after a kitchen-focused top half. I'm not shopping stockings this week.
- The bottom third is app-download and TheDesignDesk promos. That's a lot of footer real estate for things I've seen before. I also see no rewards math, like what 2x actually means on a $400 Dutch oven.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus three brand blocks, and move the stockings and the app promo out entirely. That would help more than any other change.
- 2. Put one line of rewards math under the banner, like "2x on a $400 Le Creuset = $X back." That's what makes me pick this card over the competition.
- 3. Replace the repeated "Shop Now" with "Shop mixers," "Shop Le Creuset," "Shop espresso machines."
- 4. Add a ship-date line for the large appliances. I'm buying for two houses and need to know delivery timing.
- 5. Space out the sends. I've now had eight in about three days, and each one dilutes the next.

- **Subject Alt B:** `2x Rewards ends soon: kitchen faves up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to $500 off De'Longhi espresso machines. Last Double Rewards of the year.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive CSS. The automated QA found 0 hard failures and 4 warnings. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes (53 images flagged, and the list looks truncated) and an unverifiable unsubscribe and authentication posture. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, UTM parameters and landing URLs could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **100 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5`. They load from `rlcdn.com`, a third-party ad-data domain, and carry a hashed per-recipient `s=` value. The same pixel repeated five times adds requests without adding information.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` (7 images) and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`. These are by design, but they mean those images aren't cacheable or shareable across recipients.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (20,156 of 28,625 chars), which is a deliverability-scoring risk. The tracking-heavy link structure is the likely cause.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 53 images.** QA lists 53, and the output appears cut off at the last entry, so the true count may be higher.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`, for example `bf3053a9-…jpg` and `6e5bbd3c-…jpg`.
  - One GIF, `24335f96-…gif`.
  - Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - Tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c9d3dbe1-…`).
- **Pixels need an empty alt.** The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. The content images need descriptive alt text. With images blocked, the email would show broken-image placeholders in the product grid.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` has `maximum-scale=1`, which stops pinch-zoom and is an accessibility problem.
- **Unescaped ampersand in the title.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;`, because the document declares XHTML 1.0 Transitional.
- **Template cruft in the source:**
  - A leftover `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` placeholder comment.
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv` declarations.
  - Three `format-detection` metas, including a redundant `telephone=no` and `address=no`.
  - These are not functional failures but add payload and show an unclean template.
- **Overlapping image-width rules.** `img {max-width:640px}` at `min-width:640px` and `img {max-width:600px}` at `min-width:768px` conflict. The 768px rule wins above 768px, and the 640px rule applies between 640 and 767px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=` values are populated. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but its alignment is unverified.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address** were not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unable to assess. The 100 click-tracking links were skipped by QA and the HTML is truncated, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are not visible. The only parameters seen are `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` on image and tracking URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the content images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Deduplicate the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels (`n=1` to `n=5`) to one.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment and the duplicate meta tags.
5. Reduce the URL share of the plain-text part (currently 70.4%) with shorter or fewer tracked links and more descriptive text.
6. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers, not the AgentMail relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
7. Re-run with the full HTML and resolved tracking links to check the unsubscribe link, postal address, and UTM or landing-page continuity.
