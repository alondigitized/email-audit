- The "Open Box Deals" banner is the biggest element on the page. It has no price and no product, and it fights the subject.
- Pergolas and gazebos are in the hero again. That's a duplicate of the 11/16 "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" send, and I'm not buying a gazebo.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional, a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block, and per-link tracking IDs. The main confirmed defects are missing alt text on about 27 images and 20 impression pixels served over plaintext `http://`. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because of the capture method, not because the email failed them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 links were probed. All 8 warnings are HTTP 429 rate limits from wayfair.com and onelink.me, which means the bot was throttled. They are not dead links.
- **4 links were never probed** (the 70s budget ran out). These include `/daily-sales/filters/area-rugs-you%27ll-love-se63020.html`, `/daily-sales/filters/gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se8…` and `/daily-sales/clearance?…`. Their status is unknown.
- **20 impression pixels use plaintext HTTP.** The URLs follow `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=a3850974-…`. Some clients may block or rewrite non-HTTPS images, which would undercount ad impressions. There is also a mixed-content risk.
- **Campaign label mismatch on the app link.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me` URL sends `c=Triggered` but also `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labeled as a triggered campaign will skew AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Per-recipient `_eml` values on every link.** The `_emr=a3850974-…` value is the same across links, while `_eml` differs per link. This is expected for link-level tracking. It does mean the URLs can't be cached or deduplicated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images with no alt text, confirmed in the QA data:**
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`

- **Several of those images are linked.** The `blankBanner` image is wrapped in `/daily-sales?…sltid=4` and the `batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3` image in `/?…sltid=5`. With images off, or in a screen reader, those links have no accessible name. The probe logged the link text as `[image: filename]`.
- **Tracking pixels with no alt** (`t.wayfair.com/b.php`, and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp`). This is low severity. They should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech.
- The responsive CSS is present and uses `u + .Gmail` and `body[data-outlook-cycle]` client hooks. The source was truncated, so I did not audit the rest of the markup (dark mode, `lang` attribute, preheader).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output. The tracking IDs are fully rendered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found.** Not confirmed as a sender defect. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Verify against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results`: not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, with the same relay caveat. Nothing here suggests failure.
- The QA output says an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set, and no unsubscribe failure was reported. The footer's physical address and unsubscribe text were not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Wayfair uses its own scheme (`source`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`). That works for first-party analytics. Any GA-based reporting will classify the sessions as direct or unattributed unless it's mapped.
- **The subject-line CTA matches its landing page.** "Kitchen Mats Under $30" goes to `/daily-sales/filters/kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.html?eid=63966`, and the URL slug matches the subject. The 429 prevented checking that the page renders.
- The `%2430` and `%27` encoding in the paths is valid. Check that the `%3a` in `on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.html` resolves. It could not be tested because of the 429.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content and banner images above, especially the linked ones. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Fix the `c=Triggered` campaign label on the onelink.me URL so it matches the batch send (`graymatter_batch`).
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC from raw headers that don't pass through AgentMail.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links and the 8 rate-limited links with slower pacing and retries. Then confirm there are no real 404s or redirect loops.
6. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that downstream analytics maps the proprietary parameters.
