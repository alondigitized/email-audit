## What's working

- The hero copy, "Beautifully Curated Layers," over a moody dark-wood bedroom is on-brand. It looks like the same dark, plaid-and-evergreen world as the 9/30 emails.
- "Bedding ships free" is a concrete, visible perk, even though it's small.
- Each room scene has a named collection and designer credit (Heritage Medallion, Lilah Suzani, Cozy Cloud, Lydia Palampore). I can look those up and note the palette.
- The "Recommended Just for You" grid includes a rug, a sectional chair, a dining table and a bed. That's a sensible mix for someone redoing two houses.
- The Design Services "Book a Free Appointment" block is a real showroom-style hook for a renovator.

## What's weak

- There's no percent-off or dollar offer. Crate & Barrel's 50% off bedroom send and CB2's 25% off send both led with a number.
- It's a very long scroll with six near-identical "Shop the Look" links. I can't tell which one matters, and the tap targets look tiny and low-contrast.
- The "Recommended Just for You" block has captions but no prices. Wayfair at least puts prices on its tiles.
- The Christmas Shop banner, the Free Design Services banner, the app promo and the Affirm block all come after the bedding story. The email loses focus by the bottom.
- The cadence is the problem. I got a lookbook this morning, and the Cotswolds and rug emails from Crate & Barrel landed the same day. A bedroom-only send in the same week is more of the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer in the hero, like "Up to 25% off bedding + free shipping," in large type above or on the image. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the six "Shop the Look" links down to two or three, with one big "Shop All Bedding" button right under the hero.
- 3. Add prices and a swatch or color-palette note to the recommended tiles. Mention fabric samples or free swatches, since I'm matching rooms across two houses.
- 4. Space out the sends. A bedding-only email three days after a holiday lookbook would land better if it were segmented to people who browsed bedding.
- 5. Add Pottery Barn credit card or rewards math near the top. I buy on the highest-rewards card, so the 10% back line is buried.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout with working preheader and Outlook conditionals. It has about 50 images with no alt text, three non-HTTPS image sources, stray PHP code inside a `<style>` block, and the recipient address exposed in plain-text tracking URLs. The List-Unsubscribe and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 60 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.e.potterybarn.com/u/?qs=...`). Destination health is unverified. No broken links were found.
- **Mixed tracking hosts:** Opens and clicks go through several domains:
  - `click.e.potterybarn.com`
  - `clickcp.potterybarn.com/o/88589b75-...?mi_cid=...`
  - `svopn.potterybarn.com`
  - third-party `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/...`
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true` is loaded over HTTP.
- **Third-party pixel:** `d.turn.com` is an ad-network pixel that fires on open.
- **Stray tracking markup:** `coreemail.html?email=...` is rendered as an `<img>`, so it is a tracking/beacon request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 50 images.** Examples:
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_17.jpg` under `/0924_AM_THU_NewBeddingLooksEvergreen/`
  - `slices_01.jpg` to `slices_18.jpg` under `/Email-Footer/`
  - product images such as `cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg` and `reeva-handwoven-rug-3-l.jpg`
  - banners such as `H26_Bottom_ChristmasShop.jpg` and `DesignCrewPrimaryBottomBanner.jpg`

  With images off, or in a screen reader, the message is blank and unreadable because the content is sliced images.
- **HTTP image sources (mixed content, may be blocked):**
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/...`
- **Stray PHP in CSS:** The second `<style>` block contains `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store..."); header("Pragma: no-cache");`. It is invalid CSS and a template-leak artifact. It sits at the end of the block, so the rules before it likely still apply, but some clients' parsers may drop the block.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1 />` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Stale asset:** The footer banner from the `/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/` path suggests a reused 2022 asset.
- **Cross-brand asset host:** `edm.westelm.com/we/evergreen/spacer_0.gif` is a Pottery Barn email loading a spacer from another brand's CDN.
- **Font dependency:** Montserrat is loaded from `fonts.googleapis.com`. Many clients ignore it and fall back, and no fallback stack is visible in the truncated source.
- **Positive:** The preheader is hidden with `display:none` plus `mso-hide:all`, and `role="presentation"` is set on layout tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered tokens:** No `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or similar merge-token failures appear in the visible source.
- **Recipient address in URLs:** The address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` is embedded unencoded in at least two URLs:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `http://svopn.potterybarn.com/?...&sv_sveme=...`

  The second is an HTTP URL, so the address is sent in cleartext. This is PII in query strings.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are not found. The QA note says the relay may not preserve them, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` aligns with the tracking domains.
- **Footer not verifiable:** The HTML is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.
- **Plain-text part:** 10755 of 14958 characters (about 72%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a deliverability and readability risk, caused by the long tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters:** None are visible in the truncated source. Attribution appears to rely on the opaque `qs=` token and on `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters, so UTM continuity can't be verified.
- **Destinations unresolved:** Link destinations weren't resolved because the tracking links were skipped, so landing-page alignment with the "bedding looks" and "ships free" content is unverified.
- **Direct product images:** Product images are served from `www.potterybarn.com/pbimgs/...`, the same domain as the site. That is fine.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Prioritize the 17-slice body and the footer slices.
2. Move the three `http://` assets to HTTPS, especially `svopn.potterybarn.com`, and replace the 2022 footer banner.
3. Remove the PHP `header(...)` lines from the second `<style>` block, fill in `<title>`, and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Stop passing the plain-text email address in query strings. Use a hashed or tokenized ID, and never over HTTP.
5. Host the spacer on the sender's own CDN instead of `edm.westelm.com`.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message before treating them as defects.
7. Resolve a sample of the 60 redirect links to confirm destinations and check for UTM or equivalent campaign tagging.
8. Shorten tracking URLs, or put more visible text in the plain-text part, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
