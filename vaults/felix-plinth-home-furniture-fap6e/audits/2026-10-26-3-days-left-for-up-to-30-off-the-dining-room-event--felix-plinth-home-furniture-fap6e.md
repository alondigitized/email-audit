# 3 DAYS LEFT for up to 30% off The Dining Room Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- The dining room hero finally sells me something. A round pedestal table with chairs sits under "up to 30% off," and it's the first thing I see. I'm furnishing two houses, so a dining event is on target. I just don't feel any urgency. The subject says "3 DAYS LEFT," but I can't find a deadline date anywhere in the body I can read. The hero tagline is a Thanksgiving framing, and the email then runs on for miles.
- It's another Crate & Barrel send in a week where I've logged about six. The Dining Room Event is the right beat for me. But the Thanksgiving Hosting Guide, two TheDesignDesk blocks, a $5000 sweepstakes, a Rewards Dollars card and an app download all stack up below it. That's the "everything on one page" problem CB2 had with "STEALS OF THE WEEK." I'd click once, on the dining tables tile. I wouldn't forward it. This is for me, not for a friend.

## What's working

- The hero is a real dining set with "up to 30% off" in big type. I get the offer in a second.
- The four-tile grid splits the offer by category: tables, seating, storage and lighting. I can jump straight to what I'm shopping for.
- The lighting tile at 20% off is a nice surprise. Chandeliers are on my list.
- The "we love these for you" product row shows actual prices. That's more useful than the last few C&B sends.
- The TheDesignDesk free design appointment ("no purchase required") fits my two-house renovation.

## What's weak

- The "3 DAYS LEFT" urgency from the subject never shows up as a visible end date, countdown or banner in the body.
- Six CTAs sit in the first third. "Shop Now," "Shop Thanksgiving," "Shop The Dining Furniture Event" and "Shop All Dining Furniture" all say nearly the same thing.
- TheDesignDesk appears three times, with two different "Book a Free Appointment" blocks and a sweepstakes between them. It's repetitive.
- The "refresh your home" sweepstakes and the app giveaway are both $5000 chances. That's noise stacked on a sale email.
- The cookware and air fryer in the product row aren't dining room furniture. They dilute the event.
- The body text and legal fine print are tiny on my phone. I'd have to zoom to read the offer exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Wed 10/29," and use a countdown. The subject promises urgency the body doesn't deliver.
- 2. Cut the CTAs to one primary "Shop the Dining Event." Fold the Thanksgiving guide into a single secondary link.
- 3. Keep TheDesignDesk to one block and drop the sweepstakes and app promo from a sale send.
- 4. Fill the product row with dining items on sale, showing the was and now prices. Show a sample or swatch option for the wood tones.
- 5. Say plainly what the 30% applies to and whether delivery timing works for Thanksgiving. Furniture shipping dates are what actually decide whether I buy.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard ESP template (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, VML/MSO fallbacks, a mobile breakpoint at 460px) and renders without hard errors, but the QA pass rate is only 57%. The failures are mostly missing `alt` attributes on about 20 content images and the viewport and text-size settings. Compliance headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** QA skipped all 60 tracking links because they use click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests fire per open (LiveRamp). They carry a hashed per-recipient identifier in `s=`. This is not a defect, but it adds five external requests and each pixel lacks `alt`.
- **ESP open pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/639262fa-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are both present, so there are two first-party open trackers.
- **Per-recipient token in image URLs.** Image URLs on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` embed the recipient identifier. They are likely dynamic product recommendations, which means the images are generated per user and may not cache.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (27 images flagged by QA).**
  - About 20 are content images:
    - 12 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `ef861136-…jpg`).
    - 1 on `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide…`.
    - 7 recommendation tiles on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - Screen-reader and images-off users get no content for these. If these tiles carry the offer, product and CTA, those are lost to them.
  - The other 7 are tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, the `dv.` pixel). They should have `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{… -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable user text scaling on iOS. They also conflict with the earlier `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%` rules.
- **Markup hygiene (minor).**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8 />` and a bare `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present, and the latter is unclosed under an XHTML doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - The placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the shipped code.
  - A `<!--V2-->` comment sits before `<html>`.
  - The title is generic rather than the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` appear in the truncated HTML. The tail of the document could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. QA attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer not visible.** The physical address and unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source, so they could not be confirmed.
- **Sender domain.** The visible From is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain of the brand. That is aligned with the image and tracking hosts, which is good.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Could not be assessed. The 60 links were skipped and no final destination URLs or `utm_*` parameters appear in the truncated source. Click tracking appears to be handled by the ESP redirect domain, so UTM presence on the landing page needs a live click-through. The subject promises "up to 30% off The Dining Room Event", and whether the landing page matches it is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (the `image.mail…/m/1/*.jpg` hero and promo tiles, the scene7 image, the `mi…/p/rp/*.png` product tiles). Set `alt=""` on the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. Re-check the headers on the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (spf/dkim/dmarc=pass). Don't treat the WARNs as real failures until then.
4. Run a live click-through on a sample of the 60 tracked links to confirm the final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
5. Clean up the duplicate meta tags, escape `&` in `<title>`, and delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
6. Review the necessity of five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels per open.
