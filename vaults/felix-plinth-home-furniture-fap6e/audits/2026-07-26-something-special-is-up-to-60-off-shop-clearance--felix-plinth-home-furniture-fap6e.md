# Something special is up to 60% off! Shop clearance →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Forty-five. That's my July Crate & Barrel count now, and this one at least earns its open — "up to 60% off clearance" with a beach hero shot is a real offer up top, not another vague "quality favorites" filler send. Clean grid of category tiles (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, lighting, rugs) is honestly the most legible clearance layout I've gotten from this brand all month.
- Then it just... keeps going. Lupita Nyong'o's L.A. home makeover, a $5,000 sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" product rail with six items that have nothing to do with the sale up top, a credit card pitch, an app download push. By the time I hit the card ad I've forgotten what the subject line even promised. This is the same problem CB2 had with "NOW & FUTURE CLASSICS ON SALE" — one email trying to be five emails.
- I'd click into clearance from the hero, then bail before the celebrity content and card offer. Not forwarding this — it's not tight enough to be worth someone else's time, and Article's dining room/living room sends this month were sharper single-purpose pitches than this kitchen-sink approach.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — beach photo, "up to 60% off clearance," one button, no guessing what this email is about.
- The category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding) is genuinely useful for scanning what's actually on sale, better than Pottery Barn's vaguer "Save on Furniture & More" approach.
- "While quantities last" is honest urgency language, not a fake countdown.
- Colors are on-brand and photography is clean — no render bugs, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- The Lupita Nyong'o "home makeover" editorial and $5,000 sweepstakes have zero connection to the clearance offer — I opened this for a discount, not a celebrity design feature.
- "we love these for you" is not personalization, it's a generic product rail — a $3,299 dining table and a $2,199 bed sitting right under a "60% off" hero reads like bait-and-switch on price.
- The new Crate Card pitch buried near the bottom feels like a fifth different email got stapled on.
- This is way too long for a clearance email — by the time I scroll past the sweepstakes I've lost the thread on what to actually shop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — clearance hero, category grid, "Shop All Clearance" CTA, done. Move Lupita Nyong'o, the sweepstakes, and the credit card pitch into their own separate sends.
- 2. If "we love these for you" stays, make it actual clearance items with strikethrough pricing, not full-price furniture that contradicts the 60% off headline.
- 3. Add a real deadline to "While quantities last" — even a soft date would beat CB2's habit of running the same "ends tomorrow" banner on repeat.
- 4. Move the "Shop All Clearance" button up near the hero too, not just at the bottom of the grid — I shouldn't have to scroll past eight tiles to convert.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance markdowns across every room — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rugs, lighting, outdoor, and more — up to 60% off, today only.`
