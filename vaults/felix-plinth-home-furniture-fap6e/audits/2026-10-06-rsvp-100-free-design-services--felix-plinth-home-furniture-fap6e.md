# RSVP: 100% free design services
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Pottery Barn again, and this is the first send this week that offers me something other than a catalog page. "Free design services" is an actual service, and I'm renovating two houses at once, so my attention went to it. The hero is clean: a cream box over a lodge-style room, with a "what / when / where" layout that reads like an invitation. It never says when, though. The "when" line only says "Choose a time—spots go fast!" Spots are limited, but no date is given.
- After that it turns into the usual Pottery Barn stack. There's a Shop by Style bedding block, a class promo, a "Recommended just for you" grid, and then a long footer of app, credit card and Affirm blocks. The "just for you" grid is a curtain rod, ghosts, a bed, a dining table, a curtain and a rug. That tells me nothing about what I've been browsing. I'm on the entryway and bedroom lists, and none of it matches.
- Would I click? Probably "Book a free appointment," but I'd do it later, once I'd sorted out which house needs help first. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it to my inbox folder, because the design-crew service is useful even though the rest is filler.

## What's working

- The hero names one clear thing, free design services, with a dark "Book a free appointment" button that's easy to spot and easy to tap.
- "In your home, virtually, or in-store" fits someone with two houses in different places. That's the logistics answer I wanted.
- The "Shop by style" tiles (rustic lodge, timeless neutrals, coastal, shades of green) help with color palette direction, which matters when I'm matching two houses.
- The Oct 25 10AM guest-ready bedroom class has a specific date and an RSVP button. It's the only concrete time in the email.
- It's less repetitive than 10/5's holiday catalog drop and the 10/4 "Heritage holiday quilts" send. The service pitch is a change of pace.

## What's weak

- The hero never says when. A "when" line without a date or deadline is a miss, and "spots go fast" reads as filler urgency.
- "Recommended just for you" doesn't feel personal. A ghost collection and a curtain rod don't match anything I've saved, and there's no price on any tile.
- Nothing explains how the design service works. I can't tell if it's a consult, a paid plan, or if I'm expected to buy. "100% free" in the subject makes me look for the catch, and it's not answered anywhere in the body.
- The footer is long and heavy: the Pottery Barn credit card at 10% back, Affirm, the app, and a wall of fine print. The card block says "Earn 10% back" with no rewards math, so it doesn't help the way I'd use my highest-rewards card.
- This is the same-day cadence problem. I've had a pile of Pottery Barn sends since 9/29, and this one carries no sign that it knows me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a date and a real offer in the hero. For example, "Book by Oct 15 and get a free room plan within 48 hours." Answer "what do I get?" in one line.
- 2. Show how the service works in three steps: book, meet your designer, get a shopping list with swatches. Include a sample designer board image, since a result photo beats a lodge photo.
- 3. Make the product grid actually personal, using my saved items or recent browsing, and add prices. Pull it up above the app, card and Affirm blocks, and cut those down.
- 4. Add shipping and lead-time info for furniture (Cayman bed, Toscana table). This is the thing that decides whether I buy at all.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific, like "Book my free design consult."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free design help for your next room`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Book a free designer by Oct 15`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet a Pottery Barn designer at home, online, or in-store. Takes 30 minutes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free guest-bedroom class on Oct 25.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an image-sliced layout (about 7 body slices and 18 footer slices) with every link wrapped in Salesforce Marketing Cloud click tracking. Confirmed issues: broken CSS, a media query that matches nothing, no useful alt text on linked images, two `http://` images, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in pixel URLs. Several compliance checks can't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverifiable.** 46 links go through `https://click.e.potterybarn.com/?qs=...` and weren't probed. Their destinations, HTTP status and UTM parameters can't be confirmed.
- **Plaintext email in pixel URLs.** The recipient address `lila-hearth-potterybarn-fap6e@etell.app` appears unhashed in:
  - `https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/coreemail.html?email=...`
  - `https://svopn.potterybarn.com/?sv_cid=0053_00597&sv_emopen=true&sv_sveme=...`
- **Third-party ad pixels.** `secure.leadback.advertising.com/adcedge/lb?site=695501...` and `d.turn.com/r/dd/id/.../cat/1` are retargeting pixels. They add third-party requests and disclose open and recipient data to ad-tech domains.
- **Dead code.** A commented-out credit-card banner block (`...credit-card/?brandCode=POTTERYBARNCB...`) is still in the payload. It's harmless but adds weight.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS.** The second `<style>` block contains stray PHP: `header('Content-Type: image/jpeg'); header("Cache-Control: no-store...")`. The parser reads this text as part of the next selector, so the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` rule is probably dropped. Apple auto-link styling then goes unreset.
- **Dead mobile rules.** The media query targets `table[class="wrapper"]` and `img[class="photo"]`. The visible tables are fixed `width="650"` with no class, and the images have no `class="photo"`. The 320px and 100% overrides never apply, so the layout stays 650px wide on mobile. This is based on truncated source.
- **Alt text.** The linked slices (`slices_01.jpg` to `slices_07.jpg`, plus the footer slices) have `alt=""` or no alt attribute. Each sits inside an `<a>` with no text, so the links have no accessible name. With images blocked, the email renders blank apart from the nav alt "POTTERY BARN". Also missing alt: the product images (`cayman-platform-bed-1-l.jpg`, `toscana-extendable-dining-table-60-1245-1-l.jpg`, etc.), `spacer_0.gif`, and the tracking pixels.
- **Mixed content.** Two images load over HTTP and may be blocked or flagged:
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/thekey/CC_Banner/PB/Foot_NonMem_PB.jpg`
  - `http://edm.potterybarn.com/pb/2022/0311_Key_CC_Email_BrandBanner/slices_02.jpg`
- **Document structure.** The head has no `<title>` and the `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute. The XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype is used with `<center>`, `bgcolor` and `align`, which Strict disallows.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The AMPscript slot is empty, no unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens are visible, and the recipient address resolved in the pixel URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** weren't found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require them.
- **Authentication-Results** wasn't captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.potterybarn.com` matches the click domain, which suggests aligned sending, but that isn't proof.
- **Footer unverified.** The HTML is truncated before the footer text. Because the footer is 18 image slices without alt text, the unsubscribe link and postal address can't be confirmed as live text. If they're image-only, they're inaccessible when images are blocked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverifiable.** All destinations sit behind opaque `qs=` redirects that weren't probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page match can't be checked.
- **Campaign name.** The asset path `1006_TUE_AM2_DesignCrewInviteSBS` matches the send date (Tue, Oct 6).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `header(...)` PHP text from the `<style>` block so the Apple data-detector rule parses.
2. Add `class="wrapper"` and `class="photo"` to the matching elements, or rewrite the media query to match the real markup, so the mobile override applies.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all linked slices and product images, and `alt=""` to decorative spacers and tracking pixels. Add `<title>` and `lang="en"`.
4. Change the two `http://edm.potterybarn.com/...` image URLs to `https://`.
5. Stop putting the plaintext recipient email in pixel query strings. Use an opaque subscriber key or hash instead.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message, not the relay copy. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, and that the unsubscribe link and address are live text.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolved redirect chains to check UTM and landing-page continuity.
