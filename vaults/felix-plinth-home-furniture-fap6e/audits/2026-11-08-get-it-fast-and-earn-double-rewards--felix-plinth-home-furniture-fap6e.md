# Get it fast AND earn Double Rewards? 🤯
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- Seventeenth Crate & Barrel family send in about four days, and this one is from the Kids sender. My gut reaction was "I'm not buying a nursery." The subject promised speed plus Double Rewards, but the hero says "favorites that arrive fast" over a bedroom shot with a wreath, and the actual rewards pitch sits below it. Then I saw "last Double Rewards this year" with a Nov. 17 deadline. That's the only part that matters to me. I'm furnishing two houses and I put everything on the highest-rewards card, so the rewards math is the one thing here I care about.
- The rest is a long scroll of strollers, nursery gliders and playroom storage. The same "last Double Rewards" block shows up twice, once in white and once in a big red band, and there's a third Fall Sales Event banner between them. It's repetitive. Compared to this morning's "Up to 40% (!) off the kitchen upgrades you're eyeing" email, which I scored 8, this one is the weaker send. I would open it for the rewards line, skim, and not click. I wouldn't forward it. If a friend had a baby, maybe. They don't.

## What's working

- "Last Double Rewards this year" with Nov. 17 is a credible deadline, and it's the one message that reaches a rewards-driven buyer like me.
- The hero room shot is gorgeous and on-brand, with a warm, styled bedroom.
- The tiles are clean and consistent, and the "shop rugs" and "shop lighting" tiles are the only ones that apply to my reno.
- The "Shop In-Stock Furniture" button fits the subject's "get it fast" angle and answers my shipping-timeline worry.
- The prices under the product picks are readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- Almost all of it is baby and nursery product: strollers, gliders, baby gear. I'm not the target, and the Kids sender name should have warned me.
- The Double Rewards message repeats in two big blocks, and there's a third promo block ("fall sales event: up to 50% off") between them. Three competing offers, no single hero.
- The red Double Rewards band is a big wall of red, and the button hierarchy there is muddy: "Shop Top Categories" next to "Learn More".
- The legal fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny. On a phone I couldn't read it without zooming.
- The "Fall Sales Event" banner looks stale on Nov. 8, just like the CB2 "Long Weekend Sale" banner that keeps resurfacing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer, Double Rewards through Nov. 17, and cut the duplicate blocks. Say it once, high up, with one clear button.
- 2. Segment by purchase history. I buy furniture, rugs and lighting, so show me those instead of strollers.
- 3. Make the hero match the subject. Put "ships in X days" and the Double Rewards math, such as 2x on a $2,000 order, right under the image.
- 4. Drop the stale Fall Sales banner, or fold it into the single offer.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Sixteen sends in four days across the C&B family trains me to snooze them. Pottery Barn's recent "Up to 60% off furniture made to last" at least gave me a real category.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email loads images from first-party hosts (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com`) and has a conventional table layout. The QA run (57% pass, 0 errors, 3 warnings) found widespread missing alt attributes, several header checks that couldn't be confirmed, and a few HTML hygiene problems. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and merge-token checks are only partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations or redirect chains are verified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a…&n=1` through `&n=5` differ only in `n`.
  - They send a long hex token in `s=` to a third-party sync domain.
  - If that token is a hashed identifier, this is a privacy and consent exposure. I can't confirm what it encodes from the data given.
  - Five calls per open is redundant.
- **Pixel hosts.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times, plus `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/0d62136f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are first-party, but the repeated `mi_u` and `mi_cid` identifiers put per-recipient IDs in image URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 22 content images.** All are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` or `/m/47/`, for example:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `ec997ddd-48db-4d16-bffd-48b5b834d0f5.jpg`
  - `0782bf25-0357-43a5-8ed2-cca28dcd0d82.gif`
  - `8c4ce5d9-cf7b-4fbe-ba17-b9e2435a0e16.gif`

  If these images carry the offers or product names, image-blocked and screen-reader users get no content.
- **Tracking pixels flagged for missing alt.** The `f3c0d…png`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif` and `dv.…` pixels were flagged too. For pixels the correct fix is an explicit `alt=""`. The QA data can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
- **Zoom restriction.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;`, since the doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional.
- **Duplicate and conflicting meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared five times with different values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no` separately). Client behavior is undefined.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It is harmless but sloppy, and it contains a typo.
- **Dead CSS rule.** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`, because both match at ≥768px.
- **Global resets.** `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are broad and can cause unintended inheritance in Outlook and Gmail.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear. Coverage is limited by truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`) matches the image host, which is consistent with aligned sending, but this isn't proven.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link were not in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No destination URLs were visible in the truncated HTML, and the 78 tracking links were skipped. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 22 content images (`/m/1/*.jpg|gif`, `/m/47/*.png`). Set `alt=""` on all pixels (`f3c0d…`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv.…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and collapse the five `format-detection` tags into one.
4. Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment and the dead `max-width: 600px` rule.
5. Reduce the five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels to one, and confirm that `s=` doesn't carry a plaintext or weakly hashed email address.
6. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`. If the relay is stripping them, document that as a known gap rather than a sender defect.
