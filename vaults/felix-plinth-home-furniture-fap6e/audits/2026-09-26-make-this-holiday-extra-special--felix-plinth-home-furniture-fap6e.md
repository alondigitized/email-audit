# Make this holiday extra special!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Crate & Barrel Kids, not the main C&B feed I've been drowning in all week — so at least it's a different logo hitting my inbox for once. But it's the same playbook: personalized stockings, kids' toys, holiday decor, a bunch of soft-focus lifestyle shots of kids in cable-knits by a fireplace. Problem is, I don't have kids. This isn't a targeting miss on the offer — it's a targeting miss on the entire sub-brand. I'm mid-renovation on two houses; I need hall trees, towels, and furniture that survives a contractor walking through in boots, not a personalized stocking with "Leo" embroidered on it.
- There's zero price anywhere in this email — no %, no $, no BOGO, nothing. Compare that to Wayfair's "Hall trees" send, which at least had a real category hook even if half the images were broken, or CB2's vintage reissue email that gave me a point of view I actually wanted to engage with. This is just a wall of pretty toddler photography with "Shop Now" buttons stacked eight times in a row.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — unless it's to my sister who actually has a toddler, and even then only maybe.

## What's working

- The photography is genuinely warm and well-lit — fireplace, stockings, the kid in the apron baking cookies scene has real charm.
- "a stocking just for them" with the personalized name ("Leo") on screen at least signals customization is possible, which is a real gift-giving lever this time of year.
- Consistent seasonal green/cream/plaid palette throughout — no jarring color clashes like I've seen in some Wayfair sends.
- Clean single-column layout, easy to scroll on a phone without pinching to zoom.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere. Not one percentage, dollar amount, or "free shipping" — every other C&B-family email this week (their "up to 60% off" markdowns, twice) at least led with a number. This one leads with nothing.
- Every single CTA button just says "Shop Now" or "Shop All ___" — completely generic, no differentiation in verb or urgency.
- This is Kids-specific merchandise blasted at me with no household/kids signal on file that I can tell — I'm a 36-year-old renovating two adult homes, not shopping for a nursery.
- Eight nearly identical "Shop Now" CTAs stacked down a single scroll is fatigue, not persuasion — by section 4 my thumb's already checked out.
- The travel system / stroller section at the bottom ("Nuna," car seats) is a hard left turn from holiday stockings into baby gear — feels like two different emails duct-taped together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this — if I have no kids-category signal on file, don't send me Crate & Barrel Kids at all. That's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Put an actual offer in the hero — a %, a $, anything. Every other C&B send this cycle led with a number; this one forgot to.
- 3. Vary the CTA copy — "Personalize a Stocking," "Shop Stocking Stuffers," "Build the Toy List" — not eight "Shop Now"s in a row.
- 4. Cut the stroller/travel-system section or move it to its own dedicated send — it breaks the holiday narrative and reads as a bolt-on.
- 5. If this must go to non-parents, reframe as a gifting angle ("shopping for a niece/nephew/grandkid") rather than assuming I'm the parent.
