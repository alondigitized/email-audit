# Up to 50% off even more bestselling furniture!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- It's the eighth or ninth Crate & Barrel-family email in two days, and the sender line says Kids, so my gut said "wrong list again." I'm renovating two houses and I'm not buying cribs. The hero is clean: "new deals added! up to 50% off furniture + free shipping" over a styled bedroom. Then it turns into a nursery pile: a crib, a glider, a changing-table dresser and a baby photo. This is the same "new deals added" beat as this morning's "Exclusive first look: NEW Black Friday deals added!" and "New Black Friday deals added! Open to see…".
- Would I click? Probably not. The Workpace/desk block is the one thing that made me pause, because the Weathered Natural Wood Desk with Hutch is actually in my lane. I wouldn't forward it. I'd file it in the Pinterest folder only if it were from the main Crate & Barrel, not Kids.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 50% off furniture + free shipping." Free shipping on furniture is the line I care about.
- Each section has a big "up to 50% off [category]" headline and a "Shop [Category] Furniture" button, so the layout is easy to scan.
- Product tiles show "Sale $X / Reg. $Y" pricing, so I can do the discount math without clicking.
- The room photography is warm and styled. The desk and hutch shot looks like something I'd pin.
- The "design desk" appointment block near the bottom is a decent showroom and service touch.

## What's weak

- The sender is "Crate & Barrel Kids," and most of the furniture is cribs, nursery pieces and toddler beds. I'm not in that life stage.
- The cadence is brutal. This is the third "new deals added" email in a day, with the same 50% number I've seen since 11/23.
- "Up to 50%" with a wall of dense fine print at the bottom doesn't feel clean. I can't tell which items get the 50%.
- It has a lot of stacked modules: holiday travel gear, a Christmas collection, an app download and a baby-photo block. That dilutes the furniture message.
- I can't see any loyalty or rewards math. I buy on my highest-rewards card, and nothing here helps me plan that.
- Some product text is small on a phone, and the price lines are tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send. Don't blast a Kids nursery-heavy email to someone who's been browsing adult furniture. Lead with the desk and bedroom pieces for me.
- 2. Merge the three "new deals added" sends into one email per day.
- 3. Put the end date next to the free-shipping claim, and add delivery-window info for furniture.
- 4. Show swatches or a free-sample link, and surface member pricing.
- 5. Cut the app and travel modules, and let the furniture grid carry the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping on 50%-off desks, beds & dressers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your workspace picks just dropped: up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on furniture through Cyber Monday. Desks, beds, dressers.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns added today. See what's still in stock before it sells out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with Movable Ink dynamic content. It has about 45 images flagged for missing alt text and a template-leftover comment in the head. The QA run could not confirm unsubscribe headers or authentication, and the 106 tracked links were not probed. The source is truncated, so body links and footer content were not verifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified.** 106 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so there is no data on destination status, redirect chains or landing pages.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five LiveRamp pixels fire from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5`. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string, which looks like a hashed identifier.
- **Identifier in image URLs.** Movable Ink URLs carry `mi_u=dbe8da71…` (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/71eb5ab4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carries campaign and message IDs. The `s=` and `mi_u=` values share the same prefix, so the same per-recipient identifier appears to go to a third-party domain. This is a disclosure and privacy consideration, not a defect.
- **Repeated personalized image.** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times with the same `mi_u`. That is expected for dynamic product slots, but if the Movable Ink service is slow or down, all six slots fail together.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 45 `<img>` tags were flagged. Most are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`. Examples:
  - `23c80033-9068-4aa7-b85b-ee3f61af397a.jpg`
  - `CK%5FH26%5FKidsHolidayMagic%5F24x13%5FEdit3%5FTert` (Scene7)
  - The six `f3c0d22992a01946.png` dynamic images
- **Image-only content.** If these images carry product, price or offer content, it is unavailable to screen readers and to clients that block images by default.
- **Spacers and pixels.** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon should carry `alt=""`. The checker cannot tell a missing attribute from an empty one, so confirm these.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled.** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` rule prevents text resizing in WebKit and IE/Outlook clients.
- **Template leftovers.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (note the typo) is left in the head, along with an empty `<!---->`.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses a bare `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype, which is not valid XML. Use `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate and conflicting declarations.**
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times.
  - `Content-Type` and `<meta charset>` are both present.
  - `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later-matching `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, so the 600px cap never takes effect at 768px and above.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set and the VML/Office namespaces and MSO conditionals are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the truncated HTML. The remainder of the body and the footer were not available, so this is not a full clearance.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found.** The QA check reports `List-Unsubscribe header not found`, and also `List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)` missing. The tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified defect. The sender is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a bulk-sender domain, and Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from such senders.
- **Authentication unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not checked.** The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so they cannot be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The `href` values and destination pages were not exposed, and the 106 tracking links were not followed. No UTM parameters can be confirmed on any link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, starting with the product tiles and the six `f3c0d22992a01946.png` dynamic images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop the global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule.
3. Re-run the QA check against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. These cannot be confirmed through the relay.
4. Follow the redirect chain on a sample of the 106 tracked links, checking final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
5. Review what the `sr.rlcdn.com` `s=` value and `mi_u` contain, and whether the shared per-recipient identifier going to third parties is intended.
6. Template hygiene:
   - Escape `&` in the `<title>`.
   - Remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
   - Deduplicate the meta tags.
   - Fix the conflicting `img` max-width media queries.
