# OUTDOOR IS ON SALE
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Eleven. I know it's eleven because I counted yesterday when "Wall decor that's back in stock" dropped the same calendar day as "These colors never go unnoticed" — that was two CB2 sends in a single day, and today is a third consecutive morning with a CB2 email. My tolerance for all-caps subject lines from this brand is running on fumes.
- Here's the thing though: outdoor furniture is *exactly* why I'm in this inbox. I've been circling the Bay Sectional for a solid two months. I've got a second home that needs a full outdoor dining situation, and I'm actively cross-shopping CB2, Pottery Barn, and West Elm every time something moves on price. This email serves up a 40% off hook above the fold, individual product CTAs for named pieces I recognize, and a cleaner layout than the wall-decor email from yesterday. The content is actually right for me.
- So would I click? Probably on the Bay Sectional — but only because Pottery Barn's outdoor sale email from two days ago already primed me and I've been holding off. This email didn't close me; my pre-existing purchase intent did. Would I forward it? No. Would I save it to my promo folder? Yes, if only to cross-reference pricing.

## What's working

- **40% hero is immediate and unambiguous**: landed in the first screenful without scrolling, outdoor lifestyle imagery supporting the offer — exactly what I want to see before I decide whether to keep reading.
- **Named products with individual CTAs**: Bay Sectional, Callie Dining Table, Oakley Dining Chair — not a generic "shop the collection" dump, but an actual curated edit with discrete entry points. That's how I shop CB2.
- **"Midseason Sale" framing is smarter than the alternative**: Pottery Barn's outdoor email on the 28th was still running "Memorial Day" language, which aged badly by the 29th. CB2 updating to "Midseason" shows someone was paying attention.
- **Visual hierarchy holds**: logo → hero with offer → individual product rows → more-for-you recommendations. Eye moves naturally, nothing fights for attention.

## What's weak

- **ALL CAPS subject is cheap**: "OUTDOOR IS ON SALE" reads like a clearance-rack sign, not a considered send from a premium furniture brand. This is the same executional laziness that made "LAST CALL FOR MEMORIAL DAY" feel like yelling.
- **No deadline anywhere visible**: "Midseason Sale" is a vibes window. Is this running through Sunday? June 15th? I'll assume indefinite and put off deciding, which means I don't buy. Pottery Barn explicitly frames sale windows — CB2 should too.
- **The Lotus Rug breaks the story**: I'm here for outdoor seating and dining; the rug insertion reads like someone threw in a rug because it was on the sale roster. Article would have edited this out — they understand "story of one collection" discipline. Wayfair spammed me with three rug emails this week; I don't need CB2 doing it sideways in an outdoor furniture send.
- **Midseason Sale section is illegible at render size**: There's a "$1,000" figure in that panel and I genuinely cannot parse what it's offering. If it's a member threshold, a savings cap, or a Design Insider benefit, it's wasted because I can't read it.
- **No pricing visible on individual products**: I know the Bay Sectional runs over $2,000 at full price. "40% off" means something to me if I see the final number; without it I'm doing fuzzy math in my head instead of clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the hero**: "40% off — through June 1st" changes my decision calculus from "I'll get to it" to "I need to check this today." One line in the hero, not buried in footer fine print.
- 2. **Show discounted prices under each product tile**: "$1,440 (was $2,400)" on the Bay Sectional converts me on the spot. A percentage without an anchor number forces extra mental work I won't do on my commute.
- 3. **Pull the rug, tighten to outdoor furniture only**: Make this a disciplined outdoor furniture story or don't bother. The rug muddles the editorial voice.
- 4. **Make the $1,000 panel readable**: If that's a loyalty benefit, surface it in 18pt type. Design Insider rewards are a real reason I choose CB2 over Pottery Barn when price is comparable.
- 5. **Rewrite the subject entirely — test these**:
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Bay Sectional just hit 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off outdoor — ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sectionals, dining tables, dining chairs — the full CB2 outdoor collection.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The outdoor pieces you've been watching just dropped.`
