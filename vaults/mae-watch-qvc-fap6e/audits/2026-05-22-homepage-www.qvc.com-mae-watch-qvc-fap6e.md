# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is home. QVC is leading with the Memorial Day Sale loud and proud — big red letters, red-white-and-blue backdrop, and a "Major Weekend Markdowns UP TO 40% OFF" headline I can read without squinting. It's a value-first, holiday-timed push, and it feels exactly like what I'd expect to see if I tuned in right now. Nothing surprising, but nothing broken either.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day SALE" banner + headline combo** — the pink "Memorial Day SALE" strip above "Major Weekend Markdowns UP TO 40% OFF" reads instantly and tells me exactly why I should be here today
- **"Shop the Sale" CTA** — navy button, clear label, sits right below the headline; no guessing what to do next
- **"Your Picks" tab** — love seeing that in the nav; makes me feel like QVC remembers me, not just my credit card
- **"100s of Holiday Weekend DEALS" second module** — reinforces the sale story and signals there's more depth below the hero
- **Search bar prominently placed** — I know what item numbers I want; being able to type one in immediately is exactly right for how I shop

## What's weak

- **No Easy Pay mention anywhere visible** — this is the #1 reason I buy things on QVC that I might not buy elsewhere. Hiding it below the fold on a sale weekend is leaving money on the table
- **Hero imagery is generic patriotic textiles** — red, white and blue fabric is pretty but tells me nothing about what's actually on sale; I can't tell if this is for shoes, bedding, or beauty
- **"UP TO 40% OFF" without anchor product or category** — which categories? Am I going to find Skechers Slip-ins at 40% off, or is it one beach towel at the back?
- **No host or show reference visible** — I know the hosts. Putting a familiar face or "As seen on Today's Special Value" would pull me in faster than a stock textile photo
- **"Items on Air" nav icon** — it's there, which I appreciate, but there's no "What's on now" teaser copy to make me tap it

## Recommendations

- **Add an Easy Pay strip directly below the hero headline** — something like "5 Easy Pays available · No interest, no fees" tied to the sale CTA; for customers like me, that alone can move the conversion needle
- **Swap the abstract patriotic fabric backdrop for a product-forward lifestyle shot** — a woman my age slipping on Skechers at a Memorial Day cookout or touching up with Bare Escentuals would make the hero feel like it's mine
- **Name a category in the sale CTA** — "Shop Shoes & Beauty Deals" instead of "Shop the Sale" narrows the click to the shoppers most likely to buy
- **Add a "Today's Special Value" teaser tile** — even a small module below the second banner showing today's TSV item and time remaining would remind loyal QVC viewers why they came here and not to a department store
