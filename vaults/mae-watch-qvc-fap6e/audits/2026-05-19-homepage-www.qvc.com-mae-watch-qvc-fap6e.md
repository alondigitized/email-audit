# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Well, QVC is going big on beauty today — the very first thing I see when I open the app is a bold "FREE SHIPPING on Beauty Faves" splash in that signature coral-red, and underneath it I can make out Philosophy Amazing Grace, some foundation sticks, and FRÉ skincare. This is clearly a beauty event day, probably tied to a Today's Special or a themed block of shows. It's comfortable and familiar for me, though I'd love to see my Bare Escentuals front and center instead of having to hunt for it below the fold.

## What's working

- **"Ends Tonight!" urgency on the Free Shipping hero** — I've watched QVC for years and I know how the clock works. That deadline banner does exactly what it should: it makes me feel like I need to act before I put my phone down.
- **Philosophy Amazing Grace front and center** — those big white-and-silver bottles are a QVC staple. I recognized them instantly. It signals this is a real beauty event, not a clearance dump.
- **"100s of New Deals Just Dropped" heading** — that's my language. I live for deals. I want to scroll and see what fell.
- **"Your Picks" navigation tab** — smart to have a personalized tab for a returning customer like me. That's QVC knowing I come back.
- **Search bar, prominent** — I sometimes catch an item number on the TV and then search for it on my phone. Having that bar up top is exactly right.

## What's weak

- **"New to Q? Get $15 off" banner takes prime real estate** — I have been shopping QVC longer than some of the hosts have been on the air. Putting a new-customer-only offer as the very first thing I read is a mild slap to loyal buyers. Where's my reward?
- **No Bare Escentuals visible** — It's my brand. It's a QVC brand. And I can't find it anywhere in this screenshot. FRÉ and those foundation sticks aren't for me.
- **No CTA button on the hero** — "FREE SHIPPING on Beauty Faves" is a strong message, but there's nowhere to tap to actually shop it. The whole card is just text.
- **No people in the imagery** — just products sitting on surfaces. Show me a woman my age using the Philosophy body wash or the Bare Minerals brush. That's what actually makes me add to cart.
- **Fifth nav tab is cut off** — I can see "Fath" and then nothing. I assume that's Father's Day, but I shouldn't have to guess on my own screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a tappable "Shop Beauty Faves" button inside the hero card** — a white pill button right below "Stock up on all things gorgeous" would turn this informational block into an actual conversion moment.
- **Replace the new-customer banner with a loyalty or Easy Pay callout** — something like "Q Card members: earn 5% back today" or "Easy Pay available on Beauty Faves" speaks directly to me. Push the $15 off code to the footer or a first-visit modal.
- **Surface Bare Escentuals (or my recently viewed brands) in the product tile rail** — if QVC knows I buy Bare Escentuals, those three tiles below the hero should prioritize my brands. That's a personalization win that would lock my click.
- **Add one lifestyle photo with a woman over 55 in the hero or the deal rail** — Philosophy and Bare Escentuals customers tend to skew 50-plus. A real woman enjoying the product makes me feel seen and much more likely to keep scrolling.
