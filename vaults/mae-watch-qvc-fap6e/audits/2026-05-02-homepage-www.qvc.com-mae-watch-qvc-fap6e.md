# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Well, I feel right at home — this is QVC, my QVC, and they know exactly how to get me. The minute I land on that page I see "Fashion Days" and "45% Off 1,000s of Styles" and my finger is already hovering. It feels comfortable and familiar, like tuning into the channel on a Saturday morning. They're pushing a fashion sale event, and honestly, that speaks right to me.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **"Fashion Days / Up to 45% Off 1,000s of Styles"** — that headline is the equivalent of hearing Joan Rivers say "you're going to LOVE this." Big number, big event energy. I respond to that.
- **"Thru tomorrow!"** — that little line under the hero does its job. I know I need to act now, not next week. That's urgency done right, not pushy.
- **HELLO15 new-customer code with a Copy button** — smart and easy. Even for a phone shopper like me, one tap copies the code. No fumbling, no writing it on a Post-it.
- **Two real women in the lifestyle shots** — I see a woman my size and a woman in a relaxed outfit. Not a 22-year-old runway model. That matters to me enormously.
- **"Watch," "Items on Air," and the Search bar** — these are my go-to tools. QVC put them front and center, just like on the app. I know exactly where I am.
- **"Your Picks" tab in the nav** — that tells me QVC knows my history and is curating for me. That feels like they know who I am.

## What's weak

- **The nav tab on the far right is cut off** — I can see "Fath…" and I have no idea what that is. Fashion? Father's Day? I shouldn't have to squint.
- **No Today's Special Value above the fold** — that is the heartbeat of QVC for me. If there's a TSV, I want to see it the moment I open the app. It's buried somewhere below and I can't see it here.
- **No Easy Pay callout anywhere visible** — Easy Pay is why I choose QVC over Amazon for big-ticket items. If I'm looking at shoes or skincare, I want to see "5 Easy Pays" right there, not after I click in.
- **The hero models are cut off at the bottom** — I can only see them from the waist up (or mid-thigh). The outfits are the whole point. Show me the full look.
- **The HELLO15 banner is aimed at new customers only** — I've been shopping with QVC for 20 years. That banner doesn't apply to me and it takes up prime real estate. A loyal-customer offer would land much better for someone like me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Today's Special Value strip above the hero** — even one line: "Today's Special Value: [Item name] — XX Easy Pays of $XX.XX." It is the brand's signature and it belongs at the top.
- **Swap the new-customer welcome banner for a returning-member reward** — something like "Welcome back! Q Card members save an extra 10% today." That keeps the urgency but rewards loyalty instead of ignoring it.
- **Show the full-length model images** — crop the hero taller or scroll the card down so I can see the pants and the shoes. The outfit sells itself; don't hide the bottom half.
- **Pin an Easy Pay badge to the hero** — a small "from X Easy Pays" line right under the 45% off copy would immediately remind me why QVC beats every other retailer on price-per-month.
