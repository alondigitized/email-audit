# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Well, it's my Q — and I know this site like the back of my hand. They're doing a "Celebrate You" push right now, which looks like their Mother's Day moment, with a big beauty sale front and center and some gold jewelry. That part I like. But I'm not seeing my Easy Pay anywhere above the fold, there's no Today's Special Value teaser, and my Skechers are nowhere in sight — and those are the three reasons I open this app in the first place.

## What's working

- **"Celebrate You" hero headline** — that treat-yourself framing on Mother's Day pulls me right in; I'm already in that mindset today.
- **"Big Beauty Sale: Up to 30% Off" with the Laura Mercier tile** — I don't use Laura Mercier, but a 30% off beauty tile tells me the whole category is on, and that makes me want to look.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — this is the thing that keeps me coming back to Q over every other site. I feel like they remember me.
- **Deals on Gold Jewelry tile** — a pretty ring shot is always a reason to linger.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the tiles are big enough to tap without squinting, which I appreciate.

## What's weak

- **No Easy Pay mention above the fold** — this is Q's number-one hook for me. Five-easy-payments is what I tell my girlfriends. It should be on every product tile, not buried somewhere in the product page.
- **"Inspo for the ultimate treat-yourself day"** — "Inspo" is not my word. That's what my granddaughter says. I'm 64; talk to me like a woman, not a TikTok caption.
- **The WELCOME20 banner is for new customers, not me** — I've been watching Q for years. Greeting me with a new-customer code every time I open the app makes me feel like a stranger in my own store.
- **No Skechers, no footwear, nothing above the fold** — if I came here specifically for my Slip-ins, I'd have to go hunting. That's a miss.
- **No "What's On Air Now" hook** — the Watch button is in the nav, but there's no teaser about what's live. For a daily viewer, that's the fastest way to pull me in.

## Recommendations

- **Put Easy Pay on every product tile** — even a small "5 Easy Pays of $X" tag on the beauty and jewelry tiles reminds me why I choose Q over Amazon.
- **Swap "Inspo" for "Ideas"** — one word, same message, but now it doesn't feel like it was written for someone half my age.
- **Add a "What's On Air Now" strip below the hero** — a thumbnail of the current show and Today's Special Value item would hook daily viewers like me faster than a static sale tile.
- **Surface a comfort footwear tile in the second row** — "Comfort Styles" or "Skechers + More" alongside the beauty and jewelry cards would catch my eye and remind me this is where I shop for shoes too.
