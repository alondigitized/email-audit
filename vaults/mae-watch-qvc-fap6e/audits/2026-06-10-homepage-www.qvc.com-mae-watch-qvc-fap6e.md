# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know QVC like the back of my hand, so the layout feels comfortable right away — the Watch button up top, my cart, that search bar I use constantly. They're doing a big "Summer Under the Sun" push right now, which makes sense for June, and the deals are right there front and center. But I'll be honest: this screen is showing me lemon cake and a garden hose, and what I came here for is my Skechers Slip-ins and maybe some Bare Escentuals. Still feels like home, just not quite my room yet.

## What's working

- **"Your Picks" nav tab** — I love that. QVC knows I come back. That feels personal and saves me from scrolling through everything.
- **The welcome code banner** — "$20 off $40+" with a one-tap Copy button is practical and I can read it without squinting. Simple and honest.
- **"Summer Under the Sun" headline** — warm, feels like something David Venable would say on a summer Friday show. It's the right QVC energy.
- **Partially visible beauty tile** (bottom right) — I can see what looks like skincare or beauty product bottles peeking in below the fold. That's bait that might get me to scroll.
- **Clean mobile layout** — big tap targets, no tiny text, loads fast. I've ordered from this app on a cruise ship, and it's always been solid.

## What's weak

- **No Skechers or comfort footwear anywhere** — that's my number-one reason I open QVC and there's not a single shoe above the fold. I have to go hunt.
- **No Easy Pay mention** — "5 Easy Pays of $X" is QVC's superpower. I don't see it here. That's a missed shot to hook returning shoppers like me immediately.
- **Garden Clearance hero tile** — garden tools are fine but they feel like they're talking to my neighbor Dave, not to me. I don't need a hose sprayer.
- **No Today's Special Value callout** — TSV is why half of us open the app every morning. If there's a TSV today, it should be front and center.
- **"New to Q?" welcome bar** — I am absolutely not new to Q. Showing me a first-timer discount while there's a "Your Picks" tab just down below feels inconsistent. Feels like the site doesn't know who it's talking to.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the welcome-code banner for a loyalty/returning-customer hook** — show me my Q Card balance or recent order status instead of a new-customer code. Or show both, but don't lead with "New to Q" when the nav already has "Your Picks."
- **Add a Today's Special Value strip** — one bold banner at the very top: "Today's Special Value: [Product] — 5 Easy Pays of $X." That's the one thing that gets me to open the app every single morning.
- **Replace the garden-hose tile with a footwear or beauty hero** — "Skechers Summer Styles" or "Bare Escentuals Just In" would speak directly to loyal buyers like me. Hosting and garden can live further down the page.
- **Surface an Easy Pay call-to-action on at least one product tile** — even just "from $X/mo with Easy Pay" on the hosting sale tile would remind me why I love this place more than Amazon.
