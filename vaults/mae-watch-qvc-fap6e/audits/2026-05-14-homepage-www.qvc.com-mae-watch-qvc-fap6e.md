# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, they're pushing their "Summer of Savings" event right up front, and QVC is QVC — I recognize the logo and the layout right away. It feels a little broad to me, like they're talking to everybody at once instead of talking to me. I do see a little peek of something from "Just-Dropped Specials" at the bottom, but I want to see my Bare Escentuals and my Skechers, not a generic sale banner.

## What's working

- **"Your Picks" navigation tab** — that tab tells me QVC knows I come back. It's a little nod that says "we remember you," and I appreciate that.
- **Summer of Savings hero** — the gingham check pattern is cheerful and seasonal without trying too hard to look like a fashion magazine. It feels like QVC.
- **"Shop the Event" CTA button** — dark navy, big, impossible to miss on a phone screen. One tap and I'm in. I like that.
- **"Just-Dropped Specials" section beginning to peek in** — "Two Days Only!" badge in red is exactly the kind of urgency I respond to. I know how QVC Today's Special Value works and that badge gets my attention.
- **$15 off $35+ offer** — even if it's for new customers, the fact that a clear dollar-off number is right there above the fold feels trustworthy. No fuzzy "up to" language.

## What's weak

- **"New to Q?" on the promo banner** — I watch QVC every single morning with my coffee. Showing me a new-customer code feels like they don't know me at all. That banner should not be the first thing I see.
- **No Easy Pay callout above the fold** — Easy Pay is the whole reason I buy on QVC instead of Amazon. I want to see "5 Easy Payments of..." somewhere up top. It's not there.
- **Hero is product-free** — there's no shoe, no beauty product, nothing I can point to and say "I want that." Just a gingham box and a tagline. For a daily QVC viewer, that's a missed opportunity.
- **No Today's Special Value featured** — the TSV is the heartbeat of QVC and it's not visible anywhere above the fold. That's a big miss for loyal viewers who check in specifically for it.
- **Navigation tabs cut off** — "Fath..." is clearly the start of "Father's Day" or something and it's chopped off. Small thing, but sloppy on a phone.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the "New to Q?" promo strip with a returning-member offer** — show me a loyalty thank-you discount or at least a "Welcome back" message with my name or rewards points. New-customer codes make loyal customers feel invisible.
- **Add a Today's Special Value module directly below the hero** — one big product photo, the host's name, current Easy Pay breakdown, and a countdown. That's the QVC experience. That's why I open the app.
- **Put a product in the hero image** — swap the gingham-only background for a lifestyle shot: a woman around my age, sitting on her porch, in a great pair of Skechers Slip-ins or holding a Bare Escentuals palette. Give me something to want.
- **Anchor Easy Pay in the nav or hero subtext** — even a single line "5 Easy Pay available" under the CTA button would stop me from second-guessing whether I need to call in to get the payment plan.
