# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Well, I've been watching QVC since before half these websites existed, and the first thing I see when I open the app is a big portable generator and a Cuisinart grill. I like a good deal same as anybody, but honey, I came here for my Skechers and my Bare Escentuals, not a tailgate setup. QVC's pushing their "Black Friday in July" right now, which I've seen them do before — it's a good concept — but this homepage is throwing a lot at me without telling me what I'm actually saving.

## What's working

- **"Your Picks" tab** — I love that QVC knows I come back. Seeing my own personalized tab right in the nav makes me feel like a regular, which I am.
- **"Shop Deals" button** — Simple, no fuss, I know exactly what to tap. QVC trained me on buttons like this.
- **"Black Friday in July" framing** — I've seen this campaign before and I trust it. QVC knows how to make a sale feel like an event.
- **Clean layout** — Nothing is fighting for my attention. On a phone screen at my age, that matters more than people realize.
- **"WATCH" shortcut in the nav** — I can jump straight to the live show. That's my comfort zone and it's one tap away.

## What's weak

- **Power station as a hero product** — That's a guy-in-a-garage item. I don't know what to do with it and it makes me wonder if I opened the wrong app.
- **No specific savings amount** — "Savings Are Coming In Hot" sounds great but where's the number? Even a "save up to 40%" would make me lean in. I need a reason to click.
- **No Skechers, no beauty, nothing in my wheelhouse** — Two visible category tiles and neither one is footwear or cosmetics. QVC carries both; show me.
- **No host name or show reference** — QVC's whole thing is the hosts. David, Shawn, Carolyn — I trust them. Not seeing a familiar face or name here feels cold.
- **No Easy Pay or Q Card callout** — That Easy Pay 5× is why I shop here instead of Amazon. It's nowhere on this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the power station tile for a Skechers Slip-ins or Bare Escentuals bestseller** — women 50+ are QVC's bread and butter; the hero real estate should reflect that.
- **Add a specific savings figure to the hero** — Change "Savings Are Coming In Hot" to something like "Save Up to 50% — Black Friday in July" so I have a reason to tap before I lose interest.
- **Surface Easy Pay 5× prominently** — A small badge or banner strip saying "Easy Pay available on thousands of items" right below the hero would stop me mid-scroll every single time.
- **Add a host face or name to the promotional tiles** — Even a small "As seen with David Venable" label on the Cuisinart tile would make this feel like QVC and not just another sale website.
