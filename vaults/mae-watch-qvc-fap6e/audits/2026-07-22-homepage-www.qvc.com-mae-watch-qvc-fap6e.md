# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Well, this looks like my QVC all right — I recognized that big Q logo right away. They're leading with a fashion clearance sale, which is nice, but I have to say for a gal who opens this app hoping to catch today's special or see what's on air in shoes, the first thing that pops up telling me "New to Q?" felt a little off. Honey, I have been a Q customer for years. The shoes photo down below caught my eye though — those wedge sandals are exactly my kind of thing.

## What's working

- **The wedge sandals photo** — brown, low-heeled, classic — stopped my thumb cold. That is exactly the kind of shoe I reach for.
- **"Two Days Only!" badge** — I know QVC urgency, I live by it, and that little pink circle tells me I better not put my phone down.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — knowing they remember what I like without me typing a thing is a real convenience at my age.
- **Extra 15% Off Fashion Clearance** — stacking a clearance sale with an extra percentage is exactly the kind of deal I call my daughter about.
- **Search bar front and center** — when I already know my item number from watching the show, I can go straight to it. That matters.

## What's weak

- **"New to Q? Enjoy $15 off" banner** — I am not new to Q. Leading with a new-customer offer is a little insulting to someone who has been shopping here since before the internet. Where is my loyalty recognition?
- **No Easy Pay callout** — Easy Pay is the whole reason I can say yes to a $79 shoe. Not seeing it above the fold is a miss for someone like me.
- **No brand names visible** — I'm here for Skechers and Bare Escentuals. Nothing calls out either brand. A returning customer should see what she loves without scrolling.
- **"Hello, style stock-up"** — that tagline sounds like something for a 30-year-old influencer, not for me.
- **No host or show name** — part of the QVC magic is the personalities. Joan, David, whoever's on air — I want a face, not just a graphic.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "New to Q?" banner for a Q Points or Q Card balance teaser** — returning shoppers should feel recognized the moment they open the app, not reminded that the deal on screen isn't for them.
- **Add "Easy Pay available" beneath the clearance promo** — one line, right under "Extra 15% OFF." It would push me from browsing to buying on the spot.
- **Put a brand spotlight above the fold** — even a small Skechers or Bare Escentuals logo strip tells loyal customers their favorites are in the sale.
- **Replace "Hello, style stock-up" with something like "Your clearance finds are waiting"** — warmer, more personal, speaks to a shopper who knows this store.
