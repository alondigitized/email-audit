# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Well, this feels like home — it's QVC and they are dressed up for the Fourth of July. Right off the bat I'm seeing "July 4th Spectacular, Celebratory Deals UP TO 45% OFF" with all the red, white, and blue, and then "Cheers to 250 Years, America!" just below it. This is a deal-forward patriotic push, which is right in my wheelhouse. Feels festive and timely, though I'm not seeing anything yet about my Skechers or my Bare Escentuals — it's all very broad-strokes holiday savings.

## What's working

- **"July 4th Spectacular" badge + red/white/blue textile imagery** — the patriotic setup lands emotionally for me. I'm a QVC watcher during the holidays and this hits the right note.
- **"UP TO 45% OFF" in bold red** — I see it the second the page loads. That number does the work.
- **"Cheers to 250 Years, America!"** — the copy is warm and celebratory, not corporate. It sounds like something David Venable would say on air.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — I like that they remember me. One tap and I should be back to things curated for my shopping habits.
- **Two CTAs, two different offers** — "Shop the Deals" and "Shop the Sale" give me two entry points without feeling cluttered.

## What's weak

- **No Easy Pay callout** — I live by Easy Pay 5×. It's the whole reason I buy bigger-ticket items on QVC and I can't see it anywhere above the fold. That's a miss for someone like me.
- **WELCOME20 banner leads the page** — The very first offer I read is for new customers only. As a longtime loyal shopper, that stings a little. Lead with something for *me*, not for someone just discovering the Q.
- **No show or host tie-in** — I know it's a mobile homepage, but even a small "Watch Now" or a host name tied to a featured product would pull me deeper. That's the QVC magic I come for.
- **No category navigation visible** — I have to open the hamburger menu to find Shoes or Beauty. The horizontal tab bar shows Deals, Your Picks, New, Trending Now, July — but no quick shortcut to my specific categories.
- **No imagery of products I shop** — The hero visual looks like towels or throws, which is fine, but nothing calls out footwear or beauty to tell me my departments are in the 45% off event.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Easy Pay callout in the hero or just below it** — even a small strip saying "5 Easy Pays available on select items" would stop loyal shoppers like me from scrolling past wondering if the deal structure I rely on is there.
- **Swap the WELCOME20 banner for a returning-customer loyalty hook** — something like "Welcome back, Mae — your Q Card saves you an extra 10%" or even just a loyalty points balance banner would make me feel seen instead of overlooked.
- **Pin one host or on-air segment to the July 4th hero** — a thumbnail of the live show or "Carolyn is live with July picks right now" would trigger the appointment-TV response that QVC shoppers are conditioned to feel.
- **Add a quick-link row for top categories** — a single horizontal strip of icons (Shoes · Beauty · Kitchen · Apparel) would let me tap straight to Skechers without hunting through the menu.
