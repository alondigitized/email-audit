# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know QVC the second I land on it — that navy header, the Q logo, the search bar — it feels like home. Right now they're pushing a "Summer of Savings" with 5 Easy Pays on everything through tonight, and that Easy Pay banner is the first thing that caught my eye because that's the whole reason I shop here. What's a little disappointing is there's not a stitch of footwear or beauty above the fold — it's all garden lights and a big ol' Blackstone griddle.

## What's working

- **"5 Easy Pays on everything thru tonight!"** — That's my language. I have bought entire wardrobes on Easy Pay and seeing it front and center in the hero is exactly the hook that makes me slow down and look.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — I've been shopping Q for years and knowing they're tracking what I like gives me a shortcut to my own taste without hunting through categories.
- **Summer of Savings headline** — Bold, readable font in QVC's signature navy and red. On my phone screen it pops. No squinting required.
- **$15 off $35+ welcome code** — Even though I'm not new, I appreciate that they show this openly. It tells me they're trying to grow and that keeps the brand healthy.
- **Garden & Outdoor and BBQ tiles** — Not my categories, but the photography is clean and the "Up to 45% Off" text is big enough to read at arm's length. Good execution even if it's not for me.

## What's weak

- **No shoes, no beauty anywhere above the fold** — I came here partly out of habit for Skechers and Bare Escentuals. Neither category gets a single pixel of space in the first screen.
- **No primary tap target with button copy on the hero** — The Summer of Savings card is clearly the hero but there's no "Shop the Event" or "See All Deals" button on it. I almost didn't know I could tap it.
- **No human faces** — The two product tiles below the hero are landscapes and appliances. No model, no lifestyle person, nobody who looks like they could be in my living room or at my backyard table. QVC's TV magic is hosts and real people; this page loses that.
- **"New to Q?" banner feels like it's talking past me** — I've been a Q customer since the nineties. That bar isn't harmful but it takes up prime real estate telling me something irrelevant.
- **Father's Day tab is cut off** — The nav tab at the far right is cropped. On my phone that feels like the page doesn't quite fit, even if it's just scrollable. Small thing, but I notice it.

## Recommendations

- **Swap one of the two product tiles for a Shoes or Beauty spotlight** — Put a Skechers Slip-ins image next to the BBQ griddle with copy like "Comfort footwear: Up to 40% Off + 5 Easy Pays." I'd tap that before I even read anything else.
- **Add a "Shop the Event" button directly on the Summer of Savings hero card** — One visible button with clear copy removes all the guesswork. Right now you have to just know to tap the banner.
- **Replace or supplement the welcome-code bar with a loyalty points balance** — For returning customers, show something like "Hi Mae — you have 230 points" where that HELLO15 bar sits. That's an instant reminder of why I stayed loyal.
- **Pull a Today's Special Value teaser above the fold** — Even a small strip that says "Today's Special Value: XYZ — $39.98 + Easy Pay" would give me a reason to stay on the page and buy something today.
