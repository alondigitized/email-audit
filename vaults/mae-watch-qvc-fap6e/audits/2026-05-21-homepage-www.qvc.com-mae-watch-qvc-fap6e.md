# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Well, I opened the app and the first thing I see is "40% OFF" in big red letters — Memorial Day sale, and it's still the weekend, so that's exactly right. QVC is doing what QVC does: pushing the big holiday event right at you. I do wish I could see right away if my Skechers or my Bare Escentuals are part of this, but the sale banner is front and center and those shoe pictures right below it have me curious.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 40% OFF" in bold red** — that number jumps right off the screen and tells me this weekend is the time to buy
- **"Shop the Sale" button** — dark navy, centered, no ambiguity about what to tap; even on a small phone screen I won't miss it
- **Memorial Day timing** — the banner headline matches the actual calendar; QVC isn't showing me a stale Valentine's Day graphic in late May
- **Shoe images below the fold line** — the red comfort sandal on the beach and the athletic walking shoe caught my eye immediately; that's my category without me having to hunt for it
- **"100s of Holiday Weekend Deals Are On thru Monday"** — that phrasing sounds exactly like something David or the other hosts would say on air; it feels like the show

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook** — I've been shopping QVC for years and there's no "Hi Mae, your Q Card points" or rewards reminder anywhere up top; that would pull me right in
- **"Your Picks" tab is there but does nothing for me right now** — I can see the nav label but there's nothing personalized calling me to tap it
- **No beauty product in sight** — Bare Escentuals is half of why I open this app; not a single skincare or makeup image above the scroll
- **Free-shipping info is missing** — I always check my order total against the free-shipping threshold before I start adding to cart; I'd have to go hunting for that
- **"Items on Air" icon at top but no live show teaser** — on the TV right now there might be a Today's Special Value I'm missing; the app doesn't tell me

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin personalized strip below the search bar** — something like "Your Q Card: 2,340 pts · Today's Special: [item name]" would make a returning shopper feel seen before she even taps anything
- **Swap the generic beach-towel background for a lifestyle shot of a woman in her 60s wearing the featured sale sandal** — the abstract red/white/blue is patriotic but impersonal; a real woman in comfortable shoes on a porch is more "me"
- **Add a free-shipping badge to the hero or just below the CTA button** — one line, something like "Free Shipping on Orders $35+" so I know right away whether this sale gets me there
- **Surface a beauty thumbnail alongside the shoe images** — even a small Bare Escentuals or skincare tile next to the sandal shot would keep me from bouncing to hunt Beauty in the nav
