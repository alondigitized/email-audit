## What's working

- **"Growing, Growing, Gone!" urgency strip** — that's classic QVC language, and it works on me; I know it means the offer won't last
- **25% off with a real number** — I don't have to do math or hunt for fine print; the discount is front and center
- **Clean mobile layout** — the MENU / WATCH / ITEMS ON AIR / CART row at the top is exactly what I expect; nothing is out of place
- **"Inspiring New Arrivals" label** — I like knowing there's fresh product, even if it's garden category today
- **Butterfly and lavender photos** — genuinely pretty, gives the page a warm, friendly feel that suits QVC's tone

## What's weak

- **"New to Q? $15 off" banner** — I am absolutely not new to Q. Seeing that as a loyal, long-time customer feels like a slap; where's my returning-shopper appreciation?
- **No Skechers, no Bare Escentuals, no beauty anywhere** — my categories are invisible above the fold; I'd have to go digging through MENU to find what I actually came for
- **No Easy Pay callout** — Easy Pay is one of QVC's biggest selling points for me, and I don't see it mentioned even once in this viewport
- **No Today's Special Value** — TSV is a daily ritual for me; I always check it when I open the app, and it's nowhere to be seen here
- **No host presence** — part of why I love QVC is the hosts; not a name, face, or "on air with [host]" anywhere above the fold

## Recommendations

- **Add a TSV ribbon or persistent banner** — sticky strip at the top with "Today's Special Value: [Item] — Easy Pay 5×" would grab me instantly and remind me why I come back daily
- **Personalize the hero for returning shoppers** — if QVC knows I buy Skechers, swap the plants hero for my category; even a small "Back for You, Mae" strip with recently-viewed product would do the job
- **Surface one Easy Pay call-to-action above the fold** — even a small badge on the plants offer ("5 Easy Pay available") would signal that QVC's signature payment perk is alive and well
- **Rename or hide the new-customer promo for known accounts** — show loyal customers a rewards balance or exclusive member deal in that same banner slot instead of the HELLO15 code
