# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Now I have a clear look at the homepage. Writing Mae's review.
- Well, I'll tell you — QVC knows Memorial Day is my Super Bowl. I open the app and right away I see "FREE SHIPPING on Everything" in big bold letters with those little stars, and a "Shop the Sale" button sitting right there. It feels festive and familiar, like when David Venable kicks off a Holiday Preview show. The brand is clearly pushing a one-day holiday event, and for a regular like me, that's enough to keep my thumb from going back to my home screen.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day SALE badge in red** — that little red pill with "Memorial Day SALE" printed on it over the hero tells me instantly this is a real event, not just a generic promo. QVC holiday events are when I stock up.
- **"FREE SHIPPING on Everything" in giant type** — I am a phone-order and .com shopper; shipping costs are the thing that makes me hesitate. This removes that hesitation immediately.
- **"Today only!" urgency line** — I know how QVC works. Today's Special Values are real. This deadline feels credible to me, not like a fake countdown.
- **QCard $40 off banner** — I already have my QCard, so I read this as money in my pocket. A new customer might open one just for this. Smart placement.
- **"Shop the Sale" button** — big, dark, centered, unmissable. One tap. I know exactly where I'm going.
- **"100s of Holiday Weekend DEALS" module below hero** — the copy promises volume, and I trust QVC to deliver. This makes me want to scroll.

## What's weak

- **No category entry points above the fold** — I'm here for Skechers Slip-ins or Bare Minerals, but nothing on this screen points me toward footwear or beauty. I'd have to tap the nav or scroll to get there.
- **The asterisk on "Everything\*"** — I see that little star and I know there's fine print. I've been burned before on exclusions around clearance or certain brands. It introduces doubt right when you don't want it.
- **No human face in the hero** — QVC hosts and models are why I trust this place. Stars and a navy background are patriotic but cold. A smiling woman in comfortable shoes or holding a makeup palette would land so much better for me.
- **Navigation tabs are crowded and cut off** — "Deals, Your Picks, New, Trending Now, Me…" — that last tab is sliced off the edge. On my phone I can't even read what it says. If that's "Members" or "My Account," I'm missing it.
- **Bottom module image is small and unclear** — the lifestyle shot near "100s of Holiday Weekend DEALS" shows what looks like a bag or wrapped gift. It's tiny and I can't tell what category it's pulling me toward.

## Recommendations

- **Put one category shortcut in the hero or just below it** — a row of three quick-tap icons ("Beauty," "Shoes," "Kitchen") right under the "Shop the Sale" button would get me to Bare Escentuals without an extra scroll. QVC's app does this on some days and it makes a big difference.
- **Swap the asterisk language for the actual condition** — instead of "on Everything\*" say "on Everything — no exclusions" or "on orders over $X." The fine print creates anxiety; clarity creates orders.
- **Add a host face or a lifestyle image** — put David or Mary Beth or one of the beauty hosts in the hero graphic, even a small inset portrait. That's the signal that tells me this is QVC, not just any site running a sale.
- **Fix or widen the nav tab row** — either reduce to four tabs or allow horizontal scroll with a visible indicator so I know there's more. Cutting off "Me…" means I might miss my account or loyalty tab entirely.
