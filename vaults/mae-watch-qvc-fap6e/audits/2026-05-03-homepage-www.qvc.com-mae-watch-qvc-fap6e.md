## What's working

- **"Your Picks" tab** — I spotted it right away. That tells me the site knows who I am and has things picked out for me. That's worth a tap.
- **45% Off Fashion Days headline** — Large, bold, unmissable. I didn't have to squint. That's exactly how you talk to me.
- **"Ends Tonight!" badge** — I respond to that. I'm not going to dilly-dally if it's going away.
- **The two women models below the hero** — One looks like she's my size and age range, wearing a comfortable everyday hoodie. That's my kind of look.
- **Search bar front and center** — I know my item numbers sometimes. Good to have that right at the top.

## What's weak

- **"New to Q? Get up to $15 off" banner** — I am absolutely not new to Q. Seeing that as the first thing below the nav feels like a slap. Why is QVC talking to me like I just wandered in off the street?
- **No Easy Pay callout** — That's half the reason I shop here. I'd want to see "5 Easy Payments" or my payment options before I commit to anything.
- **No footwear or beauty entry point above the fold** — I can't see a path to Skechers or Bare Escentuals without scrolling or hunting through the nav. That's a miss for me specifically.
- **No explicit "Shop" button on the hero** — The 45% off banner is big but there's no tap target telling me where to go next. A button labeled "Shop Fashion Days" would close the loop.
- **Nav tabs are cut off on the right** — I can see "Deals, Your Picks, New, Trending Now, Fath—" and then it's gone. I can't tell what "Fath" is. Father's Day? Fashion? That's a bit sloppy.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the HELLO15 new-customer banner with a returning-customer hook** — Show my Q Card balance, my Easy Pay status, or a "Welcome back, Mae" loyalty moment. I'm loyal. Treat me like it.
- **Add a "Shop Now" button to the Fashion Days hero** — The offer is clear but there's nowhere obvious to tap. A green button that says "Shop Fashion Days" would complete the thought.
- **Add a footwear or beauty category strip below the hero** — Even two tiles — "Shoes & Comfort" and "Beauty" — would give me a one-tap path to what I actually came to buy.
- **Fix the nav tab overflow** — Either shrink the tab labels or add a scroll indicator so I can see all my options without guessing what's cut off.
