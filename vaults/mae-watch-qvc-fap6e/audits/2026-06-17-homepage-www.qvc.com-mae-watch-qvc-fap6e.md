# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Well, I pulled up QVC this morning and the first thing I see is a big clearance push — "Mid-Year Markdowns, Up to 50% OFF." Now I appreciate a sale as much as anybody, but this homepage feels like it's dressed up in QVC's colors without really talking to me. No products, no hosts, no Easy Pay mention, and nothing pointing me toward my Skechers or my Bare Escentuals. It's a sale sign, not a store.

## What's working

- **"Mid-Year Markdowns / Up to 50% OFF Clearance" headline** — big, red, and impossible to miss; I know instantly there's a sale happening
- **"At Least 30% Off" and "At Least 40% Off" tiles** — I like knowing the floor before I click; "at least" is honest language
- **Clean layout** — nothing is crammed, the search bar is right at the top, and the tab row ("Deals," "Your Picks," "New," "Trending Now") is easy to read on my phone
- **"Your Picks" tab** — I spotted that; it tells me there's something personalized waiting for me if I tap it

## What's weak

- **"New to Q?" welcome banner is wasted on me** — I've been calling in orders since before they had a website; seeing that banner as the first thing below the nav feels like QVC forgot I exist
- **No products visible** — I can't see a single item, no shoe, no foundation, no jewelry. It's all percentages and peach gradients. I need to see *what* is on sale
- **No Easy Pay mention anywhere** — that's one of QVC's biggest draws and it's completely absent; I look for that every time I'm about to spend
- **No host presence or "on air now" energy** — QVC's magic is the hosts and the live feel; this homepage could be any discount retailer's clearance page
- **No category shortcut to Shoes or Beauty** — the tab row shows "Deals, New, Trending Now" but I can't get to footwear or skincare in one tap from what I can see

## Recommendations

- **Add an "On Air Now" live strip with a product thumbnail and host name** — even a small one; it reminds me what makes QVC different from Amazon
- **Surface an Easy Pay callout near the hero** — something like "5 Easy Pays on orders over $X" anchors the sale in QVC's language, not just generic percentage-off speak
- **Swap at least one of the clearance tiles for a category card with a real product image** — show me a Skechers slip-on or a Bare Escentuals compact so I know my brands are in this sale
- **Replace or supplement the "New to Q?" banner with a returning-member hook** — something like "Welcome back — your Q Points balance" or "Shop your saved items" signals that QVC knows me
