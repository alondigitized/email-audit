# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Well, this doesn't look like the QVC I click on every morning with my coffee. I'm greeted with fried chicken and a "Foodie Travel Series" instead of anything for my feet or my face, and there's not a stitch of Skechers or Bare Escentuals anywhere I can see. It looks like a nice, clean food-and-travel promotion, but it's not speaking to me at all today.

## What's working

- The "New to Q? Get 20% Off your first order" banner is easy to read and the code is right there to copy — that part's not confusing, even if it doesn't apply to someone who's shopped with them for years.
- The "Shop Kitchen & Food" button is a nice clear box, easy to tap even with my readers off.
- The layout is tidy — headline, button, then two clean photo tiles. Nothing jumbled or overlapping.

## What's weak

- Not one thing about shoes, sneakers, or makeup — my whole reason for visiting QVC — anywhere on this screen.
- I don't see my rewards or Easy Pay mentioned anywhere, and I'm a loyal customer, not a new one, so that 20%-off-first-order banner isn't even for me.
- No host names, no "on air now" personality — and I like knowing if it's one of my favorites hosting.
- No models or lifestyle photos of anyone my age enjoying anything — just chicken drumsticks and a travel graphic.

## Recommendations

- Put a Skechers or Bare Escentuals tile right up top for shoppers whose purchase history shows we buy those brands — I shouldn't have to hunt.
- Swap that "New to Q" 20%-off banner for a loyalty message for existing Easy Pay customers, or at least show both.
- Add the current on-air host's name and photo next to "Today! Live from New Orleans" so I know who I'm tuning into.
- Bring back a "Today's Special Value" callout above the fold — that's the first thing I look for every day.
