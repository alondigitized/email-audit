# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Well, they got me right away — I opened this and free shipping on shoes jumped right off the screen, and honey, I have a weakness for a good sandal deal. QVC is clearly pushing footwear hard right now, probably moving spring inventory. It feels familiar and comfortable to me, the way it always does, but I notice they left out a few things I usually look for first thing.

## What's working

- **"FREE SHIPPING on All Shoes" hero** — full stop, no minimum, no fine print fighting me. That lands.
- **"Thru Tomorrow!" urgency badge** — the orange burst makes it feel like a show segment. That's the QVC rhythm I know; it makes me want to move.
- **Product rail with comfort brands** — Ryka and MUK LUKS in the first row tells me this isn't the page for 25-year-olds in stilettos. These are my people's shoes.
- **BIG DEAL badge on the Ryka sandal** — I know what a Big Deal means on QVC. It means the price is legitimately lower than it usually runs. That badge earns trust with me.
- **Search bar front and center** — when I'm looking for a specific item number I heard on TV this morning, that bar is right where I need it.

## What's weak

- **HELLO15 promo is for new customers** — it's the first banner I see and it's not for me. That stings a little as a loyal shopper. Where's my perk?
- **No Skechers visible** — Skechers is what I come back for, and I don't see one pair in this screenshot. If I didn't already know to search for them, I'd wonder if they were still carrying the line.
- **No Easy Pay callout** — Easy Pay is half the reason I buy on QVC. I want to see "5 Easy Pays of $16.40" before I tap. It's not here above the fold.
- **No host face or show tie-in** — when I'm watching the show and they say "go to QVC.com," I want to feel that connection. This looks more like a generic e-commerce page than my TV show's website.
- **Bare Escentuals nowhere** — I know it's a shoe push right now but a little beauty strip wouldn't hurt.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the new-customer promo banner for a returning-shopper reward** — even something like "Welcome back! Your Q Card rewards: $X" or "Loyalty members: free shipping every day" would make me feel valued instead of invisible.
- **Add an Easy Pay badge to the product cards** — even just "As low as 5 Easy Pays" on the Birkenstock at $82 would stop me mid-scroll and make me do the math. That's a conversion moment waiting to happen.
- **Put a "Shop Skechers" tile in the product rail or as a category chip below the hero** — it takes one thumbnail. I'd tap it immediately.
- **Add a host photo or "Seen on TV tonight" module** — even a headshot and "Lisa Mason is on with Skechers at 7pm ET" would make this feel like QVC and not just another shoe website.
