# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- I opened QVC and a big pop-up covered almost everything. It's warm, though, because it shows three hosts smiling at me, and one of them is David in his apron. It wants my email in exchange for 15% off. I can see a red hero band and a Prepology grill pan behind it, but I can't tell what QVC is actually featuring today.

## What's working

- **The host photos (three faces, "DAVID" on the apron):** I know these people, and it feels like QVC right away. It's friendly, not corporate.
- **"We're So Happy You're Here":** This is how a host would greet me on the air. It's warm and easy to read.
- **"15% Off" in big orange type:** I can read it without my glasses. The amount is clear before I read anything else.
- **The gold "CONTINUE" button and the email box:** It's obvious what to do next, and the field is large enough for my thumb.
- **Header basics (Shop, Watch, Items on Air, search bar, Cart):** The things I use are at the top. "Today's Special Value & Deals" is at the right edge of the second row.

## What's weak

- **The pop-up shows up before I've seen anything:** I haven't looked around yet and I'm already being asked for my email. It blocks the menu and the hero.
- **"First time subscribers only":** I've watched QVC daily for years. I'm probably already on the list, so this offer isn't for me.
- **The fine print:** The paragraph is tiny and dense, with a West Chester address and three links. It's hard for me to read on a phone.
- **Hidden navigation:** The second menu row is cut off after "Your Picks" and "New," and I can only make out "Ou…". I can't reach Skechers or Beauty.
- **Nothing for my favorites:** I see no Skechers, no Bare Escentuals, and no Today's Special Value countdown. The only product showing is a microwave grill pan.
- **"Maximum savings $30":** This is buried under the big "15% Off," and it may disappoint me at checkout.

## Recommendations

- Show the sign-up pop-up only after I scroll or try to leave. Change the first screen to a Today's Special Value hero with the product photo, price, and "Easy Pay 5×" printed on it.
- Recognize returning customers. If I'm signed in or have a cookie, swap the pop-up for a slim bar that says "Welcome back, Mae – see Your Picks." Keep the 15% offer for new visitors only.
- Shorten the pop-up's legal text to one line, such as "New subscribers only, up to $30 off. Details," and link the rest.
- Put my departments in the first row of the menu, with Beauty and Shoes as plain words. Add a row of three or four big category pictures (footwear, beauty, Today's Special Value) under the hero.
