# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is home to me — I've been shopping Q for years and I know this layout the minute the page loads. Right now they're pushing a sandal sale hard, and I have to say, "Ending Tomorrow!" got my attention right away. It's classic QVC: one big offer front and center, the Today's Special Value right underneath. They know what they're doing.

## What's working

- **The 40% off sandal hero** is big, bold, and impossible to miss — the orange-red "Ending Tomorrow!" copy creates real urgency without feeling gimmicky. I've seen enough countdowns on this channel to know when it's real.
- **"Shop Sandal Sale" CTA** is exactly the right copy. Not "Discover Footwear" — I know what I'm getting if I tap that button.
- **HELLO15 welcome banner** is a nice touch with the code right there to copy — though as a repeat customer that one's not for me, it tells me Q is pulling in new people, which is good for the brand.
- **Today's Special Value — Prime Shrimp** below the fold is pure QVC DNA. I may not want shrimp today but I know that red TSV badge like I know my own name. It makes the page feel like *the show* loaded on my phone.
- **Sandal imagery flanking the hero** — the flat slide on the left and the strappy sandal on the right give me a sense of the range without having to scroll.

## What's weak

- **No Easy Pay mention anywhere** — that 5× Easy Pay is often what tips me from browsing to buying. Big miss for a higher-ticket item category like footwear.
- **No loyalty or Q Points hook** — I have rewards I could be reminded to use. A returning-customer nudge ("You have 450 Q Points!") would have me in that cart so fast.
- **Today's Special Value — Prime Shrimp** is a gear-shift from shoes. I went from "I want sandals" to "...shrimp burgers?" The page is doing two things at once and the TSV undercuts the sandal momentum.
- **No model wearing the shoes** — it's all product shots. I like to see how sandals look on an actual person, especially someone my age standing on a patio or walking a boardwalk. These flat product images are fine but they don't make me *feel* anything.
- **No free-shipping callout visible** — I know QVC has shipping policies but I can't see the threshold from this screen. That matters when I'm deciding whether to add one more thing.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Easy Pay callout to the sandal hero** — even "As low as $X with Easy Pay" right under the 40% banner. That's the number that closes the sale for people like me.
- **Swap at least one product shot for a lifestyle photo** — show a woman in her 60s walking on a boardwalk or by the pool in those sandals. I want to picture myself in them, not just look at the shoe floating in white space.
- **Move Today's Special Value below the sandal shop CTA** — let me click into shoes first, then catch me with the shrimp on the way down. Right now the TSV interrupts my decision mid-thought.
- **Add a thin loyalty/rewards bar** just below the search bar — "Q Points balance: 450 | Redeem today" — personalize the experience for returning customers without changing the overall layout.
