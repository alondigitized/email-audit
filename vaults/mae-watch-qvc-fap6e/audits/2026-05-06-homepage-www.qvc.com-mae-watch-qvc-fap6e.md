# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know my QVC and I recognize the layout the second I open the app — the "Watch" button, "Items on Air," all of it is right where I expect it to be. But I'll be honest, honey, they're pushing a Big BBQ Bash at me and I came here to check on the new Skechers or see if there's anything from Bare Escentuals on Today's Special Value. This feels like it's dressed up for somebody else's cookout, not my shopping list.

## What's working

- **"Your Picks" tab** — that's a comfort, knowing they have a lane for my history and preferences right in the nav strip
- **"Items on Air" in the top bar** — I always check what's live, and it's right there at the top where I expect it, just like on the TV app
- **"Watch" button with the play icon** — one tap and I'm watching the show; that's exactly how I use this app when I'm traveling
- **Search bar prominent at top** — when I already know the item number from the catalog or the show, I can type it straight in; that's how I shop

## What's weak

- **"New to Q?" promo banner** — I've been a customer for years and the first thing I see is an offer that doesn't apply to me; that stings a little
- **BBQ hero for a female beauty/footwear shopper** — grills and a big juicy burger don't pull me in at all; feels like a different customer entirely
- **No Easy Pay callout anywhere** — that's QVC's biggest differentiator and it's invisible above the fold; for someone like me it matters every time I'm deciding whether to tap
- **No Today's Special Value** — I open QVC expecting to see what TSV is; if I have to hunt for it, that's not right
- **No beauty or footwear category imagery** — not a single shoe, not a single blush compact in sight; I have to scroll blind hoping my categories are somewhere below

## Recommendations

- **Replace or stack alongside the BBQ hero a Today's Special Value tile** — even a small "Today's TSV" strip below the hero would anchor loyal daily visitors who come back specifically for that
- **Swap the "New to Q?" banner for a loyalty/returning-customer offer** — something like "Welcome back — $10 off your next order, Q Card members" would make me feel seen instead of left out
- **Add an Easy Pay badge or copy to the hero or sub-banner** — even "5 Easy Pays available" as a single line would remind me why I buy here and not somewhere else
- **Surface a second hero tile or category chip for Beauty or Footwear** — right below BBQ Bash, a "Shop Skechers" or "New in Beauty" chip costs one row of space and pulls in the other half of the audience
