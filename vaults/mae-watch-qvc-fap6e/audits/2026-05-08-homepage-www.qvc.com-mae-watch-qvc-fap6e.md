# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know this site like the back of my hand — I'm on QVC every single day. But I'll tell you, opening this up on my phone today, the first thing they want me to do is download their app. Honey, I already have it. The whole above-the-fold feels like it's talking to somebody who's never heard of Q, not to a loyal customer like me who's called that 1-800 number more times than I can count. I see the Today's Special Value badge peeking in at the bottom and that gives me hope, but they buried the lead.

## What's working

- **Today's Special Value badge** — that little red badge in the lower left is like a signal flare for me. I know what that means and I'll scroll down fast to find it.
- **QCard® double mention** — the 25% off with QCard® in the hero AND the 5% off strip below reinforce my loyalty. I have that card. This rewards me for it.
- **"Today Only" urgency with a hard deadline** — "now thru 11:59pm ET" is exactly the kind of thing that makes me act. I know QVC does time-sensitive offers and this one is credible.
- **Clean search bar up top** — I know exactly what I want sometimes (Skechers Slip-ins, Bare Minerals foundation) and I can just type the item number in. That's still there and easy to find.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — that's personalized for me. I see it in the nav row and I'd tap it.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is aimed at non-customers** — "Discover Our App" and "New to Q? Get $20 off" are new-customer acquisition messages. I've been shopping Q for years. This makes me feel invisible.
- **No product imagery I care about** — the only product I can see is a tiny shoe in the bottom corner from a brand called Revitalign. No Skechers, no Bare Escentuals, no comfort story, no beauty story.
- **No Easy Pay callout** — for big-ticket items Easy Pay 5× is why I buy from Q instead of somewhere else. Not a word about it above the fold.
- **The Revitalign shoe is so small I almost missed it** — if that's today's feature, give it room to breathe.
- **"Father's" tab is cut off** — whatever that last navigation tab says is truncated. Small thing, but sloppy on a phone screen.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the hero with Today's Special Value front and center** — I scroll to that every single day. Put the product, the host who's presenting it, and the Easy Pay breakdown right at the top. That's the reason I open the app.
- **Add a "Welcome back, Mae" or loyalty-balance strip** — even a simple "Your QCard® rewards: $XX" line would tell me I'm seen. Right now the page is talking to strangers.
- **Swap "Discover Our App" to "Shop Today's Deal"** — if I'm already on the site, send me to the product, not to an app store listing. The conversion is a purchase, not a download.
- **Widen the shoe image at the bottom** — if Revitalign is tied to Today's Special Value, give it a full-width lifestyle shot with the host name and price. That's what gets me to stop scrolling.
