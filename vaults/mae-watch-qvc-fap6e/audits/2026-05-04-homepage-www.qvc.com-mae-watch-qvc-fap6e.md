# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- Well, they went straight to beauty, which is right up my alley — I've been watching QVC since before they had a website, and I know a sale event when I see one. This homepage is pushing a "Big Beauty Sale" front and center, and with Bare Escentuals and skincare being my weakness, that caught my eye immediately. It feels like home, just a little lean on the little extras I'm used to — no Easy Pay callout, no Q Insider perks, no show host face saying "trust me on this one."
- **8/10**
- + Hero copy speaks to my focus area: "Big Beauty Sale" lands squarely in Bare Escentuals / makeup territory — that's my world
- + Concrete offer above fold: Two offers visible — "$15 off your $35+ order with code HELLO15" banner AND "Up to 30% off refreshers for every routine"
- + Primary CTA visible above fold: "Shop Beauty Sale" button is right there, unmissable
- + Visual hierarchy is clear: Big bold headline, clean layout, eye goes straight to the sale
- + No render bugs: Screenshot looks clean, no broken images or layout chaos
- + Current campaign/season: A spring beauty refresh in May makes complete sense seasonally
- + Offer feels honest: The HELLO15 banner includes both a "Copy" link and a "Details" link — I appreciate the transparency
- − Returning-shopper hook missing: No Q Insider badge, no rewards balance, no "Welcome back, Mae" — nothing that tells me they know I've been a customer for 20 years
- − Loyalty/membership benefits not visible: Easy Pay, Q Card perks, Q Insider — none of that above the fold
- − Demographic signals are implied, not explicit: The products fit my age (hydrating moisturizer, foundations) but nothing says "for women like me"

## What's working

- **"Big Beauty Sale" hero headline** — Big, bold, and in pink script. That feels like QVC's warmth. I know a sale event when I see one, and 30% off beauty gets my attention fast.
- **Makeup and Skin Care category tiles** — The two image tiles below the hero (Makeup with those gorgeous compacts, Skin Care with that hydrating moisturizer) are exactly what I'd tap. Clean, recognizable, easy to understand.
- **"Shop Beauty Sale" button** — Unambiguous. Not "Explore" or "Discover" — it says *shop*. I'm 64, not a 25-year-old browsing a lookbook. I want to buy.
- **HELLO15 code banner** — Visible right below the nav. Even if it's for new customers (I've been here since forever), it shows they're offering something.
- **Search bar prominence** — I know my item numbers. That search bar front and center means I can type in a number I heard on-air and go straight to it. Love that.

## What's weak

- **No Easy Pay callout anywhere above the fold** — Easy Pay is *the* reason I buy things on QVC instead of at the department store. Five payments, no interest — I budget around that. Not seeing it on the homepage feels like they forgot why I love them.
- **HELLO15 is for new customers** — I've been a QVC customer since the 90s. That banner is not for me, and I know it. Feels like they're courting strangers while ignoring the loyal crowd.
- **No human face in the hero** — QVC's whole brand is built on hosts and personalities. I know Carolyn Gracie and Amy Stran by first name. Where are they? A product flat-lay is fine, but a smiling familiar face closes the deal faster for me.
- **No loyalty hook visible** — My Q Insider status, my Q Card balance, my order history — none of that. This homepage doesn't know I exist yet.
- **No Today's Special Value callout** — That's *the* signature QVC hook. If there's a TSV running, it belongs above the fold. Its absence makes this feel like a generic retailer sale, not QVC.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Easy Pay strip directly under the hero** — Even one line: "5 Easy Payments on orders over $X — no interest." That single line is worth more to shoppers like me than any discount percentage.
- **Surface the Today's Special Value (if one is live) in the hero or in a pinned banner** — It's the most recognizable QVC franchise. A TSV strip with the product, the price, and the countdown timer belongs above the fold every single day.
- **Swap the flat-lay hero image for a host + product lifestyle shot** — Put a recognized face (Carolyn, Amy, whoever is on-air that day) holding the featured beauty product. Familiar faces convert for this audience.
- **Add a "Welcome back" or Q Insider returning-customer hook near the top** — Even a small "Your Q Insider rewards" badge with a balance number would make me feel seen and give me a reason to stay logged in and browsing.
