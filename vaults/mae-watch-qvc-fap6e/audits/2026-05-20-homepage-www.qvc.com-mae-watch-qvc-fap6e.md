# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is home — I know this site like the back of my hand. QVC is opening with a Memorial Day Clearance push, big and bold right in front of me: extra 15% off, red-white-and-blue behind it, very patriotic feeling. It's a deal-first homepage, which I appreciate, though I'll be honest — I came here hoping to see if there were any new Skechers or maybe a Bare Escentuals TSV, and clearance apparel wasn't exactly what I had in mind today.

## What's working

- **The Memorial Day banner** — red, white, and blue textile background behind the sale copy is festive and seasonal. It caught my eye immediately. I feel the holiday energy.
- **"EXTRA 15% OFF Clearance Deals"** — that word EXTRA does something to me. It's not just a sale, it's on top of already-reduced prices. That's the language QVC trained me to love.
- **"Shop Clearance" button** — dark, centered, easy to tap with my thumb. No guessing where to go.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — I noticed that right away. QVC remembers what I like. That feels personal, like the host remembering my name.
- **"Clearance Deals for You"** — that "for You" framing matters. Feels like they curated it.
- **Product thumbnails** — the women in the shots look like my kind of people, wearing my kind of comfortable everyday tops and pajamas.

## What's weak

- **No Easy Pay callout anywhere visible** — This is the single biggest miss for a loyal customer like me. Easy Pay is why I choose QVC over Amazon. If I don't see "5 Easy Payments of…" I feel like something's missing.
- **No Today's Special Value mention** — I check QVC partly to see what the TSV is. Nothing about it above the fold. That's a reason I might leave to watch the channel instead.
- **Nav cut off on the right** — I can see "Deals," "Your Picks," "New," "Trending Now," and then "Fath…" — which I assume is Father's Day — but I can't see if there's a Footwear or Beauty tab without scrolling sideways. That's frustrating with medium-sized fingers.
- **No Beauty or Footwear hook** — I shop Skechers and Bare Escentuals. Neither is teased anywhere I can see. This homepage could make me think QVC is just a clothing site today.
- **"For a limited time!" with no end date** — I like a countdown clock. David and the hosts always tell me when the TSV ends. "Limited time" with no clock feels vague and less urgent than I'm used to.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Easy Pay strip under the hero** — even "5 Easy Payments available sitewide" in small text would remind loyalists like me why we're here instead of Target.
- **Surface the Today's Special Value** — one small tile or banner with the TSV product and a countdown would pull regular viewers like me in immediately. That's QVC's signature.
- **Swap the generic lifestyle thumbnail** for a Skechers Slip-Ins or Bare Escentuals product image in the clearance rail — shows me the clearance isn't just apparel.
- **Add a visible end date or countdown timer** to the Memorial Day Sale banner — "Sale ends Monday at midnight" would have me tapping immediately.
