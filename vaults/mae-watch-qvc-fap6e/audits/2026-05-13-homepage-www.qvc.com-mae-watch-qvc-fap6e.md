# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know this site like the back of my hand — I've been a Q customer for longer than I can count — so it's always a little funny opening it fresh and seeing where they've put things now. Right now they're pushing "Summer at Home," which is very them — home goods, cleaning, décor. It's a fine store, I just wish the first thing I saw spoke a little more to me and less to somebody who just discovered QVC for the first time.

## What's working

- **"Your Picks" tab** — that's the one that tells me QVC actually knows I've been here before. I tapped it immediately in my head.
- **"Summer at Home" theme** — timely and shoppable. Floral bedding for the season, cleaning deals I can actually use. Very on-brand for Q.
- **HELLO15 promo banner** — $15 off a $35 order is a real offer with a real code. Even though it says "New to Q," the fact that a hard dollar figure is front-and-center is the kind of no-nonsense offer I respond to.
- **WATCH tab in the nav bar** — I live for the live shows. That button being right at the top is where it belongs.
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — no pop-ups jumping at me, no countdown timers screaming, just the menu and the tiles. My eyes can breathe.

## What's weak

- **"New to Q?" promo feels like a slap to loyal customers** — I've been ordering from this company since before that copywriter was born. Leading with a new-customer offer when I'm a 20-year loyalist stings a little.
- **No Today's Special Value above the fold** — that is THE reason I open QVC. No sign of it here.
- **No Easy Pay callout** — Easy Pay in 5 is half the reason I buy anything. Not one mention of it in what I can see.
- **Hero doesn't touch my categories** — nothing about footwear, nothing about beauty. Skechers and Bare Escentuals are why I'm here, and neither gets a nod.
- **No host names or live-show callout** — I know these people. Showing me a tile with David or Shawn's face would make me tap immediately.
- **"Deals on Cleaning & Org Musts"** — I'm glad it's on sale, but a Dyson vacuum is not what gets my credit card out.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the HELLO15 new-customer banner for a loyalty-member welcome back message** — even something like "Welcome back — check your Q Points balance" would make returning customers feel seen instead of skipped.
- **Add a Today's Special Value module above the fold** — it is QVC's signature offer. Pin it somewhere visible before the first scroll; it converts loyal watchers like me instantly.
- **Pull a Skechers or Bare Escentuals tile into the category row** — even one slot in that second row where I can see the product image would give me a reason to tap instead of scroll past.
- **Add a "On Air Now" or "What's Airing Today" live-show strip** — a host name and a show time turns this from a website into QVC. That's the experience I came for.
