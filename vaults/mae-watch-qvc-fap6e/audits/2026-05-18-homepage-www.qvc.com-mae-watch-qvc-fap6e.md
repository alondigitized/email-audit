# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll tell you — the first thing I see when I open the app is that big red, white and blue Memorial Day Sale banner, which is right on time since the holiday's coming up. QVC is clearly pushing clearance this week, and they're doing it loudly. It feels very much like the Q I know — familiar layout, simple enough to navigate — but if I'm being honest, I didn't come here for clearance towels. I came to check on my Skechers and see what's new in beauty.

## What's working

- **The Memorial Day Sale hero** — big, bold, red-white-and-blue. I know exactly what's being promoted. No guessing.
- **"EXTRA 15% OFF Clearance Deals"** — a specific number, not some wishy-washy "up to savings" language. I appreciate that.
- **The "Shop Clearance" button** — dark navy, full-width, can't miss it. One tap and I'm in.
- **"Clearance Picks for You"** — I like that it's trying to personalize. The women shown in that little product strip look like they could be my age, wearing comfortable colorful tops. That catches my eye.
- **"Your Picks" tab in the nav** — nice to know the Q still remembers I've been around.

## What's weak

- **The HELLO15 promo banner** — "New to Q? Get $15 off" — I've been shopping here since before that banner was an idea. That's not for me and it takes up real estate at the top.
- **No sign of Skechers, no sign of beauty anywhere** — those are my reasons to open the app. I have to go hunting.
- **No Easy Pay mention** — that's one of the big reasons I buy on QVC. Five easy payments makes a $90 shoe feel like $18. Not seeing that upfront is a miss.
- **"For a limited time!"** on the 15% off — that's vague. Is it ending tonight? Sunday? Give me a date or a clock.
- **No host names, no show references** — part of what makes QVC *QVC* is knowing David is on right now, or that Carolyn just showed something. None of that community feel here.
- **No Today's Special Value or countdown** — that's practically the heartbeat of this network and it's not above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the HELLO15 new-customer banner with an Easy Pay reminder** — "5 Easy Payments — Always Available" or highlight the current TSV with a countdown timer. That is QVC's biggest conversion driver and loyal shoppers need to see it.
- **Add a "Today's Special Value" strip** just below the search bar — even just the item name and the Easy Pay amount. That's what keeps me coming back daily.
- **Swap the hero background image** — red, white and blue terry towels don't tell me what's on sale. A lifestyle shot of a real woman in comfortable clothes, or a pair of Skechers Slip-ins, would land harder for shoppers like me.
- **Make the deadline on the sale real** — "Ends Monday at Midnight" beats "For a limited time!" every single time. I want to feel the clock, not guess at it.
