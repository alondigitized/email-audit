# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Well, QVC is doing a Big Beauty Sale and they want me to know about it the second I land — I can appreciate that kind of energy. The peach-and-coral color scheme feels very on-brand for a beauty push, and I recognized a BY TERRY compact right away, which is a brand I've seen on air plenty of times. But I'm a loyal Q customer who calls in and watches every day, and the biggest promo right there at the top is one of those "New to Q?" discount codes — honey, I've been with QVC for years, so that offer has nothing to do with me.

## What's working

- **Big Beauty Sale hero banner:** The coral-and-peach palette plus that red "BIG BEAUTY SALE" badge gets my attention immediately — it's the kind of urgency language QVC uses on air and I respond to it.
- **"Limited Time!" badge:** That little pill badge on the right side of the free shipping offer gives me just enough nudge. I know from watching the show that these windows close.
- **BY TERRY compact in "New Arrivals":** I recognized that brand immediately. Beautiful product photography — looks like something Antonella or Carolyn would hold up on camera.
- **"Your Picks" tab:** As someone who shops Q regularly, seeing a tab that sounds like it's curated just for me makes me want to tap it.
- **Search bar front and center:** I sometimes call in with an item number, but on the app I type it in. Having that search bar right at the top makes me feel like I can get to what I want fast.

## What's weak

- **"New to Q?" promo banner:** That HELLO15 code is the most prominent text offer on the page, and it's explicitly for new customers. As someone who's been watching QVC for years, this feels like they're ignoring me entirely.
- **No CTA button above the fold:** I see the beauty sale headline and the free shipping message, but there's no button to tap. I shouldn't have to guess that I need to tap the banner image.
- **No people in the hero:** The background is a blurry peachy texture and the products are floating in it. I like to see someone wearing something or using a product — that's how QVC sells on TV, and it works.
- **No mention of Easy Pay:** I depend on Easy Pay for larger beauty purchases. Not seeing it mentioned even once means I might hesitate on a bigger-ticket item.
- **Skechers nowhere in sight:** I know this visit is landing on a beauty sale, but as a loyal Skechers customer I was hoping to see at least a path to footwear without hunting for it.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the HELLO15 banner for a loyalty acknowledgment:** Show returning customers something like "Welcome back — here's your exclusive member offer" instead of a new-customer discount. That one change would make regulars like me feel seen.
- **Add a visible "Shop the Sale" button to the hero:** The banner text is doing all the heavy lifting. A single tappable button in high contrast would make it obvious where to go next.
- **Put a lifestyle model in the hero:** Even a single over-50 woman applying the BY TERRY compact would make me stop and say "that's me." Product-only imagery is fine for product tiles but not for a sale hero.
- **Add a small Easy Pay callout near the free shipping line:** Something like "Plus, Easy Pay available on select items" costs nothing and could keep me from bouncing on a higher-priced item.
