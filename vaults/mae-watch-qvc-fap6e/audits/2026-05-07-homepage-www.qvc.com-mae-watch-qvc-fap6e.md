# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is home to me — I've been watching QVC for years and the moment I see that "Q" logo and the "Items on Air" button up top, I feel right at home. They're pushing a big Summer of Savings event right now, which makes sense for May, and I can already see there are some Just-Dropped Specials below. It's a clean, friendly page — nothing fancy, nothing confusing — but I do wish they'd shown me something closer to what I actually shop for rather than a generic sale banner.

## What's working

- **"Items on Air" button in the top nav** — I know exactly what that means and I tap it on instinct. That's pure gold for a daily QVC watcher.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — makes me feel recognized as a returning customer without making a big fuss about it.
- **"Summer of Savings" with a gingham background** — the pink-and-red plaid pattern feels warm and homey, not flashy or intimidating.
- **HELLO15 promo banner** — even though I can't use it, the fact that the Copy button is right there shows they thought about making redemption easy for someone who's not super techy.
- **"Just-Dropped Specials" section** — I can already see what looks like a makeup product at the bottom, which perks my ears up.

## What's weak

- **The hero says nothing about footwear or beauty** — "Deals are coming in hot for the warm weather ahead" could be for any store in America. I've been loyal to Skechers and Bare Escentuals for years and there's no wink to either.
- **No Easy Pay callout above the fold** — Easy Pay 5× is the reason I buy bigger-ticket items on QVC and I'd love to see that right in the hero or CTA.
- **No Today's Special Value mention** — that's my ritual. I check it every day. It should be front and center for someone like me.
- **HELLO15 excludes me by definition** — "New to Q?" I've been a customer for years, dear. Leading with a new-customer discount when your loyal base is opening the app stings a little.
- **No hosts mentioned, no show schedule teased** — I know Lisa Robertson is gone but I still want to see a familiar face or a "coming up next" reminder. That's what makes QVC different from Amazon.

## Recommendations

- **Replace or supplement the hero with a Today's Special Value module** — a dedicated TSV tile with the countdown timer and Easy Pay badge is what loyal daily shoppers like me are coming back for; it only takes a banner strip above or below the main hero.
- **Add an Easy Pay callout inside the "Shop the Event" CTA area** — something like "as low as 5 Easy Pays" next to the button would move me from browsing to buying much faster.
- **Swap the gingham hero for a lifestyle shot featuring a woman 55+** — show someone my age relaxing in Skechers or glowing in Bare Escentuals. That's who's actually shopping here.
- **Pin a "What's on Air Now" strip below the nav tabs** — one-line, current show name, host, and item number. I'd tap it every single time.
