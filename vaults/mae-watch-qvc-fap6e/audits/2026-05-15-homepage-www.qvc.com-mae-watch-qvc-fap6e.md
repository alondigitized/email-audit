# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know this site like the back of my hand — I'm on QVC practically every morning with my coffee. Tonight they're going all-out on jewelry, and I have to say the "Ends Tonight! ALL JEWELRY ON SALE" banner is exactly the kind of urgency that makes me reach for the phone. It feels like a real event, like when David Venable does the Today's Special and the ticker's counting down. That said, I came in looking for my Skechers or something from Bare Escentuals, and there's not a hint of beauty or shoes anywhere on this screen.

## What's working

- **"Ends Tonight! ALL JEWELRY ON SALE"** — That red countdown language is pure QVC magic. It's exactly what the hosts say when the clock is ticking on a Today's Special. I feel the pull immediately.
- **"Your Picks" tab** — I love that QVC knows I'm a returning shopper and has a spot just for me right in the nav. That's the kind of personal touch that keeps me loyal.
- **SALE PRICE badges on every visible product tile** — I can see three rings and bracelets right away, all marked SALE PRICE. No hunting around to find out if there's a deal. Clear as can be.
- **"This is our kind of treasure hunt"** — That copy sounds just like the hosts would say it on air. It feels warm and familiar, not like some cold corporate ad. I know this voice.
- **JAI brand jewelry** — I recognize JAI from watching the show. Seeing a familiar brand name right away builds confidence that this is the real deal.

## What's weak

- **The HELLO15 new-customer code** — I've been shopping QVC for years and that banner says "New to Q?" That's not for me. It actually stings a little — where's my loyalty reward? They're rolling out the red carpet for strangers while I'm standing in the hall.
- **No shoes, no beauty, nothing for my categories** — My Skechers and my bareMinerals are nowhere on this screen. If I didn't love QVC already, I'd think this was strictly a jewelry site.
- **No people in the imagery** — Those product tiles are just rings and bracelets floating on white. I like seeing how jewelry looks on a real person, especially someone my age with hands that look like mine.
- **No visible CTA button** — The hero is bold but there's no "Shop the Sale" or "See All Jewelry" button. I have to know to tap a product tile, which is fine for me, but it's not obvious.
- **Nav tabs cut off** — I can see "Deals, Your Picks, New, Trending Now, Fath—" and then it's clipped. I can't tell if that's Father's Day or something else. Feels unpolished.

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty-member offer strip** — Replace or supplement the HELLO15 new-customer banner with something for returning shoppers: "Q Card members get an extra 10%" or "Your rewards: $12 available." That would make me feel seen instead of overlooked.
- **Add one lifestyle shot with a real woman wearing the jewelry** — Show me someone closer to my age wearing that gold chain bracelet or the diamond ring. Let me picture it on myself, not just on a jewelry pad.
- **Surface a "Beauty" or "Shoes" quick-link** in the featured nav tabs — even a small tile below the hero that says "Beauty Deals" or "Comfort Shoes" would give me a reason to explore beyond what's on screen right now.
- **Add a "Shop the Sale" button to the hero** — One clear button below "This is our kind of treasure hunt" so I don't have to guess where to tap.
