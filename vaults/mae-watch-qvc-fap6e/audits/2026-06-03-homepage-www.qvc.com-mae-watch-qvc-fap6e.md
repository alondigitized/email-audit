# Homepage snapshot · www.qvc.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Well, I opened QVC.com and the first thing that hits me is that big "Anniversary Clearance Sale — Up to 70% Off" banner right in the middle, which honestly makes me sit up straight. QVC knows how to talk to me — clearance, savings, celebrate — that's my language. But I'll tell you, the page feels a little stripped down on my phone, and I'm already wondering where my Skechers and Bare Escentuals are hiding.

## What's working

- **"Anniversary Clearance Sale — Up to 70% Off" hero** — This is exactly the kind of event language QVC has trained me to respond to over 20 years. I know what an anniversary sale means and I trust it.
- **The clearance percentage tiles ("At Least 50% Off," "At Least 60% Off")** — Giving me a clear filtering shortcut by discount level is smart. I can tap "60% Off" and feel like I'm hunting for the best deal, which is half the fun.
- **Clean mobile layout** — The top nav with MENU, WATCH, search bar, and CART is familiar and easy to read. Nothing is crowded or overlapping.
- **"Your Picks" tab in the navigation strip** — Even though it's not what's loaded, just seeing that label tells me QVC remembers me. I like that.

## What's weak

- **No product imagery at all** — The hero is entirely typographic and decorative (repeating "70%" watermark pattern). I want to see a pair of Skechers or a Bare Minerals palette, not abstract numbers. Show me something I want.
- **"All sales final" callout directly under the hero** — That stops me cold. I order by phone a lot, and sometimes things don't fit. Clearance being no-returns is a real hesitation, and putting it right there in the hero feels like a speed bump.
- **No Easy Pay visibility** — Easy Pay is my #1 reason I stay loyal to QVC. I don't see any mention of it above the fold. For a clearance sale, knowing I can split it into 5 payments would get me tapping faster.
- **No host or brand names** — QVC's magic is the host relationship. I know these hosts. Showing "Kim Gravel picks" or "Carolyn Gracie's Anniversary Favorites" would make this feel like QVC, not just a generic sale page.
- **The Q logo is highlighted/selected in the nav** — I'm not totally sure what that means. Am I on a special Q-search mode? It looks like a button I accidentally pressed.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the all-type hero for a lifestyle hero image** — Show a woman my age in comfortable, stylish shoes or holding a beauty product with a big smile. Put the "70% Off" badge over the image, not on a blank peachy background.
- **Add Easy Pay callout to the hero** — One line: "As low as 5 Easy Pays" right under the discount badge would change everything for shoppers like me who budget carefully.
- **Surface a brand rail above the fold** — A row of brand logos (Skechers, Bare Escentuals, Quacker Factory, Dooney) below the hero would let loyal customers like me jump straight to what we know and trust.
- **Replace or clarify the "All sales final" notice** — If returns are truly restricted, keep the notice, but soften or move it below the fold. Or add "Easy exchanges on select items" to reassure me.
