# Homepage snapshot · www.hannaandersson.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get the homepage — I got a bot wall. The entire viewport is a "Before we continue... Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)." challenge screen with a single blue outlined button and a reference ID at the bottom. Zero Hanna content, zero product, zero brand. On a Sunday morning coffee scroll this would have sent me straight back to my inbox.

## What's working

- The bot challenge is clean and unambiguous — the "Press & Hold" instruction is readable and the button is large enough to tap on mobile. If I had to interact with a wall, at least it's not broken.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked.** I'm a logged-in-ish loyal customer opening the site on a Sunday morning and I see a gray screen instead of stripes.
- **No brand context whatsoever.** Not a logo, not a color, not a stripe. I have no way to know I'm even on Hanna's site until I recognize the URL — a first-time visitor would just close the tab.
- **"Press & Hold" on mobile is awkward.** This interaction is designed for desktop mouse-down; on a phone touchscreen it feels clunky and slightly paranoid for what is, functionally, a kids' clothing store.
- **No fallback messaging.** There's no "having trouble? tap here" or accessibility alternative visible.

## Recommendations

- **Whitelist common mobile carriers and returning-customer cookies** so loyal shoppers don't hit the bot wall on a standard Sunday-morning Safari session.
- **If the challenge must appear, brand it.** Drop in the Hanna logo and those iconic colored stripes as a background so I at least know where I am.
- **Add a tap-hold accessibility note** — "having trouble? Try tapping and holding for 3 seconds" — since "Press & Hold" reads as a desktop affordance.
- **Log and alert on challenge-wall hit rates by device type.** If mobile organic traffic is hitting this at scale, Hanna is hemorrhaging exactly the Sunday-morning mom segment they depend on for semi-annual sale revenue.
