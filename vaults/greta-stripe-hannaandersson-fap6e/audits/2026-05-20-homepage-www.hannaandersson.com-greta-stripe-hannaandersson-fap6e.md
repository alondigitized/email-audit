# Homepage snapshot · www.hannaandersson.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened hannaandersson.com on my phone Sunday morning, coffee in hand, ready to stock up before Memorial Day — and the first thing I got was a bot-check gate. Not a hero shot of kids in stripes, not a sale banner, just a blank white screen demanding I "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human." Zero branding, zero product, zero Hanna. This is not a homepage review — this is a review of a wall that blocked me before I ever saw the homepage.

## What's working

- The "Press & Hold" button is legible and the instruction is technically clear — if I were willing to jump through the hoop, I'd know exactly what to do.
- Reference ID is visible at the bottom, which at least suggests something is running underneath — it's not a full 404.

## What's weak

- **Everything that matters is absent.** No logo, no brand color, no product imagery, no navigation — a loyal Hanna customer who opened this link would have zero confirmation they're even on the right site.
- **The bot gate is unbranded.** Competitors like Pottery Barn Kids and Gap Kids who use similar challenges still show their nav bar or logo behind the challenge. This is a completely blank white screen.
- **It creates instant distrust.** I'm a repeat customer opening my Sunday email. Being asked to "prove I'm human" before I see a single product feels hostile, not welcoming.
- **Mobile friction at the worst moment.** Sunday morning, one hand on my coffee, one thumb on my phone — a "Press & Hold" interaction on mobile is genuinely awkward and easy to fumble.
- **No brand reassurance.** If I clicked a phishing link, this screen would look exactly the same. There's nothing here that says "yes, you're safe, this is Hanna Andersson."

## Recommendations

- **Brand the gate.** Add the Hanna Andersson logo and brand-color stripe bar at the top of the challenge screen so I immediately know I'm in the right place.
- **Swap "Press & Hold" for a less friction-y mobile check.** A tap-based checkbox (like Google reCAPTCHA) is far easier on mobile one-handed than a sustained press gesture.
- **Show a ghost/skeleton of the homepage behind the gate.** Even a blurred-out hero image with stripes tells me "yes, kid clothes, you're home" before I complete the challenge.
- **Consider removing the gate for returning customers with cookies.** I've bought here a dozen times — a recognized session shouldn't be hitting a bot wall.
