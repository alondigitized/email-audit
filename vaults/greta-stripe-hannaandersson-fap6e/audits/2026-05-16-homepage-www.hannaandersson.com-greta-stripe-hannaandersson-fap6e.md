# Homepage snapshot · www.hannaandersson.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened hannaandersson.com on my phone Sunday morning, coffee in hand, ready to scope the sale — and the homepage never showed up. What I got instead was a bot-verification wall: "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)." The entire first screen is a gray void with that single button. No hero, no stripes, no kids, no product — nothing. As a shopper who visits this site constantly, this is a jarring, off-brand first impression that immediately puts me on the defensive.

## What's working

- The "Press & Hold" button is visually clear and tappable on mobile — if I must do this, at least the interaction target is big enough for a mom juggling a toddler on her hip.
- The Reference ID (`472ef961-5155-11f1-8972-3fb09fa180fd`) is present in small print, which at least means customer support *could* use it — though I'd never know to screenshot it.

## What's weak

- **The homepage is completely invisible.** I came to shop and I'm staring at a blank screen with a bot challenge. Zero brand presence, zero product, zero reason to stay.
- **"Press & Hold" on mobile is physically awkward.** Holding a phone with one hand while pressing and holding with the other, trying not to spill coffee — this is not the Sunday-morning UX I expect from a premium brand.
- **No Hanna branding anywhere.** The page doesn't even say "Hanna Andersson." A first-time visitor would have no idea where they landed. The reference ID at the bottom does nothing to reassure me I'm in the right place.
- **No fallback messaging.** There's no "We'll be right back" or "Having trouble? Contact us." It just… stops.
- **Completely kills returning-shopper momentum.** I came with intent to browse the sale and this wall resets all of that.

## Recommendations

- **Move bot detection to a less disruptive method** — passive signals (behavioral biometrics, invisible reCAPTCHA v3) so loyal customers aren't manually gated on every visit. If the challenge is truly necessary, at minimum show the Hanna logo and a one-line reassurance like "Just a quick check — we'll have you shopping in a second."
- **Add brand context to the challenge page** — the iconic stripes, the logo, even a tagline. Right now it looks like a generic Cloudflare wall, not a beloved kids' clothing brand.
- **Test the mobile "Press & Hold" interaction on actual phones.** One-handed use with a toddler around is the norm for this customer; a standard checkbox or slider would be far less friction.
- **Instrument this event in analytics.** If this challenge is firing for returning authenticated customers, that's a conversion-killing false-positive rate that should be caught and fixed immediately.
