# ❤️ Welcome To Hanna!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- A well-constructed triggered welcome email that earns its keep. Hanna Andersson deploys every standard welcome-series lever — first-order discount, brand values, loyalty teaser, category quick-links — without clutter. The beach/summer hero is seasonally right for May. The chief weakness is generic subject + CTA copy that treats "SHOP NOW" as a complete sentence; nothing personalizes this to the individual subscriber's apparent interest signal (how did they sign up? through what category?). As welcome emails go, this one executes close to the ceiling of the format.

## What's working

- **Top-banner offer is impossible to miss.** 20% off + visible code sits above the fold before any image loads. That's good defensive design for clipped-image environments.
- **Brand values module is well-executed.** "made to last / made to play / made responsibly" with simple icons builds emotional justification for the higher price point without being heavy-handed.
- **Hanna Rewards placement is smart.** Tucking the loyalty pitch after the values section — not at the very top — lets the brand earn trust before asking for the relationship upgrade. The "just join for free" copy removes friction.
- **Category grid is clean and actionable.** Girls / Pajamas / Baby / Boys covers the main buckets a new shopper would reach for.
- **Seasonal relevance.** The beach chair and umbrella hero, plus SWIM in the rewards category row, aligns with early-summer purchase timing.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" twice.** Both CTAs are identical generic verbs. Neither tells the new subscriber what to shop or why to click this one vs. the other.
- **No personalization hook.** There's zero reference to how the subscriber found Hanna — no "We noticed you were browsing pajamas" or "Here's what Greta would pick" — making this feel mass-blast rather than lifecycle.
- **No urgency on the discount.** The 20% code has no visible expiry. That's a double-edged sword: low pressure, but it can also suppress immediacy ("I'll use it later" → forgotten).
- **Rewards section is underserved.** It lists categories (RED, WHITE & NEW!, PAJAMAS, CHARACTERS, SWIM) without explaining what the reward program actually does beyond free shipping. A one-line "earn points on every order" would close the loop.
- **Hero copy is good but unremarkable.** "Say hello to comfort and quality that kids actually want to wear" is pleasant brand voice, not a reason to click today.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace both "SHOP NOW" buttons with intent-specific copy.** "Shop Girls' Summer Looks" and "Start Earning Rewards" are more clickable than two identical nudges.
- 2. **Add an expiry to the 20% code.** Even "valid for 30 days" converts better than open-ended — it creates a filing date in the subscriber's mental calendar.
- 3. **Add one sentence to Hanna Rewards.** "Earn 1 point per $1. 100 points = $5 reward." Concrete mechanics prompt sign-up; abstract benefits don't.
- 4. **A/B test a second hero variant** that shows the signature stripe print close-up — it's the brand's visual fingerprint and a stronger differentiator than beach lifestyle photography any apparel brand could buy.
- 5. **Consider a conditional variant** for the first-purchase incentive block: if the subscriber came through a pajamas landing page, open the email on pajamas. Welcome emails have 3-5x normal open rates — max the leverage.
