# Homepage snapshot · www.hannaandersson.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened hannaandersson.com on my phone and the first thing I see is not stripes, not kids, not sale banners — it's a bot gate. "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)." That's it. The entire above-the-fold experience is a blank white screen with a verification button and a reference ID. As a loyal customer who shops this site every semi-annual sale, this is a jarring, trust-eroding wall before I've seen a single product.

## What's working

- The bot-check page renders without layout errors — the text is legible, the button is clearly tappable on mobile, and the reference ID is present for support escalation.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked.** I am a loyal, high-frequency Hanna shopper and the first thing the site shows me is "prove you're human." That's an immediate trust hit.
- **Zero brand presence above the fold.** No logo, no stripes, no kids, no copy — nothing that says "you're in the right place." If I didn't already know this URL, I'd assume I landed on a phishing page.
- **No bypass or recognition for returning users.** A cookie-bearing repeat customer hitting a hard bot wall with no "already a member? sign in" escape is a UX failure.
- **The Press & Hold mechanic is awkward on mobile.** On a phone, press-and-hold triggers OS-level context menus; this interaction pattern is hostile on the device segment where Hanna's core shopper (Sunday-morning, coffee in hand, phone in lap) lives.

## Recommendations

- **Tune bot-detection thresholds to exempt known returning sessions** — a customer with a valid auth cookie or recognized device fingerprint should never hit this wall. I've been shopping here for years; my phone should not be treated like a scraper.
- **If a verification step is unavoidable, brand it.** Replace the blank white screen with the Hanna logo, a stripe motif, and copy like "Just a quick check before we show you the good stuff" — at minimum, reassure the shopper they're in the right place.
- **Replace Press & Hold with a checkbox or SMS OTP** — the press-and-hold interaction pattern is unreliable on iOS and Android and triggers system UI conflicts.
- **Add a "Sign in to skip" path** — let logged-in loyalty members bypass the gate entirely, which rewards account creation and reduces friction for the highest-value cohort.
