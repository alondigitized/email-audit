# Homepage snapshot · www.hannaandersson.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- I opened hannaandersson.com on my phone Sunday morning with my coffee, ready to check for the semi-annual sale, and got hit with a full-screen bot challenge — "Before we continue... Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)." The entire homepage is blocked. Zero product, zero promo, zero brand. As a loyal customer who has been buying Hanna stripes for six years, this is a frustrating first impression before I've even seen a single item.

## What's working

- The bot-check button renders cleanly and the instruction text is legible — at least the challenge itself isn't broken.
- The reference ID at the bottom (`3fb3aebb-5cd9-11f1-9cff-262eb3824dac`) means this is a traceable event, which is good for Hanna's ops team even if it's invisible to me.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is gated.** A loyal returning customer on mobile, opening the site the normal way, is treated the same as a scraper bot. That friction is brand damage on a Sunday morning.
- **No brand identity whatsoever.** Not a logo, not a color, not a stripe. If I didn't already know the URL I'd have no idea whose site this is.
- **The "Press & Hold" mechanic is not mobile-intuitive.** On a phone with coffee in one hand and a toddler in the other, a sustained press-and-hold is annoying in a way a simple tap-to-confirm isn't.
- **No fallback content.** Even a skeleton or a nav bar would signal that a real page is behind this wall. Right now it looks like the site is down.
- **Zero urgency or offer.** If there's a sale running today, I have no idea — and the window I had to browse is closing fast.

## Recommendations

- **Tune the bot-detection threshold for returning mobile users.** Someone hitting the site from a known device/session shouldn't clear a CAPTCHA every visit. Whitelist returning customers with cookies or loyalty tokens before triggering the challenge.
- **Add the Hanna Andersson logo and a brand color strip above the challenge.** One stripe header costs nothing and tells me I'm in the right place while I pass the gate.
- **Swap "Press & Hold" for a single tap confirm on mobile.** A sustained press is a two-handed gesture; this customer base (moms with babies) is often one-handed.
- **If the challenge clears quickly, pre-load the hero beneath it** so the transition to actual content is instant rather than a second white-screen flash.
