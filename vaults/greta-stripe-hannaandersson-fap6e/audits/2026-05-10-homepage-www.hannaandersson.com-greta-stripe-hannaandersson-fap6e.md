# Homepage snapshot · www.hannaandersson.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I opened the site on a Sunday morning expecting to browse new arrivals and maybe check if the semi-annual sale is still on — and instead I'm staring at a bot-check wall. This is not the Hanna Andersson homepage; it's a Cloudflare or similar challenge page that blocked the render entirely. There is zero brand content, zero product imagery, zero navigation visible — just a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" prompt on a blank white screen.

## What's working

- Nothing is working. The only element on screen is the bot-check interstitial. There is no brand content to evaluate positively.

## What's weak

- **The bot wall itself** — a real Sunday-morning mobile shopper hits this and most will just close the tab. Press & Hold is not intuitive on a phone browser, and there's no explanation of why this is happening.
- **Complete absence of brand identity** — white background, gray text, blue outlined button. Zero Hanna color palette, zero stripes, zero warmth. If I didn't already know the URL I'd have no idea whose site this is.
- **Reference ID exposed** (`f9ff2d0e-4c9d-11f1-886c-1afb9146a64e`) at the bottom — feels cold and technical, not the cozy family brand I'm here for.
- **No fallback or loading state** — there's no skeleton, no logo, no "hang tight" — just a blank challenge page that communicates nothing about the brand.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the bot-detection configuration for real mobile browsers** — legitimate shoppers on Safari/iOS should not be hitting a Cloudflare challenge wall. Tune the sensitivity so mobile UAs aren't flagged.
- **If a challenge must appear, brand it** — add the Hanna logo, the stripe motif, and warm copy ("Just a quick check — your stripes await!") so the friction at least feels on-brand and doesn't spike bounce rate.
- **Add a visible loading/redirect indicator** — after the hold, show a branded transition so the shopper knows something is happening rather than staring at a white screen.
- **Monitor the block rate on mobile** — if this fires even occasionally on real shoppers during the semi-annual sale, it's revenue walking out the door.
