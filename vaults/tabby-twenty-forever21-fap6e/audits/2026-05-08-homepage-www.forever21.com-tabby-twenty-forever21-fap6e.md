# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay so F21 is giving main-character energy right now — the hero is a dark, moody video of a model in a ribbed striped set with "SHOP SUMMER '26" overlaid, which is very much the vibe. But the second I landed here, a cookie consent modal slapped itself over half the screen, which is such a buzzkill. It looks like a summer-forward fast-fashion brand doing its thing, but the page is doing the bare minimum to get me to actually tap anything.

## What's working

- **"GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!" banner** — it's at the very top, contrasting yellow strip, hard to miss. That's a real incentive.
- **Hero video with Summer '26 copy** — the muted autoplay video of the model in the striped ribbed set is genuinely current and on-trend. It doesn't feel stale at all.
- **Second model below the fold** — the Black female model in the all-black one-shoulder look that's starting to come into view is exactly the kind of inspo content that makes me want to keep scrolling.
- **Clean branding** — "FOREVER 21" logo is crisp, centered, professional for a mobile nav. Brand identity is immediately clear.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocks the actual homepage** — a huge consent overlay ("We use cookies... OK! / Manage preferences") is covering the second module entirely. I can't see what's below the hero, which is supposed to be the part that converts.
- **No visible CTA button on the hero** — "SHOP SUMMER '26" is just text floating on the image. Where do I tap? It's not styled like a button.
- **No F21 Rewards hook** — as a Rewards member I want to see my points or at least a "Members get X" callout. Nothing.
- **Muted video with no sound prompt** — there's a muted speaker icon in the lower left. If this video has audio, I don't know whether to unmute. If it doesn't matter, the icon is just noise.
- **App-first discount feels extractive** — the 20% off is gated behind downloading the app for first-timers. I already have the app. What's in it for me?

## Recommendations

- **Add a visible CTA button over the hero** — put a pill button that says "Shop Summer '26" or "Shop the Drop" directly on the hero image so it's actually tappable with clear affordance.
- **Surface F21 Rewards in the top banner** — rotate the banner or add a second slide that says "You have X points — shop now" for logged-in members. That's the returning-shopper hook that's completely missing.
- **Delay the cookie modal by 3 seconds or make it a bottom sheet** — popping it immediately blocks the second module. Let me see the page first, then ask.
- **Add a "New Arrivals" or "Trending Now" category strip just below the hero** — even one row of category tiles (Tops, Dresses, Sets, Accessories) gives me a one-tap path to what I actually want to browse.
