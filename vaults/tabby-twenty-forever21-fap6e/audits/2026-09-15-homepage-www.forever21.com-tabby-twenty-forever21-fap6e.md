# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay this looks exactly like Forever 21 should look — bold, loud, sale-first. The second I land I'm hit with "UP TO 80% OFF SITEWIDE" in massive white letters over a moody basketball-court photoshoot, plus a top banner code for 20% off my first app order. It's giving "we know you're here for the clearance rack" energy, which, correct, I am.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 80% OFF SITEWIDE" hero text — huge, bold, impossible to miss, exactly the kind of headline that makes me stop scrolling
- The photoshoot itself — sweatpants + crop tank + basketball, polka-dot shacket — that's giving off-duty mall-run fit, very on-brand for me
- Top sticky banner "20% OFF FIRST APP ORDER — CODE: APPMVP20" — I'm tech-comfortable so an app incentive actually lands with me
- Nav bar has SALE in red text, separated from the rest — makes it obvious where the deals live

## What's weak

- No F21 Rewards callout anywhere above the fold — I'm a member and there's zero acknowledgment of that, no points balance, nothing
- That black "USE CODE: APPMVP20" floating chip is sitting awkwardly on top of the cookie consent banner at the very bottom — looks like a glitch, not intentional
- Nothing here says "jewelry" or "accessories" even though that's half my shopping personality — the hero is all loungewear/sport, no statement pieces in sight
- "Up to 80% off" with zero specifics feels like classic bait — I've been burned by "up to" sales where the actual stuff I want is 10% off

## Recommendations

- Swap in a small "Welcome back, [Rewards member]" strip or points teaser right under the promo banner
- Fix the z-index/overlap so the discount code chip doesn't sit on top of the cookie banner
- Add one accessories/jewelry piece into the hero image rotation or a quick second banner for it
- Replace "up to 80% off" with a real anchor stat like "60-80% off sale styles" so it doesn't feel like a stretch
