# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Now I have what I need. Writing the full review as Tabby Twenty.
- Okay so first thing I see is the cookie banner blocking like half the screen, which immediately annoyed me. But underneath that? The vibe is actually cute — "IT'S GIVING SUMMER" with two girls in matching striped sets, palm trees, the whole thing. F21 is clearly pushing summer basics right now, and the energy is right for me, but the execution has some friction I wasn't expecting from a brand I shop regularly.

## What's working

- **"IT'S GIVING SUMMER" hero copy** — this is literally how I talk. It's not corporate-trying-to-be-Gen-Z, it just *is* the phrase. I felt seen immediately.
- **The model casting** — two girls who actually look like they could be at my mall, not runway models. One lighter-skinned, one darker-skinned, both in their early 20s. Diverse without being performative.
- **The striped matching sets** — I would genuinely buy those. The styling shows me exactly how to wear the pieces together, which makes me want to shop the look.
- **App discount banner** — "20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!" is a clean, specific hook at the very top. If I didn't have the app yet, I'd be downloading it right now.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** — it's covering the bottom third of the hero and there's no way to see what's below it without tapping OK first. I should not have to interact with legalese before I can see what you're selling me.
- **No loyalty hook for existing members** — I already have F21 Rewards. The app banner says "first order" which means it doesn't apply to me. Nothing on screen tells me what I *do* get as a returning rewards member.
- **No shop CTA visible** — I can see the hero, I can see the vibe, but there's no "Shop Summer" or "Shop Now" button in sight. The hero doesn't tell me where to tap to actually buy those sets.
- **Hamburger menu only** — nav is fully collapsed. I can't see categories at a glance, so I have to do extra work to find jewelry, tops, dresses — the things I actually came for.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default or reduce it to a thin banner** — a full-screen overlay on mobile before I see a single product is a bouncer at the door. Move it to a slim bottom bar so I can see the full hero immediately.
- **Add a "Shop the Look" or "Shop Summer Sets" CTA button directly on the hero** — right now the image is just vibes with nowhere to go. One button, bright contrast, placed just above where the cookie bar sits.
- **Swap the top banner message for returning users** — if I'm logged into Rewards, show me "You have 200 points — redeem today" instead of a first-app-order offer that doesn't apply to me.
- **Expose two or three category chips below the hero** — "Tops," "Dresses," "Jewelry" as tap targets so I can get to my category in one tap instead of opening the full hamburger menu.
