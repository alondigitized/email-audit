# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- OK so Forever21 is literally my store — I'm an F21 Rewards member, I know the site — but this homepage lowkey felt like it interrupted itself before I could even see what was going on. The "BESTIE" energy in the top banner? Cute. But a cookie modal slamming down over half the page the second it loads is such a vibe killer. The brand is clearly pushing Summer '26 with what looks like a sleek editorial hero, but the execution is fighting itself.

## What's working

- **"DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW, BESTIE. SIGN UP"** — that "BESTIE" copy is *so* on-brand for F21's Gen Z audience, feels authentic not cringe
- **"SHOP SUMMER '26" hero label** — clean, current, I immediately know what season we're in
- **Hero model** — young Black woman in a strappy striped jumpsuit/set, that's literally the aesthetic I come to F21 for
- **Second image below hero** — the off-shoulder black look is clean and I'd tap it, tells me editorial product content is right below the fold
- **Hamburger + search + bag** — nav is minimal and familiar, I know how to use this without thinking

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the content** — this is a full-width overlay that covers both images the second the page loads; I can't see what I came here for
- **No offer whatsoever above the fold** — F21 almost always has a sale going and there's nothing here: no "up to 50% off," no free-ship nudge, nothing
- **Email signup banner ≠ loyalty hook** — I'm an F21 Rewards member and there's zero acknowledgment of that; just a generic "SIGN UP" that doesn't tell me what I'm signing up *for*
- **Autoplay video with mute icon** — the 🔇 icon means something is autoplaying; on mobile that can be slow-loading or jarring, and I didn't ask for it
- **No category navigation visible** — everything is behind a hamburger; I can't see Women's, Sale, New Arrivals without an extra tap

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal faster or shrink it** — make it a bottom strip banner, not a full overlay; right now it's covering two products worth of content on first load
- **Add a Sale or "up to X% off" strip** — F21 always has something going; surface it in the top bar instead of (or alongside) the email CTA so I feel the pull to browse immediately
- **Swap the generic email CTA for an F21 Rewards hook** — "EARN POINTS ON EVERY ORDER, BESTIE →" would land way harder for members like me and drive sign-up for non-members with actual context
- **Add a category quick-access row** below the hero (New Arrivals / Tops / Dresses / Sale) — one extra row means I can get to what I want in a single tap instead of digging through the hamburger
