# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay so opening F21's homepage feels very on-brand immediately — the "BESTIE" energy in that yellow banner at the top is doing the most and I'm here for it. It's pushing a "SHOP SUMMER '26" campaign with a video hero (there's a mute button so it's playing) featuring a model in a striped bodycon moment, very summer-going-out-vibes. The vibe is right but a cookie consent modal is literally covering half my screen before I can even shop, which is annoying.

## What's working

- **"BESTIE" banner language** — that yellow strip saying "DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW, BESTIE. SIGN UP" speaks directly to me generationally. Doesn't feel corporate at all.
- **Hero video** — the mute icon confirms it's a video playing, which feels modern and alive vs. a static banner. The striped bodycon look in the hero is a strong summer statement.
- **"SHOP SUMMER '26" copy** — dating it to '26 instead of just "SUMMER" makes it feel like a legit seasonal drop, not a clearance event.
- **Second lifestyle shot** — the partial image below the fold of a model in an asymmetric black top keeps me curious enough to scroll past the cookie modal.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks the entire lower half** — it's the first thing I have to deal with before I can even see the product content. That friction right at the start is a vibe killer.
- **No visible offer or discount** — I know F21 almost always has *something* going on. There's nothing here telling me prices are low, there's a sale, or free shipping exists. That's a miss.
- **"SIGN UP" is vague** — sign up for what? Rewards? Texts? Email? The banner doesn't tell me the value exchange, so the incentive to tap it is weak.
- **No visible navigation categories** — the hamburger menu is collapsed. I can't see "Tops," "Dresses," or "Accessories" without an extra tap, which adds friction for reaching my category.
- **No loyalty balance or rewards status** — as an F21 Rewards member I'd love to see "You have 200 points" or similar above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the discount or rewards hook in the hero** — even a simple "Prices start at $12" or "Rewards members get early access" line under "SHOP SUMMER '26" would give me a reason to convert, not just browse.
- **Replace the vague "SIGN UP" with a benefit** — rewrite the banner to "BESTIE REWARDS: earn points on every order → SIGN UP" so the value prop is obvious in one glance.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal faster or make it less blocking** — shrink it to a bottom strip or auto-dismiss after 5 seconds. Covering 40% of the screen before the page even loads hurts the first impression.
- **Add one category shortcut strip below the hero** — a horizontal scrollable row of "Tops / Dresses / Jewelry / Sale" gets me to my category in one tap instead of hunting through the hamburger menu.
