# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing — there's a cookie consent banner blocking basically the entire bottom half of the page, which is genuinely annoying when I'm just trying to vibe with summer fits. What I *can* see is very much F21's lane: two young girls in matching striped sets, "IT'S GIVING SUMMER" across the hero, and a 20% app order discount banner up top. The brand is clearly pushing summer matching sets and trying to get me onto the app.

## What's working

- **"IT'S GIVING SUMMER" hero copy** — using "it's giving" is peak Gen Z and it actually made me smile. F21 clearly knows their customer.
- **The hero models** — two young women, different skin tones, in matching striped summer sets. That's exactly who shops here and it reads immediately.
- **Top banner offer** — "GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!" is above the fold, in a bold yellow strip, and it's a real, specific number. Easy to understand.
- **App-download hook** — pushing me toward the app with a concrete discount is smart, and the banner has chevron arrows making it feel interactive.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is a full page blocker** — it's sitting right on top of the hero CTA and anything below. I literally cannot see what's under it without tapping OK first. That's a friction point that shouldn't be the first interaction I have with the brand.
- **No shop CTA visible** — I can't see a "Shop Now," "Shop Swim," or anything that tells me where to go. Completely hidden.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — there's no category strip at the top, so I can't one-tap into "Tops," "Sets," or "Swim." I have to dig.
- **No loyalty callout** — I have F21 Rewards and I can't see my points, a member badge, or any reason the app is better for me specifically as a returning customer. The 20% is first-order only, which doesn't help me.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal on scroll or make it a bottom sheet** — blocking the hero CTA with a cookie consent popup on mobile is a conversion killer. Move it to a slim bottom bar that auto-dismisses or make the hero CTA still tappable.
- **Add a visible "Shop Summer Sets" or "Shop the Look" CTA button** directly over the hero image so I don't have to hunt.
- **Swap the generic app-discount banner for a loyalty-member callout** for returning customers — "Hi Tabby, you have 240 points" would instantly make me feel seen and want to browse.
- **Pin one category row just below the header** (Sets, Swim, Tops, Dresses) so I can jump to my section in one tap without opening the hamburger menu.
