# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is giving exactly what it says — "IT'S GIVING SUMMER" with two girls in matching striped sets in front of palm trees, very beach-coded, very now. Forever 21 is clearly pushing warm-weather basics and the aesthetic is *there*. The only thing throwing it off is a full cookie consent modal that's literally parked on top of the hero, blocking anything below the fold before I can even see what's on this page.

## What's working

- **"IT'S GIVING SUMMER" hero copy** — that phrasing is genuinely Gen Z, not a brand trying too hard to sound like us. It lands.
- **Striped sets on diverse models** — two girls, different skin tones, same coordinated vibe. Feels inclusive, not forced.
- **Top app-download banner** — "GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!" is a clean hook for someone who doesn't have the app yet. Specific, clear, actionable.
- **Summer aesthetic is accurate** — palm trees, light colors, coordinated matching sets. Matches what I'd actually want to browse right now.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is sitting on top of everything** — it physically covers the lower half of the hero. I can't see the CTA, I can't see categories, I literally cannot interact with the actual store until I dismiss it. On mobile this is brutal.
- **No F21 Rewards callout** — I have a rewards account and there is zero acknowledgment of that. No "Hi, you have X points" or even a generic rewards badge. Miss.
- **No shop-now button visible** — the hero says "IT'S GIVING SUMMER" but there's nowhere to tap to actually shop the look. The modal is eating that real estate.
- **Hamburger nav only** — the three-line menu means I have zero category visibility without an extra tap. For a brand with this many categories, even one scrollable chip row would help.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or minimize the cookie banner after 3 seconds** — or at minimum shrink it to a bottom bar so it doesn't eat 40% of the screen and bury my first CTA.
- **Add a "Shop the Look" button directly on the hero** — "IT'S GIVING SUMMER → Shop Sets" would convert this vibe into a tap instantly.
- **Surface an F21 Rewards chip in the top nav** — even just a little star icon with a points badge for logged-in users. I'm already loyal, remind me.
- **Add a horizontal category scroll below the hero** — New Arrivals / Sets / Tops / Dresses / Sale as tappable chips so I don't have to open the hamburger to get anywhere.
