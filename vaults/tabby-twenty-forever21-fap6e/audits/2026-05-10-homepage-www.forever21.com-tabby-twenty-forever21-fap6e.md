# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Forever 21 at least *looks* like itself — the top banner is pushing an app discount, the hero is a video (muted on load, which fine), and the summer campaign is already live. But the cookie consent modal hijacked my whole screen before I could even vibe with the page, and there's basically no navigation visible — just a hamburger menu I have to dig into. Feels like the brand knows who it's for but hasn't made it easy to *shop* on mobile.

## What's working

- **App discount banner ("GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!")** — that's the first thing I see, and it's actually relevant to me since I bounce between the site and the app
- **Video hero** — the autoplay video is very on-brand for F21; it feels current and energetic even muted
- **"SHOP SUMMER '26" copy** — clean, confident, Gen Z energy; no cringe over-explanation
- **Secondary model image** — the Black woman in the black off-shoulder top below the fold is *exactly* the editorial vibe I shop F21 for; diverse casting, trend-forward look
- **Minimal header** — logo stays centered, search and bag icons are exactly where I expect them on mobile

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** — it slams down on top of the secondary image right away and forces an interaction before I can even see what's on the page; instant mood killer
- **No tappable button CTA** — "SHOP SUMMER '26" is overlaid text on the hero video but there's no visible button element; on mobile I might not know it's tappable at all
- **Hamburger menu = dead end** — zero category chips or quick-nav strips below the header; if I want Women's Tops vs. Sale, I have to tap the menu and drill, which is extra friction
- **20% offer is app-only and first-order** — that exclusion clause is going to bounce anyone who already has the app or has ordered before; it's a hook that misses returning shoppers
- **No loyalty/rewards surfacing** — I have an F21 Rewards account but nothing here reminds me of my balance or perks; total missed opportunity

## Recommendations

- **Add horizontal category chips below the header** ("New Arrivals," "Sale," "Tops," "Dresses") so I can tap my way to a category without hitting the hamburger — this is table stakes on mobile in 2026
- **Replace the cookie modal with a bottom-sheet banner** that doesn't block the hero; or trigger it after 3 seconds of scroll so I can at least see the page first
- **Swap the text-overlay "SHOP SUMMER '26" for a pill-button** in the hero — same copy, just styled as a tappable element with clear affordance on mobile
- **Surface the F21 Rewards widget in the promo strip** ("You have 200 pts — redeem today") so returning shoppers see value immediately instead of just the app-first-order offer
