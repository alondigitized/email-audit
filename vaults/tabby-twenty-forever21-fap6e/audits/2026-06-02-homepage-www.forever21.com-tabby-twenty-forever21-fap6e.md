# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay so F21 is clearly going for a whole NYC-meets-LA vibe right now with that "One Look, Two Cities" campaign, and honestly the cast is giving what it's giving — young, multicultural, sporty-cute. But the FIRST thing I actually have to deal with is a cookie banner covering half my hero, which is such a vibe killer. It's giving fast-fashion energy but the homepage experience isn't as tight as it could be.

## What's working

- **The split-city hero concept** — "ONE LOOK, TWO CITIES" is clever and the dual backdrop (Empire State Building left, palm trees right) is actually a cute visual payoff. I get it immediately.
- **The cast** — four young people who look like real 20-somethings, not fashion-week aliens. The girl with the glasses and the brown crop top could literally be in my friend group.
- **The top promo banner** — 20% off your first app order is a real incentive, and putting it at the very top in that yellow/amber strip means I clocked it right away.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the header is minimal, the logo is centered, the icons are readable. Nothing is fighting each other.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — the "We use cookies" overlay covers the entire lower half of the viewport, including any CTA buttons that might live below the hero image. I have to dismiss it before I can do anything, which is instant friction.
- **Zero loyalty / rewards hook above fold** — I'm a F21 Rewards member in-persona and there's nothing up here reminding me of points, my balance, or member-only pricing. Missed.
- **No shop CTA visible** — because of the modal, I genuinely cannot see a "Shop Now" or category button without tapping OK first. That's a dead second on mobile.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — my category is one full tap + menu open away. No quick-access tabs for Women's or New Arrivals at the top.
- **The app-only offer** — "20% off YOUR FIRST APP ORDER" is great for new users but if I already have the app, that banner is dead real estate for me.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or shrink the cookie banner** — put it in a slim bottom bar instead of a full modal overlay so the hero CTA stays visible on the first load. This is a one-week ship.
- **Add a sticky loyalty strip below the top banner** — something like "F21 Rewards: You have 340 pts — see what's redeemable →" would make returning shoppers feel seen immediately.
- **Surface a category shortcut bar** — add a horizontal scroll row of chips ("New," "Tops," "Denim," "Sale") just below the nav bar so I can tap straight to my section without opening the hamburger.
- **Swap the app-discount banner for a general offer** when user has the app already — "Free shipping on orders $35+" would be relevant to literally everyone, not just first-timers.
