# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is literally my store — Forever 21 looks like it's running a whole NYC-meets-LA campaign right now with these four young diverse models in sporty jersey looks. The vibe is fresh and the hero energy is giving dual-city editorial, which I'm into. But a cookie consent banner is eating the bottom half of my screen before I can see any actual shop links.

## What's working

- **Split-city hero:** The Empire State Building on one side, palm trees on the other — it's a cool editorial concept that feels like something I'd see on TikTok. It stops the scroll.
- **Diverse cast:** The four models (two women, two men, multiple ethnicities) genuinely look like people I'd see at the mall. It doesn't feel forced.
- **App offer banner:** That yellow "GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!" strip at the very top is impossible to miss — it's the right hook for a high-tech-comfort Gen Z shopper who already has ten retail apps.
- **Headline energy:** "ONE LOOK, TWO CITIES" is punchy and short. No paragraph copy, no over-explaining. My generation does not read walls of text.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal is blocking the CTA zone:** The consent banner is sitting right on top of whatever CTA or category nav lives below the hero. First impression is the "OK!" button, not a shop link.
- **No visible shop/category CTA in the hero:** There's no button overlay on the hero image itself — I have to scroll past a cookie modal to find out what I'm supposed to tap.
- **Zero loyalty hook:** I have an F21 Rewards account. There's no mention of points, rewards balance, or member-only pricing anywhere in this viewport. That's a miss for anyone returning.
- **No price signal beyond the app offer:** Nothing says "sale," "new arrivals," or "starting at $X" — so if I'm not a first-time app downloader, the offer doesn't apply to me at all.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Look" CTA button directly on the hero image** — button overlay, bottom-left, copy like "Shop NYC / Shop LA" so I know exactly what to tap before the cookie modal even appears.
- **Swap the cookie modal to a slim bottom bar** instead of the full-screen overlay — it shouldn't eat the entire below-fold zone on first load.
- **Surface the rewards hook in the header or hero** — something like "Earn points on every order — sign in" next to the bag icon would catch a returning Rewards member immediately.
- **Add a sale/new-arrivals strip below the hero** — even just "New In: Summer Styles" as a scrollable category row gives me a reason to keep going past the cookie dismissal.
