# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay so the first thing I see when I open F21's site is literally two popups fighting each other for my attention — an email signup AND a cookie banner are both sitting on top of the page at the same time. Behind all that noise there's a really cute summer/vacation hero with young people that actually looks like it was made for me, and the top banner is screaming BOGO on sale items which is the exact reason I'm here. But the double-modal chaos makes this feel rushed and aggressive rather than welcoming.

## What's working

- **BOGO banner ("ALL SALE ITEMS BUY 1 GET 1 FREE")** — this is exactly why I come to F21, and it's the first text I read at the very top. That placement is perfect.
- **"WANT 15% OFF?" email modal** — the yellow "UNLOCK 15% OFF" button pops hard and the copy ("exclusive offers, new arrivals & style inspo") actually sounds like it was written for someone my age, not a press release.
- **Hero lifestyle imagery** — behind the modals I can see a brown-and-white outfit girl + guy by a classic car with palm trees. Very summer, very my aesthetic. The Polaroid-style collage at the top adds a cute nostalgic touch.
- **Summer campaign coherence** — the floral accents, beach/travel imagery, and warm tones all read as one cohesive seasonal push.

## What's weak

- **Double-modal pile-up** — the email signup and cookie consent both fire at the same time. The cookie banner cuts the bottom of the email modal in half. This is genuinely bad UX and makes the page feel broken.
- **No F21 Rewards visibility** — I have F21 Rewards and I have no idea what my balance is or if there's member-only pricing. Nothing above the fold speaks to me as a returning customer.
- **Homepage completely blocked** — I literally cannot see the nav, product categories, or any sale content because two modals are covering everything. I came here to browse the sale rack and I can't even get to it without dismissing two things.
- **No free-shipping threshold callout** — nowhere on the visible screen does it tell me when I hit free shipping, and that's usually the thing that nudges me to add one more item.

## Recommendations

- **Sequence the modals, don't stack them** — show the cookie banner first (it's legally required), let me dismiss it, then maybe show the email popup. Not both at once.
- **Add my rewards balance to the email modal or nav** — if I'm already a member, swap the "WANT 15% OFF?" popup for a "Welcome back, Tabby — you have X points" moment. That's way more motivating.
- **Put the BOGO detail one tap into the sale page** — the banner is great but clicking it should go straight to a filtered "Sale — BOGO" landing page, not the generic homepage.
- **Drop a free-shipping threshold line in the banner strip** — even just "Free shipping $50+" next to the BOGO copy would boost AOV and reduce checkout drop-off.
