# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Ok so I literally opened the app and got hit with a cookie popup before I could see anything — not cute. What I can see behind it: two girls in striped swimwear, a big "UP TO 80% OFF" stamped across the hero, and a banner at the top promising 20% off my first app order. Very much a "clearance push" energy, not a "new drop" moment.

## What's working

- **App download banner** — "GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER" is actually something I'd act on if I hadn't already downloaded it. Direct, low friction.
- **Hero model casting** — both girls look like actual 20-year-olds, not editorial models. Feels real and on-brand for F21's audience, which is literally me.
- **Seasonal swimwear push** — late May is exactly when I'm thinking about beach fits, so the timing lands.

## What's weak

- **"80% OFF 80% OFF 80% OFF" render bug** — the offer text repeats three times in a row across the hero, which looks broken and makes the brand feel low-quality. If I'm a new visitor, this is a red flag.
- **Cookie consent modal** — blocks the bottom half of the hero immediately on load, killing the first impression before I can see products or CTAs.
- **No visible CTA button** — there's a deal screaming at me but nowhere obvious to actually tap and shop it.
- **No category navigation visible** — just a hamburger menu. I can't get to "Tops" or "Swimwear" in one tap.
- **No loyalty/rewards signal** — I have F21 Rewards but there's nothing here acknowledging that or showing my points/status.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero text bug** — "80% OFF 80% OFF 80% OFF" repeated three times reads as a CSS or JS scroll animation broken in mobile. Replace with a single clean "Up to 80% Off" overlay with a "Shop Sale" CTA button underneath.
- **Add a visible "Shop Now" or "Shop Sale" button** on the hero — the offer exists but there's no tap destination. One button below the offer text would immediately improve conversion.
- **Dismiss or minimize the cookie modal on scroll** rather than forcing an "OK!" block — or at least delay it 3 seconds so I can see the hero first.
- **Surface my F21 Rewards status** in the top bar or under the app banner — even just "You have 240 points" would make me feel seen as a returning member.
