# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay so it's giving very summer-at-the-pier, golden hour, Santa Monica vibes — I fw that aesthetic. But the cookie banner is literally eating half my screen before I can see anything, which is such a vibe killer. First impression is that F21 is pushing a warm-weather moment and it feels pretty on-brand, but the experience itself is getting in its own way.
- **6/10**

## What's working

- **"DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW. BESTIE, SIGN UP 💜"** — the announcement bar copy actually speaks my language, calling me "bestie" and using the purple heart feels intentional and very F21
- **Hero lifestyle shot** — girl in a yellow sundress at what looks like Santa Monica Pier, golden hour, palm trees. That's *exactly* the inspo content I'm saving on Pinterest right now
- **"TRENDING NOW" hero copy** — simple, direct, tells me what this site is about immediately, no overthinking

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent banner** — it's massive and covering the bottom third of the hero. On mobile this is brutal; I can barely see the product or CTA before I have to dismiss something
- **No visible shop CTA above the fold** — the "TRENDING NOW" headline is bold but there's no button I can tap. Where do I go? Just… stare at the model?
- **Hamburger menu only** — the nav is totally collapsed with just a burger icon. I have zero idea what categories exist without an extra tap
- **No price anchoring or promo** — the banner teases a newsletter signup but doesn't hint at any deal, sale, or new arrival drop that would make me want to sign up *right now*

## Recommendations

- **Kill or minimize the cookie banner on mobile** — replace the full-overlay format with a slim bottom bar so the hero isn't buried; most Gen Z users will just tap OK anyway
- **Add a "SHOP NOW" button directly on the hero image** — floating white pill button over "TRENDING NOW" would let me act immediately instead of hunting for a tap target
- **Tease a promo in the announcement bar** — swap generic "sign up" for something like "SIGN UP. GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST ORDER, BESTIE 💜" so the CTA has a hook
- **Surface one or two category chips below the hero** — quick-tap pills like "Dresses," "Going Out Tops," "Swim" so I can skip the hamburger menu entirely
