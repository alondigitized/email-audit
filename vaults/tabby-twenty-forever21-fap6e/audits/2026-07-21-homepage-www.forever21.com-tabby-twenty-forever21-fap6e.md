# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Forever 21 is immediately throwing a massive email popup and a cookie banner in my face at the same time — like, I literally cannot see the homepage because there are two overlays stacked on top of each other. Behind all of that chaos I can see there's a cute LA-summer hero with young people and palm trees, which is very on-brand, and the top strip is dangling an extra 40% off sale. Classic F21 energy — good vibes buried under an annoying amount of friction.

## What's working

- **Top announcement bar** — "EXTRA 40% OFF ALREADY REDUCED STYLES" is the first thing I read on the page and it immediately makes me want to dig into the sale rack. That's very me.
- **Email popup offer** — "WANT 15% OFF?" with a big yellow "UNLOCK 15% OFF" button is simple, clear, and a discount I'd actually use before I even start shopping. The "style inspo" copy feels like it was written for someone my age.
- **Hero imagery** (what I can see of it) — the couple near a classic car with palm trees is that early-2000s Y2K nostalgia energy that's everywhere right now. It matches the aesthetic I actually shop for at F21.
- **Brand consistency** — the black, white, and yellow color palette on the popup matches F21's identity cleanly.

## What's weak

- **Double-modal chaos** — an email signup popup AND a cookie consent banner are both live at the same time, stacking on top of each other and the hero. I cannot see what the actual homepage looks like. This is genuinely frustrating.
- **Navigation is inaccessible** — the hamburger menu is visible but the modals block me from tapping any category. I can't get to Women's, Tops, or Sale in one tap.
- **No loyalty recognition** — I'm an F21 Rewards member and there's zero acknowledgment of that. No points balance, no member-exclusive offer surfaced. Makes the email popup feel tone-deaf since I already gave them my info.
- **Cookie banner below the email popup** — the "OK!" cookie button is a third thing asking for my attention. Three competing actions on one screen is a lot.
- **Hero is almost fully obscured** — the most expensive creative asset on the page is invisible.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email popup for recognized returning shoppers or logged-in members** — if I have the app or a cookie, don't make me sit through a 15% signup offer I already claimed months ago.
- **Sequence the overlays** — show the cookie banner first (it's a legal requirement anyway), dismiss it, THEN surface the email popup if needed. Never layer them simultaneously.
- **Swap the email popup for a loyalty module** — surface my Rewards points balance or a "Members get an extra 10% off" callout for those of us already in the program instead of a generic email grab.
- **Pin the 40% off banner higher and make it tappable** — the sale strip at the top should go directly to the sale page in one tap. Right now there's no affordance to click it.
