# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-03

- Okay so first thing I see when I open Forever 21 is... a cookie consent pop-up blocking half the screen. Not cute. But the cookie modal is eating the whole bottom half of the fold so I can't even see if there's a shop button.

- + Hero imagery explicitly speaks to my persona — young woman, early 20s, yellow strappy dress, boardwalk/amusement park vibes. That's literally my aesthetic.
- + One unmistakable CTA above the fold — "SIGN UP 💜" in the top notification bar (newsletter, not shop, but it's there).
- + Visual hierarchy is clear — hero image dominates, "TRENDING NOW" headline reads immediately.
- + No actual render bugs — cookie modal is expected behavior, not a broken layout.
- + Demographic signals match — model age, outfit, setting all scream early-20s F21 girl.

- Score: 1 base + 5 signals = **6/10**

## What's working

- **"DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW. BESTIE. SIGN UP 💜"** — The copy tone is so right for me. "Bestie" and the purple heart feel genuinely Gen Z, not a 40-year-old brand manager guessing. I actually might sign up.
- **Hero model and setting** — She looks my age, the yellow dress is something I'd actually search, and the Santa Monica Pier backdrop makes it feel aspirational without being unreachable. That's the sweet spot for F21.
- **"TRENDING NOW" headline** — Simple, clear, and directly speaks to why I come to F21. I shop here *because* of trends, not despite them.
- **Accessibility icon** — Small thing but it shows some care for usability.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocks the bottom half of the viewport** — Whatever CTA, category tiles, or promo content sits below the hero is completely hidden until I dismiss it. That's the first interaction and it's friction.
- **No offer above the fold** — F21's whole value prop is affordable trend pieces. I see zero price signals — no "from $5," no "extra 20% off sale," nothing. That's a miss for someone who responds to price.
- **"SIGN UP" goes to newsletter, not the rewards program** — I have F21 Rewards. There's no sign of points balance, member perks, or loyalty anywhere. That's a free retention hook they're not pulling.
- **No visible shop CTA on the hero** — I see "SIGN UP" and "OK!" but no "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP TRENDING" button. If the hero is selling the vibe, it needs a door.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — One extra tap to get to any category. On mobile that adds friction when I'm already deciding whether to bounce.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP TRENDING" CTA directly on the hero image** — The photo is doing the work; give me a button so I don't have to hunt through the hamburger menu to get there.
- **Swap the notification bar copy from newsletter to a concrete offer** — "BESTIE, EXTRA 20% OFF YOUR FIRST ORDER" converts harder than "DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW." The playful voice is already there, just load it with an incentive.
- **Surface the F21 Rewards hook above the fold** — Even a small "Earn points on every order 💜 Log in" bar does the job. Loyalty members spend more; remind them they have points burning a hole in their pocket.
- **Delay or soft-dismiss the cookie modal** — Show it as a bottom sheet that doesn't cover the hero rather than a full interstitial. It's blocking the whole product pitch on first load.
