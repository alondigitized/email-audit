# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Forever 21 definitely knows their girl — the vibe is very much "beach day, golden hour, main character energy" and I'm here for it. But the second I land on the page I get hit with a massive cookie consent modal that eats half the screen, so I can't even see what's below the fold or tap into anything. It's giving trendy aesthetic on the outside, messy UX on the inside.
- **6/10**
- + Hero imagery speaks to persona: young woman, early 20s, yellow summer top, boardwalk/amusement park background = exactly my vibe
- − Returning-shopper hook: the top banner says "DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW. BESTIE. SIGN UP" — that's an email list grab, not a loyalty/rewards hook
- − Concrete offer: zero percent-off or dollar-off visible anywhere above the fold
- − Primary CTA above fold: "TRENDING NOW" is a headline, not a button — and the cookie modal is blocking the lower half entirely
- + Visual hierarchy: hero image dominates clearly, eye goes straight to the model
- + No render bugs: layout is intact, no broken images or scrambled text
- + Demographic signals match: boardwalk, summer fashion, early 20s model = me
- + Current campaign/season: warm weather, beach setting, late spring/summer = appropriate for May 2026
- − Loyalty/membership benefits: nothing about F21 Rewards visible without scrolling
- + Offer feels honest: nothing manipulative shown — because there's nothing shown at all

## What's working

- **"BESTIE" language in the banner** — "DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW. BESTIE." hits exactly the right tone for my age group; it doesn't feel corporate
- **Hero model and setting** — young woman in a yellow ribbed top with a Santa Monica–style boardwalk and roller coaster behind her is peak summer inspo content; I would absolutely screenshot that for my Pinterest
- **"TRENDING NOW" headline** — short, punchy, very scroll-culture; it signals newness which is exactly why I shop F21
- **Brand logo placement** — centered FOREVER 21 wordmark is clean and iconic; the nav bar doesn't clutter

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks everything** — it sits right on top of whatever CTAs or product categories are below the hero, which means I literally cannot shop without tapping OK first; this is the first thing I see and it's friction
- **No visible offer** — no sale callout, no "new arrivals," no free shipping threshold; nothing pulls me to tap deeper
- **No shopping CTA on the hero** — "TRENDING NOW" is just text over a photo, there's no button to actually go anywhere; I don't know what I'm supposed to do
- **Hamburger-only navigation** — I have to tap the menu icon to find Women's, Dresses, Sale, etc.; nothing is surfaced directly so my top categories are one extra tap away

## Recommendations

- **Add a "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP THE LOOK" button directly on the hero** — overlay a pill button in white over the model photo; it's one change and it immediately gives me somewhere to go
- **Surface a concrete offer in the top announcement bar** — swap "DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW. BESTIE." for something like "NEW ARRIVALS: UP TO 40% OFF + FREE SHIP OVER $50" — the bestie energy can live on a second slide in the banner carousel
- **Dismiss the cookie modal or make it a bottom sheet** — a full-overlay consent dialog on mobile kills first impressions; move it to a slim bottom bar so the hero + CTA are visible immediately
- **Show a loyalty/rewards nudge above the fold** — even a one-liner like "F21 Rewards members get early access 🤍" next to the sign-up strip would remind me my points are there
