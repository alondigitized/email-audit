# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- Forever 21 still reads like my mall go-to: yellow header, big bold type, models who look like my friends. Right now it's pushing the new "Forever Fit" activewear line with a tennis and padel look, plus 20% off if I download the app. The cookie banner eats the bottom of the screen, so I barely got to see any actual clothes.

## What's working

- **Yellow header with the black logo and 10-item nav:** it's instantly Forever 21, and I can reach Dresses, Tops, Accessories or SALE in one tap.
- **Announcement bar "20% OFF FIRST APP ORDER - CODE: APPMVP20":** a real discount with the code written out, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- **Hero collage of three young women on a rooftop court:** it feels aspirational but still relatable. The NYC skyline and the white and navy palette look good on my feed.
- **"Play in style." tagline:** short and casual, no corporate speak.
- **SALE in orange-red in the nav:** it stands out, and that's where I shop online.

## What's weak

- **Cookie banner:** it takes up about a quarter of my phone screen and cuts off the four product tiles, so I only see hair and shoulders.
- **Floating "USE CODE: APPMVP20" pill and accessibility icon:** they sit on top of the cookie bar's bottom-left corner, right next to "Manage preferences", which looks cluttered.
- **Activewear hero:** I'm a basics and statement-jewelry girl. Nothing above the fold shows tees, basics or accessories.
- **F21 Rewards:** it's completely missing from the first screen, even though I'm a member.
- **20% off is app-only:** there's no offer for shopping on the site, which is where I actually buy sale items.
- **No free-shipping or returns info, and no deadline on the offer:** there's no reason to act today.

## Recommendations

- Shrink the cookie consent to a slim single-line bar, or make it a small corner card, so the product row is fully visible on first load.
- Move the "USE CODE: APPMVP20" pill to the opposite corner or into the announcement bar so it doesn't collide with the cookie and accessibility elements.
- Add a second announcement slide for F21 Rewards ("Members: earn points on every order") and a free-shipping threshold. I'd also add an end date to the 20% offer.
- Add a "Fall Basics + Accessories" strip right under the hero, with tees, jewelry and layering pieces, so I have something in my lane besides activewear.
