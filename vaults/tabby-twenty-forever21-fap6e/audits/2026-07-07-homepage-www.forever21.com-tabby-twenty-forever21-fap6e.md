# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Forever21.com is literally my store and the second I landed here I saw the "End of Season Sale — 50% Off Everything" plastered across the whole screen and my thumbs were ready. This is a bold, graphic-forward sale page — black with yellow polka dots, very loud, very them. The vibe is all sale, no product, which is cool but also kind of like... where do I even tap?

## What's working

- **The top sale banner** — "50% Off Everything Auto Applied in Cart" is the most useful copy on the page. No code, no exclusions listed, just vibes and savings. That "auto applied" detail is actually really good UX.
- **The bold graphic design** — black background, yellow polka dots, huge white SALE type. It's loud in a way that feels very F21 and not corporate or boring. It would stop my scroll on TikTok too.
- **The offer clarity** — I know exactly what I'm getting before I even tap anything. No mystery discount.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the hero** — I literally don't know where to tap to start shopping. There's a huge "SALE" graphic but no "Shop Now" or "Shop 50% Off" button visible. I'm supposed to just figure it out?
- **Cookie consent modal is blocking the page** — It's covering the bottom of the hero and I haven't even seen product yet. First interaction I'm forced to do is dismiss a legal modal, which is annoying.
- **Zero product imagery** — This entire above-the-fold experience is a graphic and a banner. Not a single item, category shot, or look. I have no idea what's on sale.
- **No F21 Rewards callout** — I am a Rewards member. My points balance, a "members-only extra 10% off" badge, literally anything loyalty-related would have me tapping immediately. Nothing.
- **No navigation shortcuts** — I can see the hamburger menu but no category chips, no "Tops / Bottoms / Accessories" quick-links to get me where I want to go fast.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" button directly on the hero** — the graphic is great but it's a dead end right now; one big CTA button centered at the bottom of the hero would fix this immediately.
- **Drop the cookie modal into a bottom sheet instead of a full overlay** — or fire it after the first scroll; don't block the hero on load.
- **Swap one section of the hero for a lifestyle shot or product collage** — even a grid of four sale items (graphic tees, jewelry, cargo pants) would make this feel shoppable instead of just promotional.
- **Add a small F21 Rewards badge or banner** — "Rewards members get early access" or "Earn points on every sale item" above the hero banner would activate me and every other loyalty member in one line.
