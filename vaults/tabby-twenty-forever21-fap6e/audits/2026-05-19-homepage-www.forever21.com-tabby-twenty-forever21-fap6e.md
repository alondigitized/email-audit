# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay so F21 went full sale-mode and honestly? I respect the energy. The first thing I see is two girls who look exactly like people I'd run into at the beach, and "UP TO 80% OFF THRU MEMORIAL DAY" in giant letters — that's the whole pitch. It reads fast, it reads summer, and it reads like F21 knows exactly who's checking their site on a phone at the mall food court.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 80% OFF THRU MEMORIAL DAY" banner** — the yellow-on-black contrast stops the scroll instantly; I know the deal in under two seconds
- **Hero casting** — both models look like actual 20-somethings, not influencer-filtered aliens; the blonde with the headband and layered necklaces is giving exactly the jewelry-stacking energy I'm into
- **Top app promo banner** — "GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!" is a real reason for a high-tech-comfort person like me to download or open the app
- **Beach/summer imagery** — palm trees, striped swimwear, that soft-sky backdrop — it's Memorial Day weekend content done right, not generic stock

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocks everything below the hero** — the "OK!" box sits right over what I assume is a category nav or CTA; there's zero way to tap into anything without dismissing it first, and that's friction on a phone
- **No shop button in sight** — I see a deal but nowhere to actually go shop it; the ">" on the top banner isn't a CTA, it's a carousel nav
- **F21 Rewards is invisible** — I have points and I cannot see them or any member-pricing signal; that's a miss for a loyalty cardholder
- **Hamburger-only nav** — no quick-access category chips for New Arrivals, Sale, etc.; on mobile I want one-tap access, not a buried menu

## Recommendations

- **Add a "SHOP THE SALE" button** directly on the hero image or overlay — the offer is there, the tap-through is missing; it's like putting a sale sign in the window and locking the door
- **Surface F21 Rewards balance or a "member exclusive" badge** in the top banner area — swap it in rotation with the app promo so returning shoppers feel seen
- **Replace the blocking cookie modal with a bottom-sheet bar** — a slim "We use cookies / OK" strip keeps the page scannable and removes the forced interaction before I can even browse
- **Add two or three category chips below the hero** (e.g., "Swimwear," "New Arrivals," "Under $20") so I can tap directly to what I actually want instead of digging through the hamburger
