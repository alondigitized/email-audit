# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so F21 knows their audience — "IT'S GIVING SUMMER" immediately gave me the vibe, and those matching striped sets on two models who look literally my age? Super cute. But the cookie consent modal is literally sitting on top of everything below the hero, and there's zero shop button or rewards callout visible, so right now this page is just a pretty picture with a wall in front of it.

## What's working

- **"IT'S GIVING SUMMER" headline** — that copy is *chef's kiss* for my age group. It sounds like how we actually talk and makes the brand feel like it gets us.
- **The hero models** — two girls who look like they could be my friends at the mall, wearing coordinating striped sets. Aspirational but not unattainable.
- **App download promo banner** — "GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST APP ORDER!" in a bright yellow bar is impossible to miss and does the job for new customers.
- **Summer timing** — May 13th and they're already in full summer mode. That's correct. I don't want to see anything else right now.
- **Clean, simple mobile layout** — logo centered, hamburger left, bag right. Easy to navigate once that modal is gone.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** — it's blocking the bottom half of the hero and any CTA button that might exist below the fold. First thing I see after the cute models is a legal wall. Big vibe killer.
- **No shop button visible** — with the modal up, there's literally no tappable CTA to get into the catalog. Someone who bounces fast (me on a bad day) would leave without ever clicking past this.
- **App promo doesn't target loyalty members** — "FIRST APP ORDER" means nothing to me as a Rewards member who already has the app. Could've said "earn 2x points this weekend" and I'd be way more excited.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook anywhere** — I have an F21 Rewards account and the homepage isn't acknowledging me at all. No points balance, no member-exclusive offer, nothing.
- **No category quick-links visible** — the whole nav is behind a hamburger. One tap extra to get to Tops or Jewelry is one tap I might not bother with.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal by default on return visits** or at minimum make the "OK!" button more prominent and styled — right now it blends in and you have to hunt for it, which adds friction before you even see the site.
- **Add a "Shop the Look" or "Shop Summer" button directly on the hero image** so there's a tappable CTA the second you land.
- **Swap the app-only promo for a loyalty-member offer** (or run both): something like "Rewards members earn double points this week" is more relevant to the existing customer base who actually buys repeatedly.
- **Surface a horizontal category scroll strip below the hero** — New Arrivals, Tops, Dresses, Jewelry — so I can get to my category in one tap without opening the hamburger menu.
