# Homepage snapshot · www.forever21.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Forever21's homepage is giving very much "we know our audience" energy with the "BESTIE" language and a Summer '26 hero that's genuinely cute — the model, the stripes, the vibe. But a cookie consent modal is eating the bottom half of my screen before I can even see what's actually there, and there's zero offer or price point in sight, which is literally why I come to F21 in the first place.
- **5/10**
- + Hero imagery explicitly speaks to my persona: young woman, trendy striped co-ord or bodycon, very much my aesthetic
- + "BESTIE" in the top banner is dead-on Gen Z register — that's me
- + Page reflects current campaign / season: "SHOP SUMMER '26" is accurate to today (May 2026)
- + Demographic signals match: model skews young, style is fast-fashion trendy
- − No concrete offer above the fold: no % off, no $-off, no free shipping threshold anywhere
- − No unmistakable shop CTA button: "SHOP SUMMER '26" is text overlay, not a tappable button with clear affordance
- − No returning-shopper hook: that top banner is an email acquisition ask, not a rewards-balance or "welcome back" moment
- − Loyalty / membership benefits not visible: F21 Rewards is a real program and it's invisible here
- − Cookie modal is blocking below-fold content entirely, which tanks the first impression
- − Visual hierarchy is muddied by the overlay modal fighting for attention

## What's working

- **"DON'T BE THE LAST TO KNOW, BESTIE. SIGN UP"** — the copy register is *so* on-brand for my age group. "Bestie" is the right word, the yellow banner pops, and the arrow makes it feel tappable
- **The hero video** (that mute icon bottom-left tells me it's playing) with the striped look is genuinely giving summer fashion inspo — the model and outfit are exactly the kind of thing I'd screenshot for outfit ideas
- **"SHOP SUMMER '26" copy** — naming the year feels fresh and intentional, not generic "New Arrivals"
- **Clean wordmark and minimal nav bar** — "FOREVER 21" is centered and readable, the bag and search icons are right where I expect them on mobile

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal** is sitting dead-center over the second content module — I can see a Black model in a black off-shoulder top underneath but I literally cannot engage with whatever that section is saying until I tap "OK!" This is a bounce risk
- **Zero price signal above the fold** — F21's whole value prop is affordable fashion and there's not a single "starting at $15" or "up to 50% off" anywhere I can see
- **No visible CTA button** — "SHOP SUMMER '26" reads as a label, not a button. If I didn't know F21, I wouldn't know to tap it
- **Rewards program MIA** — I have F21 Rewards and there's nothing here speaking to me as a member; a small "points balance" or "members get early access" chip would make me feel seen
- **Hero video is muted by default** which is fine, but the mute icon is tiny and easy to miss — the video is presumably adding energy that I'm not fully getting

## Recommendations

- **Add a savings chip to the hero** — overlay "From $9.99" or "Up to 60% off summer" on top of or right beneath the hero image; that's one line of copy, shippable in a day, and it's the #1 reason I open F21
- **Swap the text overlay for a real tap target** — style "SHOP SUMMER '26" as a pill button with a contrasting fill (black or white) so it's unmistakably tappable on mobile
- **Surface F21 Rewards in the top banner** — alternate the email sign-up banner with a "Earn 2x points on summer styles · Rewards members only" message on a cycle; it costs nothing and converts loyalty members faster
- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default after 2 seconds or shrink it to a bottom sheet** so it doesn't eat the hero and the module below it on first load — right now it's blocking a full product image on mobile
