# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- Full holiday pivot — every pixel above the fold is Mother's Day. The brand is running a real BOGO and a secondary apparel discount, both posted upfront, which is operationally clean. As a 44-year-old male auditor here to walk the catalog, nothing on this screen was put there for me.
- **6/10**
- + Concrete offer above fold: BOGO 50% Applied at Cart is specific and transparent
- + Secondary offer above fold: "25% OFF Apparel" in the second banner strip
- + Primary CTA is present and unambiguous: "SHOP NOW" button, full width
- + Visual hierarchy is clear: banner → logo/nav → promo strip → hero → CTA, left-to-right eye path works
- + No render bugs: layout is clean, no broken images, no overlap, no placeholder text
- + Page reflects current campaign: Mother's Day, first week of May — timing is correct
- − Hero imagery/copy does not speak to my persona: mom-and-daughter lifestyle, script-font "The Mother's Day Shop" — no male signal anywhere
- − No returning-shopper hook: no loyalty balance, no recently-viewed above the fold (partially visible label at bottom cut off)
- − No loyalty/membership benefits visible above fold
- − Demographic signals are off-target: the sole human imagery is a woman and a child

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% Applied at Cart** — no coupon code required, no fine print obscuring it. That's a clean discount mechanic.
- **Dual-banner promotion** — two separate offers (BOGO on footwear, 25% on apparel) communicate breadth without being cluttered.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — full-width, high contrast, unambiguous copy. No "Explore the Collection" nonsense.
- **Visual hierarchy** — the eye goes banner → hero → CTA in one beat. No confusion about where to look.
- **Seasonal accuracy** — they know what week it is. The campaign matches the calendar.

## What's weak

- **Hero is 0% male** — a mother kissing her daughter in a flower garden. I'm a 44-year-old Gen X male. There is no entry point here for me.
- **No men's category shortcut visible** — the only nav affordance is a hamburger MENU. To reach men's shoes I'd need a tap, a submenu expansion, and a second tap. That's two steps too many.
- **No free-shipping threshold posted** — both promo banners are about discounts; neither mentions shipping. Auditor instinct: they probably have a threshold but it's buried.
- **No product-level imagery** — the hero is a lifestyle photo. I cannot identify a single specific product, SKU, or silhouette from this screen. I'm here to count shelves and I can't see any.
- **No loyalty hook** — no rewards balance, no member-price badge, no "sign in to unlock" callout above the fold.
- **"Recently Viewed" label is cut off** — partially visible at the bottom, which means it exists but the execution makes it invisible in the first viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop for Him" or "Men's Gifts" secondary CTA** beneath or alongside the hero — Mother's Day is a male gifting moment too, and right now that lane is completely dark.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — swap "details" microlink for a dollar figure: "BOGO 50% Off + Free Shipping on $75+." One line, more information.
- **Replace the lifestyle-only hero with a lifestyle-plus-product shot** — keep the mom-and-daughter scene but frame a specific shoe prominently so the product registers in under two seconds.
- **Add a returning-customer hook to the nav strip** — a "Hi, [Name] — 320 points" or "Member Price" badge costs nothing to place next to the account icon and immediately signals value to logged-in users.
