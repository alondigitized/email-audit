# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a Mother's Day campaign execution, full stop — pink florals, mom-and-daughter lifestyle shot, pink footwear. Skechers is pushing a gifting moment and a BOGO deal, not their catalog breadth. As a male auditor here to walk their full inventory, I landed on the wrong entrance point. The page is clean and technically sound; it just doesn't talk to me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner (blue top bar):** Impossible to miss. "Applied at Cart" removes friction — I don't have to remember a code.
- **Secondary apparel-sale strip:** "25% OFF Apparel | Shop | details" — stacks a second offer without cluttering the hero.
- **"SHOP NOW" button:** Large, full-width, high contrast. No guessing where to tap.
- **Floral campaign execution:** Cohesive — the hero image, the overlay florals, the pink script all read as a deliberate design system, not a cobbled-together asset dump.
- **"Recently Viewed" section:** Barely peeking at the bottom, but it signals they are tracking my session, which means personalization is in play.

## What's weak

- **Hero is a single-campaign dead end for me:** "The Mother's Day Shop" is the headline. That's a destination for buyers shopping for a mother, not for a male auditor who needs to find men's catalog pages.
- **Navigation is fully collapsed:** The only path to any category is through a hamburger MENU tap. No persistent category rail, no gender-toggle, no quick links to Men's or Comfort Tech. That's an extra step I shouldn't need.
- **No men's signal anywhere in the viewport:** Not a product thumbnail, not a nav label, not a sub-banner. If I didn't know Skechers made men's shoes, this page wouldn't tell me.
- **SHOP NOW goes to a collection, not a product:** I'm tapping into Mother's Day Styles, not a specific SKU. Conversion path is long from here.
- **No trust or credibility signals above fold:** No "Free Returns," no review counts, no loyalty-program mention. Just two promo banners and a lifestyle shot.

## Recommendations

- **Add a gender-selector or persistent top-nav row below the promo banners:** One row with "Men / Women / Kids / Sale" gives every visitor an immediate path to their catalog, even when the hero is campaign-locked. Costs nothing and catches all the non-Mother's-Day traffic landing here.
- **Swap the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA for something destination-specific:** "Shop Mother's Day Styles" takes two extra words and tells me exactly where I'm going. The current version could go anywhere.
- **Surface one trust signal above fold:** "Free Returns" or a star-rating badge (e.g., "4.5★ across 1M+ reviews") would land in the white space below the hero without touching the campaign aesthetic.
- **Add a secondary CTA for non-campaign visitors:** A smaller text link — "Shop All Men's" or "Browse New Arrivals" — underneath the primary button. Captures the bounce-prone traffic that arrived looking for something other than a gift.
