## Inventory summary

**156 styles · 468 (color, width) variants · 67.6% avg size coverage**

The Skechers men's shoes category audit reveals an average size coverage of 67.6% across 468 variants, with no failed categories but notable underperformance in specific styles and sizes. The Slip-Ons category stands out as the weakest, with only a 51% average size coverage. Additionally, Casual Sneakers (60%) and Sandals (62%) also exhibit subpar performance compared to other categories.

Across all categories, the most consistently missing sizes are 14.0, 11.0, and 12.5, which appear in fewer variants than expected. The Extra-Wide Fit category shows a strong coverage rate of 78%, but even here, certain sizes like 11.0 and 12.5 are notably absent from numerous variants.

Width-wise gaps are evident across several categories, particularly with the Wide fit being underrepresented in Casual Sneakers (60%), Sandals (62%), and Lace Ups (70%). The Extra-Wide Fit variant is well-represented but still lacks consistent coverage for sizes 11.0 and 12.5.

In summary, while Skechers men's shoes generally offer a decent size range, specific categories like Slip-Ons and Casual Sneakers need improvement to better serve customers seeking broader sizing options. The overall inventory could benefit from enhanced coverage in critical sizes such as 14.0, 11.0, and 12.5.

Bottom line: The Skechers men's shoes category scores a 7/10 for size coverage but needs targeted improvements to address gaps in specific categories and sizes.
