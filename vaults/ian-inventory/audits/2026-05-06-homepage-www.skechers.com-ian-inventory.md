# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- I opened Skechers.com and landed on a Mother's Day campaign page. Clear seasonal push — the brand knows what week it is. As a male auditor here to walk the catalog, this wasn't built for me, but I can read the strategy: dual promo stack (BOGO + apparel), female lifestyle hero, soft conversion path. Standard seasonal homepage swap. Nothing broken, nothing exceptional.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF sticky banner** — blue, high contrast, first thing I see. The "Applied at Cart" language is honest and removes friction.
- **Secondary promo strip** — "25% OFF Apparel" layered under the BOGO. Two distinct offers signal value without competing visually.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — full-width black button, clean button copy, no ambiguity about what happens if I tap it.
- **Visual hierarchy** — the page stacks logically: sticky offer → secondary offer → campaign hero → CTA. A trained eye follows it without effort.
- **Seasonal accuracy** — Mother's Day is the right campaign for this week. The creative matches the calendar.

## What's weak

- **No men's entry point above the fold** — there is no nav expanded, no men's category shortcut, no "Shop Men's" alternative CTA. A male visitor who doesn't scroll is handed a Mother's Day shop or nothing.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "Free Returns," no loyalty badge, no Trustpilot star — nothing that would make a first-time visitor trust the checkout.
- **No urgency mechanism** — Mother's Day has a hard deadline (Sunday) but the page doesn't use it. No "Shop by [date]," no countdown, no "Order by Thursday for delivery" — wasted conversion lever.
- **Floral design treatment** — heavy pink and flower overlay is on-theme for the campaign but actively signals "this is not for you" to any non-gift-buying male visitor. No alternate path offered.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — on mobile, reaching Men's Shoes requires opening the menu. No shortcut tabs, no horizontal category scroll visible.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Men's" ghost button below SHOP NOW** — Mother's Day gifting still includes men's products; give male visitors a path without forcing them into the menu.
- **Install a Mother's Day deadline banner** — swap the generic blue BOGO bar for "BOGO 50% OFF — Order by Sunday 5/11 for Mother's Day delivery." Same offer, real urgency, honest deadline.
- **Surface one trust signal above the fold** — a single line: "Free returns · 4.5M+ reviews" directly under the CTA would cost zero visual real estate and close the credibility gap.
- **Add a horizontal category shortcut strip** between the secondary promo and the hero — Men's | Women's | Kids | Apparel — so any visitor can route themselves without hitting the hamburger.
