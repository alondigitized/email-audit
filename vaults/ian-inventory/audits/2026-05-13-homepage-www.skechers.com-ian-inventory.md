# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a mid-tier lifestyle footwear brand running a double-barrel promotional push — BOGO 50% site-wide on top of a seasonal apparel sale. The hero is locked on one feature technology (Slip-ins / Glide-Step), and the page is doing the job of a campaign homepage, not a catalog homepage. It moves product; it does not move auditors through a category structure.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% top banner** — blue background, white bold text, reads instantly, the first thing the eye hits.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — full-width, high contrast, nothing ambiguous about where the tap goes.
- **Spring Apparel Sale strip** — secondary offer stacked immediately below nav; signals that the sale extends beyond footwear.
- **Recently Viewed tab** — that tab is the only above-fold hook for a returning visitor who was mid-audit last session.
- **Male model in hero** — left panel is male in shoes, so there's at least gender alignment even if the age bracket is off.

## What's weak

- **Two promo banners before the hero** — the blue BOGO strip and the gray apparel strip are stacked, which means the main hero doesn't hit until the third visual block. That's a hierarchy problem.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is destination-agnostic** — it doesn't tell me whether I land on Men's, Slip-ins, or the site root. "Shop Men's Slip-ins" would be stronger.
- **No free-shipping mention anywhere above the fold** — for a $60–$100 AOV shoe brand, that's a checkout-deciding signal that's missing.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no badge ("1M+ sold"), no return policy callout, nothing that reduces purchase risk.
- **Demographic gap in imagery** — both models are clearly in their 20s. The 35–50 male bracket — Skechers' bread and butter for comfort footwear — is not represented.

## Recommendations

- **Split the hero CTA by tab state** — when "Men" is the active tab, the button should read "Shop Men's Slip-ins," not "SHOP NOW." One-week engineering lift, meaningful conversion gain.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip or footnote to the BOGO banner** — something like "BOGO 50% · Free shipping over $75." Both offers, one line.
- **Swap one hero model for someone in the 40s range** — not asking for a catalog overhaul, just a lifestyle shot of someone who could credibly be a 44-year-old professional wearing Slip-ins to commute.
- **Pull a ratings badge onto the hero** — even "4.7★ from 180,000 reviews" in small type below the CTA would address the zero-trust-signal problem above the fold.
