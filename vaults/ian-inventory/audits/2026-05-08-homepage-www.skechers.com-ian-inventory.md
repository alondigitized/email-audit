# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is running a full Mother's Day push — hero, copy, florals, the works. As a contracted auditor walking a men's footwear catalog, I'm the wrong shopper in the wrong aisle. The promotional infrastructure is solid (two active offers stacked above the fold), but every visual signal on this page is aimed at women buying gifts or buying for themselves. This is a competent seasonal execution; it just has nothing to do with me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner (blue top bar):** It's the first line of text on the page. Clear, actionable, and applies to the full catalog — including men's. I clocked it in under one second.
- **Secondary promo strip:** "25% OFF Apparel" gives me a second reason to stay. Two stacked offers before the hero is efficient use of real estate.
- **"SHOP NOW" button:** Unambiguous. Big. Dead center. Whatever its destination, there is no friction finding it.
- **Recently Viewed (partial):** Visible just below the viewport cut — confirms the site has personalization infrastructure for returning visitors, which matters to me on a recheck visit.
- **Seasonal accuracy:** Mother's Day hero in the first week of May is the right call. The shelf is stocked for the right week.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery is entirely feminine:** Mom, daughter, pink sneakers, pink florals — I have zero product to anchor on as a male shopper. No crossover hook for the 44-year-old guy who might also be buying.
- **No men's entry point above the fold:** The nav collapses to a hamburger MENU. There is no "Men's" tab, no "For Him" block, no shortcut. I have to open the menu and hunt.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout:** No points balance, no member badge, no "sign in for exclusive pricing." The BOGO is generic — no reason to be logged in.
- **"SHOP NOW" is vague destination:** The CTA sends me to a Mother's Day collection. There is no secondary CTA splitting traffic to men's or to a category I'd actually walk.
- **No trust signals in the viewport:** No review counts, no free-returns callout, no "free shipping over $X" threshold. The offer lands but the confidence layer is missing.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop for Him" or "Men's Gifts" secondary CTA** below the hero button — keeps the Mother's Day theme while capturing the male gift-buyer and the male auditor who accidentally lands here.
- **Move free-shipping threshold into the top banner** alongside the BOGO line — e.g., "BOGO 50% + Free Shipping Over $75." One more line of text, one more reason to stay.
- **Surface a loyalty hook in the nav strip** — a small "Rewards" link or logged-in points balance next to the account icon costs nothing and makes returning visitors feel recognized.
- **Swap or add a second hero tile** that shows a men's or gender-neutral lifestyle shot — a split hero carousel takes 10 seconds to scroll and doubles the demographic reach without killing the Mother's Day push.
