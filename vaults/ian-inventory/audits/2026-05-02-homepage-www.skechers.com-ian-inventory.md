# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is running a lifestyle/celebrity play right now — two recognizable faces (Brooke Burke and Tony Hawk, by the name tags) sitting around looking comfortable in slip-ins. The promotional stack is aggressive: BOGO 50% at the top, an apparel sale banner underneath. This is a volume-driven, promotion-first brand making a simultaneous comfort-tech push with the Hands Free Slip-ins headline cut off at the bottom of the fold.
- **4/10**

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner (top strip):** Applied at cart — no code needed. That's operationally clean. I register it immediately.
- **Dual-celebrity split layout:** Brooke Burke + Tony Hawk cover both demographics in one shot. Efficient use of real estate for a broad brand.
- **"Hands Free Slip-ins" copy emerging below fold:** The product name is descriptive and functional. For someone who wants to know what the shoe *does*, that framing works.
- **Cart badge (0) visible:** Nav is clean, no clutter. I can get to checkout from anywhere.

## What's weak

- **No product-first entry point above the fold:** I'm an auditor. I want to see category links — Men's, Women's, Work, Wide Width. There are none visible. The hero is 100% lifestyle; zero catalog navigation.
- **25% OFF Apparel banner:** I'm here for shoes. Apparel is noise. It's taking up a full horizontal strip of prime real estate.
- **Hero copy is absent:** The celebrity image has no headline overlay. No "Comfort that performs" or "The shoe that fits your life." It's just two people sitting.
- **Slip-ins hero is clipped:** The most product-forward message on the page is cut off at the bottom. I'd have to scroll to see the CTA, the product image, anything actionable.
- **Name tags on celebrities are tiny:** "Brooke Burke" and "Tony" — I had to look closely. If brand ambassadors are the play, make the endorsement legible.

## Recommendations

- **Add a visible category nav row below the logo bar** — Men's / Women's / Work / Wide / New Arrivals — so auditors and intent shoppers can drill in without scrolling.
- **Overlay a headline on the celebrity hero:** Something like "Comfort. No excuses." — make the lifestyle image do double duty as brand messaging.
- **Move the Slip-ins module above the apparel sale strip:** The Slip-ins are the actual innovation push; the apparel sale is a clearance event. Rank accordingly.
- **Collapse the dual promo banners into one:** BOGO + Apparel Sale stacked is already two asks. Pick a lead offer and link the secondary in fine print.
