## Inventory summary

**156 styles · 462 (color, width) variants · 68.8% avg size coverage**

The Skechers men's shoe inventory shows an average size coverage of 68.8% across 462 variants, indicating generally solid stock levels but significant variability by category and specific sizes. The Slip-Ons category stands out as the weakest, with only 46% average size coverage. Other categories like Casual Sneakers (61%) and Boat Shoes (65%) also fall below the overall average.

The Extra-Wide Fit and Hands Free Slip-ins categories have strong performance at 78% each, highlighting a consistent presence of extra-wide sizes across these styles. However, common sizes such as 6.5, 12.5, and 14.0 are frequently missing across multiple categories, particularly in the Athletic Sneakers, Casual Sneakers, and Boat Shoes sections.

Width-specific gaps are notable in several categories. For instance, Wide fits are notably thin in Slip-Ons, where only a few sizes are available. Similarly, Medium widths in Sandals suffer from poor coverage of key sizes like 14.0, 7.5, and 8.5.

Overall, while Skechers maintains robust inventory levels for certain categories and size ranges, there is room for improvement in ensuring consistent availability across all sizes and fits, especially in the Slip-Ons category. The score reflects this mixed performance: **6/10**.
