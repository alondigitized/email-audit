# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is running a Mother's Day campaign, full stop. This is a gifting push — mom-and-daughter hero image, floral graphics, script lettering. The BOGO is stacked on top of a Spring Apparel sale, so there's actual merchandise breadth behind it. As a catalog auditor, I clocked the promo structure fast, but this page is not dressed for me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% top banner** — highest-visibility slot, high-impact promo, immediately clear. I noted it in under two seconds.
- **Secondary apparel sale strip** — "25% OFF Apparel Shop | details" catches the eye below the nav. Two promos stacked without visual collision is solid execution.
- **Full-width "SHOP NOW" button** — no ambiguity. One tap. It's not buried in the hero.
- **Hero image quality** — clean photography, no compression artifacts, well-lit. The floral overlay is busy but doesn't obscure the shoes.
- **Campaign timing** — Mother's Day content on May 7 is exactly right. No stale assets.

## What's weak

- **Zero category entry points above the fold** — if I'm auditing Men's or Work footwear, I'm starting from a hamburger menu. No shortcuts to anything other than the Mother's Day Shop.
- **Script/cursive headline** — "The Mother's Day Shop" in pink cursive reads fine for the target buyer; it does nothing for a male browsing for himself or for catalog depth assessment.
- **No loyalty or rewards signal** — Skechers has an Elite Rewards program. Nothing above the fold confirms that. The BOGO doesn't reference member pricing.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "free returns," no shipping threshold, no security badge. All deferred to deeper pages.
- **"Recently Viewed" barely visible** — it's cut at the very bottom of the viewport. Useful feature, poor placement signal.

## Recommendations

- **Add a slim men's / categories nav row below the secondary banner** — one-tap entry to Men's, Women's, Wide Fit, Work. Costs no visual real estate and serves every non-gift shopper arriving during the campaign.
- **Surface the Elite Rewards hook in the BOGO banner** — something like "BOGO 50% — Members earn double points." Keeps the promo, adds retention value, and requires zero new creative.
- **Put the free-shipping threshold on the banner or below the CTA** — "Free shipping on orders $75+" is a conversion trigger that belongs above the fold, not buried in cart.
- **Swap the "SHOP NOW" copy to "Shop the Mother's Day Gift Guide"** — the current label is generic. Specificity increases tap confidence for gift-buyers and makes it easier to audit where the CTA actually lands.
