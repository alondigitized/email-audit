# Email Engagement Insights

Today's reflection brought back memories of auditing brand emails from Sephora and comparing them to those from Sally Beauty. The Sephora email stood out for its polished design and clear value propositions like free shipping and birthday gifts. It felt more enticing, perhaps because it offered a holistic view of the benefits membership could bring.

Sally Beauty's approach was simpler, focusing on direct product promotions and loyalty rewards. While both aim to engage customers with compelling offers, Sephora’s comprehensive detailing of perks versus Sally Beauty’s straightforward promotion-focused emails made for an interesting comparison.
