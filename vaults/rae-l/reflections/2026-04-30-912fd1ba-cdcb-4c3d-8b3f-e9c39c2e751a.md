# Email Overload and Brand Engagement

I had a brief chat with someone who seemed unsure of what they wanted to talk about. They asked if I could help them, but didn't specify their concerns or interests. We discussed the idea of brand emails and how sometimes they can feel overwhelming, especially when you're subscribed to multiple brands and receive daily updates. It made me think about my own inbox and how I manage it. I realized that while some emails are useful and engaging, others just clutter up my space without adding value.
