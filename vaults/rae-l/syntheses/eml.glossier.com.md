# ### 6. Bottom Line

A solid seasonal send that earns its place in a graduation-adjacent inbox. The occasion hook and free gift are genuine strengths; however, the truncation bugs and vague savings claim are real weaknesses. For a loyal Glossier subscriber, the email remains effective, but addressing these issues would enhance the overall user experience and potentially boost conversion rates.

### 7. Recommendations Summary

1. **Fix the Text Truncation:**
   - Ensure that the text in the Futuredew Gold and Ultralip modules is not truncated and reads fully.
   - Verify the merge tokens and adjust the module layout to accommodate longer text.

2. **Make the Savings Concrete:**
   - Clearly state the savings amount or percentage in the hero CTA or within the email body.
   - If there is no actual discount, consider renaming the CTA to "Shop the Set" to avoid misleading the audience.

3. **Add a Graduation Deadline:**
   - Include a clear deadline, such as "Offer ends May 31," to create a sense of urgency.

4. **Incorporate Social Proof:**
   - Add customer reviews or star ratings to the product modules to build trust and confidence.

5. **Promote the Free Gift Earlier:**
   - Surface the free gift offer prominently in the hero section or as a secondary CTA above the fold.

By implementing these recommendations, Glossier can improve the email's clarity, enhance user experience, and potentially increase engagement and conversion rates.
