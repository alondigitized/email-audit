# Save up to 25% off all fragrance
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's fourth consecutive Friends of Glossier Sale email in four days — and it's narrowing its aperture smartly by pivoting from the general sale announcement to a fragrance-specific pitch. That category focus is the right editorial move at this stage of a sale arc: rather than repeat "the sale is live" for the fifth time, they're giving openers a curated reason to come back. The grid of Glossier You products with visible discounted prices and star ratings is well-executed product marketing.
- The problem is fatigue, not creative. Yesterday's subject (June 13: "Save up to 25% off") and today's ("Save up to 25% off all fragrance") are near-twins from an inbox preview perspective. Any subscriber who opened yesterday will pattern-match this as a duplicate and skip it. The "all fragrance" qualifier is genuinely new information but it's buried at the end of a phrase the subscriber already ignored or acted on yesterday.
- Taken in isolation this is a clean, capable promotional email. Taken in sequence — and subscribers experience it in sequence — it's the diminishing return of a campaign that should have planned more distinct chapter beats from day one.

## What's working

- **Category refinement is the right move on Day 4** — pivoting from a general sale to "all fragrance" gives a genuine reason to re-engage lapsed openers from earlier sends.
- **Product grid with visible prices and discounts** — the layout shows products, original pricing, and sale pricing at a glance; no hunting required.
- **Star rating visible above the fold** — social proof from reviews lands early and adds credibility to the hero product.
- **Subscription lock-in upsell at the bottom** — the "Own a subscription and lock in 25% off" close is a smart retention play that adds a secondary conversion layer beyond the transactional click.
- **Orange "Buy Now" CTAs are unambiguous** — the contrast works and the button copy passes the basic clarity test even if it's generic.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is near-identical to yesterday's** — "Save up to 25% off" (June 13) vs. "Save up to 25% off all fragrance" today; subscribers who opened both will experience deja vu, and those who didn't open yesterday now have two reasons not to.
- **"Up to 25% off" is the weakest version of a discount claim** — it implies some items won't hit 25%, which undercuts urgency. "25% off all Glossier You" would be more powerful if accurate.
- **No visible deadline in the email body** — the sale end date isn't visible in the render, which squanders the natural urgency of a closing sale window.
- **"Buy Now" CTA is verb-generic** — on a fragrance-specific send, "Shop Fragrance" or "Grab Glossier You" would sharpen the click intent and reinforce the category pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a hard close date in the subject and hero** — this is a time-bound sale, and there's no countdown or explicit deadline visible. "Ends Tuesday" or "Last 48 hours" would recover the urgency the campaign should be building toward by Day 4.
- 2. **Make the subject line clearly distinct from June 13's** — the two are too similar. Lead with the most specific, exclusive fragrance angle that differentiates this send.
- 3. **Swap "Buy Now" for category-specific CTA copy** — "Shop All Fragrance" or "Find Your Scent" would reinforce the editorial angle of this send.
- 4. **Add a product count or selection callout** — "8 fragrances, 25% off, ending soon" in the hero copy tells the subscriber what's new vs. the general sale page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 25% off every Glossier fragrance`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your final window to shop all Glossier scents at sale pricing — closes Tuesday.`

## 1. Overview

This is Glossier's fourth consecutive Friends of Glossier Sale email in four days — and it's narrowing its aperture smartly by pivoting from the general sale announcement to a fragrance-specific pitch.
