# 20% off ends in a few hours
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the second ILIA email to land on May 28 — a plain-text "personal note from Marissa" format dropped on top of the already-audited "Finishing touches—20% off ends tonight" (9/10). That's two final-day emails on a campaign already 18 sends deep. The deliberate pivot to full plain text is a defensible creative call: after 18 HTML-rich sends, a personal-note format can cut through subscriber numbness the way a polished template no longer can.
- The content is actually the strongest articulation of the tiered gift offer across the entire campaign. The 40% off select items callout with a scarcity parenthetical is a genuine plus, and the P.S. VIP upgrade play targeting non-members is smart acquisition work. But the execution has one glaring omission: no CTA button anywhere. The only click path is a blue text link ("final hours to shop the sale") buried near the bottom — easy to miss on mobile, invisible to skim-readers.
- Judgement: the format idea earns its keep as a final burst, but it needed a button. If your subject line is screaming "ends in a few hours," your email body cannot make the subscriber hunt for a text link to act on that urgency.

## What's working

- Plain-text format is a genuine pattern interrupt after 18 polished HTML sends — "Marissa from ILIA" reads personal in a way the prior templated emails couldn't.
- Tiered gift structure (any order / $100+ / $175+) is the clearest, most scannable version of this offer in the entire campaign.
- "40% off select items (heads up, these are going fast)" is the best scarcity signal in any of the F+F emails — specific offer + urgency cue in one line.
- P.S. non-VIP upgrade hook ("join today, it's free, get 25% off") is a concrete acquisition lever with a real incentive differential over the 20% sitewide.
- Subject-to-opening-line alignment is tight — "20% off ends in a few hours" → "20% off sitewide ends tonight" — no dissonance, no bait-and-switch.

## What's weak

- No CTA button — the only click path is a text link near the bottom of a wall of copy.
- "Hi VIP," wastes the personal-email format's strongest card: the greeting should be the subscriber's first name to earn the tone.
- Same-day double-send (this + "Finishing touches") makes both emails feel cheaper; anyone who already acted on the earlier one will tune this out immediately.
- Signed "Warmly, Marissa from ILIA" after opening from a corporate sender address (`info@iliabeauty.com`) — the split-personality (broadcast domain + personal close) undermines the authentic-note format.
- Zero product imagery: in a beauty context, a mascara wand or bronzer swatch would anchor attention in a way even a plain-text email can support with a single inline image.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a CTA button** — this is the single highest-impact change. Even within a plain-text aesthetic, an HTML button ("Shop the Sale — Ends Tonight") placed once after the gift-tier list and once after the P.S. would dramatically lift click-through.
- 2. **Personalize the greeting** — swap "Hi VIP," for "Hi [First Name]," to actually justify the personal-email format. This is table-stakes for a send that is trying to feel like a human note.
- 3. **Suppress May 28 openers/clickers from this send** — both emails should never hit the same inbox on the same day; the second send should target non-openers of "Finishing touches" only.
- 4. **Lead with the most aggressive offer** — "40% off select items" is the sharpest hook but it's buried in the second bullet group; move it into the opening paragraph to earn the open immediately.
- 5. **Give the P.S. VIP link its own destination** — linking to the VIP sign-up directly (not the general sale) removes a friction step for the acquisition play to work.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off + free gifts end at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `A quick note — your ILIA sale ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tiered free gifts still available — mascara, bronzer, Barrier Build with your order`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `40% off select styles going fast — final hours, VIP`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — ILIA "20% off ends in a few hours"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Plain-prose Klaviyo send with three redirect CTAs through `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`. The three "broken links" in QA output are quoted-printable decoding artifacts in the relay pipeline, not live link failures — but they mask whether the actual destination URLs are healthy. CAN-SPAM physical address is absent, and compliance headers were not captured.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**QA-flagged broken links — pipeline artifacts, not confirmed live breaks:**

| QA URL | Root cause |
|--------|-----------|
| `https://iliabeauty.com/c=` | URL truncated; trailing `=` is an unescaped QP soft-wrap character |
| `https://iliabeauty.=` | Severely truncated; same QP encoding corruption |
| `https://manage.kmail-lists.com/subscriptions/unsubscribe?a=3DSguDsz&=` | `=3D` is QP-encoded `=`; checker ran against encoded form rather than decoded URL |

**Assessment:** The QA tool is not decoding quoted-printable before URL validation, producing false positives. The three links in the live HTML — `_0`, `_1`, `_2` suffixed — were not independently verified and may be fine. However, the Klaviyo unsubscribe redirect (`_2`) ultimately resolves to `manage.kmail-lists.com`, which should be spot-checked against a live send.

**No UTM parameters are visible in source.** All links pass through the Klaviyo redirect layer; UTM append must be verified in account settings (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **`<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute.** Screen readers cannot determine document language. Should be `<html lang="en">`.
- **`<head>` missing `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta name="viewport">`.** Absent charset risks encoding misinterpretation in some clients; absent viewport affects mobile scaling.
- **Every text node is wrapped `<p>` → `<span>` with identical inline styles repeated per element.** Functionally harmless but increases payload size and can produce inconsistent margin rendering across clients due to varying `<p>` default margin resets.
- **Tracking pixel alt text:** HTML has `alt=""` on `<img src="https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01KSR9NXWQJDNWRB1PTBN8K3PJ">`, which is correct for a decorative/tracking pixel. QA flag is a false positive.
- **Custom font (`Karla`) loaded via `@import` inside an MSO conditional comment.** Correct pattern — Outlook will fall back to Arial as declared; no issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Greeting renders as literal `"Hi VIP,"` with no Klaviyo merge tag.** Cannot determine from source whether this is intentional segment-level addressing or a failed/missing `{{ first_name | default: 'VIP' }}` token. If intentional, no issue; if fallback-only, first-name personalization is absent.
- No other dynamic tokens or unfilled placeholders visible in source.

UTM parameters, if configured, would be appended by Klaviyo on redirect. **UTM pass-through cannot be confirmed from this source alone.** Destination landing page alignment (sale page live, correct discount applied, GWP thresholds matching copy) must be verified by clicking through from a test send.

### 7. Recommendations
