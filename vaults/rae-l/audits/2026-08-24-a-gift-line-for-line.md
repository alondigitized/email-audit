# A gift—line for line
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA back in Thru Line territory again, but with a twist: the offer is now a free Lip Sketch Hydrating Crayon GWP tied to Thru Line orders, not the eyeliner-mini-free deal from 08-19's "1 free mini, 3 ways to wear." The hero is genuinely strong — a tight close-up of a lash line with clean "In Every Line" script copy and a clear GWP callout — and the "Shop Thru Line → Get mini Lip Sketch" two-step visual is one of the clearer offer mechanics ILIA has run lately.
- Where it falls down is length and focus. After the strong hero and the shade/benefit grid, the email sprawls into an eight-product "More That Draws the Eye" grid, then a "Clean at ILIA" brand block, then a "Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better" close — three distinct campaigns crammed into one send. It reads like a merged newsletter rather than a single-offer promo, which dilutes the GWP hook that opened strong.
- Solid top half, bloated back half. This lands mid-pack — better constructed than the 08-18 render-failure send or the 08-17 manifesto pivot, but a notch below the disciplined 08-20 "prove it" execution.

## What's working

- Clear, specific GWP mechanic stated twice (hero + banner): free Lip Sketch Hydrating Crayon with Thru Line order.
- Hero image and typography are clean with no render bugs — legible script headline over a well-cropped lash close-up.
- The "Shop Thru Line → Get mini Lip Sketch" two-column visual makes the bundle mechanic easy to grasp at a glance.
- Ingredient/benefit bullets (24-hour wear, waterproof, bleed-resistant) are concrete and scannable.

## What's weak

- The email keeps going well past the offer — an 8-SKU grid plus two separate brand-manifesto blocks turn a focused GWP promo into a scroll marathon.
- "More That Draws the Eye" section has nothing to do with the stated Thru Line/Lip Sketch offer, diluting the primary CTA.
- Two competing sign-offs ("in safe hands" / "Clean at ILIA" and "Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better") feel like two different emails stitched together.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere — the GWP offer could be evergreen, which weakens the case to click today rather than later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the shade/benefit section and the "SHOP TO CLAIM" CTA — drop the 8-product grid and one of the two closing brand blocks entirely to keep the GWP offer as the singular focus.
- 2. Add a deadline or stock caveat to the GWP ("while supplies last" or a date) to create urgency the current version lacks.
- 3. Move "*See site for details" fine print closer to the GWP mention itself rather than burying it after the unrelated product grid.
- 4. Pick one brand close, not two — "in safe hands" or "Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better," not both.
