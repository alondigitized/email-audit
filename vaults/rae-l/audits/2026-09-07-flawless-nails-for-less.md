# Flawless nails, for less 💅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a nails deal — "Flawless nails, for less 💅" — but the email doesn't deliver that in the first screen. What opens is an app-download pitch ("Download the app for Appiversary exclusives"), and the actual offer (30% off nail essentials, code 30NAILS) doesn't appear until you scroll past the hero image. This is the same bait a subscriber would have seen in the 9/1 "Appiversary celebration" send — a vague headline sitting on top of the real mechanic, which was scored 5/10 for exactly this reason.
- Once you scroll, the email is actually solid: a real discount code with a real deadline (Sept 11), a clean shoppable product grid (Kiara Sky, Dashing Diva, BTArtbox), and a Sally Rewards / Labor Day multiplier push that's clearly laid out. The execution below the fold is good — it's the top of the email that undersells its own subject line.
- Net: a competent mid-funnel email wearing a mismatched headline. Subscribers who open expecting a nail deal get an app ad first, which is a credibility tax the brand keeps paying across this campaign.

## What's working

- The 30% off nail essentials offer (code 30NAILS, ends Sept 11) is a real, specific, time-bounded discount.
- The product grid is genuinely useful — real product names, prices implied by "Shop now," and a mix of polish/press-ons/tools that matches the subject's "nails" focus.
- Labor Day loyalty tiers (2x/3x/4x points) are clearly laid out with dollar thresholds, giving members a concrete reason to spend more.
- No render bugs — layout is clean, images load, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- Hero copy ("Download the app for Appiversary exclusives") doesn't match the subject's nails-and-savings promise — the eye lands on an app ad, not an offer.
- The actual nails discount is buried below the hero and its own supporting image, adding a scroll before payoff.
- Top nav is cluttered with two competing banners (BOPIS beauty box, 2-hour delivery) before the reader even reaches the hero — three different value props stacked before the "real" offer.
- No product-level specifics (shade options, sizes, price points) on any of the featured items — just names and "Shop now."

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the hero to lead with the nails offer itself, not the app download — put "30% off nail essentials, code 30NAILS" as the first thing seen, with app-exclusivity as a secondary line underneath.
- 2. Cut one of the two top banners — running BOPIS-beauty-box and 2-hour-delivery back to back before the hero dilutes attention before the reader ever sees the actual offer.
- 3. Add a price or shade-count detail to at least the top two product tiles (e.g., "12 shades available") to give the click something concrete to land on.
- 4. Drop or soften the app-download ask — three of the last five Sally sends have leaned on the Appiversary/app mechanic; subscribers who don't want the app are being asked to jump through a hoop for a discount that could just as easily run sitewide.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your nail restock is 30% off 💅`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code 30NAILS before September 11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Polish, press-ons & tools — all 30% off this week.`
