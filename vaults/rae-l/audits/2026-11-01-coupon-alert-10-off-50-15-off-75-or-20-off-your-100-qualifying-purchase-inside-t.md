# Coupon alert! $10 off $50, $15 off $75 or $20 off your $100 qualifying purchase⁺⁺ inside! (Terms & excl. apply) 🤑
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, well-built coupon send. Three stacked tiers ($10 off $50, $15 off $75, $20 off $100) sit in a big pink hero, and the eye lands on them first. A barcode, the in-store and online code GIFTS, and a "Nov 8 at 11:59pm CT" deadline make it easy to redeem. For a deal-driven beauty shopper, the offer is clear.
- The rest of the email dilutes it. A rewards-join banner is stacked above the hero, and eight "favorites" product tiles sit below it with unrelated "+ free gift" tags. After that come community, app, events and "double the love" blocks, then a wall of fine print. It is the fifth or so promo since the Oct 25 Early Black Friday kickoff and the Oct 6–7 SAVE20 push. The send carries a real incentive, but the long subject line and heavy footer undercut it.
- Verdict: solid for conversion, mediocre for the inbox. The hero does the work, and everything after the product grid adds length without adding clicks.

## What's working

- The three-tier coupon hero is the clearest thing in the email, with oversized "$10 off / $15 off / $20 off" type on pink.
- "Yep, includes prestige brands & fragrance!" removes the usual worry that prestige is excluded.
- The barcode, the GIFTS code and the "Offer ends November 8" deadline give both in-store and online shoppers a way to redeem and a credible end date.
- The product grid shows real prices (MAC $39, NARS $55, Clinique $32, Medicube $21) and a visible markdown on the Shark SilkPro ($249.99 → $199.99).
- The "Not to play favorites, but…" header gives a warm, on-brand transition into the picks.

## What's weak

- The subject line is very long (~113 characters) and stacks three price tiers, "Terms & excl. apply" and an emoji. Most of it will truncate on mobile.
- The rewards-join banner above the hero competes with the coupon. Its "Join for free" button pulls attention before the offer does.
- The "+ free gift**" tags on only some tiles (MAC, NARS, Clinique, Sol de Janeiro, Medicube, Tarte) muddy the offer. It is unclear whether the gifts stack with the coupon, and the double asterisks send the reader to fine print.
- The lower half (community, app, events, "double the love") and the dense legal block make the email very long and bury the best content.
- The CTAs are generic: "Shop now" on the hero and "Shop now" under each product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the coupon hero to the very top and demote or remove the "join Ulta Beauty Rewards" banner. For a non-member it can sit below the grid.
- 2. Cut the product grid to 4 tiles that clearly qualify, and show "$X after coupon" prices. That ties the coupon to a purchase.
- 3. Pick one gift-with-purchase story or drop the "+ free gift" tags, so they don't compete with the coupon.
- 4. Remove the community, app and events blocks from this send, and shorten the legal text.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop $20 off $100" or "Get my coupon".
- 6. Shorten the subject to about 50 characters and put the best tier first.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rae, $20 off $100 ends Nov 8 🎟️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your coupon: up to $20 off, prestige included`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code GIFTS online or scan in store. $10 off $50, $15 off $75. Ends Nov 8.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Prestige brands and fragrance count. Your picks from MAC, NARS and Sol de Janeiro inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`cl.s11.exct.net`) with an Oracle/Responsys-style `emltrk.com` open pixel. Its main confirmed problems are about 30 images with no alt text and 11 plain-HTTP third-party image calls (10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels plus a LiveContent call). The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and broken links are unverified.
- **Ten non-HTTPS cookie-sync pixels.** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1` are all plain HTTP.
  - Clients that enforce HTTPS image loading, or that proxy images, may block or rewrite them.
  - They add 10 third-party requests on open, which slows render and raises privacy exposure.
- **Non-HTTPS dynamic content.** `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7Im…` is served over HTTP and has no alt text. If a client blocks it, the personalized block fails to render.
- **Open pixel.** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=163491` has no alt text. The campaign key is `pr_fy26wk40_sunday_broadcoupon_PZ_10am_last`.
- **Other tracking images.** `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png?mi_u=202740837&mi_ign=163491` and `…/3eb758650b65389e.png` have no alt text. They are likely recommendation or personalization images.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 30 images**, per the QA output.
  - Content images:

    - `…/m/44/414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`

  - Tracking and cookie-sync pixels: the `pub*.php`, `ixtw8lub` and `mi.ulta.com` images.
  - The content images should have descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. With images off, a coupon email with no alt text may show nothing for the offer.
  - Pixels should carry `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Google Fonts `<link>`** (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans…`). Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore it, so a fallback stack must exist. I can't confirm one in the truncated source.
- **Unclosed meta tags.** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" …>` are not self-closed, under an XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype. This is minor and most clients tolerate it.
- **Dark mode.** The email declares `color-scheme: light dark`. I can't verify that image or logo handling works in dark mode from the truncated source.

No issues found. The visible portion has no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`). The subject has none either. The source is truncated, so the body below the CSS is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers.
  - Treat this as unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
  - Check the raw `.eml` from a direct inbox before concluding anything. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. Run `dig TXT _dmarc.e.ulta.com` and check the DKIM selector in a direct-delivery copy.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address.** Neither is visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 46 tracking links were skipped, and the source is truncated before any visible `<a>` tags. I could not confirm UTM parameters or landing page alignment with the $10/$15/$20 tiered coupon. The campaign key `pr_fy26wk40_sunday_broadcoupon_PZ_10am_last` suggests attribution runs through the ESP redirect rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels and the `LiveContent.aspx` call to HTTPS, or drop the Dotomi pixels. Ten third-party cookie-sync calls in an email are heavy.
3. Verify the headers in a raw-source copy: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.ulta.com`.
4. Resolve a sample of the 46 tracked links with a follow-redirect probe. Check the final URLs for UTMs, status codes, and whether they land on the coupon or promo page.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the font fallback stack.
