# Get Milky Jelly Cleanser, free!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the same gift-with-purchase offer Glossier sent on 10/22 ("Want a free gift?"). It arrives one day later with a new subject and a lightly reworked hero. The offer is clear. "Free Milky Jelly Cleanser, anyone?" sits next to a big hand-held bottle shot, with a $75+ order threshold and a "Shop now" button.
- The weak spot is that "limited time" has no date and the real terms are hidden behind an asterisk at the bottom. After the 20% run (10/15, 10/17, 10/18, 10/20) and the 10/22 gift email, a subscriber sees a near-duplicate and has little reason to act now. The lower half then dilutes the offer. A "Believe the love" review block and four "Holiday Favorites" products all carry the same generic "Shop now" button, so the free-gift message gets buried.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand repeat send with no new hook, and a decent but forgettable email.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. The offer headline, a short line of copy and a button sit beside a strong product photo, so the eye lands on the free cleanser first.
- The offer is concrete: a free full-size Milky Jelly Cleanser with a $75+ order.
- The five-star "Believe the love" quote adds social proof, and it speaks to the gentle, non-stripping feel.
- The "Holiday Favorites" grid is visually rich and seasonal, and gives gifting shoppers a way to reach $75.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlaps, and the brand voice is consistent.

## What's weak

- It is a near-repeat of the 10/22 send, with the same offer and the same cleanser, so it reads as cadence fatigue.
- "For a limited time" gives no end date or countdown, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Every button says "Shop now". None says which product or benefit it leads to.
- The cleanser appears three times (hero, review, offer copy), but nothing shows how close a shopper is to the $75 threshold or which products would get them there.
- The key condition, the "Terms apply" asterisk, sits in tiny type at the very bottom.
- The footer is cluttered, with two addresses and large doodle graphics, and it ends the email without a final call to action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Free through Sunday" or "While supplies last", and repeat it in the subject. This single change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Show the threshold math near the top: "Free Milky Jelly with $75+. Build your bundle below." Then turn the Holiday Favorites grid into "Top up to $75" with prices.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones, like "Get my free cleanser" and "Add Balm Dotcom".
- 4. Put the key terms (minimum, one per customer, while supplies last) in a line right under the hero button, not only in the footer.
- 5. If the free gift runs for several days, space the sends out, or give each one a different angle, such as a skin-routine or gifting story.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free cleanser is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get a full-size Milky Jelly on us. Gifting done early.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Add a Balm Dotcom or two and the cleanser ships free.`
