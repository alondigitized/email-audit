# Two gifts are better than one
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's most fractured send in the recent run — it leads with a mascara "Swipe Twice" hero, drops a "Latest + Greatest" six-product grid, and closes with a "Mini Tote is Back" announcement, all while the subject line promises "two gifts" that never materialize with any specificity in the body. After "That flushed feeling" (9/10, July 15) and the brown mascara launch (8/10, July 14), which were both tightly focused single-concept sends, this kitchen-sink structure is a regression.
- The mascara feature section is the strongest part — the eye close-up, testimonial quote, and clean product-on-white imagery all hold up. But "MAKE YOUR FIRST ORDER" as the primary CTA is a jarring mismatch on what is clearly a retention list. Whether this is a segmentation miss or a copy error, it undercuts trust.
- The clearest strategic problem: the subject writes a check the body doesn't cash. If a subscriber opens expecting to see two defined gifts — spend X, get Y and Z — and instead finds a mascara editorial and a grid of six products, the click becomes exploratory rather than motivated. This email needed to lead with the gift mechanic front and center, not bury it.

## What's working

- Eye close-up hero photography is vivid and lash-focused — strong visual continuation from the July 14 mascara launch.
- "Mascara Is a Mood" subhead is on-brand voice; feels like ILIA, not generic copy.
- "Latest + Greatest" product grid is clean — consistent card treatment, "SHOP NOW" buttons are clearly visible on each.
- The Mini Tote section adds a perceived-exclusivity beat that regular subscribers respond to (it's restocked, not always available).
- Render is clean: no broken images, no layout breaks, readable font sizing throughout.

## What's weak

- "Two gifts are better than one" is the subject, but the gift offer and terms are never clearly surfaced in the body — the promise goes unfulfilled.
- "MAKE YOUR FIRST ORDER" as the primary hero CTA reads as a new-customer acquisition prompt on what appears to be an existing subscriber send — this is a significant segmentation or copy error.
- This is the second mascara-forward email in four days (after July 14's "NEW—brown mascara, made mini"); the category is doing back-to-back, which risks engagement fatigue on that specific product line.
- Three distinct narrative frames (gifts + mascara tutorial + tote relaunch) compete for attention — no single message lands with weight.
- No urgency signal anywhere — the gifts feel evergreen, which removes any reason to act now rather than later.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the gift mechanic, not the mascara feature.** If the offer is "two free gifts on orders over $X," make that the full-width hero with a clear spend threshold and a list of what the gifts are. The mascara content can live below as the editorial vehicle.
- 2. **Fix the CTA copy for existing subscribers.** "MAKE YOUR FIRST ORDER" needs to be "SHOP THE OFFER" or "CLAIM YOUR GIFTS" — anything that doesn't signal this person has never bought from ILIA before.
- 3. **Cut the email to two sections.** Gift hero + one supporting product or category. The tote relaunch and "Latest + Greatest" grid both deserve their own dedicated send; stacking them here dilutes all three.
- 4. **Add a deadline.** Even "Offer ends Sunday" would force the decision and justify the open. Right now there's no friction to wait.
- 5. **Separate the tote relaunch.** The Mini Tote scored well as a standalone concept in brand history — sending it here as a footer footnote wastes it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Two free gifts with your next order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pick two gifts — on us`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, choose two complimentary products — no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your free gift picks: [Gift A] and [Gift B], while supplies last`
