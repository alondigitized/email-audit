# Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Cyber Week send in three days, after "Cyber Week daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app 👀" on 11/21 and "PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!" on 11/22. It is the strongest of the three on offer clarity. The hero reads "CYBER WEEK DEAL / Massive Haul Incoming / Get 30% off these top brands for 24 hours—only at Sephora. In store and online." Two big "30% OFF" blocks (OLEHENRIKSEN, Milk Makeup) back it up, each with a SAVE NOW button. Unlike the 11/19 Dyson send, the subject matches what's in the body.
- The email then runs far too long. After the two 30% blocks come a price grid, a rhode launch, two side-by-side promos (hair tools and MAC), a Black Friday app teaser, a store locator, a gift guide, a loyalty-points code with a barcode, and footer legalese. A purple top banner offers 4x points on gift fragrance with code CYBERSCENT. A 24-hour deal, a gift-set points offer and a 50% preview all compete for the same attention.
- Verdict: a solid, on-theme send. Its first two screens are strong and the rest dilutes them. Because this is the third Cyber Week email in three days, the repeat cadence and the "30% off" creative from the last two sends will make it feel familiar.

## What's working

- The "Massive Haul Incoming" hero states a concrete offer: 30% off, 24 hours, in store and online.
- Large "30% OFF" numerals on the purple sequin background pull the eye straight to the offer.
- Each brand block (OLEHENRIKSEN, Milk) has its own SAVE NOW button, so the CTAs are clear.
- The price grid shows real sale and original prices, such as $7.00 against $10.00 and $34.00 against $40.00. That is honest and easy to scan.
- The store block shows the nearest location (Sephora at Kohl's, Montebello) with hours. That is a useful local touch.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with at least ten distinct modules, so the 24-hour urgency gets buried.
- The top banner (4x points, code CYBERSCENT) competes with the hero 30% offer for the first read.
- The price-grid items have no names or CTAs, so shoppers can't tell what they're looking at.
- The rhode block, the "Spot it. Shop it." tiles and the Black Friday app preview each pitch something different, with no clear hierarchy.
- This is the third near-identical Cyber Week purple send in three days, and the brand history shows the same "30% OFF" hero formula.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the two 30% brands, the price grid and one app CTA. Move the gift guide, store finder and points barcode out of the main flow.
- 2. Pick one offer for the banner. Either run the 4x points offer in its own send, or make it a slim line below the hero.
- 3. Add product names and a "Shop the grid" CTA to the price grid, or link each item.
- 4. Put a countdown or "ends tonight" marker on the 24-hour deal.
- 5. Vary the creative across the Cyber Week sequence, for example an early-access send and a last-chance send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based 700px layout with first-party tracking (`mi.sephora.com`, `app.sephora.com`). The confirmed problems are a missing plain-text part, missing image alt text, a malformed `@media` block and some accessibility gaps. The compliance and authentication warnings may come from the AgentMail relay rather than the sender. The source is truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 36. It reported no broken links, but 75% of the links were not probed.
- The tracking pixels are first-party:
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1`
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…` (hidden: `display:none; mso-hide:all`)
- `mi_u=4958433775694848` is a per-recipient ID, and it is also appended to the content image URLs (`mi.sephora.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…`). The images therefore double as open and view trackers.
- Both tracking images are `display:none`, so they won't load in clients that block hidden images. That is expected, not a defect.
- No other link issues are confirmed from the available source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No plain-text part (QA FAIL, 0 chars).** Only the HTML part was captured. If the sender really omitted it, that hurts deliverability and text-only clients. It could also be a relay capture artifact.
- **Missing alt text on content images.** QA flagged `df9af91c42339c07.png`, `da0148084c4c66f6.png`, `20e4e51340afcef6.png`, `727459dc432025a7.png`, `731160b4c3175e23.png` and `8e8fdee6cedcdc03.png` (all `mi.sephora.com/p/rp/…`). If these carry the offer content, they are inaccessible and blank with images off.
- **False positives:** `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel. The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel is hidden and has no alt, which is harmless.
- **Malformed CSS in the `<style>` block.** The `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` comment sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block. In non-MSO parsers the comment markers become part of the selector prelude. That likely invalidates `img.mso-size` and the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, so the mobile sizing for the Rouge tier badge may not apply.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute**, and the layout tables have no `role="presentation"`. Both matter for screen readers.
- **Viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`**, which blocks pinch-zoom where the client honors it.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index">`** uses an invalid value (it should be `noindex`). It has no practical effect in email.
- **Fixed `min-width:700px` outer table.** Layout below 700px depends on client scaling, because only the 480px media query adjusts anything.
- **JSON-LD block:**
  - `"discountCode": ""` is empty.
  - `"@type": "PromotionCard"` is not a schema.org type.
  - Gmail will probably ignore the block, so the markup brings no benefit.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The preheader text, "Enjoy eight days of can't-miss beauty savings.", is static and rendered correctly.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw original message before treating it as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed from this capture. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated footer. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1`, on the tracking image URLs. That is Sephora's own campaign key.
- No destination URLs appear in the truncated source, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be confirmed. No mismatch was detected.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative (QA FAIL).
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six `rp/*.png` content images. Use `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden` for any that are purely decorative.
3. Move the `<!--[if mso]>…<![endif]-->` block out of the `@media` rule, or put it in its own conditional `<style>`. Then confirm `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` renders at 92×22 on mobile.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables. Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. This separates sender defects from relay stripping.
6. Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML and a larger probe budget. That would cover the footer unsubscribe and address, UTM parameters and CTA destinations.
