# Get a free Applebaby Keychain
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's second GWP (gift-with-purchase) mechanic in five days — a free "Applebaby" keychain instead of the 7/21 Mini Beauty Bag — bolted onto the same I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom lineup that's now run for roughly nine straight days across at least six emails (7/18, 7/19, 7/20, 7/21, 7/23 x2, and now 7/25). The creative execution is genuinely strong: the jean-pocket hero shot is cute and on-brand, the copy is tight, and the 20%-off-plus-free-gift stack is a real, concrete offer.
- The problem isn't this email in isolation — it's this email in sequence. A subscriber who's opened even two or three of the prior Balm Dotcom sends has already seen this product line from four other angles (flavor tour, influencer content series, GWP #1, "get the look"). Swapping the gift from a beauty bag to a keychain is a clever enough twist to not feel like a lazy repeat, but it's still the same core purchase ask for the ninth time.
- Verdict: well-executed single asset, weak as the latest entry in an over-extended campaign arc. This will perform fine on click-through for anyone who hasn't already bought in, but open rates are going to erode from fatigue, not creative quality.

## What's working

- The keychain-in-jean-pocket hero image is genuinely charming and gives the free gift its own visual identity, distinct from the 7/21 Beauty Bag shot.
- Copy is efficient: headline names the gift and the required purchase in one line, subhead states the 20% off mechanic clearly.
- The four-flavor grid (I ❤️ NY, Strawberry, Black Cherry, Mango) each carries its own image, name, scent/tint description, and "Shop now" button — good scannability.
- Layout renders cleanly with no visual bugs — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.
- Footer is minimal and non-cluttered (address, privacy/ToS, unsubscribe) with a playful smiley/hand illustration that keeps brand tone through the very end.

## What's weak

- Copy calls it "The Balm Dotcom Quintet" but the grid only shows four flavors — a subscriber who wants to know what's actually in the bundle has to click through to find the fifth.
- This is at least the sixth Balm Dotcom-adjacent send in nine days; the "Summer Suggestions" module is functionally the same flavor grid that's already appeared in the 7/20 flavor-tour email.
- "Shop now" is used five separate times (hero + all four grid tiles) with zero variation — a missed chance to differentiate CTA copy by flavor or by the gift mechanic.
- No visible deadline for the 20% off or the "limited edition" keychain — "limited" is asserted but not backed by a date, which undercuts urgency.
- No loyalty/member pricing surfaced, despite this clearly being a repeat-purchase play aimed at existing customers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill or radically vary the Balm Dotcom cadence.** This is the strongest single lever — nine days of near-identical product pushes will train subscribers to stop opening. Either pause the arc for a few days or pivot the next send to a genuinely different product category.
- 2. **Put a real deadline on the keychain.** "Limited edition" without an end date reads as marketing filler. Add "while supplies last" with a specific date, or a countdown if the ESP supports it.
- 3. **Fix the Quintet/four-tile mismatch.** Either show all five flavors in the grid or change the headline to match what's visually promised.
- 4. **Differentiate the CTA copy per tile** — e.g., "Shop I ❤️ NY," "Shop Strawberry" — instead of five identical "Shop now" buttons.
- 5. **Surface a member/loyalty angle** if any exists (points multiplier, early access) — this is a repeat-purchase-driven send and the current version doesn't reward existing customers any differently than new ones.

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off + a free keychain (today only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet Applebaby — free with any Quintet purchase, while it lasts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Five flavors, one 20%-off bundle, plus a keychain that's basically a mascot.`
