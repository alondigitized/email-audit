# Zero packaging. Double points.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a Lush Club loyalty/sustainability hybrid send — the pitch is clean and double-dutied: go naked (packaging-free products) and earn double Lush Club points on them, July 11–13. The hero visual is charming — an illustrated bath bomb and soap character next to a "double" badge — and the green palette reinforces the eco angle well. The offer is structurally sound: a specific incentive tied to a specific product category with a hard date window.
- Where it stumbles is execution: the "Download The App" CTA is the single action this email drives to, yet most established Lush subscribers are already in the app. The product grid underneath (Intergalactic, Honey I Washed the Kids, Seanik, Scrubee, Coconut Drench) does real work showcasing five recognizable hero SKUs, but there's no direct "shop now" link per product — just one generic "Shop Naked Products" button. The points mechanic is buried in copy that requires reading, not scanning.
- Compared to the July 9 hair editorial, which had a fully-developed editorial arc and clear category hierarchy, this send feels more transactional but slightly less polished in its hierarchy. The offer is genuinely good — double points on a beloved product category with a tight 3-day window — but the execution leaves some conversion on the table.

## What's working

- **Double-value hook is clear at a glance** — "More Points, Less Plastic" is a genuinely tight headline that earns both the loyalty and sustainability angles in four words.
- **Five named hero products** anchor the offer to real things you can buy, not abstract category language; Intergalactic in particular is a cult product that drives clicks on name alone.
- **Hard date window (July 11–13) is visible** — specific urgency without manufactured panic.
- **App download CTA has a distinct styled button** — stands out against the cream background.
- **"Join Lush Club" module at the bottom** reinforces the loyalty ladder for non-members without derailing the main offer.

## What's weak

- **"Download The App" is the primary CTA for an existing subscriber list** — anyone already in the Lush app (the loyalty program's obvious home) has nothing to do with this button.
- **No per-product CTA** — five featured SKUs but only one generic "Shop Naked Products" button; a subscriber who wants Scrubee specifically has friction.
- **Points mechanic requires reading body copy** — the "double points = more Lush Club Rewards" logic is explained in a small-font paragraph, not surfaced in a scannable callout.
- **"TOTEM_SQUARE" visible in the hero area** — this appears to be an unfilled or unresolved image/content token in the hero block, a visible render artifact.
- **Tiny legal/footer copy is extremely dense** — the fine print block takes up significant visual real estate and has no visual separation from the actual footer icons.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace "Download The App" with "Shop Double Points Ends Sunday"** — the CTA should close the loop on the offer, not push an app install. Subscribers who need the app already have it; new subscribers shouldn't be gated behind an install to redeem a time-sensitive offer.
- 2. **Fix the "TOTEM_SQUARE" placeholder** — this is the single most urgent fix; a broken/unfilled content block in the hero damages trust and looks unfinished.
- 3. **Add individual "Shop Now" micro-CTAs under each product** — or at minimum a "Tap to shop" link. Five products with one exit is a missed revenue capture.
- 4. **Surface the points mechanic as a visual callout** — a simple "2× POINTS · JULY 11–13 ONLY" badge or pull-quote would let skimmers grasp the offer without reading paragraphs.
- 5. **Trim the fine print block** or visually offset it — the dense gray wall of legal text at the bottom undercuts the otherwise clean editorial feel Lush has established across recent sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double points on naked products — 3 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your faves, zero packaging, 2× the points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn double Lush Club points on packaging-free products July 11–13.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop naked, earn more — double points through Sunday.`
