# Yes, even when you take it off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA back on the product-launch beat, this time for Overglaze (the lip gloss/tint line), following the same clean-editorial template as the 09-01 Motif + Gallery send. The hero shot — a tight close-up of glossy lips with the headline "The Glaze That Stays" — is genuinely well-composed and on-brand, and the "Inside the Formula" ingredient breakdown (Fenugreek Seed Extract, Seaweed Extract) with the dripping-serum product shot adds a dermatological credibility beat that ILIA's recent Thru Line sends haven't leaned on as much.
- Where it falls down is offer clarity. The top banner promises "FREE MINI EYE STYLUS W/ $75+*" but that offer is never mentioned again in the body — no CTA ties back to it, no reminder near the shop buttons — so it reads as bolted-on rather than integrated. There's also no discount or urgency anywhere in the actual product story; this is a pure product-education send dressed with a GWP ribbon, and the two are disconnected.
- Net: a strong visual/brand execution let down by a disjointed offer structure. Solid mid-tier send, consistent with the 6.00/10 average this brand has been running.

## What's working

- Hero image is striking and on-message — the lips close-up with "The Glaze That Stays" headline immediately signals the category and benefit.
- "Inside the Formula" section with named ingredients (Fenugreek Seed Extract, Seaweed Extract) gives concrete product substance rarely seen in the recent Thru Line GWP arc.
- Social proof stat ("100% said it locks in moisture + quenches dry, chapped lips") is bold and visible without needing to scroll far.
- Shade array photo (the fan of tubes) does double duty as texture proof and shade-range signal.
- Two clear CTAs ("NEW GLAZES →" and "SHOP OVERGLAZE →") bookend the content logically.

## What's weak

- The GWP offer in the top banner ("FREE MINI EYE STYLUS W/ $75+*") is never reinforced near either CTA — it's disconnected from the rest of the email.
- No specific price, discount, or urgency anywhere in the body — this is a pure brand/product story with no shopping incentive besides the orphaned GWP line.
- Subject "Yes, even when you take it off" is vague out of context; without the hero image, "it" is ambiguous (lipstick? makeup? clothing?).
- The stat callout's asterisk disclaimer ("*immediate results in a clinical study of...") is nearly illegible against the photo background — buried fine print.
- No shade names or price points shown, so the click has to do the work of surfacing basic purchase info.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Tie the GWP banner to a CTA.** Repeat "Free mini eye stylus w/ $75+" directly above or on the "SHOP OVERGLAZE" button — right now it's a top-of-email throwaway that never converts into purchase motivation.
- 2. **Add a price or bundle hint.** Even one line ("Overglaze, $26") near the product shots would give the reader a reason to click beyond curiosity.
- 3. **Clarify the subject line's referent.** "Even when you take it off" only works once you've seen the hero — sharpen it so the category is obvious from the inbox.
- 4. **Make the disclaimer legible.** Move the stat's asterisk text off the photo background onto a solid strip so it doesn't read as buried fine print.

- **Subject Alt B:** `24-hour hydration, zero smudge (new Overglaze)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Overglaze: 24-hour hydration + a free eye stylus with $75+`
