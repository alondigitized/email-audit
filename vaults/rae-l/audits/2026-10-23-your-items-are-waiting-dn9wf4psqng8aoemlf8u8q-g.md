# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email, sent under a new subject, "Your Items Are Waiting!". It is the second cart-style send in two days. The 10/22 "Did you forget something?" used the same structure. The hero is clean and the CTA is clear: "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US…", a single pink GO WALK Workout Walker, and a navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button. The cart framing is right for the trigger.
- The email undercuts itself in three ways. The members 20% off banner (valid 10/22–10/26) sits below a six-shoe grid and a black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar, so a shopper has to scroll past two other CTAs to reach the one real incentive. The hero shoe has no price, size or color, and no reassurance that the item is still in stock. The footer, with its nav, app, BNPL and social modules, is longer than the offer content above it.
- Overall it is a competent template send that wastes its best asset. The 20% discount should sit directly under the hero. It is also the same abandonment shell the brand has run on 10/17, 10/18, 10/19 and 10/22, so it reads as routine.

## What's working

- The hero is focused: one large product shot, a clear product name, and a high-contrast navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button.
- The "YOUR CART IS STILL WAITING…" line matches the trigger, and the casual headline fits the brand voice.
- The members 20% off banner is bold and legible, and adds a concrete $5 reward on $75+ with the validity window (thru 10/26).
- The recommendation grid shows six women's styles, and the product names are specific.
- Afterpay and Klarna are visible, which lowers the barrier to completing the purchase.

## What's weak

- The 20% off offer is buried under the grid and the black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar. The incentive that could recover the cart comes last.
- The hero shows no price, size, color or stock status. A cart reminder without those details gives little reason to act now.
- The bar is "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS", which competes with "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER", and the "SHOP THE SALE" link sits beside the discount. That is three or four CTAs in a recovery email.
- Product tiles under the grid are small, with tiny captions and no prices or reviews, so there is no social proof.
- The footer is heavy: a six-row nav, app and curbside modules, a text-signup prompt and dense legal copy. It dilutes the single-purpose cart message.
- The subject is generic, and a near-identical cart reminder already went out on 10/22.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the members 20% off banner directly under the hero and make the CTA "Complete Your Order — 20% Off". Lead the preheader with the same offer.
- 2. Add price, color, size and a "low stock" or "reserved for you" line under the hero product.
- 3. Cut the recommendation grid to three items, each with a price, or drop it. Remove "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" and the "SHOP ADDITIONAL STYLES" nav so the cart CTA has no competition.
- 4. Space abandonment sends so a shopper does not get two cart reminders in two days, and make the second one carry a stronger offer.
- 5. Trim the footer modules (app, curbside, texting) and keep the legal copy to what is needed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your GO WALK Workout Walker is still in your cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off your cart ends 10/26`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on your saved Slip-ins through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size is still in stock, but not for long. Finish checkout today.`

Several compliance and deliverability checks could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay did not capture headers.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually open the unsubscribe URL in a browser. If it fails there, fix it as a P0 compliance issue.
2. Re-probe the two 429 Attentive links with throttling and a browser user-agent. Follow the redirects to confirm the cart/checkout landing page and its UTM parameters.
3. Serve all images over `https://`. The image host already supports it.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two images that lack it, and correct "Complete you Order" to "Complete your Order" in both the alt and the link text.
5. Set a meaningful `<title>`.
6. Confirm the real send includes a multipart text/plain part. If it does, the 0-char result is a capture artifact. If not, add one.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC, from the raw message headers rather than through the relay.
8. Confirm the physical address is in the footer, which the truncated source didn't show.
