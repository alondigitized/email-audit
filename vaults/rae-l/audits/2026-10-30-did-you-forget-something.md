# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email, sent for the third time in a week under the same "Did you forget something?" subject. It was also sent on 10/22 and 10/29. The hero is a gold-flecked "READY TO CHECK OUT?" panel with a black "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button, and it is clean and unambiguous. The Halloween and Día de los Muertos flavor comes from the Uno "Alma De Guerrero" skull sneaker and the "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" banner. That makes it feel more seasonal than the generic versions.
- The structure undercuts itself, though. There is a large empty gap between the hero and the skull shoe, which reads like a layout hole. Below it is a second "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button in orange, with a different style from the black one above. The 20% Skechers Plus offer sits well down the page, after six product tiles and a "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar. It is the strongest reason to come back, and it is buried.
- Judgement: a competent reminder with a good seasonal hook, but the offer is hidden, the CTAs are inconsistent, and the cadence is tiring. A subscriber who has seen this subject three times this week will skim it.

## What's working

- The "READY TO CHECK OUT?" headline with the black "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is a clear, single-action hero.
- The skull-painted Uno sneaker is a distinctive, seasonal product shot that stands out from the usual white-background shoe.
- The navy "20% OFF FOR SKECHERS PLUS MEMBERS" banner is bold, has a clear "SHOP NOW" link, and gives a deadline ("Valid thru 11/2").
- The footer is well organized, with a text-to-join option, app badges, curbside pickup, Afterpay and Klarna.

## What's weak

- There is a large blank band between the hero panel and the skull shoe, so the product looks disconnected from the headline.
- The product shown is a men's-looking black sneaker with a skull, while the six tiles below are all women's styles. The cart item and the recommendations don't match.
- The two "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" buttons (black and orange) compete with each other and with the black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar.
- The 20% offer is buried below the fold, and the legal text is tiny. It also requires a Skechers Plus login, which non-members can't use.
- The product tiles have small, long captions and no prices or reviews, so there is nothing to push a click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Skechers Plus banner into the hero, directly under "READY TO CHECK OUT?". Make it the headline: "Your cart + 20% off through 11/2."
- 2. Close the gap between the hero and the cart product, and show price, size and color for the actual cart item.
- 3. Use one button style for "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" and drop the duplicate.
- 4. Replace the six generic best-sellers with items matched to the cart category, and add star ratings and prices.
- 5. Space out the cart-abandonment sends and vary the creative. Three sends of the same subject in nine days trains people to ignore it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your cart + 20% off, thru 11/2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? Your skull Uno's waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers Plus members: 20% off your cart ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Uno Alma De Guerrero is still in your cart. Check out before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from role="presentation" tables with a `lang="en"` root and a hidden preheader. The automated QA shows a 33% pass rate. The main problems are insecure `http://` image sources, a missing plain-text part, missing alt text, an empty `<title>`, and two probe failures (401 on the hero link, 400 on unsubscribe). Several of these may be probe or relay artifacts, as noted below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Hero image link returned 401.** The link is the `[image: 183890_BBK_HERO_LG]` wrapper at `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/R5rMkIh6otSE…`. Attentive tracking redirects often reject non-browser probes, so this is unconfirmed as a real break. It is the primary image CTA, so it should be re-tested from a real client.
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r…&externalId=ZvZRl`. A 400 on a token-bearing URL may be caused by the probe, such as a missing cookie or header, or by a single-use token. It cannot be confirmed from this data, but an unsubscribe link that really fails is a compliance risk (see section 5).
- **Two 429 responses are not defects.** They were on "Ready to check out? Complete your order" (`attentivemail.com/e/c/mTohrRqb…`) and the `de642685-…jpg` image link. These are rate limits and the links may be valid.

- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The From domain is `msgs.skechers.com`.
- **Physical postal address** is not visible in the truncated HTML, so it is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Destination URLs and UTM parameters are hidden behind Attentive redirect tokens. Landing-page alignment with the cart-recovery message could not be assessed from this data. The 401 on the hero link and the 429s on the checkout links leave the click-through path unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the unsubscribe link (`skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…`) and the hero link in a real client. Fix them if they still fail.
2. Switch all `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` image sources to `https://`.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the hero image and the two `.jpg` content images. Set `alt=""` on the open pixel.
4. Populate `<title>`.
5. Add a multipart text/plain part that includes the unsubscribe URL.
