# Final days for Buy 2, Get 1 FREE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth COLORfest send in eleven days, and it's essentially a rerun of 8/6's "Your Best Hair Color Starts Here" — same "Buy two, get one free select hair color" mechanic, same confetti-dot branding, same festive-model hero. The only twist is the subject line's urgency claim ("Final days") and a second BOGO tier added for Wella toners at the bottom. Execution is clean — real product shots, real SKUs, clear pricing mechanic — but the fatigue is starting to show: a subscriber who opened 8/6's email sees almost the same layout again, just with different tubes.
- The big miss is the urgency itself: "Final days" appears in the subject and nowhere else. There's no countdown, no date, no banner in the body reinforcing the deadline — so the one differentiator this send has over its 8/6 twin is invisible once you actually open it.
- Solid, on-brand, sellable email — but it's coasting on a repeated template rather than earning the urgency it's promising.

## What's working

- Clear, single mechanic stated twice in bold headline type: "Buy two, get one free select hair color" and again for Wella toners.
- Six real product tiles with brand names, shade names, and individual "Shop now" links — easy to browse and comparison-shop.
- Hero model and confetti-dot brand system are consistent with prior COLORfest sends, reinforcing campaign recognition.
- Utility bar up top (BOPIS, free 2-hour delivery) and Rewards/Klarna blocks at the bottom give secondary conversion paths without cluttering the hero.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Final days" but there's no date, countdown, or urgency banner anywhere in the body — the deadline is unverifiable once opened.
- This is a near-clone of the 8/6 "Your Best Hair Color Starts Here" layout; a recent opener will recognize the repeat rather than feel a new reason to act.
- The Wella toner BOGO is a completely separate mechanic bolted on after the main product grid with no header transition explaining why it's there or if it shares the same deadline.
- Hero copy ("Create your boldest color story yet") is generic campaign filler that doesn't reference the actual "final days" urgency the subject sets up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual deadline in the body — a banner like "Ends Sunday 8/16" directly under the hero headline — so the subject's urgency claim is substantiated and scannable.
- 2. Differentiate this from 8/6's send: swap the hero copy to lead with urgency ("Your last chance to stock up") instead of recycling generic "boldest color story" language.
- 3. Add a one-line transition before the Wella toner section (e.g., "Also ending soon: BOGO on root touch-up") so it reads as a bonus deal, not a layout accident.
- 4. Consider retiring the confetti-dot COLORfest wrapper for one cycle — four near-identical sends on the same visual system in under two weeks risks banner-blindness.
