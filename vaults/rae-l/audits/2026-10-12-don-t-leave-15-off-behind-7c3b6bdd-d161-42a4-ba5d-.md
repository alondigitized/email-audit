# Don’t leave 15% off behind
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer "last chance" send. The hero is a big pink circle on red, with "LAST CHANCE" and "15% off your next Buy Online, Pick Up in Store order." Under it are "No minimum purchase required. Ends today!" and a black "Shop now" button. The eye lands on the offer first, and the deadline is credible.
- The problems are everything around the hero. Two red strips above it say "Buy online, pick up in-store" and "Free 2 hour delivery," and a pink rewards bar says "Get $5 off $25 when you sign up." The rewards bar repeats the 10/11 "Get $5 off $25 when you join" send, so the signup ask is competing with the 15% offer. The four products below are small, sit in a lot of white space, and have no prices or reasons to buy. The subject "Don't leave 15% off behind" also reads like a cart-abandonment nudge, but this is a blast, and nothing in it is personal.
- Overall it's solid but generic. It does its one job, a time-boxed BOPIS discount, and doesn't make the product feel worth clicking. It's about on par with the 10/05 "20% off" send, which was also a single clear offer.

## What's working

- The hero has one number, "15% off," in large type, with "Ends today!" right beneath it.
- "No minimum purchase required" removes the usual objection.
- The black "Shop now" button contrasts well with the pale pink circle and is easy to find.
- The Klarna strip and the icon row (Free Shipping $40+, 2 Hour Delivery) reassure without crowding the hero.
- It's a single-offer layout after the 10/06 and 10/08 roundups, which had four offers and no headline number.

## What's weak

- Three stacked promos (the rewards signup, BOPIS and 2-hour delivery strips, and the hero) crowd the top before the hero starts. The $5 off $25 line, repeated from 10/11, dilutes the 15% message.
- The product grid is weak. The ion and L'Oréal items are small and have no prices, ratings, or "why this."
- The grid has no link to the offer. Nothing says these products are eligible for 15% off, and it looks like a random assortment of hair-color items.
- Every CTA is "Shop now," including the four product links, and none says what it leads to.
- "*Exclusions apply" under the button, plus dense legal copy at the bottom, hurts the feel of an honest offer.
- The subject's "leave behind" framing implies an abandoned cart that doesn't exist here, and it doesn't mention pickup, which is the actual condition.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer condition in the subject, and cut the competing top strips. Drop the rewards bar for this send, or move it to the footer.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific, such as "Get 15% off pickup," and add a countdown or "Ends tonight at midnight."
- 3. Rebuild the product grid with bigger images, prices, and a "15% off with pickup" tag on each item. Use bestsellers or items from the recipient's category instead of four unrelated hair items.
- 4. Give each product link a distinct CTA, such as "Buy & pick up," and show a nearest-store or "ready in 2 hours" line.
- 5. Use "Exclusions apply" as a link to the exclusion list, and move the dense legal copy lower or shorten it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off pickup orders ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 15% off, no minimum`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order online, pick up in store, and save 15% on anything. No minimum. Ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 15% off pickup order expires today. Ready in 2 hours at your local Sally.`

# Technical Audit: Sally Beauty, "Don't leave 15% off behind"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with MSO conditionals, and the QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The confirmed code-level problems are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a third-party pixel, an insecure `http://` pixel, and recipient email exposed in image URLs. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the pixel may be cached or may not register impressions.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990...` is an `<img>` over plain HTTP. Clients that block non-HTTPS images will drop it, and the request is unencrypted.
- **Unprobed links:** 50 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Pixel stack:** Four third-party pixels are in a single send: Evergage, mon-trk.com, MyVisualIQ and Dotomi. Several are measurement/retargeting pixels with no visible content role.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 7 images, all flagged by QA:**
  - Four Evergage dynamic images, `summary.png` at `.../campaign/tOkMy/Bax31/{1,2,3,4}/summary.png`. If these are visible content blocks, as the numbered slots suggest, they have no accessible text and show nothing with images off.
  - Three tracking pixels: `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`. Add `alt=""` to these, since they are decorative.
- **Fonts:** `a { font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif }` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible source. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless but unintended. The visible `@font-face` rules only declare Open Sans.
- **Positive:** The source includes `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, a viewport meta tag, a 499px mobile breakpoint, and MSO/VML namespaces. I found no rendering defects in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **PII in image URLs:** Every Evergage image URL carries `userId=rae-l@ete...` (truncated in the QA output). The recipient's email address appears in plaintext in a query string, so it is exposed to proxies, logs and any forwarded copy of the email.
- **Contact ID in pixel path:** The mon-trk pixel path ends in `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, which looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). It is a per-recipient identifier in the URL.
- No unresolved `{{ }}` or `%%` merge tokens were visible in the truncated source. The only unrendered placeholder found is the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message before treating it as a failure.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com`, an aligned subdomain, but alignment can't be proven from the data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated source doesn't show them, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 50 click-tracked links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing URLs are **not verifiable** from this data.
- The Evergage campaign path `sallybeauty_prod/campaign/tOkMy/Bax31` indicates dynamic content that depends on live personalization. The images' content at open time can't be checked against the "15% off" subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro substitution in the MyVisualIQ pixel, or drop the pixel.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or remove it if it isn't needed.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four Evergage `summary.png` images. Add `alt=""` to the three tracking pixels.
4. Stop passing the plaintext email in `userId=` and use an opaque hashed ID. Review whether the Salesforce Contact ID belongs in the pixel path.
5. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Re-run QA with headers captured directly, since the relay may have stripped them.
6. Resolve the click-tracking redirects to check UTM presence and landing-page alignment.
7. Add a `@font-face` for Founders Grotesk, or remove it from the stack.
