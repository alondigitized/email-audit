# These hot sets + you = ⚡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-set newsletter whose subject promises one thing and whose body delivers several. "These hot sets + you = ⚡" points at "Meet Must-Have Sets", but the loudest element is a giant magenta "The Healthy Hair & Scalp Kit" hero. Above it sit a Scent Mini freebie (code APPLELOVE, $40 purchase) and a "Savings Event" strip with a 30% off badge that says "Coming soon". The reader gets three offers before reaching any product.
- The eight-set grid is well built and consistently badged ("New • Limited Edition • Only at Sephora", Clean at Sephora seals). Prices and value comparisons are visible, for example Armani at $55 ($80 value). The grid is the real payload, but it sits below a long hero and a dense charity disclaimer. The lower modules (Korean haircare, Prada perfume, Haus Labs liner) split attention again.
- Judgement: competent execution, but unfocused. It is a product-catalog send with no single price-driving offer, and the "coming soon" tease signals that the real deal is later. It is about as good as the 10-13 makeup sets send, but weaker because it has more competing heroes.

## What's working

- The set grid shows a clear price and "$ value" for each product, which makes gift-set savings concrete.
- Consistent "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" labels and Clean at Sephora seals signal exclusivity and trust.
- The magenta hero has a strong, brand-consistent look, with one clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- The seasonal gift-set angle fits mid-October.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." modules break up the grid with category-specific CTAs.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete at the top: Scent Mini, the 30% Savings Event teaser and the hair kit. Only the Scent Mini has an actionable deadline.
- The "30% off" badge is labeled "Coming soon", which can make shoppers wait instead of buying.
- The hero is a hair and scalp kit, not the "hot sets" the subject promised, so the subject-to-hero match is weak.
- The charity disclaimer under the hero is a dense, small-type block that pushes the product grid further down.
- The tiny legal footer and the multiple exclusion notes ("Exclusions/terms apply" appears twice at the top) make the offers feel hedged.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the sets themselves. Lead with "Gift sets from $25" and the grid, and move the hair kit into the grid as one product.
- 2. Remove or demote the "Coming soon" savings strip until it is live. Keep one live offer above the fold.
- 3. Move the charity disclosure below the CTA or into the footer.
- 4. Cut the grid to six sets and add a "Shop all sets" button after it.
- 5. Add loyalty hooks tied to the Insider tier, for example "Members earn 2X on sets".
