# Cyber Week is really bringing it ON 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong deal-of-the-day email that undercuts itself by cramming too much in. The three stacked "30% OFF" purple sequin blocks (Kiehl's, Laura Mercier, Youth to the People) are the best part. The numerals are huge, each brand logo is clear, and each block has its own SAVE NOW button. A subscriber can see the offer in about two seconds.
- The top is the problem. A slim "CYBER WEEK IS HERE! Save up to 50%" banner sits over a "FREE* Sample Bag" module with a code, then a second "CYBER WEEK DEAL" hero. That's three competing headers before the real offer appears. After the brand blocks, the email goes on for roughly another 4,000 pixels. It adds a product grid, a Sol de Janeiro 25% block, a Korean beauty block, a gift guide and a footer of service lines. This is the fourth Cyber Week send in four days, after 11/21, 11/22 and 11/23's "Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️". The brand is shouting the same message with a different brand lineup each day.
- Verdict: a solid, on-theme send with a clear core offer, diluted by clutter and fatigue. It sits a bit below the 11/23 send.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF numerals are very large on a purple sequin background. The hierarchy in each block is obvious: number, brand logo, button.
- Each brand block has its own SAVE NOW button, so the click target is unambiguous.
- Kiehl's, Laura Mercier and Youth to the People are recognizable prestige names with product shots that match.
- "24 hours–only at Sephora" adds urgency, and the 11/30 end date appears on the secondary blocks.
- The purple Cyber Week styling matches the prior three sends, so the brand look is consistent.

## What's weak

- The top has three stacked headers: the banner, the Sample Bag module and the CYBER WEEK DEAL hero. The Sample Bag and its code compete with the 30% offers.
- The hero says "24 hours–only", but the banner and later blocks run through 11/30. The two deadlines contradict each other.
- The email is very long. The "Treat Yourself for Less" and "Chosen Just for You" grids, the gift guide and the footer dilute the 30% offers.
- Asterisks and daggers sit on almost every offer ("30% OFF†", "up to 50%*"), and the fine print is long. This weakens the sense of an honest offer.
- This is the fourth near-identical Cyber Week promo in four days. The "It's Good to Be You" headline is vague and gives no reason to act.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top to one header. Move the Sample Bag lower or drop it, and lead with the 30% blocks. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Pick one deadline and state it consistently. Either "today only" or "through 11/30", not both.
- 3. Cut everything after the third brand block, or replace it with one block. Two Cyber Week gift-guide sections and the product grids can go.
- 4. Replace "It's Good to Be You" with a concrete line such as "30% off Kiehl's, Laura Mercier and Youth to the People."
- 5. Vary the cadence. Four sends in four days needs a reason to open each one, such as a new brand lineup named in the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Kiehl's, Laura Mercier + more today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today's Cyber Week picks: 30% off Kiehl's 🤩`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free sample bag with code GRABMYBAG. Ends 11/30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Laura Mercier and Youth to the People are 30% off. In store and online.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper table is `width:700px; min-width:700px`. This forces horizontal scrolling or zoom-out on viewports narrower than 700px, even though `@media (max-width:480px)` rules exist.
- **Conditional comment in CSS:** Inside the `@media` block in `<style>` there is `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->`. Outside Outlook, a browser CSS parser will likely treat the comment markers as part of the selector and drop the invalid rules. The `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule that follows the `<![endif]-->` marker is likely dropped as well. This is inferred from the source and was not tested.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom in some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The valid value is `noindex`.
- **Document metadata:** The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute. `<title>` is the generic "Sephora" and doesn't match the subject.
- **Alt text:** The QA check flagged one image without alt, the hidden tracking pixel (`src: https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF...`). It is hidden, so the impact is low. Alt text on the visible images can't be assessed in the truncated source.
