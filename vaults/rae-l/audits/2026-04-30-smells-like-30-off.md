# Smells like 30% off
**Score:** 6.5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-04-30**
## Executive summary

- A fragrance-anchored sale email built around a 30%-off promotion. The creative leans into scent as personality ("Smells like 30% off") with a dual-image hero pairing a lifestyle shot with a product shot. The email structure is clear: hero → social proof → scrolling SALE banner → product grid → footer. The core offer is easy to understand and the visual identity is tight, but the email front-loads testimony before establishing the full offer, and the SALE ticker feels more decorative than directional. Solid promotional execution — not breakthrough.
- Communicates the offer clearly, brand stays on-character, but leaves conversion on the table with a cluttered mid-section and no strong urgency mechanism beyond the discount itself.

## What's working

- **Subject line plays well with brand voice.** "Smells like 30% off" is a Glossier-native pun — wry, confident, product-adjacent. It earns attention without overselling.
- **Visual identity is consistent.** Clean serif/sans-serif pairing, Glossier coral/red logo, and the two-panel hero feel deliberately curated rather than assembled.
- **Social proof placement is early.** Star ratings and pull quotes appear directly beneath the hero before the product grid, which is structurally sound for a fragrance category where word-of-mouth carries weight.
- **Product grid is legible.** Multiple SKUs visible with clear imagery; the grid suggests range without overwhelming.

## What's weak

- **"SALE SALE SALE SALE SALE" ticker is noise, not signal.** It repeats across the full width but doesn't anchor to any offer detail (% off, end date, category). It creates energy but no information. A customer who missed the hero still won't know what's on sale or for how long.
- **No urgency anchor.** No expiry date, countdown, or scarcity cue is visible anywhere in the render. "30% off" without a deadline is a discount, not a promotion with stakes.
- **The hero CTA ("Shop now") is undersized relative to the image real estate.** The two lifestyle/product images dominate but the action you're being asked to take is buried below them.
- **Testimonial quotes are too small to read at glance.** The star ratings and pull-quote text at this render scale are fine, but in a mobile inbox they may land below the fold before the reader has committed to scrolling.
- **Footer smiley face is charming but occupies disproportionate real estate** relative to the conversion path below it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a time boundary.** Insert "Ends Sunday" or a deadline callout into either the sub-headline or a second CTA module beneath the product grid. Even a soft deadline lifts urgency.
- 2. **Upgrade the SALE ticker to an offer ticker.** Replace "SALE SALE SALE" with alternating details: "30% OFF FRAGRANCES · ENDS MAY 4 · SHOP NOW ·" — same kinetic energy, actual information.
- 3. **Move the primary CTA higher.** The "Shop now" button should sit tighter to the offer headline, not below both images. On mobile the current layout buries the action.
- 4. **Increase testimonial font size or cut to one featured quote.** Two small-text testimonials compete with each other. One well-chosen pull quote, larger, with a product image beside it would outperform two shrunk-down reviews.
- 5. **Add a secondary CTA after the product grid** — "See all sale items" or "Shop the full offer" — so readers who browse the grid have an obvious exit ramp to PDP/collection.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Rollover/hover CSS** — The `.rollover:hover .rollover-first / .rollover-second` swap pattern is a progressive enhancement. It will silently fail in Outlook, Gmail (Android/web), and most mobile clients. Confirm the "first" state (non-hover image) is the primary content and the swap is purely cosmetic, not content-critical.

**`format-detection` meta only suppresses `telephone=no`** — Apple Mail will still auto-link dates, addresses, and emails. Full suppression requires:
```html
<meta name="format-detection" content="telephone=no, date=no, address=no, email=no">
```
