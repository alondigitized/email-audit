# Find your perfect finish
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's fourth consecutive email — and it's the most educational of the run. The finish-type framework (Natural Matte / Dewy / Natural) is a smart organizing device: it reduces decision paralysis, lets the subscriber self-select, and keeps three products on screen at once without feeling like a product dump. The casting and photography are doing real work — each model demonstrates the finish type, not just wears the product.
- The timing, though, is the problem. The Friends + Family 30% off offer debuted in the May 16 welcome email and has appeared in every ILIA send since. By day four, it's ambient wallpaper. Burying it below three full editorial sections makes it worse — a subscriber who's already decided not to buy hasn't changed their mind because they watched a finish explainer. The offer needed to be in the hero, with a deadline.
- The CTA is the clearest miss. "SHOP NOW" appears below a headline that asks "What kind of finish do you like?" — a question the email never answers with the click. If you're building a finish finder, the button should close the loop.

## What's working

- Three models with visibly different skin tones and finish results make the comparison concrete — this is the email's strongest asset
- Shade swatches visible per section pre-answer the "do they have my shade?" question without requiring a click
- Editorial photography is consistent and elevated throughout; no section feels cobbled in
- Clean vertical layout with clear section breaks keeps a long scroll from feeling chaotic
- "What kind of finish do you like?" as the editorial hook is a genuinely useful framing device, not just fluff copy

## What's weak

- The 30% off offer is below three full product sections — most readers won't scroll to it, and those who do have already lost momentum
- "SHOP NOW" as the hero CTA directly contradicts the finish-finder premise; three finish-specific buttons ("Shop Matte," "Shop Dewy," "Shop Natural") would convert better and feel coherent
- No deadline visible for the sale anywhere in the email — the urgency signal is completely absent
- Fourth consecutive day featuring the same Friends + Family promotion; the offer has lost its freshness, and this send provides no new reason to act

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lock the offer into the hero** — even a small badge or one line of hero copy ("30% off ends [day]") makes the sale impossible to miss regardless of scroll depth
- 2. **Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for finish-specific CTAs** — one per section: "Shop Natural Matte," "Shop Dewy," "Shop Natural" — this is the entire editorial premise; the CTA should honor it
- 3. **Show a deadline** — a visible end date on the F&F sale is the single highest-leverage change for urgency; without it, there's no reason to act today vs. tomorrow vs. never
- 4. **Cut the Shade Finder section or move it to a dedicated email** — it's a valuable tool but competes with the finish story and pushes the offer even further below the fold
- **Subject Alt A:** `Matte, dewy, or natural? Find your finish — 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The finish edit: 3 formulas, one sale (ends soon)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Friends + Family ends [day] — find your finish before it's gone`
