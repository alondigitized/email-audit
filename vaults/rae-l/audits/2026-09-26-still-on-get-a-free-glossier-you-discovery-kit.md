# Still on! Get a free Glossier You Discovery Kit
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same GWP send from 9/22 ("Try our fragrances for free!") re-run as a reminder/last-chance nudge — "Still on!" in the subject and "There's still time" as the hero headline both signal continuation, not a new offer. The layout is clean and on-brand: hero image of the four sample vials in the pink tray, a clear headline, one CTA, then a "Believe the Hype" social-proof section with three fragrances, star ratings, and real customer quotes. It's tighter than most Glossier sends in this history — no clutter, no fake urgency, one offer, one ask.
- The problem is this is a repeat, and the repeat doesn't earn its place. There's no countdown, no "ends [date]," nothing that tells me why I'm seeing this again three-plus days after the original — compare to 9/8's "Last call for 30% off," which used real deadline language to justify hitting the same offer twice. Here "Still on!" just reads as "we're recycling the same email." Solid execution, weak justification for a second send.

## What's working

- Single, unambiguous CTA ("Get yours") directly under a specific, quantified offer ($50+ order, free Discovery Kit).
- Hero image is appetizing and on-brand — product-forward, no lifestyle model needed for a beauty/fragrance GWP.
- "Believe the Hype" section adds real social proof (5-star ratings, named reviewers, specific quotes) which nothing in the 9/13–9/17 single-SKU sends had.
- Clean visual hierarchy: eye goes headline → CTA → hero image → social proof, no competing elements.
- No render bugs — text, images, and buttons all display correctly.

## What's weak

- This is a rerun of 9/22's exact offer with no new creative angle — "Still on!" doesn't tell me why I should act now versus when I first saw it.
- No expiration date or urgency signal despite this being framed as a reminder — "still time" implies a ticking clock that never shows a deadline.
- Preview text isn't visible/captured in this render, so inbox context is unclear, but if it repeats "free Discovery Kit" from the subject, it wastes the second line.
- Fine print ("*Terms apply.") is buried at the very bottom in small, low-contrast link text with no indication of what limits the $50+ offer (exclusions, one-per-customer, etc.).
- Fragrance images below the fold aren't linked to distinct offers — it's just "Shop now" three times, doing double duty as reminder + catalog without picking one job well.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the "still time" framing — e.g., "Ends Sunday at midnight" — turning this from an ambiguous rerun into legitimate urgency, per the 9/8 playbook that scored 7/10 for doing exactly this.
- 2. Move one customer quote or the "5-star" rating up near the hero to reinforce the offer before the scroll, not just in the catalog section.
- 3. Clarify the $50+ order terms inline near the CTA (e.g., "on fragrance orders" or "storewide") instead of relying on a buried asterisk.
- 4. Differentiate this send from 9/22 with a fresh hero crop or angle — same tray/vials shot risks feeling identical in a crowded inbox from the same brand.
- 5. Tighten the subject to lead with the deadline once one exists.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: free Discovery Kit ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free Glossier You kit is about to expire`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `4 minis, 1 tray, $0 — just spend $50 to unlock it`
