# Last chance—enter to win $2,000 of ILIA
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean "last chance" reminder for the ILIA shade-match sweepstakes. It's the third send in a week to push the $2,000 giveaway, after Nov 9 ("Gift ideas loading…") and Nov 10 ("One match. $2,000 of ILIA."). The hero is strong: a big "$2,000 of ILIA" headline over pink ILIA bags, with a "last chance" sticker that adds urgency. The black "GET SHADE MATCHED" button sits right below it.
- The second half is a distraction. The "Gift of Shimmer" block (free mini Eye Stylus Shadow Stick with a $75+ order) gets nearly as much space as the sweepstakes and has its own CTA. That's the same two-offer split as the Nov 10 send. The sweepstakes is a lead-gen play, and the free mini is a purchase play. Putting them side by side weakens the "last chance" message.
- The email is polished, on-brand and easy to read. It's also repetitive, and it never states the deadline. "Last chance" with no date or time is weak urgency. Judgement: a solid 6, held back by a vague deadline and a split focus.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "$2,000 of ILIA" in large type with the branded bags, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The "last chance" sticker adds urgency without shouting.
- The black "GET SHADE MATCHED →" button is high-contrast and specific.
- The free-mini offer has a clear threshold ($75+) and a named product (Eye Stylus Shadow Stick in Beam).
- The footer trust signals (free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, VIP) are tidy and credible.

## What's weak

- No deadline is shown. "Last chance" never says when. The Nov 13 survey send at least said "closes at midnight."
- Two offers with two CTAs compete. The eye close-up is larger than any element in the sweepstakes section.
- The sweepstakes fine print is only "*See site for details" and a small link at the bottom. The asterisk reads as buried.
- The copy "This is your last moment to match + win" is generic. It doesn't say what the quiz involves or how long it takes.
- The big pink "Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better" brand block adds height without adding anything to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a specific deadline to the hero and body, such as "Ends tonight at 11:59 PT". It would turn the vague "last chance" into real urgency.
- 2. Drop the free-mini block, or move it below a clear divider. Let the last-chance send do one job.
- 3. Say what the quiz involves: "2-minute quiz, 1 shade, 1 shot at $2,000."
- 4. Put the sweepstakes terms right under the button in plain text, not only in the footer.
- 5. Cut the brand tagline block to shorten the scroll.

- **Subject Alt B:** `2 minutes left to match + win`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Take the 2-minute shade quiz before midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One quiz, one shade, one chance at $2,000.`
