- Sally Beauty is back in hair territory — its third hair-focused summer send in eight days (June 3, June 5, now June 11). The BOGO 50% off mechanic is solid and the summer getaway hero image lands cleanly, but two competing offers fight each other at the top: a 10% sitewide code banner sits above the BOGO hero, forcing the reader to do mental math before committing to either. - BOGO 50% off is prominently headlined in the hero with a clear deadline: "Ends June 30" — no ambiguity about the offer.

- "Your summer getaway checklist starts here" is better framing than yet another "Survival Kit" — it adds a journey/lifestyle hook.
- Klarna installment option adds a purchase-lowering nudge, useful for higher-cart hair care items.
- No render bugs, no broken images — clean execution.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers (10% sitewide + BOGO 50%) in the same email with different end dates (June 14 vs. June 30) dilute focus and require mental math.
- The $15.99 Wella Lightener Bundle is a direct repeat from June 5 — no creative refresh, same price, same products — erodes editorial credibility.
- "Shop now" CTAs throughout are generic; none of the three buttons differentiates what you're shopping for.
- This is the third consecutive hair/summer send in eight days; cadence fatigue is real by this point.
- No loyalty/rewards angle is visible — a missed layering opportunity on a BOGO mechanic that already feels like it should reward members first.

- 3. **Make CTAs earn the click.** Replace "Shop now" with copy that pays off the hero: "Build Your Getaway Kit" or "Shop BOGO Hair Care."
- 4. **Add urgency to the subject.** The offer expires June 30 — that's useful, but it's buried in the body. Bring the deadline up.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Missing `@font-face` for primary font:**
The global `a {}` rule declares `font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif`, but there is no `@font-face` block loading `Founders Grotesk`. All anchor text will silently fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif in every client. Either add a self-hosted or CDN `@font-face` for Founders Grotesk, or remove it from the stack.

**Issue — Pinned stale Google Fonts URLs:**
All six `@font-face` declarations reference `fonts.gstatic.com` at version `v17` (e.g. `opensans/v17/mem8YaGs126MiZpBA-UFVZ0bf8pkAg.woff2`). Current Open Sans is v28+. Google continues serving old versions but these are subject to deprecation without notice; switch to version-agnostic CSS API URLs (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Open+Sans...`) or self-host.

**Issue — `white-space:pre` on `img` selector:**
```css
img { -ms-interpolation-mode:bicubic; white-space:pre; }
```
`white-space:pre` is a text property with no effect on replaced elements (`<img>`), but it can interact unexpectedly with inline image rendering in older Outlook versions. Remove it.

**Issue — Non-standard mobile breakpoint:**
Responsive block fires at `max-width:499px`. Industry standard is `600px`. Devices with viewport widths 500–600px (tablets, large phones in portrait) will receive the desktop layout without stacking — this includes iPad Mini and many Android phones. Change to `max-width:600px`.
