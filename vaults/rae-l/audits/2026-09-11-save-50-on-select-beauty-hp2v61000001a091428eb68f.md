# Save 50% on select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Stacking Weekend" 50%-off beauty send stripped down to its cleanest subject line yet — "Save 50% on select beauty ❤️" — but the render tells a different story than the words promise. The actual hero offer is narrower than it sounds: 50% off is scoped to just three brands (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro), "Today Only," with an "exclusions/terms apply" disclaimer sitting right under the CTA. That's honest-ish framing but a much smaller offer than "select beauty" implies.
- The bigger issue is what happens after the fold: the email pivots almost immediately into "Rewards Bazaar — September means new rewards," a long points-redemption catalog (100-point and 250-point tiers) that has nothing to do with the 50%-off hook. This is the same pattern flagged in the 2026-09-09 send ("Oooh it's 50% off select beauty" → actually a Hair Styling Awards email) — the subject sells one thing, the body is majority something else.
- Visually it's clean and on-brand, no render bugs, but structurally this is a grab-bag digest wearing a single-offer subject line. Judgement: functional but misleading in emphasis — the 50% offer deserves to be scoped honestly in the subject, and the rewards catalog should be clearly a secondary section, not the dominant bulk of the email.

## What's working

- Hero banner is crisp: bold "50% off Select Beauty" with a clean product shot and a red "TODAY ONLY" starburst creating urgency.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") is a clear, high-contrast button directly under the offer.
- "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last." is visible near the offer, which is more transparent than some past sends.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.
- Insider status ("You have 5 points") is shown at the very top, a nice personalization touch for a loyalty member.

## What's weak

- The 50% offer is only for three named brands (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro) — "select beauty" in the subject oversells the scope.
- The email spends far more vertical space (roughly two-thirds of the render) on the Rewards Bazaar points catalog than on the actual subject-line offer, which is a bait-and-switch in emphasis even if not in literal falsehood.
- "Today Only" urgency loses credibility given this brand has run near-identical 50%-off-beauty variants daily since 2026-08-24 (per brand history) — a subscriber has seen this exact framing repeatedly.
- Lower modules ("Try It Love It," "New from Prada," "Holiday Drops") pile on more competing CTAs, diluting focus rather than reinforcing the hero offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Either scope the subject to match the real offer ("50% off Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown & Azzaro") or widen the actual discounted assortment to match "select beauty" — right now the words oversell the picture.
- 2. Cut the Rewards Bazaar catalog to a single teaser tile with a "See all rewards" link instead of a full 100pt/250pt product grid — it's competing with, not supporting, the hero offer.
- 3. Drop or soften "TODAY ONLY" given the daily cadence of similar sends — repeated urgency this frequent trains subscribers to ignore it.
- 4. Move "Holiday Drops" and "New from Prada" into a separate send; a single-offer email converts better than a five-module digest.
