# Rae, ENDS TODAY! Shop what’s new with your 20% off⁺ one qualifying item coupon using code SCORE, including prestige & fragrance. Terms & excl. apply 🤑
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Ulta's third "SCORE" 20%-off-one-item send in the same promo cycle, and this time it's the last-chance push — "ENDS TODAY" stamped across the top and stuffed into a 150-character subject line. The execution itself is clean: recognizable Ulta pink/black branding, a barcode-styled coupon front and center, "Rae" personalization, and the same enormous scrolling catalog of ~30 products (skincare, fragrance, prestige makeup, tools) that Ulta ran on July 26.
- The hero does its job — 20% off, code SCORE, "Shop now" button, no scrolling required to see the offer. But this is a near-identical repeat of the July 26 "SCORE" send just six days later, and the subject line is bloated with fine print ("Terms & excl. apply") crammed in alongside the offer, which reads as noisy rather than urgent.
- Solid execution, weak differentiation from the last send — this is a reasonable last-chance nudge but not a standout, and the cadence is starting to feel repetitive to anyone who opened the July 26 email.

## What's working

- Hero offer (20% off, code SCORE) is visible instantly with no scroll — barcode treatment makes it feel like a real coupon.
- "Ends tonight" banner at the very top gives credible, immediate urgency.
- "Hi, Rae" personalization appears twice (subject line and the Rewards section near the footer).
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, prices and "with coupon" price tags display correctly across all ~30 product tiles.
- Ulta Rewards membership section reinforces loyalty value with a clear "Join now" CTA.

## What's weak

- The subject line is 150 characters — three times the mobile-friendly guideline — and buries the actual offer under repeated legal language ("Terms & excl. apply" appears mid-subject).
- This is the same "SCORE" mechanic and near-identical layout to the 2026-07-26 send just six days prior — a subscriber who opened that one will feel this is a rerun, not a new deal.
- Primary CTA copy is generic ("Shop now") rather than pointing to a specific category or product, despite the subject explicitly calling out "prestige & fragrance."
- Hero links to a general shop, not a curated prestige/fragrance landing page — a missed opportunity given that's what the subject promises.
- Heavy fine-print signaling (⁺, **, "excl. apply" repeated) undercuts the "one simple 20% off" pitch with implied exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the subject to the urgency + offer only, and move the legal language out entirely — it belongs in the footer, not the subject line.
- 2. Make the hero CTA match the subject's promise — link "Shop now" to a prestige & fragrance landing page instead of the general store, since that's the specific hook used to earn the open.
- 3. Space out "SCORE" sends — running the same single-item coupon mechanic twice within a week risks fatigue; alternate with a different offer type or skip a cycle.
- 4. Add a preview/preheader that's distinct from the subject and states what's new since July 26 (e.g., "still not used it? here's your last shot"), since none is currently visible/captured.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rae, your 20% off ends tonight — code SCORE`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call, Rae: 20% off one item (prestige & fragrance included)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your SCORE coupon expires at midnight — don't let it go to waste.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New prestige drops just landed. 20% off is still yours today.`

# Technical Audit: Ulta Beauty — "20% off⁺ SCORE" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders on a legacy XHTML 1.0 Strict / MSO-conditional template with mobile media queries, but ships with a broken name-merge field, no verifiable unsubscribe/authentication headers, and multiple insecure (HTTP) tracking pixels — automated QA recorded a 43% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 10 tracking/redirect pixels load over plain HTTP instead of HTTPS: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (all sharing param `afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`). Non-HTTPS resources are frequently blocked by mail clients (mixed-content), which would silently drop this tracking/attribution data.
- One additional HTTP-only asset: `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7...` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud dynamic content/tracking beacon) — same mixed-content risk.
- 60 additional tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (by design, not a confirmed failure) — destination validity is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 24 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` attributes, including primary content images (e.g. `08964789-...png`, `414d3e76-...jpg`, `d46f473b-...jpg`) and 1x1 tracking pixels (`mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png`, `dotomi` pixels). Screen readers will announce nothing or fall back to filenames/URLs for these.
- Document uses the deprecated XHTML 1.0 Strict DTD with heavy MSO conditional comments — standard/expected for legacy ESP (looks like Salesforce Marketing Cloud/ExactTarget) templates, not itself a defect.
- `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` meta tags and mobile media queries (`@media max-width: 480px`) are present, indicating dark-mode and responsive handling was considered — no issues found here.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed defect:** Greeting merge field renders empty — QA flagged `"Hi ,"` with no name inserted, indicating a failed/unmapped personalization token in the body.
- Note: the subject line personalization did resolve correctly ("Rae, ENDS TODAY!"), so the failure is isolated to the body greeting token/data-binding, not the personalization pipeline as a whole.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not present in the captured headers — may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not present — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not present — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
- Recommend re-checking these three items against raw SMTP headers (not the relay-processed copy) before treating as confirmed failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs were visible in the truncated HTML source provided; link destinations were not resolvable (60 tracking links skipped by QA). Cannot assess campaign-tracking or landing-page alignment with the data available — flagging as a gap in this audit rather than a confirmed issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the body greeting merge token so it degrades gracefully (e.g., "Hi there," fallback) when the name field is null — currently emits literal `"Hi ,"`.
2. Migrate the 10 `dotomi.com` tracking pixels and the `cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx` beacon from `http://` to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-enforcing mail clients.
3. Add `alt` text to content images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for pure tracking pixels, but content images need descriptive text for accessibility).
4. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` against raw source headers (not the AgentMail-relayed copy) to confirm true compliance status rather than a capture artifact.
5. Obtain full (non-truncated) HTML and resolved tracking-link destinations to complete UTM/landing-page continuity checks.
