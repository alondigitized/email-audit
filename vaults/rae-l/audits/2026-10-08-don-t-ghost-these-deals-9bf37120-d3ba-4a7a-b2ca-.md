# Don't ghost these deals 👻
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Big Deals" roundup with a Halloween coat of paint, and the pieces don't connect. The subject is a ghost pun. The hero says "No tricks here—just savings." and shows a dramatic orange-and-black nail photo, but it gives no number. The real offers sit below the fold in a four-tile grid. It's the same pattern as the 10/06 "Your biggest savings start now" send: several offers, no headline.
- The offer grid is where the email works. Each tile has a product photo, a specific price or deal, and its own "Shop now" link. Those are BOGO 50% off textured hair care, $50 off the Luxe 8-In-1 Airstyler Pro, $12 OPI GELements and a $21.99 bondbar bundle. Compare that with the clean 10/05 "Your beauty favorites, 20% off 👏" send, which had one big sitewide number and one button. This one makes the reader scroll and compare.
- Judgement: competent but diffuse. The hero image is for nails, while the offers are hair care, a hot tool, gel polish and hair color. The hero should carry the strongest offer. Right now it carries mood.

## What's working

- The offer tiles have real prices and real products, such as "$50 off Luxe 8-In-1 Airstyler Pro" and "$21.99 bondbar hair color bundle". They are scannable and clearly linked.
- The alternating image-left and image-right layout keeps the grid readable, and the tiles have clean spacing.
- The Halloween nail hero ties to the "ghost" subject and the fall season, and it reads as current.
- The Sally Rewards bar ("Get $5 off $25 when you sign up") is a visible loyalty hook near the top.
- Nothing is broken. Images load, text is clean, and the footer is tidy, with a Klarna module and service icons.

## What's weak

- The hero has no number. "Save on everything your fall routine is craving" is soft, and the "Shop now" button under it goes nowhere specific.
- Three stacked banners (Rewards, "Beauty in a dash", "Free 2 hour delivery") push the hero down. The red bars compete with it.
- The hero image shows nails, but none of the four offers is a nail product other than OPI polish at the bottom, so the image doesn't preview the deals.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop now", and there's no deadline in the visible body. The 10/31/2026 expiry is buried in the fine print.
- The cadence feels repetitive. It follows the 10/05 sitewide 20% send and the 10/06 roundup, so subscribers see a deal email nearly every day. The subject doesn't say what's new.

- 1. Put the single best offer in the hero as a number, for example "BOGO 50% off textured hair care" or "$50 off the Airstyler Pro", and keep the Halloween imagery behind it.
- 2. Make the hero image match the lead offer. Use the Mielle or Airstyler product shot, not nails.
- 3. Collapse the three top banners into one. Keep the Rewards line and move the delivery messaging to the footer, where it already appears.
- 4. Replace "Shop now" with specific CTAs like "Get BOGO hair care", "Shop the Airstyler", "Shop $12 OPI" and "Get the bundle".
- 5. Add a visible deadline near the hero, such as "Ends 10/31", or a "this weekend only" window if one is real.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML namespaces, Outlook conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 499px. It carries several third-party ad-tech pixels, two of which are broken (an unresolved macro and an empty-attribute image). The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks (links, alt text, footer, preheader) are limited to what the QA scan reported.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder. The ad server never substituted it, so the request is cacheable and impression counts may be undercounted or deduplicated.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id...` is served over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy may upgrade it, but other clients can block it or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Many third-party trackers:** The email loads pixels from `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net` and `login.dotomi.com` on top of the ESP click wrapper. That is three extra third-party hosts, which adds privacy and deliverability exposure.
- **Click links not verified:** 52 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unknown, not confirmed broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty-alt-text image with an empty `src`:** QA flagged an image with an empty `src` and no alt. An empty `src` can trigger a broken-image icon or a re-request of the email document in some clients.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (`pxl.mon-trk.com`), `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` have no alt attribute. For 1x1 tracking pixels, add `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them. Without it, some readers announce the file name or URL.
- **Visible head markup:** The `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` tag is not self-closed inside an XHTML doctype. This is minor and unlikely to break anything.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless but unintended.
- Only the head and the start of the CSS were visible, so body-level issues such as image dimensions, `role="presentation"` and content alt text can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only unresolved token visible is `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the VisualIQ pixel URL (see section 2).
- No other unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were reported by QA or seen in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed** and shouldn't be treated as a sender defect. If they really are absent, the email lacks one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be verified here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing destinations could not be evaluated. All 52 links use click-redirect wrappers that were skipped, and the destination URLs are not visible in the truncated source.
- The `cli_promo_id` parameter on the Dotomi pixel and the `ao`/`ago` IDs on the VisualIQ pixel suggest campaign tagging is handled server-side, but that can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro substitution in the `t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel` URL so the cache-buster is populated per send.
2. Change the Dotomi pixel `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` to HTTPS, or remove it if the vendor doesn't support HTTPS.
3. Remove the image with the empty `src`, or give it a valid source.
4. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels and meaningful alt text to any content images.
5. Re-run the QA against raw `.eml` headers, bypassing the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Resolve a sample of the 52 redirect links to confirm final destinations and UTM consistency.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, preheader, body images and link inventory.
