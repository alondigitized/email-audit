# Beauty deals are even better with Sally Rewards
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-acquisition email with a clear, concrete hook. "Finish your wish lists & get $5 off $25" is a specific offer, and the black "Sign up now" button sits directly under it. The hero is easy to read. The coral palette, large headline and single button make the eye land on the offer first.
- The weak spot is the offer itself. $5 off $25 is a modest incentive, and the fine print makes it narrower. It is for new Rewards accounts only and excludes tax, gift cards and shipping. It expires 10/31 and has to be used within 30 days. Compared with the BOGO 50% and 25% sitewide-code sends from 9/24 and 9/30, this reads as a membership pitch, not a deal. After a week of urgent deal emails, an evergreen "join our program" send with a month-long window feels low-urgency, much like the 10/1 "Spooky season savings" send.
- The lower half is a clean Preferred vs. Elite tier comparison, which is useful for someone deciding whether to join. But it adds length and a second button that repeats the first. Overall it is competent and honest but only mildly compelling: a mid-pack send for anyone already enrolled, and a reasonable one for non-members.

## What's working

- The headline names a specific offer, "$5 off $25", with the Sally Rewards branding above it.
- The black "Sign up now" button contrasts strongly with the coral background and is unambiguous.
- The lifestyle photo (a hand with red nails, a gift and a press-on nail box) fits the gifting and wish-list theme.
- The Preferred vs. Elite comparison is scannable. Icons and short labels make the free tier and the $200+ tier easy to tell apart.
- The footer utility row (Klarna, free shipping at $40+, 2-hour delivery, app download) adds reassurance without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The offer is small, and the fine print restricts it: new accounts only, no tax, gift cards or shipping, and use within 30 days. It is clear in the footer but not near the hero.
- The hero photo takes a large share of the screen and shows no hair or beauty product the offer applies to. The nail box is barely legible.
- The two red banners ("Beauty in a dash", "Free 2 hour delivery") compete with the main offer at the top, and each carries its own CTA.
- The "Join Rewards" button repeats "Sign up now" with different wording. Two labels for one action is a small inconsistency.
- There is no urgency. October-long validity gives no reason to act today, which is weak after the 9/30 "deals end today" send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer's key limits next to the headline, for example "New members: $5 off $25+, valid through 10/31." This fixes the honesty gap at the point of decision.
- 2. Cut or shrink the hero photo and show products that make the $5 useful, such as a few top-selling hair care items.
- 3. Remove the two promotional banners above the hero, or move them below it, so one offer leads.
- 4. Use one consistent CTA label on both buttons, such as "Join free & get $5 off".
- 5. Add an urgency device, such as a countdown or "Use within 30 days of signup," near the top.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Join free, get $5 off your $25 order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $5 reward is waiting, Sally Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free to join. $5 off $25+ plus points on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New members get $5 off at checkout through Oct 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with Outlook VML namespaces, a mobile breakpoint at 499px, and 47 click-tracked links. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). Several third-party tracking pixels have defects: a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` token, an `http://` source, and missing `alt` attributes. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 47 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Link validity, redirect chains and destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Unresolved macro:** The pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` carries a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder. It was not substituted at send time. The URL is identical for every recipient, so caches and proxies can serve the pixel without registering the impression, which undercounts ad impressions.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop it, and the Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-sync/UCM call will silently fail.
- **Third-party pixels:** The email loads `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net` and `login.dotomi.com`. These add three external dependencies and expose recipient identifiers to three vendors. This is not a defect, but each pixel is a failure point.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes:** The QA check flagged four images:
  - an image with an empty `src` and no name
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2321108/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`
  - the `myvisualiq` pixel
  - the `dotomi` pixel
- **Empty-`src` image:** The first image is a real defect. It can render as a broken-image icon in some clients and is announced by screen readers. The three pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly so assistive tech skips them.
- **XHTML validity:** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, but `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed. This is a minor validity issue and is unlikely to affect rendering.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible source. It falls back to Helvetica. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm the font is missing from the full document.
- **Truncated source:** The remaining markup (dark-mode handling, VML buttons, image dimensions, preheader) was not available to audit.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only unresolved token in the visible source and QA data is `[CACHEBUSTER]` (see section 2).
- No other raw merge syntax (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) was flagged. The visible source is truncated, so this isn't conclusive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Neither header was found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, and it is a possible Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** The header was absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.sallybeauty.com` are unverified. The cause is likely relay behavior rather than a sender defect.
- **Footer unsubscribe and address:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not in the truncated source or QA output, so they are not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be evaluated. All 47 destination links were skipped and the visible source does not expose final URLs.
- Click-redirect wrapping means UTM presence can only be checked after resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the empty-`src` image, or remove it.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and meaningful `alt` text to any content images.
3. Have the sender fix the `r=[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the MyVisualIQ pixel so it is populated per send.
4. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or confirm the vendor supports it.
5. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw `.eml` from the original send, not the AgentMail-relayed copy. This will show whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are really missing.
6. Resolve the 47 redirect links with a redirect-following probe, and record the final URLs and UTM parameters.
7. Capture the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, the preheader and the image `width`/`height` attributes can be audited.
