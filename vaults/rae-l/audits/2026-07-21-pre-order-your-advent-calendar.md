# Pre-order your Advent calendar
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Lush is selling Christmas in July, and they know it. The self-aware lede — "Look, we know it's summer. But when there are 25 days of fresh, handmade gifts..." — is the right call; it disarms the absurdity instead of pretending it isn't there. The colorful illustrated Advent calendar hero is visually loud enough to stop a thumb in a sea of August newsletters, and the "Pre-Order Now" CTA lands cleanly below it. For a subscriber who knows Lush, this email mostly works.
- The weakness is the missing price anchor. There's no "starting at $XX" or "XX value" anywhere in the visible email body. For a high-consideration seasonal item that competes with every other brand's Advent calendar, asking someone to hit "Pre-Order Now" without knowing the number is friction that will cost conversions. The app banner's "$5 off" is generic — it's not tied to the calendar and reads more like a retention promo that wandered into the wrong email.
- Notably, this is Lush's second holiday push in five days — a Halloween send landed July 16. Back-to-back holiday emails in summer is an ambitious cadence, and it works only if each one has genuinely distinct creative. The Advent calendar's illustration-heavy execution is different enough from the "Ghost the Grind" green-slime visual to not feel repetitive, but it's worth watching.

## What's working

- **Self-aware copy** — "Look, we know it's summer" converts what could feel like a calendar error into a tongue-in-cheek confidence play.
- **Hero image earns attention** — The illustrated Advent calendar is colorful, festive, and visually distinct from Lush's recent editorial photography (scalp/hair, honey, fresh).
- **CTA is unambiguous** — "Pre-Order Now" is a clean verb + noun; no waffling.
- **Product inventory in the copy** — "fizzy bath bombs, groundbreaking shower gels, buttery-smooth body care" tells you what's inside without opening the box.
- **Lush Club visibility** — The loyalty module surfaces earn-points messaging; good for members who comparison-shop Advent calendars by total value.

## What's weak

- **No price visible** — The single most important missing element for a pre-order email. "How much?" is the first question every reader has.
- **No deadline or scarcity signal** — "Pre-order" implies limits exist, but nothing says when it closes or how many are available.
- **Lush Club module is undersized** — The Lush Club section runs small and text-heavy below the fold; it should be surfacing member-exclusive early access or bonus points on this specific purchase if that exists.
- **"$5 off with the Lush app" banner is dilutive** — It's sitting above a premium seasonal pre-order and makes the email feel like two different marketing messages competing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add the price (or value) to the hero** — "25 handmade gifts, $XX" or "$XXX+ value, $XX" needs to be visible at the CTA. No price = no click for an unknown calendar.
- 2. **Add scarcity/deadline** — "Pre-orders close August X" or "First 500 get free shipping" — any specific signal that makes "Pre-Order Now" feel urgent rather than optional.
- 3. **Elevate the Lush Club hook** — If members get early access or double points on this purchase, that should be the third sentence in the hero copy, not a footnote below the fold.
- 4. **Remove or relocate the $5 app banner** — It competes with the hero offer and undercuts the premium seasonal framing. Move to the footer or drop it for this send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Lush Advent calendar is open for pre-order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25 days of Lush drops — pre-order opens now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fizzy bombs, exclusive collabs, and more — from $XX. Pre-orders close [date].`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get first access before it sells out — 25 handmade gifts, one calendar.`
