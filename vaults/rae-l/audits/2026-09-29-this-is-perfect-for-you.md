# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the browse-abandonment shell Skechers keeps sending, with a real discount attached this time. The hero is a burgundy floral Slip-ins Relaxed Fit "Easy Going - Floral Moment", followed by a six-shoe grid. Below that is a 15% off for guests / 20% off for members banner, "Thru 10/1". It renders cleanly, the hierarchy is clear, and the fall boots and suede fit the season.
- The weakness is the same as in the 2026-08-10 and 2026-08-18 sends of "This is perfect for you!". The subject makes a claim the email doesn't support. Nothing in the render personalizes the promise beyond one hero shoe, and the offer sits about two-thirds of the way down. The "Take Another Look" button gives no reason to click now. The fine print says "20% off boots & outdoor styles – online only". That contradicts the big blanket "20% for members" banner, which is a bait-and-switch risk.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand template, but a recycled subject, a buried offer and a hedged discount keep it mid-pack.

## What's working

- The headline "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT?" is large and legible, and the hero shoe is clean and centered.
- The 15% guests / 20% members split gives a clear reason to be logged in.
- The dark navy banner is the strongest visual block, and "Thru 10/1" adds a real deadline.
- The boots, suede and burgundy match the fall season.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. Secondary navigation (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The subject line, "This is perfect for you!", is a repeat. It has run on 2026-07-15, 2026-07-28, 2026-08-10 and 2026-08-18.
- The offer banner sits below the six-product grid, so the hook is hidden below the fold.
- The banner says "20% for members", but the footer restricts it to boots and outdoor styles, online only. The hero slip-on isn't clearly a boot.
- "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" is a vague CTA with no price, benefit or urgency.
- The six recommendation thumbnails are small and their captions are tiny. They are a mix of styles (boots, loafer, sneaker) with no clear logic, so "perfect for you" isn't backed up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 15% / 20% banner directly under the hero shoe, and say which products qualify ("20% off boots for members").
- 2. Change the CTA to something specific, such as "Shop the Floral Moment – 20% Off".
- 3. Cut the grid to three items that are actually related to the hero, and enlarge them.
- 4. Add urgency near the top, such as "Ends 10/1", with a countdown or "size run selling through".
- 5. Stop reusing this subject. Rotate copy that names the product or the deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Floral Slip-ins: 20% off for members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still eyeing these boots? 20% off ends 10/1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on boots through 10/1. Your pair is still in stock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Guests get 15%, members get 20%. Take another look before Oct 1.`
