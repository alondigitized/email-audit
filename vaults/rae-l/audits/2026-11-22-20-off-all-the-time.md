# 20% off—all. the. time.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a subscription-led Black Friday send. The hero is "20% Off now + forever" in huge maroon serif over a Base Face Milk bottle, and the "Subscribe + Save Forever" button follows right under it. The offer is a recurring 20% for subscribers, and the hero reads as a flat 20% off. The body copy and the "*" show the catch (the discount only applies "when you start a subscription"). A subscriber who has seen the earlier sends will notice this.
- It also contradicts the top banner, which says "Unlock 25% off sitewide* | Join VIP for free." That is the same mixed-number problem as the Nov 19 launch, where the banner and hero disagreed on 25% versus 20%. The email then spends its middle on five product tiles that all say "Shop Now" and show no prices, so the 20% never attaches to a specific product. The free-gift block at the bottom (complexion stick with any order, mini of choice with $100+) is a good closer, but it is buried.
- Overall it looks good and the hero is strong, but the message is muddy. It sits at a 6, a little below the recent 7s, because this is the fifth or sixth Black Friday send in five days and it adds nothing new except the subscription angle.

## What's working

- The hero is bold. The "20% Off" headline over the product pulls the eye straight to the offer.
- The full-width maroon "Subscribe + Save Forever" button is unmissable and sits right under the hero.
- The skincare grid zigzags cleanly, with a short benefit line under each product (SPF 50, barrier-building, firm + smooth).
- The "the free gift edit" block shows the complexion stick and the mini of choice with a "free" tag, which adds value without clutter.
- The trust footer (free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, VIP, Auto-Replenish) is tidy.

## What's weak

- The top banner says 25% sitewide, the hero says 20%, and the body says 20% only with a subscription. Three versions of the offer are visible.
- The headline "20% Off" hides the subscription condition, which is only stated in the small copy below it. That feels like fine print.
- Every product CTA reads "Shop Now," and no tile shows a price, a sale price or a review. Nothing makes me choose one product over another.
- The hero image cuts through the "O" in "Off" with the bottle, which hurts legibility a little, though the text is still readable.
- It is another Black Friday send after "The Black Friday roundup" (Nov 20), "Our very best—very on sale" (Nov 20) and the Nov 21 category and shade sends. The cadence is heavy and the creative is a recycle of the same hero style.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer hierarchy. Pick one headline number, and put the subscription condition in the hero, for example "20% off every refill." Move the VIP 25% to its own block, so the banner and the hero stop competing.
- 2. Put prices in the product tiles, with a was/now price and a star rating, and make the CTAs specific ("Shop Face Milk," "Shop SPF 50"). Swap one of the five tiles for the hero bestseller.
- 3. Move the free-gift block higher, above or right after the hero. It is the clearest "extra" in the email and currently sits at the bottom.
- 4. Add a deadline, such as "ends Cyber Monday," to give credible urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Subscribe once, save 20% every order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your skincare staples, 20% off for good`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Start a subscription in the Black Friday Sale and the discount stays on every refill.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free complexion stick with any order, and a free mini on $100+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-built HTML is structurally sound, with no QA errors and no broken links reported. Three warnings remain: two missing `alt` attributes and unverifiable unsubscribe and authentication headers. The source I received was truncated, so body-level checks (footer, UTMs, merge tokens) are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were flagged. 12 material links were probed and 13 were skipped. The skipped ones are footer, utility and social links, which are deprioritized by design.
- Click and open tracking runs through the brand-owned domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`. The open pixel is `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M81ZQY85EV5M4JYBW6J9EZS1`. This is normal.
- The 13 skipped links were not verified, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. This is a content image, so screen readers get nothing and image-blocking clients show a blank slot. It needs descriptive alt text.
  - The open-tracking pixel at `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/...`. It should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so it is treated as decorative.
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title> </title>`. This is minor, but a few clients and web-view versions use it.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** the email imports `static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css` and the Google Fonts Montserrat stylesheet. Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip `@import`. The stack `"Montserrat","Helvetica",sans-serif` falls back acceptably, so this is low severity. Headings are hard-coded to `Arial`, which is inconsistent with the body font stack but harmless.
- Outlook handling is present (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`, `mso-table-lspace`). The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`). No issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder was truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact. This is unconfirmed, not a proven sender defect. Check the raw message headers from an original delivery before treating it as a finding. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders of this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay stripping it, so it can't be assessed from this capture.
- The footer (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) fell outside the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but its result isn't shown. I can't confirm the CAN-SPAM footer elements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. No destination URLs or query strings are visible in the truncated source, and the QA output reports no landing-page results. Links appear to be wrapped by Klaviyo's tracking domain, so UTMs would be applied at redirect time. A resolved-URL capture would be needed to verify them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the `c9cefca7-…jpeg` image and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`.
3. Re-run header QA on a direct-delivery copy, not the AgentMail relay. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment).
4. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and the 13 skipped links. Follow the redirects to verify UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
5. Optional: put the Montserrat font stack ahead of a closer-matching web-safe fallback, since `@import` fonts won't load in Gmail.
