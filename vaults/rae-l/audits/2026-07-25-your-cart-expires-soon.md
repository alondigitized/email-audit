# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- The subject line promises urgency — "Your cart expires soon!" — but the email that opens doesn't deliver on it. The hero reads "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WORNG," which is soft browse-nudge copy, not cart-expiration copy. There's no countdown, no "X hours left," no expiring-cart messaging anywhere in the body. Anyone who opened because the subject said their cart was about to vanish will scroll through three product recommendations, a bestsellers grid, and a Plus-member discount banner before finding a "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button — the only nod to the subject's premise.
- Production quality is otherwise solid: clean product photography, a legible CTA, and a clearly stated 25% off Skechers Plus offer with real dates (7/22–7/27) and a code. But per brand history, this is yet another cart/browse-abandonment send in a month that's already delivered "Your Items Are Waiting!" twice (7/16, 7/22) and "Did you forget something?" three times. The creative structure — hero products, bestsellers grid, member discount banner, footer nav — is now recognizably templated across sends.
- Net: good craft, weak subject-to-body coherence, and cadence fatigue setting in. This lands mid-pack, held back mainly by the disconnect between what the subject promises and what the email actually shows.

## What's working

- Clear, unambiguous primary CTA — "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" is a specific, high-contrast button.
- The 25% off Skechers Plus offer is stated with real terms: dates, code (SAVINGS), and threshold ($100+).
- No render bugs — images, spacing, and typography all render cleanly.
- Cross-sell block (bestsellers grid) gives a second path to purchase if the hero products don't land.
- Footer nav (categories, app download, store locator, Afterpay/Klarna) is complete and functional-looking.

## What's weak

- Subject promises expiring-cart urgency; the body delivers zero urgency signals — no timer, no "hours left," no visual cue that anything is about to expire.
- Hero copy ("YOU LIKED THESE") reads like browse-abandonment, not cart-abandonment — subject/creative mismatch.
- Featured products span wildly different demographics (Care Bears kids' sneakers, men's basketball shoes, work boots) with no unifying persona thread, diluting relevance.
- The one real financial incentive (25% off) is buried mid-email, well below the fold, after three product blocks and a bestsellers grid.
- Per brand history, this is at least the fourth cart/browse-nudge send in under two weeks with the same visual skeleton — recognizable creative fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the subject: add a visible countdown or "your cart expires in [X] hours" banner near the top — right now the urgency lives only in the subject line, and it's the single biggest lever here.
- 2. Move the 25% off Plus banner above the bestsellers grid, ideally right under the hero CTA, so the incentive is visible before the scroll fatigue sets in.
- 3. Tighten the hero product selection to one persona lane instead of mixing kids', men's athletic, and men's workwear in the same three-item cart recap — feels like a generic template, not "your cart."
- 4. Vary the creative structure from the last few sends (per history: 7/16, 7/22 "Your Items Are Waiting," multiple "Did you forget something?") — same skeleton every few days trains subscribers to skim past it.
