# Spotlight on: Fresh
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Lush's "Fresh" product philosophy send — "Always fresh, naturally!" as the hero — leaning on their core differentiator: no preservatives, made-to-order freshness. The concept is sound and underused in their recent calendar; after honey, hair, summer smells, and sun care in the past week, a product-philosophy editorial is a gear-change worth making. The pink/yellow palette is on-brand, the mini-product grid gives the subscriber something concrete to browse, and the Ask An Expert video pull adds editorial depth without overselling.
- Then comes the Halloween Live-Scream module. Pumpkins. Ghosts. "Save The Date." On July 13. It is the single most dissonant creative decision in any Lush send reviewed this month, and it lands like a scheduling mistake regardless of intent. A subscriber who opened the summer travel guide eleven days ago or the beach sun-care send on the 4th is not primed for October event presales — and the tonal whiplash undercuts the "naturally fresh" vibe the first two-thirds of the email worked to establish.
- Underneath the Halloween detour, the underlying "Fresh" editorial has real legs that this execution doesn't fully pull. There's no offer, no Lush Club angle (a miss after the double-points send two days ago), and "Find Your Fresh" as a CTA tells you nothing about where you land.

## What's working

- Hot-pink hero with the "Always fresh, naturally!" headline is visually confident and on-brand — the eye lands immediately on the offer zone
- Mini-product grid (Strawberry Picking, Angels On Bare Skin, Cup O' Coffee, Pineapple) gives subscribers four specific, named products to evaluate — more actionable than a generic collection link
- Ask An Expert video module is a smart editorial beat: it educates rather than sells, and "What happens when you use fresh ingredients?" is a legitimately curious hook
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no empty fields visible

## What's weak

- Halloween Live-Scream module in mid-July is a hard context break — it reads as a deployment error, not a clever early preview
- "Find Your Fresh" CTA is too vague to drive clicks; the destination (a collection? a landing page? a quiz?) is not inferrable from the button copy
- No offer anywhere in the email — no discount, no Lush Club points — notably absent after the double-points send on the 11th set an expectation
- The "What are the minis today?" framing implies a daily rotating drop, but no urgency or scarcity copy supports that reading, so the mechanic lands flat

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Halloween module entirely.** Send it as a standalone "Mark Your Calendar" email in late September. Deploying it here at full-module size in July reads as a calendar mistake and breaks the send's theme.
- 2. **Replace "Find Your Fresh" with a destination-specific CTA.** "Shop Fresh Face Masks" or "Try a Fresh Scrub" tells the subscriber exactly where they land and anchors the click to a category.
- 3. **Add a Lush Club hook.** After the double-points send on July 11, subscribers expect a loyalty thread. Even one line — "Fresh products always earn Lush Club points" — closes the loop and gives a reason to click now.
- 4. **Put urgency copy on the mini-grid.** If these actually rotate daily, say so explicitly: "Today's fresh picks — available now" with a timestamp or countdown. The mechanic is interesting but invisible without the hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your fresh picks for today: face masks, scrubs + more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `No preservatives. No compromises. Our freshest products.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet the minis rotating today — Strawberry Picking, Cup O' Coffee + 2 more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Find out what fresh ingredients actually do for your skin`
