# Limited time only—the Crown gift
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's first co-brand send in the recent run — a Crown Affair x ILIA collab GWP, not another Thru Line "prove it" stat card or the lip roundup from 08-26. The hero is clean: soft-focus beauty shot, a clear "LIMITED TIME ONLY" badge, and one unambiguous offer (free mini Leave-In Conditioner with $75+ orders). It reads as more premium and editorial than 08-19's eyeliner-mini send, though the offer itself is a smaller lift ($75 threshold vs. straightforward free-with-purchase).
- Where it falls apart is the body. After the hero and product callout, the email pivots into a "complete the ritual" tier-unlock section with four near-identical product-grid tiles (Out-The-Door Ritual, Hero Set, The ILIA Look, Sunscreen + Color Set) that dilute the single hero offer into an unfocused merchandising wall. By the time the reader reaches "CLAIM YOUR GIFT," it's unclear whether the CTA maps to the $75 conditioner offer or the $105-value bundle two scrolls up.
- Net: strong top, cluttered middle. A tighter edit — hero + one clear upsell tier, cut to two panels — would push this from a decent mid-funnel send to a genuinely good one.

## What's working

- Clear single hero offer stated in plain language: "mini Crown Affair Leave-In Conditioner with $75+ orders."
- "LIMITED TIME ONLY" badge is visually prominent without being spammy (no all-caps subject, no exclamation stacking).
- Product hero shot (pump bottle) with a tight, scannable benefits list (nourishes, reduces frizz, prevents breakage).
- Co-brand framing (Crown Affair x ILIA) is a fresh beat versus the string of solo-ILIA Thru Line/lip sends this month.
- Clean footer with trust badges (Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, free shipping over $50) reinforces brand credibility.

## What's weak

- Four back-to-back product-grid tiles ("complete the ritual") repeat the same visual template and blur which offer is actually live — this is the same "wall of near-identical panels" problem flagged in past ILIA lip-roundup sends.
- The primary CTA ambiguity: "ALL YOURS →" appears right after the $75 offer, but "CLAIM YOUR GIFT →" appears at the bottom after four unrelated bundle callouts — two CTAs, unclear which one the reader should act on.
- No visible pricing anywhere in the ritual/bundle section — "($105 VALUE)" is the only number shown, with no sale price, making the offer feel vague rather than concrete.
- The closing brand-tagline banner ("Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better") is a dead scroll stop before the footer — no CTA, no urgency reinforcement, just brand wallpaper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the four product-grid tiles down to one — pick the single best upsell (likely "The Hero Set" or the Out-The-Door bundle) and drop the rest; this is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Collapse to one CTA. Keep "ALL YOURS →" tied to the $75 offer immediately under the hero, and drop the redundant "CLAIM YOUR GIFT" button lower down — or relabel it clearly as a distinct, second offer if it truly is one.
- 3. Add a real price anchor next to "$105 VALUE" — even "yours free" or a % savings makes the bundle section feel like an offer instead of a catalog page.
- 4. State the deadline. "Limited time only" with no date or countdown reads as evergreen urgency, not credible urgency — add "through 8/31" or similar.
- 5. Subject/preheader work:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Crown Affair mini with $75+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your hair gift, before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nourish, de-frizz, protect — free with your order`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Crown Affair x ILIA: the mini gift ends soon`
