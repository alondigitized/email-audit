# ✨ Pick Up & Save 15%
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a niche-mechanic send — 15% off is gated to Buy Online, Pick Up in Store orders only, which is a much narrower audience than the sitewide-discount hero the COLORfest campaign has leaned on since 8/16's "Ends tomorrow: Save up to $20" and 8/23's flash sale. The confetti-circle hero art is the exact same template as every prior send in this run, so visually there's zero differentiation in-inbox from the last eight emails — this is now the ninth near-identical dot-border creative in under a month.
- The layout itself executes cleanly: one hero offer, one button, clear deadline (Ends 08/31), no scroll-past-the-fold clutter. But the offer is a logistics-mechanic discount, not a product or category push, and it's followed by a generic "Shop Your Beauty Favorites" grid and a separate Rewards banner competing for attention. Verdict: well-built template, weak targeting — BOPIS discounts convert only for a shopper who already plans to drive to a store, which most email recipients don't.

## What's working

- Single, uncluttered hero with one clear offer and one CTA button ("Shop now") — no split attention.
- Concrete, unambiguous terms: 15% off, no minimum, hard end date (08/31).
- Icon row (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping $40+, 2 Hour Delivery, BOPIS) reinforces the fulfillment options relevant to this specific offer.
- No render bugs — text, icons, and product circles all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The offer mechanic (BOPIS-only) is a hard exclusion most subscribers won't qualify for interest-wise — this isn't a universal discount like 8/23's sitewide 20%.
- Same confetti-dot hero border as the last several COLORfest sends — zero visual freshness, risk of banner-blindness by inbox #9.
- Two competing offers in one email: the 15% BOPIS hero and the "$5 Reward in September" banner below dilute a single clear CTA.
- Product grid ("Shop Your Beauty Favorites") is a generic category directory, not tied to the discount — doesn't reinforce urgency or the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a broader, less exclusionary mechanic, or at minimum make the BOPIS requirement clearer in the subject/preview so opens convert to qualified clicks — don't bait a broad list with a narrow offer.
- 2. Retire the confetti-dot hero template for at least one send — visual sameness across 9 emails in a month is the single biggest drag on this campaign's performance.
- 3. Cut the Rewards banner from this send or move it to a dedicated email — one offer, one CTA, per the campaign's own best-performing sends (8/23).
- 4. Tie the "Shop Your Beauty Favorites" grid to BOPIS-eligible categories instead of a generic directory, so it reinforces rather than distracts from the hero offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pick up 15% off — order today, grab it today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Skip shipping: 15% off in-store pickup orders`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No minimum. Order online, pick up in 2 hours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends 8/31 — free 2-hour pickup on top of your discount.`
