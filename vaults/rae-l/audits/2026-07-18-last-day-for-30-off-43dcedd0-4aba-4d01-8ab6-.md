# Last Day for 30% Off 💖
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Sally Beauty's closing mechanic on a 30% off Overtone sale — the same "Last Day" playbook used to close the 20% off flash sale on July 10 (which scored 9/10). The execution here is clean and honest: a legible pink hero, real product shots of specific Overtone SKUs, and a credible deadline. Overtone has a distinct aesthetic and passionate buyer base, and this email leans into that with the brand's signature blush palette.
- The one structural problem is the disconnect below the hero fold. You're running a brand-specific Overtone sale, but "Trending Now" slots ion and Wella developer/toner products underneath — products that have zero connection to why someone would click the hero CTA. It dilutes the message and muddies the conversion path.
- Solid execution on a reliable mechanic, but not as tight as it could be given the brand-focused premise.

## What's working

- **Hero clarity**: "LAST DAY / 30% Off Overtone / Hair color that cares. Ends today." reads instantly — zero ambiguity about the offer.
- **On-brand hero palette**: The pink/blush background matches Overtone's own visual identity, not just generic Sally Beauty red — subtle but smart.
- **Specific SKUs shown**: Scarlet Red and Royal Plum Overtone masks are featured with distinct packaging — actionable for color-conscious buyers.
- **Klarna callout**: The 4-installment banner is well-placed and reduces friction for anyone hesitating at a full Overtone haul price.
- **Render is clean**: No broken images, no overlapping text, no layout issues visible.

## What's weak

- **"Trending Now" breaks the brand frame**: ion and Wella developer/toner products mid-email feel like a generic Sally Beauty homepage widget dropped into an Overtone-focused send.
- **Subject doesn't name Overtone**: "Last Day for 30% Off 💖" works for a sitewide sale; for a brand-specific offer, the brand name is the hook for fans — leaving it out costs open rate from the exact audience who would care most.
- **Generic CTA copy**: "Shop now" appears three times without ever connecting back to Overtone or 30% off — missed chance to reinforce the offer inside the button.
- **Preheader is likely leaking**: The topmost banner text in the render reads "BEAUTY IN A DASH - BUY ONLINE, PICK UP IN-STORE" — if no explicit preheader was set, that's what hits the inbox preview line.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put "Overtone" in the subject** — that name alone will spike opens among existing Overtone fans who are subscribed because of it. The current subject could be any brand's last-day email.
- 2. **CTA copy**: Change "Shop now" → "Shop Overtone" or "Get 30% Off" to carry the offer language into the button.
- 3. **Set a real preheader**: Something like "Scarlet Red, Royal Plum + all shades — offer ends tonight." gives the inbox preview actual conversion fuel.
- 4. **Cut or reframe "Trending Now"**: Either remove the ion/Wella section entirely or relabel it "Complete Your Color Routine" and curate products that pair with Overtone (color-safe shampoo, bond repair, etc.) — then it becomes a logical upsell rather than a tonal detour.
- 5. **Add a time specificity**: "Ends today" is fine; "Ends at midnight tonight" is better — it creates a mental deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Day: 30% Off Overtone 💖`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Overtone sale ends tonight — 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Scarlet Red, Royal Plum + every shade. Sale ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Overtone color haul just got 30% cheaper — until midnight.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

```css

  font-family: 'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif;

```
`Founders Grotesk` is referenced in the base `a` rule but has no corresponding `@font-face` declaration anywhere in the `<head>`. Every link in the email will render in Helvetica/system sans-serif instead. If intentional (web-safe fallback), the dead font name should be removed; if unintentional, the font file URL is missing.

**Google Fonts pinned to `v17` (current: v29+).**
All six `@font-face` `src` URLs use `/opensans/v17/...`. Google does not guarantee indefinite availability of version-pinned font URLs. Low risk today, but worth updating on next template revision.

**`img { white-space: pre }` is dead CSS.**
`white-space` has no effect on replaced elements (`<img>`). This rule does nothing and can be removed.

**Mobile breakpoint at `max-width: 499px` — non-standard.**
Most email clients report viewport widths of 375–430px (phones) or 600–768px (tablets). The 499px cutoff means a device at 500–599px gets neither desktop layout (600px table) nor mobile overrides, potentially breaking column stacks and text sizing on mid-range devices. Standard practice is `max-width: 600px`.

**Missing `format-detection` meta tag.**
No `<meta name="format-detection" content="telephone=no, date=no, address=no, email=no">` is present. iOS Mail will auto-link phone numbers, dates, and addresses in the email body, which can break layout and override link styling.
