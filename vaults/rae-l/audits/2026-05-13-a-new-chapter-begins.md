# A new chapter begins
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure brand storytelling send for Glossier You perfume — no discount, no urgency, no hook beyond vibes. The "new chapter" subject is doing no heavy lifting at all; someone unfamiliar with the campaign could mistake it for an account notification. Once inside, the email has three distinct modules: a campaign intro ("you smell good."), an editorial crossover ("Read between the lines"), and a UGC pull ("Vegas Showstopper" testimonial + story solicitation). The creative is clean and the brand voice is unmistakably Glossier.
- The problem is that nothing in this email answers the question every inbox recipient implicitly asks: "Why should I click right now?" There's no price signal, no time limit, no new product launch teased. The UGC section at the bottom is genuinely smart — social proof in the shopper's own words — but it's buried under two other modules. This is a loyalty-nurture email masquerading as a conversion email.
- Verdict: well-executed brand content that will resonate strongly with active Glossier fans and do almost nothing for anyone else.

## What's working

- **UGC testimonial ("Vegas Showstopper")** is specific, sensory, and credible — real name, real scenario, product named directly. Best trust-builder in the email.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — each section is distinct, no clutter, text is legible over images.
- **"Shop You" CTA** in the testimonial module is the most direct verb+noun combo in the email and sits right next to social proof.
- **Brand voice is tight and consistent** — "you smell good." copy sounds exactly like Glossier.
- **No render bugs** — images load, text isn't overlapping, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- **Subject "A new chapter begins" is completely generic** — no product, no offer, no curiosity hook. Could be a software update or a novel announcement.
- **Zero offer signal anywhere in the email** — no price, no discount, no free shipping, no bundle. Pure editorial.
- **"Read between the lines" editorial block is unclear** — the copy underneath is garbled/truncated ("The isn't that once I story-ship") and the purpose of this module is ambiguous.
- **"Check it out" CTA is weak** — vague verb, no noun, no clear landing destination.
- **Four CTAs across the email** dilute each other — "Check it out," "Glossier You," "Shop You," and "Share your story" compete rather than funnel.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the actual hook** — the sensory storytelling ("you smell good") is the campaign's strongest asset. Put it in the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `You smell good. Here's the story.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The book store. Glossier You. A new chapter.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One scent. Four romantic places. The book store is next.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Real stories from people who wore Glossier You in Vegas, subways, and bookstores.`
- 2. **Lead with the UGC testimonial**, not the brand campaign intro. Social proof converts; brand storytelling nurtures. Swap module order.
- 3. **Fix or cut the "Read between the lines" block** — the copy is truncated and the CTA ("Glossier You") just names the product without a clear action. Either give it a proper sentence or remove it.
- 4. **Consolidate to two CTAs max** — one for buying ("Shop You") and one for engaging ("Share your story"). The middle two buttons create decision paralysis.
- 5. **Add one light price anchor** — even "starting at $72" or "free shipping on orders over $35" signals this is shoppable, not just editorial.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Invalid tracking element** — Critical

```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KRF9MQ1WQ8JS6CM1PFQTS7JV_0"></span>
```

`<span>` does not support `rel` or `href` attributes in XHTML 1.0 or HTML5. Email clients will silently ignore both attributes. If this is meant to be a tracking pixel, it will not fire. If it is intended to load an external stylesheet, it will not load. The correct element for a CSS link is `<link rel="stylesheet" href="...">` (though external CSS has very poor email client support), or an `<img>` tag with `width="1" height="1"` for a pixel.

**Tracking domain confirmed:** `ctrk.klclick.com` — Klaviyo's standard click redirect. Normal.
