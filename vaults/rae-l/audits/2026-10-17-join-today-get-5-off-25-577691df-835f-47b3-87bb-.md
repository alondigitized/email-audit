# Join today, get $5 off $25
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third loyalty-signup push in about two weeks. The 10/02 send ("Beauty deals are even better with Sally Rewards") and the 10/11 send ("Get $5 off $25 when you join") pitched the same $5 off $25. The offer is clear: a pink hero reads "Join Sally.Rewards & save on your wish list," with the $5 off $25 line and a black "Join Sally.Rewards" button. The subject and hero match, which the 10/16 Halloween send didn't manage.
- The lower half is a long explainer. It has a two-tier Preferred and Elite comparison, a second CTA, a Klarna banner, an icon row, an app download block and about a dozen lines of legal text. The tier table is useful for someone weighing the program. For a repeat sender it buries the one thing that matters, which is $5 off $25 and an October deadline.
- The lifestyle photo of two women with Sally tote bags is pleasant and on-brand, but it sells the tote, not the offer. Overall this is a competent but stale send. A subscriber who saw it on 10/11 has no new reason to act.

## What's working

- The hero offer is visible immediately: "$5 off $25+ in October" sits under a large headline on a saturated pink block.
- There is one dominant CTA style: a black "Join Sally.Rewards" button that stands out against the pink, repeated as "Join Rewards" after the tier table.
- The Preferred vs. Elite tier cards are clean and scannable. They show free entry, points rates, free shipping and early access.
- The subject matches the hero, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- The red utility bars at the top (BOPIS, 2-hour delivery, no minimum) add useful service info without competing with the hero.

## What's weak

- The offer is repeated from 10/02 and 10/11. Nothing in the render signals a change, so the cadence feels like nagging.
- The "in October" deadline has no specific date in the hero. The actual expiry, 10/31/2026, is only in the fine print.
- The photo is large and doesn't show product, a benefit or the discount. It takes a full screen of scrolling.
- The email is long. The Klarna banner, the icon row and the app download block dilute the single ask.
- The fine print is dense, and the conditions are easy to miss. They include a new loyalty account being required, a 30-day redemption window and no coupon stacking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a hard deadline in the hero, such as "Ends 10/31," and cut the photo to a short banner. The offer and CTA should come before the first scroll.
- 2. Don't send the same offer a third time. Either suppress recent openers or add something new, such as double points on a category or a first-order bonus.
- 3. Cut the Klarna block, the icon row and the app download. Keep one tier table and one CTA.
- 4. Add one concrete use case to the hero, such as "a $25 hair color order costs $20."
- 5. Move the key conditions (new member, 30-day redemption) next to the CTA in plain language.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$5 off $25 ends Oct 31 — join free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $5 is still waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free to join. Use it on hair color, tools or nails within 30 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign up in 1 minute, save $5 on your first $25+ order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML shows a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with an MSO/VML fallback and a mobile breakpoint at 499px. The confirmed defects are in the third-party tracking pixels: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a non-HTTPS pixel, and images with no `alt` attribute. The header-based checks (unsubscribe, authentication) are inconclusive because of the capture method.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **47 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so I can't confirm destinations, redirect health, or final landing URLs.
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel URL.** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…`
  - `r=[CACHEBUSTER]` is a literal, unsubstituted placeholder. Cache-busting won't work, so the pixel may be cached or deduplicated and impressions undercounted.
  - Several parameters are `-11`, which looks like a default value for unpopulated IDs (`si`, `ci`, `pi`, `ad`). The impression may not attribute to a campaign.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel.** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy usually rewrites it, but some clients will block it or show a mixed-content warning. The pixel is likely lost in those clients.
- **Three separate third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, and `login.dotomi.com`. They add external requests and failure points.
- **Recipient identifier in a pixel path.** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2325318/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` ends in a segment with the `003` prefix, which is the Salesforce Contact ID format. It's a normal ESP pattern, but it exposes a CRM identifier in the image URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty image source.** One `<img>` has `src=""` and no alt text. Some clients resolve an empty `src` to the document URL and make a spurious request, and it can show as a broken image.
- **Missing `alt` on three tracking pixels** (`003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`). Screen readers may read out the filename or URL. These should have `alt=""`, ideally with `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"`.
- **XHTML validity.** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, unlike the other `<meta .../>` tags, which breaks the XHTML 1.0 doctype. Browsers and clients ignore this in practice.
- **Font fallback.** The CSS sets `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}`, but only Open Sans has an `@font-face` rule. Founders Grotesk always falls back to Helvetica. This is non-breaking.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so I can't check the body's image `alt` text, link text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the `lang` attribute. The `<html>` tag shown has no `lang`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq pixel URL (see section 2).
- **Possible unpopulated values:** the `-11` values and the truncated `advt=-`. This is plausible but not conclusive.
- No other visible merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) in the portion of the source I could see.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers in the original mailbox.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Sender `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain. Verify alignment from a direct-delivery copy.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 47 tracked links were skipped, and the visible source contains no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters. The only observed parameters are on the pixels (`ago=212&ao=883`, `dtm_cid=2990`), and those aren't landing-page tags.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` substitution** in the myvisualiq pixel at the ad-server or ESP level. Populate or remove the `-11` placeholder IDs.
2. **Serve the Dotomi pixel over HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController…`).
3. **Remove the empty-`src` `<img>`** or point it at a real asset.
4. **Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels** and make them 1×1 and hidden.
5. **Check the raw headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass results.
6. **Resolve a sample of the tracking links** (for example by following the redirects with a script) to check the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing page status. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer and `lang`.
7. Self-close the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tag.
