# Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a generic "Hey, Insider" catch-all with no single idea. It stacks six or seven modules: an app-only gift-with-purchase, a Huda Beauty 4X points hero, a personalized product row, a Top Beauty row, two Spot-it-Shop-it tiles, Dieux Skincare, and a tier strip. Directly below it, a pink "Hot Sellers / Shop Now" block sells nothing specific. The real hero offer, Huda 4X points with code HUDA4X, sits underneath that.

The subject says "we think you'll love this", but the body doesn't name what "this" is. The "Personalized Beauty Edit" shows Chanel, amika, and Tom Ford, then repeats Tom Ford in the next row. - Compared with the 2X/5X points sends of 10/6 and 10/7, this is calmer and less urgent, but also less focused. Verdict: a competent but diffuse brand-newsletter send. It lacks one clear reason to click.

## What's weak

- Too many modules with no single hierarchy: GWP, 4X points, a product edit, holiday drops, new and clean, and Dieux.
- The "Hot Sellers / Shop Now" block is vague and pushes the real hero offer below the fold.
