# Lush × Moomin has landed
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Lush's launch email for the Lush × Moomin limited-edition collaboration, and the hero earns its keep immediately. The illustrated Moomin forest-pond scene in teal and sage is genuinely thumb-stopping — distinctive in a way that July's Halloween ghost send (which scored 8/10 on a much stronger product hook) wasn't. The literary bridge — quoting "for if there is anything Moomintrolls really love it is bathing" from *Comet in Moominland* (1946) — is elegant IP storytelling that makes the collaboration feel earned rather than slapped-on.
- The problem is structural: this is a mood board masquerading as an email. No product appears. No price appears. "Limited edition" is in the copy but there's no deadline, no quantity signal, no "drops at midnight" framing. For a subscriber who doesn't have pre-existing Moomin affinity — a real slice of the US audience that never grew up with Finnish children's books — there is nothing to want, because nothing is shown. The CTA "See More Moomins" is playful, but it's asking for a blind click into an unknown collection.
- Compared to the July 11 "Zero packaging. Double points." send (6/10), which told you exactly what the deal was even if it was dull, this one is prettier and less actionable. The illustration earns the open; the body doesn't close.

## What's working

- The illustrated hero is genuinely beautiful — Moomin-world scene in forest/teal tones will stop the scroll in a saturated inbox
- The *Comet in Moominland* (1946) quote is a clever, charming bridge between the IP and Lush's bathing identity; it makes the collab feel inevitable rather than opportunistic
- "A FRESH COLLAB" headline doubles as brand-voice play ("fresh" is Lush's core pillar) and collab announcement — clean conceptual work
- "See More Moomins" CTA is specific, playful, and on-brand — far better than a generic "Shop Now"
- Clean, bug-free render — the illustrated aesthetic translates well, no broken images or layout artifacts
- **No product is visible anywhere in the email body** — illustration, quote, and body copy without a single product image, name, or price; you don't know what you're clicking into
- **"Limited edition" with zero deadline** — the scarcity signal is completely neutralized by the absence of any end date, quantity cap, or "before it sells out" framing
- **The BRING IT BACK / PREFERENCES module** breaks the Moomin immersion — bolted below the hero, it reads as a different email
- **Moomin recognition gap in the US** — unlike Halloween or the July 12 Honey send (nostalgia is universal), Moomin requires IP affinity; without a product hook, non-fans have no reason to click
- **Cadence pressure**: this is the 7th Lush email in approximately 12 days; a pure awareness send with no offer at this frequency is a hard ask on inbox patience

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface one hero product with a visible price** — this is the single biggest missed opportunity. Even "Moomin Bath Bomb, $X" anchored in the hero image would transform this from awareness to intent.
- 2. **Add scarcity copy with credible urgency** — "Limited edition — while supplies last" or a specific drop/sell-out date on the hero; "limited edition" alone does nothing.
- 3. **Make the Moomin quote the preheader** — it's the best copy in the email; it should be in the inbox before the open, not buried below the fold.
- 4. **Cut or relocate the BRING IT BACK / PREFERENCES block** — move it to footer or its own digest; it destroys the immersive Moomin world Lush built in the hero.
- 5. **Add collection depth signal** — "12 new Moomin products" or "5 bath bombs, 3 body washes, one gift set" gives non-fans a concrete reason to investigate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet Moomin — Lush's dreamiest collab yet`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Lush × Moomin drops today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `"Moomintrolls love bathing." So do we. 12 limited-edition products, live now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh scents, Moomin magic — limited edition, yours now`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is Lush's launch email for the Lush × Moomin limited-edition collaboration, and the hero earns its keep immediately. The illustrated Moomin forest-pond scene in teal and sage is genuinely thumb-stopping — distinctive in a way that July's Halloween ghost send (which scored 8/10 on a much stronger product hook) wasn't. The literary bridge — quoting "for if there is anything Moomintrolls really love it is bathing" from *Comet in Moominland* (1946) — is elegant IP storytelling that makes the collaboration feel earned rather than slapped-on.

The problem is structural: this is a mood board masquerading as an email. No product appears. No price appears. "Limited edition" is in the copy but there's no deadline, no quantity signal, no "drops at midnight" framing. For a subscriber who doesn't have pre-existing Moomin affinity — a real slice of the US audience that never grew up with Finnish children's books — there is nothing to want, because nothing is shown. The CTA "See More Moomins" is playful, but it's asking for a blind click into an unknown collection.
