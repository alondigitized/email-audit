# VIPs get the best + 20% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's second consecutive sale email — a follow-up to yesterday's 09-12 "The ILIA VIP Sale starts now" — but this time it's explicitly VIP-membership-gated rather than a straight sitewide discount. The hero is clean and on-brand ("nothing but the best," gold dish, 20% badge), and the bestseller carousel below does real work showing six named products with credibility markers (awards, star counts, "12,000+ five-star reviews"). The gifts-at-checkout and VIP-perks blocks are genuinely useful additions that weren't as prominent in the prior sale send.
- The problem is the offer itself is muddier than 09-12's: the subject promises "20% off" but the fine print (visible as an asterisk on the hero CTA copy) signals this is a join-VIP-to-unlock deal, not an instant discount — that's a bait-and-switch risk for anyone who isn't already a member. Back-to-back sale emails two days apart also risks fatigue after a run of six editorial/tutorial sends (09-01 to 09-10) that had already established a slower cadence.
- Solid execution, weaker honesty-of-offer than yesterday's send. This lands mid-pack — worse than a clean sitewide sale, better than the pure-editorial sends.

## What's working

- Hero clearly states the mechanic ("20% OFF SITEWIDE FOR VIPS") in the top bar, so the gate isn't hidden below the fold.
- Product carousel is well-organized: six items, each with a one-line credibility hook (awards, review counts, wear-time stats).
- "Gifts at checkout" section (free multi-stick, free bag on $100+) adds real incremental value beyond the percentage discount.
- "VIP treatment" perks block (free shipping, points, early access) gives non-members a concrete reason to join, reinforcing the CTA.
- Clean, consistent visual style — polaroid-style product photography matches ILIA's established editorial template from recent sends.

## What's weak

- Two sale emails in three days (09-12 and 09-13) reads as aggressive cadence right after two weeks of restrained editorial content.
- The subject says "20% off" flat, but the actual offer requires joining VIP first — that's a gap between subject promise and delivered mechanic once opened.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the render — no "ends Sunday" or countdown, so there's no reason to act today vs. next week.
- Six near-identical product tiles in a row with no visual break creates a long, repetitive scroll before reaching the second CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the VIP requirement in the subject line itself so there's no gap between promise and mechanic — right now the subject reads as a straight 20% discount.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero or at least the sale CTA band — nothing here signals scarcity.
- 3. Break up the six-item product carousel with a section header or divider partway through to reduce scroll fatigue.
- 4. Space this further from 09-12's sale send next time, or differentiate it more sharply (e.g., lead with the free-gifts angle instead of a second "sale" framing) so it doesn't read as a repeat.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping, points, and early access — free to join`
