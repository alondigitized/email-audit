# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment email again, the sixth abandonment-style send in a week. It follows the 11/5 and 11/4 "Your cart is one click away!" sends and the 11/1 "Pick up where you left off!" send. The subject, "This is perfect for you!", promises personalization. The hero asks "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT? YOU'VE GOT GREAT TASTE!" over a black Slip-ins Arch Fit Arcade sneaker. That's a different shoe from the UNO skull sneaker in the recent cart emails, which is a small freshness win.
- The best part is the 20% off for Skechers Plus members, valid through 11/10. It sits in a dark navy band well below the hero. The hero has no offer, so the person who stops at "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" never learns there's a discount. The hero also has a lot of empty white space above the shoe. The six-shoe grid is tiny, with small captions and no prices.
- Judgment: competent but passive. The email is a reminder with a discount buried halfway down. The subject over-promises, because "perfect for you" is just a generic reminder.

## What's working

- The hero shoe is large and clean, and the product name is clearly captioned.
- The navy "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" button is high-contrast and unambiguous.
- The "20% OFF" in the dark band is the strongest visual on the page, with a clear Plus-member framing and an end date of 11/10.
- The six recommended styles add variety and a path for people who don't like the hero shoe.
- The footer has the usual utilities: app, curbside pickup, store finder, and Afterpay/Klarna.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. The 20% off only appears after scrolling past the hero and the six-shoe grid.
- The shoe grid is small, with tiny captions, no prices, and no sale or member pricing.
- "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" is soft, low-urgency copy. The "SHOP NOW" link in the offer band is a text link, not a button.
- "Valid thru 11/10" is small gray text on navy and hard to read. The details are further buried in the footer fine print.
- The category list and footer take up a lot of space and dilute focus, and the large blank area above the hero shoe pushes the content down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus offer into the hero, for example a banner above or below the headline. Make "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" say "SHOP 20% OFF" or "Get 20% off these".
- 2. Show the member price on the hero shoe and on each grid item, with the original price struck through.
- 3. Cut the empty space above the hero shoe so the product and CTA appear in the first screen on mobile.
- 4. Make the offer band's "SHOP NOW" a real button, and state the deadline plainly: "Ends Tuesday, 11/10".
- 5. Vary the cadence. This is the sixth abandonment-style email in a week, so suppress or space them out after the second send.
