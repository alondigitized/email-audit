# We turned back the ⏰: One more day for your iconic mug
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean extension send for the Three Eyed Smiley Mug gift-with-purchase. The hero reads "EXTENDED! Ends tonight / Get a free Three Eyed Smiley Mug with your $75+ order*". The offer is concrete, the button is clear, and the lifestyle photo of a hand holding the mug with a pastry is warm and on-brand. It lands as a straightforward last-chance nudge.
- The weakness is that it is a near-repeat. This is the second "last chance" for the same mug in two days, after 11/01's "Ends Tonight! Don't miss your free iconic mug ☕". The 10/30 send was the fourth gift-with-purchase email in nine days. The "ends tonight" urgency is hard to believe after it was already used once. The one new beat is the "turned back the clock" extension, and that hook appears in the subject but only faintly in the body ("EXTENDED!").
- The lower half dilutes the offer. A second mug block repeats the hero, then four gifting tiles with four "Shop now" buttons pull attention away from the mug. Judgement: competent and clear, but repetitive and diffuse. It is a solid 6.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: free mug, $75+ threshold, and "Ends tonight" right above the headline.
- The lifestyle hero photo with the pastry and hot drink is appealing and shows the gift in use.
- The second block's marshmallow-mug close-up makes the gift look desirable and has a copy line with use cases ("sipping hot chocolate, holding your makeup favorites").
- The product tiles are tidy and labeled "Limited edition", which adds a little scarcity for gifting.

## What's weak

- The urgency is stale. A second "ends tonight" in two days weakens credibility.
- The "EXTENDED!" tag is small, so the clock-turned-back hook in the subject barely carries into the body.
- The body repeats the mug in two consecutive blocks, which spends space without adding information.
- The six "Shop now" buttons are generic. Their copy doesn't name the product or action.
- The asterisk terms sit at the very bottom in small text, so the threshold and exclusions are not visible near the offer.
- In the second block the body copy appears clipped at the right edge ("holding yo…"), and a mug photo directly below the hero image looks cropped or tight.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the extension the headline: "Extended: one more day for your free mug", with a clear deadline time. Cut the duplicate mug block.
- 2. Replace the generic buttons with specific copy, e.g. "Claim my free mug" and "Shop the Wardrobe Kit".
- 3. Move the terms line up near the hero and show the $75 threshold progress with a "spend $75, get the mug" cue.
- 4. Cut the gifting grid to two products so the mug stays the focus.
- 5. Fix the clipped copy in the second block.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get the Three Eyed Smiley Mug. Last call is midnight.`
