# It’s officially Virgo Season!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's fully committed pivot away from the Cheek Week discount arc and into pure zodiac-editorial territory — the fourth no-discount send in this thread since 8/20's "Going back to school?" bundle. There's no offer anywhere: no %, no code, no "shop the sale." Instead it's a horoscope-flavored listicle wrapped around two employee testimonials for Milky Oil and the Lip Line, which is a cute brand-voice exercise but a thin reason to click if you're not already a superfan.
- The astrology hook is genuinely well-targeted content marketing — "Virgo Season" with a warm orange hero image and a "Virgo Energy" numbered list reads as lifestyle content, not spam, and the employee-testimonial format ("NAME: Kaitlyn, BIRTHDAY: September 4th") adds a nice authenticity layer that Glossier's discount emails don't have. But structurally this is a weaker send than the Cheek Week arc: two products get "Shop" buttons buried after two full scrolls of zodiac copy, and there's zero urgency or price incentive to act now versus later.
- Compared to 8/18's "Cheek Week ends tonight!" (8/10, offer above the fold, unmissable CTA), this is a step back toward the low-converting editorial mode of 8/7-8/9. It's a fine brand-affinity piece, not a conversion email.

## What's working

- Hero image and "Celebrate Virgo Season" headline are cohesive and on-theme, with warm, seasonal color grading.
- The "Virgo Energy" 01/02/03 list format is scannable and gives the email a magazine feel rather than a hard sell.
- Employee testimonial format (name, birthday, position, quote) adds authenticity and a real editorial hook that's distinct from prior Glossier sends in this thread.
- Two clear "Shop" CTA buttons (Milky Oil, Shop Lip Line) are visible with outlined, legible styling.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together.

## What's weak

- No discount, promo code, or any concrete offer anywhere in the email — a pure content play after a run of promo sends trains subscribers to skip it.
- Primary CTA is ambiguous at the top: the first two screens (hero + Virgo Energy list) have no button at all, so a mobile reader scrolling past the fold sees no actionable next step.
- The astrology conceit is a big lift of copy and design real estate for two product mentions — Milky Oil and Lip Liner in Buff/Cherry Balm Dotcom get one testimonial paragraph each, buried below two "listicle" sections.
- Second employee quote's text is cut off mid-sentence in the render ("Ever... with a completely bare face... I also pair it with B... Cherry Balm Dotcom") — reads as a layout/width issue in this crop, worth double-checking on actual mobile render.
- Not everyone reading this is a Virgo — zero acknowledgment or fallback message for the ~92% of subscribers whose sign isn't Virgo, which weakens relevance for most of the list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a light incentive tied to the theme — e.g., "Virgo Season pick: 15% off Milky Oil" — to give the astrology hook actual purchase weight, since four straight no-discount sends risks conditioning subscribers to ignore this brand's non-promo emails.
- 2. Move a "Shop Milky Oil" or "Shop the Edit" CTA button above the fold, right under the hero copy, so mobile scrollers see an action before they decide to bail.
- 3. Fix/verify the truncated second testimonial — "I also pair it with B..." reads broken even if it's a crop artifact; confirm the live render doesn't clip real copy.
- 4. Consider a subject/preview pairing that either owns the "not a Virgo? still shop" angle or narrows to "for Virgos" with a companion send for other signs, since a single-sign email can't be relevant to most of the base.
