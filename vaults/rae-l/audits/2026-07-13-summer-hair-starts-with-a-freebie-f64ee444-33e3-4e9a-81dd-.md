# Summer hair starts with a freebie
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty has done the smart thing here: after 8 consecutive days of deep discount hammering (AMERICA25 → 20% off flash sale → "Last day" July 10 close), this send steps back from blanket percent-off mechanics and introduces a BOGO on "select hair care." That's the right creative gear-shift. The hero is immediately legible — buy two, get one free, ends July 29 — and the email renders cleanly with a clear visual hierarchy. The 3-day rest since the July 10 close was also the right call.
- What costs this email is the subject line and a confusing product grid. "Summer hair starts with a freebie" genuinely undersells the offer — "freebie" suggests a sample or GWP, not a full BOGO, and there's no urgency signal to compete in the inbox. Worse, the product grid leads with hair color and then breaks to "Beauty Secrets Drip & Clog Proof Hot Glue" — an item that almost certainly doesn't qualify for the BOGO — immediately beneath the "Buy two, get one free select hair care" hero. That's a trust tax.
- Net verdict: solid mechanic, competent execution, but a subject line and a product curation miss that leave real performance on the table.

## What's working

- Hero BOGO offer dominates the first screen — "Buy two, get one free select hair care" in large bold type, with "Ends July 29" visible without scrolling.
- Creative tone resets correctly from the AMERICA25 / flash-sale cadence — summery model, warm palette, no tired "last day" urgency language.
- Top banner surfacing BOPIS and free 1-hour delivery with a $35 threshold is useful utility that doesn't clutter the hero zone.
- Klarna 4-payment mention is well-placed below the product grid for higher-ticket hair color purchases.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, clear nav and footer structure.

## What's weak

- "Beauty Secrets Drip & Clog Proof Hot Glue" sits directly under a BOGO hero for "select hair care" — almost certainly excluded, but visually positioned as if it qualifies.
- "Shop now" is the CTA copy on every product card and the hero — identical, undifferentiated, no specificity.
- No social proof anywhere in the email — no ratings, no "bestseller" badge, no review count on any of the four featured products.
- "Ends July 29" is meaningful deadline urgency but appears only once in small subhead copy under the hero; not reinforced in the product grid or near any CTA.
- No loyalty or Beauty Club rewards callout — Sally Beauty has an active program and this is exactly the kind of promotional moment it should be amplifying.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to name the mechanic, not the vibe.** "Freebie" undersells a genuine buy-two-get-one-free offer. The subject should state the deal.
- 2. **Pull Beauty Secrets Hot Glue out of the BOGO product grid**, or add "not included" language — the current layout creates a credibility gap that damages trust at exactly the moment you want a click.
- 3. **Replace "Shop now" on product cards with category-specific copy** — "Shop ion shades," "Browse L'Oréal color" — gives the grid real click motivation.
- 4. **Add a Beauty Club / rewards callout block** — BOGO stacks with loyalty points; that's a compound value message this email throws away.
- 5. **Repeat the July 29 deadline** near the product grid so urgency follows the reader down the page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 2, get 1 FREE on select hair care — ends July 29`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Get a free product when you buy two hair care faves`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix & match ion, L'Oréal, and more. Offer ends July 29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop trending summer shades — your third item is on us.`
