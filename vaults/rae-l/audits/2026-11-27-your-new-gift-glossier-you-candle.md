# Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built gift-with-purchase send, and it follows the 11/26 "LAST CHANCE: free Crème de You" email almost beat for beat. The hero, "Get a Glossier You Candle, on us with your $100+ order!*", sits next to a glossy red candle on a checkerboard surface, with a clear blue "Shop now" button. The second block, "A $45 VALUE!", gives the free gift a price anchor, which makes the offer feel concrete.
- It has the same problem as the rest of this sale run: it is a recap in a new wrapper. Over the last week the sends have been 25% off everything, then a Copywriter picks email, then fragrance, then gift sets, then the free Crème de You. Now a free candle arrives the day after the last free-gift email. The body then lists four generic "Current favorites" tiles with SALE badges and no prices. The hero body copy ("You know the drill. Black Friday Sale: Enjoy 25% off everything or 30% off your $100+ order!") restates the sitewide offer and dilutes the gift.
- Verdict: a solid 6. It is clear and on-brand, but it is the seventh sale email in about a week and offers little new. The candle itself is the one new idea, and the favorites grid buries it.

## What's working

- The hero names the gift and the threshold together: "Glossier You Candle, on us with your $100+ order."
- The "A $45 VALUE!" label gives the free gift a concrete price anchor.
- The red candle photography is distinctive and on-brand, and it ties to the red Crème de You jar from the 11/26 send.
- The copy about reusing the glass vessel for makeup or pencils gives a reason to want the candle.
- Blue "Shop now" buttons are high-contrast and consistent, and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- The hero body leads with "You know the drill" and repeats the 25%/30% sitewide offer, which competes with the candle gift.
- The "Current favorites" grid has SALE badges but no prices, no discounted amounts and no reviews.
- Both the hero and the "Get your gift" button read as an invitation to shop, so it is unclear whether the candle is added automatically. The asterisk points to "Terms apply" in tiny print at the bottom.
- The second block's body copy is cut off at the right edge ("Once burnt through, you can reuse the glass vess…"), which looks like a layout bug.
- The email is long, and four generic product tiles dilute the single gift message. Every CTA reads "Shop now."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "25% off or 30% off" sentence from the hero and make the candle the only message. Add the threshold mechanic in plain words: "Spend $100, candle added automatically."
- 2. Fix the clipped copy in the second block so the full sentence shows.
- 3. Replace the four-tile grid with one or two items that help shoppers reach $100, with sale prices and star ratings, or add a "you're $X away" cue.
- 4. Replace the "Shop now" buttons with specific CTAs such as "Claim your free candle" and "Shop You Eau de Parfum".
- 5. Space out the free-gift sends. Two consecutive days of free-gift emails will read as repetition.
- **Subject Alt A:** `A free candle with your $100 order 🕯️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Glossier You Candle is yours (a $45 value)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100 today and it's added free. A $45 value.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Smells like Glossier You. Reuse the red glass after.`

## Technical Audit: Glossier, "Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with click tracking through `ctrk.klclick.com` and images hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). Confirmed issues are limited to missing image alt text and one malformed element after `<body>`. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay artifacts.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M8D891K3TX5E61ZXW2R87VC9` has no alt attribute. See Section 3.
- The HTML was truncated, so I could not inspect individual CTA `href` values.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `frame_4053.jpg`
  - `95339270_2980034135406876_2064437603473555456_n_1_`
  - `group_3986_16.jpg`
  - `group_3987_17.jpg`
  - `group_3986_17.jpg`
  - `group_3987_18.jpg`
  - the tracking pixel `01M8D891K3TX5E61ZXW2R87VC9`

  Six are on `stripocdn.email` and one is the Klaviyo pixel. If the six content images are decorative, they need `alt=""`. If they carry content, they need descriptive alt text. The pixel should have `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:block"` if those are not already set. A missing alt attribute lets screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Invalid markup immediately after `<body>`:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klcl…">`. `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This looks like a stray stylesheet link or tracking artifact, and it may trigger a fetch or be stripped by some clients. The truncation hides what it points to.
- **Doctype and charset mismatch:** the document declares XHTML 1.0 Transitional but uses the HTML5 form `<meta charset="utf-8">`, unclosed. This is common in Stripo output and harmless in practice.
- **`<title>` does not match the subject:** `<title>` is "Free Candle? You've got it" and the subject is "Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle". This is low severity, but the title is used by some web-view and archive renderings.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion is unchecked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, probably a capture artifact:** QA did not find `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers, and did not find `Authentication-Results`. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so none of the three can be treated as a sender defect. Check the raw original message for them.
- The sender domain `eml.glossier.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with proper SPF/DKIM alignment, but this is not verified.
- The QA tool says it always includes an unsubscribe link in its probing. I saw no unsubscribe link failure, but the footer and physical address are outside the truncated source, so I cannot confirm them.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The source is truncated before the CTA hrefs. Klaviyo click-wrapping (`ctrk.klclick.com`) hides final destination URLs, so UTM presence and landing page alignment would need redirect-chain resolution. The QA output does not report any.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` attributes to the 6 Stripo-hosted images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones and descriptive text for content. Add `alt=""` to the Klaviyo pixel.
2. Remove or fix the `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` element after `<body>`.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against a raw `.eml` received directly. Don't treat the relay warnings as sender defects.
4. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, or resolve the Klaviyo redirect chains, so the CTA destinations and UTM parameters get checked.
5. Align `<title>` with the subject line, as a minor hygiene fix.
