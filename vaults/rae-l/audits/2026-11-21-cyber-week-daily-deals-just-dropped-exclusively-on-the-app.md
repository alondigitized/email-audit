# Cyber Week daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, promo-dense Cyber Week send. The "30% OFF Benefit Cosmetics" hero is the strongest element: big numerals on a purple sequin background, with a clear SHOP NOW button. The email then works against its own subject. The subject promises "daily deals just dropped," but the first thing in the render is a "FREE* Sample Bag" banner with a BAGITFIRST code, and the real hero is a single-brand 30% off. The daily-deals calendar sits further down.
- The 11/22 to 11/30 calendar is a good idea because it gives subscribers a reason to come back. Only the 11/22 tile carries an "App Exclusive" tag, so the app-exclusive claim in the subject is thinly supported. Below the calendar, the email keeps going with gift cards, an "up to 30% off" tile, Kérastase, a gift guide, and a service strip. That is at least five more offers after the hero, so the focus gets diluted.
- Judgement: a strong hero and a smart calendar sit inside a cluttered body. It is better than the 2026-11-19 "Select Dyson is on sale" send, whose subject promised something the body never delivered. But it repeats the habit of stacking a free-sample banner above the real hero that the 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-19 sends had.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" hero is the largest element in the email, so the eye lands on it first.
- The Benefit product shot (Roller Lash, Benetint, Hoola) sits right below the offer and is visibly clickable.
- The "Great Deals Comin' In Hot" calendar lists a date and brands for each day through 11/30, which creates return visits.
- The purple sequin theme reads as Cyber Week and feels seasonal.
- The footer strip (free shipping, Sephora Texts, credit card, wish list, app) gives clear member and loyalty hooks.

## What's weak

- The sample-bag banner at the top competes with the hero. It also carries a "$95 min. spend" condition and an asterisk, so the first offer comes with a catch.
- The subject says "daily deals," but the hero is a single-brand 30% off. The calendar only shows brand names, with no discounts.
- Only 11/22 is tagged "App Exclusive," so the subject's exclusivity claim is weakly backed.
- Several CTAs are visible (SHOP THE APP, SHOP NOW, SEE ALL DEALS, BUY NOW, SHOP NOW, SHOP NOW), and the tail offers more (gift cards, up to 30% off, Kérastase, gift guide). That is too many.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is long and dense. The "Available on in-stock merchandise only" notes weaken the honesty of the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the hero. Drop the sample-bag banner, or move it below the calendar, so 30% off Benefit leads without a spend condition above it.
- 2. Show the discount for each day in the calendar tiles (for example "11/23: up to 25% off Olehenriksen"). Brand names alone give people little reason to come back.
- 3. Mark every app-exclusive day clearly, or take "exclusively on the app" out of the subject.
- 4. Cut the tail. Keep the calendar, the gift guide, and one service strip, and drop the gift cards, the "up to 30% off" tile, and Kérastase from this send.
- 5. Pick one CTA verb. Use "Shop Benefit 30% Off" and "See Daily Deals" instead of repeated generic SHOP NOW.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Benefit today, new deals daily 💜`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cyber Week: a new deal every day, starting now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Today only on the app: 30% off Benefit. See what drops 11/22–11/30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kenra, Olehenriksen, Laura Mercier and more, one day each.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a sound table-based Epsilon/Harmony structure with tracked redirect links. The only hard QA failure is a missing plain-text part, and several source-level rendering problems stand out: a fixed 700px layout, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and a malformed MSO comment inside `<style>`. The header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 material links were probed and 26 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The QA output reports no probe failures, but the 26 skipped links are unverified.
- Links use first-party redirect tracking at `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1ff7a5e1f97f474f4bbe5c898/…?__dU__=…`. This is expected for Epsilon, and the visible links show no malformed redirects.
- The hidden open-tracking pixel is at `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAn…`. It is styled `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, so it does not affect layout.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper table is `width:700px; min-width:700px` with no fluid fallback. The `@media (max-width:480px)` block only resizes a few classes (`.mobile`, `.mobileHeaderLogo`, `.mobileIcon`, …), so on phones the 700px table will likely force horizontal scroll or client-side scaling.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 resize text).
- **Malformed conditional comment:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. Clients that parse the CSS strictly can drop the rules that follow it (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, and others).
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no practical impact.
- **JSON-LD block:** it contains an empty `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type, which isn't a valid schema.org type. Most clients strip scripts, but Gmail's schema parser could reject or ignore the block.
- **Image alt text (QA WARN):** the flagged image is the hidden tracking pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…`). It has no `alt`, which is low impact. Add `alt=""` for hygiene.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The `__dU__` parameter is a per-recipient tracking ID, not a visible merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (WARN):** both are absent from the captured headers. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, so verify this on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** it is absent because of the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `beauty.sephora.com` is a subdomain, and alignment can't be checked from this data.
- **Plain-text part (FAIL):** the text version is 0 characters. This is a multipart/alternative deliverability and accessibility gap, and some filters score HTML-only mail higher for spam. It may also come from the relay's capture, so check the raw MIME.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm them. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but it reports no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided. The visible hrefs are opaque `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirects, so the final destinations and any UTM parameters are not exposed. No mismatch is confirmed. Resolve the redirect chains for the primary CTAs to check that UTMs survive and the landing pages match the "daily deals" offer.
