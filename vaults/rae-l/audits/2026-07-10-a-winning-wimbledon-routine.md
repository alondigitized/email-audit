# A winning Wimbledon routine
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- ILIA leans all the way into Wimbledon's closing weekend with a tight editorial concept — "shade. set. match." is one of the better subject-line puns the brand has deployed in this run, and the timing lands squarely in the tournament window. The email threads two jobs at once: showcasing sweat-proof complexion products (Skin Tint SPF 30, Serum Concealer, Setting Powder) with a heat-credible proof point, then recycling the mini tote GWP that launched July 6 as "the comeback tote" in a soft footer placement. The dual-job structure works well — the editorial lead earns attention and the GWP close converts it.
- What holds this back from a 9 is the same thing that held the July 9 "A mini mentality" send back: the GWP lives at the bottom of the email, below UGC and a social-proof pull quote, so it never anchors the hero. A subscriber who scans and drops out at the product grid misses the offer entirely. The UGC "Your Wimbledon moment?" interstitial adds social texture but also adds scroll distance before the close.
- Overall: this is a strong editorial execution on a timely cultural hook. It is not a conversion-maximizing send — it is a brand-building send with a soft offer appended. ILIA knows the difference, and the choice feels intentional given the "comeback tote" language is light-touch rather than urgent.

## What's working

- **"shade. set. match."** — the headline wordplay (tennis scoring + shade/setting powder) is genuinely clever and on-brand without being try-hard.
- **Pull quote is heat-credible.** "[my] base stayed fresh after 5hrs of watching tennis in the heat." is exactly the right proof point for this campaign; it sells wear-time without an ingredients lecture.
- **Three products are named and visually matched.** Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 30, Skin Blur Serum Concealer, Soft Focus Setting Powder — the routine is clear.
- **Seasonal timing is tight.** Wimbledon closing weekend, July 10 — this email would feel stale by July 14. Sending now is right.
- **"The comeback tote" framing is smart.** Reintroducing the mini GWP without rerunning the July 6 launch copy shows cadence awareness.

## What's weak

- **GWP offer is buried below the fold.** The tote + $75 threshold only appears at the very bottom, after the UGC block. The subscriber who bounces after the product grid never sees the incentive.
- **"SHOP YOUR GAME FACE" CTA is punny, not directive.** A subscriber in buying mode wants clarity on what they're clicking into — this reads as editorial, not commerce.
- **The UGC "Your Wimbledon moment?" block breaks the funnel.** It adds scroll depth without adding persuasive weight between the product grid and the GWP close.
- **No urgency signal.** Wimbledon ends in days, but the email doesn't say so. "The tournament ends Sunday" or a soft deadline on the GWP would add legitimate pressure.
- **The top banner is unreadable at mobile render size.** Whatever copy is up there is effectively invisible — that's dead real estate.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the GWP offer up.** Place "Free mini tote with $75+" in a slim banner directly below the hero — don't make subscribers scroll to the comeback tote section to find the reason to buy.
- 2. **Add Wimbledon deadline urgency.** One line under the CTA: "Tournament ends Sunday. So does this." — earns the clock without being aggressive.
- 3. **Tighten the UGC block.** Cut "Your Wimbledon moment?" to two photos max or remove it entirely. The pull quote already handles social proof; the UGC section is redundant and adds scroll cost.
- 4. **Rewrite the CTA.** "Build the routine →" or "Shop the full routine" is clearer than "SHOP YOUR GAME FACE." Save the pun for the subject; the button should convert.
- 5. **Fix the top banner.** Either make it large enough to read or replace it with the GWP offer line.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free mini tote with $75+, while the tournament lasts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The SPF skin tint, concealer, and setting powder that go the distance`
