# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email. It is stronger than the browse-abandonment sends that have filled the last two weeks (10/17 "Don't leave these styles behind!", 10/18 "Pick up where you left off!", 10/19 "This style has limited availability!"). The subject "Did you forget something?" and the hero "READY TO CHECK OUT?" with a black "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button agree with each other. The single product shown large, the Slip-ins GO WALK Workout Walker, reads as the item left in the cart. That is the clearest hierarchy Skechers has sent in this series.
- The offer is also real. The navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF AND EARN A $5 REWARD ON $75+" banner is valid through 10/26, which is an extension of the 10/21 deadline on the 10/17 send. But it sits below the fold, under six unrelated shoes and a "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar. It is not tied to the hero product, and the hero doesn't mention it. The footer (nav list, texting, app, BNPL, social) is generic.
- Judgement: a good trigger with the incentive in the wrong place. Moving the 20% to the top would turn this from a reminder into a reason to buy now.

## What's working

- The hero "READY TO CHECK OUT?" matches the subject and gives a clear intent to complete the purchase.
- One large product image, the Slip-ins GO WALK Workout Walker, is named and sits under an orange "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button, so the eye lands on it first.
- The navy banner pairs a big "20% OFF" with a $5 reward on $75+, and gives the deadline "Valid thru 10/26".
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or gaps.
- The gold-foil hero panel looks seasonal and festive.

## What's weak

- The 20% member offer sits roughly two-thirds of the way down. Someone who reads only the hero never sees it.
- The six-shoe grid dilutes the cart reminder. Floral, patent and pink-leather styles have nothing to do with the left-behind sneaker, and the grid is a near-copy of the earlier browse-abandonment sends.
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button appears twice in quick succession, in black and then orange, with different styling. That is redundant and looks inconsistent.
- The hero product caption has no price, size, colour or stock cue, so there's nothing to answer "why now?"
- The email has no reviews or social proof, and no returns or shipping reassurance, which are the usual blockers at checkout.
- The long nav list and footer blocks (app, curbside, BNPL, texting) compete with the checkout path, and the fine print is dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner directly under the hero, and say "Your cart is waiting, plus 20% off for members through 10/26." Show the member price on the hero shoe.
- 2. Cut the six-shoe grid to a row of three "You may also like" items that match the cart item, or remove it. Drop one of the two "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" buttons.
- 3. Add a trust line under the hero: free returns, a star rating, and a size or low-stock cue.
- 4. Collapse the nav list and the app, curbside and BNPL blocks into a slim footer.
- 5. Vary the cadence. If the cart is still open, send a later reminder with a different angle, such as low stock or a reviews quote, rather than the same shell.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your GO WALK is waiting, plus 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? Members save 20%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% through 10/26. Finish checkout before it ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Slip-ins GO WALK is still in your cart. Free returns on every order.`
