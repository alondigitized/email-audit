# NEW SUNSCREEN 💦 ☀️ 🏖️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's summer sunscreen launch catalog — a wide-net product discovery email with the WKND Clean Whip SPF 30 Sunscreen ($39) as the nominal hero. The subject sets a single clear expectation ("NEW SUNSCREEN") and the email mostly delivers on it, but immediately undercuts itself by opening with a "Get a Mini" fragrance cross-sell before the sunscreen content begins. - The format is editorial catalog: many products, many sections, no single compelling offer driving urgency. Compare this to the July 19 "4X points on all hair care ‼️" send (scored 9/10 here) — that one had a tight loyalty mechanic that made the click feel inevitable. ## What's working

- **WKND sunscreen is properly spotlighted** — name, SPF rating, "Clean Whip" descriptor, and $39 price all visible at a glance.
- **Seasonal timing is flawless** — a new sunscreen launch dropping late July is the right moment in the calendar.
- **"From the most popular reviews" copy** in the hero provides soft social credibility without overpromising.
- **Clean render throughout** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum anywhere visible.
- **$8.10 Auto-Replenish deal** is a concrete, scannable price anchor even if it arrives late in the scroll.

## What's weak

- **"Get a Mini" fragrance cross-sell is the first thing you see** — directly contradicts the sunscreen subject line and dilutes the opening punch.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the primary CTA everywhere** — generic, undifferentiated across eight products; gives the subscriber no reason to click one over another.
- **No loyalty points hook** — the July 19 and July 22 sends proved 4X points mechanics drive urgency; this email abandons that lever entirely.
- **The email runs too long** — sunscreen grid, Ka'oir launches, dry hair section, and the auto-replenish deal are four separate emails wearing one subject line.
- **ALL CAPS subject** ("NEW SUNSCREEN") reads like a bargain-bin blast, not a premium beauty brand's voice.

- 1. **Cut or relocate the fragrance cross-sell** — open with the sunscreen hero. The first visual should match the subject line, full stop. The "Get a Mini" offer belongs in the footer or a PS line.
- 2. **Make WKND the undeniable single hero** — full-width image, one bold benefit line ("The internet's favorite clean SPF. Now at Sephora."), CTA that says "Shop WKND →" not "SHOP NOW."
- 3. **Add a loyalty mechanic to the hero** — even a simple "Earn 4X points on all SPF this weekend, Beauty Insiders" line in the hero section transforms this from a browse email into an act-now email.
- 4. **Trim the catalog to 4 products max** — lead with WKND, pick 3 supporting SPF launches. Cut Ka'oir and dry hair entirely; save those for their own sends.
- 5. **Lower-case the subject** — "New sunscreen ☀️" is cleaner and more premium than "NEW SUNSCREEN 💦 ☀️ 🏖️."

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents users from zooming in on text. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Should be removed; email clients that need zoom restriction handle it natively.

**Broad `text-size-adjust: none` declaration (confirmed)**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applied across virtually every element. Suppresses OS-level font scaling for low-vision users. Acceptable to apply to `td` for Outlook rendering control, but applying to `body` and `a` breaks accessibility in mobile webmail.

Both `.show` and `.appear` each contain duplicate `display: block!important; display: table!important;` declarations. Harmless but indicative of template bloat; the second declaration in each pair is a no-op.

### 5. Compliance

**`robots` meta value has an invalid space (confirmed)**

The correct directive is `noindex` (no space). `"no index"` is not a recognized token in the Robots meta spec; some crawlers will ignore it and may index the email's web version. Should be `content="noindex, nofollow"`.
