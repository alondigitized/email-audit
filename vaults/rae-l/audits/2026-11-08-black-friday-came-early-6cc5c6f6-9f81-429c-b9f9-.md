# Black Friday came early 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer Early Black Friday send. The black hero carries a big "40% off" on "Select styling tools" with a white Shop now button, so the eye lands on the offer first. Four hero products sit below it on a cream panel: two dryers, a GVP dryer and a cordless flat iron. The page is tidy and easy to scan.
- It's the third Black Friday push in eight days, and the offer shifts each time. The 11/01 and 11/06 sends led with BOGO 50% off on the Big Bottle Sale. This one switches to 40% off tools, so the "early" framing now covers a different deal. The subject also promises Black Friday "came early" when the body is still labeled "Early Black Friday Deals." That's consistent, but the hero never says which deals or for how long.
- Overall it's a decent, competent send held back by thin detail. There's no deadline, no sale or original prices, and "select" tools leaves the scope vague. Nothing here is tailored to a hair-color or beauty shopper either, because this is a tools category.

## What's working

- The "40% off" hero is huge and high-contrast, and the white Shop now button is clear.
- The four product tiles are cleanly laid out with brand names (ION, Tool Science, GVP, ION Luxe) and a product name on each.
- The "The tools behind holiday hair." line ties the offer to the season.
- The Klarna strip and the icon row (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, pick up in-store) lower purchase friction.
- The top red banners promote fast delivery and in-store pickup, which are real conveniences.

## What's weak

- There are no prices at all, neither sale nor original. 40% off a dryer means nothing without a number.
- No deadline or end date appears in the render. The fine print only says "order by 4pm."
- The "Select styling tools" wording is vague, and the tiles don't show a discount badge or savings.
- Every product tile uses a generic underlined "Shop now" link. Naming the product or price would be more specific.
- The two red banners above the hero compete with the main offer and push the hero lower.
- There are no reviews, ratings or loyalty/Rewards benefits visible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add sale price and was-price (or "$X off") under each product, and put a "Ends [date]" line in the hero.
- 2. Replace the generic "Shop now" links with product-specific ones such as "Get the dryer, $XX".
- 3. Drop one of the two top red banners so the hero starts higher.
- 4. Add star ratings or a "best seller" tag to the products.
- 5. Add a Sally Beauty Rewards line, such as bonus points on tools.
