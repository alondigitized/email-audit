# 20% off all lip + cheek favorites
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's fifth consecutive VIP-sale send, and it's a return to full production values after 09-14's design-less collapse ("This could be your moment, friend") — a hero shot, styled flatlays, a real product grid, and a cat-adjacent lifestyle photo are all back. But the offer itself keeps re-shuffling the same pieces we've now seen four times: 20% off, VIP-gated, plus stacked GWPs (free Multi-Stick, free bag at $100+). The email is well-built but not well-differentiated — the "the vip suite" / "+complimentary upgrades" / "very. important. perks." structure is a clean funnel, but a reader of this thread will recognize it's the same sale wearing a new subject line.
- The lip-and-cheek framing is the first real category narrowing in this run, which is smart — it's more scannable than "everything's on sale" — but the products shown (lip gloss, lip crayon, blush, tinted balm) are generic without swatches, shade names, or a face wearing them, so it reads more like a catalog page than a persuasive push.
- Verdict: competently executed, zero novelty. Fine as the fifth beat in a sale sequence, not a standout send.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand visual system — consistent serif "ILIA" wordmark, muted rose/terracotta palette, no render bugs or broken images.
- Hero band clearly states "20% OFF SITEWIDE FOR VIPS" with a circular "20% off" badge — the offer is visible before any scrolling.
- Product grid pairs each item with a name, one-line benefit, and its own "SHOP NOW" button — CTAs are plentiful and unambiguous.
- The GWP section (free Multi-Stick, free bag at $100+) is visually called out with handwritten-style annotations, making the bonus perks easy to spot.

## What's weak

- No shade swatches, no face/model wearing the lip or cheek products — for a "lip + cheek favorites" email, that's the one piece of visual proof missing.
- Subject says "20% off all lip + cheek favorites" but the hero banner says "20% OFF SITEWIDE" — a small but real mismatch between subject promise and hero copy.
- The "20% off" claim is VIP-membership-gated (per body copy: "Want the key to 20% off... plus a free Multi-Stick"), but that gate isn't disclosed until well into the email — feels like a soft bait-and-switch for non-members skimming the top.
- Five sends in six days with the same 20%-off VIP mechanic; this one adds nothing new to the offer structure, just a narrower category framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/hero mismatch — either make the hero say "lip + cheek" explicitly, or change the subject to "sitewide" language so the promise matches instantly.
- 2. Add one lifestyle/beauty shot of the lip + cheek products actually applied (swatch strip or on-face) — right now every product is shown in packaging only, which undersells the color category this email is supposed to be about.
- 3. Move the VIP-gating disclosure up — state "VIP members get 20% off" plainly at the top instead of implying a universal discount that turns out to be membership-locked.
- 4. Vary the offer mechanic for the next send in this arc — a bundle price or "buy 2 get 1" would break the sameness better than another %-off + GWP stack.
