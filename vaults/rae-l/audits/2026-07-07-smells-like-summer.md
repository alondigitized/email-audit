# 🌴 Smells like summer
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- "Smells like summer" is a three-chapter editorial send — Sweet Summer Things, Cooling Mint Chocolate, Zest Is Best — capped with a persistent Sanrio cross-sell footer module. The yellow/citrus palette is clean and the July timing is right. The problem is the entire email is atmosphere with no promotional anchor: no discount, no deadline, no member perk. After six emails in twelve days — including a near-identical mint editorial on June 28 that scored a 9 — this send risks feeling like content wallpaper rather than a reason to act.
- The Sanrio module buried in the footer is the clearest sign of creative fatigue. This is the third send featuring that collab (June 18, June 30, July 7). By the third appearance it registers as furniture, not a call to action.
- Verdict: well-produced, seasonally on-point, but forgettable without an offer and too soon after the June 28 mint send to feel fresh.

## What's working

- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — the yellow citrus hero loads strong; three editorial chapters are clearly delineated with distinct section headers
- **Seasonal alignment is exact** — citrus and mint-cooling for July heat is the right move
- **Multiple product tiles give browse depth** — named products (Magic Crystals, Posh Chocolate, Happy Hippo, Lime Bounty) with short copy let the eye find something to linger on
- **Brand voice is consistent** — "Zest Is Best" fits squarely in the pun register Lush has built across the last six sends
- **No render bugs** — layout, imagery, and CTA buttons all rendered cleanly

## What's weak

- **No promotional offer visible** — full-price editorial only, no % off, no code, no threshold — the strongest conversion lever is missing entirely
- **Cooling Mint section retreads June 28's "Mint Condition" send** — any subscriber who opened that 9/10 email has already had the mint pitch; retreading it 9 days later as a sub-chapter undersells both
- **"Dirty" appears in two separate product sections** — the same product name in two chapters one scroll apart makes the catalog feel thin
- **Sanrio footer is the third appearance in three weeks** — it's no longer a feature, it's a boilerplate module; the June 30 re-send already showed diminishing returns
- **Subject is the least specific of recent Lush sends** — "Smells like summer" is a looser hook than "I've got chills 🧊" or the shower-gummy subject that opened with a community data point

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one concrete offer** — even free shipping above a threshold or a free sample with purchase gives subscribers a reason to click today rather than bookmark indefinitely
- 2. **Retire the Sanrio footer module** — three appearances in three weeks is overexposure; save a standalone re-engagement send for late July or cut it
- 3. **Resolve the Dirty duplicate** — assign it to one chapter and fill the other slot with something fresh
- 4. **Sharpen the subject to a pun or specific product name** — Lush has proven it can write better hooks than this
