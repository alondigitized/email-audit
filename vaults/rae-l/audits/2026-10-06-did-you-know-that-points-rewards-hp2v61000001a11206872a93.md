# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a Rewards Bazaar catalog with a thin Insider Challenge strip on top. The subject asks "Did you know that points = rewards?" The email answers it with "October rewards are here!" and a long list of redeemable items. The Laura Mercier spotlight and the 750+ point rewards grid make it a browse-and-redeem email, not a promotion. Insiders sitting on a points balance will find it useful. Everyone else gets no price offer.
- There are two competing pitches. The top banner ("Don't Miss Out on up to 500 Points*, Join the Challenge") is an earn message. Right below it, the Bazaar hero is a spend message. Both have a button, so the eye has to choose between them. After the hero, the email becomes a very long scroll of near-identical reward tiles with point costs and "available now" counts. The scarcity counts (250, 150, 12, 14, 1 available) add real urgency. The cost is a lot of scrolling and a dense footer.
- It lands as a loyalty-engagement send for existing members, not a conversion driver. Compared with the 10/6 "2X points… ends tomorrow" send earlier today, this one is calmer but just as busy. A subscriber now has two points-themed emails in one day.

## What's working

- The "October rewards are here!" hero with a clear SHOP NOW button, and a "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE" badge, makes the purpose obvious.
- Each reward tile shows its point cost and a live "available now" count, which gives credible scarcity.
- The Laura Mercier spotlight gives a recognizable brand a clear anchor and keeps the middle of the email organized.
- Sephora Insider branding and the Beauty Insider card imagery are consistent and trusted.
- The images render cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- Two competing CTAs sit at the top: JOIN THE CHALLENGE and SHOP NOW. The earn message dilutes the redeem message.
- There is no discount or dollar value. A point cost alone doesn't tell a member what a reward is worth.
- The body is a long scroll of similar tiles with small, dense copy. It's hard to tell which reward matters most.
- The Laura Mercier spotlight is well below the hero, so the strongest brand draw is buried.
- The footer legal block is very small and dense, which makes the fine print hard to read.
- It arrives the same day as the 2X points email, so the cadence feels heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job. Lead with the Bazaar and move the Insider Challenge to a single line lower down, or send it separately.
- 2. Add a "your points" element to the hero, such as a balance or a "redeem at 250 points" line, so members see what they can afford.
- 3. Cut the grid to 4 to 6 rewards, led by the scarcest or best-value ones. Give every tile a real button, not just text.
- 4. Show a value cue per reward ("$40 value") so points feel like currency.
- 5. Space this send away from the 2X points email by at least a couple of days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Rewards Bazaar drops: 250 points gets you Laura Mercier`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Only 1 left: the NYC makeup experience, for points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Laura Mercier, hair-spa kits and more. Some rewards have under 15 left.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insider points can cover these new October rewards. Shop before they sell out.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 4 images):**
  - `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`
  - `20261006_Sneak_Peek_1_03.jpg`
  - `20261006_Sneak_Peek_1_09.jpg`
  
  Without alt attributes, screen readers will typically read out the filename or URL. With images blocked, the content in the two Sneak_Peek images disappears. The tracking pixel should have `alt=""`.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1" />`:** this blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **`<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute:** screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation rules.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`:** the valid token is `noindex`. This is harmless in email but is malformed.
- **`<title>Sephora</title>`:** generic. It should be the subject or a descriptive title for web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Leftover `<!-- US shell snippet -->` comment** in `<head>`. It's cosmetic.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This stops users from scaling text on iOS.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so tokens in the body and footer could not be checked.

Not enough evidence. The truncated source and QA output don't expose destination URLs or UTM parameters. The `app.sephora.com/O/...` path suggests click-wrapped redirects, so landing-page parameters can't be verified here.
