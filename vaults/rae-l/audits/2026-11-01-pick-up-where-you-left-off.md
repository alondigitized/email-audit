# Pick up where you left off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment email, sent again. The hero says "DID YOU FORGET SOMETHING?" with a blue SHOP NOW button. Six women's leather sneakers sit below it. The 20% off for Skechers Plus members is the strongest offer, but it sits in a navy band below the product grid, so the eye hits the generic headline first. The subject, "Pick up where you left off!", is a new line for what is otherwise the same "Did you forget something?" creative from 10/22, 10/29 and 10/30.
- The email is clean and renders without bugs. It is also generic. The six products are all women's sneakers, so the persona match is reasonable, but there are no prices, no reviews and no hint of why these shoes were chosen. The "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" Halloween line is stale on 11/1 and sits next to a "Valid thru 11/2" deadline, which is tomorrow.
- The offer is real, but it is buried in the layout and tucked behind fine print. This is a competent reminder that does little beyond reminding. Compared with the 10/31 "Don't leave these styles behind!" send (8/10), it is weaker on offer clarity.

## What's working

- The 20% off for Skechers Plus members is specific and member-gated, with a "Valid thru 11/2" deadline.
- The hero is clean and readable, with a high-contrast blue SHOP NOW button.
- The six-product grid shows a variety of colors and styles in the women's category.
- The category nav (WOMEN, MEN, KIDS, CLOTHING, NEW ARRIVALS, SALE) gives easy exits.
- The footer utility blocks (app, curbside, Afterpay/Klarna, store finder) are tidy and consistent with the brand.

## What's weak

- The offer sits below the product grid, and the hero never mentions it.
- "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" is Halloween language on November 1.
- Product tiles show names only, with no prices, sale prices or ratings, so nothing shows the 20% savings.
- The hero copy repeats the 10/22, 10/29 and 10/30 sends, and the subject says something different from the body.
- The deadline text ("Valid thru 11/2. See details below*") is small and low-contrast on the navy band, and the fine print requires Skechers Plus login.
- The product names are underlined and small, and the images are not clearly framed as tappable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus offer into the hero: "20% OFF the styles you viewed, Plus members, ends tomorrow." Keep the product grid right below it.
- 2. Show the original and discounted prices on each product tile, and add star ratings.
- 3. Make the first tile the actual last-viewed item and label it "You viewed this".
- 4. Retire the Halloween language once the holiday has passed, and use a time-bound line such as "Ends tomorrow, 11/2".
- 5. Add a "Not a Plus member? Join free" line to the offer band.
- 6. Rotate hero copy so the subject and body match.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off the styles you viewed, ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your picks are still here, Plus members save 20%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members get 20% off through 11/2. Your saved styles are waiting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ins, Arch Fit and more, now 20% off for Plus members.`
