# New to rewards? Get $5 off $25
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand loyalty-signup send, but it is the fourth push of the same $5 off $25 offer in under three weeks (10/02, 10/11, 10/17, now 10/19). The hero is clear: a pink block, "Join Sally.Rewards & save on your wish list," a black "Join Sally.Rewards" button, and a lifestyle photo of two women with Sally tote bags. The offer is stated plainly in the subhead. It then gets diluted by a second tier explainer and a product grid that doesn't connect to the offer.
- The middle of the email is the weak spot. The Preferred vs. Elite comparison is tidy, but it is a lot of loyalty program explanation for a $5 coupon. The "Use Your Reward on These Favorites" grid shows hair color and developer, which doesn't match a "wish list" or holiday framing. Those products pull the eye away from the join action.
- Verdict: competent and readable, but repetitive. It is a mid-range send that will mostly get opened by people who ignored the last three.

## What's working

- The hero offer is stated in one line: "$5 off $25+ in October."
- The black "Join Sally.Rewards" button contrasts well against the pink.
- The two-tier Preferred/Elite comparison is scannable and shows free entry.
- The lifestyle photo with branded totes feels warm and on-brand.
- The BOPIS and 2-hour delivery banners at the top give a useful secondary hook.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth near-identical $5 off $25 join push in about three weeks, so it reads as fatigue.
- The product grid (hair color, developer, lightener) has no prices or reward tie-in, so "Use Your Reward" is vague.
- The fine print is long and dense. It buries the exclusions (no tax, gift cards, or prior purchases).
- There are two identical join CTAs plus the top banner links, which splits attention.
- "Save on your wish list" doesn't match the hair-color products shown below.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the same offer. Either escalate it (for example $10 off $40 for new members) or suppress it for people who were already sent this one and didn't convert.
- 2. Tie the product grid to the offer. Show "$5 off" price math, such as "$22 → $17 with your reward," on each product.
- 3. Cut the tier comparison to a single line, or move it below the product grid, so the join button and the products stay close together.
- 4. Give the fine print a short, plain-language summary at the top, with the legal text below it.
- 5. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends 10/31," next to the button.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with mobile media queries, but it carries a literal unreplaced `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a plaintext email address in an image URL, and an insecure `http://` tracking pixel. Compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the relay did not capture headers, and the HTML provided is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` contains the literal placeholder `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the cache-busting and impression-dedup value is lost. This is a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` uses plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail may block or rewrite it, so this Dotomi (Epsilon) sync pixel may not fire. It may also trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** There are at least four tracking beacons besides the ESP's own links: Evergage, `pxl.mon-trk.com`, myvisualiq and Dotomi. Each adds a failure point and a privacy surface.
- **Unverified links:** 50 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the probe (`[WARN] 50 tracking link(s) skipped`). Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** The four Evergage dynamic images (`.../campaign/pLpNA/HxBq1/1/summary.png` through `/4/summary.png`) have no alt attribute. These look like product recommendation tiles, so images-off clients and screen readers get no content. This is the most significant accessibility issue.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (mon-trk), `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` have no alt. These are decorative, so they should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Screen readers may otherwise announce the filename or URL.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless but means the intended font never loads.
- **Dark mode:** The visible CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` handling. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm this.
- **No issues found** with the viewport meta, MSO conditionals or VML namespaces in the visible portion. The `.mobileHide`/`.stack` classes are present at `max-width:499px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **PII in a URL:** The Evergage image URLs include `userId=rae-l@ete...` (truncated in the QA output), which is a plaintext recipient email address in a query string. It leaks to the Evergage/Salesforce image host and any proxy or log that sees the request, and it may be forwarded with the email. Hashing the ID would avoid this.
- **Unreplaced token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is covered in section 2.
- No other unreplaced `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`-style tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** The QA check notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation. Sally Beauty is a major sender, so the header is likely present upstream.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders, so it should be checked against the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay strips the header. The sender domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Both are likely below the truncation point, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 50 tracking links were not resolved, and the visible source contains no landing URLs or UTM parameters. The destination page and its UTM values for the "$5 off $25" offer are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ad-server macro so `r=[CACHEBUSTER]` resolves at send time, or remove the myvisualiq pixel.
2. Change the Dotomi pixel to `https://login.dotomi.com/...`, and confirm Dotomi supports HTTPS.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the four Evergage `summary.png` images. Use dynamic alt text with the product name if the platform supports it.
4. Add `alt=""` (and `width="1" height="1"`) to the tracking pixels.
5. Stop putting the raw email address in the Evergage `userId` parameter. Use an opaque or hashed ID.
6. Check the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. If this is a capture limitation, record it as a known blind spot in the pipeline.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML with link resolution enabled, to check UTM consistency, the unsubscribe link and the footer address.
