# Beauty that’s found only here ❤️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a three-offer email with no clear winner. The top banner has the 4X points deal on moisturizers (code 4XHYDRATE, ends 10/21). Directly below it is a "FREE* Sample Bag" with a $35+ purchase. Then comes a giant "ONLY AT SEPHORA" hero for Huda Beauty's first fragrance. The subject, "Beauty that's found only here ❤️", only matches the hero. The two offers people would act on, points and free samples, are squeezed into the top 15% of the email.
- The product work further down is good. There's a tidy grid of eight "New • Only at Sephora" items with prices and Clean badges, and the Huda Beauty fragrance hero is lush. But it's the same "only here" story we got on 10/13 ("MAKEUP SETS: HERE & NOWHERE ELSE"), and the 4XHYDRATE code is a repeat from the 10/18 Clean at Sephora send. A regular subscriber has seen both beats this week.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand catalog send with a vague subject and too many competing modules. The Google Pay block and a render clip in the sample-bag banner hurt it further. Probably a 6 or 7 in a real inbox.

## What's working

- The 4X points offer sits at the very top with a clear deadline (ends 10/21) and a code, so the member benefit is visible without scrolling.
- The Huda Beauty hero is striking. The magenta glove and bottle photo is strong, and the copy gives the scent notes (wild cherry, white florals, vanilla bourbon) and a price range ($30–$79).
- The "Here and Nowhere Else" grid is easy to scan. Each item has a badge, name, descriptor and price, and the Clean badges are consistent.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." modules (Caudalie, Lip's Latest Lineup) add category variety for a beauty-first reader.
- The Beauty Insider strip and the in-store barcode with the 4X code make the offer easy to redeem.

## What's weak

- The sample-bag banner is clipped on the right edge. "WITH $35+ PURCHASE" and the "*Exclusions/terms appl…" line are cut off, so the purchase threshold is partly unreadable.
- There are three competing heroes (4X points, free sample bag, Huda fragrance), and the biggest block is the least actionable one.
- "Pick Your FREE* Sample Bag" is a purchase-required offer with an asterisk. The $35+ condition is small and half-clipped, which reads as buried fine print.
- The subject is vague. "Beauty that's found only here" doesn't name the fragrance, the points or the sample bag, and the ❤️ adds nothing.
- The Google Pay block is off-theme and pulls attention from the beauty content. The CTA copy is generic throughout ("SHOP NOW", "SHOP TO REDEEM").

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with either the Huda Beauty fragrance (the newness story) or the 4X points offer. Move the other to a slim banner and cut the third.
- 2. Fix the sample-bag banner clipping and put the $35+ threshold in readable type next to the headline.
- 3. Rewrite the subject to name the product or the deadline. The fragrance launch is the most specific hook in the email.
- 4. Replace the generic CTAs with verb-plus-noun copy, for example "Shop Huda's Easy Bake" or "Earn 4X on Moisturizers".
- 5. Drop or relocate the Google Pay module. Put a ratings or "best seller" line on the grid items for social proof.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty plain-text part [FAIL]:** The text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` alternative. Clients and filters that rely on it get nothing, and a missing text part is a negative spam-filter signal.
- **Missing alt on `US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode.jpg`** (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode`): This is the most significant alt gap. A barcode or promo image is functional content, and with images off or a screen reader the offer or code is unavailable.
- **Missing alt on `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`:** If this is the navigation header image, the nav links are unusable with images blocked. It needs alt text, or live text links.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">`:** This blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none`:** These are applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. They disable user and browser text-size scaling. `auto` or `100%` is the usual safe value.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index">`:** The value is invalid. The correct token is `noindex`, so as written it may be ignored.
- **`<title>Sephora</title>`:** It is generic and doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Mobile CSS selectors:** The rules use exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=hide]`. They fail on elements with more than one class (for example `class="hide text_ctr"`). The `.hide` fallback in the same rules mitigates this, so the risk is low.
