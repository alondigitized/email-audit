- This is the plain-text collapse again — the second one in three days after 09-14's "This could be your moment, friend." No logo, no hero image, no product photography, no button, nothing but black text and blue hyperlinks on white. The "browsing the VIP sale" abandoned-cart framing is a smart, personalized hook on paper, but it's completely undercut by an execution that looks like a fallback text-alert, not a beauty brand email.
- The copy itself is actually decent — the "here's your key" framing, the bullet-listed perks, the specific product callouts (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Limitless Lash Mascara) show someone thought about persuasion. - The "browsing the VIP sale?" abandoned-browse hook is a genuinely personalized, non-generic subject/opening.
- Bulleted offer structure (20% off, free Multi-Stick, free Velvet Bag at $100+) is scannable and specific.
- Named product callouts (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Limitless Lash Mascara) give concrete reasons to click.
- Fine print at the bottom is honest and complete — exclusions, end date (09.22.26), no bait-and-switch language.

## What's weak

- Zero visual design: no logo, no hero image, no color, no button — just text and hyperlinks.
- No brand presence at all — this could be from any company; nothing signals "ILIA" visually.
- Primary CTA is a plain text hyperlink ("Sign up for free + shop the sale") instead of a button — easy to miss, weak visual hierarchy.
- No product imagery despite naming two specific hero products — a huge missed opportunity given this is a beauty brand.
- This is the second unstyled/design-less send in the last 4 days (09-14 had the same failure) — looks like a recurring template/rendering issue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix whatever is causing intermittent plain-text sends — this is the second occurrence in 4 days (09-14, 09-17) in an otherwise fully-designed campaign stream; treat as a QA/template regression, not a one-off.
- 2. Add a hero image of the Super Serum Skin Tint + Limitless Lash Mascara (the two named products) so the "browsing" hook has something visual to convert on.
- 3. Convert the text CTA into an actual button with contrasting color — "Sign up for free + shop the sale" as a link is too passive for a bottom-of-funnel abandoned-browse email.
- 4. Add the maroon/blush brand palette and logo used in 09-15/09-16 sends to restore recognizability.
