# 50% off select beauty starts today 💋
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Daily Deals" catalog send — a bold pink "GET 50% OFF" hero gives way to a "Today Only" purple product block (Clinique, Lawless, Skinfix), then a full three-week calendar of daily deals, then a hair-tools/Afterpay cross-sell, then an OLIVE YOUNG teaser, then the standard Beauty Insider footer. It's dense but at least the hero offer is legible and the pricing is visible with actual strikethrough numbers, which puts it a notch above the pure-catalog-scroll sends like the 2026-08-26 "Must-Haves" email (5/10).
- The "50% OFF" superscript-a is a red flag before you even read the fine print — the footer confirms it's carved up by exclusions and date ranges (8/26–8/29, "select products"), which is the classic Sephora daily-deals bait: a huge headline number that applies to almost nothing you'll actually see in the email. The three-week "Deals Drop Daily" calendar is also a repeat structural beat from the App Fest emails (2026-08-17, 2026-08-24) — different mechanic, same "look how much content we crammed in" approach.
- Net: solid production value, real prices, no render bugs, but it's another Sephora send leaning on a vague headline discount over a distinct hook. Middle of the pack.

## What's working

- The hero "GET 50% OFF" is impossible to miss and sits above the fold with a clear "SHOP NOW" CTA directly beneath it.
- Actual product images with real strikethrough pricing (Clinique $12.50, Lawless $23.50, Skinfix $14.50) — this beats emails that hide behind vague banner copy with no visible price.
- Clean color-blocked sections (purple → lavender-blue gradient → hair tools/Afterpay) make it easy to scan even though it's a long scroll.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The 50% figure is footnoted with a superscript "a" right in the hero — visually signals "there's a catch" before the reader even scrolls, which undercuts trust.
- Three full weeks of "Deals Drop Daily" products are shown with no prices at all, just thumbnails — it's a wall of merchandise with zero information to act on.
- The email covers five distinct promos (daily deals, hair tools, Afterpay 10%, Olive Young, Beauty Insider) with no clear single priority — attention gets split across too many CTAs.
- "Today Only" urgency is immediately contradicted by three more weeks of deals shown right below it, which muddies the time-bound claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number next to "50% OFF" in the hero itself (e.g., "Up to 50% off Clinique, Lawless & Skinfix") so the offer isn't divorced from the fine print until the footer.
- 2. Cut the Week 2/Week 3 preview blocks — they add scroll length without adding information (no prices, no names) and dilute the "Today Only" urgency of the top block.
- 3. Move Afterpay/hair-tools/Olive Young below a single hard cutoff or into a "more from Sephora" section so the primary CTA isn't competing with three other offers before the footer.
- 4. Tighten the exclusions language or move it visibly closer to the 50% headline rather than only in dense footer copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off Clinique, Lawless & Skinfix`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your daily deal just dropped — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping for Beauty Insiders, all week long`
