# Holiday glow, without the gray
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built hair color send. The hero promises "100% gray coverage for holiday-ready hair" over a big, honest photo of a woman mid-application. It's seasonal, specific and clearly about one product category. That's stronger than the 10/15 "Check off your holiday list early" send, which had no real offer in the hero.
- The offer stack is the weak point. The hero has no price or deadline. The real deals sit in a thin red strip ("$10 off $50 or $20 off $75, code SPOOKY, ends 10/27") and in a BOGO 50% block below the fold ("Ends October 24"). Both offers are small and far from the headline. A Sally Rewards bar also sits on top, which is the same $5 off $25 push from 10/02, 10/11, 10/17 and 10/19. The SPOOKY code is also odd under a holiday-glow theme.
- Judgement: good creative, muddled offer hierarchy. It's a solid 6, and it would be 7 or 8 if the hero led with the BOGO deal.

## What's working

- The hero headline names a concrete benefit ("100% gray coverage") and ties it to the season.
- The large photo of a real person applying color shows the use case at once, and the "no salon required" line adds a reason to buy.
- The BOGO 50% block on Inspired by Nature by ion has a clear product shot, a deadline and a "Shop now" link.
- The black "Shop now" button on deep red stands out and is easy to find.
- The Klarna strip and the icon row (free shipping $40+, 2 hour delivery, BOPIS) give some reassurance without crowding the design.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. The BOGO deal, the actual reason to click, is below the photo.
- Three offers compete: the $5 off $25 rewards bar, the SPOOKY code strip, and the BOGO. The SPOOKY strip is a Halloween code in a holiday-themed email.
- The rewards bar is the fifth appearance of the $5 off $25 message in about three weeks, so it reads as noise.
- The deadlines clash: 10/27 for the code, 10/24 for the BOGO, and 10/31 in the fine print. A reader can't tell which applies.
- The footer fine print is a long wall of text, and the top strips are small and hard to read on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the BOGO 50% offer into the hero. Use something like "Gray coverage, half off the second box" with "Ends tomorrow" and a "Shop hair color" button.
- 2. Cut the offer stack to one primary offer and one quiet secondary. Drop the SPOOKY strip, or fold it into the BOGO block. Rotate the rewards bar out for a send or two.
- 3. Make the button copy specific, such as "Shop gray coverage" or "Get BOGO 50% off".
- 4. Show two or three products with shade names or review stars in the product block, not one tube pair.
- 5. Use one consistent deadline across the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off hair color, ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cover grays at home. 2nd box 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Inspired by Nature by ion, buy one get one half off through Oct 24.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Salon-quality gray coverage, delivered or ready in 2 hours.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, a mobile `@media (max-width: 499px)` block and Open Sans web fonts. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 issues, 3 warnings). The confirmed defects are in the third-party tracking pixels, and the header warnings are unverified because of the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so body links, UTM parameters and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** The check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Unsubstituted macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883…`. The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never replaced, so the cache-buster is ineffective. Clients may cache the request and under-count impressions. Some clients or proxies may also reject the unencoded brackets.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is an HTTP pixel and may be blocked or rewritten by image proxies. Where it is blocked, the Dotomi/Epsilon sync will not fire.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2327825/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `myvisualiq` and `dotomi` all load in one message. Each adds an external request, and a slow third party can delay rendering.
- The `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` path segment looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). It is embedded in a third-party pixel URL, which exposes a CRM identifier. This is inferred from the ID format, not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (4 flagged):**
  - one `<img>` with an empty `src` and no alt
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`
  - `myvisualiq …/impression_pixel`
  - `dotomi …/UCMController`
- The three tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"` and `style="display:none"`. Without these, some clients show broken-image icons or expose the URL to screen readers.
- **Empty `src` image:** an `<img>` with no source can trigger a request to the page URL in some clients and renders as a broken image.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. Only Open Sans is declared. It will fall back to Helvetica. The CSS was truncated, so this could be defined later.
- `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, unlike the other `<meta/>` tags, in an XHTML doctype. This is cosmetic and has no rendering impact.
- No other rendering defects are confirmed. The MSO/VML namespaces, the `u ~ div .pattern` Gmail fix and the `x-apple-data-detectors` reset are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Failed token:** the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq URL is an unresolved macro (see section 2).
- No other unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) were found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, so it can't be called a sender defect. Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `em.sallybeauty.com` could not be validated from this data.
- The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not assessable. The 48 tracked links were not resolved, and no `href` or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source.
- The tracked links redirect through click-redirect domains, so any UTM parameters would be appended server-side. Resolve a sample of the links to confirm the final landing URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro substitution in the myvisualiq pixel template, or drop the pixel.
2. Serve the Dotomi pixel over `https://`, or confirm the vendor supports it.
3. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels. Remove or fix the empty-`src` image.
4. Review the raw `.eml` from the origin to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat the relay-stripped result as a sender failure.
5. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the 48 tracking links to check landing-page status codes, UTMs and the footer unsubscribe and address.
6. Confirm whether the Salesforce-style ID in the `mon-trk` pixel URL is intentional, and whether it is acceptable to expose it.
