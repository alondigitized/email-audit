# Layers of free gifts
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's second email on May 25 — the same day "All eyes on 20% off" already hit the inbox. Combined with the 13 emails before it, this is now a campaign that has become a daily (sometimes twice-daily) presence for two weeks. That cadence is the ceiling on everything that follows, because even the best creative can't fully recover inbox trust burned by volume alone.
- Within those constraints, the creative team made the right call: swap the "20% off" subject-line formula for a gift-with-purchase angle. "Layers of free gifts" is the biggest creative departure in this entire Friends + Family run, and the three-tier gift ladder (mini with any order → full-size at $130 → Barrier Build at $175) is a genuinely smart conversion structure. It pulls a different lever than percentage-off and gives subscribers who haven't bit yet a concrete, tangible reason to act now.
- The execution is clean. The hierarchy reads well. But two flaws blunt its potential: no visible deadline (the urgency is entirely implied), and the bottom half of the email is a copy-paste "20% off sitewide" block from previous sends, making it feel like two separate emails stitched together.

## What's working

- **Gift ladder is the right creative pivot.** Three tiers with explicit spend thresholds ($0, $130, $175) give subscribers a clear upgrade path — this is smarter than another flat-discount hero.
- **"ACTIVATE YOUR CART →" is unusually strong CTA copy.** The possessive ("your cart") implies personalization and creates a sense that something is already waiting for you.
- **Subject line finally breaks the formula.** After 13 variations of "20% off [noun]", "Layers of free gifts" creates genuine curiosity by withholding what the layers are.
- **Product rendering is clean throughout.** Three distinct product groupings, no broken images, no layout shift — the email renders without friction.
- **VIP 20% off mention in the bottom section** keeps the loyalty angle alive for long-term subscribers considering the membership.

## What's weak

- **Second email of the day, 14th of the campaign.** "All eyes on 20% off" already sent May 25 — doubling up in a single day is the most damaging cadence signal possible.
- **No deadline visible anywhere.** Friends + Family urgency is entirely implied. Without a specific end date or countdown, the gift ladder's "act now" signal is soft.
- **Bottom "20% off sitewide" block is a re-run.** It's the same copy and layout from previous sends, making the lower half of the email feel detached from the gift-focused top half.
- **Layer 2 and Layer 3 CTAs use generic "SHOP TO CLAIM →"** rather than something tier-specific that mirrors the gift offer above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the bottom "20% off sitewide" block entirely** or collapse it into a single line within the hero — the email's strongest concept is the gift ladder; the flat-discount recap undercuts it and adds scroll fatigue.
- 2. **Add a hard end date in the hero** — even "Offer ends Sunday" would materially lift urgency without requiring a redesign.
- 3. **Make CTA copy tier-aware.** Layer 1 → "Get My Free Mini →". Layer 2 → "Claim My Full-Size Gift →". Layer 3 → "Shop for My Barrier Build →". Each click should feel like it's completing the specific promise on that layer.
- 4. **Never send two emails in one day** — the second send cannibalizes the first's open rate and trains subscribers to ignore both.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Your free mini ships with your next order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 gifts. $0, $130, $175. Pick your tier.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Friends + Family ends soon — your gift unlocks the moment you add to cart.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Layer 1 is free with any order. Layers 2 + 3 are worth the scroll.`
