# Ends tonight: Take 20% off your online purchase⁺ when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards® for free (terms & excl. apply) 🫶
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing send of the 20% SAVE20 promo that began with the Oct 6 "Members, 2 DAYS!" email. It is well built and loud. The orange "20% off your online purchase" block with code SAVE20 and the "ENDS TONIGHT | MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" tag make the main offer clear, and the "Not a member? Join now" button gives non-members a way in. The subject is the most useful part. It tells you the deadline, the offer and the condition (join for free).
- The email's weakness is that it argues with itself. A "free mystery gift with any $55 purchase" hero sits above the 20% block. Under both, a "PEAK SAVINGS" headline says to stack the coupon on brand discounts, and those brand tiles run "Ends Oct 10" and are "in the app" only. A reader gets two heroes, two deadlines (tonight and Oct 10) and a member-versus-join tension in a single scroll. I'd call it competent and urgent but cluttered. It repeats the previous day's promo, so it works as a reminder rather than news.

## What's working

- The "ENDS TONIGHT" tag and the 20% block with code SAVE20 give a clear offer and a believable deadline.
- "Rae, earn points that turn into $ off future purchases" at the top is a personal touch and a loyalty hook.
- "Not a member? Join now!" gives non-members a CTA instead of shutting them out.
- The brand tiles show real discount depths (Tula 30%, R.E.M. 30%, OPI 25%, Dyson 20%) with a consistent orange layout that's easy to scan.
- The free-shipping bar at the top is a small, concrete incentive that doesn't clutter the hero.

## What's weak

- The mystery-gift hero competes with the 20% block, so the eye has no single place to land.
- Deadlines conflict: "ends tonight" in the subject and block, "Ends Oct 10" on every brand tile.
- Every brand tile is tagged "in the app," which sends email readers to a download rather than a purchase. The "Shop now" links are tiny and say little.
- The fine print is a dense wall of legal text, and the stacking and exclusion rules are hard to follow.
- The brand tiles show product shots only, with no prices, reviews or product details to help someone choose.
- It arrives one day after the near-identical Oct 6 send, and the subject says "join" while the block says "member exclusive."

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with the 20% off / SAVE20 / ends-tonight block and move the mystery gift below it, or make it a one-line bar.
- 2. Use one deadline in the main flow. Label the brand tiles "App deals through Oct 10" so they don't read as tonight's offer.
- 3. Add a prominent "Shop now with code SAVE20" button to the hero, in addition to "Join now."
- 4. Add price points or "from $X" on the tiles, and a star rating on the best sellers.
- 5. Cut the subject to the essentials and drop the "(terms & excl. apply)" clause.
- 6. Shorten the legal block and link out to full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off online ends tonight ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rae, your 20% off expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVE20 + a free mystery gift with $55. Join free to unlock it.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus Dyson, Tula & OPI up to 30% off in the app through Oct 10.`

## Technical Audit

The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most `href`s could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **10 pixels load over plaintext HTTP.** These are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`.
  - Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, and the cookie-sync calls will silently fail.
  - Each also lacks alt text.
  - They are third-party ad-network cookie-sync pixels, which adds extra third-party requests per open and a privacy and deliverability footprint.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=162237`. It is HTTPS and fine. The QA output shows the campaign string `pr_fy26wk36_wednesday_20off_dedicated_12pm_rem` and `31278` attached to it, which suggests internal campaign metadata is exposed in a rendered attribute (likely `alt` or `title`). This is a minor information leak, and I can't confirm it without the full source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 8 content-style images, all on `image.e.ulta.com`:**
