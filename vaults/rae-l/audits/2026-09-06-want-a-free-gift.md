# Want a free gift?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier running the same Labor Day Sale creative for the third time in four days — the hero banner (30% off, $75+ free Glossier You Solid), the "SALE FAVORITES" grid (You Rêve, Body Spritz, Stretch Balm Concealer at 50% off, Lip Glaze), and the fragrance-compact lifestyle shots are all identical to the 9/3 and 9/5 sends. The only real change is the subject line and inbox framing: instead of leading with the discount, this one leads with "Want a free gift?" to fish for opens from anyone who skipped the first two.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent orange CTA buttons, real testimonial with a name attached. But the strategy is the tell: three sends of the same sale in four days is fatigue-inducing, and burying the discount to test a curiosity-based subject line on an already-fatigued list is a diminishing-returns move, not a fresh idea.
- Judgement: well-executed creative, poorly-timed cadence. This is the same email wearing a different subject line, and a subscriber who opened either prior send will feel that immediately on scroll.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — no layout breaks, no broken images, buttons all visible and consistent.
- The 50% OFF badge on the Stretch Balm Concealer tile creates a genuine standout moment in the product grid.
- Real testimonial with a five-star rating and attribution ("—Adison") adds credibility.
- Free-gift mechanic (Glossier You Solid at $75+) is repeated at the bottom as its own dedicated section, reinforcing the subject line's promise.

## What's weak

- This is a near-exact re-run of the 9/3 and 9/5 hero/grid — same products, same offer, same layout — just re-subjected.
- Subject "Want a free gift?" oversells the free-gift angle relative to the actual email, which leads with 30% off, not the gift — a mild bait-and-switch on open.
- No urgency or countdown visible anywhere in the render despite "Now for a very limited time" copy — no date/deadline shown.
- Fourth discount-adjacent send in under a week risks list fatigue and desensitizes the audience to future "sale" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the identical creative — if the list didn't convert on 9/3 or 9/5, a new subject line on the same content won't fix it. Either escalate the offer or retire this campaign and move to net-new content.
- 2. If free gift is the hook, make it the hero visual too — right now the gift section is buried near the footer, disconnected from the subject line's promise.
- 3. Add a real countdown/deadline (e.g., "Ends tonight") to justify "very limited time" — currently there's no visible urgency signal.
- 4. Consider suppressing this send to anyone who opened the 9/3 or 9/5 emails to reduce fatigue and preserve open rates for future sends.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get a Glossier You Solid on us`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends soon — here's what's still 30-50% off`
