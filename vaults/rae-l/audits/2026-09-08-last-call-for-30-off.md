# Last call for 30% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier finally pulling the true urgency lever — "ends tonight," "final chance," "you only have a few hours" — after five straight sends recycling this exact Labor Day creative (9/3, 9/5, 9/6, plus the fragrance cut on 9/7). The genuine deadline is the most honest signal this arc has produced, and it's paired with the correct tactic: escalating from 30% off to a "DEEPER DISCOUNTS" section calling out 50% off Stretch Balm Concealer and Stretch Fluid Foundation.
- The problem is the creative itself is worn out. This is the fifth time this reader has seen this exact hero banner, the same "SALE FAVORITES" grid (You Rêve, Body Spritz, Lip Glaze, Generation G), and the same layout — only the subject and top copy block change. The last-call framing earns points for urgency, but a subscriber who's opened even two of the prior five will recognize the fatigue instantly.
- Net: strong tactical close to a sale arc, undercut by a creative that has been run into the ground. Solid execution, tired assets.

## What's working

- Genuine time-bound urgency ("Ends tonight," "final chance," "you only have a few hours") — the strongest urgency signal seen in this arc so far.
- Clean visual hierarchy: eyebrow ("ENDS TONIGHT") → headline (30% off) → subcopy → CTA button, all above the fold.
- Escalating offer structure — "SALE FAVORITES" at 30% off, then a distinct "DEEPER DISCOUNTS" section at 50% off, giving the reader a reason to keep scrolling.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly with consistent orange CTA buttons throughout.

## What's weak

- Hero banner "LABOR DAY SALE" typography is clipped at the right edge of the module, cutting off the "E" in "SALE" — looks like a layout bug, not intentional design.
- This is the same core creative (hero style, product grid, product selection) run for the fifth time in six days — a subscriber will feel the repetition immediately.
- No countdown timer or explicit end time — "ends tonight" is urgent language but gives no specific hour, which reads slightly generic for a "last call."
- Six near-identical product tiles with repeated "Shop now" buttons create visual monotony; nothing distinguishes the 50%-off items as more urgent than the 30%-off ones until the small badge.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped hero banner text — the "SALE" is being cut off at the image edge; re-export the header asset so headline copy isn't truncated on render.
- 2. Swap in fresh hero imagery for this final send — even keeping the same offer, a new visual signals "this is different/final" rather than "same email again."
- 3. Add a specific cutoff time ("Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") instead of the vaguer "ends tonight" — sharper urgency, especially paired with a countdown-style visual.
- 4. Lead with the 50%-off deeper discounts, not 30% — that's the stronger, less-seen offer and should be the scroll-stopper for a last-call send, not buried two sections down.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: 30-50% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day cart expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Stretch Balm, Foundation + more — gone by morning.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `This is it. Sale closes tonight.`
