# The ILIA VIP Sale starts now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's actual sale email — a genuine break from the editorial/tutorial arc that's dominated the last two weeks (09-01 through 09-10). "20% off sitewide" plus a stack of gift-with-purchase tiers (free multi-stick, free velvet bag on $100+) is the clearest, most concrete offer I've seen from this brand in this history, and the hero delivers it in one glance with a torn-fabric/pin visual that's genuinely well art-directed.
- Where it falls down is the same place ILIA always falls down: everything below the fold is undifferentiated product-grid filler. "Your picks" is six SKUs with no visible price, no indication of what the 20% actually nets out to, and a "sale's on sale" section teasing "up to 40% off" that's completely disconnected from the VIP framing above it — is that a different sale? Same one? It reads like two campaigns bolted together.
- Strongest sitewide-discount send in the recent run — worth opening and probably worth a click, but the body doesn't reinforce the urgency or value the subject promises.

## What's working

- Hero communicates one unambiguous offer — "20% off sitewide" — with no scroll needed.
- The GWP tiers (free multi-stick on all orders, free velvet bag on $100+) are visually clear with literal sticky-note/paperclip callouts pointing at the qualifying products.
- "JOIN VIP + SHOP THE SALE" CTA appears twice (hero and right after the GWP block) with consistent copy and button styling.
- "VIP Treatment" band clearly lists membership perks (free shipping, points, early access) — good for loyalty reinforcement.
- Clean, on-brand color story (blush pink, red/maroon, cream) consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- No prices or percentage-off shown anywhere in the "your picks" product grid — can't tell what 20% actually saves.
- "Sale's on sale — up to 40% off select eye, lip, and cheek items" contradicts/competes with the 20% sitewide framing and isn't explained.
- "VIP" gating is confusing: copy says "Join free to unlock" but doesn't clarify if the 20% requires signup or applies to everyone — a real friction point for a non-member.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the body (no "ends Sunday," no countdown) despite "starts now" implying a live, time-boxed event.
- Six-item product grid has no differentiation — no bestseller flags, no "new," nothing to prioritize where to click first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero and repeat it near the second CTA ("Sale ends [day]") — "starts now" without an end date kills urgency.
- 2. Clarify VIP vs. non-VIP pricing directly under the hero: state plainly whether 20% requires free sign-up or is open to everyone.
- 3. Put a price or "20% off" tag on at least the top 2 "your picks" items so the discount feels real, not abstract.
- 4. Reconcile the "up to 40% off" block with the 20% sitewide headline — either fold it into one consistent sale message or clearly label it as a separate stackable deal.
- 5. Add a bestseller/limited-stock badge to one hero product in the grid to give the eye a place to land first.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your VIP discount is live — 20% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free gifts on top. Ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free bag on orders $100+`
