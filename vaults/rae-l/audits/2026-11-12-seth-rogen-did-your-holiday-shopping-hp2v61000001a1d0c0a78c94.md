## What's weak

- No visible offer, price or deadline in the body. Free shipping is buried in a footer strip with "Exclusions/terms apply."

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing (FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. This can hurt spam scoring and text-only clients. It may also come from the AgentMail capture, so check the raw MIME at the source.
- **Two content images have no alt text:**

  - `20261112_The_Chat_12_02.jpg`
