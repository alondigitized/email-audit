# Still on: your free Mini Beauty Bag
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a two-day reminder to the 7/21 GWP email — "What's free, red, + can hold your makeup essentials?" — which scored a 9/10 here and was the strongest Glossier send of the recent arc. The mechanic is identical: free Mini Beauty Bag with $75+ order. What's new is the **Rave Reviews** section, which adds genuine social proof the original lacked, and a product-grid "Bag Essentials" section linking to Boy Brow, Glossier Pro Slide, and I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom. The hero visual is the same red bag, the offer copy is the same, and the urgency framing ("Don't wait up, you only have a limited time") never gives you an actual deadline.
- This is structurally solid Glossier — clean layout, no broken images, trustworthy brand — but functionally it's a xerox of the 7/21 send with a new subject line. The Rave Reviews module is the only meaningful creative addition. Sending this on the same day as "Your whole look, in a New York Minute" (also 7/23) means two Glossier emails land in the same inbox on the same day, which undercuts the urgency play.
- The email does its job as a conversion nudge. It would do it better with a hard deadline and a sharper CTA.

## What's working

- **Rave Reviews section delivers.** Two starred customer quotes with close-up product photos create the social proof that was missing from the 7/21 launch. This is the strongest addition over the original.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean.** Red bag hero → urgency headline → CTA → reviews → product grid. The eye moves correctly.
- **Bag Essentials grid adds click surface.** Three product tiles (Boy Brow, Pro Slide, I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom) give people who aren't sold on the bag additional reasons to transact and reach the $75 threshold.
- **"Still on" subject is calm and confident.** No exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS — the subject feels like a friend tapping your shoulder, not a brand screaming at you. 35 chars, fits mobile.

## What's weak

- **No hard deadline anywhere in the email.** "Only have a limited time" is doing zero work. The 7/21 email presumably had a deadline; this reminder omits it entirely, which makes the urgency feel invented rather than real.
- **Same creative as 7/21, zero freshness.** The hero image is the same bag shot. If you deleted the subject line, you couldn't distinguish this from the original send. Recipients who ignored it two days ago have the same visual trigger to ignore again.
- **"Shop now" CTA copy is generic.** Three separate "Shop now" buttons in the Bag Essentials grid, plus the hero CTA — no specificity anywhere. "Claim your free bag" or "Shop to unlock your free bag" would connect the action to the reward.
- **Asterisk terms unexplained in email.** The hero says "$75+ order!*" and the footer says "* Terms apply" with nothing in between. Exclusions, expiration date, or qualifying product notes — none visible.
- **Two emails on 7/23 total.** Paired with the "Your whole look, in a New York Minute" send on the same day, this creates double-tap fatigue and dilutes urgency from both.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the deadline in the email.** If the offer ends July 25, say "Offer ends July 25." This single change turns vague urgency into a reason to act today. Even "2 days left" in the hero subheadline beats "a limited time."
- 2. **Swap the hero CTA copy.** Replace "Shop now" with "Claim your free bag" or "Shop $75+ to unlock it" — connects the click directly to the reward and creates a loop the plain CTA breaks.
- 3. **Refresh the hero image.** Use a lifestyle shot of someone using the bag (packing it, pulling a product out) rather than the identical product-on-gradient from 7/21. Even a different angle of the bag signals this is a new communication.
- 4. **Surface one concrete review stat.** If 4.8★ across 2,000 reviews, say so in the Rave Reviews header. The section is there — make it quantified, not just quoted.
- 5. **Stagger the two 7/23 sends.** "Your whole look" and this reminder going out the same day splits urgency and focus. One email per day, or batch them into a single send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `48 hours left: free Mini Beauty Bag with $75+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free bag is still waiting (ends soon)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add to cart, hit $75, and it's yours — no code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab the bag that started it all — offer expires [date].`

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Issue — Semantically invalid tracking span:**
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KY8N2VQWA3BJCTCSEYKK7DVN_0"></span>
```
`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This is Klaviyo's open-tracking pixel disguised as a stylesheet call, but `<span>` cannot fetch external resources; the open track fires only if the email client renders the `href` as a load (some do via attribute sniffing, most don't). A proper 1×1 `<img>` tracking pixel is the reliable standard and should be used instead. If Klaviyo is generating this automatically, verify the Klaviyo template setting for open tracking — this pattern is a non-standard variant.

**Cannot confirm:** Full click-link inventory is not visible in truncated source. All CTA links should route through `ctrk.klclick.com` click-tracking URLs.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Preheader padding uses rendering-risky Unicode:**
The preheader span uses two distinct invisible-character techniques interleaved:
- U+034F `͏` (Combining Grapheme Joiner) — broadly safe
- U+00AD `­` (Soft Hyphen) — **risk**: some clients (notably older Android Gmail) render soft hyphens as visible hyphens, producing garbage characters in the preheader

Recommend replacing all U+00AD soft hyphens with U+200C (Zero Width Non-Joiner) or U+FEFF (Zero Width No-Break Space), which have better cross-client invisibility.

**Positive:** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection=telephone=no`, and full MSO conditional stack are all present and correctly structured.

**Cannot confirm:** `alt` attributes on images — source is truncated before `<body>` image content.
