# Meet the Soft Focus family
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- After a week of tightly sequenced Soft Focus sends — launch (June 2), ingredient story (June 3), bundle offer (June 5), social proof close (June 6) — ILIA is running a product family overview. "Meet the Soft Focus family" is a catalog email at heart: three products in a grid, a mid-email lifestyle image, and the June 5 duo offer repackaged at the bottom with the same 15% saving. It's competently produced, but the sequencing logic is off.
- Subscribers who opened June 5 and June 6 already met this family and already saw 15% off the duo. Running the same offer on June 7, dressed as an introduction, signals ILIA doesn't know I'm already deep in the funnel. The June 5 email earned a 9/10 precisely because it had one product, one offer, and a clear ask. This one has three products, a buried offer, and no deadline — which unwinds most of that conversion energy.
- The photography still carries the email. But the subject "Meet the Soft Focus family" could describe any of the previous five sends, and that's the core problem.

## What's working

- Hero product flat lay of the pink/coral powder compacts is beautiful — communicates "luxe clean beauty" instantly without a word of copy
- Talc-free + safe for sensitive skin callout in the product grid is a genuine differentiator that lands with clean beauty shoppers
- "Plays well with powder" lifestyle mid-email shot breaks up the product catalog well and shows application context
- "CUSTOMIZE + SAVE 15% →" at the bottom gives high-intent scrollers a clear, specific purchase prompt
- Layout is clean and render-bug-free throughout — ILIA's visual brand is consistently applied

## What's weak

- The 15% offer is below two full sections — any subscriber who doesn't scroll will not know a discount exists
- Subject "Meet the Soft Focus family" has zero offer signal, zero urgency, and reads identically to the June 2 launch email's mandate
- The 15% duo offer carries no deadline; running the same evergreen discount from June 5 on June 7 trains subscribers to wait rather than act
- The mid-email CTA beneath "plays well with powder" is illegible at this render size — it's doing no measurable conversion work
- Three products in the grid dilutes attention; no single SKU gets a genuine conversion moment

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lift the duo offer to the hero**: "15% off the Soft Focus family" as the opening line changes the entire email's job — it becomes a purchase prompt instead of a catalog
- 2. **Put a hard deadline on the 15%**: "Through Sunday" or "this weekend only" would change subscriber behavior immediately and stop the evergreen-discount signal
- 3. **Cut the grid to two products**: Setting powder vs. blurring blush as a decision frame converts better than three products with thin copy each
- 4. **Make the mid-email CTA legible**: Whatever that button says below "plays well with powder" — it needs to be readable; if it's driving to a product page, it should earn its space
- 5. **Retire this subject convention**: "Meet the [product family]" is the weakest possible frame after a launch arc — the subscribers who care have already met them
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Soft Focus family — 15% off the duo, this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 powders. 1 duo. Save 15% today.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Setting powder, blurring blush, and the skin tint duo — now with a reason to buy.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The full soft focus line is here. The duo is 15% off. Ends Sunday.`
