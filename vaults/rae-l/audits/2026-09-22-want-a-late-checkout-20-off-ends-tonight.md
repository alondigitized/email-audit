# Want a late checkout? 20% off ends tonight.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's strongest visual production of the streak — the styled red-glove-holding-a-shopping-bag hero is genuinely arresting and on-brand for a "checkout" concept, tying subject line to imagery in a way most of these sends don't bother with. But the subject ("late checkout") promises a hospitality/travel wink that the body never delivers on — it's just another 20%-off VIP sale, the same offer ILIA has run repeatedly this month (09-19's "Safe for sensitive eyes," 09-18's "latest + greatest + 20% off," 09-16's "Art of the Eye"). The gifting stack (free velvet bag + free multi-stick) is a real incentive and the strongest new element here.
- The mid-email product grid is generic and undifferentiated from prior sends — same four hero SKUs, same "shop now" buttons, no new copy. Overall this lands mid-pack: excellent hero art rescues an otherwise fatigued, repetitive VIP-sale format.

## What's working

- Hero image is a genuine standout — red-gloved hand holding a pink shopping bag stamped "VIP SALE ENDS TONIGHT / 20% off sitewide," clever nod to the "checkout" subject line.
- Two free-gift call-outs (velvet makeup bag on $100+, free multi-stick sitewide) are visible with clear threshold copy and hand-drawn-style annotation arrows.
- "come for the sale, stay for the perks" section clearly lists loyalty benefits (free shipping, points, early access) — good for VIP-persona targeting.
- Top banner reinforces urgency clearly: "LAST CHANCE — 20% OFF SITEWIDE FOR VIPS."
- Certification badges (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Recycle with Pact) are visible near the footer, supporting the clean-beauty trust signal.

## What's weak

- Subject's "late checkout" hook is never paid off in the body — no hotel/travel visual motif beyond the wordplay, so it reads as a bait subject line.
- Product grid is a straight repeat of prior sends (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Thru Line Eyeliner, Limitless Lash Mascara) with no new merchandising angle.
- "Sale's on sale" section (up to 40% select eye/lip/cheek) buries a second, larger discount below the fold with weak visual weight — it's arguably the better offer and gets least attention.
- This is now ILIA's Nth 20%-off VIP send in under two weeks (09-16, 09-18, 09-19, and now 09-22) — cadence fatigue is real for any subscriber reading daily.
- Fine print at the very bottom is dense and small, though not deceptive — "Must be logged into VIP account, sign in here to receive the 20% discount at checkout" adds friction not signaled up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pay off the subject line — add a small "checkout" visual motif (luggage tag, receipt, actual checkout UI) so the wordplay isn't just a headline trick; this is the single biggest lift since the hero art is already this send's strength.
- 2. Promote "sale's on sale" (40% off select items) above the standard product grid — it's a bigger number than the 20% headline offer and currently under-weighted.
- 3. Rotate SKUs in the product grid — four repeats of the same items across three sends in one week reads as stale to a daily subscriber.
- 4. Move the "must be logged in / sign in here" VIP-access caveat closer to the top CTA, not buried in bottom fine print — reduces click friction/frustration.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free gifts + 20% off — tonight's the last call`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Checkout before midnight: 20% off + a free velvet bag`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free multi-stick on every order, free bag on $100+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `VIP perks end at midnight — here's what's still unlocked`
