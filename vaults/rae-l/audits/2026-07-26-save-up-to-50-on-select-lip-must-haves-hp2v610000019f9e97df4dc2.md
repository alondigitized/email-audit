# Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves ⭐
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Sephora leads with a National Lip Day banner promising "30% off Select Charlotte Tilbury" — but the subject line promises "up to 50% on select lip must-haves," a number that never appears anywhere in the rendered email. That's a real mismatch, and it echoes the subject/hero disconnect flagged in the 7/25 dae Dreamriser send (also 8/10 here).
- Worse, the lip theme evaporates almost immediately. After the Tilbury banner, the email pivots into a generic "You Deserve to Treat Yourself" new-arrivals catalog spanning foundation, blush, eyeliner, contour sticks, hair care, skincare, and a Dyson styler. Maybe two or three items in the grid are actually lip products (Hourglass lip balm, Dior lip highlighter, Givenchy lip gloss) — the rest is unrelated. This reads like a broad catalog send wearing a lip-focused subject line as clickbait.
- Execution is clean — no render bugs, consistent badges, tidy grid — but the promise-to-delivery gap on both the discount number and the category focus undercuts trust. Solid production, weak honesty.

## What's working

- The National Lip Day banner gives a real occasion and a clear, single-day urgency cue ("Today only!").
- Loyalty signals are prominent — points balance in the top bar and the Beauty Insider/VIB/Rouge tier footer.
- The product grid is clean and scannable: consistent NEW badges, "Clean at Sephora" callouts, and visible pricing on every item.
- Zero visible render bugs — every image, badge, and button displays correctly.

## What's weak

- Subject says "up to 50%"; the only visible offer is 30% off Charlotte Tilbury — the headline number doesn't match what's shown.
- The lip theme is abandoned almost immediately in favor of a wide, unrelated catalog (foundation, blush, eyeliner, contour, hair tools) — subject sets an expectation the body doesn't follow through on.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" — none are tied to the specific product or category above them.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in the grid to support purchase confidence on unfamiliar new launches.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the headline number first — if 50% is real, show a product hitting that discount above the fold; if 30% is the real ceiling, change the subject to match. The current gap is a bait-and-switch risk.
- 2. Either commit to an all-lip send (trim the grid to lip SKUs only) or rename the subject to reflect what's actually inside (a general new-arrivals catalog with a lip promo hook).
- 3. Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for product-specific CTAs ("Shop Lip Kits," "Shop New Arrivals") to sharpen intent per section.
- 4. Add a rating snippet (stars + count) to at least the hero SKUs to build confidence on new, unfamiliar launches.
