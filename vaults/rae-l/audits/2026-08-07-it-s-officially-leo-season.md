# It’s officially Leo season!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a pivot away from the Balm Dotcom flavor treadmill Glossier has been running since mid-July — no offer, no discount, no GWP mechanic, just a "Leo season" editorial hook framing two evergreen products (Lip Line, Boy Brow) through staff testimonials. Compared to the 7/29 "Meet the popular girls" break (8.0/10) that did something similar, this one leans harder into astrology flavor text that has nothing to do with the products themselves — three numbered "Leo Energy" one-liners about "not wearing a boring outfit" read as filler, not hooks.
- The bigger problem is visible in the render itself: content is getting cut off at the right edge — the numbered list item 2 ("Give em something to talk ab...") and Ariela's product quote both truncate mid-sentence. That's a real rendering issue a subscriber would see and it undercuts an otherwise clean two-column layout.
- There's no discount, no urgency beyond the zodiac calendar, and the CTAs point to two specific products rather than a themed collection — solid for click-through on those two items, weak for overall campaign impact. This lands as a mid-tier content send: pleasant, on-brand, but thin on reasons to act right now.

## What's working

- Clean visual hierarchy — sunset hero image, bold headline, and short subhead land first with no clutter.
- Staff-testimonial format (name, birthday, position, quote) is a believable, low-cost trust signal distinct from generic influencer copy.
- Both CTAs ("Shop Lip Line," "Shop Boy Brow") are specific, verb-plus-noun, and tied to a named product rather than a homepage link.
- Timely seasonal hook — Leo season runs late July into August 22, so the send date (Aug 6/7) is genuinely inside the window, not a stale reference.
- Genuine departure from the repetitive I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom arc that's dominated the last two weeks of sends.

## What's weak

- Text is visibly cut off at the right edge of the render — "Give em something to talk ab..." and the second quote ("I also love how I can use Clear or... Blond... Dark Brown...") both truncate, a real layout bug a subscriber would see.
- Zero concrete offer — no %/$ off, no free shipping, no loyalty perk — this is pure content marketing dressed as a campaign email.
- The "Leo Energy" numbered list is disconnected from the products; "life's too short to wear a boring outfit" has nothing to do with lip gloss or brow gel.
- No deadline or urgency device beyond the implicit astrological calendar — nothing tells the reader why to act today vs. next week.
- Subject line doesn't hint at what's inside (no product, no offer), so it's relying entirely on brand trust to earn the open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cut-off copy first — the truncated list item and quote are a hard block on comprehension and look broken in-inbox regardless of copy quality.
- 2. Add a light incentive tied to the Leo hook (e.g., "Leo-approved picks, 15% off this week") — right now there's no reason to buy today over any other day.
- 3. Cut or rewrite the "Leo Energy" three-liner — replace generic astrology platitudes with something that actually ties back to Lip Line/Boy Brow ("Bold sign, bolder brow").
- 4. Give the subject a concrete anchor (product or benefit) instead of pure theme, since "Leo season" alone doesn't signal what's being sold.
