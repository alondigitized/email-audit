# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email for the third time in three days. The 11/2 send had the same subject and the same "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." hero. The cart item is still the black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" sneaker with the blue skull. The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is clear, and the hero is clean.
- The email's best asset is badly placed. The "2 DAYS ONLY / 25% OFF FOR SKECHERS PLUS MEMBERS" banner, valid through 11/4, sits below the fold, under six unrelated products. That is also the first time the offer has stepped up from the 20% in the 10/31 and 11/1 sends. The subject says "expires soon", but nothing in the top of the email shows a deadline. The email earns the subject only after a long scroll.
- Overall it is a solid but unremarkable retargeting email. The sender is trusted, the CTA is obvious, and the member offer is real. Because it follows two near-identical sends, the urgency reads as boilerplate.

## What's working

- The hero product is large, centered and unmistakable, with a single "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button directly under it.
- The 25% off for Skechers Plus members with a "Valid thru 11/4" date is a real, dated offer. It also raises the 20% from the earlier sends.
- The headline "YOU LIKED THESE AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." is confident and on-brand.
- The black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar and the banner break up the layout and give the eye clear stopping points.
- The footer fine print names the offer terms: Plus account required, online only, excludes sale styles.

## What's weak

- The 25% offer sits about two-thirds of the way down. The hero gives no discount or deadline, so the subject's urgency isn't paid off above the fold.
- The six "more great options" mix men's boots, women's sneakers and slip-ins. They aren't tied to the cart item, and they dilute the single-product focus.
- Below the offer there are six category bars, a text-signup, app, curbside, BNPL and store-finder blocks. That is a lot of exit ramps for a cart reminder.
- The hero has a lot of empty white space above and around the shoe, which pushes the CTA down.
- The "Valid thru 11/4. See details below*" text in the banner is tiny and low-contrast against the navy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 25% Plus offer into the hero, directly above or beside "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER". Put "Ends 11/4" in the headline area.
- 2. Cut the recommendation grid to three items related to the cart product (UNO styles). Remove the men's boots.
- 3. Trim the footer modules to the app, store finder and texting.
- 4. Shrink the white space around the hero shoe so the CTA lands higher.
- 5. Vary the subject and creative from send to send. Don't reuse the same hero on consecutive days.
- 6. Change the CTA to something like "Get 25% off my UNOs".
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off your UNOs ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your skull UNOs + 25% off for Plus members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members: 25% off through 11/4. Your cart is saved.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two days left to take 25% off the sneakers you liked.`
