# Don't leave a good thing behind.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment template again, and the Black Friday offer is a banner under the product grid. The hero is a single Britto S-1992 sneaker under "SELLING FAST… ADD TO CART BEFORE THEY'RE GONE!" with a solid navy SHOP NOW button. The shoe is a colorful lifestyle fashion sneaker. The six options below it are all women's running shoes, so the personalization is inconsistent.
- The strongest asset is the maroon 30% OFF banner, but it sits below the fold under two rows of product tiles. It repeats the 11/18 member-exclusive early-access send ("Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members"). The recipient already got the offer in a stronger hero treatment the day before. Here it is a tacked-on footer. The "Don't leave a good thing behind." subject is also a reuse from 11/12, which is the same creative and the same subject line a week later.
- Judgement: competent and clean, but a recycled template, not a targeted send. The scarcity hero and the discount banner compete, and neither wins.

## What's working

- The hero is clean: large single product, name, and a high-contrast navy SHOP NOW button.
- The maroon 30% OFF banner is bold and easy to read, with "Discount auto-applied at cart. Valid online thru 11/22" for credibility.
- The six-shoe grid gives browsing options with consistent product photography and clear names.
- The category nav (WOMEN, MEN, KIDS, CLOTHING, GIFT GUIDE, SALE) has large tap targets, and GIFT GUIDE in red is a seasonally apt highlight.
- The footer utilities (app, curbside, Afterpay/Klarna, text signup) are tidy and well organized.

## What's weak

- The discount sits below the fold under about 1,000px of product content. The best hook is the last thing most readers see.
- "SELLING FAST" scarcity messaging clashes with a sitewide sale, since two different urgency frames compete.
- The hero is a fashion collab sneaker, while the grid is all performance running shoes. The "options we think you'll love" don't relate to the hero.
- The banner's fine print ("See below for details*") and the dense disclaimer wall at the bottom undercut an otherwise honest offer. The "SHOP NOW" in the banner is a small text link, not a button.
- Large empty whitespace around the hero and between the grid rows makes the email longer than it needs to be.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% OFF banner to the top, above the hero, or fold it into the hero. Lead with the offer, then the abandoned product.
- 2. Make the hero price-aware: show the abandoned shoe with its 30% member price ("Now $XX with 30% off").
- 3. Make the grid relevant to the hero style (lifestyle sneakers), or label it clearly as running.
- 4. Retire the 11/12 subject and template. Rotate the creative so a daily subscriber sees something new.
- 5. Turn the banner's SHOP NOW into a real button and drop the second "See below" fine-print reference.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Britto sneaker + 30% off, through 11/22`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? Members get 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Members: 30% off sitewide is auto-applied at cart. Ends 11/22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The S-1992 Britto is selling fast. Your member discount makes it better.`
