# We interrupt your regularly scheduled program ⭐
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth sale-themed Glossier send in about five days, and the only new idea is the "resident Copywriter" picks. The framing is "What to get during our biggest sale of the year (according to our resident Copywriter)." The three "Must-Buy" cards, Lash Slick, After Baume and I ♥ NY Balm Dotcom, give it a personal, editorial voice. That is more interesting than the 11/22 recap of review cards ("Loved by you, now 25% off").
- The offer is clear: 25% off everything, or 30% off $100+ orders. The free Crème de You with $100+ appears again as the closer, as it did on 11/21. The 30% tier is new and is the strongest piece of news here. It sits in small body copy under the hero, though, and the circular "25% OFF" badge undersells it.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand sale reminder with a real editorial angle, but the subject promises an "interruption" the email doesn't deliver. Nothing in the body is an interruption. It is the same sale again, so it will perform like the other sends in the sequence.

## What's working

- The Copywriter's-picks angle gives the sale a human voice that the earlier recap sends lacked.
- The hero is clean. A product photo with a bold blue "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" badge makes the offer obvious at a glance.
- The Must-Buy cards alternate image left and right, which gives a good scroll rhythm. Each has a single product and a single CTA.
- The product copy is specific and sensory, for example the After Baume line about elbows, knees and heels.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" marquee band reinforces the season without clutter.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a break from the routine ("We interrupt your regularly scheduled program"), but the body is more of the same sale.
- The 30% off $100+ tier is buried in small body text. It is the only new incentive and should be in the hero.
- Every card CTA reads "Get 25% off", which is repetitive and doesn't name the product.
- The top banner reads "Lock in 25% off new subscriptions for a limited tim…" and is cut off at the right edge. The marquee and the right-column body copy also clip ("I swipe o", "paired with", "dryness").
- The free-gift block appears at the bottom, so it is easy to miss. It is also the third send in a row to repeat that gift.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 30% off $100+ tier in the hero headline and badge. It is the only new incentive and it raises basket size.
- 2. Make the subject honest and specific. Either deliver the "interruption" with a real news item, or lead with the Copywriter angle.
- 3. Change the CTAs to name the product, for example "Shop Lash Slick".
- 4. Fix the clipped text in the top banner and the right-column body copy.
- 5. Pair the free-gift block with the $100 threshold in the hero, so the offers read as one story.
- 6. Review cadence. Five sale sends in five days with near-identical offers risks fatigue, so consider a deadline or a new beat for each send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Stripo-built, Klaviyo-tracked email (`ctrk.klclick.com`) has no confirmed breakage in the visible source. The QA flags are missing alt text on 6 images and three headers the relay didn't capture. The HTML is truncated and 9 of 12 material links were not probed, so link and UTM coverage is incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage gap:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 9, so link health is only partly verified. No broken links were reported among those probed.
- **Stray `href` on a `<span>`:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M851V34EG2W9B63QZXZZP0XY_0"></span>` sits right after `<body>`. `href` and `rel="stylesheet"` have no effect on a `<span>`, so it is invalid markup and does nothing. It is probably a Klaviyo/ESP injection artifact. It is harmless but worth removing.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M851V34EG2W9B63QZXZZP0XY` is the open-tracking image and has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` to it.
- **Title mismatch:** `<title>` reads "…regularly scheduled promo-filled inbox ⭐". The subject line reads "…regularly scheduled program ⭐". This is low severity and only affects web-view and some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 content images** (all hosted at `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_…`):
  - `heroimage_1_4.jpg`
  - `group_3996_1.jpg` (appears twice, under two different CABINET GUIDs)
  - `group_3996.jpg`
  - `frame_3984.jpg`
- **Effect of the missing alt text:** If images are blocked, those slots render empty. Screen readers will read the filename or nothing. If any of these images carry the offer or a CTA, the message is lost with images off.
- **Structure and mobile CSS:** The doctype, `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals and the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block are all present and well-formed. No rendering defects are confirmed from the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible HTML. The truncation means the body can't be fully ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA says the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. That is unconfirmed, so treat it as unverified rather than a confirmed failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be determined from this capture.
- **Sender:** `gTEAM@eml.glossier.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment can't be verified without the headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** An unsubscribe link was included in probing per QA, but it isn't visible in the truncated HTML. The physical postal address is also not visible, so neither could be confirmed in the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The truncated HTML shows only `klclick.com` redirect wrappers, with no visible `utm_*` parameters. QA gives no landing-page destination data. Continuity can't be assessed. Klaviyo typically appends UTMs at redirect time, so verify the final destination URLs after following the redirects.
