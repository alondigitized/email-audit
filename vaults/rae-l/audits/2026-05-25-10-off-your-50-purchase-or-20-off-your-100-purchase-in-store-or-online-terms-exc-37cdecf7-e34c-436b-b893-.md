# $10 off your $50 purchase⁺ or $20 off your $100 purchase⁺ in store or online (Terms & excl. apply) ends today!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Ulta Beauty's last-chance closer on the exact same tiered discount it deployed four days ago (May 21: "$10 off $50 or $20 off $100"). The "friendly reminder..." headline is a smart tonal choice — warmer than a countdown-clock blast, framing the close as a member heads-up rather than a panic promo. The two-tier offer boxes render cleanly with strong color contrast, and the addition of a "free mystery gift" incentive below the hero gives subscribers a reason to look again even if they already saw the original.
- The subject line is the core failure. At 113 characters it guarantees heavy mobile truncation — the urgency anchor "ends today!" sits after the terms disclaimer and will be clipped on virtually every phone. A send whose entire value proposition is time-pressure buries its own time-pressure. The rest of the creative is competent but the product grid below fold is unfocused, offering no single hero product to channel the discount toward a specific click.
- Verdict: a solid mechanics execution on a proven offer, running into a cadence headwind (7th Ulta email in May) and a subject line that undermines the one thing that differentiates this send from the May 21 original.

## What's working

- "Friendly reminder..." framing is warmer than typical last-chance aggression; feels like a benefit, not a blast.
- Tiered offer boxes ($10 and $20 in distinct color blocks) create instant visual hierarchy — the math lands above the fold without scrolling.
- Free mystery gift section adds incremental incentive not present in the May 21 send.
- Render is clean: no overlapping text, no broken images, no layout breaks.
- Rewards/loyalty section below fold reinforces the Ultamate Rewards membership angle.

## What's weak

- Subject line is 113 characters — "ends today!" is buried after the terms notice and will be clipped on mobile, killing the urgency that makes this send worth opening.
- Same core creative and offer mechanic as May 21, 4 days prior: subscribers who opened the original see nothing new in the body.
- Product grid below fold is an unfocused catalog dump — no single hero product is featured to give the discount a specific destination.
- The free mystery gift threshold (if there is one) is not visible in the hero, leaving it as a curiosity rather than an actionable hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Front-load the urgency in the subject and let the preheader carry the terms** — the current subject reads like a terms doc with a promo appended.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends today: $10 off $50 or $20 off $100 at Ulta`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance — your Ulta discount expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free mystery gift with qualifying purchase. Terms & excl. apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `In store and online — use it before midnight. Terms apply.`
- 2. **Anchor the mystery gift with a clear threshold** — show the spend floor next to the hero tiers so subscribers can plan one shopping trip that unlocks both offers.
- 3. **Replace the product grid with one featured hero product** — "use your $20 off on this" or a bestseller callout creates a specific click destination rather than a catalog scroll.
- 4. **Suppress the wide catalog grid on last-chance sends** — it dilutes the CTA and signals "generic blast" rather than "closing window." Save the full browse experience for browse-intent sends.

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is Ulta Beauty's last-chance closer on the exact same tiered discount it deployed four days ago (May 21: "$10 off $50 or $20 off $100"). The "friendly reminder..." headline is a smart tonal choice — warmer than a countdown-clock blast, framing the close as a member heads-up rather than a panic promo. The two-tier offer boxes render cleanly with strong color contrast, and the addition of a "free mystery gift" incentive below the hero gives subscribers a reason to look again even if they already saw the original.

The subject line is the core failure. At 113 characters it guarantees heavy mobile truncation — the urgency anchor "ends today!" sits after the terms disclaimer and will be clipped on virtually every phone. A send whose entire value proposition is time-pressure buries its own time-pressure. The rest of the creative is competent but the product grid below fold is unfocused, offering no single hero product to channel the discount toward a specific click.

Verdict: a solid mechanics execution on a proven offer, running into a cadence headwind (7th Ulta email in May) and a subject line that undermines the one thing that differentiates this send from the May 21 original.

## 2. What worked

- "Friendly reminder..." framing is warmer than typical last-chance aggression; feels like a benefit, not a blast.
- Tiered offer boxes ($10 and $20 in distinct color blocks) create instant visual hierarchy — the math lands above the fold without scrolling.
- Free mystery gift section adds incremental incentive not present in the May 21 send.
- Render is clean: no overlapping text, no broken images, no layout breaks.
- Rewards/loyalty section below fold reinforces the Ultamate Rewards membership angle.

## 3. What didn't

- Subject line is 113 characters — "ends today!" is buried after the terms notice and will be clipped on mobile, killing the urgency that makes this send worth opening.
- Same core creative and offer mechanic as May 21, 4 days prior: subscribers who opened the original see nothing new in the body.
- Product grid below fold is an unfocused catalog dump — no single hero product is featured to give the discount a specific destination.
- The free mystery gift threshold (if there is one) is not visible in the hero, leaving it as a curiosity rather than an actionable hook.

## 4. What I'd change

1. **Front-load the urgency in the subject and let the preheader carry the terms** — the current subject reads like a terms doc with a promo appended.
   - **Subject Alt A:** `Ends today: $10 off $50 or $20 off $100 at Ulta`
   - **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance — your Ulta discount expires tonight`
   - **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free mystery gift with qualifying purchase. Terms & excl. apply.`
   - **Preheader Alt B:** `In store and online — use it before midnight. Terms apply.`

2. **Anchor the mystery gift with a clear threshold** — show the spend floor next to the hero tiers so subscribers can plan one shopping trip that unlocks both offers.

3. **Replace the product grid with one featured hero product** — "use your $20 off on this" or a bestseller callout creates a specific click destination rather than a catalog scroll.

4. **Suppress the wide catalog grid on last-chance sends** — it dilutes the CTA and signals "generic blast" rather than "closing window." Save the full browse experience for browse-intent sends.
