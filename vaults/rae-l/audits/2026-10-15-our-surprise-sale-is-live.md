# OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE! ⭐
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's first real discount email in the recent run. The brand history shows weeks of launch announcements, gift guides and a costume guide, and none of them had an offer. "Take 20% off all best-sellers*" is clear, and the blue "20% OFF" tags on every product tile make the deal hard to miss. After the 10/14 Black Cherry launch, a plain price cut will register with subscribers.
- The execution is cleaner than most promo sends. The layout is a simple hero, a 2x2 grid of four hero products, and a second offer (free Futuredew Solid at $75+). Each tile has its own "Shop now" button. The weakness is the hero. The right half is a flat blue block with repeating "20% OFF / SURPRISE SALE" text and no product, and the cropped text at the edge looks unfinished. The top banner is also cut off ("with your $75+ orde"). The email asks for "best-sellers" to carry the offer, but the "best-sellers" are four items and the fine print is hidden behind an asterisk.
- Verdict: a solid, honest promo that lands because it finally has an offer. It's weaker than it should be because of the empty hero, the same "Shop now" on every button, and no deadline despite "before it's too late."

## What's working

- The offer is concrete: 20% off, repeated as a blue badge on all four product images, so it scans in seconds.
- The 2x2 product grid (Glossier You, I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom, Cloud Paint, Boy Brow) shows recognizable best-sellers with a name and descriptor under each.
- The stacked offer (free Futuredew Solid with $75+ order) gives a basket-building reason to spend more.
- The brand look is consistent: bold blue, clean white space, and strong product photography, especially the apples behind the balm.
- "Available online + in-store" widens who can use the offer.

## What's weak

- The hero is a blue block of text with no product. It wastes the top of the email and looks like a placeholder.
- The hero text and the top banner ("with your $75+ orde") are cut off at the right edge, so the free-gift message is truncated.
- "Save now before it's too late" claims urgency, but there is no end date or countdown.
- All nine buttons say "Shop now". None names a product or the discount.
- The Cloud Paint tile has a badge covering part of the product name, so the "cloud paint" lettering is partly hidden behind the badge and reads as clutter.
- The asterisks on "best-sellers*" and "$75+ order*" push the exclusions into tiny "Terms apply" text at the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the blue hero with a product or a lifestyle shot, and keep a single line of "20% off best-sellers" on top. The most important offer should lead with something the reader wants.
- 2. Put an end date in the hero and the subject ("ends Sunday"). It fits the surprise-sale idea and makes the urgency credible.
- 3. Fix the clipped top banner and hero text so the free-gift message is fully readable.
- 4. Vary the button copy, such as "Get 20% off Glossier You" or "Shop Boy Brow". Use one primary button in the hero and let the grid buttons be product-specific.
- 5. Spell out what's excluded in one line under the hero, to reduce distrust from the asterisks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off Glossier best-sellers, this weekend only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Surprise: 20% off Glossier You, Balm Dotcom + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Futuredew Solid with $75+. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Glossier You, Boy Brow, Cloud Paint and Balm Dotcom, all 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built template that is structurally sound: it has a doctype, `lang`, a viewport meta tag, a `<title>` and a hidden preheader. Automated QA shows 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The warnings are missing header data, which is probably a relay capture limit, and 7 images without alt text. The HTML supplied is truncated, so body links, footer and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 13. No broken-link results were reported, but the skipped footer, social and utility links are unverified.
- **Click tracking:** The visible links are wrapped by Klaviyo (`ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M4ZZKN2RFKVBSWD8JXS8Q2GD_0`). I can't confirm the destination URLs resolve to glossier.com without the probe output.
- **Stray tracking element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M4ZZKN2RFKVBSWD8JXS8Q2GD_0"></span>` sits directly after `<body>`. `rel` and `href` are invalid on a `<span>`. This looks like a template artifact and should be harmless, but it adds a tracking URL that no clickable element uses.
- **Open pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M4ZZKN2RFKVBSWD8JXS8Q2GD` is the Klaviyo open-tracking pixel. It is flagged for missing alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 7 images):** `frame_3986.png`, `group_3986.jpg`, `group_3987.jpg`, `group_3986_1.jpg`, `group_3987_1.jpg` and `group_3971.jpg`, all on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_6e9d…`. The seventh is the tracking pixel above. With images blocked, or for screen readers, the content images give no text equivalent. The pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- **Conditional comment structure:** `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opens in `<head>` before the `<style>` blocks, but the matching `<!--<![endif]-->` appears after `<body>`. The conditional spans the head/body boundary. That is a common Stripo pattern, but it means Outlook desktop (MSO) skips the whole `<style>` block, including the responsive `@media` rules and the `.v` hide rules.
- **Hidden preheader:** The hidden span uses `display:none`, `mso-hide:all` and `opacity:0`, which is standard. The `͏` padding characters are the usual preheader filler.
- **Doctype and meta tags:** XHTML 1.0 Transitional is paired with a HTML5-style `<meta charset="utf-8">`. This is valid enough and renders fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{%`) appear in the visible source or in the QA findings. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** The QA note says the header "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". Klaviyo normally sets it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN]:** The RFC 8058 one-click signal is likewise missing from the captured headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so it should be verified in a raw-header capture.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** The relay strips it, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown. The `From` domain is `eml.glossier.com`, a sending subdomain. Alignment should be checked from the original message.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is "always included" in the probe set. The footer content is truncated, so the physical postal address couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Landing page:** No landing-page data is available. The preheader claims "20% off all best-sellers", and the QA output doesn't show whether the landing pages carry that offer. I can't assess this.
