# A gift for the guys
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer men's grooming send. The hero is a deep red block with "20% off select men's grooming," a deadline ("Ends October 31"), and a black "Shop now" button. Below it is a large photo of a bearded man trimming his beard, then four products. The eye lands on the offer first, and nothing is visibly broken.
- The subject undersells the email. "A gift for the guys" says nothing about 20% off, and the body is really a sale on grooming gear, not a gift guide. Nobody in a hurry would guess the offer from the inbox line.
- The stacked banners above the hero are the other weakness. There are three bars: the $5-off-$25 Rewards signup, Buy Online Pick Up In-Store, and 2-hour delivery. The Rewards bar is the fifth push of that offer since 10/02, after 10/11, 10/17 and 10/19. It competes with the hero and trains readers to ignore it. Overall this is competent but generic, and it is a niche segment send that lands as one more daily promo.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: a big "20% off select men's grooming" headline, a hard end date, and a high-contrast "Shop now" button.
- The beard-trimming photo is on-theme and large, and it sells the category immediately.
- The four-product grid (Clubman, L3VEL3 beard shampoo, styling powder, sea salt spray) gives concrete items with their own links.
- The Klarna and benefits icon row (in-store, free shipping, 2-hour delivery, BOPIS) answers friction questions without crowding the offer.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject, "A gift for the guys," hides the 20% off and the grooming angle.
- Three promo bars stack above the hero. The $5 off $25 signup repeats the offer from 10/19 ("New to rewards? Get $5 off $25"), and it dilutes the hero.
- The product tiles show no prices, no sale prices and no ratings. A "20% off select" claim is hard to verify, and "select" gives no hint which items qualify.
- Every product CTA is a generic underlined "Shop now" text link, and the product images are small against the empty white space.
- The "gift" framing in the subject and "Keep your look sharp over the holidays" don't line up with each other or with the hard Oct 31 deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the offer and the category: "20% off men's grooming, ends 10/31."
- 2. Drop the Rewards signup bar from this send, or show it only to non-members. Keep one promo bar at most.
- 3. Add sale prices and star ratings under each product, and make the CTA product-specific, such as "Shop beard shampoo."
- 4. Pick one story. Either make it a real gift guide (gift sets, under-$25 picks) or an honest grooming sale, and don't mix both.
- 5. Enlarge the product images and cut the white space so the grid carries more weight against the hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Beard care for him: 20% off through 10/31`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clubman, L3VEL3 and more. Stock up before Oct 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beard shampoo, styling powder and sea salt spray, all 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: "A gift for the guys" (Sally Beauty, em.sallybeauty.com)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with dark-mode/Outlook-safe meta tags and a mobile breakpoint at 499px. The confirmed problems are in the third-party tracking and personalization pixels: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, an empty image `src`, an HTTP image, and a recipient email address in an image URL. The header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11…`
  - The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never replaced, so the pixel URL is identical for every open and can be cached by proxies or Apple Mail Privacy Protection.
  - Impression counts will likely be undercounted.
  - Several parameters are `-11`, which looks like a default or placeholder value rather than real campaign IDs.
- **Empty image source:** QA flagged an `<img>` with `src` empty (`Image missing alt text: : src:`). It makes a request to the base document or does nothing, and renders as a broken image in some clients.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` loads over plain HTTP.
  - Gmail's image proxy and other clients may block it or show a mixed-content warning.
  - The sync and tracking call for this pixel will fail silently where it is blocked.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** the email carries at least four third-party pixels (Dotomi/Conversant, Evergage/Salesforce, mon-trk, MediaOcean/VisualIQ). More pixels mean more remote requests and a greater chance that one fails or is blocked.
- **Unverified links:** 55 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on:
  - `summary.png` ×2 (Evergage dynamic content images).
  - The empty-`src` image.
  - The `pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` pixel.
  - The VisualIQ and Dotomi pixels.
- **Which of these matter:** the two `summary.png` images are likely visible content, so they are the real accessibility problem.
  - If they carry offers or product information, screen-reader users and image-blocked clients get nothing.
  - The pixels should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so they are treated as decorative.
- **Minor:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype. This is harmless in practice.
- **Font stack:** the global `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is acceptable.
- Only the first part of the source was available, so the full table and structure and the preheader couldn't be assessed.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the VisualIQ pixel (see section 2).
- **PII in a URL:** the Evergage image URLs contain `userId=rae-l@ete…` (truncated), which looks like the recipient's plain-text email address in a query string.
  - The address is exposed to the image host, CDN logs and any proxy.
  - It also appears in forwarded copies of the email.
  - Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
- **Resolved ID:** the mon-trk path segment `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` is a resolved Salesforce Contact ID, so that token rendered correctly. It is also an identifier in the URL.
- No other unrendered `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens appear in the available source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
  - Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders, so verify against the original message source.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
  - The envelope domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
  - Alignment can't be confirmed without the raw headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** can't be evaluated. All 55 links are tracking redirects that were not resolved, so the final destination URLs and UTM parameters are unknown.
- **Landing-page alignment:** can't be assessed for the same reason.
- **Do not treat as a pass:** this category is unverified, not clean.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the VisualIQ pixel so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is replaced at send time, and replace the `-11` placeholder IDs with real values.
2. Move the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or remove it if the provider doesn't support HTTPS.
3. Remove the empty-`src` `<img>`, or populate it.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to the two Evergage `summary.png` images. Give all tracking pixels `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
5. Replace the plain-email `userId` in the Evergage URLs with a hashed or opaque identifier.
6. Re-run the check against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If the relay is stripping them, record that as a tool limitation.
7. Resolve the tracking redirects with a redirect-following probe to check UTM and landing-page continuity.
