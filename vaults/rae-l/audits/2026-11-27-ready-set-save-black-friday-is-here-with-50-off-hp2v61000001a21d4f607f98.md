# Ready, set, SAVE 🎉 Black Friday is here with 50% OFF ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Black Friday send in as many beats from Sephora today. It is a near-duplicate of the earlier "Black Friday just got real 🌟 50% OFF starts now 🎊" email. The hero is a giant "50% OFF" on a black sequin background with a SHOP NOW button, and it lands instantly. It is the right headline for the day. The caveat is that the dagger footnote sits beside "OFF" and there is no hint of which products qualify, only "Gift their faves and discover something for yourself."
- Below the hero it falls back into the Cyber Week template I have seen all week: purple sequin blocks with huge "30% OFF" numerals for Paula's Choice/Fenty Beauty and Farmacy, then a product grid, a green "30% off select brands" banner, a store locator, a holiday gift guide and a long footer. The result is a very long email that makes four or five different offers (50%, 30%, 30%, 30%, 4x points) at once. A subscriber who got the 11/27 "Black Friday just got real" email earlier will see this as the same message again.
- Verdict: strong hero, weak discipline. It is a competent promo that works as a reminder, but it gives a subscriber little reason to open a second Black Friday email on the same day.

## What's working

- The "50% OFF" hero is huge, high-contrast and above the fold, with a clear black SHOP NOW button directly under it.
- The black "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" pill tags the moment immediately.
- The purple "GIFT FRAGRANCE, GET 4X POINTS" banner at the top adds a loyalty hook and a code (CYBERSCENT).
- The 30% blocks have large numerals and named brands (Paula's Choice, Fenty Beauty, Farmacy) with "TODAY ONLY" urgency.
- The product grid shows prices with struck-through originals, e.g. Dr. Jart at $36.40 and Sol de Janeiro at $27.30.
- The local store module (Sephora at Kohl's with hours) and the barcode make the email usable in store.

## What's weak

- The email makes too many offers at once (50%, 30% ×2, 30% select brands, 4x points), so the page lacks a single story.
- It is extremely long, about 6,500px, with a store map, gift guide and fine print that bury the secondary CTAs.
- The "50% OFF" has a dagger footnote and no indication of what is 50% off. "Gift their faves" is vague.
- It recycles the purple sequin template from the Cyber Week sends of 11/24 to 11/26 and the earlier Black Friday hero. It feels like the same email twice.
- The product grid text is tiny and the product names are hard to read at this scale. "Cyber Week deals don't stop here" is stale language on Black Friday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero say what is 50% off, such as "50% off top gifts" with named categories under the number, and cut the secondary offers to one.
- 2. Stop sending a second near-identical email on the same day. Send a distinct angle (a gift set, or a last-chance send) instead.
- 3. Cut the page by half. Drop the store map and move the gift guide above the product grid.
- 4. Retire the "Cyber Week" copy and use Black Friday language only.
- 5. Put the 4x points code in the hero button area, where a member will see it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off gifts, today only 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: 50% off Sephora's best gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 4x points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT, through 11/30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fenty, Paula's Choice and Farmacy at 30% off, today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a 0-character plain-text part (QA FAIL) and several HTML robustness problems: invalid `<meta name="robots">`, a conditional comment inside a media query, `maximum-scale=1`, and a fixed 700px layout. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body links were not visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** The visible links are wrapped in `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a21d4f607f98f660369ff1d668/ff29c249.../ff29c249-7891-46b7-aacc-1314ca0041a1?__dU__=...`. The final destination URLs can't be seen in the truncated source.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 36 skipped (QA WARN). No broken links were reported among those probed, so the other 36 are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixels:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a hidden `<img>` (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`) with no `alt`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=20261127_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Fa...` also has no `alt`.
  - Both are flagged by QA as missing alt text. For hidden pixels this has little accessibility impact, but it does add noise.
- **Duplicate tracking:** Two separate open-tracking systems (`app.sephora.com/O/` and `mi.sephora.com`) load on every open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part:** [FAIL] "Text version is 0 chars." This hurts deliverability scoring and text-only clients. I can't rule out that the relay dropped it, but it is a QA failure as captured.
- **Robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` is invalid. The directive is `noindex`. It has no effect in email.
- **Conditional comment inside CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block. In non-MSO clients the stray `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` and `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge` selectors are likely invalid and dropped. That likely discards the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule on mobile. Conditional comments belong outside the `<style>` block.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. In the visible CSS, no mobile rule overrides `min-width:700px`, so narrow viewports may scroll horizontally or scale down. The rest of the CSS was truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Viewport zoom:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Document attributes:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute. The `<title>` is just "Sephora".
- **Table semantics:** Layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Preheader padding:** The preheader uses repeated `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding. That is a common technique, but the visible preheader text, "Stack 50% off with daily deals and save big.", is only about 40 characters before the padding.
- **Structured data:** The JSON-LD in `<head>` has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which isn't a valid schema.org type. Most clients strip `<script>`, and Gmail's promotion annotations need a different format, so this is dead or invalid markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- The open pixel's base64 path decodes to a null GUID (`00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000`) in the contact field. This suggests the recipient ID is blank for this address, probably because it is a seed or relay address. It isn't a defect in the email itself.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** [WARN] not found.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** [WARN] not found.
- **Authentication-Results:** [WARN] not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- All three are most likely relay capture gaps, as QA notes. They can't be confirmed as sender defects. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so these should be checked against a raw `.eml` from a direct send.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the footer, which was truncated. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result wasn't shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be verified. Links go through the `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirector with an opaque `__dU__` token.
- The tracking pixel's `mi_ecmp=20261127_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Fa...` campaign ID says "Cyber Week", while the subject says "Black Friday". This is an internal naming mismatch only.
- Landing-page alignment with the "50% OFF" offer was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative. This fixes the one QA FAIL.
2. Move `<!--[if mso]>` blocks out of the `@media` block, and confirm `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` applies on mobile.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on layout tables. Replace the generic `<title>`.
4. Fix `content="noindex"`, or drop the meta tag.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels. Remove or correct the JSON-LD (empty `discountCode`, invalid `PromotionCard`).
6. Capture a raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Re-run the QA probe with a larger link budget, since 36 links were skipped.
7. Verify the destination URLs and UTM parameters after the redirector resolves them.
