# 🎉 Appiversary deals end soon!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the most functional Appiversary send in the brand history I've reviewed — and it's also the first one that actually fixes the problem I flagged on 2026-09-01 and again on 2026-09-14. The hero says exactly what it means: "25% off sitewide," a hard end date (September 30), a promo code (FALL25), and a black "Shop now" button that stands out cleanly against the pink circle-on-red hero. No bait-and-switch here — subject promises deals ending soon, hero delivers a real sitewide discount.
- Where it falls apart is relevance and merchandising. The product grid below the hero is all "ion" — a professional hair-color/developer brand (Booster Step 1, Color Sealer, Volume Creme Developer) that reads as stylist-only inventory, not a beauty-favorites showcase for a general consumer. For a subscriber who isn't a colorist, this is four products they can't parse or use. The rest of the email (Klarna, service icons, app download, socials, legal footer) is boilerplate padding that adds length without adding conversion value.
- Net: strong hero, weak body. This earns a clear step up from the brand's recent 6.0 average because the core promise-to-payoff loop finally works.

## What's working

- Hero delivers a real, specific offer — 25% off sitewide, code FALL25, hard deadline of September 30 — no vague "deals" language.
- High-contrast, unambiguous CTA: black "Shop now" button pops against the pink/red hero.
- Fine print ("Not combinable. Excludes Happy Beauty Co. purchases & clearance") is visible but small and non-intrusive — feels honest, not buried.
- Utility bar up top (BOPIS, 2-hour delivery) and Klarna financing block give real added value beyond the discount.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- Product grid is 100% professional hair-color developer/sealer items ("ion" line) — mismatched for a general sitewide-sale send and not an obvious "favorite" for most subscribers.
- No hero imagery/lifestyle photography — it's a solid-color graphic treatment, which is fine for urgency but is a missed chance to show product/results.
- Subject line "🎉 Appiversary deals end soon!" doesn't mention the actual number (25% off) that's sitting right there in the hero — a stronger lever left on the table.
- Heavy footer/boilerplate stack (Klarna, 5 service icons, app download, 5 social icons, legal) pushes total scroll length up with no differentiated content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 25% and the code in the subject line — it's the strongest asset in the email and currently invisible until open. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Swap the "Shop Your Beauty Favorites" product grid to actual sitewide-relevant categories (color, hair care, nails) instead of four professional developer/sealer SKUs — as-is it undercuts the "sitewide" claim by showing niche pro items.
- 3. Add a product photo or short lifestyle shot to the hero to break up the flat color block and give the eye something besides text.
- 4. Trim the footer stack — Klarna + service icons + app download + socials is a lot of scroll for zero incremental offer value; consider collapsing service icons into one row already there and cutting either Klarna or app-download promotion.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off sitewide ends Tuesday — code FALL25`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Appiversary 25% off ends 9/30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sitewide savings on hair color, care & nails — use code FALL25`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two more days to save 25% storewide, no exclusions on most items`
