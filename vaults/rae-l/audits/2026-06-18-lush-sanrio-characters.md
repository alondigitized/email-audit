# Lush × Sanrio Characters
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- Lush is announcing a licensed collaboration with Sanrio — specifically featuring Pompompurin, Bad Badtz Maru, and Hangyodon — under the "THE SWEETEST REUNION" creative concept, with friendship framed as the ultimate act of self-care. The art direction is charming: a kawaii picnic hero with the Sanrio aesthetic absorbed cleanly into Lush's usual editorial playfulness. The email renders clean, the navigation signals the collection exists, and the brand × brand announcement in the subject is a genuine novelty after five consecutive own-brand sends.
- The problem is that this is all editorial and no product. Compared to the June 8 "What makes us smell so good?" send — which scored a 9 by naming four specific raw materials, four hero products, and connecting ingredient to benefit — this email names three Sanrio characters and then sends you to a "Meet New Friends" button that tells you nothing about what you're actually buying. The collab is the headline; the product is nowhere.
- The preheader adds a "$5 off with the Lush app" hook that primes value-shoppers, then the body delivers zero offer. That's a broken promise at the inbox level.

## What's working

- The subject line "Lush × Sanrio Characters" does the announcement job efficiently in 22 characters — short, novel, unambiguous.
- Hero image and headline land together as a cohesive creative unit; the kawaii picnic aesthetic is genuinely on-brand for both IPs.
- Naming specific characters (Pompompurin, Bad Badtz Maru, Hangyodon) over just saying "Sanrio" is the right call — it speaks to people who actually know the universe.
- Lush Club banner is present and clearly integrated, preserving the retention layer even in a launch send.

## What's weak

- "Meet New Friends" is the worst CTA in Lush's recent history — it doesn't say shop, browse, discover a product, or even what the friends ARE. Compare to "Meet New Friends" vs. "Shop the Collection" — the latter converts.
- The preheader "$5 off with the Lush app" promises a value moment the email body never delivers; the disconnect creates mild inbox whiplash.
- No product is shown or named — a collaboration launch should show me at least one hero item (the Pompompurin bath bomb, the Hangyodon body lotion, whatever exists); this email withholds that entirely.
- Demographic targeting is imprecise: Sanrio is a passionate fandom with a specific skew; nothing in this email signals it was sent to Sanrio-affinity subscribers vs. the general list.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show one product.** Add a single hero product shot — the most visually striking SKU in the collab — above the fold. This is the change that most directly lifts click-through.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA.** "Shop the Collection" or "Meet the Collection" beats "Meet New Friends" every time. If you want to keep the character voice, try "Meet Pompompurin's Picks →."
- 3. **Deliver the $5 off in the body.** If the preheader is promising $5 off with the app, that offer needs to appear in the email body with a clear redemption path — or pull the preheader and replace it with something that ties to the collab.
- 4. **Add one line of product specificity.** "Including a limited-edition Pompompurin Golden Latte Bath Bomb" is all it takes to turn a browse into a cart.
- 5. **Segment to Sanrio-affinity or anime-fan cohorts.** The body copy is already written for fans; the targeting should match.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pompompurin is officially a Lush stan`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fave Sanrio characters, but make it bath`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pompompurin, Bad Badtz Maru & more — see the full collection`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited collab drops now — shop before it's gone`
