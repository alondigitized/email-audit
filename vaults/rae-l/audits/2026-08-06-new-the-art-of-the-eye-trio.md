# NEW The Art of the Eye Trio
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA repackaging the Thru Line eyeliner launch into a three-piece bundle — shadow stick, the already-revealed eyeliner, and a mascara — under a "paint / sketch / frame" ritual concept. It's a smart consolidation move after three weeks of single-product storytelling (the eyeliner reveal on 07-28, the waterproof chemistry deep-dive on 07-30, the coffee-packaging story on 08-01) and a step up in specificity from yesterday's generic seasonal catalog send (08-05, "Unlock your summer glow," 9/10 on style but thin on offer).
- The standout element is the "(Eye) Color Theory" chart — a genuinely useful, scannable tool that matches eye color to recommended shades, something none of the prior sends in this arc offered. Visually it's clean, on-brand, and bug-free.
- Where it stumbles is the ask: the hero CTA ("A Daily Practice →") is mood copy, not an instruction, and the one concrete discount ("SAVE 15%") is scoped ambiguously to just the mascara rather than framed as a bundle offer. Good creative, muddled conversion mechanics — call it a solid 7-8 execution held back by a soft close.

## What's working

- The paint/sketch/frame framing gives three unrelated SKUs a single coherent narrative thread.
- The "(Eye) Color Theory" swatch chart is a novel, useful personalization tool not used anywhere else in this arc.
- The 4X VIP points banner at the very top gives loyalty members an immediate, scroll-free hook.
- Shade-range dot indicators under each product surface real product detail (shade count/variety) without a click.
- Clean macro photography and consistent navy/cream/blush palette; no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks.

## What's weak

- The primary hero CTA, "A Daily Practice →," is vague brand voice, not an instruction — it doesn't tell you what happens when you click.
- The only visible discount ("SAVE 15%") sits directly under the mascara section, so it's unclear whether it applies to the mascara alone or the full trio.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — even the VIP points multiplier reads as evergreen rather than time-bound.
- No social proof (ratings, review counts) for what's presented as a new bundle launch.
- This lands as roughly the eighth eye-category send since the 07-22 teaser — cadence is starting to feel relentless even with genuinely new content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero CTA with an action-first line — "Shop the Trio →" or "Get the Set →" — so the biggest button on the page actually tells people what happens next.
- 2. Clarify and elevate the offer: state "Save 15% on the Trio" once, near the top, instead of leaving a single button under the mascara section as the only discount signal.
- 3. Attach a deadline to the 4X VIP points banner — a passive perk with an end date converts better than an evergreen one.
- 4. Add a small social-proof strip (star rating or review count) near the trio hero or the color-theory chart to build confidence in a brand-new bundle.
- 5. Subject/preheader test:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Eye Color Just Got a Shade Match`
- **Subject Alt B:** `NEW Trio: Paint, Sketch, Frame`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: earn 4X VIP points on Thru Line liner`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Find your shade with our Eye Color Theory chart`
