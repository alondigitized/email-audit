# ▶ We’re treating you to something new
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's reset email after the Major Summer Deals arc closed hard on June 22–23. The pivot is intentional — swap discount urgency for a softer "something new" frame — but the execution spreads thin. The email is a multi-block digest: a Luxe Trial Size banner at the top, a purple hero announcing new arrivals, what appears to be a "TRY 1 + SHARE 200" referral or points mechanic, a product grid, a Sephora Collection 30%-off block, an editorial section, and a "Just Arrived" close. That's at least six distinct asks in one scroll.
- The hero copy "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" lands cleanly from a design standpoint — the purple block is prominent, and the all-caps treatment works. But it answers no question a subscriber would actually have: new what? In what category? Why now? Without the offer specificity that drove the last several Sephora sends, the email leans entirely on brand goodwill to earn engagement.
- The 30% off Sephora Collection discount — the only hard-number offer in the email — is buried below the fold, past multiple content blocks. That's a structural mistake. This email needed either a single hero offer or a genuinely editorial voice (like the "PLANE 👏🏽 BEACH CLUB 👏🏿" Chat format from June 21) — it tries to be both and achieves neither cleanly.

## What's working

- **Purple hero is on-brand and visually clean** — the "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" block breaks from the sale-heavy aesthetic of the prior arc and signals a content reset credibly.
- **Luxe Trial Size offer at the top** gives Insiders an immediate value hook before the main body even loads — smart placement for loyalty members.
- **30% off Sephora Collection** is a concrete, honest discount with a clear badge — it's real value, even if buried.
- **Cadence is appropriate** — six days after the June 23 closeout is a healthy reset window; this doesn't feel like pile-on.
- **No visible render bugs** — layout, imagery, and text blocks appear intact throughout the scroll.

## What's weak

- **Hero offer is zero-specificity** — "something new" names no category, product, or benefit; a subscriber has to scroll the entire email to learn what they're being treated to.
- **The 30% off is below the fold** — the email's only hard number is stranded three-plus blocks down, well past where most subscribers stop.
- **Digest structure dilutes attention** — six+ thematic sections with separate CTAs means no single idea has enough gravity to pull a click.
- **No deadline or urgency anywhere** — after two weeks of sale-pressure sends, "something new" with no time window is an abrupt shift with no replacement driver.
- **"TRY 1 + SHARE 200" mechanic is unexplained at a glance** — the section is intriguing but the payoff (200 what? points? dollars?) requires reading fine print that's too small to parse at a normal scroll speed.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name the new thing in the subject** — the current line generates zero information-value. If this is a product launch, name the product or category; if it's the trial program, lead with that.
- 2. **Promote the 30% off Sephora Collection to the hero** — a concrete discount beats a vague teaser every time after a sale arc; it gives subscribers a reason to click immediately.
- 3. **Collapse to two sections maximum** — the hero offer + one editorial or loyalty block. Six blocks is a homepage, not an email.
- 4. **Add a deadline to the 30% off block** — even a soft "this week only" would restore the urgency rhythm subscribers have been trained to expect.
- 5. **Clarify the TRY 1 + SHARE mechanic in one bold line** — "Try one free, earn 200 points when you share a review" or equivalent, in scannable text above the product grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New drops + 30% off Sephora Collection — this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Luxe Trial Size is waiting (+ new arrivals inside)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh arrivals landed. Plus 30% off picks just for Insiders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New launches, your free trial size, and a limited discount — all in one place.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`robots` meta typo** — `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` contains a space. Valid value is `noindex` (no space). Some crawlers may ignore the malformed directive, risking inadvertent indexation of the web-view version.
- **Zoom prevention** — `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta disables pinch-to-zoom. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, AA) requires content to be resizable up to 200% without loss of functionality.
- **Broad `text-size-adjust: none`** — Applied globally to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. Suppresses OS-level text scaling that users with low vision depend on. Should be scoped to `.sup` only.
- **Missing `lang` attribute** — No `lang="en"` visible on the `<html>` tag. Screen readers use this to select the correct voice/language profile.
- **Generic `<title>`** — `<title>Sephora</title>` provides no context in browser tab or web-view. Should reflect the subject line or offer.
