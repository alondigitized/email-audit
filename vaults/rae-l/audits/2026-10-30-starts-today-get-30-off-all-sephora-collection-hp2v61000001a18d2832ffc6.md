# STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😊
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest Sephora send in the recent run, and the reason is the offer. "Insider, Save on ALL Sephora Collection / STARTS TODAY" with a large 30% OFF badge is a concrete, dated discount (10/30–11/9). It pays off the teaser from 10/29, "Top-shelf beauty, right this way 👉🏽", which previewed this event. The subject and hero match, and the eye lands on the 30% badge first.
- The email runs out of focus after the hero. Below it are Insider perks (500 points, credit card), a "Beauty on Demand" fulfilment strip, a product grid, skincare and hair gift sets, a Kohl's store locator, a holiday guide, and a barcode. The offer is clear, but the page is very long and has too many competing sections. The Sephora Collection grid is the only part that supports the headline.
- Judgement: a good, well-timed promo with a clear hero, weighed down by catalog sprawl. Below the grid, the gift sets and Kohl's block pull attention away from the 30% offer.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF badge is the first thing the eye lands on, with the dates 10/30–11/9 right beside it.
- The subject and hero copy match, so there is no bait-and-switch.
- The two promo codes, ALLONSALE online and SCSAVE for Kohl's, are shown plainly.
- The "SHOP NOW" button is visible directly under the offer, and the free-shipping and returns line sits next to it.
- The Sephora Collection grid shows named items with prices ($13–$16, advent calendars at $99 and $190), so the discount reads as real.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, with a barcode and dense legal text at the bottom, so most readers will never reach most of the content.
- The Insider points challenge and Credit Card blocks sit right under the hero and divert attention from the 30% offer.
- "Terms apply" and the "for Sephora Collection" qualifier appear twice. The 11/3 "everything" teaser line also dilutes the message of this event.
- Skincare gift sets, Orbe hair sets and the "just arrived" cupcake item are off-theme for a Sephora Collection sale.
- The Kohl's store locator takes up significant space and is relevant only to a small share of recipients.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one Sephora Collection product grid, and a short footer. Move the points, credit card, gift-set and store-locator blocks to other sends or collapse them into one line.
- 2. Put the code ALLONSALE inside the hero button or directly under it, so the next step is obvious.
- 3. Add reviews or "bestseller" labels to the product grid for social proof, and link each item to its product page.
- 4. Add a clear "ends 11/9" countdown or line to strengthen the urgency.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code ALLONSALE through 11/9, plus free shipping, no minimum.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a hidden preheader and two first-party tracking pixels, and no unresolved merge tokens. The one hard QA failure is a missing plain-text part. The compliance and authentication gaps are unverified, because the relay may have dropped those headers. The source was truncated, so footer, body links and CTAs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 36 skipped (ranked by click likelihood). The QA data gives no per-link status codes, so no broken links are confirmed.
- **Open-tracking pixel 1:** `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`) and has no `alt` attribute. Because it is hidden, the impact is negligible.
- **Open-tracking pixel 2:** `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4958433775694848&mi_ecmp=20261030_SC_30_off_Starts_Today_BI` is 1×1 and already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. The QA "missing alt" warning for `o.gif` is a **false positive**, since an empty alt is correct for a decorative image.
- **Campaign ID:** the `mi_ecmp` value is date-stamped (`20261030_SC_30_off_Starts_Today_BI`), so it is traceable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is harmless in email but invalid.
- **Misplaced MSO conditional.** The `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` block sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` rule within `<style>`. Outlook ignores media queries, so the `.mobileHeaderLogo`/`img.mso-size` override is unlikely to apply as intended.
- **Fixed-width wrapper.** The outer table has `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The only mobile overrides visible are font-size and logo rules. `.tbl` and `.tbl_cntr` are not defined in the visible CSS, so horizontal overflow on narrow clients is possible but unconfirmed because of truncation.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. This is minor.
- **Structured data is weak.** The JSON-LD has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and a `PromotionCard` type with no properties. Gmail will gain nothing from it. The email also advertises a "30% off" offer with no code.
- **No plain-text alternative.** See section 5.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The recipient-specific identifiers are the encoded path in the `app.sephora.com/O/` pixel and `mi_u=4958433775694848` in the `mi.sephora.com` pixel. Both are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 characters.** This is a multipart/alternative deliverability defect. Spam filters penalize HTML-only mail, and text-only clients get nothing.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header, and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is **unconfirmed**. If real, it conflicts with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` could not be verified. This is a limitation of the relay rather than evidence of a failure.
- **Body compliance elements are unverified.** The visible source does not include the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked. QA states an unsubscribe link was always probed, but gives no result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unable to assess. No `<a href>` values appear in the truncated source, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked. The only attribution evidence is the `mi_ecmp` campaign ID in the pixel. Sephora appears to use Epsilon/Harmony wrapped redirects, which would hide the final destination until the click is resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Confirm the plain-text part** against the raw MIME. If it is truly absent, it should be added. If it is only a capture artifact, fix the extractor so the `text/plain` part is recorded.
2. **Capture raw headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (for example from the original message source) before treating the compliance warnings as findings.
3. **Suppress the false positive** for tracking pixels that already have `alt=""`, and skip `display:none` pixels in the alt-text check.
4. **Increase the HTML capture limit** so footer, CTA hrefs and unsubscribe markup are covered. Then resolve redirect chains to check UTMs and landing-page alignment.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag, and move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block.
