# Our very best—very on sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a review-driven Black Friday bestsellers send. The hero is "the black friday sale / our very best" in big maroon type over a model's profile, with a "20% OFF*" badge. A maroon "SHOP BESTSELLERS →" button sits right below. The "you rated: ★★★★★" block and the alternating product rows with real customer quotes are a good change from the plain-text recaps of Nov 13 and Nov 20 ("The Black Friday roundup"). Social proof is the right angle for a sale this late in the run.
- The email has two problems. The top banner says "UNLOCK 25% OFF SITEWIDE*" while the hero says 20%. This is the same mixed-percentage problem I flagged on the Nov 19 launch ("Up to 25% off, two gifts, and 2x points"). The hero headline is also partly covered by the model's head, so "very" is clipped and hard to read. A reader who skims sees two numbers and has to work out which applies to them.
- Overall it's a decent send, held back by an unclear offer and a long scroll. It is more useful than the roundup from earlier today, but it is the fourth or fifth sale email in a week.

## What's working

- Real customer quotes with names and the "you rated" star header give the sale a reason to click beyond the discount.
- Each product row has its own "SHOP NOW" button, so every product is a separate click path.
- The alternating image/text layout keeps a long scroll from feeling monotonous.
- The red VIP block ("unlock 25% off") is a strong contrast moment with a clear incentive: 2x points and free shipping.
- The "free gift edit" section shows what the gift is (complexion stick, mini of choice with $100+) with a visual.
- Brand voice and look are consistent with earlier sends.

## What's weak

- The banner says 25% sitewide and the hero says 20%. The offer is contradictory, and the asterisks add to the doubt.
- The hero headline is overlapped by the model's head, so "very" is partly obscured. This is a visible render and layout flaw.
- The email is very long, with six product rows before the VIP block, and the only gift or VIP detail above the fold is in the banner.
- The only price signal on products is a "30% OFF" badge on one item (Multi-Stick). The others show no sale price, so it is unclear whether the 20% applies to everything.
- The "SEE SITE FOR DETAILS" fine print is tiny and sits right under the gift CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer one number across the banner, hero and VIP block. For example, "20% off for everyone, 25% for VIPs" in one clear line, with the same wording in all three places.
- 2. Fix the hero so the headline is not covered by the model. Shift the type or the crop.
- 3. Show sale prices or a "20% off" tag on every product, not only the Multi-Stick.
- 4. Cut the product list to three or four rows and move the VIP block higher.
- 5. Put the gift offer (free complexion stick on any order) in the hero area, since it is one of the clearest value points.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rated 5 stars, now 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our best-reviewed picks are on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real reviews, real savings, plus a free complexion stick with every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `VIPs get 25% off. Everyone else gets 20% and a free gift.`
