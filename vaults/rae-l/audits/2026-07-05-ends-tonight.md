# Ends Tonight
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth and final email in ILIA's Buy 3, Save 20% promotional run that launched July 1 — a deliberate deadline close after four days of creative editorial variations. The strategy is sound: lead with product stories, end with a clean urgency hammer. "Ends Tonight" does exactly that. The execution is mostly tight — a prominent "LAST DAY" badge, bold offer headline, and one visible CTA — but the subject line wastes its one job by withholding the offer. Anyone who missed the prior four sends has no idea what's ending tonight.
- There is a visible render issue in the hero: the word "three" in "the power of [***] sale" appears to have failed to load in the custom script font, showing placeholder characters instead. It's a small flaw in an otherwise clean layout, but it undercuts the campaign name in the one send where brand consistency matters most.
- The offer itself is the best it's been all week in terms of simplicity — body copy is lean, the code is prominent, and the deadline framing is credible. After four editorial sends, this functional close is the right gear shift. The problem is pure subject-line execution: "Ends Tonight" without naming the offer leaves first-openers completely cold and gives engaged subscribers no new reason to click over yesterday's "3 lip shades" send.

## What's working

- **"LAST DAY" badge** is placed directly on the hero image, immediately communicating urgency before reading a word of copy.
- **Hero layout** is clean and uncluttered — product imagery, offer headline, single CTA, in that order with no visual noise between them.
- **Body copy is the tightest of the run** — one sentence of urgency, one sentence of mechanics, done.
- **Promo code ("BUNDLEUP") is surfaced in both the banner and the CTA button**, so it's impossible to miss.
- **Trust icon row** (1% for Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact Collective) consolidates brand credibility below the fold without cluttering the offer zone.

## What's weak

- **Subject "Ends Tonight" names no offer** — a subscriber who skipped the prior four sends has zero context for what's ending, which is a self-inflicted open-rate risk on the highest-stakes send of the run.
- **Font render failure on "the power of [***] sale"** — the word "three" appears as placeholder asterisk characters in the hero, breaking the campaign name at the worst possible moment.
- **CTA copy ("USE CODE: BUNDLEUP") frames the action as an errand**, not a reward — compare to "Build My Trio" or "Shop the Sale."
- **No product-specific anchor** in this send — yesterday's lip-shade focus gave subscribers something concrete to act on; today's send goes abstract ("your own routine"), which is a step backward in specificity.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — fifth consecutive promo email in five days; subscribers who opened all of them are getting diminishing returns on urgency that was already present yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the offer in the subject** — "Ends Tonight" is a deadline without a hook. "Buy 3, Save 20% ends tonight" or "Last day: 20% off any trio" converts cold opens that the five prior sends never reached.
- 2. **Fix the font render failure** — the "***" placeholder for "three" needs a web-safe fallback; add a `font-display: swap` fallback or use live text with CSS styling rather than a custom web font for campaign-critical copy.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA to describe the action, not the code** — "Shop the Trio Sale" or "Claim 20% Off" as button copy, with the code shown below or auto-applied on click.
- 4. **Anchor to one specific product or set** — even a single hero product with "or build your own" pulls readers who want something concrete rather than an open-ended routine builder.
- 5. **Trim the badge row** — 6 trust/loyalty icons in the footer is excessive for a deadline close; keep 1% for Planet + 1VIP, cut the rest for this send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 3, Save 20% — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 20% off any trio`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop curated sets or build your own routine before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code BUNDLEUP at checkout — sale closes tonight.`

## Technical Audit — ILIA "Ends Tonight" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on MJML via Klaviyo with MSO/VML conditional comments for Outlook and standard mobile breakpoints at 480px. The truncated source prevents full link and compliance auditing, but several infrastructure-level issues are confirmed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully audit** — HTML is truncated before body links appear. From what is visible:

- All anchor colors are overridden by five stacked `a:` rules (`a:not([name])`, `:link`, `:visited`, `:active`, `:hover` all set to `#5f4b3c`). The `a:not([name])` selector excludes named anchors but is parsed inconsistently in older Outlook WebView clients — verify CTA buttons use inline `style` color overrides, not class-only styling.
- UTM parameter presence on CTA links cannot be confirmed from the truncated source. Flag for manual check.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`@import` for web fonts — confirmed broken in Gmail and Outlook:**

```css
@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css);
@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap);
```

Gmail strips `@import` entirely; Outlook ignores it. Fallback chain `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` on `p, h1, h2, h3` is correct, so rendering degrades gracefully — but the Klaviyo custom font will never render in Gmail (estimated ~40%+ of commercial email opens).

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title> </title>
```
Renders as a blank tab title in webmail preview. Low severity but fix to brand name or subject line.

**`img` rule declared twice** — `img { border: 0; line-height: 100%; ... }` appears in two separate style blocks. Duplicate declarations are harmless but indicate template hygiene issues.

**Alt text on images** — cannot audit from truncated source. Confirm all `<img>` tags carry `alt` attributes; ILIA's product-forward imagery will render as broken boxes in image-off clients if omitted.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No merge tokens visible in the truncated source. Preheader text is hardcoded:

```html
<div style="display:none;..."> 20% off is over in 3, 2, 1... ͏ ͏ ͏ ...
```

The padding characters are U+034F (Combining Grapheme Joiner), a standard preheader-padding technique. No broken `{{ }}` or `{% %}` tokens confirmed in visible portion.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Cannot confirm from truncated source:**
- Physical mailing address (required by CAN-SPAM §5)
- Unsubscribe link and honor-within-10-days mechanism
- List-Unsubscribe header (not present in HTML; should be injected by Klaviyo at send time — verify in raw message headers post-send)

**Authentication** (DKIM/SPF/DMARC) — not auditable from HTML source; must be verified against received message headers. Klaviyo handles DKIM signing for `iliabeauty.com` if the domain is properly authenticated in the Klaviyo account — confirm `d=iliabeauty.com` in the DKIM signature.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot audit CTA link destinations or UTM parameters from the truncated HTML. The following should be confirmed manually:

- All CTA `href` values should include `utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=ends-tonight` (or equivalent campaign slug)
- Landing page discount code or offer state should match the "Ends Tonight / 20% off" promise — if the sale expires at midnight and the page reverts, recipients opening after expiry will see a broken experience
