# Color confidence delivered 🧡
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh COLORfest send in thirteen days, and it goes back to the campaign's founding mechanic — "Buy two, get one free select Wella hair color" — the same deal 8/6's "Your Best Hair Color Starts Here" and 8/13's "Final days for Buy 2, Get 1 FREE" already ran. The confetti-dot header, the "COLORfest" wordmark, and the single-model hero are all reused wholesale from prior sends; the only new element is a top-band "Flash Sale! 20% sitewide with code AUGUST20" banner competing directly with the BOGO offer for attention.
- Visually it's clean — no render bugs, one legible hero model, one clear "Shop Wella" CTA above the fold — but it's also the third time this exact "Buy 2 Get 1 Free select Wella" headline has anchored a send this month, and now it's paired with a second, unrelated sitewide discount in the utility bar. That's two competing offers before the reader even scrolls, which muddies what "Color confidence delivered" is actually promising.
- Judgement: competent execution, stale strategy. This has run before, almost verbatim, and cadence fatigue is now the dominant signal for a subscriber who's opened six of these already this month.

## What's working

- Single clear hero CTA ("Shop Wella") sits directly under a legible headline and is impossible to miss.
- Hero model image is high quality, well-lit, on-brand, no layout breakage.
- The BOGO offer is stated in plain language ("Buy two, get one free Select Wella hair color") with no buried fine print in the hero block itself.
- Four supporting product tiles (permanent color, toner, gloss, lightener) each have their own "Shop now" link, giving multiple paths to purchase for different needs.
- Rewards teaser ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward") is a reasonable secondary hook for loyalty members.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers stacked in the first 200px: a 20%-off-sitewide flash sale in the utility bar and a BOGO deal in the hero — no hierarchy telling the reader which one actually applies to them.
- This is a near-verbatim repeat of the "Buy two, get one free" mechanic already sent 8/6 and 8/13; nothing new is being communicated to a subscriber who's seen this offer twice already.
- Subject "Color confidence delivered 🧡" doesn't mention the offer, the deadline, or the discount at all — pure mood copy with an emoji, a step backward from 8/16's concrete "$10 off $50 / $20 off $75."
- No urgency or expiration date visible anywhere in the hero or product grid (the flash-sale banner says "Ends August 23" but that's a separate offer from the BOGO deal being promoted below it).
- Four-tile product grid dilutes focus right after a single-CTA hero — same "split attention" issue flagged in 8/14's send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer per send. Either lead with the 20%-sitewide flash sale (it has a real deadline, Aug 23) or the BOGO deal — not both in the same email. Running two live discounts forces the customer to do math on which is better, which kills clicks.
- 2. Retire "Buy two, get one free select Wella" for at least a few sends — it ran 8/6 and 8/13. Subscribers who didn't convert on those aren't going to convert on a third identical ask; rotate to a genuinely different mechanic or bundle.
- 3. Put a deadline in the subject line or hero — this is the only recent send with zero visible urgency, and it's underperforming its own history because of it.
- 4. Cut the product grid from four tiles to two — the toner and lightener are utility products, not the BOGO hero item; keep it focused on the color + gloss "system."

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off sitewide, today only 🧡`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix and match any Wella shade — the third one's on us.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code AUGUST20 saves 20% sitewide through Sunday.`
