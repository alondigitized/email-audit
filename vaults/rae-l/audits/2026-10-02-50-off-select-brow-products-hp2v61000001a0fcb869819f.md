# 50% OFF select brow products 🤨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-category Daily Deals send. The hero says "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT BROW PICKS / TODAY ONLY" in big black type on a peach-to-yellow gradient, with one white "SHOP NOW" button. The eye lands on the offer first, and the 8-product grid underneath backs it up with real names, strikethrough prices and sale prices.
- It's also more honest than the subject suggests. The subject says "50% OFF select brow products" with no "up to". The hero and the footer both say "up to 50%". Most of the grid is closer to 30%: Benefit is $28 to $14, but Anastasia is $26 to $18.20, Glossier $24 to $16.80, and GrandeBROW only $73 to $51.10. The two Sephora Collection items are the only other true half-off products. The subject over-promises, and the 🤨 emoji reads as a bit sarcastic.
- Judgement: a solid, fast-to-scan send that is better than the weaker new-arrivals grid from 9/25. It falls short of the 9/23 Hair Daily Deals send because of the subject mismatch, the narrow category, and the lack of any member-specific hook.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: "UP TO 50% OFF," "TODAY ONLY," and one SHOP NOW button, all above the product grid.
- The 8-product grid shows brand, product name, struck-through original price and red sale price, so shoppers can see the savings without clicking.
- The Benefit gel at 50% off and both Sephora Collection items at $8 deliver on the headline.
- The "Clean at Sephora" and "Sephora Quality / Great Value" badges add trust signals.
- The footer carries the exclusions/terms disclosure, and the top bar shows free shipping and same-day delivery.

## What's weak

- The subject says "50% OFF" flat, but the hero says "up to." Several items are only ~30% off, and GrandeBROW is just 30%.
- Nothing reflects the loyalty tier. The header shows "INSIDER | You have 0 points," and there is no member offer or points nudge. The 10/1 send leaned hard on 2X points.
- The "Introducing point of view" block pulls attention away right after the grid.
- Product titles are truncated ("Lamination...", "Defining...", "Eyebrow..."), which hides some product detail.
- No ratings or reviews anywhere, so the grid has no social proof.
- Brow is a narrow category, so only a subset of the list will care.
- The 🤨 emoji tone is ambiguous.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the offer: "up to 50% off" or name the actual best deal, and drop the sarcastic emoji.
- 2. Add ratings or a "Best seller" tag to the top 2-3 products to supply social proof.
- 3. Add a member line under the hero, such as "Insiders: earn points on every brow pick."
- 4. Cut or move the "point of view" promo below the footer content so the deal stays the only message.
- 5. Show full product titles, or put the shade or size on a second line.
