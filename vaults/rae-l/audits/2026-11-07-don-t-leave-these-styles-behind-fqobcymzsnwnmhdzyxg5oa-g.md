# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is another Skechers abandonment send, at least the seventh in a week. Compare 11/6 "This is perfect for you!", 11/5 and 11/4 "Your cart is one click away!", and 11/1 "Pick up where you left off!". The render is clean. A blue "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" headline sits over a single SHOP NOW button, with six linked sneakers below it. The 20% Plus-member banner comes after the products.
- The real offer is buried. Nothing above the product grid mentions a discount, and the hero only says "Go on, take another look." The only deadline, "Valid thru 11/10", is in tiny type under the banner button. The subject is a soft guilt line with no product, price or deadline.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but forgettable and a repeat of what this subscriber already got. It will get skimmed because the subject and creative don't differ from the earlier sends.

## What's working

- The 20% off Skechers Plus banner is high contrast, with a big "20% OFF" and a clear member benefit.
- The six-product grid is clean and evenly spaced. Each product has a descriptive, linked name such as "Women's Slip-ins: Aero Pulse Running Shoes".
- The product mix looks tied to the browsing, with slip-ins, max cushioning and a seasonal fall boot.
- The SHOP NOW button is large, centred and visible on the first screen.
- The render has no broken images, overlaps or layout bugs. The category stack (Women, Men, Kids and so on) is easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The discount sits below the product grid, so a skimmer sees no price incentive up top.
- The deadline "Valid thru 11/10" is small and sits inside the banner. There is no urgency in the subject or hero.
- The products show no prices, no sale marks and no sizes or ratings, so there is little detail to act on.
- The lower half is generic clutter: texting signup, app download, BNPL logos and social icons dilute the retargeting message.
- The fine print is dense: Plus login required, online only, and a long list of exclusions. That weakens how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus banner above the product grid, and put the date in the hero. For example: "Your saved styles: 20% off thru 11/10".
- 2. Show the price, and the Plus price where it applies, under each product. Add a rating or "Slip-ins" badge.
- 3. Lead with one hero product instead of six equal tiles, and keep the rest as a secondary row.
- 4. Cut the footer modules (text signup, BNPL, social) to one line. Vary the creative from the earlier sends.
- 5. Change the CTA from "SHOP NOW" to something specific, such as "Get 20% off my styles".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your saved styles: 20% off ends 11/10`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Plus members: 20% off the shoes you viewed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Aero Pulse, Endeavour and more are waiting. Plus pricing ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Log in as a Plus member and take 20% off regular-priced styles through 11/10.`
