# Proof it makes your skin better
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA back in "prove it" mode for Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 — same clinical-stat playbook that scored 8/10 on 08-20 ("Wore it to the swimming pool and it didn't run"), but applied to a different product line without the tight single-hero focus that made that send work. Here the email sprawls across four distinct pitches — clinical stats, ingredients callout, 1% for the Planet, and the brand-manifesto "Clean at ILIA" close — each with its own CTA, so no single offer or product dominates the way skin-tone eyeliner close-ups did two days ago.
- The "100% / 100% / 100%" stat block is a strong, scroll-stopping mid-email moment and clearly the best piece of creative here, but it's buried below a soft, un-specific hero ("the proof's in every drop") that doesn't actually state a claim. There's no discount, no urgency, no price — this is a pure brand/product-education send, which caps its performance ceiling regardless of execution quality.
- Solid, on-brand creative, but unfocused compared to the eyeliner arc's tighter sends — this reads like a 6.

## What's working

- The "100% / 100% / 100%" clinical stat block is the strongest visual moment in the email — bold, legible, backed by a real skin close-up.
- Ingredient callouts (hyaluronic acid, allantoin, niacinamide, squalane) pair each benefit with the actual active, which builds credibility.
- "1% for the Planet" reforestation section adds a values-based hook without derailing the whole email.
- Clean layout, consistent serif/sans pairing, and diverse model casting across skin tones throughout.
- Footer trust badges (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact, Personalized Shade Match, VIP, Auto-Replenish) reinforce brand credibility at a glance.

## What's weak

- Four separate CTAs (Shop Skin Tint, Learn More, Shop Now) compete for attention — no single clear next action.
- Hero copy ("the proof's in every drop") is vague and doesn't state what's being proven until you scroll.
- No offer, discount, or urgency anywhere — this is pure education/brand content, which caps click motivation for a deal-driven shopper.
- The email tries to do stat-proof + ingredients + sustainability + brand manifesto all in one send, diluting the "proof" concept the subject promises.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with the actual stat ("100% said moisture was boosted") instead of vague "proof's in every drop" copy — front-load the strongest clinical claim above the fold, matching what made the 08-20 send an 8.
- 2. Cut to one CTA. Drop "Learn More" and the second "Shop Now" — funnel everything to "Shop Skin Tint."
- 3. Trim the email to two sections: clinical stats + ingredients. Move the 1% for the Planet and "Clean at ILIA" manifesto content to a separate send — it diluted focus here just like 08-17's "More than just makeup" pivot underperformed the tighter product sends.
- 4. Add a concrete incentive (free mini, bundle, or %-off) since there's currently zero offer to drive urgency.
