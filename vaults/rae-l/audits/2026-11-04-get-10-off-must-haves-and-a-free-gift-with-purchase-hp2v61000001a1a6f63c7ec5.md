# Get 10% off must-haves AND a FREE gift with purchase ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "10% off must-haves AND a FREE gift with purchase", but the body opens with a lip-balm editorial called "MISTLETOE MUST-HAVES". The 10% discount and the gift with purchase aren't in the hero. This is the same mismatch as the 11/3 "10% OFF LUXE BEAUTY" send, where the subject sold a discount and the body was a product catalog.
- The product section is well built. Summer Fridays, Laneige, Eadem, Olehenriksen, Kayali, Gisou and Experiment each get a hero image, a "NEW / ONLY AT SEPHORA / LIMITED EDITION" tag, a shade or flavor name and a price. Below that sit Best of Concealer, Fragrance Gift Sets, a 250-point reward redemption and a Holiday Gift Guide. It's a good shopping email but a weak delivery of the promise in the subject.
- One limit on this review: the screenshot was heavily downscaled, so I read the fine print only partially. I could not confirm that any gift-with-purchase offer appears in the body. Verdict: solid product merchandising, mismatched to its subject, and about the 4th Savings Event send in 4 days.

## What's working

- The "Stacks with other offers" bar and the free same-day delivery code (SPEEDY, $75 minimum, ends 11/9) give a concrete, deadline-bound perk near the top.
- Each product block has a lifestyle image, a tag, a flavor or shade name and a price, which makes it easy to scan.
- The 250-point reward redemption block gives Insider members a loyalty hook.
- The seasonal "Mistletoe" framing fits early November, and the page renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlaps that I could see.
- The "Best of Concealer" and "Fragrance Gift Sets" side-by-side CTAs offer an easy way out for people who don't want lip balm.

## What's weak

- The subject's two offers (10% off and a free gift) are not the hero. The lip-balm theme leads, so the promise is delayed or missing.
- The hero button is a generic "SHOP NOW", and the product blocks have no individual CTA buttons that I could see.
- The long run of lip-product blocks is repetitive and makes the email very tall, so the secondary offers are buried near the bottom.
- The legal fine print is dense, and the 10% Insider and 30% Sephora Collection terms only appear in the barcode footer.
- This is the same Savings Event creative that has run since 10/31, so it adds to subscriber fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a "10% OFF + FREE GIFT WITH PURCHASE" banner with dates above the lip-balm hero, and show the actual gift image.
- 2. Make each product block clickable with a button such as "Shop Lip Sleeping Mask".
- 3. Cut the lip-balm list to 3 products and move the points and gift-guide blocks higher.
- 4. Say who qualifies for the 10% (Insider tier and dates) in the first screen, not the footer.
- 5. Space out the Savings Event reminders. This is the fourth one in four days.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a confirmed deliverability defect (no plain-text part) and several client-side rendering and accessibility gaps in the visible markup. The header-based compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed from this capture, because the relay likely stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirect wrapper:** Links go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1a6f63c7ec5f948f4bbcfb920/.../?__dU__=...`. The destination URL and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- **Low link coverage:** Only 12 of 53 links were probed and 41 were skipped. The QA data reports no probe failures, but the skipped links are unverified.
- **Two tracking pixels:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`, a hidden open-tracking `<img>` with `display:none`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=20261104_Holiday_Limited_Edition_Lip_Ba...`, a second tracker.
  - Neither is an error, but the second sends a `mi_ecmp` campaign ID from a separate system. Its "Holiday_Limited_Edition_Lip_Ba…" label doesn't obviously match this send's subject (10% off plus a gift with purchase). That is possibly a reused template ID. It can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing (FAIL):** The QA check found a text version of 0 chars. That is a deliverability and accessibility defect for text-only clients and spam filters.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):** Both are the tracking pixels listed above. They are hidden or decorative, so the fix is `alt=""`.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed 700px layout:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block sets no override for it, so mobile may scroll horizontally. The rest of the CSS is truncated, so treat this as likely rather than confirmed.
- **Broken MSO conditional inside `@media`:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block. Inside a CSS block, `<!--` and `<![endif]-->` aren't ignored as comments. They get parsed into the selectors, so `img.mso-size` and the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, are likely dropped by strict parsers.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`:** No layout table in the visible source has the attribute.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is harmless in email.
- **Unusable JSON-LD:** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block has `"discountCode": ""` and a `PromotionCard` type with no properties. Gmail strips or ignores scripts, so it has no effect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. Most of the body is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture it. This is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN):** Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for `shop@beauty.sephora.com`.
- **Footer not visible:** The footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) is cut off in the source, so those CAN-SPAM elements can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverifiable:** Tracking is through the `app.sephora.com/T/...` wrapper rather than visible `utm_*` parameters. Landing-page alignment can't be assessed without the redirect targets.
- **Probe results not reported:** The QA data doesn't list landing-page status codes or redirect chains, so there is nothing confirmed to flag.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Sender:** Add a `text/plain` alternative part. This is the one confirmed FAIL.
2. **Sender:** Set `alt=""` on both tracking pixels and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. **Sender:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. **Sender:** Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block and into its own `<style>` or comment, so the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule isn't dropped. Add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
5. **Sender:** Add a mobile override for the 700px table, such as `width:100% !important; min-width:0 !important`, if one doesn't already exist in the truncated CSS.
6. **Our pipeline:** Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay, or note when the relay strips them. Otherwise these WARNs can't be resolved.
7. **Our pipeline:** Resolve the redirect chains for the skipped links, especially the primary CTAs and unsubscribe, to check UTMs and landing-page alignment.
8. **Our pipeline:** Pass the full HTML to the audit so the footer and CSS can be checked rather than truncated.
