# Ready For A New Fall Shade?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Sally Beauty leaning fully into an L'Oréal Technique co-brand takeover, and it's a stronger, more focused send than most of their recent history. The hero image — a striking close-up of magenta box braids — is genuinely arresting and does the job the subject promises: a bold new fall shade. The six-product HiColor grid underneath gives real choice across the color spectrum (magenta, red, copper, auburn, violet, black onyx) without cluttering the top of the email.
- Where it falls short is specificity: there's no percentage or dollar discount anywhere in this email, just "Shop now" repeated eight times. Compared to 2026-09-21's "Last chance to buy more & save more" send, which had a real tiered discount ladder, this one is style-forward but price-silent — fine for brand-building, weaker for driving an immediate purchase decision.
- Solid seasonal creative, no render bugs, but it's a lookbook, not an offer. Judgement: a clean 6, held back from higher by the total absence of a concrete deal.

## What's working

- The hero image (magenta braids) is high-impact, on-brand for a "textured hair / bold color" persona, and renders cleanly with no artifacts.
- Subject "Ready For A New Fall Shade?" and hero headline "Keep Color Fresh All Season" are thematically aligned — no bait-and-switch.
- The six-product grid is well organized, evenly spaced, with consistent product photography (yarn/swatch shots) and legible names under each.
- Utility bar up top (curbside, 2-hour delivery) and the icon row (Klarna, in-store, shipping, delivery) add credible, non-promotional trust signals.
- Footer is clean and compliant-looking: unsubscribe, preferences, legal, account info all present and legible.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete offer anywhere in the email — no %, no $ off, no BOGO. Just "Shop now" x8. This brand has run real discounts before (2026-09-21's tiered $10/$20/$30 off), so the absence here reads like a missed lever, not a strategy.
- The hero CTA button ("Shop now") is generic and repeated verbatim under every single product — no differentiation in CTA copy anywhere in the email.
- No urgency or timing cue at all (no end date, no "limited time"), which weakens the "New Fall Shade" seasonal hook.
- The model in the hero shot is shown from behind/cropped at the neck — no face, which is a missed chance for demographic/persona connection.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete offer to the hero band ("20% off HiColor this week") — this is the single biggest lever; right now this reads as an L'Oréal ad, not a Sally Beauty promo.
- 2. Vary the CTA copy per product (e.g., "Get the shade," "Try Magenta") instead of repeating "Shop now" eight times — it's currently the flattest part of the email.
- 3. Add an end date or "while supplies last" line near the hero to manufacture urgency, matching the brand's stronger recent sends.
- 4. Show a face in the hero image or add a second lifestyle shot lower in the email — the cropped-neck braid shot is striking but impersonal.
