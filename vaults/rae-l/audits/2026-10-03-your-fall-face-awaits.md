# Your fall face awaits
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean seasonal catalog send. It's organized by Cheeks, Lips and Eyes, and each category gets a hero image, a short line of copy and a row of "We Suggest" products. It's the follow-up to 9/16's "Your fall palette" and covers much of the same ground (berries, browns, lips). Glossier's tone is intact ("a little moodier", "Basically, the whole forest floor, just prettier!").
- There's no reason to act now. The email has no offer, deadline, bundle or loyalty hook. After three fragrance GWP sends in a week and a teaser on 10/2 with nothing in it, a subscriber gets another browse-only email. The blush, balm and pencil photography is lovely, but nothing separates this from the 9/16 send.
- My judgement is that it's well made and forgettable. It reads as brand content, not a campaign.

## What's working

- The hero and section images are consistent and editorial: berry blush on a flower, a warm lip close-up, an eye with a copper liner.
- The three-part structure (Cheeks, Lips, Eyes) is easy to scan, and each section opens with a short label.
- The product rows give a shade name and a short descriptor ("A warm rose", "Roasted coffee + sheer mocha tint"), so the reader can tell what each product is.
- The models vary in skin tone and the product shots look natural, so the send feels inclusive and authentic.
- The "Shop now" buttons are consistent, and each product has its own link.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, urgency or member perk anywhere, so there's no reason to buy today.
- Every button says "Shop now", which makes 13 identical CTAs. None names the product or category.
- The layout is clipped at the right edge. The Eyes copy cuts off at "ochre an", and the right-hand images and buttons run to the edge of the frame.
- There are no reviews, ratings or prices, so the reader can't judge whether a product is worth clicking.
- The email is long, and the 9/16 send had much the same blush, lip and eye lineup.
- The footer's oversized smiley and hand graphics are playful but take up a lot of space.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one reason to act.** Put a single hook in the hero, such as a free mini with a $40+ fall order, free shipping or a member early-access window. This lifts the email more than anything else.
- 2. **Fix the right-edge clipping.** Check the layout at the container width so no copy or image is cut off.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTAs** as "Shop berry blush", "Shop lip balms" and "Shop eye pencils". Put a price or a star rating under each product.
- 4. **Cut the email to one hero and one product row per category**, or lead with a single bestseller such as Cloud Paint in Storm.
- 5. **Be clear about what's new.** If this lineup is new, say so in the hero. If it isn't, don't repeat 9/16 with different photos.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Berry blush is back in stock`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cloud Paint in Storm, Espresso Balm Dotcom and more. Free shipping over $40.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cheeks, lips, eyes: the moodier fall edit, shade by shade.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Stripo template with Klaviyo click and open tracking, and the visible source is structurally sound. The real defects are 15 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute. The three header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as sender-side problems.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Stray tracking attribute:** `<body>` begins with `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M40PS0FK4188CGS8GYNJTQS1_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` are not valid on `<span>`, so the browser ignores them and the span does nothing. It looks like a leftover from the template.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 29 were skipped, with no failures reported. The skipped footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Redirect wrapping:** Links appear to route through `ctrk.klclick.com`, and the open pixel is `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M40PS0FK4188CGS8GYNJTQS1`. This is standard for Klaviyo and not an issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (15 images):** The QA check flags `heroimage_1.jpg`, `frame_3984.jpg`, `group_3973_2.jpg`, `group_3972_2.jpg`, `group_3974_2.jpg` (twice), `group_3996.jpg`, `group_3986_1.jpg`, `group_3987.jpg`, `group_3996_1.jpg`, `group_3973_3.jpg`, `group_3972_3.jpg`, `group_3974_3.jpg` (twice) and the open pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M40PS0FK4188CGS8GYNJTQS1`. All 14 content images are hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_e148…`.
- **Impact:** With images blocked or a screen reader in use, the hero and product imagery has no text equivalent. If these images carry the offer or product names, that information is lost.
- **Pixel:** The tracking pixel should have `alt=""` so it is treated as decorative.
- **Positive:** The head includes viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` meta tags, MSO conditionals, `lang="en"` and `dir="ltr"`. The hidden preheader uses `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN, List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers really are absent, there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **WARN, Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The note says this is expected via the relay. The sender domain `eml.glossier.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Footer:** The source is truncated before the footer, so the physical address and in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be checked. The QA probe always includes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. The visible links are wrapped in Klaviyo redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs can't be seen in the source. Resolve the redirect targets to check this.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 14 content images. Give the open pixel `alt=""`.
2. Remove the stray `<span rel="stylesheet" href="…klclick.com/l/…_0">`.
3. Check the raw message at the sender, not through the relay, for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `eml.glossier.com`.
4. Resolve the klclick redirects, including the 29 unprobed links, to confirm final URLs return 200 and carry consistent UTMs.
