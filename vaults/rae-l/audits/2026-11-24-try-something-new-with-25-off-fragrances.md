# Try something new with 25% off fragrances!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the best Glossier send in the recent run because it finally has a single subject. Earlier sends in this sale were broad recaps ("Loved by you, now 25% off" on 11/22, the Copywriter picks on 11/23). This one narrows to fragrance. The hero reads "Save 25% off all fragrances*" with a blue "Shop now" button, and a 30% off roundel in the lower right of the hero image. The category-specific angle is the strongest part.
- The body is a clean zig-zag of five Glossier You variants. Each has a one-word label ("The Icon," "The Beachy One," "The Sweet One"), a short note list and its own "Shop [name]" button. That is useful for someone choosing a scent they can't smell. The weakness is the offer. The hero says 25% off, the top banner and roundel mention 30% off $100+ orders and a free Crème de You, and the image says "25% off everything." That is three overlapping deals for a shopper to untangle, plus an asterisk. It is also the sixth sale-themed send in about a week, so the novelty has to come from the fragrance angle alone.
- Verdict: a solid, scannable send with good product storytelling. The stacked offers and the tight cadence keep it from standing out.

## What's working

- The fragrance focus gives a distinct reason to open, after days of generic "25% off everything" sends.
- The "The Icon / The Chic One / The Beachy One / The Floral One / The Sweet One" labels make five similar products easy to tell apart.
- Each block has its own labeled CTA ("Shop Doux," "Shop Soie," "Shop Fleur"), so the click goes to a specific product rather than the homepage.
- The notes lists (Pink Pepper, Iris, Abrox; Rice Milk, Tiare Water) give the sensory detail a shopper needs for a product they can't try.
- The image quality is strong and the layout is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The offers are stacked. 25% off fragrances, 30% off $100+, a free Crème de You and "25% off everything" all appear in the first screen, and the asterisk adds fine print.
- The top banner is cut off at "when you spend $100…". The key gift-with-purchase condition is clipped.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" marquee bar is also cut off at the right edge. It adds a heavy blue band but no new information.
- Several product descriptions clip at the right edge ("skin-scent-enhanc", "with notes", "Jasm", "Sandalwo"), which looks unpolished. The mobile layout appears to overflow.
- There is no social proof (reviews, ratings or bestseller badges) on products that people usually buy on trust.
- Nothing says how long the sale lasts, so there is no credible deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one offer for the hero. Lead with "25% off fragrances" and move the $100+ and Crème de You deal into a single line lower down, so the shopper isn't doing math.
- 2. Fix the clipped copy. Make the description columns and the top banner fit within the viewport so nothing cuts off mid-word.
- 3. Add a fragrance-finder cue, such as "Not sure? Start with You" or a discovery set link, to help first-time buyers.
- 4. Add a star rating or "bestseller" tag to each product block.
- 5. Add a real end date ("Ends Monday") to create credible urgency.
- 6. Cut the marquee bar, or use it for the deadline.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five scents, one guide. Pick your favorite before the sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From skin-like You to creamy Rêve, find yours.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-tracked template with a valid XHTML doctype, viewport meta, responsive media queries and an MSO fallback. The source shows no hard failures. The QA run produced 3 warnings (57% pass rate), mostly missing alt text and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified. The HTML was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing coverage is low.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 11, so the click destinations are mostly unverified.
- **Click tracking runs through Klaviyo.** Example: `https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M8780KQQYY0B84BE2F184R0K_0`. This is normal for Klaviyo, but the final redirect targets weren't resolved in this run.
- **Malformed tracking element.** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M8780KQQYY0B84BE2F184R0K_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `href` and `rel` aren't valid on a `<span>`. Clients ignore it, so it is low severity, but it is non-conformant markup.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M8780KQQYY0B84BE2F184R0K` is present (see the alt text issue below).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `heroimage_1_10.jpg`.
  - `95339270_2980034135406876_2064437603473555456_n_1_` (5 instances), all hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`.
  - The Klaviyo open pixel `01M8780KQQYY0B84BE2F184R0K`.

  The hero image likely carries the offer, so the missing alt text matters most there. The pixel should have `alt=""`.
- **Mismatched conditional comments.** `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opens in `<head>`, but its closing `<!--<![endif]-->` appears after `<body>` opens. This is a known Stripo pattern, but it is fragile. The `<style>` blocks (hover rules, media queries) are inside the non-MSO region, so Outlook desktop won't get them.
- **`<title>` differs from the subject.** `<title>` is "Try something new with 25% off scents!" but the subject is "Try something new with 25% off fragrances!". This has no functional impact in most clients, but the two are inconsistent.
- The preheader uses a hidden span with `mso-hide:all` and zero-width padding characters. That is standard and correctly implemented.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|`).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN].** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `eml.glossier.com`.
- **Footer not verified.** The visible unsubscribe link and physical address are beyond the truncation point, so CAN-SPAM body requirements couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. UTM parameters and landing pages are hidden behind `ctrk.klclick.com` redirects, and 11 of 12 links weren't probed. Klaviyo normally appends UTMs at redirect time, so this needs a resolved-link check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to `heroimage_1_10.jpg` and the 5 repeated `95339270_…` images. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw message headers. If the relay strips them, capture headers from the original message.
3. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `eml.glossier.com` using the original message or a seed-list test.
4. Re-run link probing with a higher probe budget. Resolve the Klaviyo redirects to confirm the destinations and UTM parameters.
5. Align `<title>` with the subject line ("fragrances" vs "scents").
6. (Low priority) Remove the `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` and fix the conditional-comment nesting, if the template is editable.
