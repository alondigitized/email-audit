# Shop 20% off sitewide
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's twelfth email in the Friends + Family campaign, and at this point the format has officially calcified. It's the same template you've seen all week: blue-and-cream hero with the "20% off bestsellers" lockup, a vertical stack of four hero products with "CLEAN + 5-STAR-REVIEWS" credentialing, then a Cart Deco free-mini incentive and the "Makeup That Makes You Look Better" closing card. Functionally fine, creatively exhausted.
- The good news: the products are real bestsellers (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Limitless Lash, Multi-Stick, The Base Face Milk) with clean photography and individual SHOP NOW CTAs — anyone who hasn't pulled the trigger in six days now has a curated greatest-hits to click through. The bad news: "Shop 20% off sitewide" is the dullest subject line of the run, and by send number twelve the urgency has fully evaporated.
- Verdict: a competent closing-window reminder that does its job, but adds nothing the May 22 "20% off makeup + skincare + SPF" send didn't already do better.

## What's working

- Clean four-product bestseller stack — each tile has clear photography, product name, category line, and its own SHOP NOW button.
- "CLEAN + 5-STAR-REVIEWS" overline above each product is a nice review-equity nudge without quote noise.
- Cart Deco free-mini callout at the bottom adds a real secondary incentive beyond the 20%.
- Hero offer is unambiguous — "20% off bestsellers" with the "extra 5% off for VIPs" strip across the top.

## What's weak

- "Shop 20% off sitewide" is the laziest subject line in a twelve-email run that has otherwise tried to vary its angles.
- Hero image is cropped weirdly — the headline "20% off bestsellers" sits on the left while a giant pencil/wand object dominates the right with no clear focal anchor.
- The "extra 5% off for VIPs" reads as a faint top bar that you might miss entirely on mobile.
- Subject and hero both say "sitewide" but the body shows only four products — mismatch between promise and proof.
- Twelfth send in a week — fatigue is doing more damage than the creative can repair.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the cadence, not the creative.** This email shouldn't exist as a standalone — it's a duplicate of the May 22 SPF/skincare/makeup send with the bestsellers stack swapped in. Either skip today or make it the final "closing tonight" hammer.
- 2. **Lead with the deadline.** If this is the last day, the subject must say so. "Last day: 20% off" beats "Shop 20% off sitewide" every time.
- 3. **Add a real countdown** — "Ends tonight at midnight PT" in the hero, not buried.
- 4. **Replace the abstract pencil hero** with a flat-lay of the four featured bestsellers so the email's promise (bestsellers) matches the visual.
- 5. **Surface the VIP 5% extra** — make it a yellow ribbon or sticker, not faint top-bar text.
