## What's weak

- Nine sequential product blocks with identical layout (name, photo, price, Shop Now) makes this feel like a catalog dump, not a curated "something new" moment — same structural complaint as the 8/16 and pre-8/15 sends.
- Subject promises one novelty ("something new") but the body is a scroll of nine unrelated new launches across skincare, hair, makeup, and fragrance — no unifying story.

- 3. Move the Rare Beauty Fragrance teaser up near the top — new fragrance launches typically outperform hair/scalp SKUs and shouldn't be buried at position 10.
