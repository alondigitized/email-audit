# NEW Thru Line Eye Essentials set
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA finally shipping the bundle it's been teasing since the Thru Line launch on 07-22 — the eyeliner, the angled/smudge brush, and the sharpener packaged as an "Eye Essentials" set. It's the natural endpoint of the ingredient-story arc (waterproof chemistry 07-30, coffee packaging 08-01) and it lands with a coherent "artist's toolkit" concept: hold the line / one brush infinite looks / get to the point, each beat paired with clean macro product photography.
- The email is well-built but visually monotonous — five nearly identical square product-macro shots stacked vertically with the same blue-text-on-white-button treatment repeated three times ("Save 15%" appears twice plus a claim-gift CTA). By the third repetition it reads as filler rather than escalating desire. It's also missing the human element — no face, no model wearing the liner — which the Rory Gevis send (08-03) had and this one doesn't need but would benefit from.
- Solid, on-brand, slightly padded. It's a good bundle offer buried in a long scroll that could be tightened by 40% without losing anything.

## What's working

- The top banner ("Choose a free mini Eye Stylus w/ $75+") gives an immediate, concrete incentive before any scrolling.
- Hero image cleanly displays all three set components (liner, brush, sharpener) with elegant "NEW SET" typography overlay.
- The three-beat structure (hold the line / one brush infinite looks / get to the point) mirrors the successful ingredient-story format from prior sends and keeps copy tight per section.
- 15% off is a clear, specific, visible discount repeated at two CTA points so it's hard to miss.
- Consistent product photography style (soft pink/mauve backdrop, curled ribbon prop) keeps brand identity intact across all blocks.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical square macro shots in a row with no variation in composition creates scroll fatigue — nothing distinguishes block 2 from block 3 from block 4 at a glance.
- No price shown anywhere — 15% off what? A subscriber has to click through to know if this is a $30 or $90 purchase.
- The "mini gift, big color" tube shot doesn't visually connect to the eyeliner set above it — it reads as a second, disconnected offer bolted onto the bottom.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — this could run for a week or a month, nothing signals scarcity.
- No social proof (review stars, "best-seller" badges) despite this being a flagship new-set launch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by one full product block — merge "one brush, infinite looks" and "get to the point" into a single scrolling module since both are secondary tool call-outs; lead harder with the hero + first CTA.
- 2. Add the actual set price (with the 15%-off math shown, e.g. "$54 → $46") next to at least one CTA so the offer isn't abstract.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("15% off through Sunday") to the top banner or first CTA button — this arc has run three weeks with no urgency lever pulled yet.
- 4. Connect the gift-with-purchase block visually to the set (e.g., show the mini stylus sitting next to the set) rather than as an orphaned two-tube product shot.
- 5. Add one small social-proof line under the hero ("4.8★ from early access reviewers") since this is positioned as the launch capstone of a month-long arc.

- **Subject Alt B:** `15% off: the complete Thru Line kit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Liner + brush + sharpener, bundled and 15% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Everything you loved about Thru Line, one set.`
