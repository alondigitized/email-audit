# Our go-to summer lip?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This lands right back in the arc Glossier had just escaped. Yesterday's "Meet the popular girls" (8.0/10) was a clean break from the nine-plus-day I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom run — and one day later, "Our go-to summer lip" pulls the Balm Dotcom + Lip Line pairing straight back out, dresses it in the same step-by-step tutorial format Glossier already used on 7/23 ("Your whole look, in a New York Minute"), and then tacks the free Mini Beauty Bag GWP back on as "STILL ON" — a mechanic that first ran 7/21 and got its reminder push on 7/23. A full week later, reviving it here reads less like urgency and more like inventory the campaign never really let go of.
- The creative itself is fine — clean product macro shots, a legible two-step routine, a genuinely fun (if risky) "match your lip shade to your nipple" hook in Step 01 that's very on-brand for Glossier's body-positive irreverence. But the render has a real problem: right-column copy is getting clipped at the edge in three separate places ("Chris wears Lip Line in Bit and I ❤️ NY Balm Dotco...", "Define your lips with Lip Lin...", "Choose the shade closest to your nip color-..."), which undercuts an otherwise well-shot email.
- Net: solid execution, tired strategy, and a visible layout bug. This is a mid-pack send sandwiched between two stronger ones.

## What's working

- Clear top-of-email offer statement — "I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom + Lip Line" is spelled out immediately under the headline, no guessing what's being sold.
- The Step 01/Step 02 tutorial structure gives the email a legible flow: define, then blend.
- Step 01's shade-matching hook ("choose the shade closest to your nip color") is a distinctive, on-brand piece of copy that stands out from generic lip-tint messaging.
- Product macro photography is sharp and on-model — texture and finish are easy to read.
- The GWP reminder at the bottom gives a second, concrete reason to click beyond the hero product.

## What's weak

- Body copy is visibly cut off at the right edge in at least three spots — this is a layout bug a subscriber would actually see, not a hypothetical.
- This is the second time in nine days Glossier has revived the same GWP mechanic (Mini Beauty Bag, $75+) without a fresh deadline — "STILL ON" doesn't read as urgent, it reads as leftover.
- The tutorial format duplicates the structure already used on 7/23's "Your whole look, in a New York Minute" — two step-by-step sends within the same short window flattens the format's novelty.
- Primary CTA is a flat "Shop now" — generic, doesn't point at the specific product (Lip Line or Balm Dotcom) being sold above it.
- No deadline anywhere on the GWP offer, so "still have time" carries no real urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the layout clipping first — the right-column copy overflow is the single most damaging issue here since it's visible on open, not a strategy nitpick.
- 2. Replace "Shop now" with product-specific CTA copy — "Shop Lip Line" / "Get the Balm" — so each section's button matches its section.
- 3. Either give the GWP offer a hard deadline or retire it — an eight-day-old "still on" freebie with no end date reads as clutter, not urgency.
- 4. Space out tutorial-format sends — alternate structure (bag check, look book, tutorial) so back-to-back sends don't feel like reruns.
