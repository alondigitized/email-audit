# A quick note on Friends + Family
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a deliberate format break — plain-text "personal note" from Marissa, dropped after four editorial-heavy sends, attempting to cut through ILIA's own inbox noise. The strategy is sound: the 5th email in 4 days needs to look different, and the personal format does feel distinct. The VIP upgrade from 20% to 25% is a smart loyalty hook, and the tiered gifting structure (free mini → Sunshift bronzer on $100+ → Barrier Build on $175+) is genuinely compelling.
- The execution falls short, though. Stripping the template also strips every visual asset that made the earlier sends work — no photography, no button, no hierarchy. The sale's strongest selling points (the gift tiers, the VIP bump, the new-arrivals inclusion) are buried in body paragraphs. The CTA is a bare underlined text link mid-sentence, not a button. And "Hi there," is a jarring depersonalization for day five of a welcome series.
- Compared to the 5/16 welcome email — which combined editorial warmth with a hard 20% offer in a beautifully designed layout — this reads like a rushed follow-up reminder rather than a considered send. The offer is still the same Friends + Family sale; the execution regresses to a Google Doc.

## What's working

- Format contrast earns attention — plain text stands out after four polished editorial emails, signaling "this is different/personal"
- Tiered gift structure is clearly bulleted and easy to skim at a glance
- VIP 25% vs. standard 20% creates a genuine incentive to sign in before shopping
- Specific product callouts (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Sun Serum SPF 50) remind subscribers what they actually want
- P.S. fine print is complete and honest — deadline date, checkout note, site link

## What's weak

- No CTA button — "sign in to your account" as an underlined inline link is visually weak and easy to scroll past
- "Hi there," is generic; five days into an ILIA relationship, the brand should have a first name
- No urgency visible from the inbox — subject "A quick note on Friends + Family" is passive and deadline-free
- Second email on 5/19 alone — cadence fatigue is real, this should have been the only send today
- Plain-text strips the product photography and brand warmth that made the 5/16–5/18 run so effective

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a real CTA** — even within a plain-text format, a bolded "→ Shop the sale" line or a second P.S. with a direct shop link would lift clicks without breaking the personal tone
- 2. **Subject needs the deadline** — "A quick note" signals low stakes; the sale end date (5/28) or the VIP bump are both stronger hooks
- 3. **Personalize the greeting** — "Hi [FirstName]" is a trivial merge-tag fix that makes the personal-note format feel credible rather than mass-blasted
- 4. **Consolidate same-day sends** — the "perfect finish" email and this one went out on the same date; one of them should have been held for 5/20

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — ILIA "Friends + Family" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

A Klaviyo-delivered plain-HTML email with 5 tracked redirect links and one open pixel via `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`. Four destination URLs are broken due to quoted-printable (QP) decoding artifacts, including the unsubscribe link — a CAN-SPAM compliance risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

All CTAs route through `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01KS17H05P95917SSQDPERRYEQ_{0-4}`. The redirects themselves appear structurally valid, but the QA scanner resolved four broken destinations:

| Broken resolved URL | Root cause |
|---|---|
| `https://iliabeauty.com/collections/best-sellers)` | Trailing `)` — likely a template punctuation bleed |
| `https://iliabeauty.com/account/login)!=E2=80=8BHave` | QP-encoded zero-width space (`=E2=80=8B`) not decoded before URL construction; trailing body text was appended |
| `https://iliabeauty.com/pages/promotion=` | Trailing `=` — QP soft line break treated as part of the URL |
| `https://manage.kmail-lists.com/subscriptions/unsubscribe?a=3DSguDsz&=` | `=3D` → literal `=` not decoded; `&=` is a truncated second param — **unsubscribe flow is broken** |

All four failures share the same root cause: quoted-printable encoded content was not fully decoded before URL extraction. The unsubscribe breakage is the most critical — clicking the footer "click here" link would fail to process the opt-out.

**UTM tracking:** No UTM parameters are visible in the source-level `href` values. Whether they are appended at redirect resolution time is unverifiable from the HTML alone; if they are not, attribution for this campaign will be missing in analytics.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<html>` missing `lang` attribute.** Screen readers cannot infer document language. Fix: `<html lang="en">`.

**No `<meta charset>` or `<meta name="viewport">` in `<head>`.** Missing charset risks encoding misinterpretation in some clients; missing viewport degrades mobile rendering for any client that renders `<head>` meta.

**Link affordance relies entirely on `<u>` tag.** All `<a>` elements carry `text-decoration: none`, and link color (`rgb(39, 31, 31)`) is identical to body text. The only visual cue is the nested `<u>` tag on the inner `<span>`. This is fragile: if the `<u>` is stripped or the `<span>` color is overridden by a client, links become invisible.

**Tracking pixel alt text:** `alt=""` on the 1×1 open pixel — this is acceptable and correct for tracking pixels; not flagged as a real issue.

**Custom font load:** `@import url("https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css")` is correctly wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]>` to skip Outlook. No issue here.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Greeting is unpersonalized:** `"Hi there,"` — Klaviyo has first-name profile data; the absence of `{{ first_name | default: 'there' }}` appears intentional for a "personal note" tone, not a rendering failure. No unrendered tokens visible in the source.

**VIP offer not gated by segment:** The 25%-off VIP offer is rendered unconditionally for all recipients. If non-VIP subscribers receive this, it either creates expectation mismatch at checkout or signals a missing `{% if profile.vip %}` conditional block. Cannot confirm from HTML alone whether list segmentation handles this upstream.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[CRITICAL] Physical mailing address absent.** CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. The footer contains only an unsubscribe link — no street address, P.O. box, or registered address is present anywhere in the HTML.

**[CRITICAL] Unsubscribe link resolves to a broken URL.** As noted in §2, `manage.kmail-lists.com/subscriptions/unsubscribe?a=3DSguDsz&=` contains a QP artifact (`=3D`) and a truncated parameter. If this is the actual opt-out endpoint after redirect, the unsubscribe action will fail — a direct CAN-SPAM §5(a)(3) violation requiring honor of opt-out within 10 business days.

**[HIGH] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers not detected.** These are required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (>5,000/day since Feb 2024). Their absence risks inbox placement degradation and bulk-folder routing. Note: QA caveat states headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay — verify against raw headers from a direct delivery.

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown.** `Authentication-Results` header not present in captured headers. Verify that `send.iliabeauty.com` has a valid DKIM signature for `iliabeauty.com` and that the SPF record includes Klaviyo's sending IPs.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**No UTM parameters confirmed in source `href` values.** If Klaviyo's redirect layer does not append UTMs (e.g., `utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=friends-family-2026`), GA4/attribution will show these conversions as direct traffic.

**Three destination paths are currently 404-ing** (`/collections/best-sellers`, `/account/login`, `/pages/promotion`) — these are the post-redirect destinations after QP artifacts are included in the URL. The clean paths may resolve correctly; the issue is the artifact characters in the constructed URLs, not necessarily the underlying pages.

**Sale deadline:** Copy states `11:59pm 5/28/26` — 9 days from send date (5/19/26). Deadline is correctly absolute; no dynamic countdown or expiry logic present that could fail.
