# Comfort for every budget
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday gift-guide send built around price tiers. The 30% off member banner sits at the top, and three big tagged hero blocks follow: "Styles $50 and under", "$75 and under" and "$100 and under". The "Comfort for every budget" subject matches the body, and the tag-and-ribbon creative looks festive and consistent. The weakness is that the offer is split between a 30% off sitewide banner and three price ceilings, so the eye has no single clear target.
- The email is long, with three full-height lifestyle photos. Each photo has a "Shop Now" button, and the buttons say the same thing and go nowhere specific. The best-sellers row near the bottom looks like empty white space, with tiny shoes and no names, prices or reviews. This is a pleasant but generic send. It lands the day after the 11/18 "Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members" send, which had a much stronger hero, so it feels like a repeat of the same offer.

## What's working

- The price-tag motif ($50, $75, $100 and under) makes the budget framing concrete and easy to scan.
- The 30% off member banner is at the top, in maroon, with a "Shop Now" link.
- The holiday gifting tone fits the season, with red and green ribbons and tags.
- Photos of a woman, a man and a slipper show range across demographics.
- The footer nav (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, Gift Guide, Sale) and the Klarna/Afterpay row give plenty of exits.

## What's weak

- The 30% off banner is small and sits apart from the tagged heroes. The two offers compete instead of reinforcing each other.
- All three CTAs say "Shop Now", which gives no verb-plus-noun specificity and no category.
- The best-sellers section shows tiny, unlabeled shoes with no prices, names or ratings, and it looks half-empty.
- The email is very long, so mobile readers have to scroll past three full-bleed photos. The second and third tiers also repeat the same structure.
- The "AI-generated model" labels on the photos undercut the warm gifting feel.
- The 30% off banner's fine print is tiny, with the exclusions only in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make 30% off the single hero. Put it in the top banner with the price tiers underneath as "and under" shortcuts. Cut it to one hero image and one tier row.
- 2. Replace the three "Shop Now" buttons with specific copy: "Shop gifts under $50", "Shop gifts under $75", "Shop gifts under $100".
- 3. Fix the best-sellers module with product names, sale prices after the 30% discount and star ratings. Make the images larger.
- 4. Add a deadline, such as "Ends 11/22", so the urgency is credible. The footer says the offer ends 11/22, but the body doesn't.
- 5. Shorten the email by moving the three price tiers into one compact row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts $50 and under, plus 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gift picks under $100, 30% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 30% sitewide through 11/22. Shop by price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slippers, sneakers and boots for every budget.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based XHTML template with a responsive CSS block and a Gmail JSON-LD annotation. QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. Two images load over plain `http://`, one of them a tracking pixel that carries a recipient identifier. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be evaluated. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not probed:** 39 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped (e.g. `https://click.emails.skechers.com/ope…`). Redirect resolution and destination status are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_PRICE_ENGAGEDL90D_EN_11212026`
  - It uses `http://`, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and open tracking may under-report.
  - It sends `mi_u=8108776175`, a numeric recipient ID, in cleartext.
  - It is hosted on a third-party domain (`ink1000.com`), not the sender's own domain.
- **Insecure favicon:** `<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/lib/fe3515707564057f721c77/...">` is `http://` and on a different subdomain (`emails.nl.`) than the other image host (`image.emails.skechers.com`). Email clients ignore favicons, so this is low impact, but it is a stray insecure reference.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, `http://` source:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. If it is content, it is invisible with images off or to screen readers. If it is decorative, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `o.gif` has no `alt`. A pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` does not describe the message.
- **Text size adjust:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops iOS users from enlarging text.
- **Layout wrapper:** the visible outer wrapper has `role="presentation"`, which is good. I can't confirm this for the truncated remainder.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The only per-recipient value seen is `mi_u=8108776175` in the pixel URL, which resolved correctly. The truncated source can't rule out issues further down.
