# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a standard Skechers browse/cart abandonment email featuring the BOBS Misty Kiss - Summer Stroll sandal under the now-familiar "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT?" header. The production quality is genuinely solid — real product image, named product, clean grid, no broken assets, and a real discount offer (20% off with code SUMMER for Skechers+ members). If you judged it in isolation, it would hold up.
- The problem is you cannot judge it in isolation. This is the identical subject line ("This is perfect for you!") and the same creative template as the June 29 email — sent just sixteen days ago. The June 29 audit already flagged it as recycled. Skechers fired it again with no changes. At this point the email doesn't feel like a helpful reminder; it feels like the campaign manager copy-pasted the send.
- The 20% off Skechers+ banner is the only element that could justify another send — but it's buried below the product grid, well below the fold on mobile. The most valuable asset in this email is hidden where most subscribers won't see it.

## What's working

- Named hero product (BOBS Misty Kiss - Summer Stroll) with a clean, well-lit product shot — gives the email a reason to exist beyond pure brand spam.
- 20% off with code SUMMER is a real, concrete offer — not a vague "savings await" tease.
- Supplemental 3×3 product grid below the hero gives options for subscribers who aren't interested in the specific sandal.
- Seasonal coherence: sandals + "Summer" code + summer palette all align.
- No render bugs — layout is clean, all images loaded, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Subject line is identical to the June 29 send — burning subscriber trust by demonstrating zero creative investment.
- The 20% off offer is placed below the hero AND below the product grid, making it invisible to anyone who doesn't scroll — the most compelling element is buried.
- "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" is a weak CTA: no urgency, no specificity, no product name, no discount mention.
- No product details for the hero (no star rating, no review count, no "fits true to size" signal) — nothing to resolve the hesitation that caused the cart abandonment in the first place.
- No deadline on code SUMMER — an undated promo code kills urgency entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire this subject line immediately.** "This is perfect for you!" has now run twice in 16 days with zero refresh. Any subscriber who opened the June 29 email sees this as a broken record.
- 2. **Move the 20% off offer above the fold** — put the discount and code SUMMER in the hero block, not in a banner buried under a 9-product grid.
- 3. **Add a deadline** to code SUMMER. "Expires Sunday" or "48 hours only" would instantly change the click calculus.
- 4. **Replace "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" with** something that surfaces the discount: "Grab 20% Off" or "Shop BOBS Misty Kiss — 20% Off."
- 5. **Add social proof to the hero** — a star rating and review count under the product name would directly address why the subscriber didn't buy the first time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your BOBS sandal + 20% off — code: SUMMER`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still on the fence? Here's 20% off to help.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers+ members save 20% on regular-price styles — offer ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The BOBS Misty Kiss is waiting. Your discount code is SUMMER.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — SKECHERS "This is perfect for you!" (Browse Abandonment)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is sent via Attentive (`msgs.skechers.com` / `skechers.attentivemail.com`) with standard ESP click-wrapping. HTML is well-formed with MSO/VML Outlook conditionals present. Audit is limited by source truncation — body, footer, and image blocks are not fully visible.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**All links are Attentive click-wrapped** via `skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=...`. This is expected for the ESP, but:

- **UTM parameters on destination URLs cannot be confirmed** without decoding the encoded redirect payloads. If Attentive is not configured to append UTM params to the final destination, click attribution in GA4/analytics will be lost.
- The "web version" link in `row-1 column-2` resolves through the same click redirect — confirm it lands on a hosted mirror, not a 404, post-send.

**Action needed:** Decode one redirect URL and verify the destination contains `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` at minimum.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `<title>` tag is empty | `<title></title>` — screen readers announce this as the document title; some email clients surface it in thread previews |
| `alt` text on images | Cannot confirm — image blocks are in truncated portion; must verify all `<img>` tags have non-empty `alt` attributes |
| Preheader padding method | Uses a mix of U+034F (combining grapheme joiner `͏`) and U+00AD (soft hyphen `­`) — functional but CGJ characters are stripped by some aggressive spam filters, which could cause preheader bleed into visible body |

MSO VML namespaces, `role="presentation"` on tables, and the 620px mobile breakpoint are all correctly implemented.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- Subject line `"This is perfect for you!"` and preheader `"Take another look before these items sell out."` contain **no recipient name token** — acceptable for browse abandonment, but common to include `{{first_name}}` at minimum.
- **Cannot audit product recommendation tokens** (SKU, price, image URL) in the body — source is truncated. These are critical for a browse abandonment send; unfired tokens (e.g., `{{product_name}}` rendering literally) would be a P0 issue. Verify in the full source or a seed send.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Cannot fully audit** from truncated source. The following must be confirmed in the full HTML:

- [ ] Physical mailing address present in footer (CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5))
- [ ] One-click unsubscribe link present and functional (`List-Unsubscribe` header ideally present at the sending infrastructure level)
- [ ] Sending domain `msgs.skechers.com` has valid SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records — verify with `dig TXT msgs.skechers.com` and `dig TXT _dmarc.skechers.com`

From address `hello@msgs.skechers.com` is a subdomain send — confirm DMARC alignment (`msgs.skechers.com` must be covered by `skechers.com` DMARC policy or have its own).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- Cannot confirm UTM params on CTA destinations without decoding Attentive redirect URLs (see §2).
- Sending domain `msgs.skechers.com` is distinct from `skechers.com` — links should resolve to `www.skechers.com` product pages. Confirm no redirect chains that drop UTM params mid-hop.
- Browse abandonment emails should deep-link to the specific browsed product(s), not the homepage. Verify in seed send.

---
