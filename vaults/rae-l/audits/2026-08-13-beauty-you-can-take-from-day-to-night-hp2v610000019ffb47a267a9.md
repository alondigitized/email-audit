- The trial-size teaser up top (Rare Beauty lip oil with code DEWYBLUSH) is the only genuinely concrete offer, and it's buried above a much bigger, vaguer hero. ## What's working

- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breakage anywhere in the scroll.
- Trial-size + promo code (DEWYBLUSH) is a real, specific, low-friction offer right at the top.
- "NEW" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges give scannable signal on several grid items (Farmacy, Kérastase, ViktoRolf).
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) and points balance ("You have 0 points") at top personalize the send to a logged-in member.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "day to night" versatility; nothing in the hero or grid shows a look transitioning — it's just an eyeshadow product shot with generic tagline copy.
- The eight-item grid has no organizing logic (skincare, fragrance, hair, hand sanitizer, anti-aging serum all mixed together) — same unstructured-scroll problem flagged on 8/9, 8/10, 8/11, 8/12.

- Makeup by Mario eyeshadow is the closest thing to a "day to night" hero but it's disconnected from the top banner both visually and in copy.
