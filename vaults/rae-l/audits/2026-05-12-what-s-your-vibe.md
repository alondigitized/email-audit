# What's your vibe? 💫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a fragrance discovery email built around a "vibe" taxonomy — Lush takes five scent personalities (romantic, joyful, powerful, passionate, vanilla) and gives each its own section with two product recommendations. The concept is genuinely on-brand for Lush: sensory, colorful, playful. The execution is polished — clean renders, distinct visual treatment per section, consistent brand voice throughout.
- The fatal flaw is that it tries to be five emails at once. There's no single offer, no price reduction, no urgency, and no quiz or interactivity that the "What's your vibe?" hook promises. By the time you've scrolled through five vibe sections, the reader has decision fatigue and no clear reason to click any one thing over another. The email reads like a mood board, not a conversion tool.
- Verdict: well-produced brand content masquerading as a promotional email. Fine for top-of-funnel brand reinforcement; poor at driving a specific action.

## What's working

- **Consistent Lush aesthetic** — each section has its own color story (moody deep blue for "In the Mood for Love," bright citrus for "Ode to Joy") while still reading as one brand.
- **"Shop & Smell" CTA copy** — the primary hero CTA is specific and Lush-voiced; better than a generic "Shop Now."
- **Product pairing logic is clear** — two products per vibe category makes self-selection intuitive and reduces scroll-to-decision friction within each block.
- **"Absolutely Vanilla Absolute" bottom feature** — the single-product spotlight at the base gives readers one concrete anchor if everything else felt too broad.
- **No render bugs** — clean image rendering, no broken layouts, text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- **No offer** — no discount, no free shipping threshold, no GWP. For a purely editorial email, that's fine; but paired with the subject line's implied interactivity, it feels like the promise wasn't kept.
- **Five competing CTAs fragment intent** — "Shop Now" buttons across five separate sections dilute each other; there's no primary action that dominates.
- **"What's your vibe?" promises a quiz, delivers a scroll** — the subject and hero set up an interactive moment that never arrives. No quiz, no recommendation engine, no personalization payoff.
- **Hero body copy is too small to read on mobile** — the copy block under "VIBE SHIFT" is illegible at render size; the visual carries the section but the words don't land.
- **No social proof or reviews** — for a scent-discovery email where you can't smell the product, a single customer quote per vibe would do a lot of work.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one vibe and commit** — A/B or segment by past purchase: send the "romantic" edition to Rosa Jam buyers, the "energizing" edition to citrus buyers. One vibe, one hero, one CTA. This single change would likely double click rate.
- 2. **Add a real quiz CTA** — "Find your scent" → links to a Lush quiz landing page. The subject line earns this; the email currently doesn't deliver it.
- 3. **Anchor one product as the hero** — Feature Absolutely Vanilla Absolute (or whichever is the hero of this campaign) above the fold with the full product name, key benefit, and price visible.
- 4. **Add a price or offer** — Even "Free shipping on orders $65+" changes the email from editorial to transactional.
- 5. **Rewrite the hero body copy at a legible size** — whatever the value prop is, it needs to be readable at mobile resolution.
