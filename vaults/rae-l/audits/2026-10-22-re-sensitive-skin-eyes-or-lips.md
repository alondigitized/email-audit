## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style template with working fallback fonts and a hidden preheader. The QA run flagged two missing image `alt` attributes, and the HTML has no `lang` attribute and a blank `<title>`. The header-level warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are probably relay artifacts and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 material links were probed and 16 skipped. The skipped ones were footer, utility and social links, which are deprioritized by design. The QA data reports no broken or redirect-looping links among the probed set.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt.** The pixel at `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M5J1SBMAQJJK8G731AJP2DGB` has no `alt`. This is a minor, standard open-tracking pattern, but it's flagged by QA. Use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- The tracking domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` is a first-party subdomain, which is good for deliverability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on a content image.** `c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` (`https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/...`) has no `alt`. Screen readers and image-blocking clients will get no fallback.
- **No document language.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>` has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Blank title.** `<title> </title>` is whitespace only.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap)` and the Klaviyo `custom_fonts.css` are ignored by Gmail and some Outlook clients. The `font-family: "Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` stack degrades acceptably, so this is informational only.
- **Link styling.** The rule `a:not([name]) {color:#111;text-decoration:underline}` is fine, and the underline is preserved on hover.
- **Preheader.** The hidden preheader ("Our formulation approach" plus zero-width padding) is correctly hidden with `display:none` and `max-height:0`.
