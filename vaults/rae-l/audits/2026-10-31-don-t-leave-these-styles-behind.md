# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a browse-abandonment email, and the best part of it is the 20% off for Skechers Plus members. The offer sits low in the layout, though. The first screen is a big "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" headline, a soft "Go on, take another look," and a blue SHOP NOW button. It reads as a reminder with no reason to act now. The members-only 20% off, valid through 11/2, is a navy band below the six products.
- It also has a cadence problem. This is the latest in a run of near-identical nudges, after "Did you forget something?" on 10/29 and 10/30 and "Your Items Are Waiting!" on 10/30. The 10/30 "Your Items Are Waiting!" send at least had a Halloween hook. This one adds "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" and the same abandonment framing. The subject "Don't leave these styles behind!" is more concrete than "Did you forget something?", but it still doesn't mention a discount.
- Overall it's competent and clean, but the layout is backwards. Move the offer into the hero and this becomes a much stronger send.

## What's working

- The six-product grid is all women's sneakers: slip-ins, Cozy Fit and leather styles. It matches the browsing intent and the persona.
- The blue SHOP NOW button is large and contrasts well with the white background.
- The "20% OFF FOR SKECHERS PLUS MEMBERS" band is the highest-contrast block in the email, and "Valid thru 11/2" gives it a real deadline.
- The "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" copy ties into the Halloween date, so the email feels current.
- The render is clean. Nothing overlaps, no images are broken, and the footer navigation is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The 20% off is below the fold and under the products. Someone who stops at the hero never sees the incentive.
- Both CTAs say "SHOP NOW." That is generic and doesn't use the product or the discount.
- The product names are small underlined text. There are no prices, no sale marks and no ratings, so nothing helps a shopper choose.
- The fine print is dense and has many exclusions (sale styles, select accessories, prior purchases). It undercuts the "treats" tone.
- The long footer of nav, text sign-up, app, pay-later and store-finder blocks dilutes the message and adds a lot of length.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. Replace "Go on, take another look" with "Your saved styles are 20% off for Plus members through 11/2," and make the button "Get 20% Off Now."
- 2. Show prices, or a was/now price, under each product. Add one badge such as "Slip-ins" or "Low stock."
- 3. Link each product to its own PDP and make the product names bigger.
- 4. Fix the cadence. If the shopper already got a cart email in the last 48 hours, suppress this one or merge them.
- 5. Cut the footer to two or three modules.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off the styles you saved (thru 11/2)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your saved sneakers: 20% off for Plus members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% on select styles through Nov 2. Your picks are still here.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No tricks: the sneakers you looked at are 20% off, online only.`
