# Add a little glow to your holiday plans ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand holiday send, and it is nearly a repeat of the 11/09 "Finish your gift list today" email. That email used the same "Finishing touches for the season" hero and the same "Signature scents & skin care staples" line. The hero is a large, attractive portrait with a black Shop now button. It carries no price. The only real discount, "Up to 30% off" Black Friday, sits in a black block near the bottom, after four product tiles.
- The strip of three red banners at the top does the offer work. They cover store closures on Nov 26, free 2-hour delivery over $35, and 15% off a buy-online, pick-up-in-store order. These are three competing messages before the hero. The 15% off is arguably the best concrete offer, and it is buried in small caps.
- The judgement: tidy and festive, but a fifth or sixth near-identical promo this month. It leads with mood rather than money. It will probably get skimmed.

## What's working

- The hero photo is warm and polished. The model's skin-care gesture matches the "skin care staples" copy.
- The gold-glitter product tiles are consistent and easy to scan. Each has a brand, a product name and a Shop now link.
- The Thanksgiving closure notice is useful and timely, and it points to online shopping as the fallback.
- The utility row at the bottom (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping, 2 Hour Delivery, Buy Online Pick Up) answers common purchase-friction questions.
- The Klarna line gives a payment-flexibility cue for gift buyers.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. "Finishing touches for the season." is vague and repeats the 11/09 creative.
- The best number, "Up to 30% off", is buried below four products, with no clear scope for what it covers.
- The three stacked red banners at the top compete with the hero and make the header cluttered.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop now", including the four product links. Nothing is specific, such as "Get the cleanser".
- There are no prices, reviews or ratings on the products. The Q+A and Sabrina Carpenter items show no sale price or deal badge.
- The legal fine print is long and dense, and "Offers do not apply to…" is vague about what is excluded.

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Up to 30% off holiday beauty gifts" with a "Shop gifts under $X" button. Move the Black Friday block to the top.
- 2. Collapse the three top banners into one line. Keep the delivery cutoff or the 15% BOPIS offer, not all three.
- 3. Add sale prices and a "Save 30%" tag to each product tile. Add a star rating to build trust.
- 4. Swap the generic Shop now links for specific ones, such as "Shop cleanser" and "Shop Sweet Tooth".
- 5. Don't reuse the 11/09 hero copy. Give each send its own idea, or consolidate sends to protect the list.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 499px. The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The confirmed issues are third-party tracking pixels: an unresolved macro, a non-HTTPS pixel and missing alt attributes. The header-based checks are inconclusive because of how the email was captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the image `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The vendor-side macro was never substituted, so every open requests the same URL. The pixel can be cached or deduplicated, which undercounts impressions.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over plain HTTP. Clients can block it, and it leaks the request in cleartext.
- **Unverified links:** 54 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** the email carries at least three third-party tracking pixels: `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2337285/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, MyVisualIQ and Dotomi. Each adds a request, and any one of them failing or timing out can delay image loading.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on pixels:** four images have no alt text.
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`
  - `impression_pixel`
  - `UCMController`
  - One more with an empty `src` in the report, apparently a pixel that needs `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt on two other images:** the QA output lists two more images with no alt or src captured. If they are content images, they need descriptive alt text. If they are spacers or pixels, they need `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Unembedded font:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` names a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless. Only Open Sans is declared.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` isn't self-closed under the XHTML doctype. This is minor and doesn't affect rendering.
- **Truncated source:** the source was cut off, so I can't assess the body markup, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the `lang` attribute.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The visible source shows one unresolved token, `[CACHEBUSTER]` (see section 2).
- No other raw merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible portion. The body was truncated, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA couldn't find either header. The check notes that the AgentMail relay may not capture them. Their absence is **unconfirmed**: the sender may be sending them, but this capture doesn't show it. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) can't be verified.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com`, a subdomain, so alignment should be checked on the original message.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these weren't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Landing-page alignment could not be assessed. The 54 click-tracked links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the provided source.
- Because the links are redirect-wrapped, UTM parameters (if any) can only be verified by resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the tracking vendor substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send time. Otherwise remove the MyVisualIQ pixel.
2. Switch `http://login.dotomi.com/...` to HTTPS, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support it.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to any content images among the two unnamed ones.
4. Re-run QA against the raw message (full headers and untruncated HTML) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Resolve the 54 redirect links in a separate pass to check final destinations, status codes and UTM parameters.
6. Consider consolidating the three third-party pixels to reduce request count and failure points.
