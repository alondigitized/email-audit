# Not your garden-variety holiday look
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a holiday makeup-look email for ILIA, built as a six-step edit around the Ethereal Baked Face Palette. The "In Full Bloom" hero shows a hand holding lip products against white flowers. It's editorial and on-brand, and the one offer, a free mini Eye Stylus with a $75+ order, sits in a top banner. A Sam Michalska pull quote adds some authority before the numbered product list.
- The structure works as a curated edit, but it's long and the two CTAs point at a generic "Shop the Look." The free-mini offer appears three times: the banner, the "Free Mini" badge, and "The Gift of Shimmer." That is the same beat as the 10/30 "Just a few recs" email, which also led with a free mini mascara at $75+. A subscriber would see this as the same gift-with-purchase promo in a new layout.
- Verdict: polished and competent, but low on urgency and sent too close to a near-identical offer. It's a 6, like most recent ILIA sends.

## What's working

- The "In Full Bloom" hero is cohesive and seasonal, and the flowers tie into the "garden-variety" subject.
- The numbered 1–6 layout with a one-line benefit per product is easy to scan.
- Shade and finish detail, such as "Ink or Serif," "Venetian," and "Beam," helps shoppers who care about specifics.
- The "Gift of Shimmer" block uses a close-up eye image with a "Free Mini" badge, which makes the offer concrete.
- The footer carries trust signals: free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, and ILIA VIP.

## What's weak

- The free-mini offer is only visible in the thin top banner. The hero itself carries no offer.
- Both "Shop the Look" buttons go to a vague destination, with no per-product CTA among the six items.
- The email is very long, and the six-product list dilutes the hero. It also repeats the 10/30 gift-with-purchase structure.
- The pull quote is large and text-heavy, and it pushes the products below the first screens.
- There is no deadline or urgency language on the free-mini offer, and "see site for details" sits in the fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Free mini Eye Stylus with $75+," plus an end date. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Make each of the six products its own link with a specific CTA, such as "Shop the palette."
- 3. Cut to three or four products, or move the quote lower, so the palette is the focus.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the gift-with-purchase block.
- 5. Vary the offer or creative from the 10/30 send so the cadence doesn't feel repetitive.

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is a holiday makeup-look email for ILIA, built as a six-step edit around the Ethereal Baked Face Palette. The "In Full Bloom" hero shows a hand holding lip products against white flowers. It's editorial and on-brand, and the one offer, a free mini Eye Stylus with a $75+ order, sits in a top banner. A Sam Michalska pull quote adds some authority before the numbered product list.

The structure works as a curated edit, but it's long and the two CTAs point at a generic "Shop the Look." ## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template (the `kl-` classes and `static-forms.klaviyo.com` font import) with a hidden preheader, a responsive media query, and all links routed through the `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` click tracker. The QA run (57% pass rate, 0 issues, 3 warnings) turned up no confirmed defects. Several checks were inconclusive because of relay or probe limits, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** Only 12 of 28 links were probed, and 7 more were unprobed after the 70s time budget. Those 7 are neither pass nor fail. The QA output names only five of them: `.../01M6VBVK60SN9SV79E085WXTAP_1`, `_3`, `_4`, `_5`, `_6`. The count and the list don't match, which is a gap in the QA tooling.
- **HTTP 429 on three links:** `..._2` ("For the life of the party…"), `..._10` ("SHOP THE LOOK →") and `..._11` ("SHOP FALL SHADES…") returned 429. This most likely comes from the probe hitting the tracker too fast, so it is not evidence of broken links. It does mean the final destinations of the primary CTAs were never resolved or checked.
- **Opaque tracking URLs:** All links use the pattern `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/<ULID>_<n>`. This is standard first-party click tracking, but it hides the destination URLs from static inspection.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M6VBVK60SN9SV79E085WXTAP` is present as an open pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 2 images:**
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` is a content image. Without alt text, image-blocking clients show nothing and screen readers get no description.
  - The `/o/01M6VBVK60SN9SV79E085WXTAP` tracking pixel has no alt text. Low impact, but an empty `alt=""` is the correct markup for a pixel.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Missing language attribute:** The visible `<html>` tag has `xmlns` attributes but no `lang` attribute.
- **Web fonts:** The `@import` rules for `fonts.googleapis.com` (Montserrat) and `static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css` are ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The CSS falls back to `"Helvetica", sans-serif`, so this is not a defect.
- **Preheader:** The hidden preheader div ("Makeup in full bloom feat. The Ethereal Palette") is padded with `͏` characters, which is correct practice.
- **Outlook support:** The MSO conditionals (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not confirmed as a sender-side problem. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message from a direct delivery.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer content:** The truncated source doesn't include the footer. I can't confirm the physical postal address or the visible unsubscribe link in the HTML. The QA summary says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but it doesn't report the result.
- **From address:** `info@iliabeauty.com` is a monitored-style address, but the sending domain (`send.iliabeauty.com`) differs from the From domain. DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be confirmed without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. Every link is wrapped in the `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` redirector, and no probe resolved a final URL, because of the 429s and the time budget. UTM parameters and landing page alignment are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link probing with throttling or retry-with-backoff on 429, and raise the time budget. Resolve the final URLs of CTAs `_2`, `_10` and `_11` and the 7 unprobed links. Check for UTM parameters and 200 status on the landing pages.
2. Fix the QA tool's unprobed-link count mismatch (7 reported, 5 listed).
3. Capture the raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results, DKIM-Signature) from a direct inbox or a non-relay path before drawing any compliance conclusion.
4. Re-run with the full HTML, not the truncated version, to verify the footer: unsubscribe link, postal address, and preference-center link.
5. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
6. Add a `lang` attribute on `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`. Both are low-priority accessibility hygiene.
