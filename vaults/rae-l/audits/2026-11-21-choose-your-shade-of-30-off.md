# Choose your shade of 30% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-product push for the Multi-Stick. The hero says "30% Off" in giant maroon serif next to the product, and the "CHOOSE YOUR SHADE →" button follows right after. The eight-swatch "take your pick" grid is the best part. Named shades like "all of me / watermelon pink" and "dear ruby / poppy red" let you shop by colour from the inbox, and the grid makes the product tangible.
- The offer math undermines it. The top banner says 25% off sitewide for VIPs. The hero says 30% off Multi-Stick. The footer block says "the black friday sale 20% off sitewide*". The Nov 19 launch send had the same 20% / 25% mismatch, and this one adds a third number. A subscriber seeing all three has to work out which applies, and the asterisks make it feel like fine print.
- Overall it's a focused, attractive product send with a clear CTA, held back by a confusing stack of discounts. The hero also has the stick overlapping the "%" in "30% Off", which makes the headline slightly harder to read.

## What's working

- The hero offer (30% off Multi-Stick) and its CTA sit close together near the top.
- The 8-shade swatch grid has shade names and descriptors, so you can pick a colour without leaving the email.
- Two CTAs, "CHOOSE YOUR SHADE →" and "SHOP MULTI-STICK →", both name a specific action and a specific product.
- The lifestyle image with "buildable. blendable. breathable." shows the texture and wear on a real face.
- The VIP banner and footer trust badges (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact) reinforce the brand's credibility.

## What's weak

- Three discount numbers appear at once: 25% sitewide (banner), 30% off Multi-Stick (hero), and 20% sitewide (footer block). That reads as contradictory.
- The product stick overlaps the "%" and the "O" of "Off" in the hero headline, which hurts legibility.
- The email is long. The 20% sitewide block at the bottom competes with the 30% message after the reader has already been sold on the Multi-Stick.
- The asterisks on the banner and footer, with no terms visible in the body, weaken the "honest offer" feel.
- "limited time only" gives no actual deadline, so the urgency isn't credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer hierarchy unambiguous. Lead with one number, such as "30% off Multi-Stick, 25% off everything else for VIPs", and drop or fold the footer 20% block so it doesn't contradict the top banner.
- 2. Fix the hero lockup so the product doesn't cover the headline. Shift the stick right or shrink the type.
- 3. Replace "limited time only" with a real deadline ("Ends Sunday at midnight PT").
- 4. Add review stars or a "best-selling shade" tag to the swatch grid for social proof.
- 5. Make each swatch a clear deep link to that shade's product page, and add a "shop all 8" button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Multi-Stick: pick your shade`
- **Subject Alt B:** `8 shades, 30% off, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Watermelon pink to poppy red. Every Multi-Stick shade is 30% off through Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One stick for lips + cheeks. Find your match before the sale ends.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email using a branded click-tracking domain (`trk.send.iliabeauty.com`). The HTML is structurally sound, but the QA run could not verify the links, the compliance headers, or authentication. Most of the 57% pass rate reflects checks that didn't run, not defects found.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Four probed links returned 429 (rate-limited by the tracking host), which the QA tool itself says may still be valid:
  - `.../01M7XQERJ2BA77M44KXPXCP4H5_5` ("SHOP MULTI-STICK →")
  - `..._7` ("SHOP THE SALE →")
  - `..._1`
  - `..._2`
- **Coverage gap.** Only 1 of 12 material links resolved. Links `_3`, `_4`, `_6`, `_8` and `_9` were not probed after the 70s budget, so they are neither passing nor failing. The 429s also mean no destination URL was resolved for any CTA.
- **Opaque redirects.** Every link is a `/l/<id>_N` redirect, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. This is a content image, so the missing alt is a real accessibility gap. The CTA-like link text in the probe results suggests some text may be image-based.
  - `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M7XQERJ2BA77M44KXPXCP4H5`. This is the open-tracking pixel, so it is low impact, but `alt=""` is still best practice.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The root is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>`, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **`<title> </title>` is blank.** This is minor, but it affects the web-view and accessibility title.
- **`@import` for web fonts.** The `<style>` block imports `static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css` and Google Fonts Montserrat. Gmail and Outlook ignore these. The stacks fall back to Helvetica/Arial (`p, h1, h2, h3 { font-family: "Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif }`), so the impact is limited to font fidelity.
- **Preheader.** The hidden preheader div is implemented correctly (`display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`). The `͏` padding characters keep body text out of the inbox preview.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `*|FNAME|*`. The HTML was truncated, so the footer wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so it is unconfirmed. Klaviyo normally adds these, but I can't verify that from this data. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown for the same reason. The sender is `info@iliabeauty.com`, but the tracking domain is `send.iliabeauty.com`, so alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The destinations behind the `/l/...` redirects were never resolved (429s and the probe budget), so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-run link probing** with throttling or retry/backoff for 429s and a larger time budget. The goal is to resolve the final URLs for `_1` through `_9`, check UTM parameters, and confirm the landing pages return 200.
2. **Capture raw headers** (a direct-to-Gmail seed or raw `.eml`) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass and alignment.
3. **Add alt text** to the CloudFront content image (`c9cefca7-...jpeg`) and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
4. **Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`** and give `<title>` meaningful text.
5. **Check the untruncated footer** for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. **Update QA scoring** so unprobed and rate-limited links are reported as "not tested", not as shortfalls in the pass rate.
