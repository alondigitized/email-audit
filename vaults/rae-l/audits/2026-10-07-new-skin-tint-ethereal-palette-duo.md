# NEW Skin Tint + Ethereal Palette Duo
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand holiday bundle send, but it has no offer. The hero pairs a Super Serum Skin Tint bottle with an open Ethereal Baked Palette over a moody ocean shot. The serif headline "The Set With a Purpose" and the red "SHOP THE SET →" button are clear. The 1% for the Planet reforestation angle runs through the whole email, from the top banner to the leaf-and-bottles panel to the badge in the product block.
- The weakness is the payoff. A "limited edition customizable holiday bundle" with no price, no savings and no deadline gives a subscriber nothing to act on today. The cause story carries the email, and it is a values pitch, not a reason to buy now. It's also the third send in six days to feature the Ethereal Baked Palette. The 10-02 "It's back (with NEW shades)" send introduced it, and the 10-05 "Need a new coat?" send was a skincare layering routine. A fan of the brand is likely to notice the repetition.
- Verdict: well-made and trustworthy, but a soft sell. It would convert on brand loyalty and the cause angle, not on any incentive.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. Both products are visible above a calm ocean image, and the headline is large and legible.
- The red CTA button appears twice with identical copy, once after the intro and once after the product details. The red also matches the top banner.
- The product blocks give useful details: Skin Tint is SPF 40 with light-to-medium coverage and a hydration claim, and the palette has 1 bronzer, 1 highlighter, 2 blush shades and 8-hour wear.
- The 1% for the Planet cause story is told consistently, from the banner to the leaf panel to the badge.
- The footer is tidy, with free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, Pact recycling, shade match and ILIA VIP.

## What's weak

- There is no price, bundle discount or free gift anywhere. The only number is "1% of sales."
- There is no deadline or scarcity beyond the phrase "limited edition," so nothing creates urgency.
- The "Up to $100,000" fine print at the bottom of the leaf panel is faint and hard to read against the foliage. The "1% of Skin Tint sales" headline over that busy leaf background is also slightly harder to read.
- The email shows no reviews or ratings. The bundle's shade collections are also never named, so "customizable" is unexplained.
- The second panel is mostly a repeat of the cause message from the banner and intro, which pushes the product details lower.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete incentive to the hero and subject. Options are a bundle price ("Set $XX, save $YY"), a free gift with purchase, or a named deadline.
- 2. Make the CTA more specific, for example "Build your set →".
- 3. Show the three palette colorways and a few Skin Tint shades above the fold, so "customizable" is visible.
- 4. Move the cause story below the product block, or shorten it to one line, and enlarge the "Up to $100,000" disclosure.
- 5. Add one star rating or a short customer quote near the CTA.
- 6. Space out Ethereal Palette sends. It has now appeared on 10-02 and 10-07.

## Technical Audit

- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M4BMGZZ6PMDY66Z97FJ6YB6R` is an open-tracking pixel on a first-party tracking subdomain. That is normal, but its `alt` is missing (see §3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on a content image:** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. With images blocked or a screen reader, this image has no text equivalent. If it carries product or offer information, that information is lost.
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel:** it should use `alt=""` so assistive tech skips it. This is minor.
- **Empty `<title> </title>`:** it contains only whitespace. This has low impact in email but is sloppy.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat…)` and the Klaviyo `custom_fonts.css` import are ignored by Gmail and some other clients. The declared fallback stack `"Montserrat","Helvetica",sans-serif` covers this, so it is not a defect.
- **Outlook/MSO:** `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` (PNG, 96 PPI) and `mj-outlook-group-fix` are present, so there are no issues there.
- **Responsive:** the viewport meta tag and `max-width: 480px` media queries are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `*|…|*` or `%%…%%` appear in the visible source. The body past the preheader was truncated, so this covers only what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA note says the relay may not capture these headers. This is **unconfirmed, not proven absent**. If they are really missing, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for one-click unsubscribe would be violated. Verify against the raw message from the sender side or a seed-inbox test.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an artifact of the AgentMail relay, not evidence of failure. The `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` tracking domain does suggest a branded sending setup.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA note says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing. The footer was truncated, so neither the link nor the postal address was verified in the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The link hrefs were truncated, and 10 of 12 links were not probed, so there is no evidence on UTM parameters or landing page alignment. Nothing is flagged.

2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message, bypassing the relay. Add both if they are absent.
