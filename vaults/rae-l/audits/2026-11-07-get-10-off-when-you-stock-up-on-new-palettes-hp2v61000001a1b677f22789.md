# Get 10% off when you stock up on new palettes 💖
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, on-theme Savings Event send, and the subject lines up with the hero. The hero reads "Hot New Palettes" and has a SHOP NOW button. The Insider 10% and Sephora Collection 30% badges sit just above it. The body then runs a creator trio (Huda Beauty, Patrick Ta, Danessa Myricks), a priced 2x4 palette grid, a gift-with-purchase block, a Kohl's store locator and a barcode footer.
- It stacks too many messages for one send. A same-day-delivery bar, the savings banner, a gift-with-purchase offer, a hair gift-set module, the Kohl's store-opening block and the holiday gift guide all compete with the palette story. The subject says "stock up", but the body never builds a stock-up case with a bundle, a set or a multi-buy. The 10% offer also reads as Insider-only. I could only view the render at a very small size, so I'm judging structure and hierarchy rather than fine print.
- Compared with the 11/5 "10% off our most-searched beauty" send, this one delivers on its subject better. The product grid is genuinely palettes. It is still the sixth send of the Savings Event week, and the creative is largely the same banner each time.

## What's working

- The subject, "Hot New Palettes" hero and product grid all tell the same palette story.
- The savings badges (30% off 10/30–11/9, Insider 10% off 11/3–11/9) put the offer and the deadline at the top.
- The 2x4 grid shows price and shade name on every palette ($55, $72, $56, $64, $69), so there is little guesswork.
- The creator-led editorial blocks with large portraits give strong visual interest and a clear holiday-makeup angle.
- The barcode footer gives in-store shoppers a usable coupon.

## What's weak

- The "stock up" promise in the subject isn't supported by any bundle, set or multi-buy message.
- Three or four stacked banners before the hero (free delivery, savings event, promo code) dilute the focus.
- The generic "SHOP NOW" CTA links a whole hero rather than a specific product.
- The gift-with-purchase, hair gift sets, Kohl's store locator and gift guide drag the email away from palettes after the grid.
- No reviews, ratings or best-seller markers appear on the palettes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Keep the savings banner and palettes, and move the Kohl's, hair and gift-guide modules to a different send.
- 2. Give the hero a specific CTA such as "Shop 10% off palettes", and add "Insider" to the discount so eligibility isn't a surprise.
- 3. Make the stock-up angle real with a "build your palette wall" bundle or a "3 palettes, one haul" row.
- 4. Add star ratings or "Best seller" tags to the grid to give social proof.
- 5. Collapse the top bars into a single offer line.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Insider 10% off ends 11/9. Shop shades from $55.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is structurally sound XHTML 1.0 Transitional with MSO/VML namespaces. However, the QA run reports a failed plain-text fallback, and header-level checks (unsubscribe, authentication) couldn't be verified. The HTML is truncated after the logo block, so links, footer and compliance markup are unreviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link coverage is partial.** 12 of 58 links were probed and 46 skipped. QA reports no broken links among the probed set, but the skipped footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixels.** Two are present:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAn…` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4958433775694848&mi_ecmp=20261107_Holiday_Makeup_Palettes_Insider` is a 1x1 image.
- No issues found beyond that in the visible portion.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text, `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel:** The `<img>` has no `alt` attribute at all. The QA warning is valid, but impact is low because the image is hidden. Adding `alt=""` would fix it.
- **Alt text, `mi.sephora.com/.../o.gif` pixel:** The source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative image. The QA warning is a **false positive** from the checker treating empty alt as missing.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **Fixed-width wrapper:** The "Gmail Wrapper" table is `width="700"` with `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The only `@media (max-width:480px)` rules shown don't override it, so narrow viewports may scroll horizontally. Rules for later sections may be in the truncated part.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments inside CSS rules are invalid and can cause some clients to drop subsequent rules, such as `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Layout tables:** The visible tables carry no `role="presentation"`.
- **Generic title and robots value:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` uses a non-standard value (`noindex` is correct); this is harmless in email.
- **Structured data:** The JSON-LD block has an empty `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip `<script>`, so it has no effect on rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The subject and preheader are static. The only per-recipient value seen is `mi_u=4958433775694848` in the tracking pixel, which is populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders over 5,000 messages a day.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 characters.** A missing text/plain alternative is a deliverability and accessibility issue. It could also be a relay capture artifact, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from this capture. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) fall in the truncated section and are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** None could be assessed. The CTA hrefs are not in the visible source.
- **Campaign identifier:** The only campaign marker is `mi_ecmp=20261107_Holiday_Makeup_Palettes_Insider`, which is tied to the tracking pixel.
- **Landing-page alignment:** This can't be checked without the resolved destination URLs. QA didn't report any redirect or landing results.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the raw MIME for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the text/plain part before treating them as sender defects. Fix the relay capture if it is dropping them.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel. Fix the checker to treat `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` as valid.
3. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `lang` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML, then confirm UTM tagging, footer unsubscribe and address, and destination URLs. Probe more than 12 of the 58 links, including footer links.
