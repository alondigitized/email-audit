# ⭐ Memorial Day Sale starts NOW ⭐
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Glossier shows up for Memorial Day exactly as expected — concrete discounts, recognizable brand aesthetic, clean orange CTAs, and a GWP sweetener at the bottom. The "SALE SALE SALE" stacked typography in the hero is the strongest single creative choice: it communicates the message in half a second at any scroll speed. The product grid below it with visible "50% OFF" badges gives subscribers something to browse immediately, and the free Lip Glaze incentive at $35 adds a second conversion hook for fence-sitters.
- The miss is tactical: this lands in inboxes the same day as the pet UGC "The real hoodie thief" send — two emails in one calendar day with completely different tonal registers (playful brand content → hard promotional). That cadence friction will cost opens on whichever email arrives second. The other structural gap is the missing sale deadline. "Starts NOW" is good; "Ends Monday" would double the urgency pressure. Without it, subscribers can defer, and deferral is where conversions die.
- Net verdict: a solid, functional promotional email that does everything right at the base level but leaves a measurable conversion point on the table with no end date and generic CTA copy.

## What's working

- **"SALE SALE SALE" stacked hero typography** — visually dominant, impossible to misread, loads the message before any copy is processed.
- **50% OFF badges on individual product tiles** — browsable grid means subscribers can slot-match to their own wishlist without reading anything.
- **Free Lip Glaze GWP section** — second incentive layer (spend $35, get a freebie) that appears just as scroll momentum slows.
- **Orange CTA buttons throughout** — consistent, high-contrast, and tap-friendly; no visual ambiguity about where to go next.
- **Memorial Day timing is on-point** — sending May 21 for a "starts NOW" sale is credible and seasonally appropriate.

## What's weak

- **No end date anywhere in the email** — "Starts NOW" is only half the urgency equation; without "Ends Monday" or a specific deadline, subscribers can safely defer.
- **Two sends in one day** — the pet UGC email earlier today puts this in the inbox right behind a tonally opposite brand-content send; that double-tap fatigues the channel.
- **Star emojis flanking the subject** — ⭐…⭐ is the soft edge of spam-signal territory; major retail brands increasingly dropping emoji bookending from promotional subjects.
- **Product tile names are too small to read on mobile** — the images carry the load but product copy is unreadably small at typical render sizes.
- **"Shop now" on every CTA** — zero specificity; "Shop 30% Off" or "Get the Lip Glaze Free" on at least the hero button would immediately lift CTR.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a sale end date — this is the single highest-impact change.** "Sale ends Monday, May 26 at midnight" transforms a browsing send into a decision-forcing send. Add it to the hero subhead and the footer. The GWP section could also use a deadline separately if it's shorter-window.
- 2. **Rewrite the hero CTA:** "Shop now" → "Shop 30% Off Now" — same tap target, specific offer, no ambiguity.
- 3. **Suppress the second same-day send** — if the UGC email was already deployed today, this sale email should have been held to tomorrow. The inverse order (sale first, brand content second) would at least front-load the revenue signal.
- 4. **Increase product tile typography by 2-4px** — at current size, product names below the photos are unreadable on a standard mobile render without zooming.
- 5. **Pull the % discount into the subject** — the offer (30% / 50%) is the email's strongest fact; it's absent from the subject line entirely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Memorial Day Sale: 30% off starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off for Memorial Day weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus get a free Lip Glaze with $35+. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorites are on sale — 50% off selected picks this weekend only.`
