# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different animal from the "50% off Select Beauty" flash-sale run Sephora's been hammering all week — it's a Rewards Bazaar points-redemption email, and the subject "Did you know that points = rewards?" undersells what's actually a solid, well-organized send. The hero is clean and on-brand ("Grab September rewards while you can!" over a styled flat-lay of Fugazzi and Farmacy products), and the body does exactly what a rewards catalog email should: a scannable grid of point-tier products with clear point costs and scarcity counters ("16 AVAILABLE NOW").
- The catch is that this only works if you're a Beauty Insider member sitting on points — otherwise this is completely irrelevant, and the subject line does nothing to signal whether that's true for you. There's also no visible tier segmentation (Insider vs. VIB vs. Rouge) despite the nav bar showing all three tiers exist, so everyone gets the same 750-15,000 point range regardless of what they've actually earned.
- Compared to 2026-09-12's "50% off select beauty⏰" (8/10, clean single-offer execution), this is a weaker open driver but a more honest, single-purpose email once opened — no bait-and-switch, no digest-stacking. Solid mid-pack send, dragged down mainly by a forgettable subject and no personalization.

## What's working

- Hero banner delivers exactly what the subject implies (rewards you can redeem), no bait-and-switch like the 09-14 K18/50%-off mismatch.
- Clean grid layout with consistent product shots, point costs, and live scarcity counts per item.
- "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" burst badge adds a legible urgency cue without being spammy.
- Loyalty branding (Beauty Insider, tier nav bar) is visible and reinforces membership value.
- No render bugs — images, type, and spacing all look intact end to end.

## What's weak

- Subject line "Did you know that points = rewards?" is generic filler copy, not a real hook — it doesn't hint at scarcity, count, or specific products.
- No personalization: doesn't show the reader's actual point balance, so recipients with 200 points and recipients with 20,000 see an identical email.
- Tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is shown but not used — no indication which rewards are exclusive to which tier.
- Point thresholds jump steeply (1,000 → 15,000) with nothing in between contextualizing what's realistic for an average member.
- "Don't wait, they go fast!" urgency is generic — no deadline or expiration date attached.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with the member's actual point balance or a specific reward — this is the single biggest lever since the current subject gives zero reason to open over any other Sephora email this week.
- 2. Add "You have X points" dynamically near the hero (mirrors the "You have 0 points" text visible in the top nav) so the email feels personalized, not generic.
- 3. Segment the grid by tier eligibility, or at least flag which items are Rouge-exclusive, to make the tier nav bar meaningful.
- 4. Replace vague urgency ("they go fast") with a hard date — e.g., "Ends 9/30" — to make the scarcity credible.
- 5. Reorder the grid to put the lowest point-cost items (most redeemable) first, since 750-1,000 point rewards are likely accessible to the widest audience.
- **Subject Alt A:** `You have points waiting — see what they can get you`
- **Subject Alt B:** `750+ September rewards, going fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New rewards just dropped — some as low as 1,000 points`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fugazzi, Milk Makeup & more are back in the Rewards Bazaar`
