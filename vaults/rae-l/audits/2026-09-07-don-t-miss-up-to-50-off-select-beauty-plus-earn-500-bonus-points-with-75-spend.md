# Don’t miss up to 50% off select beauty! Plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 spend ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is another "Stacking Weekend" send — 50% off select beauty + 500 bonus points at $75 — but this time it's wrapped almost entirely around a single-brand launch feature for EADEM, a niche skincare line. The top banner does the subject-line job cleanly (50% circle, 500 points badge, "SHOP NOW"), but then the email pivots into a nine-product, extremely long vertical crawl through the EADEM line ("Spray and Soothe," "Boost Radiance," "Capsule Faves," "Patch It Up," "Glow On," "Get Sun-Soaked," "Mini Essentials") before finally circling back to a second 50%-off banner near the bottom.
- Compared to 2026-09-05's "Score 50% off select beauty 😎" — which nailed a clean hero + three-product proof strip — this send buries the actual subject-line offer under a wall of single-brand merchandising that has nothing to do with the 50%/500-point promo. It reads like two emails stitched together: a stacking-weekend promo email and an EADEM launch email.
- Verdict: the top banner alone would score well, but the body actively works against the subject line's promise. This is a targeting/structure miss, not an execution miss — the visuals are clean, just aimed at the wrong offer for most of the length.

## What's working

- Top banner clearly delivers both subject-line offers: "50%" hero circle and "500" points badge are immediately visible, no scrolling needed.
- "SHOP NOW" CTA button is present and legible at the very top.
- Consistent, uncluttered product photography throughout the EADEM section — clean backgrounds, no rendering glitches or broken images.
- Second 50%-off banner reappears near the bottom as a reminder before the footer modules (Real Luxe, Hair's Latest Lineup, Virtual Beauty Mixer).

## What's weak

- The EADEM section is nine near-identical product blocks in a row — a scroll marathon with no variation in layout, making the email feel much longer than it needs to be.
- Subject promises a beauty-wide 50% off + points event, but ~80% of visible content is single-brand EADEM merchandising unrelated to that offer.
- No pricing, discount, or "only at Sephora" differentiation shown for most EADEM products beyond repeated "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags — no sense of why to buy now.
- No social proof or reviews visible anywhere in the EADEM crawl, despite it being presented as "the latest and greatest."
- By the time a reader reaches the second 50%-off banner, they've scrolled past nearly the entire email — poor placement for reinforcing the actual named offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the EADEM launch to its own dedicated send, or cut it to 2-3 hero products max, and let the 50%/500-point stacking offer occupy the top 2-3 screens instead of one banner and a rushed footer callback.
- 2. Add a proof strip (3 discounted products with visible % off) directly under the top banner, like the 2026-09-05 "Score 50% off" send did — that structure converted better.
- 3. If EADEM must stay, break up the monotony with alternating layout (image-left/image-right, or a 2-up grid) instead of nine stacked identical blocks.
- 4. Show actual price-off amounts on at least a few EADEM items — "ONLY AT SEPHORA" repeated nine times isn't a purchase driver.
- 5. Reposition the second 50%-off banner to appear at the midpoint, not after the full scroll, so it isn't the last thing before someone has already bailed.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $75 unlocks 50% off + 500 points today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack the savings before this weekend's deals disappear.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New: EADEM's skin-barrier lineup just landed, too.`
