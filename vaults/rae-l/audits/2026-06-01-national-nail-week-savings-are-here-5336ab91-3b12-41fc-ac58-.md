# National Nail Week Savings Are Here 💅
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty pivots cleanly from nine days of Memorial Day fatigue into a new campaign hook — National Nail Week — and the creative earns the reset. The BOGO 50% off nail lacquer headline is big, unambiguous, and visually leads the email. Three vivid nail polish bottles in the hero do exactly what they should: signal the category and make the offer feel real.
- The pivot is the right call strategically, but the execution loses focus halfway down. The "More ways to save all month long" section drops a Wella Lightener Bundle and a hair care BOGO into what should be a nail-only send. Brazilian Glitter Wax + Wax Warmer is an odd pairing with nail lacquer. The cross-sell impulse is understandable, but it dilutes a themed send that had real focus at the top.
- Net read: this is one of Sally's better executes of the last two weeks — cleaner theme, specific deadline, current event hook — but it would be stronger without the mid-email category drift.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% off** is concrete, prominent, and framed cleanly in the hero — no ambiguity about what you're getting.
- **"Through June 14" deadline** is specific and credible, not vague urgency theater like the prior "Last Call" sends.
- **National Nail Week** is a genuine calendar hook that earns the send and explains the timing — a marked improvement over the Memorial Day parade.
- **Sally Rewards $5 reward / Klarna** add value without cluttering the hero section; they sit appropriately below the fold.
- **No render bugs** — all three nail polish product images load cleanly, layout is intact across the visible viewport.

## What's weak

- **Hair care mid-section undermines the theme** — the Wella Lightener Bundle and "buy one, get one 50% off select hair care" have no business in a National Nail Week email.
- **"Shop now" CTA is generic** — four instances of "Shop now" throughout the email, none differentiated by product category. "Shop Nail Lacquer BOGO" would do more work.
- **No preview text set** — the first text visible after the Sally logo is navigation bar content and a red banner, which will likely bleed into inbox preview text as near-junk.
- **Subject buries the offer** — "National Nail Week Savings Are Here" doesn't tell the subscriber they're getting BOGO 50% off. That's the hook; it should be in the subject.
- **After nine sends in 11 days**, the cadence remains a real subscriber-fatigue risk even with a new campaign theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the BOGO in the subject line.** "Savings Are Here" is soft. The offer — BOGO 50% off — is strong enough to headline. Lead with it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% Off Nail Lacquer 💅 National Nail Week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy One Get One 50% Off — Nail Week Starts Now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Through June 14 only — stock your nail stash for half the cost.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select nail lacquers: buy one, get the second 50% off. Ends June 14.`
- 2. **Cut or quarantine the hair care cross-sells.** The Wella Lightener Bundle and hair care BOGO actively confuse the National Nail Week narrative. Move them to a separate send or a collapsed "also on sale" accordion below the rewards block.
- 3. **Differentiate the CTAs.** Replace each generic "Shop now" with category-specific copy: "Shop Nail Lacquer," "Shop Wax Kits," "Explore Bundles." Specificity lifts click intent.
- 4. **Add one specific product callout in the hero.** Naming even one popular nail lacquer brand (OPI, Essie, China Glaze) with its BOGO eligibility would make the offer feel curated rather than blanket.
- 5. **Set a deliberate preheader.** The nav bar is almost certainly leaking into inbox preview text. A single line of preheader copy ("BOGO 50% off nail lacquer — through June 14 only") would recover that impression instantly.
