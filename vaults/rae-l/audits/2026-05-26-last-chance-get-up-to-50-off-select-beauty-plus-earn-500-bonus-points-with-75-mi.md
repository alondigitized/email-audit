# LAST CHANCE 🥺 Get up to 50% off select beauty—plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 min. spend.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-window mailer for Sephora's Stacking Weekend promotion — the same dual-offer mechanic (50% off select items + 500 bonus points on $75 spend) that launched four days ago on May 22. The creative is nearly identical to that send; what's new is an "ENDS TODAY!" urgency banner stamped across the top. The offer structure is genuinely strong and Sephora's brand equity is real, so subscribers who held off after May 22 have a legitimate reason to act today.
- The subject line is the biggest liability. At ~95 characters it's the longest in this campaign's run and tries to squeeze both offer conditions into one line — on mobile it truncates before the 500-point condition even appears. The all-caps "LAST CHANCE" opener trips low-level spam signals while the 🥺 emoji does more emotional work than the words around it.
- Below the fold the email doesn't tighten as a "last day" send should. A Rhode limited-edition feature, a store-locator map, and the standard Beauty Insider footer compete with the core urgency. Any one of those could be its own email; stacked here they dilute the "clock is ticking" energy the banner above establishes.

## What's working

- **Dual-offer badge boxes are scannable at a glance** — the side-by-side "50%" and "500" blocks communicate the stacking mechanic without reading a single line of copy.
- **"ENDS TODAY!" banner is visually dominant** — the high-contrast treatment above the headline makes the deadline hard to miss before a subscriber even reads the offer.
- **Beauty Insider integration is present throughout** — points earn, threshold bonus, and the program footer all appear, giving members a reason to check their balance before deciding.
- **Product grid shows named SKUs with prices** — concrete items with pricing give browsers a specific destination beyond a generic homepage link.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is ~95 characters — mobile hostile.** The 500-point condition, the more behaviorally interesting hook, is buried past the truncation point on most devices.
- **ALL CAPS "LAST CHANCE" reads as a spam signal** and undercuts the softer emotional register of the 🥺 emoji that follows it.
- **Same creative as May 22 with urgency banner swapped in.** Subscribers who opened four days ago will immediately recognize this as a re-skin — the scarcity feels manufactured rather than earned.
- **Store-locator section adds scroll depth without supporting the conversion.** In a same-day-deadline email, discovery navigation belongs in a different send.
- **No social proof anywhere in the product grid** — no star ratings, no review counts — on a page meant to drive product-level clicks.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the subject under 50 characters and lead with the deadline day.** "LAST CHANCE" without a deadline is noise; a specific time cue converts.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off + 500 bonus points 🥺`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stacking Weekend ends tonight — 50% off inside`
- 2. **Give the preheader a job the subject doesn't already do.** The inferred preheader ("Hurry, it's the last day to stack and save!") repeats urgency already in the subject. Use it to surface the points mechanic as a second, distinct reason to open.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 500 bonus points when you spend $75 — tonight only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 500 bonus points expire tonight. Spend $75+ to lock them in.`
- 3. **Remove the store-locator map from this send.** It belongs in a "find us" or local-awareness email; in a last-chance conversion mailer it adds scroll and no urgency.
- 4. **Add one review count under the top 2–3 products in the grid.** Even "4.7★ · 1,800 reviews" on a hero SKU would lift click-through for subscribers on the fence — this is the single cheapest, highest-yield change available.
