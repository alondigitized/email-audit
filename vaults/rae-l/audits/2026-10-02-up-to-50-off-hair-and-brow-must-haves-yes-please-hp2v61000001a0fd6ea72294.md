# Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves? Yes, please 💗
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This email is two sends fused into one 8,000-pixel scroll. The top is a clean "National Brow Day" hero: "Score up to 50% off select brow products," a LIMITED TIME ONLY badge, and a SHOP NOW button. Right below it, the Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar takes over. It runs "October means new rewards!", a "NEW REWARD OPTION" banner, then a long run of 100-point and 250-point reward tiles (Rhode, Dieux, Anastasia Beverly Hills, Jo Malone, Dior, Gisou, Color Wow). The hair half of the subject's promise only shows up near the bottom, as "Up to 50% off Hair Faves" under a "TODAY ONLY" badge.
- The subject sells a simple hair-and-brow discount, and the email delivers it in a first screen and a last screen with a points catalog between them. Further down there is also a 2X/5X points block with code SAYPOINTS, a "First access holiday drops" tile and a 20% off value-size hair care tile. A subscriber who came for 50% off hair gets a rewards-redemption brochure.
- This is also the second brow-50% send today. The earlier one, "50% OFF select brow products 🤨" (7/10), was a cleaner single-offer email. Sending a more cluttered version of the same offer hours later feels like cadence fatigue. Judgement: a strong top, but the middle buries the subject's second promise, so it's a middling send.

## What's working

- The brow hero is clear and above the fold: a specific "up to 50%" offer, a LIMITED TIME ONLY badge and a SHOP NOW button.
- The Rewards Bazaar rewards are concrete and aspirational. Real brands with point costs (100, 250) are a strong loyalty hook for members.
- The "Hair Faves" block near the bottom has a "TODAY ONLY" badge that creates credible urgency.
- The SAYPOINTS 2X/5X block carries an explicit end date (10/7), so the deadline is credible.
- There are no visible render bugs. Images load and nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- The subject promises hair and brow, but the hair offer sits at the very bottom, past the reward tiles.
- The email carries at least four unrelated messages: brow sale, Rewards Bazaar, points multipliers and holiday early access. None of them owns the page.
- There are many SHOP NOW buttons and no single dominant action.
- The reward grid is long, with small text and tiles of unequal visual weight. The "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" banner strips above and below the Bazaar hero repeat the same message.
- The rewards copy and the dense fine print with asterisks make the offers feel conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. Put the brow and hair 50% offers side by side in the hero, and move the Rewards Bazaar to a separate send or a short strip at the bottom.
- 2. Cut the reward grid to three to four tiles and link them to a single "See all rewards" button.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA and use specific copy, such as "Shop 50% off brows & hair."
- 4. Don't send a second brow-50% email on the same day as the earlier one.
- 5. Put the hair offer's "TODAY ONLY" urgency in the top hero too, not only at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: up to 50% off hair + brow 💗`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Brow Day deals + new Insider rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brows and Hair Faves, up to 50% off. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 3 new 100-point rewards, just for Insiders.`

## Technical Audit

The send-side issues are a 0-character plain-text part (QA FAIL) and two images without alt text. The missing List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results headers are probably relay-capture gaps, not confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated and 46 of 58 links were not probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken or redirecting links were reported among the 12 probed material links.
- The 46 skipped links are mostly footer, utility and social links, so their status is unverified.
- `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a long opaque token path on the ESP's tracking host. It is probably an open-tracking pixel, and it is one of the two images flagged for missing alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing:** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). This may be an AgentMail capture artifact, but if the message was sent as HTML-only it hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **Two images have no alt text.**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-...`) is a nav header image. If it is linked or carries text, it is unreadable to screen readers and with images blocked.
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/...` image is likely the tracking pixel, so the impact is low. It should still use `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). The CSS also sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`, which disables text resizing on iOS.
- **Malformed meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` carries no campaign context. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found** (WARN). QA notes the relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, but Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so the headers should be checked on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results was not found** (WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` could not be assessed.
- The unsubscribe link is reported as always included in probing, but no result was surfaced. The physical postal address was not visible in the truncated source. Both are unverified, not failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Link-level UTM parameters and landing-page results were not included in the QA data or the truncated source, so continuity could not be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify a text/plain part exists in the raw MIME. If it does not, add one that mirrors the offer and CTAs.
2. Add a descriptive `alt` to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and reconsider `text-size-adjust: none`.
4. Inspect the original message headers (not the relay copy) for List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
5. Fix `no index` to `noindex`. This is low priority.
6. Re-run QA with the full HTML and a wider link probe to cover UTM and unsubscribe-link checks.
