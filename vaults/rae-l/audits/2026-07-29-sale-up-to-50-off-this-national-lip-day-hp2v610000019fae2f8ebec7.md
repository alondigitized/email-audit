# SALE: up to 50% off this National Lip Day 💄
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's National Lip Day follow-up to a run of near-identical lip promos (07-25 through 07-28), and it's the first one in the streak where the hero copy actually delivers on the subject line — "Get up to 50% off Select Lip Picks" mirrors "SALE: up to 50% off this National Lip Day" almost word for word. That fixes the exact flaw that dragged the 07-26 and 07-27 sends down.
- But look at the actual product grid and the "50%" ceiling never gets touched — MAC's lipstick tops out at 40% off ($25→$15), everything else is 20-30% off. The tiny superscript "a" next to "50%" is doing a lot of quiet work here, flagging exclusions that never surface in the render. So the headline-vs-reality gap hasn't disappeared, it's just moved from "the number doesn't show up at all" to "the number shows up but nothing hits it."
- The email is also cluttered at the bottom with an unrelated "Buy More, Save More" Sephora Collection bundle deal competing for attention right after the lip grid. Solid, on-brand, easy-to-scan layout — but the honesty gap and the bolted-on second promo keep this from being a top-tier send.

## What's working

- Hero headline and subject line finally match — a real fix versus the 07-26/07-27 sends where the promised percentage never appeared anywhere in the body.
- Clean 2-column grid of 8 lip products with name, brand, and strikethrough pricing, easy to scan on one screen.
- "Clean at Sephora" badges on Gisou and Summer Fridays give ingredient-conscious shoppers a quick trust signal.
- Loyalty context is visible twice — the "INSIDER | You have 0 points" bar up top and the tier ribbon (Insider/VIB/Rouge) near the footer.
- No layout or image-rendering issues — everything loads cleanly, nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- No product in the grid actually reaches "50% off" — the real range is 20-40%, with the superscript "a" quietly signaling exclusions that are never shown.
- The "Buy More, Save More" black banner (unrelated Sephora Collection bundle, starts 7/31) is jammed in right after the lip grid and dilutes the National Lip Day focus.
- Zero star ratings or review counts anywhere in the grid — nothing here builds purchase confidence beyond price.
- CTA copy is just "SHOP NOW" repeated three times with no variation or specificity to what's being shopped.
- This is at least the fifth lip/beauty-adjacent send in six days per the brand history — cadence fatigue is real for a daily subscriber.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the headline number honest — either cap the claim at "up to 40% off" to match MAC's actual best price, or discount one hero SKU further so "50%" is真actually achievable somewhere in the grid. This exact mismatch has now shown up in some form across four consecutive sends.
- 2. Cut the "Buy More, Save More" block from this email entirely — it's a different offer mechanic (spend-based bundle vs. percent-off) and undercuts the single-minded lip sale message.
- 3. Add star ratings under at least the top 2-3 products (MAC, Charlotte Tilbury) — Sephora has this data and it's a proven click driver that's completely absent here.
- 4. Vary the CTA copy — "Shop Lip Deals" on the hero, "Add to Bag" or "Shop Shade" on individual products — instead of three identical "SHOP NOW" instances.
