# Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points with $75 spend ❣️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Sephora Stacking Weekend" send — the two headline offers actually match the subject for once: "50% Off Select Beauty" and "500 Bonus Points with $75+ spend" both appear as their own hero circles up top, stacked with a "+" between them, which is a legitimately clear visual translation of a compound-offer subject line. That's a step up from the brand's usual bait-and-switch pattern (see 2026-08-29's "Select brands are 50% off" send, which promised a discount and delivered a loyalty crawl instead).
- But past the fold it reverts to type: a dense stack of unrelated modules (free sample bag, points redemption, a "must haves" product grid, a second 50% off banner, free Rhode samples, "Come Hang with Us" events) that dilutes the two headline offers instead of reinforcing them. The email is also long — this screenshot runs the length of roughly 15+ modules, which is a lot of scrolling for a "stacking weekend" concept that could've been told in 4-5 screens.
- Net: better structural honesty than recent Sephora sends, but still an undifferentiated catalog-crawl body. Middle-of-the-pack score, dragged up slightly by the honest hero.

## What's working

- The dual-hero "50% Off" + "500 Bonus Points" circles connected by a "+" icon visually mirror the subject line's compound offer — no bait-and-switch here.
- "STARTS TODAY" banner and "Ends 9/8" / "Ends 9/10" deadlines on both hero offers give credible, specific urgency.
- The loyalty tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) near the footer is a nice touch for a Beauty Insider member persona.
- Free Same-Day Delivery banner at the very top adds a secondary concrete offer without competing with the hero.
- No visible render bugs — images, banners, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long with too many competing CTAs ("Shop Now," "Redeem Your Points," "Get Samples," "Learn More") — no single next action is reinforced twice.
- The "must haves" product grid (Make Up For Ever, Nest, The Inkey List, etc.) has zero connection to the 50%-off or points offers — it's generic catalog filler bolted onto a themed sale.
- A second "50% Off Select Beauty" banner reappears mid-email, redundant with the hero and diluting rather than reinforcing.
- "Come Hang with Us" events module and Rhode skincare launch feel like unrelated inserts competing for attention against the core stacking-weekend hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to hero (dual offer) → free sample bag → points redemption → one supporting product carousel → footer. Drop the "must haves" grid, the duplicate 50%-off banner, and the events module entirely — they're diluting a genuinely clear offer.
- 2. Move "Here's What You Can Do with Your Points" directly under the hero so the points mechanic is explained right where it's promised, not several scrolls later.
- 3. Pick one hero CTA style and repeat it exactly (e.g., "Shop the Sale") instead of rotating "Shop Now," "Redeem Points," "Get Samples," "Learn More" — reduces decision friction.
- 4. Since this is a real stacked-offer occasion, add a countdown or single unified deadline banner ("Sale ends 9/8, Bonus Points end 9/10") near the top instead of splitting the two dates across two separate hero circles.
