# Last call: 20% off VIP sale
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Another plain-text collapse — the fourth one in this streak (09-14, 09-17, 09-20, and now this). No logo, no hero image, no product photography, nothing but black bulleted text and two blue hyperlinks. The subject line "Last call" and "final few hours" copy actually signal real urgency, which is more than 09-20's vague "This only happens once a year" managed, but the execution completely undercuts it — a genuine last-chance sale deserves a hero shot, not a wall of bullet points.
- The offer stack itself is generous and well-organized: 20% off sitewide, free Multi-Stick, free Velvet Makeup Bag at $100+, subscription discounts, up to 40% off select items. But it's presented as a loyalty-signup pitch ("become a VIP member, it's totally free") rather than a straightforward sale email, which adds friction — the reader has to join something before they can even see what's discounted.
- This is a bottom-tier send for the streak, on par with 09-20 and 09-17, well below the production-value peaks of 09-19 (8/10) and 09-21/09-22 (7/10 each).

## What's working

- Subject line urgency ("Last call... ends tonight" territory) is concrete and time-bounded, unlike some of ILIA's vaguer plain-text sends.
- The perks list is genuinely clear and scannable — free Multi-Stick, free Velvet Makeup Bag, 20% off subscriptions, up to 40% select items are all itemized cleanly.
- Loyalty/member value is spelled out explicitly (free shipping, points, early access), which is useful content even if poorly packaged.
- Sign-off feels human and on-brand ("Warmly, Marissa from ILIA") rather than corporate.

## What's weak

- Zero visual production — no logo, no hero image, no product shot, no button, just plain text and two hyperlinks.
- The CTA is a bare blue text link ("Sign up for VIP here to shop the sale") with no button, easy to miss on mobile.
- Framing the sale behind a "become a VIP member" ask adds a signup step between the reader and the discount, which reads like bait-and-switch on a "20% off" subject line.
- No visible price example, product image, or shade/sizing detail — nothing concrete to click toward.
- This is the fourth near-identical plain-text send in under two weeks, so fatigue/spam-pattern-recognition risk is high for a regular subscriber.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Ship this with the actual hero creative** — ILIA clearly has the design capability (see 09-19, 09-21, 09-22); reusing one of those templates with a "Last Call" badge would instantly outperform plain text.
- 2. **Turn the sign-up link into a real button** ("Join VIP & Shop 20% Off") so the CTA is visually unmissable, especially on mobile where this design has no anchor point at all.
- 3. **Lead with the discount, not the membership pitch** — reorder so "20% off sitewide, ends tonight" is the first line seen, with VIP perks as a secondary bullet, not the framing device.
- 4. **Add a countdown or explicit deadline** ("Ends tonight at midnight ET") instead of "final few hours," which is vaguer than it needs to be given the urgency angle.
- 5. **Show one product** — even a single image of the Multi-Stick or Velvet Makeup Bag ggive-with-purchase would provide a visual anchor and product-detail signal this send completely lacks.
