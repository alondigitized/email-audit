# Your next summer flavor...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's sixth send in roughly ten days, pivoting from the tight I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom narrative into a broader summer-flavor catalog play. The top section briefly anchors to the I ❤️ NY moment (merch shot, product editorial), but the email quickly becomes a grid of Balm Dotcom varieties — strawberry, peach, and several others — with no single product getting a hero moment. The "Apply liberally. Lick lips accordingly." module is the strongest thing in the email: specific, playful, on-brand. Everything around it competes for attention without offering a reason to act.
- The subject "Your next summer flavor..." is vague when the brand could have named something. After five themed sends in a row, subscribers have been trained to expect a specific reveal. This email delivers a catalog instead, and it shows. No offer, no urgency, no editorial POV beyond "here are flavors." It's not a bad email — it just arrives at the worst possible moment cadence-wise and wastes the goodwill built by the I ❤️ NY arc.
- Glossier's July 16 merch drop ("New I ❤️ NY merch just arrived!") was the natural send to close this arc. This one reads like an afterthought — a reactivation email that didn't get the memo.

## What's working

- **"Apply liberally. Lick lips accordingly."** — the single best line in the email; sensory, specific, and unmistakably Glossier voice
- **Fruit photography** alongside product tubes is a strong flavor-cue technique; makes the product feel appetizing without showing someone eating lip balm
- **The I ❤️ NY merch callback** in the top-right panel gives continuity to the arc without requiring a hard reset
- **Clean render** — product shots are crisp, layout holds, no visible broken modules

## What's weak

- **No offer** — no discount, no free-shipping threshold, no limited-edition urgency; nothing gives you a reason to buy today rather than later
- **Grid of six-plus products dilutes attention** — when everything is featured, nothing is
- **"Shop now" repeated across every module** — by the fourth instance it reads as wallpaper
- **Subject line wastes a reveal moment** — "Your next summer flavor..." could have named the flavor (e.g., "Meet our juiciest Balm Dotcom yet") and earned the click rather than asking subscribers to guess
- **Sixth email in ~10 days** — at this cadence, even engaged subscribers start filtering; this one offers the least payoff of the arc

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one flavor and own it** — make Strawberry (or whichever has the best story) the hero, shoot it like the I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom launch, write three sentences about why it tastes like summer. The catalog grid can live below the fold.
- 2. **Add a reason to act now** — even "spend $35, get free shipping" or "fan favorites, back in stock" framing beats a pure browse prompt.
- 3. **Retire "Shop now"** — "Get the Strawberry" or "Try it free with $X" ties the CTA to the specific product and the offer.
- 4. **Send cadence reset** — this arc needed one more day of breathing room; the July 16 merch send was the right closer, and this email could have landed July 19 as a soft "summer flavors" re-engagement with stronger momentum.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet the flavor that's already sold out twice this year.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Lick lips accordingly. New summer picks, ready to shop.`
