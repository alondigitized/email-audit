# HAIM x ILIA
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a full-gear creative reset from ILIA — the Soft Focus arc that ran June 2–7 is over, and ILIA is now launching a celebrity partnership with HAIM, the three-sister band. The concept is elegant and tight: three sisters, one product trio, one routine. The "one trio, your whole routine" headline does that work cleanly, and the editorial photography is on-brand. Each HAIM sister gets her own testimonial block, which gives the email real depth as a campaign anchor piece.
- The sweepstakes hook ("Win A Signed Vinyl + $500") at the bottom is a smart engagement driver — it turns a brand story email into something actionable. But the giveaway is buried well below the fold, meaning most opens will read this as a pure editorial send and miss the incentive. That's a structural miss.
- The subject — "HAIM x ILIA" — is a fan's subject line, not a buyer's. Anyone who already knows HAIM is likely to open; anyone who doesn't will see two proper nouns and keep scrolling. For a list this well-targeted, that's probably fine, but it leaves conversions on the table.

## What's working

- **"One trio, your whole routine."** — The concept link between the sisters and the product trio is immediate and memorable. Strong headline.
- **Three-quote structure** — Dedicating a block to each HAIM sister (ESTE, DANIELLE, ALANA) adds editorial weight and gives the email scroll incentive.
- **Sweepstakes hook** — Win a signed vinyl + $500 is a genuinely compelling giveaway and differentiates this from a straight editorial send.
- **Clean render** — No broken images, no layout blowout, no overlapping copy; the email renders crisply at this size.
- **HAIM's sensitive-skin angle** — The opening copy tying HAIM's skin concerns to ILIA products is credible, not just celebrity name-drop.

## What's weak

- **Sweepstakes is below the fold** — The single most actionable element (Win Signed Vinyl + $500) requires scrolling past three testimonial blocks to reach; most recipients won't see it.
- **Subject has zero product/offer signal** — "HAIM x ILIA" tells you nothing about what's inside unless you already know who HAIM is; open rates for the uninitiated will be soft.
- **No product shot or product name in the hero** — The hero section is aspirational lifestyle photography; a specific product or SKU name would ground the routine claim.
- **No CTA button visible in the upper half** — The email goes deep into editorial before any clickable action appears; first-scroll readers have nowhere to go.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the sweepstakes higher** — Move "Win A Signed Vinyl + $500" to just below the hero headline. Let it serve as the CTA for the whole email, not a footnote. This single change would significantly lift clicks.
- 2. **Add a product trio visual** — Show all three products together in one shot, ideally with names. "One trio" is the concept; let the reader see the trio.
- 3. **Insert a CTA button early** — A "Shop the routine →" or "Enter to win →" button after the HAIM hero keeps mobile readers engaged without forcing them to scroll.
- 4. **Rework the subject to hint at the giveaway or the product angle**:
- **Subject Alt A:** `HAIM's go-to trio (+ enter to win)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `HAIM built their whole routine from this`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three sisters. One routine. Win a signed vinyl + $500.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ESTE, DANIELLE, and ALANA on why they actually use ILIA.`
