# Say hello to our new holiday merch!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand merch catalog with no offer. Five product blocks alternate image left and right. The photography is strong: a metallic pink bag with ribbons, hand-drawn playing cards, a balm keychain hanging off a heel, and a pink bat with a bow. The tone is playful ("Skip the meltdown and cross everyone off your list"), and the layout holds together.
- It works as a collectible showcase and fails as a conversion email. Nothing here gives a reason to act today: no price, no deadline, no gift-with-purchase, no shipping cutoff. The first four CTAs are an identical "Shop now" with no product name. Five equal-weight blocks leave the eye with no single place to land.
- It also repeats material. The Balm Dotcom Keychain was already the hero of the 10/30 send ("There's a new Balm Keychain in town"), so subscribers see it here as a rerun. Judgement: a pleasant, brand-consistent 5/10. It would do better with one hero product, a price, and a reason to buy now.

## What's working

- The photography is bright, tactile and on-brand. The beauty bag shot with ribbons is the strongest image.
- The alternating left/right layout gives a steady scanning rhythm.
- The copy has personality: "Skip the meltdown," "Deal With It," and "For decorative purposes only (mostly 😉)."
- The "NEW!" labels flag newness, and "RETAIL EXCLUSIVE" on the bat creates real scarcity.
- Each block links to a specific product. The footer is tidy and carries the brand's hand-drawn smiley.

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer or deadline anywhere. The email gives no reason to buy now.
- Four of the five buttons say the same generic "Shop now." Only the bat block's "Find your store" differs.
- The pink bag, the cards and the keychain have equal weight, so there is no clear hero. The top banner's "merry little merch" headline is the only unifying idea.
- The Balm Keychain already got its own send on 10/30, so it reads as stale here.
- The bat is available only in SoHo, LA and London, and it is the final block. That is a dead end for most recipients.
- The pale grey button text on the light background reads weak against the loud imagery.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero, the metallic pink beauty bag, and give it a price or a gift-with-purchase hook. Make the other items a smaller secondary row.
- 2. Add a concrete reason to act: a holiday shipping cutoff, a limited-stock note, or a gift-with-purchase tie-in like the recent mug promos.
- 3. Replace "Shop now" with specific CTAs, such as "Get the Beauty Bag," "Grab the Cards" and "Clip on the Keychain."
- 4. Drop the repeated keychain, or reframe it as a stocking stuffer bundle.
- 5. Move the retail-exclusive bat higher or turn it into a "visit us" teaser. Don't let it close the email.
- 6. Add a price anchor under each product, such as "$18."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-tracked, Stripo-built (stripocdn.email) XHTML template with sound base markup: `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, and MSO conditionals. The main confirmed issues are missing `alt` attributes on 6 images and incomplete link verification, since most links were rate-limited or never probed. The header warnings are limitations of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links.** Every link is wrapped in a Klaviyo click-tracking redirect (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M6SH30QN7YP4F3FYC0H98BWP_N`). The final destinations are not visible in the source, so they could not be audited.
- **Rate-limited, not broken.** Four "Shop now" links returned 429 (`..._3`, `_5`, `_7`, `_9`). A 429 comes from the tracker throttling the probe, so it neither confirms nor rules out a broken link.
- **Unprobed links.** QA reports 8 links unprobed after the 70s time budget and lists `_11`, `_1`, `_2`, `_4` and `_6`. The report lists only 5 of the 8 URLs, and "12 probed, 5 skipped" does not match the 8-unprobed count, so QA coverage is unreliable for this send.
- **Stray `href` on a `<span>`.** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/...BWP_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. It is an inert Klaviyo artifact, but it is invalid markup and creates an extra tracked URL (`_0`).
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M6SH30QN7YP4F3FYC0H98BWP` is present. It is expected, but it is one of the images with no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on 6 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `heroimage_1_2_5Wz.jpg`
  - `group_3996_9WM.jpg`
  - three `95339270_2980034135406876_2064437603473555456_n_1*` images
  - the tracking pixel (`/o/01M6SH30QN7YP4F3FYC0H98BWP`)

  All of them are served from `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_13942e29.../`. For the content images, the missing `alt` means the holiday-merch message is lost when images are blocked. The tracking pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- **Repeated generic link text.** At least four links read "Shop now" (`_3`, `_5`, `_7`, `_9`). Screen-reader users get no context, so add `aria-label` or product-specific text.
- **Dead Gmail selector.** The CSS uses `u + .body img ~ div div { display:none }`, but the `<body>` tag has no `class="body"`. That rule never matches, so the Gmail-specific fix does nothing.
- **Non-MSO styles.** The `<style>` blocks are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->`, which closes after `<body>` opens (`<!--<![endif]-->`). Outlook desktop (MSO) therefore loses the responsive and `.o`/hide rules. This is a typical Stripo pattern, but it is why `mso-hide:all` is needed on the preheader.
- **Hover effects.** The `.rollover` and `.bd:hover` effects depend on `:hover` and have no touch equivalent. This is progressive enhancement only, not a defect.
- **Good practice.** The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none; mso-hide:all`), `dir="ltr"` and `lang="en"` are set, and `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are in place.
