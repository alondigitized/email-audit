# 20% off ends tomorrow
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's final-hours push on the Friends + Family campaign — by any count the 15th or 16th email in roughly seven days. Where May 26's "Swipe on 20% off" had to dress up the same offer in lifestyle metaphor, this subject line just tells the truth: it ends tomorrow. That's the right call at this stage of the campaign.
- The email attempts a three-layer structure — deadline banner + "final hours" prose block → "Cart Collage" gift-trio → "Up to 40% off Sale" secondary pitch — which is conceptually sound: urgency first, then incentive to build a cart, then a discovery payoff. The execution stumbles on two competing CTAs and a hero that says "SALE SALE SALE" three times, which is pure filler for a subscriber who has seen this brand every single day this week.
- The strongest new information in this send is buried at the bottom: "Up to 40% off Sale" is the genuine discovery beat — sale items stacked inside a sitewide discount is actual news. That should be the hero, not a footnote after the Cart Collage.

## What's working

- "ENDS SOON" badge in the top corner creates urgency without exclamation-point spam
- VIP tier is surfaced twice (25% off for VIPs in the top banner + 2X VIP points in the Cart Collage) — rewards members and creates visible upgrade incentive in one send
- Free mini with any order is a clean zero-friction bonus; no visible minimum spend asterisk
- "Up to 40% off Sale" is genuine new information — sale-on-sale during Friends + Family is the strongest offer revelation of the entire run
- Subject line is the clearest deadline anchor the campaign has shipped

## What's weak

- "SALE SALE SALE" hero text is filler — at send 15, repetition is not excitement
- Two competing CTAs ("SHOP THE SALE →" and "SHOP NOW →") split attention with no hierarchy between them
- "A Cart Collage" headline is opaque — "trio of gifts" doesn't communicate that this is a gift-with-purchase module; subscribers shouldn't have to decode the section purpose
- Product images in the Cart Collage are small and pricing text appears truncated — the creatives don't sell the products
- Campaign fatigue is real: any subscriber who hasn't converted across 15 sends is definitively tuned out, and this send can't fix that

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the CTA anchor to the free mini.** "SHOP THE SALE" is the 15th iteration of the same button. "CLAIM YOUR FREE MINI →" is new. Lead with the bonus, not the discount everyone has already seen.
- 2. **Elevate "Up to 40% off Sale" to section two**, above the Cart Collage — it's the strongest new news in this email and it's buried beneath a section nobody asked for.
- 3. **Kill "A Cart Collage" as a headline.** Replace with "3 free gifts with your order" — functional beats aesthetic at this point in the campaign.
- 4. **Replace the "SALE SALE SALE" hero text** with "Last 24 hours. Free mini with every order." — turns a decoration into a direct value statement.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free mini with your order — 20% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: up to 40% off + a free mini`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `VIPs get 25% off + 2X points — no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab a free mini with any order before midnight`
