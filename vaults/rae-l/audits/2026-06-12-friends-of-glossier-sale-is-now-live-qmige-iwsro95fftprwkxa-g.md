# Friends of Glossier Sale is now LIVE!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's day-two push for the Friends of Glossier Sale — yesterday's "Our Friends of Glossier Sale is here..." was the announcement, and this "now LIVE!" send is meant to activate subscribers who didn't open day one. The concept is sound; the execution splits the difference between a targeted reminder and a product catalog dump, landing on an unfocused nine-product grid with zero editorial curation.
- The subscription upsell at the bottom ("Start a subscription and lock in 25% off") is the most strategically interesting element in the email — it reframes a one-time sale as a permanent loyalty benefit. That's a meaningful offer that would justify a standalone email. Here it's buried below the fold where almost no one sees it.
- Competent and clean, but this send does nothing that yesterday's didn't, and "now LIVE!" on day two is a credibility leak for anyone who opened June 11's announcement.

## What's working

- "Don't miss up to 25% off!" hero copy is direct and benefit-led — the discount is the first thing the eye lands on
- The circular "ANNUAL SALE" badge gives the creative a sense of occasion that plain sale copy can't
- Balm Dotcom row uses bright fruit imagery effectively — visually the most alive section of the email
- Subscription lock-in pitch is a genuinely differentiated value prop absent from yesterday's send
- No render bugs; layout holds, brand voice is consistent

## What's weak

- "Now LIVE!" in the subject is misleading — the sale launched yesterday; loyal openers know this and the urgency signal deflates immediately
- Nine products across three rows with no editorial hierarchy means nothing stands out; the grid reads as a catalog, not a curated send
- "Shop now" repeated on every single button creates monotony with no variation to signal what clicking each one will do differently
- The subscription upsell — the only new thing in this email — is buried at the bottom where scroll depth almost certainly kills its reach
- No visible end date anywhere; "now LIVE!" implies urgency but doesn't anchor it with a deadline

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the subscription pitch** as a co-hero or secondary banner immediately below the main offer — "Subscribe today and keep the 25% off forever" earns a second read; hidden at the bottom it earns nothing.
- 2. **Add a sale end date** — "Ends Sunday at midnight" or "3 days left" converts abstract "LIVE!" urgency into real countdown pressure. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 3. **Cut the grid to 4 curated picks** with a "Shop all sale items →" link. Let the email do editorial work, not catalog duty. Frame them: "Our picks from the sale."
- 4. **Vary CTA copy** — at minimum, the subscription section should read "Subscribe & save" to distinguish it from nine "Shop now" buttons above it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 25% off — Friends only, 3 days left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Friends of Glossier Sale picks (ends Sunday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Subscribe today and lock in 25% off permanently — or shop the sale before it ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Balm Dotcom, You Perfume, Lash Slick — all discounted right now.`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is Glossier's day-two push for the Friends of Glossier Sale — yesterday's "Our Friends of Glossier Sale is here..." was the announcement, and this "now LIVE!" send is meant to activate subscribers who didn't open day one. The concept is sound; the execution splits the difference between a targeted reminder and a product catalog dump, landing on an unfocused nine-product grid with zero editorial curation.

The subscription upsell at the bottom ("Start a subscription and lock in 25% off") is the most strategically interesting element in the email — it reframes a one-time sale as a permanent loyalty benefit. That's a meaningful offer that would justify a standalone email. Here it's buried below the fold where almost no one sees it.

Competent and clean, but this send does nothing that yesterday's didn't, and "now LIVE!" on day two is a credibility leak for anyone who opened June 11's announcement.

## 2. What worked

- "Don't miss up to 25% off!" hero copy is direct and benefit-led — the discount is the first thing the eye lands on
- The circular "ANNUAL SALE" badge gives the creative a sense of occasion that plain sale copy can't
- Balm Dotcom row uses bright fruit imagery effectively — visually the most alive section of the email
- Subscription lock-in pitch is a genuinely differentiated value prop absent from yesterday's send
- No render bugs; layout holds, brand voice is consistent

## 3. What didn't

- "Now LIVE!" in the subject is misleading — the sale launched yesterday; loyal openers know this and the urgency signal deflates immediately
- Nine products across three rows with no editorial hierarchy means nothing stands out; the grid reads as a catalog, not a curated send
- "Shop now" repeated on every single button creates monotony with no variation to signal what clicking each one will do differently
- The subscription upsell — the only new thing in this email — is buried at the bottom where scroll depth almost certainly kills its reach
- No visible end date anywhere; "now LIVE!" implies urgency but doesn't anchor it with a deadline

## 4. What I'd change

1. **Surface the subscription pitch** as a co-hero or secondary banner immediately below the main offer — "Subscribe today and keep the 25% off forever" earns a second read; hidden at the bottom it earns nothing.

2. **Add a sale end date** — "Ends Sunday at midnight" or "3 days left" converts abstract "LIVE!" urgency into real countdown pressure. This is the single highest-lift change.

3. **Cut the grid to 4 curated picks** with a "Shop all sale items →" link. Let the email do editorial work, not catalog duty. Frame them: "Our picks from the sale."

4. **Vary CTA copy** — at minimum, the subscription section should read "Subscribe & save" to distinguish it from nine "Shop now" buttons above it.

   - **Subject Alt B:** `Your Friends of Glossier Sale picks (ends Sunday)`
   - **Preheader Alt A:** `Subscribe today and lock in 25% off permanently — or shop the sale before it ends.`
