# Gift ideas loading…
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, 12-product holiday gift guide. The hero is "gift ideas loading…" over a basket of mini sets, with a top banner reading "GET SHADE MATCHED + WIN $2,000 OF ILIA*". The tone is warm and the three serif section heads (Now In Season, Customize It, Just For You) give it a clear rhythm. The problem is that it shows two offers and commits to neither. The sweepstakes sits in the top banner, and the real offer, a free mini Eye Stylus Shadow Stick with a $75+ order, doesn't appear until the last third.
- Compared with the 10/30 "Just a few recs" send, which put its free-mini-mascara offer in three places, this one hides the deal. A subscriber scrolling the grid sees a catalog of twelve near-identical "shop now" tiles. Nothing tells them which set is the best gift, and nothing shows prices or discounts.
- Verdict: pretty and on-brand, but it's a catalog with the offer buried. It's roughly a 5 to 6.

## What's working

- The hero is on-theme, with a seasonal basket of mini sets and a playful "gift ideas loading…" headline.
- The three section heads (Now In Season, Customize It, Just For You) split the grid into scannable groups.
- Consistent product photography and uniform two-column tiles make the layout clean, with no visible broken images.
- The free-mini offer block has a strong close-up eye image with a "FREE MINI" badge.
- The footer carries trust signals: 1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact recycling, and VIP and shade-match links.

## What's weak

- The free-mini offer with a $75+ order sits near the bottom, below twelve products, so most readers never reach it.
- The top banner promotes a sweepstakes ("Win $2,000 of ILIA*") that competes with the gift-set message.
- All twelve tiles use the identical low-emphasis "shop now →" outline button, so none is a clear hero.
- No prices, ratings or "best seller" tags appear on the tiles, so there's no reason to prefer one set over another.
- The fine print under the offer ("*See site for details") is tiny and faint, and the banner asterisk also has no visible explanation near the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the free-mini offer into the hero and top banner, for example "Free mini eye stylus with $75+". Repeat it once mid-grid.
- 2. Cut the grid from twelve tiles to six to eight, and tag one tile as the "best gift".
- 3. Add prices or "from $X" and a short benefit line under each set name.
- 4. Make the hero CTA specific: "Shop gift sets" instead of "Shop gifts".
- 5. Drop the sweepstakes from the banner, or give it its own send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `12 ILIA gifts, one free mini`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited-edition sets, plus a free Eye Stylus mini on $75+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with wrapped click-tracking links (`trk.send.iliabeauty.com`). The QA run found no hard failures. The confirmed issues are missing alt text and a missing `lang` attribute. Header, link and UTM checks are inconclusive because of relay capture, probe rate-limiting and truncated source.

The "57% pass rate" mostly reflects skipped and unprobed checks, not defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Rate-limited probes (not confirmed broken):** four CTAs returned 429 from the tracking domain, so the link behind each is unverified.
  - `.../l/01M70GMM5WXMNXGXACFAVCP24A_5` ("The Limitless Lash Duo shop now →")
  - `..._6` (Mini Overglaze Lip Gloss Trio)
  - `..._7` (Mini Eye Stylus Trio)
  - `..._8` (Mascara Duo)

- **No redirect chain data:** the tracking links were not resolved, so the final destinations are unknown.
- **No confirmed broken links.**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 2 images):**
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. This is a content image, so the omission leaves screen readers with nothing, or the filename.
  - The tracking pixel `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M70GMM5WXMNXGXACFAVCP24A`. It should carry `alt=""`; this one is low severity.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>` with no `lang` or `dir`. Screen readers cannot pick the correct pronunciation.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` holds only whitespace. This is minor.
- **Web font imports:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap)` are ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The stack `"Montserrat","Helvetica",sans-serif` falls back acceptably, though headings are declared as plain `Arial`.
- **Preheader:** the hidden block is correctly padded with `͏` characters. No issue.

No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{%`, or `*|`. Coverage is limited to the visible portion of the HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so the headers should be checked on the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay, so this is not a confirmed failure.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result is not in the QA output. Neither the link nor the postal address appears in the truncated source. Both are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be assessed.** Every CTA is an opaque tracking redirect, and the 429 responses and probe time budget stopped the redirect chains from resolving.
- **CTA-to-landing-page match cannot be confirmed.** The product-specific CTAs (Lash Duo, Lip Gloss Trio, Eye Stylus Trio, Mascara Duo) were not followed to their landing pages.
