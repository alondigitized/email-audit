# Don't leave a good thing behind.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a browse-abandonment email with Black Friday promo content attached. The hero is "SELLING FAST… ADD TO CART BEFORE THEY'RE GONE!" over a single black quilted Slip-ins Arch Fit Arcade sneaker. A six-shoe grid of recommendations follows. The real offer sits below that grid: a navy "BLACK FRIDAY COUNTDOWN: BUY ONE, GET ONE 50% OFF, OR 20% OFF A SINGLE PAIR" banner.
- The render is clean and the offer is concrete, but the email buries its best asset. The BOGO banner is the only thing that gives a reason to act today, and it sits about 40% of the way down. The top is generic scarcity copy. This is also roughly the ninth abandonment-style send in two weeks, following 11/7 "This style has limited availability!", 11/7 "Don't leave these styles behind!" and 11/6 "This is perfect for you!". Urgency language has been recycled so often that "selling fast" no longer sounds credible. The subject is another "don't leave…behind" variant, which a subscriber will read as the same email again.
- Verdict: competent template, weak sequencing, and fatigued messaging. Moving the BOGO up top would help most.

## What's working

- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the navy and white brand look is consistent.
- The single hero product has a clear "SHOP NOW" button, so the primary CTA is unambiguous.
- The BOGO 50% / 20% single-pair banner is a concrete offer, and the "auto-applied at cart" note lowers friction.
- The six-product grid adds relevant alternatives, including Britto collab and Slip-ins styles.
- The Black Friday framing is seasonal and current, and the 11/12–11/17 window is stated.

## What's weak

- The offer is below the fold on mobile. The first screen shows only "Selling fast…" and a shoe.
- "Selling fast" gives no stock evidence, and after weeks of "limited availability" sends it reads as boilerplate.
- The CTA copy "SHOP NOW" is generic, and the hero shoe has no price, sale price or size and fit detail.
- The email stacks a lot below the offer: six category bars, texting, app, curbside, BNPL and social blocks. This dilutes focus after the banner.
- The fine print buries the exclusions: select regular-priced shoes only, online only, and sale styles, accessories and test styles excluded. The "20% off a single pair" half is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the BOGO banner to the top, above or directly under the logo, and make it the headline. Put the hero shoe beneath it with the offer applied.
- 2. Give the hero shoe a named benefit and a price, such as "Arch Fit Arcade Slip-ins: now $X with BOGO", and make the button "Get BOGO on Arcade".
- 3. State the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends 11/17", instead of only in the fine print.
- 4. Replace "selling fast" with something real, such as "Low stock in your size", or drop it. If the data doesn't exist, don't claim scarcity.
- 5. Cut the lower modules (the five-plus category bars, app, curbside and BNPL blocks) down to one row.
- 6. Break the abandonment cadence. After a week of near-identical sends, suppress or merge them into a single reminder plus the BOGO message.
