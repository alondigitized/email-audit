# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email, and it is competent but generic. The hero is clean: a big "LET'S MAKE IT OFFICIAL" headline, "YOUR CART IS READY WHEN YOU ARE," one pale pink GO WALK Workout Walker, and a navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button. The eye lands on the cart item, and the button is obvious. The subject, "Your cart is one click away!", matches the body, which is better than the 10/26 "Your cart expires soon!" send. That one had a browse-abandonment body under a cart subject.
- The email then loses focus. A six-shoe grid, a black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar, a 20% off Plus members banner, six category rows, a texting promo, app download, curbside, BNPL and social links all follow. The only real incentive, 20% off for Skechers Plus members, sits below the fold, behind a Halloween "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" frame, with the exclusions in small print. A shopper deciding on one shoe never sees it near the cart CTA.
- For a subscriber, the cadence is the problem. This is the fourth or fifth cart or browse reminder in about a week: 10/22 twice, 10/23, 10/26, and now 10/28. The shell is the same each time. It is a solid template, but there is no new reason to buy today.

## What's working

- The subject and body agree. "Your cart is one click away!" is followed by a hero about the cart and a "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button.
- The hero is uncluttered. One large product image, one headline and one high-contrast navy button give a clear path.
- The product is named under the image ("Skechers Slip-ins: GO WALK Workout Walker"), so the reader knows what is in the cart.
- The 20% off Skechers Plus banner is bold, with a clear "Valid thru 11/2" deadline.
- The "More great options" grid gives named alternatives in different styles, so a lapsed shopper has something else to click.

## What's weak

- The 20% off Plus offer is buried in the middle of the email, well below the cart CTA. The abandoner is never told the discount applies to the item in the cart.
- There is no price, size, color or review on the hero item. The product line is only a name.
- The footer is bloated: texting, app, curbside, Afterpay/Klarna, store finder and social links all follow the offer. It reads as a template, not a nudge.
- The Halloween "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" copy sits oddly against a cart reminder, and the 20% condition (Plus members only, logged in, exclusions) is in tiny text.
- The "Shop all best sellers" bar and the category list compete with the cart CTA and dilute the single action.
- Cadence is heavy, and this shell has repeated across recent sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus offer directly under the hero: "Take 20% off this pair with Skechers Plus. Ends 11/2." Put it next to the CTA, not under the grid.
- 2. Show the price, available sizes and a star rating on the hero item, and add a CTA that names the product, such as "Get my GO WALK Workout Walker."
- 3. Cut the six-shoe grid to three, or replace it with "Others in your cart category." Remove the category rows, or collapse them to one line.
- 4. Collapse the footer to the app and one more link. Drop curbside and BNPL, or keep BNPL only if it can be tied to the cart total.
- 5. Suppress or space out repeated cart and browse reminders. If the cart has not converted after the first send, change the creative or the incentive on the next one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your GO WALK is still in your cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off your cart ends 11/2`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% on your Slip-ins pair through 11/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your size may sell out. Finish checkout in one tap.`
