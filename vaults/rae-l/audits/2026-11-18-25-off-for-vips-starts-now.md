# 25% off for VIPs starts now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, clear Black Friday VIP early-access send. The hero does the job: a giant red "25% off" under "black friday vip early access," a full-width red "JOIN FREE + SHAPE SHOP EARLY →" button, and a top banner that repeats the offer. The eye lands on the discount first. The 25% is partly cropped by the product image, but it still reads.
- The body is where it loses focus. The copy stacks sitewide 25% off, 2x VIP points, a free complexion stick with any order, and a free mini with $100+. That is four incentives in a few lines. The product grid then lists Super Serum Skin Tint twice, which looks like a copy-paste error. The "very important perks" block and the brand footer repeat points already made.
- Compared with the Nov 16 teaser ("48 hours from now…"), this one delivers on the promise. It also arrives in a week of heavy ILIA sending: the Nov 14 sweepstakes reminder, the Nov 13 survey, and the Nov 12 SPF send. The offer is concrete and the creative is polished. The duplicate product and the stacked incentives hold it back.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable. "25% off" is huge, red, and above the fold, with a matching red CTA bar.
- The top banner ("UNLOCK 25% OFF + EARLY SALE ACCESS | JOIN FREE") states the offer and the free-to-join barrier up front.
- The two polaroid-style gift callouts ("free complexion stick," "free mini of choice w/ $100+") are easy to scan and visually distinct.
- The VIP logo and the plain intro line ("Let's get straight to the point") give a clean, confident brand voice.
- The Allure and Pact badges on the products add some social proof.

## What's weak

- Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 appears twice in the four-tile grid, so one slot is wasted.
- Four incentives (25% off, 2x points, two free gifts) compete with each other, and the asterisk fine print is only a tiny link at the very bottom.
- The product grid is only four items, with a generic "first things first" header and a small, low-contrast "shop now" button on each. They are far weaker than the hero CTA.
- The "very important perks" block is low-contrast, with small mauve text on maroon, and it repeats the VIP pitch.
- The large brand block ("Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better") adds length and no new reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the duplicate tile and swap in a different bestseller. Better still, pick products with prices or a "now $X" so the 25% becomes concrete.
- 2. Cut to one headline incentive. Lead with 25% off plus the free complexion stick, and move 2x points and the $100+ mini into a single line below.
- 3. Make the product CTAs filled buttons, and name the product ("Shop the Mascara").
- 4. Add a deadline, such as the time early access ends or when the public sale starts, to create credible urgency.
- 5. Surface the fine print near the offer, not just in the footer.
- 6. Drop or shrink the brand-statement block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `VIP early access: 25% off + a free complexion stick`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday early access is open`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join ILIA VIP free to shop 25% off sitewide before everyone else.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free complexion stick with any order, plus 2x points, today only.`

## Technical Audit

**Technical audit: ILIA, "25% off for VIPs starts now"**

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) email. The QA run reported 0 errors and 3 warnings. The two real markup gaps are the missing alt text on two images and the empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links. QA probed 12 material links and reported no failures.
- 17 links were skipped as footer, utility or social links. They are unverified, not confirmed good.
- The tracking pixel is `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M7QNNZGTFJHPNPES92XRC6EC`. It is served from a branded tracking subdomain, which is correct practice.
- I can't assess the CTA hrefs or the unsubscribe link, because the source was truncated before the body.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. If this image carries the offer, it is invisible with images off and to screen readers.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M7...`. It should have `alt=""`, but this is low impact.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` is blank.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has none. Screen readers fall back to the default language.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** `custom_fonts.css` (Klaviyo) and Google Fonts Montserrat are loaded with `@import`. Gmail and several other clients ignore these and fall back to `"Helvetica", sans-serif`. The stack has a fallback, so this is minor.
- **Preheader:** the hidden preheader div uses `display:none;max-height:0;opacity:0` plus `͏` padding characters, which is the standard approach. No issue found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}` or `*|FNAME|*`. The body was truncated, so this only covers the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing it, so it is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** This is the same capture caveat. One-click support can't be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The tracking domain `send.iliabeauty.com` suggests a branded sending setup, but that is not proof.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address were not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in link probing, but the result wasn't reported.

None of these three are confirmed defects. Verify them against the raw message headers from a direct inbox, such as Gmail "Show original".

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The CTA hrefs, UTM parameters and landing URLs are outside the truncated source. The QA output reports no probe failures or redirects, so there is no evidence of a continuity problem.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c9cefca7-...jpeg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Populate `<title>` with the subject or brand name.
3. Add `lang="en"` to the `<html>` tag.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on a direct send (not via the relay). Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
5. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the raw headers.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, so CTA UTMs, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and merge tokens can be checked.
