# One, two, free
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA switching lanes entirely — after five straight sends threading the Thru Line eyeliner launch and its bundles, "One, two, free" is a pure new-customer acquisition play: spend $50, get a $65 GWP trio. The hero is clean and the offer math is genuinely good (a full-size Limitless Lash Mascara plus two minis for free), but the fine print — "New customers only" — undercuts the whole send for anyone who's already on this list, which after five recent purchases-driven emails is a lot of the audience.
- The creative itself is strong: single close-up face shot, bold "One, Two, Free" wordmark overlay, clear $65 value badge, and a numbered gift breakdown that does the explaining work a GWP email needs to do. It's one of the cleaner acquisition templates I've seen from this brand.
- Verdict: well-built as a new-customer acquisition email, but poorly targeted if blasted to the full list — a returning subscriber opens this, gets excited about a free mascara, and then reads "new customers only" in 9pt gray text after already clicking. That's the one thing that would knock this from a strong send to a frustrating one.

## What's working

- The $65 VALUE badge on the hero image gives an instant, concrete number to anchor the offer.
- Numbered gift callouts (1, 2, 3) with product name, shade, and benefit make the GWP tangible instead of vague.
- Two clear CTAs ("Build My First Order" and "Claim Your Free Trio") both point at the same action, reinforcing the path to purchase.
- Product swatch photography (lip crayon streak, multi-stick smear, mascara wand with lash swipe) shows texture and payoff, not just packaging.
- Clean, uncluttered layout with generous white space keeps the eye moving down the page in order.

## What's weak

- "New customers only" is buried in small gray fine print under the first CTA — for a subscriber list that's been engaging with product launches, that's a real bait-and-switch risk.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the body — "while supplies last" is the only constraint, which is vague, not time-bound.
- No social proof (reviews, ratings, UGC) anywhere in the email, unlike the loyalty-forward beats ILIA sometimes hits.
- The banner headline ("FREE GIFT SET W/ YOUR FIRST $50+ ORDER") already discloses "first order" up top, but it's easy to skim past in pink-on-pink low-contrast styling — the message isn't reinforced near either CTA button itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send — exclude existing customers entirely, or if sent to the full list, swap the CTA copy for existing subscribers to something honest like "Refer a friend and they get this gift" rather than showing an offer they can't redeem.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 8/16" or similar) near the top CTA — "while supplies last" doesn't create urgency, just supply anxiety.
- 3. Move the "new customers only" qualifier out of the fine print and into the hero badge or headline so it's not a post-click surprise.
- 4. Add one line of social proof under the gift breakdown (e.g., "4.8★ — 12,000+ reviews on Limitless Lash Mascara") to convert on trust, not just value.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Free mascara + 2 minis with your first order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New here? Unlock a 3-piece bestseller trio on any $50+ order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A full-size mascara, free — for new ILIA customers only.`
