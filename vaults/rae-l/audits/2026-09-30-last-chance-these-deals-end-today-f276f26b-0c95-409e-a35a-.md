# Last chance: These deals end today ⏰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a deadline email with a weak lead. The subject promises "deals end today," and the body delivers four offers: BOGO 50% off hair care, 30% off clippers, BOGO 50% off jewelry, and 25% off sitewide with code FALL25. The hero, though, is a big lifestyle photo under the headline "Don't miss these fall deals." It names no number, so a reader has to scroll past about 1,000px of image to reach any offer. The strongest deal, 25% off sitewide, sits near the bottom.
- It is also the third urgency send in about ten days, after "🎉 Appiversary deals end soon!" on 2026-09-28 and "Last chance to buy more & save more 🔥" on 2026-09-21. The BOGO hair care offer is a repeat of the 2026-09-24 "What your hair needs this fall 🍂" send. A subscriber has seen "last chance" before, so the urgency is credible (Sept 30) but tired. The render is clean, with no broken images or overlaps. Judgment: a competent month-end clearance email that leads with the wrong thing.

## What's working

- The "Ends September 30" line under each offer tile makes the deadline consistent and credible.
- The 25% off sitewide block with code FALL25 is a clear, single-number offer with a visible "Shop now" button.
- The offer tiles alternate image left and right, which keeps the scroll easy to follow.
- The hero has a real CTA button ("Shop now") above the fold.
- The Klarna, free shipping, and 2-hour delivery strip reduces purchase friction.

## What's weak

- The hero headline and subhead ("Restock your everyday must-haves with special savings") name no offer, and the biggest discount is buried in the lower third.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop now" or an underlined "Shop now" link, with no verb-plus-noun specificity.
- The 25% sitewide offer and the BOGO and 30% tiles overlap, yet the fine print says "Not combinable." That leaves the stacking rules unclear.
- Above the hero there are three stacked banners: Beauty in a Dash, the $35 2-hour delivery line, and the ENDING SOON ticker. They compete with the main message.
- The hero photo is a model with no product tie-in, which wastes the most valuable screen space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. For example: "25% off sitewide. Ends tonight. Code FALL25." Move the code block to the top and make the hero image smaller.
- 2. Give the tile CTAs specific copy: "Shop BOGO hair care," "Shop clippers," "Shop jewelry."
- 3. Say plainly which offers stack. "25% off doesn't apply to BOGO items" at the top is better than small print at the bottom.
- 4. Cut the top banners to one. The ticker and the delivery line repeat the urgency message.
- 5. Vary the cadence. Don't send a third "last chance" in ten days without a new hook, such as the final hours or a countdown.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: BOGO hair care + 25% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code FALL25 before midnight. BOGO 50% off hair care and jewelry ends too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clippers 30% off, BOGO jewelry, and sitewide savings all expire Sept 30.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional/VML template with mobile media queries. The confirmed defects are in the third-party tracking pixels: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a non-HTTPS pixel, and several pixels with no alt text. The header-level compliance checks could not be completed because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad platform never substituted it, so the cache-buster is not working. Clients or proxies that cache the pixel may under-count impressions, and the unencoded brackets can make the URL invalid.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id...` loads over HTTP. It can be blocked as mixed content or by image proxies, and it exposes the request in transit.
- **Third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2320396/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `myvisualiq.net` and `dotomi.com` are three separate ad-tech trackers. The `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` path segment looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). If so, it is a subscriber identifier passed to a third party in a URL.
- **Link validity not verified:** 51 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. I can't confirm they resolve, and they are neither passes nor failures.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 images):**
  - Two `<img>` tags have an empty name and empty `src` in the QA output. These may be spacer or placeholder images, or an extraction artifact.
  - The tracking pixels `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` also have no alt text. Pixels should carry `alt=""` (with `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them. As written, some clients read out the filename or URL.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` refers to a font that has no `@font-face` in the visible source. Only Open Sans is declared, so links fall back to Helvetica. This is minor, and the source is truncated, so I can't rule out a declaration further down.
- **Markup:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, although the doctype is XHTML 1.0. This is harmless in practice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed:** the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq URL (see section 2). It is an ad-server macro that was not resolved.
- **Not confirmed:** no other unresolved tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the visible portion. The source is truncated, so I can't check the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain is `em.sallybeauty.com`. This is a relay limitation and not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 51 click-tracking links were skipped, and the truncated HTML doesn't show the destination URLs or UTM parameters. No landing-page mismatch is confirmed.

1. Fix the ad-server integration so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is substituted at send time. Otherwise remove that pixel.
2. Move the dotomi pixel to `https://`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support HTTPS.
3. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to all tracking pixels. Give the two blank-`src` images valid sources or remove them.
