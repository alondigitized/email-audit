# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email again, now with a Halloween twist. "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." over "YOUR CART IS STILL WAITING..." is a playful hero. The cart item is the black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" sneaker with a blue sugar-skull graphic, which fits the Día de los Muertos and Halloween season. The navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button sits right under it.
- The offer is the weak spot. "20% OFF for Skechers Plus members" is in a dark banner about 60% of the way down, after a six-shoe recommendation grid and a black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar. A shopper who is hesitating over the cart would need to scroll to find the one reason to buy now. The "Valid thru 11/2" deadline is in tiny grey type. The fine print also says the discount only applies online to a logged-in Plus account. That is a lot of friction for a headline offer.
- The recommendations are all women's slip-ins and sneakers, so the email is segmented. The cart item is a unisex-looking novelty shoe, and the grid doesn't match it. The email is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. Overall it's a competent send with the offer in the wrong place. Compared with 10/30's "Did you forget something?", the creative is fresher, but it is still the same cart reminder on a rotating cadence.

## What's working

- The hero copy is distinctive. "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." has more voice than the generic "READY TO CHECK OUT?" used in the 10/29 and 10/30 sends.
- The cart item is large and well isolated. The Alma De Guerrero skull sneaker is timely for the season and gives the recipient a clear reminder of what they left.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" is a clear navy button directly under the product.
- The "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!" banner ties the 20% offer to Halloween and has high contrast.
- The footer has everything a shopper might want: app, curbside, Afterpay/Klarna, text signup and a store finder.

## What's weak

- The 20% Plus-member offer is buried below the recommendation grid and the Best Sellers bar.
- The deadline "Valid thru 11/2" is tiny, and the exclusions are only in the fine print. The offer needs a login, and it excludes sale styles and prior purchases.
- The recommended shoes are all women's and don't relate to the skull sneaker in the cart. The product captions are small and hard to read.
- Two CTAs compete below the hero: "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" is a heavy black bar and outweighs the cart button.
- The long text-only category list (Women through Sale) and the footer blocks add length without helping the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus offer into the hero, right under "YOUR CART IS STILL WAITING...". Add "Ends 11/2" and "Members: log in to save" next to it.
- 2. Add a short line next to the cart item, such as "Still in your cart: UNO – Alma De Guerrero," with the size, price and a "Low stock" cue if that is true.
- 3. Cut the recommendation grid to three items that match the cart item's category, and enlarge the captions. Delete the black Best Sellers bar.
- 4. Collapse the category list into a single row of links.
- 5. Change the primary CTA to "Claim 20% Off My Cart" for members.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your skull sneakers are still in your cart 💀`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off your cart ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members: take 20% off the UNO Alma De Guerrero before 11/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No tricks, just treats: your cart is saved, and so is 20%.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on an Attentive/Stensul-style table template (600px, `role="presentation"`, MSO conditionals, hidden preheader). The automated QA run found a failing unsubscribe link probe, no plain-text part, and 12 images served over `http://`. Authentication and list-unsubscribe headers could not be checked because the AgentMail relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe probe returned 400 (FAIL).** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&…`, which decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. A 400 may be an artifact of the probe, since the URL carries a per-recipient token and an `attn_sid`, but it can't be confirmed from this data. Verify it manually.
- **Two CTAs returned 429 (WARN), so they are unverified rather than broken:**
  - "Complete you Order" (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/-XXkc5HQ…`)
  - "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting..." (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/Q1urwf-q…`)
- **Wrapped links hide the destinations.** All links are Attentive click-tracking redirects (`/e/c/<opaque token>`), including the "web version" link. Destination URLs can't be read from the source.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 32 skipped, so most links are untested.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** 12 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/...`. These are the logo, the "Complete you Order" hero, the product-recommendation banner, the app-store badges, Curbside, Afterpay, Find a Store, and the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons. They may be blocked or rewritten by some clients, and the same host already serves other images over HTTPS, so HTTPS is available.
- **Missing alt text on three images:**
  - `021bd784-11b5-4646-95f8-35c2bd27702a.gif`
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` (also on `http://`)
  - the open-tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Typo in alt text:** the hero image alt is "Complete you Order". Screen readers will read the typo, and the link text carries it too.
- **No plain-text alternative.** See section 5.
- **Not an issue:** the `lang="en"` attribute and `role="presentation"` on the layout tables are present, and the preheader is correctly hidden.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `%%`. The cart contents are not visible in the truncated source, so product personalization could not be verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (FAIL):** the text version is 0 characters. This hurts deliverability and accessibility. It may be an artifact of the capture, but the check as run failed.
- **Unsubscribe link:** a link with the text "Unsubscribe" exists, but it returned 400 (see section 2).
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found (WARN).** The relay may have stripped them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `hello@msgs.skechers.com`, and the relay didn't expose the header.
- **Physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so it can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified. The click URLs are opaque Attentive redirect tokens, and the 429 responses stopped the CTA redirects from resolving. The unsubscribe URL does carry Attentive's own params (`attn_macro`, `attn_pos`, `attn_lid`, `attn_sid`). Nothing confirms that the CTAs land on the cart or a cart-restore URL.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually test the unsubscribe URL (`skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?…`) in a browser and confirm that it succeeds and honors the opt-out.
2. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present in the raw headers at the origin, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `msgs.skechers.com`.
3. Add a multipart `text/plain` part, which also fixes the 0-character text version.
4. Change the 12 `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` image URLs to `https://`.
5. Add alt text to `021bd784-…gif` and `a6ec5198-…jpg` (use `alt=""` if decorative), add `alt=""` to the open pixel, and fill in `<title>`.
6. Fix the "Complete you Order" typo in the alt text and link text.
7. Re-probe the two 429'd CTAs with throttling and follow the redirect chain to check the final landing URLs, UTMs and cart state.
