## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses an XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with a mobile media query and sends clicks through tracking redirects. The confirmed defects are in the third-party tracking pixels: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, an `http://` image source, and missing alt attributes. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture relay, not from the email itself.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-` was sent with a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. That means the ad-server macro was never substituted. The pixel URL may be cached, or the impression may not be counted. The `-11` values and the trailing `advt=-` also look like unpopulated placeholders.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over HTTP. Clients that block mixed content or insecure images may drop it, so the Dotomi/Conversant sync may not fire.
- **Unverified links:** 52 click-tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on tracking or redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Pixel volume:** there are at least three third-party pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com`) on top of the ESP tracking. That adds external requests and several domains that can be blocked or flagged.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 6 images.
  - Two have an empty `src` and no alt text, so they are likely stray or broken `<img>` tags.
  - The other four are the tracking pixels `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (`pxl.mon-trk.com`), `impression_pixel`, and `UCMController`.
  - The pixels should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, plus `aria-hidden="true"` or `role="presentation"`, so screen readers don't read out the filename or URL.
  - The two empty-`src` images should be removed or given a real source.
- **Font fallback:** the base `a{}` rule sets `font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif`, but the only `@font-face` rules are for Open Sans. Founders Grotesk is never loaded, so links always render in the Helvetica fallback.
- **Markup:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, which is inconsistent with the XHTML doctype. This is low impact.
- **Other checks:** no issues found in the visible source, including the viewport meta, the VML/Office namespaces, the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset, and the 499px breakpoint. The HTML was truncated, so I can't audit the rest.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the `myvisualiq.net` pixel is the only unresolved token found. See Section 2.
- The `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` path on `pxl.mon-trk.com` looks like a Salesforce 18-character contact ID, so it is probably a recipient identifier, not a leaked token. The ID is embedded in the image URL.
- No other unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find these headers, but its own note says AgentMail may not capture them. Sally Beauty's sender, `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com`, is a bulk sender, and Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders. I can't call this a violation without the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. QA notes this is expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending subdomain `em.sallybeauty.com` is aligned with the brand domain, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer and unsubscribe:** the physical address and the visible unsubscribe link were not in the truncated HTML, so they are unverified.
- No confirmed compliance defects. I can't confirm compliance from the data provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 52 click-tracking links resolve through redirects that were not probed. UTM parameters, landing-page status and promo alignment could not be verified.
- No continuity defects are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro.** Confirm the ad server substitutes it at send time or render time, and replace the `-11` and `advt=-` placeholders with real IDs.
2. **Switch the Dotomi pixel to HTTPS.** Use `https://login.dotomi.com/...`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support HTTPS.
3. **Add `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"` and `aria-hidden="true"` to all tracking pixels.** Remove or fix the two images with an empty `src`.
4. **Fix the font stack.** Either load Founders Grotesk or remove it from the `a{}` rule.
5. **Capture raw headers.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message source, since the relay copy loses them.
6. **Resolve the tracking redirects.** Follow a sample of the 52 links to check destination status, UTM parameters and landing-page match.
7. **Rerun QA on the full HTML.** The source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and address could not be checked.
