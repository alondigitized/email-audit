# Our Friends of Glossier Sale is here...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's pivot from six consecutive Pink Capsule Collection sends into their annual loyalty-program sale — a necessary gear-change, and the creative signals it confidently. The "ANNUAL SALE" tiled text in aggressive orange dominates the top-right quadrant and immediately reads as a different mode than the soft editorial work of late May and early June.
- The problem is structural: the email's primary CTA is "Create an account," not "Shop now." The 25% off offer is gated behind membership registration, which means a large portion of the list — anyone not already a Friends of Glossier member — is being asked to sign up before they can access the deal. Then, deeper in the email, a *second* conversion ask appears: "Start a subscription and lock in 20% off." Two separate registration/commitment asks in one email, with the actual product-shopping CTA ("Shop now" under each product) buried beneath both.
- The product grid is well-executed and the brand aesthetic holds, but the email conflates a sale announcement with a membership acquisition campaign. Those should be separate briefs. As it stands, members feel nudged to convert to subscribers, and non-members are asked to do homework before they can shop. That's friction stacked on friction.

## What's working

- **"ANNUAL SALE" visual statement** — the bold, tiled orange typography in the top-right breaks cleanly from the Pink Capsule Collection softness and immediately signals a new campaign arc.
- **25% off headline is front and center** — "Friends don't let friends miss up to 25% off" is punchy, friendly, and uses the brand's signature low-key voice effectively.
- **Product grid is clean** — four products with visible price tags and individual "Shop now" CTAs render without layout issues; Glossier You Dew, Boy Brow at $18, and Glossier You Deco are easy to scan.
- **Natural cadence break** — six days after the last Pink Capsule email (the mock ANTM reunion, June 5), this doesn't feel like over-send; timing the annual sale here makes sense.

## What's weak

- **Membership gate kills click momentum** — "Create an account" as the hero CTA stops would-be shoppers cold; most people receiving this email want to buy, not register.
- **No sale pricing shown in the grid** — products display what appear to be full prices (e.g., $66, $18) without any strikethrough or "member price" contrast; the discount feels abstract rather than tangible.
- **Dual conversion asks dilute both** — the subscription pitch ("lock in 20% off") competes with the Friends of Glossier membership pitch rather than supporting it; one email, two different value propositions, neither fully sold.
- **No deadline anywhere visible** — "is here" implies recency but without an end date the urgency evaporates, especially after a long editorial arc that trained readers to browse at leisure.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a "Shop the Sale" CTA, not "Create an account"** — for subscribed members, take them straight to a pre-authenticated sale page; for non-members, make the account-creation the secondary step after showing them the discounted prices. The current flow asks for commitment before delivering value.
- 2. **Show strikethrough pricing in the product grid** — $66 → $49.50 is viscerally more motivating than "up to 25% off*" in the hero. Make the math real.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline in the hero** — even "ends Sunday" changes reader behavior. The Pink Capsule emails had no deadlines and that trained the list to browse passively.
- 4. **Move the subscription pitch to a separate email** — the "lock in 20% off forever" angle is actually a strong standalone offer; burying it as a secondary block here undercuts both the sale and the subscription pitch.
- 5. **Put the % off in the subject line** — subscribers who know exactly what "Friends of Glossier Sale" is will open anyway, but new and lapsed members need the discount number upfront.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends of Glossier Sale — up to 25% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% off starts now (Friends of Glossier Sale)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Boy Brow, Glossier You, and more — at member prices. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends soon: your best prices on Glossier classics, member-exclusive.`
