- This is the strongest hero this brand has run recently. The subject "Boo-tiful nails have arrived 👻" promises Halloween-themed nails, and the hero delivers immediately: a striking close-up of purple-glitter, black-swirl press-on nails with the headline "No tricks. Just great nails. Meet the latest press-ons & stickers, just in time for spooky season." Subject, image, and copy are all locked to the same idea — a rare hit for this brand after several sends (9/17, 9/14, 9/11) where the subject over-promised and the hero delivered something else.
- Where it falls down is specificity and offer strength. There's no price, no discount, no bundle callout on the featured press-on product — just "Shop now." ## What's working

- Subject and hero image are thematically aligned — spooky-season nail art shown front and center, no bait-and-switch.
- Clear single hero CTA ("Shop now") sits right under a concise, benign headline.
- Top utility bar surfaces two real, concrete offers (BOPIS same-day, free 2-hour delivery on $35+) above the fold.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images, footer/legal fully intact.
- Persona fit is strong for a beauty-category subscriber around Halloween timing.

## What's weak

- No price or specific product name attached to the featured press-on/sticker nails — just "New at Sally... Shop now," so there's nothing concrete to click for.
- The "Or shop what's trending" section (hair color/toner) is completely off-theme from the Halloween nail hook, diluting the one clear idea the email opened with.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the body (Appiversary/2x points block has a date but it's buried in fine print, not in visual hierarchy).
- Loyalty benefit (2x Rewards on app purchases) is mentioned but only in a small pink module, not tied back to the nail purchase decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the product and price on the hero press-on nails (e.g., "Halloween Press-Ons, $7.99") — this is the single biggest lift since the subject sets up a specific product expectation the body never cashes in.
- 2. Replace the generic hair-color "trending" grid with 2-4 actual Halloween/nail SKUs (other press-on designs, top coats, nail art tools) to keep the whole email on-theme.
- 3. Add a light urgency cue near the hero ("Halloween styles won't last") since spooky-season nail sends have a natural, credible shelf life.
- 4. Move the 2x Rewards/app callout closer to the hero or add a one-line loyalty note under the CTA to reinforce value for existing members.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 52 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (not independently verified reachable) — no way to confirm destination validity from this data alone.
- One image request uses plain HTTP: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id...` — non-HTTPS resources are frequently blocked by mail clients (Gmail/Outlook proxy image loading over HTTPS), risking silent pixel failure.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images flagged missing `alt` text, including one with an empty `src` attribute (`src: ""`) — an empty src can trigger a broken request to the current page URL in some clients.
- Most flagged images are 1x1 tracking pixels (Evergage summary.png ×4, mon-trk pixel, myvisualiq impression_pixel, dotomi UCMController) — low visual impact, but best practice is still `alt=""` (not omitted) to avoid screen-reader announcing the raw src/filename.
- Custom web fonts (Open Sans via Google Fonts `@font-face`) are declared but many mail clients strip remote `@font-face`/CSS; no fallback stack issue observed since `Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is present on anchor styles.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
