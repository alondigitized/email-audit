# Don't miss 15% off your order
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean last-call Black Friday send with a clear hero: "15% off when you buy online, pick up in-store. Ends today!" in big white type on black, with a white Shop now button. The eye lands on the offer first. That's the strongest part of it.
- The email then loses focus. Two red banners stack above the hero, one for Thanksgiving closure and one for 2-hour delivery. Below the hero, an unrelated "Shop Your Favorites" grid shows four ion and Beauty Secrets products (booster, sealer packette, tint brush, nail glue). None of them tie to the 15% offer. A rewards gift-card promo and a Klarna block follow before the footer.
- The email is also close to a repeat of the 11/15 "Shop NEW! Black Friday Deals" send, which had the same black hero and the same "Shop now" button. The offer is genuinely time-bound today, but the pickup condition and the long fine print soften it. It's competent, but a 6-7 send rather than a standout.

## What's working

- The black hero with a huge "15% off" and a white Shop now button is unambiguous and high contrast.
- "Ends today!" is a credible deadline, since the Thanksgiving closure banner explains it.
- The Sally Beauty Rewards block adds member value ($5 bonus reward with a $50 gift card, ends Nov 30).
- The red SALLY. logo, red banners and black hero are consistent with the brand's recent sends.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Two red banners above the hero (store closure and 2-hour delivery) compete with the offer and push it down the screen.
- The product grid is generic. It shows four unrelated items with no prices, no reviews and plain "Shop now" links.
- The hero photo shows only tote bags and legs. It has no product and no person, so it says nothing about hair or beauty.
- The "online order, in-store pickup" condition is buried under the headline. The fine print carries exclusions and a long list of terms.
- The rewards promo, the Klarna block and the icon row make a long scroll. The 15% offer has to compete with all of it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the two top banners, or merge them into one slim line. Put the hero first so the offer is visible without scrolling.
- 2. Replace the tote-bag photo and the random product grid with three best-sellers shown at their 15%-off prices. Link each to its own product page.
- 3. Move "Buy online, pick up in-store" into the hero headline, so the condition is clear before the click.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop 15% off + pick up today". Add a countdown or "Ends 11:59 PM tonight".
- 5. Move the rewards block below the product grid and keep it as the single secondary offer. Cut the Klarna block or shrink it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 15% off before Thanksgiving`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order online, pick up today. Stores close Nov 26.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a $5 bonus reward when you buy a $50 gift card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is table-based XHTML with an MSO/VML fallback. Its main confirmed defects are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` token in a third-party pixel, an insecure `http://` pixel, and missing alt text on the dynamic images. The header-based compliance checks couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 53 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro:** the impression pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&…` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was never replaced, so the cache-buster is a constant. Clients and proxies that cache images (Gmail, Apple MPP) can then dedupe or miss the impression.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` loads over plain HTTP. Clients can block it as mixed content, and it exposes the request in transit.
- **Pixel count:** at least four third-party tracking pixels are visible: `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com` and `sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com`. The truncated source may hide more.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** QA flagged 10 images without alt text.
  - Four are the Evergage dynamic content images: `…/campaign/Vwn6U/je1h1/{1,2,3,4}/summary.png`. These look like content (product or recommendation blocks), so they need real alt text.
  - Two have an empty `src` (`Image missing alt text: : src:`). Empty-src images can trigger a broken-image icon or a request to the page URL in some clients.
  - Three are tracking pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `myvisualiq` and `dotomi`). They should have `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
- **Body CSS:** the visible `<style>` block has a mobile breakpoint (`max-width:499px`) and `u ~ div .pattern` Gmail handling. The source is truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the markup.
- **Font:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` falls back to Helvetica. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the `myvisualiq` pixel (see section 2).
- **PII in URL:** the Evergage image URLs carry `userId=rae-l@ete…` (truncated in the QA output). The recipient's email address appears in a query string in plaintext. It can leak through proxy logs, image-proxy caches and referrer data. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
- **Dynamic images:** the four Evergage `summary.png` images are rendered server-side per recipient. A failed or blocked request would leave empty slots, and without alt text there is no fallback.
