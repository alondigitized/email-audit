# Your picks, up to 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a browse-abandonment style send ("your picks") wrapped in the Black Friday sale. The hero is strong: a tray of Multi-Sticks in a foil pie dish with "now serving your favorites" and a "20% OFF*" badge. The "SHOP YOUR PICKS →" button sits right under it. It follows a Nov 21 "Choose your shade of 30% off" send and a Nov 22 subscription push, and it keeps the same maroon-serif voice.
- The "your picks" framing promises personalization, but the render doesn't deliver on it. The four products are unlabeled with no prices or reviews. Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 appears twice, with the same bottle, same badges and same label. That reads as filler, not a curated list.
- The offer stack is also muddled. The top banner says 25% off for VIP, the hero says 20%, and the second hero says up to 40%. A subscriber has to work out which number applies to them.
- Overall it's a competent, on-brand send that lands in the middle of the range. The personalization is the best idea in it and the least visible in the render.

## What's working

- The hero is distinctive and seasonal: the foil pie tin of Multi-Sticks fits Black Friday and sets ILIA apart from generic sale banners.
- The "SHOP YOUR PICKS →" button is a full-width maroon bar directly under the headline, so the primary action is obvious.
- The "extra, extra" block with a red "UP TO 40% OFF*" badge and a red "SALE'S ON SALE →" button adds a second, visually distinct hook.
- The "very important perks" panel covers free shipping, VIP points and early access, which gives members a reason to join.
- The layout is clean and has no render bugs. Images load and the type is legible.

## What's weak

- Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 appears twice in a four-item grid, so the "your picks" claim feels fake.
- The product tiles have no price, no sale price, no shade and no reviews. Each shows only a name and a generic "shop now" button.
- Three different discount levels (25%, 20%, up to 40%) compete, and the asterisks leave the real deal unclear.
- Every product button says "shop now", which is vague and repeated four times.
- This is another Black Friday send in a run of near-identical ones: Nov 20 "Our very best—very on sale", Nov 21 twice, Nov 22 and today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the personalization real. Show the actual viewed or carted items, each with a sale price such as "$32 → $25.60", and drop the duplicate.
- 2. Pick one headline offer. Lead with "20% off your picks" and move 25% VIP and 40% to a single line lower down.
- 3. Make the product CTAs specific, for example "Shop Multi-Stick" or "Add to bag", and add star ratings.
- 4. Add a deadline such as "ends Monday" to the hero.
- 5. Reduce cadence. Suppress this for recipients who got the Nov 22 subscription send in the last 24 hours, or merge it with the "extra, extra" content.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still thinking about these? 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Multi-Stick you viewed is on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your picks, now 20% off, plus a free gift on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Monday. VIPs get 25% off.`
