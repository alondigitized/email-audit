# Meet KJH.brand by Katie Jane Hughes 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine launch email — KJH.brand by Katie Jane Hughes is new to Sephora, and the "Play Makes Perfect" hero with the artfully strewn makeup collection on burgundy is a strong, distinct visual identity compared to the generic catalog-scroll Sephora has leaned on repeatedly (2026-08-16 "They're always SO good," 2026-08-15 "The beauty forecast"). Katie Jane Hughes is a recognizable name in the makeup-artist-influencer space, and leading with her product-by-product story (blush, contour stick, lip crayon, highlighter, brushes, mascara, cream serum) gives the email a coherent narrative instead of a grab-bag.
- Where it falls down is structure and offer clarity: after the top Powerbox promo banner, the email is nine near-identical product-hero blocks stacked vertically with almost no visual differentiation — same centered-photo-then-caption pattern repeated eight times. There's no single "shop the full line" CTA until the very bottom, and the actual launch offer (if any beyond the unrelated "Get a Mini" fragrance promo) is never stated. It reads like a lookbook, not an email built to convert.
- Solid creative concept, weak email architecture. This should have been a hero + 3-4 product grid with one clear CTA, not a nine-screen scroll.

## What's working

- Clear, on-brand launch framing: "KJH.BRAND IS NOW AT SEPHORA" with "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" badge immediately signals exclusivity.
- Consistent color-blocked backgrounds per product (mauve, lavender, coral, mint) make the scroll visually distinct from Sephora's usual white catalog grids.
- Each product block includes a real name + price ($24, $23, $26), so browsing is at least informative.
- "Shop KJH brand" and repeated "SHOP NOW" buttons keep a consistent CTA pattern throughout.
- Diverse, well-shot model photography across the product blocks (varied skin tones, close-up beauty detail shots).

## What's weak

- Top "Get a Mini" fragrance banner (Armani Beauty) has nothing to do with the KJH launch — it's a jarring non-sequitur before the hero even loads.
- No explicit percentage/dollar-off or bundle offer tied to the KJH launch itself — every CTA is just "SHOP NOW," no urgency or incentive.
- Nine sequential single-product blocks with identical layout create serious scroll fatigue; nothing breaks the rhythm until "Spot it. Shop it." near the bottom.
- The email has no summary/hero grid up top that lets someone scan the whole collection in one view — you must scroll the entire length to see what's in the line.
- Bottom section crams in unrelated offers (Bonus Points, Best of Hair, Exfoliants) that dilute the launch focus after already asking for a long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the nine sequential hero blocks to a 3x3 or 2x4 product grid immediately below "Play Makes Perfect," with one unified "Shop the Full KJH Collection" CTA — this is the single biggest lift, since scroll fatigue is the email's core problem.
- 2. Remove or move the Armani "Get a Mini" banner — it competes with the launch narrative in the most valuable inbox real estate (top of email).
- 3. Add a concrete incentive for the launch (e.g., "Free deluxe KJH mini with $35 purchase") — right now there is no reason to act now versus later.
- 4. Move "Spot it. Shop it." and the unrelated cross-sell offers (Bonus Points, Best of Hair, Exfoliants) into a separate follow-up send so this email stays focused on the launch.
- 5. Add social proof (KJH's follower count, a quote from her, or "as seen on") near the hero — leaning into her personal brand recognition would strengthen the click case.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Katie Jane Hughes' first makeup line is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `9 pro-artist essentials, only at Sephora`
