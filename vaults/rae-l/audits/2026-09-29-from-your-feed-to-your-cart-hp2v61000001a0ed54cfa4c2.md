# From your feed to your cart 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a Sephora Daily Deals send that promises social-media-famous products ("From your feed to your cart") and opens with a real offer: "Up to 50% off Hair Faves" with a "TODAY ONLY" badge and a "Shop Now" button. The hero is clear and time-bound. Right after it, a "SOCIAL STARS" banner ties back to the subject. Then the email loses its thread.
- The middle third is a Cult Gaia fragrance feature: a big product image, a list of prices ($48–$228), and a "Now at Sephora" headline. That is a different message from the subject and the hero. Below it sits a 10-product grid with no offer tie-in. Then come a Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal pay-later tile, a 20% off value-size hair care tile, a Korean skincare banner, and a Beauty Insider strip. That is about six competing messages, and the fine print at the bottom runs very long.
- Judgement: an average send, better than the 9/25 "New beauty to obsess over" (4/10). The hair deal is credible, but the email works against itself. Like the 9/28 Living Proof send, it uses a strong hair offer at the top and then dilutes it. The subject's "feed to cart" social angle only shows up in the "Social Stars" banner.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% off Hair Faves / TODAY ONLY" hero puts a concrete offer and urgency at the top.
- The "P.S. Get FREE Same-Day Delivery with code DELIVERED. Ends 10/7" line adds a second, low-friction incentive.
- The "SOCIAL STARS" banner matches the subject's theme with a clear "Shop Now" CTA.
- The product grid labels items with "Only at Sephora," "Clean," "Limited Edition," and "Online only," which gives real detail for shoppers.
- Every section has a visible button, and no images are broken.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is only for hair, while the subject and the Social Stars banner sell "viral" products, so the message is split.
- The Cult Gaia fragrance feature takes a huge amount of vertical space and has no discount, so the email's best real estate goes to a non-deal.
- The product grid has no ratings, reviews, or "trending" signals, so it doesn't deliver on the "social stars" promise.
- The pay-later tile, Korean skincare banner, and Beauty Insider strip add clutter near the bottom.
- The legal fine print is a dense wall of text, and the "up to 50%" and "20% off" hair offers overlap and blur together.
- The email is very long, at about 5,900px.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either make it "Today only: up to 50% off hair" and drop the social framing, or make it a true "TikTok-famous" edit with review counts and a discount.
- 2. Move the Cult Gaia feature lower or cut it, and put the Hair Faves product grid right under the hero.
- 3. Add social proof to the product tiles: star ratings, "1M views," or "Best seller."
- 4. Merge the two hair offers into one clear deal, and drop the pay-later tile from Daily Deals.
- 5. Cut the email to roughly two-thirds of its length and shorten the footer disclaimers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, confirmed by QA):**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx...`
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../20260929_Social_stars_hed_07.jpg`
  - The last two are content or header images, so image-blocking clients will show no fallback text. The first is probably a tracking pixel and should have `alt=""`.

- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` are applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. Users can't scale text in some clients.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`. The valid token is `noindex`, so this tag is likely ignored. It has low impact in email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is minimal and gives screen readers no context.
