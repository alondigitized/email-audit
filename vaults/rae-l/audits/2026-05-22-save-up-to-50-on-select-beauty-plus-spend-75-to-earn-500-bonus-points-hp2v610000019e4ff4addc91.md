# Save up to 50% on select beauty 💄 Plus spend $75 to earn 500 bonus points.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a textbook Sephora promotional send — "Stacking Weekend" is smart naming that reframes two separate offers (50% off select items + 500 bonus points on $75 spend) as a single, intentional mechanic rather than two competing banners fighting for attention. The hero executes the concept cleanly: big numbers, one CTA, done. Where the email falls apart is everything below the fold. Four product carousels, a frizz-smoothing pick section, a body minis section, and a store-locator module make the email feel like five campaigns merged into one deployment. The "Stacking Weekend" hero earns its click; the body dilutes it.
- Compared to the May 17 editorial "THE CHAT" send (9.0/10), which was tight, content-first, and highly differentiated, this send is Sephora in discount mode — which is fine, but the email doesn't know when to stop. The April 26 welcome email had the same sprawl problem. This is becoming a pattern in the brand's sends: strong top, cluttered body.
- Judgment: A well-conceived hook buried under too many product rows. Deserves a click from a loyal Beauty Insider; will lose casual openers halfway through.

## What's working

- **"Stacking Weekend" naming** is genuinely clever — it makes the dual-offer feel like a rewards strategy, not a cluttered promo.
- **Hero numbers are unmissable** — 50% and 500 in large type, SHOP NOW button directly below, no ambiguity.
- **Offer terms disclosed upfront** — "up to 50% on select" and the "$75 threshold" are stated in the subject and repeated in the hero, not buried in the footer.
- **Loyalty hook is properly featured** — the 500 bonus points line speaks directly to Beauty Insiders and creates a second reason to click beyond the discount.

## What's weak

- **Four distinct product carousels below the fold** fragment attention — Frizz-Smoothing Picks and Body Minis feel like separate campaigns appended at the last minute.
- **No seasonal or moment-driven context** — a "Stacking Weekend" event could run in any month; nothing visually or verbally signals why this weekend specifically.
- **Subject line is 75+ characters and carries two full offers** — it will be truncated on every mobile client, and the bonus-points offer (arguably more loyalty-sticky) will be cut first.
- **No social proof or editorial context** — unlike the May 17 Chat send, there's no expert voice or community signal here to justify product choices in the carousels.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Shorten and split the subject line** — the bonus points offer deserves its own send or A/B line, not second billing after a long discount phrase.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stacking Weekend: 50% off + 500 points inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider bonus starts now 💄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75 this weekend, earn 500 points — on top of up to 50% off select picks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your savings: half-price beauty AND bonus points, one weekend only.`
- 2. **Cut the body to two carousels max** — drop either Frizz-Smoothing or Body Minis (or merge them), and kill the store-locator module entirely. This is a sale email, not a discovery email.
- 3. **Add a countdown or explicit end date** — "this weekend" is vague; a visible end date ("ends Sunday at midnight") converts urgency into a real reason to act now.
- 4. **Anchor the hero to a season or moment** — one editorial sentence like "Summer starts with a full kit" or "Stock up before Memorial Day" would give the event a reason-to-exist beyond the discount mechanics.
