# All set for Black Friday?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, full-featured Black Friday catch-all. A two-model beauty hero carries "the black friday sale / All Set?" and a pink "20% OFF*" badge. A bundle grid follows, then a free-gift edit, then VIP perks. The hierarchy is clear and the hero is the first thing the eye lands on. The offer is easy to find.
- It is also the busiest send in this run. Today's earlier "Your free Complexion Stick is waiting" put one gift first and did it well. This email repeats that gift as one of four in the gift edit. The 11-26 "New. Gifts. Added." send used the same gift story. The cadence is heavy, and the "All Set?" framing is a generic nudge. The sitewide 25% banner at the very top also conflicts with the 20% badge in the hero.
- Judgement: well built, but it leads with a vague question and mixes offer numbers. It would be stronger with one clear promise.

## What's working

- The hero is striking, with a "20% OFF*" badge that is easy to see.
- The "More, Please" bundle grid has clean product shots, so the products are easy to compare.
- The "the free gift edit" section shows each gift with its spend threshold ($100+, $175+).
- The red "SHOP TO CLAIM →" button stands out against the neutral palette.
- The VIP perks block (free shipping, points, early access) gives members a reason to join.

## What's weak

- The top banner says "25% OFF SITEWIDE*" but the hero says 20% off. Two numbers dilute the offer.
- The hero headline "All Set?" is vague and gives no product or benefit.
- The four "shop now →" buttons are thin outlined boxes that look weak. They also sit below each product, so they could be missed.
- The bundles have no prices or savings shown, so the 20% has no concrete dollar value.
- The fine print is tiny ("*SEE SITE FOR DETAILS", the promotion-details link), so the offer feels conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer one number. Pick 20% or 25% and use it in the banner, hero and subject.
- 2. Replace "All Set?" with a concrete headline, such as "20% off sets + free gifts."
- 3. Show a price or "save $X" under each bundle, and make the buttons solid.
- 4. Cut the gift edit to the top two gifts, and keep the Complexion Stick as the lead since it was today's earlier hook.
- 5. Add a time marker such as "ends Monday" to the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off sets + free gifts, today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ILIA Black Friday sets are live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bundles that just make sense together, plus up to 4 free gifts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free complexion stick with any order. Sets 20% off.`
