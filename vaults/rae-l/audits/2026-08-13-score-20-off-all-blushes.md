# Score 20% off all blushes
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is "Cheek Week" — Glossier's second discount send in two days, following 8/11's straightforward 20%-off blush promo. It's a strong evolution of that idea: same 20% mechanic, but wrapped in a full campaign with a name, four product-education blocks, a four-shade "our favorite shades" grid, and a $75+ free-brush GWP stacked on top. This is the most complete, most commercially aggressive send in the recent thread.
- The hero does its job — offer, product names, and a "Shop now" button all land above the fold with a clean split-screen photo. Where it loses points is redundancy: four nearly identical "product name / description / Shop now" blocks in a row is a lot of scrolling for one 20%-off offer, and the shade grid buttons don't specify which product/shade they route to at a glance. Still, between the clear discount, the GWP, and the campaign branding, this is a well-executed, high-intent commercial email — a clear step up from the editorial-only Leo season and Lip Line sends.

## What's working

- Clear, quantified offer ("score 20% off all Cloud Paint + Cloud Paint Plush Blush") sits in the hero with an unambiguous "Shop now" CTA.
- Diverse, well-lit model casting across the shade grid (multiple skin tones) makes the color range easy to evaluate at a glance.
- The free-gift block ($75+ order gets a free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush) is a legible secondary incentive that could push order size up.
- Campaign naming ("Cheek Week") gives the send a memorable frame instead of a generic discount blast.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and copy all display cleanly with consistent spacing.

## What's weak

- Four back-to-back "product/description/Shop now" modules before even reaching the shade grid is repetitive; a subscriber skimming on a commute will bail before the GWP block.
- The shade-grid CTAs just say "Shop now" under each face — there's a shade name in the caption but the button itself doesn't reinforce which product it's tied to.
- The GWP fine print ("Offer exclusively available during Cheek Week," "*Terms apply") is buried at the very bottom with no deadline date, so the urgency is vague.
- Two straight days of discount emails (8/11 then this) risks list fatigue if this cadence continues.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the four individual product modules down to two (Cloud Paint + Plush Blush) and let the shade grid carry the rest — this shortens the scroll to the offer and GWP by roughly half.
- 2. Move the free-brush GWP block up near the hero (or add a mention in the hero itself) since it's a strong secondary hook currently buried below five other content blocks.
- 3. Add shade names to the "Shop now" buttons in the grid (e.g., "Shop Soar") so the CTA carries the same specificity as the caption above it.
- 4. Put a concrete end date on "Cheek Week" in the hero copy — "20% off through 8/17" reads more credible than an undated campaign name.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cheek Week: 20% off Cloud Paint, starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your blush drawer needs a refresh — 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: a free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush with $75+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4 shades, 1 week, 20% off every one`
