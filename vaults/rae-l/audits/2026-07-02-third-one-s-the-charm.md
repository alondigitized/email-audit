# Third one’s the charm
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- "Third One's the Charm" is a creative follow-up to yesterday's "Buy 3, Save 20% starts now" launch send, narrowing the broad "everything you've been eyeing" framing down to a focused eye-look editorial. ILIA leans into a dice/luck metaphor — "any way you roll, the odds are Buy 3, Save 20%" — which is a fun wordplay riff on "third time's the charm," but dice don't naturally read as eye makeup, and the visual is tonally looser than the brand's typical clean-skin aesthetic.
- Where this email earns its keep is in the product curation: eye cream → liner trio → mascara → shadow trio is a coherent layering ritual, and all four products conveniently require buying three to unlock the threshold. That's good commercial engineering. The "award-winning" and "fan-favorite" inline labels add lightweight social proof without a heavy review module.
- The critical gap is urgency. There is no deadline anywhere in this email — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing. ILIA ran a mechanically identical buy-3 offer yesterday and is now sending a creative refresh with the same code and no incremental reason to act today. The subject line compounds the problem: "Third one's the charm" is a riddle to any subscriber who missed yesterday's send, delivering a wink without a payoff.

## What's working

- Buy 3, Save 20% offer and code (BUNDLEUP) are stated in the top banner and the primary CTA — zero hunting required.
- Eye-look product curation is coherent: primer skincare → liner → mascara → shadow is a natural routine that happens to hit the 3-item buy threshold.
- "Award-winning" and "fan-favorite" appear inline with product copy — social proof woven into the editorial rather than shunted into a review module.
- Render is clean: no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent ILIA aesthetic throughout.
- The dice visual + wordplay lands as charming brand personality even if the metaphor is loose.

## What's weak

- No deadline anywhere — the promo feels open-ended, eliminating any urgency to buy today rather than next week.
- "Third one's the charm" is opaque without yesterday's context; a subscriber who missed the launch gets a riddle, not an offer.
- Dice visual is fun but reads off-brand against ILIA's usual clean editorial; the connection to eye makeup requires a beat to land.
- Two separate CTAs ("SHOP 20% OFF TRIOS" and "BUY 3, SAVE 20%") dilute the single-action clarity ILIA usually achieves.
- ILIA VIP/loyalty benefits appear only in the footer — no member-priority angle surfaced in the body to reward the most likely converters.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a sale end date — this is the highest-leverage single change.** "Ends Sunday" in the top banner or below the primary CTA would create the urgency this email entirely lacks. A buy-3 threshold is a decision, not an impulse — people need a reason to decide now.
- 2. **Rewrite the primary button.** "USE CODE: BUNDLEUP" instructs but doesn't sell. Replace with "SHOP EYE LOOKS — 20% OFF" to match the editorial angle and make the action feel rewarding, not procedural.
- 3. **Reorder products so the eye cream leads.** The Bright Start Retinol Alternative Eye Cream is step 1 in any eye ritual but appears last. Moving it to the top justifies the premium skincare entry point before you hit the makeup product cascade.
- 4. **Consolidate to one CTA.** "SHOP 20% OFF TRIOS" and "BUY 3, SAVE 20%" both direct to the same intent but split click mass. One unified CTA landing on a filtered eye category page is cleaner.
- 5. **Let the subject carry the offer; let the headline carry the charm.** The pun is strong enough to live in the email body — the inbox subject line needs to anchor the deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 3, save 20%—every eye look you've been eyeing`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack your eye routine. Save 20%.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Third one's the charm — any way you roll, Buy 3 saves you 20%. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The eye look, the mascara, the trio. Buy any 3, save 20% with code BUNDLEUP.`
