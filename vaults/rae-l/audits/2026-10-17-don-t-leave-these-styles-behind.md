# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment shell again, reworked as "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" over a six-shoe grid. The new part is a real incentive: a navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner, valid through 10/21. Compared with the 2026-09-30 "This style has limited availability!" send, which promised scarcity and delivered a plain second look, this one at least gives a reason to come back.
- The execution is weak where it matters. The offer sits below the fold of the product grid, and the hero headline and generic "SHOP NOW" button do the selling before the 20% is visible. The grid mixes women's sneakers, a girls' Mommy & Me slip-in, and a boot. That reads as a pile, not as "styles you looked at." The subject, "Don't leave these styles behind!", is a loose cousin of the 2026-08-16 and 2026-08-24 "Don't leave a good thing behind." sends. A subscriber would see this as the fourth or fifth rewording of the same nudge.
- Verdict: a competent, clean template with a real member discount, but the template is stale, the discount is buried, and nothing about the shoes explains why they were "saved."

## What's working

- The 20% member discount is a concrete, time-bound offer (valid thru 10/21) in a high-contrast navy block that is hard to miss once you reach it.
- The headline and the blue "SHOP NOW" button are clear and easy to tap, with strong contrast.
- Six product images with individually linked names let the click go to specific shoes, not the homepage.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or layout bugs.
- The footer category list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals, Sale) gives a fallback path for anyone the six products miss.

## What's weak

- The 20% offer appears only after the headline, CTA and full product grid, so the strongest hook isn't in the first screen.
- The product names are small, underlined text with no prices, no sale marks and no ratings. Nothing shows why these shoes matter or what 20% off would save.
- The grid is incoherent: a girls' Mommy & Me slip-in sits next to women's boots and flats. That undercuts the "we saved these for you" personalization.
- Both CTAs say "SHOP NOW", which is generic and says nothing about the shoes or the savings.
- The offer is gated to Skechers Plus members, "select full-priced styles", and online only. This is explained only in dense fine print, and the banner gives no clear cue that you need to be logged in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% banner above the product grid, or put it in the hero: "Your saved styles, 20% off through 10/21." Offer first, products second.
- 2. Show the saved shoe(s) the shopper actually viewed. Show one or two hero products at large size with the original price and the member price calculated, not a six-up grid.
- 3. Fix the product logic so the grid doesn't mix kids' and women's shoes unless the browse history did, and add star ratings or a "selling fast" signal for real social proof.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop my saved styles" or "Get 20% off", and add a plain-language line near the banner: "Log in as a Plus member to apply".
- 5. Retire the "Don't leave…" subject family. Subjects should name the offer or the product.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your saved styles: 20% off thru 10/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking about those Slip-ins?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% on select full-priced pairs through Oct 21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The sneakers you looked at are waiting, and now they're 20% off.`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is Skechers' browse-abandonment shell again, reworked as "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" over a six-shoe grid. The new part is a real incentive: a navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner, valid through 10/21. The execution is weak where it matters. The offer sits below the fold of the product grid, and the hero headline and generic "SHOP NOW" button do the selling before the 20% is visible. The grid mixes women's sneakers, a girls' Mommy & Me slip-in, and a boot. That reads as a pile, not as "styles you looked at." ## 3. What didn't

- The 20% offer appears only after the headline, CTA and full product grid, so the strongest hook isn't in the first screen.
