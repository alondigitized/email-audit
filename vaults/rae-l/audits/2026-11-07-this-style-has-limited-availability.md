# This style has limited availability!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is another Skechers browse-abandonment send, at least the eighth abandonment-style email in a week. It follows 11/7 "Don't leave these styles behind!", 11/6 "This is perfect for you!", the 11/4 and 11/5 "Your cart is one click away!" sends, and the 11/1 "Pick up where you left off!" send. The subject promises scarcity ("limited availability"). The body says "HOW ABOUT A SECOND LOOK? SEEN THIS BEFORE? WE THOUGHT SO." and shows nothing about stock. That's the same subject/body mismatch as the 11/2 "Your cart expires soon!" send.
- The render is clean. The black Arch Fit Arcade slip-in hero is clear, and "SHOP NOW" is an obvious navy button. The six-shoe grid below is tidy. The real offer is the 20% off Skechers Plus banner valid through 11/10. It sits about a third of the way down, below the hero and the grid, so it's easy to miss.
- My judgement: competent and on-brand, but it's inbox wallpaper. Subscribers have seen this layout on nearly every day of the week, and the pitch is a gentle nudge rather than a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero is a single product in clear focus, with its name below and one obvious "SHOP NOW" button.
- The 20% off Skechers Plus banner has a high-contrast dark block, large type and a stated deadline (valid thru 11/10).
- The six-up recommendation grid adds browse depth without clutter. Product names are legible.
- The render has no broken images or overlapping text, and the footer is complete (nav, app, Klarna/Afterpay, store finder).
- The "Valid thru 11/10" line and the Holiday Checklist fine print make the offer's terms discoverable and fairly honest.

## What's weak

- The subject's "limited availability" claim has no support in the body. There is no low-stock badge, no size scarcity and no countdown.
- The 20% off sits below the fold and below a generic product grid. The hero shows no price, discount or reason to click.
- Both CTAs read "SHOP NOW". The hero button doesn't say what you're buying (Arch Fit Arcade) or what you get.
- The hero image has a large dead whitespace band above and below it, which pushes the grid and the offer further down.
- The 20% off is for Plus members only and regular-priced styles only, and that is stated only in small grey text. A non-member sees the banner as exclusionary.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus offer into the hero. For example: "20% OFF the Arch Fit Arcade, through 11/10". Put the discount and deadline beside the shoe, not under a grid.
- 2. Make the scarcity claim true or drop it. If stock is low, show "Only a few sizes left" under the hero. If it isn't, don't say it in the subject.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with a specific CTA such as "Get the Arch Fit Arcade" or "Claim 20% off".
- 4. Trim the vertical whitespace around the hero so the offer is visible on the first mobile screen.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Collapse the abandonment sends into one escalating sequence (reminder, then social proof, then offer) instead of daily near-identical sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Arch Fit Arcade: 20% off ends 11/10`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking about these? Sizes are going`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers Plus members save 20% on regular-priced styles through 11/10.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Arch Fit Arcade slip-ins are waiting, plus 5 more you might like.`
