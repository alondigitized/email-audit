# 20% off ends in 3, 2, 1...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean last-chance reminder for the BUNDLEUP offer. The maroon announcement bar, the lilac "last chance" tag and the big "buy 3 = 20% off" headline all say the same thing. A single maroon "ADD 3 TO CART →" button sits below the hero, and nothing else competes with it. It's easy to understand in two seconds.
- It's also the fourth send in five days on the same offer. It follows "Add 3, subtract 20%" (10-09), "Buy 3, save 20%—limited time only" (10-10) and "Friend, your 20% off trio awaits" (10-11). The subject's countdown framing is stronger than the body delivers. The copy opens with "The biggest factor here? Time." but gives no deadline. It never says what "today" means, and it doesn't show any products to build a trio from. Urgency is claimed, not proven.
- Verdict: the creative is well executed, but it's a repeat send with no new reason to act. It will convert people who were already on the fence and train everyone else to ignore ILIA's bundle mail.

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer first: "buy 3 = 20% off" in large type over a warm product still life.
- The "last chance" tag and the "BUY 3, SAVE 20% | USE CODE 'BUNDLEUP'" bar repeat the offer and code without clutter.
- The CTA is a full-width maroon button with a verb and a noun ("ADD 3 TO CART"), and it contrasts well with the cream background.
- The code BUNDLEUP is bolded in the body copy, so it's easy to find.
- The footer trust row (free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, shade match) adds credibility without distracting.

## What's weak

- No deadline is stated. "Today's your last chance" gives no time, so the countdown subject isn't backed up in the body.
- The hero product shot (a tint, a serum, a mascara, a notebook and dominoes) is mostly mood. No product is named or priced, and none links to a specific item.
- The large pink "ILIA / Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better" banner is a brand block that takes up space and has no action attached.
- It's the fourth near-identical bundle send in five days, which means fatigue and little new information. The 10-11 "Friend, your 20% off trio awaits" send was already a reminder.
- The promotion terms are a small "Click HERE for ILIA Promotion Details" link at the very bottom. A one-line exclusions note near the CTA would feel more honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline. Put "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT" in the hero or in the bar above the CTA. That one line is what makes the countdown subject believable.
- 2. Replace the pink brand banner with three or four named hero products (tint, mascara, blush) with prices and a "build your trio" grid. This gives shoppers something to pick.
- 3. Give a reason beyond the discount. For example, show the effective per-item price or a "most-picked trio" so the offer feels curated.
- 4. Reduce cadence. Stop the three-nudge sequence, or make the last-chance send clearly different from the earlier ones (a different hero, a "last hours" timestamp).
- 5. Put a short terms line directly under the CTA, such as "Excludes gift sets. Discount applied at checkout."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use BUNDLEUP on any three products before 11:59 PM PT.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
