# Earn 4X points on hair care for your healthiest hair yet ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Sephora is running the exact same loyalty mechanic — 4X points on hair care — that it sent three days ago on July 19. That send scored a 9/10 here because it was fresh, loud, and well-timed. This one is a quieter, more editorial take on the same offer: "Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science" as a hero, with a long scrolling catalog organized by hair concern (Essentials, Dry Hair, Frizzy Hair, etc.). The science-backed framing is a credible differentiator, but it lands second — the 4X badge doesn't appear until well past the fold, which is the opposite of what the July 19 send did right.
- The bottom of the email pivots to a "Up to 50% off Select Lip Picks" section that has nothing to do with the subject line. That's audience mismatch buried at the end, which is just noise.
- Overall: a competent catalog send that feels like a cleanup lap on the July 19 promo. Opened because of brand trust, not because this email earned it.

## What's working

- **"Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science" headline** is a strong differentiating hook — more compelling than a pure price play.
- **Product catalog organized by concern** (Essentials / Dry / Frizzy) is a genuinely useful browsing structure that gives shoppers a fast entry point.
- **Complimentary trial sizes offer** at the top of the email adds incremental value beyond just the points mechanic.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout bugs, no overlapping text visible anywhere in the scroll.
- **4X loyalty callout box** is visually distinct with a bold badge, so when you reach it, it reads clearly.

## What's weak

- **This is the July 19 email reheated.** Same offer (4X on hair care), same brand (Sephora Insider), three days apart — a subscriber who opened the last one will feel like they're getting re-mailed the same promo with a new headline.
- **The 4X points hook is buried mid-scroll.** The strongest loyalty signal doesn't surface until well past the hero — the email leads with an editorial concept instead of leading with the value prop.
- **"Earn 4X points… for your healthiest hair yet" subject line is over 50 characters** (~57 with emoji) — clips on most mobile inboxes, losing the "✨" and the tail.
- **Lip picks section at the bottom** is completely off-topic for a hair care send — it reads as a last-minute cross-sell bolted onto the wrong email.
- **No visible time deadline on the 4X offer** — the July 19 send had urgency cues; this one doesn't, making the offer feel passive.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the email with the 4X badge, not the editorial concept.** The science angle can still be the headline, but the loyalty callout needs to be in the hero unit — above the fold — not in the middle of a scroll. July 19 got 9/10 because the offer was the first thing you saw.
- 2. **Add an expiration date.** One line — "Ends Sunday" or "Offer ends July 27" — turns a browse into a reason to act today.
- 3. **Cut the lip picks section entirely**, or hold it for a separate send. Cross-category noise at the end dilutes the email's identity.
- 4. **Shorten the subject line** to keep it under 50 characters on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn 4X on hair care — shop the science`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Healthy hair starts here. Plus, 4X points.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Find your fix: dry, frizzy, fine, or all of the above.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free trial sizes included. Stock up on your favorites.`
- 5. **Tie this back to the July 19 send with a "Last chance" frame** if the offer is ending — otherwise suppress to openers of the prior send to avoid fatigue.
