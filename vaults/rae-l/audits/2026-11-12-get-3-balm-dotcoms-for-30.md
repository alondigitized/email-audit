# Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, single-offer send. The subject promises 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30, and the render delivers it: a hero with three tubes and a $30 badge, then six flavors with crossed-out $16 → $10 prices. Yesterday's send (11/11, free Discovery Kit) was a pure incentive too. Compared with the no-offer merch run from 11/5 to 11/9, Glossier is clearly in promo mode now.
- The execution is clean, but the offer logic is muddy. The copy says "we've curated two balm sets," while the grid shows six individual balms at $10 each. A reader can't tell whether $30 is a pick-any-three deal or a fixed set. That confusion sits right where the click decision happens.
- The pink-tinted body copy and the Holiday Shop footer block read as seasonal. A second promo in two days is a heavy cadence, though.

## What's working

- The subject and hero headline match exactly, with a concrete price ($30 for 3).
- The two grouped rows ("Sultry tints" vs. "Subtle favs") help someone choose a shade.
- Every tile has a flavor and tint descriptor, like "Roasted coffee + mocha tint". That is useful product detail, and the photography is vivid and on-brand.
- The blue strike-through price tags ($16 → $10) are high-contrast and show the saving at a glance.
- The Holiday Shop block at the bottom gives a second path for shoppers who don't want a balm.

## What's weak

- The $30 price badge on the hero is cropped at the top right, and the second badge is cut off ("$6…"). It looks like a render bug on the key offer.
- "Two balm sets" in the copy doesn't match the six individually priced tiles. It's unclear how the 3-for-$30 bundle works.
- Every CTA says "Shop now", including all six tile buttons, so none of them is specific.
- "Limited time" gives no end date, so the urgency isn't credible.
- There's no social proof (reviews, ratings, best-seller tags) on a "cult-favorite" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer mechanics. Say "Pick any 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30" in the hero, and show the cart math ($48 → $30) once. Drop the "two sets" language, or show the two sets as actual bundles.
- 2. Reposition the hero badge so the $30 isn't clipped. Test a centered badge on the tubes.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday", and put it in the hero.
- 4. Make the tile CTAs specific, like "Add Black Cherry". Add a star rating or "Best seller" tag to one tile.
- 5. Pull the Holiday Shop block above the footer only if it doesn't dilute the offer. Otherwise cut it.
