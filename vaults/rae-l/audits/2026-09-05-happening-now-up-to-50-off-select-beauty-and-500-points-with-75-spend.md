# Happening now: up to 50% off select beauty and 500 points with $75 spend
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Sephora Stacking Weekend" send repeated — same core structure as 2026-09-04's "Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points" — but this time it's buried under a "Free Hair Sample Set" mega-module and a long undifferentiated product catalog before the actual 50% off beauty offer shows up again lower down. The hero does the job (dark red "Stacking Weekend" block, big "50%" burst, "500 BONUS POINTS" chip, clear "SHOP NOW" button), so the top of the email is fine. The problem is everything after it: a giant free-sample redemption module, eight unrelated new-launch product tiles, then the 50% off beauty offer re-appears as a second, weaker banner, followed by two more offers (15% off shampoo/conditioner, a lip launch) — five distinct offers competing in one email.
- This is a kitchen-sink send, not a targeted one. Subscribers who've seen five straight days of "50% off Select Beauty" variants (2026-08-24 onward) get yet another version of it, now diluted by a hair-sample giveaway and shampoo subscription plug. Score middling — the hero is competent and on-brand, but stacking this many offers with no hierarchy after the fold hurts both clarity and repeat-open fatigue.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible: dark red background, large white "50%" burst, and a distinct "500 BONUS POINTS" callout stacked together match the subject's dual-offer promise.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss at the top.
- Minimum-spend and points requirement ($75 spend, valid 9/4–9/10) are stated directly in the hero, not buried in fine print.
- Product photography throughout is clean, well-lit, and consistent — no broken images or render bugs.
- "NEW" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges give scannable differentiation to the catalog block.

## What's weak

- Five separate offers packed into one email (Stacking Weekend, Free Hair Samples, 50% Off Beauty repeat, 15% Off Shampoo/Conditioner, Lip Lineup) with no single clear priority — the subject promises two offers but the body delivers a grab-bag.
- The 50% off Select Beauty banner is buried roughly two-thirds down the email, after an eight-product catalog crawl, undercutting the subject line's "Happening now" urgency framing.
- This is essentially the same offer structure as 2026-09-04 ("Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points with $75 spend") sent one day later — near-identical to yesterday's send with a padded-out body, which reads as cadence fatigue rather than a fresh campaign.
- The free hair sample module competes directly with the discount hero for attention in the first screen and a half, splitting focus right when the eye should be locked on the 50%/points offer.
- "TODAY ONLY" appears on the second 50% off banner but the hero at the top says the offer runs through 9/10 — the urgency messaging is inconsistent within the same email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one primary offer per send — lead with Stacking Weekend (50% + 500 points) and move the free hair samples, shampoo subscription, and lip launch into separate, later emails. This single change would do the most to lift clarity and click-through.
- 2. Move the "50% off Select Beauty" restated banner up directly under the hero (or delete the duplicate) so the discount isn't diluted by unrelated catalog content in between.
- 3. Fix the internal urgency inconsistency — hero says valid through 9/10, second banner says "TODAY ONLY." Pick one deadline and use it everywhere.
- 4. Trim the eight-product "new launches" catalog to 2-3 items or move it below all discount content — right now it interrupts the discount narrative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stacking Weekend: 50% off beauty + 500 bonus points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $75 spend = 500 points + 50% off beauty`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop today through 9/10 — stack your rewards before it ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty Insiders: this is the weekend to earn double.`
