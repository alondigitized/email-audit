# Final hours for early access
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a membership-acquisition push dressed up as a sale email. It's the closing bell for the "Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members" send on 11/18. The creative is clean and on-brand: Glossier blue, a giant "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" block, and one blue CTA. But the only action it offers is "Create account," and the discount sits behind a signup wall.
- The hierarchy works. The eye goes to the 25% tile, then the headline "Black Friday Early Access," then the button. The "Why join" list and the model with the pink bottle are pleasant but generic. Nothing shows a product, a price or a reason to pick one thing over another. The 30% OFF $100+ badge also conflicts with the 25% headline, which muddies the offer.
- Verdict: fine as a deadline nudge, but it's the second send in two days for the same membership pitch, and it asks for commitment before it shows value. Expect modest clicks from lapsed browsers and little from existing members.

## What's working

- The 25% OFF tile is large, high-contrast and readable at a glance.
- "FINAL CALL" and "ends tonight" give credible, time-bound urgency.
- There is one clear CTA in the hero, in the same blue as the brand and the offer tile.
- The three-point "Why join" list (early access, free gifts, events) is skimmable and benefit-led.
- The visual identity is unmistakably Glossier: the wordmark, the minimalist layout and the smiley/hand footer.

## What's weak

- The offers conflict: the hero says 25% off everything, while the badge says 30% off $100+. The asterisks stack on top of that.
- Both CTAs, "Create account" and "Join today," are signup asks. Nothing is shoppable, so there's no product, price or category to click.
- It repeats the 11/18 member-access message with almost the same offer, which is cadence fatigue.
- The "SALE SALE SALE" ticker is clipped at the right edge and adds nothing. The "$100+" in the badge is tiny and nearly illegible.
- Existing members get nothing, and the copy doesn't say what happens after signup (is the code automatic?).

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the deal mechanics: "Sign up free, get 25% off sitewide tonight." Say plainly that signup is free and that the discount applies to the cart right after. Resolve the 25% vs 30% conflict or explain it in one line.
- 2. Add a second path for existing members: a "Shop now" button, or "Already a member? Your 25% is live."
- 3. Replace the generic "Why join" block with 3–4 best-sellers (Cloud Paint, Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom) showing sale prices.
- 4. Cut the SALE ticker and use the space for the product grid or a countdown.
- 5. Segment: send non-members this version, and send members a "your early access ends tonight" shop email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 25% off, free to join`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Create a free account and the discount is yours before Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members save on everything tonight. Sign up in 30 seconds.`
