# Last chance for COLORfest deals ⏳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the COLORfest campaign's closing send, and it's the fourth "last day" flavor of urgency in two weeks — 8/23's "This Flash Sale Ends Soon," 8/27's "Don't Miss COLORfest," and now "Last chance for COLORfest deals." Unlike 8/29 and 8/28's niche-mechanic sends (app download, BOPIS-only), this one gets the split-attention problem right by stacking three distinct offers cleanly at the top: 15% off BOPIS orders ending 8/31, free 2-hour delivery on $35+, and a "Last Day" repeating banner — but that's also the flaw, three competing top-of-funnel offers instead of one clear hero.
- Below the fold it degenerates into a stack of secondary campaigns (Rewards $5-back, Klarna) that dilute the urgency message rather than reinforcing it. The hero "Final call for color savings" headline is strong and on-brand for a closing-week send, but the actual dollar/percent offer is buried in a thin top banner instead of being the visual centerpiece.
- Judgement: a middling COLORfest send — better than 8/28-8/29's niche mechanics because it leads with a real "last day" urgency frame, but it undercuts itself by not committing to one offer as the hero, unlike 8/23's flash sale which nailed a single clean ask.

## What's working

- Clear "LAST DAY" repeating ticker banner creates credible, campaign-appropriate urgency for the COLORfest closeout.
- Two concrete offers are visible above the fold: 15% off BOPIS (ends 8/31) and free 2-hour delivery on $35+.
- Hero image and copy ("Final call for color savings") are on-brand, seasonally accurate, and match the closing-week moment.
- Model demographics (young woman with vivid teal-dyed hair) directly match the hair color persona/category.
- Single clear "Shop deals" CTA button beneath the hero headline is unambiguous and well-placed.
- No render bugs — clean rendering, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete for attention in the first screen (15% BOPIS, free delivery, plus the generic "Last Day" ticker) — no single hero deal like 8/23's "20% off sitewide" send.
- The 15% off offer is BOPIS-only, the same narrow-audience gate that dragged down 8/28's "Pick Up & Save 15%" (5.00/10) — repeating a mechanic already flagged as too niche.
- The actual percentage/dollar offer is stuck in a thin top banner in small text, while the large visual hero space goes to a generic model shot with no offer callout on it.
- Below-fold content (Rewards $5 back in September, Klarna financing) has nothing to do with the "last chance" urgency and dilutes the closing message.
- The "Free 2 Hour Delivery" offer has no visible expiration tied to it, undercutting the urgency the rest of the email is built around.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and put it in the image itself. Overlay "15% Off Sitewide" or the single best deal directly on the hero photo the way 8/23's confetti-circle hero did — don't make readers hunt the top banner for the actual discount.
- 2. Drop or de-gate the BOPIS restriction for the last-chance push. If online-only 15% off isn't available, replace it with the campaign's strongest sitewide mechanic instead of reusing 8/28's already-underperforming niche offer.
- 3. Cut or move the Rewards and Klarna blocks below a hard divider so they don't compete with the closing urgency — or drop them from this send entirely and save them for a non-clearance email.
- 4. Add a hard deadline (date + time) to the free 2-hour delivery offer so it reinforces rather than undercuts the "last day" framing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: COLORfest deals end tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `15% off + free delivery — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hair color, care & nails — before COLORfest ends`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop now, save before midnight`
