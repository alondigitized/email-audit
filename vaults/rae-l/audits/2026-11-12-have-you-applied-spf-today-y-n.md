# Have you applied SPF today? (Y/N)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, educational SPF send. The "always on" hero over a close-up face, followed by "(yes, even in winter.)", is a smart seasonal reframe. It gives people a reason to buy sun protection in November. The Coverage / Benefits / SPF spec rows for Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 and Sun Serum SPF 50 are scannable and answer the real purchase questions.
- The email is unfocused, though. The top banner (free mini Eye Stylus with $75+), the SPF story, and a 30% off Multi-Stick block at the bottom all compete. The only real discount sits below two long SPF sections, so the strongest hook is the last thing a reader reaches. Three yellow CTAs in a row are also nearly identical.
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand 6. It teaches well, but it mixes an evergreen SPF message with a promo and never picks one lead. Compared with the 11/10 "One match. $2,000 of ILIA." send, which at least led with a single offer, this one buries its offer.

## What's working

- "(yes, even in winter.)" is a timely, honest answer to the obvious objection to buying SPF in November.
- The Coverage / Benefits / SPF rows give concrete, comparable product detail.
- Each section has its own CTA, and the yellow buttons stand out against the cream background.
- The 30% off badge on the Multi-Stick image is visible and clear.
- The footer carries trust cues: free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, VIP and Shade Match.

## What's weak

- The 30% off offer, the strongest incentive, sits at the very bottom after two long product sections.
- The top banner promotes a different offer (free mini Eye Stylus with $75+) than the body does.
- The hero is only the "always on" tagline. There is no offer and no product, and the first CTA, "Shop SPF," is generic.
- The Multi-Stick block has nothing to do with the SPF theme and reads as a bolted-on promo, with "See site for details" fine print below it.
- There are no reviews or social proof, and the two SPF products give no price or sizing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead. Either put the 30% off Multi-Stick up top with an SPF tie-in, or drop the promo and let the SPF story run alone. Moving the offer higher would lift the email the most.
- 2. Make the top banner and the body agree on a single offer.
- 3. Put the SPF 40 tint in the hero and give the first CTA a concrete verb and noun, such as "Shop Skin Tint SPF 40."
- 4. Add a star rating or review quote under each SPF product, plus a price.
- 5. Make the three yellow CTAs distinct so each names its product.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two mineral SPF picks, plus 30% off Multi-Stick for a limited time.`
