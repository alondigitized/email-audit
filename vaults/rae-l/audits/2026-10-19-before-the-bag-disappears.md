# Before the bag disappears
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send of the same VIP gift offer in four days, after 10-16 "The (2) gifts of VIP" and 10-17 "Friend, these gifts have your name on them". The creative is nearly identical: the same "The Gifts of VIP" hero, a velvet bag and mini mascara against blue sky and red blossoms. The new part is a "LAST CHANCE" tag and a subject that finally carries some urgency. The hero is attractive and the CTA is clear, but a subscriber has now seen this three times.
- The body contradicts itself on the offer threshold. The top bar and the bottom block say "any $75+ order", while the body copy says "any $100+ VIP order". A shopper can't tell what they need to spend, and that undercuts the honesty of the offer. The six-product grid adds little. The "shop now" buttons are weak outlined boxes, and nothing says which product earns the gift.
- My judgement: a competent reminder with a sensible urgency angle, held back by repetition, a conflicting threshold, and no real deadline. It is a 5 or 6.

## What's working

- The "LAST CHANCE" badge and the "Before the bag disappears" subject add urgency the earlier sends lacked.
- The hero is clean and on-brand, and the gift is visible immediately.
- The solid red "JOIN VIP + CLAIM GIFTS →" button stands out against the cream background and is repeated after the grid.
- The two-column product grid is tidy and scannable, and each product shows the product name.
- The closing "a free lift" block restates the mascara gift with its own button.

## What's weak

- The threshold conflicts: "$75+" in the top bar and the closing block, "$100+ VIP order" in the body copy.
- "Last chance" has no date or time, so the urgency isn't credible.
- It is the third near-identical gift email in four days, so it reads as cadence fatigue.
- Six "shop now" outlined buttons are visually weak and generic, and the grid dilutes the gift offer.
- No prices, reviews or social proof anywhere, and the asterisk fine print says only "See site for details."
- The "From: us. To: you." copy is cute but doesn't earn its space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the threshold so every mention says the same number, and state the VIP requirement plainly.
- 2. Add a real deadline in the top bar and hero, for example "Ends tonight at midnight PT".
- 3. Cut the grid to two or three products, or replace it with a "build your gift order" path with prices and ratings.
- 4. Change the CTA to specific copy such as "Claim my free mascara + bag" and give the product buttons a filled style.
- 5. Refresh the hero between sends, for instance with a shot of the bag next to the mascara, so it doesn't feel like a resend.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: free velvet bag + mini mascara`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight: your 2 VIP gifts expire`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get a mini Limitless Lash Mascara and a velvet makeup bag. Ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Join VIP free and the gifts are yours. Last day.`
