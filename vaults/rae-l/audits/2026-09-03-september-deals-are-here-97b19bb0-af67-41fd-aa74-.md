# September deals are here!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built seasonal reset email, and it's a step up from Sally's recent COLORfest closers. "Refresh your beauty routine for fall, for less" gives a clear seasonal hook, backed by a genuinely warm hero image (model, red lip, autumn-toned circle crop) instead of another confetti graphic. The offer stack below is dense but organized — BOGO 50% hair care, 30% off BaByliss tools, BOGO 50% jewelry, and a $15.99 color bundle — each with its own product shot, end date, and Shop Now link, so nothing gets buried the way 8/29's "Spend $35, Save 15%" did.
- The weakness is the subject line: "September deals are here!" is the same generic seasonal-opener pattern as 8/16-era sends and undersells the strongest, most concrete offer in the body (BOGO 50% off hair care). It reads like a placeholder rather than a hook. Overall this is a solid mid-tier send — good execution, forgettable subject.

## What's working

- Hero section has one clear headline, one clear subhead, and one clear CTA button ("Shop now") with no competing elements.
- Product imagery is real and specific (actual clippers, jewelry, hair color boxes) rather than stock/lifestyle filler.
- Each offer block pairs a bolded discount, a product photo, an end date ("Ends September 30"), and its own Shop now link — easy to scan.
- Persistent utility bar up top (BOPIS, free 2-hour delivery) and a footer icon row (In-Store/Online/Free Shipping/2 Hour Delivery/BOPIS) reinforce omnichannel options without cluttering the hero.
- Klarna financing callout and app download/social row round out the footer without feeling tacked on.

## What's weak

- Subject line doesn't name a single offer — no %, no BOGO, no dollar figure — despite four concrete offers being available to headline.
- Four different offers with four different end dates (Sept 30 x3, Sept 14 for the color bundle) creates split urgency instead of one clear deadline.
- The BOGO 50% and 30% off blocks alternate cream/maroon backgrounds abruptly, which reads a little busy stacked vertically versus one consistent card treatment.
- No loyalty/Rewards points messaging visible despite footer referencing "Sally Beauty Rewards points" in the fine print — a missed chance to surface member value in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with the strongest offer instead of generic "deals are here" — specificity drove better opens on 8/23's flash sale send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off hair care — this week only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall refresh: buy one, get one 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off BaByliss Pro tools and $15.99 color kits.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hair, tools, jewelry, color — all on sale through September.`
- 2. Pick one hero offer and end date for the top of the email; move the other three into a "more ways to save" secondary section so urgency doesn't fragment.
- 3. Add a small Rewards points callout ("Earn X points on this order") near the offer blocks since footer copy confirms a loyalty program exists.
- 4. Unify the card backgrounds (pick cream or maroon, not both alternating) for a cleaner scroll.
