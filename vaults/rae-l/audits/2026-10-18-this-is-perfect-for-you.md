# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment shell for the third time in about three weeks. The subject "This is perfect for you!" is a verbatim repeat of the 9/29 send. That one at least led with a floral Slip-ins hero and a real discount. This version opens with a kids' Skechers Garage sneaker, an orange and blue light-up style, under "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT?" and "YOU'VE GOT GREAT TASTE!". The six-shoe grid below is all women's styles, so the hero and the grid disagree about who this email is for.
- The offer is the same one from 10/17's "Don't leave these styles behind!": members take 20% off select full-priced styles through 10/21. Here it sits in a navy band below the grid, roughly 55% of the way down. A reader who stops at the hero sees a product and a button, and never sees the discount. Two sends in a row on 10/17 and 10/18 already pushed the same member offer, so this reads as inbox filler rather than a new reason to come back.
- Judgement: competent template, weak targeting. The "perfect for you" subject over-promises, and the hero contradicts it.

## What's working

- The member 20% off band is high-contrast and easy to read, with a clear "Valid thru 10/21" deadline.
- The "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" button is solid navy and visible, so the primary action is obvious.
- The Skechers wordmark and the clean white layout look trustworthy, with no render bugs, broken images or overlap.
- The footer fine print explains the 20% Skechers Plus offer (online only, exclusions, must be logged in) and the dates match the banner.

## What's weak

- The hero is a boys'/kids' Skechers Garage shoe, while all six grid items are women's styles. "Perfect for you" fails on the first image.
- The only real offer, 20% off, is buried below the product grid. The hero CTA, "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK", is generic and carries no price or deadline.
- The grid items have no prices, no ratings and no "Slip-ins" or comfort callouts, so nothing helps someone decide.
- "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" and "SHOP NOW" both go somewhere generic.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals, Sale) and the app, Klarna and texting blocks make the bottom half a long footer-style scroll that dilutes the offer.
- The subject repeats the 9/29 send word for word, and this is the third near-identical abandonment email in about three weeks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the member 20% off banner above the product grid, or into the hero as a headline ("Members: 20% off through 10/21"). Make the hero product match the category the recipient browsed.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific ("Shop Skechers Garage" or "Get 20% off your styles") and link it to that exact product.
- 3. Add price, a rating or review count, and one comfort or fit callout under each grid item.
- 4. Cut the secondary modules (texting, app, Klarna, curbside, social) to one row so the offer isn't diluted.
- 5. Don't send this alongside a same-week member-discount email. Suppress it for people who got the 10/17 send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% member discount ends 10/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? Members save 20%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The styles you looked at are still here. Members save 20% through 10/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ins and comfort picks, 20% off for Skechers Plus members. Ends Tuesday.`
