# A gift for you
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's Lip Sketch Sharpener "gift" email — the creative hook is a free or gifted Large Sharpener tied to the Lip Sketch product line. The hero opens with a tightly cropped lip swatch shot and a "stay sharp" headline, which is a clean, brand-native pun. Below the fold, the email structures itself as an editorial rundown of Lip Sketch's four core benefits (12-hour wear, bleeding fading resistance, hydration, sea succulent barrier) followed by three product shoppable tiles and a "custom crafted" closer on the sharpener accessory.
- The execution is polished and on-brand, but the payoff is buried. The "gift" promised in the subject line doesn't surface with any clarity until the very bottom of the email — no dollar value, no "add to cart / get your gift" button above the fold. The four benefit lines are compelling copy but they read like a product page, not a promotional email with a hook driving urgency. Given ILIA just sent two GWP closes (June 14–15) and a lip color send (June 18), this email risks feeling like a brand loop rather than a fresh reason to engage.
- The overall impression: well-shot, well-written, but the gift mechanic is structurally weak. A subscriber who opened expecting a gift will have to scroll past three product modules to find out what they're getting and how to claim it.

## What's working

- **"Stay sharp" headline is genuinely good** — on-brand wit, tight connection to the product, earns attention immediately.
- **Benefit stack is specific and credible** — "12-hour lipstick + liner," "resists bleeding + fading," "hydrates, moisturizes + plumps," and the sea succulent barrier detail are real product reasons, not vague claims.
- **Product tile photography is clean** — consistent warm-toned lip swatches across four shades read luxe and editorial without looking catalog-cheap.
- **"Custom crafted" sharpener close** — the Large Sharpener product section at the bottom gives the subject line's "gift" some payoff and adds a hero accessory angle that differentiates Lip Sketch from standard lip liners.

## What's weak

- **The gift offer is invisible above the fold** — the subject line says "a gift for you" but the email opens on a brand/product story with no visible gift mechanic, value, or CTA to claim it.
- **No urgency signal anywhere in the render** — no deadline, no "while supplies last," no expiry — the gift angle evaporates into a generic product feature email.
- **Three "Shop Now" CTAs competing with each other** — no primary CTA hierarchy; the eye doesn't know which product to click.
- **Cadence concern** — ILIA sent a gift-with-purchase close on June 15, another lip-color email June 18, and now a "gift" email on June 20. The GWP mechanic is starting to feel like every send, which erodes the value signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the gift offer in the first scroll** — Move the sharpener GWP mechanic (what you get, what triggers it, CTA button) to immediately below the hero. Subscribers who opened on "A gift for you" need the payoff within the first swipe or they bounce.
- 2. **Add a single primary CTA tied to the gift** — Replace the three competing "Shop Now" buttons with one anchoring button: "Get your free sharpener →" or "Claim your gift with $65+ →" with the threshold visible.
- 3. **Add urgency** — Even a soft deadline ("this weekend only" or "while supplies last") makes the send feel like an event worth acting on now rather than a brand story to revisit later.
- 4. **Collapse the benefit stack** — Four benefit lines is one too many for mobile scroll. Cut the weakest (sea succulent barrier is the most opaque to a casual reader) and let the other three breathe more.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free sharpener — with any Lip Sketch order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stay sharp. Your gift is waiting.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Claim your Large Lip Sketch Sharpener — today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `12-hour lip color + a gift. Here's how to get it.`
