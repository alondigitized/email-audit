# Love your curls all summer long
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Sally Beauty's soft product-marketing play — a curl-care "collection" send with no discount attached, a real step down in mechanic strength from the flash-sale and BOGO emails this brand has been running all month (the 30% off Overtone close on 7/18, the stacked $10/$20-off + BOGO send on 7/25). The creative itself is fine: a warm, seasonally-appropriate hero shot of a curly-haired model paired with clean "hydrated, healthy-looking curls" copy and an unambiguous "Shop now" button.
- Where it falls apart is the merchandising underneath. The "Or shop these trending favorites" grid — the section that should reinforce the curl-care promise — is entirely permanent hair color and toner (ion Color Brilliance, L'Oreal HiColor, ion 14V Blonde, Wella toner). None of it has anything to do with curls. That's a visible disconnect between hero and body, not a hunch about backend logic — the products are just wrong for the theme shown.
- The loyalty section compounds it: it says "Join now" rather than showing an existing member's reward balance, which reads as generic rather than targeted to a recipient this brand has been emailing for weeks. Net: a clean-looking but underpowered send. Compares closest to the 7/17 ion Luxe launch (5/10) and 7/22 L'Oreal spotlight (7/10) — no-discount product pushes that scored mid-pack for the same reason: nothing pulling the reader to act now.

## What's working

- Hero visual and copy are on-theme and seasonally coherent (curly model, "warm weather care," summer framing).
- "Shop now" CTA is a clear, high-contrast button that's easy to find.
- Render is clean — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the email.
- Standard trust/utility elements (Klarna financing, delivery/pickup icons, app download, social links, footer nav) are all present and legible.

## What's weak

- The "trending favorites" product grid is all hair color/toner — zero curl-care products — undercutting the hero's entire premise.
- No direct discount or time-bound offer tied to the curl-care push itself; the only incentive is a delayed Rewards mechanic (spend July 24–31, redeem Aug 10–31), which is a confusing two-window ask, not an instant reward.
- Loyalty banner says "Join now," which reads as generic acquisition copy rather than recognizing an existing subscriber/member.
- Subject and implied preview give no urgency, offer specifics, or personalization — it's pure mood copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the merchandising mismatch first — swap the product grid to actual curl-care SKUs (diffusers, curl creams, leave-ins, curl-defining gel) so the body delivers on the hero's promise. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Put a real, immediate incentive on the hero itself (e.g., "20% off curl care") instead of relying solely on the delayed $5 Rewards mechanic.
- 3. Personalize the loyalty block for known members — show a points balance or "You have a reward available" instead of defaulting to "Join now."
- 4. Simplify the Rewards mechanic copy or drop the split-window structure — a single active window reduces confusion and follow-through drop-off.
- 5. Tighten the subject line for specificity and urgency:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your curls, hydrated all summer — shop now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Frizz-free curls start here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New curl-care picks + a $5 Rewards bonus through July 31`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Warm-weather hair care for every curl type — shop the edit`
