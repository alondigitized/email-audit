# Stay with them long after you leave
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier deep in fragrance-editorial mode, and it's the fifth no-discount send in this stretch since 8/20's "Going back to school?" bundle — a run that's included the zodiac-themed "It's officially Virgo Season!" (8/22) and the personality-matched "Types of best friends" (8/27). Today's angle is the "signature scent" hook: five Glossier You variants, each paired with a first-impression quote ("That's the coolest person I've ever met," "I need their travel recommendations") tied to the scent's character. It's well-executed as editorial — clean hero collage, consistent portrait-plus-bottle format repeated five times, legible copy blocks — but it's the same fragrance-only, discount-free playbook as 8/27, just with a different narrative device swapped in.
- There's no price, no bundle discount, no urgency, and the offer is "buy a $34+ perfume" dressed up in lifestyle copy. For a subscriber conditioned by the Cheek Week arc's actual deals (8/17, 8/18 both scored 8/10 on urgency and clarity), this reads as pleasant scrolling, not a reason to click today.

## What's working

- Hero collage is a clean, on-brand visual (stacked bottles amid florals) that immediately signals "fragrance."
- Consistent repeating module (portrait photo + product callout + quote headline + "Shop now") makes the email easy to skim even though it's long.
- Diverse, well-lit model casting across the five sections — different skin tones, styles, settings (beach, studio, closeup).
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content anywhere in the scroll.
- Copy has a consistent, distinct voice per scent (playful, romantic, mysterious) that differentiates five very similar products.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, price, or urgency anywhere in the email — sixth email in ten days with no concrete offer.
- Primary CTA is "Shop now" repeated six times with no differentiation — none of the six links to anything but a generic shop destination implied.
- No social proof, ingredient/scent-note detail, or size/price info visible for any of the five fragrances — a real purchase decision blocker for scent, which is famously hard to sell without in-person testing.
- Hero subhead ("You've already got the charming smile. Now find a signature scent to match it.") is vague — it doesn't name a single product or benefit upfront.
- Very long single-column scroll (six full sections) with no way to jump to a specific scent — no anchor nav or "shop all 5" summary at top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a decision aid, not just mood copy — add a one-line scent-note key per fragrance (e.g. "Soie: sandalwood, iris, musk") so subscribers can actually choose, since blind scent shopping without ingredient info is the biggest click blocker here.
- 2. Give the CTAs distinct copy per section ("Shop Coolest Person," "Shop Soie") instead of six identical "Shop now" buttons — improves scan-ability and click intent signal.
- 3. Add a top-of-email mini nav or "5 new scents" grid so mobile users aren't forced through a six-screen scroll before finding their pick.
- 4. Reintroduce even a soft incentive (free travel-size with purchase, sample set, loyalty points) — five straight non-discount sends risks training subscribers to skip Glossier promos entirely.
- 5. Tighten the subject/hero pairing — "Stay with them long after you leave" is scent-appropriate but doesn't hint this is a 5-fragrance showcase, which undersells the scope.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Find your signature scent`
