# Take a closer look—select beauty is 50% off 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is another "Beauty Insider Celebration" cross-promo dressed up with a "50% off Select Beauty" hook — the subject line ("Take a closer look — select beauty is 50% off") promises a discount hero, but what actually loads first is a burgundy Beauty Insider $5-off-250-points banner, then a full "The Next Big Thing" new-arrivals module (Natasha Denona, Violette_FR, L'Occitane, L'Oréal Professionnel, Sephora Favorites, Iconic London, Touchland, Messy by Alli Webb, Skylar, Jung Saem Mool). The actual 50%-off Clinique/Origins/Fresh/Living Proof banner doesn't appear until roughly two-thirds of the way down. This is the same bait-and-switch structure flagged in the 2026-08-29 "Select brands are 50% off 🤑" send (5.00/10) — subject sells a discount, body sells a new-arrivals catalog.
- The creative execution itself is clean — no render bugs, good product photography, clear section dividers — but the offer the subject promises is buried under two full screens of unrelated new-launch merchandising before it appears "Today Only." For a recipient opening specifically because of "50% off," that's a long, frustrating scroll.
- Judgment: solid production values undercut by a subject/body mismatch this brand keeps repeating send after send. This lands in the same mediocre band as its recent siblings.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy anywhere in the scroll.
- The "50% off Select Beauty" banner, when it finally appears, is visually bold (red background, clear product shot, "TODAY ONLY" urgency tag).
- The "New Swipe Serum Foundation" before/after acne-treatment visual for Tower 28 is a genuinely differentiated, specific product story — a rare non-generic beat, echoing the strong hook called out in the 2026-08-23 send.
- Section labels (NEW, ONLY AT SEPHORA, CLEAN) give quick scannable context on each product tile.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "50% off select beauty" but the hero is a Beauty Insider points redemption banner, not the discount — the actual 50% banner is buried far down the scroll.
- The "Next Big Thing" new-arrivals block dominates the top of the email with 10+ product tiles before any offer-relevant content appears.
- No hierarchy signal tells the reader where to look first; the eye has to travel past two full modules to find the thing the subject line advertised.
- This is functionally the same structural complaint as the 2026-08-29 send ("Select brands are 50% off 🤑," 5.00/10) — the brand hasn't fixed the subject/hero mismatch across sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 50% off Select Beauty banner to the very top**, above the Beauty Insider points module and the new-arrivals catalog — if the subject sells the discount, the discount should be the hero, full stop.
- 2. Cut the "Next Big Thing" new-arrivals block down to 3-4 tiles max, or move it below the discount section entirely — right now it's the biggest single use of scroll real estate and it isn't what the subject promised.
- 3. Add specific brand/product names to the 50% banner itself (it currently just says "Select Beauty" — the copy below mentions Clinique, Origins, Fresh, Living Proof, but that's not visible until you scroll into the banner's sub-copy).
- 4. Keep the Tower 28 before/after module — it's the strongest asset in this send — but consider pulling it up higher since it's the only true product-specific hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off Clinique, Fresh & more — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off pick: Clinique, Fresh, Origins`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the skincare and makeup edit, half off through midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: see Tower 28's swipe-on serum foundation in action.`
