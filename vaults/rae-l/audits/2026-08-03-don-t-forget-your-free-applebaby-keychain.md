# Don't forget your free Applebaby Keychain
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a third reminder for the same GWP mechanic — the 7/21 "Mini Beauty Bag" launch, the 7/25 pivot to the "Applebaby Keychain" GWP, and now this "still on" nudge for that keychain offer. The hero is clean and does its job: red keychain on a chain-link fence, clear "STILL ON" eyebrow, explicit 20% off + free keychain with Balm Dotcom Quintet purchase, unambiguous "Shop now" button. It's a well-built, on-brand template — the photography is consistent, the layout is scannable, and the review block adds credible social proof.
- The problem is cadence, not execution. This is the third GWP-flavored send in under two weeks (Mini Beauty Bag → Applebaby Keychain intro → this reminder), stacked on top of the nine-day I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom arc underneath it. The bottom half — four flavor tiles (NY Apple, Strawberry, Black Cherry, Mango) — is the exact same "Summer Suggestions" grid Glossier has run repeatedly. - Solid single-send execution, weak as the Nth touch in an over-extended arc. Judgment: 6.5/10 — good craft, fatigued strategy.

## What's working

- "STILL ON" eyebrow instantly signals this is a reminder, not a new offer, avoiding confusion for anyone who saw 7/25.
- The offer is concrete and stacked clearly: 20% off + free keychain, both visible without scrolling.
- Two "Shop now" CTAs above the fold (hero + product image) plus one per flavor tile — no ambiguity about where to click.
- The 5-star Taylor review adds real social proof language, not generic filler.

## What's weak

- Third GWP-related email in roughly two weeks (7/21 Mini Beauty Bag, 7/25 Applebaby Keychain, now this) — a subscriber like Taylor from the review has likely already seen this offer twice.
- The four-tile "Summer Suggestions" grid is identical in structure and near-identical in product roster to the 7/27 "bag check" and 7/23 sends — no new creative angle.
- No countdown or explicit deadline — "still on" implies urgency but gives no date, undercutting the reminder's job of creating urgency to act now.
- Subject line "Don't forget your free Applebaby Keychain" is a near-exact rehash of 7/25's framing, offering no new information to someone who already opened that email.

- 1. **Add a hard deadline.** "Still on" with no date is the single biggest miss for a reminder email — swap in "Ends Sunday" or similar so it reads as genuinely time-bound rather than an evergreen nag.
- 2. **Retire or restructure the flavor grid.** These four tiles have appeared in at least two prior sends this arc; either drop them for reminder emails (let the hero + review carry the send) or rotate in different flavors/products to avoid visual fatigue.
- 3. **Suppress this send for openers/clickers of 7/25.** If someone already engaged with the original Applebaby Keychain email, this reminder adds little — segment it to non-openers only.
