# Smell rich with Glossier You Doux
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier running a full-price, no-discount fragrance launch push for Glossier You Doux — a sharp departure from the discount-driven Labor Day cadence that dominated 9/3–9/8, and closer in spirit to the single-SKU "Brow down" send from 9/13. The hero image (a warmly lit, richly pigmented model cradling the bottle) is genuinely striking and on-brand, and the note breakdown (top/middle/base) is a nice educational touch for a scent nobody's smelled yet.
- But the execution has a real problem: text is clipped at the right edge in at least two sections ("A soft, woody scent made f—" and the "fit for fall" body copy, plus the fragrance-notes table where "Sweet Violet," "Palo Santo," etc. run off-frame). That's not a stylistic choice, it's a rendering bug, and it undercuts an otherwise premium-looking email.
- Net: strong visual concept, no offer to anchor urgency, and a layout bug that actively breaks copy comprehension in the middle of the email. This lands mid-pack — better crafted than a generic promo blast, but held back by the clipping and the total absence of any incentive to act now.

## What's working

- Hero image is high-production and on-brand — warm, tactile, "rich" framing matches the "you smell rich" headline.
- Fragrance-notes table (top/middle/base) gives concrete product education, which is rare and useful for a scent-based product.
- Multiple CTAs ("Shop now," "Shop Doux") are repeated at logical intervals down the page.
- Clear size-based product grid at the bottom (100ml/50ml/8ml) gives a legible purchase path with price-point options.

## What's weak

- Body copy is visibly cut off at the right edge in the "Fit for fall" section and the notes table — a real layout/render bug, not stylistic.
- No offer of any kind — no %, no bundle, no free-shipping threshold — after a summer of discount-trained subscribers, this may read as a step down in value.
- Headline "You smell rich" is lowercase/muted in the hero and easy to miss against the dark image.
- No urgency or time-boundedness anywhere in the email — it's pure evergreen product marketing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the text-clipping bug in the "Fit for fall" and notes-table sections before this ships again — right now the copy is unreadable, which is a basic QA miss.
- 2. Add a concrete hook (intro offer, bundle price, or "new fall scent" urgency framing) since fragrance launches without an incentive tend to underperform Glossier's discount-trained list.
- 3. Make "you smell rich." pop more in the hero — it's currently low-contrast white-on-dark and easy to skim past.
- 4. Tighten preview text to reference the actual notes (violet, palo santo, frankincense) to differentiate from a generic "new product" email.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Meet You Doux — our warmest scent yet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweet violet, palo santo, and frankincense in one bottle.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The fragrance made for fall's finer things.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Standard Stripo/ESP-built HTML email with acceptable rendering fallbacks, but missing accessibility alt text and unverifiable authentication/unsubscribe headers at the relay layer.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
No issues found in the visible source. The preheader/tracking pixel uses klclick.com (Klaviyo) redirect infrastructure (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M2KDYYPRBQ2KRJDF634EJ661_0`), consistent with standard open/click tracking. QA reports 12 material links probed with no broken-link failures.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
Multiple images missing `alt` attributes, confirmed by QA:
- `heroimage_1_1.jpg`
- `group_3996_5.jpg`
- `image_13.png`
- `group_3973_1.jpg`
- `group_3972_1.jpg` and `group_3974_1.jpg` (appears twice)

Missing alt text on content images degrades screen-reader experience and leaves blank space if images fail to load. The tracking pixel itself missing alt is low-impact (should just be `alt=""` since it's non-content) but is flagged by QA as a blank-alt gap.

MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media` breakpoints are present and correctly structured — no rendering defects found in the truncated source.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge tags or personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) are visible in the truncated source. No issues found based on available evidence.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected (QA WARN) — noted as possibly an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm from HTML body alone since unsubscribe links are typically footer-rendered (truncated out of the provided source).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe bulk-sender compliance) also not detected.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be verified from this data; same relay-capture caveat applies.
These are WARN-level findings tied to header visibility through the relay, not confirmed sender-side failures — recommend re-verifying against raw headers at the mailbox/MTA level before treating as a compliance defect.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Cannot be assessed — the truncated HTML does not expose the actual destination `href` values for the 12 probed links (only the tracking wrapper domain `ctrk.klclick.com` is visible). No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are present in the provided source to evaluate.

7. **Recommendations**
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 6 content images flagged by QA; set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and any purely decorative images.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are actually present in raw SMTP headers at the source MTA — if confirmed present but only lost at the AgentMail relay, this is a relay-capture issue, not a sender compliance gap; if genuinely absent, add both for RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules).
- Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via raw `Authentication-Results` header at the receiving MTA, since it wasn't captured through this relay.
- Obtain full (non-truncated) HTML to audit actual link destinations and confirm UTM parameter consistency with the landing page.
