# Look who we ran into
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a founder-letter send, not a product push — Sasha Plavsic tells the story of running into Crown Affair founder Dianna Cohen in Ibiza and uses it to introduce the Crown Affair x ILIA "Out-the-Door Ritual" collab. It's a real departure from the brand's usual playbook: no hero product editorial, no discount, no obvious CTA button — just a photo grid of vacation snapshots and a single underlined text link buried in paragraph three.
- The narrative is warm and on-brand for founder-voice content, and the photo grid (candid shots, sunset, coastline, a peek of the product) does create some scroll-stopping curiosity. But as a marketing email it's underpowered: there's no price, no urgency, no button, and the actual products (Leave-In Conditioner, Face Milk, Limitless Lash Mascara, the hair towel) are only described in prose, never shown or linked individually.
- Compared to this week's cadence — "Over(glaze) tonight" (9/10) and "These go well with free gloss…" (8/10), both built around a clean hero shot + GWP mechanic — this send trades commercial clarity for brand storytelling. It's a fine trust-building piece in small doses, but on its own it under-delivers on click intent. Judgement: a nice-to-have brand touchpoint, not a strong sales email.

## What's working

- The 4-photo grid at top is a genuine scroll-stopper — candid, personal, and visually distinct from ILIA's usual polished lip/skin editorial.
- Founder signature (name, title, signature graphic) adds authenticity and differentiates this from a templated promo blast.
- Specific named products (Leave-In Conditioner, Face Milk, Limitless Lash Mascara, hair towel) give the collab concrete substance rather than vague "new collection" language.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, footer trust badges (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact) and ILIA VIP/Auto-Replenish icons all display correctly.

## What's weak

- No CTA button anywhere in the email — the only path to purchase is one small underlined text link ("Crown Affair x ILIA collection") halfway through a long paragraph.
- No offer, discount, or urgency of any kind — this is pure brand storytelling with zero shopping incentive.
- Subject line "Look who we ran into" gives no indication this is about a product launch or collab; it reads as personal-blog curiosity, not commerce.
- Products are described but never shown — no photo of the actual Leave-In Conditioner, Face Milk, mascara, or towel, which is a missed opportunity given how visual ILIA's other sends are.
- Generic "Hi there" greeting with no personalization or segmentation signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a clear CTA button ("Shop the Ritual" or "Shop the Collab") directly under the founder letter and again near the closing signoff — right now there's zero button in the whole email.
- 2. Insert a product shot of the actual trio (Leave-In Conditioner, Face Milk, Lash Mascara, blush-pink towel) — the closing pink card is just a logo/tagline slab and wastes prime visual real estate that should show the product.
- 3. Sharpen the subject line to signal the collab, keeping the curiosity hook:
- **Subject Alt A:** `My Ibiza run-in with Crown Affair's founder`
- **Subject Alt B:** `We ran into Crown Affair (and made something together)`
- 4. Add a preheader — none is rendering usefully right now, so inbox real estate is being wasted:
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Out-the-Door Ritual: our Face Milk + Mascara meet Crown Affair's Leave-In`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A poolside reunion inspired our first haircare collab`
- 5. Consider a light incentive line (even just "free shipping on the Ritual" or a GWP) to give the send commercial teeth without abandoning the story format.
