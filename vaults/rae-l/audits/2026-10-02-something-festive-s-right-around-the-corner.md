# Something festive’s right around the corner…
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser with nothing in it for the reader. A pink crumpled-paper image, a headline ("A little holiday magic + meltdown are right around the corner…"), one line of copy, and a "Sign up" button. There's no product, no offer and no date. "Meltdown" is a nod to Glossier's holiday Meltdown line, but the email never says so. A reader who doesn't already know that reference gets nothing from it.
- The design is clean and fits the brand. The hero image is on-brand, the type is simple, and the eye goes from headline to button without effort. But "Sign up" for what? The button doesn't say whether it's a waitlist, SMS, or early access. Glossier has just sent three near-identical gift-with-purchase emails in a week (9/22 "Try our fragrances for free!", 9/26 "Still on!", 9/28 "Last call!"). Going from a concrete free-gift offer to a vague "something's coming" feels like a step backward. Subscribers are already on the list, so asking them to sign up again is odd.
- Verdict: pretty, but empty. It's a brand-awareness beat, not a conversion email. At best it earns a few curious opens and almost no clicks.

## What's working

- The hero image is on-brand: soft pink paper with a red tape accent, and it reads as seasonal and giftable without a stock holiday cliché.
- Hierarchy is clean: headline, one supporting line, one button, no clutter.
- The copy is short and has a light brand voice ("when it all unwraps").
- There are no render bugs. Image, text and footer all load and align.
- The footer smiley and wave illustration keep the brand feel through to the bottom.

## What's weak

- No offer, product or date. Nothing tells the reader what is coming or when.
- "Sign up" is a vague CTA with no object, and nothing says what signing up gets you.
- "Meltdown" is an unexplained reference. It only works for people who already know the product line.
- The send asks existing subscribers to sign up, which is a muddled ask.
- After three gift-with-purchase emails in seven days, this is a weak follow-up with no payoff.
- The button is a low-contrast grey box that doesn't stand out against the white background.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the thing. Say "Meltdown is back" or show the actual holiday product, and give a date. Even a teaser needs one concrete hook.
- 2. Rewrite the CTA to say what the reader gets, such as "Get early access" or "Remind me on launch day". Make the button a high-contrast colour, such as Glossier red or black.
- 3. If this is aimed at existing subscribers, offer a real early-access perk, such as a first-look window or member-only pricing, instead of "sign up".
- 4. Show a product silhouette or a teaser shot in the hero, not abstract paper.
- 5. Space this out from the GWP run, or acknowledge it with something like "Thanks for the free-gift frenzy, here's what's next."
