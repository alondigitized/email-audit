# Fragrance, hair and nail savings inside 👀
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud Black Friday follow-up with a clear hero: "up to 40% off" in huge white type on black, a gold glitter fragrance image, and a white Shop now button. The eye lands on the offer first, and the stacked black-and-gold category tiles (fragrance, Mielle & Aussie, nail glue, scrunchies) are easy to scan. Nothing is broken in the render.
- The problem is message clutter. A red banner at the top says "20% off sitewide with code BLACKFRIDAY20, ends 11/29". That is the same offer as the 11/26 send, "Get 20% off sitewide right now!". The hero then says "up to 40% off", the rewards block says "$5 bonus reward", and the tiles say 20%, 30% and "Buy 6 & save". That is five different offers in one email. It also arrives the day after the 11/26 and 11/25 sends, so it reads as the fifth near-identical promo this week.
- Judgement: well built and on-season, but it is a fatigue risk. The "up to 40%" headline is vague, and the specific deals are 20–30% off.

## What's working

- The hero is bold, with "up to 40% off" dominating the top and a single white Shop now button.
- The sticky red banner carries a code and a deadline (BLACKFRIDAY20, ends 11/29), which gives credible urgency.
- The tile layout alternates image and copy, so each product category has its own specific offer.
- The Beauty Secrets Nail Glue tile shows real pricing ($29.99 online, $29.94 in-store). It is the only tile with a concrete price.
- The Sally Rewards block (a $5 bonus reward on a $50 gift card, ends 11/30) gives members a reason to act.
- The footer is helpful: the Klarna line, the four service icons (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping Orders $40+, 2 Hour Delivery) and the app download link.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers: sitewide 20%, up to 40%, 20% fragrances, 30% Mielle & Aussie, Buy 6 & save, 20% Beauty Secrets and a $5 reward. Nothing is the single "one thing."
- The "up to 40%" headline implies more than the tiles deliver, since no tile shows 40%.
- It is a repeat of 11/26's 20% sitewide message, only a day later, so the cadence is heavy.
- Every button says only "Shop now", with no product or category named.
- The rewards block is in-store only and its fine print is dense, so it undercuts the online hero. The footer terms are long and buried.
- There are no reviews or social proof anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and drop the rest to secondary. Either lead with "20% off sitewide, ends 11/29" (the code is already in the banner) or name what is actually 40% off.
- 2. Cut the tile count from four to two or three. Keep fragrance, which is the strongest image, and one hair tile.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop fragrance deals" and "Shop curl care."
- 4. Move the rewards block below the tiles, or send it only to members.
- 5. Add a social proof line, such as a star rating on the nail glue or a "best seller" tag.
- 6. Space the sends out. Day two of the same promo should either escalate ("Last day") or be skipped.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is truncated, so unsubscribe links, the footer address and UTM parameters could not be checked. The confirmed issues are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a third-party pixel, an `http://` tracking pixel, and missing alt text on tracking pixels. The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and don't confirm a defect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **52 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Four third-party tracking pixels are embedded:**
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2338739/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`
  - `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?...`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...`
  - Two image tags with empty `src` and alt (see section 3).
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` loads over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed content, or that proxy images over HTTPS (such as Gmail's image proxy), may drop the pixel, so the tracking silently fails.
- **Subscriber ID in a URL path:** The mon-trk URL ends in `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, which looks like a Salesforce contact ID. It is exposed in the pixel path.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on 5 `<img>` elements:
  - Two have an empty `src` and alt. The QA output shows `: src:` for both, which suggests empty or malformed image tags.
  - The mon-trk, myvisualiq and dotomi pixels also have no alt. These are 1x1 tracking pixels and should carry `alt=""`.
- **Possible empty-`src` images:** if the two empty-`src` images really have an empty `src`, some clients request the page URL as the image, or show a broken-image icon. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm this.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. Helvetica is the fallback, so this is low severity.
- The source declares an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, viewport meta, VML/Office namespaces and MSO conditionals. No issues were found there in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved macro:** the myvisualiq pixel contains the literal `r=[CACHEBUSTER]` in `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...`. It was not substituted at send time. The result is that the request is cacheable, so impressions can be undercounted, and the unencoded brackets are not valid URL characters.
- No other unresolved merge tokens (`%%`, `{{ }}`, `${ }`) were visible in the truncated source.

The sender is on `em.sallybeauty.com`, so verify them in the raw message from a direct-delivery inbox. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverifiable from this capture. Check DNS for `em.sallybeauty.com` or use a seed inbox. This is not a confirmed failure.
- The visible source doesn't show the physical address or the unsubscribe link, because the HTML is truncated. No violation is claimed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
