# Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a membership-acquisition email, not a sale email. The subject says "Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members." The hero says "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" while a badge in the corner says "30% OFF $100+." Both CTAs, "Create account" and "Join today," ask me to sign up. I'm already on the list, so I'm unsure whether this is aimed at me or at non-members.
- The creative is on-brand, with electric blue, big type and a clean layout. The offer is muddled, though. Two discount numbers compete for the same spot. The asterisks on "EVERYTHING*" and "member today.*" point to fine print I can't read in the body, and the only terms link is in the footer. The email never tells me which Wednesday or when the sale ends.
- After the 11/11 Discovery Kit send and the three-email balm run (11/12, 11/15, 11/17), this is a welcome change of subject. It still gives me the least concrete incentive of the lot. Verdict: a decent-looking teaser that undersells its own offer.

## What's working

- The blue hero with a big "25% OFF" is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The brand look is consistent: Glossier wordmark, blue buttons, minimal sans-serif type.
- The "Why join?" list (early access, free gifts, event invites) is scannable in three short lines.
- The model photo with the pink bottle adds warmth and breaks up the blue.
- Both CTAs are full-width-contrast blue buttons and are easy to find.

## What's weak

- The hero shows two discounts at once, 25% off and a "30% OFF $100+" badge. It's unclear which one I'd get.
- "EVERYTHING*" and "member today.*" both carry asterisks, but the terms are only a tiny italic line at the very bottom.
- Neither the subject nor the body gives a date or time for when the sale starts or ends. "Wednesday" is the only timing cue and it's missing from the body.
- Both CTAs say "Create account" or "Join today." That's generic, and it doesn't fit existing members.
- The "SALE" ticker strip is cut off at the right edge, and the footer smiley and hand graphics are cropped. The ticker is decoration and takes up space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline offer. Make it "25% off everything" or "30% off $100+," and put the threshold in plain text beside it.
- 2. Put the start date and time in the hero, for example "Wednesday 11/19, 12am ET," and add a countdown or deadline line.
- 3. Segment the send. Members should get a "Your early access starts Wednesday" version with a "Shop early access" button. Non-members should get the sign-up version.
- 4. Move the key terms (exclusions, code, dates) up under the hero instead of leaving them in the footer.
- 5. Rewrite the CTA copy to be specific, such as "Join free, shop Wednesday."
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything starts Wednesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early Black Friday access: Wednesday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join free today and shop the biggest sale of the year before everyone else.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `25% off everything, plus 30% off orders $100+. Members go first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built XHTML template with a Klaviyo-injected tracking wrapper. The automated QA found no hard failures, only 3 warnings. Most compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML source was truncated before any links or the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All visible tracking goes through Klaviyo's click domain, `ctrk.klclick.com`. The ID `01M7P27Z15ZB379CVZN7HR5KC2` appears in the `/l/…_0` link and the `/o/…` open pixel.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 2. The skipped links aren't identified, and the source shows no broken ones.
- **Stray tracking element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7P27Z15ZB379CVZN7HR5KC2_0"></span>` is the first element in `<body>`. `rel` and `href` are invalid attributes on a `<span>`. It looks like a Klaviyo injection artifact. It is harmless but not valid markup.
- No hrefs beyond this are visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify the CTA destinations.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `frame_4042.jpg` (`qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_877f…`)
  - `group_3986_5.jpg` (same CDN path)
  - The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7P27Z15ZB379CVZN7HR5KC2`. It should carry `alt=""`.
  - The two content images should have descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. Without it, screen readers may announce the filenames.
- **Conditional-comment structure:** `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opens in `<head>`, but its closing `<!--<![endif]-->` only comes after `<body>` and the tracking span. The `<style>` blocks, `</head>` and `<body>` therefore sit inside the non-MSO block. Outlook desktop (Word engine) won't see them, so it won't get the responsive CSS or the body tag. Content still renders there, but the structure is fragile. This is low severity and only partly confirmed, because the source is truncated.
- **Title mismatch:** `<title>` is "Unlock early access to Black Friday!", while the subject is "Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members". This matters little, but it is inconsistent where a client shows the title.
- `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, the viewport meta tag and a hidden preheader are all present.
