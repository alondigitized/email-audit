- The free Sephora x Parachute gift with a $100 spend is a concrete second hook.

## What's weak

- The subject says "most-searched," but the body is new exclusives, so the two don't match.
- The offer and hero are in conflict. The 10% banner sits on top, then a large fragrance hero takes over, and the sale gets forgotten.

- The Insider 10% applies to a lot of products, but many tiles are limited-edition sets priced $90–$349, which undercuts "10% off" as a reason to act.

- 2. Cut the product grid to 4 tiles and drop the redundant modules. A shorter email will make the 10% offer clearer.
- 3. Put "10% off" on the hero or product tiles. Show sale prices instead of only list prices.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop top-rated foundation," not just "Shop Now."
- 5. Vary the cadence. Don't send the fifth identical Savings Event banner in a week.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with Epsilon-hosted images and `app.sephora.com` redirect tracking. The QA data shows one hard failure (no plain-text part) and a few accessibility and markup defects. The compliance and authentication warnings look like relay capture gaps rather than confirmed sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links found.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 40, and reported no probe failures. The skipped links, mostly footer and social, are unverified.
- **Opaque redirect tracking.** Links are wrapped as `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1ac312d6d9870caf4bbcfbb48/...?__dU__=...`, so the final destination can't be read from the source. The truncated HTML shows no resolved landing URLs.
- **Open-tracking pixel is hidden correctly.** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...' style="display:none; max-height:0px; font-size:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all"/>` has no `width`/`height`/`alt`. QA flags the missing `alt`. For a hidden pixel this is low impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] No plain-text alternative.** QA reports the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability scoring and text-only clients. It may be a relay artifact if AgentMail only captured the HTML part, so check the raw MIME.
- **Missing `alt` on content images.** `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_14.jpg` and `..._15.jpg` (images.harmony.epsilon.com) have no alt text. If these carry offer copy, it disappears when images are blocked and screen readers get nothing. This is the one real accessibility defect confirmed.
- **Fixed-width wrapper.** `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">` has no override in the visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block, which only resizes logo, icon and font classes. This probably forces horizontal scroll or scaling on mobile. I only saw the truncated source, so confirm with a render test.
- **Malformed CSS.** A `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` conditional sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Browsers will drop that rule, and Outlook won't process the conditional there. The `mso-size` override is therefore ineffective.
- **Invalid meta value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email.
- **Questionable JSON-LD.** `"discountCode": ""` is empty and `PromotionCard` is not a recognized schema.org type. Gmail will likely ignore the block, so there's no benefit and a small validation risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers unverified, not confirmed missing.** QA found no `List-Unsubscribe`, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and no `Authentication-Results`. QA itself attributes these to the AgentMail relay not capturing them. Check the raw message headers for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `beauty.sephora.com` and for one-click unsubscribe support, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical address and in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing but gives no result, so that item is not confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data. The redirect wrappers hide the destination query strings, so UTM presence and landing-page match can't be confirmed. The preheader says "Save on EVERYTHING through 11/9" and the subject says "10% off", and the QA data doesn't show whether the landing pages carry the same offer or end date.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text MIME part. First check the raw message to confirm it's actually missing.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `..._Stitched_14.jpg` and `..._15.jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Inspect the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Treat the QA warnings as unresolved until you do.
4. Follow the redirect chains for the primary CTAs and record the final URLs, UTM parameters and offer text.
5. Add a mobile override for the `min-width:700px` wrapper (a fluid `max-width` pattern) and move the MSO rules out of the `@media` block.
6. Clean up the `robots` meta value and the empty `DiscountOffer` JSON-LD.
