## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's clean break from the nine-day-plus I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom arc that's dominated recent sends (7/20 through 7/27). Instead of another flavor riff, "Meet the popular girls" reframes four bestsellers — Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom, Glossier You Travel Spray, Pink Mini Beauty Bag — as a "starter kit" for new customers, with a bag-flatlay hero and one dedicated tile per product. It's a smart cadence reset after over a week of the same Balm Dotcom well.
- The execution is mostly on-brand and legible: minimalist type, one clear product per section, repeated "Shop + save" CTAs. But there's a visible copy-truncation bug in the Boy Brow section ("It visi—" cut off mid-word), and the headline offer — "Shop and save" — never states an actual number, which softens what could've been a real hook for new-to-brand shoppers.
- Net: a well-timed pivot away from campaign fatigue, competently laid out, dragged down by a render glitch and a vague, numberless "save" claim. Solidly average — better than the flavor-catalog sends, weaker than the GWP mechanic.

## What's working

- Genuine format break from the Balm Dotcom flavor arc — bag flatlay + "starter kit" framing feels new after a week-plus of repetitive creative.
- Clear one-product-per-section structure (Icon 01–04) with a named product, a benefit line, and a CTA each — easy to scan.
- The bag flatlay hero (mascara, Balm Dotcom, travel spray peeking out of a bag) doubles as a mini "what's in her bag" callback without repeating the 7/27 bag-check email outright.
- "EVEN MORE SETS" footer cross-sells The Face Kit and Fragrance Two Ways without cluttering the main narrative.

## What's weak

- Visible text-truncation bug in the Boy Brow section — copy cuts off mid-word ("It visi—"), a clear render defect a subscriber would notice.
- Headline offer "Shop and save" never names an actual discount, percentage, or bundle price — it reads like an offer but isn't one.
- Subject line "Meet the popular girls" doesn't hint at the product-bundle mechanic at all; a reader has to open cold to learn this is a bestseller starter kit.
- No urgency, no loyalty/member pricing, no social proof anywhere in the send — nothing pushes a browser toward converting today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the Boy Brow copy truncation first — it's the single most visible defect in the render and undercuts trust in an otherwise clean layout.
- 2. Put a real number in the hero: if "save" means a bundle discount, state it ("Save 15% when you build your set") — vague "save" language reads as filler, not an offer.
- 3. Rewrite the subject to signal the bundle mechanic instead of relying on a pun that requires opening to decode.
- 4. Add a light urgency or scarcity cue (e.g., "while the set lasts") since nothing currently gives a reason to act now vs. later.
