# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment email again, and the layout is clean. A big blue and navy "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" headline sits over "Go on, take another look." and a solid SHOP NOW button. Below that is a 3x2 grid of women's running and walking sneakers, then a navy Black Friday Countdown banner: "BUY ONE, GET ONE 50% OFF or 20% OFF A SINGLE PAIR." Nothing is broken, but nothing is new either.
- The subject "Don't leave these styles behind!" is a recycled line. I audited it on 11/7 at 7/10, and the 11/7 "This style has limited availability!" and today's earlier "Don't leave a good thing behind." are the same template. This is at least the ninth abandonment-style send in about ten days, and the second one today. The strongest asset, the BOGO 50% offer, is buried under the product grid. The hero sells "we saved these for you" and not the deal. After this many sends, the saved-for-you angle has stopped being a reason to act.
- My judgement: a competent template that fatigues the reader. It converts a warm browser, but the cadence and the buried offer cap it at mid-pack.

## What's working

- The headline is large and two-tone, and the SHOP NOW button is high contrast, so the primary action is obvious.
- The BOGO 50% banner is a real, concrete offer, and the "discount auto-applied at cart" note removes a coupon-code hurdle.
- The six product tiles are clean and consistently sized, and each has a text link with the style name.
- The Black Friday Countdown label fits the season.
- The sender and branding are instantly recognizable, and the layout renders without broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The offer sits below the product grid, so a mobile reader may never see BOGO 50% before deciding to scroll on.
- The product tiles show no prices, sale prices or ratings, so nothing supports the "save" promise or social proof.
- "SHOP NOW" appears twice and is generic. Nothing says which pair or which deal.
- The deadline (11/12 to 11/17) appears only in the tiny fine print. The "countdown" has no visible clock or end date.
- The bottom third is a long stack of nav bars, app badges, BNPL logos and social icons, which dilutes the message.
- The exclusions (sale styles, online only) are in tiny type, and "BOGO or 20% off" leaves the reader unsure which deal applies.
- The subject repeats the 11/7 "Don't leave these styles behind!" line, and today's earlier send used a near-identical one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the BOGO 50% banner into the hero position, above the product grid, and put the end date in it: "Ends 11/17."
- 2. Cut abandonment sends to one per browse session and put a suppression window on repeat sends. Right now the brand is training the subscriber to ignore it.
- 3. Add prices or "BOGO eligible" tags to each tile so the grid supports the offer.
- 4. Change the button copy to "Shop BOGO 50% Off" or "Get My Saved Styles."
- 5. Trim the footer modules to two or three.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your saved sneakers: BOGO 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? 2nd pair is half off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one, get one 50% off through 11/17, applied automatically at cart.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Max Cushioning styles you viewed are in the Black Friday countdown.`

# Technical Audit: "Don't leave these styles behind!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is well-formed, with `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables and an MSO fallback block. Delivery-side checks are weak, though. There is no plain-text part, 10 images load over `http://`, and both the unsubscribe and SMS-signup links returned HTTP 400 when probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r…`. A 400 on a token-bearing Attentive link may come from the probe (missing UA or method, or a single-use token). It is not confirmed as a real user-facing failure. Verify it manually, because a broken unsubscribe is a compliance risk.
- **"LET'S GET TEXTING" returned 400.** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/pUK/joinsms/?utm_content=footer&utm_term=text-signup…`. The same probe caveat applies.
- **Tracking domains differ from the sender domain.** Links go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com`, `skechers.attn.tv` and `skechers.attentivemail.com`, while the From address is `hello@msgs.skechers.com`. This is standard for Attentive, but it means click tracking is not first-party branded.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 22 skipped, so the status of the skipped links is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **10 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...`.** Non-HTTPS sources can be blocked or rewritten with mixed-content warnings. The affected images are:
  - `dde00662-…png`
  - `49468f73-…png`
  - the App Store and Google Play badges
  - Curbside Pickup
  - Afterpay
  - Find a Store
  - the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons
- **Missing alt text on `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`.** If it is a content image, it renders as blank or broken when images are off.
- **Missing alt on the open-tracking pixel** (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`). This is low severity.
- **Empty `<title></title>` in `<head>`.** This is confirmed in the source.
- **Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The QA check reports it as a FAIL. This affects text-only clients and spam scoring, and some filters penalize HTML-only mail.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. Links carry `externalId=ZvZRl`, which is resolved. The source is truncated, so the later body sections were not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so the audit cannot confirm or deny authentication.
- **The unsubscribe link failed its probe** (see section 2).
- **The physical postal address is not visible** in the truncated source, so it cannot be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTMs are visible only on the footer links.** They are `utm_source=attentive`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=TRG_US_EN_ABANDONPLP_1_10122026`, and `utm_content=footer`.
- **Final destinations and their UTMs are unverified.** Most CTAs are wrapped in `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` redirects, and the truncated source and probe limits hide where they land.
- **Trigger-style campaign tag.** The `ABANDONPLP` tag and the `10122026` suffix look like a creation or version date from about a month before this send. This is informational and not necessarily a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually test the unsubscribe URL and the SMS-signup URL in a real browser, and fix them if they fail. The unsubscribe link is the priority.
2. Add a multipart text/plain alternative. The current one is 0 chars.
3. Serve every image from `https://image.emails.skechers.com/...`, or move to an HTTPS CDN. This covers all 10 flagged assets.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to `dde00662-…png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Populate `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against raw headers from a direct inbox, not the AgentMail relay. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `msgs.skechers.com`.
7. Follow the primary CTA redirects to their final URLs, and check that the UTMs persist and the landing pages match the abandoned-PLP intent.
