- Compared to the brand's recent run, this beats the flat, unstyled "2 days. 2 free minis." send (2026-07-25, 5.00/10) on production value, but it doesn't carry the punchy hook of "It's Over(glaze) tonight" (2026-07-26, 9.00/10), which paired a strong visual with a GWP incentive. ## What's working

- Hero pencil fan + "thru line" wordmark communicates "new launch" instantly, no scroll needed.
- The three-step technique tutorial (smudge+soften, line+define, wing it) builds usage confidence before the ask.
- Full 10-shade swatch chart with names and stroke samples gives real product detail pre-click.
- Logical cross-sell sequencing — eyeliner → smudge brush → sharpener — reads as a kit, not a bolt-on upsell.
- Consistent blue pill CTAs at every section make the email easy to scan and act on.

## What's weak

- No discount, bundle price, or GWP anywhere — this is discovery-only, no urgency to buy today.
- It's a very long single-column scroll; the shade chart (the actual decision-making content) sits deep past the hero, model shot, and full tutorial.
- Zero social proof — no star rating or review count for a brand-new product launch, where trust-building matters most.
- The teaser built anticipation for a week; nothing in this send acknowledges that build-up (e.g. "the wait is over").

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a launch incentive (limited first-run gift, free travel case for early orders) — this is the single biggest gap versus ILIA's own higher-scoring sends, which almost always pair strong visuals with a mechanic.
- 2. Pull the shade chart up directly under the hero/CTA — don't make shoppers scroll through the full tutorial to see if their shade exists.
- 3. Add a star rating or "as seen in" credibility line near the hero or shade chart for a first-time product.
- 4. Tighten the technique tutorial to one condensed image with a "see full tutorial" link to shorten the scroll.
