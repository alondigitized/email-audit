# It’s back (with NEW shades)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-structured product-launch send. A tight close-up hero carries a serif "It's Back" headline. One product, the Ethereal Baked Face Palette, runs through three named colorways (Botanical Blur, Coastal Bloom, Poppy Fields), each with a model photo and a four-shade list. It's easy to scan, and it's more polished than the plain-text collapses in ILIA's 09-14 to 09-23 streak.
- The weak spot is hierarchy between the two offers. The palette is the story, but the real incentive, a free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with a $100+ order, sits in a thin banner at the very top and again in a second block at the bottom. The mascara block carries a different CTA ("Shop Now") and a "See site for details" footnote. Two products and two CTAs dilute a very focused first half.
- Verdict: a solid 7. It's good creative for a returning fan, but it has no price incentive and no hard deadline. "Not for long" is the only urgency, and it never says when.

## What's working

- The hero is confident and on-brand. A close-up face with the palette and a serif "It's Back" headline sells the return in one glance.
- The shade collections are well organized. Each has a name, a model photo, and a four-line list with finish descriptors (highlighter, bronzer, blush), so you can see what's in each palette without clicking.
- The "Shop the Palette" CTA is a clear red button, placed once under the hero and again after the shade grid.
- The trust strip ("Safe for sensitive skin · Non-comedogenic · Formulated without talc") answers common objections before the shade grid.
- The footer reassurance (free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, Pact recycling, VIP) is tidy, and nothing renders broken.

## What's weak

- The gift-with-purchase offer is buried in a small top banner. The "$100+" threshold is also high for someone who came for one palette.
- The mascara section at the bottom pulls attention to a different product, with a "Shop Now" CTA and a "See site for details" footnote that makes the offer feel conditional.
- There's no deadline, and no scarcity beyond "(But not for long.)". Limited-edition shades with no end date or stock signal is a missed urgency lever.
- No price or review proof appears anywhere. The email never says what the palette costs, and a "sold-out last year" claim is the only social proof.
- The large "ILIA" wordmark block near the bottom is a heavy, nearly empty tan banner that adds length without adding information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift offer into the hero, either as a badge on the image or one line directly under the CTA ("Free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with $100+"). It's currently the least visible thing in the email.
- 2. Add a hard deadline or stock cue to the limited-edition claim, e.g. "Limited edition. While supplies last" or an end date for the gift.
- 3. Put the price and a review snippet or star rating next to the first CTA. The palette is the product, so it should show a price.
- 4. Cut the second block's CTA to match the first ("Shop the Palette"), or remove the mascara block and leave the gift in the header banner. One product and one CTA would make the email stronger.
- 5. Make the shade names tappable, with a link per colorway to its product page rather than one generic CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sold out last year. Back with 3 new palettes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Baked Face Palette returns (limited)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three new limited-edition shade collections, plus a free mini mascara with $100+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Botanical Blur, Coastal Bloom, Poppy Fields. Grab yours before they sell out again.`

## Technical Audit

The QA run could not verify links or authentication because probing was skipped and the relay dropped headers. The only confirmed code-level defects are missing `alt` text on one content image, a missing `lang` attribute, and an empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link status unverified.** QA reports "12 material link(s) probed, 12 skipped", so no HTTP status, redirect chain or destination was checked. Treat link health as unknown, not as passing.
- **Click and open tracking.** The tracking pixel is served from `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M3Z9KWKZEJJYWW7BXZHHEV62`, a first-party branded tracking domain. That is fine, but the pixel could not be validated because probing was skipped.
- The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm the CTA hrefs or whether any are empty, `#`, or malformed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image (real issue).** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` has no `alt`. If it is meaningful, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. If it is decorative, it should have `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel (low severity).** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M3Z9KWKZEJJYWW7BXZHHEV62` has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and a 1x1 size so it is treated as decorative.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Empty `<title>`.** It is `<title> </title>`, which is a minor accessibility issue in web-view and browser contexts.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap)` are ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The fallback is `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif`, so degradation is acceptable. This is informational only.
- The preheader is correctly hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0; overflow:hidden`) and padded with `͏` characters to block body-text bleed-through.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}` or `*|FNAME|*`. The truncated HTML means the rest of the body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA says the headers "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe. Verify against the raw message from the sending ESP, which is likely Klaviyo given the `SguDsz` company ID.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is flagged as an expected relay artifact, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer.** The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be verified here. QA states an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set but was skipped, so its status is unknown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. Link probing was skipped for all 12 links and the HTML was truncated, so there is no evidence for or against UTM presence, naming consistency, or landing-page alignment. Don't read this as a pass.

1. Add a descriptive `alt` (or `alt=""` if decorative) to `c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
