# Lush × Sanrio Characters
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Lush's second send of the Sanrio collab campaign — same subject line as June 18, twelve days later. Whether it's a re-send to non-openers or a fresh push to the full list, any subscriber who opened the first round will experience a déjà vu that undermines urgency. The creative itself holds up: "TO THE BATHROOM!" as the hero pun lands cleanly against the Sanrio characters-in-a-bubble visual, and the "Choose Your Character!" grid organizes six distinct product lines in a way that gives the email genuine browsability. The Club section at the bottom is welcome as a closer.
- The core weakness isn't creative — it's commercial. There's no offer: no discount, no limited-time bundle, no exclusive-to-members pricing, no "get it before it's gone" signal. For a collab that first launched June 18, by June 30 the natural subscriber instinct is "if I haven't bought yet, why now?" The email doesn't answer that.
- Verdict: polished brand execution, no commercial hook. It will drive casual browsers but leave most subscribers with no reason to click today over tomorrow.

## What's working

- **"TO THE BATHROOM!" hero copy** — delivers the Lush-signature pun cleanly and in-brand; the Sanrio characters floating in product bubbles above it reinforce the playfulness.
- **"Choose Your Character!" grid** — six polaroid-style character cards give the email real structure and let a subscriber self-select into their character, which is smart for a collab with distinct fans.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout collapse, no overlapping text; the email is visually tight top to bottom.
- **Club section** — loyalty section at the bottom provides a reason for members to feel the collab has an extra layer for them.
- **Short, brand-clear subject** — 24 characters, zero spam signals, both brand names front-and-center.

## What's weak

- **Exact repeat subject line** — "Lush × Sanrio Characters" appeared June 18; using it verbatim on June 30 signals either a bulk re-send or creative fatigue. A subscriber who opened the first one gets zero new information from the inbox.
- **No offer or urgency** — seven emails in twelve days from this brand, and still no "X% off," no free gift, no member-exclusive, no deadline. For a collab product that's been live nearly two weeks, there's no conversion trigger.
- **"Explore Now" CTA is generic** — verb + adverb rather than verb + noun (e.g., "Shop Pompompurin"). It doesn't commit to anything.
- **No product-level detail visible** — the character grid shows product photography but no prices, sizes, or ingredients — exactly the detail that would tip a near-purchaser.
- **Cadence fatigue risk** — this send lands as email #7 in 12 days from Lush. Even subscribers who love the brand are likely filtering aggressively by now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give this send a reason to exist** — add a 48-hour offer tied to the collab. "Last chance before the collab ships out" or "Free Sanrio tote with $60+ collab purchase" would answer the "why click today?" question. Without it, this email is an editorial catalog, not a conversion email.
- 2. **Change the subject line** — anything that signals this isn't a dupe of June 18. Even adding a character name or a "still available" frame helps.
- 3. **Upgrade the CTA copy** — swap "Explore Now" for something character-specific like "Meet Your Match" or "Find Your Character" to match the "Choose Your Character!" logic.
- 4. **Surface one hero product** — pick one character's product, give it a close-up, show the price. The grid is good for discovery; a hero SKU drives the click.
- 5. **Add scarcity signal** — even a subtle "limited collab" label on the character cards would add purchase intent.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Pompompurin, Badtz-Maru & friends — pick your Lush`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Sanrio collab is in stock — but not forever.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `6 characters. 1 bathroom. Which one's yours?`
