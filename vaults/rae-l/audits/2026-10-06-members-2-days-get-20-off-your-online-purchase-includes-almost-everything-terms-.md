# Members, 2 DAYS! Get 20% off your online purchase⁺. Includes almost everything! Terms & excl. apply 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, loud, well-built member promo. A big orange "20% off your online purchase" hero with code SAVE20 sits right at the top, and a "TWO DAYS | MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" tag gives it a credible deadline. The eye lands on the offer first, which is what a promo email needs.
- It then loses focus. Below the hero are two gift modules (100+ free gifts, and a free 12-piece gift with an $80 purchase). Then comes a "GO ALL IN" Fall Deal Days banner and four "up to 30% off" in-app tiles (hair, skin, makeup, nails). That is five or six competing offers stacked on a long scroll. For a shopper with no stated category, nothing is wrong, but nothing is specifically for them either. The "Rae," greeting is only in the header strip, which is a small touch of personalization.
- Compared with the Aug 1 "ENDS TODAY" SCORE send, this one is cleaner and the offer is simpler. It is still the same high-cadence Ulta pattern of a coupon plus a catalog. Verdict: solid and effective, but long and over-stuffed. The fine print is dense, and the exclusions (fragrance, CHANEL, gift cards) undercut "includes almost everything."

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable: a giant "20% off" with the code SAVE20 and a clear "Online use code" line.
- The "TWO DAYS | MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" tag gives credible urgency and a sense of exclusivity.
- The "Free Shipping on any $35 purchase" banner and the rewards-points strip add loyalty value above the fold.
- The consistent orange/red modules and large product photography make the layout easy to scan.
- The stackable offers ("stack with your coupon") encourage a bigger basket.

## What's weak

- Too many competing modules (free gifts, 12-piece gift, four 30%-off tiles) dilute the single 20% message.
- The four "up to 30% off" tiles are near-identical, with generic "Shop all" buttons and no specific products or prices.
- "ONLY IN THE APP | Ends Oct 10" sends people to a different channel than the "online" hero, which is confusing.
- The legal and fine-print block at the bottom is a dense wall of text, and the exclusions are only partly visible near the hero.
- The "Not a member? Join now!" button competes with the main shopping action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the module count. Keep the 20% hero and one gift module, and push the in-app tiles into a single "Fall Deal Days" row.
- 2. Show 3-4 specific hero products with prices below the hero, so the 20% turns into something concrete.
- 3. Put the exclusions in one visible line directly under the code, so the promise matches the terms.
- 4. Replace "Shop all" with category-specific CTAs such as "Shop hair care" or "Shop makeup deals."
- 5. Add a countdown or a "Ends Oct 8" date to make the deadline harder to ignore.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 20% off almost everything, 2 days only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free 12-piece gift with $80. Use code SAVE20 online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack it with up to 30% off in the app — ends Oct 8.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict template with dark-mode meta tags. The main confirmed issues are about 10 non-HTTPS third-party pixels from `ads.dotomi.com` and widespread missing `alt` attributes. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **35 tracking/redirect links were skipped** by the HTTP probe, so destination validity, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **10 `http://` pixels** (`pub1.php` to `pub10.php`) at `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pubN.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`.
  - Clients that block non-HTTPS content won't load them, so any cookie-sync they do will silently fail.
  - Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will also swallow them.
  - Ten sequential third-party cookie-redirect calls add weight and a third-party data-sharing surface.
- **`https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=162044`** is the open-tracking pixel. It is HTTPS and has no `alt`.
- **`mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=202740837&mi_ign=162044`** are two dynamic-content images, probably Movable Ink. They have no `alt`, so if they fail to render the module has no fallback text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 5 content images** (`image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/44|45/`):
  - `08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
  - `4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` (flagged twice, so it appears twice)
  - `f6048146-d0e9-4590-9040-5734168c9dbb.png`
  - `70c7f19b-7d40-41be-a24e-f04cc770ef04.png`
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these may be unreadable if they carry offer content. I can't tell from the truncated source whether they're decorative.
- **Missing `alt` on 12 pixel/tracking images** (the 10 dotomi pixels, the `emltrk.com` pixel, and the `mi.ulta.com` images). Spacer and tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans...">`.
  - Gmail and Outlook don't load it, so those clients depend on the fallback stack.
  - I couldn't confirm that stack because the source is truncated.
- **Markup validity:** the doctype is XHTML 1.0 Strict, but `<meta name="color-scheme">` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes">` aren't self-closed. This is cosmetic and doesn't affect rendering.
- The HTML source was truncated, so the body markup, such as nested tables, `role="presentation"` and the preheader, couldn't be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%`, `{{` or `[%`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) weren't found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.ulta.com` can't be assessed from this data.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are probably in the footer, which was truncated, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 35 links were skipped as redirect-wrapped, so no landing URLs or UTM parameters are visible. The only campaign identifier in the QA output is `pr_fy26wk36_tuesday_20off_6am_rem`, which appears alongside the tracking pixel. I can't confirm it is carried through to the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop them. Ten `http://` cookie-redirect pixels add no value in most clients and are a privacy risk.
2. Add `alt=""` to all tracking and spacer pixels. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images in `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/44|45/`, and give the Movable Ink images fallback `alt` text.
3. Check that the sending ESP emits `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC align. Capture the raw headers outside the relay to confirm.
4. Resolve the redirect-wrapped links in the QA pipeline, for example by following one hop. That would make landing URLs and UTM parameters auditable.
5. Make sure the `DM Sans` stack falls back to Arial/Helvetica, which the `.helvetica` class suggests.
