# A little thank you, from us 🫶 Get $10 off your $50 qualifying purchase (Terms & excl. apply)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, well-built Ulta send. The hero is a big "$10 off" with the code THANKS, set on a loud pink and magenta background. The eye lands on it right away. The offer is concrete: $10 off $50, in store or online, and it includes prestige brands and fragrance. The deadline is stated (ends Saturday, November 28 at 11:59pm CT), and there's a visible "Shop now" button.
- The rest of the email dilutes it. After the hero come a "thank you" banner, a product collage, a member-exclusive throw, Black Friday "up to 50% off", a free 7-piece gift, a sweepstakes, and an app promo. That's six or seven competing offers in one scroll, and the coupon gets buried. The legal footer is also very long. Since the last Black Friday sends from other brands focus on a single loud message, this reads as unfocused by comparison.
- Overall it's a good coupon email with a top-of-fold hero that works, but it's over-stuffed. Cut the stack and it would be a clear 8.

## What's working

- The "$10 off" hero is huge, high-contrast, and sits right under the header.
- The offer is specific: a dollar amount, a threshold, and the in-store barcode and online code THANKS.
- The deadline is credible and stated: Saturday, Nov 28 at 11:59pm CT.
- The top banner personalizes to Rae and the loyalty program: "Rae, earn points that turn into $ off future purchases", with a "Join for free" button.
- The Black Friday "up to 50% off all week long" block and the "Shop all Black Friday deals" button fit the season.
- The "Choose from 5" free-gift block uses clear product imagery and a "Get the gift" button.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete: the $10 coupon, up to 50% off, a free 7-piece gift, the throw, sweepstakes, and the app. No single one wins after the hero.
- The "a thank you from Ulta Beauty" script banner and the 3x3 product collage push the hero's "Shop now" far from the products and add scroll without adding clarity.
- The sweepstakes block carries several paragraphs of fine print inside the body, and the footer is dense. Both undercut the "honest offer" feel.
- The collage tiles have no visible prices, labels, or individual links, so there's little product detail to click on.
- The "Shop now" button is small relative to the giant "$10 off" above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle: drop the sweepstakes and app promo, and keep only the coupon, the Black Friday block, and the free gift. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Add a second "Shop now" button right after the product collage, so the coupon CTA reappears where the shopping happens.
- 3. Label the collage tiles with brand names and prices, or "$10 off" eligibility, so each tile has a reason to click.
- 4. Move the sweepstakes terms to a short link instead of paragraphs in the body.
- 5. Make the "Shop now" button larger and pair it with the code THANKS.

- **Subject Alt B:** `$10 off $50 ends Saturday 🫶 Use code THANKS`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Prestige brands and fragrance count. In store or online through Nov 28.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`exct.net`, `emltrk.com`) with Movable Ink dynamic images. The automated QA ran at a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are missing alt text on many images and ten insecure third-party ad-sync pixels. The unsubscribe and authentication checks could not be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so final destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **10 `http://` cookie-sync pixels from Dotomi:** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`.
  - They are non-HTTPS, so clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings.
  - Ten sequential third-party ad-network calls add load time, privacy exposure and a possible spam-filter signal.
- **`http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...`:** this Salesforce live-content image is also served over HTTP.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=165147` is HTTPS and is working as an open pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 18 distinct images.** All are served from `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/...`, for example:
  - `m/45/08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `m/44/414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
  - `m/45/4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` (flagged twice)
  - `m/1/639322af-edf6-4a3b-9518-0b1533c1fa3e.jpg`
  - `m/1/a49f4dae-0bba-4467-a9b8-486c22bb5bbd.jpg`
  - `m/1/7a6ce1eb-dbf0-4f3e-a10f-1013027a27e2.jpg`
  - `m/1/601fd567-42f4-4379-885d-9c13c73c4ea7.jpg`
  - `m/1/852db034-b7e7-4f49-bcce-3b881d164154.png` (flagged twice)
- **Movable Ink images without alt:** `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png` and `3eb758650b65389e.png`. If these carry the offer or product content, it is invisible with images off or to screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** The Dotomi pixels, `LiveContent.aspx` and the `emltrk.com` pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly. This is low severity, but the attribute is currently absent.
- **Doctype and language attributes.** The page uses an XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype with `lang="en-US"` but no `xml:lang`. This is minor and unlikely to affect rendering.
- Mobile CSS is present (`@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)`), along with Outlook VML/`o:OfficeDocumentSettings` handling and dark-mode meta tags. Nothing here needs fixing.
- The hamburger-menu CSS is commented out, so it has no effect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source and the QA data show no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the cause is again probably the relay.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated HTML doesn't show them, so I can't confirm or rule out either.
- **From domain:** `U-Mail@e.ulta.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified.** All 57 links were skipped as click-redirect domains and no resolved destinations were captured.
- **Campaign identifier:** the `emltrk` pixel carries `pr_fy26wk43_thursday_broad_10off50_10am_pz_last`. It lines up with the "$10 off $50" offer in the subject, but I can't confirm the landing page honors it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text** to the 18 or so content images. Use descriptive alt for the offer and product images and `alt=""` for decorative images and pixels.
2. **Audit the Dotomi `http://` pixels.** Move them to HTTPS or remove them, and consider consolidating the ten sequential calls.
3. **Serve `LiveContent.aspx` over HTTPS.**
4. **Re-run QA with raw headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) captured before the relay, to confirm RFC 8058 and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status.
5. **Resolve and follow redirect chains** for a sample of the 57 tracking links, to check the UTMs and that the landing page shows the $10-off-$50 offer.
6. **Check the full, untruncated HTML** for the unsubscribe link and the physical address.
