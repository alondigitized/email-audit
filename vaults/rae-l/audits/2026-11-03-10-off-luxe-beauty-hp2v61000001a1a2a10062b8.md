# 💎 10% OFF LUXE BEAUTY 💎

## Executive summary

- The subject sells luxe makeup at 10% off, but the body is a catalog of price points, not a discount. The hero is "Treat Yourself to Luxe Makeup" with a SPLURGE NOW button. The hero carries no 10% badge. Below it, three tiers (Luxe Under $40, $60 and $70) list Armani, Dior, Prada, YSL, Gucci and Tom Ford at $29–$65. The promised 10% is never visible in the main creative. It is only implied by the "Savings Event" label and the fine print.
- The creative itself is polished and clearly laid out. The price-tier structure is a smart way to browse, and the product shots are rich. But a recipient who opens for "10% off" has to hunt for it. ## What's working

- The price-tier blocks ("Luxe Under $40/$60/$70") give concrete, scannable price anchors.
- Prestige brands (Armani, Dior, YSL, Gucci, Tom Ford) make the "luxe" angle credible.
- Free same-day delivery with code SPEEDY and its 11/9 deadline sits near the top as a secondary perk.
- The barcode at the bottom turns the offer into an in-store redemption.
- The product photography is rich and consistent with the jewel-tone theme.

## What's weak

- No visible "10% off" badge in the hero or tier blocks, so the subject's offer isn't confirmed on sight.
- The stack of top banners (event header, "Come hang with us", free delivery) pushes the actual hero down the screen.
- The tier blocks show prices but not discounted prices, so it's unclear whether $29 already includes the 10%.
- Fine print and the three-part footer make for a very long email.
- The 11/2 and 11/3 sends were near-identical, so this adds to fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a clear "10% OFF LUXE BEAUTY" badge with the dates and code directly in the hero, above the SPLURGE NOW button.
- 2. Move the free-delivery and "Come hang with us" banners below the hero, or collapse them to one thin strip.
- 3. Show "was/now" pricing on each product so the discount is visible.
- 4. Add one star rating or a "best seller" tag to a hero product for social proof.
- 5. Space out the Savings Event sends, or vary the angle (category-specific, as here) instead of repeating the sitewide message.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text alternative (QA FAIL, 0 chars), and several markup problems affect mobile and accessibility. The HTML is truncated, so I couldn't check the footer, the unsubscribe link or the CTA URLs. Header-level compliance findings are unverified for the same reason.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Open pixel, no `alt`:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; …"/>` has no `alt` attribute. This is a hidden Epsilon/Sephora open-tracking pixel, so the practical impact is low.
- **False positive in the QA flag for `o.gif`:** the source shows `<img src="https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…" width="1" height="1" alt="" aria-hidden="true" />`. `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden` is the correct way to mark a decorative image, so only the `app.sephora.com` pixel is a real finding.
- **Two tracking pixels:** one from `app.sephora.com` and one from `mi.sephora.com`. The `mi_u=4958433775694848` parameter is a per-recipient ID in a URL.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 46 skipped. No per-link status codes were supplied, so I can't confirm any broken links or redirect chains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed 700px layout:** the wrapper is `<table width="700" … style="min-width:700px; width:700px;">`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` block only resizes fonts and specific images (`.mobile`, `.mobileHeaderLogo`), so I found no rule that collapses the wrapper. This risks horizontal scroll or shrink-to-fit on mobile.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` rule in the `<style>` block. Conditional comments are not valid inside CSS, and some parsers may drop the rules that follow it, such as `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`, which screen readers need.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email clients.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the message.
- **Unused JSON-LD:** the `application/ld+json` block has an empty `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip `<script>`, and the block carries no usable promo data.
- **Plain-text part missing (FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and text-only or accessibility clients.
