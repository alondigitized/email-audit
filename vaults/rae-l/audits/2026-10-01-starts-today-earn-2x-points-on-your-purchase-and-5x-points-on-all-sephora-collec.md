# STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection 😍
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-multiplier send. The hero is an oversized red "2X" with "STARTS TODAY" above it. Below it sits a "USE CODE SAYPOINTS" button, so the offer and the action are clear in the first screen. After that the email turns into a long catalog. It runs from a "What your points can do" ladder through a cardmember bonus, a six-product "Earn 2X points on top picks" grid, a Hair Faves banner, two content tiles, a gifting block and Beauty Insider tier cards. The hero is strong, but everything below it competes with it.
- The subject names two tiers, 2X on your purchase and 5X on all Sephora Collection. In the render, the 5X for Sephora Collection is secondary to the 2X hero. The subject leads with a stacked offer and the body leads with a single number.
- This is also close to the fifth points or daily-deals send in about a week. Compare "4X points on fragrance (yes way!)" on 9/22 and "Hair Daily Deals starts today" on 9/23. The multiplier message is credible and well executed, but it's unlikely to feel new. Judgment: strong hero, solid loyalty framing, diluted by length and by cadence.

## What's working

- The giant "2X" with "STARTS TODAY" gives an instant focal point and a clear value read.
- The "USE CODE SAYPOINTS" button sits right under the hero, with a deadline of 10/7.
- The product grid pairs each item with its price and a points-earned figure, for example "EARN 348 points" on the Armani Beauty fragrance at $174. That makes the loyalty value tangible.
- The "What your points can do" ladder (samples at 100+, $10 off at 250, experiences at 750+) shows what the points are worth.
- The Cardmembers earn-faster callout and the Beauty Insider tier cards add real member benefits.

## What's weak

- Too many competing modules. The Hair Faves banner (with its "TODAY ONLY" badge), Founders Face to Face, Under $50, Gifting and the tier cards all pull attention from the points message.
- The subject's second offer, 5X on all Sephora Collection, is not featured visibly in the render. The grid is mostly premium brands.
- Two separate free same-day delivery bars with the code DELIVERED sit above and below the hero. They compete with the SAYPOINTS code and give the shopper two codes to remember.
- The legal and fine-print block at the bottom is very long and dense, and it makes "2X on your purchase" read as heavily conditioned.
- The email is extremely long, with a barcode at the very bottom. Most readers won't reach the later modules.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job. Keep the 2X hero, the code button and the product grid, and move Hair Faves, content tiles and gifting to another send.
- 2. Make 5X Sephora Collection visible as its own module, since the subject promises it, or drop it from the subject.
- 3. Pick one code for the hero. Move the delivery perk to a single line near the footer.
- 4. Shorten the grid to 3–4 products and make the points math more visible (for example, "$174 = 348 points").
- 5. Put the end date, 10/7, in the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2X points starts today — 5X on Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Double points on everything, 5X on Sephora Collection`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAYPOINTS through 10/7. See what your points can get you.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 348 points on one fragrance. Insiders get it all week.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The QA run found one hard failure: the email has no plain-text alternative (0 chars). Three images are missing alt text, and the viewport and text-size settings in the source restrict zoom. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the AgentMail relay.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 43, ranked by click likelihood, and no probe failures were listed.
- The 43 skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, are unverified.
- Tracking pixel: `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261001_PME_Stacking_Starts_Today_BI…`. It carries a campaign ID and a subscriber ID in the query string. This is standard open tracking, not a defect, but it has no alt text (see section 3).
- A second `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` image is also likely a tracking beacon. The same image appears in the alt-text warnings.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (3 images, from QA):**
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…/2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`. This is a content or navigation header image, so it is the only one that matters to screen-reader users. It will be announced by its filename or skipped.
  - `mi.sephora.com/.../o.gif` and `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…`. These are tracking pixels. They should have `alt=""` to be explicitly decorative.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This overrides the user's text-size preference on iOS.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">`. The valid token is `noindex`, so this is a harmless typo.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is the same for every send.
- No issues found with the doctype, charset, or Outlook/VML namespace declarations.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`, `${}`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the body below the CSS is unchecked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** QA says these may not be captured by the relay. They are unconfirmed rather than confirmed missing. Because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, this should be re-checked against raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, so alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **No plain-text part [FAIL].** The text version is 0 chars. This is a deliverability and accessibility issue, and it can hurt spam scoring. It is also a real defect in the message itself, not a relay artifact.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the footer, which was truncated, so they were not verified. QA reports that an unsubscribe link is always included in link probing.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Not assessable. The truncated source doesn't show the CTA hrefs, and the QA output has no destination URLs or redirect chains.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261001_PME_Stacking_Starts_Today_BI…` on the pixel. Standard `utm_*` parameters on the CTA links are unconfirmed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Also drop `text-size-adjust: none`, or limit it to the Outlook and iOS cases that need it.
3. Add a descriptive `alt` to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message headers. Also check `Authentication-Results` there, outside the relay.
5. Change `content="no index"` to `noindex`, and give the `<title>` content that varies per send.
6. Re-run QA on the full HTML, with link probing extended to the skipped links. That would let the footer compliance elements, the CTA UTMs and the landing-page alignment be checked.
