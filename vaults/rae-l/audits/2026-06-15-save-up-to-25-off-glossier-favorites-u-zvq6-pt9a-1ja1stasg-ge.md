# Save up to 25% off Glossier favorites
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's sixth sale email in five days — the Friends of Glossier Sale arc that opened June 11. The most notable structural difference from yesterday's send (which earned a 7/10 and was itself an exact subject-line repeat of June 14) is that today widens the aperture: "fragrance" becomes "favorites," fragrance products share the email with a "Forever Favorites" section (Boy Brow, Glossier You Duo), and a subscription upsell appears at the bottom for the first time in the arc. These are genuinely smart choices at day five of a sale.
- The problem is that the email doesn't signal to the subscriber that anything has changed. The tiled "ANNUAL SALE" header, the "25% off" hero, and the four identical "Shop now" buttons are indistinguishable from the last three sends. There is no hard end date anywhere in the email — "won't last forever" is the weakest urgency language available. Anyone who hasn't clicked after five emails needed a new reason, and this email doesn't give them one.
- The subscription upsell ("lock in 25% off*") is buried as the last section with the same visual weight as a product card. That's the freshest idea in the arc and it deserved a dedicated send or at minimum the hero position.

## What's working

- **Two-section merchandising** ("Save on Fragrance" / "Forever Favorites") creates a logical browse path and broadens the aperture beyond fragrance in a logical way.
- **Visible discounted prices** ($72, $66, $18) with product-level discount badges do the math for the subscriber — no guessing what 25% off means.
- **Subscription upsell is new creative.** "Lock in 25% off forever" is the only genuinely differentiated value message in this entire arc.
- **Tiled "ANNUAL SALE" header + bold hero** reads cleanly and immediately on mobile — hierarchy is unambiguous.
- **No render bugs** — all images load, buttons are clearly styled, footer is intact.

## What's weak

- **No hard deadline.** "Won't last forever" is not urgency — it's a shrug. Day five of a sale demands a countdown or end date.
- **Four identical "Shop now" buttons** across four different sections — no specificity, no guidance on where to click first.
- **Cadence has hit the wall.** Six emails in five days on the same offer means most active subscribers have already decided. This send needed a sharper new hook to break through that inertia.
- **Subscription upsell is buried.** The one new idea in the email sits last, under a low-key "DON'T FORGET" header, and receives no visual prominence — most eyes will have glazed over by then.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a real deadline.** Make the end date visible in the hero — "Sale ends Sunday midnight" beats "won't last forever" by a wide margin.
- 2. **Differentiate every CTA.** "Shop Fragrances," "Get Boy Brow," "Start a Subscription" — each button should name what you're clicking toward.
- 3. **Promote the subscription upsell.** "Get 25% off every order, not just this week" is categorically different from the sale offer. It should be above the fold or a standalone email, not a buried afterthought.
- 4. **Cut two product cards.** Four product cards + a subscription section is too many asks. Two bold hero SKUs with larger imagery will convert better.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 25% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Glossier favorites are 25% off — ending soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Boy Brow, Glossier You, Fragrance Trio — all on sale now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Or lock in 25% off forever with a subscription.`
