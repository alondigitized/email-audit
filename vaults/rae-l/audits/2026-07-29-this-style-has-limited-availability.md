# This style has limited availability!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the third time "This style has limited availability!" has hit this inbox — first on 2026-06-30 (6/10, flagged for the same urgency mismatch), then again on 2026-07-16 (9/10), and now a third time on 2026-07-29. The subject promises scarcity, but the actual creative that renders is a browse-abandonment template: "HOW ABOUT A SECOND LOOK? SEEN THIS BEFORE? WE THOUGHT SO." That's soft re-engagement copy, not "this is about to sell out." The subject/body disconnect that got flagged two weeks ago hasn't been fixed — Skechers is reusing the same subject line on a completely different creative concept.
- Buried under the mismatch is a genuinely decent email: a clean single hero shoe, a tidy 6-item "more options" grid, and a bold, legible "2 FOR $100" banner with a real end date. The execution is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text — but the offer sits below two full screens of scrolling on mobile, and the hero itself carries no discount at all.
- Net: solid production values undercut by a lazy, recycled, mismatched subject line and an offer buried too far down. This lands as a middling browse-abandon send wearing an urgency subject it doesn't earn.

## What's working

- Hero product shot (Arch Fit Arcade slip-in) is large, clean, and renders with no artifacts.
- "SHOP NOW" button under the hero is high-contrast navy-on-white and easy to spot.
- The "2 FOR $100" banner has real specificity — a dollar offer, category scope, and an explicit expiration date (8/3).
- Six-item product grid gives real alternatives with legible names, not filler.
- Footer utility block (app download, Afterpay/Klarna, store locator, text club) is complete and on-brand.

## What's weak

- Subject says "limited availability" but the rendered hero says "second look" / "seen this before" — zero scarcity language anywhere in the body. This is the exact mismatch flagged on the June 30 send, still unresolved.
- No discount or offer is visible in the hero section at all — a shopper has to scroll past the hero and the entire 6-item grid before hitting the "2 FOR $100" deal.
- Subject line has now run three times in a month on three different creative concepts — recipients who remember it will feel baited.
- "SHOP NOW" CTA copy is generic and repeated twice; neither instance names a product or category.
- No sizing, fit, or availability detail on the featured shoe despite the subject explicitly claiming limited stock.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/body mismatch first — either write a subject that matches the browse-abandon creative ("Still thinking about the Arch Fit Arcade?") or rebuild the hero to actually show scarcity (low-stock badge, "almost gone" tag on the product card). Don't ship urgency copy against a re-engagement template again.
- 2. Move the "2 FOR $100" banner above the hero shoe, or add a smaller offer callout directly under the hero CTA — right now the one real discount is invisible without scrolling.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with product-specific CTA copy, e.g. "Shop Arch Fit Arcade" — ties the click directly to the pictured item.
- 4. Retire this subject line for at least a full cycle — it's been used on 6/30, 7/16, and now 7/29; rotate in fresh phrasing even if the underlying campaign logic (browse retarget) repeats.

- **Subject Alt B:** `2 for $100 — your size is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The slip-ins you looked at, plus 6 more picks you'll like`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mix and match: 2 for $100 on select styles thru 8/3`
