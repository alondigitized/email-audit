# Straight from our community
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's tenth email in the Friends of Glossier Sale arc — and it's running the same UGC curation play Glossier already used on June 16 (the send that earned a 9/10). The inside of this email is genuinely strong: real community quotes, three specific product picks, and a clever subscription lock-in pitch buried at the bottom that could convert cautious buyers into repeat customers. The format works. It worked eight days ago.
- The failure is entirely at the inbox. "Straight from our community" is the weakest subject this arc has produced. It hides the 25% off offer completely, brings no urgency, and for any subscriber who has already seen nine Glossier sale emails since June 11, it reads as soft brand content that can safely go unread. The June 16 UGC email succeeded partly because its subject — "What to buy with up to 25% off" — combined the editorial frame with the discount signal. This one stripped the discount out entirely.
- Strong email, wrong door. The format has proven it can pull clicks; the subject is what's being wasted here.

## What's working

- Community attribution ("RECOMMENDATIONS FROM YOU") makes the social proof feel legitimately earned rather than curated by a copywriter.
- Three distinct product panels (Glossier You, Lash Slick, Balm Dotcom) give the subscriber optionality rather than a single forced hero.
- The "ENDS SOON" badge at the top of the hero establishes sale urgency before the eye moves anywhere else.
- Subscription lock-in pitch at the bottom ("Start a subscription and lock in 25% off*") is a smart LTV play that hasn't appeared in prior sends this arc.
- Layout is clean and renders without visible bugs.

## What's weak

- "Straight from our community" hides the 25% off sale entirely from the subject line — on day 8 of a sale arc, that's the one thing that drives opens.
- "ENDS SOON" has no date or time attached; for a subscriber who has seen this copy five times this week, the urgency is functionally meaningless.
- All five CTA buttons read "Shop now" — no differentiation by product, which squanders the specificity the UGC quotes built.
- This is Glossier's second UGC-curation email in the same arc (June 16 was the first) — running the same format twice in three days dulls the novelty that made June 16 a 9/10.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the sale in the subject.** The community angle is a creative wrapper, not a reason to hide 25% off. A hybrid subject retains the UGC hook while restoring the commercial signal.
- 2. **Make the deadline explicit everywhere.** Swap "ENDS SOON" and "ENDING SOON" for the actual close date ("Ends Sunday at midnight"). The subscription section especially needs this — the pitch is strong enough to warrant a real deadline.
- 3. **Differentiate the CTA buttons by product.** "Shop Glossier You," "Shop Lash Slick," "Shop Balm Dotcom" — each panel should name the product in its button so the UGC quote and the click destination feel connected.
- 4. **Promote the subscription pitch.** "Lock in 25% off forever" is a more interesting hook than any remaining sale email angle — it deserves its own send, not a tertiary panel below the fold.
- 5. **Break the UGC repetition.** If the arc needs another day, switch angles: an urgency countdown, a bestseller rank ("our top 5 sold since June 11"), or a "last chance" personal stylist message.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Community picks: save 25% before the sale ends`
- **Subject Alt B:** `They said it first — 25% off ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Glossier You, Lash Slick, Balm Dotcom — real picks from real fans, all 25% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `What our community stocks up on. Grab their favorites before the Friends Sale closes.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Gmail Android viewport mismatch (`max-width: 384px` → `width: 414px`):**
```css
@media screen and (max-width: 384px) {
  .mail-message-content { width: 414px !important }
}
```
The media query triggers at ≤384px but forces a 414px width — wider than the triggering viewport. This is a documented Gmail Android workaround (Gmail on Android reports 384px but renders at 414px), but it will cause horizontal scroll on true 384px displays (e.g., iPhone SE 1st gen). Flag for QA on that device.
