# Save 15% with the Sally app
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a different animal from the last six Sally Beauty sends I've reviewed: instead of a flash sale, BOGO, or the COLORfest campaign push, it's an app-download incentive — "Get 15% off any $35+ purchase. Download the Sally app in August to save." The creative is clean and on-brand (confetti dots, red/pink palette, a friendly product-in-hand hero shot), and the offer itself — 15% off a $35 minimum — is genuinely competitive.
- The problem is the ask. The primary CTA isn't "shop now," it's "download now" — meaning the reward for opening this email is an app-store detour, not a purchase. For someone who just wants to buy shampoo, that's an extra step between them and the discount, and it dilutes what's otherwise a strong, specific offer. The email does hedge with a secondary "Shop now" CTA further down (tied to the Sally Rewards $5-back mechanic), which is the smarter click path here.
- Compared to 8/6's COLORfest BOGO send (8.00/10, real SKUs, real product) and 7/29's loyalty push (9.00/10, "$5 reward when you spend $50"), this one reads like a lower-effort fill-in between campaign beats — competent execution, weaker mechanic. Solid but not a top performer.

## What's working

- The hero offer ("Get 15% off any $35+ purchase") is bold, oversized, and impossible to miss — first thing the eye hits below the nav.
- Two-hour delivery and BOPIS are called out in the top utility bar, giving convenience-minded shoppers an immediate reason to stay.
- The disclaimer ("Offer expires in 30 days. Not valid with other promotions or pro discount.") is displayed prominently in its own red band, not buried in 6pt legal text — feels honest.
- The Sally Rewards module ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") gives a real, low-friction shopping CTA as a fallback to the app push.
- Klarna financing and the five-icon shopping-options row (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping $40+, 2-Hour Delivery, BOPIS) are a nice trust/convenience close near the footer.

## What's weak

- The primary CTA is "Download now," not a shopping link — the 15% off can't actually be redeemed by clicking anything in this email; it requires leaving to an app store first.
- No product is shown or linked anywhere in the email — just a lifestyle shot of a woman holding a phone, with zero connection to hair color, hair care, textured hair, or nails (the categories in the nav bar).
- The offer isn't tied to the COLORfest campaign momentum built over the last two weeks (7/30, 8/3, 8/6) — it reads as a disconnected, generic evergreen ask dropped into an otherwise escalating campaign arc.
- Subject line gives zero urgency or seasonal/campaign hook, which matters given this is at least the sixth Sally send in under two weeks — fatigue risk is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a shoppable click, not an app-store detour.** Make "Shop now" (linking to $35+ eligible categories) the primary CTA, and demote "Download the app to save 15%" to a secondary banner. Right now the biggest offer in the email is the hardest one to redeem.
- 2. **Tie this to COLORfest or a specific category** instead of a generic "any purchase" hero — even a single hero product (a COLORfest hair-color kit, a rewards-eligible tool) would give the click somewhere concrete to land.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline to the subject/hero**, not just "in August" — "3 days left" or a specific date converts better than a full-month window.
- 4. **Show the app's actual value, not just a phone-in-hand.** A screenshot of the app UI (rewards tracker, exclusive deal) would sell the download harder than a lifestyle photo.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off $35+ — unlock it in the Sally app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $35 purchase just got 15% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Download once, save all August long — plus track your rewards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New: app-exclusive 15% off, no code needed.`
