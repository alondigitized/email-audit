# Last call for your free gift!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the last-chance send for the Milky Jelly Cleanser gift-with-purchase. The hero says "FINAL DAY / Get a free Milky Jelly Cleanser with your $75+ order*" and a large cleanser bottle sits next to it. The urgency is credible, the offer is specific, and there's one "Shop now" button.
- It is also the third send of this offer in five days. Glossier sent "Want a free gift?" on 10/22 and "Get Milky Jelly Cleanser, free!" on 10/24. Last call works as a closer, but a subscriber who ignored the first two has little reason to open this one. The "START HERE" row of three review cards (You Doux, Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom) adds social proof. It also pulls attention away from the offer, because each card has its own "Shop now" button.
- Overall this is a competent deadline email that matches the 7/10 range of the earlier gift sends. The cadence and the diluted lower half keep it from going higher.

## What's working

- "FINAL DAY" and "Today is your LAST DAY" make the deadline clear without relying on exclamation marks.
- The headline states the offer and the $75+ threshold in plain words.
- The hero bottle image is clean and easy to read, and it matches the product being given away.
- The review cards with star ratings and named quotes give real social proof.
- The asterisk and "Terms apply" note are standard disclosure and don't hide the threshold.

## What's weak

- The offer has now been sent three times in five days (10/22, 10/24, 10/26). That is fatigue, and "last call" carries less weight because of it.
- None of the three review cards features the free gift or the Milky Jelly Cleanser. The products shown are a perfume, a brow pomade and a lip balm.
- Each card has its own "Shop now" button, so there are four identical buttons and no clear primary action after the hero.
- The third product column and the footer graphics are cut off at the right edge. The Balm Dotcom quote ("I dont need… around everywhere") is truncated.
- "Shop now" doesn't name the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the lower half serve the offer. Show Milky Jelly with its reviews, or products that help a shopper reach $75 ("Add these to hit $75"). Right now the cards pull the reader toward unrelated products.
- 2. Put a countdown or an exact deadline ("Ends 11:59pm ET tonight") in the hero. "Today" is vague.
- 3. Change the hero button to "Get my free cleanser" and give the cards distinct, product-specific button text.
- 4. Space out the sends. After two gift emails, send something different, or send last call only to people who didn't click the first two.
- 5. Fix the clipped third column and the cropped footer so the layout fits the width.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: free Milky Jelly Cleanser`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free cleanser disappears at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get Milky Jelly Cleanser on us. Today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last chance: the gift that comes with every $75+ order.`
