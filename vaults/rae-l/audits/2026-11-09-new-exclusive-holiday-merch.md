# NEW: exclusive holiday merch!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-repeat of the 11/6 send ("Say hello to our new holiday merch!"). It shows the same four products (Hot Take Tumbler, playing cards, metallic pink bag, balm keychain) and adds one new item, the SoHo/LA/London-only Breaking Point Baseball Bat. The "NEW: exclusive" subject promises more than the body delivers. Only the bat is exclusive, and it can't be bought online.
- The creative is polished. The hero collage is strong, the product photography is warm and on-brand, and the layout is clean. But there is no price, no offer and no deadline, and every product CTA is a generic "Shop now". Glossier ran a gift-with-purchase four times in nine days, then a tumbler launch on 11/5, then this merch catalog on 11/6. Subscribers now get a third merch pitch in a week with nothing new to act on.
- Verdict: pretty and on-brand, but redundant. The only new thing, the bat, is the one item most recipients can't act on. It's a solid 5.

## What's working

- The hero collage of bat, bag, tumbler and cards gives an instant "gift haul" feel, and "cross everyone off your list (yourself included)" is a good holiday line.
- The 2x2 product grid with "Limited edition" labels adds scarcity without pricing clutter.
- The "Retail Exclusive" bat block is a distinct, playful moment, and "for decorative purposes only (mostly) 😉" matches the brand voice.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer's hand-drawn smiley artwork keeps the brand personality to the last pixel.

## What's weak

- The email has no price, offer, free shipping or deadline. "Limited edition" is the only urgency, and it's vague.
- Every product CTA reads "Shop now". Six identical buttons don't say which product or why.
- The subject's "NEW" and "exclusive" are overstated. Four of the five products were already shown on 11/6, and the one exclusive item is in-store only.
- The Hot Take Tumbler was announced as live on 11/5 and the merch catalog went out 11/6, so this is a third touch in a week with the same creative.
- The hero's button is a small low-contrast outline, and the wide gaps around the hero copy weaken the eye path to the CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with what is genuinely new: the bat and the in-store exclusivity. Or fold this into the previous send. If it must go, add a reason to act now, such as a ship-by date for holiday delivery or a stock warning ("selling fast").
- 2. Show prices on each product ("Hot Take Tumbler · $38") so there's a purchase signal.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Get the tumbler", "Grab the cards".
- 4. Add a holiday-shipping cutoff or a gift-with-purchase tie-in to create credible urgency.
- 5. Make the hero button solid and higher contrast, and tighten the whitespace around it.
