# Enjoy 20% off this season’s scent
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand fragrance push, and the offer is clear: "Save 20% off the scent of the season" sits in the hero with a blue "Shop now" button. It's the third 20% send in four days, after the 10/15 "OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE!" and the 10/17 "STILL ON!" reminder. The body even opens with "STILL ON!", so it is openly a rerun. Nothing here is new, and a subscriber who ignored the last two has no reason to act on this one.
- The structure is good. There's a hero, then a scent-notes story for Doux, then a three-up of You, You Fleur and You Rêve with "20% OFF" badges. A free Futuredew Solid at $75+ closes it out. The weak point is focus. The Doux story gets its own "Save on Doux" button, the three-up repeats the discount, and the Futuredew offer competes with all of it in the top banner and again at the bottom.
- Verdict: competent execution on a tired, high-frequency promo. It will convert people who were already going to buy, but it adds little for the rest of the list.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer in the first line, and the "Shop now" button is high contrast and obvious.
- The "20% OFF" blue badges on each fragrance carry the discount down the page without extra copy.
- Product-specific CTAs ("Save on You", "Save on Fleur", "Save on Rêve") link to a specific product and name it, which beats a generic "Shop".
- The scent-notes block (Sweet Violet, Palo Santo, Frankincense, Myrrh, Ambrette, Ambrox) gives fragrance shoppers the detail they use to choose.
- The fall framing ("Smell rich. Then richer.") fits the season and the Glossier voice.

## What's weak

- The top banner text is clipped at the right edge, so it reads "Get a free Futuredew Solid with your $75+ orde". It's a visible render bug on the first line of the email.
- This is the third 20% send since 10/15. "STILL ON!" and no new reason to act make the email feel like fatigue marketing.
- Two offers compete. The Futuredew free gift appears in the banner and again as its own block, pulling attention from the fragrance discount.
- The Doux section never names the fragrance until the "Save on Doux" button. The headline "A soft and serene woody blend" has no product name or price, and the scent-notes table is sparse and airy.
- Nothing is time-bound. "Still on" implies an end but gives no date, and there are no reviews or social proof anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero and subject ("Ends tonight" or "Ends Sunday"). It's the one signal that would separate this from the two prior sends.
- 2. Fix the clipped banner so the full Futuredew line fits, or shorten it ("Free Futuredew Solid at $75+").
- 3. Pick one offer per send. Either drop the Futuredew banner and block, or move it to a different email so the fragrance discount stays the single message.
- 4. Name the Doux fragrance in the scent-notes headline and put the price or a rating next to it, so the section sells before the button.
- 5. Add one proof point under the three-up, such as a bestseller flag or a star rating on Glossier You.
- 6. Space out the 20% sends. Hold this one until the offer is changing, like a final-hours push or a bundle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off Glossier You & Doux`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall scent, 20% off — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Doux, You, Fleur and Rêve are all 20% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Palo santo, frankincense and violet, now at 20% off.`
