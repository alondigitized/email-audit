# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email, and it's the same one audited on 10/22 under this exact subject, "Did you forget something?" The headline "READY TO CHECK OUT?" and the black "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button work, and the GO WALK Workout Walker hero shoe is a sensible reminder. The hero is clean and the next step is obvious.
- It goes soft after that. A second orange "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button sits under the shoe, with a large empty gap above it. Six unrelated best-seller thumbnails follow, then a full-width "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar and a Skechers Plus 20% off banner. The reminder turns into a mini-catalog. The 20% off is the only real incentive, but it sits below the fold, is members-only, and is "valid thru 11/2" in tiny type. With "No tricks, just treats," the email reads as Halloween-adjacent.
- My judgement: a competent but generic template. The cart-recovery job is done, and the cadence is the problem. The 10/22, 10/23, 10/26 and 10/28 sends all used the same structure, so a subscriber gets a near-identical nudge every couple of days.

## What's working

- The headline and black button give a clear, immediate next step: "READY TO CHECK OUT? / COMPLETE YOUR ORDER."
- One large hero product, the Slip-ins GO WALK Workout Walker, with a plain caption keeps the cart reminder focused at the top.
- The 20% off Skechers Plus banner is high-contrast, and the navy block stands out from the white body.
- The "No tricks, just treats" framing and the "valid thru 11/2" dates fit the season.
- The footer utilities (app, curbside, Afterpay/Klarna, text-to-join) are tidy and well organized.

## What's weak

- The large white gap between the headline banner and the hero shoe pushes the product down and wastes the first screen.
- There are two identical "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" buttons plus "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" and "SHOP NOW" within one scroll. That is too many competing CTAs.
- The six best-seller thumbnails are generic, with no prices, ratings or sizes, and they dilute the cart reminder.
- The member discount is buried below the products, and its terms sit in 8-point footer text.
- The text-only nav list (WOMEN, MEN, KIDS and so on) and the long footer undercut an email meant to recover one specific cart.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the gap and put the Workout Walker hero at the top, with its price, a size and color line, and a single CTA. Remove the duplicate orange button.
- 2. Move the 20% Plus offer up directly under the hero as a reason to finish checkout now. Add a visible deadline such as "Ends 11/2."
- 3. Replace the six generic best-sellers with 2–3 items related to the abandoned item (similar walking shoes), each with a price and star rating.
- 4. Vary the cadence. After the first reminder, suppress or space out repeats, and change the creative (reviews, comfort proof, an urgency or low-stock cue) so the fourth send isn't a copy of the first.
- 5. Drop the category list and shorten the footer utilities.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Workout Walkers are still in your cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off ends 11/2 — your cart's waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers Plus members save 20% through 11/2. Finish checkout before your size sells out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your GO WALK Workout Walker is saved. Complete your order in one tap.`

The source is truncated, and the relay dropped the authentication and `List-Unsubscribe` headers. Compliance and destination findings are therefore partly unverifiable.

  - The token URL may reject non-browser or bot requests, so this is not proof the link is dead. It is also the only link on this email that failed outright, and it is the most important one to confirm manually.

No issues found. No unrendered tokens (such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The cart contents are not visible in the truncated HTML, so cart-item personalization could not be verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the relay may strip them. They are unconfirmed rather than confirmed missing, but without them one-click unsubscribe can't be verified.
  - Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `msgs.skechers.com`, while links and the pixel use `attentivemail.com` and `attn.tv`. Third-party link domains are normal for an ESP, but they cannot be checked for alignment from this data.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link returned 400** (see section 2), so the body unsubscribe mechanism is unconfirmed.
- The physical postal address was not visible in the truncated source and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is wrapped in an Attentive redirect, and the two redirects probed for the cart CTA and image link returned 429. This means:
- UTM parameters on the final URLs are unknown.
- Whether the cart CTA lands on a restored cart or on a generic page is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually test the unsubscribe link `skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…` in a browser. If it fails there too, treat it as a critical compliance defect.
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` plus `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers. To confirm they're present, check the raw message headers outside the relay.
3. Add a plain-text MIME part, or confirm that the Attentive send includes one and the relay is dropping it.
4. Change all 11 `http://image.emails.skechers.com/…` image sources to `https://`.
5. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero `217196_OLV_HERO_LG` and the two `.jpg` images, and `alt=""` to the open pixel.
6. Populate `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
7. Re-run the link probe with throttling or retries to resolve the two 429 links and the 31 skipped ones. Then record the final destination URLs to verify UTMs and cart-restore behavior.
8. Capture the raw `Authentication-Results` header to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
