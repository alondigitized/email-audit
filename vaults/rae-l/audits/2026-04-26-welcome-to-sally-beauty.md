# Welcome to Sally Beauty!!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-04-26**
## Executive summary

- A well-intentioned welcome email that tries to do too much at once. The 15% off welcome offer is the right lead — but it's buried beneath a competing loyalty pitch, a spend-threshold reward, product recommendations, a DIY content module, and category browsing before the recipient has even engaged with the brand. The email reads more like a homepage dump than a curated first impression. The welcome gift code itself is so long and visually heavy it feels like a burden to use. One flagged visual issue: the discount code expiry reads "2071-12/08," which is almost certainly a data error and looks broken to a first-time recipient.
- Solid bones — loyalty program, welcome discount, content utility — but the execution dilutes conversion by scattering attention across six competing modules. A new subscriber seeing all of this at once is more likely to scroll past than act.

## What's working

- **Welcome discount is present and front-loaded** — the 15% off first online purchase is the right hook for a welcome email and it's placed early.
- **Category grid at the bottom** is clean and visually organized — Hair Care, Hair Color, Nails, Skincare, Styling Tools — gives browsers a clear onramp.
- **Loyalty program inclusion** (Get Rewarded for EVERY Beauty Haul) is smart for a welcome series; capturing opt-ins early matters.
- **"Start with these faves" product strip** provides concrete browsing anchors without requiring a decision.
- **DIY University / content module** is a differentiator — positions Sally as a resource, not just a retailer.

## What's weak

- **Six competing value propositions** — the welcome discount, loyalty join, spend-$50-get-$5, product recs, DIY content, and category browse all ask for different actions. There's no clear primary CTA.
- **The welcome discount code is unusably long** — "887716CN-513-HUQ-QTP" is 16 characters of alphanumeric noise. New subscribers won't hand-type that.
- **Expiry date "2071-12/08" is a visible data error** — this is the most damaging issue in the email. A welcome gift that expires in 2071 signals broken personalization and erodes trust immediately.
- **Hero copy is generic** — "As beauty besties, we'll share discounts..." reads like boilerplate; it doesn't establish a distinct Sally voice.
- **Email is very long** — five distinct modules before the fold creates scroll fatigue. The DIY University section, while interesting, is misplaced in a welcome flow where the primary job is conversion.
- **Double exclamation mark in subject** ("Welcome to Sally Beauty!!") undersells the brand's sophistication.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the expiry date immediately** — "2071" is a clear data error; it will generate unsubscribes and trust damage if left live.
- 2. **Shorten the welcome code** — use a memorable, human-typeable code like WELCOME15 rather than the 16-character hash.
- 3. **One primary CTA above the fold** — strip the welcome email to: hero offer → single CTA → brief loyalty mention → footer. Move DIY University to email #2 or #3 in the onboarding sequence.
- 4. **Give the loyalty join its own email** — competing with the welcome discount dilutes both. Welcome email should close on the discount; follow-up email closes on loyalty enrollment.
- 5. **Rewrite hero copy** — lean into what makes Sally distinctively useful: professional-grade products accessible to consumers, DIY education, etc. "Beauty besties" is vague.
- 6. **Tighten subject line** — drop one exclamation mark, consider adding specificity (e.g., the 15% offer).

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Undeclared primary font on all anchor tags:**
```css
a { font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif; }
```
`Founders Grotesk` is declared as the primary font for every `<a>` element, but there is **no `@font-face` block for it** anywhere in the `<head>`. The six `@font-face` declarations load only `Open Sans`. Every link in the email falls back to Helvetica/sans-serif silently. If `Founders Grotesk` is intended, it must be added as a `@font-face` rule or the `a` selector must be corrected to `Open Sans`.

**Issue — Non-standard `white-space:pre` on `img`:**
```css
img { -ms-interpolation-mode:bicubic; white-space:pre; }
```
`white-space` is a non-inherited property that does not apply to replaced elements (`img`). It is harmless in most clients but signals a stale or copy-paste template artifact. Remove it to keep the stylesheet clean and avoid unexpected behavior in edge-case renderers.

**No issues found:** DOCTYPE (XHTML 1.0 Transitional), viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `X-UA-Compatible IE=edge`, VML namespace — all correct for broad email client support.
