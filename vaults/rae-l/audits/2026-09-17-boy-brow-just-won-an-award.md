# Boy Brow just won an award!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third single-SKU, no-discount Boy Brow send in two weeks (following 9/13's "Brow down"), and this time Glossier has an actual news hook — an Allure 2026 Best of Beauty award — instead of just recycled social proof. The award badge is a legitimate, credible reason to open, and it's a smart escalation from the plain product-worship angle nine days ago. But the execution is undercut by a real render problem: the hero image and the second module's headline/image both appear cropped off the right edge of the frame, cutting off text ("up brow." trails off-screen) and the product swatch shot.
- The content strategy is sound — award credibility, then heritage story, then a genuine 5-star testimonial from an 8-year user. That's a complete trust-building arc for a single SKU. But a subscriber skimming on mobile will see truncated copy and half-visible images, which undercuts the polish the "award winner" positioning is trying to project.
- Judgement: strong concept, sloppy execution — the layout bug caps this well below what the creative idea deserves.

## What's working

- The Allure "Best of Beauty 2026" badge is a real, specific trust signal — a step up from "Brow down"'s generic social proof.
- "An award-winning brow" headline is concrete and ties directly to the subject line.
- The testimonial block ("Boy Brow has been one of my Makeup staples for 8 years...") with 5-star rating and attribution ("—Sara") adds credible, specific social proof.
- Three distinct CTAs (Shop now / Get yours / Shop now) give repeated, low-friction paths to purchase.

## What's weak

- The hero product shot and the "boyish, brushed up brow" module are both cut off at the right edge — text and imagery run off-frame, a visible render bug.
- No discount or offer of any kind — after 9/13's "Brow down" also skipped a discount, this is now two consecutive no-promo sends for the same product, which risks fatigue without an incentive to act now.
- No urgency or timing signal — nothing makes "now" the moment to buy versus any other day.
- Swatch/shade image at the bottom is also cropped at the right, same layout issue as the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the layout crop first** — the hero and second-module images/text are bleeding off the right edge in this render; this is the single biggest fix since it makes the email look broken regardless of how good the creative concept is.
- 2. Add a light incentive (e.g., "free mini with any Boy Brow purchase") to convert the award-news traffic instead of relying on pure brand love two sends in a row.
- 3. Move the testimonial higher — "8 years and it never fails" is stronger proof than the heritage paragraph and could anchor the email closer to the top.
- 4. Show the award badge in the preview text so it surfaces in-inbox, not just in the hero image.
