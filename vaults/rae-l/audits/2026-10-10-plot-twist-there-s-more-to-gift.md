# Plot twist: there’s more to gift!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the second drop of the 2026 Holiday Gift Guide, and it's a pure product parade with no offer. The hero is "The 2026 Glossier Gift Guide" with a "Get gifting" button. Below it are three new lip products, each in its own image-and-copy block: Just Baked Balm Dotcom, Toasty Nuts Balm Dotcom and the Black Cherry Lip Line + Balm Duo. The art direction is strong. The food-styled photography, with the balm tube in a pile of candied almonds, is the best thing in the email. But there's no price, no deadline and no gift-with-purchase.
- A real subscriber would notice the cadence. The 10/2 teaser ("Something festive's right around the corner…"), the 10/6 "Our 2026 Holiday Gift Guide is here!" and now "Drop Two" make this the third gift-guide beat in nine days. Each beat has been polite and pretty, and none has given a reason to act today. The "Plot twist" subject promises a surprise, but the email contains no twist. It's new lip products.
- There are also visible layout bugs. The body copy in the right-hand text columns is clipped at the edge of the frame. The footer smiley/hand illustration is cropped too. For a brand this design-forward, that looks careless. Overall it's a good-looking, low-urgency catalog send, and I'd score it around 6.

## What's working

- The product photography is distinctive and on-brand. The Toasty Nuts tube in the almond pile is a strong, shareable visual.
- The "NEW!" eyebrow tags make it clear these are fresh launches.
- Each product has its own "Shop now" button, so a click goes to a specific product rather than a generic page.
- The alternating image-left and image-right layout gives the scroll a good rhythm.
- The copy has personality. "Everyone on your list is covered. Including you, obviously." fits the brand voice.

## What's weak

- There's no offer anywhere: no price, no set, no free shipping, no gift-with-purchase and no deadline. Nothing gives a reason to click now.
- Body copy is cut off on the right in the Just Baked block ("delici…", "comforting l…") and the Black Cherry block ("anything bu…"). It's a visible clipping bug.
- The large crumpled pink paper image near the bottom is a big empty-feeling block. It has no product and no information, and it's a recycled teaser visual from the 10/2 send.
- The gift guide is the hero, but the email below it is about individual lip products. The "Drop Two" framing doesn't pay off, and the closing "The gifting doesn't stop there" repeats the hero CTA.
- The subject's "plot twist" promise isn't delivered, and nothing in the email is time-bounded.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one concrete reason to act.** Use a gift set with a price, a "free mini with $X" or a ship-by date such as "order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery." This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Fix the clipped text columns** so the full body copy shows in the right-hand blocks. Re-check the footer illustration crop at the same time.
- 3. **Cut the crumpled-paper block, or put a product or gift set in it.** Merge the closing CTA into the hero so the email is shorter.
- 4. **Make the hero a specific gift** (a named set, priced) rather than a general "gift guide." Lead with the best-looking product, which is Toasty Nuts.
- 5. **Make the CTAs specific.** "Shop the Balm Dotcoms" or "Shop gifts under $30" beats "Get gifting" and the repeated "Shop now."
- 6. **Add social proof.** A star rating or "bestseller" tag on the lip products would help.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-sent template with structural HTML problems (a tracked `href` on a `<span>` and an unbalanced MSO conditional comment) and five content images flagged without alt text. The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results warnings are probably relay artifacts, so they are unconfirmed rather than failures. The provided source is truncated, so links, footer and tokens could only be partly assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracked URL on a non-link element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M4K4YADQF54MWAVK4JT7GKQF_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `href` and `rel` are invalid on `<span>`. It looks like a `<link rel="stylesheet">` that Klaviyo's link rewriter converted. It is inert, so it is not a functional break, but it adds a pointless tracked URL and fails HTML validation.
- **Third-party tracking domain:** Click and open tracking use `ctrk.klclick.com`, while the sender is `eml.glossier.com`. A tracking domain that doesn't match the sender can slightly hurt reputation and link-rewrite trust. This is a low-severity observation.
- **Link health:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 5 (footer, utility and social). It reported no failures, but nothing is confirmed about the 5 skipped links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 5 content images):** `heroimage_1_5.jpg`, `2colimageright_1.jpg`, `2colimageright_1_1.jpg`, `2colimageright_1_2.jpg` and `group_3971_6.jpg`, all on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_13942e29…`. With images blocked or a screen reader, the hero and 2-column content may render as nothing or as filenames. I can't tell from the QA output whether the `alt` attribute is absent or just empty.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M4K4YADQF54MWAVK4JT7GKQF` should carry `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions. This is minor.
- **Unbalanced conditional comment:** `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opens in `<head>`, but its closer `<!--<![endif]-->` appears after `<body>` begins. In Outlook (MSO), the intervening `</head><body>` is hidden inside the comment. Non-MSO clients are unaffected, and Word's parser is usually forgiving, so the real impact in Outlook is unverified.
- **Dead CSS selector:** The rule `u + .body img ~ div div { display:none }` expects a `.body` class, but the tag is a bare `<body>`. The Gmail fix it targets never applies, though the body tag may have been altered in truncation.
- **Pass:** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta tag and a hidden preheader with `mso-hide:all` are all present.
