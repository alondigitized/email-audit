# Last chance—Lip Sketch 4 Thru Line
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA closing out the Thru Line/Lip Sketch GWP arc with a hard deadline push — "tonight is your last chance," a "LAST CHANCE" badge on the hero, and a clean two-step visual (buy eyeliner → get free Lip Sketch mini) that's easier to parse than 08-24's "A gift—line for line" launch of the same offer. It's a tighter, more urgent execution of a promo this brand history has now run at least three times (08-19 eyeliner mini, 08-24 Lip Sketch GWP launch, now the 08-31 last-chance close), so a subscriber who's seen the prior sends will recognize this instantly as the closing beat, not new news.
- The hero image is striking (close-up lash/liner shot) but the deadline itself — "tonight" — never gets a clock, date, or time, which is the one thing a last-chance email needs to be airtight on. The rest of the email is standard ILIA scaffolding: a well-organized "More That Draws the Eye" cross-sell grid and the recurring "in safe hands" clean-beauty closer.
- Given the offer clarity and urgency framing, this beats the 08-26 lip roundup and 08-22 skin tint retread, but the vague deadline and heavy repetition of a now-familiar GWP mechanic caps it below the 08-20 "prove it" high point.

## What's working

- "LAST CHANCE" badge in top-right of hero image is immediately visible and unambiguous.
- Two-step "Shop Thru Line → Get mini Lip Sketch" visual clearly explains the mechanic without requiring copy-reading.
- Product benefit bullets (24-hour wear, waterproof, bleed-resistant, etc.) are legible and specific under both featured products.
- "LINE UP" and "SHOP TO CLAIM" are on-brand, punny CTAs that are still clear in intent.
- Cross-sell grid ("More That Draws the Eye") is clean, evenly spaced, and each tile has its own visible CTA.

## What's weak

- No actual time or date on the deadline — "tonight is your last chance" gives no hour, timezone, or countdown, so urgency is asserted but not proven.
- This is the third send built around the same Thru Line/Lip Sketch GWP mechanic in under two weeks (08-19, 08-24, now 08-31) — fatigue risk for anyone who opened the earlier two.
- "LAST CHANCE" badge also appears on two cross-sell tiles (Eye Stylus Shadow Stick, Bright Start Retinol Eye Cream) which dilutes the badge's meaning — if everything is last chance, nothing is.
- Subject doesn't repeat the specific deadline urgency visible in the hero copy, so open-stage urgency is weaker than the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hard deadline to the hero copy itself — "tonight is your last chance" → "Offer ends tonight at 11:59pm ET" — this is the single highest-leverage fix since urgency without a timestamp reads as filler.
- 2. Remove the "LAST CHANCE" badge from the two unrelated cross-sell tiles so it stays a one-time signal tied only to the GWP offer.
- 3. Reference the % or minimum spend detail (if any) near "SHOP TO CLAIM" — right now the offer is "free gift with order" but no threshold is visible, so add it or confirm none exists.
- 4. Vary the CTA copy across sends — "LINE UP" and "SHOP TO CLAIM" are fine here, but three sends in a row on the same mechanic risk blurring together; a countdown-style CTA like "CLAIM BEFORE MIDNIGHT" would differentiate this as the closer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Midnight cutoff — grab your free mini before it's gone.`
