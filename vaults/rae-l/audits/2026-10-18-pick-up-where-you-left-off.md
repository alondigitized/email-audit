# Pick up where you left off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment shell again, and the 10/17 send "Don't leave these styles behind!" already did the "we saved these for you" version of it. The new copy is "DID YOU FORGET SOMETHING?", which suggests a cart abandon. The body doesn't back that up. It's a six-shoe grid of women's styles with no cart item, no price and no hero. The strongest part is the navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner, valid through 10/21. It's the same offer as the day before, so this works as a reminder send.
- The layout is clean and nothing is broken. The top half has a lot of white space around a generic headline and a "SHOP NOW" button that goes nowhere specific. The 20% offer only appears below the grid, so a reader has to scroll past six unpriced shoes to find the one reason to act. Below that, a long stack of footer modules (category links, texting, app, curbside, pay later, social) dilutes the click.
- Judgement: competent but forgettable, and the subject is fine but generic. The email's value is the 20% offer, and it's hidden. It also lands one day after a near-identical send, so cadence is working against it.

## What's working

- The navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner has strong contrast, and its large numeral is the clearest hierarchy moment in the email.
- The offer has a credible deadline ("Valid thru 10/21") with fine print below it.
- The six-product grid is tidy and evenly spaced. All the images load and the product names are descriptive.
- The blue "SHOP NOW" button under the headline is unmistakable.
- The women's skew is consistent across all six styles, so the email reads as targeted.

## What's weak

- The 20% offer sits below the fold, under the product grid, instead of in the hero.
- "DID YOU FORGET SOMETHING?" implies a cart, but no specific item is shown. The grid is generic, with no prices, reviews or sizes.
- The product names are small underlined text with no price or sale marker, so none of them signals value.
- The footer is long and has six or more competing modules (category list, text signup, app, curbside, pay later, store finder, social).
- This arrives one day after "Don't leave these styles behind!", which had the same 20% member offer and the same shell.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% member banner into the hero, directly under the headline, and make the first button read "Take 20% off".
- 2. Feature the one item the recipient actually viewed as a larger hero, with its price and the member price. Cut the grid to 3 related styles.
- 3. Add prices, or strikethrough prices, under each product, plus one proof point such as a star rating.
- 4. Cut the footer to the category links plus one secondary module, and drop the app, curbside and pay-later blocks.
- 5. Suppress or space this send from the 10/17 abandonment email. Fire it only if the recipient didn't click, and escalate the offer or the deadline rather than repeating it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% member discount ends 10/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking about these? 20% off inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on select full-priced styles through 10/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your saved styles are waiting. Take 20% off before Tuesday.`

It also loads 10 images over plain `http://`, has no plain-text part, and has an empty `<title>`. Several header-level checks could not be completed through the AgentMail relay.

  - Token-based unsubscribe endpoints often reject non-browser probes, so this may be a probe artifact. I can't confirm it is a real break from this data.

- **Authentication-Results.** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Verify from the original headers.
- **Plain-text part.** The text version is 0 chars (FAIL). The relay may not capture it, but if it is truly absent, that hurts deliverability and accessibility. Multipart/alternative should include a text part.
- **Physical address.** I can't see a postal address in the truncated source, so it is unverified.

- **Landing page alignment.** The abandon-PLP product and CTA links weren't in the probed or visible data, so landing-page continuity is unverified.

5. Confirm the email ships a multipart text/plain part.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the original message headers, since the relay likely masked them.
7. Raise the probe budget, or run a manual pass, to cover the 23 skipped links, especially the product CTAs.
