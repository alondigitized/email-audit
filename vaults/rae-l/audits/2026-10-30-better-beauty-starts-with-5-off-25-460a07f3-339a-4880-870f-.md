# Better beauty starts with $5 off $25
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth push of the same $5 off $25 loyalty-signup offer in about four weeks. The earlier sends were 10/02, 10/11, 10/17 and 10/19. The creative is clean and the offer is clear: a big pink hero reading "Join Sally.Rewards & save on your wish list," a black "Join Sally.Rewards" button, and a holiday-flavored gift-wrap photo. The problem is repetition. A subscriber who ignored the 10/19 "New to rewards? Get $5 off $25" send has no new reason to act now.
- The body is well organized. The Preferred and Elite tier comparison is easy to scan, and the Klarna strip and service icons are tidy. But it adds weight without adding urgency. The only deadline is "in October" in the hero copy. "Expires 10/31/2026" appears only in the fine print, so a deadline that should drive the click is buried.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand template with a stale offer and a hidden deadline. It fits loyalty acquisition, but it does little to pull a lapsed or already-enrolled subscriber forward.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: a large pink block, one headline, one offer and one black CTA button.
- The black buttons contrast well against the pink, and the CTA is repeated after the tier comparison.
- The Preferred vs. Elite two-column card makes the program tiers easy to understand. Free entry is clear, and the $200+ threshold for Elite is stated.
- The lifestyle photo (a hand wrapping a gift next to a nail product) suits the holiday season and the beauty category.
- The top utility banners (2-hour delivery, 15% off buy online/pick up in store) add secondary value without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The email is the fifth send of the same $5 off $25 offer in four weeks. Compare "Join today, get $5 off $25" on 10/17 and "New to rewards? Get $5 off $25" on 10/19.
- The deadline is soft. The hero says "in October," and the exact 10/31 expiry is only in the fine print, which is very small.
- The two red banners at the top offer a different deal (15% off buy online/pick up in store) and compete with the hero for attention.
- The body is long and spends a lot of space on tier details, the Klarna strip and the app download. It dilutes a single-offer email.
- The hero photo shows a nail product and a gift, but nothing connects it to a specific product or category, so the click has no product anchor.
- The fine print is dense, with minimum-purchase and "new account" conditions that a reader only finds at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the deadline the headline. Use "Last day: $5 off $25 when you join" and put the date next to the button. Since this is the final day of the offer, urgency is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Segment. Send this only to non-members, and send existing members a product or holiday-gift email instead.
- 3. Cut the lower half. Drop the Klarna strip, the icon row and the app block, and keep one tier explainer and one button.
- 4. Add a concrete example, such as "Spend $25, pay $20," next to the CTA so the saving is visible at a glance.
- 5. Pick one top banner, or move the secondary offers below the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: $5 off $25 when you join`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $5 reward expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join free before 11:59 p.m. ET and take $5 off your first $25.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus points on every purchase and birthday surprises, all free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Sally Beauty email from `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com` ("Better beauty starts with $5 off $25"). The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA output. The main confirmed defects are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a tracking pixel, an HTTP-only pixel, and several pixels with no alt text. The header warnings can't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **47 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro in a tracking pixel.** The `myvisualiq` impression pixel is `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...`. The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was not substituted by the ad server, so the cache-buster is the same for every send. Clients that cache the image may undercount impressions.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel.** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy tolerates it, but other clients may block it as mixed content, and the pixel will fail to fire.
- **Empty image src.** One `<img>` has an empty `src` (QA output `: src: `). An empty src can trigger a request back to the document URL in some clients and renders as a broken image.
- **Five or more third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net`, `dotomi.com`, and others. They add load and privacy-filter exposure (Apple MPP, Outlook blocking), but this is a minor risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 images.** These are the empty-src image and the three tracking pixels: `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, and `UCMController`.
  - Pixels should have `alt=""` explicitly, and ideally `width="1" height="1"`. Without an `alt` attribute, some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
  - The `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` filename is read aloud as the image name when alt is absent.
- **Font fallback.** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font that has no `@font-face` in the visible CSS (only Open Sans is declared). Most clients will fall back to Helvetica. This is minor and may be handled in the truncated portion.
- **Markup nit.** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. This is harmless in email clients.
- **Positive.** The viewport meta tag, MSO conditional comments, and the `max-width:499px` mobile breakpoint are present. Full responsive behavior can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq URL (see §2).
- **Subscriber ID in a pixel path.** `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2330383/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` has a trailing segment that looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix). It is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party URL. This is a privacy and data-governance consideration rather than a rendering bug.
- No visible merge tokens such as `{{first_name}}` or `%%name%%` appear in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Sally Beauty sends at volume, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it is probably present at origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Verify against the original message headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address can't be checked.
- The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** All 47 links are on tracking and redirect domains and weren't resolved. UTM parameters, landing page alignment with the "$5 off $25" offer, and promo-code carryover can't be confirmed.
- **Dotomi pixel parameters.** The `dtm_cid=2990` and `cli_promo_id` parameters suggest a promo ID is passed to a retargeting platform. The value is truncated, so it can't be checked against the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro** in the myvisualiq pixel so the ad platform substitutes it at send time, or remove the pixel.
2. **Serve the dotomi pixel over HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`).
3. **Remove the empty-src `<img>`**, or give it a valid source.
4. **Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`** to all tracking pixels.
5. **Re-verify headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) on a direct-delivery copy to a seed inbox. The relay is the likely cause of the missing headers.
6. **Resolve the redirect chains** with a follow-redirect probe to confirm UTMs and the landing page match the offer.
7. **Review the Salesforce Contact ID** in the `mon-trk` pixel path for privacy and data-sharing disclosure.
