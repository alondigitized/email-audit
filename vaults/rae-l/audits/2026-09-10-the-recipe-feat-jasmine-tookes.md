# The Recipe feat. Jasmine Tookes
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA doing an influencer-editorial "recipe" send — model/makeup artist Jasmine Tookes walking through a 3-step monochromatic eye look, with the Paint/Sketch/Frame product steps stacked underneath a hero shot and pull-quote. It's a well-executed version of the celebrity-tutorial format the brand has leaned on before (echoes of the 09-05 "Build a look around blush" routine send), and visually it's clean: consistent typography, clear step numbering, good product photography with swatches.
- The problem is the offer, not the execution. A GWP banner ("FREE MINI EYE STYLUS IN BEAM W/ $75+") sits at the very top, but the actual free-gift CTA block doesn't appear until near the bottom of a very long scroll, and the primary mid-email CTAs ("SHOP THE RECIPE," "SHOP NOW") are generic, not tied to a single specific product or shade. This is a beautifully art-directed brand story that under-monetizes itself — strong for brand equity, weak for driving an immediate cart.

## What's working

- Clear narrative structure — Paint/Sketch/Frame steps are numbered, easy to scan, and each pairs a product shot with a one-line usage tip.
- Strong, on-brand hero image and consistent muted color palette (blush/mauve/cream) throughout.
- Celebrity/founder credibility is used well — Jasmine Tookes' quote and bio ("Model. Mother. Founder of Brunel") add authenticity beyond a generic model shot.
- GWP threshold and mechanic are stated clearly twice (top banner + dedicated block) with a "*See site for details" disclosure instead of buried fine print.
- No render bugs — images, swatches, and text all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- The email is very long (multiple full scrolls) before reaching the "SHOP NOW" and "CLAIM NOW" buttons — high risk of drop-off on mobile.
- Primary CTAs ("SHOP THE RECIPE," "SHOP NOW") are generic verbs with no specific product/shade named, unlike the click-friendly, specific-product pattern from the 09-01 Overglaze launch.
- No price, no discount — this is a GWP-only send, which caps the offer strength versus recent sends with clearer value props.
- Three products are introduced but the email never bundles them into one obvious "get the whole look" purchase path (e.g., a single kit/bundle CTA).

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a single, specific CTA ("Shop the Eye Stylus in Relic") directly under the hero/quote instead of a generic "SHOP THE RECIPE" button — specificity drives clicks more than a broad category link.
- 2. Add a "Shop the Look" bundle CTA that lets readers buy all three products (Stylus, Eyeliner, Mascara) in one click, rather than forcing three separate browses.
- 3. Pull the GWP mechanic higher — reference the free mini earlier in body copy (not just the top banner) so it survives if the banner gets clipped by inbox preview.
- 4. Shorten the email — consider cutting to 2 steps or condensing product shots so "SHOP NOW" and "CLAIM NOW" are reachable within 1–2 scrolls on mobile.
