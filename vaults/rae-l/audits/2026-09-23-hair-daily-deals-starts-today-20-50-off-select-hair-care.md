# Hair Daily Deals starts today 🎉 20–50% off select hair care.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's fourth flavor of daily-deals creative this week, and it's the most category-specific one yet: a dedicated Hair Daily Deals send with an actual hero product grid (Vegamour, Shark Beauty, Amika) instead of the generic "50% off Select Beauty" banner they've been running since 9/15 and 9/18. The hero is clean — "Get up to 50% OFF³ Hair Faves" stacked with a free same-day delivery offer and a "TODAY ONLY" badge — and the specific price call-outs ($64→$44.80, $299→$209.30) are a genuine improvement over the vaguer sends earlier this week.
- Where it falls apart is length and repetition of structure: this is a marathon scroll (six-plus distinct offer modules — hair deals, markdown calendar, value-size hair care, holiday teaser, fragrance teaser, value sets, Beauty Insider, footer) that dilutes the "TODAY ONLY" urgency by page 3. The 7-day markdown calendar grid is a nice loyalty-building touch for a daily shopper, but it's visually dense and easy to skip past on mobile.
- Net: stronger and more targeted than 9/18 or 9/20, on par with 9/17's stacked-offer send, but still suffering from the same "too many offers in one email" problem that's dogged this brand all week.

## What's working

- Category-specific hero copy ("Hair Daily Deals," "Hair Faves") directly matches the subject line — no bait-and-switch.
- Real price anchors are visible on the hero products (crossed-out original price next to sale price), which builds trust.
- "TODAY ONLY" badge plus a hard deadline ("Ends 10/7 at 1pm PT") gives credible urgency.
- The 7-day markdown calendar is a smart loyalty/repeat-visit hook for a daily-deal shopper persona.
- Clean product photography with no overlapping text or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long with at least six separate offer blocks after the hero (value-size hair, holiday, fragrance, value sets, Insider, footer) — attention drops off a cliff after the first two screens.
- "Get FREE Same-Day Delivery" is a secondary, unrelated offer competing with the hair discount for the same hero real estate, which splits focus in the most important above-the-fold space.
- The markdown calendar grid is legible but visually busy — small text, dense columns, easy to skim past without registering the deals.
- Unrelated fragrance/holiday teaser blocks near the bottom dilute what should be a focused hair-care send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the hero + hair deals + markdown calendar; move fragrance/holiday/value-set teasers to a separate send — this email is trying to be three emails at once.
- 2. Simplify the hero to one offer: either the 50% off hair or the free same-day delivery, not both stacked in the same visual block.
- 3. Enlarge the markdown calendar type size or convert it to a simpler 3-day preview with a "see full week" link, since it's currently too dense to scan on mobile.
- 4. Add a specific top hair product image directly under the hero CTA button so the eye has one clear next action.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your hair routine, up to 50% off (ends today)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free same-day delivery with code DELIVERED`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop markdowns on Vegamour, Amika, K18 and more`
