# Finish your gift list today
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand holiday send, but it is vague. The red hero reads "Finishing touches for the season," followed by "Signature scents & skin care staples, plus free 2-hour delivery," and a black Shop now button. The subject, "Finish your gift list today," promises gifting urgency. The body delivers a generic "Shop Your Favorites" grid of four unrelated hair-care and nail items, with no gift framing at all.
- The strongest real offer, BOGO 50% off the Big Bottle Sale, sits below the fold. It is the same offer as the 11/01 and 11/06 "Shop Early Black Friday Deals!" sends, so subscribers have seen it three times now. The hero has no price, no percentage and no deadline. The model photo is a skin-care shot, and the products below are hair color tools, a tint brush and nail glue. Hero, grid and subject don't tell one story.
- Judgement: tidy execution and a good seasonal angle, undermined by a missing hero offer and a mismatch between the promise and the content. It's a middle-of-the-road send, and it's weaker than the single-offer 11/08 "Black Friday came early 👀" email.

## What's working

- The red hero with the gold-glitter bow divider reads as holiday at a glance, and the headline is large and legible.
- "Free 2-hour delivery" is a concrete, useful benefit that fits last-minute gifting.
- The Big Bottle Sale block has strong contrast, with a black background, gold glitter and clear product shots, and its Shop now button stands out.
- The Klarna strip and the icon row (free shipping over $40, 2-hour delivery, pick up in store) lower purchase friction.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero has no price, discount or deadline. "Signature scents & skin care staples" doesn't match the hair-care and nail products shown below.
- Nothing in the body says "gift". The "Shop Your Favorites" grid is four random items. Two are ion, one is a nail glue and one is a tint brush, and none is gift-worthy.
- All four product tiles have the same generic "Shop now" link, with no prices and no reviews. The TikTok badge on the nail glue is the only social proof.
- The one real offer, BOGO 50% off, is buried in the lower third and repeats the 11/01 and 11/06 sends.
- The subject promises a "gift list today", but the hero image is a close-up of a woman touching her cheek. It has no gift or holiday cue other than the bow.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific offer in the hero, such as "Buy one, get one 50% off, ends tonight," with a deadline. Then swap the grid for gift-ready picks (sets, fragrances, stocking stuffers) with prices.
- 2. Keep the hero and products in one category and one story. If it's gifting, show gifts. If it's skin care, show skin care.
- 3. Move the Big Bottle Sale up to second position, or drop it if it was in the last two sends. Vary the creative.
- 4. Replace the generic "Shop now" links with "Shop gifts under $25" or "Shop the 2-hour delivery picks". Add star ratings.
- 5. Tie the 4pm cutoff in the footer to the "today" urgency. Mention "order by 4pm for 2-hour delivery" in the hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by 4pm: BOGO 50% off + same-day gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 4pm and pick up in store or get it in 2 hours.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with MSO/VML support and a responsive media query. Automated QA passed 57% with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main concerns are third-party tracking pixels (one with an unresolved macro, one over plain HTTP), the recipient's email address exposed in image URLs, and header-level compliance checks that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed:** 51 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains. Destination resolution, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. It was never substituted, so the pixel is likely cached or deduplicated and impression counts will be unreliable.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over HTTP. Clients and proxies may block it, and it can trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Stacked third-party trackers:** Evergage, `pxl.mon-trk.com` (`003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`), MyVisualIQ and Dotomi are all loaded in one message. This adds request overhead and more places for failures.
- **Empty `src` attributes:** two `<img>` elements have `src=""`. These can trigger a request to the document URL in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** the four Evergage `summary.png` images (`.../campaign/88L7Q/YBZc1/{1..4}/summary.png`) have no alt text. They look like dynamic content blocks rather than pixels, so with images blocked those slots have no text equivalent.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` have no alt. For 1×1 pixels the fix is `alt=""`, which is low severity.
- **Font stack mismatch:** the `a` rule sets `font-family:'Founders Grotesk'`, but no `@font-face` for it appears in the visible source (only Open Sans). It falls back to Helvetica, so rendering will differ from what was intended.
- **No other issues visible:** the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and a `<title>` are present, and Outlook is handled with MSO conditionals. The source was truncated, so the rest of the markup is unchecked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Email address in image URLs:** the Evergage URLs carry `userId=rae-l@ete...` (truncated in the QA output). The recipient's email appears in plain text in image URLs, which are logged by proxies, CDNs and image caches. Confirm whether it is hashed or encoded.
- **Unresolved macro:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the MyVisualIQ pixel (see section 2).
- **No other unrendered tokens:** none were found in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA could not find either header. The report notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these were not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none could be verified, because the 51 click links go through redirect domains and were never resolved.
- **Landing page alignment:** the final destinations were not captured, so alignment with the "Finish your gift list" subject can't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the MyVisualIQ pixel with a real per-send token, or remove the pixel.
2. Move the Dotomi pixel to HTTPS (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`) or drop it.
3. Add `alt` text to the four Evergage `summary.png` images, and use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. Remove or fix the two `<img src="">` elements.
5. Stop putting the recipient's email in the `userId=` query parameter. Use an opaque or hashed ID.
6. Check the raw original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating these as defects.
7. Resolve the 51 skipped tracking links to confirm final destinations and UTM tagging.
8. Declare the `Founders Grotesk` font or remove it from the stack, so the font fallback is intentional.
