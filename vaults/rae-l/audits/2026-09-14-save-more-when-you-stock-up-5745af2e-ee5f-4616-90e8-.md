# Save More When You Stock Up 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is another Appiversary-branded send, and it repeats the exact structural problem I flagged on 2026-09-01: the subject promises a concrete deal ("Save More When You Stock Up 🎉") but the hero doesn't deliver it. The first screen says "Our Appiversary is still going strong" with a vague "Check the app weekly" CTA and a "Shop in app" button — the actual tiered offer ($10 off $40 / $20 off $80 / $30 off $100, code SAVEMORE) is buried in a secondary red band below the hero, not the headline.
- Once you scroll to that red band, the offer is genuinely good — tiered, specific, code-based, dated (ends 9/21). But it's competing with an app-download pitch (again — this brand leans on "download the app" as a crutch almost every send this month), a hero model with no product context beyond a spray bottle, and a product grid (four "ion" haircare SKUs) that has zero visible connection to "stock up and save."
- Compared to 9/10's "$8 OPI polish" send (8/10, my top score for this brand), this doesn't have the same subject-to-hero coherence. It's a middling execution of a decent underlying offer.

## What's working

- The tiered discount block ($10/$40, $20/$80, $30/$100) is specific, legible, and has a real deadline (9/21).
- Promo code SAVEMORE is visible and unambiguous.
- Product grid images are clean, no broken renders or overlapping text anywhere in the email.
- Utility footer (In-Store / Online / Free Shipping $40+ / 2 Hour Delivery / BOIS) is a nice consistent trust builder.

## What's weak

- Hero doesn't mention the stock-up/save offer at all — it pushes "check the app weekly," which is a completely different message than the subject line.
- Primary hero CTA ("Shop in app") requires an app download/open, adding friction before the recipient can even see the offer that got them to open the email.
- Product grid (ion haircare) has no visible tie to "stock up and save" — no pricing, no bundle framing, just four SKUs with generic "Shop now" links.
- A second, unrelated offer ($14.99 bondbar Essential Kit, ends 9/14 — today) is dropped in below the grid, further diluting the "stock up" narrative and creating urgency-code confusion (three different deadlines: 9/14, 9/21, and implied ongoing "weekly" app deals).

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the hero with the tiered offer, not the app-check pitch.** Put "$10 off $40 | $20 off $80 | $30 off $100" as the hero headline with SAVEMORE prominently displayed — that's what the subject sold, deliver it in the first screen.
- 2. **Make the primary CTA "Shop Now" (web), not "Shop in app."** Forcing an app download before showing the deal is the single biggest drop-off risk here.
- 3. **Cut or clearly separate the bondbar kit offer.** It has its own deadline (today, 9/14) and undercuts the stock-up message — give it its own send or move it well below with a clear divider.
- 4. **Tie the product grid to the offer.** Show prices or a "spend $40, save $10" framing next to the ion products so the grid reinforces the hero deal instead of floating disconnected.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$10-$30 Off When You Stock Up 🎉`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy More, Save More — Ends 9/21`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVEMORE at checkout on in-app purchases`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tiered savings up to $30 off, ends September 21`
