# Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection 💄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises one thing and the body leads with another. "Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection" is a clear, honest offer. But the 30% banner is a thin strip at the top, and the email's real hero is "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" with a large Jo Malone London Holiday Collection feature. A subscriber who opens for the discount has to look for it. The Jo Malone block doesn't mention Sephora Collection at all.
- Below the hero it becomes a long, dense catalog: eight "New / Only at Sephora" items with prices, then "Spot it. Shop it.", Lip Gift Sets, Skincare Gift Sets, a Holiday Gift Guide nav, and a footer barcode for in-store redemption. The render is clean and on-brand, with no visible bugs. The problem is focus. At about 6,900px tall, with competing CTAs ("Shop Now", "Shop Now", "Shop Now", category tiles), the 30% offer gets diluted.
- This is also the latest in a tight run of near-identical Sephora Savings Event sends. The 10/30 "STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection" and 10/31 "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍" emails carried the same offer. The 10/30 one led with it properly and scored 8. This one is the weakest execution of the same promo. My verdict is a competent send that undersells its own offer.

## What's working

- The subject states a specific, quantified offer (30%) on a named line (Sephora Collection).
- The banner at the top shows the dates (10/30–11/9) and the code ALLONSALE, so the offer is credible and time-bounded.
- Product tiles show names, sizes, and prices with strikethrough values (e.g. "$68 ($92 value)"). That makes the gift sets feel like real deals.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "Clean" badges add useful trust and exclusivity cues.
- The in-store barcode at the bottom, with the 30% and 10% terms, makes the offer easy to redeem.

## What's weak

- The 30% banner is a small strip. The big "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" and Jo Malone hero pushes the subject's promised offer below the eye line.
- The Jo Malone collection is not Sephora Collection, so the hero doesn't match the subject. It reads like a bait-and-switch.
- The email is very long and has many competing CTAs. There is no single dominant button tied to the 30% offer.
- The CTAs are generic ("Shop Now"). The product tiles don't show any reviews or ratings.
- The deal terms (30% on Sephora Collection, an extra 10% on other items from 11/3, exclusions) sit in tiny footer print and the barcode block. This is the 4th or so promo for the same event in a week, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the 30% Sephora Collection offer the hero: a large badge, the code, and the dates above the fold, with a single "Shop Sephora Collection" button. Move Jo Malone and the gift guide below it.
- 2. Show 4–6 Sephora Collection products with sale prices and ratings in the first scroll. Cut the catalog sections that aren't part of the offer.
- 3. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with specific ones: "Shop 30% off Sephora Collection", "Shop gift sets".
- 4. Add a real end-date line near the top ("Ends 11/9"). If a later send in this run is a last-chance email, make that the urgency beat.
- 5. Put the 10% Insider and in-store barcode terms in a short visible callout instead of the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Sephora Collection ends 11/9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Sephora Collection code: ALLONSALE 💄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code ALLONSALE on lips, eyes & face through 11/9, plus gift sets from $26.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Insider, your 30% savings is live: shop bestsellers before the holiday rush.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** both pixel images above have no `alt`. Impact is low because they are non-content images. Adding `alt=""` would be correct.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper uses `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The only mobile handling is a `max-width:480px` media query, and the table won't shrink on narrow clients that ignore it.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments don't work there, so the rule is likely ignored or breaks parsing of the following rules.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no effect in email clients.
- **JSON-LD:** the `DiscountOffer` has an empty `"discountCode": ""`, and the `PromotionCard` block has no properties. Most clients strip the script, and Gmail may ignore it as incomplete.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The preheader renders "Sephora Shopper, now's the time to stock up." as a resolved generic fallback, and no unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. I can't check the truncated remainder.
