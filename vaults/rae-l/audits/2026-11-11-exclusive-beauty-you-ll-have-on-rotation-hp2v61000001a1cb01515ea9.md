# Exclusive beauty you’ll have on rotation
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a Gisou-led holiday gift send from Sephora Insider. The top is strong. A "Complimentary Gisou" banner with code GIFTGISOU (online only, $30+ purchase) sits above an "ONLY AT SEPHORA" hero with a $45 ($54 value) lip mask and oil set. The gift sets are real, priced products with "value" claims, which makes the offer feel concrete.
- The problem is focus. The email stacks a free-gift banner, a Gisou hero, a Gisou gift-set block, a Gisou bestsellers row, an 8-product "Great Gifts" grid, two promo tiles (mini sets and makeup gifts), a gift guide, and a long list of footer perks. The subject, "Exclusive beauty you'll have on rotation," promises something general and never mentions Gisou, gifts, or the free item. The eye has nowhere to settle after the hero.
- Overall this is a competent, product-rich holiday catalog email with a weak subject and too many competing blocks. It's a solid 6.

## What's working

- The "Complimentary Gisou" banner with a code gives a clear free-gift hook right at the top.
- The hero shows a specific $45 ($54 value) set with an "ONLY AT SEPHORA" label and a SHOP NOW button.
- The product grid shows named brands, prices, and value claims such as "$74" and "$56 value". Badges like "NEW", "LIMITED EDITION" and the Clean at Sephora seal add useful detail.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." and gift-guide category buttons (Skincare, Makeup, Fragrance, Hair) let shoppers browse by category.
- The branding is consistent: the Sephora logo and the gold and black palette match what the brand has sent before.

## What's weak

- Nothing at the top is a clear primary CTA. "SHOP TO REDEEM" and "SHOP NOW" compete within a single screen.
- The free Gisou offer needs $30+ and a code, and "*Exclusions/terms apply" is tiny. That reads as fine print, which hurts how honest the offer feels.
- The email is very long, with a Gisou section, a second grid, promo tiles and a footer list of perks. Value is diluted, and the Gisou story (hero, gift sets, bestsellers) is repeated three times.
- The subject doesn't match the content. A shopper expecting "beauty on rotation" gets a Gisou gift-set push.
- There is no Insider or loyalty benefit up top. Sephora's recent savings emails (11-07 through 11-09) led with Insider 10%, and that member pricing is gone here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject name the actual hook, the free Gisou gift, and echo it in the preheader.
- 2. Cut the top to one offer and one button. Merge "Complimentary Gisou" and the hero, and put the code in the button area.
- 3. Trim the repeated Gisou blocks to a single hero plus one bestseller row.
- 4. Move the terms for the free gift next to the code in readable type.
- 5. Bring back an Insider tie-in, such as a points or member-price line, near the top.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Gisou Hair Oil trial size with $30+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gisou's $45 lip + hair set is only at Sephora`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code GIFTGISOU online. Plus 8 new holiday sets to gift.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Full-size lip mask and oil, $54 value for $45. Limited edition.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible HTML is mostly sound. The one confirmed defect is a missing plain-text part (0 chars). Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them, and the HTML was truncated before the footer and most links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage is partial.** QA probed 12 of 59 links and skipped 47. No probe failures were reported, but the skipped links, including most footer and utility links, are unverified.
- **Redirect wrappers hide destinations.** CTAs use opaque tracked redirects such as `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1cb01515ea919916e96638858/4fa26791.../4fa26791-4486-4350-b2a0-6b802f79fd23?__dU__=...`. The final landing URLs can't be seen from the source, so they can't be checked.
- **Two tracking pixels are present.** The first is `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF...` (open pixel, hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all`). The second is `mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4958433775694848&mi_ecmp=20261111_Exclusive_Gifts`. Neither is a defect. The second carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Both tracking images lack `alt`** (QA warnings). Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Likely broken CSS in the mobile `@media` block.** A `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` conditional comment sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` CSS block. Browsers don't parse HTML comments or conditionals inside CSS. That probably invalidates `img.mso-size` and may also drop the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, because the stray `-->` becomes part of its selector. This is parser-dependent and I haven't confirmed it by rendering.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. Most email clients ignore this tag.
- **Fixed-width wrapper.** `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">` has no override in the visible mobile rules, so it may force horizontal scroll on narrow clients. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm this.
- **Minor:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid (it should be `noindex`) and has no effect in email. `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The `DiscountOffer` block has `"discountCode": ""`, and the `PromotionCard` block has no properties. Gmail will ignore both, so the markup does nothing.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tier-specific classes (`mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `mobileHeaderTierVIB`, `mobileHeaderTierBI`) suggest conditional tier modules. I can't verify them from the truncated HTML.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is missing** (0 chars). This is a deliverability and accessibility defect. If AgentMail strips the text part, confirm it in the raw message.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the relay not capturing them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation. Check the raw `.eml`, since Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `shop@beauty.sephora.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated source doesn't show them. QA states an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
The source gives no basis to assess this. No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible links, which use `__dU__` and path-based redirect tokens. Only the pixel carries a campaign identifier (`mi_ecmp=20261111_Exclusive_Gifts`). To check UTM and landing-page alignment, follow the redirect chain on the 12 probed links and record the final URLs and parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative (fixes the FAIL).
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the original message, not the relay copy, before treating them as defects.
