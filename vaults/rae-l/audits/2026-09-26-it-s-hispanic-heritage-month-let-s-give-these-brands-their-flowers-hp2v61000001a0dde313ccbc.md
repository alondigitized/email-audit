# It’s Hispanic Heritage Month—let’s give these brands their flowers 🌺🌼🌸
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora leaning into a heritage-month tentpole rather than another daily-deals spin, and structurally it's one of the more thoughtful sends in the recent run — a real narrative (founder quotes, brand stories) instead of a flat product grid. But it buries its own lede: the top banner promotes "Up to 30% off Hair Faves," a completely unrelated offer, before the Hispanic Heritage Month content even starts, so the hero message and the actual subject-line promise are fighting each other visually.
- Once you scroll past that banner, the execution is genuinely warm — founder photos, direct quotes ("Rodrigo Peñafiel and Near Tayares, Founders"), and a clean brand-by-brand structure (AORA Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia, Rare Beauty) that feels editorial rather than promotional. The "Founders Face to Face" video block is a nice touch of social proof/authenticity.
- The problem is length and offer clarity: this is a very long scroll with six+ brand sections before any transactional CTA reappears, and there's no unifying discount tied to the heritage-month story itself — it's essentially a curated storefront with "shop and support" links, not a deal. Verdict: strong storytelling, weak commercial hook — good for brand equity, mediocre for a deals-focused subscriber base that's been trained all week to expect a number in the subject line.

## What's working

- Founder quotes and photos (AORA Mexico, Ceremonia, Rare Beauty/Selena Gomez) give the email a human, credible voice instead of generic ad copy.
- Clear, consistent "Shop and Support" CTA repeated under each brand section — unambiguous even if not urgent.
- The colorful papel-picado-style banner graphic under "Unidos, Somos Más" is a nice, specific cultural visual rather than generic stock imagery.
- Product tiles are clean, well-lit, with prices and "New"/"Online Only" badges clearly legible.
- The "Founders Face to Face" video CTA adds a distinct, non-shopping engagement point.

## What's weak

- The top banner ("Up to 30% off Hair Faves") is a jarring, off-theme distraction sitting above the Hispanic Heritage Month hero — it competes with the subject line's promise before the reader even gets there.
- No single hero offer tied to the heritage-month theme itself — six-plus brand blocks with no discount anchor makes this feel like a catalog, not a campaign.
- The email is extremely long (six brand sections plus a points offer, a haircare sale, and a credit card pitch at the bottom) — high risk of drop-off before reaching anything transactional.
- Unrelated offers (4X points on K-beauty, 20% off value-size hair care, credit card 25% off) tacked on after the heritage content dilute the narrative focus established up top.
- Visual hierarchy doesn't clearly separate "story" from "shop" — Cadence-wise, this is the brand's first heritage-month specific send this week, but it reads more like an evergreen "shop these founders" page than a timely campaign moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or move the "Up to 30% off Hair Faves" banner below the heritage-month content — don't let an unrelated deal lead the email when the subject line promises something else.
- 2. Add one unifying offer (e.g., "15% off Hispanic Heritage Month brands" or a bundle) so there's a concrete reason to act now, not just browse.
- 3. Shorten to 3-4 featured brands with a "see all heritage-month brands" link, rather than a full six-section scroll — protect completion rate.
- 4. Move "Founders Face to Face" video higher, right after the hero — it's the most differentiated, scroll-stopping content in the email.
- 5. Drop or clearly separate the K-beauty points/haircare/credit-card blocks into a distinct "also this week" section so they don't dilute the heritage narrative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the founders behind Hispanic Heritage Month's must-haves`
- **Subject Alt B:** `15% off the Latino-founded brands we love 🌺`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `AORA, Touchland, Ceremonia & Rare Beauty — shop the founders' stories`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Their words, their brands, their moment — shop now`
