# Try our fragrances for free!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's cleanest merchandising send in weeks — a full fragrance catalog (You, You Doux, You Fleur, You Rêve) anchored by a genuinely good hook: a free Discovery Kit with any $50+ order. It's a real GWP, not a discount-on-discount repeat, and it's a logical follow-up to the standalone Doux launch on 9/15 and the Rêve-focused 30%-off fragrance push on 9/7 — this send finally unifies the whole scent line into one catalog instead of one-SKU pushes.
- The layout is clean, on-brand, and scannable: each fragrance gets its own hero image, one sensory sentence, and a "Shop now" CTA. No render bugs, no clutter. The weakness is the offer mechanic — "$50+ order" and "automatically applied at checkout" bury the actual value of the free kit, and the subject line ("Try our fragrances for free!") overpromises relative to the body, which requires a $50 spend to get a sample kit, not a full-size free fragrance.
- Overall a solid, well-executed catalog send that's let down by a subject line mismatched to the real offer.

## What's working

- The $50+ GWP mechanic gives a concrete, visible offer with a clear CTA ("Get yours") right in the hero.
- Four fragrances are cleanly organized with consistent structure: eyebrow label, name, scent notes, CTA — easy to scan on mobile.
- Product photography is distinct and moody per scent (berries/florals for You, dark wood for Doux, wildflowers for Fleur, fruit for Rêve), reinforcing scent personality without needing copy overload.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Subject line says "free" but the real offer is a sample kit gated behind a $50+ spend — a bait-and-switch gap between subject and hero copy.
- The asterisk on "$50+ order*" and "Terms apply" footnote signal fine print without surfacing it, which reads as slightly evasive for an offer already central to the send.
- No urgency or end date anywhere — after 9/8's "last call" urgency and 9/7's dated 30%-off, this send has zero time pressure, so it doesn't create any reason to act today.
- All four fragrance CTAs say "Shop now" — a missed chance to differentiate or reinforce the GWP thread (e.g. "Shop now — unlock your free kit").

## Recommendations

- 1. Align subject and hero: don't say "free" for a $50-minimum GWP — reframe the subject around the discovery kit itself, which is the real news.
- 2. Add a visible end date or "while supplies last" to the GWP banner — every fragrance email this month has had either urgency or a clean no-discount premise; this one has neither.
- 3. Move the $50 threshold and "automatically applied" mechanic into plain body copy instead of leaving it to the asterisk/footnote.
- 4. Vary CTA copy per section (e.g. "Shop You Doux," "Shop You Rêve") for clarity and slight SEO/tracking benefit.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free Glossier You Discovery Kit is waiting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $50, get 4 fragrances free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sample all four Glossier You scents — on us with any $50+ order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Iris, Doux, Fleur, Rêve — try them all before you commit.`
