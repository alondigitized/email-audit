# LA wildfire resilience—an update
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-values send, not a promotional email, and ILIA executes it with restraint. The premise is clear: the January LA wildfires hit, ILIA's 1%-for-the-Planet commitment funded tree planting in response, and six months later they're showing receipts. The "every drop counts" hero and the "Skin Tint Plants Trees" bottom section create a coherent arc from purchase → mission → real-world outcome. That's a harder story to tell than a product drop, and this email mostly lands it.
- The cost is that this send does no commercial work at all. There's no offer, no urgency, no membership benefit — just a well-told mission update. That's a deliberate trade: ILIA burns one slot in the inbox to deepen brand trust rather than drive revenue. After the June 29 Super Serum send (scored 9/10) and four other product emails in eight days, this cadence break is well-timed and earns goodwill. But a subscriber who opened hoping for a product deal will bounce.
- The execution is clean. No render bugs, consistent editorial photography, organized sections. If ILIA had threaded a soft product CTA ("The Skin Tint that funded this") with a real link higher in the email, this would convert the mission credit into commercial lift. As shipped, it builds the brand but leaves revenue on the table.

## What's working

- **Cadence break is earned.** After five product sends in eight days, a values-led email doesn't feel cynical — it feels like ILIA remembers they have a mission.
- **"Skin Tint Plants Trees" closes the loop.** The bottom section directly ties the product to the environmental impact, which is the most persuasive thing this email does.
- **1% for the Planet badge anchors credibility.** Third-party certification up top prevents the greenwashing read.
- **"Guess what we planted?" is a great section header.** Conversational, specific, curiosity-driving — stands out against ILIA's more editorial headline style.
- **Visual continuity with prior sends.** Earth tones, naturalistic photography, and clean editorial layout are consistent with the June 22 and June 27 sends — no identity whiplash.

## What's weak

- **Hero does zero product work.** The outdoor photography and body copy fill most of the visible email before any product surface. The Skin Tint tie-in arrives late; a subscriber who skims stops before seeing it.
- **"SEE HOW WE DO IT" leads nowhere commercial.** Both CTAs send readers to learn about the initiative, not toward a Skin Tint PDP — a missed chance to close the loop with a click.
- **Six months is a long lag.** The wildfires were January 2026. Sending this in late June without acknowledging the timeline gap risks a "why now?" read. One line of copy — "six months later, here's what grew" — would fix it.
- **Body copy is dense and unbroken.** The "Always Growing" section appears to run several uninterrupted paragraphs, which is a mobile readability hazard. No pull quotes or bold breakups visible.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a soft product CTA mid-email.** Under "Skin Tint Plants Trees," surface a button: "Shop Skin Tint — every purchase plants more." That converts mission credit into revenue without undermining the values story.
- 2. **Acknowledge the six-month timeline explicitly.** Lead the body copy with "Six months after the fires, here's what your purchases helped grow:" — turns a potential credibility gap into proof of sustained commitment.
- 3. **Break up the dense body copy.** Pull a striking stat ("2,500 trees planted") into a large-type callout between sections. Doubles as a shareable visual hook.
- 4. **Move the 1%-for-the-Planet badge inline near the Skin Tint section,** not just the top. Repeat the certification where the commercial nudge lives.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Six months after the fires — look what grew`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Skin Tint planted these trees`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `ILIA's 1% commitment is on the ground in LA — here's the proof`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `2,500 trees later: here's what your purchases helped grow`
