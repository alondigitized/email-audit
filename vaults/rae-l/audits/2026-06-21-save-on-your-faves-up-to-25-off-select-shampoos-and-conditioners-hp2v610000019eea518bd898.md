# Save on your faves: up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a second-wave follow-up to the June 19 "Woo-hoo 🎉 Up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners" send — same offer, same category, two days later, different subject framing. The hero delivers: 25% off is prominent, the SHOP NOW CTA is visible, and the hair care category is the clear entry point. That's where the discipline ends.
- Below the fold the email dissolves into a Sephora Collection Pro Brushes feature, an "Everyday Icons #71" product callout, a New From Kerastase module, and a Best of Skincare section — none of which are anchored to the core discount. A subscriber who opened for the hair care deal is now being asked to browse an editorial digest instead.
- The bigger problem is cadence. Sending a near-identical promotion two days after the same offer was already live, without escalating the deal or adding a deadline, trains the list to wait. Sephora is one of the best in the business at promotional email, but this send reads like a cadence-fill rather than a campaign decision.

## What's working

- **Hero is unambiguous.** "Up to 25% off Shampoos & Conditioners" and SHOP NOW are the first things the eye lands on — no guessing required.
- **Sender trust is high.** "Sephora Sale" from beauty.sephora.com is immediately recognizable and passes the sniff test.
- **Free same-day delivery** is surfaced near the top — a meaningful conversion driver for beauty replenishment purchases.
- **Beauty INSIDER branding** at the bottom reinforces loyalty program membership context.

## What's weak

- **Same offer, two days later, no escalation.** June 19 was already 25% off the same category. This send adds nothing new — no deadline, no expanded selection, no bonus points kicker layered on top.
- **Content drift kills focus.** Pro Brushes and Best of Skincare have no connection to the shampoo/conditioner hook. This should be a tight category conversion play; instead it's a mini-newsletter.
- **No urgency mechanism visible.** No "ends Sunday," no countdown, no "while supplies last" — nothing to move the subscriber off the fence today vs. tomorrow.
- **"Save on your faves" is generic.** After the energetic June 19 subject, this is a flat reframing that doesn't add new information or emotional pull.
- **Subject line is too long.** At ~68 characters it'll truncate on most mobile inboxes, cutting off "conditioners 🌟" — the only detail that distinguishes it from a generic beauty promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline or escalate the offer.** "25% off — ends tonight" or "25% off + 500 bonus points this weekend only" gives the second send a reason to exist. Right now it's a reminder with no new information.
- 2. **Cut the mid-email drift.** Remove Pro Brushes and Best of Skincare entirely. Every module below the hero should link to shampoos and conditioners — editorial variety kills conversion on a promo send.
- 3. **Tighten the subject to under 50 chars.** Drop "select" and the trailing category list.
- 4. **Add a visible deadline to the hero banner** — even "offer ends June 22" would be enough to create honest urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your faves are 25% off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off shampoos & conditioners 🌟 ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free same-day delivery + your Beauty Insider points stack.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the sale before it disappears — conditioners, shampoos, and more.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `maximum-scale=1` blocks user zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 violation):**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents users from pinching to zoom on mobile. This violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Remove `maximum-scale=1` or replace with `user-scalable=yes`.

**Issue — `robots` meta value has a space:**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
The correct value is `noindex` (no space). `no index` is non-standard and may not be honored by all crawlers. Low risk for email but indicates a template defect.
