# Add 3, subtract 20%
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's first send of the run with a real price offer. Recent sends were "NEW Skin Tint + Ethereal Palette Duo" (10-07, no offer) and "Need a new coat?" (10-05, a layering routine). This one is a straight bundle promo: buy 3, get 20% off with code BUNDLEUP. The hero says "buy 3 = 20% off" in big maroon type over a product still life. The pitch is clear in about two seconds, and the subject line "Add 3, subtract 20%" matches the hero's "beauty math" idea.
- The weakness is what comes after the hero. The "prime picks" and "multiples" sections are a plain product grid. Nothing tells me which three products to pick or what the bundle comes to. The bundle sets (Eye Stylus Trio, Art of the Eye Trio, Hero Set) are the natural answer to "buy 3", but they sit below the fold with no prices. The deadline, "Sale ends at 11:59pm 10/12/26", is in tiny footer type, so the urgency is mostly lost. The email is competent and on-brand, but it is the fourth ILIA send in eight days (10-02, 10-05, 10-07, 10-09), so fatigue is a real risk.
- Verdict: a solid promo with a clear hook and a diluted middle. It scores a notch above ILIA's recent editorial sends because it has an actual offer.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer outright: "buy 3 = 20% off" in large type, with "beauty math" as a playful tag.
- The top banner repeats the offer and the code, and the dark maroon "ADD 3 TO CART →" button sits right under the intro copy.
- The subject's math wordplay carries into the hero, so the subject and creative feel like one idea.
- The "multiples" section uses pre-built trios and a set, which fit the "buy 3" mechanic.
- Typography, palette and product photography stay consistent with recent sends. I saw no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The deadline (10/12) appears only in footer fine print. The body says just "limited time," so the urgency is weak.
- The "prime picks" grid shows no prices or savings, and nothing tells me how to choose three.
- There are two button styles in the grid: outlined "shop now →" boxes in "prime picks" and a different "SHOP NOW" style in "multiples". That reads as inconsistent.
- "Add 3 to cart" goes to an unknown destination, and the copy doesn't say whether it adds three specific items or opens a collection.
- The product-led body has no reviews, no shade or fit detail beyond "super serum skin tint spf 40", and no model imagery.
- The cadence is heavy: four sends in eight days, with a fourth promo-type message following three different ones.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero or banner, for example "Ends Sunday 10/12", and drop the vague "limited time."
- 2. Add a "Build your 3" module right under the CTA. Show three hero products with a price, a strikethrough, and the bundle total.
- 3. Add prices or "from $X" to every product tile, so the discount feels real.
- 4. Move the trios above "prime picks". They are the easiest way to act on the offer.
- 5. Use one button style throughout, and make the CTA copy specific, such as "Build my 3 →".
- 6. Add one review line or star rating to the best-sellers, for example the mascara.
- 7. Space out sends or give this one a distinct cadence slot, so subscribers don't see four ILIA emails in a week.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code BUNDLEUP at checkout. Mix any three, ends 10/12.`
