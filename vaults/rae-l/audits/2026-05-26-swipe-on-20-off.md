# Swipe on 20% off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's entry into the final stretch of the Friends + Family run — by any count, the 14th or 15th email in the campaign — and it makes the single smartest creative choice of the entire late-stage sequence: anchor to one product. "Swipe on 20% off" is a clean mascara double-entendre, and the email delivers on it end-to-end: hero application imagery, a features callout ("flake-resistant / smudge-resistant / safe for sensitive eyes"), an eye-looks grid showing range, and a 5-star review block right before the primary CTA. The narrative coherence here is meaningfully better than the sitewide-generic template that dominated the first week.
- The problem isn't the email — it's the timing. Subscribers have already seen the eye-focused May 25 "All eyes on 20% off" just the day before. Sending a second consecutive eye-category email 24 hours later doesn't compound the strategy; it cannibalizes it. Open rates on this send will reflect campaign fatigue, not creative quality.
- If ILIA had deployed this mascara story on day two instead of day fourteen, this would be a 9/10 send. Arriving here, it's a good email doing uphill work.

## What's working

- **Subject wordplay lands**: "Swipe on 20% off" earns its cleverness — mascara application plus the offer in 16 characters. One of the better subjects in the run.
- **Product narrative is coherent top to bottom**: hero, features callout, eye-looks grid, and social proof all orbit the same mascara. No creative drift.
- **"ALL EYES ON LIMITLESS" features callout is precisely targeted**: "safe for sensitive eyes" is the exact reassurance clean beauty buyers need; it differentiates from conventional mascaras at a glance.
- **Social proof placement is textbook**: "The Reviews Are In" with a 5-star block sits directly above the final CTA, exactly where conversion momentum should peak.
- **Eye-looks grid expands perceived versatility**: showing multiple distinct results for one product broadens the perceived addressable buyer.

## What's weak

- **Second consecutive eye-category email in 24 hours**: the May 25 "All eyes on 20% off" already staked out this territory; repeating the angle the next day dilutes both sends.
- **No visible deadline in the email body**: the F+F urgency built across previous sends doesn't carry over — a subscriber opening this cold sees no reason to act today vs. tomorrow.
- **CTA copy is generic**: "SHOP IT" and "SHOP NOW" are wasted opportunities at this product-specific moment. "Shop Limitless Lash" would convert better.
- **Cart Deco product section at the bottom breaks the mascara narrative**: pivoting to a face product after a tight mascara story loses the thread and dilutes the CTA path.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an explicit deadline to the hero** — "Ends midnight tonight" or a visible countdown. This is the single highest-lift change; without it, the offer has no forcing function for first-time openers.
- 2. **Replace "SHOP IT" / "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Limitless Lash"** — specificity converts better, and the product name is already established in the layout by the time the reader reaches the CTA.
- 3. **Cut or reframe the Cart Deco section** — either remove it or fold it into a "More on sale" module that doesn't interrupt the mascara arc.
- 4. **Space out eye-category sends** — the May 25 eye email and this one should be separated by at least two days and a different product category in between.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Limitless Lash — 20% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The mascara that's safe for sensitive eyes. Now 20% off.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Flake-free, smudge-proof, sensitive-eye safe — sale ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Every look in this email uses the same mascara. It's currently 20% off.`
