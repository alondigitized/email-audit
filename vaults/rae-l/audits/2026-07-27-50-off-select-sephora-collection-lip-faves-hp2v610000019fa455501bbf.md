# 50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves 🪩
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's second lip-themed "50% off" send in two days — yesterday's 7/26 "Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves" scored 8/10 here specifically because the subject's number never showed up in the render. This one actually fixes that: the pink hero banner up top says "Get 50% off Select Sephora Collection" and ties it to National Lip Day, so subject and hero finally agree. That's real progress.
- But the fix is shallow — the 50% off banner shows generic product silhouettes with no names, no prices, and no visible "was $X now $Y." The instant you scroll past that first banner, the email pivots hard into an unrelated new-fragrance launch (RUHVEDA, INFLUXIOUS) and then a long new-arrivals catalog (blush, body butter, BB cream, scalp toner, perfume) that has nothing to do with lips or the sale. The "50% off lip faves" promise is a one-screen teaser bolted onto what's actually a generic weekly catalog blast.
- Sending this the day after an almost identically-framed lip discount email is the bigger structural problem — this reads like a rerun, not a follow-up.

## What's working

- Hero banner copy now matches the subject line ("50% off Select Sephora Collection," "lip faves") — unlike yesterday's 7/26 send, which had a subject/hero mismatch.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Clear, repeated SHOP NOW CTAs at every section break, so there's never ambiguity about what to click.
- Beauty Insider tier bar and 4X points mechanic (PATRICK TA, code P T4XPTS) give a loyalty member something concrete beyond the discount.
- "NEW • ONLINE ONLY," "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA," and Clean badges on the product grid add useful scannable detail.

## What's weak

- The 50% off offer is never shown applied to an actual product — no product name, no crossed-out price, just illustrative lip imagery under the banner.
- Immediately after the lip sale banner, the email jumps to a completely unrelated new-fragrance campaign ("Just Landed at Sephora" / RUHVEDA & INFLUXIOUS), diluting the one offer that got someone to open.
- The bulk of the email (8-product new-arrivals grid, App Early Access, 4X Points, Latest Lineup) has zero connection to lip products or the stated 50% off — it's a generic catalog wrapped around a lip-sale headline.
- This lands one day after 7/26's near-identical "50% off lip" email — back-to-back same-theme sends read as repetitive rather than escalating.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" appears twice with no detail on what's actually excluded, reinforcing that the headline discount is vaguer than it looks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the actual discount in the hero: name 2-3 specific Sephora Collection lip SKUs with strike-through pricing (e.g., "Lip Liner $10 → $5") instead of a generic product collage — this is the single biggest lift, since right now the offer is a headline with nothing behind it.
- 2. Cut the fragrance-launch block or move it to a separate send — stacking an unrelated hero campaign directly under the lip sale banner competes with and buries the offer that got the open.
- 3. If sending lip-discount emails on consecutive days, differentiate the framing (e.g., escalate to "last chance" or add a new mechanic) rather than repeating near-identical subject/hero copy from 7/26.
- 4. Trim the new-arrivals catalog or retitle the email to reflect what it actually is (a general new-arrivals roundup with a lip promo attached), so expectations set by the subject line match the bulk of the content.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new RUHVEDA & INFLUXIOUS fragrances just landed`
