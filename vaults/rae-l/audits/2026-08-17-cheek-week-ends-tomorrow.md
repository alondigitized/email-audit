# Cheek Week ends tomorrow!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the Cheek Week arc closing out on a deadline, and it's the right call structurally — "Ends tomorrow" is the one thing this thread was missing. 8/11 opened the promo flat, 8/13 wrapped it in a campaign name, and now 8/15's "current favorite blush looks" gave it an editorial breather. This send finally puts a clock on it, which is exactly the beat a discount arc needs before it dies quietly.
- Execution-wise it's a long, review-heavy scroll: three testimonials, a "match the weather" section, a GWP at $75+, and the urgency hook buried at the very top only. The urgency never resurfaces after the hero — no reminder near the GWP or final CTA that this is the last day. Social proof is strong (four 5-star quotes across skin tones), but the email leans on reviews to do the selling instead of the deadline.
- Solid, on-arc send that undersells its own best asset — genuine urgency — by using it once and letting six more sections drift away from it.

## What's working

- Clear "ENDS TOMORROW" eyebrow + hero headline puts the deadline front and center immediately.
- Hero image is a clean, well-lit closeup with visible flush color — on-message for a blush promo.
- Four different customer quotes with 5-star ratings and diverse models build credible social proof.
- GWP section (free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush at $75+) gives a second reason to buy beyond the discount.
- Consistent "Shop now" CTA buttons repeated after every section reduce scroll friction.

## What's weak

- Urgency appears once at the top and never resurfaces — the GWP and closing sections don't remind the reader time is running out.
- The email is very long (7+ full sections) for a "last day" message that should feel urgent and scannable, not leisurely.
- "Set for summer" section headline and copy read like evergreen editorial, diluting the deadline framing right after the hero.
- CTA buttons are visually flat (light gray/pink outline boxes) and don't stand out against the pink-toned product photography.
- No visible countdown or specific end time/date — "tomorrow" is vague without a timestamp.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a second urgency reminder right before or inside the GWP section ("Cheek Week + your free brush — both gone tomorrow") since that's the section most likely to get missed at the bottom of a long scroll.
- 2. Trim the email — cut or combine the "Set for summer" editorial block with a testimonial block; a last-day email should feel short and pressing, not like a full catalog send.
- 3. Make the CTA buttons higher-contrast (solid black or Glossier pink fill) so they don't blend into the pink product shots.
- 4. Put an actual cutoff time in the hero copy ("Ends tomorrow at 11:59pm ET") to make the urgency concrete and credible.
- 5. Move the GWP threshold ($75+) into the hero or a repeated banner so it isn't only discovered after scrolling through three testimonials.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cheek Week ends tonight — 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, get a free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush at $75+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorite blush shades, 20% off through midnight`
