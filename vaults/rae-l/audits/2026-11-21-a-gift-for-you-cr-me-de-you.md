# A gift for you: Crème de You
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-with-purchase send that arrives on top of a sale the subscriber already knows about. "STILL ON / Get a free Crème de You with your $100 order*" is the actual new news. It gets tucked under a hero that carries three competing badges: 30% OFF $100+, 25% OFF EVERYTHING, and a clipped "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" sticker. Glossier has run 25%/30% creative on 11/18, 11/19 and 11/20 ("Our biggest sale of the year is live!"). A free product is the freshest hook of the week, and the email undersells it.
- The render is clean, on-brand and easy to scan. Blue CTAs, red product photography and a real customer review give it good rhythm. But the hero crops badly: the right image bleeds off the edge and cuts "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" and "EVERYTHING" mid-word. The middle section also has a lot of dead white space and a leg-lotion photo that doesn't show the product. Overall it's a competent, slightly cluttered fourth send in a row on the same sale.

## What's working

- The "Get a free Crème de You with your $100 order" headline is large and readable, and a single "Get yours" button sits directly beneath it.
- The five-star Emily review gives credible social proof, with real texture ("scent lasts so long").
- The "Plays well with" two-up pairs Glossier You Eau de parfum with the candle, each with a name and a full-width Shop now button. This is a sensible cross-sell.
- Strong brand consistency: Glossier blue, red product styling, and the footer smiley and hand.

## What's weak

- The hero stacks three badges (30%, 25%, Black Friday) and the right-hand ones are cut off at the image edge ("BLA… FRID… SALE", "EVERYTHI"). It looks like a crop bug.
- The "Plus get 25% off everything or 30% off with your $100 order" subline buries a second offer under the gift and muddies which deal matters.
- The mid-section photo is offset right with a large empty left column. The skin close-up doesn't show what the product is, and the "BELIEVE THE ❤️" caption is cryptic.
- The asterisk in "$100 order *" leads to "Terms apply" in tiny grey text at the very bottom. That's a weak fit for an offer headline.
- This is the fourth sale-related send in about three days (11/18, 11/19 twice, 11/20), so fatigue is a real risk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the gift the only hero message. Show the Crème de You jar clean, with one badge, "FREE with $100", and drop the 25%/30%/Black Friday stickers. Move the percent-off line to a small secondary strip.
- 2. Fix the cropping so no badge text is cut off at the right edge.
- 3. Name the product benefit in the hero (for example "whipped body butter") and state the spend threshold and deadline clearly, such as "Ends Sunday".
- 4. Replace the leg photo with the jar or a swatch, and align the review block to the full width to remove the empty left column.
- 5. Put "Terms apply" next to the offer, not at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Crème de You with $100`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free body butter ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, get the whipped body butter free, plus 30% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The scent that lasts: free with any $100 order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built (`stripocdn.email`) email from `gTEAM@eml.glossier.com`. The automated QA shows no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as sender defects. The only confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes and one invalid-markup oddity. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks couldn't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links appear to be wrapped in Klaviyo click tracking (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/…`). Final destinations and UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 7, which were footer, social and utility links, deprioritized by design. The probe results for the 12 weren't included, so link health is **not confirmed**. The unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't shown.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7XSG9J2057DSJP2KA3PRAVG` has no `alt`. This is minor (see §3).
- `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/…">` directly after `<body>` is invalid markup. `rel` and `href` aren't valid attributes on `<span>`. It's likely a tracking or preheader artifact, and it's harmless in practice, but it's non-standard.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 5 images** (confirmed by QA):
  - `heroimage_1_3.jpg`
  - `frame_3992.jpg`
  - `group_3986_7.jpg`
  - `group_3987_8.jpg`
  - `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7XSG9J2057DSJP2KA3PRAVG` (tracking pixel)
- With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the hero and content images carry no text. The tracking pixel should use `alt=""`. The missing alt attribute on the content images is the real issue.
- The structure looks sound:
  - viewport meta is present
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is set
  - MSO conditional comments and `mso-hide` are used
  - `@media (max-width: 600px)` rules are present
  - `lang="en"` and `dir="ltr"` are set
- Hover states and rollover images (`.rollover`, `.bh:hover`) are in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks, so they degrade safely in Outlook.
- No issues found beyond the alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`). The truncation means the lower body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** These are warnings, and QA itself notes the relay may not capture these headers. This is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw `.eml`. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** This is expected through AgentMail, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. It isn't a defect in the email itself. Verify with the original headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address** (CAN-SPAM) fall outside the truncated source and couldn't be checked. The unsubscribe link was reportedly probed but the result isn't shown.
