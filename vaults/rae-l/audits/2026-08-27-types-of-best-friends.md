# Types of best friends
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier fully off the discount arc and into pure fragrance-layering editorial — "Friends forever," pairing up Glossier You variants (Doux, Soie, Fleur, Rêve) as personality-matched "best friend" duos. It's the fifth no-discount send since 8/20's "Going back to school?" bundle, following 8/22's zodiac angle and 8/20's back-to-school essentials — the brand is clearly mid-quarter in a pure-lifestyle stretch with zero price mentions anywhere in the render.
- The concept is well-executed as editorial: five distinct product pairings, each with its own styled lifestyle shot (curtain-lit bottles, a handbag, a coffee-and-earbuds flatlay) and a "type of friend" framing that's genuinely on-brand for Glossier's voice. But there's no offer, no urgency, and every single CTA reads identical "Shop now" — five times in a row with zero differentiation. For a subscriber checking email during a commute, this reads as pleasant scrolling, not a reason to click today.
- Judgement: a nicely art-directed but commercially flat send. It'll perform on brand affinity, not conversion — consistent with the post-Cheek-Week pattern of this account drifting toward soft engagement content.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent visual language across all five product pairings — each has its own color-graded lifestyle photo (window light, pink flatlay, off-white texture, coffee scene, dark moody bottles).
- The "types of best friends" concept is a coherent creative through-line that ties fragrance-layering education to a relatable, shareable idea.
- Eyebrow labels (DYNAMIC DUO, PERFECT OPPOSITES, KINDRED SPIRITS, PARTY ANIMALS) add scannable structure and personality-quiz flavor without cluttering the layout.
- No render bugs — every image loads cleanly, text doesn't overlap, and the footer/legal block is intact.

## What's weak

- Every CTA button says "Shop now" — five identical, generic buttons with no product-specific copy to distinguish which pairing is being clicked.
- Zero offer or price signal anywhere in the email — no %, no bundle price, no loyalty perk — after a summer of promo cadence, this is a pure brand-content send with nothing pulling a price-sensitive subscriber to act now.
- No urgency or timing hook at all — nothing to open or click today versus next week.
- Five near-identical hero+CTA blocks back to back make the email feel repetitive by the third pairing; there's no summary/bundle CTA at the top or bottom to shortcut the scroll.
- Continues the pattern from 8/20 and 8/22 of dropping discount messaging entirely — three straight no-discount sends risks reader fatigue for anyone who engaged specifically because of Cheek Week pricing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate the CTAs — swap all five "Shop now" for pairing-specific copy ("Shop the Duo," "Shop Soie + You," etc.) so each button reads as a distinct decision, not a repeated click target.
- 2. Add a scent-quiz or "find your match" CTA at the top that routes based on the friend-type framing — this content is basically a personality quiz already; make it interactive or at least give one clear primary action above the fold.
- 3. Reintroduce even a soft incentive (free sample with any 2-pack, loyalty points multiplier) — five sends without a single offer is a meaningful gap versus the Cheek Week cadence that converted well (8/18 scored 8/10 largely on offer clarity).
- 4. Trim to 3 pairings max, or add a "shop all 5" bundle CTA at the very top so scrollers who don't want to read five vignettes still have a fast path to purchase.
- 5. Add a light urgency layer for a future send in this series (e.g., "layering kit, this week only") to break the pattern of purely evergreen editorial.
