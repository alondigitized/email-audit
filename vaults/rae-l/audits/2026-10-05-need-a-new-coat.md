# Need a new coat?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand fall skincare send, but the subject line and the layout work against each other. "Need a new coat?" sounds like outerwear, and the payoff is a layering routine of face milk, barrier cream, eye cream and lip balm. The "a new fall coat" pun in the hero does land once you open it. In the inbox, though, a reader expecting jackets has no reason to connect it to ILIA.
- The actual money offer, a free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with a $100+ order, only appears in a thin banner at the very top. It then shows up again about two-thirds of the way down under "a free lift." The first screen is a pretty close-up face with a generic "Shop Fall Skincare" button. The "layer it" four-step routine is a good idea, but it ends in four identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- Compared with the 10-02 "It's back (with NEW shades)" send (7/10), which had one hero and one product story, this one is less focused. It does beat the plain-text collapse on 09-23. Verdict: pleasant and competent, mid-pack, and a weak subject line limits it.

## What's working

- The "a *new* fall coat" serif hero headline is a clever payoff, and the italic emphasis fits ILIA's editorial voice.
- The "layer it" step-by-step structure (Step 1–4) is easy to follow and makes the products feel like a routine rather than a catalog.
- The alternating left/right product modules give the middle of the email some rhythm. Nothing in the render looks broken.
- The mascara gift module has a strong image with a red "Mini Mascara Gift" badge. It is the most eye-catching element in the email.
- The footer is tidy: free shipping over $50, VIP, auto-replenish, and the planet-partner and Pact badges all reinforce brand trust.

## What's weak

- The subject "Need a new coat?" promises outerwear and is ambiguous in the inbox. It also makes no mention of skincare or the gift.
- The offer is buried. The gift appears in a small top banner and again below the fold, and it needs a $100+ order with an asterisk and "see site for details."
- Five near-identical "SHOP NOW" buttons are generic. The four product CTAs don't name the product, and there are no prices.
- The products have no prices, benefits or reviews, so there's little to convince a shopper who has never used them.
- The hero CTA, "Shop Fall Skincare," competes with the gift offer, so the email has two competing messages.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject so it says skincare and carries the gift. The current one reads like a clothing retailer and hides the one concrete offer.
- 2. Move the free-mascara offer up. Put it in the hero as a clear line ("Free mini mascara with $100+") instead of only in the top banner, and add a deadline if one exists.
- 3. Replace the repeated "SHOP NOW" buttons with product-specific copy ("Shop Face Milk," "Shop Barrier Cream"). Add prices and a one-line benefit for each.
- 4. Add a review count or a star rating under at least the hero product.
- 5. Make the $100+ threshold clearer (for example, a "spend $100, get the gift" progress line) instead of leaving it in a footnote.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-derived HTML message with tracked links on `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`. Two images lack alt text, and the QA run couldn't verify the unsubscribe headers or SPF/DKIM. Those header gaps are probably capture limits of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed sender defects.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- The QA probed 12 material links and skipped 13. It reported 0 broken links among the probed set. The 13 skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, were not verified.
- The tracking-pixel image `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M4691J7KGMZ6C325N37Y7A5B` is served from a tracking subdomain. That is normal for an open pixel.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't check individual `href` values beyond what the QA probed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` has no `alt`. If this image carries content or a link, a screen reader or an image-blocked client will show nothing or the filename.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M4691J7KGMZ6C325N37Y7A5B` has no `alt`. This is low impact for a 1×1 pixel, but adding `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title> </title>`. This is minor for email, but some clients and web-view versions use it.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** The source imports `https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css` and `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat`. Gmail and Outlook ignore `@import`. The stack `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` does fall back, so this isn't a defect.
- **Preheader padding:** The hidden preheader uses `͏` padding characters to stop body text from leaking into the inbox preview. This is standard practice, and the preheader text "(Of hydration, that is.)" renders as intended.
- **Responsive and Outlook handling:** The source includes viewport meta, `mso` conditionals, and a `max-width: 480px` media query. No issues found.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{ first_name }}` or `{%`. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the portion I could see.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** The QA warned that the header was missing. It noted the header "may not be captured by AgentMail relay," so this is unconfirmed. Without it, Gmail and Yahoo won't show a one-click unsubscribe.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support, so confirm it at the source.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA attributes this to the relay.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** The QA says it always includes an unsubscribe link in its probe set, and it reported no failure. I can't confirm the link's presence or the physical postal address from the truncated HTML.
- **Sender:** `From: "ILIA" <info@iliabeauty.com>` is on the brand domain. The tracking domain `send.iliabeauty.com` is also on the brand domain, which suggests aligned custom tracking and sending.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not assessable. The truncated HTML doesn't show the CTA destination URLs, their UTM parameters or the landing pages. The QA reports no broken probed links, but it doesn't report UTM or landing-page data. Links are wrapped by `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`, so the final UTM values may only be visible after redirect.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Check the raw message headers from a direct send, for example to a Gmail test inbox. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. The AgentMail relay may be stripping them.
