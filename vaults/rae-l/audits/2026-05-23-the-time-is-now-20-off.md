# The time is now—20% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's eleventh email in the Friends + Family run, and it's a regression. After yesterday's clean, direct "20% off makeup + skincare + SPF" scored an 8, the team returned to the plain-text Marissa format — the same device they used five days ago on 5/19 — but this time it's packed with so many nested offers (20% base, 25% VIP, GWP tiers at $0/$100/$175, plus up to 40% on select items) that the email reads like a terms-and-conditions footnote rather than a closing-window nudge.
- The subject "The time is now—20% off" is trying to do two jobs: urgency AND offer. It mostly succeeds on clarity, but it's doing the same work as a half-dozen prior sends. The plain-text format with a "Warmly, Marissa" sign-off was a strategic choice on 5/19 to cut through email noise — but when you're on email eleven, the "authentic personal note" disguise has worn thin.
- The gift-with-purchase ladder ($0 threshold → $100 → $175) is genuinely compelling offer depth that competitors don't run. But burying it in body-copy bullets, with no visual hierarchy, no product imagery, and no clear single primary CTA, means most skimmers will not register it. This email should have been a hero-image send with the GWP tiers front-and-center — not another plain-text Marissa note.

## What's working

- **Concrete deadline visible**: "Sale ends at 11:59pm 5/28/26" in the footer — that's a real, specific end time, which is more credible than "limited time."
- **GWP ladder is real value**: Three escalating gift tiers (mini mascara/Lip Sketch → full-size bronzer/highlighter at $100 → Barrier Build moisturizer at $175) is a sophisticated incentive structure that rewards higher spend.
- **Barrier Build farewell mention**: Calling out the Liquid Powder Eye Tint discontinuation creates genuine urgency distinct from the generic sale pressure.
- **VIP upsell is clean**: One sentence, links to free sign-up, adds 5 points of discount — low friction for high reward.

## What's weak

- **No visual hierarchy**: Wall of text with no hero image, no product photo, no color block — the eye has nowhere to go and the most valuable information (GWP tiers) is buried in bullets.
- **Plain-text Marissa format is a spent trick**: ILIA already used this on 5/19. On email eleven in the run, repeating the "authentic personal note" format without escalating content feels formulaic.
- **No single dominant CTA**: The only linked actions are "everything is 20% off" inline and "Become an ILIA VIP" (twice) — neither is a button. A committed shop-now button would dramatically increase click conversion.
- **"Hi there" instead of a name**: For an email this deep in a loyalty-heavy campaign, the anonymous greeting signals list-wide broadcast, not personalization.
- **Offer complexity undercuts urgency**: Mixing 20%, 25%, 40%, and three GWP tiers in one plain-text block is cognitively exhausting. The closing message should simplify, not stack.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the GWP ladder visually**: One hero grid — three panels, three thresholds ($0 / $100 / $175), three product images. This is the most differentiated offer in the run and has never gotten its own visual moment.
- 2. **Add a single, prominent CTA button**: "Shop 20% off — ends 5/28" in a solid button above the fold. Every conversion point should funnel through one action.
- 3. **Cut the offer stacking**: Pick one lead offer (the GWP ladder) and one hook (deadline). Move VIP upsell to post-purchase or a dedicated follow-up.
- 4. **Retire the Marissa plain-text format for this send**: It's been used twice in five emails. Use it once more only at the true last-day wire, not the "few days left" moment.
- 5. **Personalize or cut the greeting**: Swap "Hi there" for a first name or drop the greeting entirely; "Warmly, Marissa" already makes it feel personal.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three tiers of gifts — the more you spend, the more you get.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free Barrier Build moisturizer with $175+. Ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — ILIA "The time is now—20% off" (Friends + Family)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Three hard 404s are live in the email — two caused by a trailing `)` character appended to destination URLs, one caused by a quoted-printable encoding corruption in the unsubscribe link. Compliance posture is weak: no physical address in the HTML body and both `List-Unsubscribe` headers are absent.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Trailing `)` breaks two destination URLs**

Both links below return 404 because a literal `)` was captured as part of the URL, consistent with a markdown-to-HTML template parsing bug where `[text](URL)` was not properly split:

- `https://iliabeauty.com/collections/best-sellers)` → should be `.../collections/best-sellers`
- `https://iliabeauty.com/account/register)` → should be `.../account/register`

**[FAIL] Malformed unsubscribe URL**

```
https://manage.kmail-lists.com/subscriptions/unsubscribe?a=3DSguDsz&=
```

Two distinct errors:
- `a=3DSguDsz` — quoted-printable encoding artifact; `3D` is the encoded form of `=`, so the intended value `a=SguDsz` was double-encoded at send time.
- `&=` at the end — orphaned key-value separator with no name or value; likely a truncated second parameter.

Both will cause Klaviyo's unsubscribe handler to reject or mis-route the request.

**[INFO] Body CTAs use Klaviyo redirect tracking**

All in-body CTAs route through `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01KSBEH33SRQCT8VCZM8NVHQ5C_N`. Redirect-layer UTM passthrough cannot be verified from source alone; confirm in Klaviyo flow settings or by resolving the redirect chain.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Tracking pixel missing alt attribute**

```html
src="https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01KSBEH33SRQCT8VCZM8NVHQ5C"
```

No `alt` attribute present. Should be `alt=""` to suppress screen-reader announcement of the open-tracking beacon.

**[INFO] `@import` for custom font inside MSO conditional**

```html
<!--[if !mso]><!-->
<style>@import url("https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css");</style>
<!--<![endif]-->
```

Correct Outlook exclusion pattern. Gmail suppresses `@import` in some rendering modes; fallback stack (`Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif`) is present and sufficient.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

Greeting renders as literal `"Hi there,"` — no first-name merge token in source. No unresolved `{{ }}` or `{% %}` tokens visible in the truncated HTML; either personalization was intentionally omitted or the token resolved to empty and no fallback was configured. Not a hard break, but worth confirming intent in the Klaviyo profile property mapping.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Physical mailing address absent from HTML body**

CAN-SPAM §7(e) requires a valid physical postal address. None detected in the visible source. If it exists below the truncation point, no action needed — but QA confirms it was not found.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**

Both `List-Unsubscribe` (RFC 2369) and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers are absent from what the AgentMail relay surfaced. If Klaviyo injects these at the MTA level, they may simply not be forwarded by the relay. Verify against raw SMTP headers in Klaviyo's message log for the sent copy. Gmail and Yahoo require `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for bulk senders since February 2024.

**[FAIL] Unsubscribe link is non-functional** (see §2 above — double-encoding + truncated parameter will cause the handler to fail silently or error).

### 7. Recommendations

**Priority 1 — Fix before next send**

1. **Strip trailing `)` from direct `iliabeauty.com` URLs.** Root cause is likely a markdown parser not splitting `[text](url)` correctly in the template editor. Audit all direct links in the template, not just these two.

2. **Rebuild the unsubscribe URL.** The correct Klaviyo parameter is `a=SguDsz` (not `a=3DSguDsz`). Identify where quoted-printable encoding is being applied to URL parameters — this is typically a misconfigured MIME encoder in the sending pipeline. The `&=` fragment also suggests the second parameter was dropped; restore it from the Klaviyo template source.

3. **Add physical mailing address** to the email footer if not already present below the truncation point.

**Priority 2 — Compliance hardening**

4. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** in raw SMTP headers via Klaviyo's delivery log. If absent, enable one-click unsubscribe in Klaviyo account settings (required for >5K/day senders under Gmail/Yahoo 2024 policy).

5. **Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel** to suppress screen-reader noise.

**Priority 3 — Operational**

6. **Verify UTM passthrough** on Klaviyo redirect links by resolving one redirect chain end-to-end and confirming `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` parameters land on the destination page.
