# All eyes on 20% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- ILIA's thirteenth email in the Friends + Family run finally does something useful with the format: a category-focused eye edit. The hero is a tight close-up of an eye with a mascara wand grazing the lashes, "eyeing these?" set in script over the lid — the brand's most visually arresting opener of the entire campaign. Below the fold, a three-tier grid (Prep / Color / Frame) walks through Skin Blur Serum Concealer, Blending Brush, Eye Stylus Shadow Stick, Liquid Powder Eye Tint, and Limitless Lash Mascara, each with its own "Shop now" button.
- The "Cart Deco" footer add — "just some gifts to decorate your cart, choose a free mini with every order" — is the most differentiated content beat of the week. It's a real reason to click that isn't just "20% off again."
- After twelve repetitive sends, this one earns its place by going narrow (eyes only) and adding a GWP layer. Strong send, especially compared to yesterday's "Ends Soon — Extra 40% Off" sale-rack pivot.

## What's working

- Hero photography is the strongest of the campaign — macro eye shot with mascara wand reads premium and stops the scroll.
- Category-narrow framing ("eyes" as the lens) gives this email a reason to exist separate from the other twelve sends.
- Three-act structure (Prep → Color → Frame) is a clean teaching device that makes a multi-product email feel like a routine, not a dump.
- "Cart Deco" free-mini GWP at the bottom is a fresh hook the prior twelve emails didn't have.
- Subject "All eyes on 20% off" finally puts a category word in the line — first time this week.

## What's weak

- Same five "Shop now" buttons stacked vertically — no product is given hierarchy, every CTA looks equal.
- The "Cart Deco" GWP is buried at the bottom; if I don't scroll, I miss the only fresh news in the email.
- "20% off Friends + Family" lockup top-right is now visual wallpaper — after thirteen sends, the eye skips it.
- Top utility bar ("20% off sitewide / 30% off for pros") repeats the offer twice before the hero — competes with itself.
- No price anchoring on individual products — I can't tell if Eye Stylus is $28 or $42.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move "Cart Deco" GWP above the product grid.** The free mini is the only new news. Lead with it, then show the eye products as "what to add to qualify."
- 2. **Pick a hero product.** Mascara is in the hero shot — give Limitless Lash a bigger card with a price, reviews, and a primary CTA. Demote the other four to a smaller "complete the look" strip.
- 3. **Add prices.** Five products with no prices is a friction wall. At least show "from $28" per tile.
- 4. **Kill the redundant top utility strip** — the hero already carries the 20% offer; the "30% off for pros" line confuses the primary message.
- 5. **Tighten the subject** — "All eyes on" is a pun without payoff. Make it a destination.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free eye mini + 20% off — Friends + Family`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The eye edit: 20% off + a free mini`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Choose your free mini at checkout. Mascara, shadow stick, eye tint — all 20% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Prep, color, frame — your eye routine, 20% off through Monday.`
