# 20% off makeup + skincare + SPF
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's ninth email in six days, and the creative team made the right call: drop the editorial complexity, go direct. "20% off makeup + skincare + SPF" is the most plainspoken subject line of the entire Friends + Family run — and that's a feature, not a bug. After eight increasingly elaborate sends, subscribers who are still engaged deserve a simple, no-nonsense closing prompt.
- The problem is the body doesn't match the promise. The subject sets up a discount email; the hero delivers "makeup that makes your skin better™" — brand positioning, not a promotional payoff. The 20% offer lives in the thin promo bar at the very top, where eyes rarely linger. A subscriber who opened for the discount has to hunt for it.
- "Cart Deco" at the bottom is the most interesting creative decision here — it reads like an implicit cart-abandonment nudge, but it's buried after a long editorial scroll. If that section is doing real conversion work, it deserves to be higher.

## What's working

- **Subject line clarity** — "20% off makeup + skincare + SPF" covers all three categories explicitly; no ambiguity about what's on offer.
- **Hero photography** — the close-up skin texture photography is on-brand and visually arresting; it earns the scroll.
- **"Made for All Types" framing** — inclusive positioning that's been consistent across the campaign; reinforces the brand's core promise without feeling forced.
- **"Cart Deco" closer** — compact/palette product spotlight is a smart last-chance nudge for subscribers who've been browsing but not buying.
- **Layout is clean** — no render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text across the visible render.

## What's weak

- **Offer is buried in the promo bar** — the 20% discount is the subject-line hook; it should be the hero headline, not a banner afterthought.
- **No visible deadline** — nine sends in and there's still no "ends tonight" or "ends Sunday" deadline visible in the body; urgency is implied but never credible.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA copy is generic** — after nine sends, "shop 20% off makeup" or "shop your skin type" would do more work than the default.
- **Cadence fatigue** — the brand history makes this explicit: this is send nine in six days. Even a perfectly executed email is fighting unsubscribe impulses at this volume.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the body with the offer, not the tagline.** The hero should say "20% off everything — makeup, skincare, SPF" and put the promo code or CTA immediately below. Move brand positioning to secondary real estate.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline, prominently.** "Ends midnight Sunday" or "Ends tonight" in the hero or immediately below it. This is the single highest-lift change available.
- 3. **Make "SHOP NOW" specific per section** — "Shop Lip Tints" / "Shop SPF" / "Shop Skincare 20% Off" turns generic CTAs into segmented entry points.
- 4. **Move "Cart Deco" up.** If this is targeting near-converters, it should come before the subscriber loses interest scrolling.
- 5. **Consider sending to an engaged-only segment.** At send nine, a suppressed fatigue segment would protect deliverability without sacrificing revenue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends + Family ends tonight — 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 20% off makeup, skincare + SPF`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your discount expires soon. Shop makeup, skincare, and SPF before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Made for all skin types — now 20% off through tonight.`
