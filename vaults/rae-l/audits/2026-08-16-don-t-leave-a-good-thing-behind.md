# Don't leave a good thing behind.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the same cart-abandonment template Skechers has run at least three times now — "SELLING FAST... ADD TO CART BEFORE THEY'RE GONE!" — and the subject line, "Don't leave a good thing behind," is a verbatim repeat of the 2026-08-03 send audited 13 days ago. Nothing in the creative has changed: same urgency banner, same hero product treatment, same "HERE'S SOME MORE GREAT OPTIONS" grid, same 40% off summer sale strip at the bottom.
- The bigger issue this time is the recommendation grid: the hero item is a men's/unisex GO WALK Workout Walker slip-in, but five of the six "more great options" underneath are Care Bears kids' sneakers and one BOBS leopard print — a jarring mismatch that reads like generic merchandising rather than anything based on the recipient's browse history. If I'm the person who looked at a workout walker, a wall of pastel Care Bears kids shoes does nothing for me.
- This lands as a low-effort repeat send. The layout renders cleanly and the 40% off offer is legible, but there's no new creative, no new subject, and a cross-sell block that actively undercuts relevance.

## What's working

- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") directly under the abandoned product.
- 40% off banner is a real, visible offer with a clear "SHOP THE SALE" link and dated validity in the fine print (8/14–8/19).
- Hero product image is large, well-lit, and product name is specified (not just "your item").

## What's weak

- Subject line and full template are an exact repeat of the 2026-08-03 send — zero creative refresh in under two weeks.
- Recommendation grid is badly mismatched to the hero item: a workout walker shoe is followed by five Care Bears/kids-themed sneakers, which reads as random filler, not personalization.
- "SELLING FAST" / urgency framing is the same manufactured scarcity beat flagged repeatedly in this brand's history (6/30, 7/16, 7/29 "limited availability" sends, 8/3 "leave a good thing behind") — no evidence of actual low stock.
- No loyalty/member pricing surfaced despite Skechers having a rewards program.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the recommendation logic first — if the hero is a men's/adult workout shoe, the "more great options" row should be adjacent adult styles, not kids' Care Bears sneakers. This is the single biggest credibility problem in the email.
- 2. Retire "Don't leave a good thing behind" — it's an exact repeat subject from 8/3. Rotate creative and copy on cart-abandonment sends after 2 weeks max.
- 3. Replace generic "SELLING FAST" with a real signal if available (actual low-stock count, or drop the urgency framing entirely and lead with the 40% off, which is the more honest and more compelling hook).
- 4. Surface loyalty/member points or reward tier progress near the CTA if the recipient is a rewards member — none is visible here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via standard MSO/table-based markup but ships with a broken unsubscribe link, no plain-text part, and multiple missing compliance headers — all confirmed by the automated QA scan.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returns HTTP 400**: `https://skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=u001.LNc6VorXOZu5avyAvDTfGQbua6CZqR1Er...` (link text: "Unsubscribe") — confirmed broken by QA probe. This is a hard compliance/deliverability defect, not just a UX issue (see §5).
- All other probed links route through the Attentive click-tracking redirector (`skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=...`), consistent with standard ESP link-wrapping; no anomalies found in the "web version" link.
- 25 of 37 material links were skipped by the QA probe (footer/utility/social deprioritized per probing methodology) — those are unverified, not confirmed-passing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **9 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`**, e.g.:
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/.../dde00662-...png` (Skechers logo)
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/.../737031d9-...jpg`
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/.../49468f73-...png` (Text)
  - App Store/Google Play badges, Curbside Pickup, Afterpay, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook, Youtube icons — all same `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/` path.
  Mixed content on HTTPS-loaded mail clients can cause these images to be blocked or flagged.
- **3 images missing `alt` text**:
  - `126150_NVBL_HERO_LG` (hero product image) — no alt attribute despite being a primary content image.
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`
  - Open-tracking pixel at `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` (low-impact, tracking pixel).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization blocks detected in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken (400)** — this is a CAN-SPAM violation risk, since the required functioning opt-out mechanism is inoperative.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than a true absence, but as observed, one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI (Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements) may not be supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay; flagged as unknown, not confirmed-failing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use Attentive's opaque `upn=` tracking token rather than visible UTM query parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, etc.) — standard for this ESP's link-wrapping, but means UTM alignment with the destination landing page cannot be verified from the wrapped URL alone (final destination is only resolved after redirect).
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment for the broken unsubscribe link since it 400s before resolving.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe link immediately** — it currently 400s, which is both a broken-experience and CAN-SPAM compliance issue.
2. **Serve all images over HTTPS** — update the 9 flagged `image.emails.skechers.com` asset URLs from `http://` to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in mail clients.
3. **Add `alt` text** to the hero image (`126150_NVBL_HERO_LG`) and `a6ec5198-...jpg` for accessibility and fallback-rendering when images are blocked.
4. **Add a plain-text MIME alternative** — current text part is 0 chars, which hurts spam-filter scoring and accessibility for text-only clients.
5. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent** at the MTA level (may be a relay-capture gap in QA rather than a true absence — confirm via raw header inspection outside AgentMail).
6. **Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass** via a direct header capture, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observed through this relay.
