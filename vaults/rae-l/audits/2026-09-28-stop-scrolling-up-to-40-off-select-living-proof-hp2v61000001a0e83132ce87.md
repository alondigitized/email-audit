# Stop scrolling: up to 40% off select Living Proof 🤍
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "up to 40% off select Living Proof" but that offer is nowhere near the top of this email — it's buried roughly two-thirds of the way down in a small purple "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" banner, and even there the screenshot shows no visible Living Proof product or branding tying back to the subject line. What actually opens the email is a "Complimentary Kérastase" gift-with-purchase banner, then a "Beauty's Most Wanted" hero, then an enormous new-arrivals grid (Kérastase, YSL, NARS, Sincerely Yours, MAC, Gisou, Sol de Janeiro, REFY) that has nothing to do with the subject's hair-deal promise.
- This is a bait-and-switch structure: the subject is written to get the click, but the body delivers a completely different, unrelated new-arrivals catalog first. A subscriber who opens specifically for the Living Proof deal has to scroll past 7+ unrelated product blocks to find it — most won't make it that far.
- Compared to the brand's recent run — 9/23's "Hair Daily Deals" (7/10) led immediately with a dedicated hair hero and scored well specifically because the subject and hero matched — this send regresses hard on that lesson. Judgement: this is one of the weaker structural executions in the recent history, saved only by decent individual product cards. Fix the hero-to-subject mismatch before anything else.

## What's working

- Individual product cards (YSL, NARS, MAC, Gisou, Sol de Janeiro, REFY) are clean, well-lit, and legible with clear pricing.
- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges are used consistently to flag exclusivity, which is a real differentiator.
- The green "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" seals are a nice trust/credibility signal for ingredient-conscious shoppers.
- No render bugs — images all loaded, text is crisp, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The subject's core promise (Living Proof, up to 40%) is not the hero — it's a small banner far down the page, and it isn't even visibly branded "Living Proof" in the render.
- The email leads with an unrelated GWP (Kérastase) and a generic "Beauty's Most Wanted" arrivals grid, forcing a long scroll before the subject's offer shows up.
- Visual hierarchy is muddled — with so many nearly-equal-weight product blocks stacked vertically, nothing reads as "the" offer at a glance.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency on the hair banner is disconnected from a subject line that doesn't mention urgency at all — inconsistent messaging between subject and body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Living Proof hair offer to the very top as the hero — match the subject/body pairing the way the 9/23 "Hair Daily Deals" send did (that one scored 7/10 specifically for this reason).
- 2. Cut the new-arrivals grid down to 2-3 items or move it below the hair offer entirely — don't let it eat the top of the email.
- 3. Add explicit Living Proof product imagery/branding to the hair banner so the subject's promise is visually confirmed immediately.
- 4. Either drop "TODAY ONLY" or make it the subject's headline urgency too, so the two aren't sending mixed signals.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your hair wants this 40% off Living Proof deal`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Frizz control, shine, and smoothing — all discounted through midnight.`
