- Category-specific framing (fragrance only) is sharper than the generic "sale favorites" grids from 9/5 and 9/6.
- Hero product shot (pink gradient bottle) and the hand-holding-bottle lifestyle photo are strong, on-brand visuals.
- Five-star customer testimonial adds real social proof, which prior audited sends lacked.

## What's weak

- Body copy under the Rêve headline is cut off at the image edge — an actual rendering/layout bug, not just tight copy.
- Subject line "Save 30% off fragrance" is nearly identical to 9/3's "Save 30% off select favorites" — same discount, same sentence shape, low differentiation for anyone who's seen this brand's last few sends.

- No urgency/deadline shown anywhere in the visible copy — "Labor Day Sale" implies a window but no explicit end date is on screen.

- 3. Differentiate the subject from 9/3's near-duplicate by naming the actual product (Rêve) instead of the generic category.
- 4. Given four sale sends in five days, vary hero creative more aggressively next time — this hero layout is visually close to 9/3–9/6's structure and asset it wouldn't be discouraging repeat opens as much.
