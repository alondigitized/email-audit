# This style has limited availability!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the same browse-abandonment shell Skechers ran on 2026-08-11 and 2026-08-19, under the same "This style has limited availability!" subject. The subject promises scarcity, but the body is "HOW ABOUT A SECOND LOOK? SEEN THIS BEFORE?" with nothing limited about it. The hero is the burgundy floral Slip-ins Relaxed Fit "Easy Going - Floral Moment", the same shoe as the 2026-09-29 send. A subscriber who saw that email yesterday is now seeing the same shoe again.
- The render is clean, and the 15% guests / 20% members banner is the first real discount this template has carried. But the banner sits below the fold, under the hero and a six-shoe grid. The fine print is also a problem. It says the offer was valid 9/23/26–9/28/26 and covers "20% OFF BOOTS & OUTDOOR STYLES", while the banner says "Thru 10/1" and quotes different percentages. A careful reader sees conflicting terms and a possibly expired promo.
- Verdict: a competent template with a mismatched subject, a repeated hero and contradictory offer terms. The offer is a good one, but it's buried.

## What's working

- The hero is large and clean, with no broken images. The product name and a navy "SHOP NOW" button sit directly under it.
- The 15% guests / 20% members split in the dark banner is easy to read and gives members a reason to sign in.
- The six-shoe grid uses seasonal boots and slip-ins, which suits late September.
- The footer has a clear text-to-join line, app badges, and Afterpay/Klarna, so there are plenty of secondary paths.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "limited availability", but the body is a soft "second look" nudge with no scarcity cue.
- The offer is buried below the hero and the grid. The hero shoe has no price and no discount callout.
- The fine print says the offer was valid "9/23/26 - 9/28/26" and is for "20% off boots & outdoor styles", while the banner says "Thru 10/1" and "15% for guests". The terms conflict.
- The six-shoe grid has tiny captions and is non-specific. Three of the six are near-duplicates of "Easy Going - Floral Moment Boots".
- The hero repeats yesterday's "This is perfect for you!" send. The cadence is near-daily with the same creative.
- The long category list (Women/Men/Kids/Clothing/New Arrivals/Sale) adds clutter and dilutes the single CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 15%/20% banner above the hero, or overlay it on the hero. Put "20% off for members through 10/1" next to the shoe.
- 2. Fix the fine print so the dates and percentages match the banner. Right now it reads as an expired offer.
- 3. Match the subject to the body. Either use real scarcity (low-stock sizes) or drop the "limited availability" claim.
- 4. Don't resend the same hero within 24 hours. Suppress it, or rotate in a different viewed item.
- 5. Cut the grid to 3 products with prices, and trim the footer category list.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Still eyeing the Easy Going Slip-ins?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Guests save 15%, members save 20% on boots and slip-ins through 10/1.`
