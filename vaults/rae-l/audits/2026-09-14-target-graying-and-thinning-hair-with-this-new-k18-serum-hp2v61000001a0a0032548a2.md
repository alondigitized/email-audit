# Target graying and thinning hair with this NEW K18 serum
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a K18 anti-graying, anti-thinning hair serum — but that's not what greets you when the email opens. The hero banner is a generic "50% off Select Beauty" flash sale (Drybar, Dr. Jart+, Verb), and you have to scroll past a "TRENDING NOW" placeholder banner before the actual K18 content — a genuinely well-built before/after scalp comparison with clinical stats — shows up. This is the same bait-and-switch pattern flagged in the 2026-09-09 "Hair Styling Awards" send and the 2026-09-10 "Stacking Weekend" send: subject line optimized for one product, inbox click lands you somewhere else first.
- Once you get to the K18 block, it's the strongest part of the email — a real before/after scalp photo, "19,000 more hairs, 70% fewer greys" clinical claim, and clear SKU/pricing ($120 serum, $75 mask). That's exactly the kind of specific, persona-relevant content that should have been the hero, not buried under an unrelated 50% off promo and a content-free "Thank us later" banner.
- Net: strong individual components, poor sequencing. The K18 section alone would score well as a standalone send; wrapping it behind a mismatched hero and a filler banner drags the whole email down.

## What's working

- The K18 before/after scalp image is a genuinely compelling, specific visual — it's the kind of proof point that drives clicks in hair-thinning content.
- Clinical stats ("up to 19,000 more hairs, 70% fewer greys," 12-week study) give the offer real substance instead of vague "new and improved" copy.
- Pricing is transparent and visible right under the product claim ($120 serum, $75 mask), not buried.
- Beauty Insider tiers (Insider/VIB/Rouge) are visible at the footer, reinforcing loyalty relevance.
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breaks.

## What's weak

- Subject says K18/hair; hero banner says 50% off select beauty (Drybar, Dr. Jart+, Verb) — a mismatch, not a delivery of what the subject promised.
- The "TRENDING NOW / Thank us later" banner between the hero and the K18 section is pure filler — no offer, no product, just a CTA button with no information.
- The 50% off deal has an asterisk and "exclusions/terms apply. while supplies last" — the exact kind of fine print the rubric flags as eroding offer honesty.
- No urgency or personalization anywhere in the subject or hero — this reads as the 6th+ near-identical Sephora send in two weeks per prior audits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: lead with the K18 before/after image and clinical stat immediately below the header, not three scroll-lengths down. This single fix would most improve open-to-click follow-through, since right now the promised content is effectively hidden.
- 2. Cut the "TRENDING NOW / Thank us later" banner entirely — it adds a scroll with zero information value between the mismatched hero and the actual content.
- 3. If the 50% off beauty deal must stay, demote it to a secondary module below the K18 section, or split into two separate sends targeted by interest.
- 4. Add a "shop the study" or "see full results" CTA directly under the clinical stat — right now the only CTA near K18 is a generic block, weaker than the specificity of the surrounding copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New K18 serum: fewer greys, more hair in 12 weeks`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clinically shown: up to 19,000 more hairs, 70% fewer greys.`
