# Loved by you, now 25% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth sale-is-on send in five days, and it is mostly a recap. The hero is "Get 25% off everything!*" with a blue "Shop now" button. Below it sit three five-star review cards and a repeat of the free Crème de You offer from the 11/21 "A gift for you: Crème de You" send. Nothing is new, and the subject's "Loved by you" hook only pays off in the middle third.
- The structure works: offer first, social proof second, gift-with-purchase third. The reviews are the best part, because they are the one thing the previous Black Friday sends didn't have. But the render looks cropped on the right edge. The top banner, hero circles, third review, gift headline and footer hand all clip. The hero also shows two competing badges, "30% OFF $100+" and "25% OFF EVERYTHING". That repeats the muddle from the 11/18 send.
- Verdict: a solid reminder with a good social-proof idea, held back by clipped layout, stacked conditional offers and a cadence that is wearing thin.

## What's working

- "Get 25% off everything!*" is readable in one glance, and the blue "Shop now" button is easy to find.
- The review module ("Believe the love") has star ratings, named quotes and product names for Glossier You, Boy Brow and Lash Slick. It adds credibility that the earlier sale blasts lacked.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" marquee band is a strong brand-colour divider that keeps the seasonal context.
- The copy is on-voice: "5-star staples" and "very limited time" fit the brand and tie the offer to the reviews.

## What's weak

- Content looks clipped at the right edge. I can see "spend $100" cut off in the top banner, "EVERYTHIN" and "BLACK FRIDA" in the hero badges, a cut-off Lash Slick quote ("natural look tha…"), and the gift headline running off as "free Crème de You w…".
- The two badges, 30% off $100+ and 25% off everything, plus the free-gift asterisk, make three conditional offers in one email. The only terms are a tiny "*Terms apply" in the footer.
- "Very limited time" gives no date or deadline, so the urgency is not credible.
- Both CTAs read "Shop now" and none link to a named product. The reviews are not visibly clickable and have no "Shop Boy Brow" style button.
- This is repeat creative: the same 25% and Crème de You messaging as 11/19–11/21, so there is little reason for a subscriber to engage again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the right-edge clipping so the banner, hero badges, review cards and gift headline are fully visible. This is the highest-impact change because it affects the first screen.
- 2. Pick one headline offer. Drop the 30% badge, or explain it clearly as a tier ("25% off everything, 30% off $100+").
- 3. Put an explicit deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Monday 11:59pm PT", in place of "very limited time".
- 4. Make each review card its own link with a product CTA, such as "Shop Boy Brow", and use "Shop the 5-star favorites" for the hero button.
- 5. Skip the gift-with-purchase block on the next send, or merge it into one line, so it doesn't repeat 11/21.
- **Subject Alt A:** `5-star favorites, 25% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Reviewers love these. 25% off ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Boy Brow, Glossier You, Lash Slick: our most-reviewed staples, all 25% off through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free Crème de You with $100+. Sale ends Monday at 11:59pm PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-tracked XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a preheader, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and responsive media queries. The confirmed issues are six images without `alt` attributes and some invalid markup. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because of the relay, and all 12 link probes were skipped.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation is incomplete.** The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped all 12, so no link has a confirmed HTTP status or redirect chain. Nothing is confirmed broken, and nothing is confirmed working.
- **Non-standard tracking markup.** The body contains `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M82Q5580B0ZMS728DFQAZSEP_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. It sits ahead of the `<!--<![endif]-->` closer, which also looks misplaced. It is probably a Klaviyo injection artifact. It is harmless in most clients but fails HTML validation.
- **Unbranded tracking domain.** Click and open tracking go through `ctrk.klclick.com` (Klaviyo's shared domain), while the sender is `eml.glossier.com`. A custom branded tracking domain would align link and sender domains. This is an observation from the source, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Six images are missing alt text** (QA-confirmed):
  - `frame_3985.jpg`
  - `3colreview01.jpg`
  - `3colreview02.jpg`
  - `3colreview03.jpg`
  - `95339270_2980034135406876_2064437603473555456_n_1.…`, a UGC-style image, all hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`
  - the open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M82Q5580B0ZMS728DFQAZSEP`
- **Tracking pixel.** It should have `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip it. The `3colreview*` images look content-bearing, so they need descriptive alt text. With images blocked, those slots render as blank or broken boxes.
- **`<title>` mismatch.** The `<title>` is "Don't forget to shop 25% off!", but the subject line is "Loved by you, now 25% off". This is a minor inconsistency that only matters in web-view or archive contexts.
- **No other issues found.** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, the viewport meta tag, Outlook (MSO) fallbacks and the hidden preheader (`display:none; mso-hide:all`) are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the truncated source or the QA output. The source is truncated, so the footer and lower sections were not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN).** QA states the AgentMail relay may strip it. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed gap.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` was not found (WARN).** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, so it should be checked against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `eml.glossier.com` cannot be assessed from this capture.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source. The QA run reports that an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but the probe was skipped.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no destination URLs or UTM parameters, and the link probes were skipped, so landing-page alignment with the "25% off" offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four content images (`frame_3985.jpg`, `3colreview01–03.jpg`) and the UGC image. Give the open pixel `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
2. Check the original `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass in `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat the relay-based warnings as findings until then.
3. Re-run link probing, which was fully skipped, and confirm the discount CTAs return 200 with the expected UTM parameters and a promo code or landing page that matches "25% off".
4. Remove or fix the `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` artifact and the misplaced conditional comment close.
5. Align `<title>` with the subject line.
6. Consider a branded click-tracking domain in place of `ctrk.klclick.com`.
