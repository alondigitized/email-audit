# Build a look around blush
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA doing a routine-building/tutorial send — "Build a look around blush" walks the reader through a four-step face using Soft Focus Blurring Blush as the anchor product. It's a departure from the GWP-deadline beats (08-24 through 08-31) and closer in spirit to the clean product-launch template from 09-01, but with an editorial "how-to" structure layered on top. The flat-lay hero, numbered step list, and close-up final result shot are all well-executed and easy to follow.
- The weakness is the offer: "Get a free mini eye stylus w/ $75+" sits in a thin top banner and never resurfaces, so the actual incentive to buy today is buried under styling content. This reads as a mid-funnel engagement email, not a conversion push — fine as a cadence variety play, but it won't move the needle on urgency or click-through the way the Thru Line GWP arc did.

## What's working

- Clear numbered step structure (Base → Blush → Bigger Lashes → Bonus Blur) makes the routine easy to scan and follow.
- Product shots are clean and consistently styled against the neutral ILIA aesthetic.
- The final close-up face shot sells the actual result of the routine, which is more persuasive than product-only shots.
- "SHOP THE LOOK" CTA is overlaid directly on the payoff image, connecting the aspiration to the action in one glance.
- Allure "Readers' Choice" award badge next to the mascara adds a credible trust signal.

## What's weak

- The free-mini-with-$75+ offer is confined to a thin top banner and isn't reinforced anywhere else in the body — easy to miss, easy to forget by the time you scroll to the CTA.
- Two competing CTAs ("SUMMER LOOK →" and "SHOP THE LOOK →") do different things but look nearly identical, creating ambiguity about which one actually leads to checkout.
- No pricing or bundle offer for the 4-product routine — a "shop this look" moment is a natural place to show a bundle discount, and none is shown.
- Subject line ("Build a look around blush") is generic instructional copy with no urgency, offer, or specificity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Repeat the $75+ GWP offer as a persistent footer or sticky strip near the final CTA — right now it only exists in the top banner and gets lost by the time the reader is ready to act.
- 2. Differentiate the two CTAs — e.g. "SHOP THE ROUTINE" for the top step-by-step vs. "SHOP THIS LOOK" for the bottom payoff shot — so it's clear they serve different intents.
- 3. Add a bundle price or "get the 4-step set" callout near the bottom CTA to convert routine-builders into full-cart buyers.
- 4. Tighten the subject to reference the actual incentive, not just the activity.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your everyday blush routine (+ free mini gift)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 steps to your best blush look yet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Soft Focus Blurring Blush, styled top to bottom.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: a free mini eye stylus with $75+.`
