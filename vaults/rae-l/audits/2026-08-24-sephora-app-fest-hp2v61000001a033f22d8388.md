# ✨ Sephora App Fest ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a return to the "App Fest" concept Sephora already ran on 2026-08-17 ("💫 Sephora App Fest starts today 💫", scored 7/10) — same calendar structure, same day-by-day discount reveal, same Beauty Insider bonus-points push. A week later, it's the identical creative skeleton with a fresh trial-size teaser bolted on top and a new roster of brands (Tarte/Dyson, Anastasia/Make Up For Ever/Amika, Sephora Collection, Charlotte Tilbury). The calendar format is still the strongest structural idea in Sephora's current rotation — it reads more like a planner than another catalog dump — but running the same event skeleton twice in eight days starts to feel like a template being refilled rather than a distinct moment.
- The trial-size hero at the very top ("Choose a Trial Size... Get a Medik8 retinal serum, AESTURA moisturizer, or Ulta Violette sunscreen") is oddly disconnected from App Fest itself — it's a different offer, competing for the same above-the-fold real estate the actual campaign needs. By the time "APP FEST" appears, a scroller has already spent their first glance on an unrelated GWP mechanic.
- Overall this is a competent, on-brand execution of a format that's already proven itself once this month — solid mid-tier, dragged down by repetition and a cluttered opening.

## What's working

- The Tuesday–Friday calendar layout is genuinely scannable — each day gets a discrete card with its own discount badge and brand logos, no wall-of-thumbnails scroll.
- Concrete, dated offers throughout ("25% OFF... Tuesday, 8/25," "Ends 8/28") give real urgency instead of vague "limited time" language.
- The Beauty Insider "+500 Bonus Points" card is visually distinct (purple badge, clear "JOIN NOW" CTA) and ties the campaign to loyalty status, which rewards existing members.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent pink/purple/yellow color-blocking that reads as one campaign.

## What's weak

- The top-of-email trial-size offer is a different promotion competing with App Fest for the first screen — it dilutes the hero moment rather than building toward it.
- "APP FEST" banner text has almost no contrast information beyond the name itself; a first-time scanner doesn't learn *what kind* of deals are coming until they scroll past it.
- Every single offer is stamped with "Exclusions/terms apply" — repeated five separate times — which reads as defensive rather than confident and slows trust-building.
- This is structurally identical to the 8/17 App Fest send; a subscriber who opened that one gets déjà vu, not a new reason to engage.
- CTA copy is "SHOP NOW" on every single block (six-plus repetitions) — no differentiation by day or brand, so nothing points a shopper toward what's actually best for *them*.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or move the trial-size GWP block below the App Fest calendar — let App Fest own the hero position it's named for in the subject line.
- 2. Vary CTA copy per day/brand ("Shop Tarte + Dyson," "Shop Charlotte Tilbury") instead of six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons — gives scanners a reason to look at each block individually.
- 3. Consolidate the "exclusions/terms apply" language into a single footnote instead of repeating it five times — it currently reads as a wall of caveats.
- 4. Since this is a repeat of the 8/17 structure, differentiate the subject/preheader explicitly ("Round 2" / "New brands this week") so loyal openers don't dismiss it as the same email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New brands, same App Fest energy 💫`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off Tarte + Dyson starts tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tuesday: Tarte, Dyson & Sephora Collection at 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider points just got more valuable`
