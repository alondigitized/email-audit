# Makeup that makes your skin better
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA doubling down on the VIP sale — a third consecutive send after 09-12's "The ILIA VIP Sale starts now" and 09-13's "VIPs get the best + 20% off." The subject has quietly pivoted away from the sale itself ("Makeup that makes your skin better") toward a skin-benefit claim, but the hero banner still leads with "20% OFF SITEWIDE FOR VIPs" and a "20%" starburst, so the creative hasn't caught up to the copy shift. Below the fold it turns into a straight product-benefit catalog for the Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, stacked with individual SKU cards (concealer, foundation, complexion stick, powder), then a color-match quiz, GWP tiers, and a VIP-perks recap.
- This is a long, dense send that's really two emails stitched together: a VIP-gated sale banner up top and a skin-tint product education piece underneath. The 100%/100%/100% stat block on a real skin close-up is the strongest single asset in the email — it's specific, visual, and on-brand. But by the fourth near-identical send in this sale cadence (09-12 → 09-13 → 09-14), fatigue is a real risk, and the subject/hero mismatch will cost clicks from anyone scanning fast.

## What's working

- The 100%/100%/100% skin-tint stat block over a real, textured close-up skin photo is a strong, credible, non-generic proof point.
- Clear visual hierarchy at the top: eye goes starburst → hero product → "THE ILIA VIP SALE" badge → headline, in that order.
- Each SKU card is consistent and scannable — shade count, coverage, finish, and benefit listed identically across concealer, foundation, complexion stick, and powder.
- GWP section ("gifts at checkout") is concrete — free multi-stick on all orders, free velvet bag on $100+ — with visible product photography, not just text.
- No render bugs: images loaded cleanly, no overlapping text, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- Subject line ("Makeup that makes your skin better") doesn't match the hero creative, which is 100% sale-focused — a scanning reader sees no connection between subject and top banner.
- This is the third VIP-sale-themed send in three days (09-12, 09-13, now 09-14); nothing here signals urgency/deadline or why today's email is different from yesterday's.
- The email is very long and multi-purpose (sale banner + skin tint launch + 4 SKU cards + quiz + GWP + VIP recap) — no single clear primary CTA, just six-plus competing "Shop Now" buttons.
- "20% off" appears only in tiny top-banner text and the starburst — it's never restated near the skin-tint hero or SKU cards, so anyone who scrolls past the top loses the offer entirely.
- No deadline/expiration is visible anywhere for the 20% off, which after three sale emails in a row starts to feel indefinite rather than time-bound.

## Recommendations

- 1. Align subject and hero: either commit the subject to the sale ("VIP Sale: 20% off is still on") or drop the sale banner and let the skin-tint stats be the actual hero — right now they're fighting each other.
- 2. Add a visible deadline/countdown to the 20% off banner — three sale sends with no stated end date reads as a permanent discount, which trains subscribers to wait.
- 3. Restate "20% off" or "VIP pricing" on at least the skin-tint hero and the first SKU card, since most readers will never make it back up to the thin top banner.
- 4. Cut this to one primary narrative — either the sale send or the skin-tint launch — and move the other into its own email; right now six+ CTAs dilute the click decision.
- 5. Vary the creative from 09-12/09-13 — a third near-identical VIP banner in three days risks list fatigue; lead with something visually distinct even if the offer is the same.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `VIP pricing ends soon. New Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 inside.`
