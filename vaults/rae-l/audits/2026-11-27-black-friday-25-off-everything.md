# Black Friday: 25% off everything
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday launch email, and it lands on the day of the sale itself, after a week of recaps. The hero is loud: a saturated blue panel with a giant "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" next to the headline "Enjoy 25% off everything*" and a clear "Shop Black Friday" button. The eye goes straight to the offer, which is the right job for a hero.
- The body undercuts it. The 25% claim collides with a "30% OFF $100+" badge clipped at the top right of the hero, a "free Glossier You Candle when you spend $100" banner at the top, and a candle block at the bottom. That is three offers in one email, and the render is clipped on the right edge in several places. Compared with the 11/24 fragrance send (8/10), which had one idea, this one tries to say everything. Judgement: a solid, on-brand send that is less focused than it should be on the biggest day of the year.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: giant "25%" in white on electric blue, with a plain headline and a single button.
- Copy matches the moment. "Our biggest sale of the year is now live!" is credible urgency for 11/27.
- The four product tiles (Glossier You, Balm Dotcom, After Baume, Boy Brow) each have a name, a one-line descriptor and a "Shop now" button, so each is clearly linked.
- The "Black Friday Sale" ticker band and the candle block carry the gift-with-purchase beat from 11/26 and the earlier candle send.
- The brand voice is consistent: clean, minimal and recognizable.

## What's weak

- Offer stacking muddies the message. The hero says 25% off everything, the badge says 30% off $100+, and the top banner and bottom block offer a free candle at $100+. The reader has to work out which deal applies.
- Several elements are clipped at the right edge: the top banner ("spend $100"), the "30% OFF" badge, the "OFF" on the 25% graphic, and the second ticker "BLACK FRIDAY SALE". These look like layout bugs.
- Four of the product tiles have no price or discounted price. Reviews and social proof are also missing, despite the "Community Favorites" label.
- The candle offer is the sixth or seventh near-repeat of the 11/26 and 11/27 candle and Crème de You sends, so it feels like fatigue.
- The footer smile and hand graphics are cropped and add nothing. The asterisked terms are tiny at the bottom, and the hero offers no hint of the exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. Lead with 25% off everything, and move the $100+ tier and the candle into a single, clearly separate line under the hero. Drop the competing 30% badge.
- 2. Fix the right-edge clipping on the banner, badge, tickers and footer graphics.
- 3. Add sale prices to the product tiles ("$18 → $13.50") and one review snippet per tile.
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm ET", to the hero.
- 5. Reduce the near-duplicate candle content that was sent earlier in the day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our biggest sale: 25% off, today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Glossier You Candle on $100+. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Balm Dotcom, Boy Brow and more, all 25% off.`
