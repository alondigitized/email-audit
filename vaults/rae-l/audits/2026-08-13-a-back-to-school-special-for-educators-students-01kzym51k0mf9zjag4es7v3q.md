# A back to school special for educators + students
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a complete departure from ILIA's usual visual playbook — no hero image, no product shots, no brand styling, just plain black text on white, formatted like a personal note from "Marissa (from ILIA)." Compared to the brand's recent run (the polished "Art of the Eye Trio" at 9/10, the seasonal "Summer Skin" push at 9/10), this reads like a lifecycle/plain-text email rather than a campaign send, and it's a jarring downgrade in production value even if the plain-text format is intentional for authenticity.
- The offer itself is solid and well-targeted — 15% off year-round for students/teachers via ID verification is a real, ongoing loyalty-style program, not a one-off gimmick — but the execution buries it. There's no visible discount code, no button, just a text link ("Our Student Teacher Program") and a two-step verify-then-wait-for-an-email flow that adds friction before any purchase can happen.
- Judgment: the concept (educator/student discount) is a good segment play, but this specific render is underbuilt for a paid beauty brand — it reads like a transactional confirmation, not a campaign email, and would benefit from at least minimal branding and a clear CTA button.

## What's working

- The plain-text, personal-note format ("Marissa (from ILIA)") could read as authentic and trustworthy rather than salesy, if intentional.
- The offer is concrete and ongoing: 15% off all products year-round, not a vague "save big" tease.
- The P.S. forward-to-a-friend line is a smart low-cost virality mechanic targeted precisely at the segment (students/teachers).
- No render bugs — text is clean, links are visibly styled, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- Zero visual branding — no logo, no hero image, no color, nothing to signal this is ILIA at a glance if forwarded or seen out of context.
- No clear CTA button — the only actionable elements are small inline text links, easy to miss on mobile.
- No visible discount code or amount reinforcement beyond the paragraph text — nothing scannable for a skimming reader.
- The redemption flow has real friction: click, verify, wait for a follow-up email with a one-time code — three steps before any code exists.
- Feels categorically different from every recent ILIA send in this history (all scored 7-9 with strong visual hierarchy); this could read as a phishing-style plain email in an inbox trained on ILIA's usual polish.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add minimal branding — logo header and one product image tied to a "back to school" moment — even a lightly-styled HTML wrapper around this same copy would restore trust and scannability.
- 2. Turn "Our Student Teacher Program" into a visible button ("Verify My Student/Teacher ID") — a text link this small will get missed, especially on mobile.
- 3. Surface the offer as a headline stat ("15% Off, Year-Round") above the fold instead of burying it in paragraph two.
- 4. Consider pre-empting the friction by explaining the value of the wait ("takes under 2 minutes") near the verify link, since a code-by-email flow needs reassurance it's fast.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Students + teachers: 15% off, all year`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Your ID = 15% off ILIA, year-round`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Verify your status, get your code in minutes`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Clean makeup for your busiest semester yet`
