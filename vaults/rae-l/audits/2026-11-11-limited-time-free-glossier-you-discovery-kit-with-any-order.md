# LIMITED TIME: Free Glossier You Discovery Kit with ANY order
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, single-minded offer: a free Glossier You Discovery Kit with any order, through Thursday. After the 11/5–11/9 run of tumbler and merch catalog sends with no offer, a real incentive is a welcome change. The hero is clean. The red kit box and vials sit beside plain copy that says "Offer automatically applied at checkout," and the "Get yours" button is obvious.
- The body then turns into a four-fragrance catalog (You, Doux, Fleur, Rêve) with identical "Shop now" buttons. The product storytelling is good: "You smell good / rich / pretty / yummy" is on-brand, and the scent notes are useful. But it pulls attention away from the free kit. A visible rendering flaw also hurts it: the text in the right-hand column is clipped.
- Judgement: a good offer let down by a repetitive body, an all-caps subject, and a cadence that is getting heavy. It's better than the recent merch sends, but not by as much as it should be.

## What's working

- The offer is concrete and low-friction: a free kit with "ANY order," applied automatically at checkout.
- "NOW THROUGH THURSDAY" gives a credible, short deadline.
- The hero puts the product, headline and "Get yours" button together above the fold.
- The scent copy ("You smell rich," "You smell yummy") is on-brand, and the notes (Iris, Pink Pepper, Plum Butter) give real product detail.
- The alternating image/text layout and the photography feel premium and consistent with prior Glossier sends.

## What's weak

- Text is clipped at the right edge in the "You smell good" and "You smell pretty" blocks. It reads "best-sell" and "Glossier Y".
- Four near-identical "Shop now" buttons below the hero compete with "Get yours" and dilute the one offer.
- Nothing in the body repeats the free-kit offer or deadline after the hero, so a scroller loses it.
- "Terms apply" is a tiny asterisk link at the very bottom. "ANY order" is the headline claim, so the terms (any minimum, exclusions) deserve more visibility.
- The footer is a large pink block with a smiley and a hand illustration that is cropped at the right edge. It adds visual weight but no function.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped text in the right-column modules. It is the only visible render bug and it undercuts the polish.
- 2. Collapse the four fragrance blocks into one compact row or grid, with a single CTA back to the kit, or repeat the offer banner ("Free kit through Thursday") before the footer. The email should have one job.
- 3. Make the hero button more specific, e.g. "Claim my free kit," and add the "no minimum" or terms detail near it.
- 4. Drop the all-caps "LIMITED TIME:" and "ANY" from the subject. Say the offer and the deadline plainly in a shorter line.
- 5. Space sends out. Given the 11/5, 11/6 and 11/9 sends, a clear offer deserves a cleaner inbox slot.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built email has no automated errors, only 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The confirmed defects are 5 content images with no `alt` text and a malformed `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` tag. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links go through Klaviyo click tracking (`ctrk.klclick.com/l/...`), so the final destinations and any UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML.
- 12 material links were probed and 6 were skipped, because footer, utility and social links are deprioritized. The QA output reports no failures, but it also doesn't list status codes, so it confirms nothing about link health. The unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't shown.
- The body opens with `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M742GHW100KQVYSXM6TSR1ZQ_0"></span>`. A `<span>` has no valid `rel` or `href` attributes, so this is invalid markup. It looks like a Klaviyo or ESP injection that is meant to be a tracked link.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M742GHW100KQVYSXM6TSR1ZQ` has no `alt`. This is minor, and a pixel should have `alt=""`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The destination URLs are hidden behind Klaviyo redirects, and the QA output doesn't give resolved final URLs. I can't confirm that UTM parameters survive the redirect.
