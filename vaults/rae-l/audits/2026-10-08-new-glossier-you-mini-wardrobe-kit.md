# NEW! Glossier You Mini Wardrobe Kit 🎀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-art-directed product announcement for a kit that has already been pushed three times in the last two weeks. The hero is the Glossier You Mini Wardrobe Kit: four small scent bottles in a gift box on pink crumpled paper, with a "Shop now" button. The body then walks through the four scents (Doux, You, Soie, Fleur), each framed as a gift for a type of person or moment. That framing is the best idea in the send.
- There is no price, no offer and no urgency anywhere. A reader can't tell what the kit costs or whether it's available for a limited time. The 10/6 gift guide had the same gap. The 9/22, 9/26 and 9/28 sends were all built around the free Mini Glossier You Wardrobe, so "NEW!" reads as the same product coming back as a paid item. The subject doesn't say that the free-gift offer is gone or that this is now a purchase.
- The layout has a visible bug. On the Doux, Soie and the first row's right-hand copy, the text runs to the edge of the frame and clips ("makes people say...", "tropical vacation", "bea..."). Five identical "Shop now" buttons also give the eye nowhere to go. It's pretty and gift-appropriate, but it works as a catalog page, not a sales email.

## What's working

- The hero image is strong. The four bottles in the gift box on pink paper make the product obvious at a glance.
- The "For their dinner parties / weekday errands / winter getaways / Sunday mornings" copy turns each scent into a gift idea. It fits the holiday season and is more useful than a list of notes.
- The zig-zag image and text layout is easy to scan and keeps the scroll moving.
- Each scent has a short, on-voice line ("You smell rich") that sounds like Glossier.
- The closing block links to the 2026 Gift Guide, which gives a natural next step for anyone not sold on the kit.

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer or deadline. The kit's cost never appears, so the email asks for a click without saying what you'd pay.
- The copy clips at the right edge in the Doux, Soie and "Fleur"-adjacent blocks, so sentences are cut off mid-line ("makes", "tropical vacation", "bea").
- All five "Shop now" buttons are identical, and none of them names the product or scent.
- The "NEW!" subject ignores the earlier free-gift sends. Someone who saw "Free Mini Glossier You Wardrobe" on 9/28 gets no explanation of why it's now a paid product.
- The hero headline is plain and small. The first screen has little visual weight beyond the image, and the cookie photo at the bottom is unrelated to fragrance.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price or a value claim in the hero (for example "$XX for four minis") and state whether any gift-with-purchase still applies. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Fix the clipped text so every block fits within its column.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones: "Shop the kit" in the hero and "Shop Doux", "Shop You", "Shop Soie", "Shop Fleur" in the body.
- 4. Add a gift-by date or shipping cutoff so there is a credible reason to buy now.
- 5. Add a price or reviews line under each scent so the scent blocks give more than mood.
- 6. Swap the cookie image for a fragrance or gifting image, or cut it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-sent (`ctrk.klclick.com`), Stripo-built email with valid `lang`/`dir` and a viewport meta. The confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes on 6 content images and the tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay capture limits, not confirmed sender defects. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body links could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 7 were skipped. The skipped links are unverified, not broken.
- **Stray tracked `href` on a `<span>`.** The body opens with `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M4CCFMFNMVK18MX2TTV6JQ0E_0"></span>`. `href` and `rel="stylesheet"` have no function on a `<span>`, so this looks like a Klaviyo/Stripo artifact. It is harmless to rendering, but it puts a tracked URL in invalid markup. Some scanners may follow it as a click.
- **Open-tracking pixel present.** The pixel is `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M4CCFMFNMVK18MX2TTV6JQ0E`. It has no `alt` (see section 3). It is also blocked by default in Apple MPP-off clients and by image-blocking clients, so open data is unreliable.
- No broken links were reported among the probed links.

Not assessable. Every link is wrapped in a `ctrk.klclick.com/l/…` redirect, and the destination URLs and UTM parameters are not in the truncated source or the QA output. Whether the redirects land on a page matching the "Glossier You Mini Wardrobe Kit" subject is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 6 Stripo images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or fix the `<span rel="stylesheet" href="…klclick.com/l/…">` artifact in the template.
3. Check the raw headers at the origin for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC). Do not rely on the relay copy.
4. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. Then verify the unsubscribe link, the physical address, the 7 skipped links, and the final URLs and UTM parameters after the Klaviyo redirects.
