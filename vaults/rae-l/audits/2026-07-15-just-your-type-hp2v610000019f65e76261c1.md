# 💘JUST YOUR TYPE 💘
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- "Just Your Type" leans into a matchmaking/personalization metaphor that's clever in concept but jarring in July — the double heart emoji and romance-pun subject land like a Valentine's Day draft that missed the calendar by five months. Inside, the execution is a multi-section editorial-meets-commerce send: a thin "Buy 2 Minis, Get Both 50% Off" banner sits above a vague "This is for YOU" hero featuring a new Jo Malone London launch, followed by a curated product grid, a 3X Points loyalty push, a leave-in conditioner section, and a new ONE/SIZE by Patrick Starrr launch. Five things simultaneously, none of them dominant.
- The 50% off minis deal and the 3X Points offer are genuine hooks for a Beauty Insider member — but they're attached to a hero that promises personalization it never delivers. "This is for YOU" with no quiz integration, no name, and no category tailoring is table-stakes copy dressed up as something special. Compare this to the July 13 send ("New products have arrived"), which at least named what was new; this email implies a curated match and then shows a standard multi-category grid.
- Verdict: a serviceable multi-product send that punches below its weight. The romantic subject creates expectations the body can't meet, and the personalization promise is pure theater.

- **Multi-category coverage** (fragrance, makeup, hair care) spreads the net and increases the chance something lands for any given subscriber.

## What's weak

- **"JUST YOUR TYPE" + hearts in mid-July** reads as a Valentine's Day draft that slipped — the seasonal mismatch undercuts inbox credibility before the email opens.
- **"This is for YOU" hero is empty personalization** — the matchmaking framing implies a quiz or tailored picks; nothing inside is actually tailored.
- **Five content blocks compete with no single hero** — minis banner, Jo Malone launch, curated grid, 3X Points, leave-ins, ONE/SIZE new launch — each dilutes the next.
- **Zero urgency visible anywhere** — neither the 50% off banner nor the 3X Points carry a visible end date, removing any reason to act now vs. tomorrow.
- **"SHOP NOW" as the primary CTA** is generic; it could link anywhere and says nothing about the offer driving the click.

- 2. **Make the hero do one job** — lead with either the Jo Malone launch as an editorial feature OR the minis deal as a commerce send. The current hybrid dilutes both.

- 4. **Rewrite the primary CTA** to match the offer — "Get 50% Off Both" or "Shop the Mini Sale" passes the verb + noun specificity test; "SHOP NOW" doesn't.

**Confirmed issue — viewport `maximum-scale=1`:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
This disables user-initiated zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 — Resize Text). Users with low vision cannot enlarge content. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**No dark mode CSS detected:** No `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block is present in the embedded stylesheet. On iOS Mail and macOS Mail with dark mode enabled, black text on dark backgrounds or inverted image colors is possible. Increasingly standard to include.

```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
Correct value is `noindex` (no space). While most crawlers handle this gracefully, it is technically incorrect per the spec.
