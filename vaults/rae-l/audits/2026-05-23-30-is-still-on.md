# 30% is still on!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a day-two sale-extension reminder for Glossier's Memorial Day 30%-off event — the same creative framework as yesterday's "Memorial Day Sale starts NOW" send, retooled with a softer "still on!" angle. The offer stack is genuinely good: 30% sitewide, 50% off Saving Room products, and a free Lip Glaze GWP on $75+. Three product blocks with real star-rated customer quotes (Body Form, Monochromes, No. 1 Pencils) add meaningful conversion support below the hero.
- Where it loses points is urgency and originality. "30% is still on!" doesn't tell you when it ends — there's no deadline visible anywhere in the email, which makes "still on!" feel like a passive shoulder-tap rather than a countdown. The GWP is the strongest new hook over yesterday's send, but it's buried below three product blocks instead of sitting in the hero where it could actually drive incremental orders.
- Solid fundamentals, low ambition. If you opened yesterday, this one reads as déjà vu with smaller type.

## What's working

- **Multi-tier offer clearly stated above the fold**: 30% off sitewide + 50% Saving Room + free Lip Glaze with $75+ are all legible in the first hero module.
- **Social proof is specific and functional**: Three named customer quotes with 5-star ratings tied to actual SKUs (not generic brand praise) — these do real work.
- **GWP is a genuine sweetener**: Free Lip Glaze with $75+ is a meaningful add-on that could move fence-sitters to cart completion.
- **Clean, bug-free render**: No broken images, no overlapping text, layout is tight and on-brand throughout.
- **Subject line is mobile-perfect**: 17 characters renders without truncation on any client.

## What's weak

- **No end-date anywhere**: "Still on!" signals urgency without delivering it — no "ends Sunday," "48 hours left," or any clock visible in the email.
- **GWP is buried**: The free Lip Glaze is the best new creative hook over yesterday's send and it sits below the fold after three product blocks.
- **Four identical "Shop now" CTAs**: Every section resolves to the same undifferentiated button copy; no signal to the reader about what they're clicking into.
- **Double exclusion hedge**: "(almost) everything" in the headline plus "* Some apply" in the footer footnote is two separate hedges on the same promise — one undercuts the other.
- **Hero SALE stacking is a straight reuse**: The exact "SALE MEMORIAL DAY SALE" tile from yesterday's send is recycled; subscribers who opened both get no new creative signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Inject the deadline.** This is the single biggest lift available. Without an end-date, "still on" is passive. "Last day: 30% off everything" or "Sale ends tonight" changes the behavioral math immediately.
- 2. **Move the free Lip Glaze to the hero.** Make it a second hero line or a dedicated callout directly below the main offer module — not the last thing above the footer.
- 3. **Differentiate CTA copy per block.** "Shop Body Form," "Shop Monochromes," "Claim your free Lip Glaze" — each button should close the loop on what the subscriber is clicking into.
- 4. **Consolidate the exclusion language.** Use one hedge, not two. Either keep "(almost) everything" or the asterisk footnote — not both.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 30% off (almost) everything`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale ends tonight — 30% off + free Lip Glaze`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 50% off Saving Room + a free Lip Glaze with $75+. Don't miss it.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free Lip Glaze yours with any $75+ order. 30% off ends tonight.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Invalid element used as tracking hook**
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KS92561ACNHCRD3J53F7XD81_0"></span>
```
`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This sits inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` so Outlook never sees it, but strict-parsing clients (some webmail sandboxes, aggressive spam filters) may strip the `href` silently, causing Klaviyo's link rewrite/open tracking to fail for those recipients. If this is an open-tracking pixel, it should be an `<img>` with a 1×1 transparent src; if it is a click-tracked URL, it must be an `<a>`.

**Scope limitation:** The HTML is truncated before the body links appear. UTM parameter coverage and destination URL integrity cannot be verified from the provided source.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Preheader padding with ZWNJ characters**
```
Shop the Memorial Day Sale ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏…
```
U+200C (zero-width non-joiner) characters are used to pad the preheader. This is a widely-used technique and renders correctly in major clients, but it is invisible to screen readers and will be read aloud as whitespace bursts by some AT implementations. Low severity, noted for completeness.

**MSO conditional structure:** Properly formed `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso]>`, and `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` guards are all in place. `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` and `<w:WordDocument>` blocks are correctly wrapped in `<noscript>` and `<xml>` respectively.
