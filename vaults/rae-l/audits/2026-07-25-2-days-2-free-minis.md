# 2 days. 2 free minis.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This send drops the visual layer entirely — it's rendered as flat, unstyled text with no header, no product photography, no CTA button. That's a real departure from ILIA's usual polished lip/skin editorial hero shots (see "The gift of glaze," "These go well with free gloss…"). The mechanic itself is fine: free mini Overglaze Lip Gloss with any order, plus a free ILIA Mini Tote at $75+, both gated to 48 hours.
- But the execution reads more like a plain-text fallback than a finished campaign email — everything is hyperlinked words in a wall of paragraphs, with no visual anchor to tell the eye where to land first. Compared to the pairing-concept send from 07-24 (8/10, hero lip image, one clean CTA), this is a clear step down in production value even though the offer stacking (2 GWPs) is arguably more generous.
- Judgment: the offer is good, the execution is not — this reads like a broken or degraded render rather than an intentional plain-text strategy.

## What's working

- Two stacked, concrete GWP mechanics (free lip gloss on any order, free tote at $75+) are stated clearly and early.
- The 48-hour window gives real, credible urgency without resorting to caps-lock or exclamation spam.
- Product list at the bottom cross-sells four specific SKUs with one-line descriptors, not just generic "shop now" links.
- No broken images or overlapping text — because there are effectively no visual elements to break.

## What's weak

- No hero image, no branded header, no product photography anywhere — the email is indistinguishable from a plain-text fallback version.
- No CTA button at all; every action is a bare blue hyperlink, so there's no visual hierarchy pointing the reader to a single next step.
- The tote offer's fine print is buried behind a bare asterisk ("*See site for details.") with no explanation of what the tote looks like or its value.
- Nothing here signals membership/loyalty tier relevance, even though ILIA has run tiered/loyalty messaging before.

## Recommendations

- 1. Restore a proper hero module — a product shot of the mini lip gloss + tote side by side, with a single bold CTA button ("Claim Your Free Minis") above the fold. This is the single biggest lift available; right now there's nothing for the eye to land on.
- 2. Turn the two GWP tiers into a visual ladder (e.g., a simple two-step graphic: "Any order → free gloss" / "$75+ → + free tote") instead of burying the tote condition in paragraph three.
- 3. Replace the bare `*See site for details` with a one-line plain-English caveat (e.g., "while supplies last") so the offer doesn't feel like it's hiding terms.
- 4. Add at least one thumbnail per linked product in the "cart inspo" list — bare text links under a beauty brand undersell the products.
