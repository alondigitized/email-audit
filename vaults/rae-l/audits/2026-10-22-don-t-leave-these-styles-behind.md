# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' browse-abandonment shell again. Subject, headline and template are the same ones used on 10/17 ("Don't leave these styles behind!"), 10/18 and 10/19, and the cart-abandonment send earlier today ("Did you forget something?") was only a few hours ago. The new content is a six-shoe grid and the navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner, now rolled forward to run through 10/26.
- The email is clean and easy to scan. The weakness is that the only real incentive sits below the product grid, so the opening screen is a headline and a generic "Shop Now" button. A subscriber who has seen this four times in a week will treat it as wallpaper.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but it's the fifth send of the same message in a week. It would be a 7 on its own, and the repetition holds it back.

## What's working

- The headline "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" is large, clear and friendly, and it signals the browse-abandonment reason for the email.
- The 20% off banner is high contrast, with a specific offer, a $5 reward on $75+ and a credible deadline (valid through 10/26).
- The six-product grid shows varied women's styles with product names, so each tile reads as a distinct, linkable item.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer utilities (app, curbside pickup, Afterpay/Klarna, store finder) are tidy and useful.

## What's weak

- The 20% off offer is below the product grid, so it isn't visible on a first mobile screen.
- "SHOP NOW" is generic and doesn't name a product or the offer.
- The tiles show no prices, sale prices, ratings or sizing, so there is nothing to confirm interest.
- The fine print is long and exclusionary (online only, excludes sale items, requires login, excludes prior purchases), and it undercuts the "20% off" banner above it.
- The page ends with a long stack of generic navigation (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals, Sale) and a text-signup block, which dilutes the abandonment message.
- The creative and subject are close to identical to the 10/17 to 10/19 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% members banner above the product grid, or into the hero, so the offer and the deadline are the first thing seen.
- 2. Change the CTA to something specific, such as "Get 20% off my styles", and put prices on the tiles. Show the member price if possible.
- 3. Add one reason to act, such as a low-stock flag on a specific style or a ratings badge on each tile.
- 4. Cap the cadence. Suppress this shell if a similar send went out in the past 72 hours, and rotate the subject.
- 5. Cut the lower navigation list and condense the fine print into a short "Details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your saved styles: 20% off ends 10/26`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? Members save 20%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get 20% off plus a $5 reward on $75+ through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Mark Nason Slip-ins and 5 more picks are waiting in your list.`
