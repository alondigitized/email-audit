# A cool kiss of color
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy lip-focused seasonal email whose strongest asset is a clean four-product lineup: crayon, balm, gloss and lipstick, each in a named shade with a one-line benefit. The red hero and the "Color's in Season" headline set a clear winter-lip mood. The weakness is the offer. A 30% off deal on Swipe + Go is buried below the fold in a second module, while the lip products the email leads with carry no price incentive.
- The email has two jobs and only one gets the hero treatment. The top half sells lip color editorially. The bottom half sells a different product, a cheek and lip stick, on discount. A reader who clicks "Shop Winter Lip Shades" gets no deal and no urgency. The email feels like a catalog page with a promo attached.
- Judgement: well-executed, on-brand and easy to scan, but the offer and the hero don't line up. It's a mid-tier send. The "A cool kiss of color" subject is pleasant, but it says nothing concrete.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and on-brand: a red gloss wand against a saturated red shape, with a matching serif headline.
- The four-product stack has a consistent pattern: product name, shade and a one-line benefit, with a model and a swatch for each. It is easy to scan.
- The model shots show a range of skin tones, so the shade story is credible.
- The top banner ("Free mini eye stylus w/ $75+") adds a second incentive at the very top.
- The footer trust row (free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, ILIA VIP) reassures without clutter.

## What's weak

- The 30% off offer sits below roughly 70% of the email, and it applies to a product the hero never mentions.
- The lip modules have no prices, no reviews and no star ratings. The individual products have no links I can identify from the image, only the two big buttons.
- There are two competing buttons plus a third ("Shop Now") in different colors: red for the first two, brown for the last. The brown button looks like a different brand.
- Product shots are small and set in circles, so the shade detail is hard to judge on mobile. The Color Block Lipstick image is partly cropped at the left edge.
- Fine print ("*See site for details", "Click HERE for ILIA Promotion Details") hides the terms of the 30% offer and the free mini stylus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% off to the hero and make it the headline, for example "30% off Swipe + Go lips and cheeks," with the lip lineup underneath. Alternatively, put a price or discount on the lip products.
- 2. Make each of the four lip products clickable, with a "Shop [shade]" link under each blurb. Add a star rating or a "bestseller" tag.
- 3. Use one button color throughout, and make the verbs specific ("Shop winter lip shades" is good; "Shop now" is vague).
- 4. Put the offer terms next to the offer (end date, exclusions) instead of in a footnote.
- 5. Enlarge the product images and drop the circle crop so the shade is visible.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Berry, cherry and marsala lips for winter`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Four lip formulas in cool cherry reds. Plus 30% off Swipe + Go for a limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free mini eye stylus with $75+. Shop the winter lip edit.`

Link validity, authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified (relay limits, rate limiting and a truncated HTML source), so most findings are unverified rather than failures.

  - `.../l/01M6BX4ZYS6Z9M70B3RN495ZNR_14` ("Join ILIA VIP")

- **Every link is click-wrapped** (`/l/<id>_<n>`). Destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible in the source, so they can't be audited.
- **The open-tracking pixel is present** at `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M6BX4ZYS6Z9M70B3RN495ZNR`. This is expected behavior and not an issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image.** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` has no `alt`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no description.
