# New. Gifts. Added. (Up to $120 value)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built Black Friday gift-with-purchase update, and it works as a "the offer just got better" nudge. The hero is a pink canvas tote with a "new. gifts. added." headline and an "up to $120 in gifts" badge. The body copy gives the full offer: 20% off sitewide, up to four gifts, $120 in value. It's a close cousin of the 11/24 "Double the gifts" send (7/10), which used the same "free gift edit" module. The difference is that this one adds the Color Block Lipstick and Mini Tote as the new hero.
- The structure is clean: one offer, one button, then three gift tiers stacked below. The weakness is the offer maths. The gifts unlock at $175+, $100+ and "any order", and the tiers only show up after the hero. The top banner says 25% off for VIP while the body says 20% off, which reads as two competing discounts. The "*" disclaimers also send you off-email for the details.
- Overall it's a solid 7. It's clear and on-season, but it's the sixth ILIA send in three days. The 11/25 Skin Tint and eye sends each had a single product to chase. This one asks the reader to work out which gift they qualify for.

## What's working

- The headline "new. gifts. added." is short and fits the "just in" urgency. The "just added" sticker on the tote tile backs it up.
- There is one dominant CTA, "GET YOUR GIFTS →", in a full-width maroon button right under the hero copy.
- The three tiles each have a clear value tag ($51, up to $19, $50) in a red label, so the value of each gift is easy to see.
- The "any order" Complexion Stick gift removes the spend barrier for at least one tier.
- The VIP perks block and the trust row (free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet) give the loyalty and reassurance a member would want.

## What's weak

- Two discounts appear at once: "Unlock 25% off sitewide | Join VIP" in the top banner and "20% off sitewide" in the body. A reader can't tell which one applies to them.
- The gift thresholds ($175+, $100+, any order) are in small, low-contrast white text on busy beige tiles. This is the information that decides whether the reader clicks, and it's hard to read.
- None of the three tiles is clearly clickable. They look like static images, with no button or link cue under each one.
- The "Up to $120 value" total is never added up. The tiles show $51, $19 and $50, which is $120, but "up to four gifts" is claimed and only three are shown.
- The email is a near repeat of the 11/24 "Double the gifts" send. Anyone who got both will see the same layout and a very similar offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the discount message to one number. Either lead with 20% for everyone and put VIP's extra 5% in the perks block, or lead with 25% and drop the 20% line from the body.
- 2. Turn the gift tiers into a clear ladder, such as "Any order → Complexion Stick", "$100+ → Mini of choice", "$175+ → Lipstick + Tote". Use dark text on a solid background so the thresholds are readable.
- 3. Give each tile its own CTA, such as "Shop the tote set →", so the reader can click through to the gift they want.
- 4. Show a gift total or progress bar, such as "Spend $175, get all four, $120 value", to explain where the $120 comes from.
- 5. Don't send this as a separate email so soon after "Double the gifts". Put it in a single email with the earlier gift offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `A free tote just joined the gift list`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free tote + lipstick with $175 (20% off too)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three gifts, up to $120 in value. The tote unlocks at $175.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Complexion Stick free on any order, plus 20% off sitewide.`
