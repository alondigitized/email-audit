# We picked this beauty just for you! 🍒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Chosen Just for You" hero that isn't personalized. The big orange "YOU" banner promises curation, then delivers a generic Gisou limited-edition pair: Honey Infused Lip Oil ($28) and Honey Glaze Collagen Lip Mask ($26). Below that is a "More Gisou Bestsellers" row and a "Your Next Beauty Obsessions" grid of mass-market bestsellers. The hero is bold and the CTA is clear. But "just for you" is a promise the rest of the email doesn't keep.
- The real offer sits in the top strip: a free Topicals trial size with code HISKINLOVE on a $30 purchase. It is easy to miss under the giant hero, and the code and conditions are in tiny type. A second offer, 25% off a first purchase with the Sephora Credit Card, sits far down the page. The two offers don't point at the same product, so the page splits attention.
- It is polished and on-brand, but it is a catalog send. Over the last week the brand has run the same format: Oct 26 "These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️", Oct 25 "This beauty is the talk of the town 📞" and Oct 24 "Beauty in high demand 📈". Each had a generic beauty hero and a promo buried above it. This one follows the pattern.

## What's working

- The "Chosen Just for You" hero is huge and instantly legible, with a clear "SHOP NOW" button under it.
- The hero products are named with prices and "ONLY AT SEPHORA · LIMITED EDITION" tags. That gives a concrete reason to click.
- The Topicals free trial size is a real, tangible offer with a visible code.
- The Gisou value callouts ("$35 ($51 value)" and "$79 ($101 value)") frame the savings well.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlap, and the sections are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero say "just for you", but the content is bestsellers and a brand spotlight. Nothing visibly reflects the recipient.
- The best offer (Topicals free trial) is in the top strip, while the hero sells unrelated lip products. The email has no single focal offer.
- The grid below the hero is a long scroll of unrelated items (perfume, serum, blush, cleanser). It reads as a catalog, not a pick.
- The credit card offer, Beauty Scan and Bestselling Vanillas modules add four more messages. The offer terms are dense legal text at the bottom, and "Exclusions/terms apply" shows twice.
- There is no urgency. "Limited edition" is vague, and no date appears on any offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with the Topicals free trial with code, or one Gisou product with a price. Don't stack a promo above a product hero that promises something else.
- 2. Make "just for you" true. Name the category or past behavior ("Your lip picks are here") or drop the claim.
- 3. Cut the lower modules (credit card, Beauty Scan, vanillas) to one secondary block.
- 4. Add a deadline to the offer, such as "free trial size through Sunday."
- 5. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Gisou lip" instead of "Shop Now."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Topicals trial size + Gisou lip picks 🍒`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gisou's limited-edition lip duo is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code HISKINLOVE at $30+ for a free trial size.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Honey Infused Lip Oil $28 · Only at Sephora, limited edition.`
