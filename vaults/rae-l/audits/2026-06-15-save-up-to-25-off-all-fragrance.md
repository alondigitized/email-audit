# Save up to 25% off all fragrance
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- This is day five of the Friends of Glossier Sale arc, and Glossier has repeated the exact subject line from June 14 — "Save up to 25% off all fragrance" — verbatim. For any subscriber who opened yesterday's email, this reads like a duplicate or an accidental resend before the first line of body copy even loads. That single mistake is the dominant fact about this email.
- Inside the render, the creative is competent: a two-column hero leads with the offer, a strong editorial shot of a crystal-clear bottle follows, a five-star pull quote provides social proof, and two product grids cover the full fragrance range from full-size to travel sprays. The photography is clean and consistent with Glossier's aesthetic. But the bottom-of-email subscription pitch — "Start a subscription and lock in 25% off!" — actively undercuts the sale's urgency by offering the same percentage with no clear differentiation, leaving subscribers confused about whether the sale is even a better deal than subscribing.
- Bottom line: a structurally solid fragrance catalog email with real merit in its social proof and photography, hamstrung by a repeated subject, no visible sale deadline, and a subscription CTA that sows doubt rather than converts.

## What's working

- **Five-star social proof is well-placed** — the pull quote about wearing fragrance anywhere and keeping a mini in a purse lands in the right position between the editorial hero and the product grid.
- **Travel spray section is smart inventory logic** — featuring lower-price-point entry items widens the conversion funnel for subscribers who aren't ready to commit to full-size.
- **Photography is on-brand and editorial** — the large-format hands-holding-glass-bottle image is aspirational without feeling disconnected from the sale context.
- **Friends of Glossier badge/stamp at top** — the circular stamp visually signals the membership event without heavy copy.

## What's weak

- **Identical subject line to June 14** — "Save up to 25% off all fragrance" is word-for-word the same send from yesterday; engaged subscribers will skim past this as a duplicate.
- **No visible sale deadline anywhere in the email** — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing to create urgency beyond the ambient fact that a sale is happening.
- **Subscription CTA dilutes the sale's value** — "lock in 25% off!" at the bottom matches the sale discount exactly, making neither offer feel special.
- **All CTAs read "Shop now"** — twelve-plus identical button labels across six product tiles give the eye no hierarchy to prioritize.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Change the subject line immediately** — this is the highest-leverage fix. Repeated subjects tank open rates for engaged subscribers who already cleared their inbox yesterday.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fragrance sale: 48 hours left to stock up`
- **Subject Alt B:** `You've been eyeing these — they're 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Glossier You, You Dew, and every travel spray — all on sale through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The bottles your bathroom is missing. 25% off, ending soon.`
- 2. **Add a sale end date** — a single line in the hero ("Sale ends June 16") closes the loop on urgency without redesigning anything.
- 3. **Differentiate or cut the subscription pitch** — if a subscriber can get 25% off right now from the sale, the subscription CTA needs to explain what happens *after* the sale ends (ongoing 25%? That's a real hook). As written, it's noise.
- 4. **Give each product tile a specific CTA** — "Shop Glossier You" or "Get You Dew 25% off" converts better than repeated "Shop now" labels and gives mobile users a clearer tap target.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issues found:**

- **Heavily obfuscated class names** — All CSS classes are minified to single/short tokens (`a`, `b`, `bl`, `bz`, `bw`, etc.). This is not a rendering bug, but it makes QA diffing and future debugging extremely difficult and is a maintainability risk if the template is ever hand-edited.

- **Rollover/hover effects** — `.rollover:hover .rollover-first / .rollover-second` swap pattern is present. This works in Apple Mail but silently no-ops in Gmail, Outlook, and most webmail clients. Confirm fallback state (`.rollover-first`) is fully readable on its own.

- **Conditional comment targeting is fragmented** — Three separate MSO blocks:
  ```html
  <!--[if (mso 16)]>  →  anchor text-decoration fix
  <!--[if gte mso 9]>  →  sup font-size fix
  <!--[if gte mso 9]>  →  AllowPNG / PixelsPerInch
  <!--[if mso]>        →  Word DontUseAdvancedTypography
  ```
  Splitting `gte mso 9` into two separate blocks is redundant; no functional defect, but merge them to reduce parse overhead in Outlook's Word renderer.

- **`lang="en"` present on `<html>`** — Correct. No issue.

- **`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`** — Present. No issue.

- **`format-detection telephone=no`** — Present. Verify whether any phone numbers in the body *should* be tappable; if so, this meta suppresses them globally.
