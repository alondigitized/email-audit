# 20% off best-sellers ends tonight!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the last-call send for the 20% best-sellers sale, and it does the job. "ENDS TONIGHT / Take 20% off all best-sellers" is the first thing the eye reads, with a blue "Shop now" button right under it. The subject and hero match, and the urgency is believable. This is the fourth send in the same sale: the 10/15 "OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE!", the 10/17 "STILL ON!" reminder and the 10/18 scent email all sat in the same 20% window. A subscriber is ready for a deadline by now.
- The body is a long, evenly weighted grid of 10 products. Every tile has a blue "20% OFF" badge, but none shows a price or a sale price. The grid is grouped under Scent, Makeup and Skincare, which helps browsing but makes the email very long for a "few hours left" message. The "free Futuredew Solid with $75+" banner at the top is cut off ("$75+ order" runs off the edge), and the offer repeats at the bottom.
- The email is clean and on-brand, and it gets the deadline across. It would be better if it were shorter and showed real prices.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" and "You only have a few hours left to save" give a credible deadline.
- The hero gives the offer in one line: "Take 20% off all best-sellers", with a clear blue "Shop now" button.
- The blue "20% OFF" badge on every product tile keeps the offer in view while scrolling.
- Section labels (Scent, Makeup, Skincare) make the grid easy to browse.
- The $75+ free Futuredew Solid gift gives a second incentive, and the bottom block repeats it as a final push.

## What's weak

- The top banner is cropped: "Get a free Futuredew Solid with your $75+ order" is cut off at the right edge.
- No tile shows a price or a sale price. The shopper has to click to find out what 20% off means.
- The hero banner has a repeating "20% OFF / SURPRISE SALE" strip that is cropped at the sides and sits close to the product image. It reads as visual noise.
- Ten near-identical tiles is too much scrolling for an urgent send. The "Shop now" buttons are grey and weak next to the blue hero button.
- The scent tiles show the same product twice (You Doux and You Doux 100ml, and You and You 100ml), which looks like padding.
- The footer's large smiley and hand graphics add height and nothing else.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the grid to 4–6 true best-sellers. Show the original price, the sale price and a star rating on each tile. That lifts click-through more than any other change.
- 2. Fix the cropped top banner so the full gift-with-purchase line shows, and drop the repeated "20% OFF" strip from the hero.
- 3. Put a countdown or "Ends midnight PT" line in the hero so the deadline is exact.
- 4. Merge the duplicate scent sizes into one tile each, with a size selector or a "from $X" price.
- 5. Make the product "Shop now" buttons blue, or use product-specific copy like "Get Boy Brow".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your best-sellers are 20% off for a few more hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Futuredew Solid with $75+. Sale closes tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Glossier You, Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom and more, all 20% off until midnight.`
