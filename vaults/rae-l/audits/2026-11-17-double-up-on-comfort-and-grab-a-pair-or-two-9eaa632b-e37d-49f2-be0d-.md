# Double up on comfort and grab a pair (or two)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' Black Friday countdown send, and it's a last-chance push: "LAST CHANCE" banner, "Valid online only thru 11/17", and today is 11/17. The maroon hero is strong. The offer is a two-way choice: buy one, get one, or 20% off one pair. Below it, Women / Men / Kids tiles, price-point tiles and a best-sellers row make it easy to browse.
- The hero has a visible problem. The BOGO discount figure is rendered in a split-flap style and looks like "10% / 00% OFF", which reads as garbled. The footer says BOGO 50% off. A reader can't confirm the headline deal without scrolling to the fine print. That undercuts the one thing the email exists to say.
- Compared with the recent run of browse-abandonment sends (11/12 through 11/14, all 5-7/10), this is a real promotion with a deadline. The subject line is soft for a last-day deal, though. Verdict: solid structure and a credible deadline, held back by an unreadable hero number and a subject that doesn't state the offer.

## What's working

- The "LAST CHANCE" banner and "thru 11/17" give credible same-day urgency.
- The maroon hero with a black SHOP NOW button draws the eye first.
- Women / Men / Kids each get a large product image and a specific "SHOP WOMEN'S / MEN'S / KIDS'" button.
- "Pick your price. Then double up." with Under $50 / $75 / $100 / $125 tiles gives a useful shopping shortcut.
- The Skechers Plus bonus-points line at the top surfaces loyalty value without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The hero discount is garbled. The numerals look like "10%/00% OFF", so the headline offer is unclear.
- The key terms (50% off, exclusions, regular-priced styles only) sit in tiny footer text, and the hero asterisks point readers there.
- There is no single hero product. The three category images and the best-sellers row have no names, prices or reviews.
- The best-sellers row is a gap-heavy strip of small, unlabeled shoes with no CTA.
- The long nav list, Klarna/Afterpay row, app download and social icons below the offer dilute a last-chance email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero number so it reads clearly, for example "BUY ONE, GET ONE 50% OFF", as static text. Put "or 20% off 1 pair" below it, and a "Ends tonight" line near the button.
- 2. Put the deadline in the subject, and the offer too.
- 3. Add a price and a star rating under each best-seller, and label each shoe.
- 4. Move the key terms (50% off the second pair, exclusions) into a readable line directly under the hero.
- 5. Cut the footer clutter (Klarna, app and social blocks) from last-chance sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: BOGO 50% off or 20% off a pair`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: buy one, get one 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only, ends at midnight. Pick your price and double up.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Women's, men's and kids' styles included. Plus 1,000 bonus points with Skechers Plus.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is structurally sound for an ESP-built email (XHTML doctype, VML/MSO namespaces, responsive media queries, Gmail promo annotations). The QA data flags two plain-HTTP images with missing alt text, one of which is a third-party tracking pixel that carries a recipient identifier. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and are unconfirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, body links and most `<img>` tags were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **41 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains and were skipped, so destination status (200/404/redirect chains) is unverified.
- **Third-party pixel over HTTP.** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_FULL_EN_11172026`
  - It is non-HTTPS, so it can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
  - `mi_u=8108776175` looks like a recipient identifier sent in cleartext.
- **Gmail annotation URLs are untracked.** The three `PromotionCard` URLs (`https://www.skechers.com/m/sitewide-offer/?prefn1=gender&prefv1=W|M|G%7CB`) are direct links with no click-tracking redirect and no UTM parameters. Clicks from the Gmail Promotions tab will therefore not be attributed like body clicks.
- **Favicon on a different host.** `<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...">` is HTTP and on the `nl` subdomain. The other assets use `image.emails.skechers.com`. This has no practical effect, since email clients ignore favicons, but it is inconsistent.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on two images:
  - `o.gif` (the tracking pixel; should be `alt=""`).
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (src `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`). Its role is unknown. If it is content, it has no accessible name.
- **HTTP image source.** The same `dde00662…png` loads over `http://`, so it may be blocked or trigger a mixed-content warning. The other images in the JSON-LD use `https://`, so this appears to be an inconsistency rather than a platform limitation.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Focus outline removed.** `a { outline: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.
- **Text-size-adjust disabled.** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables text-size adjustment on iOS.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 4766 of 6624 characters (72%) are URLs, which is above the 70% heuristic. This is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.
- **Annotation timestamp may be a typo.** The Gmail annotation `"availabilityEnds":"2026-11-17T23:11:59Z"` reads as a typo for `23:59:59Z`. If it is, the Gmail promo badge will expire about 48 minutes early on the last day of the offer.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. The body and footer were not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (5,000+ messages/day) require them. Verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain is `emails.skechers.com`. Check headers on a direct-delivery copy.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link sit in the truncated footer, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters** are visible on the `sitewide-offer` URLs. Attribution depends on the ESP redirect layer, and the 41 tracked links were not resolved to confirm what gets appended.
- **Landing page matches the offer.** The links go to `/m/sitewide-offer/` with gender prefilters (`W`, `M`, `G|B`) and the annotation says "BOGO 50% off", so they are consistent. The pages themselves were not fetched.
- **Campaign code in the pixel.** `mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BUNDLE_FULL_EN_11172026` encodes the send date as 11172026, which matches today.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve `dde00662…png` and the `ink1000.com` pixel over HTTPS. If the pixel vendor does not support HTTPS, replace it.
2. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif` and a descriptive `alt` to `dde00662…png` if it carries content.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and restore a focus style (drop `outline: none`).
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message from the sender, bypassing the relay.
5. Capture SPF, DKIM and DMARC results from a direct-delivery copy.
6. Fix `availabilityEnds` to `23:59:59Z` if the offer runs through the end of the day.
7. Probe the 41 tracked links through a redirect-following check to confirm destinations and appended UTM values.
8. Add UTM parameters to the `PromotionCard` URLs so Gmail Promotions-tab clicks are attributed.
9. Shorten the plain-text version, or reduce raw URLs in it, to get below 70% URL content.
