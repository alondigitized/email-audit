**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier fully back in "sets" mode — three bundled kits (No-Makeup Makeup, Icons, Skincare Icons) framed around a back-to-school routine, following directly on from 8/20's standalone "Going back to school?" essentials bundle. The bigger story here isn't the copy, it's the render: every two-column module has its right-hand text column and hero images clipped at the edge of the frame, so headlines like "The No-Makeup Makeup K[it]" and body copy like "Featuring [Boy] Brow, Stretch Balm Concealer and Lash Sli[ck]" are cut off mid-word, and the product photography bleeds off-screen entirely.
- There's no discount anywhere in this send — same no-promo pattern as 8/20 and 8/22, this is now the third editorial/bundle send in a row with zero price incentive. That's a deliberate brand choice, but combined with the clipped layout it reads unfinished rather than intentional.
- Judgement: functionally broken as rendered. Even ignoring the render bug, the offer-less bundle push is a weak follow-up to the Cheek Week arc's 8.00/10 close — this is back to the flat, promo-less territory that scored 4-5 on 8/20 and 8/22.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand visual identity — soft pink palette, product photography styling matches every prior Glossier send.
- The three kits map logically to a back-to-school routine (makeup touch-up, gym/on-the-go, skincare), which is a coherent narrative thread.
- CTA buttons ("Shop + save," "Shop now") are present and consistent across every module.
- Hero section leads with a named benefit ("Best in class") before the kit breakdown.

## What's weak

- Critical render bug: headline and body text is truncated at the right edge in every two-column module ("The No-Makeup Makeup K", "Featuring... Lash Sli", copy cut off in all three product blocks).
- Hero and product images bleed off the visible frame — none of the three kit photos are fully visible.
- No discount, price, or urgency anywhere — third promo-free send in a row after the Cheek Week discount arc closed on 8/18.
- "Shop + save" implies a savings mechanic that never appears anywhere in the email.
- Weak visual hierarchy — nothing marks a primary hero product vs. the three kits; the eye has no clear place to land first.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the render bug first** — the two-column text/image modules are clipping content off the right edge; this needs to go back to QA before any copy changes matter. This alone is costing most of the click potential.
- 2. Add a concrete offer or bundle discount (e.g., "Save 20% when you buy any 2 sets") — three sends without a promo is starting to feel like Glossier abandoned the momentum Cheek Week built.
- 3. Drop or justify "Shop + save" — if there's no savings mechanic, change the CTA to "Shop the sets" so it doesn't over-promise.
- 4. Establish one clear hero product/kit above the fold instead of three parallel, equally-weighted bundles.
- 5. Add urgency (back-to-school is inherently time-bound — "before the semester starts" or a date) to give the subject/preview some teeth.
