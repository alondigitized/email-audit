## What's working

- The "NEW AND CLEAN AT SEPHORA" headline is huge and easy to read, and it ties directly to the subject.
- The green clean badge on every product gives the grid a consistent visual signal.
- Each grid item has a brand, a product name, a price and a tag (NEW, ONLY AT SEPHORA, ONLINE ONLY). That makes the products easy to scan.
- The free Kosas trial size with a visible code is a concrete, redeemable offer. It matches the Kosas shades in the hero.
- The barcode and promo code at the bottom let in-store shoppers redeem the offer without opening the app.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers (4X points on moisturizers and free Kosas) sit above the clean headline. That muddies the hierarchy.
- The 4X moisturizer offer has nothing to do with clean makeup, and the hero copy never mentions moisturizers.
- The top banner and the Kosas hero both have clipped right-edge text. "Exclusions/terms apply" and "WITH $30+ PURCHASE" are cut off.
- The Spot it. Shop it., auto-replenish and rhode modules pull attention away from the clean story. The auto-replenish box shows a tiny product.
- The footer is long and dense. It has a wall of fine print, a Beauty Insider strip and two barcodes, and several offers carry exclusions that are only visible in small type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Either make the Kosas freebie the hero with a clean-themed headline, or move the 4X moisturizer banner to a separate send.
- 2. Cut the rhode and auto-replenish modules, or move them below a clear "more" divider, so the clean grid is the body of the email.
- 3. Fix the clipped text on the right edge of the top banner and the Kosas hero.
- 4. Make the CTA specific. Use "Shop clean makeup" instead of "SHOP NOW".
- 5. Trim the footer to one barcode and shorter legal text.
