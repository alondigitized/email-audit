# STARTS TOMORROW: 10% off EVERYTHING 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the Sephora Savings Event "starts tomorrow" teaser. It's a strong, well-built hero: a black-and-gold banner with two big badges, **Insider 10% off (11/3–11/9)** and **Sephora Collection 30% off (10/30–11/9)**, then the code ALLONSALE and a "BUILD YOUR BASKET" button. The offer and the dates are the first thing the eye lands on.
- The subject undersells it, though. It leads with "10% off EVERYTHING," while the better deal (30% off Sephora Collection) is already live and was the subject of the 10/30, 10/31 and 11/1 sends. A subscriber who has seen "STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection" and "Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection" now gets a fourth promo in the same week with a lower number up front. "EVERYTHING" also over-promises, because the hero's own footnotes carry limitations and exclusions.
- Below the hero, the email is competent but long. It has category tiles, a "Beauty on Demand" fulfillment strip, a "Save on these faves" product row with prices, Jumbo Sizes, Foundation, Gifts Under $50, and a heavy fine-print and barcode footer. Verdict: a good execution of a repeated message, with a subject that spends the urgency on the smaller offer.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: two large badges with dates and the code ALLONSALE sit directly above a "BUILD YOUR BASKET" button.
- "Insider" is named in the headline, so loyalty members see the benefit at once.
- The "FREE GIFT WITH PURCHASE drops 11/3 at 9AM PT" strip gives a second, credible reason to come back tomorrow.
- Category tiles (Makeup, Skincare, Hair, Fragrance, Value Sets, Tools & Brushes) make the click paths easy.
- The "Save on these faves" row shows real product names, sizes and prices, for example "$24" and "$174".

## What's weak

- The subject leads with the weaker offer (10%), although the hero shows 30% on Sephora Collection.
- "EVERYTHING" is contradicted by the footnote markers and the dense legal copy.
- This is the fourth Sephora Savings Event send since 10/30. The 30% story has already been told, so "starts tomorrow" is a repeat.
- The footer is very long: a fine-print wall plus two barcodes at the bottom, which is a lot of scroll after the last CTA.
- The mid-email offers (Jumbo Sizes, Foundation, Gifts Under $50, Holiday Gift Guide) compete with the sale and dilute the "10% off" message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put both offers in the subject and keep the date: the Insider tier is what's new and time-bound, and the 30% is the stronger hook.
- 2. Replace "EVERYTHING" with a qualified claim. Drop the ALL CAPS, which is the main spam-style signal in the subject.
- 3. Add a real preheader. Use it for the gift-with-purchase drop time or the code, not a repeat of the subject.
- 4. Cut the mid-email modules to two. Keep the product row and one gift module so the discount stays the story.
- 5. Move the code and the "Insider mention at checkout" instruction closer to the button, and shorten the legal copy or collapse it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Insider 10% off starts tomorrow (30% already on) 💄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow: 10% off + a free gift drops 9AM PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off Sephora Collection through 11/9. Code ALLONSALE.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free gift with purchase drops 11/3 at 9AM PT, while supplies last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a 700px fixed-width, table-based XHTML Transitional build with Epsilon/Harmony click-tracking redirects. It has no plain-text part (0 chars, FAIL) and several viewport and markup defects. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay did not capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect wrapping:** The links I could see are wrapped in `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a19cbeec56b…/939fd687-…?__dU__=…`. The final destinations aren't visible in the truncated source, so I can't check them.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 52 links were probed and 40 were skipped. The QA data reports no broken links among the 12, so the other 40 are untested.
- **Tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…'>` is an open-tracking pixel. It is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. The QA "missing alt" warning is for this pixel. It has no accessibility impact because the pixel is hidden. It uses single-quoted attributes and has no `width`/`height`, which is harmless.
- **JSON-LD:** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block contains `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type. Most clients strip `<script>`, and Gmail only honors specific schema types. The empty `discountCode` could make the markup invalid for clients that do parse it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. `min-width:700px` forces horizontal scroll on narrow viewports in clients that ignore the `@media (max-width:480px)` rules. Gmail app with non-Google accounts is one example.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is cosmetic in email.
- **MSO conditional inside CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. The comment markup is not valid CSS there. It will likely not apply in Outlook, and it risks breaking the rule that follows it in some parsers.
- **Document structure:** The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute. The layout tables (`class="tbl tbl_cntr"`) have no `role="presentation"`. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Plain text missing:** The text version is 0 chars (FAIL). This hurts accessibility and spam-filter scoring, and it breaks display in text-only clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The `__dU__=` URL parameter is a resolved per-recipient identifier.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both headers were not found (WARN). The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unverified rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, so the real message should be checked at the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found (WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `shop@beauty.sephora.com`. This needs verification against the original message headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** The visible source is truncated before the footer, so neither can be confirmed. The probing note says an unsubscribe link is always included, but no result for it is shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. UTM parameters and final landing URLs are hidden behind the `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirects. Landing-page alignment with the "10% off EVERYTHING" subject cannot be assessed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part.** This is the only hard FAIL. Include the key offer, the CTA URLs and the unsubscribe link.
2. **Verify headers on the original message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) at Sephora's sending side, not via the relay.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Replace `min-width:700px` with a fluid pattern.** Use `max-width:700px; width:100%`, plus an MSO ghost table if Outlook needs it.
5. **Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables and `lang="en"` to `<html>`.**
6. **Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block** into its own `<style>` block.
7. **Fix the `robots` meta value** to `noindex`, or drop it.
8. **Probe the remaining 40 links**, including the footer unsubscribe and preference links, and resolve the redirect chains to confirm the UTMs and landing pages.
9. **Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`** to the tracking pixel to clear the QA warning.
