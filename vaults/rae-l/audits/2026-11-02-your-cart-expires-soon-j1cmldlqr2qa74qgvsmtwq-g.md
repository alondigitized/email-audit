# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email again, and the subject promises something the body doesn't deliver. "Your cart expires soon!" implies urgency, but the hero says "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." There's no countdown and no expiry date. The cart item is the black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" sneaker with the blue skull art. It's the same item as the 10/31 "Your Items Are Waiting!" send.
- The actual deadline is a 20% off Skechers Plus banner, "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS!", with "Valid thru 11/2". It sits well below the fold, after the product grid and the black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar. This email went out 11/2, so the offer expires the same day. That's the real urgency, and it's buried. The hero is clean and the product is large, but a big white gap sits above the shoe. The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is clear.
- Judgement: a solid, functional reminder that wastes its best asset. Moving the 20% to the top would make the subject's promise true.

## What's working

- The hero shoe is large and clean, with the product name and a navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button directly under it.
- The "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." headline is friendly and confident.
- The 20% off Plus member offer is a concrete price incentive with a stated end date (11/2).
- The six-shoe grid of women's leather sneakers gives alternatives, and the product names show material.
- The footer fine print spells out the offer terms, so the promo isn't misleading.

## What's weak

- The subject says "expires soon," but nothing in the hero shows what expires or when.
- The 20% off banner is far below the fold, and "Valid thru 11/2" is tiny.
- Offer wording is split: "No tricks, just treats" is Halloween language, and Halloween ended 11/1. This is the same stale-seasonal problem as the 10/31 sends.
- The hero has a large empty white band above the shoe, which pushes the CTA down.
- The cart item has no price, size, or reviews. The "YOU LIKED THESE" framing is also thin. The email only implies a cart.
- The long list of category links, app badges, and Afterpay/Klarna blocks dilutes focus after the main CTA.
- This is another reminder in a cadence that has been heavy: 10/29, 10/30, 10/31 and 11/1 all had similar sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% off Plus banner to directly under the hero, and state the real deadline: "Ends tonight".
- 2. Fix the subject to match the content, or add a real expiry line to the hero ("Your cart is held until midnight").
- 3. Tighten the hero by cutting the white gap, so the shoe and button show on the first mobile screen.
- 4. Add price, with the 20% member price shown, plus a star rating under the cart item.
- 5. Retire the Halloween copy now that it's November.
- 6. Throttle abandonment sends to one per cart per 48 hours.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off your UNO ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking about the Alma De Guerrero?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% on your cart through 11/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your skull-art UNOs are still saved. Check out before midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a mostly sound table-based structure (`role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, MSO conditionals, a mobile `@media` block). It has four confirmed problems: all footer images use `http://`, there is no plain-text part, `<title>` is empty, and the Unsubscribe link failed its probe with a 400. The automated pass rate is 38%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The link with text "Unsubscribe" is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HQWE2dTluTTBlcGFWek1XeBIDMVN5GLHs9wUgAg&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&...`. A tokenized Attentive endpoint may reject a bare automated request, so this isn't proof the link is broken for recipients. It is the highest-risk link in the email and should be verified manually.
- **429 responses are not defects.** "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." both returned 429 from `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...`. This is rate limiting by the click tracker, and the QA data marks them "may be valid".
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 32 skipped, so footer and social link health is unverified.
- **All links are wrapped in redirects.** Every probed link goes through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/<opaque>`, so the final destinations can't be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources.** 12 images load over `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`. They include the logo (`dde00662-...png`), the hero "Complete Your Order" image (`dfcc8fd7-...jpg`), and the App Store, Google Play, Afterpay, Curbside Pickup, Find a Store, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube icons. Clients or proxies that block mixed content may drop them, which would remove the hero and the footer.
- **Missing alt text.** `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` has no alt. It sits in the `m/5/` content area next to the hero, so it is likely meaningful. The tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` also lacks alt, which is minor.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` in the `<head>`.
- **Poor alt text.** The footer image has alt "Text" (`49468f73-...png`), which says nothing about what it shows.
- **Plain-text part is missing.** The QA check reports a 0-character text version. This could be an AgentMail capture artifact, but if it's real it is a deliverability and accessibility problem.
- No other rendering defects are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The cart contents sit in the truncated portion and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.

- The physical postal address is not in the truncated source, so it could not be verified.

Landing-page alignment with the "cart" message was not tested, because the 429s blocked resolution of the "Complete Your Order" link. Whether the abandoned-cart link restores the cart is untested.
