# Just in time for Halloween 🎃
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-season Halloween send, but the hero and the offer don't match. The hero is a moody photo of black spiderweb nails under "Last-minute look? Start with your nails." The only offer, three stacked "Buy one, get one 50% off" blocks, sits below roughly two screens of dark maroon and photo. The first screen has no price hook at all.
- Cadence matters here. Sally has hit Halloween on 10/08 ("Don't ghost these deals 👻") and 10/09 ("Your Halloween look starts here 🎃"). This send reuses the pumpkin emoji and the same theme, so it reads as a repeat. The 10/12 "Don't leave 15% off behind" put a hard number in the subject, and this one doesn't.
- Verdict: good creative and a decent offer, wrapped in a vague subject line and an offer-free hero. It will do fine, but it's not sharp.

## What's working

- The hero photo of matte black web-pattern nails is striking, and it ties the Halloween theme to a product category.
- The "Buy one, get one 50% off" headline repeats cleanly across Nailboo dip powders, lashes and vivid hair color.
- Each product block has a brand name, shade or product name and its own "Shop now" link, so the clicks go to specific items.
- The "Buy online, pick up in store" and "2 hour delivery" strips fit last-minute costume prep.
- The layout is tidy. I can see no broken images, overlaps or empty gaps.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. The BOGO deal doesn't appear until after a very tall hero image.
- There's no deadline anywhere visible, so a "last-minute" theme comes with no urgency.
- Three categories (dip powder, lashes, hair color) dilute the nail story the hero sets up. Lashes and hair color aren't obviously Halloween.
- The product tiles show no prices or sale prices, so the "50% off" can't be translated into dollars.
- The top bars (Sally Rewards, BOPIS, 2-hour delivery) stack three competing messages above the hero.
- The legal copy is long and dense, and it's where the BOGO terms are buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Halloween nails, BOGO 50% off dip powder" with a specific CTA, so the first screen carries both the theme and the price.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends 10/31" or "Order by Thursday for 2-hour delivery."
- 3. Cut the lashes and hair color blocks, or make them Halloween-specific (glitter lashes, black or orange hair color). Show the nails story in more depth instead.
- 4. Show sale prices on the product tiles.
- 5. Use a verb-and-noun CTA like "Shop dip powders" instead of repeating "Shop now."
- 6. Collapse the three top bars into one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off Halloween nails 🖤`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spiderweb nails, ready in 2 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one dip powder, get one 50% off. Pick up in store today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dark Dreams, Wine Time and more. Ends 10/31.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML namespaces, a viewport meta tag and a 499px mobile breakpoint. The QA data shows 0 hard failures and 3 warnings: missing header data that is likely a relay artifact, plus several third-party tracking pixels with alt or HTTPS problems. The HTML source was truncated, so anything past the `<style>` block is unverified.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 57 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is an unencrypted third-party pixel. Gmail's image proxy and other clients may block or rewrite it, so the Dotomi/Epsilon conversion tracking may silently not fire.
- `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro. It was never substituted, so the URL is identical on every open and can be cached or deduplicated, which undercounts impressions. `&advt=-` is also cut off in the QA output, so it may be a truncated value or an empty parameter.
- `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2324947/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` carries what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003...` prefix) in the path. This is a per-recipient identifier embedded in a third-party pixel URL. It is a data-exposure concern, not a functional break.
- Four third-party pixels (mon-trk, MediaOcean/VisualIQ, Dotomi, plus an empty-`src` image) add load and privacy surface. The empty-`src` image is a defect in its own right.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- One `<img>` has an empty `src` and an empty alt (reported as "Image missing alt text: : src: "). Empty-`src` images can trigger a request to the page or message URL in some clients, and the empty alt gives screen readers nothing.
- The alt text is missing on three tracking pixels (`003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`). For 1x1 pixels, `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` is correct. The QA tool reports a missing attribute, and the truncated source can't confirm which case applies. Where the attribute is absent, screen readers may read out the filename or URL.
- `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, unlike the other meta tags in this XHTML doctype. This is a minor validity inconsistency and unlikely to affect rendering.
- Open Sans is loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com`, but `a { font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif }` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible source. It falls back to Helvetica, so link typography differs from the intended font.
- `img { white-space:pre }` is an odd property on an image and has no effect.
- `<title>Sally Beauty</title>` is generic, which is minor.
- The `.boxShadow` and `.filter-item:hover` rules are unsupported in Outlook and Gmail. They are progressive enhancements and not defects.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the VisualIQ pixel is the only unresolved token found in the visible source and QA data (see section 2).
- The Contact ID in the mon-trk pixel is a resolved token, so personalization is working there.
- No other merge-token problems are confirmed. The truncated body could not be checked for `%%`, `{{`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` patterns.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both. Verify against the original message at the sender.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, with SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be confirmed here.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these could not be verified because the footer is past the truncation point.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be inspected. All 57 links are click-redirect URLs that were skipped by the probe, and the visible source is truncated.
- UTM presence, landing-page alignment with the Halloween messaging, and redirect-chain length are all unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the empty-`src` image: remove it, or give it a valid source and explicit `alt=""` if it is decorative.
2. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` to every tracking pixel.
3. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://` (`login.dotomi.com`). If Dotomi doesn't support HTTPS, drop it.
4. Have the ESP substitute the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the VisualIQ pixel at send time, or use a per-recipient unique value.
5. Review whether embedding the Salesforce Contact ID in a third-party pixel URL (mon-trk) is intended.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw message at the sender, since the relay may be stripping them.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. That would cover the footer unsubscribe link, physical address, UTM parameters and landing-page continuity. Also add redirect-following for tracking domains so destinations can be validated.
