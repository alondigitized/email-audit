# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' second consecutive cart abandonment email — yesterday's send was "Did you forget something?" and today's is "Your Items Are Waiting!" The bones are solid: three personalized cart items are rendered cleanly with product names, a clear blue "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" CTA, and a logical visual flow from hero → products → button. The playful headline ("WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US...") fits Skechers' casual brand voice.
- The fatal problem is cadence, not creative. Firing a second cart abandonment trigger within 24 hours of the first — without adding any new incentive to complete the purchase — is pure pressure without value. Yesterday's send had a stronger subject and the same mechanic; today's is a warmed-over repeat that does nothing to change the shopper's calculus.
- What this email never answers: *why should you complete the order TODAY?* No price drop, no inventory scarcity signal, no time-limited offer tied to the cart. The $5 reward banner is a generic members perk, not a cart-recovery hook. A shopper who passed on the first nudge needs a reason to act, not a second identical nudge.

## What's working

- **Personalized product recall** — all three cart items (Glide-Step Pro, BOBS Sport Skiit, Mommy & Me Glide-Step Pro) are named and rendered cleanly with product photography.
- **Unambiguous primary CTA** — "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" in a bold blue button is the clearest possible instruction.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — logo → playful headline → products → CTA → recommendations → utility. The eye follows the right path.
- **No render bugs** — layout is crisp, product images load, no broken elements visible.
- **Short, readable subject** — 23 characters, no spam signals, mobile-safe.

## What's weak

- **Second cart abandonment in 24 hours with no new incentive** — Yesterday's "Did you forget something?" (8/10) already made this ask. Repeating without a discount, deadline, or scarcity signal adds pressure but no new reason to act.
- **Zero pricing information** — None of the three cart items show a price. A shopper who forgot what was in the cart can't even recall what they're committing to.
- **No urgency mechanism** — "Still waiting" is atmospheric, not actionable. No "Only 2 left," no "Offer expires in 24 hrs," nothing.
- **$5 reward is buried below the fold and decoupled from the cart** — The loyalty banner is a generic perk, not a cart-completion incentive ("Complete your order today and earn $5 toward your next purchase" would be better).
- **Product recommendations dilute the focus** — Six "you might love" products below the CTA scatter attention before the primary action is taken.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a cart-specific incentive** — "Complete your order in the next 48 hours and we'll add free shipping" or a one-time 10% off on abandoned items. The shopper saw the last email; they need a reason to act, not a reminder they already have.
- 2. **Show prices on cart items** — Even just "$89.99" below each product name gives the shopper context and reduces friction. Right now the cart feels abstract.
- 3. **Add an inventory urgency line** — "Glide-Step Pro is going fast in your size" under the product image is a legitimate, product-specific nudge.
- 4. **Kill or delay the recommendations module** — Move "Great Options We Think You'll Love" to *after* the "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" section, or remove it entirely. It competes with conversion.
- 5. **The $5 reward banner should be rewritten to tie to this cart** — Replace generic "Members earn $5 on $75+" with "Your cart qualifies — complete it now and earn your $5 reward."
- 6. **Suppress the second cart trigger entirely if the first was opened** — If the subscriber opened yesterday's email and didn't convert, a same-offer second trigger looks spammy. A different value prop (free shipping, inventory nudge) would be far more effective.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your cart expires soon — grab it before it's gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking? Here's a reason to decide today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Glide-Step Pro + 2 others are still saved — complete your order and earn rewards`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping if you check out in the next 48 hrs — your cart is ready`
