# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Skechers cart abandonment email in eight days — and the second one sent on May 26 alone, following "This is perfect for you!" earlier the same day. The sequence has officially crossed from reminder into harassment. The fundamental tension here: the subject ("Your cart expires soon!") promises urgency but the body opens with "YOU LIKED THESE AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." — an affirmation, not a closer. These two tones are fighting each other.
- The email's one genuine evolution in the sequence is the 20% off a single pair offer, which none of the prior three cart emails included. That's a meaningful discount incentive that could actually convert a fence-sitter. But Skechers buried it below three product images AND a six-up best sellers grid, placing their only escalation lever roughly four scrolls into a mobile view. That's a strategic placement error.
- The preheader appears to be leaking a tracking ID ("005.45560") rather than real copy — meaning every inbox that renders preheaders shows gibberish next to the subject line. Clean execution elsewhere makes that miss stand out even more. This email has the right ingredients for a strong sequence-ender; the assembly just isn't there.

## What's working

- **Three specific cart items rendered cleanly** — Glide-Step Pro (red), BOBS Sport Skitz, and Glide-Step Pro (Rosy Glow) are named and visually clear with no broken images.
- **"COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" is an unambiguous CTA** — verb + object, prominent blue button, no softening.
- **20% off a single pair is a real offer** — this is the first actual discount incentive in the cart abandonment sequence; it gives a fence-sitter a reason to act that wasn't present in emails 1–3.
- **Subject line does the core job** — 24 characters, mobile-friendly, no spam signals, concrete urgency signal.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, text is readable throughout.

## What's weak

- **The discount is buried four scrolls deep** — the 20% off is placed after the cart items AND a six-up best sellers grid. The incentive that should convert a holdout is the last thing they see.
- **Subject urgency and body copy are mismatched** — "expires soon" in the subject, "YOU LIKED THESE AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." in the hero. The body doesn't deliver on the urgency the subject sets up.
- **No specific deadline anywhere in the body** — "expires soon" without a date or time is increasingly unbelievable on the fourth email; the body needs to back up the claim with a concrete timestamp.
- **Preheader leaks a tracking ID ("005.45560")** — inbox clients show this as the preview text, wasting prime real estate and signaling an operations miss.
- **Second email on the same day** — already sent "This is perfect for you!" on 5/26; two cart abandonment emails in one calendar day is the fastest way to burn the unsubscribe link.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Promote the 20% off to the hero** — Make this the headline above the cart items. "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER — 20% OFF INSIDE" as the H1, with a countdown or deadline below. That's the one change that would lift this email the most; the discount is the only new information in this send and it's being hidden.
- 2. **Add a specific deadline to the body** — "Offer ends May 27 at midnight." Match what the subject promises. Without this, "expires soon" is just noise on the fourth send.
- 3. **Rewrite the hero to close, not compliment** — Replace "YOU LIKED THESE AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." with urgency copy that mirrors the subject: "YOUR CART EXPIRES TONIGHT — AND SO DOES YOUR 20% OFF."
- 4. **Fix the preheader** — Replace the tracking ID with real copy that complements the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off — but your cart expires tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: your Skechers + 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your three pairs are still here. 20% off until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `This is the nudge. 20% off, one pair, today only.`
- 5. **Cap this sequence here** — Four emails in eight days with two on the same day is the ceiling. Let the cart expire cleanly and suppress for 30 days before re-engaging with a catalog send.
