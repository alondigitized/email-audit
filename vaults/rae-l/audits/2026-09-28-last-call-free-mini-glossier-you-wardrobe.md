# Last call! Free Mini Glossier You Wardrobe
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in seven days for the same fragrance gift-with-purchase. It follows 9/22 ("Try our fragrances for free!") and 9/26 ("Still on! Get a free Glossier You Discovery Kit"). The hero is clear: a bold headline, a photo of the red Discovery Kit, and a "$50+ order" threshold. The "Last call" framing is fair, since this really is the end of the promo.
- The email loses points on naming and follow-through. The subject calls the prize a "Mini Glossier You Wardrobe," but the body only ever says "Discovery Kit," so a subscriber has to work out they're the same thing. The body then drifts into a generic four-product "Start Here" grid (Cloud Paint, Boy Brow, Lip Line, Lash Slick) that has nothing to do with the offer. There's also no deadline date, even though the subject promises urgency.
- Verdict: a competent reminder that is too repetitive, with one visible render flaw. It's a 6, in line with the last two sends in this run.

## What's working

- The hero is clean: "Get a free Glossier You Discovery Kit with your $50+ order*" sits beside a strong product shot.
- The offer and its threshold are in the headline, not hidden.
- "LAST CALL" as an eyebrow label reinforces the subject and reads as credible urgency.
- The "Mini yet mighty" block adds a second product image of the minis, which sells the sampling idea.
- The layout is easy to scan, with plenty of whitespace and consistent Glossier branding.

## What's weak

- The body copy in the "Mini yet mighty" block is clipped at the right edge. "musky" and "collect[ion]" are cut off, which looks sloppy.
- The subject says "Mini Glossier You Wardrobe" but the body says "Discovery Kit." The naming doesn't match.
- There is no deadline date or time, so "last call" has nothing behind it.
- Every CTA is the generic "Shop now," including the four in the product grid, which looks identical and has no link to the offer.
- The "Start Here" grid of Cloud Paint, Boy Brow, Lip Line and Lash Slick dilutes the single offer. It also gives no prompt to reach the $50 threshold.
- The terms are a small "*Terms apply" link at the very bottom.
- The cadence is heavy: this is the third near-identical fragrance GWP send since 9/22.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a real deadline** ("Ends tonight, 9/28" or "Ends Sunday") in the hero and the subject. This is the single biggest lift, because it turns "last call" into a real reason to act.
- 2. Use one name for the prize everywhere, "Discovery Kit," in both the subject and the body.
- 3. Fix the clipped copy in the "Mini yet mighty" block so it wraps inside the column.
- 4. Replace the generic "Start Here" grid with "Add to reach $50" picks and a price under each product. Tie the grid to the threshold.
- 5. Change the CTAs to specific copy such as "Get my free kit" and "Build a $50 order."
- 6. Move the terms up as a short line under the hero CTA.
- 7. Give this promo a rest after this send. Three emails in a week is enough.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: free Glossier You Discovery Kit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free Glossier You minis expire tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $50, get four fragrance minis free. Offer ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Layer You, You Doux, You Fleur and You Rêve. Last chance to add them.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** QA reports "12 material link(s) probed, 12 skipped", so no status codes, redirect chains or landing-page checks exist for this send.
- **Stray tracking element.** The source has `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M3N0J61ENWW54E8Y8V0P2SAA_0"></span>` at the top of `<body>`. `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`, and the element does nothing. It looks like a Klaviyo or Stripo artifact and should be harmless. It is markup noise, not a functional defect.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M3N0J61ENWW54E8Y8V0P2SAA` is present as expected. It has no alt attribute (see section 3).
- The truncated source does not show the CTA hrefs, so I can't confirm whether they use `klclick` click-wrapping or have malformed URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 7 images).**
  - Six content images from `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_13942e29.../`: `heroimage_1_12_0b9.jpg`, `group_3996_4.jpg`, `group_3986_4_gNB.jpg`, `group_3987_4.jpg`, `group_3986_5_QGi.jpg` and `group_3987_5.jpg`.
