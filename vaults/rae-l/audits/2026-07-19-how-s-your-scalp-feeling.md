# ✨ How's your scalp feeling?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Lush's second scalp/hair editorial in ten days — July 9's "The root of good hair" scored an 8 and covered the same scalp-to-length funnel with a nearly identical product grid structure. This July 19 send zooms in on "calming" the scalp, leans into "CALM YOUR CROWN" as the hero, and then runs through a familiar multi-card product walkthrough: Rehab, Glory, Super Milk, American Cream, Avocado. The creative is competent, the photography is strong, and the grid is cleanly executed.
- The structural problem is repetition. A subscriber who opened the July 9 hair care send, and the honey send before that, and the Summer send before that, gets another long scroll of product cards with no price incentive and no reason to act today. The Moomin cross-sell at the bottom ("Available from July 30th") is the one genuinely new piece of information in the email — and it's buried at the bottom of a long scroll, treated as an afterthought.
- The scalp question framing in the subject is emotionally smarter than "The root of good hair" because it centers the reader's problem. But the body doesn't answer it with any specificity — no quiz link, no symptom-to-product mapping, no "if your scalp is [X], try [Y]." The hook gets dropped immediately for a standard editorial grid.

## What's working

- **"CALM YOUR CROWN" hero headline** — lands the benefit and the double meaning (hair crown + calming) cleanly, stronger than the July 9 "ROOT FOR YOUR HAIR."
- **Product naming is specific** — "Rehab," "Glory," "Super Milk," "American Cream" — distinctive Lush names that carry personality and help subscribers remember what to search.
- **Visual diversity in models** — range of hair textures (curl patterns, locs, straight) is well-matched to a scalp-care story.
- **"Shop Remedy >"** as the hero CTA — specific verb+noun, not generic "Shop Now."
- **Moomin cross-sell is visually distinct** — the illustrated Moomin module breaks the photo grid pattern and draws the eye, a good anchor for the bottom of the email.

## What's weak

- **No offer, no urgency** — pure editorial with no pricing, discount, or time-bound reason to click today; the Moomin "July 30" date is the only deadline and it's at the very bottom.
- **Scalp question dropped immediately** — the subject sets up a diagnostic ("How's your scalp feeling?") that the body never answers; there's no symptom-to-product logic or quiz.
- **Cadence repetition** — hair care was July 9, Honey franchise July 12, Fresh philosophy July 13, and now this on July 19; this send blends into the streak rather than standing out.
- **Super Milk lemon imagery recycled** — the yellow citrus palette echoes the July 7 "Smells like summer" summer send; the visual feels like reused creative rather than a dedicated hero.
- **Preview text is leaking junk** — the thin top banner appears to be "Is this email not displaying correctly? View it in a browser," which is wasted real estate in the inbox preview slot.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Answer the diagnostic** — the subject sets up a scalp problem; the body should resolve it. Add a 3-question symptom breakdown above the grid: "Dry and flaky? → Rehab. Oily roots? → American Cream. Itchy? → Avocado." That transforms a generic editorial into a personalized recommendation.
- 2. **Move Moomin up or make it the hero** — "Available July 30th" is the only time-bound signal in the email. Either make it the hero to drive anticipation (a dedicated Moomin send), or add a countdown module near the top.
- 3. **Fix the preheader** — replace the view-in-browser leak with a line that complements the subject.
- 4. **Differentiate from July 9** — if the creative team is running back-to-back hair sends, the second one needs a different angle (ingredient deep-dive, before/after story, #lushcommunity UGC) rather than another product grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your scalp called. It wants Rehab.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dry scalp? Oily roots? There's a fix.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Calm, nourish, restore — find your crown match.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: Lush × Moomin drops July 30.`
