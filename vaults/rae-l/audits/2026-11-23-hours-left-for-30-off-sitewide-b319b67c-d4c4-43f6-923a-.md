# Hours left for 30% off sitewide
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the final-hours version of the Black Friday early-access push. The hero is blunt and effective: a "LAST CHANCE!" bar, "Member Exclusive," and a huge white-banded "30% OFF SITEWIDE" on maroon, with a black "SHOP NOW" button. The eye lands on the offer first, and the creative doesn't change the offer, which is the right call for a closer.
- The body is a four-tile category grid (Slip-ins, Cozy Fit, Court & Classics, Performance), then a $49.99 Doorbusters block, size chips and a footer nav. The structure is sound, but it is the same template Skechers has sent all week. A subscriber who got "30% off early access is almost up ⏱️" yesterday and "Early access is open!" on 11/18 sees almost no new information. The one real novelty is the Doorbusters block from $49.99, and it sits below four category tiles.
- There is also an inconsistency. The subject says "Hours left," but the hero fine print says "Valid online only thru 11/22." The send is dated 11/23, so the deadline may already have passed in the recipient's mind. That hurts credibility. Overall it is a competent closer with a strong hero, but it is repetitive and the deadline is muddy.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable. "30% OFF SITEWIDE" is the largest element, and the maroon and white contrast is high.
- "LAST CHANCE!" and "Member Exclusive" give urgency and loyalty framing in the first screen.
- The Doorbusters block ("Starting at $49.99") adds a concrete price point and a second reason to click.
- The four category tiles have clear, specific button labels such as "SHOP SLIP-INS" and "SHOP PERFORMANCE."
- Size chips for women and men are a useful shortcut that cuts friction for ready-to-buy shoppers.
- The footer carries Klarna/Afterpay, app download and store-visit options.

## What's weak

- The deadline is unclear. The subject says "hours left," but the fine print says "thru 11/22."
- The creative is nearly identical to the 11/18 opener and the 11/22 last-call send, so it looks like a fourth near-duplicate.
- Doorbusters is the freshest news but sits below the category grid, with no product shown.
- The category tiles are lifestyle shots with no product names, prices or reviews.
- The fine print is dense and small, and "Exclusions apply" under the hero undercuts the "sitewide" claim.
- The top strip ("Join Skechers Plus and Earn 1000 Bonus Points") is tiny and asks for membership in a member-exclusive email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the deadline. Put a clear end time in the hero ("Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT") and make the subject match it exactly.
- 2. Move Doorbusters directly under the hero and show three real products with prices. A specific $49.99 shoe will out-click category tiles.
- 3. Cut the category grid to two tiles, or replace it with top sellers with ratings.
- 4. Add a live countdown timer to the hero so the urgency is visible instead of claimed.
- 5. Shorten and relocate the legal copy, and replace "Exclusions apply" with a one-line plain-language note.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 30% off sitewide`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 30% off + $49.99 doorbusters`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members: your early-access discount expires at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ins, Cozy Fit and doorbusters from $49.99 before it's gone.`

The confirmed issues are two plain-HTTP images without alt text (one is a tracking pixel carrying a subscriber ID), accessibility-hostile viewport and CSS settings, and a deliverability and compliance picture that can't be verified from this capture.

- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (the pixel) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The second is a content image, not a pixel.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Campaign ID matches:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026_PM` appears in both the pixel (`mi_ecmp`) and the `utm_campaign` value.
- **Annotation link:** it carries `utm_source=trig&utm_medium=email&utm_content=annotation&utm_term=googleannotation1` plus `src=2026Q4BFEARLYACCESS`.

- **Landing page:** the page was not probed, so the offer match is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all images, including the pixel and the favicon, over HTTPS. Move the tracking pixel to a first-party HTTPS domain.
2. Add `alt=""` to the pixel and a descriptive `alt` to `dde00662-…png`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and restore a focus style on `a`.
4. Check the sent message's raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Don't rely on the relay capture.
5. Confirm `availabilityEnds` and the `utm_source=trig` value on a full-list campaign.
6. Re-run the QA on the untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address and the merge tokens.
