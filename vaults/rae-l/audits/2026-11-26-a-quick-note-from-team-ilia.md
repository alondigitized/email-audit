# A quick note from team ILIA
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a Thanksgiving thank-you note with no offer and no button. The headline reads "We hope your plate is *full* today", over a tray of ILIA products styled like a serving platter. The copy is two lines and a "Team ilia" signature. It's on brand and quiet, and it ties back to the "now serving your favorites" pie-dish creative from the 11/23 send, "Your picks, up to 25% off".
- Strategically it works as a pause after a week of heavy promotion. In the last three days ILIA sent the 11/24 "This week in beauty: 20% off" recap, "Double the gifts", "Barrier Build is back", and two 25%-off category sends on 11/25. A no-ask note on the holiday itself is a good change of pace. The cost is that it has nothing to click, and its only commercial content is a free-shipping line in small grey type. It will build goodwill, not revenue. Judgement: a nice brand moment, but it leaves a Black Friday-eve slot unused.

## What's working

- The "plate is *full*" headline is large, warm, and the italic "full" adds personality.
- The tray image turns products into a table setting, so the holiday theme is clear without showing a turkey.
- The copy is short and sounds like a person wrote it. The handwritten "Team ilia" signature supports that.
- The layout is clean, with generous white space and no broken images or overlapping text.
- The send is seasonally right for 11/26 and gives subscribers a break from the promo run.

## What's weak

- There is no CTA button anywhere. A reader who feels warmly toward the brand has no obvious next step.
- The sale isn't mentioned. The only commercial line is "Free U.S. shipping over $50," which is small and easy to miss.
- The tray image has no product link, and the products are small and hard to tell apart, so there's no hero product to click.
- The footer is crowded with six badges (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact, Shade Match, ILIA VIP, Auto-Replenish). The only features that could drive action, VIP and Auto-Replenish, are tiny and unlinked-looking.
- The subject line "A quick note from team ILIA" doesn't say what's inside or give a reason to open it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one soft CTA under the signature, such as "SHOP THE SALE →" or "SHOP 20% OFF →". The email is still a thank-you, but it stops being a dead end.
- 2. Add a single line with the offer, for example "Our 20% sitewide sale is on through Cyber Monday." That's one honest sentence that doesn't spoil the tone.
- 3. Make the tray image shoppable. Link it to a "Thanksgiving favorites" or best-sellers page, or add small product labels.
- 4. Cut the footer badges to two or three. Keep VIP and Auto-Replenish, and make them look like links.
- 5. Add a preheader that carries a reason to open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `We hope your plate is full today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Thank you, from all of us at ILIA`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A small thank-you, and 20% off is still on.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Happy Thanksgiving. Your sale favorites are waiting.`
