# Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- The subject, "Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle," is odd. It is a Rewards Bazaar email, and the 250-point tier it names sits near the bottom, below the 100-point rewards. The top of the email carries a Savings Event banner with a "10% off, 11/3–11/9" badge. Right under it is a Rewards Bazaar hero, "November means new rewards!" with a SHOP NOW button. The subject and the discount banner pull in different directions.
- The rewards grid is the strongest part. It is a clean catalog of 100-point items (Saie, Huda Beauty, Moroccanoil, Dior, Phlur, Fenty, Aklonton, Gisou) and 250-point items (Byredo, Moroccanoil). A banner reads "Now you can get THREE 100-point rewards for just 250 points." That is a real, concrete change to the program.
- The email is very long and stacks several jobs: Savings Event, Rewards Bazaar, gift-with-purchase, holiday gift guide, Kérastase and legal text. The "0 points" in the subject reads like a personalization slot that may have been unintentionally exposed to the recipient. It makes the subject feel awkward, because it tells the reader they have nothing to spend. Overall this is a middling send that is decent in execution and muddled in focus.

## What's working

- The Savings Event banner with the gold "10% off, 11/3–11/9" badge is visible at the very top and gives a clear, dated offer.
- The "THREE 100-point rewards for just 250 points" callout is a concrete value change and is easy to scan.
- The rewards grid is orderly. Each tile has a product image, brand, name and point cost, with "ONLINE ONLY" labeled.
- The point tiers (100 and 250) are separated by large headers, so the structure is easy to follow.
- The gift-with-purchase block uses the code "UNREADY" and a "SHOP TO REDEEM" button, which makes that offer actionable.

## What's weak

- The subject's "0 points" tells the reader they have nothing to spend, which undercuts the pitch. The 250-point bundle it names is not featured in the hero.
- The email is very long, and several competing offers dilute the primary message.
- The Rewards Bazaar hero image is small and cluttered. The product shots in it are hard to read at this size.
- The 10% banner and the rewards hero compete for attention at the top, so there is no single hero.
- Product tiles have no pricing context, such as the value of each reward, which weakens the "bundle" appeal.
- A dense wall of fine print runs at the bottom and has little visual hierarchy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Lead with the rewards offer, "3 rewards for 250 points," and move the Savings Event banner below it, or the reverse.
- 2. Fix the subject. Don't surface a zero balance. Use the real balance when it is above zero, and otherwise use an offer-first line.
- 3. Give each reward tile a retail value, such as "$38 value," so the points feel like a good deal.
- 4. Cut the gift guide, Kérastase and extra modules, or move them to a separate send.
- 5. Collapse the legal text into a short expandable-style footer with the key dates only.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 rewards for 250 points, now at Sephora`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Insider points just went further 💄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick three 100-point rewards for 250 points, plus 10% off through 11/9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Rewards Bazaar picks from Dior, Fenty, Saie and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a fixed-width (700px) XHTML table layout with Epsilon-style redirect tracking. The QA run found one hard failure (empty plain-text part) and several rendering and accessibility defects visible in the source. The truncated source and relay-stripped headers leave compliance and UTM checks unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking redirects.** Links use `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1b2082480b138e36e966a3578/.../b450e8af-0781-4f8d-902f-90d875f499e6?__dU__=…`. The destination can't be read from the source.
- **Link probing is partial.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 39. No probe failures were reported for the 12 probed. The 39 skipped are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…'>` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. This is expected for an open pixel, but it has no `alt`, `width` or `height`. It is also the image behind the QA "missing alt text" warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is empty.** QA reports [FAIL] "Text version is 0 chars". Clients and filters that prefer or score the text part get nothing. If the relay stripped it, this is a capture artifact, but the QA data shows a failure.
- **Fixed-width layout with no mobile override.**
  - The outer table has `width="700"` and `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`.
  - The `@media (max-width:480px)` block only targets `.link`, `.mobile`, `.mobileHeader*`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, `.footerBanner` and `.mobileDisc`.
  - Nothing overrides `.tbl`, so the container stays 700px wide on narrow viewports, which likely causes horizontal scroll or client-side shrink-to-fit.
- **Malformed CSS inside the media query.** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block. Browsers parse `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` and `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge` as selector text, so both rules are likely invalid and dropped. That would silently remove the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` mobile sizing and the MSO image rule.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing semantics.**
  - `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
  - Layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.
  - `<title>` is the generic "Sephora".
- **Invalid meta value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is low impact.
- **JSON-LD block.** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` is stripped by nearly all mail clients. It also contains `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard` type, so it adds no value.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered tokens** (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **Dynamic points value is `0`.** The subject "Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle" and the preheader "Get three 100-point rewards for just 250 points" show a dynamic points balance rendering as `0`. This is either a real zero balance or a null/default fallback in the points field. The email's own offer needs 250 points, so the value is worth verifying against the recipient record. The send fell back to a value that makes the subject self-contradictory.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found** [WARN].
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** [WARN]. Without it, one-click unsubscribe isn't confirmed, which matters for the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results not found** [WARN]. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` can't be assessed.
- **Not confirmed either way.** All three header warnings are attributed to the AgentMail relay and may be capture artifacts, so none is a confirmed violation. The footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) is past the truncation point, so it's unverified from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from this data. The visible hrefs are opaque redirect URLs (`app.sephora.com/T/…?__dU__=`) with no UTM parameters. Final destinations and landing-page alignment would need redirect-chain resolution, which the 12-link probe didn't surface.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Generate a plain-text MIME part. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` rule out of the `@media` block into its own conditional `<style>`, and add a `.tbl { min-width:0 !important; width:100% !important }` override for ≤480px.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, `role="presentation"` to layout tables, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixel.
5. Check the points-balance field for a null/default fallback. Suppress or alter the "Use your N points" subject when the balance is below the offer threshold.
6. Re-run QA against the raw MIME (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also extend probing to the full link set, including the footer unsubscribe and address.
