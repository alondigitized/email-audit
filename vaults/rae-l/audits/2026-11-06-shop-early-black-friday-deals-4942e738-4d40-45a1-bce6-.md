# Shop Early Black Friday Deals!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized Early Black Friday send, and it is the follow-up to the 11/01 "Shop Early Black Friday Deals!" teaser. The subject is identical to that one, and the same BOGO 50% Big Bottle Sale offer is back. The black-and-gold hero has the right tone, and the three stacked offers (BOGO 50% off, an $18.99 color bundle, 40% off styling tools) are easy to scan. This version is more of a catalog than the single-offer teaser.
- The weakness is focus. The hero has no offer in it, only "Early Black Friday Deals" and a Shop now button. The actual deals sit below the fold, so the headline promises a sale without naming a price. Two red banner strips above the hero (pick up in-store, 2-hour delivery) compete with it. The "Shop now" links are small underlined text, not buttons. Repeating the 11/01 subject verbatim, five days later, will feel stale to a regular subscriber.
- Judgement: solid and clear, but safe and repetitive. It's a 6, held back by a hero with no offer and a recycled subject.

## What's working

- The black hero with gold type gives a strong, seasonal Black Friday signal and a clear first read.
- Each offer gets its own block with a bold headline: "Buy one, get one 50% off," "$18.99," "40% off."
- Product photos on a gold glitter background are consistent and stand out against the cream panel.
- The Klarna strip and the icon row (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, in-store pickup) remove friction near the bottom.
- Hair color, hair care and styling tools match a hair-focused persona.

## What's weak

- The hero names no offer. The deals only appear after the first scroll.
- Two red promo bars sit above the hero and compete with the main message.
- "Shop now" under each offer is a small underlined link, much weaker than the white hero button.
- The model photo in the $18.99 block shows no product and has no caption, so it adds nothing.
- There is no deadline or end date, so "early" deals carry no urgency.
- The product captions are tiny, and no prices are shown on the individual items.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the best offer in the hero: "Early Black Friday: BOGO 50% off Big Bottles" with a real button. This one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown.
- 3. Cut the two red banner strips to one, or move them below the offers.
- 4. Turn the per-section "Shop now" links into buttons, and show a price or savings on each product.
- 5. Replace the model photo with the product it is selling, or caption it.
- 6. Don't reuse the 11/01 subject. Lead with the offer instead.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off big bottles starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: $18.99 color + 40% off tools`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hair color bundles, styling tools and big bottles, all marked down early.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Deals end Sunday. Plus free 2-hour delivery on $35+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a dark-mode and mobile `@media (max-width: 499px)` setup. The QA run found no hard failures, but it shows an unresolved ad-pixel macro (`[CACHEBUSTER]`), an insecure `http://` tracking image, and several unlabeled images. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 55 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were validated. Broken redirects or expired campaign links can't be ruled out.
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…`
  - The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never substituted.
  - Several parameters are `-11`, which is typically a "no value" placeholder.
  - The pixel will probably cache or mis-attribute impressions.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` loads over plain HTTP. Gmail's proxy and Apple Mail privacy protection may block or rewrite it, and it adds a non-TLS third-party request.
- **Third-party pixel stack:** the email loads at least three third-party pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com`) on top of the ESP's own tracking. The `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` URL ends in what looks like a Salesforce contact ID (`003…` prefix), so subscriber identity is embedded in a third-party URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged five images with no `alt`.
  - Two have no src or identifier in the report.
  - The other three are `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, and `UCMController`, all tracking pixels. Those should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` explicitly. The two unnamed images can't be classified from the truncated source.
- **Markup validity:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, while the rest of the document is XHTML (`<meta .../>`). This is harmless in practice, but it is invalid under the declared doctype.
- **No other confirmed rendering issues:** the visible CSS has the mobile breakpoints, the `u ~ div .pattern` Gmail fix, and the `x-apple-data-detectors` reset. The source was truncated, so the body markup (and any VML or Outlook fallbacks) wasn't reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The unsubstituted `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the myvisualiq URL (see above) is the only token failure confirmed.
- No visible `{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, or `${…}` tokens appeared in the truncated source or QA data. The rest of the body wasn't available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping or not capturing them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Since Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements make both headers mandatory, they should be checked against the raw message at the sender.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. Again this is likely a relay artifact. The From domain is `em.sallybeauty.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper alignment, but that can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none could be verified. Links go through click-redirect domains and weren't probed, so the final landing URLs, UTM params, and landing page match to the "Early Black Friday Deals" offer are unknown.
- **Impression pixel parameters:** most are set to `-11` (see above), which suggests campaign, placement, and creative IDs aren't being passed to the ad platform.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro so it resolves at send time or is replaced with a static value, and populate the `-11` params, or remove the myvisualiq pixel.
2. Switch the `http://login.dotomi.com/...` pixel to HTTPS, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support TLS.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels, and give the two unlabeled images meaningful alt text if they are content.
4. Check the raw message at the sender to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click), and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't treat the QA warnings as findings until then.
5. Run a redirect-following check on a sample of the 55 skipped tracking links to confirm final destinations and UTM tags.
6. Review whether embedding a CRM contact ID in a third-party pixel URL is intentional.
