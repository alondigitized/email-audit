# Freshly picked California color
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid product-launch email for the Ethereal Baked Face Palette. It has a clear hero, a strong editorial model shot with labeled shades, and a three-collection lineup (Botanical Blur, Poppy Fields, Coastal Bloom). The pink-red watercolor header and the red CTA buttons make the whole thing feel cohesive and seasonal. It reads like a real brand email.
- The weakness is the offer. The only commercial hook is a free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with a $75+ order. It sits in a thin top banner and again near the bottom, so it's easy to miss on the first screen. The email never gives a price for the palette itself and never says when the offer ends. The "Reuse or Recycle" block is a nice sustainability touch, but it takes a lot of space for a message that doesn't push anyone to buy.
- Judgement: a polished, on-brand 6. It's well made but low on urgency. Since 10-27 "How to find your shade" and 10-25 "On everyone's lips," ILIA has sent three educational, editorial emails in a row. This one is a bit more promotional, but it still doesn't give a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero ("California Color," "Limited Edition") sets the idea quickly, with a clear red "Pick Your Palette →" button right under it.
- The model photo with arrows to Highlighter, Bronzer and Blush is useful and shows how the palette works.
- The three shade collections sit side by side, so it's easy to compare and choose one.
- The short benefit list (8-hour wear, weightless, smoothing, buildable) is easy to scan.
- The mascara gift is repeated mid-email and in the top banner, so it's hard to miss entirely.
- The footer is clean, with free shipping over $50, VIP, shade match and auto-replenish.

## What's weak

- The offer is only a small banner at the top. The mascara gift gets its real moment only at the bottom of a very long email.
- No palette price is shown, and the three palettes have no individual links or "shop this shade" buttons.
- The "Reuse or Recycle" section takes a lot of space and has only a tiny footnote. It doesn't help conversion.
- The free-gift offer has no deadline, and "$75+" is a high threshold for someone who just wants one palette.
- The blush labels repeat ("BLUSH" appears twice), so the shade guide looks a bit sloppy.
- There are no reviews or social proof anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the mascara gift up into the hero area as a visible offer. Add an end date, such as "Ends Sunday."
- 2. Add the palette price and a shade-specific button under each of the three collections, such as "Shop Poppy Fields."
- 3. Shorten or cut the Reuse or Recycle section, or fold it into one line near the footer.
- 4. Add a review or rating line near the hero.
- 5. Fix the duplicate "Blush" label, naming one "Blush 1" and one "Blush 2," or giving them real shade names.
