# It’s not over yet
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA back in full production mode with a genuinely well-built VIP-sale send — a styled velvet hero shot with pencil/mascara/gel-liner fanned out and the "20% off" tag, a clean two-day urgency line, and then a long, well-organized scroll through gift-with-purchase, extras, sets, and VIP perks. It's the same visual DNA as 09-16's "The Art of the Eye" jewelry-tray shot and 09-19's "Safe for sensitive eyes," both of which scored well in this streak — this one belongs in that tier, not with the 09-17/09-20 plain-text collapses.
- The weak point is the top: "YOU HAVE 0 VIP POINTS TO SPEND" as the very first line a non-VIP subscriber sees is a strange, slightly deflating opener, and the subject "It's not over yet" is vague filler compared to the actual offer sitting one line below it in-email ("20% off sitewide for VIPs"). The email is doing real selling work in the body; the entry points (subject + top banner) undersell it.
- Judgement: strong body execution, weak front door. Fix the subject/preheader and the 0-points banner and this is an 8; as sent, the mismatched opener caps it.

## What's working

- Full styled hero image (velvet background, "20%" tag, product fan-out) — no plain-text collapse this time.
- Clear urgency line "two days. left." directly under the hero, reinforcing a real deadline.
- Distinct, well-labeled sections (gift shop, sale's on sale, extras, sets, VIP treatment) with a dedicated button per section — no ambiguity about what each CTA does.
- VIP perks are spelled out as a bulleted list (free shipping, points, early access) rather than vague "member benefits" language.
- Trust icons at the footer (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact) add credibility without cluttering the offer area.

## What's weak

- Top banner "YOU HAVE 0 VIP POINTS TO SPEND" is a poor lead signal for a non-member — it reads like a negative balance notice, not an invitation.
- Subject "It's not over yet" gives zero indication of category (beauty), offer (20%), or urgency (2 days) — all of which are sitting right in the hero.
- Two competing offers stacked at the very top (VIP 20% banner + "20% off sitewide" ribbon) before you even get a single primary CTA is redundant messaging in a small space.
- No visible pricing, product names, or shade/ingredient specificity anywhere in the body — every CTA links to a category, not a specific hero product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the top banner copy — lead with the offer, not the points balance: "20% OFF SITEWIDE — VIP SALE ENDS IN 2 DAYS" instead of "YOU HAVE 0 VIP POINTS TO SPEND."
- 2. Pull the concrete offer and deadline into the subject line itself instead of vague "not over yet" filler.
- 3. Move the free gift-with-purchase (velvet bag, multi-stick) up near the hero — free-gift thresholds are strong click drivers and are currently buried mid-scroll.
- 4. Add one named hero product with a price or before/after price to at least one CTA (e.g. "Shop the Super Serum Skin Tint") so the click isn't purely a homepage/category jump.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off sitewide — VIP sale ends in 2 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: free gift + 20% off VIP sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free velvet makeup bag on orders $100+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `2 days left to join VIP and save 20% on everything`
