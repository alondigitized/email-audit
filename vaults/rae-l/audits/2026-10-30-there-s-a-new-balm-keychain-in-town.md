# There’s a new Balm Keychain in town
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-launch send for a collectible, not a sale. The hero is strong: "NEW! Balm Dotcom Keychain" sits beside a bright shot of the blue balm clipped to keys with a smiley charm. The second block repeats the product with an "FAQ" explainer ("Screw your favorite balm onto the functional cap, clip it on and take it everywhere!"). That is a smart way to answer the obvious "what is this?" question.
- The email is weakest on commerce. The keychain shows no price, no discount and no member perk. Four of the five "Shop now" buttons use identical copy. The one real offer, the free Three Eyed Smiley Mug with a $75+ order, is buried at the bottom. After the 10/26 "Last call for your free gift!" send and the 10/20–10/18 run of 20% sales, this feels like a soft-sell lull. It is pretty and on-brand, but it gives no reason to act today.
- Verdict: a solid brand-building send with a mid-range score. A price on the keychain or a promoted gift offer would lift it.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a "NEW!" tag, a product name and a lifestyle image that shows the keychain in use.
- The "So, how does it work?" FAQ block explains an unfamiliar product in one line.
- The Holiday Favorites tiles (Toasty Nuts, Just Baked) add shade names and tints (copper shimmer, mauve) and keep the seasonal tone.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace and no visible render bugs or broken images.
- The mug gift-with-purchase continues the gift theme from the 10/22–10/26 sends, and the "STILL ON" tag signals continuity.

## What's weak

- The keychain has no price, so there is nothing concrete to weigh before clicking.
- Every CTA reads "Shop now". Nothing says "Get the keychain" or "Shop Balm Dotcom".
- The only real offer, the free mug with a $75+ order, is at the bottom. Its asterisk points to "Terms apply" in tiny footer text.
- The product tiles show no reviews, ratings or social proof.
- Two near-identical keychain images in the top half add little. The space could have shown the keychain colors, the price or a bundle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price and a purchase hook in the hero, for example "$14 · Clips onto any Balm Dotcom". Name the CTA after the product: "Get the keychain".
- 2. Move the free mug offer up as a thin banner under the hero. It is the only offer that reduces cost, and a $75+ threshold pairs well with a $14 add-on.
- 3. Replace the second keychain image with a bundle: balm plus keychain at a set price, or a "complete your balm" module.
- 4. Add star ratings or a short review quote to the Holiday Favorites tiles, and vary the CTA copy ("Shop Toasty Nuts", "Shop Just Baked").
- 5. Add a stock or limited-run line if it is true, so the "new collectible" framing carries urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The Balm Dotcom keychain just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Screw it on, clip it on, go. Plus a free mug with $75+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorite balm, now attached to your keys.`
