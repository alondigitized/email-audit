# The Black Friday roundup
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**

- This is a plain-text-style recap with no hero image, no button and no product shots. It restates the Black Friday sale that launched on Nov 19: 20% off everything, 25% for VIPs, a free Complexion Stick, a free mini on $100+, and up to 40% off select lip and cheek items. The content is clear and the voice is warm. But it's the fourth Black Friday send in five days, after Nov 16 "48 hours from now…", Nov 18 "25% off for VIPs starts now" and Nov 19 "Up to 25% off, two gifts, and 2x points". Nothing here is new.
- The offer stack is also muddled. 20% and 25% sit next to each other, and every bullet ends in an asterisk with no terms visible. The only links are two small default-blue inline text links. The only CTA is "join free to shop" in parentheses on the VIP bullet, so the email never tells you where to go to shop.

## What's working

- The bulleted offer stack is easy to scan, and the bold lead-ins ("25% off for ILIA VIPs", "A free Complexion Stick") make each perk readable at a glance.
- The "Hi friend" voice and the "Warmly, Team ILIA" sign-off feel human and on brand.
- The VIP bullet offers a clear path to a better deal: "join free to shop" turns a non-member into a signup.
- It's short, with nothing broken or overlapping, so it reads fine on mobile.

## What's weak

- There's no hero and no button. The only links are small blue underlined text, so there is no single dominant CTA.
- The offers conflict: "20% off everything" in the opening line sits next to "25% off for ILIA VIPs". It's the same confusion flagged in the Nov 19 send.
- Every bullet ends in an asterisk with no visible terms, which makes the offers feel less trustworthy.
- There's no product imagery, no hero item and no deadline ("before it's over" is vague), so nothing creates urgency or a reason to click now.
- The cadence is heavy: this is the fourth Black Friday send in five days with no new reason to open.

- 1. Add a full-width button above or below the offer list, such as "SHOP THE BLACK FRIDAY SALE →", in the style of the Nov 18 VIP email. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Lead with one offer and tier the rest. For example: "20% off everything. VIPs get 25%, free to join." This removes the 20/25 collision.
- 3. Add two or three hero product tiles (Complexion Stick, the free mini, a lip item) so there's something to click.
- 4. Put the terms behind a visible "*See details" link, and add a real deadline such as "ends Monday at midnight PT".
- 5. If it's a roundup, say what's new or what's selling. A bestsellers row would earn the send.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: ILIA, "The Black Friday roundup"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a simple text-only Klaviyo send with two tracked links, a working Klaviyo unsubscribe link and a preheader. The main confirmed defect is the missing physical mailing address in the footer. Header-level checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified from the relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Only 2 body links, both tracked redirects:**
  - `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01M7V38SZ67ZTH6MAP24CCV195_0` ("20% off everything")
  - `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01M7V38SZ67ZTH6MAP24CCV195_1` ("join free to shop")
- The final destination URLs are hidden behind the redirect, so the HTML alone can't confirm that they resolve or that UTM parameters are present.
- The QA "image missing alt text" warning is a **false positive**. The open-tracking pixel (`https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M7V38SZ67ZTH6MAP24CCV195`) has `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative 1×1 image.
- The unsubscribe link carries `clicktracking="off"`, which is correct. It points to `manage.kmail-lists.com`, a Klaviyo-hosted domain.
- The sending-side tracking domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` is a branded subdomain, which is good for alignment.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No `<title>`, `lang` attribute, `<meta charset>` or `<meta viewport>`.** The `<head>` contains only the conditional `@import` of Klaviyo custom fonts. The `<html>` tag is bare (`<html>` with no `lang`), which hurts screen-reader language detection. The missing viewport meta may cause inconsistent mobile scaling.
- **No width-constrained container.** Content sits in bare `<p>` and `<ul>` elements directly under `<body>`, with no centered table or max-width wrapper. Line length is unbounded on wide clients.
- **Footer contrast fails WCAG AA.** The footer text and link are `color:#888` on `background:#fff`, about 3.5:1 at `font-size:12px`. AA requires 4.5:1.
- **Footer layout table has no `role="presentation"`.** It is `<table align="left">`, so some screen readers will announce it as a data table.
- **Non-descriptive link text.** The unsubscribe link reads "click here".
- **Font stack depends on a web font.** `Karla` is loaded only inside `<!--[if !mso]>`, so Outlook falls back to Arial, which is acceptable.
- **Preheader implemented correctly.** `data-kl-pt` holds "20% off, 2 free gifts, and more", with a `&nbsp;` spacer in `data-kl-sp`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `*|FNAME|*`. The greeting is a static "Hi friend,".

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No physical postal address (confirmed).** The footer contains only "You received this email from ILIA. If you would like to unsubscribe, click here." No address appears anywhere in the HTML. This is a CAN-SPAM violation, and the QA warning is accurate.
- **Asterisked offers have no terms.** Four offers are marked with `*` ("25% off for ILIA VIPs", "free Complexion Stick", "free mini", "Up to 40% off"), but the HTML contains no matching disclaimer text or terms link.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Authentication-Results not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay.
- **In-body unsubscribe link present and functional-looking.** It uses a Klaviyo `manage.kmail-lists.com/subscriptions/unsubscribe` URL with signed parameters.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the source. Both CTAs are opaque Klaviyo redirects, so the UTM parameters and the landing pages for "20% off everything" and the VIP signup can't be checked. A live click-through is needed to confirm that the destination matches the promise and carries UTMs. Nothing in the HTML indicates a problem.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a physical postal address to the footer** (CAN-SPAM). This is the only confirmed hard defect.
2. **Add the terms for the `*` offers** in the footer, or link to a terms page.
3. **Verify the raw headers.** Check for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass on `iliabeauty.com`, from an original message rather than the AgentMail relay.
4. **Add `<html lang="en">`, a `<title>` and `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">`.**
5. **Wrap the content in a centered table or div** (about 600px max-width) and add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
6. **Darken the footer text to at least `#767676`** for WCAG AA contrast, and make the link text descriptive, for example "Unsubscribe".
7. **Run a click-through test** on both tracked links to confirm UTM params and landing-page alignment.
8. **Suppress the QA false positive.** Treat `alt=""` on 1×1 tracking pixels as passing.
