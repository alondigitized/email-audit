# Our survey closes at midnight
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text-style survey reminder. It's the follow-up to the Nov 4 "$15 for your thoughts" send. That earlier email had a hero image and a clear button. This one has neither: a few short paragraphs, one underlined "2026 Survey" link, and a signature from "Marissa (from ILIA)." The personal-note format is believable, and a human-sounding sender helps the ask feel less like a blast.
- The core offer is solid and clear. It takes 5 minutes, you get a $15 iliabeauty.com credit, and the survey closes tonight at 11:59pm. The weakness is the call to action. The only click target is a small inline link inside a sentence, with no button and nothing to draw the eye. The subject creates real urgency, but the body doesn't build on it. The deadline sits in the fourth paragraph.
- Verdict: a credible, low-effort reminder that will pull some responses from people who already meant to complete it. It would do better with a button and with the deadline and credit up top.

## What's working

- The $15 iliabeauty.com credit is bolded, so it's the one thing the eye catches while scanning.
- The "5 minutes to complete" claim lowers the effort barrier.
- The 11:59pm deadline matches the subject's "midnight" and gives a real reason to act today.
- The plain, personal tone with a named sender ("Marissa") feels human and fits a feedback request.
- Nothing is broken. The text renders cleanly with no layout problems.

## What's weak

- There is no button. The only CTA is a small underlined "2026 Survey" link mid-paragraph, easy to miss on mobile.
- The deadline ("tonight at 11:59pm") is buried in the fourth paragraph instead of leading.
- "Hi there" is generic, with no personalization.
- The opening paragraph is soft ("wanted to follow up with a quick reminder"), so it doesn't convey urgency.
- No ILIA logo or imagery. It reads as a generic text note, which is weak brand reinforcement compared with the Nov 4 send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a prominent button, "Take the survey, get $15," near the top. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Move the deadline and credit into the first line: "Tonight at 11:59pm, last call: 5 minutes = $15 ILIA credit."
- 3. Add a small ILIA logo header for brand recognition and trust.
- 4. Say what the credit can be used for, such as any order, and whether it has an expiry. This makes the incentive feel concrete.
- 5. Cut the closing paragraph about reaching out with questions. It adds length and no value.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a plain-text-style Klaviyo send with working click tracking and an in-body unsubscribe link. The HTML has no physical mailing address, and the footer text and document structure have accessibility problems. Header-level checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified from the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects.** All links use the `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01M7B25HY2CE5FFGJR35QVMA88_{0,1}` redirect. The final destinations, including the survey URL, can't be confirmed from the source.
- **Anchor text and href mismatch.** Link `_1` shows the anchor text "iliabeauty.com" but points to `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/...`. This is the same brand domain, so it is low risk. Link-mismatch heuristics may still flag it, and the click target is not visibly the homepage.
- **Unsubscribe link is not wrapped.** It carries `clicktracking="off"` and points to `manage.kmail-lists.com/subscriptions/unsubscribe?...`, so it won't break behind a redirect. No issue.
- **Tracking pixel QA warning is a false positive.** The `<img src="https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/...">` has `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative 1x1 pixel. No fix needed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Low-contrast footer.** The footer text is `color:#888` on `background:#fff` at `font-size:12px`. That is about 3.5:1, which fails WCAG AA (4.5:1 for small text). The unsubscribe link uses the same `#888`, so the legally required link is hard to read.
- **Missing document structure.** There is no `lang` attribute on `<html>`, no `<title>`, and no `<meta name="viewport">`. The body has no width-constrained container, so content spans the full client width on desktop. Mobile scaling is left to the client.
- **Layout table has no semantics.** The footer `<table align="left">` has no `role="presentation"` and no width. Screen readers may announce it as a data table.
- **Empty paragraphs.** Two `<p>​</p>` elements contain only a zero-width space and act as spacers. They may be announced as blank by assistive tech.
- **Custom font.** `PostGrotesk` is loaded via `@import` inside an `<!--[if !mso]>` block. Most clients (Gmail, Outlook) ignore `@import`, but the `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` fallback is declared, so this is acceptable.
- **Link styling.** The "2026 Survey" link has no inline `text-decoration` or color override, so its styling depends on client defaults.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|...|*`) appear in the source. The greeting is the static fallback "Hi there,".

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No physical postal address (confirmed).** The footer contains only "You received this email from ILIA. If you would like to unsubscribe, click here." and no postal address. This is a CAN-SPAM requirement. The HTML is complete (it ends at `</html>`), so this is not a truncation artifact.
- **Unsubscribe link present and functional.** It sits in the body and works without a login, but its contrast is poor (see section 3). The anchor text "click here" is also non-descriptive.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers not captured.** The QA tool flags this as possibly a relay artifact (AgentMail), so it is unverified rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click support. Check the raw message headers from the sending platform.
- **Authentication-Results not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown from this data. The tracking and sending domain `send.iliabeauty.com` is aligned with the From domain `iliabeauty.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs can't be assessed.** Every link is behind the `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` redirect, and no UTM parameters are visible in the source. Whether the survey link or the iliabeauty.com link carries `utm_*` tags depends on the redirect target.
- **Likely off-domain survey.** The survey is probably hosted on a third-party form tool, so site analytics won't capture it. The destination should be confirmed by following the redirect.
- **Survey close time.** The email says the survey closes "tonight at 11:59pm" with no timezone stated. The subject says "midnight". This is a copy-level ambiguity, so it is noted here only as a landing-page alignment check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a physical mailing address to the footer. This is required by CAN-SPAM.
2. Raise the footer text and unsubscribe link contrast to at least 4.5:1, for example `#595959` or darker, and use descriptive anchor text such as "Unsubscribe".
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers from the sender, outside the AgentMail relay.
4. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass from the original message headers.
5. Add `lang="en"`, `<title>` and a viewport meta tag. Constrain the content to a centered container of about 600px, and add `role="presentation"` to the footer table.
6. Follow the `_0` and `_1` redirects to confirm the destinations. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` if the survey is on an owned property.
7. Ignore the tracking-pixel alt warning, since `alt=""` is already correct. Adjust the QA rule to skip 1x1 pixels.
