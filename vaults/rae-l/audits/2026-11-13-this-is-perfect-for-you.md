# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is another Skechers browse-abandonment send. It is clean and on-brand, but it is the same template as the 11/12 "Don't leave these styles behind!" and "Don't leave a good thing behind." emails. The hero is "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT? YOU'VE GOT GREAT TASTE!" over one product, the Britto S-1992 Landscapes sneaker. A navy TAKE ANOTHER LOOK button sits below it. The tone is friendly, but it is a nudge with no reason to act today.
- The real offer, BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair for the Black Friday Countdown, sits below a six-shoe grid. It is buried, and the strongest reason to click is not in the hero. The Britto shoe is a loud, art-collab sneaker. The recommendation grid is neutral women's cushioning and running shoes, so the "great taste" flattery and the "more options" don't quite match.
- The render is bug-free and the hierarchy is tidy. Cadence is the problem: this is at least the ninth abandonment-style send in about two weeks, and it reuses the "This is perfect for you!" subject from 11/6. Competent execution, tired concept.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and uncluttered. A single large Britto sneaker with a navy button gives a clear primary CTA.
- "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" is a clear action and the button is visible.
- The Black Friday banner is high-contrast navy with a specific offer: BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair.
- The six-shoe grid is women's-focused and each shoe is labeled with its name.
- The footer is tidy and useful: text signup, app, curbside, Afterpay and Klarna, and a store finder.

## What's weak

- The Black Friday offer is below the fold and under the product grid. The hero has no price or discount.
- "Take another look" is a soft, low-urgency CTA. There is no deadline in the top half, and the 11/17 end date appears only in the small fine print.
- The hero shoe (a colorful art collab) and the grid (neutral cushioned runners) don't clearly relate. The grid has no prices, reviews or ratings.
- The subject "This is perfect for you!" is vague and generic. The hero says "Still thinking about it?", so the two don't line up.
- The cadence is repetitive. A subscriber has seen this abandonment template several times in two weeks, including twice on 11/12.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the BOGO / 20% off banner to the top, ideally as the hero headline, and use the abandoned shoe as the visual. Put the "ends 11/17" deadline next to the offer.
- 2. Rewrite the CTA to be specific: "Get BOGO 50% off" or "Shop your Britto pair".
- 3. Add price, ratings or a "selling fast" cue under the hero shoe.
- 4. Make the grid relevant to the hero shoe (similar styles or colors), and add prices.
- 5. Cap abandonment sends at one every few days, and suppress recipients who have already received two this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Britto sneakers + BOGO 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? Save 20% through 11/17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one, get one 50% off ends 11/17. Your pair is waiting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Britto S-1992 is still in your size. Black Friday pricing applies.`
