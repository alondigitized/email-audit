# Up to 25% off, two gifts, and 2x points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday sale launch, and the hero is its weak point. The headline says "20% Off" in giant maroon type over a model's face. The banner above it and the VIP block lower down both say 25%. The subject line says "up to 25%." A subscriber has to work out that 20% is the public price and 25% is the VIP price. This follows the Nov 18 send ("25% off for VIPs starts now"), whose hero was a clean, giant 25%. Today the headline leads with the smaller number, and the "20%" overlaps the model's forehead, which makes it harder to read.
- The rest is solid. Two gifts are shown as polaroid-style product shots: a free complexion stick with any order and a free mini of choice with $100+. There is one dark "SHOP THE SALE →" button, a red VIP upsell block, and a 2x2 product grid with Allure badges. It is on-brand and seasonal, and it is the first email where the sale is actually live, not a teaser.
- My judgement is a good layout with a confusing offer. Fix the 20%/25% hierarchy and this goes up a point or two.

## What's working

- The two free gifts are shown visually, with a clear threshold on each: "with any order" and "w/ $100+".
- A single, full-width dark "SHOP THE SALE →" button sits right after the gifts, so the primary CTA is clear.
- The red VIP block stands out and spells out the member perks: 2x points, free shipping, and 25% off.
- The mascara and serum tints in the product grid have "shop now" buttons and Allure/Clean badges, which add social proof.
- The email is timely. "Starting today" fits the Nov 16–18 build-up of teasers.

## What's weak

- The headline says 20% while the top banner and VIP block say 25%. The two numbers compete.
- The "20%" in the hero overlaps the model's face and hairline, which hurts legibility.
- The Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 appears twice in the 2x2 grid, which looks like a copy-paste error.
- The gift asterisks and the "Click HERE for ILIA Promotion Details" link are tiny and sit at the very bottom, so the fine print is buried.
- The shop-now buttons are small and outlined. They are weak next to the main CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the headline say "up to 25% off," or lead with the VIP number. Put the 20% vs 25% split in one line under the hero.
- 2. Move the headline text off the model's face, or use a solid background behind it.
- 3. Replace the duplicate serum tint with a different bestseller, such as a lip product or the Eye Stylus Shadow Stick already shown elsewhere.
- 4. Move the gift terms up next to the gift images.
- 5. Add a clear deadline, such as "ends Cyber Monday." Right now the urgency is only "starting today."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `VIPs get 25% off, 2x points, and free shipping. Join free.`
