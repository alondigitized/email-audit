# 1, 2, or 3 sweeps?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, visually strong blush email built on one idea: layering Soft Focus Blush. "The build-up" hero is a stack of blush pressings, and the one/two/three-layer callouts beside the close-up face (blushed, blushier, blushiest) show the build-up concept well. The subject, "1, 2, or 3 sweeps?", matches that idea. It's a good educational hook, and the Nov 2 "[YOUR SHADE HERE]" and Oct 27 "How to find your shade" sends were similar how-to-choose emails.
- The weak spot is the offer and the CTAs. The email carries two offers, a free mini eye stylus with $75+ and 30% off Multi-Stick, and neither gets hero treatment. The stylus offer sits in a thin top banner. The 30% badge is on the Multi-Stick, a different product from the blush the hero is selling. The two CTAs, "Shop Soft Focus Blush" and "Shop Multi-Stick," also split attention.
- Overall it's a good editorial email that's under-optimized commercially. It's a 6, in line with ILIA's recent run. The Oct 30 send had a clearer single offer.

## What's working

- The "build-up" concept is visual and easy to grasp. The one, two and three layers labels sit right on the face image.
- The hero is a striking, on-brand close-up of blush pressings. The coral CTA button stands out against the cream background.
- The brush image ("clean sweep") links the product to the technique and cross-sells the Precision Powder Brush.
- The footer carries trust cues: free U.S. shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, and ILIA VIP.
- The Multi-Stick section has a clear 30% off badge and a plain CTA.

## What's weak

- The two offers compete. The free mini eye stylus (with $75+) is a small banner, and 30% off applies only to Multi-Stick, not the hero blush.
- The hero product has no price, no shade names and no reviews. The reader can't tell what Soft Focus costs or which shades exist.
- The first CTA button sits above the fold with a plain "Shop Soft Focus Blush" label. It gives no reason to click now.
- The brush section is a large image with only tiny caption text. It takes a lot of space and has no clear CTA beyond a small underlined link.
- The disclaimer text ("*See site for details") and the footer are very small and hard to read. The 30% limited-time offer has no stated end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer first. Either apply the 30% to the blush that the hero sells, or move the Multi-Stick deal into the hero banner with a deadline.
- 2. Add shade names, a price and a star rating under the layered face image, with a "Shop [shade]" CTA.
- 3. Turn the brush section into a bundle or a clear product block with a button.
- 4. Add a deadline to the "limited time" offer, such as "ends Sunday."
- 5. Make the fine print larger and link it clearly.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Blush, layered: 1, 2, or 3 sweeps`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off Multi-Stick + the blush build-up`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soft Focus Blush builds from soft to bold. Find your sweep count.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 30% off Multi-Stick, this week only.`
