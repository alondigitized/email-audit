# Thoughtful gifts under $25 ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand gift-guide send, but it is the sixth Glossier sale email in about a week. Following 11/24's "Try something new with 25% off fragrances!" (8/10), it reads as a recap, and the only new idea is the "under $25" gifting frame. The hero is "It's the little things" with a clear "Shop under $25" button, and the 25% off sticker sits in the hero image.
- The render has a real problem. The right edge is cropped on the hero sticker ("30% OFF" is cut to "30%"), on the right-hand "Shop now" buttons, on the "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" banner, and on the Crème de You headline ("Get a free Crème de You w…"). That is layout breakage across the whole email, and it weakens the offer message.
- Verdict: the concept is good and the product photography is strong. The grid has no prices, though, even though "under $25" is the whole pitch, and the cropping makes the email look unfinished. It's a solid 5-6.

## What's working

- The "under $25" gift frame is a new angle after a week of generic sale recaps.
- The hero copy is warm and specific: "A balm in their favorite flavor or a gloss in their signature shade."
- The "Shop under $25" button is a high-contrast blue and clearly the primary CTA.
- The product photography is tight and tactile, and the 2x2 grid is easy to scan.
- The Crème de You free-gift block reinforces a threshold offer that sits alongside the sale.

## What's weak

- The right edge is cut off across the email. The sticker reads "30%", the right-column "Shop now" buttons run off the edge, the banner is truncated, and the Crème de You headline is clipped mid-sentence.
- The grid shows no prices or sale prices ("$14", "$12"), so the "under $25" promise isn't proven.
- The first product image is a tight crop of an eye with a brow brush. "Boy Brow" doesn't read as a gift, and the first tile is the weakest one for a gifting theme.
- There are four "Shop now" buttons with identical copy and no product names in the CTA.
- The "Black Friday Sale" banner and the free-gift block repeat what the preceding sends said.
- There are two competing offers in the header: "25% off everything" and "30% off $100+" in the sticker. The sticker also contradicts the strip above it, "free Crème de You when you spend $100".

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the right-edge overflow. Make sure the hero sticker, the buttons and the banner fit within the container width, since the clipped "30%" and the half-sentence headline hurt trust.
- 2. Add price and sale price under each product name, for example "$16 → $12", to prove the "under $25" claim.
- 3. Lead the grid with the most giftable items, such as Balm Dotcom and Ultralip, and use gift-oriented crops instead of an eye close-up.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Gift Balm Dotcom" and "Gift Lash Slick".
- 5. Cut the repeated Black Friday banner and either remove the free-gift block or shorten it to one line.
