# Score 50% off select beauty 😎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest "50% Off Select Beauty" send Sephora has run since the pattern started on 2026-08-24 — the hero is a giant "GET 50% OFF" headline with an immediate "TODAY ONLY" three-product proof strip (Benefit, Supergoop, Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez) showing actual strikethrough prices ($28→$14, $38→$19, $27→$13.50). That's a real improvement over the vague "select beauty" hooks in prior sends — you can see the discount math working before you even scroll.
- Below the fold it reverts to the same undifferentiated catalog crawl this brand has run daily for two weeks: eight products spanning conditioner, mascara, sunscreen, lipstick, clay mask, moisturizer, and primer, with no organizing theme beyond "also 50% off." Compare to 2026-09-01's Marc Jacobs Beauty launch or 2026-09-03's "point of view" send — those had a reason to exist as a distinct email. This one is the sixth-plus iteration of the same discount-blast template, just with a tighter top section.
- Net: strong hero, forgettable body, and a subject/cadence problem — this is functionally the same email subscribers got on 08-31 and 09-02.

## What's working

- Hero pricing is concrete and visible without heavy scrolling: three named products with actual before/after prices.
- "TODAY ONLY" creates real urgency tied to a specific mechanism (not just a vague "Today Only" banner with no products behind it, as in 09-02's send).
- Clean single CTA ("SHOP NOW") directly under the hero offer — no competing buttons up top.
- No render bugs — images, pricing, and text all display correctly with clear section breaks.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) gives loyalty members a visible anchor even though this shopper has 0 points.

## What's weak

- The body catalog (8 products) has zero curation logic — skincare, makeup, and haircare are jammed together with no grouping, same complaint as 08-30's "Iconic Beauty" edit.
- "$0 points" banner sits right under the header, a slightly deflating start for a loyalty-forward brand voice.
- No product-specific "why this" signal (skin type, shade range, hair type) despite the range of categories shown — just name, description, price.
- This is at least the 6th nearly identical "50% off Select Beauty" structural send in two weeks (08-24 through 09-05) — fatigue risk is real and visible in the brand history.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body grid from 8 products to 3-4, and reuse the "TODAY ONLY" strikethrough-price format from the hero all the way down — right now only the top 3 items show real discount math, the other 8 are just full-price-implied listings with no visible before/after price.
- 2. Add a one-line category anchor per body section ("Skincare Steals," "Makeup Musts") so the grid doesn't read as a random dump.
- 3. Vary the subject/hero mechanism next send — the brand has run "50% off select beauty" as the headline nearly every day since 08-24; even a category-specific cut ("50% off Skincare Today") would break the sameness.
- 4. Move or shrink the "0 points" line — leading loyalty real estate with a null-state stat undercuts the Insider upsell right below it.
