# 3 lip shades, 20% off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's fourth consecutive send in the Buy 3, Save 20% promotional run that launched July 1. Where "Rock, Paper, Save" (July 3) leaned on playful creative, today's send pivots to a focused product-editorial — "Color by Numbers" — built entirely around a red lip trio. The concept is smart: rather than just restating the discount, it gives a fence-sitter a curated selection and a shade guide to make the decision easy. That's genuinely useful work for day four of a promo.
- The execution is clean. Hero model with a striking red lip, code banner at the top, three numbered products with tight copy, then a "Red Scale" cool/neutral/warm selector before the CTA. The visual logic flows. The 20% offer is never in doubt.
- The critical miss is urgency. This is the fourth email on the same mechanic and there is no visible end date anywhere. A subscriber who hasn't converted in four days needs a deadline, not another creative angle. Without it, this reads as "the promo is still running" rather than "act now."

## What's working

- **"Color by Numbers" concept lands.** Framing the trio as a curated editorial rather than a raw discount lifts the email above pure promotion.
- **The Red Scale shade guide is genuinely useful.** Cool / Neutral / Warm with named shades solves the decision paralysis that stops mid-funnel buyers.
- **Subject line is exceptional.** "3 lip shades, 20% off" is 21 characters, concrete, no filler — one of the cleaner subject lines in this run.
- **Three numbered products with distinct benefit copy.** Each product gets a single tight descriptor; no padding.
- **Code is prominently surfaced twice** — banner and dedicated button — reducing friction to zero.

## What's weak

- **No urgency anywhere.** Four emails in, no "ends tonight" or "last day" signal. The most compelling lever for a non-converter and it's absent.
- **The hero badge/pill on "the power of [tag]" is unreadable.** The small decorative element before "Color by Numbers" is visually noisy and the text is indistinguishable at normal reading size.
- **Cadence is at its limit.** Day four of the same offer with no new hook risks unsubscribes, not conversions.
- **No social proof.** The July 2 "Third One's the Charm" send had editorial context; this one has product copy but zero reviews or community signals despite ILIA's strong UGC catalog.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline.** A "Offer ends July 5" line in the banner or below the CTA would be the single highest-lift change. This is the last reasonable send before the promo needs to close.
- 2. **Clarify or remove the hero badge.** The "power of [tag]" pill before the headline reads as a render artifact to a fast-scrolling reader. Either make the text legible or cut it.
- 3. **Pull one review into the product section.** Even a single star rating or a quote under Lip Sketch Crayon would add the proof point the product copy lacks.
- 4. **Collapse to one CTA below the Red Scale.** "USE CODE: BUNDLEUP" and "CHOOSE SHADES + SAVE 20%" are both present; the second CTA is stronger — lead with it and drop the generic code button.
