# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth cart-abandonment send in five days, and it's the same email again. The hero is "LET'S MAKE IT OFFICIAL / YOUR CART IS READY WHEN YOU ARE." over the black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" skull sneaker, with the navy COMPLETE YOUR ORDER button. It's clean and the CTA is clear. This subscriber has now seen the identical cart item on 11/2, 11/3, 11/4 and today, so the creative does nothing new.
- The strongest part is still the 20% OFF band for Skechers Plus members, valid through 11/10. It's a real offer with a deadline. It sits below the fold, under the six-shoe grid and a black SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS bar. The hero never mentions it, so the person most likely to convert doesn't see the discount until they scroll. The cart item is also a niche Day of the Dead novelty sneaker with no price, so there's no reason to buy it today rather than yesterday.
- Verdict: competent template, but it's wearing out the subscriber. The recommendations are generic "More great options" shoes, mostly John Deere work boots, and the footer is a long list of utility links. This is a 6, mostly on the strength of the member offer.

## What's working

- The hero headline is large and legible, and the navy COMPLETE YOUR ORDER button is unambiguous.
- The cart item is shown big and centered, with its name (UNO - Alma De Guerrero) under it.
- The 20% OFF Skechers Plus band is a high-contrast block with a clear deadline ("Valid thru 11/10").
- The render is clean. There are no broken images and no overlapping text.
- The footer is useful: app download, curbside pickup, Afterpay and Klarna, and find a store.

## What's weak

- The cart item has no price, size or color, so it gives no reason to act now.
- The 20% member offer is buried below two product blocks, even though it is the only real incentive.
- The six recommended shoes have no prices and no ratings, and they're mostly John Deere boots. They don't relate to the skull sneaker in the cart.
- This is the fifth near-identical cart email in five days, which is spammy.
- The subject "Your cart is one click away!" is generic, and it's the same promise the 11/2 and 11/3 subjects made.
- The category nav and utility links at the bottom dilute the single job of the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus member offer into the hero, next to the cart item: "Your cart + 20% off through 11/10." This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cap abandonment sends at two or three, and change the creative each time. Use the cart item, then a review or social proof, then the member deadline.
- 3. Add the price, a sale price, and a size or "low stock" line to the cart item.
- 4. Replace the generic grid with items similar to the cart item, with prices and star ratings.
- 5. Cut the footer clutter. Keep the nav and drop the texting block and the app and pickup tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your UNO skull sneakers + 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Plus members: 20% off ends 11/10`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your cart is saved, and members take 20% off regular-priced styles through 11/10.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Still thinking about the Alma De Guerrero? Check out with 20% off before Nov 10.`
