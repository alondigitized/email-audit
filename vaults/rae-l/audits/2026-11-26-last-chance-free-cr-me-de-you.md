# LAST CHANCE: free Crème de You
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built last-chance send with one clear idea: spend $100 and get a free Crème de You. The hero is strong. The headline "Get a free Crème de You with your $100 order *" sits next to a red jar and blue "30% OFF" and "25% OFF" badges. The eye lands on the offer first. The deadline is where it falls short. "Last chance" and "Act fast" carry the urgency, and no date or hour appears anywhere.
- The offer is also muddled. The hero carries three things at once: a free gift at $100, 30% off the body butter, and 25% off everything. The copy then says "treat yourself to our silky soft body butter and save 30% off". A reader can't tell whether the 30% applies to the butter or to $100+ orders. The asterisk with "Terms apply" is tiny and sits at the very bottom. This is the seventh sale send in a week and a half, after "Good things come in pairs" (11/25) and "Thoughtful gifts under $25" (11/25). The "LAST CHANCE" subject is at least a real change of pace after those recaps.
- Judgement: solid execution, middling reach. It beats the 11/25 gift-guide recap because it has one hero idea. The product grid below the hero drifts into fragrance, which is a different story.

## What's working

- The hero puts one idea first. The headline, body butter jar and blue discount badges sit together at the top.
- The subject and the hero repeat "last chance", so the urgency carries from inbox to body.
- The "Don't miss out" button is a bold blue block and easy to find.
- The second block ("More than a luxurious scent", hydrated for up to 24 hours) adds a real product benefit and a skin-on-skin texture image.
- The brand look is consistent: blue Glossier wordmark, flat blue buttons and simple sans-serif type.

## What's weak

- The offers are stacked and unclear. Free gift at $100, 30% off and 25% off everything all appear in the hero.
- Urgency is vague. "Last chance" and "Act fast" have no date or time.
- The badges are cropped at the right edge. "25% OFF EVERYTHI…" and "BLA… FRID… SALE" are cut off.
- The CTAs are generic: "Don't miss out", "Get yours" and "Shop now" (twice). None names the product.
- The fragrance block under "EXTRA SAVINGS — 30% OFF" has no prices, ratings or reviews. It also changes the topic from body butter to perfume.
- The "Terms apply" disclaimer is tiny and at the bottom, while the free-gift condition is only a "*" in the headline.
- The layout has large empty areas on the left of the second block, and the lower image is offset oddly.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the hero to one offer: "Free Crème de You with $100+. Ends tonight." Move the 25% and 30% details to a single line below.
- 2. Add a real deadline in the headline or on the button, such as "ends midnight ET".
- 3. Fix the cropped badges so "25% OFF EVERYTHING" and "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" are fully visible.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific. Use "Claim my free Crème de You" in the hero and name the fragrances on their buttons.
- 5. Add review stars, a price and a "30% off" tag on the fragrance cards, or drop that block and keep the email focused on the body butter.
- 6. Put the minimum spend and exclusions in plain text near the hero instead of only in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Crème de You ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free body butter, until midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, add Crème de You free. Plus 25% off everything through tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `24-hour hydration, on us. Last day to qualify.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built (`qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`), Klaviyo-tracked (`ctrk.klclick.com`) XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive CSS and Outlook conditionals. The confirmed problems are missing alt text on 5 images and a malformed tracking `<span>`. The header-based compliance warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tracking element:** the first body element is `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M8ANWHHC6PZCCD1HDBT9E7NM_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` are not valid on `<span>`, so it is inert. It is likely a Klaviyo or Stripo artifact, and it carries a click-tracking URL that nothing can follow.
- **Incomplete link probing:** the QA probe covered 12 material links and skipped 6. The skipped links are footer, utility and social links, which are deprioritized. The unsubscribe link is always probed, but the QA output doesn't give its result, so it is not confirmed.
- **Wrapped links:** every link goes through the `ctrk.klclick.com` redirect, so final destinations can't be read from the HTML. See section 6.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes** (QA-confirmed):
  - `heroimage_1_3.jpg`
  - `frame_3984.jpg`
  - `group_3986_6.jpg`
  - `group_3987_6.jpg`
  - `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M8ANWHHC6PZCCD1HDBT9E7NM`, which is the open-tracking pixel
- The four stripocdn images are content or hero images. Without `alt`, they leave blank space and no fallback when images are blocked, and screen readers announce the filename.
- The tracking pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so the omission isn't flagged and assistive tech skips it.
- **Document setup is fine:** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a viewport meta tag, `<title>` and the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` are all present.
- **Nothing to fix from the visible source:** the `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` block opened in `<head>` and closed in `<body>` is the standard Stripo pattern. The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body was not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `*|…|*` or `{%` appear in the visible source or in the subject, "LAST CHANCE: free Crème de You". The `è` renders correctly under `<meta charset="utf-8">`. The body is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed defects:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` is not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) is not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` is not found.
- The QA output itself says these headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. Nothing here shows Glossier failed to send them, so this should not be treated as a sender-side compliance failure.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the in-body unsubscribe link and the postal address are in the truncated footer and weren't visible. The QA data doesn't confirm either.
- **Sender domain:** the From address is `gTEAM@eml.glossier.com`, a dedicated subdomain. That is consistent with good practice, but SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be verified from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverifiable from the data supplied:** all links are wrapped in `ctrk.klclick.com/l/...` redirects, and the HTML is truncated before the CTAs. UTM parameters and landing-page destinations can't be confirmed.
- **Offer continuity:** the subject promises a free Crème de You. Whether the landing page and cart honor that offer was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four stripocdn images.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `ctrk.klclick.com/o/...` pixel.
3. Remove or fix `<span rel="stylesheet" href="…klclick.com/l/…_0">`. It is invalid markup.
4. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Follow the Klaviyo redirects to confirm the final URLs, UTM parameters, and that the free-gift offer carries through to the landing page and cart.
6. Re-probe the 6 skipped links, or at least confirm the unsubscribe link resolves.
7. Use the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
