# These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a holiday gift-set catalog send. The top banner has the strongest offer: a free K18 trial size (HeatBounce leave-in conditioner) with code, and a 4X points deal on K18 gift sets with 4XK18 through 10/28. The hero, "MERRY MUST-HAVES," is a pink-and-yellow K18 collage. It reads as festive and clear, but the offer is buried in small print under the image.
- The body is a long stack of brand tiles: EADEM, M·A·C, Glow Recipe, L'Oréal Professionnel, Point of View and Prada. Each tile has a "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA" tag and a "$X value" line. The tiles look good, but there are no price-led CTAs and no per-tile buttons that I can see. The email is also at least five stacked sections long. The Sephora Savings Event banner and the 15% off moisturizers block at the bottom both compete with the gift pitch.
- Judgement: a polished but unfocused send, mid-pack for this brand. It matches the 6/10 range of the last week of Sephora sends. Offer clutter and a subject line that promises heat but names nothing hold it back.

## What's working

- The free K18 trial size is a concrete, high-value hook at the very top, with a visible "SHOP TO REDEEM" button.
- The "$X value" framing on each gift set (for example $38, $44 value) signals savings without a coupon.
- "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags add exclusivity and give each brand tile a consistent structure.
- The bright, saturated K18 hero is seasonal ("Merry Must-Haves") and stops the scroll.
- The Beauty Insider tier strip near the bottom keeps loyalty visible.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete: the free K18 trial size, 4X points on K18, 15% off moisturizers, Same-Day Unlimited and the Savings Event banner.
- The 4XK18 code and the 10/28 deadline are in tiny text under the hero, so the urgency is almost invisible.
- Only the hero has a clear "SHOP NOW" button. The six brand tiles show no distinct CTAs, so the click path is vague.
- The email is very long, and the lower half (moisturizers, Same-Day Unlimited) has nothing to do with gifts.
- The subject's pepper emojis don't say what the email is about. The "Hot picks" copy in the hero only half-explains the joke.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer in the top banner. Lead with "4X points on K18 gift sets, ends 10/28" and move the trial-size offer into a smaller secondary slot.
- 2. Add a visible "Shop [brand] set" button under each tile, with the price in the button or the line above it.
- 3. Cut the email after the Prada tile, and move the moisturizer and Same-Day Unlimited blocks to a different send.
- 4. Make the 4XK18 code and the 10/28 deadline large and bold in the hero, not in fine print.
