# ILIA VIPs get more
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's sixth email in five days — and it's the one that breaks from sales mode entirely. After yesterday's "quick note from Marissa" about Friends + Family pricing, ILIA pivots to loyalty program recruitment. The timing is the core problem: a subscriber who just received a sale email is now being redirected to program mechanics, with no acknowledgment that F+F even exists. These two messages undercut each other rather than compound.
- The execution itself is clean and honest. JOIN / EARN / ENJOY is scannable, the design stays fully on-brand, and the free-to-join framing is upfront. But "50 points for signing up" with no dollar anchor is a hollow benefit — what does 50 points buy? That answer is absent from the entire email.
- The net read: well-built email for the wrong moment. Loyalty recruitment belongs in the welcome stream — which ILIA already runs confidently (the May 16 welcome hit 9/10) — not dropped as send #6 mid-sale-window.

## What's working

- **Honest mechanics** — JOIN / EARN / ENJOY breakdown is scannable and direct; "points never expire" gets its own line and earns it.
- **Dual CTA placement** — "SIGN UP NOW" appears above and below the explainer section, reducing scroll dependency.
- **Free-to-join framed upfront** — First sentence confirms it's free; no bait-and-switch buried below the fold.
- **On-brand editorial visual** — Warm photography, minimal layout, cream/rose palette stays fully ILIA.
- **Trust badges in footer** — B Corp, Leaping Bunny, Pact Collective reinforce credibility without cluttering the body.

## What's weak

- **No point-value anchor** — "50 points" means nothing without knowing the conversion rate; the email never says 50 points = $X or what the redemption floor is.
- **Dropped F+F context** — Yesterday's Marissa email promoted F+F pricing; this send ignores it entirely, creating a disjointed mid-campaign pivot.
- **Sixth email in five days** — Cadence fatigue is real; loyalty recruitment shouldn't be the 6th consecutive send.
- **Zero urgency or joining incentive** — No limited-time bonus, no "join before Sunday and earn 2x points on your F+F order" — nothing to act on today versus next month.
- **CTA copy is generic** — "SIGN UP NOW" doesn't say what you're signing up for; a subscriber three seconds into the email doesn't know yet.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Merge with the active F+F campaign** — The single highest-leverage change: make VIP recruitment a module *inside* the F+F email. "Join ≋VIP now and earn 3x points on your F+F order this weekend" ties both asks into one and rewards immediate action.
- 2. **Anchor the point value** — "50 points = $5 reward" (or whatever the conversion is) makes the joining incentive tangible. Without it, 50 points reads like Monopoly money.
- 3. **Add a time-bound joining incentive** — "Sign up before Sunday, earn 2x points on your next order" creates a reason to act today over deferring indefinitely.
- 4. **Rewrite the CTA** — "SIGN UP NOW" → "Claim Your 50 Points" or "Join ≋VIP Free" adds specificity and carries the offer into the button.
- 5. **Add a real preheader** — The email shows no distinct preheader; inbox clients will likely bleed the hero headline, which repeats the subject. Write one that extends the pitch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Join ≋VIP free — 50 points when you sign up`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free perks for ILIA insiders — join VIP today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early access to launches, points that never expire — takes 30 seconds.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Join free, earn points on every order, redeem at checkout. No catch.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Google Fonts via `@import` (high risk)**
```css
@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap);
```
`@import` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. Montserrat will silently fall back to the declared stack `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` — acceptable fallback — but the import itself is wasted and **exposes recipient IP addresses to Google on open**, which is a GDPR/privacy signal concern.

**Klaviyo custom font import also present**
```css
@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css);
```
Redundant with the Google Fonts import for Montserrat. Two external font requests on open increases render latency.

**Link text-decoration suppressed across all states**
```css
a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover { color:#5f4b3c; text-decoration:none }
```
Removing underlines and using a near-body-text brown (`#5f4b3c`) for all link states eliminates visual affordance. Inline text links (if any) will be indistinguishable from body copy for low-vision users and in plain-rendering environments.

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title> </title>
```
Some clients (notably Outlook on Windows) surface the title tag. A whitespace-only value may display awkwardly; should be set to a meaningful string or removed.

**Alt text on images** — Cannot assess; body is truncated. Flag for verification.
