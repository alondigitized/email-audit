# Our blushes are 20% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier running a straightforward 20%-off promo on Cloud Paint and Cloud Paint Plush Blush, and it's a step up from the string of no-discount editorial sends this thread has been tracking (8/7 "Leo season," 8/8 lip deep-dive). A real, specific offer with a clean hero and a stacked GWP mechanic at the bottom ("free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush with $75+ order") gives this send more to click than most of the recent run.
- But execution drags it down. The "Cloud Paint" product-detail module has body copy that's visibly cut off mid-sentence ("achieving a gl...", "blend away with your fing...") — text is running off the right edge of its column, a real render bug that undercuts the section meant to sell the hero product. The "Cloud Paint Plush Blush in Puff..." caption is also truncated in the third "We Recommend" tile.
- Net: a genuinely better offer than the last several Glossier sends, sabotaged by sloppy layout QA in the exact module carrying the primary product story.

## What's working

- Clear, quantified offer up top: "Save 20% off Cloud Paint + Cloud Paint Plush Blush for a limited time" — first concrete discount in this thread's recent history.
- Hero product art (blush compacts + Cloud Paint tubes) is vivid and on-brand, sets color expectation immediately.
- Shade-diversity model casting across "Our Favorites" and "We Recommend" rows is strong — varied skin tones, all shown with visible product application.
- Bonus GWP stacked at the bottom (free Cheek Brush at $75+) gives a second reason to convert beyond the base discount.
- Consistent "Shop now" CTA button repeated across every product module — no ambiguity about the action.

## What's weak

- Body copy in the "Cloud Paint" section is cut off mid-word ("achieving a gl...", "blend away with your fing...") — a visible layout bug in the send's most important sell copy.
- Product caption in the third "We Recommend" tile is truncated ("Cloud Paint Plush Blush in Puff...") — same column-width issue recurring.
- The 20% off headline offer isn't restated anywhere near the actual product CTAs further down — once you scroll past the hero, the discount isn't visually reinforced.
- No urgency detail — "limited time" is undefined; no end date or countdown despite the asterisk implying fine print exists.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the copy-truncation bug in the Cloud Paint module first — widen the text column or shorten the copy so no sentence is cut off; this is the single biggest credibility hit in the send.
- 2. Repeat "20% off" as a small badge or line near each Shop Now button so the discount stays visible through the scroll, not just in the hero.
- 3. Replace "limited time" with an actual date/countdown to create real urgency (the fine-print asterisk is currently doing narrative work the copy never pays off).
- 4. Tighten the third "We Recommend" caption so "Puff" isn't clipped — same fix as #1, different module.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cloud Paint + Plush Blush: 20% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Cheek Brush when you spend $75+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two blush formulas, one discount, ends soon`
