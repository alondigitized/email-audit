# ENDS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🫣
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the campaign-close send for Sephora's Major Summer Deals arc — the same window that opened June 11, triggered a "chapter is closing" email on June 22, and now arrives June 23 as the definitive last call. Structurally it's doing everything right at the top: "ENDS TODAY" + 50% off in the subject, 500 bonus points badge in the hero, free same-day delivery callout, and a clearly staged dual-offer layout. The urgency mechanism is sound.
- Below the hero it falls apart. Six distinct content sections — must-haves grid, Spot It/Shop It, Just Arrived, Beauty INSIDER, footer nav — all compete for scroll depth in a send that should be doing exactly one thing: closing fence-sitters. An "ENDS TODAY" email should sprint; this one meanders.
- A subscriber who opened yesterday's "chapter is closing" send is now receiving their third near-identical urgency signal from Sephora in 48 hours. The subject line "up to 50% off select beauty" is a near-verbatim repeat of June 16's Father's Day send. That's not a dealbreaker at campaign close, but it increases the chance that opened = acknowledged rather than opened = clicked.

## What's working

- **"ENDS TODAY" + specific discount in subject** — the combination is the highest-conversion close-out formula; creates genuine decision pressure for anyone who bookmarked the campaign.
- **Dual offers (50% and 25%) displayed side by side** — lets price-sensitivity tiers self-select without a second email.
- **500 bonus points badge anchored in the hero** — loyalty upsell is prominent and in the right place, not buried in a footer disclaimer.
- **Free same-day delivery callout near top** — practical benefit stacked on top of the discount; removes a friction point for last-minute shoppers.
- **Clean render throughout** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds across the visible viewport.

## What's weak

- **Six content sections compete with the close-out message** — must-haves grid, Spot It/Shop It, Just Arrived, and Beauty INSIDER all pull scroll depth that belongs to the primary urgency CTA.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA copy is repeated verbatim across every section** — specific copy ("Shop 50% Off Deals," "See What's 50% Off") costs nothing and gives the click somewhere to land.
- **Subject is a near-repeat of June 16's** — "up to 50% off select beauty" appeared in the Father's Day send two weeks ago; a subscriber tracking the arc has now seen it three times.
- **Hero stays vague on which categories are 50% off** — "select beauty" means everything and nothing; two or three named categories (skincare, fragrance, makeup) sharpen intent.
- **Dual "up to" qualifiers on both offers** — standard, but with no anchor product or category named in the hero, both discounts feel soft.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut to four sections max** — hero, dual-offer CTAs, one product row tied to the 50% off, loyalty callout. Strip the must-haves grid, Spot It, and Just Arrived entirely; those are mid-funnel sends, not last-day sends.
- 2. **Make CTA copy specific** — "Shop 50% Off Deals" and "Shop 25% Off Picks" over two SHOP NOW repeats.
- 3. **Name two or three categories in the hero** — "50% off skincare, makeup & fragrance" converts measurably better than "50% off select beauty."
- 4. **Differentiate from June 22's send** — lean into the timestamp rather than restating the discount: "Deals expire at midnight" tells a different story than "chapter is closing."
- 5. **Test a loyalty-led subject** — the audience most likely to convert at campaign close is the Beauty Insider who's been tracking points all week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: 50% off ends tonight 🫣`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Beauty Insider: last chance — 50% off + 500 bonus pts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skincare, makeup, fragrance — all on sale until midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free same-day delivery on everything you pick today`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). This is a known Sephora template choice but is still a real accessibility defect.

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied globally (confirmed)**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Suppresses OS-level font scaling for low-vision users. Applying this to `body` and all block elements is overly broad — the intent (prevent iOS auto-scaling phone numbers) is achieved with `a[href^=tel]` alone.

**Alt text on images** — cannot confirm from truncated source. Must verify that all `<img>` tags carry descriptive `alt` attributes, especially hero and product images.

**`<title>Sephora</title>` (confirmed)** — generic; screen readers announce this as the document title in webmail. Low severity but could be more descriptive.

**`robots: no index` (confirmed)** — correct practice for emails with a hosted web version; not an issue.
