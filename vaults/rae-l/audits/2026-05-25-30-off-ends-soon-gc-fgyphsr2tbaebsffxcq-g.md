# 30% off ends soon
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the third and final email in Glossier's Memorial Day 30%-off arc — "Memorial Day Sale starts NOW" (5/21), "30% is still on!" (5/23), and now the close on 5/25. The creative formula is nearly identical across all three: hero product shot, product grid with orange "Shop Now" CTAs, GWP sweetener at the bottom. What's new here is a prominent **50% OFF badge** on a specific product and the **"Get a free Lip Glaze on us!"** offer — the only fresh moves in an otherwise recycled layout.
- The subject line does its job cleanly. The email renders without issues. But by send three in five days on the same discount, the body needs to work harder than it does: no hard deadline date, no social proof, and the most compelling detail (the 50% OFF product) is buried in a product grid rather than leading the hero.
- Verdict: a competent final push that will convert fence-sitters who've been waiting for the right moment, but it misses the opportunity to genuinely escalate urgency. If you ignored the first two emails, this one doesn't give you a dramatically better reason to act right now — and that's the job it needed to do.

## What's working

- **Subject is 18 characters of clarity** — "30% off ends soon" has a specific discount, an urgency signal, and zero ambiguity.
- **50% OFF badge is a genuine escalation** — a specific product at a deeper discount gives this send something the prior two didn't; it rewards subscribers who held out.
- **GWP ("Get a free Lip Glaze on us!") is a new sweetener** — not prominently featured in the prior two sends; it's a credible AOV-driver that justifies acting now over waiting.
- **Clean render, no broken assets** — hero product loads sharp, layout intact throughout, orange CTAs are consistently visible.
- **"CURATED FOR YOU" product grid** — at least gestures at personalization, even if it's likely templated.

## What's weak

- **Three sends, one creative shell** — same grid layout, same orange CTAs, same GWP-at-bottom structure as the 5/21 and 5/23 sends. Nothing signals "this is it — you're about to miss it."
- **"Ends soon" is vague** — no date, no countdown, no time. The 5/23 email was already doing urgency; "ends soon" on 5/25 needed to harden to "ends tonight at midnight."
- **50% OFF product gets less real estate than it deserves** — the deeper discount is the most compelling thing in this email, yet it's a badge in a product grid rather than the hero.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic throughout** — with a catalog-style multi-product layout, "Shop Now" doesn't direct attention. It's the laziest possible button copy.
- **GWP buried at bottom** — the free Lip Glaze is a strong incentive to cross the $50 spend threshold; it should appear immediately after the hero, not after several product rows.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pin a hard deadline everywhere** — replace "ends soon" with the actual close ("Sale ends tonight at midnight" or "Ends May 26"). This is the single highest-lift change; fence-sitters on email three act on specifics, not vibes.
- 2. **Make the 50% OFF product the hero** — if one item is at half price, that's above the fold. Swap the hero product shot for the 50% product, name it explicitly, and let 30% be the supporting offer.
- 3. **Upgrade CTA copy** — "Shop Now" → "Shop 30% Off" at minimum. Ideally product-specific buttons where the grid allows: "Shop Body Spray," "Shop Toner Spray."
- 4. **Move the GWP above the product grid** — one line under the hero: "Spend $50 and get a free Lip Glaze — no code needed." Puts the incentive before the browsing, not after.
- 5. **Add a best-sellers signal** — this audience has ignored two prior sends; "our top 5 during the sale" anchors the undecided instead of presenting a full catalog.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sale ends tonight — 30% off + free Lip Glaze`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 30% off (and one thing is 50% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Lip Glaze with $50+. Sale ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The sale closes tonight. These are the top sellers still in stock.`
