# All the complexion + up to 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a category-focused complexion send, and the product grid is the best part of it. Five complexion products sit in a clean zigzag, each with shade count, coverage, finish and benefits. That detail is useful when you're choosing a base. It works as a shopping guide rather than a banner.
- The offer story is muddled. The top bar says "25% off sitewide" with VIP. The hero badge and the copy say "20%". The VIP block at the bottom says "unlock 25% off". The subject says "up to 25% off". Two numbers, one conditional on VIP, make the headline offer hard to trust. This repeats the 11/19 launch, where the hero said 20% and the VIP block said 25%. The brand has had two days to fix it and hasn't.
- The email is also long. It runs a hero, a product grid, a shade-quiz banner, a free-gift edit, a VIP block and a trust row. After the 11/21 "Choose your shade of 30% off" Multi-Stick send, a third complexion-adjacent promo in two days feels repetitive. This is a decent shopping page in email form, but it has no single sharp reason to click today.

## What's working

- The product cards give shade count, coverage, finish and benefits for each item, which answers the questions a base buyer has.
- The "free" and "NEW" tags on the Complexion Stick, concealer and powder add quick signals.
- The shade-match quiz banner ("your shade here") addresses the main barrier to buying complexion online.
- The maroon CTA buttons are consistent and clearly visible, and the hero is clean and on brand.
- The free-gift section lays out the mini-of-choice at $100+ and the free Complexion Stick with any order.

## What's weak

- The offer is inconsistent: 25% in the top bar and subject, 20% in the hero and badge, and 25% again in the VIP block. The reader can't tell what they actually get.
- The secondary CTAs are all "SHOP NOW" with the same grey outline, so there is no hierarchy and no product-specific verb.
- The length is excessive. Five products, a quiz, a gift edit and a VIP block dilute the hero action.
- The deadline is missing. It is Black Friday week, but there is no end date or countdown anywhere I can see.
- The fine print is small and pushed to the bottom ("*SEE SITE FOR DETAILS"), which weakens the honesty of the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer hierarchy. Lead with one number: "20% off everyone, 25% for VIPs," stated the same way in the banner, hero and badge.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday midnight," to the hero or top bar.
- 3. Cut the grid to three products and move the shade quiz up. Make the quiz the second block, since shade matching is the real conversion lever.
- 4. Give each product CTA a distinct label, for example "Shop Skin Tint" and "Find my shade."
- 5. Combine the free-gift and VIP blocks into one "what you get" module so the email ends on one close.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your shade, 20% off (25% if VIP)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Find your shade + a free Complexion Stick`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skin tints, concealers and foundations, each with shade, finish and coverage. Free gift included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Take the 1-minute shade quiz, then save 20% sitewide through Sunday.`
