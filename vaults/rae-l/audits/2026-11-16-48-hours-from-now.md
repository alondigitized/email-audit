# 48 hours from now…
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser for the ILIA Black Friday sale, aimed at getting people to sign up for VIP early access. The hero, "VIP early access starts in two days…", sets up urgency well. The six-tile beauty collage and the "our biggest sale loading…" sticker echo the "Gift ideas loading…" send on Nov 9, so the voice is consistent. The offer, 25% off sitewide plus two free gifts*, is concrete but sits below the hero image.
- The email is a funnel into VIP membership and doesn't sell product yet. Both big buttons say "Become a VIP" and "Join VIP to shop early", and neither tells me whether VIP is free or what it costs. The four "prep mode" product tiles are generic: two of them are the same Super Serum Skin Tint, and every link just says "shop now".
- This is the sixth ILIA send in about a week, after the $2,000 sweepstakes (Nov 10, Nov 14) and the survey reminder (Nov 13). The subject is vague and doesn't say what the email is about. It's well built, but it leans on inbox fatigue and a mystery subject.

## What's working

- The "in two days…" hero is bold and easy to read, and it frames the whole email around a deadline.
- The offer is specific and sits right under the VIP badge: 25% off sitewide plus two free gifts.
- The dark maroon CTA buttons are large and high contrast, and the same CTA repeats after the product grid.
- The Allure and 1% for the Planet badges on the product tiles add credibility.
- The footer trust block covers free shipping over $50, free returns, Leaping Bunny certification and shade match.

## What's weak

- The 25% off offer is below the hero image, so a mobile reader sees the "loading" tease first and has to scroll for the deal.
- The subject "48 hours from now…" doesn't mention ILIA, a sale or beauty, so it relies on curiosity alone.
- The Super Serum Skin Tint appears twice (the second tile is the right-hand tile in the row above, repeated at lower left). That wastes one of only four product slots.
- The "shop now" buttons are small and low contrast, and there's no price, shade or review detail to pull a click.
- There's no actual sale date or time, only "in two days".
- The asterisk on the offer points to fine print ("Click HERE for ILIA Promotion Details") that the email doesn't show.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "25% off + 2 free gifts" in the hero image, and state the actual start date and time ("Nov 18, 9am PT").
- 2. Say what VIP is and what it costs, for example "Join free" on the button.
- 3. Replace the duplicate serum tile with a different best-seller. Add star ratings and prices to the tiles.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Hold this send until the sweepstakes reminders are done, or suppress people who already entered.
- **Subject Alt A:** `VIP early access: 25% off ILIA, starts Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ILIA Black Friday head start (2 free gifts)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join free and shop 25% off sitewide before everyone else.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two free gifts + our biggest sale, 48 hours early.`

- **`@import` is used for web fonts.** The source imports `custom_fonts.css` from `static-forms.klaviyo.com` and `fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat`. Gmail and several other clients ignore `@import`. The `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` stack falls back correctly, and `h1`–`h4` fall back to Arial. This is a degradation, not a defect.
