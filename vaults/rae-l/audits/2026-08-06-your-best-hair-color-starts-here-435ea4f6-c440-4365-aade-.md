# Your Best Hair Color Starts Here ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the third COLORfest send in eight days, and it's the strongest execution of the three — this one finally delivers the campaign's actual mechanic: "Buy two, get one free select hair color," with real SKUs, real prices implied, and product shots stacked in a clean grid. Compare to 7/30's "first look" teaser (all vibe, no offer) and 8/3's "Deals of the Month" (a roundup dressed in COLORfest branding) — this is the payoff email that finally earns the branding.
- The confetti-dot border, bold hero model shot, and dark "Shop hair color" button all land well, and the offer is dated (8/7–8/9) which gives it real urgency instead of the vague "starting soon" language from two sends ago. Where it stumbles is stacking a second, unrelated Wella BOGO plus a rewards teaser plus a "starting tomorrow" 2x points plug all in the same scroll — three separate offers competing for the same click, which dilutes an otherwise sharp hero.
- Solid, on-brand, dated offer — but the email tries to do too much after a strong opening.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: bold headline, single clear CTA button ("Shop hair color") visible without much scrolling.
- The offer is dated and specific — "Buy two, get one free select hair color. Mix & match. 8/7–8/9" — a real improvement over the vaguer past sends.
- Product grid shows six distinct SKUs with brand, shade name, and a "Shop now" link each, giving shoppers actual choices instead of a generic banner.
- COLORfest branding (dot border, logo lockup) is consistent with the 7/30 teaser and 8/3 email, so the campaign reads as a coherent arc.
- Top utility bar calls out 2-hour delivery and free shipping over $35, adding low-friction value signals.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers stacked vertically (hair color BOGO → Wella toner BOGO → rewards 2x points "starting tomorrow" → $5 reward in August) fragments attention after a strong single-offer hero.
- The "Earn 2x points... Starting tomorrow" banner is oddly timed — it's not the current offer, so it reads as a distraction rather than a call to action today.
- Product cards feel visually flat/dim compared to the vivid hero photo — lighting on the swatches (especially the two purple/pink ones) makes the actual color result hard to judge.
- No visible pricing or "member price" anywhere in the product grid, so the "get one free" value isn't anchored to a dollar amount.
- Repeat of the "buy two get one free" mechanic on a *second* unrelated brand (Wella) two-thirds down dilutes the primacy of the hero offer instead of reinforcing it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer. Lead with the hair-color BOGO, drop the separate Wella BOGO block (or fold it into "more colors on sale" without a second distinct mechanic), and move the rewards/points content to its own send — this is the single highest-leverage fix since it currently splits attention three ways.
- 2. Move or delete the "Earn 2x points... Starting tomorrow" banner — a future-dated offer inside a today-dated offer email undercuts urgency. If it must stay, put it in the footer, not mid-body.
- 3. Add a price or "starting at $X" under at least the top 2 products so "buy two get one free" has a concrete anchor.
- 4. Brighten/color-correct the product swatch photography so the purple and neon-pink shades read clearly — right now they're murky against the cream background.
- 5. Add a countdown or "ends in X days" microcopy near the hero CTA to sharpen the 8/7–8/9 urgency visually, not just in body text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 2, Get 1 Free Hair Color — This Weekend Only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Boldest Color Yet, No Salon Required 💇`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix & match any 3 shades — deal ends 8/9`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Semi-permanent, permanent, or toner — all included`
