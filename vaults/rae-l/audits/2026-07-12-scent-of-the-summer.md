# Scent of the summer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's UGC-led launch push for what appears to be a new summer fragrance — "Making waves" is the hero, styled against coastal rock-and-glass photography that earns its beach-season positioning. Coming five days after the July 7 teaser and eight days after the editorial reset on July 4, the cadence is right; Glossier is letting the product breathe after a suffocating sale arc.
- The creative bet is entirely on social proof: three consecutive star-rating + consumer quote blocks drive the scroll. That's a legitimate strategy for a fragrance, where you can't smell it, so you buy the feeling others describe. The problem is that two of the three pulled quotes are awkward — one says "Feels like doing home with my hip boss after a day at the beach," and another reads "This is such sensual smellings...it has it smells like summer." Authentic UGC is a feature, but these specific pulls undercut the premium positioning Glossier worked hard to rebuild since June 19.
- The secondary "I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom / COMING SOON" block at the bottom splits this email into two products. It's a scheduling convenience, not a creative choice, and it dilutes the fragrance narrative. No offer, no price, no urgency — this is a pure editorial send, which is fine right now, but it leaves conversion entirely to ambient desire.

## What's working

- Hero image is genuinely beautiful — glass bottle among sea-pebbles reads premium and seasonally specific, earns "scent of the summer" without any text
- UGC framing is smart for fragrance marketing: scent is imaginary until you read someone else's sensory memory
- Cadence is good — one email, clean focus, not piling onto the sale arc the brand just escaped
- "Making waves" headline is brand-voice-consistent and mildly clever without trying too hard
- Multiple "Shop now" CTAs are well-placed after each review block, keeping purchase path short

## What's weak

- Two of the three UGC pulls have grammar issues or odd phrasing that undercut the brand's editorial polish ("sensual smellings," "doing home with my hip boss")
- Zero price anchor, no offer, no urgency — for a new product launch this leaves the email doing awareness work but not conversion work
- Tacking the Balm Dotcom "COMING SOON" block at the bottom splits the send into two products and weakens both
- "Shop now" is repeated three times but is generic — doesn't link to the specific fragrance product page versus sitewide
- Reviews are rendered small and low-contrast; on mobile they'll require zooming to actually read

## Recommendations

- 1. **Curate the UGC more aggressively.** Replace the two grammatically shaky quotes with ones that are sensory-specific AND well-written. "Easy and slightly androgynous / creamy but still clean" is almost great — cut everything after "clean." The other two need replacement.
- 2. **Add a price or a soft hook.** Even showing the product price ("$XX — free shipping on orders $30+") gives the reader a reason to act now rather than later. This email does zero conversion work.
- 3. **Split the Balm Dotcom block into its own email.** A "COMING SOON" tease deserves its own send; buried here it's wasted and it dilutes the fragrance story.
- 4. **Make the CTA specific.** "Shop Making Waves" or "Try the fragrance" outperforms "Shop now" every time for a new product.

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on Klaviyo (klclick.com), uses XHTML 1.0 Transitional with standard MSO conditional comment structure. One invalid tracking beacon implementation is confirmed; full link and compliance audit is blocked by the truncated HTML source.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Invalid open-tracking beacon element**

```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KXCAPPQT6MKM7XV6967T2MV9_0"></span>
```

`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This is semantically malformed HTML. Standard Klaviyo open tracking uses a 1×1 `<img>` pixel; this pattern will be silently ignored by strict parsers (e.g., some corporate gateways, newer Gmail rendering paths). Open-rate data may be undercounted.

**Fix:** Replace with a standard 1×1 tracking pixel:
```html
<img src="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/..." width="1" height="1" border="0" alt="" />
```

**Click-link audit:** Blocked — HTML is truncated before the body links appear. Cannot confirm whether all CTA hrefs resolve through Klaviyo redirect or direct, or verify UTM parameter presence.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
