# NEW Overglaze shades—Motif + Gallery
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA doing what it does best: a clean product-launch send for two new Overglaze shades (Motif, Gallery). It's a clear departure from the Thru Line GWP arc that's dominated the last two weeks (08-19 through 08-31) — this is genuinely new product, not another eyeliner mini push, which should feel fresh in the inbox. The swatch card, lip-application hero shot, and "100% said it locks in moisture" stat card are all on-brand and well-executed visually.
- But the send buries its own lede. A GWP banner ("FREE MINI EYE STYLUS W/ $75+") sits above the new-shade headline, competing with it before the reader even knows what's being launched. And the actual new-shade offer has no discount or urgency attached — it's a pure product announcement wearing a promotional structure, which caps how hard it can convert versus the deadline-driven Thru Line sends that scored higher.
- Solid, on-brand, well-shot — but a straightforward launch email, not a standout.

## What's working

- Hero headline "two *new* shades" is immediate and unambiguous — no guessing what this email is about.
- Swatch card (Motif / Gallery side-by-side) lets you compare shades before scrolling further, which is the exact product detail a lip-gloss shopper needs.
- The "100% said it locks in moisture + quenches dry, chapped lips" stat card continues the clinical-proof playbook that scored well on 08-20 and 08-22.
- Clear five-item benefit list (24-hour hydration, smoothing, plumping, resists bleeding + feathering, safe for sensitive lips) gives concrete reasons to buy beyond "new colors."
- Bottom-of-email GWP (free mini Eye Stylus in Beam w/ $75+) is a nice secondary incentive without overshadowing the shade launch — once you get past the top banner conflict.

## What's weak

- The top banner GWP offer and the "two new shades" hero headline compete for the eye in the very first fold — no clear single focal point.
- No launch incentive for the new shades themselves (no %-off, no "shop now and save") — it's a bare product announcement, which softens urgency to click.
- CTA copy is a mix of generic ("SHOP OVERGLAZE →") and specific ("CLAIM YOUR GIFT →") — the primary new-shade CTA reads as the weaker, less compelling one.
- Two competing "gifts": a mini eye stylus GWP at top and again at bottom, but the actual hero product (Motif/Gallery shades) has no matching urgency device.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill or move the top GWP banner — let "two new shades" own the first fold alone; put the GWP mention as a secondary banner after the hero, not competing above it.
- 2. Add a launch incentive tied to the new shades specifically (e.g., "20% off Motif + Gallery this week only") — right now there's no reason to buy today versus next month.
- 3. Make the primary CTA button read "Shop Motif + Gallery →" instead of the generic "SHOP OVERGLAZE →" — name the actual new products.
- 4. Move the customer quote ("like buttering your lips up...") higher, near the swatch card — social proof works better before the ask, not buried mid-scroll.
