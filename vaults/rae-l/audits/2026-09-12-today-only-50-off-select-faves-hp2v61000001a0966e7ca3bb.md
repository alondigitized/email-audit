# Today only: 50% off select faves 🌶️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest execution the "50% off select beauty" campaign has had this week, and it edges out even yesterday's stripped-down send. Subject says "50% off select faves 🌶️," and the hero banner immediately delivers "50% off Select Beauty" with a "TODAY ONLY" starburst and named brands (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) — no bait-and-switch, no digest-stacking, no fake urgency theatrics like 09-08's "LAST CHANCE ⚠️" banner.
- Where it loses points against the top-tier sends is structure, not honesty: the deal banner is a thin strip at the very top, then the email pivots hard into a "Hot and Trending" full-bleed Kilian Paris fragrance launch that eats the next full screen before any discounted product appears. A shopper scrolling for the 50%-off items has to get past an entire single-brand launch feature first — reminiscent of 09-07's EADEM problem, just less severe since at least the top banner is honest this time.
- Net: an honest, well-branded, seasonally-relevant send that's let down by putting its best asset (the sale) above the fold only in miniature, then burying it under unrelated new-launch content.

## What's working

- Subject and hero both say "50% off," and the hero literally states "50% off Select Beauty" — full alignment, no mismatch.
- Named-brand specificity in the hero subtext (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) gives the offer teeth instead of vague "select brands" language.
- "TODAY ONLY" starburst badge creates real, credible urgency tied directly to the discount.
- Clean Sephora branding throughout — consistent black/white/pink palette, Beauty Insider tier bar, loyalty points ("You have 0 points") visible in the pre-header strip.
- Grid of "New" and "Clean at Sephora"-badged products is well organized with visible pricing on every item.

## What's weak

- The 50%-off deal gets one thin banner, then the email spends a full screen-height on the unrelated Kilian Paris fragrance launch before anything discount-relevant resumes — the offer isn't visually dominant.
- No product-level tie-back to the 50% off — none of the pictured SKUs (Kilian, SAIE, LANEIGE, NARS, etc.) show a struck-through or discounted price, so it's unclear which specific products the deal applies to.
- "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last" sits in tiny fine print under the hero — the kind of hedge the brand has been called out for burying in past audits.
- The email is very long (multiple unrelated modules: Trending, New Arrivals grid, Favorites Kits, Defined Curls, Holiday Drops) — cadence risk on top of an already-daily promo pattern the brand has run since 2026-08-24.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a "Shop the 50% Off Edit" product grid immediately under the hero banner, before the Kilian Paris feature — let the discounted products, not the new launch, get the prime real estate.
- 2. Add visible discount pricing (strikethrough original price + sale price) directly on eligible product tiles so the 50% off is tangible, not just a banner claim.
- 3. Trim the email — Favorites Kits, Defined Curls, and Holiday Drops modules dilute a "today only" urgency message; save those for a non-flash-sale send.
- 4. Surface the exclusions line more honestly-sized (not necessarily larger, just not buried under the CTA) to preempt the "buried fine print" critique from past audits.
