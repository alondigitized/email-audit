# Find your holiday hair color
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand category send for hair color. The red hero, the smiling model and "Quick color refreshes. No salon required." make the pitch clear within a second. It's a good angle for the season, and it follows the 10/23 "Holiday glow, without the gray" send. That one led with gray coverage, and this one leads with a lighter at-home refresh.
- The weak spot is the offer. The product grid has four hair color and gloss items with no price, no discount and no reason to buy today. The only concrete deal is the Rewards bonus ("Buy a $50 gift card, get a $5 bonus reward"), and it sits below the fold. It is in-store only, ends 11/30, and has nothing to do with hair color. The top banners about 2-hour delivery and buy online/pick up in-store are logistics, not an offer. The email looks pretty, but it gives no price or deadline to act on.
- Judgement: a decent, focused editorial send that would need a real price hook to move anyone. Its closest comparisons are the 10/31 and 11/01 sends, which had one clear offer each, and this one is weaker on that point.

## What's working

- The hero copy is specific and benefit-led: "Quick color refreshes. No salon required."
- The single black "Shop now" button in the hero is unambiguous and high contrast against the red.
- The four-product grid with brand names (ION, Keracolor, AGEbeautiful) is easy to scan and shows real products.
- The gold glitter and bow styling matches the holiday season without cluttering the layout.
- The footer utility row (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping $40+, 2 Hour Delivery) and the Klarna strip are useful and tidy.

## What's weak

- No price, percent off or promo code appears anywhere in the hair color content, so there is no reason to click now.
- The only real offer, the $5 bonus reward, is in-store only, tied to a $50 gift card purchase, and unrelated to the hero category.
- The four product cards each have a small "Shop now" text link, which is generic and weak. There are no product prices, ratings or shade availability.
- The two thin banners above the hero compete with it, and "Shop now" appears three times in a row in the top section.
- The fine print at the bottom is long and dense, and it covers an offer that isn't the hero. That adds some "buried terms" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hair color incentive, such as "$3 off any color" or "Buy 2, save 15% on permanent color", and put it in the hero. A named deal would lift this email more than anything else.
- 2. Add prices and star ratings to the product cards, and use "Shop ION Gloss" style CTAs instead of a bare "Shop now".
- 3. Drop the redundant top banners or merge them into one line, so the hero is the first thing the eye lands on.
- 4. Make the Rewards block relevant. Either tie it to color purchases or move it lower.
- 5. Add a real deadline, for example "before your holiday party", with a date.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible markup is a conventional XHTML 1.0 Transitional, table-based template with a mobile breakpoint at 499px. The QA run flagged several third-party tracking pixels with problems: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a non-HTTPS pixel, and missing alt text. It could not confirm compliance headers or authentication, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 53 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was not substituted at send time, so the cache-busting parameter is a static string. Impression counts can be under-reported if clients or proxies cache the image.
- **Non-HTTPS tracker:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy images, but other clients may block it or show a mixed-content warning.
- **Many third-party pixels:** Besides the pixels above, there is `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2330821/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`. The trailing `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` looks like a Salesforce Contact ID, so it is a recipient identifier in a third-party pixel URL (see section 4).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 5 images:
  - two with empty `src` and no alt
  - `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (the mon-trk pixel)
  - `impression_pixel`
  - `UCMController`

  The tracking pixels are not a screen-reader concern if they are 1x1. They should still carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` so clients don't announce the filename or URL.
- **Empty `src` images:** Two images have an empty `src`. These can render as broken-image icons, and some clients re-request the page URL for them. The truncated source doesn't show whether they are template placeholders or visible content.
- **Font fallback:** The `a{}` rule sets `font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif`. No `@font-face` for Founders Grotesk appears in the visible source, so the stack falls back to Helvetica. This is harmless but unintended. The Open Sans `@font-face` blocks are valid and use `font-display: swap`.
- **Markup nit:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype. This has no practical effect in email clients.
- **Not assessable:** The HTML was truncated, so the body markup, `lang` attribute, role attributes and button implementation could not be reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq pixel URL (see section 2).
- **Identifier exposure:** The Salesforce-style ID `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` appears in a third-party pixel path and as that image's alt/name. This is a privacy and data-minimization concern, not a rendering bug.
- **Visible tokens:** None were found in the visible source. The subject line, "Find your holiday hair color", has no tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is unverified, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain `em.sallybeauty.com` matches the From address, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer:** The CSS defines `.legal a` and `.EID a` styles, which suggests footer and legal link blocks exist. The visible source doesn't include the unsubscribe link or postal address, so those CAN-SPAM elements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Landing pages and UTM parameters could not be assessed. The 53 click-tracking links were not resolved, and the final destinations are not visible in the truncated source. Check whether the redirects preserve `utm_*` parameters and land on the holiday hair-color page that the subject line implies.
