# FYI! 3 for $30 balms is still on
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer reminder. The hero reads "STILL ON / Get three balms for just $30!" with "save 38%" in the body copy. A model applying balm sits beside it. It is a near-repeat of the 11/12 send ("Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!"), only three days later, and it has no new angle. The "FYI" subject and the "STILL ON" eyebrow admit that. Balm Dotcom is also a long-running best-seller, so the offer isn't a new find.
- The layout is tidy. The hero is clear. A "Did you know" block adds a product claim (moisturizes for up to 12 hours). A flavor guide in three groups follows: Classics, Romantics and Fresh + Fruity. Every tile has the same "Shop now" button. The 12-hour claim and the flavor groupings help a shopper choose, which is better than a plain product grid.
- Overall it is competent but low-urgency. There is no deadline. The $30 price and 38% savings are stated, but the strikethrough pricing from 11/12 is gone, so the value is less visible. Nine identical "Shop now" buttons dilute the one offer. The email also doesn't say whether the $30 applies to any three balms.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Get three balms for just $30!" with "save 38%" in bold.
- The "STILL ON" eyebrow signals a continuing promo and avoids implying something new.
- The flavor groupings (Classics, Romantics, Fresh + Fruity) turn the grid into a guide for the "Not sure which flavors to get?" question.
- The "Did you know" block adds a concrete product benefit, "moisturize lips for up to 12 hours".
- The photography is strong and consistent, and the pastel footer with the smiley and hand matches the brand voice.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline or end date. "Still on" gives no reason to act today.
- The email repeats the 11/12 offer ($30 for 3) with no new creative angle. Subscribers who already skipped it have little reason to open this one.
- Nine "Shop now" buttons in the grid carry identical copy and no prices. The $10 per-tube value from 11/12 is missing, so the grid doesn't reinforce the deal.
- The rules aren't clear. Nothing says whether the three balms can be mixed, or that the deal is applied automatically.
- The hero CTA is a small, low-contrast outlined button that is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero and subject, such as "Ends Sunday" or "Ends tonight", so the reminder has urgency.
- 2. Show the mechanic: "Mix any 3, $30 auto-applied", with price tags or a "3 for $30" badge on the grid tiles.
- 3. Make the hero CTA a filled, high-contrast button with specific copy, like "Build my 3-pack".
- 4. Cut the grid to six or so top sellers and give each tile a distinct CTA such as "Add to bag", or one "Build your trio" link.
- 5. Restore the crossed-out $16 → $10 pricing from the 11/12 send to make the value visible.

## Technical Audit

The only confirmed defect is missing alt text on 15 images. The header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, and the truncated HTML doesn't let me verify the body links or footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 links were probed and 29 were skipped, and the QA output lists no failures.
- The source is truncated before any `<a href>`, so I can't check redirect chains or tracking-wrapper consistency.
- `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7EAYEQ4FA4S7YG0YJGNXKVY_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `rel` and `href` are invalid on a `<span>`. This looks like a Klaviyo-injected tracking artifact and is harmless in practice, but it is non-conformant markup.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7EAYEQ4FA4S7YG0YJGNXKVY`. It was flagged for missing alt text, which is trivial for a pixel but easy to fix with `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - `glossiersuperpackcarousel01_1_1.jpg`, `group_3972_3.jpg`, `group_3973_4.jpg` and `group_3987.jpg`
  - `glossiermjcfuturedewcarousel01_1_3_rGY.jpg` (twice), `glossierbdctriocarousel01_1_4_33Y.jpg` (twice) and `glossiermjcfuturedewcarousel01_1_2_sh6.jpg` (twice)
