# Ready to claim it all?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text-style Black Friday recap from ILIA. It has no hero image, no product shots and no button. It's the same idea as the Nov 20 "The Black Friday roundup" send (5/10), which also restated the sale as a list. The offer stack is clear: 20% off everything, 25% for VIPs, a free Complexion Stick, a free mini on $100+, and up to 40% off select lip and cheek items. Nothing in it is new, though.
- The personal-note format ("Hi there," "Warmly, Team ILIA") feels human and cuts through the glossy promo clutter. The cost is that nothing tells you where to click. The only CTAs are two inline blue links, "20% off everything" and "join free to shop". There's no visual anchor and no product. After "20% off—all. the. time." on Nov 22 and the Multi-Stick and complexion sends on Nov 21, this is a fourth or fifth near-duplicate of the same offer in four days.
- Verdict: it's an honest, readable reminder, but it's low-energy and hard to act on. A recap should make the click easy, and this one doesn't.

## What's working

- The bulleted offer stack is easy to skim, with bold lead-ins ("25% off for ILIA VIPs", "Free Complexion Stick").
- The personal-note tone differs from the brand's usual image-heavy sends and reads as a nudge from a person.
- The offers are specific and concrete: a percentage, a free gift, and a $100 threshold for the free mini.
- The VIP bullet gives a free-to-join route to the better 25% price.
- The copy is short enough to read in about ten seconds with no render bugs.

## What's weak

- There's no button and no hero. The only links are two inline blue text links, so the primary CTA is ambiguous.
- Every bullet ends in an asterisk and no fine print is visible, so the conditions are hidden. That undercuts how honest the offer feels.
- There are no product images, reviews or visual hierarchy. Nothing makes you want to shop.
- There's no deadline. "In full swing" and "now's the time" give no end date, so the urgency isn't credible.
- It repeats the Nov 19 to Nov 22 offers almost word for word, which adds to subscriber fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a single clear button, such as "SHOP THE SALE →", under the bullets, plus one hero product or model image. This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the copy, such as "Ends Monday at midnight PT," so the urgency has teeth.
- 3. Show the fine print once, in a footer line, instead of leaving four unresolved asterisks.
- 4. Link each bullet to its own destination: the VIP sign-up, the Complexion Stick, the lip and cheek edit.
- 5. Skip the send, or segment it to people who haven't opened this week's sales emails. It's a fourth recap in four days.
