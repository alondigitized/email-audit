# New techniques
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-made editorial tutorial email with almost no commercial pressure. The "the art of the eye" hero is a tight close-up, and the three illustrated looks (nuanced neutrals, color theory, smoke effect) are numbered, easy to scan and look like ILIA. As content it works. As a promotional send it is weak, because the only offer is a small "MINI MASCARA WITH ANY $75+ ORDER" strip at the very top.
- Compared with the 10-16 "The (2) gifts of VIP" send, which built its whole hero around a gift, this email demotes the same gift to a thin bar. For a subscriber who has had three VIP gift emails in four days (10-16, 10-17, 10-19), the change of pace helps. The subject "New techniques" is generic and gives no reason to open.
- Verdict: good brand-building and a good click path into eye products. It is a soft sell with a forgettable subject and no urgency.

## What's working

- The hero is arresting and on-brand. A tight eye close-up with the "art of the eye" headline sets a clear theme.
- Each look uses the same structure: illustration, then three numbered steps with product thumbnails. It is easy to scan and teaches something.
- Shade names are listed under each illustration (Faun, Filigree, Relic and so on). That helps a shopper who wants to buy the exact look.
- The dark full-width "SHOP THE LOOK →" buttons are consistent and unambiguous.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text. The footer reinforces trust with free shipping over $50, VIP and Shade Match.

## What's weak

- The mini mascara offer is tiny, in the top bar, with a "SHOP NOW" link. It is the only commercial hook and the eye misses it.
- All three CTAs read "SHOP THE LOOK" and look identical. It's unclear whether they go to a product, a collection or a bundle.
- No urgency, deadline or price benefit. Nothing says why to buy today.
- The subject "New techniques" says nothing about eyes, makeup, ILIA or the offer.
- There are no reviews, ratings or social proof, and no price points on any product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero or directly under it, for example a "Free mini mascara with $75+" banner with a visible end date. This one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Give each CTA its own product-specific label: "Shop the Eye Stylus trio", "Get the smoky look", "Add Limitless Lash". Link each to a real set or bundle.
- 3. Add one social proof line, such as a star rating for Limitless Lash or Eye Stylus.
- 4. Add a shop-all-three bundle with a price, so there is a clear path to the $75 threshold.
- 5. Use a subject that names the benefit and the category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 eye looks, 3 steps each`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free mini mascara + 3 easy eye looks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Neutrals, color, smoke: step-by-step with Eye Stylus + Limitless Lash.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Add $75, get a mini mascara. Pick your look inside.`
