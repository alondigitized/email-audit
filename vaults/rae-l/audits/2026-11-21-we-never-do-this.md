# We *never* do this...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises something rare ("We *never* do this..."), and the hero delivers a smaller idea: Glossier hoodies are 25% off. The reveal is fine, but it is a merch discount sitting inside a sitewide Black Friday sale. That sale has already been announced four times this week. The curiosity gap is also oversold, because a subscriber who has seen "25% off EVERYTHING" since 11/19 won't be surprised that a hoodie is 25% off.
- The render is clean and on-brand. The hero is clear: a bold headline, a blue "Shop now" button, and a pink hoodie with the 25% sticker. There is a real customer review with five stars, and a three-product "Collect them all" row. The weak point is the offer logic. The hero sticker shows both "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" and "30% OFF $100+*", so the hoodie headline competes with the sitewide deal. The top banner also pitches a free Crème de You, the gift from yesterday's send, and it is cropped at the right edge ("$100"). This email tries to do four jobs and none of them lands cleanly.
- Judgement: a solid, well-built merch spotlight with a misleading subject and a muddy offer. For a subscriber who has had five or six sale emails in a week, it reads as another Black Friday send with a curiosity hook that doesn't pay off.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "Glossier Hoodies are 25% off!" next to a large product shot, with a bold blue "Shop now" button.
- The review block (★★★★★, "Glad to see the embroidered logo...") adds social proof and a fit/quality detail.
- The lifestyle photo caption gives model sizes ("Ana (size Small) + Georgia (size Large)"). That helps with sizing.
- The "Collect them all" row has product names and "Permanent Collection" / "Limited edition" labels, which add some scarcity.
- Blue, pink and the wordmark are consistent with Glossier's brand, and the layout is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The "We *never* do this..." subject overpromises. The payoff is a merch discount, and it doesn't match the sitewide sale the subscriber already knows about.
- The sticker shows two competing offers (25% OFF EVERYTHING and 30% OFF $100+) on a hoodie-specific hero. It's unclear what the discount actually applies to.
- The top banner is cropped at the right edge ("...when you spend $100"). It is also a third offer, and it repeats yesterday's "A gift for you: Crème de You" send.
- All five CTAs say "Shop now". None is specific, and they don't tell the reader which hoodie they lead to.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" marquee is cut off at the edge, and the footer hand graphic is clipped. The page looks truncated at the right.
- No deadline or end date is visible, so there's no urgency despite the "rare occasion" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject honest about the payoff. Name the product and the discount ("Hoodies: 25% off, first time ever") instead of teasing something unspecified. If "never" is true, say what never happens.
- 2. Resolve the offer stack. Pick one hero offer, hoodies at 25% off, and remove the competing 30% sticker and the gift banner. If the gift has to stay, move it to a footer strip.
- 3. Fix the cropped banner and marquee so the right edge is fully visible.
- 4. Replace "Shop now" with specific CTAs: "Shop the Pink Hoodie", "Get the Terrazzo", "Shop the Cream".
- 5. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Monday", to give the offer credible urgency.
- 6. Show the sale price next to each hoodie in the "Collect them all" row so the discount is visible at the product level.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Embroidered Pink, Terrazzo and Cream. Limited editions, while they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-reviewed merch, now 25% off through Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-tracked send with sound baseline markup (`lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals, preheader). The confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes on 6 images and a malformed tracking `<span>` in the body. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, and the HTML was truncated before the footer and links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tracking element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7Z2R2VVEHDTKCR9BNNF2D4B_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`.
  - `href` and `rel="stylesheet"` are not valid on a `<span>`, so the element does nothing and is invalid markup.
  - It looks like a Klaviyo click-tracking rewrite hitting a stray attribute. It is likely harmless, but it is a template defect.
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 14 skipped. The QA output reports no broken, redirected or erroring links, so I'm not flagging any. The 14 skipped footer and social links were not verified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7Z2R2VVEHDTKCR9BNNF2D4B` is flagged for missing `alt`. For a tracking pixel the fix is `alt=""`, which is low severity.
