# Holiday Ready, from party to pajamas
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' holiday lifestyle send, and it works as a brand moment more than a sales push. The 30% off member offer is a slim maroon strip at the top, and the hero is "Holiday Ready" over a couple in a red doorway. It is warm and seasonal, but it keeps the offer from leading. Compare 11/18's "Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members" (8/10), where the discount was the hero. Here it is a header bar.
- The "Where Are You Headed?" four-tile grid (party, family, winter, cozy) is a good idea. It sorts shoppers by occasion instead of dumping a product grid on them. Every tile still goes to a category, not a product, and there is no price, rating or fit detail anywhere. The email reads as pretty and generic.
- Verdict: a clean, on-brand, season-appropriate send with a good segmentation device, but a buried offer and category-only CTAs hold it back. It is better than the browse-abandonment repeats, and clearly weaker than the 11/18 opener.

## What's working

- The occasion-based grid ("The Holiday Party," "The Family Get-Together," "The Winter Getaway," "The Cozy Morning") gives shoppers a quick way to self-select.
- The "SHOP NOW" button in the offer bar sits next to "30% OFF" and "Black Friday Early Access," so the offer and a CTA are visible without scrolling.
- The hero's serif "Holiday Ready" headline on a festive background is a clear visual anchor and fits the season.
- Color-coded buttons (maroon, forest green, navy, tan) match each tile's mood and add variety.
- The footer fine print is explicit about dates (valid 11/18 to 11/22, online only, login required), which keeps the offer honest.

## What's weak

- The 30% off banner is small and sits above the hero, so "Holiday Ready" wins the eye over the actual discount.
- No individual product is featured, so there is no price, no product name and no reviews. Every CTA goes to a category.
- The 11/22 deadline is in 6-point-style small print in the offer bar. There is no countdown or visible urgency.
- The two lifestyle images are labeled "AI-generated models." A few repeated "Shop Holiday Styles" CTAs also overlap in purpose.
- The member requirement is easy to miss. "For members" is small, and a non-member could miss that they must log in. The "Join Skechers Plus" bar above is easy to ignore.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer the hero. Put "30% OFF SITEWIDE · MEMBERS · ENDS 11/22" at hero scale and shrink "Holiday Ready" to a supporting headline.
- 2. Replace at least two category tiles with a specific product, price and a "30% off" member-price callout.
- 3. Add a visible deadline line near the top, such as "Ends Sunday 11/22," and a member-login prompt.
- 4. Drop the second "Shop Holiday Styles" banner, or swap it for a best-sellers row with ratings.
- 5. Pare the nav list at the bottom to the two or three categories that drive revenue.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member early access ends 11/22. Find your holiday pair.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has no hard failures (QA reports 0 issues, 3 warning categories). The confirmed problems are two non-HTTPS images without alt text, a third-party tracking pixel on a different domain, and a few accessibility and structured-data defects. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most `<a>` tags could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 33 links were skipped for HTTP probing because they are tracking-redirect domains (e.g. `click.emails.skechers.com`). Final destinations and link health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel over HTTP:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_SEASONAL_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11192026`
  - It is plain HTTP, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
  - It sits on a separate domain from the sending domains.
  - It carries a resolved subscriber ID (`mi_u`) in the query string.
  - It has no alt text.
- **Primary open pixel:** `https://click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?qs=...&d=100235&bmt=0` is HTTPS. It is wrapped in a `display:none` div, which is standard.
- **Inconsistent asset domains:** the `<link rel="icon">` points to `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...` (HTTP, `nl` subdomain). The rest of the email uses `image.emails.skechers.com`. Favicons in email are ignored by most clients, so this is low impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000 pixel).
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` at `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/`. This is a content image, not a pixel, so it is the more serious of the two.
- **HTTP image source (QA-confirmed):** the same two images load over `http://`. Gmail proxies them, but some clients block or warn on them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Invalid structured data:** the JSON-LD block uses `"@type":"PromotionCard"` and `"@type":"DiscountOffer"`. Neither is a valid schema.org type, so it will likely be ignored. The `Organization` entry has only a `logo`.
- **Desktop/mobile toggle:** `.mobile-content {display:block}` and `.desktop-content {display:none}` depend on `@media (min-width:768px)`. Clients that ignore media queries (some Outlook desktop versions, Gmail with non-Google accounts) would show only the mobile variant. I couldn't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Layout tables:** the first table has `role="presentation"`. The rest were not visible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`. The only personalized value is the resolved `mi_u` ID in the ink1000 pixel.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA tool itself says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw original message. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the QA tool attributes this to the relay. It is unverified, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer not inspected:** the visible HTML doesn't reach the body footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or the physical postal address.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address. CAN-SPAM doesn't prohibit this as long as the unsubscribe mechanism works.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Only one destination URL is visible:** `https://www.skechers.com/holiday-gift-guide/` in JSON-LD. It has no UTM parameters, though it is not a clickable link.
- **Tracking wrappers hide the real destinations:** the 33 redirect links were not resolved, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are **unverifiable**.
- **Campaign ID is exposed:** `mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_SEASONAL_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11192026` appears in the ink1000 pixel. The campaign code carries the date and segment ("ENGAGEDL30D"), which supports analytics tagging but cannot be matched to site-side UTMs from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to `dde00662-...png` and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
2. Serve both images over HTTPS, including `image.emails.skechers.com` assets and the ink1000 pixel (or drop the pixel if it is redundant with the ESP open pixel).
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and make `<title>` match the subject line.
5. Fix the JSON-LD: use valid types (`Offer`, `OfferCatalog` or `Organization` with `potentialAction`), or remove the block.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw MIME source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also capture the full HTML so the footer and links can be checked.
