# New from Marc Jacobs Beauty 💌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine single-brand launch email — a real departure from the "50% off Select Beauty" catalog-crawl template Sephora has run five days straight (2026-08-24 through 2026-08-31). The hero is clean: "MARC JACOBS BEAUTY," a clear model shot, a GWP hook up top ("Free Marc Jacobs Beauty Gift... $100+ purchase"), and a well-organized product grid walking through the new concealer, eyeshadow, lipstick, bronzer, blush stick, eyeliner, and mascara — one shade/product per row with a short benefit line.
- The problem is focus dilution: after seven product tiles for one collection, the email still tacks on App Early Access, a Chanel fragrance launch, and yet another "50% off Select Beauty (Today Only)" banner before the footer. That's three different offers competing with the Marc Jacobs story the subject line promised, and it undercuts what could've been a tight, high-converting single-brand drop.
- Net: better than the recent daily-deals sludge, but still an "and everything else" send rather than a disciplined launch email. Call it a mid-pack performer that's stronger on brand story than on offer clarity.

## What's working

- Single, coherent hero brand story (Marc Jacobs Beauty) instead of the multi-brand grid Sephora's been running all week.
- Free GWP offer at top is a concrete, quantified incentive ($100+ purchase → free pouch), not vague "sale" language.
- Each product tile pairs a clean image with a one-line, benefit-focused description (e.g. "flake-free... panoramic definition") — easy to scan.
- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" badge on the concealer gives a real exclusivity signal.
- No visible render bugs — images, text, and buttons all appear intact and aligned.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers stacked below the Marc Jacobs section (App Early Access, Chanel fragrance, 50% off Select Beauty) — this is the same "50% off Select Beauty" beat Sephora has hit in every recent send (08-24 through 08-31), and it dilutes a subject line that promised a focused brand feature.
- No pricing shown anywhere in the product grid except the concealer ($36) — seven products, one price point, which will frustrate anyone trying to gauge cost before clicking.
- The GWP banner condition ("$100+ MARC JACOBS BEAUTY purchase") is easy to miss — it's in small gray caps beside a black circle badge, not restated near the CTA.
- Generic repeated CTAs ("SHOP NOW") throughout — no product-specific link language, so it's unclear which button goes where once you're several tiles deep.
- Single model, single skin tone/look across the hero — no visible size/shade range signal for a color-cosmetics-heavy assortment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email at the Marc Jacobs product grid — move App Early Access, Chanel fragrance, and the 50% off Select Beauty banner into a separate send or a lighter "also new this week" footer strip. A brand-launch subject line should deliver a brand-launch email.
- 2. Add price under every product tile, not just the concealer — shoppers comparison-scan by price before they click.
- 3. Restate the GWP threshold directly under the hero CTA ("Spend $100+ on Marc Jacobs Beauty, get this pouch free") so it's not just small print near a badge.
- 4. Vary CTA copy per tile ("Shop the Concealer," "Shop the Blush Stick") instead of repeating generic "SHOP NOW▸" seven times.
- 5. Show shade range or a swatch strip (at least for the lipstick/blush/eyeshadow) — single-tone imagery undersells a color line.
