# Shop NEW! Black Friday Deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud, on-theme Black Friday send, but it is the fourth or fifth near-identical promo in two weeks. The black hero with a giant "up to 30% off" and a white Shop now button puts the offer first. Below it, three category blocks (4 for $30 hair care, 30% off styling tools, 30% off skin care) are easy to scan. Nothing is broken.
- The problem is repetition and vagueness. The 11/13 "Black Friday Savings Came Early!" and 11/08 "Black Friday came early 👀" sends already carried the same styling-tools and BF framing. Here the "NEW!" tag is the only novelty, and the subject doesn't say what's new. "Up to 30%" is a soft headline, and the three blocks run to roughly 30% anyway. The gold-glitter product tiles look uniform and show no models or prices, so nothing is specific enough to pull a click.
- Verdict: competent execution of a stale idea. Expect average engagement, with no sense of a deadline to push action.

## What's working

- The hero is high contrast, with a huge "30% off" and a white Shop now button. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The three offer blocks are cleanly separated, each with a bold headline and a "Shop now" link. Scanning is easy.
- The category mix covers hair care, styling tools and skin care, so it fits several shopper intents.
- The utility bar (free 2-hour delivery over $35, 15% off a buy-online, pick-up-in-store order) and the Klarna strip add value without crowding the hero.
- There are no render bugs. Images load and the text is clean.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline anywhere. For a Black Friday email, nothing says when the deals end.
- "Up to 30% off" is vague, and the blocks repeat the same 30% three times, so the email never gives one hero deal to chase.
- Every CTA is "Shop now". That is generic, and it is repeated about eight times.
- The product tiles show no prices, no sale prices, no ratings and no models. They also use identical gold-glitter backgrounds, so the products blur together.
- The fine print is a dense wall at the bottom. The "up to" claim and exclusions are buried there rather than shown up front.
- The email is a close repeat of the 11/13 and 11/08 sends, which makes this the fourth near-duplicate BF promo in under two weeks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown. This is the single biggest lift, because the email has urgency in no place.
- 2. Make one offer the hero, either "4 for $30 hair care" or the single best 30% item. Drop the "up to" framing and show the sale price on the product tiles.
- 3. Replace "Shop now" with specific CTAs like "Shop 4 for $30" and "Shop styling tools".
- 4. Add ratings or a "best seller" tag to the product cards.
- 5. If this is a repeat of the 11/13 send, cut it or segment it. Send it only to people who didn't open or click last time.
- 6. Add one line near the top saying what "NEW" refers to, or drop the word.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4 for $30 hair care + 30% off tools`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday deals start now — ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hair care 4 for $30, styling tools and skin care 30% off. Free 2-hour delivery over $35.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our new Black Friday prices are live. See what's 30% off before it's gone.`

# Technical Audit: Sally Beauty, "Shop NEW! Black Friday Deals"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with VML/MSO fallbacks and mobile media queries. The QA data shows two real defects in the third-party tracking pixels: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro and a non-HTTPS pixel. The header-level warnings (unsubscribe, authentication) can't be confirmed because of the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro in a tracking pixel:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883&aca=-11&si=-11&ci=-11&pi=-11&ad=-11&advt=-…`
  - The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never substituted.
  - The `-11` values for `si`, `ci`, `pi` and `ad` look like default or empty placeholders. This suggests the impression may not attribute correctly, and an identical `r` value can let proxies cache it.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` loads over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or insecure content may drop it, and it exposes the request in transit.
- **Link validation not performed:** 56 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Pixel stack:** At least three third-party pixels are present (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net`, `dotomi.com`). This adds third-party dependencies and privacy exposure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** The QA run flagged 5 images with no alt text:
  - 2 images with an empty name and empty `src`, so possible empty or broken image elements.
  - `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (`pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2334570/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`)
  - `impression_pixel`
  - `UCMController`
  - The three tracking pixels should carry explicit `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech, for example with `width="1" height="1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`.
  - The two empty-`src` images are more worrying, since empty `src` can trigger extra requests or broken-image icons in some clients.
