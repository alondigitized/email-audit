# Cheek Week ends tonight!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Cheek Week's closeout, and it's the strongest of the five sends in this arc — hero copy leads with "Ends tonight," the offer is unmissable above the fold, and the CTA button is right there before any scrolling. It follows the exact structural predictable pattern this thread has been building since 8/11: flat discount → campaign wrapper → editorial deep-dive → deadline close. That's smart sequencing, not accidental.
- Where it loses points is repetition fatigue and shopping-detail thinness. This is now the fourth Cheek Week email in seven days hitting the same 20%-off-blushes mechanic with near-identical product grid layout to 8/13 and 8/15 — a subscriber who opened those will feel almost no novelty here beyond the swapped hero model and the added free-gift tier at the bottom. The product grid itself is well organized by shade family (soft+natural, summery+bright, deep+vampy) but gives zero price, size, or review signal — just swatch, name, one-line descriptor, and a flat "Shop now."
- Net: this is a well-executed final beat of a tired arc. The urgency is real and the layout is clean, but five sends of the same discount in a week will train subscribers to wait for the next one instead of acting now.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" eyebrow + "Save 20% off all blushes" headline is immediately legible and time-bound, unlike the flatter 8/11 and 8/13 subject treatments.
- Hero CTA button ("Save now") is visible without scrolling, in brand-consistent pink-outline styling.
- Product grid is cleanly organized into three shade-mood categories (Soft + Natural, Summery + Bright, Deep + Vampy), which helps browsing even without a search bar.
- Free-gift tier at the bottom ("free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush with $75+ order") adds a second, distinct incentive beyond the blanket discount, deepening AOV motivation.
- No render bugs — images, swatches, and buttons all display cleanly with consistent spacing.

## What's weak

- Zero price information anywhere in the product grid — no $ shown for any of the six blush/liquid-blush products, so the "20% off" promise is never made concrete against a number.
- No countdown clock or specific end time — "ends tonight" without a timestamp (e.g., "ends 11:59pm ET") reads less credible as true urgency.
- This is the fourth near-identical blush promo in a week (8/11, 8/13, 8/15, now 8/17/18) — cadence fatigue is a real risk, and the visual template barely changes between sends.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in the body despite six products being pushed.
- Free gift section buries a second good offer at the very bottom, past six products — most recipients on mobile won't scroll that far.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add the countdown or explicit cutoff time to the hero ("Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") — the single biggest lever for real urgency, since "tonight" alone is vague and low-stakes.
- 2. Move the free-gift-with-purchase teaser up near the hero (e.g., as a subhead under the 20% offer) instead of burying it after six products — it's a strong secondary hook getting no visibility.
- 3. Add prices or "was $X / now $Y" under at least the hero product to make the 20% concrete instead of abstract.
- 4. Trim the grid from six products to three or four best-sellers — six near-identical shade options with no differentiation beyond swatch color is choice overload for a last-chance email.
- 5. Vary the creative template from 8/13 and 8/15 — same grid layout three sends running risks recipients pattern-matching this as "another blush email" and skipping straight to delete.
