# 💖 Spend $35, Save 15%
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This send buries its own subject-line offer behind a hoop the reader has to jump through: "Spend $35, Save 15%" reads like a straightforward checkout discount, but the hero directly under it says "Download the Sally app in August to save" — the 15% isn't a cart discount, it's an app-exclusive unlock. That's a bait-and-switch pattern the rubric explicitly penalizes, and it's the same complaint that dogged 8/28's "Pick Up & Save 15%" — Sally keeps finding narrower and narrower gates for the same 15% number instead of just running it sitewide.
- Visually this is a cleaner build than recent COLORfest sends — one hero offer, one supporting app-benefits block, one loyalty block, no four-tile grid dump. But the top bar stacks two competing CTAs ("15% off BOPIS ends 8/31 — SHOP NOW" and "Free 2-hour delivery — ORDER NOW") before the reader even reaches the hero, which fragments attention right out of the gate. The hero photo itself is oddly staged, with a second partial model visible behind the primary one, plus a red glow ring that reads a bit low-budget.
- Net: an app-download play dressed up as a spend-and-save offer, competing with two other CTAs above the fold. Fine production, muddled offer.

## What's working

- Confetti-dot brand border is consistent and on-campaign, reused cleanly at top, mid, and bottom.
- Hero headline "Get 15% off any $35+ purchase" is large, bold, and unambiguous in isolation.
- Disclaimer ("Offer expires in 30 days. Not valid with other promotions or pro discount") is visible in-body rather than buried in tiny legal footer text only.
- Sally Rewards cross-sell ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") is a clear, distinct secondary offer that doesn't compete visually with the hero.
- Utility icon row (In-Store / Online / Free Shipping $40+ / 2 Hour Delivery / BOPIS) gives quick scannable service info near the bottom.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a spend-based discount; the hero copy reveals it's actually gated behind an app download — that mismatch is the single biggest trust issue in the email.
- Two competing top-bar CTAs (BOPIS 15% off / free 2-hour delivery) dilute focus before the hero is even seen.
- The hero photo shows a visible second person/model partially behind the main subject — reads as a layout or crop error, not intentional.
- Primary "Download now" button has no visible incentive framing near the tap target itself (bullet benefits are generic: "shop faster," "save bigger") — nothing product-specific.
- Preview text is missing/not distinguishable from junk, so the inbox line does no work to complement the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and commit: either it's "Spend $35, Save 15%" sitewide (drop the app requirement from the subject/hero framing) or it's an app-download incentive — don't let the subject promise one thing and the hero deliver another.
- 2. Cut the top bar to a single CTA; running BOPIS-15% and free-2-hour-delivery side by side above the hero splits attention that should be going to the main offer.
- 3. Fix the hero image — the ghosted second model behind the subject looks like a render/crop bug and undermines the polish of the rest of the email.
- 4. If the app-download mechanic stays, be upfront about it in the subject line so it doesn't feel like a switch after the open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Download the app, save 15% on $35+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $35+ order just got 15% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Get the app this August — 15% off any $35 purchase, plus $5 back in September`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `App-exclusive: 15% off $35+, valid all August long`
