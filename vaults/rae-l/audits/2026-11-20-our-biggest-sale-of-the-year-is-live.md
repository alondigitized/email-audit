# Our biggest sale of the year is live!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the sale-is-live payoff after a three-send runway: "Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members" (11/18), "Final hours for early access" (11/19), and "Enjoy 25% off EVERYTHING" earlier today (11/19). The subject is a generic "biggest sale of the year" line, even though the body leads with 25% off everything. Glossier's blue and the product photography are on-brand and the layout is easy to scan. But the email carries no new news for someone who has already seen three sale emails this week.
- The body is a fragrance catalog: four Glossier You scents, then two 100ml bottles under "Extra savings — 30% off", then a free Crème de You gift-with-purchase module. The two offers (25% off everything, 30% off $100+) sit side by side in the hero badges. Neither is explained in the body, so the reader has to work out which discount applies to them.
- The strongest part is the product grid, with scent descriptors and a button under every product. The weakest part is the crowded, clipped hero and the repeated cadence. It's a decent send, but the fourth sale email in three days. A skimming subscriber would likely treat it as a duplicate.

## What's working

- Every product tile pairs a name with a scent descriptor ("Soft, warm + familiar", "Creamy, sensual + beachy") and a full-width "Shop now" button. The buttons are unmistakable.
- The "Extra savings — 30% off" section break gives the 100ml bottles a clear reason to exist.
- The blue and white palette and the editorial product photography are clearly Glossier, so the brand voice stays consistent with the earlier sends.
- The free Crème de You gift-with-purchase module near the bottom gives a reason to raise basket size.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" marquee band separates the product grid from the gift module and keeps the seasonal framing.

## What's weak

- The hero badges are clipped. "25% OFF EVERYTH…" runs off the right edge, and the top bar's "Get a free Crème de You when you spend $100…" is cut off.
- The gift module headline is also cut off mid-sentence ("Get a free Crème de You w…" and "your $100+ order!*"), so the offer is hard to read.
- Two different discount numbers (25% and 30%) appear in the hero with no explanation of which one applies.
- The hero button and CTA copy is generic ("Shop now" × 8), with no product-specific or benefit-specific verb.
- The fine print is a tiny "Terms apply." link at the very bottom. The asterisks on "25% off everything*" and "$100 order*" are not explained in the body.
- The email is almost all fragrance. There is no skincare or makeup. If the sale really is "everything", the email doesn't show it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped hero and top bar so the offer is fully readable, then state a single headline: "25% off everything, 30% off $100+". Say plainly how the two stack or differ.
- 2. Add a deadline ("Ends Cyber Monday" or a countdown). Today's email has no time pressure beyond the word "live".
- 3. Show range beyond fragrance: one row each for skincare (Balm Dotcom) and makeup, since the sale claims to cover "everything".
- 4. Replace the eight "Shop now" buttons with specific verbs ("Shop Glossier You", "Get the 100ml").
- 5. Move the gift-with-purchase up, directly under the hero, and spell out the $100 threshold in plain text.
- 6. Cut the cadence. Four sale emails in three days is too many. Send one email on the live day, then one reminder.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale's on: 25% off + a free Crème de You`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off $100+ and a free gift. Ends Cyber Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Glossier You, Balm Dotcom and more. All 25% off.`
