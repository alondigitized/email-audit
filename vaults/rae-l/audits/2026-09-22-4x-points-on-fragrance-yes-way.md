# 4X points on fragrance (yes way!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third time this week Sephora has sent the fragrance/4X-points offer as the headline (9/19 "Get 4X points on all fragrance until 9/23 with code NEWSCENTS," 6/10), and this send is structurally the strongest execution of that idea — it actually leads with the offer bar at the very top ("4X POINTS†† ON ALL FRAGRANCE... Earn more points on purchases with code NEWSCENTS through 9/23") and repeats it a second time mid-scroll with a proper subhead and CTA. It's also better organized than most Sephora sends this cycle: instead of one generic new-arrivals grid, it's built around named "combo" concepts (Scent Stacking, Dessert-Core Duo, Soft Girl Combo, Date-Night Stack) that each pair two products with a mood, which gives the eye something to latch onto beyond just SKU photos.
- Where it falls down is length and offer stacking — after the fragrance section it tacks on a Dior Mini GWP teaser, a holiday-drops preview, a haircare launch, a Beauty Insider rewards push, and a membership tier CTA, all before the fine print. That's five distinct asks in one email, which dilutes the 4X points hook the subject line promised. It's a good top and a bloated middle-to-bottom.

## What's working

- The 4X points offer and promo code are visible in the very first screen, with a clear black "SHOP NOW" CTA directly under it — no scrolling needed.
- The "Scent Stacking Made Easy" section reinforces the same offer with a second CTA and a visual sampler-set bundle, so the message doesn't feel like a one-line banner.
- Named combo concepts (Dessert-Core Duo, Soft Girl Combo, Date-Night Stack) give each product pair a clear positioning hook rather than a bare product grid.
- "NEW" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges are legibly placed on product tiles, adding exclusivity signal without clutter.
- No visible render bugs — images, badges, and text all appear intact and aligned.

## What's weak

- The email keeps going well past the fragrance offer into Dior GWP, holiday drops, haircare, and rewards tiers — by the bottom it's an everything-email, not a fragrance-focused send.
- Six+ product tiles plus three unrelated promo blocks makes this a very long scroll for a single campaign idea.
- The fine print ("Exclusions/terms apply") appears twice near the top without ever surfacing what's excluded, which reads as the same soft exclusion pattern flagged in the 9/19 send.
- "New 100-Point Rewards Are Here!" and the Beauty Insider tier module compete with the fragrance CTA for attention in the same email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the "Date-Night Stack" section — move the Dior GWP, holiday drops, and haircare launch into separate sends. One clear offer per email will lift click-through more than any copy tweak.
- 2. Surface at least one concrete exclusion or cap (e.g., "up to X points" or "excludes X brand") near the top CTA instead of only "Exclusions/terms apply" — repeated vague disclaimers erode trust over a week of near-identical sends.
- 3. Move the "New to fragrance layering? Chat with our AI Beauty Chat" module higher, right under the layering hack section, since it's directly relevant there and currently gets buried after four product blocks.
- 4. Vary the creative from the 9/19 send more — same code (NEWSCENTS), same 4X points mechanic, same fragrance category three times in eight days risks fatigue.
