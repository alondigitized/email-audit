# Friend, your 20% off trio awaits
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text-style reminder with no images. It's a follow-up to the 10-10 "Buy 3, save 20%—limited time only" send and the 10-09 "Add 3, subtract 20%" send. It's the third nudge on the same BUNDLEUP offer in three days. The offer is clear and the code is repeated. The note is written as a personal letter from "Marissa (from ILIA)", which feels human and stands apart from the brand's polished creative.
- The weakness is that it's all text. There are no product images, no button and no hero. The only CTAs are underlined inline links, and the eye has nothing to land on. For a beauty brand where the product visuals do the selling, three bulleted product blurbs are a thin way to carry a bundle promo. "Ends soon" gives no date, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Judgement: a decent personal-feel reminder that underdelivers on click mechanics. It's fine as a lightweight tail-end nudge, but it's the third send on one offer, and it needs visuals and a real button to earn the click.

## What's working

- The offer is stated plainly: Buy 3, Save 20% with code BUNDLEUP. The code appears in bold twice.
- The letter-style voice, signed "Marissa (from ILIA)", reads as personal and breaks up the run of designed promos.
- The three suggested products (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Overglaze Lip Gloss, Limitless Lash Mascara) take the "which three?" decision off the shopper.
- The product descriptions are specific: light-to-medium coverage with mineral SPF, a non-sticky gloss, a dual-sided wand.
- The copy is short and scannable, and it closes with a simple "Easy as 1, 2, 3."

## What's weak

- There are no product images, so the render is a wall of text with no visual hierarchy.
- There is no CTA button. The only links are underlined inline text, and none says exactly where it goes.
- "It ends soon" gives no date or time, so the urgency is vague.
- The products aren't individually linked or priced, so there's no way to jump straight to a shade or product.
- The "Hi Friend," greeting reads as a fallback with no real first name. The "*" asterisk and "See site for full promotional details" bury the fine print.
- This is the third send on BUNDLEUP in three days, after 10-09 and 10-10, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hero image of the three products together with a solid "Build your trio" button. That would lift this email more than any other change.
- 2. Give each of the three picks a thumbnail, a price and its own "Shop" link.
- 3. Replace "ends soon" with a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday at midnight PT."
- 4. Put the fine print (exclusions, minimums) in a short visible line instead of an asterisk plus a "See site" link.
- 5. Fix the "Hi Friend" fallback, or drop the greeting and open with the offer.
- 6. Suppress this send for people who already clicked or purchased from the 10-10 email.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your trio pick: skin tint, gloss, mascara`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use BUNDLEUP at checkout. Ends Sunday at midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

The QA run found no hard failures, but the captured data can't confirm unsubscribe headers, a physical address, or authentication. **2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All links use the redirect domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01M4P4Y50H7VT17T5PCGGWRJH7_0` through `_4`. The final destinations can't be checked from the source.
- Links `_1` and `_3` both wrap the BUNDLEUP code text, and `_0` and `_2` both wrap the promo CTA. This is probably the same destination, so the click data will be split across them.

- QA WARN: the tracking image `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M4P4Y50H7VT17T5PCGGWRJH7` has no alt text. It is an open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. An `alt=""` would be the correct form.
- The `<head>` contains only a Klaviyo `@import` of custom fonts, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. There is no `<title>`, no `lang` attribute on `<html>`, and no viewport meta in the visible source. These could be real gaps or truncation artifacts.
- The font stack is `Karla, sans-serif` at 15px, with `font-size: 11pt` on the `<li>` elements. The unit mix is inconsistent. Karla loads only through `@import`, so clients that strip it fall back to `sans-serif`.

- The layout is a plain `<p>` and `<ul>` with no `role` attributes, which is fine for accessibility. The footer table uses `align="left"` with an inline `background:#fff`, and the source is cut off there.
