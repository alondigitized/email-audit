# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the third time "This is perfect for you!" has landed in this inbox — audited here 2026-07-15 and again 2026-07-28, and the creative is unchanged: "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT? YOU'VE GOT GREAT TASTE!" over a single GO WALK Workout Walker hero, the same six-shoe recommendation grid, and the same 25% off kids styles banner bolted on underneath (only the dates have moved, now valid 8/5–8/13). At this point it's not a browse-abandonment nudge, it's wallpaper — a subscriber who's seen this exact template twice in the last four weeks has no reason to believe there's anything new here.
- The execution itself is clean: no render bugs, clear hierarchy, a legible CTA, and honest fine print. But cadence has caught up with quality. The kids sale banner is also disconnected from the browse item (adult running shoes) and from most of the recommendation grid, which is a mismatched cross-sell bolted onto a personalization email.
- Judgement: competently built, badly rotated. Score reflects the send quality, not the redundancy — but the redundancy is why this brand keeps landing in the 7-8 range instead of higher.

## What's working

- Clear, unambiguous hero: single product, single "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" CTA button, easy to act on.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breaks across the full length of the email.
- Six-item recommendation grid gives real alternatives without cluttering the top of the email.
- Fine print at the bottom is specific and non-deceptive (dates, exclusions, auto-applied discount clearly stated).

## What's weak

- Identical subject line and near-identical creative to the 2026-07-15 send — this is a repeat within four weeks, not a fresh campaign.
- The 25% off kids-styles banner has nothing to do with the browsed item (a men's running shoe) or most of the grid (also adult styles) — it reads like an unrelated insert.
- Preview/subject combo is generic enough that "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT?" and "YOU'VE GOT GREAT TASTE!" is now a template the recipient can recognize on sight.
- No urgency or new offer tied to the browsed product itself — the only offer (kids sale) is oddly aimed at a different segment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rotate the creative template — a subscriber getting the same "great taste" copy for the third time in a month will start ignoring the subject line entirely. Vary hero copy, layout, or at minimum the headline treatment each send.
- 2. Swap the cross-sell banner to match the browsed category (men's/running) instead of kids sale — a men's shopper doesn't want a kids' offer front and center.
- 3. Add a small urgency or incentive tied to the actual browsed item ("still in stock," "price drop," "low stock") rather than relying on recognition alone to drive the click.
- 4. Suppress this template for subscribers who've already received it within the last 2-3 weeks, and substitute a genuinely different browse-abandonment variant.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your GO WALK Workout Walker is still here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Back in stock: the shoe you looked at`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 25% off kids styles, ends 8/13`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on your size, while it lasts`

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot confirm UTM parameter presence — all sampled links use Attentive's obfuscated `ls/click?upn=...` redirector format rather than plain UTM-tagged destination URLs, so parameters (if any) are only visible post-redirect and weren't captured in the QA data provided.
- Image asset hosting is split across two domains: `image.emails.skechers.com` (ESP-hosted creative assets, all served over **http://**) and `images.skechers.com` (hero product image, served over **https://**) — inconsistent protocol use across the same brand's asset pipeline.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe link immediately** — regenerate/re-validate the Attentive unsubscribe merge tag; a 400 on this link is a compliance blocker, not just a UX bug.
2. **Add plain-text MIME alternative** — current text version is 0 chars; several inbox providers (and accessibility tools) rely on it, and its absence can hurt spam scoring.
3. **Serve all `image.emails.skechers.com` assets over HTTPS** (12 images affected) — HTTP images are commonly blocked/flagged by modern mail clients (Outlook, Gmail) and Apple Mail Privacy Protection.
4. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to `126150_NVBL_HERO_LG` and `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`; set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent** at the MTA level (not just missed by the relay capture) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
6. **Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status** independently (e.g., via a direct header dump or mail-tester) since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured through this relay.
