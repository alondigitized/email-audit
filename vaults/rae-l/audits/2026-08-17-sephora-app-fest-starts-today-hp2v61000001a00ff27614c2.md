# 💫 Sephora App Fest starts today 💫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different structure from the last six Sephora sends — instead of an unstructured catalog grid, "APP FEST" is a calendar: a big hero banner, then a day-by-day rundown (Tuesday through Sunday) with a distinct deal attached to each day, then a five-more-days recap and a member-points push. It's the most organized single send in the recent run, and it actually delivers what the subject promises — App Fest starts today, and the hero says so immediately with a clear 10% off + app-access hook.
- The tradeoff is length and repetition of mechanism: seven near-identical "day card" blocks (colored banner, product shot, callout, CTA) stacked vertically make this an extremely long scroll, and every single deal is gated behind "app only" or a specific credit card / promo code, which adds friction even though the structure itself is clean. It reads as an event calendar rather than a shoppable offer, and that's a real design choice — but it costs clicks from someone who just wants one clear deal.
- Compared to 8/16's "They're always SO good" (5.00/10, over-promised curation) and 8/14's rhode drop (7.00/10, single hero focus), this lands in the middle: better structure than the catalog-scroll pattern, but it trades one problem (unstructured grid) for another (offer fatigue via repetition).

## What's working

- The hero unambiguously states the event, timing ("STARTS TODAY"), and headline offer (10% off) in one glance — no digging required.
- Day-by-day structure with color-coded banners (Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun) gives real visual rhythm and scannability versus a flat grid.
- Multiple concrete, specific offers are visible: 25% off + 500 bonus points (credit card), 4X points, free gift with purchase, brand-specific early access (YSL, Clinique, Too Faced, Pureology).
- No render bugs — images, banners, and CTA buttons all display cleanly across every section.
- "Sephora x Olive Young — Coming Soon" teaser at the bottom adds a fresh, non-repetitive beat versus prior sends.

## What's weak

- Every deal in the calendar requires the app, a specific day, or a credit card signup — there's no single easy "just click and save" offer for someone scanning quickly.
- Seven repeated day-card blocks make this one of the longest scrolls in the recent run; visual variety across cards is thin (same layout, just recolored banners).
- The bonus-points CTA ("+500 Beauty Insider Bonus Points") duplicates the credit-card 500-point offer higher up, creating confusion about which "500 points" is being referenced.
- "FIVE More Days of App Fest" section essentially repeats the hero's message (event span, % off) a second time before finally showing the real CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a single unmissable, no-strings offer (not app-gated, not card-gated) to the very top so low-effort shoppers have something to click before wading into the calendar — right now every offer has a catch.
- 2. Cut the day-by-day section from seven cards to the 2-3 highest-value days (e.g., the credit-card 25%/500pt day and the free-gift day) and link out to "see full calendar" for the rest — shortens the scroll dramatically.
- 3. Differentiate the two "500 points" mentions with distinct copy so they don't read as a duplicate/error.
- 4. Swap "STACK NOW" for a more concrete verb+noun CTA like "Shop 10% Off" so the primary action is obvious to someone skimming.
- **Subject Alt A:** `App Fest is live — 10% off starts now 💫`
- **Subject Alt B:** `7 days, 7 drops: Sephora App Fest starts today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free-shipping and a new deal every day through 8/28`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only: stack 10% off with your first App Fest pick`
