## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The HTML was truncated, so the body links and footer weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and none were flagged. 13 were skipped as low priority (footer, utility, social), so those are unverified.
- **Click tracking.** The one tracking URL visible in the source is `https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M4VTHDY403MXKKHBXFDYQPJ5_0`, a Klaviyo click-tracking wrapper. It sits on a stray `<span rel="stylesheet" href="...">` directly after `<body>`. A `<span>` with `href` and `rel` is invalid markup and does nothing, so it is harmless but pointless. It looks like an artifact of the ESP's link-rewriting.
