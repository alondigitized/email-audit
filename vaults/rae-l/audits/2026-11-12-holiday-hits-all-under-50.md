# Holiday hits, all under $50 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-guide send. The subject promises "Holiday hits, all under $50," and the hero ("Gifts Under $50 / Beauty that made the nice list") matches it, with a SHOP NOW button. The body then lists six products, each with a price: Laneige $26, Unove $37, Dolce & Gabbana $50, Tarte $32, Salt & Stone $32 and Phlur $26. The Lys Beauty set also appears, but I couldn't read its price at this size. The red-and-burgundy holiday look fits the season, and the products sit on clean tiles.
- The problem is the very top. A purple "Spend More, Get More Fragrance" banner with a code and a "SHOP TO REDEEM" button sits above the real hero. It sells a different offer, tiered fragrance trial sizes, so two offers compete for the first glance. That's the same issue as the 11/10 "best NEW holiday sets" send, where a freebie banner pulled attention away from what the subject sold. The email also runs very long. The product list is repetitive, and several items get a green "Clean at Sephora" badge that adds clutter. The footer is dense with stacked modules for Klarna, Shark Beauty, rhode and a gift guide.
- Overall it is a competent, on-theme send. The price-capped gift angle is a clear hook, but the email is cluttered and the top is split between two offers. I'd call it a 6.

## What's working

- The subject and hero line up: "Gifts Under $50" repeats the subject's promise at the top of the email.
- Every product has a visible price, so the "under $50" claim is checkable at a glance.
- The red and burgundy holiday styling feels seasonal and fits the gift-giving moment.
- The "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA · LIMITED EDITION" tags give each product a reason to be a gift.
- The "Same-Day Delivery" mention is useful for last-minute shoppers.

## What's weak

- The "Spend More, Get More Fragrance" banner sits above the hero and competes with it. Two CTAs and two offers appear before the first product.
- The email is very long, with six near-identical product tiles before the secondary modules.
- The product CTAs are weak. I see no per-product buttons, only the single hero SHOP NOW, so shoppers have to tap images and titles.
- The secondary modules (Spot it. Shop it., Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal, Shark Beauty, rhode, gift guide) dilute the gift-under-$50 message.
- The Dolce & Gabbana set at exactly $50 sits at the edge of the "under $50" claim, and there's no loyalty or Insider benefit shown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the fragrance trial-size banner from above the hero, or move it below the product grid. The subject's offer should own the top.
- 2. Cut the product list to 4 tiles and add a visible button to each, such as "Shop Laneige set."
- 3. Order the tiles by price and appeal, and show a short tag like "Under $30" on the cheapest ones.
- 4. Remove the Klarna and Shark Beauty modules, or push them lower, and keep one gift-guide link at the end.
- 5. Show an Insider angle, such as points or member pricing, since this is the Sephora Insider sender.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a 700px fixed-width, table-based layout with a working preheader and two hidden tracking pixels. The main confirmed problems are a missing plain-text part (0 chars), an unverifiable unsubscribe/authentication setup, and several accessibility gaps in the markup. The source was truncated, so footer, link and CTA findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect links:** Links use opaque wrapped redirects, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1d01fd895a6bf51f4bbc780a0/...?__dU__=...`. The final destinations can't be seen in the source.
- **Probe coverage:** The probe covered 12 links and skipped 37 (QA: "12 material link(s) probed, 37 skipped"). No link failures were reported. 37 links, including footer and social links, were not verified.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are two:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF...` is the open pixel (`display:none; mso-hide: all`).
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=20261112_Gifts_Under_50_Stitched` is a Movable Ink pixel.
  
  Both are flagged for missing `alt`. They are hidden, so the practical impact is low. Add `alt=""` to silence the warning and keep screen readers from reading the filenames.
- **Structured data:** The JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which isn't a recognised schema.org type. Most clients strip `<script>` anyway, so this has no effect and is only noise.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No `lang` attribute:** The tag is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation language.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`:** The visible tables (e.g. `class="full-wrap tbl_cntr"`) have no role, so assistive tech may announce them as data tables.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper uses `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` rules don't override it, so narrow viewports may scroll horizontally. The CSS is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> ... <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Some parsers may drop the rules that follow it (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileDisc`, etc.).
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. It's harmless in email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Preheader:** Correctly closed and padded with `&zwnj;&nbsp;`. No issues found.
- **Plain-text part (FAIL):** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". Multipart emails without a text part raise spam-filter scores and give no fallback. This may be a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay, but it is unconfirmed.
