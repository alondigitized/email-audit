# The Appiversary celebration starts now 🎉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a niche-mechanic send dressed up as a celebration. The hero headline "Our Appiversary is here!" is vague as an offer — it's not until deep in the body that you learn the actual mechanic is "15% off when you buy online, pick up in-store with code APPBOPIS," gated to BOPIS orders only. That's the same narrow-audience trap as 8/28's "Pick Up & Save 15%," just wrapped in new branding. This campaign is explicitly app-driven (the primary CTA is "Shop in app," not a web checkout), which is a fundamentally different ask than the sitewide-discount sends this brand has leaned on since COLORfest's 8/16 "Ends tomorrow" hero.
- The visual execution is clean — confetti/rose-petal art, a well-lit phone-in-hand product shot, tidy four-product grid, clear rewards/Klarna/shipping-icon footer blocks. But the email asks two different things of the reader (download/open the app vs. use a BOPIS-only promo code) without ever landing a single, unambiguous hero offer. It reads like a brand-relaunch/anniversary announcement that got a coupon bolted on, not a deal email.
- Judgement: solid production values, muddled offer. This won't convert much beyond app loyalists who were going to open the app anyway.

## What's working

- Strong, on-brand hero image — a well-lit lifestyle photo of a phone showing the Sally app, tying the "Appiversary" concept to a concrete visual.
- Clear visual hierarchy at the top: logo → utility bar → hero headline → CTA button → image, no clutter.
- The red "Shop this week's Appiversary deals" band isolates the actual offer (15% off, code APPBOPIS, ends September 3) with good contrast and a visible deadline.
- Footer is comprehensive without being messy — rewards callout, Klarna financing, 5-icon service row, app download, social icons all cleanly separated.

## What's weak

- The hero section (headline + first CTA) never states an offer — "Our Appiversary is here! Find new & exclusive deals each week" is filler copy with no number, no percentage, nothing concrete until you scroll past the hero image.
- The one concrete offer is BOPIS-gated (buy online, pick up in-store), which most subscribers can't use if they're not near a store or prefer shipping — same narrow-audience issue flagged on 8/28's "Pick Up & Save 15%."
- Primary CTA ("Shop in app") requires leaving the inbox context entirely to open a separate app, adding real friction versus a one-tap web link.
- Two competing asks — "download/use the app" and "use this promo code in-store" — dilute what should be one clear action.
- "Your Beauty, Your Way" product grid (booster, sealer, toner, tint brush) is generic salon-supply merchandising with no connection to the Appiversary theme or offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero. Replace "Our Appiversary is here! Find new & exclusive deals each week" with the actual 15% off headline — lead with the offer, not the anniversary framing.
- 2. Drop or clearly caveat the BOPIS restriction in the hero copy itself, not just in the fine print band — "15% off online pickup orders" told upfront sets correct expectations and avoids the bait-and-switch feel this brand has repeated since 8/28.
- 3. Pick one CTA. If the deal only works via code at pickup, the primary button should say "Shop with code APPBOPIS," not "Shop in app" — don't force an app download to redeem a coupon.
- 4. Tie the product grid to the promo — swap "Your Beauty, Your Way" for "Deals of the Week" featuring items actually discounted, so scrolling readers have something to click that reinforces the offer.
- 5. Consider a non-BOPIS-gated version of this send for shipping-only subscribers, so the anniversary campaign doesn't repeatedly exclude the same segment it excluded on 8/28.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% Off In-Store Pickup — Our Appiversary Starts Now 🎉`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New App-Only Deals Every Week (Starting With 15% Off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code APPBOPIS on pickup orders through Sept 3.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 2x Rewards points on every app purchase this month.`
