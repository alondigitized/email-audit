# Shop Early Black Friday Deals!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer Black Friday teaser. The black hero with a huge "50% off" and "Buy one, get one… Big Bottle Sale" is the strongest part. The eye lands on the offer first, and the Shop now button is clear. The ion shampoo grid under "Your routine deserves a restock." gives it a concrete product angle.
- It gets muddier below the hero. A second offer, "15% off buy online, pick up in-store orders", sits in a red banner labelled "Last day to save". It competes with BOGO 50% and has no deadline date. The four product tiles also have weak CTAs: tiny underlined "Shop now" links, and the ion bottles are small and washed out. The footer carries a long block of fine print, and the "Offer valid online only… buy online and pick up in-store" line sits oddly against the BOGO hero. Nothing in the email says the hero deal has a deadline.
- Compared with the 10/30 "Better beauty starts with $5 off $25" send and the 10/29 "Final days of these deals" send, this is a fresh hook rather than a repeat. It's a decent send, but the two stacked offers and the missing end date hold it back.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and high-contrast. "50% off" is unmissable and reads in a second.
- BOGO on big bottles is a concrete, understandable offer.
- The ion shampoo grid ties the offer to real products with names and sizes.
- The Klarna strip and the icon row (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery) lower purchase friction.
- The nav (Hair Color, Hair Care, Textured Hair Care, Nails) is clean and category-led.

## What's weak

- The "15% off buy online, pick up in-store" banner competes with the BOGO hero, and "Last day to save" clashes with an "Early" Black Friday framing.
- Neither offer shows an end date or a code.
- The product tiles are weak. The bottles are pale and small, with no prices, no savings math and no reviews. The "Shop now" links are plain underlined text.
- "Shop now" appears six times with no variation, so the CTAs are generic.
- The fine-print block is long, and the "online only / buy online pick up in-store" wording makes the hero offer's conditions unclear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Drop or demote the 15% BOPIS banner, or move it into a footer strip, and add a firm end date to the BOGO hero.
- 2. Show price and savings on each product tile (for example "$X each, 2 for $Y") and make the CTAs real buttons, such as "Shop color-defense shampoo".
- 3. Add social proof such as star ratings, and use larger product images on a clean background.
- 4. Shorten the legal block and put the offer's conditions (online only, BOPIS) in the hero's sub-line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off big bottles starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shampoo restock: buy 1, get 1 half off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early Black Friday: ion big bottles, buy one get one 50% off through 11/3.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on color-safe and hard-water shampoo before the real rush.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has unresolved and incomplete tracking markup: an unreplaced `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a plain-HTTP tracking pixel, and a recipient email address in image URLs. It also has several `<img>` tags with missing alt text or empty `src`. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks (unsubscribe link, footer address, UTMs) are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The third-party ad-verification pixel was never substituted, so cache-busting and impression de-duplication won't work.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is served over HTTP. Mail clients may block it or show mixed-content warnings, and the Dotomi tracking will silently fail in those cases.
- **Unprobed links:** 54 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Many third-party pixels:** The email loads Evergage, mon-trk.com, MyVisualIQ and Dotomi. Each adds a failure point and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `summary.png` ×2 (`sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com/.../campaign/SSGvV/XwBy1/1/summary.png` and `.../2/summary.png`). These appear to be dynamic content images, so missing alt leaves that content invisible with images off or to screen readers.
  - `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (`pxl.mon-trk.com/...`), `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`. These are tracking pixels and should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Empty `src`:** Two images were reported with no alt and an empty `src:`. Empty `src` can trigger extra requests or broken-image icons in some clients.
- **Not an issue:** The source includes `<!--[if !mso]>` conditionals, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, a viewport meta tag and a 499px mobile breakpoint. The `Founders Grotesk` font is declared with a Helvetica/sans-serif fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the MyVisualIQ URL (see section 2).
- **PII in URLs:** The Evergage image URLs carry `?userId=rae-l@ete...` (truncated in the QA output). A plain email address in an image query string is exposed to the image host, proxies and logs.
- **Not confirmed:** Whether the visible body has broken first-name or other merge tokens can't be determined from the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN, unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe` was not found. QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
- **WARN, unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) was not found, with the same caveat.
- **WARN, unverified:** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com`, and alignment with the From domain can't be confirmed.
- **Not confirmed:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source.
- These are relay limitations as much as sender defects. Re-test against raw headers before treating them as failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** All 54 tracking links were skipped, and no final URLs or UTM parameters appear in the source or QA data. No issues are confirmed.

1. Fix the MyVisualIQ macro so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is replaced at send time, or remove the pixel.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixel (`login.dotomi.com`) to HTTPS, or confirm the vendor supports it.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in Evergage `userId=`. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to the two Evergage `summary.png` images. Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels, and remove or fix the empty-`src` images.
5. Re-run QA on the raw message to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment).
