# ‼️ APP EARLY ACCESS: Glossier, LANEIGE, and more ‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a bundle of at least four campaigns in one 8,700-pixel scroll, and the subject only covers one of them. The hero delivers on the subject: "App-Only Deals Start Now," "24 hours – app early access," and a Glossier mini wardrobe set. But the very top is a 2X/5X points banner (code SAYPOINTS, ends 10/7), the same loyalty promo as the 10/3 and 10/1 sends. Below the product grid sit three more offers: 10% off $100 with SAVETEN (Oct 5–6), a Monday teaser for "Spend More, Get More" with MORE4U, and a Hispanic Heritage Month block. Three promo codes appear in one email.
- The product grid is the best part: REFY, LANEIGE, Marc Jacobs, Westman Atelier, Violette_FR and Sephora Favorites kits. Each tile has a "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" or "LIMITED EDITION" tag and a real price. A beauty shopper who likes department-store prestige would find that appealing. Under the hero, though, there is no single obvious next step.
- The send is solid but cluttered. Sephora sent a Daily Deals or loyalty email almost every day from 10/1 to 10/5, so the cadence is heavy. The hero is the only part that is clearly "early access." Everything else is the usual Sephora promo load.

## What's working

- The hero ties the subject to the email: "App-Only Deals Start Now" and "HAPPENING NOW! 24 hours – app early access" sit right above the Glossier set.
- The product tiles are specific. Each has a brand, a product name and a price (for example, "Mini Glossier You Wardrobe Set, $80 value"). The "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags add exclusivity.
- The SAVETEN block is a clean, bold offer: "10% OFF, Oct 5–6, Get 10% off $100+," with a "SHOP THE APP" button.
- The email is seasonal and current. It has a dated "Mark Your Calendar" teaser for Oct 7 and a Hispanic Heritage Month block.
- The Beauty Insider tier strip (Insider / VIB / Rouge) and the 2X/5X points banner keep the loyalty message visible.

## What's weak

- The top banner and the hero fight for attention. "Earn Points Faster on Top Brands" (2X/5X) sits above "App-Only Deals Start Now" and pushes the early-access hero down. The 10/3 "Hot drops" send had the same stacking problem.
- There are too many offers: SAYPOINTS, SAVETEN, MORE4U, early access, Hispanic Heritage Month, and Korean hair care and Huda Beauty fragrance blocks. The email gives no ranking among them.
- The "SHOP HOLIDAY" button under the hero doesn't match "App-Only Deals" or Glossier. The hero promises app deals, but the button sounds like a different campaign.
- The footer is very long, with dense fine print, a barcode and "For use in Sephora stores" text. It buries the app-install nudge and makes the email feel heavier than it is.
- The 10% off $100 spend threshold, the "app only" gating and the fine print take away from the "early access" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one story: early access. Make the Glossier hero and the product grid the whole body, with one offer (SAVETEN) as the only code. Move SAYPOINTS and the Oct 7 MORE4U teaser to their own sends. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Put the early-access hero above the points banner, or drop the banner on this send.
- 3. Rename the hero button to match the content, such as "Shop app-only drops" or "Get early access." Give each product tile a visible "Shop" link.
- 4. Add a real countdown or "ends at midnight" line to the 24-hour early-access window, so the urgency is specific.
- 5. Add a clear "Open in the app" button near the top. The email is app-only, but the app prompt sits near the bottom.
- 6. Cut the Hispanic Heritage Month block and the Korean hair care and Huda Beauty modules from this send. They dilute the app message.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML namespaces and Apple/Gmail data-detector resets. The QA run shows a 0-character plain-text part, two images without alt text, and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. The List-Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be checked because the relay doesn't capture them. The HTML source is truncated, so body-level checks were limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed, 52 were skipped by click-likelihood ranking, and the unsubscribe link was included in the probe set.
- The first image uses an opaque tracking path: `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYxMDAwMDAxYS0xMGMyLTkxNDUt…`. That is a tracking-redirect or open-pixel pattern, not a content image, so it is a link with no alt text. See section 3.
- Images are served from `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/…`, which is a third-party ESP CDN. Nothing indicates a problem, but it is a deliverability dependency.
- The 52 skipped links, mostly footer and social, were not validated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 2 images):**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`
  - `…/2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`
  - The second is a nav header image, so its link labels are invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom in supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">`. The valid token is `noindex`, so this is a no-op. It is low impact for email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` does not describe the message. Minor.
- **Text-size-adjust:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` is applied globally to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This can prevent user text resizing on iOS.
- Outlook handling is present (`mso-table-lspace`, `mso-line-height-rule`, `#outlook a`, VML namespaces). No rendering defect was confirmed from the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so tokens later in the body are unverified.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This is a deliverability and accessibility problem, since spam filters weigh multipart/alternative and some clients and assistive tools use the text part.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. It is not evidence that Sephora omits them.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. Nothing is confirmed either way.
- The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source. The QA run says an unsubscribe link was probed, but the result isn't detailed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The QA output reports no UTM parameters or landing-page results, and the truncated source doesn't show the CTA hrefs. The campaign tracking appears to go through opaque `app.sephora.com/O/…` redirect tokens rather than visible UTM query strings. Final-destination alignment would need the redirect chain resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` part.** This is the only hard QA FAIL.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to the tracking/nav image and to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, or `alt=""` for purely decorative images.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Fix the robots meta value** to `noindex` or drop it, and set a descriptive `<title>`.
5. **Re-verify headers on the raw message**: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. The relay capture is likely the cause, so check the original MIME before concluding anything.
6. **Capture the full HTML and resolve the redirects** for the 52 skipped links. This would allow checks of unsubscribe and address presence, token leakage and UTM continuity.
