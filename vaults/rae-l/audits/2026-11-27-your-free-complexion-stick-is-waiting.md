# Your free Complexion Stick is waiting 
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-product Black Friday send for the Skin Rewind Complexion Stick, with the free-with-any-order gift as the hook. The structure is strong: a "The Lineup" hero of stick tubes with a "free w/ any order" sticker, a clear headline, a look-and-feel spec list, ingredients, two reviews, and a shade-finder button. It's a full product-page story in one email. The offer is stated clearly in the banner ("Unlock 25% off sitewide* | Join VIP for free") and in the body ("Get it free with any order—plus, save 20% sitewide*").
- The weakness is mixed offer math. The banner says 25%, the body says 20%, the "extra, extra" block says up to 40% off select items, and VIP perks sit at the bottom. That's three discount numbers plus a free gift. The email is also long, with six or seven CTAs. The subject is concrete and gift-led, though, and it follows the "new gifts added" and "double the gifts" beats from 11/24 and 11/26. Overall this is a solid, well-built send that is let down by offer clutter.

## What's working

- The free gift is in the subject, on the hero sticker and in the first body paragraph, so the hook is consistent.
- The "SHOP + CLAIM IN CART" button is a clear, high-contrast primary CTA directly under the pitch.
- Sections for look and feel, ingredients and reviews answer the real objections about shade, coverage and finish.
- The "$50 value" badge anchors the gift's worth.
- The two five-star reviews with a swatch image add credible social proof and a visual cue about texture.
- The footer trust row (shipping, returns, shade match) and the VIP block add reassurance.

## What's weak

- The banner says 25% sitewide while the body says 20% sitewide. A skeptical reader will wonder which is right, and the asterisks hide the conditions.
- The "up to 40% off select items" block pulls attention away from the gift and adds a second, vague offer.
- The email has too many CTAs: claim, find your shade, sale, and VIP. The "Find your shade" and "Sale's on sale" buttons dilute the main action.
- The hero shows a row of tubes with the headline "The Lineup" and tiny text. Neither says "free complexion stick", so the gift offer relies on a small pink sticker.
- The VIP perks panel uses small, low-contrast text on dark maroon that is hard to read.
- The "Shop + Claim in Cart" CTA is on-brand but doesn't say how the gift is claimed (a code, or added automatically), which creates friction.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer math: use one sitewide percentage in the banner, hero and body. Then lead with a single line, "Free Complexion Stick ($50 value) with any order."
- 2. Rewrite the hero so the headline carries the gift, for example "Your free Complexion Stick", with a product image instead of a tube lineup.
- 3. Cut the "up to 40% off" block, or move it into a small text link, so the gift is the one story.
- 4. State how the gift is claimed: "Added automatically at checkout" or a code.
- 5. Drop the "Find your shade" button or turn it into a text link, and keep the one primary CTA repeated.
- 6. Make the VIP copy larger and higher in contrast.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Complexion Stick ($50 value) with any order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free gift is still in the cart`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% off sitewide. 42 shades, buildable coverage, 12-hour wear.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Add it at checkout. Today only, while gifts last.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 1 of 12 material links and skipped 11. Destination status, redirect chains and tracking-redirect behavior for the other 11 are unverified. This is a coverage limit, not a defect.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M8DBQ658KMWDCGSF1JBA7VFF` is the open-tracking pixel. It sits on a first-party subdomain of the sender domain, which is good for alignment and for avoiding blocklists. It has no alt text (see section 3).
- No broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. It is a content image on the Klaviyo CDN, so it needs descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if it is decorative. With images blocked, this slot renders blank.
  - `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M8DBQ658KMWDCGSF1JBA7VFF`. This is the tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. Most clients ignore it, but it affects "view in browser" and screen-reader context.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`, and there is no `role="presentation"` visible on layout tables in the truncated source. Both are accessibility gaps that screen readers use.
- **Font `@import`:** The `<style>` block uses `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat...)` and `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/...)`. Gmail and several other clients strip or ignore `@import`. The declared stack (`"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif`) falls back safely, so this is low severity. The headings declare `Arial` only.
- **Preheader:** The hidden preheader div uses `display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0` with `͏` padding characters. This is the standard pattern and has no issues.
- **Viewport and Outlook:** The viewport meta tag, `IE=edge` and the `mso` conditional blocks are present. No issues found.

Verify it in the raw message at the original receiving mailbox. Klaviyo normally adds both. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so if they are truly absent this becomes a real deliverability issue.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is not present. The QA note attributes this to the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, not failing. No finding can be made from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but the link itself isn't shown here, so I can't confirm it or the postal address.
