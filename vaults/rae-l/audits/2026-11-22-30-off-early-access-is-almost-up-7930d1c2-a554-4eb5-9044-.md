# 30% off early access is almost up ⏱️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the last-call version of the 11/18 Black Friday opener ("Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members"). The hero is still the strongest part. A red block with a "LAST CHANCE!" bar, "Member Exclusive," and a huge white-banded "30% OFF SITEWIDE" puts the offer and the deadline in the first screen. The "thru 11/22" line sits in small print under the button, so the urgency is credible but easy to miss.
- Below the hero it turns into a catalog. There are four category tiles (Slip-ins, Cozy Fit, Court & Classics, Performance), a $49.99 Doorbusters block, size grids, a nav list, and heavy legal copy. All of it is clean and well organized. But after four or five Black Friday sends in five days, nothing here is new except the "almost up" framing.
- Verdict: a solid, honest deadline reminder that works best for someone who hasn't bought yet. It's a repeat for anyone who has been following the thread. The "$49.99" doorbusters block is a good second hook, but it's buried below the fold.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable. "30% OFF SITEWIDE" in giant type on a white band is the first thing the eye lands on.
- "LAST CHANCE!" in a black bar above the hero gives the deadline weight and matches the subject's urgency.
- The "SKECHERS Plus Member Exclusive" label makes the member benefit clear.
- The four category tiles use big lifestyle photos with overlaid words and specific CTAs ("SHOP SLIP-INS," "SHOP COZY FIT"). They give clear next steps beyond the generic hero button.
- The Doorbusters block with "STARTING AT $49.99" gives a concrete second price anchor.
- The size grids for women and men are a useful shortcut. They let a shopper act on fit straight away.

## What's weak

- The deadline "Valid online only thru 11/22" is tiny gray-on-red text under the button. The "LAST CHANCE!" bar doesn't say when it ends.
- The top bar says "Join Skechers Plus and Earn 1000 Bonus Points" with a JOIN NOW button. That is wrong for a member-exclusive send aimed at people who are already members.
- It looks almost identical to the 11/18 opener. This is the same creative with new urgency copy, and the cadence is heavy.
- The offer isn't quite as simple as it looks. "Not stackable with other offers" and "Exclusions apply" are visible, and two dense legal paragraphs sit at the bottom.
- The hero "SHOP NOW" button is small compared with the headline above it. The category tiles and the Doorbusters block compete for the click.
- The email is very long, with sizes, nav, Klarna/Afterpay, the app download and social links after the real offer. The bottom half has no urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the deadline into the hero headline area, for example "ENDS TONIGHT" or "ENDS 11/22 AT MIDNIGHT," in large type instead of fine print.
- 2. Remove the "Join Skechers Plus" bar for members. Replace it with a status line such as "You're in: Plus member pricing applied at checkout."
- 3. Make the "SHOP NOW" button bigger and more contrasting. The headline is huge but the button is easy to skip.
- 4. Lift the $49.99 Doorbusters block above the category tiles. It's the strongest price hook after the 30%.
- 5. Don't send the same creative a fifth time. Rotate the hero (for example, feature one hero shoe at its member price) so the last-chance send feels different from 11/18.
- 6. Trim the lower half. Keep the sizes and the "Shop additional styles" list, and drop the app, social and payment-partner rows.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 30% off sitewide + $49.99 doorbusters`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your member discount applies automatically at checkout. Doorbusters from $49.99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access closes at midnight. Slip-ins, Cozy Fit and more, all 30% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with working UTM tagging. The QA run found only warnings: two non-HTTPS images without alt text, and compliance and authentication headers it could not capture. The HTML source was truncated, so findings below cover only what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 44 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of those destinations were validated.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026` is loaded over plain HTTP. Image proxies or strict clients may block it, which would break open or personalization tracking.
- **Mixed content:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is also HTTP. The other images on that host (the JSON-LD logo and promo image) use HTTPS, so this one is inconsistent.
- **Favicon domain mismatch:** `<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...">` uses HTTP and a different host (`emails.nl.`) from the rest of the email (`emails.`). It has no effect in most email clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and `dde00662-...png` have no `alt` attribute. For the `.png`, this matters if it is content rather than a spacer.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the language reliably.
- **Text resizing blocked:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops text from resizing in WebKit clients.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `Skechers&reg;` does not describe the email.
- **Non-standard JSON-LD:** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block uses `PromotionCard` and `DiscountOffer` types, and `PromotionCard` is not a standard Schema.org type. Gmail annotations may ignore or reject the markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved tokens:** None were seen in the visible HTML or subject.
- **Identifier in the pixel URL:** The pixel URL carries `mi_u=8108776175`, a 10-digit identifier tied to the recipient. I can't tell from the data whether it is a subscriber ID or something more sensitive, such as a phone number. If it is the latter, it is exposed in a plain-HTTP URL.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** The check did not find it. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain is `emails.skechers.com` (sender `no-reply@`).
- **Footer content:** The visible HTML is truncated before the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Landing URL:** `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals/?srule=top-sellers&src=2026Q4BFEARLYACCESS&utm_source=trig&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026&utm_content=annotation&utm_term=googleannotation1`.
- **UTM tagging:** All the standard UTM parameters are present, and the campaign name matches `mi_ecmp` in the pixel URL.
- **`utm_source=trig`:** This is a non-descriptive value, and `utm_content=annotation` / `utm_term=googleannotation1` mark this as the Gmail annotation link. Check whether regular body CTAs carry different values, because the visible HTML was truncated.
- **Offer expiry:** `availabilityEnds: "2026-11-22T23:11:59Z"` is an unusual timestamp, since it ends at :11:59 rather than :59:59. Check it against the real offer end time shown in the email, because Gmail may display the wrong expiry.
- **Skipped links:** The 44 tracking links were not checked, so landing-page alignment for body CTAs is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all assets over HTTPS, including `ink1000.com/.../o.gif`, `dde00662-...png` and the favicon. Use one image host.
2. Add `alt` attributes to `o.gif` (`alt=""` is fine) and to `dde00662-...png` (descriptive, or `alt=""` if decorative).
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and replace the generic `<title>` with the email's subject or topic.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message from the origin ESP. Don't rely on the AgentMail copy, and check the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results the same way.
5. Check that `mi_u` is not a phone number or other personal data, and stop exposing it over HTTP if it is.
6. Correct the JSON-LD `availabilityEnds` value and the non-standard `PromotionCard` type, and validate the markup in Google's Email Markup Tester.
7. Re-run the QA with the full, untruncated HTML and a sample of the 44 skipped tracking redirects, to check the footer and that the CTAs land correctly.
