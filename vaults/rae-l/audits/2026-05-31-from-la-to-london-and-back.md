# From LA to London and back
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-story newsletter, not a promotional send — and that's by design. After 18+ Friends + Family emails hammering 20% off and the May 29 teaser ("Coming 06.02"), ILIA is now in cooldown mode: world tour recap, two Allure 2026 Readers' Choice Award callouts, and a second nudge toward the June 2 launch. The format is plain text with a collage strip at the bottom — deliberately personal, no discount pressure.
- The problem is the subject. "From LA to London and back" reads like a travel influencer's vlog title, not a beauty email. A subscriber scanning their inbox after a week of ILIA promo fatigue has no clear signal that this contains anything worth opening. The brand has earned some goodwill by stopping the sale cadence, but this subject squanders it.
- The email itself is solid once you're inside: warm voice, real news (Allure wins are concrete social proof), and a clean tease of the June 2 launch. But the lack of any CTA button means every click depends on a subscriber finding and tapping a hyperlinked product name in plain text — a structural miss for a brand that clearly has more to say about this launch.

## What's working

- The Allure 2026 Readers' Choice Award callout (Multi-Stick for Best Clean Blush, Limitless Lash for Best Clean Mascara) is concrete social proof that rewards the subscriber for opening.
- Cadence reset is earned — after the F+F blitz, a no-offer brand story actually feels generous.
- World tour recap with specific city names (LA → NYC → Mexico City → Vancouver → London) makes the community narrative tangible rather than vague.
- The June 2 soft-launch tease lands better here because it's embedded in real news rather than standing alone as a teaser-only send.
- The collage strip at the bottom gives the email visual warmth without overselling anything.

## What's weak

- Subject "From LA to London and back" signals travel content, not beauty — low relevance cue for the inbox scan.
- No CTA button anywhere. Every product link is buried inline in plain text; the only "action" signal is "our newest launch" at the end of a paragraph.
- "Hi there" opener signals no segmentation — ILIA has purchase data, city data from this very world tour, and award-winning products — none of that personalization is used.
- The collage feels decorative rather than functional — none of the images are clickable or labeled in any visible way.
- The June 2 tease is now the second send repeating the same message ("putting the finishing touches on our newest launch") without adding any new detail.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to signal beauty + news.** "From LA to London and back" is the biggest open-rate leak here. The Allure wins and the June 2 launch are both stronger hooks.
- 2. **Add a single CTA button** pointing to the June 2 launch landing page or a "what we've been up to" editorial. Plain text is fine for brand voice; a button is still necessary for measurable clicks.
- 3. **Lead with the award win in the email body** — bury the travel recap, surface the credibility. "We won two Allure 2026 Readers' Choice Awards" is the most objectively compelling sentence in this email and it's the second bullet.
- 4. **Use first-name merge or at minimum city/region personalization** — this email was literally about city-specific events; "Hi there" wastes the setup.
- 5. **Give the June 2 tease one new detail** (texture, category, a color name) rather than recycling "something soft."

## Technical Audit — ILIA "From LA to London and back"

---

### 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-delivered email using `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` redirect tracking and a Klaviyo-hosted custom font via `@import`. The truncated source cuts off before the footer, so compliance elements (unsubscribe, address) cannot be fully confirmed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Inconsistent `text-decoration` on anchors** — Several links carry inline `style="text-decoration: underline;"` (e.g., `_2`, `_3`, `_5`, `_6`, `_7`, `_8`) while others do not (`_0`, `_1`, `_4`, `_9`). This is a rendering inconsistency, not a stylesheet rule — likely a copy/paste artifact from the template editor.

**`rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"` on all links** — `nofollow` is semantically meaningless in email (crawlers don't follow email links), but it also means inbox preview renderers that do fetch links won't pass link equity. Not harmful, but it's noise and can interfere with some link-prefetch behaviors in iOS Mail.

**UTM parameters not confirmed** — All hrefs resolve through `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/...` redirects. UTM injection at redirect time cannot be verified from HTML source alone. If Klaviyo's UTM append setting is off or misconfigured, clicks will land untagged.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Zero-width space spacers** — `<div><span>​</span></div>` (U+200B, decimal `8203`) is used as a vertical spacer between paragraphs. Screen readers may announce these as blank elements or pause unexpectedly. Replace with `margin-bottom` on the preceding element or a proper `<div style="height: 12px;">` spacer.

**No `lang` attribute on `<html>`** — Missing from the visible source. Required for screen reader language detection and WCAG 2.1 SC 3.1.1.

**No `<meta charset>` visible in `<head>`** — The truncated `<head>` shows only the `@import` style block. If `<meta charset="UTF-8">` is absent, non-ASCII characters (e.g., `—`, `​`, `é`) are at risk of garbling in some clients.

**Custom font via `@import`** — Wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <![endif]-->` MSO conditional, which is correct Outlook handling. Fallback stack `Karla, Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif` is well-formed.

**No `alt` attributes confirmed** — HTML is truncated before image tags; cannot audit. Flag for QA verification.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Generic salutation "Hi there,"** — No Klaviyo personalization token present (expected: `{{ first_name|default:"there" }}`). This is either intentional for a newsletter format or a dropped merge tag. Either way, the profile database has first names — leaving value on the table or masking a template error.

No broken/unfired merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%7B%7B`) visible in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Auth Headers)

**Cannot confirm from truncated source** — The HTML cuts off mid-sentence (`<span style="…`). The CAN-SPAM-required elements — physical mailing address, one-click unsubscribe link — are typically in the footer and are not visible. Manual verification required.

**Authentication headers** — Not derivable from HTML source. DKIM/DMARC pass/fail must be checked against raw message headers. Klaviyo-sent mail from `iliabeauty.com` should be signing with ILIA's DKIM key; verify `d=iliabeauty.com` in the `DKIM-Signature` header.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

**UTM parameters unconfirmed** — As noted in §2, all destination URLs are opaque redirects. Klaviyo can auto-append UTMs, but this must be verified in the campaign settings (`utm_source=klaviyo`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=...`). If missing, GA4/analytics will attribute these clicks as direct traffic.

**Link count: 10 tracked links** (`_0` through `_9`) across product pages and event recap content. Destination alignment cannot be verified without resolving redirects, but the link anchor text maps logically to expected destinations (product PDPs and editorial content).
