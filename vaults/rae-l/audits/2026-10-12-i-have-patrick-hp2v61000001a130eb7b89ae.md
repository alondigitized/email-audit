## What's working

- The "Experts Know Best" headline is big, and the single-column tip layout is easy to scan.
- Each tip pairs a named expert with a face, a signature, and a concrete technique (blending upward, "bouche mordue" lips, color-correcting with peach).
- The photography is polished and the color blocking changes with each tip, which keeps the long scroll interesting.
- The Breast Cancer Awareness section, with the $25 donation to Tides Foundation, feels authentic and gives the email a purpose.

## What's weak

- No visible offer, discount, or points hook anywhere. That is a sharp step down from the 2X/5X points sends on 10/6 and 10/7.
- No product names, prices, or "shop this" buttons appear beside the tips, so interest has nowhere to go.
- The subject promises "Patrick", but he is tip #4 and the hero never mentions him. The phone emojis also don't connect to anything in the body.
- The email is very long, with six or seven blocks, and the CTAs are tiny and buried at the bottom.
- The Breast Cancer Awareness block adds a second topic after the beauty tips, and its fine-print paragraph is dense.

- 2. Put Patrick in the hero, or lead with his tip, so the subject pays off immediately.
- 3. Add one concrete Insider offer or points boost, such as a points multiplier on the featured brands, to give the content a reason to click now.
- 4. Cut the scroll to three tips, or move the Breast Cancer Awareness block to its own send, with a clear donate or shop CTA.
