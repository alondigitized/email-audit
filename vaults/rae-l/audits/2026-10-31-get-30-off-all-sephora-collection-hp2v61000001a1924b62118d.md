- The code ALLONSALE is visible next to the offer, so the next step is clear.
- The subject names the offer ("30% off ALL Sephora Collection"), which matches the badge.

## What's weak

- The hero ("Beauty for You") features two non-Sephora Collection moisturizers, so the offer and the hero don't match.
- The 30% offer is not repeated below the top banner. Six unrelated brand products, Valentino, K18 and a gift guide dilute it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Epsilon/Harmony redirect-tracked links and two tracking pixels. The one automated FAIL is a missing plain-text alternative (0 chars). Several other items couldn't be verified because the relay dropped headers and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect tracking:** CTAs go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1924b62118d34146e96c569c8/...?__dU__=...`. The final destination and any UTM params can't be seen in the truncated source.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 links were probed and 38 were skipped (QA INFO). The source and QA data give no per-link status codes, so I can't confirm any broken link.
- **Two open pixels:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=20261031_CS_Curated_For_You_BI` comes from a second tracking system.
- **Campaign ID:** the `mi_ecmp` value reads `Curated_For_You_BI`, but the subject is a sitewide "30% off Sephora Collection" promo. This may be a reused template ID. Worth checking, but I can't confirm it from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is empty (0 chars).** Without it, some spam filters score the message worse and text-only clients show nothing.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** both are tracking pixels, the `app.sephora.com/O/...` image and `mi.sephora.com/.../o.gif`. Impact is low because they are hidden, but add `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. On narrow viewports this can force horizontal scroll in clients that ignore or strip the `@media (max-width:480px)` rules.
- **Malformed CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` conditional is nested inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Clients will probably ignore the MSO rule.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **Empty structured data:** the JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard`. Empty values may be ignored by Gmail or produce validation noise.
- **Missing attributes:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute, and `<title>` is only "Sephora".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`). The `__dU__=` values are encoded per-recipient tokens, as expected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header** and **no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this may be an artifact rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`.
- **Footer not verified:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the source. Attribution appears to rely on the `app.sephora.com/T/` redirector and `mi_ecmp`.
- **Landing page alignment:** it can't be checked without the resolved destinations. Whether the offer matches the landing page is unverified.

2. **High:** re-run the QA against raw `.eml` headers (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
3. **Medium:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and replace the fixed `min-width:700px` with `max-width:700px; width:100%`.
