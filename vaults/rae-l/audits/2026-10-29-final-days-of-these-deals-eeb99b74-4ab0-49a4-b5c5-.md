# Final days of these deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a "last call" deals roundup. It has the same bones as the 10/26 send, "Deals are disappearing soon...", which ran 3 days ago. The hero is a glossy burgundy nail photo with "The last call on this month's best," a deadline (October 31), and a black "Shop now" button. The photo is the most striking thing in the email, but it sells no offer.
- The actual deals sit below the fold, in a three-tile grid: 25% off As I Am, Schwarzkopf, göt2b and EBIN hair care, 20% off men's grooming, and BOGO 50% off lashes. Each tile has its own "Shop now" link. Three red promo bars above the hero (rewards signup, 2-hour delivery, 15% off BOPIS) compete with the hero before it has a chance to land.
- My judgement: tidy and on-brand, but it's a repeat of a message this subscriber just got. The subject is vague and the hero image doesn't match the offers. Nothing here is broken, but nothing here makes you act today.

## What's working

- The three offer tiles are clean and scannable, with a bold red percent-off figure and a product photo for each.
- The hero states a clear deadline ("These deals wrap October 31"), and the "Buy online, pick up in-store today" line adds a practical hook.
- The Sally Rewards bar and the Klarna block give a price-sensitive reader several ways to save or spread out the cost.
- The icon row (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, BOPIS) is a useful reassurance block, and the layout shows no broken images or overlap.
- The men's grooming tile reuses the 10/20 "A gift for the guys" offer, which helps if grooming is your lane.

## What's weak

- The hero image (a nail close-up) has no connection to any of the three offers below it. Nails aren't even among the offers.
- No percent-off figure appears in the hero, so the first screen asks for a click without saying what's on sale.
- Three stacked promo bars above the hero split attention. The rewards bar is also the same $5 off $25 pitch that went out four times in three weeks.
- The cadence feels repetitive. "Final days" follows "Deals are disappearing soon..." (10/26) closely, and the urgency language has lost credibility.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop now." The tiles use vague labels ("hair care," "lashes") rather than specific products, and the fine print is dense, about a screen's worth.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the strongest offer in the hero. Swap the nail photo for a hair-care shot with "25% off" in the headline, or lead with the BOGO lash deal, and make the CTA say what it opens.
- 2. Cut the three top bars down to one. Move the rewards signup lower, or drop it for existing members.
- 3. Give each tile a specific CTA, for example "Shop men's grooming" or "Shop lash BOGO," and name one product with a price.
- 4. Name the deadline in the subject ("Ends Oct 31") instead of "final days," and space this send further from the last deals email.
- 5. Add a social-proof line, such as a star rating on one hero product.
