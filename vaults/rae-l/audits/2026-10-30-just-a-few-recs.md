# Just a few recs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent holiday gift-guide email with one real offer: a free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with a $75+ order. The offer shows up three times (top banner, the "Mini Mascara Gift" badge, and the "a free lift" block), so nobody misses it. The pale blue hero with the pencils and a white flower is on-brand, and the "Our Gift Recs" headline matches the subject.
- The body is a long, three-section scroll: gift sets, "More Holiday Hits," then the mascara promo. Three "shop" buttons plus a fourth in the promo block ("Shop Now") split attention across sets, bestsellers and the gift-with-purchase. The email never commits to one hero product. It reads as a catalog with a promo attached, and it is the 5th beauty-education or catalog send in 10 days (compare "Freshly picked California color" on 10/28 and "How to find your shade" on 10/27). The gift-with-purchase is the first real incentive in that run, and it sits at the very top and very bottom instead of leading.
- Judgment: a solid 6. It is well-built and honest, but the offer is a free gift behind a $75 threshold, and the layout spreads the shopper across too many destinations.

## What's working

- The free mini mascara offer is concrete and repeated in the banner, a red badge on the image and the closing block.
- The "No Wrapping Required" set list gives each product a short, specific description, such as "Three shimmery waterproof shadow sticks with 12-hour wear."
- The hero is clean and editorial, with a visible "Shop the Collection" button.
- The trust row at the bottom (Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, Pact, ILIA VIP, Auto-Replenish) supports the brand voice.
- The free shipping line (over $50) lowers the barrier to reaching the $75 threshold.

## What's weak

- The red banner text is tiny, and the "*" qualifier is never explained in the top fold.
- The "More Holiday Hits" grid is staggered, with a left-aligned headline beside the first product. The product images are uneven in size, so it looks a bit chaotic.
- Four shop CTAs go to different places, which dilutes the click.
- There are no prices, reviews or ratings anywhere, so there is little social proof.
- "Our Gift Recs" is generic. It does not say who the gifts are for or give a deadline for holiday ordering.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the gift-with-purchase. Put "Free mini mascara with $75+" in the hero headline, not only in a thin banner, and use one primary CTA.
- 2. Add prices to the three gift sets so shoppers can see how close each is to the $75 threshold.
- 3. Cut "More Holiday Hits" to four evenly sized tiles, or drop it, and add a star rating to each remaining product.
- 4. Add a deadline, such as "Order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery."
- 5. Replace the generic "Shop Now" with "Claim your free mascara."

- **Subject Alt B:** `3 gift sets worth wrapping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Eye, lip and lash sets, plus a free mini mascara with $75+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gift-ready sets that ship free over $50.`
