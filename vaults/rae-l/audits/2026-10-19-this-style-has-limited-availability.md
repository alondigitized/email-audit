# This style has limited availability!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the "HOW ABOUT A SECOND LOOK?" browse-abandonment shell again. Skechers sent it under this same subject on 9/30, and the 10/17 "Don't leave these styles behind!" send already carried the same members 20% off through 10/21. The subject promises scarcity, "limited availability," but the body never mentions stock. It is a generic retargeting template with a kids' light-up Skechers Garage sneaker as the hero.
- The structure is clean and the footer is thorough. The real incentive, a navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner with a deadline, sits below a hero and a six-shoe grid. The hero is a kids' novelty shoe and the grid is all women's lifestyle styles, so it doesn't read as one coherent story for an athletic shopper.
- It's the fourth browse-abandonment send in about three days (10/17, two on 10/18, now 10/19). The creative is stale and the subject doesn't match the body. The offer is fine, but the send reads as automation.

## What's working

- The "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" banner is a concrete, high-contrast offer with "Valid thru 10/21" beside it.
- The single hero with one "SHOP NOW" button is a clear primary CTA.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and it is easy to scan.
- The SKECHERS wordmark and the brand navy give instant recognition.
- The footer includes useful reassurance: Afterpay/Klarna, curbside pickup, the app, and texting opt-in.

## What's weak

- The subject promises limited availability, but nothing in the body shows low stock or urgency. This is the same mismatch as the 9/30 send.
- The hero is a kids' Skechers Garage light-up shoe, and the grid is all women's lifestyle sneakers. None of it is athletic or running, so it feels off-target.
- The 20% offer is buried below the fold and below the product grid. The eye lands on the kids' shoe first.
- The grid shows product names but no prices, ratings or sizes, and the six tiles have no visible CTA. The category list at the bottom is a generic text stack.
- The fine print is dense and tiny. "Select full-priced styles" is vague, and the exclusions are only partly visible above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% member banner above the hero, or make it the hero. Lead with the money.
- 2. Match the subject to the body. If you say "limited availability," show a real low-stock badge or a "few sizes left" line on the hero product.
- 3. Pick the hero and grid from the recipient's actual browsing category (athletic and running for this persona), not a mixed kids/women assortment.
- 4. Add price, a star rating, and a "your size" cue to the grid tiles. Make each tile a clear link.
- 5. Cap browse-abandonment cadence at one send per 3–4 days, and rotate creative so the shell isn't identical every time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still eyeing these? Members get 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your picks + 20% off through 10/21`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on select full-priced styles through 10/21. Sizes are moving.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `We saved your styles. Take 20% off before Tuesday.`
