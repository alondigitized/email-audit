# Your first look at Sally COLORfest 💗
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a different animal from anything else in Sally Beauty's recent send history — not a flash sale, not a BOGO, not a single-brand spotlight, but a full campaign-launch teaser for "Sally COLORfest," a hair-and-nail event dropping July 31. The confetti treatment, the bold "COLORfest" wordmark, and the vivid teal-haired model give it more production value than the 7/22 L'Oreal spotlight or the 7/27 curl-care send — this reads like Sally's version of a seasonal tentpole moment, and it's a smart change-up after a month of flash-sale/BOGO repetition.
- The execution is clean: no render bugs, a legible top-to-bottom hierarchy (promo bar → 15% BOPIS ribbon → hero → bestseller grid → Klarna → footer), and the bestseller grid gives skimmers something to click even if they don't care about the "coming soon" campaign itself. The weak spot is that the actual event hasn't launched yet — the primary CTA ("See what's new") sends people to browse a collection that technically isn't live until July 31, and the only concrete, actionable offer in the email (15% off BOPIS) is visually subordinate to the COLORfest branding it's meant to support.
- Verdict: strong creative swing, decent execution, but it's fundamentally a teaser asking for attention before the payoff exists — that's a real ceiling on click-through even though the design earns high marks.

## What's working

- The confetti "COMING SOON" ticker and COLORfest wordmark create a genuine event feel, distinct from Sally's usual promo-of-the-week format.
- The teal-hair hero image is a strong scroll-stopper and directly signals "bold hair color" to a color-motivated shopper.
- The bestseller grid gives seven concrete, differently-priced product options (toner, permanent color, nail gel, nail glue) so there's something to click even without the full COLORfest assortment live yet.
- "As seen on TikTok" badge on the Beauty Secrets nail glue is a nice, specific trust signal that past sends in this brand history haven't leaned on.
- 15% off BOPIS + free 2-hour delivery banners at the top give an immediate, usable offer for anyone not interested in waiting for July 31.

## What's weak

- The primary CTA ("See what's new") points to a campaign that isn't live yet — there's no way to act on the hero right now, which caps click-through versus the 7/25 stacked-sale email that had three immediately shoppable mechanics.
- No loyalty/rewards benefit is surfaced anywhere in the visible body, despite Sally having a rewards program — a missed callback to the 7/29 "Get rewarded every time you shop" send just one day prior.
- "Shop now" is repeated seven times across the bestseller grid with zero variation — generic CTA copy that doesn't differentiate toner from nail glue.
- The 15% BOPIS offer, the one truly actionable deal in this email, is visually the smallest element on the page — easy to skim past under the bolder COLORfest branding.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA do double duty: swap "See what's new" for something that either drives to the shoppable bestseller section immediately ("Shop the bestsellers") or gives a reason to come back July 31 ("Set a reminder for July 31") — right now it promises a click into nothing new.
- 2. Pull one loyalty/rewards line into the hero or sub-hero band, echoing the 7/29 send, so members don't feel like the program vanished between emails.
- 3. Vary the "Shop now" CTAs per product category (e.g., "Shop hair color," "Shop nail care") to reinforce category relevance and break up the repetition.
- 4. Give the 15% BOPIS ribbon more visual weight (bigger type or a color block) since it's the only redeemable-today offer in the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `COLORfest is coming: 100s of new shades, July 31`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Get a first look before COLORfest drops 💗`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 15% off Buy Online, Pick Up In Store — today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New hair color, nail care & more — all in one celebration`

# Technical Audit: Sally Beauty — "Your first look at Sally COLORfest 💗"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard multipart HTML template (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, MSO/Outlook conditional comments, mobile media queries) served via a third-party ESP relay (em.sallybeauty.com). Automated QA returned a 57% pass rate with 0 hard failures and 3 warning categories — primarily missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and unlabeled tracking pixels — with no rendering-breaking issues identified in the available source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 52 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (redirect-domain links are not resolved by the QA tool), so **destination validity of these links is unconfirmed** — recommend manual click-through QA before send.
- Three third-party tracking pixels are embedded: `pxl.mon-trk.com` (campaign tracking), `t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel` (IQM/Visual IQ media attribution), and `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` (Merkle/Dotomi conversion sync).
- **The Dotomi pixel (`login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`) is loaded over plain `http://`, not `https://`.** This is a mixed-content risk — many mail clients (Gmail, Apple Mail with strict transport settings) block or strip non-HTTPS image requests, which would cause this pixel to silently fail to fire.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- All 3 tracking pixels flagged above are missing `alt` attributes. Impact is low (they are 1×1 invisible pixels, not content images), but flagging for completeness/spec conformance.
- No accessibility issues found on content images in the available source (truncation limits full coverage — see note below).
- Web fonts (`Open Sans` via Google Fonts `@font-face`) are declared with appropriate fallbacks (`'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif` on the base `a{}` selector), so clients that block remote font loading (Outlook desktop, some Gmail contexts) will degrade gracefully. No issues found here.
- MSO conditional comments and standard mobile breakpoint (`max-width: 499px`) are present, consistent with typical Outlook/mobile-safe patterns. No structural rendering issues identified.

**Note:** The HTML source provided is truncated at the `<style>` block before the message body, so link markup, image inventory, and merge-tag usage in the body could not be fully audited. Findings above are limited to what QA data and the visible `<head>`/CSS confirm.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens, AMPscript, Handlebars, or personalization placeholders are visible in the available source. Cannot confirm presence/absence of personalization in the message body due to HTML truncation — recommend re-running this audit against the full source if personalization logic needs verification.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** Required for CAN-SPAM/bulk-sender compliance and Gmail/Yahoo 2024 sender requirements. QA notes this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail may not be capturing/forwarding the header rather than it being genuinely absent) — verify against raw SMTP headers from the actual sending ESP.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058).** Without this, one-click unsubscribe is not supported, which is now a de facto requirement for bulk senders at Gmail/Yahoo.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found**, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture. Same relay caveat applies — flag for verification against original headers rather than treating as a confirmed auth failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable — the 52 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated prober and no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters were captured in the QA data or visible HTML. Recommend a manual pass: click through primary CTAs and confirm `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` values land on matching, live product/landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the Dotomi pixel (`login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`) to `https://` or confirm with the vendor that an HTTPS endpoint exists — current `http://` load will silently fail in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
2. Add `alt=""` (empty, decorative) to the three tracking pixel `<img>` tags for spec conformance.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers exist on the raw send — confirm whether their absence here is a relay-capture artifact or a genuine gap, since one-click unsubscribe is required by major mailbox providers.
4. Verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) directly from the ESP's outbound headers rather than the relay capture used for this QA pass.
5. Manually validate the 52 skipped tracking links (destination + UTM parameters) since automated probing did not cover them.
6. Re-audit with the full (untruncated) HTML body to confirm link inventory, merge-token usage, and any content-image accessibility gaps not visible in this capture.
