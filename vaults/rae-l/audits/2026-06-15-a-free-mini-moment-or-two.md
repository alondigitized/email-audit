# A free mini moment (or two)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- ILIA pivots out of the HAIM arc with a gift-with-purchase mechanic: a free Mini Lip Sketch Duo (lip liner + lipstick + sharpener) with a first $50+ order. The email leans on that hook to introduce four hero products — the Skin Tint + Blur Duo at 25% off, The Base Face Milk, the Eye Stylus Trio, and then the GWP claim section at the bottom. The "free" angle in the subject is the strongest signal here, and the visual execution is clean as expected from ILIA.
- The structural problem is the word "first." "BUILD YOUR FIRST ORDER" and "free with your first $50+ order" reads like a new-subscriber acquisition email that has landed in a recurring subscriber's inbox. Anyone who has already bought from ILIA would reasonably assume this offer doesn't apply to them — and might not click at all. Yesterday's "Last chance for free mini Face Milk" scored well precisely because the offer was framed inclusively; this one introduces exclusionary language that undercuts the perceived value.
- The email is also doing too much. Four distinct product pitches plus a GWP mechanic across a long scroll dilutes focus. The June 14 send was a tight, single-offer close — this one expands back out to a catalog feel without a clear narrative throughline.

## What's working

- "A free mini moment (or two)" is short (30 chars), mobile-friendly, and "free" is the right hook word.
- The GWP mechanic bookends effectively — promised at the top, claimed at the bottom with its own dedicated section and "SHOP TO CLAIM" CTA.
- The "25% OFF THE DUO + FREE MINIS" banner on the Skin Tint + Blur section stacks two incentives visibly without burying either.
- "CUSTOMIZE + SAVE 25%" on the Eye Stylus Trio is a more active CTA than "SHOP NOW" and implies personalization — a small but smart differentiator.
- Render is clean: no broken images, no text collision, layout holds.

## What's weak

- "BUILD YOUR FIRST ORDER" and "free with your first $50+ order" are new-subscriber language dropped into an existing subscriber's inbox — creates immediate confusion and likely opt-out of clicking.
- This is the second consecutive "free mini" GWP offer (June 14: Face Milk, June 15: Lip Sketch Duo). Back-to-back "free" sends cheapens both and signals deal fatigue, not abundance.
- Four product sections plus the GWP close is too long for a single narrative — the email has no clear hero product beyond the GWP, which is pushed to the bottom.
- No deadline visible anywhere — the June 14 "last chance" urgency is gone and nothing fills that role here.
- The Base Face Milk reappears here one day after being the featured product in the June 14 close, with no new angle or framing — it reads as filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the "first order" language immediately.** If this is targeted at non-purchasers, segment it to that list only. If it's intended for all subscribers, reframe: "free with any $50+ order" or "on the house with your next order." This single change removes the biggest friction point.
- 2. **Cut two product sections.** Run the Skin Tint + Blur Duo (highest-value offer with 25% off) and the GWP Lip Sketch Duo as the two narrative pillars. Drop the Base Face Milk (just featured June 14) and fold the Eye Stylus Trio into a secondary module if needed.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** Even a soft one ("while supplies last" is weak — a real date is stronger). "Free through Sunday" would restore the urgency ILIA just trained subscribers to respond to.
- 4. **Lead the preview with the specific product.** "Mini Lip Sketch Duo, on us" sets up the subject perfectly.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free: the Mini Lip Sketch Duo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Two free minis, one order — here's how`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dual-ended lip sketch + liner, yours with any $50+ order through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get the Mini Lip Sketch Duo free — plus 25% off the Skin Tint duo.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **Google Fonts loaded via `@import`** — `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap)` is blocked by Gmail (webmail), Outlook (all versions), and Samsung Mail. The fallback stack `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` is present and will activate correctly, but the email will not render Montserrat for the majority of recipients.

- **Klaviyo custom font import** — `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css)` carries the same client-blocking risk.

- **All anchor pseudo-states identical** — The following CSS removes all hover and visited-state affordance:
  ```css
  a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover { color:#5f4b3c; text-decoration:none }
  ```
  Users who rely on color shift or underline to identify interactive elements get no visual feedback. This also flattens `:visited` state, which some assistive tools use.

- **Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title> </title>` contains only a space. Some clients (notably certain versions of Apple Mail) surface this as a tooltip or tab label. Should be set to the email subject or a descriptive string.
