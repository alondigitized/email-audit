# Last Chance: BOGO 50% Off Hair Care ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- After five straight "up to 40% off" percentage-discount emails, Sally Beauty finally broke the mold: "BOGO 50% Off" is a mechanically distinct offer that reads as genuinely new value rather than another volume-discount extension. The subject is the best in this run — concrete, compact, action-ready. The hero delivers too: a large model shot, clean "Buy one, get one 50% off / Select hair care. Ends June 30." copy, and a "LAST CALL" urgency banner stacked on top of a free delivery strip. From the fold up, this is a tight, well-structured email.
- The body loses its nerve below the hero. The "Don't Miss What's Trending" section immediately introduces a nail product (Beauty Secrets Fold & Dry Proof Nail Seal) inside what was pitched as a hair care BOGO. There's no unambiguous CTA button anchored to the hero — just a constellation of "Shop now" links scattered across product tiles. The Pride Month callout near the footer reads as an afterthought rather than an integrated brand moment.
- Net judgement: better than anything sent in the June 23–27 run, but the email is fighting its own category dilution from the waist down. The subject promises a tight hair care BOGO; the body delivers a general promotional roundup. Fix the body and this is a genuinely strong send.

## What's working

- BOGO mechanic is a meaningful departure from the endless "up to 40% off" percentage framework that dominated the June 23–27 streak — feels like a new offer, not a reheated one.
- "LAST CALL" urgency strip stacked on the free delivery banner creates two independent reasons to act before scrolling.
- Hero lifestyle photography is clean and demographically on-point — woman with vibrant, styled hair for a hair care email.
- Subject line is the tightest in this campaign run: specific mechanic, clear category, concrete deadline signal, under 40 characters.
- "Ends June 30" deadline in the hero copy gives the urgency a real anchor rather than vague scarcity language.

## What's weak

- No primary CTA button attached to the hero BOGO offer — the call-to-action dissolves into a grid of small "Shop now" links rather than one decisive button below the headline.
- "Don't Miss What's Trending" injects a nail product (Beauty Secrets Nail Seal) into a send explicitly headlined as hair care BOGO — breaks category coherence immediately below the fold.
- Pride Month panel near the bottom is disconnected from the offer; it floats as a separate story with no CTA and no tie to the BOGO mechanic.
- Multiple competing sections (trending grid, color freshness block, Pride callout, Klarna note, app download) fragment attention and dilute the single-offer clarity the hero establishes.
- Cadence fatigue is real — this is at minimum the seventh Sally Beauty email in ten days; "Last Chance" urgency is harder to believe when the subscriber has heard "last call" before (see June 26 "Going... Going... Gone!").

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a single bold CTA button directly under the hero text** — "Shop the BOGO" or "Buy One, Get One" — anchored to a landing page filtered to BOGO-eligible hair care only.
- 2. **Remove the nail product from the trending grid** — or replace with a second BOGO-eligible hair care hero (e.g., "Also try: It's a 10 Miracle Leave-In, 50% off your second bottle") to reinforce the mechanic with a specific product callout.
- 3. **Cut the trending grid to two tiles max**, both hair care, both clearly labeled as BOGO-eligible so the email reads as curation rather than a catalogue dump.
- 4. **Integrate Pride Month at the hero level or cut it** — a one-line badge on the hero ("Celebrating Pride: 10% of BOGO proceeds to [cause]") would be far more powerful than a floating panel near the footer.
- 5. **Rethink the Klarna callout placement** — it's useful context but buried; move it to a sub-line under the hero CTA button ("Buy now, pay later with Klarna") so it reduces friction at the decision moment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% Off: Buy One Hair Care, Get One Half Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy One, Get One 50% Off — Hair Care Only, Ends Tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop It's a 10, Schwarzkopf, L'Oréal and more — BOGO ends June 30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free 2-hour delivery on your BOGO order. Ends June 30.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Outdated Google Fonts CDN version**: All six `@font-face` declarations pull `opensans/v17/...` from `fonts.gstatic.com`. Current Open Sans version on Google Fonts is v40+. While many email clients block external font requests entirely, senders that do serve them get stale assets.
- **Click-tracking URLs**: Not visible in the truncated source — cannot confirm tracking parameters are appended. Verify in full source.
- **Sender subdomain**: `em.sallybeauty.com` — confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are configured for this ESP subdomain, not just the root domain.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **`white-space:pre` on `img` elements** (confirmed): `img { -ms-interpolation-mode:bicubic; white-space:pre; }` — `white-space:pre` is not a valid or meaningful property on replaced elements and can cause unexpected whitespace rendering in some clients. Remove it.
- **`'Founders Grotesk'` with no `@font-face` declaration** (confirmed): The global `a` selector specifies `font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif`, but no `@font-face` for Founders Grotesk appears in the visible CSS block. All link text will silently fall back to Helvetica.
- **Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`** (confirmed): The opening `<html>` tag carries only namespace attributes (`xmlns`, `xmlns:v`, `xmlns:o`). No `lang="en"` is present — screen readers cannot infer document language.
- **Alt text on images**: Cannot confirm from truncated source. Must verify all `<img>` tags carry descriptive `alt` attributes; missing alt text is a common failure point in this template pattern.
- **Table `role` attributes**: Not visible — verify decorative tables carry `role="presentation"` for screen reader pass-through.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

- **Unsubscribe link**: Not visible in the truncated source. CAN-SPAM requires a clear, functional opt-out mechanism in every commercial email. Confirm it is present in the footer of the full HTML.

- **Sender domain authentication**: `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com` — the `em.` subdomain must have its own SPF record and a valid DKIM signature published. A DMARC policy at `sallybeauty.com` covering the `em.` subdomain is required for alignment.
