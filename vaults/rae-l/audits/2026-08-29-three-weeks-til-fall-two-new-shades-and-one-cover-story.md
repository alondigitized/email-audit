# Three weeks til fall, two new shades, and one cover story
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text "monthly recap" email from ILIA, and it's a clear format departure from every prior send in the brand history — no hero image, no bold visual hierarchy, just a bulleted list of five separate items (waitlist, press mention, GWP offers x2, content roundup) with a thumbnail strip tacked on at the bottom. It reads like an internal newsletter digest, not a marketing send with a single offer to act on.
- The copy itself is fine — casual, on-brand, "Hi friend" tone — but there's no single CTA to rally around. Five links compete for attention with zero visual priority given to any of them, and the actual product thumbnails are crammed into a low-resolution grid at the very bottom, disconnected from their corresponding bullets above. Compared to 08-24's "A gift—line for line" (7/10, one clean Thru Line GWP offer) or 08-20's "prove it" stat-card send (8/10), this is a regression in focus and execution.
- Judgement: this is a "content roundup" email dressed as a promo blast, and it will underperform on clicks because it forces the reader to choose among five asks instead of one.

## What's working

- Multiple offers are genuinely stacked (Overglaze waitlist sweepstakes, free Lip Sketch mini, free Leave-In Conditioner) — recipients with different interests each have something to click.
- Founder/press mention (BCBusiness cover) adds credibility and a human angle absent from ILIA's usual product-stat playbook.
- Copy tone is warm and conversational, consistent with brand voice.
- No render bugs — text and images all display cleanly, no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- No hero image, no bold headline, no single focal point — the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- Five competing bullets/CTAs dilute intent; nothing signals which offer matters most.
- Thumbnail image grid at the bottom is small, generic, and disconnected from the bullets it's meant to illustrate — you have to scroll back up to match photo to offer.
- Asterisked fine print ("*See site for details") appears three times with no upfront explanation, which reads as vague/bait-and-switch for the free-gift offers.
- Subject line ("Three weeks til fall, two new shades, and one cover story") bundles three unrelated topics, none of which is the strongest offer (the free GWPs).

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE lead offer — the Crown Affair free Leave-In Conditioner with $75+ order is the most concrete/time-bound — and build a real hero section around it with a product image and a single button-style CTA. Move the recap bullets below as secondary content.
- 2. Add a proper header image instead of opening cold with "Hi friend" — even a simple lifestyle banner would give the email an anchor.
- 3. Move the thumbnail images up next to their respective bullets, or turn the whole email into an image-forward layout matching prior ILIA sends (e.g., 08-20's stat cards).
- 4. Cut this from five offers to two max — waitlist sweepstakes + one GWP — and drop the "Hometown Hero" press item to a footer mention, since it has no conversion value.
- 5. Replace "*See site for details" with the actual minimum spend/threshold inline so it doesn't read as buried fine print.
