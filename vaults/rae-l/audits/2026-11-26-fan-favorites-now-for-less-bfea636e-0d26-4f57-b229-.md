# Fan favorites, now for less
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the Thanksgiving-day send in a week-long Black Friday run. It is a clean, competent social-proof email. A red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner leads, then a family-unwrapping-gifts hero reading "LOVED BY THOUSANDS". Four review-quoted product cards follow (Slip-ins, BOBS, Cozy Fit, Arch Fit), and a second red banner closes with "EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING, CODE BF2026" for Plus members.
- The structure works. The offer is on top, the hero is warm, and the quote cards give a reason to believe. But it is a fifth or sixth near-identical sale reminder since 11/21, and it adds nothing new. Compared with 11/23's "Hours left for 30% off sitewide" and 11/24's "The coziest deals of the year", there is no urgency on Thanksgiving day itself. Nothing says the sale ends 11/29, and the dates are buried in the fine print. The hero image also isn't clickable-looking and has no product in focus. The "Fan favorites, now for less" subject is a decent hook, but "for less" is vague against a 30-50% offer that could be named.
- Verdict: solid and trustworthy, but a low-urgency repeat. The cards carry it. A reader who has seen the last five sends gets no reason to act today.

## What's working

- The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner with a "SHOP NOW" button sits at the very top, so the offer is the first thing you see.
- The customer quotes on each card ("Easy to slip into. True to size fit.") answer the fit and comfort questions that matter for shoes.
- The four cards are tidy. Each has a category label, a tagline, a product image, and paired Women's/Men's buttons.
- The second red banner puts the Plus member perk (extra 15%, code BF2026) in a clear, separate block.
- The Klarna/Afterpay row adds payment-flexibility reassurance for gift buyers.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline in the visible body. The 11/29 end date appears only in the fine print, and the email never says "ends Sunday".
- The "LOVED BY THOUSANDS" hero is a lifestyle photo with no product and no link cue, so the biggest block is just decoration.
- The product images are small, one shoe per card on a lot of white space, and the quotes are in faint grey italic that is hard to read.
- "Shop Best Sellers" is a generic CTA, and the four cards each carry two buttons (eight in all), which dilutes focus.
- The fine print is a dense block of tiny text with exclusions and two stacked offers, which undercuts the "honest offer" feel.
- The Plus promo is repeated: the "Join Skechers Plus" strip at the top, then the "EXTRA 15%" banner below.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add real urgency. Put "Ends Sunday" or a Thanksgiving-to-Cyber-Monday countdown inside the top banner, and cut the repeated sale-reminder feel.
- 2. Make the hero shoppable. Overlay one hero product or a "Shop gifts under $X" button on the photo.
- 3. Collapse the eight buttons to four (one per card) or a single "Shop Women's / Men's" toggle, and enlarge the product images.
- 4. Darken the quote text and give the best quote a star rating for stronger proof.
- 5. Merge the two Plus mentions into one block, and shorten the fine print to a single line with a "see details" link.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Our best-reviewed styles, 30-50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real reviews, real comfort. Plus members get an extra 15% with BF2026.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive CSS. QA reports 0 errors and 3 warnings. The real findings are two non-HTTPS, alt-less images, third-party beacons that carry recipient identifiers, and zoom and text-resize restrictions. The source is truncated, so the footer, the links and the UTM parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 41 tracking links were skipped because they are on click-redirect domains. This probably includes `click.emails.skechers.com`, but the QA output doesn't list the skipped URLs. Link validity and final destinations are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel with a recipient ID:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11262026`
  - It loads over plain HTTP.
  - `mi_u=8108776175` appears to be a per-recipient identifier. It is sent in cleartext to a third-party domain.
- **Hashed email sent to a third party:** `https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=…_sha25&partner_uid=7e8656d1…c412624` carries what looks like a SHA-256 hash of the recipient address. A second beacon carries `partner_uid=122908675`, which looks like a subscriber ID. The third beacon (`1x1_ad_impression.gif`) carries the campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11262026`. These are HTTPS but add third-party data sharing.
- **Primary open pixel:** `click.emails.skechers.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is HTTPS and has `alt=""`. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - The three `krxd.net` beacon `<img>` tags also have no `alt` attribute.
  - The `dde00662…png` image may be content rather than a pixel, since it is a hosted library asset. If so, the missing alt is a real accessibility gap.
- **Mixed content (QA-confirmed):** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/…/dde00662-….png` and the ink1000 `o.gif` use `http://` and may be blocked or flagged by clients. The `<link rel="icon">` also uses `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/…`, which is ignored by email clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables iOS text resizing.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.
- **Invalid markup against the XHTML doctype:** the krxd `<img>` tags use unquoted attributes (`width=0 height=0`).
- **Not assessable:** `role="presentation"` is confirmed only on the first table. The rest of the document is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers (QA warnings):** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA itself attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. This is unconfirmed, and it is a capture limitation rather than a verified defect.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** these are in the footer, which was truncated, so they could not be checked.
- **Domain split:** From is `emails.skechers.com`, while assets also come from `image.emails.nl.skechers.com`. Alignment can't be judged without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The visible source shows no `utm_*` parameters, because the destination links are not in the truncated portion and all 41 tracking links were skipped. The only campaign identifier visible is the `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDL14D_EN_11262026` value, which appears in the ink1000 and krxd beacons. Whether it matches the destination UTMs is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve both `http://` images over HTTPS: `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/…/dde00662-….png` and the ink1000 `o.gif`. Confirm the ink1000 beacon is needed at all.
2. Add `alt` text to the `dde00662…png` image and `alt=""` to the pixel images, including the three krxd beacons.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` from the `*` rule. Restore a focus style for `a`.
4. Review the third-party beacons (ink1000 `mi_u`, krxd hashed email and subscriber ID) against the privacy policy and consent. Prefer an HTTPS, non-identifying parameter where possible.
5. Fix the invalid pixel markup by quoting the attributes and self-closing the tags.
6. Re-run QA on the raw `.eml`. This will confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and it will check the footer unsubscribe link and address.
7. Re-run QA with the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the 41 click-tracker redirects to check destinations and UTMs.
