# Save up to 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Friends of Glossier sale email in three days, and the creative pivots sensibly from event-launch mode to a product-led format — Summer Scents and Community Favorites are designed to convert the openers who didn't click on the June 11 announcement or the June 12 activation push. The tiered hero offer (20% sitewide / 25% off $100+) is the clearest Glossier has been about how to maximize savings in this arc, and the fragrance-first framing is seasonally sharp.
- What undercuts it: the badge labels on the product tiles are ambiguous — it's genuinely unclear whether "$66" represents the sale price or the savings. The CTA copy is an undifferentiated "Shop now" across every block, which collapses the scroll into noise rather than intent. And crucially, "LIMITED TIME" in the hero with no end date is a conversion killer — subscribers have now seen three sale emails in three days with no deadline anchor.
- The cadence is at saturation risk. Glossier has earned the right to run a multi-day sale push, but without a visible countdown or end date, each additional send dilutes urgency rather than building it.

## What's working

- Tiered offer in the hero is cleanly structured — 20% sitewide is the floor, 25% off $100+ is the reward for basket-building, no fine-print ambiguity.
- "Annual Sale" tiled-text background treatment is visually assertive and consistent with the June 12 send, so brand continuity is intact.
- Fragrance-forward section (Summer Scents first) is the right call — Glossier You products fit the heat of the campaign and the "soft, warm + familiar" copy earns its place.
- Subscription upsell at the bottom is well-positioned — it catches people who are interested but hesitant about a one-time full purchase.

## What's weak

- Product tile badges are visually ambiguous — "$66" adjacent to a sale context reads as either the sale price or the savings amount, and the switch to "OR $22" on Big Brow compounds the confusion.
- "Shop now" is repeated identically across five separate product CTAs — zero differentiation by category or product name.
- Subject line "Save up to 25% off" is fully brand-agnostic; Glossier doesn't appear, no product category hint, no deadline.
- "LIMITED TIME" in the hero is empty urgency — this is the third consecutive day of sale emails with no end date anywhere in the visible render.
- No social proof with real texture — "Community Favorites" is a section header, not a review count or a quote.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an explicit end date.** "Ends Sunday" converts harder than "LIMITED TIME" in any A/B test. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Differentiate CTAs by section** — "Shop Summer Scents" and "Shop Community Favorites" turn passive scrolling into declared intent and improve click attribution.
- 3. **Clarify the product badges** — use a struck-through original price + new price format (`~~$88~~ → $66`) so the value is unambiguous at a glance.
- 4. **Make the subject do more work** — it's 18 characters but tells you nothing brand-specific or time-bound.
- 5. **Consider combining Summer Scents + Community Favorites into one send** on future sale arcs rather than spreading across days; the cadence is burning subscriber patience before the weekend.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Glossier annual sale: 25% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% off window is closing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `20% off everything, 25% off $100+. Fragrance, brow, skincare — shop before it ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Summer Scents + Community Favorites are in the sale. Don't let a friend pay full price.`

## 1. Overview

This is the third Friends of Glossier sale email in three days, and the creative pivots sensibly from event-launch mode to a product-led format — Summer Scents and Community Favorites are designed to convert the openers who didn't click on the June 11 announcement or the June 12 activation push. - Fragrance-forward section (Summer Scents first) is the right call — Glossier You products fit the heat of the campaign and the "soft, warm + familiar" copy earns its place.

## 3. What didn't

- Product tile badges are visually ambiguous — "$66" adjacent to a sale context reads as either the sale price or the savings amount, and the switch to "OR $22" on Big Brow compounds the confusion.
