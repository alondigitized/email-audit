# 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection ends today❗
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in 24 hours for the same 2X/5X points event, so it is a last-call reminder. It is clearer than yesterday's "ends tomorrow" version (2026-10-06). The hero is a giant red "ENDS TODAY" over "2X INSIDER", followed by a "USE CODE SAYPOINTS" button. Both the urgency and the offer are legible at a glance. A pink strip below adds a second incentive: free Same-Day Delivery with code DELIVERED, ending 10/7 at 1 PM PT.
- After the hero, the email dilutes itself. A "See What Your Points Can Do" rewards ladder, a cardmember banner and six product tiles showing points earned follow it. A Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal strip, a holiday gift-vault pair, a $105 free-trial-size promo and a Beauty Insider tier strip come after that. Every block is a separate pitch, and the fine print and barcode at the bottom run very long. The tile idea is good, because "810 points" on a Tom Ford perfume makes the 2X math concrete. The "Use code SAYPOINTS" repeated on each tile is clutter, though.
- Overall it is a competent, urgent loyalty send that buries its simple message under a catalog. It is also the same offer as yesterday's email and likely to be skipped by anyone who already acted.

## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" in oversized red type makes the deadline impossible to miss.
- A single code, SAYPOINTS, is in a clear button directly under the hero.
- Product tiles show the points earned ("810 points", "350 points"), which turns the multiplier into something concrete.
- The free same-day delivery strip, with its 1 PM PT cutoff, adds a second, credible time limit.
- The 2X-for-everything and 5X-for-Sephora-Collection framing is consistent with the subject line.

## What's weak

- Below the hero, the email adds the rewards ladder, the BNPL strip, the holiday gift vault, the trial-size promo and the tier strip. It reads as a catalog rather than a last-call reminder.
- The "SAYPOINTS" code appears about eight times, including under every product tile, which is repetitive.
- The tiles lead with products like a $405 Tom Ford perfume and a $175 Jo Malone. These make a poor "last chance" impulse buy next to the $10–$24 items.
- The 5X Sephora Collection offer in the subject is almost invisible in the body; the tiles are all 2X-brand products.
- The legal text and barcode at the bottom are very long, and the small type makes the terms feel buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the product tiles except the footer. Keep the hero, the delivery strip and one row of tiles, so the email is a single clear reminder.
- 2. Replace the top-priced tiles with $10–$30 items, and add at least one Sephora Collection tile, since that is the 5X offer.
- 3. Show the code once, in the hero, and let the tiles link directly to products without repeating it.
- 4. Differentiate this send from yesterday's by naming the hours left or the 1 PM delivery cutoff in the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 2X points, 5X on Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Points event ends tonight, code SAYPOINTS`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free Same-Day Delivery with code DELIVERED until 1 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance to earn double on everything in your cart.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure looks standard for table-based marketing HTML. The QA run flags a missing plain-text part (0 chars), four images without alt text, and a viewport and text-size setup that restricts user zoom. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, because the AgentMail relay didn't expose the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. Only 12 of 54 links were probed, and 42 were skipped as footer, utility, or social links. Those 42 are unverified, not confirmed healthy.
- The tracking pixel `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261007_PME_Stacking_Ends_Today_BI` has no alt text. It is functional and not a defect, but add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` so screen readers skip it.
- The `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` image has no alt text. Its path looks like a second open or tracking beacon, so the same fix applies.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (QA WARN):**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (epsilon.com/ContentHandler)
  - `2026-10-07_email_format_ded_us_3734_06.jpg`
  
  These look like navigation and body images. With images blocked, the recipient sees nothing, or sees the filename.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing is disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` are applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has none, so screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the email.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. This is low impact in email.
- **Plain-text fallback:** QA [FAIL] reports 0 chars. This may be an AgentMail relay capture issue, but as received there is no text/plain alternative. See section 5.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u=4958433775694848` in the pixel URL is correctly resolved. The source was truncated, so the body copy is not fully covered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool says the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is relay-related and not evidence of failure.
- **Plain-text part:** 0 chars is a deliverability negative. Some spam filters score HTML-only mail higher, and a missing text/plain part is more likely a real defect than the header gaps above.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't in the QA output. The footer address is outside the truncated source, so neither can be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found that can be confirmed. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261007_PME_Stacking_Ends_Today_BI` on the pixel. The CTA hrefs and any UTM parameters fall in the truncated section, and no landing-page results were provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain MIME part** that mirrors the offer and includes the unsubscribe link. Confirm first whether the part exists upstream of AgentMail.
2. **Add descriptive `alt`** to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` and `2026-10-07_email_format_ded_us_3734_06.jpg`. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust: none` where it isn't needed for Outlook or Gmail quirks.
4. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and make the `<title>` descriptive.
5. **Confirm headers on the raw message.** Check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results at the origin, or through a seed inbox that preserves headers.
6. **Re-run link probing with a larger budget.** Include the 42 skipped links, and capture the CTA hrefs to check UTM and landing-page continuity.
