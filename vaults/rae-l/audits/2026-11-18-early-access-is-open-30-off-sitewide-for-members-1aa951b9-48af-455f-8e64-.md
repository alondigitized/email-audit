# Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' Black Friday opener, and the hero is very strong. The maroon block says "SKECHERS Plus Member Exclusive," then "BLACK FRIDAY EARLY ACCESS," then a huge white-banded "30% OFF SITEWIDE." The eye lands on the offer immediately, and the black SHOP NOW button is clear. It's a real discount with a real deadline (thru 11/22), a big step up from the browse-abandonment sends that filled the 11/12–11/14 stretch ("Don't leave these styles behind!", "This style has limited availability!").
- The execution beyond the hero is competent but generic. The four-tile category grid (Women, Men, Kids, Apparel) is clean. The Doorbuster band ("starting at $49.99") adds a second hook. But the fine print is heavy: "Exclusions apply," "Not stackable," and two dense legal blocks. For a "sitewide" claim, that undercuts honesty. The category-level targeting is also broad, with nothing tied to one product or one persona.
- Judgement: a well-built, high-impact promo that beats the recent abandonment sends. It's held back by hedged "sitewide" language and no hero product.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF SITEWIDE hero is huge and high-contrast, so the offer is unmissable.
- The Skechers Plus member framing makes the exclusivity feel earned, and "Early Access" adds credible urgency.
- A concrete deadline ("Valid online only thru 11/22") and "Discount auto-applied at cart" remove friction.
- The 2x2 category grid with labeled buttons gives clear paths for different shoppers.
- The Doorbuster band ($49.99) gives a price-anchored second hook for deal hunters.
- The Klarna/Afterpay row and the app download block add useful, trust-building footer utility.

## What's weak

- "Sitewide" is undercut by "Exclusions apply," "Not stackable," and two long legal paragraphs, which reads like fine print buried at the bottom.
- The category tiles are lifestyle shots with no product names or prices, so there is no specific shoe to click on.
- The nine-item footer stack (links, BNPL, texts, app) dilutes focus after the hero.
- There are no reviews or social proof anywhere.
- The "Join Skechers Plus and Earn 1000 Bonus Points" top bar is irrelevant to a recipient who is already a member and competes with the hero.
- The Women/Men tiles have faint model credit text and the overlaid labels sit directly on the faces/bodies, which is visually busy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero's exclusions honest and visible near the CTA. Say what's excluded in one short line instead of "Exclusions apply, see below."
- 2. Add a "Member favorites" row of 3 to 4 specific shoes with sale prices and star ratings between the category grid and the Doorbusters.
- 3. Swap the top bar to a member-specific message (points balance or "You're in") for members.
- 4. Put a countdown or "Ends 11/22" badge on the hero button for urgency.
- 5. Shorten the footer so the legal text is condensed and the BNPL/app blocks are merged into one row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: 30% off everything starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early access: 30% off through 11/22`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Auto-applied at cart. Plus doorbusters from $49.99, members only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop before everyone else: sneakers, apparel and kids' styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound: table layout, MSO conditional comments, responsive media queries, and JSON-LD promo annotations. The QA run found two non-HTTPS images without alt text, and a few accessibility attributes in the `<head>` are a concern. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 33 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were probed. I can't confirm they resolve or what they land on.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `o.gif` is served over plain HTTP from a third-party host: `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11182026`.
  - It has no alt text.
  - Clients that block or upgrade HTTP images may drop it, which would undercount opens.
  - `mi_u=8108776175` looks like a per-recipient identifier sent in the clear. The source doesn't show what it encodes.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` is HTTP and has no alt text. Other images on the same host use HTTPS in the JSON-LD, so this one is inconsistent.
- **Favicon:** `<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...">` uses HTTP and a different subdomain (`emails.nl`) from the sending domain (`emails`). It has no function in email and is a possible leftover from another template.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Both flagged images lack `alt`. If the images are blocked, the tracker is invisible but the PNG leaves a blank slot.
- `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` disables text-size scaling in WebKit clients.
- `a { outline: none }` removes the keyboard focus indicator on every link.
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the language.
- `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- The `.mobile-content` / `.desktop-content` toggle duplicates content. Clients that ignore `@media` (some Gmail account types) may show only the mobile block, which is the default `display:block`. I can't verify this from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The only personalization seen is the `mi_u` parameter above. The source is truncated, so the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing from the captured headers. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` is unknown.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so I couldn't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Destination:** all three JSON-LD `PromotionCard` entries point to the same URL, `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals`. That URL has no UTM parameters, though the tracking pixel does carry `mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11182026`. Campaign attribution on the site side depends on the 33 unprobed redirect links.
- **Offer window:** the `DiscountOffer` runs from `2026-11-18T00:11:00Z` to `2026-11-22T23:11:59Z`. The odd minute values (`:11`, `23:11:59`) suggest a hand-entered timestamp. They may not match the intended window.
- **Members-only claim:** I couldn't verify that the landing page shows or gates the "30% off for members" offer. That needs a live page check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Pull the raw headers and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before filing any compliance bug.
2. Switch the `dde00662…png` image and the favicon to HTTPS. Ask the tracking vendor for an HTTPS `ink1000.com` pixel, and confirm `mi_u` isn't PII.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and meaningful alt text to the content image.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, change `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` to `100%`, restore a visible `a:focus` outline, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Resolve the 33 redirect chains manually to check final URLs, UTMs and the members-only landing behavior.
6. Verify the `availabilityStarts` and `availabilityEnds` timestamps.
