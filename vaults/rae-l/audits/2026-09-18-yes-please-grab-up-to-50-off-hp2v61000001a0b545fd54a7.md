# Yes, please! Grab up to 50% off.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "50% off Select Beauty" flash-sale creative Sephora has run at least twice this week (2026-09-15 "Select brands are 50% off today 🤑," 9/10), except this send buries the offer detail slightly worse and pivots almost immediately into a "New Chris McMillan Detox Shampoo" product feature that has nothing to do with the discount headline. The subject "Yes, please! Grab up to 50% off" is punchy but vague on what's included, and "up to" signals exclusions before you've even opened it. Once the hero passes, the email sprawls into a long, undifferentiated grid of new arrivals (Kosas, Biodance, Crown Affair, Decocool, Make Up For Ever, Urban Decay, Ole Henriksen) with no single clear second CTA, then a rhode plug, a YSL/fragrance points push, and a Marc Jacobs concealer teaser — five separate campaigns crammed into one email.
- The hero banner itself is clean and the offer is visible without scrolling (50% off + extra day + free same-day delivery), which is a real strength. But everything below the fold is kitchen-sink merchandising rather than a focused send, and it dilutes the strong open.

## What's working

- Hero banner leads immediately with "SURPRISE! 50% OFF Select Beauty" plus a stacked bonus ("An EXTRA day, just for you") and free Same-Day Delivery — offer is visible with zero scrolling.
- Clear "SHOP NOW ▸" button directly under the hero offer.
- "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" badge and product photography for the McMillan shampoo are crisp, well-lit, no render bugs.
- Loyalty/member context is present at the bottom (Insider/VIB/Rouge tier bar) for Beauty Insider members.

## What's weak

- Subject promises a % off deal, but the first full product section is a shampoo launch — no bridge or explanation, so the "50% off" reader lands on a distraction.
- No urgency/deadline visible in the hero itself (no end date on the 50% banner), unlike the 09/15 send which stated the offer clearly with a date.
- The email crams five distinct campaigns (flash sale, new arrivals grid, rhode, 4X points/fragrance, Marc Jacobs concealer) with no visual hierarchy prioritizing one over another after the hero.
- "Up to 50% off... Exclusions/terms apply" language in tiny fine print under the hero undercuts the headline offer's honesty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tie the subject directly to the hero creative — don't let the shampoo launch be the first thing readers see after a discount-focused subject. Either lead the body with the sale in a second banner reinforcing % off, or change the subject to reflect the new-launch angle.
- 2. Add a visible end date/countdown to the 50% off hero banner — "ends 9/23" language exists later in the fine print at the bottom; pull it up top for urgency.
- 3. Trim the new-arrivals grid to 2-3 hero items max, or split into a separate send — eight competing tiles with no ranking dilutes click intent.
- 4. Consolidate the rhode / 4X points / Marc Jacobs sections into a single "more from Sephora" strip instead of three separate mini-campaigns.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Surprise: 50% off Select Beauty, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra day of 50% off is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free Same-Day Delivery on your order`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `An extra day of Select Beauty savings, just for you`
