- Hero photography is sensory and on-message — water, skin, intimacy — sets the shower mood immediately.
- Product grid is well-organized with clear category labels (Hair Treatment, Body Butter, Shower Gel) so you know what you're looking at.
- The Jennie influencer panel adds a human face and editorial credibility mid-scroll.
- Color palette (teal hero, pastel product blocks) feels cohesive and on-brand.
- "Good Karma" tie-in keeps continuity with the 2026-05-22 Karma send without feeling repetitive.

## What's weak

- Hero copy ("Shower Thoughts") is cute but vague — no offer, no urgency, no reason to click now.
- No visible discount, free shipping, or promo anywhere in the email — it's a pure catalog send with no incentive.
- Subject line "🚿 The perfect shower" is generic and doesn't tease any specific product or hook.
- The grid is heavy — four near-identical product tiles with limited differentiation; eye fatigues by the third.
- CTA copy is the generic "Shop Now" on the hero with no urgency or product-specific verb.
