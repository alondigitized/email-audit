- The subject promises a "personal holiday shopper", but the sale banner is the first thing you see and the AI feature comes second.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a fixed-width 700px table layout and wraps links in Sephora's `app.sephora.com` redirect tracker. The main confirmed issue is a missing plain-text part. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay doesn't expose those headers. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and most links were not visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect-wrapped links:** The visible logo link is `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a192b4245fbd48ea6e96638918/…?__dU__=…`. It is a per-recipient tracking redirect, and the final destination can't be seen in the truncated source.
- **Probe coverage:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 26. The data doesn't report any broken or failing links, so none are flagged. The 26 skipped links, mostly footer and social, remain unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…'>` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height` attributes, and it is the image QA flagged for missing alt. Because the image is hidden, this has no accessibility impact. It is a tracking beacon, not content.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks. It could also be an AgentMail relay artifact, so check the raw MIME.
- **Fixed-width layout:** The outer table has `width="700"` and `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. On viewports under 700px this will likely force horizontal scrolling or zooming-out in clients that ignore the 480px media query. The inner table does use `max-width:700px`, which suggests the layout was meant to be responsive.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments are not valid inside CSS. Parsers may drop the rules after it (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileHeaderTierVIB`, `.mobileHeaderTierBI`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, `.footerBanner`, `.mobileDisc`) on mobile.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Minor:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is not a valid value (it should be `noindex`) and is harmless in email. `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. The `application/ld+json` block in `<head>` has `"discountCode": ""`, an empty value, and most clients strip it anyway.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). Tokens in the truncated portion can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN):** QA itself says AgentMail may not capture these headers. Treat this as unconfirmed, not as a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results missing (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `shop@beauty.sephora.com`, a subdomain, and alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Inconclusive. The only visible href is a redirect-wrapped `app.sephora.com/T/…` link with a `__dU__` token and no visible UTM parameters. The final landing URLs, and whether they carry UTMs or match the "AI Beauty Chat" preheader, can't be determined without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative. This is the only confirmed QA FAIL.
2. Check the raw MIME for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC). Don't treat the relay-driven warnings as confirmed failures until then.
3. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule and into a proper conditional `<style>` block, so the following mobile rules aren't dropped.
4. Make the outer table fluid (`width:100%; max-width:700px`) and remove `min-width:700px`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` to the tracking pixel.
7. Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML and the full 38-link probe. That would cover the footer and compliance block, the redirect destinations, and the UTM and landing-page checks.
