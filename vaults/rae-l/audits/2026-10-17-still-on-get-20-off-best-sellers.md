# STILL ON! Get 20% off best-sellers
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the reminder to Glossier's 2026-10-15 "OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE! ⭐" send, and it works. The offer is stated in the first line of hero copy: "Take 20% off all best-sellers." A blue "20% OFF" badge then repeats on each product in the Forever Favorites row. Where the sale email was pure promotion, this one adds customer reviews (Alejandra, Charmaine, Ada), which fits a mid-sale push.
- The weak spot is the top. The hero image crops "20% OFF SURPRISE SALE" text at the right edge, so it reads as cut off. The blue banner "Get a free Futuredew Solid with your $75+ orde…" is truncated too. That free-gift offer then gets a second, bigger billing at the bottom, so the email stacks two offers.
- Overall it is a solid sale reminder with good social proof, held back by a cluttered middle. Three of the four review CTAs go to categories (fragrance, brows, blush) and not to the discounted items. The hero "Shop now" goes to a generic best-seller page.

## What's working

- The offer leads: "Take 20% off all best-sellers" sits right beside the hero image.
- Customer quotes with five-star ratings (Alejandra, Charmaine, Ada) add credibility.
- Blue "20% OFF" badges on Balm Dotcom, Glossier You and Lash Slick make the discount clear on each product.
- "STILL ON!" in the subject and the eyebrow signals that the sale is continuing, not new.
- The free Futuredew Solid with a $75+ order gives shoppers a reason to build a bigger cart.

## What's weak

- The hero image is cropped, so "20% OFF SURPRISE SALE" reads as cut off. The top banner text is also truncated at "orde".
- The free-gift offer appears in both the banner and a closing block, which competes with the 20% message and dilutes it.
- The middle review modules (fragrance, brows, blush) show no discount badge or price, so it is unclear whether these items are in the sale.
- The CTAs are generic "Shop now" or category buttons. They don't name the best-seller or the savings.
- The sale deadline is missing. "STILL ON!" implies urgency but never gives an end date, and the asterisk "Terms apply" is small and sits at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a clear end date, such as "Ends Sunday," next to the hero headline. A deadline gives "STILL ON!" its urgency.
- 2. Fix the cropping on the hero and the top banner so the full "20% OFF" and the Futuredew copy are legible.
- 3. Put the Forever Favorites row with the discount badges right under the hero, and move the review blocks below it. The offer then sits next to the products that carry it.
- 4. Add "20% off" badges or sale prices to the review-module products, or say which are excluded.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop Glossier You, 20% off" or "Get 20% off Boy Brow."
- 6. Pick one secondary offer. Either lead with the free gift or keep it as a small footnote.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing:** 12 material links were probed and none were reported as failing (0 issues). 23 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so those are unverified.
- **Click tracking:** Links are wrapped by Klaviyo (`ctrk.klclick.com/l/...`). Final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Malformed tracking element:** The body opens with `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M53J0YD1APZETG3K2RDKTP7C_0"></span>`.
  - `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`, so it does nothing functional.
  - It sits right before the `<!--<![endif]-->` close of the non-MSO conditional. That placement is likely a Stripo/Klaviyo template artifact.
  - Low severity, but it is invalid markup.
