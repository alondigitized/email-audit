# On everyone’s lips
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA reversing course — instead of another Thru Line eyeliner "prove it" send, "On Everyone's Lips" is a full lip-category roundup, closing with the same free Lip Sketch mini GWP that anchored 08-24's "A gift—line for line." Six products stacked in a clean 2x3 grid (crayon, balmy tint, lipstick, gloss, balm, overnight mask) gives this the feel of a category catalog rather than a single-hero push, which softens the visual hierarchy compared to the stat-card sends that scored well (08-20's 8/10).
- The hero image is striking — two overlapping close-up mouths/lips in different tones, reinforcing "anyone can wear them" — but it's also the kind of tight beauty-macro crop that can read ambiguous at a glance on mobile. The GWP offer reappears at the bottom under "line for line," essentially recycling 08-24's exact hook rather than giving today's send its own distinct offer.
- This is a competent, on-brand catalog email but not a standout. It's cadence filler between the stronger single-offer sends — solid execution, unclear whether the eye knows where to land first.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent product-grid layout with matching photography, names, and "SHOP NOW" buttons — no render bugs or broken images.
- "Trending on TikTok" and "Free Mini" badges add social-proof and offer signals directly on product cards.
- The closing GWP ("line for line," free Lip Sketch mini with Thru Line orders) gives a concrete, specific offer near the CTA.
- Brand trust bar (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact Collective, VIP, shade match, auto-replenish) is legible and reinforces credibility.
- Free shipping over $50 / free returns is visible in the footer.

## What's weak

- Six products with six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons dilutes the primary CTA — there's no single clear action, unlike 08-20's single-hero focus.
- The GWP offer is buried at the very bottom of a long email; a mobile reader has to scroll past the entire product grid to find it.
- "On Everyone's Lips" is vague/pun-forward copy with no concrete offer signal in the hero — the actual free-gift deal isn't visible until far below the fold.
- This is the second send in three days leaning on the same free Lip Sketch mini mechanic (08-24), which risks feeling repetitive rather than escalating.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the GWP offer up top — lead with "Free Lip Sketch mini with any Thru Line order" in the hero instead of burying it at the bottom; that's the one concrete offer in this email and it's currently the last thing seen.
- 2. Cut the grid to 3-4 hero products max, or promote one flagship item with the others as a smaller "shop the edit" strip — six equal-weight CTAs kill hierarchy.
- 3. Differentiate this from 08-24 — if the same mini-GWP mechanic is running again, say so explicitly ("still live") or pair it with a new angle so subscribers don't feel like they're rereading the same email.
- 4. Swap the ambiguous macro-lips hero for one that reads instantly as "lip products" (product-in-hand or clear swatch shot) to reduce first-glance confusion.
