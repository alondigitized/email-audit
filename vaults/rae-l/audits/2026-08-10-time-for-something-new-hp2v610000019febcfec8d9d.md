# Time for something new 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora falling straight back into the "new arrivals" catalog-scroll format it just ran on 8/8 ("Ready, set, shop—new beauty has landed") — swap the header from "Just Dropped" to "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" and the brand lineup, and it's structurally the same email two days later. Ten products across ten brands (MAC, Too Faced, Shaz & Kiks, Dr. Idriss, Urban Decay, Josie Maran, Dae, Fenty, Nest, Banu) stack with zero narrative thread tying them together, which is the same flaw that dragged the 8/8 send to 7.00/10.
- The top of the email is doing real work — a $10 bonus card GWP banner, a HiMascara trial-size offer, and a clean "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" hero with one clear Shop Now CTA. If the email stopped there it'd be tight. Instead it runs into an unfocused ten-SKU grid that dilutes the hero, then tacks on App Early Access, Try It Love It, and a Fragrance banner at the bottom — three more distinct offers competing for the same click. Too much surface area, no single thing to remember.

## What's working

- Hero banner is clean: bold "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" headline, one product-cluster image, one unambiguous "SHOP NOW" button.
- Top-of-email GWP offer ($10 bonus card with 75+ purchase, code BEAUTY10) is a concrete, specific incentive placed above the fold.
- Trial-size HiMascara callout gives a low-commitment, low-price entry point right under the header.
- "NEW" and "NEW — ONLINE ONLY" / "LIMITED EDITION" badges are legible and consistently placed on every product tile.
- No render bugs — images, badges, and pricing all display cleanly with consistent tile layout.

## What's weak

- Ten unrelated products from ten brands with no unifying theme — nearly identical structural problem to the 8/8 "new arrivals" send that scored 7.00.
- No single hero product or SKU to anchor the click — the grid format spreads attention thin instead of building toward one offer.
- Three additional offers stacked after the grid (App Early Access, Try It Love It, Fragrance Worth the Hype) dilute focus further instead of closing the email out.
- Subject "Time for something new 🛍️" is generic and could headline almost any Sephora arrivals email — it doesn't tell me what's actually new.
- No pricing tiers or Beauty Insider point context tied to the arrivals grid, despite loyalty tiers being shown at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the ten-item grid to 3-4 standout new arrivals and give the strongest one (Fenty Beauty x Rihanna limited-edition duo is the most newsworthy) its own hero treatment instead of Grid #7-of-10.
- 2. Merge the bottom three banners (App Early Access, Try It Love It, Fragrance) into a single "more ways to shop" strip — right now they compete with the main offer instead of supporting it.
- 3. Tie the $10 bonus card and BEAUTY10 code more directly to the new-arrivals grid ("spend $75 on new arrivals, get $10 back") so the incentive and the content are one story, not two.
- 4. Sharpen the subject line to name an actual product or category instead of "something new."
- **Subject Alt A:** `New arrivals: Fenty x Rihanna lip duo just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10 new beauty launches + $10 bonus card`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop $75+ in new arrivals and get a $10 Digital Gift Card`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `MAC, Fenty, Urban Decay and 7 more brands just landed`
- 5. Add Beauty Insider point-multiplier framing near the grid, since the brand has loyalty infrastructure already built into the footer nav.
