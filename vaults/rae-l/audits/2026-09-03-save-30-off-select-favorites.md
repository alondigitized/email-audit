# Save 30% off select favorites
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier finally breaking the discount drought — a real Labor Day Sale with 30% off, tiered up to 50% off select items (Stretch Balm Concealer, Stretch Fluid Foundation), plus a $75+ free-gift threshold. After a five-send stretch of zero-discount editorial (Virgo Season, best-friend fragrance pairings, back-to-school bundles), this is the strongest commercial send since 8/18's "Cheek Week ends tonight!" (8.00/10). The offer is legible immediately: hero copy states "Save 30% off select favorites*," backed by a bold SALE/SALE/SALE/ALE typographic block that's genuinely eye-catching.
- The structure is smart merchandising — "Top Picks" at 30%, then "Deeper Discounts" calling out 50% off in red corner badges on two specific products, then a free-gift upsell at the bottom. Every product tile has its own "Shop now" CTA, so there's no ambiguity about what's clickable. This is a well-organized, offer-forward send that should outperform the recent editorial run on both opens and clicks.
- The rough edge is the asterisk fine print: "select favorites*" and "on us with your $75+ order*" both need the reader to hunt for "*Terms apply." at the very bottom with no visible detail on which items qualify for 30% vs. 50% vs. excluded entirely.

## What's working

- Clear numeric offer in the hero ("Save 30% off select favorites") reinforced by an eye-catching SALE typographic collage.
- Sale is tiered and visually differentiated — 50% off items get a bold red corner badge, making the best deals scannable at a glance.
- Every single product block has its own "Shop now" button — zero friction between seeing a product and clicking to buy.
- Clean product photography with consistent styling (fruit-and-glass still life for fragrance, texture swatch for Lidstar) keeps visual quality high.
- Free-gift-with-purchase at $75+ is a smart secondary hook to lift average order value.

## What's weak

- "Select favorites*" never clarifies what's excluded — the asterisk resolves only to generic "Terms apply" at the very bottom of the email.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere — unlike 8/18's "Cheek Week ends tonight!" which led with expiration and scored 8.00, this send has no end date visible, which softens urgency to click now vs. later.
- The free-gift CTA button ("Shop now") is styled in flat gray/white, visually weaker than the orange CTAs above it — it reads deprioritized even though a free gift is a strong hook.
- "Labor Day Sale" eyebrow is small and easy to miss above the fold; the holiday-tie-in doesn't get the emphasis a seasonal sale usually deserves.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible end date/countdown to the hero ("Ends Monday" or similar) — the brand's own history shows deadline-driven copy (8/18) outperforms open-ended sale language.
- 2. Replace "select favorites*" with a concrete qualifier in the hero itself (e.g., "Save 30% off fragrance, cheek & eye" or "up to 50% off") so the reader isn't sent hunting for fine print.
- 3. Restyle the free-gift "Shop now" button to match the orange CTA treatment used elsewhere — right now it visually signals "less important" when it should close the sale.
- 4. Surface the 50% off framing higher — right now shoppers scroll through two rows of 30%-off product before reaching the best deal.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day discount: 30-50% off favorites`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, a free Glossier You Solid with $75+`
