# LAST CALL: up to 40% off hair faves 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises one thing: "LAST CALL: up to 40% off hair faves." The email delivers it in a thin strip at the top, with the "Up to 40% off* Hair Faves" headline, a TODAY ONLY badge, a PATTERN/Vegamour product shot and a SHOP NOW button. Then the email changes topic. The real hero is a huge pink "Minis Make It Easy" block with a Miu Miu perfume, followed by a grid of eight mini products. Hair is an afterthought in an email sold on hair.
- It is also the third hair-discount send in a week. On 2026-10-02 it was "Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves," and on 2026-09-29 it was "From your feed to your cart" with up to 50% off Hair Faves. "LAST CALL" at a lower 40% reads as the tail of a promo the subscriber has already seen twice. The Earn Points strip (2X/5X, SAYPOINTS, ends 10/7) is the same one from the 2026-10-03 "Hot drops" send.
- Judgement: a competent but unfocused send. The top offer is clear and credible, but the minis hero takes the eye first and the rest of the scroll dilutes the urgency.

## What's working

- The top strip states the offer plainly: "Up to 40% off* Hair Faves," a TODAY ONLY badge and a SHOP NOW button.
- It names specific brands (PATTERN by Tracee Ellis Ross, Vegamour), which gives the hair deal some substance.
- The Minis grid shows prices and size or value details (for example "$40 ($48 value)"), so shoppers can judge the deals.
- The "P.S." line for free Same-Day Delivery with code DELIVERED, plus the SAYPOINTS strip, gives clear secondary reasons to buy.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text, and the layout is consistent.

## What's weak

- The Minis hero is about ten times the visual weight of the hair offer. The eye lands on Miu Miu, not on the offer the subject promised.
- Hair gets one thin strip. The "Glossy Hair" tile near the bottom is the only other hair content, and it has no discount visible.
- The asterisked "up to 40%" has no visible exclusions near the offer. The fine print sits in the footer, far below it.
- Four or five competing messages stack up: hair, minis, points, skincare sets and Same-Day Unlimited. That is very long for a "last call."
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" or "TRY IT," and none names a product or category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hair deal the hero. Put a full-size "Up to 40% off hair faves, ends tonight" block above everything and move Minis below the fold or to a separate send.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT," and a countdown if possible. "TODAY ONLY" on its own is vague, and three hair sends in a week need a stronger reason to believe it.
- 3. Show 3–4 priced hair products with the discount applied (for example "Was $38, now $26") instead of just a brand collage.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop hair deals" and use matching CTAs on the products.
- 5. Cut the length. One secondary module (points or Same-Day) is enough.
