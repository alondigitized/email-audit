# 15 x 14 = 210 lip combos
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA doing a math-gimmick bundle send — "15 x 14 = 210 lip combos" pairs the two products from the recent product-launch arc (Lip Sketch liner + Overglaze, the same Motif/Gallery shades from the 09-01 "NEW Overglaze shades" send) into a single cross-sell offer: 15% off "The Sketch + Glaze Lip Combo." It's a clean, on-brand layout — soft editorial photography, a clear two-step "how to build your combo" structure, plenty of shade swatches — but the subject line is a novelty hook that doesn't actually tell you what's for sale or that there's a discount attached.
- The email itself executes well once opened: hero shot, clear step 1/step 2 breakdown, a bundle CTA, then a familiar "Infinite Color + Hydration" cross-sell grid and a mini-gift banner. But this is the third time in three sends (09-01, 09-03, now 09-06) that Overglaze has anchored the email, and the "we did the math" bundle framing is a thinner hook than a straightforward "15% off your lip combo" would have been. Solid execution, weak subject strategy — this lands as a mid-pack promotional send.

## What's working

- Clear two-step build structure (Step 1: Lip Sketch, Step 2: Overglaze) makes the bundle logic easy to follow visually.
- Shade swatches are shown clearly for both products, addressing a real purchase-decision need (color matching).
- The "Save 15%... Only at iliabeauty.com" banner gives a concrete, visible offer near the top.
- Consistent brand editorial photography and color palette match prior sends (soft pink/cream, serif logo treatment).
- Loyalty/VIP banner at the very top ("JOIN ILIA VIP FOR FREE + EARN 50 POINTS... 4X VIP POINTS ON OVERGLAZE LIP GLOSS") surfaces a second, timely incentive.

## What's weak

- Subject line "15 x 14 = 210 lip combos" buries the actual offer (15% off) and doesn't say what product category this is about — a reader has to do the math themselves or open to find out.
- This is the third Overglaze-centric send in six days (09-01 launch, 09-03 Overglaze feature, now this bundle) — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who already saw those.
- The 15%-off banner is small, dark, and easy to skim past compared to the large "Lip Sketch + Overglaze" headline above it — hierarchy favors the product name over the deal.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the visible copy — no reason to act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with the offer, not the riddle — "15% off your Lip Sketch + Overglaze combo" converts intent faster than a math puzzle.
- 2. Move or enlarge the "Save 15%" line so it's the first thing the eye hits, ahead of the product name.
- 3. Add a deadline to the bundle offer (even a soft one like "this week only") to create urgency currently missing from the send.
- 4. Vary the hero visual from the 09-01/09-03 Overglaze close-ups — reader has seen this shade/product treatment twice already this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save 15% when you bundle Lip Sketch + Overglaze`
- **Subject Alt B:** `210 lip combos, 15% off all of them`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pair any liner with any gloss — 15% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your perfect lip combo, now for less.`
