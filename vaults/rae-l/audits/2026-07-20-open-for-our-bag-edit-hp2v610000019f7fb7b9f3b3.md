# Open for our bag edit
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a Sephora editorial-format send — "bag edit" framing, magazine-style layout, warm glowy model imagery — rather than a discount-first blast. The email leads with a **Charlotte Tilbury free mascara** offer in a salmon-pink top banner, then drops into a "WHAT'S IN OUR BAG?" hero, followed by a "JUST IN FROM CHARLOTTE TILBURY / FIRST AT SEPHORA" exclusive launch section, a 6-product grid (Chanel, L'Occitane, Maison Margiela, and others), and closes with "Oil-Balancing Hydration" and a "BEST OF LIP" module.
- The editorial voice is consistent and the photography is aspirational. The problem is the **subject line does nothing**. "Open for our bag edit" is 22 characters of intrigue with zero payoff signal — no brand name, no offer, no category, no urgency. Given yesterday's 4X points push (July 19), subscribers are mid-loyalty-beat and this pivot to unanchored editorial may land flat in the inbox.
- The free mascara offer at the top is the strongest asset here, but it's leading from the wrong position — it's in a banner strip, not the subject line. An email with a Charlotte Tilbury freebie should be shouting that in the subject and preheader, not burying it behind "bag edit" framing.

## What's working

- **Charlotte Tilbury free mascara banner** is the first visible element — credible, high-perceived-value incentive for the opening scroll.
- **"JUST IN FROM / FIRST AT SEPHORA" exclusivity signal** is well-positioned right after the hero; scarcity framing works for luxury.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — the eye follows: banner → editorial image → headline → product grid without confusion or clutter.
- **No render bugs visible** — images load, text is legible, layout is intact across all modules.
- **Product diversity in the grid** (Chanel, Maison Margiela, L'Occitane alongside accessible picks) reinforces a genuine "what's in the bag" editorial voice rather than a paid-placement dump.

## What's weak

- **Subject line squanders the Charlotte Tilbury hook** — the single biggest conversion lever isn't mentioned anywhere in the inbox view.
- **"We have quite the content"** reads like unfinished copy or an accidental internal note — that subheadline under the hero erodes trust.
- **No visible deadline or urgency** — the free mascara offer has no expiry, no threshold ("while supplies last," "first 500 redeemers") — easy for a reader to defer and forget.
- **"SHOP NOW" is used three times** — once after the bag edit hero, once in the Oil-Balancing module, once in the footer — each CTA button says the same generic thing and nothing is differentiated by product or offer.
- **Cadence whiplash** — July 19 was a loyalty/points push, today is unanchored editorial. No connective tissue linking yesterday's earning event to today's spend prompt.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put Charlotte Tilbury in the subject.** This is the obvious single change — a recognizable luxury brand name + a free product is a top-5% inbox hook and it's not there.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Charlotte Tilbury Mascara — inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Charlotte Tilbury just dropped. Plus: our bag.`
- 2. **Rewrite or remove the "We have quite the content" subheadline** — it reads like placeholder copy. Replace with a one-line promise like "Six products we've been reaching for this month."
- 3. **Fix the preheader** — pull in the free mascara CTA or the Charlotte Tilbury exclusivity line rather than leaking whatever default text the client picks up.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free full-size mascara + Charlotte Tilbury's Sephora debut — inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `First at Sephora: Charlotte Tilbury. Plus a free mascara — tap to redeem.`
- 4. **Differentiate the CTA buttons** — swap "SHOP NOW" for action + category: "Redeem Free Mascara," "Shop Charlotte Tilbury," "Shop Hydration," so each module has its own destination signal.
- 5. **Add a soft deadline to the mascara offer** — even "While supplies last" beneath "TAP TO REDEEM" creates enough urgency to prevent deferral.
