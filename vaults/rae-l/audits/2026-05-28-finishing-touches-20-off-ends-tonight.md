# Finishing touches—20% off ends tonight
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- This is ILIA's final send on the Friends + Family campaign — the 18th email in roughly seven days — and it earns some credit for actually finding a new narrative hook. "Finishing Touches" works as a last-day metaphor: you're putting the final polish on your cart, not just hearing "sale ends soon" for the eighteenth time. The hero is clean, the LAST CHANCE badge is visible without being garish, and the A Cart Collage section does the most useful work of the email by stacking every benefit (free bronzer at $107, barrier cream at $175, 2× VIP points, +20%, +40%) into one scannable block.
- The structural problem is that three independent sections — Finishing Touches, Choose Your Mini, A Cart Collage — each carry their own CTA, and each CTA competes with the others. A fatigued subscriber who has seen 17 prior emails isn't going to read three sections; they'll skim the hero and either click or delete. The A Cart Collage content, which is the most compelling offer summary in the email, is sitting in the third section where scroll attrition is already significant.
- Clear execution on a template that has worn out its welcome. Good enough to capture the genuinely interested holdouts; invisible to everyone else.

## What's working

- **"Finishing Touches" is the best thematic hook since "Swipe on 20% off"** — it frames the last day as completion, not desperation.
- **A Cart Collage is genuinely useful** — stacking all five benefits with specific spend thresholds ($107/$175) in one section is the clearest loyalty-value summary of the entire campaign.
- **LAST CHANCE stamp on the hero** reinforces urgency without cluttering the copy.
- **VIP upside is prominent** — 25% off in the top banner, 2× points in the body, making the upsell visible at two separate scroll depths.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, text is legible at mobile width.

## What's weak

- **Three competing CTAs (SHOP NOW / SHOP TO CLAIM / SHOP THE SALE)** split attention; after 17 prior emails there is no reason not to consolidate to one decisive button.
- **"Choose Your Mini" section is redundant with A Cart Collage** — both dangle the free-gift angle; one should be cut or merged.
- **A Cart Collage is buried in section three** — the most benefit-dense content is the last thing a fatigued subscriber will reach.
- **Hero copy ("for Friends + Family") is a stale label** after 18 sends; it no longer signals exclusivity.
- **No product close-up or texture shot** — after the eye macro of May 26's "Swipe on 20% off," this hero feels generic by comparison.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut to one CTA and one section.** Kill "Choose Your Mini" as a standalone. Merge the free-mini offer into A Cart Collage so there is a single scroll-stopping block below the hero, with one "SHOP THE SALE >" button. Three CTAs is two too many on a last-day send.
- 2. **Move the VIP spend math to the hero.** "Save 25% + a free bronzer at $107" in the hero subhead is more compelling than "20% off sitewide" repeated for the 18th time.
- 3. **Anchor the headline to the specific deadline.** "Finishing Touches" is good framing but burying "ends tonight" in body copy is a missed opportunity — put a clock or deadline line directly under the headline.
- 4. **Replace "Friends + Family" with a concrete countdown.** "Ends tonight at midnight PT" is more actionable than a campaign label that lost its in-group feel around day four.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off + free bronzer at $107`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only — your free gift is still here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends midnight PT. Stack free gifts at $107 and $175.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `VIP gets 25% off — one click to join free.`
