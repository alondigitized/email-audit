# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email again, with fresher copy and a Halloween offer attached. "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." over "YOUR CART IS STILL WAITING..." is a better hero than the generic "Ready to check out?" from 10/29. The single GO WALK Workout Walker under a navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button gives the email one clear job.
- The weakness is order. The only real incentive, 20% off for Skechers Plus members, sits below a six-shoe grid and a black "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" bar. It's a Plus-only discount that needs a login. Fine print hides the 11/2 deadline. The subject "Your Items Are Waiting!" was already used on 10/23, and it's the fifth cart or browse nudge in about two weeks (10/22 twice, 10/23, 10/26, 10/28, 10/29). A subscriber stops reading these.
- Verdict: competent and clean, but it spends its best card, the discount, in the wrong spot and runs on a recycled subject. A solid 6–7 that could be an 8 with one reorder.

## What's working

- The headline "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." is playful and on-brand, and clearly different from the 10/28 "LET'S MAKE IT OFFICIAL."
- The hero is one shoe with a full-width navy "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button. The CTA is a verb plus a noun and is unambiguous.
- The "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS! 20% OFF FOR SKECHERS PLUS MEMBERS" banner fits the Halloween season. It has its own "SHOP NOW" link and shows "Valid thru 11/2."
- The render is clean. There are no broken images, overlapping text or layout glitches.
- The footer utilities (app download, curbside pickup, Afterpay/Klarna, store finder, text signup) give useful options without cluttering the top.

## What's weak

- The 20% offer is below the fold, after the six-product grid and the black bar. It never touches the cart CTA.
- The discount is Plus-members-only and has to be read in small print ("Must be logged in… online only… exclusions"). A lapsed shopper reading "20% OFF" in the banner will assume it's for them.
- The six "more great options" have tiny captions and no prices. Two black or navy bars ("SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" and the six-row category list) compete with the main CTA.
- The hero image has a lot of empty space above the shoe, which pushes the button down the screen.
- There is no price, review, sizing or stock signal on the abandoned shoe. Nothing says why to finish now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus banner directly under the hero button and tie it to the cart: "Your cart + 20% off with Skechers Plus through 11/2." This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Add the abandoned shoe's price (and the Plus price) under its name, plus a star rating or "Machine washable" style detail.
- 3. Trim the hero's blank image space so the CTA sits higher on mobile.
- 4. Cut the grid to three shoes, or drop it for abandoned-cart sends. Keep one primary button colour so "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" doesn't compete.
- 5. Retire "Your Items Are Waiting!" and vary the subject by send, with a distinct reason to return each time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Workout Walkers + 20% off through 11/2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking it over? Plus members save 20%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members get 20% off online through 11/2. Your cart is saved.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No tricks, just treats: finish your order and save.`
