# Get a free Three Eyed Smiley Mug!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Glossier's fourth gift-with-purchase send in nine days, and the gift has just changed. The hero reads "LIMITED TIME / Get a free Three Eyed Smiley Mug with your $75+ order*". The mug is a pink cup with a red smile, shown in a lifestyle shot with a pastry and a hand. The offer is clear and the creative is charming. The mug replaces the Milky Jelly Cleanser from the 10/22, 10/24 and 10/26 sends, and the $75 threshold is the same.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. A second mug block repeats the hero. Four "Get ahead of gifting" tiles follow: the Wardrobe Kit, the Balm Dotcom Keychain, Just Baked Balm Dotcom and the You Duo. The keychain is the same product that headlined this morning's "There's a new Balm Keychain in town" send. Every tile has an identical "Shop now" button and none shows a price. The result reads as a catalog rather than a single push.
- Verdict: a solid, on-brand send with a clear hero. It's held back by repetition across sends, a diluted lower half, and a threshold that stays hidden behind an asterisk. I'd call it a 6.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: free mug, $75+ order, "LIMITED TIME" label.
- The lifestyle photo of the mug is warm and seasonal and shows the smiley clearly.
- The mug is a fresh gift after three cleanser sends, so the offer feels new.
- The two-column layout with a short headline and one "Shop now" button is easy to scan.
- Brand voice is consistent: casual, friendly copy and the smiley motif carry through to the footer.

## What's weak

- The second mug block repeats the hero and spends scroll space on the same message.
- The four product tiles have no prices and identical "Shop now" buttons. Nothing tells the reader how to reach $75.
- "Limited time" has no end date, so the urgency isn't credible. It follows the 10/26 "Last call" send, which already used up the deadline pressure.
- The body copy in the second block is clipped at the right edge ("holding yo…"), which looks like a render cut-off.
- The Balm Dotcom Keychain was this morning's launch, so the email looks recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the duplicate mug block and replace it with a "How to get yours" strip: add $75 of product, the mug is added automatically, plus a real end date. This is the biggest lift because it addresses both the hidden threshold and the urgency.
- 2. Put prices on the tiles and group them as "Get to $75" picks, for example sub-$25 items, so each product helps the shopper reach the threshold.
- 3. Use specific button copy such as "Claim your mug" in the hero and "Add to bag" on the tiles.
- 4. Fix the clipped text in the second block, or remove the block.
- 5. Space out the gift-with-purchase sends. Four in nine days will teach subscribers to wait for the next one.

- **Subject Alt B:** `A free mug with $75+ (ends Sunday)`

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hot chocolate, makeup brushes, pencils. This mug does it all.`
