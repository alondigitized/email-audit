# Want a free gift?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-with-purchase email with a clear offer. The hero says "Something good, on us!" and "Get our Milky Jelly Cleanser with your $75+ order* for a very limited time." A white Milky Jelly bottle sits next to the copy. The offer is concrete, and it's the first thing the eye reads. It's also a change of pace. Glossier's last four sends (10/15 to 10/20) were all 20% off, and this swaps a percentage discount for a free product.
- The execution is weaker than the offer. "Shop now" appears six times, and every instance is a plain grey outlined button. The "very limited time" has no date. The offer sits behind a $75 threshold and an asterisk. Below the hero, the email turns into a four-product grid (Boy Brow, Ultralip, Cloud Paint, Futuredew) that has nothing to do with the free gift. Those products dilute the one idea the email is about.
- Overall it's a solid, honest concept, but the layout and CTAs are generic. It's a mid-range send. The subject line is short and curious, but nothing in it says the offer needs a $75 spend.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: free Milky Jelly Cleanser with a $75+ order.
- The bottle image next to the copy shows the exact gift, so there's no guessing.
- "Something good, on us!" is warm and fits Glossier's voice.
- The second block ("A super gentle cleansing experience!") gives product benefits: dissolves dirt, oil and makeup, never stripped.
- The product grid is clean and easy to scan, with a name and a short descriptor under each item.
- A GWP is a fresh angle after four straight 20% sends.

## What's weak

- "Very limited time" has no date, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Every CTA is "Shop now" in a pale grey button with low contrast. Earlier sends had a blue button that stood out. None of these CTAs name the cleanser or the offer.
- The body copy in the second block is cut off on the right edge ("ingredi…", "this cleanser dissolves dirt, oil and makeup" runs past the edge). This looks like a visible render bug.
- The four-product grid has nothing to do with the free gift and has no pricing or loyalty content.
- The $75 threshold is only in the hero line, with the asterisk deferred to a tiny "Terms apply" at the very bottom.
- The "A super gentle cleansing experience!" block doesn't say what it costs or how the gift works.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a deadline in the hero, such as "Free through Sunday," and make the CTA "Get my free cleanser." A dated deadline and a named CTA would lift this email most.
- 2. Fix the clipped body copy in the second block.
- 3. Use a high-contrast button (the blue from earlier sends) and use it once or twice, not six times.
- 4. Cut the four-product grid to one or two items that pair with the cleanser, or add a "build your $75 order" strip with price points. As it stands, the grid doesn't help shoppers reach the threshold.
- 5. Show the threshold ("$75+") and the gift's value near the top instead of leaving the explanation to the asterisk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Milky Jelly Cleanser with $75+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free cleanser is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get our Milky Jelly Cleanser on us. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The gentle cleanser fans love, free with your order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-sent template (`stripocdn.email` images, `ctrk.klclick.com` tracking) with sound structure (`lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals). The confirmed defects are missing image alt text, a few CSS quirks, and a hidden-span oddity. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture relay, not from the email itself. The source is truncated, so footer and link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity is unverified.** QA probed 0 of 12 material links ("12 probed, 12 skipped"), so no status codes, redirect chains or landing-page checks exist. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Invalid markup in the body.** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M5J18D5GZ6E06GRHY7JP7MZW_0"></span>` is a `<span>` with `rel`/`href` attributes, which are not valid on a span. It looks like a Klaviyo link-tracking artifact and is harmless in practice. It does mean the tracked URL sits in a non-anchor element that clients may strip.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt.** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M5J18D5GZ6E06GRHY7JP7MZW` is the open-tracking pixel and has no alt text. This is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Six content images have no alt text (confirmed by QA):** `heroimage_1_73u.jpg`, `group_3971_Vsc.jpg`, `group_3986_VDp.jpg`, `group_3987_AUC.jpg`, `group_3986_1_kue.jpg`, `group_3987_1_jxI.jpg`. With images blocked, the hero and the following blocks render blank. Screen-reader users get no content, or just the filename.
- **The Gmail fix selector can't match.** The CSS contains `u + .body img ~ div div { display: none }`, but the tag is a bare `<body>` with no `class="body"`. That hack never applies.
- **Mobile `h2` is small.** The `@media (max-width: 600px)` rule sets `h2 { font-size: 12px !important }`, below the 14px used for `p`/`a` (`.bo p, .bo a`). If `h2` carries real content, it will be hard to read on mobile.
- **No other issues confirmed.** Preheader hiding (`display:none; mso-hide:all`), the dark-mode image rule and the Outlook `PixelsPerInch` block are standard. The markup after the preheader is truncated, so table structure, `role="presentation"` and button fallbacks can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them. That makes it a capture limitation, not a confirmed sender non-compliance. Both should be checked against the raw message from the original delivery.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sending domain is `eml.glossier.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **The footer is not in the truncated source.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link can't be verified here. QA notes an unsubscribe link was included in link ranking, but its URL isn't shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible, because the links are Klaviyo-wrapped and the source is truncated. The probes that would show the final landing URL were all skipped. Nothing here is confirmed as broken.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the six content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Add `class="body"` to `<body>`, or remove the dead `u + .body …` selector.
3. Check whether `h2` content at 12px on mobile is readable. If it is body or offer copy, raise it to 14px or more.
4. Re-run link probing against the top CTAs, and capture the final URLs and UTM parameters after Klaviyo redirects.
5. Capture the raw RFC 822 headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before logging these as sender failures. Treat the three QA warnings as pipeline gaps until then.
