# Good things come in pairs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Glossier send in the sale run that stacks a third discount on top of the sitewide offer. "Stock up on your favorite sets and get 30% off! Plus, get 25% off everything and 30% off $100+ orders and a free Crème de You!" is four offers in one sentence. The "Good things come in pairs" theme is clean, and the bundle grid (Boy Brow Duo, Fragrance Duo, Trio, Two Ways) gives the sale a new angle. It is a real change from the 11/25 "Thoughtful gifts under $25" recap and the 11/22 "Loved by you, now 25% off" review-card send.
- The execution has problems. The hero copy buries the actual news (sets are 30% off) under repeated sale terms. The image badges say "25% OFF EVERYTHING" and "30% OFF $100+", which contradicts the "30% off sets" headline. The grid shows no prices, no savings amounts and no set contents. That is the sixth sale email in about a week, so fatigue is the main risk.
- Verdict: a decent concept with a muddled offer. It lands around a 6, and it would be stronger with one clear number.

## What's working

- The "pairs" theme gives a new reason to click after days of generic "sale is on" sends.
- The hero headline "Two is better than one" is short and ties to the subject.
- The solid blue "Shop sets" button on the hero stands out clearly.
- The 2x2 product grid is easy to scan. Each tile has a name, a short descriptor and its own "Shop now" button.
- The "Get a free Crème de You when you spend $100" banner at the top keeps the gift-with-purchase visible.

## What's weak

- The hero stacks four offers (30% off sets, 25% off everything, 30% off $100+, free Crème de You). The reader can't tell which one applies to them.
- The image badges say "25% OFF EVERYTHING" and "30% OFF $100+", while the copy leads with sets at 30% off. The hero looks inconsistent.
- The four "Shop now" buttons are identical and generic. The tile names are all the same "Choose your scents" and "Grooming pomade" style, with no price, savings or deadline.
- The hero and product images are cropped at the right edge. The top banner text ("spend $100") and the 30% badge are cut off, and the full-width buttons run past the edge in the right column.
- Nothing credible signals urgency, such as a deadline or "ends Monday". The email has no reviews or social proof.
- The "Black Friday Sale" ticker strip near the footer is empty visual weight. It repeats the hero and adds nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the hero to one offer: "Sets are 30% off." Move the 25% sitewide and the $100 gift to a single line below or to the top banner.
- 2. Make the badges match the headline. Show "30% OFF SETS" on the image instead of "25% OFF EVERYTHING."
- 3. Put the savings on the tiles. For example, "Boy Brow Duo, $34 $24" replaces "Grooming pomade." Use CTAs like "Shop the Boy Brow Duo" instead of four identical "Shop now" buttons.
- 4. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Cyber Monday", to the hero and the subject.
- 5. Fix the right-edge cropping on the banner, the hero image and the buttons.
- 6. Add one review line or star rating under the best-selling set.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sets are 30% off. Doubles, trios & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pair up: 30% off Glossier sets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Boy Brow Duo, Fragrance Trio and more. Plus a free Crème de You at $100.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30% off sets through Cyber Monday. Stock up on your favorites.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. The QA run found no hard failures, but 6 images lack alt text and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and most link markup were not available for inspection.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links go through Klaviyo click tracking (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM_0`). The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped 13 (footer, utility, social). It reported no broken or redirecting links among the probed set. The 13 skipped links are unverified.
- Invalid markup in `<body>`: `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` have no meaning on a `<span>`. It looks like a tracking-injection artifact and is harmless but non-conformant.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM`) has no alt attribute, and QA flags it. It should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 images, flagged by QA):**
  - `heroimage_1_8.jpg`
  - `group_3986_15.jpg`
  - `group_3986_13.jpg`
  - `group_3987_16.jpg`
  - `group_3986_14.jpg`
  - the tracking pixel `01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM`

  The five `stripocdn.email` content images need descriptive alt text. If any of them are product or CTA images, the links around them will be unlabeled for screen readers and for image-blocked clients.
- **Doctype/markup mismatch:** The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is declared, but the head uses unclosed HTML-style tags such as `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1" name="viewport">`. Most clients tolerate this.
- **Conditional comments:** `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opens in `<head>`, and its closing `<!--<![endif]-->` sits in `<body>` after the span. Because the comment straddles head and body, some parsers may handle it inconsistently. I couldn't confirm any rendering failure from the source.
- **Mobile CSS:** The mobile rules sit in a standard `@media only screen and (max-width: 600px)` block, and I found nothing broken in the visible CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. Because the source was truncated, the body and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect both headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sender is `gTEAM@eml.glossier.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment couldn't be verified.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** Neither could be checked because they fall in the truncated section. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but it reported no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The links are wrapped in `ctrk.klclick.com` redirects, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs. I can't confirm whether UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present or whether they survive the redirect. The QA data reports no landing-page mismatches among the 12 probed links.

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five `stripocdn.email` images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove or fix the stray `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` in `<body>`.
3. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers, bypassing the relay, to confirm the following:
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present.

4. Capture the full, untruncated HTML so the unsubscribe link, postal address, and footer can be checked. Also resolve the redirect chain on the 13 skipped links and record the final URLs and UTM parameters.
