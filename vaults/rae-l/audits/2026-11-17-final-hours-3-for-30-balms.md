# Final Hours: 3 for $30 balms
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer reminder, but it's the third send for the same promo in six days. It follows 11/12 ("Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!") and 11/15 ("FYI! 3 for $30 balms is still on"). The render is good: a "FINAL HOURS" eyebrow, the headline "Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for just $30," a "save 38%" line, and a lip-balm hero image. Below that sit six flavors in two labeled groups, each with a $16 → $10 badge.
- The urgency is the weak point. Two days ago the brand said "still on." Now it says "final hours" with no date or time anywhere in the body. A subscriber who has seen all three sends will read this as a recycled deadline. The creative is nearly the same as 11/12 with the flavor grid trimmed, so it adds no new reason to act.
- Verdict: a competent, readable email that is mostly a repeat. It will convert people who were already going to buy and do little else.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "FINAL HOURS" sits above "Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for just $30," and the image of a balm being applied shows the product in use.
- The "save 38%" line gives a concrete value claim. 3 × $16 = $48, so $30 is about 37.5% off, which rounds fairly.
- The flavor grids are grouped by intent: "If you're looking for sultry tints" and "If you're looking for subtle favs." This helps shoppers pick without deliberating.
- Short descriptors under each product ("Roasted coffee + mocha tint," "Cool mint and untinted") answer the tint and flavor question quickly.
- The Espresso and Birthday balm shots are strong, craveable, on-brand photography. Nothing is broken or overlapping.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline. "Final hours" gives no date or time, so the urgency isn't credible, especially after "still on" two days earlier.
- Every tile shows "$16 → $10," but the deal only works as a bundle of three. A skimmer could read it as $10 each with no bundle required, or miss that three are needed.
- "Shop now" appears seven times with identical copy. The CTAs are visually weak: pale grey, low-contrast buttons that blend into the page.
- The email never says how the 3-for-$30 is applied, such as auto-discounted in the cart or via a code. It also has no reviews or social proof, despite calling the balms "cult-favorite."
- The creative is essentially a re-send of 11/12 and 11/15, which adds to fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM ET." Add a countdown or date line. This is the single biggest lift, because it makes "final hours" believable.
- 2. Replace the per-tile "$16 → $10" badges with "3 for $30" or "Pick any 3," so the price matches the offer.
- 3. Make the hero CTA a high-contrast filled button with specific copy, such as "Build my 3-pack." Use "Add to bag" on the product tiles.
- 4. Say once how the deal applies: "Discount applied automatically at checkout."
- 5. Add a star rating or a short review quote near the hero to back up "cult-favorite."
- 6. Don't send a third reminder. Consolidate to one launch and one last-call send, or change the creative on the last-call.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: any 3 balms for $30`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 3 Balm Dotcoms, $30 (midnight)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick any 3 flavors, save 38%. Offer ends at 11:59 PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Birthday, Espresso, Wild Fig and more. Mix and match before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

Its main confirmed defects are 9 content images with no alt text and a stray tracking element in `<body>`. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Stray element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7KFTE3V1387PTYDV37CMM4H_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `rel` and `href` have no effect on a `<span>`, so this is a leftover from the ESP's stylesheet and tracking injection. It is harmless but invalid markup.
- **Open pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7KFTE3V1387PTYDV37CMM4H` has no alt attribute (see section 3).
- **Probing coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 17 were skipped as footer, social or utility links. The QA data reports no broken links or redirect failures.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML is cut off, so I can't verify the CTA hrefs or the footer links from it.
