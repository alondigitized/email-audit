# Don't miss these summer deals 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Sally Beauty stacking three separate promotions into one email — a sitewide flash sale ($10 off $50 / $20 off $75, ends July 26), a BOGO on select hair care (ends July 29), a BOGO 50% off Sauce Beauty (ends July 29), and a standalone $29.99 wax bundle — under a subject line that names none of them. Compared to the July 20 "More hair care, more savings" send (6/10), which ran the same core BOGO mechanic but at least kept the email to a single offer, this one piles on three more mechanics and two different end dates, which muddies the "what do I actually click" decision the moment the email opens.
- The top of the email is visually busy: a red flash-sale block immediately followed by a scrolling "ENDS SOON" ticker, then a beige hero panel with a *different* headline ("Last call for summer savings") that doesn't reference the flash-sale code at all. A subscriber skimming on mobile gets three different offer framings before they've even scrolled past the fold. The execution below the fold — the four-product hair care grid, the two-product Sauce Beauty grid, the wax bundle card — is clean and well-photographed, consistent with the brand's usual quality bar (see the July 16 "Summer Must-Haves" send, 8/10, which used similar clean grid layouts).
- The subject line "Don't miss these summer deals 🤩" is the weakest link — it's the vaguest subject this brand has sent in the recent history reviewed, more generic than even "More hair care, more savings." Judgment: solid production values undercut by a cluttered, over-stacked offer structure and a subject line with zero specificity.

## What's working

- Clean, high-quality product photography throughout the hair care and Sauce Beauty grids, consistent with brand standard.
- Each offer has a clear, legible end date (July 26 or July 29), so urgency is at least present in the body even if not in the subject.
- The hero model and warm-weather "waxing must-haves" framing keeps the email seasonally coherent with the brand's July cadence.
- Klarna financing and the in-store/online/delivery icon row are included without cluttering the main offer stack.
- No visible render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy.

## What's weak

- Four distinct offers (flash sale, hair care BOGO, Sauce Beauty BOGO, wax bundle) compete for attention with no single clear hero offer — this is the "everything email" problem, a step backward from the more disciplined single-offer sends this brand has run (July 18, July 24).
- Two different offer end dates (July 26 for the flash sale vs. July 29 for everything else) inside one email creates confusion about what's actually expiring when.
- The subject line references none of the four offers or even the hair care category specifically — it's the most generic subject in the recent history reviewed.
- The scrolling "ENDS SOON" ticker sits directly above a hero panel with an unrelated headline ("Last call for summer savings"), so the first two visual beats of the email don't reinforce each other.
- "Shop now" is repeated as the CTA on nearly every block with no variation, so nothing signals which offer is the priority click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero offer and demote the rest.** Lead with the flash sale ($10/$20 off with code) as the single headline offer, and push the BOGO hair care, BOGO Sauce Beauty, and wax bundle into a secondary "more ways to save" module below. Stacking four mechanics dilutes all of them.
- 2. **Align every end date or clearly separate them.** If the flash sale genuinely ends a different day than the BOGO offers, label the sections "Ends July 26" and "Ends July 29" more prominently so no one assumes one universal deadline.
- 3. **Give the subject line teeth.** Name the strongest, most concrete offer instead of "summer deals."
- **Subject Alt A:** `$20 off $75 + BOGO hair care — ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your flash sale code: SUMMERSAVINGS 🤩`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 2, get 1 free on hair care. Plus $10-$20 off sitewide.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Flash sale ends July 26 — stack your code before it's gone.`
- 4. **Vary the CTA copy per section** ("Shop the flash sale," "Shop hair care BOGO," "Shop wax bundle") instead of repeating "Shop now" five times, so each block reads as a distinct, trackable action.
- 5. **Move the hero headline and the flash-sale banner into agreement** — either lead with the code-based flash sale as the hero, or drop the competing "Last call" headline entirely.
