# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers' cart-abandonment email again, the fourth in four days. The hero is "LET'S MAKE IT OFFICIAL / YOUR CART IS READY WHEN YOU ARE." over the black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" skull sneaker, with a navy COMPLETE YOUR ORDER button. The creative is new, but the cart item is the same one from 11/2 and 11/3. A subscriber would read this as the same nag in different wording.
- The strongest asset is the 25% off for Skechers Plus members, valid 11/3–11/4. It sits in the middle of the email, below the product grid and the black SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS bar. A cart-abandoner should see it right under the hero, because it is the only reason to buy today. As built, the hero gives no reason to act now, and the real deadline is buried.
- The "more options" grid is also weak. The men's UNO skull sneaker is repeated in it, so it is the same shoe twice. Nothing in the email is broken, but it is a generic template with a good offer hidden inside it. I'd call it a competent reminder that wastes its best lever.

## What's working

- The hero copy is clean and direct. "Let's make it official" is a bit more playful than the earlier "Your cart expires soon!" sends.
- One large, linked cart product with a clear navy COMPLETE YOUR ORDER button gives a single obvious action.
- The 25% off for Plus members is a specific offer with a real date window ("2 days only," valid thru 11/4).
- The product grid has six items with names, so there is some choice for people who changed their mind.
- The footer is complete, with category nav, app download, Afterpay/Klarna and a text sign-up.

## What's weak

- The 25% off block sits far below the hero, after the product grid and the black SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS bar.
- "2 days only" is credible, but the first screen has no urgency. It also has no price, no stock cue and no reviews.
- The cart item is the same skull sneaker as in the 11/2 and 11/3 sends. The grid repeats it as the men's version.
- The "Shop all best sellers" bar and the six-category nav compete with the cart CTA. The email has four or five CTAs in a row and no real hierarchy after the hero.
- The Plus-only restriction is visible only in small footer type and a small "See details" line. A non-member would feel baited when they click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 25% Plus offer directly under the hero and tie the cart button to it. For example: "Your cart, 25% off for Plus members through tonight."
- 2. Vary the cart-abandonment sequence. Day 1 is a reminder, day 2 adds social proof or reviews, and day 3 is the offer with a deadline. Stop after three sends.
- 3. Remove the duplicate UNO skull sneaker from the grid and replace it with items related to the cart item.
- 4. Add a price, a star rating and a stock or size cue under the cart item.
- 5. Cut the navigation list and the best-sellers bar so the cart CTA isn't diluted.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off your cart ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your UNO skull sneakers + 25% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 25% through 11/4. Your size is still in stock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last day: finish checkout and take 25% off.`
