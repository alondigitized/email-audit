# Our 2026 Holiday Gift Guide is here!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy, pretty first holiday drop with no offer in it. The hero reads "The 2026 Holiday Gift Guide" with a "Shop now" button. Below it are two real products, the Glossier You Duo and the Mini Glossier You Wardrobe. The photography is warm and on-brand, with pink paper, ribbon and fragrance bottles. As a catalog it's easy to scan.
- The email gives a reader no reason to act today. There's no price, no percentage, no gift-with-purchase and no deadline. The 9/22, 9/26 and 9/28 sends all pushed a free Discovery Kit with a $50+ order, and the Wardrobe is the same product that GWP was built around. Here it shows up as a full-price gift with no incentive. That's a step backward after three sends with a clear hook. The "Gift Guide" is also only two items, and the last block is a "Sign up" teaser, much like the 10/2 "Something festive's right around the corner…" send.
- Verdict: pleasant but thin. It works as a seasonal announcement, not as a conversion email.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and the headline is clear. "The 2026 Holiday Gift Guide" matches the subject, so there's no mismatch.
- The photography is cohesive, with pink paper, ribbon and bottles that feel festive and on-brand.
- The alternating image-left and image-right layout gives the scroll a good rhythm.
- The "GIFT 01 / GIFT 02" labels make the guide structure obvious.
- The cookie block adds some personality and hints at more drops to come.

## What's weak

- No price, offer or deadline appears anywhere, so there's no urgency.
- The Glossier You Duo body copy is clipped at the right edge ("signatur", "T the perfect gift"), which is a visible render bug.
- The "Shop now" buttons are identical, grey and low-contrast. They don't name the product, so the eye isn't pulled to them.
- Only two gifts make a thin "guide," with no price tiers, recipients or skincare/makeup picks.
- The "Sign up" button on the "Keep your eyes peeled…" block is confusing for someone already subscribed. It repeats the 10/2 teaser pattern.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hook. Bring back the free Discovery Kit or set a gift-set price, and put it in the hero headline.
- 2. Fix the clipped copy in the Glossier You Duo block so the full sentence is readable.
- 3. Show prices on both products and rename the buttons ("Shop the You Duo", "Shop the Wardrobe").
- 4. Expand the guide with 4–6 gifts, grouped by price (under $30, under $50) or by recipient.
- 5. Replace "Sign up" with a useful action, such as an early-access or gift-reminder signup, or cut the block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gift sets are here — from $28`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our first holiday gifts just landed 🎁`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Glossier You Duo, the Mini Wardrobe and more, wrapped and ready.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fragrance sets that arrive gift-ready. Shop the first drop.`
