# Enjoy 25% off EVERYTHING
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the sale-is-live send that the last two emails set up. On 11/18 ("Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members") and earlier today ("Final hours for early access"), the 25% hook was gated behind membership. Now it is open to everyone, and the subject gets straight to it: "Enjoy 25% off EVERYTHING." The hero leads with the offer, a blue "Shop Black Friday" button, and a 25% OFF badge on a lip product.
- The render has real problems, though. The right edge is clipped throughout. The top banner reads "…when you spend $100" and the text cuts off. The "25% OFF EVERYTHING" badge is cut off. The right-hand "Shop now" buttons run off the edge. The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" marquee band is cut off. The gift block's headline reads "Get a free Crème de You w…" and its body copy is cut off. The layout is wider than the viewport. The product grid is also generic: four tiles with no prices, no sale pricing and no sense of what 25% saves.
- Overall it's a clear, on-brand sale announcement, but the clipping hurts credibility and the "*" fine print is buried. Compared with the 11/12–11/17 balm sends, which had one specific offer and crossed-out prices, this one is vaguer.

## What's working

- The headline "Enjoy 25% off everything*" is large and sits in the top-left, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- Brand voice and styling are consistent: the Glossier wordmark, saturated blue buttons and clean product photography.
- The hero has one blue "Shop Black Friday" button, so the primary CTA is unambiguous.
- Two added incentives (30% off $100+, and a free Crème de You with $100+ orders) give a basket-building reason to spend more.
- The "Community Favorites" grid mixes categories (fragrance, lip, skincare, brow), and each tile has a short descriptor.

## What's weak

- Content is clipped at the right edge: the top banner, the 25% badge, the right-column "Shop now" buttons, the marquee band and the gift headline and body copy.
- The 25% badge and the "30% OFF $100+" badge sit in the hero together, which muddles the offer. It's unclear whether it's 25% or 30%.
- The product tiles show no prices or discounted prices, and every one has the same generic "Shop now" button.
- The offer's conditions are only marked with "*" and tiny "Terms apply" text at the very bottom, so the exclusions are hidden.
- Three of the four hero-grid pictures are abstract (cream texture, eye close-up), which makes the products harder to identify.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the layout so nothing clips at the right edge. This is the biggest single lift, since the banner, badge and gift copy all lose text.
- 2. Pick one headline offer. Use "25% off everything" in the hero and move the "30% off $100+" tier into a clearly separate strip below.
- 3. Add sale prices to the product tiles, for example "$24 → $18," and name the product in the button ("Shop Balm Dotcom").
- 4. Put the key exclusions in plain text next to the offer, not just behind the "*".
- 5. Acknowledge the cadence. After two membership-teaser sends, open with "It's live" and a deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `It's live: 25% off all of Glossier`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday is here, 25% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Crème de You when you spend $100. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Balm Dotcom, Glossier You and more, all 25% off through Cyber Monday.`
