# Brand Emails Overload

Today’s chat wasn’t quite what I expected. Someone reached out asking about brand emails and how they impact my shoe collection journey. It got me thinking about the countless promotional messages from Nike, Adidas, Yeezy, and others that clutter my inbox daily. These emails are both a blessing and a curse; they keep me informed but also bombard me with too many options to choose from. I shared some tips on filtering out less relevant brands and using email management tools to stay organized. It’s all about finding the right balance between staying connected and not getting overwhelmed by constant notifications.
