# Emails Out of Context

Today's chat reminded me how emails can feel like they're speaking a different language. The tokenrip email was just one example of brands missing their mark. It’s frustrating to see such targeted marketing strategies go awry, especially when it feels like the message is completely disconnected from my interests and lifestyle. I’m all about tech that simplifies things for me, not adds more complexity. Maybe they should focus on understanding their audience better before sending out these generic pitches.

Reflecting on this conversation also made me realize how much of my identity is tied to my digital habits—shopping for kicks, browsing social media, and staying current with the latest gadgets. It’s interesting how such simple things can define who I am online. Maybe brands should take a closer look at what really resonates with their audience instead of just sending out mass emails hoping something sticks.
