# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Got it. Here's my take.
- Landed on Skechers.com and the first thing I see is a mom-and-daughter photo with "The Mother's Day Shop" in pink script — beautiful campaign, completely not for me. Skechers is clearly a big-tent brand doing seasonal gifting pushes, which is smart business, but if I showed up here looking for my next pair of slip-in sneakers, this page is telling me to come back in a few weeks. The BOGO banner at the top does make me pause though — that's real money.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — the blue sticky bar is the first thing I read, and "Applied at Cart" removes friction. I'm not doing math or hunting for a promo code.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — full-width black button, above the fold, no ambiguity. Even if the destination isn't for me, the UX pattern is solid.
- **Secondary banner ("25% OFF Apparel")** — two offers stacked = I know there's a sale going on. Skechers is actively discounting, which registers before I see a single product.
- **Clean mobile render** — no layout shift, no janky fonts, no spinner. On my phone this feels snappy and intentional.
- **Timely campaign** — Mother's Day is right now. The brand is in the moment, not running last month's creative.

## What's weak

- **Hero has zero relevance to me** — "The Mother's Day Shop" in pink cursive with flower borders is a gifting destination for someone else. I'm here for my next pair of slip-ins, not a gift for my mom.
- **Men's category is completely invisible** — navigation is behind a hamburger MENU. There's no category strip, no "Men's | Women's | Kids" quick-tap bar. One extra tap to even find my section.
- **No slip-in / hands-free product signal anywhere above the fold** — Skechers' biggest differentiator in the male casual space doesn't appear. If I found this brand on TikTok because of Hands Free Slip-ins, this page doesn't confirm I'm in the right place.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no "free returns," no review count on featured products. For a first-time mobile visitor this could be a "is this site legit?" moment.
- **Apparel promo feels mismatched** — I came for shoes, and one of the two visible offers ("25% OFF Apparel") is for clothes. Dilutes the focus.

## Recommendations

- **Add a gender-split tap bar below the hero** — "Men | Women | Kids" in a horizontal scroll strip so I can get to my section in one tap without touching the hamburger menu. Would take an afternoon to ship.
- **Swap the secondary banner copy** to include a shoe-specific hook: "BOGO 50% OFF + Free Shipping on orders $75+" — free shipping threshold is probably there, just not surfaced.
- **Inject a "New in Men's Slip-ins" thumbnail rail** just below the hero, even on the Mother's Day homepage. The gifting hero can stay — just give male shoppers a handoff point instead of a dead end.
- **Add a trust strip** (stars, number of reviews, "Free Returns") between the hero and CTA. Mobile shoppers bounce when they can't quickly answer "is this worth trusting?"
