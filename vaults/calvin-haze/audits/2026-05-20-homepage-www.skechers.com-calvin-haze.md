# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is running a Memorial Day "Red, White & Blue Collection" push and honestly the timing is dead-on — opened this on May 20 and the campaign feels fresh, not stale. The BOGO and members-only banner are right at the top so the deal registers immediately. That said, if I came here because I saw a slip-in drop on TikTok, I'd have to bounce past this patriotic hero to find it — the homepage isn't directly speaking to the thing Skechers is most famous for right now.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — first thing I see, no ambiguity, no math required. That's how you surface a deal on mobile.
- **"Members Only – Now until 5/25!" banner** — time-bounded AND tied to a dollar reward. Two conversion levers in one line.
- **"Slip-ins" in the nav** — whoever put that first in the tab row knew what they were doing. One tap from the homepage to my actual category.
- **Male lifestyle hero shot** — guy's in jeans and a denim jacket with clean white Skechers, sitting relaxed. That's my Saturday. It doesn't feel like a Nike performance ad or a dad-shoe clearance rack — it looks like a weekend I'd actually have.
- **Seasonal timing** — Red White & Blue on May 20 is sharp. This could've easily been a stale winter-clearance page. It's not.

## What's weak

- **No primary CTA button in the hero** — the big patriotic imagery has no "Shop the Collection" or "Shop Men's" button attached. I'm expected to either scroll or use the nav tabs, which means drop-off for anyone not already motivated.
- **Hero doesn't push the slip-in angle** — Skechers' biggest differentiator for someone like me is hands-free technology. "Red, White & Blue Collection" doesn't say that. I came for the shoe innovation; they're leading with seasonal color palette.
- **"G..." nav tab cut off** — that nav row is truncating. On my phone I can see Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs, and then a clipped "G". That's a UX fracture on mobile — the category I can't see might be the one I want.
- **No trust signals visible** — no review count, no free-returns badge, no "as seen on" credibility. For a new visitor on mobile, that gap is real.
- **Members reward feels gated** — "$5 Reward on $75+ Login" only works if I'm already a member. No "Sign up" language visible, so non-members might scan past it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Men's Slip-ins →" button directly inside the hero image** — doesn't need to replace the collection branding, just anchor the hero to an actionable destination for the biggest product category.
- **Fix the nav tab overflow** — either widen the scroll area or swap the label order so the most-tapped categories (Slip-ins, Men's) stay visible without truncation.
- **Surface "Free Shipping" or "Free Returns" in the top banner strip** — BOGO is strong but adding a one-liner like "Free shipping on $50+" or "Free returns always" immediately after gives me a reason to trust the cart before I've even scrolled.
- **Swap the second banner to include a non-member "Join free" nudge** — something like "Members Only · $5 Reward on $75+ · [Login] or [Join free]" so I can see the path in without being told it's not for me.
