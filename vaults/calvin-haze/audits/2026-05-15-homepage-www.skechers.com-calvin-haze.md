# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Opened Skechers.com and honestly, the hero did its job — Hands Free Slip-ins front and center is exactly what I've been seeing everywhere on IG and it's exactly why I'm here. BOGO 50% in the first banner is hard to scroll past. But past that first screen it's basically a blank check — everything interesting is buried below the fold on this mobile view.

## What's working

- **Slip-ins hero lead:** "HANDS FREE Slip-ins GLIDE-STEP" is the headline and I don't have to go looking for it — it's the entire screen. This is the tech I actually hunt for.
- **BOGO 50% banner:** Top of the page, blue, impossible to miss. That deal makes me want to bring Martha along mentally — buy one pair each, half off the second.
- **Spring Apparel Sale strip:** Secondary banner is tidy, not screaming. Gives me a reason to linger even if I'm shoe-first.
- **"Men" tab in the bottom nav:** One tap to get to my section. Mobile navigation is dead simple.
- **"SHOP NOW" button:** Full-width, high-contrast, sits right under the hero. No hunting for the CTA.

## What's weak

- **Hero splits male/female 50-50:** I'm shopping for me (and watching Martha's wishlist secondarily). The female model by the pool gets equal billboard space, which dilutes the targeting. A guy-forward hero would've hooked me harder.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic:** In the context of Slip-ins the intent is clear, but it doesn't say "Shop Men's Slip-ins" — I don't know if I land on men's, women's, or a gender-neutral landing page.
- **No free shipping mention:** That's a real purchase decision for me and it's nowhere above the fold. I have to assume or hunt.
- **Zero social proof above the fold:** No review counts, no "10 million pairs sold," no ratings. For a $70-120 shoe, trust signals matter and they're invisible here.
- **25% off Apparel feels off-topic:** My session is about shoes. The secondary banner is doing work for a different category I didn't come here for.

## Recommendations

- **Make the SHOP NOW gender-aware:** Change the CTA to "Shop Men's Slip-ins" or add a two-up button split ("Shop Men / Shop Women") below the hero — it takes the guesswork out and boosts tap confidence.
- **Add a free shipping line to the hero:** Even a small "Free shipping on orders $50+" tucked under the CTA button converts fence-sitters. It's invisible right now.
- **Drop a star-rating strip under the hero:** "Rated 4.7 stars by 2M+ walkers" or similar — one line, before the nav tabs. The Slip-ins line has reviews; surface one number here.
- **Lean the hero male:** Keep the split image but give the male model the dominant left-side frame and make the product on-foot the focal point. Right now the female model by the pool reads like a fashion/lifestyle brand, not a comfort-tech pitch for guys.
