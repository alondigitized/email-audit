# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is going hard on Memorial Day weekend right now — the Red, White & Blue Collection is front and center, and you feel that patriotic energy the moment the page loads. As a brand it reads cleaner and more adult than I expected from Skechers; the male model in the hero has my exact energy: denim jacket, casual jeans, relaxed. The BOGO 50% banner at the very top tells me this weekend is a good time to buy.

## What's working

- **"Slip-ins" is the first tab in the category nav** — the thing I specifically came to look at is one tap away, front row, no digging. That's huge.
- **BOGO 50% banner leads the page** — it's the first thing I read before even the logo. Skechers is making the deal impossible to miss, which is how I want to be greeted on a holiday weekend.
- **Male hero model earns the look** — denim jacket, white tee, relaxed jeans. He looks like someone I'd see at a coffee shop Saturday morning. That's me. That's Martha's husband.
- **"Now until 5/25!" deadline** on the Members reward creates real urgency without being pushy — a specific date beats "limited time" every time.
- **Patriotic campaign is right on time** — I was literally just seeing Red White Blue drops on TikTok this week. The page reflects the cultural moment.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the hero** — the collection title and models are there but I'm left hunting for "Shop the Collection" or "Shop Slip-ins." The nav tabs are workable but a hero without a button is a friction point.
- **The Members reward feels thin** — $5 on $75+ is not moving the needle when the BOGO is 50% off. The two offers feel uncoordinated, and the Members pitch gets buried beneath a bigger deal.
- **Hero copy sells season, not product benefit** — "The Red, White & Blue Collection" tells me nothing about fit, comfort technology, or why I should care about this drop vs. any other patriotic shoe line.
- **No slip-in or hands-free callout above the fold** — the tab is there but zero hero messaging around slip-in tech, which is Skechers' actual differentiator and my #1 shopping intent.
- **Female model gets equal real estate** — I get it, but half the hero is aimed at someone who isn't me, which dilutes the targeting.

## Recommendations

- **Add a hero CTA button**: "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop the Collection" directly under the title — mobile shoppers need something to tap, not just a nav tab to hunt for.
- **Lead with the technology, not just the collection**: Swap one of the hero text lines to something like "Slip into the Weekend — No Laces, No Bending" so the Slip-in benefit hooks people with my intent.
- **Consolidate the two offer banners**: Combine BOGO + Members reward into a single "Members get BOGO 50% — Log in to activate" strip. It reads cleaner and drives account creation.
- **Pull one slip-in product into the hero as a close-up**: A single clean shoe shot — ideally a clean white or navy sneaker from the Red White Blue line — gives me something to visualize wearing this weekend.
