# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Alright, first thing I see is exactly what I came for — Hands Free Slip-ins front and center, with a guy who looks like he could be me, chilling in some clean low-profile slides. Skechers is clearly pushing this Glide-Step Slip-ins campaign hard, and the BOGO 50% banner at the top means they want me to buy now. Feels like a brand that knows its moment and is going for it.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — that blue bar at the very top is the first thing I see. Applied at cart means no code hunting. That's how it should work.
- **Hands Free Slip-ins hero** — this is exactly the technology I follow on TikTok. Seeing it in the hero validates I'm in the right place the second the page loads.
- **Male model in the hero** — he looks my age, casual fit, relaxed. Not a marathon runner, not a retiree. That visual fit matters to me.
- **"Now until 5/25!" deadline on the members reward** — a real date is a real reason to act. I respect that.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — that tells me Skechers knows I've been here before and wants to bring me back into my own browse history instantly.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — full-width, high-contrast, no ambiguity. One tap and I'm moving.

## What's weak

- **"Women" tab is underlined/active in the category nav** — I land on the page and the default state says Women. Minor but disorienting for me as a male shopper.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — does it go to Slip-ins? Men's? The brand? That button could say "Shop Men's Slip-ins" and I'd feel way more confident tapping it.
- **No trust signals above the fold** — no star ratings, no "free returns," no review count on the hero product. For a tech-forward shoe drop, I want some social proof before I commit.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I shouldn't have to hunt for that; it's a basic conversion lever missing from the fold.
- **The hero is split between male and female models** — the female in the floral dress reads more lifestyle/fashion. Fine, but the Slip-ins story feels slightly diluted when it's trying to speak to both of us at once.

## Recommendations

- **Change the CTA from "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Men's Slip-ins"** — or at minimum "Shop Slip-ins" — so I know the tap is taking me directly into the product category I care about. One word change, measurable CTR lift.
- **Default the category nav to "Men" when the hero features a male model** — or better yet, personalize based on prior session. I have a "Recently Viewed" tab, so you already know who I am.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the top bar alongside the BOGO** — something like "Free shipping $50+" next to the BOGO. That bar is already doing work; make it do more.
- **Drop a review count or star rating badge on the hero image** — even a small "★ 4.7 | 12,400 Reviews" overlay on the Slip-ins shot gives me the social proof that moves a millennial who's been influenced on Instagram but needs one more signal to tap.
