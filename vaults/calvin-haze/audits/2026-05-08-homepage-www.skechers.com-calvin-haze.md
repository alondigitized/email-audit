# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- I open Skechers and get a flower-covered Mother's Day landing page — warm, sweet, totally not me. This reads like a gift-guide destination, not a place for a guy hunting Slip-ins for a Saturday coffee run. The deals are real (BOGO 50% is hard to ignore), but the whole visual frame is pointed squarely at the woman in my life, not at me.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — top of the page, blue bar, impossible to miss. Even if I'm not the intended audience, that offer makes me pause before bouncing.
- **25% OFF Apparel strip** — a second deal stacked immediately below the nav. Two promos without scrolling is solid.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — the button is large, high contrast (dark text on light), and unambiguous. Tap target is generous on mobile.
- **Clean render** — the page loads crisp. The floral photo is sharp, the typography is legible, nothing is broken or jittery.
- **Mother's Day timing** — if I'm here to grab something for Martha, this campaign landing page actually works. The relevance to the calendar is spot on.

## What's weak

- **Zero men's entry point above the fold** — no "Men's" pill, no "Shop Men's Slip-ins," nothing. I have to go digging through a hamburger menu to find myself on this site.
- **Apparel promo strip** — I came for shoes, not a hoodie. "25% OFF Apparel" is noise to me; it makes the page feel like it's diluting its own shoe identity.
- **Imagery is entirely female** — mom, daughter, pink flowers, pink shoes. If I opened this on TikTok I'd assume it was a women's brand. No hook for the guy scrolling for his next pair.
- **"SHOP NOW" is vague** — it presumably drops me into the Mother's Day collection. I want a shortcut to Men's Hands Free Slip-ins, not a detour through gifting.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no "free returns," no "ships in 2 days." The page gives me nothing to reduce friction if I were close to buying.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Men's / Slip-ins pill row just below the hero** — even a horizontal chip strip ("Men's | Slip-ins | New Arrivals") would let me self-route in one tap without blowing up the Mother's Day layout.
- **Swap or split the SHOP NOW CTA** — two buttons side by side: "Shop Her Gift" and "Shop Men's Slip-ins." Both audiences stay served without a full redesign.
- **Bring trust signals up** — "Free returns · Ships in 2 days" deserves a one-line strip directly above or below the CTA button. It costs nothing to render and reduces bounce for first-timers.
- **Rotate the secondary banner copy** — "25% OFF Apparel" buried under the shoe brand leads with the wrong category. Test "BOGO 50% — Shoes + More" to keep the foot-first positioning.
