# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I open Skechers expecting to see what's new in Slip-ins or maybe some fresh colorways I've been seeing on the 'gram, and instead I get a Mother's Day gifting campaign front and center. I get it — it's May, it's timely — but I'm a guy who came here for himself, and nothing above the fold is talking to me. The brand reads comfortable and family-oriented right now, which is fine, but I'm not feeling that streetwear-meets-function energy I associate with the Slip-ins line.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — the blue sticky strip is the first thing I see and a genuine deal. Even if the campaign isn't for me, I'm filing that offer away.
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary banner** — two stacked offers before I even hit the hero? That's aggressive in a good way. Stacks as a reason to at least browse.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — clean, full-width, high contrast. No guessing where to tap. On mobile that matters.
- **Mother's Day timing is accurate** — the campaign is literally for today's date. It doesn't feel stale.
- **Recently Viewed tab** — even half-visible, the fact that it's there tells me the site remembers me. That's friction reduction.

## What's weak

- **100% female-coded hero** — mom in pink Skechers, daughter in pink dress, pink flowers everywhere. I'm not the target of this shot, and Skechers has a huge men's business that's completely invisible here.
- **"The Mother's Day Shop" hero copy** — as someone who came here to shop for myself, this read sends me sideways. Not a gifting trip today.
- **No men's navigation shortcut visible** — the only nav is a hamburger MENU. One extra tap to find Men's shoes when I'm already impatient on my phone.
- **No slip-in / hands-free product cue anywhere above fold** — the whole Hands Free Slip-ins line is Skechers' signature tech differentiator right now, and it's completely absent from the opening screen.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no "free returns," no shipping threshold callout. The deal banners are there but there's nothing reassuring me about the transaction.
- **No urgency on the Mother's Day offer** — no "ends May 11" or countdown. If I'm gift-shopping, I want to know how much time I have.

## Recommendations

- **Run a split-tile hero on mobile** — top tile for Mother's Day gifting (current), bottom tile for "New in Slip-ins for Men" or "Fresh drops for you." Martha and I shop together on weekends; serve both of us.
- **Add a persistent nav tab row below the header** — "Men | Women | Slip-ins | Sale" as a swipeable pill row, so I can get to my category in one tap without hitting the hamburger menu.
- **Drop a Slip-ins lifestyle chip on the hero** — a small badge or secondary CTA ("Shop Hands Free Slip-ins →") overlaid on the hero would catch my eye without disrupting the Mother's Day campaign.
- **Add a deadline to the BOGO banner** — "BOGO 50% OFF · Ends May 12 · Applied at Cart" turns a passive discount into urgency and removes the "is this still live?" doubt.
