# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers in full collab-drop mode — the Romero Britto art collection is front and center, very editorial, almost fashion-week energy. The BOGO 50% banner and the Slip-ins tab in the nav tell me they know their business. But the hero is doing absolutely nothing for me — I came here after seeing slip-on streetwear on TikTok, and I'm being greeted by a woman in paint-splattered pop-art kicks posing like she's at Art Basel.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% sticky bar** — that blue strip at the very top is the first thing I read. I'm already calculating whether Martha and I could each grab a pair.
- **"Slip-ins" in the category nav** — it's the first tab. One tap. That's exactly the category I came here for, and it's surfaced without having to dig through MENU.
- **Member Month $250 Gift Card Giveaway** — even though I'm not a loyalty completionist, a $250 giveaway with a deadline of 6/28 creates a reason to actually make an account right now.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the stacked hierarchy (promo bar → header → member banner → category nav → hero) is logical and doesn't fight itself.

## What's weak

- **The hero is the wrong story for me** — Skechers Britto is a collab for someone else. The shoes are wildly colorful pop-art prints, not the clean slip-in silhouettes I've been watching on IG. I feel like I landed on the wrong page.
- **No CTA button in the hero** — the Britto hero is a dead zone. There's no "Shop the Collab" button, no "Men's Britto" link, nothing tappable in the actual image area. I have to already know to tap "Slip-ins" in the nav.
- **Female-only model** — "Available for Men and Women" is written on the hero, but the only human visible is a young woman. That text is doing a lot of heavy lifting.
- **No product price anchoring** — I can see a BOGO deal but I have no idea what the actual price of anything is. Even a "Starting at $65" would help me decide if the BOGO is worth engaging.
- **Trust signals are thin** — no star ratings, no "Free shipping on $X," no returns callout. All the things that would push me from browsing to buying are absent above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero or add a Men's Slip-ins module directly beneath it** — a lifestyle shot of a guy in a clean outfit slipping into a pair at the gym door or coffee shop would speak directly to me. The Britto collab is fine as a secondary module, not the lede.
- **Add a "Shop Slip-ins for Men →" CTA button inside or below the hero** — the nav tab is not enough; give me a button I can tap without thinking.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold somewhere above the fold** — even in the existing promo bar, "BOGO 50% OFF + Free shipping on $X" would close the loop on whether this deal is actually worth acting on now.
- **Show one product with a price in the hero or immediately below it** — even one card with an image, name, and BOGO price would anchor the value and pull me into the PDP.
