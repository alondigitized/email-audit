# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is playing this like a streetwear drop, not a shoe-store homepage — big hero shot of a guy in an oversized jacket and joggers coming down some stairs next to a chunky new sneaker launch. It reads clean and current, closer to what I'd see reposted on a sneaker IG account than a typical "Buy 1 Get 1" footwear site. Right now they're pushing the D'Lites 330 launch plus a stacked "up to 40% off" sitewide sale.

## What's working

- **The hero lifestyle shot** — guy in a beanie and oversized jacket coming down stairs by the Skechers HQ sign. That's exactly the kind of scroll-stopping content I'd see on TikTok, not a stock "runner mid-stride" shot.
- **The nav pill row** (Slip-ins, Aero Burst, Arch Fit, BOBS, GO WALK) sits right under the top banner — I can jump straight to Slip-ins without hunting through a menu.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** on the D'LITES 330 hero is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.
- **"LIMITED TIME ONLY! Up to 40% off"** banner is specific enough to make me want to tap "Shop" instead of scrolling past.

## What's weak

- **Product grid defaults to Women's**, not Men's — I land on the homepage and the first four shoes I see are all women's colorways at $110. I have to actively tap "Men" to see my stuff.
- **The hero shoe itself is a laced D'Lites 330**, not the slip-in tech I actually hunt for — the "Slip-ins" branding only shows up in the product cards below, so the headline moment doesn't speak to my main focus area.
- **No loyalty/rewards visible up top** — I'm not a completionist, but if there's a member price or points hook I'd want to see it without digging.
- **No review stars or ratings on any product card** — for $110 sneakers I'd want some social proof before I even tap in.

## Recommendations

- Default the product carousel to "Men" (or make it gender-neutral/alternating) instead of opening on Women's every time.
- Swap the hero product shot for an actual slip-in silhouette, or add a one-line "no laces, no bending down" callout next to SHOP NOW — that's the tech hook that gets me to tap.
- Add star ratings/review counts to the product cards — even a small "4.6★ (2.1k)" under the price would push me toward a tap.
- Surface a loyalty/rewards badge or member-price tag near the top offer banner so returning shoppers feel recognized.
