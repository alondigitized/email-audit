# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first scroll and I'm not mad at this. Skechers is clearly going all-in on Hands Free Slip-ins right now — that's the whole hero — and as someone who's been eyeing those for the gym and Sunday errands, they caught me. This feels like a brand that knows who's buying slip-ins and isn't wasting my time with a cluttered homepage. The double-promo banner situation up top is a bit aggressive, but the offer is real.

## What's working

- **Hands Free Slip-ins hero** — this is the exact technology I search for on TikTok. Leading with "GLIDE-STEP" above a lifestyle shot of a dude who looks like he's heading to a coffee run is dead-on for me.
- **BOGO 50% OFF banner** — it's pinned to the very top, always visible. That's not a small deal and I notice it immediately.
- **Male model in the hero** — mid-30s, casual outfit, relaxed setting. He doesn't look like a performance athlete or a senior citizen. That's me.
- **"Men | Women | Kids' | Recently Viewed" tab row** — one tap to Men's. Clean. Mobile-native thinking.
- **Full-width "SHOP NOW" CTA** — the button fills the screen width. On mobile that's the right call.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — I'm staring at a Slip-ins hero and the button says "SHOP NOW." It should say "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Men's Slip-ins." I don't know if that button drops me on a Slip-ins PLP or the generic homepage.
- **No free shipping callout** — I need to know if I'm paying for shipping before I get emotionally invested. That threshold should live on this screen.
- **25% Off Apparel doesn't move me** — I'm here for shoes, not a Spring apparel sale. That secondary banner feels misaligned with the hero below it.
- **No social proof above the fold** — not a single star rating, not a "4.7 out of 5 from 30K reviews." For a brand competing in the slip-in space, that's a miss.
- **No urgency** — BOGO 50% is a great offer but there's zero deadline. No "ends Sunday," no countdown. I might bookmark and ghost.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Men's Slip-ins"** — specificity on the CTA costs nothing and immediately closes the gap between what I'm looking at and where I land.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the top banner** — even just "Free Shipping Over $75" next to the BOGO copy. It's the #1 friction point before tapping into a PLP.
- **Drop a deadline or urgency signal on the BOGO banner** — "BOGO 50% OFF · Ends Monday" would get me to act faster instead of keeping it in a mental wish list.
- **Add a star-rating badge to the hero** — something like "⭐ 4.7 · 90K+ Reviews" on the Slip-ins product shown. If you're betting the whole above-the-fold on one product line, back it up with social proof right there.
