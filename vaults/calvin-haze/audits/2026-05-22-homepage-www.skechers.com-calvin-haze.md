# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is going full Memorial Day mode right now — red, white, and blue collection front and center, BOGO banner at the top, and a clean two-shot hero. The brand reads approachable and slightly lifestyle-adjacent, not high-end, not discount-bin. For me specifically, it's solid because I can literally see "Slip-ins" as the first nav tap and there's a dude my age wearing clean white sneakers in the hero — but the page doesn't give me a reason to buy *those* shoes specifically, just vibes around a seasonal campaign.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF sticky strip** — first thing I see, no hunting required. That's a legit deal and it doesn't make me work for it.
- **"Slip-ins" as the first nav tab** — I don't have to dig through Men's → Categories → Hands-Free. It's surfaced right at the top, which is exactly what I came for.
- **Male hero model** — casual denim jacket, rolled jeans, white low-profile sneakers. He looks like someone I'd pass at a coffee shop. That's the vibe I'm buying into.
- **Memorial Day timing** — the "Now until 5/25!" on the members reward banner and the Red/White/Blue campaign is tight with the holiday weekend. Feels current, not lazy.
- **Members reward banner** — $5 back on $75 is modest but it tells me there's a reason to log in, which is a signal to return shoppers.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button on the hero** — "THE RED, WHITE & BLUE COLLECTION" is just a headline. I don't know where to tap in the hero block to actually browse it. That's friction.
- **Hero copy says nothing about Slip-ins or hands-free tech** — it's all seasonal/patriotic. If I came here from a TikTok about Slip-ins, I'm not being reinforced at all in the hero.
- **No trust signals visible** — no star ratings, no "Free Returns," no "X people bought this today." For a first-timer on mobile, that's a cold landing.
- **Members reward is login-gated** — the strip says "Login | details," which adds a step. If I'm not already logged in I'm probably skipping this entirely.
- **The nav tabs cut off** — I can see Slip-ins, Sandals, Arch Fit, BOBs, and then it's clipped. There's clearly more but I can't see what without swiping. On a small phone that's a bit janky.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Slip-ins" button directly on the hero image** — the male model is wearing them, tie the visual to the product with one tap.
- **Swap the generic patriotic subhead for a benefit line** — something like "Slip on. Walk out. No bending." under the collection name would speak directly to why I'm here.
- **Surface one product card in the above-fold area** — even a single featured Slip-in with a price and star rating would dramatically lower bounce for guys like me who came with intent.
- **Pre-apply the BOGO to the Slip-ins nav** — small badge or label on the "Slip-ins" tab saying "BOGO 50%" so I know the deal applies to my category before I tap in.
