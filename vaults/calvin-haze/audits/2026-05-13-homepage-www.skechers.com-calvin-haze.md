# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Alright, first screen and they're already speaking my language — "Hands Free Slip-ins Glide-Step" is literally the technology I look up on TikTok at 11pm. This reads like a lifestyle brand that knows its product is the hook and isn't hiding it. Right now they're pushing spring apparel and a BOGO deal, and the page feels fresh, not like they forgot to swap out the winter banner.

## What's working

- **"HANDS FREE Slip-ins GLIDE-STEP" hero copy** — this is exactly the product language I look for. They put the technology front and center, not a vague brand slogan. That earns attention immediately.
- **BOGO 50% Off banner at top** — auto-applied at cart means no promo code hunting. That alone keeps me from bouncing to check if there's a better deal somewhere else.
- **Dual-lifestyle imagery** — a young guy in casual gear sitting poolside-adjacent, a woman in a sundress. It reads like a Saturday, which is when Martha and I actually shop.
- **"Men" and "Recently Viewed" tabs in the nav strip** — I can tap either within a second. No buried hamburger menu archaeology.
- **25% Off Apparel secondary banner** — layers a second deal on top of the BOGO without cluttering the hero.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — the hero is laser-focused on Slip-ins, but the button just says SHOP NOW. If it said "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Men's Slip-ins," that's a cleaner handoff from scroll to click.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I have no idea what the threshold is before I tap through. That's friction I notice, especially when BOGO math can get tricky at checkout.
- **No social proof visible** — no star ratings, review counts, or "bestseller" badges anywhere in this viewport. For a product category I'm considering based on TikTok hype, even one "4.7 stars" would push me.
- **Imagery leans women-forward** — the woman in the floral dress takes up more visual real estate than the male model, and she's styled fashion-first. For a guy who wants to see what these look like on someone at the gym or grabbing coffee, the vibe is slightly off.

## Recommendations

- **Swap generic CTA for category-specific copy** — change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Men's Slip-ins" so the path from hero to product page is obvious. One word change, ships this week.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the top banner or below the CTA** — even "Free shipping on orders over $X" in small text under the SHOP NOW button removes a mental blocker before I even tap.
- **Drop a star rating or bestseller badge on the hero module** — something like "4.8 ★ — 45,000+ reviews" anchored to the Glide-Step name would validate the TikTok hype I walked in with.
- **Swap or balance the lifestyle shot** — put the male model slightly more prominent or add a gym/errand context shot. Right now the female model in a sundress is doing more work than the male in the hero pair.
