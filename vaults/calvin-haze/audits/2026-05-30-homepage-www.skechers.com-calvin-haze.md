# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Got the screenshot. Writing the full review now.
- Skechers is leaning hard into deal-first energy — BOGO 50% is the very first thing I see, and that's actually kind of respect. The lifestyle imagery below the fold cuts close to my lane: young guy, casual streetwear, city sidewalk. But before I even get to that photo, the page throws a contest module at me asking me to win a soccer prize package, which feels like a detour when I came here to look at shoes. Brand vibes are clean, the Slip-ins nav tab is right there, and the offers are real — but the page is fighting itself for priority.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — dead first thing I see, applied at cart, nothing buried. That's a deal I take seriously.
- **"Slip-ins" as the first nav tab** — whoever ordered that nav knew what they were doing. One tap to my exact category, no hunting.
- **Left lifestyle shot** — young male, casual streetwear, city sidewalk, clean silhouette. That's me on a Saturday. It earns a second look.
- **"Spring into Summer Sale! 25% OFF Apparel"** — a second stacked deal makes the page feel like there's real money to be saved right now.
- **Skechers Plus module visibility** — at least the loyalty program is surfaced above the fold, even if it's dressed up as a sweepstakes.

## What's weak

- **"WIN THE ULTIMATE SOCCER FAN PRIZE PACKAGE"** — came to cop some slip-ins, not enter a soccer contest. This module takes up prime real estate and sends me sideways.
- **No shop CTA button above the fold** — "ENTER TO WIN" is the only real button I can see. I have to know to tap the nav tab to get to shoes. That's friction.
- **Five stacked horizontal bands before the hero** — BOGO strip, header nav, apparel sale strip, category nav strip, Skechers Plus module. By the time the lifestyle image loads visually, my attention is scattered.
- **No product shots in the viewport** — just lifestyle photography. I can't see a shoe with a price, a rating, or a "Shop Now" button on an actual product yet.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no free returns callout, no "free shipping over $X" visible anywhere in this scroll position.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the soccer contest module with a Slip-ins hero card** — something like "New: Slip-ins for Summer — hands-free, all day" with a "Shop Men's Slip-ins" button. Keep the contest in a sidebar or email.
- **Add one anchor CTA button in the hero** — "Shop Men's Slip-ins" or "Shop the BOGO" right below the lifestyle image. The lifestyle shot is doing its job; now close the loop with a tap target.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold** — something like "Free Shipping over $75" in the BOGO banner strip. That single line would meaningfully push conversion on a deal page.
- **Collapse the apparel sale strip into the BOGO banner** — two deal banners stacked on mobile competes with itself. One clear banner with both offers reduces band clutter and keeps the eye moving to the imagery faster.
