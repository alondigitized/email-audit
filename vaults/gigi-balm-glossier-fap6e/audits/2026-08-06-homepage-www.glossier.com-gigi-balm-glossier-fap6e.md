# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay this is very much still my Glossier — clean, skin-first, one hero product doing all the talking. They're pushing the "In A New York Minute Set" plus a Balm Dotcom Quintet GWP (free Applebaby keychain), which honestly tracks because Balm Dotcom is the one thing I will never let expire in my bag. It reads like a brand that knows exactly who it is, not a Sephora wall trying to be everything.

## What's working

- The top banner GWP — "free Applebaby Keychain when you buy the Balm Dotcom Quintet" — is speaking directly to me, I buy the Quintet anyway
- The extreme close-up hero shot of the Balm Dotcom tube on skin is very on-brand, feels like a real IG post not a studio ad
- "Shop + save" as a CTA is low-key and not screamy, which fits Glossier's whole vibe
- Zero clutter — no popup, no spinning carousel of 10 products fighting for attention

## What's weak

- "Shop + save" doesn't tell me what I'm saving on or shopping for specifically — I have to click to find out
- No loyalty/rewards visibility at all, and I'm literally a repeat customer who's on both email AND SMS — where's my "welcome back" moment?
- The GWP is cute but it's not a real discount, and I already own three Balm Dotcoms so a keychain isn't pulling me into a purchase

## Recommendations

- Swap "Shop + save" for something that names the product, like "Shop the NY Minute Set"
- Add a small "welcome back" or recently-viewed strip right under the hero for repeat shoppers like me
- Put an actual dollar or percent savings next to the GWP banner if there is one — right now it just reads as a bonus gift, not a deal
- A tiny reviews/rating badge on the Balm Dotcom Quintet link would push me to click faster
