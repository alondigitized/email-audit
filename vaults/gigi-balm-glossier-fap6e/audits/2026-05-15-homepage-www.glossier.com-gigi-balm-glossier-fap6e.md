# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the second this loaded I felt it — very us, very them, very *Glossier*. They're leading with Glossier You fragrance right now, wrapped in that whole "everything in my bag" aesthetic that's all over TikTok. It's a soft sell that doesn't feel desperate, which is exactly the Glossier brand promise. But as someone who has been here since the cloud paint days, I want more than one beautiful image — I want to know what's new.

## What's working

- **"you smell good." headline** — lowercase, casual, zero cringe. Sounds like a text from a friend, which is exactly Glossier's whole thing. I felt it.
- **Bag dump hero image** — the Glossier You bottle tucked in among pastel flatlay items, pearls, that black patent bag? That's a mood board I've pinned. It makes me want the *life*, not just the product.
- **Free shipping banner** — $40+ is achievable for me (I can hit that with one Balm Dotcom + Boy Brow combo), so this actually registers as useful, not just noise.
- **"Glossier You" as the CTA** — using the product name instead of "Shop Now" is confident and editorial. They know I know what Glossier You is.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no crowding, no janky text overlap. The nav is minimal (MENU + BAG) and gets out of the way.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-shopper recognition** — I've been buying here for years and there's no "welcome back," no loyalty balance, no "restock your favorites." Feels like I'm a first-time visitor every single time.
- **No new arrivals signal** — I'm an early-adopter who hits drops within 24 hours. Nothing above the fold tells me what just dropped or what's selling out. That urgency gap is a miss.
- **CTA copy could confuse newcomers** — "Glossier You" is intuitive to me, but it's a product name masquerading as a CTA. Someone who's never heard of it might not know to tap.
- **No social proof anywhere** — no review counts, no "best-seller" badge on the fragrance, nothing to reinforce that this is worth $**. For a hero push, that's a gap.
- **The hero slide has nav arrows** (the `< >` in the top banner) — that tells me there are more offers cycling through, but the first one is all I can act on. If there's a better offer two slides over, I'd never know to look.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "New In" or "Just Dropped" strip directly under the hero** — even a single horizontal rail of four recent SKUs with "new" badges would catch me before I tap away. I should feel FOMO, not flatness.
- **Personalize the banner for logged-in users** — swap "Free shipping on $40+" for "Hey Gigi — you've got [X] points" or "Your favorites are back in stock." The data is there. Use it.
- **Add a micro star-rating or "bestseller" label to the Glossier You CTA card** — one line like "4.8 stars · 14k reviews" under the button would close the loop on social proof without touching the visual.
- **Swap the ghost/outlined CTA button for a filled button** — on mobile in natural light, that outlined "Glossier You" button can disappear against busy background images. Fill it in.
