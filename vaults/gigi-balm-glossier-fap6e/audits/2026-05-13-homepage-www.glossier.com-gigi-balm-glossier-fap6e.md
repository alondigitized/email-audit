# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is unmistakably Glossier — the lowercase intimacy, the bag flat lay, the pearl-adjacent softness — and they're leading with Glossier You right now, which, honestly? Correct. This is the fragrance push and the whole vibe speaks to someone who's been in the cult since the pink pouches era. It feels warm and familiar in the exact way the product description promises, which is a nice bit of self-referential writing.

## What's working

- **"you smell good." headline** — lowercase, second-person, three words. It's disarming and it made me actually smile. Totally in the Glossier voice I fell for.
- **The bag flat lay** — I can see what looks like Balm Dotcom tubes, the Glossier You bottle, a pearl bracelet. This is my bag. Literally. Showing the product living in a real person's life (even if it's a styled one) over a clinical white background? Right call.
- **Free shipping threshold in the banner** — "$40+" is a believable, reachable number for anyone already planning to pick up one or two things. It's not aspirational, it's just true.
- **"Glossier You" as the CTA** — using the product name as the button copy keeps the intimacy going. It doesn't say "SHOP NOW." It says the name of the thing. Feels less transactional.
- **Clear, minimal nav** — Glossier logo, search, MENU, BAG (0). Nothing cluttered. I know exactly where everything is on mobile.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook whatsoever** — I'm a repeat customer, I have purchase history with this brand, and nothing above the fold acknowledges that. A "Welcome back" or even a subtle member tag would go a long way.
- **No human face or body** — the flat lay is beautiful but there's zero representation of who actually uses this product. Glossier built its brand on showing real people. A bag doesn't have skin.
- **"Glossier You" CTA is product-name-only** — for someone who doesn't already know what Glossier You is, this button says nothing about what happens when you tap it. Shop it? Learn about it? Explore the collection?
- **No price anchoring anywhere** — I don't know if Glossier You is $30 or $100 from this screen. Even a "from $X" would reduce friction before I commit to tapping through.
- **Zero trust signals below the CTA** — no review count, no "10M+ sold," no "as seen in," nothing that reinforces why I should click right now.

## Recommendations

- **Add a review badge or social proof snippet to the hero card** — even "★★★★★ from 12K reviews" under the subhead would do it without breaking the minimal aesthetic.
- **Swap generic CTA copy for an action-forward variant** — something like "Smell it. Shop it." or just "Shop Glossier You" makes the button's destination unambiguous without losing the brand voice.
- **Surface a returning-customer nudge in the announcement bar** — rotate the banner to include something like "You've got points waiting →" or "Members get early access" alongside the shipping offer. One line, same bar, zero new real estate needed.
- **Test a version of the hero with a person** — even a wrist shot wearing a bracelet while holding the bottle, or a neck/collarbone close-up. The bag is cute but Glossier's equity is in skin and faces.
