# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Glossier is leading with You — their fragrance — and doing it in that classic "products scattered artfully inside a fashion-girl bag" flat-lay that feels like peak Glossier-core. The page is clean, the copy is soft and punchy, and the vibe is unmistakably them. But if I opened this to restock my Boy Brow, getting a perfume pitch in my face first has me one tap from hitting search instead of scrolling.

## What's working

- **"you smell good." headline** — lowercase, conversational, exactly the register Glossier nails. Feels like a text from your most put-together friend, not an ad.
- **The handbag flat-lay hero** — pastel Balm Dotcom tubes and a little Glossier You bottle stuffed into a black patent bag? That's my bag. Literally. I have this exact setup. It's not staged-fake, it's staged-right.
- **Free shipping banner** — $40+ threshold is visible immediately and it's achievable on basically any Glossier order. Low friction, honest.
- **White card treatment** — the copy block floating over the image is clean and readable without competing with the product imagery. Good mobile hierarchy.
- **"Glossier" wordmark at scroll horizon** — pulling you to keep scrolling. Clever use of the brand name as a visual anchor.

## What's weak

- **No path to makeup/skincare above fold** — as a person who has Boy Brow in her drafts right now, I have zero shortcut to my actual category. The whole viewport is Glossier You.
- **"Glossier You" as a CTA label** — that's the product name, not an action. "Shop Glossier You" or even just "Shop Now" would reduce the half-second of "wait, is that a button?" hesitation.
- **No loyalty signal** — I've been a Glossier customer for years. The page has no idea who I am. No "hey member" badge, no points reminder, nothing.
- **No human face or body in frame** — products in a bag are aspirational but they're cold. A single shot of someone *wearing* Glossier You would close the emotional loop.
- **Zero trust signals above fold** — no review count, no "free returns," nothing. For someone new to the brand this is a miss; for me it's just a missed opportunity to reinforce.

## Recommendations

- **Add a skincare/makeup entry point to the hero** — even a small "or shop your faves →" text link below the Glossier You CTA would catch the huge chunk of visitors who aren't here for fragrance right now.
- **Rename the CTA** — change "Glossier You" to "Shop Glossier You" so the button reads as an action, not a label. One word, ships tomorrow.
- **Drop a member/loyalty nudge into the banner strip** — the top banner is rotating; use one of those slots for "You have X points — redeem now" for logged-in users. The infrastructure is presumably there.
- **Swap at least one hero image in the carousel for a lifestyle shot with a person** — the bag flat-lay is beautiful but a wrist spritz or a mirror-selfie setup would add warmth and the "imagery includes someone like me" signal that's currently missing.
