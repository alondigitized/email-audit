# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first of all — they're pushing a limited edition "I ♥ NY" Balm Dotcom and I'm physically incapable of being neutral about that. The whole homepage is one focused campaign drop: Big Apple vibes, red apples, the tube nestled in a mesh bag like it just came from the farmer's market. This is Glossier doing what Glossier does when it's firing — one product, one moment, one feeling. Classic soft-launch energy.

## What's working

- **The "I ♥ NY Balm Dotcom" product in the shot** — I can read the label. Showing the actual product in a lifestyle context instead of a white background sells the vibe without being try-hard.
- **"Take a bite out of the Big Apple!"** — The wordplay is exactly right: cheeky, not cringey. It earns the product shot.
- **GWP tied to a real spend threshold** — $75 feels reachable for any Glossier regular. The free Red Mini Beauty Bag is an actual reward, not a 10% discount nobody asked for.
- **The announcement bar doubles the offer** — "Get a free Red Mini Beauty Bag when you spend $75+!" is above the hero. Two touchpoints for the same message before I even read the card. Smart.
- **Single focus** — There is one thing happening on this page. No competing modules, no cluttered nav pushing five categories. Restraint is a flex.

## What's weak

- **"Shop now" is a little cold** — "Shop the drop" or "Get the Balm Dotcom" would have more personality and tell me what I'm actually tapping into.
- **No model or human in frame** — The apple/product shot is lovely, but I have no reference for how this product actually looks or feels in use. Even one skin-on-skin lifestyle still would close that gap.
- **No urgency signal** — "Limited edition" is implied by the copy but there's no "selling fast," no countdown, no "only at glossier.com" callout. I'd want some signal that this is actually scarce before I decide whether to buy now vs. later.
- **Zero loyalty/account acknowledgment** — I'm a customer. I'm on their SMS list AND their email list. They don't know I'm here.

## Recommendations

- **Change CTA from "Shop now" to "Shop the NYC Drop"** — makes it feel exclusive and specific to this moment, ships in a copy update.
- **Add a "Limited run — while supplies last" line** under the offer block — no timer needed, just one sentence of honest scarcity to drive immediate action.
- **Layer in a soft loyalty nudge** — something like "Members get early access" or "You're 25 points away from a perk" in the announcement bar rotation would reward existing customers without cluttering the hero.
- **Add a secondary lifestyle image** below the hero with skin application context — even a GIF of the balm being swiped on a lip would dramatically increase intent to buy.
