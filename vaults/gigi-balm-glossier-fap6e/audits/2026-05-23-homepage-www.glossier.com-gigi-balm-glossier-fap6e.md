# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Glossier is in full fragrance-push mode for Memorial Day and I'm getting mixed feelings — the bag flatlay is *so* my vibe (pearl necklace, pastel Balm Dotcoms, glossy hardware) but the whole above-the-fold is about Glossier You, which, cool, but I came here for my Boy Brow and they're leading with a scent. The Memorial Day 50% off banner is the real headline and they buried it in a tiny red strip at the top.

## What's working

- **The top promo banner** — red strip, star icon, "up to 50% off" for Memorial Day is immediately scannable and the first thing my eye goes to; that's the CTA I actually want
- **The bag flatlay aesthetic** — I have that exact black patent bag. The Balm Dotcoms, the pearls, the glass Glossier You bottle — it's deeply on-brand and visually satisfying
- **"you smell good." headline** — lowercase, short, a little smug in a Glossier way. The copy voice is completely right; it sounds like a text from a friend, not a marketing deck
- **Clean nav** — MENU / BAG (0) / search are all visible and uncluttered; the wordmark is doing its wordmark thing

## What's weak

- **Fragrance-first hero for a makeup/skincare shopper** — leading with Glossier You when most of your loyalists are here for Balm Dotcom, Boy Brow, and Cloud Paint is a category mismatch; I'm already suspicious of every expansion beyond the core lineup
- **"up to 50% off" is buried in a thin red banner** — this is the most compelling reason to shop today and it's in 12px text at the very top while a full-screen fragrance shot takes up the viewport
- **No category navigation above the fold** — I can't jump to Lip or Skin without tapping MENU and then navigating; for someone who hits every drop within 24 hours, frictionless category access matters
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — for a 50% off weekend this is a conversion miss; I need to know if my cart will qualify before I start adding things
- **No returning-customer recognition** — I've been on the email AND SMS list for years and the homepage treats me exactly like a first-time visitor

## Recommendations

- **Surface the 50% off offer in the hero, not just the banner** — make it the sub-headline of the white card: "Up to 50% off for Memorial Day Weekend." Don't make me parse the fine-print strip to understand why I should shop today
- **Add a category shortcut row below the hero** — "Lip | Skin | Sets | Fragrance" with four tappable chips; one tap to Boy Brow instead of hunting through MENU
- **Swap the pure-fragrance hero for a multi-product flatlay** — if you want to push Glossier You, show it alongside Balm Dotcom and a You Dew as a "long weekend kit" concept; that way you're still pushing fragrance but it speaks to loyalists who care about the full lineup
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the banner** — "Celebrate with up to 50% off + free shipping on $35+ — Terms apply"; one line, huge conversion lift for cart-builders
