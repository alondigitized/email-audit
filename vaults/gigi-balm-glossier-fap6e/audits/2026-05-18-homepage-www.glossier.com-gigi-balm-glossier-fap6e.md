# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- OK so Glossier is in their soft-fragrance era right now — the whole hero is Glossier You, which, fair, it's one of their cult products. But as someone who came for Boy Brow and stays for Phase Set, landing on a fragrance push when I just wanted to check if the Balm Dotcom Vitamin E restocked feels a little off. The aesthetic is still unmistakably Glossier — bag flatlay, pearls, pastel chaos — and the copy is doing *exactly* what Glossier copy does. But this homepage is brand-mood, not shopper-utility.

## What's working

- **"you smell good."** — three words, lowercase, no period drama. This is the Glossier copywriting I'm here for. Instantly recognizable, no try-hard energy.
- **The bag flatlay** — overhead shot of a bag stuffed with pastel products and a pearl necklace is exactly the aspirational mess I want to live inside. Very scrollable.
- **Free shipping bar** — $40+ threshold in the announcement bar is clear and reachable. I will absolutely calculate whether my cart hits it.
- **"Glossier You" as the CTA** — product-name-as-CTA is a flex move that works when the product is iconic enough. Glossier You is.
- **Zero clutter** — the header is three elements (search, MENU, BAG). No pop-ups, no newsletter takeover visible, no cookie banner blocking the hero. Clean.

## What's weak

- **No skin-first entry point** — I am a skin-first, makeup-second person. The hero is 100% fragrance. Where's the skincare hook? Milky Jelly, Futuredew, something? I have to go hunt in the menu.
- **No returning-customer signal** — I've been on the SMS list AND the email list since forever. This page has zero idea who I am. No "back for more?" no loyalty nudge, nothing.
- **"Glossier You" CTA is ambiguous** — Is it a link to the product? A quiz? A campaign hub? "Shop Glossier You" or "Get the Scent" would tell me exactly where I'm going.
- **No second message below the hero** — at least on this viewport, all I see is the hero and the big "Glossier" wordmark. If the fragrance push doesn't grab me, there's nothing else to catch me before I leave.
- **BAG (0)** — small thing, but showing an empty bag to a returning customer who probably has wishlist items or order history feels cold. Even surfacing a "pick up where you left off" would help.

## Recommendations

- **Add a skincare rail below the hero** — something like "while you're here" with Milky Jelly / Futuredew / Solution thumbnails. Fragrance is the lead, skin is the net underneath it.
- **Swap "Glossier You" button copy to "Shop Glossier You"** — one word change, zero design lift, removes the ambiguity about where the tap goes.
- **Surface a loyalty/returning-customer hook in the announcement bar** — rotate "Free shipping $40+" with "You've got [X] points — use them" or even just "Member exclusive: free full-size with $65+" so subscribers feel seen.
- **Give the fragrance copy a physical anchor** — "soft, warm and familiar" is lovely but abstract. One line like "the scent that's been in everyone's bag since 2017" gives it stakes without killing the vibe.
