# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay they led with Boy Brow and I feel personally seen — like they pulled my search history. The whole vibe is still that clean, product-obsessed minimalism Glossier does better than anyone, wands arranged like a museum exhibit of my entire brow history. What it's pushing right now is the OG hero product, which honestly tracks for a brand that keeps trying to remind people why they fell in love in the first place.

## What's working

- **The Boy Brow wand arrangement** — stacking all the shades side by side is genuinely smart product storytelling. I can immediately see the range and it makes me want to compare which one I'm running low on.
- **"So easy anyone can do it"** — that copy is doing real work. It's not intimidating, it's not a tutorial, it's a vibe. Very Glossier-voice, reads naturally on mobile.
- **The shipping threshold banner** — "Free standard shipping on orders $40+" sits right at the top where I'll actually see it before I start adding to cart. That's useful placement.
- **Clean nav** — Logo, search, MENU, BAG(0). No clutter. I can get wherever I need without squinting.
- **Product-as-hero** — they didn't put a model here. It's just the product, isolated, confident. I respect that.

## What's weak

- **"Shop now" is generic** — you just told me all about Boy Brow and then gave me the blandest CTA possible. "Shop Boy Brow" or even "Get mine" would keep the voice going.
- **Zero social proof above fold** — no star rating, no "100k+ sold," nothing. Glossier built on community. Where is it? Even one small signal would help close the loop.
- **No loyalty / membership hook** — I'm a returning customer, I'm on the SMS list AND the email list, and this homepage has zero acknowledgment that I exist as a known shopper. No "welcome back," no rewards balance, nothing.
- **No urgency or editorial freshness** — Boy Brow is a classic but this hero reads like it's been here for six months. Nothing signals "this is *the* moment to shop."
- **No model or person** — for a skin-first brand that built its identity on real girls using real products, a completely person-free hero feels a little cold and catalog-like.

## Recommendations

- **Swap generic "Shop now" for "Shop Boy Brow" or add a shade-finder micro-CTA** — something like "Find your shade →" under the hero would turn a passive browse into an active journey without adding friction.
- **Add a "back in stock" or "fan fave" badge to one of the wands** — scarcity signals work even for classics. A single "Blonde just restocked" note would make me tap faster.
- **Surface a returning-customer hook in the banner strip** — rotate the shipping message with "You're X points away from a reward" or a logged-in member greeting. The banner real estate is wasted running a single static message.
- **Drop one lifestyle shot alongside the product wands** — even a thumbnail of a real person doing their brows in a bathroom mirror would re-anchor the community identity Glossier is known for.
