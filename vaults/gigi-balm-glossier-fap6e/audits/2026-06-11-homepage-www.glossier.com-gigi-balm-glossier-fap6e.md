# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I opened Glossier.com and the first thing I see is a dog in a pink harness. A *dog.* The hero is literally pet accessories — "Fetch the look, turn the dog walk into a catwalk with our seriously-cute pet accessories." I came here for my Boy Brow restock and the brand is out here doing PetSmart. The banner at the top at least knows who I am ("Hi BFF! The Friends of Glossier Sale is almost here + G Members get early access") but the entire hero is a product category I did not come here for.

## What's working

- **The BFF Sale banner** — "G Members get early access" is exactly the kind of thing that makes me feel like being in the ecosystem pays off. I would tap "Sign up now" if I wasn't already a member.
- **Clean, minimal layout** — Very Glossier. White space, clean logo, single dominant image. No visual clutter, no pop-ups eating my face. The brand still *looks* like itself.
- **Playful copy register** — "Fetch the look" and "seriously-cute" are written in the exact voice that made me fall in love with this brand in the first place. Gen Z-fluent without trying too hard.
- **"Shop now" CTA placement** — above the fold, not buried, clearly readable. Good.

## What's weak

- **The hero is a dog.** I'm a beauty OG. I opened Glossier.com to look at skin products and the above-the-fold experience is a pet accessories launch. This is the Sephora-aisle expansion energy I've been suspicious of.
- **No beauty product visible above the fold at all** — not a skin tint, not a serum, not a Balm Dotcom. If I didn't already know this brand I would have no idea what Glossier actually *sells*.
- **Sale is "almost here" but there's no date** — the banner creates urgency but gives me nothing actionable right now. When is the sale? Tomorrow? Next week? I can't plan around "almost."
- **Zero trust/credibility signals** — no review counts, no "free returns," no loyalty point balance, nothing that rewards me for being a repeat customer beyond the vague early-access nudge.
- **No navigation shortcut to my category** — MENU is a hamburger, not a quick-tap category rail. Getting to skincare or makeup from here requires an extra step.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a beauty lead** — even something as simple as a summer skin/body oil flatlay or a real-person lifestyle shot with visible product. Put the pet accessories in a secondary carousel slot, not the hero.
- **Put a date on the BFF Sale** — "Friends of Glossier Sale starts June 13 — G Members shop early June 12" turns a tease into something I put in my calendar. Right now it's just anxiety.
- **Add a quick-category strip below the hero** — Skincare / Makeup / Body / New — one tap to where I actually want to go without hunting in the hamburger menu.
- **Show me my points or a returning-member reward** — even a small "You have 240 points" or "Members save X% on the sale" above the fold would make me feel seen as a loyal customer, not just another email address.
