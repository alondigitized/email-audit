# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, immediate reaction: this is still *the* Glossier. Clean wordmark, soft product flatlay, that unmistakably intimate copy register — it's pushing "Glossier You" fragrance right now and the bag-full-of-product imagery is genuinely doing it for me. It's not the most aggressive homepage I've seen from them but it's intentional, cozy, and 100% on-brand.

## What's working

- **The bag hero image** — products spilling out of a handbag with a pearl necklace is so specifically *my* aesthetic. This isn't aspirational-runway, it's real-girl-bag and I feel seen.
- **"you smell good."** — lowercase, no punctuation drama. Exactly how Glossier talks and it lands every time.
- **"Glossier You" as CTA** — using the product name as the button is clever; it doubles as brand philosophy and product navigation.
- **Free shipping strip** — "$40+" is front-and-center in the banner, zero hunting required.
- **No modal assault** — I opened the page and it just... let me look. No pop-up begging for my email in the first two seconds.

## What's weak

- **Fragrance-first on a skin/color hero** — I'm here for Boy Brow and Balm Dotcom. Glossier You is a repurchase for me, not an entry point. Pushing fragrance as the sole above-fold story might slide past casual returners.
- **Zero loyalty acknowledgement** — I have a purchase history here. The page treats me exactly like a first-time visitor. No "hi, you have X points" or "back for another Balm?" moment.
- **"MENU" as navigation** — one tap gets me to a full menu, not directly to Skincare or Makeup. That's friction.
- **No social proof above fold** — no review count, no "bestseller" badge, nothing anchoring the trust beyond the brand name itself.

## Recommendations

- **Add a returning-customer hook in the nav or banner** — even a small "sign in to see your rewards" prompt would make loyalists feel like the brand knows them.
- **Swap the CTA copy to include Balm Dotcom or Boy Brow in a secondary module** — let the fragrance hero breathe but add a "Skin & Makeup" shortcut below the fold so the rest of the catalog is one tap away.
- **Add a star rating or "X sold" micro-signal under the Glossier You CTA** — the product is beloved; let the social proof do the work.
- **Make the banner link actionable** — "Free standard shipping on $40+" is great copy but clicking it should open the full catalog or a "how to get there" explainer, not nothing.
