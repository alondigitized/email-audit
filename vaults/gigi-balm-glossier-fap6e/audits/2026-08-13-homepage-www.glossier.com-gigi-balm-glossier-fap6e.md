# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay this is giving classic Glossier — dewy freckled close-up, minimal type, one clean offer. It's very much "we don't need to yell, our skin does the talking" energy. Right now they're pushing Cheek Week, 20% off blushes plus a free Cheek Brush at $75+, which, fine, but I clicked in hoping for a Balm Dotcom refill and got blush discourse instead.

## What's working

- The hero shot itself — real skin, real freckles, a little shine, zero airbrush. That's the whole reason I trust this brand over a Sephora aisle full of filtered ads.
- "Get Cheeky" is a cute, not-trying-too-hard headline that matches how I'd actually talk.
- The pink "CHEEK WEEK" strip with the "20% OFF" cloud badge is loud without being tacky — good scroll bait right under the hero.
- Nav has BALMS as its own tab, separate from MAKEUP. Somebody there gets that Balm Dotcom is basically a personality trait for people like me.

## What's weak

- Whole above-the-fold is 100% blush. I'm a skin-first, Balm Dotcom in every pocket girl — there's nothing here for me until I go hunting.
- No "LOG IN" personalization cue, no rewards balance, no "welcome back" — I'm subscribed to the email AND the SMS list and the site has zero memory of that.
- No product thumbnails, no shade range, no reviews/star ratings anywhere visible — for a blush drop I'd want to see the actual pans before tapping through.
- The top banner and the pink strip below both say basically the same offer twice in a row — feels a little redundant instead of layering new info.

## Recommendations

- Rotate the hero banner to at least acknowledge Balms/Skincare shoppers too — even a quick "new Balm Dotcom flavor" chip next to the blush push would keep me from bouncing.
- Add a tiny "Welcome back, Gigi" or rewards-points nudge near LOG IN since I'm already opted into email + SMS — show me you know that.
- Swap the second promo strip for a shade-range or best-seller carousel instead of repeating the same 20%-off message.
- Drop a review count or star rating badge on the "Shop + save" CTA area so I know what I'm tapping into before I commit.
