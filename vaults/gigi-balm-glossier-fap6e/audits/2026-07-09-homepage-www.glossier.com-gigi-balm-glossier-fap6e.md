# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I literally screamed a little — they opened with Boy Brow and I have it in *three colors*, so this homepage came for me specifically. The whole page is minimal, editorial, product-focused in that classic Glossier way: no noise, no Sephora-aisle chaos, just one hero product done right. Right now they're clearly in a "remind you why Boy Brow exists" mode, and honestly? It's working.

## What's working

- **The Boy Brow product wand close-up** — shooting five wands lined up against black is a strong visual, lets me see shade variety at a glance without anyone telling me what to think about it
- **"The original boyish, brushed-up brow."** — that copy is doing a lot. "Original" is a brand-equity claim. "Boyish, brushed-up" is the exact language I use when I describe my brow look to friends
- **"Shop Boy Brow" CTA** — specific product name in the button, not "Shop Now" garbage. I know exactly where I'm going
- **Free shipping banner** — $40 is achievable (Boy Brow is $18, I'd probably grab two to hit it), clearly stated, no hidden conditions visible

## What's weak

- **No person, no face** — I love a close macro product shot but there is zero human in this frame. Part of what made early Glossier magical was seeing *real skin, real brows* — this reads more clinical than community
- **No shade called out in hero copy** — I have Boy Brow in three colors but a new shopper looking at five mystery wands doesn't know which is theirs without tapping through
- **No loyalty hook whatsoever** — I'm subscribed to the email AND the SMS list, I'm a power customer, and there's nothing here that acknowledges me as a repeat buyer
- **Hamburger menu only** — there's no horizontal nav to jump to Skincare or Makeup quickly; I have to hunt inside MENU if Boy Brow isn't what I came for

## Recommendations

- **Add a face** — swap the pure-product wand lineup for a lifestyle shot where someone with already-good brows is getting them *even better* with Boy Brow; keep the wand grid as a secondary module below
- **Name the shades in the hero** — overlay the shade names (Blond, Brown, Black, etc.) on each wand in the image; zero additional scroll, massive help for new shoppers and shade-switchers like me
- **Surface a loyalty nudge in the banner** — the top strip already rotates; add a frame like "You're 3 points from free shipping" or "Glossier members get early access to drops" to reward repeat visitors
- **Add one bestseller row below the fold** — a horizontal scroll rail with Boy Brow → Balm Dotcom → Cloud Paint would sell the system without breaking the minimal aesthetic
