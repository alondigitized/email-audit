# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- OK, this is very Glossier — the bag flatlay, the lowercase copy, the "you smell good" energy. It's a clean, confident fragrance push for Glossier You, and honestly the aesthetic hits. But I'm a skin-girl first and they're leading with scent, which makes me pause for half a second before I lean in.

## What's working

- **The bag flatlay hero image** — open patent leather bag with Balm Dotcoms, a glass Glossier You bottle, a pearl bracelet. That's my bag. That's my actual bag. The visual does 80% of the selling before the copy even registers.
- **"you smell good."** — lowercase, punchy, conversational. That's exactly how Glossier talks and it still works on me every time.
- **Free shipping banner** — "$40+" is right in my wheelhouse. I never spend under $60 here so the threshold feels genuinely reachable, not a tease.
- **Single, clean CTA** — "Glossier You" is one tap. No decision fatigue, no carousel of conflicting buttons.
- **The big wordmark at the bottom** — "Glossier" in oversized type acts as a visual anchor. It makes the whole screen feel like a brand moment, not a storefront.

## What's weak

- **No skincare or makeup entry point above the fold** — I'm a Boy Brow and Cloud Paint person first. The entire above-the-fold is fragrance. I'd have to tap MENU and dig to find what I actually came here for.
- **No person in the imagery** — the bag is gorgeous but there's no face, no skin, nobody to ID with. Glossier has always been about real-looking people; a product-only flatlay feels slightly Sephora-aisle for them.
- **Zero social proof** — no review count, no "4.8 stars," no "cult favorite" badge. For a fragrance I haven't smelled yet, I want someone's receipts.
- **BAG (0) in the nav** — the empty bag is a missed opportunity; if I were logged in, a wishlist item or past order nudge would pull me back into a purchase mindset immediately.
- **Rotating banner but no visible reward/loyalty hook** — the banner cycles (I can see the arrows and pause button) but nothing about You Points or member perks. That's the whole reason I stayed subscribed.

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-liner fragrance descriptor to the hero subhead** — "soft, warm and familiar" is fine but "earthy musk, iris, ambrette" tells me *why* I want it. One more sentence or a scent tag would push wavering skin-girls into a tap.
- **Layer in a "Back to Skin" or "Your Routine" secondary CTA** — a ghost/outline button below "Glossier You" that goes to skincare lets me self-sort without hitting the MENU. Don't make skin-first shoppers hunt.
- **Add a UGC or star-rating callout** — even just "★★★★★ 4,200+ reviews" beneath the product name converts fragrance skeptics like me who need the crowd to vouch.
- **Swap the empty bag indicator for a logged-in welcome** — "Hi Gigi, you have 240 You Points" above the fold would make this feel like coming home, not cold browsing.
