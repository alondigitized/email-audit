# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the dog in the pink hoodie? I audibly laughed and immediately wanted to send it to my group chat — that's a hit. But real talk: I came here for skin stuff, and Glossier is leading its entire homepage with *merch*. This feels like a brand that's leaning hard into its cult-of-personality era and trusting loyalists to follow wherever the pink goes. Cute energy, genuinely, but I'm giving it a side-eye on behalf of my Balm Dotcom situationship.

## What's working

- **The dog.** I don't know how to explain it but a golden retriever in a pink Glossier hoodie is exactly the kind of drop this brand was built to do. It's screenshot-able, it's shareable, it hit — they know what they're doing.
- **"Fetch the look" button copy.** The pun is corny and I love it. It makes me want to tap just to see if the product page keeps the bit going.
- **Free shipping banner.** "$40+" is completely reachable for anyone buying even one Glossier product. Framing it as a strip at the very top means I see it before anything else — smart placement.
- **Typography and hierarchy.** The headline is large, left-aligned, easy to read on mobile. The subhead explains exactly what the drop is without being a wall of text. Nothing competing for my attention.
- **The Glossier wordmark at the bottom of the image.** Branded merch photography that still feels editorial. It doesn't look like a mall kiosk.

## What's weak

- **Merch first, beauty second.** I have six Glossier products in my bathroom right now and I came here to see what's new in skincare. Leading with a dog hoodie collection makes me feel like I took a wrong turn — even though I love the dog.
- **"Fetch the look" is vague for conversion.** It's cute copy but it doesn't tell me what I'm about to see. Is it the hoodie? A full pink set? A dog-owner bundle? I don't know until I tap.
- **Zero beauty above the fold.** No product, no skin, no makeup, no new shade. For a "skin-first" brand this is a bold omission.
- **No loyalty or member callout.** I'm subscribed to both the email and the SMS list. The homepage has no acknowledgment that I exist as a returning customer — no rewards balance, no "back in stock" signal, nothing.
- **No human model.** The dog is iconic but it means there's nobody who looks like *me* or someone I can imagine wearing the actual product.

## Recommendations

- **Add a secondary hero module** — even a thin strip or a second slide in the carousel — that surfaces a beauty product. "New: Balm Dotcom [shade]" or "Back in stock: Cloud Paint" would speak directly to loyalists without touching the Pink Capsule hero.
- **Swap "Fetch the look" for something that sets expectations**: `"Shop the Pink Capsule"` keeps the aesthetic without leaving me guessing what I'll land on.
- **Put a loyalty nudge in the banner.** That top strip already has real estate — rotate it or split it: "Free shipping on $40+ · Members earn 2× points on drops" would reward the people who hit every launch.
- **Shoot a BTS or lifestyle frame with a person next to the dog** — keep the dog energy, but show a girl in the hoodie holding her Balm Dotcom. That bridges merch and beauty product in one image.
