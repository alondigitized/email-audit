# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the bag dump is sending me — I see my Balm Dotcoms, I see the pink bottle, I get the vibe immediately. This is peak Glossier aesthetic and they know it. Right now they're in full Glossier You fragrance era, which fine, I own a bottle, but walking onto the homepage and being led with scent instead of skin feels like a soft pivot I'm still watching closely.

## What's working

- **Bag dump hero image** — seeing actual Balm Dotcoms and a Glossier You bottle spilling out of a black patent bag is instant brand recognition for me. It says "Glossier girl" without typing it.
- **"you smell good."** — the lowercase, conversational headline is so distinctly Glossier voice. It doesn't scream, it whispers. I respect that.
- **Free shipping banner at the top** — "$40+" is easy to hit, and it's the first thing I see. Practical and appreciated.
- **Clean mobile nav** — MENU, search, and bag all visible, zero clutter. One tap to get anywhere.
- **Hero card contrast** — the white floating card on the lifestyle image is legible and doesn't fight with the background.

## What's weak

- **Fragrance as the lead** — I'm a skin-first Glossier loyalist. Leading me with "Glossier You" when I'm here to restock Boy Brow or grab a Cloud Paint is a slight miss on priority. The homepage should know I'm a repeat buyer, not a fragrance-curious newcomer.
- **"Glossier You" as CTA copy** — that's the product name, not an action. "Shop Glossier You" or even "Find Your Scent" would be clearer. Right now it reads more like a label than a button.
- **No person in the frame** — the bag dump is gorgeous but I can't see myself in it literally. A subtle lifestyle shot with someone my age actually wearing/using would close that gap.
- **No returning-user acknowledgment** — I'm on the SMS list AND the email list. The homepage has no idea I exist. No points, no "back for more?", nothing.
- **No new-arrivals signal above fold** — I hit every drop within 24 hours. There's no "new" hook visible here at all.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "What's New" entry point** in or directly below the hero — a pill badge or a secondary CTA ("See what just dropped") would keep drop-chasers like me from bouncing to check my emails instead.
- **Swap the CTA copy** from "Glossier You" to "Shop Glossier You" — one word of difference, but it reads as an action instead of a product label.
- **Surface loyalty/points status** in the top nav bar for logged-in users — even a small "✦ 240 pts" next to the bag icon would make me feel recognized and pull me toward a purchase to hit the next tier.
- **Layer in one person** — either in the hero image itself or in a tight lifestyle strip below it — someone in their mid-20s with actual skin showing. The flat-lay is gorgeous but a face creates connection.
