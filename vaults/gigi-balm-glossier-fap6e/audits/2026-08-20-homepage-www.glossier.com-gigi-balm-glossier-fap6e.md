- Zero people in the hero image — just product and flowers, so there's nothing here that visually says "this is for you specifically"

- Swap the hero to a Balm Dotcom or skincare-led module given the banner is already promoting balms — right now the banner and hero are pulling in two different directions
- Add a specific discount or bundle price under "Play matchmaker" instead of just "save" — vague savings language makes me suspicious
- Put a small "Welcome back" or rewards-points callout near LOG IN so repeat shoppers like me feel recognized immediately
