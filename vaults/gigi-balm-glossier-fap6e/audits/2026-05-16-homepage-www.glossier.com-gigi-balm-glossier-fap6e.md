# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, it's giving "bag drop" energy in the best way — the hero is a flat-lay of a luxe patent bag with Glossier You front and center, and the copy is doing that soft, confident Glossier thing I fell for in like 2019. But this is clearly a fragrance campaign, which is fine, I already own three Glossier Yous, so I'm not sure this is gonna move me on *this* visit.

## What's working

- **The bag imagery**: That dark patent leather bag spilling Glossier products is genuinely beautiful — it's the exact vibe of my actual bag and it pulls me in immediately
- **"you smell good." headline**: Lowercase, conversational, zero try-hard energy — this is exactly how Glossier used to write and I'm glad it's still there
- **Free shipping threshold in the top bar**: $40 is reachable and I need to know this before I start mentally building a cart
- **"Glossier You" as CTA text**: Using the product name as the CTA instead of "Shop Now" or "Discover" feels specific and confident — I know exactly what I'm clicking into

## What's weak

- **No returning-shopper hook whatsoever**: I've been buying since 2019, I'm on email AND SMS, and the homepage treats me like a stranger — no loyalty nudge, no member pricing, nothing
- **BAG (0) in the nav**: Small thing but seeing zero when I'm about to shop is a tiny discouragement; wish it showed my loyalty points or something instead
- **Fragrance-only above the fold**: I love Glossier You but I already own it — there's no pathway to Balm Dotcom, Boy Brow, or skincare without hitting MENU first, which is a friction point
- **No model or person visible**: The bag is pretty but there's no human in the frame — for a skin-first brand, I want to see skin; the product aesthetic alone doesn't close the loop for me
- **Trust signals are completely absent**: No review count, no "4.8 stars from X reviews," nothing — Glossier has a cult following, lean into it above the fold

## Recommendations

- **Add a "back for more?" loyalty nudge** in the announcement bar carousel — something like "You've got X points. See what they unlock →" for returning visitors; the carousel already has three slides, use one for this
- **Swap one carousel slide for a Balm Dotcom or Boy Brow moment** so the hero isn't 100% fragrance — Gigi wants to see her core products in the first screen, not hunt for them behind MENU
- **Add a star rating chip to the hero card**: literally just "★ 4.9 · 12K reviews" on the Glossier You card — it costs nothing and converts fence-sitters
- **Show a person wearing Glossier You** (wrist spray, candid moment) instead of or alongside the bag flat-lay — skin-first brand should show skin
