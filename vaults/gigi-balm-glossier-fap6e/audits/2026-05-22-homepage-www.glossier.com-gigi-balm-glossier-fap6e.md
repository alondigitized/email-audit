# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing I see is the red Memorial Day banner and then a whole fragrance push — "you smell good." with the Glossier You flatlay. Very them, very lowercase-minimalist energy, and honestly the bag flatlay is cute. But I'm a little like... you've got 50% off running and you're leading with a scent? Where's the Balm Dotcom bundle, where's the Boy Brow drop? This feels like it's pitching to someone who just discovered Glossier You, not me.

## What's working

- **The top promo banner** — red background, star emoji, "up to 50% off" is immediately readable. I clocked it in under a second.
- **"you smell good."** — three words, lowercase, period. That's the Glossier voice I subscribed to. It doesn't oversell.
- **The flatlay hero image** — the black leather bag spilling with beauty products and pearls reads like my actual nightstand. Very aspirational-realistic, not a white studio shoot.
- **The Glossier wordmark watermark at the bottom** — adds a design-forward touch that reminds me I'm on a real brand page, not a knockoff.
- **"Glossier You" as the CTA** — it doubles as product name and destination, keeps the page copy tight.

## What's weak

- **No skincare or makeup surface above the fold** — I'm a Boy Brow girl first, and there's zero pathway to my actual category without tapping MENU. That's an extra friction point.
- **"Terms apply" with no easy expansion** — the 50% off banner doesn't tell me what qualifies. For a drop-hunter like me, vague offers make me second-guess whether my wishlist items are included.
- **No human face in the hero** — the flatlay is pretty but there's nobody in it. I want to see someone wearing the vibe, not just objects on a bag.
- **MENU-only navigation on mobile** — there's no persistent category strip. One tap to reach fragrance → one more tap to skincare → I've lost the plot.
- **No trust layer** — no review count, no star rating, no "free shipping over $X." If I'm a first-timer I'm bouncing before I convert.

## Recommendations

- **Add a sticky nav strip under the logo** with the top 3-4 categories (Skincare, Makeup, Fragrance, Sets) — one tap to any shelf, no hamburger menu dig.
- **Overlay a "50% off applies to…" qualifier chip** on the banner — even just "Sitewide" or "Select styles" would stop me from clicking away to Google the fine print.
- **Swap the pure-product flatlay for a lifestyle shot** that includes a real person using or holding Glossier You — someone my age, no-makeup-makeup, everyday moment. The object flatlay reads editorial; a person reads relatable.
- **Surface a "For You" returning-customer module** below the fold — recently viewed, your routine staples, loyalty points if they exist. I'm logged in, use that data.
