# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is peak Glossier. The bag flatlay is giving "my actual tote bag" energy and "you smell good." made me do a little smile — lowercase, no punctuation overload, very them. Right now the whole page is a Glossier You push, which tracks because that fragrance has been having a moment. It feels like classic Glossier: soft, confident, not trying too hard.

## What's working

- **"you smell good." headline** — lowercase, punchy, confident. Instantly recognizable Glossier copy voice. It doesn't overexplain.
- **Bag flatlay hero image** — this is an aesthetic I actually recognize as "my life." Glossier products spilling out of a black bag with a pearl necklace draped across? That's art direction that knows its customer.
- **"Glossier You" CTA button** — it's the product name, not "Shop Now" or "Discover More." One tap and I know exactly where I'm going.
- **Free shipping banner** — $40 threshold is visible at the very top, auto-rotating or static. Easy to clock on a first glance, and $40 is genuinely reachable (two Balm Dotcoms and I'm there).
- **"Glossier." wordmark at the bottom** — the giant logo peeking up from the bottom of the viewport creates depth and feels intentional, not accidental.

## What's weak

- **No navigation categories visible** — "MENU" is a hamburger that requires a tap. If I land here and I'm not immediately vibing with the Glossier You push, there's no obvious path to skincare or lip color without a step.
- **No people in the hero** — the product flatlay is gorgeous but there's zero human in the frame. As someone who buys into Glossier partly because their model casting is so real, this feels like a missed opportunity.
- **No trust signals or social proof** — no star ratings, no "as loved by X people," no "reviews." For someone already in the ecosystem this doesn't matter, but it's a cold experience for anyone who got here from an ad.
- **Fragrance-first feels slightly off for the OG** — Glossier built its whole thing on skin-first and the clear wins (Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom). Pushing fragrance as the entire homepage hero sidelines the products that made us believers. It's fine, but I notice it.
- **BAG (0) is a tiny friction flag** — showing the empty bag count in the nav is weirdly deflating on first load. Nothing in the cart, nothing feels urgent.

## Recommendations

- **Add one person to the hero** — swap or layer in a lifestyle shot of someone actually wearing/smelling Glossier You, not just the product arrangement. The flatlay is pretty but a face (ideally someone who looks like me, not a runway casting) makes it something I want to be in.
- **Surface a secondary CTA or category strip below the hero** — even a "Shop Balm Dotcom / Boy Brow / Serums" quick-tap row right below the Glossier You section keeps me in the funnel if fragrance isn't what I came for.
- **Show a review count or social signal on the Glossier You block** — something like "48,000+ reviews" directly adjacent to the button instantly removes the "should I trust this" friction for new visitors and reminds OGs like me that this product has a real following.
- **Replace "BAG (0)" with nothing or a bag icon only** — the zero feels empty and slightly sad. Just a bag icon keeps it functional without broadcasting that I haven't added anything yet.
