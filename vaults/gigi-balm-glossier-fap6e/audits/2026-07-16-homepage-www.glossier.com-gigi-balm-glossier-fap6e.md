# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I actually gasped a little. This is the Balm Dotcom homepage I've been waiting for — a hero built around the exact product I have in literally every bag I own. "I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom" with an apple-flavored clear variant and NYC merch? This is a drop, and it's dressed like one. Glossier is doing what it does best: one tight campaign, one irresistible product, no noise.

## What's working

- **"I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom" headline**: It's a product name, a cultural reference, and a lifestyle statement simultaneously. If you know, you know — and Glossier OGs know.
- **"NEW!" callout above the headline**: Tiny but effective. I'm already on the alert that this is a drop, not just a product page.
- **The apple-flavored pitch**: "Take a bite out of the Big Apple" is a pun but it doesn't feel try-hard. It tells me exactly what the flavor is without a boring ingredient list.
- **Model energy**: She's not smizing at me. She's mid-application, looking slightly off-camera, totally relaxed. That candid skin-first vibe is peak Glossier and it works on me every time.
- **Free shipping strip**: $40 threshold is prominent and honest. For Glossier AOV it's achievable — I'm mentally calculating a Balm Dotcom + something already.
- **NYC backdrop**: This isn't a studio shot. There's a real street behind her. The whole campaign feels like a summer city thing and I'm in.

## What's weak

- **"Shop now" is doing a lot of lifting with zero context**: I know from the hero it's Balm Dotcom, but the button could say "Shop NY Balm Dotcom" and be 10x more satisfying. Generic CTA on a specific drop feels lazy.
- **No merch preview**: The copy mentions "rep our city with all new merch" but I can't see a single merch item. If that's a selling point, show me a tote or a tee in the frame. I won't scroll to find it if I don't believe it's real.
- **Zero social proof above the fold**: No review count, no "4.8 stars, 12k reviews" stamp, nothing. For a returning OG this doesn't matter much, but it would have pushed me over the edge faster.
- **No Glossier You / rewards hook**: I have loyalty points from years of buying. Show me what I can apply. The fact that there's no "Rewards" badge anywhere above the fold is a miss for someone who has spent real money here.
- **Navigation is just "MENU"**: One tap to get there, sure, but a hamburger labeled MENU on a brand this design-forward feels like placeholder copy. Even "Shop" would feel more Glossier.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA**: "Shop NY Balm Dotcom" or "Get the Drop" — something that mirrors the campaign language and makes the button feel like part of the editorial, not an afterthought.
- **Add a single merch teaser below the CTA**: One small image strip of the NYC merch collection (even a corner of a tote) validates the "plus rep our city" claim and gives me a reason to scroll without leaving.
- **Show loyalty balance or a rewards callout in the nav**: Even "You have 140 points" or a small "Rewards" link next to BAG (0) would close the loop for returning shoppers and feel like Glossier actually knows me.
- **Drop a star count on the Balm Dotcom name in the hero card**: One line — "4.9 ★ · 14,800 reviews" — under the headline. Doesn't break the aesthetic and converts fence-sitters instantly.
