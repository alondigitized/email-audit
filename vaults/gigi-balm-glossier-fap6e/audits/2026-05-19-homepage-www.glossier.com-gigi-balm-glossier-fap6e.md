# Homepage snapshot · www.glossier.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- okay so Glossier is leading with fragrance — "you smell good." over a bag flatlay, which is a very specific flex — and I get it, Glossier You is a classic and the shoot is doing the most in the best way. but as someone who came here to restock Boy Brow and grab another Balm Dotcom, I'm being handed a fragrance CTA when I haven't even hit skincare yet. the brand DNA is intact, the voice is perfect, but this homepage is routing me away from my actual mission.

## What's working

- **"you smell good." headline** — lowercase, second-person, intimate without being cloying. this is exactly the Glossier voice I've trusted since the beginning and it lands immediately.
- **The bag flatlay** — a packed black patent bag with product tubes in pink/blue/cream and a pearl bracelet draped across it. it reads like *my* bag, not a campaign set. aspirational but real.
- **Free shipping banner** — "$40+" is easy math for a restock run. it doesn't feel like a wall, it feels like an invitation.
- **Outlined CTA button** — choosing a ghost button over a big filled rectangle keeps the visual register calm and on-brand. it asks rather than demands.
- **Mobile layout discipline** — the text card doesn't swallow the hero image, and the oversized "Glossier" wordmark bleeding at the bottom is a nice scroll invitation.

## What's weak

- **Fragrance hero for a skincare loyalist** — I hit this page to re-up on my staples and the entire above-the-fold experience is pushing fragrance. Glossier You is not a spontaneous purchase for me; it's a considered one.
- **Zero returning-customer acknowledgment** — I'm on the SMS list AND the email list and this homepage is treating me like a stranger. no loyalty points, no "early access" hook, nothing that signals the brand knows I've been here before.
- **"MENU" hamburger nav** — to get to skincare or makeup I have to tap Menu, wait for it to expand, then tap the category. that's at least two extra taps on a visit where I already know what I want.
- **No social proof anywhere above the fold** — no reviews, no star counts, no "X people have this in their bag." for a brand that built its reputation on community, there's radio silence on trust signals.
- **No urgency signal** — nothing tells me to tap now. no new shades, no back-in-stock, no drop timer. Gigi hits every drop within 24 hours but only if she knows one's happening.

## Recommendations

- **Add a skincare shortcut to the top nav** — a single "Skincare" chip next to the search icon means returning shoppers can bypass the fragrance hero entirely in one tap. takes one sprint to ship.
- **Surface loyalty points or early-access copy in the banner strip** — the top bar is rotating; one of those slides should say something like "OG members get first access to new drops" to activate the loyalist segment.
- **Add a "what's new" or "back in stock" micro-signal near the CTA** — even two words like "New shades" or "Just dropped" anchored below the Glossier You button would create the urgency that triggers the "tap now, think later" instinct.
- **Include a secondary hero tile or category strip below the fold** — one horizontal scroll of Skincare / Makeup / Sets / Fragrance would mean fragrance loyalists AND skincare loyalists both see their destination without extra navigation.
