# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Fenty is in full summer sale mode — the "SUMMER GLOW-UP" hero is very on-brand, and the 60% off headline hits hard. But as someone who's here for the complexion lineup and already clocked that A$AP Rocky x Fenty Skin "COMING SOON" banner before I even scrolled, this feels like it's selling me a discount first and Rihanna's actual vision second. The tropical palm-leaf aesthetic is cute but it's not showing me a single shade, a single Match Stix swatch, nothing.

## What's working

- **"THE SUMMER GLOW-UP" headline** lands immediately — it's Fenty's register, not generic mall-brand speak, and I feel seen as a beauty millennial who actually uses that word
- **"UP TO 60% OFF SELECT ITEMS"** is bold and above the fold — that number stops my thumb
- **The A$AP Rocky x Fenty Skin "COMING SOON" announcement bar** at the very top is genius placement; as someone who cross-shops Fenty Skin for bundles, a collab tease from Rocky makes me want to tap that arrow immediately
- **Category tabs** — "Fenty Beauty," "Fenty Skin," "Fenty Body" — visible immediately under the nav, so I can skip the sale and go directly to complexion in one tap
- **"Look lit from sunrise to sunset"** subhead is specific enough to evoke a product benefit (long-wearing, all-day glow) without being clinical

## What's weak

- **No complexion imagery** — the hero is a palm-leaf flat-lay in olive green. I'm a Match Stix devotee. Where is the skin? Where's the swatch? A sale on complexion products with zero skin in the hero is a miss.
- **"Exclusions apply."** is dangling there with no link or qualifier — after a 60% off claim, that sentence alone might make me pause and wonder if anything I actually want is excluded
- **No model, no face, no shade representation** — Fenty's entire brand equity is in inclusive shade range and I see exactly zero evidence of that above the fold
- **No urgency signal** — no "sale ends Sunday," no countdown, no stock callout. The 60% off feels evergreen rather than truly urgent
- **No loyalty hook** — I've spent real money here, and there's no rewards balance, no member-pricing callout, nothing acknowledging I'm a repeat customer

## Recommendations

- **Put skin in the hero.** Swap the palm-leaf product flat-lay for a lifestyle shot of a model with visible Fenty complexion coverage — Match Stix on cheekbone, Pro Filt'r on skin — in a summer setting. One image can sell both the sale and the shade philosophy.
- **Make the exclusions clickable.** "Exclusions apply." should tap directly to a filtered list — or at minimum, call out what's included: "Complexion, Fenty Skin, and Lip all on sale." Don't let me guess.
- **Add a loyalty nudge above the fold.** A one-line strip under the CTA — "Fenty Beauty rewards members save an extra 10%" — turns a good offer into a reason to log in right now.
- **Put the A$AP Rocky collab on a timer.** If it's "Coming Soon," give me a countdown or a "notify me" tap on that announcement bar. That's a conversion event hiding inside a teaser.
