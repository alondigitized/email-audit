# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- OK so Fenty is in full launch mode right now — they're pushing the Sun Stalk'r Soufflé Pressed Mousse Cream Bronzer hard, and Rihanna is front and center in that deep burgundy lip looking unreal. This reads like a brand that knows exactly who its customer is and isn't apologizing for it — luxury complexion focus, deeper skin tones centered, no fluffy lifestyle filler. The account-creation nudge for free shipping is smart but I wish there was a louder push for me to actually tap into the product.

## What's working

- **Rihanna's presence in the hero** — she's not just a logo, she's wearing the product and she looks incredible. As someone who tracks which products Rih actually posts about, seeing her face next to the bronzer is credibility I can't manufacture
- **"NEW" label on the Soufflé Bronzer** — Fenty knows I'm a launch-day buyer. Flagging new product at hero level speaks directly to me
- **20% Off Select Items in the nav strip** — that's the first thing I'm tapping after I finish reading the hero. The placement is smart even if it's small
- **The product shot split** — left side is the compact, right side is Rihanna. I can see the tortoiseshell amber packaging clearly. The color of the compact tells me this is a warm bronzer, which is useful signal before I even tap through
- **Top banner free shipping + returns** — "Create an account to unlock free U.S. standard shipping + returns" is a genuinely useful hook, even if it's acquisition-first
- **Copy register** — "bouncy + blurring, airbrushed blending" is insider-fluent beauty language. I know exactly what that means and it makes me want to try it

## What's weak

- **No hero CTA button visible** — I can see the product name and the subtext but I don't see a "Shop Now" or "Get It" button in this viewport. That's a conversion miss at the exact moment I'm most primed
- **Shade range not hinted at** — As a Match Stix devotee I care deeply about whether this bronzer comes in shades that actually work on my skin. Zero shade range signal above the fold
- **The category strip feels cramped** — "20% Off Select Items | Shop All | Fenty Beauty | Fenty…" — that last item is cut off. On mobile this is a usability issue
- **No loyalty hook at all** — Fenty has its Beauty Insider community, but I see nothing about points, rewards, or member perks. A returning customer like me gets the same homepage as a first-timer
- **Chat bubble placement** — the floating chat icon sits right over the product copy in the bottom right. It doesn't block anything critical but it feels like clutter at the worst spot

## Recommendations

- **Add a CTA button directly in the hero** — "Shop the Launch" or "Try Soufflé Bronzer" below the product description, above the fold. Right now the hero is a beautiful editorial that doesn't ask me to do anything
- **Show a shade swatch strip beneath the hero copy** — even three shade dots with names tells me "yes, this works for my complexion." That's one of Fenty's strongest differentiators and it's invisible here
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards badge for signed-in users** — if I'm logged in, show me my points balance or "member exclusive" pricing in the top nav. That's what keeps me from opening a competitor tab
- **Untruncate or carousel the category strip** — "Fenty…" cut off mid-word reads like a bug. Either shorten the label or make the strip swipeable with visible arrows
