# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Fenty doing what Fenty does — drop a collab that makes culture and sell it as a moment, not just a product. The A$AP Rocky × Fenty Skin "BACK AGAIN" banner tells me immediately that this is a limited-edition relaunch and I should move fast. But I came here for complexion — Match Stix, foundation range, the stuff Rih actually puts on her face — and the hero has zero makeup energy. The brand looks expensive and intentional, but the homepage is in full Fenty Skin mode right now, which means I'm one tap away from where I actually want to be.

## What's working

- **The A$AP Rocky visual** is arresting — pink rollers, sunglasses, muted background. It's the exact kind of editorial flex that makes me think "Fenty gets it." This is not stock-photo energy.
- **The announcement banner at top** with "A$AP Rocky × Fenty Skin | SHOP NOW" does double duty: it surfaces the drop and provides an immediate tap-to-shop path. Clean.
- **Category tabs** — Shop All / Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Body — are visible and tappable right below the logo. The one tap to "Fenty Beauty" to get to my makeup category is frictionless.
- **"LIMITED EDITION" + "BACK AGAIN" copy** creates real urgency without a fake countdown timer. I know this collab has a shelf life.

## What's weak

- **Zero complexion representation above the fold**: my entire reason for having this app is shade — Match Stix, Pro Filt'r, Soft'lit. The hero is a Fenty Skin collab with a male model. If I weren't already a devotee, this homepage gives me no reason to think Fenty is the shade-range queen.
- **No offer hook**: no free shipping callout, no bundle savings, no "spend $75 get free shipping" — for someone who bundles Fenty Skin and Fenty Beauty in the same cart, I want that threshold visible.
- **No social proof**: no star ratings, no review counts, no "10K+ five-star reviews" badge. Fenty has a cult following — let me see it.
- **No loyalty callback**: if I have Pro Filt'r points sitting in my account, I have no idea right now. That's a missed conversion hook for a returning shopper.

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty balance strip or "You have X points" bar** under the main nav for signed-in users — that alone pulls returning shoppers straight to cart.
- **Rotate the hero carousel to include a complexion/Match Stix slide** alongside the A$AP Rocky drop. One swipe should show me a deep shade range lifestyle shot with a woman of color applying the product, not a separate page hunt.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** in the announcement banner alongside the collab CTA — "Free shipping over $50 | A$AP Rocky × Fenty Skin SHOP NOW" would pull double duty.
- **Add a "New Shades / Fan Favorites" horizontal scroll rail** below the hero so I can tap directly into Match Stix or Soft'lit without navigating the full category page.
