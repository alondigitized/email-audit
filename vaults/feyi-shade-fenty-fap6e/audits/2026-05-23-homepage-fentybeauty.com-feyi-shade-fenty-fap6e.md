# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- OK Fenty, the Summer Warm-Up sale energy is real — 60% off is not a whisper, that's a statement. The hero is all Fenty Skin, which tells me Rihanna is pushing the skincare cross-sell right now, not the complexion drops I came for. Still, this is the brand I know, and the vibe is clean enough that I'm not bouncing immediately.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 60% OFF SELECT ITEMS" as the hero headline** — Fenty doesn't run deep sales often, so when they do, it feels like an event. I'm taking that seriously.
- **The Fenty Skin product placement in the hero** — That lavender jar + bronze applicator + the hot pink lip product is a bundle shot. I'm a Fenty Skin cross-shopper and this is exactly the kind of visual that makes me want to see what's in the sale.
- **"Savings as hot as you."** — That copy is so Fenty. It's not generic sale language, it's the brand's voice. I feel seen.
- **Top banner with free shipping + returns as the account hook** — Smart. Putting the incentive for logging in or signing up at the very top means I know before I even look at anything what I get for being a member.
- **"The Summer Warmup Sale" as a nav tab** — It's right there, one tap, no digging.

## What's weak

- **No complexion product in the hero** — I came to this site for shade range. I am a Match Stix devotee. The entire hero is Fenty Skin + lip. Where's the Pro Filt'r? Where's the Match Stix? Where are the complexion drops? The sale might include complexion but you'd never know from this screenshot.
- **No model on screen** — This is a product-flat hero. I want to see these shades on skin that looks like mine. The whole point of Fenty is the inclusive casting — a product shot alone misses that entirely.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no detail** — I've been burned before hitting 60% off and finding out the thing I actually want isn't included. That "exclusions apply" with no link or list is a small trust chip.
- **No countdown or end date** — The sale feels like it could run forever, which undercuts urgency. If this ends Sunday, tell me it ends Sunday.
- **Free shipping threshold not specified** — The banner says "free standard shipping" for account holders but doesn't say the minimum order. Is it $0? $35? I have to dig.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the product-flat hero for a lifestyle shot on a model with a deeper skin tone** — Show the Match Stix, the Pro Filt'r, the Gloss Bomb on actual skin. That's the Fenty promise and it's missing above the fold.
- **Add a "Sale ends [date]" line directly under the sale headline** — One line of copy, ships next hour, immediately adds real urgency without manufactured countdown spam.
- **Put a single hero button below "Savings as hot as you."** with copy like "Shop the Sale" — Right now the only tap target is a nav tab. A full-width button in the hero converts faster on mobile.
- **Add a "Complexion deals inside" sub-tag under the hero** — Even a four-word line that calls out complexion or shade-range products in the sale would stop shade-focused shoppers like me from assuming the 60% is just skincare.
