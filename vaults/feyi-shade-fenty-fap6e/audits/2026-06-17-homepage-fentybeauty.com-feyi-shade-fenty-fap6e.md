# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- OK so I opened this and my first reaction was — yes, this is Fenty doing what Fenty does. The model is stunning, the shade on her lips is exactly the kind of deep nude that makes you go "wait, what is that?" The hero is laser-focused on Gloss Bomb Stix and the "Bomb Nudes" campaign, and it feels like a launch moment, not a leftover clearance vibe. But I'm already past the hero on a scroll and I haven't seen a single button yet — that's a problem.

## What's working

- **"YOUR NEW BOMB NUDES" hero headline** — this is the exact language I use in group chats. "Bomb nudes" is a community phrase, not a marketing invention. It signals Rihanna's team actually knows the culture.
- **Model casting** — deep skin, confident gaze, lip color that reads true on her actual complexion. As someone who buys by shade range first, seeing a rich nude on a dark-skin model tells me the shade will translate. That's the entire pitch.
- **"GLOSS BOMB STIX" product label above the headline** — the label-first hierarchy tells me exactly what we're selling before I read the tagline. No mystery.
- **Top banner promo** — "Buy 2 body products, get 15% off" is visible at load. Even if it's not for the hero product, it anchors the expectation that a deal exists on this page.
- **Category tabs** — "Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Body" in a horizontal scroll lets me pivot to Fenty Skin bundles in one tap, which I do when I'm restocking.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible** — I have scrolled past the subhead copy ("Glossy, luscious + made just for you") and there is still no "Shop Now" or "Shop Bomb Nudes" button in my viewport. If I weren't already a Fenty loyalist, I might not know what to tap.
- **Body promo on a lip launch page** — the banner offers 15% off body products, but the hero is a lip product. The cross-category disconnect feels like two campaigns running at once without a hierarchy decision.
- **Subhead copy is cut off** — "Glossy, luscious + made just for you" begins and immediately gets clipped at the bottom of the screen. It's half a sentence. That's sloppy on mobile.
- **Zero loyalty touchpoints** — I have a Fenty account and I know there's a loyalty program, but there's no "Your Points: 420" or member-pricing badge anywhere visible. I shouldn't have to tap the account icon to know I have status.
- **No shade count or range signal** — the entire draw of Gloss Bomb Stix for someone like me is how many nudes they've dropped. "Bomb Nudes" without "in 12 shades" or a shade picker visible is a missed conversion hook.

## Recommendations

- **Pin the CTA button inside the hero image** so it's visible at load — "Shop Bomb Nudes" styled in the dark nude palette. No scrolling required to find the first action.
- **Swap the body promo banner for a lip-launch offer** — "Buy any 2 Gloss Bomb Stix, get 15% off" keeps the promo mechanic but aligns it to what the hero is selling.
- **Add a shade-count signal in the hero** — "12 new nudes. Find yours." as a secondary subhead turns browsing intent into shade-matching intent immediately.
- **Surface loyalty balance near the account icon** — even a small "Your Points: •••" chip next to the account icon reinforces that returning shoppers get something for showing up.
