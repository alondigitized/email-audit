# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Fenty doing its thing — clean, editorial, unmistakably the brand. Right now it's leading with a Summer Warm-Up sale anchored by a Fenty SKIN bundle (that lavender tub, the glass serum, the rose-gold pencil stick, and a hot pink lip product), which honestly is exactly how I shop: I came for Match Stix and I leave with a Fenty Skin routine. The 60% off hook is loud enough to make me stop scrolling Reels, but the missing "Shop Now" button in the actual hero is a fumble.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 60% OFF SELECT ITEMS" headline**: I clocked this immediately. 60% on Fenty? That stops the scroll.
- **Fenty SKIN cross-brand bundle in the hero**: The rose-gold pencil stick, the serum, the lavender tub, the hot-pink lip — this is the Fenty multiverse and I shop all of it. Showing the bundle tells me this is a stacked cart opportunity, not a one-SKU sale.
- **"Savings as hot as you." tagline**: Corniness tolerance is a personal thing but for this brand, this voice is exactly right. It's not trying to be Glossier or Aesop — it's Fenty.
- **"The Summer Warmup Sale" nav pill**: Easy to find, clearly tappable, labeled with intent. First tap off the homepage is friction-free.
- **Announcement bar with account creation perk**: "free U.S. standard shipping + returns" is a clear reason to sign in. I'd tap SIGN IN before I tap the sale.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the hero**: There's a great offer and beautiful product imagery but I have to find the nav pill to act on it. A "Shop the Sale" button under "Savings as hot as you." would convert right there.
- **No model on screen**: This hero is a product flat-lay on a gray background. I need to see what this looks like on skin — specifically, on a complexion like mine. The no-model approach works for minimal skincare brands, but Fenty's whole brand identity is in the shade range and the people wearing it.
- **"UP TO 60% OFF" is a weasel range**: What is actually 60% off? Match Stix? Foundation? Fenty Skin only? "Exclusions apply" with no category preview makes me suspicious the best stuff isn't in the sale.
- **No shade-range signal**: Fenty's biggest differentiator — the reason it exists — is invisible on this homepage. Even a "40+ shades" callout somewhere above fold would remind me why I'm here over MAC or Charlotte Tilbury.
- **Loyalty program completely absent**: I know Fenty has one. I don't see my points balance, a member-only offer, nothing. If I'm a returning customer, the homepage doesn't feel like it knows I exist.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" button** directly in the hero section under the offer copy — button text: "Shop Up to 60% Off" with the destination being the Summer Warmup Sale landing page. Ships in a day.
- **Swap the pure product flat-lay for a model-on-skin shot**: Show the bronzer stick blended on a deep complexion, the lip product worn. The Fenty customer needs to see themselves. Keep the product stack as a secondary thumbnail if needed.
- **Surface one specific sale category in the hero** so I know what's actually 60% off: e.g., "Fenty Skin Bundles up to 60% off" rather than the vague catch-all. Trust over hype.
- **Add a returning-customer loyalty nudge** in the announcement bar: "Welcome back — you have X points. [View Rewards]" would convert me immediately and costs nothing to add for logged-in users.
