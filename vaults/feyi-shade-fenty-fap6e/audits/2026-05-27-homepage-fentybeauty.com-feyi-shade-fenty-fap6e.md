# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- I come to Fenty ready to restock my Match Stix and they greet me with a Fenty Skin Body and Fenty Hair bundle story. "All Things Amber Bouquet" is a new scent launch — Butta Drop plus The Mista — and the editorial shot is gorgeous, very "elevated apothecary dark academia." But this is a complexion brand to me first, and the hero is giving body-and-hair when I came in hot looking for face.

## What's working

- **"ALL THINGS AMBER BOUQUET" headline** — big, bold, white all-caps on dark background. Instant read. The scent-layering concept ("Butta Drop + The Mista") feels curated and intentional, not random.
- **Product photography** — the coconut half, calla lilies, amber botanicals, dark backdrop. This is *editorial*. It doesn't look like a drugstore brand having a moment; it looks like a brand that knows what it is.
- **"bloomin' + bougie" copy** — as a 28-year-old that word is literally in my group chat. The copy register matches me exactly without trying too hard.
- **Free shipping + returns top banner** — I can't miss it, and the sign-in / sign-up split gives me an action either way without being aggressive.
- **Category tabs** — Shop All / Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Hair. I know exactly where to tap to get where I'm going. One tap.

## What's weak

- **No hero CTA button** — there is genuinely no "Shop the Collection" or "Discover Amber Bouquet" button anchoring the bottom of the hero. The products just... sit there. For an e-commerce homepage that's a real miss.
- **Zero complexion presence above the fold** — as someone who came here for face, I have to mentally file this hero away and go hunt for my products. Fenty Beauty (makeup) is the brand's core, and it's invisible at open.
- **No price or product link** — I can see the bottle names, I cannot tap to a PDP. There's no price to set expectation. The hero reads editorial but not shoppable.
- **No people / no skin** — for a brand built on 40 shades and radical shade inclusion, showing me products on a dark shelf instead of real, diverse skin feels like a step away from the brand's whole identity.
- **Loyalty is mentioned as acquisition, not recognition** — if I already have an account I have to sign in myself to see my perks. There's no "hey, you have 300 points" energy here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a hero CTA button** — even something as simple as `SHOP AMBER BOUQUET →` anchored to the bottom of the product shot converts the editorial into a click path. Right now this is a lookbook, not a store.
- **Swap or split the hero** — run the Amber Bouquet story in the hero but add a secondary module immediately below showing one complexion hero (Match Stix, Pro Filt'r, or whatever's in the current makeup drop). Fenty Beauty the makeup brand should not be invisible on fentybeauty.com.
- **Show skin** — a real person wearing the Amber Bouquet scent family (or even a quick lifestyle cut of someone spraying The Mista in a mirror) grounds the product in the brand's equity. The all-product still reads beautiful but cold.
- **Surface a loyalty hook for signed-in users** — if you know I have an account, show me my points balance or a member-only offer in the top banner instead of the generic sign-up line.
