# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, visually this is giving me exactly what I expect from Fenty — moody, editorial, "we know we're the moment" energy with that diamond-prism flatlay. But this "Shine Like Diamonds in the Dark" drop is a shimmer/highlight collection, not shade range news, and there's not a single face or skin tone on this screen to tell me if it's actually for me. Right now it reads like a brand pushing a limited-edition glam capsule with a generic 25% off banner riding shotgun.

## What's working

- **The prism/diamond product flatlay** — genuinely gorgeous, feels expensive and intentional, exactly the aesthetic bar I expect from a Fenty launch.
- **"NEW + LIMITED EDITION" tag** — that's my trigger word. I show up for limited drops.
- **Top banner offer with a visible SHOP NOW** — clean, no scavenger hunt required to find the deal.
- **Category nav row (Shop All / Beauty / Skin / Body / Hair)** — one tap from anywhere to get to what I actually came for.

## What's weak

- **No face, no skin tone diversity anywhere on screen** — for a brand literally built on "40 shades changed the industry," a product-only flatlay this high up feels like a missed flex.
- **"Shine Like Diamonds in the Dark" tells me nothing about shade or formula** — is this new Match Stix shades, new Gloss Bomb, new highlighter? I can't tell from the headline alone.
- **25% off "summer staples" banner doesn't connect to the diamond hero below it** — two different campaigns competing for my attention in the same fold.
- **No loyalty/Rewards callout visible** — I'm a repeat Fenty buyer, and there's nothing here acknowledging that.

## Recommendations

- Swap in at least one campaign shot with a model wearing the collection so I can see undertone/finish on actual skin before I scroll further.
- Add a one-line subhead under "Shine Like Diamonds in the Dark" naming the actual products (e.g. "New Gloss Bomb + Killawatt shades").
- Surface a Rewards/points teaser near the top nav — even a small "Earn points on this order" would keep me from bouncing to check my balance elsewhere.
- Reconcile the two offers — either fold the 25% off into the diamond drop story or make clear they're separate collections.
