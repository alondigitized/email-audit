# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Fenty Beauty opened with the launch I actually already knew was coming — the Sun Stalk'r Soufflé Pressed Mousse Cream Bronzer, which means they're doing their job. This is a drop-centric brand acting like a drop-centric brand: Rihanna front-and-center in full glam, new product above the fold, 20% off in the nav strip. It feels current and confident. What I'm not getting is a button to actually tap.

## What's working

- **The Rihanna creative**: She's not doing a perfume-campaign stare-into-the-distance shot. She's wearing the bronzer in full glam, and I can read the application. That matters to me — I want to see the product ON, not next to.
- **"20% Off Select Items" in the nav strip**: I clocked it before I even hit the hero. I'm immediately thinking: is Match Stix in that sale?
- **Product name and description**: "SUN STALK'R SOUFFLÉ" with "bouncy + blurring, airbrushed blending" is exactly the claims language I evaluate when I'm deciding whether to try a new complexion formula. Short, functional, credible.
- **Account sign-in with a benefit hook**: "Create an account to unlock free U.S. standard shipping + returns" — it's not just "sign in," it's a reason to sign in. I respect that.
- **Clean mobile render**: No layout chaos, the split product/model layout looks intentional on a small screen.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button above the fold**: The description trails off at "bouncy + blurring…" and there's no "Shop Now" or "Get It" button visible. For a launch product, that's leaving conversion on the table.
- **"20% Off Select Items" with no specifics**: I want to know if this is complexion, or if it's only lip/eye. "Select Items" is vague enough that I have to click through to find out — which is friction, not excitement.
- **No shade range signal**: This is a bronzer launch. For someone like me, the first question is always: does this come in deep shades or is it four medium tans? Nothing on the homepage tells me.
- **No loyalty depth**: The top banner is really just an account-creation incentive. There's no mention of a rewards program, points balance, or Fenty Insider-style hook. If I already have an account, what's in it for me?

## Recommendations

- **Add a visible CTA button below the product name**: "Shop the Launch" or "Get Sun Stalk'R" — just give me something to tap. The product is right there, I'm interested, don't make me scroll to find the action.
- **Replace "Select Items" with a category call-out**: "20% Off Complexion" or "20% Off Foundations & Match Stix" would convert me faster than a vague discount.
- **Show the shade range in the hero**: Even a single line — "12 shades, deep to fair" — removes the question that makes me hesitate on bronzer launches. It also signals that Fenty actually built this for all of us, which is the whole brand promise.
- **Add a loyalty balance or member badge if SIGN IN is visible**: Something like "You have 240 points" or "Member exclusive: extra 5% off" for signed-in users would turn that top banner from acquisition to retention.
