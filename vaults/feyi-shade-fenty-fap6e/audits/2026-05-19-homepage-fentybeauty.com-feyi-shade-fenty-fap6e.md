# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- OK so Fenty is clearly in their Amber Bouquet era — this is a full Fenty Skin body + Fenty Hair launch moment, products staged beautifully with the coconut and white lily, very editorial. It reads like a prestige fragrance house, not just a beauty brand. But I came here for complexion and I'm landing on a body milk and a hair mist. That's not necessarily a bounce, because I do cross-shop Fenty Skin — but it's not the hero that speaks my language.

## What's working

- **Top banner account hook** — "Create an account to unlock free U.S. standard shipping + returns" is low-friction and specific. That's a tangible benefit, not just "join our community."
- **Sub-navigation tabs** — Shop All / Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Hair. I can get to makeup in literally one tap. That's clean IA.
- **"bloomin' + bougie" copy** — That's Rihanna's energy transferred into product language. It's speaking my generation's slang without trying too hard. Feels like Fenty, not a copycat.
- **Product staging** — The amber glass Butta Drop bottle against the lily and coconut is genuinely beautiful. I'd stop scrolling for this visual if I were in a Fenty Skin headspace.
- **Category breadth visible at a glance** — Fenty Beauty, Fenty Skin, and Fenty Hair all surfaced in the sub-nav, so even if the hero isn't my thing, I know my category is right there.

## What's weak

- **No complexion product in the hero** — I'm a Match Stix devotee landing on a body milk. The hero doesn't know why I came. That's a miss even if I'll navigate anyway.
- **No CTA button in the hero** — "ALL THINGS AMBER BOUQUET" is a statement, not a tap. Where do I go from here? There's no visible "Shop Now" or "Shop Amber Bouquet" button to close the loop on this launch.
- **No model, no shade story** — Shade range is why I trust Fenty. Showing products without a model wearing them near the fold means the thing that makes Fenty *Fenty* isn't present. That's a real miss on their own homepage.
- **Loyalty = free shipping only** — The top banner dangles the carrot of account creation but there's no hint of a loyalty program, early access, or rewards. For someone who buys at launch, that's leaving value uncommunicated.
- **No social proof** — No "as seen on" badge, no review count, no "X people love this." For a product I've never tried (Butta Drop), that frictionless proof is missing.

## Recommendations

- **Put a model in the hero** — Swap the product-flat for a lifestyle shot showing the Amber Bouquet line on a brown-skinned model. Fenty's whole equity is shade inclusion. Use it.
- **Add a CTA button** — Below "ALL THINGS AMBER BOUQUET," drop a pill button reading "Shop Amber Bouquet" so the launch has a click path. Right now it's a billboard.
- **Surface a complexion module below the fold** — Add a "Back to your roots: Match Stix, Soft Matte Filt'r" strip for returning makeup customers who just landed on a body care hero. Don't lose us.
- **Upgrade the loyalty callout** — Replace "create an account" language with something like "Members get early access + free shipping" — even if the program is the same, the framing does more work.
