# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Fenty Beauty right now is in full Fenty Skin body mode — the hero is pushing their moisturizer line with that signature glowy-skin energy and a gorgeous deep-toned model. As someone who opens this app looking to check if the new Match Stix drops are in yet, I appreciate the aesthetic but I'm already mentally tapping past the body stuff. This homepage reads luxe, intentional, and very Rihanna — it just isn't talking to my complexion habit first.

## What's working

- **The top banner's account-unlock offer** — free shipping AND returns as the immediate hook to create an account is genuinely compelling, especially for a repeat buyer like me who orders multiple shades to compare. That's a real retention mechanism.
- **"20% Off Select Items" pill** — I clocked it immediately. Even if I don't know what's in scope, it primes me to browse before I decide to bounce.
- **The hero model** — shot from neck down, deep-warm skin tone, absolutely glowing. That's the Fenty promise made visual. Even selling body cream, that image is aspirational for me in the right direction.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no pop-up assault, no auto-playing video blocking the scroll, the chat bubble tucks neatly to the corner. Fenty's mobile UX is not fussing with me.

## What's weak

- **Body care hero when I'm here for complexion** — I cross-shop Fenty Skin for bundles, yes, but I come to fentybeauty.com to check Match Stix restocks and new shade launches. This hero doesn't acknowledge that a huge slice of their audience is color shoppers, not skincare shoppers.
- **"SHOP NOW" is a dead-end CTA for me** — it's going to a body moisturizer PDP, not the complexion category I want. It's unambiguous but it's pointing in the wrong direction for my intent.
- **No shade range signals above fold** — Fenty's entire brand promise is inclusivity and range. None of that is visible here. No "40 shades" nod, no complexion imagery, nothing that says "we built this for your skin tone specifically."
- **"Fenty" pill in nav is cut off** — it's clearly "Fenty Skin" but showing a truncated label on a scrollable pill row looks sloppy on first load. A new visitor has no idea what's hidden right.
- **Zero urgency on the 20% offer** — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing. The discount pill floats there with no reason to act now vs. later.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a complexion-first carousel with a Fenty Skin slot second** — show a Match Stix or Pro Filt'r hero with a "New in your shade range" callout, then let the body moisture hero be slide 2. Color shoppers land, cross-sell happens naturally on scroll.
- **Add a shade-finder or "find your match" shortcut above the fold** — even a single pill in that nav strip that says "Find My Shade" would speak directly to the audience Fenty earned. That's a one-day dev lift.
- **Put a deadline on the 20% offer** — "20% Off Select Items — Ends Sunday" turns a passive banner into a reason to browse now instead of saving the tab for later.
- **Fix the truncated "Fenty" nav pill** — either shorten the label to "Skin" or widen the pill so "Fenty Skin" reads complete on first visible load. Small thing, but Fenty is a precision brand.
