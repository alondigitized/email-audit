# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- OK, so Fenty Beauty loaded and the first thing I see is Rihanna literally serving concert stage realness in a full leather-and-fur moment — and my thumb stopped. This homepage knows exactly who it's talking to: the person who watched that performance and immediately started googling what she had on her face. What it's pushing right now is pure editorial hype, a "get the look" moment tied to a Rihanna appearance, plus a teaser for an A$AP Rocky x Fenty Skin collab up top. High on the vibe, light on the product.

## What's working

- **The A$AP Rocky x Fenty Skin "COMING SOON" banner** — I cross-shop Fenty Skin for bundles, and I already clock every Rihanna announcement. Seeing A$AP in the collab universe immediately makes me want to check what's dropping. That's effective.
- **"GET RIHANNA'S CONCERT DRIP" headline** — the phrasing is aspirational and possessive ("get" not "see") and it positions me as someone who can actually recreate this, not just admire it from afar.
- **"we've got all the deetz" subtext** — that's not corporate speak, that's my group chat energy. The copy register is dead-on for a 28-year-old who speaks Millennial-blending-Gen-Z.
- **Rihanna as the hero model** — not a "campaign model," not a face of the brand placeholder, but Rihanna in an actual real-world moment. As someone who knows which complexion product she actually reaches for vs. which she just has gifted to her, this distinction matters.
- **Category tabs in nav** — "Fenty Beauty," "Fenty Skin," "Fenty Body" are one tap away and immediately visible below the logo. No hunting required.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button visible above the fold** — I can see the headline and the vibe but there's no "Shop the Look" or "Get the Kit" button. I don't know where to tap to actually buy anything.
- **Zero complexion product specificity** — as a Match Stix devotee, I want to see what she's wearing. The subtext promises "all the deetz" but the homepage doesn't show me a single product, SKU, or shade in this viewport.
- **No offer or hook for a returning customer** — I've bought from Fenty before. There's no loyalty balance, no member-exclusive anything, no "welcome back" signal. I'm being treated the same as a first-time visitor.
- **"COMING SOON" teaser is exciting but stranding** — the A$AP x Fenty Skin drop is interesting but there's no countdown, no waitlist CTA, no "notify me" hook. They surfaced the energy and then left me with nowhere to put it.
- **No pricing, no offer** — absolutely nothing in this viewport tells me what anything costs or that buying right now has any advantage over buying next week.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Look" button directly on the hero** — floating CTA over the image, something like "Shop Rihanna's Kit" that lands on a curated PDP or editorial collection page. The look is the hook, make it shoppable immediately.
- **Turn the A$AP x Fenty Skin teaser into a waitlist capture** — add a "Notify Me" or "Join the Drop List" tap right on that top banner. Right now it's a tease that disappears the moment I scroll past.
- **Surface at least one complexion product or shade in a sub-banner** — even a secondary module under the hero showing "Match Stix Skinstick used in her concert look — 3 shades left" would activate people like me who came to shop, not just browse.
- **Show my rewards balance or a loyalty hook** — a small member badge or "You have 200 Fenty Beauty points" note would instantly re-engage me as a returning buyer.
