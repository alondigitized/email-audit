# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- OK so Fenty opened strong with a full-bleed close-up and a real discount — 25% off $75+ is not a soft offer, that's legitimate. But I'm immediately clocking a light-medium complexioned model in the hero when I, a Match Stix devotee, showed up specifically because Fenty's whole identity is the shade spectrum — and that choice doesn't land the same for me. Brand feels premium and current, but this particular homepage push is more "general beauty shopper" than "Fenty faithful who knows her 430W from her 445N."

## What's working

- **The 25% off $75+ headline** — it's big, it's white, it's on top of the model. I read it in under a second. That's the only job of above-the-fold copy and it does it.
- **"Viral hits, routine faves + bestsellers are calling—come thru"** — this is 100% Fenty's voice. "Come thru" is exactly how the brand talks to me and I feel it.
- **Sub-nav bar ("Shop All / Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Fragrance")** — one tap to the category I want. No hunting through a buried hamburger menu for my product world.
- **Purple background with close-up skin shot** — the art direction is distinctly Fenty, not generic beauty. I know what brand I'm on immediately.
- **Free shipping threshold visible** — $100 is noted in the banner, even with the "Testing" weirdness. Better than hiding it.

## What's weak

- **"Testing" in the announcement bar** — "Free Shipping on Orders Over $100 Testing" is a straight-up staging artifact live on production. It undermines every offer on the page. If I'm a first-time visitor I'm questioning whether this site is real.
- **Model shade skews light/medium in the hero** — Fenty's entire brand equity with me is built on "every shade, every skin." Putting a light-medium complexion front and center on the hero when I'm here for the full range is a miss. Show me the 495N or the deep-tone blush play.
- **No CTA button visible above the fold** — the offer copy cuts off and I have to assume there's a shop button below. On mobile, that's a friction point. Don't make me scroll to find the action.
- **No loyalty hook** — I have a Fenty account. I've bought at launch events. There's zero acknowledgment that I exist as a returning customer — no points balance, no member pricing callout, nothing.
- **No product specificity** — "viral hits" and "bestsellers" is vague. I know Match Stix, Gloss Bomb, Pro Filt'r. Name them. Fenty has iconic SKUs — lean on them.

## Recommendations

- **Kill "Testing" from the announcement bar immediately** — this is a one-line fix that prevents every visitor from questioning site legitimacy.
- **Swap the hero model for a deeper-complexion look** — feature a 430W–495N range shot or a side-by-side multi-shade moment. This is Fenty's moat and the hero image should say it without a word.
- **Pull the CTA button above the fold** — on this phone viewport the button is cut off. Move it up so the entire "25% OFF + SHOP NOW" unit is visible without scrolling.
- **Add a returning-shopper hook in the nav or banner** — a "Your Shade: 435W — Shop Your Matches" or a small loyalty points display turns a generic promo page into a personalized one.
