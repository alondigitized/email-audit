# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I land on fentybeauty.com and the first thing I see is that A$AP Rocky x Fenty Skin collab teaser — "COMING SOON" — and then the hero is pushing the new Vanilla Flowers + Bouquet Butta body milk launch. Very Fenty Skin era right now, which makes sense, but as someone who pulled up specifically to see what's happening in complexion? I feel like I opened the door and nobody checked my ID. This reads like a clean luxury skincare brand mid-launch, not the shade-range innovator I trust with my undertone.

## What's working

- **A$AP Rocky x Fenty Skin banner** — "COMING SOON" across the top is exactly the kind of thing I need to see to know the brand is moving. I'm already plotting the drop.
- **"VANILLA FLOWERS + HEY, BOUQUET BUTTA DROP" headline** — the naming is pure Fenty voice, I hear it in Rihanna's cadence, it lands
- **Product photography** — the two bottles styled with botanicals against a clean background is elevated without being inaccessible. Feels like something she'd actually post.
- **"SHOP BODY MILK" CTA** — no mystery, no "discover," no "explore." You tap, you get there. I appreciate directness.
- **Nav tabs** — "Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Body" in a row means I can pivot to complexion in one tap, which saves me.

## What's weak

- **Hero is body care, not complexion** — I'm a shade devotee. I'm here for what's happening in foundation, blush, and Match Stix. A full hero dedicated to body milk doesn't give me a reason to engage beyond curiosity.
- **Zero shade-range energy** — not one mention of new shades, expanded ranges, or undertone innovation above the fold. That's the whole reason I trust this brand over anyone else.
- **No loyalty hook at all** — I have Fenty account history. There's no rewards balance surfaced, no "welcome back," no "you've earned X." That's a real miss for a returning buyer like me.
- **"COMING SOON" with no sign-up option** — the Rocky collab teaser doesn't convert me into anything. No early-access opt-in, no notification link. The hype has nowhere to land.
- **No pricing, no offer, no threshold** — I don't know if I'm one item away from free shipping and that matters when I'm deciding whether to add to cart.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "New in Complexion" rail directly below the hero** — even two or three product tiles with shade names would re-anchor this page for makeup shoppers like me who arrived with intent.
- **Wire the Rocky collab banner to an SMS or email opt-in** — "COMING SOON" is dead weight without a capture. One tap → "notify me at drop" turns banner viewers into a list.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards widget in the nav or hero subtext** — even a single line like "You have 240 points — shop now" would pull me into the funnel immediately.
- **Swap "Get lightweight, silky moisture in two irresistible scents" for a scent-personality hook** — something like "Vanilla Flowers for the soft-glam days, Bouquet Butta for the rest" gives me an identity reason to choose, not just a feature list.
