# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- OK so Fenty is pushing the Summer Warm-Up sale hard — this is a Fenty Skin-forward moment, not a Fenty Beauty makeup moment, which is a whole choice. The homepage feels like a clearance event dressed up in lavender and magenta, and "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT ITEMS" is doing a lot of heavy lifting with "Exclusions apply" right underneath. It's cute, but I'm a complexion girl — where's my Match Stix?

## What's working

- **The Fenty Skin bundle visual** — that lavender round container with the pads and the fuchsia lip product is gorgeous, it catches the eye immediately and I already know I want to understand that bundle
- **"THE SUMMER WARM-UP / UP TO 60% OFF" headline pairing** — clean, punchy, and "Savings as hot as you." earns its cute-factor for someone in their late 20s without being cringe
- **The top banner doing double duty** — free shipping + free returns communicated in one line is genuinely useful and it's the first thing I read before the hero
- **The secondary nav tabs** — "The Summer Warmup Sale / Shop All / Fenty Beauty" gives me exactly three paths and I can feel the navigation logic without hunting
- **No forced modal or pop-up** — the page lets me breathe, which is rare and I appreciate it

## What's weak

- **No complexion/makeup products above the fold** — I'm a Match Stix devotee coming to Fenty Beauty and the hero is all Fenty Skin; that crossover only works for me because I already know both brands, but it's leading with the wrong foot for a makeup-first customer
- **No CTA button visible** — "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT ITEMS" is a statement, not a tap target; I don't see a "Shop the Sale" button in this viewport and that's a conversion miss
- **No model** — the product-only hero is beautiful but Fenty's whole identity is showing the shade range ON someone; a product shot with no face on a brand built on inclusivity feels like something is missing
- **No shade-range signal** — if this is a complexion brand, where's the visual proof of shade depth? Nothing in this viewport tells me if my shade is in the sale
- **"Exclusions apply" with no quick link** — I'm going to immediately wonder if Match Stix or the Eaze Drop I've been eyeing are excluded; there's no quick tap to check

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Makeup" secondary CTA** alongside the Fenty Skin hero — a split layout or a quick-link pill underneath the sale headline that takes complexion shoppers directly to makeup in the sale
- **Swap the product-only shot for a model-inclusive shot** — show the Soft'lit Luminous Foundation or the Match Stix on a real face; Fenty's shade-range story IS the trust signal, it needs to be on the homepage
- **Add a "Complexion in the Sale" category tile** directly below the hero — something like "Foundations | Concealers | Match Stix — See what's included" to answer the exclusion anxiety immediately
- **Put a tappable "Shop the Sale" button below the headline** — right now there's copy and then nothing; that gap is a bounce waiting to happen
