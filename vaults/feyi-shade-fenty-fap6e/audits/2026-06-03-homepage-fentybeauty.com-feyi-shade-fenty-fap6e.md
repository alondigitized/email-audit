# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Fenty Beauty's homepage is in full Fenty Skin Body mode right now — warm amber tones, calla lily, "Amber Bouquet" fragrance story. It's a clean, editorial launch moment. But I'm here for Match Stix and complexion, and the first thing I see is body lotion and a hair mist. The brand feels premium and on-point aesthetically, but it's not speaking to my reason for showing up.

## What's working

- **"ALL THINGS AMBER BOUQUET" headline** — the copy is confident and brand-right. "Bloomin' + bougie" is exactly Fenty's millennial-luxury register and I clock it instantly.
- **Category nav bar** — "Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Fragrance" is one tap to wherever I'm going. Doesn't bury anything.
- **Product art direction** — the warm terracotta background with a calla lily against the amber pump bottles is aesthetically coherent. Even if it's not my category, it looks intentional and expensive.
- **The carousel indicator** — left/right arrows signal there's more below; I'm not assuming this is the whole story.

## What's weak

- **No complexion product above the fold** — as a Match Stix devotee, I see zero shade-range messaging. If I didn't already know Fenty does complexion, this page wouldn't tell me.
- **Zero CTA button** — there's no "Shop Amber Bouquet" or "Shop Fenty Skin" button anywhere visible. Carousel arrows are not a CTA. That's a conversion hole.
- **No offer or incentive** — no free shipping threshold, no launch promo, no bundle deal. For a product launch this feels like a missed opportunity, especially since Rihanna's bundles are a reason people like me cross-shop Fenty Skin.
- **No model** — it's a product flat lay. Fenty's whole identity is diverse real women wearing the products. Seeing product bottles next to a flower feels more like Aesop than Fenty.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'm a repeat buyer who buys at launch. There's nothing here acknowledging me as a returning customer.

## Recommendations

- **Put a model using the product in the hero** — even a close crop of a deep-skin hand applying Butta Drop would ground this in Fenty's identity. The calla lily reads fragrance brand, not Fenty.
- **Add a visible CTA button** — "Shop Amber Bouquet" or "Get the Bundle" directly under the subtext copy. A carousel arrow is not an invitation to buy.
- **Surface a launch offer or bundle price** — "Butta Drop + The Mista: bundle and save" with a real number would give me a reason to tap immediately instead of continuing to scroll.
- **Add one complexion teaser module** — a secondary band that says "New Match Stix shades" or "Shop Soft'Lit Foundation" reminds me this is still the brand I came for.
