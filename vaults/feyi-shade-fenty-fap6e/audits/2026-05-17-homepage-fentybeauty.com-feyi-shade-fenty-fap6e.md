# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Fenty Beauty's homepage is pushing their new Sun Stalk'r Soufflé Pressed Mousse Cream Bronzer hard — split hero with the product compact on the left and Rihanna herself on the right, which is exactly the kind of visual I come here for. The brand looks elevated, editorial, and intentional. Right now it's all about this bronzer launch, and honestly the placement feels clean and confident — very on brand.

## What's working

- **Rihanna is literally in the hero.** She's holding or wearing the product adjacent to the compact — that's the ultimate trust signal for this brand. I don't need a review badge when Ri's in the shot.
- **"NEW" label in bold** makes the editorial intention immediately clear. This isn't a sale page — it's a launch event, and I shop launches.
- **Product + founder pairing** — the left-right split (product closeup / founder portrait) is a smart dual register: I see the compact in detail AND I see how it reads on a deep complexion. That's shade-range signaling without a shade chart.
- **"Bouncy + blurring for airbrushed blending"** — the sub-copy speaks to formula nerds like me. I want to know *how* it performs, not just that it exists.
- **Top banner** calling out free U.S. standard shipping + returns on account creation is a clean incentive visible before anything else.
- **"20% Off Select Items"** pill in the nav strip — deal-forward, immediately scannable.

## What's weak

- **No shade story yet.** This is a bronzer launch and I'm already wondering: how many shades? What's the range for deeper complexions? Nothing above the fold tells me this works for my skin tone beyond Rihanna being in the frame.
- **Rewards / loyalty is invisible.** I've bought here before. Where's my points balance? Where's the member-price badge? The top banner nudges account *creation* but says nothing to existing members.
- **The nav-strip promos feel disconnected.** "20% Off Select Items" and "Fenty Beauty" in the pill row look like category chips, not campaign messaging. I can't tell if the bronzer is in that 20% or not.
- **CTA button is cut off.** I can see a button starting to peek at the very bottom of the viewport but the copy is cropped — I have to scroll before I know what I'm tapping.
- **No urgency signal on the launch.** If this is a new drop, is it limited? Can I pre-add to cart? Launch energy without scarcity copy feels like a missed beat.

## Recommendations

- **Add a shade-count badge or callout directly on the hero** — "Available in 10 shades" or "Built for all skin tones" overlaid on the product compact would convert shade-loyal shoppers like me before they even scroll.
- **Surface the loyalty member benefit in the hero section** — a small "Fenty Beauty members: earn 2X points on new launches" ribbon would keep returning shoppers from bouncing to look for their account.
- **Make the "20% Off" pill scannable** — add tooltip or anchor text clarifying whether the new bronzer qualifies; ambiguous promo copy creates friction.
- **Bring the CTA above the fold completely** — "Shop Sun Stalk'r Soufflé" should be a full, visible button in the hero, not something I have to scroll 10px to find.
