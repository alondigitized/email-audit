# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay Rihanna is literally on the screen, looking like a whole moment in a silk top with pearls, introducing a new bronzer — this is the homepage flexing exactly what Fenty does best: product launch energy meets founder face. The brand is pushing the Sun Stalk'r Soufflé Pressed Mousse Cream Bronzer hard, and as someone who's clocked every Fenty complexion drop, I'm immediately curious. But the page cuts off before I can see what shade range looks like, and that's where I start having questions.

## What's working

- **Rihanna's face as hero image** — not a model, the founder herself, which signals this is a product she personally curated. That carries weight with me.
- **"NEW" label + product name front-and-center** — "SUN STALK'R SOUFFLÉ PRESSED MOUSSE CREAM BRONZER" reads like a launch event product, and I show up to those.
- **Split product + face layout** — one side shows the tortoiseshell compact up close, the other shows it worn. I can see texture and finish without tapping anywhere.
- **Top banner: free returns** — this matters enormously for a complexion product. I return shades that oxidize. Knowing returns are free removes friction before I even land on the product page.
- **"bouncy + blurring for airbrushed blending"** — that's beauty-native copy that tells me what the texture does, not just what the product is called.

## What's weak

- **Zero shade information above the fold** — for a bronzer targeting a complexion devotee who will scrutinize undertone, the hero copy doesn't mention shade count, range, or how to find my match. Packaging-first was exactly what I said I don't shop by.
- **Primary CTA is cut off** — I can see a button starting at the bottom edge of the screen but can't read what it says. If I'm impulse-shopping from a launch post, that friction costs the tap.
- **No price visible** — not a dealbreaker but as someone who cross-shops Fenty Skin bundles, I want to know if this is a $38 or $52 decision before I go deeper.
- **Navigation tabs (Fenty Beauty / Fenty Skin / Fenty Hair) have no subcategory signal** — I know to look in Fenty Beauty for complexion, but there's no sub-label like "Complexion" to confirm I'm one tap from bronzers vs. lip products.
- **No reviews or star ratings** — this is a new product, so thin reviews are expected, but even a "be first to review" or a waitlist indicator would feed urgency.

## Recommendations

- **Add shade count to the hero copy** — even just "8 shades" or "for all skin tones" beneath the subheadline. That one line is what makes a complexion devotee tap through vs. scroll past.
- **Pull the CTA button fully into the viewport** — reduce hero image height slightly or push the CTA up so the button is fully readable without scrolling. If the copy is "Shop Now" swap it to "Explore Shades" — that's what I'm actually deciding.
- **Show Rihanna wearing the product in a close-up crop** — the current image shows her face but the bronzer application isn't obviously visible. A blended-skin close-up of the warmth and finish would sell the texture faster than the product shot alone.
- **Surface a "new arrivals" chip or "just dropped" strip** — even a slim secondary rail below the hero showing 2–3 other recent Fenty complexion launches would give me a reason to keep scrolling instead of bouncing after the hero.
