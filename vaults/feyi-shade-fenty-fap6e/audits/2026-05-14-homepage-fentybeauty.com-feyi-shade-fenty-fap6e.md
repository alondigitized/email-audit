# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- OK so Fenty is pushing body care right now — the whole hero is a Fenty Skin moisture moment, dewy skin, that pink jar, very tactile and aspirational. It reads luxury beauty brand that knows its aesthetic, but as someone who came here hunting complexion, I'm immediately a half-step off my intent. The site looks clean, the model is giving the right energy, and that top-banner offer pulled my eye the second the page loaded.

## What's working

- **Top banner "Create an account to unlock free U.S. standard shipping + returns"** — this lands immediately for a returning shopper. I already have an account; seeing that SIGN IN link confirms the benefit is waiting for me.
- **"20% Off Select Items" in the category strip** — having the promo at navigation level (not buried in a modal) means I see it before I even decide where to tap. That's correct placement.
- **The hero model** — deeper skin tone, dark nails, gold necklace, no filter-scrubbed skin. This is Fenty's whole thing and it works. It says the brand still knows who it's for.
- **"MAKE WAY FOR MAJOR MOISTURE" headline** — bold all-caps, playful without trying too hard. Millennial-coded without being cringe.
- **Page cleanliness** — no layout jank, no overlapping elements, loads looking polished on mobile.

## What's weak

- **No visible CTA button** — the hero copy ends at "Irresistible body care for soft, smooth skin." and the viewport cuts off before any shop button appears. On mobile that is a conversion dead zone; if I'm not a scroll-first person I might bounce thinking there's nothing to tap.
- **Hero is body care, not complexion** — if I came to check whether my Match Stix shade is on sale, this hero does nothing to confirm I'm in the right place for complexion. The category strip is my only hint.
- **Category nav strip is cut off** — "Fenty" is truncated on the right edge, probably "Fenty Skin" but I can't confirm. On a small viewport that unfinished tab reads sloppy.
- **No loyalty/rewards visibility** — Fenty has a rewards program but there's zero mention of points, earned rewards, or member-exclusive pricing above the fold. For someone who buys at launch events and tracks their hauls, that's a missed hook.
- **No social proof** — no star ratings, no "X reviews," no "community favorite" badge on any featured product. The brand has earned it — use it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Complexion" or "Shop Match Stix" CTA button directly on the hero** — even a secondary text link below the body-care headline that routes to the complexion category. Two seconds of work, covers the shopper who came for a different reason.
- **Swap the truncated category nav for a sticky horizontal pill nav** that shows full labels ("Fenty Skin," "Complexion," "New Arrivals") — the cut-off text is avoidable and looks unfinished on standard mobile widths.
- **Surface rewards balance for signed-in users** — a one-line "You have 240 points — redeem on your next order" in the top banner for logged-in visitors would punch the loyalty hook that's currently missing.
- **Pin a "Most-loved shades" or "Just restocked" module directly below the hero** — a two-tile editorial showing Pro Filt'r or Match Stix would catch the complexion shopper without killing the body-care hero.
