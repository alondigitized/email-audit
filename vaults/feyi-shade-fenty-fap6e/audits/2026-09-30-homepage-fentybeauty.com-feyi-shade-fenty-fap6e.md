# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Fenty in full holiday-gifting mode. The hero is a stack of pink "storefront window" gift boxes with Gloss Posse, Fenty Skin minis and a backpack kit. It reads playful and giftable, but it's built for someone buying presents. I'm here for complexion, and nothing above the fold says shades, foundation or Match Stix.

## What's working

- **The holiday hero art.** The pink boxes with the illustrated storefront windows are clever and on-brand. I'd screenshot it, and it makes the "Holiday Windows" idea land.
- **"THE HAULIDAYS CAME EARLY".** The pun is cute and the tone is right for me. "For you and the crew" reads like a friend, not a corporate blast.
- **Nav.** Makeup, Skincare, Bestsellers and "New! Fluid Flex" are one tap away, so I can skip the holiday stuff and get to product fast.
- **A single CTA.** "SHOP HOLIDAY" is a clean, high-contrast button. There's no popup or overlay in my face.

## What's weak

- **Nothing for shade-range people.** For a brand whose whole story is 50 shades, the first screen shows no complexion product, no shade lineup and no shade finder. That's the reason I'm here.
- **The product rail is empty grey boxes.** I see "NEW" badges on blank tiles. If this is a slow load, it makes the page feel unfinished right where I'd start browsing.
- **No offer or reason to act now.** "Peek inside" and "Shop Holiday" tell me nothing about bundles, gift-with-purchase or savings. I cross-shop for Fenty Skin bundles, so I'd want to see a bundle price.
- **No loyalty hook.** "Sign In" is tiny and nothing tells me why to bother. There's no rewards balance, no early-access perk and no launch-event ping, and I buy at launch.
- **No people.** The hero is all packaging. I don't see a face or a skin tone, which is odd for a brand built on inclusivity.

## Recommendations

- Add a second hero slide or a split-hero tile with a row of foundation or Match Stix swatches across the full shade range, plus a "Find My Shade" button. Right now that's the highest-intent thing missing.
- Put a real offer in the black bar beside "Peek inside", such as "Free shipping over $X" or "Gift with purchase on holiday sets", so the first screen has a number in it.
- Fix the rail so product images and a price or shade count load in the first view. Use skeleton loaders if the images are slow.
- Add a small member strip under the nav ("Join Fenty Rewards, early access to launches") and put faces of different skin tones next to the gift boxes.
