# Homepage snapshot · fentybeauty.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Fenty came to play — this is a full summer sale push and they're leading with a Fenty Skin bundle moment, not just makeup. As someone who cross-shops Fenty Skin specifically for the bundle deals, I see what they're doing. The "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT ITEMS" is loud, the product shot is clean, and the voice ("Savings as hot as you.") is unmistakably Fenty. But there's no hero button, no model, and no shade-range flex in sight above the fold — which is a miss for a complexion loyalist like me.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 60% OFF SELECT ITEMS" headline** — big, bold, above the fold. No guessing what this page is about.
- **The bundle product shot** — a Fenty Skin cleanser, what looks like a Cosmic Jelly or lip oil, a mascara, and a bright magenta lip product all styled together. This is exactly the cross-category bundle play that gets me to spend $150 without blinking.
- **"Savings as hot as you."** — the copy register is perfect. It's Rihanna-coded, not corporate-coded. I feel like I'm being spoken to, not sold at.
- **The Summer Warmup Sale sub-nav tab** — one tap to get to the sale. That's frictionless.
- **Top banner free shipping hook** — free U.S. shipping + returns for account holders is a real perk and it's the first thing I read.

## What's weak

- **No hero CTA button** — I see the campaign, I see the discount, but there's no "Shop the Sale" button in the hero itself. I have to figure out that the sub-nav tab is the entry point. That's a micro-gap that costs taps.
- **No model/person in the hero** — For a brand built on shade inclusivity and Rihanna's personal story, a product-only flat lay feels like a missed identity moment. Show me someone in my complexion range wearing these products.
- **"Up to 60% off" with "Exclusions apply"** — standard retail language but the "up to" sets an expectation that usually gets dashed. I've been burned enough to click with moderate skepticism.
- **No Match Stix, no foundation shade, no complexion anything** — the hero is entirely Fenty Skin + a lip product. As a complexion devotee, I'm not seeing my core category represented at the fold.
- **Loyalty program is nowhere visible** — Fenty has an account system with perks but there's no mention of points, rewards, or member-exclusive pricing. That would have hooked me harder than the generic banner.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the Sale" button directly in the hero** — place it below the offer copy, same treatment as the headline. Right now the CTA pathway lives in a sub-nav tab, which reads as navigation, not invitation.
- **Swap the product-only flat lay for a lifestyle shot with a model** — show someone with a medium-to-deep complexion actually using or holding the bundle. The shade range is Fenty's biggest flex; don't hide it in a product shot.
- **Surface the complexion category** — add a second sub-nav pill or a quick-link below the hero: "Foundations & Concealers on Sale" or "Match Stix Deals." One tap to my category without digging through Shop All.
- **Add a deadline or sale urgency signal** — "Ends Sunday" or a visible countdown under the headline would transform this from a passive "sale exists" moment into a "I need to decide now" moment.
