# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a genuine baby-specialty retailer, not a generic big-box afterthought — the nav is organized exactly how I shop (Strollers, Car Seats, Furniture, Bedding, Safety & Wellness) and Registry is sitting right up top where I need it. Right now they're pushing a vague "Settle Into the Season / fall favorites" push, which is fine but doesn't feel tailored to someone actively building a registry — it's more seasonal filler than a targeted offer.

## What's working

- **"welcome rewards+" badge** top nav — I clock loyalty programs fast when comparing Babylist vs. a store registry, and this is front and center.
- **Registry link in the header** — one tap away, no digging. This matters a lot to me right now.
- **Free Shipping Over $49.99* banner** — a threshold I'll clear on basically any registry-related order, so it reads as a real, usable perk, not a tease.
- **Category tiles (On the Go, Indoor Play, Newborn Necessities, Little Pretenders)** — genuinely useful groupings for someone stocking a registry across stages, not just "Shop Now" mush.

## What's weak

- **The "Settle Into the Season" hero** is generic seasonal copy — it could belong to any home or apparel brand. Nothing says "new parent" or "registry" in the actual headline.
- **Account dropdown rendering oddly** — the Sign In / Create an Account box is floating over the nav bar in a way that looks like a stuck hover state, not an intentional design element.
- **Promo strip text is cut off** on the right ("...e" — looks like a third promo item got clipped). Small, but it's the kind of sloppy detail that makes me side-eye a site when I'm about to trust it with gift money.
- **No trust signals above the fold** — no review counts, no "as seen in," nothing that reassures me these are vetted, well-reviewed products before I scroll.

## Recommendations

- Swap the hero headline for something registry-specific this time of year — e.g. "Building Your Registry? Start Here" with a "Start My Registry" CTA instead of the generic "Shop Now."
- Fix the Account dropdown so it doesn't overlap the nav bar — right now it looks broken, not helpful.
- Un-clip the third promo strip item so I'm not left wondering what I'm missing.
- Add a small trust element near the hero — even a "500K+ 5-star reviews" or "Registry Perks" callout would build confidence before I scroll.
