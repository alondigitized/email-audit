# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened this and immediately felt seen — it's a baby store running a 45% off Memorial Day sale on nursery furniture right as I'm mid-registry, which is almost too perfectly timed. The "Dreamy Neutrals" crib shot below the hero is straight off my Pinterest board. That said, there's no "Build My Registry" button anywhere in sight, and a Sign In overlay is already fighting me for screen space.

## What's working

- **"Little Rooms, Big Savings" hero timing:** The Memorial Day hook + a clear percentage off baby furniture lands exactly when I'm actively pricing out a nursery. No ambiguity about what the deal is.
- **"Dreamy Neutrals" module:** That walnut-finish crib in a neutral, plant-filled nursery is aspirational in exactly the right direction — it's the aesthetic I'm building toward.
- **45% off badge:** Prominent, positioned on the hero image itself. I don't have to hunt for the number.
- **Welcome Rewards+:** Signals a loyalty program exists, which matters to me since I'm cross-shopping Babylist and Target and I want to know if accumulating points here is worth it.
- **Memorial Day relevance:** Page feels live and current. Nothing stale.

## What's weak

- **No registry CTA above the fold:** I'm literally here to build a registry — that's buybuybaby's entire differentiation — and there's no "Start Your Registry" button, banner, or hint of it above the fold. That's a major miss.
- **Sign In overlay blocking navigation:** The top nav strip — where "Safety Esse[ntials]" would be — is partially covered by what looks like an auto-triggered or accidentally triggered Sign In panel. I didn't ask to sign in yet.
- **No people in either image:** Both the hero glider product shot and the Dreamy Neutrals crib are purely product/room compositions. I want to see a mom or a baby and project myself in.
- **Duplicate generic CTAs:** Two "Shop Now" buttons with zero contextual differentiation — I have to guess which one leads where.
- **No free-shipping info:** I cross-shop aggressively on price. If I don't know the shipping threshold, I can't calculate my real savings.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent "Build Your Registry" CTA:** Sticky bar or a second hero button — this is the primary job-to-be-done for people like me landing on buybuybaby. It belongs above the fold.
- **Swap the isolated glider product shot for a lifestyle image:** A mom reading in the glider, or a baby in the crib — let me see myself in the room, not just the furniture SKU.
- **Give the CTAs context:** "Shop Memorial Day Furniture" and "Shop Dreamy Neutrals" tells me more than two identical "Shop Now" labels. One copy pass, immediate lift.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip:** A slim banner at the very top ("Free shipping on orders $49+") costs nothing to render and directly removes my hesitation to add to cart.
