# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the vibe is immediately right — warm, neutral nursery aesthetic, wooden crib, soft textures, exactly the Pinterest board I've been building for three months. This feels like it knows who I am. But the moment I look past the hero I'm hit with a sign-in overlay and a nav bar that's cutting off half its own text, and there's not a single price, deal, or safety callout in sight — which is a problem for someone like me who opened this tab specifically to compare against my Babylist list.

## What's working

- **"Dreamy Neutrals" hero with the wooden crib**: I literally have this aesthetic saved in three separate inspiration folders. This image makes me want to tap immediately — it sells a whole nursery vision, not just a product.
- **"welcome rewards+" in the nav**: I notice loyalty programs right away because I'm always trying to offset the insane cost of setting up a nursery. It's there, even if thin.
- **"Hello, Little Bugs!" category tile**: The overhead shot of a baby with a sensory toy is adorable and immediately makes me think developmental play — exactly where my head is at 31 weeks in.
- **"Safety Ess..." nav strip**: Even truncated, seeing a "Safety Essentials" category callout registers with me. I read every recall notice. The fact that safety gets its own nav slot earns trust.
- **Clean, warm visual design**: No clutter, no neon sale banners, the beige/wood palette is on-brand for where millennial nursery taste is right now.

## What's weak

- **Zero price signal above the fold**: I have this tab open next to my Babylist and a Target tab. If you don't give me a reason to believe you're price-competitive in the first three seconds, I'm not staying.
- **Sign-in overlay covering navigation**: The "Sign In / Create an Account" dropdown is open and physically sitting on top of the nav text — "Safety Ess..." and "must-ha..." are clipped. That's a friction snapshot whether it's a screenshot artifact or not.
- **"Shop Now" is the laziest CTA possible**: On a hero this beautiful, "Shop Dreamy Neutrals" or "Build Your Nursery" would actually convert me. "Shop Now" feels like a placeholder.
- **No registry CTA anywhere above the fold**: I came here specifically because you're a registry destination. Babylist and Target both surface "Start Your Registry" within the first scroll. BuyBuyBaby's whole value prop is the registry and it's not on screen.
- **No safety/recall credibility above the fold**: I read every safety note before I buy anything. A badge or one-liner ("JPMA certified products" or "recall alerts in your account") would immediately differentiate you from generic retailers.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Start Your Registry" CTA to the hero or immediately below it** — this is your single biggest acquisition driver and it's not visible anywhere on screen. If a first-time mom lands here and doesn't see a registry hook within 5 seconds, she's going to Babylist.
- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Dreamy Neutrals" or "See the Full Nursery"** — the hero has built-up emotional intent; the CTA should harvest it with specificity, not a generic tap.
- **Add a persistent offer strip above the hero** (e.g., "Free shipping on registry purchases over $99" or "Rewards members get 10% back") — price-comparing shoppers like me need a financial reason to stay before we explore.
- **Surface the rewards program value** — change "welcome rewards+" to something like "Earn 5% back · Free returns · Exclusive registry discounts" so I know immediately what membership is worth.
