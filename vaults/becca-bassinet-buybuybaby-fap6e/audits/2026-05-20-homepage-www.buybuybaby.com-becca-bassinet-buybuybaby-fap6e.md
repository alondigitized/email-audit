# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- buybuybaby is speaking my language right now — a Memorial Day furniture sale and a jaw-dropping nursery lifestyle shot are exactly why I'd open this app at 11pm while scrolling between Babylist and Target. It reads like a baby specialty retailer that knows its customer is me, not some generic shopper. That said, "save big" is doing a lot of heavy lifting where an actual percentage should be.

## What's working

- **The holiday sale peg** — "Little Rooms, Big Savings" tied to Memorial Day gives me a social story for my partner: "it's on sale right now, we have to buy the glider this weekend." That's a real conversion driver.
- **The glider in the hero** — That white recliner is the exact product category I'm currently researching. No ambiguity about what this sale covers.
- **"Dreamy Neutrals" nursery shot** — Dark wood crib, knit blanket, stuffed giraffe, monstera, wicker basket. This is my actual Pinterest board. It's aspirational and functional at the same time — I can see the crib style and the full room story.
- **"welcome rewards+"** — Even just seeing the program name tells me there's a loyalty angle worth exploring before I commit my registry here vs. Babylist.

## What's weak

- **"Save big" means nothing to me** — I need a number. "Up to 30% off" or "Save $150 on select gliders" gives me something to screenshot and send to my partner. "Save big" could mean 5%. I don't click on vibes.
- **Sign-in modal overlapping the nav** — The "Sign In / Create an Account" overlay is sitting on top of the top navigation strip, partially covering the category links. That's friction on a first impression.
- **No people anywhere** — Not one parent, not one baby, not even a hand in the crib shot. The lifestyle photo is beautiful but abstract. I need to see someone like me in it.
- **Truncated nav label** — "Safety Esse..." is unreadable. Safety recall info is literally the reason I read every label on this site — that link needs to be legible.

## Recommendations

- **Drop a specific number into the hero subhead.** "Up to 30% off baby furniture & decor — Memorial Day weekend only" ships in a copy paste. Without a number I'm not urgency-shopping, I'm browsing.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the hero strip.** Something like "Free shipping over $49+" anchors the page and directly addresses the Target price comparison I'm already running in another tab.
- **Fix or dismiss the sign-in modal before it covers the nav.** Make it a corner tooltip or a non-blocking state — not a banner that eats the nav strip.
- **Add a human to the "Dreamy Neutrals" shot** — even just a pair of tiny baby socks on the crib rail or a hand tucking in a blanket. One human signal connects the product to the experience.
