# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- This feels like a brand that knows its aesthetic — the neutral nursery photography is gorgeous and it's landing right in the middle of what I'm currently pinning for my registry. But the Sign In panel is overlaid on the page uninvited, there's zero deal energy anywhere above the fold, and I can't find a single "Start Your Registry" button. For the brand that built its whole identity around registry, that's a glaring miss right out of the gate.

## What's working

- **"Dreamy Neutrals" hero photography:** The crib scene with the neutral linen blanket draped over the rail and the soft natural light is *exactly* my registry mood board. High aspiration, immediately relevant.
- **"welcome rewards+" in the header:** Small but it signals there's a loyalty program — I'll be spending a lot here, so I notice that.
- **Nav strip quick links:** "The Toddler Shop" and what appears to be a safety link give me fast paths to actual departments without digging through a menu.
- **Category tiles below the hero:** "Get Outside," "Hello, Little Bugs!" with a baby photo — it starts to feel like an intentional editorial path, not just a random product grid.
- **"Shop Now" placement on the hero:** My eye goes: beautiful crib → action button. The flow is instinctive even if the copy is generic.

## What's weak

- **Sign In overlay on page load:** A Sign In / Create an Account panel is sitting on top of the navigation area. I didn't tap anything — this is friction before I've even seen the page.
- **Zero promotional energy:** No registry completion discount, no "free shipping over $X," no welcome offer. I'm actively cross-shopping with Babylist and Target right now — I need a reason to build my registry here vs. there, and there is none visible.
- **"Dreamy Neutrals" is a vibe, not a value prop:** I don't know if this is a new collection, a sale, or just a banner with no deadline. "Shop Now" for what reason, exactly?
- **No registry CTA above the fold:** Building a registry is the single biggest reason first-time moms come to this site. "Start Your Registry" is completely absent above the fold — that's a significant structural miss.
- **No trust signals:** No "free returns," no "price match guarantee," no safety certification badges. I read every recall notice — showing me upfront that safety matters to this brand would go a long way.
- **"Safety Esse..." truncated in the nav:** I would actually click a "Safety Essentials" link, but the text is cut off and I can't confirm what it says. Sloppy on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Add a registry CTA to the hero:** Replace "Shop Now" or add a secondary button — "Start Your Registry" or "Add to Registry." This is the core use case and it's invisible above the fold.
- **Drop one concrete offer into the hero or a sticky banner:** Even "Complete your registry — get 15% off" closes the gap with Babylist's and Amazon's completion discounts. Show me a number or lose me to a competitor who does.
- **Kill the Sign In overlay on passive page load:** If I haven't tapped the account icon, don't pop a sign-in screen. It reads as either a bug or an aggressive dark pattern and creates friction before the page even renders.
- **Fix the truncated nav labels:** "Safety Esse..." and "must-ha..." need to either be shortened intentionally or given more space. Unreadable labels are wasted real estate on a small screen.
