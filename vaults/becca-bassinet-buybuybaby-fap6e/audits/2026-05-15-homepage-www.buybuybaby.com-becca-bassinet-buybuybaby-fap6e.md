# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- buybuybaby is leading with a styled, aspirational nursery scene — the "Dreamy Neutrals" hero is basically a Pinterest board come to life, which honestly? I'm here for it. This feels like a brand that knows its shopper is aesthetic-driven and anxious about getting the nursery right. What they're pushing right now seems to be their soft-goods/bedding assortment, with a side of outdoor + developmental play below fold.

## What's working

- **"Dreamy Neutrals" hero imagery** — that walnut crib with the dotted muslin blanket draped over it, the boucle sofa, the woven basket, the stuffed giraffe on the floor: it's exactly what my nursery inspo folder looks like. I'd want to shop that exact setup.
- **"Shop Now" button placement** — centered in the hero, teal on warm-neutral background, easy to tap on mobile.
- **"welcome rewards+" header callout** — even as a first-time visitor, I notice there's a loyalty program. That matters when I'm about to spend $3,000 on a registry.
- **"Hello, Little Bugs!" category tile** — the baby lying on the playmat image is warm and relatable; it visually signals this site covers the infant stage, not just furniture.
- **"The Toddler Shop" nav strip** — registry-building moms think ahead; knowing they have toddler coverage reassures me this isn't just a one-and-done store.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal overlay blocking nav** — a Sign In dropdown is sitting on top of the navigation strip when I land, covering "Safety Esse..." entirely. Safety features are literally one of my top priorities, and it's the nav item I can't read.
- **Zero offer above fold** — no "free shipping on registry orders," no "save 15% on your first purchase," nothing. Babylist and Target both lead with registry perks. I'm comparison shopping and this gives me nothing to anchor to.
- **No registry CTA anywhere visible** — I came here to build a registry. The word "registry" is not visible in this viewport at all. That's a massive miss for my use case.
- **"welcome rewards+" is a tease with no details** — what do I actually get? Without a visible benefit (points per dollar, member discount, birthday perk), it reads as noise.
- **Category tiles below fold are cropped** — "Grow Wi..." is cut off mid-word. On mobile, if I can't read the category name, I'm less likely to tap it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a registry entry point above fold** — swap one of the nav strip items (or add a persistent "Start Your Registry" pill button below the logo) so registry-building is one tap away from load. This is the #1 job-to-be-done for this audience.
- **Replace the editorial-only hero with a hero that has an offer** — "Dreamy Neutrals — Free shipping on all nursery orders this week" or "Registry Complete Your Look — Save 15% when you buy 3+ nursery items." Same aesthetic, but now it gives me a reason to act.
- **Fix the sign-in dropdown state** — whatever caused this dropdown to render open on page load (or on first tap of the account icon) needs to close on outside-tap immediately so it doesn't bury the Safety nav item.
- **Surface the rewards program value prop** — under "welcome rewards+," add one line: "Earn 1 point per $1 · Redeem for cash back." That's conversion fuel for first-time buyers who are about to spend big.
