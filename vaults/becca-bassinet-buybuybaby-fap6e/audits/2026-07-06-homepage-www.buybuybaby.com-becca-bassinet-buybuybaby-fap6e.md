# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- buybuybaby is leading with a toddler outdoor-play summer campaign and I'm here building a registry for a newborn — those are two completely different shopping missions. The site reads like a solid specialty baby retailer with a real loyalty program, but the hero is aimed at existing parents of 3-to-5-year-olds, not a first-time mom who hasn't even had her baby yet. The Sign In modal that fired over the nav is the first thing that hit me, and that's a rough welcome.

## What's working

- **"welcome rewards+"** in the header — I immediately know there's a loyalty program and I can compare this against what Babylist and Target offer me
- **"Hello, Summer!" hero** — at least it's seasonally current; not a stale Valentine's Day leftover
- **The category tile row** ("Play with Purpose," "Ready, Set, Travel," the third tile cut off) — these feel more like actual baby shopping and each has a clear "Shop Now" button
- **"Safety Esse..." nav link** — as someone who reads every safety recall note, the fact that "Safety" is surfaced in the nav strip without scrolling is genuinely useful to me
- **Clean visual design** — the colors are cheerful and on-brand, the logo reads clearly, the layout isn't chaotic

## What's weak

- **The hero itself** — two toddlers at a mud kitchen play station have nothing to do with why I'm here; I'm building a registry for a baby who doesn't exist yet, and this content makes me feel like I landed on the wrong site
- **Sign In modal overlay** — it fired right over the navigation and is blocking part of the nav strip; this is a dark-pattern-adjacent friction that makes me want to hit the back button before I've even seen the page
- **Zero registry mention above the fold** — buybuybaby is literally famous for baby registries; I came here because my friend said it's a great registry tool, and I can't see the word "registry" anywhere in this viewport
- **No concrete offer or threshold** — no "free shipping over $75," no "get 15% off to complete your registry," nothing with a number that anchors value
- **"Shop Now" is generic** — same button copy on the hero AND all three category tiles; there's no differentiation and no signal about what I'm shopping *toward*

## Recommendations

- **Replace or supplement the summer hero with a registry-first entry point** — even a slim banner strip above the hero that says "Building your registry? Start here →" would catch me immediately and convert my visit into a registry session
- **Kill the auto-fired Sign In modal or at minimum delay it** — let me see the page for five seconds before you hit me with a login prompt; replace it with a passive "Sign in for rewards" link in the header (which already exists)
- **Add a concrete offer with a number above the fold** — "free shipping on orders $49+" or "registry completion coupon: 15% off" tied to the rewards program would immediately tell me what I'm getting out of this relationship
- **Swap one category tile for a "New to buybuybaby? Build your registry" tile** — the existing tile row has space and this is table stakes for a specialty baby retailer competing with Babylist
