# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- buybuybaby landed on what feels like a Memorial Day furniture push — and as someone who just started pulling together a nursery wishlist, the timing is actually pretty good for me. The homepage looks clean and on-theme, but I'm immediately annoyed that a sign-in flyout is hanging open on top of the nav, and there's no "Start Your Registry" CTA anywhere above the fold, which is wild for the one brand that's supposed to own that moment for me.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day furniture deal** — "Save up to 45% on baby furniture and decor" is the exact type of registry-period offer that makes me pull out my phone. I'd absolutely screenshot this for my partner.
- **"Dreamy Neutrals" nursery module** — the styled crib shot with the wicker basket and brass tones is aspirational in exactly the way Instagram made me, and the "Shop Now" button is right there.
- **Welcome rewards+ header callout** — it's subtle, but knowing there's a rewards program at least signals this brand rewards loyalty, which matters when I'm going to be buying a lot over the next 12 months.
- **Glider/recliner product in the hero** — visually one of the higher-ticket items I'm actively comparing, so it's not generic baby clutter.

## What's weak

- **No "Start Your Registry" CTA above the fold** — this is buybuybaby's signature product. The entire reason I chose this URL over Target's registry page, and the word "registry" is nowhere on the visible screen. That's a massive miss for a first-time mom.
- **Sign-in flyout is open and covering sub-nav** — the "The Toddler Shop" and "Safety Esse..." nav items are partially obscured. Whatever triggered that flyout created immediate confusion on page load.
- **"Up to 45% on select"** — the double hedge makes me suspicious before I even tap through. Am I going to find 45% off the glider in the photo, or is it 10% off burp cloths?
- **No people in either module** — both the glider shot and the "Dreamy Neutrals" nursery scene are product/set photography with zero human presence. I can't see myself or anyone like me in this page.
- **Trust/safety signals are absent** — I read every safety-recall note before I buy anything. There's no badge, no "JPMA Certified," no "free returns," nothing that would make me feel safer about a big-ticket item.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Start Your Registry" or "Build Your Registry" CTA** in the hero or as a persistent sticky strip — this brand's entire value prop for me is the registry experience, and it's invisible above the fold.
- **Put a hard end date on the Memorial Day deal** — "ends Sunday, May 25" is more believable and urgent than just "this Memorial Day." A countdown chip would be even better.
- **Add one human to the "Dreamy Neutrals" module** — a 30-something mom reaching into the crib, or a couple assembling furniture, makes this feel like a life event, not a showroom catalog.
- **Fix or auto-dismiss the sign-in flyout on page load** — if this triggers on a new session, it's covering navigation and needs to be a non-blocking banner or bottom sheet instead.
