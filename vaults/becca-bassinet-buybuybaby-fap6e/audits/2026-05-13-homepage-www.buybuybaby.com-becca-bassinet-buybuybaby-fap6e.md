# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- buybuybaby is leading with pure nursery aesthetics right now — a warm, neutral-toned crib scene that looks like it was pulled straight from my Pinterest board. The brand feels premium and style-conscious, which I appreciate, but it's almost entirely vibes above the fold with zero pricing signal or registry-specific hook. For a first-time mom who lives and dies by comparison shopping, that's a miss.

## What's working

- **The hero crib photo** — dark wood crib, knit blanket, stuffed giraffe, rattan basket, warm neutrals. This is 100% the aesthetic I'm building my nursery around and it made me stop scrolling immediately.
- **"Dreamy Neutrals" headline** — short, specific, and genuinely category-relevant. I know exactly what I'm clicking into.
- **"welcome rewards+" in the nav** — I see there's a loyalty program and I'm logged in (or at least acknowledged). Makes the brand feel like it knows me.
- **Category tiles below the fold** — "Get Outside," "Hello, Little Bugs!" and a third partially-visible tile show me the breadth of inventory fast, and the baby photo on the middle tile is charming.

## What's weak

- **Zero pricing signal above the fold** — as someone who cross-shops Babylist and Target, if I don't see a deal or free-shipping threshold immediately, I'm already opening another tab to compare.
- **No registry CTA anywhere visible** — this is literally a registry brand and I don't see "Start Your Registry" or "Add to Registry" anywhere above the fold. That's the single biggest job I have on this site right now.
- **"Shop Now" is too generic** — every tile says "Shop Now." For the hero specifically, something like "Shop Nursery Bedding" would reduce my cognitive load and tell me exactly what the click delivers.
- **Sign In overlay covering the nav** — it's sitting awkwardly over the header and I can't read the sub-nav categories beneath it. Feels broken on mobile.
- **Sub-nav text is cut off** — "Safety Esse...", "must-ha..." — I can't read these. On a safety-recall-conscious buyer like me, anything safety-related that I can't fully read immediately reads as friction.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Start Your Registry" hero CTA alongside "Shop Now"** — this is the primary intent for a huge slice of buybuybaby's audience; it should live on the hero, not buried in a nav dropdown.
- **Swap the generic "Shop Now" hero button for "Shop Dreamy Neutrals Bedding"** — specific CTA copy converts better and tells me exactly what I'm getting into.
- **Add a free-shipping strip or offer banner above the hero** — even "Free shipping on registry orders over $X" would give me a reason to stay vs. checking Target right now.
- **Fix the mobile Sign In dropdown overlap** — it's covering the nav and creating an immediate trust/cleanliness issue on first load.
