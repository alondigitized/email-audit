# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I land on buybuybaby.com and the first thing I see is a sign-in dropdown half-covering the nav, two toddlers playing in a backyard mud kitchen, and "Hello, Summer!" in big bold letters. It feels like a store for parents who are *already* in the thick of it with a 3-year-old — not for me, a first-time mom who is literally in the middle of building her newborn registry and trying to figure out if I need a Snoo or can get away with a regular bassinet. Where's the registry CTA? Where's the newborn section? The vibe is fine, but it completely missed me.

## What's working

- **"welcome rewards+" in the header** — I know a loyalty program exists, which matters to me as someone cross-shopping every item on my registry. I want to know if points stack with registry completion discounts.
- **"Hello, Summer!" hero timing** — it's July, and the page reflects that. At least the creative team is paying attention to the calendar.
- **Category tiles below the hero** — "Play with Purpose" and "Ready, Set, Travel" are two categories that actually live on my registry (developmental toys, travel system). They load visually clean and have their own "Shop Now" buttons.
- **Clear visual hierarchy** — the hero is immediately dominant; I'm not scanning a cluttered layout. The page structure is legible on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in dropdown is open and blocking navigation** — "Safety Esse…" and "must-hav…" are cut off behind the modal. I can't even read what safety content is being teased, which is maddening because I read every safety recall note.
- **"Hello, Summer!" hero targets toddlers, not newborns** — the kids in the photo look 3–5 years old playing with a mud kitchen. I'm buying a bassinet. Completely different shopping mode.
- **Zero registry entry point above the fold** — buybuybaby's entire brand promise for someone at my stage is the registry experience. There's no "Start Your Registry," no "Registry Completion Discount," no hook to pull me into what this store is actually known for.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is the vaguest possible copy** — shop what, exactly? Shop the mud kitchen? The travel section? I have no context.
- **No price, discount, or shipping threshold anywhere** — I cross-shop Babylist and Target constantly. If you don't show me a reason to buy here vs. there in the first scroll, you've already lost me.
- **No trust signals above the fold** — no "free returns," no review counts, no "safety-tested" badge. For a baby gear store I'm buying hundreds of dollars from, that matters.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero creative for a registry-first experience** — show an expectant mom or a nursery flatlay with copy like "Build your registry. Get 15% off to complete it." That's what I came here for.
- **Add a persistent "Start / Manage Your Registry" button to the header** — it should sit right next to the heart/wishlist icon where it's reachable in one tap, not buried in the hamburger menu.
- **Surface a concrete offer above the fold** — even "Free shipping on orders $49+" or "Registry members earn 5% back" gives me a reason to stay instead of clicking over to Target.
- **Fix the sign-in modal UX on mobile** — the dropdown is covering navigation copy; either dismiss it faster or reposition it so it doesn't block the category strip.
