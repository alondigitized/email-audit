# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- OK so buybuybaby is doing exactly what I hoped it would — it's clearly a baby-first brand, not a general big-box trying to carve out a corner. The hero is straight off my Pinterest board: a warm, moody walnut crib, neutral textiles, soft lighting. But I opened this to build a registry, and nothing above the fold is screaming "start here." The rewards program is tagged in the nav but there's zero payoff — no benefit, no hook. Nice aesthetic, incomplete pitch.

## What's working

- **"Dreamy Neutrals" hero with the walnut crib**: This is the exact aesthetic a first-time millennial mom is pinning. The warm neutrals, the textured rug, the stuffed giraffe — it reads "I've thought about this nursery for two years" and I'm here for it.
- **"Hello, Little Bugs!" category tile**: The copy is charming and the photography (baby lying on soft surface) is age-appropriate and immediately identifiable. Millennial-mom-coded.
- **"Shop Now" CTA placement**: Center-of-hero placement means I can't miss it. No hunting.
- **"welcome rewards+" nav badge**: At least the loyalty program is acknowledged above the fold. I know it exists.
- **Category strip under nav**: "The Toddler Shop" and a partially visible "Safety Esse..." strip tells me they've thought about navigation hierarchy. Safety content surfaced this early is exactly the kind of thing I look for.

## What's weak

- **No registry CTA above the fold**: I came here to build a registry. This is buybuybaby's entire brand differentiator. Not a single "Start Your Registry" button or callout is visible. That's a major miss for my use case.
- **Sign In panel overlapping nav**: The Sign In / Create an Account modal is partially covering the top navigation and clipping the "Safety Esse..." category label. That's friction I don't want on my first visit.
- **Zero promotional offer visible**: No "registry completion discount," no "20% off when you buy from your registry," no free-shipping threshold. This brand has known, well-publicized registry perks — none are surfaced here.
- **No trust signals**: No "free returns," no "safety-tested" badge, no rating counts on any product. For baby gear — where I read every recall notice — this matters a lot.
- **Hero has no person in it**: Beautiful nursery, but no mom, no baby, no lifestyle context. The crib is floating in an empty room. I want to see myself in the scene.

## Recommendations

- **Put a "Build Your Registry" CTA in the hero**: Swap the generic "Shop Now" for "Start Your Registry" — or add a second CTA button. This is the number-one reason I'm here and it's invisible above the fold.
- **Drop a registry perk teaser under the hero headline**: One line like "Registry members get 15% completion discount" before I even scroll would stop me cold. You have the offer — show it.
- **Add a mom + baby lifestyle shot to the hero**: Replace or A/B the empty-nursery hero with a real person setting up the crib or doing a registry scan. I need to see myself in this.
- **Fix the Sign In overlay clipping the nav strip**: Whether it's a triggered state or a real render bug, the overlapping panel should close cleanly or not cover navigation content. It looks broken.
