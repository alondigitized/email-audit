# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the store I expected to land on — nursery-first, soft neutrals, baby-centric all the way down. The hero is clearly registry season content (crib, bedding, neutral nursery aesthetic), which speaks right to where I am in life right now. It's a little light on deals and feels more editorial than promotional, but the brand vibe is warm, safe, and trustworthy for a first-time mom who is cross-referencing every purchase.

## What's working

- **"Dreamy Neutrals" hero** — the warm-wood crib on a neutral rug with soft plush animals is the exact nursery moodboard I have saved on Pinterest. It feels aspirational without feeling unattainable.
- **Hero "Shop Now" CTA** — it's center-frame, high contrast on the image, easy to tap on mobile. No guesswork.
- **Category tiles below the fold ("Get Outside," "Hello, Little Bugs!", "Grow Wi...")** — these start to break the registry into approachable sub-missions, which I appreciate as someone who doesn't know where to begin.
- **"Safety Esse..." nav strip** — even truncated, surfacing a "Safety Essentials" navigation link immediately builds trust for a safety-recall-reading mom like me.
- **"Welcome Rewards+"** — visible in the top nav, signals there's a loyalty program. Even if the benefit isn't spelled out, I know to look for it.

## What's weak

- **No deal or offer above the fold** — I cross-shop Babylist and Target's registry tool specifically to find the best price. If buybuybaby isn't leading with a registry discount or completion coupon, I have no reason to prioritize it over Amazon or Target right now.
- **"Welcome Rewards+" is just a label, not a hook** — it says nothing about what I get. "Earn 5% back on registry purchases" would actually make me stay.
- **Category tiles are cut off** — "Grow Wi..." is truncated, which makes the mobile scroll feel unfinished and slightly broken even if it's intentional carousel behavior.
- **No registry-specific CTA** — I came here to build a registry. Where's the "Start Your Registry" button above the fold? That is the single most important action for someone like me and it's not surfaced anywhere visible.
- **"The Toddler Shop" in the nav strip** — I'm 7 months pregnant. Toddler content is not my world yet. This nav choice wastes prime real estate.
- **No social proof** — no star ratings, no "bestseller" badges, no "1,200 registry families chose this" signals anywhere on screen. I'm a first-time mom who reads everything — give me the reviews.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Start Your Registry" CTA to the hero** — swap the lone "Shop Now" for a split CTA: "Shop Now" + "Build My Registry." Registry creation is the top conversion event for this audience and it's invisible above the fold.
- **Replace or supplement "Dreamy Neutrals" with a registry completion offer** — something like "Registry Perk: 15% off one item after your shower" as a sticky banner would immediately differentiate buybuybaby from Target.
- **Spell out the Rewards+ benefit in the nav** — change "Welcome Rewards+" to "Earn 5% Back — Join Free" so I understand the value prop in one glance.
- **Fix the truncated category tile copy on mobile** — "Grow Wi..." needs either shorter text or a wider tile so I can see what the category is before deciding to tap.
