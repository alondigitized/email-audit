# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a clean, warm, mainstream baby retailer with a soft palette and lots of whitespace. Right now it's pushing a generic "milestones" brand message and four lifestyle category tiles, not any specific sale. As a first-time mom building a registry, I like how calm it feels, but I don't see a single reason to act today.

## What's working

- **Top bar "Free Shipping Over $49.99*":** the threshold is low enough that one car seat accessory or a few crib sheets gets me there.
- **Header "Registry" and "Lists" icons:** they're right next to Cart, so I can find the registry fast. That's the thing I came for.
- **"Get Ready to Go" tile (couple with stroller and infant car seat):** it's exactly the big-ticket category I'm researching, and the photo is realistic.
- **Nav row (Strollers, Car Seats, Nursery, Safety & Wellness):** these are the categories I actually shop, listed plainly. "Safety & Wellness" also reassures me because I read every recall note.
- **Search bar "Find all things baby & beyond":** it's big and obvious, so I can search a specific brand to compare against Babylist and Target.

## What's weak

- **Open Account dropdown ("Sign In" / "Create an Account"):** it covers part of the nav and the third strip item, so the page looks broken on first load.
- **Hero headline "For Their Milestones & Every Day in Between":** it's pretty but vague. It says nothing about registry, deals or anything I could do.
- **"Shop Now":** shop *what*? It could go anywhere.
- **"welcome rewards+":** it's tiny and gives no hint of what I'd earn, so there's no reason to sign up.
- **No trust signals:** I see no star ratings, review counts, return policy or safety-certified badges above the fold. Those are my dealbreakers, since I return aggressively.
- **No registry hook:** there's no "Start your registry" button, no completion discount or gift card, and no comparison reassurance, even though I'm obviously cross-shopping Babylist and Target.

## Recommendations

- Close the Account menu by default and let it open only on tap, so the full nav (including Apparel & Accessories) and the third promo strip are visible on load.
- Change the hero CTA from "Shop Now" to "Start Your Registry" or "Shop Strollers & Car Seats". Put a short line under it, like "Free returns on registry items for 1 year", if that's true.
- Add one concrete member or registry offer to the top bar next to free shipping, for example "Registry perks: 15% completion discount" with a visible rewards+ explainer, and a holiday/gifting hook for the season.
- Add star ratings, review counts or a "Safety-tested" badge to the tiles. A small "Shop top-rated car seats" strip under them would help too.
