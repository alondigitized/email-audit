# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- OK, so buybuybaby is leading with a calm, earthy nursery aesthetic right now — "Dreamy Neutrals" over a beautifully staged wooden crib scene. For where I'm at (building my registry, obsessing over the nursery), this actually hits. That said, the first thing that greeted me was a Sign In modal sitting on top of the hero, which is a little annoying when I just landed. As a brand it reads aspirational-but-approachable, and it's clearly pushing nursery decor and newborn products right now.

## What's working

- **"Dreamy Neutrals" hero with the wooden crib** — this is exactly the nursery aesthetic I'm researching. Warm wood tones, macrame hanging, neutral bedding, soft stuffed animals. I could screenshot this for my mood board.
- **"Shop Now" CTA on the hero** — it's clearly labeled, sits centered in the image, impossible to miss. One tap and I'm in.
- **"Hello, Little Bugs!" category tile** — cute, age-appropriate copy that lands in the right register for a millennial mom. It doesn't feel corporate.
- **"Safety Esse..." tab in the nav** — even though it's truncated, the fact that safety essentials gets a top-nav slot signals that this brand takes recalls and product safety seriously, which matters a lot to me.
- **"welcome rewards+" in the header** — I like knowing a program exists before I start building my cart.

## What's weak

- **Sign In modal blocking the hero on load** — before I could even read the page, a "Sign In / Create an Account" overlay appeared over the top right of the content. I didn't ask for that. It's a friction bump on a first visit.
- **Zero offers or discounts visible** — I'm a price-comparer. If there's a registry discount or a welcome offer, I need to see it above the fold to stay engaged. Right now there's nothing.
- **"The Toddler Shop" is the first nav tab** — I'm registering for a *newborn*, not a toddler. That headline makes this feel like maybe I'm in the wrong section of the store.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I'm going to be spending hundreds of dollars building this registry. If free shipping kicks in at $50 or $75, tell me right now. I shouldn't have to go hunting for it.
- **No trust signals whatsoever** — no review counts, no "10 million babies registered," no safety-certification badges. For a baby store, the absence of credibility cues is a miss.
- **Category tiles partially cut off** — "Grow Wi..." on the right tile is clipped off the screen. It looks unfinished on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Add a registry welcome bar** — a sticky top banner ("New here? Get 15% off your registry completion purchase") would speak directly to me the second I land. Make it disappear after I dismiss it, not appear as a modal.
- **Swap "The Toddler Shop" as the lead nav tab** — put "New Arrivals" or "Build Your Registry" first. I'm not a toddler mom yet; leading with that tab signals misalignment.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the nav bar or under the hero** — a single line: "Free shipping on orders over $49" is enough. It changes my behavior immediately.
- **Drop the auto-triggered Sign In modal** — at minimum, delay it or tie it to a scroll trigger. Don't block my first impression of the page with an account gate.
