# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- OK so I opened buybuybaby — literally the brand I have tabs saved for comparing against Babylist — and the first thing I see is "Hello, Summer!" over two toddlers playing with a mud kitchen outside. I'm not there yet. I need a crib, a car seat, a monitor. This feels like I accidentally landed on the wrong store inside the right store. The vibe is cheerful and on-brand visually, but nothing above the fold is talking to me as a first-time mom who has a 47-item registry draft and zero patience for being sold toys my kid won't use for three years.

## What's working

- **"welcome rewards+" header badge** — I see it immediately and it signals there's a loyalty program I should probably join when I start registering. That's relevant to me since I'm going to spend a lot here.
- **"Shop Now" button in the hero** — it's big, teal, centered, easy to tap on mobile. No guessing what to do first.
- **"Ready, Set, Travel" category tile** — this one actually catches my eye because I'm already researching travel systems and diaper bags. A tile for travel gear makes me want to tap it.
- **"Safety Ess..." nav label** — even though it's partially covered, I can tell there's a Safety Essentials section. That's my jam. I read every recall notice; this feels like a section built for me.
- **Seasonal consistency** — it's July, they're running a summer push. At least the calendar isn't broken.

## What's weak

- **Hero is toddler-focused, not registry-focused** — "Hello, Summer!" with a mud kitchen scene is great for a parent of a 5-year-old, not for someone who hasn't given birth yet. This is the single biggest miss for my visit.
- **No registry CTA anywhere above the fold** — buybuybaby's entire competitive moat vs. Amazon is the baby registry. It is literally in their name. I see no "Start Your Registry," no "Registry" link, nothing. That's staggering.
- **No price signal at all** — I compare buybuybaby vs. Target on price constantly. There's no offer, no threshold, no "save X% with rewards." I have no reason to buy here vs. leave.
- **Sign-in overlay covering the nav strip** — that dropdown is sitting right on top of the category nav, obscuring the second category name. On a phone this is friction I didn't ask for.
- **No trust signals above fold** — no "free returns," no review counts, no "safety-tested" badges. For a safety-obsessed first-time mom, this is a missed opportunity every single pixel of it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a registry CTA to the hero or sticky header** — something like "Build your registry" or "Start your free registry" directly in the top bar. This is table stakes for buybuybaby and it's nowhere to be seen.
- **Swap the mud-kitchen hero for a newborn or registry lifestyle shot** — show a mom-to-be curating a Babylist-style checklist, or a nursery flatlay with a crib and monitor. Summer outdoors play is a page-2 campaign, not the front door for new parents.
- **Surface a concrete offer above fold** — even "free shipping on registry orders over $50" gives me a reason to stay vs. flip to Target. Right now there's nothing anchoring me financially.
- **Fix the sign-in dropdown UX** — it should not cover the nav strip on load. Either collapse it automatically, or move it so the category nav remains readable underneath.
