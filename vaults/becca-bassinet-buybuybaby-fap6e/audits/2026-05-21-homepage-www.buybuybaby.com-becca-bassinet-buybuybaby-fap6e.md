# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is clearly a baby specialty retailer leaning hard into Memorial Day nursery furniture savings — which, honestly, is exactly where my head is right now as I build out my registry. The aesthetic skews warm and neutral, which I'm into, but the homepage feels like it caught me mid-login-flow: there's a sign-in overlay sitting right on top of the nav, which is a little jarring before I've even gotten my bearings.

## What's working

- **"Little Rooms, Big Savings" hero** — speaks directly to the nursery-building mindset. I'm staring at a 10x10 room and this headline gets it.
- **"Dreamy Neutrals" lifestyle module** — that wooden crib with the neutral textile layering is gorgeous and Pinterest-ready. It made me stop scrolling.
- **Memorial Day timing** — they're actually running a current campaign, not recycling a generic "Shop Now" banner from two months ago.
- **"welcome rewards+" header hook** — I see the loyalty program immediately; if I already have an account, I know it's there.
- **buybuybaby brand recognition** — I already cross-shop this with Babylist, so the brand instantly has context for me.

## What's weak

- **"Save big" with no number** — this is 2025 (well, 2026), I need a percentage or a dollar threshold. "Save big" tells me nothing about whether this is worth my time vs. Target's 15% registry completion coupon.
- **Sign-in overlay partially obscuring nav** — the "Sign In / Create an Account" modal is floating over the navigation strip, cutting off "Safety Esse..." and "must-ha..." text. First impression friction right out of the gate.
- **Nav strip shows "The Toddler Shop" first** — I'm building a newborn registry, not shopping toddler clothes yet. Leading with toddler feels slightly off-phase for where I am.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — as someone comparing buybuybaby vs. Target vs. Babylist, I need to know the shipping math before I commit to adding anything.
- **No people in the imagery** — the crib shot is beautiful, but there's no mom, no baby, nothing that places me *in* the experience. It's a catalog shot, not a lifestyle shot.

## Recommendations

- **Put a number on the Memorial Day offer** — swap "Save big" for "Save up to 25%" or "Up to $200 off gliders." I will click a specific number; I will bounce on a vague one.
- **Dismiss or minimize the sign-in overlay** — don't let a login state eat my first 3 seconds. Either auto-dismiss after 2 seconds or move sign-in to a sticky header icon tap.
- **Lead the nav strip with "Nursery & Furniture"** — I'm building a registry, not a toddler wardrobe. Reorder the category strip to match the hero message.
- **Add a trust strip above the fold** — one line: "Free shipping over $X · Free returns · Registry benefits." That's the info that keeps me from jumping to Target to price-check.
