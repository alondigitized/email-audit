# Homepage snapshot · www.buybuybaby.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a solid mainstream baby retailer homepage — clean, on-brand teal-and-cream palette, easy category nav — but it's pushing "Routine Refresh" toddler content (a toddler bed, sleep routines) instead of anything that screams "help me build my registry." I clicked over expecting registry tools front and center and instead got a lifestyle refresh campaign.

## What's working

- **Free Shipping Over $49.99*** banner — that's a threshold I'd actually hit on almost any registry-related order, and it's the first thing I see.
- **Category icon row** (Car Seats, Strollers, Furniture, Bedding, Apparel, Gear & Toys, Bath, Feeding) — this is exactly the mental checklist I'm working through for my registry, laid out in one tap.
- **Registry icon in the top nav** — glad it's there and not buried, even if it's not emphasized visually.
- **Nursery Essentials tile** (crib, elephant plush, folded blanket) — this is the one module that actually looks like it's talking to someone furnishing a nursery, not just a toddler's room.

## What's weak

- **The hero, "Routine Refresh Starts Here"** — bed, teddy bears, a toddler girl playing. That's toddler-parent content, not "I'm building my registry" content. I'm at the stage of comparing car seats and cribs, not toddler sleep routines.
- **Sign In dropdown overlapping the nav** — "Ap[parel]" is cut off and "Toddler Shop" is half-covered by the account flyout. Looks like a stuck hover state that didn't dismiss. Sloppy for a site I'm about to trust with a $2,000+ registry.
- **No registry-specific CTA or messaging** — no "Start Your Registry," no "Registry Perks," nothing calling out completion discounts or welcome boxes, which is table stakes when Babylist and Target both lead with that messaging.
- **No parent or caregiver in any hero/lifestyle imagery** — every image is product or child-only. As a first-time mom I'd connect faster with a shot of a parent using the gear.

## Recommendations

- Swap the "Routine Refresh" toddler hero for a "Start Your Registry" hero with a pregnant or new-parent lifestyle shot and a clear "Start My Registry" CTA.
- Fix the Sign In dropdown so it doesn't auto-overlap the nav — it's covering real navigation ("Apparel," "Toddler Shop").
- Add a visible registry incentive strip (completion discount %, welcome box, free gift) right under the free-shipping banner — that's the first thing I compare against Babylist.
- Add star ratings or review counts to the category/promo tiles so I can gauge trust before I even click in.
