# Score up to 50% off during our sale!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a stacked-discount email — at least four separate percentage-off tiers (25%, up to 50%, 60%, and a 20% members layer) crammed into a single scroll. Michaels is clearly burning down sale inventory in the days after Memorial Day, and the creative reflects it: no editorial concept, no trend hook, just a grid of offers. Compared to last week's smarter sends — the "BRB: Our Sale is ON" email on 5/28, which at least gave the discount a personality, or the Grandmacore content pivot on 5/26 — this one regresses to the weakest version of Michaels' playbook.
- The Michaels Rewards callout near the bottom ("Earn $5") is the one genuinely useful signal for loyalists, but it's buried so deep that most subscribers will never see it. The visual hierarchy problem is real: the eye has nowhere to land because every section is competing at the same visual weight.
- Blunt verdict: this is a clearance email wearing a sale email's clothes. It works as a last-resort conversion play for high-intent shoppers, but does nothing for casual browsers or brand equity.

## What's working

- **Multiple concrete discount tiers are clearly visible** — 25%, 50%, 60% — so a deal-seeker can quickly find the ceiling offer.
- **Michaels Rewards section is present**, giving loyalty members a reason to click that generic shoppers don't have.
- **No render bugs** — layout appears clean, images loaded, no overlapping text or broken blocks visible.
- **Brand is instantly identifiable** via logo at the top; no confusion about who sent this.

## What's weak

- **Discount stacking creates decision paralysis** — four-plus offer tiers with no clear hierarchy means the eye bounces without landing anywhere.
- **"Up to 50% off" in the subject is a self-undermining phrase** — "up to" primes skepticism before the email is even opened.
- **No editorial spine** — unlike the 5/27 "personal lore" or 5/26 Grandmacore sends, there's no trend hook or seasonal narrative to give the offers context or urgency.
- **Time-bound urgency is invisible** — "during our sale" in the subject doesn't tell me when it ends; nothing in the visible body reinforces a deadline either.
- **Sale fatigue is real here** — this is at minimum the seventh or eighth consecutive discount email from Michaels in recent days; a subscriber paying attention is likely numbed.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one hero offer with a hard deadline.** Pick the single strongest discount (60% off a category, or the BOGO) and make the entire above-the-fold about that one thing. Secondary offers can live below the fold.
- 2. **Add an end date.** "Sale ends Sunday" or "48 hours left" converts lookers into buyers; "during our sale" does nothing.
- 3. **Lead the Rewards angle in the subject.** The Earn $5 hook is the most differentiating element and it's buried at the bottom.
- 4. **Vary the creative format.** After a week of back-to-back sale grids, a lifestyle image or a single curated picks module would stand out in the inbox.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last days: 60% off + Rewards bonus inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your biggest savings of the season — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members earn $5 back on top of up to 60% off.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed document structure — DOCTYPE misplaced:**
The rendered HTML begins with a `<table>` block *before* the `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>` declaration. The DOCTYPE is mid-document inside a `<td>`. This causes browsers/webmail clients to enter quirks mode for the pre-DOCTYPE content.

**Stray `</custom>` tags in rendered output:**
```html
<img src="https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>
</custom>
```
These are unrecognized ESP template artifacts left in the final render. While most clients ignore unknown tags, they indicate a template compilation error.

**Images missing `alt` text — 4 instances:**
| Image | Issue |
|---|---|
| `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` | Content image, no `alt` |
| Thunderhead `pixel` | Tracking pixel, no `alt=""` |
| IGo Digital `track_event` | Tracking pixel, no `alt=""` |
| Silverpop `svopn.e.michaels.com` open pixel | Tracking pixel, no `alt=""` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string) to suppress screen reader noise. The content image missing alt text is an accessibility gap.

**Two images served over HTTP (mixed content):**
- `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg`
- `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&...`

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy/block non-HTTPS image sources. The content image may fail to render; the Silverpop open pixel will fail to fire in those clients.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`customerEmail` param is base64-encoded, not a merge token:**
The value `bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==` decodes to `mira-glitter-michaels-fap6e@etell.app`. This is a static encoded value in the click URL, not a dynamic ESP merge field. If the template is shared across subscribers and this value is subscriber-specific, it must be injected at send time. If it was hardcoded during QA, it will deliver this address to all recipients.

**`sfmc_Sub` and `emId` params present** — appear to be SHA-256 hashed subscriber identifiers. These look correctly templated (long hex strings, not exposed raw email).

No unresolved `%%placeholder%%` or `{{merge_tag}}` tokens observed in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not captured** (QA WARN):
The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. This header is required for bulk senders under Gmail/Yahoo 2024 sender requirements and is best practice under CAN-SPAM. May be present on the actual SMTP envelope but not passed through the relay — needs verification against raw message headers.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN):
One-click unsubscribe (the `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` mechanism) is absent or not captured. Required for Gmail bulk sender compliance as of February 2024.

**Authentication-Results header not captured** (QA WARN):
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available relay data. Sending domain is `emdeals.michaels.com`; alignment with the `michaels.com` DMARC policy cannot be verified here.

**Plain-text body is 80.5% URLs** (28,386 / 35,271 chars):
High URL density in the plain-text part (`text/plain` alternative) is a known spam filter trigger. This typically results from auto-generated plain-text that includes every tracking URL verbatim.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Coremetrics params present, standard UTM params absent:**
Links use `cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Circular/EOW/Promo-_-20260529_S1_EOW_MichaelsSale_WKND_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME` and `cm_mmca2=94630743`. These are IBM Coremetrics attribution params, not standard `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign`. If the site has migrated off Coremetrics to GA4/GTM, these params will not be attributed correctly.

**Landing page resolution not confirmed:** All 22 click links were skipped by the HTTP probe (tracking redirect domains). Final destination URLs and their HTTP status codes (200 vs. 404/redirect chain) were not verified.
