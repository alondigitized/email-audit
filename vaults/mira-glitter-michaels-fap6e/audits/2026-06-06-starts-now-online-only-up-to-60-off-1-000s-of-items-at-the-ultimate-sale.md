# 🎉 Starts NOW – online only! Up to 60% off 1,000s of items at The Ultimate Sale.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels' ninth promotional email in three days, and the subject line finally delivers a genuine escalation: 60% off rather than the 50% ceiling that defined the prior run. That upgrade is real, and it's the most defensible thing in this send. The hero is clean, vibrant, and the "Ultimate Sale" branding is well-executed visually.
- But the email undercuts itself immediately. The subject shouts "online only" and the body quietly promotes in-store shopping ("Shop the sale in store tomorrow!") — a direct contradiction that erodes trust in the offer framing. Compounding that, the Party Shop LIVE event invite is recycled from the June 4th send, which already ran a dedicated event email and a same-day reminder. Stuffing it into this email makes the layout feel like a content dump, not a campaign.
- The net verdict: the 60% off escalation earns the open, but the cluttered body — four or five distinct offers competing for attention — makes the click a coin flip. A subscriber who opened the last eight emails is showing up for diminishing returns.

## What's working

- The hero escalates from 50% → 60%, which is a real upgrade and the clearest reason to open this specific email vs. the last five.
- "The Ultimate Sale" naming and the rainbow/confetti visual treatment give this a distinct identity versus the prior generic discount grids.
- Michaels Rewards "Earn $5" module is visible and reinforces member value without competing with the hero.
- "Starts NOW" in the subject delivers genuine time urgency — not the vague "limited time" copy used in earlier sends.

## What's weak

- Subject says "online only" but the email body promotes in-store shopping — a visible contradiction that undermines credibility.
- Party Shop LIVE content is recycled from the June 4th send (dedicated invite) and June 4th reminder — a subscriber recognizes this as stale.
- Four or five distinct offer blocks (60% sale, in-store notice, Party Shop LIVE, 30% coupon, Rewards) have no visual hierarchy below the hero; the eye stalls mid-scroll.
- The 30% off single-item coupon sits below the 60% off hero, creating confusion about which offer to use and when.
- Ninth email in three days on what is essentially the same sale: cadence fatigue is a real force working against click-through at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Party Shop LIVE block entirely.** It ran in two emails two days ago. Keeping it here dilutes the sale message and tells subscribers this is a content recycle, not a new reason to shop.
- 2. **Resolve the online/in-store contradiction.** If the sale is online only, remove the "Shop the sale in store tomorrow!" module. If both, change the subject to remove "online only."
- 3. **Collapse the 30% coupon into the hero or drop it.** Having a 60% off hero AND a 30% coupon in the same email creates a hierarchy problem — the 30% looks like a downgrade. If the coupon is for exclusions, make that clear in the module itself.
- 4. **Drive to a category landing page, not the sale homepage.** A single "Shop Yarn & Needle Arts →" or "Shop Seasonal →" CTA converts better than a generic "Shop the Sale" button for engaged subscribers.
- 5. **Tighten the subject.** It's 81 characters — truncates badly on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `🎉 60% off starts NOW — The Ultimate Sale is live`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your biggest Michaels sale yet: up to 60% off online`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `1,000s of items — no coupon needed, online only through the weekend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The 60% off sale just opened. Shop before the weekend crowd hits.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Michaels "Ultimate Sale" Email (2026-06-06)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has significant structural integrity issues (misplaced DOCTYPE, orphaned `</custom>` tags) and relies on four separate tracking pixel systems, two of which use unencrypted HTTP. Compliance headers were not captured by the relay, leaving SPF/DKIM status unverified.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**DOCTYPE placement — critical rendering defect**
The `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">` declaration appears *inside* the body, after an opening `<table>` and two orphaned `</custom>` closing tags. Any content rendered before the DOCTYPE falls outside the document model, triggering quirks mode in strict parsers.

**Orphaned `</custom>` tags**
Two `</custom>` closing tags appear with no corresponding opening element:
```html
<img src="https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?...">
</custom>
</custom>
```
Invalid; behavior is parser-dependent.

**22 tracking/redirect links unprobed**
All CTAs route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` click-redirect URLs. Final destination URLs were not verified — broken redirects would not be caught by QA.

**Four concurrent tracking pixel systems**
| Pixel | Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| Open pixel | `click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx` | HTTPS |
| Thunderhead/ONE | `na5.thunderhead.com` | HTTPS |
| iGoDigital/Collect | `nova.collect.igodigital.com` | HTTPS |
| Silverpop | `svopn.e.michaels.com` | **HTTP** |

The Silverpop pixel (`svopn.e.michaels.com`) fires over HTTP — blocked by Gmail and most modern clients.

**Plain-text URL density**
QA flags 79.4% of plain-text characters are URLs (24,755 / 31,181). This crosses the threshold for spam filter penalties on Proofpoint, Barracuda, and Exchange Online Protection.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image source — content blocking risk**
One product/content image also loads over HTTP:
```
http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg
```
Gmail, Outlook 365, and Apple Mail will block this by default, leaving a broken image in the email body (not just a tracking pixel).

**`max-height: auto` — invalid CSS**
```css
img { max-width: 100% !important; height: auto !important; max-height: auto !important; }
```
`auto` is not a valid value for `max-height`. Browsers silently ignore it; no functional breakage, but it indicates template hygiene issues.

**Missing alt text — 4 images**
- `e323ae89...jpg` — content image, no `alt` attribute
- Thunderhead pixel — no `alt`
- iGoDigital collect pixel — no `alt`
- Silverpop open pixel — no `alt`

Tracking pixels conventionally use `alt=""` (empty string). The content image (`e323ae89`) is the substantive gap — image-blocked recipients see nothing.

**Coremetrics content block appears empty**
```html
<!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->
<!-- Email content lookups code -->
```
The lookup comment is present but no rendered content follows. If this block was intended to inject personalized product recommendations or analytics setup, it silently failed.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Subscriber email exposed in UTM parameter**
The header home link includes:
```
customerEmail=bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==
```
Base64-decodes to: `mira-glitter-michaels-fap6e@etell.app` — the persona's subscriber address is passed as a base64-encoded query parameter on every tracked link. This is intentional SFMC behavior for cross-channel identity stitching but means the subscriber email is readable by any intermediary with URL access.

**SFMC subscriber hash also exposed**
`sfmc_Sub=6a0b108e8b75a227d95acdb55aac28a1c4325c544a88543985a0a0aa55b1b862` — a SHA-256 subscriber identifier is present on CTAs. Standard SFMC practice; noting for completeness.

**No broken merge tokens detected** — no unresolved `%%variable%%` or `{{variable}}` placeholders visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — not captured**
The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. This is a deliverability and compliance gap: Gmail and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (≥5k/day) under their 2024 bulk sender policy. The email presumably has a footer unsubscribe link, but header-level unsubscribe is a separate requirement.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header — not captured (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (`POST` method) was not detected. Gmail demotes bulk mail lacking this to promotions tab and may begin blocking.

**Authentication-Results — not captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture. The sending domain `emdeals.michaels.com` is a subdomain ESP envelope; DMARC alignment with `michaels.com` should be verified independently.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not visible in truncated source but presumably present in footer; flag for full-source verification.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters present and well-structured**
The header home CTA carries:
```
cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Other_Promo/Value-_-20260606_S2_UltimateSaleOnlineStart_OtherPromo_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME
```
Campaign date (`20260606`), campaign name, and placement are all encoded. Attribution should resolve correctly on Michaels' analytics stack (Coremetrics/Adobe).

**Final destination URLs unverified**
Because all 22 CTAs route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` redirects, it is unknown whether UTM params survive the redirect chain to the landing pages. This requires a live click-through probe to confirm.
