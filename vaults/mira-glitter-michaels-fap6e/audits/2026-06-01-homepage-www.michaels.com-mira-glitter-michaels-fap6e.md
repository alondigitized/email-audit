# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the very first thing I see is a giant cookie consent wall eating up basically my entire screen — I can barely make out anything behind it. What I *can* see: Michaels has a "20% Off All Regular Price Purchases" banner with code **HELLOJUNE**, "MakerPlace" is right there in the top nav (that's my rewards program!), and there's a "Buy Again" tab which I appreciate. But the cookie overlay is a serious first-impression killer — it's like showing up to a craft fair and immediately being blocked by a clipboard person asking for your email before you even step inside.

## What's working

- **"MakerPlace" in the top navigation strip** — as a rewards member, seeing my program right at the top without even having to hunt tells me this brand remembers I exist
- **"Buy Again" tab in the bottom nav** — for a craft mom who restocks the same Mod Podge, glitter, and foam sheets on repeat, this is genuinely useful one-tap territory
- **The HELLOJUNE promo banner** — 20% off all regular price is the kind of blanket discount I actually wait for; it's visible, the code is printed right there, and the exclusions are disclosed inline. No fine print shenanigans
- **Red color language** — the brand palette is consistent and the promo strip reads as urgent without being garish; it matches my mental image of Michaels

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie consent modal** — this is the single biggest problem I can see. On mobile it covers the hero completely. The "Got it" button is at the very bottom, and "Cookie Preferences" is below that. I have to dismiss this wall before I see *any* of the actual homepage content
- **No visible hero or CTA** — because of the modal, I have zero idea what Michaels is featuring this week. Summer crafts? Kids' projects? Wedding DIY? Completely obscured
- **"Exclusions apply" with no detail inline** — fine, it's disclosed, but I can't tell from here if my go-to Cricut vinyl or adhesive is excluded. That ambiguity makes me hesitate before I even get excited
- **Search bar feels cold** — "Search Products" is a totally generic placeholder. Something like "What are you making?" or "Search by project" would speak my language

## Recommendations

- **Slim the cookie banner to a bottom toast, not a full-screen takeover.** A thin bar at the bottom of the screen with "We use cookies — Got it / Customize" preserves the hero and lets the actual homepage do its job. This is a ship-next-week fix.
- **Surface a seasonal project hook in the hero.** If it's June, show me a summer party activity kit, a kids' camp craft, or a patriotic wreath — something lifestyle and project-oriented that makes me tap instead of search blind.
- **Add a "MakerPlace Member Pricing" badge to the promo banner.** Right now the 20% off reads as available to everyone. A small badge or secondary line like "Stack with your Member Rewards" would push loyalty members like me to engage more seriously.
- **Swap "Search Products" placeholder for project-intent copy** — "Find your next project" or "What are you making?" costs nothing to change and immediately feels more like talking to a fellow crafter.
