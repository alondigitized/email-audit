# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Michaels doing what Michaels does: a big red 20% off strip, then a purple "Up to 60% off Halloween" hero with "Monster Deals" party décor and balloons. It's October 26, so I'm in last-minute Halloween party mode, and it speaks to me. But the page doesn't mention my rewards membership at all, and a cookie box is sitting on top of the content.

## What's working

- **SAVE20NOW strip:** I always hunt for a coupon code before checkout, and here it's right at the top. No digging through my inbox.
- **"Up to 60% off Halloween" hero:** it names the seasonal markdown I stock up on, with a big bold number.
- **"kids' crafts and more!" copy:** this is the party-planning mom talking to me directly.
- **Nav labels:** "Crafts & Hobbies", "The Knit & Sew Shop", "Balloon Bundles" and "Christmas" get me to my aisles in one tap.
- **Local store line ("LA-PASADENA, Open at 9am"):** helpful for a quick pickup run, and "Buy Online Pick Up In-Store Today" backs it up.
- **"Monster Deals" imagery:** the skull plates, "Trick or Treat" napkins, ghost balloons and pinwheel fans look like a party table I could copy from Pinterest.

## What's weak

- **No Maker's Place rewards presence:** I'm a member, and the page doesn't recognize me. There's no points balance, no member price, no "join" prompt, just "Sign In".
- **Cookie panel:** it takes up a big chunk of the lower right and covers part of the first product tiles. The Feedback tab on the right edge also sits on top of the hero.
- **"Last chance to save!":** it has no date or time, so I don't know whether it's today, this weekend or just filler.
- **"Exclusions apply" / "Excludes candy & carving kits":** I have to guess what's excluded and whether 20% off and 60% off combine.
- **No people:** it's all product, with no kids' party or craft table scene to show how it looks in real life.
- **No reviews or ratings:** nothing visible tells me which products are well-loved.
- **The carousel has 7 dots:** I can't tell if the red strip and hero are separate offers, and the pause button is a lot of chrome.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member strip** under the red promo bar, like "Maker's Place members: extra 10% off Halloween + Christmas kits. Sign in to see your points." That would give me a reason to log in before I buy.
- **Put a real date on the urgency:** swap "Last chance to save!" for "Ends Oct 31 · Pickup ready in 2 hrs". Put the exclusions in a one-line tap-to-expand "See details" instead of "Exclusions apply".
- **Make the cookie panel a slim bottom bar,** or let it auto-collapse, so the first two product tiles and the 50% off cards show cleanly on a phone. Move the Feedback tab off the hero.
- **Add a lifestyle shot** of a kids' Halloween craft or party table, plus a tiny star-rating line under the first row of products.
