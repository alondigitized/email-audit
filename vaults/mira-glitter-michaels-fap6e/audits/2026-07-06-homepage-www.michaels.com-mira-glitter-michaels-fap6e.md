# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the first thing I hit is a giant cookie consent modal that basically ate the entire hero — I can't even see whatever campaign they're running below it. What I *can* see is the red promo bar promising 25% off with code HELLOJULY, the MakerPlace tab (my program!), and a clean nav. Michaels is clearly doing a July push, but they're burying the lede behind a full-screen privacy wall.

## What's working

- **MakerPlace tab in the top nav** — it's the second link, right after "Custom Framing." As a rewards member, I feel seen immediately without having to dig for it.
- **25% Off All Regular Price promo bar** — bold, red, code visible (`HELLOJULY`), no mystery about the discount amount. I'm already mentally scanning my cart.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — that is *so* useful for a repeat buyer like me who restocks the same Mod Podge and acrylic paints every season.
- **"Ask Mike" AI assistant button** — that's a clever helper right in the search bar; I'd probably poke at it to find which Cricut mats are on sale.
- **Search bar is dominant and central** — clean placement, easy to hit on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal blocking the entire hero** — I cannot see a single product, lifestyle image, or below-the-fold offer. This is the single biggest friction I'm experiencing right now. I came to browse summer craft kits, not consent to analytics.
- **No loyalty balance or reward points visible** — MakerPlace is linked, but there's no "You have 340 points" hook to pull me in. My Hobby Lobby app shows my points on the homescreen; this doesn't.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no detail above fold** — I know from experience that seasonal items are often excluded, and I've been burned before. One click to the exclusions list would build trust faster.
- **No visible product imagery or hero scene** — outside the modal, there's literally no visual inspiration — no project idea, no seasonal flat-lay, nothing that says "here's what to make this July."

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a bottom strip** — a persistent bottom bar doesn't kill the hero. The full-screen modal is costing them the first impression entirely on mobile.
- **Add a points-balance chip next to the MakerPlace nav link** — even a static "Earn rewards on every purchase" line would convert better than a naked text link.
- **Put a lifestyle hero shot of a summer kids' craft project in the banner** — I'm here for birthday party craft kits; a hero of kids painting birdhouses or making tie-dye would stop my scroll cold.
- **Make the HELLOJULY code copyable with one tap** — right now it's static text in a banner; a tap-to-copy chip would reduce drop-off at checkout when people forget the code.
