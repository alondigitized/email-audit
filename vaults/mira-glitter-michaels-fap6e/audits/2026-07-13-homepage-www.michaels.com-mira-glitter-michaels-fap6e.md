# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Michaels is leading hard with a "Mega Clearance Event" — up to 70% off, code CRAFTY20 for 20% off regular price — so this is clearly a markdown moment and they know it. The top nav is surprisingly clean for a craft store, with MakerPlace right there in the header which I appreciate. But I'm only seeing the very top of the page, and the fold is almost entirely consumed by this clearance event, which honestly? Works for me.

## What's working

- **The CRAFTY20 promo bar** — right at the top, specific code, specific discount. I don't have to hunt for the deal. This is exactly how I shop: open email, grab code, verify it's on the site.
- **"Mega Clearance Event — Up to 70% Off"** — the yellow-on-black hero is loud and impossible to miss. As someone who stocks up at seasonal markdowns, this headline is my reason to stay.
- **MakerPlace in the top nav** — I'm a Maker's Place rewards member, so seeing it instantly in the navigation tells me this experience is built for people like me, not just casual browsers.
- **"Buy Again" tab in the nav strip** — this is a gift for returning shoppers. I can jump straight to my repeat purchases without digging.
- **"Ask Mike" AI button** — this is a smart UX move. If I need help finding a specific glitter weight or paint type, having a quick-answer tool right there reduces friction.

## What's weak

- **No MakerPlace rewards balance or points callout above the fold** — I'm a member; show me what I have to spend. A "You have 250 points" nudge would accelerate my click.
- **Hero imagery is dark and product-less** — I can see what looks like a yellow/green abstract background. There's no actual craft product, supply, or lifestyle scene visible. For a clearance event, I want to see *what's* on sale.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no click-through** — this is the exact thing that makes me nervous about whether CRAFTY20 stacks with clearance. A "see details" link would defuse the hesitation.
- **No category signaling in the hero** — "Mega Clearance Event" on what? Kids' crafts? Yarn? Floral? I have to scroll to find my category, and that's a bounce risk on mobile.
- **Search bar is prominent but generic** — "Search Products" placeholder is fine, but a seasonal suggestion like "Search: kids party kits, summer florals…" would feel more curated.

## Recommendations

- **Overlay the hero with 3-4 category tiles** (Yarn, Kids' Crafts, Party Supplies, Floral) so I can tap directly into my section without hunting through the nav — this is a one-week ship for a mobile homepage.
- **Add a MakerPlace points summary banner** just below the promo bar ("Your 250 pts = $2.50 off — use today!") to reward members at first glance and create urgency.
- **Swap "Exclusions apply" for a linked tooltip or modal** that explicitly says "Stacks with clearance? No. Use for reg-price items." — one sentence saves me from bouncing to Google to find out.
- **Inject a craft lifestyle image into the hero** — even a flat-lay of glitter, paint brushes, and a kids' birthday craft kit would signal the emotional payoff of the 70% off deal.
