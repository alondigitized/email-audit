# 📵 Phone off, savings on! Up to 50% off happening NOW!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels' fourth "Screen Free Summer / up to 50% off" email in five days. The June 14 original, a June 16 near-carbon-copy (scored 9/10 in this trail), a June 17 clone, and now this. The subject-line twist — "Phone off, savings on!" — is the most creative variation yet, and the USA patriotic hero gives the body at least a seasonal visual refresh for mid-June. But it is still the same discount, same campaign, same creative chassis.
- The email is a kitchen-sink with at least five distinct offer sections crammed into one scroll: a 30% single-item coupon at the top, a "Screen Free Summer Savings" 50% off hero, a "Summer Savings" product grid, a "Party Finds 40% off" section, a second "40% off all regular price" banner, a birthday-package section, Michaels Rewards, and a savings-checklist footer. Nothing is prioritized because everything is. The only section that earns its space is the 30% coupon at the very top — that's a clean, single-action offer.
- The birthday-party section is the biggest visible problem: the copy reads "Friend, Kindly reminder the day…" — broken phrasing that looks like a localization artifact or a half-finished merge. A rendering defect like that in the middle of the email erodes trust and reads as careless. At this point in the cadence, Michaels needed a reason to email again. This email doesn't deliver one.

## What's working

- **30% single-item coupon leads the email** — a concrete, single-item offer at the top is the clearest action in the entire send; easy to copy a code or click.
- **USA / patriotic hero art** — the flag imagery gives the "Screen Free Summer" banner a seasonal angle that the previous three sends lacked; it's timely for mid-June.
- **Party Finds section is genuinely differentiated** — the 40% off party supplies block adds product variety that wasn't in prior sends and cross-sells a real category.
- **Michaels Rewards callout** — the "Earn $5" loyalty section reinforces membership value and gives a reason to click even for casual browsers.

## What's weak

- **"Friend, Kindly reminder the day…"** — the birthday section header is visibly broken copy; reads like an untranslated template string or failed merge token.
- **Five competing offer blocks** — 30% off, 50% off, 40% off party, 40% off all regular price, plus birthday + loyalty; no single offer wins, so attention diffuses.
- **Fourth Screen Free Summer email in five days** — the subject tweaks ("Phone off, savings on!") can't mask that this is the same email list receiving the same discount for the fourth time this week. Fatigue is real.
- **No visual anchor below the fold** — the Summer Savings product grid, Party Finds grid, birthday block, and rewards section all compete with similar visual weight; the eye has no clear path.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill three of the five sections** — pick one primary offer (the 30% coupon OR the 50% Summer Savings, not both) and give it the full email. Every additional block after the first dilutes conversion.
- 2. **Fix the birthday copy immediately** — "Friend, Kindly reminder the day…" must be caught before send. Audit the birthday-party module for localization/merge issues before the next deployment.
- 3. **Give the $149 birthday party package its own dedicated send** — it's a high-AOV, specific offer buried six sections down. It deserves a standalone email with context ("Planning a summer party? Here's a complete package for $149").
- 4. **Retire the Screen Free Summer creative** — after four sends, this concept is spent. The next email needs a genuine new hook (back to school, Fourth of July, Lemax collector drop — anything with new news).
- 5. **Tighten the subject** — "Phone off, savings on!" is the best line in the send; lead with it and let the offer follow more cleanly.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Phone off, savings on — 30% off any one item today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Screen-free summer just got 30% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off summer crafts while it lasts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab your coupon — no phone required`

## Technical Audit — Michaels Email (2026-06-18)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has no broken destination links but carries two confirmed HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources, six images missing alt text, malformed HTML comment syntax at the document head, and missing RFC-required unsubscribe headers — all of which create rendering, deliverability, or compliance exposure.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links not probed** — all route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com`. Health of destination URLs is unverified; any dead product or category pages will not surface until a live check is run against the decoded destinations.

**Plain-text body is 82% URLs** (30,884 of 37,563 chars). A ratio this high is a spam-filter signal. The plain-text alternative likely contains only raw redirect URLs rather than human-readable copy.

**Three distinct open/event tracking systems firing simultaneously:**
- `click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx` (ExactTarget/SFMC)
- `na5.thunderhead.com/one/rt/track/ONE-PFSLHYI824-2685/pixel` (Thunderhead/ONE CDP)
- `nova.collect.igodigital.com/c2/100010552/track_event` (iGoDigital/SFMC)
- `svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847` (SoundVector)

Four open-tracking pixels is redundant and inflates reported open rates across systems. Two of the four (`igodigital`, `svopn.e.michaels.com`) fire over HTTP (see §3).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Two images served over HTTP (mixed content — will be blocked by most email clients):**
```
http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg
http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&sv_svemi=...
```
Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy or block HTTP image sources. The product image will render as a broken placeholder; the SoundVector pixel will not fire.

**Six images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA):
| Image | Type |
|---|---|
| `31528c47-4196-49bb-a540-b78900f21a07.jpg` | Content image |
| `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` | Content image |
| Thunderhead pixel | Tracking (alt="" acceptable) |
| iGoDigital track_event | Tracking (alt="" acceptable) |
| svopn.e.michaels.com pixel | Tracking (alt="" acceptable) |
| SFMC open pixel (click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx) | Tracking (alt="" acceptable) |

The two content images need descriptive alt text for accessibility and for image-off rendering. The tracking pixels should have `alt=""` explicitly set to suppress screen-reader announcements.

**Malformed HTML comment at document head:**
```html
<!-- <!-- -->
```
This is a double-opened comment. Most parsers recover silently, but it can cause comment-boundary misreads in Outlook's Word rendering engine, potentially leaking comment text into the visible body.

**Non-standard closing tags in body:**
```html
<img src="https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?..." ...>
</custom>
</custom>
```
`</custom>` is not a valid HTML element. These appear to be ESP template artifacts that were not stripped before send. They are harmless in practice but indicate the template cleanup pipeline is incomplete.

**DOCTYPE:** HTML 4.01 Transitional — standard for email, no issue.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Subscriber email exposed in URL parameters (base64, not encrypted):**

The header logo link contains:
```
customerEmail=bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==
```
Base64-decoded: `mira-glitter-michaels-fap6e@etell.app`

Base64 is encoding, not encryption. The subscriber's email address is recoverable by any intermediate system (CDN, proxy, analytics tool) that logs the full URL. This is a PII-in-URL pattern.

The same subscriber identifier appears redundantly across three additional parameters in the same URL:
```
sfmc_Sub=6a0b108e8b75a227d95acdb55aac28a1c4325c544a88543985a0a0aa55b1b862
mi_u=6a0b108e8b75a227d95acdb55aac28a1c4325c544a88543985a0a0aa55b1b862
```

The Thunderhead pixel URL contains a partially captured `emailaddress%3DMIR` query parameter (truncated in source), and the iGoDigital pixel contains `"email":"mira-glitter-micha` in a JSON payload — also truncated. Both suggest email address is passed in plaintext to third-party tracking endpoints.

**`cm_mmca2` parameter is empty** in the header logo link:
```
cm_mmca2=
```
If Coremetrics segmentation relies on this field, attribution will be incomplete for clicks on the header logo.

No unresolved merge token syntax (e.g., `%%`, `{{`, `[[`) detected in visible source.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — not captured by AgentMail relay. As of February 2024, Google and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (>5,000 msgs/day). Missing header can cause Gmail to display a "Report spam" prompt in place of a native unsubscribe button.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** (RFC 8058). One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` mechanism required by Google/Yahoo since June 2024) is not confirmed present. If absent at the MTA level, Gmail will not surface the one-click unsubscribe UI.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown due to the AgentMail relay not capturing these headers. Cannot confirm DKIM domain alignment between the `From` domain (`emdeals.michaels.com`) and the signing domain.

An unsubscribe link is present in the email body (standard footer pattern visible in the source structure), satisfying CAN-SPAM's visible opt-out requirement — but this does not substitute for the header-level mechanism.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Tracking parameter format is IBM Coremetrics, not GA4 UTM.** All links use `cm_mmc` and `cm_mmca*` parameters:
```
cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-DCE-_-20260618_S1_US_TempD_DCE_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME
```
If the Michaels site has migrated to GA4, these parameters will not be parsed by Google Analytics and organic/direct traffic will be over-attributed. Confirm the site's analytics stack can consume `cm_mmc`.

**`cm_mmca2` is unpopulated** on the header logo link. If other links populate it, the header click will appear as a separate, uncategorized campaign segment in Coremetrics reporting.

**All links pass through `click.emdeals.michaels.com`** before redirecting to `www.michaels.com`. The redirect chain is: click-tracker → destination URL with `cm_mmc` params appended. If the click-tracker strips or re-encodes parameters before the final redirect, UTM/Coremetrics attribution will be lost at the landing page. This should be validated with a live click-through trace.
