# 🏃 DON’T MISS: 50% off Memorial Day Sale ends today.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels' Memorial Day finale send — the fourteenth (or thereabouts) email in roughly ten days — and the urgency lever is finally credible: "ends today." The render is a long, dense scroll of red "50% off" callouts stacked over t-shirts, summer bunting, framed wall art, sewing machines, and a $5 Rewards reminder. Visually it's the same toolkit we've seen all week, just with the deadline tightened to TODAY.
- The strongest read: the email is doing its job for the deadline, but it is creative-fatigued. The hero is still "Pick up to 25% off your order" with a Memorial Day Sale badge — almost identical to the May 22-24 sends. A subscriber who has seen this layout four times in a week is unlikely to discover anything new here; the email is essentially a last-call reminder for people who haven't pulled the trigger yet.
- Judgement: 6.5/10 — directionally correct urgency, exhausted creative, no single hero offer punching through the wall of 50% off blocks.

## What's working

- "Ends today" deadline is finally a real, single-day urgency hook — the most honest urgency of the entire campaign.
- Multiple specific price anchors visible: t-shirts from $7.99, Buy 1 Get 1 Free, $29.99, $189.99, $5 Rewards.
- Memorial Day Sale red/blue banner ties the email to the holiday weekend cleanly.
- Categories are diverse — apparel, framing, sewing, summer décor — so something likely matches a subscriber's interest.
- "Earn $5 Michaels Rewards" block is a clear loyalty hook near the bottom.

## What's weak

- Creative is nearly identical to the May 22, 23, and 24 sends — the subscriber has seen this layout four times this week.
- The hero "Pick up to 25% off" is awkward language and undersells against the dozens of "50% off" callouts below.
- Visual hierarchy is flat — every block is the same size with the same red 50% badge; the eye has nowhere to land first.
- No single hero product or curated "ends today" pick — it's a category catalog, not a finale.
- Email is extremely long for a last-call send; the urgency dissipates by scroll three.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut length by 60%.** A last-call email should be one hero offer + one CTA + a deadline counter. Trim the catalog.
- 2. **Lead with the deadline, not the discount.** Replace the "Pick up to 25%" hero with a giant "Ends tonight at midnight" countdown panel.
- 3. **Pick ONE hero product** (the $29.99 Cricut-style item or the $189.99 sewing machine) as the visual anchor — give the scroller something to remember.
- 4. **Drop the 25% hero entirely** — it conflicts with the 50% blocks and reads as a downgrade.
- 5. **Rotate creative** — after 13 sends with the same red badges, even a different color palette would re-engage fatigued eyes.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — your Memorial Day 50% off`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud + Coremetrics with multiple tracking pixels and redirect domains; structurally sound but has mixed HTTP/HTTPS asset references, missing alt text, and missing one-click unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 21 tracking/redirect links present (`click.emdeals.michaels.com`, `nova.collect.igodigital.com`, `svopn.e.michaels.com`, `na5.thunderhead.com`) — not HTTP-probed, so final destination health unverified.
- Open-tracking pixel uses HTTP: `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true...` — modern clients (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy) may block, distorting open metrics.
- Stacked redirect chains visible (Coremetrics → click.emdeals → final URL) increase latency and risk of broken hops.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Mixed DOCTYPE: HTML 4.01 Transitional declared *after* stray `<table>` and Coremetrics comment block, before `<html>` — invalid document order; some clients may fall into quirks mode.
- Stray unmatched `</custom>` tags inside the tracking-pixel `<div>` — invalid markup.
- Hero/content image `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` served over **HTTP** (`http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/...`) — Gmail/Outlook may block or rewrite.
- Same image is missing `alt` attribute; tracking pixels also lack alt (acceptable for pixels, but the content image is a real a11y gap).
- `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` in viewport meta disables user zoom — accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).
- Outlook MSO conditional sets `line-height:1` on `body, table, td` — likely to clip descenders in Outlook desktop.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Merge tokens appear fully resolved: `customerEmail=bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==` (base64 of recipient), `sfmc_Sub=6a0b108e...`, `emId=...`, `mi_u=6a0b...`.
- No visible unrendered `%%token%%` or `{{handlebars}}` artifacts in the truncated source.
- Note: recipient email is exposed in plaintext-decodable base64 in every link — privacy/PII consideration if forwarded.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header: missing** (QA WARN) — required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules (Feb 2024).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post: missing** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe not supported; risks bulk-sender penalty.
- **Authentication-Results header: not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unverified through the AgentMail relay (may be a relay artifact rather than a real sender issue).
- Physical address / CAN-SPAM postal footer not visible in the truncated HTML — cannot confirm presence (likely below the truncation point).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM-equivalent tracking uses Coremetrics `cm_mmc` params, not standard `utm_*`:
  - `cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Circular/EOW/Promo-_-20260525_S1_MemorialDaySale_Promo_OtherPromo_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME`
  - `cm_mmca2=37976313`
- Consistent campaign tagging across links observed; landing destinations behind `click.emdeals.michaels.com` redirects — final URL alignment with "Memorial Day Sale" not verifiable without following redirects (QA skipped tracking-domain probes).
- No standard `utm_source/medium/campaign` — GA4 attribution will rely on Coremetrics-only tagging unless server-side mapping exists.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers (RFC 8058) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk delivery.
2. Force HTTPS on all `image.emdeals.michaels.com` and `svopn.e.michaels.com` asset URLs.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero content image (`e323ae89-...jpg`).
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from viewport meta to restore zoom accessibility.
5. Clean up invalid markup: stray `</custom>` tags and DOCTYPE positioned after content nodes.
6. Add standard `utm_*` parameters alongside `cm_mmc` for GA4 / cross-tool attribution.
7. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass at the actual MX (AgentMail relay strips Authentication-Results) before treating the WARN as real.
8. Consider not exposing base64-encoded recipient email in every querystring — replace with an opaque server-side ID.
