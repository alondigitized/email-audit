# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Michaels — I'm here for it, I'm already a Maker's Place member, and the first thing I get is a cookie consent wall covering literally everything below the fold. The 30% off banner with code BONUS30 catches my eye immediately, which honestly is the most Michaels thing ever — lead with the coupon, yes — but I cannot see a single product, hero image, or lifestyle shot because the cookie modal is sitting right on top of all of it. It looks like a craft store that knows its audience's wallet but hasn't figured out that blocking the homepage is not the vibe.

## What's working

- **30% off with BONUS30 banner** — this is exactly how I shop Michaels. I came here for the coupon code and it's the first real content I see after the nav. I'm already mentally applying it to my summer birthday party kit haul.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — as a returning member, I love that this is in the quick nav. I have reorder patterns (Cricut vinyl, foam sheets, floral wire) and one tap to my history is genuinely useful.
- **MakerPlace in the top nav strip** — it's above the logo, which is prime real estate. I know immediately this page knows I'm a member.
- **Search bar is prominent and accessible** — big, full-width, labeled "Search Products." When I have a specific supply in mind (rainbow ribbon, acrylic craft paint), I'm going straight to search anyway.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal eating the entire page** — I can see maybe 20% of what looks like an image behind it. The whole hero is gone. This is a massive first impression fail on mobile.
- **No visible hero CTA** — I have no idea what Michaels is pushing right now. Summer camp crafts? Wedding season? Memorial Day decor? The modal killed all of that context.
- **"Exclusions apply" on the 30% off** — always a little squint-worthy. As a regular, I know Cricut machines are usually excluded, but a first-timer might feel bait-and-switched.
- **No loyalty reward balance visible** — I'm a Maker's Place member. Showing me my points or a "You have $X in rewards" nudge above the fold would get me converting faster than a generic percentage-off.
- **No seasonal or lifestyle imagery visible** — it's mid-May, prime graduation/summer camp/party planning season. I want to see kids doing crafts or a graduation banner kit, not a greyed-out page behind a legal wall.

## Recommendations

- **Minimize the cookie banner to a bottom strip** — move it to a slim footer bar that doesn't block the hero. The hero is why I came; the cookie notice is not.
- **Add a loyalty balance or "Your rewards" chip in the header** — even a "You have 450 points" badge next to the MakerPlace link would pull me deeper immediately.
- **Make the 30% off banner link directly to a sale landing page** — right now it has a code but no tap destination. Add a "Shop Now" arrow that takes me to the sale collection, not the generic homepage.
- **Swap the nav strip ordering** — "MakerPlace" deserves more than a cold text link at the top. Give it a small icon or highlight treatment so members feel recognized on arrival.
