# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is very "I've got 45 minutes before pickup and a Pinterest board open in another tab." Michaels is leading hard with the Maker Haul stock-up sale and a sitewide 20% code, which is exactly the kind of coupon-first shopping I do anyway. It reads like a craft supply warehouse that knows its customer buys in bulk when things are cheap, not like a lifestyle brand — which honestly, for me, is fine.

## What's working

- **"Score up to 50% off" hero** — this is my exact shopping trigger, stock-up-and-save, no persuasion needed
- **The deal grid ("Stock up now, thank yourself all year")** — yarn at $9.99, frames 65% off, beads and vinyl 50% off — these are literally my birthday-kit and craft-closet categories
- **MAKER20 sitewide code in the top banner** — I always hunt for the code first, and Michaels just handed it to me before I even asked
- **Nav row with Art Supplies, Kids, Balloon Bundles** — birthday party planning categories are one tap away

## What's weak

- **No visible rewards info** — I'm a MakerPlace member and there's nothing showing my points, tier, or a "welcome back" nudge, just a static nav link
- **Cookie consent banner covering the corner of a product tile** — it's blocking the $24.99 tile's details until I dismiss it
- **"Celebrate Art Month" banner peeking in at the bottom** — Art Month is a March thing, seeing it in August makes the page feel a little unmaintained
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic** — doesn't tell me if I'm shopping frames, yarn, or the whole sale

## Recommendations

- Swap "Shop Now" for something like "Shop Maker Haul Deals" so the CTA matches the specificity of the deal grid below it
- Surface my MakerPlace points/tier right in the header instead of burying membership behind a plain text link
- Pull the stale "Celebrate Art Month" module and replace with a seasonal August tie-in (back-to-school crafts, birthday kits)
- Move the cookie banner so it doesn't sit on top of live product pricing
