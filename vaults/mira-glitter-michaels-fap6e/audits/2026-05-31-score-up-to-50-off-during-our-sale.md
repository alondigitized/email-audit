# Score up to 50% off during our sale!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Michaels sale email in four days, and it opens with the exact same subject line — word for word — as the May 29 send ("Score up to 50% off during our sale!"). That alone makes it dead on arrival for anyone with even moderate inbox awareness. The email itself follows the now-familiar Michaels template: a 30% off hero, then an immediate cascade of seven or eight competing discount blocks (50% off, 30% off, BOGO, 60% off, 25% off, Rewards) stacked top to bottom in what amounts to a visual noise machine.
- There's nothing technically broken here — the render is clean, the brand colors are consistent, and real deals are present. But the creative is completely interchangeable with every Michaels email from the past week. There is no hook, no seasonal angle, no product hero that makes you stop scrolling. The Rewards section that could differentiate for members is buried at the very bottom after a punishing scroll.
- The brand is clearly burning through a sale window, but the identical subject line on a two-day cadence suggests the email program is running on autopilot. A subscriber who opened the May 29 send and didn't buy has no reason to open this one.

## What's working

- **30% off hero lands immediately** — the discount is the first thing you see, no hunt required.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout failures.
- **BOGO section adds genuine variety** — Buy 1 Get 1 deals break the monotony of stacked percentage blocks.
- **Rewards callout is present** — "Earn $5" with step icons gives members a reason to engage beyond the raw discount.

## What's weak

- **Exact duplicate subject line from May 29** — the single worst signal in this send; subscribers who ignored it two days ago get no new reason to reconsider.
- **No urgency or deadline anywhere in the visible render** — seven competing discount blocks but zero "ends Sunday" or "48 hours only" language.
- **Visual hierarchy collapses immediately** — the 30% hero gives way to eight competing blocks within one scroll unit, making it impossible for the eye to prioritize.
- **BOGO "50% off" framing is ambiguous** — visible in the render but the mechanics require a second read to understand; it reads like 50% off anything.
- **No product specificity** — every block links to a category or generic sale, not a single standout item worth clicking for.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire this subject line immediately** — it ran on May 29 and again today; even a small word-order change signals a new send. Any of the alts below would outperform an exact repeat.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the hero** — "Sale ends tonight" or "Last day" would create the urgency the discount alone cannot. Every discount block could echo that date.
- 3. **Surface Rewards earlier** — the "Earn $5" block is the clearest member differentiator; move it to position 2 (right below the hero) rather than after a six-block gauntlet.
- 4. **Collapse to three blocks maximum** — hero (30% off), one supporting tier (60% off clearance), one BOGO — then CTA. The 8-block wall trains subscribers to skim and close.
- 5. **Pick one product hero with a price** — a specific item (e.g., a $12 floral wreath now $8.40) converts better than a wall of abstract percentages.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off ends tonight — shop the sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off everything + earn $5 Rewards today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your member discount stacks — here's what's left at these prices`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Florals, frames, yarn, and more — all on sale through midnight`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Michaels "Score up to 50% off" (2026-05-31)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally functional but has two HTTP mixed-content image sources, missing alt text on multiple tracking pixels, and unverifiable authentication/unsubscribe headers due to AgentMail relay capture gaps. No broken links or merge token leakage detected.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by HTTP probe** — all route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com`. Final destination health is unverified; if any redirect target is down, click-throughs silently 404.

**Tracking pixel stack is heavy and fragmented** — three separate open-tracking systems fire in parallel:
- `https://na5.thunderhead.com/...` (Thunderhead/One)
- `https://nova.collect.igodigital.com/...` (Salesforce IGO/Marketing Cloud)
- `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/...` (SilverPop — HTTP, see §3)
- `https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?...` (primary ESP pixel)

Redundant pixels inflate load weight and produce conflicting open-rate counts across systems.

**Plain-text body is 81% URLs** (27,262 / 33,621 chars) — QA flag confirms this. Spam filters that analyze plain-text ratio may score this negatively. The plain-text version appears to be auto-generated with unexpanded link wrappers rather than human-readable fallback copy.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Two HTTP image sources** — will be blocked by Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail when proxy-fetched over HTTPS:

| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| `e323ae89-...f33897013cd1.jpg` | `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` |
| SilverPop open pixel | `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&...` |

The first is a content image — if blocked, a visible content gap will appear. The second is a tracking pixel — blocked silently, but SilverPop open metrics will be undercounted.

**Four images missing `alt` text:**
- `e323ae89-...f33897013cd1.jpg` — content image; missing alt means screen readers and image-blocked clients get no context
- Thunderhead pixel (`/pixel?uri=outbound://emailpixel/...`)
- IGO pixel (`/track_event?payload=...`)
- SilverPop pixel (`svopn.e.michaels.com`)

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty, not absent) to satisfy accessibility validators without exposing tracking intent.

**`<!DOCTYPE>` is misplaced** — the doctype declaration appears mid-document, after an opening `<table>` and `</custom>` tags. This is a malformed document structure; the `<!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->` template block is injecting content before the doctype. Rendering is client-dependent but technically invalid.

**Unclosed `<custom>` tags** — two `</custom>` closing tags appear in the body with no corresponding opening tags. These are likely leftover ESP template artifacts.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `%%FIRST_NAME%%`, `{{{field}}}`) detected in the truncated source.

`customerEmail` URL parameter in nav links contains a Base64-encoded value (`bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==`) — this decodes to a recipient email address. This is expected for click personalization but confirms PII is embedded in link parameters; see §5.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** — QA confirms the header is absent from what the relay recorded. If the header is present at SMTP level but stripped by the AgentMail relay, this is a relay configuration issue. If it is genuinely absent, the email is non-compliant with Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (mandatory since Feb 2024 for senders >5K/day).

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not captured** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unverifiable. Same caveat as above re: relay stripping vs. genuine absence.

**`Authentication-Results` header missing** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed. Sending domain is `emdeals.michaels.com`; authentication chain should be verifiable but was not captured.

**PII in URL parameters** — `customerEmail` and `sfmc_Sub` query params carry recipient email address (Base64) and hashed subscriber ID in plaintext GET parameters across all nav/header links. These values appear in server logs, referrer headers, and analytics pipelines for every click. This is a data hygiene concern under GDPR/CCPA if any third-party analytics (e.g., GA4) is present on landing pages.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters are present on the home link: `cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Circular/EOW/Promo-_-20260531_S1_Circular_MichaelsSale_WKND_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME`. This confirms Coremetrics/IBM attribution is wired.

All 22 tracked links route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` before landing — final URL health is unprobed. If the sale landing page is time-gated (ends before email expires in inboxes), late openers will land on a non-sale page with no redirect or messaging update.
