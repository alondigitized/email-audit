# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I popped Michaels.com and the first thing I see is a giant cookie consent wall sitting right on top of whatever they were trying to show me. Michaels has ALWAYS been my go-to for stocking up during seasonal sales, so I know this store — but right now this homepage is screaming "legal team won" over "customer team won." The one bright spot: that red promo banner peeking above the modal with SPRING30 is exactly why I opened this site in the first place.

## What's working

- **SPRING30 promo banner** — That red strip with "30% Off All Regular Price Purchases" is the first real content I can read, and as someone who opens Michaels specifically to use a coupon code, this lands instantly. The code is spelled out plainly.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — One tap access to my reorder list is genuinely useful for a repeat buyer restocking Mod Podge and glitter glue. Smart placement in the persistent nav.
- **MakerPlace in the top strip** — I'm a rewards member; seeing MakerPlace up top without having to hunt for it reminds me I have points. Good.
- **"Savings" tab in the nav** — A dedicated savings tab in the main navigation means I don't have to dig. That's exactly how I shop.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is eating the entire homepage** — I opened Michaels to browse spring craft kits and instead I'm reading about analytics partners. The modal covers everything below the promo banner. This is the biggest above-the-fold failure on the page.
- **No hero image visible** — I have zero idea what Michaels is featuring this week. Is it Mother's Day crafts? Summer party supplies? I can't tell, and that's a missed opportunity to pull me in.
- **No primary shopping CTA** — Other than the nav tabs, there is no "Shop Now," no category shortcut button, nothing that says "tap here and go buy something." The only button visible is "Got it" — a consent action, not a shopping action.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — I always check what I need to hit for free shipping before I start filling my cart. It's nowhere above the fold.
- **"Exclusions apply" on the 30% off** — Not exactly weak, it's standard, but as a Hobby Lobby cross-shopper I know this sometimes means the exact item I want is excluded. Would appreciate clarity earlier.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default on return visits** — If I'm logged into MakerPlace, you already know I've agreed. Stop interrupting my second, third, and fourteenth visit with this wall.
- **Swap the generic cookie overlay for a slim top banner** — Compress the consent notice into a one-line sticky footer so the hero and categories are visible on first load, especially on mobile where screen real estate is precious.
- **Surface a "Party Planning" or "Kids' Crafts" shortcut tile above the fold** — I plan birthday party craft kits every few months. A seasonal category tile ("Summer Party Kits," "Tie-Dye Sets") would pull me straight to my highest-intent category.
- **Add the free-shipping threshold to the promo banner** — Change "30% Off All Regular Price Purchases" to "30% Off + Free Shipping Over $49 with code SPRING30" if that's the deal. That one line would bump my AOV immediately.
