# 🎈 The Party Shop: Discover everything you need to plus up the occasion – for less!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels' second Party Shop email in 48 hours — the June 17 "NEW arrivals have landed at The Party Shop!" send scored 9/10 in this trail — but this one justifies the follow-up by building a coherent Father's Day narrative rather than just re-announcing a store section. The email earns its scroll: balloons at $9.99, tableware, a "10-minute centerpiece" DIY, and a gift section give a subscriber multiple buying intents to land on. The dual-offer structure (30% off banner up top + 40% coupon at bottom) gives both deal shoppers and project shoppers a reason to click.
- The subject line is where this email breaks down. Eighty-plus characters, no mention of Father's Day, and "plus up the occasion" is the softest possible way to describe a sale that has a natural hard deadline: Sunday. A subscriber scanning their inbox on June 18 has no idea this is time-sensitive based on the subject alone. The June 17 Party Shop send had the better subject ("NEW arrivals have landed") — shorter, more concrete, more ownable.
- Inside, the execution is clean. Pricing is visible, CTAs are present, Rewards are shown. No render bugs. But the absence of a deadline anywhere in the email — not in the hero, not in the CTA — leaves conversion on the table for what is objectively an urgency-driven occasion.

## What's working

- **Father's Day theming is thorough and credible**: Balloons, tableware, gifts, and a quick DIY project form a coherent occasion story, not a random product dump.
- **30% off + 40% single-item coupon**: The dual-offer covers both bulk buyers and cherry-pickers.
- **Michaels Rewards section present**: The $5 earn signal reinforces member value mid-scroll without hard-selling.
- **Pricing visible throughout**: Specific price points ($9.99, etc.) appear in product tiles — this signals real deal specificity.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, no overlapping text, no obvious layout issues.

## What's weak

- **Subject never mentions Father's Day**: The single most relevant urgency signal is completely absent from the subject line, costing open rate from the most motivated segment.
- **No hard deadline anywhere**: "Father's Day is almost here" appears in body copy but there's no "ends Sunday" on the offer — the urgency exists implicitly but isn't activated.
- **Second Party Shop email in two days**: The June 17 send already introduced this section; sending again this quickly without a distinctly fresh hook creates cadence fatigue.
- **"Shop the Party Shop" CTA is category-generic**: It sends to a section page, not a curated Father's Day collection — one extra click for the most motivated buyer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put Father's Day and a deadline in the subject**: This is the single biggest lever. The email has real urgency — Sunday is days away — but the subject line throws it away.
- 2. **Add "ends Sunday" to the hero offer banner**: One line of copy on the 30%-off banner converts a browsing scroll into a purchase-mode click.
- 3. **Change the primary CTA to "Shop Father's Day gifts"**: More specific, maps to the buyer intent this email is actually building.
- 4. **Differentiate from the June 17 send**: A "top 5 gifts under $25" module or a single featured bundle SKU would give this email distinct new news.

- **Subject Alt B:** `🎈 Father's Day in 2 days: The Party Shop picks for less`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Balloons, tableware, quick centerpieces — sale ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last-minute Father's Day sorted: party supplies, gifts + a 40% coupon.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Michaels "Party Shop" Email (2026-06-18)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has structural integrity issues (DOCTYPE placed mid-document after content), two mixed-content HTTP image sources, and multiple tracking pixels missing alt attributes. Authentication header capture is incomplete via the AgentMail relay, leaving SPF/DKIM status unverifiable.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**DOCTYPE placement error** — The `<!DOCTYPE HTML>` declaration appears mid-document, after an opening `<table>` block and ESP template markers (`<!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->`). The actual document root begins inside what should be body content. This is a malformed document structure that parsers will handle inconsistently across clients.

**21 click-redirect links unprobed** — All CTAs route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com`. Final destinations were not verified by the QA probe. Broken redirect chains would be silent failures.

**Unclosed custom tags** — Two `</custom>` closing tags appear in the open pixel `<div>` with no matching opening tags:
```html
<img src="https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?...">
</custom>
</custom>
```
These are invalid HTML and indicate ESP template tag leakage.

**Plain-text body is 83% URLs** — QA reports 30,492 of 36,827 plain-text characters are URLs. This is a strong spam signal for filters that score the plain-text alternative part.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Two HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources** — These will be blocked or generate mixed-content warnings in most modern clients:
- `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg`
- `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&sv_svemi=...`

The second is also a tracking open pixel — HTTP delivery means it will silently fail in Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook on secure connections.

**Six images missing `alt` text** — Including two content images and four tracking pixels. Content images without alt text degrade the experience when images are blocked (common in Outlook). The tracking pixel alt omissions are lower severity but technically non-compliant with WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1.

```
87cb1e22-6e5c-4d47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg   — content image, no alt
e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg   — content image, no alt (also HTTP)
thunderhead.com pixel                        — tracking pixel, no alt=""
igodigital.com track_event pixel            — tracking pixel, no alt=""
svopn.e.michaels.com open pixel             — tracking pixel, no alt="" (also HTTP)
```

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string) to suppress screen reader announcement.

**`max-height: auto` is invalid CSS** — Appears in the mobile media query:
```css
img { max-width: 100% !important; height: auto !important; max-height: auto !important; }
```
`max-height: auto` is not a valid value and will be ignored. This is benign but indicates template debt.

**Duplicate `p { margin: 1em 0; }` rule** — Declared twice in the same `<style>` block. No functional impact, but signals template copy-paste issues.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens detected in the truncated source. The `customerEmail` parameter in the visible URL fragment contains a Base64-encoded value (`bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==`) which decodes correctly — no raw token leakage observed.

The `sfmc_Sub` and `emId` parameters are present and appear populated, consistent with Salesforce Marketing Cloud send-time personalization.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** — QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` header via the AgentMail relay. This header is required for Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender compliance (enforced since Feb 2024 for >5k/day senders). If the header exists upstream but is stripped by the relay, the relay configuration needs review. If it is genuinely absent, this is a deliverability and compliance violation.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not captured** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (required by same Gmail/Yahoo policy) was also not detected. Same caveat applies re: relay stripping.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — Not verifiable from truncated source. Assumed present in footer; should be confirmed.

**Authentication-Results not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Sending domain is `emdeals.michaels.com`; alignment with DMARC policy on `michaels.com` is unverified.
