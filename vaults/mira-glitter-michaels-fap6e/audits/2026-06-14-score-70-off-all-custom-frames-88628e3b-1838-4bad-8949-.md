# Score 70% off all custom frames! 😁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels Custom Framing's third send in ten days carrying the same 70% off custom frames offer. The June 7 email literally opened with "Score 70% off all custom frames during The Ultimate Sale" — and today's subject is "Score 70% off all custom frames! 😁." The creative team swapped the lightning bolt for a grinning face emoji and dropped "The Ultimate Sale" copy, but any subscriber who opened either of the last two sends will recognize this immediately as a repeat. That cadence problem overshadows what is otherwise a clean, well-produced email.
- Inside the render the hierarchy works: the dark blue hero panel puts "Frame anything you love." and the 70% badge front and center, followed by a feature section ("Custom frame anything, easily"), a $10 in-store bonus offer mid-scroll, a lifestyle family photo, and a second CTA panel at the bottom. No broken images, no overlapping text — the layout holds. The offer layering (70% off + $10 secondary) is a legitimate lift over the May 24 send, which introduced that same two-punch structure.
- The fundamental problem is fatigue. By the third near-identical send, the 70% badge has lost its price-anchor power — it's just Michaels' permanent price as far as this subscriber is concerned. The email needs either a harder deadline or a creative pivot to earn its slot in the inbox.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is immediate.** 70% off badge and "Frame anything you love." headline land above the fold with no competing clutter.
- **Offer layering.** The $10 secondary offer mid-email gives a second reason to click for subscribers who hesitate at the hero.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, no layout collapse, no overlapping text — the email displays correctly.
- **Lifestyle photo integration.** The family/group photo in the lower section grounds the pitch in a real use case (framing memories) rather than just a product shot.

## What's weak

- **Near-duplicate of June 7.** Subject line and core offer are functionally identical to last week's send; subscribers who opened that one will feel this is a copy-paste mistake.
- **No deadline anywhere visible.** "70% off" without a "through Sunday" or "48 hours left" line reads as the permanent price, killing urgency entirely.
- **Emoji selection is off.** 😁 on a 70%-off promo feels like filler personality — the June 7 send used ⚡ (energy/urgency), which was the better choice.
- **CTA copy unreadable at this resolution** — but the buttons are small and numerous, which dilutes click intent. Multiple "Shop Now" style buttons compete rather than direct.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the subject and hero.** A 70% off offer with no expiry is indistinguishable from the catalog price at this point. "Ends Sunday" or "3 days left" would be the single highest-impact change.
- 2. **Retire this creative.** Three sends with the same headline discount in 10 days will train subscribers to ignore the next send. Rotate to a new angle — product spotlight, a specific frame style, a customer gallery — and hold the 70% for the final deadline push.
- 3. **Consolidate CTAs.** Two or three competing buttons spread across sections dilute click intent. Pick one primary destination per scroll zone.
- 4. **Father's Day had a clear emotional hook.** The June 5 "If you frame it, Dad will hang it" line was stronger than anything in today's email — if Father's Day (June 21) is the actual deadline, make that explicit.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Frame a memory before Father's Day — 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $10 off your next in-store visit. Two ways to save.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by [date] to get it in time for Dad.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**DOCTYPE misplacement (critical):** The document begins with a `<table>` block before the DOCTYPE declaration:
```html
<table ... class="stylingblock-content-wrapper"> ... </table>
<!-- -->
<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>
<html>
```
Any content before `<!DOCTYPE>` forces IE and legacy Outlook into quirks mode for that initial block. This is a WCA template assembly bug — the Coremetrics wrapper block is injected outside the document root.

**CSS syntax errors in `<style>` blocks:**
- `a:visted` typo (line ~43) — should be `a:visited`. The rule never applies.
- `@-ms-viewport { width: device-width; s }` — stray `s` character inside the rule block; invalid CSS that will be ignored by IE/Edge.
- `max-height: auto !important;` — `auto` is not a valid value for `max-height`; the declaration is ignored. Likely intended as `max-height: none !important;` or removed entirely.

**Images missing `alt` text (3 instances, confirmed by QA):**
- Thunderhead pixel
- iGoDigital collect pixel
- Aaron Brothers open-pixel (`http://svopn.aaronbrothers.com/...`)

All are tracking pixels and should carry `alt=""` explicitly to suppress screen-reader announcements.

**Open pixel uses no `alt` attribute:**
```html
<img src="https://click.em.michaelscustomframing.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
```
This one correctly has `alt=""` — the three flagged above do not.

### 7. Recommendations

**Priority 1 — Compliance (blocking for inbox placement):**
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at the MTA level, not just in the AgentMail relay capture. Contact WCA/ESP support if headers are being stripped.
- Add a plain-text MIME part. Zero-character text body fails Gmail bulk sender requirements and will degrade deliverability scores.

**Priority 2 — Security/Privacy:**
- Remove or hash the `customerEmail` Base64 parameter from UTM strings. Pass an opaque subscriber ID instead if click attribution per-subscriber is needed.
- Audit Thunderhead and iGoDigital pixel URLs for email-in-querystring patterns; replace with hashed or tokenized identifiers.

**Priority 3 — Stale infrastructure:**
- Remove the `http://svopn.aaronbrothers.com/` open-pixel. It is HTTP-only, will be blocked by most modern clients, and the Aaron Brothers brand was retired. If Aaron Brothers open-rate data is still needed, replace with an HTTPS endpoint.

**Priority 4 — Template/rendering:**
- Fix the WCA template so the Coremetrics wrapper block (`MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content`) is injected inside `<body>`, not before the DOCTYPE.
- Patch the three CSS errors: `a:visted` → `a:visited`; remove stray `s` from `@-ms-viewport`; replace `max-height: auto` with `max-height: none`.
- Add `alt=""` to all three tracking pixel `<img>` tags.
