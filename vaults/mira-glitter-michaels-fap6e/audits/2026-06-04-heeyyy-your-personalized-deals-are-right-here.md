# Heeyyy! 👋 Your personalized deals are right here →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Michaels has dressed up its nth deal-stack email with a "personalized" subject line and a casual "Heeyyy!" opener — but the body is the same cascading discount grid that ran on June 3 ("👀Check out these deals") and June 2 ("Fill your cart for less"). The personalization claim is completely cosmetic: there's a "You'll love these personalized deals" section header, but what follows is identical category blocks with no evidence of any actual subscriber-level targeting. That broken promise is the single biggest thing dragging this email down.
- The creative itself is competent. The red 25%-off coupon hero anchors the visual hierarchy cleanly, and stacking multiple 50%-off category blocks beneath it follows a formula that reliably generates clicks for Michaels. The Rewards "Earn $5" block near the bottom is a nice retention touch. But the recycled Party Shop LIVE invite (last seen June 2, scored 7/10) now feels like creative inventory running dry rather than a fresh event push.
- Bottom line: this is Michaels on autopilot, wrapped in a subject line that overpromises. It will convert existing intenders but does nothing to earn attention from a subscriber who has now received five near-identical emails in three days.

## What's working

- **Red coupon hero is immediately dominant** — the eye goes straight to 25% off without any hunting.
- **Offer density is high** — multiple 50%-off category blocks give something for multiple buyer types (arts, kids, party).
- **Rewards block ("Earn $5") adds a loyalty hook** that the June 3 deal-stack email lacked.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent column structure throughout.

## What's weak

- **"Personalized" is a lie** — the section header promises it, but every block is a generic category tile. Savvy subscribers notice this.
- **"Heeyyy!" subject opener is trying too hard** — casual tone with no substance behind it; the subject delivers zero information about what the offer actually is.
- **Party Shop LIVE block is recycled** — this exact invite appeared in the June 2 send. Running it again without a new angle (or a deadline) just signals that Michaels is filling vertical space.
- **Competing CTAs dilute click intent** — coupon + 6+ category blocks + Rewards + Party RSVP + savings checklist is not a hierarchy, it's a fire sale.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — this is the fourth consecutive deal-stack email from Michaels in under 72 hours; the subscriber's inbox is signaling diminishing returns.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the personalization real or cut the word entirely.** If the platform can surface one category block based on past purchase behavior (e.g., "Based on your last order — 50% off yarn"), that earns the subject line. If it can't, remove "personalized" — it's actively eroding trust.
- 2. **Put a hard deadline on the 25%-off coupon in the hero.** "One-Day" buried in small print should be the headline: "Today only: 25% off one regular-price item." Urgency converts.
- 3. **Cut the Party Shop LIVE block** or timestamp it explicitly ("3 days left to register"). Recycled evergreen content reads as filler.
- 4. **Collapse to one CTA above the fold.** Everything below the hero coupon should support that single action, not compete with it.
- 5. **Rest the deal-stack template for one send.** A product-forward email or a single-category deep-dive would reset subscriber attention before the next promotion.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 25% off one item at Michaels`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% off coupon expires tonight →`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off select categories — no code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your savings before midnight — rewards members earn $5 back.`

## Technical Audit — Michaels "Personalized Deals" (2026-06-04)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is sent via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget/emdeals.michaels.com) with Coremetrics click tracking and three concurrent tracking pixel vendors. Two HTTP image sources and missing authentication header capture by the AgentMail relay are the primary technical risks.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click links unverified** — all route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` redirect domain, which the QA prober correctly skipped. No broken destinations confirmed, but final-URL resolution is blind.

**Three simultaneous tracking pixel vendors** detected in source:
- Thunderhead ONE: `https://na5.thunderhead.com/one/rt/track/ONE-PFSLHYI824-2685/pixel`
- iGoDigital/Salesforce Collect: `https://nova.collect.igodigital.com/c2/100010552/track_event`
- Silverpop open beacon: `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847` *(HTTP — see §3)*

**Pixel div contains orphaned `</custom>` tags** (two instances, no matching opening tag):
```html
<img src="https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?...">
</custom>
</custom>
```
Malformed HTML; may cause parser errors in strict renderers.

**Plain-text body is 81% URLs** (29,145 of 35,827 chars). Spam filters scoring plain-text parts will flag this. SFMC should generate a stripped plain-text alternative, not a URL dump.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (2)** — will be blocked by iOS Mail, Gmail, and most modern clients that enforce HTTPS:

| Asset | Source |
|---|---|
| Product image `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` | `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89...` |
| Silverpop open pixel | `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true...` |

The product image HTTP source means it will render broken in Gmail/iOS for any recipient whose client auto-blocks mixed content.

**Missing `alt` text on content image** — `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` has no `alt` attribute. Tracking pixels without alt are acceptable; a content/product image is not.

**Invalid CSS**: `max-height: auto !important` appears in the `@media` block. `auto` is not a valid value for `max-height`; should be `none` or a length. Low impact in practice but triggers validator warnings.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** by AgentMail relay. Michaels as a major sender almost certainly emits this header at the MTA level; the issue is relay-side capture, not sender omission. However, the QA pipeline cannot confirm compliance without the header value.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured** — same relay gap. One-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender rules) cannot be verified.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail unknown. AgentMail relay should be configured to preserve or forward this header from the receiving MX.

Preheader text present and correctly hidden: `"Get your faves on sale: Up to 50% off décor, floral, Pride and graduation."` — implementation is correct (`display:none !important`).

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link expected in footer — not visible in the truncated source, but standard for Michaels sends.
