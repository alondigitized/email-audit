# Hello, Party People! You’re invited to party at the one-stop-party shop 🥳 🎉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a same-day reminder for The Party Shop LIVE stream (Thursday, June 5 — the day after send), and on its own terms the event-invite top half is the freshest creative Michaels has sent in this entire run. The invite box, the date signal, and the festive visual language create a real, time-bounded reason to act — something entirely absent from the June 3 ("Check out these deals") and June 4 ("Heeyyy! Your personalized deals") send pairs.
- The bottom half immediately squanders that goodwill. The two 25% coupon blocks are pixel-for-pixel identical to what ran on June 3 and June 4. By this point in the week, a Michaels subscriber has been conditioned to skip straight past those red boxes — they've stopped reading them as offers and started reading them as furniture.
- The net result is a structurally interesting email with a weak second act. The event invite is doing real work; the coupon stack is reflexive filler that undercuts the occasion.

## What's working

- The Party Shop LIVE invite creates genuine, named urgency — a Thursday stream is a specific reason to act that generic "sale ends soon" language never provides.
- Festive visual palette (balloons, confetti background) is consistent with the subject line's party theme and gives the hero an actual identity.
- Two-tier coupon structure (single-item 25% → Rewards all-purchase 25%) gives loyalty members a clear reason to feel differentiated.
- Render is completely clean — no broken images, no layout bleed, no empty fields.

## What's weak

- The coupon stack is the third consecutive day running the identical 25%-off-any-one-item / 25%-off-all format — by now it reads as wallpaper, not an offer.
- The "Complete your savings checklist" grid at the bottom is all-category spray; zero party-specific products are surfaced despite the entire email being party-framed.
- The subject line clocks in at 75+ characters — "Hello, Party People!" burns the first 21 characters before the actual news, and the most important phrase ("Party Shop LIVE") lands past where most mobile clients truncate.
- No social proof for the stream itself — no past viewer count, no featured host name, nothing that creates FOMO for a consumer deciding whether to tune in.
- The event date/time is visible in the hero box but absent from the subject line, so the urgency is invisible until after the open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the event invite the full email — remove the coupon stack entirely.** The Party Shop LIVE is the rare differentiated concept in Michaels' current run; burying it behind two lines of the same-week coupon grid dilutes both. Push the coupon to a post-event email on June 6.
- 2. **Replace the savings checklist with 4–6 specific party products** linked directly to the Party Shop category — tableware, balloons, custom banners. Let the email simultaneously drive stream viewers AND seed a cart.
- 3. **Add one credibility signal to the event section** — "Join 15,000+ subscribers" or a named host creates FOMO the current invite completely lacks.
- 4. **Rewrite the subject to lead with urgency and trim to under 50 characters.**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Party Shop LIVE is tomorrow 🎉 You're in`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your invite expires Thursday — Party Shop LIVE`
- 5. **Write a preheader that adds event specifics the subject skips.**
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stream starts Thursday · Live demos, deals & party inspo.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Join us Thursday at 7 PM + 25% off any regular price item today.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Two structural HTML errors (misplaced DOCTYPE, stray `</custom>` tags) will force quirks-mode rendering in strict clients. Four concurrent tracking pixel vendors embed subscriber PII in query strings across HTTP and HTTPS origins, and header-level compliance signals are unverifiable through the relay capture.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**21 click links unverifiable.** All destination URLs route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` redirect. QA probes were skipped for this domain — broken link detection is blocked for this send.

**Four independent tracking pixel vendors active simultaneously:**

|---|---|---|

| Thunderhead (One) | `na5.thunderhead.com` | HTTPS |

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — DOCTYPE not first element.** A `<table>` block containing `<!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->` and the email content lookup wrapper precedes the `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">` declaration. Clients that enforce document-order DOCTYPE detection will render in quirks mode.

**Stray `</custom>` tags (×2).** Two closing tags with no corresponding opening element appear immediately after the open-tracking pixel `<div>`:
```html
<img src="https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?...">
</custom>
</custom>
```
Invalid HTML; behavior is client-defined.

**Missing alt text on 4 images** (confirmed by QA):
- `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` — content image, HTTP source, no `alt`
- Thunderhead pixel — no `alt`
- iGoDigital pixel — no `alt`
- Sitecore Send open pixel — no `alt`

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string); the content image needs descriptive alt text.

**`max-height: auto !important`** in `@media (max-width: 480px)` is invalid CSS. `auto` is not a valid `max-height` value; intended value is `none`.

**Duplicate CSS rule.** `p { margin: 1em 0; }` is declared twice in the `<head>` style block. Second declaration has no effect.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** from relay capture. RFC 2369 compliance cannot be confirmed from this audit — requires direct ESP-side verification in SFMC send settings.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** (RFC 8058). One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender guidelines for high-volume lists. Cannot confirm compliance.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown for this send; the AgentMail relay did not surface these headers. Deliverability posture is unverifiable from captured headers alone.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not visible in the truncated source. Footer was cut off; presence cannot be confirmed or denied from this capture.

**Preheader hardcodes day of week:** `"Join us in store this Sunday..."` — if the send or the event timing shifts, this copy becomes factually incorrect with no dynamic fallback.
