# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Ok this is very much "we know exactly what a craft mom scrolling at 9pm looks like" energy — moody Halloween hero, a screaming 60% off, a coupon code already sitting right there. It reads like a proper seasonal-markdown event, which is my whole shopping personality, but the page is a little cluttered with pop-ups fighting for my attention before I've even scrolled.

## What's working

- **"Up to 60% off" hero with "Boo It Yourself Sale"** — punny, on-brand, and it's exactly the seasonal clearance moment I wait for before I stock up on décor.
- **The dual CTA setup ("Shop Now" / "Shop All Sale")** — gives me a fast lane into the sale without hunting.
- **GETMY30 code sitting right in the top banner** — I always hunt for the coupon code first, and this one's handed to me before I even scroll.
- **"The Knit & Sew Shop" and "Crafts & Hobbies" in the main nav** — one tap and I'm exactly where I shop.

## What's weak

- **The "Ask Mike" AI chat bubble popped open unprompted** and is literally covering the top promo text ("30% Off All...") so I can't read what the deal even is.
- **The cookie consent banner is parked over the bottom-right of my screen**, blocking one of the second-row Halloween promo tiles I'd probably want to tap.
- **No loyalty/rewards presence** — I'm a Maker's Place member and there's nothing above the fold telling me my points, member pricing, or a personalized nudge.
- **No people in any of the imagery** — it's all product/set-dressing shots, so nothing here signals "this is for someone like you."

## Recommendations

- Don't auto-open the Ask Mike chat bubble on page load — let it sit as a closed icon so it's not blocking the promo banner.
- Push the cookie banner into a slim bottom strip instead of a floating box that eats a whole promo tile.
- Surface my Maker's Place rewards balance or member-pricing badge somewhere above the fold — even a small "Welcome back, Members save an extra 10%" line would do it.
- Swap "Shop Now" for something more specific like "Shop Halloween Décor" so the CTA copy matches the visual (the gothic bookshelf/frame scene) instead of reading generic.
