# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the first thing I hit is a full-screen cookie wall that basically buries the entire homepage — I can barely see a sliver of what looks like a hero image peeking out behind it. The promo strip is doing its job ("20% Off All Regular Price Purchases Online only with code SAVE20MAY"), and I love that "Buy Again" tab because I literally re-order the same Mod Podge and glitter cardstock every month. But that cookie modal needs to go — or at least shrink — because right now Michaels is showing me a privacy disclaimer instead of a reason to shop.

## What's working

- **The 20% off promo strip** — a red banner with a clear code ("SAVE20MAY") is the first thing my eye lands on after the logo. That's actually good placement; it survives the chaos.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — as someone who re-orders the same three supplies constantly, a dedicated "Buy Again" shortcut in the main nav is a smart returning-shopper hook.
- **MakerPlace in the top nav** — that's my people. Seeing it up front tells me Michaels is still leaning into the maker/DIY identity, not just acting like a generic big-box craft store.
- **Search bar** — prominent, above the nav tabs, easy to tap on mobile. If I know exactly what I need (Cricut mat, 12" x 12" cardstock pack), I'm one tap away.
- **"Savings" nav tab** — quick path to deal-hunting for a coupon-first shopper like me.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal covers the entire viewport** — this is the biggest problem. On my phone, I'm staring at a privacy policy wall instead of a homepage. I should not have to dismiss a full-page overlay before I can even see what Michaels is selling today.
- **No loyalty/rewards visibility** — I'm a MakerPlace member and I can't see my points balance, a member-exclusive deal, or any "welcome back" signal. That's a missed re-engagement moment.
- **Hero is invisible** — whatever campaign Michaels is running (Mother's Day crafts? Summer party kits?) is completely wasted because the cookie modal sits on top of it.
- **"Exclusions apply" on the only visible offer** — I know it's legally required, but without being able to see *what's* excluded, I'm already skeptical the 20% will apply to what I actually want to buy.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always want to know if I can get free shipping before I start building my cart. That number should be in the promo strip or a sticky bar.

## Recommendations

- **Make the cookie banner compact and bottom-anchored** — shrink it to a slim bar at the bottom of the screen (à la GDPR banners in Europe). Let me see the hero and the first content rail without having to tap "Got it" first.
- **Add a loyalty balance chip to the header** — show me "✦ 240 pts" next to the account icon. One small change that immediately makes me feel like a known, valued customer.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold in the promo strip** — append "Free shipping on $59+" to the SAVE20MAY banner. That's the number that turns browsing into a cart-building mission for me.
- **Pin a seasonal/occasion category above the fold** — Mother's Day was just last weekend; pivot that hero immediately to summer birthday party kits, end-of-school-year teacher gifts, or summer camp crafts. That is *exactly* what I'm shopping for right now.
