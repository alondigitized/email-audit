# Extended online – TODAY ONLY! Get up to 60% off at The Ultimate Sale.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels doing what Michaels does by June 14 — stacking coupon on top of sale on top of rewards on top of dollar-off tiers, all under the same "Ultimate Sale" banner they've been running for the better part of a week. The twist here is "Extended online – TODAY ONLY," which lands as cynical rather than exciting given that the June 13 send explicitly said "deals END SOON: The Ultimate Sale is almost over." Subscribers who saw that email know the deadline was fake, which hollows out the urgency in this one.
- The email itself is technically competent. The 60% off hero is large and visible, the "Shop the sale" CTA is unambiguous, and the Michaels Rewards "$5 earn" block at the bottom rewards loyalty members. But the sheer density of competing offers — 25% single-item coupon, 60% off sale, 30% all regular price, tiered $20/$15/$25 off, Rewards — creates decision paralysis rather than a clear action path. None of the offers are resolved into a single clear benefit.
- Honest read: a functional promo blast from a brand that has been over-mailing for days. The "extended TODAY ONLY" framing is the most interesting creative decision here, and it undermines itself.

## What's working

- **60% off hero is unmissable** — the large yellow/orange banner with the "ULTIMATE SALE" seal anchors the eye immediately above the fold.
- **25% coupon placed above the hero** — putting the stackable single-item coupon before the sale section is smart sequencing; it primes the reader with a personal win before the broader sale.
- **Cricut product section** adds a specific aspirational product moment that breaks the repetitive grid layout.
- **Michaels Rewards "$5 earn" block** surfaces loyalty value for members without being buried in the footer.

## What's weak

- **Fake urgency backfire** — the June 13 "almost over" send makes "Extended online TODAY ONLY" read as a manufactured extension, eroding trust rather than creating urgency.
- **Five competing offer mechanics** (25% coupon, 60% off sale, 30% coupon, tiered $ off, Rewards) have no hierarchy — no single "here's the best deal for you" moment.
- **"TODAY ONLY" in subject caps** reads like a spam signal; combined with the identical "Ultimate Sale" campaign label, inbox fatigue is near-certain for subscribers who have received every email in this series.
- **No personalization or category signal** — same blast whether you buy yarn, Cricut supplies, or kids' art. The circular product grid feels random, not curated.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one offer and lead with it.** Five concurrent mechanics (coupon + sale + tiered $ + 30% + Rewards) cancel each other out. Either lead with the stackable story ("25% coupon + up to 60% off = your actual price") or with the Rewards earn. Not both.
- 2. **Retire "The Ultimate Sale" branding.** It has appeared in every single Michaels email this week. Rename this send or segment it so it doesn't feel like the eighth identical campaign name in four days.
- 3. **Make the extension honest.** If you're extending, say *why* ("We had so much response we added more items online") — don't just say "TODAY ONLY" after having already said it was ending.
- 4. **Cricut section deserves its own send.** "Is your clutter making you a Cricut?" is a genuinely curious hook buried mid-scroll under six other offers. That sentence could anchor a standalone email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 25% coupon stacks with the sale — last chance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off + your coupon = biggest Michaels savings this month`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your single-item coupon on top of 1,000s of sale prices — online only, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The extension is real: shop sale + coupon together before midnight`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a malformed document structure (content precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`), two mixed-content HTTP image sources, and missing one-click unsubscribe headers. Authentication status is unverifiable via the relay. QA pass rate is 43%.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Multiple tracking stacks active simultaneously** — four separate systems are firing:

| System | Evidence |
|---|---|
| IBM Coremetrics | `<!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->` comment; `cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-...` param on links |
| Salesforce Marketing Cloud | `sfmc_Sub=6a0b108e8b75a227d95acdb55aac28a1c4325c544a...` in click URLs |
| Thunderhead (ONE) | `https://na5.thunderhead.com/one/rt/track/ONE-PFSLHYI824-2685/pixel` |
| SilverPop/IBM Watson | `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true` |
| iGoDigital / Salesforce Collect | `https://nova.collect.igodigital.com/c2/100010552/track_event` |

**22 click-tracking links** all route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` — destination URLs unverifiable by QA probe.

**PII exposed in URLs:** `customerEmail` parameter carries a base64-encoded subscriber email (`bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==`) in every click link. The Thunderhead pixel also appends `emailaddress=MIR...` (truncated in source). Email addresses in query strings are logged in server access logs, CDN logs, and browser history — high PII exposure risk.

**Open pixel uses HTTP:** `http://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?AFYFXGAB5PKE5M6VW7URMMSF2M.100216` — insecure; will be blocked by Gmail and Apple Mail.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed document structure:** Content (a `<table>` block with `<!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->`) appears *before* the `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">` declaration. This causes rendering-mode ambiguity across clients.

**Invalid custom tags:** Two unclosed `</custom>` tags appear in the pixel `<div>`:
```html
<img src="https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>
</custom>
```
These are not valid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict clients.

**Mixed content — two HTTP image sources (will be blocked by modern clients):**
- `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg`
- `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&sv_svemi=...`

**Missing alt text on 4 images**, including all tracking pixels (expected) but also at least one content image (`e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg`) — fails WCAG 2.1 AA for email.

**`max-height: auto` is not valid CSS** — appears in mobile media query (`max-height: auto !important`). Should be omitted or set to `none`.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Links use **Coremetrics `cm_mmc` parameter format** rather than standard `utm_*` parameters:
```
cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Other_Promo/Value-_-20260614_S2_UltimateSaleOnlineExtension_OtherPromo_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME
```

This only attributes correctly if the receiving site has Coremetrics/IBM tag active on landing pages. If the site has migrated to GA4 or another analytics platform, these sessions will arrive with no campaign attribution. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign` params are present in any visible link.

Campaign date embedded in `cm_mmc` (`20260614`) is June 14 — this extension email is dated June 17, so the tracking code appears to be reused from the original send without updating the date segment.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Compliance/Deliverability:**
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to all bulk sends — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
2. Rebuild plain-text alternative with actual copy, not link dumps — current 79.5% URL density will damage sender reputation.
3. Verify SPF/DKIM pass via a relay that surfaces `Authentication-Results` headers.

**P1 — Privacy/Security:**
4. Remove `customerEmail` (base64 PII) from click URL query strings — use an opaque subscriber token instead. Same fix needed for the Thunderhead `emailaddress` pixel param.
5. Upgrade all HTTP image/pixel sources to HTTPS — specifically `image.emdeals.michaels.com` and `svopn.e.michaels.com`.

**P2 — Rendering:**
6. Move `<!DOCTYPE>` to the very top of the document — pre-DOCTYPE content forces quirks mode in some clients.
7. Remove the two invalid `</custom>` tags.
8. Fix `max-height: auto` → `max-height: none` in the mobile media query.
9. Add `alt` text to the content image `e323ae89-...jpg`.

**P3 — Attribution:**
10. Confirm Coremetrics tag is still active on michaels.com landing pages, or add `utm_*` params in parallel to ensure attribution survives any analytics platform migration.
11. Update the `cm_mmc` date segment from `20260614` to `20260617` for this extension send to maintain accurate campaign segmentation in reports.
