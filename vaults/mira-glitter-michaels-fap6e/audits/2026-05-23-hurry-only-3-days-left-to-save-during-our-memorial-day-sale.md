# Hurry! Only 3 days left to save during our Memorial Day Sale.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels' Memorial Day countdown email — the twelfth (or close to it) in roughly nine days — and it reads exactly like that. The subject swaps "4 days only" (May 23 morning send) to "3 days left," the body is the same stacked-block waterfall of 30/40/50/60% off offers, and the creative is functionally identical to every send since May 20. The hero is a patriotic blue banner with a 30% off lead, followed by Party deals, a kitchen-sink middle section, a Creatology/kids block, and a Rewards module at the bottom.
- The email renders cleanly — no broken images, no obvious layout failures — and the Memorial Day timing is on-brand. But the signal-to-noise is brutal: six to eight distinct discount tiers compete for attention in a single scroll, no single offer has a hero product moment, and at this point in the cadence any subscriber who hasn't bought has learned to ignore Michaels until the sale ends.
- The single thing this email has going for it is credible deadline urgency. "3 days left" is specific and time-bounded. That's the one lever. Everything else is recycled.

## What's working

- **Concrete deadline in subject** — "3 days left" is specific and believable, which is the strongest open driver left after nine days of promos.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, all blocks load correctly.
- **Patriotic/Memorial Day hero** — the blue banner with flag imagery correctly signals the campaign occasion; it's visually appropriate for the moment.
- **Loyalty module still present** — the "Earn $5 Rewards" section gives members a reason to click that goes beyond the sale discount.
- **Party deals section** — continuing the Party Shop thread from the May 21 send ("🥳 Make every occasion special") is smart category consistency.

## What's weak

- **Cadence has destroyed open intent** — this is email 12 in ~9 days; the subscriber who hasn't converted has been trained to scroll past every Michaels send until the sale disappears.
- **No hero product** — six to eight discount tiers with no single featured item means the eye has nowhere to land and nothing specific to want.
- **"Hurry!" opener is a mild spam-signal** — combined with the subject length (61 chars, truncated on most mobile clients), the inbox preview is doing no work.
- **Visual hierarchy collapses** — every block shouts at equal volume (30%, 40%, 50%, 60%, BOGO); by the third block the reader is numb.
- **No differentiation from yesterday's send** — the May 23 morning email already used "4 days only — 50% off"; this email adds no new offer, no new product, no new reason to care.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero product and feature it above the fold** — a single in-stock party supply, frame kit, or Memorial Day craft at the lead offer percentage would give the reader something concrete to want instead of a menu of percentages.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the offer, not the clock** — the deadline is the hook but a specific product or category makes it land harder.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 days: 50% off party supplies & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day ends Monday — 50% off inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Party decor, frames, kids crafts — all on sale through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance to stock up before prices go back up.`
- 3. **Collapse from 6-8 blocks to 3** — lead offer + one secondary category + Rewards. The current depth buries click intent.
- 4. **Add one product image with a price** — "Patriotic balloon arch kit — was $24.99, now $12.49" converts better than "50% off party supplies."
- 5. **Suppress or throttle to engaged-only segment** — subscribers who have not opened in the last 5 sends should be rested; sending to the full list at this cadence accelerates list fatigue.

## Technical Audit — Michaels Memorial Day Sale (2026-05-23)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) with three overlapping analytics stacks (Coremetrics, IBM Thunderhead, iGoDigital/SFMC Collect) and a SalverStar open-pixel. Two mixed-content HTTP sources and missing authentication headers are the highest-severity findings.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Multiple analytics stacks firing in parallel**
- Coremetrics open pixel: `click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?OV7O7W6A3J3URBS6SSITHU2I6Q.100219`
- IBM Thunderhead: `na5.thunderhead.com/one/rt/track/ONE-PFSLHYI824-2685/pixel`
- iGoDigital Collect: `nova.collect.igodigital.com/c2/100010552/track_event`
- SalverStar: `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847` *(HTTP — see §3)*

22 click links all route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` redirect. Redirect chain depth is unverifiable (QA skipped probing those domains), so broken-destination risk is unconfirmed.

**PII exposure in click URLs**
The header logo href contains the recipient's email address as base64 in a query parameter:
```
customerEmail=bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==
```
Base64 is encoding, not encryption. The raw address is visible in server logs, proxy logs, and referrer headers at the destination. This is a data-handling concern regardless of whether it's intentional for Coremetrics attribution.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed content (HTTP image sources) — will be blocked in most clients**
1. `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/lib/fe4015707564057d701173/m/1/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` — product image, no alt text
2. `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&...` — SalverStar open-track pixel

Gmail and Outlook proxy all remote images over HTTPS; HTTP sources that cannot be proxied (e.g., dynamic tracking URLs) silently break.

**Missing alt text — 4 images**
| Image | Issue |
|-------|-------|
| `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` | Product image, empty alt — invisible to screen readers |
| Thunderhead pixel | Tracking pixel — `alt=""` acceptable but not set |
| iGoDigital `track_event` pixel | Same |
| SalverStar `svopn` pixel | Same |

Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly; the product image needs a descriptive alt string.

**Malformed HTML**
- Two stray `</custom>` closing tags appear immediately after the open-tracking pixel `<img>` — these are not valid HTML 4.01 or HTML5 elements and may confuse parser-based spam filters.
- Comment `<!-- <!-- -->` is malformed (nested open comment syntax).
- `p { margin: 1em 0; }` is declared twice in the same `<style>` block (lines ~26 and ~56 of the embedded CSS) — harmless but indicates copy-paste template debt.

**DOCTYPE**
HTML 4.01 Transitional. Functionally fine for email but predates common email client normalization expectations.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved `%%variable%%` or `{{token}}` placeholders visible in the truncated source. Subscriber identifiers are populated correctly:

The `customerEmail` base64 value decodes cleanly to the test-inbox address (`mira-glitter-michaels-fap6e@etell.app`), confirming merge is resolving at send time.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — missing from relay capture**
QA did not find `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers. If these are absent at the SMTP level (not just missing from AgentMail relay capture), this violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (enforced since Feb 2024 for >5k/day senders) and weakens CAN-SPAM compliance.

Action needed: confirm whether the SFMC sending infrastructure injects these headers at the MTA level before attributing to relay capture gaps.

**Authentication-Results — unverifiable**
SPF/DKIM status not captured. Sending domain is `emdeals.michaels.com` (subdomain of `michaels.com`). DMARC pass requires SPF or DKIM alignment on that subdomain. Cannot confirm from available data.

**Plain-text body is 80% URLs**
25,959 of 32,590 characters in the plain-text part are URLs. This ratio is a strong spam-filter signal and suggests the plain-text part is auto-generated from the HTML without stripping tracking parameters.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM/attribution parameters are present on all observable click links:
```
cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-DCE-_-20260523_S2_US_TempA_DCE_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME
mi_ecmp=DCEmail20260523
```

Campaign code `DCEmail20260523` matches the send date. Both Coremetrics (`cm_mmc`) and the secondary `mi_ecmp` param fire on the same link — dual attribution is expected given the Coremetrics/SFMC stack overlap.

Destination URLs are not verifiable (all click links are wrapped in `click.emdeals.michaels.com` redirects and QA skipped probing them), so landing page alignment cannot be confirmed programmatically.
