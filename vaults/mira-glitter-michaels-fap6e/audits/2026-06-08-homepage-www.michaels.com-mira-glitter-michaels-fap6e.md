# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the first thing I see opening Michaels on my phone is a wall of cookie consent legalese that completely buries whatever is actually on the homepage. Once I peek past the nav and the promo strip up top I can tell this is a craft store in promo mode — there's a 30% off code flashing right there — but I literally cannot see the hero, any product imagery, or any reason to stay until I dismiss that modal. Not the warm crafty welcome I was expecting from my go-to store.

## What's working

- **30% off with code ULTIMATE30** — visible in a high-contrast red carousel strip before the modal hits. As someone who always checks for a coupon code first, this lands exactly where I need it.
- **"MakerPlace" in the top nav** — my rewards program is the very first link at the top of the page. That's knowing your audience.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — this is a returning-member shortcut I would absolutely use. I'm not browsing cold; I re-order the same Crayola bulk packs every birthday season.
- **Search bar prominence** — big, full-width, right under the logo. I know what I want; I search first.
- **"Custom Framing" and "Digital Downloads" in top nav** — speaks to the breadth of what they do beyond just aisle supplies, though neither is my lane today.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal dominates the entire screen** — I cannot see the hero, any product imagery, any seasonal campaign, or a single reason to keep scrolling. This is the homepage equivalent of a store that locks the door until you sign a waiver.
- **No shopping CTA above the fold** — the 30% offer is there but there's no "Shop Now" or "Redeem" button attached. It's a carousel strip with arrows; where does tapping go?
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always do the mental math before I add to cart. Not seeing it upfront is friction.
- **No loyalty balance or personalized greeting** — I'm a MakerPlace member. Knowing my rewards balance or "Hi Mira, you have 400 points" would hook me in two seconds. Nothing.
- **Promo strip copy "Exclusions apply." is small** — I know that fine print is real, and as someone who cross-shops Hobby Lobby, I've been burned by it.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal automatically after 3 seconds or make it a slim bottom bar** — burying the homepage behind a full-screen legal overlay on mobile is an immediate bounce trigger. A bottom sheet that doesn't hide the hero is table stakes in 2026.
- **Attach a "Shop the Sale" button directly to the 30% promo strip** — the offer is doing the work but there's no tap destination. Add a red pill button: "Shop Sale — Use ULTIMATE30."
- **Surface free-shipping threshold and loyalty points balance in the sticky top bar** — swap one of the top nav slots (or add a thin bar above it) that says "Free shipping over $XX | You have X MakerPlace points." Every Millennial mom knows her points balance.
- **Swap the promo-code hero (once the modal is gone) for a lifestyle scene** — a flat-lay of a birthday party craft kit spread, maybe a mom and a kid making something, signals the Pinterest-craft audience immediately before they even read a word.
