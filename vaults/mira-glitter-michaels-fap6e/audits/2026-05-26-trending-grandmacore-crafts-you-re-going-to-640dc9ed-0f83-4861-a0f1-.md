# Trending Grandmacore crafts you’re going to ❤️ ❤️ ❤️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- After fourteen Memorial Day sale emails and yesterday's Jonathan Adler collab launch, Michaels is doing something smarter here: leaning into a genuine cultural trend — Grandmacore — to frame a product discovery email rather than hammering another discount headline. The subject is the most specific and culturally-aware thing this brand has sent in weeks. The email itself earns that promise with a grid of on-trend crafting categories (cross-stitch, pressed flowers, needlepoint-adjacent) anchored by a 25% off hero, a buried 50% clearance section, and a 20% off lifestyle section lower in the scroll.
- The weakness is architectural: three distinct discount tiers (25%, 50%, 20%) buried in a very long scroll with no obvious expiration creates an offer-confusion problem. A subscriber who came for Grandmacore vibes has to work to figure out what exactly is on sale and for how long. The editorial energy set up in the subject doesn't carry all the way down the page.
- Still, this is the second consecutive send that breaks from the fatigue of the Memorial Day blast cycle. The creative team deserves credit for naming the trend rather than just running a product grid.

## What's working

- **Subject is the best this brand has sent in two weeks.** "Grandmacore" is a real, searchable trend — naming it signals curation and cultural awareness, not just inventory clearance.
- **25% hero is clean and dominant** — the eye lands on the offer immediately, the Grandmacore aesthetic imagery supports the trend claim.
- **Product grid maps to the brief** — cross-stitch, pressed flowers, and other cottagecore-adjacent categories are actually visible; the editorial promise and the product grid are aligned.
- **Michaels Rewards "Earn $5" section** gives loyalty members a secondary reason to click even if the discount tiers don't land.
- **Cadence reset is working** — two editorial/trend sends in a row (this + Jonathan Adler) after the sale marathon is the right recovery move.

## What's weak

- **Three discount tiers (25%, 50%, 20%) with no clear expiration** create discount-soup mid-scroll; subscribers can't tell what's the "real" deal.
- **Triple heart emoji stack (❤️ ❤️ ❤️) in subject** reads as low-effort padding after a strong two-word trend anchor — the subject doesn't need it.
- **Email is very long** — the 50% and 20% sections are so far down the scroll that most mobile openers will never see them.
- **No urgency anywhere visible** — unlike yesterday's Jonathan Adler launch or last week's Memorial Day countdown, there's nothing giving the subscriber a reason to act today vs. next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one offer tier with a deadline.** Pick the 25% lead, cut or footnote the 50%/20% sections, and add a "through Sunday" or "this week only" deadline. Discount hierarchy confusion is the single biggest drag on click intent here.
- 2. **Drop two of the three heart emojis.** One ❤️ lands as warmth; three reads as filler. The trend credibility is in "Grandmacore" — lean on the word, not the hearts.
- 3. **Add a light urgency line in the hero.** Something like "Shop through Sunday" under the 25% callout would convert editorial browsers to buyers without changing the tone.
- 4. **Promote the 50% section above the fold.** If there's a genuine 50% clearance, that's the stronger conversion hook — don't bury it below two sections of 25% editorial content.

## Technical Audit — Michaels "Grandmacore" Email (2026-05-26)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders functionally but carries several infrastructure-level risks: two content/tracking assets served over plain HTTP, missing one-click unsubscribe headers now required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy, and a malformed document structure with a misplaced `<!DOCTYPE>` declaration. Authentication status cannot be confirmed from the relay capture.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-wrapped links not probed.** All links route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` — destination URL integrity could not be verified by the automated checker. No broken-link confirmation is possible without following redirects.

**Multiple open/engagement pixels present — three separate tracking systems active simultaneously:**
- IBM Coremetrics/Acoustic: `click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?ODI735VMV3ZUZOLUJPP3LGBFSQ.100218`
- Thunderhead ONE: `na5.thunderhead.com/one/rt/track/ONE-PFSLHYI824-2685/pixel`
- iGoDigital (Salesforce MC): `nova.collect.igodigital.com/c2/100010552/track_event`
- SilverPop: `svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847` *(also HTTP — see §3)*

This is a high pixel load; stacked tracking can inflate open-rate numbers and inflate message size.

**`<!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->`** comment block at document top is empty at send time — Coremetrics dynamic content substitution fired no payload, leaving an empty `<td>` at the email root.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**DOCTYPE misplacement (high severity).** The document structure is:

```html
<table ...><!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->...</table>
<!-- -->
<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>
<html>
```

Content appears *before* the `<!DOCTYPE>` declaration. This forces email clients that honor doctype into quirks-mode rendering for the leading table and may cause layout inconsistencies in Outlook.

**Invalid custom tags.** Two `</custom>` closing tags appear in the body with no matching open tag — these are not standard HTML and may cause parse errors in strict renderers.

**Two images served over HTTP (not HTTPS):**
- `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/.../e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` — a content image, missing alt text
- `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&...` — SilverPop open pixel

Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook proxy/block non-HTTPS image sources. The content image may not render for a significant portion of recipients.

**Four images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). The content image `e323ae89...jpg` is the most impactful — screen readers receive nothing and image-blocking clients show a blank. The tracking pixels lacking alt text is lower severity but adds up with WCAG compliance.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**No unfired tokens detected.** The subscriber ID (`sfmc_Sub=...`), email ID (`emId=...`), and subscriber email (`customerEmail=bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==`, base64-encoded) are all populated in the sampled send.

**`cm_mmca2=37976313`** is present in header links — segment/audience identifier is firing correctly into Coremetrics.

No `%%TOKEN%%` or `{{merge_field}}` placeholders visible in the rendered source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent.** As of February 2024, Google and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (>5,000/day). Its absence increases the risk of Gmail classifying the message as promotional with suppressed delivery, and may trigger bulk sender policy enforcement.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058).** One-click machine-readable unsubscribe is required by the same Google/Yahoo policy. Without it, Gmail's "Unsubscribe" button routes to a web flow rather than firing automatically — this contributes to complaint rates.

**Both headers may be present at the MTA level** but not captured by the AgentMail relay — QA flagged this as a capture gap, not a confirmed absence. Should be verified against raw SMTP headers from a seeded send.

**Authentication-Results header not captured.** SPF/DKIM pass/fail is unknown from this capture. The sending domain `emdeals.michaels.com` should be checked directly via a seeded inbox test (e.g., mail-tester.com or GlockApps) to confirm alignment.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Tracking parameters present and structured.** Header home link uses:
```
cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Brand_Story-_-20260526_S1_Variable_GrandmacoreAnalogCrafts_Story_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME
```
Campaign slug, date, and placement are correctly encoded in Coremetrics format.

**Non-standard parameter scheme.** Michaels uses `cm_mmc`/`cm_mmca*` (IBM Coremetrics) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters. If any downstream analytics tools (GA4, Segment) are not configured to map these, attribution will be dropped on the landing page. This is an existing platform decision, but worth flagging if GA4 is also in use.

**`customerEmail` query parameter appended to landing page URLs** (base64, not encrypted). This transmits the subscriber's email address in the URL, which will be captured in server logs, referrer headers, and any third-party scripts on the destination page. This is a data minimization concern under GDPR/CCPA if EU/CA recipients are in scope.
