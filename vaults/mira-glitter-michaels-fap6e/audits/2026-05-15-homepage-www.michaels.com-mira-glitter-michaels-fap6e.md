# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Oh, Michaels — you're my happy place, but you sure know how to greet a girl with a cookie wall first thing. What I can see above the fold screams "craft store running a site-wide promo," which, fine, that's exactly my speed — but the giant cookie consent modal is eating almost the entire homepage experience. The MakerPlace callout in the top nav and that 30% off banner are doing their best, but they're fighting for attention against a dismissal screen.

## What's working

- **The 30% off BONUS30 banner** — this is exactly how I shop. Email coupon first, then the site. Having the code right on the homepage confirms the deal before I even dig into my inbox. Love it.
- **MakerPlace in the top nav** — I'm a member, so seeing my rewards program called out in the top bar without scrolling feels like the store recognizing me at the door.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — as someone who re-stocks the same Mod Podge and foam brushes for every birthday party kit, this is a genuinely useful shortcut that Hobby Lobby does not have.
- **"Savings" tab in the sub-nav** — one tap to deals. That's my whole shopping strategy condensed into a single button.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the entire hero** — I landed on the homepage to shop, not to negotiate with a privacy screen. This is friction before I've seen a single product.
- **No visible shopping CTA above the fold** — the only actionable button is "Got It." A craft mom with 20 minutes before pickup needs to see "Shop Now" or "Browse Crayola Deals," not a data policy acceptance.
- **Hero content completely invisible** — I have zero idea what campaign or seasonal moment Michaels is pushing right now. If it's a Mother's Day or graduation craft promo, I can't see it.
- **No loyalty balance or personalized greeting** — MakerPlace is in the nav but there's no "Hi Mira, you have 500 points" hook. That's a missed warm-welcome moment for a returning member.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a slim bottom bar** — standard practice now, and it keeps the hero fully visible on first load. A full-screen modal for cookie consent is a 2019 pattern.
- **Promote the MakerPlace points balance front and center** for logged-in members — one line like "You have 750 points — redeem at checkout" would make me feel seen and push me closer to a purchase.
- **Add a "Party & Birthday" or "Kids' Crafts" shortcut tile** above the fold or in the first featured category row — that's my highest-frequency use case and it's invisible here.
- **Attach a deadline to the BONUS30 offer** — "Ends Sunday" or "Today Only" would light a fire. Right now it reads like a standing deal, which is less motivating.
