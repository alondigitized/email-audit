# 🥳 Make every occasion special at The Party Shop
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Michaels' Party Shop department showcase — a clean, well-photographed category email that leans into summer entertaining at exactly the right moment (Memorial Day weekend). The 25% off offer is anchored at the top, the hero is elegant, and the product range is genuinely broad: engagement centerpieces, outdoor party setups, wedding themes, birthday supplies. It does what it sets out to do.
- The problem is it sets out to do too little. There's no urgency, no deadline, no reason to act today rather than Thursday. The email reads as evergreen catalog copy with a coupon slapped on top. That's a missed opportunity on the most party-focused weekend of the year.
- Bigger context issue: this is Michaels' second email today (the DMC 280th anniversary send also dropped on 5/21) and the seventh or eighth touch in seven days. Subscriber fatigue is now the primary risk to open and click rates — the email content itself is fine, but it's arriving in a crowded inbox from the same sender.

## What's working

- 25% off coupon block is the very first thing you see — offer lands before any scroll.
- Hero photography ("Everything to celebrate every occasion") is aspirational and styled well; the table setting reads premium.
- Category range is genuinely useful — engagement, outdoor, wedding, birthday — covers real planning scenarios in one send.
- Clean render throughout; no broken images, no layout breakage, no lorem ipsum.
- Seasonally appropriate: Memorial Day weekend is peak party-planning time, and the creative matches it.

## What's weak

- No urgency anywhere — "The Party Shop" is evergreen. Nothing signals why to click today vs. next week.
- No single dominant CTA above the fold; the email fragments into five-plus small product blocks, each with its own link, creating visual noise.
- The 30% offer appears buried at the bottom of a long scroll — most readers won't reach it.
- Zero Rewards/loyalty mention — especially glaring given the 5/16 Rewards email was the highest-performing send in this brand history (9/10). That hook should carry through every promotional email.
- Second Michaels email today: the DMC anniversary thread sent this same morning. Two sends on one day from the same brand in an already-heavy week is inbox abuse territory.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a Memorial Day deadline to the 25% offer.** "Ends Monday" turns a passive coupon into a conversion driver. Right now there is zero urgency baked in.
- 2. **Choose one hero CTA and make it dominant above the fold.** "Shop The Party Shop" in a full-width button under the hero image. Kill the fragmented mini-block links or demote them below a clear primary action.
- 3. **Surface Rewards earn messaging.** One line — "Every party purchase earns Rewards points" — reminds subscribers of the ongoing loyalty hook the 5/16 email built so effectively.
- 4. **Consolidate with the DMC email or send on a different day.** Two sends on 5/21 cannibalizes opens and trains subscribers to tune out Michaels entirely.
- 5. **Surface the 30% offer higher** — if it's additive to the 25% (different category), call that out explicitly in the hero block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Party supplies up to 30% off — ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day hosting starts at The Party Shop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Engagement parties, outdoor setups, weddings & more — shop your 25% coupon inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From centerpieces to birthday bashes — everything you need, now 25% off`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Michaels "The Party Shop" Email (2026-05-21)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is functionally deliverable but carries several infrastructure issues: two HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources that modern clients will block or proxy-strip, missing unsubscribe headers that violate Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, and a malformed document structure where a `<table>` block is injected before the `<!DOCTYPE>` declaration.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click links unverified**
All destination URLs are wrapped in `click.emdeals.michaels.com` redirects. The HTTP probe was unable to follow them, so broken-link detection is not possible without click-redirect resolution. Evidence:
```
https://click.emdeals.michaels.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ4ODN9AA...
```

**Plain-text body is 81.5% URLs**
QA reports 27,668 of 33,944 plain-text characters are URLs. Spam filters (SpamAssassin, Google Postmaster) penalize high URL-density plain-text parts. This is the single largest deliverability risk in the message.

**Four open-pixel trackers active**
| Pixel | Domain | Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| Primary open pixel | `click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx` | HTTPS |
| Thunderhead CDP | `na5.thunderhead.com` | HTTPS |
| IGO Digital / SFMC | `nova.collect.igodigital.com` | HTTPS |
| SilverPop/Watson | `svopn.e.michaels.com` | **HTTP** |

The SilverPop tracker is HTTP-only (see §3 below).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed document structure**
A Coremetrics `<table>` block is injected *before* the `<!DOCTYPE>` declaration. The DOCTYPE appears mid-document:
```html
<table ... class="stylingblock-content-wrapper"> ... <!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->
</td></tr></table>
<!-- -->
<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>
```
Clients that enforce strict parsing (Outlook desktop, some WebKit clients) may trigger quirks-mode rendering for the entire message.

**Two images served over HTTP**
```
http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/.../e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg
http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847&sv_emopen=true&...
```
Gmail and Outlook 365 proxy all images through HTTPS; non-HTTPS sources show a broken-image placeholder for recipients whose client does not proxy. This is the product image — a visible render failure.

**Invalid CSS: `max-height: auto`**
In the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block:
```css
img { max-width: 100% !important; height: auto !important; max-height: auto !important; }
```
`auto` is not a valid value for `max-height` (valid values are `none`, a length, or a percentage). Should be `max-height: none` or removed entirely. Behavior is undefined across clients.

**4 images missing `alt` text**
- `e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` — product/content image, images-off rendering shows blank
- Thunderhead pixel (`/pixel?uri=outbound...`)
- IGO Digital pixel (`/track_event?payload=...`)
- SilverPop pixel (`svopn.e.michaels.com`)

Tracking pixels with no `alt=""` attribute (even an empty one) fail WCAG 2.1 §1.1.1 and trigger some corporate mail gateway filters.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
QA: `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found`. As of February 2024, Gmail requires this header for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day to Gmail addresses. Its absence will eventually result in mail being deferred or bulk-foldered. This may be an AgentMail relay capture issue rather than a genuine absence — the actual SMTP session headers should be verified directly.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured**
One-click machine unsubscribe is now a hard requirement for Gmail and Yahoo bulk senders. Missing from captured headers.

**SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. The sending domain `emdeals.michaels.com` is a subdomain; its SPF/DKIM alignment with `michaels.com` DMARC policy cannot be confirmed from this capture. Should be verified via MX Toolbox or Google Postmaster Tools.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**
Not verifiable from the truncated HTML — footer content was cut off. Assumed present given Michaels' compliance infrastructure.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Coremetrics UTM format, not standard GA4**
Click links use IBM Coremetrics parameter format:
```
cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Brand_Story-_-20260521_S2_PartyShopSummerEvents_Story_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME
```
If the site has migrated to GA4, these `cm_mmc` params will not be recognized by GA4 natively. Coremetrics is a legacy IBM product; verify whether the destination site still has a Coremetrics tag or if this attribution data is being silently dropped.

**Destination URLs unverifiable**
All 22 click links resolve through `click.emdeals.michaels.com`. Landing page alignment (product availability, UTM → page content match) cannot be confirmed without redirect resolution.
