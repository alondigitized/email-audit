# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Michaels, I see you — but barely, because the first thing I get hit with is a cookie consent wall covering literally everything below the promo banner. I know it's my go-to craft store and the MakerPlace rewards tab is right there in the nav, but that modal is doing me dirty before I can even see what you're promoting this week. The one bright spot: a 30% off promo code banner is sitting right above the chaos.

## What's working

- **The SPRING30 banner** sits above the fold in a bold red strip with the exact code printed out — I can copy-paste that immediately without hunting for it
- **"MakerPlace" in the top nav** — as a rewards member, seeing my program name front-and-center in the utility bar feels like they know I'm a regular
- **"Buy Again" tab** is a lovely shortcut for someone like me who re-orders the same Cricut vinyl and mod podge every few weeks — that's a genuine time-saver
- **Search bar is prominent** and labeled "Search Products" — when I have a specific party-kit supply in mind, I can get to it in one tap without wading through categories

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal covers the entire hero** — I have no idea what's on sale, what the season feature is, or whether there's a Cricut deal this week; it's the first thing I see and it forces me to take action before I can even browse
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — as someone cross-shopping Hobby Lobby, that number matters a lot to me before I commit to filling a cart
- **No loyalty balance or points visible** — I'm a MakerPlace member but I have no idea if I'm close to a reward; that's a missed hook right on load
- **"Digital Downloads" in the top nav** feels techy and out of place as a primary utility link — that's niche, and it's eating real estate that could call out Classes or Project Ideas, which is what actually brings me in
- **Zero imagery visible above the fold** — after the cookie dismiss there might be a great lifestyle hero, but from this screenshot it's a red banner over a blocked screen; nothing to spark craft inspiration

## Recommendations

- **Downgrade the cookie modal to a slim bottom banner** — don't wall off the entire homepage on mobile; a sticky bottom strip for GDPR consent is just as compliant and keeps the hero visible
- **Surface the MakerPlace points balance in the header icon** (like "Hi Mira — 420 pts") or add a one-line loyalty nudge near the promo banner ("Members: earn 3x points this week") — that pulls returning shoppers like me straight into cart mode
- **Replace "Digital Downloads" in utility nav with "Classes & Events"** — that's the reason craft moms plan around Michaels vs. just ordering from Amazon
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line to the SPRING30 banner** (e.g., "30% off + free shipping over $49") — one line, same banner, removes my biggest hesitation before I even land on a product page
