# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open Michaels and the very first thing I'm hit with is a cookie consent wall that buries the entire homepage — I can barely see a sliver of whatever hero they have going on underneath. What I *can* see is a red promo banner promising 30% off with code BONUS30, which honestly is the most useful pixel on the page right now. This looks like a brand that knows its coupon-code audience but fumbled the welcome mat badly today.

## What's working

- **The BONUS30 promo banner** — that red strip is the first thing my eye catches after the logo, and a straight 30%-off-all-regular-price offer is exactly what I came for. No hunting through the nav, no "up to X% off select items" weasel language.
- **MakerPlace in the top nav** — as a rewards member, seeing my program called out in the very first row of links makes me feel like this site knows who I am.
- **"Buy Again" tab** — brilliant for someone who refills the same glitter glue and foam sheets every two months. One tap to my reorder history is a real time-saver.
- **Search bar prominently placed** — I usually know what I need (Mod Podge, foam core, washi tape), so a big, accessible search field is a win.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal is a full-content wrecking ball** — it covers the entire hero and everything below. I have no idea what season or campaign they're pushing. This is the first thing I see and it's not crafts, it's a legal disclaimer.
- **No above-fold loyalty benefit detail** — "MakerPlace" is in the nav but nothing tells me *what* I get as a member. No points balance, no member-exclusive pricing, no "Welcome back, Mira."
- **No free-shipping callout visible** — as someone who regularly loads a cart past $50, I want to see that threshold right away so I can mentally plan my haul.
- **Hero completely inaccessible** — I can see a sliver of what looks like orange and yellow items (seasonal? food craft? birthday?), but the modal cuts it off entirely. The brand's storytelling is invisible to me right now.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal on return visits or trigger it after first scroll** — I am clearly not a first-time visitor (I'm in MakerPlace, I have a Buy Again history), and this modal nukes the entire experience before I've seen a single product.
- **Add a logged-in welcome strip** — a thin banner above the promo strip that says "Welcome back! You have 420 Maker's Place points" would cost almost nothing and convert members like me immediately.
- **Move free-shipping threshold into the promo carousel** — add a second slide to the BONUS30 banner strip: "Free shipping on orders $49+" so that I can do the math in my head before I even start browsing.
- **Make "MakerPlace" in the nav a member-pricing badge trigger** — clicking it should land me on a member deals page, not a generic program explainer. Swap that destination or add a flyout with current member perks.
