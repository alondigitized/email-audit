# Up to 50% off! ☀️ Shop Screen Free Summer Savings for deals on creative supplies ➡️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-carbon copy of the June 14 "Screen Free Summer Savings" send — same subject line, word for word, fired at the same list 72 hours later. In the span of four days, Michaels has now hit subscribers with the Screen Free Summer message at least three times (June 14, the June 16 "less screen time, more crafting" variant that scored 9/10 here, and now this June 17 clone). Where the June 16 version earned its high score partly by refreshing the language and leaning into inspiration, this one resets back to the June 14 subject as if that send never happened. Any subscriber who opened June 14 will read the subject line and mentally file this as a duplicate.
- Inside the email, the execution is technically clean — bright summer florals, bold percentage callouts, a Same Day Printing callout, and the reliable Michaels Rewards reminder at the bottom. But the layout is the same mega-grid Michaels defaults to for every promotional blast: eight-plus competing sections, no dominant hero offer, and a fragmented CTA structure that asks the subscriber to do all the work of figuring out what to actually click.
- The verdict: the creative team built a functional email; the send decision undermined it. The cadence error is doing more damage to this email's performance than anything in the design.

## What's working

- Summer florals and product photography are visually appropriate for mid-June and give the email a genuine seasonal feel.
- Multiple concrete discount callouts (30% off, up to 50%, BOGO) are legible even in a dense grid layout.
- The Michaels Rewards "Earn $5" block is a consistent strength — it reminds loyalty members of tangible member value on every send.
- Same Day Printing appears as a dedicated section — a service differentiator Amazon can't match, even if it's buried.
- The email renders without any visible bugs, layout breaks, or broken image placeholders.

## What's weak

- The subject line is a verbatim repeat of the June 14 send — the single most damaging decision in this email.
- No urgency signal anywhere in the visible body: no countdown timer, no "Ends Sunday," no hard-close date.
- The visual hierarchy is flat — every section competes equally, so the eye has nowhere to rest and nothing prioritizes itself.
- Same Day Printing is buried below the fold after multiple product grid rows, wasting the one offer that is genuinely time-sensitive and Amazon-proof.
- Eight-plus CTA buttons with no primary, making the next step feel genuinely unclear.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Never send the same subject line twice in a 72-hour window.** This single error is why this email will underperform the June 14 version it's imitating. The fix is free.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off custom framing + up to 50% on supplies — Screen Free Summer ☀️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer craft haul: up to 50% off + same-day pickup`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New deals this week — BOGO, category discounts, and Same Day Printing available now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $5 in Rewards when you shop Screen Free Summer Savings.`
- 2. **Pick one hero offer and own it.** Either the 30% off framing deal or the up-to-50% creative supplies message should dominate the top third — not both simultaneously split across competing panels.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** "Ends Sunday" is free copy. Without it, every offer in this email is open-ended, which removes the primary reason to act now rather than later.
- 4. **Promote Same Day Printing above the fold.** It's the one thing in this email that is genuinely differentiated and time-sensitive. It deserves hero placement, not row 5 of a grid.
- 5. **Consolidate to one primary CTA.** Replace the fragmented grid buttons with a single action tied to the hero offer, and demote secondary categories to a scrollable rail below the fold.

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally malformed (DOCTYPE appears mid-document after live markup), contains mixed HTTP/HTTPS image sources, and is missing one-click unsubscribe headers. Authentication chain cannot be verified through the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links unverified**
All CTAs route through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud). Destination URLs and redirect health are unconfirmed — QA skipped probing these.

**Multiple overlapping open-tracking pixels**
Three separate vendors fire open-tracking pixels:
- Salesforce MC: `click.emdeals.michaels.com/open.aspx?KZLRHZ6YM2IUDMKUMS7JV5JWNA…`
- Thunderhead: `na5.thunderhead.com/one/rt/track/ONE-PFSLHYI824-2685/pixel`
- iGoDigital: `nova.collect.igodigital.com/c2/100010552/track_event`
- Silverpop: `svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847`

Redundant pixels add page-weight and create conflicting open-rate attribution across systems.

**PII in query strings**
The home page link passes `customerEmail=bWlyYS1nbGl0dGVyLW1pY2hhZWxzLWZhcDZlQGV0ZWxsLmFwcA==` (base64-encoded email address) as a URL parameter. This gets logged in CDN/server access logs and is visible in browser history. Should be moved to a hashed identifier or server-side session.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**DOCTYPE placement is malformed**
The `<!DOCTYPE HTML>` declaration appears _after_ an already-open `<table><tr><td>` block:
```html
<table ...><tr><td ...><!--MASTER_Coremetrics_SLPH_Content-->...</td></tr></table>
<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>
<html>
```
Any content rendered before the DOCTYPE is outside the document structure. Outlook and some webmail clients may refuse to parse or may double-render.

**Unclosed `</custom>` tags**
Two `</custom>` closing tags appear in the body with no corresponding opening tag — likely unprocessed ESP template artifacts. These produce invalid markup and may confuse renderers.

**4 images missing `alt` text**
| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| Content image | `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/…/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg` |
| Thunderhead pixel | `na5.thunderhead.com/…/pixel?…` |
| iGoDigital pixel | `nova.collect.igodigital.com/…/track_event` |
| Silverpop pixel | `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847…` |

Missing `alt=""` on tracking pixels is a minor lint issue; missing alt on the content image is a real accessibility gap (images-off clients show a broken placeholder).

**2 images served over HTTP (not HTTPS)**
- `http://image.emdeals.michaels.com/…/e323ae89-6f89-4b47-8f9a-f33897013cd1.jpg`
- `http://svopn.e.michaels.com/?sv_cid=0415_03847…`

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy images over HTTPS. An HTTP source either gets rewritten (breaking the Silverpop open-track) or blocked as mixed content, causing the image to fail silently.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not detected**
The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail requires this header for bulk senders (>5k/day) to show the unsubscribe button; Yahoo requires it under their 2024 sender requirements. If the header exists at the MTA level but is stripped by the relay, this is a monitoring gap — not a send-level failure, but needs confirmation.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not detected**
One-click unsubscribe (POST-based) is absent or not captured. This is now required by both Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders and directly affects deliverability reputation.

**Authentication-Results not captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status are unknown through the relay. Sending domain is `emdeals.michaels.com` — confirm DKIM is signed at the ESP level and DMARC policy on `michaels.com` is not `p=reject` in a way that would affect the subdomain.

**Physical address**
Not visible in the truncated source — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address in the body. Assume it's in the footer (not captured), but flag for verification.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM / attribution**
Michaels uses `cm_mmc` (IBM Coremetrics format) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters. The visible parameter on the home link:
```
cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-Other_Promo/Value-_-20260617_S1_Circular_SummerUnplugged_OtherPromo_FF_US-_-HEADER_HOME
```
This is consistently structured. Any analytics tooling expecting `utm_source`/`utm_medium` will not pick this up — confirm Coremetrics/GA mapping is in place on site.

**Destination pages unverified**
All 22 CTAs redirect through `click.emdeals.michaels.com` — actual landing page URLs and their UTM continuity are unconfirmed.
