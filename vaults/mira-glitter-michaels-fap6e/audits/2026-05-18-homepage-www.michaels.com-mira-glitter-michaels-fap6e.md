# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Michaels is clearly my store — the top nav literally says "MakerPlace" which is the rewards program I'm in, and there's a 20% off coupon code staring me in the face the second I land. But I barely got to enjoy any of that because a giant cookie consent wall dropped right on top of everything and buried the actual homepage underneath it. Feels like showing up to a craft fair and getting stopped at the door for a form.

## What's working

- **The 20% off promo banner** is above the fold and formatted like a coupon I can actually use — it has the code right there (MAY20NOW). That's how I shop. I check the code first, then browse.
- **"MakerPlace" in the top nav** is a direct nod to me. I see my rewards program before I even scroll — that's smart real estate.
- **"Buy Again" in the bottom nav strip** is perfect for a repeat purchaser. I reorder the same paints, the same adhesive, the same glitter packs constantly. That tab is made for me.
- **"Savings" tab** right in the nav bar is an instant yes — I'm not going to dig for a coupon page, and they're surfacing it front and center.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal is the entire above-the-fold experience.** It's covering the hero, categories, and any lifestyle imagery that might have made me feel something. I opened Michaels to browse craft kits — I got a legal disclaimer instead.
- **No visible free-shipping threshold.** As someone who cross-shops Hobby Lobby based on price, I need to know immediately whether my cart hits free shipping or not.
- **The hero is essentially invisible.** There's a sliver of what looks like a food or craft image peeking out behind the modal, but I genuinely cannot tell what the brand is pushing right now or whether it speaks to me.
- **No product or category imagery above the fold.** The nav says "Shop" but I can't see what — seasonal? kids' crafts? yarn? Paint? The modal erased all of that discovery.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie banner with a single tap on load or push it to a slim bottom bar** — a full-screen overlay on mobile is a conversion killer for a shopper who came to browse seasonal markdowns, not read a privacy policy.
- **Pull the free-shipping threshold into the promo banner strip** alongside the coupon code — something like "20% off with MAY20NOW + free shipping over $49" would make me feel like I'm winning on two fronts at once.
- **Add a rewards balance chip near the MakerPlace nav link** — something like "482 pts" next to my program name would immediately anchor me as a returning member and push me toward spending to hit a reward tier.
- **Swap whatever the hero is for a May seasonal craft kit lifestyle shot** — think a mom setting up a Mother's Day card kit at the kitchen table, not a product-on-white. That's what makes me pin-save and then buy.
