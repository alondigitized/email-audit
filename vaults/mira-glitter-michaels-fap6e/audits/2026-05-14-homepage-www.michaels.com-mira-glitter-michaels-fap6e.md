# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing I see when I land? A giant cookie consent modal eating the entire screen — which is exactly the kind of thing that makes me want to tap back to Pinterest instead. Past that wall, Michaels is running a 20% off sitewide code right now (SAVE20MAY, very timely for May projects), and I can see MakerPlace and a "Buy Again" shortcut in the nav, which tells me they know I've been here before. But the modal is basically robbing this homepage of its first impression.

## What's working

- **The 20% off banner** — specific code, online-only, May-coded. As someone who always hits the coupon first, this banner is the first thing that registers above the clutter. I'm already mentally noting "SAVE20MAY."
- **"Buy Again" in the nav** — this is a little gift for repeat shoppers. I don't want to re-discover yarn I've bought six times. One tap to reorder is smart.
- **MakerPlace in the top strip** — it signals that this is a community platform, not just a big-box store. That resonates with me as a maker-identity shopper.
- **Search bar front and center** — mobile-first thinking. I usually know what I want (Cricut vinyl, foam sheets, ribbon spools), so a prominent search bar gets me there faster than browsing.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal covers the entire above-fold hero** — I can see maybe 10% of a warm-toned image peeking out from behind the dialog. I have no idea what Michaels is actually pushing right now. This is the single biggest conversion killer on the page.
- **No rewards balance or personalized loyalty hook** — I'm a MakerPlace member and I can't see my points, my tier, or any "Welcome back, Mira!" signal. Hobby Lobby doesn't do loyalty either, so this is Michaels' differentiator — and they're wasting it.
- **Primary CTA is invisible** — whatever the hero button says, I can't see it. The only tappable CTA above fold is "Got it" for cookies. That is not a conversion moment.
- **No urgency on the promo** — the 20% code has no expiration shown. "Ends when?" is the question that tips me from "I'll use it later" to "I'll use it now."

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or minimize the cookie banner after 3 seconds** — or at minimum, make it a slim bottom sheet instead of a full-screen takeover. Every second I can't see the hero is a second I might bounce.
- **Add a countdown to the 20% off promo banner** — even "Offer ends Sunday" would create the push I need to start filling my cart today instead of saving the tab for later.
- **Surface loyalty balance above fold for logged-in members** — a small chip like "248 pts · Redeem" next to the account icon costs nothing and keeps me in the Michaels ecosystem instead of price-comparing at Hobby Lobby.
- **Make the hero copy visible on load** — once the modal clears, the first thing I should see is what Michaels is celebrating this week (Mother's Day gifts? Summer camp kits? Graduation party decor?). If the hero is seasonal and relevant, I'm staying.
