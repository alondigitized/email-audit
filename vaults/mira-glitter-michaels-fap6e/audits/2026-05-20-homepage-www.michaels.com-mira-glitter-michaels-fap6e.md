# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Michaels, you had me at "20% off with a code" — that's exactly the energy I show up for. But the second the page loaded I got a full-screen cookie consent wall blocking literally everything, so the experience I'm actually reviewing is mostly a gray overlay with a "Got it" button. This reads like a craft retailer that knows its deal-seeking core customer but fumbled the welcome mat on mobile.

## What's working

- **The promo banner** — "20% Off All Regular Price Purchases / Online only with code MAY20NOW" is exactly how I shop Michaels. Code is visible, discount is concrete, no need to dig.
- **"Buy Again" nav tab** — I love this as a repeat shopper who bulk-restocks glitter and foam sheets. One-tap to reorder is genuinely useful.
- **"MakerPlace" in the top nav** — I'm a Maker's Place member so seeing my rewards program named in the first row of navigation is a good signal, even if no benefit detail is shown.
- **"Savings" tab** in the sub-nav — this tells me the deals section is one tap away, which is always where I start anyway.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie consent modal** — this is the entire above-the-fold experience on mobile. The hero, any lifestyle imagery, featured categories, all of it is gone behind a wall of legalese. Huge friction on first impression.
- **No loyalty balance or member benefit callout** — as a Maker's Place member I want to see "You have 200 points!" or "Members get extra 10%" somewhere, not just a nav link.
- **No visible primary CTA for shopping** — the only prominent button is "Got it" for the cookie modal. There is nothing pulling me toward a product category.
- **Hero is completely inaccessible** — I can see the very top edge of what might be a colorful craft image, but it's instantly buried under the overlay.
- **"Exclusions apply."** — this is honest, but on a mobile banner with no detail link visible, it introduces doubt. Which 20% is the real 20%?

## Recommendations

- **Lazy-load the cookie banner below the fold on mobile**, or at minimum show it as a bottom sheet rather than a full-page takeover — let me see one hero frame before blocking the screen.
- **Add a loyalty status chip to the header** ("Hi Mira · 320 pts") so returning Maker's Place members know instantly their balance is there — this is one line of personalization that changes the whole vibe.
- **Swap the promo banner navigation arrows for a single "Shop the Deal" CTA button** — right now there's no action I can take on the offer except mentally note the code. Give me a tap destination.
- **Surface a "Party Planning" or "Kids' Crafts" quick-link** in the sub-nav or hero — that's a high-intent category for Millennial moms and Michaels already owns it.
