# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open Michaels on my phone and the first thing I see is a cookie wall taking up half the screen — I can barely make out what's behind it. The brand clearly knows I like a deal (that 20% off banner is front and center), and I can see "MakerPlace" up top which, yes, that's my rewards program, great. But right now this homepage is basically a legal disclaimer with a craft store behind it.

## What's working

- **"MakerPlace" in the top nav bar** — I see my rewards program named immediately, without scrolling; that's a fast recognition signal for returning members
- **"Buy Again" tab in the bottom nav strip** — this is a huge deal for a repeat shopper like me who re-orders the same Crayola paint packs every birthday season
- **20% Off banner with the actual code** — "MAY20NOW" is right there in red, unambiguous; I don't have to hunt for it or click through a pop-up to find the discount
- **"Savings" tab** in the nav strip — one tap to get directly to deals is exactly how I shop

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal blocks the entire hero** — this is the biggest issue; I landed on the page and immediately cannot see any product, imagery, or CTA; it's the worst possible first impression on mobile
- **No visible free-shipping threshold** — I always check whether my cart qualifies before I start adding items; it's nowhere in sight
- **No loyalty balance or reward status** — I'm a MakerPlace member but I have zero idea if I have points to burn right now
- **"Exclusions apply" with no detail** — I've been burned before when my favorite yarn was excluded; I now instinctively distrust banners that say this without linking to the list

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or minimize the cookie banner on return visits** — I've been here before; stop greeting me with a legal wall every single time I open the app or revisit on Safari
- **Surface my MakerPlace points balance in the header** — swap the generic account icon for a "Hi Mira, you have 320 pts" chip; Starbucks does this and it's a conversion machine
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line to the promo banner** — something like "20% off + free shipping over $49" keeps me from bouncing to Hobby Lobby just to check if the total makes sense
- **Move the promo code to a sticky strip that stays visible as I scroll** — the MAY20NOW banner disappears the moment I engage with the page; it should be persistent
