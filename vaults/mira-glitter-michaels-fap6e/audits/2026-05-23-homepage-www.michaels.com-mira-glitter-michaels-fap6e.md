# Homepage snapshot · www.michaels.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Michaels is FULLY in Memorial Day mode and honestly I'm here for it — the patriotic star ornaments and the styled mantle vignette with the framed flags are basically my Pinterest board come to life. The 30% off code front-and-center is exactly what I came for after checking my email this morning. This feels like a brand that knows its customer.

## What's working

- **The SPRING30 promo banner** — red background, high contrast, specific discount with the code printed out. This is why I check my inbox before I shop. Seeing it confirmed here is reassuring.
- **The split Memorial Day hero** — left panel has the DIY star ornaments that scream "make this for the party table," right panel has the finished home-styled look. That before/after craft logic is smart.
- **"Buy Again" in the nav** — that is such a convenience win for a repeat buyer like me who restocks the same ribbon colors and glitter glue.
- **MakerPlace in the top bar** — I'm a member and spotting it immediately tells me my points world is one tap away.
- **Seasonal timing** — Memorial Day landing on Memorial Day weekend is the bare minimum but it still matters. No stale summer-sale carryover here.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button above the fold** — the hero looks gorgeous but there's no "Shop the Sale" or "Get the Code" button. I have to guess that tapping the image does something. That friction matters on mobile.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always factor shipping into my Michaels vs. Hobby Lobby math. If I have to go hunt for it, I might just drive to Hobby Lobby instead.
- **Zero loyalty personalization** — the banner doesn't say "Extra 5% for MakerPlace members" or show my points. A returning member should feel recognized, not like a cold visitor.
- **The bottom "days only" element is cut off** — there's clearly a countdown or flash-sale indicator at the bottom of the viewport and I can't read it. That's a missed urgency hook.
- **No product or category quick-links visible** — below the hero there's nothing pulling me toward yarn, floral, or party supplies. The homepage is betting everything on the hero when I might want to browse by category.

## Recommendations

- **Add a visible "Shop Memorial Day Sale" button on the hero** — even a simple red pill button over the right-panel image would give me a clear tap target instead of hoping the whole image is clickable.
- **Surface a MakerPlace member callout in the promo banner** — something like "30% off · MakerPlace members earn 2x points this weekend" turns a generic coupon into a loyalty reinforcement.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold in the banner or directly below it** — one line like "Free shipping on orders $49+" would keep me from doing the Hobby Lobby math in my head.
- **Make the countdown / flash-sale element readable above the fold** — if there's a "3 days only" timer, move it up where it can actually create urgency instead of getting clipped at the bottom of the screen.
