# ### Summary and Recommendations for Michaels' Party Shop Email (5/21)

#### Overview:
The email is a well-crafted showcase of party supplies with aspirational photography and a broad product range. However, it lacks urgency and clear call-to-action, which reduces its effectiveness despite the strong visual appeal.

#### What Worked:
- **Top-of-email 25% off offer**: Prominent placement at the top.
- **Hero Photography**: Aspirational and styled well.
- **Product Range**: Covers real planning scenarios (engagement parties, outdoor setups, weddings, birthdays).
- **Clean Render**: No broken images or layout issues.

#### What Didn't:
- **No Urgency**: Lacks a deadline that would prompt immediate action.
- **Multiple CTAs Above Fold**: Fragmented into several small product blocks, creating visual noise.
- **Buried 30% Offer**: Not easily discoverable without scrolling extensively.
- **Lack of Rewards Messaging**: No mention of loyalty points despite recent high-performing email focused on rewards.
- **Overcrowded Inbox**: Second email from Michaels today; subscriber fatigue is a concern.

#### Recommendations:

1. **Add Urgency**:
   - Include a deadline in the subject line and hero block: "Ends Monday" or similar.
   
2. **Simplify CTAs**:
   - Choose one dominant CTA above the fold (e.g., "Shop The Party Shop").
   - Demote secondary links or consolidate them into fewer, more prominent blocks.

3. **Surface Rewards Messaging**:
   - Include a line like "Every party purchase earns Rewards points" to remind subscribers of ongoing loyalty benefits.
   
4. **Consolidate Sends**:
   - Combine with the DMC anniversary email if possible, or send on a different day to avoid inbox fatigue.
   
5. **Reposition 30% Offer**:
   - If it's an additional offer, highlight it prominently in the hero block.

#### Alternative Subject and Preheader Texts:

- **Subject Alt A:** `🥳 Party supplies up to 30% off — ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Engagement parties, outdoor setups, weddings & more — shop your 25% coupon inside`

- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day hosting starts at The Party Shop`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From centerpieces to birthday bashes — everything you need, now 25% off`
