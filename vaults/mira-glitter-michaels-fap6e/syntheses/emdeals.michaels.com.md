# ### Summary and Recommendations for Michaels' Party Shop Email (5/21)

#### Overview:
The email showcases the Party Shop department with a wide range of party supplies, including engagement centerpieces, outdoor setups, wedding themes, and birthday supplies. The 25% off offer is prominently displayed at the top, but there's no urgency or clear primary CTA above the fold.

### What Worked Well:
- **Prominent Offer:** 25% off coupon block is visible immediately.
- **Hero Photography:** Aspirational and well-styled images that set a premium tone.
- **Broad Product Range:** Covers various party planning scenarios effectively.
- **Clean Render:** No broken images or layout issues.

### What Didn't Work:
- **Lack of Urgency:** No deadline or reason to act today rather than tomorrow.
- **Fragmented CTAs:** Multiple small product blocks create visual noise and dilute the primary CTA.
- **Buried Offer:** 30% off offer is at the bottom, likely missed by most readers.
- **No Rewards Mention:** Misses an opportunity to reinforce loyalty messaging.
- **Over-Sending:** Second email from Michaels on the same day; subscriber fatigue is a risk.

### Recommendations:

1. **Add Urgency:**
   - Include "Ends Monday" or similar deadline in the subject line and hero block.
   
2. **Dominant Primary CTA:**
   - Use a full-width button above the fold with text like "Shop The Party Shop."

3. **Surface Rewards Messaging:**
   - Add one line below the hero image, e.g., "Every party purchase earns Rewards points."

4. **Consolidate Sends:**
   - Combine this email with the DMC anniversary send or schedule it for a different day.

5. **Optimize 30% Offer Placement:**
   - If additive to the 25% offer, surface the 30% off higher in the email and call it out explicitly.

### Revised Subject Line Options:
- **Alt A:** `🥳 Party supplies up to 30% off — ends Monday`
- **Alt B:** `Memorial Day hosting starts at The Party Shop`

### Revised Preheader Text Suggestions:
- **Alt A:** `Engagement parties, outdoor setups, weddings & more — shop your 25% coupon inside`
