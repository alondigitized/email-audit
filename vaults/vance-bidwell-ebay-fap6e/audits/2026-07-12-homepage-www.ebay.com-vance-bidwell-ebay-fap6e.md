# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- eBay opened directly on a card-collector pitch — a Price Guide with a QR code and images of slabbed cards and a Pokémon card being scanned. For me, that's almost eerie in how on-point it is. This is a marketplace that knows its core collector base and is actively building infrastructure to keep us from price-checking on PSA or TCGPlayer instead. The push is tool adoption, not a flash sale, which is a smarter play for someone like me.

## What's working

- **The hero headline:** "Level up with our new Price Guide" — "level up" speaks Gen X/gamer-collector and "Price Guide" is exactly the intel I want before I snipe. That's two collector nerves hit in one line.
- **The card imagery:** A PSA/BGS-style slab on the left and someone phone-scanning a Pokémon card center frame. Those two images together tell me eBay sees me — the grader and the flip-checker in the same person.
- **The QR code placement:** Smart. I'm already on mobile; I can scan that with my camera immediately. Zero friction for a high-tech user like me.
- **"Saved" in the top nav:** One tap to my watchlist. That's the first thing I do every time I open this app.
- **Search bar with camera icon:** Visual search for cards is underrated. The camera icon signals it's there without requiring me to dig.

## What's weak

- **"Learn more" CTA is under-powered:** For a new product feature, that button should say "Try the Price Guide" or "Scan a Card." "Learn more" reads like marketing copy avoiding commitment.
- **No sign-in state:** I'm presented as a guest ("Hi! Sign in"). As someone who logs in to manage 30+ watched items, seeing a generic greeting instead of my username and watch-count immediately feels impersonal.
- **No loyalty or eBay Bucks signal:** I run PayPal/eBay Bucks loops. Not seeing any rewards balance or members-only hook above the fold is a missed retention lever.
- **No price urgency anywhere:** Nothing is time-bounded. For an auction platform, that's a strange omission on the front door. Even "Ending soon in your watchlist" would hook me.
- **"More" nav catch-all:** Collectibles, Trading Cards, Pokémon — none of those category labels are visible. I have to assume they're buried under "More v." One more tap between me and my category.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA:** Change "Learn more" to "Scan a Card Now" — ties the QR action to a verb and makes the feature tangible for first-timers and returners alike.
- **Personalize the greeting:** For signed-in users, replace "Hi! Sign in" with their first name + watchlist count ("Hi Vance — 34 watched items"). That alone would triple the emotional hook for power collectors.
- **Add a "Watchlist ending soon" strip:** A thin urgency bar below the nav ("3 items in your watchlist end today") would be conversion gold for someone who snipes. It's data eBay already has.
- **Surface a Collectibles/Trading Cards shortcut in nav:** Swap "Motors" (low-relevance for a card collector) for "Trading Cards" or make "Saved" first. The existing nav prioritizes wrong for this audience.
