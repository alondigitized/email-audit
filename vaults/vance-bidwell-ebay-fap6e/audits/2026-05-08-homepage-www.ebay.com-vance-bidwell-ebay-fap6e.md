# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- eBay opened on a big purple eBay Live hero pushing fashion livestreams — "Iconic styles now streaming, level up your look with must-have fashion on Live." This is eBay leaning hard into QVC-for-Gen-Z territory. I use eBay every single day and I know exactly where I need to go — Saved listings, auction countdowns, recently-watched Rolex Daytona references and 1st-edition Charizards — and none of that is anywhere near this fold.

## What's working

- **"Saved" tab in the nav bar** — this is the single most important piece of UI for me. It's one tap to my watchlist. That earns this page real credit.
- **"My eBay" dropdown** — my bid history, purchase history, seller feedback queue: all one tap. eBay knows returning power users need that anchor.
- **Search bar is front and center** — "Search for anything" with the blue magnifying glass. I know exactly what I'm looking for and this lets me bypass the hero noise immediately.
- **eBay Live thumbnails include what looks like sneakers/kicks** — marginally more interesting than pure clothing, but still not my lane.
- **Clean, fast render** — no layout shift, no spinner, no lazy-load blanks in the hero. On mobile that matters.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is wrong for me** — "Level up your look with must-have fashion" is not a collector value prop. I'm not here to look good; I'm here to snipe a PSA 9 Charizard with 4 seconds left.
- **"Tune in" CTA is ambiguous for commerce** — it means watch a stream, not buy something. I clicked on eBay to shop, not to watch someone unbox hoodies.
- **No auction urgency signal above the fold** — eBay's killer feature is the countdown timer. There's not a single one visible. That's the hook that keeps me refreshing.
- **Young-male livestream thumbnails alienate me** — the faces in those LIVE cards are 20-somethings in streetwear. I'm 52. I see nothing of myself or my hobby here.
- **No offer with a number** — no "15% off collectibles," no "free shipping on watches over $50," no eBay Bucks multiplier. Just vibes and a streaming button.
- **No rewards/loyalty status** — I run PayPal and eBay Bucks loops. My balance/status should be visible in "My eBay" but there's nothing surfaced to remind me it's there.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the fashion Live hero with a rotating collectibles/trading-cards hero for returning users** — eBay has enough session data to know I'm a collector. A hero that says "Pokémon auctions ending soon" or "Vintage watches with bids" would stop me cold before I hit search.
- **Surface at least one live auction countdown above the fold** — even a strip: "3 auctions you're watching end in the next hour." That's the heartbeat of eBay for people like me and it's completely absent.
- **Add my eBay Bucks or loyalty balance as a persistent chip** next to "My eBay" — something like "238 pts" would remind me to chase the threshold spend.
- **Swap "Tune in" for a split CTA** — keep the Live button, but add a secondary "See open auctions" or "Browse collectibles" button so power buyers aren't dead-ended at a fashion stream.
