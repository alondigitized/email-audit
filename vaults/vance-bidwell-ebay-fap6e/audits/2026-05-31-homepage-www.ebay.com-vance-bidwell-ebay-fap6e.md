# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- This is eBay knowing exactly who I am before I even tap "Sign in." "Your next collectible awaits / Find what you've been hunting for" — they're talking directly to the slab-and-snipe crowd, and the Trading Cards tile sitting front and center seals it. The platform looks clean, minimal, and purpose-built for browse-and-hunt, not casual impulse buying. Right vibe for a marketplace that understands its power users.

## What's working

- **"Your next collectible awaits" hero headline** — lands like a sniper scope on exactly who I am. The word "hunting" in the subtext is pitch-perfect for a guy who sets auction alerts.
- **"Trading cards >" category tile** — not buried, not generic. First visible category card, with what looks like graded/slabbed cards in the image. One tap to my hunting ground.
- **"Saved" tab in the top nav** — my watchlist is the first thing I check after the logo. Having it up top without scrolling is the right call for a returning power user.
- **Search bar front and center** — experienced eBay buyers don't browse; we search. Making the search bar the dominant UI element on mobile is exactly right.
- **Clean, uncluttered mobile layout** — no popup overlays, no cookie banners eating screen real estate, no forced modals. I can get to my categories immediately.

## What's weak

- **Zero pricing or offer signals above the fold** — I don't know if there's a free-shipping promo running, a eBay Bucks bonus event, or any reason to move today versus next week. The urgency that makes me snipe is completely absent.
- **Not logged in — so no personalization** — I'm seeing a generic collectibles hero, not my recently-viewed Rolex Submariner or the PSA-10 Charizard I've been watching. The experience that eBay does best (remembered searches, watch list counts) is invisible until I authenticate.
- **"Toys >" as the second tile** — for me, this is noise. Action figures aren't watches or Pokémon. The slot would be better used for Jewelry & Watches, Trading Cards (expanded), or Sports Memorabilia for a user like me.
- **No auction urgency cues** — no "X auctions ending in the next hour" signal, no "ending soon" rail. For a platform whose primary differentiation over Amazon is live auctions, there's nothing on screen to remind me that the clock is ticking on something I want.
- **"Shop now" CTA is generic** — it reads like retail. eBay isn't retail. "See what's ending soon" or "Search collectibles" would feel more native to how I actually use this platform.

## Recommendations

- **Add an "Ending Soon" urgency strip** below the hero — even a single card showing "14 auctions ending in the next hour — Pokémon / Vintage Watches" with a countdown would pull power users like me directly into engagement mode. This is eBay's weapon; use it.
- **Swap the second category tile from "Toys" to "Jewelry & Watches"** — that's the other half of my wallet. Collectibles + Watches = the full Vance session. Toys is a broad miss for this demographic.
- **Inject a logged-in state with watchlist count** — even a "You have 4 items ending today" nudge in the "Hi! Sign in" bar would dramatically change my behavior. Show me what I'm missing by not being authenticated.
- **Surface the eBay Bucks / member pricing hook** in the hero subtext or a thin banner — something like "eBay Plus members save on every purchase" gives me a reason to log in immediately rather than browse as a guest.
