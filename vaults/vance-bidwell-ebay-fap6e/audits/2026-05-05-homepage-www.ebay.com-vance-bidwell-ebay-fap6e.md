## What's working

- **Search bar front and center:** As a power user I'm going straight to search anyway — "Search for anything" with the blue button is exactly where I expect it and it's large enough on mobile.
- **"Saved" in the nav strip:** One tap to my watch list is the one thing in this viewport that actually speaks to how I use eBay. That link earns its keep.
- **"Motors" visible in nav:** If I were here for a vehicle part or classic car watch, I've got a direct lane without digging through menus.
- **Clean, fast-loading render:** No jank, no cumulative layout shift, no placeholder images. The page came up solid.

## What's weak

- **Hero is a help-center pitch, not a marketplace hook:** "Get help exactly when you need it / From shipping to returns" — this is what you show someone who just had a bad transaction, not a returning collector ready to buy.
- **"Start here" CTA leads nowhere I want to go:** Completely misdirected. I'm not here to read FAQs; I'm here to snipe a Tudor Submariner before the auction closes.
- **Zero personalization for a signed-out returning user:** No "Welcome back, sign in to see your 32 watched items." Nothing. I'm treated like a brand-new visitor who doesn't know how eBay works.
- **Hero image is a demographic mismatch:** Young woman on a phone call. I'm a 52-year-old man checking auction countdowns. This image speaks to no one in my cohort.
- **No promotional hook whatsoever:** No deal, no coupon, no eBay Bucks multiplier, no "Ends today." eBay runs promos constantly — none of them made it above the fold.
- **Carousel controls visible but no content context:** I can see the back/forward/pause nav for a hero carousel but I have no idea what else is in the rotation. That "?" bubble is also unexplained.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the help-center hero with a returning-user hook:** Even for signed-out sessions, serve a "Sign in to see your 32 watched items — 4 ending in 2 hours" prompt. I will tap that immediately.
- **Surface an active promo above the fold:** Show the current eBay Bucks event or a sitewide coupon code in a persistent banner below the nav. This exists on desktop — bring it to mobile.
- **Swap the hero image for collectibles/trading-card lifestyle imagery:** A well-lit shot of a graded Pokémon card or a vintage watch case would stop me cold. That's what eBay's marketplace is actually famous for — lean into it.
- **Add a "Collectibles" or "Trading Cards" quick-link to the nav strip:** Right now it's eBay Live, Saved, Motors, More. Drop "Motors" down into More and surface "Collectibles" for the segment that actually drives GMV on this platform.
