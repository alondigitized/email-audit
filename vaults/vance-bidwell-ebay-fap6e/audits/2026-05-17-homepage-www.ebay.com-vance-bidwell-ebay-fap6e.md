# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is eBay — my actual hunting ground — and it opens with Anthropologie women's fashion for under $50. I've got 30-plus items on my watchlist right now, three auctions ending tonight, and eBay is showing me a denim jacket and a sundress. The bones are right — search bar front and center, My eBay accessible, Saved tab a tap away — but whoever is running the homepage carousel this morning has no idea who I am or what I'm here to do.

## What's working

- **"My eBay" dropdown + "Saved" tab** — as a power user, I'm here for my watchlist and these are one tap away, which is all I actually need
- **Search bar is dominant and centered** — "Search for anything" is eBay's real homepage for me; it's prominent, it's functional
- **"Shop now" CTA** — clear white pill button on high-contrast blue; whatever the content, the CTA execution is clean
- **"eBay Live" tab** — I actually use Live for auction sniping; seeing it in primary nav signals eBay knows power bidders exist
- **Clean render, no jank** — page loaded crisp, product cards fanned neatly, no layout shift or broken images

## What's weak

- **Anthropologie hero** — zero relevance to me; this is algorithmic or paid-partner placement that completely ignores my collecting history
- **No collectibles, no watches, no trading cards** anywhere in the above-the-fold viewport
- **No eBay Bucks / eBay Plus mention** — I actively loop Bucks on every purchase and there's no hook for it on the homepage
- **"Motors" in primary nav but no "Collectibles"** — Motors gets a named tab; my entire use case is buried under "More"
- **No urgency signals** — as an auction sniper, I live by countdowns; nothing here tells me an item I'm watching is ending soon
- **Young female model imagery** — a 25-year-old in a white blouse is not demographically aimed at a 52-year-old male collector

## Recommendations

- **Surface the personalized watchlist above the fold** — if I'm signed in, show me "3 items ending in the next hour" before any editorial banner; eBay has this data, use it
- **Rotate the carousel hero to category-relevant banners** — even a generic "Collector's Deals This Week" or "Vintage Watches" tile would land better; let paid partners like Anthropologie live further down
- **Add an eBay Bucks / rewards strip under the nav** — a one-line banner ("Your eBay Bucks: $14.20 — Use before June 1") would convert power users immediately
- **Give Collectibles a named primary-nav tab** — swap or add it alongside Motors; it's one of eBay's biggest GMV categories and currently invisible above the fold
