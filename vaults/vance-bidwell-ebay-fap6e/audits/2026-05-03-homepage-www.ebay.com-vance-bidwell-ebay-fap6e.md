## What's working

- **The watch hero image** — a white-gloved hand presenting a blue-dial Rolex-style piece immediately reads as "collector auction," not mass retail. I stopped.
- **"LIVE • 201" badge** — 201 viewers right now. That's social proof AND urgency in four characters. A snipper reads that as a hot room.
- **eBay Live as the first nav item** — they're not burying the live-auction product. It's the first tap in the nav bar, which tells me they're committing to this format.
- **"Saved" as the second nav item** — my watch list is the second tap. That's muscle memory for someone like me.
- **Search bar placement and size** — "Search for anything" is front and center, big enough to tap easily. I know what I'm hunting; I can get there fast.

## What's weak

- **I'm not signed in and eBay doesn't care** — "Hi! Sign in" sits in the top corner like a suggestion, not a priority. For a returning buyer with 30+ active watches, my personalized feed should be the homepage, not a generic live event.
- **Hero text is truncated** — "Vita Veloce Experience: Car..." cuts off. I don't know if this is a car auction that happens to have a watch, or a watch auction at a car event. That ambiguity costs a tap.
- **No Bucks balance, no rewards hook** — eBay Bucks loops are a core part of how I shop. Zero mention of it here. They're leaving money on the table.
- **No bid count or price on the hero** — I can see 201 viewers but I can't see what the current bid is or what's up for auction. Makes the live event feel opaque.
- **No category rail** — below the hero there's nothing visible. No "Watches," no "Trading Cards," no "Collectibles." Just the truncated live event card.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my watchlist** — if I'm signed in, swap the generic hero for a "X items ending soon" banner with the top 3 items from my saved searches. That's the hook that gets me scrolling.
- **Add a bid/price callout on the live hero** — even "Current bid: $1,200 — Watch now" would turn the hero from a teaser into a conversion surface.
- **Fix the truncated hero copy** — "Vita Veloce Experience: Car..." tells me nothing actionable. Either fit the full text or write a headline that works in two lines: "Live Watch Auction @ Lamborghini Houston."
- **Drop an eBay Bucks/rewards strip just below the nav** — one line: "You have $X in eBay Bucks expiring soon." That alone gets me signed in and clicking.
