## What's working

- **The search bar** is front and center, large, labeled "Search for anything" — on eBay that IS my homepage feature. First thing I'd use.
- **"Saved" tab in the nav** — that's my watchlist, and it's one tap from this screen. That matters to me more than any promo banner.
- **Concrete offer with a real deadline**: "Ends 5/7" and "10% off" are the kind of specifics I respect. No weasel words on the headline itself.
- **Clean mobile render**: no layout shift, no janky image crop, arrows and carousel dots working. Fast load implied by crispness.
- **Fine print is visible**: "Max $75 off. Select items. 4x use." — I can actually make a decision without tapping through to discover the catch.

## What's weak

- **The hero is dead wrong for me**: overlanding gear is a perfectly valid eBay category, but showing it to an unrecognized returning user wastes the most valuable real estate on the app. eBay has my entire bid/purchase history — use it.
- **Not signed in, so zero personalization**: "Hi! Sign in" means I see the same page as a brand-new visitor. A power user who has spent thousands on this platform gets no differential treatment at all.
- **"Motors" gets nav priority over "Collectibles"**: Watches and Pokémon cards are among eBay's highest-GMV collectible categories, and they don't earn a single tap in the visible nav. I have to tap "More" to find my world.
- **No trust signals**: no "Top Rated Seller" badge, no feedback count, no buyer-protection callout — nothing that reminds me why eBay beats a random Facebook Marketplace seller for a $400 vintage watch.
- **Coupon UX is a friction add**: "Get the coupon" routes me to a redemption flow I wasn't looking for. For categories I actually shop, I want a direct path to listings, not a coupon-clip step.

## Recommendations

- **Detect the signed-in state and personalize immediately**: If I'm logged in, show me my active watches count, my eBay Bucks balance, and "3 auctions ending in the next hour" — that is the hook that makes me open the app compulsively. This is table stakes.
- **Rotate the hero to a multi-category carousel and weight by browse/bid history**: Overlanding is fine for someone who shops that category; I should never see it as the lead tile. Even a dumb "you've viewed watches recently" hook would double engagement for power users.
- **Add "Collectibles" or "Trading Cards" as a visible nav tab**: One tap should get me to Pokémon cards. Right now the nav is eBay Live / Saved / Motors / More — Motors gets priority over Collectibles? Fix the tab order or surface it.
- **Surface a trust signal above the fold**: One line — "Money Back Guarantee on every purchase" or "Top Rated Seller badge" — reminds me why I'm here instead of on PWCC or StockX.
