# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- eBay is pushing its live-commerce product hard right now — the entire above-the-fold experience is a live fashion stream I have zero interest in. For a guy who's got 30+ active watches on sneaker-and-card lots and checks his eBay Bucks balance before breakfast, this landing page acts like I don't exist. It's a generic door that knows nothing about me, because it's not even bothering to check.

## What's working

- **Search bar prominence:** Huge, centered, "Search for anything" — for a power user like me who already knows exactly what I want (Seiko SKX013, PSA 9 Charizard), this is how I actually operate eBay. It's the right anchor.
- **"Saved" in the nav bar:** One tap to my watchlist. That's real. That's the one nav label that speaks to how I use this site every single day.
- **"eBay Live" tab visible:** At least they're surfacing the live product in a discoverable nav spot — I could theoretically find a watch or card livestream from there if I knew to tap it.
- **Clean render:** No layout shift, no broken images, no overlapping text. The page is technically sound.

## What's weak

- **Fashion livestream as the hero:** A young woman walking through handbags and clothing racks is the first thing I see. eBay has millions of collectible and watch listings — this is the worst possible hook for my persona.
- **No signed-in state / personalization:** I'm a repeat buyer who runs eBay Bucks loops religiously. The page doesn't know me. "Hi! Sign in" is the same greeting I'd get on day one.
- **Zero auction urgency:** No "ending soon," no bid counts, no countdown. The one thing that makes eBay eBay — the auction adrenaline — is completely invisible above the fold.
- **No collector-relevant category shortcut:** Watches, Trading Cards, Coins — none of them are surfaced in the visible nav. "Motors" gets a spot but collectibles don't.
- **No eBay Bucks / rewards hook:** I'm actively running a Bucks redemption cycle right now and there's no signal of my balance or a member-only deal.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the live-fashion hero for a rotating "Live Now" tile that respects my recently-viewed categories** — if I've been browsing Pokémon cards and vintage watches, show me a cards or horology livestream, not a fashion haul.
- **Add a "Watchlist · 32 items · 4 ending today" pill** just below the search bar for signed-in users — I'd tap that before anything else on the page.
- **Surface a collectibles/trading-cards shortcut in the nav** alongside Motors — even one tap to "Trading Cards & Comics" would pull me into my natural browsing flow.
- **Inject a Bucks/rewards banner above the fold** for logged-in loyalty users: "You have $12.50 in eBay Bucks — redeem before May 31." That's the kind of nudge that converts me same-session.
