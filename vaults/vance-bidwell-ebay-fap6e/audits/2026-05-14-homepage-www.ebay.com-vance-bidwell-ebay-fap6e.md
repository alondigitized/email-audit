# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- eBay opened on me with a car-selling pitch — "Sell your ride. Skip the hassle." That is the dead opposite of what I'm here to do. I'm here to snipe a vintage Seiko or grab a first-edition Charizard before the timer hits zero, not list a Camry. The platform looks clean and familiar, but whoever built this homepage served me a seller funnel when I'm a buyer who has 31 items on his watch list right now.

## What's working

- **Search bar, front and center** — I can bypass the whole hero disaster and search "Rolex Datejust" or "PSA 10 Charizard" in one tap. That's muscle memory and eBay always nails this.
- **"Saved" in the nav bar** — direct link to my watch list. One tap and I'm back in my workflow.
- **"My eBay" dropdown** — signals the platform knows I have an account. Familiarity keeps me from bouncing on principle.
- **Clean, no-clutter render** — no popups, no modals, no cookie banners eating the viewport. The page just loads. That earns respect.

## What's weak

- **Hero is a complete whiff for me** — "Sell your ride" is a seller-acquisition campaign. I'm a buyer. I have never sold a car on eBay and I'm not starting today.
- **Zero collectibles signal anywhere** — No watches, no Pokémon, no trading cards, no vintage anything. eBay's entire identity is built on collectibles and there is not a single nod to that above the fold.
- **No eBay Bucks / rewards balance** — I have a balance. Show it to me. That hooks me immediately and drives a purchase loop. It's not here.
- **"List now" CTA** — completely wrong action for this visit. I'm here to *buy*, not list.
- **No urgency cues anywhere** — No "ending soon," no "X watchers," nothing to trigger my auction-sniper instincts. That's half of why I open eBay in the first place.
- **Carousel dots suggest more slides** — but the default slot is wasted on a car-selling pitch. Whatever's in slides 2 and 3, it shouldn't have to compete with a hero that already lost me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a collectibles or "what's hot" surface** — show a grail watch or a BGS/PSA slab with a live-auction countdown. That's the content that triggers my dopamine loop and keeps me scrolling.
- **Surface eBay Bucks balance right in the hero or sub-nav** — something like "You have $14.72 in eBay Bucks" converts returners instantly by making the next purchase feel subsidized.
- **Add a "Trending in Collectibles / Watches / Trading Cards" rail above the fold** — or at minimum a category shortcut strip so I can hit my category in one tap instead of hunting through "More."
- **Replace the seller-acquisition hero with a buyer-retention hero for signed-in users** — eBay knows I'm logged in ("Hi! Sign in" implies session awareness). Personalize the default hero state for known buyers.
