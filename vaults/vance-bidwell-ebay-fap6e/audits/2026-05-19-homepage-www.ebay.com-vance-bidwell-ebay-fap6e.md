# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- eBay knows how to lay out a mobile homepage — I'll give them that. Clean nav, prominent search bar, one fat hero with a concrete number. Right now they're pushing Nike athletic gear at up to 60% off, which tells me the merch team is running a spring apparel blitz. Zero percent of that is why I opened this app. I'm here to check on my Pokémon first-edition lot sitting at $312 with four hours left and a graded Rolex Submariner I've been tracking for two weeks. The hero is completely beside the point.

## What's working

- **Search bar, front and center:** As a power user I'm going straight to search anyway, and it's the most prominent element after the logo. Muscle memory satisfied.
- **"Saved" in the top nav:** That tab is my actual home base. The fact it's one tap from cold open means my watch list is always reachable without scrolling.
- **"My eBay" dropdown:** Quick access to active bids, purchases, and feedback — the tools I actually use every session.
- **Concrete offer number:** "60% off" is a real claim. I respect specificity over vague "big savings" copy even when the category doesn't apply to me.
- **Clean mobile render:** No jank, no overlapping text, no broken images. Four product thumbnails load sharp. eBay's mobile team has this dialed.

## What's weak

- **Nike athletic hero is a complete miss for me:** Sport bras and running shoes. I collect graded vintage watches and Pokémon cards. This carousel is speaking to someone else entirely.
- **Zero collector or enthusiast signaling above the fold:** No "Trending in Collectibles," no "Auctions Ending Soon" ticker, nothing that speaks to the auction-hunter mindset that makes eBay different from Amazon.
- **No eBay Bucks or rewards balance visible:** I actively manage Bucks accrual. Not seeing my balance is a missed engagement hook — I'd tap the app more often if my running total greeted me.
- **"More" as the gateway to my actual categories:** Watches and Pokémon cards live behind a dropdown. That's one extra tap I shouldn't need.
- **No urgency for active bidders:** No "auctions ending in the next hour" module above the fold. That's the one thing that would make me stay instead of navigate straight to search.

## Recommendations

- **Surface an "Auctions Ending Soon" strip above the fold:** Even two or three live countdowns — any category — would hook every active bidder the moment they open the app. That's eBay's core differentiation and it's completely absent here.
- **Add a personalized "Your Saved Searches" or "Watched Items" nudge beneath the hero:** Even a single line — "3 items in your watch list are ending today" — would make me interact before I even reach search.
- **Expose "Collectibles" and "Watches" as first-class nav tabs:** Replace or supplement "Motors" with category tabs tuned to the user's browsing history. Motors is a huge vertical but it's not everyone's first tap.
- **Show eBay Bucks balance or loyalty status in the header:** One small number next to "My eBay" would make every logged-in power user feel seen and create a reason to spend before the session ends.
