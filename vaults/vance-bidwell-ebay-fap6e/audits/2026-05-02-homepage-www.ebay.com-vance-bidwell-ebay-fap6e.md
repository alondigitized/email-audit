# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Full review
Not signed in — that's the first thing I notice. The hero is pushing 10% off overlanding gear, which is trucks and camping equipment. I have literally zero interest in that. I'm here to check my watch alerts and see if any PSA 9 Charizards moved overnight, and eBay's leading with a coupon for people who strap tents to their Jeeps.

The nav bar actually has the stuff I care about buried in there — "Saved" is right up top, that's useful. "Motors" is there too, though I'm looking at vintage Seikos, not F-150s. "eBay Live" I've poked at a few times for trading card drops. But none of that matters because the entire viewport below the nav is swallowed by this overlanding promotion. Max $75 off, ends 5/7, select items, four-time use limit — I read the fine print automatically and none of it applies to me.

The carousel dots at the bottom tell me there are more slides, but I'm not swiping through three promotions hoping one is relevant. When I open eBay I want to see my watchlist activity — did anything end, did I get outbid, are there new listings matching my saved searches? Instead I get trucks in the desert.

To eBay's credit, the search bar is front and center and the "Saved" tab is genuinely one tap away. But a platform that knows I've got 30+ items watched and snipe history going back a decade should be serving me personalized signals the second I land, not a generic coupon carousel for the overlanding community.

**3/10**
