# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- This is home turf — I've been on eBay since dial-up, and the second I see that multicolor logo I'm in buying mode. Right now eBay is pushing eBay Live hard, and honestly the watch thumbnail peeking out of that livestream panel caught my eye before anything else did. The yellow sports-memorabilia split feels like it's fighting for space with the purple eBay Live panel, and the hero is a carousel I already want to scroll past.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **eBay Live panel with the watch thumbnail** — that dark-dial dive watch visible in the live stream preview is exactly the kind of thing that stops my thumb. The "Score rare finds" copy is on point for a collector mindset.
- **"Stream now" CTA** — clear, one-word action. I know what I'm getting into.
- **"Saved" tab in the nav bar** — I live in that tab. The fact that it's a top-level nav item and not buried in a dropdown tells me they understand how power users actually browse.
- **eBay Live badge + "LIVE" indicators on the stream thumbnails** — the red LIVE chips create real urgency without a countdown timer. That's the right kind of scarcity for a sniping mindset.
- **"Search for anything" bar front and center** — I almost never browse; I search for exact references. The bar is right where it needs to be.

## What's weak

- **The yellow sports-memorabilia panel** — Lakers jersey and a football helmet? That's not my world. I understand it's a carousel, but leading with sports merch before I've even logged in is a miss for a collector like me. Feels like a generic "popular category" filler.
- **Left-panel hero copy is truncated** — "as a story" and "rt the next chapter" are cropped so badly I can't read the headline. Whatever story eBay is trying to tell, it's not landing because the text is cut off at the edge of the panel.
- **Not signed in** — "Hi! Sign in" at the top means eBay has zero personalization working for me here. My watch alerts, my saved searches, my feedback history — all of it invisible. A returning power user should never see a generic homepage.
- **Carousel with four dots** — I can see there are more panels, but with no indication of what's in them, I'm not motivated to swipe. The panels I can see don't establish a strong enough reason to explore.
- **No Pokémon or TCG signal anywhere** — not a single hint that this is a destination for graded cards or singles. That's real money left on the table for the collector segment.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero on login** — if I'm signed in, swap the sports panel for a "Recently watched" or "Ending soon in your saved searches" module. I have 30+ items on my watch list; show me the ones expiring in the next two hours.
- **Surface the watch thumbnail bigger** — that dive watch in the eBay Live preview is doing more work than the copy. Give it a dedicated card or expand the live stream preview so the item is identifiable at a glance.
- **Fix the hero copy crop** — the left yellow panel's headline is cut off. Either reduce font size or reflow the layout so the full line is readable without tapping through.
- **Add a "Trading Cards / Pokémon" quick-access chip** below the search bar alongside Motors and eBay Live — TCG is one of eBay's highest-volume collectibles verticals and it's completely invisible above the fold.
