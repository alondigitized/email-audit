# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- I've been on eBay nearly every day for twenty years, and this is the first time their homepage has hit me with a celebrity charity gig before showing me a single watch listing or Pokémon pack. It looks like a crowdfunding pitch crossed with a morning-show segment — not a marketplace for serious collectors. I get it, it's a cause event, but they've handed the entire above-the-fold real estate to a pre-qualification auction that probably 98% of their core buyers can't enter.

## What's working

- **"My eBay" and "Saved" in the nav bar** — I can go straight to my 30+ watch-list items without hunting. That's the one thing saving this page for me.
- **Single CTA button ("Join the auction")** — clean, high-contrast, no ambiguity. I know exactly what they want me to do.
- **Time-stamp "Live 5/7-5/14"** — I respect a hard deadline. That's the language of auctions, and it's how I think. If this were in my category I'd already be setting a sniping timer.
- **Search bar front and center** — "Search for anything" with the blue button is still my fastest path to a Charizard holo or a Seiko SKX. It's always been eBay's ace.

## What's weak

- **Zero collectibles/trading-cards/watches surface area** — the entire hero is a charity experience auction. I have to go to "More ▼" just to find my categories. That's burying the lead for the platform's most obsessive buyers.
- **"Must prequalify" fine print** — they built hype and then immediately told me I might not qualify. Classic bait-and-switch energy, even if unintentional.
- **No eBay Bucks / eBay Money balance visible** — I run Bucks loops religiously. Showing me my balance or a "You have $X Bucks" nudge would have me in the auction funnel instantly.
- **Celebrity imagery is tone-deaf for my use case** — I don't know who two of the three people in those portraits are, and none of them are holding a vintage Rolex or a first-edition Base Set box.
- **No category shortcuts below the hero** — on desktop there are quick-links to Electronics, Collectibles, Fashion. On mobile I'm getting none of that above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent eBay Bucks / account balance chip in the header** — even just "Hi Vance · $14.22 Bucks" would pull returning power-users straight into a purchase loop before they hit the hero.
- **Swap the celebrity hero for a rotating collector spotlight** — "Top Pokémon deals ending today" or "Grail watches under $500" with a live countdown. Same auction urgency, directly relevant to the people who live on this platform.
- **Surface a category shortcut strip below the search bar** — Collectibles | Watches | Trading Cards | Motors — one tap to my world, no "More ▼" hunting required.
- **Rewrite the fine print: "Live 5/7–5/14 · Pre-qualification required — apply now"** and make that a tappable link. Either commit to the pre-qual flow or pull the CTA. Don't let "Must prequalify" just sit there killing intent.
