# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- eBay is playing to its core mechanic here — live flash sales, $1 starts, timed events. As someone who wakes up early to snipe Pokémon first-edition lots and has eBay's auction countdown burned into his nervous system, "Big savings are about to drop" with a 7/23-7/24 window hits differently than a generic retail homepage. That said, this is a broadcast pitch, not a returning-user experience — I see zero personalization, no eBay Bucks balance, no "you're watching 32 items" hook to pull me back in.

## What's working

- **"$1 starts" and "flash sales" in the hero subhead** — this is auction-sniper vocabulary. I read those two words and I'm already calculating what category I want to park myself in for eBay Live.
- **"RSVP now" CTA** — clear, high-contrast white button on a dark background. One tap. Exactly how this should work on mobile.
- **The 7/23–7/24 date** — timed events are my oxygen. I already know I'll be setting a reminder.
- **"Saved" tab in the top nav** — my watchlist is the first place I go. Putting it in the persistent nav bar is smart. That's a power-user signal eBay got right.
- **"48 Hours of Deals" module visible below the hero** — stacked urgency. Flash sale + timed deal hub = I'm not leaving.

## What's weak

- **Zero personalization** — I have 30+ saved searches and an active watchlist. This homepage treats me like a brand-new visitor. Where's "resume watching," or even "Hi Vance, you have 4 auctions ending soon"?
- **No eBay Bucks / rewards visibility** — I run eBay Bucks loops. Showing me my balance above the fold would have pulled me straight to checkout mentality. It's not here.
- **The hero thumbnail strip is unreadable at this viewport** — I can see there are product thumbnails below the eBay Live banner, but I can't make out categories. Watches? Cards? Impossible to tell from this screenshot.
- **No category shortcut to Trading Cards or Watches** — the nav shows eBay Live, Saved, Motors, and More. My two primary hunting grounds aren't surfaced. "More" is a dead end for impulse taps.
- **Seller reputation and feedback signals absent** — this is where eBay beats every marketplace for me, and there's none of it visible above the fold. Even a "Top Rated Sellers" badge would reassure me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Watching X items — Y ending soon" pill** to the top bar for signed-in users, right next to "My eBay." I check that count every time I open the app; put it in my face immediately.
- **Surface eBay Bucks balance (or equivalent rewards currency) in the header** — even a "$12.40 in rewards" line next to My eBay changes my purchase calculus instantly.
- **Swap the thumbnail carousel below the hero for personalized categories** — if eBay knows I search "vintage Seiko," "PSA graded Pokemon," and "horology," show me those. The generic live-feed thumbnails tell me nothing.
- **Add "Collectibles & Trading Cards" and "Watches" as explicit nav tabs** alongside Motors — those are two of eBay's top collector categories and they're buried under "More."
