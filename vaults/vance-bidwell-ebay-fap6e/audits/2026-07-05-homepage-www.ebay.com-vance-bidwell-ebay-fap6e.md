# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- eBay's loading up a Carhartt workwear sale on a bright orange hero — durable tees, a hoodie, some shorts. The "saves on durable styles that just get better with age" line is a nice nod to my demographic, but this is absolutely not collector-first territory. No auctions, no watchlist callout, no collectibles category in sight above the fold. Feels like eBay is trying to be a discount retail destination today, not the auction house I know and love.

## What's working

- **"Durable styles that just get better with age" sub-copy** — that line is doing work. Whoever wrote it knew they were talking to someone 40+. I respect it, even if the product isn't my thing.
- **"Up to 45% off" headline offer** — percentage is specific and above the fold, no hunting required.
- **Single clean CTA ("Shop now")** — no competing buttons, no confusion. Tappable pill on a busy hero, good contrast.
- **"Saved" in the top nav** — quick access to my watchlist is right there. That's genuinely useful to me as someone sitting on 30+ saved searches.
- **Search bar front-and-center with camera icon** — visual search is exactly how I'd hunt down a specific Rolex dial variant or card grade. That camera icon is legitimately valuable.

## What's weak

- **Zero collectibles surface area above the fold** — no Pokémon cards, no vintage watches, no trading cards, no auction-format nudge. I'm eBay's core power user and I'm invisible on my own homepage.
- **No watchlist / active auction count in the header** — "My eBay" shows nothing about how many items I'm watching or if any auctions are ending soon. That's the one thing that would make me stop and check right now.
- **No eBay Live or auction-ending urgency** — "eBay Live" is in the nav but it reads like a dead label; there's no "auction ending in 4:23" hook or anything that triggers my sniper instincts.
- **Carousel dots with no content preview** — there are three more slides I can't see, with zero indication of what's in them. I might tap through, but there's no reason to.
- **The pause button on the carousel** — tiny "II" icon, easy to miss. Minor, but this carousel controls UI feels like a 2018 pattern.
- **Sign-in prompt is tiny** — "Hi! Sign in" at top-left is easy to miss. If I'm not signed in, I get none of my personalization, and eBay isn't doing much to motivate me to log in immediately.

## Recommendations

- **Surface an active auction count or watchlist countdown in the sticky header** — even a badge on "My eBay" showing "3 auctions ending today" would be enough to hook a power user like me before I even reach the hero.
- **Swap the Carhartt hero for a collectibles or eBay Live carousel slide** — or at minimum make the collectibles slide first by personalizing hero order to account activity. I've bought watches and cards; that data exists.
- **Add "Trending in Collectibles" or "Ending Soon" rails** — a horizontally scrolling row of hot-ending auctions below the hero (vintage watches, graded cards, sneakers) would give me a reason to stay and scroll.
- **Make the "Saved" nav tab show a count badge** — "Saved (34)" would be an immediate anchor that pulls me back into the platform loop I'm already in.
