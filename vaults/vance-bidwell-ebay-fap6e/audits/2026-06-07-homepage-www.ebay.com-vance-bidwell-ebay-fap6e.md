# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- eBay knows how to keep the chrome clean — the nav is snappy, the search bar is front and center, and I see my Saved tab without hunting. But the moment my eyes hit that green hero, I'm already swiping past it: "Score Nike at up to 60% off" is targeting somebody who buys gym shorts, not a guy refreshing a 1999 Pokémon Booster Box listing six seconds before it closes. This is a marketplace homepage defaulting to mass-appeal merch instead of surfacing what eBay actually does better than anyone — collector-grade, one-of-a-kind inventory with real time pressure.

## What's working

- **Saved tab in primary nav** — as someone watching 30+ items at any given moment, having that one tap away in the top bar is the single most relevant feature on this whole screen for me
- **"Search for anything" with camera icon** — the visual search tool is legitimately useful for identifying mystery lots and variant cards; glad it's prominent
- **"My eBay" in the header** — my account hub is accessible without hunting, which matters when I'm checking feedback or bid history between meetings
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — no pop-over sign-in nag, no dark-pattern interrupts; eBay lets me get to work, which I respect
- **"up to 60% off" is a real number** — more credible than "huge savings," even if the category is irrelevant to me

## What's weak

- **Nike athletic hero is completely off-target for collector personas** — there is zero acknowledgment that eBay is the world's largest marketplace for vintage watches, sealed Pokémon product, and graded cards; that story goes untold
- **No auction urgency signal above the fold** — eBay's real superpower is live auctions with ticking countdowns; none of that energy is visible here
- **No personalization hook** — no "recently viewed," no "auctions ending soon in your categories," nothing that says eBay knows I've been here a thousand times before
- **"Saved" tab is present but dormant** — it doesn't show a badge count or tease "3 saved items ending in 2 hours," which would immediately grab me
- **No eBay Bucks / loyalty indicator** — I run PayPal/eBay Bucks loops deliberately; if there's a balance or multiplier active, I'd want to see it without going two taps deep
- **Carousel with 4 dots but zero context on what else is there** — I have no reason to swipe since Nike is already a miss

## Recommendations

- **Replace or rotate the hero to surface collector categories** — a split hero with "Vintage Watches" and "Pokémon & Trading Cards" alongside the Nike deal costs nothing and triples relevance for the platform's highest-AOV segments
- **Add a "Watchlist ending soon" nudge to the Saved tab** — badge it with a count and a "2 ending in 1 hr" teaser; that single change would pull power users from the hero directly into the funnel eBay actually wins
- **Surface eBay Bucks balance or active multiplier at the top of the hero** — a "$14.72 in eBay Bucks available" chip next to My eBay would trigger a spending loop immediately for users who optimize rewards the way I do
- **Add an auction countdown strip below the hero** — a slim horizontal rail showing "Ending in under 1 hour · Watches · Cards · Coins" with live timers does more for urgency than any percentage-off banner
