# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened eBay expecting to see my watchlist or at least a hook toward the categories I live in — vintage horology, Pokémon cards, maybe some graded sports cards — and instead I'm staring at a young woman elbow-deep in an engine bay. eBay is clearly pushing their auto-parts Guaranteed Fit program hard right now, which makes sense as a category they're trying to own, but for me it's a complete cold start. This reads like a general-marketplace homepage that happens to be running an auto campaign, not a collector's hub.

## What's working

- **"My eBay" nav link** — one tap to my watchlist. That's the only thing keeping me from bouncing immediately.
- **"Saved" shortcut in the nav bar** — for someone running 30+ active watch-list items, having that one tap away is quietly excellent UX.
- **eBay Guaranteed Fit badge + "your money back" line** — for auto-parts buyers this is a strong trust signal. The copy is tight, the checkmark reads at a glance.
- **Search bar is front and center** — I know exactly what I'm searching for. Giant search field is the right call for a power user like me.
- **No modal takeover or cookie-consent wall** — nothing forcing me to act before I can see anything. Respect.

## What's weak

- **Hero is completely irrelevant to me** — vintage watches and Pokémon cards are among eBay's highest-velocity collector categories, and there's zero signal toward either.
- **No returning-user hook** — I've spent tens of thousands on eBay over the years. Show me my Bucks balance, my recent bids, something that says "welcome back, Vance." Instead I get treated like a first-time visitor.
- **No offer with teeth** — there's no coupon, no % off, no eBay Bucks multiplier, no sale event. The only "offer" is a return guarantee, which is table stakes.
- **Zero collector-category entry point above the fold** — Trading Cards, Watches, Collectibles are flagship eBay categories. None visible without scrolling or searching.
- **Carousel with no visible count** — I know there are more slides (there are prev/next arrows) but I have no idea how many or what's in them. Makes the other promotions invisible.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for signed-in returning buyers** — if I'm logged in (or even cookie-identified), swap the auto-parts hero for a watchlist-activity hook: "3 auctions ending in under an hour" or "New listings in Vintage Watches." This is table stakes for a marketplace in 2026.
- **Surface eBay Bucks / rewards balance in the top bar** — one number next to "My eBay" changes the whole emotional tone. I'm here to spend, remind me what I've earned.
- **Add a collector-category shortcut strip below the nav** — Trading Cards · Watches · Coins · Comics · Vintage · Motors. One row of chips. Lets every buyer type self-sort in under a second.
- **Put a time-bounded offer on screen** — a flash coupon, a limited multiplier event, anything with a clock attached. I'm a sniper; urgency works on me. Right now there's nothing to pull the trigger on.
