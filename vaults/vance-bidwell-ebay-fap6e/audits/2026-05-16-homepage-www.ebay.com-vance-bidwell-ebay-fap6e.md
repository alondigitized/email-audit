# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- eBay's serving me overlanding trucks on the hero — which, fine, gear is gear, but that's not why I'm here. I came to check my watch alerts and snipe a graded PSA 10 Charizard before someone else does. The platform knows I'm a returning user ("Hi! Sign in" + "My eBay"), but it's burning its above-the-fold real estate on a camping trip I'm not taking.

## What's working

- **"Saved" tab in the top nav** — that's a direct link to my 30-item watchlist. One tap. That's the most useful thing on this screen for me.
- **"My eBay ▼" link** — confirms the platform recognizes me as a returning user, even if it's pushing me to sign in explicitly.
- **Orange hero with prominent search bar** — I know how to use eBay; the search bar does the heavy lifting and it's front and center.
- **Carousel format** — four dots visible below the hero; I can swipe to find something more relevant without digging through a cluttered nav.

## What's weak

- **Overlanding hero is a miss for me** — I have zero alerts set for car parts or camping supplies. This is wasted prime real estate.
- **Left-edge text clipping is a real bug** — "verlanding gear for y terrain" and "op now" look broken on mobile. If I didn't already know this platform, I'd question the site's quality.
- **No active pricing signal on the hero** — "Shop now" for what, at what price? There's no hook. The right-side panel teases "Up" (probably "Up to X% off") but it's half off-screen and unreadable.
- **Navigation tabs are too general for a power user** — "eBay Live," "Saved," "Motors," "More ▼" — collectibles, trading cards, and watches are all buried under "More." That's two taps minimum.
- **No seller trust signals** — no feedback scores, no "X million listings" reassurance, nothing to reinforce why I should trust this session's results.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a personalized returning-user module** — show me my recently viewed auctions, active bids, or "ending soon" watched items. I know eBay sells overlanding gear; I don't need to be told.
- **Fix the mobile left-edge text clipping** — "Overlanding gear for every terrain" and "Shop now" should be fully legible. This is a straightforward viewport padding fix and it's embarrassing on a flagship hero.
- **Add a "Trending in Collectibles" or "Ending Soon in Your Saved Searches" rail above the fold** — even one module tuned to my saved searches would increase my engagement dramatically.
- **Surface the reward/loyalty balance** — if I have eBay Bucks or cashback pending, show me the number. That's a conversion trigger for a buyer running PayPal loops.
