# Homepage snapshot · www.ebay.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- I've been on eBay since the dial-up era and I know what I'm looking for the second I open the app — and this isn't it. Right now eBay's pushing "Refurbished Tech" hard with an orange hero and a grid of power tools, kitchen appliances, and Dysons. Nothing wrong with the execution, but I'm here for a vintage Seiko or a first-edition Charizard, not a refurb KitchenAid. The platform I rely on for auction sniping is basically showing me a Best Buy clearance aisle.

## What's working

- **"Saved" nav tab** — that's my watch list. One tap from the hero to the 30+ items I'm tracking. That's the single most useful piece of real estate on this screen for me.
- **"My eBay" dropdown** — top-right, always there, means I'm never more than a tap from my active bids and purchase history.
- **Concrete discount language** — "Up to 50% off" with a warranty detail is honest, specific, and doesn't bury the lead. I respect copy that doesn't waste my time.
- **Search bar front and center** — "Search for anything" with an immediate blue button. When the homepage doesn't serve me, I go straight to search, and the bar is right there.
- **Orange hero with clear CTA** — the visual contrast is sharp. "Shop for less" button reads instantly, no hunting.

## What's weak

- **Hero is completely off-target for collectors** — "eBay Refurbished" is not why I'm on this platform. The product grid behind the hero shows a Dyson, a saw, a chair. That's eBay for someone else, not me.
- **No auction-specific hooks above fold** — I snipe auctions. Ending-soon listings, hot bids, auction countdown timers — none of that is visible. The most compelling thing eBay has over every other platform is live auction tension, and the homepage buries it.
- **No Pokémon or Collectibles category in the visible nav** — "eBay Live," "Saved," "Motors," "More" — Collectibles is hidden behind "More." That's a click tax on my most frequent path.
- **Not signed in** — "Hi! Sign in" means personalization is zeroed out. No "recently viewed," no recommended searches based on my history. All the signal eBay has on me is invisible.
- **Zero trust signals for high-value transactions** — I'm buying a watch that might run $400-$2,000. No seller-feedback badge, no money-back guarantee callout, no "authenticity guaranteed" for watches. Those matter to me at that price point.

## Recommendations

- **Surface an "Ending Soon" or "Hot Auctions" strip above the fold** — even a horizontal scroll rail of 4-5 auction items with countdown timers would immediately signal that eBay is still the auction platform, not just another retail store.
- **Add Collectibles and Trading Cards to the visible nav bar** — swap or expand "More" to expose those categories directly; "Pokémon," "Trading Cards," or "Collectibles" as a named tab is one tap vs. two.
- **Add an Authenticity Guaranteed badge or watch-specific trust callout** — "Watches over $500 verified authentic" in the hero strip or a persistent banner would lock in high-intent buyers like me before we even search.
- **Personalize the hero for returning users** — if I'm logged in, replace the generic Refurbished hero with "3 watched auctions ending today" or "New listings in Vintage Watches." eBay has the data; use it.
