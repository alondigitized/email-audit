# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, clean layout, great shoe taste — three really pretty women's styles laid out on a green wall, and the "SERVING CLEAN & CLASSIC" headline is giving exactly the vibe I came for. But a cookie consent modal eating half my screen the second I land? Rude. And the thing that should be front-and-center for me — that Zappos ships overnight and takes everything back, no questions — is completely invisible. This is a fashion-forward shoe brand playing dress-up when it should be selling me on the service that makes it irreplaceable.

## What's working

- **The hero imagery** — three curated women's styles (woven raffia sneaker, clean white low-top, silver-studded strappy sandal) laid flat on a green textured background. This is Pinterest-board-worthy and I actually want to tap all three.
- **"SERVING CLEAN & CLASSIC" headline** — it's punchy, it's confident, it matches the aesthetic of the shoes below it. I read it and immediately felt like this page was for me.
- **Top banner with "Shop Clogs"** — quick, low-friction entry point to a category. Slip-ons are a whole mood and I appreciate a fast lane.
- **Clean mobile layout** — Zappos logo, search icon, bag, hamburger. Nothing is fighting each other for attention (beneath the modal).

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero CTA** — I cannot see if there's a primary button under that modal. I'm landing on a page and immediately being asked to manage my data preferences. It's a full-screen interrupt that erases the momentum of the hero.
- **Zero mention of free returns or free shipping** — this is Zappos's entire superpower for someone like me who buys 3 sizes and returns 2. It should be in the first sentence of every touchpoint. It's not here at all.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no "4.8 stars on 10k reviews," no badges, no "as seen in." I'm a stranger to the site in this moment and nothing is reassuring me I'm in the right place.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'm a VIP customer and there's nothing acknowledging that or dangling my rewards balance at me. A returning-customer hook here would convert me instantly.
- **"SERVING CLEAN & CLASSIC" is vague as a call-to-action** — I love the vibe but there's no button attached to it that I can see. Vibes don't drive taps.

## Recommendations

- **Surface free returns + free overnight shipping in the hero or a persistent strip** — "Free overnight shipping. Free returns. No excuses needed." That sentence is worth more than any design asset on this page for someone like me. Put it directly under the headline.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default and move to a less intrusive bottom sheet** — or at minimum, don't let it obscure the hero. Right now the first tap on this page is forced to be cookie management, not product.
- **Add a loyalty/VIP callout above the fold** — even a one-liner like "VIP members: your perks are waiting →" turns a browse into a mission.
- **Attach a CTA button to the hero** — "Shop Clean Styles" or "Shop the Edit" directly under the headline, visible before any scroll, so the mood translates into movement.
