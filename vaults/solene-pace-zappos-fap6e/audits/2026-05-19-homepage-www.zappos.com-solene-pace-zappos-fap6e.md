# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I opened Zappos expecting to be greeted like the loyal, size-buying-in-triplicate customer I am, and instead I'm watching a teenage boy kick a soccer ball in cleats. This feels like I accidentally landed on a sports gear site curated for a high school coach. The brand I know as "free returns, every shoe you can dream of" is leading with a soccer campaign that has zero relevance to me.

## What's working

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — large, high-contrast black button, no confusion about what to tap. Zappos at least knows how to put a button on a page.
- **Visual cleanliness** — no overlapping elements, no layout chaos. The yellow panel over the field photo is bold and readable on a phone screen.
- **Seasonal relevance** — launching a Soccer Season push in May is seasonally coherent. Points for not still running a Valentine's Day banner.
- **App download banner** — "Faster checkout & a curated shopping experience at your fingertips" is a reasonable conversion hook for a high-tech user like me, though it's a little easy to dismiss.

## What's weak

- **Completely wrong persona targeting** — I am a 39-year-old woman who buys dress sandals in three widths and returns two of them. A teenage male soccer player kicking a ball is not my mirror.
- **Zero free-returns callout** — Free returns are literally Zappos's most powerful weapon for someone like me. It's not anywhere above the fold. That's a cardinal sin.
- **No loyalty or VIP hook** — I'm presumably a known customer with purchase history. Nothing acknowledges that. No rewards balance, no "welcome back," nothing.
- **"Zappos / In Motion" nav strip** — three identical chips that all say the same thing and give me no information about what I'd actually find. Wasted real estate that could show Women's, New Arrivals, or Sale.
- **No price signal whatsoever** — not a discount, not a threshold, not a "free shipping on orders over X." Zappos is not a luxury brand, so why is it acting like price is irrelevant?

## Recommendations

- **Swap the soccer hero for a personalized or gender-inclusive hero** — even a lifestyle shot of someone actually wearing interesting shoes (not playing a sport) would cast a wider net and stop alienating half the customer base immediately.
- **Put the free returns promise above the fold** — trade the app download banner for "Free returns. Always." That one line is what separates Zappos from every other online shoe retailer and it should be shouting.
- **Surface a concrete offer in the hero block** — even "Free overnight shipping for VIP members" or "Up to 40% off spring styles" gives me a reason to scroll. Right now the CTA is asking me to "shop" a category I don't care about.
- **Make the nav strip actually navigable** — replace the three "Zappos / In Motion" placeholder chips with real category pills (Women's, New Arrivals, Sale) so I can get where I'm going in one tap.
