# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- The vibe here is curated occasion-dressing editorial — Zappos is clearly pushing a spring wedding-season moment with the Guest Edit and RSVP-Ready angle. I can see the brand knows what month it is and knows its audience, which I appreciate. But I'm already clocking what's missing: no VIP mention, no free returns badge, none of the things that actually made me fall in love with Zappos in the first place.

## What's working

- **The RSVP-Ready / Guest Edit hero**: I panic-buy event shoes the Thursday before a Saturday wedding — this framing catches me at exactly the right moment. It's not "Women's Occasion" buried in a nav dropdown, it's a whole editorial moment with copy that earns the click.
- **20% off HOKA banner**: HOKA is a comfort brand I actually care about, and a specific product at a specific discount is actionable. I tapped "Shop Now" in my head before I finished reading the sentence.
- **Dual gender CTAs**: "SHOP WOMEN'S" and "SHOP MEN'S" — clean split, no overthinking, no dropdown required. I know exactly where I'm going.
- **Zero modal spam above the fold**: No newsletter interstitial, no app download prompt, no cookie wall obscuring the hero. The page just lets me look at shoes. Genuinely refreshing.

## What's weak

- **No free returns callout above the fold**: This is the reason I use Zappos instead of every other shoe site. I buy three sizes and return two. Not seeing "free returns" anywhere in my first screen is a missed hug from a brand I trust.
- **No VIP / loyalty hook**: I'm a Zappos VIP. Free overnight shipping is the whole point. The homepage treats me like a cold stranger who wandered in from Google. No rewards balance, no member pricing, nothing.
- **No people in the hero**: Those shoes are stunning as objects but I'm buying them to wear, not display. A flat-lay tells me the style; a real person at a garden party tells me the vibe. I want the vibe.
- **No trust signals anywhere visible**: No star ratings on featured shoes, no "free returns always" badge, no social proof. Zappos has built a cult following on customer service and returns — none of that DNA shows up here.
- **Redundant "Shop All" text link**: There's a text link sitting right above the two big CTA buttons, creating three ways to proceed instead of two. Minor friction but it dilutes the decisiveness of the layout.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent returns strip below the nav**: One line — "Free overnight shipping + free returns, always" — would do more conversion work than any visual element on this page for buyers like me. It's the thing that separates Zappos from everyone else.
- **Personalize the promo banner for logged-in VIPs**: Replace the generic discount strip with "Hey Solene — your VIP overnight shipping is ready to use." I gave you my email and my credit card history. Recognize me.
- **Inject one lifestyle shot into the hero flat-lay**: Swap one of the product images for a 35–45-year-old woman walking into a cocktail hour. Let me see myself in the scene. The shoes are gorgeous; put them on a foot.
- **Add a micro-trust line under the CTA buttons**: "Free returns. Free overnight shipping. No asterisks." — three short lines, right under "SHOP WOMEN'S." The buyer I am makes decisions partly based on exit ease; put it where I'm already looking.
