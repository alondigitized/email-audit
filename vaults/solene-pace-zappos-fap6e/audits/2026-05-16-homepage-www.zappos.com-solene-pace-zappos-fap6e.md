# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Zappos is going full summer mode, and honestly, the vibe is right — strappy block heels, floral skirt, warm-grass lifestyle energy. This is clearly a women's footwear brand that knows summer is the shoe season. But above the fold it's one hero and a "SHOP NOW" — no free returns callout, no overnight shipping badge, nothing that reminds me why I specifically choose Zappos over everywhere else that also sells sandals.

## What's working

- **"SUMMER STARTS HERE" headline** — short, confident, seasonally on-point. Doesn't oversell.
- **The sandal lifestyle shot** — block heel, strappy, paired with a boho floral skirt on grass. That's exactly the aesthetic I'm shopping for heading into summer. I want to know what shoe that is immediately.
- **"Pairs for long days, warm nights & everything in between"** — this subtext is doing real work. It's basically "you need multiple pairs," which is me. I own 40 pairs and I'm nodding.
- **Yellow "SHOP NOW" button** — high contrast against the blue gradient background, impossible to miss on mobile.
- **App-promo banner** — as a high-tech shopper, "faster checkout" at the very top actually registers. I'm mildly interested.

## What's weak

- **Zero mention of free returns or free shipping above the fold** — this is Zappos' entire competitive identity for someone like me. I choose Zappos *specifically* because I can order three sizes and ship two back for free. That's invisible here.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the laziest CTA possible** — shop what? Women's sandals? Summer heels? Click into the sandal category directly. I want to be one tap from those shoes in the hero.
- **No loyalty hook visible** — I'm a repeat customer. Zappos VIP is real. My overnight shipping perk should be screaming at me from the header or somewhere above the fold.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — no category strips, no quick-access tabs. For a mobile shopping session, I have to tap the menu, wait, then find Women's Shoes. That's friction.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "4.8 stars from 2M customers," nothing that reminds me I'm on solid ground. The hero is beautiful but anonymous.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent free-returns/overnight-shipping strip** below the nav — one line, always visible: "Free overnight shipping. Free returns. Always." That's the whole brand promise and it's absent.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Summer Sandals"** — link it directly to the women's sandal category matching the hero imagery. Get me to the product faster.
- **Surface the VIP/loyalty status** in the nav bar or hero — even a small "VIP Member" badge next to the cart icon tells me this page knows me.
- **Add a category shortcut strip** below the hero (Women's / Men's / Kids' / Brands) — standard mobile e-comm UX that reduces the hamburger-menu tax.
