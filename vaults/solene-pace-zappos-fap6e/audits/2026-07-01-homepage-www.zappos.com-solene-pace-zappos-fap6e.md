# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Zappos opened with the HOKA Clifton 11 launch front and center, and honestly — I'm already reaching for my cart. This is a comfort-first sneaker brand landing on a free-returns platform, which is basically my entire personality. But Zappos is leaving money on the table: their signature flex (free returns, fast shipping) is nowhere on this screen, which is the one thing that makes me choose them over anyone else.

## What's working

- **"JUST DROPPED" rail with the HOKA Clifton 11 hero** — a new-drop announcement hits me exactly right. I don't browse; I respond to launches.
- **"UP TO 30% OFF" promo banner on Brooks** — specific discount, visible immediately, with a right-rail shoe image. Easy to understand at a glance.
- **"Shop Women's" button** — one tap to my category. No guessing. Zappos doesn't make me figure out where I fit.
- **Top announcement bar** — "ultra-sleek updates" is the kind of copy that makes me curious enough to tap before I've even read the rest of the page.
- **The HOKA pile image** — multiple colorways stacked together reads as abundance. As someone who buys 3 sizes anyway, seeing multiple options laid out like that is visually appealing.

## What's weak

- **Zero mention of free returns above the fold** — this is Zappos' whole identity and my entire reason for having a Zappos bookmark. That signal should be in the header or right below the nav, not buried wherever it is off-screen.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I have VIP, but new or lapsed shoppers don't know if shipping is free until they're deep in checkout. That's a missed conversion lever.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I have no idea if I have points sitting there. A simple "You have 240 points — use them today" would've made me feel recognized.
- **The 30% off banner is for Brooks, but the hero is HOKA** — the two promos don't connect. It creates a slight visual mismatch where neither one feels like the clear campaign.
- **CTA on the HOKA hero is cut off** — "Featuring enhanced comfort & a refined…" trails off at the bottom of the screenshot. There's no CTA button visible attached to the HOKA hero itself, which is the most prime real estate on the page.

## Recommendations

- **Add a free-returns badge to the nav bar or directly below it** — even a one-liner like "Free returns, always" anchored under the Zappos logo pays the trust dividend immediately and it costs one line of CSS.
- **Pin a loyalty/rewards balance callout in the header** — "VIP: 340 points" or "Your rewards balance" visible before scroll keeps me engaged and feeling like a known customer rather than a stranger.
- **Give the HOKA hero a visible "Shop Now" button inside the hero frame** — right now the CTA lives only in the thin black top bar; the hero image itself has no button. A high-contrast button overlaid on or directly under the HOKA image converts the launch moment.
- **Unify the promo story** — either lead with the 30% off drop or lead with the HOKA launch; trying to do both above the fold dilutes both messages. If it's HOKA launch week, own it fully.
