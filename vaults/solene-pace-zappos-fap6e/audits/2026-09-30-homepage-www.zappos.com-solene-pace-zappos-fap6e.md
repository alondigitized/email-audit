# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a clean, editorial shoe retailer, and today it's pushing "The Loafer Shop" with a lifestyle photo of two people on stoop steps. It's pretty, but for a VIP who shops Zappos mainly for free overnight shipping and free returns, I can't see a single reason to stay. There's no shipping promise, no returns line and no member callout. A cookie panel is also covering the next module.

## What's working

- **The hero photo:** it's stylish, with the brown suede loafer against the red-orange socks. It sells the mood well.
- **"SHOP WOMEN'S / SHOP MEN'S" buttons:** the two big black buttons sit right under the headline. I know where to tap and I don't have to hunt through the nav.
- **Top nav (Women, Men, Kids, Brands, Sneakers, Sports, Sale):** a plain, familiar structure. "Sale" in red stands out, and I'd go there first.
- **Search bar:** it's prominent and says "Search for shoes, clothes, etc.", which is how I actually shop.
- **Carousel pause button and arrows:** I can stop the auto-rotation, and I appreciate that.

## What's weak

- **No returns or shipping message anywhere:** free overnight shipping and free returns are the whole reason I buy three sizes here. The screen never mentions them.
- **Cookies & Privacy panel:** it's tall and mostly blank, with buttons crammed in the bottom right. It covers the second banner, so I can only see "All Day…" cut off.
- **Hero is all vibe, no product info:** there's no brand name, no price and no reason to pick these loafers over anything else.
- **The people are cropped to legs and hands:** I can't picture myself in this. It's aesthetic, but not relatable.
- **Nothing about being a VIP:** I'm a loyal customer and the page treats me like a stranger.
- **Three carousel dots, no preview of slides two and three:** if the loafer slide isn't my thing, I get nothing else above the fold.

## Recommendations

- Add a slim strip under the nav: "Free shipping · Free returns · 365-day returns". It's a one-line change that would matter most to me.
- Show a VIP or "Welcome back" state when I'm recognized, such as a small "VIP: free overnight shipping" chip by the account icon.
- Shrink the cookie panel to a slim bottom bar so it stops hiding the second module.
- Add a small product row under the hero: three loafers with price and star rating, so one tap gets me to a product page.
