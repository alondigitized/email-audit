# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so — the first thing I see is not a homepage, it's a cookie wall parked on top of whatever the homepage is trying to do. There's a back-to-school campaign going on with a kid I'd guess is maybe 12, and the tagline "ACE the DAY" in that very loud neon green. This looks like a brand that's pivoting hard toward parents shopping for their kids, which is not me.

## What's working

- **"Get The App" top banner** — I'm high-tech comfort and I actually respect when a brand leads with a mobile-first pitch; the copy is clean and direct
- **The color energy** — neon green with oversized bold type has genuine shelf presence, even through a cookie wall
- **Back-to-school timing** — July 15 is exactly when parents are panic-buying; the campaign calendar is right even if I'm not the target

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal as the primary experience** — I tapped on Zappos and my whole viewport is "Cookies & Privacy." That is not a welcome mat, that's a door in my face
- **Kid model, kid campaign** — Zappos *made its name* on free returns for adults obsessing over fit. This hero is aimed at a parent buying sneakers for a 12-year-old, not a woman who buys 3 sizes and returns 2
- **Zero price signal above fold** — not a single dollar sign, not a "free shipping," not a "30% off." I need a reason to stay and there isn't one
- **No returning-customer hook** — I've shopped Zappos before. There's no "welcome back," no rewards balance, no "based on your last order" — they have my data and they're wasting it
- **No CTA button visible** — "ACE the DAY" is a tagline, not a tap target. What do I do next?

## Recommendations

- **Surface a free-returns or free-shipping badge in the hero** — that's Zappos' entire identity as far as I'm concerned; put it above the fold where it lives
- **Swap the kid model for a lifestyle adult-women shot** or at minimum run an A/B with a shoe-forward close-up — the back-to-school campaign can exist, but the hero should have a version that speaks to people who aren't chaperoning field trips
- **Add a returning-member strip** just below the top banner — "Hi Solene, your rewards balance is X" is one line of personalization that would make me immediately trust this page more
- **Lose the cookie modal as a full-screen blocker** — or at least bottom-sheet it on mobile so the hero stays visible; right now the first scroll event is just me dismissing a dialog
