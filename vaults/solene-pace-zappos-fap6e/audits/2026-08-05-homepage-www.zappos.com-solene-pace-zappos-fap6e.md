# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- I open the app expecting shoes and I get a cookie banner shoved in my face over two models in Arc'teryx windbreakers standing in the woods. This reads like an outdoor gear catalog cosplaying as Zappos, not the shoe warehouse that lets me order three sizes and mail two back for free. Nothing here tells me this is even a footwear site.

## What's working

- The "Faster checkout & a curated shopping experience" top banner at least gestures at convenience, which is the one thing I actually care about as someone who buys in triplicate and returns most of it.
- The photography itself is well-shot and not cheesy — clean outdoor light, good production value.
- Search icon and cart icon are right up top where I expect them, so if I wanted to just search "sneakers" I could skip all this.

## What's weak

- The hero is 100% outdoor jacket/apparel energy — "FEAT. ARC'TERYX," "All-Terrain // All-Day" — with zero shoes in frame. I'm here for footwear, not a hiking windbreaker.
- The cookie consent modal eats the bottom third of my screen the second the page loads, blocking whatever content was supposed to come after the hero.
- No mention of free shipping, free returns, or VIP perks anywhere visible — that's Zappos's whole reputation and I don't see it.
- Zero product imagery, pricing, or a "shop shoes" button. I have nothing to tap toward my category.

## Recommendations

- Swap the Arc'teryx jacket hero for a shoe-forward lifestyle shot — feet, a stack of shoeboxes, something that signals "this is a shoe site."
- Add a "Free Shipping & Free Returns" strip above or right below the top banner — that's the actual hook that gets me to buy three sizes.
- Shrink the cookie modal to a slim bottom bar instead of a nearly-full-screen card blocking the hero.
- Put a real shopping CTA up top — "Shop Women's Shoes" or similar — not just "Get The App."
