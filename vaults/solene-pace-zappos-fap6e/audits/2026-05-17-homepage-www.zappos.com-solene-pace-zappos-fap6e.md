# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened Zappos expecting to feel immediately seen — this is my platform, I basically live here — and instead I got a soccer pitch. Literally. The whole above-the-fold is a yellow-and-black editorial about "The Beautiful Game" featuring a young male athlete and some expert named Jaydon Rodriguez. I know Zappos carries everything, but right now the homepage is telling me they're selling match-day cleats, not the wide-width slingbacks I've been eyeing for three weeks.

## What's working

- **"SHOP NOW" button** — high contrast, black on yellow, can't miss it. One tap and I'm moving somewhere, even if I'm not sure I want to go to the soccer section.
- **Bold editorial headline** — "The Beautiful Game" is clean, confident typography. The layout breathes. No visual chaos.
- **App download banner at top** — it's non-intrusive and useful. I'm already on mobile, so a gentle "faster checkout" nudge actually lands okay for someone like me who checks out constantly.
- **No modal ambush** — I wasn't hit with a popup or email-grab overlay the second the page loaded. That's a low bar but it matters for bounce rate.

## What's weak

- **100% male, 100% soccer** — I'm a 39-year-old woman who has bought from Zappos for 15 years. The entire hero is a young man on a grass pitch. I feel invisible.
- **No free-returns mention anywhere** — This is literally Zappos' superpower and the reason I religiously use this site. It's not on screen. That's a miss for someone like me whose entire shopping strategy depends on it.
- **"Zappos / In Motion" tab strip** — Repeated three times in identical yellow pills, scrolls horizontally apparently, but on first glance it looks broken or decorative. Doesn't help me navigate.
- **No price signal at all** — No "from $XX," no "up to 40% off," no member exclusive. I have no reason to believe clicking "SHOP NOW" leads anywhere interesting for me personally.
- **Jaydon Rodriguez of Modern Elite Training** — I do not know who this is and I don't care about building champions on the pitch. This influencer context means nothing to me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the soccer hero for a women's lifestyle shot** — 3 sizes of the same sandal laid out flat, or a woman loading a return box with a smile. That's the Zappos fantasy for people like me: low commitment, high reward.
- **Add free-returns badge to the top banner** — The "Get The App" strip is fine real estate but wasted. "Free returns, always. Get The App →" would speak to my entire reason for being here.
- **Surface a personalized returning-shopper hook** — If I'm logged in (and I always am), show my rewards balance or "pick up where you left off" with a recently-viewed slot. The app already knows my history.
- **Add one concrete offer line below the hero** — Even "Free overnight shipping for VIP members" or "Shop women's — new arrivals this week" as a secondary module immediately below the fold would give me a reason to keep scrolling.
