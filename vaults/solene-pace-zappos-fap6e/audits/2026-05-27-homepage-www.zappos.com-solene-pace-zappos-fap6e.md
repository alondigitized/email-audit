# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Zappos is doing its "casual summer lifestyle" thing and I get it — the vibe is right, the sneakers are cute, the copy is speaking my language. But I'm already a Zappos loyalist and there's nothing on this screen reminding me why I should stay loyal or why today specifically is the day to shop. It reads like a nice editorial page, not a homepage that knows me.

## What's working

- **"WEEKEND VIBES" headline + subhead**: "Effortless ease — for coffee shop runs to backyard BBQs" is exactly the tone that makes me feel seen. It's not "PERFORMANCE GEAR" or "ATHLEISURE." It's Saturday morning energy.
- **The hero image**: Beige low-top sneakers surrounded by a farmer's market haul is a genuinely cool creative choice. Artsy without being pretentious, seasonal without screaming SALE.
- **"SUMMER STARTS HERE" banner**: Small but effective — the seasonal framing makes the page feel alive and current.
- **Yellow "SHOP NOW" CTA**: High contrast against the earthy tones, can't miss it, sits right in the visual gravity zone.
- **Clean mobile render**: No jank, no layout shift, no overlapping elements. The app download banner isn't intrusive.

## What's weak

- **Zero free-returns mention**: This is Zappos. Free returns is their entire identity — it's the reason I use them instead of going to a store — and it's nowhere on this screen. That's a miss.
- **No free-shipping callout**: I'm a VIP free overnight shipping person. Nothing above the fold acknowledges that I get anything special for being a repeat customer.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook**: No rewards balance, no "Welcome back," no member-pricing badge. The homepage doesn't know I exist.
- **"SHOP NOW" is too vague**: Shop now… what? Women's sneakers? All summer styles? The CTA would hit harder with "Shop Weekend Sneakers" or "Shop the Look."
- **No navigation rails visible**: Just a hamburger menu. If I want sandals instead of lace-ups, I have to hunt for them rather than tap a visible category chip.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent free-returns badge or banner line**: Even a one-liner — "365-day free returns. Always." — somewhere in the hero would close more millennial hesitation than any discount.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for something specific**: "Shop Weekend Sneakers" or "Shop the Look" makes the tap feel more intentional and reduces bounce from shoppers who aren't sure where it goes.
- **Surface the loyalty/VIP hook above the fold**: Even a small "VIP member? Your perks are waiting →" nudge would re-engage the repeat buyers who are the highest-value segment.
- **Add a quick-tap category strip below the hero**: Three or four icon-chips (Sneakers, Sandals, Heels, Sale) would let me route myself in one tap without opening the hamburger.
