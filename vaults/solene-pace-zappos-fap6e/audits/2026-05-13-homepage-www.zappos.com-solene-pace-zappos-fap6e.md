# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, classic Zappos — clean, lifestyle-forward, clearly pushing a summer footwear moment. The hero image is pretty, those sandals are doing something for me, but I'm immediately noticing there's no mention of free returns anywhere on this screen, which feels like a miss given that free returns are literally the reason I'm here instead of Nordstrom. It reads like a brand that knows its identity but isn't leaning into the things that actually close me.

## What's working

- **The sandal hero image** — those terracotta block-heel sandals in a lifestyle outdoor setting are genuinely appealing; I want to know what they are
- **"SHOP NOW" button in yellow** — high contrast, impossible to ignore, good placement anchored to the copy
- **"Pairs for long days, warm nights & everything in between"** — copy has a nice millennial-casual rhythm, doesn't talk down to me
- **"Summer Starts Here" framing** — seasonal relevance is immediate; I know exactly what moment they're selling
- **App download banner** — as a high-tech-comfort shopper who's on her phone anyway, I actually register this

## What's weak

- **Zero mention of free returns** — this is Zappos' single biggest differentiator and it's not visible. I have to buy 3 sizes and return 2 every time; if I didn't already know about the returns policy, this screen gives me nothing
- **No loyalty or VIP hook** — Zappos VIP is the whole reason I'm loyal to this brand over competitors, and it's completely invisible here
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — takes me somewhere in summer footwear, but where exactly? No category specificity
- **No price anchoring at all** — not even "free shipping on orders over $X" to set expectations on what I'm walking into
- **Navigation is fully hidden behind a hamburger** — one tap to see categories is one tap too many when I'm already mentally shopping

## Recommendations

- **Add "Free returns always" or "365-day returns" as a persistent trust badge** directly below the CTA — it costs nothing and it's the reason I buy here
- **Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Summer Sandals"** or the specific category the hero image represents — specificity converts better
- **Surface a VIP/loyalty callout** as a thin strip or badge over the hero: "VIP members: free overnight shipping on every order" would stop me cold
- **Add a second hero CTA or supporting copy line** with even a soft offer: "Free shipping. Free returns. Always." — just one line under the button
