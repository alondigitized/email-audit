# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Zappos is leading with a trail running play — "Trail Cred:" and an earthy outdoor shoe shot — and I get it, it's a brand drop moment for On. But the first thing I actually see when I land is a cookie consent modal blocking half the hero, which is not the vibe when I'm trying to decide whether to stay or bounce. This looks like a solid retailer homepage that's speaking to a very specific customer today, and that customer is not me.

## What's working

- **The On banner at the very top** — "Cloud X Tempo from On just dropped. Shop Now" is clean, fast, and product-specific. I know exactly what I'm clicking if I care about On.
- **The trail shoe photography** is gorgeous. Moody, earthy, well-lit. Even if it's not my category, it communicates quality curation.
- **Hamburger + search + bag icons** are where I expect them. Zero friction to navigate out if the hero isn't for me.
- **Cookie modal is actually clean** — both "Customize Cookies" and "Accept" are clearly labeled, no sneaky pre-checked boxes. That's table stakes but Zappos does it right.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal blocks the hero** — "Trail Cred:" is cut off and whatever CTA lives below is buried. I have to dismiss the modal before I even know what this page is about. That's a lost first impression.
- **No free-returns mention anywhere above the fold.** That's the single reason I open Zappos instead of anyone else. It should be the first thing I see.
- **No VIP or loyalty hook.** I'm a repeat customer, a big spender — there's nothing here that acknowledges me or gives me a reason to feel like a preferred shopper.
- **"Trail Cred:" is very niche for the first scroll.** Trail running is a subset of a subset. Leading with it today means everyone else (casual, fashion, work, comfort) is just collateral.
- **No price incentive visible** — not a sale, not a member discount, nothing. Zappos competes on experience not price, but a little acknowledgment would help.

## Recommendations

- **Move the free-returns and free-overnight-shipping callout above the fold** — it's Zappos' #1 competitive advantage and it's invisible here. A thin strip below the logo ("Free overnight. Free returns. Always.") would pay for itself in bounce rate alone.
- **Add a returning-customer rail or "Recently viewed" module** just below the hero — high-tech shoppers like me expect the site to know us; cold-start homepages feel like Amazon for new users.
- **Don't let a cookie modal eat the hero on mobile** — either trigger it after 3 seconds or anchor it to the bottom of the screen so the hero copy reads before the consent wall goes up.
- **Rotate hero to multi-category or women-first on days without a specific brand drop** — "Trail Cred" is a fine moment for On fans but it's a dead end for the majority of the catalog.
