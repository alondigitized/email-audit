# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Zappos knows exactly what they are and they're leaning into it — sandals for spring, keep it simple, get out of your head. The whole above-the-fold is one clear mood: easy footwear for warm weather. It's clean and it doesn't try too hard, which I genuinely appreciate. That said, as someone who is literally *on* Zappos all the time, there's nothing on this screen telling me I'm welcome back or that my VIP perks are waiting.

## What's working

- **"Slip On / Head Out" headline** — copy that sells the vibe before it sells the product, which is exactly how I shop. I'm not researching, I'm impulse-buying based on a feeling.
- **The sandal spread** — five distinct styles laid out flat, showing real range: strappy green, cork Birkenstock, pink platform slide, red slide, navy print. I can see at a glance they've got the full spectrum of what I might want to try in three sizes.
- **Self-aware promo banner** — "Sandals for Spring. Groundbreaking — we know." Actually made me smile. That's the Zappos I know, not taking itself too seriously.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no clutter, no pop-up modal eating the screen, no aggressive discount banner strobing at me. I can actually see the page.
- **"Summer Shoe Checklist" teaser** — the yellow section just starting at the bottom is doing its job: it tells me there's more below and it's organized, not just another scroll of product tiles.

## What's weak

- **Zero free-returns / free-shipping callout** — this is Zappos' entire identity and it's not visible. For a new visitor this is a huge miss. For me as a buy-3-return-2 loyalist, seeing my VIP overnight shipping acknowledged would have me tapping faster than any sale could.
- **No loyalty hook at all** — no "Welcome back, Solene" type energy, no rewards balance, no "your VIP perks are active." I'm a repeat buyer and this homepage treats me like a stranger.
- **No price signals** — not one dollar amount, no % off, no "starting at $X" anywhere above the fold. I know Zappos runs constant promos; this screen tells me nothing about what I'm walking into.
- **No model** — just product flat-lay. When I'm buying sandals I want to see how they sit on a foot, not a museum-style overhead shot. A quick lifestyle image would sell the "75 & sunny" mood much harder.
- **Hamburger-only nav on mobile** — no quick-tap category strips. If I wanted boots right now I'd have to open the menu and hunt.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent VIP/free-returns strip** directly under the nav or inside the hero: "Free overnight shipping · Free 365-day returns" — one line, always visible. This is your conversion superpower and it's invisible right now.
- **Swap the flat product spread for a lifestyle shot** with a real person wearing the sandals in an outdoor setting — someone in their late 30s in a casual weekend context. Keeps the "Slip On / Head Out" mood but makes the product wearable in my mind.
- **Surface a concrete offer on the promo banner** — even "Free shipping on any order, always" counts. Right now the banner is just seasonal copy with a Shop Now link that could go anywhere.
- **Add a "Recently Viewed" or "Your picks" row** below the hero for logged-in users. If I'm signed in, show me the three sandals I was eyeing last week. That's one tap to add-to-cart.
