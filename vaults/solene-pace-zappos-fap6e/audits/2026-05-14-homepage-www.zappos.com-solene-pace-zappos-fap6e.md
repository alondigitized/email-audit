# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Zappos is going full summer lifestyle right now — the hero is a close-up of block-heel sandals paired with a boho print skirt, standing in the grass with blue sky behind it. It reads aspirational and warm-weather-ready, but it's more mood board than shopping engine. As someone who buys three sizes to find the one that fits and ships back the other two, I'm immediately looking for the practical stuff — free returns, overnight shipping, size tools — and none of that is above the fold.

## What's working

- **The hero image is sharp and on-target.** Block-heel strappy sandal, colorful printed skirt, outdoor lifestyle — this is exactly the visual language that makes me click instead of scroll past.
- **"SUMMER STARTS HERE" lands.** Short, declarative, seasonal. My brain reads it in half a second and knows this is a warm-weather shopping moment.
- **"Pairs for long days, warm nights & everything in between."** This supporting copy does actual work — it's the plural "pairs" that gets me. Like, yes, I need more than one. Good nudge.
- **The yellow "SHOP NOW" button pops.** Against the blue-sky gradient, it's impossible to miss. No guessing where to tap.
- **App download banner is low-pressure.** It's there at the top if I want it, but it doesn't interrupt anything.

## What's weak

- **Free returns — Zappos's entire identity — is invisible.** I cannot see the words "free returns" anywhere on this screen. That's the number-one reason I use Zappos over every other shoe retailer. Not showing it above the fold is a missed layup.
- **No free shipping callout.** I live for overnight shipping. If I have to dig into a footer to find out if my order ships free, I'm already annoyed.
- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic.** Shop *what*, exactly? Women's sandals? All summer shoes? Take me somewhere specific — "Shop Women's Sandals" would cut the friction in half.
- **No loyalty hook for returning customers.** I've ordered from Zappos dozens of times. There's no "Welcome back" signal, no rewards balance, no VIP tier acknowledgment. This page treats me exactly like a first-time visitor.
- **No price or deal signal.** There's zero reason to act today versus tomorrow. No offer, no urgency, nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Add a free-returns badge or strip directly in the hero area.** Even a small "Free returns. Always." line under the CTA button would do it — it's the one thing that separates Zappos from every other shoe retailer.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Women's Sandals."** The hero is already scoped to women's summer footwear; the CTA should match. This also makes the destination obvious so I don't second-guess the tap.
- **Add a loyalty/VIP strip below the nav.** Something like "VIP members: free overnight shipping on this order" with a member rewards balance. Give returning shoppers a reason to feel recognized.
- **Surface a concrete offer or threshold.** Even "Free 2-Day Shipping on All Orders" in a visible spot above the fold would give me a behavioral reason to shop now instead of adding it to a tab to check later.
