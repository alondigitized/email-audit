## 1. Executive Summary

Zappos is leading with a summer running campaign — golden-hour lighting, two people jogging through the trees, punchy "SEIZE THE DAYLIGHT" headline. No free-returns callout, no loyalty perks front and center, no "hey, welcome back" energy whatsoever. It's a clean, well-made page — it just doesn't feel like it was made *for me*.
