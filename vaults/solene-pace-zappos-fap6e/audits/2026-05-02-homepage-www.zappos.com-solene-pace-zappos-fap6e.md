# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Zappos is leaning hard into Mother's Day gifting right now, which is fine — but I came here for shoes for *me*, and the whole above-the-fold is asking me to shop for someone else. The brand feels warm and approachable, that yoga mom-and-daughter hero is genuinely cute, but it's pushing a gifting occasion when I just want to browse my usual haul-and-return ritual.
- **6/10**

## What's working

- **The top banner ("Sandals for Spring. Groundbreaking—we know.")** — the self-aware, slightly sarcastic copy is exactly my sense of humor. That little wink made me not immediately scroll past it.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — dark, high-contrast, hard to miss. One tap and I'm in the funnel. No friction.
- **Lifestyle imagery** — real women doing real things (yoga) instead of a mannequin-stiff studio shot. The white sneakers casually placed on the mat are a nice product placement, subtle not shoved.
- **Clean nav** — search icon is right there, cart is visible, hamburger keeps it tight. Zero clutter for a first screen on mobile.

## What's weak

- **"Mother's Day Edit" hero is a detour for me** — I'm 39, no kids in the picture, and I did not open Zappos to gift-shop. This entire hero is asking me to exit my own shopping intent and think about someone else's feet.
- **No free returns/overnight shipping mention above the fold** — this is literally *why* I use Zappos over everyone else. That value prop is invisible right now. I have to take it on faith that it's still true.
- **No product category entry points visible** — I can't tap into "women's sneakers" or "sandals" without hitting the hamburger menu. The hero gives me zero shortcuts into browsing.
- **"SHE DESERVES IT" framing** — I know it's Mother's Day marketing, but to a non-mom it reads like a reminder that the page isn't for me. Mild but real friction.

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin trust strip just below the banner** with "Free overnight shipping. Free returns. Always." — this is Zappos' single biggest advantage and it's not on screen at all.
- **Swap or split the hero** with a second slide for non-gifters: a "New in Women's" or "Spring Sandals" panel so people not in gifting mode get an on-ramp immediately.
- **Add 2–3 quick-tap category pills** below the CTA ("Sneakers · Sandals · Boots") so I can get moving without opening the full menu.
- **Lean into the banner copy's tone** — that "Groundbreaking—we know" line is charming. Bring that voice into the hero subhead too; "way better than flowers" is cute but it's still selling me on giving it away.
