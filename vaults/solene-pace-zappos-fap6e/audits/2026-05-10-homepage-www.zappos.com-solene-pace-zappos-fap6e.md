# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Zappos knows summer is happening and they want me to know it too. The vibe is warm-weather sandal fantasy — boho skirt, block heels, outdoor grass — which honestly is very much my Pinterest board. But this homepage is living entirely on aesthetic right now; there's zero mention of the thing that makes me loyal to Zappos in the first place: free overnight shipping and painless returns.

## What's working

- **The sandal hero image** — orange block-heeled strappy sandals against a boho patterned maxi skirt on grass. That's genuinely aspirational and it maps directly to the kind of shoe I'd be buying three sizes of and returning two.
- **"SUMMER STARTS HERE"** — short, punchy, seasonal. Millennial brains respond to declarative statements that feel like permission to shop.
- **The yellow CTA button** — color contrast against the blue-wash background is strong. It doesn't blend in. I can find it with half a glance.
- **"Pairs for long days, warm nights & everything in between"** — that subheadline is doing lifestyle-speak well without being annoyingly twee. It's specific enough to feel intentional.
- **National Runners Month banner** — timely, themed, has a clear CTA ("Shop Running"). Even if it's not for me, it signals this brand knows what month it is.

## What's weak

- **No free returns mention anywhere above the fold** — this is Zappos' entire identity and my entire reason for being loyal to them. I buy three sizes and ship back two. If a newcomer landed on this page they'd have no idea free returns is even a thing. Massive miss.
- **"SHOP NOW" is vague** — shop what? Summer sandals? All summer? Women's? Men's? I'd tap it, but only because I already know the site. For anyone less familiar, this CTA has no destination promise.
- **No nav categories visible** — just a hamburger menu. I can't see Women's, Men's, Sandals, anything. One tap to get me to my category should be visible without opening a flyout.
- **The National Runners Month banner is irrelevant to me** — it's above everything, it's the first thing I read, and I don't run. It's eating prime real estate for a segment that isn't me.
- **Zero loyalty or VIP signal** — I have VIP status. I get free overnight. Show me that. Even a small "Your VIP perks are active" chip would land emotionally and reinforce why I'm here vs. a competitor.

## Recommendations

- **Add a trust-strip below the hero** — one line: "Free overnight shipping. Free returns. Always." That's the entire Zappos value proposition and it's invisible right now. Ship this next week, no design overhaul required.
- **Swap the announcement banner content for returning shoppers** — or make it dynamic. If I'm logged in (VIP), show me my shipping status or rewards points, not National Runners Month.
- **Make the CTA destination-specific** — change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Women's Sandals" or "Shop Summer Arrivals." Two words of specificity removes ambiguity and increases click confidence.
- **Add a quick-access category strip below the hero** — Sandals / Sneakers / Comfort / New Arrivals — four chips, one row. On mobile this is easy to implement and gets me to my category without opening the hamburger.
