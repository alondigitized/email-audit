# Homepage snapshot · www.zappos.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Zappos opened with sandal energy and a Friends x Crocs collab — which is honestly a mood. The hero copy "You manage the family calendar, we'll handle the footwear" made me do a double-take in the best way; that's writing for *me*. But I'm looking for the free-shipping badge, the returns line, the loyalty perks — all the stuff that made me a Zappos loyalist — and none of it is above the fold. That absence stings.

## What's working

- **"You manage the family calendar, we'll handle the footwear."** This subhead is doing real work. It's self-aware, it respects my time, and it makes me feel seen without being cringey about it.
- **"MAJOR SANDAL MOMENTS" hero** — sandal season, multiple styles displayed (athletic, slides, strappy, kids'), it reads like a genuine editorial edit not a clearance dump.
- **Three-CTA split (Women's / Men's / Kids')** — zero friction to get where I'm going. One tap, I'm in women's sandals.
- **Friends x Crocs collab banner** — niche pop-culture energy, bright yellow, hard to miss. Collab drops feel exclusive even when they're not, and that pulls my hand toward tapping.

## What's weak

- **No free-shipping / free-returns call-out above the fold.** This is Zappos's entire identity and brand promise, and it's nowhere I can see. For someone like me who buys three sizes and ships them all back, this is the thing that keeps me loyal. Bury it and I start wondering if the policy changed.
- **Zero loyalty / VIP recognition.** I'm a repeat customer with VIP status — I want to see that acknowledged. Even a "Welcome back, Solene" or a rewards balance chip would change my mood.
- **No price signal anywhere.** Not a discount, not a price range, not a "starting at $XX." I can't pre-qualify whether I'm about to waste time browsing out-of-budget product.
- **App download banner eats prime real estate.** Top strip pushing the app is fine in principle, but it's the *first* thing I see and it's not an offer — it's an ask. Move it down or swap it for a returns badge.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the app-download top strip for a free-returns + VIP shipping callout.** Something like "Free overnight shipping. Free returns. Always." — one line, black bar, same real estate. That's the brand promise and it belongs at the top.
- **Add a "Welcome back" or rewards-balance chip** to the nav area for logged-in users. Even a small "Points: 4,200" badge next to the cart icon tells me I'm a known customer.
- **Put a "starting at" price or % off** on the sandal hero. "Sandals from $29" or "Up to 40% off" converts browsers like me into clickers.
- **Replace the generic product-only hero shot with a lifestyle image** — a woman my age at a Saturday farmers market or a backyard barbecue in sandals. Product shots sell categories; lifestyle shots sell the feeling.
