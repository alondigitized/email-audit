# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll say this — Walgreens knows I'm coming in for my pills, because "Manage prescriptions" is right there on the front page, big as you please. The rest of it, though, is throwing diapers and weight-loss shots at me, and at 67 I haven't needed Pampers in about four decades. It looks like a tidy store circular — organized, not flashy — but it's clearly not talking to me past that one tile.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick tile** — This is why I open the app. Having it right up top, no digging, is exactly right for someone like me who's here to refill her blood pressure medication, not browse.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — Smart placement for my age group. Flu shot, shingles vaccine — I'm the customer who actually books these.
- **The promo is legible** — "15–20% OFF" in large type with both codes written out plainly is exactly how my generation reads deals. No spinning wheel, no mystery discount. It reads like the Sunday circular insert.
- **Store location is shown** — Seeing "2331 S Atlantic Blvd" at the top feels personal. It knows my store.
- **BOGO deals on vitamins** — Partially visible at the bottom, but vitamins and supplements are something I actually buy here.

## What's weak

- **Pampers Swaddlers in the hero** — I have grandchildren but I'm not buying their diapers at Walgreens. This is the most prominent visual on the page and it's talking to a 30-year-old new parent, not me.
- **No MyWalgreens rewards hook** — I have points on that card. I check them. The account link is there but there's no "You have 480 points" or "Members save an extra 10%" anywhere I can see. That would get my attention instantly.
- **GLP-1s for Weight Loss tile** — I understand why it's there, but placing it right next to my prescription management tile feels a little presumptuous. It's not the warmest welcome for a senior who's here for her regular refills.
- **No free shipping threshold** — I sometimes order refills for delivery. Nothing on this screen tells me what it costs or how to avoid the fee.
- **Huggies at the fold** — More diapers. I'm seeing a pattern and it's not mine.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero product showcase** — Replace Pampers and baby products with vitamins, pain relief, or a seasonal health product (sunscreen, allergy, senior wellness) that reflects who actually walks through the door daily.
- **Add a MyWalgreens rewards nudge above the fold** — Even a small banner: "You have [X] points — redeem in-store or online." That is a returning-shopper hook that would make me feel seen before I've done anything.
- **Put a clear CTA button on the promo section** — The 15–20% off deal is right there but I don't know where to tap to use it. Add a "Shop Wellness Deals" button so it's not just information — it's an invitation.
- **Surface prescription reminders or "ready for pickup" status** — If the app knows my refill is ready, that's the one message that converts me instantly. Even a small "Check your Rx status" link near the prescription tile would close the loop.
