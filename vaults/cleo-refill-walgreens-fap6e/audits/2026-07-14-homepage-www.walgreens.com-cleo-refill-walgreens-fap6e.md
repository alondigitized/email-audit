# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, I opened Walgreens.com expecting to find my prescriptions or maybe check this week's deals, and the first big thing I see is a woman half my age flexing her arm over a GLP-1 weight-loss pitch. Walgreens is clearly a full pharmacy and drugstore, and there IS a "Manage prescriptions" tile right up top — that part is nice — but the hero is pushing weight-loss injectables like this is a med-spa website. The Sunday circular has more personality than this.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-action tile** — it's right there in the four-tile grid, second row left. That is exactly why I open this app. One tap and I'm where I need to be.
- **Top promo banner** — "20% OFF $50 sitewide* with code JULY20" is the first thing I see. Concrete, specific, useful for picking up household essentials on the same run as my prescriptions.
- **Store location pre-populated** — "2331 S Atlantic Blvd" already shows my store. That's a small thing but it tells me the app knows me.
- **Clean layout** — no clutter, no pop-up modal, no auto-play video demanding my attention. Easy enough to read on my phone.

## What's weak

- **GLP-1 hero takes up the whole screen** — I understand it's a hot product right now, but I did not come to the Walgreens homepage for weight-loss injections. It shoves aside any sense that this site knows me as a regular.
- **No MyWalgreens hook anywhere above the fold** — I earn points every single week. Where is my balance? Where is a "Welcome back, Cleo" or even a generic "Earn points on this order"? The Sunday paper shows my rewards more prominently than this.
- **"Learn more" is the weakest CTA imaginable** — Learn more about what, exactly? I want a button that says "Refill now" or "Shop weekly deals," not a vague invitation to read marketing copy about a prescription service I may not qualify for.
- **GLP-1 disclaimer asterisk** — "*Medical evaluation and other restrictions may apply" is fine print sitting right under the headline. It's honest, but for someone my age it raises more questions than it answers and makes the whole section feel like a commercial for a medication ad.
- **"Shop contact lenses" quick tile** — that's two of the four prime quick-action spots dedicated to GLP-1 weight loss (the tile AND the whole hero). That's heavy rotation for one product.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a personalized returning-customer module** — if I'm logged in or location-matched, show me my MyWalgreens points balance and a "Refill a prescription" CTA front and center. I'd actually tap that.
- **Replace "Learn more" with an action verb** — "Start GLP-1 consult" or "See if you qualify" tells me what clicking does. "Learn more" tells me nothing.
- **Add a loyalty strip above the fold** — one thin banner under the promo bar that shows "You have X points — redeem on your next order" would keep regulars like me from feeling ignored.
- **Rotate the hero to match the weekly circular** — I check the Sunday paper every week for the Walgreens deals. If the site reflected those same featured deals (vitamins, allergy, household staples), I'd feel like the two are connected instead of being two different stores.
