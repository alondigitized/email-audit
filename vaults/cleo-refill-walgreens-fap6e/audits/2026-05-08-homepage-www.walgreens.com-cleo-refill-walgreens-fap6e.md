# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll say this — Walgreens knows I'm there for my prescriptions, because those tiles jumped right out at me. But the big splash in the middle is all Mother's Day gift bags and nail vitamins, which isn't quite why I open this app. It feels like they're trying to be a department store and a drugstore at the same time, and the drugstore side is winning — barely.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tile** — it's the second thing my eye hits after the top banner, exactly where it should be for someone making a refill run
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — relevant to me at 67; I keep up with my shots and appreciate this being one tap
- **Top-of-page promo banner** — "20% OFF $50+ with code SPRING20" is easy to read in plain text; no decoding required
- **The spring promo hero** — the copy is simple and honest: here are two codes, here are the thresholds; no guessing
- **Mother's Day timing** — I have grandchildren; the gift bag imagery at least reminds me to think about picking something up

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens balance** — I collect those points every single week; they should greet me with "You have 850 points" right at the top after I'm logged in
- **Hero is beauty and gifting, not health essentials** — the big center section is about fragrance and "Hair Skin Nails" vitamins, not the everyday household and health staples I come in for
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — if I'm buying online to skip the walk on a bad-weather day, I need to know right away what it costs to ship
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — feels clinical and trend-chasing; a bit out of place alongside the Mother's Day theme
- **No loyalty reward hook** — MyWalgreens is one of the main reasons I shop here, but the page reads like it doesn't know I'm a member

## Recommendations

- **Add a MyWalgreens points balance chip** next to the Account link or just below the search bar — one line, "Hi Cleo — 850 points available," would keep me engaged every single visit
- **Swap one of the hero banners for a "Refill & Save" block** — something that ties the 20% sitewide offer directly to pharmacy essentials like vitamins, bandages, and OTC meds, not just beauty gifts
- **Show a free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — something like "Free shipping on $35+" right alongside the promo code would close the loop for people deciding whether to order or walk in
- **Promote the weekly ad** — I check the Sunday paper insert every week; a "This Week's Ad" tile would feel like home and pull me deeper into the site
