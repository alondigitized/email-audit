# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Well, this feels like my Walgreens — it's got the prescription management right up front, which is exactly why I'm here. The sitewide sale is hard to miss, and I appreciate that they're calling out the Memorial Day deals. But that big product image with the Huggies diapers threw me off; I'm not shopping for baby things, and it doesn't feel like this page was laid out with someone like me in mind.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick tile** — this is the first thing I'd tap every single time. It's right there, big, obvious, and doesn't make me dig through a menu.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — I get my flu shot and shingles booster here. Putting this front and center is smart for people my age.
- **The sitewide promo block** — "15–20% OFF" in large text with the two codes spelled out clearly is exactly how I like to see a sale: no guessing, just copy the code. The tiered $35/$50 structure makes sense to me.
- **Location shown at top** — I can see it already knows my store address, which means my prescription profile is probably linked. That's reassuring.
- **Memorial Day hook** — I do pay attention to holiday sales for stocking up on essentials. Seeing it in the screenshot tells me there's more if I scroll.

## What's weak

- **Huggies diapers as the hero product image** — I haven't bought diapers in forty years. This is the biggest visual on the page and it's completely irrelevant to me. It undercuts the goodwill earned by those prescription tiles.
- **No MyWalgreens / rewards balance visible** — I earn points on almost every visit. If I'm logged in, I want to see my balance or a prompt to redeem. That's missing entirely from this view.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — It's a real health category, but framing it as a quick-launch feature feels abrupt. No context, no "talk to a pharmacist" reassurance — just a stark tile. Feels like it belongs deeper in the Health section.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I sometimes order online for delivery between trips. I can't see what it takes to qualify from this screen, so I can't plan.
- **No trust signals or reviews** — nothing about ratings, return policy, or pharmacy credentials is visible here.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Huggies hero image for a seasonal essentials photo** — sunscreen, vitamins, pain relief — the kinds of things a Memorial Day basket looks like for adults over 60. The product creative is doing the opposite of what the prescription tiles earned.
- **Add a visible MyWalgreens rewards strip under the nav** — even a one-line "You have 1,240 points · $1.24 to redeem" would make me feel recognized as a loyal customer, not just a visitor.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo block** — put "$X+ ships free" right next to the discount codes. I'd spend up to hit it.
- **Add a "Pharmacy & Health" category shortcut** to the quick tiles row or just below — one tap to vitamins, OTC meds, and pharmacy FAQs would make this homepage feel like it was built for regulars like me, not just deal-hunters.
