# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I can see the screenshot. Writing the review now as Cleo Refill.
- This looks like the Walgreens I know — the "Manage prescriptions" tile is right up top where it belongs, which tells me whoever designed this at least knows why I open this app. Right now they're pushing a Mother's Day sitewide sale with coupon codes, which is fine, but the big banner is all about perfume and hair vitamins, not a pharmacy. It feels like my corner drugstore decided to moonlight as a gift boutique for the week.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tile** — this is the whole reason I open the app, and it's the second tile in the first grid I see. One tap and I'm done.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at 67 I'm booking a shingles booster, not a 5K. Seeing this up top tells me the site understands who actually walks through that door.
- **Coupon codes spelled out in full** — SPRING15 and SPRING20, clear thresholds, large enough to read. No magnifying glass required.
- **Mother's Day timing** — the lavender banner with hearts is cheerful and legible. Feels like a page from the Sunday circular, which I actually read.
- **"50% OFF Everything Photo"** — I print holiday photos every year. That tile caught my eye immediately and I'd tap it.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens points balance** — I've earned points at this store for years. I'd expect to see my balance the moment I open the app, right next to "Account." Instead I get nothing.
- **Hero banner promotes gift fragrances and hair supplements** — lovely for someone shopping for their mother, but I came to refill lisinopril, not browse perfume. The banner doesn't speak to me at all.
- **No loyalty or member-pricing signal** — the coupon works for anyone, which is fine, but there's nothing that makes me feel like a longtime regular versus a first-time visitor.
- **No free-shipping information** — the top bar tells me 20% off $50+, but does that order ship free? I genuinely cannot tell from this screen.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile in the utility grid** — I know what those are from the news, but it feels out of place next to vaccinations and prescription management. That belongs further down the page.

## Recommendations

- **Show my MyWalgreens points balance in the header** — right next to "Account," give me a number. Seeing "1,240 pts" would make me feel recognized and might nudge me to add something to hit the next reward tier.
- **Replace the "GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile with "Weekly Ad" or "Pharmacy Savings"** — I plan my shopping around the Sunday flyer. A direct link to this week's circular would be more useful to me than a trending drug category.
- **Add an expiration date to the sitewide coupon** — "Ends Sunday 5/11" would feel natural and credible, not pushy. I live by the weekly sale rhythm; give me a deadline I recognize.
- **State the free-shipping threshold in the announcement bar** — even one extra line like "Free shipping over $35" gives me a reason to consider ordering online versus walking over.
