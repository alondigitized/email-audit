# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Well, this looks like my Walgreens app, and I recognize it right away — that big red "W" and the search bar feel familiar. Right now they're pushing a sitewide sale with a coupon code, which is the kind of thing I'd clip out of the Sunday paper, and I'm glad to see my "Manage prescriptions" button front and center. ## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-action tile** — this is my number one reason to open the app and it's right in front of me, no digging required
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at my age, I'm always thinking about my flu shot or shingles booster; nice to have it one tap away
- **Sitewide sale banner** — "20% OFF $50+" is easy to understand and the code MAY20 is right there; I don't have to hunt for it
- **My store address is shown** — "2331 S Atlantic Blvd" up top means the app knows where I shop, which feels reassuring
- **Memorial Day timing** — the seasonal hook makes the deal feel real and now, not like leftover content

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens points balance visible** — I check my points every time I open the app; hiding that balance makes me feel like the rewards program isn't the priority here
- **Huggies swim diapers in the promo** — I'm 67 and my grandchildren are grown; a big baby product photo doesn't make me feel like this sale is for me
- **Promo codes require me to remember them at checkout** — MAY15 and MAY20 are fine in the Sunday paper, but on my phone I forget by the time I get to the cart; a "coupon already applied" message would be better
- **No "Refill Now" type button** — the "Manage prescriptions" tile is small; for my main use case, I'd expect a bigger, more prominent action
- **GLP-1s for Weight Loss tile** — I understand why it's there, but placing it alongside my prescriptions tile feels a little awkward, like a sales pitch mixed in with my healthcare

## Recommendations

- **Show my MyWalgreens points balance in the top bar** — right next to "Account," put something like "1,240 pts" so I know immediately what I have to spend
- **Swap the Huggies lifestyle shot for household essentials or vitamins** — something I actually buy, like paper towels, vitamins, or Lubriderm (I can see a tube of it peeking out on the right)
- **Auto-apply the sitewide promo at cart** instead of asking me to type in MAY20; I'm on my phone, not at a keyboard
- **Make the "Manage prescriptions" tile full-width and prominent** — it's the most important action for loyal pharmacy customers like me; give it a real button treatment, not a small square
