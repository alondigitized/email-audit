# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- I opened Walgreens.com to check my prescription refill and the first big thing I see is a weight-loss drug ad — that's not why I came here. Walgreens clearly knows I need my pharmacy, though, because those four quick-action tiles right below the header are genuinely useful. The site feels like a big-box drugstore more than a pharmacy I trust with my health, but the bones are decent.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-action tile** — this is the first thing I actually came here to do, and it's right there in the second row. One tap. That's exactly how it should be.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at my age I'm keeping up with flu and shingles shots. Nice to see it surfaced without digging.
- **"20% OFF $50+ with code JULY20" banner** — I stock up on vitamins, bandages, paper goods. A sitewide discount with a clear code is something I'd actually write down like I do from the Sunday paper.
- **Location line "2331 S Atlantic Blvd…"** — the site knows where my store is, which makes me feel like my account is connected. That matters to me.
- **"Dive into savings / 15–20% OFF sitewide\*"** — reassuring that there's a real deal happening, not just a teaser.

## What's weak

- **GLP-1 weight loss hero** — I understand this is a big health topic right now, but leading the whole page with it feels like they're fishing for new patients, not taking care of regulars like me. I'm 67 and I walked in (digitally) to check my blood pressure medication, not start a weight loss program.
- **No MyWalgreens points balance** — my physical Walgreens card earns me points on every visit. I should see my balance the moment I land here. I have no idea if I'm close to a reward.
- **"Learn more" CTA copy** — learn more about what, exactly? This button is attached to a GLP-1 pitch that doesn't concern me, and the copy tells me nothing. It's the kind of vague button I'd scroll past.
- **No weekly ad link above the fold** — every Sunday I look at the circular. This page doesn't surface it anywhere I can see, and that's a missed connection with someone like me.
- **No pharmacy hours or store info shortcut** — I walk to this store. If I could check hours or whether a prescription is ready to pick up from the homepage, I'd trust the site more.

## Recommendations

- **Surface MyWalgreens points balance prominently for signed-in users** — put it in the header next to "Account," the way a bank shows your balance. That's the number-one reason a loyal customer like me checks the app.
- **Add a "Weekly Ad" shortcut tile** — put it in that 2×2 quick-action grid alongside prescriptions and vaccinations. It's how my generation shops and it drives basket size.
- **Swap the hero for prescription-management or pharmacy-services messaging** — "Your refills, ready when you are" with a CTA to the Rx dashboard would land for the core Walgreens customer. Save the GLP-1 push for a secondary banner.
- **Add a "Rx Ready for Pickup" status module for logged-in users** — a simple green checkmark and prescription name above the fold would make this the first app I open every morning.
