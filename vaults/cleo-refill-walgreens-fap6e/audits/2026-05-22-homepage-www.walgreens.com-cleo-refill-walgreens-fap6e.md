# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Well, this looks familiar — the big red "W," the little search box, and right there at the top a sitewide sale with codes I'd actually have to type in. It feels like my Walgreens, not some fancy department store, which I appreciate. Right now they're pushing Memorial Day savings and a percent-off deal, and I notice they put that "Manage prescriptions" tile right up front, which is basically the reason I open this app in the first place.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick tile** — that's my number-one reason for opening Walgreens, and it's the third tile I see. One tap and I'm where I need to be.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at 67, I keep up with my flu shot and shingles booster. Nice that it's front and center without me digging.
- **Sitewide promo strip ("Save sitewide* 15–20% OFF")** — the tiered codes (MAY15 / MAY20) are laid out simply. Even if I have to write the code down, I understand exactly what I'm getting.
- **Location pin showing my store address** — I walk to that Walgreens. Seeing my address confirmed tells me this is set to my store, which matters for pickup.
- **Memorial Day framing** — the seasonal peg feels current and honest, not like a leftover banner from Easter.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens points balance** — I earn points on every purchase and check them like my bank balance. Not seeing them here makes me feel like a stranger, not a loyal customer.
- **Hero product imagery is Huggies and Kettle Chips** — I'm 67 with no grandkids in diapers and I'm watching my sodium. These products are fine, but they aren't speaking to me at all.
- **Promo requires coupon codes** — MAY15 and MAY20 mean I have to remember to enter them at checkout. For someone my age using an app instead of the register, that's an extra step I might forget.
- **GLP-1s for Weight Loss tile** — feels a little clinical and blunt as a homepage tile. Not offensive, just odd positioning next to prescriptions.
- **No explicit free-shipping callout** — I sometimes order online for things too heavy to carry. I shouldn't have to hunt for the threshold.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Huggies/chips hero imagery for healthy living or pharmacy essentials** — vitamins, blood pressure monitors, reading glasses. Products a 60+ loyal customer actually buys.
- **Add a MyWalgreens points balance chip near the Account link** — even something small like "1,240 pts" keeps regulars like me feeling seen and rewards-motivated.
- **Convert the promo codes to auto-apply member pricing** — or at minimum add "Tap to copy code." Asking me to type MAY20 at checkout is friction that costs you conversions.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line to the promo strip** — one short line, "Free shipping on $35+ orders," would answer the question before I even think to ask it.
