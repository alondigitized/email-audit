## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-tile** — It's front and center in that 2×2 grid, right where I need it. That's my whole reason for opening the app most days, and I don't have to hunt for it.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — Relevant to my age group and a health topic I hear about constantly. Shows me Walgreens knows their pharmacy customers.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — As a 67-year-old I'm always thinking about my next shingles booster or flu shot. Love that it's one tap.
- **Top banner offer** — "20% OFF $50+ sitewide* with code SPRING20" is concrete and readable. I can see the code, I can see the threshold, I can decide in three seconds if it matters to me.
- **Mother's Day theme** — Those flowers and the "MOM" heart feel warm and timely. I was already thinking about getting something for my daughter-in-law.
- **"50% OFF Everything Photo"** — I print photos every holiday season. Seeing that tile starting to peek in is exactly the kind of thing that would make me scroll down.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens points balance** — I earn points every single week and I like to know what I've got. That number being absent means I have to go hunting in Account, and sometimes I just don't bother.
- **The asterisk on "sitewide\*"** — I've been burned by that asterisk before. It probably excludes prescriptions, which is the main thing I'm here to buy. That fine print erodes the excitement of the offer fast.
- **No people who look like me** — The imagery is all products and decorations. A friendly face — a woman my age picking up a prescription or smiling with a gift bag — would make this feel more personal.
- **GLP-1s tile label** — "GLP-1s for Weight Loss" is pretty clinical language. I know what it means now, but plenty of my friends my age would squint at that. Could say "Weight Loss Medications" and reach more of us.
- **No free-shipping or pickup callout visible** — I walk to my store, but plenty of my friends order online and would want to know the free-shipping threshold up front.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the MyWalgreens points balance** right below the nav bar — even just "You have 847 points" would pull returning customers like me in immediately and reward loyalty visibly.
- **Add a warm lifestyle photo** to the Mother's Day hero — a 60-something woman handing a gift bag to her adult daughter would land much better for my demographic than just product-on-purple.
- **Clarify the asterisk exclusions upfront** — a one-liner like "Excludes prescriptions and photo" next to the offer code would actually build trust instead of shrinking it. Hiding exclusions in fine print feels like a trick.
- **Relabel "GLP-1s for Weight Loss" as "Weight Loss Medications"** — simpler language, same destination, but more people my age will recognize it immediately and tap.
