## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-access tile** — that is exactly why I open this app. It's right there above the fold, no hunting required.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at my age I pay attention to this. Flu shot, shingles, whatever's next. Seeing it front and center feels appropriate.
- **Sitewide sale banner** — 15–20% off with a code I can actually read is the kind of thing I'd cut out of the Sunday paper. Clear and simple.
- **Spring theme feels fresh** — the lavender and flowers don't feel stale or out of season. They got the timing right.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — whether I need it or not, it signals the pharmacy takes health seriously, not just lipstick.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens balance visible** — I have points and I want to know how many. That's a big reason I check the app. Not seeing it feels like a miss.
- **Hero imagery is Mother's Day gift shop, not pharmacy** — a gift bag and perfume aren't why I came here. It's a little confusing about what kind of store this is.
- **No button CTA in the promo block** — the sale is clear but I can't tap into it. Where does the offer actually go? There's no "Shop the Sale" button.
- **No visible free-shipping threshold** — I know I could order something, but I don't see what I'd need to spend. For a regular customer that matters.
- **No trust signals or review counts** — nothing tells me the vitamins in that hero are worth buying.

## Recommendations

- **Add my MyWalgreens points balance to the top of the page** — even a small "Hello, Cleo — 2,340 pts" banner above the tiles would make me feel seen and more likely to shop online instead of just in-store.
- **Put a "Refill a Prescription" button in the hero or right below the tiles** — it's the number-one reason people like me open this app; don't make it just a tile, make it a prominent CTA.
- **Replace the gift bag and perfume in the hero with pharmacy-adjacent imagery** — vitamins, health essentials, or even a senior couple — something that says "this sale is for your regular shopping," not "buy mom a present."
- **Show an expiration date on the spring sale** — "SPRING15 through May 11" would create honest urgency. Right now it's floating with no deadline.
