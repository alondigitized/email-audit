# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Well, they put my prescriptions right up front, which is smart — that's why I'm here nine times out of ten. Walgreens is coming across as a drugstore that knows its bread and butter, with a pharmacy-first layout and a beauty sale running underneath it. Right now they're pushing 15–20% off Garnier and the like, which is something I actually buy, so I'm not annoyed by it.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-action tile** — that's my number one reason for visiting, and it's in the second row, second position, no digging required
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at 67 I care about my flu shot, my shingles booster; putting this on the front page tells me they understand their core customer
- **The 15-20% OFF beauty promo with tiered codes** — Garnier micellar water and hair color are things I buy regularly; seeing a specific dollar threshold makes the math easy
- **Store location visible at the top** — "2331 S Atlantic Blvd" tells me I'm looking at MY store's page, which builds trust immediately
- **BOGO 50% OFF tiles peeking in at the bottom** — reminds me of the Sunday circular; I'll scroll down to see what those are

## What's weak

- **No rewards balance shown** — I collect MyWalgreens points faithfully; the banner tells me I could *earn* $15, but nowhere does it show what I've *already* accumulated. That number would keep me here.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — this is fine for some people, but it feels like it was put there for a different kind of customer than me; takes up one of only four prime tiles
- **Beauty promo imagery skews young** — the Nutrisse box has a young woman, the Fructis branding reads college-age; I still buy these products, but I don't quite see myself in that imagery
- **No photo-prints entry point** — I use Walgreens every holiday season for photo gifts; nothing on this screen nudges me toward that
- **No urgency on the beauty offer** — the codes are there, but there's no "ends Sunday" or "this week only" to give me a reason to act now rather than think about it

## Recommendations

- **Replace the "GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile with a "Weekly Ad" or "My Rewards Balance" tile** — the Sunday paper circular is how my generation shops; putting that front-and-center would feel like home
- **Add a "Hi, Cleo — you have X points" strip below the location bar** — even one line of personalized loyalty data would make me feel like a regular, not a stranger
- **Add a deadline line to the SHINE promo** — something like "Sale ends Saturday, June 7" printed right under the code; it's what the newspaper ad always does and it works
- **Swap the Nutrisse model crop for a product-flat shot or a woman closer to 55+** — the product itself is the reason I buy, not the model; or show someone who looks like me using it
