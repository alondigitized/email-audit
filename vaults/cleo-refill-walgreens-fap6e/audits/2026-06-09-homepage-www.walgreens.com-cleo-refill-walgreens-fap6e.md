# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll say this much — Walgreens knows I'm here for my prescriptions. "Manage prescriptions" is one of the first things I see when the page loads, and that "Schedule vaccinations" tile right beside it tells me this is still my drugstore, not just some discount website. Right now they're pushing a sitewide sale with coupon codes, and there's a summer barbecue food spread below it — which is fine, but honestly I came here to refill my metformin, not pick up potato chips.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick tile** — one tap and I'm in my refill queue. That's exactly why I opened the app. Walgreens put this front and center and I appreciate it.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — as a 67-year-old I pay attention to this. Shingrix booster is on my list and this saves me a phone call.
- **The sitewide offer block** — "15% OFF $35+ / 20% OFF $50+" is spelled out plainly with exact codes. I can understand that. No "see terms" rabbit hole.
- **Store address shown** ("2331 S Atlantic Blvd") — confirms my local store is loaded, which matters when I want to know if something is in stock before I walk over.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens rewards hook** — I have points built up and I can't see my balance anywhere. That balance is what brings me back. Hiding it on the homepage is leaving money on the table.
- **BBQ food promo imagery** — Kettle chips, Heinz ketchup, a Coca-Cola. I know they sell snacks, but this is not what I think of when I open Walgreens.com. It feels like the grocery store, not my pharmacy.
- **No loyalty member pricing badge** — I'm a MyWalgreens member. If there are member prices on vitamins or health items, I don't know that from this screen.
- **No weekly ad callout** — I look at the Sunday circular every week. A "View this week's ad" link would be the first thing I tapped.

## Recommendations

- **Add a MyWalgreens rewards balance chip near the top** — something like "You have 480 points" right under the account icon. That's a proven retention hook and it costs nothing to surface.
- **Swap the BBQ food promo image for a pharmacy-lifestyle scene** — show a woman around my age picking up a prescription bag, or a wellness shelf with vitamins and blood pressure monitors. That's what I came here for.
- **Add a "Weekly Ad" tile to the 2×2 quick-access grid** — replace or add alongside "Shop contact lenses." Boomers still rely on the circular and it's a high-frequency engagement driver.
- **Add a deadline to the promo** — "Ends June 30" would give me a reason to use the code today instead of thinking "I'll do it later" and forgetting.
