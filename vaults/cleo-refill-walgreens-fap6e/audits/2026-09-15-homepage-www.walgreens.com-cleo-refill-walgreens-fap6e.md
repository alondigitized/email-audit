# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Well, this looks like my Walgreens, all right — sensible, a little busy at the top, but it knows who I am. Right off the bat it's telling me the COVID vaccine is approved for folks 65 and up, which is nice, they're speaking directly to me instead of some 25-year-old. Feels like they're pushing vaccinations and rewards cash right now more than anything else.

## What's working

- The COVID vaccine callout that spells out "approved for adults ages 65+" — that's talking to me by name, practically
- "Manage prescriptions" quick-link with the little pill bottle icon — that's exactly what I come here to do
- "$15 Walgreens Cash" for getting vaccinated — real money back, not some vague promise
- The older gentleman and the woman with the curly gray hair and glasses in the photo row — that's who I picture when I picture a Walgreens shopper

## What's weak

- Too many horizontal bars stacked at the top (three offer lines, then the vaccine notice, then four quick-link buttons, then the big maroon banner) — my eye doesn't know where to land first, it's a lot to take in before my coffee
- No "Welcome back" or my rewards points balance showing — I use the MyWalgreens app for that, would be nice to see it here too
- No mention of the Weekly Ad, which is the whole reason I check the site on Sundays
- Small print type in spots — the search bar and top offer bar text is tiny for my eyes

## Recommendations

- Move "Manage prescriptions" and "Schedule vaccinations" up into one clear hero instead of splitting attention across four separate bands
- Add my MyWalgreens rewards balance or points count right under "Sign in" so I know what I've earned without opening the app
- Put a Weekly Ad tile or thumbnail above the fold — I look for that every Sunday
- Bump up the font size on the top offer strip; the "$10 in rewards" text is easy to miss at a glance
