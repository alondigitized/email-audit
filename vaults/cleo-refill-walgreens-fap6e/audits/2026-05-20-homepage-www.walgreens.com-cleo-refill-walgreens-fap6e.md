# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Well, this feels like my Walgreens — it knows I pick up prescriptions and right there on the front page it gives me a "Manage prescriptions" button, which is exactly what I come here for. Right now they're pushing a big sitewide sale with coupon codes for Memorial Day weekend. It's a pharmacy-first site that also sells snacks and diapers, which is a bit all over the place, but I can find what I need.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick tile** — this is the first thing I look for every time I open the app, and here it is right in the second row, no hunting required
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at my age I'm always keeping up with shots; glad they put this up front
- **Store address visible ("2331 S Atlantic Blvd...")** — confirms I'm looking at MY store, not some generic page
- **Sitewide discount with clear codes (MAY15, MAY20)** — easy to understand, no mystery; if I'm picking up a prescription anyway I might grab some Listerine and use that 15% off
- **"Memorial Day essentials from $1.99"** — that strip at the bottom is the kind of thing I'd see in the Sunday circular, feels familiar

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens points balance** — I earn points on every prescription and every purchase; I should see my balance the moment I open the homepage, but there's nothing here
- **Hero imagery (Huggies + Kettle chips)** — baby diapers and barbecue potato chips are not speaking to me; this feels like it was built for a younger shopper or a bulk-buying household
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — I understand it's a big health trend, but that language feels clinical and a little pointed for a homepage quick tile; could unsettle an older customer
- **No loyalty or member-pricing badge on the sale** — the 15-20% off is sitewide but I can't tell if MyWalgreens members get anything extra or if this is the same deal for everyone
- **Time limit missing** — the codes say MAY15 and MAY20 so I can guess it ends sometime in May, but there's no "offer ends Sunday" or any deadline; I like to know when I need to act

## Recommendations

- **Show my MyWalgreens points balance in the header or in a loyalty strip** — even a simple "You have 412 points" line near "Account" would make me feel like a regular, not a stranger
- **Swap Huggies/chips for pharmacy or wellness product imagery** — show vitamins, a pill organizer, a blood pressure cuff, or a senior couple at the pharmacy counter; that's who walks through the door
- **Add a clear expiration date to the promo** — change "Save sitewide* 15–20% OFF" to include "through Monday 5/26" so I know to act before my next Sunday walk
- **Highlight MyWalgreens member pricing separately** — if members get an extra percent or early access, say so with a small badge; right now the codes feel like a newspaper circular, not a loyalty reward
