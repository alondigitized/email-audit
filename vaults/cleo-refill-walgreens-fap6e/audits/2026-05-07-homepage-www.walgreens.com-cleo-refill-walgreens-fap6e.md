# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, they know I'm coming for my prescriptions — "Manage prescriptions" is sitting right there at the top, plain as day, which I appreciate. This feels like a Walgreens homepage trying to straddle two things at once: my pharmacy needs and a Mother's Day gift push. The spring sitewide discount is a nice bonus, but I'm not here to shop for perfume — I'm here to refill my metformin.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tile**: First thing I see below the navigation. One tap and I'm where I go every single day. Whoever put that there deserves a raise.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile**: Right next to prescriptions. I'm due for a shingles booster and I didn't even have to hunt for it.
- **Top banner discount code**: "SPRING20" for 20% off $50 is easy to read and remember. I might actually write that on my wrist before I shop.
- **Mother's Day tie-in**: I have three grandchildren. The "MOM" gift bag imagery reminds me I still need to pick something up for my daughter-in-law, and now I know Walgreens has fragrances and photo prints on sale.
- **50% off Everything Photo tile**: I print photos every Christmas and Mother's Day — 50% off is a deal I will use before Sunday.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens rewards balance or points reminder**: I've been a member for years and I have no idea if I have points to use right now. A simple "You have 2,400 points" would keep me on this app instead of calling the store.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile**: I don't need this and neither do most of the ladies at my church who are picking up blood pressure meds. It takes up a quarter of my pharmacy quick-links grid.
- **No refill reminder or "your Rx is ready" hook**: If I came here because the app pinged me about a refill, there's nothing on this homepage reinforcing that path — no "Your order is ready" banner, no personalized nudge.
- **Hero banner is generic sitewide promotion**: The big purple block is a mass-market Mother's Day promo. As someone who comes here specifically for prescriptions, it feels like a department store ad pasted into the top of my pharmacy.
- **Promo codes require me to remember or copy them**: At my age, making me type "SPRING20" at checkout is friction. A "clip coupon" button would be much easier.

## Recommendations

- **Add a MyWalgreens rewards summary chip in the header** — right under my location, show "3,200 pts · $3.20 Cash" so I know what I have before I browse. That alone would keep me on the app longer.
- **Replace the GLP-1 tile with a "Your prescriptions" personalized tile** that shows my next refill due date or "Ready for pickup." The pharmacy is your moat — lead with it harder.
- **Put a "Clip & Save" button on the promo codes** rather than forcing me to memorize SPRING20. One tap to wallet, done.
- **Swap one Mother's Day gift image for a lifestyle shot of a woman my age** using the photo print service or picking up a prescription — something that signals "this place is for me" rather than "we sell perfume too."
