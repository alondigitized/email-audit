# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll say this — they know I'm probably here for my prescriptions, because those four quick buttons right at the top are exactly what I need: "Manage prescriptions" and "Schedule vaccinations" front and center. But the big sale banner underneath is showing me Huggies diapers and Kettle chips, and honey, I haven't bought diapers in forty years. It's a pharmacy I walk to every week, and it mostly feels like it — though I wish it showed my points balance without me having to hunt for it.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" utility tile** — one tap to refill is exactly why I open this app; having it at the top of the page is the single most useful thing they could do for someone like me
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — seniors get more vaccines than any other age group; this is smart placement and I'd actually use it
- **The sitewide promo copy** — "15–20% OFF" with two clear code tiers and dollar thresholds; I can do that math right away and know if my weekly run qualifies
- **Location line at the top** — showing my nearest store address makes this feel like *my* Walgreens, not some anonymous website
- **Memorial Day hook** — "From $1.99 Memorial Day essentials" starting to appear is timely and the price point is attractive for everyday items

## What's weak

- **Hero product imagery (Huggies + Kettle chips)** — these are the two most prominent products visible in the sale section and neither one is anything I buy; makes the page feel like it was built for a 30-year-old parent, not me
- **No MyWalgreens points balance** — I earn points on every prescription pickup; that number should greet me on the homepage the way my bank shows my balance when I open the app
- **No loyalty/rewards callout above the fold** — the whole MyWalgreens program is invisible here; I don't even see a "member price" badge or points multiplier mentioned
- **No countdown or end-date on the sale** — "MAY20" tells me it's probably May-only, but there's no explicit "offer ends May 31" to give me urgency
- **GLP-1s for Weight Loss tile** — this is a fine category but it's one of the four precious quick-access tiles; I'd rather see "Photo prints" or "Weekly ad" there, things I actually use

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Huggies/chips hero products for pharmacy-adjacent items** — vitamins, over-the-counter pain relief, or even a blood pressure monitor would make the sitewide sale feel relevant to me instead of like I wandered into the baby aisle
- **Add a MyWalgreens points banner** — a single line like "You have 847 points — worth $8.47 on your next purchase" above the sale section would make me feel seen and give me a reason to add to cart today
- **Replace one quick-access tile with "Weekly ad"** — I still look at the Sunday paper insert; a digital version of the weekly circular in the tile row would be the most Boomer-friendly thing they could add
- **Add an explicit offer end date** — "MAY20 code expires May 31" takes three words and turns the discount from a casual note into a reason to buy today
