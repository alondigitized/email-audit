# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is my Walgreens — I know that "W" anywhere. Right now it's pushing a Mother's Day sitewide sale with promo codes, which makes sense for this time of year. What I notice immediately is that they put "Manage prescriptions" right up top in a big tile before you even get to the sale banner, which is exactly why I pull this app up most days.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tile** — that's the first reason I come here. It's big, it's obvious, it's right below the navigation. Good.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — relevant to me at my age. I appreciate that they surface this without me hunting for it.
- **Spring sale banner at the very top** — "20% OFF $50+ sitewide* with code SPRING20" in the top strip. Before I've even looked at anything, I already know there's a deal.
- **Mother's Day timing** — the purple hero with the gift bag and "MOM" heart feels fresh, not stale. It's May, this makes sense.
- **"50% OFF Everything Photo"** tile at the bottom — I always print holiday photos. That catches my eye every time.
- **Location is already set** ("2331 S Atlantic Blvd") — they remember where I am, which feels like they know me.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens rewards balance** — I have points sitting there and I have no idea how many from this screen. That's the number-one thing that would make me want to spend more.
- **Hero is gift-shopping, not health-shopping** — the big purple banner is all gift bags and hair-skin-nails supplements for gifting. I'm here for my Lisinopril refill, not a Mother's Day basket.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — the promo is clear, but I don't know if my order ships free. That matters when I'm deciding whether to add something to a cart vs. just picking it up.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — feels medical-trendy and a little out of place alongside "Schedule vaccinations" and "Manage prescriptions." It disrupts the pharmacy-as-healthcare tone.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "free pickup today," nothing telling me my order is safe or my refill will be ready.

## Recommendations

- **Add a MyWalgreens points chip to the Account row** — even just "1,240 pts" next to "Account" would make me tap it and think about spending to the next reward tier.
- **Swap the hero imagery for a pharmacy-first message** — something like "Your refill is ready — plus 20% off everything else today" with a soft medicine-cabinet lifestyle shot. Keep the Mother's Day tiles but don't lead with gift bags.
- **Surface a free-pickup or free-shipping line** — one short line below the promo codes ("Free same-day pickup at your store") would keep me from second-guessing whether to add to cart or just go in person.
- **Move "50% OFF Everything Photo" to a more visible spot** — that's a real deal for me around the holidays; right now it's half-hidden at the bottom edge of the fold.
