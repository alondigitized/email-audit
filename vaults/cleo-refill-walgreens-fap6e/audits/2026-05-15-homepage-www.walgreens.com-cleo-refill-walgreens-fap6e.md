# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This looks like my Walgreens, not some fancy boutique I wandered into by mistake — and that's a relief. Right up top I can see the prescription management tile and the vaccination scheduler, which is exactly why I open this app. They're running a wellness and beauty sale with percentage-off codes, which is fine, though I'll be honest: I'd rather see my MyWalgreens points balance front and center than hunt around for a coupon code I have to type in.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-action tile**: This is the whole reason I open the app. Putting it above the fold, labeled plainly with an icon, is exactly right for someone like me who comes here with a specific errand in mind.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile**: Flu shots, shingles vaccine, pneumonia — this is my age group's business. I appreciate seeing it up top.
- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile**: Speaks to a real health concern that people my age are asking their doctors about. That's relevant pharmacy content, not a fashion ad.
- **15–20% OFF hero with explicit codes**: I know what I'm getting. WELL15 for $35+, WELL20 for $50+ — no mystery, no "see store for details."
- **Top promo banner**: "15% OFF $35+ on select wellness or beauty with code WELL15" repeats the offer immediately. Good reinforcement for eyes like mine that sometimes miss things.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens points balance**: I earn points on everything, and I check my balance constantly. If I could see "You have 3,240 points" right at the top, I'd be more inclined to spend. Completely absent here.
- **Pampers Swaddlers in the wellness hero**: I have grandchildren, not babies at home. Diapers in the main promotional image make this feel like it's not quite talking to me.
- **Coupon code friction**: I'm 67. I don't love typing codes. My Sunday paper circular just says "show your card." WELL15 means I have to remember to type it at checkout — that's a hurdle.
- **BOGO Deals section barely visible**: I can just barely see "BOGO Deals on select same-brand vitamins" at the bottom of the screen and it's cut off. If vitamins are on BOGO, that's something I'd actually act on — don't make me scroll to find it.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout at all**: Not even a "Earn points on this purchase" badge anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Show my MyWalgreens balance in the header**: Even a small "1,240 pts" next to the Account icon would pull me in immediately. That's the digital equivalent of the cashier telling me my points at the register.
- **Swap the Pampers out of the wellness hero**: Replace the diaper pack with an image of a multivitamin, a blood pressure monitor, or a pharmacist — something in my wheelhouse. Keep GLP-1 and OLLY; those belong.
- **Surface the BOGO vitamins deal above the fold**: If same-brand vitamins are BOGO, that banner deserves prominent placement — vitamins are a repeat purchase category for my generation.
- **Replace code-based discounts with card-scan discounts in the CTA copy**: Instead of "use code WELL15," say "scan your MyWalgreens card" — it's the behavior I already do in store and removes the copy-paste step.
