# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know my Walgreens the moment I see that red W — and the first thing they're showing me is a weight-loss drug ad. Nice to know they put "Manage prescriptions" right there on the buttons, because that tile is the whole reason I opened this app. It looks clean enough on my phone, and there is a sitewide discount code right at the top, so they're at least trying to get me to spend a little.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-action tile** — that's my primary reason for being here, and it's right on the second row without digging through any menu. That's smart placement.
- **Top banner promo** ("20% OFF $50 sitewide* with code JULY20") — I see it immediately, I understand it, I know I could use it when I pick up my next order of vitamins or shampoo alongside my prescriptions.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — that speaks to someone my age. Flu shot season is always on my mind, and seeing it front and center tells me this is still a health destination, not just a candy store.
- **Clean mobile layout** — nothing is crashing into anything else. The font is big enough for me to read without squinting.

## What's weak

- **GLP-1 hero takes over the whole screen** — I came to refill my metformin, not read about a weight-loss drug. It feels like I walked into a clinic waiting room instead of my neighborhood pharmacy.
- **No MyWalgreens points or rewards balance visible** — I earn points on every visit and I check them like I check the Sunday paper. There's nothing here reminding me I have points or what I could redeem. That's a miss.
- **"Learn more" CTA is vague** — Learn more about what? Whether I qualify? What it costs? I wouldn't tap that.
- **No pharmacy-specific deal** — the 20% off is nice but it's sitewide with an asterisk. I'd love to see something like "Earn 1,000 bonus points on your next refill" — that would actually get me to act.
- **The model in the hero is too young** — she looks like she could be my granddaughter. I'm not the target of this ad, and I can feel it.

## Recommendations

- **Replace or supplement the GLP-1 hero with a pharmacy loyalty hook** — something like "You have 2,400 MyWalgreens points — here's what they'll get you" for signed-in members would make me feel seen immediately.
- **Add a "Your prescriptions" returning-member module above the fold** — even a simple "Ready for pickup" or "Refill due in 3 days" ribbon would make me stay and engage instead of just tapping the tile and leaving.
- **Change "Learn more" CTA copy to something specific** — "Check if you qualify" or "Talk to a pharmacist" would be far less ambiguous for a health audience my age.
- **Add a loyalty promo strip** — something like "Earn 500 bonus points this week on vitamins" tied to the weekly ad would bring the Sunday paper experience to my phone, which is exactly why I downloaded this app.
