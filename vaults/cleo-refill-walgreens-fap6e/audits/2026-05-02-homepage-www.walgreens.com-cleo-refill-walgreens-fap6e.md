# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Well, this looks like my Walgreens, and that's both the good and the bad of it. They're clearly pushing a spring sale right now with those sitewide discount codes, and the four quick-access tiles up top — vaccinations, prescriptions, contact lenses — are right in my wheelhouse. It feels practical and familiar, like walking through the front door of a store I already know.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" tile** — that's my number-one reason for being on this app, and it's front and center. I don't have to hunt for it.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile** — at my age, that matters. I'm due for my shingles booster and it's good to know I can do it right here.
- **Sitewide spring sale banner and promotional block** — 15–20% off with simple coupon codes is the kind of thing I actually clip from the Sunday paper, so seeing it spelled out plainly online works for me.
- **Store address visible at the top** — it shows my local store, which tells me they know I'm a regular. That "2331 S Atlantic Blvd" line makes this feel like my Walgreens, not some generic website.
- **Search bar prominent** — easy to find, and at my font size on a phone it's readable.

## What's weak

- **"GLP-1s for Weight Loss" tile** — I don't know what a GLP-1 is without Googling it. Just say "weight loss medication" or "Ozempic alternatives" if that's what they mean. The jargon puts me off.
- **Spring promo image** — hair, skin and nails gummies, a perfume bottle, and a gift bag. None of that is why I came here. It feels aimed at a younger woman doing Mother's Day shopping, not me picking up my blood pressure medication.
- **No loyalty / MyWalgreens points callout above the fold** — that's the whole reason I stay loyal to this chain, and I don't see a single mention of my points balance or a member-only deal in this first screen.
- **Promo codes require typing** — SPRING15 and SPRING20 are codes I have to remember to type at checkout. That's easy to forget, and I'm not great about copying codes on my phone.
- **No personalized greeting or prescription-ready notification** — if my refill is ready, tell me right here at the top. That would make me feel taken care of.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Your refill is ready" or "Rx status" strip** just below the store address line — one tap, tells me if my prescription is waiting. That's the conversion moment I actually come for.
- **Replace the spring gummy/perfume hero image with a lifestyle scene of a woman my age** — maybe picking up a prescription bag, walking in the store. The current image reads "25-year-old beauty haul," not "trusted neighborhood pharmacy."
- **Surface my MyWalgreens points balance** in the header or right below the account icon — even just "You have 2,450 points" would keep me engaged and shopping.
- **Rename "GLP-1s for Weight Loss" to something plain** like "Weight loss medications" — I shouldn't need a medical degree to read a pharmacy homepage.
