## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick-action tile:** This is the number-one reason I open Walgreens on my phone. Having it right there in the top row — with a little prescription-pad icon — means I don't have to hunt through a menu.
- **"Schedule vaccinations" tile:** At my age I pay attention to flu shots, shingles boosters, all of it. Glad it's easy to find.
- **Mother's Day hero with the spring sale:** The lavender background, the "MOM" card, the gift bag — it's warm and seasonal. And pairing it with a real dollar-off offer (15–20%) gives me a reason to browse beyond my refill.
- **"50% OFF Everything Photo" banner below the hero:** I always print photos for holidays. Seeing that at a glance is genuinely useful for someone like me.
- **Top address bar showing my store location:** Seeing "2331 S Atlantic Blvd" right up top reassures me I'm connected to my store. That matters when I'm thinking about in-store pickup.

## What's weak

- **No MyWalgreens points or rewards balance:** I earn points on every purchase and I want to know if I have enough to redeem. A little "You have 3,200 points" line would make me feel like a valued regular instead of a stranger.
- **Asterisk on "sitewide\*" with no explanation in view:** The fine print isn't visible in the screen I can see, so I'm left wondering what's excluded. That skepticism slows me down.
- **GLP-1s for Weight Loss tile feels like it came from a different audience:** Not bad that it's there, but it lands a little clinical and buzzwordy for a homepage that's otherwise friendly and practical.
- **No free-shipping threshold or delivery mention above the fold:** I walk to the store, but plenty of days I'd rather have things delivered. I have no idea from this screen if that's free or how much I need to spend.
- **No trust signals or review stars on featured products:** The photo prints and fragrance deal tiles at the bottom are bare — just a discount and a product. A star rating or "4,200 reviews" would make me feel more confident tapping through.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the MyWalgreens rewards balance on the homepage banner** — even a one-line "Hi Cleo, you have X points" below the location bar would make returning customers feel seen and give a reason to keep shopping instead of just refilling and leaving.
- **Replace the asterisk-only caveat with an inline note** — something like "excludes pharmacy & select items" in small text right below the offer so I'm not left wondering; surprises at checkout drive me away.
- **Swap the "50% OFF Everything Photo" tile image to show a printed photo collage**, not just a product shot — a picture of grandkids or a family moment would stop someone like me cold and push a tap.
- **Add a free-shipping callout strip** below the hero (e.g., "Free shipping on orders $35+") — one line, no code required — so I know my options before I commit to browsing.
