# Homepage snapshot · www.walgreens.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This looks like my Walgreens, all business and pharmacy-first. The top of the page is pushing vaccines: a COVID notice, a "Get vaccinated and earn $15 Walgreens Cash" banner, and sitewide promo codes. It's a lot of maroon boxes at once, but I can find things, and the "Manage prescriptions" button is right where I'd want it.

## What's working

- **"Manage prescriptions" quick tile:** it's in the row of four buttons under the notice, so I can reach my daily refill in one tap.
- **Vaccine banner with "Schedule now":** it's a big white button on dark maroon and impossible to miss. At my age I want the shots, and the cash reward is a nice extra.
- **Top nav:** Pharmacy, Weekly Ad, Photo and Health Services are all there. Weekly Ad and Photo are two things I use, so I don't have to hunt.
- **Plain wording:** "Relief when you need it" and "Schedule vaccinations" don't try to be cute.
- **Codes shown right in the promo:** NOW5 and NOW10 are printed on the page, so I don't have to dig for them.

## What's weak

- **Nothing tells me my points are here.** The little myW logo doesn't say "You have X points." I shop here for MyWalgreens points and can't tell whether I'm signed in.
- **The top strip and the "$5–10 OFF sitewide" box repeat the same offer.** That's wasted room.
- **The three dots next to the location pin.** I have no idea what they mean and they look like a glitch.
- **No people in any picture.** I see a bandage and product boxes but no one my age.
- **The product pictures are cut off at the bottom.** The "SITEWIDE" text, the vitamin bottles and the Cold-EEZE box are half hidden, so I can't tell what's on offer without scrolling.
- **"GLP-1s for weight loss" sits next to "Manage prescriptions" and gets equal billing.** It's not for me, and it takes up the same space as my refills.
- **The tiny "Feedback" tab on the right edge.** It floats over the page and is easy to bump.

## Recommendations

- Put a "Welcome back, Cleo — you have 1,240 points / Refill ready" strip above the vaccine banner for anyone signed in. Move "Manage prescriptions" out of the small tile row and into a full-width button.
- Delete the repeated offer, either the top strip or the "$5–10 OFF sitewide" box, and give the space to one big weekly-ad or points-on-essentials card.
- Add a photo of a real older customer picking up a prescription or getting a shot at the counter. I'd trust it more than a bandage graphic.
- Put the product pictures fully inside the boxes, and add a plain line under each offer such as "Ends Saturday" so I know how long the deal lasts.
