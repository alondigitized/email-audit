# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- I saw this drop coming — watched the keynote live and already had the color picked out. Apple is doing what Apple does: full-bleed black backdrop, one product, make you feel the launch energy. Right now it's all iPhone 17 Pro, and that Desert Gold / copper-orange finish is doing serious work on my screen. Clean, confident, exactly on-brand for a company that knows its customers are already sold before they load the page.

## What's working

- **The orange.** That Desert Titanium/copper finish filling the bottom half of the hero is the whole vibe. I immediately want to pick it up. It reads limited and luxe without screaming "sale."
- **Dual CTA strategy.** "Learn more" and "Buy" side by side — one for the considerers, one for me. I'm tapping "Buy."
- **"All out Pro." tagline.** Four words, no fluff. It speaks to the millennial who already knows the product line and just wants confirmation that this is the one.
- **Dark hero on a mobile viewport.** It doesn't feel like a website, it feels like a poster. That's the right move for a launch.
- **Second product glimpse below fold.** "iPhone 17 / Magichromatic." peeking below creates natural scroll momentum — I know there's more.

## What's weak

- **No trade-in hook anywhere.** I trade in every cycle and have Apple Card. My estimated trade-in credit on my current iPhone should be front and center — this is easy money for Apple and it's invisible.
- **Zero price anchoring.** Not even a "From $999" or "0% APR with Apple Card." For a product this expensive, I want the payment story before I tap "Buy."
- **No Apple Card 3% badge.** This is their own card, on their own site, and they're not surfacing the biggest loyalty incentive they have.
- **No lifestyle context.** Just a product shot. I love the hardware but I'm a creative — show me what I'd actually do with the camera system. A single frame of someone shooting film or editing on-the-go would close me faster.

## Recommendations

- **Add trade-in CTA inline:** A small pill under "Buy" — "Get up to $XXX for your current iPhone" — directly targets my renewal cycle and makes the price feel lower instantly.
- **Surface Apple Card 3% cashback:** Add a one-liner below the CTA buttons: "3% Daily Cash with Apple Card." It's a differentiator no other brand can match and it's buried right now.
- **Swap static product shot for a motion loop or lifestyle frame:** Even a 3-second cinemagraph of the camera capturing something cinematic — golden hour, urban detail — would make the creative pro use case concrete without breaking the minimal aesthetic.
- **Inject a payment line:** "From $41.62/mo. for 24 mo. before trade-in†" keeps the page clean while making the price psychologically accessible.
