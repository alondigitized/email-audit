# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is classic Apple — obsessively clean, one message, zero clutter. The M5 MacBook Air is front and center, and honestly, I felt that personally because I'm literally due for an upgrade. That said, it's pure broadcast mode: Apple's talking at me, not with me. No trade-in callout, no Apple Card 3% reminder — nothing that says "we know you."

## What's working

- **"Now supercharged by M5."** — short, punchy, chip-nerd energy without being jargony. That lands for me.
- **The blue MacBook Air colorway** — that colorway choice is not an accident. It's aspirational and on-trend for a creative who replaces on the color drop. Caught my eye immediately.
- **Dual CTA structure ("Learn more" / "Buy")** — respects that some people are still on the fence and some (me, honestly) just want to go straight to configure. Both paths offered without friction.
- **Visual breathing room** — the layout doesn't feel desperate or cluttered. It projects confidence, which matches the brand.

## What's weak

- **Zero Trade-In or Apple Card mention** — I literally budget my upgrades around 3% cash back and Trade-In credit. This is money Apple could use to close me right now, and it's completely absent.
- **No lifestyle presence** — it's a product float on a gradient. No one is using this MacBook. For a creative professional audience, showing the device in context (studio, on location, coffee shop shoot) would resonate far more.
- **iPhone teaser below fold is an afterthought** — "Meet the latest iPhone lineup." with no image above fold means that module is doing nothing for me until I scroll.
- **No personalization signal** — I'm logged into my Apple ID constantly. There's no "Hey, your MacBook Air is two years old — here's your upgrade path" moment anywhere on this page. That data exists. Use it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Trade-In strip directly beneath the hero CTAs** — something like "Get up to $XXX for your MacBook Air with Apple Trade-In." One line, no modal. Closes the loop for loyalty buyers like me immediately.
- **Swap the product float for a lifestyle hero** — show a MacBook Air open on a drafting table or creative workspace, someone's hands on the keyboard mid-project. The product is beautiful; let it live in my world, not a void.
- **Surface Apple Card 3% back** — a small badge or line beneath the "Buy" CTA ("3% Daily Cash with Apple Card") would convert me faster than almost anything else on this page.
- **Add a "Compare models" link** — one tap below the CTAs. Configuring matters to me; if I'm not sure which MacBook Air to buy, that link keeps me in the funnel instead of heading to YouTube.
