# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Apple doing Apple — clean, confident, zero noise. The hero is all iPhone, three models fanned out in that signature color-forward layout that makes me stop and mentally compare Desert Titanium against the pink. Below the fold I can already see a MacBook Neo teaser with the line "Amazing Mac. Surprising price." — which is a rare move for Apple and honestly has my attention even more than the phone section.

## What's working

- **The three-color device spread** — midnight, white/silver, and pink laid out overlapping and fanned — is visually confident and hits me right where I live. I'm immediately doing the mental "which finish" exercise, which is exactly the engagement they want.
- **Dual CTA pattern** — "Learn more" (filled blue, exploratory) + "Shop iPhone" (outlined, transactional) — gives me two entry points without forcing a choice. Low friction.
- **"MacBook Neo / Amazing Mac. Surprising price."** peeking below the fold is a rare affordability signal from Apple and genuinely makes me want to scroll to find out what they're pricing it at.
- **Zero clutter above the fold** — nav is four icons (logo, search, bag, hamburger), headline is one word, subhead is six words. The restraint is on-brand and does not exhaust me.

## What's weak

- **No Trade-In hook anywhere visible** — I trade in every cycle and Apple knows this. The homepage misses an obvious "See your iPhone's value" or "Get up to $XXX toward a new iPhone" moment that would speak directly to me.
- **No Apple Card or financing copy** — 3% daily cash and the Monthly Installment Plan are genuinely my reasons to buy here over anywhere else. Not even a ghost of it above the fold.
- **"Learn more" button is doing unclear work** — learn more about what? The lineup I can already see? Next to "Shop iPhone" it reads as the weaker of two CTAs without telling me what differentiated content I'd get.
- **No social proof or urgency of any kind** — no "new colors just dropped," no limited availability, no "carrier deals starting at," no review count. For a device category where people agonize for weeks, a single nudge toward FOMO would help.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Trade-In value estimator as a persistent banner** — even a single line like "Get up to $400 toward iPhone 16 — see your value" would speak directly to upgrade-cycle buyers like me and create an immediate reason to tap.
- **Add an Apple Card / financing callout below the CTAs** — "From $XX/mo with Apple Card. No interest. No fees." is a three-second conversion argument that I know is true, I just can't see it here.
- **Rename "Learn more" to "Compare models"** — that's what I actually want to do when I open the lineup page. More specific copy, same tap, meaningfully higher click confidence.
- **Pull the MacBook Neo section above the existing break** — "Amazing Mac. Surprising price." is the most intriguing copy on this page and it's buried below the fold. For a brand known for premium pricing, leading with a value signal is news worth surfacing immediately.
