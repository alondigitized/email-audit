# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Apple is in full Mother's Day mode right now, and the homepage makes that impossible to miss — floral Apple logo, warm color palette, gift-finder energy. As someone who comes to apple.com to spec out my next M-chip upgrade or see what dropped at the latest event, this feels like I walked into a Hallmark store instead of a tech showroom. Clean and beautifully executed as always, but it's not here for me today.

## What's working

- **The Mother's Day illustration** — the floral Apple logo in warm coral, pink, and yellow is genuinely beautiful. Apple's illustration language is so distinctive that even a gift campaign looks like a design portfolio piece to me.
- **The "Shop" CTA button** — blue pill button on a light background, center-weighted. Impossible to miss, no ambiguity about what to tap.
- **The iPhone tease at the bottom** — "iPhone / Meet the latest iPhone lineup." starting to peek below the fold gives me a reason to scroll even if the hero isn't for me. Smart scroll hook.
- **Clean mobile nav** — search, bag, hamburger in the header. No clutter. Apple knows I already know where things are.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-customer recognition** — I have an Apple Card, an active Trade-In cycle, and AirPods on my wish list. None of that is acknowledged. The page treats me like a first-time visitor in December.
- **No hardware featured above the fold** — as someone who watches keynotes live, I want to see what's new or what dropped. A Mother's Day campaign that surfaces zero product specs above the fold misses my intent entirely.
- **"It's not too late" urgency is soft** — no deadline, no countdown, no date. "Not too late" for what, exactly? Sunday? Tuesday? I live by calendar invites; give me a date.
- **No Apple Card / financing hook** — I get 3% back on every Apple purchase. That's a genuine differentiator and it's nowhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add an "Also for you" scroll strip or a persistent banner** showing the 3% Apple Card Daily Cash or the current Trade-In value for iPhone/MacBook — one line at the top of the page for logged-in users would do it.
- **Surface the deadline explicitly** — swap "It's not too late" for "Arrives by Mother's Day if you order by May 11" with a concrete date. That's actionable and I'd trust it.
- **Run a second hero card in rotation** — alternate the Mother's Day hero with a product-led card ("Designed to create. MacBook Pro with M4.") so visitors who aren't in gift-buying mode get an immediate on-ramp.
- **Test a "New this season" shortcut rail** just below the hero — three chips: iPhone / Mac / iPad — so I can bypass the gift funnel in one tap.
