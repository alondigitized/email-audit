# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I opened apple.com expecting to see the new iPhone lineup or maybe a push toward the upcoming hardware drop — and instead I get Formula 1. Apple TV is making a big swing on live sports, which I get strategically, but this is the entire homepage hero real estate on the day I showed up to browse. It's a confident, clean brand execution as always, but it's clearly not talking to me right now.
- **4/10**

## What's working

- **F1 x Apple TV co-branding** — The red F1 wordmark against Apple's usual gray/white palette is actually striking. Apple rarely lets outside brands this loud onto their homepage, so it signals this partnership is real, not just a banner ad.
- **Date/time specificity** — "May 3, starting at 1 p.m. PST" is clear and urgent. No vague "coming soon" — if you're an F1 fan, you know exactly what to do.
- **Pause button** — There's an animation or video running behind this. I appreciate that Apple gives you control to pause it rather than forcing motion on you. That's good accessibility UX.
- **iPhone peek below the fold** — The "Meet the latest iPhone lineup" copy is just barely visible at the bottom, which gives me hope that the hardware content I actually came for is one scroll away.

## What's weak

- **Zero hardware urgency above the fold** — I'm an Apple Card holder who trades in every cycle. There's no "3% Daily Cash," no trade-in value callout, no new model teaser — nothing that speaks to why I specifically opened this app today.
- **The hero image is mostly empty gray space** — Above the F1/Apple TV lockup there's a large dead zone. If this is meant to be a video hero, it's not loading anything visually compelling in this viewport — just logos floating on a pale background.
- **"Learn more" is the CTA** — Apple defaults to this constantly, and it's the softest possible call to action. For a live sports event with a hard date, "Watch Now" or "Add to Calendar" would actually convert intent.
- **No personalization whatsoever** — I'm logged into my Apple ID. Apple knows I have an M-chip MacBook, AirPods, an Apple Watch. The homepage treats me like a stranger walking off the street.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the F1 hero for a hardware module for non-sports-intent visitors** — If Apple TV has the rights to serve this to F1 fans, great, but the .com homepage should be smarter about who's showing up to shop hardware vs. stream sports.
- **Surface a trade-in/Apple Card banner** — Even a slim strip below the nav saying "Get 3% back with Apple Card + up to $XXX trade-in credit" would immediately activate someone like me.
- **Change "Learn more" to "Watch Live — May 3"** — Make the urgency part of the button, not just the subhead copy.
- **Fill the dead gray zone** — Either let the video/animation actually load something visual in that upper half, or replace it with a product shot so the screen doesn't look half-broken.
