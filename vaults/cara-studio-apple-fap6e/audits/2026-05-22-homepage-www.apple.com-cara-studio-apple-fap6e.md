# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is peak Apple — clean, minimal, confident. The homepage is pushing the current iPhone lineup hard with a gorgeous three-phone colorway shot, and I can see the MacBook Neo just starting to load below. For someone like me who already lives inside the Apple ecosystem, this feels like walking into a store I already know — aspirational but familiar, zero clutter, total focus on the product.

## What's working

- **Three-color iPhone lineup shot:** The indigo / white / blush pink spread is doing serious work — it's the color-drop moment I live for and it stops the scroll immediately.
- **Dual CTA structure:** Giving me both "Learn more" and "Shop iPhone" at the same level is smart. I'm an informed buyer — I want the direct path, and "Shop iPhone" lets me skip the editorial.
- **Typographic hierarchy:** "iPhone" in large, confident serif weight, sub-headline below, then CTAs — eye travels exactly where Apple wants it.
- **MacBook Neo peek:** Just the headline "MacBook Neo / Amazing Mac. Surprising price." teasing below the fold is a genuine scroll trigger for me — I want to know that price.
- **No noise:** No cookie banners, no chat overlays, no modal interruptions. As someone who's used this site a hundred times, I appreciate the respect.

## What's weak

- **Zero trade-in or Apple Card hook above the fold:** I literally use Apple Card for 3% back on Apple purchases. That should be front and center for a returning power user — it's nowhere.
- **No pricing or offer signal on the iPhone:** Not even "From $799" or "Save up to $X with trade-in." Every other premium phone maker leads with this now. The confidence play lands, but I'm left doing math in my head.
- **Product images are static, no color selection hint:** I can see three colors but there's no interactive color-swatch signal — I don't know if I can configure here or if I need to tap through.
- **No personalization:** I'm presumably logged into my Apple ID on this phone. There's zero "Hi Cara, your iPhone 15 Pro is eligible for upgrade" or recently-viewed signal. Apple has this data.
- **"Learn more" CTA is vague:** Paired with "Shop iPhone," the "Learn more" button feels like filler. For a tech-literate user who watched the keynote live, I already know the specs. This button slot could be "Compare models" — something I'd actually use.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Apple Card 3% + trade-in value above the fold:** Add a small strip or badge beneath the iPhone CTAs — "Get 3% Daily Cash with Apple Card. Trade in your iPhone 15 Pro for up to $X." This is a direct conversion lever for loyal buyers and Apple already owns the data.
- **Swap "Learn more" for "Compare models":** "Learn more" wastes prime CTA real estate on an audience that's already Apple-literate. "Compare models" would drive decision-making and reduce time to cart.
- **Add a subtle color-selection affordance to the hero image:** A row of three color dots beneath the phone lineup could signal interactivity and get fingers tapping before the shop page loads.
- **Surface a personalized upgrade nudge for logged-in users:** Even a one-line banner — "Your iPhone 15 Pro qualifies for trade-in" — would convert returning buyers faster than any promo.
