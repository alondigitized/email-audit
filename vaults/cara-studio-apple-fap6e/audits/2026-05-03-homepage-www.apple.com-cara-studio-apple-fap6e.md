# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**

- Apple's homepage is currently leading with an F1 x Apple TV collab for the Miami Grand Prix — live streaming, today, 1 p.m. PST. It's a bold, timely content push, but as someone who comes here to shop hardware, this feels like a detour. Clean as always, but the hero is doing Apple TV's job, not mine.
- **4/10**
- + Visual hierarchy is clear — the F1 logo / Apple TV lockup, headline, subhead, CTA land in clean order
- + No render bugs — page renders crisply, no broken images or layout breakage
- + Page reflects current campaign / season — Miami Grand Prix is literally happening today (May 3)
- + One unmistakable primary CTA above the fold — "Learn more" button is visible and styled
- − Hero copy doesn't speak to my persona — F1 streaming is not my shopping focus (hardware, new color drops, Trade-In cycles)
- − No returning-shopper hook — no "recently viewed," loyalty balance, or Apple Card 3% back callout
- − No concrete offer above the fold — no % off, no Trade-In estimate, no financing hook
- − No loyalty or membership benefit visible (Apple Card, Apple One, nothing)
- − Demographic signals don't match — F1 skews male and a different audience than creative urban millennial
- − Offer feels incomplete — "Learn more" is the weakest possible CTA copy for conversion

## What's working

- **F1 x Apple TV timing** — launching this hero the morning of the Miami Grand Prix is genuinely sharp. It's reactive and timely, which is rare for Apple.
- **Typography and layout** — the bold red F1 headline against the light gray background uses Apple's visual language perfectly. No clutter, no noise.
- **CTA button** — the blue "Learn more" pill is visible, high contrast, and unmissable above the fold.
- **iPhone peek below the fold** — "iPhone / Meet the latest iPhone lineup." starting to appear at the very bottom tells me the hardware content is there — just one scroll away.
- **Pause button** — the gray pause circle top-right implies there's an animation running. That's a clean, unobtrusive control.

## What's weak

- **"Learn more" CTA copy** — this is the vaguest possible button text for a live event. "Watch Now" or "Stream Live" would match the urgency of a same-day broadcast.
- **No hardware above the fold** — I came to browse or config a device, and the first thing I see is a streaming event. That's misaligned with my purpose.
- **No Apple Card / Trade-In hook** — as someone who runs Apple Card and trades in every two years, I expect a persistent nudge toward those programs. Zero visibility here.
- **Trademark disclaimer visible above fold** — "The F1 logo and related marks are trademarks of FOL BV." is sitting in prime real estate below the CTA. Legal boilerplate shouldn't be this prominent on mobile.
- **No personalization signals** — this is apple.com, not a random brand. They know my device, my Apple ID, my purchase history. None of that is surfaced here.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Learn more" for "Watch Now — Live at 1 PM"** — inject actual urgency into the CTA copy since the event is literally today. One copy change, ships in minutes.
- **Add a persistent Trade-In or Apple Card strip below the hero** — even a single-line banner ("Trade in your device. Get credit toward the next one.") gives returning Apple shoppers a reason to stay.
- **Push the legal disclaimer to footer** — the FOL BV trademark line is eating the bottom of the hero on mobile. Move it to fine print at the bottom of the page.
- **Personalize the second card** — if I'm logged in (or even just on a known device), surface "Your iPhone 15 Pro → iPhone 16 Pro: see your Trade-In value" as the second module instead of a generic "Meet the latest iPhone lineup."
