# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-03

## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Apple — minimal chrome, confident typography, zero clutter — but the hero isn't talking to me at all. They've handed the entire above-the-fold real estate to an F1 streaming promotion, which reads like a calculated pivot toward a sports-fan audience I don't identify with. I came here thinking about hardware or maybe checking in on a new color drop, and instead I'm getting a racing paddock.
- **6/10**
- + Primary CTA ("Stream now") is visible above the fold — clear pill button, hard to miss
- + Visual hierarchy lands: big red F1 headline → supporting copy → CTA, clean reading path
- + No render bugs — layout is pixel-clean on mobile
- + Campaign is current: Miami Grand Prix is a May event, this is timely promotion
- + Offer feels honest — no dark fine print, trademark disclaimer is standard and appropriately small
- − Hero copy and imagery doesn't speak to urban creative pro / female millennial persona

## What's working

- **The "Stream now" CTA button** — that blue rounded pill is distinctly non-Apple-default, which actually makes it pop against the gray/white background. I tapped it in my head immediately.
- **F1 × Apple TV co-branding** — the lockup is clean and the partnership feels premium. This is Apple saying "we're serious about live sports" and the execution is confident.
- **Timing is right** — Miami Grand Prix in May is correct. Nothing stale here, props for seasonal accuracy.
- **The pause button** — that little gray circle in the corner tells me this is an animated/video hero. Good for engagement if the motion is on-brand.

## What's weak

- **Zero hardware presence above the fold** — I'm an Apple Card holder who upgrades every two years on color drops. The entire reason I visit apple.com is hardware. Nothing here speaks to that.
- **F1 audience mismatch** — The red-on-white racing aesthetic and "Catch all the Miami Grand Prix action" copy is targeting sports fans, not creative professionals. It feels like I landed on the wrong sub-brand.
- **No navigation category strip** — Only a hamburger menu. One of Apple's strengths is their top nav (Mac, iPhone, iPad, Watch…). Hidden behind a hamburger, I have to work to get to my category.
- **iPhone teaser barely visible** — "Meet the latest iPhone lineup" is cropped at the very bottom. That's the section I actually want, buried below a full-screen sports promo.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the product nav** — even on mobile, a single-row horizontally scrollable chip bar (Mac / iPhone / iPad / Watch / AirPods) would let me tap to my category without opening the hamburger. Apple does this on some campaign pages; it should be default here.
- **Swap the full-screen hero for a split or carousel** — let F1 be one card in a three-card rotating hero, with the second card being the current iPhone or MacBook story. F1 fans get their moment; hardware buyers don't bounce.
- **Add a Trade-In / Apple Card callout strip** — even a thin banner ("Get up to $X off with iPhone Trade-In · Apple Card 3% Daily Cash") below the hero would activate the loyalty buyer in me instantly.
- **Pull the iPhone module up** — "Meet the latest iPhone lineup" should not require scrolling past a full-screen F1 takeover. Move it into the hero rotation or directly below the fold break.
