# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is peak Apple — minimal, confident, no fluff. The homepage is basically "here's the iPhone lineup, go shop it" and as someone who watched the keynote live the day these dropped, I respect the restraint. It's a brand that knows I already know what I want. What it's pushing right now is clearly the iPhone as lead product with a MacBook Air M5 tease just below the fold.

## What's working

- **Dual CTA structure ("Learn more" / "Shop iPhone"):** Perfectly maps my two modes — I'm either in research mode or I already know and I'm buying. Apple got that right.
- **Three-phone hero shot:** Showing the dark blue, white, and pink colorways together in one image is doing real selling work. I immediately clock which one I'd want (white, obviously).
- **"Meet the latest iPhone lineup" subhead:** Understated and confident — speaks to someone who's already Apple-fluent, which I am. No over-explaining.
- **Clean nav with bag icon:** Minimal global nav keeps my focus on the product. Search icon means I can self-serve if I want to jump to AirPods or Watch.
- **MacBook Air M5 tease just below fold:** Smart sequencing — if iPhone isn't my reason today, M5 Air catches me on the scroll.

## What's weak

- **Zero Trade-In callout:** I literally trade in every cycle with Apple Card and there's no hook for that behavior anywhere above the fold. That's leaving conversion on the table for someone like me.
- **No Apple Card 3% back mention:** This is the single biggest loyalty signal Apple has and it's invisible. I came in pre-sold and I still want the reminder.
- **No pricing or financing anchor:** Even "From $799" or "Pay monthly with Apple Card" would compress my decision time. The page treats me like money is no object when actually the monthly payment framing matters.
- **Product-only hero, no lifestyle:** I'm an urban creative — I want to see this phone in a context that reflects my life. Three phones floating on white is elegant but cold.
- **No reviews or social proof:** I know the product is good but a "4.9 stars, 50K reviews" badge would still move me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Trade-In / Apple Card financing strip directly under the dual CTAs** — "Trade in your current iPhone. Get up to $XXX off." That's a one-line change that targets the upgrade-cycle customer I am.
- **Swap the floating-product hero for a lifestyle hero on desktop breakpoints** — an urban creative using this phone in a real environment. The three-phone colorway grid could live one scroll below.
- **Surface "From $XXX/month with Apple Card Monthly Installments"** under the Shop CTA — anchor the price without discounting, which Apple would never do anyway.
- **Add an Apple Card badge or "3% Daily Cash" callout** somewhere above the fold — I have the card, I want the dopamine hit of knowing I'm earning on this purchase before I tap Shop.
