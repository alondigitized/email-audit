# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is pure hype mode — Apple dropped WWDC26 front and center, and as someone who literally blocks her calendar for the live stream, I respect it. The hero is cinematic: that glowing logo on deep space black is doing its thing. But this is event marketing, not a shopping page — there's zero purchase path above the fold, and my Apple Card isn't getting any action here.

## What's working

- **WWDC26 hero timing** — launching this as the homepage the day before the conference is genuinely smart; for power users like me it signals Apple knows their audience
- **"Add to calendar" CTA** — low-friction, no-commitment action that still deepens brand engagement; I'd tap it immediately
- **Visual design** — the gradient-lit Apple logo against near-black is the most on-brand possible execution; it looks like a poster I'd want on my wall
- **iPhone 17 Pro tease below fold** — "All out Pro." starting to appear at the bottom creates curiosity scroll without being pushy

## What's weak

- **Zero shopping path above fold** — I came here to check out the iPhone 17 Pro and configure a new machine; the homepage is telling me to RSVP to an event instead
- **No returning-customer recognition** — Apple has my purchase history, my trade-in eligibility, my Apple Card — none of that is surfaced
- **No person in the hero** — the WWDC logo is abstract and beautiful but I can't see myself in it; a creative at a desk with AirPods in would land better for the consumer side
- **No navigation categories visible** — the hamburger menu hides everything; someone less Apple-literate would have zero idea what products this company even sells

## Recommendations

- **Add a sticky top banner with a product CTA** — a one-line strip like "iPhone 17 Pro — Shop now, pick up today" sits above the WWDC hero without disrupting it; keeps the shopper path alive
- **Surface Apple Card / trade-in hook for logged-in users** — even a small "You're eligible for $X trade-in credit" chip in the nav would be enough to flip this from event page to conversion opportunity
- **Replace the generic hamburger with an icon-labeled nav bar** — even three icons (iPhone, Mac, more) would reduce cognitive load and let me jump to my category without hunting
- **Add a second hero card below WWDC** — a two-card layout showing "WWDC26" + "iPhone 17 Pro" side-by-side lets the event and the commerce coexist without one cannibalizing the other
