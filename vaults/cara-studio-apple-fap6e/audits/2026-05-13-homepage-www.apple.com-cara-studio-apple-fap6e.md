# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Apple in peak confidence mode — minimal copy, gorgeous product photography, zero clutter. The homepage is leading with the iPhone lineup and already teasing the M5 MacBook Air below, which tells me they're in a hardware refresh cycle and leaning into it hard. For me, this is practically a personalized homepage — I live inside this ecosystem, I watch every keynote, and the color options on display are doing exactly what Apple wants them to do.

## What's working

- **Three-color product display** — showing desert titanium, white, and pink side by side is subtle but effective. It's not a spec sheet, it's a vibe selector, and I'm immediately mentally trying on the pink one.
- **Dual-CTA pattern** — "Learn more" for the curious vs. "Shop iPhone" for the ready-to-buy is a smart split that doesn't force commitment. I can research first, convert later.
- **"Meet the latest iPhone lineup"** — the copy is clean and confident. No superlatives, no desperation. That restraint signals premium.
- **MacBook Air teaser below the fold** — the M5 line appearing just below the iPhone hero gives me a reason to scroll. It's a breadcrumb and it works.
- **Minimal nav** — search, bag, and hamburger menu only. No dropdown clutter on mobile. Every pixel is the product.

## What's weak

- **Zero trade-in or upgrade hook above the fold** — I'm literally their target for the iPhone Upgrade Program and Apple Card 3% back, and neither is mentioned. This is a miss for a returning customer.
- **No lifestyle context** — it's a product shot against a light background. Beautiful, but nothing showing the iPhone in a studio, on a shoot, clipped to an iPad workflow. For a creative pro, showing the device in use matters.
- **No pricing signal whatsoever** — not even a "starting at $X" or "from $X/mo." For a considered purchase like this, even a soft anchor helps conversion.
- **"Learn more" is doing too much work** — that button is vague. Learn more about what? The new camera? The chip? Color options? It's a wasted CTA slot that could say "Explore colors" or "See what's new."

## Recommendations

- **Add an Apple Card 3% / Upgrade Program badge directly beneath the "Shop iPhone" CTA** — "Get 3% Daily Cash with Apple Card" is a one-liner that meaningfully changes the decision for existing ecosystem members like me.
- **Swap "Learn more" for something specific** — "Explore iPhone 16 Pro" or "See the camera system" gives it a destination instead of a dead label.
- **Inject a trade-in value estimator teaser** — "Your iPhone 15 Pro could be worth up to $X toward a new one" right below the CTA row. This is the hook that actually moves upgrade-cycle shoppers.
- **Add one lifestyle shot in the rotation** — a creative at a laptop with iPhone propped on a stand, or pulling a shot off iPhone into Final Cut. Remind me this is a tool, not just an object.
