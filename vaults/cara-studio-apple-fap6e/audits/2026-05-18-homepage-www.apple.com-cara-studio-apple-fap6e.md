# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Apple — minimal, confident, zero clutter. The homepage is leading hard with the M5 MacBook Air right now, which feels fresh and current. It's the kind of product drop I actually wait for, and seeing it front-and-center with two clean CTAs tells me exactly where Apple wants me to go.

## What's working

- **M5 callout in the tagline** — "Now supercharged by M5" is exactly the three-word spec drop I need to know it's new. I don't have to dig.
- **Dual CTAs side-by-side** — "Learn more" vs. "Buy" respects where I am in the journey. I can research or I can just buy. That's smart.
- **Product visual** — the laptop shown open at an angle with what looks like a blue/sky colorway is beautiful. I immediately want to configure that color. The device is the hero, not a lifestyle scene, and for a hardware launch that's the right call.
- **iPhone section loading in below** — even just the headline peeking in signals there's more to scroll, it doesn't feel like a one-card dead end.
- **Minimal nav** — search, bag, and hamburger only. No nav bar fighting for attention on mobile. Clean.

## What's weak

- **No trade-in hook anywhere** — I trade in every cycle on the Apple Card. A "Your current device could be worth $X" or even "Trade in and save" callout above the fold would hit me directly. It's absent.
- **No Apple Card 3% callout** — this is free money Apple is leaving on the table. I'm already an Apple Card holder and seeing "3% Daily Cash back with Apple Card" would reduce friction to zero.
- **No color variant display** — I buy on the new color drop. If there's a new color on the M5 Air, I can't tell from this shot. Showing multiple colorways would convert me faster.
- **"Learn more" CTA is generic** — every Apple product has a "Learn more." It doesn't tell me what specifically I'm learning. "See what's new" or "Explore M5" would feel more purposeful.
- **No pricing anchor** — even "Starting at $1,099" would give me a quick gut-check. Sending me to click before I know the price range adds a small friction that Apple can afford to eliminate.

## Recommendations

- **Add a trade-in teaser line** under the two CTAs: "Trade in your Mac. Get up to $XXX toward a new one." One line, no design overhaul needed.
- **Show the color lineup** — display 3–4 color swatches as small dots or a side-by-side thumbnail strip below the product image. Color is a top purchase driver for my demographic.
- **Swap "Learn more" for a spec-flavored CTA** — "Explore M5 performance" or "See the new colors" is specific and creates urgency without pressure.
- **Surface Apple Card cashback** — a small badge or one-liner ("3% back with Apple Card") sits naturally between the CTAs and the product image without cluttering the hero.
