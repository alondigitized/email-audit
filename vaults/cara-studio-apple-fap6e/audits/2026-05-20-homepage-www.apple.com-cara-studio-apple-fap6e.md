# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the Apple I know — minimal, confident, zero noise. They're leading with what looks like a brand-new MacBook called "MacBook Neo," and the lime-gradient lid and the "Surprising price" tagline tell me this is a launch moment they want me to pay attention to. As someone who watches every keynote live and is basically already pre-sold on whatever M-chip comes next, this feels like home — but it does absolutely nothing to close the deal on the spot.

## What's working

- **"MacBook Neo" as the hero product** — launching a new Mac name on the homepage is big energy and I noticed immediately
- **The lime/yellow color of the MacBook lid** — that colorway in the product shot is the whole pitch; it says "new drop" without a word of copy
- **Dual CTAs ("Learn more" + "Buy")** — respects that some people are explorers and some are ready to transact; I don't feel forced
- **Dead-simple layout** — logo, search, bag, menu; Apple trusts its brand to do the work and it does
- **"Amazing Mac. Surprising price."** — punchy two-beat rhythm, feels like it was written for someone who already knows what a Mac is

## What's weak

- **"Surprising price" is a tease, not an offer** — no number, no trade-in estimate, no financing CTA; as someone who does Apple Card + Trade-In every cycle, this is the thing that would actually move me and it's invisible
- **Zero trade-in hook above the fold** — I have a MacBook I'd swap right now if the math was shown to me; the homepage doesn't even hint that's an option
- **No Apple Card 3% back callout** — I literally get 3% back on Apple purchases and there's nothing here reminding me that buying direct is a financial win
- **Hand holding the MacBook is the only human presence** — there's no lifestyle context, no studio, no creative workflow; as a creative I want to see it in use, not floating on a white background
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — one more tap to get to anything; feels fine on Apple's brand equity alone, but there's no shortcut to "MacBook" or "Trade In" for someone ready to move

## Recommendations

- **Show the trade-in number or at least the CTA:** "Get up to $XXX for your current Mac" above the fold would convert creative pros who are already in the upgrade cycle — ship a dynamic Trade-In estimator teaser right under the Buy button
- **Add an Apple Card badge to the Buy CTA:** "3% Daily Cash with Apple Card" is a one-liner that turns the Buy button into a financial argument; it already exists in checkout — surface it here
- **Swap the floating-hand product shot for a desk/studio lifestyle still:** show the MacBook Neo open on a minimal studio desk with the colorful lid visible — same product, but now I can picture my own workspace
- **Put one category strip below the fold break:** "iPhone · Mac · iPad · AirPods · Watch" as tappable chips would let me hop to my next product without opening the hamburger
