# Homepage snapshot · www.apple.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Apple's homepage is doing what it always does — minimal, confident, beautifully rendered — but right now it's fully pivoting on Mother's Day gifting mode, which honestly isn't where my head is at when I open apple.com. I'm here to check out the latest lineup or see what's new, not browse gift ideas. That said, the brand polish is impeccable and the iPhone teaser below the fold is the first thing that pulls me back in.

## What's working

- **Festive Apple logo illustration** — the floral, ribbon-wrapped Apple mark is genuinely delightful and on-brand in a way most brands can't pull off; it's Apple being playful without breaking character
- **"It's not too late"** urgency copy — low-pressure but effective; it acknowledges procrastinators without being aggressive
- **Blue "Shop" CTA** — high-contrast against the light background, instantly tappable, no ambiguity that there's an action here
- **Clean nav bar** — search, bag, menu in the top right; I can get anywhere in one tap even though no categories are surfaced directly
- **iPhone teaser below the fold** — just the headline peeking in, but it's enough to make me want to scroll

## What's weak

- **"Shop" copy is too vague** — shop *what*? The CTA doesn't tell me what Mother's Day at Apple looks like — AirPods? Apple Watch? An iPhone? One more word would do a lot of work
- **No product imagery** — the hero is all illustration; there's not a single product shot above the fold, which means I have no idea what to expect when I tap Shop
- **No Apple Card 3% callout** — I'm literally earning 3% back on every Apple purchase with my Apple Card; this should be on the homepage encouraging me to buy directly, but it's nowhere
- **No trade-in hook** — "Trade in your old device and get up to $X toward a new one" is the most powerful conversion lever for a repeat customer like me, and it's completely absent
- **Mother's Day framing might not click for all Millennials** — I'm 29, I may be the *giver* or not involved at all; the page doesn't give me an easy out to "shop for myself instead"

## Recommendations

- **Add a secondary "Or shop for yourself →" link** below the CTA — one small text link that pivots me out of the gifting frame into the product lineup; costs nothing, rescues the self-purchaser
- **Swap the "Shop" button copy for "Find the perfect gift"** or even "Shop Apple gifts" — makes the destination less ambiguous and the offer more tangible without changing the button at all
- **Surface a product thumbnail or two in the hero** — even a single AirPods shot or an Apple Watch wrist would ground the illustration and tell me what's on sale
- **Add a persistent Apple Card badge** — a small "3% back with Apple Card" chip near the CTA is a genuine incentive for loyal customers and takes three pixels of space
