- The red burgundy knit hero photo looks cozy and on-season. Nothing is broken or misaligned.
- The "Gift cards" block at the end is a clean last-minute option.

## What's weak

- This is the ninth sale-themed send in a week. Compared with the 11/24 "Up to 50% off for party people" and "denim deals from $19.99," the offer and the hero are basically the same.
- The best-looking trend pieces, the cargo and cardigan styling, never get a name or price. I can't tell if anything is actually trending.

- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny gray text full of exclusions, which makes the 50% feel less honest.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: H&M "Gifts on gifts: Holiday deals now up to 50% off!"

### 1. Technical Summary
The sampled HTML has missing alt text on 26 images, no `lang` attribute, and an empty schema.org JSON-LD block. The QA run reports 13 links returning 403, but all of them are `t19.email.hm.com/r/` tracking redirects probed by a non-browser client. That points to bot filtering more than to broken links, and I can't confirm either way. The source was truncated, so the body and footer were not fully inspectable.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking redirects returned HTTP 403** to the probe. They include "Shop now", "Find a store", "shop e‑Gift cards", the BOPIS banner link and Unsubscribe. Example: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h9c50f5e7-…,5516da20,f8e020fa&did=1427560992&rid=3326930539&…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`.
- **The 403 is probably bot filtering.** It affects every probed link on the same redirect host, including unsubscribe, so it is uniform and not tied to specific links or recipients.
- **Not verified:** whether these links resolve for real clients, because the final destinations were never reached. Re-test with a browser-grade user agent before calling them broken.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 102 links were probed, and 90 were skipped by priority ranking.
- **Redirect-wrapped links:** every observed link goes through the redirector, so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on 26 images.** Each image appears as a `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pair, which looks like a desktop/mobile swap via `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`. Both variants lack alt, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. Affected sources include `…/wk48/us186_s4/CT1-Gifting-US186-1359326004_20…` and `…/global_campaigns/season_05/women/cocc265p01a/FNP-CFUL271P07-2x3-gift-guide-2026-2.jp…`.
- **Image behind a tracking URL:** the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h9c50f5e7-…,1` image also lacks alt. It is probably the open pixel and should carry `alt=""`.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">` has no `lang` attribute, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` uses `auto`, which isn't a valid `line-height` value, so some clients will drop the declaration.
- **Duplicated rule:** `.set_font_heading_xlarge` and `.cta-link-mobile-border-ns` are declared more than once in the mobile media query. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Font loading:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css)` are stripped by Gmail and some other clients. Whether fallback font stacks exist is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Viewport meta:** `width=device-width` has no `initial-scale=1`. This is minor.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The tracking parameters (`rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) are populated in the observed URLs.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** the QA run did not find either header. It notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured through the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Unsubscribe link:** present (`…f8e020fa…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`), but the probe got a 403, so I couldn't verify its behavior.
- **Physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't evaluate it.
- **Plain-text part:** 512 of 590 characters are URLs, about 87%. The text alternative is almost entirely tracking links, which is a spam-filter risk.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.** The redirector hides the final URLs, and the 403s blocked resolution. Nothing here confirms or rules out UTM loss or landing-page mismatch.
- **Structured data is empty and stale.** The JSON-LD block has `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"image":""` and `"url":""`. Its `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, years out of date. Gmail may ignore this markup or treat it as invalid, so the promo card never gets a rich presentation.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a real-browser user agent, following redirects, to separate bot-blocking from real failures. Confirm the final landing URLs and their UTM parameters.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to all content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and any decorative images.
3. Add `lang="en"` (or the correct locale) to `<html>`.
4. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`. If they are missing, add them with one-click support, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
5. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block. Fill `subjectLine`, `description`, `image` and `url`, and fix the stale `availabilityStarts` date.
6. Fix `line-height:auto` and deduplicate the repeated CSS rules.
7. Rebalance the plain-text part with readable copy, not mostly URLs.
8. Add `initial-scale=1` to the viewport meta.
