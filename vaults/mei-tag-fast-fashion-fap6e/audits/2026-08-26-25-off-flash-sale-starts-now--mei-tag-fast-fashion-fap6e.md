# 25% off flash sale starts now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, 25% off EVERYTHING until midnight ET — that's a real flash sale, not the same "20% off $125" H&M's been running on loop since Weekend Deal on 08-21. Points for actually escalating the offer this time instead of reheating it again.
- But "Last-minute back-to-school shopping? Lesson learned." — girl, who is this for? I'm 24. This copy is aimed at a mom buying a kid's first-day-of-school fit, not me. And the entire layout after the hero is just a category directory — Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Beauty grid, then the SAME six categories again as photo tiles, then AGAIN as a third "Shop By" row. That's three redundant nav modules and zero actual product. No trend callout, no "this is what's flying off shelves," nothing that tells me why 25% off matters today versus any other Tuesday.
- Verdict: I'd screenshot the subject line for the deal itself (25% off everything is genuinely good), but I wouldn't scroll past the hero, and I definitely wouldn't forward this — there's nothing trend-specific to send a friend. Marking as read.

## What's working

- 25% off EVERYTHING with a hard "until midnight ET" deadline is a real, clear, honest offer — no fine print buried up top.
- The red FLASH SALE / bold percentage hero is legible at a glance, no zooming needed.
- Category buttons are big, thumb-friendly, easy to tap on mobile.
- No render bugs — images loaded clean, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- The "back-to-school" preheader is off-target for me — I'm not shopping for a kid, and it makes the email feel like it wasn't written for a 24-year-old at all.
- Triple redundancy: full category grid, then a photo section per category, then a "Shop By" row repeating the exact same six categories again. That's just filler, not trend content.
- Zero actual product callouts — no "this top is trending," no specific item link, nothing to click toward. It's a directory, not a drop.
- No urgency reinforcement below the hero — after the countdown line, the email forgets it's a flash sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut two of the three redundant category sections — keep one clean grid, use the freed space for 3-4 actual trending pieces with the 25% price shown.
- 2. Swap "back-to-school" framing for something that actually speaks to a Gen Z shopper — timer urgency, TikTok-style "everyone's grabbing this before midnight."
- 3. Add a visible countdown or "ends in X hours" element so the urgency doesn't just live in the hero text.
- 4. Link category tiles straight to filtered "on sale" pages, not generic category landing pages.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything ends at midnight — no exclusions`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Flash sale: 25% off, gone by midnight ET`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed. Everything's 25% off till midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The whole site, 25% off — ends tonight`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Marketing email from H&M with a broadly broken click-tracking redirector — 12/12 material links probed return 403 — plus missing unsubscribe headers and 13 images lacking alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed links route through `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h392d5826-...&did=1715451062&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM5981475&p2=...&p3=20260826` and every one of the 12 probed (Shop now ×2, H&M logo, nav: Women, Men, Baby, Kids, HOME, BEAUTY, Women, Men, Unsubscribe) returned **403 Forbidden**.
- This affects primary CTAs ("Shop now") as well as navigation and the unsubscribe link — a 403 on the tracking redirector likely means the entire link infrastructure is unreachable (auth/allowlist/referrer issue on `t19.email.hm.com`), not an isolated bad URL.
- Link text on both "Shop now" CTAs contains zero-width/invisible characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`) — U+200C/U+FEFF artifacts embedded in anchor text, likely from template tokenization gone wrong.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 13 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, including product imagery (`1354570003_202604_LB_1882.jpg`, `1342245001_202604_LB_3229.jpg`, `1349541001_202604_LB_0139.jpg`, `1345310002_202604_LB_1126.jpg`, `2539020_7003c3_0193.jpg`, `2607021_5113pp09_0210.jpg`, each at two resolutions) and two tracking pixel images.
- Extensive `@media` responsive CSS and MSO/Outlook-specific hiding rules are present (`.is-hidden-mobile`, `.is-hidden-desktop`, mso-hide) — no structural issues found in the truncated markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `application/ld+json` schema.org block has multiple empty string fields left unpopulated: `subjectLine`, `availabilityEnds`, `discountCode`, `description`, `url` (PromotionCard) — these appear to be unresolved merge tokens that should have been filled by the ESP before send.
- No other visible personalization/merge-tag artifacts in the truncated body.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present in-markup but returns 403 in the QA probe (see #2) — functionally broken if reproducible outside the audit tool.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be verified; also a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement as of 2024.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found — confirms no RFC 8058 one-click support detected.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data.
- Note: both header warnings are qualified as possibly an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding these headers, not necessarily proof they're absent at origin — flag as needing header-level verification against the raw source (not just this audit).

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use H&M's proprietary redirector query params (`id`, `did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — no UTM scheme to validate for continuity; final landing destinations cannot be assessed since the redirector itself 403s before any redirect target is reached.
- Cannot verify landing-page alignment (promo code, category pages) because the redirect layer is failing.

7. Recommendations
- Priority 1: Investigate the `t19.email.hm.com` redirector 403s — check whether the QA fetcher's User-Agent/referrer is being blocked (common bot-protection false positive) vs. a genuine broken deployment; if genuine, this is a full click-tracking outage affecting every CTA and the unsubscribe link.
- Priority 1: Verify unsubscribe path works from an actual inbox client, independent of the automated probe, given CAN-SPAM functional requirements.
- Priority 2: Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist at the true SMTP source (check with the ESP directly, since AgentMail relay may simply not be surfacing them).
- Priority 2: Fill or strip the empty `ld+json` fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts/Ends`, `description`, `url`) — empty structured data can suppress Gmail promo card rendering.
- Priority 3: Add `alt` text to the 13 flagged product/tracking images for accessibility and broken-image fallback text.
- Priority 3: Strip the zero-width/BOM characters (U+200C, U+FEFF) from "Shop now" anchor text — cosmetic in most clients but can affect screen readers and text-based spam filters.
