# SALE | Today at 9 pm ET online
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is actually the email I've been waiting for from Zara. After getting hit with a full menswear lookbook on June 20 (literally had to double-check I hadn't accidentally subscribed to the men's list — they pulled this same stunt June 13), a children's collection drop on June 14, and a Zara Home editorial for lounge chairs I will never buy — this one finally makes sense in my inbox. The subject is specific, the hero is one clear message, and the urgency is real. I actually glanced at the clock when I saw "9PM ET."
- But I open the email and... that's it. Gorgeous underwater hero, "SALE BEGINS TODAY / ONLINE 9PM ET / STORES 06/25," a nav bar, and a prompt to download the app. No discount range, no product tease, no "here's what drops at 9." Zara showed up to the party just to announce they arrived. Compare that to ASOS dropping "Extra 10% off all Sale" last week with actual mechanics — that email told me exactly what I was walking into before I even clicked.
- I'd open this from my inbox without question. I'd probably end up on the site at 9pm out of FOMO anyway — but the email itself didn't do the work of pulling me there.

## What's working

- The underwater turquoise hero is genuinely stunning — feels current, summery, editorial without trying too hard
- "SALE BEGINS TODAY. ONLINE 9PM ET. STORES 06/25" is shockingly clear for a brand that usually just dumps lookbook grids at me with zero direction
- Subject line is the most concrete thing Zara has sent me in weeks — a specific time creates real urgency, not the vague "explore what's new" energy of the June 19 send
- Clean, minimal layout — no render bugs, no overlapping text, no broken image blocks; the restraint is a choice and it works here
- Timing is right — a summer sale announcement in late June feels season-appropriate and credible

## What's weak

- Zero sale specifics in the email body — no discount range, no "up to X% off," no category callout; ASOS gave me a number ("Extra 10%"), Zara gives me vibes
- The only visible CTA is **"DOWNLOAD OUR APP"** — not "Shop Women's Sale," not "Shop Now" — just an app download ask on what should be the hype email for a major sale launch
- No product, no model — just water; H&M at least showed me visual product on their recent sends even when the overall email was too cluttered
- Fine print block at the bottom is completely unreadable at this render size — if there are exclusions or limits I should know before I build a cart, burying that is a trust issue
- Navigation still has MAN / KIDS / BEAUTY / HOME / MASSIMO DUTTI — I've never clicked those and the nav isn't personalized at all; feels like a stranger's inbox

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a "Shop Women's Sale →" CTA button** directly below the hero text — this is the single biggest miss; there is no clear action in this email beyond navigating away on your own
- 2. **Surface the discount range in the hero** — even "up to 50% off starting 9PM" would give me a reason to come back at the exact right time instead of just "knowing a sale exists"
- 3. **Show 2–3 hero product teasers below the fold** — trend items going into sale builds FOMO before the clock hits; the anticipation is the whole mechanic here and they're not using it
- 4. **Replace "DOWNLOAD OUR APP" with a contextual app push** — "Set a 9PM reminder in the app" or "Get notified the second the sale goes live" actually makes the app ask relevant to this email
- 5. **Trim the nav to WOMAN and BEAUTY** for my segment — I don't need the full site architecture in my sale email
