# Freshly picked & up to 30% off
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Freshly picked & up to 30% off" — and it's basically "fall-coded" from 09-22 wearing a different hat, plus "Weekend deal" from the weekend before that, plus the 09-20 "ends tonight" one. That's four sends in a week hammering the same "up to X% off" markdown format with cardigans and sweatpants. This one at least has kids sections that are legible instead of the softness-and-structure catalog mess from 09-21, but there is nothing here that says trend, nothing says "we called this first," it's just a knitwear/loungewear liquidation sale with a "you need these" section that reads like generic merchandising copy, not a drop.
- The hero girl in the cream cardigan is fine but she's basically the same styling as the maroon wide-leg girl from Tuesday — cozy neutral top, styled loose. Zara's "ZARA TIMELESS" family-nostalgia email was worse (a 3 for me), and ASOS's Lioness drop with the camo cargo culottes and cobblestone strut is still the only email in my inbox this month that felt like someone actually thought about what I'd want to wear out. This is the opposite — cardigans, hoodies, leggings, laid out like a JCPenney circular.
- I'm not clicking this one. It's not offensive, it's just background noise — I've already seen this offer shape three times this week and my thumb is trained to scroll past "UP TO X% OFF SELECT STYLES" from H&M at this point. Marking as read, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 30% OFF SELECT STYLES" hero is bold, black, unmissable — no hunting for the offer.
- Prices with strikethrough originals are visible under every product tile, so the math isn't hidden.
- Category buttons (Women/Men/Baby/Kids) repeat twice, so navigating by department is frictionless if I actually wanted to shop.
- Grid layout is clean, no overlapping text or broken images this time — a real improvement over some of H&M's messier sends.

## What's weak

- Zero trend language — no "TikTok," no "everyone's wearing," no "sold out restock." Just "your fall wardrobe in a nutshell." That's H&M chasing a season, not a trend.
- This is the fourth near-identical percent-off promo from H&M in about a week (09-19, 09-20, 09-22, now this) — cadence fatigue is real and I'm tuning it out.
- "You need these" section is presumptuous generic copy, not personalized — it's the same sweatpants/hoodie combo everyone gets.
- No urgency anywhere — no "ends tonight," no countdown, so there's zero reason to act today vs. next week.
- Kids sections keep getting jammed into what should be a Gen Z fashion email, same complaint I had about 09-21.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the generic "up to 30% off" framing and lead with an actual trend hook — tell me this cardigan/sweatpants combo is the layered look blowing up right now, the way ASOS framed Lioness as a moodboard, not a markdown.
- 2. Split kids/baby into their own send — it's diluting the fashion-forward positioning every single time and it's the same note I left on 09-21.
- 3. Add a real deadline. No urgency = no reason to open this over the next promo.
- 4. Rotate the hero model/styling — this cream cardigan look is one degree off the maroon wide-leg hero from Tuesday, and repeat casting reads as "same email, new day."
