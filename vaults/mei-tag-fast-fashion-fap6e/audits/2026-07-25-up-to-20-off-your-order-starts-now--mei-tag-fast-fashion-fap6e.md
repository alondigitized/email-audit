# Up to 20% off your order starts now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, four days in a row now — H&M hit me with "Today only: 15% off sitewide" on the 22nd, "New arrivals: Back to school" on the 23rd, bedding on the 24th, and now THIS on the 25th: "Weekend Deal, 15% off $60 or 20% off $80." I'm getting sitewide-discount déjà vu. This is basically the same beat as the July 22nd email just wearing a different hat, and my brain has started to tune it out the way you tune out a roommate who says "we should hang out more" every week and never means it.
- The layout itself is clean — I'll give them that, it's not fighting me visually — but there's nothing here that says "trend." No callout to a specific drop, no "as seen on TikTok" energy, just a tiered discount and a wall of category tiles (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Beauty). Compare that to ASOS's "Fringe is in" email, which went full editorial with zero discount code and still scored a 9 with me because it felt like they were showing me the NEXT thing. This is H&M showing me the SAME thing, again.
- Verdict: I'm not clicking this one. I might glance at Women since the tile's right there, but the "spend $60/$80 to unlock" math combined with the fine-print novel at the bottom (no sale items, no Final Sale returns, max one $5 reward, can't combine) makes it feel like a discount with an asterisk the size of a Birkenstock. Not forwarding it — this is the kind of email you forward when it's the GOOD H&M email, like the Parisian tableware one from the 21st, not this one.

## What's working

- The offer is at least stated in real numbers — "15% off $60 or 20% off $80" — no vague "big savings" nonsense.
- Category tiles make it fast to jump straight to Women if I actually wanted to shop, no forced scroll-through.
- The models feel like an actual size/diversity range across tiles — the Women shot and Beauty close-up both look like people I'd see on my FYP, not stock-photo energy.
- Clean, on-brand black-and-white H&M layout — no render bugs, nothing broken, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- Zero trend language. No "new in," no "as seen," no styling story — just a percentage. That's the exact gap between this and ASOS's Fringe email or even H&M's own Parisian tableware send.
- This is the SAME "% off sitewide" beat H&M already ran on the 22nd, three days ago. Cadence fatigue is real — my inbox is not a clearance rack.
- The spend threshold ($60/$80) plus the fine print (no sale items, no combining, Final Sale exclusions, one $5 reward max) makes the "up to 20% off" headline feel bigger than what I'd actually get.
- No urgency that actually shows up in the visible email — the deadline (7/26) is buried in fine print, not in the hero, so it doesn't feel like an actual weekend countdown even though it's basically over by tomorrow.
- Beauty tile is one moody close-up crop with zero context — I can't tell if that's skincare, makeup, or a fragrance push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with an actual product or trend moment instead of a bare discount tier — even one hero look with "styled 3 ways" beats six generic category boxes. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Put the real deadline in the hero, not just the fine print — "Ends Sunday night" next to the discount would create actual urgency instead of implied urgency.
- 3. Simplify the offer to one number instead of a two-tier spend threshold — the $60/$80 split reads like a puzzle, not a deal.
- 4. Cut the sitewide-discount cadence — after 7/22's near-identical email, this needed to be a different kind of send (styling content, restock, trend roundup) to not feel repetitive.
- 5. Give Beauty an actual product shot with a name/price, not just a mood crop.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your weekend cart just got 20% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New early-fall pieces, no fine print required`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop Women, Men, Kids & more before it ends Sunday`
