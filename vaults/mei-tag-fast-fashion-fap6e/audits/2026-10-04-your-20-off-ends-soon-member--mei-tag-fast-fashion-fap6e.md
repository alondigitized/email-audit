# Your 20% off ends soon, Member
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, H&M, it's been four days of the same "up to 30% off fall essentials" banner. I opened this one because the subject said my 20% was ending, and the email wasn't built around that. The banner on top still shouts 30% off in stores. Then the hero says "20% OFF FOR MEMBERS" with no date anywhere I can see. The only clock is the line "The clock is ticking," which tells me nothing. The actual deadline is buried in the fine print at the bottom (10/04/2026, which is today).
- The photos are good. The dark quilted jacket and the leather and shearling looks are very "I'm already wearing this." But it's the same leather-coat, bomber and chore-coat story as The Outerwear Edit on 10/1. It reads like a recycled campaign with a different discount on top, not a new drop. Zara's "Re{form}" send at least felt like it owned a moment. This one feels like H&M chasing its own tail.
- Would I click? Maybe SHOP NOW, since I'm a member and 20% is 20%. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and open ASOS to see if anything's actually new.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF FOR MEMBERS" headline is big and clear, and it's the first thing my eye hits.
- The hero model in the dark quilted jacket is on-trend for fall, and the styling feels like something I'd screenshot.
- The women, men and kids split gives me quick paths to what I want, and the Women tile is easy to tap.
- The "Shop by" grid with beauty and home is a nice browse option if I'm bored.

## What's weak

- The deadline isn't visible. The subject says "ends soon," but the body never gives a date or time, and the real date is in microscopic fine print.
- Two offers are stacked: up to 30% off in the banner and 20% for members in the hero. I don't know which one applies to me.
- This is the same banner I've seen since 9/30. The 10/3 "20% off all weekend for Members" was nearly identical, so the urgency feels fake.
- The CTAs are generic. SHOP NOW and JOIN NOW sit side by side, and as a member I don't need a JOIN button. The copy never names the jacket I'm looking at.
- The fine print is a huge wall of text, and the exclusions (no sale items, no collabs) make the 20% feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "20% off for Members. Ends tonight, 11:59 PM ET." Drop the old 30% banner or move it below the hero.
- 2. Replace JOIN NOW with a member-aware CTA like "Shop your 20% off," and link the hero image to the quilted jacket or a layering edit.
- 3. Don't reuse the 10/1 outerwear creative. Show one new item or a styled try-on that feels like a trend moment, not a repeat.
- 4. Add a price or a "member price" tag on one or two items so I can see the value.
- 5. Cut the exclusions down to one short line and link out to the details.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bombers, leather and chore coats. Member price applies at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: H&M, "Your 20% off ends soon, Member"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is built from a table-based template with a mobile media query and a desktop/mobile image pair. Every probed link returned 403 from the click-tracker domain, and the JSON-LD schema block is almost entirely empty. The 403s look like probe blocking rather than real breakage, so the destinations could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403** on `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h8d2b2dfe-…&did=1418603346&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6105495&p2=…&p3=20261004`. This covers "Shop now", "Join now", the hero, the logo ("H&M"), the image links and "Unsubscribe".
- **Likely cause: the tracker is blocking the probe.** All 12 URLs share the same host and pattern, and the unsubscribe link fails the same way. It is not confirmed as a user-facing failure. Real recipients may resolve these links normally.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 68 links were probed and 56 were skipped, so the footer, social and secondary links are unverified.
- **Redirect-only links:** every link is wrapped in the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirector, so the final destinations are not visible in the source or QA data.
- **Bad link text:** several links use a bare image filename as text, for example `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`. One link text begins with zero-width characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`). That suggests invisible spacer characters inside the anchor text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images.** Ten are the `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-40/…` and `wk-41/…`. The other two are on the tracked `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` links. These images are also wrapped in links, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get an unlabeled link. The fallback text is the filename.
- **Duplicate image pairs (1400px and 750px)** for each tile. This is probably a desktop/mobile swap using `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop`. That adds image weight and doubles the number of alt-less images and links.
- **`@import` font stylesheets** in `<style>` (`fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css`). Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip `@import`, so web fonts will fall back there. This is expected behavior, not a defect.
- **Plain-text part is 518/596 characters URLs (87%)**, so the text alternative is nearly all tracking links and gives little readable content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The subject renders as "…ends soon, **Member**". This looks like a fallback or generic salutation rather than a resolved first name, but the source doesn't show whether it is deliberate. It is not a visible unresolved token such as `{{first_name}}`.
- **The JSON-LD block is empty.** In `<script type="application/ld+json">`, `EmailMessage.subjectLine`, `DiscountOffer.discountCode`, `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds`, `DiscountOffer.description`, `PromotionCard.image` and `PromotionCard.url` are all `""`. `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`. These look like template defaults that were never populated. Gmail promo annotations will not work with this block.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Do not treat it as a sender defect until the raw headers are checked.
- **Authentication-Results was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **In-body unsubscribe link is present:** `…f85b0971…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`. It returned 403 to the probe, so it is unverified. It carries per-recipient parameters (`p4`, `p5`), which is normal.
- The physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source, so it could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Destinations are unresolved** because of the 403s, so landing page alignment can't be checked.
- **No UTM parameters** appear on any observed URL. Attribution relies on the redirector's own `p1=DM6105495`, `p2=…` and `p3=20261004` parameters. This is not necessarily an issue, because it depends on the destination redirect appending UTMs server-side.
- **Possible offer mismatch:** the subject says "20% off" and the hero link text says "UP TO 30% OFF FALL ESSENTIALS IN STORES & ONLINE*". This is a content question, so I have not scored it as a technical defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a browser-like User-Agent and `GET` instead of `HEAD`. Alternatively, mark that host as bot-protected in the QA tool so these 12 are not reported as broken links. Pull the raw message headers directly to check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
2. Resolve the redirect chain for the main CTA and the unsubscribe link to capture the final URLs and UTMs.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 flagged images, and replace the filename-style link text. For the hero and tile images, the alt text should describe the link destination.
4. Populate or remove the empty JSON-LD fields. The `2018-12-31` `availabilityStarts` value should not stay as a stale default.
5. Strip the zero-width characters from the anchor text of the "Shop now" links.
6. Cut down the plain-text part so it has readable copy and fewer raw tracking URLs.
