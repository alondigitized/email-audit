# Kids | Discover what’s new
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the third kids send from Zara in a week and a half. First "Kids | Comfort Capsule" on 10/11, now this one. It's a pretty lookbook, but it isn't for me. Every section is labeled "GIRL / 6–14 YEARS," "BOY / 1½–6 YEARS" or "BABY / 0–18 MONTHS," and I'm 24 with no kids. The models are children, so nothing here says "you." I'd mark it read and move on.
- The layout is nice, I'll admit. The big Helvetica-style headlines next to the photos are very editorial. But there's no price, no offer, no deadline, and no "new" tag anywhere. The subject says "Discover what's new," and I can't tell what's new or why I should care right now. This is the same "Discover" energy as the 10/17 "Discover the latest arrivals" send, and that one wasn't for me either.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. The one exception is the "Trendy fits: balloon pants" block. Balloon pants and baggy denim are trends I actually track, so I noticed it. But the pants are sized for toddlers, so it's a dead end for me.

## What's working

- The big stacked headlines ("ATHLEISURE," "NEW DENIM FITS," "THE GIRLS SOCIETY") are easy to scan on my phone, and they give each block a clear category.
- The photography is cohesive. The muted browns, olive and plaid feel like fall, and the shoot feels like it has a point of view.
- The cast looks diverse in age, hair and skin tone, which is nicer than the same-face lookbooks I usually get.
- The age and gender labels under each headline are clear, so a parent would know right away which block is theirs.
- The footer has the Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest links, so the brand does show up on my platforms.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is aimed at a 24-year-old. It's all kids and babies, so I'm not the audience at all.
- There's no price, no promo, no deadline and no shipping info. Compared with H&M's "Up to 20% off your entire order," there's no reason to act today.
- Nothing is marked as a new drop, even though the subject says "what's new." "Balloon pants" and "new denim fits" are the only trend hints, and they're buried in kid sections.
- There are no visible CTA buttons. The only button-looking thing is a tiny "THE NEW" under the home-kids block, and it's hard to tap.
- The "DISCOVER HOME KIDS COLLECTION" strip at the bottom is tiny and reads as an afterthought.
- Zara's own history shows no trend-first signal. I get "Pre-Owned," "Woman | The New," then kids twice, so the cadence feels random.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send. Women who've browsed womenswear shouldn't get a kids lookbook. Send it to parents and send me the Woman | The New style instead.
- 2. Add a visible "NEW" tag and a clear shop button on each block, like "Shop balloon pants" or "Shop the denim fits." Right now there's nothing to tap.
- 3. Put one concrete trend, price or dated hook in the hero, so the subject has something to deliver on. ASOS did this with "Meet the new ASOS-exclusive Lioness drop," where the email names the drop and I know what's in it.
- 4. Make the subject specific to the content. "Discover what's new" says nothing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 chars.** The email has no usable `text/plain` part. This hurts deliverability scoring and affects text-only clients and some assistive setups.
- **Missing alt text on 10 content images:** `img_01.jpg`, `img_02.jpg`, `img_03A.jpg`, `img_03B.jpg`, `img_04.jpg`, `img_05A.jpg`, `img_05B.jpg`, `img_06A.jpg`, `img_06B.jpg` and `img_07.jpg`.
