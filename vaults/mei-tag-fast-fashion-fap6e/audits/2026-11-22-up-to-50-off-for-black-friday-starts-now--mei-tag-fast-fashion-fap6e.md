# Up to 50% off for Black Friday starts now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay H&M, this is the third "last chance / starts now" energy email I've gotten from you in four days. Yesterday it was "20% off your app order ends tonight!" and the day before "Big deals! 20% off app orders through tomorrow." Now it's "up to 50% off" like nothing happened. The red "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" on the black-and-white hero does read better than the dark-red-on-black mess from 11/20. But it's still red text sitting on a moody photo and it's a little muddy at the edges.
- The body is basically a coat catalog: women's shearling-ish bomber, men's overcoat, kids' faux fur. That's fine, it's the season. But nothing in here feels like a trend. There's no "this is the jacket everyone's wearing on TikTok," no "new in," nothing that says H&M got there first. It's just "here are jackets, they're discounted."
- Would I click? Maybe the women's bomber, because it's the only thing that's actually my vibe. Would I forward it? No. If Zara or ASOS sent the same Black Friday email I'd be at least curious about the styling. This one is a mark-as-read.

## What's working

- The hero is finally legible. Big red "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" on the monochrome model shot is way better than the 11/20 black-box-red-text situation.
- "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" is a clear, concrete headline with two buttons right under it (Shop Now / Find a Store).
- The women's bomber with the shearling collar is a good look and the model matches my age and style.
- Each section has one clear category button (Women, Men, Baby/Kids), so I can skip straight to mine.
- The top banner mentions member exclusive access, so I know I'm getting something extra as a subscriber.

## What's weak

- Cadence is exhausting. Three near-identical sale emails in four days, and this is the same "deals" story I got on 11/18 ("Flash sale: 25% off starts now") with a bigger number.
- "Up to 50%" is the oldest trick there is. I have no idea if the jackets in the photos are 50% or 10%, and no sale prices show anywhere.
- The only product-specific content is photos. There are no names, no prices, and no "was/now," so I can't judge price-per-trend at all.
- The "Your one-stop holiday shop" section, with a smiling girl in a fur hat, is generic gifting copy. It reads like filler, not a trend moment.
- The fine print is a giant wall of tiny gray text at the bottom, and the member offer is buried in it. The banner says "+15% off your first purchase" in tiny red-on-black text, and I can barely read it.
- The Shop By thumbnails and the app/store links are tiny, and the tap targets are cramped on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show real prices on the hero products (e.g. "$59.99, was $89.99") so "up to 50%" becomes something I can actually act on.
- 2. Cut the cadence. One strong Black Friday email beats three that all say "deals."
- 3. Lead with a trend angle: "the shearling bomber that's all over TikTok, now 40% off." That would make it feel like H&M owns the trend instead of chasing it.
- 4. Make the member perk bigger and put it in the hero, not the thin top strip.
- 5. Make the tap targets bigger in the Shop By and app sections.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (responsive CSS, viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`). However, all 12 probed tracking links returned 403 to the automated probe, 14 images lack usable alt text, and the JSON-LD block ships with empty template values. Header-level checks (SPF/DKIM/List-Unsubscribe) could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on all probed links (12/12):** Every probed `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h0e714891-…` redirect returned 403. This covers "Shop now", "Find a store", the "H&M" logo link and "Unsubscribe". Because every link fails identically, including the unsubscribe link, this is most likely bot or WAF blocking of the probe against the tracking redirector, not a broken campaign. It is **unverified from this data**, so confirm with a real-client click test. Only 12 of 72 links were probed.
- **Zero-width characters in link text:** Two "Shop now" links (`…f8d7f12f`, `…f8d7f131`) have link text beginning with invisible characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`, i.e. ZWNJ and BOM-type characters). These can affect screen-reader output and link-text matching.
- **Fully opaque tracking:** Every link goes through `t19.email.hm.com/r/`, and the destination is not visible in the HTML.
- **Empty link text in QA output:** The probe reports blank link text for the failed URLs. This is probably image-only links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 14 images are flagged, including:
  - the tracked image links at `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h0e714891…`
  - `…/black-friday/women/CLOC265P01-BF26-Women-2-3x2.jpg`
  - `…/CLOC265P01-BF26-Women-2-2x3.jpg`
  - `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs under `…/wk48/us186_s4/…`

  Where the audit reports an identifier, it is an AEM filename (`cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`), not descriptive text. Most images are desktop (1400px) and mobile (750px) pairs. If both variants are in the DOM, as `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` suggest, screen readers may hit duplicates unless hidden-variant images use `aria-hidden` or `display:none`.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` is invalid, because `auto` is not a valid `line-height` value. `@media screen yahoo { … }` is a malformed media query, with no `and` and no valid feature.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css)` and `@import url(…/oces.css)` are ignored by Gmail and some other clients. Fallback font stacks should be verified. They are not visible in the truncated source.
- **Truncated source:** The source is truncated, so I could not check for a preheader, table roles, `lang` or `dir` on `<html>`, or `role="presentation"`. The `<html>` tag shown has no `lang` attribute.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the tracking links and the unsubscribe link with a browser-like User-Agent, or by manually clicking them. This tells you whether the 403s are bot blocking or real failures.
2. Check the raw message headers from a direct (non-relay) inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and use `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden` on decorative or duplicate desktop/mobile variants.
4. Strip the zero-width characters from the "Shop now" link text.
5. Populate the JSON-LD `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts/Ends`, `image` and `url` fields, or remove the block.
6. Fix `line-height:auto` and the `@media screen yahoo` query.
7. Shorten the plain-text version, or add readable copy around the URLs.
8. Capture the destination URLs of the redirects, with a proper client, to verify UTM and landing-page continuity.
