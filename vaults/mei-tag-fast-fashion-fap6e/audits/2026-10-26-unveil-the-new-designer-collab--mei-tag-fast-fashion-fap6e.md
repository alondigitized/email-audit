# Unveil the new designer collab
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, an Elie Saab x H&M collab is actually a real event, and this is the first H&M email this week that feels like H&M got there first instead of chasing. The Paris red-carpet shoot is gorgeous and the hero is strong. But I scrolled the whole thing and it's a launch announcement for something I can't buy yet. Everything is "Preview Now" with a date of Oct 29.
- It's also very couture-coded, not TikTok-coded. Embellished peplum top for $169, a leather jacket for $429 and a sequined blazer for $329 aren't "price-per-trend" for me. I'd screenshot it for the group chat, but I wouldn't add to cart. Compared to H&M's "20% off expires tonight, Member" send yesterday (7/10), this one has no discount and no deadline. It's a mood and a date.
- I'd open it and click through once to see the collab. I'd forward it to one friend who loves wedding-guest fits. Otherwise it's a look-and-leave.

## What's working

- The hero is a full-bleed model in a black jumpsuit on a red carpet in Paris, and it reads like a campaign, not a catalog.
- The "ELIE SAAB x H&M" lockup plus "Launching October 29 in the U.S." gives me one clear date to remember.
- The store list (5th Ave, Soho, Brickell, Beverly Drive) tells me where to go in person, which is the kind of drop-day info I want.
- The member early-access callout, with the wedding dress and veil an hour before launch at 9am ET, is a real perk and makes me feel like a known customer.
- Prices are shown on the products, so I'm not guessing. That's better than the "Cozy holiday decorations" send from Oct 23, where nothing felt shoppable.

## What's weak

- Both CTAs say "Preview Now", but there's nothing to buy, so I don't know what I'm previewing. It's vague and low-energy.
- There's no discount and no real urgency beyond a date. Compared with yesterday's 20% member offer, it's easy to skim past.
- The $429 leather jacket and the $329 blazer aren't Gen Z "H&M price" territory. Zara would at least show a range of price points.
- The product grid is hard to act on. Only one tiny "$329" tag sits on the hero, and the product shots are small with tiny gray text.
- The wedding dress block is pretty, but it's not what I'd wear on a Saturday. It feels like it's aimed at a different shopper.
- Models are mostly editorial and thin. Zara's "STUDIO COLLECTION AW26" has the same moody energy, so H&M isn't ahead there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA specific and tied to something I can do: "Set my Oct 29 reminder" or "Get early access". A real button beats "Preview Now".
- 2. Add one entry-price hero item, like a $59 piece or an accessory, so it doesn't feel out of reach. ASOS's Lioness drop worked better because it showed pieces at a lower price.
- 3. Put the member early-access (9am ET) in the top third. It's buried below the grid and it's the best hook.
- 4. Show how it's styled for TikTok-style looks: a "how to wear it" tile or a 3-outfit grid, not only campaign stills.
- 5. Make the product text bigger and the product shots tappable. Right now they look like a lookbook.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Red-carpet looks from $59. Members get early access at 9am ET.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based HTML template with every link routed through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirector. All 12 probed material links returned HTTP 403, so landing pages could not be verified. Alt text is missing on most images, and the JSON-LD block ships with empty and stale fields.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** This includes the primary "Shop now" CTAs (`...f892db97`, `...f892db99`), "Preview now" (`...f892dbae`, `...f892dbaf`), the logo link "H&M" (`...f892db96`) and the image links (`...f892dbac`, `...dbad`, `...dbb0`, `...dbb1`, `...dbb4`, `...dbb5`).
- **The 403s are probably bot blocking.** The redirector most likely rejects the QA probe's user agent. All 12 failures share one host, one `id=h8b458aab-...` campaign ID and one `did`/`rid` pair. The probe cannot confirm whether real recipients are affected, so treat the links as unverified rather than confirmed broken.
- **The unsubscribe link also returned 403.** `...f892dbec&...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...` could not be probed either. Its deliverability is unverified.
- **Link text contains invisible characters.** The "Shop now" anchors have a leading zero-width run (`‌  ﻿`, ZWNJ plus BOM, per the QA output). This is a preheader-style padding hack that leaks into link text and can affect screen-reader announcements and link-text matching.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 61 links were skipped by the probe, so the 12 failures are not a full link audit.
- **Redirect URLs are long.** Each carries `id`, `did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p3`, and `p4`/`p5` on the unsubscribe link. `rid` and `erid` are the same value, one raw and one base64-encoded (`MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` decodes to `3326930539`). This is not a defect, but it means the recipient identifier is visible in every URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 24 `<img>` elements**, per the QA warnings:
  - Hero and product images show their filenames as alt text, for example `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`.
  - `FNP-2024-LOGO-2-v2.png` is repeated at `imWidth` 584, 654, 750 and 1400.
  - One image shows a base64-like string (`KK2btRKUIfC+GSlwZkRkBSADwh92r169Hg8=`) as its alt text.
  - The tracking-pixel-style `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1` image has no alt text.
- **Each image appears to be duplicated.** The 750 and 1400 variants of the same asset (for example `FNP-2024-preview-2x3-SEC.jpg`, listed twice at both sizes) suggest separate desktop and mobile image blocks toggled with `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop`. This doubles the DOM and the alt-text burden. Screen readers may read both if `display:none` is not honored, and `mso-hide` support varies.
- **Images link through opaque redirect URLs.** Images with no alt text inside links (`...f892dbac`, `...dbad`, `...dbb0`) produce anchors with no accessible name.
- **The head uses `@import` for web fonts** (`fabric.hmgroup.com`, `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`). Gmail and several other clients ignore it, so rendering depends on fallbacks.
- **The rule `img.image + div { display:none !important }` is a client-specific workaround.** It is fragile and not something the source can validate.
- **The HTML source was truncated.** Conditional comments, the footer markup and the fallback font stacks could not be inspected.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated source limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify them on the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain `email.hm.com` was not independently validated.
- **A visible "Unsubscribe" link exists** (`...f892dbec`) but returned 403 to the probe. Its function is unconfirmed.
- **The physical postal address could not be checked.** It is in the truncated portion of the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Tracking is carried entirely by the redirector's `p1=DM6167305`, `p2=0982dd0a...` and `p3=20261026`. The email's analytics may be applied after the redirect, but this could not be verified.
- **Landing pages could not be checked.** The 403s prevented following the redirects, so destination URL, final status and campaign alignment are unknown.
- **The JSON-LD Schema.org block is mostly empty.** It contains `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"image":""` and `"url":""`. `availabilityStarts` is the stale `2018-12-31T19:59:59-04:00`. It also uses `http://schema.org/`. The block is a leftover template default and does not match this campaign's subject, "Unveil the new designer collab". Gmail may ignore or reject it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the redirector** with a realistic browser user agent, or from an allowlisted IP, to separate bot blocking from real failures. Confirm the unsubscribe link first.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to all images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones, and drop filename-style alt values. Add alt text to the linked hero and product images so each anchor has an accessible name.
3. **Remove the zero-width and BOM padding** from the CTA anchor text. Keep it in the preheader only.
4. **Fix or remove the JSON-LD block.** Populate `subjectLine`, `url` and `image`, or delete the template defaults, including the 2018 `availabilityStarts`. Switch to `https://schema.org`.
5. **Check the raw `.eml`** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
6. **Confirm UTM or analytics tagging on the landing pages** after the redirect.
7. **Reduce the plain-text part's URL share** (518 of 596 characters). Add real descriptive copy to the text version, since it is more than 70% URLs.
