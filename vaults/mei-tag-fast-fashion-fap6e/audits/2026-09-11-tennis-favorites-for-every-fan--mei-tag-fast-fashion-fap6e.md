## What's weak

- The "FORM & FUNCTION" home decor section coming right after tennis apparel is jarring — feels like two different brand emails got stapled together
- Zero social proof — no "shop the look on @so-and-so," no UGC, nothing showing real people actually wearing this off the court

- Copy is generic ("Comfortable, breathable, flexible") — doesn't say anything about who's actually wearing tennis-core off-court right now, no cultural hook to the trend it's clearly riding

- 2. Add one line connecting this to the actual cultural moment ("the look everyone's copying off Wimbledon" or similar) — right now it reads generic when the trend itself isn't
