## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 10 probed material links failed with a read timeout** on the `official-link.asos.com` redirect domain (e.g. "Shop now", "Download the App", "ASOS.World", "ASOS", "ASOS Premier", "Gift Vouchers", and 4 brand-logo links: mango, bershka, stradivarius, adidasapparel, topshop).
