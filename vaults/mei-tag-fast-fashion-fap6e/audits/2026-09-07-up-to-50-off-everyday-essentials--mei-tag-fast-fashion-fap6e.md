## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is at least a rebrand of "THE LABOR DAY EVENT" into "EVERYDAY ESSENTIALS," which is more effort than the last four H&M emails put in combined — but it's still Labor Day 50% off sitewide dressed up in a new outfit, and I'm not fooled. The actual prices are giving me something to work with though: $19.99 tie-detail top, $29.99 leather-look jacket, $23.99 blouse — that's real price-per-item info, which none of the last few sends bothered with. The models are also doing something — texture, movement, actual outfits styled head to toe instead of a floating product shot.
- But it's still H&M being H&M chasing, not leading. Zara's puffer email screwed up worse (kids collection, zero adults) so H&M wins that comparison, but ASOS's Lioness drop last week had actual attitude — cargo culottes, kitten mules, a girl strutting like she meant it. - I'd scroll past this one. Not deleting it, not forwarding it — maybe tapping the jeans just to see if $35.99 for baggy low-waist is real or a bait price.

## What's working

- Real prices with strikethroughs visible on every product (top $19.99/$24.99, jacket $29.99/$39.99) — first H&M email in this run that actually shows me the math.
- The rebrand to "Everyday Essentials" at least breaks the "Labor Day Event: up to 50% off" copy loop they've been stuck in since Sunday.
- Product grid at the bottom (jeans + blouse) gives me two options side by side instead of one hero shot — that's a real "compare" moment for me.
- Shop-by-category icons (Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) are clean and give me a fast tap-through if I don't care about this specific drop.

## What's weak

- Still zero actual trend language — no "TikTok," no "viral," no "back in stock," nothing that signals H&M knew about a trend before I did. Just "feminine tops" and "easy denim favorites."
- The extra 10% off banner up top is for NEW MEMBERS ONLY, and I've been getting these daily — so that banner is instantly irrelevant to me and just wastes the first screen.
- This is the fifth-ish H&M email in a row leading with a %-off sitewide structure; I've now audited "Labor Day Event: Up to 50% off," "Denim for the whole fam," "(f)all things cozy," and "It knits from $14.99" — all reskins of the same sale. Fatigue is real.
- Hero model shot is moody streetwear-adjacent but not styled to any specific trend I'd recognize — no cargo, no barrel jeans, no going-out top. Feels like stock photography, not a drop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with ONE trend-specific hero product and say why it's trending ("the barrel jean everyone's wearing right now"), not a generic "everyday essentials" umbrella — give me a reason this feels new, not recycled.
- 2. Cut or gate the "extra 10% for new members" banner for existing subscribers — it's dead weight for anyone who's already on the list, and I've seen it before.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" (used twice) with product-specific CTA copy tied to what's below it — "Shop the Jacket" hits harder than a generic button.
- 4. Add a scarcity or trend-cred line under the hero — "restocked after selling out," "as seen on [X]" — literally anything that signals I'm not late to this.
- 5. Space out the sitewide-sale sends — four in five days is why I stopped opening without checking the subject first.
