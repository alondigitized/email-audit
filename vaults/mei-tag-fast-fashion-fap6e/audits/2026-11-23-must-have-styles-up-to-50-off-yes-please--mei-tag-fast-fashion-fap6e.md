# Must-have styles up to 50% off (yes, please!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay H&M, this is the fourth Black Friday send in five days, and honestly I'm fried. Yesterday it was "Up to 50% off for Black Friday starts now!", and before that the 20% app emails twice. Now it's "up to 50%" again with "(yes, please!)" added. The hero is a girl in a cream square-neck top and black wide-leg jeans. That's actually a very me, quiet-luxe look, so I paused for half a second.
- Then it turns into a catalog. The offer is up to 50%, the grid shows $15.99 and $24.99 items, and the holiday shop block sits at the bottom. There's no trend angle at all. Nothing says "this is going viral" or "this is what's on my FYP." Shein would've dropped a trend name in the subject line, and Zara's Studio drops at least feel like H&M is setting the tone instead of chasing it.
- Would I click? Maybe the cream top, if I were already on my Saturday three-tab compare. Would I forward it? No. It's a mark-as-read, and I'm skimming the next one.

## What's working

- The hero model and styling (cream knit, dark wide-leg denim) match my aesthetic and feel current.
- "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" is big, bold, and readable without zooming.
- Prices are shown with red sale and struck-through originals, e.g. $15.99 and $24.99, so I can do the price-per-trend math fast.
- "New Arrival" tags on a few items give a tiny trend-first signal.
- The Women / Men / Kids / Baby buttons are full-width and easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- It's the fourth sale email in five days, and the "50%" repeats yesterday's "Up to 50% off for Black Friday starts now!" almost exactly. The cadence is exhausting.
- "Up to 50%" is vague, and the actual items I see are mostly not 50% off. The wall of fine print at the bottom is long and buries the terms.
- Half of the product grid is kids and menswear (the sweatshirts, the corduroy shirt, the twill pants). That's irrelevant to me, and it dilutes the women's hero.
- There's no urgency or deadline in the visible copy, and the red gift-card banner at the top competes with the sale. Two messages fight for the first look.
- There's no trend language and no "viral" or "as seen on" cues. It reads like a markdown dump, not a drop.
- The "THE FULL LOOK" section is basically more kids and menswear. There's no women's outfit, so it doesn't even deliver on its own name.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a women's-only hero and one named trend, such as the cream knit plus wide-leg denim, with a specific "from $15.99" price.
- 2. Cut the gift-card banner above the logo so the offer owns the top.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "ends Cyber Monday", instead of a vague "before sizes sell out".
- 4. Segment the product grid by what I clicked before. I'm a women's browser, so skip the kids' sweatshirts.
- 5. Shorten the legal block and link to terms.
- 6. Space out the BF sends. Pick one hero per day instead of four versions of the same one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The cream knit + wide-leg combo, from $15.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off ends Monday: our top 5 women's picks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tops, denim and knits you'll actually wear, marked down before sizes go.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals are in the sale. Shop women's first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's links all go through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirector, and every one of the 12 probed links returned 403 (16% pass rate). The 403s most likely mean the tracker blocks automated clients, so the destinations were not verified. The HTML also has widespread missing `alt` text and a JSON-LD block with empty fields.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** These include "Shop now" (`...f8da4a32`, `...f8da4a33`, `...f8da4a36`, `...f8da4a37`, `...f8da49e2`, `...f8da49e4`), "Find a store" (`...f8da4a08`, `...f8da4a0d`), the "H&M" logo link (`...f8da49e1`), two image links (`...f8da4a02`, `...f8da4a03`) and Unsubscribe (`...f8da4a5a`).
  - A uniform 403 across every link, including Unsubscribe, points to bot filtering on the redirector rather than 12 separate broken links. Treat it as **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
  - Real recipients' clicks may work fine. The audit could not confirm any final destination.
- **Coverage is limited.** Only 12 of 98 links were probed (86 skipped), so the remaining links are untested.
- **Link text is poor.** Some "Shop now" links have zero-width characters in their text (`‌  ﻿Shop now`). Image links expose the filename (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`) as their accessible name.
- **Tracking pixel lacks alt.** The `<img>` at `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hde973156-...,1` is flagged for missing alt.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 522 of 600 characters are URLs, so the text alternative has almost no readable content.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on every content image.** The QA run flagged about 26 images. Each product or campaign image appears as a 1400px and 750px pair, and both lack `alt`. Examples:
  - `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk48/1367285001_202604_LB_0184.jpg/...`
  - `.../wk48/us186_s4/FP3-US186-1361447005_202604_LB_...`
  - The tracking pixel `<img>` from section 2.
  - Because many of these images are also wrapped in links, their link purpose is unclear to screen readers.
- **Stale asset path.** Two images come from `.../season-04/start-page-assets/home/week-43/HHMI265P02-FN-3x2.j...` in a `wk48` send. This looks like reused or stale creative paths and may be worth a content check.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** The `<style>` block uses `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css)`. Gmail and several other clients ignore these, so a fallback font stack should exist. This can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Mobile-only CSS.** The `@media (max-width:699px)` block sets `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop`. The `mso-hide` properties are present, which is good. The truncated source can't confirm Outlook handling beyond that.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). Recipient-specific IDs (`rid`, `erid`, `p2`, `p4`, `p5`) are properly resolved in the tracking URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe header found** (WARN). The QA notes AgentMail may strip this header, so it is **unconfirmed**. Verify against raw headers from a direct inbox.
- **No List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header found** (WARN). The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **In-body unsubscribe link exists** but returned 403 to the probe: `.../f8da4a5a&...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...`. It is likely bot-blocked, but it should be tested manually.
- The visible From address is `newsletter@email.hm.com`, which is consistent with the sending subdomain. The truncated source doesn't show the physical postal address, so that can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified.** Every link is wrapped in the redirector, and the probes stopped at the 403, so destination URLs and campaign parameters were never seen.
- **Tracking is carried by the redirector's own parameters** (`did=1427529851`, `p1=DM6235507`, `p3=20261123`). That is a closed-system tracking scheme, so any UTMs would have to be appended at redirect time.
- **JSON-LD structured data is empty.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains:
  - `"subjectLine":""`
  - `"discountCode":""`
  - `"description":""`
  - `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`
  - `"url":""` and `"image":""` in the `PromotionCard`

  These are template defaults that were never populated. Gmail promotion annotations will therefore not render, and the 2018 date is a stale default.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe links** with a browser-like client, or allowlist the audit's IP or user-agent with H&M's ESP. Manually click-test at least the Unsubscribe link and the primary "Shop now" CTAs.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to all content images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones and `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` on the tracking pixel. Replace filename-based link names with descriptive ones.
3. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD block.** Fill `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `description`, `url`, `image` and a current `availabilityStarts`/`availabilityEnds`, or drop the block.
4. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** on the raw message, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment via a direct send. These warnings may be artifacts of the relay.
5. **Improve the plain-text alternative.** It should include readable copy, not mostly URLs.
6. **Check the `week-43` asset paths** in this `wk48` send.
7. **Verify a web-font fallback stack** for clients that ignore `@import`.
