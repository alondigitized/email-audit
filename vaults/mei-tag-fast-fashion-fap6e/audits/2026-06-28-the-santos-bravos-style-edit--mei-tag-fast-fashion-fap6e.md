# The SANTOS BRAVOS style edit
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- So H&M has been in my inbox every single day this week and I've genuinely lost count — this has to be send ten or eleven at this point. And then they show up with "The SANTOS BRAVOS style edit" and I had to actually pause mid-swipe because I had no idea who Santos Bravos is. A quick mental note later: Brazilian soccer club, LOTTO x H&M collab. Okay, that's actually an interesting angle? LOTTO is very Y2K, very thrift-archive-core, and that energy is genuinely having a moment right now. If this were a tight, editorial drop email, I'd have been into it.
- But H&M can't commit. The first thing I see is a fat banner screaming 60% off and new styles from $7.99 — the exact same sale banner from the June 27 "New sale styles added" send. Then the hero pivots to a curated collab story. These are two completely different emails badly duct-taped together. Zara's June 22 sale launch at least had a specific time and didn't try to dress it up as a fashion editorial. ASOS's stacked-discount email from this week was fully committed to the math and it worked cleanly. H&M is trying to be both and landing as neither.
- Would I click? Once, out of genuine LOTTO curiosity. Would I forward this to a friend? Absolutely not — there's nothing here that feels shareable or like I discovered something first.

## What's working

- "GAME DAY WITH SANTOS BRAVOS" is at least a different headline energy after a week of "bestsellers" and "flash sale" subject lines — it registered as something new
- The LOTTO name-drop in the body copy does real work — that brand has Gen Z nostalgia cache and naming it specifically adds credibility to the collab
- The sale banner is factually clear: 60% off and a starting price point show up without effort
- The layout is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, the eye lands on the hero naturally
- 30-character subject line reads perfectly on mobile without truncation

## What's weak

- The sale banner and the Santos Bravos editorial are fighting each other — I literally don't know which story this email is telling
- "SHOP NOW" is doing zero lifting — ASOS stacked "25% extra off" on an existing 60% discount and told me exactly what the math meant; H&M just wants me to shop, right now, for reasons
- The email assumes I already know who Santos Bravos is — one line of context ("Brazil's hottest football club" or anything) would have fixed this cold-drop problem instantly
- That "SHOP BY Women / Men / Kids / Home / Beauty" grid at the bottom is a homepage navigation element dropped into a collab editorial, and it completely kills whatever momentum the hero built
- Cadence has reached a breaking point — this is the same sale-banner wrapper I saw on June 27, and the H&M fatigue in my inbox is real at this point

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the sale banner from the hero block and let Santos Bravos own the email** — the LOTTO x H&M collab story is the most interesting creative H&M has put in my inbox this week; burying it under a generic sale header is a self-own. Move sale info to a slim footer strip.
- 2. **Add one sentence of context on Santos Bravos** — even "Brazil's Santos Bravos FC, now kitted out in LOTTO x H&M" orients me immediately and makes this feel like an event, not just a product dump.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA** — "Shop the LOTTO drop" or "Get the Game Day look" over a blank "SHOP NOW." ASOS and Zara both use action copy that tells me what I'm clicking into.
- 4. **Fix the preheader mismatch** — the preview text is pulling the sale banner, which contradicts the Santos Bravos subject line. The inbox experience reads as incoherent before I even open.
- 5. **Delete the "SHOP BY" category grid** — it's not an email section, it's a nav bar. No collab drop needs it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `LOTTO x H&M just dropped with Santos Bravos`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Santos Bravos drop is here — shop LOTTO x H&M`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brazil's Santos Bravos FC meets LOTTO in H&M's game-day edit`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sporty, Y2K, and very now — the LOTTO x H&M pieces dropping today`
