# It's official: Up to 60% off online & in stores
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- H&M, this is the fourteenth Black Friday-season email in about two weeks, and I'm numb. The hero is a black block with "BLACK FRIDAY BLACK FRIDAY" in red. It tells me nothing about a trend or a drop. Shein and Zara at least show me a piece of clothing before they shout a percentage at me.
- The "up to 60% off online & in stores" headline sits right under the hero and is easy to read. The women's block with the brown oversized coat and the wide-leg trousers is the only part that feels like a look instead of a clearance rack. Then it goes straight to menswear, kids and a Minnie Mouse tee, and I lose interest. The "give $50+, get 30% back" e-gift card section is a gift-giver thing, not something I'd buy.
- I'd open it out of habit and give it a ten-second scroll. I'd tap Women once, maybe. I wouldn't forward it, because nothing in it is a "bestie, look at this" moment. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" headline is big and bold, and I see it right after the hero.
- The women's tile has the dark coat and wide-leg trousers styling, which is on-trend for me.
- Every block has one clear button (Women, Men, Kids), so nothing is confusing to tap.
- The "Shop by" grid at the bottom is a quick jump to a category, and the layout is clean with no broken images.
- It's obviously a current campaign. The Black Friday framing matches the week.

## What's weak

- The hero is almost all black with red text and no clothes in it. It feels like a placeholder, and I don't see any product in the first scroll.
- Menswear, kids and the Minnie tee take up half the email. I'm 24 and shopping womenswear, so that's wasted space.
- The "Natural Winners" and "Best of All" labels say nothing. There are no prices, no names for the pieces, and no "just dropped" or "trending" cues, so I can't tell what's new.
- The fine print is a huge wall of text. It has exclusions, a member bonus reward and an e-gift card bonus, and it's tiny. The "up to 60%" feels like it has strings attached.
- The sale ends 12/01, but nothing near the top says so. The urgency is missing.
- The top banner pushes 10% off for becoming a member. I'm already on the list, so it feels like the email is talking to a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the black type hero with the women's coat look and put the offer on top of it. Lead with a model in a trend piece, not a Black Friday wordmark.
- 2. Add a visible deadline like "Ends Monday" right under the headline.
- 3. Give each tile a named piece and a price, like "Faux-leather bomber, $39.99", so it works as price-per-trend and I know what I'm tapping.
- 4. Put the women's section first and with more than one look. Cut the Minnie tee and the e-gift card to a smaller strip lower down.
- 5. Hide or personalise the "become a member" banner for people who are already subscribers.

# Technical Audit: H&M, "It's official: Up to 60% off online & in stores"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly sound. It has no unresolved merge tokens and has responsive CSS. The automated QA reports a 16% pass rate, but 12 of 12 probed links returned 403 on the ESP redirector (`t19.email.hm.com/r/`), which points to bot-blocking rather than confirmed breakage. The real code-level gaps are missing image alt text and empty JSON-LD fields.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** This includes "Shop now", "Find a store", "shop e‑Gift cards", "BECOME A MEMBER & GET 10% Off Your First Purchase" and "Unsubscribe". Every URL is on `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h06ecd382-…,5517d327,<hex>&did=1427624743&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6244709&p2=…&p3=20261127`.
  - The failure is uniform across every link, including the unsubscribe link, and the links share one redirector. That pattern suggests the ESP blocks the QA prober's user agent or IP rather than the links being dead.
  - **These links are unverified, not confirmed broken.** A real-client check is needed to settle it.
- **Coverage is thin.** Only 12 links were probed and 66 skipped, so the other 66 links are untested.
- **Duplicate CTAs use distinct tracking IDs.** For example, "Shop now" appears as `…f8e44f7e` and `…f8e44f80`, and "shop e‑Gift cards" as `…f8e44f96`, `…f97`, `…f9c` and `…f9d`. This is normal for per-placement click tracking, so no issue.
- **Link text contains invisible characters.** Two "Shop now" anchors (`…f8e44f5c`, `…f8e44f5e`) carry leading zero-width characters (`‌  ﻿`, a ZWNJ plus a BOM). Screen readers or text-based link checks may handle them inconsistently.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images are missing alt text.** QA flagged 22 images:
  - the tracking pixel, `src=https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h06ecd382-…,5517d327,1`;
  - the animated GIFs `CLOC265P01-animated-BF-PYRAMID2-black-3x2.gif` and `…-2x3.gif`;
  - roughly 18 `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` product and lifestyle images on `image.hm.com`.
  - The 1400px and 750px images appear as pairs, so each image is probably rendered twice, once for desktop and once for mobile.
  - If these are the content images, it's a real accessibility gap, and with images blocked the email would show empty slots. The tracking pixel can legitimately use `alt=""`, but the attribute should be present.
- **The `@import` web fonts may not load.** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css)` are stripped or ignored by Gmail and some other clients. This depends on the CSS fallback stack, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- **The email depends on `<style>` blocks.** The layout relies on a `<style>` block with a `@media (max-width:699px)` query plus `mso-hide:all`. Gmail clients that strip `<style>` would lose the responsive behavior, which I can't confirm from the truncated source.
- **The JSON-LD block has empty fields.** In `<script type="application/ld+json">`, `EmailMessage.subjectLine` is `""`, and `DiscountOffer.discountCode` and `availabilityEnds` are also `""`. `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, and `PromotionCard.image` and `url` are `""`. Empty or stale values are invalid markup, and Gmail ignores them or may fail to render a promotion card. The `viewport` meta and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) was not found either. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. They are probably not missing, but I can't confirm that. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `newsletter@email.hm.com` is unknown, so it's not verifiable here.

- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** All links go through the ESP redirector with opaque IDs (`p1=DM6244709`, `p3=20261127`). No UTM parameters are visible on the email side.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be checked.** None of the destination URLs resolved, because the redirector returned 403. Whether "Shop now" lands on the 60%-off page is unverified.
- **The tracking IDs themselves are well-formed.** The `did`, `rid` and `erid` parameters are consistent across all links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the redirector links with a real-browser user agent or an allowlisted IP before treating the 403s as breakage, and re-run at least one 403 link per CTA type.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the product and GIF images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.

7. Confirm that a footer postal address is present.
8. Raise the probe cap above 12 links, or run a second pass on the 66 skipped links.
