# Technical Audit: H&M "Cozy holiday decorations"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based template with responsive CSS. The probe results show 12 of 12 material links returning HTTP 403, including the unsubscribe link. That is most likely bot-blocking on the `t19.email.hm.com` redirector, not real breakage. Accessibility has a clear gap: 28 images have no alt text. The source was truncated and no raw headers were captured, so compliance and authentication can't be confirmed.

The hex suffixes include `f88be686`, `f88be687`, `f88be6d1`, `f88be6d2`, `f88be64d`, `f88be64f`, `f88be64c`, `f88be684`, `f88be685`, `f88be688`, `f88be689` and `f88be707`.
  - The affected links are the "SHOP NOW" CTAs, the "H&M" logo link, the image links and "Unsubscribe".
  - A uniform 403 on a tracked redirector points to bot or user-agent filtering. I can't tell from this data whether recipients see a working redirect, so verify with a real-client click.
- **Separate links for one image.** Each image is linked twice, with separate IDs for the 1400px and 750px variants (`…f88be684`/`…f88be685`, `…f88be688`/`…f88be689`). These are the desktop and mobile versions of one block. This is normal for the template but doubles the distinct tracked URLs.
