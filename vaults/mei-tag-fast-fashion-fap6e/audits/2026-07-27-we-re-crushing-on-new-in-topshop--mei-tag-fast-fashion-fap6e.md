## What's working

- The hero image is stunning — moody field-and-fence backdrop, model styling feels directional not stock-catalog.
- "Floaty chiffon, sheer jerseys, lace trims and heritage-inspired accents" is specific trend language, not generic filler.
- TOPSHOP wordmark stamped huge on the hero makes it instantly clear which brand this is, good for someone like me who tracks who's got what first.
- "Shop Our Top Brands" row (Mango, Bershka, Stradivarius, adidas, Topshop) is a nice touch — feels like ASOS flexing its multi-brand marketplace, which is genuinely part of why I keep them in rotation over a single-label site like Zara.

## What's weak

- No offer, no code, no urgency anywhere in this email — compare to ASOS's own "Final hours of Sale" from July 26th (40% extra off, LASTCHANCE code) — this just feels like a random Tuesday.
- Three near-duplicate crops of the same cargo pants waist detail is filler, not curation — wastes scroll real estate that could've shown more distinct pieces.
- The green utility jumpsuit at the end doesn't match "floaty chiffon and lace" copy at all — visual whiplash, feels like a leftover asset dumped in.
- Product grid has zero prices or links called out per item — I can't tell what anything costs without tapping through, which is friction on mobile.
- This is ASOS's second Topshop-adjacent-feeling send in a week after "New arrivals" on July 22nd — starting to blur together, no distinct hook each time.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE trend thesis and stick to it — either the floaty/lace chiffon dresses or the utility cargo moment, not both crammed into one grid. Swap the green jumpsuit for another chiffon piece.
- 2. Cut two of the three cargo-pants-waist crops — replace with two more complete outfit shots so the grid shows range, not repetition.
- 3. Add a price or "from £X" under at least the hero piece — I shouldn't have to tap through blind.
- 4. Give me a reason to act NOW — even a soft one like "new-in, selling fast" beats zero urgency, especially right after a 40%-off LASTCHANCE email trained me to expect deals from this sender.
