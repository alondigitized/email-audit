## Executive summary

- Okay so this is ASOS's FIFTH email in about a week — May 18 "what's hot this summer," May 20 embellished pieces, May 22 leopard print, May 25 "up to 30% off," and now ALSO May 25 "Need some inspo?" Two emails in one day??? My thumb is twitching toward the unsubscribe button at this point. The cadence has officially crossed into "this is becoming background noise."
- That said — the creative itself is actually kinda cute? The red maxi is giving wedding-guest-summer, the brown coord is *very* TikTok-trending right now, and the chunky mesh ballet flats are everywhere on FYP. The grid layout is clean, it scrolls nicely, and there's a "back in stock" callout which is the one thing that actually makes me tap (FOMO is real).
- But the headline is so generic. "Need some inspo?" reads like a placeholder. No offer, no urgency, no trend name, no price point. After getting hit with "up to 30% off" earlier today, this one feels like ASOS just throwing spaghetti. I'd probably mark as read and move on — unless I was already in shopping mode, in which case maybe I'd tap the back-in-stock block. Not forwarding to anyone.

## What's working

- The brown one-shoulder coord and the strapless black playsuit are *exactly* the silhouettes blowing up on TikTok right now — feels current.
- Clean grid layout, products breathe, easy to scan on my phone without zooming.
- "YOUR MOST HYPED — missed it the first time around?" back-in-stock block is the kind of FOMO bait that actually works on me.
- Real model diversity — Black, white, brunette, blonde — feels less tokenized than Zara's usual cast.
- Two "SHOP NEW IN" buttons spaced through the scroll = I don't have to hunt for the CTA.

## What's weak

- "Need some inspo?" is the laziest possible subject — no trend, no offer, no hook. H&M would call this out the same way.
- TWO ASOS emails on May 25 alone. This + "up to 30% off" earlier = inbox spam. Even H&M's five-in-seven-days run feels more restrained than this.
- Zero price anchors anywhere on screen. The May 25 morning email at least gave me a 30% number — this gives me nothing.
- "Shop our top brands" with New Balance, Tommy, Levi's etc. logos at the bottom feels random and disconnected from the hero edit.
- No trend NAME. ASOS's May 20 "embellished pieces" and May 22 "leopard print" worked because they named the search term. This is just… vibes.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name the trend in the subject.** "Need some inspo?" tells me nothing. Pick one of the silhouettes in the email (the brown coord, the maxi, the mesh flats) and lead with it. This was the lesson from your own May 20 send.
- 2. **Stop double-sending in a single day.** If you already ran "up to 30% off" this morning, don't send a content email at night. Pick one beat per day.
- 3. **Add a price anchor.** Even "From $25" under the hero would give me a reason to scroll. Right now it's pure browse-bait with no commerce hook.
- 4. **Cut the "Shop our top brands" logo wall.** It dilutes the ASOS-own-brand edit at the top. Either lead with brands OR with trends, not both as an afterthought.
- 5. **Use the back-in-stock block as the HERO.** That's the one section here with actual urgency — bury it in the middle and you waste it.
