# Up to 25% off + free shipping ends today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the same tiered-spend promo H&M already ran last week, and "ends today" is doing a lot of work. The puffer hero is clean and the "15% off $75 / 20% off $100 / 25% off $125 + free shipping" stack is readable. But it's a math problem again. On 10/24 and 10/25 they gave me a flat 20% member offer with no tiers, and that was easier. This feels like a clearance-rack email, not a "we got the trend first" email.
- Puffers in late October are a seasonal staple, not a TikTok moment. Zara's CARAMEL LONDON collab at least felt like an event, and the Elie Saab x H&M send on 10/26 was the one that felt like H&M got there first. Today's email is H&M chasing the weather. I'd screenshot the offer for my Saturday three-tab compare and probably not click through. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero puffer shot is clean and wearable, and the model looks like someone I'd follow.
- The offer stack is big, bold and centered, so the eye lands there first.
- Free shipping is in the headline, not buried.
- The Women/Men/Kids tiles each have a clear category label.
- The models are a decent mix of ages and looks, which matches the "every age and adventure" line.

## What's weak

- Three discount tiers means I have to work out what my cart needs to hit. The flat 20% on 10/24 was easier.
- The deadline isn't in the hero. "Ends today" is only in the subject, and the actual 10/28 date is in the fine print at the bottom.
- "Discover new puffer coats" is generic. Nothing says what's trending or new, and nothing about why this puffer matters now.
- One "SHOP NOW" button and the small "WOMEN" text links aren't very tappable on my phone. The nav and footer links are tiny and grey.
- The footer fine print is a wall of text. It says the offer excludes sale items, collabs and more, so the "up to 25%" feels less honest.
- The Home and Beauty tiles at the bottom have nothing to do with puffers or me.
- No member perk or loyalty mention, even though I'm subscribed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PM") next to the offer. That's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Simplify to one headline offer, like "25% off $125 + free shipping", and leave the other tiers for the landing page.
- 3. Name a hero product with a price and make it tappable, like "The Puffer Everyone Keeps Reposting, $49.99". Right now it's all generic category links.
- 4. Add a trend or social-proof hook so it feels like H&M got there first, not like it's chasing.
- 5. Make the buttons bigger, and cut the Home and Beauty tiles from a puffer send.

- **Final destinations can't be verified.** Every URL is an opaque redirect, so the QA data doesn't show where any link lands.
- **Coverage is partial.** 44 of 56 links were skipped by the probe, so footer, social and utility links are untested.
- **Link text contains invisible characters.** Two "Shop now" links have the text `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which is zero-width characters plus whitespace (`…f8994d79`, `…f8994d7b`). Screen readers may announce this oddly, and text-based link matching breaks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 10 images.**
  - The `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs have no alt.
