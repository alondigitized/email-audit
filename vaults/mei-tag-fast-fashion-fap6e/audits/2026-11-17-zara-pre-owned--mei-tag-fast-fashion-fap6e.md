## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has real accessibility and markup defects (19 images without alt text, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, invalid `<div>` placement inside a `<table>`) and a 0-char plain-text part. The compliance and authentication warnings may be relay capture artifacts rather than sender faults, because the HTML is truncated and AgentMail may strip headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Incomplete link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 6 were skipped. The QA data shows no link failures in the probed set, so this is a coverage gap rather than a defect. The 6 unprobed links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=…` is a Responsys open-tracking image (`/pub/as`). It was flagged for missing alt text but is a 1x1 pixel, so the flag is benign.
- **Link destinations:** The truncated source does not show the CTA hrefs, so redirect chains and UTM presence can't be confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 19 images:**
  - Campaign images: `img_01_en.jpg`, `img_02_en.jpg`, `img_03.jpg`, `img_04_en_US.jpg` through `img_08_en_US.jpg`, `img_09.jpg`.
  - Footer images: `latredes142.jpg` (twice), `download_en_b.jpg`, `rrss1b.jpg` through `rrss6b.jpg`.
  - Images-off clients get blank space, and screen readers get nothing.
  - The `rrss*` social icons are presumably wrapped in links. If so, those links have no accessible name.
  - The campaign images appear to carry the content, so the email is effectively unreadable with images blocked.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;">`
  - `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0` block pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
  - Semicolon separators are non-standard, since the spec uses commas.
- **Invalid markup:** `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` sits directly inside `<table width="640">` and wraps `<tr>` elements. A `<div>` is not a valid child of `<table>`, and clients may foster-parent it out of the table.

- **Web font:** `fonts.googleapis.com` Roboto Condensed loads via `<link>`. Gmail and Outlook ignore it, and no fallback font stack is visible in the truncated source.
- **Link colour:** The global `a { color: #999898 }` is about 2.8:1 against `#ffffff`, below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA minimum. This applies to any link without an inline colour override.
- **Preheader:** The preheader is present ("New second-hand arrivals" plus `&#8199;&#847;` padding), so no issue there.
