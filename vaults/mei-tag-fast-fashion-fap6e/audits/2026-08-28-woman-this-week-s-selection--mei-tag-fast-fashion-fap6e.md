## What's weak

- No prices anywhere. Not one. I can't even tell if this is a $30 tee or a $90 blazer without tapping through — that's real friction on a Saturday scroll where I'm comparing three tabs at once.
- Nine separate category sections with zero narrative — it reads like a sitemap, not a "here's what's trending" email. TikTok trend energy is completely absent.
- The Seoul flagship opening at the bottom is just noise to me — I'm not flying to Gangnam for a store opening, cut it or make it its own send.

- Model wearing the trench/blazer with literally nothing under it in the ZW section — cute for the shoot, useless for me trying to picture wearing it to brunch.
