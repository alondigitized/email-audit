# This is it: up to 50% off sitewide
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- So H&M's calling this "THE LABOR DAY EVENT FINALE" now, which — okay, at least that's a new label, but it's the same "UP TO 50% OFF SITEWIDE" I've now seen from them five sends running. I opened it off the subject "up to 50% off sitewide" purely out of morbid curiosity to see if they'd finally trot out something new, and nope — same headline, same font stack, same beige-and-lavender cardigan model standing there with her arms crossed like she's also over it.
- There's genuinely no trend content here — no "new drop," no "restocked," nothing that says a single human looked at TikTok this week before hitting send. It's a sitewide markdown wrapped in urgency language that's been recycled since Sunday. Compare that to Zara's Massimo Dutti FW26 send — at least that one was trying to sell me on a *collection*, even if it was a weird sister-brand mixup. This is just "everything's on sale, again."
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding. Marking as read and mentally filing H&M under "check back in October."

## What's working

- The hero image is clean — no render bugs, no overlapping text, image loaded fine.
- "SHOP BY" category grid (Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) is genuinely useful navigation if I did want to browse.
- Fine print at least discloses the extra 10% is member-only and lists exclusions, so it's not a total bait-and-switch.
- Two clear CTAs (Shop Now / Become a Member) sitting right under the offer, no scrolling needed for the ask.

## What's weak

- "THE LABOR DAY EVENT FINALE" is just Tuesday's "This is it" energy recycled — Labor Day was literally a week ago, this dead-horse urgency isn't landing anymore.
- Zero trend signal — no "new in," no specific product drop, nothing that tells me this was styled for *now* vs. copy-pasted from the last four emails.
- One model, one look, one skin tone represented — H&M usually does better on diversity than this single beige-cardigan shot suggests.
- Cadence is brutal — this is the fifth nearly-identical "sitewide % off" email this week, and my brain has fully filtered it to noise at this point.
- "Score premium picks and luxe looks for less" is generic filler copy that could sit under literally any past H&M sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop the streak — if you're going to send a 6th sitewide-percent-off email, at minimum swap the hero product for something trend-specific (the cargo/utility stuff ASOS's Lioness drop leaned into would actually cut through).
- 2. Cut "FINALE" unless it's actually the last one — I've seen "last day," "ending soon," and now "finale" in one week and none of it felt true.
- 3. Lead with ONE product story instead of sitewide — pick the trending piece and build the email around it, categories below.
- 4. Add a real urgency element (countdown timer, actual hour cutoff) instead of vague "finale" language.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The cardigan everyone's restocking — 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: sitewide sale ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New fall knits just dropped, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members get an extra 10% — ends 9/8`

# H&M Email Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's redirect/tracking domain (`t19.email.hm.com`) is returning HTTP 403 on every probed link, making the message non-functional despite otherwise standard ESP markup (Adobe Campaign / responsive MJML-style CSS).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links return 403**, including primary CTAs ("SHOP NOW" x2, "Shop now" x2), header logo, both hero images, "BECOME A MEMBER" x2, "Women", "Find store", and **Unsubscribe**. Evidence: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hdac4bf82-...&did=1418152446&rid=3326930539&erid=...` — every link shares the same `did`/`rid`/`erid` redirector pattern, indicating the tracking domain/redirector itself is down or blocking the QA client, not an issue isolated to one link.
- Link text hygiene issue: two CTAs render as `"‌  ﻿Shop now"` — contain zero-width space (U+200C) and BOM/zero-width no-break space (U+FEFF) characters in the anchor text, likely leftover from template merge logic.
- 37 lower-priority (footer/social/utility) links were not probed per QA methodology; scope limited to material links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 images missing `alt` text, including both hero product images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, sourced from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-36/us142_s4/1339884005_202604_LB_2064.jpg`) and 2 tracking-pixel images. Missing alt text on hero images breaks the experience for screen readers and when images are blocked by default.
- CSS includes standard Outlook/Yahoo/webkit conditional overrides (`#MessageViewBody`, `@media screen yahoo`, `mso-hide`) and a mobile breakpoint at 699px — no structural rendering issues found in the visible markup.
- `<script type="application/ld+json">` (schema.org EmailMessage/DiscountOffer markup) has several **empty required-looking fields**: `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url` are all `""`. This will likely cause Gmail annotations (e.g., promo/goto-action markup) to fail validation or simply not render.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) found in the visible source.
- The JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` block has an unpopulated `discountCode` field alongside a hardcoded stale `availabilityStarts` value of `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00` — this looks like a template default that was never overwritten by the send-time merge/personalization step, not live data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is present but returns 403** (same redirector failure as above) — functionally, this campaign currently has no working one-click unsubscribe path, a CAN-SPAM functional-compliance concern if not resolved before/at send.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: **not found**.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058 one-click): **not found**. Combined with the broken in-body unsubscribe link, there is currently no reliable unsubscribe mechanism visible to this audit.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): **not found** — authentication status cannot be confirmed from available data.
- Note: the two header-related warnings are flagged by QA as possibly attributable to the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding these headers rather than the sender omitting them — treat as inconclusive, not confirmed absence, until verified against raw sender headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links do not use standard UTM parameters; instead they use Adobe Campaign-style redirector params (`id`, `did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`, e.g. `p3=20260909` as a send-date stamp, `p1=DM6038684` as a campaign/message ID). Continuity to a landing page cannot be verified because the redirector itself 403s before any destination/landing page resolution occurs.
- Plain-text version is 87% URLs (520/598 characters) — consistent with a redirector-heavy template, but offers no readable fallback content if HTML rendering fails.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority 1:** Investigate why `t19.email.hm.com` redirects are returning 403 for this send — this affects 100% of tracked links including CTAs and unsubscribe, and should block send until resolved.
2. Fix or repopulate the empty JSON-LD `DiscountOffer`/`EmailMessage` schema fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url`) or remove the block if not in use, to avoid broken Gmail markup.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to both hero product images at minimum.
4. Strip the zero-width space/BOM characters (U+200C, U+FEFF) from the "Shop now" anchor text.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (verify against raw SMTP headers, not just the AgentMail-relayed copy) to meet RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
6. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly from the sending MTA logs, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observable here.
