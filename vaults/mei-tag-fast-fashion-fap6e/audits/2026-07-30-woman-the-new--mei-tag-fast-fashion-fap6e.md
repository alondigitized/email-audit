# Woman | The New
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay so "Woman | The New" — this is basically Zara's whole catalog dumped into one scroll. ZW Collection, Stripes, TRF, Lingerie, Accessories, Selected Pieces — it's SEVEN category modules stacked on top of each other with zero hierarchy telling me which one is actually the moment. There's no offer, no price, no "this just dropped" urgency copy — just category headers in tiny black caps and product shots. It reads less like a campaign and more like someone exported the site nav into an email.
- The stripes section is genuinely cute — that striped shirt-and-orange-pants combo has main character energy and I'd screenshot it for my group chat. But I have to scroll past six other collections to even get there, and there's no single hero telling me "THIS is the trend right now." Compare that to ASOS's adidas Originals drop I audited — one collab, one face (Ramla Ali), one story, done. This is the opposite: no story, just inventory.
- Would I click? Maybe on the stripes tile specifically, but I'm not opening the email again for a re-read, and I definitely wouldn't forward it — there's nothing "OMG look at this" enough to send to a friend. This is a mark-as-read-while-walking-to-class email.

## What's working

- The stripes editorial shot (model draped over a chair in the striped set) actually has a directional, trend-forward mood — that's the one visual I'd stop scrolling for.
- Model lineup is diverse across the accessories grid — different hair textures, skin tones, one shaved-head look — that's more representative than Zara's usual all-blonde run I've flagged before.
- Clean section labels (DRESSES, CO-ORD SETS, etc.) make it skimmable if I already know what I want.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Zero offer, zero price, zero urgency — nothing here gives me a reason to act today vs. next week.
- Subject says "The New" but the email is just... categories. No "new arrivals this week" framing, no drop date, no "first to get it" language that would actually justify the word "New."
- Seven collections in one send means nothing gets real visual weight — it's the layout equivalent of a run-on sentence.
- The lingerie section models look moodier/older-skewing than the rest of the email — tonal whiplash between "TikTok trend girl" and "editorial noir shoot."
- This is basically a re-skin of the "Woman | The weekly edit" send I already flagged on 7/24 for reusing the same subject/format — now it's happening again with a different label slapped on the same scroll-everything structure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero — the stripes co-ord is the strongest asset here, lead with it full-width up top instead of burying it under ZW Collection.
- 2. Cut the email from 7 sections to 3 max. If it's a "new arrivals" send, show me the 3 newest drops, not the entire site map.
- 3. Add actual news: a drop date, a "just landed" badge, or literally any specificity — right now "The New" is an unearned claim.
- 4. Fix the subject-line repetition problem — stop reusing "Woman |" formatting every few days, it's starting to blur together in my inbox (see 7/24's "Weekly edit").
- 5. Give the lingerie section its own send or at least visually separate it — the mood mismatch pulls me out of the scroll.
