# Your 20% off ends soon, Member
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is the THIRD "20% off Members" email H&M's sent me in three days — 08-29 "20% off for Members starts now," 08-30 "Don't wait: 20% off ends tomorrow," and now this one, "Your 20% off ends soon, Member." Same offer, same six outline buttons, same generic "Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Become a Member" nav dump. The one thing that's new is the hero image — a floral kimono-jacket moment on a model with actual texture in her hair, which is honestly the best styling shot they've sent me all week — but it's buried under three days of "hurry up and use your 20%" fatigue.
- The floral jacket photo did stop my scroll for a second, it's giving a little bit of "put this on for a reason" energy like the ASOS Lioness drop did for me, but then it just dumps into the same boring outline-button grid every other H&M send has. There's no trend story here, no "this is what's trending on TikTok right now" hook, nothing that tells me why THIS piece over anything else in the app.
- Would I click? Maybe, just because the jacket photo is genuinely cute and I'm mildly annoyed enough to check if it's actually 20% off. Would I forward this to a friend? No — there's nothing shareable here, it's just an expiring discount code with a nice product shot slapped on top. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The floral kimono jacket hero shot is actually stylish — moody lighting, real texture, feels more editorial than H&M's usual flat product grid.
- "LAST DAY" urgency is at least clear and specific, not vague.
- Discount terms are spelled out in the footer instead of hidden — I can see exactly what's excluded (final sale, gift cards, etc.) if I bother to read that far.
- Layout is clean, no overlapping text or broken images, everything renders fine on my phone.

## What's weak

- This is the exact same offer as 08-29 and 08-30 — I've now seen "20% off for Members" three days running, it's giving desperate, not exclusive.
- Zero trend context — no "here's why this jacket is having a moment," no TikTok tie-in, no styling tips, just a photo and a discount.
- The six outline buttons (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Become a Member) are copy-pasted from the last two emails, no effort to freshen the CTA hierarchy.
- The "Shop By" thumbnails below are tiny and generic — the Beauty one is literally just a nose/piercing close-up with zero context, feels like an afterthought.
- Nothing here treats me like a known Member beyond "Member" in the subject line — no personalized picks, no "items you saved," no size/availability info on the hero piece.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the third consecutive identical-offer send — if the discount hasn't changed, change the creative concept entirely (styling story, trend explainer, UGC) instead of recycling the same button grid.
- 2. Make the hero jacket shoppable directly — link straight to that product, not just "SHOP NOW" to the homepage. If it sells out or ships slow, tell me now, not after I click.
- 3. Add ONE line of trend context under the hero — even "the floral moment everyone's doing this fall" would beat silence.
- 4. Drop or redesign the "Shop By" beauty thumbnail — a random face close-up with no product context reads as filler, not merchandising.
- 5. Since this is the third urgency email in three days, actually escalate the urgency mechanic (a countdown timer, a real hour-based deadline) instead of reusing "LAST DAY" text with no visual pressure.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The kimono jacket everyone's saving right now — 20% off ends tonight`
