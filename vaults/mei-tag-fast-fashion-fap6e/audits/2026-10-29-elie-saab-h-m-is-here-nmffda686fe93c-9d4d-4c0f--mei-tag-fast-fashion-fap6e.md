# Elie Saab H&M is here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the second Elie Saab send in four days, and honestly it's the better one. The 10/26 "Unveil the new designer collab" email got me because it felt like H&M was first to something for once. Today's is the "it's actually live" follow-up, and the Paris red-carpet styling is gorgeous. The sequined blazer and the embellished peplum top are very "I'd screenshot this for my Saturday three-tab compare." But I have no price anchor on the hero beyond a tiny $219 tag, and no sense of how fast this sells out.
- What bugs me is that it's a pure lookbook. There's no deadline, no "online now / in stores Nov X," no size or availability info. I know collabs drop and vanish, so a good drop email should scream "go now." This one just whispers. Compare that to the 10/28 "Up to 25% off + free shipping ends today" email: that one had urgency but zero trend energy. These two H&M sends are basically different brands.
- Would I click? Probably, once, to check the sequined blazer and see if it's already gone. Would I forward it? Yes, to my group chat, but only the lace top, because $329 for a blazer isn't a Gen Z budget. I'd mark it read and go check Zara's version of the same look.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong red carpet shot in a fur-trim jacket and sequin skirt. It reads "event," not "catalog."
- The Paris backdrop, with the Eiffel Tower and the river, makes this feel like a real campaign rather than a studio shoot.
- Each product has its name, price and a "New Arrival" tag, so I can scan the cost fast. That's the price-per-trend info I want.
- The blurb explains why Elie Saab matters, which helps me understand the collab as an event.
- The "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE" buttons repeat after the product block, so I don't have to scroll back up.

## What's weak

- There's no drop date, no "limited" and no stock cue. For a collab, that's the one thing that should create FOMO.
- The prices are high for me: $429 for the leather jacket and $329 for the blazer. Nothing softens that, like a member perk or a cheaper entry piece up top.
- The product images aren't individually labeled as links, and the buttons are generic "SHOP NOW" to the whole collection. I can't tell where a tap on the lace top would land.
- The Women / Men / Kids / Home / Beauty block at the bottom is irrelevant to this email's story and dilutes it.
- The model casting is on the older, more elevated side, so it feels less like me and more like a gala guest list. The 24-year-old TikTok lens is missing, with no styling for a night out or a party.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real drop line in the hero, like "Live now online + select stores, while it lasts." Collab scarcity is the whole hook.
- 2. Make each product image a direct link with a tappable "Shop the blazer" style label, not a generic SHOP NOW.
- 3. Lead with the most accessible piece (the $109 lace top) to lower the barrier before the $429 jacket.
- 4. Cut the department grid on this send and keep the focus on the collab.
- 5. Show one styled-for-real-life look so it feels wearable, not just red-carpet.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based layout with a responsive `<style id="optimize-css">` block and routes every link through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracker. The QA run flagged 12 probed links as HTTP 403 and 14 images with missing alt text. Authentication and list-unsubscribe headers could not be checked because of the relay. The HTML was truncated, so the checks below cover only the visible portion.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed material links returned 403.** This includes "Shop now" (`...f89b4500`, `f89b4502`, `f89b4514`, `f89b4516`, `f89b44e9`, `f89b44eb`), "find a store" (`f89b4501`, `f89b4503`, `f89b4515`, `f89b4517`), the "H&M" logo link (`f89b44e8`) and "Unsubscribe" (`f89b453a`).
  - A uniform 403 on a signed ESP redirector is more likely bot or WAF blocking of the QA probe than a broken link, so this is **unconfirmed**.
  - The redirects were never followed, so no destination URL was verified. Re-test with a browser-like user agent or a real seed-inbox click.
- **Zero-width characters in link text.** Two "Shop now" links (`f89b44e9`, `f89b44eb`) have anchor text prefixed with invisible characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`, which looks like U+200C and U+FEFF plus spaces). Screen readers and link-text parsers may announce or read this inconsistently.
- **Repeated generic link text.** "Shop now" appears 6 times, and "find a store" or "Find a store" appears 4 times. Each points to a different tracker ID, but the text is identical.
- **Only 12 of 69 links were probed.** The other 57 are unverified.
- **Empty JSON-LD fields.** The `application/ld+json` block has `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"url":""`, `"image":""` and `availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T19:59:59-04:00"`. The schema is present but unpopulated, and the 2018 date looks like a stale template value. Gmail may ignore the markup or treat it as invalid.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **14 images are missing alt text** per the QA data. Examples:
  - Hero images: `FNP-2024-launch-16x9-DT-TOP.jpg` and `FNP-2024-launch-2x3-M-TOP.jpg`.
  - Logo: `FNP-2024-LOGO-2-v2.png`, at both `imWidth=1400` and `imWidth=750`.
  - Product tiles: `FNP-2024-launch-2x3-1.jpg`, `2x3-2.jpg`, `3x2-3.jpg` and `2x3-4.jpg`, each at 1400 and 750.
  - Two linked images whose `src` is a `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1` tracker URL.
  - These are desktop/mobile pairs, so the images are likely swapped with the `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` classes. Alt text is missing on both variants.
- **Image-only calls to action have no text fallback.** Images that carry the meaning, such as the logo and hero, have no alt text, so nothing displays or is announced when images are blocked.
- **Invalid CSS in `#optimize-css`:**
  - `line-height:auto!important` (in `.is-hidden-desktop`) is not a valid value and will be dropped.
  - `@media screen yahoo { ... }` is malformed (it should be `@media screen and (...)`) and will be ignored.
  - The breakpoints `(max-width:699px)` and `(min-width:699px)` both match at exactly 699px.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** `fabric.hmgroup.com` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css` are loaded this way. Gmail and several other clients strip `@import`, so these clients fall back to system fonts (a progressive-enhancement concern, not a bug).
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 522 of 600 characters are URLs (>70%), so the plain-text alternative is close to unusable and may raise spam-filter scores.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `rid`, `erid`, `did` and `p1`–`p5` parameters in the tracker URLs are resolved to values. The truncated HTML means the body copy was not checked.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is not confirmed as a sender-side gap.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be determined. Check the raw headers.
- **Body unsubscribe link is present** (`f89b453a`, with `p4` and `p5` per-recipient tokens). It returned 403 to the probe, so it is unverified. Confirm manually that it resolves.
- **Physical postal address.** The footer was truncated, so this could not be checked.
- The From address (`newsletter@email.hm.com`) is on the same organizational domain as the tracking host (`email.hm.com`), so domains are aligned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No visible UTM parameters.** Every link is a tracker redirect (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...&p1=DM6179990&p3=20261029`). Campaign attribution is carried in `p1` (campaign ID `DM6179990`) and `p3` (send date `20261029`). Any `utm_*` parameters would only appear after the redirect.
- **Landing pages not verified.** All redirects returned 403, so there is no evidence the "Elie Saab" subject matches the destination. Resolve the redirect chain to check this.

2. **Add alt text to all 14 flagged images.** Use descriptive text for the hero and product tiles, and "H&M" or the collection name for the logos. Use `alt=""` only for purely decorative images.
3. **Remove the zero-width characters** from the "Shop now" anchors (`f89b44e9`, `f89b44eb`). Differentiate repeated link text (for example, "Shop Elie Saab H&M women").
4. **Fix the CSS:** replace `line-height:auto` with a numeric or `normal` value and correct the `@media screen yahoo` query.
5. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD.** Fill `subjectLine`, `url`, `image` and the discount fields, and fix the 2018 `availabilityStarts` date, or drop the empty block.
6. **Check the raw headers** (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC pass). Confirm one-click unsubscribe is supported.
7. **Shorten the plain-text alternative's URL load.** Add readable copy so URLs are under 70% of the text.
