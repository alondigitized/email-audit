# 20% off Face + Body
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this one actually stopped my thumb. The hero is doing the thing — colorful, kind of chaotic editorial makeup energy, very BeautyTok — and the brand logo row lands almost immediately: Biossance, anua, Gisou. Like, ASOS actually pulled the TikTok-viral SKUs into one discount drop and I didn't have to scroll to find that out. That's rare for them. The 20% off Face + Body is clean, the hierarchy makes sense, and the model is giving expressive artsy makeup which I'm here for.
- That said, this email can't decide if it's a beauty email or a fashion email. By the time I get to "Selling fast on ASOS" I'm looking at denim minis and white tees and I've completely lost the thread. The "Last Minute Gifting" section is also bizarre — like, it's June, I'm shopping for myself, nobody told me this was a Father's Day beauty email and I'm definitely not buying my dad anua toner. It undercuts the self-care energy the top half built.
- I'd probably click to check what's in the Face + Body drop because anua and Gisou showing up in one promo with 20% off is genuinely good. I wouldn't forward this — the fashion pivot makes it feel too general to share. Mark-as-read-and-maybe-shop-later energy.

## What's working

- **The brand logo row is doing heavy lifting** — Biossance, anua, Gisou are literally the brands my FYP is obsessed with right now. Seeing them in the same email stops me cold.
- **Hero aesthetic is right** — the colorful editorial face paint on the model reads pure BeautyTok, not some sterile department store beauty shot.
- **Offer is clean and front-loaded** — "20% off Face + Body" is in the hero, no decoding required. Beats H&M's habit of making me scroll to find out what the "up to 60% off" actually covers.
- **"FIND YOUR SHADE" CTA in the foundation section** is a rare moment of specificity — finally a button that tells me what I'm clicking into.
- **"What's trending?" section** is a direct nod to how I actually shop — I check trends first, then buy. That framing clicks with me.

## What's weak

- **"Last Minute Gifting" section is completely off for me** — I came here for my skincare routine, not to buy someone's dad a face serum. Zara would have never mixed those briefs in one send.
- **The fashion pivot ("Selling fast on ASOS") tanks the email's focus** — by the time I hit denim minis below the fold, I've forgotten I was looking at a beauty drop. One email, one brief.
- **No expiry on the 20% off** — compared to ASOS's own June 15 Premier send which at least had "ASOS Premier" context, this offer just floats with no deadline. Why would I act now?
- **"SHOP NOW" is everywhere and means nothing** — three or four generic CTAs stack up instead of one clear pull like "Shop Gisou + anua" that would actually land with me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the fashion section or send it separately.** Beauty is the hero — protect the brief. A split email like this would never come from a brand that actually owns a trend.
- 2. **Add a deadline.** Even "ends Sunday" would push me from "maybe later" to "add to cart now." The June 15 Premier send didn't have one either and it was the same failure mode.
- 3. **Move the Gisou / anua brand logos higher** — right below the hero, before any product grid. That's the hook for someone like me and right now it sits in the middle.
- 4. **Swap generic CTAs for brand-specific pulls**: "Shop anua," "Shop Gisou," "Shop NYX" — one button per brand block, not five "SHOP NOW"s.
- 5. **Drop the "Last Minute Gifting" section or reframe it** as "gift yourself" if ASOS insists on the gifting angle. At least make me the recipient.
