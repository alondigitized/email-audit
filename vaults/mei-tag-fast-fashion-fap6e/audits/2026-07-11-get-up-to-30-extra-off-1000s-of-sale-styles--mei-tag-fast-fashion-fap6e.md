# , get up to 30% extra off 1000s of Sale styles
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I opened this and the FIRST thing I clocked was the subject line — it literally starts with a comma. Like, ", get up to 30% extra off." That's a busted first-name merge tag and it's glaring. I'm Mei, I've audited H&M, Zara, this same brand a dozen times this month — I know what a broken personalization token looks like, and it immediately signals that ASOS is blasting this to however many million people without QA-ing their own send. That's the vibe I walked into.
- Once I'm actually inside the email though? It's cleaner than the subject deserves. The hero is punchy, the product photography is genuinely on-trend (wide-leg linen, strappy sandals, that black strapless set is giving), and the "SALE: SELLING FAST" FOMO block mid-email is a smart pull. The code BONUS is front and center with an "Ends Monday" deadline, which at least gives me something to act on. Six outfit looks in the product grid, styling that feels current, decent model diversity. The bones are solid.
- But — and this matters for my cadence antenna — I just went back through my inbox and this is ASOS in sale mode again. They did the editorial reset on July 7 (the palette email actually slapped) and the dress lookbook on July 9 felt promising. And now we're back to "extra off sale styles" territory. Not quite the four-in-a-row grind they did before the editorial pivot, but the broken merge tag plus the retreaded offer structure means I'm clicking more out of muscle memory than genuine excitement. I'd click SHOP WOMEN'S, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediate and legible** — "UP TO 30% EXTRA OFF 1000s OF SALE STYLES" on a black background hits on first scroll, no ambiguity about what this email is doing.
- **Stacked discount logic is actually shown** — the sub-copy explaining "and there's already up to 75% off, so that means double discounts" is a smart way to make the math feel real rather than gimmicky.
- **Code + deadline combo** — BONUS with Ends Monday visible in the body is the right structure; I have a specific action and a timer.
- **Product grid styling is on-trend** — wide-leg silhouettes, linen textures, the strappy sandal moment, black strapless. This is where the summer 2026 aesthetic actually lives.
- **"SALE: SELLING FAST" urgency block mid-scroll** — a re-hook before the footer that works; stops the casual scroll-and-bounce.

## What's weak

- **Broken merge tag in the subject** — ", get up to 30% extra off" is sloppy in a way Zara would never let ship. That comma is the first character a subscriber sees and it signals mass-blast energy, not a curated send.
- **No pricing in the product grid** — I can see the items but I can't see if this specific dress or those sandals are actually 30% cheaper. The grid is editorial but it's selling air without price anchors.
- **SHOP MEN'S CTA on a women's send** — I know ASOS does both, but that "SHOP MEN'S" button in the hero is wasted real estate for me and splits the visual focus right where it shouldn't.
- **Brand logos section is filler** — the "SHOP OUR TOP BRANDS" block with tiny logos breaks the momentum after the outfit grid. Compared to H&M's "Sorted: your city summer wardrobe" which stayed editorially coherent top-to-bottom, this feels like a stitched-together template.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the merge tag before anything else** — QA the first-name token or remove it entirely. A clean "Get up to 30% extra off 1000s of Sale styles" subject is better than a broken personalized one. This is the single change that would lift this send most.
- 2. **Put prices or savings amounts on the product grid tiles** — even a small "was £45 now £28" overlay on each item converts the editorial grid into an actual shopping prompt.
- 3. **Cut the SHOP MEN'S hero CTA for the women's segment** — if ASOS is segmenting at all, the hero should go all-in on the women's offer. One primary CTA is more powerful.
- 4. **Replace the brand logos block with one more outfit look or a "trending right now" module** — editorial momentum shouldn't stall at a logo wall.
- 5. **Surface the Ends Monday deadline in the subject line** — it's doing work inside the email but it's buried from the inbox view.

### 1. Technical Summary

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Invalid meta tag placement (severity: medium)**
`<meta>` elements (Content-Type, charset, viewport, format-detection, x-apple-disable-message-reformatting) are placed inside a `<p>` tag within `<body>`, not inside `<head>`. Spec-compliant parsers may silently relocate or discard these, potentially breaking viewport scaling on iOS and disabling Apple data detector suppression.

```html
<body>
  <div …preheader…></div>
  <p>
    <meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>   ← invalid
    <meta charset="utf-8">               ← invalid
    …
```

**Empty preheader (severity: low)**
The preheader `<div>` is present but contains no text content. Inbox clients will pull the first visible body text as preheader copy instead, which is uncontrolled.

**Media query breakpoint at 460px (severity: low)**
`@media screen and (max-width:460px)` is below the de-facto email standard of 600px. Devices with viewports between 461–599px (many Android tablets, some landscape phones) will receive desktop layout with no mobile overrides.

**SVG font format included (severity: info)**
`FuturaPT-Book.svg` and `FuturaPT-Heavy.svg` are declared in `@font-face`. SVG fonts have zero email client support and add unnecessary payload. Remove both `url(…svg) format("svg")` lines.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**CRITICAL — Unresolved first name token in subject line**

```
Subject: , get up to 30% extra off 1000s of Sale styles
```

The leading comma confirms a first-name token (e.g., `[FIRST_NAME]`, `{{first_name}}`, or `%FIRSTNAME%`) evaluated to an empty string rather than falling back to a default. Every recipient received the broken subject. The `<title>` tag contains the identical broken string:

```html
<title>, get up to 30% extra off 1000s of Sale styles</title>
```

This indicates the title is generated from the same template variable with no null/empty guard.

**Root cause candidates:**
- No fallback value configured in the ESP (e.g., `{{first_name | default: ""}}` used instead of `{{first_name | default: "there"}}`)
- Segment included contacts with NULL or empty `first_name` fields that were not suppressed pre-send
