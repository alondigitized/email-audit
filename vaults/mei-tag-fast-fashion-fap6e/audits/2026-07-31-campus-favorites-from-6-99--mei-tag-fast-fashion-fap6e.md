I count "Straight A's in back to school style," "Marvel Bombas," "Discover must-have backpacks for campus," "Make it count 50% off everything ends soon," and now this one — all dated today. That's not a campaign, that's carpet bombing my inbox, and it makes "Campus favorites from $6.99" feel less like a curated drop and more like whatever's left in the queue.
- The actual email is fine, visually — clean model shot, sale prices are legible, the grid is organized. But the subject promised me stuff from $6.99 and the hero banner just says "up to 30% off select styles" with zero specific mention of that price point. I had to scroll past six other items (some $20-40+) before I found the one $6.99 piece, tucked in as a "New Arrival" long-sleeve tee. That's a bait-and-switch move and I clock it immediately.
- Then randomly it pivots into "THE FRENCH BEDROOM" — bedding, towels, loungewear — which has nothing to do with campus or the trend cycle I actually track. I'm not opening this again today, I'm definitely not forwarding it, and honestly this is the one I'd start marking as read without clicking just to make the volume stop.

- The hero shot is styled well — I'd actually screenshot that jacket-and-trousers fit for my "outfit inspo" folder.
- Sale prices are struck through clearly (Harper jeans $27.99, balloon-leg pants $19.99) so the discount math is visible at a glance.
- Two CTAs up top ("SHOP NOW" / "FIND A STORE") give me an easy tap target either way.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, nothing overlapping, images are crisp.

## What's weak

- The $6.99 promised in the subject is nowhere near the top — it's the 7th product down, one long-sleeve tee. That's the classic move ASOS never pulls when they lead with an actual named collab like the adidas Originals drop I scored a 7 on.
- This is my fifth H&M email today. Zara's "Woman | The New" catalog-dump got a 4 from me for being lazy, but at least it was ONE email — H&M is out here sending five separate sends the same day and none of them feel distinct.
- "The French Bedroom" section derails the whole "campus" narrative into home goods I didn't ask for and don't care about.
- No urgency, no deadline, no loyalty pricing shown anywhere — nothing pushing me to act today vs. whenever.
- "Campus" framing doesn't even land on me — I'm 24, not shopping dorm fits, and this feels like recycled back-to-school copy dressed up as trend content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the actual $6.99 item in the hero if that's the subject line hook — don't make me hunt for it. Either put a real cheap hero product up top or change the subject to match what's actually featured (30% off).
- 2. Cut the send frequency. Five emails in one day is how I unsubscribe, not how you build loyalty — batch this into ONE strong send instead of scattering it across the day.
- 3. Drop "The French Bedroom" from this email entirely — it breaks the "campus fashion" thread and reads like two different emails stapled together.
- 4. Add a deadline or urgency cue ("ends Sunday") — right now nothing tells me why I should act now vs. next week.
- 5. If this is meant to be "trend-first," say so — show me what's new-in vs. what's just restocked, since that's literally the thing I'm comparing across Shein/Zara/ASOS every Saturday.
