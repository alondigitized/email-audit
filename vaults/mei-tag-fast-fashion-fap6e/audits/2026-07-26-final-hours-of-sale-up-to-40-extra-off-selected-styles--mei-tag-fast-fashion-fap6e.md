# Final hours of Sale: up to 40% extra off selected styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, LASTCHANCE energy — I respect it. "SALE: FINAL HOURS" scrolling across the top like a stock ticker, hot pink block, 40% EXTRA off on top of 70% off, code LASTCHANCE, ends Monday 8am BST. That's aggressive and I'm kind of here for it because the math is actually insane if you stack it right. But also... ASOS, we talked about this on the 20th when you sent me TWO emails same day and I called out inbox fatigue, then the 22nd where you went full editorial "Fringe is in" with zero discount talk right after a 70% off blast. Now we're back to sale mode again. This is like the fourth different energy from you in a week and it's giving whiplash more than urgency.
- The product grid is doing real work though — the gold strappy heels and that black going-out set with the sneakers are genuinely items I'd screenshot for my group chat. Nothing here is trend-editorial the way "Fringe is in" was, it's just straight sale merchandising, which is fine for what it is.
- Would I click? Probably yes, purely because "up to 40% extra off" + a code is a concrete enough hook that I'll at least tap SHOP WOMEN'S to see what's left. Would I forward to a friend? Only if she's already got something in her cart — this isn't a "OMG look at this drop" forward, it's a "use this code before it expires" forward, more transactional than exciting.

## What's working

- The stacked discount math (40% extra on top of 70%) is a legit hook — bigger number than H&M's "up to 20% off your order" send from a few days back, no contest there.
- Clear countdown pressure with an actual deadline (Monday 8am BST) instead of vague "ends soon" — more credible than most urgency copy I see.
- Product grid mixes silhouettes — slip dress, chunky knit, denim, heels, going-out set, midi skirt — so it doesn't feel like one dumped category.
- Two clean CTA buttons up top (Shop Women's / Shop Men's) sit right under the offer, no scrolling needed to find them.
- Code is visible without digging (LASTCHANCE), not buried in fine print like some sites do.

## What's weak

- This is sale-fatigue territory — three sale-flavored sends in a week (70% off on the 20th, now this 40% extra on the 26th) with an editorial breather in between. Zara at least spaces theirs out more, even if "ATHLETICZ | CLIMBING" was a miss content-wise.
- Zero social proof, zero "selling fast" indicators on the actual product tiles — just static images, no "only X left" or reviews stars anywhere.
- The bottom half is dead weight — "Shop Our Top Brands" circles, a lime green app download banner, then a giant ASOS wordmark logo. That's a lot of scroll for zero additional offer info.
- No sizing/fit callouts anywhere near the products, and as someone who actually buys off these emails, that's the first thing I'd need before tapping through on the heels or the knit.
- This feels like every other sale email in every other inbox right now — nothing here says "ASOS specifically" the way the Rina Lipa drop or "Fringe is in" did. It's generic retail-sale mode.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the cadence, not the creative — stop stacking sale-sale-editorial-sale in one week. Pick a lane per week so LASTCHANCE actually feels like the final beat instead of the fourth alarm.
- 2. Add a tiny social-proof or scarcity line on 2-3 of the product tiles ("selling fast," a star rating) — right now the grid is just pretty pictures with no reason to act on THIS item specifically.
- 3. Trim the bottom third — the brand-circles + app banner + giant wordmark block before the legal text is pure scroll tax on mobile with no payoff.
- 4. Pull the code (LASTCHANCE) up into the subject or preview text since it's the single most actionable piece of copy in the whole email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Code LASTCHANCE: 40% extra off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — stack 40% more off Sale before Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gold heels, the going-out set, everything you saved — LASTCHANCE at checkout`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `8am Monday it's gone. Shop before the code expires.`
