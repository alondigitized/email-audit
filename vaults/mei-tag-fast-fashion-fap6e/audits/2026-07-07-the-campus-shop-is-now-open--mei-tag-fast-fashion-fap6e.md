# The Campus Shop is now open
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay so after five straight 20% off emails last week — literally the July 3rd "red, white & all new," the July 4th "main event," the July 5th "ends today" — and then yesterday's full-price lookbook that came in like nothing happened, H&M is now pivoting to a back-to-campus launch. The tonal whiplash is real. But honestly, as a standalone email? The "COZY-CHIC" section with the oversized striped sweater and the relaxed layered look is actually serving — that aesthetic is everywhere on my FYP right now and it belongs in a BTS story.
- The problem is the 15% off offer buried three full scrolls down requires me to bring old clothes into a physical store to redeem. Like… I am not hauling a bag to an H&M to get a digital checkout discount. The whole email feels like H&M had one good creative instinct (cozy campus) and then also threw in "PLAY IT COOL" for men, "HOME COMFORTS" for dorm décor, and a sustainability pledge — none of which I asked for.
- I would not click, I would not forward. I'd mark it read and feel mild relief that at least they stopped counting down the sale.

## What's working

- The "COZY-CHIC" section nails a real trend — oversized striped sweater, relaxed layering, that's the TikTok cozy-girl aesthetic that ASOS led with in their palette email and it works here too
- Subject line is clean at 30 chars and reads well on mobile — "The Campus Shop is now open" is a launch statement, not another sale countdown
- "TAX-FREE DAYS ARE COMING" as the preheader adds a different hook from the subject instead of just repeating it — ASOS does the same subject-as-preheader thing constantly and it's lazy
- The hero model reads young and college-coded, demographic signal at least points at me
- Switching formats entirely after the sale tunnel was the right instinct — another 20% off email this week would have been unsubscribe territory

## What's weak

- The 15% off offer requires an in-store clothing donation — digital email, physical friction, no thank you
- "HOME COMFORTS" sitting inside a fashion email is completely random — it breaks the campus energy and I do not need H&M throw pillows right now
- This is the sixth H&M email in one week — whatever BTS goodwill exists is buried under cadence fatigue
- "PLAY IT COOL" is the men's graphic tee section but there's no women's equivalent for that vintage-graphic trend, which is actually huge right now — missed
- The 15% off is invisible on first open, buried below the fold on multiple scrolls

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 15% off above the fold** — either into the hero or directly below it; if that's the conversion hook, lead with it
- 2. **Reframe the lead section as a trend, not a category** — something like "the cozy layer is your campus uniform this year" tells me H&M owns this aesthetic; just saying "BACK TO CAMPUS" tells me they're following everyone else
- 3. **Cut the HOME COMFORTS block** — or send it as a separate home email; it dilutes the fashion story
- 4. **Surface one specific hero product with a visible price tag** — Zara doesn't do this either and I've clocked them for it; every section here links to a category landing page, not an actual item
- 5. **Implement send suppression** — anyone who received all five previous 20% off emails should not also get a sixth email this week regardless of topic
