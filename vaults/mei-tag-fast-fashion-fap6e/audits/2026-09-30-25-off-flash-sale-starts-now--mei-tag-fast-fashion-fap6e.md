# 25% off flash sale starts now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the subject says "flash sale starts now" but the banner at the very top says "LAST DAY! UP TO 30% OFF FALL ESSENTIALS*", and then the hero says "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET". So is it starting or ending? Is it 25% or 30%? I'm a Saturday three-tab comparer, and I notice when a brand can't keep its own story straight. It made the sale feel like clearance rather than a moment.
- The hero is fine. It's a model in a cobalt jacket and baggy jeans, and the big "25% OFF" type is impossible to miss. But "comfy sweats, classic sweaters & everyday basics" is the opposite of what I open H&M for. Nothing here says trend. Below the hero it's a long scroll of joggers, a sweater vest, and then cushion covers and a stoneware vase. I'm 24 and I rent, and this is the second home-goods push in two days after "Stylish Halloween decor" on 09-29.
- Would I click? Maybe once, for the wide-leg jeans, since 25% is real money. I wouldn't forward it. It's the fifth discount-flavored H&M send in a week, after 09-26's "cardi party", 09-27's "Last day: 20% off or 15% off" and 09-28's "Fall's signature looks". Zara's "Re{form}" teaser at least felt like it owned the trend, and ASOS's Lioness drop had a point of view. This one is just a sale.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" hero is huge and readable on my phone, and the deadline is clear.
- The hero model's blue jacket and baggy denim is actually my aesthetic, and the image is crisp.
- Prices sit right under each product. The red sale price next to the crossed-out one on the flannel shirt and the wide-leg jeans makes the savings easy to see.
- The "New Arrival" tags under items tell me what's fresh, which I care about when I'm checking who ships a trend first.
- Women / Men / Baby / Kids / Home / Beauty sit as tap-friendly buttons right under the hero.

## What's weak

- The message contradicts itself: "starts now" in the subject, "LAST DAY" in the banner, 25% in the hero, and "up to 30%" in the header.
- The asterisk and the wall of fine print at the bottom make the offer feel less honest. I can't tell which items are actually 25% off.
- Product names are cut off ("RIB-KNIT BATWING-SLEE... SWEATER", "KNIT FOLDOVER-WAI... PANTS"). That looks sloppy, and I can't tell what I'm buying.
- The "Cozy Touches" block (marble box, vase, cushion covers) is home decor again, right after 09-29's Halloween decor. It's off-persona and dilutes the sale.
- Nothing says TikTok, trending, or viral. It's "basics" and "essentials", so H&M is following the trend, not chasing it.
- The only CTAs are small "SHOP NOW" text links, and the product grid has no button, so nothing clearly says where to go first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer story. Pick one: 25% off, ends midnight ET, and use it in the subject, banner and hero. Cut "LAST DAY" and "starts now" from one of them.
- 2. Lead with trend, not "essentials". Make the hero the wide-leg denim or the trending outerwear and say so, like "The jeans everyone's wearing, 25% off".
- 3. Drop the home block from a fashion-first send, or split it into a separate segment for people who click Home.
- 4. Fix the truncated product names, or shorten them so I can read them.
- 5. Add one real button like "Shop 25% off women's" under the hero instead of a tiny text link.
- 6. Put the exclusions in plain words near the hero, so I don't have to scroll to the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off ends tonight at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wide-leg denim is 25% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jeans, jackets & knits — 25% off till midnight ET. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The fall fits you've been saving, now 25% off. Today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an H&M table-based template served through a `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirector. Automated QA shows a 16% pass rate: all 12 probed links returned 403, and dozens of images have no alt text. The 403s most likely come from the redirector blocking the QA probe, so they are unconfirmed as real breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe results:** All 12 probed links returned 403. These include the "Shop now" CTAs (`...,f8528f19`, `...,f8528f1b`, `...,f8529019`, `...,f852901a`, `...,f8529037`, `...,f8529038`), the logo link ("H&M", `...,f8528f18`), the hero image links (`...,f852900b`, `...,f852900c`), "Women" (`...,f852900d`), "Men" (`...,f852900e`) and "Unsubscribe" (`...,f85290c1`).
- **Likely cause:** The uniform 403 across every link, including the unsubscribe, points to bot or WAF filtering of a non-browser user agent. The redirector does not appear to be genuinely broken. It could not be verified either way, so treat it as unresolved rather than as a confirmed defect.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 192 links were probed. The 180 skipped links include footer and social links and are unverified.
- **Invisible characters in CTA text:** Two "Shop now" links have link text beginning with zero-width characters (`"‌  ﻿Shop now"`). These can be announced or selected oddly by assistive tech and can break text-based link matching.
- **Empty link text:** Image-only links surface as `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]` and `[image: cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg]`. Their accessible names depend on alt text, which is missing (see section 3).
- **Recipient identifiers in URLs:** Every link carries per-recipient parameters (`rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==`, which base64-decodes to the same value, `p2=0982dd0a...`). The unsubscribe URL also carries `p4=3326930539` and `p5=9be06746...`. This is standard for ESP tracking, but it is a forwarding and privacy consideration.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** The QA check flags about 40 images as missing alt text. They include:
  - the hero images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, both from `.../wk-40/us150_s4/1364426002_202604_LB_2733.jpg/`);
  - every product image in the grid (for example `6c5672b9...jpg?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322`);
  - the tracking-pixel-style image at `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1`.
- **Duplicate renditions:** Each product image appears twice, at `imWidth=344` and `imWidth=322`. This is presumably for desktop and mobile variants. Both lack alt text, so screen readers may encounter them twice unless one is hidden correctly.
- **`@import` fonts:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/.../fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css)` are used for web fonts. Gmail and several Outlook clients ignore or strip `@import`, so they fall back to system fonts.
- **Invalid CSS value:** The `is-hidden-desktop` rule sets `line-height:auto!important`. `auto` is not a valid `line-height` value, so the declaration is dropped.
- **Plain-text part:** 516 of 594 characters are URLs, so the text alternative is almost entirely tracking links.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The source was truncated, so the remainder was not checked.

The JSON-LD block has empty fields: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"description":""`, `"image":""` and `"url":""`. The `availabilityStarts` value is a stale static date, `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`. This looks like an unpopulated template. It produces an invalid or empty Gmail promotion annotation and could prevent the email from being eligible for Gmail promo cards.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is not confirmed as an sender-side gap. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, which is expected through the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link:** A link with text "Unsubscribe" is present in the footer, so the CAN-SPAM requirement for an unsubscribe mechanism appears to be met in the HTML. However, it returned 403 to the probe (`...,f85290c1`), so its functionality is unverified.
- **Physical postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so it could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking redirects:** Every link is an opaque `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` redirect. The final destinations could not be resolved because the probes were blocked, so landing-page alignment and UTM parameters are unverifiable.
- **Campaign identifiers:** `p1=DM6094679` and `p3=20260930` are present and consistent across all links. No visible `utm_*` parameters are on the email-side URLs. Any UTMs would have to be appended at redirect time.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracked links with a browser-like user agent or a headless browser, or allowlist the QA probe. This would confirm whether the 403s are real. Verify the unsubscribe URL (`...,f85290c1`) first.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all product and hero images. Use `alt=""` only for decorative images, and verify the two image sizes are not both exposed to screen readers.
3. Remove the zero-width characters from the CTA link text ("Shop now").
4. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block. The empty `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `image` and `url` fields and the 2018 `availabilityStarts` date should be fixed or dropped.
5. Confirm the raw headers at the sending side for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The relay may be hiding them.
6. Replace `@import` font loading with `<link>` or web-safe fallbacks, and fix `line-height:auto` to a valid value such as `normal`.
7. Improve the plain-text part with readable text rather than mostly URLs.
