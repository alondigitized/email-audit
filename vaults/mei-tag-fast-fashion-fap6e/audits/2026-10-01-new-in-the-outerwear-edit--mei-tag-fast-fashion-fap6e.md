# New in: The Outerwear Edit
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, H&M finally sent me clothes with a point of view, but it's still wearing the same discount banner as yesterday. The hero is a model in a black leather coat with sunglasses and a "WINDSWEPT" headline, and it's pretty. But the very top still says "UP TO 30% OFF FALL ESSENTIALS*". That's the same banner from yesterday's "LAST DAY!" send, which was supposed to be the last day. So was it? The 09/30 flash sale email already made me side-eye their deadlines, and this one doesn't fix that.
- On trend credibility, this lands in the middle. "Windswept" and "The Outerwear Edit" sound like an editorial drop. But the products are a trench, a drawstring jacket and a varsity bomber, all priced the same $69.99. That reads like a catalog with mood lighting. Zara's "Re{form}" email had me wanting to scroll. This one has me scrolling to check prices.
- Would I click? Probably one tap on the trench coat, since it's cute and the price is fine. Would I forward it? No. I'd text my friend about the bomber if it were in the product grid, but it's only in a hero shot with no price. Mark as read, maybe save the trench for Saturday's three-tab compare.

## What's working

- The trench coat on the oversized hero crop looks expensive for $69.99, so the price-per-trend math works.
- The leather jacket with sunglasses has a real "it's giving fall TikTok" feel. The model and styling look like someone my age.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images and nothing overlapping. It's easy to thumb through on my phone.
- Prices are on the product tiles, so I don't have to tap to find out if I can afford it.
- The varsity bomber in "The Outerwear Edit" section is the freshest piece. It feels like a trend, not just a coat.

## What's weak

- The "up to 30% off" banner is tiny text at the very top, with an asterisk. It doesn't tell me which coats are discounted, and the deadline (10/07) is only in the fine print at the bottom.
- It's the same banner as 09/30's "LAST DAY!" email. The urgency doesn't feel credible when it comes back the next day. This is the fourth H&M email in a week, after 09/27 and 09/28, and I'm tired of it.
- Every product is $69.99, including the leather jacket. That looks lazy, and I don't trust it.
- All the CTAs say "SHOP NOW", with no verb plus noun like "Shop the trench".
- The bomber hero at the bottom has no price and no product link that I can see. It's the best piece and it's buried.
- The fine print is a wall of text. Exclusions, member rules and a $1 bonus reward are all hidden down there. ASOS at least tells me what's in the drop up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero, with a specific deadline. Something like "30% off outerwear through 10/07" in big text, not a tiny banner with an asterisk.
- 2. Give me one hero per product, with its own link and a clear label like "Shop the trench". Right now it's three "SHOP NOW" buttons that all go who-knows-where.
- 3. Move the varsity bomber up. It's the trend piece, so lead with it. Zara's Re{form} drop did this well, with product first and the story second.
- 4. Stop recycling the same banner across sends. Rotate the offer, or I'll stop reading the banner at all.
- 5. Show real price variety and a "new" tag on the pieces that are actually new. That's what tells me H&M got there first and isn't chasing.

# Technical Audit: H&M, "New in: The Outerwear Edit"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly sound. It uses a table layout, a viewport meta tag, mobile media queries and a `prevent-auto-links` class. The main problems are that all 12 probed tracking links returned 403 (including Unsubscribe), widespread missing image alt text, and empty or stale JSON-LD markup. The 403s are most likely the prober being blocked rather than 12 independent breaks, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403**, including 10 "Shop now" links, the hero link ("UP TO 30% OFF FALL ESSENTIALS…") and Unsubscribe. All share one click-tracker pattern: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h6f8ba707-…,548dbe79,<linkid>&did=1418575481&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6101831&p3=20261001`.
- **Likely cause:** a uniform 403 across every link on one tracker host points to bot or WAF filtering of the QA probe, not 12 separate broken links. The data can't confirm whether real recipients are affected. The 16% pass rate is largely an artifact of this.
- **Unverified:** the Unsubscribe 403 matters most. Confirm it manually with a browser user agent, since a failure there would be a compliance problem.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 links were probed and 55 were skipped, so the footer, utility and social links were not validated.
- **Link text:** the two "Shop now" links with link text `"‌  ﻿Shop now"` (ids `f854c880`, `f854c882`) contain invisible characters (zero-width non-joiner and BOM) in the anchor text.
- **Every link is wrapped in a redirect.** Final destinations can't be seen from the source or the probe.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA check flagged 14 images (identifiers derived from `src`), for example:
  - `image.hm.com/.../wa10264c02/FNP-WA10264C02-3x2-2.jpg`
  - `.../FNP-WA10264C02-2x3-7.jpg`
  - `.../men/mf05264p03/FNP-MF05264P03-2x3-5.jpg`
  - two images wrapped in `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` links
  - Images inside links with no alt text leave those links with no accessible name when images are blocked.
- **Duplicate image variants.** Several images appear as both `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` (for example `2x3-7`, `2x3-5`, `3x2-4`, `mf05264p03-2x3-5` ×2). These look like paired mobile/desktop renditions toggled by `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop`. If the hidden copy isn't removed from the accessibility tree, screen readers may announce the image twice.
- **Repeated link text.** At least 10 links read just "Shop now", with no distinguishing context for screen-reader link lists.
- **Invalid CSS in `<style>`:**
  - `line-height:auto!important` in `.is-hidden-desktop` is not a valid value.
  - `@media screen yahoo` is not a valid media query.
  - Both are harmless in most clients but are dead rules.
- **Fonts.** The `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...)` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css` imports are stripped by Gmail and ignored by some clients. Confirm a web-safe fallback stack exists; the truncated source doesn't show one.
- **Plain-text part is thin.** 520 of 598 characters (87%) are URLs, so the text alternative is almost entirely tracking links with little readable content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient parameters (`rid`, `erid`, `p2`, and `p4`/`p5` on Unsubscribe) are populated. The source was truncated, so the rest of the body couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this can't be confirmed as a sender-side omission. Check the raw message at the source mailbox before treating it as a defect. One-click support is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `newsletter@email.hm.com` with click tracking on `t19.email.hm.com`. This is a limit of the relay, not a confirmed failure.
- **In-body unsubscribe link present** (`…f854c8d1…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`), but it returned 403 to the probe and is unverified.
- The truncated source doesn't show the physical postal address footer, so it couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Attribution runs through the ESP redirector (`p1=DM6101831`, `p3=20261001`), so any UTMs would be appended server-side after the redirect.
- **Destinations couldn't be resolved.** The 403s blocked the redirect chain, so landing page alignment with the hero offer ("Up to 30% off… in stores & online*") can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a browser-like user agent or by manual click-through. This matters most for Unsubscribe, to separate prober blocking from real breakage.
2. Check the raw headers at the origin for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before reporting them as gaps.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 14 flagged images, with `alt=""` for purely decorative ones. Make sure hidden responsive duplicates are removed from the accessibility tree, for example with `aria-hidden="true"`.
4. Make the "Shop now" link text unique, for example by adding the category via `aria-label`, and strip the invisible characters from the two affected anchors.
5. Fix or remove the JSON-LD blocks: `subjectLine`, `DiscountOffer.description`, `discountCode`, `PromotionCard.image` and `PromotionCard.url` are all empty, and `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`. Empty or stale markup is ignored at best and may be rejected by Gmail's schema validation.
6. Enrich the plain-text alternative with readable product and category text so URLs are under 70% of it.
7. Clean up the invalid CSS (`line-height:auto`, `@media screen yahoo`).
