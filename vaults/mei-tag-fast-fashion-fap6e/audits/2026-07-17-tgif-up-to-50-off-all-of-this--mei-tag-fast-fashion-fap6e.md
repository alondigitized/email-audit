# TGIF: Up to 50% off ALL of this
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I need to preface this with: H&M has been in my inbox literally every single day this week, and as of yesterday they sent me *two* in one day — the hiking MOVE thing AND the $12.49 shorts email. So when TGIF: Up to 50% off ALL of this landed this morning, I already had inbox scar tissue. That said — the TGIF hook is the most personality I've seen from H&M all week, and 50% off almost everything is genuinely compelling if you need a summer wardrobe reset.
- The email itself is clean and renders fine. The women's section upfront has the right pieces — the short vest and wide-leg jeans are actually on-trend — but then I had to scroll through MEN, KIDS, BABY, and HOME before I got to a "SHOP BY" grid that finally let me navigate. As a 24-year-old woman with no kids and no yard, that middle section is pure scroll tax. The offer is real. The editorial energy isn't.
- Would I click? Probably, but grudgingly — straight to the Women's sale section, not through this email's journey. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and shop later? Yes, if I remember.

## What's working

- **TGIF framing is the freshest H&M has felt all week** — it's still a sale email but "TGIF" actually carries weekend-energy that yesterday's hiking email didn't have
- **The 50% off headline is crystal clear** — big, bold, above the fold, no math required
- **Women's hero items are on-trend** — the short vest in both colorways and the wide-high jeans are genuinely current; H&M is showing it knows what's selling right now
- **Member perk upfront** — "FREE SHIPPING OVER $50 FOR H&M MEMBERS" as the very first line is a smart pull, makes being on the list feel worth it
- **Renders clean** — no broken images, no layout blowout, product names and prices visible throughout

## What's weak

- **The scroll tax is brutal** — Men, Kids, Baby, and Home before I see a "Shop by" option; for a solo 24F this email is three times too long
- **50% off feels like reruns** — July 15 was "Up to 50% off is on the rise" (8/10), and now this is the same discount wearing a TGIF hat; H&M is burning its own urgency
- **No trend language anywhere** — ASOS dropped a Rina Lipa collab email that opened with genuine cultural heat; this opens with "Off-duty pieces, on-point prices — find effortless looks for every plan," which is the most beige copy I've read all week
- **Deadline is buried** — the fine print at the bottom says "through Sunday" but the hero acts like this discount exists forever; one line under the hero saying "this weekend only" would change the entire click calculus
- **"RELAXED" and "LAD-BACK" as editorial labels** — those vibes read 2019, not summer 2026; Zara's "The weekly edit" framing at least tries for something current

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the Sunday deadline in the hero** — add "Ends Sunday" directly under the 50% off headline; urgency that's only in fine print doesn't count as urgency
- 2. **Women-only send or women-first scroll** — MEN + KIDS + BABY + HOME should either be cut or collapsed to a single small row for a segment this young and female-skewing; the scroll drops off a cliff past Women
- 3. **Swap "find effortless looks for every plan" for something trend-forward** — something like "this week's most-copied looks, now half off" would actually sound like H&M knows why I'm subscribed
- 4. **Lead the Women's section with the trending item, not just the vest** — show me what's blowing up on TikTok this week, then tell me it's 50% off; that's a click
- 5. **Add a single editorial hook** — ASOS used Rina Lipa, Zara uses named edits; H&M's Women section could be "The quiet-luxury haul" or anything with a cultural handle vs. just "RELAXED"

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your TGIF haul: free shipping for members, offer ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop women's, kids, home — up to half off through this weekend`
