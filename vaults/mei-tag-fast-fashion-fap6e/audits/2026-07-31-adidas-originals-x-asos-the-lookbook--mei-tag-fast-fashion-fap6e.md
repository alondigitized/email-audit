## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the second adidas Originals x ASOS lookbook email in two days from them (they did "Collection 04 is here" on 7/30, now it's "the lookbook" on 7/31) and honestly? I'm not mad about it, but I am side-eyeing the back-to-back timing. This one leans hard into the "styled by real people" format — tagging @lozvassallo, @lissyroddy, @rebeccajackson with actual outfit call-outs, which is giving TikTok GRWM energy instead of studio catalog, and that's exactly the vibe that makes me trust a drop over just seeing it on a mannequin. The plaid co-ord on the first girl in front of the black door is genuinely a "I'd screenshot this for my Pinterest board" moment.
- But there's zero offer here. No price, no %, no "shop before it sells out" — just four outfit vignettes and then a "shop our top brands" carousel that feels bolted on. I'd scroll through this for the styling inspo, maybe screenshot the striped polo fit, but I'm not clicking through to buy today because nothing here is telling me why NOW versus next week. I'd mark as read, not forward — my group chat gets Zara Woman-dump screenshots and H&M five-email-a-day chaos roasted, this one's too tame to roast or hype.

## What's working

- The influencer-style tagging (@lozvassallo, @lissyroddy, @rebeccajackson) makes this feel like a lookbook I'd actually save, not an ad — way more "trend is theirs" than ASOS's own "We're crushing on new-in Topshop" send from 7/27 which just used generic model shots.
- Four distinct color stories (grey plaid, red matching set, pastel blue/buttermilk, mixed stripes) show range in one scroll without dumping the whole catalog on me like Zara's "Woman | The New" did.
- Styling captions actually tell me HOW to wear it ("mix and match your pastels," "stripes on satin is your new summer uniform") — that's useful info I don't get from H&M's flat product grids.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text, images all loaded, easy to scroll on my phone without pinch-zooming.

## What's weak

- No offer, no price, nothing — I have literally no idea what these pieces cost, which kills any urgency to tap through right now.
- This is the SECOND adidas x ASOS email in 24 hours (7/30's "Collection 04 is here" then this "lookbook" follow-up) — cute concept, but back-to-back sends on the same drop is starting to feel like the Guess-who's-back restock email from 7/29 that also had zero substance beyond pretty pictures.
- CTA is just "SHOP THE COLLECTION" up top — generic, not linked to a specific hero piece, so I don't know where I'd even land.
- "Shop our top brands" icon row at the bottom feels totally disconnected from an adidas-focused email — random Mango/Bershka/Topshop logos when I'm here for the collab, not a brand directory.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or "from $X" under at least the hero look — right now this reads like a lookbook, not a shopping email, and that's the single biggest thing keeping me from tapping through.
- 2. Space this out from the 7/30 "Collection 04 is here" send — either merge into one email or wait a few days, back-to-back same-collection emails read as filler the way the restock email did.
- 3. Make each outfit photo directly tappable/shoppable to that specific piece instead of one generic top CTA.
- 4. Cut the "Shop our top brands" module — it dilutes the collab focus and feels like a different email got pasted in.
- 5. Add a real urgency beat (limited drop sizes, "selling fast") since adidas collabs actually do sell out — that's a legit reason to click today, not later.
