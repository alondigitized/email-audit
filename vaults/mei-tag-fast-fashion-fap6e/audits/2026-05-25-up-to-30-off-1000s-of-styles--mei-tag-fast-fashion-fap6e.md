# Up to 30% off 1000s of styles
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay so ASOS is in my inbox AGAIN — this is hit number FOUR in a week. May 18 "what's hot this summer," May 20 embellished pieces, May 22 leopard print, and now May 25 "up to 30% off 1000s of styles." The first three at least had a *trend* hook I could chase — leopard print is literally a TikTok search term. This one? It's a generic sitewide discount with a code (BANK30LS) and a tired "Early Tuesday Bank" line I can barely read because the body copy under the headline is microscopic.
- The hero stack is so random — a candle, a perfume bottle, a foundation, a black dress on a model, a green book(?), and then some white packaging. There's zero point of view. It reads like H&M's "tops & shorts for all" email where they're just throwing the catalog at the wall. At least Zara's emails commit to one category, even if it's the wrong one (kids, lol).
- I'm not clicking. Not forwarding. Straight to archive. The 30% off isn't even good enough — they had embellished pieces with a real trend angle five days ago and that scored higher with me.

## What's working

- The headline "Up to 30% off 1000s of styles" is at least legible and the % is the biggest thing on screen.
- Split CTAs for Women's and Men's right under the hero — that's smart, faster tap than scrolling.
- The model in the black dress gives me one human signal in a sea of product cutouts.
- "DOWNLOAD THE APP" green bar pops — actually the most eye-catching element on the whole email, which says something.

## What's weak

- The body copy under the headline ("Hurry, a little treat for the Bank holiday! We think you're going to love these. Use code: BANK30LS / Ends Tuesday 8am.*") is so small I had to squint. The code is the whole offer and it's buried in 8pt grey text.
- The hero product grid is incoherent — candle, perfume, foundation, dress, book, packaging? I'm a fashion shopper. Why is beauty + homeware leading?
- "SHOP OUR TOP BRANDS" row with Vans, New Balance, Levi's etc. is fine but there's no offer attached to any of them — am I getting 30% on those? Unclear.
- Bank holiday framing is UK-coded and doesn't really land for me — feels like a copy-paste from the British market.
- This is the fourth ASOS email in a week. The cadence is killing the trust I had after the embellished-pieces send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick a category.** Lead with one trend (the black dress, or revisit embellished pieces) and let the discount support it. ASOS's wins on me have always been trend-first. The May 20 send proved this — drop the catalog grid.
- 2. **Surface the code.** Put BANK30LS in the hero, big, with the deadline. Right now it's tucked in a sentence smaller than the legal disclaimer.
- 3. **Kill the beauty + homeware in the hero** if this is a fashion send. Or split into two emails. Don't make me decode what's on sale.
- 4. **Slow the cadence.** Four in a week is Zara/H&M territory and I'm tuning out.
