# Up to 30% off 100s of knits just dropped
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, my gut reaction is that this is H&M's fifth sale email in about a week, and I'm tired. The hero is a guy in a beige knit holding a black tote, with "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" in red over his legs. It's clean, but it's not trend-first. It's just "sweaters are 30% off." On 11/09 H&M at least gave me a "Red basics" color story, and on 11/06 through 11/08 it was the 25% tiered spend-more-save-more thing. Now the offer is smaller (30% vs. 25% plus free shipping) and the subject says "just dropped" like something's new. It's a markdown, not a drop.
- Below the hero I get a 2x2 grid: Women, Men and Kids. The red cardigan under "Fan Favorites" is cute, but it's one model and no styling. The huge black "30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE" block in dark red on black is hard to read, and I'm not walking into a store on a Tuesday for a sweater. Zara and ASOS would have put a girl in an oversized knit with a styled outfit and a trend name. I wouldn't forward this. I'd skim it and maybe tap "Women" if I'm bored on the bus.

## What's working

- The 30% number is huge, black and bold, and easy to read on my phone.
- Putting "Shop Now" and "Find a Store" side by side tells me what to do.
- The red cardigan tile under "Fan Favorites" is the one thing that felt on-trend for me.
- The category grid (Women, Men, Kids) lets me jump straight to my section.
- Knits in November is a believable seasonal angle, and "they make great gifts" is a nice touch.

## What's weak

- The dark red text on the black "30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE" block is low contrast. The body copy and the "Find a Store" button are nearly invisible.
- The top banner, "Become a member for exclusive Black Friday access + 10% off your first purchase," is also red on black and tiny. It's a juicy perk buried in a strip.
- The grid is half men's and kids' looks, so only about a third of this is for me.
- "Just dropped" in the subject doesn't match a plain sale. Nothing here feels like a trend I'd see on TikTok first.
- The fine print is a massive wall of legal text. "Up to 30%" on "select styles" with a long exclusions list doesn't feel honest.
- There's no free shipping mention, which H&M had on 11/08.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one trend-named knit story. Think "cherry red cardigan szn" or "oversized knit season," styled on a woman, instead of a generic sale hero.
- 2. Fix the contrast in the black block and the top banner. White or bright text on black is fine. Dark red isn't.
- 3. Make the member perk a real button, like "Join free, get 10% off," and put it up high.
- 4. Cut the in-store block or make it a small footer link. It competes with the online offer.
- 5. Space out the sends. Four "spend more, save more" emails in a week trains me to ignore them.

# Technical Audit: H&M "Up to 30% off 100s of knits just dropped"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound, with responsive media queries and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`. Several real defects remain: empty JSON-LD schema fields, missing image alt text, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The 12 "broken link" 403s are very likely probe artifacts, not confirmed breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They all go through the ESP click-tracker `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h7b2d5438-…`, covering "Shop now", "Find a store", the logo link "H&M", image links and "Unsubscribe". A uniform 403 on every redirect from a single host points to bot/WAF blocking of the automated probe, not 12 independently broken links. This is **unverified, not confirmed broken**. Destination URLs could not be resolved, so landing-page health is unknown.
- **The unsubscribe link also returned 403** (`…f8b9de86…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`). It is probably blocked for the same reason, but it should be verified manually (see Recommendations).
- **Link text contains invisible characters.** Two "Shop now" links (`…f8b9de33`, `…f8b9de35`) have link text `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which includes zero-width non-joiner and BOM characters. These are likely preheader/spacing hacks. They can affect screen-reader output and link-text matching.
- **Repeated CTA labels.** Four "Find a store" links (`…de56`, `…de58`, `…de62`, `…de63`) and two "Shop now" links (`…de55`/`de57`) have identical text with different IDs. This is not an error, but it is poor for accessibility (see section 3).
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 61 links were probed, and 49 were skipped as footer or social links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image alt text is missing on 11 images.** The QA check lists `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs for these assets:
  - `…/us/8149345_CLOC265P01`
  - `…/wk-46/us173/US173-CT5-1359579001_202604_LB_02`
  - `…/US173-CT5-1356969003_202604_LB_03`
  - `…/US173-CT5-1364908002_202604_LB_05`

  Two more images inside `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…` links also lack alt text.
- **Linked images with no alt produce an unlabeled link.** The link-text probe fell back to the filename (`[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`), so screen readers announce nothing meaningful for these links.
- **Likely desktop/mobile duplication.** The 1400px/750px image pairs match the `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` pattern in the CSS. The missing alt text is therefore doubled, though a screen reader should only encounter the visible variant.
- **External `@import` for fonts.** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/…)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css)` are ignored or blocked by Gmail and Outlook. This is only an issue if no fallback font stack is set, and the truncated source doesn't show one.
- **Truncated source:** no further rendering analysis is possible, including the MSO conditionals and the footer.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient values exist only inside tracker URLs (`rid=3326930539`, `p2=079e07e2…`), which is expected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are truly absent, one-click unsubscribe fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, and this is a relay limitation, not a sender defect. The sender `newsletter@email.hm.com` is on a subdomain matching the tracker host `t19.email.hm.com`, which is consistent with aligned sending.
- **An in-body unsubscribe link is present** (`p5=9be06746…`). The 403 is likely probe-related, as in section 2. The physical postal address could not be confirmed because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Every link is an opaque redirect (`/r/?id=…&p1=DM6210066&p3=20261110`), so no `utm_*` values are visible in the source. They may be appended server-side after the redirect.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be checked** because the redirects returned 403 to the probe.
- **The JSON-LD block contains empty or placeholder values** (real defects in the source):
  - `"subjectLine":""`
  - `DiscountOffer` with `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, and `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` (a stale 2018 default)
  - `PromotionCard` with `"image":""` and `"url":""`

  Gmail may reject or ignore the annotation, and the stale date could produce a wrong or expired offer badge.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the tracker links** with a realistic browser User-Agent and a retry, or whitelist the QA probe. Treat the 403s as unconfirmed until a manual click on `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…de86` (unsubscribe) and the primary "Shop now" link succeeds.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to all 11 images. Use `alt=""` only on purely decorative images that sit inside a link with text.
3. **Fix the JSON-LD.** Populate `subjectLine`, `url`, `image`, `discountCode` and `availabilityEnds`, and correct `availabilityStarts` to the real offer window, or remove the empty `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` objects.
4. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** in the raw headers at the sending MTA, not through the relay.
5. **Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment** from the original message headers.
6. **Make repeated CTAs distinguishable.** For example, use `aria-label="Find a store near you"`, and remove the zero-width characters from link text.
7. **Add a plain-text part with readable copy** rather than mostly URLs (522 of 600 chars). Shorten or drop the tracker URLs in it.
8. **Confirm UTM or campaign parameters** survive the tracker redirect to the landing page.
