# Kids | The Cozy Fleece and Faux Shearling Edit
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- Kids again. This is the fifth Zara send in about three weeks that isn't for me. The 10/30 "Woman | This week at Zara" email had me thinking they'd finally remembered who I am. Then came menswear on 10/31, and now a fleece edit for children. The kids in it are adorable. The Hello Kitty fleece and the red heart sherpa are cute, and I'd screenshot them if I were shopping for a niece. I'm not.
- I scrolled it in about four seconds. The hero is a big photo with an oversized ZARA logo, and then a wall of kid portraits. I can't see a price, a deal, or a ship date anywhere. The only words I can read are "The Cozy Fleece and Faux Shearling Edit" and a tiny "VIEW." H&M's holiday pajamas email also had no offer, but at least that category was mine. This one is a mark-as-read, and I wouldn't forward it.
- Zara is supposed to be the one that ships the trend first. Teddy and sherpa outerwear is already everywhere on my TikTok FYP, and this email says nothing about it. There's no "trending now," no "back in stock," and nothing that makes me feel Zara got there first.

## What's working

- The hero photo is bright and cheerful, with a big blue sky and kids in saturated reds and greens. It stands out from the moody black-and-white Zara sends I've been getting.
- The model casting is diverse across skin tones, hair textures and ages. That's a real plus.
- The 3x3 grid is easy to scan. Every jacket is shown clearly, and the Hello Kitty and Snoopy-style prints are fun.
- The theme is clear. It's all fleece and faux shearling, so nobody has to guess what the email is about.

## What's weak

- It's a kids category, so it's irrelevant to me. It's the fourth kids send in about three weeks, after Caramel London on 10/27 and the earlier ones.
- There's no price, no offer and no shipping info. Nothing tells me why to click today.
- The CTAs are tiny and weak. "VIEW" sits in small type beside a huge headline. "DISCOVER THE COLLECTION" is small black text floating in an empty white grid cell. Nothing looks like a tap target on my phone.
- There's no product naming and no price per piece. I can't tell which jacket is which or what it costs.
- The home kids bedding strip at the bottom is a random add-on, and "DISCOVER HOME KIDS COLLECTION" is hard to read.
- The footer says "DOWNLOAD OUR APP" with no button or incentive, and the social links are plain text. TikTok is listed, but there's no trend content to back it up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send so it goes to people who actually buy kids' clothes. I'd keep sending me Woman content. The mismatch is the single biggest drag on this email.
- 2. Add real CTAs. Use a visible button that says "Shop kids fleece" and put a price on each jacket.
- 3. Add a reason to click now, such as "New in," "Selling fast," or a free-shipping threshold. ASOS at least tells me when a drop is exclusive, and Zara just says "edit."
- 4. Link each tile to its own product page and add a short name and price under each one.
- 5. Make the app promo a real button with a reason to download it, such as early access to drops.
