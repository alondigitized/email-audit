# Last day: Up to 40% off for Members
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, H&M, the "last day" email arrived and I'm not even mad, I'm just tired. This is the tenth member-sale send in about two weeks. After "Up to 40% off premium knits" on 11/15 and "Up to 40% off gifts for her" on 11/14, I could recite the "up to 40% off for Members" headline from memory. The only thing new is the subject line saying it's the last day. The email body itself doesn't say that anywhere I can see.
- The hero is a girl in a burgundy oversized set, sitting on a bench, very soft-life loungewear. It's cute, but it isn't a trend. Nothing here says "TikTok made me buy this." It's cozy basics and gift filler. Compared to ASOS and that moody Lioness drop, which felt like a moment, this feels like clearance with a nice font.
- Would I click? Maybe for the burgundy sweatpants at $17.99, but I wouldn't hunt for them. I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read, and if I'm bored on Saturday during my three-tab compare I might peek.

## What's working

- The burgundy loungewear hero is a cohesive, on-palette image, and the oversized-set look matches the cozy-girl vibe I'm into right now.
- Member pricing is shown right under each product in red, so I can see the savings quickly, like $22.99 down to $17.99.
- "Up to 40% off" is big and bold, so the offer is obvious right away.
- The product grid has a mix of men's, women's and kids' models, so the lineup doesn't feel one-note.
- Sweatpants at under $20 is a real price-per-item win versus Zara.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the body says "last day." The urgency lives only in the subject line. The fine print has the 11/16 deadline, but nobody reads that.
- The black banner at the top, "Become a member…extra 10% off," is a bit loud and confusing. If I'm already a member, why is it pitching me?
- Five stacked button rows (Women, Men, Kids, Sign In, Sign Up) look like a menu, not a CTA. There's no single "shop now" energy.
- "Gifts to unwrap" with a scarf, beanie and bear slippers is giving generic holiday filler. Nothing in it is trend-first.
- Repetition is real. 11/14 was gifts for her and 11/15 was knits, and today is loungewear and gifts again, with the same 40% hook. Zara Kids at least changed the story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tonight at midnight" with a countdown. That would be a big lift for an email the subject calls the last day.
- 2. Cut the CTA stack to one big button, "Shop member deals," and drop the sign-in and sign-up rows for people who are already members.
- 3. Lead with one hero product, like the burgundy set with its member price, instead of a vague mood shot.
- 4. Cap cadence. Ten sends in two weeks trains me to ignore you.
- 5. Add a trend tag, like "cold-girl cozy" or "what's trending now," so it doesn't read like a clearance rack.
