# Trending now: Party
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the first Zara email in a week that is actually dressed for me. The 11/07 "weekly edit" was all menswear and the 11/08 capsule was kids and babies. This one is women: black jumpsuits, a satin halter, and a white double-breasted blazer dress. "Party Story" is the section I'd screenshot for the group chat. I want that blazer dress.
- The problem is the same one as the 11/12 SRPLS lookbook. There's no price, no offer and no deadline. Every button is a tiny "VIEW". "Trending now" in the subject promises me what's hot right now, but the body is an AW26 editorial with a villa shoot, leather bags, an athleisure line and a running ad. It's gorgeous, but it's a magazine and not a shop.
- Would I click? Probably once, on the blazer dress or the satin pyjama trousers, and only so I could check the price on my phone in another tab. Would I forward it? Only the party section, and only to one friend. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- "Party Story" is the clear hero. The blazer dress is big, with the hair in motion and good light, and it's the one thing my eye lands on.
- The models look like women my age and the styling is going-out: halter jumpsuit, backless green dress, black trousers. It matches what I'm planning for the holiday party season.
- The "The Item | The Satin Pyjama Trousers" block gives me one named product, in a green with side stripes that reads as the trend piece. That's how a retailer tells me "this is the thing".
- The three small category tiles (Jumpsuits / Trousers / Dresses) give me a quick way into what I'd actually shop.
- The brand voice is consistent: black-on-white type, moody photos, no confetti. It feels like Zara set the trend and isn't chasing it.

## What's weak

- There's no price anywhere. H&M's "Member-exclusive outerwear deals from $29.99" at least told me what I'd pay before I tapped. Zara makes me guess.
- Every CTA is a tiny "VIEW" in small black text under the headline. On my phone those are hard to hit, and they say nothing about what's behind them.
- The email is too long. Villa Borsani, Projects AW26, Leather Edit and Athleticz Speed Run all compete with the party story I was promised. By the athletic section I'd stopped scrolling.
- The subject says "Party", but only about a third of the email is party. The rest is a general brand brochure.
- Nothing is time-bound. I don't know if the party edit is in stock, or when it ships. That's a miss on delivery honesty for someone who has three tabs open on a Saturday.
- Cadence is heavy. This is the fifth Zara send in about a week, after SRPLS on 11/12, Zara Home on 11/09, the capsule on 11/08 and the weekly edit on 11/07.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the party edit first and cut everything else. Lead with the blazer dress, add the price and a real button ("Shop the blazer dress, $89.90"), and send the villa, leather and Athleticz content as separate emails.
- 2. Replace every "VIEW" with a verb and a noun, in a proper tap-sized button: "Shop party dresses", "Shop satin trousers".
- 3. Add one honest availability line, such as "Ships in 2-3 days, in time for the 20th". That's the delivery-time honesty I check for.
- 4. Add a price band or a "new this week" tag. ASOS's Lioness drop at least told me it was exclusive, and Zara's "trend-first" claim needs something visible to back it up.
- 5. Segment by gender and category so I stop getting menswear and kids emails.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New party dresses and satin trousers, ships in time for the holidays.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The blazer dress is back in stock. Tap to shop.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (WARN):** 16 images lack `alt`: `img_01.jpg` through `img_13.jpg`, `img_14A.jpg`, `img_14B.jpg` and `img_15.jpg`. All are under `news.zara.com/assets/responsysimages/industria007/contentlibrary/campaigns/2026/11.noviembre/...`. With images blocked, the email renders as blank cells. The `.imagemobile` class styles alt text for mobile, but it has nothing to display.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=...` also lacks `alt`. For a 1x1 pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Viewport meta is malformed:** `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;"` uses semicolons, where the spec calls for commas. It also sets `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0`, which block pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Web font in `<head>`:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Roboto+Condensed:400,700&display=swap&subset=cyrillic,cyrillic-ext">` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `subset=cyrillic` parameter is unneeded for a `US_en` send. The MSO fallback stack (`HelveticaNeue, Helvetica, Arial`) covers Outlook.
- **Default link color:** `a { color: #999898 }` is about 2.8:1 on white, below WCAG AA's 4.5:1. This only applies if the links render on a light background, which the truncated source doesn't confirm.
- **Plain-text fallback (FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` part for text-only clients or accessibility tooling. Spam filters also penalize HTML-only mail.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `${ }`, `%%`, `$firstName`) appear in the visible source.
- Responsys macro comments leaked into the delivered HTML: `<!-- Macros para el contenido -->`, `<!-- Recogemos los países del PAISES_SKIP... -->` and `<!-- CAMPAIGN_CODE:zara;2026_11_14_Norte_woman_weekly_14_11;US_en -->`. They are harmless to rendering but expose internal segmentation and campaign naming, and they add bytes. Stripping them in the send pipeline is cleaner.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **No physical address detected (WARN):** CAN-SPAM requires one. The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected. Treat this as unconfirmed until the full HTML or the raw message is checked.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, because the relay doesn't expose it. Check the original message headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Landing page alignment can't be assessed, because no post-redirect URLs were captured.
