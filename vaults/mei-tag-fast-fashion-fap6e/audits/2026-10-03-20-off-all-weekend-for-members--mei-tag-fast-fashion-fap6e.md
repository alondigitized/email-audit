# 20% off all weekend for Members
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, H&M is hitting me with the same "up to 30% off fall essentials" banner for the third day in a row, and now there's a second offer stacked under it. The top says 30% off, the hero says "20% OFF YOUR ORDER" for members, and the fine print says 25% for Plus members. I can't tell which number is mine, and the subject only mentions 20%. The brown belted wrap coat is a nice hero, but it isn't a trend piece. It's the coat Zara would have dropped three weeks ago.
- I don't see a single TikTok-core item in here. No viral cardigan, no "this is the one everyone's styling" moment, just category tiles for Women, Men, Kids, Home and Sport. It's a catalog with a coupon on top. Zara's "Re{form} Zara. Vol. 1" at least had a point of view. This reads like H&M trying to clear the shelves.
- Would I click? Maybe one tap on "Shop Now" because the coat is pretty and I'm already in the app on Saturday. Would I forward it? No. It's nothing I'd send to a friend. I'd mark it read and go check what Shein and Zara are doing.

## What's working

- The hero coat photo is clean, well lit and tall, so my eye lands there first.
- "Member Exclusive: 20% off your order" is a big, bold, readable offer block.
- Two buttons ("Shop Now" and "Join Now") sit right under the offer, so the next step is obvious.
- The Women tile has a cozy knit and the Men tile has a striped sweater, so the fall mood reads consistently.
- The Kids cherry cardigan is cute, and the cherry print is actually on-trend. It's just buried and not for me.

## What's weak

- Three discount numbers fight each other: "up to 30%" at the top, "20%" in the hero, and "25% for Plus members" only in the fine print. That feels like bait-and-switch.
- It's the same "30% off fall essentials" banner as the 10/2 "Up to 30% off cardis, jeans, sweats" send and the 9/30 flash sale. I'm already tired of it.
- No trend story at all. Nothing says what's new, what's viral or what's selling out, so I can't tell if H&M is leading or chasing.
- Kids, Men, Home and Sport tiles take up most of the scroll, and none of it is for me. Beauty is hidden at the bottom.
- The fine print is a huge wall of tiny text, with exclusions like "not valid on sale items" and "excludes designer collabs". That's exactly what I'd want to know before I tap.
- Nothing about delivery, free shipping or return windows, which I check across my three tabs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one number and one trend item. Pick "20% off for Members" and put a single hero product, like a viral cardigan or the cherry knit, next to it.
- 2. Drop the "up to 30%" banner on member sends. Two offers in one email looks like a hidden catch.
- 3. Replace the generic category grid with 3-4 "what's trending now" products, with prices and a "selling fast" cue.
- 4. Add a line about shipping speed and free returns for Members.
- 5. Move the exclusions up to one short sentence under the offer, such as "excludes sale items, ends Sunday."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source and the automated checks surfaced no confirmed code-level defects. All 12 probed tracking links returned 403, but this is probably bot-blocking by the ESP redirector, not real breakage. Confirmed issues are limited to missing image alt text, empty JSON-LD schema fields, and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. Header-based compliance and authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They cover "Shop now", "Join now", the hero/image links, the "H&M" logo link and "Unsubscribe", all on `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h92067ac8-…`. A uniform 403 on every redirect, including the logo and unsubscribe links, points to the redirector rejecting the automated probe (bot or UA filtering). It does not point to dead destinations. The failure is unconfirmed either way, because the final destinations were never resolved.
- **Probe coverage was limited.** 12 links were probed and 56 skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized), so most of the email is unverified.
- **Link text is polluted.** Two "Shop now" links have link text that begins with zero-width characters (`"‌  ﻿Shop now"`). This is likely a preheader-style spacer hack, but it can confuse link-text analytics and screen readers.
- **Click-through paths are not human-readable.** Every link is a wrapped `/r/?id=…&p1=DM6105507&p2=…&p3=20261003` URL. That is normal for ESP click tracking, but the real destinations cannot be audited from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 17 images.** These include the 1400px and 750px variants from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-40/…`, plus a tracking-redirect image (`https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h92067ac8-71d9-4aa1-82ae-046e…,1`). The link text for these images falls back to the filename (`[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`). With images blocked, or for screen readers, the hero and product links have no meaningful label.
- **Each image appears twice, as a 1400px and a 750px variant.** This is consistent with the desktop/mobile pattern (`.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`, `.image-mobile`). The missing alt text applies to both variants.
- **Hiding rules include `mso-hide:all`.** This is correct for Outlook, and no rendering defect is evident in the visible source.
- **Fonts are loaded with `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css)`.** Gmail and several other clients strip `@import`, so the email will fall back to system fonts there. Whether the font-family declarations include a fallback stack could not be checked because the HTML was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible** in the truncated source.
- **The JSON-LD schema block contains empty template values.** It has `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""` and `"url":""` and `"image":""` in `PromotionCard`. `availabilityStarts` is `"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`, a stale default. The block is unpopulated, so Gmail promo annotations will not render or are invalid.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was not found.** The QA tooling itself says these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are therefore unknown and cannot be called failures.
- **An in-body unsubscribe link exists:** `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,f859b52e&…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`. It returned 403 to the probe, so it is unverified.
- **The physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source.** Verify it manually.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be assessed.** Every link goes through the redirect with opaque identifiers (`p1=DM6105507`, `p3=20261003`) and no visible `utm_*` parameters. The 403 responses prevented resolving the final landing URLs.
- **No continuity issue can be confirmed from the available data.**

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the redirect links** with a realistic browser UA, or resolve the destinations manually. This confirms the 403s are bot-blocking before anything is treated as a defect. Also confirm the unsubscribe link resolves.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text to all hero and product images and their wrapping links.** Avoid filename fallbacks, and use `alt=""` only for purely decorative images.
3. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD blocks.** Fill `subjectLine`, `description`, `discountCode` and `availabilityEnds`, replace the 2018 `availabilityStarts` value, and set `url` and `image`, or drop the empty `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries.
4. **Rebalance the plain-text part**, which is 522 of 600 characters URLs, with more readable copy.
5. **Capture raw headers from the original message** (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. **Remove the zero-width characters from the "Shop now" link text** if they are not intentional.
