# Member forecast: 20% off all weekend
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the first H&M email this week that gives me an actual discount without making me do math. After the $100/$125 tier thing and the 10/20 "Don't ghost up to 50% off" send, a flat 20% off is easy to understand. The hero is a mauve mohair sweater, which is a real fall-trend piece and looks good. But the sweater isn't linked to anything I can see, and it just sits there. The "cool front of sweaters" line is cute, though it's a weather pun and not a trend.
- Then it turns into a catalog. Women, Men, Baby and Kids tiles come first, followed by a "Shop By" grid with Home and Beauty. I'm 24 and I'm scrolling past a toddler in a cardigan to find anything for me. That's the same problem as the 10/23 "Cozy holiday decorations" email, where I got a bunch of categories and nothing I'd screenshot for the group chat. The fine print is also huge: Plus members get 25%, there's a long exclusion list, and the offer ends 10/25. Honestly, I'd tap it once for the 20%, maybe browse sweaters, and then forget it. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- "20% OFF YOUR ORDER" is big and bold, and it's the first thing I read. It's way clearer than the tiered-threshold emails from 10/16–10/18.
- The mauve fuzzy sweater hero is on-season and moody, and it feels like fall right now.
- There are two buttons, JOIN NOW and SHOP NOW, so I know what to do whether or not I'm a member.
- The Women tile has a white turtleneck and the Men tile has a quarter-zip, so the layering story matches the "cool front" copy.
- The sale ends 10/25, so the deadline feels real and it's a weekend deal.

## What's weak

- The hero image isn't clearly linked to a specific product, and the buttons are generic, so I don't know which sweater I'm looking at.
- Half the email is Baby, Kids, Home and Beauty, which is irrelevant to me. The Shop By section repeats the same categories again right after the tiles.
- Nothing is trend-framed. There's no "quiet luxury" or "mob wife" or any TikTok-ish language. Zara's STUDIO COLLECTION AW26 was also vibes-only, but at least it felt like the trend was theirs.
- The exclusions are brutal: not valid on sale, guest designer collabs, or special collections. That means the ELIE SAAB drop on 10/29 isn't covered, and I can't use it on the one H&M thing I actually care about.
- The outlined buttons are thin and low-contrast, and the legal text is a wall. The big white gap before "Shop By" also looks like a spacing glitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the category grid with 3–4 specific trending sweaters, each with a price and a "20% off" tag. Link the hero to that exact sweater. ASOS at least gave me one clear drop with its Lioness email.
- 2. Say what's excluded in one line near the button, not buried at the bottom. If the ELIE SAAB drop is out, I'd rather know up front.
- 3. Make the CTA a verb plus a noun, like "Shop 20% off knits", and fill the button so it pops.
- 4. Put the deadline in the hero, not just in the fine print. Shein would have a countdown in there.
- 5. Cut Baby/Kids/Home/Beauty out of a women's-sweater send, or segment so I don't get them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows a few real defects: unpopulated JSON-LD schema fields, images with no alt text, and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. All 12 probed links returned 403. That looks like the ESP click-tracker rejecting the QA probe, so it doesn't show the links are dead for recipients. The headers needed for compliance and authentication checks weren't captured, so those items can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on every probed link (12 of 12), including Unsubscribe.** All point to the same redirector, `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h075981e9-...,5493e993,<linkid>&did=1418979731&rid=3326930539&erid=...`. Every URL returned the same status, whatever its CTA or link ID (`f88e7e4b`, `f88e7e17`, `f88e7e49`, `f88e7e7d`). So this is most likely bot or user-agent filtering on the click-tracking domain, not 12 independent broken links. The 16% pass rate is therefore not a reliable measure of recipient experience. It should be re-probed with a mail-client user agent or checked manually.
- **Unverified destinations.** Because the redirects were blocked, none of the final landing URLs were resolved. 49 of 61 links were skipped by the probe's ranking.
- **Per-recipient identifiers are in the URL.** `rid`/`erid` (base64 of `3326930539`) and `p2` (a 32-character hash) appear in every link. The unsubscribe link also carries `p4=3326930539` and `p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`. This is normal for ESP tracking and is not a defect.
- **Zero-width characters in link text.** Three "Shop now" links (`...f88e7e17`, `...f88e7e19`) have link text like `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`. They contain U+200C and U+FEFF plus spaces, so the anchor text is not clean for screen readers or link-text matching.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images.** This covers 10 product or lifestyle images, for example `.../us164_s4/1359453006_202604_LB_0324.jpg/cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and the `.750.750` variants, plus 2 tracking-wrapped images (`https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1`). With images blocked or read aloud, these links have no accessible name. The QA output shows the filename (`[image: cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg]`) as the link text.
- **Duplicate image pairs.** Each product appears twice, as 1400×1400 and 750×750 renditions. This is consistent with a desktop/mobile swap using the `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` classes. `.is-hidden-desktop` is only set to `display:inline` inside the `@media (max-width:699px)` block, so the pattern works if the base styles hide it. With alt missing on both, a screen reader may read both copies. The truncated source doesn't show enough to confirm.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** `fonts.css` and `oces.css` are loaded by `@import` in `<style>`. Gmail and some other clients ignore this, so those clients fall back to system fonts. This is a minor issue and expected.
- **`<html>` has inline styles but no `lang` attribute** (`<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">`). This hurts screen-reader pronunciation.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`, `${}`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `rid`, `erid` and `p4` values resolved to real numbers. The source is truncated, so the body copy was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a failure. They should be checked against the raw message from a direct-to-Gmail send. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **In-body unsubscribe link present** (`...f88e7e7d...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...`), but its target couldn't be probed (403). A manual check is still needed.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `newsletter@email.hm.com` and the click domain is `t19.email.hm.com`, which are aligned subdomains of `hm.com`. That is a good sign but not proof.
- **Physical postal address.** It is not visible in the truncated source and can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters on the wrapped links.** The ESP campaign IDs (`did=1418979731`, `p1=DM6163649`, `p3=20261024`) are the only attribution inputs. UTMs may be appended at redirect time, but that can't be confirmed because the redirects were blocked.
- **Landing page alignment can't be assessed** for the same reason. The subject promises "20% off all weekend" for members, and there is a "Join now" CTA. Whether the landing pages carry the offer needs a manual click-through.

Gmail ignores or may distrust empty or stale markup. This is a real source defect and not caused by the relay.
2. **Add `alt` text to all 12 flagged images**, using `alt=""` for decorative duplicates and descriptive text for the image-only links.
3. **Clean the link text.** Remove the U+200C/U+FEFF padding from the "Shop now" anchors, or move it outside the `<a>`.
4. **Fix the plain-text part.** 532 of 610 characters are URLs. Add readable text, such as section headings or short link labels, around the tracking URLs.
5. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`.
6. **Re-run link QA** with a browser-like user agent or a seeded inbox, and check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
7. **Confirm UTMs and the landing page** by following one or two real redirects by hand.
