# Up to 40% off sitewide ends tonight
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the fourth 40% off send this week, and the "Last day" label is the only thing that's new. The 10/8 banner, the 10/10 sweaters send, "Your fall aesthetic" on 10/11 and the 10/12 activewear email all carried the same sale. The urgency would work better if I hadn't been seeing "up to 40% off" every day, so "ends tonight" reads like a bluff.
- This one is at least a denim email, and wide-leg and barrel-leg jeans are a real trend for me. The hero girl in the leather bomber and dark wide-leg denim looks good, and the product grid has actual prices. It still doesn't feel like H&M got to the trend first. It feels like a clearance sign with a nice model. Zara's "Kids | Comfort Capsule" didn't speak to me at all, but it at least looked like an editorial moment. ASOS's Lioness drop felt like a story. This is a price tag.
- Would I click? Probably one tap on the barrel-leg jeans, because $39.99 for a trending silhouette is fine. I wouldn't forward it to my group chat, because nothing in it is a "you have to see this" moment.

## What's working

- The hero model in the leather bomber and wide-leg jeans looks current and is my age range, so I get the vibe straight away.
- "Last day" in small caps above the big "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE" headline makes the deadline clear.
- Jeans are the whole theme: baggy, wide-leg, barrel-leg and straight. Copy like "trending wide-leg jeans, relaxed fits" speaks my language.
- Prices show strikethrough and sale side by side, like $26.99 against $44.99, so I can do my price-per-trend math without clicking.
- The "New Arrival" tag on the barrel-leg jeans is the one thing that hints at newness.

## What's weak

- It's the fourth identical 40% off send since 10/8. After 10/12's "Cozy & versatile activewear," the fake-urgency fatigue is real.
- The "Need it today? Order by 4PM ET" banner at the top is a different offer fighting the main one, and it pushes the sale down.
- There's no TikTok or trend language. "Trending wide-leg" is generic, with no "viral" or "as seen on" angle, so it feels like H&M following a trend rather than starting one.
- The fine print at the bottom is a huge wall of tiny text. "Up to 40%", "select special collections" and "not valid on sale items" make the offer feel less honest.
- Two buttons, "Shop Now" and "Find a Store," sit side by side with the same weight. The store one is a distraction for me because I shop on my phone.
- The "Shop By" grid with Men, Kids and Home is filler for someone who came for jeans.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the repeat. Retire the daily "up to 40%" banner and give this send one real hook, like a trend moment with one hero product plus a deadline that's actually new.
- 2. Make one CTA. Drop "Find a Store" and use something specific like "Shop Barrel-Leg Jeans."
- 3. Move the same-day pickup bar below the hero, or cut it.
- 4. Put the top three jeans above the fold with the sale price in the hero image.
- 5. Add social proof, like "Selling fast" or review stars on the jeans, since I'd trust that more than a banner.
- 6. Shorten the legal text and link out to the full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Barrel-leg jeans, $39.99 — last day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The jeans everyone's wearing, 40% off till midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wide-leg, baggy and barrel-leg from $19.99. Sale ends 11:59 PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our denim wall is almost sold out in your size. Last chance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is well-formed and responsive, with an `@media (max-width:699px)` block and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`. QA could not verify the click-tracking links because every probed link returned 403. The automated checks also couldn't see the List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results headers, and many images lack alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403**, including the unsubscribe link. Every one is a `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hdc52d9c8-…` redirect. The same status on every link, including `Unsubscribe`, points to the tracking domain blocking the QA bot rather than 12 independent broken links. Treat these as unverified rather than confirmed broken. The final landing URLs were never resolved.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 54 links were skipped, so most of the email is unverified.
- **Link text contains invisible characters.** Two "Shop now" links (`…f8711683`, `…f8711685`) have the text `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which includes zero-width non-joiner and BOM characters (U+200C, U+FEFF). This can break text-based link matching and screen-reader output.
- **Duplicate destinations.** "Shop now" and "Find a store" each appear twice with distinct tracking IDs (`f871174a/c`, `f871174b/d`). That is normal for per-placement tracking, so no issue.
- **JSON-LD block is empty.** The `application/ld+json` block has `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"description":""`, and `"url":""`. `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, a stale template default. Gmail promotion-annotation parsing gets no usable data, and the stale date could cause the annotation to be ignored.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 14 images.** Product images are `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` at `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-41/us154_s4/…` (SKUs `1222670035`, `1349409004`, `1297530004`, `1357712007`, `1366775001`, `1326630011`). There is also an image at `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1`, which looks like the tracking pixel.
- **Filename fallback in link text.** Image links surface as `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]` because the alt is missing. The links are `…f8711748`, `…f8711749`, and `…f871174e`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will read or show the filename.
- **Duplicate image sizes per product.** Each product has a 1400px and a 750px version, presumably toggled by the `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` classes. Both are missing alt, so the accessibility problem is doubled.
- **Webfonts via `@import`.** `fabric.hmgroup.com` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css` are loaded this way. Gmail and Outlook ignore `@import` fonts, so they fall back to system fonts. Confirm that fallback stacks are declared.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML shown was cut off, so MSO conditionals and table structure beyond the `<style>` block were not audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source. The part of the source I could see is only the head, so I can't speak to the body.
- The tracking URLs carry per-recipient identifiers: `rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` (base64 of the same `rid`), and `p2=0982dd0aa7b117bf086dee6ed4af7d0c`. The unsubscribe link adds `p4=3326930539` and `p5=9be06746…`. These are expected for ESP click tracking, not a defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found.** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for senders over 5,000 messages a day, and H&M is certainly over that.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status are unknown because of the relay. No conclusion can be drawn.
- **In-body unsubscribe link is present** (`…f8711780`, with `p4`/`p5` tokens), but its 403 means the probe could not confirm that it works. The physical postal address is not in the truncated source, so it is unverified.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (522/600 characters). This is a spam-score risk, caused by long tracking URLs in the text alternative.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters** (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) appear on any link. Attribution is carried by the ESP redirect parameters `p1=DM6130173` and `p3=20261013` instead, so this may be intentional.
- **Landing page alignment can't be checked.** The redirects returned 403, so I can't confirm that the "ends tonight" offer matches the destination page. I can't confirm that the "Find a store" or "same-day pickup" links resolve to the right pages either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracking links with a realistic browser User-Agent or a seeded-inbox click-through. This will tell you whether the 403s are bot-blocking or real. Confirm the unsubscribe link resolves.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all product and logo images. Give the tracking pixel `alt=""`.
3. Strip the zero-width characters (U+200C, U+FEFF) from "Shop now" link text.
4. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block. If kept, fill `subjectLine`, `availabilityEnds`, and `discountCode`, and replace the 2018 `availabilityStarts`.
5. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from raw message headers, not through the relay.
6. Shorten the plain-text alternative or reduce its link density.
7. Declare web-safe font fallbacks for clients that drop `@import` fonts.
