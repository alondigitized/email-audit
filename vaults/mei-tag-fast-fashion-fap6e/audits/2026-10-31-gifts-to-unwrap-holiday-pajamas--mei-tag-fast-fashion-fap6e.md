## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based H&M template with Northstar web fonts and a mobile `@media (max-width:699px)` layer. Every probed tracking link returned 403 to the QA probe, and about 30 images are missing alt text. The header-based compliance checks are inconclusive because of the relay, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403**, including the Unsubscribe link, the logo ("H&M"), both "Shop now" CTAs, the hero images, and the Women/Men/Kids nav (each appears twice). They all go through the ESP redirector `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h3ed521b0-…,5495f9da,<linkid>&did=1419114970&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6184724&p2=…&p3=20261031`.
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking, not dead links.** The probe is an automated, non-browser client, and the 403 is uniform across link IDs (`f8a0efcf`, `f8a0efd0`, `f8a0efd2`, `f8a0eff0`–`f7`, `f8a0f06e`). I can't confirm the links work for real recipients or that they don't. Treat the "16% pass rate" as unreliable for that reason.
- **Probe coverage was limited:** 12 links were probed and 123 were skipped, so the product-grid links are unverified.
- **Tracking tokens:** `rid`, `erid` (base64 of `3326930539`), `p2` (a 32-char hash) and `p5` (on the unsubscribe link) are per-recipient identifiers in the URL query string. This is normal for ESP tracking, not a defect.
- **Fragile image-link markup:** one image link has an empty `alt` and its link text is the raw URL (`https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h3ed521b0-2305-4ebc-b692-6d5e…`). This looks like a tracking-pixel or image-wrapper link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 30 images.** This covers the two hero images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`) and the product-grid images, which appear at both `imWidth=344` and `imWidth=322`. Examples:

  - `ca2d884f…jpg`
  - `0f7e0f31…jpg`
  - `d2d07104…jpg`
  - `475b63ad…jpg`
- **Image links without text equivalents:** the hero image links expose only `[image: cq5dam…]` as link text. With images blocked, or for screen readers, the link is unlabeled.
- **Duplicate image sets:** the same images appear at two widths (344 and 322), which suggests desktop and mobile variants toggled by `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`. Screen readers may read both sets if the hidden variant isn't also `aria-hidden`. I couldn't confirm this because the HTML is truncated.
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** `<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">` has no `lang`. I also saw no `<title>` in the visible head.
- **Web-font dependency:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/…)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css)` only work in clients that support `@import`. Gmail ignores them, so confirm the fallback font stack.
- **Mobile layout:** `.email-layout{width:375px!important;min-width:375px!important}` and `.footer-table{width:375px!important}` hard-code the mobile width. Screens narrower than 375px could scroll horizontally.

- I could not verify the postal address in the footer because the HTML is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the tracking links** with a browser-like User-Agent or a headless browser to separate bot-blocking 403s from real failures. Check the unsubscribe link first.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to all product and hero images, and `alt=""` to decorative or duplicate responsive variants.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and a `<title>`.
4. **Clean up the JSON-LD** by removing the empty `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries or populating them, and dropping the 2018 `availabilityStarts` date.
5. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment,** from the original SMTP message rather than the AgentMail relay copy.
6. **Improve the plain-text part** so it has more readable copy relative to URLs.
7. **Confirm the final landing URLs** by following the redirects, and record the UTM or campaign parameters applied.
