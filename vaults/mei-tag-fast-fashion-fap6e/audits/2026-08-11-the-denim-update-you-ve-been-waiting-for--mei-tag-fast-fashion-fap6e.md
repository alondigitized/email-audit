# The denim update you've been waiting for
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "FRESH FITS" and "GUESS WHO'S BACK" — that model doing the little bent-over pose in the baggy tinted denim actually caught my eye for half a second, ngl. But then it's the exact same H&M formula I've been getting all week: WOMEN / MEN / KIDS 2-8Y / KIDS 9-14Y buttons before I even know what the "fresh fits" actually are, then FIVE separate category modules (Guess Who's Back, Right on Trend, Built for Play, Back to Campus, Shop By) stacked into what feels like an entire denim catalog dumped into my inbox at once.
- Subject promised "the denim update you've been waiting for" and I was kind of hoping for like... an actual trend callout, wide-leg vs. baggy vs. barrel, something I could screenshot for the group chat. Instead it's a scroll-forever category directory with zero pricing anywhere. Compare that to the ASOS Lioness drop I looked at — moody cargo culottes, one clear story, felt like a "put this on for a reason" moment. This is just H&M reorganizing their site nav into an email.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. Marking as read and moving on — this is basically the same email H&M sent me for "school staples" two days ago, just with denim instead of backpacks.

## What's working

- The hero model's pose actually made me stop scrolling for a sec — feels more editorial than H&M's usual flat product shot.
- "FREE SHIPPING OVER $50 FOR H&M MEMBERS" at the very top is at least a concrete, honest offer, even if it's buried in tiny gray text.
- Kids denim section shows actual kid models in the fits, so at least that segment isn't just a stock swap.

## What's weak

- No price, no % off, no actual "new drop" language anywhere in the body — just category buttons repeated five times over.
- Zero denim silhouette callouts (wide-leg, barrel, baggy) despite the subject literally promising "the denim update."
- This is the fourth H&M send this week hitting the same WOMEN/MEN/KIDS grid structure — "Up to 20% off school staples" on 8/8 did this exact layout, just swap the hero.
- "GUESS WHO'S BACK" is a genuinely confusing headline with zero payoff — back from where? What style? It just leads to a WOMEN button.
- Way too long to scroll through on my phone before hitting anything resembling social proof or a real reason to buy now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE hero product with an actual price and % off — right now there's nothing to anchor a click to. Zara Home's catalog-style send at least stayed pretty; this one's just long.
- 2. Kill 3 of the 5 category modules or turn them into a single scrollable carousel — nobody's reading five headline blocks in a promo email.
- 3. Actually explain "GUESS WHO'S BACK" — is it a silhouette, a collab, a restock? Say it in the subhead, not just as a vague teaser.
- 4. Move the free shipping/member offer up into an actual visual banner instead of tiny gray legal-adjacent text at the top.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA returned a 16% pass rate: all 12 probed material links (including the unsubscribe link) return HTTP 403, both `List-Unsubscribe` headers are absent, and 26 images ship without `alt` text — this is a broken-experience email as currently deliverable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links return HTTP 403**, including primary nav (Women, Men, Kids 2-8Y, Kids 9-14Y), the hero CTA ("Shop now" ×2), the H&M logo link, the membership banner link, and the unsubscribe link. Example: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h33893320-cc97-4e32-b8ed-9eb9a37837c2,663a8e21,f7bcd124&did=1715113505&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM5932321&p2=0982dd0aa7b117bf086dee6ed4af7d0c&p3=20260811`. All links share the same `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirector host, `did`/`rid`/`erid` tracking params, and `p1`–`p5` campaign params — the 403 is consistent across all of them, pointing to a redirector-side block (bot/UA filtering, expired click-tracking token, or IP/geo restriction on the probe) rather than per-link misconfiguration. Recommend re-verifying from a real mail client/browser before treating as a true broken-link defect, but as measured, no link in this email currently resolves.
- Two link text strings contain embedded invisible characters (ZWNJ `‌` and a BOM/zero-width no-break space `﻿`) before "Shop now" — e.g. `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`. This won't break the click but can corrupt link text exposed to screen readers or copy-paste.
- 82 non-material (footer/social/utility) links were deprioritized and not probed; unsubscribe was force-included per probing policy and also failed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **26 images missing `alt` text**, spanning the tracking pixel (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...`), the logo/header tracking pixel, and all 12 product images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` variants across `NA1536_FNP_*` and `AME108_FNP_*` product images at 1400px and 750px breakpoints). Screen reader users get no context for any product imagery.
- Link text `"Free shipping over $50 for H&M Members Join now for exclusive benefits."` bundles two sentences and a CTA into a single anchor's accessible name — this reads as an oversized, ambiguous link label to assistive tech (a rendering/markup issue, not a copy issue).
- Mobile responsive CSS (`.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`, `image-mobile`) and Outlook/webkit-specific rules (`#MessageViewBody`, `@media screen yahoo`) are present, indicating standard multi-client targeting; no structural rendering fault found in the truncated markup beyond the alt-text gaps above.

- No customer-name or other merge-tag artifacts (e.g., unresolved `{{first_name}}`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link returns HTTP 403** (`...f7bcd442...p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`) — recipients cannot self-service opt out via the in-body link as tested.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — no one-click unsubscribe support detected, which major mailbox providers (Gmail, Yahoo) now require for bulk senders. Note QA flags this may be a relay artifact ("may not be captured by AgentMail relay") rather than a true absence — verify against raw SMTP headers before treating as confirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this pipeline; same relay-capture caveat applies.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test all 12 failing links (especially unsubscribe) from a non-automated client/IP to rule out redirector bot-blocking before filing as a sender-side bug; if confirmed broken in production, this is release-blocking (unsubscribe failure is a compliance risk).
2. Add `alt` attributes to all 26 flagged images, particularly the 12 product images — currently empty for both 1400px and 750px variants.
3. Populate the empty `ld+json` fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url`) or remove the schema block if promo-card rich rendering isn't intended for this send.
4. Strip the embedded ZWNJ/BOM characters from the two "Shop now" link text nodes.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` presence directly against raw SMTP headers (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to rule out a capture artifact versus a true gap.
