### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`viewport` meta uses semicolons as delimiters (should be commas):**

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;" />
```

The spec requires comma-separated values. Most modern parsers are lenient, but this is technically malformed and may fail in stricter environments.

**`user-scalable=no` / `maximum-scale=1.0` blocks zoom** — violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Users with low vision cannot zoom the email content. This is the dominant accessibility defect in the visible source.

**Google Fonts `<link>` in `<head>`:**

```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Roboto+Condensed:400,700&display=swap&subset=cyrillic,cyrillic-ext" rel="stylesheet">
```

External font requests are blocked by Gmail, Outlook, and most corporate email clients. The stylesheet will silently fail in those environments. A system-font fallback stack is needed in the CSS.

**Structural violation — `<div>` wrapping a `<tr>` outside table context:**

```html
<div style="mso-hide:all;">
  <tr>
    <td align="center" height="0" width="0" class="hiddenmobile">
```

A `<tr>` as a direct child of a `<div>` (outside `<table>`) is invalid HTML. Rendering behavior is undefined; some clients will drop the row entirely, others will attempt recovery that breaks layout. The wrapping `<div>` should be inside a `<table>` or the conditional comment structure needs to close/reopen the table correctly.

**`alt` text on images** — unverifiable from truncated source; must be confirmed in full HTML.
