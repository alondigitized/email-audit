# 15% off or 20% off all weekend
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "refresh your denim stack with new baggy, barrel, and loose-fit pairs" is a real, specific hook, and denim silhouette calls (baggy, barrel, loose) are exactly the kind of trend-specific language that makes me feel like they know what's in my TikTok FYP right now. But then it just... collapses into the exact same grid-of-buttons, WOMEN/MEN/KIDS/FOR HER/FOR HIM/FOR THEM, SHOP BY WOMEN/MEN/KIDS/HOME/BEAUTY format I've seen from H&M like four times this week already (8/3's "Last day: Up to 30% off fall layers" ran this same skeleton). The tiered discount math ($80=15%, $100=20%) is genuinely a decent offer, but it's buried under a wall of near-identical category tiles with zero product-level detail — no price, no "shop the barrel jean," nothing.
- This is a mark-as-read for me. It's not offensive, the denim shots are decent, but it's the least differentiated H&M send I've seen lately — way less interesting than 8/6's WARDROBE.NYC collab (8.00/10, actual editorial energy) and honestly not even as fun as 8/4's "Contrast Dressing" with the peplum-and-leather-jacket styling. I wouldn't forward this to a friend — there's nothing here worth screenshotting.

## What's working

- "15% OFF $80 OR 20% OFF $100" is crystal clear tiered math, no squinting required — better spelled out than most of H&M's recent "up to 30%" vague-ceiling offers.
- The denim callout copy ("baggy, barrel, and loose-fit pairs") is trend-literate — this reads like someone who knows what's actually selling right now, not generic filler.
- Models for "FOR HER" and "FOR HIM" both look styled for the actual trend (baggy jeans, relaxed fits), not just generic denim stock photos.
- Kids section has genuine skin-tone/style diversity across the two kid models, which I always clock.

## What's weak

- Zero product-level detail — no price tags, no "shop this exact jean," not even a fabric/fit callout under the hero images. I can't tell what I'm even shopping for beyond "some pants."
- This is the fourth-ish "category grid + SHOP NOW buttons" layout from H&M this week — it's the same bones as 8/3's "Last day: Up to 30% off fall layers," just reskinned with denim. Cadence fatigue is real.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the visible email — "all weekend" in the subject never even shows up on the page itself, so there's no clock pushing me to act now vs. later.
- "SHOP BY" section at the bottom (Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) feels like dead weight — after already seeing three "shop this category" CTAs above it, this is just repetition, not new info.
- No social proof, no "trending now" badge, nothing that says this drop is exclusive or first — compare that to Zara's July 30 "Woman | The New" (5.00/10) which at least tried a collection-naming gimmick, even if it also felt repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual price or "from $X" under at least one hero product — right now there's a discount % but nothing to apply it to mentally. This is the single biggest miss.
- 2. Cut the redundant "SHOP BY" grid at the bottom entirely — it repeats the WOMEN/MEN/KIDS/HOME/BEAUTY split that's already covered by the top nav and the FOR HER/HIM/THEM sections.
- 3. Put the "all weekend" urgency ON the page, not just in the subject — a small banner like "ENDS SUNDAY 11:59PM" near the hero would actually create pressure.
- 4. Swap one generic "SHOP NOW" button for something denim-specific like "SHOP BARREL FITS" — specific noun, not generic verb.
- 5. Add a one-line "as seen on" or trend callout (e.g., "the fit everyone's wearing right now") to lean into the trend-first angle instead of playing it safe.
