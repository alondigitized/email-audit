# Your fall aesthetic is up to 40% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- "Your fall aesthetic" is a good hook, and I'd open it. Once I'm in, it's the same "Fall Event: Up to 40% off sitewide" banner I've seen all week. It was in the 10/8 send and again on 10/10 ("Up to 40% off fall's best sweaters"). The hero is a girl in a chocolate-brown corduroy jacket with wavy hair, and it looks good. But the copy under it is generic, "outfit-making jackets, versatile athleisure, and moody home finds", and that does not feel like a trend.
- This is H&M chasing, not leading. When I do my Saturday three-tab compare, Zara's kids' "Comfort Capsule" send was at least styled like an editorial, and ASOS's Lioness drop had a real point of view. This email is a sale with an aesthetic label on it. I'd skim it, maybe tap into the Women tile, and not forward it. I'd save the 10/7 "Night Out Edit" instead.

## What's working

- The hero model looks about my age, and the brown corduroy is very current. It stops my scroll.
- "Up to 40% off sitewide" in big bold type is impossible to miss. The hierarchy is clean.
- Two buttons, Shop Now and Find a Store, sit right under the headline. They're easy to tap on my phone.
- The zig-zag category tiles (Women, Men, Kids, Sport, Home) are easy to scan, and the Women tile is a real outfit with layers.
- It's a familiar sender and the fall timing is right.

## What's weak

- It's the third or fourth near-identical 40%-off send this week (10/8, 10/10, now 10/11). It has no urgency and no "new" angle.
- The offer deadline (online only through 10/13) and the $5 Member Bonus Reward are buried in the tiny fine print at the bottom. A time limit that real would have made me click.
- Every tile just says "Elevated Layers" or "Subtle Details" with no price, no product name, and no sign of what's actually on sale. Fall's best sweaters got more specific than this.
- The tiles have lots of dead white space, and the labels are small. The whole thing feels like a template.
- Nothing says what's trending or new. Shein would have shown the viral piece and Zara would have shown the new-in drop.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends 10/13") and link the hero to one specific product or collection. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Swap the vague tile labels for real picks with prices ("Cord jacket $49.99 → now $34") so I can see the price per trend.
- 3. Show the member perk, the $5 reward, up top instead of in the legal text.
- 4. Vary the creative from the 10/10 send. Don't recycle the same banner.
- 5. Lead with a trend name ("Brown-on-brown", "Barn jackets") like Zara or ASOS would.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brown-on-brown is fall's it-color (up to 40% off)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Monday: 40% off the cord jacket everyone's wearing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Corduroy, cargo, and cozy layers. Sale ends 10/13.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members get an extra $5 off. Online only, 2 days left.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on essentially every image.** Examples:
