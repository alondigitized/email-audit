# @Anitta on the world stage in custom H&M Studio
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- OK so H&M drops "@Anitta on the world stage" in my inbox and I'm swiping past day-four-of-H&M-daily-emails — and this one actually stops me. The @ handle framing feels native, not corporate. Anitta is very much TikTok-coded for me, I know the "Envolver" choreo by heart, and the idea that she's doing some massive international moment in a *custom H&M Studio look*? That's a real flex, not a manufactured one.
- The email opens into a full editorial hero — white cutout co-ord, immaculate staging, clean "TAILORED PRECISION / ANITTA TAKES CENTER FIELD" headline — and the quote they pulled from her is actually personal ("representing my country, while the whole world watches"). It doesn't read like PR copy, it reads like something she actually said.
- But then it all lands on "LEARN MORE." I can feel that energy drain out of me in real time. I can't buy the custom piece — it's one of one. The shop section at the bottom is the same generic WOMEN / MEN / KIDS grid I see in literally every H&M email. I'd screenshot the look, type "white cutout co-ord" into ASOS, and have three options in my cart before this email finishes loading. I'm not clicking "LEARN MORE." Mark as read.
- Also worth noting: looking at my inbox, H&M sent the "In focus: Summer partywear" editorial on the same day — June 15. **Two emails in one day** after already sending June 12, 13, and 14. The Anitta email deserved to breathe. It's sandwiched between Summer partywear and a week of discount countdowns and the specialness leaks out.

## What's working

- Anitta as the hook is genuine Gen Z catnip — she's globally recognized, TikTok-native, and the "world stage" moment feels earned not manufactured
- The white cutout styling is exactly on-trend — aspirational but replicable, which makes me want to find the dupe immediately
- The Anitta quote is emotionally specific and personal, not boilerplate celebrity copy
- "TAILORED PRECISION" tagline is a clean brand-positioning line that actually lands
- The email renders without any visible bugs — clean layout, images load, hierarchy is clear

## What's weak

- "LEARN MORE" is the weakest CTA possible for a hero this strong — I want to shop the vibe, not "learn" about it
- No shoppable row featuring similar H&M Studio pieces below the editorial — the conversion path is completely missing
- Two emails on June 15 plus four consecutive daily sends erodes the sense of occasion this deserved
- The SHOP BY footer (WOMEN / MEN / KIDS) is a complete vibe crash after Anitta — feels pasted from a different template
- No urgency or offer anywhere — purely editorial with zero path to purchase if you're not already motivated

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace the CTA** — "LEARN MORE" needs to be "Shop H&M Studio" or "Get the look" with a direct link to the closest available pieces in the collection
- 2. **Add a "Inspired by Anitta" product row** — three or four shoppable pieces in white, cutout, or tailored fits that capture the look even if you can't buy the custom piece itself
- 3. **Rebrand the SHOP BY section** to something that feels connected — "Her edit" or "Studio picks" so it doesn't feel like a generic footer swap
- 4. **Save this email for a standalone send** — the Zara "Summer Edit" email I saw recently suffered from the same problem (stunning visuals, no purchase bridge), but at least Zara doesn't send two emails in one day
- 5. **Cross-link the social moment** — a "See the full performance" or TikTok-native clip link would give this editorial staying power beyond the inbox

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**External font imports (blocking concern):**
```css
@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/0/northstar/css/fonts.css);
@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css);
```
`@import` inside `<style>` tags is blocked by Outlook (all versions) and some corporate proxies. Fallback system fonts must be present on all `font-family` declarations — cannot confirm without full source.

**Duplicate CSS rules in `#optimize-css`:**
- `.department-title` defined twice at different font sizes (`24px/30px` vs `16px/20px`)
- `.cta-link-mobile-border-ns` declared twice identically
- `.cta-link-mobile, .set_font_interactive_standalone_link_default` declared twice identically
- `.set_font_heading_xlarge` declared three times

These duplicates are parse-harmless but bloat payload and create maintenance risk for future edits.

**`<script>` tag in `<head>`:** The JSON-LD `<script type="application/ld+json">` block is correctly typed and inert in email clients — no rendering risk.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

Multiple JSON-LD fields are unpopulated, indicating template variables were never substituted:

| Field | Value | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| `EmailMessage.subjectLine` | `""` | Actual subject line |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` | Current campaign start date |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` | Campaign end date |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` | Promo code or `null` |
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` | Offer description |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` | Hero image URL |
| `PromotionCard.url` | `""` | Destination URL |

The `2018-12-31` timestamp is the most critical: it is clearly a stale default from template creation and will produce incorrect structured data signals in Gmail's promotions tab.

**Schema context uses HTTP, not HTTPS:**
All four JSON-LD objects use `"@context":"http://schema.org/"` — should be `https://schema.org/`.
