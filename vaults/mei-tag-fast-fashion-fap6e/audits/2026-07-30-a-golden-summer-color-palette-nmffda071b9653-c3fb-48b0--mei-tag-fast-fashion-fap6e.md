# A golden summer color palette
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is DAY EIGHT in my inbox from H&M, and the "Golden Summer" mood board they're running is actually the first one this stretch I don't hate on sight. No price cut anywhere, no urgency, no "%off" banner — just a straight-up lookbook of earthy-tone separates and one genuinely pretty smocked mustard dress. It's giving Zara's overproduced catalog-dump energy a little, but at least it's ONE cohesive palette instead of Zara's seven-category scroll-fest I roasted last time.
- The hero shot — model in the crochet cami — is moody and pretty, but there's zero offer here, so my thumb has no reason to move. This is a lookbook, not a sell. I'd scroll past it on my Saturday three-tab compare because there's nothing time-bound pulling me in, and after "office wardrobe," "bedding," "jeans," and "back to school" all in one week, I'm cadence-fatigued regardless of how nice the styling is.
- Would I click? Maybe, only for the smocked dress. Forward to a friend? No — nothing here is "OMG look at this" enough. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The mustard smocked dress is the one piece I'd actually screenshot and send to my group chat — good color story, not overstyled.
- Model diversity in the hero and crochet-dress shots is real, not tokenized — that's more than I can say for some of Zara's identical-model grids.
- The "golden light" copy actually ties to a visible color palette (mustard, cream, tan) instead of being generic seasonal filler — better follow-through than H&M's own "pre-fall office wardrobe" send that didn't even look fall.
- Clean single-column layout, no overlapping text or broken images — reads fine scrolling on my phone without zooming.

## What's weak

- Zero offer, zero %off, zero urgency anywhere in this email — after ASOS ran an actual adidas collab drop with a boxer as the face, this feels like a plain product dump by comparison.
- "NOTES ON SCANDI STYLE" mid-email is a jarring pivot — I'm two seconds into a golden-summer mood and suddenly it's beige minimalism, no transition, feels like two separate campaigns stapled together.
- This is the EIGHTH straight day of H&M in my inbox — I am fatigued, and a no-offer lookbook is the weakest possible way to spend send #8.
- No loyalty/member pricing shown even though I know H&M has a membership program — feels like they're treating me as a stranger, not a repeat subscriber.
- Prices are scattered ($99, $89.99, $19.99, $34.99, $49.99, $59.99) with no through-line — doesn't read as a cohesive "shop the palette for X" moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an actual offer or member perk — even 15% off the Golden Summer edit would give me a reason to tap instead of just admire.
- 2. Cut the Scandi section entirely or spin it into its own send — two moodboards in one email dilutes both and confuses the scroll.
- 3. Lead with the mustard dress, not the crochet cami — it's the strongest, most shareable piece and it's buried mid-scroll.
- 4. Add a "member price" callout since H&M has a loyalty program — right now known customers get treated like cold traffic.
- 5. Space this out — after 8 days straight, even a great lookbook reads as noise. Skip a day before the next send.
