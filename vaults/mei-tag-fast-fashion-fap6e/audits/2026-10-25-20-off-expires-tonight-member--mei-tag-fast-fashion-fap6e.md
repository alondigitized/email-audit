**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "LAST DAY" at the top got me. It's the same 20% member offer from yesterday's "Member forecast: 20% off all weekend," just with a countdown on it. I get the urgency, but I'd already seen this one. The hero is a model in a black strapless corset-style top. It's pretty, but it isn't a trend moment, and it's not what I'd screenshot to the group chat.

- "LAST DAY / 20% OFF FOR MEMBERS" is big and bold at the top, so the offer and deadline hit instantly.
- Prices are visible under every product ($18.99 lace bodysuit, $22.99 mock turtleneck), which suits my price-per-trend check.
- The "Ready to Celebrate" block has the purple top, lace bodysuit and mesh pieces, which actually fit my going-out wardrobe.
- The "New Arrival" tags help a bit, since I care who ships first.
- The top nav has a SHOP NOW link, so I don't need to scroll to find a path to the site.

## What's weak

- The hero CTAs are SIGN IN and SIGN UP, not "Shop now." That's friction for someone already on the list.
- This is the third 20%-off-ish send in a few days (the 10/24 member forecast, the 10/20 "Don't ghost up to 50% off" and now this), so the "last day" claim feels less credible.
- The grid is scattered: men's suiting, kids' dresses, flats, a cutting board and salt cellar all share one email. Nothing says what's trending.
- The fine print is a huge block with exclusions and a 25% off Plus tier, so the "20% for members" feels a little buried.
- The hero model doesn't tie to any product below, so I can't tell what the hero actually sells.

- 1. Replace SIGN IN / SIGN UP with a single "Shop 20% off" button for people already logged in, and add "Ends tonight midnight" next to it.
- 2. Pick one lane. Make it a "party tops under $25" edit and drop the home decor and kids.
- 3. Link the hero to the exact corset top, with its price.
- 4. Add one social-proof line, like "most-saved this week", so it reads as a trend and not a clearance.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Every probed link goes through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirector, and all 12 probes returned 403. That is most likely bot filtering on the redirector, so link health is unverified rather than confirmed broken. The most solid findings are widespread missing image alt text and malformed JSON-LD markup. The QA tool couldn't see the auth and unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** That includes "Shop now" (6), the "H&M" logo link, two image links, "Sign in", "Sign up" and "Unsubscribe". Example: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h1ccdac36-a265-4731-a3eb-0c324aad47ca,5493e71a,f88fffa2&did=1418979098&rid=3326930539&...`.
- **Likely probe artifact:** the 403s are uniform across every link type. That points to WAF or bot filtering of the automated client, not 12 independently broken destinations. Real delivery can't be confirmed from this data, and the final destinations are unknown.
- **Zero-width characters in link text:** two "Shop now" links (`...f88fffa3` and `...f88fffa5`) have link text of `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`. That is a ZWNJ and BOM plus spaces. It's probably deliberate preheader or spacing padding, but it pollutes accessible link names.
- **Duplicate link text:** several links read just "Shop now" with no distinguishing context.
- **Coverage:** only 12 of 173 links were probed (161 skipped, footer and social deprioritized), so the other 161 are unchecked.
- **Per-recipient tracking parameters:** `rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` (base64 of the same rid) and `p2=fb32f588...` are all present in every URL. That is standard ESP tracking, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 37 warnings.** Most are `image.hm.com/assets/hm/...jpg?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322` product images. Each product appears twice, once per width, which suggests duplicated desktop and mobile blocks. The hero images `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` also lack alt text.
- **Linked images without alt:** these links' accessible name falls back to the filename, as in `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`. Two tracking-redirect images (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1`) also lack alt.
- **Duplicated blocks:** the `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` classes in the visible CSS use `display:none!important`. The source suggests duplicate desktop and mobile markup, which would inflate the DOM and cause duplicate reads in some screen readers. The truncated HTML doesn't let me confirm this fully.
- **`@import` web fonts:** `fabric.hmgroup.com/.../fonts.css` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css` are loaded via `@import` in `<style>`. Many clients ignore these, so the email falls back to system fonts. This is only an issue if fallback stacks aren't defined. I couldn't verify that from the truncated source.
- The `<meta name="viewport">` is present, and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is set. No issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The subject "20% off expires tonight, Member" renders without unresolved tokens. "Member" is a literal fallback, not the recipient's name.
- **JSON-LD is mostly empty in the source.** `EmailMessage.subjectLine: ""`, and `DiscountOffer` has `description: ""`, `discountCode: ""`, `availabilityEnds: ""`, and a stale `availabilityStarts: "2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`. `PromotionCard` has `image: ""` and `url: ""`. ## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay limitation. Nothing can be concluded.
- **In-body unsubscribe link present** (`...f89000e9...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...`), with a recipient-specific token. It returned 403 to the probe, so its function is unverified. The QA tool's guidance says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and this one is the only unsubscribe path visible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** Every link is wrapped in the redirector, and the destination URLs aren't exposed in the source or the 403 responses.
- **Landing page alignment can't be assessed** for the same reason. The probes never reached a destination page, so offer and landing consistency (the "20% off" claim) is unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 522 of 600 characters are URLs. That is a `[WARN]` for URL-heavy text. It's driven by the long tracking URLs, and a plain-text version of this length is mostly unreadable.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` text to all 37 flagged images.** Use `alt=""` for purely decorative ones. Give linked images a descriptive alt, such as product name or "Shop women's sale", instead of falling back to filenames.
2. **Re-probe the links with a real-browser or mail-client user agent,** or from an allowlisted IP, before treating any 403 as broken. Verify the unsubscribe link works end to end.
3. **Inspect raw headers in the original mailbox** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't rely on the relay's copy.
4. **Remove or populate the JSON-LD block.** Empty strings and the 2018 `availabilityStarts` make the markup invalid.
5. **Strip zero-width characters from visible link text,** or move them to a non-link element. Give each CTA a unique accessible name, using `aria-label` or fuller text.
6. **Trim the plain-text part,** or pass destination URLs through shorter redirect links.
7. **Resolve the redirector to final URLs in a follow-up** to check UTM parameters and landing-page continuity.
