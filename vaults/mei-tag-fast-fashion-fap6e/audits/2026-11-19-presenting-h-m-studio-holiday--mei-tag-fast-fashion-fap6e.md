# Presenting H&M Studio Holiday
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the first H&M email in a minute that doesn't scream a percentage at me. After two weeks of "Up to 40% off," "Last day," and "Flash sale: 25% off starts now," a quiet, editorial Studio drop feels like a palate cleanser. That cream shearling coat in the hero is giving old-money winter, which is exactly the vibe my TikTok feed has been on.
- But it's very "look, don't touch." There's no offer, no urgency, and the only CTA is a small "Shop Now" outline button at the top. The long scroll of beige and black is pretty, but it all blurs together after the second model shot. I'd click the shearling coat to see if the $749 is real, then bounce at that price. I'd screenshot it for my Saturday three-tab compare, but I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The shearling hero is a real trend moment. Fur-trim and shearling is exactly what's circulating on my FYP.
- The photography is editorial and moody. It feels more "brand with a point of view" than the usual H&M sale grid.
- Prices are listed right under each product with "New Arrival" tags, so I can tell it's fresh without hunting.
- There's no sale-spam energy here. After the last twelve sale emails, that's a relief.
- The Shop By row (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) at the bottom gives me a quick way out if nothing grabbed me.

## What's weak

- The CTA is tiny and sits way up top. By the time I'm 3,000 pixels down, there's no button, just product tiles I have to guess are tappable.
- The palette is cream, black and beige the whole way through, with no pop. The middle of the email blurs together on my phone.
- Two model shots carry the identical "Embellished Open-Back Body $69.95" caption. It looks like a copy-paste slip, and I can't tell which product the second image is.
- Pricey pieces like the $749 coat and $379 wool coat have no member perk or loyalty hook, even though I've been getting "Members" emails all month.
- It's very long, and the footer fine print is mini-sized and hard to read without zooming.
- The model casting is narrow. It's all the same editorial look, with no range in body type or style.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear hook under the hero, like member early access, free shipping, or a "limited run" note. Right now there's zero reason to click today versus tomorrow.
- 2. Repeat a full-width "Shop the Studio edit" button mid-scroll and at the end.
- 3. Fix the duplicate caption and label each hero with its own product name and price.
- 4. Break up the beige with one colorful or party-ready look, since it's holiday.
- 5. Add a "ships by" date for holiday delivery. Shein and ASOS both do this better.

- **Subject Alt B:** `H&M Studio Holiday: new arrivals, just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Faux-fur, satin and sculpted knits, plus member early access.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shearling coats from $379. Order by 12/15 for holiday delivery.`

And H&M Fashion News's "Welcome, Fashion Lover" was just bare-minimum corporate. This one sits above that and below ASOS on mood.

# Technical Audit: "Presenting H&M Studio Holiday"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is well structured, with a responsive `optimize-css` block and a preheader/JSON-LD scaffold. However, the JSON-LD markup is empty and about 35 images lack alt text. The probe's 403 on all 12 tracked links, including Unsubscribe, is almost certainly bot-blocking by the ESP redirector rather than dead links. It still means the destinations couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** Every one is a `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h4164778f-f920-4816-8ec8-96d3a38309e0,5515f998,<hex>&did=1427503512&rid=3326930539&...` redirect. The CTAs ("Shop now" ×4), the logo link ("H&M"), image links and Unsubscribe all failed the same way.
  - A uniform 403 across one tracking host, with an identical `id` prefix, points to bot filtering on the click-tracker. It does not point to broken destinations.
  - **Not confirmed:** whether any link is actually dead. The final landing pages were never reached.
- **No direct-to-site fallback.** All 155 links (12 probed, 143 skipped) are wrapped in the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirect. If that redirector rate-limits or blocks automated clients, QA tooling can't validate any destination.
- **Duplicate CTA text with distinct link IDs.** Two "Shop now" links carry zero-width characters in their text (`‌ ﻿Shop now`, with ZWNJ/BOM). They resolve to different link IDs (`f8cfa1df`, `f8cfa1e1`). Per-link tracking is fine, but the invisible characters can break text-based click-attribution and text matching.
- **Image links with no useful text.** Seven links have anchor text like `[image: cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg]`, which are filenames, not descriptions.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 35 flagged images).**
  - **Hero and campaign images:** `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm-images/global-campaigns/season-04/campaigns/women/na90265b01/NA90265B01-3x2-1.jpg`, `...-2x3.jpg`, `...-2x3-3.jpg` and `...-2x3-5.jpg`.
  - **Product grid images:** `image.hm.com/assets/hm/ee/e4/eee4d17d….jpg?imWidth=344` and the matching `?imWidth=322` variants. Each product image appears twice (344 and 322 widths), consistent with a desktop/mobile duplicate pair.
  - **Tracking pixel:** the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` image also lacks alt text.
  - **Effect:** with images blocked, the email is blank except for the CTAs. The linked images are unlabeled for screen readers.
- **Duplicated image markup.** The 344px and 322px copies of each product image suggest hidden/shown duplicate blocks (`.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`, `.show-webkit-table` / `.hide-webkit`). That doubles the image and link count in the DOM, and screen readers may announce hidden duplicates.
- **`@import` web fonts.** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css)` are ignored by Gmail and several Outlook clients. This is only a concern if fallback font stacks aren't declared; the truncated source doesn't show them.
- **Not assessable:** the source is truncated, so layout tables, `role="presentation"` and `lang` attributes couldn't be verified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the supplied source.
- **Empty JSON-LD (Gmail promotions markup).** The block ships with blank values:

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (`WARN`).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (`WARN`).
- **Authentication-Results not found**, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown (`WARN`).
- **Caveat on all three:** QA notes the relay may strip these headers. They are unconfirmed, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against raw headers from a direct-to-inbox test.
- **In-body unsubscribe link exists** (`...f8cfa286&...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`), but the probe got a 403. Its functionality is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Tracking uses opaque redirect params (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1=DM6232508`, `p2`, `p3=20261119`) instead. Any UTMs would be appended server-side after the redirect, which this audit could not observe.
- **Landing-page alignment could not be assessed.** Every redirect returned 403, so no destination URL or page was reached.
- **Identifiers in URLs.** `rid`/`erid` (base64 of `3326930539`) and `p4=3326930539` in the unsubscribe URL are per-recipient identifiers. This is normal for ESP tracking but means the links are not safe to forward.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the 403s.** Re-probe with a browser-like User-Agent or from a seeded inbox with real clicks. If the redirector blocks only bots, no action is needed.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to all ~35 flagged images, including the hero campaign images, product tiles and the logo link. Use `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` for the tracking pixel.
3. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD block.** Fill `subjectLine`, `description`, `url`, `image` and the offer dates, or drop it. Remove the `2018-12-31` placeholder.
4. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** and DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment from raw headers of a direct send.
5. **Rebuild the plain-text part** with human-readable copy and fewer URLs, using short links or fewer links.
6. **Strip the zero-width characters** (ZWNJ/BOM) from the "Shop now" anchor text. Use the visible-text-only pattern for preheader padding.
7. **Re-run the audit** with the full, untruncated HTML to check the physical address, table roles, font fallbacks and any merge tokens.
