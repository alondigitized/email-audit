# New semester, new essentials
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "New Semester Loading..." — no percentage off, no flash sale, just a straight-up back-to-school lifestyle drop and honestly? I'm kind of into it after three days straight of H&M hitting me with 15-20% off, then 25% flash sale, then denim, then prep. This is a full switch-up in tone — dorm shop, campus life, occasion edit, even a live acoustic performance from LA on the 18th. That's actual content, not just another discount banner.
- That said, it's a LOT of scrolling for basically zero payoff. Six different category blocks (Campus Life, Got Plans?, Just Like Home, Live From LA, Shop By) and not one single product I can tap into and buy right now — it's all just category links to "WOMEN" or "THE DORM SHOP." Compare that to Zara's "Woman | The New" which I dinged for burying the offer but at least felt like one cohesive moody shoot — this is H&M throwing five different mini-campaigns into one email and hoping something sticks.
- Would I click? Maybe the "LIVE FROM LA" thing out of curiosity since I'm a sucker for a livestream event, but I'm not buying anything off this email. Wouldn't forward it — it's not trend-specific enough to text a friend about.

## What's working

- "New Semester Loading..." is a genuinely fun, on-theme headline — feels like it was written for someone going back to campus, not just generic filler.
- The Dorm Shop / Just Like Home section with the soap dispenser and bedding is a smart category I haven't seen H&M email me before — differentiates from Zara and ASOS who stay strictly apparel.
- Live acoustic performance streamed on YouTube is a genuinely novel hook — no other brand in my recent inbox (Zara, ASOS, H&M itself) has done an event tie-in like this.
- The striped top + baggy denim hero model has that off-duty college girl look that tracks with the semester theme.

## What's weak

- Zero prices, zero percent-off, zero specific product I can tap and buy — every single CTA is a category button ("WOMEN," "OCCASION EDIT," "THE DORM SHOP"), which means I have to do all the work myself.
- This email is stupidly long — six stacked sections before I even hit the footer, most retailers (H&M's own "25% off flash sale" send from yesterday) get to the point in half the scroll.
- No urgency anywhere except the LA event date — nothing telling me why I need to shop today vs. next week.
- The "Campus Life" guy in the band tee doesn't match my Gen Z girl energy at all, feels like a generic filler shot to check the "men" box rather than an intentional style story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero product from each section and actually link it with a price — right now it's all traffic-routing to category pages, which kills impulse clicks. Show me the exact jean or the exact dorm bedding set.
- 2. Cut the email in half. Campus Life, Got Plans, Just Like Home, Live From LA, Shop By — that's 5 mini-campaigns fighting each other. Pick two and go deeper.
- 3. Move the LIVE FROM LA event up near the top — it's the most unique, scroll-stopping thing in this whole send and it's buried two-thirds down.
- 4. Add a "new student discount" or bundle price for the dorm essentials section — Target and Amazon own back-to-school pricing convos, H&M needs a hook to compete there.
