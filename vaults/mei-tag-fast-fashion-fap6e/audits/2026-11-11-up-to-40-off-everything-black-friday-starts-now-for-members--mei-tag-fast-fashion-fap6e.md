# Up to 40% off everything! Black Friday starts now for Members
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'm tired. This is the sixth H&M sale email in about a week, and the "Black Friday starts now" energy is getting repetitive. The hero is a close-up of a model with a sleek look and big red "MEMBER-EXCLUSIVE BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW" text across her chest. The red text sits on top of the photo and is hard to read where it overlaps her clothing. The "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" block right under it is much clearer, so that's where my eye actually lands.
- The body is a stack of category tiles: women, men, baby/kids, home. Nothing in it says "this just dropped on TikTok." Last week's "Color drop: Red basics" at least had a trend angle. This one is a generic sale grid. The "RSVP READY" and "PARTY STARTERS" headings do nod to holiday party season, which I like, but they don't name any product.
- Would I click? Maybe the Women tile, since there's a red dress in it. I wouldn't forward it. Zara and ASOS would make me feel like the trend was theirs. This just says "everything is cheaper."

## What's working

- "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" is big, bold and obvious. I get the offer in one second.
- The member framing makes me feel like I get early access, and the "extra 10% off" join nudge is a nice touch.
- The "RSVP READY" tile has a red satin dress and a black dress. That's party-season styling I'd actually wear.
- The layout is clean, with one CTA per category, so the buttons are easy to tap on my phone.

## What's weak

- The red hero text overlaps the model's clothing and gets muddy. It's the one visible readability issue.
- "Up to 40%" is vague. I have no idea which items get 40% and which get 10%.
- The black "30% OFF select styles in-store" block has dark red text on a black background. It's low contrast and hard to read.
- The fine print takes up a huge chunk of the bottom, and it's tiny. It feels like the offer is buried in exclusions.
- It's the same cadence of sale blasts as "Up to 30% off 100s of knits" from yesterday. The offer escalates, but the creative doesn't change.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero product or look, with real product callouts and prices. Skip the generic category grid.
- 2. Fix the hero text contrast by moving the headline off the model's body, or using white text.
- 3. Show the discount on the tiles, for example "From £9.99", so the savings feel real.
- 4. Make the in-store block readable with brighter text on black.
- 5. Cut the cadence. One strong Black Friday email beats five near-identical ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Member-only 40% off is live`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Party fits, 40% off, Members first`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Red satin dresses, tailored coats and gifts, all up to 40% off before everyone else.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access ends 11/16. Shop party looks before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links, including the unsubscribe link, returned HTTP 403 from the `t19.email.hm.com` click tracker. That pattern points to bot-filtering of the QA probe, so I can't confirm the links are broken. Separately, many images lack alt text, the JSON-LD block is unpopulated, and the header checks were inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on every probed link (12/12):** this covers "Shop now" (×4 variants), "Find a store" (×2), the "H&M" logo link, both image links (`...f8bc9d19`, `...f8bc9d1a`), "Sign in", "Sign up" and "Unsubscribe". Example: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h23ac8803-3a71-4684-93b3-6497fddce19d,5514638f,f8bc9d1b&did=1427399567&rid=3326930539...`.
- **Likely a probe artifact:** a uniform 403 across every link, including one that must work for real recipients (unsubscribe), is consistent with the ESP tracker rejecting non-browser clients. It would not be expected if the campaign's links were actually dead. This was not verified with a real client, so treat "broken" as unconfirmed.
- **Destinations can't be audited:** every link is wrapped in the redirect tracker, and 68 further links were not probed.
- **Duplicate tracked links:** the "Shop now" links appear in two variants each (`f8bc9d1b`/`f8bc9d1e`, `f8bc9cf9`/`f8bc9cfb`), and "Find a store" appears twice (`f8bc9d3b`/`f8bc9d3c`). This is consistent with separate desktop and mobile blocks (`.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`), so it is not a defect in itself.
- **Link text from alt fallback:** the QA tool reads the image links as `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`. This is a symptom of the alt-text issue below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 20 `<img>` elements flagged:**
  - Content images under `https://image.hm.com/content/dam/bf2026-ame/na/8198524_CLOC265P01B_Black_Friday_Pre_deals_2x3...` and `.../wk-46/us174_s4/1367952001_202604_LB_0778.jpg/`, `1360156001`, `1362972002`, `1353295001`, `0718908018`, `1357800001`, `1354145002` and `1348251001`. Each appears as a 1400px and a 750px variant.
  - The tracking pixel `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h23ac8803-...,1`, flagged twice.
- **Effect:** with images off, or in a screen reader, the image links have no accessible name, and the filename `cq5dam.web.*.jpeg` is read out instead.
- **Plain-text part:** 520 of 598 characters are URLs (above the 70% warning threshold). This can hurt spam scoring and plain-text readability.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** `fabric.hmgroup.com` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/...oces.css` are imported in `<style>`. Gmail and some other clients ignore these, so fallback fonts will be used there. This is not confirmed as a defect without seeing the font stacks, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- No other rendering issues are confirmable from the truncated source. Viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` meta tags are present, and mobile breakpoint CSS (`@media (max-width:699px)`) is in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) are visible in the available source.
- **Empty schema.org fields:** the JSON-LD block ships with blank values. `subjectLine: ""`, `discountCode: ""`, `availabilityEnds: ""`, `PromotionCard.url: ""` and `image: ""` are unpopulated. `availabilityStarts` is the stale placeholder `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`. Gmail may ignore or reject the markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA output itself says this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation. Because of that, it is inconclusive, not a confirmed violation. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (expected via the relay). No finding either way.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** it is present, but it returned 403 under the probe (see section 2). It carries per-recipient identifiers (`rid`, `erid`, `p4=3326930539`, `p5=9be06746...`), so it is not a generic link. Functionality is unconfirmed.
- **Physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified. No finding.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM/campaign parameters can't be assessed:** all hrefs are opaque tracker redirects. The only visible campaign identifiers are tracker params (`p1=DM6220625`, `p3=20261111`).
- **Landing page alignment can't be verified:** the probes were blocked, so no destination URLs or landing pages were resolved.
- **Possible locale mismatch (low confidence):** the lead hero asset path contains `..._en-CA` (`bf2026-ame/na/8198524_CLOC265P01B_Black_Friday_Pre_deals_2x3_Display_Static_Owned_en-CA`). The other assets sit under `us174_s4`, which suggests a US segment. If the destination or creative is Canadian for US recipients, that would be a continuity issue. Confirm this by resolving the tracked links with a browser user agent.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the tracked links** with a browser-like user agent or a real client. Confirm that `t19.email.hm.com/r/...` resolves to a 200 or 302 and record the final landing URLs and their UTM params. Do not treat the 403s as a send defect until then.
2. **Check raw headers:** confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass on the original message, bypassing the relay.
3. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to all content images and to the tracking pixel (`alt=""`). Fix the image-link names that currently fall back to `cq5dam.web.*.jpeg`.
4. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD block:** fill `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts/Ends` and the promo `url`/`image` fields, or drop the empty markup.
5. **Rebalance the plain-text part:** add more descriptive text so it is below roughly 70% URL content.
6. **Confirm the `en-CA` hero asset** is intended for the recipient's region.
