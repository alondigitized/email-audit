- Okay wait, I thought Zara FINALLY figured it out with the women's email yesterday (09/11), and today it's a full swing back to an entire men's AW26 "Origins" lookbook — shirts, suits, blazers, leather jackets, trench coats, jeans, sneakers, every single model is male. This is the third men's-skewed or off-target send from Zara in a week and a half (men's dump 09/05, kids 09/06, now this), and I'm genuinely starting to wonder if I'm even on a real list or just getting whatever Inditex fires off that day.
- Visually it's actually not bad — moody AW26 editorial, the brown/olive/cream leather-and-knit palette is giving "quiet luxury menswear" and I can see why someone would click if they were shopping for their boyfriend. But I am not shopping for my boyfriend, I'm shopping for me, and there's zero women's product anywhere in this entire scroll.
- Not clicking, not forwarding — unless I'm sending it to a guy friend as a "hey this is kind of fire" text, which, fine, that happens, but it's not a Zara-for-me win. Marking as read and moving on, same as the puffer jacket kids email.

## What's working

- The AW26 "Origins" editorial styling is genuinely elevated — moody lighting, exposed brick, that black-and-white suit shot looks like a fashion magazine spread, not a stock catalog.
- Clear category navigation with labeled sections (Shirts, Suits, Blazers, Jackets, Trench Coats, Jeans, Sneakers) makes it easy to jump to what you want, if you wanted any of it.
- No render bugs — images all loaded, text is clean, no overlapping or broken layout.
- The 6-grid outfit lineup gives a real "how to wear it" trend story instead of just isolated product shots.

## What's weak

- I'm a 24-year-old woman and there is not a single women's product, model, or category in this entire email — it's 100% menswear top to bottom.
- Compare this to H&M's tennis-core send that scored 6/10 in my inbox this week — they at least led with something I could actually wear. This is a full skip.
- "ZARA ORIGINS | AW26" as a section header tells me nothing about what's inside — no hint it's a men's-only drop until I actually see the models.
- No offer, no % off, no loyalty callout, nothing time-bound — just a lookbook with zero urgency to open now vs. whenever.
- Same segmentation problem as the Massimo Dutti email (09/08) and the kids puffer email (09/06) — feels like I'm getting whatever's in the queue, not anything picked for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the segmentation, immediately.** Three of my last five Zara emails have been off-target for a female shopper (men's, kids, Massimo Dutti cross-promo). If I'm on a women's list, send me women's product. This is the single biggest lever here.
- 2. Add a real offer or urgency hook — right now this is pure lookbook with no reason to act today.
- 3. If Zara wants to send a men's-focused editorial to its full list, at minimum add a "shop women's AW26 →" module so non-target subscribers have somewhere to click.
