# Up to 40% off gifts for her
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the eighth H&M member-sale email in about two weeks, and I'm tired. The fluffy cream shearling coat in the hero is cute and very "cold girl aesthetic," so my eyes did land there first. Then the black banner at the top shouts "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" in red, and the headline says "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" again. That's the same offer as the 11/11 "Black Friday starts now for Members" send. It doesn't feel like a trend moment, it feels like a clearance rack.
- The "gifts for her" angle is a miss for me. I shop for myself on Saturday three-tab compares, and I'm not building a list of names. The grid is fine but safe: lounge, stocking stuffers, active, sweaters. Nothing says "this is what's blowing up on TikTok right now." Zara's "Trending now: Party" at least tried to say what's hot. I'd skim it, maybe tap the sweaters, and not forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The shearling coat hero is a strong, cozy image, and it fits mid-November weather.
- "Up to 40% off for members" is easy to read, and the black "Shop now" button is obvious.
- The alternating image/text blocks are scannable on a phone, and the models look like my age.
- The $10 e-gift card banner at the top is a second hook for people who are buying gifts.

## What's weak

- It's the same member 40% off I've been getting since 11/11, so there's no urgency or novelty. The 11/13 outerwear email at least had a specific price, from $29.99.
- The hero never shows the coat in the headline or tells me what it costs. There's no price-per-piece anywhere.
- The "gifts for her" framing pushes me away. I want "what's trending," not "what to buy someone else."
- Sign in and Sign up buttons sit right under the CTA. I'm already a member, so that's clutter and it reads like a stranger email.
- The legal block at the bottom is a huge wall of tiny text, and the fine print on the e-gift card and exclusions makes the offer feel less honest.
- There's no social proof, no reviews, and no "trending" or "back in stock" signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one specific hero product and price, like "The faux-fur coat, $59.99 for members," instead of a generic percentage.
- 2. Drop the Sign in/Sign up block for existing members, and show a real member status instead.
- 3. Replace the generic category tiles with trend labels like "Cold-girl knits" or "Y2K lounge," so it feels trend-first, not me-too.
- 4. Add a real deadline in the body. The 11/16 end date is only buried in the fine print.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Skip a send or pair the offers, because this one is repeating the last four.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 40% off ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy knits, lounge sets & puffers. Your member price ends 11/16.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a $10 e-gift card when you spend $50. Today's picks inside.`

The source also has missing alt text on every probed image and empty fields in its JSON-LD block, and the relay stripped the headers needed to verify compliance.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links failed with 403.** They are `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h5b7c26c0-…,551451df,<hex>&did=1427395039&rid=3326930539…`. That covers 8 "Shop now" CTAs, the "H&M" logo link, the "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" link, an image link, and "Unsubscribe".
- **Likely false positive.** Every failure is the same host and path, and the sample is only 12 of 64 links (52 skipped). A uniform 403 across a redirector usually means bot or WAF blocking of the scanner's user agent or IP, not dead links. It cannot be confirmed from this data, so the failures should be re-probed with a browser-like client or from a different IP.
- **Link text is polluted.** Two CTAs have the text `‌  ﻿Shop now`, which has zero-width characters (U+200C and U+FEFF) and padding spaces ahead of the visible text (`…f8c2dd0c`, `…f8c2dd0e`).
