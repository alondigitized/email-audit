# Up to 30% off ends tomorrow
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, deja vu — this is literally the SAME "up to 30% off ends tomorrow" urgency beat H&M already ran on 8/1 with "Score 30% off in-store all weekend," except now they've stapled a hard grid of category buttons (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Find a Store) up top and gone full "For Her / For Him / For Them" family catalog mode with a graphic-tee model lineup. Nothing here says trend, nothing says TikTok, nothing says "we called this first" — it's just back-to-school inventory clearance dressed up with urgency copy.
- The "For Him" model literally has a Metallica band tee on and there's a kid-in-jerseys tax-free section — I am a 24-year-old who does not have kids and does not shop menswear, so half this email is dead weight for me. Zara's "This week at Zara" got dinged for being a men's editorial with zero female signal; H&M is now doing the same thing but worse because it's ALSO doing toddlers.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding. This is a skim-and-archive, maybe day nine of H&M back-to-school fatigue in my inbox and I'm actively tuning it out now.

## What's working

- The offer is upfront and unmissable — "UP TO 30% OFF IN STORES & ONLINE" in big bold caps right under the header, no scrolling needed.
- "Ends tomorrow" urgency is at least clear and specific, not vague "limited time" filler.
- Clean white layout, no render bugs, no broken images — reliably on-brand H&M minimalism.
- Buy-online-pickup-in-store banner up top is a real convenience flag if I were actually shopping today.

## What's weak

- The hero model wears a "PARIS" graphic sweatshirt — that's giving 2019 airport merch, not a trend I'd screenshot for my group chat.
- "For Him" and the tax-free kids-in-jerseys section are pure dead space for me — I'm not buying menswear or kidswear, so that's like a third of this email I'm scrolling past.
- Zero social proof, zero "trending now" or "back in stock" language — nothing here signals H&M got ahead of a trend versus just clearing back-to-school stock.
- This is the same "ends tomorrow / all weekend" urgency framing they used 8/1 — the cadence is starting to feel like the boy who cried wolf on deadlines.
- No price-per-item, no specific product callouts — just a blanket "up to 30%" with generic category buttons, which is way less compelling than ASOS running an actual adidas Originals x ASOS lookbook with named product drops.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut "For Him" and the kids/tax-free block entirely from my segment — replace that real estate with a "For Her" trend drop (the actual thing my persona cares about) and maybe a "trending now" strip of 3-4 specific SKUs with prices.
- 2. Swap the generic "PARIS" hero for an actual trending graphic/silhouette that's currently blowing up — give me a reason to believe H&M caught this before Shein or Zara did.
- 3. Add urgency that isn't recycled from yesterday's send — a countdown timer or store-specific stock language would feel more credible than the third "ends tomorrow/this weekend" this week.
- 4. Show a price next to at least one hero item — "up to 30%" with zero anchor price makes the offer feel abstract.

# Technical Email Audit — H&M "Up to 30% off ends tomorrow"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's core CTAs, "Find a store" links, and the unsubscribe link all return HTTP 403 through H&M's `t19.email.hm.com` click-tracking redirector; the embedded JSON-LD schema also ships with multiple empty/stale merge fields, indicating a broken personalization or QA-environment render.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 material links probed, all 12 failed with HTTP 403**, including every primary CTA:
  - `Shop now` (×6) — e.g. `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h0fa25fd4-...&p3=20260802`
  - `Find a store` (×2)
  - `Need an item sooner? Buy online, pick up in-store`
  - Two zero-width-character-obfuscated `Shop now` links (link text contains `‌ ﻿Shop now` — ZWSP/ZWNBSP characters embedded in anchor text)
  - `Unsubscribe`
- All failing links share the same redirector host (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...`) and campaign params (`did=1714914932&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM5903006&p2=0982dd0aa7b117bf086dee6ed4af7d0c&p3=20260802`), consistent with a single expired/misconfigured redirect mapping rather than isolated per-link bugs — this looks like a tracking-domain or campaign-ID issue on H&M's ESP side, not a per-link content error.
- Each URL differs only in a trailing hex ID (`f79fadb4`, `f79fadb5`, etc.), suggesting per-asset click IDs behind one shared redirect service that is currently returning 403 for this campaign.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 images missing `alt` text**, including all primary product/promo imagery (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` variants under `NA1534_Email4_FNP_*` and `US133_FNP_1340299002*`) and two tracking-pixel-style images served from the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` redirector itself.
- Screen-reader users get no fallback content for any hero/product image if images are blocked — a significant accessibility gap given the entire offer message ("Up to 30% off") appears to be conveyed via image, not live text.
- Mobile responsive CSS (`optimize-css` block) is present and extensive (`.is-hidden-mobile`, `.image-mobile`, breakpoint at `max-width:699px`), so responsive layout itself appears intact — no rendering-breakage evidence beyond the alt-text gaps.
- Outlook/webkit conditional handling present (`#MessageViewBody .hide-webkit`, `@media screen yahoo`), standard defensive CSS — no issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `EmailMessage` JSON-LD block ships with **unresolved/empty fields**: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"image":""`, `"url":""` under the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` schema objects.
- `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` is a **stale hardcoded date** nearly 8 years old relative to today, strongly suggesting this JSON-LD template block was never repopulated for the current campaign (subject line is "Up to 30% off ends tomorrow" per the email header, but the schema's own `subjectLine` and `discountCode` are blank).
- Net effect: Gmail/Google Workflow-style promotional markup (annotations, structured snippets) will not render correctly for this send since required fields are empty strings rather than omitted or populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken (403)** — the link exists (satisfies CAN-SPAM's visible-unsubscribe requirement structurally) but is non-functional as delivered, which is a functional CAN-SPAM risk if unresolved.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI is unavailable; note QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it, so this should be confirmed against the raw source at the MTA before treating as a sender-side defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — one-click unsubscribe (POST) not confirmed present, same relay caveat applies.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data; flagged as unknown, not failing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All outbound links route through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` click-tracker before redirecting to the actual landing page; because every probed link 403s at the tracker layer, **the final landing-page destination and any UTM/query-param alignment cannot be verified** — this is a blocking issue for continuity testing, not just a link-health issue.
- Tracking params observed (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) are H&M's internal campaign/click IDs, not standard UTM parameters — no `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` visible at this layer (they may be appended post-redirect, which is unverifiable given the 403s).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority:** Investigate the 403 on `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` — check whether the redirect/tracking domain or campaign ID (`p1=DM5903006`) has expired or was deployed before the tracker mapping was live. This affects 100% of tested links including unsubscribe.
2. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers directly against the raw SMTP source (not via the AgentMail relay) to rule out a relay-capture artifact before flagging as a compliance failure.
3. Populate or strip the empty JSON-LD fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url`) and correct the stale `availabilityStarts` date so structured promotional markup renders correctly in supporting inboxes.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 11 flagged images, particularly the product/offer imagery carrying the core "30% off" message.
5. Once links are restored, re-run continuity checks to confirm final landing-page URLs match the promoted offer and carry consistent tracking parameters.
