#  Up to 50% off denim — yes, all denim
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay so H&M is back in my inbox — again, twice today — and this one is actually catching my attention more than the swim email they sent earlier. Denim is having a full-on TikTok moment right now: barrel jeans, wide-leg, double denim, denim-on-denim sets, I'm actively saving inspo. The hero image delivers on that promise — young woman, red ribbed crop, oversized wide-leg jeans, looks like it was styled for Pinterest circa this exact week. The "yes, all denim" parenthetical in the subject is doing something smart: it's preempting my skepticism before I even open. Like they know I'm going to ask "but what counts?"
- That said, this is the second H&M email I've gotten today alone. May 20 was sitewide 50% off, May 22 was "kick-start summer," earlier today was swim, and now this. I'm not mad at the denim angle — it's genuinely more focused than the vague "sitewide" spam — but my thumb is getting tired. The email does the job: clear offer, right product, right model energy. I'd click once, probably won't forward.
- Mark as read? No. Actually click through? Yes, but only because denim is the one category I can justify to myself right now.

## What's working

- The hero model is styled exactly how I want to dress — red ribbed cami, oversized wide-leg denim, cool-girl slouch. H&M nailed the demographic signal here.
- "Yes, all denim" in the subject line is a smart conversational hook — it pre-answers my first objection ("but does that include the good stuff?") before I've even scrolled.
- Denim category focus is a real upgrade over the last two sitewide blasts. Zara would have called this "Woman | Denim Edit" and I'd have to guess at the offer; H&M put the price upfront.
- Product grid below the hero shows range — washes, cuts, shorts, likely jackets — which tells me the 50% off is genuinely broad, not just two clearance SKUs.
- No render bugs, layout is clean, eye goes hero → offer → grid with no visual friction.

## What's weak

- This is literally the second H&M email I got today. The swim send came first. I haven't processed one and the next is already here — cadence fatigue is real and it cheapens both.
- The 50% off number has now appeared in every H&M email since May 20. It's lost all urgency — I assumed there was a sale before I even opened.
- No deadline anywhere visible. "Up to 50% off" with no end date feels like a markdown rack, not a denim moment. ASOS at least frames limited-time edits even when the sale is ongoing.
- The H&M Studio Resort cross-sell at the bottom is jarring — that email already came on May 21 and pushing it again here breaks the denim-only focus.
- No trending denim callouts (barrel leg, wide-leg specifically, denim sets) — I need to know if the discount touches the styles TikTok is actually talking about, not just "jeans."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a soft deadline to rescue the offer.** "50% off all denim — 3 days only" instantly changes the math for me. Without it, I'll come back "later" which means never.
- 2. **Lead with a trend-first headline, not just the discount.** Something like "Wide leg. Barrel leg. All of it. 50% off." makes this feel like a moment, not a markdown.
- 3. **Cut the Studio Resort cross-sell.** You already sent that email. Let denim own this real estate.
- 4. **Either skip the swim send today or hold this one for tomorrow.** Two emails in one day from the same sender is inbox noise; one sharp denim email lands better alone.
- 5. **Show the trending cuts explicitly** — if you have barrel jeans at 50% off, say it. That's an instant click from anyone who's been in the TikTok denim rabbit hole.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Wide leg, barrel, all of it — 50% off denim`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your TikTok denim wishlist just got 50% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Every wash, cut, and style — denim sale ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The wide-leg moment is here. Shop denim at half price.`
