# Today only: 20% off in the app
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Opened this and my first reaction was: oh, another app download push dressed up as a flash sale. The "TODAY ONLY 20% OFF APP ORDERS" hero is legible and clear, but the moment I processed it I was already vaguely annoyed — I'm on your list, H&M. I have the app. Why are you selling me on downloading it again?
- Then I spotted the tiny banner at the very top: Stella McCartney x H&M giveaway. That's the one that actually made me sit up. A designer collab giveaway is a real trend moment — the kind of thing I screenshot and send to my group chat. But H&M buried it in six-point font while the main hero is... a download prompt. The model is wearing a gorgeous red floral maxi that's peak summer TikTok right now, and there's zero "shop this look" link in sight. There's a disconnect between the vibe the image creates and the action the email is asking me to take.
- Would I click? Probably yes, but chasing the Stella McCartney thing, not the 20% off. Would I forward it? No. Nothing here screams "you HAVE to see this."

## What's working

- The "TODAY ONLY / 20% OFF APP ORDERS" hierarchy is immediately readable — no hunting for the offer
- The model's red floral maxi is genuinely on-trend: romantic-boho, summer-ready, the silhouette blowing up on TikTok right now
- H&M is a name I trust and the email doesn't feel spammy or off-brand
- The Stella McCartney x H&M giveaway banner, small as it is, signals this isn't just another generic markdown blast — there's a collab behind it
- "SCAN & FIND" feature callout resonates with me personally (I do this in-store constantly)

## What's weak

- The Stella McCartney giveaway — the most interesting thing in this email — is treated like a footnote while the app download push gets the entire hero treatment
- The primary CTA is "DOWNLOAD IOS / DOWNLOAD ANDROID," which tells me H&M doesn't know I'm already a subscriber and app user; it treats me like a cold stranger
- Two unrelated promotions (app discount + designer giveaway) in one email split my attention without a clear winner
- There is no direct shop link to the 20% off sale — I have to download/open the app and find it myself, which is friction I won't bother with
- The feature explainer section ("MEMBER HUB / APP EXCLUSIVES / SCAN & FIND / VISUAL SEARCH") is onboarding content that has no business in a promotional email sent to active subscribers

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the hierarchy — make the Stella McCartney collab the hero.** Lead with "Win a piece from our Stella McCartney drop" and make the 20% off a secondary callout. The collab is the trend moment; the discount is the sweetener.
- 2. **Replace the download CTAs with a deep-linked "SHOP THE SALE" button** that opens the app directly to the sale for existing users. Non-app users can get the download prompt — don't make everyone jump through that hoop.
- 3. **Link the model's dress.** The floral maxi is doing all the visual work in this email and there's no path to buy it. A "Shop this look" tag underneath the hero image would do it.
- 4. **Cut the app feature explainer entirely.** Active subscribers don't need to be told what a Member Hub is. Save that content for a new-user welcome flow.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stella McCartney x H&M — you could win it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off + a designer giveaway: today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your in-app discount is live — plus enter the Stella McCartney drop`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The collab giveaway is members-only. You're already in.`
