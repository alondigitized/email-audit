# New winter staples, elevated style
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is "okay, another H&M member-sale email." This is the seventh in about a week, after "Black Friday starts now for Members" yesterday and the knits drop on 11/10. The black-and-white street shot with the fringed skirt and black turtleneck is moody and well shot. It looks like a Zara campaign, not an H&M one. But "winter staples" is the opposite of what I open emails for. I want to know what's trending, and staples are what I buy when I'm not chasing a trend.
- The offer sits in a red-on-black banner at the top, and it's easy to miss. "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS*" is small red text on black, and the asterisk already makes me suspicious. The products are better. The red polo-collar knit, the red merino jumper and the pile-lined coat give me a little of the "red basics" angle from the 11/09 send. I'd probably tap through once, but I'm not forwarding it to anyone. I'd mark it read and go check Zara and ASOS.

## What's working

- The hero photo is gorgeous and editorial. It feels premium, not clearance-bin.
- The member prices in red under each item ($279 → $222.99, $59.99 → $47.99) make the deal clear without doing math.
- The red knit and red jumper are the only real "of the moment" pieces, and they pop against all the black.
- One "SHOP NOW" button under a short, clear headline means I know exactly where to tap.
- The "New Arrival" tags are the closest thing to a "we got it first" signal in the whole email.

## What's weak

- "Staples" and "elevated" are boring words for a trend chaser. Nothing says TikTok, viral or "everyone's wearing this."
- The 40% off is buried in a thin banner, and the fine print is a wall of text. It has exclusions, a bonus-reward clause and a return window through January. The offer doesn't feel fully honest.
- The prices don't match the headline. A $279 coat at $222.99 is only 20% off, and the turtlenecks are about 20% off too. I see no 40% anywhere in the body.
- There's no model diversity. The hero is one slim model, and the product tiles are mostly flat-lay turtlenecks and jackets with no people in them.
- The product name "OVERSIZED FUNNEL-COLLA… JACKET" is cut off mid-word, and there's a big blank gap before "SHOP BY."
- The cadence is exhausting. This is the same member-pricing pitch I got yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline and the real deal in the hero, e.g. "Members: 40% off ends 11/16," not tucked into the footer. Then make sure the products shown actually hit that percentage.
- 2. Lead with the trend angle (red, fringe, oversized coats) and show the pieces on a range of body types and ages.
- 3. Cut the fine print down to one line with a link, so it stops feeling like a trap.
- 4. Space sends out, or change what each one is about. Seven member-sale emails in a week trains me to ignore all of them.
- 5. Fix the truncated product name and close the empty gap above "SHOP BY."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 403 probe failures all hit H&M's `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirector, so they are probably bot-blocking and not dead links. That means link health is unverified. The confirmed code-level issues are widespread missing image alt text, empty JSON-LD schema fields, and a link-heavy plain-text part. Compliance headers could not be assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** These include the primary "SHOP NOW" CTAs (`...f8bec048`, `...f8bec049`), the hero image links (`...f8bec046`, `...f8bec047`), the logo link (`...f8bec03b`) and the unsubscribe link (`...f8bec095`). Every URL has the same shape: `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hae83966e-...&did=1427379252&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM6217332&p2=...&p3=20261112`.
- **The uniform 403 points to the ESP's bot or WAF filtering of non-browser probes.** It does not point to individually broken links. The probe could not confirm that any link resolves, so real-user behavior is unverified. I am not treating this as a confirmed outage.
- **Every link is an opaque redirect.** Final destinations cannot be checked from the source or the QA data.
- **Only 12 of 89 links were probed** (77 skipped), so coverage is partial.
- **Several links have zero-width characters in their text.** The "Shop now" link text is `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which contains ZWNJ and BOM characters. This is likely preheader or spacing padding that leaked into anchor text, and it can affect screen readers and click-text analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images are missing alt text (confirmed, 17 warnings):**
  - Hero images `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, repeated three times each (desktop and mobile variants).
  - Product images `64065ec5...jpg`, `65729a94...jpg`, `28503191...jpg` and `70d1d75e...jpg`, each at `?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322`.
  - Two images wrapped by `t19.email.hm.com/r/...` links.
- **Image-only links have no accessible name.** The hero image links (`f8bec046`, `f8bec047`, `f8bec04a`, `f8bec04b`, `f8bec04e`) fall back to the filename (`[image: cq5dam.web...]`) or nothing when images are blocked.
- **Duplicate desktop and mobile image variants** (1400px and 750px) are used with `is-hidden-mobile` / `is-hidden-desktop` classes. Both sets are in the DOM, and screen readers may read both if `display:none` isn't honored.
- **Fonts are loaded with `@import`** (`fabric.hmgroup.com`, `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`) in a `<style>` block. Gmail and some clients strip these, so the web fonts fall back.
- **The viewport meta tag is minimal** (`width=device-width`, no `initial-scale`). This is a minor point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source or QA data. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the portion provided.
- **The JSON-LD block has empty fields:** `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, PromotionCard `"image":""` and `"url":""`. `availabilityStarts` is a stale `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00` default. This is template boilerplate that was never populated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **The `p1`/`p2`/`p3` parameters** are `DM6217332`, a hash, and `20261112` (the send date). They are internal tracking IDs. Any UTMs would be appended after the redirect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe a sample of the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links** with a real browser User-Agent or headless browser. This will separate WAF blocking from real failures, and should include the unsubscribe link.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text to all content images.** Use `alt=""` for purely decorative ones. Give image-only links descriptive alt text so the links have an accessible name.
3. **Strip the zero-width and BOM characters** from anchor text ("Shop now").
4. **Populate or remove the empty JSON-LD fields.** The empty `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `url` and `image` values and the 2018 `availabilityStarts` are invalid.

6. **Rebuild the plain-text part** with real copy and fewer inline tracking URLs.
