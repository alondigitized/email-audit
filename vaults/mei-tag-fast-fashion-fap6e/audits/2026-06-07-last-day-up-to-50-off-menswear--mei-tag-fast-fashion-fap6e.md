# Last day: Up to 50% off menswear
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay so H&M is really gonna send me the exact same Father's Day menswear email twice in three days, huh? I literally saw this campaign on June 5th — "Up to 50% off gifts for every dad" — and now we're back with the same male model, same polo shirt hero, same 50% off menswear offer, with a "Last day" sticker slapped on top like that changes anything. This is email number five from H&M in five days. Five. Two of those are the same Father's Day pitch.
- The one thing that made me pause was the member giveaway below the fold — a $250 gift card is actually genuinely interesting regardless of the category, and I'm not going to pretend it didn't catch my eye. But the hero is still a guy in a polo. The primary CTA is still "SHOP GIFTS for dad." I am not buying my dad anything from H&M this weekend, and H&M clearly has no idea whether I buy menswear or not.
- Would I click? Only to enter the giveaway. Would I forward to a friend? No — I'd text "H&M is truly going off the rails this week" to my group chat and archive it.

## What's working

- "Last day" in the subject is clean, punchy urgency — I believe it, it's not screaming at me with exclamation points
- The $250 gift card member giveaway is the only above-average hook in the whole send — free money transcends category
- Layout is clean and renders perfectly; no broken images, H&M's minimal aesthetic holds up
- The SHOP BY module at the bottom with a WOMEN thumbnail is a subtle reminder that I actually *do* shop here — just not for this
- 50% off is front and center, zero scrolling required to see the deal

## What's weak

- This is functionally a rerun of the June 5th email — a "last day" subject does not make it a new email when the hero, model, and offer are identical
- Male model is the only human in this email and he's wearing a polo shirt; not one womenswear piece visible above the fold
- Five emails in five days has pushed me into auto-archive instinct — ASOS has also been emailing me aggressively this week but at least they served me "Summer pieces everyone's wearing" aimed at women, not their dad's wardrobe
- The preheader "NEED AN ITEM SOONER? BUY ONLINE, PICK UP IN STORE" is dead weight — it's a logistics note when I needed to be pulled in by the last-day energy
- Zara would have made the giveaway the headline if they had one; H&M buried it under the fold like it was an afterthought

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment this list** — anyone who bought menswear in the last 90 days gets the Father's Day hero; everyone else gets a women's flash sale framed around the same deadline. The urgency is already there, just redirect it.
- 2. **Lead with the $250 giveaway** — surface it above the fold as the hook, then let the Father's Day sale be the secondary module. Right now it's inverted.
- 3. **Swap the preheader** — "NEED AN ITEM SOONER?" tells me nothing new; use it to amplify urgency or the giveaway.
- 4. **Break the cadence** — after five emails in five days, even a one-day gap would make the "last day" hit harder. Zara would have spaced this out.

- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off + a surprise bundle — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member giveaway closes tonight — plus last call on the sale`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The deal and the giveaway both end today`
