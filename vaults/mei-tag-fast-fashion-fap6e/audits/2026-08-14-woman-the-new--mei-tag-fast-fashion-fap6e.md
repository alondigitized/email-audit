# Woman | The New
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually giving something — florals, fringe, the bustier top, that whole moody boho-with-an-edge vibe that's genuinely on my FYP right now. No cheesy discount banner, no "SALE" screaming at me, just a straight-up runway-style catalog drop that feels like Zara actually made this trend instead of chasing it. That's a nice change of pace after three straight capsule/home/kids misses this week.
- But it's SO long. I'm scrolling forever — ZW Collection, floral prints, blazers/jackets/co-ords, TRF jeans, "THE ITEM," selected pieces — it's basically their entire catalog dumped into one email with zero personalization and zero offer. There's not a single price, discount, or reason to act NOW. It's a lookbook, not a marketing email.
- Would I click? Maybe on the bustier top honestly, that's cute. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is urgent or exclusive enough to text a friend about. This gets a scroll-through and a close, not a screenshot to the groupchat.

## What's working

- The floral/lingerie/leather styling on the "Floral Prints and Delicate Details" and "Selected Pieces" sections is genuinely on-trend — this feels like Zara setting the trend, not copying H&M's "preppy layers" drop from last week.
- "THE ITEM | THE BUSTIER TOP" calling out one hero piece is smart — it's the one section that reads like an actual product spotlight instead of a catalog page.
- Model diversity is decent across the sections — different skin tones and styling, not the same face repeated.
- Clean typographic layout, no render bugs, no overlapping text — everything's crisp.

## What's weak

- There is not a single offer, price, or CTA button anywhere — it's all just underlined text links (KNITWEAR, DRESSES, JEANS) which barely register as tappable on mobile.
- Zero urgency, zero personalization — no "for you," no member perks, nothing that says they know who I am.
- It's exhausting to scroll — six+ collection sections back to back with no hierarchy telling me where to look first.
- Compare this to ASOS's Lioness drop — that one at least had a single moody hero shot that made me stop scrolling. This just keeps going and going.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE hero moment — lead with the bustier top or the floral co-ord set and link straight to it, save the rest for a "shop the full collection" link at the bottom.
- 2. Add actual CTA buttons instead of tiny underlined text links — "SHOP NOW" tap targets, not hyperlinks I have to zoom to hit.
- 3. Give me a reason to act today — new-drop urgency, "just landed," or early access for app users since they're already pushing the app download at the bottom.
- 4. Personalize the order — if I've been browsing dresses, lead with dresses, not jeans I didn't ask for.
