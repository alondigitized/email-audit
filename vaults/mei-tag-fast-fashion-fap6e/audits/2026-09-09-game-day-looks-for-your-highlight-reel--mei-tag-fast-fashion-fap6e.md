- Okay, "GO TEMA" football content from H&M is actually a nice break from the fifth "up to 50% off sitewide" email in a row — at least this doesn't feel like the exact same Labor Day corpse being reanimated again. The "Fashion Playbook for tailgates, kickoff, and every game-day moment" copy is trying, and the sweatshirt/scuba jacket layout is giving me actual new product instead of another discount banner. But there's zero price cut anywhere in this email — full price across the board — and after a week of 50%-off spam, jumping straight to full-price game-day fits with no offer feels like a weird pivot with no bridge.
- The "FREE SHIPPING OVER $50 FOR H&M MEMBERS" banner up top is honestly the only offer in this whole email, and it's buried in tiny text before I even get to the hero image. Compared to what ASOS did with that moody Lioness drop shoot, this hero image is just a plain studio shot of a girl in a raglan top — no vibe, no story, just product on a body.
- I'd skim this, maybe tap into "WOMEN" out of curiosity since I do like layering pieces for fall, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone and I'm not clicking with urgency. It's a scroll-and-close.

## What's working

- "GO TEAM" and the game-day tailgate framing is a genuinely fresh angle after five straight "sitewide" discount emails — first real theme shift I've seen from H&M this week.
- Clear WOMEN/MEN segmentation buttons under both "GO TEAM" and "DENIM MVPS" sections, so I'm not stuck browsing the wrong closet.
- Product grid is clean — price, name, and "New Arrival" tags are all visible and readable without zooming.
- The green corduroy shacket on the denim guy actually looks like a real fall layering piece I'd consider, not just filler.

## What's weak

- No actual discount or promo code anywhere except the tiny "free shipping over $50 for members" line crammed at the very top — after a week of 50%-off blitzing, that's a jarring offer-cliff.
- The hero shot is just a static studio pose, no game-day energy, no stadium, no tailgate — the theme is all in the copy, none of it shows up visually like ASOS's Lioness campaign did with actual scene-setting.
- Model diversity is thin — every model shown is thin, mostly the same body type and vibe, nothing that feels like it's actually speaking to different girls watching the game.
- "DENIM MVPS" section has zero connective tissue to "game day" — it just reads like a bolted-on second email mid-scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer in the hero, not buried above it — even a "new arrivals, no discount" framing needs SOMETHING (loyalty points, early access) to justify a full-price ask right after a discount binge week.
- 2. Make the hero visual actually say "game day" — tailgate background, stadium seats, a friend group, not a plain white studio backdrop.
- 3. Cut "DENIM MVPS" into its own separate send or at least bridge it with copy tying jeans to game-day fits — right now it reads like two different emails glued together.
- 4. Add at least one plus-size or different body-type model in the football section — right now this doesn't feel inclusive of who's actually going to tailgates.
