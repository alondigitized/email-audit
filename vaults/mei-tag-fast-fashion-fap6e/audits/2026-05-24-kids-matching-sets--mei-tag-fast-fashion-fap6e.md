# Kids | Matching sets
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is the FOURTH Zara email I've gotten in like ten days and the second time this month I've ended up in the Kids tab when I'm a 24-year-old with no children. I'm not a mom. I'm not a kid. I'm not shopping for a niece. Zara's segmentation is just… not segmenting. The May 17 "Man" email already put me in the wrong inbox lane and now Kids — that's two misfires out of four sends, and the only one that actually hit my demo was the BENITO ANTONIO drop and even that one underdelivered on the actual product.
- Visually the email is pretty — soft pastel matching sets, that signature Zara editorial-magazine layout with the giant logo hero, the close-up of the laughing girl in the middle is genuinely a great shot. But pretty doesn't fix the targeting. I don't need to see two kids holding hands in a field when I'm trying to figure out if anyone shipped the bow-front trend before Shein did.
- Verdict: archive. No click, no forward. If anything I'm now actively considering switching to "promotions only on weekends" because the cadence is wearing me down.

## What's working

- The hero photo of the two girls in pastel sets is genuinely well shot — Zara's editorial DNA still slaps even when the content is wrong for me.
- Model casting is diverse (Black boy in green, white kids, mixed range) which I always notice — H&M's welcome email was a monochrome corporate ghost compared to this.
- The grid layout is clean and the product flatlays make it easy to scan price/color at a glance.
- Pastel sage/lavender/pink palette feels seasonal and correct for late May — at least the *trend* read is on-time, unlike ASOS recycling leopard print mid-summer.

## What's weak

- I am 24 and childless. Why am I in the Kids segment. This is a data hygiene problem, not a creative problem.
- Zero offer. No %, no free shipping, no "members get early access" — just "MATCHING SETS, EVERYDAY PIECES IN SOFT TEXTURES." H&M's "Up to 50% off denim" earned an 8 from me partly because there was an actual *number* on the screen.
- CTA situation is unclear — I see product tiles but no real button hierarchy. The only button-shaped thing is "DOWNLOAD OUR APP" at the bottom, which is the lowest-intent ask possible.
- Fourth Zara email in ten days and the second mis-segmented one. The cadence isn't the problem — the targeting is. Shein at least pretends to know what I clicked last week.
- Tiny footer text under DOWNLOAD OUR APP is unreadable on mobile without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the segmentation first.** Stop sending Kids and Man to a 24-year-old woman who only opens Woman. This is the single biggest lift — creative is wasted if the audience is wrong.
- 2. If the Kids send has to go wide, **lead with a "shop the look for mini-me" angle** that ties to womenswear (matching mom-and-daughter sets, gifting for a niece) so non-parents have a reason to stay.
- 3. Add a **concrete offer or member benefit** — Zara almost never discounts, fine, but at least "free shipping on Kids over $50" or "new arrivals — early access for ZARA members."
- 4. Kill "DOWNLOAD OUR APP" as the only visible CTA. Replace with **"Shop matching sets"** going to a real collection page.
- 5. Cut cadence. Four sends in ten days for a brand that's supposed to feel editorial and exclusive is starting to feel like Shein-level spam.
