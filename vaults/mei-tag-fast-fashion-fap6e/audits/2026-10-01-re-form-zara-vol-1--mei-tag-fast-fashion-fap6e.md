## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is Responsys-built, with 640px tables and a hidden preheader. The QA pass rate is 38%. The main technical gaps are a 0-character plain-text part, missing alt text on all 25 flagged images, no detected physical address, and invalid table markup. The HTML source was truncated, so link targets, the footer and the unsubscribe URL could not be checked directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 2. No per-link status (4xx/5xx, redirect chains) was supplied, so there are no confirmed broken links.
- Tracking uses first-party Responsys paths on `news.zara.com`. The open pixel is `https://news.zara.com/pub/as?_ri_=...`.
- Leftover template markers appear in the source: `<!--PREHEADER-->` and two `CAMPAIGN_CODE` comments with different locale values (`...;US_en` and `...;en`). They are harmless to rendering, but they expose internal campaign naming (`2026_10_01_bbdd_johngalliano_launch_01_10`) and suggest the template was concatenated from two locale variants.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flags 25 images, including `img_01.jpg` through `img_15_en.jpg`, the footer social icons `rrss1b.jpg` through `rrss6b.jpg`, `latredes142.jpg` (twice) and `download_en_b.jpg`. The campaign images appear to carry the content. With images blocked, the email would render as blank space.
- **Tracking pixel:** `/pub/as` is also flagged. For a pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Invalid markup:** `<div style="mso-hide:all;">` sits directly inside `<table width="640">`, between `<!--[if !mso 9]><!-->` and `<tr>`. A `div` is not a valid child of `table`, so clients may hoist or drop it. The `mso-hide` intent may not apply reliably.
- **Viewport meta:** `content="width=device-width; initial-scale=1.0; maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no;"` uses semicolons instead of commas. It also sets `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale=1.0`, which block pinch-zoom and fail WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Roboto+Condensed...">` is in `<head>`. Gmail and several other clients strip it, so those clients fall back to the system font.
- **Mobile classes:** `.font5` and `.font7` set 5px and 7px text on mobile, which is below legible size. I can't tell from the truncated source whether they are used on real content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `${...}` or `%%...%%`. The `{form}` in the subject line "Re{form} Zara" is stylized brand text, not a merge field. The remainder of the HTML was not available, so this is not verified for the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports `[FAIL]` with 0 chars. A missing text/plain alternative is a deliverability and spam-scoring negative.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, but it matters because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one, and CAN-SPAM requires it. The source was truncated, so the footer may contain it as image text or beyond the cutoff. The footer images (`latredes142.jpg` and others) have no alt text. If the address is image-only, it is not machine-readable.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and this is probably a relay artifact rather than a sender fault.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** QA says one is always included in probing, but no result was shown, so I can't confirm it works.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data provided. No destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page probe results appeared in the truncated source or QA output. Tracking looks to be handled by Responsys redirects (`_ri_=`) rather than visible UTMs. I did not verify whether the redirects append UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations

2. Add descriptive `alt` to every content image and `alt=""` to the `/pub/as` pixel and decorative spacers. Give the social icons names such as "Instagram". Put the product or campaign message and CTA text in the `alt` of the main images.
