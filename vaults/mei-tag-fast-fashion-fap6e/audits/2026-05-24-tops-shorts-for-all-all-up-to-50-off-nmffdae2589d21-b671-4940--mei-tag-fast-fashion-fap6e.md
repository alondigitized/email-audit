# Tops & shorts for all, all up to 50% off
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay so H&M is in my inbox AGAIN — this is like the fifth "up to 50% off" email in seven days. May 20 sitewide, May 22 sitewide, May 23 swim, May 23 denim, and now tops & shorts. At this point the 50% off has lost all meaning. It's not a sale anymore, it's just… the price. When ASOS hit me three times in four days with edits I at least got *different angles* — what's hot, embellished, leopard. H&M is just rotating the same sitewide promo with new product categories pasted on top.
- The creative itself is fine — that striped strapless top in the hero is cute, very TikTok-coded right now — but then I scroll and it's KIDS. A whole kid in a pink tank, a kid in shorts, a kid mix-and-match section. I'm 24. I'm not shopping for kids. This is the same energy as that Zara "Kids | Matching sets" email that scored 4/10 because I'm a childless adult who keeps getting routed into the kids' aisle.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward? Definitely not. Marking as read and moving on — Shein already dropped three new trend drops while I was opening this.

## What's working

- The hero striped top is actually cute and on-trend — barrel/strapless summer pieces are everywhere on my FYP right now.
- "Up to 50% off sitewide" is at the very top so I get the offer instantly.
- Section labels ("Perfect Combo," "Summer Uniform," "Mix & Match") at least try to give the email some narrative.
- "See what's new in store" near the bottom is a nice secondary beat for people who shop physical.

## What's weak

- Half the email is KIDS. I'm 24 with no children — why am I getting routed into a kids-fashion edit again? Zara did this exact thing to me last week.
- This is the FIFTH "up to 50% off" send in a week. I'm numb to the number now — ASOS rotates angles, H&M just repeats itself.
- "Tops & shorts for all" — the "for all" is doing the heavy lifting for "we're including kids in this" and it's misleading on open.
- The CTAs are tiny outline buttons that disappear into the white space — Shein's CTAs are loud red blocks you can't miss.
- No model that looks like me front and center. The adult hero is a faceless torso shot, then it's kids, kids, kids.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment me out of kids content.** I've never bought kidswear from H&M. Send me the adult tops & shorts edit and route the kids' version to actual parents — Zara is already losing this exact battle with me.
- 2. **Stop running "up to 50% off" five times a week.** Rotate the angle: "barrel-leg shorts under $20," "the strapless top everyone's wearing," "weekend fits restocked." ASOS is doing this better with category edits.
- 3. **Make the hero a real outfit, not a category headline.** Show the striped top styled with the shorts, on a model who looks 20-something. Less "Perfect Combo" label, more actual combo.
- 4. **Bigger, filled CTAs.** The outline buttons read as decorative on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The strapless top from your FYP — 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Barrel shorts + cropped tops, under $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two pieces, one outfit, picked for your weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The trend tops Shein copied — back in stock.`
