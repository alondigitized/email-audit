# This week’s selection
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "This week's selection" and I scroll down expecting literally anything for me and... it's Zara doing the exact same thing they did on August 1st with "This week at Zara" — an entire email of menswear. Street Fits, New Collection, Everyday Rhythm, Zara Athleticz — every single section is guys in cargo pants, bermudas, polo shirts, and sneakers on a street-style shoot. Not one women's piece, not one female model, nothing.
- I'm not mad about the photography — the styling is genuinely cool, very off-duty downtown-guy energy, and the "ZARA" wordmark hero is a strong visual moment. But that's exactly the problem: they clearly put effort into this and aimed it at a customer that isn't me. This is the second time in eight days I've gotten a men's-only send from this list, so either their segmentation is broken or I've somehow been bucketed into the wrong audience.
- Definitely not opening this twice, definitely not clicking, definitely not forwarding — unless it's to roast it in the group chat as "Zara emailed me a whole men's lookbook again." Straight to archived.

## What's working

- The street-style photography is genuinely good — real NYC locations, not studio backdrops, which is the trend-authentic feel Zara usually nails.
- The oversized "ZARA" wordmark hero is a clean, confident brand moment.
- Clear section dividers (Street Fits, New Collection, Everyday Rhythm, Zara Athleticz) keep the long email scannable even though none of it applies to me.
- Individual category labels (Knitwear, Jeans, Bags, Footwear) are simple and legible without zooming.

## What's weak

- Every single model across every single section is male — this is a men's-only email, full stop, and I'm a woman on this list.
- Zero product pricing or offer anywhere — no %, no $, nothing to actually click "shop" for.
- No CTA buttons at all, just underlined category text links — feels more like a lookbook PDF than a shoppable email.
- This is a near-repeat of Zara's August 1 "This week at Zara" men's editorial — same miss, second time in a week.
- "Zara Athleticz" section is just more menswear, no crossover athleisure for women even as a token nod.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix segmentation immediately — I should never get two all-men's sends in eight days when I've only ever browsed women's. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Add an actual offer or at least a "Shop New In" CTA button — right now there's nothing to click toward, just category labels.
- 3. If this is meant to be gender-neutral "trend report" content, say so in the subject/preheader so I know not to expect womenswear — right now it reads like a targeting bug, not an intentional editorial choice.
- 4. Compare to H&M's "refresh your denim stack with baggy, barrel, loose-fit" send — that's specific trend language tied to a real offer. Zara's giving me a moodboard with no hook.
