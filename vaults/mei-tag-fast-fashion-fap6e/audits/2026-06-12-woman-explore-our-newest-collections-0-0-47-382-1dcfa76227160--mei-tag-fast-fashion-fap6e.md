### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] Viewport meta uses semicolons as delimiters — syntax error:**
```html

```
The `content` attribute requires comma-separated tokens, not semicolons. iOS Safari and some Android browsers may silently ignore the entire directive, falling back to desktop rendering on mobile.

`maximum-scale=1.0; user-scalable=no` both present. Combined, they prevent pinch-to-zoom entirely. This fails WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is flagged by accessibility auditors on iOS 10+.
