# In focus: The new classics
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is a whole vibe shift from the last five H&M emails I've gotten — no "UP TO 50% OFF SITEWIDE" screaming at me, just quiet trench coats and "THE NEW CLASSICS." Points for range. But also... this is giving me nothing. There's no percentage off, no price cut, just full-price basics ($59.99 jacket, $29.99 polo) styled like it's a Toteme lookbook. That's a big swing for H&M, a brand I know as fast-fashion trend-chasing, not quiet-luxury tailoring.
- The "CELEBRATE A/W '26" section further down actually has the more interesting hook — a launch event on September 18th with a gift card and free A/W beauty bundle — but it's buried under an entire scroll of product tiles I have to get through first. I'm not opening this to shop crisp shirts and washed denim; I open H&M for the TikTok-viral piece that dropped an hour ago. This feels like it's trying to be Massimo Dutti (which, lol, Zara already pulled that exact "quiet basics" move on me and I scored it a 4).
- Would I click? Maybe scroll past the models, land on "LEARN MORE" for the event out of curiosity, but I'm not buying a $60 bubble-hem jacket off this email. Not forwarding it — my group chat wants the trend piece, not "refined tailoring."

## What's working

- The hero model's styling (trench, tank, straight denim) is genuinely clean and shot well, not the usual cluttered flat-lay grid.
- "THE NEW CLASSICS" as a section header is at least a clear theme, unlike H&M's last few emails that just kept relabeling the same 50%-off sale.
- The A/W '26 launch event with a store list and a free gift card threshold ($150-300 = free beauty bundle + $15 gift card) is a real, specific offer once you scroll to it.
- Model diversity is fine — curly-haired brunette model reads relatable, not overly polished editorial.

## What's weak

- Zero discount anywhere in the main product grid — every single item is full price, which is wild for a brand whose last five sends to me were sitewide 50% off.
- The good offer (event + gift card) is at the very bottom after like 8 product tiles I have to scroll through on my phone — total buried lede.
- Nothing here screams trend-first; it reads like H&M trying to cosplay a premium basics brand, which isn't why I'm subscribed to H&M over Zara or ASOS.
- No urgency, no "new drop" energy — just static product tiles with "New Arrival" tags that don't tell me if this ships before the trend dies or two months late.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the September 18th event and gift card — that's the only thing with real urgency and a dollar value, don't bury it under a denim blouse I don't want.
- 2. Add an actual price signal to the hero — even "new season, prices from $19.99" would help since there's no discount to lean on.
- 3. Cut the product grid in half — 8 near-identical items before the CTA is a lot of thumb-scrolling for someone comparing three other retailer tabs on a Saturday.
- 4. Tie this to a trend hook, not "timeless" — "timeless" is the opposite of why I open H&M over Massimo Dutti or Everlane.
