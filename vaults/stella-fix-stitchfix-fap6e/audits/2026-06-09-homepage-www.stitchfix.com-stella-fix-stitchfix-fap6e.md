# Homepage snapshot · www.stitchfix.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure new-user acquisition page — it's basically shouting "sign up!" at me, a person who already has a Stitch Fix account and gets monthly Fixes. The $30 off first Fix offer is lovely branding, the page renders cleanly, and the "your stylist does the work" messaging is literally why I'm a customer — but there is zero acknowledgment that I've been here before. No "welcome back," no Fix preview, no shortcut to manage my upcoming box. I feel like the bouncer doesn't recognize me.

## What's working

- **"Your Stylist sends pieces in your size, style and budget. Easy."** — this lands hard. Three words capture exactly why I outsource my wardrobe. Perfect pitch.
- **The white card overlay on the hero** — clean visual hierarchy on mobile. I know exactly where to look first. No confusion.
- **The teal announcement banner at top** — the $30 + waived styling fee double-dip is compelling framing for new users. Clear benefit stack.
- **The lifestyle imagery** — the collage of women in relaxed, real-world clothes (not runway poses) feels age-appropriate and relatable. The brunette in the floral crop top looks like she could be me on a Saturday.

## What's weak

- **Entire page assumes I've never heard of Stitch Fix.** I have a monthly subscription. There's a tiny "Sign In" link in the top right corner and that's it. No "Welcome back," no "Your next Fix," no link to my queue. I have to go hunting.
- **The $30 off banner is noise for me.** As a returning customer, seeing "Limited time First Fix offer!" in a dismissable banner feels almost taunting — I can't use this, so why is it my entire above-the-fold experience?
- **No Freestyle access visible.** I know Freestyle exists (I'm skeptical of it, but it's there). Not a single link to browse or shop independently — it's all quiz-funnel, all the time.
- **"Limited time" without a deadline** — this is the weakest kind of urgency. If it ends tomorrow, tell me. Otherwise it's filler copy.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Sign In to manage your Fix" CTA alongside the quiz button** — or better yet, detect returning users and swap the hero to "Your next Fix is ready" with a preview link. I'd tap that immediately.
- **Swap "Take the quiz" for a split-entry module**: "New here? Take the quiz" / "Already a member? Log in" — it takes two seconds to build and stops alienating your best customers.
- **Surface one trust signal above the fold** — a single "4.5 stars · 3M+ Fixes sent" line under the CTA costs nothing and converts skeptics.
- **Replace "Limited time" with an actual date or a count**: "Offer ends June 15" or "3 days left." If the offer is real, commit to the deadline.
