# Homepage snapshot · www.stitchfix.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight-up acquisition page — the whole thing is tuned to drag in someone who has never heard of Stitch Fix, and I've been a member for years. The jeans-fit hook is relatable, the $20-off offer is clear, but I'm standing here holding my phone thinking "I already took the quiz, where do I manage my next fix?" Feels like I walked into the wrong entrance of a store I already have a loyalty card for.

## What's working

- **The headline** — "Why can't I find jeans that fit?" is a real pain point. Even as a returning member who already solved this with Stitch Fix, it still lands. It's the kind of question I sent three texts about before I signed up.
- **The CTA button** — teal, full-width-ish, centered. "Take the quiz, get $20 off" is specific and benefit-forward. No "Learn More" or "Discover." Whoever wrote this knows what they're doing.
- **The banner** — the teal "First Fix offer!" strip is immediately visible and doesn't fight the hero. It reinforces the same offer without creating noise.
- **Model diversity** — the trio below the card (white woman, Black woman, man) keeps the page from feeling like it's for one narrow slice of the population.
- **Mobile layout** — this is clean on a phone. Typography is legible, nothing is cramped.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-member real estate** — "Sign In" is the only acknowledgment I exist. No "Schedule your Fix," no "Your next Fix is ready," no rewards balance. For a subscription brand, that's a miss.
- **The quiz CTA doesn't apply to me** — "Take the quiz, get $20 off" actively excludes loyal paying customers. Every month I open this app I get shown a door I already walked through.
- **No navigation categories visible** — hamburger menu only. I can't tap into Women's, Freestyle, or my account in one tap. I have to hunt.
- **The Freestyle store goes unmentioned** — even though I'm skeptical of it, at least showing it exists would tell me things have evolved. Right now it looks like the same pitch from three years ago.
- **No social proof or trust signals** — no star ratings, no "X million members" badge, no press logo. If I were a first-timer, I'd want some validation. If I'm a returner, I still want to feel like I made a smart choice.

## Recommendations

- **Add a returning-member hero variant** — even a simple split: if there's a session cookie or "Sign In" state detected, swap the hero to "Your next Fix is one tap away" with a "Schedule Now" button. This is a subscription product; treat customers like subscribers.
- **Pin a category strip below the hero** — Women / Men / Kids / Freestyle — so first-timers and returners both have a one-tap path without touching the hamburger.
- **Add one trust signal to the hero card** — something like "Styled for 5M+ people" or a 4.7-star badge pulls in skeptics and reassures fence-sitters without cluttering the layout.
- **Make the Freestyle store a secondary CTA** — a small text link or chip below the main CTA ("Or shop Freestyle, no subscription needed") acknowledges the product has expanded and gives browsing-mode users an exit ramp that still converts.
