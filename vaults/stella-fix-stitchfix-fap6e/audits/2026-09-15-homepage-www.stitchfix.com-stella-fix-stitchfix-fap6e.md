# Homepage snapshot · www.stitchfix.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a brand actively fishing for new sign-ups, not talking to someone who already has three Fixes sitting in her closet history. The whole above-the-fold is built around "Take the quiz, get $20 off" — great if I were new, but I've been doing this monthly for a while and there's no acknowledgment of that anywhere I can see.

## What's working

- The "$20 off + waived styling fee" banner up top — I'll always register a concrete number like that even if it's not for me.
- "Take the quiz, get $20 off" is a real, tappable, unambiguous button — no guessing what it does.
- "No subscription required / Free shipping + returns" under the CTA answers my two biggest objections before I even ask them.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — nothing fighting for my eye, no popups shoving themselves in my face.

## What's weak

- Everything here — the banner, the hero copy ("Stop shopping + get styled"), the quiz CTA — is aimed at someone who has never used Stitch Fix. I'm a repeat customer; there's no "Welcome back" or account-aware messaging.
- No "Sign In" prominence — it's just plain text next to Help, buried next to a much louder green quiz button.
- "All Style, No Risk" is a fine tagline but it's not telling me anything new about this month's Fix or what's changed since my last box.
- No mention of the algorithm/style preview I actually use to flag returns early — that's the exact feature that keeps me subscribed, and it's invisible here.

## Recommendations

- Swap the top banner for account-aware copy when a logged-in-feeling visitor lands — something like "Your next Fix ships in 3 days" instead of a first-timer discount.
- Add a "Sign In" button styled with the same visual weight as "Take your style quiz" so returning customers aren't an afterthought.
- Surface the style preview / rate-your-picks feature above the fold — that's the tool I actually use every month to cut down returns, and right now it's nowhere to be seen.
- Replace one of the two hero photos with a quick "here's what shipped to members like you this week" carousel to make it feel current, not evergreen.
