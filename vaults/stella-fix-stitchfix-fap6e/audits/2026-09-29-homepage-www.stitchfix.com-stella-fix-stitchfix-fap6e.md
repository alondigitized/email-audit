# Homepage snapshot · www.stitchfix.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a clean, friendly styling-service brand, and it's selling one thing: take the quiz and get 20% off your first Fix. As a monthly Fix client I'm not the person this page is talking to. It's clearly built to bring in first-timers, and nothing above the fold acknowledges that I already have a stylist relationship. The "Stop shopping" line does speak to my "someone else pick my outfits" brain.

## What's working

- **"All Style, No Risk" card.** The subhead "Stop shopping + get styled" is my whole value prop. I want someone else to decide, and it says so in five words.
- **"No subscription required / Free shipping + returns."** These sit right under the button and answer my two biggest fears (getting locked in, and paying to send things back). That matters since I keep 2 and return 3.
- **Teal banner "20% off + waived styling fee."** The waived styling fee is a concrete number, not vague "savings." The banner is also dismissible with the X, so it doesn't nag.
- **Two-photo hero.** The real-street shots of denim, knit and a structured bag look like outfits I'd actually wear. They don't look like runway looks.
- **Nav.** Women / Men / Kids on the left, Help, Gift cards and Sign In on the right. I can find my way around one-handed on my phone.

## What's weak

- **Nothing for existing clients.** I'm a monthly Fix customer, and the only nod to me is a small "Sign In." There's no "See your next Fix" or "Preview your box" link, and that preview is the feature I use to flag returns early.
- **"Limited time" with no date.** It reads as generic urgency, and I don't trust it without a deadline.
- **"See details" hides the terms.** I'd want to know right away whether the 20% applies to the whole Fix or only to what I keep.
- **No sign of the new Freestyle store.** I'm skeptical of it and would want to see how it fits in, but it isn't in the nav or the hero. That may be intentional, but it leaves me wondering whether it's a separate thing.
- **The models don't look like my life.** There's nothing that says busy parent, work wardrobe or "I have 10 minutes."
- **No proof.** I don't see any review counts, star ratings or "X million Fixes shipped" above the fold.

## Recommendations

- Add a returning-client strip under the banner when a session is recognized: "Welcome back, Stella. Your next Fix ships [date]. Preview it." Swap the "Take the quiz" button for "See my Fix."
- Put the deadline on the banner, e.g. "Ends Oct 6," and show the terms in a one-line tooltip, e.g. "20% off your first Fix, applied to items you keep."
- Add a slim trust row under the hero button, e.g. "4.6★ from 100K+ reviews · Free returns · 30-second quiz," so the risk-free claim has evidence.
- Swap one of the two hero photos for a lifestyle shot of a woman in workwear, with a caption like "Styled in 5 minutes between meetings."
