# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Vrbo opens with exactly the four words I need to hear — "Entire place, just for you" — and that headline does real work for someone like me who won't even glance at a shared listing. The whole experience feels clean and intentional, like a brand that knows its lane. Right now it's pushing a friction-less search flow up front, with a hint of deals starting to appear below the fold.

## What's working

- **"Entire place, just for you"** — This headline is the exact framing that keeps me loyal to Vrbo. It's not "find a place to stay," it's a statement of brand promise. I feel seen.
- **Pre-populated dates** — Dates are already set to a real summer week (Jul 18–25). Saves me a tap and signals the site knows I'm probably planning a trip.
- **Search widget layout** — Location, dates, guests, Search. Nothing extra, nothing buried. I'm a high-tech user but I also don't want cognitive load when I'm trip-planning on my phone after the kids go to bed.
- **"My trips" in nav** — Exactly where I expect it. Lets me pick up where I left off without signing in first.
- **"Premier Host" badge** — Partially visible below fold, but it's a meaningful trust signal when I'm handing over thousands of dollars.

## What's weak

- **"2 guests" default** — I'm booking for a multi-generational group — my parents, my husband, my kids. Two guests is almost insulting as a default. It makes the search feel like it's built for couples, not families.
- **Hero imagery** — The dark aerial/forest background is atmospheric but generic. Nothing about it signals "spacious family home with a great kitchen" or "room for Grandma." Airbnb uses lifestyle imagery; Vrbo should be countering with warm, sprawling-home shots.
- **Zero urgency or offer above the fold** — I'm a planner, but even I respond to "deals available for your dates." The deals section is below the fold and barely peeking in. That information should be higher.
- **Dates mismatch** — Search widget says Jul 18–25 but the emerging deals section shows "Jul 24–26." That inconsistency is small but noticeable and slightly erodes trust.

## Recommendations

- **Raise the default guest count to 6** — or make it a more prominent field that prompts "how many guests?" A multi-gen traveler instantly feels catered to when the site assumes groups, not couples.
- **Swap the dark aerial for a bright interior lifestyle shot** — a family around a big dinner table, a pool with three generations, or a kitchen big enough to actually cook a holiday meal. That image is the whole pitch.
- **Pull one deal callout into the hero area** — something like "Summer deals from $X/night for your dates" right inside the search widget or immediately below it, above the fold.
- **Add a "Whole homes only" filter chip** below the search bar — even if it's technically the default, making it visible and tappable reinforces the brand promise and distinguishes Vrbo from Airbnb at a glance.
