# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get Vrbo — I got a bot wall. The entire viewport is a CAPTCHA challenge: "Show us your human side… We can't tell if you're a human or a bot," with a slider puzzle I have to complete before seeing anything. As someone who plans multi-gen trips four to five months out and books whole homes, I'm not fighting a slider on my phone before I even get to search for a place to sleep. This is a complete dead end for any real homepage evaluation.

## What's working

- The Vrbo logo is legible and correctly rendered at the top — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- The "Submit feedback" link is present if I'm truly stuck, which is a minimal lifeline.

## What's weak

- The entire above-the-fold experience is a bot gate — no search, no imagery, no copy about whole-home rentals, nothing that speaks to why I came here.
- The slider CAPTCHA is a mobile-unfriendly friction point; on a phone, these are notoriously finicky.
- The error explanation ("You are browsing and clicking at a speed much faster than expected of a human being") is condescending and unhelpful to a real user.
- The tracking ID displayed at the bottom (`55fdc4a4-61ad-bbcf-6776-5c49e335f76f`) is meaningless jargon to any real visitor — it's developer-facing noise pushed into the user-facing screen.
- Zero accommodation for legitimate users who hit false positives — no "I'm human, let me in without the puzzle" alternative.

## Recommendations

- **Tune bot-detection thresholds** so typical mobile browsing patterns (fast scrolling, automated testing tools) don't trigger the wall for the majority of real users — false positive rate here seems high if it's catching a standard page load.
- **If a challenge is unavoidable, inject brand value into it** — show a background of a gorgeous lake house, keep the Vrbo value prop visible even while the user solves the puzzle.
- **Add a one-tap "Send me a verification link" escape hatch** for users who can't solve the slider on mobile — keep them in the funnel rather than sending them straight to Airbnb.
- **Remove the raw session ID from the user-facing screen** — that's noise that makes the experience feel broken and technical, not trustworthy.
