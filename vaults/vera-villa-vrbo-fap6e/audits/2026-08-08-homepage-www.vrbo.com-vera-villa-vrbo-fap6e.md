# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This looks exactly like the Vrbo I expect — a clean, confident "the whole house is yours" pitch with real early-booking savings right up front. It's not trying to be a boutique-hotel booking site, it's leaning hard into whole-home rentals, which is the entire reason I'm here instead of on a hotel site. Right now it's pushing early-booking discounts on specific properties, which is smart since I'm the type who books months ahead.

## What's working

- **"Entire place, just for you"** — that headline is basically written for me. It's the "no strangers under one roof" promise in five words.
- **Early booking deals rail** — actual struck-through prices ($227 → $207, $237 → $200) with "Early booking $X off" tags. That's exactly the kind of concrete offer that gets me to book 4-5 months out.
- **"All fees included" checkmark** on every card — no hidden resort-fee surprise later, which matters when I'm pricing out a place for eight people.
- **Ratings visible on every card** ("10 Exceptional," "9.6 Exceptional") — quick trust check without a click.
- **Search bar pre-filled with dates and guest count** — low friction, I can just adjust and go.

## What's weak

- **Hero image is a solo woman, not a family** — I'm booking for grandparents, kids, the whole crew. A pool shot with one person doesn't say "multi-generational trip" to me.
- **No loyalty or rewards element anywhere visible** — I'm a repeat shopper and there's nothing acknowledging that (no "welcome back," no member pricing).
- **Getaway property photos so far are pretty generic** (empty living rooms, a patio) — nothing yet screams "built for big groups."
- **No bedroom/guest-capacity info visible on the cards** — for a multi-gen trip, "sleeps 10" matters more to me than the star rating.

## Recommendations

- Swap the solo hero shot for a lifestyle photo with a multi-gen group — grandparents, parents, kids together at a rental, poolside or around a dinner table.
- Add a small "sleeps X / X bedrooms" chip to the deal cards so I can filter for group size at a glance without tapping in.
- Surface a loyalty or "welcome back" element near the search bar if I'm a returning visitor — even a simple "Your recent searches" strip would do it.
- Add a visible deadline to the early-booking deals ("Book by Aug 31 to lock this rate") — right now the discount feels open-ended, which makes it less urgent.
