# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- Vrbo's homepage leads with the one thing I need to hear — "Entire place, just for you" — and that headline does a lot of heavy lifting for me. The brand feels clean, purposeful, and family-forward without being sugary about it. Right now it's pushing the search-first experience above everything else, which makes sense, but it leaves a lot of value on the table for a returning planner like me who already knows what I want.

## What's working

- **"Entire place, just for you" headline** — That's the whole reason I'm on Vrbo and not Airbnb. No shared spaces, no strangers. It's the first thing I read and it lands immediately.
- **Pre-filled search form** — Dates (Jun 13–20) and guests (2) are already populated, which reduces friction. I just need to drop in a destination.
- **Blue "Search" CTA** — High contrast, impossible to miss. One tap and I'm in the funnel.
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — No carousel chaos, no pop-ups, no forced modals. I can think.
- **"Getaways near you" section starting below fold** — Tells me the homepage isn't just a blank search bar; there's browsing on deck.

## What's weak

- **Guests defaults to 2** — I'm booking for multi-generational trips: me, my partner, my kids, possibly my parents. A 2-guest default undersells Vrbo's core strength and makes the whole thing feel like it's aimed at couples weekend trips, not the family reunion crowd I actually represent.
- **No family/group framing anywhere** — "Just for you" (singular) misses the "whole family under one roof" angle that is Vrbo's actual moat over Airbnb. Even one word — "yours alone," "your whole crew" — would sharpen this.
- **No trust signals above fold** — No review counts, no "4.8 stars across 2M properties," no secure-booking badge. For a first visit or a returner who hasn't booked in a year, there's nothing to anchor confidence.
- **Truncated nav item ("Op...")** — Whatever that button is, it's cut off. Feels like a broken element even if it isn't.
- **No loyalty hook** — I've booked through Vrbo before. There's zero acknowledgment that I'm a returning customer. "My trips" is in the nav but there's no personalized welcome or member benefit callout.
- **Hero image is underworked** — A dark, moody wooded background is atmospheric but tells me nothing about the type of home I could rent. A bright, spacious vacation home with a big dining table and room for 10 would speak directly to my use case.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "2 guests" default for a group-size nudge** — Add a secondary line under the Guests field like "Traveling with family? Add everyone." or default to a higher suggested count for vacation rental context. Groups are Vrbo's bread and butter.
- **Punch up the hero copy for the family framing** — Change "just for you" to "just for your crew" or add a sub-headline like "The whole home. No strangers." That one shift re-anchors Vrbo's competitive edge against Airbnb.
- **Add a social-proof strip above the fold** — A single line: "4.8★ · 2M+ whole-home rentals worldwide" or a trust badge row costs nothing and converts returners and skeptics alike.
- **Fix or label the truncated "Op..." button** — If that's an app-download prompt, label it. If it's an offer, show the offer. A cut-off button reads as broken.
