# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- I opened Vrbo to start thinking about our family's summer trip and instead got stopped dead by a CAPTCHA wall before I could even see the homepage. The page says "Show us your human side… We can't tell if you're a human or a bot." — that's the entire above-the-fold experience. I have no idea what Vrbo is pushing right now because I can't get past the front door.

## What's working

- **Vrbo logo** at the top is clean and recognizable — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- **"Submit feedback" link** is present, which gives me an off-ramp if I'm stuck — small but decent UX.
- **Clear explanation copy** ("You are browsing and clicking at a speed much faster than expected of a human being") tells me *why* I'm blocked, which is less maddening than a silent block.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is behind a CAPTCHA wall** — I can't see a hero, a search bar, property imagery, any deals, or any of the "whole home" family framing I came for.
- **"Show us your human side"** is trying to be clever but it reads as condescending; I'm a real person trying to plan a real trip.
- **Slider CAPTCHA UX on mobile** is notoriously finicky — if this doesn't work on the first swipe I'm closing the tab.
- **No escape hatch** to reach the homepage without completing verification — no "continue browsing" or lightweight alternative.
- **IP-level network block (IP 75.140.9.0)** mentioned in the copy — this isn't my IP, it's a shared network flag, and I have zero context or recourse.

## Recommendations

- **Tune bot-detection thresholds** so that a real mobile user opening the app for the first time isn't immediately flagged — the friction cost here is an entire lost booking session.
- **Add a "Continue to homepage" light option** (e.g., email verification or simple math challenge) so blocked users aren't at a dead end.
- **If a CAPTCHA is unavoidable, brand it harder** — swap the generic slider widget for something that at least shows a vacation image in the background so I'm still in vacation-booking headspace while I solve it.
- **Surface a static fallback hero** (logo + search bar + "Search whole homes") below the verification widget so I can at least see what I'm unlocking while I complete the challenge.
