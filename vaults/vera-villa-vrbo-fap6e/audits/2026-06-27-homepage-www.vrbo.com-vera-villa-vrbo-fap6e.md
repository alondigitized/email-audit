# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- What I got is not Vrbo — it's a bot wall. The page Vrbo served me is a CAPTCHA challenge: "Show us your human side... We can't tell if you're a human or a bot." Before I ever see a hero image, a search bar, or a single property listing, I'm being treated like a suspect. For a platform that markets itself on trust and family warmth, this is a brutal first impression. There's nothing to evaluate as a homepage experience because there is no homepage — just a slider puzzle and an apology.

## What's working

- **Transparency about the block:** The page at least explains why verification is required ("browsing at a speed not expected of a human being") instead of showing a cryptic error.
- **Feedback link:** "Having problems accessing the site? Submit feedback." gives me a real recovery path — that's more than most bot walls offer.
- **Clean layout:** No clutter, readable font, logically structured — the verification challenge itself is unambiguous.

## What's weak

- **Zero homepage content delivered:** I'm Vera Villa. I'm on my phone, ready to start looking at 5-bedroom lake houses for spring break. Instead I get a CAPTCHA. I will not slide that slider — I'll go to Airbnb.
- **"Show us your human side" is tone-deaf for a family travel brand:** That headline might be fine for a security company. For a vacation rental platform whose entire identity is warmth and family trust, it lands like a accusation.
- **The IP explanation ("75.140.9.0") is shown to the user:** Surfacing the technical diagnostic to a consumer is confusing and slightly alarming — it reads like a security warning, not a friendly speed bump.
- **No visible Vrbo brand identity below the logo:** One logo, one challenge form, nothing else. If I'd never heard of Vrbo, I'd have no idea what this company sells.
- **No bypass or sign-in option:** A logged-in returning user should never see this. There's no "sign in to skip this" or SSO shortcut visible.

## Recommendations

- **Detect and skip CAPTCHA for signed-in sessions:** I've booked with Vrbo before. If I'm cookied or logged in, this wall should never fire — solve it server-side.
- **If a challenge is unavoidable, inject brand copy around it:** Replace the sterile white page with a Vrbo-branded background (a gorgeous whole-home exterior, a family on a deck) and softer copy — "Just a quick check before we find your next getaway." Keep the challenge small and centered; don't let it be the whole screen.
- **Add a "Sign in instead" link:** A returning booker can authenticate to prove human status without a puzzle. Visible, prominent, above the slider.
- **Remove the raw IP address from consumer-facing copy:** Move the technical detail to a "learn more" expand — don't lead with it.
