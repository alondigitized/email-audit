# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened Vrbo on my phone looking for a 5-bedroom lake house for spring break and the first thing I see is a bot wall. A slider CAPTCHA — "Show us your human side... We can't tell if you're a human or a bot." I haven't even seen the homepage yet. As someone who has booked on this platform multiple times, this is a cold slap before hello.

## What's working

- The Vrbo wordmark at the top is clean and recognizable — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- The "Submit feedback" link at the bottom gives me an escape valve if I'm stuck.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is hidden behind a bot challenge.** I'm a 41-year-old Gen X woman on my personal iPhone trying to plan a family trip — I am the customer. This is a catastrophic first impression.
- **The error copy is cold and accusatory.** "We can't tell if you're a human or a bot" reads like I've done something wrong. No apology, no brand warmth, no reassurance.
- **The explanation list is anxiety-inducing.** It calls out browser behavior, JavaScript issues, and even a shared IP (75.140.9.0) — this is IT-department language on a consumer travel app.
- **Zero brand personality.** Vrbo's whole angle is family-first, whole-home warmth. None of that shows here.
- **No estimated wait or fallback path.** If the slider doesn't work (which happens on mobile), I have no clear next step besides "Submit feedback."

## Recommendations

- **Replace the accusatory copy with an apologetic, branded tone.** Something like "Just a quick check — we want to make sure our best homes are reserved for real families." Keeps the spirit, loses the hostility.
- **Trigger the CAPTCHA only on suspicious signals, not broad IP ranges.** A returning customer on a residential mobile IP should never see this cold.
- **Add a "Sign in to continue" bypass.** Logged-in users should skip the bot wall entirely — this would also serve as a loyalty hook.
- **If the block must exist, make it fast.** A one-tap invisible reCAPTCHA or Face ID auth would clear this in under a second on iPhone.
