# Homepage snapshot · www.vrbo.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Vrbo doing exactly what it's supposed to do — leading with "Entire place, just for you," which is the whole reason I'm here and not on Airbnb. The homepage feels purposeful and uncluttered, with a search widget front and center that treats me like someone who already knows what I want. Summer timing is baked in, but it's not doing much to earn my loyalty or push me past a search to an actual booking.

## What's working

- **"Entire place, just for you"** — This is the line that keeps me from defaulting to Airbnb. It's the brand promise, and it's the first thing I read.
- **Pre-populated dates (Jul 25–Aug 1)** — Shows Vrbo is thinking in terms of real booking windows, not just inspiring browsing. That's how I shop.
- **"Getaways near you / Showing deals for: Jul 31–Aug 2"** — The deals rail starting below the fold signals there's inventory worth seeing, and "near you" suggests localization is working.
- **"Premier Host" badge** — The moment I see a trust signal on a property card, I know vetting is happening. For a multi-gen trip where I'm responsible for the whole family's experience, that badge matters.
- **Clean, minimal nav** — "My trips" and "Sign in" are exactly what I need fast. No cluttered menus, no app-download nag.

## What's weak

- **2 guests defaulted** — I'm booking for a multi-generational group of 8–12. A 2-guest default makes me feel like the product is thinking about couples, not families. I have to fix this before I even start.
- **No family-size framing** — The hero copy is good, but there's no secondary message about "sleeps 10," "whole house," or "bring the whole crew." The differentiation stops at the tagline.
- **No personalization above the fold** — I'm clearly returning (Vrbo knows my trips), but I get the same cold-start homepage as a first-timer. No "Welcome back, pick up where you left off."
- **No visible price range or deal anchor** — "Showing deals" is vague. What does a deal mean? $50 off? 20% off? I need a reason to believe.
- **Hero imagery is dark and abstract** — Trees and shadow read more as moody nature than "here's the beautiful house your family will remember forever." I want to see a porch, a big table, a pool — something that sells the multi-gen experience.

## Recommendations

- **Increase the guest default or add a "Group trip" quick-select** — A one-tap option for "6+ guests" or "multi-family" would shortcut straight to the inventory that actually serves me and signals Vrbo understands my trip type.
- **Swap the dark forest hero for a lifestyle shot of a large family home** — Show a wraparound porch with mismatched generations: grandparents in chairs, kids on a lawn, adults with drinks. That's the dream I'm booking.
- **Add a returning-user personalization layer** — "You searched Outer Banks last spring — deals are live now" above the search widget would stop me cold and keep me on the page.
- **Anchor the deals section with a concrete number** — "Save up to 25% on stays this week" beats "Showing deals" every time. Give me a reason to tap.
