# Don’t be a Scrooge!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a co-branded send for Paramount's *Ebenezer* (Johnny Depp, in theatres Nov 13) that sells mattresses with a Scrooge pun on top. The creative is fun and on-theme. The movie art takes the first screen, and the actual sleep offer doesn't show up until the second block. The hero is a movie poster with the line "Bad sleep can be scary." A subscriber scanning it sees an ad for a film, not a mattress sale.
- The offers are thin and scattered. The free Humbug Hush Cap needs a mattress purchase and only applies to "select mattresses." The only price offer, "Save 25% on bundles*", is a small badge on the second image. Three red buttons each go to a different place (cap, bundles, accessories), so the email has no single action. The footer fine print carries the real conditions. Compare it with the clean 10/26 "Free mattress removal*" send, which had one idea, or the 10/22 "Pay for a Queen, sleep on a King." send, which had a clear hook.
- Judgement: a charming brand collab that dilutes the commercial message. It will get opens from the novelty and the Casper name, but it should convert worse than Casper's single-offer sends.

## What's working

- The "Don't wake up Scrooge-y" and "De-scrooge" copy ties sleep to the film in a witty, on-voice way.
- The Humbug Hush Cap photo is distinctive and shareable, and the navy and snowflake palette is cohesive.
- The red pill buttons stand out on navy and are easy to tap.
- The section structure is simple: image, headline, short copy, button.
- The nav and the footer links (mattress quiz, find a store, SMS signup) are intact, and I see no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The first screen is a Johnny Depp movie poster with no Casper product or price.
- "Save 25% on bundles*" is a small rotated badge on the bundles image, and the asterisk hides the terms. The offer should be the headline.
- There are three competing CTAs ("Shop now," "Shop bundles," "Shop accessories") and no deadline in the body. The Nov 3 expiry appears only in the fine print.
- "Shop now" is generic, and the free cap is gated behind an unspecified "select mattresses."
- The Ebenezer logo and "Only in theatres Nov 13" appear twice, which spends space on Paramount's message.
- The "Gift of a Better You" and "Sleepily Ever After" sections are generic, with no product, price or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the commercial offer above the movie art. Lead with "Save 25% on bundles + free Hush Cap" and the code or deadline, and put the Ebenezer art below as a supporting element.
- 2. Cut to one primary CTA, such as "Shop bundles," and make the other two links secondary text.
- 3. Show the actual end date ("Ends Nov 3") in the body and spell out which mattresses qualify for the cap.
- 4. Add a product, price and review stars in the bundles block, and drop the generic accessories block.
- 5. Show the Ebenezer logo once.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Humbug cap + 25% off bundles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bad sleep makes a Scrooge. Fix it by 11/3`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Get a limited-edition Hush Cap free with select mattresses, plus 25% off bundles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Offer ends Nov 3. Bundles, pillows and sheets to build a bed with no wrong side.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** The QA check reports 17,148 of 19,116 characters (about 90%) as URLs. That points to long tracking-wrapped links in the text/plain alternative, which can hurt spam scoring and readability.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** Only 12 material links were probed and 20 were skipped, so link health is verified for the top-ranked links only. The QA output doesn't list any broken links among those 12.
- **Open pixel:** `https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/CgxqCN…/open`, 1×1, `display:none`. It is a standard Exponea/Bloomreach tracker. The base64 path appears to decode to a `frequency_segment` value of "Core Sleepers Test". That suggests the send came from a test segment, and the identifier is embedded in the URL. This is low-confidence because the string is truncated.
- The click-tracking URLs are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't comment on redirect chains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The "missing alt text" QA warning looks like a false positive.** The flagged element is the open pixel, and the source shows `alt=""`. That is correct for a decorative or tracking image. The check should ignore 1×1 tracking pixels.
- **Preheader.** There are two hidden blocks. The first is `<span style="…display:none!important;…mso-hide:all">` with the preheader text. The second is a `<div style="display:none;max-height:0px;overflow:hidden;">` that holds the `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;` padding characters. The padding div lacks `mso-hide:all`, which is minor and only matters for Outlook desktop.
- Web fonts are loaded via `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is correct, with fallback to system fonts in Outlook and in clients that strip `<link>`.
- The responsive CSS uses the `@media (max-width:620px)` breakpoint with `!important` overrides, and the layout is table-based (`class="nl-container"`, `role="presentation"` expected). I found no rendering defects in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated and unverified.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found [WARN].** The QA note says AgentMail may not relay it. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check this against the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown from this data. The From domain is `e.casper.com`, a subdomain, so alignment should be checked in the raw message.
- The footer, unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows no destination `href`s, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be checked. The QA output doesn't report landing-page mismatches among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Capture the full raw message headers (not the relay's rewrite) and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment.
2. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to verify the unsubscribe link, the postal address, and UTM tagging on the CTA links.
3. Reduce URL weight in the plain-text part, for example by using shorter tracking links or a cleaner text/plain version.
