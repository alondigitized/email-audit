# Save 35% when you go hybrid.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- The headline story this email tells is not the one the subject line promises. Casper's subject reads "Save **35%** when you go hybrid" but the email hero opens with "Save up to **30%** on hybrid and max mattresses." That's a discount mismatch the subscriber catches in under two seconds, and it reads as either a production error or a bait-and-switch — both are bad. The June 17 send was anchored on 35% sitewide and the June 14 send was also 35% on bundles; stepping back to 30% for a mattress-specific push while the subject still promises 35% is the kind of inconsistency that quietly hemorrhages trust with your most engaged openers.
- The structural bones are genuinely good. The three-module education funnel — "What is a hybrid? → Should you go hybrid? → Which hybrid is best for you?" — is exactly the kind of buying-guide content that earns a click rather than begging for one. The side-by-side comparison of Dream, Dream Max, and Snow Max with feature dots is clean and self-qualifying. The Father's Day angle ("Not sure what dad needs?") is correctly timed — June 20 is the eve of Father's Day — but it's buried below the fold and pointed at gift cards rather than letting the mattress itself be the gift.
- The Hulu / *Not Suitable for Work* cross-promo appears again at the bottom, 11 days after its debut in the June 9 send. With 7 emails in 11 days, that block has now appeared to every engaged subscriber at least twice. It's becoming furniture.

## What's working

- The hybrid education funnel (What is it → Why hybrid → Which model) is genuinely useful content that earns scroll depth rather than demanding it.
- Three-model comparison grid lets readers self-select between Dream, Dream Max, and Snow Max with specific attribute signals (cooling, support, best-for) without leaving the email.
- "Complete your summer setup" category row (Mattresses, Pillows, Bundles, Accessories) with consistent 30% badges creates clean upsell momentum below the fold.
- Subject line is tidy — 31 characters, no spam signals, mobile-safe.
- Father's Day timing is right — eve-of send is the correct trigger moment for a last-minute gift angle.

## What's weak

- Subject says "35%" but the hero says "30%" — the most damaging single observable error in this send; it will read as bait-and-switch to any subscriber who notices.
- No urgency mechanism anywhere in the body — no end date, no "offer expires Sunday," no countdown signal — which kills the reason to click today rather than tomorrow.
- Father's Day section is gift-card-only and buried; the hybrid mattress itself is the obvious Father's Day gift and the email never makes that case.
- Hulu *Not Suitable for Work* cross-promo is a repeat from the June 9 send — creative fatigue is visible at this cadence.
- Seven emails in eleven days; even a well-executed send is landing in a fatigued inbox at this frequency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reconcile the discount immediately** — 35% or 30%, pick one and use it in both the subject and the hero. If 35% only applies to bundles, say "Save up to 35% on bundles" and run a separate subject. Mixing the numbers between subject and body is the single highest-impact fix.
- 2. **Add an expiry date** — "Offer ends Sunday, June 22" or "Father's Day pricing ends tonight" gives the reader a concrete reason to act. Without it, "save 30%" is just a standing offer.
- 3. **Lead the Father's Day angle** — reframe the hero as "Give Dad the best sleep of his life — 30% off hybrid mattresses." Drop the gift card section entirely or move it to the footer; a mattress is a better Father's Day story than a gift card.
- 4. **Cut the Hulu cross-promo** — it's been in two sends. Either dedicate a standalone email to it or retire it from the modular footer rotation.
- 5. **Trim the module count** — education funnel + comparison grid + summer setup + Father's Day + Hulu is five distinct asks. Pick three and strip the rest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Give Dad 30% off — hybrid mattresses, built to last`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dream, Dream Max, or Snow Max? Save 30% on the one that fits.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Find your hybrid match before Father's Day — offer ends June 22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Responsive springs + cooling foam. 30% off through Sunday.`
