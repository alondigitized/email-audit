# ⏰ Final hours: Our doorbuster ends tonight.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent last-call Black Friday send. It has a clear hero: a lime "Black Friday Sale" pill, a giant "Save up to 35% on everything.*" on navy, and a white "Shop now" button. The "Ends Tonight" badge sits on the furniture block, not the hero. The email's own urgency is weaker than its subject's.
- It also repeats the 11/24 "Black Friday just got better" and 11/27 "Black Friday is officially here" sends almost block for block. The 35% hero, the product grid and the lime closer are all the same. It's the third Black Friday-day-or-near send in four days, and the "final hours" framing is the only new thing. The page is long, with ten or more CTAs, and the deadline is buried in one badge and the fine print.
- Verdict: solid execution, low novelty, and weak urgency inside the body. It will convert people who were already going to buy, and it does little for anyone fatigued by the run since 11/20.

## What's working

- The 35% hero is large and sits first, so the eye lands on the offer immediately.
- The navy-and-lime palette matches earlier sends, which keeps the brand consistent.
- Each product tile pairs a specific discount with a named product ("The One: Save 30%", "Dream Max: Save 35%").
- The "Ends Tonight" badge on the furniture image gives a credible deadline.
- The lime closing block repeats the offer with a "Shop mattresses" button, which works as a clean last chance to click.

## What's weak

- The hero has no deadline. "Ends Tonight" appears only on the furniture block, so the subject's urgency isn't echoed where the eye lands first.
- The hero says "up to 35% on everything" with an asterisk, and the fine print is tiny and dense at the bottom. That is mildly dishonest-feeling.
- "Shop now" is generic, and the repeated "Shop Bundles" buttons are indistinguishable from each other.
- The furniture block outranks the mattress best-sellers, even though mattresses are the core product.
- The email is very long, with five product tiles plus a text-signup block, so the offer is diluted below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Ends Tonight" countdown into the hero pill, for example "Ends tonight: Black Friday Sale". Match the subject's promise in the first screen.
- 2. Lead with one hero product, like the Dream Max at 35%, and cut the grid to three tiles to shorten the scroll.
- 3. Replace "Shop now" with "Shop mattresses, ends tonight" and give each bundle button a distinct label.
- 4. Put the exclusions in plain words under the hero, not only in the footer.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11/24 and 11/27 sends so subscribers see something new.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 35% off mattresses`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Dream Max at 35% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Black Friday pricing disappears at midnight. Shop mattresses, bundles and pillows.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best sale of the year ends tonight. Free returns on every bed.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is well-formed, with a bilingual-safe `lang="en"`, an MSO conditional block, `role="presentation"` tables and a responsive `@media (max-width:620px)` block. The QA run raised no hard failures. Its four warnings are mostly header-capture gaps from the relay, and the source was truncated, so link, UTM and footer checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 23 skipped. The QA data doesn't list probe results, so I can't confirm any broken, redirecting or 404 links.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 18,665 of 20,780 characters (about 90%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This points to long wrapped tracking links in the text/plain alternative. That raises spam-filter risk and makes the plain-text version hard to read.
- **Open pixel.** `<img width="1" height="1" alt="" src="https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/…/open" style="display:none;">` is a Bloomreach/Exponea tracker.
  - The base64 token appears to contain internal segment labels, such as "frequency_segment" and "Core Sleepers Test", which suggests test or segment naming is leaking into a public URL. This is my reading of a visible fragment of the token, not a confirmed decode.
  - It is a low-severity hygiene issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The QA "Image missing alt text" warning is a false positive.** The open pixel has `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative or tracking image. No action is needed.
- **Preheader.** The `<span>` ("Save up to 35% site-wide.*") followed by the padding `<div>` of `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;` characters is a standard pattern.
  - The padding `<div>` lacks the `mso-hide:all` and `visibility:hidden` fallbacks that the `<span>` has. This is minor.
- **Title.** `<title>` carries the emoji entity and a trailing space. This is cosmetic.
- **Not verifiable.** Gmail's ~102KB clipping threshold, image alt text on content images and dark-mode handling can't be checked because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]` or `*|MERGE|*` appear in the visible source.
