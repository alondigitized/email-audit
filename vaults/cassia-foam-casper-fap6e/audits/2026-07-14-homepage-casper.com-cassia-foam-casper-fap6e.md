# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- I know Casper — I *own* Casper — and opening this on my phone feels like trying to window-shop through a frosted glass door. The brand is clearly mid–summer sale mode, pushing a "Stay Cool Sale" at 20% off, but a full-screen cookie consent wall ate the entire homepage before I could see anything they actually wanted me to buy. Casper still reads like the trustworthy sleep company I remember, but whoever let a modal swallow the hero on mobile made my first second feel like homework.

## What's working

- **Promo strip at the very top** — "Save 20% on cooling mattresses" with carousel arrows is the first thing I read, and as a returning buyer who watches for markdowns, that hooks my attention immediately.
- **Category shortcuts row** — Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz: exactly the four things I actually use. Adding a pillow to hit free shipping is my move, and that row makes it one tap away.
- **"STAY COOL SALE" hero headline** — what little I can see is punchy and seasonally correct. Red star accents feel like a real sale event, not a placeholder.
- **Casper logo + nav** is clean and instantly recognizable — I know I'm in the right place.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is a brick wall** — it covers roughly 70% of the viewport. Nothing below the partial hero is visible. On mobile this is brutal; I have to make a legal decision before I see a single product.
- **No CTA button visible** — the primary shop button is completely hidden under the modal. The most important click on the page is inaccessible.
- **No returning-customer signal** — I've owned a Casper for years and this page treats me like a stranger. No "shop your next upgrade," no trial reminder, no member pricing callout.
- **No lifestyle imagery visible** — the hero is typographic only (from what I can see). No bed, no bedroom, no person sleeping cool — for a sleep brand, that's a miss.
- **No free-shipping threshold or trial callout** — the 100-night trial is the reason people trust Casper; it's not visible anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Compress the cookie consent to a slim bottom bar** — a one-line "We use cookies — Accept / Manage" strip preserves 90% of the viewport and is fully compliant. The current full-screen wall is killing first impressions and blocking the actual sale.
- **Add a lifestyle hero image behind the sale headline** — a cool, airy bedroom shot (white linens, morning light, someone in their 30s) would make the cooling narrative land emotionally instead of just typographically.
- **Surface the 100-night trial in the promo strip** — swap one of the carousel slides for "100-night trial — sleep on it before you decide" to reinforce the core trust signal that converts returning browsers like me.
- **Add a returning-customer nudge in the nav or hero sub-copy** — something like "Already own an Original? Upgrade to the Nova Hybrid — 20% off this week" would make me feel recognized and accelerate the upsell.
