# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Casper is running what looks like a Memorial Day sale — I can see a "Save up to 30% on mattresses" banner at the top — but I can barely confirm that because a massive cookie consent wall has taken over the entire screen. As a returning customer who knows the brand well, this is a frustrating first impression: I know what I came for (checking if there's a pillow deal I can stack with a mattress discount), and I immediately hit a wall.

## What's working

- **Top announcement banner** — "Save up to 30% on mattresses" is the first thing I read. That's a solid hook. As someone who waits for holiday markdowns, this confirms I came at the right time.
- **Category nav strip** — Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz are all icon-labeled and visible above the modal. I can tap Pillows in one touch if I dismiss the modal.
- **Brand recognition** — The Casper wordmark is clean and instantly readable. No confusion about where I am.
- **Memorial Day campaign hint** — I can see the word "Memorial" partially behind the modal, suggesting the page is season-appropriate.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is the entire page** — Three big buttons (Reject / Accept / Your Privacy Choices) eat up 80% of the viewport. I haven't even seen the hero and I'm already doing compliance admin.
- **No returning-customer signal anywhere** — I own the Original. There's nothing acknowledging me as an existing customer — no account icon with a name, no "Welcome back" in the banner.
- **Offer detail is missing** — "Save up to 30%" is vague. Is that on the Original I might upgrade from? My current model? New SKUs only? I can't tell from what's visible.
- **The quiz CTA is premature friction** — "Take the Quiz" is in the top nav before I've seen any product. First-timers need that; I don't. It competes for real estate that could be a loyalty hook.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a bottom strip** — a slim bar with Accept/Reject lets the hero breathe and kills the wall effect. This is table-stakes mobile UX in 2026.
- **Add a returning-customer layer to the announcement banner** — "You've got 30% off — shop your next upgrade" with a login prompt would convert existing Casper owners far better than a generic percentage.
- **Make the 30% offer specific in the banner** — "Save up to 30% on mattresses — Original from $X" so I can immediately qualify whether this is worth my time.
- **Swap "Take the Quiz" in the icon nav for "My Account"** — returning buyers don't need the quiz; they need to check order history, trial status, or saved cart.
