# Last chance: Extra 5% ends tonight*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper closing out the exact "Labor Day Subscriber Exclusive / extra 5% / code L-2026" arc it's been running since 2026-08-23 — same badge, same green highlighter-strike on "extra 5%," same hero bed shot, same two-button stack ("Shop subscriber exclusive" / "Shop mattresses"). The one real addition is the "ENDS TONIGHT" circle badge overlapping the bed image, which finally gives this campaign the urgency it's been missing all week. It's the right move for a last-chance send, and the layout below the fold — Maxes/Hybrids/Foams broken into clean tiered save-badges, then bundles/pillows/furniture/bedding — is legitimately well organized and easy to scan.
- The problem is this is the fourth-plus time this exact creative has hit the inbox in eight days, and the subject line undersells the one thing that should be doing the work: true deadline urgency. "Last chance: Extra 5% ends tonight*" is fine but the asterisk immediately signals fine print, which cuts against the urgency it's trying to create. Everything about this email works mechanically — clear CTA, honest layering, no render bugs — but fatigue is now the biggest risk, not execution.
- **Judgement: solid execution of a stale campaign — the urgency badge is a good save, but this needs to be the last time this exact hero runs.**

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" circular badge over the hero image adds real, visible urgency that prior sends in this arc lacked.
- Clear two-tier CTA hierarchy (orange "Shop subscriber exclusive" over navy "Shop mattresses") makes the primary action unambiguous.
- Product tiering (Maxes/Hybrids/Foams) with consistent green highlighter-style save badges makes scanning easy and mirrors a proven bundling pattern.
- Bottom grid (bundles, pillows, furniture, bedding) gives subscribers a reason to click even if mattresses aren't the need right now.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- This is the same hero bed photo, badge shape, and green-highlight "extra 5%" treatment as 2026-08-23 and 2026-08-24 — visually indistinguishable at a glance from two prior sends this week.
- The offer structure hasn't escalated or changed at all across five sends; only the deadline framing is new, which reads as running out the clock rather than a fresh reason to act.
- The asterisk on "extra 5%" in the subject line undercuts urgency before the email is even opened.
- "*Savings based on comp. value" sits directly under the hero — the fine print is small but visible right at the point of decision, slightly softening the offer's credibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire this exact hero creative after tonight — five near-identical sends in eight days is the top complaint a real subscriber would have; the next markdown push needs a new photo/badge treatment even if the offer logic repeats.
- 2. Move urgency into the subject line itself instead of just the hero image — "Ends tonight" is buried after a colon; lead with the clock.
- 3. Drop or soften the asterisk in the subject — it's a credibility tax on your strongest lever (deadline pressure).
- 4. Add a countdown timer or explicit time ("Ends 11:59pm ET") near the "ENDS TONIGHT" badge — a vague badge with no clock reads as manufactured urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — your extra 5% expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack code L-2026 with up to 35% off — gone after tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your subscriber discount closes at midnight ET.`
