# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- The first thing I see when I hit casper.com is not a hero or a mattress or a Memorial Day deal — it's a full-screen cookie consent wall. The brand behind the modal clearly has a seasonal push going (I can glimpse "Memorial" in the buried hero and "Save up to 35% on mattresses" in the slim banner at the top), but the actual homepage is functionally inaccessible until I make a choice about tracking cookies. Casper is a premium sleep brand trying to run a holiday sale, and its first impression is a boilerplate legal screen.

## What's working

- **Top promo banner ("Save up to 35% on mattresses")** — even at 14px in a slim strip, it tells me exactly why to care before I dismiss the modal
- **Category quick-nav (Mattresses / Pillows / Furniture / Take the Quiz)** — visible above the modal fold, one-tap access to everything I'd shop
- **Memorial Day timing** — partial hero text and the seasonal sale match the calendar; Casper is clearly running the right campaign for the week
- **No dark-pattern cookie design in terms of parity** — "Reject" and "Accept" are given equal button weight; no deceptive "Accept All" giant / "Reject" tiny micro-copy

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal as first experience** — this is the worst possible first screen for a returning customer. I already own the Original. I want to see the deal, not consent to analytics.
- **Hero completely obscured** — I cannot see the Memorial Day creative, lifestyle imagery, or the actual headline. All of that brand investment is invisible.
- **Zero conversion surface visible** — there is literally no way to add to cart, start a quiz, or even tap into a product page without first clearing the modal. The funnel is paused.
- **No trust signals** — 100-night trial, free returns, review counts — none visible. For a first-time buyer, this cold open would create zero confidence.
- **No urgency / deadline copy** — the promo banner says "save up to 35%" but there's no "ends Monday" or "Memorial Day only" language to make me move fast.

## Recommendations

- **Move cookie consent to a bottom bar or corner tray** — full-screen interstitials on mobile are a conversion killer; a bottom-pinned GDPR bar keeps the hero fully visible and gets better acceptance rates anyway
- **Add "Ends Monday" or a countdown to the promo banner** — I buy at the holiday markdown; a deadline would pull me in before I close the tab
- **Surface the 100-night trial in the promo strip** — "Save up to 35% + 100-night trial" in one line answers the two biggest objections before I even see the hero
- **Add a "Welcome back" or account-login nudge in the header** — I own the Original; a tiny "Hi, Cassia" or rewards-balance indicator would tell me this brand knows me and might convert me on a pillow upsell
