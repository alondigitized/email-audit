# Black Friday is officially ON!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud Black Friday kickoff, and the hero does its job. "Save 30% on mattresses and up to 35% on everything else.*" in lime on navy is the first thing the eye lands on. A single "Shop now" pill sits directly below it. The family photo adds warmth, and the offer is easy to understand in about two seconds.
- Below the hero, the email becomes a four-block category stack: foam, hybrid + max, adjustable bases and bundles. Each block has the same headline, photo and pill button. It is tidy, but it is repetitive. Three of the four blocks say "Save 30%", so the percentage stops meaning anything. It also reads as a catalog, not a reason to act today. There is no code and no deadline in the body. The only date is the expiry in the fine print, 12/2/26. That is nearly a month away, so the "ON" in the subject carries no urgency.
- Judgement: competent and on-season, but generic. Since 10/21 Casper has sent size-up, free-removal, book-month and Scrooge hooks, each with a clear idea. Here the idea is "it's Black Friday", which every competitor says too. It is a decent anchor send, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable. A huge "30%" in lime on navy, with "up to 35%" as the second tier.
- There is one clear primary CTA. A white "Shop now" pill contrasts strongly against the navy.
- The category blocks have consistent structure, so the email scans quickly. Foam, hybrid, bases and bundles each get a photo and a button.
- The "Black Friday Sale" script badge and the starry navy background signal the season at once and fit Casper's night-sky brand.
- The SMS signup block and the mattress quiz give lower-intent readers a next step.

## What's weak

- Three of the four blocks repeat "Save 30%", and the hero says it too. The repetition dilutes the offer, and only bundles at 35% stands out.
- There is no urgency or deadline in the body. No countdown, no "ends Monday", and no code is visible. The terms are buried in grey fine print at the bottom.
- The fine print is very small and low-contrast. The asterisk, the "Comp. Value" basis and the exclusions are barely legible, which weakens how honest the offer feels.
- There are no prices, reviews, star ratings or product names beyond "The One" and "Cloud One". Nothing helps a reader choose between the four blocks.
- The CTA labels are generic. "Shop now" appears twice, and the hero button doesn't say what it links to.
- The navy section is long and the dark photo blocks feel uniform. Nothing breaks the rhythm.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a real price anchor. Show something like "Queen from $XXX" in the hero so the 30% becomes a dollar figure, and add one credible deadline such as "Ends Dec 2" or "Early access ends Sunday".
- 2. Cut the repetition. Merge foam and hybrid into one "30% off mattresses" block, and give bundles a bigger "35% off" treatment as the real upgrade.
- 3. Add social proof, such as a rating line or a "best seller" tag under each product, and name the hero product.
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific: "Shop 30% off mattresses", "Shop bundles".
- 5. Raise the contrast and size of the terms, and put a one-line summary of the exclusions near the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off mattresses starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Casper Black Friday: 30% off, 35% on bundles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 35% off bundles, bases and bedding. Sale ends Dec 2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The One, Cloud One and more. Early prices are live.`
