# Save up to 20% on mattresses during our Back to School Sale.*
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's second "Back to School Sale" send in six days — the first ran July 22 at 25% off, this one leads with "Save up to 20% on mattresses," a quieter number wrapped in the same seasonal wrapper. The creative execution itself is Casper's cleanest structural template: a lifestyle hero, a three-way mattress-type selector (Foam/Hybrid/Max), then three parallel product blocks each following the identical pattern (image → pick badge → highlighted name → description → Save 20% badge → Shop button). It's disciplined, on-brand, and easy to render on any device.
- But the strategy underneath is shakier than the design. Six days after "Back to School sale" (7/22, 7/10) and five days after "Last chance to save up to 25%" (7/21, 9/10), reusing the exact same campaign name with a lower discount reads as a walked-back promise to anyone who was paying attention — the "last chance" wasn't actually last, and the "up to 25%" quietly became "up to 20%." Layer on the "*Pricing based on Comp. Value" disclaimer sitting directly under the hero offer, and the honesty signal takes a real hit.
- Verdict: strong template execution, weak campaign hygiene. This is a 6-7/10 send that would be an 8 if it either kept the 25% number or gave the reused title a fresh enough hook to not feel like a rerun.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("Save up to 20%") is unmissable — large type, red-circled percentage, zero scroll required.
- "The ABCs of better sleep start here" is a genuinely clever, restrained back-to-school pun — doesn't overplay the theme.
- The Foam/Hybrid/Max selector at the top gives shoppers a fast self-sort before they hit three near-identical product blocks below.
- Each product section is internally consistent — badge, highlighted product name, description, "Save 20%" tag, CTA — making the email easy to skim in a repeatable pattern.
- Clean render throughout: no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breaks across the full length of the email.

## What's weak

- The 20% headline is a step down from the 25% offered in the July 21 "Last Chance" and July 22 "Back to School" sends — repeat subscribers will notice the discount shrank, not grew.
- "*Pricing based on Comp. Value" sits directly beneath the hero offer, undercutting the "Save up to 20%" claim before the reader even scrolls.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the hero or subject — after two "last chance"-style sends, this one has no clock at all, which flattens the case for opening now versus later.
- REVIEWS is a nav link only — no actual star ratings, testimonials, or review counts appear anywhere in the body to back the "best-selling" and "sweet spot" claims.
- Reusing the identical "Back to School Sale" title from six days ago, at a lower percentage, reads as recycled creative rather than a fresh beat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't reuse the "Back to School Sale" name at a lower discount than the prior send — either hold at 25% or give this a distinct campaign hook (e.g., a specific mattress-type spotlight) so it doesn't read as a quiet downgrade of last week's offer.
- 2. Move or shrink the "Pricing based on Comp. Value" disclaimer so it's not directly undercutting the hero claim — or better, state the offer in real dollar terms to sidestep the comp-value question entirely.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero copy — a specific end date gives shoppers a reason to act in this email instead of waiting for the next one.
- 4. Pull one or two star ratings or a review snippet into the "best-selling mattress" block — the claim is made three times (best-selling, sweet spot, coolest) with zero supporting proof visible.
- 5. Tighten the subject line under 50 characters for mobile inboxes — the current one runs long and gets truncated on most phone screens.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 20% off mattresses — Back to School`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your dorm/new room deserves better sleep. 20% off*`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Foam, Hybrid, or Max — find your match and save.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping, 100 nights to try it, 20% off today.`
