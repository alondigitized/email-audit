# Wellness starts with sleep.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper pivoting away from the discount-blast format into an editorial "National Wellness Month" content play — four "wellness tips" each mapped to a product, with discount badges (20%, 10%, 35%, 35%, 10%) tucked into small circular stickers rather than a bold hero number. Compared to the last six sends in this run — all of which led with a specific percentage in the hero ("Save 30%," "Save up to 35%," "Save 25%") — this is the first to lead with soft lifestyle copy ("Better days begin the night before") and bury the offer info entirely off the hero.
- The structure is actually well organized once you scroll: each tip has a clear rationale, a product image, a badge, and a "Shop now" link. But that's the problem — this send asks for four scrolls of engagement before a single click, with no unifying number or urgency anywhere. It reads more like a blog post than a marketing email.
- Given the brand has been running an aggressive Back-to-School/Labor-Day discount cadence, this is a tonal reset that will likely underperform on clicks relative to the last several sends, even though the content itself is more thoughtful and less repetitive.

## What's working

- Clean, warm hero image (person waking up in bed) pairs well with the "Better days begin the night before" line — good mood-setting.
- The four-tip structure (schedule, routine, environment, recharge) is a genuinely different creative idea vs. the repetitive percent-off hammering in prior sends.
- Each product block is self-contained: image, badge, name, one-line benefit, and its own "Shop now" — no ambiguity about what's being sold.
- Second CTA at the bottom ("Refresh your sleep routine") gives a clean exit point after the scroll instead of leaving the reader hanging.
- No visible render bugs — images, badges, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer — the discount badges (20%, 10%, 35%, 35%, 10%) are scattered and inconsistent, unlike the "Save up to 35%" clarity of 2026-08-18.
- Primary hero CTA is just "Shop now" with zero offer context, a step down in clarity from prior sends' "Shop Labor Day Sale" / "Shop mattresses" buttons.
- Six products across four sections is a lot of scrolling for mobile before a reader reaches anything decisive — no product is prioritized as *the* offer.
- Subject "Wellness starts with sleep." is generic and could be from any sleep or wellness brand — it doesn't hint at a sale at all, breaking cadence with the last six subjects, which were all specific.
- Urgency is entirely absent — no deadline, no "ends tonight," a sharp contrast to the Aug 17 send's "ENDS TONIGHT!" energy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one hero number.** Pick the best offer (35% on bundles) and put it in the hero copy and button, e.g., "Save up to 35%" over "Better days begin the night before" — content and commerce don't have to be mutually exclusive, and Casper's own history shows leading with a number converts.
- 2. **Cut to 2 tips, not 4.** Six products is fatigue-inducing; trim to the two strongest sellers (Dream Bundle, Original Pillow) and let the wellness framing feel like a curated pick, not a full catalog dump.
- 3. **Make the bottom CTA carry the number too** — "Refresh your sleep routine" should become "Shop up to 35% off" to give it commercial teeth.
- 4. **Unify the badges** — five different percentages (20/10/35/35/10) reads chaotic; consider a consistent "Save up to 35%" framing applied per relevant item.
- 5. **Add a light urgency line** in the hero subcopy — even "This week only" would help, given Casper has trained subscribers to expect deadlines.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save up to 35% — plus real ways to sleep better`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your wellness month: 35% off + a better bedtime`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Four simple upgrades for deeper, more consistent sleep — up to 35% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New wellness picks, from Glow Light to Dream Bundle — save while you reset.`
