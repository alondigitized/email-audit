# Save 35% on bundles + an extra 5%*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Casper takes the "Labor Day Subscriber Exclusive" angle from yesterday's 2026-08-23 send and doubles down on it, now stacked with a much bigger headline: "Save 35% on Bundles + an Extra 5% for Subscribers." That's a jump from yesterday's flat "extra 5%" ask, and it reads as a real escalation in the Labor Day arc — bundles get top billing, then the layout methodically walks through mattress, adjustable, bedding, and build-your-own bundles, each with its own hero image, repeated "Save 35% + an extra 5%" badge, and dedicated CTA.
- The hero is clean and unambiguous — big bold number, hand-drawn green circle around "35%," one orange CTA button, code L-2026 visible without hunting. But the email is punishingly long: four near-identical bundle blocks followed by a second "subscriber benefits extend sitewide" section with four more product tiles. By the time a reader scrolls past "Build your own bundles," the same badge has appeared five times and the eye has nothing new to latch onto. This is a strong hero buried under repetitive execution — worth opening, but it overstays its welcome and dilutes the very urgency it opens with.

## What's working

- Hero is scroll-free and instantly clear: "Save 35% on Bundles + an Extra 5%," code L-2026, one orange "Shop Labor Day Sale" button.
- Hand-drawn green circle draws the eye directly to the 35% number — same device Casper used successfully in the 2026-08-18 and 2026-08-20 sends.
- Each bundle section pairs a real lifestyle/product photo with a specific contents list ("Mattress + Foundation + Mattress protector"), which adds concreteness other Casper sends have lacked.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- The "Save 35% + an extra 5%" badge repeats five times (hero + 4 bundle sections) plus a sixth "extra 5%" reminder in the subscriber-benefits block — numbing rather than reinforcing.
- Email is extremely long (4 bundle sections + 4 more sitewide product tiles) with no visual break or hierarchy shift — it reads as a full category page rather than a promo email.
- "Build your own bundles" carries a second badge ("Most customizable bundle") competing with the savings badge for the same visual space, muddying which claim matters more.
- Fine print at the bottom ("*Savings based on comparable individually priced items...") isn't visible in-hero, but no in-body clarification of what "bundle" actually requires (e.g., minimum items) appears before the CTAs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half: keep the hero + one merged "shop bundles" grid (4 thumbnails, 1 CTA) instead of 4 full sections with repeated badges — this is the single biggest lift, since scroll fatigue is currently the email's main weakness.
- 2. Vary the badge language across sections (e.g., "Bundle & Save," "Ends Monnight," "Subscriber Price") instead of reprinting "Save 35% + an extra 5%" five times — repetition reads as filler, not reinforcement.
- 3. Drop or shrink the second "subscriber benefits extend sitewide" block into a single small footer CTA ("Shop sitewide savings →") rather than four more product tiles — it's redundant after four bundle sections.
- 4. Add a visible deadline (Casper used "ENDS TONIGHT!" effectively on 2026-08-17) — this send has no urgency cue beyond "Labor Day," which is vaguer than the codebase's own past best performers.
