# Build your coolest bed and save up to 35%
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's seventh email in nine days, and the brand has finally found a fresh angle. Instead of yet another "35% off mattresses" hero, this one builds a "summer bed, step by step" narrative — start cool (mattress), amp the airflow (sheets/duvet), stay chill (pillows), then bundle. It's the most editorially considered creative I've seen from them this month and a genuine relief after the doorbuster repetition.
- The 35%/35% banner still anchors the top, but the offer is now in service of a thematic story (hot-sleeper summer prep) rather than the raw discount being the entire point. The three-step structure gives each product category its own row and CTA, which means a non-mattress shopper finally has a reason to keep scrolling.
- That said, the offer remains the same one I've been seeing all week — only the wrapper changed. For a subscriber on the seventh near-identical promo, the question is whether "summer cooling bed" is enough to differentiate from "Memorial Day mattress sale." I think it is, marginally. Best Casper send of the run.

## What's working

- Editorial "step 1 / 2 / 3" structure gives each category (mattress, bedding, pillows) its own CTA — finally segmenting beyond mattresses.
- Hot-sleeper angle ("coolest bed," "stay chill") is a real seasonal hook, not just "Memorial Day" wallpaper.
- Bundle row at the bottom ("Bundle up and cool down") is a clean cross-sell with two clear pack options.
- Specific products named (Snow Max, Hyperlite Sheet Set, Lightweight Down Air Duvet, Foam Snow Pillow) instead of generic category links.
- New customer trust strip at the bottom ("See what's new at Casper") with category nav is a nice secondary entry point.

## What's weak

- Same 35%/35% offer as the last six emails — no new economic reason to act today vs. yesterday.
- Top banner repeats "Up to 35%" twice with nearly identical sub-copy on each side; feels redundant.
- Hero copy "Your summer bed, step by step" is cute but soft — no offer, no urgency, no product noun above the fold.
- No visible Memorial Day deadline anywhere I can see, even though that's been the campaign frame all week.
- "Shop now" repeated as the CTA on every block — verbs are all identical when each step could have its own action ("Shop cooling mattresses," "Shop bedding," "Shop pillows").

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. "Ends Monday" or "Memorial Day ends 5/26" — a credible end date is the single thing missing across this whole campaign.
- 2. Differentiate CTAs by step. "Shop cooling mattresses" → "Shop breathable bedding" → "Shop cooling pillows." Repeating "Shop now" four times kills the editorial conceit.
- 3. Lead with a hot-sleeper claim, not "step by step." Subject and hero should both promise the outcome (sleep cooler) — that's the buyer benefit; the "step by step" is just the structure.
- 4. Trim the duplicate "Up to 35%" banner at the very top. One side could carry the deadline; the other the discount.
- 5. Add one social-proof line per row — review count or temperature stat for Snow Max, thread count for Hyperlite, etc.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sleep cooler this summer — up to 35% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hot sleeper? Build your coolest bed (35% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Memorial Day ends Monday — cooling mattress, sheets & pillows.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Step 1: a Snow mattress. Step 2: breathable bedding. Save up to 35%.`
