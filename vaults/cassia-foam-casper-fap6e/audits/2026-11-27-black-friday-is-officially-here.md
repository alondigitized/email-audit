# Black Friday is officially here.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's Black Friday launch email, and the offer is clear: a lime "Black Friday is here" pill, a giant "35%" on navy, "Casper's best sleep savings of the year are officially live," and a "Shop Black Friday" button. The hero is easy to read and the eye lands on the number first. It is the same 35% offer as the 11/24 send ("Black Friday just got better: Save up to 35% on mattresses*"), so the news is that the sale is live, not that the deal got better.
- Below the hero the email turns into a long catalog: Max mattresses at 35%, furniture at 20%, The One and Cloud One at 30%, hybrids at 30%, bundles at 35%, adjustable bases at 30%, pillows at 20% and "everything else" at 10%. Every block has its own percentage and its own button. That is a lot of discount tiers for one scroll. The 35% headline only applies to Max mattresses and bundles, and the asterisk fine print is tiny. By the sixth block the hierarchy has flattened.
- My judgment: solid and on-season, but a routine send. It has no code, no deadline and no reason to act today, after a month of near-identical Black Friday emails. The 11/23 "Ends tonight! Extra 5% off your wishlist" send gave the reader a reason to act. This one doesn't.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: a giant "35%" in lime on navy, with one "Shop Black Friday" button.
- The brand look is consistent: navy with stars, the lime pill and the Casper wordmark match the earlier Black Friday sends.
- Each product block pairs a heading with the % off, a short line of copy and a button, so it is easy to scan.
- The "Save 30%" badges on the product photos reinforce the offer visually.
- The footer is useful: a text-signup prompt, a mattress quiz and a store finder.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline, code or urgency anywhere. "Officially here" has no end date.
- Eight different discount tiers (35, 20, 30, 30, 35, 30, 20, 10) dilute the headline. The reader can't tell what 35% actually covers.
- The "up to 35% on everything*" claim conflicts with the lower tiers below it, and the asterisk fine print is barely legible.
- The email is very long, with a vertical stack of near-identical blocks. Most readers won't reach pillows or bundles.
- Every button says "Shop [category]", with no verb that adds a benefit or a reason to click now.
- Nothing suggests loyalty or subscriber status, even though the 11/21 send used "Subscriber Exclusive."

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Through Cyber Monday" or "Ends 11/30 at midnight," and a countdown if possible. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four blocks: Max mattresses, the One and Cloud One, bundles, and a "More deals" link. Group the 20–30% items under one block.
- 3. Make the claim precise: "35% off Max mattresses and bundles, up to 30% off the rest." That removes the mismatch with the lower tiers.
- 4. Use benefit-led CTAs ("Get 35% off Max," "Build my bundle") instead of "Shop Hybrids."
- 5. Bring back a code or subscriber-only angle so there is an incentive beyond the public sale price, as in the 11/21 and 11/23 sends.
- 6. Add review stars or a sleep-trial line near the Max block as social proof.
- **Subject Alt A:** `35% off Casper's best mattress, live now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday pricing starts now: up to 35% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Max mattresses and bundles at 35% off. Sale ends Cyber Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our lowest prices of the year on mattresses, pillows and more.`
