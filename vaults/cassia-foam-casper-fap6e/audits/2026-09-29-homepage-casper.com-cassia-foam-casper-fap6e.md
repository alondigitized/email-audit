# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- Casper looks like a polished, sleep-first DTC brand, and right now it's pushing "September Savings": 20% off select mattresses and 25% off bundles. I know this brand from the podcast ads, but the first screen here is mostly sale banners and a cookie wall. I don't see the 100-night trial anywhere, and that's the thing I actually tracked.

## What's working

- **"Fall asleep faster this Fall."** The pun is cute without being cringey, and it fits my podcast-ad-listener brain.
- **Pillow perk, "Plus, save 10% on 2+ pillows."** This is my exact habit of adding a pillow to hit a threshold, so it lands.
- **Nav bar (Mattresses, Bundles, Pillows, Furniture, Bedding).** It's clean and I can get to my category in one tap. Red "Sale" stands out.
- **Warm bedroom hero photo.** The lamp glow and the person in bed feel cozy and on-topic for sleep.
- **"SEPTEMBER SAVINGS" pill.** It tells me at a glance which sale this is.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal.** It covers the hero copy and CTA ("We mak…", "Tak…" are cut off), so I can't tell what the hero is asking me to do.
- **"UNLOCK OFFER" tab and "Chat" bubble.** Stacked with the modal, the bottom of the screen feels crowded and pushy.
- **Sparkle over "select."** It makes the headline read "sel*ct" and looks sloppy.
- **Two competing promos, 20% vs. 25%.** I don't know whether I should shop mattresses or bundles, and both use the asterisk fine print.
- **No trial, free shipping, or returns messaging.** The 100-night trial is Casper's core story and I don't see it above the fold.
- **"Refer a friend" in the top bar.** It's a weird spot for it, and it isn't a reward for returning shoppers.

## Recommendations

- Put "100-night trial · free returns" in the top announcement bar in place of the "Save up to 20% on Mattresses" carousel. The carousel repeats the banner directly beneath it.
- Move the sparkle graphic away from the headline text, or drop it.
- Delay the "Unlock offer" tab until after scroll or a first-visit timer, and shrink the cookie banner to a slim bottom bar so it stops covering the hero.
- Add a real end date ("Ends Sept 30") to the September Savings pill. Also put the free-shipping threshold next to the pillow deal, so a pillow add-on to hit free shipping is easy.
