# Save up to 35% on comfort for every forecast.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Casper swaps the flat markdown headline for a seasonal hook — "Made for in-between weather" — and it's a nicer angle than the last two weeks of "35% off" banner spam, but the offer is still the same Labor Day Doorbuster 35% on Max mattresses that's been running since 2026-08-23. The hero mattress-cross-section diagram (Cozy Cover / Breathe Flex Foam / Zoned Support) is genuinely good product education, and the warm lifestyle photo of a couple in bed reads as autumn-appropriate rather than another studio product shot.
- The email is long — mattress hero, then a full ingredient-diagram section, then Dream/Dream Max cards, then a full bedding/bundle section — which is a lot of scroll for a single campaign send, and it dilutes the headline 35% offer across five different sub-offers (30%, 35%, 35%, 10%, 10%). It's a competent, on-brand seasonal creative, but it's trying to be both a mattress email and a bedding catalog at once.

## What's working

- Seasonal "in-between weather" framing feels timely for September and differentiates from the flat discount headlines of prior sends.
- The mattress cross-section diagram with labeled callouts (Cozy Cover, Breathe Flex Foam, Zoned Support) is clear, informative, and rare to see done this cleanly in an email.
- Lifestyle photography (couple relaxing in bed, warm cardigan) matches the "cooler mornings" copy and reads as genuine seasonal content, not stock filler.
- Clear visual hierarchy at the top: badge → "Save 35%" in green highlight → subhead → orange CTA button, all above the fold.
- No render bugs — clean layout, all images loaded, text is legible throughout.

## What's weak

- Six separate offers stacked in one email (35% headline, 30% Dream, 35% Dream Max, 35% Comfy Bundle, 10% Percale Sheets, 10% Down Duvet) blur which deal is actually the hero.
- Email is very long for a single promo — a reader has to scroll through a full product-education section and two more offer tiers before reaching bedding.
- This is the fourth "Save 35%" Labor Day send in under two weeks (08-23, 08-24, 08-31, now 09-04) with no new discount ceiling — cadence fatigue risk is real.
- Small orange "Shop sale" CTA under the hero is easy to miss against the busy weather-map background graphic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one primary offer above the fold and move the diagram/bedding sections to a secondary "explore more" block — right now five discount percentages compete for attention.
- 2. Make the hero CTA bigger and higher-contrast; the current orange button is small relative to the green "35%" callout that dominates the eye first.
- 3. Since this is the fourth "35%" send in two weeks, either escalate the number or pivot fully to the education/seasonal angle without leading on discount — repeating the same ceiling risks numbing subscribers to it.
- 4. Trim the bottom third (Percale Sheets / Down Duvet 10%-off cards) into a smaller single CTA — they're minor offers that dilute the mattress-focused hero message.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New foam, made for shifting fall temps`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Warm nights, cool mornings — Casper has both covered`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Breathable foam that adjusts as the seasons do — plus 35% off Max.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Meet Dream Max: our most breathable mattress yet.`
