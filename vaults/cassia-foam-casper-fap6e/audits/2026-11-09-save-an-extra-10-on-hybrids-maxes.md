# Save an extra 10% on Hybrids & Maxes.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-repeat of the 2026-11-06 send, "Black Friday just got better. Only for subscribers." It has the same "extra 10%" and the same code, 10BFCM, on the same Hybrids and Maxes. The hero is strong. A giant lime "10%" sits next to a relaxed woman holding a pillow, with the code and a white "Shop now" pill right under it, so the offer lands in about a second.
- The body repeats the offer too much and doesn't help the reader choose. Four mattress tiles each carry a "Save 30% + Extra 10%" badge. Two all-foam tiles follow. None of them show a price, a rating or a reason to pick one over another. A subscriber who got the 11/06 email has no new reason to act today.
- Overall it's a competent, clean promo, but it's the second send in four days with the same offer. It reads as a reminder, not news. The fine print says the extra 10% ends 11/13, but nothing in the visible copy says so.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. The oversized lime "10%" on a photo gives instant hierarchy, and the code sits right beside the button.
- The lime-on-navy palette matches the 11/04 and 11/06 sends, so it's recognizably Casper.
- Each product tile has a short line that separates the models, such as "Cooling and conforming" for Snow and "Plush, pressure-relieving" for Cloud One.
- The all-foam fallback ("Prefer all-foam?") catches readers who don't want a hybrid.
- The footer has useful extras: a text-signup block, a mattress quiz and a store finder.

## What's weak

- Nothing visible says the offer ends, so there's no urgency. The 11/13 end date appears only in the grey fine print.
- The four identical "Save 30% +Extra 10%" badges repeat the offer without adding anything, and the stacked math is confusing.
- There are no prices, reviews or star ratings, and no "from $X" figure, so the 30% and 10% have nothing concrete to attach to.
- The "Shop now" pill under the four hybrid tiles covers all of them, and the two-tile section has an unlabelled gap in clarity. The first two Max and Dream photos are also nearly identical, so the tiles blur together.
- The "Use code" line is lime on a light photo background. The lime "10BFCM" is lower contrast than the rest of the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Friday 11/13", and a countdown if possible. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Show real prices. "Queen Dream Hybrid from $X after code" turns percentages into a number a reader can act on.
- 3. Cut the four repeated badges to one line of offer copy. Use the freed space for a star rating and a review count on each tile.
- 4. Give each tile its own CTA (like "Shop Dream") so the click goes to the product, not a category.
- 5. Darken or outline the code so "10BFCM" is easy to read against the photo.
- 6. Space out sends. A different angle (for example a sleep-quality story, or a Max vs. Hybrid comparison) would stop this feeling like a rerun of 11/06.
