# Save up to 30% before midnight tonight!*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the last-day send for the Friends & Family sale. It is clear and well organized, but it is not special. The hero says "Save up to 30% on everything.*" and "It ends when you go to sleep!" on a dark navy photo, with a single "Shop Friends and Family Sale" button. Below it, a light-blue tier ladder runs mattresses 25%, bundles 30%, pillows 20% and everything else 10%. The eye lands on the big "30%" first, and every tier has its own button.
- The urgency is real, since the fine print gives 10/21 at 11:59pm PT. It is also the fifth F&F-themed send since 10/13, after "Your extra 5% ends tomorrow ⏰*" (10/13), "Discountmaxxing" (10/15), "Make every room the good room" (10/16) and the adjustables push on 10/19. A subscriber has seen this offer repeatedly, so "last day" is the only new information. The email gives that no visual weight beyond the subject line, and "It ends when you go to sleep!" is vaguer than a stated deadline.
- "On everything.*" overpromises. The headline number applies only to bundles, mattresses top out at 25%, and the fine print excludes Final Sale, Clearance, Nap Pillow and more. The email works but would not be remembered, so I'd call it a solid 7 rather than a standout.

## What's working

- The "30%" in the hero is huge and high-contrast, so the offer is readable in a glance.
- The tier ladder (25 / 30 / 20 / 10) lets a shopper self-select by category, and each tier has its own button.
- The "before midnight tonight" subject matches the fine-print deadline, so the urgency is credible.
- Product photography is clean and the layout has no render bugs, no broken images and good spacing.
- The SMS signup strip and the quiz and store-locator links are well placed and don't compete with the offer.

## What's weak

- "30% on everything" is contradicted by the tiers below it (10% to 25%) and by the fine-print exclusions.
- The hero has no date or clock time, only the cute "when you go to sleep" line. The real deadline is buried in the footer.
- The body has no reviews, ratings, trial-period or free-returns reassurance, and no specific hero product.
- The sale tiers feel familiar from the earlier F&F sends. Nothing signals "final day" beyond the headline.
- The nav bar is tiny, grey and low-contrast, and I can't see any preview text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the exact deadline ("Ends tonight, 11:59pm PT") in a badge or countdown near the hero button, and make the headline honest, e.g. "Up to 30% off bundles, 25% off mattresses."
- 2. Add one social-proof strip, such as a star rating or "100-night trial · free returns", between the hero and the tiers.
- 3. Make the first tier a specific hero product (e.g. the best-selling mattress) with a price or "from $X" instead of a category line.
- 4. Tighten the footer fine print and surface "Excludes clearance and Final Sale" closer to the offer.
- 5. Write real preview text so the inbox row carries more than the subject.
