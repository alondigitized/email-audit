# Make every room the good room
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the Friends & Family sale again, now organized by room. The hero says "Save 30% and make every bedroom the good one." Below it are three sections (Primary bedroom, Guest bedroom, Kids' room), each with two bundle tiles and a "Save 30%" badge. The room framing is the one new idea in a week of near-identical sends. The layout is clean and the hierarchy reads well.
- It is still a catalog email with seven identical "Shop now" buttons. The room-by-room logic is nice, but nothing says why to buy now. The 10/21 deadline is only in the fine print, and the subject line doesn't mention the offer. After "Discountmaxxing" (10/15), "Your extra 5% ends tomorrow" (10/13) and the three FF-EXT5 sends before that, this is roughly the sixth Friends & Family touch in ten days. The format is fresh, but the cadence is wearing.
- Verdict: a solid, well-built email with a vague subject line and no urgency. It will do fine with people already shopping, and it won't pull in anyone who isn't.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. A smiling woman making a bed with a Casper One mattress, with "Save 30%" in a large size, makes the offer clear in a second.
- The room-based structure (primary, guest, kids') gives shoppers a reason to pick a section, and it differs from the earlier mattress-card grids.
- Round "Save 30%" badges on every tile keep the offer visible. They also show the exceptions clearly ($100 off Snug, 20% off 2+ pillows).
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. Each tile has a short bundle name and its own button.
- The footer has a text sign-up block, a mattress quiz link and a find-a-store link. These give people who aren't ready to buy somewhere else to go.

## What's weak

- The subject "Make every room the good room" has no offer, no deadline and no product. It reads like a brand tagline.
- All seven buttons say "Shop now." Nothing differs between the primary-bedroom and kids' tiles, so the copy gives no reason to click one over another.
- The 10/21 end date appears only in the grey fine print at the bottom. There is no visible urgency in the hero or anywhere else in the body.
- "Save 30%" is qualified by "*Savings based on Comp. Value" and a long exclusions list in the footer. Several tiles carry a different discount, and none show a price.
- The Friends & Family message has appeared in each of the last six sends, so the email adds little new for a repeat reader.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero and the subject line, for example "Ends 10/21" as a badge next to "Save 30%". This is the single change most likely to lift clicks.
- 2. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones, such as "Shop the Adjustable Max Bundle" or "Shop kids' bundles." Add a price or "from $X" under each bundle name.
- 3. Move the room framing into the subject so it carries real information, and add the discount.
- 4. Vary the cadence. Give one send a different angle, such as the room-by-room story without the sale framing, so Friends & Family doesn't dominate the inbox.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off bundles for every bedroom — ends 10/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Primary, guest, kids' room: bundles up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Friends & Family ends 10/21. Pick a bundle for each room.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save 30% on bundles, plus $100 off Snug for the kids' room.`
