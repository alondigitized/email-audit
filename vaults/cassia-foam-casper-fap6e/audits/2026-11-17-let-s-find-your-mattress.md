# Let’s find your mattress
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday send with an educational wrapper. The hero is a navy "Save up to 35% on everything, plus an extra 5% off" block with code S-BFCM and an "Unlock your savings" button. A close-up of a hand pressing into foam sits under it, followed by "Let's find your type" and a "Find Your Mattress" button. The offer is clear and the hero is strong. But "up to 35%" with an asterisk is softer than the flat "extra 10%" sends on 11/9 and 11/12. It also continues the 11/15 step down to "extra 5%."
- The body is a "Firmness Fallacy" explainer plus six product cards: The One, Dream, Snow, Cloud One, Dream Max and Snow Max. Each card has a firmness and feel label, a short description, a "Save 30% + Extra 5%" badge and a "Shop ___" button. It is well organized, and it is useful for someone who doesn't know which mattress to pick. Six near-identical cards make a long scroll, though, and the page has no single hero product.
- The subject line is the weak link. "Let's find your mattress" is generic and has no offer, no urgency and no mention of Black Friday. The best content in the email is in the hero, and the subject hides it. The email is good once opened, but the inbox view undersells it. It is a solid send, but a mid-funnel one, and the subject doesn't fit a week where Casper has been leading with "extra 10%" and "subscribers."

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer first: "Save up to 35%," "extra 5%," the code S-BFCM and a white CTA, all on high-contrast navy.
- The "Black Friday Subscriber Exclusive" tag fits the subscriber-first thread running through the 11/6 and 11/15 sends.
- The firmness and feel labels (Medium Firm / Feel Responsive, Medium Soft / Feel Conforming) help a shopper compare quickly.
- Every product card repeats the discount badge, so the deal stays visible all the way down.
- The CTAs are consistent: navy pill buttons with a "Shop [Product]" verb plus noun.
- The footer SMS signup and the mattress quiz link give a fallback for readers who aren't ready to buy.

## What's weak

- The subject "Let's find your mattress" gives no offer, no urgency and no Black Friday hook.
- "Up to 35%" and "Save 30% + Extra 5%" read inconsistently. It is hard to tell the real deal, and the asterisked fine print adds to that.
- The discount is at its lowest point of the run: 30% plus an extra 5%, versus the extra 10% on Hybrids and Maxes in earlier sends.
- Six near-identical product cards make a long, repetitive scroll with no clear hero product or best-seller.
- The "Firmness Fallacy" block is a small text section with a "Learn More" link. It sends a second, competing action ahead of the product grid.
- Product cards show no prices, reviews or star ratings, so there is no social proof or concrete dollar savings.
- This is the fifth or sixth Casper promo in about 12 days, and the creative leans on the same S-BFCM and extra-percentage-off messaging.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject and preheader to carry the offer.** Right now the email's best asset, the discount, is invisible in the inbox. Lead with the percentage, the code or Black Friday.
- 2. Reduce the product grid to 3 cards: a best-seller, a hybrid and a premium Max. Add a "Shop all mattresses" button after them.
- 3. Add a "from $X" price and a star rating to each card to give shoppers a concrete reason to click.
- 4. Make the offer consistent. Use "Save 30% + extra 5%" everywhere, or "up to 35%" everywhere.
- 5. Move the quiz CTA ("Take our mattress quiz") up under the hero if "find your type" is the theme. It is currently buried in the footer.
- 6. Add a clear end date, such as "Ends Monday," to the hero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Black Friday subscriber pricing is live. Use code S-BFCM for an extra 5% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Firm, medium or soft? Find your fit and save up to 35% through Monday.`
