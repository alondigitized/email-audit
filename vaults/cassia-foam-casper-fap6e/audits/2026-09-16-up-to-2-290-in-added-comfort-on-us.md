# Up to $2,290 in added comfort—on us.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper pivoting off the Labor Day "35% + extra 5%" arc into a bundle offer: free Adjustable Base (up to $2,290 value) with a Snow or Snow Max mattress purchase. It's a genuine change of pace from the discount-hammering the brand has been running since 08/23 — a GWP framed around premium sleep tech rather than another percentage-off code — which should help against fatigue after six-plus near-identical "extra 5%" sends.
- The execution is clean: soft mint/cream palette, a lifestyle hero image, clear stacked CTAs ("Shop Snow mattresses" / "Claim free Adjustable Base"), and a logical scroll — Snow Mattresses features, Adjustable Base features, then a secondary discount ladder (20%/25%/10%) for anyone not converting on the GWP. No render bugs visible.
- The weak spot is value clarity: "up to $2,290" is a big number with a vague asterisk, and the offer only applies to two specific mattress lines (Snow/Snow Max), which isn't obvious until you've scrolled past the hero. Still, this is a solid, on-brand send — better differentiated than the last several Labor Day repeats.

## What's working

- Clear value proposition in the hero: free Adjustable Base GWP, tied to a specific dollar figure.
- Two distinct, well-differentiated CTA buttons in the hero (shop mattress vs. claim base) give two paths to convert.
- Product education sections (cooling, support zones, wireless remote, zero-gravity) build a case beyond price.
- Secondary discount ladder (mattresses, bundles, pillows) captures browsers who don't want Snow/Snow Max specifically.
- Consistent navy/mint/cream brand palette and clean typography throughout, no layout breakage.

## What's weak

- "Up to $2,290" is an eyebrow-raising number with no immediate context — feels like classic MSRP inflation until the asterisk is chased down.
- The GWP is restricted to Snow/Snow Max only, but that restriction isn't flagged until deep in the email; a shopper eyeing the "Bundles" or "Premium Foam" sections lower down may not realize those don't qualify.
- Hero lifestyle photo (woman reading in bed) doesn't actually show the Adjustable Base, undercutting the headline offer visually.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the hero — "September Savings" is a month-long window with no countdown, which is a step back from the brand's usual "ends tonight" urgency that (per history) actually performs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible deadline to the hero (e.g., "Ends 9/30") — this brand's own history shows true urgency outperforms vague "monthly savings" framing.
- 2. Clarify the Snow/Snow Max restriction directly under the headline, not just in the fine-print asterisk, so scrollers don't assume the base applies to bundles/foam mattresses too.
- 3. Swap or augment the hero image to actually show the Adjustable Base in use, not just a mattress — the free item is the headline offer and should be visible.
- 4. Tighten "$2,290" with a plain-language anchor line ("that's the base, free") right in the hero so it doesn't read as an inflated MSRP trick.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your mattress just got smarter—base's on us`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Zero-gravity, anti-snore, wireless remote — free with Snow or Snow Max.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends 9/30: claim your free Adjustable Base today.`
