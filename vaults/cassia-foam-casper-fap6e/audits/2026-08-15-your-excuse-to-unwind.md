# Your excuse to unwind
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a "National Relaxation Day" tie-in built as a full product catalog dressed up as a lifestyle send — and it's the widest offer spread Casper has run in this stretch: 20% on mattresses, 25% on bundles, 20% on 2+ pillows, all stacked in a tri-panel header before the hero even loads. Unlike the August 8th send, where two conflicting discounts fought for attention, this one leads with a clean three-way menu and lets the reader self-select, which actually resolves the confusion problem from that earlier send.
- The hero itself — "Relaxation deserves a holiday" over a couple napping in bed — is soft and on-brand, but it's also the weakest link: the CTA is a plain "Shop The Sale" button with no percentage or dollar figure attached to it, so the specific offers from the top banner get orphaned the moment you scroll past them. Everything below is a long, well-organized product cascade (mattress, adjustable base, pillows, sheets, topper, foundation, robe) with individual "Save 20%"/"Save 25%" badges reappearing per item, which recovers some of the lost urgency but asks for a lot of scrolling to get there.
- Net: solid organization, real and varied discounts, no render bugs — but a generic subject line and a hero CTA that undersells its own promo cap this in the middle of the pack, on par with the August 9th send rather than the August 6th high point.

## What's working

- Three distinct, concrete offers (20% mattresses, 25% bundles, 20% on 2+ pillows) are visible above the fold with no scrolling.
- Clean visual rhythm through the product cascade — alternating white/lavender section backgrounds make each product module easy to scan.
- Individual "Save X%" badges are repeated on each product photo (Cloud One, Adjustable Pro Bundle, Hybrid Pillow, Mattress Topper), reinforcing the discount at the point of decision.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.
- Product range is genuinely broad (mattress, base, pillow, sheets, topper, foundation, robe) — good for a subscriber who's not just mattress-shopping.

## What's weak

- Hero CTA "Shop The Sale" is generic and disconnected from the specific percentages sitting right above it in the header banner.
- Subject line "Your excuse to unwind" carries zero offer information — a reader has to open blind to learn there's a sale at all.
- "Relaxation deserves a holiday" is a soft, vague headline that doesn't reinforce any of the three concrete discounts directly beneath it.
- The email is very long (7+ product modules) before reaching a clear single "best deal" — no single hero product is elevated above the rest.
- Copy like "He had a point" (referencing a fictional kid's National Relaxation Day origin story) is a cute aside but adds no functional information and delays getting to the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number in the hero CTA — change "Shop The Sale" to "Shop up to 25% off" so the button itself carries the offer instead of relying on the banner above it.
- 2. Pull the strongest single discount (25% on bundles) into the subject line instead of using vague "unwind" copy — offer-forward subjects have out-performed lifestyle subjects in every recent send this brand has tested.
- 3. Reorder the cascade so the highest-discount item (Adjustable Pro Bundle, 25%) leads, not Cloud One (20%) — lead with your best number.
- 4. Trim the "National Relaxation Day" origin story to one line max; it's charming but it's pushing the actual offer further down the scroll.
- 5. Add urgency ("ENDS MONDAY" style, as used in the August 8–9 sends) somewhere visible near the top banner — this send has no deadline at all, which softens the "why now."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 25% off mattresses, bundles & pillows`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your relaxation upgrade is 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mattress, base, pillows, sheets — all on sale for a limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `National Relaxation Day savings: 20-25% sitewide.`
