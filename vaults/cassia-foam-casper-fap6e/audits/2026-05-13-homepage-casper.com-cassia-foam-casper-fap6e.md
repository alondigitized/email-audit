# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened Casper expecting to see the Memorial Day sale I've been hearing about in pod ads for three weeks — and instead I got a privacy wall before I could see a single mattress. The sliver I can make out above the modal is promising: there's a "Save up to 30% on mattresses" banner at the very top, and the nav icons (Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz) are clean and scannable. But Casper buried the entire first experience under a cookie modal. Not a great first impression for someone who already owns the brand and came back specifically to shop.

## What's working

- **"Save up to 30% on mattresses" banner** — it's the first thing I see, above the nav. Concrete and percentage-specific. That's the one thing Casper got right above the fold.
- **Category icon strip** — Mattresses / Pillows / Furniture / Take the Quiz is a clean one-tap path. I know I can get to pillows (my add-on play) in one tap without scrolling.
- **"Casper." wordmark** — brand recognition is instant. I know exactly where I am.
- **Memorial Day peeking through** — the decorative "Memorial" copy and holiday-appropriate star/snowflake motifs are barely visible behind the modal, suggesting the seasonal hero is on-theme.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocks 80% of the viewport** — This is the dominant experience. Three full-width black buttons ("Reject Optional Cookies," "Accept Optional Cookies," "Your Privacy Choices") wipe out the hero, any imagery, the CTA, and any promo detail. Brutal.
- **No visible primary CTA button** — There is no shop/buy button accessible without dismissing the modal first. As a mobile user, this is a friction wall before I've seen a single product.
- **Zero personalization for a returning customer** — No "Welcome back, Cassia" moment, no account nudge, no "Complete your sleep setup" hook. I own the Original; nothing on this screen knows that.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I specifically add a pillow to hit free shipping. That threshold is nowhere on screen.
- **No trust signals visible** — no review count, no trial-length reminder. The 100-night trial is Casper's headline differentiator and it's completely absent in the visible area.

## Recommendations

- **Defer or shrink the cookie modal on return visits** — For users with a Casper cookie already set (i.e., anyone who visited before), collapse this to a slim bottom banner. Blasting the full modal on a returning shopper is conversion sabotage.
- **Anchor the free-shipping threshold in the promo banner** — Change "Save up to 30% on mattresses" to "Save up to 30% on mattresses + free shipping over $X." That's the line that gets me to add a pillow.
- **Add a "Back to sleep" or "Complete your setup" CTA for returning visitors** — Even a sticky nav badge ("Your next pillow ships free") would keep me from bouncing if the modal dismissal feels like too much work.
- **Bring the 100-night trial up to the banner or nav** — It's Casper's most-cited purchase driver in podcast ads. It should be scannable before I even see a product.
