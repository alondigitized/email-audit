# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay so the first thing I see when I hit casper.com is not the mattress I came here to check out — it's a full-screen cookie consent wall blocking literally everything. I can see a countdown timer up top screaming "Last chance for big savings!" and a glimpse of a "July 4th Sale" banner peeking out behind the modal, which is exactly the kind of holiday markdown moment I live for. But I can't get to any of it until I click through the privacy choices, which is already annoying me.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer ("44 HRS 58 MIN 40 SEC")** — the urgency is real and it lands for me because I actually wait for these sale windows. That ticking clock made me pay attention before anything else.
- **"July 4th Sale" banner** — barely visible behind the modal, but the patriotic star treatment is on-brand for a holiday markdown event. It signals "this is the moment" without feeling desperate.
- **Category nav row (Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz)** — Pillows is right there, one tap away. I always add a pillow to clear the free-shipping threshold, so having it in the primary nav is genuinely useful.
- **"Last chance for big savings!"** — the banner copy is direct and urgent without being weird about it. It reads like something I'd click.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie consent modal** — this is the biggest friction I'm hitting. Before I can see a single product image or offer detail, I'm being asked to make a legal decision. That's a bounce trigger for anyone who's impatient, and I am.
- **No specific offer amount visible** — "big savings" tells me nothing. Is it 10%? 30%? Free pillow? I need a number to decide if it's worth fighting through the modal.
- **No returning customer signal** — I've owned a Casper Original. There's zero acknowledgment of that. No "welcome back," no loyalty badge, no hint that they know I exist.
- **Primary CTA completely missing** — the only action buttons above the fold are "Reject Optional Cookies," "Accept Optional Cookies," and "Your Privacy Choices." Not exactly a shopping mood.
- **Zero trust signals visible** — no review count, no trial-length callout, no free-returns badge. Casper's whole thing is the 100-night trial and I can't see it anywhere.

## Recommendations

- **Shift the cookie modal to a slim bottom banner, not full-screen takeover** — let the hero and sale content breathe. The sale urgency is doing real work; the modal is killing it. Ethyca supports a compact bottom-strip layout.
- **Add a specific discount amount to the countdown banner** — swap "Last chance for big savings!" for "Last chance — up to 30% off" (or whatever the actual number is). One number makes the urgency concrete.
- **Surface the 100-night trial in the nav bar or a sticky strip** — it's Casper's single strongest trust signal for first-time *and* returning buyers. Pinning it above the fold costs nothing and closes doubt.
- **Add a "returning customer" or account-login nudge to the nav** — even a tiny "sign in" icon next to the bag signals that Casper remembers me. I'd tap it.
