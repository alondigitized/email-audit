# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I opened Casper and the first thing I see is a privacy consent modal covering basically the entire screen. Behind it I can barely make out something that looks like a Memorial Day campaign, but I have to accept or reject cookies before I can see any of it. The top banner does say "Save up to 30% on mattresses" which is relevant to me, but that headline is competing with legal copy for my attention. Not a great first impression for a brand that's supposed to feel approachable and trustworthy.

## What's working

- **Promo banner ("Save up to 30% on mattresses")** — visible at the very top before the modal, and that's the one message I actually care about as someone who buys at the holiday markdown. Memorial Day timing is right.
- **Category quick-nav (Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz)** — the icon row above the modal is still visible and I could navigate away from this mess immediately. Mattresses is one tap.
- **"Take the Quiz" CTA** — visible in the nav strip. As someone who owns the Original and is eyeing an upgrade, I'd actually use that to figure out if I should step up to the Wave or Snow.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal obliterates the hero** — I cannot see the headline, the hero image, or the main CTA. This is the first thing I see. Casper's entire brand premise is "we make buying a mattress easier and less stressful" — and then they open with a legally dense three-button wall.
- **Promo banner is vague** — "Save up to 30% on mattresses" with arrow controls but no linked details. Which mattresses? All of them? Starters only? I need one more word of specificity to trust it.
- **No returning-customer recognition** — I own a Casper. Nothing on this screen acknowledges that. No "welcome back," no "explore upgrades," no loyalty hook.
- **Memorial Day campaign is teased but hidden** — I can see decorative stars/snowflakes and the partial word "Memorial" behind the modal, which means the seasonal hook exists but is completely inaccessible.

## Recommendations

- **Defer the consent modal by 5 seconds or move it below the fold.** Let me see the hero first. The banner offer and campaign imagery should land before legal friction.
- **Add specificity to the banner offer** — "Save up to 30% on mattresses — Memorial Day sale, ends 5/26" tells me it's real and time-bounded without requiring a click.
- **Surface a returning-customer rail** — "You own the Original. Ready for the Wave?" This is a milk-the-base moment. I'd tap that immediately.
- **Make the promo banner tappable and link directly to the sale landing page**, not just the homepage — right now the arrows suggest a carousel but nothing invites me to act on the deal.
