# Sleep care is self-care.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a content-led World Mental Health Day send, and it's a real change of pace for Casper. The "Snoozeletter" hero reads "A little care. A little quiet. A little more rest." It has a calm lifestyle photo and two "Why it matters / Try tonight" cards on feelings and focus. It's well made and on-brand. It also barely sells anything, and the discount is crowded into a strip at the top.
- Compare it with the last two weeks. The 10/10 and 10/08 sends were both mattress and offer pushes built around FF-EXT5. This one keeps the same "Subscriber Exclusive: Extra 5%*" banner but drops the product grid. The result is a send with two jobs and neither done fully. The offer strip says "shop the sale", and the body says "here are some sleep tips". Product doesn't show up until one bedroom photo about two-thirds of the way down, and the only product CTA is "Find your comfort".
- My judgement is that it's a good brand-building beat after a run of near-identical promos, and a weak conversion email. The sleep-tips CTAs lead away from purchase, and the offer sits above a hero that doesn't mention it.

## What's working

- The "Sleep care is self-care." subject pays off in the hero copy and the World Mental Health Day framing.
- It breaks the promo cadence. The 10/10 and 10/08 sends were back-to-back FF-EXT5 mattress pushes.
- The "Why it matters / Try tonight" cards are scannable and give a concrete reason to read on.
- The photography is warm and consistent, and the navy hero has strong contrast.
- The book pick (*Hotel Du Lac*) and the "text SLEEP to 32006" strip are nice brand-voice touches.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried. Extra 5% with code FF-EXT5 sits in a light-blue strip above the hero, then never reappears. The asterisk disclaimer is small.
- The two "Explore sleep tips" CTAs, plus "Explore more sleep tips", are vague and non-commercial. They send the click away from the sale.
- The only product moment is one bed photo, and the mattress isn't named. There are no prices, reviews, or product links.
- The body is long and text-heavy for a retail email, and the hero CTA is salmon on navy while the offer button is a different blue.
- The Friends & Family message is mixed with a wellness message, so the email has no single clear job.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job. For a conversion send, move the 5% offer into the hero. Keep the wellness copy but tie it to a product, such as "Rest better tonight: extra 5% on mattresses and bedding."
- 2. Replace "Explore sleep tips" with a verb-plus-noun CTA, such as "Shop better sleep" or "Shop the sale".
- 3. Add a product row after the cards. Show two or three hero items with names and a "from $X" price, and add reviews.
- 4. Shorten the tip modules to one card, or collapse them behind a "Read the tips" link.
- 5. Say what "Find your comfort" leads to. Name the mattress or bedding, and link directly to it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `A little more rest + extra 5% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two small habits for tonight, plus your extra 5% with code FF-EXT5.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `World Mental Health Day: how better sleep supports how you feel.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound in the visible source: it has a `lang`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` layout tables and a preheader. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, and the HTML provided is truncated. The QA run reports 0 issues and 4 warnings, and most of those warnings are artifacts of the relay or the capture rather than defects in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and none were flagged. 19 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so those are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/CgxqCN4759eg.../open` (Bloomreach/Exponea) is a 1×1 image with `style="display: none;"`. This is standard, but it is the only tracking element visible. Click-tracking URLs are in the truncated portion and could not be inspected.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** QA reports 16,754 of 18,785 characters (about 89%) are URLs. This points to long tracked or redirect URLs dominating the text alternative. It is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The QA "missing alt text" warning is a false positive.** The flagged `open` pixel has `alt=""` in the source (`<img width="1" height="1" alt="" ...>`). An empty alt is correct for a decorative or tracking image. No action is needed, though the checker should treat `alt=""` as valid.
- **External stylesheets:** two `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags point to `brxcdn.com/.../media/original/9c4a4f1a-...` and `9c30fa4c-...`. They are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and I'm assuming these load web fonts. Gmail and several other clients strip `<link>` tags, so those clients fall back to system fonts. This is low severity, and I can't confirm the fallback stacks from the truncated source.
- **Preheader:** hidden text ("How sleep supports your well-being.") is followed by a padding block of `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;` entities. This is the standard technique and is applied correctly.
- **Outlook:** MSO conditional blocks include `OfficeDocumentSettings` and `PixelsPerInch`. No issues found.
- **Mobile:** the media query at `max-width:620px` includes `.stack .column`, `.mobile_hide` and `.desktop_hide`. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|…|*` or `[[ ]]`. The remainder of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Check the raw message at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay does not expose it. No claim can be made either way.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and raised no failure, but the footer is outside the truncated source. This is not confirmed.
- **Sender:** the From address is `support@e.casper.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain. A "support" local part on a marketing send is an observation only. Whether replies are monitored is unknown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no CTA `href` values, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified. The link probe reported no redirect or landing failures for the 12 links it checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the unsubscribe headers at the origin.** Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. If AgentMail strips them, update the QA check to avoid repeated false warnings.
2. **Fix the QA alt-text rule.** Treat `alt=""` on tracking pixels and decorative images as compliant, so the `open` pixel stops being flagged.
3. **Reduce the plain-text URL ratio.** Shorten tracked links or trim the text alternative so URLs are not about 89% of it.
4. **Confirm web-font fallbacks.** The `brxcdn.com` `<link>` stylesheets are stripped in Gmail, so check that the font stacks degrade cleanly.
5. **Re-run with the full HTML.** The truncation blocks checks of CTA hrefs, UTM parameters, the footer unsubscribe link and the physical address.
6. **Capture authentication headers.** Pull `Authentication-Results` from the original delivery path (for example a seed-inbox "show original") to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.casper.com`.
