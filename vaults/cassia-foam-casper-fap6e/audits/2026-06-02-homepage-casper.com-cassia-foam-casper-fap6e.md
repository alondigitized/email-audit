# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- I landed on Casper and immediately got buried under a cookie consent wall — the hero is almost completely blocked, though I can just barely make out what looks like a "Memorial" banner underneath, so they're running a Memorial Day push. The top strip says "Save up to 30% on mattresses," which is the brand doing what it does: discounting its way into my cart. Clean nav, recognizable logo, familiar category layout — this is still unmistakably Casper.

## What's working

- **"Save up to 30% on mattresses" top banner** — I'm a holiday-markdown buyer, and this is the exact sentence that gets me to stick around. I see it before I see anything else.
- **Category nav icons (Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz)** — four taps, nothing wasted. I can get to pillows in one tap when I want to hit the free-shipping threshold.
- **"Take the Quiz" nav item** — this is the 100-night trial brand's natural extension. It signals Casper still thinks of itself as an education-first company, not just a box-shipper.
- **Casper logo and clean header** — I recognize the brand instantly. Zero confusion about where I landed.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal covering the entire hero** — this is the single biggest problem. I have no idea what the hero image is, what the lead copy says, or what I'm supposed to feel. On mobile, a full-screen modal on page load is a bounce risk.
- **No returning-customer acknowledgment** — I own a Casper Original. There's nothing here that knows that or rewards it. No "Welcome back," no loyalty points tease, no "You've tried us — here's what's new."
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always add a pillow to hit free shipping. I can't do the math yet because the number isn't shown anywhere above the fold.
- **"Up to 30% off" language** — the "up to" hedge is soft. Without a floor (e.g., "at least 20% sitewide"), this reads as bait-and-switch territory to me.
- **Memorial Day campaign partially obscured** — I can barely see "Memorial" behind the modal. A timely campaign that the UX is actively preventing me from seeing is a wasted spend.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie consent to a bottom bar or slim banner.** Full-screen cookie walls on mobile kill first impressions. A compact GDPR-style bar at the bottom keeps the hero visible and still satisfies compliance.
- **Add a "Welcome back" or account-state hook in the header.** Even a small "Sign in for member pricing" CTA next to the cart icon would acknowledge that I'm not a stranger to this brand.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold explicitly in the top banner** alongside the 30% off callout — e.g., "Save up to 30% on mattresses + free shipping over $X." I need both signals to decide to stay.
- **Make the Memorial Day deadline explicit.** "Ends Sunday" or a small countdown next to the discount percentage adds real urgency without being a dark pattern.
