# Ends tomorrow: Extra 10% off Hybrids and Maxes.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, competent last-call reminder, but it's a near-copy of the 11/9 "Save an extra 10% on Hybrids & Maxes" and 11/6 "Black Friday just got better" sends. Same 10BFCM code, same Hybrids and Maxes, same 30% + 10% stack. The only new thing is "Ends tomorrow," and that's the right urgency to add. It still reads as the fourth variation of one offer in about a week.
- The body is well organized: mattresses, adjustables, pillows, then bedding, each with a green "Save X%" badge. The weak spot is the top. The hero says "Build a better bed" in soft, generic copy, and the actual deadline and code sit in a small subhead. The punchy "Ends Tomorrow / Save 30% / Use code: 10BFCM" block is at the very bottom, so it's the strongest part of the email and almost nobody will see it. The long cross-sell tail also dilutes the one thing the subject promised.
- Verdict: solid execution, tired concept, and the urgency is buried. It will convert people already in the funnel but won't win anyone new.

## What's working

- The subject leads with "Ends tomorrow," a credible deadline, and states the offer plainly.
- The green "Save 30% + Extra 10%" badges on the Hybrid and Max photos reinforce the offer visually.
- The layout is clear and scannable, with one CTA per section and no render bugs.
- The closing navy block repeats the offer, deadline and code in large type, with a "Shop now" button.
- The SMS signup and the mattress quiz give a path to non-buyers.

## What's weak

- The hero headline "Build a better bed." has no offer or deadline. The "extra 10%" and "ends tomorrow" are in small body text.
- The pillow, bedding and adjustable sections each carry a different discount (20%, 10%, 30%). That muddies the single "extra 10%" message in the subject.
- The hero CTA, "Start building," is vague and doesn't say what you're shopping.
- The code 10BFCM appears only in the small hero copy and the bottom block. The mid-email buttons are generic "Shop…" links with no code reminder.
- It's another near-identical send after 11/6 and 11/9. The credible "ends tomorrow" urgency is also weakened by the Black Friday push that follows.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the bottom block's content into the hero. Use "Ends tomorrow: extra 10% off Hybrids + Maxes" in lime on navy, with the code as a sticker. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the pillow, bedding and adjustable sections, or collapse them into one "Complete the setup" row. Let the two mattress modules carry the email.
- 3. Change the hero button to "Shop Hybrids & Maxes" or "Use code 10BFCM."
- 4. Repeat the code in each mattress module's CTA area.
- 5. Space the sends out, or make this one the true last call with a countdown instead of a fourth restatement.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow it's gone: 40% off your Casper hybrid`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code 10BFCM stacks on 30% off. Offer ends tomorrow night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last day to stack an extra 10% on Casper's best-selling mattresses.`
