# Your extra 5% ends tomorrow ⏰*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the 10/08 and 10/10 send again with a new subject line. It has the same Subscriber Exclusive banner, the same FF-EXT5 code and the same mattress cards. The new hook is "ends tomorrow", but the body never says that. There is no deadline, countdown or date anywhere in the render, so the urgency lives only in the subject.
- The design itself is clean. The hero leads with a big "extra 5%" and one button. Below it, five "Need something ___?" cards (dependable, cool, supportive, soft, stability) each pair one mattress with one Shop now button. That structure is easy to scan and the copy has personality ("Caution: extra cozy"). The weak spot is the offer. The extra 5% is stacked on 20–25% off, and the hero's "up to 25%" is hedged with an asterisk and "Savings based on comp. value." On the cards, the "Save 20% + Extra 5% off Use code" badges are small and crowded.
- Verdict: competent but redundant. A subscriber who got the 10/07 "Final hours: Extra 5% on bundles*" and the 10/10 repeat has already seen this deadline claim twice, so it reads as manufactured urgency. Casper has now sent four near-identical Friends & Family emails in a week.

## What's working

- The hero puts "extra 5%" and the FF-EXT5 code first, with one clear "Shop the sale" button.
- The "Need something dependable/cool/supportive/soft?" framing turns a spec list into a choice. Each card has one product, one line and one button.
- The product copy is playful and on-brand ("Caution: extra cozy", "attacks heat").
- Every card has the same layout, with a deal badge on the photo and a button below it, so the email is quick to scan.
- No broken images or layout breakage are visible.

## What's weak

- The body has no deadline, countdown or end date, so "ends tomorrow" is never backed up.
- The badge stack ("Save 25% + Extra 5% off Use code: FF-EXT5") is small, cramped and repeated on every card. The code has already been given in the hero.
- The hero's "up to 25%" with an asterisk and "comp. value" fine print undercuts the offer.
- Every CTA says "Shop now", so the buttons tell you nothing about the product.
- There are no reviews, ratings or trial and returns reassurance on a high-consideration purchase.
- The cadence is stale. It is a re-skin of the 10/08 and 10/10 sends, and the "final hours" claim was already used on 10/07.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tomorrow, 10/14, 11:59 PM PT", ideally with a countdown timer. If the deadline is real, show it. If it isn't, drop the urgency subject.
- 2. Collapse the five cards into a shorter set of two or three bestsellers. Use one "code applied at checkout" line and let each badge show only the final percentage.
- 3. Name the price or savings in each button, for example "Shop The One, from $X" or "Get The Snow, 30% off".
- 4. Add a star rating and review count under each mattress, plus the trial and returns line (e.g. "100-night trial").
- 5. Space out the sends. If the code runs through 10/14, send one reminder, not a fourth near-identical email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 5% off your Casper mattress, ends 10/14`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow's the last day for FF-EXT5`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 5% on top of up to 25% off. Code FF-EXT5 expires tomorrow night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The One, Snow, Dream and more. Subscriber pricing ends 10/14.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard Bloomreach/Exponea build with a preheader, a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, and a mobile breakpoint at 620px. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and links could not be inspected. The automated QA shows 0 failures and 4 warnings, and most of the warnings are about missing data rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 20,088 of 21,952 characters (about 91%) are URLs. This points to long tracking-wrapped links in the text alternative and is a possible spam-filter signal.
- **Link coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 24 were skipped (footer, utility, social). No broken links were reported, but the skipped links are unverified.
- **The open pixel is correct.** `<img width="1" height="1" alt="" src="https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/…/open" style="display: none;">` is the Exponea open-tracking pixel.
- **The QA "missing alt text" warning looks like a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on that image, which is correct for a tracking pixel. No real alt-text issue is confirmed from the visible HTML.
- **The pixel path appears to carry a test label.** The tail of the token seems to decode to "Core Sleepers Test", which suggests the send is tagged as a test segment. This is an inference from the token and not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Web fonts depend on external stylesheets.** Two `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags point to `brxcdn.com/…/media/original/…`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and Outlook strip these links, so those clients will use fallback fonts.
- **Preheader and preview padding are present.** The span reads "One more day to save even more on your next mattress.", followed by `&#847;`/`&shy;` filler. This is standard.
- **Outlook handling is present.** The head has the MSO conditionals (`PixelsPerInch`, `sup`/`sub` fixes) and `mso-hide:all` on the hidden elements.
- **No other issues confirmed.** The body, tables and content-image alt text were truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The truncated body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the headers, so it is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this needs checking against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. Nothing was verified.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible.** They are probably in the truncated footer. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported.
- **The subject ends in `*`.** This implies fine-print terms. Whether the body carries the matching disclosure could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No link hrefs, UTM parameters or landing-page probe results were provided. The only evidence is that the CTA links were probed with no failures.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the raw `.eml` at the origin (not through the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. If they are present at the origin, mark these QA warnings as relay artifacts.
2. Check why the plain-text alternative is about 91% URLs. Consider unwrapping or shortening the tracking links in the text part, or trimming the text part.
3. Suppress the QA "missing alt" rule for 1×1 tracking pixels that already have `alt=""`.
4. Confirm the "Core Sleepers Test" label on the open pixel is not a test-segment send going to production.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML so the UTM parameters, footer compliance elements and the 24 skipped links can be checked.
