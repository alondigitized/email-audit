# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened casper.com and immediately got wall-blocked by a full-screen cookie consent modal — three giant buttons, dense legal copy, and zero of the homepage visible behind it. What I *can* glimpse tells a familiar story: a Memorial Day sale banner at the top and the usual category nav (Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz). Casper feels like a brand that knows its products but apparently hasn't figured out that a privacy modal the size of a highway billboard is not a great first impression for someone who already owns one of their mattresses.

## What's working

- **Top promo banner** — "Save up to 35% on mattresses" is the first thing I read after the Casper logo. That's the right product for me and it's above the fold, even above the nav.
- **Category nav strip** — Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz as icon-linked tiles is clean and scannable at a glance. I can reach my category in one tap without digging through a hamburger menu.
- **Memorial Day timing** — I can make out decorative holiday lettering behind the modal. Holiday markdown is exactly when I buy, so the campaign alignment is correct even if I can't read it.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal as the literal first experience** — Three oversized buttons shove the homepage completely off screen on mobile. As a returning Casper customer, I'm already in their database. This feels like a stranger stopping me at the door of my own house to ask for permission to know me.
- **Zero returning-customer acknowledgment** — No "welcome back," no order history hook, no loyalty reference. Casper has my email and my mattress serial number. None of that is used here.
- **"Up to 35% off" hedged language** — Classic bait. I've learned to read "up to" as "probably 15% on the thing I want." No specific mattress or dollar amount anchors the promise.
- **"Take the Quiz" in the primary nav** — I already own the Original. Surfacing a quiz discovery tool at nav level is optimized for new buyers, not re-buyers coming for pillows or accessories.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default for return visitors or slim it to a single bottom banner** — A cookie notice that eats 80% of the viewport on mobile is a bounce machine. Drop it to a slim bottom sheet and let the homepage breathe.
- **Add a "Back for more? Shop accessories" banner for return traffic** — I came back. Casper knows I own a mattress. A single personalized strip ("Your next sleep upgrade: pillows, sheets, a dog bed") converts accessory trips without requiring a new-mattress decision.
- **Anchor the Memorial Day offer to a specific product and price** — "Original Mattress — $200 off this weekend" beats "save up to 35%." Give me a number I can act on without clicking through to find the catch.
- **Move "Take the Quiz" out of the primary category nav** — Replace it with "Pillows" or "Bedding" to serve the accessory purchase I'm most likely to make as a returning owner.
