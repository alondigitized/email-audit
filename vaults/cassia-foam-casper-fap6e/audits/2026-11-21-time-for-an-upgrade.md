# Time for an upgrade!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent Black Friday subscriber send whose subject sells the wrong thing. The hero is strong: a lime "Subscriber Exclusive Ending Soon" pill, a giant "35%" on navy, "extra 5% off that," and code 5-BFCM above a white "Shop now" button. The eye lands on the offer immediately. Below the hero, though, the email turns into a store-visit and education piece ("Meet Casper IRL," "Find your feel," "Meet our sleep experts").
- "Time for an upgrade!" promises nothing specific. It is the vaguest subject in this run of sends, even though the body has a concrete "up to 35% + extra 5%" offer. It is also the same offer as 11/15 ("Extra 5% off, just for subscribers*") and 11/17 ("Let's find your mattress"), so a subscriber who has been getting Casper mail all month will see it as repetition. Only the "Ending Soon" pill gives any urgency, and it has no date or time.
- Overall it's a solid 6 to 7: a well-built email that makes little effort to stand out from the others. A subject line with the offer in it would help most.

## What's working

- The hero offer is clear. The oversized "35%" in lime on navy is the first thing you see.
- The subscriber-exclusive framing and the "5-BFCM" code give loyal readers a reason to feel rewarded.
- The "Shop now" button is high-contrast and appears in the hero, mid-body, and again in the closing "Shop your exclusive" block.
- The lifestyle photos (a sleeper sprawled on a bed, a hand pressing foam, a showroom) are warm and on-brand.
- The nav bar, category row (Mattresses, Pillows, Bedding, Accessories), and SMS signup give an easy way to browse past the hero.

## What's weak

- Three of the four content blocks push store visits or education ("Find a store," "Visit us") rather than the sale. That dilutes the click path, and it assumes the reader lives near a Casper store.
- The CTAs are generic: "Shop now" appears twice, and "Visit us" and "Find a store" are vague. Nothing links to a specific product.
- The "Ending Soon" urgency has no date. The deadline (11/23) only appears in the fine print at the bottom.
- There is no product detail, price, or social proof anywhere. You never see a mattress model name, a sale price, or a review.
- The "*Savings based on Comp. Value" footnote under the hero button and the dense legal text at the bottom make the "35% on everything" claim feel less than fully honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer and the deadline in the subject, and make the hero deadline concrete ("Ends Sunday 11:59pm PT").
- 2. Replace one of the three store/education blocks with a hero product (for example, the Original or a Hybrid) showing the sale price and a star rating.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop mattresses" and "Get my extra 5%," and link each to a category or product.
- 4. Keep "Find a store" to one small block, or segment it to subscribers who live near a Casper store.
- 5. Show the code as a copyable element and add a note that it applies automatically at checkout.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 5% off ends Sunday: your subscriber code`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 35% off + an extra 5% for you`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code 5-BFCM stacks on up to 35% off everything. Ends 11/23.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Subscriber-only pricing on mattresses, pillows and bedding ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound (doctype, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals, `role="presentation"` tables, hidden preheader). The QA run found no hard failures. All 4 warnings are either capture limitations of the AgentMail relay or low-severity. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and most link `href`s could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and none were reported broken. 17 were skipped by design (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized).
- **Plain-text part is URL-dominated.** 16,129 of 17,975 characters (~90%) are URLs, so the plain-text alternative is mostly tracking-wrapped links. This is a real but minor deliverability and accessibility concern. I couldn't confirm the cause because the multipart source isn't shown, but wrapped redirect URLs are the likely one.
- **False positive on the "missing alt" warning.** The flagged image is the Exponea open pixel (`https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/.../open`). Its source does have `alt=""`, `width="1" height="1"` and `display:none`, which is correct for a decorative tracking pixel. This is not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No confirmed issues in the visible source.**
  - `<title>`, `lang="en"`, the viewport meta and the `@media (max-width:620px)` stacking rules are present.
  - The preheader is hidden with `display:none`, `mso-hide:all` and zero-width padding characters.
  - The external font stylesheets from `brxcdn.com` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Clients that strip `<link>` (Gmail) will fall back to system fonts. I couldn't verify whether the font stacks define fallbacks because the source is truncated.
- I couldn't check body image alt text or link text because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source, the subject or the preheader.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers at the sending ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From address is `support@e.casper.com`, a subdomain, so alignment should be confirmed in the raw message headers.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be assessed because they fall in the truncated footer. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable from the data provided. No `href` values or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source, and the QA output doesn't report destination or redirect details. The probe reported no broken or erroring links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the original message headers, not the relay copy.
2. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.casper.com` from the raw headers.
3. Review the plain-text MIME part and replace the tracking-wrapped URLs with short, readable text.
4. Suppress the "missing alt" check for 1×1 tracking pixels that already have `alt=""`.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check UTM consistency, footer compliance (unsubscribe link and postal address) and body-image alt text.
