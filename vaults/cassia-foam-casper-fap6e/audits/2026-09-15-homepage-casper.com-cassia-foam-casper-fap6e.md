# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is very much "corporate mattress brand mid-sale" energy — a countdown timer up top, a Labor Day banner, and a cookie modal all fighting for my attention before I've even scrolled once. I already own the Original, so I'm not shopping for a mattress, but the vibe here reads as generic sitewide discounting rather than anything that recognizes me as a returning customer.

## What's working

- The countdown timer ("20 HRS 58 MIN 11 SEC") does create real urgency — as someone who buys at markdown, a ticking clock is exactly the kind of nudge that gets me to act instead of bookmark-and-forget.
- "Shop mattresses" and "Shop sale" are both dead-simple, unambiguous buttons — no guessing what happens when I tap.
- The nav bar up top has Mattresses, Pillows, Bundles, Accessories all one tap away, which matches how I actually shop (add a pillow to hit free shipping).

## What's weak

- That cookie consent modal is sitting directly on top of the "Discover the Casper difference" section, cutting off the headline mid-word ("Discover the Casper diff...") — it's blocking real content, not just a footer nag.
- "Labor Day Sale" as the banner theme on September 15th is stale — Labor Day was over a week ago, and seeing it still front-and-center makes the site feel unattended.
- There's a floating "UNLOCK OFFER" tab AND a chat bubble AND the modal all stacked in the same corner — it's a lot of competing UI asking for a tap before I've seen a single mattress.
- No mention of the 100-night trial or free returns anywhere visible — that's the exact story I associate with Casper and it's the thing that would actually reassure me, and it's just not here.
- Nothing acknowledges I might already be a customer — no "welcome back," no account nudge, no loyalty perk, despite there being an account icon in the header.

## Recommendations

- Dismiss or auto-collapse the cookie modal faster, or move it to a slim bottom bar so it's not blocking the hero content on first load.
- Swap "Labor Day Sale" for whatever the actual current September promo is — a dated banner erodes trust fast for someone who already knows your brand.
- Add a "100-night trial, free returns" line near the top offer banner — that's the reassurance that gets trial-conscious repeat shoppers like me to actually click.
- Consolidate the "UNLOCK OFFER" tab, chat bubble, and promo banner so they don't all compete in the same visual space.
