# Back-to-School savings end tonight
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's cleanest hero yet in this run and closes out the Back to School arc with real urgency: "Savings up to 25%" and "ENDS TONIGHT!" sit right at the top, hand-drawn orange circle around the pillow stack, unambiguous "Shop Back to School Sale" button — no scroll, no ambiguity, no competing headline module directly underneath (unlike the August 8th "extra 5%" send that got buried by a bigger 20% module one scroll down).
- Where it loses points is the same place every recent Casper send loses them: it's a full catalog scroll (mattresses, Snug for kids, bundles, pillows, toppers, Glow Light) with a different %-off badge on nearly every module — 20%, $100, 25%, 20%, 20%, 20%. None of those individually is dishonest, but stacked together they dilute the "ENDS TONIGHT" urgency into "everything is basically always on sale," which is the brand's recurring structural issue across this history (2026-08-15, 2026-08-13, 2026-08-11 all scored 7 for the same reason).
- Verdict: strong top-of-funnel execution, same mid-funnel bloat as the rest of the run. This should score in line with the better sends in this stretch — comparable to 08-11's "Best in Class" — not the standout 08-06 send, because the true deadline urgency ("ENDS TONIGHT") isn't reinforced anywhere below the hero, which wastes the strongest lever in the whole email.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "Savings up to 25%" + "ENDS TONIGHT!" in bold lime highlight, hand-drawn circle drawing the eye straight to the product, single clear CTA button.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" is genuine same-day urgency, the sharpest deadline language seen in this brand history so far.
- Product photography is clean and on-brand throughout, no render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- Footer utility block (text signup, mattress quiz, find a store) is well organized and doesn't compete with the sale messaging.

## What's weak

- Urgency evaporates after the hero — none of the six product modules below repeat "ends tonight," so a scroller has no reason to feel time pressure by module three.
- Offer sprawl: 20% / $100 / 25% / 20% / 20% / 20% across six categories reads as "the whole catalog is on sale" rather than one focused push, diluting the headline offer.
- No social proof or reviews anywhere in the body despite Casper being a review-heavy category (mattress fit/trial period is a classic purchase blocker that goes unaddressed).
- "*Pricing based on Comp. Value" sits directly under the hero CTA — a fine-print caveat this close to the primary offer nudges toward the "buried asterisk" pattern flagged as a rubric risk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Repeat "Ends tonight" (or a countdown) at least once mid-scroll — e.g. a sticky reminder above the "Everything you need, better together" module — so the urgency carries through the whole catalog scroll, not just the hero.
- 2. Cut the module count from six down to three: lead category (mattresses), the bundle (best margin/story), and one accessory. A shorter, tighter scroll converts better against a same-day deadline than a full catalog.
- 3. Move or shrink the "*Pricing based on Comp. Value" disclaimer so it's not sitting directly under the CTA button — it currently reads like a hedge on the headline offer.
- 4. Add a one-line trust signal (e.g. "100-night trial" or a star rating) near the hero or mattress module — reviews/trial info is notably absent given how purchase-critical it is for mattresses.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Back to School sale expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mattresses, bundles, and pillows — all discounted, all gone by morning.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours to save up to 25% on your sleep setup.`
