# Save up to 35% on restorative sleep.*
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "35%" headline Casper has now run three times in nine days — 08/31 ("Save 35%: Labor Day Doorbusters are here"), 09/04 ("comfort for every forecast"), 09/05 ("everything"), and now 09/06 "restorative sleep." The number hasn't moved, only the adjective wrapped around it, and a subscriber who's opened even two of these will recognize the identical hero shot, orange highlighter-strike "35%," and blue "Shop Labor Day Sale" button before reading a word.
- The render itself is clean and well-organized: a clear hero, then a tidy category cascade (Mattresses → Bundles → Pillows → Bed Frames → Bedding) each with its own discount tier, product image, and CTA. That structure is genuinely good email craft. The problem is entirely creative fatigue — this is the fourth near-identical "35% off" send this campaign, and "restorative sleep" reads as a thin repackage rather than a fresh idea.
- Verdict: solid execution wrapped around a stale idea. Ship the layout, kill the subject-line treadmill.

## What's working

- Clear single hero offer ("Save up to 35%") with unmissable visual hierarchy — eye lands on the headline and orange CTA immediately.
- Good category segmentation below the fold (Mattresses, Bundles, Pillows, Bed Frames, Bedding), each with its own discrete discount tier, which helps a shopper self-select.
- No render bugs — clean images, no overlapping text, no broken layout.
- Consistent brand voice and blue/orange color system throughout.
- Useful post-purchase real estate: SMS signup, mattress quiz, store locator.

## What's weak

- Fourth "35% off" hero in nine days with the same layout, same hero photo style, same orange button — this reads like the exact same email with a new hat.
- "Restorative sleep" in the subject is vague marketing filler; it doesn't tell the reader anything concrete beyond the discount already implied by "35%."
- Asterisk on "35%" and "*Savings based on comp. value" is buried small print under the hero — undermines the headline honesty signal.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the render — no "ends tonight," no countdown, despite this being deep into a Labor Day arc that should be closing.
- Category CTA copy ("Shop mattresses," "Shop bundles") is generic-safe but interchangeable with any Casper send from the last six months.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add explicit time-bound urgency to the hero — this is Labor Day, and competitors' sends in this same window ("Extra 5% ends tonight," 08/30) show Casper knows how to do urgency; this send has none. A countdown or "Sale ends Monday" line would lift both open and click.
- 2. Differentiate the subject from the last three sends by naming an actual mechanism or category, not another adjective bolted onto "35%."
- 3. Move the comp.-value disclaimer or at least a hint of it closer to the headline — right now the real math is hidden in tiny italic text.
- 4. Vary the hero creative — same pillow/face close-up crop has run across multiple sends; a bundle or bed-frame hero would signal "new campaign," not "same email again."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends Monday — mattresses, bundles, pillows all included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping + 35% off, but only through Labor Day.`
