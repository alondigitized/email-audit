# Save up to 35%. Labor Day starts now.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's biggest headline number in the run — "Save up to 35% on everything" hits right at the top with the hand-drawn green circle and a bright orange "Shop Labor Day Sale" button, no scroll required. It's a clean, confident open to the Labor Day arc and structurally the strongest hero of this whole stretch.
- Where it falls down is the same place recent Casper sends keep falling down: the moment you scroll past the hero, the tiered breakdown (30% mattresses, 35% bundles, 20% pillows, 10% everything else) creates a "wait, which number is real" effect. "Up to 35%" in the hero is technically true only for bundles specifically — everything else is lower — so the headline oversells relative to the actual per-category offers shown two inches down.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel hook, decent execution below, but the now-familiar Casper pattern of a headline max-percent that doesn't map cleanly to the itemized offers underneath.

## What's working

- Hero headline is large, legible, and impossible to miss — "35%" and "everything" are the largest text on the page.
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop Labor Day Sale") in high-contrast orange against the blue background.
- Category breakdown is well organized with its own image, benefit line, and CTA per product (mattresses, bundles, pillows).
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.
- Model/lifestyle photography (person sleeping peacefully) reinforces the sleep/rest category without being generic stock.

## What's weak

- Hero claims "up to 35% on everything," but the body shows mattresses only at 30% and the catch-all "everything else" bucket at just 10% — the headline number is doing more work than the actual offer supports.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the render — unlike the August 17 "ENDS TONIGHT!" send, this one has no date, countdown, or "ends" language, which is odd for a sale-launch email.
- Four full CTA modules (mattresses, bundles, pillows, "everything else" grid) turn this into a long scroll-heavy catalog rather than a focused offer, diluting the strong hero.
- Asterisk on "35% on everything*" with no visible disclaimer nearby (fine print sits far below in the footer) — the qualifier is doing legal cover work the reader can't see without scrolling all the way down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tighten the headline-to-body math: either say "Save up to 35% on bundles" in the hero (matching the biggest real number) or restructure the tiers so "everything" genuinely tops out near 35%. The gap between hero claim and body reality is the single biggest credibility issue here.
- 2. Add a visible deadline to the hero module — this is a sale *launch*, so a "Now through [date]" line would set expectations and create urgency the current render completely lacks.
- 3. Consider trimming the "everything else" 4-image grid (furniture/bedding/accessories/protectors) or moving it further down/into a secondary email — it's the weakest, most catalog-like section and pulls focus from the three strong hero categories.
- 4. Move a short version of the "savings based on comp. value" disclaimer directly under the hero asterisk instead of only in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `35% off bundles. Labor Day sale is live.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Labor Day starts now: up to 35% off sleep sets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mattresses, bundles, pillows — all on sale through Labor Day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The biggest sale of the season just went live.`
