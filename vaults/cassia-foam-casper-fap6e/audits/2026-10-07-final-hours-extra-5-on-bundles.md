# Final hours: Extra 5% on bundles*
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Final hours: Extra 5% on bundles*", but the body doesn't deliver on it. The hero is a "Friends & Family Sale" with "Save up to 30%". The extra 5% only shows up as a small badge on three bundle tiles, with the code PRIME26. There is no countdown, no "ends tonight" badge and no deadline anywhere in the render. "Final hours" is an urgency claim with nothing visible to back it up. I could also see the two promos competing: Friends & Family is still the headline, and the bundle code lives inside the lower modules.
- This is the fifth or sixth discount-led Casper send in about 10 days. The Friends & Family 25% run was 09/27, 09/29 and 09/30. The 10/03 and 10/04 sends were catalog-style bed-layering emails. Now Friends & Family comes back with a different code (PRIME26) and a different headline number (30%). A subscriber would read this as a perpetual sale, so "final hours" loses credibility. The layout itself is tidy: product cards in a consistent pattern, each with a discount badge and a "Shop now" button. It's a long scroll of nine near-identical modules, and the eye has no clear order to follow.
- Verdict: a competent catalog send undercut by a mismatch between subject and body, and by too many stacked promos. Given the history, I'd expect it to perform like the other recent sends, with little urgency lift.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a big "Save up to 30%" with a single "Shop the sale" button on a calm, warm photo.
- Each product card has a discount badge in the same position (20%, 25%, $100, 10%), so the savings are easy to scan.
- The product mix covers the range, from mattresses to bundles, a base, sheets and pillows. The Snug card ("Loved by parents") speaks to a specific buyer.
- The bundle cards show the code PRIME26 directly in the badge, so the shopper doesn't have to hunt for it.
- The nav, the footer category links and the SMS signup are all present and don't get in the way.

## What's weak

- The subject's "Extra 5% on bundles" and "Final hours" aren't the hero message. The hero is Friends & Family, and no deadline or countdown is visible anywhere.
- The offers are layered and confusing. "Up to 30%" in the hero, "30% + extra 5% off" on the bundles and 10–25% on single items all appear together. Only the bundle cards carry the extra-5% code, which fits poorly with a Friends & Family theme.
- The email repeats "Shop now" on eleven buttons, with no verbs tied to the product. The page is very long, and there's no top pick to focus on.
- The asterisks on the subject and the hero ("Savings based on comp. value") hint at fine print, and the legal text at the bottom is tiny.
- The Friends & Family branding is stale. It was already pushed three times from 09/27 to 09/30, so it reads as recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Lead with "Extra 5% on bundles. Ends tonight." with the PRIME26 code, a dated deadline and a visible countdown. Move the single-item discounts below it.
- 2. Cut to one offer story. Either run Friends & Family or run the bundle code, not both. Drop the "up to 30%" hero claim or make it the bundle number.
- 3. Shorten the scroll. Keep the best-seller bundle, one mattress pair and one add-on, and cut the rest so there's a clear order to follow.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop the bundle" or "Get the mattress + base".
- 5. Add social proof such as star ratings or review counts on the hero or the best-selling bundle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: extra 5% off bundles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bed bundle, 35% off, until midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code PRIME26 on The One, a base and sheets. Expires at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mattress + base + protector in one cart. Code PRIME26 ends tonight.`
