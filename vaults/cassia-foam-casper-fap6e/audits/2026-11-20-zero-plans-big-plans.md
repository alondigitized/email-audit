# Zero plans = big plans.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's sixth Black Friday-adjacent send this month, and it has the weakest hook. The "Zero plans = big plans." subject is a cute theme line with no offer in it. The email itself is solid: a navy "Black Friday Subscriber Exclusive" banner with "Save up to 35% … plus an extra 5% off," code 5-BFCM, then a cozy-night hero. The offer and the tone point in different directions, so the hero reads as a lifestyle piece and not a sale.
- After the 11/17 "Let's find your mattress" and the 11/15 "Extra 5% off, just for subscribers*" emails, a subscriber would see this as the same 35% + 5% offer wrapped in a streaming and movie theme. Below the hero it's a long, even grid of eight product tiles, then a "what we're watching" content block. Nothing is broken, but nothing is prioritized, so the email feels like a catalog.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand email that is mostly filler. It would do better with a sharper subject and one hero product.

## What's working

- The offer banner at the top is clear: "Save up to 35%" in large type, the extra 5%, the code and an "Unlock your savings" button.
- The "Subscriber Exclusive" badge gives the offer a loyalty frame.
- The seasonal theme (cozy night in, streaming) fits late November and gives the email some personality.
- Each tile has a green badge ("Save 35% + Extra 5%", "Save $100", "Only $199") that makes the discount scannable.
- The coral "Let's Get Cozy" button stands out well against the dark photo.

## What's weak

- The subject has no offer, product or deadline, so it tells the reader nothing about the sale.
- The hero headline "Choose Your Cozy Night In" and the "Let's Get Cozy" button don't mention savings or a product. The photo is a cropped close-up of a blanket and feet, with no product shown.
- The eight-tile grid and the shows/movies/books block dilute the message. There's no single hero product and no deadline, so nothing creates urgency.
- Badge math is inconsistent ("10% + Extra 5%", "20% on 2+", "30% + Extra 5%", "$100", "$199"), and it sits on top of an asterisked "up to" headline. That feels busy, and the fine print makes the offer look less honest.
- Cadence fatigue: the creative and the 35%/5% offer repeat the 11/17 and 11/15 sends. There's also a faint cropped "Cas…" / "Snug" watermark at the right edge of the hero that looks like a clipped graphic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the subject and hero. Name the deal and the deadline, and give the hero a product (such as the Dream Max or the Original Pillow) instead of a lifestyle crop.
- 2. Cut the grid from eight tiles to three or four and drop the entertainment block, or move it under a single "Cozy picks" row. Lead with the best-selling mattress.
- 3. Make CTA copy specific, for example "Shop mattresses up to 35% off," and make the hero button match the banner button.
- 4. Add a real deadline ("Ends Cyber Monday") and one or two reviews or a star rating near the hero.
- 5. Clean up the stacked-discount messaging so each tile shows one clear price or percentage, and fix the clipped graphic in the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 35% off + extra 5%: your cozy night in`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Subscriber-only: Casper Black Friday, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code 5-BFCM takes an extra 5% off mattresses, pillows and bedding.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your early Black Friday pricing on sheets, duvets and the Dream Max.`
