# Today only: Save an Extra 5% on bundles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Casper's eighth email in ten days, and the brand is now stacking offers on top of offers — the previous "35% off Memorial Day Sale" got an "extra 5% on bundles, today only" bolt-on. It's a legitimate escalation tactic for the final day of a long-running sale, and the bundle-builder construction at least gives the reader a different shape of offer than the flat-percentage sends that came before.
- The execution is competent: clean hero with the "Today Only" pill, a "Build Your Own Bundle" interactive-feeling block, then four pre-built bundles (Best-Selling, Snore, Adjustable, Cool Sleep) each with their own 35% callout. Every product has a sale badge, a clear inclusions list, and visible CTAs. Visual hierarchy holds up — the eye lands on the headline and the offer pill first.
- The judgement: this is a respectable last-day-of-sale send, but it's the third "Memorial Day" beat in a single week and the urgency claim ("today only") only resonates if you ignore that the brand has been crying "ends soon" for ten straight days. For an existing subscriber, the bundle framing is the only thing keeping this above pure fatigue territory.

## What's working

- "Today Only" pill plus a single, time-bounded headline gives this send a real reason to exist on the last day of the sale.
- "Build Your Own Bundle" reframes the offer as a configurator instead of a flat discount — fresh shape after a week of identical sale heroes.
- Each bundle card carries its own "Save up to 35%" badge, inclusions list, and CTA — easy scan path.
- Footer category strip (Mattresses / Pillows / Bedding / Accessories) gives a fallback path for non-bundle shoppers.
- Sale banner at the bottom restates "Save up to 35% on mattresses / Up to 35% off everything else" — useful anchor for stragglers.

## What's weak

- The "extra 5%" hook in the subject line never gets a dedicated visual moment in the body — the hero says "5% on bundles" but it's swallowed by the bigger 35% callouts everywhere else.
- Eighth send in ten days. The "Today Only" urgency reads as boilerplate after three prior "ends soon" beats this week.
- Bundle inclusions are tiny and dense — the value math (what you actually save by bundling vs. buying separately) is invisible.
- "Save an extra 5%" + "Save up to 35%" creates offer-math confusion: is it 5% off bundles, or 35% off bundles, or 35%+5% stacked?
- No social proof or review snippets in any of the four bundle cards — Casper has used these before and dropping them here weakens the click case.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the 5% the hero, not a footnote.** The 35% has been the headline for ten days — today's news is the stacked extra 5%. Show the math: "35% + 5% = your best price of the sale" on the lead bundle card.
- 2. **Show bundle savings in dollars.** "Save $X when you bundle" beats four identical "Save up to 35%" badges. Concrete dollar savings is the one thing this send is missing.
- 3. **Drop one bundle, add proof.** Four bundles is one too many; replace Adjustable or Snore with a "★★★★★ 12,000+ reviews" strip — Casper has the social proof, the bundle cards don't use it.
- 4. **Resolve the offer-stack ambiguity in the headline.** "Last day: extra 5% on every bundle (on top of 35% off)" is clearer than the current split.
- 5. **Cool down the cadence.** This is post-purchase territory after eight sends — segment out openers from the last three Memorial Day emails and only resend to non-openers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: extra 5% on bundles ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack 35% + 5% — bundles, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Final hours of Memorial Day savings. Build yours before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `35% off mattresses + extra 5% when you bundle. Ends tonight.`
