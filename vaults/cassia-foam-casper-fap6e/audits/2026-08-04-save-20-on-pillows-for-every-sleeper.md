# Save 20% on pillows for every sleeper
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's most product-dense send since the July sprint — nine distinct pillow SKUs organized into three clear categories (Classic comfort, Cooling comfort, Beyond the bedroom), each with its own construction/feel/best-for spec block. It's a genuinely useful catalog page dressed as an email, and the "Save 20% on 2+" hero clears the fold cleanly with a bright green highlight, a lifestyle photo, and an unambiguous "Find your pillow" CTA.
- The problem is depth, not the top. Once you scroll past the hero, this becomes an undifferentiated wall of nine near-identical product cards on the same pale blue background — there's no second hero moment, no bestseller callout, no reason to stop scrolling at pillow #4 versus #7. Compare this to the July 30 bundle email, which segmented into three distinct visual zones; here everything after the fold reads as one long list.
- Judgement: strong top, weak middle. This works as a reference/catalog email for someone already shopping pillows, but it won't convert a browsing subscriber past the hero.

## What's working

- "Save 20%" in green highlighter against the cream hero background is the clearest visual hook in the email, consistent with the brand's recurring green-highlight discount treatment.
- "Back to School Savings" pill anchors this to the ongoing seasonal campaign without repeating prior subject lines verbatim.
- Each product card includes genuinely useful shopping data — Construction, Feel, Best for — which few mattress/bedding competitors surface at the email level.
- The closing "Which pillow is right for you?" quiz module gives an off-ramp for anyone overwhelmed by nine options, which is a smart inclusion given how dense the body is.
- Category dividers (Classic comfort / Cooling comfort / Beyond the bedroom) with green circle underlines do real work breaking up the scroll.

## What's weak

- Nine near-identical product cards on the same flat blue field creates monotone fatigue — nothing visually distinguishes the "Hybrid Pillow" from the "Foam Snow Pillow" except the product photo itself.
- The 20% offer only appears twice (top hero, "Classic comfort" and "Cooling comfort" badges) and is absent from "Beyond the bedroom" — unclear if Nap/Backrest pillows are included in the discount, which reads as inconsistent rather than intentional.
- No urgency or end date anywhere in the email — "Back to School Savings" implies a window but never states one, which is weaker than the Aug 2 mattress send's clean single-offer framing.
- No pricing shown anywhere, even loyalty/member pricing, despite this being deep enough in the funnel (construction, feel, best-for) that a shopper would expect to see a number before clicking.
- Nine CTAs of identical visual weight ("Shop X Pillow" repeated nine times) dilutes the primary action — there's no single dominant next step for someone who isn't ready to pick a specific pillow yet.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the catalog in half for the email, push the rest to the landing page.** Lead with 3-4 hero pillows (bestseller, new arrival, cooling, budget) and let "Shop all pillows" carry the long tail — nine SKUs in one send is a spec sheet, not a marketing email.
- 2. **State whether "Beyond the bedroom" is included in the 20% deal.** If it's excluded, say so; if included, add the badge for consistency — right now it just looks like an oversight.
- 3. **Add a price or "from $X" under at least the hero pillow.** A shopper this far into construction/feel details wants a number before clicking through.
- 4. **Give the offer a deadline.** "Back to School Savings" without an end date reads as evergreen, not urgent — even "ends 8/18" would lift click intent.
- 5. **Differentiate section backgrounds** (alternate white/blue banding per category, as seen in some prior sends) so the scroll has visual waypoints instead of one continuous blue field.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off pillows: find your perfect fit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Every sleep style, 20% off pillows`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Side, back, or hot sleeper — there's a pillow (and a discount) for that.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `9 pillows, one deal: save 20% when you buy 2+.`
