# Vacation's Over. Sleep Isn't.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest hero Casper has run in weeks — "Save up to 25%" sits directly under the Back to School Sale badge with a hand-drawn green circle around "25%," followed by an unambiguous "Shop the sale" button, all before a single pixel of scroll. It borrows the winning formula from the July 30 "Moving in made easy" send (8/10) rather than the buried-discount mistake of the August 1 "Back to School" email, where the 25% was four modules deep.
- The "vacation's over" creative concept is a genuinely fresh angle — pairing post-summer fatigue with product need (cooling tech, staycation recovery) gives the send a narrative the last four Back to School variants lacked. That said, this is now the sixth Casper promotional email in roughly two weeks, and the fifth to lean on a Back to School frame in some form — the cadence is starting to blur into noise even when individual creative is strong.
- Net: a well-built, hero-first email let down by subject-line softness and brand fatigue from over-cadence, not by execution on the page itself.

## What's working

- The hero offer ("Save up to 25%") is legible, high-contrast, and above the fold with a clear single CTA — no hunting required.
- The "vacation's over" narrative thread carries through the whole email (hero → "did summer plans affect your sleep" → "staycation after vacation"), giving it more cohesion than prior grab-bag product dumps.
- Product tiles are organized into two clean, purpose-driven sections (cooling/Snow Technology, then bundles for "recovery"), each with visible % off badges (20%, 25%, 25%, 25%).
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, badges are legible.
- Diverse model representation across the two lifestyle photos (sleeping woman, robe model).

## What's weak

- The subject line "Vacation's Over. Sleep Isn't." carries zero mention of the 25% discount — a wasted opportunity given how strong the hero offer is once opened.
- Two products (Lightweight Down Alt Duvet, Blanket robe) have no visible discount badge, breaking the otherwise consistent "everything's on sale" signal.
- The "up to 25%" framing plus the tiny "*Based on Comp. Value" disclaimer under the hero is the same soft-hedge pattern that's dinged honesty on past Casper sends — most shoppers will only find their actual discount tier once they click through.
- No urgency or end-date anywhere in the email — nothing signals why to act today versus next week.
- This is the sixth Casper send in about two weeks; recipients who opened July 30, August 1, and August 2 are seeing a fourth near-identical layout pattern (badge → green-circled %→ CTA → product grid).

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the number in the subject line — "Save up to 25%" is your strongest asset and it's completely absent from the inbox view. This is the single highest-leverage fix.
- 2. Add a real deadline (e.g., "ends Sunday") to the hero and subject to manufacture urgency that's currently missing entirely.
- 3. Badge every product tile with its discount — the two unbadged items (duvet, robe) look like they were left out of the sale, which undercuts trust.
- 4. Tighten "Shop the sale" / "Shop Now" CTA copy to something category-specific like "Shop Cooling Mattresses" to lift relevance for scanners who won't read the body copy.
- 5. Space this out — three back-to-back "Back to School"-adjacent sends in a week (Aug 1, 2, 4, 6) risks list fatigue; consider skipping a cycle before the next promo.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Snow Technology bundles, bedding, and bases — all discounted through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your best sleep since before the trip. Shop 20–25% off sitewide.`
