# Extra 5% savings just for you.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper leaning into a "subscriber exclusive" angle rather than another flat markdown — hero copy reads "Labor Day Subscriber Exclusive" with "Get an extra 5% savings on Labor Day deals," code L-2026. Compared to the 2026-08-20 send (8/10, "Save 30% on Dream Mattresses") this hero is weaker on its own: 5% stacked on top of an unstated base discount is a much thinner headline number, and the extra-5%-on-top structure only becomes clear once you scroll into the product tiles below, where you see it's actually 20-35% + 5%.
- The exclusivity/loyalty framing is a smart differentiator from the pure discount blasts, and the layout is clean and on-brand — hand-drawn green circles, consistent navy/orange color system, three clear product tiers. But the hero undersells the real offer: a reader who doesn't scroll thinks this is "just 5% off," when the actual stack (up to 35%+5%) is the more compelling story and it's buried below the fold.
- Solid, well-produced send, but it's a step down from the 08-20 and 08-18 sends because the headline number is the weakest one Casper has shown in this run.

## What's working

- Clean visual hierarchy in the hero: badge → headline → offer → code → single orange CTA, no scrolling needed to see the core message.
- "Subscriber Exclusive" framing and personalized "Your best exclusive offer of the season" copy differentiate this from generic promo blasts.
- Consistent, legible product-tier layout below the fold (Premium Foam, Hybrid & Max, Bundles) each with its own discount badge, product shot, and "Shop now" CTA.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, badges are legible against both dark and light backgrounds.
- Promo code (L-2026) is prominently displayed and easy to screenshot/copy.

## What's weak

- The hero only advertises "extra 5% savings" — the real headline number (20-35% base) is hidden until you scroll into the product grid, making the top-of-email offer look weak in isolation.
- "Your best exclusive offer of the season" is a big claim not backed up by a big visible number right next to it.
- Three near-identical product tiles with the same badge/photo/headline/CTA pattern repeated three times gets monotonous and pushes total email length long for a single offer.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the render (no "ends tonight" or date), unlike the 08-17 send which used real urgency to strong effect.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real number in the hero. Change the headline to lead with the stacked total ("Up to 35% + an extra 5% = up to 40% off") instead of just "extra 5% savings" — the current hero undersells the actual deal shown two screens down.
- 2. Add a visible deadline to the hero (e.g., "Ends Labor Day") — this run has proven urgency drives Casper's best scores (08-17, 08-18).
- 3. Trim the three-tile repetition — consider condensing Hybrid/Max and Bundles into a single secondary panel to shorten the scroll and reduce redundancy.
- 4. Move the promo code disclosure/base-discount math closer to the hero so the "extra 5%" doesn't read as the whole offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 35% + an extra 5%, just for you`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day subscriber bonus is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack an extra 5% on already-reduced Labor Day prices.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code L-2026: your reward for being a subscriber.`
