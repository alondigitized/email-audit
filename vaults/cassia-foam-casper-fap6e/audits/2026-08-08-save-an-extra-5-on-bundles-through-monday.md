# Save an extra 5% on bundles through Monday
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is one of the more confusing sends in Casper's recent run. The subject and hero promise "an extra 5% on bundles," but the very next module — bigger, brighter, positioned right below the fold — is "Save 20% on premium foam mattresses & $100 on Snug," a completely different offer with far more visual weight. Then four screens later, a "Bundles" tile flashes **"Save 25%"** — a third, contradictory bundle discount in the same email. A subscriber skimming this on their phone gets three different numbers attached to two different products and no clear sense of which one is actually true.
- Structurally it's a competent, on-brand Casper build — polished product photography, consistent navy/cream palette, clean CTA buttons — but the internal math doesn't hold together, and that undercuts trust more than a weak hero would. Compare to 8/10 sends like Aug 6 ("Vacation's Over, Sleep Isn't") or Jul 30 (bundles), where one number was hammered consistently throughout; this one buries its own headline offer under a bigger unrelated one and then contradicts it later.
- Judgement: mid-pack execution with a real credibility problem. Fix the 5%/25% mismatch and reorder the hero and this jumps a full point.

## What's working

- Clean visual hierarchy within each individual module — badge, headline, CTA button in consistent order throughout.
- "ENDS MONDAY" urgency badge is a legible, credible deadline signal.
- Product photography (lifestyle shot of the woman on the mattress, bedroom scenes) is warm and on-brand, not stock-generic.
- Good category segmentation at the bottom (Mattresses, Pillows, Bedding, Accessories) plus a text-signup CTA and mattress quiz — decent secondary engagement paths.
- No broken images or overlapping text — the render itself is clean.

## What's weak

- **Subject/hero offer (5% on bundles) is visually subordinate to a bigger, unrelated offer** (20% mattresses / $100 Snug) directly beneath it — the eye lands on the wrong deal first.
- **Direct numeric contradiction**: hero says "extra 5% on bundles," but the "Finish your sleep setup" bundles tile says "Save 25%." Two different bundle discounts in one email reads as a bug, not a promo.
- The promo code (BUNDLES) is only tied to the 5% offer, but nothing on the page clarifies whether it also applies to the 25% bundles tile or the 20%/$100 mattress offers — creates real checkout confusion.
- This is Casper's fifth+ Back-to-School-adjacent send in three weeks (Aug 6, Aug 4, Aug 2, Aug 1, Jul 30, Jul 28) — fatigue risk is climbing, and this one adds confusion on top of repetition instead of a fresh hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one bundle number and use it everywhere.** If 25% is the real, larger bundle discount, lead the whole email with it — don't undersell with "extra 5%" in the subject/hero while a 25% tile sits five screens down.
- 2. **Reorder the hero so the subject-line offer is the visually dominant one.** Right now the second module (20%/$100) out-weighs the module that matches the subject line — swap their sizes or their order.
- 3. **Clarify the promo code's scope** — either apply BUNDLES to the whole email's bundle offers or visually separate it from the 25% tile so shoppers don't feel misled at checkout.
- 4. **Cool the cadence** — six Back-to-School variants since July 22 is a lot; consider a non-BTS theme for the next send to avoid list fatigue.
