# Final hours to shop our Labor Day Doorbuster.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's Labor Day arc hitting its actual closing bell — "Last chance," "ENDS TONIGHT," "Save up to 35%" — and after seven straight sends citing the same 35% number, the true-urgency version finally lands with a clean, legible hero. The layout is well-organized: hero offer up top, model comparison table, then a long scroll of category tiles (bundles, pillows, bedding, furniture) each with its own %-off badge and Shop now button, which gives shoppers multiple entry points without feeling cluttered.
- The problem isn't this send in isolation, it's the campaign fatigue: per brand history this is the fourth-plus time "35%" and green-highlighter styling have appeared verbatim, and today's "ENDS TONIGHT" urgency is undercut by the fact that Casper has said some version of "final hours" or "today only" on 09/07 already. A subscriber who's seen the last three emails will read this as more of the same, not a genuine deadline event.
- Visually it's clean and bug-free, and "ENDS TONIGHT" is a stronger, more specific urgency cue than prior "final hours" copy. Net: solid execution, weak novelty — the creative well is running dry on this campaign.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: bold "35%" in a green highlight circle, dark "ENDS TONIGHT" badge, orange Shop now button all visible without scrolling.
- Mattress comparison table (Dream Max vs Snow Max) with support/cooling dot ratings gives real product differentiation, not just a discount wall.
- Every product tile below the fold has a specific %-off badge (20%, 30%, 35%, 10%) and its own CTA, so browsing shoppers aren't forced back to a single link.
- Categories are broad (mattresses, pillows, bedding, furniture) which suits a full-catalog "doorbuster" close-out.
- No render bugs — images, badges, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- "ENDS TONIGHT" is the fifth-plus urgency claim in this campaign (see 09/07 "FINAL HOURS," 09/07 "Today only") — repeated deadlines erode credibility.
- Same green-highlighter-circle treatment on "35%" and same layout bones as multiple prior sends — visually indistinguishable at a glance from the last two emails.
- Long, undifferentiated tile grid (8+ products) below the fold dilutes the "last chance, ends tonight" urgency into a generic catalog scroll.
- No countdown timer or specific end time given the "ENDS TONIGHT" claim — feels asserted rather than credible.
- Woman-jumping-on-bed hero photo has now also recurred across the campaign per prior audits, adding to the sense of creative reuse.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "ENDS TONIGHT" concrete — add an actual countdown timer or a stated cutoff time (e.g., "Ends 11:59pm ET") to convert this from campaign boilerplate into real urgency.
- 2. Trim the product grid — with true last-chance framing, cut to the 2-3 strongest sellers instead of 8 tiles; a genuine closeout should feel focused, not like a full catalog browse.
- 3. Swap the hero image — the jumping-on-bed photo has run before; a fresh visual would help this specific send stand out from the rest of the arc.
- 4. Lead the subject with the deadline mechanic, not the recycled "Doorbuster" label, to differentiate from 09/07's near-identical subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Mattress sale ends at midnight — up to 35% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Labor Day mattress savings end tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dream Max, Snow Max, and bundles — all on sale until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at 35% off before Labor Day pricing ends.`
