# Back-to-School savings on mattresses built to last.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Casper is repeating the exact same structural mistake from the August 8th "extra 5%" send, only two days later: the top module hard-sells "Save an extra 5% on bundles" with urgency ("ENDS MONDAY"), and then the very next module — bigger, brighter, better-photographed — undercuts it with "Save 20% on mattresses." A subscriber's eye and click both go to the 20% offer, making the entire top third of the email (and the whole 5%-bundle premise in the subject line) irrelevant. This is now a pattern, not an accident.
- Below that confusion, the email actually recovers well: the "Best-in-class comfort" and "Built for restorative sleep" product grids are clean, well-labeled, with consistent 20% badges and unambiguous per-product CTAs (Shop The One, Shop Cloud One, Shop Dream, Shop Dream Max). That's good, competent execution — it's just buried under a broken hero.
- Net: solid product merchandising, undermined by a hero section fighting itself for the second time this week.

## What's working

- The jumping-on-bed hero photo is energetic, on-brand, and clearly seasonal (back-to-school/back-to-routine framing).
- Consistent "Save 20%" badge treatment across all four product tiles makes scanning easy.
- Each product card has a specific, differentiated one-line value prop (targeted zones, pressure relief, etc.) rather than generic copy.
- CTA buttons are specific and per-product ("Shop Dream," "Shop Cloud One") rather than a single vague "Shop Now."
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- Two competing hero offers (5% bundles vs. 20% mattresses) stacked back-to-back — this is the same structural flaw flagged in the 2026-08-08 send, now repeated.
- The subject line promises "Back-to-School savings on mattresses" but leads visually with a bundle-code offer, not mattresses — mismatch between subject and top-of-fold content.
- "ENDS MONDAY" urgency is attached to the weaker 5% offer, not the 20% one, so the more compelling deal has no deadline pressure driving action.
- Fine print asterisks ("Savings based on Comp. Value," "Pricing based on Comp. Value") appear twice, which reads as extra friction/hedging in a promo email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the 5%-bundle module entirely, or move it below the product grid as a secondary offer. Lead with "Save 20% on mattresses" as the single hero — it's the stronger, clearer, better-designed offer and it's what the subject line already promises.
- 2. Move "ENDS MONDAY" urgency onto the 20% mattress offer so the best deal also has the deadline pressure.
- 3. Align the subject line and hero 1:1 — since the mattress-grid is the real strength here, make the subject about the 20% mattress sale, not vague "savings."
- 4. Drop or shrink the redundant "based on Comp. Value" disclaimers — one mention is enough; two reads as evasive.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save 20% on mattresses this back-to-school season`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Back to School mattress sale: 20% off, ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The One, Cloud One, Dream & Dream Max — all 20% off through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New routine, better sleep — shop 20% off Casper mattresses.`
