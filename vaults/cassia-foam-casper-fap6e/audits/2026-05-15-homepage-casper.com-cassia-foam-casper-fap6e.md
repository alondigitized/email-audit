# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened Casper and the very first thing that filled my screen was a massive cookie consent modal — I literally cannot see the homepage. There's a promo banner at the top teasing "Save up to 30% on mattresses" and a partial hero peeking behind the wall of legal text, but the brand made a privacy form the dominant experience. Casper looks like a clean, direct-to-consumer sleep brand, but right now it's leading with legal friction instead of mattresses.

## What's working

- **"Save up to 30% on mattresses" banner** — the one thing I can read instantly, and it's exactly what I'd come for. It's specific, it's above the fold, and it maps to my category.
- **Top nav category strip** (Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz) — clean four-option layout that lets me jump straight to mattresses or pillows in one tap, no hunting required.
- **"Take the Quiz" nav item** — this is peak Casper brand behavior and it's smart for a first-apartment buyer who might want to justify the spend with some science-backed matching.
- **Header chrome is clean** — hamburger, search, location, cart all render at normal size. No janky overlap.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal ate the entire homepage** — I genuinely cannot see the hero, the seasonal campaign, any product imagery, or any CTA. The modal is taller than my viewport. This is the single biggest friction I've ever seen on a homepage.
- **Partial "Memorial" hero text visible** — I can barely make out what looks like a Memorial Day campaign peeking behind the modal, but the decorative script font and the overlap make it completely illegible. If there's a sale tied to a deadline, I have no idea.
- **Three cookie buttons, no clear default** — "Reject Optional Cookies," "Accept Optional Cookies," and "Your Privacy Choices" are all rendered at equal visual weight with the same dark button style. There's no visual shortcut that says "just let me in." It's friction stacking on friction.
- **No social proof, no trial reminder** — the 100-night trial is Casper's whole identity and there's zero mention of it in the visible area. That's the thing that would close me.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — I always add a pillow to hit free shipping. That callout is nowhere in the above-the-fold zone.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or minimize the cookie banner** on return visits — if there's a cookie already set, don't show the modal again. At minimum, make "Accept" the visually dominant button (filled, high-contrast) so people can clear it in one tap and actually see the sale.
- **Pin the 30% off + trial message to a sticky sub-header** so that even with the modal up, the two most persuasive facts (the discount and the 100-night trial) are readable at the top of the visible chrome.
- **Make the Memorial Day deadline legible** — swap the decorative script hero font for something that reads at partial visibility; if the sale ends Sunday, I need to see that even before I dismiss the modal.
- **Add a "free shipping on orders $X+" note to the promo banner** next to the 30% off copy — one line, no extra tap required, and it would immediately set my pillow-add strategy in motion.
