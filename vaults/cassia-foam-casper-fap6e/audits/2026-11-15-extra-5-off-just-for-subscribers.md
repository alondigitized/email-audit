# Extra 5% off, just for subscribers*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, well-organized subscriber-exclusive send, and it reads as a downgrade from the 11/9 and 11/12 emails. Earlier sends gave a stronger "extra 10%" on Hybrids and Maxes with code 10BFCM. This one drops to "extra 5%" with code 5-BFCM. The hero stacks "Up to 35% savings on everything" with a lime "extra 5%" on navy. The code and a "Shop exclusive" pill sit directly below, so the offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The body is a long, repetitive stack of five mattress cards: The One, Dream, Snow, Dream Max and Snow Max. Each has a "Save 30% + Extra 5%" badge, a "top-rated 2026" claim and its own button. The Consumer Reports line is a credible trust hook. A bedroom-setup grid follows (bundles, pillows, adjustables, "everything else"), then an SMS signup and a mattress quiz. It's a competent catalog, but nothing is singled out as the hero product.
- Judgement: solid execution of a weaker offer. For a subscriber who has had three near-identical Casper promos this week, the 5% reads as a trickle, and the "extra" framing leans on stacked fine print (asterisks on the headline, the "Comp. Value" note and the "Top-rated" double asterisks).

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer first: a lime "extra 5%" on navy with "Use code: 5-BFCM" and a "Shop exclusive" pill beneath.
- "Subscriber Exclusive" is shown as a script badge at the top, which gives members a reason to feel special.
- The Consumer Reports callout adds third-party social proof.
- Each mattress card has a consistent "Save 30% + Extra 5%" badge, a short description and its own CTA, so the cards are easy to scan.
- The bedroom-setup grid gives clear category entry points with specific savings (35% bundles, 20% pillows, 30% adjustables, 10% everything else).

## What's weak

- The extra 5% is a step down from the extra 10% on Hybrids and Maxes in the 11/9 and 11/12 sends. It feels like a diminished version of an offer subscribers have already seen.
- Five near-identical mattress cards make the email very long, with no single hero product to anchor the click.
- Product descriptions lean on "top-rated hybrid mattress of 2026" repeated four times, which blurs the models together. Sizing, price points and comfort differences are missing.
- Asterisks and double-asterisks on the headline and product claims, plus a dense legal block at the bottom, undercut the honesty of "up to 35% savings on everything."
- There are no visible reviews or star ratings on the product cards, only the Consumer Reports mention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero mattress and its price, for example the best-selling Snow, and cut the rest into a compact comparison row. This would do the most to lift clicks.
- 2. Show an actual after-discount price ("Queen from $X with code 5-BFCM") instead of stacked percentages.
- 3. Add star ratings and review counts to each card, and write one differentiating sentence per mattress instead of repeating "top-rated hybrid."
- 4. Add a credible deadline, such as "Subscriber pricing ends Sunday," since none is visible.
- 5. Move the mattress quiz higher for undecided shoppers.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code 5-BFCM on top of our best sleep savings of the year.`
