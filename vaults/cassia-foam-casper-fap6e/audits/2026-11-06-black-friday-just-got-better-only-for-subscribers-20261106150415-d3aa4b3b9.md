# Black Friday just got better. Only for subscribers.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, well-organized subscriber-exclusive Black Friday send. The hero stacks "Save 30% on Hybrids and Maxes" with "Plus, save an additional 10%" and code 10BFCM, in lime on navy, so the offer is the first thing you see. It follows the Nov 4 "Black Friday is officially ON!" kickoff and gives subscribers a sweetener on top of the public sale. That is a real reason to send a second BF email.
- The body is the weak part. Four product tiles (Dream, Dream Max, Snow, Snow Max) repeat the same "Save 30% + Extra 10%" line and the same pill button. The page runs very long, and the repetition dilutes the hero. The stacked-discount math is also confusing. "30% + extra 10%" with a code means the shopper has to work out the final price, and the asterisk on "Savings based on Comp. Value" hints at fine print.
- Overall this is solid and on-brand, but it is a catalog scroll. A tighter version with a clear "up to X% off" headline and fewer tiles would convert better.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: a big lime "10%" and "Save 30%" on navy, with a "Subscriber Exclusive" badge.
- The code 10BFCM is shown right in the hero, so there is no hunting for it.
- The exclusivity angle ("Only for subscribers") gives a reason to open even though the Nov 4 send already announced the sale.
- Each product block follows the same pattern of image, one-line benefit and button, which makes it easy to scan.
- The "More ways to upgrade for less" grid adds quick secondary offers (35% off bundles, 30% off adjustable bases, 20% off pillows, $100 off Snug) without a lot of copy.

## What's weak

- All four mattress tiles carry the identical "Save 30% + Extra 10%" line, so there is no differentiation or price anchor.
- The stacked discount ("30% + extra 10%") is hard to turn into a final price. Nothing shows a dollar amount, such as "Queen from $X".
- The email is very long, and the mattress lineup repeats near-identical bed photos.
- The hero has three competing elements: "Our absolute best discounts!", "Save 30%" and the giant "10%". The extra 10% visually outweighs the 30% base.
- The dense, tiny fine print at the bottom, plus the asterisked "Savings based on Comp. Value", weakens the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one number. Use "Subscribers: up to 40% off" or show a real price, and treat the code as the secondary step.
- 2. Cut the four product blocks to two (Hybrid and Max picks), or move to a side-by-side 2×2 with a price on each.
- 3. Add "from $X" pricing and a star rating or review count on each product to give social proof and anchor value.
- 4. Put a deadline in the hero (for example "Subscriber pricing ends Sunday"). Right now the email has no time pressure.
- 5. Auto-apply the code through the link so the shopper doesn't have to enter it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 10% is inside (code 10BFCM)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack it on 30% off Hybrids and Maxes. Subscribers only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use 10BFCM before the public sale catches up.`
