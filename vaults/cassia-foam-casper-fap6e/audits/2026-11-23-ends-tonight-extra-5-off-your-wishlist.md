# Ends tonight! Extra 5% off your wishlist.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built last-call send that sells the right thing: a "Your Bed Has a Wishlist" theme, an "ENDS TONIGHT" kicker, and code 5-BFCM repeated under every block. The navy-and-lime palette matches the 11/21 "Time for an upgrade!" email. The wishlist idea gives each category its own mini-headline ("Support. Lots of It.", "Pillows You Don't Fight With.", "Sheets Worth Canceling Plans For."), and the copy has more personality than most Black Friday sends.
- The structure is also the problem. The hero offers no number, only "your extra 5% savings tonight." The actual discounts (30%, 20%, 10%, 35%) sit five scrolls down, and the 35% bundle deal is buried fourth. Six or more CTAs of equal weight compete, so no single path stands out. This is the 7th or so Casper send this month, and the same 5% code appears in the 11/21 and 11/15 emails. The urgency is credible, but the offer is thin.
- Verdict: good creative and copy on a weak hero. Lead with the biggest number and cut the module count.

## What's working

- "Ends tonight" is the first line of the hero and is a credible deadline on 11/23.
- The wishlist concept is cohesive. Each category block gets a short, voicey headline and a one-line benefit.
- Code 5-BFCM is repeated under each offer, so it's easy to find without scrolling back up.
- The lime offer lines ("Save up to 30% + Extra 5%") stand out against navy and are easy to scan.
- Photography is warm and consistent, and the product imagery looks premium.
- The gift card block at the bottom is a smart catch-all for gifting.

## What's weak

- The hero has no headline discount. "Extra 5%" is the weakest number in the email, and the 35% bundle is buried in the fourth block.
- Six or more CTAs ("Shop the Wishlist," "Shop Mattresses," "Shop Pillows," "Shop Bedding," "Shop Bundles," "Shop the Gifting Bundle," "Shop Gift Cards") dilute the click. The "Shop the Wishlist" button is vague.
- The email is very long, and the hero image of a woman making a bed doesn't show the product or the offer.
- The footer has dense fine print under a bare asterisk subject, and the exclusions aren't visible up front.
- Cadence fatigue: it's another 5% code send after the 11/21, 11/20, and 11/15 emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the strongest offer in the hero: "Up to 35% off + extra 5% tonight," with the code and one button. Move the bundle block up.
- 2. Cut to three modules (mattresses, bundles, gifts) and drop the separate pillow and sheet blocks, or fold them into a grid.
- 3. Replace "Shop the Wishlist" with "Shop up to 35% off."
- 4. Add a countdown or "Ends midnight PT" line to make the deadline concrete.
- 5. Add social proof, such as star ratings or review counts, near the mattress block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 35% off + extra 5%`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bed's wishlist closes at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code 5-BFCM stacks on mattresses, pillows, and bundles. Last call.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Support, pillows, sheets, and a $199 gift bundle. Tonight only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is well-formed. It has a doctype, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals, `role="presentation"` layout tables and a hidden preheader. The automated QA run raised only warnings, and the main gaps are unverifiable compliance headers and a truncated HTML source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing:** 12 material links were probed and 20 skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). QA reported no failed or redirecting probes, so I found no broken links.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img width="1" height="1" alt="" src="https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/…/open" style="display: none;">`.
  - This is the Bloomreach/Exponea open pixel. It is `display:none`, so clients that ignore that style or block remote images may not record opens.
  - Open counts are unreliable regardless, because Apple MPP and image blocking both distort them.
- **Plain-text part:** 17,983 of 19,838 characters (about 91%) are URLs. This points to long tracking-wrapped links in the text alternative, which hurts readability and can add spam-score risk. It is a real warning, but low severity.
- **Verification gap:** the HTML is truncated after the first `<table class="nl-container">`. I could not check the CTA hrefs, the footer links or the unsubscribe URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image alt text:** QA flagged the open pixel (`cdn.us1.exponea.com/.../open`) as missing alt text. This looks like a false positive, because the source has `alt=""`. An empty alt on a decorative tracking pixel is correct. No action is needed on the pixel.
- **Other images:** I can't assess alt text on content images because the source is truncated.
- **Outlook:** the MSO conditionals are in place (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `PixelsPerInch 96`, sup/sub fixes).
- **Web fonts:** the two stylesheets from `brxcdn.com` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which is correct. Clients that strip `<link>` tags will fall back to system fonts.
- **Preheader:**

Unconfirmed. The truncated source doesn't show the destination hrefs, so I can't verify UTM parameters or landing-page alignment. The probes returned no errors, but QA doesn't report final URLs or parameter retention.
