# $11 Original Pillow for 11/11!*
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy, on-brand 11/11 send with one hook: an $11 Original Pillow when you buy a mattress. The hero puts "11/11, make a wish!" in big lime type on navy. A "Use code: PILLOW11" sticker sits on a model hugging a pillow, so the offer and the code are the first things you see. The subject matches the hero, which helps.
- The problem is that the body then turns into a standard category catalog: Foams, Hybrids, Maxes, each with a "Shop X" pill. A pillow re-pitch and a Black Friday "up to 35%" banner follow. That gives the reader three offers in one scroll: the $11 pillow, the BF sale, and the 11/11 theme. Compared with the 11/09 "extra 10% on Hybrids & Maxes" send, this one is a fresh hook. It still repeats the same navy and lime Black Friday template that has run since 11/04, so a subscriber will find it familiar.
- Judgement: solid, and the creative holds up. The offer has a catch, though. The pillow only costs $11 if you also buy a mattress, and the hero buries that in tiny type. The email would do better if it said so up front.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. Big lime "11/11" and "Get an Original Pillow for $11" on navy puts the offer first.
- The "Use code: PILLOW11" sticker makes the mechanic concrete, and the code is easy to spot.
- The 11/11 and "make a wish" theme ties the date to the price, so the offer feels timely and playful.
- The layout is clean, with paired product tiles and consistent pill CTAs, so the email scans quickly.
- The brand voice is on point ("a gift that blows your own socks off", "not the sale to snooze on").

## What's weak

- The "when you buy a mattress" condition is only visible in the hero subline and a tiny asterisk. The $11 reads as free-standing in the subject, which is a bait-and-switch risk.
- Three offers compete in the body: $11 pillow, BF "up to 35%", and the product tiles. There is no single dominant ask.
- The three mattress sections (Foams, Hybrids, Maxes) show no prices, specs or reviews, so there is no reason to prefer one over another.
- The pillow gets its own mid-email block, but its "Shop now" link is generic and the $11 headline is smaller than the hero's.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, with a double asterisk on the Black Friday line.

## Recommendations

- 1. State the condition in the hero: "$11 Original Pillow with any mattress". Put the real deadline in visible type, not only the footer.
- 2. Cut the body to the hero, one featured mattress with price and a review snippet, and the pillow block. Drop the three category sections or collapse them into one row of links.
- 3. Make the hero CTA specific, such as "Add my $11 pillow", and show a "from $X" mattress price.
- 4. Add the Black Friday banner only if it adds something new, because it echoes the 11/04 and 11/06 sends.
- 5. Add real preview text. Nothing visible in the render suggests one is set.

- **Subject Alt B:** `11/11 only: pillow for $11 + 30% off beds`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add an Original Pillow for $11 when you buy a mattress. Code PILLOW11, today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus save up to 35% on everything. Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT.`
