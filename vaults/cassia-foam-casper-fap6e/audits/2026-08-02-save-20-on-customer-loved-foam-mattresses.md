# Save 20% on Customer-Loved Foam Mattresses
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, hero-first send — "Save 20%" hits above the fold in bright green highlighter, backed by an unambiguous "Shop Foam Mattresses" button before you even scroll. Unlike the July 30 bundle email or the July 22 "Back to School" send, this one drops the seasonal wrapper entirely and goes straight at product: foam mattresses, specifically The One and Cloud One, each with a real customer quote doing the persuasion work.
- The structure is smart — hero offer, then two dedicated product modules each with its own "Save 20%" badge, testimonial, and CTA — so a scroller gets the pitch twice before hitting the trust-building "10-year warranty" section. It's a more product-led, less promo-fatigued read than the last several Casper emails in this history, and reads as legitimate cadence-breaking rather than another repaint of the same sale.
- The weak point is that this is Casper's fifth-ish discount email in under two weeks (25% on 7/22, 20% on 7/28, bundles 7/30, back-to-school 8/1, now 20% again on 8/2) — the discount itself has stopped being news, so the win here is entirely in execution, not offer novelty.

## What's working

- "Save 20%" in high-contrast green highlighter is the first thing the eye lands on — strong visual hierarchy.
- Two named products (The One, Cloud One) each get a photo, a badge, a real customer quote, and their own CTA — this is specific, not generic.
- Customer quotes ("I am blown away," "I'm not in pain anymore") function as social proof directly next to the offer.
- The 10-year warranty / 100,000 rollator-cycle stat adds credible differentiation beyond price.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- This is at least the fifth markdown email since July 22 — "Save 20%" lands as background noise for anyone who's opened the last few.
- The asterisk on "Save 20%" ("*Savings based on Comp. Value") is a soft trust-eroder — comp-value discounts read as inflated-then-discounted pricing to a skeptical shopper.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the email — no end date, no "ends tonight," nothing that would push a fence-sitter to act now versus later.
- The mattress quiz CTA near the bottom competes with and dilutes the primary "shop now" intent this late in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Save 20% — through Sunday") — this email has zero urgency mechanics, and Casper has used credible urgency well before (the 7/22 relaunch); reintroduce it here.
- 2. Drop or footnote-shrink the "*Savings based on Comp. Value" so it doesn't visually compete with the headline offer — keep the badge clean, put the disclaimer in the fine print at the bottom only.
- 3. Cut the mattress quiz block or move it above the warranty section — right now it's a third CTA competing with two product CTAs and dilutes intent right before the footer.
- 4. Since this is the nth 20%-off send in two weeks, consider bundling in a loyalty/repeat-customer angle (e.g., referral credit, early sale access) to justify why this email exists versus last week's near-identical one.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `"I'm not in pain anymore" — see why customers love these two`
