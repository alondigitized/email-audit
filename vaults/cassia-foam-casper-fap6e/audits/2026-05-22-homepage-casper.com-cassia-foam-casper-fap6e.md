# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Casper is clearly running a Memorial Day sale — the announcement banner and what I can barely make out behind the privacy modal both point there. The problem is I literally cannot see the homepage: a full-screen cookie consent wall is blocking every inch of the hero and content below. For a brand that built its whole identity around a frictionless, anxiety-free mattress buying experience, leading with a wall of legalese is a pretty painful irony.

## What's working

- **"Save up to 35% on mattresses" announcement banner** — this hits me exactly where I live. Holiday markdown is my buying moment, and Casper leads with it in the sticky top strip. That's the right message above the fold.
- **Category nav row** (Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz) — clean, icon-forward, one tap to where I need to go. "Pillows" being right there is great because I always add one to hit free shipping.
- **"Take the Quiz" shortcut** — smart for a confused first-time buyer, and it signals Casper still plays the helpful-not-pushy card.
- **Casper logo and wordmark** — clean, confident. Brand recognition is immediate.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie consent modal** — this is the homepage experience right now. Nothing else matters until I dismiss it. For a DTC brand whose whole ethos is "we made buying a mattress easy," this is a brutal first impression.
- **"Save up to 35% on mattresses" — "up to" language** — immediately reads as hedge-y. I've been around long enough to know "up to 35%" usually means most things are 10-15% off. It's not dishonest, but it's not clean either.
- **Hero is totally invisible** — I cannot see imagery, primary CTA button copy, or what the Memorial Day campaign actually looks like. Whatever Casper spent on that creative is wasted on me right now.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always add a pillow to hit it, but I can't see if that deal is still running.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no star ratings, review counts, or "sleep trial" badge visible before the modal.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal on scroll or after a 2-second delay** — or at minimum shrink it to a bottom bar so the hero isn't fully blocked. Every second this modal lives is a bounce risk.
- **Make the banner copy more precise**: swap "Save up to 35%" for the actual best deal ("Save up to 35% — Memorial Day Sale ends Monday"). Time-box it.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the announcement banner strip** — even just "Free shipping on orders $X+" next to the sale message. I will tack on a pillow to hit it; tell me the number.
- **Put a review count or "100-night trial" trust badge in the nav area** — something like "⭐ 4.8 · 50K+ reviews" costs nothing and gives first-timers and returning shoppers instant reassurance before they even see the hero.
